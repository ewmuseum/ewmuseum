Asian Pride Within.

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-06-2002 Ain't No Traditional Chinese... Nope! I ain't your normal fob you guys think I am. I'm Yoshio Akinaga! Best RUSH jobber in your face! Perfect Yoshio Akinaga! Perfect... except with feelings this time! Arr... damn it how many times do you want me to say it? Don't you know wrestlers are very sensitive to the j word? Jobber? Shut up already! God damn it... last time! I'm Yoshio Akinaga! Best RUSH jobber in your face!!! Is there a word in Chinese, Japanese, Korean or whatever you are that says means RUSH? What the hell are you talking about? Of course not! Now can I get the hell out of here? Uh huh... just let Sally over here take a pictures with you posing then your free as a panda. What the hell... was that just a racist comment? No of course not fish eyes! What the hell... your asking for a beating! Ummm... heh... No? Yoshio Akinaga throws a paper towel into the trash can as he walks by a crowd of visitors taking pictures left and right. Akinaga is asked by a fellow asian blood to take a picture with him. He glady accepts as Akinaga takes the camera and gives it to a older lady who seems to have lost her teeth. Excuse me... could you? MMMmmmm! The lady takes the picture of Akinaga and his fellow asian brother. Akinaga pats him as some girl in her mid 20's walks up to him wearing a short skirt and a pink top. Mr. Akinaga! Yes? Haha... great to meet you Mr. Akinaga, I'm Sally Dao! I'll be taking pictures of you today! Ah... vietnamese? Yep! Proud of being asian too! Good, good… I love pho for some reason, maybe its the texture of the noodles or something. Haha... well maybe we'll go out sometime and eat that shall we? Sure why not? Ok now follow me... Hmmm ok, but dinner? 7:00o clock tommorow sound good? Yeah sure why not? Sally gives a smile as Yoshio looks at the sky and mouths the words 'Thank You God' as he brushes his RUSH t-shirt looking at Sally's body parts. You know.. that was a great match between you and Joe Bananas! Heh.. I guess so... except I don't see the use in being tagged with a fruit. Literally huh? Haha Ahh I don't care, its the fight that matters. And if a fruit is going to get into my way, he’s going to get squashed. Haha… ok well you know that white guy and his big mouth. We better get this done before he has a fit. Lets do it then… Akinaga and Sally walk down a hall where they enter a room on there left, nowhere to be seen. Fade To Black.
