Statuz Que?

Date: I think this makes up for my lack of presence lately. The sun hangs low and red in the sky. Clouds dissipate, unable to hold their shape against the blistering Southern heat. The entirety of the earth below is tinted in a sickly yellow-orange. It’s as though the ground and everything on it had been dusted with a thin layer of clay. Our vantage point trickles to the ground like a feather caught up in a fresh breeze. Slowly, slowly we make our way to ground level from the heights of the air above. Amidst the barren, rust-colored streets and houses, toys and “pimped-out” cars litter the grass. Lawns are covered in discarded remains of fantasy, those of both child and adult. Screen doors flap idly in the breeze, the “clack-clack-clack” sound echoing down the streets for no one to hear. The trees add a rustle to the urban cacophony, swishing in the flowing air in the only scream that foliage has ever been permitted to scream through the pitiful torments of nature. Where were we? Oh yeah, never got to that part. ATL, mah fuckaz! Otherwise known as Atlanta, Georgia. Home of the Falcons, the Gold Club, and everyone’s favorite Piru, Statuz Quo. Well, second favorite, if you don’t count that guy who always brings Quo and Jackson the bad news. Sure, he’s only on TV for a few seconds at a time, but damn if he ain’t good at what he does, right? So on a pound for pound level, that guy, (I dunno what his name is – let’s call him Darnell) is the best. But on a strictly finesse level, I suppose it’d be fair to say that Quo takes the nod. Of course, that also depends on how heavily you weigh screen time when making your rankings. I mean, there could easily be a kick-ass Blood who’s just a bit camera-shy, and so you never get to see him in action. I suppose on some levels he’d be like the “underground” version of the Bloods. Again, if we were to focus only on primary, mainstream cats, then again, all signs point to Statuz. But then you neglect the….you know what? Let’s just get back to the story. So we’re on a street in Atlanta. It’s red out. Very gloomy-red-bright-type of day. Sorta. Right? Right. And here we are, in this glimmering Hub of Atlanta, the crimson-brown tinted southern locale taking its position as temporary epicenter of the universe. And why, you ask? Why for the undersized, pale, mask-wearing hero of our story, that’s why! There he stands, in the middle of the Atlanta street, hands pressed firmly against his hips in the most impressive pose he could muster. The Ecko jeans slide down his backside, revealing the word “Spiffy!” across the tights. The matching jacket flaps in the wind, and in between ripples, we can make out a title scrawled across the back in embroidered script. “Statuz Que?” Yeah. That’s right. I’ll pause here so you can read that again. … Yup. That’s what it said. Seems kind of a shame that all that ambience was wasted on a joke like this, huh? But they say hindsight is 50/50, right? Wait, that’s not right. Maybe it’s- “HEY!” What? “While the Spiffy! is still young, eh?” Oh, right. The promo. My bad. “Stupid cheap narrators. Where’d they get you from?” I used to work for J. Rish. “Yikes. Is no wonder…” What’s that supposed to mean? “Nothing. Just get on with the Spiffy!’s narration!” Fine. prima donna… “What was that?” Nothing. “Yeah. That’s what El Spiffy! thought.” *sigh* Finally breaking the champion pose, he removes the backwards Yankees hat from his head and fans it wildly against his masked face, trying desperately to fight the sweltering Georgia heat. “It is time for the Spiff Star to find out what is so damn-ed great about Atlanta, why it is the sh*t hot!” “Hot sh*t.” “Why it is the hot sh*t!” “Though I guess sh*t hot would work, too.” We swing 180 degrees to behind our hero, to a shot of a familiar decked-out gold El Camino that hasn’t been around since before the NTICW merger. And why is it back now? Because RUSH only covers plane flights to Atlanta if you’re the Syndicate champ. Inside the gleaming vehicle sit the three men – well, two men and one android – who accompany El Spiffy! everywhere…less for his company and more for the safety and well-being of those he may encounter over the course of the shallow groove of destruction/annoyance that he carves through the world. One: Juan Carlos Smith. Sombrero removed and waving against the heat on the driver’s side of the car. “We really should’ve stopped letting him make the decisions.” Two: Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil.. Reading a magazine as usual. Watching Spiffy! out of the corner of his eye. “He makes the money. Unless you get us jobs replacing Raves and Williams.” Three: RoboGates. In matching Ecko gear. In the backseat. *beep*“Buy an Xbox!”*boop* “Shut the f*ck up.” “Should we follow him?” “Of course we should. If his ass dies on the streets of Atlanta, we’ve got five days to find a replacement.” “The robot can fight him.” *beep*“Go to Bill Gates’ Burrito Sha-” *CLANG* *boop* “You know, that’s not what The Club is for.” “It worked, didn’t it?” “I guess so.” El Spiffy! begins to saunter down the empty street, with a purpose in mind that only he would be able to understand. Reluctantly, Juan Carlos shifts the Camino into gear. “We’re going?” “We’re going.” “That’s all there is to it?” “That’s all we can say.” We begin to pan up and away again, into the sky. Back on the ground, we see our hero triumphantly stomping down the street, with a golden El Camino rolling slowly behind him. “That narrator used up half of our air time anyway.” F*ck you, pal.
