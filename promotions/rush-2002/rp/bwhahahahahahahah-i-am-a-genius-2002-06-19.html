BWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH!!!!! I Am A Genius!

19 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 19th, 2002 A.D. 2 Hours Before Deamont Receives The Police Phone Call. Our scene opens up inside the locker room of one Chris Matthews. This is only a few minutes after his triumphant match with Brock Newbludd, D-Kay Calyx and friend, Tyler Valentine. Clothes and sports equipment are scattered all over the locker room, a few open bags sit in the corner, with Matthews’s belongings spewing out of the top. Conversing in a corner, with a phone to his ears is none other then Chris Matthews himself. Matthews taps his foot on the hard, thin carpet as the phone rings, obviously impatient for the other line to pick up. “Come on” Matthews annoyingly says into the phone, “hurry up.... bloody phone.” Matthews folds his arms as he continue’s waiting, the tiny red Cell-Phone pressed tightly against his ear. It continue’s ringing for a few seconds, Matthews growing even more annoyed. “How long does it take to answer to a call?” he spits out at the un-answered end. Matthews, annoyed, lightly punches the wall. From the other end of the line, there is a scrunching sound heard and a rustling of a voice. Someone had answered it. “Hello?” the other line answered, it was a woman’s voice. “This is the New York Police Service, how may I help you?” Clearing his throat, Matthews speaks into the small phone with his response. “Yes, my name is Chris Matthews....” “Wait” the police officer interrupts Matthews’s request. “Are you Chris Matthews, the one from RUSH Wrestling?” “Yes, yes I am he.” Matthews responded, shaking his head. “Anyway, before we get sidetracked, I’d like to request a restraining order against someone.” “Okay” the officer replies, rustling of a pen can be heard in the background. “And whom would you like this restraining order to be taken out on?” “First name: Cassius, last name: Deamont” Matthews quickly said, hushing his voice slowly as a few RUSH employees walked past. Typing of a keyboard quickly follows the request of Matthews. “Okay Mr. Matthews” the officer replies, happily. “Just need to get a reasoning for this restraining order from you and it’ll legal.” Matthews, still with his arms folded, leaning against the door wall, began to stroke his chin. “Well” he began to say to the police officer. “Over the last few weeks Mr. Deamont has been making numerous threats to me off-camera, and I’d just like to feel safe knowing that I won’t be attacked for no reason when I’m at home....you get the picture.” “Okay” the police officer replies back, typing of a keyboard is once against heard in the background. “And how far is the distance on this restraining order?” “I want him kept away from me by at least 20 feet, until our match next week on Adrenaline!” Matthews excitedly speaks into the phone, then looking out the obscure door of his locker room and into the hallway, checking for the presence of unwanted others. “Okay, this is pretty much legal.” The officer said back. “But before I finish this, if Mr. Deamont breaks the restraining order, what shall his fine be?” “Oh I don’t know” Matthews confusingly said, then a grin spread across his face. “Since old Cassius has a loads of cash stashed away, make it...$500,000 American.” A bigger grin spread across Matthews’s face. “Ok” the officer replied, a sound of a computer printing can be heard in the distant background. “We have it in writing here, if Mr. Cassius Deamont breaks a restraining order of 20 feet, he will be fined $500,000 US.” “Excellent!!” Matthews replied, with a big grin and then hanging up the phone. Walking over to his bag, he stuff’s a few of his belongings into it, and the phone as well. Quickly zipping it up Matthews grabs his jacket and starts to head out the door, with a big grin on his face, he runs into none other then...Tyler Valentine. Valentine and Matthews both jump back, startled. Matthews regains his composure, still with the big grin on his face, as Valentine changes the straps on his bag. “Why have you got such a dumb grin on your face” Valentine enquires, also smirking back at him. “Oh Tyler, Tyler...Tyler...poor misguided Tyler.” Matthews chanted, slowly making Tyler frown. “Tell me!” Valentine protested, making Matthews snicker. Valentine’s frown intensifying. Matthews smiles a sheepish smile, then slaps his friend Tyler on the back. “Tyler” Matthews begins to say, that same big stupid grin on his face. “I’ll tell you.... over a burger at McDonalds!” As Matthews walks out the door, Tyler pumps his fist into his chest before calling out to Matthews. “Dude! You’re paying!” Tyler slams the door shut before running down the hallway, trying to catch up after Matthews, as our scenes closes we hear another slamming of a door shut within the hallways of The Hudson Valley Civic Center, Poughkeepsie, New York. OOC Note- Tyler Valentine was used with full permission from Kenny (Tyler Valentine).
