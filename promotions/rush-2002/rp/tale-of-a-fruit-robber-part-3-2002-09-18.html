Tale of a Fruit Robber (part 3)

18 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th September 2002 The old bus; rusted and well beyond its years, was on its way to Canada. Fuelled by the wheezing smoke and pathetic propulsion system, this was no transportation fit for kings. That is probably why it is no surprise to see Joe Bananas taking advantage of such cheap prices, and travelling in poor and smelly style. Instead of the usually monotonous routine of staring out the window into the mundane scenery flying by, Joe had taken a shine to an old man who was sat right next to him. Speaking of theology, philosophy and the overpricing of basic groceries; this was a conversation for the ages. “But I thought all you Jamaican folk liked quality and plenty of it.” Said the old man, scratching his liver spot roofed head with a wrinkled finger. “Dat’s stereotypes mon. Da man jus spread round bad quality food anyway, Ah only eat da bes quality Ah can find personally.” Joe replied, a serious look of intrigue and thoughtfulness on his face. “Doesn’t that just mean you’re cheap?” The old man asked, a small smile peaking from the many folds of what would possibly considered a face. “Ya can’t say dat! Da man jus sell fruit around. If dey sellin dat, Jah knows what e’eryt’ing else has in it!” Joe snorted, unknowing that his attempt at satire was totally misunderstood. “What’s wrong with fruit?” The old man asked, trying as hard as he could to disallow the confusion excite him too much. “It’s evil! Dat’s what’s wrong with it. How can ya eat people, no matter how misunderstood dey are, and say it’s all sweet as a potato pie?” Joe asked, horror stricken and disgusted. “Huh? People, I thought we were talking about fruit.” “You, mah old brejerin, have been brainwashed by da powers dat be. Dey did it ta Jesus and now they doin it ta fruit. From Jews ta da Juicy minority. It aint right, Ah tell ya!” Joe exclaimed, slamming his fist repeatedly into the back of the seat before him. The occupant of this seat was a middle aged woman, with a shrill voice and piercing stare. She had no difficulty in turning around and peering over at the man who had punched her seat so blatantly. “What do you think you are doing young man?” The woman asked, sticking her nose up to the roof. “Ah can’t contain mah rage when dis subject comes up, da man had ta be stopped for dis slavery!” Joe replied without lowering his voice in the slightest. “There’s no need to shout.” Said the old man, soothingly. “I’ve got every right ta shout! Da oppression has got to stop!” Joe wailed, his volume growing instead of decreasing. “What is this about? Black rights?” Asked an overweight lady from a nearby row. Everybody turned their eyes to Joe, standing up from his chair and raising his arms. His eyes popping out from his head, as if unattached to him at all. He clenched his fists and waited as his throat bulged, his sound level increasing to a huge shout. “Ah preaching about da rights of fruit! Dis business cannot be supported by ordinary people, it is awful and Jah knows it aint humane. Murder most rahtid happens every day, an da minority needs support from people like me wit limbs.” Joe preached, his hand moving down to his heart with pride. A mumbling erupted from every passenger, the small children and babies beginning to cry their eyes out. Many scuffled across to the driver at the front of the bus and exchanged sour looking comments, looking back at Joe every now and then in case any more declarations of the needed fruit freedom were made. Before long the bus came to a sudden stop, the dusty road squirting a brown haze up from the tyres. The chubby man in a green uniform followed the pointed fingers and grabbed the accused maniac by the scruff of the neck. “You’re disturbing the other passengers in a big way, you’re gunna have to get off I’m afraid.” The bus driver declared, forcing his prisoner along towards the already opened door. Joe squealed as he flew through the opening and his jaw hit the dusky road, hitting the ground hard. Without a second thought the pneumatic system allowed the door to close back up and the bus was back on its way north. Joe picked himself up and dusted his RUSH t-shirt and jeans off. He peered around him and found nothing more than an old man at a stall. Joe began a slowly paced walk towards the man, he needed to get to Canada as quickly as he could. “Hello sir, how can I help you today?” Asked the stupid looking man, his gap toothed grin setting off his greasy complexion perfectly. “Ah need ta get ta… whas dis ya sellin?” Joe asked, looking down at the half broken table before him. “Ah, I’m selling fruit from that there orchard ya see. They’re good quality and good eat in my opin…” Before the hillbilly could come to the end of his sentence, Joe dived forward over the take. With his arm extended he managed to knock the man own and begin his assault. Freeing the poor, defenceless fruit from his evil clutches at long last.
