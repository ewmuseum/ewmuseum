Birth of a Starr: The Wonder Years (part 2)

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/6/02 “Mr. Starr, doesn’t it take you aback one bit that the fortune you are now in possession of, the inheritance of Joshua Dubois or better known as “Josh the Starr”, has been controversially known to have been amassed in some of the most illicit of ways through the years which Starr Enterprises existed as an business entity?” Oooh…. I think this is the part where she drops the bombshell and I break out in cold sweat… A familiar lady in a white crisp business suit which emphasized simplicity as elegance spoke those very words out in a hard-line manner meant to strike a chord with somebody in particular which would most likely be the man sitting opposite her reading the morning papers in a posh sitting room. As she pauses to let that question sink in, the man across her simply continues flipping through the headlines in the paper as if that was a casual question meant to be answered at his leisure. As he finally puts down his newspapers, folding them simply to his liking and convenience, the man in question finally reveals himself to be no other than the self-titled “Natural Born Talent” Ricky Starr, part of the RUSH talent roster. After taking this “serious” question into a slight consideration, he utters his one-worded reply. “No” The time when the first Starr, the legacy carried on by the second generation which many praise and hail today in the pro-wrestling entity known as RUSH, proved himself to be an adept not in his first successful corporate move, nor was it even in his first racket… It was in the simple secondary education institution known as Hartford High Many of them taught of him as nothing more than a chestnut haired, hazel-eyed, broad shouldered, smiling All-American boy with the simple yet decent intention of being the best he can be at every aspect of high-school life. It was a brilliant, if not THE best façade he could ever have. While Joshua proved himself to be more American than apple pie in the many an eye of his peers, not many intellects could even half-fathom what truly went on in that furious mind of his, the mind of an opportunist, a ruthless mental vassal, a brilliant sentient organ with an array of senses that perceived more than so many others around him could in an hour with just a momentary glance. It was the mind of a Starr Everybody knew he excelled in the English language, the arts and even the Math and Sciences, although it is a controversial piece of history in Hartford that he was the first winner in both Math and Sciences category in the school’s history but also the first winner denied the prize due to high-school politics. Everybody knew he was a prize quarterback, wearing the Hartford High jersey with pride (or what resembled pride), second only to a boy who slept, dreamt, ate and talked football, excelling in nothing else but that. Everybody also knew that he was Vice President of the student body council, delivering inspiring and astounding addresses with the skill of an orator beyond his years. He was almost everybody’s friend, everybody’s pride right up to being everybody’s hero (Joshua insisted for the rest of his life that he never wanted it, it was shoved right down his throat by everybody else) However, there were also things that not everybody knew. “Joshua told and taught me many things, lessons which he learned, wisdom which he wished to impart with one who would soon bear his legacy. There was a particular one that went something like this, one he took from and early age…” Ricky my boy, this one thing as an opportunist, as a man of intellect, as a Starr you have to learn… “When there’s something you can exploit, exploit it the best you can. When there’s profit to be make, go out there and make it”However.. “There are always and will be those who resent you because of that, because you prove yourself to be the sharper eye, the faster hand and the smarter mind. They view it with a wide variety of negative perspectives right up from jealousy to the plain ridiculous, morally wrong for instance. You just have to accept that as a part of life Ricky. Nothing in this world is without opposition. At the end of the day, it just might be you against the world…you against the world…” Then again… “When they move from push to shove, when things start getting murkier, dirtier, smellier and a lot more uglier than they really should be, when they just won’t give up on the call then it’s time to take action” Just remember… “When the smoke clears and the action’s over, just make sure that nothing, absolutely nothing is pointing towards you at the wake” Now to the other side of the man we all know as Joshua Dubois, a side probably darker than the dark side of the moon, one very well kept secret if not the best kept secret. With a beautiful façade like that, Joshua Dubois could carefully pull out his moves without raising the slightest of suspicions. Wheeling and dealing all over the place without the shadow of an indication that it was even remotely connected to young Josh. His hands were never soiled, nor was his countenance ever fouled the slightest. Those who were closer than blood to him said that he, Their Mastermind, created these opportunities out of nowhere. Only the closest to him knew, a long time after, that he saw the gigantic niche in front of him since day one. In a short span of time, privileges became profit, rights became tradable commodities, if not currency and most importantly… The greenback came flowing in. And this was just in his high school years mind you. He didn’t push drugs, no, too much competition and blood being shed on the urban pavement. It blew way too big out of proportion for Joshua to even half-consider it as an option. Brute force? Hell no, that was the way of the brawny idiot who has no other alternative in life. Organized crime? Close but no cigar. Joshua liked the money that comes with it but having way too much blood on one’s way to the top was too heavy a price for the young Dubois. The bootleg industry took too much time and money to set-up and the constant risk of a bust was just too much of a hassle to bear in a backdoor business. Joshua Dubois conducted trade in a commodity that fitted perfectly in the high-school environment, one that brought with it minimal risk, if not none, and maximum profit. The perfect business for an opportunist and an intellectual. Josh spotted it and saw a large, empty niche just waiting to be exploited, very few dealt in it and usually not for profit. What people saw at the time to be a source of elightment, Joshua saw it as a profit-making scheme just waiting to be worked. It was a game ahead of its time yet graspable and plausible once learnt. It would become apart of the world in half-a-century to come. By the end of the semester, Joshua Dubois, an All-American 16 year old high-school student of Hartford High was running a successful business, call it racket if you wish, trading in one commodity and one commodity only that brought with it a handsome profit on a near-daily basis… Information “With all that unfolding clearly before me, I must honestly admit that I am still dumbstruck and in awe at such a complex labyrinth of furious calculation and thought churning at such a tender age. How did you take it Mr. Starr when he related such a narrative for you to hear?” Ricky Starr casually sips on a Moccachino as the attractive lady seated across him in a crisp business suit awaits a response with a focused look intended to put pressure on Ricky. He looked up at his leisure and smiled charmingly at her. “It’s understandable for anybody to feel the way you do but then again, just try to remember…” ”You are dealing with a Starr”
