The Ultimate Opposition down and out...... or NOT??

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 4 RUSH... I thought the work to myself once more like I had about a million times today already. RUSH Wrestling. A federation that was supposed to be ran by Day Johnson, a legend in my books. A person who had good skills and would run it well. Then it was found out that Jeremy Howard was indeed the owner of RUSH. At that time, I didn’t care. I was riding high in NTICW, friends with Scott Taylor, Teresa Tomas, and Andi the Magnificent and in a hot feud against Semore Smoke and his MSCW buddies. I would have never thought that I might be working for Jeremy Howard or feuding with other RUSH members. They were in their fed and I was in mine. Then a week later, I show up at Mayhem, pumped for the main event later that night. I had all my stuff ready and I get a message to “come to the ring this instant”! I didn’t know what it was gonna be about and had no clue that I would soon be fired and then re-hired by RUSH. The announcement was both good and bad. A bad thing was that Scott Taylor was off to OSW, meaning that the Ultimate Opposition was done for and fallen apart. Also, Semore Smoke was off to PCW, meaning that I was out of luck for a good feud. The list went on and on about the bad things about being in RUSH but I tried to forget all of the bad things and focus on the good things that would be ahead. One thing that was both good and bad was that I would get a chance to build myself up in RUSH like I did in NTICW. It was good because I could become better than I was in NTICW but it was bad because it would take time, starting all over again. Either way, I hoped that one day I would face Cassius Deamont for the RUSH title and hopefully beat him. Another good thing was that there was more talent for me to face. Instead of the thirty that were in NTICW. There were now not only the twenty that went over to RUSH but also the thirty that were on the RUSH roster before that I had never faced before but hoped that I would face. This wrestling business was all about strategy and you got thrown around a lot. One minute your in NTICW, a main eventer with friends and a good stable, on top of the world. The next minute your in RUSH, your group broken up, your thrown around, don’t know where you stand, whether you’ll be a jobber or a main eventer and your basically fighting for a job! All these things happened in an instant. One thing that was really on my mind was Scott Taylor. I didn’t really know Scott that much. When NTICW opened, Scott was just another wrestler that I might be able to face some day but he really meant nothing to me. When I had won the NTICW Extreme Championship, I had held it for a bit but one night, I checked for my match and who would I be facing? Scott Taylor! The match was great. We both had strengths and weaknesses and we had respected each other from that point on. I had lost that match, losing my extreme title. I never heard of Scott for a little bit. I never heard of him until the Rock On! PPV that was. I had my three-way match for the US title against Lance Perfection and Caleb McGlocklan. Scott had his World title match against Semore Smoke. I had lost the match but after the PPV, Mona, who was owner at the time, came up to me and said that I had talent and I should expect a world title push somewhere in the near future. I was totally pumped! I had no clue that Scott had won the world title until at the hotel when I saw the news report on a wrestling channel of the new NTICW World title. I then realized something. I realized that if I was getting a world title push, I would be facing the one man that had cost me my only title in NTICW, Scott Taylor. I started hating Scott Taylor, feuding with him and fighting him. When I looked at the Battle in Brazil PPV card, I saw my name down. Main event. Three-way between me, Semore Smoke.... and Scott Taylor. I was pumped for the match and the thought of being the world champion, still hating Scott. I entered the ring for the match, excited. The match started off great with nobody getting the upper-hand. After fighting for about ten minutes, we were all tired. Nobody had the upper-hand until something happened. I don’t exactly remember. I remember a big bump and then being pinned for the three count. It wasn’t until after the pin that I had realized that I had been pinned by Scott. The minute I got pinned. The SECOND I got pinned, I really respected Scott. I really respected how he wrestled. It was odd but true. I then decided that if I can’t beat him, join him. Semore Smoke had Boda and the MSCW goons and we were all outnumbered. I started talking to Scott Taylor and after some talk, the Ultimate Opposition was formed. It was me and Scott, obviously. Then there was Teresa Tomas. Tomas was the manager of Scott and also a wrestler so she was perfect. We picked Andi for his magic. We knew that his magic could come in advantage at one point or another and we were formed. We basically were now a team and my hatred for Scott was all gone and filled with respect and friendship. Now that the Ultimate Opposition is basically put down now that Scott Taylor is in OSW, I have a big decision to make. Everyone knows that to be successful in a fed, you need to make friends and alliances with the good people. In RUSH, just because Scott Taylor is now jamming in Old School Wrestling, why does that have to automatically mean that the Ultimate Opposition are done? We have three members of the group still here and there is plenty of talent in RUSH. In fact, I think I know a person that would fit the Ultimate Opposition quite nicely. Is the Ultimate Opposition down and out...... or not?
