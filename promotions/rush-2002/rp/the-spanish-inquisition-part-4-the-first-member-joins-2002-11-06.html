The Spanish Inquisition! (Part 4, the first member joins)

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/6/02 Earlier, Stimpo had been at the parade for America declaring that the latinos would soon rampage the world--I mean...gather together.... Stimpo set out on a quest to find as many latino helpers for his justice league that needed a name and that name came to his mind... Stimpo: THE SPANISH INQUISITION! Stealing the old Monty Python catchphrase, he decided it will be the true name of the group of latinos he planned to start up. Stimpo knew he needed something to make a Spanish Inquisition, members. Stimpo needed some people of latino origin or some others that would join their cause, or lack of. Stimpo found himself at an old Rochestarian park with many colorful, leaf-full trees in amazing abundance spreaded throughout the park along with the required parks and always, the small rides to entertain the kids while some people gossip about the week's hard work. It was a Saturday morning and the park was buzzing with people, in search of the select few to become members, Stimpo started immediatly. Stimpo: Eenie... (Stimpo pointed his finger out at a woman wearing sunglasses, tan color, a spanish shall and a small dimple that stood out on her cheek) ...meenie... (He moved to a man dressed in a blue button down shirt, black docker pants with stain defender with a dark skin color.) ...minee.... (His finger passed over the figure of an amazingly large white man who was at the moment, scarfing a meatball sub, missing the meatball as it slided down his white shirt with pit stains and wearing baggy wind pants that seemed that would soon break while in his other hand, a comic book: Vol.6, Number 3 of Shaquille O'neal, the aftermath.) ...MO! Stimpo's finger rested on a built man sitting with his legs crossed intellectualy. Bearing a mask upon his face with long blonde hair booming out of the covering. Seeming as an outcast, the same man had what looked like white eyes staring straight in the face of Stimpo's. Wearing a 'gothic' type black see through shirt with blood red lettering and covering areas under his waist were stylish, plain gray pants. Stimpo: Could it be....? It was. The same Masked Mullet in the RUSH Federation of Wrestling. Thought Stimpo and Mullet had different views in the wrestling business, Stimpo knew he needed other 'masked man' to stay known, but yet, anonymous. Stimpo proceded into sitting down next to DA MULLET~! Mullet: To what do I owe the horrible honor of you, Stimpo? Stimpo: I was just on a.....walk. Mullet: No one walks anymore. Stimpo: Latinos walk. Mullet: What are you getting at? Stimpo: I don't know. Long pause flows through the air as the wind blows a tumbleweed by the two men. Mullet: Why are you here fool. Stimpo: Latino Uprising will come. Mullet: And? Stimpo: Will you be there with the cause? Mullet: What for? Stimpo: I have a mask, you have a mask. Mullet: We got a genious folks! Stimpo: Thank you, now will you join us? Mullet: What's in it for the mullet? Stimpo: You'll see when you arrive there. Don't be late, toodaloo. Stimpo drops a small piece of paper folded up professionally, although there are many wrinkles as if Stimpo tried many times to fold it right. Mullet was purplexed when Stimpo said toodaloo, because no one says toodaloo anymore, not even....latinos! Mullet shook his head as he started to read the note to himself, nodded his head, folded it back up right and walked away from the bench as Stimpo was already gone with a grin on his face as his first person had seemingly joined up in....THE SPANISH INQUISITION!!!!!
