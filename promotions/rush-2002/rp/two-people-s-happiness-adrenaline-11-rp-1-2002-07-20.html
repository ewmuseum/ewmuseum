Two People's Happiness (Adrenaline 11 RP#1)

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/20/02 Two people’s happinessare the same... Two people’s happiness are differently done... Two people’s happinessare shielded... G.O.D. was happy, but more importantly Dr. Jenkins was happy. It had been truly The Genetic Oriented Drone’s first squash match. He had decimated the competition. He beat every single one of them. Thunderous moves. “Thunderous.” That one word had gone through both men’s minds during the match. As G.O.D. continued through it he had gone from his hulking artificial self to something much more. In his mind he had become a piece of Greek mythology. He had become “thunderous.” He had gone from G.O.D. to the God of thunder, the king of the Gods, Zeus. He had imagined himself as Zeus. Now in his mind G.O.D. was atheist. He had come to his own beliefs as Dr. Jenkins had suggested. He had thought about this for awhile. He had tried to get all his thoughts straight and eventually came up with what he believed. He was an atheist because he believed that there was no evidence to support it. The fact that there were so many religions also had a factor in that statement. It was what he believed. However he did think the stories were interesting. They were as realistic as dreams. They were fiction books that Gene enjoyed reading. They were characters that one could dream about. That one could ponder and think of as they slept. … G.O.D. had daydreamed. In the match G.O.D. had imagined himself as Zeus. He was the God of thunder letting out his bolts of lightning on his victims. He was all powerful… because he was Zeus… but he could not tell Dr. Jenkins… Dr. Jenkins enjoyed the match thoroughly. He saw G.O.D. smashing and crashing his opponents without fail and he lusted for it. It made him feel strong that G.O.D. overpowered these people and Jenkins overpowered G.O.D. It made him feel good, egotistical, arrogant… and slightly sinister. ‘More will come.’ He had thought. ‘More, more, more. I need more. I will have more. No one can stop almighty G.O.D.’ His insane ways went on. Needless to say Jenkins had become eager and vile. But he was right. He would have more…
