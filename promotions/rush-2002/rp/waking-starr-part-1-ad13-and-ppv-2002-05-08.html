Waking Starr {part 1} (AD13 and PPV)

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/02 In the light of recent events I have taken a new stance towards many other things in other areas of my life. Notably the legacy, which I had the good fortune to inherit It used to be common knowledge that I never gave a damn about what that racket did as long as the hard, cold cash rolled into my pockets. As life takes me to even stranger places, I deem it appropriate to change my attitude to the legacy that served no more a purpose than a bill-printing machine. Changes do happen and I seem to be the one making them And it starts now. Hit it. It was just another day at the HQ office for Starr Enterprises, which occupies an entire 14 floor building saturated in reflective glass. It was a symbol of elegance and pride, a towering imposing figure over other, smaller offices and operations in the business district. Staffed with a more than adequate amount of manpower with operations ranging from trading to more global business entities such as manufacturing, retailing, provision of services to the co-owning of countless of other business entities. Simply put, the place had money written all over it, money both old and new. A board of experience hands and minds in the Starr collective, having ties to the organization that spanned decades, are the brains of the operation so to speak. Joshua DuBois, or better known as Josh The Starr, the reclusive and enigmatic self-made millionaire who reeked of business charisma and skill founded the empire and hand-picked these heads, mostly recognizing them as underachievers and ignored students in his college batch. When he passed away, the primary shareholder position fell to his adopted son, a Bosnian refugee he adopted, now a renowned name in world of sports entertainment… Ricky Starr The old man saw the quickness and potential in the frail teenager he took in. Educating him but never coercing him into any kind of position, he gave the boy, now a strapping young man, and the freedom to grow into whatever pleases him, as long as he didn’t tarnish the name of Starr too much. He pretty much knew that Ricky would stray from the affairs of the enterprise and old man Josh never resented him for that. However, Josh knew that the enterprise, like every other business entity out there, would have its sharks, kissing ass one moment and stabbing you on the back the next. He paid them well but this was a fact of life, there was bound to be an ungrateful bastard out there somewhere wanting more. He wouldn’t be watching over the enterprise forever and Josh knew that more than anybody. Never has there been somebody so aware of his own mortality. He never feared it coming but he knew for the enterprise to survive, he needed a contingency plan, more of an automatic failsafe. This was where Ricky came in. Josh, although he was a radical in his times, never ignored the old sayings for they always had an amount of factual truth in them. “All roads lead to Rome” He believed that in the end, it would all lead back to the enterprise. Josh was sure of that Right down to his last breath. Things were going along the lines of ordinary at the lobby for the Starr Enterprises building. Most of the life consisted of men and women in crisp three-piece suits carrying briefcases or folders. Ground floor receptionists were either mostly on the phone or conversing with others while the occasional white uniform of security staff make their slow trot along the walls of the lobby, some sitting behind panels in a dedicated vigil. Most of the day would be progressing in this fashion right down to closing time. However, this was not an ordinary day. The doors burst open. A man, fair skinned, handsome in his own roguish way, bursts into the lobby wearing a dark blue collared shirt with a black jacket on top of it, both straight off the Hugo Boss catalogue .The young yuppie look is topped off with a pair of black pants from the same collection and a pair of Clarke leather shoes to match. Flanked by quite a number of people, he had the look that became the envy of so many others around him that moment’ He was young He was in charge. To his right was a younger, slightly taller man in sunglasses. His attire, although casual, was no less costly than our yuppie wear. A zip-up gray collared shirt and a long black leather trench coat on top of it followed by a pair of cream-colored cargo pants. A pair of sleek sunglasses from the Killer Loop variety with lime-green lenses adorns his eyes but does not completely conceal the seriousness that his face carries. To his left was a lady, attired in a crisp white business suit slinging a small handbag on her right side while holding a notebook with her left hand, hinting that she’s from the media. Flanking the trio are six security personnel, their faces as emotionless and humanly possible while crowds of reporters make an attempt to swarm in on the trio. With all the noise, movement and flashbulbs going off like repetitive lightning, it was quite the unusual spectacle at the normally settling atmosphere of the lobby. The yuppie trots up to the reception desk and in a low voice, called a young, strawberry blonde who had just hung up a phone. With a practiced smile and effortless courtesy, she attended to the yuppie, who did not really seem to mind all the activity and attention around him. “How can I help you sir?” The yuppie looks at the strawberry blonde in the eyes with a roguish smile meant to curtsey and tease at the same time. She blushes slightly but quickly regains her composure. “I would like to call an assembly of the board of directors. Tell them that the next best thing since Joshua is in the house” The strawberry blonde motions her desk partner to come over and whispers into her ear. She nods in approval and trots off. The blonde’s attention is concentrated back on Ricky as she gives a reply. “You may wait at the conference room at the top floor but it may take a while for them to reach the office” The yuppie straightens his jacket as the man in sunglasses beside him surveys everything around them. The lady in the crisp business suit smiles at the blonde receptionist as a gesture of thanks. The yuppie, ready to move, points a finger at the elevators. “Lets go” They make their way to the gleaming elevator doors in a brisk pace, flanked by more security personnel as the reporters follow them through. A burly guard greets them at the elevators and in a matter of seconds, an elevator door opens and a courier boy exits the lift but not without glancing at it’s next occupants as he walks out. The line formed by security personnel prevents the reporters from storming into the lift. The yuppie waves and poses for one last photo for the press before the doors shut. With three of them alone in the lift, a conversation ensures. The yuppie makes the opening move “Good to see you back bro” The man beside him, still wearing his sunglasses despite the soft artificial illumination around him responds as he scratches the back of his hair which seems to be dyed red save for the black roots. “Yeah, yeah. Sure you know what you’re doing?” As the lift comes to a halt, the doors slide open slowly and three of them walk out. The yuppie leans towards his companion and speaks a delayed response. “Of course, should’ve done it a long time ago” As they reach the end of the corridor, two guards open up a large wooden door, leading into what looks like a conference room. The room is empty but some preparations have been made. A loaded slide projector is visible sitting on a stand at one end of the room as the screen has already been pulled down for convenience. Folders, agenda for today’s meeting have been arranged into a neat stack at one corner of the large black marble table, which is practically empty save for a black plastic name tag at the very end of the long table. Everything seemed to be in order in the room as the trio enters. The yuppie reaches for a large, plush looking chair usually reserved for the highest in an organization’s hierarchy and seats himself on it, looking out onto the view of the urban horizon from the large window before focusing his attention back on his two companions, who seem to have no objection to his actions as of late. The man in sunglasses and trench coat speaks out as he strokes his fairly short hair back. “So…what do we now?” The yuppie moulds his face into a smirk that seems startlingly familiar. A smirk that speaks of confidence, strength and even a touch of arrogance. He picks up the black plastic name tag that happened to be in front of him and on it are printed these words in gold. Ricky StarrCEO of Starr Enterprises With a smile, he looks back up at a man with a brotherly expression on his face. “We wait” .::To Be Continued::.
