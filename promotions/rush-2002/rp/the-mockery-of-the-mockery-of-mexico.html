The Mockery of the Mockery of Mexico

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: How's this for a workrate? 'Wrapped up' my ass. Dante Jackson smells like poop. That is all. ---- Oh, what a surprise? We’re in Jersey again. In a hotel. At night. Rudimentary to the point that it’s almost becoming a cliché. And monotonous to the point that you want to get the hell out of East Rutherford. We (as in the camera and you lucky viewers along for the ride) make our way down the hallway, past the jilted workers plastering over the hole in the wall that showed up from an “altercation” the night before. We reach the door, and suddenly, we’re inside. Sitting on the two beds in the room, the Spanish Announce SuperTeam of Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil are dissecting tapes of other RUSH matches involving Playaz, Inc. At the table by the window, El Spiffy! is doing possibly one of the stupidest things he’s ever done to date: playing Bloody Knuckles with the Bill Gates android known commonly as RoboGates. “Did you see that?” Juan Carlos requests, pointing anxiously at the screen. “He lags a bit when he hits that.” “Yeah, it’s a small window though.” “If you’re fast enough you can catch him on it though. You hear that, Spiff?” There is no verbal response from the other side of the room. Instead, there’s a slight metallic whirring sound, followed by a resounding smack and the sound of a man trying his best not to cry out loud. With that, of course, we head over to check out the action. El Spiffy!’s hands are a wreck. Wrapped in paper towels and taped up to avoid long-term damage, they look…well, they look like someone threw them in a blender and then asked a kid from remedial kindergarten to sew them back together again. Pain-inducing is a word that comes to mind. RoboGates, however, is slightly better for wear. In a “Vote Spiffy!” T-shirt and one of those fancy green visors that card dealers wear, he doesn’t seem to have and problems at all, not surprisingly. A lit cigarette hangs from his mouth, but doesn’t burn too fast, what with robots not having lungs and all. Maintaining his rigid consistency, he raises both hands again in preparation for another round. El Spiffy! tries his best to look pitiful as he prepares to launch another attempt. He’s saved by the interrupting voice of Juan Carlos. “Spiffy!? Are you paying attention to this?” “Of course the Spiffy! pays attention! What would make you think the Spiffy! does not pay attention?” “Who’s your match against this week?” “…” “Well?” “The Spiffy! is thinking…” “Jesus, man, this is a HUGE match for your future and the future of the Shack Pack! Could you at least TRY to care?” “El Spiffy! will do his best.” “You’re lucky that Dante Jackson is an idiot and Quo’s a pompous ass. The former is down in Mexico trying to dig up dirt on a guy who doesn’t exist, and the latter is pulling the old ‘stay in Atlanta until hours before showtime’ game.” “The Playerz Ink is not here?” “Neither of them. We have a full three day jump on them if you’ll do something worthwhile instead of playing games with that stupid robot.” RoboGates turns his head slightly and gives Juan Carlos a raspberry. As his robotic tongue sticks out, the cigarette in his mouth falls to the ground. He leans one hand down to grab it, and El Spiffy!’s eyes light up. In an instant, the Spiff Star pulls out a ketchup packet (a Bill Gates’ Burrito Shack Catsup Pack!) and squirts the red condiment onto RoboGates’ unattended other fist. Then, without thinking, he tosses the pack away and throws a right cross at the ketchup-covered fist, and another sharp CLANG resounds throughout the room. With a cry of pain, El Spiffy! falls to the ground. “El Spiffy! (wince) made you (groan) bleed, RoboGates! (gasp) El Spiffy! wins! (moan).” RoboGates looks at his fist, then at El Spiffy!, then back at his fist, and hangs his head in shame. El Spiffy! hops – well, hobbles – back to his feet and tries to raise his arms in the air, before painfully bringing them back down to his side. “Will you quit goofing around?!” Juan Carlos demands. “At least do something constructive!” “Fine,” El Spiffy! says, turning back to RoboGates. “El Spiffy! challenges the RoboBill to slap-boxing.” “Jesus Christ…” El Spiffy! puts up his dukes, so to speak. RoboGates rises methodically from his chair and begins to do the same, until El Spiffy! catches him on the side of his metal head with an open-handed strike. “A-ha! The Spiffy! is already ready for-“ *SMACKACLANG!* A lightning-fast retaliation from the android catches El Spiffy! full-bore in the face. His eyes roll up into his head as he hits the ground, out like a light, with ketchup smeared across his mask. RoboGates admires his work, then returns to his seat and his cigarette. The announcers, having witnessed all of that, are dumbfounded. As they both just stare at the fallen form of their luchadore, Julio breaks the silence. “Should we call medical personnel?” “Nah. I don’t want to have to explain this like I did last time. “Good point. So what should we do?” “Hope he wakes up and forgets he’s a wrestler, if you ask me.” “You wanna call Livewire and fill him in on what we know.” “Yeah. Might as well use this somewhere.” Julio turns towards the phone as the camera catches a close shot of the unconscious form of the Spiffster and we fade to black.
