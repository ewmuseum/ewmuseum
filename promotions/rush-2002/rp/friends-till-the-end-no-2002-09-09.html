Friends Till The End......? No.

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 9th, 2002 A.D. My RUSH World Championship: Gone. My Best Friend: Gone. My Fans: Gone. My Self Respect: Gone. My Family: Gone. All gone. Everything that was once held dearest to me, gone. My entire life revolves around one prize, well, at the moment, that prize is the RUSH World Championship that I only had the joy of holding for eleven days.... eleven days. My best friend. It was my best friend who cost me my World Championship. He tried to act like a ‘good guy’ and do what’s right by the fans – to make them cheer him to swell his girly looking head even further. It was him, the person I thought was my best friend in the whole world just had to do the right thing. The right thing cost me the World Title. Why couldn’t he of done the wrong thing? If he had of done the wrong thing I would still have the twelve-pound World Championship in my lap. I would still be called the leader of RUSH after a mass exodus of talent, the man who leads RUSH into the future.... but no. All because of Tyler Valentine’s good heart. It was all taken away from me. All taken away from me by some prissy little good guy with blonde hair, and named after the ‘day of life’. Fuck that day. The objects I hold closest to my heart, all gone. This is what I thought in my head as I sat in front of the Television, in my Five Star hotel room, in Philadelphia. I kept playing the tape over and over again, listening to James Williams and Kris Raves shout and scream over the thunderous screams of the Pittsburgh faithful. By now, I must of replayed the tape ten or fifteen times, I just couldn’t believe it.... ....he beat me. Nobody happens to realize when a drunken figure came stumbling out from the back, a steel chair in hand. The only real thing you can tell from him is a twenty-pound title belt hanging over his chest.James Williams: BROCK NEWBLUDD IS OUT HERE!Kris Raves: NO! HE'LL FUCK IT ALL UP! TURN AROUND CHRIS, TURN THE HELL AROUND!Matthews doesn’t hear the words Kris Raves begins shouting from his microphone piece. And then, of course, something he doesn't notice, the World Champion standing behind him. He hops down, signalling that it is all over, and Valentine isn't getting up. As soon as he turns around, he and Brock Newbludd's chair have an intimate conversation.THWACK!James Williams: CHAIR SHOT! BROCK NEWBLUDD HAS JUST KNOCKED CHRIS MATTHEWS THE FUCK OUT, AND THE CROWD IS GOING APESHIT FOR IT!Crowd: Brock! Brock! Brock!Kris Raves: DAMN YOU NEWBLUDD! GET UP CHRIS!Matthews has his arm dragged up into the air, still feeling the pain out of Brock Newbludd's chair shot, before being spun around, and DROPPED ON HIS HEAD WITH THE FLASHBACK, causing the fans to jump up in excitement, bringing an amazing cool over the entire crowd. I watches those scenes countless times, hitting rewind every twenty seconds to watch the one moment of my sixteenth match in RUSH, my sixteenth in succession for this company which I’ve stayed loyal at, even after the mass exodus after Crossroads. I am RUSH. I couldn’t believe it, Tyler Valentine, beat me. The guy I met down at the Primetime Wrestling Academy when I was asked to come down for some demonstrations for the new lot of guys and girls. I knew the kid had talent, I knew he’d make the big time, but I never thought the kid would actually beat me in a one on one match. Heh, I still remember the day he phoned me when I was on my way to Adrenaline 2, about his acceptance to another of the Primetime based promotions – NTICW.... * * * * Ring. Ring. Went the sound of my Cell Phone as I sat in the Taxi, speeding from the airport in Indianapolis. I shoved my hand into my pocket and pulled out the tiny little red Cellphone, pressing ‘Go/Speak’ to start the conversation with whoever was on the line. “Hello?” I asked in the phone, breathing through my nose. “Hi Chris.” Said the person on the other line. Who was this person? “You might not remember me, but I was at the PWA Training Facility when you came down there – Tyler Valentine.” “Ahh” I said back, only now remembering that blonde haired kid. He was the kid who could pull off a Suplex and DDT out of nowhere. “I remember, now.” “Yeah” he said back, obviously a little intimidated by me. Odd. “I was just calling you, to tell you that I got promoted from the PWA!” “Awesome.” I responded back, I wonder if it was RUSH...nah, Jeremy would of told us after last week. “Where to....?” “NTICW!” he said back, really excited. I felt my stomach groan at the thought of a very talented kid like Valentine going to a cursed place like New True Internet Championship Wrestling. “I can’t wait till I debut on Mayhem, and to wrestle with the likes of Scott Taylor!” “Scott Taylor?” I retorted back quickly, the name bringing a sour taste of hatred, and agony of an injury I’d rather forget back into my mind. “Yeah, Scott Taylor.” He said back enthusiastically, completely oblivious of my past run in with Taylor. “Do you know him or something?” “Ye... it doesn’t matter.” I said, not willing to discuss this with the upstart young rookie. “Well, great to know that, but I’m just about to leave for some promotional event. I’ll catch you later.” “Later.” He said, as the phone line went dead. * * * * Those memories soon faded from my mind, once again. I didn’t know that that particular phone call, on a warm May morning, would lead to an upstart rooking named Tyler Valentine becoming my best friend – something I’ve never been able to hold onto for longer than a month. Lucky for me, NTICW shows were on a Tuesday, and usually in the Mid-West, or the New England areas so I could go along and watch Valentine wrestle the mid-card, and still be able to get to Adrenaline the five hours prior to the dark matches – like how Jeremy wanted. Our friendship grew, surprising me even more. Normally, I’d be making fun of NTICW with the rest of the RUSH guys after our normally better than great shows, but whenever Valentine phoned me or I hung out with him after shows – all was forgotten about that. Growing up I never had a lot of friends, especially through high school. When I got a friend, it was short lived. Nobody wanted to be around a loser in High School. I guess when I got into wrestling, and got on Television I lost that loser moniker I had picked up in High School. I never really had friends in wrestling either, I’ve never been a Tag Team wrestler, so I couldn’t form a Tag Team with someone competitively and on a consistent basis. I always played a heel, which probably is the reason why I never had many friends. Something was different about this Valentine kid. I didn’t want to punch his face in when I saw him after shows, kind of like what I wanted to do everyday to the people at my High School. I thought it’d be friends till the end. But his good guy image ruined our friendship. He’s going to pay for that. Pay for costing me the World Championship. We’re friends no more.....
