Victoria Caprice...Foxy Lady?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show the outside of the Iron Wolf Fitness Center located in the heart of Milwaukee, Wisconsin on a warm and breezy morning. The sun can just be seen peering over the top of buildings in the eastern sky, indicating that it is still quite early in the morning. We pan around the parking lot of the fitness center to see that it almost completely except for three cars. A little rust covered Geo Metro and an another rust-bucket in a Ford Escort are over shadowed by a mint condition 1969 Boss 429 Mustang painted jet black. The license plate reads “Blood-1” signifying that this classic American muscle car is owned by none other than Rush superstar, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd. We see that the car is still running, for we can hear the low rumble of the massive engine that sits inside of the classic car. Then, the car shuts off and the drivers and passenger’s side doors swing open. Stepping out of the drivers side is “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd. Brock is clad in a pair of baggy fitting blue jeans with black jump boots. A tight fitting wife beater shows off his quite impressive physique, we notice that Brock has a new tattoo on his right bicep, it is of an eagle perched upon the mythical sword, Excalibur. Brocks short brown hair is hidden underneath a white baseball cap that reads “Innovator” across the front of it. A lit cigarette hangs from Brock’s top lip, and a can of Miller High Life is in his hand. Out steeping from the passengers side is Brocks best friend, “The New Legend” Stephen Blood, who has now hung up the wrestling tights for good. He is clad in a pair of baggy fitting black denim shorts and a red wifebeater. Blood has a can of Miller High Life in his hand also. Both men shut the doors from behind them, and walk around to the trunk of the car…) Stephen: Man, you really showed that jobberhead Nasir… (Brock just smirks as he pops the trunk of the car…) Brock: Yeah, that f*ckin jobber didn’t need my help, he practically destroyed himself a few times in that match. I mean, lighting a pool table on fire? That’s just child’s play for me… ( We zoom over the top of the to see the contents inside the trunk…) Stephen: I see you got the workout supplies… Brock: When don’t I? (We see that inside the trunk is two gym bags, presumably belonging to the two Blood Brothers. The other contents of the trunk are two twelve packs of beer, and a carton of Marlboro Lights cigarettes. Brock grabs his bag and the carton of cigarettes, while Stephen grabs his bag and the two twelve packs of beer…) Brock: God, I love working out! (Brock and Stephen share a laugh and highfive as Brock shuts the trunk and the two begin to walk off towards the front entrance of Iron Wolf. As they start to walk, we can notice that Stephen has a considerable limp from his knee injury That ultimately ended his wrestling career…) Brock: (Looking down at Stephens rehabilitated knee…) How’s the knee? (We zoom in on Blood’s knee to see a long scar from where the doctors cut him open…) Stephen: Great, man, I can walk on it now. Brock: Sweet ass…now I don’t have to go fetch you beers anymore, lazy bastard. (Both men take a swig of their beers and continue to walk on…) Stephen: You sure that b*tch secretary this place has is gonna let us bring beer inside? (Brock slams the rest of his beer, throws it over his shoulder and shrugs his shoudlers…) Brock: If she doesn’t, f*ck her we’ll go in anyway… Stephen: True… (Brock and Stephen make it to the front doors, when we hear the squeal of tires and the rumble of a motor. Brock and Stephen stop in their tracks, look at each other and spin around, looking at what caused the noise. We also pan around to see a rusted out white van pull up in front of the two men and stop. The van has “Rush Crew” spray painted on the side of it. Brock looks at Stephen curiously…) Brock: Tell me that Rush reporters are not like Cal, I hated Cal… Stephen: Dude, f*cking anything’s better than Cal! (Brock nods his head in agreement and looks back at the van which is now parked a few spaces down from him and Stephen. The drivers side door opens and a middle aged pot bellied man holding a camera step out. The man scratches his balding head and waves at Brock and Stephen, who just frown at the man…) Stephen: Why are all cameramen bald and fat? (Brock gets a look of confusion and curiosity on his face…) Brock: Ya know, I’ve always wondered that too… (The chubby cameraman walks around the truck and waits for the passenger to get out. The door on the passengers side opens and stepping out is something that is the totally opposite of a fat cameraman….) Brock: Holy… Stephen:…Shit… (Stepping out of the van is a woman that is well known to most Rush wrestling fans, but apparently not to Brock or Stephen. This sharply dressed woman is none other than Rush’s lead reporter, Victoria Caprice. Her long blonde hair is wrapped in a bun and she is wearing a tight blue dress that matches her equally colored eyes and also shows off her shapely physique. She sees Brock standing there and waves at him, smiling. Brock gawks at her and waves dumbly…) Brock: Dude, this chick is hot. Stephen: Uh-huh… (Victoria walks up to Brock and Stephen with a bounce in her step. She walks right up too Newbludd and smiles at him…) Victoria: Well, Mr. Brock Newbludd, nice to meet you. Same for you Stephen. I’m Victoria Caprice from Rush. I’m sure we’ll get to know each other better some other time. So, I’ll get straight to the point, can I have a interview with you Brock? You’re a hard man to track down, you know that? (Brock scratches the back of his neck trying not to look at Victorias cleavage which is hanging out of the tight blouse she is wearing…) Brock: Um…I try my breast, I mean best, best! Hehe… (Victoria gets a strange looks on her face and Stephen chuckles at Brock who gets red in the face…) Victoria: Erm…yeah…so, how about that interview? Brock: Yeah, right, shoot. (Stephen pats Brock on the shoulder and throws his gym bag over his shoulder…) Stephen: I’ll see ya inside bro, later Veronica. Victoria: It’s Victoria. Stephen: Right, like the underwear! (Stephen smiles and walks off through the front doors of the fitness center. Victoria gives her overweight cameraman a thumbs up signal, and the large man slings his camera on top of his shoulder, turning it on as well. The cameraman nods at Victoria who immediately produces a mic from her purse and smiles into the camera as it foes from live to dead air…) Victoria: Hello RUSH fans, your very own Victoria Caprice here with a man who has indeed left his mark in Rush since joining the fed. His demeanor is as nasty as ever and he proved to the world at Ignition that he is indeed dangerous. This man is none other than the man, the myth, the legend, Brock Newbludd. Brock, congratulations on defeating Mohammed Nasir at Ignition, it was a great match. (Brock lights up a smoke and smiles at the camera as Victoria puts the mic up to his lips…) Brock: Victoria, was there really every any doubt that I wouldn’t win? I mean, c’mon, it’s “The Inno-F*ckin’-Vator” in a bar room brawl against a foreign assclown who has no talent at all. Of course I was going to win! Nasir was just my stepping stone to bigger and better things in Rush, and I would have to say that he did his job well. (Brock takes a drag of his cigarette and runs his hand through his short brown hair…) Victoria: Well, what about your upcoming no DQ matchup against Ricky Starr on Adrenaline? Brock: Man, the fun never stops does it? First I get to demolish a jobber at a bar, now the next week I get to do it in a no DQ match. Life is good! But, seriously though, I have no heat with Starr. Hell, between you and me Vicki, I didn’t even know the jobber existed until now. *Chuckles* But, never the less, I will bring the pain like only I can do. Victoria: Brock, what is your goal for this match against Ricky Starr, anything to prove? Anything to gain? (Brock strokes his goatee and smiles…) Brock: I’ve already proved myself to the higher ups that I can beat the best, so this Ricky dude will just be the exclamation point on it all. So, my goal for this match is the same as all my other bouts, kick some f*cking ass and do it in style! And that is Sad But…True! (Brock puts out his cigarette, pats Victoria on the ass and gives the camera the middle-finger before turning around and walking straight into the fitness center. Victoria shakes her head and looks back at the camera…) Victoria: This has been Victoria Caprice for Rush… (And to that we slowly fade to black…)
