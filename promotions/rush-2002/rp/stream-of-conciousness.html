Date: on one of those frequent whims When you cut the heads off of chickens, they don’t run around. They die. Go fig. I mean, not like The Spiff Star is some sick chicken-killing weirdo. It was more of an experiment. A lesson in analogy. I skipped out on the Posse for a bit. Needed to try some work on my own. Juan Carlos and Julio are off bitching out the RUSH brass for what went down on AD10 and what’s planned for AD11. Gates is filming a TV commercial. I’m slaughtering poultry in a hotel bathroom. And it looks like this was just a figure of speech. And that doesn’t bode well for the Spiffy!. What last week effectively did is remove me from my head. From my feud with Quo. Ever since I got here, I’ve been his bane, his beating toy. Someone to piss him off, and someone for him to beat. I did it with Shack. I did it better than Shack, for that matter. And now what? Now that Irish guy’s getting a shot at the Quo title. Now I’m a chicken with no head. No direction. But I won’t die. That wouldn’t be kosher. Let’s just say I’ll be running around a bit for the time being. If you’ll humor me for a second, I’ll share with you a dramatic interpretation of my initial reaction to being booked in a Hell In A Cell Match against William “Biohazard” Perry. Ahem… “Who?” *bows* Thank you. I suppose there would be little more to say after that. Maybe one of the other guys know about him. All I know is that he used to play in the NFL as “The Fridge.” And he got knocked out by Manute Bol in that Fox Celebrity Boxing special. And Jesus, if Manute Bol can knock the guy out, I’m pretty set. I wonder why he changed his nickname to Biohazard. Meanwhile, everything’s out of whack. Jeremy is gone. Shack is gone. My title shot is gone. There’s no place to go. But you know what that means? That means I’m in charge. I snuck the keys to the asylum off one of the guards. You know, that joke isn’t quite as funny when there’s a fed named the Asylum with a champion named the Inmate. Goddamned Campbell. The agenda seems pretty empty these days. I’ve got to start filling it. Places to go. Stuff to do. Black people to piss off. So plan your schedules accordingly, folks. There’s a Little Mexican Bastard around with RUSH’s name on it. I’ve got a lot of things I can do…and plenty of time to do it. Regrets? Unpossible. But in the meantime, I’ll be here, lopping the heads off these chickens. I learned my lesson after two…but I’ve still got three more. No use letting them go to waste, right?
