Date: RP#1 for AD (Our scene fades in to show the outside of “Wally’s Stop and Shop” grocery store located in the heart of suburban Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Wally’s is just one store in the larger shopping center that it is located in. The shopping center also includes a Blockbuster Video store, a K-Mart and many other small stores that would take to long to name off. It is a bright sunny day out in Milwaukee, the sun nags high in the sky signifying that it is somewhere around noon. Despite the sun being especially bright and warm today, a chilly breeze floats through the air.. We pan around the large parking lot that is in front of Wally’s to see that it is mostly empty, except for a few sparse cars parked here and there. All of a sudden, we hear the squeal of tires and the roar of a muscle car engine that sounds like it’s getting closer and closer to us. All of a sudden, we see the source of this noise and it is none other than Rush superstar “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd’s 1969 jet black Boss 429 Mustang. We zoom in closer to the vintage car as it careens at a dangerously high speed across the snow-covered parking lot. As we zoom in closer to almost eye level with the car, we see Brock Newbludd, sitting in the driver’s seat casually with one hand on the wheel and the other hand holding a can of beer, a cigarette hangs from his top lip as well. We notice his wife Lindsey Newbludd sitting in the passenger’s seat, with a frightened look on her face. We can see her yelling at Brock and hitting him in the shoulder. We also see little baby Brock sitting in the backseat of the car in his baby chair, laughing at the fun little car ride. All of a sudden, we pan back out and watch as Brock slams on the brakes and cranks the wheel of the car, doing a powerslide across the parking lot. Snow and slush flies high in the air as the car slides across the parking lot, and then stops suddenly, perfectly parked in one of the many unoccupied parking spaces. We zoom in on the muscle car and watch as the drivers side door swings open and Brock steps out with a devilish smile on his face and a beer in his hand. Brock is clad in a pair of baggy fitting khaki pants with brown Doc Marten boots, a “Metallica: Metal Up Your Ass!” t-shirt can be seen underneath his favorite black leather Harley jacket. His hair is hidden beneath the brand new Blood Brothers black stocking cap, with “BB” etched in the front of it red. Brock shuts the door behind him and looks at his work…) Brock: I’m f*ckin’ good at that! (Right after Brock says that, the passengers side door flings open and Lindsey walks out, carrying little Brock in her arms. We notice that little Brocky’s stocking cap matches his dad’s. (Lindsey proceeds to hit Brock in the ribs with her free hand , quite hard. Brock lets out a “Oof!” and doubles over, but still standing…) Brock: What…*gasp*…was…*wheeze*…that for? Lindsey: If you ever drive like that again with our baby in the car, I will cut you off from sex for a month! (Brock straightens up with a surprised look on his face and his hand holding his ribs…) Brock: But, I was careful and little Brocky, liked it didn’t ya big guy? (Brock grabs little Brocky’s foot and sticks out his tongue at him, the little baby boy laughs…) Brock: See, he liked it! (Lindsey just rolls her eyes at her husband…) Lindsey: Listen honey, little Brocky and me am going to go grocery shopping. Um, why don’t you run over to Blockbuster and get some movies for us to watch tonight. Ok? (Brock shrugs his shoulders and nods his head agreeing fashion…) Brock: That’s cool with me… (Brock gives Lindsey a kiss and pinches little Brock gently on his fat baby nose…) Brock: See ya later little man! Little Brock: Goo! Bababajoh! Brock: Exactly! (Brock and his family part ways, Lindsey heading towards Wally’s, while Brock heads towards Blockbuster, we slowly fade to black as Brock enters Blockbuster…) “Hmm…I’m sure they like this one, Texas Chainsaw Massacre rules and besides it’s a classic!” (We fade in to show Brock browsing through of of the many rows of videos inside Blockbuster, this one being the horror and thriller section. We see that Brock has about four or five movies under one arm, and about ten Playstation 2 games under his other arm. Brock picks up another movie and looks at…) Brock: “Attack of the Six-Eyed Afro Whore?” Hmm…sounds sweet, I’ll get it! (Brock stuffs the movie under his arm and continues looking through the section when he finally stops and scratches his head…) Brock: Oh sh*t, that’s right I gotta get something for Brocky. Hmm…what wold he like? (Brock looks over to the Disney videos section and shakes his head no…) Brock: No, too gay… (He then looks at the ultra kiddy Teletubbies/ Barney section and shakes his head no again…) Brock: No, that sh*t could make Ron Jeremy gay. (Brock then turns his head over to the wrestling section of Blockbuster and shakes his head yes…) Brock: There we go… (Brock walks over to the wrestling section, nearly dropping all his videos and games along the way. He stops and looks over the section, before his eyes stop on one video and get a surprised look on them…) Brock: Hey! I didn’t know…COOL! (Brock picks up the video, “The Very Best of the MWF” and it has a picture of him, Frisco, Doug Troy and Judas Steele on the box. Brock picks up the video and sticks it under his arm…) Brock: He’ll love this! (Brock looks at all the movies and games under his arms and smiles…) Brock: This should be enough. (Brock begins to walk towards the front desk, when he hears something that catches his attention…) “Also on Adrenaline, Milwaukee native and fan favorite, “The Innovator: Brock Newbludd will be a very busy man, facing off against fellow Milwaukee resident, Tyler Valentine.…” (We see that a little preview of this week’s Adrenaline was on one of the many televisions that hang from the ceiling, or are on the walls.
