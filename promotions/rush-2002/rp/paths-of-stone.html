Date: Today “Hit the drum roll…” A sold-out victim… A human fruit bowl… An emotionless textbook sociopath… An underachieving luchadore… A clone… A hip-hopping party animal… A born again electric current… A reformed junkie… A so-called innovator… An angry man on a vendetta… A child of war on a road to stardom… A German who saved New York… A Thug… A lost identity… A moniker of magnificence… Who are these people? What degree of significance do these individuals have? Why exactly do they actually deserve the time to be mentioned and actually listed out not in any particular order? Well, it’s simple really… Out of the whole lot Only one of them is going to walk out a sure… Definite… Winner Yes, these are the heart, soul and backbone of the sports entertainment organization most of the world know as RUSH. As strange as they may be, as eccentric, bizarrely unique and just plain weird as they may seem… What counts about them is: They bring in the dough The Greenbacks The Dead Presidents The cold, hard, raw… -=$CASH$=- Even the loud-mouthed, trash-talking, rotten-to-the-core, unethical, complete A**hole-cum-Dickhead who happens to be a poor excuse for a primary shareholder in this reputable organization KNOWS that bringing in the money is the highest of all priorities. Tickets are selling, televised events are booked full… A job well done RUSH has done well for itself In fact, they should be handing out the cigars Popping champagnes Throwing parties, balls and whatnot Announcing holidays and public festivities to commemorate these monumental achievements. But deep inside the mind of each and every member of the RUSH talent roster Even as a significant cut from the earnings rolls into their pockets Even as they drive the flashy cars Even as they reside in their mansions, penthouses and residencies that are marks of wealth Even as they eat Even as they sleep Even as they wake up each morning All the cash nor material beauties in the world can erase the one thing that still makes the individuals in the RUSH talent roster strive with passion and fury, even when they have successfully filled the roles and achieved the foundation goals that the organization has set… It drives them further… It fuels the strife… Intensifying the atmosphere… The one thing that makes them tick… Ambition Imagine a world without ambition Sure, that’s one less factor to consider in life Sure, there would be a lot more less brutalizing and casualties in the name of self-advancement Sure, we wouldn’t have to deal with overly-nasty folks like Scott Cage in our everyday lives. Sure, at least everybody would have a smile on their face without having to pay any dues, make any sacrifices and kiss too much ass. Hell, we would be halfway to world peace and contended with everything we have to boot. Boring isn’t it? It would be a Dropkick Murphy world, a world in a permanent drunken stupor, no Betty Ford center or a glass of ice-cold water to wake up to. It’s not a world without alcoholics; it’s a world OF alcoholics. If anything good ever came out of being sober most of the time in this day and age, It would be the sure sense of direction A World without Ambition? The Grass Is Always Greener That is why there will always be ambition For want of a better circumstance A better life A better you. To Be Number One? Like they say, all roads lead to Rome It has come full circle
