The Last Slatkovic (part 4) { PPV}

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/6/02 “Steffan…” ”Yes Remus?” ”I need to talk to you for a second” His hand, Ricky’s hand, held something that told Steffan more than enough, more than he ever wanted to know for the moment. It was a yellow file, yellow-ochre to be more specific but a file nonetheless. At least like the many before him in the same situation, he actually had the decency not to slam the file onto the table and make with the verbal hammering Instead, it went something like… “You don’t have to hide anything from me Steffan. I’m your brother by blood” Steffan just sat there, his face blank as always. Without even looking at Ricky, he mouthed off “There’s nothing else that needs to be said. I suppose you already had a thorough look through the whole damn thing” Ricky placed the file gently beside him on a lamp stand as he takes a seat right next to his younger brother. He spoke to him in a soft, calm and reassuring voice. “It’s not too late you know” Steffan lets out a laugh, not one of humor, it was a cold laugh and it make Ricky shudder “It’s not a question of being too late or on time. You won’t understand…” Ricky cuts straight in “Understand what?” Steffan shook is head, in a gesture of no confidence in his elder brother “It wasn’t about the fast money, or the fast life. It isn’t because I’m cold-hearted enough to like it” He looks at his palms as if there was something there only he could see as Ricky looked on “I did it because I just wanted…” His words collapsed into nothingness, as if the effort took everything out of him. He tried again “I wanted…” And again “I wanted…” This time he just blurted out “I wanted to get by, just to get through the day…or night” He still looked at his hands, first his palms, and then turning them over to study the back of his hands like some sort of rare specimen “These hands…they shall haunt me ‘till the day I die” Steffan was engrossed in them, until Ricky held him on the shoulder “There’s still one other chance…” His focus already shifted, Steffan looked at him “And what would that be?” Ricky takes something out of his pocket and places it in Steffan’s palm. Steffan looked at it and looked back at Ricky as if understanding all he needed to understand in just one glance It was the pendant of a star Ricky gripped both his shoulders in a brotherly manner “Steffan, this offer I give to you is good now and will always be in my lifetime” He leaps to his feet “Look at all I have, the life I lead, yes, the good life, the life I’ve always wanted since my days, OUR days in the concrete hovels. You don’t have to resort to whatever you did just to get by if you choose this fresh new path. You don’t even need to remember” Ricky drew closer to his brother, speaking in a gentle voice meant to soothe “This life has no misery, no worries and most importantly, no bloodshed. That I promise you” Steffan clasped the pendant tightly and looked back up at his brother, who seemed serious about this “This chance you’re giving me…I’ll have to think about it” Upon finishing the final sentences, Ricky seemed surprised, if not shocked at those words “Think about it? This is the life with all the riches you never had!” He takes a step back and spreads his arms wide open, as if to highlight every single detail in that living room “You see all of these? This is why we left the Eastern Bloc for. Compared to where we came from, this is Utopia, this is not just a Belle Reve, it is REALITY!” He drew back closer to his brother again, gripping him on the shoulders to emphasize on his convictions “This is the American dream, my American dream…” Ricky unfurls Steffan’s hand to reveal the star pendant sitting in his palm “…OUR American dream” Steffan looked up and smiled at his elder sibling. He turned around and walked off, heading towards the winding stairs to the second floor. His departure signifies his standing on the decision, undecided. Ricky looks at him as he walks up the stairs slowly but before he disappears at the top, he shouts out to him. “One question Steffan: Was your life before our reunion much different than it was back at the concrete hovels? In fact, was it any different?” Steffan halted in his steps, as if affected by that question. In a matter of moments, he resumed his slow climb up the steps, disappearing to the second floor of the Starr residence. Underneath his breath, thinking aloud, Ricky Starr muttered “Bloodshed will always be bloodshed, no matter where, how and why”
