Damn public transport (Part 2)

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25-06-02 (Voice of Livewire) Hhmmmm…the plot thickens. It seems that no sooner than when I get rid of one idiotic ghetto goon that another seems to walk into my life. It is almost like I attract trouble to myself, that I have tendencies to infuriate the lesser man because of my boyish good looks and superior intelligence. Firstly Dark Haze believed that he could make an impact by taking me out in his first ever RUSH appearance. The kid has balls I give him that, but he really is more stupid than he looks. Haze underestimated me. He thought I would just simply lie down and let him take his place on the RUSH roster with no fight. Ha, Ha…how wrong could he be….I took him out the week later and made him my personal bitch. Now after proving I was the better man at Ignition by defeating him and the now presumably deceased Allan Novakayne, a new ghetto goon comes on the block. Ali Khadafi, a man who says fuck as much as I get laid. Khadafi is the new nemesis that I have to deal with and once again Playaz Inc and Shack Pack will lock horns with members of each stable looking to take the other right out of the equation. Khadafi will suffer the same fate as Haze as I will systematically take him out on my way to becoming the new Network champion. Oh yes of course, the Network title. This coming Thursday I will once again go one on one in a match with that pschyo Scott Cage. I know folks…it is, very belittling having to wrestle that whack job in the ring but when the Network title is the prize at the end of it, it all becomes so worthwhile. Livewire, Network champion…how cool is that? The Network title has been a part of me ever sine I first stepped into the marble halls of RUSH wrestling. My first ever competitive Rush match was a eight man clusterf*ck for the Network title, my second match was a number one contenders match for the Network title, at Ignition I was wrestling in a triple threat number one contenders and now I finally get my shot I can finally stake my claim on the RUSH hall of fame by becoming the federations third Network champion. I’m in the best physical shape of my life and the gods are looking down on me, I can almost hear god himself say “Hey Wire, you have GOD given talent…so use it and beat that son of a bitch Scott Cage and become the Network champion”. Ok, maybe in not those exact words but close enough. There are other issues that will be decided this week on Adrenaline such as my Shack Pack stable mates Christopher Shackleton and El Spiffy taking on Dante Jackson and Ali Khadafi. Another chapter looks like it will be written in the Playaz Inc, Shack Pack feud on Thursday. I will be there watching out for my stable buddies, but as if two small cruiser weights under 235 lbs need the help of a 6’8 310 lb powerhouse like me against two sadistic gang warfare loving …..actually on second thoughts I will definitely be watching their back on Thursday. The scene opens with the rickety high speed train slowly grinding to a halt. The sound of the loud brakes creaking awakens Livewire from his slumber and he manages to sit up and clear his head. He glances at his watch and notices that it is indeed three hours since he left the outskirts of Edmonton. He then stands up to stretch his numb limbs and look out the window. There is a red a white sign in the colours of Canada saying “Central Edmonton”. Livewire smiles knowing that he has finally arrived at his destination. He nonchalantly puts his hands on his hips and once again surveys all the other passengers on the train. The businessman from earlier now looks rather stressed and is punching the keys of his laptop furiously while shouting the words “STOCKS”, “SHARES” and “THAT DAMN BILL GATES” randomly with a lot of venom. He then waves his fist in the air and repeatedly presses the return button on his laptop, most likely trying to send a last minute e-mail. Livewire picks up his luggage from underneath his business class seat and stares across at the two punks who we talking rather loudly when he first entered the train. Maybe his eyes are deceiving him but it seems one of them is actually a female. I think it was the fact that she has 12 ear rings opposed two the 4 of the male whom we presume is her boyfriend. “I wonder if they prescribe glasses for punks because that guy definitely needs one to realise how ugly his girlfriend is” says Livewire in his head as he buttons up his long leather coat. “She has more chins than a Japanese phone directory and her purple tights and orange skirt don’t really show off her best features, whatever they may be” he adds. The two then indulge in a scene of affection that can only be bettered in the realms of disgusting by the affection Kris Raves shows for Cassius Deamont every week on Adrenaline. Firstly the female punk or “IT” as Livewire would prefer to refer to her as puts her hands in the leather jeans of her boyfriend. The two then interlock with a kiss, their tongue rings clanging as they kiss more. Two things come to the complex mind of the Lively one as he sees these two people who are blissfully in love. Firstly “How many disgusting bodily fluids are they passing as they kiss? and secondly “Why the hell do I have nothing better to do than watch two sick freaks publicly display their love for one another!?!” Livewire picks up his luggage and steps out of the train. The Edmonton air is crisp and very cold. Livewire puffs out his cheeks and sighs as he walk to make his way to Edmonton High. “I was told by Ted that it was only a five minute straight walk from the train station to the school. Knowing Ted, a five minute straight walk is probably a half an hour bus ride” Livewire laughs to himself before asking a local passing by for directions to Edmonton high. Livewire notices a diminutive grey haired old woman standing by a nearby bus stop. He approaches her and gently taps her on the shoulder. “Hey ma’am, I’m a famous professional wrestler and I’ve been chosen to do a speech on achievement to the pupils of Edmonton High school. Could you tell me how to get there please?” Livewire asks doing his best coy young boy voice. “Sure sonny” replies the short old women “Say you sure do have a funny accent, you’re not from around here are you, yah? she asks. “No, I’m from the great country of England” replies Livewire with a proud smile. The old woman puts her finger to her jaw and thinks “England huh, isn’t that the country that started the second world war, yah?” she asks. Livewire shakes his head and gives the women a menacing scowl “No! No! that was Germany, England won the first and second world war!!!” The old women nods her head and smiles showing a toothless grin “ENGLAND!HA! my daddy used to say that all you English are gay because you speak with posh accents, yah.” Livewire sighs and brushes back his long black hair with his hand before lowering himself to get closer to her “Really?” Livewire giggles sarcastically “Well I think all you Canadians are stupid because you are all incapable of finish a sentence without saying the word, yah”. The old woman frowns and gives Livewire a stern look “Now, you’re just being silly” she moans before walking away. Livewire throws his luggage to the ground and starts jumping up and down on the spot angrily. “No, wait come back…..I need to know how to get to the High school…I’m going to be late!!” The old women ignores Livewire and continues walking and crosses the road. Livewire rolls back his eyes and turns to the camera “I hate Canada!” he says before picking up his luggage and continuing to walk down the street mumbling profanities to himself as he does so. Will Livewire get to Edmonton High school on time to deliver his speech? Or will he run into more unintelligent Canadians? Livewire has a date with destiny and the Network title this Thursday on Adrenaline. Be sure to watch him and the other members of RUSH wrestling’s leading stable Shack Pack on Thursday night Adrenaline from Madison Square Gardens, New York city. Take care, Livewire-ites.
