The Most Important Choice I'll Ever Make

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 30, 2002 “Man…I can’t believe it’s finally gone under.” “Wow Scott, congratulations!” “Heh, I’ll miss you guys. Keep in touch, boys.” Such was the commotion backstage that fateful evening of Tuesday, June 18th, 2002, after the ex-NTICW owner, Chris Sloboda, announced that he had sold the contracts of each and every member of the roster to various federations in the wrestling conglomerate that is the Primetime Central Network. Chatter like this spouted out of the mouths of every single superstar, who were all curious as to what the future held in store for them. “PEANUT BUTTER JELLAY! PEANUT BUTTER JELLAY! PEANUT BUTTER JELLAY! PEANUT BUTTER JELLAY! Dothepeanutbutterjelly, peanutbutterjelly, peanutbutterjellywithabaseballbat!” echoes a voice from inside the shower of a locker room. Um, well, maybe there was one exception. You see, Tyler Valentine isn’t your ordinary wrestler. Hell, since we’ve got a new audience here we may as well take some time to get acquainted with him. Valentine knocks off the obnoxious singing and emerges from the shower with a purple terry cloth towel wrapped around his waist, soaking up the water that he neglected to dry off of himself. His shoulder-length brown hair, streaked with dark violet, hangs down in dripping strands obscuring his face. Physically, he’s like you’d expect a wrestler of his height and weight to be – lean, but not too bulky. His skin is a fair shade of tan, not too pale but not burnt to a crisp like a certain Mr. Terry Bolea. Tyler whips his hair back, showing his facial features clearly without the veil of his wet hair. He’s pretty handsome. Hey, don’t blame me for saying that, that’s just how it is. The most noticeable thing about his face is his shining, cerulean eyes, complemented with a set of pearlescent white teeth. So, he’s a good-looking guy, in good shape, has a great look for a wrestler – what’s so abnormal about him? Well, simply put, he thinks he’s a peanut butter and jelly sandwich. Yes, you heard me. A peanut butter and jelly sandwich. So how does a normal guy with a perfect look for success and an optimistic attitude come to think that he’s a child’s lunchtime treat, of all things? It all started one day in Detroit… …Nah. I’ll save you the details of a cliché flashback and just tell you what happened. Ya see, Tyler was going through a small bout of depression at the time, and sought hypnosis as an easy way out to soothe his down-in-the-dumps mood. Now, if he had visited a first-class psychiatrist and gotten proper treatment, it would’ve been all fine and dandy – but such is not the case for a struggling wrestler trying to scrounge up enough money to put himself through the Primetime Wrestling Academy. So, he traveled to the inner city of Detroit, seeking a hypnotist. He found two of them – a pair of midgets named Tweedledee and Tweedledum inside a filthy, one-room shack – hey, what else can you expect for ten bucks? Little did he know that these two were merely con artists, looking to make a quick buck rather than provide any real mental help for a patient. Thus, they decided to have a little fun with him. They coaxed him into believing he was a living, talking, breathing peanut butter and jelly sandwich. And the rest, as they say, is history. That leads us to today. Thankfully, his change in mind didn’t affect his wrestling skills, and he was able to leave the PWA and become the first-ever NTICW Cruiserweight Champion. But, as you know (or else you wouldn’t be here watching this Impulse), NTICW folded, sending Tyler, along with seventeen other wrestlers, to Jeremy Howard’s RUSH. And Tyler’s not concerned one bit. Hell, as long as he’s got a steady supply of jelly to keep his vital signs up, he’s as happy as can be! So here he is, lounging around in his locker room (which, coincidentally, is painted a festive violet and brown.), away from the rest of the ex-NTICW roster who are busy chattering away after the bombshell announcement. After adjusting his towel, he walks back toward the shower… …But slips on an ominous puddle of soapy water. “AW SNAPS!” he shouts, as he falls backward and hits his head, sending him into unconsciousness on the linoleum floor… So yeah, here we are inside the wildly imaginative mind of Tyler Valentine, a hazy shroud of snow white obscuring whatever image we can see. Apparently, we’re on a cloud, white light shining in Tyler’s eyes, who has awoken in his dream. No, don’t worry. He’s not dead. “Uh…am I in Heaven?” asks a befuddled Valentine to nobody in particular. “Well, of course I’m not – THERE IS NO SMUCKER’S JELLY HERE! GAHAHAHA! Okay…somebody respond. I’m scared.” “Tyler,” chimes in a sweet, feminine voice. “Don’t be afraid.” Suddenly, the source of the voice is revealed – a beautiful woman, standing tall in a white lace dress with brunette, curled hair that drapes down over her shoulders. She stares at Valentine with welcoming hazel eyes, a small smile on her rosy face. “I have something to tell you, Tyler,” she continues. “You see…there’s more to you than you may think. I can’t go into details, but it goes without saying that it is extremely vital to you, and you may be happier if you knew what it is.” Tyler stares at her with a confused expression. “Well…” he tries to ask. “Shhh,” whispers the lady, putting a finger up to her pink lips. If this were Heaven, she could easily be mistaken for an angel. “Don’t speak…just listen to what I have to offer. Tyler…I want to offer you the chance to find out who you really are. There is something plaguing your mind that is preventing you from living the life you should be – and I have the ability to take that away and give you complete freedom.” Valentine listens, intrigued as the woman paces back and forth on the marshmallow surface of the cloud. “You don’t have to accept…” she continues with a sadder tone. “You can go back down there to the locker room, I can wake you up, and you will never know that this happened and you’ll continue to live the way you are…” She stops on a section of the cloud, looking up into the blue sky, away from Tyler. “Or…I can lift the ‘curse’ from you, and allow you to find out who you really are, and you’ll finally know the truth…” Valentine looks down, pondering his choice. “So, Tyler. Which is it?” Valentine peers up at the beautiful woman, his gaze locked on hers as he thinks long and hard about a decision that could change his future forever as we fade to white… TO BE CONTINUED. -fin-
