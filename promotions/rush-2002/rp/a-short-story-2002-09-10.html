A Short Story

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10/02 Watching family members being beaten up before your very own eyes at a young age. That is something that always changed my life. Watching my older brother get beaten up with I was the age of three. He always just took it, he never fought back. When I grew up I was going to be different; I was much different from my brother. I had a lot more size then he did, I could always handle my own. I always avoided big crowds but when they approached me, I never backed down. I am not saying I always won the fights that I was in, but I never backed down. Whatever didn’t kill me only made me stronger. When I beat up guys I could go weeks without seeing them, when someone beat me up I went back to the exact same spot, if they tried beating me up again once again I fought back. Sooner or Later they were bound to respect me, I never entered the gangs in my area. The gangs always wanted me, I mean I was the only guy I knew that could put up a fight against five guys, and come out still strong. I never had many friends, whatever friends did get usually ended up turning their back on me; I just let it roll of my back. I absorbed all the shit I went through, if they ever did anything to me, I would get my revenge, because paybacks a bitch. Anyone who fucked with me realized that pretty quick. I always got revenge, most of the time it would not even involve me beating the living crap out of them. I was always smarter then that, none of the people ever knew it was me that got them back. So if anyone in Rush messes with me, they will get what’s coming to them, they will not even know its me, and hey in RUSH, I don’t have to worry about the people calling their moms on me.
