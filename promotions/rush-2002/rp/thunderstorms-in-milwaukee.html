Thunderstorms in Milwaukee.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Thunderstorms tear through Madison, Wisconsin, knocking out power and knocking down trees everywhere. It's a tumultuous evening outside, so everyone is staying indoors. At the Florist Garden Apartments, home of The Blood Brothers's Fully Functional Babe Lair, this is no exception. The Innovator Brock Newbludd howls with laughter as he watches the television. He is clad in baggy blue jeans, his red Blood Brothers tank top and a pair of black boots. 'Dude, Vinnie Jones is the funniest man alive.' Sitting on the couch, his partner and best friend former wrestler The New F~cking Legend Stephen Blood shakes his head and takes a swallow of Badger Red Ale. 'Jason Statham. I swear, if they ever make 'Blood Brothers: The Movie', he's beefing up and playing me if I don't get the part.' Stephen is wearing a pair of khaki cargo shorts and his Got Blood? t-shirt with the sleeves cut off. He stares at the television screen and nods. 'I love this f~cking movie.' Brock puts his arm around Lindsey, who is dressed in a pair of tight tiny black shorts and a red tube top with her curly red hair tied back. 'Is that what they'd call it? 'Blood Brothers: The Movie'? Are we really going to sound like the Spaceballs franchise?' Lindsey takes a sip of wine cooler. 'It WOULD be cool to get it directed by Mel Brooks though.' Brock lights a Marlboro. 'Yeah, although I'm not so sure about the name. I'd call it. . . 'Bloodbath'. That sounds pretty good.' Stephen merely bows his head and shakes it from side to side. 'I just want to watch this f~cking movie,' he mutters. His girlfriend Stacey sits beside him and soothingly rubs the back of his neck. She is dressed in a Blood Brothers baby tee and just a pair of black bikini bottoms. Lindsey plays absently with Brock's spiky brown hair. 'But then it might imply that the movie is going to be about Gangrel.' 'Good point,' Brock says in agreement. 'We don't want the theatrical fans thinking he's going to pop through the screen and eat all of their popcorn and Junior Mints. 'Cause hey, you can tell that dude's an eater. So let's think of something else. Like um, 'The Greatest Tag Team In Wrestling History'. That just rolls right off the tongue. I like it.' 'There's already a movie with that theme,' Stephen points out, still not looking up. 'There is?' Brock says, taking another drag from his cigarette. Stephen nods. 'It's called “PCW Tag Division 2000-2001”and it debuted on pay per view last week. I thought you were there.' Brock smiles and nods slowly. 'Oh yeah. Right. So I guess we've gotta talk sequel here.' Stephen pounds more beer and tosses one to Brock. 'Here, drink this. Fast. Get these movie delusions out of your f~cking head. Between 'Blood Brothers: The Major Motion Picture' and space aliens pulling us over because I'm drinking Shazbot Orange Soda, I'm really starting to get concerned, dude. Seriously.' Lindsey crosses her arms over her ample breasts defiantly. 'Well you brought up the fact that there might be a movie.' Stacey rolls her eyes. 'No he didn't. All he said was 'if'. At this point the only movie being made about Stephen and Brock is probably being made by a few college guys with a camcorder and a few beer kegs.' Stephen sighs. 'Well that really IS all you need to tell the story,' he mumbles. Brock reaches around to the side of the couch and grabs his “PCW Tag Team of the Year-2000” plaque. 'Well, that and these, bro.' Stephen hoists plaque as well and clanks it against Brock's. 'F~ck yeah, dude.' They both laugh and bump fists. Suddenly the light on The Promo Channel Camera starts blinking red, indicating a random live broadcast in seconds. 'Hey, looks like we can start shooting right now,' Brock says with a cocked eyebrow. All four get themselves situated in front of the camera. Skoll, Stephen's pet black wolf looks up at the commotion, grunts, then falls back asleep in front of the fireplace. The light turns a solid green and Brock clears his throat. 'Welcome to The Blood Brothers' Fully Functional Babe Lair. I'm the Innovator, Brock Newbludd, future winner of the Crossroads main event.' 'I'm The New F~cking Legend Stephen Blood, the retired ass-kicker who drinks all of Brocks free beer,' Stephen says before taking a huge swallow of beer. Brock opens a beer of his own and takes a drink. 'Last Thursday, I had the match that showed that I am the man who will win at Crossroads, I cleaned Matthews f*cking clock.' Both Blood Brothers, Stacey and Lindsey raise their bottles to the camera and take huge swallows. Brock wipes his mouth on his forearm and continues. 'Now when I first publicly arrived here in Rush, I was still coming off knee surgery so I wasn’t in top shape, and I still won almost all my matches that weren’t f*cking screwjobs. No problem there, as I’ve had almost as much singles glory as in the tag division. But now that I’ve made my mark here in Rush and shown everyone exactly why I’m as good as the experts say, I think its time for me too up the stakes, raise the bar and win that f*cking bloodbath.' Stephen laughs. 'F~ckin' sucks to everyone else, dude.' 'InDEED,' Brock adds. 'I’ve already kicked the crap outta most of them so far in Rush. I'll hand it to the rest of the roster, if they show up, even though the deck was stacked is heavily against them. So now everybody gets a chance to mix it up with just me. No sweat.' 'You know,' Stephen interjects, 'I'm supposed to be your cornerman, dude. But that doesn't seem fair everyone, does it? I mean, they’ll just prolly look at me and sh*t there tights, so I’ll sit back and watch from home. Drink, some beers and get laid… Stephen looks slyly at Stacey… 'Sounds fine to me,' Brock says. 'Just don't drink all of 'em. I have yet to show Rush fans the coolness of the Sky Twister Press and I'm gonna need at least several cold ones waiting for me when we toast my victory.' 'What if you lose?' Stacey asks. 'In that highly unlikely event?' Brock asks her. 'Then we'll toast my defeat. In any event, we're going to drink lots of cold beer afterwards. That's what I'm trying to say here. But, again, me losing is very unlikely event.” Brock lets out a cocky grin as he lights up a smoke. “Well, I’ll be a nice guy and invite everybody back to my dressing room after the match for my annual PPV beer party, because quite frankly they’re going to need some alcohol to kill the pain I’m going to dish out. And that my friends is…” 'SAD BUT TRUE!' the other three say in unison. 'See everybody at Crossroads,' Brock says before cutting the camera feed.
