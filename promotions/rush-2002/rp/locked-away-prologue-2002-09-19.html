Locked Away [Prologue]

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 19th, 2002 A.D. Struggling, my eyes barely opened. My clogged nostrils sucked in the musty, humid and dry air of this room. I was locked away in a dark, desolate room at disclosed location that I’d rather not talk about. I’ve been in here for hours, pounding my fists into bags of sand, smashing my feet into a cold hard wall of desirable pain. This is a desire I have. A desire to hurt just two people. Brock Newbludd. Tyler Valentine. The man who dethroned me as Rush World Champion, and my former best friend. Two men I want to hurt, hurt more than I’ve ever wanted before. More then Cassius Deamont, more than Chris Sloboda... and more than Scott Taylor. I locked myself into this room, this room of horror just so I could test my own physical and mental endurance under times of strain. I am in a living hell. “Fuck...” I muttered to myself as I looked down at the crimson, raging river of blood that was spewing out from an opened wound on my left leg, a strewn cotton bandage wrapped around it. Raging emerald eyes flew down to the cotton, blood stained bandage, my fist tightening as I surveyed my own blood. My fingers ran a streaming line over the bloody wound, my eyes tightening shut as the sensual movement over the torn whole in my thigh. “Motherfucking fuck...” A fist balled up, clenched as far as I could clench it. I spun my body around, only wearing sweaty, black fighting pants – no shirt. The tattered cotton board embedded my footprints, dark markings from each blow from my foot to the wall. I screamed at the top of my lungs and leaped into the air, I could feel tears flying from my eyes as my body twisted and contourted in the air. I am totally free. For that split second I am in the air, twisting and contorting my body I am invincible. Why I feel invincible is beyond me, but maybe its because I’m in my zone.... a zone where Brock Newbludd and Tyler Valentine can’t get to me. I am totally free. Collapsing to the floor in a heap, I spread my legs and arms and laid on my back. Breathing intensified for a brief few seconds, until my mind started to clear of all this rage. Rage that had been building up inside of me for months. Tyler Valentine. Brock Newbludd. I sit up after the kick that slammed itself into the wall; my breathing seemed to have slowed down. I dust some of the grime off my pants, whipping my hands on the dusty floor... I am the dusty grime on this floor. My nails are lined with dirt, for I have no showered or washed myself in sometime. I scrunch up a ball in my hand, burying in my fist into my own dirty, grimed lined fighting pants. Leverage is what I call it; I pull my body up with the use of my pants. ‘Flick.’ I just turned on a light switch in the bathroom. The dirty, uncleaned sink stood before me as I looked down into it. Some hard, moulded toothpaste was stuck to the pipe leading down into the sewers; I rubbed my finger into it a little trying to snap of it off. No luck. Taking the disgusting soap I washed my hands, cleansed my hands of the dirty shit that it’s in that room I’ve been locked away in. ‘Clang.’ What the fuck was that? It came from the kitchen, who the hell is in the kitchen? I want to know who this person is, off turns the fossette. My feet thump along the hard floor, kicking one of my shoes out of the way. I ducked under a towel as this hallway of my home plunged into darkness, the floor creaking with every step I took. “Vanessa?” I asked through the darkness, no response came from my question. I frowned a little in the darkness, walking cautiously as to not crash into anything and hurt my leg any further. Meet Vanessa. My former-significant others sister. After I went and saw her family the other day, first time in months since I’ve seen them... I last saw them at Leah’s funeral. She’s only staying in my house because her ex-husband just left her on her own and moved to New Mexico. The other day she phoned me while I was icing up my knee after a RUSH house show, I was reluctant at first but I knew it’d be doing the right thing by a 22 year old, homeless girl. I am her saving light...Pfft. I’ve known Vanessa for sometime, ever since I was around fifteen or so – she was eleven at the time. She kind of looked up to me as she grew up, though I’m possibly the worst person someone could choose as a role model. Anyway. I walked through the darkness, trying to find my way to the kitchen. She had to be in there, it’s the only place in his shit hole where there’s enough room to put pots and pans in. As I walk toward the kitchen, I can see faint signs of light from a nearly drained globe hanging over the exact middle of the dull kitchen. “Vanessa?” I call out once more, immediately spotting her leaning next to the Stove with a spoon in her hand. “I hate this place,” she says to me, her voice vague with each syllable spoken. “Why they hell did I let you talk me into staying here, I want to forget this place...” “I know,” I snapped back, in my constant bad mood. “But I need to be in top physical condition... I’ve lacked a bit lately in the ring, despite being totally dominant in my last three matches....” “...that you lost.” Vanessa quietly said. Oh how I hated that word. My eyes snapped toward her, and she looked down. Anybody who knows me fairly well... Tyler Valentine. ....know’s that I hate losing. I hate being told that I’ve lost, that I’ve been beaten. “What did you just say?” I said back, fire raging in my green eyes. “N-nothing” she stammered, turning her back to me. She had sparked some sort of feeling through me with her last comments; I had felt like I wanted to slap her for talking me down. I turned my back, not uttering a word back to her quiet figure. I parted a fragment of sheet cloth that dangled over a kicked out down doorway; it’s hinges broken from when the police kicked it in. I picked up a padlock just outside the room, with a dirty white towel in tow. I’m locking myself in this room. Locking myself away. To beat Tyler Valentine. To kill Tyler Valentine. Locked away....
