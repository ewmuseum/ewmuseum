A meeting of sorts

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-08-02 (Voice of Livewire) Sometimes being in the stable of the resident funnymen of Rush isn’t all that it’s cracked up to be. Ok, we get to hear many humorous stories about El Spiffy! and his companion Robogates and those damn Spanish commentators. And we often see the idiotic actions of a Jamaican cruiserweight who has an alarming fetish for fruit. But what happens behind the scenes of the wrestling ring or the RUSH locker-room? What happens when I try to take these two imbeciles out to settle their differences? Read on and you might find out. A Victorian bar on the outskirts of Beverly hills, California is something of an odd sighting. But to Livewire, RUSH wrestler and honorary American it is a regular hang-out where many regulars have seen Livewire spend reckless alcohol fuelled nights with his agent Ted Wanstead or girlfriend Fiona Johnston. The bar manager knows that whenever Livewire is in the bar they are due a very profitable night. So it is in this small contemporary family bar that we find three men who collectively make the stable formerly known as Shack-Pack, gees how long is it going to be until these guys come up with a new stable name? “Well guys what do you think? Nice place huh?” “Where is the fruit, ma mouth is getting dry?” “Que, Liveywire this place boring, El Spiffy! wants entertainment, get El Spiffy! entertainment!!” Not for the first time that evening Livewire felt like throttling both men and ridding the world of their infuriating mannerisms, but he withheld his frustrations and simply took another swig of his beer. El Spiffy! is yet again decked out in his Fubu clothes and doe rag and is knocking back tequilas at an extraordinarily quick rate. The mouth area of El Spiffy’s! mask is covered in alcohol and it seems that it will only be a matter of time before the Spiffster is out for the count. Joe is dressed in a red gold and green string vest and for once isn’t eating a great truckload of fruit. He is instead enjoying a wide variety of drinks, fruit juice drinks of course. “Ok guys, Adrenaline 13, our biggest night of the year respectively. One of us on Adrenaline 13 gets the chance to walk out of the fleet centre World champion” smiles Livewire, the froth of his beer now skimming the top of his lip. El Spiffy! immediately stands up and shouts out “EL SPIFFY! WILL KICK MUCHOS I.R.S. BUTT ON ADRENALINE!!”. The revelers in the bar who had all been quietly enjoying their drinks turn around to look at El Spiffy! who quickly sits down a little embarrassed. “Look at what cha done now ya stupid pickney fool” whispers Joe Bananas as the people in the bar continue to stare in El Spiffy’s! direction. “Shut up, they are looking at your stupid fake hair” retorts El Spiffy!, again insinuating that Joe isn’t black but instead an imposter. Livewire covers his face in anguish and just simply sighs as the two continue to argue causing more people in the bar to turn around and stare. “It is ma real hair ya fool, why don’t I shove ma fist into ya face and show you how real I am?” threatens Joe, now starting to lean forward in his chair. “Que, why not, maybe El Spiffy! can then expose to the public the imposter that you really are!!” replies the diminutive luchadore. “Oh yeah” laughs Joe, now starting to clench his fists. “YEAH!!” booms El Spiffy! now also clenching his fist and rising to his feet. Livewire finally pulls his hands away from his face and separates the two hot-headed wrestlers from doing something that they may regret. “NO!” says Livewire sternly before putting his large hands on the chest of both cruiserweights. “Stop this silly bickering…moans Livewire with a scowl….we are a team. A team that has a purpose, the purpose of stopping all the wicked and evil men that make RUSH an unbearable place to work in. So lets stop trying to fight each other and lets put all our collective energies together to fight the Cassius Deamont’s, D-Kay Calyx’s and Chris Sloboda’s of this world. Now shake hands and put a stop to this foolishness.” Livewire relinquishes his tight grip on the chest of both men and for a moment they just stand looking at each other, neither man prepared to make the first move. “Come on…roars Livewire, in a school teacher like fashion…get on with it”. The two look at Livewire and then at each other before grudgingly shaking hands, a very quick handshake, but a handshake nonetheless. Livewire grins and finishes off his beer as El Spiffy! and Joe Bananas both sit down. “Good, Shack-Pack is back together and once again a team” says Livewire nodding his head approvingly. “Er, Liveywire, we are not Shack-Pack anymore, we are now called Int…”. Before El Spiffy! can finish Joe covers the Spiffster mouth with his hand. “No, not before the pay-per-view” says Joe in an assertive tone wagging his finger in El Spiffy’s! face. El Spiffy! immediately knocks Joe’s hand away from mask covered face and stands up. “Who do you think you are telling that they cant say our new name before the pay-per-view?” asks El Spiffy! angrily. Livewire slaps his head in annoyance at the stupidity of the question that El Spiffy! had just asked. “You, ya stupid Mexican!!” retorts Joe now also standing up with a defensive stance. “Stupid Mexican?..chuckles El Spiffy!…at least I’m not a black imposter!!” The veins in Joe’s neck look like they are about to pop out as the anger builds up inside him. “How many times do I have to tell you ya pickney? I’m not an imposter!!!” screams Joe and with that he dives across the table and spears El Spiffy! knocking drinks and chairs to the floor in the process. “Oh!!! Now you have made El Spiffy mad, prepare to die Joe Grapefruit!!!” booms El Spiffy as the two continue to brawl on the carpet of the bar rolling from side to side. Livewire stands up and wipes the beer from off his leather jacket before shaking his head and walking away to leave the infantile duo to sort out their differences. “I give up!” Livewire sighs to himself as he opens the door to leave. The sound of scuffling can still be heard as he shuts the bar door. So the questions start. Will El Spiffy! become the new world champion on Adrenaline 13? Or will sanity save us from a world title reign propelled by Bill Gates, re-fried beans and more uncontrolled mayhem? Be sure to tune into Adrenaline live from the Fleet centre, Boston, to find out? Take care Livewire-ites.
