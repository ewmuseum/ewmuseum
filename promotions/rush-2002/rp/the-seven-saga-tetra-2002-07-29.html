The Seven Saga: Tetra

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/29/02 The night air was cold, but pleasant. McGlocklan stood in front of a grave marker, his sword already readied in his hand. A shot had been fired in his direction. His eyes narrowed, his bangs flowing quietly off to the side. His teeth were gritting in anticipation. A bead of sweat dripped from his forehead and slowly, quietly, slid off his nose and to the floor. Another shot rang out… in came from in front of him this time. It glittered through the air and whistled like a pot on a stove – hitting the edge of the tombstone and chiseling pieces of rock in every which way. Ducking from behind that stone, he rolled to the right and stormed up the front, shots piercing the ground just ahead of his feet. With eyes narrowed, he suddenly stopped, his blade held out to the side. A howl – the bullet. His eyes narrowed – a wrist motion. A clatter. He swatted it clean out of the air. Perception. He sheathed his sword and continued up field, ducking and dodging between crosses and tombs. A clearing… he had made it. And there was his target. McGlocklan: Tetra… and Prometheus, unfortunately. The sword, emerald colored, with it’s jagged, diamond edged blade pierced the earth just 5 yards in front of him. Behind him, however, was a strange looking man with a smoking pistol gripped in his hairy hand. He resembled a goat, actually. His chin hair was curly and thick. His ears were slightly pointed, his teeth extremely yellow, and his skin quite red. He was wearing a suit, blood red in color with a black dress shirt and red tie. Topping it off were pentagram cufflinks and a red top hat. Quite the fashionable little demon, wasn’t he? Prometheus: Hm… quite unfortunate, indeed. For you, anyway. A weighted clip fell from his pistol. He reached behind his back and loaded another one, pulling back on the chamber and locking it in place. Caleb grinned. He knew he wouldn’t shoot. But, strangely enough, he did. The bastard did. McGlocklan hunched to the ground, holding his cheek. Grazed. He reached up with his fingers and dabbed his cheek clean of the red fluid and rubbed it in between his index and thumb fingers. Prometheus chuckled and rested his finger on the trigger, squeezing slightly, but not enough to send a bullet off. Caleb narrowed his eyes, taking in a handful of earth. Rubbing his hands together, he stood and took in a deep breath of air. McGlocklan: Your chances of survival are slim to none, you know. Prometheus: Futile angel… you cannot thwart Lucifer again… Caleb, a smirk crossing his face, reared back with his foot and shot forth, kicking up a portion of sand towards the demon. Groaning, Prometheus shot blindly, several yards in the wrong direction. Caleb dashed forth and leapt, kicking his left foot out, clashing it together with the demon’s gun-toting hand. The pistol flew away, in the distance, leaving the vile weaponless. Landing on his right foot, Caleb brandished his sword with a motion of his arm, came around quickly in a windmill-type maneuver, and swiped it across the reddish imp’s throat, severing his head. Spinning like a top, it dropped off his shoulders and rolled a few yards before coming to a dead stop. McGlocklan: Pathetic. He stepped over the now limp body and approached the sword, which was now humming rather noisily. Tetra was infamous for ringing when imps were either plotting something or preparing for war. This occasion was no different. Ignoring the gentle hum, Caleb took hold of the hilt of the sword and held it above his head. One sword retrieved, six more remaining. And then… it began. The sword rumbled in Caleb’s hand, almost demanding to be let go. McGlocklan’s eyes narrowed and he took hold of it with his remaining. Behind him, the floor cracked and rumbled. A loud screech filled his ears. McGlocklan quickly turned, his trench waving in the wind. Prometheus, apparently, wasn’t dead. Prometheus: [A WORLD OF PAIN AND AGONY!] His voice bellowed from his headless neck. Suddenly, his suit tore off his body and fat turned into rippling muscles. The imp sprouted a head, fully horned, with sharp razor like teeth, flaring nostrils, and giant, serpent like teeth. Caleb readied Tetra and swung up at the now ten-foot monster. A swat with his giant arm. The sword fell from his hand and he landed several feet away from the battlefield. Prometheus snorted fire and stomped over towards McGlocklan, crushing tombstones under his giant feet. Caleb groaned, holding his head. A single swat created quite a setback. McGlocklan: Gah… That hurt, surprisingly enough. Prometheus: [WORLD OF PAIN! WORLD OF AGONY! WORLD OF PAIN AND AGONY!] McGlocklan: I see your brain hasn’t grown any larger… Caleb rolled off to the side, avoiding being crushed by a heel, and sprung to his feet – he had to get to Tetra. The demon roared behind him, snorting and spitting flame. McGlocklan ducked under a ball of flame and hit the ground chest first, crawling towards the shimmering sword. Ten feet away, he crawled, reached for it… caught by the heel. Prometheus lifted Mick up and brought him above his mouth. Caleb fought, shaking in his hand, trying to get free. Prometheus smirked, open his mouth, and dropped Caleb – straight into his mouth. Swallowed. Prometheus laughed and patted his stomach. Prometheus: [World of pain.] A grin crossed his chopped lips, only to be interrupted by a vicious groan. A metallic blade was now protruding out of his stomach area, and was shimmering under the moon. It pulled up, slicing a thin hole upward across the demon’s belly. Out stepped McGlocklan, quite wet and horrid smelling and covered in bile. He strolled up the lawn, ignoring the death calls of the overgrown imp, and took hold of Tetra – one of the blessed seven. Sheathing his katana, Caleb returned to the demon, Tetra in hand. Lifting it above his head, he looked at the demon, and without remorse, brought it down upon his head, splitting it in twain. No more screams. Prometheus’s eyes crossed and he exploded in a heap of dust. McGlocklan: Filth. Caleb held the blade up in an offering fashion, his eyes turned toward the sky. Dropping to his knee, he muttered an enchantment. A beam of light shined down toward the warrior and engulfed the sword. Caleb lowered his hands, the sword floating. Slowly, it vanished. Offered. It was done. Six swords remained. Six demons left to slay. End.
