Could things get any worse

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-9-02 The empty feeling in Andi’s belly slowly started to subside as he looked out in to the vast space where now only a few particles remained and the small pedestal he sat upon. “What did I do?” the young magician thought to himself. Slowly getting up his face turns a sullen shade of white as he sees a familiar face looking directly at him. “How the hell?” A sly sick smile slowly starts to appear on the strange transparent figure yet colour was still very see able as if it was solid. Quickly Andi shaking of his shock stares in wonderment at the figure before him. “What you looking at dickwod?” The voice from earlier said. Suddenly realisation dawned on the young magician’s face as he realised the voice goading him was himself. “Ah you finally realised have you?” Andi could do nothing but nod as he stood before his evil self. Now everything about the figure was Andi except his eyes where no longer blue but where now a deep red and almost emotionless, his hair was still blue but the points of his spiky hair where now also red. “So Andi!” he said mockingly. “Are you scared?” Still the normally eccentric magician was in a word lost for them. “What’s wrong you pathetic fool, can’t the magnificent one even comprehend that fact that he has an evil side.” He said with a sneer. Andi could do nothing but back slowly away and as he did so he almost feel of only his quick reactions saved him from plummeting to what would be certain death, a death at that moment he would gladly embrace. The look on Andi’s face as he almost fell made the magician’s evil form laugh hysterically. The evil one trying his best to hold in his evil laugh began to speak. “AH the silly goody two shoes cant even comprehend the fact that to everything they must be two sides or they would be no balance and with out balance they would be utter chaos.” Finally the young magician’s senses started to come back to him as the strange ‘figure’ continued to smile evilly at him. “So you are my evil twin?” Andi said exploring. The evil-Andi laughed as he shook his head in bewilderment. “And I know you ain’t that stupid.” “Please explain it to me.” The evil-Andi slowly floated towards him as he spoke once again this time his tone had a very evil edge to it as he sneered at him. “Its simple so listen cause I ain’t explaining it again, When you finally realised that you had magical power and decided to use it for good, the magic was forced to create a ‘evil side’ to you within you in order to keep the balance, because you know even the most good intentions can turn sour.” Andi continued to listen carefully as his evil twin continued. “So in short if fate decided you needed to be evil in order to maintain a better balance then I would be the result.” The young magician just couldn’t help himself as he shouted. “I will never turn evil.” The evil one just laughed as he started to disappear and said. “We will see.” With that he was gone leaving Andi alone with his thoughts.
