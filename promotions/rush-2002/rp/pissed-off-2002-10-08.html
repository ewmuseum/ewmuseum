Pissed Off

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/08/02 “Hey! You!” The idiot turned his head, the bloody, broken beer bottle still held in his hand, and was immediately met with my raging fist. He hit the ground like a rock rather silently, allowing me to sit on his shoulders and shatter his nose into fragments with a blazing left. He groaned as I grabbed him by the collar and sat him upright, only to crack his skull with a vicious head butt, and put him right back down. Blood trickled from pours I never knew existed, and it made me laugh. It made me fucking laugh. “Swine,” I shouted at him, spitting a wad of phlegm into his face. Climbing to my feet, I dusted my jacket and cracked my neck, a blotch of red fluid oozing from a wound in my shoulder. His buddies swarmed around him, protecting him from another vigorous right. A pair of them with billiard sticks in their hands turned toward me and grinned black, ‘tabacee’ juice gleaming between the atrocious gaps in their ‘yellar’ teeth… what remained of them, anyway. “If there’s one state that deserves an Iraqi nuke up it’s ass, it’s this shit hole.” That really got those Georgian bitches rolling. One of the drunken biker pricks swung at me with his stick, only for me to palm it and snap in half with a flick of the wrist. The other roared and was silenced by a fist to the throat. His breath was disgusting me… The group of them stepped back, cautious. “Come on… you call that fighting? Who conceived you shit-mops? The same morons who brewed this piss-water beer?” I motioned with my hand at the bar where a number of American drafts were sitting stale. That had sent three of them over the edge. Each with their faded denims and wife beaters came at me, brandishing their 20-inch belt buckles and three-feet spurs. So cliché. “So which one of you fucks your sister?” I laughed, ducking under a bear-like punch. Driving my knee into the attacker’s crotch, I blasted him across the top of the head with an axe kick as he dropped to his knees in agonizing pain. Another was tripped off his feet with a sliding sidekick, and a third was put head first through a jukebox. Fuckin’ Hank Williams. Can’t stand the shit. “Not those three, apparently. They fight like fags. How ‘bout you, Jimbo? I bet you like to honey dip.” A giant oaf in the back shaked the floor as he stepped toward me… and knocked over tables as he hit the ground, clutching his eye. Adrenaline alone was enough to waste these rednecks. There were about ten remaining, but they were regretting the moment their buddy started a conversation with me. Striking a pose, I lowered my stance like a cat on the prowl, waiting for the next to step forward. “So, candy ass, where’s your daughter? I need some easy game tonight.” Yep, gotta love shots at one’s daughter. He stood there, probably contemplating pulling out a 6 barrel, but shrugged and pulled a knife out of his buckle instead – definitely some weird, weird weaponry down here. He stepped forward, the skimpy knife blowing in the nonexistent wind, and shook it in front of my face, ready to slice. I stood up, leaving my pose. “What the hell is that?” I pointed to the wall behind him. The fucking IDIOT turned his head, allowing me to snatch the knife out of his hand and stab him in the shoulder… tried to anyway. The stupid ass blade bend around his arm. A false weapon meant to scare, apparently. He turned back around and lunged at me, not realizing that the knife was gone from his hand… or that he had tried to been cut. “Adurhur?” I mocked, and slammed my foot into the floorboard. The opposing end came up and slammed him hard between the legs. Even their pubs were of shit quality. He hit the floor, nearly coughing up blood, and I decided to finish up the mess I made. Charging at the remaining five or so drunks, I lept up and shot my foot through one of their skulls… not literally, unfortunately… although the blast DID carry them into closest wall. “Yeeehaw!” I said to another, before knocking a tooth out of his mouth with a rising knee strike. He collapsed to the ground, clutching his bloody mouth, giving me time to deliver a mighty backhand to his gawking friend. That slapped him into consciousness… back into unconsciousness. Minutes later, they were all floored, passed out on alcohol and a severe ass kicking. I turned to the barkeep. “Yo! This asshole’s got my tab.” I kicked one of them in the rib, making him groan. The keep nodded and went on cleaning the crystal mugs. “And if they find their tires slashed and doors keyed, it wasn’t me.” With a wink, I stepped out into the night, my bloodied arm dangling at my side. Wiping the salty dew off my forehead, I walked off into the shadows of the night. God, I hate Georgia.
