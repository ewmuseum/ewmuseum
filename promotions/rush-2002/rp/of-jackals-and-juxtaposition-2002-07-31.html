Of Jackals and Juxtaposition...

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7.31.02 'Only the dead shall see the end of War.'--Homer Tick...Tock...Tick...Tock. We are coming closer and closer to the day that all the fans are waiting for. We are coming closer and closer to the day that all the brass are regretting. We are coming closer and closer to the day that RUSH will be lead by someone with talent, someone with skill, someone respectable. Someone who can actually lead it in the right direction. The clock is ticking away the hours before I will step into the ring to once again prove myself to the world. The clock is ticking away the minutes before I lay an ass-kicking on Rich Cannon and G.O.D. The clock is ticking away the seconds before I pin down Cannon or G.O.D’s shoulders down for yet another win, and for instant recognition all around the world. The clock is ticking away. From the past, straight to the future, with no present in between. However while we look ahead at what is in fact inevitable, we must first look directly in front of us. While we await to drive down a new road, a road never before been traveled, and a world not even known existed, we must first figure out a way to get around the barricades first. We must first find a way to get by all of the potholes, all of the gaps, all of the bridges. We must first find a way to step past all of the childish, moronic bumps standing high in the middle of the road. One little question comes to mind... How do we get by all these roadblocks? Walk right over top of them, one by one. First block in the road? G.O.D. Look at yourself. You may have been something special, something important back in NTICW, but look at yourself now. I hope that this isn't too blunt for you, but in the past few weeks, you have been used over and over again in blunt angles, just like a common whore. It is obscene the way you receive everything from nothing, while every other wrestler strives for each rung they climb, strive for each victory they win, and strive harder for each loss they suffer. It is ridiculous the way everyone has to work from ground up, and work even harder after every loss, how we have to earn each match, and even earn the right to stay a member of RUSH, while you can be silent and lose matches always to be under contract, and not only that, but be in the Title picture. But starting this Thursday, you are going to have to earn everything like the rest of us, because I am going to make sure it happens. Come to think of it, you haven't been in an actual match in the longest time, yet you still seem to always be up near the top of the contenders. You seem to always try to play with your ways and will most likely use it as an excuse or a defense as to why you can't win, and that is nearly as ridiculous as your need to hide behind other things. G.O.D., you are what I like to call...a joke. You are still living off what you think you are, when now all you are is a someone that does what his ego wants him to. Actually, perhaps not a complete joke yet, but you are on your way for sure. Now? You are just plain sad. The second roadblock? Rich Cannon. You are also a sort of has-been in this federation. Isn’t that enough? I hope that I don't offend you by saying this, and if I do...quite honestly, I really don't care, because you are simple-minded. Anyone who would be beaten by the same man, and then come back to fight again, is not smart, not at all. Rich, that is you. It may be something that you feel proud of, it may be something that you believe will make you take a step forward, leading your band of merry-men in the former NTICW to the bottom of the barrel, digging a deeper hole for yourself to try to climb out of, trying to push everyone else’s shoes out of your eyes that are above you on the ladder. Unfortunately Rich, you will never get to the top of the ladder. The spot that you hope to have clung to so tightly, that you thought would always be yours, has been already filled. Filled by me, filled by people who deserve to be where they are. People that you wish you could be like. Rich Cannon, or whatever you wish yourself to be... you are going to be shown to everyone what you really are on Thursday…a loser. I remember the day that I watched the older federations such as Primetime Championship Wrestling. From the seats myself, watching some of the people I thought were possibly the best ever. Giving me second thoughts about signing a contract do to the fact that I quite honestly, can't stand losing. Yes, you heard correctly, I was a little nervous at possibly not being one of the best in the federation, and through the fights in my mind, through the battles inside my head, through the wars that I put myself through I decided to sign a contract. What was the first thing that I did? I defeated, quite easily, one of the men that I had previously been nervous about. One of the men that had almost given me second thoughts of trying out somewhere else, proved to be nothing but hype and hope. What am I trying to say, Rich? I am trying to say that in most of your matches, you hold the upper hand, you hold the advantage of having your opponents worried about facing a veteran wrestler, worried about basically falling like everyone else thinks. Rich, I hold the upper hand over you, for you have never beaten me, whereas when the situation is reversed, I have. Rih, when you look at me, you see someone who you detest for taking your spotlight, who you despise for filling your role, and who you loathe for doing it all better then you ever could. The truth of the matter is, I am that spice that makes RUSH Wrestling extra nice, and the sooner that you realize the truth, the sooner you will realize something else. You missed your window of opportunity, and now it is my time to flourish... I don't know what it is about me that makes you all want to stop me so much, that makes you all so terrified of me, that makes you all wish to bury me for now and forever. What is it about me, a man that seems to frighten even the tallest and the biggest of monsters. What is it about me, that worries everyone so much, that needing two or three men to help put me down is the only choice to make. What is it about me, that you all just can't stand? I understand that I may be a treat, I understand that I may be very good, and I understand how you may be a tad nervous Boda, I understand how you may feel a few butterflies in your stomach, but this? Chris Sloboda, a man who stands nearly double my size, nearly double my experience, nearly double my strength...seems to be trying to find someone to hide behind, double fast. Now listen Boda, cause what I am going to say to you now is very important, and I hope that you understand what I am trying to do. Do I want to be remembered? Of course. Do I want to be a legend? Without a doubt. Do I want to be in every single Hall of Fame that I could possibly handle? Naturally. Yet if none of that is achieved, if I do not grasp any or all of my goals that I plan on attaining, then I will still be pleased with everything else I have accomplished. See, I wrestle for the love of wrestling, for the pride of holding myself above everyone else, for the ability of being able to do something that few people can do. Succeed above everyone else in there field. If I don't win that RUSH Heavyweight Title, which I will, then so be it, there will be another chance. If I don't make it into the Hall of Fame? Too bad, I know that I could beat anyone in that Hall. If I don't become a legend in the world of wrestling, then I will be curious as to what you have to do. So, to the millions of fans out there...everything I do, I do it for you. (Fade in as we see Scott Cage sitting on a black leather couch. As we look at him, we see a certain change in his hair has he has clipped it recently, in the past day. His bleach-white hair looks short enough to have been 'buzzed', yet just long enough to still be able to gel it. It looks good, giving him more of a classy, sophisticated look while also giving him a more scary, fierce look as well. His hard blue eyes stare off into something that we will never see, and it seems to intrigue him as he never closes his eyes, not for a second. His beater, which he always wears, is held tightly against his muscles, holding them inside in a sense, as they look to try to push through as they always do. His wife-beater is tucked into his white shorts that he always wears, and he has them hanging just down below his knees, accentuating his muscular calves as they stand out from it. His eyes swing lightly as he finally tears his gaze away from whatever he was staring at, and focuses on the camera...his eyes seem to search the camera, looking for something, looking for anything...) Sirius Cage: Well Scott, how has your day been? Scott Cage: Well, you know...I woke up, than I went over to the couch, made my way to the chair...and then ordered room service. Sirius Cage: Well, that sounds like an eventful way to spend a day in Jersey. Scott Cage: I do what I can, bro. Sirius Cage: And that is very little, isn't it? Scott Cage: You know it, Sirius. Hey, I’m going to head over to Johnny’s Nightclub. Wanna join me? Sirius Cage: Well, I can’t go. Scott Cage: Why not? Sirius Cage: I don’t smoke marijuana. Scott Cage: Pffft, yeah right. Sirius Cage: Well, it's the truth. (Scott sighs...and turns towards the door...) Scott Cage: Fine Sirius, I understand... Sirius Cage: Don’t worry about me. I'll just watch Baywatch, it's starting in a few minutes... (Scott heads out the door behind him...and Sirius takes off his glasses…) Sirius Cage: Well, Baywatch Hawaii here I come! Cheap effects, lack of clothes... (Sirius slouches into the couch, as he watches the television…) Sirius Cage: Just look at all the silicone...and I haven't even gotten past the introduction for the show...isn't that something? All this silicone just reminds me of how absolutely fake the world is...whether it be fake breasts, or fake personas. How we are so filled with fake personas. They're everywhere...filling the gaps between idiocy and intelligence. Fake as breasts. You can bounce them, squeeze them, throw them around. Hell, if you're lucky, you can press them against the comics...and get those cool little impressions. Was I talking about the boobs, or the egos? (The television changes to a commercial...and Sirius begins to chuckle…) Sirius Cage: But just because RUSH has an overwhelming amount of people clawing for the Network Title...does not mean by any rights that they are talented, skilled, or respected. We here are home to the fake. Seemingly, they have gotten out of their cribs, hiked up their britches...and have dominated the brackets. Why, we could throw a stone, and unfortunately only hit most of them. If only I had a large enough boulder...I could send out the entire first wave of silicone implants. Why...a simple glance at the infestations of NTICW to RUSH can show we have an abnormal amount of pure, raw silicone... (The television comes back to Baywatch…) Sirius Cage: Excellence is but a fine wine...but excellence is not fine whine. If excellence was but a fine whine, then the bottles would possibly be named after Rich Cannon and the newly presented G.O.D. Three men going in on Thursday for the RUSH Network Title. The odds of a certain RUSH Network Champion walking out with his RUSH Network Title? Well...if there were odds, they would be diminishing at an extremely quick pace. There are many possiblities going into Adrenaline...one being my brother walking out with his Network Title in tact. To you...good luck. To all of you...good luck. (Camera fades to black…)
