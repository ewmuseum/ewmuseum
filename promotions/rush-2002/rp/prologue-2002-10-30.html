Date: 10-30-02 -Prologue-~Scenery Description~+Thoughts of one Josh Marshall+ -Fear not what one comes to fear, but fear the ever longing thought of having to fear the fear of many fears. One can only find a true meaning out of those words as they do ring clear to one in one’s head. Surely not, one can not be confused up until this point as the meaning of the beginning is only beginning to take full shape to the other and the hand that reaches out if merely that of the hand that bites the one it feeds. Can one begin to comprehend the beginning from the end as one slowly begins to make one’s way down the narrow path that one must take to find the true meaning of it all? Sense is not something one must have a handle on to fully come to a conclusion on, but only does one need to find out a simple few tidbits about the hand to come to an agreement with it. Potential is something one does not need to truly gain the things one needs to find themselves in a position of envy, nor does this mean that one must work at a tremendous rate to find out the on going process of the apocalypse. Nothing to occur through the changes of the past can make one a better thing in the future, nor can this be taken into consideration as vice versa to another.- ~Slowly we begin to fade into what now seems to be a small, dimly light area, enclosed by a large glass casing and in the center of it all sits a large pool, but not an Olympic sized one, normal in comparison, but relevantly large than normal in-ground pools. The glass casing begins to move ever slowly as the door from the side begins to open, revealing a figure as it makes it’s way into the room and nearing the water. Wearing seemingly nothing, but a pair of tightly fit Speedo-ish swimming tights, the face looks into the water and the long flowing hair of a man that seems to familiar to all of us strikes back through the light of the shining moon in the distance, making its presence known through the stained glass above. The face, the one looking back at us, with no smile at all, belongs to one, Josh Marshall. Nowhere in sight is Michelle to be seen, as Josh slowly begins to dab one toe into the water, checking to make sure everything is at an accurate temperature for him to make his way in. He dives in and suddenly we go jolting into his mind and into the dreams that he does have.~ +True it may be that I have begun to wage a war with a man that is truly more capable of doing things than I had thought, but the games that I’m playing may work out and prove more handy to me than I had thought in the beginning. Letting the beast know, or think for that matter, that he’s going to begin to slowly seep into my heart and steal everything I hold dear, that’s the key to the root of all evil, and maybe just the thing I need to tame the savage beast. Through heavy analysis, I must figure these things out on my own and letting him know this early on that things are not what they seem would truly be the means of the end for myself. Never have I dealt with such a force in my entire career, nor my life. A challenge is at hand and I’m not about to let it go astray because of simple-minded people trying to interfere. A heavy training is at hand and this I must do alone.+
