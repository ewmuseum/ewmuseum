SyNer's Birthday Party!!! (Part 1)

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20th (OOC: Here is the party, part one. If you weren't in this one, you will be in the next ones so watch out. Thanks for signing up and I hope you enjoy reading!) “Shh, someone is coming up the steps to the hall.” The place is a dark hall obviously where people are quiet. “Shh.” “Remember to yell surprise!” A bunch of hushed voices are heard as footsteps are heard coming up the stairs to the hall entrance. The door opens and a figure steps inside as everybody yells. SUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRRPPPPPPPRRRRRRRRIIIIIIIIIISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSEEEEEEEEEE! The lights flicker on and everybody in the hall looks at the figure in the doorway and almost boo. “That’s not SyN!” Hollers a voice from across the room. “That’s...” Comes another voice. “Stand back you evil-dooers. I could take on any of youuuuu!” “But Rent-A-Villian, we don’t wanna fi—” “So you wanna be a wise guy. Fine, then call someone who wants to fight, I’ll wait. **tapping foot**” All of a sudden, from behind Rent-A-Villian comes some more footsteps from outside the door. The lights quickly shut off and everybody ducks down as another figure steps through the door. From the shadow of the figure, you can see the spiked hair of SyN already. From all around the room, voices erupt. SUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRRRPPPPPPPPPRIIIIISSEEEEEEEEEE!!!! HAPPY BIRTHDAY SYN!!!!!!! “What iz zis?” “It’s your surprise birthday party man. I brought some weed, want some?” The voice was from PCW wrestler, Semore Smoke. He was already shirtless and no doubt stoned as well. On his chest was a marijuana leaf. “No Zanks, maybe later, I vant to meet some other guests.” “Well what about some hoes? I got them too!” “Maybe later.” Semore nodded to SyN and SyN nodded back and continued walking into the hall with a smile on his face. He didn’t have to walk far when Rent-A-Villian stopped him again. “Are you SyN, the person who this party is for?” “Uh, yes?” “Then you are not an evil dooer but a good person. It doesn’t matter though because my evil plan will still happen!!!” “What?” “I cannot speak of the evil plan, for it is....uh..... evil!” “Ok, Rent-A-Villian. Thanks for stopping by.” “Don’t thank me!!! HATE ME!!!!” “Sorry, Rent-A...” “Ooh, there is a bad man with weed and hooooooooes. I’m off!” With that, Rent-A-Villian walks off to meet Semore Smoke and his hoes, leaving SyN. SyN walks ahead and sees some of his RUSH friends but as he is walking there, a red blast of ray goes across the room, hitting the window, smashing it. “Hola, it works!” The person who fired the ray was none other than G.O.D of RUSH. He runs across the room to see the window. Follow close behind is Dr. Jenkins, G.O.D’s manager/friend. Beside the now broken window is A.J. Trininad, DWO World Champion. He is already pissed and beside him is any kind of alcohol available. He looks to be selling the alcohol. SyN promptly walks over to Trininad. “Hey, you’re ze DWO champion, eh?” “Yeah, who are you?” “Uh, SyN. This party is for me.” “But your not special, rig—” Another ray gun goes off from G.O.D as him and Dr. Jenkins go running across the room to check out the mark it left. SyN turns around when he runs into an old friend. “Duuuuude, whats up?” The friends of SyN’s is none other than old NTICW roommate, Amy ‘Aimz’ Campbell. “Amy!!” SyN and Amy embrace like old friends do. “Whats up. Your kicking it in RUSH!” “Amy, your drunk, aren’t you?” A grin goes across SyN’s face as Amy smiles as well. “Damnit, you good. Got some 151. Want a bit?” “Actually, sure.” Amy dumps some into a glass and hands it to SyN and SyN drinks. “So, I’ll see you around then?” Sais SyN after taking a sip of his drink. “Yeah, definitely.” Aimz walks off, going to her friends. Syn then focuses his attention to the stage, a good twenty-five feet away. “It ain’t no lye, baby, BYE BYE BYE!!! (BYE BYE!)” SyN nods his heads with a happy shame, smiling. He walks up the stage and Pretty Cool guys nod to him. They end there song and come down to meet SyN. “Any requests SyN?” SyN stops and thinks a bit and then says. “Play anything hip-hop?” “Yeah sure. We got N*SYNC, Backstreet Boys, a cool Britney Spears, O-Town...” “Then nothing for me.” SyN turns to leave when all of a sudden, the microphones that Pretty Cool Guys were using for there songs are snatched up and threw away and the PCG turn. Te person who is doing this is none other then D-Kay Calyx. Or “D-J Calyx”. “I got plenty of hip-hop to jam!” As PCG goto the corner to no doubtly cry, D-J Calyx goes to his turn table and a hip hop jams starts playing with jam mixed in as he smiles at SyN. “Thanks man.” SyN sais as he listens to the music. All of a sudden, from behind SyN comes a huge flame of smoke that burns against SyN’s skin. The flame dies down fast as SyN turns around. Who made the fire and with what? Who else is at SyN’s birthday party? Find out in part 2 of SyNer’s Birthday Party!
