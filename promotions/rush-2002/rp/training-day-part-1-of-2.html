Date: Today (The scene fades in to show the inside of Brock Newbludd’s fully functional babe lair in the Florist Gardens Apartment complex in suburban Milwaukee on a bright sunny summer morning. We can hear the birds chirping outside and it looks to be an all around gleeful day. But, that is not the case in the apartment today. For, we see Brock Newbludd sprawled, sleeping, across his black leather couch, wearing only a pair of Nike athletic shorts and a wife beater. We see that an ice pack is sitting on his head, and a large bandage wrapped around his forehead. The camera pans around to show that the apartment is pretty clean, except for a few sparse beer cans and bottles lying here and there on the floor. We see that the pile of Playstation and PS2 games that are usually strewn in front of the entertainment center are now stacked neatly on one of the shelves of the entertainment center. We also notice that the carpet is missing the “décor” of potato chips, beer stains and other miscellaneous items. In fact, the floor is the cleanest we ever seen it. And to boot the air has that minty “Pine Sol” smell. Stacey and Lindsey cleaned house…inDEED! We pan over to show one of the bedroom doors open up and Stephen Blood and Stacey Blitsch walk out and into the kitchen. Blood is clad in a pair of black denim jeans and a “Assclown=Ass-Kicked” t-shirt. Stacey on the other hand is clad only in the red satin nightgown she wore to bed the night before. Stephen rubs the sleep from his eyes and shuffles his way across the kitchen’s linoleum floor to the large steel refrigerator that sits in the corner of the room. Stephen opens the huge fridge’s door and pulls out two ice cold frosty cans of Heineken and tucks them under his arm. He then walks across the kitchen and opens up a cabinet door and digs around in it before pulling out a bag of Doritos. Stacey grabs the milk out of the fridge and pours herself a glass. She then sits down at the kitchen table, drinking her milk and scanning over the morning newspaper. Stephen strolls into the living room and plops down on the black leather recliner that sits next to the couch in which Brock is sleeping on. Stephen cracks his beer open and takes a swig. He then tosses the other beer onto Brock, it lands directly on Brocks ice pack on his forehead. Brock opens his eyes suddenly and takes the beer off of his forehead. The man who has been hungover so many times before title then sits up and rubs his temples…) Brock: I feel like I was run over by a bus…. Stephen: Dude, you went through hell at Adrenaline, you shouldn’t feel peachy… (Brock cracks open his beer and takes a long swig. He then sets his beer down and grabs his pack of Marlboros and a lighter off of the coffee table that sits next to the couch. Brock takes a cigarette out of the pack and lights one up, taking a long drag…) Brock: I sure did send that damn jobber Nasir a message though…. Stephen: That was a wicked bottle smash, bro. (Brock shrugs his shoulders and takes another drag…) Brock: He fucking gave me a DVD through a table though.\n Stephen: Heh, you landed with a bottle of beer still in your hand, didn’t spill a drop. Brock: Really? Stephen: Don’t you remember? Brock: I was out cold the second we hit the floor… Stephen: Damn… (Brock rubs the sleep from his eyes and adjust the bandage on his head, causing him to wince in pain…) Brock: Owwww…What time is it? Stephen: Um, (Stephen checks his wristwatch…) About 9:30. Why? Brock: Well, we better get going pretty soon then… Stephen: Where? Brock: Training for my match with Nasir of course! Stephen: We are going to the gym later today, dude. Brock: No! I mean some “extreme training”… Stephen: Huh? Brock: Just get your coat, I’ll explain on the way, let’s just say this will be the wildest bar room brawl in wrestling history. Stephen: This better be good… (Brock and Stephen get up and out of there chairs. Stephen walks to the front door and grabs his black leather Harley jacket off of a hook on the wall and puts on his biker boots. Brock gets up and walks into his bedroom. Then a minute or two later he walks out wearing a pair of blue jeans and a plain white t-shirt that shows off his chiseled physique. Brock walks over by the front door and puts on his leather jacket and boots. The scene fades out as Brock and Stephen walk out the front door and shut it behind them…)
