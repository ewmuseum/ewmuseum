The Dying of the Light!!!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: MEET THE FOCKERS! Arrogance. Yes, arrogance is a word that some people use in the wrong pretense. When the word is said to describe someone, that someone is automatically looked at in a different manner. Some people look at the person as a superior, some as an equal, however more then than half of the world thinks of the person the same way...inferior. Why? Simply because this person may know that they are as good as they say they are, or as good as they know they are. It's true, most of the people that claim to be the top of the mountain, who claim to be the pick of the litter and the cream of the crop, are actually bottom feeders just believing the lies that they tell themselves. Yet, because many people fall into the later category, does that mean that there are none who know that they are the best? No, yet everyone else, everyone who watch and categorize, who actually know that they are in fact as good as they say, as good as they project, and as good as they want everyone to know they truly are. Need an example, kids? Statuz Quo. A man who everyone detests because of his arrogance, because of his utter arrogance towards everyone he sees, talks to and even comes within a twenty yard radius of him. Everyone dislikes him for that reason. Another Example? Me. I do the same thing, pushing the arrogance and confidence towards anyone that will hear me and listen to my words, yet the reaction from the people is completely different. A complete opposite in fact. To Quo, the hatred flows coldly, yet for me, the coolness is still intact. What is the difference between us? Simple, whereas Statuz Quo will lie to his own grandmother trying to get her to believe him, I simply say the truth, the honesty of the whole situation. Whereas Statuz Quo will try to keep his nose above everyone else at all times, I simply am one of the people, just a supremely talented person. Whereas Statuz Quo isn't what he claims to be, I am exactly what I claim myself to be. It is simple enough, lies and tells, yet the truth sells. …and Rich Cannon, don't think that I have left you out of this equation, for you are one that will never be forgotten. Why? Because you run around with the same intentions as Quo, trying as hard as you can to change the minds of the viewers who already know the truth. Trying to alter the mind state of those who you continuously try to get to believe you. The fans know that you are simply a fraud, simply an imposter. They are not stupid, they know what they see, they know what they hear and they know that you are desperately trying to push yourself into the top shelf of wrestlers, into the stable where the thoroughbreds are kept. Everyone can see that you are tired of looking through the window and rubbing of your hot breath on the pane. Everyone can see you trying to change yourself, trying to alter yourself to get to that level. It is evident that you are not the same as you were just a few months ago, and can you tell me why? Everyone else can see it, but do you see it? Rich Cannon, do you think that if you keep silent about me, you will be saved from the verbal onslaught that has made me famous in this world? Did you believe that by trying to make it look like you are concentrating on Bob Barker, I would not notice your cowardice shining through after all? Guess again. The sorry thing that I see in you Rich, is a two part effect. Let's face the facts, Rich. You are living a lie, and it starts with your cowardice. You are trying to live your life through stealth, hoping that the people don't look through your facade and into the reality of you, the low-quality of your wrestling, and your profound luck in the ring. Above all of that talent, and skill, you are carrying far too much baggage. Far too many lies. Far too many tales that you have spun. Rich Cannon, a man who is trying desperately, as hard as he can to besomeone, to be a winner. To be what he dreams of himself to be…a Champion, which is a Lie unto itself. You are nothing more then a pretender to the real crown, to the real Champion Scott Cage. It's sad, but unfortunately true. Where as you just follow the trail, follow the path, follow the road that has been walked over and over, and you follow the words that tell you what to do, and how to do it, I am nowhere near that status. I create my own trail, I blaze my own path and I build my own roads. I do whatever I wish to do, and I say whatever I wish to say. I am on top of my world, and I listen to no one Rich, and that is why I am the one prospering in RUSH now, winning fight after fight, match after match and battling for my shot at Statuz Quo and ultimately Cassius Deamont, while you are losing petty matches in NTICW, which isn't even a company anymore. I am my own man Rich, I follow no rules but the ones I set for myself. I am Scott Fucking Cage, and that in essence, is enough to explain everything is it not? Rich Cannon, I only have one more thing to say to you before moving on to another subject, and that is simply this... Doesn't everyone know how good they are? Unfortunately not Rich, not everyone knows the limits that they can strive to, the abilities that they have and how far they can stretch them. See, I know mine and I know that no one else could reach the lengths that I would go to, especially with my situation now, and it pleases me greatly. You may know of your ability, Quo may think he knows his ability, but is that everybody? No, and quite frankly, less people then you would beleive do know there is. Think before you speak on some cases Rich, because with interviews, that is the tricky part, trying to get your point across and stamp it down, while trying not to leave yourself open by sputtering someonthing unintelligent or moronic. Unfortunately you just did that. You may only have been taken to your limits once, taken to your farthest region one single time, but Thursday will be another for you as not only do I drive you to your limits, I will drive you past what you thought your limits were on your way to another unfortuante loss. This has nothing to do with you Rich, as I do not know you at all, but you have stepped onto the path of righteousness, and that is a path I plan on obliterating completely. Yes…to be alone with your thoughts isn't only something that you need for a few minutes of the day, but for some, for the intelligent it takes longer then that. Luckily enough I was given a reason to have my thoughts, and now I have the time to do that. Bruises won't stop me, blood won't stop me, beatings won't stop me, you nor Statuz Quo won't stop me. The truth of the matter is, that only I have enough to stop myself, and I am not ready to be stopped yet, not by a long shot… (Our camera fades into the airport, where Scott and Sirius are sitting in the terminal, awaiting their flight. As the camera focus' in on Scott’s face, you can see the large patches under his eyes that are getting purpler and purpler, aren't completely from shiners at the hands of Marvin Harris at Hell on Wheels. But more from the lack of sleep which will not seem to come to him, which seems to avoid him at all costs. His body looks empty, his arms hang down just off his body, and he leans back in the uncomfortable chair not for the uncomfort, but because he cannot sit up anymore…) Scott Cage: Hey Bro… (Sirius sets down his book, and turns to Scott…) Sirius Cage: Yes, Scott? Scott Cage: Did Statuz Quo ever call back? Sirius Cage: Umm...no Scott, I don't think so. Scott Cage: That pussy...I knew he'd never call back. I think I'm going to have to call again. Sirius Cage: I don't see why you must do this. Scott Cage: Why? Because he didn't return our phone call, that's why! Sirius Cage: It's no big deal, Scott. Scott Cage: No big deal? He ran up a nine thousand tab on our room, and you say it's no big deal? Sirius Cage: We don't even know if it's him Nathanial.. Scott Cage: Well, I know how we can figure this out... Sirius Cage: And how is that, Scott? (Scott pulls out his cell phone...and starts punching away at the numbers…) Phone: HELLO! CALL ME NOW! Scott Cage: Uh...I just did. Phone: You've reached Miss Cleo's Pyschic Hotline! Scott Cage: Hey…I’m Scott. Miss Cleo: I knew you were going to be calling Scott. Scott Cage: You did? Miss Cleo: Of course I did...I'm Miss Cleo...call me now. Scott Cage: Uh....ok. I have a ques... Miss Cleo: You have a question Scott? Ask me now! Scott Cage: I have this phone bill Miss Cleo. Miss Cleo: Well child...you'll have another phone bill after this. Scott Cage: What the hell? Has Quo been messing with my phone? Miss Cleo: Call me now. Scott Cage: He's been calling you? Miss Cleo: Calling me now. Scott Cage: That bastard... Mr. Belvedere: What is it, Scott? Scott Cage: Miss Cleo, hold on for a minute, please? Miss Cleo: Go ahead...put me on hold. (Scott lowers the phone from his ear, and looks at Sirius…) Sirius Cage: So? Scott Cage: She’s telling me Statuz Quo made those calls, and is making some right now. Sirius Cage: Are you sure? Scott Cage: Wait...let me ask... (Scott waves off Sirius and raises the phone...) Scott Cage: Miss Cleo? Miss Cleo: Hello child...call me now! Scott Cage: Yes...now, about Statuz Quo. Miss Cleo: Ah...yes, Statuz Quo…you're friend, no? Scott Cage: No...Miss Cleo: Yes...he's betrayed you, no? Scott Cage: Not really. Miss Cleo: I know...I'm Miss Cleo. Scott Cage: Yeah...anyways, I'm challenging him to... Miss Cleo: Ah yes, I see this now...a challenge, between you, and a bitter rival...one you despise. Scott Cage: No...against Statuz Quo.Miss Cleo: That is what I meant. One you respect... Scott Cage: I really don't respect him that much either... Miss Cleo: One you tolerate... Scott Cage: Wow! You really are psychic, aren't you? Miss Cleo: I'm Miss Cleo....call me now. Scott Cage: You so know me...it's like we've been friends forever. Miss Cleo: I see riches in your future... Scott Cage: Almost like gold? Miss Cleo: Yes...gold, and diamonds... Scott Cage: Hmm...Miss Cleo: Yes...you shall find gold in your path. Scott Cage: Really? So is it from Deamont or Quo? Miss Cleo: No...you, Deamont and Quo will find gold in your journey. Scott Cage: All three of us? You know...I'd really think that only one of us could win... Miss Cleo: Yes...I see a bitter battle over this gold... Scott Cage: Really? Miss Cleo: Oh yes...I see robots, weapons, guns, bombs… Scott Cage: Wow... we're going to get really ugly over this title. Miss Cleo: Title? Yes...I'm Miss Cleo... Sirius Cage: Scott, do you really believe her? (Scott sets down the phone and glares at Sirius…) Scott Cage: Why shouldn't I? She's Miss Cleo! Sirius Cage: And that is another thing, do you even know she is Miss Cleo? Scott Cage: I'm sorta sure... Sirius Cage: What does she sound like Scott? Scott Cage: Well, she sounds…she sounds like Gilbert Godfrey. Sirius Cage: From every infomercial I've seen, she has a Jamaican accent. Scott, I believe you're getting advice from a phony...a phony that isn't even this Miss Cleo. Scott Cage: Seriously? Sirius Cage: Yes Scott. Scott Cage: Well, I'll have to find out myself... (Scott walks away from Sirius, and puts the phone back to his ear...) Scott Cage: Miss Cleo...are you there? Miss Cleo: Miss Cleo is off solving the mysteries of the universe...call her later! (Scott frowns...as he hangs up the phone and walks back to his seat...) Sirius Cage: Well? Scott Cage: She isn't there anymore... Sirius Cage: I'm sorry to hear that. Scott Cage: Yeah...now I don't know what the answer is. Sirius Cage: The world may never know... P.A. Announcer: Attention: Flight 39 to Calgary, Alberta, Canada is ready to board, first class may now approach the gate. Sirius Cage: Yes, well, it is time to board. Scott Cage: Sigh…fuck mysteries. (Scott grabs his luggage and walks ahead of Sirius, boarding the plane...)
