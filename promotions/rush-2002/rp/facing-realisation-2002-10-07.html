Facing realisation

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-07-02 (Voice of Livewire) Ha!Ha!Ha! That is of course the sound of laughter. But why am I laughing? Why would a person who has just seen his opportunity to become the new Network champion be in the slightest bit amused? Well it’s simple really. After losing to Scott Cage on Adrenaline a fortnight ago, a lot of things became a clearer. Firstly of course I would have kicked out of Cage’s implant DDT otherwise known as “The Overdose”. Ricky Starr’s interference aided his nemesis Scott Cage more, than the innocent bystander, me. However the loss has not affected me as much as you would believe. Ok I did drink and smoke heavily for a few days straight as you will find out in soon enough time, but at the end of it all I realised. Realise what? You may ask. Well I realised that being the Network champion so early in my RUSH tenure may not be such a good thing. I mean think about it. All those press conferences, television interviews, weekly title defences. Nah, that’s not for me. I’m a fun loving, relaxed Brit. I should be left to do what I do best and that is being every school girls dream and kicking the ass of those less talented than myself for pure enjoyment, not RUSH title gold. So therefore it is my turn to deal with other pressing matters such as wrestling warped black men like Ali Khadafi in no DQ matches. A scary thought I know. Wrestling somebody as mentally disturbed and angry-ridden as him is a pretty perilous task. But it does have quite a encouraging flip side. You see, what one thing could really infuriate an ingrate like Khadafi? No prizes for answering “The Ku Klux Klan taking over Kentucky Fried Chicken either.” Despite the fact that the thought of seeing those white-hooded retards serving every black person their favourite mid-afternoon meal is quite comical and infuriating for oppressed black folk everywhere I have something far more distressing for our friend Ali in store. You see Khadafi calls himself a self-proclaimed talented brawler. I have personally never seen the skill or talent in brawling, all it does is dignify those who cannot construct three different consecutive wrestling moves. But could you imagine a mat technician like myself beating Ali Khadafi at his own game….a no DQ match! That would single-handedly put the biggest smile on my face since the day I saw my female gym teacher undress and reveal a laced bra and thong!!! Anyway, amidst those superb teenage fantasies and dreams coming true, beating Khadafi is neither a fantasy nor a dream. No, no beating Khadafi on this Friday’s Adrenaline is very much reality. Ok, admittedly it will be a hard task. For Khadafi to beat me in a no DQ match without the interference of the two ghetto goons Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson is near impossible. But I say to Ali Khadafi to bring those ebonics mumbling douche bags to the ring, heck I would even thank him for it, because the chance to take out three menaces to society is far better than taking out just the one. Thursday night in the Nassau Coliseum New York I will re-start my surge up the RUSH ladder and once again gain my shot at the Network title, but this time I will punk out a little ghetto thug in the process. Ha! Ha! Ha! Oh yes this is certainly a time for laughter. It is just a few minutes past midnight on Wednesday10th of June. It has now been precisely 13 days since Livewire lost in a one on one match with Scott Cage for the Network title. The cold mid-summer night air cuts like a crisp pane of glass across Livewire’s face as he sits in solitude at the top of a hill somewhere on the outskirts of his home of residence in Los Angeles. Livewire is dressed in a pair of tatty stone-washed jeans, a red and black flannel shirt and a pair of black workmen boots. His face looks pasty from a lack of sleep and food and his once boyish good looks are replaced with an unshaven jaw line which bares some uneven stubble. In Livewire’s right hand is a bottle of cheap Russian vodka, in his left hand is a lit cigarette to accompany it. Livewire had sworn from the day one of joining RUSH wrestling to never fall back into the black hole of cigarettes and alcohol, but it seems at times like this hallucinogenics and mood changers such as cigarettes and alcohol are needed. Livewire takes another puff of the cigarette as the smoke slowly wafts to the sky surpassing the late evening Beverly hills skyline and a backdrop of a giant poster of the U.S box office smash “Spiderman”. As the cigarette smoke glides by the face of Manhattans very own fictional superhero, we focus once again on the face of Livewire, a man also seen to some wrestling purists as a hero of sorts, but now more of a fallen hero. “Am I really as bad as I believe I am at wrestling?” Livewire asks himself, struggling with the stark reality that he just might not be the entertainer, rising star and icon that he likes to believe that he is. Wrestling is a strange sport, one that very few call just a sport nowadays and instead call that catchy term “Sports entertainment” I believe that wealthy businessman Vincent McMahon invented it. Well whatever, you Joe public, the many Internet smarks and casual followers of the game may call it, it is indeed a strange and some times ego-sapping profession. “One day I’m the number one contender to the Network title and the fifth highest selling marketable name in RUSH, the next I’m at the bottom of the wrestling ladder and unable to sell my T-shirts for $2.00 each” Livewire says, reinforcing the point that was just being explained. Basically unless you are a mega-star and wrestling celebrity like a Jason Snow or a Sebastian Toomes, then you are highly unlikely to remain remotely popular in the publics eye. The honest truth is that you cant be the darling of the fans forever, it is a honest truth that Livewire is struggling to come to terms with, and unfortunately for the past 13 days straight it has lured him to the top of this very hill that he is crouched upon and has left him pondering…..thinking his future through, under the intoxication of alcohol and cigarettes, both used to dull even the numbest of pains. “But I haven’t become a failure in just the space of 13 days. I’m not some has-been at the tender age of twenty-one. I still have a lot to offer, I just need to get my spark back, my certain flamboyance and style that separated me from the rest.” Livewire mumbles these words whilst taking another puff of his cigarette, no doubt filling his lungs with various toxins. He then takes a mouthful of the strong, cheap Russian vodka. The vodka’s acidic taste causes Livewire to spit out half the contents of the drink out of his mouth and onto a mound of grass on the hill. Livewire’s pupils now seem to be dilating and his eyeballs have turned a fiery red colour. “I am now a NOTHING” Livewire begins putting his head into his hand. “I have achieved nothing in my professional career, just like I achieved nothing back in England as a soccer player. My life is destined for failure, only men like Chris Sloboda and Cassius Deamont are SOMEBODIES in Rush. They are both present and former World champions, they both aspire to be better than their rivals. Being a failure is my rival as Chris Matthews is to Cassius Deamont as Jeremy Howard is to Chris Sloboda. Hold on….what am I saying?” Livewire calls out. “I hate Cassius Deamont and I hate Boda even more. Why am I heralding these two men as somebody’s? They are just two spineless jackasses who get kicks out of hurting others. I don’t want to be like that, I don’t want to inflict pain on others. Damn it! I need my spark, and I know just the person to help me.” Livewire stands up and throws the half-empty bottle of Vodka off down the side of the hill. He takes one last long puff of his cigarette and stubs it out onto the wet grass of the hill. He takes a moment to breathe in the early Wednesday morning and buttons up his flannel shirt before walking away. He can be heard saying something under his breath as he gingerly climbs down the steep slippery hill. “Fiona is the ying to my yang, the vital cog that gets me ticking” he says smiling. “If she cant get my spark back nobody can”. Livewire nods his head in a defiant fashion before falling into the darkness of the L.A night as morning sets in. So what lies next for our plucky crestfallen hero? Will he find the ying to his yang….whatever that means? Take care Livewire-ites.
