New Experiences: Have Fun Gene

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 Dr. Jenkins’ plan was simple. Give G.O.D. the drive to win. Now G.O.D. is a monster, a beast, indestructible, the perfect specimen, and he is an astonishing athlete, however his drive is not what it could be. His drive is the drive of an athlete to win. His drive is the drive of a human to be the better in competition. He has drive, but his drive is that of a child’s to be better. G.O.D. is only a handful of months old. This is where the great doctor’s plan comes in. You see, G.O.D. is a creature of joy. The joy he understands is simplistic for he has not experienced a great deal of joy in his short life. His many pleasantries include working out, sweet fruits, and water. Many simple things that so many people experience on a daily basis or even consider a chore. However G.O.D. does not know of so many things so comparing these to others is a great deal better than his normal life of reading, talking strategy, learning, and sleeping. Very simple. So the doctor had an idea. Introduce G.O.D. to certain things and promise them to him if he won. Simple, eh? Yes, very. So the great doctor told Gene Otto Droidman of all these wonders that he would see for the first time. Things he had not seen before. Things that would make him hungry for more. Things that would make him smile so much he would become happy for so long. He would wait for days, weeks, months, maybe years to experience the joy that was all G.O.D. had been missing out on for the past six months. And so the two headed from one venue to the other while making so many stops on the way. And with each event Jenkins first said with a smile, “Have fun Gene.”
