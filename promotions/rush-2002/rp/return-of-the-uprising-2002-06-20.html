Return of the Uprising...

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20, 2002 Yes, the silence has been broken... Silence, a word that can protect you, a word that some people live by as a rule, as a way to keep themselves sane and in charge. Silence, a word that RICKY STARR should learn the meaning of. Silence, a word that would have kept Marvin Harris's lack of intelligence hidden. Silence, a word that would have made a lot of people's lives a lot easier… Silence. And in the little time it took for Dark Haze to break his silence, he showed why he will never be main event material. He showed why he will always be doomed to suffer the horrible wrath of the card opener status. He showed that while a good gimmick will get you places, those places aren't always where you wish yourself to be... Your skills as a wrestler are limited, your skills as a brawler are limited, and your skills as a gimmick are mediocre. All in all? Haze, you just don't make the grade. I suppose if role-call were to continue, next on the list would be Ricky Starr... Is there some sort of requirement in RUSH that says the moment that someone joins, they must completely rip off someone original like myself? Is there a rule that states that once a member of RUSH, you must steal what I have made famous? What I have made stand out, and what I have made? Whether we count down the list of names, and as it goes on and on, with no end in sight, what does it all mean? It means quite simply that you are all aware of my overabundance of talent, skill, knowledge and intelligence, that you just have to be like me? That with us prospering so simply into legendary status, that the only way to stop me, is to become me? Quite sad when the truth becomes evident... but truth is exactly what it is. But, Ricky...I suppose I should give you something. I welcome you to continue your ascent to superstardom, I welcome you to continue your rise to glory, and I welcome you to attempt to do all that while walking through me. See Ricky Starr, you can say things, and I can say things, but words are words, and actions are actions, and wrestling is wrestling, and winning is winning. You can say I will make fun of your life, and you are right, I did and I still will. You, a dirty cheater, someone who uses every trick in the book to win matches, someone who uses anything that isn't on his body to overcome, someone who plays by different rules then what real men do...and you say that to Me? The one man on the wrestler that is a real wrestler. A real man, and a real athlete. I am, in essence, the prototypical wrestler. How? Well let me lay this on the line for you Rick, I wrestle not for money, not for a job, but for my love for it. Because I can do this, and I excel while I do this. Ricky, try to tell yourself the truth next time, because what you have said is nothing but exhaust, nothing but dribble which I can't tolerate to listen to anymore. I will not deny that you are great at what you do, you are near excellent, and I don't mind admitting that... however, what you do is exactly why you will lose. Because the game that you play is a violent, grab what you can, no talent, hard-headed one. The game that I play however, which are the rules you will be playing by, is an intellectual, talentful, skilled, important game. It is a game that everyone wishes they could do exactly like me, but have to settle for second best. You, Ricky Starr, are that person standing in second best, you are that person struggling to reach, jumping and scraping your fingers on the bottom of my boots, stretching for everything you are worth to be better then you are... to be as good as you wish to be... to be as good as I am. You aren't though, and that is what separates us. Quite simply, you are good, I am, for lack of a better word, uncanny. I am uncanny in my abilities, my intelligence and my uniqueness... No one is anything like me, not you, not Marvin Harris, not Dark Haze. No one is like me, because when I was made... They broke the mold. Haze. Dark Haze. Your reaction I can almost see already, visualizing in my head the complaints and attempts you will be spewing. I see the image with the back of my eyes, watching you struggle to make a point of me being repetitive, or me being unoriginal, or me lying like a rug... But deep down inside, you know the real truth is quite simply that, you have nothing you can say about me, towards me, around me or anything. Unlike the rosters you are used to being a part of, and the opponents you are used to fighting, in my interviews, cocky, conceited, arrogant... whatever, I am still near Flawless. So the little accusations that you will be forced to make will not sullen your character. You will simply be in the same giant boat with everyone else that realized there was nothing they could simply say or do to me. Do not worry, because a loss to Scott Cage is nothing to be ashamed of, and I believe that you could actually use this as a learning experience... Something along the lines of don't do drugs, don't drink and drive, Don't mess with Scott Cage. Yes, Dark Haze. You make rap yourself a new rhyme soon enough, trying to get a point across that I am cocky, arrogant, and conceited... And point in fact, you are half-correct. I have said it numerous times against Lazarus, as he never seemed to catch the point I was throwing him. I am all of those things, but it is for a reason. I am convinced, Haze, I am convinced because I know my abilities, I know my strengths and my weaknesses, I know my opponents and I know my limits. Would you like to know my limits? My limits are quite simply endless, because while you will do anything to win your types of matches, I will do the exact same thing, although my pressure that I apply, is nothing more then glistening skill and shining talent. That's right Haze, I can talk the talk, which you can quite simply see me doing this very second, and in a few short days you will see me walk the walk. All catch-phrases aside, all nicknames thrown out, all gimmicks pushed back and all angles vanished... Standing eye to eye with me Haze, you haven't a chance, you haven't a prayer, you are up the creek without a paddle, and that creek seems to be leading to nothing more then trouble. Lose your smile, drop your bottle, cowar or come forth, whichever you finally decided on doing, head this advice... I will let this go when I want to let this go, and only at that time. THIS...is not the time. Be careful Haze…Starr, you aren't in Kansas anymore. (The camera fades into the hotel room...as Scott walks out of the bathroom. His muscular calfs are visible due to the shorts he wears, the white shorts that hang down just below his knees. His upperbody is held tightly by the white wife beater that hugs his muscles, showing them off for all the world to see. They may not be the biggest, but perhap the most toned. His hair is all white now, not a hint of Blonde left in it, and it is spiked up on all directions, giving him a lethal weapon if he were to stab someone with them... but who would do that? His body is covered in jewellery, silver, the only good looking color there is, other then the sweet gold he wears around his waist. Most attentive is the bandages wrapped around the top-left half of his face, badly burnt from the explosion at Ignition! Still, a stylish bastard... Damn that man is Stylish.) Sirius Cage: Hey Scott… Scott Cage: Yeah? Sirius Cage: May I ask you something? Scott Cage: I swear...I didn't know he was still alive... Sirius Cage: Huh? Scott Cage: Oh...umm, sure, ask away. Sirius Cage: Where have you been? Sirius Cage: What do you mean? Scott Cage: A few days ago, I found you gazing out at the stars. Now for the past few days, you were no where to be seen. Scott Cage: Oh, I was doing some site seeing. Sirius Cage: Of what? Scott Cage: The sites. Sirius Cage: ...right. Scott Cage: Yeah...well, I'm going to go for a walk. (Scott heads towards the door, but is stopped by the hand of Sirius…) Sirius Cage: What are you up to, Scott? Scott Cage: Huh? Sirius Cage: You...going out, on these 'walks'...what are you cooking up? Scott Cage: Nothing, I swear... Sirius Cage: You can't lie to me Scott, I will eventually find out. Scott Cage: I'm not lying to you... Sirius Cage: So all you're doing is telling the truth... Scott Cage: I know! (Sirius mutters to himself... and then removes his hand from Scott's shoulder...) Sirius Cage: Go on Scott... but stay out of trouble. Scott Cage: When have I been known to get into trouble? Sirius Cage: I ask that question every time I need a laugh. Scott Cage: What's that supposed to mean? Sirius Cage: Just…just go on your walk Scott... (Scott shrugs his shoulders, and reaches for the doorknob...) Scott Cage: I'll be back later... Sirius Cage: Just remember to think before you act... (Scott smiles at Sirius, and walks out the door…) Sirius Cage: Something, something is wrong with him. I don't know what it is, but something is fishy about the way he's been acting. Possibly drugs, sleep deprivation, or that explosion to his face, he is more askew than normal... (The camera fades to black as Sirius walks off-screen…) TO BE CONTINUED…
