The Phone Call.

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/7/02 The wind howled out side as the wrestler known as Andi the Magnificent continued to sit vigilantly at Fluffy, his half rabbit and half human valets side. It had been some 4 weeks since Jason Jameson had hit her over the head with a steel chair on a NTICW broadcast of Tuesday Mayhem. In those four weeks Andi had been signed along with most Syn and Teresa and most of the other NTICW Roster had been signed to RUSH when Boda had sold NTICW’s contracts to Jeremy Howard. Also Fluffy had had an operation to remove the Blood clot from her brain, which had left her in a coma since. Andi stood up from the dark green chair that he had been sitting in since seven thirty this morning and took a good look around the room taking in Fluffy’s drawn face trying to ignore the insistent bleeping of the ventilator machine and the cardboard like white sheets but still Fluffy didn’t move. The one thing Andi couldn’t understand was why she looked so content when he believed that she must be in so much pain seen as the doctors told him that she could hear everything that he said. Andi sat there thinking as he held tightly to her hand as he thought about the first time he would step into a RUSH ring in front of a RUSH fans the only thing that was worrying him was what would Boda have install for him, for even though they where in a totally new federation they was sure to be know way Boda could forgive him for what he had done. Andi took a glance at the clock and was almost shocked to see the time was 2am the Wednesday he knew he would have to leave soon to be able to make it time for his first RUSH show where he his tagging with his OU partner Syn against Status Quo and Dante Jackson. Suddenly the phone rang. Andi almost jumped at the unexpected interruption to his solitude almost cursing at being disturbed he made his way to the phone from the tall glass window that over looked the New York skyline. “Hello, Andrew Ashby speaking how can I help you?” Andi waited as he listened to the noise in the background before a woman with a southern accent replied. “Hello is that Andi the Magnificent?” She said in her rather lofty manner “Yes, I am speaking how may I help you?” Andi said getting a little annoyed. “Hello Andi my name is Anita Aresable the new PA to Chris Sloboda.” The woman said trying to make herself important. “Hello Anita is there a problem?” “No there is not but Mr Sloboda asked me to mention to you that he has given you permeation to stay in New York with Miss Fluffy.” She said although Andi thought he could here a little piece of uncertainty in her voice. “Erm ok but if Boda was willing me to stay here with Fluffy why did he book me in a match this week?” Andi said suspiciously. “You know Mr Sloboda he is always optimistic and was kind of hoping Miss Fluffy would have been a little better.” The woman said but unforeseen to Andi she sounded even more unsure than she had perversely. “Yeah so why does Boda want me to stay here.” Andi said suspiciously. “ Mr Sloboda does not inform me of his intensions all he said was to ring you at the hospital and inform you your services aren’t required this week have a nice day. With that the woman hung up without Andi being unable to answer but still Andi couldn’t shake of the feeling that he had been set up but thinking no more of it he sat back downed and continued to watch over Fluffy hoping against hope that she will make a recovery and soon.
