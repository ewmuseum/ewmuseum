Unconsciousness- A State of Bliss

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 29th, 2002 A.D. Just a mere minutes after the cameras stopped rolling for the latest edition of RUSH Wrestling’s Adrenaline, the fans had already spilled out of the Hartford Civic Centre and back to their homes. I was still laying face down, in a pool of my own crimson blood. People thought it was an act, nobody even came out to help me. I was barely even conscious when it happened, it’s not like I could barely feel anything flowing through my body. Hearing? Hell no, the screams from the crowd mixed in with the thundering blackjack shot from Deamont pretty much ceased all my hearing. Seeing? None. All I saw was a wall in front of my face, a wall painted crimson. WHACK! That was the second blackjack shot, I fell down to the mat with a loud thud. My blood began to stain the mat. With my eyes closed, I was unconscious. Deamont and his bitch lackeys had left the ring, Deamont’s hideous theme song playing in the background, I could hear Kris Raves shrieking like a little woman in the background. “Well, we're out of time now.” He shrieked. “So, I'm Kris Raves, this has been the best edition of Adrenaline ever, and we'll see you again next week for more Matthews-beating action!” I could barely hear it, and then lights turned off with a loud thump type sound. By now most people had made their way out of the arena, heading back to their cars and to their homes. Cough. I spluttered up some blood, my eyes squinted together as some vomit was spurted up staining the mat with not only my crimson blood, but also my disgusting green vomit. With my knees weak, I tired to stand back up, no luck. My knees gave way and I fell down onto one knee, crawling over to the ropes. My arms were weak, after 30 minutes of pure hell on my body against the son of a bitch Deamont; my arms could pull myself up. I managed to limp my way up the ramp way, the lights were off as I pulled myself up along the cold, hard steel ramp. Normally there would be static purple lights hazing down from above the RUSHtron, but they were off. Blackness from above. I slowly parted the curtains, a pained look on my face as I saw the staircase leading down to backstage area, where all the workers were and where the wrestlers locker rooms were. I didn’t want to climb down the stairs, my legs were so sore. My arms writhing in pain. My head with a searing agony. As I took my first down the cold metal steps, I immediately lost my footing. I was sent tumbling down the stairs, landing with a thud on the black carpet. I got back onto my knees, holding onto my back with a loud scream. Splat. Blood spat down onto the carpet from the large open wound on my forehead, black and red mixed together on the carpets as I coughed, spluttered....up my own blood. I was on my knees, trying to stand back up but the sheer pain aching all over my body wouldn’t allow me to do so. I could hear a faint scuffing sound not too far away, feet walking along the carpet. All I saw was a blur of silver walking quickly, metallic and bright this silver was. It slowly walked up to me, stopping before my blood stricken face. Tyler Valentine. I groaned in pain as I held onto my head, trying to stop the profuse bleeding. Valentine frowned as he looked down at me, my red and black wrestling pants with a hole on them, showing a gash on my thigh. Valentine gasps as I squint my eyes together, trying not to look at the blood flowing from my head...from my lips...from my legs.... “Damn” he said, shaking his head. Valentine frowned down at me, as blood spat out from my mouth. Red lined my teeth, I had spat out more blood then I ever had in my entire wrestling career...no wait....my entire life. Not even the time I defended my child hood friend, Sean in a five on one beat down by a group of seventeen year olds when the two of us fourteen. Battered down to a pulp, even getting a piece of wood smashed over my book....not even that compared to the pain and agony of the situation I’m in right now. Valentine looked down at me, kneeling down on my left knee I breathed heavily. Valentine extended his right arm out to me, my cold hard hands slapped into his as I was pulled back to my feet, limping heavily. Barely able to stand. I coughed, I spluttered, I spat blood. I did all these things and more as I walked along, limping, using Tyler’s arm as a support. Where were we going? Hopefully we’d make it to the locker room, safe from anyone like Deamont.... Walden.... Calyx... Flare... Cannon and Boda. I held my head low, the sour taste of blood still on my lips. I grunted in pain as Valentine kicked open the door. “Jesus” he said, looking down at the blood flowing from my body. “Remind me..... never fucking cross Cassius fucking Deamont.” Tyler turned around and looked over at the workers outside, none of them seemingly interested in what had happened to me. He cursed at them under his breath, annoyed that none of them had bothered to help them when I was writhing in pain after the brutal IRS Plan beat down. “Fucking workers” is what he cursed, I could faintly here him. When Tyler turned around he saw me, laying face down in a pool of my own blood. “FUCK!” is what he cursed; loud enough that people in Honolulu would of heard. Valentine bent down and began to shake my arms, trying to wake me up....but he couldn’t of. I was slipping from consciousness at a faster, more rapid rate then one could think. Valentine was frantic. I was his best friend, slipping from consciousness. I felt sorry for him, seeing me like this. He kicked the door in annoyance, sprinting outside to where all the workers. “SOMEBODY CALL 911!” he yelled at them at the frantically, finally one of the backstage workers complied with his request and took out his cell phone and phoned 911. When Valentine walked back into the room, my eyes were shut and breathing was soft. As Valentine quickly ran outside, thoughts began to chalk up inside my head.... ...Deamont had destroyed almost every single member of the original Main-Event scene. If he didn’t do it himself? He had something to do with it, I’d know. Count the people who’ve fallen to Deamont, those from the main event that are currently in a state like mine.... Skye Stone Jonathan Drachen Maxx Crimson Hiro Saotome Tex Kelley Chris Matthews Brock Newbludd Steve Brent Most of us are like me, laying in a pool of our own blood or incapacitated in a hospital bed....or sitting at home, thinking about what could have been. I’m leaning that way at the moment....to hell I’ll let it happen to me. No way in hell Deamont is going to destroy me like he did to Skylar, Jon, Max, Tex, Hiro and Steve. No way in fucking hell. But now I think about, being unconscious....it ain’t that bad after all. At least I can’t feel pain. That’s always a plus. Maybe when I wake up...errr....if I wake up, I can pay Deamont a visit down at Danse Macabre and...smash a plank of wood over his skull. Yeah, that’d be cool. We’ll see. We’ll see who draws first blood. I’ll see, when I wake up.
