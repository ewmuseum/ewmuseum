Hate...

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/02 Hate? You really want to know what Hate is really all about? Like Anger, Lust, Love, Pain and Sadness, it is a natural part of the human mechanism, the human complex. It’s all biological damnit. All of them, chemical reactions stimulated neural impulses running up and down the body constantly. It’s so automatic yet leaves a seething, lingering feeling that one has the disturbing urge to satisfy. Probably one reason why I never did hallucinogens Just like happiness, it is triggered. Just like Love or Lust, it comes with a lingering, growing urge. Just like Anger, it burns deep and broods. Do we see any similarities forming there? It possesses an aspect of every other feeling in the book, making it one complex chemical reaction. Even when one feels it to be so deep, so serious, and so significant until nothing else matters besides IT… It’s still a chemical reaction No different to a professor mixing shit around in a lab to produce some sort of result Hate is just that A result Over history, many have failed to recognize that one crucial point. Some say that Hate was given to us from a Pandora’s box, opened by an unwitting recipient. Some say that Hate is poetic, therefore penning it down in gold-lined books, as if presuming one could turn it into something dark and sadistically beautiful. A Pandora’s box and many similar narratives are just what they are, narratives and nothing more than that. It was used to explain things we cannot explain at the time… Or simply cannot accept. It is natural for humanity to be ashamed, ashamed of its darker aspect. The Will Of God? Is it really like that? I don’t know anybody whose automatic instinct would be to stand up and said “My Fault”. Hate? There is nothing else out there that can be blamed for that Except You You think I wanted to fight? You think I wanted to go out there and do just that night after night? You think it gives me some sadistic pleasure to do just that? You think breaking somebody and bleeding anybody half-dead brings me some sort of sweet rush? You think I live for those 15 minutes or so? In the minds of many there can be only two obvious answers: Yes or No To me, it’s not really that straightforward of an answer It’s not black or white, goodie or baddie… More like because I have to Remus Slatkovic would have shuddered in horror or even throw up if he knew that was the life he had to live.To eat by the fist and fall by it That’s just not Remus But as for Ricky Starr Now that’s different You see it wasn’t really Ricky’s choice. Nor did he refuse it either The spotlight seduced him and the rush flew through him faster than the Niagra Falls. It made him what he is What he never wanted to be What he embraced Fighting it proved to be futile So in the end, he became an embodiment of it The real dead person in the story is Remus Slatkovic Remus Slatkovic is dead Long live Remus Slatkovic Long live the rush Long live Ricky Starr The Starr felt the rush a long time ago Now the real question is Can YOU feel the RUSH?
