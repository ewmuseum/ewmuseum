Date: because I felt like it. A dark hotel room. Well, semi-dark. The bluish-white glow of a television screen illuminates most of the scantiliy-furnished temporary accommodations. The rest is bathed in shadows. Sitting on one of the double beds, Indian-style, with his Syndicate Title pressed close to his chest, is El Spiffy!. He rocks nervously back and forth, covered in sweat. It’s a position he’s been in for several days now. The shots swings around to show the TV screen. The 22 inch monitor meekly displays what looks to be a video of Scott Cage. He’s cutting a promo on somebody. We can’t hear what he’s saying, though, because there is no sound to the video. The only thing we hear is the superimposed conversation between the Spanish commentators, Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil. They’re nowhere to be found in the room, so we can only assume they’re conversing somewhere else. :”How long’s he been like that?” “Since Crossroads.” “Any idea what’s causing it?” “I think he’s worried about running out of material. Now that PI have shipped off to job their brains out in PCW, he doesn’t have a foil anymore.” Over El Spiffy’s shoulder, Cage’s first match ever in RUSH is playing. He does everything that every other 6’5, surprisingly-agile-but-still-powerful, wannabe-cool-heel does. “He could always make fun of Joe Bananas.” “I don’t think it’s racism, man. It’s creatively. He’s drained.” “He should be. We milked that thug angle for all it was worth. Since we debuted in April or May.” “That’s probably the issue. He’s spent so long making fun of the same person. Now he’s gotta change and he doesn’t think he can do it.” The video shows another promo. It looks a lot like the first one. In fact, is exactly like the first one, save a name or two. El Spiffy!’s frenzied rocking slows a bit. “I’m sure he can think of something stupid.” “That’s what I’m thinking, too. He just has to snap out of it. Come out of hiding for a little.” Transition to yet another promo. It could easily be mistaken for on of the first two. In fact, if you had blinked, you would’ve missed the transition between them and just thought it was one big long one. El Spiffy!’s rocking slows a little more. He blinks his eyes, as the eyelids suddenly seem heavier. “He hasn’t even turned in the belt yet, has he?” “Nope.” “Jesus. Still carrying the custom-made ‘Quo’ Title, huh? All those diamonds and everything.” “Gaudy piece of sh*t. We should just pawn it and use some of the cash to send Quo a lesson of Hooked on Phonics.” El Spiffy! yawns a little as he keeps watching. The screen shows Ignition. Cage holds up the Network Title. Marvin Harris doesn’t seem to care, even if he is unconscious. “I guess it is hard to stop mocking Quo.” “No kidding. He was an easy target. Maybe we should work on moving on ourselves before we start convincing him to.” “Good point. Does he know about his match on Adrenaline?” “He knows of it. Whether or not he’ll be prepared is a different story.” Another promo, this time with the belt…but still bearing an uncanny resemblance to every other one on the tape. El Spiffy!’s rocking has stopped completely. He yawns again and blinks a few times to keep watching. “First title defense. That’s sorta important.” “I guess. He’s been on a roll though. If we can help him pull it together in time, he’ll be alright.” “Has he been resting at all?” Promos galore. If you were watching the tape, you’d swear to God that you were watching the same one on a loop. El Spiffy yawns uncontrollably and fights to keep his eyes open. “Nope. Just holding onto the title, rocking back and forth, muttering ‘holla back youngin’ over and over.” “That’s kinda freaky.” “You’re telling me.” Scott Cage loses the Network Title, then finishes second in the Crossroads main event. El Spiffy! wavers a bit, lets out a huge yawn, then slumps over on the bed. “So what’s he doing now?” “I told him to relax in the hotel room.” “That’s it?” “Well, yeah. And I gave him something to help him sleep.” The shot zooms to a video box on top of the VCR in the room. “The Best of Scott Cage” is written across the side. The camera pans out in silence, to the head of the bed, where El Spiffy! is out cold, snoring hard. The Syndicate title slips from his open arms to the floor, with yet another eerily similar Cage interview playing in the background. Fade to static.
