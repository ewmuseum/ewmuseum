Stimpo shoots a commercial, latino style.

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/3/02 Another day in the life of Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson, that's me! The scene begins at this point where I am getting freshened up for somekind of commercial. A backdrop behind me reads, 'RUSH Wrestling' in big blue and white jagged letters ahead of a black background making RUSH Wrestling be seen as clear as day. Make-up people and woredrobe people are making me up and freshening me up as if I were some kind of barbie doll, not that I wouldn't mind, but this treatment was getting irritating. Many cameras were all around me as long as lights shining brightly in my eyes. I stood there with my black suit looking stunning as usual with my short blonde hair shining in the light, glinting as I turned my head every which way, testing the cameras. Finally, a man wearing a blue cap and a tan shirt with his white dockers stood up from what seemed to be a director's chair. He got up from his chair and walked straight up to me pushing the others away, stopping my grueling grooming process. 'Alright, you know what you're doing here don't you?' The director asked me for clarification. 'Yea, I'm here to promote the wonders of kitty litter, right?' I answered to the best of my knowledge. The director sighed as he covered his eyes with his hands in grief. 'No, no, NO! Who got this guy?!' The director seemed angered by my reply as he screamed at the entire crew using the cool gizmoes for the cameras and such. 'Well, what AM I doing here?' I asked in curiosity still wondering about the quesiton the Director asked me in the first place. 'Look kid, your here to get RUSH more noticed, by discussing possible outcomes for Adrenaline 15.' The Director sighed as he told me. 'Well, then with all due respect sir, why do we have a huge crew of cameras and such? Shouldn't we just need a few guys and a few lights?' It seemed common sense to ask that. But, then again, my version of common sense can be many others' insane. The Director went silent. As did everyone from the crew all staring back at me in absolute silence. The cameras all pointed to me with the big red light on. Sweat started to cumulate around my forehead as I knew I did something wrong. I looked from right to left and all I saw were these jaws dropped to the floor and the same eyes all gawking at me as if I was the most hated and crazy man on the face of the earth, well their earth anyways. The Director finally looked back at all the crew. 'He's right...' The Director told his men, but he wasn't done. 'Let's get MORE lights, MORE cameras and especially MORE latino singers in here, Stimpo can't be the only one now can he?' It seemed that the Director had motivated his troops. 'Wait no, I meant for less.' It was too late. People were up and about and out of no where, latino singers just happen to show up behind all the cameras warming up for what they thought was a performance. I was the commercialist. I was the one getting the credit. Who were these people? Get them off my stage, I am the star! The Director finally was done getting triple the amount of light and cameras and latino singers especially and was ready to shoot the commercial. 'Ready folks? And.......ACTION!' The director pointed to a camera and the little red light shine right into my eye and I knew that I was on TV for this commercial. 'Hi. My name's Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson. You might remember me from such wrestling education films as, 'Grease that mat up,' or my personal favorite, 'Who let those spandex out?' But I'm here today to talk to you about RUSH wrestling, hit it boys!.' I pointed to the latino singers as they came running up to the stage. They came right up in the vie of the camera and stood corresponding with me in a horse shoe fashion around the microphone in front of me facing upwards towards the ceiling for it to hear us clearly. 'Five, Six, Seven, Eight!' I counted musically, even thought it looks the same to everyone. As in the last episode of mine, the same trumpeters from my battle with that latino Imposter back at the RUSH arena. Just as before, the trumpets began loudly in the style of 'Livin' La Vida Loca' as all four of us, being latino singers, had to steal the show by singing perfectly for the camera. The song finally came to a close with the cheering of the crew. 'Well, that's all we needed to tell you since this already costs millions of dollars which I can spend and you can't! Isn't that great?' I said with a smile. 'CUT!!!!' The director seem quite angered by the way I did commercials. 'What? I did it just as you said.' 'You sang.' 'Right. 'That has nothing to do with Wrestling.' 'Oh, right.' 'What do you have to say for yourself?' 'Umm....erm....COME MY LATINO FRIENDS, WE MAKE HASTE!' I said running away from the director along with the latino singers out of the commercial area as the camera suddenly cuts to a black ending this 'promo' if you would like to call it that.
