The Changing of the Guard...

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.8.02 'To reign is worth ambition though in hell;Better to reign in hell, than serve in heav’n.'-- John Milton Once again, my voice graces the world. My friends, I have just received what quite possibly is the most pleasing challenge of my life, morally and physically. Chris Matthews, the man who has been an unstoppable annoyance since his debut in RUSH Wrestling a long way back, the man who has challenged the RUSH World Title from arguably the best wrestler in the world today, Cassius Deamont. The man who would do anything to make you believe he is something more then he is. More then that, the man that has pissed me off since day one, the man that I said would be the biggest triumph of my life, and the man that I swore I would do everything in my career to end the idiotic ramblings that Chris Matthews constantly spews out. Matthews, you may have bored me to sleep timeless times in the past, but I will do what I say I will do. I will beat you in the ring, because I am the better wrestler, I am the smarter thinker and as much as I despise the term, I am a far more intelligent ring general. Matthews... I have what belongs to me and what I deserved, although in that match, I will get far more then I deserved. Matthews, I give you everything that I can, respect if you wish, accolades for a well fought match and even a hand shake for taking me to the limit, but truthfully, if I never see you in the ring again, it would be more then a joyous day for me. What do I have to look at now? The weak attempts that have already begun to surface in RUSH by means of the NTICW Division brought in by Chris Sloboda. The always expected but never needed push of someone who doesn't deserve it. Although it has to be someone new this time, someone who is obviously not ready for it, and is obviously not smart enough for it. G.O.D.? Perhaps the lowliest of the possibilities. Rich Cannon? Perhaps staying as a jobber would serve you better. Do you not recall how I decimated you, in your own style of wrestling no less? In the exact same way that you said you couldn't be beaten. Now you come after the spotlight I have snatched, well I have some advice for you. Keep that very real and very painful loss that I handed you in the front of your mind, keep thinking back to it when you are deciding whether to stick your little nose into my business. Then think about how angry and immortal I am now that I do not have control of my own Life. Now what Cannon? G.O.D.? Statuz Quo? I don't know you, I have seen you wrestle and do promos for a few months, and you are by far the most ill-witted, irrepressible, idiotic and un-intelligent person I have ever met. Do you think that by make a pathetic sitcom in front of the camera so much it helps your character? It helps you? Wrong, It makes you look small, it makes you look petty, and it shows the reason why you are nothing more than a One-Hit Wonder. Because you are not smart enough. 'I'll fucking kill you' this, 'I smoke so much weed' that. Quo, you are an imbecile, and if you dare try to ignore me again I will make your life Hell. Why? Because I don't like you, I don't respect you and quite frankly you are what a wrestler, let alone a champion, should never be. End of fucking story. I really don't have very much to say right now, and I really didn't want to talk at all, but I hope that I got my point across. Chris Matthews, I am going so be ready, I haven't even come close to showing my Full Potential, and I guarantee that I will make you feel it all. And the NTICWers who think that they will be pushed harder then they ever thought possible...you can be pushed and pushed and pushed, but that does not mean you still won't lose. I am the epic of wrestling today, the pinnacle. I am what every wrestler wants to be, but can't. Run your circus, do what you must but be ready for certain demise, because I am not in this game, this life now for playing, for fun. I am in it for revenge, and until I get Statuz Quo, everyone else WILL BE Statuz Quo...and that is someone that you don't want to be. (Our camera fades in to the streets of Beverly Hills...where Scott and Sirius Cage are walking back to their Godfather’s mansion…) Sirius Cage: Well Scott… Scott Cage: Yes? Sirius Cage: You've accomplished your goal. Scott Cage: I scored the game winning touchdown in that all female football game? Sirius Cage: ..... Scott Cage: I'll take that as a no.Sirius Cage: Scott, you’ve made Marvin Harris into an MIA. Scott Cage: Oh yeah, but that wasn't really anything significant though. Sirius Cage: All right... Scott Cage: I mean, sure...nearly killing his brothers and taking the Network Title from him…yeah...that was funny. Sirius Cage: It was evil... Scott Cage: Funny, evil...good, bad...whatever. (Sirius frowns...as a stranger walks up to Scott…) Stranger: It's...it's you! Scott Cage: Why...hello there, little guy… (Scott smiles as he pets the man on the head...and gets his hand slapped away...) Stranger: Get off of me...I'm not a child. Scott Cage: Well, look at the tyke who's got his panties all tied up... Stranger: I'm not a tyke...and I don’t wear panties either! Scott Cage: Aren't you special...what do you want, an autograph? Stranger: An autograph? From you? Scott Cage: Why of course...I mean, that is why you wanted to see me, isn't it? Stranger: Not really. Scott Cage: Ah...so you want a picture so that all of your friends will believe you met the unstoppable Scott Cage, right? Stranger: Not exactly. (Scott scratches his head...) Scott Cage: So...what is it you want? Stranger: Well...do you see this cookie sheet? Scott Cage: Oh yeah...that's a nice looking cookie sheet. Did you happen to bake me some cookies? Stranger: Well, sorta. See, I got this nice cookie sheet here, and I'd love you to make it worth something... Scott Cage: Anything for a fan... Stranger: Well...umm...could you turn around? I'm kind of embarrassed. Scott Cage: Sure thing... (Scott turns around, and the stranger pulls out the cookie pan. He winds up...and slams Scott in the back of the head...dazing the atlelete…) Stranger: That's for fucking ending Marvin Harris’s career! He didn't deserve to be fucked over like that! You and your brother should rot in hell...you're a disgrace to humans... (The stranger gives Sirius the middle finger good measure and storms off, as Sirius runs to Scott’s side...) Sirius Cage: Scott! Scott...are you all right? Scott Cage: Huh? Sirius Cage: That man...he attacked you with the cookie sheet... Scott Cage: Did I sign my name? Sirius Cage: Shit...Scott, how many fingers am I holding up? (Sirius out 3 fingers in front of Scott’s face...as he just stares blankly at them...) Sirius Cage: Well Scott....how many fingers am I holding up? Scott Cage: I'm...I'm thinking... (Sirius glances at his watch, back to Scott, and back to his watch again...) Sirius Cage: Scott...you couldn't have told me how many fingers I was holding up, regardless of you being smacked, could you? Scott Cage: Probably not...but that was disturbing... Sirius Cage: What part about it? Scott Cage: Well...he ran off before I could sign the cookie sheet... (Sirius smacks himself in the head...) Scott Cage: Nah...the back of my head is what hurts. Sirius Cage: Well, it certainly is the frontal lobe of mine that is disturbing me. Scott Cage: Did you get hit with a cookie sheet too? Sirius Cage: Something like that...something very similar. (Sirius turns a street corner, and Scott shakes his head...) Scott Cage: Did I do the right thing? Sirius Cage: About what? Scott Cage: Retiring Marvin Harris... Sirius Cage: You don't know you retired him yet Scott.Scott Cage: C'mon...you saw the glazed look in his eyes... Sirius Cage: You plowed your convertible through his truck, of course there was a glazed look... Scott Cage: You didn't see the fear in his eyes when I blasted his brothers with the truck...you didn't see his heart sink down into his toes after I won the Network Title… Sirius Cage: No, no I didn't. Scott Cage: I practically killed him. Physically, emotionally, and mentally... Sirius Cage: That may be so Scott...but the fact remains... Scott Cage: I probably killed the kid. Harris...poor kid, he couldn't even try to mess with me. Sirius Cage: Yes, Scott, I suppose so. Scott Cage: Well, I'm right, aren't I? Sirius Cage: Only time will tell, won't it? Scott Cage: Nonetheless, I'm still not feeling good about retiring Harris… Sirius Cage: Well Scott, if he does in fact retire, he's nothing to be worrying about anyway. Scott Cage: But of course… (Sirius breathes a sigh of relief...as the brothers both walk off into the sunset…)
