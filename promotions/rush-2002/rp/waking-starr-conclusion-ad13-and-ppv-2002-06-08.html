Waking Starr {conclusion} (Ad13 and PPV)

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/02 The large doors swing open once more. They shuffled in… In front of Ricky Starr and his two companions are a collection of some of the most brilliant minds in the business world. They also happen to be the most under-recognized for their efforts and achievements but that is not without some form of merit: Choosing prudence and cautiousness over notoriety and future idiocy. Even the heir to Josh The Starr’s empire and amassed fortune bowed his head in a sign of courtesy and reverence in the presence of these esteemed veterans in the field of entrepreneurs, aiding the late Joshua DuBois in creating and making the business giant known as Starr Enterprises. For a moment, Ricky felt a pang of guilt in his throat for he had done nothing to aid the legacy of his adopted father, his mentor and his namesake. Each man took his seat along the long table that represented the organization’s elite. With each person seated, Ricky felt smaller and dwarfed. For a moment, this was not the Ricky Starr who had burst into the lobby, blatantly asking for the board, THE board to assemble on a short notice. Each man has taken his seat; their hardened, serious faces look towards Ricky to be briefed on the agenda of the day. In fact, all Ricky wanted to do right now was to hide under a rock and stay there until hell freezes over. However, one man stood out from the group. He was a hearty looking fellow that Ricky wouldn’t mind downing a pint or two with in the local irish pub on a regular night with the exception that this was not a regular day in the enterprise. With a wide smile and a warm voice to match his rosy-cheeked complexion, he speaks out to Ricky as if an uncle would speak to a favorite nephew. “Ah Ricky! Haven’t seen you in AGES! How has life been?” His English accent was a proverbial contradiction, an upper-class tone mixed with the colloquial delights of casual English pleasantries. For a moment, Ricky could revert to just being himself as he addresses the hearty man. “Haven’t broken a bone, a few bruises here and there, a cut or two, could say I’m doing pretty well so far” A hearty laugh bellows from the gentleman, relaxing the mood of the gathering as the others smiled at this exchange. The rosy-cheeked man, attired in a golf shirt and a gray jacket on top replies at his sarcasm-laced statement “Couldn’t be happier. Mind you, it’s quite the business there. You take a bump here, throw a punch or two even and there you have it, a healthy cut from a good night’s earnings as the end result! What is this world coming to…” The man besides him chuckle while Ricky at the head of the table rolls his eyes at the sarcasm being passed around the room. Ricky snaps his fingers and in an instant, a man with lime sunglasses and a long black trench coat switches on a slide projector as the lights in the room dim. All attention turned to a series of switching slides that showed detailed statistical charts. The noticeable patterns in the charts are that figures are rising most of the time save for the odd light slump or two. After a minute or so, the projector is switched off and the lights brighten again. Ricky Starr focuses his attention back onto the assembly, his cool, confident air of a charming rogue restored. “Now gentleman, the purpose of that was to illustrate a message to each and every person contributing to the successful business entity that we all know now as Starr Enterprises, the legacy of a man far greater than me, Josh The Starr…” Some of those present bow their head slightly in a gesture reverence. “Starr Enterprises has grown beyond anything we could ever imagine since it opened up shop and you all know that better than I for you were TRUE foundation that this organization was built upon.” Most nod their head in approval at the recognition. “This pertains to my first order as the heir and CEO to this established and renowned empire. I want business to move on better than before, which pretty much means, do what you do best” More nod their head in approval of this statement, which served as an official order as well. “From now on, I want detailed reports of operations straight to my desk with carbon copies in sealed envelopes straight to the Residence. Nothing escapes my eye. This is a new stance for a dawn of a new age” A murmur starts among the members of the board, the tones heading along the road of interest at this prospect. “I will try to emulate the precedent that Josh set for me and the rest of you to follow. I expect only guidance and co-operation on the part of the board and that is all I ask” All of them nod their head in approval “I take that as an Amen” Laughter breaks out among the board members as Ricky Starr rises to his feet. “I declare this meeting adjourned!” The doors swing open once more and the leading trio, followed by the board of directors fold out of the room. Most of them go their own separate ways upon reaching the corridor while some head in the direction of the elevators with Ricky Starr’s trio. As an elevator opens, Ricky Starr steps in with his companions along with the hearty Englishman from the meeting. The elevator doors closed and it proceeds to take them to the Lobby. The gentleman looks at Ricky with a smile “It’s a refreshing change for a start and I quite like it” Ricky Starr looks at him with a smile of his own “Yes, a change like that is always good, applies to other areas as well” “I do wonder what you mean by that” The elevator comes to a halt and the doors slide open. Ricky bades the board member farewell as they trot towards the exit, accosted by more reporters who are blocked off by security personnel. Starr is escorted to a waiting limousine but before he steps in, a question catches his attention. “Mr Starr, while being an active employee of the sports entertainment company known as RUSH and now being an active CEO of Starr Enterprises, what can you speculate on the forked road of your future sir?” After a moment of hesitation, the roguish smirk comes over his face again and he simply replies “Hit it”
