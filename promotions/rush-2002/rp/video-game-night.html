Date: Today (Our scene opens up to show a far out shot of the main highway leading Milwaukee, on a nice but cold Wisconsin. The sun can be seen just above the horizon signifying that it is still quite early in the morning. A warm breeze drifts through the air, cutting down on the normally low temperatures. We notice that the highway isn’t that busy this morning, not really crowded or filled with cars considering this about the time everybody goes to work. Then, coming into view of the picture is a 1970 Dodge Charger painted blood red that’s going into the city. The car cruises along, passing other cars with the ease and skill of a professional race car driver. As the car careens along, we slowly but surely zoom closer to it. We then come to as if we were eye level with the car. We see “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd sitting in the drivers seat of the car, with a beer in his hand. While sitting in the passengers seat is Brock’s little brother, Charles “Chili” Newbludd. Chili has a annoyed look on his face as he stares out the window car. We then zoom in closer to the car and cut out for a second, we cut back in to see that we are now in the backseat of the car, we can hear the thunderous sounds Led Zeppelin pumping through the juiced up speakers of the car. Brock nods his head simultaneously to the adrenaline pumping rhythm of Jimmy Pages guitar. Brock turns down the radio and lights up a smoke…) Brock: Quit, yer pouting, dude! (Chili just gives his brother the middle finger and continues looking out the window…) Brock: You best not do that again if you know what’s good for you. (We see a small smirk grow on Chili’s face and he puts his middle finger up again. Brock quickly reaches over and grabs a hold of his finger and starts bending it back. Chili grabs onto Brock’s fist with his free hand and tries to pry it off his finger, but Brock has to good of a grip.…) Chili: Let go you cock licking sonuva…AHHHH! (Brock twists on his finger even more…) Brock: Who’s the b*tch? Chili: You’re a b*tch! A nasty one at that! ( Brock just gives his brother a cocky smile and twists a little more…) Brock: Now, lets try this again, who’s the b*tch? Wait, lets change that who’s the “bitches”? Chili: Ah! AH! Ok…um me! I am a bitch! (Brock eases up a bit and Chili gets a relieved look on his face…) Brock: Bitches is plural, who else is a b*tch? Chili: What!? I dunno!? Gary Coleman!? (Brock wrenches on his finger again…) Brock: Nope! Guess… (Chili’s eyes start watering in pain…) Chili: I dunno, Tex Kelley, he’s a bitch, right!? (Brock lets go of Chilis finger and nods his head with a smile. Chilis holds his finger in his hand an rubs it. He then slugs Brock in the arm with his good hand…)Chili: You d*ckhead! Don’t take your tension out on me, go hit a punching bag or something. Brock: I could, but then again, a punching bag doesn’t make the little whimpering noises that you make. (Brock snickers as Chili glares at his big brother…) Chili: And another thing, I can’t believe you’re making me go back to school! I hate f*cking school! (Brock rolls his eyes and takes a drag of his cigarette…) Brock: What the hell else are you gonna do? Chili: Well, I planned on just being a slacker for the rest of my life, duh. (Chili then looks up like he just got a idea…) Chili: I know what I can do, I can become an alcholic wrestler, just like you! (Brock flicks his brother in the ear and laughs to himself. We watch as Brock pulls off the interstate….) Chili: Where you going man, this ain’t the way home? Brock: I gotta stop and get some beer and munchies, tonight’s video game night. Chili: Sweet! Are we going to get drunk tonight? (Brock frowns at Chilis remark…) Brock: What’s this we sh*t? Schoolboys don’t drink alcohol. You’re gonna be studying tonight young man! (Chili gets a dumbfounded look on his face…) Chili: What’s studying? (Brock looks at Chili with a equally dumbfounded look…) Brock: Have you been watching too many Chris Matthews promos or something, because that was one of the stupidest thing I think I’ve ever heard. Chili: F*ck you! (Chili turns and faces the window as Brock takes a swig of his beer, still shaking his head about what his little brother just said. Chili then turns back around with a smart look on his face…) Chili: But one thing schoolboys do is throwing underage drinking parties. (Chili gets a devilish look on his face, knowing that he got Brock there…) Brock: I never said you couldn’t throw beer parties. Chili: Sweet! Hey, maybe I can throw one if you win against Chris Matthews on Thursday. Brock: Dude, if I win against him on Thursday, I’m throwing the biggest party that this town has ever seen.(Brock and Chili highfive and share a laugh together. Chili starts playing with one of his eye piercing, he has a distant look in his eyes, and probably thinking about how big the party will be if Brock wins. Chili knows that if his big brother wins the town of Milwaukee will throw a big party in itself. The first time that the Blood Brothers won the tag team belts, small riots broke out in the streets from people, mostly drunk people celebrating their victory. Every tavern owner in this town knows that the best night for their business is a night that either Brock or Stephen are wrestling on TV…) Chili: I got confidence in ya bro, I mean, you do got some of me in ya. (Brock chuckles and scruffs his younger brothers hair…) Brock: Thanks bro, but you still can’t drink with me tonight! Chili: You suck! Brock: No, Tex Kelley does… (We zoom out as Brock pulls the large muscle car in front of “Wally’s Liquor Haven”. We slowly start to fade to black as Brock walks into the liquor store with a confident grin on his face…)
