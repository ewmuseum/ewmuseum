Barry Horowitz Discussions, and a Stewart Hix Cameo!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show the outside of the Florist Garden Apartment complex on a bright and sunny Milwaukee morning. Florist Garden, is the most upscale apartment complex in all of the Milwaukee area, many famous stars from around the area call the Florist Garden home, including The Blood Brothers, former 5-time PCW Tag Team Champions and all around local heroes. Despite it being bright and sunny, it is kinda chilly and a cool wind blows through the streets, not many people are out and about in town today. We can see a few small raindrops starting to fall down from the blue sky, signifying that sping has indeed not left Milwaukee. We even see the old bum that lives in a cardboard box in the alley next to the apartment building, whom usually is begging passerby’s on the street by now, is cuddled inside his small box chewing on a moldy piece of bread that he found in his pocket. We pan away from the old bum and up towards a window on the fourth floor of the large brick walled building. We then hear a loud, familiar, voice cut through the quiet air…) Voice: Damn you Sephiroth! DAMN YOU!!!!! (We start to slowly peer into the window, until we are inside of the room. We pan around to see that we are inside of Rush superstar, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd’s apartment or as he calls it, his “fully functional babe-lair”. We see Brock sitting in front of his wide screen television right in the middle of a vigorous game of the classic RPG Final Fantasy IIV on his Playstation. We see Brock is clad in a pair of plaid boxers and a white wifebeater a-shirt. His brown spiked up hair is messed up and sticking out all over the place. His eyes are bloodshot and a half-gone Marlboro cigarette hangs from his top lip. As he pounds on the buttons furiously, he takes drags of his cigarette, sips the bottle of Heineken that sits in front of him and eats the bowl of Fruity Pebbles cereal that sits in his lap. We watch as Brock dies at the hands of the games final boss Sephiroth, for the fifteenth straight time. Brock takes a drag of his cigarette and blows it out of his nose and just stares at the game over screen on the TV, not blinking, not moving. He slowly puts out his cigarette in the ashtray also sitting next to him, still not taking his eyes off the screen. He then slowly reaches over and unhooks the Playstation from the TV and gets up, gathering the Playstation with all it’s cords and controller in his arms. Brock, looks down at the Plyastation in his arms and smiles a devilish, almost twisted grin. He then walks over to the window and opens it with one arm. He breathes in the fresh air for a second, admiring the view of the city and then lets out a yell of anguish so loud that it seems the whole metropolitan of Milwaukee can hear it he then throws the Playstation with all its cords and controllers out the window. Brock lets out a sigh of relief as he does so…) Brock: F*ckin thing! (We then cut out to show infamous PTC star of yesteryear or should we say bust, Stewart Hix standing outside of the Florist Garden Apartment lighting up a joint as he asks people who walk by him on the sidewalk to sign a petition for him to get back into PTC since his latest jobber fed, has probably closed down. We see that the only people who have signed the petition so far are Hix himself and…that’s it, just Hix has signed. As Hix stands on the sidewalk smoking the joint, a Playstation controller falls in front of his feet, seemingly out of the sky. Hix looks at the joint he is smoking and shakes his head…) Hix: Woah…this is some potent sh*t! (As soon as Hix finishes his sentence, the rest of the Playstation falls conveniently enough right on top of his mangy haired head, knocking him out instantly. As Hix lies on the ground in a heap, face down, Playstation right next to him broke in half, the old bum quickly runs up to Hix and robs Hix of his wallet and his clothes. As the bum scurries back to his box and Hix lies on the sidewalk, naked and unconscious, we cut back inside to inside of Newbludd’s apartment. Brock is looking down at the fallen Hix and he grows a sinister grin for what he’s just done…) Brock: Sweet! (Brock shuts the window and turns around to see his best friend and former tag team partner, the now retired, “New Legend” Stephen Blood staring at him curiously. Stephen is clad in a pair of black Nike athletic shorts. He has no shirt on and a can of Heineken is in his hand…) Stephen: Um, Brock, where’d the Playstation go? (Brock shrugs his shoulders innocently…) Brock: Um…. (Stephen cocks a eyebrow at Brock suspiciously….) Stephen: Um what? Brock: Wha-what do you mean where’s the Playstation? Heh… Stephen: What we’re you looking at? Brock: Nothing! (Stephen walks by Brock and looks out the window and just shakes his head at the smashed up Playstation and equally smashed up Hix, he turns around and just stares at Brock who is giving Stephen a weak smile…) Stephen: Couldn’t beat Sephiroth again, huh? Brock: Uh huh… (Stephen just shrugs his shoulders and smiles at Brock, which Brock takes a surprise…) Stephen: Don’t worry bro, it took me like three times to do it. Brock: Aren’t you mad about the Playstation, I mean, it was yours. Stephen: Naw dude, I got like fifteen of them sitting in your room? Brock: You do? Why? Stephen: Yeah, doofus, don’t you remember, since we’re sponsored by Sony, we can have as many f*ckin Playstations as we want. And since I know how you get when you can’t beat a game I asked for a couple extra Playstations from em’. Brock: Cool! Then I can tell you about the four other Playstation’s I broke when I couldn’t get my memory card to work. Stephen: You dillhole! (Brock and Stephen walk over and sit down in front of the TV, Brock sitting in the black leather recliner and Stephen the black leather sofa. Stephen picks the remote for the TV off the oak expensive looking end table and turns on the TV. We are greeted with the local news on the screen…) Stephen: F*ck this! (Stephen goes to change the channel, but Brock stops him…) Stephen: You wanna watch the news!? Brock: Dude, look! (Brock points to the screen, and Stephen looks. On the screen it shows the young blonde news anchor smiling into the camera and above to the right of her head a small box reads ”Mohammed Nasir fired from Rush.” Brock and Stephen look at each other curiously and look at the TV again. The young anchor woman looks into the camera and speaks…) TV Anchor: And in other news, Rush Wrestling has fired one of it main superstars, Mohammed Nasir who was better known as “The Prophet” to his fans has been fired by the wrestling company. Nasir’s last match was at Rush first PPV, Ignition where he lost to Milwaukee native, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd in a bar room brawl. You can catch Rush every Thursday night on Primetime Central Television. This Thursday, Newbludd will face off against Ricky Starr in a no disqualification matchup… (Brock turns off the TV and grows a confident grin as he takes a drink of his beer. Brock rubs his scrubby unshaven face and lets out a laugh…) Brock:: Dude, I told you I would end Nasir’s career! I f*cking told you! (Stephen just shakes his head and chuckles….) Stephen: Yeah, yeah, you did. But, forget about that assclown, you gotta get ready for your match against Ricky Starr on Thursday? (Brock grows a frown and scratches his head…) Brock: Who the f*ck is Ricky Starr anyway? Is he like the Barry Horowitz of Rush? (Stephen shrugs his shoulders….) Stephen: Either way Barry Horowitz rocked! (Brock and Stephen highfive and both let out a laugh. Brock stands up and lets out a groan as he stretches…) Brock: Well, I’m going to go buy a twelve pack and go hit the gym, you coming bro? (Stephen lets out a defiant chuckle…) Stephen: Dude, I don’t wrestle any more, why would I work out? I can just sit here and play video games, duh! Brock: Very good point, I’ll be back in a couple of hours, you want Dominos or Pizza Hut? Stephen: Most definitely Dominos, bro. Pizza Hut blows ass. (Brock and Stephen bump fists and we fad out as Newbludd exits his apartment through the front door….)
