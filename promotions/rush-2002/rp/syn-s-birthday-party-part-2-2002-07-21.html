SyN's Birthday Party! (Part 2)

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 21st (OOC: Part three will be coming soon!) Just a second earlier at SyN’s party, D-J Calyx kicked Pretty Cool Guys off the stage and started doing some hip-hop music. Then a huge flame of fire shot up in the room. SyN promptly turned around and saw a table on fire. Right beside the table was a person with blood running down his chin. “Mordred!” SyN shouted. He didn’t know Mordred personally but he had seen him in NTICW. Mordred obviously didn’t listen for he threw some of his blood that was in a can at a candle on another table, letting it explode and flame up the whole table. SyN stepped forward to stop Mordred when all of a sudden he caught something in the corner of his eye. Beside the table that was on fire was another table where RUSH superstar, Chris Matthews sat. He was writing something on paper. SyN couldn’t help notice him and walked over him, politely saying. “What are you writing?” Chris Matthews sat up and looked at SyN with a smile. “Happy Birthday man.” “Thanks. So what are you writing?” “I’m plotting my attack on Deamont.” SyN looked at him with a grin. “You can’t forget about RUSH and your feud for one minute can you.” “Nope. Deamont will pay!” Chris Matthews looked down and continued writing. SyN was about to walk away when all of a sudden, the table that Matthews sat at, engulfed in huge flames, making Matthews pull his chair away from the table, his writing in the flames. “NO! Mordred!” Matthews saw Mordred and ran after him, shouting. “My plans against Deamont!” Matthews grabbed away the glass can of blood from Mordred and threw it to the ground, smashing it to the floor. After that, there was silence. Braking the silence, was a fight brewing in the corner of the hall. There was Aaron Cole, NTICW wrestler and Jason Marah. Aaron had a microwave to himself and around the microwave were Pizza Pops and Nachos. As the rest of the building lightened up again with chit-chat, SyN walked over to the corner. “Come on. Just share some!” SyN knew instantly that Jason wanted some food and Aaron would give it to him. SyN thought about braking up the fight but instead, he watched. “There mine. I can do whatever I want!” “Please!” “Fine, only two dollars a pizza pop and four dollars a nacho!” Jason looked at Aaron with disgust. “Four dollars for nachos?!” “No, not nachos. A nacho. ONE nacho.” “WHAT!” Jason looked like he would start a fight any minute but just then, Kidage, NTICW superstar, walks into the arguement. “Anybody call for money? Take it all.” With a flick of the wrist, Kidage throws a handful of money in the air. Jason Marah catches most of the money and smiles at Aaron Cole who looks in disgust that he might have to share his food. As Kidage throws money into the air, Paige Binsley, a free agent hops onto the table and in the middle of the money being thrown, dances around on the table, removing her shirt and bra and Aaron, Kidage, and Jason quiet down and start cheering, leaving SyN to walk away. SyN walks into the other corner where Semore Smoke, Jake, Adrian Voltage, Perfect Jay, and A.J. Trininad are having a party of there own. Semore Smokes, hoes are on the table, performing as each one of them has weed or booze or some kind of drugs in there hands. “Yeah man, SyN is back. This time you gotta have some weed man!” Semore Smoke shouts to SyN as he sees him walking over. “Ok, alright.” SyN decided that he would visit everybody else after. It was time to have some fun! Almost drunk after ten minutes. SyN is about to leave the group when all of a sudden, Hardcore Hoefel, with some drugs in hands, starts running across the room. “I got acid!!!” Some hoes and Semore Smoke walk over and Hardcore Hoefel asks Semore. “You want some acid?” “Well, lets see. I got a match in PCW coming up so no. What kinda drugs you got?” “Ecstasy. Beautiful ecstasy!” “SWEEEEEET!” Semore Smoke takes an ecstasy pill as the rest of the druggies in his group come over to try some as well. SyN walks away, smiling at them to meet other people when all of a sudden, the doors to the hall snaps open. All of a sudden, a midget in a car drives in, doing a wheel-e as he enters the hall. Behind him, come The Freak and Igor Castro of International Terror, long time friends of SyN’s from back in NTICW. They walk into the hall, pumped. “Whoo, sup everyone!” People all around the hall start cheering for the midget in the car as he does some spineroonie’s and pops some more wheel-e’s. People continue to enjoy themselves when all of a sudden, from on the stage comes Aimz onto the microphone as she pronounces. “We would now like to make a toast to SyN. So everybody come up to the stage to sing happy birthday.” SyN walks up to the stage like the others. As he reaches the stage, Aimz points at him to come up onto the stage as she gets down. SyN gets onto the stage, not really knowing what will happen. What will happen in the third part of SyN’s birthday party? Keep reading to find out!
