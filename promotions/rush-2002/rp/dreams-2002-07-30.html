Dreams

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30/02 G.O.D. had tasted gold before and he liked it. He like the responsibility. It brought him joy and a sense of pure power. It brought him everything he needed. It brought him respect. I brought him achievement. I brought him praise. It brought him Dr. Jenkins’ joy. And when Dr. Jenkins is happy, G.O.D. is happy. But there were obstacles. Three really. However they seemed pitiful and weak. G.O.D. defeated his first opponents to get to the title match with ease. It was like taking candy from a baby. Not that The Genetic Oriented Drone ate candy, that would be against his diet. However, it was very simple. The next two obstacles were the champion and another man. Rich Cannon has lost to Gene more than once… actually, more than twice. He certainly has not beaten G.O.D. once. However he does have help in the champion. The Network champion. It’s two men against one. Well, it will have to be. No one can expect either of them to take out G.O.D. one on one. The next to obstacles are a greater challenge as they are stronger and come on at once. The obstacles known as Scott Cage and Rich Cannon will see what it takes. They will see that they don’t have what it takes. And that is why now G.O.D rests… He has tired after long days and nights of fighting and working. Some people work in factories. Others are carpenters or mechanics. Some people are door-to-door salesmen. His job is to be the best and he’s very good at his job. G.O.D. is unstoppable. Just like his namesake. God is infallible. God is almighty. God is great. God is who God IS. But He is nowhere to be found. For hundreds of years, decades, centuries, millenniums, God is watching at best. He is not here. He has no say. He has no strategy. God is weaker than anyone can possibly imagine in today’s world. God is nowhere to be found. … But G.O.D. is right here… And so he sleeps and dreams and dreams and sleeps. The Genetic Oriented Drone has more work ahead of him and he will do his job. He will do it for G.O.D. is like his namesake in that he is unstoppable however his work of punishing those who oppose is more like G.O.D.’s wrathful angel of death, Loki. … And Gene Otto Droidman dreams. He dreams of Loki. He dreams of the ten plagues. He dreams of slaughtering and raining down sulfur. He dreams of the flaming sword. He dreams of all this. He just… dreams…
