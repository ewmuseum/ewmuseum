The Spanish Inquisition! (Part 1)

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10/02 The scene opens with Stimpo, that's me, sitting on my couch at my American sized home with a Latino sized heart. I hold a remote in my hand and sit there with an import beer from Mexico named: 'Latino Breeze.' The ingrediants on the back included old needles, orange juice, urine and even corn from old crap in Mexico. Seemingly to follow in the footsteps of my idol, the Spiffy!, I had to do everything Mexican in my eyes. One thing that I couldn't give up was the pigskin sport in American they call Football. I thought I was doing the Mexican thing in watching football, but I didn't know futbol was spelled differetnly. I sat there and flicked the television on and started watching the game on hand between the New York Jets and the Buffalo Bills. (OOC-I went to this game.) 'Futbol es muy bueno para Stimpo!' I told myself impressing even me speaking in my spanish. 'Now...Spiffy! said to watch more Mexican things and experience the Mexican energy then I will be worthy of being taught by the Spiff! But, I'm not learning anything by watching this...Futbol.' Never the less, I sipped the deadly liquid and spit it back out and watched the opening kickoff and was awaiting some type of latino sensation to occur..... Nothing seemed to happen except for the Bills' special teams to do what they do best.... SCREW UP! 'YAY! PEOPLE CHEERING! PEOPLE CHEERING! This MUST be what Spiffy! was talking about!' The fans of the NYJ had been cheering for the man named Chad Morton wearing a number 20 Jets jersey proudly. I stood up still cheering then sat back down quickly seeing I was not being Latinish. I heard a voice over the speaker phone from the TV and listened in. 'Yes folks, the J-E-T-S Jets Jets Jets have done it again with the quick feet of Chad Morton riding the Bills all day and now twice here returning it for a touchdown in overtime yes, the Jets have won this game!' cheers grow stronger for the jets as the announcer comes back on the microphone. 'For statistics and some meetings on the post game show here on ABC at 7:30 pm. All are welcome!' I took a look at a clock hanging above the television and saw it was 4:00 pm, american time. 'Seven thirty? I could make that...I must do it, Spiffy! would love for me to spread Spiffy!ness everywhere! I will also spread the latino styles to everyone who is worthy...American sports show...here Stimpo comes!' I told myself chugging my mexican death substance and stood up with my mask on and my casual wear of...my wrestling attire. I threw the can down and turned the TV off and went out of the door. The camera stayed on the view of the door for a while. I finally came back in my room and grabbed my keys and went back out...locking the door this time. Music played in the background of a latino hit not named but had a beat as I walked, almost strutting to a sleak car in front of me. I reached the car with the cool music and put in a key. BEEP!! BEEP!! BEEP!! An alarm sounded as I ran away from a car that I thought was mine. I continued running away from the car until I bumped into another car but this time, it did not beep. I tried my key in and turned the lock and it automatically unlocked. 'I didn't know I had a buggy...' I looked around confused. I shrugged as I got into the car and started the little Mexican buggy's ignition. Instantly, the radio turned on with high blast on playing 'La Cucaracha' out of the the red bug with a sombrero as the sunroof covered in Mexican leather (skin from a Mexican cactus, that's right, a cactus.) The front of the car has a stashed, imprinted figure of a cactus waving wherever the car goes. I slickly put my hands on the wheel and press the gas as the Mexican Buggy screeches out of the parking lot with the a bumper sticker across his license plate reading 'if ya see this car a rockin, dont come a knockin' and another sticker above the first one reading: 'latin love lasts longer' The car drove on down the american highway until this happened............ ******Scene Change - ESPN New York HQ****** 'Prepare to feel the RUSH of Latinish! Man, I swear I come up with the lamest catchphrses!' I sighed as I parked the latino mobile and took myself and my brain, or the rest of it from the ride up to ESPN to be sure to comment on the game that I was watching just a few short hours ago. I walked all the way up some 36 flights of stairs until I arrived at the recording stage where the desk for ESPN newscastors talk about things formerly known as sports. 'Yes, hello and welcome to Sportscenter. Some things are on the sports menu for today and one includes the Bill's special teams.' Said the man they call Stewart Scott. 'That's right Stewart, let's hope we get some good comments from our guest, if we could bring him out right now...yea, that'd be great.' Said the statistic genius, Rich Eisin. I looked around as someone on the camera crew pushed me out into the camera view as I casually took a seat in a black chair under the neon designs of the scenery in Sportscenter. Stewart and Rich look at me as they seem to have never seen someone as high as me in their lives. 'Don't worry boys, autographs are later, I'm here to give the advice of the latinoes.' I said casually. 'Right...Well, what were your thoughts of the game, if you even watched it.' Stewart questiones me. 'Easy, The Buffalo Bills didn't have the aura.' 'The Aura?' Rich asked butting in. 'The aura of the Latinoes! If the Bills had the Latino Aura today...they would've easily won that game before overtime.' I said straightening my outfit of...my wrestling tights to fit my approval. 'Right...The LATINOES to everything right?' Sterwart said to Rich as they both broke out laughing at me. 'SILENCE AMERICAN SCUM!' I stood up appauled as I continued. 'YOU'LL SEE! YOU'LL ALL SEE WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU MESS WITH THE LATINOES! BE PREPARED FOR THE SPANISH INQUISITION!!!' I stormed out of the room in a rage leaving Stewart and Rich left dumbfounded and confused and at the same time...more intelligent than ever before. TO BE CONTINUED.....DUN DUN DUNNNNNNNN
