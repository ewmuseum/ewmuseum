Date: Shottily at best The scene fades in to a late-night New York City skyline. It quickly pans down to the lights and the traffic of the citizens below. And just as quickly, as the opening chords of a very familiar song start up, it pans back to the skyline, and one building in particular. I am so high, I can hear heavenI am so high, I can hear heavenWhoa, but heaven...no, heaven don't hear me As the music climaxes, a familiar figure steps onto the crest of the skyscraper. With one foot planted mightily in front of him, and his hands perched on his sides, he oversees the city below, a cape behind him flapping in the breeze. And they sayThat a hero could save usI'm not gonna stand here and waitI'll hold onto the wings of the eaglesWatch as we all fly away The camera pans in to the face of our protagonist, only to find it hidden by a mask. Actually, two masks. A luchadore wrestling mask, and then ANOTHER one, a la Robin or the Green Lantern, on top of that. “The world needs the hero. The world needs the good guys. The world needs no more the doer-evils!” “Evil doers.” shouts a voice behind the ‘hero.’ “EVIL DOERS!” ..A world full of killingAnd blood spillingThat world never came… ”The Solution, I sol-ved the Solution. The Player Company? I pla-yed them. I keep pieces.” “Keep the peace.” “Keep the piece!” As the camera stays on the masked crusader, it swings and pans to capture the rest of the roof. About 20 feet behind, close to the entrance door to the roof, stand Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez O’Neill, the Spanish Announce Team. Both look rather annoyed and keep their arms crossed in front of them. The camera shot switches to behind the two of them, picking up their voices as they watch the vigilante. “What the hell is he doing?” “It looks like he’s cutting a promo.” “Since when did he get put in charge of writing promos?” “Since we stopped paying attention to him.” “OK, fine…but what’s with the cape?” “It’s actually a bed sheet from his hotel room. I saw him earlier, ‘saving the universe’ at the candy machine.” “Christ…” “You’re telling me.” Suddenly irritated by the voices behind him, the hero spins around and jumps down from his ledge, finally in enough light to be recognized as El Spiffy!. The music stops abruptly, and a confused Bill Gates stands up from the darkness, holding a boombox on ‘pause.’ El Spiffy! flails madly and stomps over to the commentators before tripping over his own cape and falling to the ground. He bounces back up and dusts himself off quickly, then points a finger into the faces of the announcers. “Have you a problem with the Spiffy!” “Oh, of course not.” With that comment, El Spiffy! levels Juan Carlos with an open-handed slap. “Don’t sass the Spiffy!” Juan Carlos lunges for him, but Julio holds his peer back. “Just calm down, man. Don’t let him get to you…” “Yeah, don’t let the Spiffy! get to you!” Juan Carlos lunges again, but is held back again. He snarls through gritted teeth at the ‘luchadore.’ “What are you trying to pull here anyway? The hero angle? It’s been done, pal. Try watching PCW sometimes!” El Spiffy! gives a smug look and turns, running back to his ledge. Julio takes the responsibility of explaining the situation to his partner. “Uh, buddy, RAH dropped the hero gimmick.” “He did WHAT?” “He’s Rent-A-Villain now.” “Holy shit…” “I know, I know. It was a bit of a shock. Well, to everyone but Spiffy!. He was first in line to claim the abandoned angle.” “Geez…” “I wouldn’t worry too much. The guy’s got the attention span of an eight-year-old. And when he and his playpal Gates get tired of playing this game, they’ll just move on to another one…” “Unbelievable.” “I guess so.” The camera pans past them and back to the front of the building, where El Spiffy! continues his gargoyle-esque posing and Bill Gates hits the play button on his stereo. And they're watching usThey're watching usAnd they're watching usThey're watching usAnd they're watching usThey're watching usAs we all fly away The camera pans past El Spiffy! and into the skyline as we fade to black…
