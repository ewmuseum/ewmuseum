When You Point Your Finger...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2 days late “This is the shit of bulls!” “Bullshit.” “This is the bullshit!” He never gets that right. Anyway, here we are again, back in a hotel room. This time it’s in , the site of AD12. A very, very important AD13. But the people in the room don’t know that. For it’s just a few days after AD12, and El Spiffy!, Joe Bananas, and Livewire are still dwelling on the aftermath of that show, much less the one coming up. As the scene fades in, El Spiffy! stalks angrily across the room, pacing back and forth. On the twin beds, Livewire and Bananas sit quietly, only their eyes following the stalking luchadore. At a side table, slouched low with a slight smirk on his face, is Juan Carlos Smith. His broadcast partner, Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil, left long ago to get an update on what’s in store for this week’s show. And of course, seated Indian-style on the floor, is the android known as RoboGates, intently watching C-Span. El Spiffy! pauses to step over it each time he paces through the room. “The Spiffy! was given the what-for twice by Player’s Ink! Two times! Dos! How can the Spiffy! be the major player in RUSH when he’s on the floor getting stomped?” No one responds. El Spiffy! continues. “The group cannot keep their members from being beaten, what is it for good?” “Good for,” corrects Juan Carlos. “What is it good for?” Still no response. El Spiffy! keeps pacing. “How can the Spiffy! beat the Quota if he-“ Getting a little too worked up, El Spiffy! forgets to step over the seated RoboGates, He catches his foot in the metal armpit of the humanoid and pitches face first into the hotel room’s shag carpeting. Juan Carlos tries to hold back a guffaw. Livewire and Joe rise from their beds to try and help their fallen peer, but El Spiffy! quickly jumps back to his feet. On the floor, RoboGates turns its head slightly and brushes some lint off of its shoulder. El Spiffy! hauls off and kicks it…then drops to the ground again, holding his left foot in excruciating pain. “Were you saying something, Spiffster?” Juan Carlos chuckles as the luchadore makes it back to his feet. Still wincing and rubbing his injured tootsies, he continues. “What…the Spiffy!…was saying…is that…there…is only…one person…can be blamed….” “Oh really?” Juan Carlos scoffs back, thinking that his charge will finally ‘fess up to his own mistakes. On the bed, Livewire lowers his head, assuming he’s the culprit. But the next outburst from El Spiffy! surprises them both. “Jack Cantelope!” El Spiffy! points a shaky finger at Joe Bananas, who responds with nothing buy confusion. “Wat? Watt are ye talkin’ ‘bout? I wun me match an Addreenahline!” “You were supposed to tell the Spiffy! how to beat the black people! “Leesen har, ya-“ “You fail-ed El Spiffy!” “I ain’t playen yer raysist gayme-“ “El Spiffy! was beaten by the black people twice because of Jerry Honeydew!” “Dis be a joke, rite?” El Spiffy! lunges at Joe, tackling him back onto the bed. Livewire shakes his head before getting up to break them apart. Juan Carlos Smith just looks on with a smile. RoboGates….watches C-Span. “This is not even your real hair!” El Spiffy! shouts, yanking away at the dreadlocks on Bananas’ head. “It is wig!” “Geet offa mee, ya filty pickney!” “Spiffy!, please. You’re being ridiculous. Of course that’s his real hair.” El Spiffy! pauses for a moment to reflect on Livewire’s words. He gives another tug of the strand of dreads wrapped around his hand, prompting a yelp from Joe. Then, reluctantly, he releases the hair. “El Spiffy! sees your point….but this isn’t his real skin!” He lunges again, this time rubbing the skin on Joe’s face violently. “Geet offa mee!” “What is this, makeup?” El Spiffy! spits onto his hand and scrubs away at the Jamaican’s face, causing him to stay yelling even more. “Aye! Ah didn’t sayn up for dis! Geet heem awaye!” Livewire finally succeeds in yanking the Spiffster off of Bananas, ripping him off the bed and through the air. He slams into the wall and crumples to a heap on the floor. Joe sits back up, wiping the excess saliva off of his face with his shirt. Livewire surveys the damage and looks at Juan Carlos, who shrugs in return. “Don’t look at me. You guys agreed to be his partners.” Everyone’s attention is diverted once again when El Spiffy! kips up from the floor, with a rabid look in his eye. “OK, Jesus Apricot, you’re complex disguise will do for now, but the Spiffy! is onto you, white boy!” “Heepocritt!” El Spiffy! dives again but is caught in the air by Livewire, who does his best to restrain the luchadore. The Spiff Star flails wildly at the air, convincing Joe to back up to the head of the bed and look nervously at the Spanish commentator. “Not a problem, Joe. He’ll forget it all by morning. Hell, he could forget it within the hour if you buy him a Coke.” “Ees dis shite gonna happen evry time?” “I won’t say yes, and I won’t say no, but….yes.” “Well watt in da-“ He’s cut off by the sound of the hotel room door opening. All eyes turn to the entryway, where a visibly happy Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil stands, a freshly printed copy of the AD13 card in his hand. “You’re not going to believe this…”
