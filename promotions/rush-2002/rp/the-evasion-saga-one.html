Date: A Long Assed Time Ago... The Evasion SagaChapter OneTaverns and Sirens There I sat, the stench of unclean beer taps working my gag reflexes like a bulimic after a face full of cake, a small glass held tightly in my right hand, my heart aching from the latest swig of whiskey that managed it’s way down my esophagus. The clack of ball on ball echoed through my ears, as two gents stood in front of a billiard table, conversing about the game that was on last night. I smirked, my golden eyes gleaming under the lids. The tender poured another glass of Jameson’s and slid it in front of me, motioning with his hand about ‘on the house’ or something to that effect. Whatever. The sounds of sirens filled the tavern, overwhelming the soft sounds of chatter and Gaelic music. The police. After two months of not bothering me, the pigs were finally ready to act. This was my life, though… the life of a vagabond, the life of a highlander, the life of a rogue, the life of a killer. I smirked, and shook my head, the red and blue lights flashing across my eyes, nearly blinding me. The sounds of choppers mixing with the screaming pitches initiated the madness. I could hear guns being loaded and cocked, doors slamming, people shouting… chaos. I loved it. The televisions in the joint flashed black, then glowed, tickers running across the bottom of the screen. “Murderer found.” Found my ass. Since when were the police competent? I laughed and wiped the amber fluid from my lips and nodded at the tender, dropping a fifty on the counter. I headed for the coat racks. Pulling off my leathers, I draped the coat across my shoulders casually – the claymore concealed within the folds of the fabric. I nodded at the patrons, bidding my adieu, and opened the front door. The clicks of their chambers echoed through my skull. With a shotgun pointed at my face, I smiled and politely said, “Fuck.” With lightning quick reflexes, I reached out and grabbed hold of the barrel of the pump-action. Pointing it away from my body, I aimed it towards the frame of the building. An explosion echoed from the chamber and sent shrapnel into the wall. With vibrations running up and down my spine, I tore the weapon out of the man’s hands and clubbed him in the neck with it before tossing it aside and returning to the safety of the pub. Nodding at the gaping people, I entered a corner of the room and vanished into the shadows. *** “That fuckin’ Mick,” shouted Agent Morales, chewing on the end of a cigar, the black tar gluing his teeth together. The overweight, Spanish police chief lifted a walkie-talkie to his mouth and shouted some orders to the choppers above, which began to circle the areas surrounding the tavern. His tie flapped in the wind created by the propellers of the floating war machines. He lifted a pistol out of a leather holster strapped to his shoulder. “Get schome fuckin’ men around disch buildin’. I ain’t wantin’ disch prick outta dat baw. Pronto, bitsches. Pronto!” he swore aloud, grabbing his men by their collars and pushing them towards the door. They shivered under the winter night and surrounded the front entrance. 2…1… with a kick, the door flung off it’s hinges and man after man poured into the building. Expecting a sword through his chest, Pristino’s teeth chattered together, his weapon shaking like a rookie surgeon about to make the first incision. Flashlights flickered on and glowed in every corner of the room, only cobwebs and spiders hissing at the interruption. The patrons looked on, their mugs and bottles tipping out of their hands, as their normal routine was fucked with. Who was that kind guy? What’d they want with him? The hell is going on? Morales entered the room, his face burning in anger. How? There was only one way in and one way out of that building. He roared and aimed his handgun at a bottle on the bar and shot the neck clean off the rest, swearing at the top of his lungs. One by one, the officers filed out of the building, the man still out on the loose and considered highly dangerous. *** I stood there, in the woods, a cigarette burning between his lips and his eyes narrowed. ‘Fucking mortals,’ I thought to myself and wandered through the tangles, the sounds of justice echoing miles away. …to be continued…
