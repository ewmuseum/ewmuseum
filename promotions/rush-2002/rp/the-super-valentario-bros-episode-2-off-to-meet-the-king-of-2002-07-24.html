The Super Valentario Bros. - Episode 2 - Off to Meet the King of this Crunk Kingdom!

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24, 2002 When we last left our trio of heroes - Tyler and Waldo Valentario, and Marsh Mallowbludd - they were in quite the predicament, indeed! Not only had both Waldo AND Mallowbludd's car broken down, the heinous Scott Taylorio's henchmen committed a vile act of larceny and stole Marsh's Golden NOS Injector of Happiness! Without a mode of transportation, the unsuspecting heroes have no possible way of heading to the outcast Taylorio Island and tracking down the good brothers' evil counterpart. Their only hope? Track down the majestic King Crunkstool, and seek his aid in finding transportation. Without further adieu... Tyler Valentario: Okay...the map says we should be just about five minutes away...any second now, we should see Crunkstool Castle come into view. Tyler marches on enthusiastically, seeking their destination. Waldo, his brother, walks just behind him, lowering his shoulder with each step. However, at least twenty feet behind them, Marsh Mallowbludd sulks along, tired out - seeing as he is usually used to travelling in his 1969 Mustang with the increased speed of Nitrous Oxide. Marsh Mallowbludd: Guys! *puff puff* Wait up! *puff puff* I'm out of breath... Tyler and his brother stop, letting him catch up and rest. Waldo turns to him, irritated as usual. Waldo Valentario: (expletive), dawg, how y'all (expletive)in' tired out already, mother-(expletive)ing puffball... Tyler: Now, now...Waldo, be nice. It's not his fault... Waldo: (expletive)ing (expletive)... Tyler: Wait, look! There it is! It's Crunkstool Castle! The camera pans forward a bit, past the travelling trio of uh...some word for good guys that begins with T, I'm bad with alliteration. What we see is a grand castle, filled with spectacular neon lights, a rainbow of fireworks detonating in the midnight sky, glorious cerulean and rouge banners hanging down from the white marble towers... Oh wait. I was reading the brochure for the Excalibur Hotel in Las Vegas. Damn, that place looks nice! Anyhoo, what we REALLY see is one CRUNK-ASS castle. This thing is fuckin' shining with diamonds, and, even from this distance, we can tell that the entire structure is made out of platinum, or at least some type of similar metal. Waldo: Awww snaps, dawg, this (expletive)in' castle is (expletive)in' shinin' all (expletive) over. Tyler: Uh, yeah...it's quite nice, I like the diamonds, it adds a nice touch. Waldo: Shut the (expletive) up, (expletive)...CRACKA! The entire land of Crunkstool Kingdom gasps in horror, except for of course Statuz Quo who would be laughing were he to appear in this promo. Jordan X Shabazz: Aww...no the FUCK you didn't. Booker T: Tell me you did not just say that...TELL ME...you did NOT just say that! JR: MY GOD! MY GOD! MY GOD! HE SAID IT! HE SAID IT! THE FORBIDDEN WORD! Tyler: You do realize that...that...you could go to Crunkstool jail for life if someone heard you say that... Waldo: (Expletive)! I don't give a (expletive)...SCREW THE RULES! Jordan H Helmsley: Aww...no the FUCK-ugh...you-ugh...DIDN'T-ugh... Man, what a lame humorous attempt at filling up RP space...anyway, back to our heroes! They are currently trekking off toward the prestigious Crunkstool Castle, less than two miles in the distance...and they walk... ...and walk some more... Tyler: JESUS CHRIST, JUST FAST FORWARD TO THE FLIPPIN' CASTLE!!! *ZOOOOOOOOOOOOMY!* Approximately fifteen minutes later, our heroic triumvirate finds itself standing in the midst of a majestic throne room. Beautiful diamond-encrusted chandeliers hang from the ceiling, a fly shag carpet covers the floor, and quality rap and R&B blares from speakers in all corners of the room. On the other end of the room, sitting in a quite expensive black leather recliner is the benevolent leader of this world - King Dante Crunkstool II. King Dante Crunkstool II: Hey, my citizens, you got a prob? King Dante here can help ya with any job! If you need a car, a van, or any kinda ride, Crunkstool gon' be able to hook you up with tha glide. King Crunkstool is quite the accomplished freestylist, indeed. Tyler: Um, yeah. Anyway, you see, poor Marsh Mallowbludd here got his Golden NOS Injector of Happiness stolen by that vile Scott Taylorio's henchmen! We need some kind of transportation across the Sunderian Sea... King Dante Crunkstool II: The Sunderian Sea you say, that ain't a big deal, King Dante can get you just about ANY wheels. Tyler: Well, I was thinking that we need some type of boat... King Dante Crunkstool II: A boat you say, a boat, a boat? Somethin' to keep you afloat while you cross dat big moat and you buy a new goat? Mallowbludd: Um...King, I don't mean to be disrespectful or anything, but that didn't even make sense. King Dante Crunkstool II: Aw come on little guy, it rhymed, did it not? Don't be such a rude little marshmallow snot. Look, I can hook you up with some fly jet skis, but you just gotta promise to cooperate with me. Go to the back where you'll find a cute shawdy, she'll lead you to the boats, down stairs...about forty. When you get there you'll find three Kawasakis, but be careful - the Sunderian Sea is really damn rocky. Tyler: Okay! Thanks! We'll come back when we've got the Injector. Waldo: Holla. King Dante Crunkstool II: Why thank you, kind Waldo, I will holla back...but as a final reminder guys - don't get off track. Mallowbludd: We won't. Thanks King! Our good guys head back behind his throne, opening a secret door in the marble wall by smashing a rotating Backstreet Boys CD. They encounter a beautiful lady in the back, with one fiiiiiiine booty...Waldo wants to hit on her, but remembers what the King said - don't get off track. He settles for watching that thang bounce up and down as they descend the stairs to find three color-coded QUALITY jet skis. Tyler: Holy shizni... Somewhere, Rent-A-Villain sends a telepathic message somewhere along the lines of 'DON'T STEAL MY CATCHPHRASE!' Tyler: Holy cow! Tyler and Mallowbludd each hop on their cherry red and golden jet skis, respectively. Waldo takes his sweet time, checkin' out the girl before he leaves, but finally hops on to his lime green jet ski and starts it. Tyler: Alright guys, just follow me. I know the way across the Sunderian Sea perfectly - stick by me and you'll be safe. It's going to take a while to get there...but we will get Mallowbludd's Golden NOS Injector of Happiness back soon, I promise. Let's go! Tyler and Waldo Valentario, and Marsh Mallowbludd speed out of the basement docks and into the sunny Sunderian Sea, in search of Taylorio Island as a voice above them, high up in the sky, cackles... *dun dun DUNNNNNNN!* TO BE CONTINUED!
