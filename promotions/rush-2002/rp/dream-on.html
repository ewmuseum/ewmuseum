Date: today “The unexamined life is not worth living.”--Socrates Have you ever sat there, watching a match burn? Watching a lighter spout it's flame, or watching a bonfire go, listening to each little crackle that the burnt wood makes. With the match, watching it slowly burn down the little cardboard stick, giving you ample time and notice to blow it out. With the lighter, watching the base of the flame, the blue part, the hottest part as it leads everything else up in such a beautiful shape. With the bonfire, holding your hands out over it, keeping them warm and then falling into the undeniable urge to, yes you guessed it, prod the ashes with a skinny little stick. You may be thinking what does this have to do with wrestling? What does this have to do with anything, and if you are asking that... then you are a mental misfit. There are two reasons for this little appetizer, and I will tell you why... These are all things, all things that people, whether they know it or not, all stare at. These things all have something in common, with a wrestler who seems to be immortalized under the name of Scott Cage. People are amazed, dazzled even by the ability, the technique, the skill, the charisma and most definitely the staggering ability to make others look so... so... amateurish. There stare themselves blind, watching as he does things that people wouldn't dream of trying, or even dream of dreaming. They stare quite frankly, because they cannot look away. Is it chemical? Possibly. Is it Mechanical? Most likely not, but in some cases you never know. Is it Admiration? Most likely. Brock Newbludd, are you staring now? Most definitely. The second reason, is, while staring over the burning enigma, the spots just above, become cloudy, wavy even as the heat from the fire burns away into the air. You can't help but look through that, wondering why it does that, wondering why it leaves the fire so peacefully and quietly into the cold air, when it was perfectly safe, and warm in the raging fire. Wondering why it would leave everything it has been with for its entirety, wondering how it lives surrounding the main focus, the thing that everyone came to see. You watch it slowly burn away and then evaporate into nothing, into the exact same thing that everyone else sees...everyone else sees nothing. In case you haven't noticed, this is a perfect metaphor in itself. The fire, unstoppable in all aspects of itself...get that Brock? IN ALL FUCKING ASPECTS OF ITSELF! What it means is that the fire, the long burning one that no one person can keep there eyes off of is ME. And the lowly little exhaust coming from it, the little haze parts surrounding the fire, living off what it makes, what it creates, encompassing the main focus of everyone's eyes... is you, Brock, it’s you. Trying to prove yourself and get everyone's attention like I have. Trying not to evaporate into thin air like so many others do... (Our view fades into Miami...where a poker table is set up...)
