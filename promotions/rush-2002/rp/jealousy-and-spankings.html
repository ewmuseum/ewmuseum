Jealousy and Spankings...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show RUSH superstar “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd and ex-wrestler “The New Legend” Stephen Blood sitting in “The Blood Bullet”, Stephen’s 1971 blood red Dodge Charger as it careens down the highway, somewhere in suburban Milwaukee on a bright and sunny early July morning. The sun hangs lowly in the east, signifying that it is still quite early in the morning. The car’s interior is mint, leather bucket seats, nitrous oxide boosters that Stephen acquired in some illegal fashion, the steering wheel is a custom made chrome hard chain. The car has probably around 50,000 dollars invested into it. We watch as Brock maneuvers in and out of traffic with on hand as he puffs on a cigarette and sticks a CD into the cars pimped out stereo system. After a few seconds “Killing in the Name Of…” by Rage Against the Machine pumps through the speakers, the whole screen shakes from the utter power of the bass. Stephen nods his head approvingly as he reaches into the backseat and opens the large cooler that sits on it. He grabs two beers from it and tosses one on Brock’s lap. Brock looks at the beer and frowns, seeing that it’s a plain generic can of beer, a plain white can that just has “Beer” written on it in black letters…) Brock: Dude, what the hell is this sh*t!? (Stephen looks at Brock curiously…) Stephen: Beer, duh… Brock: Yeah, I realize this, but does this beer have a name? Stephen: Yeah, the brand name is “beer”… Brock: Dude, what the hell is this generic stuff doing in my hand!? Stephen: Aren’t you gonna drink it? (Stephen cracks open his can of beer, and takes a swig, he grimaces as he swallows the alcohol down…) Stephen: Mmmmm…barley goodness! (Brock looks at the beer in his hand and rolls down his window and tosses the can of beer out the window, we watch as the can explodes upon impact and sprays all over the car behind them…) Stephen: Dude, what are you doing! Brock: I’m not drinking that sh*t! Where did you even find that stuff!? (Stephen sighs as he plays with the tab on the can…) Stephen: That generic brand was the only guys I could find to sponsor me, all the other beer companies said I’m too “violent” for their image, the assclowns…But, hey as long as I’m this brands sponser, I get as much of it I want! I got four kegs sitting in the living room right now ready for us to drink! (Brock just chuckles to himself as he takes a drag of his almost done cigarette. Stephen takes another swig of his beer and gags a little. He then reaches under his seat and pulls out the latest issue of “RUSH: The Magazine”. He flips through it before raising a eyebrow and smiling, he slugs Brock in the arm playfully, Brock looks over at Stephen…) Brock: Wha…? Stephen: Dude, you got a number one contenders match on Thursday! Fuckin’A! Brock: Dude, I already knew that, dinkus. (Stephen reads some more before looking back up…) Stephen: Really? Well, you’re facing just a bunch of piss lapping pissclams. Brock: Yeah, yeah…Although these guys are pretty f*ckin tough, Matthews did beat me once, albeit cheaply but he did beat me. Stephen: Does it really matter? Brock: Not really, but still… (Brock finishes his smoke and tosses it out the window…) Stephen: Hmmm…well I’d watch out for Valentine, he is a sorta a wildcard and you know… (Brock just rolls his eyes…) Brock: Pfft! Valentine’s just a jealous prick, jealous that he can’t be the top dawg in Milwaukee. Stephen: Well, at any rate watch out for him. What about the last guy, D-Kay Calyx? Brock: Who? Stephen: You know, the dud who ran into the match last week, punked out Valentine at the end. (Brock makes the jacking off motion with his hand…) Brock: I really don’t have a problem with the guy, since quite frankly, he did a damn good job of taking out that pretty boy Valentine, but I could have done a way better job than that. I mean, Calyx gave him a spanking after I got done giving him a beating. (Stephen takes a drink of his beer and wipes his mouth with the back of his hand…) Stephen: Well, bro, I think I’m sitting next to the next Rush #1 contender. (Brock lets out a cocky grin…) Brock: I know, and that my friend is Sad But True! (Brock and Stephen bump fists as we fade to black…)
