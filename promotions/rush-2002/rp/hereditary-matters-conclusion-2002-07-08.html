Hereditary Matters {conclusion}

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/08/02 Albert Townsend stands alone in his 13th floor office, looking out at the urban scenery through a large window. His slightly wavy blonde hair parted in the middle, his eyes a deep, handsome blue but with a small, almost pointed jaw-line that contrasted with his Nordic features. He also lacked the Nordic height being around 5’7 with his built being at best, somewhat “lanky”. However, his face spoke arrogance and pride showed in the way he carries himself. For a moment, he felt like some sort of king, king of the world… King of the Enterprise Fuck anybody else around him, fuck his manager, fuck the old crones up in the board, and fuck Starr… He stood alone with deep pockets and a deeper well to dig from. Being a senior accounted in Starr Enterprise was no small matter despite what other people said about him. Arrogant? Fuck, he had every right to be that. Am I a punk? Do I look like a punk to all of you half-assed fucks? Those were the thoughts that go through his head on a daily basis each time he faced a client, a subordinate, a superior or even a board member. He maintained that he would have had a 14th floor office a long time ago had it not been for those old senile crones who didn’t know real professionalism if it fucked them in the ass. At least he got his “compensations”. Pocketing a few from here, a handful from there, always remembering to clean and cover it all up at the end of the day. Fuck, that’s what they paid him to do right? Handle their figures. A criminal? No, he thought that wasn’t broad enough to cover him. He always thought of it as, “relieving excess baggage”. Not like they really need that much dough anyway. Hell, he wasn’t surprise if all they did with it was stash it and lock it somewhere to gather dust. Albert wasn’t one to see good money going to waste. At least if he retired with the same goddamn job, he was going to retire with a fat wallet. Sometimes he wished they could find out. To see the expression on their face when they found out exactly how much they got screwed over in the past decade or so. Especially that Starr kid, the spoiled son-of-a-bitch, did jack shit and got the company to his name. Josh was a fuckwit of a big boss to start out with anyway. It was all going his way and he loved every minute of it. For ten years it goes like that and he was damn sure that it would keep on rolling that way. It was the start of a very unfortunate morning. Ricky Starr burst into the lobby with the exact same roguish confidence he had yesterday, accompanied by the man in lime sunglasses, except today he was attired in a white shirt with the collars turned up and buttoned to the third hole from the top, the rest revealing a simple silver chain and a part of a well-built pectoral region. His pants were jeans, extremely casual and contrasting with the rest of the world around him in three-piece suits and business wear. Ricky at least had the decency to put on a black coat on top of his red-collared shirt, buttoned up to the second hole from the top. His shirt was un-tucked, giving the impression that he was a rogue in the corporate world. Hugo Boss pants, Clark leather shoes, the smell of CK One following him everywhere he went and the attitude he carried just topped it all off for the CEO of Starr Enterprises. The duo head towards the reception where a strawberry blonde receptionist greets them. “Good morning Mr. Starr, what can I do for you?” Ricky greets the receptionist back with his handsome roguish smile “I would like to know where Albert Townsend’s office is” The receptionist, trying to restrain herself from overreacting, manages to come up with a short and calm reply “13th floor, second door to your left sir” “Thank you very much” Ricky Starr flashes his pearly whites once more before moving on with his companion towards the elevators. He presses the up button and waits. The man following him turns to face him and speaks. “So we got a case against him..” Ricky nods his head “The old man put a lot of tabs on detail. He jotted down every single cent of enterprise money that this prick spent. He even got the illicit transactions on a separate ledger” Ricky produced a rectangle shaped red book and opened it to reveal neat columns and rows of figures, words and dates. His companion moves in for a closer look and whistles lowly. “Can’t say the punk is gonna’ slip away from this ‘un” A shrill sound resonates from above them, indicating that an elevator has just reached the lobby. A pair of doors moves open slowly and from it emerged a few men and women in crisp business suits toting folders, briefcases and whatnot. Ricky greets each of them with a smile and a nod with mutters of “Good morning” in between. The elevator doors soon shut, leaving the duo in there alone. Their conversation continues as Ricky straightens his jacket and collar. “I knew old man Josh long enough to know that he could never fight a dirty, hot, messy open war well. He always did things in the shadow, making sure his moves were as inconspicuous as possible, using others to strike the physical blows for him, taking risks that were calculated and evaluated over and over again. In short, Josh was an intellectual, but too much of an intellectual to use his own hand when he really needed to…” The man beside him nods in a gesture of understanding. “I see your point bro, but at least he made a fat bundle while he was at it” Ricky sighed and continued talking “But he knew that there were wars that he needed to fight himself, wars and problems that can only be solved with his own hand” His companion stays solemnly silent as Ricky looks at the red ledger book under his shoulder and a little black book in hand “That’s why he wanted me to find it” As the elevator doors open, both men walk out onto the corridor. Ricky manages a few last words “Because at the end of the day, no matter how you look at it…” They reached their intended door, just like the receptionist told them it would be “We were never the same inside” Albert Townsend received an intercom from his secretary, notifying him that the CEO of Starr Enterprise wants a personal audience with him. Albert gave his approval. To him, it was just another dickwad to deal with To him, that was just one more ass to kiss. To him, it was yet another suit-wearing jackass to pacify To him, it was all just a dance, a masquerade to attend, and a charade to perform. To him, it would be all over in a couple of minutes the most. Never was he more wrong in his entire life. KER-PLANG! The sound of broken glass is heard throughout most of the thirteenth floor. Employees on the floor rush to the scene, all wanting a view of what just happened. Some looked in shock, some turned to the shoulders of more calmer and stronger colleagues, some even lost control and threw up on the spot. A bruised, broken and bloodied man lays crumpled on the corridor floor. Blood is dripping out from mostly his forehead and eye region. It seems that almost every part of him that could be busted open was busted already. Those who did it were pretty professional at it, although lacking the smoothness of a pro-hitman, it certainly had the qualities of a seasoned boxer or a trained professional in the fighting arts. This attack was not meant to cripple nor kill but it was meant to bloody and wear down the intended victim. Blood soon smears over the hard, tiled corridor floor as the victim inched himself slowly and painfully. His blonde hair has been matted in with the colors of crimson red, an ugly crest to an even uglier person. As he pulled himself up using the water cooler as leverage, he turns to face a shocked staff body. Albert Townsend, head accountant of Starr Enterprise, was attacked and put through a glass sheet that acted as part of the divider between his office and the corridor. Despite his miserable state, nobody seemed to be landing him a helping hand. In fact, some were even shunning away from him, as if confronted with an abomination. Albert felt his nose, it was broken and he was bleeding from it like a tap. He leaned against the water cooler before getting up on his feet. It took more than one attempt but he soon got the hand of standing upright when one’s ribs felt like caving in. Two men walk out onto the corridor. One was sporting a pair of lime-colored sunglasses and a white shirt with a pair of jeans, carpenter cut to be precise. On his face was a huge grin and he was wiping his knuckles with some Kleenex. They seemed to be soiled with an all-too familiar substance of the moment, blood. The other was a man in a red shirt with the top two buttons undone with a black jacket on top of it, a pair of black Hugo Boss pants and Clark shoes to match. The fragrance of CK One was evident in the air as he entered the scene and took one look at the staggering figure of Albert Townsend. “Damn, what did I tell you about running into closed fists huh?” Albert could only manage one word through his bloodied, bruised mouth. “Bastards…” He spat out a tooth to add emphasis to that statement. “Right”, Ricky blurted out as his companion grabbed Albert by the arms in a position that immobilizes his upper body. He went quite limp in that position as he was brought face-to-face with the CEO of Starr Enterprise, Ricky Starr. The gathering simply watched on as events just happened in front of their eyes. “Just one last thing, compensation? That’s going to be a small problem compared to what you owe the Enterprise right now. Any last words?” Albert could only manage one, slurred word from his immobilized stance “ugh…C’mon…” He ran straight into another closed fist. Ricky Starr and his companion sporting the ever-present lime-lenses sunglasses walk down the steps of the local police precinct. They make their way down the sidewalk leisurely and slip into conversation. “How did we get outta’ that one?” Ricky smirks at his companion as he puts his hands into his jacket pockets. “Lets just say that Josh has a lot more friends than you think” His companion looks up with a smile on his face, a wide-smile you would associate with pro-hustlers and high-rolling members of the underworld. “Ah…the American justice system. I love it even more every day bro” A pack of Malboro Lights emerges from Ricky’s pocket. The flip-top is flipped open and a cigarette pulled out using his lips. He lights it with a silver Zippo lighter with the engraving of his namesake on it and inhales deeply. As he blows out a cloud of smoke, he looks at the gleaming, towering, imposing Starr Enterprise HQ building. Ricky’s roguish smile returns to his face yet again. “We’re back in business”
