Date: CAUGHT! “Where the hell have you been?” It’s early morning. Just outside Philadelphia. In the hotel room of one Christopher Shackelton, the Shack Pack is having one of their first meetings since the aftermath of Ignition. Well, most of them are having the meeting. One member, El Spiffy!, has just mindlessly stumbled in the door. And that, friends, is where we join them. “El Spiffy! was checkin-“ “I don’t mean right now!” Juan Carlos Smith continues. “I mean for the past few days! You’ve been flying in, then flying right back out to Los Angeles! What the hell is going on?” “I, uh, the Spiffy...that is...” “Unbelievable.” Juan Carlos throws his hands up in the air and collapses on one of the beds, sending Julio Diaz Perez O’Neill bouncing a little. In chairs across the room, Shackelton and Livewire, in their street clothes, gaze inquisitively at their tights-clad stablemate, then shoot each other a look. “I’m telling you,” Julio interjects, “he’s in that damn Wrestling God tournament.” The ‘luchadore’ is not pleased: “What! That is the shit of a bull! El Spiffy! is not even on the list!” “I don’t mean as Spiffy!, puta. I meant as whatever you like to call yourself when you ain’t wearing that mask.” “HIPPOPOSTEROUS!” “Preposterous.” “PREPOSTEROUS!” Shackelton slowly rubs his temples as he leans forward in the chair. “Spiffy!,” he strains, “what have you been doing in L.A.?” “El Spiffy! had the business to take care of.” “Horse sh*t.” “You’re earning money on the side! You’re taking independent bookings!” “No!” “If you win that tournament and leave us high and dry, I swear to God....” “El Spiffy! is in no tournament! El Spiffy! is in a cent!” “Innocent.” “El Spiffy! is innocent!” Shackelton jumps up from his chair, silencing the bickering. As he sits back down he gives another look to Livewire, who shrugs in confusion. “OK, ok,” Shack assures, “we need to work this out. Spiffy!, do you have something going on in L.A. that you need to tell us about?” El Spiffy! hangs his head sheepishly. “Yes.” “Well, why don’t you go ahead and let us in on the deal?” Defeated, the luchadore slumps his shoulders and turns back to his bag. As he turns around, he holds up a T-shirt with four words emblazoned across the front: BILL GATES’ BURRITO SHACK The other four men take a look at the shirt, then burst out laughing. “Burrito Shack?” “Bill Gates?” “What the hell is this?” As the laughter and tears subside, El Spiffy! chokes an answer: “Is new franchise. El Spiffy! was asked to be the, the, the...speaksman.” “Spokesman.” “Spokesman.” “Why do they want you as a spokesman for some Mexican restaurant?” “Well, it is a white guy pretending to know Mexican food. Who else would you get?” “Good point.” “El Spiffy! is Mexican!” “Yeah, right. And the meat in those Burritos are 100% beef.” “I-that is-but they-I just...” The group starts laughing again as El Spiffy! gets flustered. “You wasted all your time in L.A. for that?” “And here we thought you were being productive...” As they continue laughing, El Spiffy! throws down the shirt and storms out of the room, only making them laugh harder. Only their laughter and voices can be heard as the scene fades out... “I can’t wait to see the commercial.”
