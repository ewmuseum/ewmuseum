Adrenaline 20 American Airlines Arena, Miami, Florida ---- Livewire Ends Joe Bananas's Fruit Protest Metallica’s early metal cult favorite hit, “Seek and Destroy” plays as the usual Adrenaline introduction plays – no fancy video package or anything. We see an old, dusty, room with a ring in the middle and one light hanging above the ring. The light flickers and then goes out and flashes. As it flashes, we see several wrestlers – those highlighted are McGlocklan, Joe Bananas, and The Masked Mullet. Our feed then switches into the jam-packed American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida, panning around the tens of thousands of fans in attendance, all holding signs up such as “LUCHA LIBRE WILL DIE ON SUNDAY!” and “I’M THE KILLER!” The camera then switches to the familiar faces of Kris Raves and James Williams. James Williams: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN WELCOME TO A SOLD OUT AMERICAN AIRLINES ARENA, AND BOY HAVE WE GOT A— The Jamaican groove of ‘Iron Lion Zion’ by Bob Marley cuts James off, and the crowd releases some vented up boos as loudly as their vocal chords will allow. Joe Bananas steps out from the curtain, an orange juice carton and signs stapled to picket sticks and held together with some sort of metal chain. He walks down the rampway, sliding into the ring and placing the objects onto the mat. He calls for a microphone, receiving it fairly quickly to his surprise. James Williams: Why the hell is he out here? Kris Raves: Freedom of speech, James, that’s what it is all about. James Williams: Joe Bananas is not right in the head, the fact he calls himself Bananas should be enough to show you that. Kris Raves: I thought like you, but I’ve started to take to his way of thinking. James Williams: Jesus Christ. The crowds’ boos begin to die down a little, Joe holding the microphone up with one hand and keeping his other tightened in a clenched fist. As he prepares himself to speak the audience bursts out with another round of loud boos. Kris Raves: Let meh Yawdie brudda speak, ya pickneys! Joe Bananas: RUSH’s next Pay Per View event is comin’ dis Sunday, Risin’ From Da Ashes. The crowd cheer for the mention of, what is set to be, a big night for RUSH. Joe Bananas: Ah have a match, already set and on the card, against Livewire for his Network Champyonship belt. The crowd cheers once more at the name of RUSH’s Network champion. Kris Raves: This audience is stupid. Joe Bananas: An’ a lot of people be askin’ meh what I have ta gain, dey t’ink jus cause Livewire is bigger dan me dat Ah don’ have nah chance. James Williams: Livewire weighs 310 lbs, a muscle bound heavyweight whilst Joe is 230 lbs. Kris Raves: Joe cheats though, don’t forget that. James Williams: You say that with such a sparkle in your eye, I can tell you actually mean it with gusto. Kris Raves: Cheating is God’s way of stopping all the goody goody two shoes from winning all the time. James Williams: I’m pretty sure God would want people to earn what they got, rather than cheat. Kris Raves: Are we talking about the wrestler, G.O.D., now or something? Joe pauses for a moment, waiting for the boos to die a little and the barrage of various items being thrown at him to cease. Joe Bananas: Dat title, da RUSH Network Champyonship, is mah megaphone. Dat title is mah soapbox on which Ah can stand. Wid dat title belt Ah will be able ta preach an strengthen mah campaign. If Ah da Network Champyon, den nahbody beh able ta stop mah preachin. Joe lowers the microphone and moves to his bundle of signs, he slides the with ease out of the chain and drops them back down to the mat. He then brings the microphone back up to his lips, the knuckles of his other hand turning white as he grips tightly around the long, steel chain. Joe Bananas: Ah will be able ta fight stronger than ever, beating back and revealing da horrific truth about da way fruit are treated around da world. Da sweatshops, the overwork in fields, da slavery, cruelty, death. All of it shall come ta light, an dat title will only help ta raise mah voice ta more people. James Williams: This guy is truly off his rocker. Kris Raves: Rocker? MARTY JAN… James Williams: Can you go one sentence without making stupid comments? Kris Raves: What’s stupid about Marty Jane… James Williams: Don’t say it! Joe begins shifting towards the nearest corner of the ring. He takes the chain in his hand and begins wrapping it around the various turnbuckles, slinging it round very tightly. James Williams: What the hell is he doing now? Kris Raves: I don’t know, but I like the looks of it. Joe then lifts the carton of orange juice he brought with him, squeezing it tightly in his hand. Joe Bananas: Blood of da innocent. Dis is what Ah protest for! James Williams: Did he just say… protest? Joe throws the carton of orange juice far into the audience, much to the displeasure of the particular row. He then walks back to the corner and begins wrapping the chains around himself. James Williams: He can’t be serious! He continues to chain himself to the turnbuckles, retrieving a lock and key from his pocket and locking himself in. James Williams: Joe Bananas has chained himself to the turnbuckles! Kris Raves: Woah! The crowds’ boos grow louder and louder, the onslaught of things being thrown at and into the ring increasing dramatically with every second. Joe Bananas: We shall overcome! We shall overcome! James Williams: The crowd do not like this one bit. Kris Raves: They mus’ be mad den, brejerin. The crowd boo and boo, completely blocking out Joe’s protest chant. James Williams: This crowd absolutely hates Joe, and everything he has become. Ever since he… Before James can finish, he is interrupted by a familiar sound from the PA system. ‘Do you know what I mean?’ by Oasis begins to play, and the heavy boos suddenly burst into an excitement of cheer and applause. Livewire steps out into full view, fully focused on the ring ahead. James Williams: IT’S LIVEWIRE; LIVEWIRE HAS COME TO STOP THIS NONSENSE! Kris Raves: NO! Livewire stands at the top of the ramp and smiles smugly, fully absorbing the adulation of the Miami, Florida crowd. He raises his arms in the air and nods his head with the rift of his entrance music. After much posing at the top of the ramp he finally makes his way towards the ring. Kris Raves: He better not lay a finger on my rastafarian brudda!!! Livewire’s music finally dies down as he climbs into the ring, his long black leather jacket sways as he walks over to the chained Joe Bananas. Livewire smirks as he looks down at the withering Joe Bananas who has a poignant look on his face as he tries to continue with his protest. Joe Bananas: Wha cha want? Cant ya pickney ead see that I’m trying ta make a peaceful protest here? Livewire nods his head and suddenly a serious stare shoots onto his face. He paces around the ring for a few seconds before finally being handed a mic of his own. Livewire then turns to Joe and puts his mic to his lips. Livewire: I can see exactly what you are doing Joe. And that is why I have come into this ring to stop it. Livewire then delves his right hand into the inside pocket of his leather jacket and takes out a rather large set of wire cutters to a monstrous pop from the crowd. James Williams: I think Livewire is about to put an end to Joe’s fruit protest. Kris Raves: Why, doesn’t that big dumb lummox know that Joe is educating us on the plight of poor fruit? James Williams: You really are an idiot, aren’t you Raves? Livewire walks closer to Joe this time almost in the face of the crazed Jamaican. The two stare at one another for a few seconds before Livewire finally lets loose with the wire cutters and swipes away at the chains that tie Joe Bananas to the turnbuckle. Joe angrily tries to kick Livewire away but much to the pleasure of the fans isn’t having much luck as Livewire slowly cuts all the chains into pieces, freeing Joe much to his disgust, from the turnbuckle. James Williams: Livewire has just unchained Joe Bananas from the turnbuckle. The Lively One has officially ended Joe’s pathetic diatribe and his equally ineffective protest. Thank God! Kris Raves: There you go again, bringing that fool Droidman into this matter. In the ring an irate Joe Bananas is jumping up and down on the spot and is screaming in hysterics. Joe Bananas: Ya pickney! Ya damn pickney, you’ve ruined everything, ya gonnna pay fa dis. As Joe says this he wraps one of the now cut pieces of metal chain around his fist and attempts to swing at Livewire. The taller and more powerful Livewire, however, manages to shrugs this off and pushes Joe Bananas into the corner of the ring and starts delivering furious left and rights, with thudding impact to Joe’s mid-section. Each punch is met with a cheer from the crowd as the Network champion administers a beat-down on his former friend. James Williams: Yes, all those blind-side attacks and ambushes by Joe Bananas are being thrown back in his face by the frustrated Livewire. Bananas is finally getting his comeuppance. Kris Raves: No, evil will always over-come good. Livewire hits one last hay-maker to the jaw of Joe Bananas causing the fruit activist to slump into the turnbuckle. Livewire wipes a bead of sweat off from his forehead and points to the crumpled heap that is Joe Bananas. The crowd cheer loudly, some even snigger at Joe Bananas as the condescending Jamaican lies barely conscious on the canvas. Kris Raves: Ok, Livewire you have made your point. Just let things be, just leave the ring. James Williams: Hmm…I’m not so sure. Something tells me Livewire isn’t quite finished yet. Just as if Livewire has heard James Williams words he climbs out of the ring and looks underneath it as he tries to find something. He finally stops his frantic searching and smiles as he reaches underneath the ring and pulls out a table. James Williams: A table, what does Livewire have planned now? The crowd pop loudly as Livewire lifts the folded table and tucks it under his arm as he climbs into the ring. Livewire props the wooden table up in the middle of the ring and smiles, this time however his smile has a sadistic overture to it. Livewire walks back over to the groggy Joe Bananas and lifts his 230lb frame into the air with one hand. Livewire then effortlessly positions Joe on top of the nearest turnbuckle, his body facing backwards and ominously close to the wooden table that Livewire had set up in the middle of the ring. Kris Raves: I don’t like the look of this James, I’m gonna cover my eyes. Tell me when the carnage is over. James Williams: (Smiling wickedly) Yeah of course, just make sure you keep your eyes closed until I tell you to open them. Kris Raves: (Now closing his eyes) Ok, thanks. James Williams: (Under his breath) Ha, ha sucker. For the first time in the night the crowd have gone silent as they attentively watch Livewire ascend the same turnbuckle as Joe Bananas. Livewire stands on the top of the turnbuckle and the place is unusually high for the 310lb brawler. Livewire uses his massive arms to steady himself before clasping them around the neck of Joe Bananas. Bananas, who is now slowly starting to re-gain consciousness tries to elbow Livewire in the head and block the hold, but Livewire just grits his teeth and keeps the hold firmly on. Livewire looks out to the watching American airline crowd one final time before breathing in and jumping backwards, taking himself and Joe Bananas through the below wooden table in a modified version of the- James Williams: THE ELETRICITY! OH MY GOD, LIVEWIRE HAS JUST DROVE HIMSELF AND JOE BANANAS THROUGH A TABLE, WITH A MODIFIED VERSION OF THE ELECTRICITY!!! Kris Raves: (Still closing his eyes) Can I open my eyes now? James Williams: No, the carnage isn’t finished yet. Kris Raves: (Eyes still closed) But I just heard you say that Livewire, drove Joe through a- James Williams: Shut up! I said the carnage isn’t over yet. Kris Raves: (Keeping his eyes closed) Hmph…ok James Williams: (Under his breath) Ha,ha this is getting way too easy. Kris Raves: (Trying to peek a look) What did you just say? James Williams: Nothing! In the ring Livewire and Joe Bananas lie motionless amongst the rubble of the broken wooden table. Most of the American airline arena crowd, who are now on their feet, start a chant that has become synonymous in RUSH at times when great feats have been witnessed. Crowd: RUSH! RUSH! RUSH! RUSH! RUSH! Kris Raves: (Eyes still closed) That’s it Williams, I’ve had enough. I’m going to take a look at the action in the ring James Williams: Ok, Kris, but I don’t think you’re going to like what you’re about to see. Kris Raves: (Now opening his eyes) Screw you Williams, I’m not a pussy like yo- Oh my God Livewire has decimated Joe Bananas. Damn, that longhaired son of a bitch! James Williams: (Grinning arrogantly) Told you so. Kris Raves: Shut up Williams, get back to your goody goody, good guy lovin’ commentary. Livewire amazing starts to make signs of life in the middle of the ring amongst the splinters of wood and uses the ropes to hoist himself up. The crowd give their biggest pop of the night as Livewire stands on his own two feet, smiling sadistically. James Williams: How on earth is he standing? Livewire’s body just crashed through a wooden table. Kris Raves: He’s on steroids of course, how else could he manage to be standing? James Williams: Do you have to be an asshole all the time Raves? Kris Raves: Nope, only when I’m in the company of you. The fans in the arena continue to clap, cheer and applaud Livewire as he staggers around the ring, holding his ribs as he tries to come to his senses. He finally manages to pick up the mic and look out to the crowd, smiling and grinning throughout the torturous pain that he is in. Livewire manages to weakly raise his hand and point towards the grounded Joe Bananas. Livewire: This here folks, is what happens when people piss me off! The crowd cheer and pop loudly in encouragement for their Network champion. Livewire stumbles towards Joe Bananas, who is now slowly starting to regain consciousness. Livewire notices this and kneels down by his head. Livewire: I’ll see your ass at Rising From the Ashes, bitch! Livewire throws his mic to the canvas and prods Joe Bananas in the head with his foot before raising his arms in the air to a jubilant crescendo of cheers and screams from the crowd. He groggily climbs out of the ring and slowly walks up the ramp as the American Airlines Arena continue to chant his name. James Williams: Will we see such brutality when these two face each other for the first time in a one on one match? Kris Raves: I hope that Joe gets back at Jobwire for this. James Williams: I honestly cannot wait to see that proud gladiator, Livewire, defend his Network Title against that scumbag Joe Bananas at Rising From the Ashes. Kris Raves: That’s pronounced ‘Jobwire’ James… Jobwire. James Williams: Be quiet. Well, as we get the mess cleared out of ringside, we’re going to take a quick break and have our first match of the night! Stay tuned! (Commercial: RUSH Wrestling’s Adrenaline 20 is brought to you by the RUSH roster and staff, duh!) ---- Rush Mason Addresses the Killer at Large The scene fades from a commercial showing RUSH MASON, CEO of Rush Wrestling at a press conference. Rush Mason: No we still do not know who the murder is…and yes he is at large. Reporter 1: What is being done for protection? Rush Mason: Our top security staff is on high alert prepared to dismiss any body with whom seems to be even a minor threat. No further questions. Rush walks away from the podium and the scene fades to black. Kris Raves: Now how are we supposed to feel safe sitting here with a killer in the back. He could be plotting to kill me right now! All the clues point to him as the prime killer. Kris Raves: Please, stop pointing fingers at everyone for all I know it could you. Sitting right next to me the whole time. Raves stares at Williams with a shocked face. Kris Raves: IT WAS YOU! Suddenly from behind 3 young men dressed in business suits and briefcases appear behind Raves. They whisper in his ear and pull out a piece of paper from a case. Raves whispers back and nods, all 4 men glancing nervous smiles at Williams. James Williams: What is this? Raves signs the piece of paper and the three men then each shake Raves’ hand and then hop over the barrier and back to their seats. James Williams: What the hell was that about? Kris Raves: My lawyers have instructed me not to talk to you unless in court. James Williams: Oh you have got to be fucking kidding me…but it seems to me that I am not the only one who thinks Mordred is the killer. Kris Raves: Oh yeah? Who is dumber than you? ---- Matt Elia Accuses Mordred of the Murders On the ring side screen a backstage shot of BOOBIE BOY! Matt Elia walking around. He has a barbwire chain wrapped around his body ending around both of his clenched fists. Kris Raves: I had to ask…. James Williams: You are just ruining your chances of getting an interview with him. Boobie Boy continues to walk around, he is in a total rage he is kicking garbage pails and slamming open doors. Boobie Boy!: MORDRED? Where are you? Come here and face me. Look me square in the eye. You are a coward! Crew dude…where is RUSH Mason, I think I have just found out who the killer is. Better yet let me go let everyone know. James Williams: Where is he walking to? Kris Raves: Ohh god no…he is coming our way. James Williams: Looks like we are gonna get a special guest right about now…. CRASH CRASH CRASH CRASH…Banned from the Back Porch begins to blare of the PA system as Matt Elia steps out on to the stage to a loud pop from the crowd. He takes no time to stop and schmooze with the fans as he jogs quickly down to the ring and walks in. He calls for a mic and paces around the ring frustrated and somewhat excited. Matt Elia: Ok, listen up! I have a huge announcement for all of RUSH. We all know that there is a killer on the loose, but that mystery has just been solved! The crowd begins to spin with excitement. Matt Elia: Rush Mason, and fellow RUSH Wrestlers. Right now I have a camera crew waiting in Mordred’s locker room. And in just one second I will reveal the true proof that will point to Mordred’s apprehension as the mystery murderer. James Williams: See what did I tell you? Kris Raves: How can you take the word of a guy who has only seen Boobies in his life once? The camera switches to the locker room of Mordred and begins to pan around the room.
