Adrenaline 5 Gund Arena, Cleveland, Ohio ---- Jonathan Drachen Gives Tex Kelley Last Week's Win The show fades in to the wrestler entrance of the Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. There, cameras pick up the silhouette of a man leaning against the door, waiting for someone. Suddenly, the body of Coach Tex Kelley can be seen stepping out of a white stretch limo in front of the entrance. The silhouette steps forward, revealing himself as Jonathan Drachen! Jonathan Drachen: Tex, can I get a word with you? Tex stops dead in his tracks, turning his head toward Drachen, suspiciously. Drachen pays no mind to Kelley's suspicions as he moves toward the rear door of the limo. Coach Tex Kelley: Make it quick. I have some scouting to do. Jonathan Drachen: Oh, I think this'll be worth your while. Coach Tex Kelley: Oh yeah? Drachen reaches a hand out, wanting to shake Kelley's hand. Jonathan Drachen: I just wanted to shake the hand of the winner of last week's Adrenaline main event. Coach Tex Kelley: Huh? Kelley's look of suspicion quickly turns to confusion as he slowly reaches his hand out and shakes Drachen's hand, before pulling away. Coach Tex Kelley: You're proclaiming me the winner? Jonathan Drachen: If Deamont thinks I'm going to award him the match after he blasted me in the back of the head with a (expletive)ing blackjack, he can go and (expletive) himself. Tex steps back, unsure of how to take Drachen as the Pizza Delivery Boy appears to be taking on a hostile tone in his voice. Jonathan Drachen: He's tried to burn me alive, he's tried to injure me and he's tried to give me a concussion. Now, I just gave him his first loss in RUSH... but not his last. Coach Tex Kelley: Well, if him and his Solution boys cross Ol' Tex again, a loss is going to be the last thing they have to worry about. Just ask Bob Knight. Drachen looks quizzically at Tex. Tex just shrugs as he begins to enter the arena. Suddenly, the scene cuts to a completely black background. In the far distance, and with "Awake" by Godsmack serving as the musical highlight, an oncoming train begins to appear. As it gets closer and closer, various RUSH superstars begin to show from last week's Adrenaline. Among those highlighted this time around, are Marvin Harris' taxi colliding with Scott Cage's truck, Maxx Crimson throwing Chris Matthews through the side of the cage, Livewire going through the announce table via a devastating back bodydrop from Dark Haze and Cassius Deamont blasting Jonathan Drachen with a blackjack! After Deamont's clip fades off, the oncoming train blasts through the screen, creating the glass shattering effect. Once the last piece falls, the scene cuts into the filled to capacity Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. As soon as the cameras go live within the arena, pyros begin shooting off and fire begins breathing from the ringposts as the raucous crowd's volume level increases with each passing second. Cameras then begin panning the crowd, showing their enthusiasm and excitement for the upstart wrestling federation. The first stop on the panning trip, is that of a man with a red mask on. In his hands, he holds a sign that reads, "I HAVE THE NOVOKAYNE, DO YOU HAVE THE NIGHTMARE POET?" The cameras move on, coming to a group of men that look to have had a few beers too many before the event has even started. As high above them as they can get before they topple onto their drunken faces, they hold a sign that says, "NEWBLUDD FOR PRESIDENT!" Moving on, the cameras come to a halt on a group of punk kids in the crowd. All of them hold various signs in the air. But, the one most prominent, is the one that reads, "CAN THE SOLUTION BE SOLVED?" Finally, the cameras cut to a direct shot of RUSH Wrestling's wild and wacky announce team.
