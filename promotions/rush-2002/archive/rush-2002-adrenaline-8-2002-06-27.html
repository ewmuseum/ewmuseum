The University of Nebraska Fight Song fills the Garden’s arena, sending the New York crowd into a chorus of boos as Coach Tex Kelley makes his way into the fan-filled arena. Sheila Tyrone: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the RUSH WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP…introducing first, your challenger, hailing from Jefferson, Nebraska and weighing in at two hundred eighty-five pounds, “Coach” Tex Kelllllllleeeeeeeeeeyyyyyyy! CTK makes his way to the ring slowly, making various rude gestures and comments towards the fans on either side of the entryway. Kris Raves: WOO, that’s right Tex! You tell ‘em who’s boss! I guess you could say, “HOOSIER DADDY?” Get it, Williams, who’s your daddy…bahahaha! James Williams: You idiot, the Hoosiers are the University of Indiana’s team, not Nebraska’s. Kris Raves: I knew that. Tex steps through the ropes and calmly rests against a turnbuckle as his music fades away. The crowd is already roaring in disapproval at the anticipation of the RUSH World Champion. Finally, the distorted intro of “Disposable Teens” by Marilyn Manson wails throughout the arena, signifying the entrance of the ever hated RUSH World Champion, Cassius Deamont. As expected, the New York crowd fills the venue with a loud, collective jeer. Sheila Tyrone: Introducing now, your RUSH World Champiiiiiiion…hailing from Toronto, Ontario, Canada and weighing in at two hundred sixty-three pounds… Sheila scoffs at the next thing on the introduction card, but hey - it’s her job, she has to read it. Kris Raves waits in anticipation. Sheila Tyrone: …The Number One Superstar in RUSH Wrestling Today…Cassssssssssiussssssss Deeeeeeeeeeammmmmmmmoooooontttttt! Kris Raves: Damn right, woman. Cassius grins at hearing that statement as he slowly and coolly makes his way to the ring, sauntering up the steel stairs and stepping into the ring. He removes the RUSH World Title from the shoulder of his trademark leather jacket and hands it to Mike Sinclair, our ever-faithful Head Referee. James Williams: Now, of course we remember how this all got started, with CTK beating Chris Matthews to earn the number one contendership to the title at Ignition… Kris Raves: …which is where Cassius won not one, but TWO…TWO matches in the same night! No wonder he’s the champ! James Williams: Yes, yes, I’ve heard enough of that. Well, anyway…WHOA, it looks we’re going to get things started early! Tex Kelley immediately rushes (no pun intended) at Deamont as he hands his belt to the referee and drives him to the canvas with a textbook spear, laying into him with stiff punches. Deamont rolls Kelley over, but doesn’t have the advantage for long, as the heavier Kelley rolls back over on top of him and continues laying on with the punches. James Williams: Tex Kelley is HUNGRY to get that title and he will stop at no costs to get it! Kris Raves: Meh…I want Deamont to win. But then again…Kelley is talented…oh geez, I don’t know who to root for! Finally, Kelley lets up after a good ten or fifteen seconds of beating the brains out of Deamont. Deamont, however, is never one to easily be subdued - he shakes the cobwebs out of his head and uppercuts the showboating Kelley in the ‘lower midsection’ as they say. Kelley drops to the mat. James Williams: Low blow by Deamont, and Tex goes crumpling to the canvas. Kris Raves: And we’re only thirty seconds into the match…hahaha, I love it! Deamont capitalizes on Kelley’s vulnerability, laying into his ribs with hard stomps. He lifts Tex off the mat, and whips him into the ropes. On the rebound, he sends Kelley crashing to the mat again with a high-elevation dropkick. Kelley rolls over on to his stomach, and Cassius picks him up from behind and wastes no time in sending him flying backward with a German Suplex. Kelley rolls out of the ring. James Williams: As much as I hate to give him credit…beautifully executed German Suplex by Deamont. Kris Raves: You SHOULD give him credit! He’s the goddamned World Champion! Show some respect, Williams! Cassius casually steps out of the ring after him, looming over the kneeling CTK, who is trying to collect his thoughts. He waits for him to get up, and no sooner knocks him down with a clothesline as the referee begins the mandatory ten count. Mike Sinclair: ONE! Cassius lifts Kelley up by the collar of his royal blue coaching shirt, and places his head under his arm. He peers around the arena for a moment before swiftly dropping CTK on his head with an Evenflow DDT. Mike Sinclair: TWO! James Williams: Dear God! Cassius just dropped Kelley’s head on that unforgiving concrete! Kris Raves: Smart man plus talented wrestler equals World Champion, baby. Mike Sinclair: THREE! Cassius decides he’s had enough fun with Kelley on the outside and rolls back into the ring, resting against the turnbuckle nonchalantly, hardly winded at all. The crowd, obviously, boos, but Deamont seems to bask in it. Mike Sinclair: FOUR! Kelley stirs, rolling over on to his stomach and getting to one knee. Mike Sinclair: FIVE! He grasps the top of his head in agony, but somehow manages to make it to his feet and slide into the ring before Sinclair can count six. As soon as Tex gets to his feet, he gets taken down with a running, leaping forearm smash courtesy of Cassius Deamont. Deamont arrogantly covers him with his boot. ONE! T…not even close as Kelley easily slides underneath the boot of Cassius Deamont. James Williams: What a ridiculously arrogant pin by Deamont. There’s no chance in hell that he’ll beat Kelley like that. Deamont again lifts Kelley up by the back of his shirt, and whips him into the corner. He punches him a couple times in the chest, before whipping Kelley to the opposite corner and following him with a hard clothesline that nearly sends him over the top of the turnbuckle and out of the ring. He then lifts Kelley up and slams him down to the mat with a body slam. This time, he crawls on top of CTK and pins him properly. ONE! TWO! T…nope. Kelley kicks out just after the count of two. James Williams: Cassius Deamont has totally been dominating this match. Not including his initial burst of energy at the beginning of the match, Tex has gotten zero offense in on Deamont. Kris Raves: Just goes to show you why Cassius is our world champion and Tex is not. Cassius drags Kelley to the center of the ring and lifts him up again, placing his head underneath him in a Pedigree position, teasing a Fall Before Me. Instead, Cassius grabs hold of Tex’s pants and pulls him backward, crashing his head into the mat with a piledriver. Then, Cassius takes one of his legs, spins around it, and crosses it with the other before falling to the mat and locking on a classic figure four leglock. James Williams: Figure four by Deamont, and Kelley’s nowhere NEAR the ropes! Kris Raves: Woo! Retaining the title in less than five minutes baby, that’s what I’m talkin’ about. Kelley struggles, the pain shooting through his legs obvious because of his facial expression. After ten seconds of struggling, he falls back to the mat, shoulders down. Sinclair falls to the mat to count - ONE! TWO! …Kelley lifts his shoulders off the mat, obviously not wanting to lose in that manner. Meanwhile, Cassius realizes that he’s right by the ropes, and grabs hold of the middle one to increase the elevation of the hold - and thus, increase the pain. All this, of course, is oblivious to the referee. James Williams: OH COME ON! As if Cassius hasn’t dominated this match enough already, he just HAS to use the ropes illegally, doesn’t he? Kris Raves: Um, well…you said it, not me. James Williams: GRR! Tex continues struggling as Deamont holds on to the ropes, and finally succumbs, falling back to the mat, his shoulders down. ONE! TWO! THRE…thankfully, Mike Sinclair looks at Cassius holding on to the ropes just in the nick of time, and gets up to reprimand him. He orders Deamont to break the hold, and, after utilizing the five-second count fully, of course, he does so. Deamont saunters around the ring cockily as Kelley breathes heavily, obviously exasperated. He lifts Kelley up once again and whips him into the turnbuckle, sending a few knife-edge chops his way before lifting him up to the top. He then hooks Kelley’s arm under his head and lifts him up… …And falls backward to the mat with a huge superplex. Cassius quickly crawls over and hooks his leg. ONE! TWO! THRE…NO! Kelley lifts a shoulder up just in the nick of time. James Williams: Cassius ALMOST won it there with that superplex! Kris Raves: Yeah, almost…but guess what’s coming up, pal? Cassius grins to nobody in particular and lifts Kelley up off the mat, placing him underneath him. He hooks his arms in a double underhook position, signaling for the Fall Before Me pedigree… …But miraculously, Kelley uses all of his strength to lift Deamont high up into the air on his shoulders, and slams his face-first, backwards on to the mat. James Williams: WHAT A REVERSAL BY TEX KELLEY! He could be back into this match! Kris Raves: BAH~! Kelley rushes toward Cassius, not wanting to squander his second wind at all. He lifts him up by the hair, and slams him back down with a powerslam. He lifts him up once again, but this time delivers a huge lariat before turning it into a reverse bulldog. James Williams: THE WHISTLE WHIP BY CTK! HE COULD HAVE IT HERE! Kelley crawls on top of Cassius. ONE! TWO! T…No, Cassius kicks out. Kelley isn’t going to waste his chance, however - he lifts Cassius up yet again by the hair, and whips him violently into the turnbuckle. He then goes all the way to the opposite turnbuckle and signals to the crowd, actually eliciting a cheer. He then rushes at him with all of his speed, aiming to hit a huge lariat on Deamont… …But Deamont pulls referee Mike Sinclair in front of him at the last moment, and Kelley can’t stop in time, accidentally blasting his arm into Sinclair’s face. Mike crumples to the mat. Kris Raves: REF BUMP! James Williams: Damnit, Kelley was about to nail Cassius with a huge lariat but Deamont underhandedly pulled the referee in front of him! That doesn’t stop Tex Kelley from continuing his attack, however. Deamont still had Sinclair - who is by no means a little man - crashed into him, and is still slumped in the corner. Kelley climbs up to the second rope and punches Cassius not once, not twice…but the good ol’ ten times, the crowd chanting along with him. He then picks Deamont up in a suplex position, but holds it for at least ten seconds before crashing him down to the mat with a high-impact Brainbuster DDT. James Williams: WHAT A BRAINBUSTER DDT BY TEX KELLEY! Kris Raves: NOO! How dare he hurt Deamont’s beautiful skull! James Williams: Dude…did you just call Deamont beautiful? Kris Raves: Uh…man, I guess I did, didn’t I? James Williams and Kris Raves go into an awkward bit of silence - but not for long, as Kelley signals to the crowd, picking Deamont up into a powerbomb, and delivering… James Williams: …THE FINAL CUT!!! THE FINAL CUT! DEAMONT IS OUT COLD! Kelley crawls over him and pins him confidently, lying on top of him for at least five seconds before looking over and realizing that Sinclair is down and out! James Williams: DAMNIT! TEX KELLEY WOULD HAVE THIS WON IF CASSIUS WOULDN’T HAVE GOTTEN SINCLAIR KNOCKED OUT! Kris Raves: Hahaha…and just like always, the plan falls right into place for Cassie D. Tex Kelley stomps on Deamont, more in anger rather than in an attempt to weaken him. Suddenly, the crowd goes into a fit of booing… James Williams: I wonder what’s going on? Kris Raves: Look, over there - at the entrance! A monstrous figure appears in the entryway, as everyone finally realizes the unmistakable form of… Chris Sloboda. James Williams: IT’S CHRIS SLOBODA! HE’S HERE OUT HERE ON ADRENALINE ONCE AGAIN, AND THIS CAN’T BE GOOD FOR CTK! He makes his way to the ring, walking at a brisk pace, but seemingly in no hurry. He gets up on the apron and steps over the top rope, Kelley oblivious to his presence. After a few seconds, he walks over behind him, and Kelley freezes in fear. Boda taps him on the shoulder, as Tex turns around slowly… …To be met with the huge hands of the one and only Boda around his throat. Boda lifts him high in the air and holds him there for a few seconds before slamming him down violently with his trademark Bounced Czech. He slides out of the ring as both Sinclair and Deamont begin to stir, while Kelley lays unconscious. James Williams: DEAR GOD, BODA HIT THE BOUNCED CZECH ON TEX KELLEY! CASSIUS IS GOING TO WIN THIS! Deamont slowly crawls over and slumps his arm over Kelley’s chest as Sinclair comes to his senses and makes the count. One……… James Williams: NO, DAMNIT! NOT LIKE THIS! NOT LIKE THIS! Two………… Kris Raves: Mwahahaha, and Cassius reigns supreme once again! Three. Winner and STILL RUSH World Champion: Cassius Deamont. James Williams: NO!!! DAMN THAT CHRIS SLOBODA! DAMN HIM! FIRST ATTACKING JEREMY’S WIFE AND NOW THIS!!! Kris Raves: HAHAHA! This is TOO rich! Tim Walden walks out from the back as Boda climbs into the ring, joining the pair in celebration. Boda shakes Deamont’s hand and shares a hug with Walden and Deamont as they celebrate together with the World Title, and all seems happy with the World Champion…until a frazzled Erica, one of Deamont’s valets, comes out to the ring holding a microphone. She climbs in hurriedly and talks to a concerned Cassius. Erica: CASSIUS! HURRY, COME QUICK…IT’S KATHRYN! Deamont: Whoa, whoa, whoa…what’s wrong? Surely… Erica: LOOK!!! Erica points to the RUSH-Tron, which shows none other than a grinning… …Chris Matthews. On top of that, he has Deamont’s other valet, Kathryn, slumped over his shoulder unconscious. James Williams: CHRIS MATTHEWS HAS KIDNAPPED KATHRYN? FOLKS, WE’RE GOING TO LOSE OUR FEED! Matthews: Cassius, Cassius, Cassius, good “buddy” of mine - I bet you were wondering what happened to your sweet little Kathryn, weren’t you? Heh, just goes to show you…if you ever think about screwing me over like you did at Ignition again - I will personally make your life a living hell. Kris Raves: WHAT! HOW DARE HE! James Williams: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN WE HAVE TO GO, FOR KRIS RAVES THIS IS JAMES WILLIAMS SAYING GOODNIGHT RUSH!
