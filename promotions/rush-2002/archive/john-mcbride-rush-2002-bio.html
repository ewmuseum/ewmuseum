John McBride Biography Real Name Jonathan Jeremiah McBride Nicknames 'The Uncanny Malefactor', 'Socities Rebel', 'Frondeur' Hometown Hackensack, New Jersey Birthday July 31, 1977 Height 6'5' Weight 243 Fed Titles (None) Fed Awards (None) Biography John McBride came from Hackensack, New Jersey, living with his parents since he was 20. Not being corrupted his life, he had a great education and a nonlachant life to his liking. But calamity struck when both parents died in a supposed car accident as they went to the grocery store to pick up some needed items. He was pretty much maddened from the incident, but serenely took it easy coming back with his life, still residing in his three-story house home, managing to pay the mortgage. When he turned 22, he was invited to a wrestling teaching by a man named 'Deadly' Davie, who really had no talent but John thought he was good. From there he wrestled in-and-out of his college: Rutgers University, where he graduated when he was 23, earning a bachelors degree in medicine. He couldn't do anything with such things, only to become some sort of nurse. From there he dropped the thought of being in the medicinal locus, he went wrestling. Fighting in truncated indy feds, located in local bars and clubs where they would earn a living. Until' 24, John McBride was allowed in to PWA, only to quickly get promoted after a few weeks of training to the NTICW. He has an approximate two month tenure, winning the NTICW Tornado Champion and being undefeated at a record of 3-0-0. The NTICW closed, and after that he just went back to a normal life, trying out the medicinal locus again but an incident took place. He was out drinking one night himself, finally getting some time off from his job. As he left the bar, five hoodlums decided to decimate him with lead pipes and wooden pipes, until' they ran while hearing sirens. Although, the damage was done he hadn't been hurt so seriously to die but his memory neurons were destroyed and he received amnesia. (MORE COMING SOON.)
