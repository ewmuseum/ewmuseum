Mr. UK Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames CRUMPET~! King. Hometown London, England Birthday 07/01/1970 Height 6'4 Weight 237 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Mr.Uk. was born to a wealthy Crumpet making company boss. His mother and father lived great lives, in the lap of luxury. Mr.Uk. spent pointless hours reading through books at his boarding school, as his fathers business looked to be getting stronger and stronger. One day, after meeting someone he enjoyed being with, a girl named Melissa, he took a trip into the poor school in the middle of a very run down estate. There people brawled, scrapped and generally fought over nothing. Someone teased him about his posh tank tops and posh accent. Wasting no time, he unleashed a clenched fist and took the 'kid' down. Generally he hated people being hurt, but the rush he had and the adrenaline pumped through hard. Thoroughly enjoying hitting people though was a trouble, and so he looked for other ways of forcing himself to attain the same adrenaline rush as hitting someone. He saw wrestling on, where such greats as Bret Hart and Shawn Michaels fought for the supremacy of the mat, and to win a victory. Mr.Uk. became instantly hooked. He immediately began training with a top wrestling, who taught him the technical side of wrestling, and said weapons should never be relied on for the win. Releasing competition was only hot in America, he headed on over to the other side of the Atlantic. He joined some 'bush league' federations and swept the board aside with his amazing technical wrestling skills. Dominating the fed, he only lost once, before being spotted by the PCW. PCW was on the rise and he joined up straight away. Forming the European Union with three other European based stars he generally enjoyed himself, until injuries and lack of time with wife Nikki, led him to quit. He spent some 18 months in the wilderness, before heading back to WT to sign with RUSH. Mr.Uk. is an arrogant asshole, prepared to do anything for his country or for himself. Good looking, great with the mic and an awesome wrestling, what more could you want?
