Seeing this Cassius fakes a look of surprise and speaks. Cassius Deamont: Oh look… All out of ink… What a surprise. Oh well… Without hesitation Cassius lifts the pen up and runs it through the blood that was produced when he burst Matthews’ head open with the blackjack earlier. Cassius then takes the pen back and signs the paper, before passing them both to Boda. Kris Raves: Whoo! This is great! Boda also moves forward and runs the pen through Matthews’ blood, before also signing it. Kris Raves: This is great! Cassius smiles at this point as Boda hands the contract and pen back to him. Cassius Deamont: Well Chris, I’ll be honest… You don’t seem to be in any state to sign this. So, I’ll just leave the pen and contract here, and you can sign when you’re good and ready. Cassius drops them both to the ground, before stooping down and picking up the Blackjack, which he dropped earlier after hitting Matthews with it. Kris Raves: The Blackjack! Cassius now goes back to standing at his full height and draws back the Blackjack. For a moment Matthews tries to free himself from Flare and Cannon, but to no avail… He soon falls victim to yet another Blackjack shot! Kris Raves: Whoo! Flare and Cannon let go of Matthews’ arms and he drops to the mat, unconscious. Cassius just smiles as he sees this and turns to leave the ring, followed by the others, as “Hate To Say I Told You So” hits over the PA and the fans begin to throw trash into the ring, hating what they are seeing. Kris Raves: Well, we’re out of time now. So, I’m Kris Raves, this has been the best edition of Adrenaline ever, and we’ll see you again next week for more Matthews-beating action! Adrenaline fades out leaving one last look at the bloodied, unconscious Chris Matthews in the ring…Did you feel the RUSH!?!?! FADE TO BLACK!
