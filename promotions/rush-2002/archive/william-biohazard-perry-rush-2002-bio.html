William Biohazard Perry Biography Real Name William Perry Nicknames Biohazard, The Biological One, Hometown Racoon City Birthday 13th July 1975 Height 6'5'' Weight 278 Fed Awards N/A Biography Biohazard was born on July 13th 1976; he was born into a happy, upper class family under the name of William David Perry. He lived with his parents in Racoon City very happily, enjoying the finer things in life due to his aristocratic background. Sadly at the age of just 2 on September 18th 1978 his parents died in a tragic car accident whilst his day carer was looking after him at the Raccoon City mansion. In a matter of days young William was rushed off to New York City where he stayed with his cousin Michael Williams and his aunt and uncle, Mr. and Mrs. Williams. William developed an interest for biochemistry whilst his older cousin Michael indulged in the street life of New York before setting up his own backyard wrestling school. Despite Biohazard’s interest in biochemistry he aspired to be like his cousin, he watched him in and out of the ring valiantly defeating people. Michael was snapped up in an organisation known as the BYO where he would take on men like Chris Chase, and Livewire, two people who are approaching legendary status to this day. As soon as William watched Michael capture the BYO Heavyweight championship in 1990 he flipped, his older cousin at the age of 18 was a true champion yet all he was doing was studying biochemistry. William took up a bit of backyard wrestling in 1990 at 14 years of age but he just didn’t have any skill he looked at himself as a failure, always wrestling at European level as opposed to the World level of his cousin. In 1994 at 18 he inherited the mansion that he had previously lived in before his parents were killed, he moved back there and had a very happy life almost forgetting wrestling and gaining a degree in biochemistry in 1998. William was a happy man and had a great future in front of him, the man then struck it lucky once more when he was entered into Mr. Universe USA, delighted he set up a party at his mansion on September 18th 1999 to celebrate, all his friends were invited. At 7:03pm that night nobody had turned up, the party was scheduled to kick off at 6:45pm, William was depressed. William went down to his lab and started experimenting in his private laboratory; throwing on his lab coat he mixed together a few concoctions. As he was experimenting there was a knock at the door he turned around to see a familiar figure, a friend, or perhaps family he wasn’t sure. Perhaps somebody had turned up for his party? Alas the figure through in petrol bomb and vanished, the mansion blew up and William was proclaimed dead. On September 25th seven days later William crawled out of the mansion, the mansion was still blazing and a helicopter skimmed past presumably scanning the wreckage. William was scarred mentally and physically; all he said for a few months were the words “argel barh”. He was filled with anger and wanted revenge. Dr. Boris George, the first man to have found him after the fire, looked after biohazard briefly. He was taken into rehab and the doctors tried to improve his life. Biohazard escaped from rehab and immediately went to wrestling school where he was trained by D-Vonty, instead of being a mid class wrestler he was now one of the best competitors in the business, it was said that anger fuelled his wrestling abilities. Biohazard went on to multiple federations to capture various championships including a partnership with Livewire, a man whom had teamed with his cousin The Executioner in the past. Biohazard’s problem was glory, no matter how much he achieved, however many championships he won his cousin The Executioner would surpass him. Now Biohazard is still an intensely angry man, reluctant to make friends after the tragic events of his life so far, he is looking for the bastard that through the petrol bomb into the mansion whilst at the same time he is trying to better his brother. Perhaps the next championship belt Biohazard collects will be the big one, I don’t mean the World Championship because he has done that before, I am talking about the big one, the World belt in a big company, then he will finally surpass the seemingly better cousin, The Executioner… Other Feds BYO, NJA, LSWF, SCW, PWA, NTICW Other Fed Awards BYO Heavyweight Championship (1st ever), BYO Hardcore Championship, NJA Heavyweight Championship, NJA Internet Championship X2, NJA Tag Team Championship, NJA 24/7 Championship, SCW Heavyweight Championship, SCW Hardcore Championship, LSWF Heavyweight Championship, PWA Graduate, Last NTCW Extreme Champion Wrestling Style Powerhouse/Brawler Finisher Move Tonga Death Grip Finisher Description A nerve hold in the goozle turned into a pin Finisher Setup Move Lamb Of God Setup Description The Impaler Special Moves Pulling piledriver, double chokeslam, pendulum backbreaker, double armed ddt Entrance Music Moonlight Sonata by Beethoven
