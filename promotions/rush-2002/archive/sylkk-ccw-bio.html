Sylkk Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames 'The Career Killer' & 'Rookie Slayer' Hometown Souix Falls, SD Birthday 4/11/67 Height 6'6 Weight 268 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Sylkk was born and raised in Soux Falls, North Dakota. When he first appeared on the CCW scene back in September of 1998, he decided to make the biggest impact as possible upon entering. He started his career as a face and was to square off against Iron Barbarian, in his very first match. It was a hard fought match, however Sylkk did manage to pull through. This was a confrontation that was far from over, as Iron Barbarian, and the remaining members of Dark Syde, relentlessly went after him. He found security and closure in meeting up with JC Superstar and GOD, whom happened to end up being one of the best tag teams in CCW history. The three of them had quite a few battles against Dark Syde, before finally forcing them out of CCW for good. A short time after this, Sylkk had decided he was being over-shadowed by this tag team duo, and decided it was time to split. He challenged the two of the to several matches, but the commissioner would not approve of it. Eventually, Sylkk and JC would meet up in one on one competition, where JC got the better end of the stick. Not to long following this match, JC and GOD much like that of Dark Syde disappeared from CCW. Sylkk went on about a singles career that was going no where. He was making all enemies and no friends. He was pissing off everyone from Tim Inman to Phil Burner. Then one day out of the blue, he demanded a shot at, then CCW great, Supreme Machine. This match was signed and Sylkk was destroyed. He was very unhappy with the referee's in CCW, and decided to take a leave. While on leave however, his younger brother, The Camargo Kid, decided to try his luck in CCW. Upon hearing this, of course Sylkk was going to return. In 1999, Sylkk (Independent Champion) teamed up with Bobby Blades (Heavyweight Champ), Sid Powers (Trans-Atlantic Champ), Corey James (Hardcore Champ),Ace Hawk, Debbie Carmichal, Candy Bytch (Women's Champ), Lin Xio, and The Camargo Kid. Within this group they held every title except for the ever popular lightweight title and the Tag Team titles. This group was known as The Franchise. However after certain arguments arose over copyright infringments, one member, Sid Powers, took the rights to The Franchise, and signed them over to Sylkk, seeing as he was the final remaining member, of an earlier 'Franchise'. This group of fun loving kids, soon turned against the world, as they would do anything to keep their titles. Soon they found Ace to be a liability and released him from the group with a formal beating on the following show. WAI immediately accquired this soon to be superstar, and this sparked a whole new fued between WAI and The Franchise. In the following months Sid Powers left the group and joined WAI, in what turned out to be a angle to get at them from the inside. However, before it was all said and done, Sylkk took it upon himself to end the career of Sid Powers. With the Franchise beginning to fall apart, several other members faded away, and eventually it only left, Sylkk, Bobby Blades, The Camargo Kid, Lin Xio, and the newly acquired Jaicee Davis. After another month or so, Lin Xio had been kidnapped by terrorists which led to her death, and this really weighed on Sylkk's mind. He eventually snapped. He threw acid into the face of his brother, which was to intentionally end his career, and this left just the three of them to remain. That year as Gold & Glory came to pass, Sylkk turned on Blades, and launched him backwards off of a 75'platform, basically ending his career. Blades has attempted to return twice, but his back has not allowed it. Sylkk has been referred to since, by several people, as 'The Career Killer'. The times passed and Sylkk ran rampant. Trying harder everyday to end more careers, until one day, he recieved his waivers from the CCW. Sylkk spent 2000 mostly in rehab, trying to straighten out his mental state, before finally being released. Upon his release he went back to the CCW to seek work again. Upon his return he found that, CCW was under new management, and he decided to test the waters. During this stint, he managed to capture the Hardcore title. Before once again, being released. 2001 rolled around and CCW had yet again come under new management. Sylkk again, appealed to the board of directors who allowed him to return yet again. This time upon his return, he and Brad Lamen captured the tag team titles. This makes, Hardcore, Indy, & Tag Team Titles that he has held. He eventually was disgusted with CCW enterprises, and thought about turning in his resignation. However, as he watched CCW deteriorate, he decided to proclaim a mutiny and take over CCW Ent. He ran the CCW smoothly, keeping budget good, and the locker room happy. Aside from one person that is. 6 months later, Sylkk was shot 6 times, by an accused, Eric Clark. There were no witnesses however, he was the main suspect. Sylkk was rushed to the hospital where he underwent massive treatment. Reports kept CCW updated on his condition, as Spider, stepped in to office to take over. Suddenly one day, he vanished from the hospital with no reports from the doctors. Many had thought him dead. Many had thought him paralyzed. What they did not expect was for him to return. Sylkk did return, once he made his return he was teamed with the man who tried to kill him in Eric Clark, at Group Therapy where the two of them could not co-exist and cost their team the win. About a month had passed when at the following Pay Per View, Sylkk uncovered the plot of his attempted assassination. Spider had arranged it all and was the man behind the attempt. Sylkk had him arrested and immediately reclaimed his throne as CCW President. Upon hearing of this comeback, another CCW legend posed a comeback. Sylkk's one time friend and partner, Bobby Blades. Bobby Blades, was on his way back to CCW to end a feud that had been about 2 years in the workings. One month had passed, and Bobby Blades was set to face Sylkk at the Pay per View. Career vs Career This was for all the marbles and the loser would be forever banished from CCW competition, or staff positions. The match never aired. Sylkk never showed up at the arena. The Pay Per View was cancelled. This was leaving all of CCW in disarray, and it brinked with disaster. One Month later, Brady Jacobson appeared as a favor to Sylkk, and CCW was rebuilt and refashioned to his liking. Sylkk was never heard from again......until now. With this storied past, and his leader by fear attitude, what will he have in store for RUSH, and all of the wrestlers here? Only time will tell. Other Feds CCW Other Fed Awards CCW Independent, Hardcore, and Tag Champion. CCW Commissioner, Vice President, and President.
