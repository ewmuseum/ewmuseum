The Masked Mullet Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Mullet, TMM, Clem, Da Mullet~!, and Idiot. (Mainly by Cianan.) Hometown Lemmon, South Dakota Birthday April, 1968 Height 6'2 Weight 157 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Clem came from a small town, called Lemmon, South Dakota. He always dreamed of being a wrestler, though, he was always deemed too small, so he got a job close to the action. He joined the wrestling world as ring crew. He happily worked along side the wrestlers, constantly the butt of jokes, due to his looks, and redneck ways, but he still felt like he was living his dream. He joined CCW, as ring crew, and stayed there for quite a while. At least, until his dream came true. An injured Cianan, CCW champion at the time, and owner of CCW, as well. Became injured, and his increasing paranoia caused him to look for some 'muscle.' no one applied, exscept Clem. Cianan begrudgingly took him on, and gave Clem a shot at being a >wrestler, which Clem has never forgotten and to this day thanks, and is loyal towards his 'dream maker' Cianan. As The Masked Mullet, he struggled to over come adversity, beating opponents like Bojangles The Klown, and Devyl, and eventually winning #1 contendership to a hardcore title. The title shot never came. However, he did make quite a name for himself being as a 'hardcore icon.' (though, he never won the title) After struggling and adversity, he managed to beat several people, and win the newly re-instated Light Weight Champion, over the likes of Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen, Kensuke Suzahara, and Jesse Ramey. After a small, and rather painful experiance in the MET Tournament, (and losing to Lilith in a house show). He sank back into obscurity Commonly Used Moves: Hurricanrana, Body Splash, Cross Body, Knife Edge Chops, Top Rope Leg Drop, Bear Hug, Scoop Slam, Off the Top Clothesline, Bulldog, Missle Drop Kick, Drop Toe Hold, Spinning Toe Hold, Spring Board 3rd Rope Splash Suicida. Other Feds CCW, OSW (MET) Other Fed Awards CCW Lightweight Champion (Until Close) Wrestling Style Brawling, and a bit of High Flying Finisher Move The Mullet Drop Finisher Description A swandive, headbutt from the top rope. Finisher Setup Move The Mullet Buster (Also can be used as a secondary finisher) Setup Description An Emerald Fusion Piledriver, or an over the shoulder, side piledriver Special Moves Southern Justice: (Any move that hits the groin, usually involves a headbutt.) The Red Neck: (Reverse Calf Branding) The Mullet Drive: (A running headbutt/spear usually to the stomach or genitals) Entrance Music 'Good Ol' Boys' By Waylen Jennings
