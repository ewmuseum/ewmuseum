He hobbles to his feet - but Matthews dropkicks his leg right out from underneath him. He does so again as D-Kay tries to get up, following it up with a standing leg bar. James Williams: It looks as though Matthews is going to work the leg here. Kris Raves: Oh shoot…doesn’t D-Kay have a history of leg injuries on that right leg? Er…gah…I shouldn’t have said that! Matthews obviously can’t hear Raves, but still decides to work on that right knee nonetheless. D-Kay howls in pain and tries to make it over to his partner Deamont, but Matthews drags him right back over and begins kicking that leg! James Williams: Matthews is finally getting the upper hand in this match - this doesn’t look good for D-Kay with his leg! Kris Raves: I.R.S for life! Ahhhhh! Matthews leans back into the turnbuckle for a moment, seemingly letting D-Kay have a rest. Unable to wrestle with his leg in this condition, he begins scooting his way toward Cassius as fast as he can. When he gets there, he finds that…CASSIUS HAS HIS BACK TURNED, TAUNTING THE CROWD! D-Kay gets fed up and simply slaps Deamont on the back, rolling out of the ring to recover. James Williams: D-KAY JUST SLAPPED CASSIUS’ BACK AND TAGGED HIM IN! DEAMONT HASN’T EVEN BEEN PAYING ATTENTION! Kris Raves: COME ON CASSIUS! GO KILL MATTHEWS! THIS IS YOUR CHANCE! Matthews writhes around on the ground as Deamont steps into the ring, confused. He begins to go toward Matthews to attack, but he sees D-Kay on the outside, seemingly just sitting around. He stalks over to the ropes, leaning over the top and yelling at D-Kay, telling him to get his lazy ass back in the ring. Cassius Deamont: D-KAY! WHAT THE (EXPLETIVE) DID YOU TAG ME IN FOR!? Senior Referee Mike Sinclair drops to the mat to count as Deamont is caught totally off-guard! ONE! TWO! THREE! James Williams: I DON’T BELIEVE THIS! CHRIS MATTHEWS JUST CAUGHT CASSIUS DEAMONT OFF-GUARD AND BEAT HIM WITH A ROLLUP!!! Kris Raves: ARE YOU KIDDING ME!?!? James Williams: AND EVEN BETTER, D-KAY CALYX COULD DO NOTHING ABOUT IT! HAHAHA! D-Kay Calyx is still struggling on the outside. James Williams: CHRIS MATTHEWS HAS SINGLE-HANDEDLY SCORED A VICTORY OVER TWO OF THE MOST POWERFUL MEMBERS OF THE I.R.S FACTION JUST TWO WEEKS BEFORE CROSSROADS! FOLKS, FOR KRIS RAVES, I’M JAMES WILLIAMS - WE’LL SEE YOU NEXT WEEK! Kris Raves: AHHHHHH! THIS SUCKS!!! WAIT..............This isn’t over yet.............. here comes Boda and Brock. Kris Raves: No they don’t. THIS IS AWESOME!!!!!!!! James Williams: What the hell?! Kris Raves: Who cares? Cassius and D-Kay are destroying Matthews! As Deamont drives a knee into Matthews’s abdomen, the camera’s backstage flicker to show the parking lot area of The Continental Airlines Arena. James Williams: Can anyone tell me why our camera’s have gone back to the parking lot? Kris Raves: No clue, Jimmy! The echoing footsteps of the cameraman walk along the concrete a little, getting a better view of the parking lot. By now, the attention of Cassius Deamont and D-Kay Calyx has been focused up to the parking lot, allowing Matthews to roll out of the ring. Kris Raves: Cassius! He’s getting away! Matthews though, is barely standing on the outside, Cassius not even looking at him.
