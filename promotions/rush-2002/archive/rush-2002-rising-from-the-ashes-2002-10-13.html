Rising From the Ashes
Live From The Ocean Center in Daytona Beach, Florida
Sunday, October 13th, 2002

Our feed opens up by cutting backstage to a camera following the recently arrived Scott Cage, accompanied by his personal entourage of bodyguards, as they make their way toward his dressing room. He struts casually past the rows equipment and assortment of congregated backstage technicians, not even a respectful nod directed toward them, which is fair because they didn’t seem too happy to see Cage either. He makes it past them quickly enough, and soon the group finds the room that’s been delegated to Scott for this event. Scott enters, but stops his protectors before they can follow. He shoots them an annoyed look.

Scott Cage: You mind if I get a little privacy for once?

The guards collectively sigh and nod, taking positions up at either side of the door and looking adversely at the cameraman, who takes a few cautious steps back. Cage smirks at this, but doesn’t linger, moving inside the dressing room and shutting the door behind him. The screen then cuts to another camera, positioned inside the room, which watches as Cage examines his rather humble accommodations and shrugs, moving over to a comfy chair positioned between a small table with a phone, and a closed-circuit television airing the PPV. However, just as he takes his seat and begins watching the show, the phone rings. He snatches it up quickly.

Scott Cage: Yeah? What do you want?

Sirius Cage: Scott! I-I-I’ve got bad news! You won’t believe it! He’s coming for you, Scott! You have to get out of there, now! You may still have time!

Scott Cage: Look, just slow down! What the hell are you babbling on about? Who’s coming? Is it that killer that’s been running around!?

Sirius Cage: (silence)

Scott Cage: Sirius! Who is it? Are you there?!

The dead silence on the other end of the phone sets Cage, who begins furiously trying to get a dial tone with no luck, on edge. He rushes over to the door, but he strangely can’t seem to get the knob to turn. He begins kicking and knocking loudly on the door, getting the attention of his guards on the other side, who we see as the scene cuts momentarily back to the first camera, cannot get the door open either. We cut back inside the room, where Cage can only barely hear his guard’s voice through the door saying;

Lars Deville: Boss, me and Leo are going to go find someone who has the key for this door, just hold on okay?

Scott Cage: No you idiots! Don’t just leave me here, someone’s is after me! Come back!

It’s too late, as the two goons have already raced off to search for the maintenance room, leaving Cage alone inside the room. He paces and curses for a long moment, then notices a strange warbling hum coming from the CCTV. He moves over to it and stares in awe as the feed from the PPV is cut and begins showing scenes from a different event. It shows a high-speed chase going on between a beat-up truck and an equally damaged looking sports car, the two exchange bumps and life-threatening maneuvers until the truck manages to send the car careening off the road toward a grove of trees, but instead of going past the accident the truck follows right after the car into the fray. Suddenly, however, the car makes a hair-pin turn to the right, narrowly missing the heavy foliage. The truck isn’t so lucky, crashing violently into an unforgiving oak. The force of the impact launches the truck’s driver through the windshield and into the tree trunk with a sickeningly wet thud. The camera goes in for a close-up of the seemingly lifeless figure, almost unable to make him out clearly from all the blood and debris all around him. Our camera pans back, showing the look of utter shock and surprise on Cage’s face. He remains silent though, as the screen fades to show a figure, heavily bandaged and covered with pipes coming out of him, in a white hospital bed. A voice in the background, which we assume belongs to a doctor, breaks the silence.

Doctor: The patient has sustained a very severe concussion, and it’s sent him into a coma. The chances of him coming out of it are almost next to nothing, but we can keep him comfortable here and observe his condition. We’ll alert you if there are any changes.

Again the screen fades out, returning to show Cage his own visage in what seems to be a collage of some of his promos. The volume of his voice is very weak, but shoots up at seemingly key locations.

Scott Cage (video): I mean, sure...nearly killing his brothers and taking the Network Title from him…yeah...that was funny.

Scott Cage (video): You didn't see the fear in his eyes when I blasted his brothers with the truck...you didn't see his heart sink down into his toes after I won the Network Title…

The screen then changes back to the hospital bed, focusing on the limp hand of the patient, the dull, rhythmic beep of his EKG in the background. In the foreground, however, Cage’s voice is still audible.

Scott Cage (video): I practically killed him. Physically, emotionally, and mentally...

Suddenly, the hand begins to twitch, slowly at first, and then steadily with more force.

Scott Cage (video): …poor kid, he couldn't even try to mess with me.

The hand spasms wildly, the EKG in the background becoming faster and more erratic, till it forms a shaking fist. It then suddenly moves upward, tearing free the pipes, the wires until finally ripping the ventilator from its mouth, and letting out a loud guttural scream. With that, the power in the room suddenly begins to flicker and brown out. Cage, now thoroughly startled, backs against the wall and looks around worriedly. Suddenly, his eyes dart to the door, listening intently as someone unlocks the door and slowly pushes it open. Cage’s eyes widen and his breath catches for a brief moment, but settles once he realizes in the dim light that it’s just his bodyguard Leo. He begins to chuckle to himself, motioning Leo over to him.

Scott Cage: Jesus man, you scared the hell out of me. Now listen, I need you to stick close, I think a ghost form my past has come back to haunt me. What’s that in your hand?

The guard silently stumbles over to Cage, and only then does he realize that someone has really beaten the hell out of him. His eyes are swollen, and blood oozes from broken nose and several cuts along his face. Cage catches him as he slumps to the floor, cradling him in his arms as Leo tries to hand something to him. While Cage can’t understand what Leo’s saying, the item he gives him says quite enough. It’s a blood soaked, broken gasmask. Cage begins to involuntarily shiver as he hears steps coming down the hall, and a large shadowed figure stands in the doorway. As the figure begins to approach the lights finally go completely black. What follows is the sound of Cage screaming ‘NO!’ at the top of lungs, then three loud sickeningly wet thuds, a loud crash, and then nothing but silence. When the lights come back on, the cameraman hurries into the room, and looks upon a scene of great carnage. Cage’s head has been sent through the television and large blood spot streaks on the nearby wall where apparently someone slammed Cage’s head repeatedly against the concrete. Contrary to Matthews’ predicament, however, this hold is not far from the ropes, and Brock is able to force a break after a mere five seconds. The two rise to their feet again, and Valentine uncharacteristically throws a wild clothesline – he pays for it by getting ducked, Newbludd locking on a full nelson, AND SLAMMING HIM DOWN FACE FIRST WITH THE LAST CALL.

Kris Raves: HAHAHA! THE LAST CALL! YES, RUIN HIS PRETTY FACE!

Argyle: Newbludd hit The Last Call and Matthews is OUT! HE COULD RETAIN RIGHT HERE!

Newbludd crawls over and presses his hands on his fellow Milwaukeean’s chest, going for a pin.

ONE…

TWO…

THREE..NO! Valentine throws a shoulder up JUST in the nick of time!

Argyle: Weeee!

Kris Raves: BAAAAAAAAH!

Argyle: Hey, I know that sound…

Brock gets up first, obviously, and decides to leave Valentine alone, heading over towards a rising Matthews. He tries to apparently roll him up in a Mahistral Cradle for a fancy pin – that proves costly; with a sudden burst of strength, Matthews lifts him up on to his shoulders and DRIVES HIM SKULL-FIRST WITH THE INFINITE CHAOS.

Argyle: …Where the HELL did that come from!?

Kris Raves: I don’t know, but my Canadian homey fucking RULES.

All three men, now, lay spent on the mat, each of them breathing heavily. Sinclair thinks about a ten count, but none of that is going to happen here – this is a world title match, and nobody’s going to have it end in a triple count out.

Matthews is the first to stir, pulling himself with all his might toward the fallen Brock Newbludd, and finally getting close enough to drape an arm over him for a pin.

ONE…

TWO…

THREE…NEWBLUDD SHOOTS A SHOULDER OFF THE MAT.

Argyle: Wow…you have to give it to these three men so far, folks. They’ve been out here at least twenty minutes and have been giving it every damn thing they’ve got.

Kris Raves: DA BROCKSTAH kicked out, yes…but it was Matthews’ pin…AH, WHO DO I CHEER FOR!?

Vanilla Skillz: Canadian homey?

Kris Raves: Canadian homey, dawg.

Matthews quickly picks up Newbludd and stuns him with a snap DDT, before going over to work on his ex-best friend, Tyler Valentine. He lifts him up by the hair as he starts to rise, obviously speeding up that process a little. Matthews begins clubbing him harshly in the back of the neck, but then gets an ingenious idea – he nails Valentine in the back of the hamstring with a swift heel kick, and takes the poor man’s legs out once again.

Argyle: Oh, come on…he needs to leave that leg of his alone, honestly…

Kris Raves: I HOPE HIS MUSCLE RIPS RIGHT OFF HIS PANSY LITTLE BONE.

Vanilla Skillz: Geez, dawg…

Matthews starts laughing maniacally, firing stiff right hands into his thigh repeatedly, gaining speed as he does so. Eventually, he jabs the right leg of Valentine rapidly, and finally ends his little sickening show of inhumanity with a knee drop straight to the femur. Valentine roars in pain, attempting to escape the ring – but Matthews pulls him back in for more punishment each time.

Argyle: Okay, seriously…you NEVER want to see someone get hurt, and I think this is a big problem right now.

Kris Raves: You’re such a wuss.

Matthews drags the nearly spent Valentine into the middle of the ring and flips him over, wrenching his leg back and a sickeningly vicious angle with a half crab. The crowd boos immensely, and it seems almost as though tears are welling up in Valentine’s blue eyes; all the while, Brock is just barely recovering.

Argyle: God…he’s going to rip that thing right out of the socket…

Kris Raves: GOOD!!

Valentine screams in agony, trying to reach the ropes, but simply can’t muster up the energy. Refusing to give into the pain, however, he continues to fight it, far too proud to let his hand slap that mat forgivingly.

And suddenly, as though by divine intervention…

…Brock Newbludd kicks up to his feet, and the crowd goes nuts.

Argyle: You’re kidding me.

Kris Raves: …

Matthews stares up at Newbludd fearfully, and has two choices – break the hold or receive a severe beating. Matthews, unwilling to let that chance to cause Tyler Valentine just those few extra precious seconds of pain slip away, keeps the hold on, and catches a sucker kick to the face right away. He drops Valentine’s leg and falls backward on to him, trying to get to his feet while holding his jaw. Newbludd foils his attempt at escaping through the ropes, and lifts Matthews up by his brown hair before punching him in the back and taking him right down with an Inverted DDT.

Argyle: And Brock is back on top. I don’t believe it.

Kris Raves: Right where DA BROCKSTAH, OUR GLORIOUS CHAMPION, should be!

Newbludd takes advantage of his second – or third, fourth, who knows by now – wind, blasting elbows, fists, his head, shoulders, ANYTHING on his body into Matthews to inflict whatever damage possible. He NAILS Matthews in the jaw with a stiff elbow before lifting him up and punching him in the gut, doubling him over. As soon as Matthews gets in that position, Brock pounces and SNAPS MATTHEWS’ HEAD INTO THE MAT WITH THE OLD STYLE DDT.

Argyle: OLD STYLE DDT!

Brock goes for the cover, FULL of confidence, the crowd chanting along with him!

ONE!!!

TWO!!!

THREE—NO!!! Matthews WEAKLY shoots a shoulder up off of the mat, escaping in the nick of time.

Argyle: SO CLOSE! SO DAMN CLOSE!

Matthews isn’t going anywhere in Brock’s eyes, however, and signals to the crowd, setting them on fire as he heads up top for…

Argyle: THE SKY TWISTER PRESS!

Brock climbs one rope…

Two ropes…

…Three topes, and gets all set to jump, when…

…TYLER VALENTINE HOPS TO HIS FEET, SPRINTS TO THE ROPES AND HURLS BROCK NEWBLUDD ACROSS THE RING WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX.

Argyle: HOW THE HELL DOES TYLER DO IT!?!?!

Kris Raves: Just shoot me.

Vanilla Skillz: Shit…this is off da CHAIN.

Valentine flies up to his feet as Newbludd holds his back in agony. Adrenaline coursing through his body, Valentine springs at Newbludd, knocking him over just as he gets to his feet with a VICIOUS spear. Newbludd slams against the turnbuckle, and Valentine hops up to the second ropes, the crowd chanting wildly along with him as he fires right fists rapidly into the champion’s jaw!

ONE!

TWO!

THREE!

FOUR!

FIVE!

SIX!

SEVEN!

EIGHT!

NINE!

TEN!

Newbludd reels in the corner, wobbling drowsily after the tenth punch connects, and Valentine takes that opportunity to whip Newbludd swiftly into the opposite turnbuckle. Brock strikes it chest-first, and Valentine sprints over to him as he reels backward, SPRINGBOARDING OFF THE TOP ROPE AND SPIKING NEWBLUDD WITH THE FLASHBACK OFF THE TOP ROPE, SENDING THE CROWD ABSOLUTELY APESHIT.

Argyle: OH MY GOD YES! THE FLASHBACK OFF THE TOP ON THE CHAMPION!!! TYLER IS GOING TO WIN IT!!! PIN HIM!!!

Tyler ROARS with energy, immediately flying over to Newbludd and HOOKING HIS LEG!

ONE…

Kris Raves: PLEASE KICK OUT!!!

…

Vanilla Skillz: AW SNAPS, THIS IS TOO DAMN EXCITING!!

TWO…

…

THREE!!

NO!! CHRIS MATTHEWS GRABS A HANDFULL OF BLONDE HAIR AND RIPS VALENTINE OFF OF NEWBLUDD AT THE LAST NANOSECOND.

Argyle: SHIT!!

Kris Raves: HAHAHAHAHAAAAAA! YES!

Valentine and Matthews SPRING up to their feet, both operating on pure desire. Matthews fires a quick jab to Valentine’s cheek, but Valentine catches his fist and kicks him in the stomach, doubling him over for the FLASHBACK!!!

Argyle: IF HE HITS THIS ON MATTHEWS, IT’S DONE.

Kris Raves: NOOOOOOOOO!

Valentine wraps his arm around Matthews’ neck and signals for The Flashback…

…But before he can deliver, Matthews desperately lifts Valentine up and DUMPS HIM STRAIGHT OVER THE TOP ROPE, SENDING HIM FLYING DOWN TO THE CONCRETE FLOOR.

Kris Raves: MATTHEWS, YOU ARE MY GO—OH SHIT, TURN AROUND!

Matthews turns around and laughs at Valentine heartily, mocking him, and then turns around…TO MEET BROCK NEWBLUDD FACE-TO-FACE.

Argyle: And he’s in trouble now!

Newbludd grins, and Matthews tries to beg off, but Newbludd has none of it – he CREAMS Matthews in the jaw with a stiff jab that sends him spinning, and then DROPS HIM WITH AN INVERTED DDT. He then climbs to the top, and with Valentine out of the picture, has clear sailing to retain his title…

Argyle: Brock just has to hit this Sky Twister Press…and it’s finally over.

Brock makes it to the top, and takes a deep breath, and finally leaps…

…And with flashbulbs going off non-stop in the roaring crowd…

…BROCK NEWBLUDD HITS THE SKY TWISTER PRESS ON CHRIS MATTHEWS.

Kris Raves: DA FRICKIN BROCKSTAH!!!

As Valentine lay motionless on the outside, Brock wrenches back and hooks Matthews’ leg for the pin.

ONE…

TWO….

THREE!!!

Sheila Tyrone: Your winner, and STILL RUSH WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…BROCK…NEWBLUDDDDDDDDDD!

Argyle: NEWBLUDD DID IT! BROCK NEWBLUDD RETAINED HIS TITLE IN A GRUELING MATCH UP!

Kris Raves: DA BROCKSTAH! WOO!

Vanilla Skillz: Now THAT…was crunk.

Argyle: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, I’M YOUR NEW COMMENTATOR ARGYLE, AND FOR KRIS RAVES AND VANILLA SKILLZ, THIS HAS BEEN RISING FR—

Blackness.

Argyle: Huh!?

Nothing but pure blackness visible in the arena, shrouding any celebration from Newbludd in a veil of darkness.

Kris Raves: Oh great…

Voice: Heh…you know what, Newbludd?

The voice this time is no mystery – it is clearly that of the killer that has been terrorizing RUSH in recent weeks, McGlocklan.

McGlocklan: I was never one for flashy entrances, turn the fucking lights back on.

As if on command, the Ocean Center lights re-illuminate, revealing a trenchcoat clad McGlocklan walking in the aisle, on his way to the ring…

…His sword sheathed on his back.

McGlocklan paces slowly toward the ring, placing his short but to the point words in between steps.

McGlocklan: When I said all of you deserved to die…

*pace*

McGlocklan: You, Mr. Newbludd…

*pace*

McGlocklan: THE infamous Brock Newbludd…

*pace*

McGlocklan: THE RUSH World Champion, Brock Newbludd…

*pace*

McGlocklan stops in his tracks, stepping up on to the ring steps and entering in the ring slowly. Newbludd is kneeling inside the ring, title belt strapped over his shoulder – Matthews is out cold, and Valentine lay recovering outside of the ring.

McGlocklan: YOU, Mr. Newbludd…

*schling*

The sound of McGlocklan unsheathing his impressive blade fills the arena with horror.

McGlocklan: …You are no exception.

McGlocklan grins at Newbludd, who simply spits in his face in response.

Argyle: Uh oh…McGlocklan can’t be happy after not only having his identity revealed two days ago, but losing to The Masked Mullet tonight…

Kris Raves: Oh shit…if he kills DA BROCKSTAH…DEATH WILL BE ADMINISTERED.

McGlocklan calmly wipes the saliva off of his cheekbone, and glares at Newbludd.

McGlocklan: That….that was a mistake.

McGlocklan takes a swipe swiftly at Newbludd’s neck with his sword, but Newbludd THANKFULLY ducks in time, avoiding the blow. He swings his belt, going for a quick, knockout blow to McGlocklan, but the murderer twirls his sword one hundred eighty degrees calmly and smashes the hilt of his sword into the side of Newbludd’s neck with a sickening crack.

Argyle: DEAR GOD!!!

Newbludd growls, and retreats back into the turnbuckle nearest the announce table, falling down to a seated position, holding his shoulder and grimacing, looking down.

McGlocklan: You fool. That was merely a scratch compared to what I’m about to—LOOK AT ME WHEN I’M SPEAKING TO YOU.

McGlocklan jabs his blade forward, stopping centimeters from Brock’s neck before tipping his chin upward with the cold steel sword.

McGlocklan: Die, Newbludd.

McGlocklan retracts his sword, and holds it high in the air.

McGlocklan: Die!

McGlocklan swings his sword violently down towards Newbludd’s neck, and just before it connects, TYLER VALENTINE BLINDSIDES HIM WITH A SHOULDER BARGE, KNOCKING THE SWORD OUT OF HIS HANDS.

Argyle: Oh my…

McGlocklan lunges for his blade, but one man’s hand wraps around his wrist to stop him – Chris Matthews. McGlocklan curses, and rolls out of the ring, leaving his sword behind, and retreating to the back with an utterly pissed-off look in his eyes.

Argyle: Ladies and gentlemen, Tyler Valentine and Chris Matthews in a surprising display of brotherhood oddly enough worked together to save Brock Newbludd’s life…but his shoulder may be a different story! For Kris Raves, and Vanilla Skillz, barring any strange electrical blackouts, this has been Rising From the Ashes…we will see you on Friday!

The camera zooms out from a ¾ view above the ring, showing Chris Matthews uncharacteristically tending to Brock Newbludd’s shoulder in the ring…and RUSH’s hero, Tyler Valentine, staring down its most heinous villain, McGlocklan…as the RUSH logo appears in the lower right corner of the screen, and we fade to black.
