RUSH Wresling Staff: Rush Mason RUSH CEO and 100% Owner Current PCW superstar Rush Mason made a surprise appearance at RUSH Wrestling's second Pay-Per View, Crossroads, on August 18th, 2002. As if his appearance wasn't shocking enough, his announcement was an even bigger bombshell - he had gained control of not only both former 75% owner Boda's share of RUSH, but former World champion Cassius Deamont's 25% share as well, thus making him the full, uncontested owner of RUSH Wrestling. So what's in store for RUSH under the guidance of The Voodoo Villain? Stay tuned, because it's sure to be a wild ride! ??? RUSH Talent Director With Rush Mason coming in as the new CEO, Byron Calver has left to pursue a career in badminton, and RUSH has given him their blessing. However, this leaves the position of Talent Director wide open, but Rush Mason is said to be close to a decision on this matter.
