SyN Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Synner, The German Master Mind Hometown Somewhere in Germany, New York City now Birthday July 20th, 1979 Height 6'0 Weight 237 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography RUSH Match History July 11 and VS and SyN and Biohazard .VS. Stauz Quo and Dante Jackson from Playaz Inc. Result: Win and then attacked by Biohazard and dragged into the van, being drived away. July 18 NO MATCH Signature Moves Confession: An Inverted DDT Mastermind Death Flip: A double moonsault (usually off of a laddder) Germancanrana: A hurricanrana pin Hit it up: A solid Superkick flip Business: A Senton Bomb Germanality: I 540 splash pin SyN was born and raised in Germany as Steff Neirlinger, an average kid. He had a brother and a mom. His dad a was special enforcer for the army and was shot on the night that SyN was born. SyN and his brother, Sven went on with normal lifes in an average house with friends and everything. Then one after noon, when SyN was just 12, his mom had gotten tickets on a boat that was travelling to New York city. It was an offer for a new life and to be more than just average. There was just one thing wrong. SyN's mom had only gotten two tickets. It was all she could get and afford at the time. SyN was 12 and Sven, 14. SyN's mom figured that Sven was old enough and took SyN, who wasn't old enough to live on his own, with her to America, leaving Sven behind. New York had been great. SyN's mom had got a good job at a bakery on the street down from SyN's apartment. SyN went to school, made friends and explored new things. At the age of sixteen, SyN's mom asked SyN what he wanted to do with his life. Facinated with wrestling and the high-flyers at the time, SyN decided to start training for wrestling, hoping a break in a federation would come along. SyN went under the guidance of Dabadindoo Shackabargeay, a wrestling analogy expert that lived down the street to SyN. There, SyN learned Karate and Martial Arts, but more importantly, how to fly like an eagle. You see, SyN was at his full size of 6'0, 240 at the time, which was a bit larger than the high flyers in the wrestling business but with help from Dabadindoo, who always believed in him, SyN learnt how to do it like the pros, doing flips and moonsaults and anything crazy. SyN now had all the wrestling talent, and all he needed was a federation. An ad was showing around the city of the New True Internet Championship Wrestling and how it would be opening soon under the guidance of White Mexican. SyN applied, a hopeful like some of the others and miraculously got accepted. SyN started in the fed a nobody, feuding with another nobody, Karep. After battling with Karep for a week, a match came up. And Extreme title match in a Playboy mansion match. SyN had won, using all his strength. He had became the NTICW Extreme Championship. From that point on, SyN was no longer a nobody but someone who had impressed everybody in the locker room, even joining forces with Scott Taylor, Andi the Magnificent, and Teresa Tomas to form the Ultimate Opposition. SyN was riding the high life. Then an e-mail came. And e-mail that would put SyN through a whole lot more. The email was from a man named Nick Ramsey, a terrorist group that threatened to put New York on fire and burn it down by placing six bombs in three buildings in New York. After going through a lot and making some new friends, SyN finally made it to Nick Ramsey, only to find out that the man wasn't Nick Ramsey, but Sven Neirlinger, his brother! After a grueling battle, SyN pushed the about button at the last second to save New York. Nick Ramsey however, escaped in a plane to who knows wherem disappearing. Saving the world in time, SyN went back to America, only to find out that NTICW had closed, Scott Taylor was in a different fed, and SyN was now a member of RUSH wrestling. What will happen in RUSH for SyN? The story can be written in any direction...
