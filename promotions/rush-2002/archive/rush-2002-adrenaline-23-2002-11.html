RUSH — Card

Adrenaline 23 preview, 8 November 2002

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own preview page for Adrenaline 23, as it stood on its capture: the card billed for Friday 8 November 2002, which the page itself marks “Card Subject To Change”. The capture holds a preview only; no result of the show is on it.

Adrenaline 23
Live From The Rupp Arena in Lexington, Kentucky
Friday November 8th, 2002

Card Subject To Change

First Round World Title Tourney Match
Singles Match
Oktoberfest Match

Matt Hunt vs Josh Marshall

Hunt made the ludicrous request for an 'Oktoberfest Match,' but somehow Sylkk took the offer and thought it might equal more ratings with the German demographic, so what the hell?

Singles Match
Non-Title Match

Livewire (c) vs Mr. UK

Mr. UK debuted last week by attacking Matt Elia, but now he will face off against fellow Brit, Livewire, in a big in-ring debut. With the pride of the Queen on the line, which Brit will stand taller, fight the better fight, and have worse dental work? Who knows, but if Mr. UK wants to make waves in RUSH, he'll have to try and pull a gigantic upset over the Network Champ.

First Round World Title Tourney Match
Singles Match

The Masked Mullet vs Mordred

As we saw last week, the tourney is off and kicking, and now we'll have one of the most highly-contested matches of the first round, featuring Mordred and The Masked Mullet. Mordred hates everyone. Mullet doesn't really know what's going on anyway. Well, we're in for a hell of a fight anyway.

First Round World Title Tourney Match
Singles Match

Matt Elia vs Chris Matthews

Another first round dual goes on here, with Matt Elia searching for what could be a huge win. However, after Matthews consecutive losing streak was snapped, can the Canadian continue his winning ways? More importanly, will Mr. UK play a part, or will that dastardly Vanilla Skillz make an appearance? Only this week's show will tell...

Triple Threat Match


G.O.D vs Joe Bananas vs Ebola III

The biggest.
The sleaziest.
The fittest.
The men who hold those three titles will square off in a good old triple threat match on Adrenaline, with only one leaving as winner. Will Ebola get his first win, will Bananas stop both men, or will the huge Drone take care of his two smaller opponents? Matt Hunt will certainly have a say, and perhaps Spiffy! as well after the heinous attack last week.

#1 Contendership Match To The Network Title
Special Referee: El Spiffy
Singles Match

We saw Marvin Harris come out last week and declare that he's ready for the Syndicate Title, but unfortunately, Stimpo rained on his parade, claiming that he's the rightful contender to the belt which he couldn't quite capture at Rising From The Ashes. This week, though both men have an equal shot at getting their title shot. Well, that is, until you throw the Spiffy! One into the mix. With the Syndicate Champ as the referee, things are bound to be wacky, as Harris and Stimpo battle it out to face El Spiffy!

MAIN EVENT
No DQ Match
Singles Match

Tyler Valentine vs Caleb McGlocklan

These two have been at each other's throats since Caleb McGlocklan was revealed as the killer of RUSH's unwanteds. However, in the recent weeks, this has become more personal, because when McGlocklan injured the champ, he issued a slap in the face to Tyler Valentine, who holds that title more dear than anything. Caleb and Tyler now certainly have personal issues, and as two of the favorites to make it to the finals of the World Title Tourney, this battle will mean more than just pride, it will give one man the edge if they do meet. However, Valentine will be fighting for the honor of the title, while McGlocklan will simply be out for blood...

For this, more signings announced by Sylkk, and much more, check out Adrenaline 23!
