Matt Hunt Biography Real Name Matthias Hunt Nicknames 'Das Dangerous Technik' or /and 'DDT' Hometown Hamburg, Germany Birthday April 7th 1971 Height 6'5' Weight 290 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography Matthias Hunt was born into a German working class family on April 7th 1971. His father, Karl, is an executive within his father’s (Matthias’ grandfather’s) contracting company (the biggest in Northern Germany, third largest in Germany) and his Mother, Kristina, works as a head nurse at the local hospital. He also has an older brother called Andreas who is five years his senior, who continues the family tradition of working in the family business alongside his father and grandfather. Sister Gina is two years younger and works as a freelance photographer for the numerous local newspapers. Matthias - or Matt for short - is married to Manchester-born Stephanie and has two children; Carl (4) and Jessica (7). The three of them currently reside in a New York suburb after deciding to move to the United States, as Matt’s wrestling career made the leap across the pond from Europe. At grammar school at Lons-Sherber Gymnasium (German grammar school) in Hamburg, Matt excelled in his favourite subject; English. He was the polite and well-mannered student, but often had his moments where he would act the class clown to get attention. He was also the sportsman, although he only had eyes for one sport in particular and it wasn’t wrestling…hell, wrestling wasn’t even known of in Germany until the late 1980’s! Matt’s main love in life was football, or soccer, and often used his size advantage (he was 16st at aged 17) on the pitch. Of course, people debated whether he should chase his dream of following in his hero’s – German football captain Franz Beckenbauer – footsteps and become a professional footballer. By the time he left the grammar school, Matt was a hefty 17st and stood at 6’4” and had his heart set on playing professional football. However, the top German clubs in the area turned him down because of his abnormal bulk and his dreams of playing the field were slowly fading quickly. He decided to hop across the English Channel and try a number of English clubs to trial at. Using his vast knowledge of the game, he noticed that the English style was much more physical than his local German approach. So, with this in mind he thought he would be at an advantage and knocked on the doors of certain teams in London area. But his dream were dashed once more, but it wasn’t the language barrier as Matt had excelled in the English subject at school and spoke the dialect fluently. He was simply too big to be a footballer, despite his young age and talented potential, and to save time and money on other clubs of the country, officials advised that he should change the direction he wanted to go in. At 20, Matt was obviously crushed and seeked a new career path. But what would he do? He didn’t want to return to Germany, he had tasted the English environment and wanted more, but what exactly? He obtained a working pass and worked in a supermarket. He wasn’t paid that much a week, but what he did earn was enough to survive. He also found accommodation nearby in a bed and breakfast establishment…again, not ideal living situations, but at least he had a roof over his head and some hot food. After a few months of stacking shelfs and huffing and puffing in the back stores, he noticed an advertisement for a new wrestling school that was soon opening in the area. He hadn’t given the idea much thought as he’d always had football as his career path. But this appealed to him and after hearing the news that he was “too big for football”, was he too big to be a wrestler? He enquired and enrolled in the school for a costly fee that took away nearly half of his weekly wage, but Matt didn’t mind and made the necessary sacrifices. He slowly but surely worked on his physic and in-ring style, while suffering taunts about his nationality and often suffering racist remarks. Instead of reacting face-to-face, Matt took the abuse and worked his frustration off in the ring and gym…it soon paid off and after six or so months in the school, he was up for independent booking by the local small organisations. However, the small-time promoters often ignored his wrestling ability and prodded at his nationality, for the entertainment factor. Matt began to tour on the indy scene, receiving abuse inside the ring and outside…but this only pushed his determination even further. As the years rolled by, Matt began excel while trying his best to ignore the taunts and provocation from those working alongside him. He rose up the ladder in the English wrestling scene and got recognition for his technical wrestling ability…as well as his nationality. It was during a tour of the Greater Manchester area, where Matt met his wife, Stephanie in January 1993. After a whirlwind romance, they married in Salford, Manchester on August 31st 1993 – Stephanie’s birthday. Two years later, in November 1995, baby girl Jessica was born and Carl soon followed in April 1998. While Matt was making changes to his personal life with the marriage and births of two children, his goal of becoming one of the best in the business did not stop for one second. With the family settled in Manchester and Matt being a big name in the English wrestling scene, he felt it was time for a change and pondered about the hop across the large pond to the North Americas. The family stood by his decision and his mind was made up, the Hunt’s were coming to America! Matt Hunt is a name well known around the England wrestling scene, especially in the North-West and London. Now that he’s made a big sacrifice and joined the thousands upon thousands of up and coming wrestlers in the United States, how long will it take for Matt Hunt to be a well-known name in these parts? Only time…and his focus, determination and drive…will tell. Other Feds PWA, CHZ Other Fed Awards former CHZ Commissioner Wrestling Style Techincal.
