Tex Kelley Biography Real Name Coach Tex Kelley Nicknames Coach, CTK Hometown Jefferson, NE Birthday November 20, 11978 Height 6'4 Weight 285 Fed Titles TBA Fed Awards TBA Biography Tex has been a gay star since day 1. In little Jefferson, Nebraska he broke records in little league, Biddy Basketball and pee-wee football. While attending Jefferson High, Tex led the nation in yards per carry. His small 1A school even won the state championship. He has always been a huge kid and his lifting regiment has only augmented what the good Lord gave him. After being all state, it was only natural that Tex would become a cornhusker and he accepted a full athletic scholarship to Nebraska. While at Nebraska, he was converted to defensive end. He won a national championship. His senior campaign saw Mr. Kelley named the ‘husker Defensive MVP'. He had lived a dream. The dream fell apart that spring. Every pro team ignored him at the combines. He was too short. He was too slow. He was a man with out a position. ESPN even did a report on him. Bob Levy could not comprehend the idea that this stud could not find a way to get paid to play. Broken, sad, and depressed Tex went home to Jefferson. In Jefferson, this returning hero could do no wrong. Overnight, he was named Head Football Coach and PE teacher at his old high school. The story book ending took a terrible turn. Coach Tex Kelley became a bitter, mean man. He hated the kids and what they stood for. He was constantly at war with parents and the Board of Ed. Finally, after losing a state sectional game (Jefferson’s third in a row) a parent confronted Tex about his clock management at the end of the game. This incident is now called the Arvin Incident. This parent knew nothing about football but made a mistake. He got in Tex’s face. Tex proceeded to slam the man’s face through a clock that was hanging in Tex’s office. That was the end. State and local officials could not bring themselves to persecute the fallen hero. Mr. Kelley and his legal team cut a deal. The clock guy got $50,000 from Tex. Tex forfeited his teaching certificate and had to find a new line of work.
