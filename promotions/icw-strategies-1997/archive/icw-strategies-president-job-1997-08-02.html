Wanted: President for E-Fed

2 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wanted:President for E-Fed Explanation: ICW (Internet Championship Wrestling) is based on strategies. What the President must do is decide which wrestler has the better strategy for a match. That is all he will do. The matches will be written by the Vice President, unless the President wants to write them. The President must also decide how the winner of the match will win it. The strategies will be sent directly to the President from the wrestlers. The President must be able to check his e-mail every day, and must send the list of winners for a card after the deadline. The deadline for sending in strategies for wrestlers will be two days before the card. (Ex: The deadline for the Saturday night's card would be Thursday at midnight, Tuesday's is Sunday at midnight, and so on…) This job is not as tough as most Presidency jobs are. Trust me. If you are interested, write to [detail withheld] .
