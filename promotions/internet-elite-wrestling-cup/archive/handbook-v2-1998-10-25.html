The Internet Elite Wrestling Corporation Handbook v2.0, 25 October 1998

25 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"E L I T E + B Y + C H O I C E" [Table of Contents] I - Introduction II - Rules [A] - Member Rules [B] - Flash Rules + Flash Tips III - Cards IV - Championships [A] - World Championship [B] - Americas Championship [C] - World Tag Team Championship [D] - World Featherweight Championship [E] - Television Championship V - IEWC Fictional Staff [A] - IEWC Real Staff VI - Application VII - Final Words -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- I. - I N T R O D U C T I O N -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Hello everyone... And welcome to The Internet Elite Wrestling Corporation. Those of you who are receiving this handbook are invited to join a federation that once was a tournament. It started out as a sixteen man tournament to crown only one winner... But now that's changed. By popular demand, the IEWC will be ran as a full federation. The winner of the tournament will be crowned the first ever World Heavyweight Champion. Right now, I'd like to tell you all a little bit about myself. Most of you know me, but for the ones that don't... I am the handler of "Dangerous" Dusty Love. I've been in eWrestling for about a year and a half now. I've been in numerous federations and I've won my share of Championships... So I know what I'm doing. Some of you may remember the federation that I made... The Global Wrestling Alliance. I had it opened for a few months and had to close it up when I lost a lot of help. Then, I reopened it, only to close it again due to me getting a job. I know... It sucked. But since I was disappointed in doing that... I got back into eWrestling as an eWrestler. But now... I've decided to gather the best group of superstars to hit eWrestling rings... And now they reside in the IEWC. This is fun, competitive, and it will last for as long as you guys want it to. And right now, it looks like that'll be a long period of time! We're gonna make this fed the best... And all we need is _YOU!_ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- II. - R U L E S -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- There's not very many rules that you'll need to follow. Just a few. Regular member rules... And of coarse, flash rules. Here they go - A - M E M B E R + R U L E S This part is very simple. You must flash for your matches [See Flash Rules]. No whining or crying about a decision that is made by the President [Me!] All decisions are final. You can do a strategy along with your flashes. That always helps. You can even ask to do an in-ring for the card. An in-ring does _NOT_ count as a flash, but as an extra. It helps! And if you want to, you can even do a commercial for your wrestler's merchandise. You'll be given extra points for that too. Strategies and commercials help win matches... But most of all... Flashes! B - F L A S H + R U L E S Flash rules are very simple as well. There _IS_ a limit for flashes. You can only do _TWO_ flashes per _MATCH_. That's it. Anymore will not count. That keeps it pretty much fair. That keeps some from spamming others that don't have as much time. And most of all... It really shows who the best flasher is. But with the commercials, strats, and flashes... You have many opportunities to gain the points you need to win. But your flashes will play the biggest part in winners, just as it should. And it is a flash sheet federation, so send them to me only. The flash deadline will be Saturday night at midnight. The strategies will be due by Friday night at midnight. III - C A R D S -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- There will be one card a week. It will be in live format, of coarse. All the details and commentating will be included. Matches will be action-packed... And they will be performed in front of the greatest fans and in the best arenas. The television coverage will be broadcasted on eSN. So you can add the IEWC to the list... Along with LAW and EWC [And possibly a few more.] The card will take place on Monday nights. It will be called Monday Night Elite. The card will be out on time... Unless, there is a major problem of some kind. But they should be out on time every week. That's the way it should be, so that's the way it _WILL_ be. There will also be an update weekly and a flash sheet daily. The update should be out on Monday or Tuesday. The flash sheets, as stated before, will be out daily. Unless, I have none to send. But, that should _NEVER_ be the case. ;-) -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- IV - I E W C + C H A M P I O N S H I P S -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- What's a fed without Champions? Of coarse you gotta have belts! There are a total of five Championships. Four singles and one tag. Below are the belts... A little bit about them and all you need to know. A - W O R L D + C H A M P I O N S H I P Like any other federation out there, the highest ranked Championship title is the World Heavyweight Championship. The very best wrestler in the IEWC will hold this one... And only the best out of the rest will get opportunities to win it. This belt will most likely be defended less than every other week or so. The World Championship titles will be the least defended belt in the federation seeing how it's the most presteigious. The World Heavyweight Champion will headline the IEWC's pay-per-views, nine times out of ten. Unless there's a special match that would rank above the title match. ;-) B - A M E R I C A S + C H A M P I O N S H I P This is the second highest belt that you can win in the IEWC. The Americas Championship is similar to the United States Championship... Just with a modified name. This belt will be defended more than the World title... But probably not even once a week. It really just depends. C - W O R L D + T A G + T E A M + C H A M P I O N S H I P These belts will be held by the single _BEST_ tag team in the IEWC. This is the World Championship, but for the tag teams. D - W O R L D + F E A T H E R W E I G H T + C H A M P I O N S H I P This belt is for the wrestlers that are right at or under the 230 pound mark. This Championship is for the smaller guys that might have trouble in the heavyweight division. This division should be more than action-packed and interesting... Those little guys can really fly! ;-) E - T E L E V I S I O N + C H A M P I O N S H I P This is the lowest ranked belt in the IEWC. It will be defended the most frequent. Possibly ever single card. It might be the lowest ranked, but the toughest will hold it. It'll be extra hard to hold on to it, due to it being the most defended belt. Don't underestimate this Champion... He will be one of the very best on the roster. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- V - I E W C + F I C T I O N A L + S T A F F -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Owner - Michael Carwile President - "Dangerous" Dusty Love Ring Announcer - Robert Bowen Commentator [Face] - John Hamilton Commentator [Heel] - Dan Gibbons VII - F I N A L + W O R D S -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Well, that's about all there is to know. I hope to see everyone sign up and join the elite... The Internet Elite Wrestling Corporation! And just remeber... We're... . . . E L I T E . . . . . . B Y . . . . . . C H O I C E ! ! !
