The Internet Elite Wrestling Cup Tournament Handbook, 28 September 1998

28 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Current Roster - IWC World Champion CandyMan, ICW International Champion Tony Paseno, ICW North American Champion Jack Vars, ICW TV Champion Westside Walavan, Arthur Essex, "Dangerous" Damien Cooper, Jimmy Cerone, Evan Manchester, and Brian Farmer. And Possibly - Tommy Rifles, Shawn Walsh, and Fang -------------------- -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [THE + INTERNET + ELITE + WRESTLING + CUP] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Table of Contents] I - Introduction II - Rules [A] - Tournament Rules [B] - Member Rules [C] - Flash Rules + Flash Tips III - Cards IV - IEWC Fictional Staff [A] - IEWC Real Staff V - Application --------------------------------------- I. - I N T R O D U C T I O N --------------------------------------- Hello everyone... And welcome to The Internet Elite Wrestling Cup Tournament. Those of you who are receiving this handbook are the ones that I would like to have participate in this thing. I hope all of you sign up and get ready to be put to the test. You'll be facing off with the best in the eWrestling world. Only one will win... And maybe it will be you. Right now, I'd like to tell you all a little bit about myself. Most of you know me, but for the ones that don't... I am the handler of "Dangerous" Dusty Love. I've been in eWrestling for about a year and a half now. I've been in numerous federations and I've won my share of Championships... So I know what I'm doing. Some of you may remember the federation that I made... The Global Wrestling Alliance. I had it opened for a few months and had to close it up when I lost a lot of help. Then, I reopened it, only to close it again due to me getting a job. I know... It sucked. But since I was disappointed in doing that... I got back into eWrestling as an eWrestler. But now... I've decided to do something that won't last too long. Something that will be fun and very competitive. Since I'm not eWrestling at this point in time. But, this tournament will be great... And all we need is _YOU!_ -------------------- II. - R U L E S -------------------- There's not very many rules that you'll need to follow. Just a few. Tournament rules... Regular member rules... And of coarse, flash rules. Here they go - A - T O U R N A M E N T + R U L E S This tournament will consist of 16 of the _BEST_ wrestlers in the business. That means that there will be four rounds, basically. The tournament is _SINGLE_ elimination. You lose and you're out. Just like the NFL play-offs. You snooze, you lose. The first card will have 8 total matches. All being first round matches. But the second card would seem to have only 4. But that's not the case. If you lose your first round match... You will remain in action until the end of the tournament. That means, you might have a chance to get revenge at the man that eliminated you. It helps fill the card up, and it continues the great action of each wrestler throughout the entire tournament. You sign up... And you're in until the end. That's the way it should be. The first round matches will be determined by a draw. Most likely, I'll put the names in a hat and draw from it. That's the easiest and fairest way to do it. The last man standing when the smoke clears... Is declared the First Annual: Elite Internet Wrestling Cup Winner / Champion. B - M E M B E R + R U L E S This part is very simple. You must flash for your matches [See Flash Rules]. No whining or crying about a decision that is made by the President [Me!] All decisions are final. You can do a strategy along with your flashes. That always helps. You can even ask to do an in-ring for the card. An in-ring counts as a flash. And if you want to, you can even do a commercial for your wrestler's merchandise. You'll be given extra points for that too. Strategies and commercials help win matches... But most of all... Flashes! C - F L A S H + R U L E S Flash rules are very simple as well. There _IS_ a limit for flashes. You can only do _TWO_ flashes per _MATCH_. That's it. Anymore will not count. That keeps it pretty much fair. That keeps some from spamming others that don't have as much time. And most of all... It really shows who the best flasher is. But with the commercials, strats, and flashes... You have many opportunities to gain the points you need to win. But your flashes will play the biggest part in winners, just as it should. And it is a flash sheet federation, so send them to me only. The flash deadline will be Saturday night at midnight. The strategies will be due by Friday night at midnight. III - C A R D S -------------------- There will be one card a week. It will be in live format, of coarse. All the details and commentating will be included. Matches will be action-packed... And they will be performed in front of the greatest fans and in the best arenas. The television coverage will be broadcasted on eSN. So you can add the IEWC to the list... Along with LAW and EWC [And possibly a few more.] The card will take place on Monday nights. It will be called Monday Night Elite [Could change]. The card will be out on time... Unless, there is a major problem of some kind. But they should be out on time every week. That's the way it should be, so that's the way it _WILL_ be. There will also be an update weekly and a flash sheet daily. The update should be out on Monday or Tuesday. The flash sheets, as stated before, will be out daily. Unless, I have none to send. But, that should _NEVER_ be the case. ;-) ---------------------------------------------------------------- IV - I E W C + F I C T I O N A L + S T A F F ---------------------------------------------------------------- Owner - Michael Carwile President - "Dangerous" Dusty Love Ring Announcer - Robert Bowen Commentator [Face] - John Hamilton Commentator [Heel] - Dan Gibbons
