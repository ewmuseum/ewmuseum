Part Two Commences.... We look backstage and see Myst being attended to by a physician. Doctor - I don't see any signs of a concussion right now, but you should be taken in for a further look. You are done wrestling for tonight, young lady. Myst - The hell I am. Doctor - I hope your match is over fast and you don't take any head shots. You cannot say that you weren't warned. Days Grace plays alongside the montage. We then fade to ringside where Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher are all set to call the action once again. Superior Battle Royal The crowd cheers as a thirty minute break in this event ends and the mammoth second half begins. Max Cameron, Gerard Angelo, Artifice, Pyro, and Anthony Hicks make their way towards the ring, the five wrestlers who lost in the first round and are well enough to compete in the match to decide the newest title holder in AW. As they file into the ring, they start jawing at each other. Outside the ring, referees Mark Davis and special guest referee Japhy Ryder stand ready to call who is out and when. The crowd cheers as the bell sounds and all five men start brawling in the middle of the ring.
Ricky Simms - This insanity continues and what a match we start with! Henry Lesher - Look, it's all crap. Without Tommy or someone I think is all right in it, it sucks. Max and Gerard work on Anthony Hicks in the corner as Artifice and Pyro trade punches in the center of the ring. Hicks eye gouges Gerard. Max gives Hicks a clothesline in the corner. Hicks slumps in the corner and takes a series of kicks from Gerard Angelo. Gerard and Max then pick Hicks up and dump him over the top and to the floor as the crowd lets out a cheer. Gerard then turns and takes a thumb to the eye from Pyro as
Artifice clotheslines Max over the top. Max manages to land on the apron and pulls himself back into the ring where Artifice greets him with a series of kicks to the ribs. Pyro gets half way over and holds on to prevent himself from falling to the floor. Gerard moves in on Pyro, but gets caught in a head scissors and then Pyro pulls Gerard over the top rope. Gerard lands on the apron, but Pyro pulls himself into the ring and kicks his feet out in front of him, sweeping Gerard off the apron and to the floor where Japhy
Ryder instructs him to exit ringside. Ricky Simms - Wrestling legend Ryder telling Gerard his quest is over. Henry Lesher - Legend? Not to me he isn't. Max then responds with a right hook to the jaw on Pyro and then spears him so that Pyro's back hits the apron. Max then rolls himself into the ring and then quickly gets to his feet. Pyro pulls himself into the ring where Max stands him up and then tosses him right back over the top and to the floor. Referee Mark Davis calls for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Max Cameron is the new Superior champion of AW! Henry Lesher - The crap from GWO is becoming the crap of AW. I feel the urge to punch kittens. Max drops and exits the ring with his title belt held high. He then starts for the back as the camera views backstage and we see Ray Dees with Jason Knight, marking the first time tonight we have seen the usual commissioner for AW.
Ray Dees - Jason, we hear you have January off so that Shelia Lavin may book the shows. Jason Knight - Oh I don't have the month off. You see, Vesuvius wanted me to come up with something AW needed. I have decided we needed to spice things up a little. As McCoy grabs at his knee, Renske goes to the ropes himself and lands a clothesline from the top rope on McCoy and goes for the pin. 1...2... Daniel McCoy manages to kick out. McCoy then bulldogs Renske to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Renske.

Renske then pulls himself to his feet while a "Holy shit" chant starts in the crowd.
