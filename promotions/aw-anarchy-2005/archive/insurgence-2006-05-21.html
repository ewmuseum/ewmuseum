Atlanta, GA It's a capacity crowd as we get set for another exciting edition of Insurgence. We hear "Gold Dust Woman" by Hole and see Kelly Buchanan making her way towards the ring.
Ricky Simms - This is odd. We were supposed to start the show with a segment and yet here comes Kelly. Henry Lesher - This doesn't bode well for this show. Kelly is half way to the ring when Pyro attacks her from behind with a chop block to take the feet out from under her. He then pulls her by her hair to ringside and then rolls her into the ring.
Pyro rolls himself into the ring and smiles as the bell sounds to get things started. Pyro vs Kelly Buchanan Pyro stands Kelly up, whips her off the ropes and then gives her a big boot to the face and then a knee to the forehead. Pyro goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kelly Buchanan. Pyro slaps the mat and shows three fingers to referee Steve Blake. Blake shakes his head and holds up two. Pyro mumbles to himself as he stands Kelly up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a clothesline down to the mat. Pyro then goes to the nearest corner and climbs to the second rope. He waits for Kelly to stand and face him and then lands a clothesline on her from there. Pyro then stands over her and smiles while the crowd starts a chant for her.
Henry Lesher - You have to feel sorry for fans at moments like this. Kelly has zero chance and they are still trying to believe in her.
Ricky Simms - It has been proven anyone on this roster can score an upset on any given week. Henry Lesher - Not a former Glamour Girl. That was the worst stable in history and I saw the New Blood. Pyro stands Kelly up, whips her into the corner buckle and then gives her a clothesline in the corner. He follows that up with a knee to the chest on Kelly and again goes for the pin.
1...2... kick out by Kelly Buchanan. Pyro shakes his head as he again pulls her to her feet. He whips her into the corner buckle and goes for a shoulder block from there, but Kelly steps out of the way and then gives Pyro a series of knife edge chops to the chest that send him reeling to the center of the ring. Kelly then grabs his feet, pulls him down to the mat and goes for a sharpshooter, but she is unable to turn Pyro over. Pyro then rolls onto his back and violently kicks her into the corner.
Henry Lesher - That five seconds of offence means she is done for the night. Ricky Simms - Good to see the objectivity is alive and well from the good doc. Pyro gets to his feet and grabs Kelly by the hair. She drops and lands a jawbreaker on Pyro. He falls back into the corner buckle. Kelly then lands a handspring back elbow to the jaw on Pyro before headlocking him and then she gives him a DDT to the mat before she goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Kelly then goes to the ropes and makes her way quickly to the top. She poses once there for the fans and then leaps off for a flying elbow only to find no one home as Pyro sits up and moves out of the way. Pyro then grabs Kelly around the throat and pulls her to her feet. Kelly knows the choke slam is coming, so she thumbs Pyro in the eye and then dives out of the ring to the floor. Pyro shakes this off and looks enraged, rolling out of the ring after his prey. He grabs an audio cable and starts strangling her with it. Inside the ring, Steve Blake warns Pyro to release her or risk a disqualification. Pyro refuses to release her and the referee is forced to call for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Pyro got himself disqualified. Henry Lesher - Yes, but he appears to be killing Buchanan, so that makes up for it. Pyro maintains his choke on Kelly while the bell rings repeatedly. The crowd then cheers as we see a group of four women rushing to ringside. Kathy Smith is leading the way with a chair in hand and clubs Pyro across the skull with it while the other girls pull Kelly from his grasp. They then all enter the ring and surround Kelly.
Henry Lesher - Oh no.... Ricky Simms - Let's see now. That's Corina, Kathy, Ophelia, Chastity and Kelly Buchanan. Henry Lesher - GWO's original Spirit Squad... Ricky Simms - It appears they may have reunited. Henry Lesher - There's not enough liquor to make this pain go away. It's a black day in wrestling.
