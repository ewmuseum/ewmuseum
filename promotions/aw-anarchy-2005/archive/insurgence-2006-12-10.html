AW (Anarchy, 2005) — Card

AW Insurgence, 10 December 2006

10 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 10th, 2006 St. John's, Newfoundland A long white limo pulls up backstage as you see Ray Dees making his way to the back end of the limo waiting to see who's inside. The limo driver hustles around the long limo and opens the door.
Out steps a man in a fancy white suit and expensive looking sun glasses to boot. Ray Dees- Garth Riv.. The man puts his fingers on Ray's Lips so he can't say anymore. Man in Suit- It's Mister Rivers to you. Mister Rivers lifts his fingers from Ray's lips. Ray Dees- You don't have a contract with AW do you? Mister Rivers- Ha no I am here to watch the show, So why don't you do some work around here and escort me to the Sky boxes.
Ray Dees- That's not my job! Mister Rivers slides out a hundred dollar bill from inside his jacket and slides it in Ray's shirt pocket.
Mister Rivers- It is now. Mister Rivers snaps his fingers as another man in a suit steps out from the limo but this man has nearly a foot on Mister Rivers and his build makes Dees look like a toothpick. The man towers over Dees as Dees points off camera view.
Ray Dees- This way. Mister Rivers- Thank You. The bigger man just smirks as Ray Dees hustles to lead the way. "Coming Undone" by Korn plays while images of Moose, Rated R, Tommy Purr, Max Cameron, Donovan Blake and Parker
Davis scroll across the screen. We then head to ringside where Ricky Simms and
Henry Lesher are set to call the action.
Craven Broderick vs. De'Anton Maybon "Creep" by Radiohead plays as Maybon comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd tonight. Maybon quickly makes his way down the ramp towards the ring and then makes his way up the ring steps and then stands on the apron for a few moments before he steps through the ropes into the ring. Maybon then has a few words with referee Francis Clarke while his music fades and then we hear Craven's entrance theme and the crowd also gives
Craven Broderick a mixed reaction when he starts making his way towards the ring.
Ricky Simms - This is a really odd match up here. Henry Lesher - Well, of course. Maybon is a real wrestler who has spent time in a fine company like UCW while
Craven is probably the best looking woman we have here in AW.
Ricky Simms - Craven's a man. Henry Lesher - Which speaks volumes. Craven reaches ringside and looks into the ring at Maybon and scowls, then pulls himself onto the apron. Craven then steps through the ropes into the ring and the referee calls for the bell as the two men lock up in the center of the ring. Maybon backs Craven into the ropes where the referee asks for a clean break. Maybon slowly backs off, but
Craven gives him a knee to the gut for his trouble and then Craven steps out of the corner, shoving Maybon into the corner where he delivers rights and lefts to the ribs. Maybon then answers with a knee to the gut on Craven and then gives him a series of head butts that drive him backward before he then gives
Craven a clothesline to the mat where he goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by
Craven Broderick.
Ricky Simms - Both men showing a lot of effort thus far in this match. Henry Lesher - People don't want effort. They want results. Maybon stands Craven up, whips him off the ropes and then both men clothesline each other, leading the referee to start counting them down. On the four count, Maybon rolls towards the ropes. On the six count, Craven suddenly springs to his feet and adjusts his dress before he then starts kicking away at De'Anton Maybon. Craven then stands Maybon up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a kick to the chest that sends him down to the mat. Craven then goes to the ropes and makes his way to the top, then poses there and blows kisses to his fans. He then leaps off and attempts a flying elbow to the throat on Maybon, but Maybon rolls out of the way and Craven hits the mat with a solid thud. Maybon then uses the ropes to quickly get to his feet and he starts putting the boots to Craven.
Ricky Simms - Great back and forth action between these two. Henry Lesher - In reality, though, Craven's at a bit of an advantage as the woman here. Maybon will hold back a little with someone so fragile.
Ricky Simms - What the hell are you talking about? Maybon stands Craven up, whips him off the ropes and then misses with a clothesline when Craven ducks it and then he gives Maybon a backslide into a pin attempt, turning it so he can grab the tights for added leverage out of sight of the referee. 1...2...3! Craven
Broderick wins the match! He rolls himself out to the floor where he tells the camera that win was for Tommy Purr. He then makes his way up the ramp, skipping as he does so which draws laughter from some of the fans and concerned looks from others.
Ricky Simms - Well, the man woman won. Henry Lesher - As it should be, the best looking woman in AW wins the match. We look backstage where Crystal Braun is with Wyatt Gibson. Crystal Braun - Wyatt, this week we saw the return of the CWA Originals. What will AW do to battle that?
Wyatt Gibson - Battle what? They're has been wrestlers on a nothing show. All I need to do tonight is name the new
GM of my show and we're all set to do that.
Crystal Braun - Who's the new GM? Wyatt Gibson - Fans, I gove you the man who hired me to help run the GWO.... Hector Braun! Crystal Braun - Dad? Hector Braun steps into the picture with a smile on his face. He nods his head and embraces Crystal.
Hector Braun - You see, I was the man behind the GWO and I created the most successful wrestling company that there was. Now I look at AW here and it saddens me. My two princesses are working for a failing company. I must help bring back whatever prestige that this company ever had.
Crystal Braun - And you think you're the right man for the job? Hector Braun - Crystal, I have never failed before. You know that. I will not fail now. Allow me to begin with next week's main event. The tag team titles have been idle for too long. Moose and
Vigilante will defend those belts against both the teams of 6Pac and Bombshell as well as Tommy Purr and Hoshi Yakamoto. That will start the rejuvenation of this company.
Crystal Braun - Good luck, Dad. Hector Braun - Baby, I never needed luck before. I don't need it now. He and Wyatt exit the shot together and we head back to the ring. Leech vs. The Moose "I Feel Alright" by RCPM plays as Moose shoots out of the back, down the ramp and into the ring. He bounces off the ropes a few times then stands dead center in the ring.
Ricky Simms - Fresh off his victory at Restricted is our CWA champ and Doc, I don't think I've seen him look this interested in a match for a long time.
Henry Lesher - Yeah, yeah...Leech is more important. "As I Am" by Dream Theater plays and Leech enters, angrily clutching a cancelled check in his hand. He slides in under the bottom rope as the bell rings. They circle each other a few times. As they go for a lock up Leech ducks under and pushes Moose into the ropes. Moose ducks under a clothesline, rebounds off the other side and hits Leech with a shoulder block. Leech falls flat on his back and lays there with a twisted smile on his face. Moose yells at him to get back on his feet, Leech shakes his head. The Ref shrugs and starts counting Leech out.
Ricky Simms - This is disgusting. Henry Lesher - You show them Leech!!! Moose removes the title belt from around his waist then whips Leech across the chest with the leather strap. Leech rolls over and gets hit again as the ref stops the count and grabs the title. Moose turns around to fight him and Leech leaps up hitting Moose with a low blow.
Ricky Simms - This display from Leech....it's inexcusable considering the classics these two have fought.
Leech grabs the title from the ref and marches around the ring with it. He folds it up and drops it on the mat.
Henry Lesher - That title looks better on him. Leech grabs Moose by the hair and pulls him to his feet. He throws a punch,
Moose blocks and hits one of his own. Leech flops backwards onto the mat once again.
Henry Lesher - He's just toying with Moose now. Moose kicks the bottom rope, shrugs to the crowd then goes for the pin by placing his foot on Leech's chest. At the count of two Moose removes his foot.
Leech sits up and angrily yells at Moose to pin him. Moose folds his arms and stands in the corner staring Leech down.
Ricky Simms - We're not going to get a conclusion tonight. Leech gets to one knee, turns his back to Moose and complains to the ref about the speed of the count. Moose rushes over and places Leech in a full nelson dragging him up off the mat.
Ricky Simms - Maybe I spoke too soon. Leech kicks his legs in the air as Moose tightens his grip and begins to walk
Leech around the ring as if showing him off to the crowd. Moose positions him in front of the ropes as Leech struggles to get a foot on the ropes. As the ref checks the hold Leech kicks backwards and hits a low blow causing Moose to release the hold. He spins around and pushes Moose into the ropes then goes for a schoolboy, hooking the belt loops in Moose's jeans and putting his feet on the ropes for leverage. Moose kicks out at 2 1/2 and rolls into the ref. In the confusion Leech picks up the title belt and smashes Moose in the face with it.
Henry Lesher - That should do it. Our new champion Ricky Simms - This isn't a title match. Leech stands over Moose mocking and yelling at him. Moose lurches up and hits
Leech in the stomach, he then whips Leech into the ropes. Leech catches himself on the top rope and slides out, he flips Moose off and heads back up the entrance ramp.
Ricky Simms - I can't believe this. Moose rolls out the ring and starts up after him. They brawl back and forth,
Moose rakes Leech's eyes then lifts him in the air with a chokehold. He slams
Leech against the steel of the entrance ramp and continues to choke him, yelling
"You think it's funny now Leech?"
Henry Lesher - What's going on? He can't do this. The ref runs up the ramp and tries to break the hold. After trying unsuccessfully the ref starts a five count, at the count of four he tries to break the hold again, is unsuccessful and calls for the bell. Moose begins to punch Leech in the face while continuing to choke him. A few more refs run out and pull Moose off of Leech and towards the back while he yells "It's not over
Leech!!!" The Ring announcer announces Leech the winner as the result of a disqualification as the crowd boos and begins to throw things.
Henry Lesher - As predicted, Leech is the victor. Ricky Simms - Does he look like a winner to you? Henry Lesher - Always. Leech gets up slowly, looks to the crowd and makes the dollar sign with his fingers, a big smile on his face. Moose suddenly reappears with a chair in his hand and cracks it across Leech's back. He grabs Leech by the back of the head, walks him down the ramp and throws him back in the ring. Leech quickly rolls to his feet and attacks Moose as he enters the ring. More referees, ring crew and road agents flood the ring and separate them. Leech is lead away first as Moose is held back in the ring. They stare each other down until Leech is lead out of sight. Moose is then allowed to return to the locker room. We then look backstage where Hector Braun is talking to an unknown man to AW fans.
Ricky Simms - I recognize that man. That's Nigel Underwood. Henry Lesher - He is part of a great company. Ricky Simms - Well, I won't deny that UCW is a fine company in its own right. I just don't understand why he would be here. The camera man tries to get closer, but security guards rush in and keep them at a distance. Whatever is being talked about is not for public ears, apparently.
Henry Lesher - Nice to see Hector Braun is doing what he has to do, sell this company to UCW. Ricky Simms - He isn't doing that. Henry Lesher - Prove it. Donovan Blake vs. Bombshell "I Am the Body Beautiful" by Salt N Pepa plays as Bombshell makes her way towards the ring. She gets a loud ovation from the crowd as she slinks her way down the ramp, only to be attacked from behind by Donovan Blake who gives her a clothesline to the back of the head, sending her face first to the steel ramp. Donovan Blake then grabs her by the hair and pulls her to ringside where he then slams her head into the steel guard railing before he stands her up and rolls her into the ring. He then rolls himself into the ring and referee Jay Lawrence calls for the bell to get this match officially started.
Ricky Simms - Donovan Blake takes the low road before the match begins. Henry Lesher - No one cares which road is taken. It is all about who wins the match. Blake stands Bombshell up, whips her off the ropes and then gives her a backdrop to the mat. He then stands her up and whips her into the corner buckle. Blake then charges into the corner shoulder first, but finds no one home as Bombshell steps to the side. She then gives Donovan a German suplex to the mat and then goes for the quick pin.
1...2... kick out by Donovan Blake. Bombshell then stands him up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a dropkick back down to the mat. Bombshell then goes to the ropes and jumps to the top rope. She leaps off and lands a moonsault into
Donovan Blake before she goes for the pin again. 1...2... again Donovan kicks out in time.
Ricky Simms - Blake's early advantage has disappeared. Henry Lesher - Much like his credibility. Bombshell stands Donovan Blake up, sets for a whip into the corner only to have him reverse it, sending her into the corner where he manages to hit the shoulder block this time. He then gives her a series of shoulder blocks before he then gives her a knee to the gut and then a double under hook suplex to the mat from there. Blake then tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Bombshell. Donovan Blake then stands Bombshell up and whips her through the ropes and out to the floor before he drops to the mat and rolls himself out to the floor as well. Blake stands Bombshell up and then whips her shoulder first into the steel corner post. He then picks her up and gives her a body slam to the floor. Donovan then rolls himself into the ring to break up the count before he rolls himself back out.
Ricky Simms - Bombshell is in some trouble here. Henry Lesher - Well, she's involved with 6Pac. I mean, it can't get much worse than that. Blake then stands Bombshell up and whips her shoulder first into the steel guard railing. He then stands her up and rolls her into the ring before he rolls himself into the ring. He gets to his feet and stands over Bombshell with a sadistic smile on his face. He then grabs her around the throat and stands her up. He throws her into the corner and starts putting the boots to her. We then see 6Pac Dudley rushing to the ring with a steel chair in hand. He slides under the bottom rope into the ring and then gets to his feet before slamming the chair across the back of Donovan Blake which causes the referee to call for the bell and disqualify Bombshell.
Ricky Simms - Donovan was drawing 6Pac into a fight with this match and it looks like he got more than he bargained for. Henry Lesher - Yeah, well no one cares. Security rushes into the ring to pry 6Pac off of Donovan Blake while we see Bombshell rolling herself out of the ring to the floor where she is trying to regroup. We then look backstage where
Vigilante is seen pacing when Ray Dees enters the picture. Ray Dees - Vigilante, you weren't booked for tonight, but next week you will defend the tag team belts. Vigilante - Ray, I will stand in that ring next week and I will destroy two tag teams. I will then sets my sights on
Ryan Yorke and his title belt. EOS will rule this company.
Ray Dees - There have been a lot of rumors of backlash against your group by some of the rest of the roster.
Vigilante - People are always jealous of EOS. It's part of the job, being the best in the world. I will show everyone next week why we are still the elite.
Ray Dees - You have personally, however had some bad luck of late in not getting to last week's main event among other things.
Vigilante - Ray, I'll declare every match I have from now on a no DQ match if I have to. I will get to the top of this company. I'll knock Wyatt himself out if I have to. Nothing means more to me than being number one.
Ray Dees - Well, next week will show whether you're still number one in the tag department.
Vigilante - Tag department? Moose and I are the only team that matters. Everyone else is just slapped together. Moose and I have been brothers for years. No one has a chance to get the titles against us. You'll see Leech and Max as a tag team before that happens.
Ray Dees - That's not likely. Vigilante - That's precisely my point. Now get out of my locker room. Ray complies with the demand and leaves while we head back to the ring. Halloween Jack vs. Max Cameron vs. Tommy Purr "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays as Max Cameron comes out to a mixed reaction from the eastern Canadian crowd. He pays no attention to them and marches down the ramp, declaring to the camera that he will be champion soon. He then rolls himself into the ring and quickly gets to his feet where he has a few words with referee Mark Davis. His music then fades and we hear "Walk Idiot Walk" by The Hives and Tommy Purr steps out onto the stage to a loud ovation from the crowd. He nods his head to the music as he then makes his way down the ramp. He then stops at ringside and looks in at Max and pauses before pulling himself onto the apron. He then steps through the ropes into the ring and he and max shake hands. Tommy's music then stops and "Survival of the Sickest" by Saliva plays as Halloween Jack comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He quickly makes his way down the ramp and then slides into the ring before getting to his feet. Tommy and Max offer to shake hands with him. He just smiles and shakes his head as the referee calls for the bell. Max and Tommy then both start kicking Jack in the ribs, backing him into the corner from where they give him a double vertical suplex to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Jack refuses to shake hands with his opponents and now they are double teaming him.
Henry Lesher - I have to side with Jack on this one. This is a fight, not golf. Tommy and Max stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and he ducks a double clothesline before he comes back off the ropes and gives them a double shoulder block to the mat. Jack then starts putting the boots to Max which causes Max to roll himself out of the ring to regroup. Jack then stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a backdrop to the mat. Jack then picks Tommy up and gives him a power slam to the mat before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tommy. Max then grabs the feet of Halloween Jack and pulls him out of the ring to the floor. Max starts trading rights and lefts with Jack and then Max gives Jack an eye gouge followed by a head butt and then rams his head into the steel ring steps. Max then whips
Jack shoulder first into the steel railing before rolling himself back into the ring. Tommy is on his feet now and starts trading punches with Max as well. Max eye gouges Tommy and then whips him through the ropes and out to the floor before posing for the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Max looks good so far in this one. Henry Lesher - That doesn't say much for competition. On the floor, Jack is on his feet now and he starts landing rights and lefts to the jaw on Tommy Purr. Jack then rolls
Tommy into the ring where Max greets him with a baseball slide back to the floor. Jack then grabs the feet of Max and pulls him out to the floor. Jack then gives Max a clothesline to the floor from there. Jack then rolls himself into the ring and gets to his feet before taking a bow to the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Now it's Jack who has control. Henry Lesher - If they would go for pins as much as they pat themselves on the back, this could have been over by now.
On the floor, Max and Tommy start trading punches. Halloween Jack then launches himself through the top and middle rope and out to the floor where he lands on both Max and Tommy, sending them down to the floor. Jack then stands Max up and rolls him into the ring, then rolls himself into the ring. Jack picks Max up and slams him down to the mat.
Jack then goes to the ropes and makes his way to the top and then launches himself off and lands a somersault leg drop across the throat of Max Cameron before he goes for the pin. 1...2... Tommy pulls Jack out of the ring to break up the count. Tommy then picks Jack up and drops him onto the steel guard railing, the steel catching his throat. Tommy then rolls himself into the ring where he stands Max up, whips him into the corner buckle and then he gives him a monkey flip to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Now the control rests with Tommy. Henry Lesher - Well, Tommy is the best wrestler in this match, so it is natural that he would start to dominate. Tommy stands Max up, whips him off the ropes and misses with a clothesline when Max ducks it before Max gives Tommy a reverse neck breaker to the mat. Max then stands Tommy up and whips him off the ropes, bouncing him off of Halloween Jack who had just pulled himself onto the apron. Max then gives Tommy a kick to the ribs and a DDT to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Max Cameron has won the match!
Ricky Simms - In a fine match that could have gone to any of these men, Max gets the win. Henry Lesher - And I lose my faith in mankind a little more every week. Max rolls himself out of the ring and starts to make his way up the ramp while Halloween Jack realizes what happened here this week and he charges up the ramp to start brawling with Max. Security is then forced to come out again as Max and Jack show no signs of stopping their brawl here. We then look backstage where Hector Braun is with Wyatt Gibson in
Wyatt's office.
Wyatt Gibson - Okay, I trust you to book the shows then. I can do other things. Hector Braun - *nods* The meeting went well, you know. Wyatt Gibson - I told you they would take a chance to make some extra cash. There are always the bragging rights to consider as well.
Hector Braun - This roster has a history of not working well with others. Wyatt Gibson - They had better learn, then. Hector Braun - Agreed, this could bring the company back from the brink of bankruptcy. Wyatt Gibson - I have to clean up the Skanes mess and then we'll be rolling in money. Hector Braun - You did a fine job sending them out of here. Wyatt Gibson - There's still a lot of work to be done. I'll be in contact. Hector Braun - Sounds good. Wyatt exits the office and we head to the ring for the main event. Rated R vs. Hoshi Yakamoto "Surfing with the Alien" by Joe Satriani plays as Hoshi Yakamoto starts to make her way towards the ring. Bart
Phillips is close behind as the crowd gives her one of the loudest ovations of the night. She then jumps onto the apron and leaps over the top rope into the ring. She has a few words with referee Casey Braun as her music then fades and we hear "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace and we see Rated R come out to the loudest boos of the night.
Ricky Simms - Halifax's love for Rated R does not extend to the island here. Henry Lesher - I'm sure it's not a big deal. He told me backstage that most of this province has IQs in the single digit range. Half of them still think they are a British colony. Canada took until the mid twentieth century to declare them a province because they lost a bet to the queen on who had to take them.
Ricky Simms - Okay, that's enough out of you. Rated R reaches ringside and regards the fans with disgust before he makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron where Hoshi greets him with a dropkick to the chest that sends him back to the floor. Hoshi then waits for Ryan to stand and then lands a springboard body press onto Rated R that sends him to the floor much to the delight of the crowd.
Hoshi then stands Rated R up and rolls him into the ring before she rolls herself into the ring, allowing the referee to call for the bell and make this match official. Hoshi then stands Rated R up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a dropkick to the mat. She then goes for a split legged moonsault off the top rope only to take raised knees to the chest from Rated R.
Ricky Simms - She shouldn't have gone to the ropes there. Henry Lesher - If she was smart, she wouldn't have shown up for this match at all. Rated R uses the ropes to get to his feet and then he gives Hoshi a series of knife edge chops to the chest that drive her into the corner buckle. Rated R then gives her a series of kicks to the chest that drop her to her knees. He then plants a boot to her throat and uses the ropes to gain added leverage while choking the young star. The crowd starts a chant for Hoshi while Bart Phillips slaps the mat and looks on at the proceedings. Rated R then stands Hoshi up, whips her to the opposite corner and then charges into a standing dropkick from Hoshi that sends him down to the mat.
Hoshi then lands the split legged moonsault onto the fallen Rated R and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R while the crowd groans.
Ricky Simms - She nearly had him there. Henry Lesher - Hoshi does not defeat Rated R. It simply does not happen, not tonight and not ever. Hoshi stands Rated R up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a round house kick to the chest that drive him back to the mat. Hoshi then goes for an elbow smash onto the fallen champ, but he rolls out of the way and then does a kip up to his feet. Hoshi does a kip up as well only to take a vicious clothesline from Rated R that sends her back down to the mat. Rated R then picks Hoshi up over his shoulder and runs her face first into the corner buckle. Rated R then gives Hoshi a power slam to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Hoshi Yakamoto. Rated R then stands
Hoshi up and whips her through the ropes and out to the floor.
Ricky Simms - This doesn't looks good for the fan favorite here. Henry Lesher - It didn't look good for her when she signed on for this match. Rated R rolls himself out of the ring to the floor. He then gives Hoshi a double axe handle smash across the back. He then whips her into the ring spron, which catches her chest and winds her. Rated
R then gives her a side Russian leg sweep to the floor. Rated R then stands
Hoshi up and rolls her into the ring before rolling himself into the ring. Rated
R then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Hoshi. Rated R has a few words with Casey Braun about the speed of her count and then stands Hoshi up where
Hoshi then gives Rated R a series of punches to the gut. She then gives Rated R a knee to the gut as well and then a snap suplex to the mat.
Ricky Simms - A little power shown by Hoshi there. Henry Lesher - Stop cheering for her. Your bias makes me sick. Of course, Rated R is simply better than her in every way. Hoshi goes for the pin on Rated R. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Hoshi then stands Rated R up, whips him off the ropes and ducks her head for a backdrop. Rated R gives her a knee to the face and then tries for a Rock Bottom, but she blocks it and then she gives Rated R a
DDT to the mat. She rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... Rated R gets a foot on the bottom rope and then he pulls himself out to the floor. Hoshi follows and stands Rated R up and whips him towards the corner post. Rated R stops his forward momentum and then whips Hoshi into the steel corner post. He then puts the boots to her while Bart charges around the ring and manages to get between
Ryan and Hoshi. The two of them have words while Hoshi tries to pull herself to her feet. Hoshi then rolls herself into the ring while her husband continues his argument with Rated R. Referee Casey Braun then reaches the ten count and calls for the bell.
Henry Lesher - Well, that's bogus! Ricky Simms - The CWA champ forgot all about the count out when he got into a heated argument with Bart Phillips. Your winner is Hoshi! Henry Lesher - This match should have been without rules. Rated R could have sent Hoshi to not only defeat, but he could have retired her. This is garbage.
Ricky Simms - Well, Hoshi gets the win regardless of what you think. Hearing the judgment, Rated R clotheslines Bart to the floor, then grabs a steel chair and slams it into the ribs of Bart Phillips. He then exits ringside with the chair still in hand while
Hoshi checks on the fallen Bart. Rated R screams at the camera that he is the winner tonight while the crowd boos him.
Ricky Simms - That does it for this week. Tune in next week for another edition of Insurgence!
Henry Lesher - Rated R will kill Hoshi to make a point. This was a fluke. We fade to black on Hoshi checking on Bart and helping him to his feet.
