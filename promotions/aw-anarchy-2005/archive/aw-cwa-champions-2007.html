AW (Anarchy, 2005) — Champions

The Champions of the AW/CWA

undated (captured 27 April 2007)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: The Champions of the AW/CWA The Best of the Best In the AW/CWA Listed Below December 21st, 2005 - The Nameless defeated Daniel McCoy to win the vacant title. February 26th, 2006 - Rated R defeated Nameless using everything but the kitchen sink at Bloodshed.
June 4th, 2006 - Gerard Angelo defeated Rated R at BiTCHSLAP to become champ. July 1st, 2006 - Tommy Purr defeated Gerard Angelo to become AW champ for the first time. September 30th, 2006 - Halloween Jack defeated Tommy Purr to become a champion for the first time.
October 28th, 2006 - Tommy Purr won the title for the second time in a rematch. May 28, 2006 - The Nameless beat Trinity Holmes to win the title in the tournament final. July 1st, 2006 - The Moose defeated The Nameless to win the Alpha title, becoming CWA champ once again.
October 28th, 2006 - Rated R defeated The Moose to become the CWA Alpha champ. December 21st, 2005 - Hoshi Yakamoto defeated Tommy Purr and 6Pac Dudley to win the vacant Xtreme Title.
February 5th, 2006 - Renske defeated Hoshi Yakamoto to take the Xtreme title on
Assault. March 12th, 2006 - Rodd Gordon won a battle royale on Insurgence to win the title. April 9th, 2006 - Spartacus Conway defeated Rodd Gordon for the Xtreme title
May 7, 2006 - Pyro defeated Parker Davis and Rodd Gordon to win the Xtreme title.
June 4th, 2006 - Wren SilverPhoenix defeated Max Cameron and Pyro to become
Xtreme champ. July 1st, 2006 - Krayne defeated Wren SilverPhoenix to become the new Xtreme champ. July 22nd, 2006 - 6Pac Dudley wins Open Challenge for the Xtreme title. September 2nd, 2006 - Hoshi Yakamoto defeats 6Pac Dudley, Pyro and Psycho Soldier in a four corners match for the Xtreme title. October 7th, 2006 - Frank Merritt defeated Hoshi Yakamoto to become Xtreme champ, first time champion with the company.
October 28th, 2006 - Hoshi Yakamoto becomes a three time champ by pinning Frank
Merritt. January 2006 - Myst February 2006 - Dwayne Irving Carter Kingston March 2006 - No PPV for March. April 2006 - Dragon May 2006 - Simon June 2006 - 6Pac Dudley July 2006 - 6Pac Dudley August 2006 - Reyna Preciosa September 2006 - Jack Spade October 2006 - Gerard Angelo November 2006 - Hoshi Yakamoto January 18th, 2006 - Max Cameron won a Battle Royale at BOTW 2006 to become the first Superior champ.
February 12th, 2006 - Bombshell defeated Max Cameron and Hoshi Yakamoto to become Superior champ.
April 9th, 2006 - Diego Alvarez defeated Bombshell on Insurgence to become
Superior champ. June 17th, 2006 - The Moose defeated Diego Alvarez to become Superior champ. July 1st, 2006 - Title Dissolved when Moose won Alpha title February 5th, 2006 - Aftermath and Cameron Jacques won an Open Challenge to win the title on Assault.
February 12th, 2006 - Air Canada defeated Circus of Pain to win vacated Tag Team titles. April 2nd, 2006 - De'Anton Maybon and Parker Davis defeat Air Canada, now knows as Total Darkness.
June 4th, 2006 - S.E.X. won a tag team Gauntlet to become the new tag champs. June 24th, 2006 - Delirium won an open challenge to become the new tag team champions. September 2nd, 2006 - New Age Dogg Pound wins a triple threat match for the titles. September 30th - Vigilante and Moose win a four corners tags match to become the new champs.
