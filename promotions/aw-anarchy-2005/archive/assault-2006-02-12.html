AW Assault, 12 February 2006

12 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Assault - February 12th, 2006 It's a packed house at the Savvis Centre as we get set for another edition of Assault. We look backstage and find Vesuvius in his office when Max Cameron is called in. Vesuvius throws down GWO jackets.
Vesuvius - What's this shit? Max Cameron - *smiles* We could start with theft. Vesuvius - Youthful Aggression, your whole pathetic group.... is hereby released from your AW contracts. Your titles will be fought over by others tonight and you will be watching from home.
Max Cameron - *leans in close* You know what? I bled GWO for a year only because it was the old CWA. You are running the old CWA. I will be back someday and, when that happens, I'll see you inside the ring.
Max then spits in Vesuvius' face before leaving his office with Malice, Cameron Jacques and Aftermath right behind him. They take their title belts with them and nearby security guards are clearly unwilling to risk their health to get the titles back. The door slams behind them as they leave. We cut backstage to YPM’s locker room as Hoshi is playing around with her long hair ahead of her big title match, whilst Tanja’s listening on her iPod.
Hoshi: Tanja, you mind leaving while I get changed? Tanja: Sure. Tanja leaves as Hoshi takes off her shirt as we can hear the male fans go crazy. The camera is behind her, so there’s no trouble with the censor. We hear a door slam.
Hoshi: Hey, baby! Listen about the match tonight. Hoshi turns around, this time the camera focuses from the shoulder up. Hoshi screams as AW referee Steve Blake is stood there, his jaw drops. Hoshi scrambles and covers her self with her shirt.
Hoshi: Steve! Knock next time! Steve Blake: Sorry Mrs. Phillips. Hoshi: That’s alright. Hoshi puts her shirt back on, back turned to Steve. Hoshi: So, why did you come round? Social call? Steve Blake: I came to apologize. Hoshi: For what? Steve Blake: For not doing my best in your match last week. I watched the tape, and…I feel awful.
Hoshi: Oh, don’t worry. You were incapacitated. Trust me, you did what you had to do. Steve Blake: I appreciate the kindness, it’s just… Hoshi: It’s nothin’. Steven, you’re a hell of referee. I’m okay. Really I am. Don’t beat yourself up about it, ‘kay bud?
Steve Blake: Alright. Thanks Mrs. Phillips. Hoshi: It’s Hoshi. Steve Blake: Right. Oh, and tell Bart he’s a lucky son of a bitch. Steve leaves as Hoshi turns around confused. Hoshi: Will do. We then see the opening credits with images of Nameless, Myst, Rated R, Gerard Angelo, Jake Matthews, Tommy Purr with Bombshell, Hoshi Yakamoto, 6Pac Dudley, Scavenger and Diego Alvarez while "Take Me Out" by Franz Ferdinand echoes in the background. We then head to ringside where Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher are all set to call the action.
Air Canada vs Circus of Pain "Born To Burn" by Static X starts to play and "Mad Dog" McNulty and Gothic come out brawling with FUBU and Lightning. The crowd cheers as they all trade punches as they make their way down the aisle towards the ring. Gothic then slams FUBU's head into the ring steps before rolling him into the ring. McNulty and Lightning continue brawling their way around the ring. Gothic rolls himself into the ring and then starts putting the boots to FUBU.
Ricky Simms - These two teams are battling for the now vacant gold and apparently started brawling as soon as they heard YA was gone.
Henry Lesher - This company is the greatest, huh? Last one out, turn out the lights. Gothic stands FUBU up, whips him into the corner and then snap mares him out of it and back towards the middle of the ring. Gothic then climbs to the second rope and misses with a clothesline from there as FUBU rolls out of the way and then rolls himself out of the ring. FUBU makes his way around the ring with Gothic in tow. McNulty picks up a chair to smash Lightning over the head with as FUBU passes. Lightning ducks the chair shot and McNulty instead crowns Gothic. Lightning then gives McNulty a jawbreaker as FUBU turns back and now rolls Gothic into the ring.
Ricky Simms - McNulty drilled his partner there! Henry Lesher - Thanks for last week's news Rick. FUBU climbs to the top rope and looks out to the crowd and gives the thumbs up. He then turns back towards the ring and launches himself for the CN Tower Bomb and goes for the pin, even while Lightning tosses McNulty over the guard railing and into the crowd. 1...2...3! Air Canada are the new AW tag team champions!
Ricky Simms - The tag team gold wears white and red tonight! Henry Lesher - My will to live takes another hit. As the tag team titles are handed to Lightning and FUBU, we take a look backstage where Myst is entering the building. There she is headed off by Shannon Skanes.
Shannon Skanes - Can we talk? Myst - About what? Shannon Skanes - Our son came to me last week and said you had something to do with what happened to the GWO. Is he telling me the truth?
Myst - *looks uneasy* Our son said that? Look, honey, you know I love you. I'll talk to Noah and clear this up.
Shannon Skanes - Why would he think that? Myst - That's what I will clear up for him. She then hurries off and Shannon looks at her, appearing to be both baffled and suspicious.
Bombshell vs Hoshi Yakamoto "I Am the Body Beautiful" by Salt N Pepa starts to play as Bombshell makes her way out from the back to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Gerard Angelo follows close behind her. They talk strategy as they make their way down the aisle, fully aware of what is on the line here tonight, now that Max Cameron has been let go from the company.
Ricky Simms - This is a huge opportunity for Bombshell and Hoshi. One of them will leave the ring as the new Superior champion.
Henry Lesher - Bombshell's association with Tommy makes her winner by proxy. Ricky Simms - I see. Your objectivity never fails to stun me. As Bombshell steps onto the apron and then into the ring, her music fades and we hear "Surfing With the Alien" by Joe Satriani and Hoshi Yakamoto is walked towards the ring by Bart Phillips to a loud ovation from the crowd. Hoshi reaches ringside and signs an autograph for a young fan before she then jumps up onto the apron and then steps through the ropes into the ring. The bell sounds as she and Bombshell lock up in the
middle of the ring. Bombshell shoves Hoshi into the corner. Hoshi then comes out and clotheslines Bombshell to the mat. Hoshi then lands a somersault leg drop across the throat of Bombshell and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Bombshell. Hoshi then stands Bombshell up, whips her off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat. Hoshi then goes to the ropes and makes her way to the top. Hoshi poses there and goes for a flying elbow smash only to have Bombshell roll out of the way. We then see the Anarchy Angels making their way towards the ring.
Ricky Simms - Here come those girls who were scouting Hoshi and Bart's tag match last week.
Henry Lesher - Maybe they have a thing for that stupid Hoshi girl. The Angels take their place at ringside as Bombshell rolls herself out of the ring and talks things over with Gerard Angelo. Hoshi gets to her feet and then runs towards Bombshell and baseball slides under the bottom rope, kicking her into the guard railing. Gerard then says a few things to Hoshi as Bart Phillips makes his way around the ring to confront Gerard. Hoshi, meanwhile, stands Bombshell up and rolls her into the ring. Hoshi then rolls herself into the ring where Bombshell greets her with a small package pin attempt. 1...2... kick out by Hoshi. Bombshell then rolls away from Hoshi and quickly gets to her feet. Hoshi gets to her feet in time to take a round house kick from Bombshell that sends her to the mat. Outside the ring, we see the Anarchy Angels commenting to each other on this match. Bombshell stands Hoshi up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a clothesline to the mat before then giving her a knee drop to the forehead. Bombshell then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Hoshi.
Ricky Simms - Pretty good match going on here and the Angela haven't done anything yet other than observe.
Henry Lesher - That's all most of these wrestlers should do, but it hasn't worked out that way.
Bombshell picks Hoshi up and power slams her to the mat. Again, she goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Hoshi. The dark-haired Anarchy Angel gets to her feet and steps closer to the apron. Bombshell stands Hoshi up, whips her off the ropes and Hoshi ducks a clothesline and then does a hand stand while placing Bombshell into a head scissors and then brings her down to the mat. Hoshi then jumps to her feet and looks towards the crowd for approval when she sees one of the Angels standing too close to the ring for her liking. She asks her what she's doing and Bombshell rolls her up for the pin, complete with a hooking of the tights. 1...2...3! Bombshell is the new Superior champion!
Ricky Simms - The Angel cost Hoshi the match. Henry Lesher - No she didn't. Hoshi allowed herself to be distracted. Bombshell rolls herself out of the ring and embraces Gerard who hands her the title belt. She holds it high as she and Gerard make their way to the back. The Anarchy Angels leave ringside with smiles on their faces as Bart enters the ring and is filled in on what happened by Hoshi.
Ricky Simms - Hoshi's not impressed here. Henry Lesher - No one cares. We then look backstage where Steve Blake has just watched Hoshi lose. He shakes his head and exits his locker room, coming face to face with the Anarchy Angels.
Steve Blake - What do you two have against Hoshi? Ariel - It's not my fault she can't keep her mind on her match. Steve Blake - That's two weeks in a row. Athena - Steve, you're clearly stressed. Go have a drink and settle yourself down. Steve Blake - What do you girls do anyway? Ariel - *smiles* At Bloodbath, everyone will find out what we do. We do it well, too. The two laugh to themselves as they walk away and Steve Blake look on concerned. He then turns and comes face to face with Jason Knight.
Jason Knight - Steve, you're a referee. Why are you bothering my talent? Steve Blake - I don't like what happened to Hoshi. Jason Knight - Then talk to Francis Clarke. He called the match. Don't bother Ariel or Athena about it. They're my friends. People who bother my friends.... well, most of them don't work here anymore. You understand?
Steve Blake - *lowers his head* Yeah, I got it. Jason Knight - Just be glad you have a job. Rizz vs 6Pac Dudley "Southtown" by POD plays as 6Pac Dudley starts making his way towards the ring, a smile on his face. He tells the camera more gold will be coming to Canada soon. He then runs the rest of the way to the ring while the crowd gives him a warm reception. He poses on the second rope inside the ring as his music fades and we hear "Her Portrait in Black" by Atreyu and Rizz is led by Bonnie towards the ring. The crowd gives him a mixed response and 6Pac taunts him from the corner where he is still perched on the second rope. Rizz rolls himself into the ring and has a few words for 6 Pac. 6Pac jumps off the ropes and stands nose to nose with Rizz as the bell sounds to get things underway. Rizz and 6Pac start
trading punches and 6Pac eye gouges Rizz before giving him an arm drag into the corner. 6Pac then stands Rizz up and slams his head into the corner buckle. Rizz answers by slamming 6Pac's head into the corner buckle and then rakes his eyes along the top rope before giving him a side Russian leg sweep to the mat where Rizz then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac.
Ricky Simms - Good starts to this match as these two jockey for position and a shot at some gold here in AW.
Henry Lesher - I really don't care. I try and yet I don't care. Rizz stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Rizz then lands a knee drop to the forehead on 6Pac and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac. Rizz stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. Referee Mark Davis starts counting them both down. On the four count, Rizz rolls over and starts pulling himself up to his feet. On the six count, 6Pac does the same. They then start trading knife edge chops from their knees and that ends the count. Rizz gives 6Pac a forearm upper cut and then head butts him down to the mat where Rizz goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac.
Ricky Simms - Both men impressing well here tonight. Henry Lesher - I have higher standards before I call myself impressed. Rizz gets to his feet and pulls 6Pac up. 6Pac then eye gouges Rizz, whips him off the ropes and gives him a drop toe to the mat where he then applies a camel clutch. Referee Mark Davis checks for a submission as the crowd gets behind 6Pac in this fight. Rizz refuses to submit and starts trying to pull himself up from the mat. As pushes up to his knees and 6Pac releases the hold, then stands Rizz up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Rizz rolls himself out of the ring to regroup and 6Pac follows him.
Ricky Simms - Now they're taking the fight to the floor. Henry Lesher - If only one of them had talent, I might care. 6Pac and Rizz start trading punches at ringside as referee Mark Davis starts the count. 6Pac hip tosses Rizz to the floor and then pulls him to the announce table. 6Pac sets Rizz on the announce table and then climbs onto the apron, turns and dives for a splash onto Rizz only to find no one home as Rizz rolls out of the way. Referee Mark Davis reaches a ten count and calls for the bell, declaring this match to be a draw.
Ricky Simms - Both men counted out and we now have no table. Henry Lesher - Idiots.... they're like children you don't take anywhere. If you're going to break the damned table, at least impress me when you do it.
We then look backstage to Vesuvius' office. He is looking through contracts when Jason Knight walks in.
Jason Knight - Going over things here, boss? Vesuvius - Shelia Lavin signed some contracts while you were off in January and she signed one I haven't found yet.
Jason Knight - I figured she was hinting at Clemenzo. Vesuvius comes across a contract and his eyes widen. Vesuvius - No, not him... Jason Knight steps around the desk and looks at the name and shakes his head. Jason Knight - Now that's a person we DON'T need here. Tommy Time The crowd is quietly chatting amongst themselves as we hear the tickle of unfamiliar piano keys throughout the audience … the arena blacks out as Tommy Purr’s entrance video begins to play. As the keys stop playing, we hear the roar of a wild cat and then Jack Off Jill’s “When I Am Queen” kicks into high gear as the crowd is on its feet. A white spotlight shines on Tommy as he makes his way out onto the stage, flanked by Mrs. Robinson … the arena lightens up a bit as violet and blue-colored lights began swirling around the arena. Tommy points out to the crowd and makes his way to the ring … which is set up for the return of “Tommy Time.” The ring is set up with the usual black and white zebra-print carpet, black leather couches, glass coffee table, and flat-screen video player. As the arena returns to normal, Tommy is handed a microphone and begins addressing the crowd as his music fades out.
Tommy Purr: Welcome, ladies and gentlemen, to the RETURN OF “TOMMY TIME”!!! The crowd cheers as Tommy soaks up the spotlight. Tommy Purr: Now, as per usual, Tommy will be asking all of the question you want to ask … Tommy will be getting all the answers that you want answered … Tommy will be the one to deliver all of the hard-hitting news that all of you people want to know and hear. So, before I initiate Jake Matthews into The
Tommy knocker Academy Hall of Fame … beating him within an inch of his miserable, back-talking, pathetic life in this very ring … allow me to introduce a man who has made a living out of beating the miserable and the pathetic … a man who has delivered his fair share of wrestling moments … a man who you’ve cheered AND jeered … former G.W.O. Canadian Champion … former G.W.O. World Heavyweight Champion … and one of the leading members of the now-defunct and BEST VERSION of Youthful Aggression … “the Hollywood Hero” … GERARD ANGELO!!!
The crowd greets Gerard with a mixed reaction … 75% cheers, 25% boos … as “Jamestown” by Movielife begins to play throughout the arena. Gerry comes out to the ring dressed really casually as Tommy and Catherine applaud in the ring. Gerry makes his way to the ring before shaking hands with Tommy and having his music fade out. Catherine hands Gerry a microphone.
Tommy Purr: Gerry, even though we’ve been friends for a long time now, I do believe this is the first time that you’ve ever had the honor and privilege of being a featured guest here on “Tommy Time.”
Gerard Angelo: I think you’re right Tommy … and it truly is an honor to be the focus of attention here tonight.
Tommy Purr: Well … with that being said … let’s just get straight to the point … as we all the Internet has become a useful tool for researching and sending information. Earlier this week, a rumor broke out on www.Rajah.com that stated that you, Gerry, were planning on retiring. Everyone is wanting to know, Gerry … is this true? Is “the Hollywood Hero” really retiring from the squared-circle?
Gerard Angelo: *Solemnly* I have to admit, Tommy … yes … it’s true … Gerard Angelo is retiring from wrestling.
This news is rather shocking and the crowd is making their opinions heard. Tommy Purr: But … why? Gerard Angelo: I just feel completely stuck … I’ve been losing almost every week, Tommy … and after a while, you just need to step back and ask yourself what it’s all for.
Henry Lesher: Retirement is for losers.... so Gerry's the right guy to retire then. Ricky Simms: Would you stay quiet so we can listen to what the man has to say? Henry Lesher: There will be plenty of time to remain quiet when I’m placed in a boredom-induced coma.
Gerard Angelo: I just feel at this point in time it’s good to bow out … before things start getting really ugly around here.
Tommy Purr: You mean between Ryan Yorke and Vesuvius? Gerard Angelo: Hey, you said it … not me. Tommy Purr: Proof that this war that Vesuvius and Ryan have started has already damaged the very fabric of this great promotion. The fact that this feud has now caused you to retire, Gerry, is just ridiculous. Losing is one thing, Gerry … I can help you fix that. We’re buds, Gerry … practically brothers … and this feud doesn’t even involve us; to allow it to force you to retire is just plain ludicrous and just goes to show that the people in management don’t give a flying f—
“She Sells Sanctuary” by The Cult begins to play as the arena is filled with a chorus of boos. Jason Knight makes his way out onto the stage, a microphone in hand and an unsatisfied look on his face. Knight angrily steps into the ring and stands nose-to-nose with Tommy!
Jason Knight: CUT THE MUSIC!!! The music fades out as the boos get louder. Jason Knight: You know what, Tommy Purr, I’m getting really sick and tired of you coming out here and spreading your “make love, not war” propaganda. You talk about this “war” as if you know everything that’s truly going on … as if you know what’s truly on the line or how high the stakes are.
Tommy Purr: Oh, trust me, I know how high the stakes are … the fact that innocent people’s jobs are on the line depending on where they stand tells me all I need to know.
Jason Knight: And what makes you think that you’re safe? Answer me that, Nancy Drew! You like to get to the bottom of things? Dig down for all of the answers and the “hard-hitting truth”? Well, the “hard-hitting truth” is this … you’re either with us, or against us … it’s as simple as black and white, and there isn’t any gray in between. Think of it like this … if you’re not with Vesuvius, we’ll make an example out of you … and your little “family” … just to show Ryan and his band of misfits just how serious we are in taking them down.
Tommy Purr: Jason … I’m not scared of Ryan … and I’m not scared of Vesuvius … what makes you think YOU are going to be any different you bald, albino, moron!
The crowd cheers as Tommy and Knight stare one another down … Gerry and Catherine looks concerned. Knight smirks as he stares at Tommy.
Jason Knight: The only thing you have to do to save yourself Tommy … is to choose a side … that’s all anyone wants from you; and quite frankly … if you can’t MAKE a decision, perhaps I can help SPEED UP the process …
Tommy looks confused and then suddenly Gerry attacks Tommy from behind with a Clothesline! Tommy falls forward to the ground as Gerry and Knight begin stomping on him! The crowd boos uncontrollably.
Ricky Simms: I can’t believe this!!! Henry Lesher: Gerry turned on Tommy Purr … doesn’t he know that’s against the rules!?!? Ricky Simms: Gerry attacking the very man he’s come to know as his brother!! Catherine begins shrieking and jumps on Knight’s back, choking him from behind with a Body Scissors. Knight reaches up and grabs Catherine by the hair and flips her forward onto her back. As Catherine gets back to her feet, her high-heels falling off to the side, Gerry knocks her down and out with a
Super kick! Ricky Simms: This is not right … not right at all!! Gerry knocking out Mrs. Robinson when all she’s doing is trying to protect her stepson!
Henry Lesher: I’m guessing Gerry has sided with Vesuvius???! Gerry grabs Tommy by the head and sets him up for a Power bomb. The crowd chants “asshole, asshole” as Knight sticks his thumb up in the air and then points it downward! Gerry viciously lifts up Tommy and snaps Tommy down with a
Power bomb right through his glass coffee table! “Holy shit!” chants begin spreading around the arena, along with a number of boos as Gerry picks his microphone back up.
Gerard Angelo: You know, Tommy … I wasn’t lying before … Gerard Angelo IS retiring … the OLD Gerard Angelo … the OLD Gerry who kept losing … the OLD Gerry that actually gave a damn what people like YOU and your slut stepmother thought! The OLD Gerry is long gone … “the Hollywood Hero” is no more … now you’re looking at Gerard Angelo … “the Anarchy Hero.”
Knight raises Gerry’s arm in victory as the crowd boos unmercifully. The crowd cheers as an incensed Bombshell comes sprinting down to the ring. Bombshell runs into the ring and goes for the cut-open Tommy … doing her best to see if he’s okay. Bombshell glares at Knight and Gerry as they laugh mockingly at her. As Knight and Gerry leave the ring, “Jamestown” begins to play again and Bombshell goes over to help up a dazed Catherine. EMT’s push past Knight and Gerry as they back up the ramp. The EMT’s rush into the ring to check on Tommy as he seems scratched up from the glass coffee table. Bombshell begins cussing up a storm and screaming, “IT’S OVER GERRY!!!”
Ricky Simms: This shocking … shocking … turn of events … Gerry putting Tommy through a glass table in the middle of the ring!
Henry Lesher: I’m at a loss for words myself … I mean … I’m impressed with Gerry … the balls, the courage … and he’s siding with Vesuvius … but … poor Tommy; he should just side with Vesuvius before he gets his ass handed to him again.
Ricky Simms: I thought you liked Tommy? Henry Lesher: I do, that’s why he should side with Vesuvius, so he doesn’t get hurt again … for his sake at least.
Ricky Simms: One has to wonder how this is going to affect Tommy’s match later in the night against Jake Matthews.
The EMT’s, with Catherine and Bombshell, carry Tommy to the back on a stretcher; he’s wide awake yet slightly confused as to what happened. Once the EMT’s make it backstage, Tommy begins pushing himself off of the stretcher.
Bombshell: Tommy, try not to move! Tommy Purr: Yvonne … I’ll be fine … just a few scratches. Mrs. Robinson: Thomas! Lie down, allow the EMT’s to help you. Tommy Purr: This is nothing I haven’t been through before … just a table made of glass … Tommy gets up onto his feet and winces slightly, his back a little sore. Bombshell: Tommy, I don’t think you should go through this match with Matthews! Tommy Purr: Yvonne, quit worrying … I can’t let Knight get one over on me; I’m more emotionally wounded than anything else … how could Gerry do that to me? How could Gerry do this to YOU?
Bombshell: *Upset* I feel horribly, Tommy … I should’ve been out there sooner, I should’ve gone with you to the ring.
Mrs. Robinson: You had no idea what your boyfriend … Bombshell: EX-BOYFRIEND!!! Mrs. Robinson: I stand corrected … you had no idea what your ex-boyfriend was capable of. It’s not your fault … you were freshening up after you match; no one is pointing the blame at you.
Bombshell: I just feel horrible, perhaps I could’ve calmed Gerry down before he did this to Tommy … and Catherine, it should’ve been me who took that
Super kick, not you. Mrs. Robinson: Darling, I’ve felt worse … besides, Gerry always did hit like a girl, no wonder why he always loses his match.
Bombshell: Tommy, Cat, I swear I’m going to rectify this— Clapping is heard off camera as Tommy looks sympathetically at Bombshell. The trio turn and glare at Vesuvius as he steps into the shot, grinning from ear-to-ear.
Tommy Purr: What the hell do YOU want? Vesuvius: Oh nothing … just enjoying this tender Hallmark moment. Hey, I just wanted to let you know, Tommy, that scratches or no scratches … injured or in perfect health … you’re STILL wrestling against Jake tonight.
Tommy Purr: As if I was expecting any special treatment from you. Vesuvius: That’s good, Tommy; it’s nice to see that you have low expectations … [Looking at the Divas] … given the crowd you hang around with. I bet you didn’t expect to hear this also … I’ve made a change to your match with Matthews. Catherine and Bombshell are BOTH barred from ringside during the course of the match … and should anyone try to interfere on your behalf, I’ll fire your ass in a heartbeat. Have a good match!
Vesuvius continues grinning as he walks off. Bombshell and Catherine are both left stunned as Tommy looks a little worried.
Scavenger vs D.I.C.K. "Tear in My Beer" by Hank Williams Sr. plays as Dwayne Irving Carter Kingston steps out from the back and tips his hat to the camera. He has a big grin on his face and says to the camera that tonight marks another win for the wooly bully. He then reaches ringside and looks around at the thousands of fans here to watch Assault tonight and seems impressed as they give him a mixed reaction.
Henry Lesher - I really like this guy a lot. Ricky Simms - I can imagine you do. Kingston steps onto the apron and into the ring as his music fades and then we hear "Herzeleid" by Rammstein and we see Scavenger on his way out from the back, Lauren close behind him. He quickly makes his way to the ring, jumps onto the apron and takes a dropkick from Kingston that sends him back to the floor. Kingston then rolls himself out of the ring and starts putting the boots to Scavenger while Lauren looks on and shakes her head. Kingston then stands Scavenger up and rolls him into the ring before rolling himself into the ring. Referee Steve Blake then calls for the bell to get the match officially started. Kingston stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and knees him in the gut, doubling him over where he then delivers a swinging
neck breaker. Kingston then goes for the pin. 1...2... Scavenger rolls a shoulder out. Kingston stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him back down to the mat. Kingston then goes to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and lands a double ax handle smash across the back of Scavenger, then rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger.
Ricky Simms - Kingston is really taking it to Scavenger here this week. Henry Lesher - That's because Scavenger is second rate. Rumor has it Vesuvius has a replacement for him within the Anarchy group.
Kingston stands Scavenger up, goes for a whip off the ropes only to have Scavenger reverse it and then Scavenger gives Kingston a kick to the gut followed by a German suplex. He then stands Kingston up and gives him a second German suplex to the mat. Scavenger then holds him down with a back bridge and attempts the pin. 1...2... Kingston kicks out. Scavenger then leaps to his feet, then stands Kingston up and whips him into the corner buckle. Scavenger then slams Kingston face first into the corner buckle and then gives him a reverse neck breaker. Scavenger then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kingston.
Ricky Simms - Scavenger not quite able to put Kingston away here. Henry Lesher - That's because he sucks. Scavenger stands Kingston up, whips him off the ropes and misses with a clothesline as Kingston ducks it. Kingston then gives Scavenger a gut wrench suplex to the mat. Kingston then rolls himself out of the ring and grabs his cowboy hat off of the ring steps and puts it on, then poses once for the camera as Scavenger rolls himself out of the ring. Fed up with Kingston, Scavenger grabs a chair from one of the fans, folds it and clubs Kingston across the back, causing the referee to call for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Scavenger has been disqualified! Henry Lesher - He's allergic to success. Scavenger rolls himself into the ring, chair in hand. The crowd cheers him while the referee raises Kingston's arm and he is named the winner. Halloween Jack rolls himself into the ring and chop blocks Scavenger to the mat, grabs the chair away from him and drills him in the ribs with it. Jack then rolls himself out of the ring and exits through the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Halloween Jack attacked a man here who is supposed to be his ally. Of course, he won a match earlier tonight of the dark match variety. He's been doing quite well since Battle of the Warriors.
Henry Lesher - He has direction and success. Scavenger has a chair and is an idiot. On a good note, Kingston is still undefeated!
Ricky Simms - He is proving to be quite a handful for the AW roster to handle. Backstage we see Diego Alvarez entering Jason Knight's office. Jason Knight - Diego, what's on your mind? Diego Alvarez - Last week I almost won the tag team titles by myself. This week I won my match and it wasn't on TV.
Jason Knight - What's your point? Diego Alvarez - Bloodbath is in two weeks. I'm not sticking around the opening match. Jason Knight - *smiles* You know what? I agree with you.
