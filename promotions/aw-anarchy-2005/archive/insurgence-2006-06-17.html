AW Insurgence, 17 June 2006

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 17, 2006 It's a capacity crowd in Winnipeg, Manitoba for the latest edition of Insurgence. We hear "The Hand That Feeds" by Nine Inch Nails and we see Shannon Skanes making his way towards the ring, a microphone in hand and he doesn't look impressed. He stops at ringside and looks around at the three thousand plus fans all set to listen to him and he nods his head once before he then steps onto the apron and then into the ring.
Shannon Skanes - Sometimes..... sometimes it sucks being me. See, this past couple of weeks saw the departure of Dwayne
Kingston. People come and go all the time, but this one left kicking and screaming to anyone who would listen. This comes less than a month after the same thing happened with Simon. Now, this left me very upset and wondering... am
I the right man to be leader? He pauses and the crowd starts a CWA chant. He just silently nods, but doesn't seem to be affected by their words. Shannon Skanes - See, last week was hard on a few people. Let's talk a little bit about that. When someone goes out screaming, all eyes are on me. How will Shannon handle it? Will he close this company up again? Is this the end for the venerable CWA before it really got going again? I really thought maybe it was time to get out of this business for good.
He lowers the microphone and the crowd boos the idea of losing this company and their chance to see these wrestlers all together again. Shannon smiles.
Shannon Skanes - Of course, that would mean letting the assholes win, wouldn't it? See, tonight is about giving the entire wrestling world outside of our little company... our clique if you will, the finger. Tommy Purr, come on out here.
"Walk Idiot Walk" by The Hives plays as Tommy Purr comes out and makes his way towards the ring, the crowd cheering him on. He looks tired and a little worse for wear as he steps through the ropes and into the ring.
Tommy Purr - What do you want? Shannon Skanes - Tommy, a lot was said over the past seven days and I want to take the opportunity to say thank you. Tommy Purr - You're thanking me? Shannon Skanes - See, leading this company is no easy task and there are always sharks circling and watching, trying to make sure you cannot succeed. You succeeded and for that I thank you.
Tommy Purr - What's the catch? Shannon Skanes - Oh there is no catch. I just want you to know the work you did last week in allowing me to focus on Terror in the Aisles was admirable. No matter how well you do, there will always be detractors from what you have done and I'm not going to do that here tonight.
Tommy shakes hands with Shannon, but clearly isn't comfortable being out here. He is looking around as if anticipating an attack from somewhere in the crowd.
Shannon Skanes - You're free to go now, Tommy. I have said what I came here to say to you. Tommy then turns and leaves the ring, still looking for an attacker coming from somewhere. He even looks under the apron after leaving the ring, then heads to the back.
Shannon Skanes - See that? There's more to this company than run ins. There's more to this company than a clique. There's more to this company than anyone can take down, whether they try screaming to the media about it or whether they try to mirror our company and steal whatever they can get from there. See, I have something to say to those people. I am the hardest working son of a bitch in this business and you're going to have to work twice as hard as I do just to catch up with me. I have been in this business since 1984 and no one will ever run me out of it again. I may have a few rude and obnoxious people in that locker room, but we're willing to go to war together and we're willing to do whatever it takes to make this company great. How many of you have that going for you? I can answer that for you. Not a damned one of you do. Now watch as another edition of Insurgence beats the shit out of anything your sorry asses can produce!
Shannon tosses the microphone down and the crowd cheers as he then leaves the ring and heads for the back. We then go to the opening credits with the song "Coming Undone" by Korn playing as we see images of Gerard Angelo, Rated R, Max Cameron, Freak, Himself, Leech, Slim
Pickens, Wren SilverPhoenix and 6Pac Dudley before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Ricky Simms are all set to call the action this week. Ricky Simms - That was quite the speech. Henry Lesher - He's just covering his ass. Everyone knows this company is going down the tubes. Ricky Simms - Well I heard Dustin Walters has turned into Tom Zenk about this place. Henry Lesher - In your eyes, that's how it is. I see Tommy against another inferior opponent. Tommy and Slim trade rights and lefts with Slim backing Tommy into the corner. Slim then gives Tommy a knee to the gut and then a double under hook suplex to the mat.
Slim then applies a sleeper hold on Tommy. Tommy then snap mares Slim to the mat in order to break the hold and then applies a sleeper of his own. Slim snags the bottom rope with his hand to force the break and then rolls out to the floor to regroup. Tommy gets to his feet and opts to stay inside the ring. Slim paces for a few moments and then makes his way up the ring steps and then steps into the ring. Tommy moves in and takes an eye gouge from Slim. Slim then whips Tommy into the corner buckle and then gives him a shoulder block there. Slim follows that up with a spine buster on Tommy and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tommy Purr.
Ricky Simms - Slim is really doing well here. Henry Lesher - That won't last. It's Slim. Slim stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and ducks his head for a backdrop. Tommy instead gives Slim a kick to the face and then gives him a side Russian leg sweep to the mat. Tommy then goes to the ropes and makes his way to the top.
Slim gets to his feet, turns and takes a missile dropkick from the top by Tommy Purr. Tommy goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Slim Pickens as the crowd start a chant for him. Tommy stands Slim up, whips him into the corner and then moves in for a clothesline on Slim, but Slim moves out of the way and then sends
Mark Davis down to the mat with an accidental shoulder block. Mrs. Robinson then climbs onto the apron and calls to Slim. He turns and blocks a handful of salt thrown by Robinson. He then clotheslines her off of the apron and then turns and takes a low blow from Tommy who then quickly delivers the Tommy Knocker on Slim and then goes for the pin while a groggy referee comes to. 1...2...3! Tommy gets the win this week, partly due to Mrs. Robinson. Tommy then exits the ring and helps Mrs. Robinson to her feet.
Ricky Simms - Tommy gets the win, though I don't like how he got it. Henry Lesher - No one actually cares what you think. As Tommy leaves the ring victorious, we look outside where Noah Skanes is seen with car keys in his hand. He walks upon Wren who is destroying a black Porsche with a two by four. He looks shocked. Wren smiles.
Wren SilverPhoenix - See this? This is what you get for taking my match away from me. Your precious car ruined. Noah Skanes - Boy, this is really great work, but.... Max Cameron - MY CAR!!!! Wren SilverPhoenix - Dammit.... Wren takes off running with Max close behind. Noah then moves along to the next parking space where his red Viper is waiting for him. He whistles to himself as he starts his car and then drives off.
Rated R vs. Gerard Angelo "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace plays as Rated R starts for the ring. He pauses at the entrance while the fans cheer him. He shakes his head and looks at the cage now sitting on the ring. He then continues his walk to ringside and then pulls at the wiring on the cage as if to see how strong it is. He then makes his way inside the door and paces inside the cage. "Ink" by Finch plays and we see Gerard Angelo coming out, the AW title belt over his shoulder. He hears the jeers of the crowd as he makes his way towards the cage and Rated R waits patiently in the corner furthest from the door to the cage.
Ricky Simms - We have reached the main event, a rematch from the big upset two weeks ago! Henry Lesher - I wasn't upset. Ryan is not the man he was a year ago. Gerard steps into the cage and both men stand face to face, then they both look around as they realize the bell hasn't sounded yet and there in no referee. We then hear "The Hand That Feeds" by Nine Inch Nails and we see Shannon Skanes coming out towards the ring. He has a referee's shirt on and both Rated R and Gerard
Angelo look upset with this decision. Shannon steps into the cage and calls for the bell. Rated R and Gerard start trading rights and lefts. Rated R whips
Gerard off the ropes and then gives him a dropkick to the mat. Rated R then stands Gerard up and slams his face into the cage before giving him a German suplex to the mat. Rated R then gives Gerard a second German suplex and then holds his shoulders down for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard.
Ricky Simms - Ryan in control of this one so far. Henry Lesher - Won't last. He's not the man he once was. Rated R stands Gerard up and whips him face first into the cage. Rated R then picks Gerard up and power slams him to the mat. Rated R then goes for the pin a second time. 1...2... kick out by Gerard. Rated R slaps the mat and accuses Shannon of a slow count. Shannon holds up two fingers and shrugs. Rated R gives the owner the finger and then pulls Gerard to his feet and whips him into the corner.
Rated R goes for a shoulder block on Gerard, but Gerard raises his knees and then gives Rated R a clothesline to the mat and then Gerard gives him a knee to the gut followed by a series of elbows to the forehead. Gerard then two handed chokes Rated R on the mat while Shannon looks on and doesn't start a five count.
Ricky Simms - Why is Shannon just watching this? Henry Lesher - Maybe he's tired of the wimp that Rated R is, too. Gerard releases the choke, stands Rated R up and whips him off the ropes. He clotheslines Rated R down to the mat and then heads to the ropes. Gerard climbs to the top rope. He leaps off for a five star frog splash, but Rated R rolls out of the way and then pulls the feet out from under Shannon Skanes. Rated R starts wailing away on the AW co-owner and then stands Shannon up and whips him face first into the steel mesh of the cage, then gives him a DDT to the mat. Rated R then steps out of the cage to the floor and grabs the steel ring steps. Gerard holds Shannon steady and Rated R clobbers the owner across the skull with the steps. Gerard smiles and looks to high five Rated R, but Rated R gives him a shot with the steps as well. Rated R then motions to Kosmic Kitty for the microphone and she tosses it in the door to him.
Rated R - See, this is a big day for me and this bastard was planning to ruin it. Shannon, you had better play your cards right. I have an offer from another company, one you found when you looked through MY stuff. That's right, your daughter let me know what was going on. You want to go through my stuff? Go to hell. As far as I am concerned, every damned wrestler in AW can go to hell and I might as well tell these fans the same thing. I had you all behind me for months and that didn't stop this hack Gerry from taking my world title. I am the champion dammit!
I will not allow anyone to stand in my way! I don't give a damn about anyone anymore except Sam. She's the only one who has understood.
The camera shows Samantha is smiling, but it is a nervous smile. She doesn't appear to have had any idea what was coming here tonight.
Rated R - I will tell you this. If I am not given the respect I damned well deserve as the greatest star this company or any company has ever seen from this moment forward, I'll be gone somewhere else by July 1st. Make wise choices, Shannon or you'll see this meal ticket in the Bad Lands!
Rated R throws the microphone down and the crowd boos him as he gives both Gerard and Shannon shots to the head with the ring steps. He then exits the ring and starts towards the back while EMTs rush to the ring to attend to the fallen inside the ring.
Henry Lesher - Shannon's a loser of an owner. Ricky Simms - I can't believe what we just saw. Henry Lesher - I can. Ryan is back!!! Rated R poses in the entranceway as the crowd boos him louder with each passing moment. Rated R seems to revel in it and bows to them before he disappears into the back.
