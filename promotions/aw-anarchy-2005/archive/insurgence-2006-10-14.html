AW Insurgence, 14 October 2006

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 14th, 2006 from Osaka, Japan Backstage we see Shannon Skanes entering the building when he is headed off by Wyatt Gibson.
Wyatt Gibson - I don't think you should be here tonight. Shannon Skanes - You know what, Wyatt? It was kinda cute when you were all assertive with me in the beginning.
Now.... you're risking your job and your physical well being. Get out of my way. Wyatt Gibson - I run the show. Shannon Skanes - My son runs the company. Now move. Shannon shoves Wyatt out of the way and makes his way down the corridor while we go to the opening credits to the rune of "Coming Undone" by Korn with images of Moose, Vigilante,
Tommy Purr, Gerard Angelo, Nameless, Rated R, Halloween Jack and Vesuvius before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Ricky Simms are set to call the action. Frank Merritt vs. Phoenix "The Last Day On Earth" by Marilyn Manson plays as Phoenix comes out to a decent pop from the crowd. He high fives a few fans as he makes his way down the ramp, but as he reaches ringside, he is attacked from behind by Frank Merritt who gives him a series of double axe handle smashes across the back. Frank then whips Phoenix shoulder first into the ring steps and then rolls him into the ring. Frank then rolls himself into the ring and referee
Bruce Miller calls for the bell. Ricky Simms - It looks like Phoenix's attempt to prove himself in AW is meeting the brick wall this week.
Henry Lesher - Frank is a monster and no one who challenges him really has much of a chance in there.
Frank stands Phoenix up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a spear to the mat and goes for the pin.
1...2... Phoenix rolls a shoulder out and the crowd starts a chant for the young star. Frank stands Phoenix up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a series of shoulder blocks in the corner. Frank then whips Phoenix into the opposite corner. Phoenix staggers out of the corner and then does a face plant to the mat.
Frank Merritt then kneels over Phoenix and tells him to stay out of his business. He screams that he needs to teach him a lesson and that it's his own fault.
Ricky Simms - Frank's choked. Henry Lesher - He's right. He has to make an example of Phoenix. He has to show everyone why he is the Xtreme champ. He is bringing new respect to that title, elevating it to the same status as those held by Halloween Jack and The Moose.
Frank sets for and delivers the Blunt Trauma and looks out to the crowd. He nods his head and smiles sadistically. He then gives Phoenix a second Blunt Trauma and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Frank wins a quick one and Phoenix looks like he is hurt as he lies motionless on the canvas. Frank raises his arms to the boos of the crowd. He shakes his head, turns to the fallen Phoenix and then stands him up for a moment before giving him a third Blunt Trauma. Frank then rolls himself out of the ring to the floor where Destiny is applauding the result. Frank again raises his arms, grabs the Xtreme title off of Destiny's shoulder and then shows it to the camera, asking who dares to try and get it from him.
Ricky Simms - Well, I don't know who will dare to oppose him for that title. Henry Lesher - He might keep it for years with efforts like that one tonight. Backstage, we see Rated R enter the building, with Ashlee Skanes staying a few feet behind him. He is approached by Shannon Skanes, who calls out to his daughter.
Shannon Skanes - Ashlee! Come with me, NOW! Rated R - She's not going to answer you Shannon. Until you agree to my deal, she's mine, and she knows it.
Shannon Skanes - I'm not agreeing to anything until you let her go! Rated R - Then I keep her. Come on, Ashlee. We'll get you a nice glass of Kool-aid to drink.
Rated R smiles as he pushes past Shannon, into the arena, and Ashlee follows, without so much as glancing at her father.
Vesuvius vs. The Nameless "Mine Teil" by Rammstein plays as Vesuvius comes out to a strong reaction from the crowd, though it is split between love and hate. He is walked to the ring by both Xandra and
Noah Skanes, and he takes his time coming down the ramp as fireworks are lit off from the stage. Vesuvius then makes his way up the ring steps and then steps over the top rope into the ring. Vesuvius then paces inside the ring while his music fades and then we hear "Biledriver"
by GWAR and we see The Nameless coming out to loud boos from the crowd. Nameless stands on the stage for a few moments and looks around at the crowd, then starts down the ramp towards the ring.
Ricky Simms - Vesuvius wants to make a name for himself and he has this match with Nameless to do it.
Henry Lesher - Well, he will fail, but he's at least stupid in his efforts. Nameless makes his way up the ring steps, steps through the ropes into the ring and referee Jon Shocker calls for the bell. The two men lock up. Nameless shoves Vesuvius back into the ropes and then gives him a knee to the gut and then gives him a series of elbows to the spine. Nameless then whips Vesuvius off the ropes and Vesuvius ducks a clothesline and then gives Nameless a reverse neck breaker. Vesuvius then quickly gets to his feet and starts putting the boots to Nameless. Despite the abuse from
Vesuvius, Nameless gets to his feet and then starts trading rights and lefts with him. Nameless then gives Vesuvius a head butt, then a series of knife edge chops that back him into the corner buckle. He then tries to whip Vesuvius to the opposite corner, but Vesuvius plants his feet and instead whips Nameless into the corner buckle.
Vesuvius then gives Nameless a series of kicks to the ribs and then gives him a hip toss over the top rope and down to the floor.
Ricky Simms - I think I felt the floor shake over here on that one. Henry Lesher - Hey, at least Vesuvius has an idea of what he needs to do against Nameless. He can't win this one on one inside the ring.
Vesuvius drops and rolls out of the ring to the floor. He then starts in with punches to the side of the head on
Nameless. Vesuvius slams Nameless' head into the ring apron and then gives him a vicious knife edge chop to the chest. Nameless then thumbs Vesuvius in the eye, slams his head into the ring apron and then rolls him into the ring before rolling himself into the ring.
Nameless stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a backdrop to the mat. Nameless then gives Vesuvius a knee drop to the forehead and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by
Vesuvius. Nameless then stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a dropkick down to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Nameless moves well for a big man. Henry Lesher - He's the grim reaper with speed. He's going to be the champion again. Nameless stands Vesuvius up, whips him into the corner buckle and then walks into a clothesline from Vesuvius.
Vesuvius then boot chokes Nameless, using the ropes for added leverage. Vesuvius then stands Nameless up, whips him off the ropes and shoulder blocks him, though Nameless merely staggers and doesn't go down. Both men then clothesline each other to the mat. Referee
Jon Shocker starts counting them both down. On the three count, Nameless sits up. Nameless then turns and starts choking the still downed Vesuvius. Vesuvius responds with a thumb to the eye on
Nameless and then Vesuvius rolls himself out of the ring to the floor. Nameless follows close behind and starts trading rights and lefts with Vesuvius once again.
Ricky Simms - These two are evenly matched thus far. Henry Lesher - Well, Nameless wants him to think he is doing well. Nameless gives Vesuvius a side Russian leg sweep to the floor. Nameless then stands Vesuvius up and rolls him into the ring before rolling himself into the ring. Nameless then looks to the crowd and appears to be setting up the finish to this one. Nameless stands Vesuvius up. Vesuvius then gives Nameless a jawbreaker and then pulls the feet out from under him before turning him over into a Boston Crab. Referee Jon Shocker checks for a submission, but Nameless says nothing inside the ring as usual.
Ricky Simms - Vesuvius trying to wear the former AW champion down. Henry Lesher - Good luck with that. Nameless pulls himself up from the mat and walks on his elbows towards the ropes. He then lunges and manages to grab the bottom rope to force a break of the hold. Vesuvius lets him go and turns, stands Nameless up and whips him off the ropes only to have Nameless duck a clothesline. Nameless then gives Vesuvius a
German suplex to the mat. Nameless goes for the pin. 1...2... Vesuvius kicks out. Nameless then stands Vesuvius up, whips him right over the top rope to the floor and then drops and rolls himself out to the floor once again.
Ricky Simms - These two giants are taking this to the floor again. Henry Lesher - I am not complaining. Nameless stands Vesuvius up and then slams his head into the ring steps. Nameless then picks Vesuvius up and body slams him to the floor, then gives him a leg drop across the throat.
Nameless then stands Vesuvius up and whips him shoulder first into the steel corner post. He then turns Vesuvius around and takes a head butt from Vesuvius that seems to stagger both men as the referee reaches the ten count and declares this match a draw.
Ricky Simms - Vesuvius held his own this week against one of the best as he continues his march towards Rated R.
Henry Lesher - The United Front is a weak faction. This result is a fluke. We see Vigilante lacing up his boots for tonight's action when Crystal Braun enters the picture.
Vigilante - I didn't order a stripper. Crystal Braun - Vig, I wanted your reaction. Vigilante - You didn't take your clothes off yet. Crystal Braun - No, I mean the news about 6Pac. Vigilante - What? Crystal Braun - He didn't show up tonight. It seems Total Darkness has him at an unknown location and they did not allow him to be here tonight.
Vigilante - You know, I thought I heard all the stupid reasons for a person to not show up for a match with me, but that's new.
Crystal Braun - You didn't know? Vigilante - No, but that pisses me off. I'm not just sitting in the locker room tonight. Vigilante leaves the room and we head back to the ring. Hoshi Yakamoto vs. Jack Spade "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to play as Jack Spade comes out to a solid ovation from the crowd. He then tests the ropes while his music fades and we hear
"Surfing with the Alien" by Joe Satriani and Hoshi Yakamoto comes out to cheers from the crowd. She poses on stage and blows kisses to fans that are holding signs declaring their love for Hoshi. Hoshi then makes her way down the ramp to the ring, then jumps onto the apron. She waves to the crowd and then steps through the ropes while
Bart Phillips takes his place at ringside and referee Casey Braun calls for the bell.

Hoshi takes a shove down to the mat from Jack, but springs right back up to her feet. Hoshi then lands a standing dropkick on
Jack that sends him backwards. Hoshi then kips up to her feet and then Spade kips up to his as well. They move in for a lock up, but
Hoshi ducks under, gives Jack a knee to the ribs and then she gives him a DDT to the mat and then she goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Jack Spade. Hoshi then stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and lands a lariat on him that takes him down to the mat and then she again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Jack Spade.
Ricky Simms - That's twice she nearly pinned him early here. Hoshi is fired up tonight. Henry Lesher - I don't care. Hoshi stands Spade up, whips him off the ropes and misses with a dropkick as Spade side steps it. Spade then lands a knee drop to the chest on Hoshi. Spade stands her up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a clothesline down to the mat. Spade then climbs the ropes in the nearby corner. He makes his way to the top, poses there for the fans and launches himself and hits a flying elbow smash to the chest on Hoshi and then he goes for the pin.
1...2... kick out by Hoshi. Spade shakes his head as he stands Hoshi up, whips her off the ropes and lands a dropkick on her, sending her back to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Hoshi showing some toughness kicking out of Jack's pin there. Henry Lesher - She's a jobber, Rick. Even they sometimes kick out when they are not supposed to.
Jack Spade goes to the ropes again. He climbs to the top and poses there for the fans, launches himself and this time misses with the flying elbow as Hoshi rolls out of the way and then she quickly gets to her feet. She gives Jack a series of kicks to the ribs, then stands him up and whips him into the corner buckle. Hoshi then does a hand spring into a head scissors before bringing Jack down to the mat. Hoshi then opts to go to the ropes herself and climbs to the top. She launches herself and hits a moonsault onto the fallen Jack and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3!
Hoshi with an impressive win this week over Jack Spade. Ricky Simms - That was an excellent recovery and victory by Hoshi. Maybe momentum is picking up for her as we count down to Dead By Dawn.
Henry Lesher - And maybe you're an idiot. Backstage we see Wyatt Gibson in his office going over matches for next week. Corina enters the office.
Wyatt Gibson - I didn't order a stripper. Corina - Pardon? Wyatt Gibson - Sorry, thought it was a funny line by Vig and.... what do you want? Corina - I have a match with Xandra at Dead By Dawn. The winner is a Playboy cover girl and the loser will simply be relegated to back up. I have no intention of losing that match.
Wyatt Gibson - What's your point? Corina - I want a match next week. Wyatt Gibson - Maybe I'll say no. Corina - I want a match with the one woman in this company who would challenge me and get me ready for this match.
Wyatt Gibson - Boom doesn't work here anymore. Corina - Funny, look, I want a match with Hoshi. Make it happen. Wyatt Gibson - But Bombshell is higher ranked and Myst's a former champion. Corina - Hoshi is hungry enough to challenge me this week. That's what I need heading into my match with Xandra.
Wyatt Gibson - If you say so, fine. You have yourself a match. Tommy Purr vs. FUBAR "Last Resort" by Papa Roach plays as FUBAR is led towards the ring by Black Lightning. Inside the ring, FUBAR sees Vigilante there and rolls himself out of the ring to get between his partner and the former CWA champion. Vigilante turns away and takes a double axe handle smash from Black
Lightning. Vigilante then turns back and starts trading rights and lefts with Lightning and then gives him a low blow before pulling him to the ramp. FUBAR rolls himself into the ring and takes a knee to the forehead from a now rested Tommy Purr. Tommy then stands

FUBAR looks to the referee and questions the speed of the count to no avail. FUBAR then stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and Tommy ducks a clothesline and then gives FUBAR a knee to the back and then gives him the TommyKnocker to the mat, seemingly out of nowhere before going for the pin. 1...2...3! Tommy Purr gets a win this week over one of the members of Total Darkness.

Moose then picks Leech up and dumps him into the aisle between the fans. Moose steps over the guard railing, stands Leech up and takes a thumb to the eye. Leech then knees Moose in the ribs and gives him a DDT on the floor as security moves in to break it up. They pull
Leech away while a now bloodied Moose pulls himself up to his feet and starts off after him.
Ricky Simms - That was impressive. Henry Lesher - Damned straight and next week they go at it again. Ricky Simms - However, that is the end for this week. We will see you all next week as the Asian tour by AW continues and we count down towards Dead By Dawn.
Henry Lesher - You notice we're live and BLPW is four days late with an event in the US? What's up with that?
Ricky Simms - Must be the time change. See you next week, everybody. We fade out as Leech and Moose beat the hell out of security guards to get at each other while Rated R refuses to fight.
