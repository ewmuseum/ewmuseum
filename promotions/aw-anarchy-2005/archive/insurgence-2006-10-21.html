A dazed Jack Spade rolls himself out after her. He gets to his feet and takes a chair shot from 6Pac Dudley, causing referee Francis
Clarke to call for the bell and award the win to Spade by disqualification. Bombshell turns to 6Pac and isn't pleased. As she asks him what's wrong with him, he drops the chair and starts up the ramp. He doesn't even seem to hear her as he walks and Jack Spade is helped to his feet by the referee.
We then look backstage and fight Parker Davis is the latest to barge into Wyatt's office. Wyatt is clearly drunk now, the bottle empty on his desk. He is twirling it and has set up a deck of AW diva cards around it.
Wyatt Gibson - I kiss where the bottle stops. Parker Davis - Wyatt, tonight's result can't stand. It's wrong. Wyatt Gibson - I am playing with the girls. Come back later. Parker Davis - Look, mate, next week I want into the octagon with my last shot at being the big champion here.
Wyatt Gibson - You hear that girls? He wants a title shot. Parker Davis - If I lose the match next week.... I quit. You;re rid of me for good, no more Parker.
Wyatt Gibson - *finally looks at Parker* You know what? We have the CWA title.... the Xtreme title..... the
Monarch title.... all on the line. You want to put your career on the line? The ladies and me say you got yourself a match. See you next week, Parker. Hey, maybe Johnny Richter and the guys will come out to see you go.
Parker Davis - Not bloody likely. Parker storms out of the office and Wyatt falls off his chair. We then fade to an image for Dead By Dawn....
Next week....... Ricky Simms - See you next week, everybody!
