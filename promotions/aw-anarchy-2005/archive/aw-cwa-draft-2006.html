The AW-CWA Draft

undated (May 2006)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The AW-CWA Draft This is simple. Whichever company drafts you, that's the company that pays your check. There will be no name changes. There will be no split shows at this time. At this time, you can go for either title. We all know, of course, this is subject to change. Enjoy the draft. Shannon Skanes and Vesuvius step out onto the stage with Everest pulling out a large barrel full of names. Shannon Skanes rolls his eyes. Shannon Skanes - What the hell? We don't have that many people working for us. Vesuvius - You know what, Shannon? I don't want to leave any stone unturned. Shannon Skanes - Fine, whatever. This is just about who pays who. Vesuvius - Yeah, that's what it is right now. I want to protect myself for the future. Shannon Skanes - You know what, I don't care. Have it your way. Now, we agreed that I would choose first. Vesuvius - *nods* That's right. This is when the barrel is opened at the top and Vesuvius' ally Everest backs away. Shannon gives him a suspicious glance and then reaches in and draws out the first name. The way this works is simply they each read names in alternating order.
