Vigilante/The Moose vs. 6Pac Dudley/Bombshell "Southtown" by POD plays as 6Pac and Bombshell comes out from the back, arm in arm as the crowd cheers them on. They make their way down the ramp and Bombshell high fives a few fans at ringside before the two of them step up onto the apron and then they both jump over the top rope into the ring.
They then start talking strategy as we get set for our main event tonight. Their music fades and we hear "I Feel Alright" by Roger Clyne and the Peacemakers and
The Moose and Vigilante come out, the tag titles raised high above their heads.
The crowd gives them a loud ovation and Vigilante motions for them to get louder, egging them on. He then turns and high fives Moose and the two of them march down the ramo side by side.
Ricky Simms - Two legends defend the titles here tonight. Henry Lesher - How do you figure? See, just because you call them legends does not make them actual legends.
The tag team champions hand their titles over to the timekeeper and then pulls themselves onto the apron. Vigilante then steps through the ropes into the ring with 6Pac Dudley across from him. Vigilante then motions for the microphone from Kosmic Kitty and she tosses it up to him.
Vigilante - Okay, I have something to say before we successfully defend these belts tonight. I understand Max Cameron has retired twice in the locker room tonight, tapped out once when he was in the crapper and someone knocked on the door and plans to have a comeback challenge with Pyro every week until the end of 2008.
Vigilante then tosses the microphone back to Kosmic Kitty and the bell then sounds to get the match officially underway. Vigilante locks up with 6Pac and then backs him into the corner buckle where referee Jon Shocker asks for a clean break. Vigilante backs off and 6Pac shoves him. Vigilante then kicks 6Pac in the gut and then places him in a headlock and then gives him a bulldog to the mat. Vigilante then goes for the early pin. 1...2... kick out by
6Pac. Vigilante then stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Vigilante then motions for the microphone again. Kosmic Kitty tosses it into the ring.
Vigilante - Max Cameron has retired again. Oh, wait, he's unretired and has challenged Ken Shamrock to a UFC match. What a trooper!
Vigilante tosses the microphone back to Kosmic Kitty and turns his attention back to 6Pac who is half way to his feet. The two of them start trading punches. 6Pac then eye gouges Vigilante and then pulls him to his corner where he tags in Bombshell. The two of them then gives him a vertical suplex to the mat. Bombshell then places Vigilante in a sleeper hold while 6Pac exits the ring.
Ricky Simms - Vigilante was in control until he called for the microphone again. Henry Lesher - I'm no fan of his, but that's pretty funny stuff. Vigilante refuses to submit while in the sleeper hold and then reaches for the ropes and manages to grab the bottom rope to force the break. Vigilante then pulls himself out to the floor and makes his way around to the announce area and grabs the microphone from Kosmic Kitty.
Vigilante - Rumor has it that Max Cameron will tag with everyone on the roster at once before the end of his farewell tour next week. Of course, he'll appear the week after tagging with Pyro and then the race will be on to see who can turn on the other and retire the fastest.
Vigilante hands the microphone back to Kosmic Kitty and rolls himself back into the ring where he takes a series of knees from
Bombshell. She then stands Vigilante up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a dropkick that sends him down to the mat. Bombshell then goes to the ropes and makes her way to the top. She leaps off and lands a head butt to the chest on
Vigilante and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Vigilante.
Ricky Simms - Vig either needs to make a tag or forget the Max thing. Henry Lesher - I have been saying the same things he is for years. It's good to see he's such a fan.
Bombshell stands Vigilante up, whips him off the ropes and then both wrestlers clothesline each other to the mat. The referee starts counting them both down. On the four count, Vigilante rolls towards his corner and reaches for the tag. Bombshell does the same at the five count. Vigilante lunges and manages to tag in The Moose as Bombshell manages to tag in 6Pac
Dudley. The to trade knife edge chops in the middle of the ring. Moose then ducks one and turns 6Pac around and gives him an atomic drop followed by a side
Russian leg sweep to the mat. Moose then stands 6Pac up, whips him into the corner buckle and then charges into raised knees from 6Pac. 6Pac then climbs to the second rope and gives Moose a double axe handle smash to the chest from there, sending him to the mat where 6Pac goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by
The Moose. 6Pac then stands Moose up, whips him off the ropes and Moose ducks a clothesline and then gives 6Pac an eye gouge and then picks him up for a scoop slam to the mat. Moose then stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a spinning power slam to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2...
kick out by 6Pac. Ricky Simms - Moose looks good in there tonight. Henry Lesher - Well, he does, but that's against 6Pac. The Moose stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and 6Pac ducks a clothesline but steps right into a super kick from Vigilante who has snuck into the ring. Vigilante then spears a charging Bombshell to the mat while
Moose picks 6Pac up and delivers the Moosedriver and then goes for the pin.
1...2...3! Moose and Vigilante win to retain the tag team titles! Ricky Simms - Vig outsmarted 6Pac there and helped his team get the win. It wasn't a legal move, but it didn't get them disqualified either.
Henry Lesher - He was not the legal man. That was not a legal move. You're an idiot. Moose and Vigilante hold the tag titles high above their heads inside the ring as we see Rated R and Max Cameron making their way towards the ring with steel chairs in hand. Vigilante calls for the microphone.
Vigilante - Ladies and gentlemen, the currently retired.... oh no, he's not.... Max Cam.... wait, he quits. No he doesn't. The Moose then grabs the microphone from Vigilante. The Moose - Okay, you two are stopping right there. See, you got my title belt Ryan. I should have been ready for what happened at Dead
By Dawn. I should have been prepared for that. This week, I am prepared you you two. This week, I have the numbers. This week, you two fall down.
Frank Merritt hops over the guard railing behind Max and Rated R and then gives them both clotheslines to the back of the head and sends them face down to the floor. Frank then rolls himself into the ring and quickly gets to his feet, blood trickling down his cheek. He grabs the microphone from
The Moose.
Frank Merritt - See, there's something rotten in AW. I knew it when I came back here, but now I have seen it up close and personal. Wyatt
Gibson wants to make Ryan the champ? Max wants to get three more years inside the ring at all costs? I couldn't care less what anyone thinks of me personally.
I don't do anything for popularity, but I have really wanted to beat the living hell out of Rated R. Now, I saw what happened last week and I knew it was time to make it official.
Frank hands the microphone to Vigilante and then signals to Destiny who is standing in the front row of the crowd and she tosses three
T-shirts into the ring. Frank puts his on and hands the other two to Vigilante and Moose as he takes the microphone back.
Frank Merritt - Wyatt... Ryan.... you made this happen. It's a dawn of the new age, you bastards. Enemies of the Fucking State, bitches! Vigilante - You think you can stop EOS this time around? Just try it! The three of them stand inside the ring as the crowd cheers them on and Ryan and Max back off, thinking better of any kind of attack right now.
Ricky Simms - That's some pretty big news there. Henry Lesher - Not Frank! Frank needs no one! Frank, turn on them! Crap..... my night is ruined.
Ricky Simms - How will this play out as we head towards Restricted? Tune in to Insurgence next week and find out! Don't forget we are moving to Sundays starting next week. It should be very interesting to see how this turns out.
We fade to black on the new EOS celebrating inside the ring and the crowd eating it all up.
