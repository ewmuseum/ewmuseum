AW Assault, 5 February 2006

Assault - February 5th, 2006 The crowd takes their seats as another edition of AW Assault gets set to go to air. Minneapolis is the home to tonight's show, one that promises to be phenomenal by all accounts.
Backstage we see Vesuvius pacing and then Shelia Lavin enters the office. For a moment the two stare at each other and say nothing.
Vesuvius - Your month is over. Shelia Lavin - I am aware of that. I came here tonight as a wrestler and manager to Q. Vesuvius - Well, I have had enough of the crap you and Q bring to the table. I'm letting you go.
Shelia Lavin - *shaking her head* You'll regret this. Vesuvius - You think so? Shelia Lavin - See, Q and I were here as Shannon's representatives inside the ring, but there is something else to this as well. You see this simply as the Skanes family being a threat to your little empire here. There's much more to it than that.
Vesuvius - Enlighten me. Shelia Lavin - Q and I have a payment clause from a very interested third party in the event of our dismissal. I took the pleasure of signing someone to a contract after the show last week and got paid handsomely for this acquisition.
Vesuvius - Stop with the damned riddles. What the hell are you talking about? Shelia Lavin - Can Anarchy compete with the Mafia? She then turns and is heard humming to herself as she exits the office. No sooner has she left than Jason Knight enters the room, clearly having overheard everything said.
Jason Knight - Could that mean...? Vesuvius - She's bluffing. She has to be. Max Cameron vs D.I.C.K. "Tear In My Beer" by Hank Williams starts to play as Dwayne Irving Carter Kingston starts to make his way to the ring. The crowds booing doesn't phase him as he tips his hat to the camera and asks if we're ready for some wooly bully. He then lets out a shout and quickly makes his way to the ring. He leaps up onto the apron and looks around at the crowd for a few moments before he steps into the ring. He then has a few words for referee Mark Davis as his music fades. We then hear "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC and see Max Cameron being led to the ring by Aftermath and Cameron Jacques. Malice is following behind and watching out for anyone who might try to attack the Superior champion.
Ricky Simms - This is an interesting match to start the show off. Henry Lesher - Shelia signed Sal Clemenzo? Ricky Simms - She didn't say that. Henry Lesher - Sal is a dangerous man, a known killer, a man with a very checkered past who actually turned against his beloved Mafia at one time. Man, would it be good to see him back!
Max stops at ringside and looks up at Kingston. Kingston is motioning Max into the ring with that cocky grin on his face. Max makes his way up the ring steps, not taking his eyes off of Kingston as Malice places the Superior title over his shoulder. Max then steps into the ring where Kingston greets him with a series of kicks to the ribs. Kingston then gives Max a German suplex to the center of the ring and then gives him a leg drop across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Max Cameron. Kingston stands Max up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him down to the mat. Kingston then places Max in a wrist lock while planting a foot on the back of Max's neck. Referee Davis checks for a submission, but Max refuses.
Ricky Simms - Kingston really taking it to the champ here so far. Henry Lesher - Kingston's a good looking guy, isn't he? I mean, so rugged and ruthless. Ricky Simms - We are five minutes into the show and I already feel like I am going to throw up.
Kingston abandons the wrist lock, stands Max up and whips him into the corner buckle. He then gives Max a double under hook suplex from there and then goes to the ropes. He climbs to the second rope and then lands a double axe handle smash across the back of the neck of Max before he has a chance to get all the way to his feet. Kingston goes for the pin. 1...2... Max kicks out. Kingston stands Max up, whips him to the ropes and Max grabs them and pulls himself out of the ring to the safety of his
Youthful Aggression stable. Kingston shouts at Max to get back into the ring and even goes to that side of the ring and spits at all four of them. Kingston then retreats to the middle of the ring as Max pulls himself onto the apron. Max steps into the ring and locks up with Kingston. Max knees Kingston in the gut and then gives him a series of knife edge chops that back him up to the middle of the ring. Max then eye gouges Kingston, puts him in a headlock and DDTs him to the mat. Max tells the camera that this is over, rolls Kingston over and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kingston.
Ricky Simms - Max clearly felt he had the match won there. Henry Lesher - I guess he's dumber than he looks. That's really saying something. Max stands Kingston up, whips him into the corner buckle and then clotheslines him in the corner. Max then bulldogs Kingston out of the corner and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kingston. Max begins to argue with the referee about the speed of the count. Davis holds up two fingers and refuses Max's request to call it a match right there. Kingston then rolls Max into a school boy while hooking the tights. 1...2...3! Referee Mark Davis missed the hook of the tights and Kingston remains undefeated in AW. As Kingston exits the ring, referee Shannon Skanes is seen making his way towards it. Kingston waits until Shannon is close enough and then drills him with a kick to the chest followed by a DDT to the floor. Kingston then laughs as he looks down on the fallen official and tells him that's what he gets for sticking his nose where it didn't belong. Kingston then takes the time to pick up his cowboy hat and gives the other members of Youthful Aggression the finger before he then starts for the back.
Henry Lesher - I love that man. Ricky Simms - Pardon? Henry Lesher - I said that was a great match. Ricky Simms - No you didn't, but let's move on. As Kingston disappears into the back and Max rolls himself out of the ring, we look backstage and see Jason Knight with Scavenger.

Scavenger - He'd better show up. Scavenger then turns and leaves the office. Vesuvius appears from what appears to be the closet?
Vesuvius - I am very disappointed in Scavenger. Jason Knight - *nods* Kingston will destroy him. We need a new ally. Vesuvius - Agreed. I have someone in mind, actually.
