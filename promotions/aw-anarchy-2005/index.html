A fantasy federation that ran a weekly Assault, renamed Insurgence in 2006, and monthly pay-per-views from November 2005, staging cards in the United States and Canada and on a Eurasian tour of Japan, Germany and Britain, and whose surviving record ends on a Battle of the Warriors in Calgary on 14 January 2007; it also carried a CWA-branded show and a CWA Alpha title in 2006. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Anarchy Wrestling ran a weekly Assault, renamed Insurgence in March 2006, and a monthly pay-per-view from 27 November 2005, written by its owner's on-screen persona Shannon Skanes, who says it is the old CWA reborn. Its first belts, the AW and Xtreme titles, are awarded vacant on 21 December 2005, and it carried a CWA Alpha title and a CWA-branded Terror in the Aisles in June 2006.

It toured Japan, Germany and Britain in the autumn of 2006, staged its last card in the mirror at Battle of the Warriors in Calgary on 14 January 2007, and published a schedule through 5 August 2007; by February 2008 the same address is called the C.W.A. again, with titles of its own.

Affiliations

Global Wrestling Organization (GWO, Global, 2004)

Predecessor to AW (Anarchy, 2005)
Anarchy Wrestling's AW and Xtreme titles are awarded vacant on 21 December 2005, no GWO belt continues, and its card of 25 January 2006 says it fills a void that the GWO left behind when it closed its doors.

Championship Wrestling Association (CWA, Revival, 2007)

Successor to AW (Anarchy, 2005)
The CWA of 2007 and 2008 comes back on Anarchy Wrestling's tenant after a one-year layoff with new World, Aerial, Diva and tag lists beginning on 23 October 2007; none of Anarchy Wrestling's titles continues.

CWA Originals (Originals, 2006)

Shared continuity with AW (Anarchy, 2005)
Concurrent telling of the same owner's company: its card of 23 December 2006 has Myst and Xandra defect to it from Anarchy Wrestling.
