AAPW All American Pro Wrestling "You Won't Be Disappointed" _______________________________________________________________ ~Opening~ Hello, I am president [name withheld] of AAPW. I am proud to present to you personally a personal ivitation to join AAPW. We haven't started yet, but we plan to start Monday, June 8th. AAPW is like a lot of other federations, but we have one thing on our side, that is experience. I have ran a few federations in my time that were top notch and my good friend [name withheld] has experienced legendary respect as an ewrestler and has decided to become a co-fed head. Anyway, I am hoping that you read this sheet of information and decide to join AAPW. We are awaiting your application.
~Rules~ The rules of AAPW are very simple. You just need to read this set of rules and AAPW won't be hard to have a good behavior in. 1)NO TOSSING! I hate little children who can't stand the heat. 2)You can only role play up to 3 times a day. No more than that. 3)You are only allowed up to 10 people to a stable. It would be a small fed if it were more.
~RolePlays~ Roleplays are what this federation is based on. If you don't roleplay, you lose! If a lot of you don't roleplay, the federation loses. Anyway, I am pretty sure that all of you are very talented roleplayers. Most of you have been champions in some form or anothr. This is why I have some pretty big goals for you guys.
You must roleplay once a week to be considered to be on one of the cards. The roleplay also has to be at least 50 lines. If not, it won't even be counted.
Finally, I want your roleplay to contain quality. Without quality, you will lose your match.
I judge roleplays like this, Quality is judged 60%. Quantity is judged 30%. Grammer is judged 10%. Now, I sometimes will be giving your wrestler a push and I may count this a little differently. You will gain a push by the way if you have worked hard for the past few weeks when the federation is started.
~Titles~ AAPW will be holding a huge battle royal for the declaration of the titles on their first card. You will have to roleplay hard to win one of these titles.
Also, titles with a * by them will be added if needed. AAPW World Title-You will defend this title at all PPV's and major cards. AAPW All American Title-You are in a way representing AAPW if you win this title. It is defended regularly.
AAPW Intercontinental Title-Hey, this is another American based title, but it is defended quite often.
AAPW Toughman Title-You got to go through hell to earn this title and continue to defeat the biggest beasts in the business to retain it.
*AAPW Television Title-You defend this title every card againt fresh young stars. *AAPW Lightweight Title-You have to be 240 or less for this title, but don't worry because it isn't here yet. *AAPW Tag Team Titles-You and your friend will be the best team in AAPW if you win this title. ~Cards~ AAPW will be holding two major cards and one taped card a week. The two major cards will be for angles and titles to be started and won while the taped card is to keep a push going or just for a warm up match. You WON'T have to roleplay for a taped card, but you will be fined if you don't roleplay for a major card.
AAPW's first major card of the week is held on Wednesday. It is called Wednesday Night Assault. It will contain many matches including some that are PPV quality. The second AAPW major card is held on Saturday. It is called Saturday Intensity and it is very much like the Wednesday card. The taped card is held on Monday. It won't be written out, it will just tell who won and summarize the main event.
AAPW will be holding a PPV every month starting June 27th when AAPW presents AAPW Summer Bash 98'. Other PPV's will be announced as time progresses. ~Money System~ AAPW is adopting a money system like many other federations. It will be very efficient and very effective though.
1)You will be able to buy your own card when you get $400,000. 2)You will be able to buy a PPV when you get $800,000. 3)You can get a regular PPV and card when you get $2,000,000. 4)You can add stable members when you get $100,000. I bet that you are asking right now, how do I earn money? Well, check out this little table and you will find out.
1)You start out with $10,000. 2)You get $5,000 by winning a normal match. 3)You get $15,000 if you win a title. 4)You get $10,000 for each match you win if you have a title. 5)You get $10,000 also if you win a gimmick match. 5)You get $2,500 for a loss, so you aren't left in the cold. 6)You can lose up to $20,000 if you don't roleplay for a major card or if you complain about something to the entire federation.
~Newsletter~ AAPW will send out a newsletter every month telling about who is number one in rankings and showcasing the biggest matches. You can also buy a column in the newsletter for $50,000.
AAPW is also going to have a webpage on a small note. It too will contain the newsletter. ~AAPW Gurantee~ I, the president of AAPW am promising an excellent federation. That is if I can get all of you to cooperate. You have to work with me if this is going to be a good federation. I am challenging all of you to join AAPW and go for the
AAPW World Title. The more, the marrier. Just join this federation and work, I am promising nothing but good things out of AAPW within three weeks. I hope to see you all turning in applications.
~Application~ Be sure to send all applications to Lord [detail withheld] after filling out all essential information. I hope to see all of you in the AAPW very soon.
