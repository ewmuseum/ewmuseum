A roughly two-week June 1998 promotion sharing the bare acronym AAPW with the long-running held wing of the same initials, staged from Long Beach, California, and unrelated to it in every other respect. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

All American Pro Wrestling introduced itself on 3 June 1998 with a founding rules document under the slogan “You Won't Be Disappointed,” announcing plans to open five days later, on Monday 8 June, and leading with its own leadership's experience: a returning federation president joined by “a good friend” the post credits with “legendary respect as an ewrestler” as co-fed head.

Its rules barred “tossing,” capped roleplaying at three times a day and stables at ten wrestlers, and required a roleplay at least once a week of fifty lines or more, graded sixty percent on quality, thirty on quantity, and ten on grammar; its titles were to be settled in a battle royal on its first card.

Its arrival drew a public denial the same week from an unrelated, already-established federation sharing the same initials, which said in its own post that it had no connection to the new promotion.

The last surviving post from All American Pro Wrestling is dated 15 June 1998. Whether its planned opening card, or its battle-royal title decisions, were ever held is not known, and no closure notice survives.
