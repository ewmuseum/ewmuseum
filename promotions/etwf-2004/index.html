Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Extra Terrestrial Wrestling Federation billed itself as an intergalactic hybrid of professional wrestling and science fiction, its roster built entirely from super-powered humanoid stables rather than individual wrestlers. A January 2005 post pointing to its own Tripod-hosted site, SlobberKnocker Moon City, recalled “2 matches from the beginning of the ETWF 2 1/2 years ago” and previewed an upcoming card, Thunder Struck.

Its own fullest surviving document announces Slammer Jammer Special #3 — ETWF Stable Wars, an elimination tournament run entirely by stable rather than by wrestler. Seven established stables and a new “synthoid” heel stable opened the first round in two-out-of- three-fall matches with every power unrestrained; the four winning stables met in a second round of sudden-death tag matches in which each wrestler had to give up one of their own powers; and the two survivors were to close the tournament in a Survivor Series–style elimination tag match with every power blocked, its last man standing earning a shot at the ETWF Galaxy-Wide Wrestling Overlord and the federation's own top title.

The bracket itself reads like a roster of the federation's own imagination: fan favorites the Slayers of Repton, a stable of humanoid reptiles and arachnids joined by the super-strength duo Law & Order, opened against the villainous Dimension of Fear, led by a shapeshifter called the Pale Rider; Inter-Galactic Honor, headed by a coyote-morphing lawman, faced the werewolf-and-monster stable the Block Busters; the practical-joking Pranks 'R' Us drew the widely disliked Lethal Enigma; and Constellation, four flight-and-speed powered heroes, opened against a freshly formed heel stable, Synthoid-oramma.

No result from Stable Wars survives, and no further document carries the federation's own record forward; how the tournament played out, or how the Extra Terrestrial Wrestling Federation itself ended, isn't in the surviving record.
