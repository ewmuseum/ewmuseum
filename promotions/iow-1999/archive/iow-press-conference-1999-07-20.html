IOW Press Conference

20 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Darkness. The commercial break has just finished. 'The Beautiful People' by Marlyin Manson comes on very quietly and slowly gets louder. As the second drum beat comes in the screen explodes with a flash of colour and the music is at its loudest.]
  [The words IOW-Insane Online Wrestling fly across the screen while clips of wrestlers performing in the background are shown]
  [The music steadly dies down until there is a final explosion and a voice says 'Insane Online Wrestling...IT HAS
BEGUN!!']
  [The intro quickly fades out and then the picture cuts to a large board room where President Richard Stephenson is seated behind a large oak desk. To the left of him is a podium where four or five microphones are positioned.]
  [About 15 to 20 reporters are seated on plastic chairs in front of Stephenson, all with cameras and tape recorders.]
  [The IOW camera man zooms in on Stephenson who is dressed very smartly, in a black Armani suit, a white shirt and black tie. He takes a sip from a glass of water on his desk and the stands up and walks to the podium]
  Stephenson: I have called this press conference today to let the world know what is going on in the IOW. I started campaigning for this federation about a month or two months ago, then suddenly everything went silent on my part. I have been inundated with calls, faxes, e-mails, and letters wanting to know what is wrong.
  [He stops to take another sip of water]   Stephenson: Well, about a month ago I was envolved in an accident which left me in hospital for 3 weeks and since then I have been recooperating. Now I am fine so it is time to get the rollercoaster of wrestling on the road!!
  Stephenson: I know you must have many questions for me so fire ahead...   [There is a loud commotion as all the reporters try to ask questions and once]   Stephenson: Calm down, calm down!! We'll get through the questions one at a time...first...you....yes...the Scoop
Reporter.   [A stocky reporter stands up]   Reporter 1: Yes. Well, I am from Scoop's Wrestling Insider and I would like to ask what talent have you got signed up already?   Stephenson: Good question. There are masses of wrestlers out there in the world, but only a few with the calibre to perform in the IOW, so my booking team had to be very careful, and selective when booking wrestlers to the fed. We have got quite a few up and coming superstars such as
Clay Boy, ''The Savage Warrior'' Shamoke, The 60 Second Assasin, The Stitch
Master, Rago Razi, Dr Evil and Sir Facchino and The Maniac Mike
Ghoram.   Reporter 2: I'm from CNN Sports Wire and I would like to know when you plan on holding your first card?
  Stephenson: Well, pretty soon. I'll tell you the date a bit later.   Reporter 3: I'm here on behalf of Pro Wrestling Illustrated. I'd like to know what are your plans to bring in new talent to the federation?
  Stephenson: Well, I mentioned a few names earlier of wrestlers who are bound to be superstars in the near future. We do not live in the past like certain other federations I could mention, but we look forward to the future. You could say we are the new millenium's wrestling federation.
  Stephenson: As for bringing in new talent to the federation our large team of bookers are constantly scouring the globe looking for free agents, the biggest names in wrestling as well as rookies who look like a good prospect. At the moment I have sent contracts out to a number out wrestlers who are all big players in the game, but I cannot disclose who. All I can say is I have over 30 contracts that have been sent out. It just remains for the wrestler's to sign the contracts, and for us to come to some agreement on personal terms.
  [Stephenson takes a sip of water and coughs to clear his throat]   Stephenson: Any more questions? If not I would like to tell you about the IOW's first card.
  [Most hands inthe audience go down, as they listen intently to the most important announcment of the evening]
  Stephenson: No more questions? Good. Okay. On Monday the 26th of July, The IOW Proudly Presents the first ever Insane Online
Wrestling Warfare, coming live from Ceasar's Palace Las Vegas!!!!!!
  Stephenson: The program will be live, uncut and un-edited, and it will be broadcast on numerous channels to make sure we have the widest viewing audience possible!!
  Stephenson: Also, the main event that night will be The 60 Second Assasin VS Rago Razi, in a Hospital Match!   [The crowd mutter in disbelief.]   Unknown Reporter: What the hell is a Hospital Match?   Stephenson: A hospital match is a match with no rules, no time limit etc. To win the match you must _hospitalise_ your opponent!
  [Crowd murmer in approval]   Stephenson: I am very short on time as I have a number of things to get through so if you will excuse me, I will be leaving now, and I hope to meet up with you later in the week. Thank you for your time.
  [Stephenson gets up and walks out quickly as the reporters try to follow him shouting out questions. He is quickly ushered outside and steps into a waiting limo]
  [The screen fades to black, and 'The Beautiful People' plays again with The IOW logo on the screen. On the final beat of the song, the logo explodes and the copyright notice is shown]
