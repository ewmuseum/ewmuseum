Information Needed To Join   a) Singles Information   b) Tag Team Information   ----------------- (1.) Introduction: ------------------- Hello future IOWites. My name is Richard Stephenson and I am president of  Insane Online Wrestling. This is quite a new federation in the e-wrestling world and, with your help I hope to make it a great one. I am sick of federations folding or presidents that screw you. If you are a number one contender you will get a shot. If you don't roleplay you will be stripped. Remember: I don't care if you are the biggest name in e-wrestling...if you deserve to lose you will lose. The only way someone will ever lose unfairly in the IOW is to persue an angle or draw some heat. Remember:
Losing is part of the game.   ------------------------------- (2.) Brief History of the IOW -----------------------------------   The Insane Online Wrestling federation was formed long before I was on the net. It was my first e-federation run by a man Craignj123. I didn't know the name of the federation and it was NOT called the IOW. As time went on card became later and later and soon became non-existant. A man called Don23BBall decided to run for president and so did I. I won by underhanded tactics and promises of titles. The federation was soon on the up. New members such as Nautc78 and Suicide49 joined and epic battles between the federation's number one stable the Team Extreme and the Suicide Maniacs begun. Soon, I became busy and I could not find time so on the first of January 1998 the federation was shut down. I joined a real federation run by James Molloy called the IWC. I am sure many of you have heard of it. There I learnt to roleplay properly  but most importantly I learned what a real fedeation was like.
  Now, the IOW is making a return. We want to be one of the best feds on the net giving it all each week. For that we need you.
  ----------------- (3.) Cards and Titles -------------------    The IOW currently promotes two cards a week. They are the Extreme Zone and Saturday Night Showdown. Mixed in with the cards is the IOW magazine which hits the shelf every Sunday. We also promote 6 PPV's a year.
Well, here is a more in depth look at each of our cards:   (a) Extreme Zone....The biggest card of the week. The Zone is known to generate good angles and much heat between all the wrestlers. It is composed of around 10 LIVE matches. The Zone is held at a different arena from around the world every week.
The Extreme Zone should never be missed.   (b.) Saturday Night Showdown.....A minor card in the IOW.  A DARK match is a match which is summarised instead of using live play by play.   Has one BIG main event match and the rest are darks.
This is the card for your debut or if you are struggling. If you are on a losing streak, try and get on this card. The live televised match comes straight to you from the IOW arena.
  (b.)Pay per Views...Without a doubt, the pay per view events are the makers and breakers of EWrestling. You definately need to put on a great event. Remember, no one does Pay Per View like the IOW.
  Here is the rundown of the names and months of the PPV's:   Extreme Warfare: January Wargames: March Wrestle Extreme: May <-- Equivalent of Super Brawl and Wrestlemania
IOW Explosion: July/August IOW Blood Sports: September December to Remember: December   (d.) Titles....There will be three singles titles and one tag team title. The singles titles are the World title, Intercontinental title and the TV title. The tag titles are the IOW World tag titles.
