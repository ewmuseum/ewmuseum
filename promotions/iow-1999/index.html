Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Insane Online Wrestling's own handbook (5 June 1999) traces its roots to an earlier, unnamed federation, not yet called the IOW, that Richard Stephenson took over as president by his own account through “underhanded tactics and promises of titles,” building a rivalry there between two stables, Team Extreme and the Suicide Maniacs, before folding it on 1 January 1998 for lack of time and relaunching under the Insane Online Wrestling name in 1999 after a stint learning to roleplay at another federation, the IWC.

Insane Online Wrestling ran two weekly cards, the Extreme Zone and Saturday Night Showdown, a Sunday magazine, and six pay-per-views a year (Extreme Warfare, Wargames, Wrestle Extreme, IOW Explosion, IOW Blood Sports, and December to Remember) across three singles titles and a tag title, staffed on air by ring announcer John Pinchbeck, referee Alan Connel, and broadcasters Michael C. Cleary and James Elia.

Its own 20 July 1999 press conference had Stephenson explain a two-month silence as recovery from a car accident that hospitalized him for three weeks, name a roster of incoming wrestlers including Clay Boy, “The Savage Warrior” Shamoke, and Rago Razi, and announce the promotion's first card, Insane Online Wrestling Warfare, for 26 July 1999 from Caesars Palace in Las Vegas, headlined by a no-rules Hospital Match between The 60 Second Assassin and Rago Razi.

No document survives past this press conference; nothing establishes whether the 26 July card ever aired, who held any of the promotion's four titles, or when Insane Online Wrestling's own second run actually ended.
