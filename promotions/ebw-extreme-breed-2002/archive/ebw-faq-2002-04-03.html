Yeah, I kind of tweaked the app a little. ------ Extreme Breed Wrestling FAQ I. Introduction (Reality & Fantasy) II. Rules and Regs III. The Setup of EBW IV. Personalities of EBW V. Application ----- I. Introduction (Reality & Fantasy) In Reality: I, [name withheld], run Extreme Breed Wrestling. I'm a real nice guy, I think. I used to head the short-lived Atlantic Coast Wrestling (this was last year) and I currently handle the characters Jamie Roth, Vic Morrison, and Alex Hawkins. Some people think that I rule, apparently. In Fantasy: I honestly don't know who the hell is running the ship in Extreme Breed Wrestling right now. I mean, I'll figure it out sooner or later and it'll be a cool-ass angle...but right now, it's kind of up in the air. :) II. Rules and Regs The rules are pretty simplistic. You have to roleplay in order to have a glimmer of a chance at winning your match. I decide if your work rules or drools, basically. Strats are optional, but they _do_ count towards the match if you do them. After all, it gives me a better idea of how to use your character as I write a match and all that jazz. No offensive shit in roleplays; you know what I mean. Cursing's allowed (you'll see why later), but don't take it to extremes. No breaking kayfabe. Also, no bitching to me about other handlers, no disputes with other handlers if it has a possibility of disrupting the structure of the e-fed. If you don't get along, well then, I'm apathetic towards the situation and won't do anything about it. However, if your "problem" does disrupt things somehow (i.e. have everyone talk about your situation, scaring away potential members, etc., etc.), I'm getting the rid of both of you, simple as that. You won't be welcomed back. III. The Setup of EBW - Extreme Breed Wrestling has indeed have a TV program that is shown on the premium cable channel, HBO2. It's about an hour-long and is basically uncensored. That's good for EBW. How did they manage to get a show on HBO2? Well, you'd have to ask the person in charge, who just happens to be in some kind of seclusion. Each show holds about 3-4 matches. Each match, at K length, would mostly be an easy-to-read 10-15k. - EBW is a bi-weekly e-fed. Basically, this allows me more time to take care of matches and stuff and make them more high quality. - Also, EBW _does not_ have a flash show; promos/flashes/RPs are displayed on EBW TV. Pretty simple. In-rings won't be allowed unless you ask permission; they're kind of on a first come, first serve basis (no, please don't tell that Japanese businessmen joke; I've heard it a million times). - No pay-per-views...however, there will be supercards. Yeap. A whole one hour, forty-five minutes all for us on HBO2. IV. Personalities of EBW a) Absent President James Cross Okay, yeah, I said I didn't who I'd pick to be the "fantasy head" of things at the beginning of this thing...but guess what? James Cross is now your boss and your daddy. Thing is, he's not exactly around to run things. Hell, he doesn't even know that he's running Extreme Breed Wrestling; he's in prison because of an apparent drug possession charge and someone's raided his bank account. b) Vice President Jeff Styles Styles is pretty much a fresh-outta-college slacker and is Cross's rich asshole nephew. Styles is a preppy sleazeball who happens to have a "like" for pro-wrestling and decided to take advantage of his uncle's situation and start a federation for himself. It should be noted that Jeff did try to be a pro-wrestler...but it didn't really work out for him that well since it was "too rough." c) Commissioner Ken Hashimoro Hashimoro is Cross's good friend from overseas. He became curious when Styles offered him this position and decided to take it to see what's up. He's very by-the-books and follows kind of a code of honor. d) Chris Lansberry Chris is the face commentator. He used to work for ACW. e) Josh Davis Josh is the heel commentator. Very nasty and mean. He used to be a rather stable and tame backstage interviewer for the now-defunct LVPWA, but he snapped... V. Application Well, here it is, finally. Lastly, I'd like to say that this fed isn't meant to be a big deal; I wouldn't really prefer it if you joined it with one of your oh-so-important, big name, good God-he-made-#1-on-a-stupid-poll characters (although it'd probably be flattering)...this is more like a thing for people to experiment with new ideas and just have fun.
