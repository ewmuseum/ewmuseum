EBW (Extreme Breed, 2002) Card Archive

Alex Reaver, Intro Flash for EBW

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[FADE IN. INT. A SUBWAY CAR - NEW YORK STATE. The particular car we find ourselves filming inside of is completely empty, save for two passengers important to EBW's ever present camera eyes. One of them is FEMALE; she is asleep in a two-person seat, curled up in a ball. Long brown hair covers most of her face. She is wearing tight, dark colored jeans, black two inch heeled boots, and a black knit hooded sweater. The other passenger, a MALE hovers above her, with one hand holding onto a metal pole in the aisle of the car. The man has chin length hair which appears to be dyed jet black. He wears black Sketcher boots, baggy dirty denim jeans, and a white and black muscle t-shirt. His face is not immediately visible to the camera.] MAN: Chloe… shh… you sleep now. [The MAN bends down, brushing the WOMAN's hair gently. She stirs for a few seconds, then stops. Apparently satisfied, the MAN slowly backs away and turns his face to the camera. A pair of piercing green eyes stare back at us. This MAN, just twenty four years of age, wears a weariness across his face well beyond his years. This face is unforgetable to East Coast wrestling fans. The MAN is the nightmare from Newton himself, the former poster boy for teenage angst... ALEX REAVER.] ALEX REAVER: I'm alive. [He deadpans this statement to the camera, not with relief, not with uncertainty, but with authority. This man, battle scarred from five years of bloodshed and personal tragedies, stands before us with a look of defiance flickering inside those eyes.] ALEX REAVER: I consider it a miracle. An act of God. [He stops, glancing back at the sleeping woman, who we are now able to idenity as CHLOE BRANDON, the once and current girlfriend and personal savior to our own golden boy.] ALEX REAVER: Because of her. My angel. [He smiles warmly. It's been a long time since a wrestling company's television camera has caught this look on his face. We are used to sullen, bitter stares or wickedly devilish smirks... but this... a genuine smile... this is new.] ALEX REAVER: I have paid for my sins. I have paid in blood. I have faced every demon from my past... every indiscretion, every addiction, every vice... I have died a thousand deaths for eact act. Now, my soul is purged. Now... I have my redemption. [ALEX closes his eyes, inhaling deeply. He slowly crouches down, one hand drawn to his forehead, rubbing. ALEX rocks on the balls of his heels, eyes still closed.] ALEX REAVER: For the past five years, I have walked through the valley of dispair... I have stood at the mouth of Hell itself... and through it all, I have survived. Now, after all these years, after all the blood I've spilt and all the bones I've broken... I find myself looking at the path I've walked, the battles I have fought, the restless nights I endured... and I wonder... what was it all for? [Slowly, ALEX opens his eyes. He rises to his feet.] ALEX REAVER: The love of of my family? No, they abandoned me a long time ago. The adoration of the crowd? No, I was their golden boy once, but those days have long since past. The chance at fortune and fame? No, the idea of glory has lost its luster to me. What then? What is it that has drawn me back to this industry that destroyed my youth and corrupted my soul? What is it that brought me to EBW like a stranger in the night? [ALEX pauses, trying to keep his voice from rising any further. He looks back at CHLOE, a faint smile again crossing his lips. He turns back to the camera and suddenly, the intensity in his eyes is back... the look of a man hell-bent on achieving whatever it is that he has come back for.] ALEX REAVER: I look around... I see all the filth and garbage... I see the wreckage of an industry gone bad... I see the destruction and decay of society... I see the evil... the evil I once fell victim to... and I know what it is that I must do. I know now why I was put through so many tragedies early in life. It was a test. A hero's trial. For that is what I must be... A hero. [ALEX nods his head, fully believing these words to be true. He brings a hand to his face, slowly rubbing it over one of the many scars his body bears.] ALEX REAVER: And like every great hero, I must fight the evil in this world... I must confront it head on and wash it all away. Slowly, one step at a time, I will destroy the evil. EBW is the first step. Lupe Blanco... I look at you and I see no face. All I see is one of the Devil's own. You are a not a pure man, Lupe. You have done wrong. You are full of hate. Lupe Blanco, you are an evil man... and thus, you must be dealt with accordingly. Don't blame me for the blood that I must spill. Don't blame me for the chaos I must create. You brought this upon yourself, Lupe. [REAVER pauses, licking his lips. A hint of a smirk curls upon his face.] ALEX REAVER: Justice must be served. [FADE OUT.]
