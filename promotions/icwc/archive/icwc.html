INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING COUNCIL The International Championship Wrestling Council was first developed by another writer under the UWOW banner and even after he left, ICWC remained a force to be reckoned with. Bringing a new attitude and featuring stars from the independent scene, ICWC became a more traditional wrestling organization with an emphasis on winning gold. The new ICWC will attempt to draw from the same spirit which made the original ICWC great, drawing upon the great NWA tradition which inspired it. ***** ICWC NEWS & EVENTS ICWC has been changed forever after a major changing of the guard as GM Eric Bischoff has been replaced by the new GM, Vince Russo! The announcement came at the UWF's School of Hard Knocks in September of 2008 and quickly things changed as Russo was instrumental in helping his Chosen One, Jeff Jarrett, defeat Hulk Hogan 1, 2, 3 in the middle of the ring to win the title! From there, Russo staged his first event in October and it was simply RUSSO'S REVENGE! In a huge main event, Kurt Angle won the Chamber of Horrors Match and then faced, and shockingly defeated, Jeff Jarrett to become the new ICWC World Heavyweight Champion in his very first night in the company... but have Russo's plans been derailed OR is there a master plan? We'll find answers next time as the ICWC continues in winter 2008. ***** ICWC ~ Events October, 2008: Russo's Revenge featuring the Chamber of Horrors is up September, 2008: UWF School of Hard Knocks is up feat. the Hogan/Jarrett Title Bout May 11th, 2008: Ultimate Supercard part 1, part 2, and part 3 are up April 13th, 2008: Spring Stampede is up March 16th, 2008: Capital Combat is up February 29th, 2008: Sin City is up January 20th, 2008: Greed is up Part 1 & Part 2 ICWC ~ Title Histories (January 2008 -present) ICWC ~ Roster (updated June 2008)
