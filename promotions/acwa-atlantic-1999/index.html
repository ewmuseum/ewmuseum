A New Jersey federation active from September 1998, running twin weekly broadcasts and periodic supercards: a ten-man cage match crowning a new champion, a title change at its own first pay-per-view, and three title lineages traced across five served shows. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Atlantic Coast Wrestling Alliance dated its own first card, on air, to 2 September 1998 at the Atlantic City Convention Center, under founder and ring announcer Jonah Rosch and commentators Paul Jackson and “Sweetstuff” Scott, who went on to call every broadcast the promotion's surviving record holds. It ran regular weekly television alongside house shows and periodic supercards out of towns across Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

At Ground Zero (9 April 1999), its main event, a steel cage match, ended when William Craven grabbed the ACWA Championship belt off the top of the cage and fell through the timekeeper's table on the way down — ruled the winner regardless, since he landed holding the title.

At Painful Declaration (7 July 1999), a masked stranger billed as “Mickey Mouse” interrupted a bout involving the Rose and unmasked as the returning Steve Starr, head shaved and still scarred from an earlier attack by Craven, who then pinned the Rose alongside Craven himself — the two former enemies leaving the ring together as allies.

Square One (1 September 1999), billed on air as “the first Pay-Per-View in ACWA history,” crowned the promotion's first Television Champion through an eight-man tournament.

A house show on 18 December 1999 debuted a masked giant tag team, Towers of Havoc, managed by Howie Harding, squashing the jobber act the Pain Brothers ahead of a Tag Team Championship tournament that opened four days later. Its opening round saw the debuting Team Tradition advance, and a comedic fairy-themed pair calling themselves Precious Moments upset ACWA Heavyweight Champion “Showstoppa” Steve Starr and his partner Chad Shakespeare to advance as well.

That December broadcast, the promotion's own last surviving document, closes on plans for a supercard the following January; nothing dated later survives.
