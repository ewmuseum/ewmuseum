"Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart, 29 January 2000

29 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Perpetual darkness...Nothing more, nothing less is seen. The camera moves slightly as a moon lighted area is seen. The dim light shows a steel fence.
Metal bars going across connecting a series of bars stuck into the ground going up to a point..almost like a spear. The camera pans down the side of the fence until we come to a sign that reads "Eternal Rest Cemetery". The camera continues down a bit further and then turns as a hole in the fence is seen where two of the spear like bars have been parted...]
"AH-WOOOOO!!!"" [The sound of a howling stops the camera dead in it's tracks as it looks around the graveyard. Everything is completely silent however...The type of silence that makes your hair stand on end and ideas race through your mind. The camera turns once more almost as if to leave when the glow of a light is seen in the near distance. The camera begins to move towards it as we heard the sound of dirt being pelted into the air and hitting the ground. The camera now close as we see a lantern sitting on top of a headstone. It then moves in closer as nothing else is seen.]
[A brief moment later a clump of dirt is hurled into the air and crashes to the ground as the camera moves around the side of the headstone as we see a good sized grave, a pile of dirt is right next to it and a man with a shovel in hand stands inside of it. The man looks up as he jams the shovel into the ground and leans slightly on it. The dim mixture of the light from the moon with the light from the lantern hits his face as we see a face that has surely been the star of nightmares of many small children since he has walked into the ACWA, Vincent
Lockheart. Vincent is dressed in his usual attire, dirt is all over his face and body. His jet black hair hangs down, clinging to his face from sweat...]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - Is this something you all have wanted to see? [Lockheart lifts his arms up, holding them out almost in a crucifix..] VINCENT LOCKHEART - Me, standing in a grave...The dirt, the maggots... [Lockheart reaches to the side and pulls something from the dirt. He then holds it up as we see he is holding a worm in his hand..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - The worms...How so many of you would love to see me covered in this dirt..See me eating by the maggots and the worms, but I'm afraid...
[Lockheart closes his fist tightly, crushing the worm between his fingers and his palm before wiping the remains off on his shirt..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - That's not to be... [Lockheart says nothing more. He lifts the shovel and toss it out of the grave into the pile of dirt before lifting himself up and out as well. He gets to his feet, not bothering to dust himself off as he walks to the side of the headstone and takes the lantern from it..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - For it is not me who shall lay here..Nor any body at all, merely the soul of one man. The soul as well as everything that made him the man he was. The man who was changed forever at Y2K and will never be what he once was...That man <chuckles> Is..
[Lockheart moves the lantern to the headstone, the light shining on it as we see two simple words..]
"THE ROSE" [We hear the sound of maniacal laughter echo through the night. The sound of wings fluttering as birds quickly fly off of the limbs of trees is heard as the camera moves back to Lockheart..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - The Rose, he has walked away with his gold around his waist..and I will ask you the same question I asked just hours before you stepped into the ring knowing the consequences..
[Lockheart lifts the lantern up, the light gleaming off his face as he takes his free hand and moves his hair from his face showing the dried up blood of
TCK and The Rose still on his face..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - Was it worth it? Was it worth it Rose..Was the gold worth seeing your own flesh and blood..Your son beat within an inch of his life as you had to lay there, unable to protect him?
[Lockheart removes his hand, letting his hair fall back over his eyes as he laughs..] VINCENT LOCKHEART - Was it worth the pain...and the punishment I unleashed onto you? You were carted out on the stretcher..with the wonder of if you will ever be able to step into the ring again...or even walk again racing through your head. I have to ask you...
[Lockheart turns and picks a bunch of dead, black roses off of the tombstone..] VINCENT LOCKHEART - Was it worth it? [Vincent turns to the grave. He holds the roses to him and tilts his head down as if praying. He then moves his arm out and drops the roses into the grave before turning and picking the shovel up. He then begins to fill the hole in with dirt as the camera slowly pans into the hole as the roses are covered with dirt and the scene...]
[Fades to black...] [name withheld] Handles - James "Simply The Best" Pillow[GLOW,UEW] "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockhart[ACWA] The Best of the Best("Hollywood Gigolo" Shaun Riche & Jonathan Riche)[WWO]
