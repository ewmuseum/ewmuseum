http://www.1wwf.com/acwa/Home.html ---------- February 24th 1999 Fairless Hills High School, Fairless Hills, PA Match Writers: Jonah Rosch, [name withheld], [name withheld], and [name withheld] The house lights go down, and Nine Inch Nails, "Closer" is heard over the PA system. As the house lights go up, Jonah Rosch is standing in the ring. The crowd goes wild!!! Jonah: Ladies and gentlemen, are you ready to intense wrestling action? The crowd screams wildly in anticipation. Jonah: Are you ready Fairless Hills? Once again, the crowd goes nuts. Jonah: I said, "Are you ready?!!!" An even louder pop is heard from the crowd. Jonah: Well then prepare yourself because the Atlantic Coast Wrestling Alliance goes live in 5 4 3 2 1 Jonah: Hello everyone and welcome to the Atlantic Coast Wrestling
Alliance!!!!! The fans cheer wildly, waving their signs and clapping their hands. Jonah: The ACWA returns to Fairless Hills, Pennsylvania with some intense ring action. Tonight, you will see The Rose go up against the mysterious stranger, who revealed himself the other day on
ACWA television to be The Canadian Kid, as he returns to action tonight. Plus, you will see Maxx Madhouse take on the man known as The Disciple-Ian Brown. Dark Angel is to face one half of the ACWA Tag Team Champions as he faces Greg "The Tower"
Tallman. And in the main event, "Fabulous" Mark Farrow and Steve Starr take on the team of Flipstyle and Chad Shakespeare. The fans go nuts at the card. Jonah: Now, let's take you to the two men who are going to be calling the action. Paul Jackson, and the man who teaches stretching exercises for the interns at the White House, "Sweetstuff"
Scott Lane! Jackson: Thank you Mr. Rosch, and welcome to another night of exciting
ACWA action. Everyone is in anticipation for next week's big event. ACWA's big March event: ACWA: Take No Prisoners.
Lane: And the match that everyone has been talking about is the four-way dance between Flipstyle, Steve Starr, Chad
Shakespeare, and Hiroshima. It's going to be great. Jackson: Tonight's action should set all of that up rather nicely folks, and with that said, let's get down to ringside with our ring announcer, David Sturbridge.
===================================================== The Rose vs The Canadian Kid - Ladder Match ===================================================== Sturbridge: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is the ladder match, scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit, your referee for this contest is referee Will Adams.
Stone Cold Crazy by Metallica is heard over the loudspeakers, as The
Canadian Kid emerges from the backstage area wearing his black pants and red boots. He is wearing a T-Shirt that says
"Hardcore, eh?"
Sturbridge: Introducing competitor #1, he weighs in at 223 pounds and hails from Edmonton, Alberta Canada. This is The
Canadian Kid. The emergence of The Canadian Kid gets a pretty good pop from the fans. Obviously the underdog has won them over.
Jackson: TCK looking very good as he makes his way down to ringside. The question on everyone's mind is whether The Canadian Kid is going to be the one to stop the win streak of The
Rose. Lane: I doubt it. The Canadian Kid doesn't have the fortitude to defeat The Rose. I mean come on, TCK is from Canada. You can't expect someone from Canada to defeat The Rose can you?
TCK rushes towards the ring, dives through the bottom ropes jumps to the top turnbuckle and throws his T-Shirt to one of the fans in the first few rows. He then stretches in anticipation for his match.
Sturbridge: And his opponent.... The Rose by Bette Midler is heard over the PA system and the fans immediately start booing.
Sturbridge: He weighs in tonight at 225 pounds and hails from Coppenhagen, Denmark. He is accompanied to the ring by his manager Jesse Love. This is The Rose!
Emerging from behind the backstage area, Jesse Love wearing a floral print from the 60's leads the entourage. Following Jesse are four, bare chested, muscular, men. Jesse has a microphone and once
The Canadian Kid is looking his way, he begins to speak.
Jesse: 'The roses are for you, Canadian Baby. Me, and my boys, are going to lay them round your cretinous body when The
Prince Of Divinity shows you the real meaning of the word 'vengeance'. Before my son graces you unworthies with his magnificent presence he would like to make a statement."
Jackson: Oh what's this all about.... Lane: shhhhhh The Rose emerges from the backstage area and hobbling makes his way into the arena. Rose: As many of you probably know, I was attacked by The Canadian Kid a few nights ago. I had the doc take a look at my knee and he said it was in no shape to wrestle. I'm going to have it scoped in a couple of days and I'm supposed to stay off it.
But I talked it over with my manager Jesse before the fight. I owe it to you, the fans, to wrestle tonight. And one more thing…LIKE HELL SUCKERS!
At this the lights go down... And the crowd goes crazy Lane: What's that? Jackson: What's the matter Scott, do you need your teddy bear. Lane: Shut up Paul, I'll kick your butt if I can find you. The lights come up and The Rose is in the ring and nails TCK with a chair to the head. Rose immediately runs off the far ropes and nails TCK with a kick that throws him to the outside of the ring.
<DING...DING> Jackson: There's the bell, and it is obvious that The Rose needed that edge to try to take The Canadian Kid off of his game. The last time that these superstars met, TCK nearly came away with the victory except for the interference by Jesse Love and Mark
Farrow causing the injury to The Canadian Kid that has put him out of action for a little while.
Lane: Oh there was no chance of The Canadian Kid defeating this man. Look at him. He is the perfect specimen of manhood. Jackson: So, you've spent some time at Rose's Love Shack huh. Lane: What do you mean. Jackson: TCK is catching a breather outside the ring. He sees the four bodyguards making their way towards him, so he starts running around the outside of the ring and slides underneath the bottom rope and into the ring.
Lane: And he's immediately met by the boots of the Rose. You cannot stop this man. Jackson: TCK is pushed into the corner turnbuckle by The Rose. Reverse
Knife Edge by Rose. Man, you can hear that one all the way over here.
Lane: Ouch, that was a good one. Jackson: Rose is putting The Canadian Kid up on the top turnbuckle. He's going to superplex him. Lane: Say goodbye to the comeback. Jackson: The Canadian Kid grabs the head of The Rose. Tornado DDT! What a maneuver! Lane: What! Jesse, Do something. Jackson: The Canadian Kid is now in control. Canadian Kid pulls Rose over to the corner turnbuckle and he props The Rose's leg on the bottom rope.. TCK on the second turnbuckle facing away from the ring and drives his knee into the leg of The Rose.
The Rose hows in pain. TCK exacting the revenge that he has sought for weeks now. The crowd is starting to chant, TCK, TCK!!!! Lane: This is terrible. I can't believe that these inbred idiots are cheering for this loser and cheering when he is trying to injure such a great man as The Rose.
Jackson: Well, Rose tried to injure TCK. Lane: That's his perrogative. Jackson: TCK has The Rose by the legs with the Rose on his back. Looks like a wishbone is a comin'.
Lane: NO! Jackson: Rose is shaking his head no. And The Canadian Kid drops a leg right between the two. Rose is in trouble.
Lane: Disqualify him ref! Jackson: It's a ladder match, Sweetstuff! There are no rules. Lane: This is awful. Look at Jesse on the outside, he's biting his hand. He's so worried about his son.
Jackson: TCK, not letting up at all. Say what you will about the Rose, and I've heard the snickering, but he is an exceptional athlete, but so is the Canadian KID! OW MAN. TCK just nailed Rose with
"Cupid's Arrow". The flying dropkick that sent
Rose out of the ring. TCK now going outside to get the ladder. Lane: The Rose is knocked out. Jackson: TCK has the ladder and throws it under the ring ropes and into the ring. But TCK gets nailed by the Rose's bodyguards as he tries to get into the ring. They're holding him straight. And Jesse Love comes over and kicks TCK in the midsection.
Lane: Look, TCK is hurt by that move. Jackson: Jesse calls off the body guards, and they drop TCK who then nails Jesse Love with a clothesline and starts nailing him with a series of punches. But from behind, the Rose comes up behind
TCK and nails him with the chair.
The crowd immediately falls silent as TCK crumples to the floor. The Rose stands there with his arms outstretched as Jesse sprays him with perfume from a spray bottle.
Jackson: Oh good lord. Jesse then hands the spray bottle to The Rose who sprays The Canadian
Kid in the face. Jackson: Good god! You can't do that. That's alcohol. That'll kill his eyes. Lane: Well, he may be blind now, but he smells a lot better now. Jackson: I cannot believe you. Rose is bringing TCK over this way. Wait a minute. Lane: This is our table. The Rose plants TCK on the announcers table and climbs up to the top turnbuckle. Jackson: The Rose nails TCK with a splash and both of them went right through our table. Both men are down.
Lane: I can't believe that. Either Jonah has been really cheap with his tables or Rose came off that with a tremendous impact.
Jackson: Rose is now getting to his feet and he's climbing into the ring. Lane: It's all over. He's setting up the ladder. He's climbing up. Jackson: TCK is getting to his feet, but Rose has already begun his ascent. TCK climbing the outside. Oh my god, he nailed
The Rose with a dropkick. Rose falls face first ontop of the ladder. Lane: That's unbelievable. But TCK is down too. Jackson: Both men getting to their feet. Rose takes TCK down with the clothesline. And Rose is rolling TCK over into a Fab
Crab. Lane: Uh-oh, that got him in trouble last time. Jackson: He's really synching back on it. And here comes Mark Farrow. And he does not look happy. Lane: Doesn't he realize this is important. Jackson: TCK is able to reach the ropes and the referee is making him break the hold. Lane: But the damage has been done. I don't know if TCK will be able to climb the ladder. Jackson: Farrow has a steel chair in his hands, he's motioning to Rose to move TCK over to him.
Lane: Rose has TCK by the arms and is moving him over to Farrow. This is gonna be great. Jackson: Farrow swing, but TCK is able to duck out of the way. Rose gets nailed by the chair. Farrow looking in disbelief.
TCK grabs Farrow by the neck and nails his head across the top of the ring ropes. Lane: I can't believe this. Jackson: The Canadian Kid is now setting up the ladder. He's climbing it to Rose's gold plated shovel over top the ring.
Lane: Get up. Jackson: Jesse Love is slamming his hands against the mat. Rose is coming to. He sees The Canadian Kid climbing the ladder.
Canadian Kid almost has the shove. Lane: He's not going to make it. Rose is going to crash him to the ground. Jackson: Rose is to his feet. He drop kicks the ladder. TCK grabs the shovel as the ladder is kicked out from underneath him!!!! TCK has won the match. The Canadian Kid has gotten the shovel first, but TCK lands knee first on the ladder.
<DING DING> Sturbridge: The winner of the match, the Canadian Kid. Jackson: TCK won the match, but Rose is laying the boots to him. Here comes Jesse Love and the body guards. Rose has the shovel and is slamming it into the knee of The Canadian Kid.
Lane: Well, it looks like The Canadian Kid may have been the first to get the kiss. But it is The Rose who is screwing The
Canadian Kid. Jackson: Rose completely enraged. He slams that shovel right into the knee of The Canadian Kid. The Canadian Kid howling in pain. Here comes Mark Farrow, who starts laying the boots on TCK.
Lane: Uh-oh, Rose has that look in his eye. Jackson: The Rose pushes Mark Farrow. He's asking him what happened. Lane: He can't think that Farrow hit him with the chair on purpose. Jackson: I don't know what he thinks, but the whole situation with
Rose stealing Farrow's move and Farrow nailing Rose with the chair. Well, The Rose and his entourage have left The Canadian Kid a bloody mess in the middle of the ring. Here comes
The Disciple. Lane: What's he doing here. Jackson: The Disciple has come down to help out The Canadian Kid. Rose and Farrow and the entourage are leaving the ring, but you have think that the Alliance of Violence are licking their chops right now. If Farrow and Rose are not focused on the match next week, the Alliance will have them for breakfast.
Lane: That's true. Jackson: Here comes the stretcher. The Canadian Kid has gotten a victory over The Rose, but at what cost to this young man's career. I just hope that The Canadian Kid will return to action in the
ACWA quite soon.

Almost immediately, loud booing fills the entire area. Cadrillo appears at the top of the ramp with a huge smile on his face, unfazed by the crowd’s reaction. He begins to make his way down the ramp.
Behind him follow Tristan Singhal with an expression of disdain on his face and his manager Manny Crispoli who seems to be arguing with somebody on his cell-phone.
Lane: Cadrez looks full of energy tonight. Diamond Dog is in big trouble. Jackson: Actually Sweetstuff, if you’d ever bother reading the stuff you get handed on fight nights, you’d know that Diamond
Dog hasn’t been seen yet this evening. Lane: What? I knew it! He is afraid of the “Local Flava”! Jackson: That’s not likely, Scott. Ah, here comes Jonah Rosch, President of the ACWA to let us know the scoop. Rosch steps into the ring and his handed a microphone. He holds his hands up for quiet and the crowd noise slowly subsides.
Rosch: Ladies and gentlemen, are we having a great time, or what? Once again the crowd erupts into cheering until Jonah signals for quiet once again. Rosch: Unfortunately, Diamond Dog had some very important personal business to take care of, so our scheduled match between Diamond Dog and Luiz Cadrillo Cadrez will not happen this evening.
Jonah is practically knocked a step back by the volume of noise filling the room. Over in the corner a gleeful Cadrez give
Manny a high-five and an “I-told-you-so-look”. Yet again Jonah signals for quiet which seems to take a few more moments to achieve.
Rosch: Fear not! Would I ever let you down? The ACWA always delivers excitement! You came here to see a match and that’s what you’re gonna see!!
Deafening cheers follow as Rosch hands back the microphone. Turning to leave he finds Manny Crispoli standing in front of him, obviously not happy. They seem to exchange a few heated words and
Rosch waves him away and exits.
Jackson: Well, it appears that despite not having an opponent, Cadrez gets to wrestle tonight anyway. I’ve just been handed a note saying that the win will officially go to Cadrez by forfeit, so the fight we get to see now is going to be purely two men giving it their all.
Lane: Well, I guess it’s the same result had DD shown up or not. Cadrez would’ve wiped up the floor with him. Jackson: Uh, yeah sure. The audience seems to be as curious as Cadrez is about who his opponent for the match is going to be.
Let’s go back to ringside where it looks like Sturbridge is about to enlighten us. Strubridge: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for twenty minutes with one fall. In this corner, with his manager and his tag-team partner, the “Local Flava’”, Luiz Cadrillo Cadrez!!!!
Cadrez raises his arms in the air and makes a few rude gestures in response to the boos that follow.
Sturbridge: And his opponent….. The room is absolutely silent as Sturbridge milks the pause for all he can get. Sturbridge: ……from the Age of Legends….Rapier!! “Princes of the Universe” echo throughout the arena and the crowd is on its feet as Rapier makes his way to the ring.
Lane: <moan> Great, so now he gets to fight the teachers pet. Jackson: Well, it looks like Rapier’s short hiatus is definitely over for now as he makes his second appearances in as many weeks.
Rapier enters the ring and sets his sword in his corner, pauses and turns to face his opponent. Cadrez, seeming oblivious to his presence, is in the middle of a heated argument with his manager,
Manny. Rapier walks slowly across the ring and taps Cadrez on his shoulder. Cadrez turns to look at him and Rapier nails him with a backhand across the mouth. Manny turns and scuttles out of the ring.
Jackson: Oh my goodness! Cadrez does not seem happy about that, it looks like they've locked up but Rapier’s size seems to be giving him the advantage. Whoah, Cadrez just reversed it and threw
Rapier into the ropes!
Lane: Yeah, throw that Don Quixote wannabe around! Jackson: Tristan grabbed Rapier and is choking him out against the ropes. Where’s the ref?
Lane: Actually, I don’t remember one being announced, go figure. Looks like Rapier is going to have to deal with Cadrez’s superior tactics without help.
Jackson: Tactics? That’s just plain cheating. Lane: Whatever it is, it seems to be working. Cadrez is pummeling away at Rapier who can’t seem to get leverage. Way to go
Singhal! Good tag team cooperation. Jackson: Gee, Sweetstuff, did you happen to forget this isn’t a tag team match? Rapier is up! He got his feet under him and managed to drag Singhal up into the ring with him. Cadrez off the far ropes, going for a clothesline…
Lane: Uh-oh, not a smart move right now. Jackson: Good call, Scott, it looks like Rapier managed to drop under it and Cadrez just took down Singhal. He’s down on the mat clutching at his throat.
Lane: Ugh, that’s gonna leave a mark. Jackson: Rapier goes low and takes Cadrez to the mat where he can do his thing. Cadrez doesn’t like being nailed down, he’s a fast mover and Rapier’s strength is going to dominate him if he doesn’t get out of that hold.
Lane: Just until Manny nails Rapier with that chair. Now, there’s what having a manager is for!
Jackson: Well, as “Sweetstuff” said, Manny Crispoli has entered the ring with chair in hand and is raising it above his head.
Neither of the men on the mat appear to have noticed him. This is going to hurt! He swings….Rapier let go of the hold and rolled out of the way!!! Manny hit his own wrestler with the chair!
Cadrez is completely stunned he’s having trouble getting to his feet. Lane: Gee, great shot, Manny. That’s why I always did my own stuff. You just can’t count on somebody else to do it right. Jackson: Rapier has turned his attention to Crispoli who shrinks away holding up his hands in supplication. Rapier glares at him for a moment and turns toward Cadrez who is still quite dazed. Oh no, here comes Crispoli again. It looks like he’s got his cell phone in his hand and is swinging towards the back of Rapier’s head.
Rapier sidestepped it! He must have been expecting it! Lane: Looks like Rapier is getting sick of these people, he just chucked Manny out of the ring using his own momentum against him. Someone stop that man, he’s beating up innocent bystanders!
Jackson: Hardly. But it does appear that you may, for once, be correct. Rapier rolls both Singhal and Cadrez, who are both still quite stunned from hits they received from their own people, out of the ring. Manny helps them both up the ramp and out of the arena.
Lane: That’s it?!? That’s how this thing is going to end?! Jackson: It would appear so. The crowd seems to love it though, they’re on their feet cheering Rapier who has his arms raised in victory.
Lane: If it wasn’t for Manny, they would’ve had him. Jackson: Stop pouting. Well, I guess Rapier wins the match, but Cadrez wins it because of Diamond Dog’s no-show. Looks like everybody wins this time around.
