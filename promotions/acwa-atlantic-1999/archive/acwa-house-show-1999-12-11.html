House show, 11 December 1999

The following is the ACWA house show for December 11, 1999, exactly as it appeared to the mailing list at the time. Enjoy this first writing sample, as it is indicative of the writing style in which JJG dark matches will be portrayed.
-- [name withheld]. E-Wrestling's most polarized non-entity. "* TheHolyEyebrow raises. * TheSacredSpleen vents. * EnlightenedNipple tweaks. * BlessedToeNail claws. [TheHolyEyebrow] Excellent. Now if only Mat would join us as Jesusbeard, we could be complete~!" --- Keep in mind that this house show summary is written from the POV of a fictional wrestling fan. IE - there is no real person masquerading as "The
Mysterious ACWA Fanboy"... He's written as something of a young fan, not a professional writer, just remember that when you read the below.
Pretty obvious, but I may as well say it anyway. :) ACWA Fedhead, [name withheld]. ---- Wow, what a week. As a wrestling fan, I've been watching the ACWA since it first started more than a year ago. There were only a few hundred people during that first taped show, and I couldn't believe my luck to be there. I knew that I was witness to history, that this league would really be something.
For the first time ever, the ACWA came to my home town of Westchester new york this week, and I was excited. My brothers and I all managed to grab some tickets, and we went down to the County Center, and we saw a great show.
Looking around, I saw that there was just one cameraman who was using a camcorder to record all the action. And then it occurred to me: Nobody's going to see this, and nobody's even going to know what happens tonight! No film footage (except from that cameraman, who I think might have been just another fan), no nothing, no witnesses.
So I decided to write up a summary of what went on for all the fans who would otherwise never know what happened! It went like this.
Warmup matches. Sulkeys vs Pony Express. Nobody cared about these, and neither did I, quite frankly. First off, some guys named the "Pony Express" came out and just destroyed a pair of brothers called "The Sulkeys". The Sulkeys looked like depressed, skinny, science geeks with long blonde hair. If nothing else, the Pony Express was at least in shape. They came out in their black trunks with horse logos on the hips and bounced those Sulkeys around the ring. Their finisher was pretty basic, though. They ran at one Sulkey from opposite corners and both clotheslined him. It was a neat sandwich move, but again, basic.
Crackhead vs Motown Man. Then there was Crackhead taking on Motown Man. Motown was a bizarre, chunky asian man dressed up as a 60's jive turkey. Very funny. Crackhead's just...Crackhead. He's got red hair, wears all black, carries a pipe to the ring and talks about being hardcore. Apparently these two have a feud going, although this was the first I'd heard of it. The match started out weakly, with mostly restholds and punches. We all started to chant "boring"
over and over and both men ran around the ring covering their ears. That was funny. The whole thing ended when Motown Man took a shot from
Crackhead's pipe. The referee didn't see it (of course), but Motown went down hard. I guess Crackhead had a match with Johnny Rebel, but Rebel was fired or quit or something. No big loss, although it would have been better than this.
Preston Mayfield vs El Loco Gato. I was actually marking out when Preston came to the ring. Gato is just
Gato, I mean, he's like a midget. I'm a head taller than the little luchadore, and I'm only 5'11. But Mayfield came staggering out like he was drunk and looking very hung over (if you can do both at the same time, he did it). He seemed to have trouble getting in the ring at first, and when he got in, the referee backed him up to the corner and was asking him if he could wrestle. He just stared around. The referee slapped him a few times, and Mayfield just stared at him. Then Gato came up behind the referee to try to hurry up Mayfield, and Preston jabbed him in his eye!
It was funny. Mayfield shunted the referee aside and immediately hit Gato with a low blow. The referee acted like he was going to DQ the guy and he like just cringed back. Then, when the referee finally decided to let the match go, Mayfield just walked up to Gato (who was still hurt by the poke and sacking) and hit him with a backhand slap. Not only did it knock down the little lucha, but it actually turned the mask to the side of his face!
It was hilarious! After turning the mask around completely backwards, Mayfield seemed to have the match well in hand. He punched him in the kidneys, kicked at his knees, and even got the mask halfway off! Once Gato's mouth was exposed, Mayfield shoved both thumbs in the sides of the little guy's mouth, and tried to rip his cheeks off! My brothers and I were rollin'!
The referee got Mayfield off though. Most of the guys in the building were booing him right about now, so we did too. Gato got his mask back on straight, and Mayfield rushed him again. A drop toehold put him down, and
Gato started beating on Mayfield's face. After a minute, he layed still.
While Gato was celebrating though, Mayfield got up and tried for another nutshot. Gato turned around and Mayfield fell to his knees, begging for mercy. Gato asked if he should cut Preston a break, and while he was looking at the crowd, Mayfield hit him with some metal thing from his pocket!
(I think it was a liquor tin. I don't know for sure, though.) After that it was 7 more minutes of all Mayfield. He caught the staggered lucha dude, hit him with a DDT, then went to doing hamstring pulls, dropping elbows on the guy's knees, and finally he applied a leglock submission. The announcers called it "The Preston Mayfield Manifesto", even though all he did was hook the guy's toe in his armpit, and wrap his legs around the other guy's leg and fall down. I mean, it's just a leglock, but it worked pretty well on a guy who's only 135 pounds.
Gato lost, and had to be helped away from the ring. Preston just went into the crowd, following some redhead girl. I like that guy.
"Precious Moments" (Binky and Winky Fairy) vs "Fat Man and Little Boy" ("Hippo" Higgins and Superfreak). That was Preston's debut, and then I got to see another debut. First, out came "Hippo" Higgins. He talked about how the Fairies were the "biggest fruit of all" and he was going to "chomp them". The crowd called him boring
(just like always), and his partner, Superfreak acted like he was horribly embarrassed.
Why they'd call themselves "Fat Man and Little Boy" I'll never know. I mean, who wants to be called fat or called a boy? Nobody. Hell, why are these jobbers even together? I don't get it.
Then I heard that weird Disney song, "It's a small world" or something. Of all things, two guys dressed as Fairies came out! They were dressed in tutus! It was funny as hell!. One guy looked like a real powerhouse.
Probably weighed over 250 or something. The other guy was a borderline cruiser/heavy. Not too bad off, but not exactly a badass.
Like anyone could look like a badass when they're dressed up like a fairy. Anyway, the big fairy started out against Hippo. It was kinda cool, because the big fairy matched Hippo for power. I guess since he was in shape and about 250, he could be as strong as Hippo who's like 300. I could see Hippo carrying around about 50 extra pounds.
Anyway, he bodyslammed the chubby guy, hit a slingshot bodyblock, a somersault splash, etc, and almost finished the match with a tigerbomb.
Superfreak made the save, though, when he pulled off a guillotine legdrop on the back of big fairy's head. While the ref was chewing out Superfreak, little fairy ran in to help his partner, who was getting choked by Hippo.
Superfreak pointed it out, and suddenly the ref was chasing the Fairy out! Hippo and Superfreak must have had like an HOUR to work over the big fairy. Okay, maybe not an hour, but they did for awhile. So Superfreak gets tagged in, and he's kicking the big fairy in the head, in the knee, and then he tries for a bodyslam. Big fairy's like 100 pounds heavier though, right? So he just hit a belly to belly suplex and fell down.
It took a minute, but he tagged in little fairy. Superfreak was holding onto his legs the whole time too, which was really funny, seeing him get dragged across the ring like that. When little fairy came in, he hit
Superfreak with an asai moonsault right away. After that, he ran at Hippo, who was coming in, and knocked him out of the ring. He took a second to yell at how Hippo was evil for coming in when it wasn't his turn or something. I don't get that, but it was funny.
By now, everybody's cheering the fairies, which was cool. I got into it too, because the rest of the guys did. They started tagging in and out really quick, getting off some fancy moves. First, the big fairy powerbombed the little fairy onto Superfreak's chest, but Hippo made the save before the pin could happen. Then Hippo got tagged, and they doubleteamed him. They both climbed the same turnbuckle and did a double splash, holding hands the whole time! I thought this was kinda gay, but my brothers dug it.
I kinda wonder about those guys sometimes. Anyway, superfreak tried to make the save, but got cut off by big fairy. It didn't matter, Hippo was too big for little fairy to hold down. He managed to get to his corner and tag in the Freak.
It was all downhill from there, though. A big miscommunication from Hippo and Freak, and Hippo fell on the floor again. I still don't know how it happened exactly, but Superfreak knocked the big guy off the apron when he dropkicked the little fairy into Hippo. The little fairy took a few more shots, but got to his corner. Big fairy picked up Superfreak and little fairy somersaulted over him, hitting a neckbreaker!
It looked pretty cool. Sorta like Joe Gonzo's finisher, "Mind Eraser". Anybody else remember Joe? I hope he gets back soon, last I heard, somebody had seen him upstate in Buffalo. That kinda sucks, since most ACWA shows don't take place in New York, much less in Buffalo. So I guess he's gone.
Anyway, the fairies both pinned little Superfreak, and celebrated. When they left, one of them touched my nose with his wand! My brothers told me I can fly now... I hate them.
Samuel "Mule" Lowenstein vs "Tomorrow Man" Dillon De Stride. Here's one I've been waiting on. Dillon's like...badass, and he's been kicking everybody around lately, you know? He destroyed Bobby Pride, he killed Rupert Shark (RIP Rupert's career now that he's gone, I guess), and he just basically beat everybody he's fought.
Then there's Samuel Lowenstein, who's allied with that McMullen guy now, I guess. He's been running in against Vincent Lockheart lately, which is a stupid thing to do, really. I mean, would you attack a 300 pound goth freak who uses swords and fire for toys? Me neither.
Anyway, these guys started off pretty heavy. First off, Dillon charged
Mule and started beating on his head with fists and stuff. In about 2 minutes he was choking the guy out on the ropes, which made the referee threaten to DQ him. Everybody started booing, but I don't know why. I mean, just about everybody in wrestling cheats anymore. And it was just a little choke.
So Dillon gets up off of Mule, and starts yelling at the referee. On the outside Summer Jewell Jestuss grabbed Lowenstein's head and pulled down! I mean, this hot chick is dangling from the guy's neck and hanging him across the top rope. I like having chicks hang on me, but this wasn't cool.
(She was right in front of me, btw, and man, great butt, I tell you.) Dillon backed the referee up against the ropes and stuff, and yelled at him loud enough so everybody could hear him. Just general threats and claims of
Lowenstein dissing him or something, nothing special. Finally he turned around, and Summer let go. He went back and started laying knees into the guy's neck, it was kinda cool.
As the match wore on, Lowenstein started fighting back. He hit a quick bodyslam, did a fireman's carry takedown, and finished his combo with a butterfly suplex. While he was celebrating though, Dillon hit him from behind with a low blow. It kinda sucked, because everybody was just popping like mad for him. He's caught on lately by taking on Lockheart and helping
McMullen. Kinda funny for a guy who hadn't even won a match yet at the time.
Anyway, it kept going like this for a few minutes. Dillon hammers Mule,
Mule gets a quick few moves in, Dillon does a cheap trick. Summer grabbed
Mule's ankle once, and Dillon knocks him to the floor. Summer almost got hit with the guy's flying body, and she backed right up to me!
(She smells as good as she looks, btw.) Oh, and although my brothers won't admit it, Preston Mayfield walked right up next to me about then. He was checking out Summer too. It was wicked, he dared me to touch her butt because she was right there, but when DDS came down, he ran out. De Stride didn't see him at all, but he threatened to hit my brother Frank! A few security guys came over to stop him, though. I mean, yeah, Summer got close to us, but he still wasn't allowed to hit fans, right?
Mule just layed there for a minute, getting stomped, and Dillon picked him up to toss him into some ringsteps. Suddenly, he did something cool, though. He grabbed DDS by the back of the head and hit a drop toehold!
DDS' face hit the steel, and Mule shoved him back in at the 9 count! Following the guy in, Lowenstein ducked a wild swing from DDS. After that, he hit 3 rolling german suplexes, tossing the guy around like mad! Then it was on to his finisher, the "Lowest Common Denominator", but DDS grabbed the ropes, and Mule couldn't lift him.
DDS seemed to come back about then. He hit a quick backdrop out of the DDT setup, pulled the tape off of Mule's nose (I guess McMullen broke his nose on wednesday), and punched him repeatedly. Out of the blue, though, Mule grabbed the guy's head and knee and pulled him down for a pin. It didn't take, and DDS got up kicking, nailing Mule in the groin with a kick, and then hitting his finisher! Killing in the name!
It was over! Right!? WRONG! Mule had his feet in the ropes. I don't even think he put them there, they just got tangled up when DDS rolled him onto his back. The referee saw it, and called off the pin. Too bad, because it looked like you could make a 5 count on Mule about then and he wouldn't twitch. He pulled the guy up again, for a second KiTN, and Mule hit a backdrop!
I couldn't believe it. Everybody was screaming! People leaned forward and
I almost fell over the guardrail with about 20 people on my back! It was just that cool! DDS got up first and tried to grab Mule, but just got shoved into the corner. 4 punches, a drop toehold, and then Mule headbutted
DDS in the back of the head like 10 times!
(I guess that's called the "Mule Butt", although I didn't know Mules hit things with their heads.)
After all that, DDS still got up swinging! I was really impressed. Summer started screaming on the outside and jumping up and down. He hit Mule like
4 times, knocking him backwards, and kicked Mule in the gut. Mule caught the kick, and DDS tried to punch him again. Mule caught the punch, pulled him in, grabbed his head and hit the ugliest DDT I've ever seen! It was like a fisherman's suplex DDT thing! He held onto the guy's leg, and pinned him!
Everybody was screaming, and Mule got up, looking satisfied. He played to the crowd and had his hand raised, and afterwards, he walked around with a pen, signing autographs. That sold me. I'm a Mule fan now.
All in all, a great night (except for the warmup matches, I don't care to see Crackhead ever again). My brothers say that they want to start driving around and catching the ACWA shows wherever they go, which I really dig. I can't catch the Revolution shows, because I have school, but hell, these house shows are all on Saturdays. So I guess if you're at one, you might see me. I'll be the one in the front row with a pad of paper, taking notes for these writeups.
Man, I love ACWA. The Mysterious ACWA Fanboy.
