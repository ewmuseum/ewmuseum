ACWA House Show — 18 December 1999

18 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another great house show for the ACWA! I almost didn't get to enjoy it, however, as this week it was clear in
White Oak, Pennsylvania. The White Oak Athletic Association housed this one, and it was a relatively large venue compared to the bars, clubs, and highschools the ACWA was using exclusively until recently, so I really rather wanted to see it. My brother (the one who actually owns the car we use to go to these shows) didn't want to drive the many miles from
Westchester, New York clear down to PA. I'm glad I went, though. Even though we were fighting all the way there. Walking in, you could almost smell the blood. The ACWA was alive tonight! But first, we had to sit through the (yuck) warmup matches. If you didn't read what I wrote last week, or just forget what a warmup match is, let me tell you. It's a crap match with crap wrestlers that nobody's ever heard of because they can never get on TV. Or if they do, it's just to lose horribly to a more badass wrestler.
Starting out, we had a real stinker... Warmup matches. "Hippo" Higgins vs the Sulkeys. Well, one of the Sulkeys, but they look alike, and they kept switching when the ref wasn't looking.
First, out came the Sulkeys. Rob and Jake. They look totally alike, I swear. Twins, or something. And they're so little! There was some generic
1980's "hair band" music playing as they came out. I guess that's the 80's equivalent of heavy metal, but it sounded pretty gay to me.
Now, I hate to see Higgins as much as the next guy, but there he was, coming down the aisle just RUINING AC/DC's "Thunderstruck" for everyone...
I mean, I liked that song before, but he just sucks so bad. Before he even said anything on the mic (which he always has to do before a match for some reason) we were all chanting "BORING"...
I swear, I don't even want to see this guy. Why is he working for the ACWA? Anyway, Rob was supposed to be the one fighting, and he started out against
Hippo. They locked up, and Hippo shoved him down. Sulkey ran at him, and bounced off. Etc, etc, just kept getting bounced around. Then, when the referee wasn't looking, they switched out, and Jake was in the ring.
(Well, I think it was Jake.) And the same thing happened...about 4 times. Finally, Higgins hit his
"finisher" (in quotations because it is LAME!) which he calls the "Fruit chomp" on one of the Sulkeys... Not sure which one, but it finished the match.
All the "Fruit chomp" is is an ear clap. Y'know, he slaps his hands on either side of a guy's head? Lame.
Superfreak vs nondescript fellow. I know, it sounds like a joke, but I don't think Sturbridge even said the guy's name. He was about 6' tall, had brown hair, wore a T-Shirt and jeans.
Just looked like a high school jock to me. No music, just got in the ring. I don't get it. Then there was Superfreak, who came out to "Superfreak" (natch) by Rick
James. That was kinda cool. He danced a lot as he came out, which wasn't cool, because he was doing these weird 70's dances that my parents always seem to talk about. Stupid disco crap.
Anyway, Superfreak started out with some high flying stuff. He bounced the
"fellow" all over the place. The nondescript guy came back, though, hitting a bodyslam, backdrop, clothesline, and other basic moves.
This one was very short for some reason, ending with Superfreak hitting a hurracanrana on the guy. He was happy, and the crowd cheered him a little.
I guess it was his first win, or something. Then we got onto the good stuff. Actual cool matches. Masahiro Takashi vs El Loco Gato. I liked this one just because we had the little lucha, Gato, taking on a
Japanese Pure-oso (or whatever it's called) star, Masahiro Takashi.
Anyway, takashi's actually got a little hype in ACWA because he's one of only a few japanese guys in the fed. And the first Pure-oso guy. Add to that the fact that Strickland supposedly imported him special and, well, he just has to be good, right?
He didn't fail to impress. Starting out, Gato seemed to have a major speed advantage. He bounced all over the place, hitting dropkicks to just about every spot on Takashi's body, and finishing with a top rope headscissors into a pin.
But let's face it, he's outweighed by about 100 pounds, right? He's not going to pin a guy that quick who's that much bigger than he is.
So Takashi kicked out real quick, and Gato was on him with a headlock instantly. They both got to their feet, and Masahiro hit a bigass backdrop driver! Everybody started screaming because of the snap he put on the move, and it hit so fast and hard!
Gato got up quick, holding his neck, and was hit with a T-Bone suplex right off the bat! Gato layed there, and Takashi hit a frog splash off the top!
We were all going wild as the referee counted to 3... ...But Gato's foot was on the ropes... The lucha recovered some. He knocked Takashi out of the ring with a spinning leg lariat, then off the apron with a dropkick to the face.
Finishing off the combo, he nailed a tope suicida right on Takashi's head! Then he ran around celebrating and got in the ring. When Takashi got in, he tried a hurracanrana, but got powerbombed out of it!
That pretty much ended Gato's offense. Instead of trying for a pin, Takashi decided to make sure Gato'd stay down. He got him up in a torture rack, then hit what looked like a DVD, but uglier, because it was backwards. Then another frogsplash.
Convinced that Gato was gone, he put him on the top rope, got up on the turnbuckle, and grabbed him in a crucifix position.
(This was cool) Then he jumped off, nailing a crucifix powerbomb, and grabbing the legs for the pin! Gato couldn't move at all! The referee counted to three as we all screamed for the awesome move!
Then Takashi picked Gato up to shake his hand. It almost didn't work, because Gato was really out of it. The little lucha managed to stand, and shake hands, but still had to be helped away from the ring. He was really, really banged up after taking those nasty bumps.
Damien Omega vs Crackhead. Crackhead (who my brothers and I call "the redheaded stepchild") came out, pipe in hand, claiming to be "hardcore" and crap, etc, etc. He's pretty boring, but he doesn't suck as bad as some.
Then out came Omega. I've never seen this guy before, but he's from New
York, so I cheered some. Damien's a total rookie, I guess. Talented, but green, straight out of training. He looks kinda nondescript, but not too bad.
When the bell rang, Crackhead grabbed a microphone and demanded that the match be fought under his rules... Meaning that he wanted it to be a garbage match. I kinda liked garbage, but I don't like Crackhead, so I just kept quiet.
Most everyone else was booing though. Damien looked at Crackhead funny, not understanding what he was talking about. He might have been a little intimidated by this guy who was pretty darn big, a lot bigger than he is. The referee finally just took
Crackhead's pipe away, and told both men to get it on. Instead, Crackhead just pursued the referee, demanding his pipe back.
Damien then rolled him up, and we all cheered! He kicked out, and the match wore on like this. Crackhead tried to brawl and punch at Damien, who kept throwing Crackhead around. Everytime
Crackhead would get in a few punches, he'd then get armdragged or something. I thought Damien had him when he hit a nasty looking german suplex, but the big doofus kicked out again.
Crackhead actually got off a moonsault and almost pinned Damien. Omega was a little bit staggered after a haymaker, and got squished under the move, but he kicked out...barely.
From there, Crackhead actually managed to rain a little pain on Damien. Punches, kicks, and a nasty powerbomb (sloppily applied, I think he landed on his head!) that I thought ended the match. Damien managed to come back, however, and hit a quick legwhip to put down Crackhead before applying some weird, crooked figure four move. Crackhead fought against it, but had to give up or have his leg broke sideways. Damien won, and we were all impressed, cheering for him.
Crackhead limped to the back as Damien celebrated. If nothing else, I know
Omega can wrestle, now.
Towers of Havoc vs The Pain Brothers. Okay, so everybody knows what the Pain Brothers look like, right? They're kinda stupid looking, they're from "California" (where in Cali, I dunno), and they're like techno punk guys. Kinda intimidating, right?
Well, then you have to see the Towers of Havoc. First off, this goofy manager guy came out with the microphone and a cell phone. He turns off the phone, and starts talking. 'The Towers are the most devastating" etc etc,
"dangerous, maniacal", etc ,etc...
So, I'm wondering what all the hype's about, right? Then, suddenly, Howie screams that they're coming, and the curtains part... This whacked out
Megadeth song (just the music, not the words) blasts over the PA, and out come two SEVEN FOOT TALL MEN!
Two big black guys, they just walk out, calm as you please! We all sorta mumbled and muttered as they walked to the ring, put _on_ their masks (plain black vinyl things), got in, and attacked the Pain Brothers before the bell.
From here on in, there's not much to describe. Just clotheslines, doubleteaming, powerbombs, chokeslams, Pain Brothers hitting mat, turnbuckle, steel, concrete, post, guardrail, etc, etc. They pushed the 10 count, they almost got DQ'ed, then they dragged both men in, and doubleteam finished them.
First, they did a double chokeslam on Wayne Pain. Then they did a Powerbomb/neckbreaker on Shane Pain! I thought they broke his neck! He just layed there, twitching.
Then, they both pinned, and the referee counted, but they both pulled up their victim's heads before the 3 count!
From there, it was more of the same. Chokeslams, powerbombs, Howie Harding
(Towers' manager) got in and started screaming for them to do this and that to their opponents. Both the Pain Brothers were like limp dishrags. We were all booing by now. Finally, the referee DQ'ed both of them. They covered their ears for a second while we booed, then kept beating on the
Brothers until about 20 security guys came down and dragged them from the ring!
If they wanted to send a message, I guess they did that. These two are scary, just really scary. I'm not sure what happened to Shane and Wayne, because they never said anything about them after they got stretchered out.
I'm no fan of the Pain Brothers, but damn, they were bleeding and twitching and convulsing and twisted. It was just that bad!
Bobby Pride vs "H.A.T.E." Kraven Smith. This one I was sorta looking forward to. Pride's a cool guy, and Smith's a badass. I thought it'd be good, but starting out, they both seemed distracted. Pride made with a few boxing punches, Smith came back with a mafia kick. The fight quickly went outside. Rather than count both guys out, the ref went outside with them, and sorta watched them brawl.
Pride seemed to have a major advantage at first, nailing Smith with punch after punch. Lefts, rights, hooks, jabs, to the body, to the head, kidneys, just everywhere. Smith turned it around by running him headfirst into the ringsteps. Pride just sorta collapsed then. Smith picked him up and rammed him headfirst into the steps again and again, finishing with a posting
(ramming him into the ringpost). He then dragged him back in and tried for a pin.
The pin didn't take, and they started up at it again. Pride hit a running powerslam, and a fistdrop. Smith got right back up, and was taken down in a suplex by Pride. A few more shoot-style punches (sorta hunching down on the guy, too close for him to fight back, and punching him in the ribs and head), and Smith rolled him over. A few shots from Smith, and Pride rolled him over, and they were both in the ropes.
The referee separated them, and they both got up. Pride tried a clothesline, but got hit with a reverse russian legsweep from Smith. Smith tried a pin, but it didn't take. Pulling him up, he nailed a double underhook powerbomb.
And on and on, more brawling, a few more power moves. Smith got up to the top, but got belly to belly suplexed down to the mat! We all popped big for
Pride, because that move kicked ass! His manager, on the outside, got up and cheered Bobby. Pride told him to get down, but he didn't react at all.
It looked to me like he wanted to hand Bobby something, but all he wound up doing was distracting his own man, and the referee, for that matter.
Coming up from nowhere, Smith hit with a lowblow, then nailed Pride with a
DVD! That did it, the referee turned around just in time to count, 1, 2, 3!
Most everyone booed the dirty tactics. I just booed because I didn't really like the match. I could've sworn these two were better. Oh well, maybe it's just 'cause it was a house show. I guess I'll never know.
So, the highlight of the evening seemed to be the Takashi matchup, and the rest was pretty good, save a few things. I sure am glad I made the trip to
Pennsylvania. Even though my brother's car needs a new fuel pump now.
(We ran it past empty. I guess you can't do that. At least we got home before it gave out on us, and I got all my notes for the house show report.)
For now, I'm outtie. With any luck I'll get to the Revolution taping this week. Ogdensburgh's pretty close to where I live. Closer than White Oak,
PA, anyway.
You might see me in the crowd, but if not, I'll see you all next week when
I put out my next house show report!
The Mysterious ACWA Fanboy.
