[It's 8pm on Wednesday night and the few fans who watch SSN's (Strickland Sports Network) World Championship Curling don't have a chance to change the channel before a pure black screen full of nothing appears upon their collective televisions.
First, the distant, quiet din of a large crowd is heard, a sustained cheer piped in against the black. Then, a baritone voice, recognized as ACWA ring announcer, David Sturbridge, is heard.]
DS (David Sturbridge): Jay Ralston's R-Cup. The site for two years running of cataclysmic confrontations. This year, it was something special for the
ACWA. [Cut to a still shot of The Canadian Kid walking the aisle to the ring before the largest crowd he's ever performed for. His TV title slung over one shoulder, he seems ready for action.]
DS: It was a match like no other. First, the challenger. The ACWA Television Champion. A man who has bludgeoned his way to the top as of late. The Canadian Kid!
[The din of the crowd changes, becoming a sustained heel heat. The still image changes, becoming an image of the Rose. Surrounded by reddish smoke, he stands with a microphone.]
DS: The champion, the Rose. Missing for weeks and months, nobody thought the champion would show.
[The crowd noise changes again, becoming a loud, heavy face heat.] DS: He did. Coming to the ring with an apparent back injury, he seemed driven by will alone. Hobbling, barely able to walk, he struggled up the aisle to meet his challenger.
But that's not all. [The crowd noise raises to an all new high as the image upon the screen changes again. This time to a shot of Steve Starr on the mic.]
DS: "Showstoppa" Steve Starr demanded that the Rose let him into the match. [The shot changes again, TCK and the Rose bickering.] DS: In spite of the Kid's protestations, Starr was accepted into the matchup. [Cut to a shot of the action.] SL: This whole thing is just a bigass cluster*BLEEP*! Nobody can hold any sort of advantage!

PJ: The crowd is going wild, and so are the wrestlers! SL: Oh no...TCK, leave that table alone! [No, Scott, he won't. In true TCK fashion, he's sprawled the horribly stunned Starr over the table, and he's gone back into the ring.]
