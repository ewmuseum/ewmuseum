------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------ ACWA UPDATE, December 17, 1999. ------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------ TABLE OF CONTENTS. ------------------------------------------------------------ 1) Opening spiel. 2) Deadlines. 3) Website. 4) Matchwriters. 5) Results of last Revolution. 6) Injuries, fines, suspensions, etc. 7) IRC Channel. 8) Upcoming shows. 9) Roster. 10) Other news. ------------------------------------------------------------ 1) OPENING SPIEL. ------------------------------------------------------------ Y2K hit, and hit big. Then we had another Revolution, and the impact wasn't quite as hard.
Okay, um, yeah... Seriously, we're doing good, I think. My writing staff's not optimal at the moment, unfortunately, so if you can write a match at any point in time, feel free to volunteer. Help is always needed, be it one time or as a regular matchwriter.
------------------------------------------------------------ 2) DEADLINES. ------------------------------------------------------------ Deadline for flashes and strats for the February 2 Revolution is 8pm, Tuesday, February 1. Let me explain why. The normal deadline is always on Sunday, but with the card as late as it is, I had to delay some stuff. Now, Revolution will almost assuredly not come out on Wednesday, but I'm hoping that we can get it out on Thursday or
Friday, shaving a day or so off of our schedule. It was this, or delay the card a whole week, which I don't want to do. Regardless, you've got the normal amount of time to do flashes and strats, so no excuses. Remember to send everything to this address.
I'll be sending out match assignments tonight, hopefully, sans booking and strats. If you want your strat used to it's fullest, I'd recommend sending it in quickly, waiting 'till the deadline only for flashes. I'll edit the endings onto the matches after I get all the material, so booking will be delayed even if the actual matchwriting isn't.
Anyway, moving on. ------------------------------------------------------------ 3) WEBSITE. ------------------------------------------------------------ ACWA Online is being updated every few days now by our loyal Webmaster, [name withheld]. Go there to read all kindsa stuff that you wouldn't know about otherwise.
Just so you know, the URL's: http://welcome.to/ACWA ------------------------------------------------------------ 4) MATCHWRITERS. ------------------------------------------------------------ If you didn't know, an efed such as ACWA is always in need of matchwriters. If you can write, please volunteer. (Restated from the opening spiel, I know, but it does bear repeating.) ------------------------------------------------------------ 5) RESULTS OF LAST REVOLUTION (plus supercard) ------------------------------------------------------------ I've put in some spoiler space so those of you who haven't read the card can do so. The winners and losers are listed below.
ACWA Y2K Preston Mayfield d Hiroshima via pinfall. Tha Flizoyd won a 6 man, 4 corners threat-style matchup via pinfall. The We'll Sees d Social Distortion via pinfall. The Rose d "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart via decision. Precious Moments d Team Tradition via pinfall. The Towers of Havoc vs Fire and Ice resulted in a draw. Precious Moments d the We'll Sees via pinfall. Steve Starr d Chad Shakespeare, The Canadian Kid, and Joe Gonzo. ACWA Revolution, January 26, 2000. "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines d Tha Flizoyd via pinfall. (Flizoyd has since left the ACWA) "Tomorrow Man" Dillon De Stride d Masahiro Takashi via pinfall. Preston Mayfield d Damien Omega via pinfall. "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart d "Psycho" Jason Sykora via countout. Fire and Ice d the We'll Sees via pinfall. As always, these are meant as a recap, not as a substitute for the actual results. Please read all ACWA cards as they come out.
------------------------------------------------------------ 6) Injuries, fines, suspensions, etc. ------------------------------------------------------------ Suspensions. ------------ "Showstoppa" Steve Starr was suspended from the ACWA for 3 weeks after striking veteran announcer Paul Jackson. The suspension was basically pointless, as two of those weeks were during a hiatus for the ACWA as SSN's
"Racin' Station" took it's timeslot. Fines. ------------ "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart has reportedly been fined heavily for his various attacks on various people since joining the ACWA. The fines have just come recently after Lockheart upped the ante, attacking David
Sturbridge and Wil Adams, but they have been backlogged to include every assault that the "Black Flagg" has made on officials and wrestlers outside of a wrestling match. The amount is undisclosed, but Vince is said to have lost a good chunk of his yearly pay.
Injuries ------------ Numerous injuries were accounted for at Y2K. All four participants in the Heavyweight title match went home with minor injuries, but won't miss any time. Given the long break after the supercard, no missed time is expected for anyone hurt at Y2K.
Paul Jackson had two molars in the back of his right, lower row of teeth loosened when Steve Starr punched him. The teeth tightened back up, and other than a swollen lip, Jackson's pride was the only thing injured.
Wil Adams and David Sturbridge went home sore after Revolution, but neither was injured. Sturbridge, in fact, was able to introduce the final matchup and name the winner, but was still checked out by a physician after the show.
Masahiro Takashi required stitches after repeated shots from Dillon De Stride's "Jewell Knucks". He will miss no time. ------------------------------------------------------------ 7) IRC CHANNEL ------------------------------------------------------------ #ACWA is still there on the starchat server, waiting for you to come into it. Not enough of you use it. C'MON, ALREADY! ------------------------------------------------------------ 8) UPCOMING SHOWS ------------------------------------------------------------ Want to know if you're scheduled for an ewrestling match in the ACWA? You'd better read below then. :)
ALL CARDS SUBJECT TO CHANGE! Main event, for the ACWA TV Title, The Rose (c) vs "Black Flagg Vincent Lockheart. After Lockheart's brutal attack on both the Rose and his "son" Stefan, Rose himself challenged for this matchup. It should be a brutal one as Rose attempts, again, to fight through pain and adversity to triumph against the monster.
One question to this match is "Will Stefan be at ringside again this week?". Double tornado rules, 8 man tag match, Tower of Havoc ("Ruthless" Ron Lane and "Bad" Bobby Reed with Howie Harding) and the We'll Sees (Psicko and Kenny Vann) vs Precious Moments (Binky and Winky Fairy) and Fire and Ice ("Red Hot" Alex Perkins and "Iceman" Michael Ice). This past Revolution's Main Event was less about the competitors involved and more about the interferers who made themselves known. The Towers of
Havoc made an assault on both teams, and then brawled with Precious Moments when the tag champs came down to help Fire and Ice and the We'll Sees out.
A mixup caused Vann to get squished by both members of Precious Moments and one of the Towers, and Psicko attacked Binky for it!
The end result is this. 8 men, one ring, two teams in the ring at one time. When one man on one team tags out, both members of his team exit the ring, and both members of the tagged team enter. There will be two referees there to keep order, and the whole thing will be contested under theat rules!
Can the Towers co-exist with the We'll Sees? Can Fire and Ice ignore the fact that, on the 9th, they have a tag title match with their partners!?
It's sure to be pure chaos on this week's REVOLUTION! Singles action, Joe Gonzo vs The Canadian Kid. A long standing rivalry here. While they haven't actually actively feuded, both The Canadian Kid and Joe Gonzo have had quite a time clawing their way up the rankings. TCK was the man to break Gonzo's winning streak before the
Badass from Buffalo blew town, and the man to eliminate him from the ACWA Heavyweight Title match. TCK was eliminated himself at the same moment, falling victim to Gonzo's Mind Eraser. As such, nothing is settled between these two. These two, the top ranked superstars in the ACWA, will butt heads on Revolution. The winner having proven something, and, most likely, having won the right to face Steve Starr for the ACWA Heavyweight Title again.
Singles action, Partner vs Partner, Samuel "Mule" Lowenstein vs "Bulldog" McMullen. The newcomer team that started with a bang and ended with a whimper. Miscommunication, accusations of laziness, and every other problem that you could imagine a tagteam having happened to these two. Now, Team Tradition is no more, and Lowenstein and McMullen want a piece of one another. If nothing else, we'll find out who really carried the team throughout their short career.
