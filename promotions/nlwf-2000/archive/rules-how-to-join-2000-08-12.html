[NLWF] Rules/How to join

12 August 2000

Here are the rules you must follow at all times while competing in the NLWF. If you violate these rules you could be suspended from or kicked out of the federation. The Rules To Join, read all of these rules first, and then click here Do not use other wrestlers in your roleplays unless you have their permission! You have to roleplay AT LEAST TWICE a week You can roleplay being in the locker room, in the ring, from your house, in an alley, or any where else you want to. Be creative. You can roleplay interviews if you would like to. Do not get mad if you are not chosen to win. Just try role playing better. If you want a title shot: (1)You have to be ranked in the top 5 (2)You have to e-mail me and ask. (3)Your opponent has to agree to the match also! Rankings/Point System -A win in a title match............... = 3Pts. -Hold a title for a week............. = 3Pts. -A win in a non-title match........ = 2Pts. -A Draw or a Disqualification.. = 1Pt. -Roleplayer of the week........... = 1Pt. Rules For The Belts: -World Heavyweight Title- everyone can compete for this title. -Luchadore Title- Only wrestlers weighing in at 245 lbs. or less can compete for this title. -Tag Team Titles- Tag teams compete for this title. Tag teams include the teams on the tag team roster or two members on the singles rosters. If you want to join, send an e-mail with the information below to [detail withheld] . You can control 3 westlers. Managers don't count as wrestlers.
