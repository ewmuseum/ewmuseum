Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World Internet Wrestling ran as a site-based board federation; its own president reported in May 2000 that it had been open for almost eleven months. Two shows ran every week, on Monday and Thursday, and a January 2000 recruitment post sought a writer for a planned third, Saturday card modeled on WCW's own Saturday Night, for rookies and lower-skilled wrestlers to get work.

Two full cards survive from November 2000. Tuesday Night Turmoil, taped for 21 November, ran a Fight Club-rules opener between Jimmy Fixer and Deadbolt, a Boiler Room Brawl, an Armageddon Match settled by chokeslamming an opponent through a casket, and a main event Lumberjacks match pitting Pretty Boy Floyd against Tito Demo for “Title for a Title.”

Shockwave, taped for 26 November and billed by “WIW's own prophet” Phreak as the moment “nothing will be the same,” carried a HardKore Title brawl, an Internet Title match that was “Drastic” O. Skiller's fourth Punishment Match, a Women's Title mud-wrestling brawl between Cammy and Lady Emma, a Tag Team title bout between Darkhart, Inc. and Dark Justice, a Stables Championship elimination match, a battle royal for the next Internet Title shot, and a main event “I Quit” match between HellFyre the Demon Spawn and Mr. Smith for outright control of the promotion.

No document survives after that card, and how or when WIW closed is not known.
