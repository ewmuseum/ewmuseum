Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RWWF, the Real World Wrestling Federation, was a real-wrestler-portrayal federation of the newsgroup rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy, built on storylines and angles rather than a simulation engine. Its lone surviving document, posted 9 August 1997, opens by describing itself as already eight months old: “we've been running for 8 months.” It ran two shows a week, aimed at two pay-per-views a month, and circulated a semi-daily mailer of interviews and challenges called “It's All Talk” alongside a weekly “Insiderz Report” of “rumours, news and articles.”

It asked a prospective member for a wishlist of thirty real singles wrestlers and fifteen tag teams, in exchange for two singles wrestlers and one tag team to actually use — “less if you want.” Five singles titles and one tag title were on offer, and the ad closed its pitch aimed squarely at one kind of joiner: “EXCELLENT ROLEPLAYERS ONLY!”

RWWF's own record does not say how it ended. The date comes from elsewhere: the same operator, founding a later federation roughly two months on, wrote that this one “survived for 11 months” — placing its close around October 1997, three months after the one ad that is all that survives of it.
