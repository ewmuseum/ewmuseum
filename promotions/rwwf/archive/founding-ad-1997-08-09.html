Real World Wrestling Federation =============================== If you would like to join a great E-fed,than make it RWWF.We have 2 shows a week (Saturday and Wednesday) and we try to have 2 PPV's per month!We use real wrestlers!This e-fed isn't simmed.RWWF is based on storylines and angles,so it will get exciting when you get in a great angle!You also write interviews and challenges in this league.We have a semi-daily mail called "It's All Talk" which contains all the interviews and challenges written.We also have a weekly email called the Insiderz Report,which contains rumours,news and articles on RWWF.If you would like to join than email with 30 real single wrestlers and 15 tag teams and I will give you 2 singles and 1 tag team to use.(less if you want).We are growing everyday so sign up quick!We also have 5 single titles and 1 tag team belt.If you would like to write matches for RWWF than email me too!You aren't a flash in the pan league,we've been running for 8 months.If you want to join email me at [detail withheld] EXCELLENT ROLEPLAYERS ONLY! Thanks for your time! JR
