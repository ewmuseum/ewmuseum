Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The World Wrestling Alliance will begin as soon as I get 8 men to start a singles tournament. I will have a 2 single belts, (1)The WWA World Heavyweight Title and (2)The WWA Television, title. There will also be one tag team title, another one might be added later. I will be using WLS 5.3! Men and Women can wrestle in these league and against each other. I will have many speciality matches which I will document in your "Welcome to the WWA" reply you will get back when you send me your wrestlers.
