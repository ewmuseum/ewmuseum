XCW Trust No One (part 3)

July 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall, and is an
Four Way, IRON MAN Match for the XCW United States Title. First coming down to the ring...from Short Hills, New Jersey. Here is Raven!!!
["Keep em' seperated" starts to play and Raven comes out to a mixed reaction. He climbs into the ring and slouches against the turnbuckle.] FINK: And his opponent, from Philladelphia, PA. He weighs in at 260 pounds, here is The Sandman!!!!
["Enter the Sandman" starts to play and he comes out through the curtain for the first time ever. He's got nothing in his hands, and he rolls into the ring to a HUGE ovation.]
FINK: And their opponent...from Battlecreek, Michigan. Weighing in at 245 pounds here is Rob Van Dam!!!!
["Walk" by Pantera starts to play and RVD comes down to a great face ovation.] FINK: And their opponent...he is the current XCW United States Champion, here is "Mr. Perfect" Curt Hennig!!!
["Exodus" starts to play, and every boo's right away. Perfect comes out with the title around his waist, and climbs into the ring...]
[All four men begin to form somewhat of a circle type shape in the middle of the ring. Sandman and Raven begin to exchange words with each other, while RVD and Perfect do the same.]
Joey: Only one can wonder what they're saying to each other. You can be sure they're not asking for baking tips.
Jesse: Baking tips?? Joey: 30 Seconds has passed, and Raven grabs Sandman and tosses him into the corner. Raven fires with rights to the face, and now RVD and Perfect are going at it with lefts and rights. Fans bare with us, this is going to be a tough match to follow but we'll do our best.
[Raven hammers away on Sandman in the corner, while Perfect swings RVD into the ropes. RVD returns and leapfrogs over Perfects backdrop attempt...he turns around quickly and goes for a spinning leg lariat. Perfect ducks, and the kick hits Raven in the back of the head.]
Joey: Raven stumbled forward and walks into a clothesline by The Sandman. Raven falls back against the ropes, and gets himself tied up in them.
Jesse: Well that might be a good thing for Raven...less punishment. Joey: Yeah, but he won't get any pinfalls. [Perfect takes RVD down with a high knee, and now he stomps away on him. Sandman comes over to join the action. Earl Hebner is doing his best to get
Raven out of the ropes, but it's not working.]
Joey: 3 minutes has passed, and still no pinfalls. I'm sure we'll see some very shortly. [Sandman and Perfect stomp away on RVD, and the Sandman forearms Perfect on the side of the head. Another forearm, and another one and that takes Hennig into the corner. Sandman with a few kicks to the stomach, and then with an Irish
Whip. Sandman charges in, but Perfect lifts himself up in a kick position via help from the ropes. Sandman runs right into his boot face first, and staggers out backwards. RVD with a roll up!!!!]
Joey: ONE!!!! TWO!!!! NO!!!! Perfect breaks it up with a double ax-handle to the back on RVD. Now Perfect with a pinfall attempt....ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! RVD breaks it up with a legdrop across the back of Perfect's neck.
Jesse: Now RVD covers Sandman... Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Raven has broken himself free, and he breaks the count with a kneedrop fo the back of RVD. Raven now brings Sandman to his feet and whips him into the ropes...huge powerslam!!!!
Jesse: That was nice. Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!! WOW, RVD and Perfect work together and pull Raven off of him. Double Snap Suplex on Raven, and I can't believe they're working as a team. What a move, and Raven goes down hard to the canvas....fifty four minutes, and what a match we've seen so far.
Jesse: How are you going to do the play by play for this? I might not like you, but if you pull this off...I have a whole new respect.
Joey: Thanks. [Sandman whips Perfect to the ropes, and he swings with a clothesline. Perfect counters with a Crucifix pinning combo....]
Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! Raven is up and he grabs Perfect and tosses him down to the mat by the hair. Sandman is up, and Raven kicks him in the stomach. He lifts him up for a Crucifix Powerbomb...but RVD grabs Sandman and pulls him down to the other side.
Jesse: RVD and Sandman double team Raven. Double clothesline sends Raven into the corner. Joey: Thanks for the help. Raven rests in the corner, and RVD grabs Sandman and swings him into the other corner. RVD charges in with a handspring back elbow and hits it. OH MAN!!!!
[As RVD walks out of the corner Perfect kicks him in the stomach and hits the "Perfect-Plex"] Joey: HE HITS IT!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! At fifty one minutes in, Perfect has secured the very first pinfall of the match. Perfect goes for another pinfall attempt, but Sandman breaks it up at two.
Jesse: So Perfect 1, and everyone else 0. [Sandman sends Perfect into the ropes, but Perfect reverses it. Sandman returns and Perfect with a drop toe-hold right into Raven who turns the falling Sandman into the mat with a DDT!!!!]
Joey: What a double team type move, and I don't think that was planned. Raven with a cover...
Jesse: He could tie it. Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!! Kick by Perfect breaks the count. Curt wasn't pleased that Raven interfeared with his plan. Perfect now stands over Raven and tells him what he thinks of him, and from behind...RVD with a dropkick that sends
Perfect into the corner chest first!!!!
[RVD follows him in and hits the turnbuckle smash several times before turning him around and unloading with a chop!]
Joey: Another chop, and another on!!! Jesse: This place with their "WHOOOO's" right on target. Joey: The chest of Curt Hennig is bright crimson red, and now RVD unloads with another while Raven is busy sending Sandman into the ropes. Bulldog Lariat!!!
Jesse: Sandman goes down hard. Joey: RVD sends Perfect out of the corner...Raven is there, and he hits a backdrop on the US Champion.
Jesse: Rob doesn't look happy..and he charges Raven. He sommerrsaults through a clothesline, Raven turns around and connects with a lariat. Raven brings RVD up, and hits the Evenflow!!!!!
Joey: Raven with the cover....ONE!!! TWO!!! NO Sandman comes over and tries to break it up...Raven moves though, and he elbowdrops RVD. Raven goes for the cover again...ONE!!! TWO!!!
[Perfect comes over and goes for a kneedrop, and once again Raven moves out of the way, and he hits RVD.]
Joey: Raven with another cover....ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! And with eleven minutes passed this far in the match, Perfect and Raven have each secured a pinfall.
Jesse: Raven 1, Perfect 1, and everyone else O!!!! Joey: Raven now up to his feet, and both Perfect and Sandman charge and bombard the man with forearms. They back him into the ropes, before whipping him into the other side. Raven returns and he goes for a double clothesline...Sandman ducks, and Perfect gets taken down.
[Raven turns around, and catches the running Sandman by the waist and hits a
Stun Gun.] Joey: And Sandman didn't go down...he's staggering around holding his throat. Raven with a boot to the midsection...EVENFLOW!!!!! Jesse: RVD charges, Raven side steps and grabs him by the head! He sends him flying over the top rope onto the floor.
Joey: Raven walks over and covers Sandman. ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!!! Raven has gotten another pinfall, and he so far leads the match with two falls to only one that was scored by the US Champion...Curt Hennig.
[Raven gets up and Perfect charges him this time, Raven backdrops him over the top rope and Hennig ends up landing on RVD who was just getting up.]
Joey: Raven turns around and Sandman is up. He delivers a left and a right, he sends Raven into the ropes. Raven with a reverse, and he takes Sandman down with a short knee to the stomach. Raven goes for the cover!!!!
Jesse: ONE!!! TWO!! THREE!!!! Joey: And Raven has scored three consecutive pinfalls. Unbelieveable, Raven is dominating this match. Say what you want about Raven, but this strategy has been very impressive so far.
[RVD and Perfect now fighting on the outside. Perfect sends RVD towards the ring steps, but RVD reverses it and Perfect goes flying into the ring steps shoulder first. Back in the ring and Raven is looking very confident.]
Joey: Listen to these people boo him. He brings Sandman up and holds him by one hand, and it looks like he's going to chokeslam him? Raven stops and looks at the crowd....Oh and Sandman with a low blow!!!!!!
Jesse: Right in the Raven's nest! Joey: Raven bends over holding himself, and now Sandman is recovering. Sandman goes for a spingboard bulldog, but Raven pushes him off...and Sandman goes flying into the cengter of the ring.
Jesse: And Sandman is taking a beating! [Raven now in the corner still holding himself. Outside of the ring RVD stops stomping on Perfect, and climbs up onto the apron. Raven is right next to him, and he kicks Raven through the ropes in the stomach. Raven comes out of the turnbuckle holding his stomach, and RVD hits a Slingshot Rocker Droper.]
Joey: What a move there by RVD, and he goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! But
Perfect is back up and he pulls VD back to the outside. Perfect with a right hand, and RVD blocks it.
Jesse: RVD with a punch of his own that connects, and RVD with another, and another. Rob points to himself, and then takes Perfect down with a spinning leg sweep.
Joey: NO!!! Perfect jumped it, and Hennig with a thumb to the eye. Hennig with a right hand, and OH!!!! Sandman takes both men out with a baseball slide.
Sandman turns back and see's Raven getting back up near the corner. Sandman walks over and grabs him by the legs...and Raven is holding onto the turnbuckle.
Jesse: He's not letting go. [Sandman pulls as hard as he can, and Raven hits face first onto the canvas.] Joey: And Sandman now goes to the outside...he grabs Raven's leg, and he's going to crotch him. He's going to pull his legs between that ring post. Here comes Perfect though....Sandman lets go, and he backdrops Perfect over the guardrail into the seam of fans!!! OH MY GOD!!!
Jesse: Twenty minutes into the match, and Raven leads it three pinfalls to one. We're one third of the way done here...you have to wonder who is running on fumes here. Raven even though he leads has to be tired...everyone is targeting him now.
Joey: RVD is positioning himself on the ring apron, on the other side of the ring. Raven is getting up inside now, and he turns...he spots RVD...too late!!!
Slingshot Missile Dropkick to the jaw!!! [On the outside, Sandman turns his attention and looks in awe as Perfect landed on his feet, and is now being yelled at by all the fans...telling him what a horrible idea it was to wear the pink singlet tonight.]
Joey: Sandman walks away, and he see's RVD going for the cover in the ring. Jesse: ONE!!! TWO!!! No!!! It's going to a lot more than a missile dropkick to keep Raven down tonight. Rob works him over with quick legdrops, and then a corkscrew legdrop.
Joey: RVD brings Raven back up, and he whips him into the ropes. Reverse by
Raven, and he kicks RVD in the stomach. Another Evenflow???
[RVD escapses by backing out. Raven charges him and RVD with a leapfrog, Raven turns around gets kicked in the stomach, and then gets hit with a HUGE leaping
DDT. Perfect has just now escaped the sea of fans, and now he and Sandman are staying away from each other...but watching the action in the ring at the same time.]
Joey: RVD goes for the cover...but Raven kicks out. It looks like Sandman and
Perfect are talking, and maybe they're forming a plan?
[RVD looks at Perfect and Sandman suspciously as he exits the ring before going to the top rope. Sandman is trying to yell something to RVD, and RVD just looks at him. This buys time for Raven, who is up now and he reaches over and grabs
RVD and throws him off the turnbuckle.]
Joey: WOW!!! RVD goes flying onto the canvas, and he's got to be in some serious pain. His back met the mat with such velocity he'll be calling a chiropractor tonight, not even waiting until tomorrow.
Jesse: I wonder what Sandman was trying to tell him? Joey :Raven goes for the cover, Sandman and PErfect make a last second attempt yo stop it...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Raven has secured his fourth consecutive pinfall and this one coming at 24 minutes into the match.
[Sandman and Hennig however go back to the outside, and they're started to actually plot something. Raven regains his barrings in the ring as RVD slides out onto the floor.]
Joey: Remember, the only way in this match to score a falll is to pin your opponent OR make them submit. You connot get DQ'd or counted out, and pinfalls don't just count anywhere. It has to be in the ring.
Jesse: Keep it up, you're doing a great job! Joey: 35 minutes and 27 seconds remain in the match. Raven leads the match with four pinfalls.
[Sandman and Primetime now - seeing that Raven is dominating - trying to form an alliance with Rob Van Dam who is nursing his back in the outside. RVD staggering around as Sandman and Perfect meet him on the outside. Raven hasn't seen this, as he's turned in the other diretion, somewhat out of breath, but confident that he's going to win the match tonight.]
Joey: All three men are talking about the plot on the outside, and it appears they're going to try to take Raven out as a team...rather than work against each other and try to catch up to Raven's dominating amount of pinfalls.
Jesse: Ooo and Raven turns around and spots all three men, Sandman looks underneath the ring...and he pulls out a chair, and he hands one to RVD, he hands one to Perfect, and he takes one himself.
Joey: Raven is daring all three men to come in and take him on, and you can tell this man fears nothing. Not even three men with chairs in their hands.
