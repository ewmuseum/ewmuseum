RSPWF Dying? Seeking Members

May 24, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It looks like the RSPWF has seen better days. I haven't been around for about 3 months, and since I've come back the posts are almost "0" and the stuff that is around isn't that great. I think it's because people got sick and tired of being in feds that shut down after two weeks, or dealing with egos who actually run the fed. That's why I became a fed head because I was tired of dealing with fed heads who knew everything and their fed closed down in a week.
Now I'm not saying I'm the best, I'm not saying that the XCW is everything you wanted in a fed. What I am saying is that the XCW is FUN...the XCW is probably the only fed out there right now where you can look forward to a card.
Yeah it's real wrestler, but if it's fun and the cards are good...then why would you complain?
I ran the XCW about 3 months ago, and almost ALL the talented people on the
RSPWF joined. It was a damn good fed...Brian J was and is in it now, Maz same deal...BigH0RT was.
So my offer to you is this...try the XCW. I'm not here to make money, I'm not here for glory. I'm here because I like to make this fun for other people...and
I'll work my ass of for you.
E-mail or IM me if you're interested. - Chad
