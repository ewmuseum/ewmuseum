XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore

June 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to Steve Armstong who is entering the building. All of a sudden Scott
Steiner and Triple H attack him with wooden bats. Both men are beating over his skull relentlessly, and Steve can't do anything about it. Then from out of no where comes Cactus Jack with a chair wrapped in barbed wire...and he swings wildly at both men. Triple H and Scott run away laughing, and Cactus turns around to Steve. Steve makes it to his feet and Cactus cracks the chair over his skull....]
Cactus: NOW we're even!!!! [Camera fades onto the Hardcore intro, and once it's over we cut to a live sold out crowd in Dallas, Texas...live from the Cotton Bowl. Jim Ross and Joel
Gertner are standing at ringside excited and ready for the night to begin.]
Jim: And we don't waste any time with the action here tonight. Cactus Jack coming to the rescue of Steve Armstrong, and then attacks him. Now they're even he says!
Joel: What an idiot...does he realize that he has a tag match and Steve is his partner? Jim: Well an eye for an eye. Fans we've gotten the 1st round of the qualifying matches done, and I'm suprised at some of the winners yesterday.
Joel: You gotta give Sho Funaki credit for winning his match...he fought a long hard battle and came out on top.
Jim: He only won because Raven got Tommy's attention! Joel: A win is a win is a win. Jim: Yeah and that's why Steve Armstrong beat Raven... Joel: That was a rip off right there. Jim: The tournament is shaping up to have some HUGE suprises in store, and we're going to add some matches to the card as well. Another man who won yesterday was Mikey Whipwreck, and tonight he's going up against two other men in a three way dance. Sho Funaki, and Tatanka....that should be a great battle.
Joel: Well last week Tatanka was no where to be found, and now he has to pay up. Jim: Lets not waste anymore time...FINK take it away!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. First coming down to the ring...hailing from parts unknown. Here is Vampiro!!!!
[Vampiro comes down to the ring with some errie kind of music, and he's got face paint on. He gets onto the apron and flips into the ring...the crowd gives him a decent pop.]
FINK: And his opponent...from Minnesota, weighing in at 275 pounds. Here is
"The Bad Guy" Scott Hall...
["Last Call" is heard all over the PA, and Hall staggers out to a mixed reaction. He is obviously a little wasted or hung over. He climbs into the ring, and he burps right in the face of the ref. The ref waves the smell away, and calls for the bell.]
Jim: And this should be an interesting match. Joel: Ya think? [Hall and Vampiro lock up and Hall takes the advantage with a knee to the stomach. Right hand to the side of the face by Hall, and another right hand. He sends Vampiro to the ropes, and Hall misses the clothesline.]
Jim: Vampiro ducks that clothesline, and takes Hall down with a spinning cresent kick!!! Joel: I like both of these guys, but if I have to go with one...well heh, I'd have to with Scott Hall. He can out drink me, and I respect that totally.
Jim: Is he drunk again tonight? Joel: I'm not saying anything. [Hall gets up, and Vampiro takes him down again with an armdrag. Hall up quickly and Vampiro takes him down with another quick armdrag, and the crowd claps with apporval.]
Jim: Look at those deep armdrags by Vampiro, and he waits for Hall to get up again. Scott staggers back up, and Vampiro hits a spingboard flying forearm.
Joel: He's got some great moves. Jim: Yes he does. Vampiro is a talented young wrestlers, and we're glad to have him in the XCW.
[Vampiro gets a running start and hits a sommersault legdrop, and the fans in the crowd "Ooooo" at the same time over that move. Vampiro goes for the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO! Hall kicks out at the last second, and that move was incredible...wow.
Joel: When you've had a few drinks that's a hard move to take! Jim: Ahhh...so he's drunk again? Joel: Did I say he was? NO....I made a statement. [Vampiro up first, and when Hall gets up Vampiro runs and hits a Hurrcanrana.] Jim: That was the best hurracanrana I've seen in ages. Joel: Vampiro has had total control of Hall, and it's because of Hall's friend
Jack Daniels.
Jim: HE IS DRUNK!!! Joel: No you idiot...Jack Daniels is a friend of Halls. Just because a guys name reminds you a drink you down before you go home to your wife and kids, doesn't mean that Hall beens drinking it.
Jim: I have a great wife, and two great kids. Joel: I heard you had a third but you ate her. Jim: You should talk. Joel: No that's your other daughter that I ate. Jim:.................. [Both men are silent for a few minutes, and Vampiro is up top and waits for
Hall to make it up. Scott Hall makes it up, and Vampiro hits him with a
Springboard Moonsault Bodyblock...he hooks the leg.] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! Hall kicks out. [Vampiro brings Hall to his feet and kicks him in the stomach. Vampiro kicks
Hall in the face, and then hits an axe kick to take him down to the canvas.
Vampiro hits an Asai Moonsault, and goes for the pin again...] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!!! NOT YET!!! Joel: And Hall isn't about to let some rookie beat him in the ring. Jim: Vampiro has been around forever. Joel: But he's only been in the XCW for 5 minutes. [Vampiro brings Hall to his feet and drives a sidekick into the ribs, and Hall catches it and spins Vampiro around. Vamp comes back and Hall clotheslines him down to the canvas.]
Jim: That almost took his head off. Joel: Yes it did. Jim: Hall going for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!! Kickout by Vampiro... [Scott Hall brings him to his feet and hits a fallaway slam. Instead of pinning him though Scott celebrates by working the crowd. Behind him, Vamp does a
kip-up and waits for Hall to get up.] Joel: Turn around...turn around!!! Jim: Vamp is waiting for him...and this could be it. [Hall slowly turns around, and Vampiro hits a flying scissor kick. Hall goes down hard, and Vampiro rolls him up.]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!! Joel: He didn't get him!! His shoulder was up!!! Jim: You're right....his shoulder was up, but Tim White didn't see it. FINK: Here is your winner...Vampiro!!! Jim: Scott Hall is fighting with the ref, and Tim White isn't going to take any of his crap. Time is pushing up right back, and wait a minute...Ric Flair is down here.
Joel: Now Tim White is in some serious trouble. Jim: Flair and Hall are in the face of Tim White, and this isn't fair. Two wrestlers backing a ref into a corner?
Joel: Well that's what he gets for not paying attention. [The crowd pops big time as we see Arn Anderson run into the ring. He slides in and gets in between White and the other two.]
Jim: Arn Anderson!!! Arn Anderson has slid into the ring, and he's telling them to go to the back.
Joel: Look at Flair...he's not a happy man. He's turning red just looking at
Arn Anderson. Jim: Well Arn has locked Flair into the trunk of a car two times in two weeks, and I think that would make anyone mad.
Joel: Hell yeah! Jim: Hall and Flair turn around, and Arn is follo - OH!!! Flair turned around and decked Arn!!
Joel: WOOOO!!!! Jim: Flair and Hall are beating the hell out of Arn Anderson. They're beating up our commissoner.
Joel: HAHA how do you like that Arn!! Pay me less than Ross...take that!!!! [Flair throws off his suit coat and starts to kick away on the knee of Arn. Ric then locks in the figure-four, and Hall is just laying in the punches.]
Jim: Oh come on!!!! Joel: Pay back is a bitch! Jim: Here comes the officials....and they're trying to break it up. Ric Flair isn't stopping, and Hall has just busted Arn Anderson open!
Joel: Beautiful. [Finally Ric lets go and gives Hall a big high-five. Flair gives out a WHOOOO, and both men walk to the back. Arn is being helped up by the officials and we break for a commercial.]
