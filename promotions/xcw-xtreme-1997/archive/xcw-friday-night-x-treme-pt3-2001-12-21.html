XCW Friday Night X-Treme (part 3)

December 21, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: This next match is going to be amazing. We've got Shawn Michaels taking on Lance Storm. We're going to see every move you can imagine in this bout!!
This is goi...hey Bret Hart is sitting in the crowd. What's he doing here? Jerry: WHAT!? Bret Hart is here? Joey: Well he must be here to support his good friend Lance Storm. Lets take you to the match.
STACY: The following contest is a Falls Count Anywhere match. First coming down to the ring from Calgary, Alberta, Canada....weighing in at 229 pounds. Here is
Lance Storm!!!
(Lance Storms walks down to the ring with a blank look on his face. He yells at a kid as he walks by and then slides into the ring.)
STACY: And his opponent....from San Antonio, Texas. He weighs in at 245 pounds, here is "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels!!!
("Oh, Oh, Shawn...." is heard throughout the arena, and the fans are giving him a huge pop. HBK comes out from the curtain and the fans go wild, and Shawn eats up every single minute of it. He climbs into the ring and poses for the crowd...)
Joey: Shawn Michaels looks to be in the best shape of his life. Both men meet at the center of the ring, and this match is going to be huge. There's the bell, and we've got them jawing back and forth.
Jerry: What do you think they're saying to each other? Joey: Something we can't put on national TV. Oh and Storm slaps the taste right out of Shawn's mouth, and HBK just smiles. He's telling him to do it again, and Storm does it again!!!
Jerry: This kid has some Maple Leaves!!! Joey: Shawn tells him to do it one more time...this time Lance goes for a right hand, and HBK with a block and right of his own. Another one, and another one knocks Storm down to the canvas.
Jerry: Storm doesn't know what hit him.... Joey: HBK whips Lance into the ropes, Lance ducks the clothesline...leapfrog by
Shawn Michaels. Storm puts on the breaks and spins Michaels' around. Oh and he forearms Shawn, and another one to the side of the head. Lance with a go-behind and he executes a back suplex.
Jerry: You think Bret Hart is going to do something here tonight? Joey: Bret has made it clear that he's retired. There's no way that's he's going to do something here tonight. Lance brings Shawn to his feet and he pulls of a spinning head scissors!!
Jerry: I don't think HBK is used to wrestling someone who has as much talent as he does. Joery: Talent yes, but HBK wins most of his matches with the chrisma that he has. Lance Storm with a perfect dropkick, and Shawn Michaels doesn't seem to know how to handle this young Canadian.
Jerry: Have you seen Shawn Michaels' kid? Joey: No I have not. Jerry: I heard that he's always tired, just like his old man. Joey: What? Why? Jerry: Because he doesn't lay down for anyone. Joey: Shawn gets up and Lance Storm with a cartwheel kick, but HBK ducks. Lance
Storms lands onto the mat hard, and Shawn waits for Storm to make it to his feet. Shawn runs to the ropes and takes Storm down with a swinging neckbreaker.
Jerry: You know if Lance Storm wins here tonight he can be just another addition to the list of famous Canadians?
Joey: Umm okay. Jerry: Michael J. Fox, Pam Anderson, Jim Carey...Tom Green. Joey: Tom Green is not famous... Jerry: Yes he is...The Bum Bum song. You know that one right? Joey: No can't say that I do know it. OH AND SHAWN WITH A JUMPING CLOTHESLINE!!!! Jerry: Storm almost lost his head. Joey: Shawn gets up and brings Lance with him....bodyslam by Shawn, and he drops a knee onto the face of Storm.
Jerry: Ya know this is a falls count anywhere...but they're still in the ring. Joey: Well these are two guys who don't rely on weapons, or tables or chairs. These two will go to it on the mat, and they'll do it until one of them tire. Whoa, quick armdrag takedown by Lance Storm. Both men make it back up and Storm with a side headlock.
Jerry: Where did this offense come from? Joey: Storm jumps to his feet INTO A DROPKICK!!!! Shawn needs to regroup....HBK makes it back up and Storm with a jumping sidekick, and that catches Shawn right on the jaw...
Jerry: LOOK OUT!!! Joey: OH and Lance Storm tosses Shawn over the top rope, and HBK lands hard right next to us. Storm with a plancha now, and that is it. I can't believe the offense that Storm is dishing out to Shawn.
Jerry: I can. Joey: Storm throws Shawn into the guardrail, and Shawn reverses it. Storm hits hard, and Shawn gets a running start and just nails him with a suicude body attack of some kind.
Jerry: Good way to use those technical terms. Joey: Shawn goes for the Superkick and Lance Storm ducks...OH MY GOD!!! He hits
Bret Hart!!!! He Superkicked Bret Hart on accident. And Storm with a clothesline, and HBK goes over the guardrail....
Jerry: Did you see the impact of that kick on Bret's face? Joey: Man!!! Storm now hops over the guardrail and he's punching away on the face of Shawn Michaels. Storm lets up on Shawn Michaels, and Bret pushes Lance
Storm aside and now he's stomping away on HBK!!!!
Jerry: BRET!? Joey: Bret Hart is a madman!!! He's trying to get some revenge for HBK and his son being friends.
Jerry: You know how much of an insult it is for Brets own son to like HBK more? Joey: Bret throws Shawn over the guardrail, and he goes out after him. Lance
Storm is smiling and watching Bret Hart attack Shawn Michaels...Lance Storm jumps over the guardrail and now they're both beating up on HBK!! This isn't fair....
Jerry: Of course it's fair...Bret Hart has been insulted. His family has been disgraced. Joey: Bret is telling Lance Storm to do something....Storm throws Shawn into the ring and drags him over to the turnbuckle. Oh no...Bret grabs Shanw's legs..FIGURE FOUR AGAINST THE RING POST!!!! This will knock a knee out of place, and Shawn is screaming in pain.
Jerry: This is all legal!!! Joey: Bret Hart had to have knocked a knee out of place doing this move. You can't walk away from this move without injury, and Shawn Michaels looks like he's in a whole lot of pain.
Jerry: HAHA and Lance Storm is stomping away on the face of Shawn Michaels. Joey: Lance Storm is stomping on the face and chest, and Bret Hart has the figure four on him. This doesn't look good for The Heartbreak Kid! Bret lets go finally, and now Lance drags him into the center of the ring. HALF BOSTON
CRAB.... Jerry: It's over!! Joey: Shawn isn't even moving...the ref is calling for the bell, and Lance
Storm has won this match tonight.
STACY: Here is your winner...Lance Storm!!!! Joey: Lance Storm and Bret Hart are parading around the ring now like they've won the Superbowl. This is horrible. Shawn can't even move, and Bret and Lance are celebrating.
Jerry: WHAT A TEAM!!! Joey: Ugh....we've got the XCW medical staff taking Shawn Michaels back to the dressing room.
(Camera cuts to Taz walking down the hall, and Vader bumps into him on purpose. Taz and Vader have a huge staredown...) Taz: What the fuck is your problem? Vader: ................... Taz: I SAID what the fuck is your problem fat man? Step out of the way or get stepped on. Vader: You know not who you speak to. I fear no man, I fear nothing...you see fear in my eyes?
Taz: Do you see fear in these eyes? (Taz steps up to Vader, and Vader starts to breathe heavier.) Taz: Make a move.... (Vader turns around to walk away, and Taz goes from behind and hits him with the Tazmission. All of a sudden security shows up and tries to break the hold, and they're not able to. We even get some jobbers to come out and try to come out and stop this, but nothing is able to. Vader is still moving though, but
Taz keeps the chokehold on. Finally he lets go and Vader doesn't move...Taz gets up and walks to the ring.)
