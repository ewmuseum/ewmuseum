XCW Monday Night Malice, 29 July 2003

29 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we comeback from the commercial we see Paul Heyman walk into Ric Flair's dressing room. Flair stands right up, and both men are face to face.] Paul: How's it going Ric? Ric: What do you want? Paul: I want to make you an offer... Ric: What? Paul: My man Goldberg wants to add a little to the match tonight. If he beats you, he wants your spot at Wrestlecade. Ric: So what if I beat him? Paul: Then you have the right to pick his opponent at Wrestlecade. Ric: Really? Paul: Yes...really. Ric: Fine...we'll do that. Now get out of here before you become apart of this room. Paul: Don't you realize Ric Flair...don't you see it?? I mean come on, do you really think that you stand a chance against Goldberg? Do you really think in your heart that you're going to be able to go out there and beat this monster of a man? Ric: I've never backed down from anything, and I'm not about to back down now. Paul: Well when that bell rings tonight, when all the eyes are on you...just remember that I told you so. [Paul leaves, and Flair isn't bothered in the slightest. We cut back to the ring and Taz is standing in the ring holding the XCW TV Title in his hands. The man looks somber, he looks sad...not the normal angry Taz we're used to seeing.] Taz: Ya know...for the past few weeks I've been thinkin'. Somethin' has been on my mind, just diggin' into my skull. I've tried to go on, I've tried to keep wrestlin', fightin', doin' whatever it takes to be the best XCW TV Champ ever...I think I did a pretty damn good job. [Crowd pops.] Taz: But there's somethin' more important than this belt, and that's my family. I have a family back in Red Hook that never gets to see their fatha....I have a little girl at home who I only get to talk to on the phone maybe 5 minutes a night. Yeah...the big bad Human Suplex Machine is gettin' all sentemental...shut up and listen. [Crowd quiets down.] Taz: Ta-night I had a match with The Undertakah. The dead man, but it ain't gonna happen. As a matter of fact he can have this belt, because I need to be a real man. I need to go home and be a fatha, a person that my kids can look up to. I don't want my kids watchin' someone like Kobe Bryant and wantin' to be like them. I want them to grow up with morals, and values. So it breaks my heart to say this...but I quit. [Taz gentley sets down the XCW TV Title waves to the crowd and is about to leave when all of a sudden Randy Orton's music hits. Randy comes out to a huge heel response, but he doesn't seem to care. He climbs into the ring, asks for the mic...and Taz gives it to him.] Randy: HAHAHA what a damn loser you are. You go on and on about how you want to be a role model for your kids? First of all your kids can't look up to you because they're taller than you are. Second...they want someone that wins matches. You Taz are nothing but a glorified Taxi Cab Driver that got lucky one day... [Taz steps up to Randy.] Randy: Boy you don't want this at all. I will knock you into next week...before you "retire" why don't we tell the world what you really are? [Taz doesn't move.] Randy: Oh yeah you're a bad ass here in the XCW, but before this. Before you're big bad wrestling career you were a Taxi Driver weren't you? You were a nothing...and if it wasn't for Paul Heyman, you'd still be asking people..."WHERE TO?". [Taz takes a step back and puts his head down.] Randy: Yeah I bet your kids didn't know that did they? [Orton looks right in the camera.] Randy: Kids...daddy used to drive Taxi Cabs for a living. He used to make $4.50 an hour, and that's your role model. So learn how to drive really well, because that's what you're furture looks like. So kiddies...I'll be taking this title, because it's mine anyway. [Taz takes a step in front of the XCW TV Title and the crowd goes wild.] Randy: Oh come on...now you want it back? Taz: No...I don't want a mutha (bleep) like you to have it. [Crowd pops.] Randy: Fine.. [Randy turns around to walk away, and then quickly turns back around and levels Taz with the microphone. Taz falls to the floor, and Randy begins to kick him like a dog. Randy is laughing the whole time, and Taz gets kicked onto the floor. Randy smiles to the crowd, and then walks over to the TV Title...he bends over to pick it up and... BONG!!! The lights go out... BONG!!! Camera flashes go off like mad... BONG!!! When the lights comeback on The Undertaker is standing in front of Randy Orton. Orton was still bent over to pick the title up, but once the lights went back on...he slowly looked from Taker's toes to his head. Once he got all the way to his head, The Undertaker grabs him by the throat and chokeslams him down onto the canvas. The announcers pick it up from there.] Jim: HOLY HELL THE UNDERTAKER JUST CHOKESLAMMED RANDY ORTON!! GOOD!!! GOOD!!! THAT'S WHAT HE GETS!!!! Don: My wallet has been jacked again! Jim: Randy Orton has been chokeslammed, and this was The Undertaker's way of saying this title ain't yours!!! You're gonna have to go through me!!!! Don: Why is Taker picking on Orton anyway? Jim: He's not...Randy is nothing but a punk. [Undertaker's music blares over the PA, and he gets down on one knee before the title. He flips his hair back, his eye balls raise into the back of his skull and he slowly lifts one arm up....Orton holding his throat rolls to the outside, and the fans erupt into a huge face response for the Man From The Darkside.] Jim: You can bet that the Undertaker will be at Wrestlecade!!! But will that punk Randy Orton be there?? Don: Well not if he's going to get crucified! Jim: BY GOD THE UNDERTAKER IS HERE AND HE'S HERE TO STAY!!! [We see the camera man focusing in on Mikey Whipwreck. It goes from clear to blurry to clear to blurry and then finally to clear. Mikey is standing in front of a house on what appears to be a beautiful and sunny day. Below his face, the words "Miami, FL" pop up soooooo apparently, that explains the weather. Mikey is wearing a Hawaiian button-up shirt which is buttoned to the top, nearly choking his chubby neck, a pair of black slacks and holding a bouquet of flowers. His hair is slicked on the sides and combed, as if he were trying to make an impression on someone. He clears his throat.] M.W: Fans and stupidheads alike, this is Mikey Whipwreck reporting to you, live via satellite in front of The Rock's beautiful yet overly priced Florida residence. You know, The Rock played college for the Hurricanes right here in this state. It's a shame that he failed at that, because if he hadn't, he'd be a super NFL All-Pro Super Bowl Nine Time Mega World Plex Champion. Oh well. I guess not everyone is perfect. Anyway, I'm not here to yammer on about nothing, I'm here to see if The Rock's beautiful wife is at home, because I plan on seducing her with my amazing super kinetic powers. Trust me, I have super kinetic powers. This blind dude begging for change told me so once. Let's go! [Mikey jogs up to the steps where he stumbles and almost crashes headfirst into the door. He puts his face up against the door.] M.W: Goddamn stubbed toe, motherfu---Sorry. [He composes himself once more and knocks on the door. After waiting 15 seconds at the most, he knocks again, even harder and longer than before. Footsteps are heard and the door swings open, revealing a Hispanic maid.] M.W: Hola! Como estas? Bien. Ummm... Que... Tengo... Bar Mitzvah? Maid: I speak English. M.W: Oh, right. Of course you do. Duh! Stupid me! Is Mrs. Rock home? Maid: Mrs. Rock? M.W: Yeah, you know, The Rock's old lady, the chick, the female, the clitoris-bearing human being, the... you get the point. Maid: No, I'm afraid she is away. Did you have an appointment? M.W: Ah, yes. My name is Duane Johnson. Maid: It is not. M.W: It most certainly is and I'll thank you to not call me a liar again! As I was saying, my name is Duane Johnson, I'm from... Chad Costello's Big Bustle Of Flowers. I was just going to drop this off for her and maybe chat a bit... Some free coffee or tea.... Take advantage.... Free food... Drinks.... ? [The maid stare at Mikey as he just babbles on. He stops and shrugs.] M.W: Come on lady, have a heart. I'm just a huge Mrs. Rock fan and I've got to tell her that. Maid: Ah... Okay. But if you try ANYTHING funny, you'll be escorted off the premises, arrested, beaten, raped, electrocuted, cut, shot, stabbed, prodded and poked until your eyes explode and your scrotum sizzles into a nice charred piece of flesh. M.W: ........Fair enough. [Mikey steps into the house and gasps at the beauty. However, on every picture that includes Mrs. Rock, her face has been replaced with The Rock's.] M.W: Wait a minute, you mean to tell me there are MORE than ONE Rock? And it's female? Weird! Maid: No, sir. Those are pictures of The Rock and his wife, however, his ego gets the best of him and, well... Let's just say he's a bit fond of himself. M.W: I guess! That's nuts. If I had a chick like that, I'd be.... Well, doing dirty things. Maid: Of course you would. Let me get you a cup of coffee. M.W: Please, I'm a refined gentleman. I'd prefer a Pepsi. No ice. No poison or laxatives. Maid: Whatever you say. [The maid exits to the kitchen as Mikey runs and jumps into a huge plush chair in the den. Above the entertainment center in the room are framed championship belts, autographed pictures from several Hollywood stars and even more photos of The Rock with other celebrities. Mikey smiles like a little kid and takes it all in. He motions for the cameraman.] M.W: What a house! Rocky must've cheated a lot through high school and college to save up for this one! Since being a three-move wrestler doesn't pay that well, I wouldn't expect him to afford this from his wrestling career. [Mikey gets to his feet and scavenges through the pictures. He comes across one that is obviously a fake! Unless The Rock has a time travel machine....? Which he does, in his backyard, but the picture is still a fake. It features George Washington sitting on The Rock's lap. Weird!!!! As Mikey tries to wipe the dust off, he drops it and the picture shatters. He yelps like a little girl and looks around, but no one seems to have heard it. He kneels down to pick it up and notices a little slip of paper behind the picture. He looks at it... And appears extremely shocked. He holds it up for the camera and it reads..] JOURNAL ENTRY 09/17/98 Dear Journal; I saw this little lady today who asked for my number. I told her I don't swing that way, but she didn't listen. I then tried to show her the pink panties I was wearing as proof, but she still didn't buy it. So, to get her off me, I... [Scribbled over the last bit, obviously edited by Mikey, it reads...] "MIKEY WHIPWRECK IS BETTER THAN ME FOR EVER!" [After the camera picks it all up, Mikey crumples the piece of paper up and smiles. However, the maid walks in and catches him with the broken picture. Again, Mikey screams like a girl and makes a getaway. He breaks through the door before The Rock's twenty foot medieval dragon can get a hold of him. After he bolts down the street, he laughs out loud and then trips, eating a face full of concrete. Fade out.] [We comeback from break and we see in the back that AJ Styles has been laid out, and over him is the Canadian Flag. Gee...who could that be? We cut back to an empty ring the sound of glass breaking is heard all over the arena...Steve Austin walks down to the ring, and he looks like he's a man on a mission. He climbs into the ring and grabs the microphone, and a chair. He sits the chair down in the middle of the ring...and has a seat.] Steve: Since ol' AJ Styles ain't gonna be wrasslin' ta-night, why don't you Stan Hansen bring your filthy fat ass down to the ring so I can kick it. I got all damn night, and a whole lotta beers. [Crowd pops.] Steve: If you think that Stan Hansen is nothing but a total complete jack ass give me a hell yeah... Crowd: HELL YEAH!!! Steve: Stan the people have spoken...co - [Stand Hansen's music starts up and he walks out with bull rope around his neck. He's all smiles as he climbs into the ring. Steve Austin stands up and the two meet in the ring for an amazing show down.] Steve: See you decided to show up...I'm proud of ya. Stan: YOU KNOW WHAT THIS IS BOY? Steve: It's a bull rope. Stan: YOU KNOW WHAT THE HELL I CAN DO WITH IT?? Steve: What? Stan: YOU KNOW WHAT I CA - Steve: WHAT?? Stan: DON'T PLAY GAMES WITH ME AUSTIN....THE ONLY REASON I AIN'T KILLIN YOU RIGHT NOW IS BECAUSE YER NOTHING TO ME...I WAS SUPPOSED TO FIGHT FOR THE XCW US TITLE...BUT SOMEONE ELSE BEAT ME TO IT. Steve: So you're not down here to fight? You're just down here to wave yer little bull rope around, hopin' that you'll scare ol' Stone Cold? Is that it? Stan: YUP. Steve: Well I think you're dumber than you look. Stan: Watch is boy. Steve: First of all I ain't a boy, and second of all stand back a little because your breath smells like old peoples feet. I'm tried of having to come out here week after week and look and your ugly mug. Are we gonna dance at Wrestlecade or not? Stan: YES WE ARE AUSTIN...YES WE ARE!!! Steve: Hell...I'm okay with dancin' right now. I even brought my dancin' boots!!! [Austin goes for a KICK!!! WHAM!!! STUNNER....but Hansen grabs the foot, and Lariats him into the canvas. Hansen wraps the bull rope around his neck, and yanks him over the top rope...Austin is hanging there as Hansen holds on.] Jim: MAW GAWD someone help him...he's chokin' the damn life out of Austin. GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY...he's turning blue. Someone get the hell out there and stop this...Steve Austin is hurtin'...DAMN YOU HANSEN!!! Don: Here come the XCW officials finally... Jim: Hansen finally lets go and Austin drops to the floor below. Austin is hurt, and Hansen is a sick sick man. He needs to be locked up... Don: Well not until after Wrestlecade, because he's going to make some money with this Austin match. Jim: Hansen leaves the ring area...and they're attending to Austin. Fans our next match is the Main Event, and while they're helping Austin to the back lets take you to Ric Flair... [Camera cuts to Ric Flair who is standing by with comment...] Ric: WHOOO...Goldberg we're just a few minutes away from what is about to be a lesson you needed to learn so long ago. You have gone on to win many matches, but NEVER...and I mean NEVER will you be able to be in the same class as people like ME, Arn Anderson...Hulk Hogan....the list goes on and on. You are nothing but a flash in the pan pal....WHOOO!!! Do you know who I am? [Flair is starting to get upset and he's turning red.] Ric: I'M RIC WHOOO FLAIR....I'M THE MAN THAT YOU WISH YOU COULD BE. YOU COME TO THE RING BABY...AND YOU TRY TO MESS WITH ME, BECAUSE GOLDBERG....GOLDBERG....ONLY TWO THINGS ARE CERTAIN IN LIFE. ONE...YOU DIE...TWO...YOU GET BEAT BY RIC WHOOOO FLAIR!!!!! HOW DO YA LIKE THAT? [Flair leaves the interview area...and we cut back to the ring.] FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this is the MAIN EVENT.... [Goldbergs music starts up and he walks out to a huge heel response. He walks right through the fireworks, and ignores them. He climbs right in....as FINK announces his stats.] FINK: And his opponent.... ["2001" starts up and the lights go out. The spotlight hits the XCW walkway, and out walks Ric Flair in his blue velvet robe. He gives a huge WHOOO to the crowd, and continues to walk as fireworks go off behind him. Flair climbs into the ring, and undoes his robe.] Jim: This should be an interesting match here tonight...there's the bell, and both men meet in the center of the ring for a lock up...GOLDBERG TOSSES HIM INTO THE CORNER!! Don: WHOA... Jim: Now that was some power... [Flair looks to the crowd, and then gets back into the center of the ring to lock up. Right when they're about to lock up, Flair hits a thumb to the eye. Goldberg staggers back, and Flair unloads with a series of chops to the chest.] Jim: Flair with those chops...and they're even hurting Goldberg! Don: They must hurt then, because Goldberg has all that hair to cover his chest. Jim: Flair now with a jab, and another...he backs him into the corner, OH and Flair slaps him in the face. Don: He's the on - Jim: Goldberg didn't like that at all...he grabs Flair, and he hits him with a high knee to the midsection. Another high knee, and he whips him to the ropes...he lifts him up in a Gorilla Press...and he slams him down!!! [Goldberg waves his hands like he's going for the Spear....he backs into the corner, and he waits for Flair to get to his feet. Flair staggers back up and waves for Goldberg to charge him...as he's charging for the spear...we see Flair reach into his tights.] Jim: WHOA....HE NAILED GOLDBERG IN THE HEAD!!! Don: What did he just toss out? Jim: Flair goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!! THREE!!!! FLAIR WINS!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Ric Flair!!!! Jim: He used...those are...BRASS KNUCKLES, and Goldberg is out cold. Don: I can't believe he put Goldberg out like that!!! Jim: No kidding...this is two weeks in a row that the winner of the main event has figured out a way to cheat. Kurt Angle and Ric Flair...both men will meet each other at Wrestlecade..and the more things are different, the more they are the same. Don: I can't wait for that match. Jim: Flair is leaving the ring area...and Goldberg is still seeing stars. Fans we're out of time...SO LONG EVERYONE!!!! [Camera fades.]
