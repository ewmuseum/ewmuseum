XCW Friday Night X-Treme (part 2)

December 21, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: Welcome back to X-Treme...I was just telling "The King" just now that I think we're going to see Mikey Whipwreck pull off a victory tonight. I think we'll see The Rock ref in the main event on Monday, and I think that Mikey will win the title with his help.
Jerry: I don't think Mikey has a chance in hell. Joey: Well fans lets take you to our next match.... STACY: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall and is a
"Last Man Standing" match. Coming down to the ring first...from Canada. Here is
Edge!!!
("Yeah my Durango, #95....." is heard all over the arena and the fans are going wild. Edge comes out of the curtain with a black over coat on, and slides into the ring. He climbs up onto the turnbuckle, and the crowd gives him a decent pop.)
STACY: And his opponent....from the Hurri-cave...here is Hurricane Helms!!!! ("Stand back..." is heard all over the arena, and the crowd waits for a hurricane. Edge stands there and waits, but there is no Hurricane...)
Joey: Well obviously he's not going to make it tonight because someone beat the hell out of him in the parking lot. You think that Hurricane is going to be able to wrestle after w...OH.....MY.....GOD.
Jerry: What's he doing here? Joey: Oh my god...he is a monster. Vader is down here and he throws a chair into the ring, and he's telling Edge to pick it up.
Jerry: What the hell is Vader thinking? Joey: Vader climbs into the ring and charges Edge...Edge swings the chair, but
Vader blocks it with his arm. Vader with a right hand, and another right hand...he drives a left into the stomach of Edge.
Jerry: That's like fighting a bear! Joey: OH Vader with a clothesline and that takes Edge down like a ton of bricks. Vader now brings him back to his feet....POWER BOMB by the huge beast!!! Vader roams around the ring, and this guy is a beast.
Jerry: Why don't you get in there and tell him to leave? Joey: That's like taunting a shark with blood... Jerry: He is huge... Joey: He picks up Edge...not another one!!! POWER BOMB AGAIN!!!! Jerry: That's going to cause some serious damage. Vader leaves the ring and these fans are in awe of his size...so am I!!!
Joey: VADER IS BACK!!! (Camera fades onto Mikey Whipwreck who is entering the arena. He's whistling a familiar tune "Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck.".....he walks into his dressing room and The Rock is there. Mikey can't believe he's face to face with
The Rock. )
Mikey: Holy cow, can I have an autograph?? The Rock: A what? Mikey: An autograph, Mr... Uhhh.. Maivia? The Rock: The Rock comes into your dressing room to give you some advice about tonights match, and you want an autograph?
Mikey: Oh... Well, could you write the advice down, then autograph that? (The Rock puts his hand in Mikey's face) The Rock: Do not speak again until The Rock says you can... Mikey: Um... Ok.. The Rock: Now The Rock comes in here because he's damn sick and tired of being in the Land Of The Rising Sun. He's sick of all these little people, and he's damn sick and tired of the thick garlic smell...which reminds The Rock...do you like sushi?
Mikey: Well.... No. The Rock:You don't like sushi? Well then Little Mikey Whipwreck you must like eggrolls...do you like eggrolls?
Mikey: I used to. But ever since I came to Japan, every street vendor, every biker, every whatever has been stuffing their eggrolls in my mouth.
The Rock: You make The Rock sick...taking anyone eggrolls you can find. So does that make you a little cumsi-coomsa?
Mikey: Cumsi-coomWHAT? Uh, sure. The Rock: Maybe The Rock isn't speaking your language...do you like pie? Mikey: Oh definitely. My mom's was the best. The Rock: The Rock thinks that you are one messed up kid, and tonight if you don't listen to The Rocks advice we might all be stuck in this hell hole. Now
The Rock loves to make people bleed... The Rock has made Triple H bleed.. And if there's ONE place...that Triple H bleeds it's that big ol' nose of his.
Mikey: Ah, got'cha. The Rock: Now if you let the Rock down...he will have to Rock bottom you. Now
The Rock doesn't want to do that, but he will....
(Rock turns around to leave and before he leaves he says this...) The Rock: Why don't you grow some facial hair kid...it'll make you look like a man. (After Rock leaves, Mikey's lip trembles) Mikey: I SHAVE EVERY DAY FOR YOUR INFORMATION!!!!!!..........damn you...... (Camera fades)
