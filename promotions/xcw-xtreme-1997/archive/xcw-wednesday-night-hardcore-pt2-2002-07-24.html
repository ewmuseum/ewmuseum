XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 2)

July 24, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We come back from commercial and we see Bret Hart talking with Christian. Both men are laughing, and talking back and forth.]
Joey: Fans this next match should be a big one. Christian and Chris Benoit will meet in the ring tonight.
Joel: Benoit last week basicaly said that he doesn't want any friends, he doesn't wany any help. The man is a loner, the man doesn't care about anyone.
Tonight he shows us that he's not pro-Canada, or anti-Canada. He is just Chris
Benoit. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen , making his way to the ring, weighing in at 220 pounds, from Edmonton, Alberta, Canada he is "THE RABID WOLVERINE" CHRIS
BENOIT! [Chris Benoit comes out and his pryo behind him fire off purple. He climbs into the ring and as he drags his thumb across his neck, fireworks go off in the ring. He stretches a little and then waits for his opponent.]
FINK: And his opponent...hailing from Canada. He weighs in at 222, here is
Christian!!!! ["Christian....Christian....At Last Your On Your OWN!!!!" is heard all over the
PA, and he walks out to a huge heel response. He's got the Canadian Flag waving in the air, and he stops to look at a fan...and laugh. He climbs into the ring and points the the Flag, and Benoit just smirks.]
Joey: There's the bell, and both men with a lock up. Benoit with a go-behind, and a double leg takedown. Christian eats nothing but canvas...
Joel: And that's not going to make the ladies in Canada very happy. Joey: Benoit waits for him to get up and he charges him with a follow through lariat. [Christian gets up, and Benoit unloads with nasty knife-edge chops. Over and over and over again. Christian holds his chest in pain, and Benoit takes him down with a snap mare. Christian is in the sitting position, and Benoit dropkicks the back of his neck.]
Joey: Whew...he means business tonight. Joel: Benoit is a mean man. Joey: Christian now trying to get back to his feet, and Benoit brings him up once again. Drives a boot to the stomach and he lifts him up....for a Northern
Lights Suplex.
[Christian bounces off hard, and Benoit goes to the ropes. He tries a kneedrop, but Christian moves out of the way. He waits for Benoit to recover, and he takes him down with a dropkick.]
Joey: Both men back up now, and Benoit swings and misses. Christian catches him...Torture Rack. NO!!! OH MY GOD!!!! He turns it into a neckbreaker...what an amazing move that was!!!
Joel: How do you like that? Joey: Benoit holding his neck in pain, and now Christian has full advantage. Christian now puts his hands together and drops a HUGE double ax-handle across the back of the neck on Benoit, causing him to scream out in pain!!!
[Christian whips Benoit into the ropes, but he reverses it and sends Christian in.] Joey: Benoit catches him with a nasty powerslam!!! Here's the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Joel: NO!! Joey: Christian shows his toughness and gets out. Benoit brings Christian back up and he hits him with a headbutt that sends him against the ropes. Benoit charges...oh!!!!
Joel: Ouch man!!! Joey: Benoit went through the top - middle rope and he's hung himself. He's trying to break free, but it's not working.
Joel: Christian starts to kick away on Benoit...what a smart idea. Keep doing it. Joey: Benoit flips himself back over onto the canvas, and finally he breathes. Christian charges him though and dropkicks him in the face!!! [Benoit rolls out of the ring, and Christian follows. Christian whips him towards the steel steps, but Benoit with a reverse. Christian hits the ring steps and the top on goes crashing to the floor.]
Joey: Just when you think you have Chris Benoit, just when you think the man is down he gets right back into the game.
Joel: Benoit doesn't want this match outside. Joey: Chris throws him into the ring, and he follows in. [Benoit climbs up to the top ropes...he flies off with the Swandive Headbutt, but Christian moves out of the way. Christian gets up and brings the stunned
Benoit to his feet...he locks in the Unprettier, and Benoit reverses it into a backslide.]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!!!! Joel: WOW that was close. Joey: Chris Benoit almosr lost, and then he turned it around and Christian almost lost. [Christian walks right into a boot to the stomach, and Benoit follows up with a
Brainbuster DDT. Benoit locks in the Crossface right after....]
Joey: And there's the ref checking on Christian. This match could be over... Joel: Christian is about to tap. Joey: WAIT!!! Bret Hart slides into the ring...and he smashes Benoit across the back of the head with a Steel Chair!!!!
[Ref calls for the bell and takes off.] Joey: Bret Hart is beating the hell out of Benoit with that chair. He's smashing his damn head in.
Joel: Christian is still out in pain. Joey: Bret Hart brings Benoit to his feet, and he executes a huge swipe with that chair. Benoit's nose is busted wide open.
Joel: This is awesome. NEVER MESS WITH CANADA!!!! Joey: Bret is going to do it a - NO!!!! WHAT!?!? DYNAMITE KID!!!!! [In a flash of cameras we see Dynamite Kid run into the ring in his jeans an t-shirt. He spins Bret Hart around, and dropkicks the chair right into his face. Christian is up, and Dynamite catches him in a hip toss.]
Joey: WHOA....Dynamite Kid is here. Joel: I KNOW!!! I WET MYSELF!!!! Joey: Bret is up again, and he gets right in Kid's face...and Benoit is up now!!! [Benoit's face is a bloody mess, and he German Suplex's Hart from behind. Christian instead of fighting again...leaves the ring area. Dynamite turns around only to walk into the bloody face of Benoit.]
Joey: And the crowd gets silent. Joel: I NEVER thought I'd see this. Joey: Dynamite Kid and Chris Benoit...the teacher and the student. Joel: AWE I WET MYSELF AGAIN!!! Joey: I think we're all wetting ourselves. [Dynamite extends his hand, and Benoit punches him.] Joey: OH MY GOD!!!!! Dynamite now with a series of lefts and rights, Benoit isn't stopping either.
Joel: They look like two machines going at it!!!!!!! Joey: Here come the XCW officials to break things up. Dynamite Kid was trying to be nice, and Chris Benoit just embarassed him. Fans as they try to clear the ring we're going to take you to a Anna - wait a second.
[Camera cuts to Sho Funaki, who seems very upset. Jim Ross is running right behind him telling him no, but he's going to the ring anyway. Sho's intrupreter
Bob Kurimoto is following as well....]
Joey: Sho has something to say!!! We'll be right back!!!
