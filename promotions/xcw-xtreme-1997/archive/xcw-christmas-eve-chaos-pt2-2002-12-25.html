XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 2)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Steve Austin is here, and by God is looks like he's ready for a fight. Steve Austin looks like he's got his sights set on whoever he has to fight tonight. [All of a sudden "O' Canada" starts up and Don Callis walks out from the curtain. The crowd gives him a huge face response, and he's kinda taken back by it. He soaks it up and then walks to the ringside area. He picks up the headset, and picks up like he never left.]
Don: Oh man is it good to be on this Pay Per View. One damn day away from
Christmas...how the hell do you like that?
Jim: It's exciting. Don: Yeah well I got my present last night after I met these two Hula girls. Oh man. They unwrapped my package fast that you can say "Mooka Laka Heekey".
Jim: I'm thrilled. Don: Ugh I saw you yesterday trying to get some sun. You almost made me throw up in front of those two girls. Lucky I turned around before you big Oklahoma boobies started flopping around.
Jim: You know Lenny Lane was so much better. Don: You've got to be kidding me. [Cut backstage. Sitting front and center, lacing up his boots is none other than HBK, looking flamboyant as usual. Later tonight he'll be stepping into the ring with none other than Val Venis, a man who isn't even fit to shine HBK's boots. But like the outstanding individual that he is, he accepts his task and prepares for tonights pay per view event.]
HBK: Tonight's a night I didn't look forward to, Venis is a young gun, shooting for the stars...but tonight I'm gonna have to knock him off his high horse and show him what a ring veteran is capable of.
[HBK stands up revealing his attire to be black pants, with red hearts covering them. As he stands, he throws a towel over his shoulder.]
HBK: Venis, it's not something I wanna do...but if kicking your teeth down your throat gets me one step closer to the world title, then by all means...that's my intention.
You can take this as a big step in your career, but after your loss tonight you can take it as a big set back. Your not ready to step in the ring with the likes of me, and I'm not even trying to sound cocky....But we gotta do what we gotta do, let the games begin....[Fade.]
Jim: Fans lets take you to our next match! [The FINK announces Val Venis first, and he comes out to a decent reaction. He slides into the ring and stretches a little before FINK announces his opponent who is "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels. The fans pop for him as he makes his way down the ring area...he dances and prances, and climbs in. He poses as the fireworks go off behind him, and then the ref holds up the vacant XCW TV
Title.]
Jim: This should be a good match for the Televison title. Don: Yes sir. [Both men lock up and Venis sends HBK down to the mat.] Jim: Val Venis has a lot of power behind him, and it might not look like it but he does. Don: So was Lane really better than me? Jim: I'm not saying anything. Don: Bastard. Jim: Shawn gets up and he's eyeing Val Venis. Shawn charges Venis, and Venis goes for a clothesline, but Shawn slides between his legs.
[Dropkick to the back of the right knee by Shawn Michales, and another one. Val drops to one knee, and HBK takes him down with a jumping face smash.]
Jim: He goes for the cover...ONE!!! Don: And that's all he's going to get with that one. Jim: Val back to his feet, and Shawn catches him and hits a Dragon Screw. Shawn hangs onto the knee and flips over...which snaps the knee back of Venis.
Don: Whoa...he means business tonight. Jim: HBK with a side leglock now, and Venis is reaching for the ropes. [The ref tells Shawn to let go, and he doesn't fight with him. Shawn gets up and goes to the outside apron and waits for Venis to get back to his feet.]
Jim: Val up once again, limping around. He turns his focus to Shawn, and Shawn with a slingshot clothesline!!!
Don: He's pulling off some nice moves tonight, I wonder if God is managing him? Jim: Please don't say that with me so close to you. Don: Hmm lightning might strike, but hopefully he'll mistake me for some fat guy with a cowboy hat.
[Shawn Michaels drops a knee across the kneecap, and another knee. Val screams out in pain, and HBK takes that as a sign to do more damage. He brings Val's bad leg over to the ropes and sets it up. He leaps up and sits down on it.]
Jim: I think Shawn is taking a page out of Hunter's book. Shawn with another one, and another one. That's three times, and Venis is really struggling here in the begining of this match.
Don: Well I was expecting Venis to take over right away, and then see Shawn dazzle us all with a comeback. Isn't that how it works?
Jim: That seems to be the case for most of his matches. [Shawn Michaels sends Venis limping into the turnbuckle. Shawn goes charging it but just stops short of making impact. He backs up a little and dropkicks that knee of Val.]
Jim: HBK up once again, and he charges in at Venis. VENIS MOVES!!!! Don: Turnbuckle city! Jim: Val rolls to the outside and grabs HBK's feet. CROTCHED!!!! Don: Dear Lord...that looks like it hurts. [Venis rolls back into the ring and pushes Shawn against the turnbuckle.] Jim: Right hand by Venis, and another right hand. He unloads with a chop, and another chop to the chest. Venis backs up and HBK staggers out of the turnbuckle, and Venis hurls him down to the mat with a NASTY Spinebuster.
Don: That'll wear on his back. Jim: Venis brings him back up and whips him into the ropes. He catches him in a sidewalk slam, and Shawn crashes down onto his back hard. Venis brings him back up almost right away...
Don: This is for the TV title isn't it? Jim: Thanks for paying attention. Don: I'm sorry, I've been busy here in Hawaii. You expect me to do all kinds of research? There are hot women out here to persue...that's why you're here. You do all the homework.
Jim: Just like in High School. Don: How long ago was that? Jim: Venis once again throws HBK into the ropes, and he hits ANOTHER Sidewalk slam. [Shawn arches his back in pain, and Venis drops over him to make the cover...] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Shawn barely gets his shoulder up here tonight. Don: That had to take a lot from HBK. Jim: Venis brings Shawn back to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Shawn bounces off and goes for a flying forearm...Venis ducks!!! Val ducked out of the way, and HBK hits nothing but air.
Don: And he hit that air hard. Jim: Venis makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Shawn Michaels kicks out at the last second once again, and the fans are really into this match.
Don: I'm starting to get into it. Jim: Val brings up Shawn Michaels and whips him into the ropes, and he catches him off with a HUGE powerslam!!! He hooks the leg, and there's ONE!!! TWO!!! No
Kickout...
[Venis doesn't wait at all and he whips Shawn into the turnbuckle. Val charges in and Shawn lifts his boot up right in the nick of time. Venis staggers back and Shawn grabs the bad leg...]
Jim: Shawn rolls out of the ring and he crotches VAL this time!!! Don: Oh Lord... Jim: Shawn now...FIGURE FOUR AGAINST THE POST!! Don: Talk about intense pain. Jim: Even FINK can barely look. The ref starts in with the five count, and HBK breaks it at four. The damage has already been done though. Michaels rolls back into the ring and pulls Venis out of the turnbuckle by the hair...
[Michaels brings him to the center of the ring and goes for the Figure Four...] Jim: SMALL PACKAGE BY VENIS!!!! Don: He's got it locked in!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout...HBK kicks out at the last second. Shawn gets up right away and he's undaunted. He starts kicking away on that bad leg of Val
Venis and this time he goes for the Figure Four and he doesn't get any trouble.
Don: Look at Venis, and how his face is all contorted. Jim: He's not giving up though, the man isn't going to give up. Here comes the ref to check on him, he's trying to see if he's ready to call it quits. Val is shaking his head no, but he drops back.
Don: This could be a pin! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Don: Shawn almost had him there. Jim: Val quickly sits up...OH GOOD GOD!! Shawn with a closed fist right to the forehead of Val.
[Val goes back down, but sits right back up.] Jim: Shawn goes for another punch, but Val blocks it. He's twisting that arm now, and Shawn is screaming out in pain. He's not letting go.
Don: That has to hurt. Jim: Venis lets go and unloads with a series of fists to the ribs, and Shawn releases the hold finally. Both men crawl out of harm's way, and struggle to their feet.
[Shawn charges Val, and Val backdrops him HIGH over the top rope and to the outside onto the cement.]
Don: SPLAT!!! Jim: Shawn landed right on that bad back, and Val rolls out to join him. Shawn is trying to make it back to his feet using that guardrail, and Val charges him...CLOTHESLINE OVER THE TOP OF THE GUARDRAIL!!!
Don: He's with the Hawaiin fans now!!! Jim: Venis goes after him, and he unloads with lefts and rights. Venis charges
Shawn now, and Shawn WITH A DROP TOE-HOLD...Venis lands right onto a fan on his chair, and the fan goes falling to the ground.
Don: I love that when a fan gets hurt. [Venis struggles back to his feet. He turns around and walks right into a
SUPERKICK by Shawn Michaels.]
Jim: How much more can these two men take? Don: Obviously a whole lot more. Jim: The officials in the XCW upper office decided it would be best to have
Venis and HBK fight, and Don...I think they're right.
Don: Cena wouldn't have lasted three seconds in the ring with HBK. Jim: HBK throws Venis over the guardrail and back to the outside. HBK hops over and brings him to his feet. Venis swings wildly, and Shawn grabs his arm. SNAP
SUPLEX ON THE CEMENT!!! [Shawn slowly gets up, holds his back for a minute and then rolls Venis back in. Shawn rolls back in as well and hooks the leg for the pin...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!!! Don: Barely. He was almost beaten right there. Jim: Shawn brings him up once again...go-behind by HBK, and he hits a Back
Suplex. Val's neck hits hard, and Shawn rolls him up again for the cover. This could be it...ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!!!! Once again Shawn Michaels comes so close, but doesn't get close enough.
Don: I can't believe the fight that Val Venis has in him. Jim: Shawn talking to the ref for a brief moment, and he brings Val up. He whips him into the ropes, Val reverses with a short whip...TILT-A-WHIRL SIDE
SLAM!!!!!! Don: He's not making the cover. Jim: Val is trying to shake off the cob webs..he's trying clear his head, and he drops down to the mat and covers Shawn. ONE!!! TWO!!! NO....MY GOD!!!! Shawn
Michaels won't give up either.
[Val Venis gets up and whips Shawn into the ropes and takes him down with a hard back elbow to the jaw. Val covers again...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Don: And look at Venis waste time. Jim: Val is trying to think of something else he can do to take Shawn Michaels out of the game here tonight. Shawn staggers back up and walks into
Val...go-behind by Venis, and he goes for a Pumphandle Slam...POWERSLAM!!! Don: This is it! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!!! Michaels barely got his shoulder up, and this place is so quiet you can hear a pin drop. They thought Val Venis was going to win this one here tonight.
Don: Venis thought it was over too. That has to be the worst feeling in the world, when you think you won and really you're just prolonging the fact that you have to do more in order to win.
[Venis brings HBK back to his feet and HBK fires back with a series of jabs to the jaw. Shawn runs to the ropes, but Venis follows. Venis clotheslines himself and HBK over the top ropes.]
Jim: Both men end up on a sitting position on the apron, and Venis with the sweat just pouring from his head reaches up and pulls himself up with the rope.
He grabs HBK by the hair. Don: I can't imagine what he's going to do. Jim: He grabs Shawn by the throat...CHOKESLAM!!!!! CHOKESLAM OFF THE RING APRON!!!!! Don: And that'll put HBK right next to Dynamite Kid. Jim: Shawn Michaels is out...he must have passed out from the pain. Val Venis is barely standing, the match has been so much. I haven't seen anything like this out of Val Venis or Sean Morley EVER. The kids got guts, the kids got fight and damnit he's taking it to a true legend right now.
Don: Yeah yeah... Jim: Val rolls HBK back into the ring, and he rolls back in as well. Venis brings him to his feet, and he is going for a POWERBOMB!!!! If he hits this, then it's all over. No way is Shawn Michaels going to come out of this.
Don: Yeah this could be it. Jim: He lifts him up...no Venis sets him back down. That chokeslam must have taken everything, and I mean everything out of him.
Don: Just try to pin him. Jim: Venis tries for it once again. He can't do it...he slumps down once again, and HBK WITH A ROLL UP!!!!!! Small Package!!!
Don: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Jim: NO!!! NO NO NO NO NO!!!! Val kicked out at the last second. Don: That almost worked. He caught Venis off guard, and we almost had a new TV
Champion in Shawn Michaels.
[It seems like that whole scene gave both men a second wind. Val Venis gets up and starts to back Shawn Michaels into the corner. Shawn drops to his knees and starts to beg...]
Jim: Shawn backs into the turnbuckle and he's got no where to go. Venis points to him and it's time...Venis is ready to wrap this match up.
[Venis slowly goes to HBK, and Shawn reaches out and pulls the tights of Val. This causes him to crash hard into the turnbuckle. Val stumbles out of the corner, and HBK chop blocks that bad knee.]
Jim: And Val crumbles to the mat. Don: What a smart move there. See Val is so new to this sport, he doesn't realize what or who he's in the ring with. Shawn Michaels is the Jordon of
Wrestling...
Jim: HBK brings him up and starts in with stinging chops to the chest, over and over...he backs Venis into the turnbuckle. Shawn climbs up on top and he's starting to unload with a series of fists to the forehead.
[1...2...3...4...5...6...and all of a sudden Venis reaches up and grabs him by the waist. Venis starts to march forward...]
Jim: VENIS!!! POWERBOMB FROM THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE!!!!!! Don: WHAT!?!?! Jim: There's the count...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Val Venis has won the XCW
Television Title!!!!
[He falls down to the mat as the ref hands him the new XCW TV Title. Val raises it into the air, and he helps himself up using the ropes. Val holds the title up high, and the fans in Hawaii are going wild.]
Jim: Val Venis has beaten HBK here tonight in a match that was not only good, it was epic. Don: Oh please...now he's had his greatest match. Everything after this will be just your standard TV Title match.
Jim: Way to kill a moment. Don: My pleasure. Jim: Val holds his title high into the a - SUPERKICK!!!!! Venis ducks it!!! Val ducked it and now he's pointing at Shawn!!
[Chris Jericho and Triple H run down to the ring...] Jim: Jericho and Triple H are here...and OH MY GOD!!! Triple H smashes the chair into the back of Val Venis.
Don: NICE!!!! Jim: Shawn tells them to hold him up...someone please do something about this. [You can hear the fans start the chant of "Taz", but he hasn't shown up.] Jim: SUPERKICK!!!! HBK WITH A SUPERKICK!!! Don: Sweet Chin Music!!! Jim: Shawn throws the title down onto the knocked out Venis, and all three men are leaving. Ugh this is a horrible way to celebrate after Venis' first big title win.
Don: Hey I don't mind...where's my party hat? [DX leaves the ring area, and Val Venis is being helped up by the XCW officials. We cut to the back of the arena once again and this time we see a
Black and Silver Escalade pull up. Who steps out but Bill Goldberg...he has an extremely serious look on his face, and doesn't awknowledge any of the fans as he walks in.]
