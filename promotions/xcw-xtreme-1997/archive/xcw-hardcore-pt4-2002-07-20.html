XCW Hardcore (part 4)

July 20, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FINK: And his opponent, from Boston, Massachusetts, weighing in a 260 pounds,
He is John Cena!
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen , making his way to the ring, from New York City, he is the current reigning television champion, MIKEY WHIPRECK!
[Both men shake hands, and they circle each other. Mikey with a lock up, and they both struggle for position. Cena backs Mikey up against the turnbuckle, and gives a clean break.]
Joey: What a different John Cena we've seen in the past few days. After he came to the aid Sting, and his family...the man has just turned into this nice guy.
Joel: Yeah a nice guy...means gay. Joey: It does not. Both men circling each other once again, and Cena with a side headlock. Mikey sends him into the ropes, and he goes for a duck down.
Cena bounces off the ropes..and Mikey with a backdrop. Joel: Cena with a sunset flip...so he can check out Mikey's balls. Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! Mikey rolls out, and both men back to their feet. Cena extends his hand to Mikey, and Mikey quickly slaps it. Both men start to circle each other again, and Mikey goes for a lock up...Cena with a quick arm drag.
[Cena locks in an armbar, and Mikey quickly gets up and heads to the ropes. Cena has to let go, and Mikey turns around. He walks right into a side headlock takedown, but Mikey counters with a head scissors.]
Joel: And look how Cena isn't fighting it. Joey: He is too...Cena ia not gay. Joel: And nither is Richard Simmons. Joey: John kips up and breaks out of the hold, and Mikey makes it back up. John walks right into a sideheadlock, and this time he executes a head scissors.
Joel: SEE!!! SEE!!! [Mikey goes for a kip up, but it doesn't work. Cean notices that he's trying to break out and he takes his leg and smashes it across the bridge of Mikey's nose. Not once, but twice. Cena lets go, and makes it to his feet.]
Joey: That was rather brutal. Joel: It's because people are starting to catch on. If he holds Mikey there long enough we're going to see him start smiling, and then it's just going to be a dirty little scene that even Santa Monica Blvd...can't handle.
[Mikey makes to his feet and he's checking his nose out. Cena runs to the ropes, and he charges Mikey. Mikey drops to his stomach, and Cena flies over the top rope and out onto the floor.]
Joey: Whoa, what a mistake made there by Cena. Joel: You're telling me... Joey: Mikey rolls out of the ring, and he brings Cena to his feet. Mikey with a right hand, and another right hand. Whipwreck now with a chop...he whips Cena into the guardrail. Things are starting to get a little rough here tonight.
Joel: John and Mikey have taken off the gloves. They're not playing by the rules anymore. Joey: Mikey rolls him back into the ring, and he climbs back in as well. Mikey brings him back to his feet and drives a foot into the stomach. Mikey goes for a suplex, and he takes him down.
[Mikey rolls back to his feet and drops a leg across the throat, and hooks the leg.] Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Cena kicks out. Joel: What a match Whipwreck had with Randy Savage. Joey: They had the best 1st match on a PPV I have ever seen. Mikey with a bodyslam, and he brings Cena right back up again. Mikey sends him into the turnbuckle, and Cena comes out...Mikey with a dropkick.
[John slouches against the turnbuckle, and Mikey climbs to the second rung. He starts firing in punches to the side of the face...1....2....3..4...5...6....7..8..right before he gets to 9, Cena hits a nice sitting powerbomb.]
Joey: Cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Mikey kicks out at the two count. Joel: Notice all the pins Cena has executed tonight have been between the damn legs. Joey: NO. [Cena gets back up, and Mikey staggers to his feet. John with a boot to the stomach, and he lifts him up...TURNBUCKLE POWERBOMB.]
Joey: OH MY GOD!!! What a move that was. Joel: I don't think that move has ever been used here in the XCW... Joey: Leave it up to John Cena to do something like that. He pulls Mikey out of the corner, and he hooks the leg once again. Mikey kicks out at the two count though, and this is turning out to be a very good TV Title match.
Joel: People have wanted to see this for a very long time. Joey: They have? Joel: No I was being told to say that. [Cena brings Mikey back up, and takes him down with a snap mare. Cena hits a chinlock, and Mikey starts to wave his arms.]
Joey: And slowly his arms are getting heavier. Joel: Look at the sweat drip from Cena's head. I bet his boyfriend in the back is having a jolly time with this.
Joey: I'm going to tell him you think he is gay. Joel: NO. Please don't. [The crowd starts to rally behind Mikey Whipwreck, and he starts to make it to his feet. Elbow to the stomach, and another elbow to the stomach. Cena lets go of the hold, and Mikey with a Tornado DDT!!!]
Joey: Wow, and lights are out for John Cena. Mikey is going for the cover, but
Cena gets his shoulder up quickly.
Joel: Don't count that man out, he's had his bootie flakes tonight. Joey: Mikey brings Cena up once again, and he kicks him in the stomach. He goes for the Whipper - NO!!! Sho Funaki is down here...he's on the apron.
[Mikey walks over to Sho as the ref is dealing with Cena.] Joey: OH!!! SHO NAILS HIM WITH A RIGHT HAND!!! Joel: He had brass knucks in his hand. Joey: Mikey is down, and Cena makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! FINK: Here is your winner and NEW XCW Television Champion, John Cena!!! Joey: Sho is leaving the ring area, and Cena squints his eyes and realizes he got a tainted win.
Joel: What the hell is he doing now? Joey: Cena is talking to the ref, and he doesn't want the title. [Cena hands Earl Hebner the belt, and says he doesn't want it. He wants to give it back to Mikey, who is still out cold. Earl is shaking his head no, and he gives the title right back to Cena.]
Joey: And Mikey is up now, and he knows what happened. Cena spins him around, and he hands Mikey the title.
Joel: What the hell? Mikey drops the title on the floor.... Joey: He's got bigger fish to fry now. Fans as John Cena is yelling with the ref, lets take you to our Annihilation update....
