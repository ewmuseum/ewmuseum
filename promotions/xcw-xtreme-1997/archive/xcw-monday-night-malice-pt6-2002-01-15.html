XCW Monday Night Malice (part 6)

January 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Now it's time for the Main Event. The big question of the night is will
Austin turn on Whipwreck? We saw Austin earlier today talking to Costello, and he seemed pretty happy about what he was asked to do.
Jesse: Well he shook hands with Chad, and then told him they'd get a beer later. I don't think that he was joking.
Jim: We're going to find out right now!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall, and is the Main Event, Tag Team match. First coming down to the ring at a total combined weight of 620 pounds. Here is "The Franchise" Shane Douglas, and
Vader!!!
(Both men come down to the ring to a huge deal of heel heat. Things are being thrown at them, and they don't seem to care. Although they do seem to be getting along very well.)
FINK: And their opponents...weighing in at a total combined weight of 525 pounds. Here is "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, and Mikey Whipwreck!!!
(The duo comes out to Austin's music, and the fans are popping like crazy. They climb and and work the crowd in their own special way.)
Jim: There's the bell and it looks like Whipwreck is going to start off against
Shane Douglas.
Jesse: I've been looking forward to the return of the "Franchise" for a long time. Too bad Francine ain't here to support her man. I would've liked to see her too.
Jim: I don't know where Mikey got it, but he doesn't seem scared at all to face
Douglas in the ring tonight. We all remember on Christmas Eve when Shane
Douglas dressed up as Santa and DDT'd the hell out of Mikey! Jesse: Yeah that was supposed to be Mikey's night, and it was ruined. <Both men lock up, and Douglas goes for a real quick rake to the eyes. The ref gets in his face, and Douglas pushes him out of the way. Shane walks right into a fist though, and Mikey takes him down with a bulldog.>
Jim: Bulldog by Mikey, and that takes Douglas down by suprise. Both men back up now, and Mikey with a suplex!!!
Jesse: Can you believe the offense coming from Whipwreck? Jim: I can't, Jess. This man suprises me every single time he climbs into the ring. <Mikey waits for Douglas to get back to his feet and he dropkicks him down to the canvas. Both men get up again and Douglas wipes the blood from his busted open lip. Mikey tells him to come at him....>
Jim: Ric Flair said that he had someone to watch Mikey's back...who do you think it is? Jesse: Maybe it's David Flair? Jim: I sure hope not...for Mikeys sake anyway. <Mikey kicks Douglas in the stomach and hit s swinging neckbreaker on him.>
Jim: Mikey is up now and he goes to make the tag to Steve Austin, and Austin isn't tagging. WHAT THE HELL!?
Jesse: Well I guess he is doing what Chad told him to do. Jim: He's telling Mikey that he's doing a good job...Douglas from behind!!! <Douglas takes him down with a Side Russian Legsweep, and starts putting the boots to him. He see's the Vader wants in...so he makes the tag.>
Jim: Vader makes the tag into the ring, and he brings Whipwreck up. Mikey gets bombed with lefts and rights....and this match is going to be over in a matter of seconds if he doesn't tag.
Jesse: Vader is going to pummel him into the ground! <Vader backs him into the corner and just works him over with jabs, and swinging punches. Mikey falls down in the corner and Vader gets a running start....>
Jim: I'm afraid to see what's going to happen.... Jesse: LOOK OUT!!! <Vader's knees collide with Mikey's face, and the crowd gasps in disbelief. Mikey holds his nose in pain, and Vader drags him to the center of the ring. Vader with about 3 elbow drops, and then he tags Douglas back in.> Jim: These two men seem to be working fine as a team here tonight. Looking at
Whipwreck, I'd have to say I think his nose is broken.
Jesse: It is...nobody takes that kind of impact from a huge man like Vader and then walks away from it. He does have a broken nose, and Austin should be in there trying to help him out.
Jim: Well Costello doesn't want Mikey to compete in the Main Event at the PPV, and he's going to do whatever it takes to see that happens.
Jesse: Douglas is like a shark. If he see's blood he's going to attack even harder than before, and he's stomping all over that nose.
<Douglas runs to the ropes and drops a knee across the bridge of Whipwreck's nose, and the camera cuts to Austin who is just casually sitting there.>
Jim: Damnit Austin...be a man and help the kid out. You're his tag team partner... Jesse: Not is Costello is going to soil his pockets. <Shane grabs Whipwreck and throws him into the corner. Douglas tags Vader into the ring and both men start hitting him with lefts and rights. They both grab
Whipwreck and hit him with a Double Suplex.>
Jim: This doesn't look good for Mikey Whipwreck... Jesse: No, no it doesn't. Jim: Damnit Chad Costello, I hope your happy. You're ruining a man's life here tonight, everything the boy and hoped for...and tonight you're throwing it away for a couple of bucks you'll make if Vader or Douglas is champion instead.
Jesse: UH oh and Vader is calling for the Power Bomb. Jim: And wait a minute that doesn't sit too well with Shane Douglas. Douglas gets into the ring and I think they're fighting over who's going to finish
Whipwreck first.
Jesse: Now is not the time to do this. Jim: Whipwreck is dragging himself over to his corner, and Vader just pushed
Douglas. Now Douglas pushes Vader...we're about to have a brawl here right now!!!
Jesse: WHIPWRECK MAKES THE TAG TO AUSTIN!!!!!! Jim: Austin is helping Mik....KICK!!! WHAM!!! STUNNER!!! DAMNIT HE DID IT!!!! Jesse: Well he said he was going to do it and he did...Austin just turned on his tag team partner, and now he's walking to the locker room. Steve Austin proved me wrong here tonight...I thought he was going to stay and fight.
Jim: Douglas and Vader stop fighting after they saw Austin stun Whipwreck, and now they're smiling. Both men know that it's over...
<Vader brings the knocked out Whipwreck to his feet and executes a VADER BOMB!!!!> Jim: GOOD GAWD ALMIGHTY THAT BROKE HIM IN HALF!!! HELL!!!!! Jesse: WHOA!!!! Jim: Somebody stop this....where the hell is security? Jesse: I think Douglas wants to get a piece of the action too. <Douglas and Vader bring Whipwreck back up to his feet but he falls right back down.> Jim: The man is out of it, and they're still trying to bring him up for another move. This is disgusting...what a disgrace to the XCW. Chad Costello is a sick, sick man, and I hope he gets what he deserves sooner or later.
Jesse: This isn't right....first Brooklyn Brawler, and now his son Mikey Whipwreck. Jim: Stop it Jesse...nothing is funny at this point. The kid could be seriously hurt, and they're trying to put him out of wrestling for good. Why don't you go in there and do something?
Jesse: HA!! Against Vader AND Shane Douglas...you're out of your mind. Jim: Where is Ric Flair when you need him? <Shane Douglas puts the knocked out Whipwreck in a front facelock, and hits the
Single Arm DDT. Mikey starts to convulse on the canvas, and both men continue to kick and stomp him into the mat.>
Jim: I'm about to go in there and stop this!!! THIS IS ABSOLUTELY HEINOUS!!!!! SICK BASTARDS!!!! GET THEM OUT OF THERE!!!! Jesse: They won't be satisfied unti..... (All of a sudden the sound of a Candance starts to play...the snare starts up and then the lights go out....)
Jim: WHAT IN THE HELL? BOOM!!!! (When the lights comeback on Goldberg is standing in the ring, and everyone stands to their feet and he screams out...both Douglas and Vader stop what they're doing and stare at him.)
Jim: GOLDBERG!!!! GOLDBERG!!!! BY GOD, GOLDBERG HAS COME TO SAVE MIKEY WHIPWRECK!!!! Jesse: What in the hell?! Jim: SPEAR!!!! SPEAR ON DOUGLAS!!!!!!!!!!! <Douglas gets speared and rolls out of the ring onto the canvas. Vader and
Goldberg meet in the center of the ring, and neither of them are about to move.
Vader is telling Goldberg to hit him, and all of a sudden Paul E sprints down to the ring...>
Jim: Paul E is down here, and he's calming Vader down. He's telling Vader to fight another day.
Jesse: Smart move. Jim: Vader is pushing Paul away, and we're going to have Vader vs. Goldberg right now, right here on Colorado!!!!
Jesse: The roof is about to blow off here tonight!!!! Jim: Paul E finally gets Vader to leave the ring area, but the damage has already been done.
<Goldberg walks over to Mikey and checks on him...> Jesse: I don't think Mikey is going to be up for the Main Event....things don't look to good for him.
Jim: Fans what a night it's been. We're just ONE week away from the PPV, and we'll see you there. Goldberg is BACK!!! Al Snow is BACK!!! Raven is BACK!!!!
Who's next!?!?!?! SOO LONG!!!!
