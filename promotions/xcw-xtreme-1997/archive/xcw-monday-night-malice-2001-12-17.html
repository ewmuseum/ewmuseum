XCW Monday Night Malice

December 17, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera fades to a sold-out crowd in Tokyo, Japan. The crowd is chanting something in Japanese simultaneously. You can see signs everywhere that say
"Misawa is God", "Game Over", and "Ching Wa Bing Caw"....whatever that means.
We pan across the crowd one more time and then zoom into Jim Ross and Jesse
Ventura. Good Ol' Jr has on his usual cowboy hat with a sweater and some jeans.
Jesse Venutra has decided he's going to dress old school tonight and has on a tye-dye shirt, and a bandana over his head. The fans go wild when they show him on the X-Treme Tron.]
Jim: Fans welcome to the very first LIVE edition of Monday Night Malice!!! I'm going to be taking you through the next two hours of some amazing wrestling along with this man right here. Jesse it's a pleasure to be standing next to you here in the Egg Dome.
Jesse: Well let me tell you something Jimbo...the electricity in this arena is something that measures up to Wrestlemania 3 or even a Rolling Stones concert.
You've got people waiting to see Triple H take on Mitsuharu Misawa for the XCW
World Title....that is going to be one hell of a match.
Jim: No doubt about it Jesse....not only do we hav..... ("If ya smell....what the Rock...is cookin'" is heard all over the arena and the crowd goes wild as The Rock steps out through the curtain. The fans bust out into a "Roo-kee, Roo-kee" chant, and he climbs into the ring and grabs the microphone.)
Fans: ROO-KEE, ROO-KEE, ROO-KEE! (The Rock puts his hand up as to shut the crowd up.) The Rock: Finally The Rock has comeback to Japan!!! (The crowd cheers.) The Rock: Ya know the Rock travels all the way here from The United States. The
Rock takes the little airplane, gets waited on by Yoko Ono....sits next to
Yokozuna, and has get a hotel room all the way in Yokohama. The Rock says he's damn tired of this place already and he can't wait to get back in the US.
(Crowd gives him mixed reviews.) The Rock: The Rock comes all the way out here only to find out that he's not on the card tonight. The Rock...
(The crowd starts to chant his name again...Roo-Kee, Roo-Kee.) The Rock: The Rock....is not on the card. Well ya want to know something? Sonny
Ono...The Rock wants you to bring your Monkey Ass down to the ring right now!
(Sonny comes down looking very worried at the fact that he's going to met the
Great One, face to face. Sonny steps into the ring and the Rock hands him a microphone.)
The Rock: Now The Rock wants to know why you invited him all the way down to
Japan, made him come in here with no shoes on, and then tell him that he's not welcome here?
Sonny: Noooo Rocky you have it all wrong. There is a reason you are here...you have a perfectly good reason. I have a good reason. You see the reason you are here is because you're going to special guest referee the match between Triple
H and Mitsuharu Misawa.
The Rock:............The Rock doesn't understand. You see when The Rock came out here he said the he didn't like this place. He doesn't like the eletrontics, he doesn't like the cards...the only thing the Rock likes is
Sushi.
(The crowd pops big for that one, because they know the double meaning behind it.) The Rock: Do you like sushi? Sonny: Actually I hate sushi. The Rock: So that must mean you like eggrolls...do you like eggrolls Sonny Ono? Sonny: MMmmmm I love them!!! (Crowd gets grossed out over it, and Sonny has no idea why.) The Rock: Enough!! What's the real reason you want The Rock to guest ref this match? And you better tell the truth, because if you're lying...The Rock is going to take his boot and stick it stright up your monkey ass!!!
(Crowd goes wild!!) Sonny: You see if you guest ref I know things will be run right down the middle. No Japanese Ref, no real American ref...just you Rocky. I know you don't like Triple H, and I know you don't like Misawa...BUT...if Misawa happens to win you're the next in line to get a title shot.
The Rock: Are you trying to bribe the Rock? Sonny: We.... The Rock: Are you trying to bribe the Great One? Sonny: Ye.... The Rock: The Rock says...Of course he'll do it. (Rocky smiles and shakes hands with Sonny Ono. Both men hug and then parade around the ring to a chorus of boo's. Even though the fans want the XCW to stay in Japan they don't want Rocky to side with Ono. Rocks music starts up and he walks away with Sonny Ono.)
Jim: DAMN YOU ROCK!!!! DAMN YOU STRIGHT TO HELL!!!! I can't believe he turned his back on these fans. I can't believe he's turned his back on his country.
You make me sick Rock!!! Jesse: Ya know there are a lot of things I can tollerate in Wrestling, but being a turn coat isn't one of them.
Jim: Fans we just witnessed a heinous act by The Rock here tonight. Sonny Ono makes me sick, the Rock makes me sick, and personally all these fans make me sick.
Jesse: Jim.... Jim: NO......for two long years we've been stuck here. We haven't be able to go home, we haven't been able to see our familes. I hate the food here, I hate the people...I wanna go home.
Jesse: Well Jim...you're not going to make it home if you keep this up. These people are listening on their TV's at home, and they can hear you so shut up.
Jim: I'm sorry, but it just gets to me. I miss America! Fans lets take you to our first match here tonight on Malice!!!
FINK: Ladies and gentlement the following contest is set for one fall. First coming down the ring from Ottawa, Canada....weighing in at 234 pounds. Here is....EDGE!!!
("Yeah my durango, #95...." yells Rob Zombie as his voice is heard all over the arena. The smoke fills the enterance way and out steps Edge who has the fans already going wild. Edge walks down to the ring with those black glasses, and blck trench coat he wear. He slides into the ring and stays in that position for a second, and then works the crowd from the turnbuckle.)
!!! FINK: AND...his opponent.... [The XCW-tron flickers on and suddenly the crowd falls into a hush as the
Hardcore Legend appears upong the big-screen. Wearing the infamous "wanted"
shirt and sitting back against a wall in a darkened room he flashes a toothless grin. He speaks in an almost whining high-pitch voice...]
!!! Cactus Jack: A lot of things in this world bring me down...things that are simply out of my control as I sit here trying to hold in the hate that the XCW wants me to release. If I seem distant Edge, it's because I am...several thousand miles distant in fact. As you stand there inside the ring I sit here in America wishing ever so badly that I could for one moment, one second of one hour be there to show you just why they call me the "Hardcore Legend"...but I can't be there right now...try as I might I can't materialize the plane or the ticket neccessary to travel all the way over to Japan today..tonight, or perhaps even within the next week. You're lucky...you know that? Because your life has been extended about that much longer...
[Cactus Jack pierces the air with a loud scream.] THAT MUCH LONGER! And when the XCW manages to scrounge up the extra change to send for me...you will have your match...you'll tell your friends you stepped into the ring "with a man who simply did not feel pain"..."a man who had no feelings whatsoever"...just like everyone else you'll say those things. But let me let you in on a little secret...
I FEEL PAIN...and tonight, regardless of my location so will you. So now...meet your new opponent and my replacement for the night...and have a
nice-...evening. [The hands go up and Jack fires off.] BANG BANG! [The screen fades to black.] (Cactus blips off the screen and Edge is standing there looking very puzzled. He's talking to Howard Finkel and the ref, but it doesn't look like Cactus is going to come out.)
Jim: That's odd....extremely odd for Cactus to pass up a challenge like this one. Jesse: I hope Edge doesn't walk away with an easy win like this. I mean this guy has such tremendous talent it'd be a shame not to see him wrestle here tonight.
Jim: Wait a second...who's running down to the ring? Jesse: That's Magnum TA.... Jim: Magnum TA is our producer slash coridinator for Malice, and he's telling everyone in the ring something. Well it seems Edge can't believe what he's hearing, and now I guess Howard's going to make the announcement....
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen....Magnum TA has just informed me that there will be a match here tonight with Edge on Malice. His opponent...making his Winter
Residence in Okahama....led down to the ring by Virgil, here is "The Million
Dollar Man"...Ted Dibiase!!!
["Money...Money....Money....Money" is heard all over the arena, and out steps
Ted DiBiase with Virgil. The crowd is in total shock as they haven't seen Ted for a few years. Ted looks to be in the best shape of his career as he walks down to the ring. Virgil has that Stone Cold look on his face with the money in his hand. Ted steps into the ring and grabs the microphone...]
Ted: That's right...The Million Dollar Man is back in the XCW, and back in wrestling. Edge, you're too young to understand what I used to do to little punks like you. I would give you a hundred dollars and tell you to take a hike, but the fact is that I want to show these people that I can still handle myself in the ring. BWHAHAHA!!!!
(Ted throws the microphone down and Virgil helps him disrobe.) Jesse: Ted DiBiase is back!!! Jim: I can't believe my eyes!!! Edge can't believe his eyes!!! Jesse: I thought he was on the Christian Channel or something? Jim: Well I guess he wanted to comeback. There's the bell and this match has begun...I can't wait to see how these two lock up against each other. Both men with a collar and elbow tie-up, jocking for position.
Jesse: DiBiase is a total vet in the ring, Edge looks green compared to him. Jim: Ted with a knee to the stomach, and a double ax-handle to the back. Another one sends Edge down to one knee. Edge tries to make it to his feet and
DiBiase nails him with a right fist to the side of the head, and another right first....
Jesse: Right on the same spot too. Jim: Ted goes for another one, but Edge blocks it. He returns with some right hands of his own...he backs DiBiase against the ropes, and now sends him to the other side. Leap Frog by Edge, DiBiase comesback and Edge takes him down with a
HUGE Armdrag!!!
Jesse: Well you know The Million Dollar Man has to have some ring rust. I mean he's been out of the game for awhile now, and Edge as green as he might be is going to be one up on him tonight.
Jim: Edge goes to the ropes, ducks a wild swing by DiBiase...and he returns with a high crossbody!!! 1!!! 2!!! No he didn't get him....Edge now up and he takes DiBiase down with a bodyslam, and he follow up with a kneedrop.
Jesse: Didn't Ted DiBiase used to be the commish around here? Jim: He most certainly did. Jesse: I wonder if he pulled a few strings with Ono to comeback? Jim: Or maybe he pulled a few with Cactus Jack. Jesse: You think that Jack and DiBiase might be in on this together? Jim: You never know with the XCW Jess...you never know. Backdrop Suplex by
Edge, and DiBiase lands right on his shoulder. Ted rolls out of the ring to take a breather....Edge is going right back after him though. Edge grabs
DiBiase and throws him into the ring post, and here comes Virgil from behind!! Jesse: Oh but Edge caught him!!! Jim: Edge is gonna punch him, DIBIASE clips the leg from behind!!! He took that knee out right from under Edge, and DiBiase throws Edge back into the ring. I'm surpised Edge didn't know that was going to happen.
Jesse: Well Edge has to be lost here...I mean do you think he really thought that his opponent was going to be "The Million Dollar Man" Ted DiBiase? I mean the odds are so insane it would probably never happen unless it was tonight!!
Jim: Ted with a nice clothesline, and that takes Edge down. DiBiase now with those trademark firstdrops of his. Another fistdrop...this is going to make three!!! Now DiBiase looks to the crowd...he shouldn't be wasting his time like that.
Jesse: He can do whatever he wants. He's the Million Dollar Man! Jim: Edge makes it to his feet, and DiBiase with a boot to the stomach. Vertical Suplex by Ted, and he rolls over to make the cover. He's got a hand full of tights!!!!
1!!! 2!!! 3!!!! Jesse: He got him!!! Jim: No he didn't. He kicked out just in time, and boy I had a feeling DiBiase was going to pull some tricks out of his hat. Ted pulls Edge up by the hair and executes a swinging neckbreaker.
Jesse: That must have gotten Edge pretty bad...look how he's holding his neck. Jim: DiBiase softening that neck for the Million Dollar Dream. If he locks that in Edge can kiss his win good-bye. Million Dollar Man now brings Edge back up and he whips him into the turnbuckle....EDGE WITH A SPEAR!!!! EDGE WITH A
SPEAR!!!! Jesse: That came out of no where!!! Jim: Edge is going for the pin!! 1!!! 2!!! Virgil is on the apron, and now Edge goes over to him. He nails Virgil with a right hand, and he turns around...HOT
SHOT by DiBiase, and now he's going for the cover. HAND FULL OF TIGHTS!!!! 1!!
2!!! 3!!! FINK: Here is your winner....Ted DiBiase!!! Jim: DiBiase has pulled off a tainted win here tonight, but a win is a win. Jesse: You're right...win if you can, lose if you must, but always cheat. Jim: Ted and Virgil leave the ring area, and I'm sure Edge isn't going to be pleased with this loss. Oh look who's just arrived....
[Triple H is shown getting out of his car with Stephine McMahon Helmsley. Both of them look very serious about tonights match, and he doesn't even bother to look at the camera. As he walks by.]
Jim: Triple H is here...we'll be right back!!!!
