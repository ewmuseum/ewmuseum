XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 3)

June 13, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: And how do you like that...Shane Douglas has snunk his way into Arn Anderson's match at the Pay Per View. Joel: Never count the man out...never. Jim: Wait until Arn Anderson hear about this. You think he's stressed out now, you think he's going to boil over now...heh, you just wait and see what happens.
Joel: Arn is coming out now to talk to Flair right? Jim: In just a few seconds we're going to see Arn Anderson and Ric Flair meet in the center of the ring.
["My Sacrafice" starts to play and Arn Anderson walks down to the ring, and the crowd erupts. He holds his arms up in the air, and climbs through the ropes. He grabs the microphone and before he can speak Ric Flair's music starts up...Flair comes down in a suit and tie, and a HUGE smile on his face. Flair gets into the ring and meets eye to eye with Arn Anderson.]
Flair: There is no way in hell that I'm going let you get the first word in. For the past week and a half, I've been thinking. Heh, thinking about you and me, because with all this crap that's going on...we have to admit that our history together is awesome.
[Crowd pops] Flair: You have to admit that not only are we friends, but we're family. You and I, Flair and Anderson, Four Horsemen, Tag Teaming it up...WHOOOO, The
Enforcer, and the WHOOOO Nature Boy.
[Crowd pops louder.] Flair: And now it's come to this. It's come to me against you, something I never wanted to see mah friend. I never wanted to see you and I fight like this, I never wanted to see you and I make each other bleed. Hell Arn...we made other people bleed, not each other.
[Flair looks like he's getting emotional.] Flair: It's damn shame that on Sunday...at Safeco Field, in front of 40 thousand plus. In front of the whole world, you're going to try to take me out.
Damnit Arn do you get what I'm sayin' to ya? [Arn takes a deep breath and starts to talk.] Arn: Yeah we were friends, we were buddies. You and I, causing all kinds of hell in the NWA, the WCW....even the WWF for a brief period. Things change Ric, things change big time. I'm the boss around here, and you need to learn how to respect that. The only way I see fit is to that is to kick your ass in the ring.
[Flair looks at him with that cocky stare, shaking his head yes.] Arn: For all these years I've sat back and watched you in the drivers seat, well it's not like that anymore. You're not going to do that to me, and you're not going to get away with crap like that with my wrestlers. You're here to wrestle, and by God I will break you down and make you realize that. You're not in charge anymore...I AM.
Flair: Well Arn...that's fine. You want to prove that you're the man, then that's fine. It's going to be an honor to dance with a man like you.
[Flair extends his hand, and Arn shakes it. The crowd goes berzerk, and Flair drops to his knees and hits a low blow. JR and Joel take over....]
Jim: OH COME ON!!! Damnit Ric Flair!!!! Joel: A going away present? Jim: And now here comes Scott Hall, and this doesn't look good. Hall and Flair start stomping away on Arn Anderson, and we need some help here. Somebody do something.
Joel: I've never seen Hall hit such perfect punches. WOW...those are nice. Jim: Flair whips Anderson into Scott, and he drops him down with a spinning clotheslines. [All of a sudden the crowd goes wild...] Jim: And somebody has just slid into the - TULLY BLANCHARD!!!!! TULLY BLANCHARD IS HERE!!!!! Joel: What the hell? [Hall charges him and Tully throws him right over the top rope to the outside. Ric Flair drops to his knees and begs, and Tully flips Flair off. Tully kicks him in the stomach, and brings him to his feet. Arn is up now and he drives a foot into the stomach, and Tully makes his way to the top turnbuckle...]
Jim: COULD THIS BE??? Joel: OH NO!!!! Jim: YES!!! Arn lifts him up....SPIKE PILEDRIVER!!!! This place is going crazy, and The Brain Busters have reunited!!!
[Arn and Tully embrace, and raise each others hands. Arn's music begins to play and Flair lies in the ring knocked out. Scott Hall is no where to be found, and we cut to a Beach Blast Update...]
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Camera cuts to the control center as we see Tori sitting behind a desk. She is looking hotter than ever, and she totally knows it.]
Tori: Hi fans, and welcome to the 3rd Beach Blast 2002 Report. I'm Tori Wilson, and I'll be giving you all the information that you need to know about the
XCW's return Pay Per View.
Sunday, June 16th Live from Safeco Field, in Seattle Washington XCW presents Beach Blast 2002 This is going to feature a 8 Man Tournament for the XCW World Title. Now we all know the Main Event will be a Hell In The Cell Match for the vacant XCW World
Title. So the winner will not only have to go through 3 matches, but they will also have to be apart of a HITC at the very end. The brackets are as followed...
Mickey Whipwreck vs. Roddy Piper Sho Funaki vs. Tatanka Steve Armstrong vs. Chris Jericho Triple H vs. Cactus Jack Other matches announced for the PPV.... Arn Anderson/??? vs. Ric Flair/Scott Hall Tommy Dreamer vs. Stevie Richards vs. Raven (US Title) RVD/Sabu vs. Al Snow/Chris Jericho in a Ladder Match (World Tag Team Titles) The Rock will also make his return to the XCW, and he's going to let us know if he's going to stick with movies...or join the XCW. Who knows what his answer is going to be...
And Arn Anderson has offered "Big Van Vader" Leon White a front row seat to see all the action. Is Vader going to accept or will he no show?
Fans this event has SOLD OUT. If you can't make it you can always catch it LIVE on Pay Per View. Call your local cable opperator for availability.
Fans...Beach Blast is going to be one for the record books. Be there or order it today...Safeco Field, Seattle Washington!
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [From the report we cut to the locker room and Jeff Hardy is knocked out in his locker room, and standing over him is Chris Chavis with an ax in his hand. He grabs Jeff by the hair, and he's about to scalp him when Magnum TA and the other officials come in and break it up...cut back to Joel and JR.]
Jim: What the hell was about to happen there? Joel: What do you think? Chris Chavis has lost his damn mind, and he was about to scalp Jeff Hardy!
Jim: They need to get that man under control before Beach Blast. He could seriously hurt someone.
Joel: Nah...and if he did scalp Jeff Hardy would it be a big loss? OH no we lost the moody Hardy brother with the weird hair. Big deal!
Jim: Well I'm sure a lot of people don't feel the same way you do! Joel: They do, but they just won't admit it. Jim: I guess we're not going to have that match here tonight... [We cut back to the dressing room, and this time we see Mikey Whipwreck and
Piper going at it. Both men taking it to each other, back and forth...Mikey throws Piper into the door, and he goes flying to the outside of the arena.]
Joel: Follow em'!!! Jim: Can we get any order here tonight? Joel: Hell no...not before a damn PPV!!! [Mikey grabs Piper and throws him into the side of a rental car. Mikey continues to attack him, and to the left of the corner we see Stevie Richards,
Tommy Dreamer and Raven fighting.]
Jim: And now Dreamer, Stevie and Raven are going at it. Joel: How did they all meet up? [Raven and Stevie toss Dreamer through the front window of the car. Stevie then
"Stevie-kicks" Raven in the jaw, and he goes down like a ton of bricks. Stevie pulls the busted open Dreamer out of the windshield, and opens the hood. He puts Tommy's head in between it, and slams it shut.]
Jim: OH HELL!!!! Joel: WOW.... [Mikey and Piper are in the picture again, and Mikey hits a back suplex on the cement. He gets up and starts to stomp away on Piper...]
Jim: And fans back in the ring they're starting the main event. Joel: WHAT??? Jim: Steve Armstrong is going to be left to face Sho Funaki and Triple H!!!
