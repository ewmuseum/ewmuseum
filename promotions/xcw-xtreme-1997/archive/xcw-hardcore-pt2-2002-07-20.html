XCW Hardcore (part 2)

July 20, 2002

[When we comeback Shane Douglas is in the ring, and Deb is sitting in a steel chair. Shane has a microphone in his hand, and a smile on his face. He begins to speak..]
Shane: It was only a matter of time before I ran that no-talent piece of shit out of my federation. Steve Austin, I told you...I told the world that I would be making your life a living hell. I'd be the man that you wake up sweating about in the middle of the night. I'm the man who you thought you killed, but yet I came back. I came back and I destroyed you. I stole your wife, and I took away your wrestling career. Steve Austin as you sit at home cussing me out, as you sit at home listening to me laugh at your pathetic ass..listen to what Deb has to say.
[Deb stands up and Shane hands her the microphone...] Deb: Steve...did I tell you that I would get revenge? I believe I did. Stev -
[WHAM!!! Shane Douglas picks up the chair and smashes it across Deb's head. She falls to the canvas in a heap. Shane stands over her and smiles...the smiles slowly turns to a laugh, and the laugh slowly turns to a face filled with anger. Shane picks up the microphone..]
Shane: Austin...heh...it strikes me funny how easy she was. All I had to do was lie to her about you, all I had to do was talk. Dumb blonde bitch....Hey Steve,
I hope you just spit out all the cheap beer in your mouth...and as you're wiping the screen, know this...I WON. Not you...Austin...Shane Douglas won.
This is his damn fed, I own this fed...and tonight I proved that Austin 3:16 is nothing more than a fable. Go to hell you son of a bitch!!!
[Shane throws down to the microphone kicks the knocked out Deb, and walks to the back to a huge amount of heel heat.]
Joey: OH MY GOD!!!! I can't believe what we just saw. Shane Douglas has taken that envelope and pushed it beyond belief. Shane Douglas has just shown us that he has no heart, he has no passion. Just hate.
Joel: How awesome was that? Steve Austin is probably going crazy at home right now. Joey: Deb is being helped away from the ring by the EMT's...the back of her head was split open, and blood is pouring out of it. Ugh...Shane Douglas deserves the beating of his life.
Joel: He did get a beating of his life, at Trust No One. Joey: But he walked away from it. Shane Douglas needs to get the beating of his life and never comeback. Austin will be back, and when he is...oh man all hell is going to break loose.
Joel: Speaking of all hell breaking loose...Chris Benoit is about to come out. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen , making his way to the ring, weighing in at 220 pounds, from Edmonton, Alberta, Canada he is "THE RABID WOLVERINE" CHRIS
BENOIT! [Benoit comes out to HUGE heel heat.] FINK: And introducing now, from Short Hills , NJ, weighing in at 225 pounds , RAVEN!!! [Raven comes down to heat.] [Both men lock up, and Benoit with an armbar. Raven sends him to the ropes, and
Benoit takes him down with a shoulderblock. Raven clutches the back of his head, and Benoit lays in with a series of boots to the side of the head.]
Joey: Chris Benoit maming Raven here. What a battle both of these men had on
Sunday. Raven came so close to winning the XCW US Title, and Chris Benoit and
Ron Simmons had a tremendous battle.
Joel: I loved that Iron Man match...oh man was that awesome. Joey: Benoit brings Raven to his feet and hits him with a few chops to the chest. Chris with a whip to the ropes, and he brings him back for a short clothesline. That almost took Raven's head off.
[Chris brings Raven back up and drives a fist into his head, and another fist into his head. He lifts Raven up and takes him down with a bodyslam. Raven quickly back to his feet, and he ducks a clothesline and counters with a drop toe hold.]
Joey: OH and Benoit goes crashing down to the mat face first. Raven now stomping away on the head of Chris Benoit. These two have to be tired after the matches they had. They have to be bruised.
Joel: I saw Benoit with an Ice pack on his shoulder before the match, and Raven has his ribs taped up.
Joey: Raven brings Benoit to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle. Chris bounces out, and Raven with a boot to the stomach, and a swinging neckbreaker.
[Raven sits up and just stares. He makes to his feet, and waits for Benoit to get up. Raven goes for a high knee, but Chros catches it and Raven lands hard onto the mat. Benoit gives him a couple of stomps, and then brings him back up.
Benoit rips Raven's shirt off, and unloads with a series of chops.] Joey: Listen to the sound of those bad boys. Chris Benoit with a boot to the stomach, and a snap suplex. He goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO, a kickout by Raven.
Joel: You know I was watching a porno the other day, and a guy looked an awful lot like Chris Benoit.
Joey: You were looking at the guy? Joel: Well you know how the - Joey: Yeah sure thing. Joel: NO!! Really...they showed the gu - Joey: Chris Benoit brings Raven up again, and he whips him into the turnbuckle. He charges in, but Raven charges out with a clothesline. [Benoit's head snaps back hard, and Raven waits for him to get up. Raven runs to the ropes and takes him down with a nice bulldog. Raven hooks the leg, but
Chris Benoit kicks out....]
Joey: It's going to take a whole lot more than that. Chris Benoit wants to prove something to the XCW, and he's been doing so ever since he's joined the ranks here in the fed.
Joel: He's taken out Cactus Jack, Terry Funk, he beat Ron Simmons and almost broke his damn neck.
Joey: No doubt that Benoit is on a roll here in the XCW. Joel: Raven is starting to get some steam here in the XCW too...the man was so close to winning that XCW US Title.
[Raven gives a couple of quick knees to the stomach, and then follows up with a
Inverted Russian Legsweep. He hooks the leg, and Benoit once again kicks out before the 2 count.]
Joey: Raven getting a little upset now. He makes it back to his feet and he starts to stomp away on the head of Benoit. Those kicks are finding the mark big time...
Joel: That's gonna make you forget your childhood. Joey: Raven no - UH OH. Here comes trouble. Bret Hart and Christian are making their way down to the ring. Raven sees them walking towards the ring, and they're climbing in.
Joel: Umm... [Bret walks over to Raven and he's talking to him. Christian is talking to the ref, and from behind Chris Benoit locks in the Crossface.]
Joey: CROSSFACE!!!!! HE'S LOCKED IT IN... Joel: The ref is still talking to Christian... Joey: Raven is tapping but the ref isn't paying attention. Wait a minute...Bret
Hart locks in the Sharpshooter. OH COME ON!!!!
Joel: I can hear Raven's bones snap! Joey: WAIT the ref turns around and he see's it. OH he's calling for the bell... Joel: UH OH!!! Joey: Benoit thinks he's won the match, and he gets up just as Bret lets go. FINK: Here is your winner as a result of a disqualifacation...RAVEN!!! Joey: And Benoit doesn't look happy at all. Joel: Raven looks like he's been plucked. [Raven slides out of the ring with his arm being raised. Chris Benoit is in the ring shaking his head and smiling. Christian and Bret are arguing with the ref, and the ref isn't moving. Benoit gets a running start and double clotheslines both men...]
Joey: WHOA!!!!! Joel: NICE!!!! Joey: Benoit now putting the boots to both men. Chris Benoit is getting even, but he's beating on his fellow Canadians.
Joel: White on white violence never solves anything. Joey: Benoit grabs Christian and throws him over the top ropes, and it's just
Bret and Benoit.
[The place starts going crazy, and Benoit asks for the microphone. The crowd gets to a hush as they wait to hear what Chris has to say.]
Chris: Listen...I don't know what your plans are, but I don't want any part of them. I don't like you, I don't like anybody. My goal is not to make friends or fans, my goal is to hurt people. To make people remember my name. So I'm giving you a chance to get out of here before I make your life a living hell.
[Bret tries to talk to him but Benoit tuns the mic off and hands it back to the announcer. He walks right past the the stunned Bret Hart, and bumps shoulders with him. Chris leaves the ring area, and we get a shot of Christian who is in the ring talking with Bret. We cut to Chad Costello's office...he's pacing back and forth with Magnum TA trying to talk to him...]
Magnum: Okay security is air tight. Nobody we don't want can get through, and that's a promise.
Chad: Are you sure? Magnum: Hell yes. Chad: Because if Hall, Nash, or Hogan show up...you're fired. I'll hire someone who can make a difference here.
Magnum: I ass - [All of a sudden Kurt Angle walks in.] Kurt: Listen, Mr. Costello...I saw your little problem with the NW - Chad: DON'T SAY IT!!! Kurt: NW - Chad: WHAT DID I JUST SAY? Kurt: Not to say NW - Chad: KURT!!!! Kurt: Uptight? Anyway listen I saw what happened at Trust No One, and let me tell you something. I think it's a shame what they did to you on your Birthday.
I once had that happen. Chad: You did? Kurt: No, but I thought it might make you feel better if I said I did. If there's anything you need from me, you want me to get rid of the 3 stooges...you want me to watch your back...just call on me. I'll be there. Yeah
I'll be there...just call my name, and I'll be there.
Chad: ???? Kurt: Let me fill your heart with joy and laughter... Chad: Kurt - Kurt: Bec - [Curt Hennig walks in with the US Title over his shoulder. He's chewing his gum, and he's got this cocky look on his face. Kurt Angle stops his lyrics and turns his attention to the US Champ.]
Kurt: What the hell are you doing here Goldy Locks? Curt: I'm here to let Costello know that he's talking to the newest member of the NWO, Kurt Angle.
Kurt: ME? Chad: WHAT? Curt: It's true...OH YEAH It's TRUE!!! Kurt: How dare you insult me like that. Mr. Costello I can ass - Chad: KURT!!! Curt & Kurt: WHAT!?! Chad: No not you the other Curt... Curt & Kurt: WHAT? Chad: HENNIG!!!! CURT HENNIG!!!!!!! YOU'RE GIVING ME A HEADACHE KURT!!!! GO AWAY!!!! Curt: Heh fine with me. [Curt Hennig walks away.] Chad: NO!!! NOT YOU!!!! DAMNIT!!! Kurt: You know, if I were you I'd suspend him. Chad: GET OUT OF HERE!!!! Kurt: ME? What did I do? Chad: OUT!!! OUT!!!! OUT!!!! [Chad chases Angle out the door with a letter opener. He gets back to his desk to sit down, and starts to massage his temples. All of a sudden there's another knock on the door...in walks Eric Bichoff.]
Eric: Heh... Chad: What the fuc - Eric: You think you have a headache now...you haven't seen anything yet. May I have a seat?
