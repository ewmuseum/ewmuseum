XCW Monday Night Malice (part 4)

January 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jesse: Now can we get some wrestling going on? I mean this is the XCW, and it's not a soap opera the last time I checked right? So lets get on with it damnit.
Jim: You're right Jesse...lets take you to our next match between Vader and
Terry Funk!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Amarillo, Texas...weighing in at 247 pounds. Here is
"The Hardcore Legend" Terry Funk!!!
("Simple Man" starts to play and the crowd goes wild. Terry comes down to ring with a little limp that he suffered at the hands of Jake Roberts this past
Friday. Terry climbs into the ring and works the crowd a little.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Boulder, CO. He weighs in at 450 pounds. Here is
Vader!!!! ("What time, it's time, it's Vader time!" is heard all over City Walk, and
Vader walks down to the ring with his mind on Paul E., he climbs into the ring and goes to work on Terry right away...)
Jim: Well it would appear as though Vader is trying to get this match over quickly. <Vader with a jab, and another jab followed by a huge swinging punch. Funk gets backed into the corner, and Vader works him with a series of punches to the face.>
Jesse: I don't even think that blocking those is going to help. Jim: Vader has the meat hooks and he's driving them into Funks head...I'll tell ya what Terry's family can't be to pleased that he climbed into the ring with someone as bad as this man.
<Vader with an elbow to the side of the face, and Funk falls to his knees. Vader drives a knee into the face, and follows up with punch after punch to the head. Funk can't seem to fight back.>
Jim: After what Jake Roberts did to Funk...and after what Funk did to help
Jake, I don't think I'll ever be able to forgive him.
Jesse: Jake is a snake, and he's never denied it. Funk was the fool to believe that he was going to be his friend and help him get over his drinking problem.
Why would you want to be friends with Terry Funk, when you can be best buddies with Jack Daniels?
Jim: Terry Funk is a great man, and anyone who is his friend knows that. <Vader whips Funk into the turnbuckle, and Funk bounces out and falls to his knees again.> Jim: Vader is just toying with Funk tonight. He knows he can put the guy away any time he wants, but he's not going to. He's going to wait around and see how much damage he can cause.
Jesse: You think he's wondering where Paul E is? Jim: I would assume so. <Vader goes to bring Funk back to his feet, and Terry hits him with a fist to the stomach. Another fist to the stomach and a rake to the eyes stops Vader in his tracks.>
Jim: Vader backs up, and Terry drives a foot into the stomach.... Jesse: He'd better hit him with something big soon... Jim: Funk with a jab of his own, and another jab...he's got the big man's knees wobbly....Funk runs to the ropes and clotheslines Vader.
Jesse: But the big man doesn't move... Jim: Funk rakes his eyes again... <Terry gets a running start and kicks Vader between the legs, and follows up with a DDT.> Jim: WHOA!!! DDT by Terry Funk, and he's holding his own against the big man!! Jesse: That was a beautiful DDT by Terry Funk. Jim: Terry now kicks away at the gut of Vader, and he's trying to keep him grounded. Jesse: Wouldn't you want to? Jim: Sure. <Funk lays on a choke hold until the count of 3 and then lets go. He does it again and once again he stops at 3.>
Jim: Funk is pulling all the stops out here tonight...and Vader seems to have a real challenge in Funk here tonight.
Jesse: Well Terry came to the XCW to prove something, and he's not about to let this chance get away. He wants to be the XCW World Champion, and I think he's going to do it someday.
<Terry whips Vader into the turnbuckle, and Vader comes out with a clothesline.> Jim: Vader with a clothesline, and Terry goes down hard. He's pointing to the top ropes...I think Vader is going for his moonsault.
Jesse: NO WAY!!! <Vader makes his way up to the turnbuckle....> Jim: He's up there, and Funk is lying motionless. (Before he can do it the XTreme-Tron shows up with Taz on it.) Taz: Hey Vada....guess what? While you're out sweatin' like a fat pig, I think
I'm going to cool off our little friend. You can pick him up here when I'm done!!
(Taz sticks Paul E's head into the toilet, and flushes it a few times. Vader's eyes get huge as he can't believe what's going on....)
Jim: Vader is angry!!! Jesse: Vader is more than angry... <Terry all of a sudden makes it to his feet and takes Vader down with a Back
Superplex....Funk goes up top himself this time and executes the Moonsault.>
Jim: Funk makes the cover...1!!! 2!!! 3!!! FINK: Here is your winner....Terry Funk!!! Jim: Terry Funk wins the match here tonight, and he's leaving before the monster gets up. Vader is regaining himself now, and he's running down the ramp....
Jesse: Taz is pushing it so hard with Vader.... Jim: You're telling me. I'm going to hate to see it when these two men meet in the ring. [DONG!] [Thunder, lightning and the howling of the rain. In that brief flash, a glimpse of a graveyard, seen from the distance, is revealed. The plots are scattered over the side of a hill, the silhouette of a tree at it's top.]
[DONG!] [Another flash of lightning lights the graveyard as the camera zooms in for a closer shot of the hilltop for a brief moment, then darkness again.]
[CRASH!!!] [And then suddenly, a bolt of lightning so bright, it's light seemed to linger longer than before. And in the light, upon the hilltop, another silhouette joined those of the tree and the gravestones. A tall shadow of a man, in a dark loose garment, and a brimmed hat. And then, darkness. Another bolt of lightning lights the sky soon after, but the figure upon the hill stands there no longer.]
[Darkness.]
