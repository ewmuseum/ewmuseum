XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 4)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We cut to Michael Cole is walking up to Brock Lesnar...] Michael Cole: Brock...Brock can I get a word with you? What is your plan to take out Goldberg...how are you going to stop him?
Brock: How am I going to stop him? The question is Michael Cole, how is Goldberg going to stop me? I am too determined and focused to be beaten tonight, It's not going to happen.
Michael Cole: Are you afraid of Steve Austin? Brock: I am not afraid of anyone here in XCW. I am bigger, stronger, and faster. Why would I be afraid? Tonight, Austin and Goldberg are going to feel what it's like to be bitched around by the next big thing, and theres not a damn thing they can do about it.
MC: Well some say if you get a Stunner..you'll go cold. Do you fear the Stunner?? Brock: I dont fear any move. I've kicked out of them all, the rock bottom, the leg drop of doom, the last ride, the chokeslam, Austin can throw whatever he wants at me, his jawbreaker isnt going to keep me down.
MC: It looks to me like you have some type of fear in your eyes. . Brock: Are you calling me a coward Michael Cole? It sounds like you're threatening me. Go ahead, spit it the fuck out.
[[Pushes him violently against the wall]] MC: I was told to say th - [Austin walks up.] Austin: Ya got a problem here Opie? [[Brock turns around and stares down Austin]] Brock: Get out of my face. Austin: Is that all ya got? WHAT? Big tough somonabitch like yerself, I though ya might have something better to say..than get outta mah face.
[[Shaking his head while looking at Austin, then laughs.]] Brock: After I wipe the canvas with Goldberg tonight, you can consider yourself in the cross hairs. I am going to break you in half, you hillbilly bastard.
Austin: Heh, hillbilly...I think ya need to get yer eyes checked son. What you see here is Grade A, 100% red neck..born and raised, and if ya don't get ou -
[Goldberg walks up]
Goldberg: Brock...get your ass down to the ring so I can win the match. Brock: You talk a lot of stuff for a guy thats about to have his ass handed to him. Austin: If ya'll don't shut the hell up, I'm gonna kick both yer asses right now. [Austin leaves...Goldberg goes the other way. This leaves Brock and Michael
Cole together again.]
MC: Uuuuhhh good luck? Heh. [Brock shoves his mammoth hand into the face of MC , piefacing the back of his head into the wall]
Brock: Goldberg...you're next! [Brock storms off and we cut back to the ring.] Jim: And there's the bell. Don: You can cut the electrcity with a knife here tonight. Jim: Both men meet in the center of the ring, and everyone is on their feet. Goldberg has a slight hight advantage, but Brock seems to be a little bigger. They're exchanging words, and boy would I like to hear what these two men have to say about each other.
Don: Maybe they're swaping cooking tips? Jim: Fans we are live, and it doesn't get any better than this. Brock pushes
Goldberg, and Goldberg pushes Brock back!!! THEY'RE NOSE TO NOSE!!!!!
[Goldberg goes for a right hand, and Brock returns with one of his own. Brock with another right hand, and another one. Goldberg gets pushed into the turnbuckle, and he bounces out...]
Jim: BROCK F5!!!! F5!!!! Don: No...Goldberg escapes. Jim: Goldberg turns around and walks into a forearm to the side of the head, and another forearm to the side of the head. Goldberg now comes back with a series of forearms.
[Goldberg hits a spin kick to the midsection, and then drops Brock down with a
DDT.] Jim: Goldberg goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! Brock gets his shoulder up, and Goldberg was close to winning the match right there.
Don: I don't these people have sat down just yet! Jim: Goldberg brings him back up and delivers a right that pushes Brock against the ropes. Goldberg whips him across the ring, and Brock comesback and gets nailed with a FRAKENSTEINER!!!!
Don: WHAT!? Jim: Beautiful move right there by Bill Goldberg, and he reaches for the leg and makes the cover. ONE!!! TWO!! Kick out by Brock Lesnar.
[Goldberg gets up and waits for Brock to make it back to his feet and when he does he takes him back down with a flying shoulderblock. Goldberg stays on his feet though and brings Brock right back up.]
Jim: Goldberg on a tear here right now. He grabs Brock and takes him down with a suplex, and he follows up with an elbowdrop.
Don: You know this is the longest match I've seen Goldberg ever have. Jim: Goldberg whips Brock into the turnbuckle and he charges in with a shoulder. Owe and that hits the ribs of Lesnar hard.
[Goldberg pushes the head of Brock which causes him to fall on the mat. Goldberg gets a running start and drops a leg across the throat of Brock.] Jim: Goldberg isn't done though, and he brings the big man back to his feet. Brock is dazed, and Goldberg witha short clothesline. Another short clothesline...he's hanging onto that arm, and he's not letting go.
Don: He's gonna try to take his head off. Jim: Goldberg goes for another short clotheline. Brock ducks it though!!! Brock ducks it and grabs Goldberg...SOVIET SUPLEX!!!!!
Don: Which is like a Rock Bottom for those of you at home. Jim: He lifted him high into the air, and slams Goldberg down hard. He went to the well one to many times, and he came up empty on the last one. Now Brock is trying to clear the cob webs, he needs to get back on track if he wants to go to the Main Event.
[Brock brings Goldberg to his feet, Goldberg goes for a few punches to the stomach but it has no effect. Brock lifts him up in a Firemans Carry...]
Jim: Running Forward Rolling Fallaway Slam!!!! Don: I didn't know Brock was so acrobatic. Jim: Nither did I. He tolls up Goldberg for the cover, and here we go...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO...Goldberg kicks out at the last second. [Brock gets back to his feet and he brings Goldberg back up. He sends Goldberg into the turnbuckle, but Goldberg bounces and ducks out of the way.]
Jim: Brock turns around and walks RIGHT INTO A SUPERKICK!!!! Don: He's not down though!!!! Jim: He's dazed but he's not down. Brock walks right into the arms of Goldberg, and he hits a SITDOWN TOMBSTONE!!!!!
Don: Goodnight. Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO I can't believe it. Goldberg can't believe it. Brock Lesnar kicked out of that amazing move, and this place can't believe it either. [Goldberg cusses to himself and then stands back to his feet. He brings Brock back up, and throws him to the outside. Goldberg quickly goes after him and throws him right into the ring steps.]
Jim: Brock goes crashing into the steps face first!!! Don: Beautiful thud. Jim: Goldberg brings Brock back to his feet and he sends him into the ring steps again...and Brock is busted open. Brock Lesnar has been busted wide open.
Don: It was only a matter of time when one of these men would bleed. [Goldberg walks over and grabs a chair by the ring announcers table. He walks back over and sets the chair over the face of the dazed Brock Lesnar. Goldberg gets a running start...]
Jim: OH!!!! Goldberg was going for a knee to the chair, but Brock moved out of the way...Goldberg's knee crashes right into the steel steps.
Don: Brock is up now... Jim: Brock lifts up Goldberg...HE DRAPES HIM ACROSS THE GUARDRAIL!!!! [Goldberg falls down to the blue mats holding his throat, and Brock walks over with the chair. He sets it up right next to Goldberg, and he grabs him by the head and smashes him into the seat of the chair.]
Jim: This is getting ugly. Don: It's about time. Jim: Brock brings Goldberg back to his feet and he folds the chair back up. Brock throws the chair back onto the cement, and he lifts Goldberg up. Gorilla
Press...OH and he drops him onto that chair.
Don: He just dropped him like dead weight. Jim: Brock isn't done yet. He's not about to be done. He rolls Goldberg back into the ring, and he follows.
[Brock brings Goldberg to his feet and he takes him right back down with a
Double Underhook DDT.]
Jim: Brock makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! Goldberg gets his shoulder up in time before the three count.
Don: I was on the edge of my seat for that one. Jim: Brock stands over Goldberg, and he locks in a Triangle Chin Lock... Don: What a move there by Brock. Jim: Goldberg is right in the center of the ring, and he's gonna have to fight hard to make it to the ropes. Goldberg's arms are crossed across his throat, and Brock is pulling up like a Camel Clutch.
Don: You can see that Goldberg is starting to fade. Jim: Look at the blood pouring out of Brock Lesnars head. ["Goldberg" chants can be heard all over the arena, and that makes Brock lock in the move even harder. The ref is checking the status of Goldberg, and it looks more and more like he's fading.]
Jim: The crowd is getting louder and louder. Don: What? Jim: Goldberg is actually trying to get out of the hold. He's trying to get onto his knees, do you know how hard that is when your hands are - nevermind.
I'm not going to go there with you. Don: HAHAHA. Jim: Goldberg is trying everything he can...Brock lets go of the hold, and drops all his weight onto the back of Bill Goldberg.
[Goldberg starts to crawl towards the ropes and Brock stalks him...] Jim: He's helping himself up using those ropes, and Brock grabs him from behind. Crossed Arm Belly to Back Suplex....he keeps the bridge!!! There's the pin!!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!!!
Don: I can't believe it. Jim: Bill Goldberg kicked out...Bill Goldberg kicks out of that great suplex, and Brock Lesnar is amazed. Brock slams his hands down on the canvas and brings him back up...the blood has not stopped for one minute.
[Brock grabs Goldberg in a chokeslam position.] Jim: CHOKESLAM...SUPLEX!?? Don: He lifted him for the chokeslam, but he suplexed him over. Jim: That should end this match. ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Once again Goldberg kicks out, and Brock Lesnar is going to the top ropes.
Don: What's he doing? A big man like that can't go up there. Jim: Brock Lesnar on the top ropes now. SHOOTING STAR PRESS!!!!!! [Flash bulbs go off all over the arena as Brock jumps off the turnbuckle. The fans are going crazy as they've just seen a man of Brocks size hit a luchador move.]
Jim: GOLDBERG MOVES!!!!! Don: HE MOVED!!!! Jim: Brock hits nothing but canvas, and Goldberg hooks the leg for the cover. ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! NO NO NO NO!!! Brock kicks out at the last split second. I'm drained. [Both men struggle back to their feet. Brock runs to the ropes, and Goldberg runs to the ropes...DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE takes both men down.]
Jim: And both men are down... Don: If it ends like this then Austin only has one match. Jim: The ref is on 6...7...8...and Goldberg is starting to stir. Don: Brock is too. Jim: The ref stops the count, he doesn't want to see this end this way either. Both men back to their feet, and Goldberg with a forearm to the back. Another forearm, and he lifts him up...PRESS SLAM!!!! PRESS SLAM TURNED INTO A
POWERSLAM!!!!! Don: I love that move!!! Jim: He goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - No Brock gets his foot on the bottom ropes. Goldberg thinks he won it though..and the ref has to tell him no.
Don: That has to be a horrible job. Jim: Bill Goldberg drops to his knees to take a breather, and Brock is given a chance to recover.
[Goldberg gets up and Brock plows him down with a huge clothesline. Brock puts his hands on his knees to catch his air, and when he pops back up...a huge amount of blood flies off his face.]
Don: LOOK OUT!!! Jim: Brock Lesnar grabs Bill Goldberg...he sends him into the turnbuckle. Brock follows in with a clothesline, and another HUGE clothesline. These two beasts are going to kill each other in this ring here tonight.
Don: And that would be a bad thing. Jim: Brock whips Goldberg into the ropes...he lifts him up. Tilt-A-Whirl...OH
AND HE DRAPES HIM ACROSS THE RING ROPES!!!! MY GOD ALMIGHTY!!!!!
[Both men go down after that move. Goldberg holding his throat, and kicking in pain. Brock just drops to take a breather. The blood still pouring out of his face, now the mat is covered in red on certain spots.]
Jim: I have never seen that move done, and I can't believe Brock had enough in him to execute that move.
Don: Well he dropped to the canvas right after. Jim: Brock uses the ropes to get back to his feet, and he grabs Bill Goldberg. These are two warriors in the ring right now. These are two men who have done everything they can to put each other away...and still nothing has happened.
[Brock whips Goldberg into the ropes, but Goldberg reverses.] Jim: Brock comes back and Goldberg with a roll through leglock, and Brock screams out in pain. Brock Lesnar is right in the center of the ring, and he's screaming.
Don: That'll twist your knee right out of it's socket. Jim: Yeah, and that's not a good thing. Brock is reaching for the ropes, but
Goldberg has it locked in. Look at the blood fall from the face of Brock
Lesnar, look at him try to reach for that rope. [Goldberg is obviously tired, and he's not putting as much pressure on as he should.] Jim: Brock finally reaches the ropes, but as Goldberg lets go Brock cradles his leg. Don: That'll ruin a calf muscle pretty damn fast. Jim: Goldberg is up now and he screams, this place is on their feet. He's waiting, he's setting Brock up....Brock helps himself up using the ropes, and he turns around.
Don: Uh oh! Jim: SPEAR!!!! SPEAR!!!! SPEAR!!!! Don: And Goldberg pops back up like he's only wrestled for 15 seconds. Jim: He's giving the sign for the Jackhammer!!! This could be all she wrote here tonight...Goldberg is going on to face Austin? We'll find out in about 5 seconds.
[Goldberg lifts him up high into the air...he keeps him there for a pretty long time, and before he drops him...Brock falls down behind Goldberg.]
Jim: Goldberg spins around...Brock lifts him up....F5!!!! F5!!!! F5!!!! Don: He hits it!!! Jim: Brock has to crawl over and make the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!!! [The bell rings, and the crowd erupts in a massive "BOOOO". Brock rolls over onto his back, blood dripping from his face and the only thing he can do is look up. All his energy has been drained from this match, and he only knows how he's going to make it to face Austin.]
Jim: What a damn match. Don: You're right about that. [Goldberg on the other hand is still recovering from the F5. When he was last concious he remembers the Jackhammer, and almost getting the win. Goldberg knows that he's the loser though, and he rolls out of the ring as FINK announces Brock as the winner.]
Jim: I can't believe what we just saw. We're never going to see something like that in the history of our sport ever again.
Don: Yes this was epic. Jim: Fans Brock Lesnar will go on tonight to face Steve Austin for the XCW
World Title. One can only imagine the shape Brock will be in when he steps into the ring tonight.
