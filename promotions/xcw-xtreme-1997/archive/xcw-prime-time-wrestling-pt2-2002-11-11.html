XCW Prime Time Wrestling (part 2)

November 11, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We come back and they have set up Hulkamania stuff all over the set. Bobby has his face right next to Gorilla's "Hulk Hogan Wrist Band". Gorilla accidently moves his hand into the microphone which hits Bobby in the head.]
Bobby: OWW!! Gorilla: What have you got your head down there for? Bobby: I'm looking at that thing! Gorilla: My Hulk Hogan...Hulk Rules Wrist Band. Everybody's got em' where's yours? Bobby: I don't have one. Gorilla: You don't have one? Bobby: I have a bruise! Gorilla: Fans right now lets take you to the debut interview on the one and only Hulk Hogan!
Bobby: Ah what? Hogan is here? [Camera cuts to Mene Gene Okerlund who is in the ring.] Mene Gene: Ladies and gentlemen....it is my great pleasure to bring out a man who is going to bring the XCW into the future. Into the big time if you will...please welcome the one, the only....HULK HOGAN!!!!!!
[The crowd goes wild as "Real American" starts up. We cut to the curtain and
Hulk Hogan walks out to a huge ovation. He's got the "Hulkamania" shirt on, with the yellow tights, red knee pads and yellow boots. The tan on the guy is brand new, and he looks bigger and badder than ever. He slaps the fans hands as he makes his way down to the ring, and he climbs in. He parades around the ring putting his hand to his ear to each side of the ring. He finally walks up to
Mene Gene...]
Mene Gene: HULK HOGAN!!!! WELCOME TO THE X...C..W!!!!! Hulk: Well ya know something Mene Gene...people all over the world have come up to me, in the airports, in the restraunts, at the sporting events and they say...Hulkster why man? Why did you leave the WWF? Why the XCW brother? I look down into their eyes, and I can see the doubt the have. I can see the fear man, but I look at them and I tell them it's because Hulkamania is like a business brother. You need to get out there and spread the 3 Demandments. Sayin' the prayers, eating the vitimins, and trainin' hard. You need to reach out to other people, and my Hulkamaniacs will follow me and they'll spread the word of
Hulkamania to all the new people...and before you know it Mene
Gene...Hulkamania will be runnin' wild all over the US of A! Mene Gene: Hulk lets talk about your first goal here in the XCW. What do you want to do? Hulk: Well Mene Gene it goes a little something like this. There's a World
Title that has my name all over it. I want that World Title, and I will do whatever it takes to get it. I hav -
["2001" starts to play and Hulk stops his interview. Ric Flair steps out from the curtain wearing a suit and tie. He looks at Hogan and gives him a huge
"WHOOOO" before making his way into the ring. Flair waves his long flowing locks, and walks right up to Hogan. He grabs the mic from Mene Gene....the crowd is on edge to see what happens next.]
Ric: Hogan!!! HULK HOGAN!!! Heh, you think for one minute that you can come out here and claim something that's not yours? You're mistaken pal. You see because there's a new guy in town, and he's not wearing the red and yellow. He's not about sayin' prayers, eatin' vitimins or any of that garbage. This man is a wheelin' dealin, baby stealin', caviar eatin, lear jet flyin'...SONOVA GUN!!
WHOOOOO!!!! [Crowd boo's like crazy. Hulk just stands there with an angry look on his face.] Ric: So why don't we do this....Hogan. I've spoken to my man JJ Dillon, and he's ok'd it. Hogan you and me...I've wanted this for a very long time. You and me Hogan inside a steel cage. For the XCW Wor -
[Hogan rips the mic away from Flair.] Hulk: Ric Flair...WHOOOO!!! You listen to me man. You want a steel cage match? You want to do it? We don't have to wait...lets do it right now!!!! [Hulk Hogan rips his shirt off and throws it down. Ric Flair starts to un-do his tie, and the fans are going wild. Hulk tells Flair to come at him, and
Flair smiles...all of a sudden Owen Hart slides into the ring and forearms
Hogan from behind. Hulk doesn't sell it and he turns around and points at Owen who gets down onto his knees and begs Hogan for forgiveness. Hogan for a brief second forgets about Flair, and he nails him with a low blow. Hulk drops to his knees and both Owen and Flair start to put the boots to him. They send him to the ropes and he ducks a double clothesline. Hogan bounces off the ropes and he clotheslines both men down to the canvas. Owen gets up and Hogan throws him over the top ropes....Flair slides out of the ring before Hogan can get his hands on him. As Flair walks away with Owen, Hulk grabs the microphone.]
Hulk: You want the match Flair? YOU GOT IT!!!! December 1st, Steel Cage!!! WHATCHA GONNA DO BROTHER!?!?!?!! ["Real American" starts to play and Hogan starts to pose for the fans. We cut back to Bobby Heenan and Gorilla Monsoon.]
Gorilla: You play with the bull, one day you're going to get the horns. Bobby: Is that why your parents never played with you? Gorilla: Well folks Hulk Hogan and Ric Flair has been set for December 1st. A
Steel cage...it's going to be a brutal match. What was Owen Hart doing out there?
Bobby: Why are you asking me? Gorilla: I thought you might know something about it, because you're friends with Ric Flair.
Bobby: You think Flair called me to tell me that Owen was going to come in? We don't talk about that kind of stuff when we're on the phone. We talk about women, and traveling...
Gorilla: Must be a pretty sort conversation on your end. Bobby: You know I'm tired of this man. Can we get someone else? Gorilla you're done... Gorilla: Fans right now lets take you to The Killer Bee's taking on Chris
Benoit and Dean Malenko!!!
Bobby: Dean who? [Camera cuts to the match...] FINK makes the introductions, the bell rings and this one is underway. Chris
Benoit and B. Brian Blair will start this one off; both competitors circle the ring, Blair charges at Benoit but gets a dropkick to the knee. Brian back to his feet but is put back down with a dropkick to the chest.
Benoit brings Blair back to his feet for an Irish whip but Brian reverses it,
Benoit ducks a clothesline attempt by Brian and hits a hangman's neckbreaker.
Benoit backs into the ropes and drops the leg across the throat of Blair. Benoit lifts Blair back to his feet and whips him across the ring into the corner and now Benoit works over the midsection of Blair with lethal stiff kicks.
Tag to Dean, Dean lifts Blair to the top, Hurricanrana/ Frankensteiner from the top sends Blair to the middle of the ring. Dean back to the top again and looks like he'll top it off with some sort of high risk maneuver, flying kneedrop, but Blair moves!
Both men are down, but now it's a race for the hot tag…Brian's first, then
Dean! Jim Brunzell meets Chris with an arm drag, Chris back to his feet but is put back down with a hip toss. Jim with a mean right hand backs Benoit into the ropes, and then clotheslines one half of the Destructive Duo over the top rope to the outside. Jim now climbing the ropes which is not unusual for him, Chris back to his feet and moves out of the way of a plancha that probably would've killed him.
Benoit now with lefts and rights to Jim but Blair comes to the rescue with a plancha of his own but this time his connects taking out himself, Chris Benoit, and his partner, Jim Brunzell. But now Dean thinks it's a game of "Whatever you can do, I can do better" and hits a suicide dive on the team of Bee's. Chris managed to get out of the way and now they have the advantage once more, as
Benoit rolls Brunzell back into the ring. Benoit now applies a vicious figure-four leg lock in the middle of the ring while Dean pounds away on Blair on the outside.
Brunzell is in serious pain and struggling for the ropes, Blair just whacked
Dean with a steel chair on the outside while the ref was distracted by the submission move in the ring. Now Blair comes into the ring and drops the knee across Chris' forehead. Then Blair hits the corkscrew legdrop and the ref finally gets him out of the ring. Brunzell drapes the arm across Benoit's chest, 1-2-no!
Dean's pissed now and attempts to get in the ring but the ref holds him back as
Blair claps his hands making it sound like a tag was made and goes to work on
Benoit with stomps to the upper body. Blair lifts Benoit up and whips him across the ring and hits a back body drop. Blair brings Benoit to his feet once more and hits a sidewalk slam, cover, 1-2-kickout!
Blair slowly walks to the corner and ascends to the top, Benoit to his feet but
Blair hits a flying lariat bringing Benoit to the ground. Blair now grabs a handful of Benoit's hair but Benoit hits a low blow on Brian. B. Brian hits the mat clutching his crotch as Chris Benoit is slowly making his way to Dean
Malenko, he's almost there… makes the tag! Dean comes in blazing, Brian back to his feet and Dean puts him back down with a Manhattan Drop.
But Brunzell grabs Dean by the hair from the apron and pulls him to the mat. Dean back to his feet and knocks Jim off the apron with a right hand but this gives Blair time to recuperate and attack Dean from behind with forearms to the back and then an Inverted piledriver. Pin attempt, 1-2-another kickout!
Blair lifts Benoit to his feet and casually throws him into the corner, then backs up and hits a huge clothesline that shook the ring. Now Blair is up on the bottom rope, and hits a piledriver form the bottom rope! Malenko's neck looks like it was broken, cover, but it's broken by Benoit.
Blair now back to his feet and whips Malenko across the ring and out of nowhere
Dean hits a Hurricanrana with his last ounce of strength and is crawling to
Benoit. Blair quick back to his feet and makes a tag to Jim Brunzell and both members of the Killer Bee's charge after Dean but to no avail, Dean makes the tag and Benoit's shooting first and asking questions later!
Lariat to Brunzell, lariat to Blair, German Release Suplex to Brunzell, DDT to
Blair, and Brunzell attempts a clothesline but Benoit wraps around and hits a reverse DDT. Blair, a little dazed, runs towards Benoit but grabs his tights and throws him over the top to the outside. Dean drops to the outside and begins to work B. Brian over with a closed fist.
Chris is now dragging Brunzell's body into the middle of the ring and signals for the Crossface. The ref is watching the fight on the outside, as Benoit locks in the hold. He's straps it on the Jim is screaming out in pain. Ref turns around and calls for the bell right away.
Winner: Chris Benoit/Dean Malenko by submission
