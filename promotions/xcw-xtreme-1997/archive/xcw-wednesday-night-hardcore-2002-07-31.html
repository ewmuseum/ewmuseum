XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore

July 31, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chad: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls...children of all ages. The XCW proudly announces, that we have a new sponsor. Put your hands together for
Mickey Mouse, and Disneyland!!!!
["When you wish upon a star" starts up and Mickey Mouse comes out to a huge face pop. The fans are on their feet going wild, and he waves at everyone as he walks by. He climbs into the ring, and fireworks go off. He shakes
Costello's hand, and we cut to the Wednesday Night Hardcore Intro. When we comeback Joey Styles and Joel Gertner are sitting at ringside. The fans behind them are going crazy, as they begin to speak...]
Joey: Welcome everyone...we're less than a week away from Annahilation, as well as the Clash Of The Titans. So many things that are going to take place tonight, and I can't believe the card we have for you.
Joel: What the hell is with Chad Costello selling out to Mickey Mouse? That's the dumbest thing I've ever heard in my life. You know what, if I see Mickey walking around backstage I'm going to take a rat trap and snap his balls off.
Joey: Whoa where did this anger come from? Joel: I hate Mickey Mouse. Joey: Wow, umm okay. Fans start off with our first match here tonight, Chris
Benoit will be taking on the newcomer Norman Smiley.
[Norman Smileys music starts to play over the PA and he walks out in his famous yellow tights.]
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 245 pounds. Here is Norman Smiley!!!! [He climbs into the ring and gets a huge face pop. He lifts his arms, and works the crowd a little. Then it cuts off and "Disposeable Heros" starts up, and the crowd just goes crazy with boos. Chris makes his way down to the ring and totally ignores the fans. He climbs onto the apron, and he drags his thumb across his neck. He climbs into the ring and gives Norman the coldest, hardest stare of his life.]
Joey: There's the bell, and it doesn't look like Norman Smiley is to keen on locking up with Chris Benoit.
Joel: I wouldn't be either. The man has taken Mich Foley out of wrestling,
Terry Funk, Ron Simmons won't wrestle anymore because of him, not only that but he's punked out the best wrestlers here in the XCW. The man bitch slapped
Dynamite Kid last week...and Dynamite didn't do a damn thing.
[Lock up by both men, and Benoit takes the advantage with a knee to the gut. He grabs Norman by the arm and takes him down with his follow through clothesline.
Norman quickly gets up, and is caught with another quick clothesline.] Joey: Dynamite Kid is in the house tonight, as well as Christian & Bret Hart. What's going to happen between Benoit and them? He's making more enemies than friends, but he doesn't seem to care.
Joel: The mans confidence is higher that a kite. If I took out Mick Foley, Funk and Simmons in less than a month...I'd be the man too.
[Benoit waits for Norman to get back up, and he plants a boot into his stomach. Norman catches the leg though and counters with a Dragon Screw. Benoit goes down clutching his knee, and Norman hits a running Senton Splash.]
Joey: ONE!!! No...Benoit kicks out at the one count. Joel: Norman is up first though, and he's waiting for Benoit. Joey: Glad to have Norman Smiley in the ranks here in the XCW. The man is overlooked, and can be a great wrestler if he has the right focus.
Joel: I hate him, and I hate his ugly tights. Joey: You can't hate the Big Wiggle. Joel: I hate the big wiggle. [Chris Benoit gets back up, and Norman hooks his head and plants him with a
HARD DDT. Norman gets up and looks at the crowd, he points to the top ropes and heads up there.]
Joey: Norman might be taken a big risk. Joel: UGH!!! Joey: BIG WIGGLE ON THE TURNBUCKLE!!!!! Joel: Yuck. [Norman then raises his arms high into the air and leaps off with a HUGE flying elbow that hits the mark. Confident that he's won, Norman covers without hooking the leg. This gets two, but Benoit puts his leg on the ropes before three.]
Joey: And Benoit like always knows where he is in the ring at all times. The man uses that ring like a weapon, and a defense at the same time.
Joel: Norman caught some pretty high air on that Elbowdrop!! Joey: He did, but he needed to hook that leg. [Benoit and Norman make it back to their feet, and Norman hits a Superkick. This takes Benoit right back down. Norman springs off the ropes with an Asai
Moonsault onto Benoit, and quickly hooks the leg this time.]
Joey: ASAI MOONSAULT!!!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Chris kicks out, and Norman has had total control of this match so far.
Joel: I'm suprised Chris Benoit hasn't kicked it up a little. Something has to be on his mind.
Joey: Maybe it's the fact that he knows he's made enimies out of guys like
Christian, and Dynamite?
Joel: Nah...I'm thinking Nancy hasn't given him any in a while. [Norman brings Chris up, and he goes for a suplex. Benoit puts on the breaks though and lifts him up in the air in a suplex position, before turning it into a Brainbuster.]
Joey: BRAINBUSTER and just like that Benoit is in charge once again. Norman doesn't know what hit him.
Joel: Norman's neck smashed into that ring like a damn acordian. Joey: Benoit brings him back up, and he fires in with a series of chops. This is the Benoit that we all know and hate. He goes for a German Su - NO...Norman with a back elbow to the side of the face. Another one one.
[Benoit lets go and staggers back, Norman charges him and Benoit dumps him over the top rope. Norman holds the rop though, and manages to use it to break his momentum, and he ends up on the apron. Chris grabs him, and brings him in with a suplex. Benoit quickly applies the CROSSFACE.]
Joey: CROSSFACE!!!! He's got it on, and Norman is tapping. Norman Smiley is tapping... Joel: Chris does it again. [Chris lets go, and goes to the outside. He grabs a chair, and slides it into the ring. Benoit places the chair right next to the turnbuckle.]
Joey: What the hell does he have in mind? Joel: Uh oh. [Chris whips Norman into that same turnbuckle, and then sets him up onto it. He positions him....]
Joey: TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER ONTO THE CHAIR!!!!!!! Joel: Oh he killed him. Joey: Norman Smiley isn't moving and here comes the Paramedics. This is sick, this is so sick. Chris Benoit needs to be stopped...the man has seriously injured almost every wrestler he's faced.
Joel: Norman Smiley is just another member of the CRIPPLER CLUB!!!! Joey: Benoit stands over him, and he's smiling. The man is a sick bastard, he enjoys putting people out of wrestling. Chris Benoit enjoys watching EMT's try to bring him back to life...
Joel: And it's a shame because Norman did put up a good fight. Joey: Benoit leaves, and he's walking away backwards. He doesn't want to miss a damn thing. What a sick, sick man. I hope som - CHRISTIAN!!!!!
[Christian comes down and nails Chris Benoit in the back of the head with a chair.] Joey: They're facing each other at the Clash!!! Joel: What's he doing? [Christian puts Benoit's leg in between the chair, and jumps up and drives his knee onto the chair.]
Joey: Benoit holding that knee!!! Christian might have done some serious damage to it... Joel: This isn't going to be good for Benoit int he submission match. Joey: No it's not. But who cares?? [Christian locks in a half crab, and Benoit is screaming out in pain. Christian finally lets go and leaves Benoit there all alone in pain.]
Joey: Just like Benoit does, Christian leaves his opponent lying there. [We cut to the parking lot, and Sho Funaki walking into the arena. His parents are right behind him, and they look a little worried.]
Joey: Stay with us!!!
