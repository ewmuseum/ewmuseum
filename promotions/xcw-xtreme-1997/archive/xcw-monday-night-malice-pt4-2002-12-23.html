XCW Monday Night Malice (part 4)

December 23, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We're back with more XCW action, and when we return we get Jim Ross and Don
Callis. It looks as if they're listening to something in their headset...]
Jim: Fans we...fans we understand that Taz is awake now, and he's aware of what's going on around him. They're also saying that he hasn't mentioned anything about the XCW World Title match tomorrow, but he has asked about his family.
Don: Ugh talk about a guy who has his prioities mixed up. Who cares about some snot nosed little brats, and some fat wife? Give me the gold man!!! Give me the gold!!!
Jim: Is there a heart in there? Seriously do you have a heart? Don: You ever see the Grinch? My heart is smaller than that, and it only beats for two things...hot chicks and cash.
Jim: Pathetic. Fans out next match should be an interesting one. Eddie Guerrero is taking on Brock Lesnar. Earlier on tonight we heard that Brock will also be in the Main Event tonight...so he better try to make quick work of Eddie.
Don: I don't like either of these men. [Brock's music starts up and he comes down to a mixed bag of reactions. He doesn't really seem to care, although as he walks by one fan...they decide to spit on him and he gets right in his face. Brock continues down to the ring, and "MAMASITA" is heard all through the STAPLES Center, and Eddie walks out...]
Jim: Eddie is one of the most popular people here in the XCW!! Don: That's because he parks everyones cars before his match. [Both men start to circle each other, and Eddie runs to the ropes and goes for a high cross body. Brock catches him in mid-air though, and he walks around the ring with him.]
Jim: Oh Brock just walking around with him like he got a new toy for Christmas. Brock throws him over his shoulders in a nasty fallaway slam!!! What a move by
Brock, and he's making the cover.
Don: This could be over quickly... Jim: One!! Two!! No..kickout by Eddie. Brock brings him back to his feet and whips him into the corner. Eddie slams hard into the turnbuckles.
[Brock backs him and now he charges in at Eddie and his a HUGE bodysplash.] Don: Anyone have a spatula? Jim: Wait a minute, Brock backs up..he nails a huge clothesline, and another huge clothesline. Brock now starts to stomp away at Eddie...
Don: Well I thought this match was going to have some kind of competition. Jim: Eddie slumps to a sitting position, and Brock kicks him right in the damn mouth. This guy is a loon. He has no remorse for anyone or anything, and he just made a damn field goal into Eddie's mouth!!!
[Brock brings him back to his feet and nails him with a forearm to the back, and another one but he won't let Eddie fall down to the canvas. He lifts Eddie high into the air in a suplex position...]
Jim: He's holding him... Don: *YAWN* Jim: He's letting all the blood rush to his head, and now Brock drops him with a HUGE delayed suplex. What power...what unstoppable power by the big man!
Don: Ya think? Jim: Brock makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout by Eddie...Brock just smiles. He brings Eddie up again and whips him into the ropes. He catches Eddie in a Gorilla Press....and he's bench pressing him.
Don: Now can Goldberg do that? Jim: Now he's holding him...he drops him onto his shoulder and turns it into a powerslam!!! WHOA!!!!!
Don: Goodnight!!! Hey wait a minute...that's a Goldberg move. Jim: You're right about that one. He's sending a message to the big man. [Brock brings him back up and whips him into the ropes, Eddie bounces off and
Brock catches him in the stomach with a HUGE boot. He lifts him up...]
Jim: Hangmans DDT comming up...he lifts him up, and...NO!!! Eddie counters with a head scissors into a cradle. ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!! Brock kicks out...
Don: Wow...can you imagine if Brock lost to Eddie tonight? Jim: Both men back - MY GOD!!!!! Brock with a lariat that almost took Eddie's damn head off.
Don: So much for the rally. Jim: Brock pushes Eddie against the ropes and goes for another clothesline, but
Eddy with a backdrop. Brock goes to the outside.
[Eddie runs to the ropes...] Jim: EDDIE WITH A TOPE!!!!! What a great move there by Eddie...I am totally looking forward to seeing him and RVD go at it at the Pay Per View tomorrow.
Don: Should be interesting. Jim: Eddie whips Brock into the guardrail, and Eddie charges. He leaps up onto the guardrail adjacent to Brock...HURACANRANA over the damn guardrail. WHAT A
MOVE!!! [Eddie brings Brock to his feet and lands a right hand across the jaw, and another right hand across the jaw. He flips Brock back over to the ring side area, and he hops back over. The crowd starts in with the "Eddie" chants.]
Jim: Eddie rolls Brock back into the ring, and he jumps onto the apron. Eddie looks to the fans, and they love him. Slingshot Senton Bomb, and he goes for the cover...
Don: WHOA!!! Jim: Brock kicks out with authority. Eddie doesn't pay much attention to it and climbs to the second ropes. Brock is staggering to his feet...Eddie plants a
Dropkick off the second ropes on Brock, and he falls down hard.
Don: He fell hard on that move right there. Jim: Eddie climbs back up onto the second turnbuckle and he plants a lightning-quick legdrop!!! He goes for another cover..ONE!! TWO!!! NO Brock kicks out before the count of three.
[Eddie brings Brock to his feet and hits a Reverse Layout DDT. Eddie gets right back up and drops a HUGE knee across the face of Brock, and he's going for the cover once again.]
Jim: There's the cover again...ONE!!! TWO!!! No...quick kickout again by Brock. Don: Uh oh...you know what they sign means? Jim: Eddie making a sign with his hands, and I think he's going for the Frog
Splash. He's headed up top, and here we go. If he hits this it's all over...FROG SPLASH!!!!!
[Eddie catches air and lands squarely onto Brock. He hooks the leg, and the ref begins the count...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Don: WHOA!!! Jim: Brock kicked out...Brock kicked out of the Frog Splash!!! Eddie can't believe it. The crowd thought it was an upset here tonight, they thought it was three.
Don: Well it wasn't it... Jim: Eddie gets up and brings Brock with him...wait. Brock with the power lifts him up in a spinebuster. He just recovered and plants him with a HUGE spinbuster that jars the hell out of Eddie.
Don: And Brock looks to be a little tired. Jim: Eddie is down, and Brock makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!!! Eddie kicks out once again.
[Brock brings Eddie up and whips him into the turnbuckle. Brock charges in, and as Eddie crashes into the turnbuckle he hits the ref. To add to the impact
Brock charges in with a huge clothesline.]
Jim: All three men are in a daze... Don: UH OH!!! Jim: RVD is down here...he slides into the ring. VAN DAMINATOR!!!!!!! WAIT!!!! Don: Eddie ducked. Jim: Eddie ducks and Brock gets hit with it. [All of a sudden everyone in the STAPLES Center rises to their feet.] Jim: GOLDBERG IS HERE!!!! GOLDBERG HAS A CHAIR!!!!! Don: Uh oh...RVD is in some serious trouble now. Jim: Goldberg climbs in... [He swings at RVD and Rob ducks. Eddie rolls out of the way and Goldberg smashes the chair into the recovering Brock Lesnar. Brock goes down like a ton of bricks and Goldberg throws the chair down hard. He can't believe what he just did.]
Jim: The ref is comming to... the ref is starting the count. Don: HAHA this is great! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! OH MY GOD!!!! [Eddie gets up and gets his hand raised, but see's RVD walking to the back. Eddie begins to give chase. This leaves Goldberg in the ring talking to the ref about RVD and the chair.]
Jim: Eddie walks away with the win here tonight, and - Brock is coming too now. I doesn't look like this is going to be a happy ending after all. Brock Lesnar too a Van Daminator, and then walked into a chair shot by Goldberg.
Don: Yeah but that wasn't on purpose. Jim: Yes, but does Brock care? He lost his match tonight. Goldberg turns around and Brock is in his face...here we go folks. Both men are face to face...here comes XCW security. Here they come, and they're not about to let these two go at it right now.
Don: Let em' fight Jim: They still have to be a team tonight. Don: Let em' fight. Jim: Goldberg pushes the face of Brock, and Brock is a MAD MAN!!! Brock has thrown two guys off of him, and he's trying everything he can to get to
Goldberg. [More XCW officials run down to the ring, and now they break things up...] Jim: Good God...Goldberg just spit on Brock!!! Fans we'll be right back!!!
