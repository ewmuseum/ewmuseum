XCW Year In Review (part 3)

December 29, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Michael: Yes, Steve Austin has been apart of a lot here in the XCW. Right now lets take you to a classic interview of his...this was right before his "Last
Man Standing" match against Rob Van Dam...
[SCENE: The image of a wall clock is shown on the screen. The hands are spinning.] [Tick. Tock. Tick Tock.] VO: "The clock's done started ta tick, Rob." [Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock.] VO: "Pretty soon... the trash talkin's done with, and it becomes go time." [Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock.] VO: "Pretty soon... yer lights are gonna be put out." [Fade to black...] [Seconds later, the image resumes upon a pair of icy blue eyes.] "WHAT?!" [The camera whips backward, allowing us vision of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin. Austin's clad in a white "RVD 4:20" tee-shirt, stonewashed blue jeans, and black boots. In his right hand is a rather odd looking dumbell, which instead of iron weight... is two beer cans connected by a plastic pole.]
SCSA: "Four..........WHAT?!" "Five... WHAT?!" "Six...WHAT?!" "Seven...WHAT?!" "Eight...WHAT?!" "Nine....WHAT?!" "Ten...WHAT?!" [Austin drops the weight.] SCSA: "Rob Van Dam... do ya think yer special? What?! Do ya think that puttin' six billion pounds on a leg press machine... makes ya cool?
What?! Do ya think showin' us how ya work out with big men in bicycle shorts scares Stone Cold? What?! What?! What?!"
[Pause.] SCSA: "It doesn't. What?! Ah said it doesn't. What?! Ah said... it doesn't. Y'see, Jean Claude... yer not special. Yer not cool. And... ya don't scare
Stone Cold. Ya sorry sumbitch, yer not the only one who's been breakin' a sweat training for this match. Hour after hour... day after day, ah've been bustin'
mah ass." "An' well... ah don't need a fancy leg press machine ta help me none, either." [Austin smirks.] SCSA: "Stone Cold Steve Austin is a gladiator. What?! A gladiator. What?! A gladiator. What?!" "Ah said, a gladiator." "Back in the days of the What-what-what-what-what-what-what coliseum... there wasn't no damn leg press machine, yet those sumbitches busted their ass, and became the most dangerous men in the world."
"How do ah know this?" "Well, mah great... WHAT?!" [Pause.] "Mah great great...WHAT?!" [Pause.] "Mah great great great...WHAT?!" [Pause.] "Mah great great great great...WHAT?!" [Pause.] "Ya get the damn point. Mah great great great great great great great great grandfather was the most feared gladiator of 'em all."
"Hell, ah'm sure ya heard of 'em. They even wrote a movie 'bout him... with that sumbitch Russell Crowe." [Austin smirks.] SCSA: "His name... was Stone-icus Cold-imus... WHAT?!" "Stone-icus Cold-imus... WHAT?!" "Stone-icus Cold-imus...WHAT?!" [Pause.] "His name was Stone-icus Cold-imus, and he was the toughest ess oh bee to ever step foot inside a coliseum."
"Everywhere he went, all the other warriors begged for him ta squeal his secret ta success. Afterall, he wasn't the biggest. WHAT?!"
"He wasn't the fastest...WHAT?!" "He wasn't in the best shape...WHAT?!" "But what he was... was a mean sumbitch with a beer belly an' some superstrength." "How'd he build that superstrength?" "Nah, he didn't go ta some fancy gym and work with some sorry ass machine. Instead, he had his own little training regimen. He developed functional strength... and he did it by going down ta Johnicus's pub..."
"Did some one handed beer glass raises..." "WHAT?!" "Two handed beer glass raises..." "WHAT?!" "Reverse beer glass raises..." "WHAT?!" "Some keg lifts." "WHAT?!" "Bar stool presses... "WHAT?!" "And when he was done... the sumbitch did his cardio by running around the city, and givin' a Stone-icus Cold-imus Stunner ta every sumbitch breathing... _and_ their horses."
[Austin chuckles.] SCSA: So y'see Rob... ya don't stand a chance. Being a tough ess oh bee's been in my blood for thousands of years and well, like Mr. Coldimus, ah've been on a Stunning spree as well."
"And while ah was trainin'... ah decided to check out the country." "Ah went on a road trip. What?! A road trip. What?! A goddamn road trip... and on this trip, I stunned every last piece of trash from New York..."
"WHAT?!" "New Jersey..." "WHAT?!" "Pennsylvania..." "WHAT?!" "Washington..." "WHAT?!" "Florida..." "WHAT?!" "Iowa..." "WHAT?!" "Oklahoma..." "WHAT?!" "New Jersey again..." "WHAT?!" "And now, ah have mah sights set on stunnin' a certain sumbitch from Battle Creek, Michigan." "Ah hope ya like straws, Rob... because that's gonna be the only way that yer gonna eat for a long, long time... And that's the bottom line..."
"WHAT?!" "Cause Stone Cold said so!" [Fade out.]
