XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 3)

Jim: Fans that is going to be an incredible event. Joel: I hope I'm still working with you. Jim: I don't. Joel: You know I love your little fat limp face. I could never leave you...you're like a big dumb dog.
Jim: You don't want to know what I think you are. Joel: Go ahead. Jim: No...fans our next match is a tag team match. This should be interesting to say the least. Kaientai will be taking on the odd pairing of Tatanka, and
Mikey Whipwreck.
Joel: Two losers. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is a tag team match set for one fall. Introducing first...the team of TAKA and SHO....KAIENTAI!!!!
[Both men run down to the ring....] FINK: And their opponents....here is the team of Mikey Whipwreck and Tatanka!!!! [Whipwreck and Tantanka run down to the ring. Both men slide in and pick a different memeber of Kaientai to go after...]
Jim: Mikey is trading punches with TAKA, and Tatanka is trading punches with
Sho. This place is exploding here tonight!!! All four men going toe-to-toe!
Joel: I don't think I've ever seen punches thrown so damn hard before. They're beating the ever loving crap out of each other.
Jim: Mikey gets the upperhand over TAKA! He hammers him hard, huge left!! Huge right!!! TAKA is getting pounded here...and Mikey winds up....and Oh my what a right hand!!! TAKA goes flying over the top rope and onto the floor.
Joel: Check out Sho and Tatanka! [Tantanka is unloading with a series of Tomahawk chops to the head, and now he brings Sho to the center of the ring. Mikey and Tatanka hook Sho up...lift him...and hit a double snap suplex. ]
Jim: Great teamwork by the two men...and Mikey runs to the ropes. Tatanka as well... Joel: What's this?? [Tatanka hits a legdrop across Sho's throat, and at the same time Mikey hits a senton splash.]
Jim: Tatanka and Mikey have never worked together, but tonight it's like they've been a tag team for years.
Joel: Well not only that, but who knew that Tatanka would not have any sort of ring rust at all.
Jim: You're right about that. Earl Hebner now telling them they have to pick a legal man, and it looks like Tatanka is going to start off....and Sho is starting by default.
[Tantaka looks at Sho who is making it up, and just unloads on him with a series of shots to the head. Tatanka grabs Sho and sends him to the ropes.
Great leaping lariat by Tatanka...] Joel: And I think Kaientai have been taken by suprise. Both men underestimated their opponents.
Jim: Tatanka brings Sho to his feet and bodyslams him back down onto the canvas. Tatanka with another legdrop, and goes for a cover. ONE!! TWO!!! Just two for now...
Joel: Where was TAKA? [Tatanka brings Sho to his feet, holds him open and tags to Mikey. Mikey with a boot to the stomach, and he whips Sho hard into the turnbuckle. Whipwreck runs in, and hits a splash!!]
Jim: What impact that had!!! Now Mikey beginning to unload on Sho with fists to the forehead. Mikey grabs Sho by the arm...
Joel: What's he going to do here? Jim: HIPTOSS OUT OF THE TURNBUCKLE!!!!!! Joel: Did you see Sho fly? The man caught air like Jordan!!! Jim: Mikey brings Sho back to his feet, and Sho with a face rake. Mikey is trying to clear the cobwebs, and OH!!!!
Joel: Did you hear that???? Jim: Mikey walks right into a superkick!!!! [Both men fall down, and Sho crawls over and makes the tag to TAKA. The crowd boo's crazy as TAKA gets into the ring. He claps his hands, and the fans give him even more heat.]
Jim: TAKA brings Mikey to his feet and takes him down with a suplex. He doesn't let go though, and now he brings Mikey back up again....Snap Suplex!!! He still hasn't let go!!
Joel: WHAT THE?!??!? Jim: TAKA hooks him up again.....BIG BRAINBUSTER!!!!! What a set of moves there by Michinoku, and he makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!!!! Close!!
[TAKA whips Mikey into the ropes, but Whipwreck reverses. Whipwreck makes the mistake of putting his head down, and TAKA HITS A ROCKER DROPPER.]
Jim: Big mistake by Mikey, and TAKA quickly capitalized on it. You can't make those mistakes when you're in the XCW.
Joel: With Whipwreck down, TAKA makes the cover once again...and he only gets a two count. [TAKA brings Mikey up and takes him to his corner. TAKA tags back out to Sho...] Jim: TAKA with a back suplex, and Sho from the outside of the apron hits a slingshot elbowdrop!!!!
Joel: Now that's teamwork right there. That will definitly change your mind about facing these two.
Jim: Sho grabs Mikey and tosses him through the middle ropes. Joel: And Sho...he's gonna show us why he's the "WHOLE F'N SHO!" Jim: LOOK OUT!!! Slingshot somersault plancha!!! Joel: Would ya look at that??? Jim: Mikey is down, and Sho risking his own body to damage Mikey's. [Sho gets to his feet and tosses Mikey over the guardrail and into the crowd. Sho backing up...] Jim: Sho gets a running start....leaps onto the guardrail...and he leaps off for a MOONSAULT!!!!!
Joel: Mikey moved out of the way!!!! Jim: Sho missed!!!! Joel: Mikey moved, and Sho just took out some of the fans here. YES!!!! I LOVE IT!!!! [Mikey is up now and grabs Sho by the arm. Whipwreck slams the arm into the guardrail, and then dumps him back onto the other side. Mikey whips Sho into the rin post, and Sho hits shoulder first!]
Joel: OH MAN!!! It looks like Mikey wants to get some revenge for what happened to him last week on Hardcore.
Jim: Well who could blame him? Sho decided he was going to pop Mikey's arm out of his socket, and he did just that. He popped the damn thing right out, and
Mikey still won the match.
Joel: Homer. Jim: Mikey throws him back into the ring...Now he twists Sho's arm, flipping him down to the canvas, and drops a leg across it! WOW...Mikey shows us some new moves here tonight.
Joel: Cross armbreaker? What's that all about? Jim: Who is teaching this kid these moves? Mikey yanks back on that arm, and
Sho can't reach the ropes. He's trying but he can't reach them...
[Mikey pulls even harder and Sho grimaces in pain.] Jim: And wait a minute...Sho is twisting himself...he's actually trying to get out of the hold by reversing it.
Joel: He's doing a damn good job. [Sho slowly flips over, and he reverses it. Mikey is hooked in now...Sho now lifts up Mikey and hits a powerbomb.]
Jim: HUGE Powerbomb by Sho...and he hits another one!!! He collapses in exhuastion now, but his arm falls onto Mikey's chest. There's the cover...One!!
Two!! NO he only gets a two count. Joel: What will ever keep Mikey down? The kid just doesn't know the meaning of the word stop. He never stopped with Vader, he never stopped with Shane
Douglas...he never even stopped with Misawa. I hate him. Jim: Both men now are slowly crawling to their corner...whoever tags out here first will have the definite advantage.
Joel: Sho is getting closer... Jim: Mikey is too... Joel: SHO MAKES THE TAG!!! Jim: Mikey is almost there...he reaches out, and TAKA pulls him back into the center of the ring. Mikey kicks him in the face with his free leg, and he's crawling back to his corner. He reaches out again....
Joel: OH here comes SHO!!! [Sho lunges and throws himself at Tatanka with a leaping forearm smash that connects. Tatanka falls off the apron...]
Jim: And Mikey has nobody to tag. Sho kicks Mikey in the stomach, and then heads back to his corner. What a damn cheap shot that is...cheap shot.
Joel: And now TAKA has total control. [TAKA brings Mikey up....hard side kick to the ribs. TAKA runs to the ropes and nails Mikey with an axe kick. TAKA brings Mikey back up again and this time takes him down with a neckbreaker.]
Jim: TAKA now just waiting for Mikey to get up....he's headed to the top ropes. Joel: What's he going to do? Jim: TAKA measures Mikey....Mikey is up, and TAKA with a missile dropkick!!! Joel: Beautiful move..right to the jaw!! Jim: There's the cover!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!!!! Joel: Wow that was close. Jim: Indeed. Joel: Was that your attempt at humor? [TAKA goes to bring Mikey up, and Mikey with a mule kick to the stomach. TAKA goes down, and Mikey starts his way over to the corner. Tatanka is there with his hand stretched out...and Whipwreck is getting closer.]
Jim: Here comes Sho...and he grabs Mikey by the foot, and Mikey blasts him with his free foot. Mikey rolls to his corner...HE MAKES THE TAG!!!!
Joel: And here come Tatanka. [Tatanka comes in and hits a left, and another left, and anothe left and drops
Sho with a right hand. TAKA comes over and Tatanka takes him down with a HUGE backdrop.]
Jim: And look at him go!!! This place is on their feet here. Joel: Here comes Sho again... Jim: Tatanka grabs him...Samoan Drop!!!! Joel: And now TAKA IS BACK!!!! [Tatanka hits a drop toe-hold, and he quickly runs to the top ropes. He waits for TAKA to get back up and hits a Flying Tomahwak Chop!!!]
Jim: WOW!!! He knocked him out with that...there's the cover! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO
Sho breaks the pin up...and now he brings Tatanka back to his feet. He swings, but it's blocked by Tatanka.
Joel: He's like Tonto right now... Jim: Tatanka with a Double Arm DDT!!!! What a move...and Sho is up and he's staggering around again. Tatanka with a boot to the stomach...and he puts him between his legs.
Joel: OH!!!! Jim: Piledriver by Tatanka, and Tatanka isn't done. He lifts Sho up and ANOTHER PILEDRIVER!!!! Joel: That's going to crush his neck. Jim: Tatanka gets up and he's looking at the crowd...he's holding his index finger up. He's going to do one more...one more Piledriver.
Joel: Don't do it. [Tatanka goes for one more, but TAKA comes from behind and hits a seated dropkick to the face. TAKA now is helping Sho back to his feet, and they send
Tatanka to the ropes....DOUBLE FLAPJACK!]
Jim: And both men are signaling to end it. TAKA gets up onto the top ropes, and
Sho brings Tantanka to his feet.
Joel: What the hell is this move? [Sho hits a Pendulim Backbreaker, and holds him there. TAKA flys off the ropes and hits a legdrop across the throat.]
Joel: EVIL IS AS EVIL DOES!!!!!!! THEY HIT IT!!!! Jim: And TAKA makes the cover...ONE!!! Joel: Mikey's in...and he knocks down Sho! Jim: TWO!!!! THR - Mikey breaks it up at the very last possible second!!! Mikey brings TAKA to his feet..WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!! WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!!
Joel: And TAKA IS DOWN!!!! Jim: Mikey and Sho have to go back to their corners, and the ref lays on the count. 1.... 2.... 3.... 4.... 5.... 6.... Jim: And both men make it back up! [Both men start in with the punches. Right by TAKA, Left by Tatanka, Right by
TAKA, and Tatanka with a left hand. TAKA swings wildly and misses, and Tatanka hits the WARPATH.]
Jim: Leaping Samoan Drop!!!! Joel: WOW!!!! Jim: Tatanka goes for the cover now...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Joel: Tatanka wins the match for his team! Jim: What a - wait no! The ref is saying that TAKA's foot was on the ropes and
I can't believe that this match has turned into a classic.
Joel: This whole night has been a damn classic, and we're not even done yet. [Tatanka brings TAKA up, but TAKA hits a small package.] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Reversal by Tatanka.... Joel: ONE!!! TWO!!!! Jim: ANOTHER REVERSAL!!!! ONE!!! TWO!!!! NO!!!! [Both men up now and TAKA kicks Tatanka between the legs, and now they're crawling to their corners. TAKA tags out to Sho, and Tatanka tags out to
Mikey.] Jim: Sho runs at Mikey...and Mikey catches him in a Fujiwara armbar!!!! Joel: A what???? Jim: Where is he learning this moves? Mikey attacks the same shoulder from earlier...this is coming into full circle now. This could be all she wrote...Sho is screaming in pain.
Joel: He's doing anything he can to try to get out of this, but it's not working. He's not going anywhere, and Mikey has that hold locked on like it's his life. I can't see Sho not giving up right here.
Jim: His arm is totally vertical... And Sho has stopped moving. Here comes the ref to check on the arm. He lifts it up, and it drops once...he lifts it up again, and it drops a second time. This could be it...he raises his arm once more.
Joel:.......... Jim:.......... Joel:.......... Jim: IT STAYS UP!!!! GOOD GOD!!! HOW DID HE DO IT? Joel: Sho is starting to move again, and Mikey can't believe it. Sho is almost to the ropes, and he grabs them!!! He grabbed the ropes, and now Mikey waits until the count of four to let go.
[Mikey gets up to his feet and shakes his head in disbelief. He looks over at
Tatanka who is still trying to catch his breath on the apron, and then he looks over at TAKA and he's doing the same. Mikey brings Sho to his feet...]
Jim: Mikey grabs Sho...Single Arm DDT!!!! NO!!! Sho held onto that top rope and
Mikey falls down to the canvas hard.
Joel: I can't believe this match. [Mikey gets back up and Sho takes him back down with a Reverse DDT. Sho crawls back to his feet using the ropes, and slowly climbs up onto the top turnbuckle.
Sho gives the sign for the "ShoStopper" which is his Senton Splash.] Jim: He's gonna hit the ShoStopper....WAIT!!! Tatanka charges and Sho gets crotched onto the turnbuckle.
Joel: Right to the dumplings. Jim: Mikey now trying to capitilize...as he climbs up to the top of the turnbuckle. He locks Sho up...GUTWRENCH SUPERPLEX!!!!!!!! Mikey has shown me a whole new side to him.
Joel: He's an actual wrestler now...still a coward, but a wrestler none the less. Jim: Well he can do these moves to guys like Sho and TAKA, but put him in the ring with Vader or Goldberg and that's when he loses everything. Mikey gets up...and the ref is talking to Tatanka.
Joel: What the hell for? [Tatanka and Earl Hebner are having words about what looks to be the turnbuckle. TAKA slides into the ring from under the ropes, and hits Mikey right in the back with a chair. ]
Jim: SICK!!! THAT WAS SICK!!!! Joel: TAKA grabs Sho and throws him onto Mikey, and the ref is in position. This is going to be it. Jim: ONE!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!!! (The crowd gasps) Joel: Un-freakin-believable. Jim: Sho must be thinking the same thing because he can't believe his eyes either. This has been the best tag team match in the history of the XCW, and who would've thought that it'd be between these four men?
Joel: Who would've thought Tatanka would be involved in a match like this EVER. Jim: TAKA has left an opening for Sho to take the upperhand again. [Sho brings Mikey to his feet, and Mikey with a fist to the stomach. Another one and Sho is stunned...Mikey goes for a Whippersnapper again, and Sho floats over. Sho throws Mikey into the turnbuckle chest first. Mikey bounces out, and
Sho hits a back brain kick...]
Jim: Sho gets up and tags to TAKA....TAKA goes up top. Joel: This could be it.... [Sho sets up in a slingshot type position...as he rears back, TAKA flys off the ropes with a Flying Kneedrop. The moves hits Mikey right on the chest, and TAKA makes the cover...]
Jim: There's the cover...where the hell is Tatanka??? He's arguing with a fan, and there's the ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!! This match is over!!!!
Joel: What a match it was!!! This was incredible. Jim: This has to rank right up there for match of the year. All four men gave it their all and it's just a damn shame that Tatanka was on the outside of the ring.
Joel: Sho and TAKA embrace as they leave with a hard earned win here tonight. Jim: Mikey is still in the ring recovering from that move, and that was incredible. I can't wait until the tag team scene picks up here in the XCW, because this was amazing.
Joel: It looks like Mikey doesn't even know what hit him. The guy almost came back, and Sho was just quicker than him.
Jim: I'm sure that Mikey isn't done with those two just yet. Joel: Tatanka is in the ring now, and he's got some explaining to do. [Tatanka is helping Mikey back up, and the crowd stands to their feet to cheer. Mikey quickly pulls away from Tatanka, and then pushes him.] Jim: Uh oh...what are we getting here? Joel: Well I wouldn't be happy either. The man was on the outside of the ring talking to some stupid fan. You don't do that when you're in the match of the year. No way. Why the hell would you do something so stupid?
Jim: And Mikey is leaving Tatanka speechless in the ring. Fans we'll be right back!!!
