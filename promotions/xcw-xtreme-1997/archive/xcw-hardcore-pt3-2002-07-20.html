XCW Hardcore (part 3)

July 20, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We comeback and Eric is walking out, and Chad has a worried look on his face.] Eric: I'm glad we could do business. Chad: Uhh yeah thanks. I'll let the ring announcer know. [We cut back to Joey Styles and Joel Gertner.] Joey: What the hell was that all about? Joel: Well Bichoff must have something on Costello, because he looked pretty damn worried. Joey: You mean he's black mailing him? Joel: Yup. Fans Brock Lesnar is on his way to the ring, but who is he facing? The Armstrongs have left the XCW...and so has Kenta Kobashi. So Brock is going to win this match by default.
[Brock stands in the ring with that look on his face, and just keeps breathing intensely.] FINK: Ladies and gentlemen I have been told that the match will take place tonight. This match is set for one fall, and is a Handi-Cap match. First in the ring at this time weighing in at 340 pounds. Here is Brock Lesnar!!!!
[Crowd boos him like mad. He looks suprised.] FINK: And his opponents..led down to the ring by Eric Bichoff. Here is the team of Kayon, Dean Malenko, and Eddie Gurrero!!!
[All four men walk down to the ring, and the crowd boo's even more. Eric just smiles as he walks down to the ring. All three men step in and charge Brock.]
Joey: All three men charge him with forearms, and Brock is being covered. Dean now trying to stomp away on the leg...
Joel: Look out! Joey: Brock takes a wild swing and he knocks Eddie and Kayon down. Dean gets right in Brock's face...talk about David and Goliath. Dean with a dropkick to the knee, and Eddie from behind with a dropkick to the same knee.
[Kanyon clotheslines Brock, but he doesn't go down. All three men dropkick the knee of Brock, and he finally goes down. He gets right back up though, and hits himself in the chest.]
Joey: Kanyon goes to lock up, and Brock throws him into the turnbuckle. Eddie jumps on Brock's back...and Brock flips him over.
Joel: Dean is like the little bug. He's just not going away. [He dropkicks that same knee of Brock's, and Brock drops down to the knee again. Dean goes for a right hand, but Brock catches him by the throat. Lesnar gets up and chokeslams Dean OVER THE TOP ROPE.]
Joey: OH MY GOD!!! Joel: Ditto! Joey: Brock slowly turns around, and Eddie takes off to the outside. Kanyon doesn't know the big man is behind him, and Brock turns him around.
[Brock with a boot to the stomach, and a forearm to the back. He grabs Kanyon by the hair and whips him back down to the canvas. Brock stands over Kanyon and brings him back up by lifting him by the arm.]
Joey: Eddie and Dean are on the outside talking about how they're going to get rid of Brock. Both of these men are great wrestlers, but are they going to be able to stop this big man?
Joel: And Uncle Eric is coming over, and all three men are having a conversation. Eric is talking about the hair dye he uses.
Joey: I'm sure he is. [Brock with a high knee, and he excutes a Suplex...but leaves Kanyon out to dry on the top rope. Eddie and Dean slide into the ring, and Brock turns around and screams. Eddie runs out of the ring again, and Dean stands there in shock.]
Joey: Brock walks over and lifts...OH MY GOD!!! He grabs Dean under the armpit with one arm and throws him halfway across the ring.
Joel: Eddie's got a chair. Joey: Brock turns around, and that chair barely misses him. Eddie slides into the ring, and he dropkicks Brock. Another dropkick, but it doesn't work. Brock with a boot to the stomach...Kanyon grabs the chair, and he smashes it over the back of Brock!!!!
Joel: The ref is dealing with Dean...he didn't see it. Joey: Brock didn't feel it...so it wasn't a big deal. Brock turns around, and he takes another chair shot to the head. Brock bounces off the ropes, and
Kanyon with ANOTHER chair shot to the head.
[Kanyon holds the chair in front of Brock's face, and Eddie dropkicks it. Brock goes flat on his back. Kanyon and Eddie start kicking away on him, and Dean comes over and helps.]
Joey: All three men now laying the boots on hard. They're not giving Brock a moment to even breathe.
Joel: Would you? Joey: All three men bring him to his feet, Eddie with a chop, and then Dean with a chop...Kanyon stops and smiles at Brock. He goes for a chop...NO!! Brock grabs Kanyon by the arm, and he's adding pressure.
Joel: Ouch!! [Kanyon is on his knees screaming in pain. Brock lets go and goes after Eddie, and Dean is right behind him to take out his knee. Dean stomps away on Brock, and Eddie comes up and starts to help.]
Joey: Dean grabs Brock big legs, and he goes for the Texas Clover Leaf, but
Brock uses his strength and kicks him right out of the ring.
Joel: Brock makes it back up, and Eddie slaps him across the face. [Everyone in the whole place goes "Oooooooo".] Joel: Holy shit. Joey: OH MY GOD!!! [You can see Brock's face getting redder and redder. Eddie drops to his knees and starts to cry, and Brock screams. He grabs Eddie by the mullet and tosses him into the turnbuckle. Brock unloads with right hand after right hand. He whips him into Kanyon, and Eddie hits him hard.]
Joey: Eddie spins around...F5!!! F5!!! F5!!!! Joel: He's going for the cover....ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! Eric Bichoff came into the ring and kicked Brock in the head.
Joey: Brock is up...what in the hell was Eric thinking? [Brock is about to grab him when from out of no where comes JIM ROSS, and he spears Eric.] Joey: SPEAR!!! SPEAR!!! SPEAR!!!! JR spears Eric Bichoff, and he's unloading with a series of lefts and rights. Jim Ross is taking it to Uncle Eric...
Joel: OH!!! [Kanyon from the top rope plants the chair into the side of Brock's head. Brock falls like a tree being chopped down. JR turns around and gets planted with the same chair.]
Joey: All three men now working over JR and Brock.... Joel: Can you imagine the anger that Brock is going to have when he gets up from this? Joey: I don't want to be around. Joel: Me nither. I'm calling in sick next week. Joey: The Triple Threat has left JR and Brock lying in the ring, and they all leave hurt but with smiles on their faces. This match has been ruled a No
Contest.
[We cut to Mikey Whipwreck trying to have a conversation with the Rock. Even though the Rock can't speak.]
Mikey: So...umm..you fought pretty hard at Trust No One. To bad you didn't win. [Rock gives him the People's Eyebrow.] Mickey: Cool. [Rock gives him a mean face.] Mikey: Cute. [Rock takes a deep breath.] Mikey: Are you sighing because of me? Don't worry buddy, I'm going to keep this
XCW TV Title around my waist if it's the last thing I do.
[Rock points to the door.] Mikey: You're right...I need to get out there for my match. [Mikey pats Rock on the shoulder, and leaves for his match.]
