XCW Beach Blast '02 (part 3)

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is part of the 1st round of the XCW World Title Tournament. Introducing first, hailing from Calgary,
Alberta, Canada. He weighs in at 247 pounds. Here is Chris Jericho!!! [The countdown begins, and fireworks blaze when we hit "0". Chris Jericho is standing in the begining of the walk way with his arms extended. He spins around, and points his thumb to his chest. He walks down to the ring jawing with all the fans, he walks up to Vin Diesel and rips his "XCW" hat right off, and Vin just laughs at him. Chris uses the steps and climbs into the ring...]
FINK: And his opponent, here is Steve Armstrong!! [Steve comes out to no music at all, and a pair of old tights from the days of the "NWA", and whire boots. The crowd gives him a mixed reaction, and he doesn't seem to care at all. Steve rolls into the ring, and stretches using the rope.]
Jim: Not a lot of fanfare for Steve Armstrong. Jesse: Well he doesn't need it. [Both men with a collar and elbow tie-up, and Steve uses his strength to back
Jericho against ropes. Armstrong whips Jericho into the ropes, and Chris ducks a clothesline. He comes back with a spinning back heel kick!]
Jim: And that takes him down like a ton of bricks!!! Jesse: Well I'm not a huge fan of Steve Armstrong, so I'm going to be a little biased in this match.
Jim: Jericho brings him back up and hits a snap suplex. He goes for the cover, ONE!! TWO!! KICKOUT!!! [Both men make it back up and Jericho hits him in the face with a hard right, and unloads with a series of chops to the chest. Every time he connects a little harder, and Armstrong backs into the corner. Jericho with a series of boots to the midsection, and Armstrong falls down against the turnbuckle.]
Jim: Chris Jericho with a running start, and OH MY!! He just dropkicked Armstrong right in the face, and I don't think anyone here was expecting that move. Jesse: Well youth and wit is on Jericho's side. Steve Armstrong is a washed up wrestler who never used his potiental. He talked about the so-called "Curse", but the bottom line is that there was no curse. He is just a bad wrestler.
[Jericho grabs Steve by the legs and drags him to the center of the ring. Chris climbs to the top turnbuckle and goes for a moonsault, but Steve rolls out of the way.]
Jesse: WHOA and talk about a big mistake, Ross! Jim: Jericho went up for that moonsault, and Steve quickly rolled out of the way. Chris is holding those ribs in pain, and here comes Steve for the attack.
Steve drops a couple of hard elbows across the back of Chris' neck. [Steve brings Jericho up and whips him into the turnbuckle. Steve charges in with a shoulder right into those injured ribs. He backs up and does it again, and again, and again!]
Jim: And Steve is relentless! The man saw his opening and he's going to take it. Jesse: Chris Jericho must have broken his ribs. The man is writhing in pain in the corner. Jim: Armstrong lifts up Chris Jericho and he hits a gutbuster!!! Jesse: That can't be good for Jericho. [Steve whips him into the ropes, and Jericho ducks another clothesline. Armstrong sets up for a backdrop, and Jericho puts on the breaks and kicks him in the face. Jericho follows up with a piledriver...]
Jim: But he can't get him up. The ribs are too much, and Armstrong with a backdrop!!!! Jesse: There was no way Jericho could've lifted him up like that. Jim: Jericho is getting back to his feet, and Armstrong from behind with a
German Suplex...and he bridges as well!
Jesse: Oh and Tim White was out of position! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! TH - NO! He could've had him though...Tim White was out of position, and that might have cost Steve a lot.
[Steve gets up and waits for Chris...Steve locks in a bearhug from behind.] Jim: Whoa and he's got that locked in. He's squeezing with everything he has, and Jericho's ribs aren't going to be able to take that.
Jesse: Look at Jericho...the man is in such pain, and he's doing everything he can to make it to the ropes.
Jim: Steve has him smack dab in the center of the ring, and he can't reach it. Steve is pulling him towards him...and Jericho quickly backs up, and the momentum takes both men to the outside of the ring!!!!
Jesse: Well that's one way to break the hold. Steve made a mistake being so close to the ropes, and therefore he has to pay.
Jim: You just don't like him do ya? Jesse: Not at all. [Both men getting up now and Steve is using the apron to make it back to his feet. Chris Jericho comes over and levels Steve with a shot to the side of the head, and another shot.]
Jim: And Tim White is telling Jericho to bring it into the ring, and whoa...Jericho has some choice words for the official.
Jesse: You never want to piss off an official. The man can make you or break you, and trust me...cussing them out isn't going to help your cause one bit.
Jim: Chris rolls Steve back into the ring, and now he's climbing to the top. This could be a huge mistake on Jericho's part. If he missed a move from that high his ribs could be in serious trouble.
[Jericho extends his arms, and executes a Swan Dive Headbutt. After he connects he squeezes his ribs in pain. He gets enough strength to make the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Steve kicked out at the last second. Almost!!! Jesse: Yeah but almost only counts with horse shoes and hand grenades. Jim: Jericho seems to be having a rough time trying to get up after that headbutt, and Armstrong is up to his feet.
[Armstrong brings Jericho to his feet and drives a knee into the ribs of Jericho. Another knee to the ribs, and he locks in an Abdominal Stretch. Chris
Jericho is screaming out in pain, and Steve locks it in even harder.]
Jim: And Steve reaches over to grab those ropes. Jesse: Win if ya can, lose if you must, but ALWAYS cheat. Jim: A motto of yours that you lived by your whole career. Jesse: Hey I was a politician wasn't I? Jim: Jericho is trying everything he can, and Armstrong won't let go of the hold. Tim White see's Steve's hand on the rope and he kicks it.
[This makes Steve break the hold and get in White's face. Tim White isn't backing down, and is actually pointing his finger right in his face.]
Jim: He's not paying attention to Jericho who is right behind him. He spins
Armstrong around and a boot to the stomach, and GUTWRENCH POWERBOMB!!!!!
Jesse: How'd he get the power to do that? Jim: Cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THR- KICKOUT!!!! Jesse: And Jericho is shocked!!! [Chris yells "Damnit" loud enough for the whole place to hear, and he brings
Steve to his feet. He whips Steve into the ropes, and hits his Flying Forearm.]
Jim: This could be the begining of the end. Jesse: He runs to the ropes...LIONSAULT!!! Jim: And Armstrong moves!!! Jericho's knees hit hard, and Steve is up. Steve grabs his legs and locks in a Figure-Four. He's got it locked in, and Jericho is screaming now.
Jesse: Jericho is in some serious trouble. The man can barely sit up because of the ribs. How is he going to break out of this?
Jim: Jericho lays back on the mat in pain...ONE!!! TWO!!! Shoulder up by Jericho, and he's not going to lose this match like that. This is for the XCW
World Title, this is a chance of a lifetime.
Jesse: And only one man will go on. Jim: Jericho is trying to roll it over now, and he's using all he can. Jesse: Armstrong is fighting, he's fighting it Ross. Jim: Almost there....and he did it!!! Steve now screaming out in pain!!! Jesse: What is Jericho doing? Jim: He let go...Chris Jericho let go of the hold, and what's he doing? OH I KNOW!! LIONTAMER!!!! LIONTAMER!!!! [Jericho leans back as far as he can, and Armstong reaches for the ropes...] Jim: This could be it. Jesse: It is going to be it! [Armstrong with one last burst of energy grabs the ropes, and the crowd cheers. Chris Jericho can't believe it, and he keeps the hold on until the count of four. Chris grabs Steve by the feet again and drags him to the center of the ring.]
Jim: And Chris locks on the Liontamer again. Jesse: He's a smart man. A very smart man! Jim: Steve is pushing up, and with all his strength kicks Jericho down to the mat!! [Jericho gets right back up and brings Steve to his feet. Chris goes for a
Belly-to-belly suplex, and Steve breaks it with a headbutt. Steve goes for a clothesline, and misses....Jericho goes for a clothesline and misses. Steve turns him around kicks him in the stomach, and hits a Torando DDT!!!]
Jim: WHOA!!!! Steve crawling over for the over...ONE!!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Jesse: He won it. Steve Armstrong won the match!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Steve Armstrong!!! Jim: So we have Mikey Whipwreck, Sho Funkaki, and Steve Armstrong headed to the next round. Who knows what's going to happen during the Triple H, Cactus Jack match here tonight.
Jesse: This could possibly be match of the night. Jim: Well these two have met before under different circumstances. Tonight they'll go heads up to see who will be facing Steve Armstrong in the second round.
Jesse: Steve has a history with both Cactus and Triple H, so I'm sure he's going to excited to get his hands on either one.
[Camera is zooming around from seat to seat to show the celebs at ringside. We see Shaq and Kobe having a conversation. Shaq see's the camera and points it out to his little girl. Kobe ignores the camera...cut to Brad Pitt who is looking like a caveman, and he just smiles and waves. All the ladies are going wild when he face pops up on the screen.]
Jim: As you can see fans...all the celebs are here tonight. The two superstars of the Los Angeles Lakers are here, Brad Pitt is here...
Jesse: Hey...who's that on camera two? [Camera cuts and it's a blonde haired man with a pair of sun glasses on, the crowd doesn't know who it is until he yells out..."Ooooooooo", and everyone goes crazy!]
Jim: WOW STING IS HERE TONIGHT!!!! Jesse: Steve Borden, not the singer. Jim: I thought Sting was long gone, and tonight he's back here with his family. It's good to see someone like Sting here tonight. The XCW is accepted by everyone in the world. Lets get down to Howard Finkel for the next match!!!
