XCW Friday Night X-Treme (part 4)

December 21, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lawler: Wow, look at DiBiase... Best shape of his life and he's back to re-establish his legacy. I can't wait! Styles: Nevertheless, I've seen Taz work and he is an ANIMAL. I'm expecting quite a physical match here tonight.
Lawler: Oooh! DiBiase nails Taz with a running clothesline! Taz was paying attention to some fan, which isn't what you want to do when you face a veteran like DiBiase, ha!
Styles: DiBiase now taking advantage, laying the boots right into Taz. He's not allowing him to get up whatsoever, and that might be Ted's gameplan. A cowardly one, but smart.
Lawler: Coward? DiBiase didn't become a legend under the title of a coward! DiBiase gets Taz up, rams him head first into the turnbuckle. He spins him around, now throwing right hands right at Taz' face.
Styles: The Million Dollar Man, wasting no time on laying Taz out. He whips
Taz into the opposite corner... Picture perfect dropkick! Wait a minute, pinfall attempt! 1, Tw--, No, not even a two count! A dropkick isn't gonna finish Taz, I'm sorry, DiBiase or not.
Lawler: DiBiase though is staying on top. He slides Taz to his feet and whips him into the ropes again... Kick to the gut, and a swinging neckbreaker to boot! Now Ted's back to his feet and he's showing the crowd just how great he is, ha!
Styles: Wait a minute, that was just the opening Taz needed! Turn around,
Ted! You've got the angriest and meanest son of a bitch burning a hole through your back.
Lawler: Million Dollar Man turns around... NO! Taz locks on a Tazplex, throws him over and Ted is lifeless! My gosh, he landed right there on his neck!
Styles: Taz is pissed! He lays away into DiBiase, throwing boots like crazy. DiBiase crawls to the corner but there's no refuge, Taz starts choking him with his right foot and he's cussing at the legend!
Lawler: No respect. I hope the Million Dollar Man gets up and kicks his butt! Styles: Stop being bias, Lawler, we're trying to call a match. Lawler: I don't care, Ted's a great athlete and Taz is nothing but a street punk. Styles: Still recovering from his Tazmissions, I see? Lawler: HEY!! Styles: Taz gets Ted back up, whips him into the corner... He comes back, Taz grabs him again, another whip to the turnbuckle... He comes back, this time Taz boots him in the gut... Looks like a piledriver! No, a snap powerbomb! What power shown by The Human Suplex Machine! Taz now, a-GAIN, laying boots into
Ted's chest. It looks like DiBiase is day dreaming, he's gotta be out of it!
Lawler: You would be too if you had a pissed off walking mound of concrete stomping all over you!
Styles: Taz helps The Million Dollar man back to his feet, hooks him... Dragon
Suplex! That's it! That's it! This match is done!
Lawler: Wait a minute, no it isn't, look at the entrance! Styles: What the... No! No! It's Big Van Vader! He's got security holding him back but Vader's a killing machine. Somebody's got to do something. He's fighting this gang of security, thank God they're able to restrain him.
[Virgil hops up on the apron and starts talking to the ref, holding money] Styles: GET HIM OUTTA HERE! Get Virgil off the apron! Lawler: What? He's trying to tell the ref that he.. Uh, enjoys his officiating. Styles: Look at Vader! He broke his way through that barrier and here he comes! Wait, he stopped.. What's he doing? He's... No, he's not... He's stripping the mats away, he's revealing some concrete, what the hell is going on?
Lawler: Looks like fun to me, ha! Styles: Vader in the ring... He spears Taz! The man-monster just broke Taz in half! This is disgusting, get 'em out of here! He's got a lifeless Taz now, between his legs... No... No.. He's not gonna... He won't...
Lawler: HE IS! Styles: NO!! Vader just powerbombed Taz out of the ring and onto that concrete!!!! OH MY GAWWWWWD!!
Lawler: Taz is done for! That's it! Styles: Look at the Million Dollar Man, just laughing at this sick sight of violence. Vader hops outside the ring and DiBiase is outside the ring. He lifts Taz to his feet and rolls him in the ring. Virgil is still talking to the ref but the referee breaks free of the hold. DiBiase grabs Taz... Million
Dollar Dream, come on, give me a break!
Lawler: This is wonderful! Styles: The ref checks his hand... Lifts it once, it hits the mat... Come on!... Again, Taz's hand hits the canvas... For the love of God, not like
thi--His hand hits the mat again, it's over! Lawler: Yes! Yes! Haha! Styles: I can't believe DiBiase. I can't believe Vader. Something's gotta be done about this.
Lawler: I think it's great! Vader's walking out, he's got no emotion! Another one for the good guys! Haha! STACY: Here is your winner....Ted DiBiase!! (Camera cuts to Sonny Ono's office and he's smiling because he knows it's time for the Main Event. Ono cracks his knuckles and puts his tiny Japanese Flag on his desk and gets ready to watch this match. All of a sudden...who should walk in but RIC FLAIR!!!!)
Sonny: Oh...oh...Mr. Flair? Mr. Flair what are you doing here tonight? Ric: Sit down shorty...this is only going to take a minute or two. First of all
I'm just visting a friend of mine, and he's waiting outside. Can I bring him in too?
Sonny: Sure Mr. Flair...anything for you! Ric: Alright...hey Tony...come on it. (All of a sudden ANTONIO INOKI walks in and Ono almost falls over. He can't believe that Ric Flair and Inoki are side by side in his office. He actually doesn't see them, but he see's dollar signs.)
Sonny: OH OH!! Mr. Inoki...hello sir. Antonio: Hye. Ric: Now both of us are here for one reason and one reason only. You know as well as I do that the XCW doesn't belong in Japan. It belongs in the United
States the land of the free and the home of the brave. You have wrestling here in Japan right now, and you don't need us anymore. So I'm offering you this one thing...
Sonny: NO!!! ABSOLUTELY NOT!!!! Ric: Now Sonny...don't make me pull out the big weapons beacause "The Nature
Boy" will do it. Now Sonny...the XCW belongs in the US....you know it, I know it, and the whole world knows it and by God...it's going to be there by next week.
Sonny: NO!!! Antonio: Sonny...you listen to me right now. I am allowed to make whatever stipulation I want to any match here. I own this country, and I promised my friend here that they can have ONE more chance. ONE MORE this Monday at
Christmas Chaos. Now if you do not accept you are nothing more than a coward...and a loser.
Ric: WHOOOOO! Sonny: ARGH!!! NO! Antonio: I say yes, and what I say is final. Do you understand me? Sonny: Ahhh....yes sir. Ric: WHOOOOO!!! Whipwreck....Misawa...Christmas Eve....we're going back to
America baby!!! WHOOOOO!!!!
(Inoki and Flair leave with Ono sitting at his desk with an extremely sad look on his face. Ono starts to pout...)
STACY: The following contest is set for one fall, and is a "FIRST BLOOD" match. First coming down to the ring from Buffalo, NY...weighing in at 187 pounds. Here is Mikey Whipwreck!!! ("Loser" by Beck starts to play and the crowd gives him a mixed reaction here tonight. Mikey climbs into the ring and works the crowd a little before giving up and waiting for Triple H.)
STACY: And his opponent...from Greenwhich, Connecticut. He weighs in at 283 pounds here is "Triple H"!!!!
("Are you ready to play the Game?" by Motorhead is heard all over the arena and the lights go out. The spotlight flys everywhere and Triple H makes his way out. He has his head down, and a bottle of water...he pours the water over his head and makes his way down to the ring. He finally climbs up to the apron and spits the water into the air...and Whipwreck attacks him.)
Joey: Whipwreck knocks Triple H off the apron, and he runs to the ropes. RANA by Whipwreck, and he's taking it to Hunter early in this match. Mikey brings
Triple H to his feet and Hunter counters with a knee to the stomach.
Jerry: Say goodbye to Whipwrecks offense. Joey: Mikey gets thrown back into the ring and Triple H doesn't look happy right now. He climbs back in and drives a boot into the gut, and another one.
The Game brings Whipwreck up and drives another knee to the stomach and Mikey goes down again.
Jerry: The Game has made it very clear that he wants XCW to stay in Japan. Do you think he saw Ric Flair add the stip to Monday's match?
Joey: I doubt it...he was probably getting ready. Hunter drives a fist into the forehead, and another one. Chop to the chest sends Whipwreck into the turnbuckle, and another chop to the chest...The Game with a fist to the forehead, but this time Whipwreck ducks it.
Jerry: Uh oh... Joey: Whipwreck jumps onto the back of Hunter and he's got a sleeper on. Hunter with a sitdown jawbreaker though, and Mikey lets go right away. Mikey trying to make it back to his feet and Hunter with a double ax-handle to the back, and another one...Hunter brings Mikey back up.
Jerry: LOOK OUT!!! Joey: Hello!! Whipwreck with a low blow, and he takes Hunter down with a faceslam. Mikey runs to the top ropes...Flying Dropkick and that's right on target on Triple H. Whipwreck rolls out of the ring and he's looking around under it...he grabs a is that a computer keyboard?
Jerry: IT IS!!!! Joey: Triple H is on rubber street, and Whipwreck slams the keyboard into his head. Another HUGE slam!!!! Here comes a third, NO!!! Hunter with a boot to the stomach, and he takes Mikey down with a knee to the face.
Jerry: Nothing can stop The Game...is there blood on him? Joey: No not yet...not on either man. Hunter drives that keyboard into the face, and another shot...Whipwreck seems to be in a whole lot of pain. Hunter picks up Mikey...PILEDRIVER!!!
Jerry: On the keyboard no less. Joey: Hunter goes to the outside and looks under the ring...a barbedwire baseball bat? Who stores that stuff under there?
Jerry: Maybe it's that creepy janitor. Joey: Hunter slides back into the ring and he slams it into the back of Mikey
Whipwreck, and another one!!! Mikey rolls over quickly and he gets a shot to the stomach, Triple H isn't letting up.
Jerry: Would you? Joey: Triple H rips back...OH MY GOD!!! HE HIT THE REF!!! HE HIT THE REF!!!! Jerry: The ref got hit with the bat....he's out cold. Joey: The Game stands over Whipwreck and he's going to end Whipwreck's...career right now. Maybe even his life...
Jerry: AAHHH!!! Joey: OH KICK TO THE GROIN BY WHIPWRECK!!!! Mikey is bleeding though...the match is over!!! The match is over!!! Tr...wait a minute. Whipwreck see's that the ref is down and he's smiling...he wipes the blood from his own face and smears it onto HUNTERS FACE!!!!
Jerry: WHAT!? Joey: Whipwreck put the blood onto Hunter's face! Mikey pretends to be hurt on the ground and he's covering his face up. The ref is up now and he see's Triple
H and he's CALLING FOR THE BELL!!!! THIS ONE IS OVER!!!!!!!
Jerry: What a smart move!!! STACY: Here is your winner...MIKEY WHIPWRECK!!!! Joey: The Rock is special guest ref on Monday!!!! WE MIGHT BE GOING BACK TO AMERICA!!!!!! Jerry: PUPPIES!!!! Joey: Whipwreck leaves the ring as the winner tonight!!! Jerry: And Triple H just leaves.... Joey: GOODNIGHT EVERYONE!!!!
