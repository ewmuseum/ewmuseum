XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 5)

July 24, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["N....W...O" is heard all over the arena and out walks Chad Costello!??!" He climbs into the ring asks for the microphone and begins to speak.]
Chad: Yeah...who were you expecting Vince McMahon? Listen, I'm out here to say that tonight we are officially NWO free. You know I never thought I'd be able to say it, but they got the picture...they understand. HOGA -
["N...W...O" is heard once again and Chad stands right in his boots. He looks like he's just witnessed the second coming. He slowly turns around, and he sees none other than Kurt Angle!?!?! Kurt starts laughing, and walks down to the ring...]
Kurt: HAHA how'd ya like that one boss? Chad: You son of a b - Kurt: WHOOO!! I got you didn't I? Chad: You think that's funny? Kurt: Well..yeah. I mean you almost pee'd your pants. Chad: Listen Kurt...I already fired Hennig, do you want to be next? Kurt: No. Chad: Well then I suggest that you cut it out with th - [As Chad is talking Nash, Hogan, and Hall sneak in from the crowd. Kurt's eyes get real big, and he's pointing for Chad to look behind him.]
Chad: Yeah another joke Kurt? Why don't you stick to the thing your good at and just wrestle. You think you're so damn funny...well you're the reason we're back here instead of Japan. Kurt you rea -
[Chad gets tapped on his shoulder.] Chad: What the -??? [He turns around and Nash fires in a forearm to the side of the head, and another forearm. Hall clotheslines Chad down to the canvas, and Hogan drops a leg on him. We get a shot of Kurt Angle, and he's smiling. Hulk grabs a microphone...]
Hollywood: So Kurt...is there anything you want to tell these people out here?? Kurt: Yes I do...YOUR OLYMPIC HERE IS OFFICIALLY..... ["IF YA SMELL WHAT THE ROCK IS COOKIN'" is heard all over the arena, and The
Rock runs down to the ring. He slides in and chases Kurt Angle right out of the ring. Both men head into the crowd, and the NWO is left in the ring all alone.
Kevin Nash gets on the microphone...] Kevin: Ya know...Chad Costello talks a big game. The man is all talk, and no walk. Where are these tough guys he was talking about? It sure as hell wasn't the security guards we punked in the back.
Hollywood: Let me tell you somethin' bra...The NWO is here to stay. Brock
Lesnar doesn't have anything on Hollywood dude. Bob Backlund's illegit step-child might need to learn what it's like to be in the ring with a legend...NWO STYLE MAN!!!
Kevin: Since we're here...what do you say we Jacknife ol' Chad through a table. Keep the tradition alive? Hollywood: That's just too sweet brother!!!! [Hall sets up a table on the outside of the ring, and Nash puts Chad between his legs. He holds up one finger, and Hall puts another table on to the other one. Nash is about to bring Chad to his feet....'N....W....O" is heard all over the arena once again. All three men look puzzled. Until...]
Joey: OH MY GOD!!! OH MY GOD!!! OH MY GOD!!!! Joel: OY VEY!!! OY VEY!!! OY VEY!!!! Joey: FLAIR!!! OLE!!! ARN!!! AND HBK!!!!! [All four men slide into the ring with chairs and the nWo takes off like roaches when the lights turn on. Flair puts down the chair and picks up the microphone...]
Ric: COME ON!!!! WHOOOOO!!!! COME ON!!!! [Crowd pops and WHOOOOS] Ric: Ya think...ya think we weren't going to come after you? You think that these guys weren't going to stop your shit? Well by God...WHOOOO!!! Arn, Ole,
Myself and Shawn Michaels...are here to make sure that you're not going to be controling things around here.
[Just about that time Chad makes it to his feet and smiles.] Ric: You're not going to be in control Hogan....WE ARE!!!!! [Arn Anderson grabs Chad and nails him with a Spinebuster Slam. Everyone pops like mad, and Arn gets on the microphone.]
Arn: That's for lying about me quitting. I never quit...I was fired. [Arn throws down the microphone and all four men work the crowd into a fenzy until we hit commercial.]
