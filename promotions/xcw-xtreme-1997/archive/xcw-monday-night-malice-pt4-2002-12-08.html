XCW Monday Night Malice (part 4)

December 8, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we comeback from commercial we see Ric Flair still searching for Chris
Jericho, and instead he bumps into Paul Heyman. Paul stops him, and the two start a conversation.]
Ric: What are you doing here? Paul: What am I doing here? What the hell am I doing here? I'm about to unleash hell, that's what I'm doing here. You see since you haven't returned any of my phone calls, since you decided against letting me and my men participate next week you will understand the true meaning of giving.
Ric: You know what? I have had people bothering me all night, now I have to find Jericho and talk to him about something. Either get out of the way, or I will make you get out of the way pal.
Paul: Hey no problem Natch.... [Paul Heyman steps aside...] Paul: You'll see plenty of me next week. [Camera cuts back to the ring.] Jim: What the hell did he mean by that? Don: I don't know, but Ric Flair isn't very friendly is he? I mean I want a commisioner that's a little friendly, a little happy. You know what I mean?
Jim: Well ever since the show started tonight he's been bogged down with problems. Don: Oh hold a second... Jim: Huh? Don: That heartbeat was for him. Boo whooo...he's the one that wanted the damn job, and so he has to live with it. Tough!
Jim: Alright fans...so far tonight we've seen Taz and Chris Jericho go into the finals of the Main Event tonight. We're down to two more matches, and the next one I'm not even sure is going to take place. Booker T and Shawn Michaels are set to fight here tonight, but the problem is that Shawn Michaels has an injured knee.
Don: So? Jim: Well is he going to compete? Don: Do I look like carnac? Jim: Lets take you to Howard Finkel for the announcement. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen, referee James Beard has informed me that Shawn
Michaels will not be able to compete tonight due to an injured knee. Therefor -
["Oh Oh Shawn"...all of a sudden Shawn Michaels comes out in his street clothes and limps to the ring. Shawn climbs in and takes away the microphone from Fink, and the crowd is popping like mad for him.]
Shawn: Ya know, I really wanted to come out here and do what I do best. Tonight
I wanted to come out and show you all that H-B-K still has it in him to be the best in the business. Hunter...Jean Paul whatever you want to call yourself, the fact is that you took away my moment of glory tonight. NOBODY takes away my moment, and even though I have to decline tonight...you will get payback, and you know what they say..payback is a bitch!
[Crowd pops] Shawn: Before I leave I want someone to come out here...Booker T, get your ass out here right now.
["Can you dig it...SUCKAAAAAA" is heard and Booker T walks out. He has a look of intensity, and a look of interest on his face. He slides into the ring and gets face to face with Shawn Michaels. The crowd goes silent as they wait to see what happens. Shawn extends his hand out to Booker T...]
Shawn: Go into the match tonight and beat the living hell out of Triple H for me, that is if he makes it past Brock Lesnar. Can you dig it s -
Booker T: Whoa whoa whoa...I ain't here to fight your battles boy. The Book is here to do one thing and that's win the XCW World Title. Now I know Hunter done you wrong, and if I was you I'd be pretty damn pissed off myself. If Hunter crosses my path I'll take his punk ass out, but if he doesn't...I ain't losin'
any sleep. Can you dig it...SUC - Shawn: I can dig it. [Shawn throws the microphone down and walks away leaving Booker T there dumbfounded.] [We comeback from a quick commercial and both men are already in the ring... Hunter tells the ref to check Brock for any weapons, and as the ref goes over to him Hunter tackles the big man and unloads with a series of punches to the face. Brock with all his power tosses Hunter off of him.]
Jim: Whoa and I don't think Hunter has ever been handled like that before. Don: Only by Chyna. Jim: Ewe. [Both men get back up and Brock whips Hunter into the turnbuckle. Hunter backs out, and Brock catches him in an overhead belly-to-belly suplex. Brock stands over the big man and looks to the crowd, and the fans approve of what he's doing.]
Jim: Brock bends down and starts to unload with a series of fists to the face, over and over.
Don: That's not legal. Jim: But he deserves it. Don: So do you, but I'm not punching you in the face. Jim: Hunter reaches up with his legs, and pulls Brock down for a Sunset Flip. Nice counter...ONE!! TWO!!! No...Brock escapes, and both men rush back to their feet. [Hunter with a knee to the ribs, and he sends Brock into the ropes. Brock ducks a clothesline puts on the breaks and waits for Hunter to turn arouns. Hunter turns around and Brock catches him in a HUGE spinebuster slam.]
Jim: Brock goes for the cover!!! ONE!! T - NO!!! Don: You're gonna have to get up earlier than that to beat Hunter. Jim: Brock brings Hunter back up and he tosses him over the top rope to the outside. Hunter hits hard, and Brock isn't going to sit around. He's going right after the man.
Don: Brock is a beast! [Brock walks over the the dazed Hunter and lifts him up...he drapes Hunter's throat across the steel guardrail. Brock yells for Hunter to get up, and he does but very slowly. Brock grabs him by the hair, and Hunter counters with an elbow to the midsection.]
Jim: Hunter now with a series of right fists to the forehead, and he rakes the eyes of Brock. Clothesline takes the big man down on the hard floor.
Don: Gotta love Triple H's agression. Jim: Hunter now is exposing the cement floor. He just tossed that padding to the side, and what is he thinking? The Cerebral Assasian has something in mind as he grabs Brock....
Don: Pedigree onto the cement? Jim: No, it looks like he's going to Piledrive him. OH he hits it onto the cement floor, on that unforgiving concrete.
Don: That's going to make you forget your 13th Birthday. [Hunter walks over and grabs the microphone..."This match is now a no-disqualification match" the fans boo as Hunter has a huge smirk on his face.
Hunter throws Brock back into the ring, and he grabs a chair and slides that into the ring.]
Jim: Does he have the power to change a match like that? Don: I don't see why not...he's The Game! [Brock slowly gets up and Hunter plasters him with the chair. Brock falls to the ground and the crowd boos Hunter, but those boos slowly turn to cheers as
Brock is back up...]
Jim: He's back up!!!! Don: Hunter doesn't even know it!!! [Hunter seems suprised at first, but he smirks again knowing he can not him down again with it. Brock walks over to him and Hunter unloads with another chair shot that takes the big man down once again. But Brock doesn't stay down...]
Jim: TWO SHOTS TO THE HEAD!! BROCK IS UP!!!! Don: Brock is up, but he's barely able to stand! Jim: Hunter upset n - OH GOD!!! Another chair shot, and Brock is down. Three chair shots in a row and there is no way in hell he's going to get back up.
[Hunter tosses the chair aside, and hooks the leg for the cover.] Jim: Damnit!! ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!!!!! Brock kicked out!!!! [Hunter slams his hands down onto the canvas, and walks over to the chair. He grabs it and raises it high into the air and starts slamming into the back of
Brock Lesnar. Shot after shot, with each one looking like it was harder than the other. Hunter finally throws the chair back down and makes the cover once again....]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! It was jus - Don: NO!!! Brock got his shoulder up...Brock got his shoulder up. Jim: I don't think Hunter has ever had this problem. What's he going to do now? He's hit him with three chairshots, and now he's going to the outside. Don: He's getting the ringsteps... Jim: OH come on!!! [Hunter throws the ring steps into the ring, and he climbs in. He grabs the ring steps and slams them down onto Brock Lesnar's head...well at least he tried to slam it down, but Brock rolled out of the way. Frustrated, Hunter grabs Brock and sets him up for the Pedigree...]
Jim: He's going to plant him on those steps...NO!!! Brock with a backdrop!!! Don: Uh oh... Jim: Brock grabs the ring steps!!! [Brock swings wildly at Hunter as he's getting up, Hunter ducks and quickly grabs the chair. Hunter swings and the chair makes impact with the ring steps.
They fall onto Brock, and Hunter makes the pin again...] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TWO AND A HALF!!! BROCK GOT HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!!!! Don: What the hell is going through Hunter's mind? Jim: Who k - oh God no. [Hunter places Brock's head against the ring steps, and he raises the chair high into the air. Hunter gets ready to be-head Brock Lesnar...the rage in his eyes shows no remorse.]
Jim: He hits this Brock will be put out of comission!!!! Don: For good...ending a short career. Jim: NO DON'T DO IT DAMNIT!!!! [Hunter raises the chair ready to inflict serious damage...until...] - "OH OH SHAWN" is heard throughout the home of the Pacers - ["Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels came out onto the stage, limping as he does carrying a sledgehammer. Triple H seems outraged and he dares HBK to climb into the ring with him, wanting to injure his knee even further.]
Jim: Shawn is limping to the ring!!! BY GOD HE'S GOING TO TAKE THAT SOB OUT! Don: Ugh...can't we get Shawn out of here? Jim: Here we go!!! [Shawn ducks a chair shot by Hunter. Shawn swings with full force, and Hunter ducks...Shawn hits Brock Lesnar right in the head with the sledgehammer, and it looks like he knocked him out cold. Brock falls to the ground...as Shawn watches, Hunter grabs him by the hair and throws him to the outside. Hunter rolls up Brock for the win...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! DAMNIT!!!! Don: HAHA cheaters never prosper. Jim: Hunter sneaks a win, and he did so by cheating. Don: How did he cheat? Everything he did was totally legal. Jim: Who is he to change matches? Don: He's The Game!!! [Hunter gets his hand raises, and Brock Lesnar is still out cold. Shawn is on the outside screaming in pain, as he landed on his knee. Hunter stands over
Brock and raises his hands into the air before exiting the ring. One would think he'd give Shawn a couple of kicks to the knee, but instead he left him there for the XCW officials to help out.]
Jim: EMT's are down here, and all hell has broken loose. I mean tonight has become one big cluster bomb, and Ric Flair needs to really get things under control. Evil refs, wrestlers changing the way matches are being made.
Don: Just how it should be. [Camera cuts to Ric Flair who is still looking for Chris Jericho. He bumps into the "Evil" Hebner though, and the pour guy stops in his tracks.]
Ric: Listen if you give me the info I want, then I'm not going to do anything to you. As a matter of fact I'll let you go on vacation.
Dave: Really? Ric: Yes. Dave: Well Jericho found me and paid me a large sum of money to help him out tonight. He said that it was a matter of life and death if he made it into the
Fatal Fourway.
Ric: Okay..and you decided to help him out. Dave: Yes. Ric: Do you know where he is now? Dave:.......... Ric: Tell me. Dave: He said he'd be waiting in his car until the Main Event... Ric: Thank you. [Ric Flair walks away, and without even turning around he says...] Ric: By the way, you're fired. [Camera cuts to a commercial.]
