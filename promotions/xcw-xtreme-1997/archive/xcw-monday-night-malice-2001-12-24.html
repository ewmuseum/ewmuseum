XCW Monday Night Malice

December 24, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera cuts to Mikey Whipwreck walking up to the back of the Egg Dome. Standing in his way are Hiroshi Tenzan, and The Great Muta. Both men are blocking his way and not letting him in. Mikey sets his bag down and looks to be upset...)
Hiroshi: Go home... Mikey: What? I have a match...you get out of my way! Hiroshi: I sa.... (All of a sudden a limo pulls up and out steps Ric Flair. Ric has his suit and tie on, along with his famous glasses. He walks up to Mikey and puts his arm around him, and then Antonio Inoki steps out of the limo and puts his arm on the other side of Whipwreck. Mikey is in awe of the whole thing.)
Mikey: Wha....hum...hee...how? Antonio: You let us in right now.... (Hiroshi and Muta move aside and all three men walk in with smiles on their faces. Hiroshi and Muta look at each other in shock....)
(The camera pans across a sold out crowd in the Egg Dome. Fans are going wild as this might be the last time the XCW is in Japan for ahwile. You can see the
"Hell In The Cell" above the ring, and the camera just locks onto it. Camera finally stops and pans across the signs..."Rock loves his sushi", "Our Kind Of
Japanese" and on the sign is a picture of Triple H, "Sonny Ono loves eggrolls"
and "Bing wa ting taw". Fireworks starts to go off, and we cut to Jim Ross and
Jesse Ventura. Both men seem to be in good spirits here tonight, and JR has his usual cowboy hat on with a red XCW shirt. Jesse has outdone himself tonight and looks like he belongs at the circus. Jesse has on a tight red and green tye-dye shirt, some black leather pants, and a white boa....not to mention the Santa
Hat.)
Jim: Welcome everyone to the Egg Dome!!! We are live here in Japan, and tonight will be our last night here. I don't care what the odds are, damn the odds...it's time to go home Jesse.
Jesse: Well I'll tell ya what Jimbo....there's a different feeling about tonight. Maybe because it's Christmas Eve, or maybe because we're going home.
My opinion is that if I was stuck in a cage with Misawa and Chono...the chances of me winning would be highly unlikely. So I don't know...we'll have to see.
Jim: Fans our first match is going to be a HUGE one. That monster of a man,
Vader will be taking on Raven. Vader has been on a rampage as of late, and he's not going to stop tonight.
Jesse: Well now that Paul E., has him under his win this guy won't be able to be stopped. Paul E. is a dangerous mind behind a dangerous man, and when you have some beast like Vader mowing people down Paul E is going to get cockier and cockier...
Jim: You have to remember that Raven isn't going to give up easily. This is man who will do anything it takes to win...
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Coming down to the ring first....from Short Hills, NJ. He weighs in at 247 pounds...here is Raven!!!
(Ravens music starts to play and he comes down to a mixed reaction. Although
Raven doesn't care. He doesn't look to happy, and as he slides into the ring he just slouches against the turnbuckle.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Boulder, Colorado. He weighs in at 450 pounds, and is led down to the ring by Paul E....here is "The Man They Call" Vader!!!
("What time...it's time...it's Vader Time" is heard all over the arena and everyone stands to their feet. Vader walks through the curtain and the crowd goes wild over his size, and shear fear factor. He steps into the ring and parades around whistling, and swinging his arms like a gorilla.)
Jim: That guy is massive! Oh and Raven is attacking the big man right away...Raven with a right hand, and another right hand...Vader just brushes it off though and goes for a right of his own, and another one. You can swing from those punches Vader is throwing....
Jesse: That's like hitting a bus every two seconds. Jim: Raven gets backed into the corner, and Vader isn't letting up with those stiff shots to the side of the face. Vader was fined $10,000 this past weekend for attacking 3 wrestlers, and for putting Edge out.
Jesse: Yeah I don't think Edge will be back for awhile. Jim: Vader goes to drive a shoulder into the midsection of Raven, but Raven moves. Vader staggers out of the corner, and Raven with a bulldog.
Jesse: That's the only way Raven is going to win tonight...if he can stay out of Vader's way and stick and move. Raven can't go one on one with Vader, and if he tries to it's going to be over.
Jim: Raven waits for Vader to make it to his feet and he hits him with a clothesline, and another one...Vader is staggering. Raven goes for a third clothesline, and this time Vader ducks. He spins Raven around and nails him with a short clothesline of his own...and the fans can't believe the impact!!!
Jesse: Raven is going to have to ask Santa for a brain after a match like this. Jim: Vader brings Raven to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Raven bounces off, comesback...BIG POWERSLAM!!!!! Vader goes for the cover...1!! 2!! 2 count is all we get as Raven gets his shoulder up.
Jesse: Raven has a ton of fight in him, and he's not going to go down to just a powerslam. Jim: Vader brings Raven to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle. The big man is pointing up...he places Raven onto the turnbuckle. He's giving the sign for the Superplex....here we go...NO!!! Raven pushes him off the turnbuckle, and now he jumps down.
Jesse: Vader's holding his back in pain, and Raven should taken advantage of it. Jim: Raven grabs both of Vader's legs...make a wish!!! OH MY!!! He pulled those legs apart like they were presents on Christmas Day!!! Raven with series of stomps to the chest, and now he's choking Vader.
Jesse: He's got to be aware of the count.... Jim: Raven lets go at 4, and he goes for the choke again. The crowd doesn't seem to like the way Raven wrestles. Raven is a do anything, use anything kind of wrestler. You're not going to see many armlocks, wristlocks, leglocks...you're going to see weapons, and blood.
Jesse: Well I'm sure he would have a better chance at putting Vader out if he used a couple of stop signs, and maybe a chair or two.
Jim: He sure would, but it's not Raven's Rules.....Vader is back to his feet and Raven with a kneelift to the stomach, and a fist to the forehead, but Vader counters with a solid right hand of his own...and Raven goes crashing to the floor. Wait a second....what the hell is on the screen?
(Taz shows up on the XCW-Tron...) Taz: Hey Vada...listen up. Tonight is the night that Taz makes you fear him...ta-night you're going to fear me, and Paul E....you aren't going to break me you fuckin' nobody. Does it look like I'm broken to you? Does it look like
I'm gonna give up? Ta-night one of you two pieces of shit is gonna get choked out. Which one will it be? Beat me if you can, survive if I let you....
(Vader wasn't even paying attention, but as Taz is finishing up his speech....the LIVE version of Taz shows up right behind Paul E.)
Jim: Taz!!! Taz!!! He's right behind Paul E, and Paul turns around....T-BONE
TAZPLEX!!!! Oh that was on the cement.
Jesse: Rule number one in the XCW...never piss off Taz! Jim: Taz leaves the ring area with a sour look on his face. Vader can't believe it, and Raven turns him around...kick to the stomach. EVENFLOW DDT!!! There's the cover...1!!! 2!!! 3!!!
Jesse: NO!!! NO!! Jim: Vader just kicked out of the Evenflow!!! Raven brings Vader up again...short clothesline by Raven..NO!!! Vader ducks it...boot to the stomach.
POWER BOMB!!! POWER BOMB and Vader goes for the cover...1!! 2!! 3!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Vader!!! Jim: Vader goes out to check on Paul E, and he doesn't look to happy. Vader storms to the back leaving Paul E by himself, and Taz had better watch out here tonight.
Jesse: Yeah things don't look to good for Taz, but Taz isn't a push over. He's not going to wait for Vader to come to him...he's going to go right for Vader.
Jim: When those to meet it's going to be nothing short of a war! (Camera cuts to Triple H, Misawa, and Chono sitting in the dressing room. All of a sudden Kurt Angle walks in with a "Rising Suns" shirt on. All three men stand to their feet and wonder what he's doing there...)
Kurt: Hey guys... Triple H: Who invited you into the room? I don't remember asking for Kurt Angle
"The Olympic Hero". I don't remember inviting the milk drinker in to have some words with us.
Kurt: I just wanted to say that I think you are all doing a great job, and I can't wait to make this Japan deal permanent.
Misawa: You won gold medal? Kurt: Yeah, I won a gold medal. You want to see it? Misawa: No...medals are for pussies. (Everyone starts to laugh but Kurt.) Kurt: Yeah well this pussy worked hard for that medal, and I don't think you could do it. Misawa: Heh...I don't think you could get this belt away from me. Kurt: I can. Misawa: CANNOT! Kurt: I can too! Misawa: CANNOT!!! Triple H: Knock it off...we have an important match tonight. Kurt thanks for the support...maybe we'll need your help someday. Now get out before we all make you wish you were back on those sweaty mats doing God knows what to only who knows who...
Kurt: FINE...I'll leave, but before I go I have a little treat for you all. (Angle reaches into a bag and pulls out 3 Santa Hats, and a pair of Reindeer
Antlers. He gives Misawa a hat, Triple H a hat, and Chono the Antlers. He puts on the other hat...both Chono and Misawa have no expression on their face.)
Kurt: What? Misawa: What is this? Kurt: What do you mean? You've never celebrated Christmas? Misawa: No... Kurt: You've got to be kidding me? You never asked Santa for a Red Ryder BB
Gun? Triple H: I don't think people in Japan celebrate Christmas.... Kurt: Oh well...hold onto your Santa Hats and Reindeer Antlers...because tonight Misawa and Chono are going to celebreate Christmas. It's true...it's
DAMN true!!!
(Camera fades)
