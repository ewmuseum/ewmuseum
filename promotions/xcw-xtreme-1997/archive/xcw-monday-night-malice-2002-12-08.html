XCW Monday Night Malice

December 8, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We fade onto Ric Flair who is the new President/Comissioner of the XCW. He's at his desk signing some papers, working on new people to come into the fed.
All of a sudden Triple H walks in. He's got a "BEEF" shirt on, and a pair of jeans. Not to mention the God Awful belly bags that the wrestlers feel they need to wear. Hunter without saying a word just hoovers over the desk. Flair looks up casually...]
Ric: Can I help you? HHH: Yeah you can. I was at home ready to start my Christmas with my soon to be wife, I was all ready to enjoy a holiday at home...but since the XCW keeps coming back and going away my contract says I have to be here. Now I don't mind wrestling, it's my life. I bleed this damn sport, I sleep it....I just need an honest word from you that this fed ain't going to close tomorrow. You understand?
Ric: Do I look like the type of person that is going to start something and not finish it? [Flair looks a little upset, and he slowly stands up from his desk and both men are eye to eye now.]
Ric: TELL ME!!! DO I LOOK LIKE THAT TYPE OF PERSON? HHH: Flair you ne - Ric: ANSWER THE DAMN QUESTION!!! HHH: (Gets right in Flair's Face)....let's just say, you'd better not be that type of person, because if you are you're Christmas is going to be spent in a hospital.
[Hunter turns around and walks away. Ric Flair is left standing there, and all of a sudden Chris Jericho walks into the room.]
Chris: Hey listen Flair...I don't know if you know this, but I'm not in a title match tonight. I'll have you know that every single time the XCW has been opened back up, I have gotten a title shot right off the bat. What makes you so damn different clown?
Ric: You think you can come in here and demand something? You think that Chris
Jericho can come in here and ask for a title shot. Not in this XCW, not anymore pal. You will earn your shot just like every other person in this fed, and by earning it you have to win.
Chris: Just remember Flair...I AM THE KING OF THE WORLD!!! Don't you forget it. [Jericho storms out, and Flair sits down and takes a deep breath. He looks off into no where...]
Ric: This is going to be a long, long night. [Camera fades, and all of a sudden "O Fortuna" starts to play. We see pictures of Steve Armstrong and Sho Funaki inside the steel cage. We see Steve Austin tossing Shane Douglas off the 15 ft. high ramp. Then a series of rapid shots of wrestlers excuting their finishers. The shots get faster and faster, and then they stop...on the XCW logo. It starts to bleed....and we cut live to the
Conseco Fieldhouse. It's a semi-sold out crowd, a lot of people aren't there due to the opening and closing of the XCW. They don't want to put their heart and soul into it because who knows what's going to happen in the next few weeks. We cut to Good Ol' Jim Ross and with him tonight and for the rest of the
stay here on Monday Night Malice is the one and only Don Callis.] Jim: Welcome everyone!!! Welcome to Monday Night Malice, and what a night it's going to be. We've got a ton of great matches, we've got a lot of stuff we have to get to -
Don: Must you hype everything up in that horrible voice of yours. Listen Ross, we know it's going to be good. We know we're going to see some tremendous talent, but what the people really tune in for is to hear the voice of yours truely.
Jim: I beg to differ with that. Don: I'll h - ["2001" starts up slowly, and the camera cuts to the XCW walkway. Ric Flair steps out from the curtain with his suit and tie on. He's got a big smile on his face, and the crowd "WHOOOO's" him as he makes his way down to the ring.
Ric climbs into the ring and is handed the microphone.] Ric: Indianapolis...WELCOME TO MALICE!!! WHOOOOO!!!! First of all I want to say that it's good to be back, and that there are going to be some big announcements in the next few weeks. Right now, I want to give you the biggest announcement there is. Yeah...ladies and gentlemen...Christmas Eve, Live on
PPV....From Honolulu, Hawaii, the Aloha Stadium...WHOOOO!!!! Christmas in
Hawaii baby! It's going to be Jingle Brawl, and that...that's when we're going to crown the new XCW World Champion. That's right...WHOOO!!!!
["Sexy Boy" begins to play over the PA system, and out walks Shawn Michaels. The crowd gives him a mixed reaction as he changes sides more than he changes his socks. Shawn has his "Heartbreaker" shirt on, and a pair of jeans. He climbs into the ring and walks right up to Ric Flair. Flair has a grin on his face, and hands the microphone to Shawn.]
Shawn: Now...Hawaii, that fine with me. I have no complaints about that, but what I want to talk about is the fact that the world title isn't mine right at this very moment. You see I'm the "SHOWSTOPPER", I'm the guy who went an hour with Bret Hart and made it interesting, I'm the guy who carried guys like Kevin
Nash and Sid Vicious to 5 star matches. I put this business on the map, not
Hulk Hogan, not Bret Hart...not even YOU.
Ric: Whoa whoa...kid you're getting a little ahead of yourself. Now listen you may have a point with the fact that Kevin Nash and Sid couldn't be carried to a five star match by anyone but you...but you did not put wrestling on the map.
My sweat, MY BLOOD....MY HEART!!! Don't you ever forget where this sport came from, not just me but others like Terry Funk, Mick Foley, and even Hulk Hogan!
It's punks like you who make this sport a mess, with the politics, the games, the junk...well you want a shot at the XCW World Title tonight? YOU WANT A
SHOT!? FINE.....WHOOO!! Tonight every win puts someone into a Fatal Four Way later on....
[Crowd pops like crazy. Shawn Michaels puts a huge smile on his face, and extends his hand to Ric Flair. Flair is about to shake his hand, and all of a sudden "Time To Play The Game" is heard all over the PA, and out walks Hunter.
He climbs into the ring, and gets right in Shawn's face. The crowd goes silent, and Hunter extends his hand. Both men shake hands, and then Hunter turns to Ric and smiles.]
HHH: Ya know, Flair...what you just saw was a sign of respect. Shawn has earned my respect for everything he has done in wrestling. When I look at you though,
I see a man who can't get away from the sport. A man who with each day loses a little bit of himself...a man who, well...is old, pathetic, and washed up. You made the right choice tonight, and when I meet HBK in the Main Event tonight...heh, the whole world is going to be knocked on their asses.
Ric: Hunter, I have one thing to ask you. Is it your nature to come out to hog
TV time and bore us all to death, or does someone actually tell you to come out here?
[Hunter hauls off and hits Ric Flair on the side of the head. Flair falls down to the ground and Hunter continues to kick him over and over. He lifts him up real quick and drops him down with a Pedigree. Hunter stands over him with a look of disdain, and then he and Shawn shake hands again. Shawn exits the ring first, but before he can hop down off the apron...Hunter forearms him across the back, and Shawn looks to have landed right on his knee. Hunter stands in the ring watching Shawn writhe in pain.]
Jim: Oh my God!! Shawn Michaels knee has been busted up, and Hunter is smiling. Hunter is smiling, and this is sick. Ric Flair and Shawn Michaels have both been put out of comission tonight.
Don: Well the wrestling world will just have to go on without them! Jim: Fans we'll be right back!!!!!
