XCW — Roleplays

Trash Talkin'

January 29, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. Mikey Whipwreck 2. Terry Funk/Cactus Jack 3. Paul E/Vader/Chris Chetti 4. Al Snow -x-X-MiKeY-WhiPwReCk-X-x- "Please Don't Endorse Me" [Fade.  We're shown the talk show set of an infamous cartoon character, Space
Ghost.  He's sitting behind his cosmic-colored desk and he taps a pencil on it.  A TV lowers from the ceiling and hangs above a guest chair.]
Space Ghost:  Folks, friends, amigos, homies and astronauts.. Welcome to Space
Ghost Coast to Coast, I'm the main man Space Ghost.  My international super pals are unable to appear here tonight due to pending events, so I've got the whole place to myself.  If you're a good viewer, I'll invite you to my rockin'
beach party tonight!  In any event, we've got a great guest for all you kids at home... He's a wrestler, so, I guess that means he wrestles!  Isn't that fun! 
He wrestles.  Mikey Whipwreck, please come in! [Cartoon static fades into a live shot of Mikey Whipwreck.  He laughs.] Mikey Whipwreck:  Hey!  Glad to be here! Space Ghost:  Of course you are, this is Space Ghost Coast to Coast.  Why wouldn't you be? Mikey Whipwreck:  Hmmm, you got me there. Space Ghost:  Well maybe if you ate Mexican for lunch, you wouldn't be glad, but I bet you didn't eat Mexican, did you?
Mikey Whipwreck:  No, I had some pasta and some-- Space Ghost:  Good!  I like smart people.  Smart people are good to talk to.  What are you doing today Mikey? Mikey Whipwreck:  Ah, actually I'm just twiddling my thumbs until later this week.  Kinda nervous, you know?
Space Ghost:  No!  Space Ghost is never nervous.. But Space Ghost never speaks in third person either.  It's a revolution, I tell you.
Mikey Whipwreck:  Well, I've got a match with Big Van Vader.  He's a huge man. Space Ghost:  Ahh, I remember the days... I used to eat cereal, you know what cereal is Mikey?
Mikey Whipwreck:  Sure I do. Space Ghost:  Well, I used to eat it and I used to watch ol' Vader in his big black suit.  What an intergalactic meanie!
Mikey Whipwreck:  No, that's Darth Vader. Space Ghost:  Of course it is!  Who are you talking about?  Are you feeling ok? Mikey Whipwreck:  No, like I said, I'm nervous.  Vader, he's this 450 pound monster who likes to beat up people.  He's a bully.
Space Ghost:  Do you need my special laser blaster?  It could do some mighty big damage. Mikey Whipwreck:  No, that's ok.  I'm afraid cartoon toys won't help much. Space Ghost:  Good!  I don't lend my dangerous arsenal to cartoons like you anyway! Mikey Whipwreck:  Huh? Space Ghost:  You sure are a smart fellow.  But tell me, what's going to happen? Mikey Whipwreck:  Well, we're fighting for gold. Space Ghost:  Gold?!  Gold is extinct, man!  That is a valuable resource, my friend! Mikey Whipwreck:  Haha, you bet. Space Ghost:  Do you think, you know, you could supply your buddy Space Ghost with some gold?  I sure could use some around the set.
Mikey Whipwreck:  Why not?  That is, if I survive. Space Ghost:  Well, I hope you survive Mr. Whipwreck.  I love the gold, you know.  You know what gold really is, right?
Mikey Whipwreck:  Um, a mineral? Space Ghost:  No!  It's money!  You get whatever you want with gold.  I could buy all the hot dogs in the world with some gold. 
Mikey Whipwreck:  You mind if I talk real fast? Space Ghost:  Sure!  The viewers are hypnotized by your demand!  Go ahead! Mikey Whipwreck:  Judging by Vader's tenacity, I would say he doesn't watch cartoons.  But if you do, I want you to take heed of this... I'm more excited and ready for this match than I've ever been.  Sure, I'll probably be executed within five minutes but doesn't it _MEAN_ anything any more when I say I want to try?  Sheesh, people are all about results.  The old catchphrase "It's the thought that counts" just doesn't apply, does it?  Anyway, after you beat me, you'll have the gold but you know what I'll have?  Umm.. My health!  No, wait,
I won't have health... I'll have credibility!  No, actually I won't... I'll have...
Space Ghost:  Clean undies, Mikey! Mikey Whipwreck:  CLEAN UNDIES!  I will have clean underwear waiting for me in my dresser!  So what do you think about that?  I bet you're trembling.  I bet you're about to collapse and give in.  You can't stand the pressure, big man! 
Give it up! Space Ghost:  Blow the Death Star!! Mikey Whipwreck:  Huh?  No, wrong Vader. Space Ghost:  Sure it is...  Go ahead. Mikey Whipwreck:  You're going to be _MINE_ Vader.  I will own you.  I will win that XCW United States title and I will rub the victory in your face for the rest of your life. Remember that.
Space Ghost:  Mikey, ahhh... Do you really think you'll beat him? Mikey Whipwreck:  Oh, no.  It's just a wrestling routine.  He's going to murder me. Space Ghost:  Hmmm, I think I know where I'm betting my hot dogs. Mikey Whipwreck:  You mean money? Space Ghost:  That's what I said!  You need help, Mikey. Mikey Whipwreck:  Haha, yes, I do. Space Ghost:  Moltar, make it no more!  He's upsetting me! [Scene crackles and fades on the cartoon TV.] Space Ghost:  Don't go away kids, when we get back... Disco lessons from the
Space Ghost himself!
[Fade to a commercial break.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- [The camera opens up in the backstage area of the Nassau Colliseum after the
XCW PPV New Year's Brawl has gone off the air. We quickly see that we are in the locker room of none other than Terry Funk, because that's who is sitting down having a nice cold Bud Light after his victory over Jake Roberts. He pauses getting drunk for a moment to speak...]
  TF: Roberts, Ah'm not going to waste any time, any breath or any energy talkin' 'bout you because you're simply not worth it, all Ah'll say is that Ah did exactly what Ah set out to do...and that was tuh shut you up once and for all, and get plain, good ol' fashioned Texas-style retribution for dis-honorin' mah wife Vicki by givin' you an ass-whuppin that you won't soon forget. Ah hope
Ah taught you a lesson Roberts, because it seems like you've forgotten a lot of
un-spoken rules about this business...   [As the camera pans out, we see Funk is not alone...]   "BANG BANG!"   [...oh my god, somebody killed the Funker! Heh, just kidding, no Russo-booking in the XCW. But there is smart booking, because standing in front of the camera, giving the good ol' 2 gun finger point to all the fans, is the King of the Deathmatch, the Hardcore Icon...Cactus Jack...]
  CJ: Many people are probably wondering why...they are saying...why Cactus Jack? Why come out of retirement...why leave your wife and kids...why ruin the perfect life by signing up with XCW. Well I think I'll tell you why. You see, this man right here...
  [Cactus motions to the Funker, sitting beside him...]   ..._HE_ came out of retirement..._HE_ left his wife and kids..._HE_ ruined his perfect life on a nice Texas ranch to come work for this promotion...and than a man came along...a man who likes to play with snakes...and he dis-respected not only this man and everything he stands for, but he dis-respected his wife...and in this business that is a big no-no.
  So I thought to myself...maybe if I did everything he did, somebody would come out from the shadows and dis-respect MY FAMILY! This man sitting here does not
_DESERVE_ to have his family disrespected, especially not by the likes of some alcoholic crack addict who's only family is a damned rapist! So you see, if somebody would dare dis-respect _MY_ family I'd get very very angry...and when
I get very very angry...I get very very _VIOLENT_!!!
  [In a release of some obvious pent-up anger, Cactus repeatedly punchs a locker, denting it considerably. This doesn't seem to phase Funk, who has probably grown accustomed to such acts from Cactus. Yep, this definately ain't no sock-puppet in the crotch, sports entertaining Mankind, this is the violent, maniacal, should-be-in-an-institution Cactus Jack we've all been dying to see...]
  CJ: So not only am I here to give my friend any backup he may need against the next person who decides they want to make things personal, I'm also here to let out a little frustration...frustration with the indignity of having to extricate a sock puppet from my groin area and shoving it down people's throat...frustration with being seen as a comedic goof...frustration with not being who I really am...and that is one VERY MEAN LITTLE BOY...
  [As Cactus leaves the room, Funk seems to have a little grin on his face...]   TF: Ah'm sure glad that crazy bastard decided to show up in XCW, and Ah'm glad
Ah handed Roberts his ass but one thing Ah'm not too happy about is the match
Ah'm booked in on Monday. At first they tell me..."Funk, you're facing the champ on Malice", so of course Ah'm glad to hear that, but than they tell me the title isn't on the line. Now here is where Ah get confused, and Ah don't think it's because Ah'm getting old...
  [Funk shakes his head...]   TF: Now don't get me wrong, Ah'm more than happy to be fighting Shane Douglas, we've had some damn good matchs in the past, and there is no doubt in this old man's mind that we can pull out another great one, but what Ah don't like is
XCW not giving me a shot at the title. Ah'm a patient man, but Ah've gone through hell in this federation and Ah've only been here a month...so why not reward me with a title shot? Ah sure don't have many matchs left in me...do they want proof that Ah can make it for one last run on top? Do they need proof
Ah can wrestle a darn good match against the champ before Ah get a title shot?
  Or maybe they just want to see Ah hell of a fight without worrying about an old man becoming their champion...
  [Funk grins as the camera fades out...] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- [The long, black backdrop; the large, red lettering; the three men standing in front of it all.  If you don't know yet, the red lettering spells out
"Dangerous Alliance Version 2.0".  And the three men?  Paul E. Dangerously, decked out in a blue blazer and a pair of long black pants, along with a tie with the word "EXTREME" running down it; "Superstar" Chris Chetti, wearing a pair of tight black jeans and no shirt, flexing his pectorals for the camera;
and DA MAN~! known as "Big" Van Vader.] PeD: The United States title?  The United freakin' States title?  Do you know who this is, Chad Costello?
[Paul points at Vader, who growls.] PeD: Do you know who _I_ am?!  We don't want your stinking United States title.  We want the only title DA MAN~! is worthy of.  The title that The Rock and Shane Douglas _stole_ from us.  We want the World Heavyweight Championship.
[Paul begins to pace, which is never a good sign.] PeD: But that's OK... that's OK that you're putting us in the ring with a man we've beaten to a pulp time and time again.  It's OK because when all is said and done, and when Mikey Whipwreck is lying in a pool of his bodily fluids, we will _NOT_ be denied.
[Spinning on his heel, Dangerously faces the camera, anger etched on his face.] PeD: We will _not_ be denied... [Suddenly, Chris Chetti pulls Paul back, and smiles.] CC: Easy there, killer.  Don't want to give yourself a stroke there. PeD: Screw you, Chris. CC: Yes, Paul, that's what the ladies love to do. [Chetti's cocky demeanor oozes off of him, as he increases his "annoying prick" status by flexing his right arm while speaking.] CC: There are few men in the XCW with the strength... [Chris spins around, crosses his arms around to his back, and unleashes the back muscles for the ladies to swoon over.  And to make sure the swoon is warranted, he looks back over his shoulder.]
CC: ... with the drive... [And in a totally ridiculous moment, Chris flexes his left arm while pointing upwards with his right... pure face-ish Hulk Hogan at his peak.]
CC: ... and the passion... [Finally, Chris faces forward, and flexes both arms.] CC: ... to be able to contend for the World title night in and night out.  I'm one of those few.  The other?  Why, DA MAN~! of course...
[Pure anger and hatred enter the scene front and center.  Four hundred pounds of anger and hatred, for that matter...]
V: Little Mikey.  Little tiny Mikey.  There are few men with the guts to stand up to me time and time again with little regard to their bodies.  These men fear me.  These men hide from me.
[Vader decides that it's too much work to get close to the camera himself, and reaches out and drags the camera towards him.]
V: These men are much smarter than you, Mikey.  For they know who I am.  They know what I'm capable of.  And they _know_ what I'm after.  Pain...
destruction... and _gold_.  You stand in my way to get a piece of gold.  You stand in my way to the ultimate prize.
[A sadistic smile.] V: You won't be standing for long. [Vader shoves the camera away, allowing Paul E. to get right back into the frame.] PeD: The United States title might not be what we're after, but we'll take it... for now.  But once that title is around Vader's shoulder?  We'll be looking to accessorize further.  And there's nothing The Rock, Mikey Whipwreck, or Shane Douglas can do about it.
[Zoom in on Paul.] PeD: The Dangerous Alliance Version 2.0 is on a rampage.  And _NO ONE_ in the
XCW is safe.  Not even you, Chad Costello.
[The trademark Dangerously smirk.] PeD: Not even you... [Fade to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens up in a small, dimly lit room. The dim light seems to come from one area of the room as the camera moves towards it. As it does we see a television, with a chair facing it. We can't see what is on the television just yet but the sound is audible..]
"Jim: AWE NO!!!! DON'T DO IT AL!!! DON'T......" SNAP!!!! "Jim: OH HELL!!!! He just broke Rikishi's arm!!!! What poor sportsmanship by Al
Snow, and he's an ass!! He won the damn match, and now he's trying to just put the man out of wrestling."
"Paul: Well I don't think we're going to see Rikishi for awhile. " [The camera now close enough to the Television as we see the action that took place at New Years Brawl as we see Rikishi clutching his arm in pain on the mat as Al Snow stands over him...]
"Jim: He jumped off that top turnbuckle and came down onto that chair, and that broke Rikishi's damn arm."
"Paul: Al Snow has left, and he's left his mark on the XCW. The man is not to be taken lightly, and he showed us all that tonight."
"Jim: He did it by costing someone their wrestling career." [The tape is then paused as EMTs begin to work on Rikishi. From off camera the sound of laughter is heard. The camera pans onto the source as we see Al Snow.
Snow seated in the chair, barely visible in the dim light of the Television. A slight smile on his face<the first smile seen on his face since entering the XCW>]
Al Snow: I told you all what I was capable of... [Snow leans forward making his face more visible as the smile disappears from his lips..] Al Snow: But no one listened to me! I watched this match over and over, from the beginning when JR and Paul basically said I would be nothing more than a light warm up for Rikishi...to the very end as JR BEGGED me not to snap
Rikishi's arm like twig! Now because of you two and all the fans who thought I was nothing but a joke a man's career is OVER!
[Snow sits back once again..] Al Snow: I didn't want to do it! But I told you! I told you all I would be taken seriously no matter what I had to do! Now do you take me seriously?
[Snow pauses for a moment as the room sits in utter silence before he finally breaks it...]
Al Snow: Well it appears so because I have a Television title shot on Monday
Malice against Steve Austin. The man who won the title on New Years Brawl against RVD in a match many now consider one of the greatest matches in XCW history...
[Snow hangs his head down for a moment before lifting it back and looking into the camera..]
Al Snow: Well that is HISTORY! Austin, at Monday Night Malice you're title reign will come to an end. Just like after what I did to Rikishi you will take me seriously or I will take you out! Monday Night Malice will be the time...it will be my time...It will be...
[Al turns the television off bringing the room to a pitch black...] Al Snow: The Future! [Fades out...]
