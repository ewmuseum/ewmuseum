XCW Monday Night Malice (part 2)

December 24, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We come back from the commercial and Bret Hart and Lance Storm are watching the tape from last Friday. They keep watching and rewinding the part where
Shawn Michaels blows out his knee. Finally Lance Storm gets up and starts to walk to the ring...)
Jim: Can you believe that both men would have the nerve to laugh at an injury like that? Jesse: Well Bret has lost it...the man has lost it, and I don't see him coming back to Earth anytime soon. I mean Shawn Michaels has taken the one thing away that Bret never thought he could and that's his son. Blade Hart is HBK's #1 fan, and that can't sit well with Bret Hart.
Jim: Fans this next match should be a good one.... FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Red Hook, New York...weighing in at 221 pounds. Here is
"The Human Suplex Machine" Taz!!!
("War Machine" starts to play and Taz comes out with the black towel over his head, and a few guys carrying the "T" banner behind him. Taz steps up onto the apron and wipes his feet on the mat, and then climbs into the ring. He takes the towel off his head and just stares as he folds his arms across his chest.)
FINK: And his opponent...fighting out of Calgary, Alberta, Canada. He weighs in at 229 pounds. Here is Lance Storm!!!
(Lance Storms comes out to no music or fireworks. He has a very serious look on his face as he climbs into the ring. He gets up onto the turnbuckle and raises his arms into the air....)
Jim: This is going to be a great match, and there's the bell. Both men lock up in the center of the ring, and Taz goes for a Northern Lights Suplex, but it's blocked by Storm...Lance now tries a hiptoss, but taz is right back up.
Jesse: I don't think these two have ever met in the ring. Jim: Storm goes for an armdrag, but Taz isn't going anywhere. Taz blocks it, and Lance tries it again...this time Taz turns it into a short clothesline.
Lance goes to his knees. Taz with a cross punch to the bridge of the nose, and another cross punch.
Jesse: Those will knock your nose right out of place!!! Jim: Taz grabs him from behind...Tiger Release Suplex, and Lance Storm goes flying. This man Taz can suplex anyone he wants around the ring, and he's having an easy time with Storm right now.
Jesse: Well the question is...will he be able to handle someone like Vader in the ring? Is he going to be able to suplex Vader?
Jim: Good question, and we'll have to wait and see that. Kick to the gut by
Taz, and he goes for a Head and Arm Tazplex...and he hits it, and Lance Storms rolls out of the ring.
Jesse: Smart move by Storm...he needs to get out of there and take a little bit of a break. When you're getting tossed around like he is you don't have time to think, and this gives him that chance.
Jim: Storm slides back into the ring, and Taz goes for a lock up...Storm with a thumb to the eye though. Lance took the easy way out, and now he backs him into the ropes with a series of chops to the chest. Whip to the ropes....and Storm with a back drop!!!
Jesse: Whoa!!! Taz could've gone on a trip to China on that flight.... Jim: Storm waits for Taz to get up again, and he nails him with a beautiful dropkick. Taz makes it up again, and Storm with another dropkick. ANOTHER dropkick by Storm, and Taz rolls out of the ring out of frusration.
Jesse: Yeah that's not good. Jim: Taz slides back into the ring and both men are exchanging lefts and rights. It's turned into a damn sloberknocker, and we're LIVE here on Malice!!!
Storm gets the better of the encounter and nails the dazed Taz with an Enzugiri, and that about knocked his block right off his shoulders. Jesse: Do you think that Taz can make Vader tap out? Jim: I've never seen Vader tap out in my life, but I've never seen Taz get pummled like Vader loves to do. Lance Storm now with a quick run to the ropes, and he drops a leg across the throat of Taz...and he's going for the cover.
1!!! Kickout by Taz!!! Jesse: Lance Storm going for that quick win. The guy wrestles like he's been in the business for 30 years, but he's still pretty new.
Jim: Storm was trained by Stu Hart back in Calgary. He worked with guys like
Chris Jericho, Owen Hart...you name em' he's trained with them. Now that he's side by side with Bret Hart he's going to become more dangerous...
Jesse: Well Bret has lost his mind....Lance Storm should be careful. Jim: Storm now driving that foot into the sternum, and Taz is trying to get up. He's a fighter...that guy doesn't take no for an answer, he doesn't give up...he's a man that will get up after everything you throw at him.
Jesse: And believe Lance Storm when he says he WILL throw everything at him. Jim: Lance brings Taz back to his feet....go-behind by Storm, and Taz counters with one of his own. German Suplex by Taz, and he relases it and Storm falls back onto his neck.
Jesse: Whoa!!! Jim: Taz waits for Lance to make it back to his feet, and hits a double underhook suplex. He's going for the cover now...1!! 2!! NO!!! Lance kicks out of it...
Jesse: I wonder if Taz knows more suplexs than I know more ways of cheating? You know if he and I go together and shared our knowledge of the wrestling business we'd be unstoppable.
Jim: All your ways of cheating? Jesse: Of course. Jim: You don't think that telling people on TV that you cheat is wrong? Jesse: Hey I'm an ex-politician...I can say whatever I want, Jimbo. Jim: Well you have a point there Jesse. Taz brings Storm back up, and it doesn't look like Storm even knows where he is. Taz goes for another suplex, oh and Storm with a small package!!! 1!! 2!! 3!! What a quick....
Jesse: NO...Taz kicked out. WHOA!!!! SUPERKICK CITY!!!! Jim: Both men made it up to their feet quickly and Lance Storm with a superkick to the chops of Taz, and he's out cold now. Storm has one of the best superkicks in the business, and you're not going to be able to soften that blow.
Jesse: Shawn Michaels probably has the best kick with Storm coming in second. Well Shawn isn't wrestling right now...so Storm has the best kick in wrestling. Jim: Shawn Michaels was put on the self this past week on X-Treme. Bret Hart and Storm were going over the tape before he came out here, and I'm sure when
HBK returns there's going to be hell to pay for both Bret and Lance Storm.
Jesse: HBK can't handle both men by himself...he could barely handle Bret all alone. Jim: Well when revenge is on someones mind..anything is possible. Storm picks up Taz and whips him to the ropes. Taz comesback and Storm hits him with an elbow right to the face, and Taz goes down to his knees. Storm with a low dropkick to the other knee, and Taz falls to the canvas.
Jesse: That's harsh!! Jim: Storm now stomping away on that knee of Taz's. Over and over...he's softening him up for that Half Crab. Storm lifts him up and whips him into the ropes again, and Storm this time with a spinning head scissors.
Jesse: Is there anything Lance Storm can't do? Jim: Crack a smile. Taz is up again and Storm goes for a clothesline...Taz ducks, and TAZMISSION!!!! TAZMISSION!!!! Both men go down, and Storm is trying to break out of it. This could be it....
Jesse: Storm is tapping out!!! Jim: Taz has won!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Taz!!! Jim: Nobody can get out of that hold. Taz wins here tonight, but a great match by Lance Storm. You have to give that guy credit....
(Camera cuts to Misawa, Triple H and Chono who are sitting in their dressing room with their hats on. Misawa and Triple H are laughing at Chono...all of a sudden Kurt Angle busts into the room with a bunch of things...)
Kurt: Okay guys! You ready for the best Christmas you've ever had? Triple H: This is the only Christmas they've ever had. Kurt: Yeah....that's what I meant. It'll be there best because it's their first.... Triple H: What the hell did you buy? Kurt: Well I bought a little tree...it's plastic, and it's decorated, but it's still a tree. I've got some presents for you guys, and I picked them out myself while I was in the hall. And...OH...I bumped into Jake Roberts and he gave me some of his egg nog.
Misawa: Egg-ah Nog? Kurt: You mean to tell me you've never had Egg Nog before? Misawa: Egg-ah nog? Kurt: Pucker up buddy...this stuff is the best. (Kurt Angle pours Chono and Misawa two HUGE glasses of egg nog, and hands it to them. Both men take a drink leaving an egg nog mustache on them. Both men shake their head in approval. Misawa looks at his glass and drinks the rest down. He holds up his glass....)
Kurt: What? You want more? My you're a little piggy aren't you? Here you go.... (Kurt pours Misawa ANOTHER huge glass.) Kurt: Now right now I thought it'd be a good idea to sing a couple of Christmas
Carols. You know who the Carpenters are?
Misawa:........ Chono:.......... Triple H: What? What are you doing? Get out of here...do you realize that there's an important match that we have to talk about?
Chono: Egg nog!!! Kurt: You want more too? Okay hold on....Chestnuts roastin on an open fire,
Jack Frost nipping.....
(Chono and Misawa stand up in anger...) Kurt: Okay...maybe I shouldn't say the word nip. How about Jingle Bells? Here have some more egg nog, Mitsuharu!
(Kurt Angle hands Misawa another glass of egg nog and starts singing Christmas
Carols. Triple H gets up and walks out of the dressing room out of anger. The camera fades.)
