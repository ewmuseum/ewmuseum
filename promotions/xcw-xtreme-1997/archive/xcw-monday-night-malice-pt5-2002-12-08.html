XCW Monday Night Malice (part 5)

December 8, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we comeback the music of Godsmack rings through the arena. "Awake" plays as Jerry Lynn and Christopher Daniels appear on the ramp. The two take a moment to play to the fans before heading down to the ring...
Mere moments after they reach the squared circle "No Way Out" by STP starts to play, and the crowd expresses a mixed reaction to the team of Lo-Ki and Tajiri who materizlize from backstage. Tajiri and Lo-Ki only make it halfway down the ramp before the duo start to argue about something. Soon enough both men are
nose-to-nose yelling at each other. "Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck" starts to play and the Impact Players begin their slow march down to ringside, prompting Tajiri and Lo-Ki to slide into the ring where they are met by Christopher Daniels and Jerry Lynn. ]
Jim: And we're underway here tonight. Daniels and Lynn hammer Lo-Ki and Tajiri respectivly, shooting off their opponents after a few quick shots. Christopher meets Lo Ki with a hurricanrana, and Tajiri cuts Lynn off with a low blow kick!!!
Don: Nice!!!! [Tajiri takes Lynn down with a sharp savate kick to the jaw. Tajiri also hits a roundhouse to the back of Daniels' neck as Impact Players hit the ring.]
Jim: And there's the bell, James Beard is trying his hardest to get this thing organzied. He's leading everyone to their corners with the exception of Tajiri and Christopher to their corners.
Don: I have been wet about this match all day long. I don't think I've ever seen this much talent in the ring at once.
Jim: No doubt these guys are tremendous wrestlers. Don: Yeah, but you don't like them because they're not HOSSES!!! Jim: I never said that. [Tajiri lays into The Fallen Angel, and he works Daniels over into the sole neutral corner. Tajiri whips Daniels out of the corner with a snapmare, then applies a rear chinlock. Christopher fights up, but before he can fully free himself from the hold, Tajiri turns it into a side headlock. Christopher struggles, attempting to shoot Tajiri off, but Tajiri cinches down on the headlock, stopping the momentum.]
Jim: Tajiri not letting up at all, and you can tell Christopher is getting a little anxious in the ring right now.
Don: Ya think? [Daniels takes a swipe at Tajiri's leg, slamming him to the mat, yet Tajir still retains the hold, and Daniels falls with the quick man. Tajiri works his way back up into the standing position, but before he can take advantage, he feels a sharp slap on his back as Lo Ki tags himself in.]
Jim: That wasn't too nice. Don: Yeah I don't understand why these two don't like each other. Jim: Maybe it's something that stems beyond what we know about them? Don: Maybe one hates the other because he smells. Jim: Huh? [Tajiri turns, releasing the hold on Daniels, and confronts Lo Ki. The two argue for a few moments, not noticing that Daniels has recovered. Tajiri slides out of the ring, and before Lo Ki can react, he's taken down with a schoolboy for a two count.Lo Ki kicks out, and then hits a rake to the eyes of Daniels.
Ki picks Christopher up for a body slam, and Lynn, from the ropes, tosses an insult towards the small guy.]
Jim: Lo Ki turns around and hits Lynn with a right hand, but he doesn't see
Christopher tag out to Justin Credible.
Don: Here we go...THIS IS THE TAG TEAM!!! [Justin Credible hits a dropkick on Lo-Ki, knocking him back several feet, but not taking Lo Ki down. He tries another one, and Lo Ki hits Lynn's corner.
Credible goes to lay a clothesline on the reeling Lo Ki, but Lo quickly side steps the charging Credible, and tags in an unsuspecting Lynn.]
Jim: This should be interesting. Don: Ugh..Jerry Lynn. Way past his prime. The guy doesn't have a clue what the younger generation wants. Ya know?
[Lynn steps between the ropes, and shoots Credible towards the neutral corner, but Justin reverses, and follows Lynn in. Lynn hits the turnbuckles and leaps to the top, and jumps backwards, hitting Credible with a back elbow. Lynn pulls
Justin up, and sets up for Cradle Piledriver, but Credible reacts quickly, backdropping Lynn. Credible doesn't follow up, however, but rather tags in
Christopher Daniels, then bails to Impact's corner.]
Jim: And listen to these fans! Anyone can be tagged in this match even if you are tag team partners.
Don: Yeah well Daniels is going to make short work of Jerry Lynn. Don't you just love the Impact Players. I mean seriously...these guys are so damn good.
Now they're going to get two guys who are supposed to be a team and make them fight each other?
Jim: That's not fair. Don: Shut up. You're not fair...you're fat. [Christopher steps in, and Lynn recovers just in time to realize who he's now facing. Daniels then charges, but Lynn hits a Japanese arm drag, and turns it over into an armbar. The Fallen Angel rolls forward, then kips up, crisply reversing the arm bar into a hip toss. Daniels stands Lynn up, then whips him towards Lo Ki's and Tajiri's corner, following Lynn in. Before Jerry can make contact with the turnbuckle, he instead launches himself into a high cross body on the unsuspected Tajiri, knocking both of them off the apron to the outside, and Daniels follows suit against Lo Ki.]
Jim: And here comes the wild brawl. It was only a matter of time before something like this was going to happen. The ref is starting the count on the legal men in the match...
Don: And the IMP are loving every minute of this...watching, waiting. WHOOO!! Jim: Christopher Daniles jumps in and breaks the count up. He runs to the ropes, and is tagged by Lance Storm...not knowing though.
[Daniels flies over the top, dropping a huge senton bomb into Lo Ki, who catches the competitor and drops him with a tombstone piledriver on the outside. Jerry sees an opportunity, and he jumps onto the apron to climb the turnbuckle. Storm climbs the turnbuckles from the inside, intercepting Lynn, and spins Havok around into a super Nothern Lights Suplex.]
Jim: DID YOU SEE THAT!!?!? Don: Ugh ugh oh yeah baby!!! Jim: Ewe. Jerry Lynn is brou - DOUBLE SUPERKICK!!!!! Lance Storm makes the cover...ONE!! TWO!!! THREE!!! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPS!!!!
Don: WHOOOOOO!!!! [The Fink announces the winners and the ref hands them the tag team titles. Both men barely broke a sweat in the match, but they raise the titles like they did everything in it. The other team is fighting all the way to the back, and the crowd starts to throw trash into the ring.]
Jim: I don't think they're too happy about this. Don: Oh please...they just don't like the fact they have to leave!
