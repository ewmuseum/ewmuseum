XCW Monday Night Malice (part 2)

December 17, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans we're back with more XCW, and before the break we saw Triple H walk into the arena.
Jesse: Did that guy look focused or what? Triple H is ready for this match, and
I have a feeling that he's going to walk away the XCW World Champion. I mean the electricity in his eyes are just something that you can't take away. I'm looking forward to that match!!
Jim: I'm looking forward to this next match....Rob Van Dam will be taking on
The Hurricane. These two are very talented young wrestlers here in the XCW, and
I'm expecting a lot out of these two tonight.
Jesse: Don't you think it's a little odd for a wrestler to walk around acting like a Super Hero?
Jim: Not really Jesse...you walked around with a boa around your neck for your whole wrestling career.
Jesse: Good point. FINK: Ladies and gentlement the following contest is set for one fall. First coming down to the ring from Metropolis...he weighs in at 215 pounds. Here is
"Hurricane" Helms!!
["Stand back!! There's a Hurricance coming through!!" is heard over the PA and the crowd is going wild. Hurricane walks out from the curtain with his cape and super hero gear on, and does his "Hurri-Spin". He runs down to the ring, and the slides in.]
FINK: His opponent...from Battlecreek, Michigan. He weighs in at 230 pounds. Here is Rob Van Dam!!! [Van Dam's music starts up and the pryo from him is unbelievable. Rob steps out from the curtain and smiles at the crowd. He walks down to the ring without a care in the world, and he climbs into the ring. He does a jumping spin kick as soon as he gets to the center of the ring, and then walks over to his corner.]
Jim: And the bell has sounded for this one...and here comes Hurricane over to
RVD. What's he going to do? He's extending his hand...
Jesse: What showmanship by Hurricane. You don't get that very often in Professional Wrestling. Jim: RVD just looks at him and now he goes to shake his hand, but Hurricane pulls it away. This kid thinks too much of himself, and RVD thinks so too. Rob with a forearm to the side of the head, and he sends him into the ropes. Rob with a spinning leg lariat, and that takes Hurricane down to the canvas.
Jesse: Both of these men can move around the ring at such a fast pace...it's going tobe fun to watch this.
Jim: Rob now with another right hand to the side of the face, and he drives a right left into the side of Helms' ribs. Bodyslam by Rob Van Dam, and now he runs to the ropes...Rolling Thunder, but Hurricane rolls out of the way.
Jesse: That move looks cool, but is it worth the damage if you miss it? Jim: Rob Van Dam staggers to his feet and Hurricane takes him down with an armdrag...another armdrag...and Rob Van Dam is dizzy. Hurricane runs to the ropes and takes him down with a springboard dropkick.
Jesse: Great offense right there by Hurricane. Using everything around him to his advantage!
Jim: Hurricane with a chinlock now on RVD, and Rob breaks out of it right away!! Hurricane can't believe his eyes, and Rob sends him to the ropes...Monkey Flip by Rob...WOW!! THAT SENDS HURRICANE OVER THE TOP ROPE AND
CRASHING TO THE FLOOR!!!! Jesse: That's going to take it's toll on Hurricane. Jim: Rob runs to the ropes...FLYING SENTON SPLASH over the top ropes!!! This place is on their feet here watching RVD execute moves like this here tonight on Malice.
Jesse: This guy is amazing. He can almost do anything in that ring! Jim: Rob makes it back up to his feet and brings Hurricane up as well. Rob sends Hurricane into the ring steps, but Hurricane reverses the move. Rob crashes into the ring steps face first!!!! Hurricane rolls back into the ring, and where's he going?
Jesse: He's going up top Jimbo...he's headed to the top of the turnbuckle. Jim: Hurricane waits for RVD to make it up.......SUICIDE DIVE ONTO RVD!!!! Jesse: He caught air on that move. Jim: These Japanese fans are used to this kind of high flying, but I don't think they're used to this kind of high-quality high flying. Both men are down again, and these fans are going wild for this. Hurricane is on the floor staring up at the lights, and RVD is doing the same.
Jesse: You have to think with all these risks they take that they're not going to be in the business for very much longer.
Jim: I don't think so..I think their idea is win as much as you can, as fast as you can...and then get the hell out of the sport before you kill yourself.
Hurricane is up now and he rolls RVD back into the ring. Hurricane comes in now and he's stomping away on the right knee of RVD. We saw Van Dam hit that knee on the ring steps, and now Hurricane is taking full advantage of it.
Jesse: That's a great idea...if Rob Van Dam can't fly or kick he's not Rob Van
Dam. Jim: Hurricane now with a knee to the right knee, and he picks RVD up. Dragon
Screw, and that had to have taken the knee right out of the socket Jess. Did you see the knee snap right there?
Jesse: Yeah that's a very dangerous move, and the more I see Hurricane wrestle the more I like him. He is sadistic...and I like that in a wrestler. You don't see that very much anymore.
Jim: Helms now brings the injured RVD up and he sends him into the ropes...Oh and he catches him in a mid-air Diamond Cutter like move. He lifts RVD up and as he's coming down he applies the Diamond Cutter...what a beautiful move. He's going for the pin now, and this could be it....1!!! 2!!! NO!!!
Jesse: RVD might be injured but he still has a lot of fight left in him. Jim: Hurricane is telling the crowd that he's going to break the knee...how's he going to do that? He's going outside, and he grabs a chair. Hurricane climbs back into the ring and he swings it at RVD's leg...RVD dodges it. SPINNING BACK
KICK and the chair lands right in Hurricanes face!!!!
Jesse: Whoa!!! Jim: Van Dam is up and limping now...he drags Hurricane over to the corner. SPLIT LEGGED MOONSAULT!!!!! There's the cover, but the ref isn't in position. 1....2..NO he didn't get him. Van Damn is up again and this time he's going up top. Van Dam FROG SPLASH!!!!!!! FIVE STAR!!!!
Jesse: Look at the heighth!!! Jim: Hurricane MOVES!!!! Hurricane goes for the cover....1!!! 2!!! 3!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Hurricane Helms!!!! Jim: Hurricane walks away with a win today, and RVD is hurt. That Dragon Screw had to have messed up his knee, and the fans can't believe that the Hurricane has pulled off this upset.
Jesse: I like this guy!!! (The cameras cut to Rocky who is in his locker room. The crowd starts to boo at the sight of him, and then Triple H walks into the room. He stands face to face, eye to eye with the Rock. Both men not even close to backing down....)
Triple H: So you think you're just going to get over by helping Ono out? Is that is Rock? The Rock: The Rock doesn't remember inviting you into the dressing room. Triple H: I don't need an invite....I got my invite when you decided to team with Sonny Ono and the rest of those Japanese Assholes! Don't think for one second that you're going to get away with joining the other side...after I'm done with Misawa, I'm going after you. The whole damn locker room is going to go after you...
The Rock: You come into The Rock's dressing room, and you threaten the Great
One? You think that by coming in here that you can talk The Rock into joining you guys again? Never...The Rock says that I'm playing for a better team now. A team that has something that the Rock loves more than SUSHI, and that's MONEY.
Triple H: Well that money won't be any use to you when you're dead! (Triple H storms out of the locker room and The Rock stands there with a mean look on his face, and then raises the Eyebrow.)
Jim: Fans before the card we had a few dark matches....lets take you to Scott
Hudson who has the run down and the results....

He's wearing his suit and tie, and in the background you can see other wrestlers walking around.
The Undertaker just passes by, and then behind him you can see Edge talking with Hurricane Helms...)
Scott: Fans before the action here on Malice we had some amazing matches for this crowd. Let me run down the matches for you and give you a brief summary of what went down....
Tajiri vs. Mortis This match started off extremely good. Both men came in and started their own versions of offense with Tajiri getting the upperhand. Mortis though went to some of the moves that he made famous and took Tajiri down. In the end though
Tajiri got the win with the kick to the face.
Vampiro vs. Raven A match between two of the darkest people in wrestling. Vampiro and Raven came down to the ring, and before they had their match they decided that they would have a war of the words...both men talked back an forth until Vampiro got fed up and attacked Raven. This was a bloody brawl that went from the ring to the back area and then back to the ring. Raven did end up winning with The Evenflow
DDT. Christian vs. Shawn Michaels You would think this would've been a good match, but both men just weren't into it today. HBK rolls up Christian for the win!
