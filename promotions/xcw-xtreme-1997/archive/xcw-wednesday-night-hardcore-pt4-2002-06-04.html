XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 4)

June 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mene Gene: Alright thanks guys. Now, Prototype...you seem to resemble a man by the name of Sting. Have people told you that before?
Prototype: Yes they have Gene. Frankly, I dont see what the similarity is. Sting was an ugly, out of shape excuse for a wrestler. While I am the most physically conditioned wrestler in the world. Let me ask you something, Gene, whats sting doing now?
Mene Gene: Well I believe Sting is retired, and I'm sure if Sting was here right now he'd have something to say about that! People have asked me when you're going to get into the ring...instead of just talking about it.
Prototype: Nevermind me getting in the ring right now, speaking of sting, do you know what he'd say right now if he was around?
Mene Gene: Well I think Sting would say the same thing that everyone else in the crowd would, and that's you're all talk!
Prototype: No, His prayers, Scheme Gene! Sting wasnt tough enough for professional wrestling. Thats why hes on some power tour in south america, preaching to 3rd world countries. Because he heard the prototype was coming.
I'm here for the same reason every other wrestler is, this shitty world title of the XCW. Mene Gene: I think there are a lot of people in that locker room that will disagree with the status of the XCW Title. People like...Mikey Whipwreck!
Prototype: Mikey Whipwreck! *laughs* You see why the title is worthless, subjacent and SHITTY. *gets in genes face* If the only people that are in contendership for the title are anything like Steiner, Dreamer, Raven, or
Whipreck then it already de-values the title to WCW status. Not Ric Flair WCW status...but David Arquette status. And for your information, I'll be happy to get into the ring with any of them. I'll introduce them to the new breed of wrestler, gods holy blueprints of a man.
Mene Gene: I sure hope someone sets you in your place!!!! [Mene Gene forgetting where he is and who he's talking to pushes his finger into Prototypes chest....he stares down mene gene and kick him in the sternum hard, shoves his head between his thighs and lifts him up by his jelly-belly.
Sets gene on hs shoulders, quickly spinning in a 360 and drops down fast, executing a picture perfect proto-bomb on the concrete. Quickly stands up and looks at Mene Gene.]
Prototype: *points his finger at gene* Scheme -Gene, YOU RAN YOU'RE MOUTH LONG
ENOUGH. You dare insult the prototype? You better have a good proctologist because I just knocked you're teeth so far down your throat your going to have to pull them out your ass.
[Camera cuts back to JR and Joel...] Jim: What a horrible thing to do. Mene Gene is no spring chicken, and he went down hard. That is going to take him a while to recover.
Joel: I love Prototype. He is one of my favorite wrestlers, and when he starts to wrestle...things are just going to get better. I predict that once he starts wrestling in the XCW...it'll just be a matter of weeks before he gets that title.
Jim: Well he has to go through a whole hell of a lot. Joel: He will...heh...he will. Jim: Fans out next match is Stevie Richards taking on Tommy Dreamer. This should add a little more fuel to the fire that is the Tommy, Raven, Stevie fued that's been going on for the past few weeks.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall! Introducing first...weighing in at 247 pounds. Here is "Dancin'" Stevie Richards!!! ["YMCA" starts to play over the PA, and he comes out to a huge heel response. He doesn't seem to care though as he climbs in and starts dancing.] FINK: And his opponent...from Yonkers, NY. He weighs in at 259 pounds and is the "Innovator of Violence" here is Tommy Dreamer!!!
["Control" by Creed starts to play and he comes out to a huge pop. Tommy runs into the ring, and works the crowd a little more.]
Joel: And Tommy is sooo over. [Double grapple by both men, and Tommy takes him down with a hip toss. Stevie holds is back, and makes it up once again. Tommy grabs him by the hair and throws him over the top ropes...]
Jim: OH MY!!! Stevie slams his back down hard once again, and here comes Dreamer after him. Joel: Dreamer needs to give him a break. Who does he think he is? Jim: Well Dreamer is a fighter, and he's not going to let his opponent breathe. [Dreamer goes to the outside, he stands on the apron. He waits for Stevie to get up and hits a bulldog off the apron to the cement floor.]
Jim: HE PLANTS HIM!!!! Joel: That ain't fair...give Stevie some room. Jim: This is a wrestling match, not a knitting class. Joel: Thank you Mr. Cobra Kai. [Tommy searches under the ring, and grabs a trash can lid. He brings Richards up, and swings...he misses with the lid, and Stevie with a knee to the groin.
He grabs the lid from Dreamer's hand and nails him in the head with it. Another lid smash, and Dreamer falls down like a tree.]
Joel: Finally some justice. Jim: He had to use an illegal object! Joel: So? [Stevie throws Dreamer back into the ring and he goes to the top ropes. Tommy slowly makes it to his feet and Stevie comes crashing down onto the head with that lid.]
Jim: And it looks like Dreamer is busted wide open! Joel: NICE!!! [Stevie goes for the cover, but before the count of one Tommy puts his foot on the rope. Richards slams his hands down in anger, and brings Dreamer back up again. Tommy swings with a right, and misses...Stevie backs up and hits the
STEVIE KICK!!!]
Jim: Stevie has a hand full of tights...and he's got his feet on that bottom turnbuckle for leverage.
Joel: Damn!!! My monitor went off... Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! WHAT!? HE CHEATED!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Stevie Richards!!! Jim: Stevie Richards won in little time, and he's leaving the ring with a huge smile on his face.
Joel: Well you don't mess with the King Of Swing!!! Jim: And Tommy can't believe it. [Tommy leaves the ring and just as he's leaving another man walks through the curtain. Tommy can't believe his eyes, and the fans pop wildly for him. He's got an angry look on his face, and the fans start the chant of
"Benoit...Benoit....Benoit!" Chris climbs into the ring and grabs the microphone...] Chris Benoit: After a long time away from my lively hood the squared circle, I have come back to show the world what I have to offer. I will show each and every so called XCW superstar why I am the best technical wrestler in the world of professional wrestling!
Wrestling, yeah that's a word that gets thrown a lot. The media portrays wrestlers as nothing but a joke in society. Why, because guys like Vince
McMahon make it a god damn soap opera called sports entertainment. Then you have guys that like to prove their masculinity by hitting someone with a steel chair, thowing someone through a table or wrapping them in barbed wire. Yeah real classy, taking years off your life to please these vicious, blood-thirsty fans! And we all know who is at the head of that revolution yeah I'm talking about YOU Cactus Jack!
Mick Foley the "God of Hardcore" right? He gets all the attention everywhere from Japan, Europe, US and even Canada because he's hardcore while I get no respect in return! You will not make a mockery out of my sport anymore. It doesn't take a smart man to hit someone with a garbage can but takes a strong willed and disciplined one to train for years to be the best wrestler around.
Foley, one day all I have to get is hardcore JUST ONE TIME and it will be all over for you! I will rise to the top of XCW and I will be more than happy to use you Foley as a stepping stone. Come on Foley I dare you to prove me wrong!
[Chris throws down the microphone and walks away to some huge heel heet. Camera cuts to a commercial.]
