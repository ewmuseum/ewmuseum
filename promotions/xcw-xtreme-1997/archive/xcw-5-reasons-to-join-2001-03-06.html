5 Reasons to Join the XCW

March 6, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And here are the top 5 reasons to AT LEAST check out the XCW.... #5. NO MARKS! I know that most of you are probably sick and tired of joining some of these feds where the people who RP are nothing but 11 year old marks who think Steve Austin is the only thing out there. Yes this is a Real Wrestler fed, but we don't allow people who don't have talent into the fed. You will not be stuck competiting against some loser who can't do anything but "In-Ring"
Rp's. #4. High Quality Cards - I'll tell you this right now, and I'm sure a lot of you are in the same boat as I am. I hate joining a fed where the cards absolutely suck. If there is one thing that brings a fed down it's a writer for the cards. If it's "wannabe" humor, or it's that summary and two lines of commentary crap...the XCW doesn't do that. I work damn hard on the cards, and each one is so detailed and so intricate that you'd think that you were watching a real Pay-Per View. You're not going to get CRAP from this fed, and that's a promise. I will show you what the XCW has to offer.....
#3. Awesome Angles/Storylines - Have you ever been in a fed where you think that it's going to be great, the storylines are awesome...and then everything falls flat? The XCW doesn't do that at all, and as a matter of fact everyone who goes through the XCW comments on how much more fun this is as opposed to
WWF or WCW (Is anything worse than WCW though?). I've been involved with
Fantasy Wrestling since '96 and I know what is good and what sucks, and all that great stuff. I'm 21 now and I've taken writing classes, and all types of creative courses. The XCW will not be boring and I PROMISE you that.
#2. Not typical Real Wrestler Fed - Okay I know what you fake wrestler people are saying right now. Real Wrestler feds are for marks right? No...it's a matter of preference, and for those of you who haven't tried it...or have been in a crappy one have no right to say anything. You haven't been in a good one unless you've tried the XCW. I know there are a lot of mixed feds which is just crap, and then there are a LOT of AWESOME fake feds, but shouldn't there be ONE good Real Wrestler fed? You should at least give it a try...I mean prove me wrong. Show me that my fed sucks and that there is no business for a Real
Wrestler fed in the RSPWF.
#1. Such a good looking President - Okay just kidding... THE REAL #1. Fun & Friends - The title sounds cheesy and gay, but you want to know something cool...the people that I have known since I've started the XCW are still my friends today. They are a totally loyal bunch of people, and they would do anything I asked them to do. I can't think of any other feds that do that, and that is the ONE reason I want to start the XCW up again. I think the emotion that my cards and storylines give people is something that you really can't duplicate. Everyone always has a ton of fun in the XCW, and even if they don't like it after they try it they are still cool with me and we still talk from time to time. You join the XCW and it may not mean anything to you, but you'll gain some cool friends. XCW is a tight knit group...and I welcome you all to at least give it a try.
Those are my top 5 reasons to join, and I hope you all got through reading it without hating Real Feds. We're not that bad...it's just that the bad people have messed it up for ALL of us. You give the XCW a chance and you won't regret it.
If you have any questions please feel free to IM me or e-mail me. Thanks for reading and I'll see you in the XCW.
- Chad
