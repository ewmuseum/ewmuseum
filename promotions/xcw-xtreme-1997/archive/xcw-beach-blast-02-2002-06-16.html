XCW Beach Blast '02

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades onto the center of the ring where Dixie Chicks are standing.] FINK: Ladies and gentlemen here to sing our National Anthem. Please put your hands together for the Grammy Award winning, Country Trio....the Dixie Chicks!
[Crowd pops as they start to sing...] ## Oh, say can you see, by the dawn's early light ## ## What so proudly we hailed at the twilight's last gleaming? ## ## Whose broad stripes and bright stars, through the perilous fight ## ## O'er the ramparts we watched, were so gallantly streaming ## ## And the rockets' red glare, the bombs bursting in air ## ## Gave proof through the night that our flag was still there. ## ## O say, does that star-spangled banner yet wave ## ## O'er the land of the free and the home of the brave!!!! ## [Everyone is cheering, fireworks are going off...a HUGE American Flag is lowered and 2 Stealth Bombers fly by as the National Guard fire off their rifles. It's an amazing day for wrestling, an even better day for a Beach
Blast....fade to XCW logo...]
XCW [The crowd is wild on a Sunday afternoon, and Safeco is packed to the rafters with people. Fireworks are going off in the crowd, and there are thousands of signs being waved around. One fan has a lifesize cardboard cut out of Mikey
Whipwreck that he's waving around. We cut to Jim Ross, and in a nice change of pace...Jesse Ventura is standing next to him.]
Jim: Over forty thousand people here, and the XCW has never been hotter. Welcome to Seattle, Washington....welcome to Safeco Field!!! Welcome to BEACH BLAST!!! Jesse: Jim, I have been to the Stones, I have been to the Indy 500, I've been to the Superbowl...and I have never seen a crowd so loud in my life. This place is going wild, and tonight they're going to be seeing some action that IS the
XCW. Jim: We've got so many matches for you tonight...talk about an amazing card. What two men are going to end up in the Hell In The Cell match at the end of the night? Who will end up raising that XCW World Title over their head? Will it be Mikey Whipwreck? Steve Armstrong? Triple H?
Jesse: The possibilities are endless, Ross. Jim: We also see the return of Arn Anderson!!! Jesse: Who is going to be his tag team partner? The question that has been on my mind for the past 2 weeks.
Jim: We also want to wish every father out there a Happy Fathers Day. My kids are here tonight, and they're ready for this event!
Jesse: Yeah I'd like to thank my kids, Terry, Tyral, and Jane....I'm wearing the new boa that you got me. Isn't it lovely Ross?
Jim: It certainly is Jess! Jesse: Who is going to win the tournament? Jim: Well we're going to find out soon enough. Lets first take you to Howard
Finkel!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to XCW's Beach Blast 2002 ! Before I introduce the participants for the opening match, I’m here to warn you that there *will* most likely be rampaging talent in the stands in just a few short minutes. Also, videotaping, duplication, or unauthorized reproduction of this event without the express written consent of X-Treme Championship Wrestling is prohibited!!
Jim: Fans with that out of the way lets take you to our first match. This is the first round of the XCW World Title tournament, and "Rowdy" Roddy Piper will be facing the former XCW World Champion, Mikey Whipwreck!!!
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this is part of the 1st round of the XCW World Title
Tournament....first coming down to the ring weighing in at 245 pounds. He is a former XCW World Champion, here is Mikey Whipwreck!!!
["Loser" by Beck starts up and every one goes crazy. Mikey comes out with a smile on his face, and slaps hands on his way down. He walks over and Vin
Diesel gives him a high five, and then Mikey walks past Vader and flips him off. Whipwreck rolls into the ring and raises his arms high into the air as fireworks go off.]
FINK: And his opponent, from Glascow, Scotland. He weighs in at 240 pounds. Here is "Rowdy" Roddy Piper!!! ["Scotland The Brave" starts up and Piper walks out to a mixed reaction. He's got his "Hot Rod" shirt on, and he's wearing his Kilt. He starts walking down to the ring, and then stops to soak in the boo's and cheers. Roddy walks up the stairs and into the ring...]
Jim: And this should be a great match. Piper attacked Whipwreck every single chance that he got this past Wednesday.
Jesse: It's all about Piper trying to get the mental advantage! Jim: And Mikey is rolling out of the ring to jaw with Vader! [Mikey walks up to Vader and jaws with him a little, and Harley Race gets in his face and starts to yell at him. Mikey pushes Harley down, and then slides into the ring. Piper and Mikey meet in the center of the ring...]
Jim: And it looks like both of these men are ready for this match. Mikey and
Piper shake hands, and Mikey turns it into a short clothesline.
Jesse: Whoa, and what a great move there by Mikey. He's showing us from the very begining that he's going to pull out all the stops here tonight.
[Mikey brings Piper back to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Mikey goes for a backdrop, but Piper counters with a sunset flip.]
Jim: ONE!!! TW- no!!! Whipwreck kicks out!! Jesse: Now Piper isn't one to mess around. He and I go way back, and if I know
Piper he's going to be just as vicious in this match. He has never held gold here in the XCW, and he wants it bad.
Jim: Both men back to their feet and they run to the opposite ropes. High
Crossbody by Mikey, but Piper with a duck down. Whipwreck hits nothing but air...a roll up again by Piper. ONE!!! TWO!!!! Th - NO!! Mikey kicks out again.
Jesse: That was a close call right there by referee Earl Hebner. The guy has to be on his toes throughout this match, and not miss a beat.
[Piper lets Mikey get back up, and they both stare at each other. Collar and
Elbow tie-up in the center of the ring. Both men trying to jock for position, and Whipwreck stops that with a knee to the midsection.]
Jim: Mikey now with a swinging neckbreaker, and Piper goes down to the canvas hard. The former XCW World Champion now laying in with a series of kicks to the back.
Jesse: I've been watching how Mikey has transformed into an actual wrestler. Very interesting, because the more I watch him the more I notice someone elses moves. I just can't place it right now.
[Mikey grabs Piper by the shirt and tosses him to the outside through the middle and bottom ropes.]
Jim: And Earl Hebner doesn't like that at all, but Mikey doesn't seem to care. Mikey goes right out after him, and wait a minute. Jesse: He just stopped. Jim: Well he see's Vader in the crowd just sitting and watching. He's going over to him, and now Mikey is having words.
Jesse: What is he doing? Jim: Vader is just laughing, and Mikey is serious. Jesse: Why is he doing this? Does he realize that the XCW World Title could be his? Why is he messing with someone on the outside?
[Piper turns him around and drives a thumb into the eye. Roddy grabs Whipwreck by the hair and throws him into the ring post. The dazed Whipwreck gets tossed back into the ring, and Piper follows.]
Jim: What a mistake right there by Mikey Whipwreck. The man was in control, and he makes a dumb mistake by going over and talking to Vader.
Jesse: Well he's upset because he never really got a win over him. [Piper brings Mikey to his feet and executes a vertical suplex, and Mikey crashes down hard. Piper back up and he drops an elbow across his chest, and hooks the leg for the cover.]
Jim: Cover again...ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE...no! Mikey managed to kick out yet again, and Piper looks like he's getting angry.
Jesse: Well Piper is doing everything right at the moment. He needs to just keep working Mikey down..the pace is just right, and he's using the right moves. Just enough to keep Mikey frustrated, because that'll make him execute poor moves.
Jim: Can I tell you that it's so much nicer to work with you than that other guy? Jesse: Hah....you think? [Piper brings Mikey up once more, and he unloads with a series of punches to the body. Uppercut to the jaw, and Mikey slumps back down to the canvas.
Instead of going for the cover again Piper takes his time working the crowd.] Jesse: Now this has always been a flaw for Piper. The man doesn't know when to pin someone, and when to work the crowd. Get the win, get out, and work the crowd after you have the title.
Jim: True, very true. Piper needs to do something, and now he goes for the cover. One!! Two!!! Thr - no!!! Whipwreck manages to get his shoulder off the mat, and Piper complains.
[Piper gets on top of Mikey and starts slaughtering him with punches to the face.] Jesse: And listen to this crowd...they love Mikey, but they love Piper's brawling style too.
Jim: No doubt about it. [Piper brings Mikey up to a vertical base now and slams him down with a hard scoop slam. Roddy grabs Mikey and applies a Cross-Face type submission, and
Mikey starts to scream out.]
Jesse: He's got him in the center this could be it...this could be all she wrote here. Jim: Well Piper has that move on pretty good, and Mikey can't seem to reach the ropes. Jesse: You know Piper is upset when he starts using submission moves. I mean the guy knows only three, and this one is the rarest of them all.
Jim: Mikey scratching, clawing...doing everything he possibly can to get to the ropes, but Piper maintains that grip on Mikey's head.
Jesse: And Earl Hebner is right in there. He's checking every little detail, and you have to give the man some credit.
[Piper is screaming for Mikey to give up, and finally he lets go. Roddy brings
Mikey back to his feet, and shoots him into the ropes. While still holding onto
MIkey's hand, Piper hits him with a knee to the stomach. Piper follows up with a DDT, and goes for the pin again...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE....no!! Mikey has popped out, and this place is on their feet. Jesse: And now Piper is getting mad...uh oh. He's not thinking...he's going to get himself DQ'd.
Jim: Piper is un-doing that turnbuckle pad, and Mikey is recovering. [Mikey is up and charges Piper, Piper moves out of the way and Mikey hits the exposed turnbuckle. Whipwreck holds his chest in pain, and Piper spins him around.]
Jim: Piper goes pops him right in the face, and Mikey backs up into the turnbuckle. Jesse: This could be it. Jim: Piper sets Mikey up onto the top turnbuckle, and it looks like he's going to hit him with some move off the top rope.
[Piper gets about half way up, and Mikey kicks him down with a boot to the face. Whipwreck jumps down and reverses the situation with Piper being on the top turnbuckle now.]
Jim: What's Mikey thinking? Jesse: Piper needs to get out of.... Jim: SUPER WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!!! Mikey for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Mikey Whipwreck!!! Jim: And Mikey will go onto the next round. He's either taking on Tatanka or
Sho Funaki. Both men have a history with him, and it'll be interesting to see who he's going to go against.
[Mikey rolls out of the ring with his arms raised, and he laughs at Vader as he walks by.] Jesse: And Piper is still out. I'm sure he wasn't expecting that move. Jim: I don't think so either. Mikey leaves with one win in his hat tonight...now lets take you to Tori Wilson who is standing by with comments from Sho Fuanki!
[Camera cuts to Sho and TAKA who is standing by with Tori Wilson. Sho and TAKA are saying something to each other in Japanese, until Tori starts saying something.]
Tori: Umm....hi guys. So Sho, are you ready for your match tonight? Sho: Indeed. Tori: Do you think you have what it takes to get into the ring with Tatanka? Sho: Indeed. Tori: Ummm....is there anything else you can say besides indeed? (Sho and TAKA look at each other and then look at Tori's chest...) TAKA: Breasts!!! Sho: INDEEEEEED!!!!! Tori: Ugh...back to you Jim and Jesse!!!
