XCW Monday Night Malice (part 2)

January 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Curt walks into Mikey Whipwrecks dressing room while Mikey is getting changed for his match tonight.)
Mikey: Hey Mr. Perfect, how's it hanging? Probably perfectly huh? Curt: Better that yours, I can tell you that much. Now Mikey I was wondering if you could do me a favor. How would you like to be a star? We're in Hollywood, how would you like to be the next Steven Segal?
Mikey: Hollywood star? Save the glitz for HBK. Curt: Let me put it this way...How would you like to make more money than you're making now, but not get hurt?
Mikey: Hmmm... Tell me more. Curt: I have 3 producers lined up right now in my office willing to talk big movie deal to you. They'll give you at least...at the minimum 2 million dollars to just sign with them.
Mikey: I'm not making a goddamn movie. I don't do kung fu or big dramatic scenes or jump off a building while shooting a 400 pound villain. I wrestle, that's why I'm here Great offer, Curtis, but no. I've got all the root beer,
Poison albums and Boba Fetts I need right now with this World title.
(Curt gets right in Mikeys face...) Curt: Listen I WANT YOU TO DROP THIS DAMN TITLE TONIGHT!!! I don't care how it has to happen, but you will drop the damn title.
Mikey: I'm afraid I can't do that, sir. Over my dead body. And if that's what it takes, so be it.
Curt: I would love to see that. Mikey you're making a mistake... Just so you know... Mikey: The only mistake I'm making is wasting my time with a washed up wrestler like yourself. Get outta my way, I got root beer to buy.
Curt: Angle isn't in the Main Event tonight, but I've put someone in who is better than he is. Someone who you won't know until that bell rings
Mikey: I don't care. I'm always prepared, I'm the local boy scout. I've had more line-ups changed on me than tune ups on an old Chevy!!!
Curt: Mikey...you...will...not...be champ...for long. Mikey: Unable...to...make complete...sentences...argh! Shut your mouth, I've never been this confident in my whole life.
Curt: Yeah well your piece is showing.... Mikey: What the... Damn it... [Mikey turns around quickly] (Camera cuts back to the ring...) Jim: Can you believe the anger that Curt has for Whipwreck. I can't believe it...the man wants Whipwreck to lose that title so bad that he's changing the card around. Over and over...Whipwreck won't be able to keep up with who's wrestling tonight.
Jesse: Well Hennig isn't going to pull a Commish/Wrestler fued, I know that for sure. What he's trying to do is give the title to a champion who is worthy of it...not some punk kid that won on a fluke.
Jim: Fans lets take you to our next match here tonight. Nikita Koloff makes his return to the ring, and his debut in the XCW tonight..against Taz!!!
Jesse: I'm looking forward to this match... FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from the Land OF Mother Russia...he weighs in at 245 pounds. Here is Nikita Koloff!!! (The Russian National Anthem sung by the Red Army of Russia is heard all over the arena, and he comes out to a huge heel response. Everyone is chanting "USA"
as he walks down to the ring. His face has no expression on it, and he makes his way into the ring.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Red Hook, NY. He weighs in at 221 pounds. He is the "Human Suplex Machine" Taz!!!
(Taz walks to the ring with the black towel over his head, and the crowd gives him a HUGE face pop. He climbs into the ring and both men meet face to face.)
Jim: Nikita obviously has a huge height advantage, but that has never stopped
Taz before. There's the bell, and these two men are talking back and forth to each other, and Nikita looks like a bad man.
Jesse: I look at him and it makes me glad I retired, but then again so does
Taz. Jim: Oh and Taz just slaps the taste right out of Nikita's mouth, and he does it again....Taz is slapping Nikita like he's a little girl.
<All of a sudden Nikita snaps and punches Taz right in the face which knocks him down. Taz gets up and Nikita takes him down with a scoop slam.>
Jim: Nikita isn't going to take that for anything... Jesse: Hey where's Paul E? Jim: Taz has Paul E, locked up somewhere. We saw him last week take Paul E, and now nobody knows where he is.
<Nikita grabs him, but Taz goes for a belly-to-belly suplex. Nikita counters with a headbutt, and another headbutt.>
Jim: And there's blood pouring out of Taz's nose now. Nikita must have broken his nose, and the fans don't seem to happy about this.
Jesse: The impact on that headbutt smashed Taz's nose like a water balloon. <Koloff with an elbow to the back of the head, and a forearm that sends Taz down to the mat.>
Jim: Look at the power on that man. Nikita Koloff is just a bear, and I don't think he knows how strong he really is.
<Nikita gets a running start and drops Taz down with a clothesline, but Taz gets right back up...only to get knocked down again.>
Jim: Taz needs to start mounting some offense or the match is going to be over very soon. Taz makes it back up, and Nikita goes for a boot to the stomach...Taz catches him though. T-Bone Tazplex, and Nikita goes down.
Jesse: But it didn't hurt him Jim!! He is just suprised, and he gets back up. <Nikita runs up to Taz, and Taz catches him in a float over suplex.> Jim: Taz now with the upperhand, and the fans are loving it. Nikita Koloff is holding that back, and here comes Taz now. Taz with a right hand, and another one. Taz grabs him again and executes a Northern Lights Suplex!!!
Jesse: He's tossing him all over the ring. <Nikita tries to get to his knees and Taz drives a fist into the forehead...Nikita comes up with a thumb to the eye though.>
Jesse: Always will get you!! I don't care who you are, but a thumb to the eye will always work.
Jim: Nikita now lifts up Taz...GORILLA PRESS!! Jesse: He's benching Taz!!! <Nikita gets a running start and throws onto the turnbuckle.> Jim: What the hell kind of move was that? He tosses Taz onto the top turnbuckle, from a Gorilla Press.
Jesse: I don't know, but Taz is in a serious amount of pain. Jim: Nikita walks over to Taz like a damn bear. He drops a forearm across the back, and another forearm across the back.
<Nikita picks up Taz from the turnbuckle and draps his throat across the ring ropes.> Jim: My God!! Jesse: Taz underestimated this guy... Jim: Well you don't go into the match wanting to toss your opponent all over the place. You have to want to beat him down first, weaken him up.
Jesse: Weaken him up? Jim: Soften...same thing. Bodyslam by Nikita, but Taz counters with a small package. 1!!! 2!!! NO!!! KICKOUT by Nikita, and he makes it up to his feet, Taz as well.
<Both men start hitting each other with everything they have. Nikita goes for a huge haymaker, but Taz ducks and nails him with a lariat.>
Jim: Ta...wait a minute. PAUL E. IS HERE... Jesse: He's got his hands tied behind his back, and a ball gag in his mouth...but he's here!!!
Jim: Paul is in the ring, and Taz can't believe he got out. Taz goes after him... <Taz is chasing a bound up Paul E all over the ring. Paul E slides out of the ring, and Taz turns around into a.....>
Jim: RUSSIAN SICKLE!!!! RUSSIAN SICKLE!!!!! Nikita makes the cover...1!!!! 2!! 3!!!! FINK: Here is your winner Nikita Koloff!!!!!! Jim: Nikita Koloff wins his first match here tonight, and what an impressive win it was. I see gold in his future...
Jesse: Taz is up now and he sees Paul E, and he's running after him. Jim: PAUL E trips up the stage and Taz catches him. Taz lifts him up over his shoulder, and he's taking him back to the dressing room.
Jesse: That ball gag worries me a little. (Camera cuts to The Rock...who is being interviewed by Michael Cole.) Michael: Rock, tonight you go up against 3 other men. We don't know who the 3rd man is.... The Rock: Michael Cole...The Rock has only hard a few words come out of your mouth and he's already sick of you.
(Rock pushes Michael Cole out of the way.) The Rock: People are talking, people are talking about the Rock going heel. Are you turning tonight Rock? Are you going to Rock Bottom little Mikey Whipwreck so hard that he starts to grow hair on his peter???
(Crowd pops loud for that one.) The Rock: Are you going to Rock Bottom Flair so hard that his flabby titties fall right off?
(Crowd pops again....) The Rock: Are you going to Rock Bottom the mystery man so hard that he turns into a big disapointment like these suprises always do? Well the answe is YES,
The Rock is going to do all of that, but he's not going to change. The Rock is going to be The Great One and will ALWAYS BE the great one....until The Rock decides that it's time to hand over his title to Scotty 2 Hotty.
(Crowd laughs.) The Rock: Now the Rock is glad to be here in Hollywod, CA...he's filmed many movies here, but the bottom line is this. Tonight here on New Years Eve...The
Rock will become the XCW World Champion. And there's not a damn thing anybody can do about it.....
Michael Cole: But Rock...you're not in the Main Event anymore. The Rock: What? Michael Cole: Go home... The Rock: Huh? The Rock is confused.... Michael Cole: We're having this interview because we wanted to know what your feelings are about not being IN the Main Event.
(Rock just storms off.) (Rock walks off upset.)
