XCW Monday Night Malice (part 5)

December 23, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We comeback from the commercial break and we see a re-cap of what just went down.] Jim: We were about to see an explosion of epic proportion. Don: I say let em' go at it...let me get my Christmas present early damnit. Jim: I think so - ["Money" by Pink Floyd is heard and Lenny Lane makes his way down to the ring. He's got on a Armani suit, some Bruno Magli shoes, and a pair of sin glasses. His hair is slicked back into a pony tail. He climbs into the ring and asks for the microphone...]
Lenny: First of all I want say that this is the most disgusting arena I have ever been in. No seriously, and I've even been to Cleveland. This place reaks of homeless people, and fat losers who are too lazy to make some kind of honest living.
[Crowd erupts in a heel pop.] Lenny: Now I want to talk about something. It's bad enough that I have to fight that toothless bastard at the PPV, but it's uber bad that I wrestle him before the lights even go on in the arena. Now tell me who is in charge of that?
Seriously...who the hell is in charge of that? How dare you try to keep Lenny
Lane from wrestling at the first Pay Per View and the last Pay Per View of the year. You people need me there, and yet I get rejected. I'm not Mick Foley, I'm not some hardcore piece of garbage. I am a self made millionare, and the fact is that when I get into the ring with Mick Foley..when I get my hands on him
I'm going to beat him senseless. Mick kiss your ugly kids goodbye tonight, kiss them goodbye because this will be the last chance you get to see them. I should be fighting someone like Chris Jericho, or Shawn Michaels. Someone with some kind of talent...and I get stuck with Mick Foley. What has he ever done for wrestling besides make it an ugly, violent place? So Foley tomorrow night...I end your hardcore career. Love, leave it...but whatever you do, you live with it.
[Lenny throws down the microphone and his music starts up as he leaves.] Jim: Who does he think he is? The man isn't even on the undercard and he decides to come out here and say something to Mick Foley? Has Mick Foley even arrived tonight?
Don: I didn't even know Mick Foley was in the XCW! Jim: Fans we understand that something has taken place with the XCW US Title here tonight...Chris Benoit has up and quit the XCW, and now we're going to take you to the owner of the XCW...the CEO...Chad Costello.
Don: What the hell is he doing here? [Chad's face pops up on the XCW-Tron and he's all smiles...] Chad: Ahh it's good to be back, but you know I'm not out here for me. I'm out here to talk about a pathetic piece of Canadian garbage by the name of Chris
Benoit. Yes. You know who I'm talking about the former XCW US Champion. When he signed with the XCW we both had an agreement...he wrestles, he gets pushed.
Well in order to be pushed you must wrestle, and even though sometimes you must do the job...you still work. Benoit failed to understand the meaning of give and take...so I had to fire him. So now that leaves us with a vacant XCW US
Title...what must we do about it?
[Crowd waits in anticipation.] Chad: I think we should have a Ladder Match tomorrow for it...and Test will take on Chuck Palumbo for the US Title. I've spoken with both of these men, and
I assure you that they are more than ready to put on a show tomorrow. Give these guys a chance, and tomorrow night you'll be on your feet cheering for one or the other. NOW...lets get on with the Main Event.
[Chads face bleeps off the XCW-Tron and with that we return to the ring to see
Fink ready to introduce the Main Event. He begins the announcements, and DX's music starts to play. Shawn Michaels, Chris Jericho and Triple H all walk out to a huge ovation of boos. They climb into the ring and it's crotch chops all around.]
Don: Merry Christmas from DX...they're sharing their package with everyone here tonight. Jim: That's disgusting. Don: Now you drinking egg nog for the past hour and a half is disgusting. I don't know what the hell you like about that stuff. I heard that it's nothing more than elf jiz.
Jim: *Burp*...sorry folks. [With that "You Think You Know Me" is heard all over the arena and Edge pops out from the curtain. The fans going crazy and he's got his glasses, and long leather coat on. He stops right before the ring and waits for his partners to show up. All fo a sudden the snare drum starts up, and then the music. Goldberg marches out to the ring, and the fans explode into a "Goldberg" chant. Goldberg doesn't heistate and just slides into the ring. All of DX slide right back out in the other direction...]
Jim: And where is Brock Lesnar? He said he was going to be here tonight. Don: Maybe he's getting his nails done or something. Jim: Maybe. [Edge looks like he's going to start off against Chris Jericho. Both men circle in the center of the ring, and Edge with a side headlock. Jericho whips him into the ropes, and Edge bounces off. Edge catches him with a shoulderblock, and Jericho goes down.]
Jim: Kip up by Jericho though... [Jericho points to his head and laughs, and Edge dropkicks him down to the canvas. Chris slides back against the turnbuckle and starts to beg Edge not to attack him, but Edge continues the assult. Edge with a series of boots to the midsection, and he brings him back to his feet. He whips Chris into the opposite turnbuckle, but Chris reverses...Edge slides out of the ring, runs to the other side and slides back in.]
Don: That was interesting. Jim: They are going to have one hell of a match at the Pay Per View tomorrow. I am extremely excited about seeing that one take place. Both men with a collar and elbow tie-up.
Don: Nice!! Jim: Jericho with a boot to the stomach, and he plants Edge into the mat with a
DDT. Chris rolls over and makes the tag to Shawn Michaels.
[HBK and Jericho whip Edge into the ropes, and they nail him with a double clothesline. HBK drops a fist across the face of Edge, and then goes for the cover.]
Jim: ONE!!! TW - No a kickout by Edge. Don: Where is Brock? Jim: Well after what happened between he and Goldberg maybe he's not going to come out. Don: I wouldn't. Did you see the size of Goldberg's loogie that flew onto the nose of Brock? That could feed kids in Somalia.
Jim: Ugh. [Shawn Michaels brings Edge back to his feet and unloads with a series of fists to the side of the face. He backs Edge into the corner, and unloads with chop after chop. Shawn Michaels goes for a splash, but Edge hoists himself onto the turnbuckle, flips over and hits a Sunset Flip.]
Jim: Great move by Edge...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! Don: That was a close one there. [Both men make it back to their feet. Edge ducks a Superkick thrown by Shawn, he spins Shawn around and nails a nice back suplex. Edge crawls over and makes the tag to Goldberg and the fans go wild.]
Jim: GOLDBERG!!! GOLDBERG IS HERE....and Shawn tags out to Triple H. Don: Oh come on Hunter get into the ring and show this guy a thing or two. Jim: Hunter slowly gets into the ring, and both men meet. These two have a history, and a history of bad blood.
[Hunter and Goldberg start to exchange words, and H pushes him. Goldberg goes no where, and he pushes Hunter back. Hunter comesback and hits him with a fist to the side of the head, and another one. He plants a boot into the stomach, and nails him with a knee face smash.]
Jim: Goldberg goes do - WAIT he's back up. The man has gotten back to his feet,
Hunter turns around...OH!!! SPEAR!!! SPEAR!!! SPEAR!!!!
Don: Damn that almost broke him in half. Jim: SPEAR ON HBK!!!! Don: Okay that did break him in half. Jim: SPEAR BY EDGE ON JERICHO!!! Don: I have seen so many Spears since I saw the last Ron Jeremy Orgy. Jim: Goldberg and Edge stand in the ring alone as DX collects themselves. Listen to this crowd go crazy... Don: I don't think it's for those two. [Brock Lesnar slides into the ring and clotheslines Edge from behind. Goldberg turns around and walks right into an F5.]
Jim: F5!!! F5!!! Goldberg is down, Edge is down, and Brock brings the man back to his feet. He throws Goldberg over the top ropes, and he's going out after him.
Don: Brock and Goldberg are fighting all the way to the back. Jim: Edge is all alone, and Jericho slides back into the ring. WALLS OF JERICHO!!! Don: Edge is tapping!!!! Jim: The bell is being rung, and I can't believe what the hell just happened. Goldberg and Brock are somewhere in this arena ripping each other from limb to limb. Edge was left alone in the ring, and now all of DX are tearing him apart.
["Break it down" plays to the arena and Jericho and HBK leave to a huge heel response. For some reason though Triple H decides to stay in the ring...]
Triple H: You know this is getting God Damned ridiculous. I mean Taz has been taken out of the Main Event, Goldberg and Brock are off somewhere palying tag.
Is there ANYONE in the XCW that can take me out? I'm begging...no I'm pleading with someone. I'm sick and tired of being the best wrestler in the game...someone take me out?
[All of a sudden "Jingle Bells" starts to play and Santa walks out. Behind him are elves carrying a huge box to the ring. Santa climbs in and it starts to snow, and he's ho hooing all over the ring. Finally the eleves slide the box into the ring and they set it up right. Santa comes to the mic, and confronts
Triple H.]
Santa: Ho Ho Ho...are you The Game? Triple H: Damn right. Santa: I've heard you've been a bad little boy all year, but I have this present. They didn't leave a name or anything on it, but I don't know if I should give it to you or not.
Triple H: What is it? Santa: I don't know, and with that tone I don't think I should giv - [Triple H plants a boot into the stomach of Santa and hits him with a Pedigree. He walks over to the present and unwraps it...and begins to turn milk white, as he's met with the presence of a man who he wishes he'd never see again.]
VO: What's the matter there, Huntah? Don'tcha know that it ain't polite to take yer aggression out on jolly ole' Saint Nick?
What? Ah'm talkin' to ya, ya little mealymouthed sumbitch. [MONSTER POP from the crowd as the paper is torn away and standing in the center of the ring is none other than XCW legend, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin.
Austin's clad in a black t-shirt, with white lettering reading "Got Beer?" on his chest, stonewashed jeans, and solid black boots made for asskicking.]
SCSA: Ya might as well shut that big mouth o' yours, 'cause this isn't no damn
Christmas carol. Yer not lookin' at no ghost o' the past, present, or future.
What yer lookin into... are the eyes of the toughest SOB to ever grace an XCW ring. [POP!] SCSA: Ah've sat back at home fer long enough, lickin' mah wounds an' workin' on a few business deals. Ah was done wit' this here business. Stone Cold was ready to move on.
But then... somethin' happened. Ole' Costello decided to untuck his little tail and bring back the XCW. Now he knew the Rattlesnake was retired, so he tried to go out and get some new talent. Guys like yerself...
[Austin shows a sadistic smirk.] SCSA: But ya see, these guys like yerself just weren't puttin' enough asses in seats ta pay the bills. So he hit up Stone Cold's celly... threw a proposition on the table, and Stone Cold, bein' the businessman he is, gladly accepted.
But ah accepted on mah terms. [POP!] SCSA: And one of them terms... is an XCW World Title shot, right off the bat. [POP!] SCSA: So you listen up real good, son. I hope ya've been enjoyin' yer night so far. I hope that ya've been enjoyin' yer career, cuz in about one minute, all of that's about to change real fast.
For weeks ah've listened to you bitch an' moan about how damn good you are. Ah've heard you talk about how there's no competition on yer level in this place... Well, to that, ah just got one thing ta say. [...] LOOKATCHA! From havin' no competition, you've quickly found yerself face ta face with death. Yer face ta face with an angry Stone Cold Steve Austin, who ain't had no Steveweisers yet.
Yer about to go from the franchise of this damn company... to a stepping stone fer the Asskickin' Mobile that is Steve Austin.
And oh yeah... Yer about to give me my damn belt back. [DING DING DING] Jim: MY GOD IN HEAVEN!!! AUSTIN HAS RETURNED!!! AUSTIN HAS RETURNED!!!! Don: Wha... Jim: Hunter goes for a clothesline, and Austin ducks it. He spins him around and sends him to the ropes...THEZ PRESS!!!!
Don: Wha... Jim: Austin unloads with lefts, rights...lefts... [Austin gives Triple H the double bird.] Jim: STUNNER!!!! STUNNER!!!!! Don: Wha... Jim: Austin hooks the leg...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! We have a new champion!! Steve Austin has won the XCW World Championship!!! Don: Wha..WHAT THE HELL JUST HAPPENED? Jim: AUSTIN!!! HE'S BACK!!!! He's saved this damn fed!!! Stone Cold grabs the mic and says... SCSA: All you pieces of trash out there, listen up and listen good. Stone Cold
Steve Austin is BACK... Stone Cold Steve Austin is angry... and if ya wanna take Stone Cold's belt, yer gonna hafta pry it from my cold, dead fingers.
[POP! Austin chuckles, before looking up again.] SCSA: And that's the bottom line... [The crowd pops huge!] SCSA & CROWD: 'CAUSE STONE COLD SAID SO! [Camera fades.]
