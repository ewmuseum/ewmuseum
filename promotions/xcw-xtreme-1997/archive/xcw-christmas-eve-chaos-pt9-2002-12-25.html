XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 9)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Well Don, it's about that time... Don: Yes it is. Jim: What a wonderful day it's been here in Honolulu...I am glad I had the chance to come here and be apart of this amazing event.
Don: Me too. Jim: Tonight has been amazing. I mean we've seen an incredible TV Title match, an amazing US Title match...Brock and Goldberg was epic, and of course we've seen Mike Sanders take over the XCW.
Don: What about Shane Douglas and Kurt Angle? Jim: Yes the return of both of those men. Should add some problems to the already troublesome XCW. But now...now is the moment we've all been waiting for. Brock Lesnar will be taking on Steve Austin for the XCW World Title.
Don: Oh man. Jim: Earlier on tonight we saw Brock beat Bill Goldberg, and then he cost
Austin his match against Kurt Angle. This should be nothing less than amazing.
Lets take you to Michael Buffer for the ring introductions for tonights match... Michael: Ladies and gentlemen...are you ready? [Crowd goes wild.] Michael: I said...ARE YOU READY??? [Crowd gets even louder.] Michael: Then for the thousands in attendence, and the millions watching at home. Ladies and gentlemen....LLLLLLLLLLLET'S GET RRRREADY TO
RUUUUMBLLLEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Brock's music starts to play and he comes down to a huge heel reaction. Fireworks blast off behind him, he he slowly moves towards the ring. Brock climbs in and just stands in his corner...]
Michael: First in the ring at this time, weighing in 295 pounds. He stands at
6'4, he has already made an impact in the amateur sporting world. He become an
NJCAA Champion in 1998. He transferred to the University of Minnesota and promptly won the Big 10 Championship, but was a runner-up in the NCAA
Tournament. He capped off his senior season by again winning the Big 10
Championship and then securing the NCAA heavyweight title....here is the
"MASTER" of the F5!!! "The Next Big Thing" BROCK LLLLLLLLLLLLLESNAAAAAAR!!!!!
[All of a sudden the sound of glass breaking is heard all over the arena. The fans stand to their feet and Steve Austin walks out. He's got his black vest with the smoking skulls on it, his black tights, knee brace and boots. In one hand is his title which he is dragging with him...]
Michael: And his opponent wearing the black tights...he weighs in at 256 pounds, and is known throughout the world as the "Rattlesnake". He is a former
US Champion, a former XCW World Champion, and a former XCW World Tag Team
Champion....here is "STONE COLD" STEVE AUUUUUSSSSSSTINNNN!!!!!!!!
[Austin stops at the XCW walk way and tells Brock to come get some. Brock happliy obliges and goes out after him. Brock starts running towards him and both men start to exchange punches.]
Jim: Brock hitting Austin hard, and he finally gets the advantage. He lifts
Austin up and suplexes him onto the steel ramp.
Don: I don't think this match is going to last that long. You saw what both of these men had to go through.
Jim: Brock drops a leg across the throat of Austin, and he brings him to his feet. Brock brings him to the edge of the XCW ramp, and he pushes him off!!!
That's at least 7 to 8 feet...damnit. Don: And Austin cracks his jaw on the guardrail. Jim: Austin is holding that jaw, and Brock comes flying off with a shoulder block! Brock is in total control here, and maybe it wasn't such a good idea for
Austin to call out Brock...
Don: Well Brock is hungary...he wants this belt more than anything in the world. Jim: Brock sets him up...RUSSIAN LEGSWEEP AGAINST THE GUARDRAIL!!! Don: Now that was a fresh move right there. Jim: Brock slams Austin's head into the side of the ramp, and now he's bringing him back to the ring area. Brock rolls him in...
[Brock gets in and whips Austin to the ropes, and he charges in with a knee. Brock brings Austin back up again, and he whips him to the ropes once more. Austin comes off, and Brock hits him with a knee to the stomach again.] Jim: Something must be wrong, because Austin isn't fighting back at all. Don: Yeah that is kinda odd. Jim: Brock with a go-behind, and he executes a nice German Suplex. ONE!!! TWO!!! Steve Austin kicks out. [Brock goes to bring Austin to his feet, and Austin counters with a low blow. Steve takes his time getting up, but when he does he clotheslines Brock down to the canvas.]
Jim: Brock rolls back to his feet, and Austin with another clothesline. Austin mounts him and now he's unloading with lefts and rights. The ref is trying to get Austin off of him, and he's not about to.
Don: That's cheating. Jim: Austin finally lets go, but he starts stomping him now. Series of stomps to the chest, and he's up to at least 30 by now.
Don: Doesn't his leg get tired? Jim: Austin with the double bird, and another series of stomps to the chest. He brings Brock to his feet and throws him into the corner. Austin now with a stomp, and another stomp.
Don: Mudhole stomping. [Brock falls against the turnbuckle and the canvas, and Austin charges at him with a knee to the chest. Another knee to the chest, and he does it again.]
Jim: Austin is making sure Brock has a hard time getting up. Don: It looks like Brock is clutching those ribs... Jim: Not only that but he's been re-busted open from early in the night. Austin gets a running start and he spears Brock against the turnbuckle.
Don: That has to be hell on those ribs. Jim: Austin now with another boot to the stomach, and he pulls Brock out of the turnbuckle. Austin rolls him up for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout by Brock
Lesnar now...
[Austin gets up and throws Brock to the outside. Austin follows and he throws him into the guardrail rib-first.]
Jim: And...I think Brock is starting to spit up blood. Don: Ewe. Jim: And look at Austin...he's not showing any kind of compassion what so ever. Steve with all his might drapes Brock across the guardrail, and Brock clutches his throat. [Brock stumbles backwards, and Austin catches him with a back suplex onto the cement floor. Austin smiles and kicks him in the ribs once again, and then brings him back to his feet.]
Jim: Austin goes to throw Brock into the ring post, and Brock blocks it. Austin goes into the ring post face first!!!
Don: Brock just spit out a huge wad of blood. Jim: He's trying to fight the pain he has, and he tosses Steve Austin back into the ring. [Brock slides in and brings Austin to his feet. He locks him in a headlock and gets a running start up the turnbuckle.]
Jim: TORNADO BULLDOG by Brock!!!! Don: Brock can't follow up, his ribs are in too much pain. Jim: Brock crawls over and makes the cover finally, and that might have cost him the win. ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout by Steve Austin.
[Brock shakes it off and brings Austin up...] Jim: GUTWRENCH POWERBOMB!!!!! Brock holds on for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!! TH -
NO!!! Kickout by the XCW World Champion.
Don: Austin has been taken to school by Brock. Jim: He's not done with him by any means. He brings Austin back up, go-behind and he executes a Half Nelson Release Suplex...and Austin drops right on his head!!!
Don: What a move there by Brock. Jim: Brock drops to his knees and covers Austin...ONE!!! TWO!!! Another kickout, and Austin has a ton of fight left in him. Don't count the Rattlesnake out just yet.
[Brock slowly gets up, and brings Austin up with him. He lifts him up...] Jim: F5!!! F5!!! NO Austin slides down his back, and he rolls out of the ring. Steve Austin rolls out of the ring to catch his breath, and Brock turns around. Don: What the hell is he thinking? Jim: PLANCHA BY BROCK!!!! PLANCHA BY BROCK!!!! Don: NO!!! Austin moved.... Jim: Brock smashes right into our Spainish Announce team!!! Pepe: ﹐irada hacia fuera!! Juan: 。MIERDA SANTA! Don: I heard that!!! Jim: Austin now shaking off the punishment, and he grabs Brock from the wreckage, and he throws him back into the ring. Austin goes behind him and locks in a sleeper hold...right in the center of the ring.
[Brock grabs the ropes just in time though and Austin breaks at four.] Jim: Austin lets go, and he pulls Brock to the center of the ring once again. Abdominal Stretch...and he applies a rib claw. Don: What a move there by Steve Austin. You have to give him credit...he doesn't show it all the time, but his wrestling moves are outstanding.
Jim: Austin has that locked in tight, and Brock isn't going anywhere. Brock's ribs must have been destroyed earlier on tonight, and Austin has him right where he wants him.
Don: Brock can't even struggle he's in so much pain. Jim: Brock lets out a scream, but to not avail. The crowd is on their feet as they smell a victory for Austin. Here comes the ref to check on the arm of
Brock...
Don:....... Jim: It drops once... Don:......... Jim: It drops twice....and it drops....THREE TIMES!!!!! Don: NO!!! Jim: Brock raises his arm into the air. Don: Honestly who didn't see that coming? Jim: Brock switches up, twists and executes a go-behind. Just like that he broke out of the move, and he applies the Corba Clutch.
Don: OH but Austin kicks him between the legs....Austin with a kick between the legs. Jim: He throws Brock head first into the turnbuckle. Brock spins around, and
Austin catches him with a kick to the stomach...STUNNER!!! STUNNER!!!!!
Don: He's not going for the pin though. Jim: Austin isn't going for it. Austin begins to stomp away on the neck of
Brock, and he places the boot on the throat...grabs the to rope and leans back!!
[The crowd shoots Austin a mixed reaction to that, and he flips them off.] Jim: The crowd isn't too pleased with the tactics he's used in this match here tonight. Don: Do what you've gotta to do win. Jim: Austin brings Brock to his feet and whips him into the ropes. He's going for the STUN GUN...Brock ducks out of it.
[Brock lifts him up...] Jim: F5!!! F5!!!! Don: NO! Austin ducks out of it... Jim: He spins Brock around...kick!!! STUNNER!!!!! NO Brock's HUGE neck didn't let him go down. He kept his big neck up, and Austin falls down to the canvas.
Don: That's never happened before. [Brock rolls out of the ring, and looks under the mat. He grabs a table and sets it up inside the ring by the turnbuckle.]
Jim: Brock grabs Austin...what in the hell is he going to do? Don: He's bringing him up the turnbuckle with him... Jim: He's sett...he's setting up for the F5!!! Super F5 through a damn table. He's going to hit it... Don: No way... Jim: WAIT Austin is fighting out of it...Austin with a series of lefts to the face. Brock sets him down onto the top turnbuckle. Brock on the top as well...both men fighting for position.
[Brock goes for a Superplex, but Austin puts on the breaks. Brock goes through the table. Austin falls down, and covers Brock...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Don: He did it. Jim: Brock's plan back fired on him!!! Steve Austin wins the match!!! Michael: HERE IS YOUR WINNER...and STILL THE XCW WORLD CHAMPION, Steve Austin!!! [Austin helps himself up by using the ropes, and he asks for a few beers. All of a sudden the crowd pops and Austin turns around...]
Jim: TAZ!!! TAZ!!! TAZ!!! Don: Broken Arm, and all. Jim: He's got the title in his hand, and Austin is staring him down. Austin can't wrestle, and nither can Taz...what is going to happen. We're running out of time...
[Taz meets Austin in the center of the ring...] Jim: He hands him the title...he hands Austin the XCW World Title. That should have been his tonight, but look at the respect that Taz has. He gives the title to Austin, and the crowd is going wild.
Don: Ugh. Jim: Austin gives Taz a beer...so long everyone!! MERRY CHRISTMAS!!!! [We fade on Brock Lesnar walking backwards to the dressing room with an extremely pissed off look on his face.]
