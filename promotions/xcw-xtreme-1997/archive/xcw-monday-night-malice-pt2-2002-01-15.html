XCW Monday Night Malice (part 2)

January 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(When we comeback Ric Flair walking right into Chad Costello's office. Chad is on the phone and he puts up his finger to tell Ric Flair to wait a minute.
Flair takes that as a "I'll hang up right now", and does it for him.) Chad: What the hell was that? I was on the phone getting courtside tickets to the Lakers!!! You just blew my chances you blonde haired bitch!
Ric: LAKERS?!?!? LAKERS!?!?! (Ric Flair is right in Costello's face, and is turning red...Chad gulps and sits down in his chair as Flair is yelling right over him.)
Ric: You want to go see the damn Lakers, and you run a top notch fed? Listen to me pal, because I'm not going to take any crap from you. I'm Ric Flair, I'm not
Curt Hennig....so shut up and let me say what I have to say.
Chad: Well Fla..... Ric: SHUT UP....now Triple H is mine at the PPV. Chad: N... Ric: So help me if one word comes out of your mouth I'm going to break your nose! Triple H is mine at the PPV, and I want it in a Steel Cage. Do we got that? Yes or no...do we have that?
Chad: We... Ric: I asked for a yes or no...not some other answer. Chad: Yes. Ric: What? Chad: YES!!! Ric: Now who's going to deliver the message to him? Huh? Are you going to drive down to his house and talk to him?
Chad: No...we'll e-mail him. Ric: E-mail? No we're not e-mailing him nothing. How about I go to his house... Chad: He's actually in Colorado to see X-Pac wrestle in one of the independents. Ric: Where? Chad: Like right down the street. Ric: How about I go down there and teach those little punks what it's like to be a real man, and then I'll call that sonovabitch out and we'll sign the match. Give me a contract!!!!
Chad: Umm...I have to make one up. Ric: I'll give you 15 minutes to make one up, or I'll comeback and write it up in your own damn blood.
Chad: Okay. Ric: I didn't hear you! Chad: YES....Mr. Flair. Ric: Snot nosed little punk....remember you can't treat Ric Flair like that. (Ric leaves and Chad takes a deep breath and is thankful he's gone. Camera cuts back to Jim Ross and Jesse Ventura.)
Jim: I have never in my life seen anyone talk to Chad Costello like that. Jesse: Only his girlfriend!!! Jim: HAHA!!! Oops...I mean how rude is that? Fans our next match is going to be an interesting one. Billy Gunn will be taking on Jake "The Snake" Roberts! We saw Terry Funk try to light Roberts on fire this past Friday...is he going to try something again?
Jesse: I wouldn't be suprised. I mean all the wrestlers are trying to avoid him in the locker room because he just snaps. I mean he seriously goes crazy!
Jim: Well if your wife was DDT'd right in front of you, and you couldn't do anything about it then you'd snap too. Terry Funk deserves to get his hands on
Jake Roberts, and it's going to happen at the PPV!
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, led down to the ring by Chuck Palumbo...he is known as "The
Bad Ass"..here is Billy Gunn!!!
["Ass Man" starts to play and both men walk out with t-shirts that say..."I'm with gorgeous!" They're laughing and playing tag all the way down to the ring, and Gunn slides in to get a HUGE face pop. He works the crowd a little and then talks to Chuck in the corner...]
FINK: And his opponent...from Stone Mountain, GA. He weighs in at 260 pounds. Here is Jake "The Snake" Roberts!! [Jakes music starts up and he walks out with a sneer on his face, and a bag over his shoulder. He doesn't keep his eyes off of Billy and Chuck, and he slides the snake into the ring and then follows. Chuck and Billy seem a little scared by it...]
Jesse: I don't know why Gunn is afraid of Snakes...he sees Chucks every single night. Jim: He has a snake? Jesse: Yeah his name is "Pinky". Chuck plays with him every night. Jim: I hope we're talking about the same thing. Jesse: We aren't. Jim: There's the bell, and this match is started. Jake is just relaxing against the corner, and it doesn't look like he's taking this match very seriously.
Jesse: Would you? I mean Billy Gunn is basically the poster boy for NAMBLA. Jim: I'm not even going to explain what that is. <Gunn is telling the ref to get Jake up out of the turnbuckle, and Roberts comes from behind and hits him with a forearm. Jake throws Gunn into the corner, and starts to kick away on the stomach.>
Jim: Jake being the Snake that he is attacks Gunn from behind, and he's putting him down with boot after boot. Jake now with a series of jabs to the side of the face, and Gunn is trying to block it.
Jesse: Gunn needs to try to fight back. Maybe that will work. Jim: Roberts with an Irish Whip... <Gunn reverses the move though, and Jake hits the turnbuckle.> Jim: Hard hit by Gunn, and he follows in with a clothesline. Billy now walks over to Chuck and gives him a high-five.
Jesse: You think he's going to win a match doing that? Jim: No. <Gunn grabs Jake and levels him with a fist. Jake staggers back up, and Gunn takes him down with a scoop slam. Billy runs to the ropes and drops a leg across the throat.>
Jesse: Now that's how you win a match. Jim: Billy Gunn is extremely talented...and it's just about when he can get motivated to win a match. He could make a great career for himself...it's just a matter of when he's going to do it.
Jesse: I feel like you're reading me the "Ross Report". <Gunn whips Jake into the ropes and executes a Gorilla Press. Billy holds him up in the air for about a minute, and then drops him down to the canvas. Chuck gets on the apron again to get a high-five, and Gunn does it again.>
Jesse: This is going to make me sick. Jim: They're good friends...what do you want them to do? Jesse: Celebrate on your own time. Not for everyone here to see. Do it in South
Beach, Florida or something where this type of this isn't that big of a deal.
Jim: Friendship? Jesse: Are you blind and fat? Jim: ........ <Jake comes from behind the celebrating Gunn and spins him around...and hits him with a thumb to the eye. Jake then drives a knee to the stomach, and executes an arm wringer. Gunn screams out in pain...>
Jesse: Gunn knows what that's like....I mean he screamed that loud the last time Chuck ran out of KY.
Jim: STOP IT!!!! Jesse: WHAT? Jim: Nevermind...Jake has that armbar locked in, and Gunn is trying to escape it. Roberts has it locked it good though. Talk about heated fueds, talk about one angry match...Funk and Roberts are going to turn the ring into World War 3.
Jesse: Oh yeah! Jim: Funk has declared the match a Barbed Wire match, and it'll be just that. Will Jake Roberts be able to handle that? Jesse: No problem. Jim: There is going to be blood, blood, and more blood in the match and I promise you that someone is going to walk away and the other person will be taken by ambulence.
Jesse: Funk is going to be that man...Jake is just too sadistic. <Gunn is about to break the armbar, and Jake pulls him down to the ground by his hair. The ref totally see's it and makes Jake break the hold, and Jake just smiles and steps back.>
Jesse: See what I mean? Imagine what it's going to be like when there are NO rules at all, and Jake can use sharp objects. I mean the images in my head are making me sick.
Jim: Well Terry Funk is a fighting man, and you can bet that he's not going to come just to play around. Terry Funk is going to be on a mission, and you can ask anyone...Flair, Hogan, anybody. When he's on a mission...it's over.
Jesse: The match is going to be a classic that's for sure. <Gunn makes it to his feet and Jake hits him with a high knee to the face. Gunn goes down again, and Chuck Palumbo gets up onto the ring apron.>
Jim: Get him off!!! Jesse: That's Gunn's job! <Jake walks over and slaps him in the face. Chuck then comes into the ring, and as the ref is dealing with him...Jake walks over and starts to choke out Gunn.>
Jim: That sneaky SOB!!! Jesse: Sneak and a snake but a smart one right? Give the man some credit!!! Jim: And Chuck just won't leave the ring.... Jesse: HAHA!!! <Palumbo breaks through the ref, and he tackles Jake Roberts and starts hitting him with lefts and rights. The ref is calling for the bell, and Gunn is up and both men start to work Jake over.>
Jim: And Palumbo, and Gunn have had enough. Both men are working over Roberts....HERE COMES FUNK!!! He's got the Branding Iron, and it's on fire!!!! Jesse: Run for your life Jake!!!! <Palumbo and Gunn see Terry in the ring and they're holding Roberts for him. Jake has a look of confident fear on his face, and he's telling Funk to do it.>
Jim: Terry is going to do it!!! Do it for your wife!!! Do it for your family!!!
Jesse: Wait a minute...Terry is turing around. He's walking away.... Jim: No he's not...he's pointing to the Snake in the bag. Don't tell me... Jesse: He's going to smoke the snake... <Terry is pointing to the bag, and the crowd is going wild. Funk drags the bag to the center of the ring so Jake can see it, and he puts the Flaming Branding
Iron into the bag....>
Jim: OH NO!!!!! He's burning the snake!!! He's burning Jake's snake.... Jesse: This isn't right!!! Jim: Jake isn't letting any emotion show on his face. He's not going to give in, and this place is erupting here. Funk has finally gotten some revenge!!!
Jesse: In a cheap way!!! Jim: Funk is getting on the mic now... <Terry grabs the microphone and gets right in Jake's face...> Terry: This is just the icing...we haven't even gotten to the cake yet. Sit and ponder this Jake...if you didn't DDT my wife and me....then Palumbo and Gunn wouldn't have a pair of Snake Skin boots to walk around in!!! Take the bag boys...it's all yours.
<Terry hits Jake in the head with the microphone...> Jim: Oh hell that knocked Jake out!!!! Jesse: Man there's going to be all types of hell to pay when Jake wakes up!!! Jim: Palumbo and Gunn walk to the back with Funk...and they just got themselves a pair of Snake Skin boots.
["Come Out And Play" by Offspring blasts over the arena sound system as Raven emerges from the back. The crowd boos loudly as he strolls down the aisle, eyes dead. He slides into the ring and holds his arms out straight, turning slowly as the crowd continues to show their dislike. He then heads for the nearest turnbuckle and slumps down at the bottom, elbows resting on his knees. He holds his arm out to signal for a mic, and it is quickly fetched. He holds it close to his mouth and waits for the crowd noise to subside before he begins.]
Raven - "I look around and see what you don't. While all of you live in your worlds of security, I dwelve in the land of uncertainty. Many of you would love to be free, to be your own. You would love to be like Raven, but you're scared to walk the edge, that you might fall into the abyss. You cower away, and live your fantasies and dreams through what you wish to see here tonight."
[Raven pauses, the crowd awkwardly silent. A few "Raven Sucks!" chants are heard in the background.]
Raven - "You cheer when men like me get slammed through tables, tossed into fire, and thrown from tops of cages..."
[Crowd pop.] Raven - "But when when the winner is decided and the fight is over, you forget about who you just saw and take your frail little minds back to the likes of those who hold the belts."
[Crowd breaks out into a mixed chant of "MIKEY!" and "RVD!"] Raven - "One man stands out in my mind as the absolute epitome of who you idiots ignorantly cheer to no extent. He pretends to care for you, tells you to take your vitamins, say your prayers, and lift your weights. He acts like he feeds from your cheering and screaming."
[Huge crowd pop as the description of only one man is made by Raven.] Raven - _Hulk Hogan_, you are a prime example of the political, underminding man that I will destroy! You can take your _HULKAMANIA_ and see how far that gets you! I will show you that men like me _USE_ men like you to get where their destiny is... the XCW Title! You can rip your yellow shirt off and flex your pythons in the ring, but when I find you and get ahold of you, your world will suddenly come crashing down. You have _NEVER_ met a man like me, a man like Raven. I excel where many fail. I capitalize on weaknesses that you don't even know you have. _IF_ and _WHEN_ you come to the XCW and meet my challenge, you will end up my first victim, and by far not the last."
[Raven pauses as the crowd boos his promises.] Raven - "Quoth the Raven... nevermore!" [Raven drops the mic and rolls out the ring. He then heads back up the ramp in the same manner that he came down it with.]
