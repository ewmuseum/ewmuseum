XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 3)

June 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Whatelse is going to happen here tonight? Shane Douglas wants to comeback to the XCW, and Arn Anderson stright out rejects him?
Joel: Well that's a HUGE mistake. Does he realize what Shane Douglas can do for the XCW?? During XCW's Golden Years we saw Douglas carry the fed on his back, and it's a dumb move on Arn Anderson!
Jim: Fans our next match is a three way dance, and it pits Sho Funaki, Tatanka and Mickey Whipwreck against each other.
Joel: Sho has broken out into a huge superstar here in the XCW, and tonight I expect him to breakout even more.
Jim: Well never ever count out Mikey Whipwreck. The man has done so much in his time here in X-Treme...
Joel: Oh yeah, and then there's Tatanka. Jim: Oh yeah. FINK: This contest is a 3 way dance!!! First coming down to the ring weighing in at 260 pounds. Here is Tatanka!
[Tatanka comes down to "Click Click Boom" and the fans are puzzled. He's not wearing his natvie American gear...but just some jeans, and no shirt. He climbs in and works the crowd, but he gets more boo's the cheers]
FINK: His opponent weighing in at 220 pounds. Here is one half of Kaientai. "The Whole F'n Sho"....Sho Funaki!!! ["INEEEEEED" is heard all over the arena, and the crowd gives him a huge heel response. He comes out with his "Whole Fn Show"? on the front, and on the back it says..."INDEED!!". He slides into the ring and works the crowd.]
FINK: Their opponents...weighing in at 245 pounds. He is from New York City,
New York...here is Mikey Whipwreck!!!
["Loser" starts to play and Mikey comes down to the ring with a huge ovation. He's got a smile on his face, and slaps the fans hands. He climbs into the ring and gets right in Tatanka's face...]
Jim: And the ref is now putting them at their seperate corners... Joel: This is going to be a great match here tonight! Jim: No doubt about it. [All three men circle each other, and Sho stops...he looks around and extends his hand to both men. Tatanka and Mikey seem a little puzzled, and Tatanka is the first one to shake. Mikey still looking to the crowd...and he says no.]
Jim: Mikey doesn't want any part of that friendship. Joel: Why? Mikey has no friends anyway! [Tatanka and Sho back Mikey into the corner, Mikey starts to fight. Mikey hits
Tatanka with a left hand, Sho gets hit with a right hand. Mikey is holding both men off, and single leg dropkicks both men.]
Jim: Both men go down!!! Mikey is a fighting animal. Joel: Animal? No way! [Mikey waits for both men to rise, and he takes them down with a double clothesline. Sho rolls out of the ring, and that leave Mikey and Tatanka. The native America gets on his knees and begs Mikey to stop...]
Jim: And Tantanka is asking for forgiveness. Joel: He's faking it. Jim: And Mikey with a kick to the midsection. He drives a right hand into the face of Tatanka, and he brings him up again.
[Mikey whips Tatanka into the ropes, and plants a boot into the stomach. Mikey hits a jumping DDT, and goes for the cover.]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!! Sho rolls in and breaks the count with a legdrop across the back of the neck.
Joel: Anyone can win this match tonight by pinning anyone. Jim: Sho Funaki now brings Mikey up and drives a fist into the throat, and another fist to the throat. Mikey topples over, and he's gasping for air.
Joel: And Sho goes after Tatanka now. [Sho brings him up and bodyslams him down to the canvas. Sho jumps up to the second turnbuckle and hits a leaping fistdrop to the side of the face. Mikey gets up and spins Sho around...he hits a Superkick!]
Jim: WHOA!!! Sho connects and knocks Mikey back down with a beautiful superkick, and this man is in control of both men early.
Joel: I can't believe what we're seeing from him. Jim: Sho now whips Tatanka into the ropes, leapfrog by Sho...Tatanka catches him though and turns it into a powerslam!!! What a move!!!
[Tatanka gets up and starts to stomp away on the head of Sho. Mikey comes over and helps Tatanka out. Both men kicking Sho's head like a soccer ball, and the crowd pops to their feet.]
Jim: This crowd is loving it!!! Joel: OH!!! Tatanka just hit Mikey with a lariat!!! [Tatanka drops a leg across Mikey's throat, and then goes for the pin.] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THR- NO...Sho once again saves Mikey from being pinned. Joel: He didn't save him goof...he just wants to win the match. Jim: He still helped Mikey... Joel: No he was helping himself. [Tatanka and Sho exchange lefts and rights, and Tatanka gets the advantage. He charges at Sho, and Fuanki hits a backdrop which knocks Tatanka to the cement.
Mikey charges Sho and takes him out with a clothesline over the top ropes...Mikey goes with him, and the crowd goes wild.]
Jim: All men are on the floor, and this is going to get ugly!!! Joel: NICE!!!! Jim: All three men are trying to recover, and Tatanka is the first one up. He grabs Sho and whips him into the guardrail. Mikey comes from behind!!!
[Mikey spins Tatanka around and rakes his eyes. Whipwreck slams his head onto the apron, and takes him down with a back suplex. Mikey gets up and brings
Tatanka up again...he throws him into the guardrail, and Tatanka smashes into
Sho.]
Jim: And Sho was recovering, and now he has to start from scratch again. Joel: This Mikey Whipwreck is like a fly. He is always around getting into something, and just when you think that he's done...he comesback.
Jim: Well that's why we is a former XCW World Champion, and that is why he's in the tournament.
Joel: How annoying is that? [Mikey rolls back into the ring, and he runs to the ropes...] Jim: What's Mikey going to do? Joel: Ugh... [Mikey hits a tope on both men, and all of them are wiped out once again.] Jim: Mikey hits a tope, and all three men are down and out once again. Joel: Talk about wanting to win a match... Jim: All three men showing the desire to win. [Tatanka slowly crawls over to the time keepers table, and grabs the steel chair. Mikey and Sho are using that guardrail to make it back up, and Tatanka walks over and slams the steel chair into Whipwrecks back.]
Jim: And Mikey flips over onto the other side of the guardrail..he lands right onto that large women.
Joel: I bet you she's prayed for that moment all her fat life. Jim: Be nice. Joel: I am!!! [Tatanka turns around and Sho takes him over with a hurricanrana on the concrete!] Jim: WOW did you see that? Sho Fuanki just took down Tatanka with a beautiful hurricanrana, and his head smashed into the concrete!!!
Joel: That can kill someone!! What the hell is he thinking? Idiot. Jim: Well he is evil!!! [Sho rolls Tatanka back into the ring, and he goes up top.] Jim: He's giving the sign for the ShoStopper!!! That's his Flying Senton Splash, and it could be all over... Joel: WAIT!! [Mikey leaps up onto the ring apron, and pushes Sho Funaki onto the canvas. Mikey now gets up onto the turnbuckle..and he gives the sign for the "ShoStopper"!] Jim: Not to be outdone...Mikey goes up top. Flying SENTON!!!! Joel: No!!! Tatanka and Sho move out of the way and Mikey hits nothing but canvas!!! [Sho makes the cover on Mikey's sternum, and Tatanka makes the cover onto
Mikey's legs. The ref makes the count...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!! THR - Joel: Why'd the ref stop?? Jim: Well, who...who would win? Joel: He's tapping both men on the shoulder, and they are celebrating. They both think they won!!!
Jim: Sho and Tatanka are now up, and they're pushing each other. They still think they won the match.
[Mikey gets up and school boys Sho...] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Tatanka kicks Mikey in the back, and brings him to his feet. Tatanka tosses Mikey out of the ring!!!
Joel: Tatanka grabs Sho...he's got him between his legs. Jim: What's he going to do? He's hooked those arms....TIGER DRIVER '91!!!! Joel: WHAT!?!?! OH MY GOD SHO'S NECK!!!!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!!! [Just as the three count hits Mikey rolls into the ring, stands up and realizes he didn't get there in time.]
FINK: Here is your winner....TATANKA!!!! Jim: Tatanka has just pulled a move off here that hasn't been performed in ages!!! Joel: What the hell is that move??? Jim: It's a Japanese Wrestling move used by...should I even mention his name? Joel: SAY IT!!! Jim: Umm...Mit...Mitsuharu Misawa! [Tatanka gets up and Sho rolls out of the ring. Mikey is still standing there in disbelief, and Tatanks extends his hand.]
Jim: Oh come on!!! Does Tatanka really think Mikey is that dumb? Joel: Just let them shake. Jim: Mikey doesn't want to... [Tatanka asks for the microphone...] Tatanka: Listen...Mikey, I am sorry for not being there during the tag match. You and I can work as a tean, we can work together to get rid of crap like Sho
Funaki. So just shake my hand...
[Mikey looks to the crowd and they all pop in approval, and Mikey hesitantly shakes his hand. Both men walk to the back together...]
Jim: Fans right now lets take you to Mene Gene who is standing by with comments in the back with a new wrestler coming to the XCW...Prototype!!
Joel: YES!!!!
