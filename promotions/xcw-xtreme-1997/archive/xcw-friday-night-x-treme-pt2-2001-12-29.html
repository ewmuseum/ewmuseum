[The XCW house speakers shriek out something that can only be compared to that of a Dolby sound test, capturing the audiences attention, and leaving them in wonder at the same time. The XCW-Tron comes to life, capturing the outlined silhouette of a man, standing with his hands on his hips. The outline of the individual gleams in a bright purple light, almost stemming from his torso.
The crowd's not exactly sure what to think ... ] Joey: Well...this isn't in th.... [Joey is cut off by a swirling whirlwind of color, which finally comes together to form a yin yang symbol overlapping the shadowed figure on the screen.
Outlined in a thin gold bar, the black and white entangle in one another till a dragon head is embodied on the XCW-Tron. The crowd gives mixed reaction. The silhouetted figure raises it's arms up, thumbs erect, and points at it's shoulders, nodding it's head. The crowd lets out a slight roar as the words
"THE RETURN" flash across the screen while the opening rift of Pantera's 'Walk'
plays in the background, fading out as the XCW-Tron fades to black. ] Joey: What the hell was that? Bobby: I don't know, but it looks like Rip Torn is coming to the XCW!! All those colors, and whirlwind of color...it's like a Trix commercial.
Joey: How do you come up with this stuff? Bobby: Kid when you're as good as the Brain...it doesn't come up, it just comes out. Joey: You know Joel Gertner has that same problem, but it's not with his mouth. Bobby: Are you trying to be funny kid? Shut up...I'll tell the jokes around here you just call the damn matches.
Joey: Eh...lets take you to our next match. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first coming down to the ring. From Victoria, Texas...he weighs in at 256 pounds. Here is "Stone Cold" Steve Austin!!
(The sound of glass breaking his head and Austin storms out to a huge ovation. The crowd is going wild as he climbs into the ring. You can tell that Steve really doesn't want to have anything to do with the fans, but he also just doesn't give a damn.)
FINK: And his opponent...weighing in at 450 pounds. Led down to the ring by
Paul E. Dangerously. Here is "The man they call"....Vader!!!
("What time...it's time...it's Vader time!" is heard all over the arena, and
Vader walks out to a huge heel pop. Paul E takes the rear like always, and
Vader looks like a man on a mission. He climbs into the ring and Austin attacks him right away...)
Joey: Austin with a right hand, and another right...but Vader comesback with a huge right hand of his own and that stops Austin in his tracks. Austin comesback at him though, and Vader with a stiff clothesline!!!
Bobby: That's like running into a tree. Joey: Vader stands over Austin, and Austin scoots back to the ropes. He helps himself back up, and this is going to be an amazing encounter...Vader tells
Austin to bring it, and Austin flips him off.
Bobby: Smart move...make the big guy angry! Joey: Austin is taunting him, and Vader just stands there with that monster look on his face. Both men lock up, and Vader quickly breaks the tie-up.
<Uppercut to the stomach, and another uppercut to the stomach. Vader with a swinging punch to the side of the face, and this time he uses his left hand.
Vader with two jabs, and he backs Austin into the corner.> Joey: This is like fighting a bear!!! Bobby: It's like fighting your mother-in-law at a Christmas dinner....nobody gets to those mashed potatoes before she does.
Joey: Vader pummels Austin in the corner.... <Vader brings Austin out of the corner and drops him down with a bodyslam.>
Joey: Plants him into the mat with that, and he drops an elbow across the throat. This man is fast for his size...Vader can move, he can fly a little....he's just a beast.
Bobby: And Paul E, couldn't be happier. Joey: Last week Paul E was suplexed out of his shoes by Taz, and Vader never got ahold of him. Vader with another elbow to the throat.
<Vader brings Austin to his feet.> Joey: Vader lifts him up...CHOKESLAM by the big man!!! Remember that Curt
Hennig has issued a bounty on the head of Vader. The man who knocks him out first gets an XCW World Title shot...
Bobby: As smart as Curt Hennig is...I don't think it's a very good idea to play with matches. I mean seriously...Vader isn't really a match. It's more like
Curt Hennig is holding a blow torch to his face, and he's waiting for it to turn it into a huge mess.
Joey: Austin staggers back to his feet and Vader goes for an armbar. Steve gets out of it though, and now he nails Vader with a clothesline. Vader doesn't go down yet, and another clothesline....
Bobby: No that's not going to do it. Joey: Austin with a boot to the stomach, and a swinging neckbreaker takes both men down. <Austin gets back up and pulls Vader's legs apart....> Joey: Austin is talkin' some trash and he plants a foot into the groin. Steve
Austin is walking around the ring now, and the fans are cheering from him.
Bobby: He doesn't care about the fans!! Joey: Austin whips him into the ropes...THEZ PRESS!!!! He unloads with those punches to the temple!!!
Bobby: Vader doesn't know what hit him!!! Joey: Austin earlier today went to visit Vader's relatives, and stomped a mud hole him them. Will Austin do it again tonight?
<Austin brings Vader back up and takes him down with an inverted atomic drop.>
Joey: Austin to the ropes now and he drops an elbow onto the face of Vader, and another one. Vader rolls over to his knees and he's trying to get back to his feet...
<Steve begins to stomp away not letting Vader get up at all. Austin with boot after boot, and Vader can't get up.>
Joey: Steve Austin is trying to make a point here and now, and it doesn't look good for the undefeated Vader.
Bobby: Now is when Paul E needs to jump onto the apron...he needs to distract
Austin, and give Vader a chance to regroup.

Vader up, and Vader with a low blow!!! Fist to the midsection now by Vader, and he makes it back to his feet.
<Vader runs to the ropes...> Joey: VADER ATTACK!!!! Austin slumps down tot he ground, and that move will take everything out of you.
Bobby: He just throws his body at you, and knocks you to the ground. Joey: Vader is going to the second ropes now...where's he going? He's springing off those ropes...FLYING SPLASH!!!! From the second turnbuckle!!!
Bobby: That'll crush your ribs like trash compresser. <Vader picks up Austin and throws him to the outside. Vader follows....> Joey: Vader grabs Austin and throws him into the guardrail. Vader slowly walks over to Austin and whips him again into the other one. This time Austin bounces off of them, and Vader catches him in a bearhug...NO!! He turns it into a
belly-to-belly suplex, and Austin is hurt. Bobby: That's right in front of us...I heard the back crack. Joey: Vader waits for Austin to make it to his feet, and he's not going to any time soon. Vader rolls back into the ring and waits for the count to start....
Bobby: Austin had better hurry it up... Joey: Steve Austin is crawling to the apron, and here comes Paul E.....Paul E comes over and now he's kicking the ribs of Austin while Vader distracts the ref. WAIT A MINUTE!!!! There's Taz, and Paul E see's him...Taz is chasing Paul
E back to the dressing room.
Bobby: Vader see's it too. He's telling the ref, and Austin slides in from behind Vader... Joey: Vader doesn't see him, and Austin makes it back to his feet. <Austin spins him around...> Joey: Stunner!!! Stunner!! No..Vader throws him chest first into the ring ropes, Vader with a forearm to the small of the back. Austin goes to his knees, and Vader with a forearm across the bridge of the nose, and another one.

Bobby: You can tell that he's looking for Paul E., and that he's focused on the match right now.
Joey: Vader brings Austin to his feet...boot to the stomach. Powerbomb coming up...WAIT A MINUTE!!! WHAT THE HELL!?!?!?

Austin are face to face now, and what's going to happen?

Joey: Austin and Rob Van Dam are fighting back to the locker room, and Vader is still out cold. Austin hit him with that stunner and it was over....

The Rock: The Rock has ONE nerve for you, ONE count em'...nerve, and the fact is this...one you get that one nerve...The Rock will declare war on you.
