XCW Monday Night Malice (part 3)

December 8, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we comeback Michael Cole is in the ring, and the canvas is covered in red carpet. None of the fans know what's going on, well maybe the few Internet marks there are there but the majority has no idea what is about to happen.]
Michael: Ladies and gentlemen first of all let me introduce to you, "The Crippler" Chris Benoit!!!! [Benoit walks out in a suit and tie, and everyone pops like crazy for him. He smiles and climbs into the ring. He shakes hands with Michael Cole, and then they begin to speak...before they can even get a word out though the crowd starts in with "Crippler" chant.]
Michael: Listen to these fans, they love you! Chris: Well ya know what, I love them. There's two reasons why I joined the wrestling world, and one of them was because of these people. The second was because I wanted to be like my idol. I wanted to be like the legendary Dynamite Kid!
[Crowd pops] Chris: Tonight I am here to present the XCW Monthly Achievement and Goals award to my hero...the man Dynamite Kid!!!!
[Crowd is in shock as Dynamite Kid rolls out from the curtain in a wheel chair. They haven't seen him in a long time, and they're all on their feet right now to give him the respect that he deserves. Dynamite rolls down to ringside, and the XCW has built a ramp for him to get into the ring. The ropes have been taken away of course. He rolls into the ring, and Chris shakes his hand.]
Chris: Dynamite..you have inspired me beyond belief. You have shown the world that not only can small guys make it wrestling, but you showed them that you can kick some ass too.
[Crowd pops.] Dynamite: Thank you Chris. You know as I look out and see all these people, and
I look at you...it makes me proud to say I have been apart of wrestling history. Many years I've been bitter about everything, my life, my friends...a lot of people have died that I didn't get to say goodbye too.
[Dynamite gets a little choked up...] Dynamite: This is for all of them, and is for all of you! Thank you all! Chris: Dynamite I have one more question before I hand you the award. What's it like to know that I'm better at your own game than you are?
[Dynamite seems a little puzzled as he stops wiping the tears from his eyes.] Chris: I asked you a question you Crippled Brit Bastard...What's it like knowing that I'm better at your own game than you are? That I'm better than you?
Dynamite: With all due respect Chris this is not the time or pla - Chris: SHUT UP!!! [Chris Benoit smashes the wooden plaque across the face of Dynamite and he hits him so hard he falls back in his wheel chair. Chris then pulls him out of his wheel chair and starts stomping the hell out of him. He gets up looks at him, and then locks in the Crippler Crossface. Dynamite is screaming in pain, his forehead starting to show blood from the shot he took.]
Don: Where's Davey Boy when you ne - oh shit. Nevermind. Jim: This is sick!!! This is disgusting...Chris Benoit is taking out one of his heros. He's taking out the man that he patterned himself after.
Don: It's pretty funny to watch. [XCW officials run down, and Chris lets go finally. Benoit grabs the microphone and gets right in Dynamite's face...]
Chris: Do you know what it's like to be compared to you day in and day out? You were nothing but a tag team wrestler you pathetic crippled nothing!!! I took your style and I made it something you could never...I won World Titles, I have beaten the greatest wrestlers of all time. Who did you beat? Rythm and Blues?
From now on people will know me as the man who put Dynamite Kid out of wrestling for good, not the man who loved this loser.
[Chris spits on the injured Dynamite Kid and walks away. Camera cuts to Jim
Ross and Don as the EMT's are helping Dynamite Kid.]
Jim: Fans what we've just seen has to be an all time low for the XCW. I want to apologize for Chris Benoit's actions here, and to anyone who has respect for
Dynamite Kid.
Don: Cut the sappy BS...I'm so glad someone finally did something to that guy. You know people have these visions of their legendary heros...Babe Ruth, Jerry
West, Joe Montana....they were nothing when they played. All they are is hype, and that's exactally what Dynamite Kid was. He was all hype...
Jim: Dynamite Kid opened the door for so many smaller wrestlers, he showed us al - Don: He showed me tonight he can wheel up a ramp...whoopie. Jim: You're a sad man. Don: No you're just misguided...now that they got that old sack out, what's our next match?
Jim: Edge and Chris Jericho. Don: Is that all you have to say about that? Edge and Chris Jericho? Jim: I'm not in the mood to hype okay!? Don: Fine I'll do it. Edge and Chris Jericho two of the Wrestling Worlds greatest atheletes. Both men have amazing ability inside the ring, and this is going to be an exotic, bedazzlment, of epic proportions...a seething, firey, exploding ball of excitement that will knock you on your ass and make you realize that GOD DAMN WRESTLING IS GOOD!!!!!! How was that?
Jim: Not bad. Don: Damn right. -Intros- [The crowd's cheering louder and louder as Edge and Chris Jericho meet toe to toe. Jericho goes for a right hand, and Edge comesback with one of his own.
Chris goes for another one, but Edge ducks.] Jim: Dropkick by Edge, and that sends Jericho into the ropes. Edge rolls him up quickly...this match could be over fast!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Kickout by Chris
Jericho.
Don: Wow that was a close call there for the King Of The World. [Both men up and Edge walks right into a hard kick to the stomach, and Jericho runs to the ropes and takes him down with a swinging neckbreaker. He goes for the cover quickly, but Edge kicks out before the count can be made.]
Jim: Chris Jericho is back on his feet, and he's keeping Edge down with a series of kicks the the back and head. Jericho brings Edge to his feet and he bodyslams him down onto the canvas...
Don: Where's he going? Isn't it kind of early? Jim: Chris Jericho from the top rope with a Moonsault...wow. He hits it perfectly, and Chris Jericho is going to pull out all the stops here tonight.
He stands up and works the crowd a little before making the cover. Don: Same Jericho all the time. Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout by Edge, and the place is trying to get behind him right now. [Jericho brings Edge back up and he whips him into the turnbuckle. Chris yells something to the crowd, and charges in. Edge quickly moves out of the way, and
Jericho hits nothing but turnbuckle. Edge with a backslide..]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!!! Chris Jericho kicks out at the last second, and another close call.
Don: Who's going to make it to the finals with Taz tonight? Edge or Jericho, I have my money on...
Jim: Jericho? Don: No..whoever wins. [Edge picks up Jericho and whips him into the ropes, Chris runs into a boot to the stomach and Edge follows up with a nice Brainbuster.]
Jim: Edge doesn't want to make the cover. He's going to the top ropes this time, and here we go...not to be out done by Chris Jericho.
Don: Flying Kneedrop!!! Jim: Chris moves out of the way! Jericho now drops a knee across the throat, and a second one...third time a charm.
[Chris Jericho poses for the crowd and gets a loud heel reaction, but it doesn't seem to matter he knows he's in charge. Chris brings Edge to his feet and whips him into the ropes, Edge bounces off and gets caught with a jumping wheel kick to the jaw.]
Jim: Great kick there by Chris Jericho. As annoying as the man is, you still have to give him some credit for being talented.
Don: Well he's not your typical fat hoss that you usually like, but I'm sure he could outwrestle a beast like Bradshaw anyday.
Jim: Bradshaw is a monster... Don: So we've heard. [Chris pulls Egde back to his feet and hits him with a couple of knife-edge chops to the chest that draw an expected "WHOOO" from the crowd. He whips Edge into the ropes and goes for a clothesline but it's ducked. On the return trip
Jericho goes for a hurricanrana....]
Jim: EGDE WITH A POWERBOMB!!!! He turns it into a powerbomb, and Jericho holds his head in pain!!!
Don: He keeps the cover... Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO! [Edge rolls back to his feet and Jericho stumbles back up. Edge hits him with a right hand, and another one. He sends him into the ropes and hits the SPEAR!!!]
Jim: Edge hooks the le - NO....he gets off of him. Edge is going to the top rope, and what's he going to do?
Don: Well a move off the top rope no doubt. Jim: Edge raises his ar - OH MY GOD!!!! Earl Hebner just pulled onto that top rope, and Edge just crotched himself across the top turnbuckle. What in the hell is going on here?
Don: I think he was just making sure the ropes were tight so Edge didn't fall. Jim: Chris Jericho now see's this, and he climbs up onto the top turnbuckle. He hooks the arms of Edge, and...DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPERPLEX!!!!
Don: Now that was awesome!!! [Chris Jericho stands up and celebrates his move, and shakes the hand of Earl
Hebner. Both men are smiling, and Chris Jericho drops to his knees and makes the cover. With a blatant handfull of tights...]
Jim: OH COME ON!!! ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!! [Earl Hebner signals for the bell, and Chris Jericho makes it back to his feet victorious.]
Jim: Will someone tell me what the hell is going on? Don: Yeah, Chris Jericho just won the match. What's the matter? Is your monitor not working, you can look at mind if you want.
Jim: NO!! I mean what is going on w - WAIT A DAMN MINUTE!!!! [All of a sudden another Hebner runs out and he starts fighting with his brother. Chris Jericho is up, and he watching it happen...]
Jim: Ladies and gentlemen I think we've been bamboozled!!!! The second time in wrestling history, I think we've had the whool pulled over our eyes.
Don: Hey wait a minute, that's something you do in your hick town. We normal people have nothing to do with whool.
Jim: I think Dave and Earl are fighting in the ring, but who is who? [All of a sudden Jericho turns around to see if Edge has left, and Edge spears him hard. Edge continues with lefts and rights while Earl beats on Dave with lefts and rights...]
Don: Now I'm confused. Jim: Here comes the XCW officials to break it off. Whatever the case is Chris
Jericho goes onto the finals of the World Title Fatal Fourway later tonight.
Don: And he earned it. Whew did he earn it. [Edge and Earl stand in the ring while Chris and Dave are being drug back to the locker room. Edge can't believe that he lost, and puts his head in his hands. The camera cuts to Ric Flair who is wondering around the backstage area...]
Ric: JERICHO!!!! Where the hell are ya? [He passes a few XCW workers, and then he bumps into Justin Credible and Lance
Storm. Both men stand in his way, and Flair doesn't seem to back down.]
Justin: So what's with you changing the Main Event...we were supposed to be the highlight, and then you come in and screw things up. I don't like that, and
Lance doesn't like that either.
Ric: Well that's just to damn bad isn't it...we have to get this fed off with a bang, and this is just how we're going to do it. Now get out of my way before I make sure you're not even apart of the Main Event...
[Both men step aside, but don't seem to happy about it. Ric Flair walks off looking for Chris Jericho as we cut to a commercial.]
