FINK: And his opponent...from Battlecreek, Michigan. He weighs in at 237 pounds. Here is Rob Van Dam!!!

Jim: But Marty lands on his feet...just like a damn cat. Marty goes for the

Jesse: Nice exchange of moves right there.... Jim: Rob slides back into the ring, and they lock up again. RVD with an arm ringer, and Marty reverses.
<RVD with a flip, which makes Marty fall to the canvas.> Jim: Beautiful moves by both men. And they're both back up....Marty with a side headlock, and RVD whips him into the ropes. Leapfrog by Rob, OH MAN!!! Marty with a dropkick as Van Dam comes down, and Rob is in some trouble.
Jesse: Good thinking by Marty!!! <Marty brings RVD back up to his feet and executes a back suplex. Marty goes to the top ropes and hits a flying fistdrop.>
Jim: Shades of the Rockers right there. He brings Rob back to his feet and executes a headlock take down. He's got the headlock on, and Rob is trying to break free.
<Rob does a kip up, and Marty lets go of the hold and does a kip up of his own.> Jesse: Okay one of these two men have to start punching the other or I'm going to go in there and punch em'.
Jim: Marty charges at Rob and he backdrops him out of the ring. Marty lands on his feet though, and he pulls Rob to the outside. Both men exchanging blows now, and Rob getting the upperhand.
<Van Dam with a kick to the stomach, and he follows up with a legsweep.> Jim: Rob from the apron....RUNNING FLIPPING LEGDROP!!!!!! What a move, and
Marty holds his throat in pain.
Jesse: What a move that was...I can't believe that. Jim: Van Dam recovers from the move, and brings Marty back up. <Rob hits a bodyslam, and he goes up to the apron again. He waits for Jannetty to get up...he gets a running start but Woman trips him, and he falls flat on his back.>
Jim: OH!!! Woman just tripped Rob Van Dam, and he fell right square on his back. Marty is up and he kicks away on it, and he drops a knee onto the small of the back.
<Marty gets back up and takes away the blue padding around the ring. He brings the groggy Van Dam to his feet and lifts him up....>
Jim: Wha...what's he goin...OH GOOD GOD!!! A BRAINBUSTER onto the cement, and
Van Dam's neck has to be squashed like an accordion. Marty now going over to get the ladder, and he slides it into the ring.
Jesse: I don't think Rob is going to get up after that. He's had two stunners today, and now a Brainbuster onto the cement floor. If he gets up from this then he's on more than weed.
Jim: Weed? He doesn't do weed. Jesse: Yeah and you're not addicted to food. <Marty sets up the ladder in the middle of the ring, and as he does the Woman attacks RVD on the floor.>
Jim: Rob has had some much trouble tonight, and Woman is just adding to it. Hey
WAIT A MINUTE!!! Chris Benoit is HERE!!! CHRIS BENOIT IS HERE and he's yelling at Woman!!!
Jesse: Uh oh...trouble in the Benoit household. Jim: He's telling he to leave.... <Woman goes storming back to the dressing room, and Benoit follows right behind. Meanwhile Jannetty has set the ladder up inside the ring, and is starting to climb.>
Jim: Marty is almost to the top, but here comes Rob Van Dam under the ropes. Rob gets a running start and tips the ladder over. Jannetty bails out before any serious trouble.
Jesse: Marty is a thinker. Jim: Rob ducks a clothesline, and he takes Marty down with a leg lariat. Rob with a kick to the head, and another kick to the head.
<Rob lifts up Marty and Rob goes up top. Rob hits a Moonsault Armdrag Takedown, and Jannetty hits the canvas hard.>
Jim: Amazing move by Rob Van Dam, and now he sets up the ladder. Rob starts to climb, but Marty is up again. Marty is right behind him....WHOA!!!!
<Rob hits a moonsault from the middle of the ladder and both men go down.>
Jesse: That move will take it out of you, and your opponent. They need to start doing some moves that will slow the other one down, and help you speed up.
Jim: We're live from City Walk, and this is New Years Eve. What a night it's been so far...we've seen a ton of things happen, and I'm sure we're going to see a whole lot more in the next hour or so.
Jesse: Can you believe that Paul E got his head flushed down the toilet? Jim: I can hardly believe that Vader hasn't done anything about it yet. Rob is back up now and so is Marty. MJ grabs Rob and throws him into the turnbuckle.
<Marty gets a running start and drop kicks Rob in the corner. Rob falls to his knees and Marty with another quick dropkick to the side of the head.>
Jim: Brutal dropkick right to the side of the head, and Marty grabs the ladder. <Marty throws the ladder towards the turnbuckle, and it hits Van Dam right on top of the head. Rob holds his head in pain...and Marty comes over and stomps away on him again.>
Jim: Marty isn't going to be known as a bitch anymore... Jesse: JIM!!! I can't believe you said that! Jim: Well he is a bitch...he's always been 2nd best, and tonight is the night he leaves that shadow. If he doesn't want to be known as that "Other Guy From
The Rockers" then he needs to kick it into high gear.
Jesse: I think he's doing a pretty damn good job here tonight. <Marty grabs the ladder and sets it flat onto the mat. Marty grabs Rob by the hair and puts him up onto the turnbuckle. Marty goes up top....>
Jim: Don't tell me...he's not...NO!!! NO NO!!! <Marty takes Rob down from the top ropes with a Hurracanrana which drives Van
Dam into the ladder.>
Jim: He broke him in half!!! He broke Van Dam right in two with that move, and
Marty is up again. What the hell is he going to do this time?
Jesse: He's going to the top again!!! Jim: Van Dam is hurt....he's not getting up. <Marty goes to the top turnbuckle again and executes a Senton Splash onto RVD who is on the ladder.>
Jim: SENTON!!! SENTON SPLASH!!!! Marty isn't going to stop until RVD is dead! His mission has to be to kill RVD, because he's trying everything he can. Jesse: RVD doesn't look like he's even breathing anymore. Jim: All that weight just came crashing down onto the ribs of RVD, and there was no give with the ladder. His back has to be mangled into a wreckless mess.
<Marty uses the ropes to crawl back to his feet. He gets up and brings Rob back up with him, but Rob drops to his knees.>
Jesse: It's over...Rob can't even stand up right now. Marty needs to get that ladder set up and go for the win. Anything else will be a waste of his time.
Jim: Well for the sake of RVD he should stop. Why doesn't he just set up the ladder and get that TV Title? All he ha...no. What's he doing now?
Jesse: He's draped RVD across the ladder, and he's going up top again. <Marty goes to the top ropes again, and he mocks RVD with the pointing to himself bit. All of a sudden he jumps and catches air with a spectactular FROG
SPLASH.>
Jim: ROB ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY!!!!!! Jesse: OHHHH!!!! Jim: Marty eats steel ladder, and RVD barely had time to move out of the way. I think Marty bit his tongue on the impact because he's got a mouth full of blood. He must have...look at the blood spill out of the mouth.
Jesse: If RVD wouldn't have moved it would've been so long to him. Jim: Both men are down and Earl Hebner isn't sure what to do. Someone has to win tonight, and someone has to walk away with the XCW TV Title. He can't count them out, but they're not moving.
Jesse: This match has brought at least 1,000 more people around the ring area. I can see a whole sea of people right now. Everyone is here to watch this match, because it's HUGE!!!
<Rob grabs the ropes and brings himself back to his feet, holding his back in pain. Marty is still down, but he's still alive. Rob falls back against the turnbuckle, and then sits down.>
Jim: After this exchange of moves, both men seem to be out of it. Jesse: Look at Marty...he's trying to spit all the loose blood in his mouht out. He doesn't want to choke on it, or have it cause problems later in the match.
<Marty gets up and goes after RVD. Rob uses the turnbuckle to pick himself up, and he kicks Marty in the stomach. Another kick, and he goes for a right hand but Marty blocks it.>
Jim: Marty rakes the eyes of RVD...and OH DEAR LORD!!! THAT WAS DISGUSTING!!!! HE just spit his blood into the eyes of Rob Van Dam. Jesse: That's one way to stop someone. Jim: Marty grabs Rob and throws him over the top ropes. Rob crashes down onto the floor, and now Marty grabs the ladder.
<Marty quickly sets the ladder up, but he brings it over to the ropes. Marty starts to make the climb, slowly....blood pouring out of his mouth.>
Jim: I can't even fathom what he's going to do right now. Rob is up and he darts up the turnbuckle.
<Rob dives for Marty and both of them fall to the canvas in a big heap.> Jim: Rob just saved his own life right now. If Marty would've done what he was going to do...whatever it was going to be...it would've been over. Rob and
Marty now back to their feet and Rob with an Enzugiri to the back of MJ's head.
Marty falls to the floor, and Rob brings the ladder over to the center of the ring. Jesse: This could be it!! <Rob is going to the top of the ladder...he's almost there....he's reaching for the TV Title. His fingers are touching it...and Marty makes it to his feet and knocks the ladder over. Rob flies off not the canvas, but to the outside of the ring!!!!>
Jim: AHHH!! HOLY HELL!!!!! Rob is dead!!! Rob has to be dead damnit. Somebody call the damn match...this youngster can't take much more.
Jesse: I've seen everything in wrestling, but I've never seen someone take so many bumps. Jim: This match has to be one of the best matches we've had since the XCW re-opened. Rob's body is facing a million different places, and here come the
EMT's. Here comes Magnum TA, and they're checking on RVD.
Jesse: Marty is going out there too. I think he might be concerned for RVD.... <Marty grabs a chair and pushes all the officials aside. He places the chair over RVD's body and sits down. Marty begins to slap RVD, and talk smack to him as he's trying to recover.>
Jim: Oh come on!!! This isn't right.... Jesse: HAHA what a move on the part of Marty Jannetty. I'm starting to like this ghuy a lot.
Jim: Rob Van Dam is trying to get up, but he's weak by now. He won't be able to push Marty off the chair, and he's getting angrier and angrier....Marty is slapping him harder and harder!!!
<Finally Marty gets off of him and folds the chair up. He's about to hit RVD in the face with it, and Rob counters by holding his boot up. The chair hits Marty in the face hard, and he stumbles backwards.>
Jim: Great counter by RVD, and he grabs the chair. He throws it to Marty, and
VAN DAMINATOR!!!!! Oh Marty ducks though....he throws the chair back to Rob, but Rob ducks and counters with a leg sweep!!!!
Jesse: That was pure quickness by RVD!!!! <Rob picks up the chair and slams it down across the head of Marty. By this time the blood from Marty's tongue has gotten all over his own face. He can't see, and he's choking on his own blood. Rob's face is covered in Marty's dry blood from the spit earlier on in the match.>
Jim: Rob grabs the dazed Marty and throws him onto the Spainish Announce Table, and he rolls back into the ring. What's he going to do?
<Rob climbs up the ladder...all the way to the top. He flies off like a bird.....> Jim: GOD GET OUT OF THE WAY!!!!!!! CRASH!!!!! Jim: GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY!!!! DEAR LORD IN HEAVEN!!!!! ROB VAN DAM HAS FROG SPLASHED JANNETTY FROM THE LADDER TO THE OUTSIDE!!!!!! Jesse: He was at last 24 feet in the air, and he came crashing down across the body of Marty.
Jim: Both men have been through hell here tonight, and their New Years Eve is going to be spent in the damn hospital. I can't believe it...
Jesse: The Frog Splash heard around the world....that was amazing. Jim: You want to talk about wrestling, you want to talk about action? Well the
XCW is the right place to be. You're not going to get this going to any other wrestling event. The XCW is where it's at, and tonight we're proving it.
Jesse: LOOK ROB IS MOVING!!!! Jim: Rob Van Dam is up, he's the walking dead right now. He slides back into the ring, and he grabs the ladder once again. He's starting to make that climb to the XCW TV Title....
Jesse: Marty is still out of it....he's holding his ribs in serious pain. Jim: Rob is almost to the top...he's there, and he's reaching for the belt. HE GOT IT!!!! HE GOT THE TITLE!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner and the NEW XCW Television Champion. Rob Van Dam!!!! Jim: What a match that was...these people that came to see some goofy wrestling have just started to respect this sport. I can't believe this match, and Rob
Van Dam has been out through hell tonight to get this match.
Jesse: Two Stunners, and a match with MJ. He deserves this belt more than anyone right now!!!
Jim: RVD holds the title up high, and the fans are going wild. The new TV Champ is none other than Rob Van Dam!!! He's getting a huge ovation here tonight, and he's earned my respect on a whole other level.
Jesse: And Marty is still hurting...we need to get someone out here to help him. Jim: Fans we'll back back right after this....
