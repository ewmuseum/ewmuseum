XCW Role Play Packet

December 23, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here are just some of the Rp's from our active roster. These are what we get all the time from them. If you read it you'll see that there are no marks, and nobody talking about the WWF or WCW....hope you enjoy!
For those of you who don't know...the XCW is stuck in Japan ever since Mitsuharu Misawa won the XCW World Title, and Sonny Ono took over as commish. So if you're wondering WHY the Rp's are from Japan...that's why. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- 1. HBK says Hart and Storm = Ass Clowns? 2. Hart says HBK will go down 3. Vader TIME!! 4. Mikey Whipwreck isn't sure about Monday.... [The camera opens as we see a plush studio style setting. Chairs here....chairs there. Then it spins around to show a white leather couch where none other than
Shawn Michaels is sitting. The Heartbreak Kid is dressed to perfection in a pair of black Gucci slacks and a red Armani silk shirt. His hair is pulled back and he has his classic red tinted shades on. Michaels just oozes money and superiority. He takes a sip of his 007 in front of him and looks into the camera..]
HBK: Had to pick this place up a little. Overseas just isn't home without some of the finer things in life. I suppose I'm right though....what the ole'
Heartbreak Kid wants....he gets. Same goes for in between those ropes....I told you Christain would be beat...and well what happened? The band kicked up and well, that green kid bit the dust hard just like the rest of these wanna be's around here are going to come to understand..
[Shawn just smirks as another round of movers make their way in. Bringing in more love seats and couches of the leather variety..]
HBK: Friday Night X-treme I got a little dance with some of my past. I hear the cries of a little bitch boy coming back....I can almost see the face of the loser who thought he could match up with the greatest damned thing since sliced bread. Bret, you and your joy boy buddy Lance Storm are leading a parade of the attempt at attention grabbing and tear jerking vareiety....but as I've said a million times over....No one really cares. I saw something that made me laugh the other day...I saw Lance Storm, parading around in Canada with Hart talking about ME being a Homosexual. Pretty funny how those two ass clowns are running around together while I'm flanked by women every time I make my way out of this place. I've got millions and millions of female viewers just sitting there on the edge of their seats...getting wet at the thought of Shawn Michaels coming to an arena near them. I don't see many female fan club members for Lance and
Bret....seems to me they could lead that gay pride parade all the way from
Canada to Japan and not have a hard time bringing in followers..
[He shrugs and laughs just a bit before going on..] HBK: Makes no nevermind to me though....Because come X-treme, I can already here the band warming up for you Lance. I can here the whole symphony start to play...just for you it won't be the sweetest sound you've ever heard...it'll be a symphony of destruction. One, that the show stopper conducts all over the
Dojo on Friday. Falls Count Anywhere doesn't matter to me..All that matters is the fact that I'll still plant you with that Sweet Chin Music and dance my way right over your face. I don't care about your technical ability. I don't care about the fact that you have bitch boy at your side....plainly put Lance
Storm....You can't even hold a candle to HBK. Not a Candle kid...because while you were out in some bush league trying to hone your skills....while you were in some inferior bingo hall somewhere trying to play nice....I was at the Tip
TOP Of this sport. Taking care of everyone who stepped in my way. I'm the man who can go all night long just to walk away with a victory....Just ask your buddy Bret. He wanted me in the Iron man match and he got what he deserved....a
Loss to the Showstopper...the Main Event....The Heartbreak Kid.....Shawn
Michaels. [He slides his shades off and stares directly into the camera..] HBK: Ya See Lancey Boy....Your in my way. Your clogging up the path to the top where I can go back and get that XCW title that looks so damned good hanging around my waist. I can go back up there and sit atop this kingdome and be the royalty I am. I know you don't want to be the next do you Storm? Your smarter than your ass clown buddy Bret aren't you? You should just play nice Lancey and stop this facade before you get hurt. Just like Christain, your not in my league...and just like him, I'll send you home packing after a loss. You see,
I've been called everything from that whiney, self-centered, washed up excuse for a wrestler that your confiding in...he's tried every way imaginable to get by me....hasn't worked however because everyone knows....he can't get the job done in the big games. Never could...and Never can. So, go ahead
Storm....follow him until the end. Follow him until he runs you smack dab into the full brunt of a Super Kick....he just wants to see someone else take a beating....someone else lose at the hands of ole' HBK. He just wants to see someone else made a mockery of in the middle of the ring, in front of millions of fans. So, hopefully, it can take a little weight off his shoulders from being beaten by ME so many times through out his not so illustrious career.
[He smirks that ever confident smirk of his and continues..] HBK: You can keep on following Bret....I promise ya that Lance. But he'll just lead you into the same trap he lead himself into....Career Suicide at the Hands of the Heartbreak Kid...
[With that he waves them to leave and slides the shades back on as the camera fades away...]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- -[]- Out in Japan a plane lands on the small rampway. It looks to be a small plane that would fit maybe, ten to fifteen people. As the plane comes to a halt the stairs come out as the door swings open. Standing in the doorway are only a pair of feet. The feet begin to move down the stares as the camera remains even. The faded jeans are seen and another step is taken, a black belt and the beginnings of what looks to be a jersey of some sort... then he takes one more step down... and again another. He's wearing a Calgary Hitmen jersey... and this man is Bret "The Hitman" Hart! -[]-
-[]- Bret brushes a few pieces of hair away from his eye as a smirk crosses his face. He begins walking forward and is being surrounded by the Japanese press. Several of them are saying things that Hart obviously can't understand. He seems to get frustrated and finally an English speaking member of the press begins to shout "Mr. Hart, could we have a comment on why you are in Japan?"
Hart seems to shove the press a little away from him and looks back at the
English speaking man. -[]-
Hitman: Comment... you want a comment? I'll give you a comment! Ever since my son Blade showed me that Heartbreak Kid t-shirt, I've been out of my mind. I went to Canada, I thought that my career was over. The thought that my own son could turn against me... DAMNITT, IT RUINED MY LIFE!
-[]- The Japanese seem entranced by his words, probably not understanding him... but understanding his rage. -[]- Hitman: I can't stand alone in Canada letting Shawn Michaels get the best of me. I can't stand alone letting Shawn Michaels have the last laugh. I can't let
Shawn Michaels ever have the last laugh. That's not what Bret "The Hitman" Hart stands for... I stand for defeating adversity, for giving everything your best shot, and to never give up. I'm not going to change for Shawn Michaels... I'm not going to change for anybody.
Hitman: Bret Hart may have been slown down, but he will never be stopped. Bret
Hart may have been humiliated, but he will never let it destroy him. As long as even one percent of me is still in working order, I'll still be, the best there is, the best there was, and the best that there ever will be.
-[]- The Hitman brushes another few strands of hair out of his eye. -[]- Hitman: I'm here to destroy Shawn Michaels, I'm here to make Shawn Michaels feel the pain that I felt... Shawn, he doesn't know how much pain he caused me, how he almost stopped Bret Hart from ever coming back, but he didn't get the job done. I'm still standing, I'm still breathing, and I damn sure still have my head held up high.
Hitman: People say that time heals all wounds, but time does not abolish all hate. I still despise you... I still can't stand you, Shawn. I'm sick of hearing your name, hearing about far you are going in the XCW... Shawn, that's why I've returned, to once again triumph over adversity, to once again make a wrong into a right, and to once again prove that I am still, the excellence of execution!
Hitman: Shawn Michaels... boy toy... I will have my revenge, I will have my day of destroying you. As long as I'm still breathing, your life will be a living hell!
-[]- Bret shoves aside two of the Japanese press members and gets in a car and begins to drive off into the night. -[]- -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- ???: Huff... _SMACK_ huff... _SMACK_ huff... [Fade in, as we see "Big" Van Vader slamming his massive forearms into a heavy bag. Suddenly, a familiar voice rings out from off-camera.]
???: That's right, Vader. Pound that bag like it were your worst enemy. Visualize the superstars of the XCW being destroyed. Hear their cries in your head. And most of all...
[And we now see why the voice sounded familiar. It belongs to none other than
Paul E. Dangerously.]
PED: ... picture the titles you'll be winning with me by your side. [Paul notices the camera, turns towards it, and smiles in that diabolical way that only he can.]
PED: Well hello there. Looks like you're getting a first hand look at the man who will turn this organization on it's head. Impressive, huh? He's down to three hundred and ninety pounds with me by his side. He's in the best damn shape of his life. And he's just about ready to pound his message into each and every member of the XCW's skulls. What's that message, you ask? It's simple, really. Never... _ever_... stand in our way.
[A shrill ringing suddenly erupts from Paul E.'s person, and then we see what it's from; the 20 pound classic cell phone that Paul E. _still_ has from his
WCW days.]
PED: Hello? [Paul walks off screen, as "Big" Van Vader stops pummeling the heavy bag and turns towards the camera.]
BVV: It's time... [A pause, as Vader seems to get more riled up with each passing second.] BVV: It's time... [Zoom in, on the vicious face of Vader.] BVV: It's Vader Time! [The monster of a man seems to almost be foaming at the mouth, his eyes screaming out to hurt someone. It's on this terrifying view that we...]
[Fade to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- X-x-X-MiKeY-WhiPwReCk-X-x-X "Please Don't Endorse Me" [Fade in. Mikey Whipwreck is seated in a folding chair, no more than 20 feet from other wrestlers who have gathered to speak. Mikey, however, speaks to nobody. Instead he holds an icepack above his right eye and sips off a can of
Pepsi. He sighs and kicks back. He downs a little more Pepsi before Juventud
Guerrera bumps into him. Some Pepsi comes out of the can and dabbles on his cheek. He stands up slowly, as if he were some dramatically large man...
However, he's barely Juvy's height and twice his weight.] Mikey Whipwreck: You string bean! You caused some of this invaluable and expensive drink to get on my face.
Juventud Guerrera: Ease up, muchacho. I buy you another one, si? Mikey Whipwreck: Ugh. I'm not in the mood. I don't even know Spanish. Juventud Guerrera: I not speaking English, mang. I be speaking English, vato! Mikey Whipwreck: Yeah. Whatever. [Juvy slaps him like a girl and runs off giggling like a girl also. Mikey sighs and sits down, resting his elbow on the snack table, then resting his head against his fist.]
Mikey Whipwreck: Absolutely no respect. I beat Lance Storm a week ago and nobody cares. I help The Rock not become dog meat in the middle of the ring only for everyone to turn their head. Then I beat Triple H in a First Blood match, pouring out my all, only to get another dose of ignorance. WHAT IS A
MAN TO DO! Sheesh. I know I'm no champion material but lighten up a little, give me some credit. I'm going to be responsible for the XCW returning to
America. I think I deserve a little something. All I ask for is an autographed Poison CD and a kiss on the cheek from Jenna Jameson. Too much to ask? I think not.
But no, that's ok. I'll get none of it and I don't expect any. I never got credibility in ECW, never got a damn clap in WCW so why should I here in the XCW? I shouldn't, flat out. Nonetheless, I plan on walking into that ring against Misawa and.... Getting my ass beat. I'm no hero, folks. I'm an average guy who pulled out a couple upsets, so what? I'm not the man who's going to help guide the XCW into a new era, trust me on that. Did you see the way Misawa choked me out last week for back talking? Yeah, he could've killed me. But he didn't, why, I don't know.. Maybe he wanted to save it for
Christmas Eve Chaos. It's ok though, because with The Rock as the special guest referee, Mikey Whipwreck can do ANYTHING!!
[Whipwreck unleashes a big grin. Suddenly Magnum TA sneaks into the shot and hands Mikey a letter. He waves at the camera and takes off. Mikey looks puzzled but begins unfolding it. As he reads it, his face starts turning from confusion to worry. His skin goes pale and he looks to the camera.]
Mikey Whipwreck: I, uh.... Uh... Triple H is now the, uh, special guest referee. And um, it's a handicap match now, me against Misawa and Chono and ah... It's Hell in a Cell. Yeah, so now I'm totally screwed. If I lose, Japan is XCW territory forever. Good Lord, why me? Why not Angle... Oh, that's right, Captain America converted to Sgt. Kabukiman. How come we don't get The
Rock to face this guy? Yeah! The Rock! A guy who has actually won some big matches. Not me, sheesh, I'm a walking goofball. I can't fight one guy, let alone two, inside a covered steel cage.
Nuh uh. Forget it! Don't even ask! Don't even THINK! I'm sorry America. I thought maybe if I acted tough, I'd bully myself into believing I stood a chance but the situation has changed dramatically. I'm basically getting taller, not because of a growth spurt but because I am literally crapping my pants. This isn't good, I've got a sore back, weak knees, a bad neck... I can't do this anymore. I'll return my Fett action figure, the corner is a little bent but it's returnable. Please, don't make me do this. Not like I've got a choice, but if anyone's gonna stand up and speak their mind, let it be me! USA, save me. Send your army over here, you guys could kick Misawa's ass! But not me!
Lord save us all. [Mikey slumps down with a frown on his face, Beck's "Loser" cranks up and he rolls his eyes. Scene fades to black.]
