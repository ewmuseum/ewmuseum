FINK: And his opponent,making his way to the ring, he weighs in at 220 pouns, he is "Your Olympic Hero" Kurt Angle!!!
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen , making his way to the ring, the current reigining
United States Champion..."Mr. Perfect" Curt Hennig!!!
[Both men are about to lock up, and all of a sudden we hear "N...W..O". The screen turns to black and white, and out walks the trio of Hollywood Hogan,
Kevin Nash, and Scott Hall. All three men climb into the ring, and everyone looks puzzled. Hollywood grabs the microphone...]
Hollywood: Ya know somethin' bra...I know these people want to see this match take place, so does the NWO man. I'm going to make this short and simple..which one of you Kurt's called us and asked to be in the NWO? Nobody gave a last name man...
[Both men look like they have no idea what he's talking about.] Hollywood: The NWO isn't here to play games man. We're here to run things around here, and if it's going to take a beating then we'll beat you two into the ground. So what's it going to be?
[Hennig steps forward....] Hollywood: So it was you? [Hennig shakes his head yes...all of a sudden from the crowd....] Joey: ROCK IS HERE!!! ROCK IS HERE!!!! He's got a chair!!! He spins Kurt around and levels him with the chair. He drove that steel right into the head of Kurt
Angle, and Hennig charges...and he gets hit with the chair.
Joel: WHOA!!!! Joey: Rock is standing over the Kurts....and look at his face. You can tell the man isn't happy. The Ro - wait a minute. Wait a damn minute, Hogan is laughing.
Hollywood is laugh, and Rock is smiling to. Joel: COULD IT BE??? [Hollywood gets back on the microphone...] Hollywood: Ahh isn't it great Rock. The good ol' Hollywood swerve. Nobody does it better than a couple of actors. Ya know?
[Rock smiles...] Hollywood: Welcome to the family Rock. [Hogan walks over and gives him a hug. Rock hugs him back and the place is going bezerk. They can't believe what the Rock has done to his fans. Pepsi
Center has never been louder.]
Joey: NO!! THIS CAN'T BE TRUE!!!! Joel: Believe it. ROCK IS NWO!!! [Hogan hands Rock a spray paint can, and he's spraying the letters NWO on Kurt
Angle. Rock starts laughing again, and then he stops...he gives the NWO the
People's Eyebrow, and SPRAYS HALL AND NASH in the eyes with the paint.]
Joey: WHAT!?!?!/ Joel:.............!!!! [Hogan goes for a right hand, but Rocky ducks it.] Joey: ROCK BOTTOM!!! ROCK BOTTOM!!! ROCK BOTTOM!!!!! [Hall walks right into the Rocks arm...] Joey: ROCK BOTTOM ON HALL!!!!!! [Nash gets spun around by Rock...] Joey: ROCK BOTTOM!!!!!!! [The place erupts.] Joel: Wait the Rock is going to speak... Joey: The Rock hasn't spoken in 2 weeks, and tonight he's going to say something. [Rock picks up the mic, and in a VERY raspy voice he says...] Rock: Hollywood Hogan....Kurt Angle...NWO...consider this the Rock's Era. [Rock throws down the microphone, and his music starts to play. Rock leaves to a huge ovation.]
