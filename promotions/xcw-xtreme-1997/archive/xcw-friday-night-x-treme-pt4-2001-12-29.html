XCW Friday Night X-Treme (part 4)

December 29, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: Did you hear Lance's ankle break? WHOA!!! Bobby: A little too ironic don't you think? Joey: You mean Shawn Michaels and now Lance Storm? Huh...now that you mention it. Here comes the hooded man, and Woman is coming down as well.
Bobby: Maybe it's Kevin Sullivan? Joey: I doubt it...maybe it's Chris Benoit? Bobby: Hmmm.... Joey: Both of them climb into the ring, and wait a minute the Hurricane is walking down to the ring now. He doesn't want to wait....he asks the hooded man to take of....the...umm...hood. It's...It's...
MARTY JANNETTY? Joey Styles: What a match, a legendary high flyer taking on one of the hottest new sensations, that's right it's Marty Janetty squaring off against Hurricane
Helms!
Bobby Heenan: Last time I saw Janetty doing anything important, he was getting smashed through a plate glass window by Shawn Michaels! Janetty has nothing to over here.
Joey Styles: WOULD YOU STOP? Bobby Heenan: Hah, Gorilla used to say the same thing! Call the match, Joey! That's why you're getting paid, isn't it? Joey Styles: Ugh! Collar-elbow tie-up.. Helms gets pushed into the corner, clean break by Janetty. Marty runs in, Hurricane ducks to the side, Janetty gets a chest full of turnbuckle, roll up by Hurricane and!!! Oh! Only a one count!
Bobby Heenan: We've got an old geezer and a young superhero in there. I think
I know who's walking away with this one in the bag, Joey!
Joey Styles: Stop being bias! Bobby Heenan: Call the match! Joey Styles: Hurricane with a swinging uppercut, sends Marty to the ropes. He runs, jumps... Attempts the crossbody but Janetty sidesteps! Hurricane hits nothing but the mats on the outside! That has GOT to hurt! Janetty now, through the ropes... He waits for Hurricane to get to his feet... Double
axe-handle sends Helms back to the mats! Bobby Heenan: Although he doesn't impress me, it was pretty crazy seeing Marty attack Storm earlier tonight. But he broke his ankle, and that's dirty. I don't condone cheating.
Joey Styles: WHAT?! Bobby Heenan: Call the match!! Joey Styles: Argh! Yes, Storm's ankle has been confirmed as broken, no word yet how long he'll be out but one thing is for sure, Marty's looking for blood.
Shawn Michael's gotta be wondering whether Marty's here for help or for harm to his career!
Bobby Heenan: The Heartbreak Kid is a punk! If Marty wants to do anything worthwhile, he should take HBK out of commission permanently!
Joey Styles: One would venture to guess Marty and Shawn have some sort of an alliance going on, BUT LOOK OUT! Hurricane with a snap suplex on the outside!
He gets Marty to his feet and rolls him back in the ring. He's climbing to the top...He's got the cape! The cape is out... Marty's stunned, he walks around..
Turns... HURRICANE CONNECTS! CROSSBODY OFF THE TOP! Pin attempt! 1... 2.. Kick out! What a close one! Bobby Heenan: Break his nose! Break his leg! Break something! Joey Styles: Hurricane is getting Marty back up again, this time he sends
Marty into the ropes for an irish whip.. Marty comes back and an impressive dropkick by the Hurricane! Kip up and now Hurricane is flaunting for the crowd. Not a smart move by Helms.
Bobby Heenan: Not too smart of a guy anyway. Joey Styles: STOP IT! Helms has Marty up again, this time he hits a beautiful snapmare and he locks on a sleeperhold. Marty's not ready for that though, he gets back to his feet.. Elbow to the midsection, Janetty breaks the hold.. He bounces off the ropes, shoulderblock sends Hurricane down. Marty leaps over, hits the ropes, leapfrog by Marty, he comes back... Hurricane-Rana by the
Hurricane!!
Bobby Heenan: Is that what he calls it? How dumb. Joey Styles: Hurricane with the pin, one, two, kick out! Hurricane grabs the dazed Janetty... Janetty with the lowblow!! The referee is warning him against it but Marty shrugs the ref off. He backs Helms to the corner, now sending rights and lefts. Helms slumps to the canvas, Janetty now with a boot across the throat... He's trying to choke Hurricane out!
Bobby Heenan: Yeah! First a lowblow, now a choke. THIS is wrestling! Joey Styles: You make me sick! Janetty whips Hurricane to the opposite turnbuckle, Hurricane comes stumbling back.. Powerslam by Marty! He's feeling it tonight. Stomping away now, Hurricane trying to cover his chest and he rolls to the outside. Marty's being restrained by the referee and Hurricane now, trying to recover...
Bobby Heenan: Who's that?! Joey Styles: What? Whe--My God, that's Woman! That is Woman! She said she'd be here! She sneaks around the ring... She's taken off her shoe... Wait, no!
She nails Hurricane across the face!! He just got a three inch heel in the eye! Now she's choking him out on the mats, damn it ref, turn around!
Bobby Heenan: Hey, he asked for it. Joey Styles: What the hell did Hurricane ever do? Bobby Heenan: CALL THE MATCH! Joey Styles: She rolls Helms back in, Janetty's got a grin and he picks Hurricane up... Nice textbook vertical suplex, he goes for the pin, not like this... One, two, KICK OUT! Helms kicks out! I can't believe it!
Bobby Heenan: Kill him, Marty! Joey Styles: But you... Argh. Marty sends Hurricane back into the ropes, this time hitting his own dropkick! But no cover, he grabs Helms again and throws him into the ropes, a crossbody of his own! Cover attempt.. One, two, th-NO!
Kick out again! Marty looks frustrated but he's setting up for his patented super kick... Helms is up, turn around Hurricane... He turns around, Janetty fires!!!.. Helms ducks it!! Hurricane spins Marty around, small package! Pin!
1, 2, 3! It's over!! Hurricane retains the title. Bobby Heenan: Hurricane won CLEANLY over Janetty? Wow. I wouldn't have expected that. Joey Styles: Well he did it and he's still the United States champion here in XCW! [DONG!] [A graveyard.] [DONG!] [As if in response to the toll of the bell, the rumbling of thunder is heard. Raindrops fall continuously, hitting hard onto the white marble gravestones which appear to gleam in the dark.]
[A howl! It starts out soft but gradually builds up. It is only the wind. More thunder and lightning.]
[The camera pans across the graveyard and zooms in on one particular grave marker beneath the shadows of a dead tree. As the camera zooms in, the smooth surface
of the marble appears to be unmarked.] [CRASH of thunder as a bolt of lightning hits the tombstone. And almost instantaneously, the earth parts a little as a hand shoots out from below.]
[Fade.]
