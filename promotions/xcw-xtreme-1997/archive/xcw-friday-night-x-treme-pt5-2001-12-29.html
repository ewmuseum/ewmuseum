XCW Friday Night X-Treme (part 5)

December 29, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: What the hell was that? Could someone please let me know in my ear piece when we're going to have loud dongs like that?
Bobby: Huh? I can't hear you! Joey: Fans it's time for our main event!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall and is the
Main Event tonight on Malice!!!! First coming down to the ring at a total combined weight of 524 pounds...here is the team of Kurt Angle and "The Game"
Triple H!!! (Both men come out to The Games music, and it doesn't look like they're too happy. They climb into the ring and the crowd boo's them right away.)
FINK: And their opponents...weighing at a total combined weight of 452 pounds. Here is the team of the XCW World Champion...Mikey Whipwreck, and The Rock!! ("Loser" starts up and Mikey walks out alone. He doesn't look to happy about it, but he climbs into the ring anyway.)
Joey: Mikey climbs into the ring gets right in both of their faces. Bobby: What's with this kid? He has a death wish or something? Joey: There's the bell, and Mikey goes for a right hand. Angle ducks the punch, and Mikey tries a kick to the stomach and that is caught by Kurt.
<Kurt spins Whipwreck around and Triple H catches him in a clothesline.> Joey: WHOA!!! Now where the hell is The Rock? I can't believe he's not here.... Bobby: Well I think he wants Whipwreck to learn that he has to keep his mouth shut from time to time.
Joey: Mikey is back to his feet and Angle pushes Hunter out of the way to get to the champ. Angle with a chop to the chest, but Mikey comesback with one of his own. Kurt goes for another one, and Mikey comesback again!!!
Bobby: Look at the red on Angle's chest. <Mikey starts unloading with chop after chop.> Joey: This kid has so much fight in him.... Bobby: Almost too much. <Mikey whips Kurt into the ropes and Mikey picks him up for a hotshot...>
Joey: Whoa!! What a move by Mikey Whipwreck...somebody has been teaching him some moves lately. I know it's not the Rock...who is it though?
Bobby: Maybe he's watching old tapes of me? Joey: Ya think? Still no signs of the Rock here tonight, and that sucks. The
Rock was Mikey's friend when we wanted to go back to America, and now he's back he doesn't want to have anything to do with him.
Bobby: Well would you? Look at the punk kid...he's almost as bad as you are. Joey: Kurt gets sent to the turnbuckle, and Mikey follows in. Angle with a boot to the face though.
<Mikey walks backwards, and Angle hits him with a clothesline.> Joey: Mikey is down now, and Angle makes the tag to Triple H. Here is where things are going to get ugly.
Bobby: No doubt about that one... Joey: Triple H brings up Mikey and takes him right back down with a knee to the face...single leg takedown by Hunter now, and he gets back up. Triple H waits for Mikey to make it back up...
<The Game with a few punches to the side of the head, boot to the stomach, and then follows up with a nice text book suplex.>
Joey: Beatiful suplex there by Hunter, and now he's going for the pin...1!! 2!! NO he doesn't get it, and the crowd is still look at the enterance to see if
The Rock is going to show up...
Bobby: He's not...he made it clear in the interview earlier that he wouldn't show. Joey: Hunter with a chinlock on Mikey, and he's going to take all the air out of our champion. Mikey came back to little or no fanfare, and if I were him I'd be upset. He worked hard to bring the XCW back to America, and he's not getting anything for it.
Bobby: He's just a push over. This kid rolls with the punches...he's no leader. Joey: Well he's got the fight in him to be a great leader. He just needs the right person to teach him.
<Mikey fights back to his feet and executes a jawbreaker. Whipwreck gets back up and hits a dropkick on Hunter which makes him go through the middle rope.>
Joey: Mikey is running to the ropes, and Angle comes in and drops him with a leaping clothesline. He caught him hard with that one, and there's no way we're going to see Mikey win without some help.
Bobby: I think you spoke too soon!!! Joey: RIC FLAIR IS HERE!!!! Flair is in Mikey's corner...and he has no idea Ric
Flair is even there. I can't believe we're seeing this!!
Bobby: I can't believe a guy like Flair is helping a guy like Mikey. Joey: Kurt Angle is complaining to the ref, and I don't think that it's going to do much good. The Rock isn't here so Ric Flair decided to take his place, and wait until Mikey see's it...Wait until Mikey Whipwreck see's the Ric Flair is in his corner.
<Angle with a belly to belly over head suplex, and Mikey is hurting bad.>
Bobby: Flair got here right on time...right when Mikey is about to lose.
Joey: Hunter comes back into the ring and he see's Flair, and that doesn't sit to well with him either. Hunter complains to the ref, but he doesn't seem to care. Mikey rolls up Hunter from behind...1!! 2!! NO! Hunter kicks out....
Bobby: Mikey doesn't even know Flair is in his corner. So how's he going to know to tag? Joey: He'll have to look over there at some point... <All of a sudden Ric Flair gives out a huge WHOOOOO and Mikey's eyes get huge. He turns around to see in his corner the one and only Ric Flair. Ric extends his hand, and Mikey tags him.>
Joey: And Flair is in the ring against Triple H!!! Ric unloads with series of punches to the stomach...Flair with a chop to the chest!!!
(WHOOOOO!!) Joey: And another chop...he's a house of fire. In comes Kurt Angle and he gets a backdrop from the Nature Boy. Hunter runs up and he gets a backdrop for his troubles.
<Mikey comes into the ring now...> Joey: Double kick to the stomach on Triple H, and both men are working them over. Wait a minute...the Rock is down here now. The Rock is here and he's in
Ric Flair's face...both men are jawing at each other.
Bobby: Mikey is standing right behind Flair and backing everything Flair says with a YEAH! What a baby!!
Joey: Triple H turns Mikey around and nails him with a boot to the midsection,
Pedigree coming up...NO!! Flair stops it with a thumb to the eye...the ref is trying to get Rock out of the ring, but he's not leaving.
Bobby: Well he's the one that supposed to be in the match not Flair!!! Joey: Flair and Hunter are going at it against the turnbuckle.... <Mikey goes up to Rock and starts talking all kinds of smack to him.> Joey: Mikey is pushing his finger in the chest of the Rock, and the Rock doesn't look like he likes that. Angle from behind Whipwreck....OLYMPIC
SLAM!!!! Bobby: It's over...but the ref is dealing with Hunter and Ric!!! The ref doesn't even see it...
Joey: What's Rocky doing? He picks Angle up...ROCK BOTTOM!!! ROCK BOTTOM!!! The
Rock grabs Mikey and throws him onto Angle, and the ref see's it now...1!!!
2!!! 3!!! Bobby: Whipwreck wins the match!!! FINK: Here are your winners...Ric Flair and The XCW World Champion...Mikey
Whipwreck!!!! Joey: The Rock is in the ring still just staring at Mikey, and now Ric Flair figures out what happened. He knows Rock helped him, and he's going over to shake The Rocks hand.
Bobby: The Rock and Ric Flair together at last!!! Joey: The Rock extends his hand as well does Flair....OH NO!!! ROCK BOTTOM!!! ROCK BOTTOM ON FLAIR, and Rock leaves the ring!!! Fans we're out of time see you next week!!!
(All of a sudden the camera cuts to Taz. He's got Paul E, bound and gagged and he throws him into the trunk of his car. Taz gets into the front seat and takes off pealing out of the parking lot at 90 miles an hour. Camera fades.)
