XCW Monday Night Malice (part 3)

January 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans we're back with more XCW action, and before we aired the show live we had some great action. Lets take you to the dark match results from none other than Sean Mooney!!!
Jesse: Who? -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- (Camera cuts to Sean Mooney who is wearing his suit and tie and hanging out int he crowd. Everyone is going wild as the camera is on them...Sean is getting bumped around, but has a smile on his face the whole time.)
Sean: Fans I'm Sean Mooney and it's crazy here tonight at City Walk. We had some great action before we went live tonight...lets run down the results really quick.
Brainbusters vs. Pitbulls This match tonight was all Pitbulls. They took Arn Anderson and Tully Blanchard by suprise, and Arn only got a string of offense in but it didn't help him at all. The Pitbulls came, saw and conqured with a win here tonight.
Steve Austin vs. Scott Hall Steve Austin came down to the ring like the Austin of old ready to piss the hell out of his opponent. He slid into the ring and tossed Hall a tiny bottle of Jack, and that set the "Bad Guy" off. Austin took total control though, and squashed him like a grape. Austin kept yelling out..."How do ya like that
RVD?"....and finally a KICK! WHAM! STUNNER, and Austin wins the match here tonight.
Sean: There you have it....I'll see you next week. Back to you Jim and Jesse... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- (Camera cuts to RVD who is holding his neck. He takes a deep sigh and walks into the restroom. He walks over to the urinal, and is a little leary about being in a seculed place. He finishes and walks over to the sink where there is a black haired, bearded man sitting there with mints, cologne, and condoms...he has a pair of sun glasses on and says nothing. RVD looks at him and takes a mint....)
RVD: Hey thanks... Bathroom Guy: WHAT!!??!?! (All of a sudden the man rips off his long beard and wig and reveals himself to be Steve Austin again. Austin grabs RVD and throws him into the bathroom mirror. The commentators take over from here....)
Jim: MY GOD!!!! Austin is everywhere tonight...he dispenses some soap, and now he's rubbing it into RVD's eyes.
Jesse: RVD isn't going to be able to wrestle here tonight!!!! Jim: Rob is screaming in pain, and Austin is loving every minute of it. <Austin grabs Van Dam off the sink and drives some lefts and rights onto his face. Austin then kicks him in the stomach, over and over. He flips him off and walks away. Rob tries to make it to his feet only to fall back down>
Jim: I can't believe it. Jesse: How's he going to be able to compete? Jim: I haven't got a damn clue. Austin is a madman, and he's going to ruin the ladder match if he does anything else.

"BOOM!" -| With that...... The silhouette of Chris Jericho appears on the top ramp. An Uproar of Cheers from the audience as the arrival of Jericho takes place. He swings around to look back and forth at the standing crowd scream his name then begins to walk down towards the ring still looking left and right.
Once inside the ring Howard Fink hands him a mic then gets out of Jericho's way. Chris paces back and forth before stopping in the center of the ring. |-

And most importantly The most charismatic showman to ever enter your living rooms VIA Televison Screen-ah."
"Yours Truly has finally arrived. Finally, all you Jerichoholics will not have to suffer from boredom monday and friday nights from watching incompatent nimrods wrestle. Incompatent nimrods such as.... Taz.......Triple H.....
Vader.... Only to name a few." "You Jerichoholics need some entertainment in your lives-ah. Thats where I,
Chris Jericho, The Living Legend, come in."
"It's the donning of a new e-era in the XCW. And a new e-era is what this once proud and profitable company soarly needs. Chris Jericho has came to save the
X.....C.....W!" -| He looks down and raises his arms as the chorus of cheers surround the arena |- -| The crowd dies down as he slowly lifts back his head, this time, smiling |- Jericho : "You people have been led to believe that being a champion is excellence...." "Ah-Uh." "Jericho_is_Excellence." "And Now for the very first time in XCW History, You have a man who can entertain you. You have a man who is good enough for you. You have a man who can make you jump out of your chairs, raise your hands in the air and scream...... 'Go Jericho Go'..... 'Go Jericho Go'..."
Crowd and Jericho: Go Jericho Go.... Go Jericho Go. Jericho : "And now that Y2J is here, This company from the front office idiots,
To all the amateurs in the dressin' room, To all the Jerichoholics watching tonight......."
"Will Never Eh-Eh- Eh- Ever Be the same uh-gain." "And Now that Chris Jericho has come....." -| Jericho's interrupted as Commissioner Curt Hennig's music, 'Exodus' blares over the P.A. system. |- Curt: Hang on a second Chris Jericho. Who do you think you are to come out here and waste these peoples New Years Eve time?
Chris Jericho : "Would You Please-za..... Shut..... Thee.... Hell.... Up-ah." Curt: You're telling Mr. Perfect to shut up? Chris: No, But what the millions of Jerichoholics watching around the globe realize, is that you're a complete Jackass!
Curt: Cut the crap Jericho...what are you out here for? You want to join the XCW? Chris: What am I out here for junior? I'm out here because I want to improve the level of wrestling here in the XCW. Do you think for one second that you can elevate the XCW by putting people like.....Jake Roberts in the ring? I mean come on, the man is a giant bottle of alcohol. What about Bret Hart? He's gone crazy...the man is so obsessed with winning and getting the respect of his son, that he's forgotten about the people who brought him out of the Hart
Foundation.... Curt: So you want to join the XCW? Well that's fine...you're a talented wrestler, but I'm going to make you a deal. Tonight I'll throw you in the Main
Event...you will go against The Rock, Ric Flair, and Mikey Whipwreck. If you can injure Mikey Whipwreck enough to where he can't defend the title anymore here tonight...then you can join the XCW. I'm not asking you to win...I'm asking you to hurt Mikey Whipwreck. Can you do it?
Chris: My God, Mikey Whipwreck?........The Rock? Ric Flair? I Thought this was a wrestling fed not some run of the mill boyscout troop. See, Curt, The reason for the XCW not going anywhere is because, look at the wrestlers you got..... Mr. Nature Boy older than your 'Buck Fifty for a 1 night slam'
wife.....WOOOH! The Rock has got more catchphrases than wrestling maneuvers, and Mikey Wripwreck...little Mikey Whipwreck. God!... He calls himself a champ?, I got more strength in my pinky finger than he got in his whole body.
Injure him.... You got yourself a deal, junior. Curt: Great...now get out of my way and let me pass before I take these hush puppies and stick em' up your ass.
(Curt pushes Jericho away.) Chris: Nobody pushes Thee Ayatollah Of Rock An' Rollah!!! (Jericho forearms Curt from behind and he falls to the canvas.) Jim: HAHA!!! Yeah!! Jericho is looking to the crowd...he's calling for the
Walls Of Jericho, and he locks them in!!! Jericho has locked it in and the fans are going wild.
Jesse: What a way to debut...this might be the last time we see Jericho on TV. Jim: He's got Curt in the Walls Of Jericho, and here comes Magnum TA and the rest of the XCW staff. They're trying to get Chris to let go, and the crowd is loving this.
Jesse: Chris wants to make a point here in the XCW, and I think Curt is going to have a lot to say about this when he gets back to the locker room.
Jim: Jericho finally lets go, and he walks away. Jericho has joined the XCW!!!!
