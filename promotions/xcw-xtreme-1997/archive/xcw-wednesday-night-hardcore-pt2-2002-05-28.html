XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 2)

May 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As we comeback from the commercial Arn Anderson is on the phone again, and it looks like a very important phone call. All of a sudden Triple H bursts in with an injured Steve Armstrong...]
Arn: DAMNIT!!! I'm on the phone! Triple H: You think I care that you're on the phone? Arn: What the hell do you two want? Triple H: Scott Steiner tonight...Handi-Cap match. Arn: Steve's hurt...I can't take that liability. We need all the wrestlers we can get! Steve no...Triple H i -
[Steve slams his arm down onto the desk, and Arn shuts up....] Steve: Oww! I want Scott tonight.... Arn:....can you wrestle? Steve: Yes. Arn: Fine, but if you get injured we're not paying for you to be on the DL. Fair enough? Steve: Yes. Arn: Good now will you two leave. I have to get done with this call.... [Both men leave and the camera fades back to the ring.] Joel: So Ross...I have a story to tell you. Jim: We don't have time for a story of yours. We have to get to the Ric Flair,
Stevie Richards match right now.
Joel: There's always room for a Gertner story. So the other night I was at this club in LA, called Dublins. Really cool hang out spot, and I'm talking to this girl...and end up getting her to comeback to my place.
Jim: We don't need to hear this story. Joel: And she ties me up, and rips my shirt off. Jim: Yuck. Joel: And she's pouring this hot candle wax on to my.... Jim: JOEL!!!! Joel: And it hurt at first, but I can take it. So finally she sticks her.... Jim: JOEL!!! STOP IT!!!! Joel: And it was the tightest thing I've ever be..... Jim: WE'RE ON RIGHT NOW!!!!! Joel: Oh...heh...yeah so what? These people know that I am a sex machine. Jim: You and Wilt Chamberlin... FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Philly, PA...he weighs in at 230 pounds. Here is "Dancin'" Stevie Richards!!! ["Dancing Queen" starts up and the crowd boo's right away. Stevie comes out to some huge heel heat, and he takes it as if they're going crazy for him.]
FINK: And his opponent...from Charolette, North Carolina. He weighs in at 245 pounds...here is "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair!!!
["2001" starts to play and Ric comes down to the ring in his traditional robe. Fireworks blast off behind him, and he climbs into the ring...] [Stevie goes to lock up and Flair ducks out of the way. Ric turns him around and slaps him right across the face, and he does it again. Steve charges him, and Ric takes him down with a drop toe-hold.]
Joel: Ric shouldn't be messing around with losers like Richards. Jim: Stevie Richards has TONS of potiental, but he just doesn't know how to use it. Joel: No...face it he's a loser. [Stevie gets back up and Flair backs him into the corner with some nasty chops. Ric grabs him by the arm and whips him into the other turnbuckle, and Stevie staggers out only to get knocked down with a right to the face.]
Jim: And Stevie rolls out of the ring. Joel: Why doesn't he just stay in there and take it like a damn man? Jim: Would you? Joel: No... Jim: I rest my case. Joel: I'm not a wrestler...Stevie is. [Stevie rolls back into the ring, and Flair goes after him. Stevie sees this and rolls right back out. Ric Flair kicks the bottom ring rope out of frustration, and then goes out after him.]
Jim: Flair isn't going to play these games. Flair makes people play his games no vice-versa.
Joel: Damn right...except when he got locked in the trunk last week. Jim: Flair grabs Stevie and throws him into the guadrail. Flair now unloads with a series of chops to the chest, and he lets out his WHOOOO!!
Joel: Pretty good there limpy. [Flair hits him with a right to the face! And another!! And Another!!! Flair grabs Stevie by the hair and rolls him into the ring. Stevie quickly makes to up to his feet, and puts Flair in a side headlock as he gets into the ring.]
Jim: Flair shoots him into the ropes, and Stevie takes Flair down with a shoulderblock. Joel: But Flair gets right back up. [Stevie runs to the ropes again, and he ducks a clothesline. He turns Flair around and drives a knee into the stomach. Richards goes behind Flair and takes his legs out from under him.]
Jim: Stevie now with a few stomps to the back of the head, and Ric Flair is trying to make it back to his feet. Stevie not letting up, and finally the ref makes him lay off.
Joel: Flair I don't think was prepared for Stevie to give him any offense at all. Jim: Both men lock up again, and Stevie with a nice armdrag takedown. Stevie turns it into an armbar, and Flair reaches the ropes quickly. Give him credit...that was a nice attempt by Richards.
[Richards lets go, and Flair gets back up. Both men are saying something to each other, but the camera can't pick it up. Flair slaps him in the face again, but this time Stevie returns the favor with a punch to the side of the head.]
Jim: Stevie isn't going to take the humiliation tonight. Joel: What makes tonight any different? [Stevie with a scoop slam, and he brings Flair right back up. Stevie runs to the ropes and hits a bulldog, and goes for the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Flair gets his shoulder up. Joel: Who would've thought that Stevie was going to be the first one to make the pin attempt?
Jim: Anything can happen here in the XCW! [All of a sudden we see Tommy Dreamer come out...] Jim: Wait a minute...why is he down here? Joel: Well I guess he's here for revenge? [Tommy has a trash can in his hands, and he throws it into the ring and it hits
Ric Flair right on the head. Mike Kiota calls for the bell, and Dreamer climbs in. Stevie Richards is all smiles.]
Jim: And Dreamer tossed the garbage can onto the head of Flair, and now Stevie is shaking Dreamer's hand.
Joel: Tommy doesn't look to happy. Jim: Dreamer grabs Stevie by the throat, and he's going to chokeslam him. He's going to chokeslam him onto the garbage can!!!
[Dreamer lifts him up, but from the right side of the camera we see Raven climb in and hit Dreamer in the back with a baseball bat. Dreamer grabs his back in pain, and falls down to the mat.]
Jim: RAVEN IS HERE!!!! These two men have a match tonight, and they want to start it early.
Joel: Wow he whaled on him. Jim: Stevie now...he's such a damn coward. Stevie Richards is hugging Raven, and he's so thankfull that he saved him.
[Raven pushes Stevie off of him and kicks him in the stomach. Raven positions himself by the garbage can...EVENFLOW onto it!!! Stevie holds his head, and
Raven just stands there with a blank look on his face.]
Joel: How did I know that was going to happen? Jim: Wait and Dreamer is up, and he's got the bat. Dreamer swings like a madman, and barely misses Raven! Raven slides out of the ring and runs to the locker room, Dreamer is chasing him.
Joel: Follow em!!! [The camera man is running right behind Dreamer as we head back to the locker room area. As we go through the curtain we can see Raven running through a door and out to the parking lot.]
Jim: He's leaving!!! Joel: Do you blame him? [Dreamer pushes the door open, and we see Raven peeling out of the parking lot in his rent-a-car. Dreamer screams and pushes the cameraman out of his way.]
Jim: Raven has taken off...and who knows if there is going to be a main event tonight. Joel: Well back in the ring Flair is up and so is Stevie....OH STEVIE KICK!!!!! Flair just got kicked in the jaw, and Stevie leaves the ring area.
