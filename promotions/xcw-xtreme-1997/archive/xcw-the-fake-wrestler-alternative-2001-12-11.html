The Fake Wrestler Alternative

December 11, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
How many times have you watched RAW or any other wrestling programming, and you looked at your favorite wrestler and said..."Why the hell isn't he doing this?"
Or..."Why isn't he telling them that?"... Well the XCW gives you that chance to do that. Or how about the one underdog that never gets over like Tazz or maybe even
Billy Gunn? Okay maybe not Billy Gunn, but maybe Lance Storm. He's not getting what he deserves, but you have the chance to take him to the top. You have a chance to make him the next Rock.
The XCW isn't a fake wrestler fed...it's not trying to be. What we're giving you is a way to use your favorite wrestlers in a fantasy enviroment. A lot of people say there's no originality involved, but I disagree...
In the XCW you won't be just using The Rock...you can make the Rock whoever you want him to be. If you want the Rock to be a cowardly heel, or a guy who takes on anyone in the world...you can do that.
The XCW is going to have two cards a week, and 1 PPV a month. The cards will be written extremely well, and entertaining and that's a promise.
This is going to be a fed where people can come and just have fun. I want people to enjoy reading the cards, enjoy interacting with their other players, and just overall have a good time.
So if you're interested in trying something a little different, but just as much fun then e-mail me or IM me.
