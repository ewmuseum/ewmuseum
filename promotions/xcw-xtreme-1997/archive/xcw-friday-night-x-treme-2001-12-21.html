XCW Friday Night X-Treme

December 21, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Before the show even starts we see Hurricane Helms lying on the concrete floor in the parking lot knocked out. His Hurri-cycle has been knocked over, and people are all around him. He's trying to make it to his feet, but can't seem to get a grip. People start asking who did it, and nobody seems to know. )
X-TREME (Camera cuts to a small crowd in the cold Japanese Hall. Fans are packed to the rafters there though, and the signs are plenty. Everything from "Screw
Whipwreck", to "Misawa can't Wrestle"....to "Lawler is a pussy". We cut to the commentators, Joey Styles and Jerry Lawler. Both men have smiles on their faces, and Lawler looks so happy to have a job.)
Joey: Welcome everyone to X-Treme. It's late at night, the kids are in bed and it's time to get X-Treme!! With me is Jerry Lawler, and tonight we're going to have an amazing line up for you. Matches like you've never seen before....
Jerry: Joey I am so glad to be here, and I'm so glad to have a job. You see
I've made some bad choices in my life and marrying a gold digging bitch was one of them. I'm back on track now, and the XCW is going to be awesome this time around.
Joey: Umm..I guess that's good news. Jerry: Of course it is stupid. Joey: Fans lets not wait anymore. Lets take you to our first match between Rob
Van Dam and Tajiri. This is going to be a Singapore Cane match on a pole, and not only is it going to be violent...but I'm sure the action in this match is going to be incredible.
Jerry: Well you have to be an idiot to sign up for this match, and that's what these two people are. You have Rob Van Dam who is nothing but a pot smoker who does some high spots here and there, and then you have Tajiri who is just glad to have a green card in America.
Joey: You know what I just notices...everyone who works on the XCW announce teams name starts with a J.
Jerry: Hmmm... Joey: ANYWAY lets take you to Stacy Kiebler for the ring announcements.... STACY: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall and is a
Singapore Cane on a Pole Match. First coming down to the ring from Battlecreek,
Michigan. He weighs in at 245 pounds. Here is Rob Van Dam!!!
(The crowd stands up as RVD comes through the curtain. He's got a huge smile on his face and slides into the ring. Rob does a few kicks, and some stretches and waits for Tajiri....)
STACY: And his opponent...from Yokohama, Japan. Here is Tajiri!!! (Tajiri comes out and works the crowd into a frenzy. They seem to be excited that he's here and he has a Japanese Flag waving in the air.)
Joey: This is going to be an incredible match here tonight. Tajiri and Rob Van
Dam, and then there's the Singapore Cane...whew. This isn't going to be pretty.
Jerry: HAHA someones going to get their brains beat in here tonight. Joey: There's the bell and Van Dam starts up with a Spin Kick, but Tajiri ducks out of the way. Tajiri goes for one of his own, but it's blocked by Van Dam now. Van Dam with a right hand...
Jerry: This is going to be like a Kung-Fu movie!!! Joey: Tajiri comesback with a kick to the ribs, and Rob with a punch to the side of the face. Sidekick by RVD, and he nails Tajiri with jumping sidekick.
Rob goes over to the pole, and now he's reaching for that Singapore Cane.... Jerry: You think he thinks that's just one big joint, and he's reaching to smoke it? Joey: I don't think so. Van Dam reaches for it, and here comes Tajiri. Rolling kick by Tajiri and that hits RVD in the small of the back, and he falls backwards. What a cool move by Tajiri.
Jerry: These fans seem to like him, but Tajiri has been very vocal about going back to the US. As a matter of fact everyone has been....
Joey: Tajiri goes for a kick to the side of the head, but RVD blocks it with a legsweep. Rob runs to the ropes...corkscrew legdrop. He's going for the pin, but he doesn't even get a one count.
Jerry: I smell Kikoman! Joey: It's not me. Rob runs to the ropes and knocks down a dazed Tajiri. He runs up the turnbuckle now and he grabs the cane. Rob waits for his opponent to make it to his feet...he holds the stick up high. Oh no....
Jerry: He's not goi....AHHHH!!! Joey: OH MY GOD!!!! Rob Van Dam crashes that Kendo stick down on the skull of
Tajiri and the fans can't believe it either. Van Dam up now and he smashes that cane across the back, and another shot.
Jerry: Ouch!!! Joey: Van Dam with a bodyslam, and now he's going to the outside ring apron. Slingshot Bodysplash...and Tajiri puts his knees up!!! Whoa!!! Tajiri brings
Van Dam up and takes him back down with a devastating spinning thrust kick!!!
Jerry: I heard Tajiri taught Bruce Lee everything he knows. Joey: Tajiri was like 5 when Bruce Lee died. Jerry: Yeah he's that good. Joey: Tajiri grabs the cane and drives it into the face of Rob Van Dam, and that hit the bridge of the nose!!! Look at the blood pour out of the nose, and that was instant.
Jerry: Instant blood.... Joey: Van Dam is holding his nose in his hands, and Tajiri grabs the right arm and he locks into an arm bar. Smart move...Rob Van Dam won't be able to attend to that nose now, and this isn't good for "Mr Monday Night"!
Jerry: He'll choke on that blood. Joey: Van Dam is trying to get out of that arm bar, but at the same time he has to wipe the blood from his nose...and get it out of his mouth. Painting a great picture I hope for those listening on their radio!!!
Jerry: Yeah it's pretty disgusting. Joey: Tajiri has that arm bar locked in, and this past Monday we saw RVD have a knee problem. Now it's a nose problem...what's next for him?
Jerry: Maybe a Million Dollars. Joey: Rob Van Dam has that match with Ted DiBiase this week, and it looks like he's going to be a millionare. Can you imagine if RVD takes a Million Dollars away from DiBiase?
Jerry: Oh man...DiBiase will be a million dollars shy of being a millionare. Joey: RVD does a kip-up, and makes it back to his feet. That breaks the arm lock by Tajiri, and RVD runs to the ropes. Tajiri does the same...and Tajiri catches him with a Swinging DDT!!!!!
Jerry: He planted his head into the mat like it was a flower. Joey: Tajiri now with a series of kicks to the nose of Rob Van Dam, and this isn't going to be good for RVD. Rob looks like he's in some serious trouble now, and Tajiri is working him over....
Jerry: They need to use the cane more!!! Joey: As you say that Tajiri comes over and starts choking the life out of RVD. He's choking him out with that cane and anything is legal here. Van Dam now trying to break free, and he does. He makes it back up and ducks a spin kick by
Tajiri.
Jerry: He did the splits!!! Joey: Rob back up now and drives a thumb into the eye of Tajiri and he throws him to the outside of the ring!!!! Van Dam with a running start and TOPE!!!! He nails Tajiri with a tope, and look at the blood now pouring out of Rob's nose....
Jerry: It's broken alright. Joey: Van Dam grabs a chair now and throw it into the ring. Tajiri gets thrown into the ring right after that. Tajiri grabs the stick, and Van Dam grabs the chair. Van Dam swings and misses, and so does Tajiri...Van Dam throws the chair into the head of Tajiri now.
Jerry: He's seeing flying fortune cookies now.... Joey: Van Dam with a Fishermans Buster, and now he's going for the cover. 1!! 2!! Tajiri kicks out on the count of 2 and 1/2. Jerry: That was close... Joey: Van Dam is going outside again, and this time he's getting a table. He slides it into the ring and here comes Tajiri...RUNNING SPACE TIGER FLIP!!!!!!!
Jerry: A what!? Joey: Tajiri lands right on Rob Van Dam and now both men are out....this doesn't look good. Tajiri regains himself and throws Rob back into the ring.
Tajiri is going to the top...Rob back to his feet though and makes a run up the turnbuckle. JUMPING FRANKENSTEINER!!!!!!!!
Jerry: Now he's setting up the table... Joey: He throws Tajiri on the table...and now he's going to the top ropes. FIVE STAR FROG SPLASH!!!!! HE HITS IT!!!! 1!!! 2!! 3!!! STACY: Here is your winner...Rob Van Dam!!!! Jim: What a match that was here tonight on X-Treme.... (Camera cuts to Mitsuharu Misawa along with Triple H and the rest of The Rising
Suns. They're all sitting around talking about last week and laughing about it.)
Triple H: Heh...did you see the look on The Rocks face when I double crossed him? He was like..."The Rock can't believe his people's eyeballs!"
Misawa: HAHAH!!! Triple H: I'll tell you what I'm glad I'm here in Japan with you fellas. We've got great food, great women, and some great humor. I think I'm going to like it here...
(All of a sudden there's a knock at the door...) Triple H: What the hell? What's this? (The message boy hands him a letter....) Misawa: What is it? Triple H: HA...it's a little that says..."I will return". That must be from that Whipwreck kid...heh.
Misawa: He is little piss-ant. Triple H: Well I'm going to make him bleed tonight....haha! (Camera fades.)
