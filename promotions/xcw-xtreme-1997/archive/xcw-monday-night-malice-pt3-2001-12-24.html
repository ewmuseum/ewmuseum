XCW Monday Night Malice (part 3)

December 24, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades onto a man sitting at a table reading an old issue of PWI. You can tell it's cold, and that the wind is blowing fiercer than anything you've ever seen in America or Japan...the place is Russia, the man is Nikita
Koloff.)
Nikita: People forget....they forget great leaders, they forget great men. You see I have already been forgoten, and people know nothing of me. My country has been forgotten, as well as it's people. Heros come and go....but enemies live on forever. Why? Heh it's very simple...the reason is because the hate you build for someone stays inside of you, the hate builds like a small fire...and soon it rages out of control. Before you know it, your heart is beating a million miles and hour...the beads of sweat are dripping from your face, and your fists clinch and you dream about killing your worst enemy. All because of an emotion. All because of what you feel inside your heart. This is not the feeling of a hero, but of someone who is evil. Someone who cannot know what love is because they've seen so much pain. I am like this. I have seen too much pain in my country....in my family....in my wife. I come to the XCW not for titles, not for glory...I want to just share my hurt and pain with others. I want to take my hate out on others. Russia used to be the place where dreams are made, and now it's a place the bares nightmares....I will return, and I will bring honor to my country and to my people. Long live Mother Russia.....
(Camera fades) Jim: Nikita Koloff is coming back...and we're all in big trouble. Jesse: Well you have Vader, and Taz...two of the biggest, baddest bullies of them all...and now we have Nikita Koloff. Guys like the Hurricane aren't going to be safe in the XCW anymore.
Jim: Can you imagine what it would be like to see Nikita get into the ring against someone like Vader? Maybe even The Rock?
Jesse: This XCW is getting better and better by the day, and in a month it's going to beat every single fed there is out there.
Jim: Well not only is Nikita Koloff coming to the XCW, but look who's sitting in the front row tonight. Ladies and gentlmen if we can get a camera over there...look at that!!
Jesse: TERRY FUNK IS HERE!?!?! Jim: That's right...Terry Funk has joined the XCW, and he'll be competing this coming Friday.
Jesse: Wow...I thought he was dead. Jim: Fans the next match we have here tonight is The Million Dollar Match. In order to win you have to climb the ladder and grab the Million Dollars....wait a second. I understand there's some action in the back of the locker room...
(Camera cuts to the locker room and Vader is standing over a broken RVD. It looks as though Vader has just power bombed RVD through a table, and now he's screaming for Taz.)
Jim: Vader is on the loose again!!!! Jesse: We need a cage for that man!!! Jim: RVD is broken in half. He's not moving and Vader doesn't care...he just put another one of our wrestlers out!!!
Jesse: That's got to be another fine by the XCW. Jim: Vader is searching the locker room for Taz, and I think Taz has already left the building. Oh..he bumped intot he Million Dollar Man. Uh oh....wait.
DiBiase is flashing money at him, and Vader seems to be calming down. Jesse: I wouldn't say that!! Jim: Vader grabs DiBiase by the shirt and headbuts him!!! Oh!! SOMEBODY GET HIM
UNDER CONTROL!!!! Here comes security and they're spraying mace in his eyes...they've got night sticks and they're beating him down to the ground.
Jesse: The man is insane. Jim: While they're taking care of it...I guess we should kill some time. It looks like we're not going to have that match here tonight. The million dollars is still suspended in the air.
Jesse: Go get it. Jim: No. Jesse: Then we'll split it, and quit. We'll fly back to the US tonight, and not worry about wrestling anymore.
Jim: No. Steal....wait a minute. Someone's just hoped the barier and they're going up the ladder. Who is that? That's a fan!! A fan has just started climbing the ladder and he's grabbed the suitcase!!!
Jesse: Smart man!! I don't remember seeing any black fans here tonight....they don't have big black people living in Japan!!!
Jim: Wait a damn minute that's not a fan...that's Booker T!!! Booker T is here and he's stealing the money!!!! What a damn thief....
Jesse: Terry Funk feels dumb now. He should've gone in there and stolen the money. Jim: Booker T came out of no where and just stole the million dollars. Jesse: I doubt we'll ever see him again. (Camera cuts to Mikey Whipwreck and Ric Flair sitting in the dressing room talking.) Ric: Now Misawa is very technical.... Mikey: Understandable. He's a machine. Ric: Chono is very much a brawler... Mikey: Oh yeah, definitely low down and dirty Ric: Who do you go after first? Mikey: Well first, I was thinking about using the restroom, then maybe grabbing some nachos, heading to the parking lot, maybe snooze a little..
Ric: Listen kid...you're not talking to The Rock. You're not talking to your mom or dad. You're talk to Ric Flair...now if you don't want my help I'll walk right out that door. Now I need to see some confidence in you kid. Give me a
WHOOOO!!! Mikey: Ok.. Uh... Woooo.... Ric: What the hell was that? You sounded like a girl...come on...WHOOOOOO!!! Mikey: Whoooooooo.....! Ric: Man...you have anything in those boxers of yours? COME ON!!! Mikey: WHOOOO! Ric: WHOO!!!!!!! Listen kid...I'm Ric "WHOOO" Flair, I've traveled across this
World a million times, held a million belts, been with a million women...I'm sick and tired of being in Japan and I want to go home to North Carolina... Now if you're not going to be able to do that then let me know by God... Because if you can't I'll find someone else who can. Can you do it? CAN YOU WHOOOOO DO
IT?
Mikey: Of course, but for a price. (Ric Flair takes his tie off and throws it down to the ground...he turns red with anger and gets right in the kids face...)
Mikey: NO! I just want an autographed KISS album... That's all Ric: You wanna dick around with me kid? I'm not a fairy tale, I'm the real deal..and it's time for you to step up to the plate and be a man. Tonight you have a chance to be the man...not some snot nose little brat that you are right now. NOW GROW UP OR I'll BEAT THE MATURITY OUT OF YOU!!! Now give me a WHOOO and it better by God be the best damn WHOO I've ever heard or I'm going to take those knees that are shakin' and make you look like Terry Funk!
Mikey: WHOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ric: That was good...give me another! Mikey: WHOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ric: WHOOOOO!!!!! Who's the man? (Flair unbuttons his shirt.) Ric: Chop me kid!!! WHOOO!! Chop me! CHOP ME!!! (Mikey gently chops Flair and trembles. Flair returns the favor with a huge chop.) Mikey:YEWWWW-OWCH! (Flair chops him again) Mikey: WHOOOOO!!! (Flair smiles, laughs and leaves the room whooing.) Mikey: (Under his breath) I'm dead meat!!!
