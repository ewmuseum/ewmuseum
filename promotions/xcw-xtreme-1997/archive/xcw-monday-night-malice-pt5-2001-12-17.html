XCW Monday Night Malice (part 5)

December 17, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans we are ready for the last match here tonight, and hopefully the last match in Japan. It's all done to The Game, the man that most people hate back in America...but tonight I'm sure they're counting on a win here.
Jesse: If Triple H wins this match tonight he can forget about calling himself a heel, because this will turn him into a face right away!!
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall and is for the XCW WORLD TITLE!!! The stipulations are as followed...if Triple H wins the match here tonight then he will get to take the XCW and the XCW World Title back home with him to the USA.
(Crowd boos.) FINK: If Mitsuharu Misawa wins tonight the XCW doesn't have another chance to go back to the US for another 3 months. So without further wait here is the special guest ref for tonights match...ladies and gentlemen here is The Rock!!!
("If ya smell...what the Rock...is cookin'" is heard all over the pay, and the crowd boo's him right away. They hate the fact that he's siding with Sonny Ono.
Rock comes down to the ring with a smile on his face, climbs into the ring and works the crowd.)
FINK: Introducing the challenger...from Connecticut. He weighs in at 275 pounds, here is "The Game" Triple H!!!!
("Are you ready to play The Game" by Motorhead starts to play, and Triple H comes out with a very serious look on his face. He makes his way down to the ring and just has a huge stare down with the Rock.)
FINK: And his opponent...he is currently the XCW World Champion, and the legend that XCW thought they'd never have...led down to the ring by Masa Chono, The
Great Muta and Hiroshi Tenzan....here is Mitsuharu Misawa!!!
(The lights go out and fireworks start to blaze across the arena. The theme from "2001" starts to play and then it busts out into "Unforgiven" by
Metallica, and out comes the whole Rising Sun crew. They are all ahead of
Misawa....he comes out with the title draped around his shoulder, and he has no expression on his face at all. He climbs into the ring and he and Triple H have some words. He walks over to The Rock and shakes his hand for incredible heel heat.)
Jim: I cannot believe The Rock!! Damn him!!! Damn him....I hope the Rock gets the hell beaten out of him here tonight. Look at Triple H the man looks like he's ready to kill somebody, and that person better be the Rock....
Jesse: Uh oh and I think you're going to get your wish...Triple H and The Rock are face to face, and it looks like they're going to duke it out Jim...it looks lik....what!???
Jim: What in the hell? YES!!! YES!!! YES!!! The Rock and Triple H are shaking hands, and Misawa can't believe it!!! This was a set up the whole damn time, and The Rock had us all fooled!!! The Rock never turned...he was screwing with them all along, and now he's going to guest ref this match!!!!
Jesse: Smart move by The Rock!! Jim: We're going back to America by God, and the Rock and Triple H are the ones swiming us to the shore. I can't believe it!!!!!!
Jesse: Misawa can't believe his eyes, and The Rock is talking tash right to his face. HA, but Misawa has no expression on it at all.
Jim: WAIT A DAMN MINUTE!!!! WAIT A DAMN MINUTE!!! NO NO!!!! OH HELL NO!!! GOOD GAWD ALMIGHTY!!!!! DAMN YOU TRIPLE H!!!!!! Jesse: Triple H just attacked the Rock from behind, and now he and Misawa are beating the day lights out of him.
Jim: WHAT A TURN COAT!!!! Jesse: Misawa with a series of punches to the side of The Rocks face, and now
Hunter with some of his own. Someone get the hell down there and stop this crap! Where's security?
Jim: Misawa grabs Rock...TIGER DRIVER '91. This match was never going to be a match in the first place. They knew all along and they set the Rock up...they set us up, and they set everyone in the back up.
Jesse: Triple H has suprised not only everyone here, but I'm sure he's even suprised his own wife.
Jim: Pedigree by Hunter...The Rock isn't moving, and this is horrible. The worst day in wrestling history was when we had to move here, but this ranks right up there. The Rock isn't moving, and now all of Misawa's cronies are in the ring...
Jesse: Never in the history of wrestling have I seen something like this. Triple H and Misawa...I never thought I'd see them together. Jim: Everyone is taking their turns at Rocky, and he's not moving. WAIT!!! Here comes Mikey Whipwreck. That kids a damn fighter, and he's facing Misawa next week....
Jesse: He's got guts, but not a lot of brains!!! Jim: Mikey's got a chair and he's swinging all over the place. He nails Muta in the head with a chairshot, and he hits Chono with one too....he backdrops
Tenzan over the top ropes. Misawa and Triple H see him now, and Triple H kicks him in the stomach...PEDIGREE ON WHIPWRECK!!!!
Jesse: Well so much for that idea.... Jim: Mikey Whipwreck, and The Rock are laid out in the ring. Triple H and
Misawa have gotten the better of these two, and tonight we sit here and know that we're going to be in Japan AGAIN next week. This is horrible.
Jesse: I can't imagine what the next few weeks are going to bring us. Jim: Well I can damn sure promise that the Rock isn't going to just sit around and look pretty. The Rock got screwed tonight, and there's going to be hell to pay all around the table. They're telling us we're out of time....we've got to go folks. I hope you enjoyed Malice tonight...kids I hope you're watching at home. Daddy loves you and he'll see you soon!!!
Jesse: So long!!! (Camera fades on Misawa and Triple H holding up each others hands in victory.)
