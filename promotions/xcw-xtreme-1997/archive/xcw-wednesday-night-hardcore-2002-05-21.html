XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore

May 21, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Camera cuts to a sold out crowd at the Staple Center in Los Angeles, CA. Fans are going wild as the return of the XCW is here. People have been waiting in line for a week to get tickets, and tonight is the big night. Fireworks go off in traditional fashion, and the camera pans across the crowd to see all different signs. We cut to Jim Ross who is standing next to Joel Gertner....]
Jim: WELCOME EVERYONE!!!! We're live here in the Staple Center, and tonight is the night the XCW comesback!!! Arn Anderson has lined up a card that would knock the socks off anybody...and I'm excited to be back.
Joel: You're excited? I get a pay check after this!! I haven't done anything but eat since I've been away. Tonight I get my job back, a little cash...and I get to watch the XCW.
Jim: Sounds like you're about as excited as I am! Joel: Heh...no I'm really not. They're just paying me to say this crap. [With that the lights go out and the sound of Horses running is heard throughout the arena. Arn Anderson walks out from the curtain, and the crowd pops like crazy. He's got a huge smile on his face as he climbs into the ring.
Arn grabs the microphone and begins to speak...] Arn: Ya know...as I was driving into the arena tonight all I saw were screaming fans. Fans that have stuck with the XCW through thick, and thin. Tonight I pay you back with the best I have to offer. Yeah we don't have Vader roaming through the locker rooms...we don't have The Rock. Tonight we have Ric
Flair....Mikey Whipwreck...and so many other talented wrestlers who hav... ["Break The Walls Down" is heard over the PA, and Chris Jericho walks out with a big smirk on his face. He stops at the walkway, and begins to speak.]
Chris: Arn Anderson...will you do us all a favor, and PUH-LESE SHUT THE HELL
UP! [Crowd starts to boo.] Chris: You come out here and you act like you're the messiah of wrestling. You act like you're going to turn this fed around, and that the XCW is going to be some little crappy fed. Well let me tell you something...the only people who are going to turn this fed around are ME, Flair, and Scott Hall.
[Crowd starts to chant..."You Suck!"] Chris: Yeah I don't suck...this fed sucks. How dare you not book me on this card, Arn... Arn: I only book talented people on my shows. [Crowd pops, and Chris kinda laughs it off.] Chris: I've got more talent in my ass, that you have in your whole body. Arn: Heh...fine you want a match? You want a match here tonight, then this is what I'm going to do. Go find yourself an opponent, and if you beat him...then you can wrestle whenever you want.
Chris: So let me review this. I find someone...anyone...and I beat them...I can wrestle whenever I want.
Arn: Whenever. Chris: You got yourself a deal Arnie!!! [Jericho's music starts to play and he walks back to the dressing room. Arn steps out of the ring and we cut back to JR and Joel.]
Jim: So there you have it...Chris Jericho will face someone tonight, and he gets the chance to be able to wrestle whenever he wants on a Wednesday card.
Joel: Does that include title matches? Jim: I'm not sure. Joel: Well what's our first match? Jim: Steve Armstrong and Ric Flair looking to lock up here tonight, and talk about two solid vets in the ring. Both men have talent and skill...
Joel: Yeah, but then again one had success and the other one is Steve Armstrong. Jim: The Armstrong curse is a mighty one, and hopefully he'll be able to shake that this time around. Lets take you to Tammy Sytch with a word from Steve
Armstrong.....
[Camera cuts to the backstage where Steve is pacing back and forth...Tammy tries to get his attention.]
Tammy: Steve...can I get a quick word with you? Steve: I really rather not. But whatever Tammy: Many people are wondering why Arn Anderson would hire you...you really aren't what the XCW considers a superstar...Can you justify your job here?
Steve: Justify my job here? You have got to be out of pretty little blond mind. I'm a member of the South's greatest wrestling family. And as such, I go so far back with Arn Anderson that you were probably still a virgin when we met. The company may not know me well enough. But Arn Anderson does. And that's what really matters.
Tammy: I doubt it honey...now what I want to talk about is the curse. The
Armstrong curse...you think you're going to shake it?
Steve: Blondie, do yourself a favor and NEVER say those three words to me again. The so-called Armstrong Curse is a joke, a fabrication, a fraud. It was an excuse invented by redneck know-nothings so they could disguise the fact they wouldn't know talent if it bit them in the goddamn nose
Tammy: Uhh..like whatever. I was talking about your luck with the women. Not the fact that the whole family is filled with a bunch of losers.
Steve: Oh. Yeah. Very funny coming from a woman who nearly threw her career away trying to be her own pharmacist.
Tammy: Listen Steve...I think it's best that you stick to losing matches, instead of trying to be witty. Save the brain stuff for me, and the rest of the crew. You...you go on and lose that match to Ric Flair.
Steve: And you listen to me, Blondie. Ric Flair may be legend. So is Harley
Race. So is Bruno Sammartino. So is Killer Kowallski. Doesn't mean any of those old fossils still belong in a ring. Flair needs to recognize that as a wrestler he is done. That he's facing a younger, faster more capable wrestler and it's time to hang them up.
Tammy: Umm how old are you? Steve: I'm younger than dirt. Unlike Flair. And that is what's going to matter in this match. Ric, you're always dribbling about how to be the man, you gotta beat the man. Well, after all these years of being stepped on by good ole boy idiots, I'm about to become the man by beating the man. You don't have to like it. But learn to love it. Because it's what's going in the world today.
Tammy: There ya have it folks...Steve Armstrong ready to break his families curse. Steve: Yeah. Curse. Whatever. [Steve storms off....and we got to the ring for the match.] FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Atlanta, GA. He weighs in at 256 pounds...here is Steve
Armstrong!!!
[Steve comes out to no music at all, and the crowd is silent as he walks out. He tries to slap a few hands, but nobody wants to even touch him. All of a sudden a bunch of smart-ass fans start chanting "WHO IS THIS....WHO IS THIS"?
This pisses Steve off, and he starts yelling from the ring.] FINK: And his opponent...from Charolette, North Carolina. He weighs in at 245 pounds...here is "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair!!!
[The theme from 2001 starts to play, and fireworks go off. Ric Flair steps through the curtain wearing a red & gold robe. He's sporting his long blonde locks, and a very cocky look on his face. He parades around the ring before climbing in...and when he does he gives a huge "WHOOOO" which the fans respond to with delight.]
Jim: There's the bell and this one is underway!!! [Both men lock up, and struggle for position. Steve with a quick side headlock, and Flair whips him into the ropes. Steve bounces off and knocks Flair down with a shoulderblock.]
Jim: And Steve takes the advantage here tonight with a nice shoulderblock.... Joel: Yeah that's going to win him a lot of matches!!! [Steve yells for Ric Flair to get back up, and Flair does without taking his eyes off of him. Both men lock up again, and this time Flair with a side headlock take down, and Armstrong with a headscissors. Flair squirms out of it, and both men get back to their feet. Flair gets right into Steve's face, and
Steve slaps the hell out of him!]
Jim: Oh my!!! Steve just slapped the damn taste right out of Ric Flair's mouth, and I don't think Ric has ever been humiliated like that.
Joel: Only when he wrestles without his shirt on...that humiliates even me. Those boobs are about as bad as Phyllis Dillers. Jim: Flair tells him to slap him again, and Steve does it again. Joel: Can someone tell me where Steve got his balls from? He's never had the guts to do something like that before, and now he's slapping Flair like a bitch!
Jim: Well he sure is. OH MAN!!! Flair returns the favor with a knife-edge chop, and another one...and Steve is feeling that.
[Ric backs Steve into the ropes and unloads with a series of nasty chops to the chest. Armstrong's chest is dark red, and Flair follows up with a thumb to the eye. Ric with a whip to the ropes, and hits a backdrop!]
Jim: Steve goes down like a ton of bricks, and Flair has awaken. [Ric brings Steve back to his feet and hits him with a fist to the jaw, and another fist. The Nature Boy drives a knee into the stomach, and takes him down with a snap mare. Flair with a chinlock....]
Jim: Ric is trying to wear Steve down right now, and he's cutting off the air supply. This will wear you down slowly, but once it's taken effect there won't be much room for escape.
Joel: That's fine and dandy, but what the hell are the fans supposed to do during this. I mean the kid in the front row is taking a nap.
Jim: Well I sure hope you weren't expecting moonsaults, or garbage cans. Joel: No, but this is Ric Flair. He can carry a garbage can to a 5 star match. Jim: Well Steve Armstrong is no garbage can! [Steve finally makes it to his feet, and breaks free from the chinlock. Ric kicks him in the stomach, and hits a vertical suplex. Flair rolls over onto him for the cover...]
1!! 2!!! Kickout!!! [Flair gets up and complains to the ref about the slow count, and as Steve is getting up Ric starts to choke him out. The ref lays on the count, and Flair stops at 4. Then once again Flair lays in the choke...he stops at four again, and then brings Armstrong to his feet. Flair with an Irish Whip, but Steve reverses the move and Flair goes crashing into the turnbuckle.]
Jim: And Steve charges into the corner with a clothesline, and Flair gets rocked!!! [Steve lets Flair stagger out of the turnbuckle, and he climbs up onto the top rope. Flair turns around, and Steve nails a beautiful flying clothesline! Both men go down, but Steve quickly makes it back up.]
Jim: Steve is up and Flair is down! Joel: Ya think? Jim: Armstrong brings Nature Boy to his feet, and drives an elbow into the back of the head. Another nice elbow, and Flair drops to one knee. Steve with a boot to the side of the head!!!
[Flair falls to the canvas, and Steve starts stomping away on him. The ref finally pulls Armstrong away, and as he's talking to him Flair crawls over and hits a low blow.]
Joel: Check please!! Jim: That's going to make him wish he was another Armstrong! [Flair grabs Steve by the hair and throws him over the top rope. Armstrong lands and a loud THUD! is heard as he smacks the cement with his body. Ric
Flair ignores the refs warning, and goes right after Steve.]
Jim: Ric Flair grabs the camera cord and wraps it around the throat!!! He's choking the life out of him, and the ref isn't doing a damn thing about it.
Joel: Well this is X-Treme Championship Wrestling. Jim: It is, and tonight this is plain old X-Treme. Joel: Jim...don't lie to these people. You're turning into Tony Schivone. Jim: I apologize folks!! [Flair throws Armstrong into the guardrail, and as he walks towards him Steve kicks him in the stomach. Steve slams Flair's head into the rail...not once, not twice, but three times...and Flair falls down.]
Jim: That...that busted him open!!! Ric Flair is lying on the ground busted open, and look at the smile on Steve's face.
Joel: Well I'm sure he's smiling because this is the longest match he's ever been apart of.
Jim: That's so very true. Joel: Who would've known? [Steve rolls Flair onto his back, and starts unloading with a series of punches to the forehead. He stops for a moment to break the count, and then rolls back out. Steve grabs Flair and bodyslams him down onto the cement, and follows up with an elbow.]
Jim: He's showing Ric Flair no mercy. What a brutal bout this is... Joel: Yeah...it's almost as bad as the Civil War. Interestingly enough Steve fought for the south, and Flair fought for the North.
[Steve walks over and throws the time keeper off of his chair and grabs it. Steve tosses the chair into the ring, and walks over to toss Flair in. When
Steve rolls back in, Flair catches him with a knee to the side of the head.]
Jim: Steve wasn't paying any attention at all, and Flair caught him with that knee to the head.
[Flair lifts Steve up and hits a chop, and another chop. Flair with an inverted atomic drop...and he grabs the chair.]
Jim: Flair is going to hit him with that chair... [Flair puts it over his head....] Joel: Kill him!!!! [The crowd is on their feet....] Jim: Damnit Flair don't do it.... [He is about to unload when all of a sudden Arn Anderson runs into the ring and grabs the chair from his best friend. Flair turns around and is shocked that
Arn would do this, and the shock turns to anger as he gets right in Arns face.
Both men are having words with each other.] Jim: OH THIS IS ABOUT TO BLOW UP!!!!! Joel: Oh yeah!!! Clock him Flair...knock him out!!! [Flair reels back to hit Arn, and Steve grabs it and executes a backslide. The ref is in position...]
1!!!! 2!!!! 3!!!! FINK: Here is your winner....Steve Armstrong!!!!! Joel: WHAT THE HELL!?!?!?! Jim: Steve did it!! He broke the curse!!! He beat Ric Flair of all people!!! My
God!!! What did Arn Anderson do to his friend????
[Armstrong rolls out of the ring with a huge smile on his face. Arn starts to clap, and Flair gets up right away and turns him around...]
Jim: Uh oh...and Ric Flair is red. He's not a happy man. Joel: Well I guess Arn was just trying to be the Enforcer he said he would... [We can hear scattered cuss words as Flair is going insane. Arn is staying remotely calm, and Ric starts pushing his index finger into the chest of Arn.
He's getting hotter and hotter...and he finally explodes!!!] Jim: OH MY!!!! He just slapped Arn!!! He slapped his best friend...he slapped the commissioner of the XCW!!!!!
Joel: Arn doesn't look to happy... [Arn looks at the crowd and the grabs Flair by his golden locks. Arn starts to yell at him, and point his finger in Flair's face. Ric by this time is cowering, and begging him not to attack...]
Jim: Arn is going to hit him!!! Joel: Don't do it Arn...set the example. Jim: He...OH MY GOD!!!! Scott Hall with a chair to the back of Anderson's head. Scott Hall just came out of no where, and smashed that damn chair over the head of Arn...and our commish goes down.
Joel: See that's what you get when you mess with Ric Flair!!! [Scott and Flair start to stomp away on the back of Arn Anderson. Both of them get their shots in, and start to leave. As they walk away Flair stips on the commissioner of the XCW....]
Jim: Fans we'll be right back!!!!
