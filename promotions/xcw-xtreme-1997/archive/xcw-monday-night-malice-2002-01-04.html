XCW Monday Night Malice

January 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We fade into the parking structure at Universal Studios, City Walk in Hollywood. There are hundreds of cars waiting to be valet parked, and hundreds of people already walking to the area in which Malice will be held. Mikey pulls up and throws the keys to his Canyon Arrow to the Valet. Fans don't really notice him, but they notice when a huge limo pulls up, and out walks Ric Flair.
Mikey turns around and smiles...) Mikey: Umm...Mr. Flair, I don't want any hard feelings tonight between us. I have nothing but respect for you, and when I beat you tonight...I don't want you to hate me for it.
Ric: Mikey....if all the little people had your confidence, the world would be a better place. Now excuse me...
(Ric Flair walks by him, and Mikey is left speechless.) LIVE FROM CITY WALK IN HOLLYWOOD, CALIFORNIA It's MONDAY NIGHT MALICE!!!! Pop!! Bang!! Boom!! Pow!! Crack!! Pop!! Bang!! Boom!! Pow!! Crack!! Pop!! (Fireworks go off all over the place, and the fans are going wild. They standing out in the cold by the Hardrock Cafe' waiting to see some XCW action.
Some fans have signs, others have t-shirts of their favorite XCW Superstars. We pan across the crowd really fast and then come across Good Ol' JR, along with
Jesse "The Body" Ventura at ringisde. Both men seem extremely excited about tonight, and the crowd pops when they see them on the big screen....)
Jim: Fans we are live at City Walk!!! We are live in Hollywood, California...and tonight we're going to see some good ol' American action like you've never seen before. We've got a US Title match between Bret Hart and The
Hurricane here tonight...we've got the debut of Nikita Koloff, a huge ladder match between RVD and Marty Jannetty.
Jesse: But those don't amount to the big Four Way Dance here tonight, Jimbo. Tonight we see The Rock, Ric Flair, Triple H, and that little geek of a champion we have Mikey Whipwreck.
Jim: Well he's beaten Triple H a few times, and tonight is when he's really going to prove he's Championship material. If he can get past these four men then I'm going to look at him a whole lot differntly than I do right now.
Jesse: I'm looking forward to this whole card here tonight, and then after that
I'm going to party like it's 1999. This has been a really lousy year, and tonight I'm going to wipe those problems off the board. Tonight I start over, and tonight America starts over....
Jim: Well you're certainly right about that one Jesse. This year and been a difficult one, but everyone got together and united. They showed the world that
America would not crumble, and we might have been down...but how the tides are changing.
Jesse: I couldn't have said it better!!! Jim: Lets go to our first match!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen....this first contest is set for one fall. First coming down to the ring..making his Winter Home in Palm Beach, Florida...he weighs in at 256 pounds. Here is "The Million Dollar Man" Ted DiBiase!
("Everyone has a price for the Million Dollar Man" is heard all over City Walk, and the fans boo right off the bat. Virgil comes out first, followed by Ted
DiBiase....he climbs into the ring and takes off his suit costume.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Harlem, NY. He weighs in at 270 pounds. Here is
Booker T!!!! ("Can you dig it sucka?"....is heard and Booker T walks out in a pimped out jump suit. He's got gold chains around his neck, a Rolex watch around his wrist, and gold earrings. He slides into the ring and fire shoots out of the posts. It takes him about 5 minutes to take all his jewely off.)
Jim: There's the bell, and this match is underway. Both men with a collar and elbow tie-up, and they're jocking for position. Ted with a go-behind, and
Booker is looking for an escape. He tries to go for the legs, but Ted moves his leg...
Jesse: Hah...Booker T will never be able to out wrestle Ted DiBiase. <Booker T drives an elbow into the chin of DiBiase.> Jim: That'll make DiBiase let go....Booker to the ropes now, and he ducks a clothesline. Another clothesline duck by Booker T, and Ted goes for a backdrop.
<Booker puts on the breaks and kicks DiBiase in the mouth, and then follows up with a clothesline.>
Jesse: Rookie move by DiBiase...he shouldn't be making those kind of mistakes. Jim: Booker brings Dibiae back to his feet and brings him back down to the ground with a bodyslam.
<Booker applies a chinlock.> Jesse: I heard that Booker is spending his money faster than Hammer. Jim: I heard something like that too. Jesse: You think that he's going to become a gangster like Hammer...after he lost all his money? Or do you think he'll join the Christian Network like
Hammer did right after becoming a gangster?
Jim: I don't think either...I'm a big fan of Booker's, and I think that when you give that much money to someone, and you let them do whatever they want with it...they're going to big in some serious trouble.
<Booker lets go of the hold and waits for Ted to make it to his feet.> Jim: Booker with a fist to the face, and another one...he whips Ted into the turnbuckle, and Ted bounces right out.
Jesse: I think the impact on the mat was harder than the impact against the turnbuckle. Jim: Booker drops a knee across the face, and spins right back to his feet. Another knee drop, and he spins back up again. Jesse: He's just a dancin' fool. Jim: Booker goes for another one...and this time DiBiase moves out of the way. He went to the well one to many times. Jesse: I'd never do a move more than two times in a row. <Ted makes it to his feet and starts to stomp away on the knee, Booker starts to favor it, and Ted does it some more.>
Jim: DiBiase now going to work on the knee of Booker T. Jesse: Good...ya know everyone is talking about how lucky Booker T is to get the million dollars, but doesn't anybody realize that he stole it? He stole the money. He's a theif, and people don't seem to care.
Jim: Yeah well weren't you tempted to go up there? Jesse: No...I'm an honest man. Jim: I think the word Governor...voids that one out. Ted DiBiase lifts Booker to his feet and drops him down with a kneebreaker.
<DiBiase waits for Booker to get up again, and he kicks the knee from under him.> Jesse: I like this...Ted DiBiase isn't giving Booker a chance, and I think that it's almost over from here.
Jim: Booker limps back to the turnbuckle, and tries to get a breather. DiBiase walks over though and plants a foot into the stomach. Another foot into the stomach, and now a series of fists to the face.
Jesse: WHOA!!! Jim: Booker blocks a left and comesback with his own, and another block...Booker returns with a right hand that drops DiBiase right to the ground!!
<Booker screams to the crowd...and they start to get behind him.> Jim: DiBiase is backing up, and Booker's telling him to get up. <Book limps over and kicks DiBiase in the stomahc, and another kick to the stomach. He brings Ted to his feet and whips him into the ropes...>
Jim: Scissor kick to the face, and DiBiase goes down like a ton of bricks. He whips Ted into the ropes, and he hits him with a flying forearm. Oh and here comes the Spinorooni!!!!
<Booker does it and the crowd pops for him.> Jesse: I hate that move. Jim: Wait Virgil is on the apron, and Booker knocks him right down to the floor. He turns around and kicks DiBiase in the stomach. Booker to the ropes...AX KICK!!! NO!!!!
Jesse: DiBiase moves....and he clotheslines Booker back down to the mat. This was almost over...I'm suprised by the quality of this match. They both have totally different styles, and Booker is hurting after that clothesline.
<Ted brings Booker back up and locks in the Million Dollar Dream....> Jim: HE'S GOT IT LOCKED IN. Booker seems to be fading....DiBiase has is locked in tight. Jesse: Right in the center of the ring, and Booker drops to one knee. Jim: I'm telling you this could be over.... Jesse: The ref picks up Booker's arm...raises it and drops it. It falls once, twice, and there's the third time!!! It's over!!!
FINK: Here is your winner....Ted DiBiase!!!! Jim: Ted wins the match here tonight, and where's he going? He's grabbing a hundred dollar bill and stuffs it into Booker's mouth...that's disgusting.
<Ted DiBiase gets on the microphone...> Ted: (Out of breath) No matter...how rich you are, you will always be a theif, and poor black theif!!!
<Ted throws the microphone down and Virgil is standing right behind him with his fist ready to hit him.>
Jim: YEAH!!! Virgil just knocked out Ted DiBiae and he's helping Booker T up. I can't believe that comments that were made by DiBiase...that's sick.
(Camera cuts to Rob Van Dam walking into the arena. He's passing fans, and other wrestlers by and then he comes across this Rasta man who is wearing long dred locks, a beanie, and hemp clothing on. Rasta has his back turned to him.)
RVD: Hey man...what's up? Rasta Man: Hey mon...how are you doin'? RVD: Not to bad...can I have some of that? Rasta Man: WHAT!?!?!? (All of a sudden the man throws off his dred locks and it's Steve Austin. Austin grabs RVD and throws him into the wall.) Jim: Austin was pretending to be a Jamaican guy, and now he's stomping a mudhole into Rob Van Dam!
Jesse: That's why you always pass on grass!! Jim: Austin picks him up and throws him into the garbage can!!!! (Austin walks away and the XCW goes to a commercial.)
