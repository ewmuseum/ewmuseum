XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore

July 24, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: Ladies and gentlemen welcome to the XCW's Wednesday Night Hardcore. I'm
Joey Styles and with me as always is the man, the myth, the loser...Joel
Gertner. Joel: Ya know something the XCW is starting to heat up like nobody's business, and tonight...whew. Tonight I see stuff happening that nobody has even ever dreamed about. I'm excited for tonight, I'm ready for the matc -
["Epic" by Faith No More starts up and Chad Costello walks down in his suit and tie. The fans start to boo, and he just smiles. He climbs into the ring and asks for the microphone...]
Chad: Alright...yeah welcome to another edition of Hardcore. I'm your President, Mr. Chad Costello. You know lets face it, there has been a lot of crap happening in the XCW. Stuff that I have no control over, but yet get blamed for. Like the nWo...
[Crowd gives a mixed reaction.] Chad: Yeah I'm with you on that. They've been bad for business, they been bad for morale, and they've been bad just all around. Nash, Hall, and Hogan aren't getting into the XCW...they aren't going to survive more than another week or so here. You know why? It's because I have a plan...I asked a group of men to come together, to join up...and take this nWo out. I'm talking an elite force of men that not only will take them out, but they're going to embarass them. Oh yeah! So tonight I want to let you know Hogan, Hall, and Nash...tonight you're not getting into the building. If you do though...remember that I have men waiting, chomping at the bit...hoping that you'll climb into the ring so they can get their hands on you. Tonight the war starts....
["Epic" starts up again and Chad leaves the ring area.] Joey: And Chad has a plan? I can't believe it. What in the world is he going to do to the nWo?
Joel: I'll tell you what, Chad loves his money. When someone gets in the way of him making it he'll do whatever it takes. He'll do whatever is asked or whatever makes him the money...Chad is a business man through and through.
Joey: Well I'm hoping to see the nWo tonight....fans lets take you to our first match. FINK: Introducing first, from Short Hills , NJ, weighing in at 225 pounds , RAVEN!!! ["Keep Em' Seperated" starts to play and Raven walks down to the ring to a mixed reaction. He climbs into the ring and slouches against the turnbuckle.]
FINK: And his opponent...from Victoria, Texas. He weighs in at 250 pounds, here is "Stone Cold" Steve Austin!!!
[The sound of glass breaking his heard, and Steve Austin walks out for the first time since his loss to Shane Douglas at Trust No One. He looks angry, bitter, and injured. He climbs into the ring, and instead of going from turnbuckle to turnbuckle he throws is vest off and charges the slouched Raven.]
Joey: Austin no wasting any time!!! Joel: Hell no!!! Joey: He's stomping a mudhole in Raven, and he brings him to his feet. He whips
Raven into the turnbuckle, and he bounces out...Austin catches him with a clothesline to the side of the head.
[Austin continues to stomp on Raven, and he brings him up. Austin with a series of right hands to the side of the face, and he whips Raven into the ropes again. Raven reverses the move though, and follows Austin in with a knee to the stomach]
Joey: And that puts a stop to Austin's rage in just a matter of a few seconds. Raven brings Austin back to his feet and he rakes the eyes, and he smashes his head into the turnbuckle, and another turnbuckle smash.
Joel: Beat him down, Raven might have a chance to put him out with all the injuries Austin has sustained.
Joey: Austin took a severe beating at the hands of Shane Douglas at Trust No
One. Steve Austin now getting those right hands planted onto his face, and
Raven lifts him up and takes him down with a Cobra Clutch Bulldog!!!!
Joel: Awesome move right there by Raven, and he hooks the leg. Joey: ONE!!! TWO!! Austin kicks out at the two count, and can you imagine what it's going to be like at Clash Of The Titans when Steve Austin gets his hands on Shane Douglas?
Joel: I don't see either man begging for forgiveness. Joey: Both men have so much pride, so much anger inside of them...there's no way that's going to happen.
[Raven brings Austin back up, and Austin goes for a punch to the face. Raven blocks it and plants a boot into the stomach. Another boot into the stomach, and Raven sets him up against the turnbuckle. Raven climbs up to the second turnbuckle, grabs Austin's head...]
Joey: Oh and Austin pulls Raven's leg out from under him. Raven's head hits the canvas hard, and Austin with a few stomps to the chest.
Joel: I don't know what Raven was trying to do, but I'm sure if he hit it, it would've been over.
Joey: Austin picks Raven up by the hair, and he sends him to the ropes. STUN GUN!!!!!! [Raven's head bounces out hard, and Steve goes for the cover.] Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!! He didn't get it. Joel: That was close. Too close. Joey: Raven got his shoulder up at the last second, and he gets up. Austin charges him with a THEZ PRESS!!! He starts firing away with punches to the skull, and he lets go.
[Raven staggers back to his feet and Austin...KICK!!! WHAM!!! STUNNER!!!!] Joey: STUNNER!!! He hooks the leg...One!!! Two!!! Three!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Steve Austin!!!! [Steve Austin tired and sweaty grabs the microphone and begins to speak while catching his breath.]
Steve: Douglas...I ain't out here to fight people like Raven. I'm out here because I want to collect on your ass. I owe you for hurtin' my wife, my buddy
Ron Simmons, and myself...now get your ass out here like a man, so I can open a can of whoop ass on ya!
[Austin waits and nobody comes out.] Steve: Damnit! Shane Douglas...I'm not playing anymore. It's my turn to make your life a damn nightmare. I'm gonna be following you where ever you go, and damnit you won't even be able to sleep. Trust me!!!
[XCW officials starts to walk down to the ring with Houston PD.] Steve: Awe hell now you're gonna arrest me? Stone Cold? I don't think so, eh eh, you have to catch me first.
[One official comes in and Austin...KICK!! WHAM!! STUNNER!!! Another one comes in and he kicks a KICK!!! WHAM!! STUNNER!!! Two men lay in the ring knocked out and Austin is just waiting for the next one to come in. More Houston PD make their way down, and Austin flips them off, rolls out of the ring and leaves through the crowd.]
Joey: And Austin has evaded the police here in Houston. Joel: It's a good thing, because these guys never get laid. Who knows what they'd do to him in prison.
Joey: Shane Douglas had better watch his back tonight. [Camera cuts to Chris Benoit and he's lacing up his boots. All of a sudden we see none other than DYNAMITE KID walk up to him.]
Chris:............ Dynamite:............... Chris:.................... Dynamite:............. [Dynamite Kid brushes past his shoulder, and walks away. Camera cuts to a commercial.]
