XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 2)

June 4, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts back and Arn Anderson is standing in the ring....he's got blood trickling down his face, and he grabs the micorphone...]
Arn: Flair, you'd better be in the back listening to me because I'm only going to say this once. You and Hall...Beach Blast against me, and a partner of my choosing. You got that? You have no say so in it at all...and it will happen. I don't care if Hall has to fight in the tournament, I don't care if you have to fight in the tournament. We're going to end all this, and Flair...it's time that I show these people who was the man all along!
[Arn throws the mic down and walks away to a huge face response.] Jim: Fans can you believe it...Arn Anderson has just challenged Scott Hall and
Ric Flair to a tag team match, and Beach Blast has just gotten a little hotter.
We've got the tournament, and now we've got Arn Anderson taking on Ric Flair. Joel: Who is Arn going to use for his partner? Jim: I might have a few ideas. Joel: Well share them dummy. Jim: What if we were to see Arn Anderson and Tully Blanchard get back together? Joel: Ooooo that would be cool. Jim: Anything can happen...just keep watching. Lets take you to our Beach Blast
Update!!! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Camera cuts to the control center as we see Tori sitting behind a desk. She is looking hotter than ever, and she totally knows it.]
Tori: Hi fans, and welcome to the 2nd Beach Blast 2002 Report. I'm Tori Wilson, and I'll be giving you all the information that you need to know about the
XCW's return Pay Per View.
Sunday, June 16th Live from Safeco Field, in Seattle Washington XCW presents Beach Blast 2002 This is going to feature a 8 Man Tournament for the XCW World Title. Now we all know the Main Event will be a Hell In The Cell Match for the vacant XCW World
Title. So the winner will not only have to go through 3 matches, but they will also have to be apart of a HITC at the very end. The brackets are as followed...
Mickey Whipwreck vs. Roddy Piper Sho Funaki vs. Tatanka Steve Armstrong vs. Chris Jericho Triple H vs. Cactus Jack Other matches announced for the PPV.... Arn Anderson/??? vs. Ric Flair/Scott Hall Tommy Dreamer vs. Stevie Richards vs. Raven (US Title) RVD/Sabu vs. Al Snow/Chris Jericho in a Ladder Match (World Tag Team Titles) ALSO announced...The ROCK will make his return to the XCW, and tell us what his plans are for the future. Will he stick with wrestling, or will he move on with this movie career???
Vader has also been invited to the event....will he show up? Tickets are on sale at any Ticketmaster near you. This is going to go fast, so get there early. If you can't make it you can always catch it LIVE on Pay Per
View. Call your local cable opperator for availability.
Fans...Beach Blast is going to be one for the record books. Be there or order it today...Safeco Field, Seattle Washington!
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST BEACH BLAST =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this match is set for one fall. Introducing first from Short Hills, New Jersey...he weighs in at 245 pounds. Here is Raven!!!
["Keep Em' Seperated" starts to play and Raven comes out to a HUGE heel ovation. He puts his arms in a cross motion, and then slides into the ring. He slouches against the turnbuckle, and waits for his opponent.]
FINK: And his opponent...from Glascow, Scottland. He weighs in at 260 pounds. Here is "Rowdy" Roddy Piper!!! ["Scotland the Brave" starts to whale up, and he comes out with a huge smiles on his face. He stops in the middle of the walk away and extends his arms, and the fans slap his hands as he walks down. He climbs into the ring and rips his kilt off and throws it out of the ring.]
Jim: I am excited about this match. Joel: Ugh Piper is too old. [Both men meet in the center of the ring, and the crowd is behind Piper big time. Piper and Raven engage in a conversation that can't be picked up by the camera. The ref calls for the bell, and the match starts. But nither man is moving...they're just talking to each other. Piper looks to the crowd as they pop for him big time, and Raven takes the chance to hit him across the face.]
Jim: Raven goes for another right hand, and Piper blocks it. Raven now with a left hand, and Piper blocks that!
Joel: Uh oh. Jim: Piper with a right hand, a left , and another right. Raven gets bodyslammed down to the canvas.
[Piper looks at the crowd who is really behind him. Raven makes it back up and
Piper hits him with a flurry of punches which makes Raven's head a little red.
Piper whips Raven into the corner, Raven ducks the clothesline and hits Piper with one of his own.]
Jim: Devastating clothesline, and Raven brings him back to his feet. Raven with fist to the forehead, and another fist. Piper is teetering...and Raven with a boot to the stomach, and follows up with a double knee jump to the face!!!!
Joel: Whoa, and that'll clean your damn clock. Jim: Raven walks over to Piper and he digs his boot into the throat!! He's choking him out.
Joel: Perfect...good ol' choke hold. [Raven gets to the count of 4 and breaks the hold, and does this about 3 times. He finally stops, drops to his knees and starts to punch the forehead of Piper again. Raven brings Piper to his feet and sends him into the ropes again...]
Jim: Raven with a kick to the midsection...JAWBREAKER!!!! Joel: Piper might have bit his tongue. Jim: "Hot Rod" flys back holding his jaw in pain. [Raven just smiles and moves his hair back with his hand. Piper slowly gets up to his feet, and Raven clotheslines Piper over the ropes. Piper goes crashing down on the cement, and Raven goes right after him.]
Jim: And this is going to get ugly. Joel: Oh yeah... [Raven grabs a chair, and he goes for a swing...Piper ducks and hits a thumb to the eye. Another thumb to the eye, and Raven drops the chair. Piper throws him into the guardrail, and then throws him into the ring.]
Jim: And these fans love Piper!!! Joel: I don't know why... Jim: Piper is calling for the sleeper... [Piper waits for Raven to get up...all of a sudden the camera cuts to the curtain and there's a man in a kilt, and he's trying to play the bag pipes.]
Jim: That's....that's EDGE!!! Joel: What the hell is here doing in that outfit? [Edge walks down and he's acting like a total goof, and Piper has stopped paying attention to Raven. Now he's focused on Edge, and he stops right in front of Piper. He's playing the pipes, and Roddy is just laughing.]
Joel: What's he doing??? Jim: It looks like he came out here to give Piper some support. [Edge motions for Piper to come over, and he's telling him something. Raven comes from behind and spins Piper around. Raven kicks Piper in the stomach, and hits the Evenflow. He grabs the tights...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Joel: WOW... FINK: Here is your winner...RAVEN!!!! [Edge has a look of concern on his face, and he slides into the ring. Raven slides out and raises his arms in victory, and Edge is checking on Piper. Edge is helping Piper up...]
Jim: And Edge came down to support Piper...in an odd way, but he did it nonetheless. What a way for Edge to debut here tonight.
Joel: That's odd. Jim: Piper gets his hand raised by Edge...OH!!!! Edge just kicked Piper's leg out from under him, and now he's stomping away on that hip!!!
Joel: That's the bad hip...that's the bad hip of Pipers, and Edge doesn't care. [Edge rips off his kilt and puts it over the face of Piper. He slams the bag pipes down onto the stomach of Hot Rod, and the place is upset. Edge smiles and grabs the microphone...]
Edge: Hey Piper...there's a new mouth in town!!! Remember that!!! [Edge throws the mic down onto Piper, and walks away...] Jim: What a jerk!!! Joel: Why? HAHAH that was awesome...Piper had better watch his back. Edge is the man who's going to take his place!!
Jim: Nobody can take Piper's place!! [Camera cuts to Arn Anderson who is backstage being attended to. He's actually being stitched up, and all of a sudden Shane Douglas walks in. You can hear the fans in the audience go wild as he enters the room. Arn has a look of pain, and shock on his face...]
Shane: What happened to you? Arn: What does it look like? What are you doing here? Shane: Just coming in here to see if ya saw my promo out there. The fans went nuts, and rightfully so! They know what a big star The Franchise is. They know how much I mean to this company. You know a little about wrestling tradition don't ya?
Arn: What's wrong with you? I've never seen you in a good mood. Shane: Well, ya know, Arn, maybe it is nice to see someone in a position such as you that actually knows where the hell I am coming from now.
Arn: Listen if you're here to ask for a job the answer is no. I have enough on my hands right now, and I don't need to deal with the trouble you bring.
Shane: Whoa, whoa, Arn, hear me out. Ya see, how long did you bust your ass for this business? 10, 15, 50 years? How many times did ya win a World Title? In the WWF? Or WCW? Or even that damned NWA title, that once meant something, but since I realized I was bigger then that, it means nothing.
Arn: You're wasting your time...get out of here. Shane: Cool it Arn, just for a sec. All I am saying....is you know how it feels to be held back. You know, as well as I do, that I am THE biggest thing going in wrestling today. You owe it to the fans, and to yourself, to give The
Franchise a chance to grace your federation with his talent. Arn: Shane...you've been nothing but trouble here in the XCW since day one. I have a head ache, and you're not making any better SO GET OUT!!!
Shane: (wags finger threatingly) Don't forget, Arn, what happened to the last idiot who thought he could run me out of here. He's flipping burgers somewhere, while I am still the biggest star in wrestling. Don't make the same mistake he did!
Arn: My mistake would be letting you in...secuity. (Security comes over and starts to drag Shane Douglas out) Shane: Ok, Arn, if ya say so, but I promise you this, I promise you, that this is not the last time you will hear from me. Oh, and Arn.....if ya change your mind, ya know where you can find me. (Douglas smirks as security drags him away)
(Camera fades.)
