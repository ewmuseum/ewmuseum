XCW Monday Night Malice

December 23, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we fade onto the latest edition of Malice we are at the parking lot of
The STAPLE Center. We see a Taxi pull up and in the backseat is none other than
"The Human Suplex Machine" Taz...he goes to get in when all of a sudden a car slams into the side of the Taxi pinning him between the SUV and the wall.
People scramble to see if he's okay, and the camera hears the slamming of car doors. The noise is coming from the SUV, but he was unable to pick up who left.
We cut to the XCW intro... "O Fortuna" starts to play. We see pictures of Steve Armstrong and Sho Funaki inside the steel cage. We see Steve Austin tossing Shane Douglas off the 15 ft.
high ramp. Then a series of rapid shots of wrestlers excuting their finishers. The shots get faster and faster, and then they stop...on the XCW logo. It starts to bleed...
We cut to a live sold out crowd at the The STAPLES Center. Fans are going wild as the XCW is starting to catch on once again. Fireworks go off, and we cut to
Jim Ross and Don Callis sitting at ringside.]
Jim: We're just ONE day away from Christmas Eve Chaos, and all hell has broken loose already. Did you just see what happened to Taz's car?
Don: Yeah. I'm not blind I saw it...it's these damn California drivers. They're all in a hurry to be somewhere, and they don't care who they cut off or injure.
Jim: Well I smell a rat, and the rats name is DX. Fans lets take you to the parking lot once again.
[We cut to the parking lot and we see the Paramedics out there using the Jaws of life to release Taz. There's a stretcher out there ready to take him and send him to the nearest hospital which would be Cedars Sinai. Taz is screaming in pain, and we cut back to the ring...]
Jim: We might not have a Main Event tomorrow. Taz is supposed to take on Triple
H for the XCW World Title.
Don: I'll wrestle if I have to. Jim: Forget that. We want to pull fans in, not chase them away. ["I stand alone" by Godsmack is heard over the PA, and out walks John Cena for his XCW debut, getting a decent reaction from the crowd. Val Venis' music begins to play, and out he walks. As he climbs into the ring we see Shawn
Michaels slowly walk out onto the enterance ramp, watching with interest.]
Jim: What in the hell is he doing here? Don: Can't a man watch a wrestling match? Jim: I have a feeling that's not what he's here for tonight. Val Venis enters the ring, and ducks a clothesline by Cena. Val spins him around and starts to lay on him with a series of punches.
[Val whips Cena to the ropes and catches him in a beautiful spinebuster. Val lifts him up again, and sets him on his shoulders and executes a flap jack.]
Jim: This is a different kind of Val Venis we're seeing tonight. Don: Yeah I'm kinda disapointed. [Val goes behind Cena, and he locks in a Dragon Sleeper. He's working on the neck, but Cena begins to fight out of it. A few elbows to the midsection, and
Val switches to a side headlock.]
Jim: Cena backs him up against the ropes, and he goes to whip him to the other side. Val counters and catches Cena with a NICE DDT!!!!
Don: Whoa... Jim: ONE!! TWO!!! Kickout by John Cena... Don: See look Shawn Michaels has done nothing so far. He's just hanging out at the top watching the match.
Jim: I don't trust him. [Venis brings Cena up again, and whips him into the ropes. This time Cena reverses it and sends Val instead. Cena leapfrogs Venis, but Val stops behind
Cena and quickly latches on and drops him with a reverse DDT.]
Jim: Val Venis goes for the pin again...ONE!! TWO!!! NO!! John Cena kicks out once again, and some people think that Cena is a little immature but he sure has a lot of fight in him.
Don: What is with the drive that Val Venis has? Jim: Well some think he's never going to be able to make it to the Main Event, others think that he'll never be anything but that porn gimmick.
Don: Ahhh porn. I love porn. [Val lifts him up, and he goes for a powerbomb but Cena counters with a double leg takedown. He grabs the legs of Val and slingshots him into the turnbuckle.
Val turns around in a daze, but is awake enough to duck a clothesline. He grabs
Cena from behind the neck and drops him with a reverse neckbreaker.]
Jim: Anything Cena tries to do in this match, Val counters. Don: Cena is just green. Jim: Val brings Cena back to his feet, he runs to the ropes and plants him with a bulldog!! He stands over the fallen Cena and locks in a Full Nelson Clutch, and what a submission move!
Don: Ow! Jim: And Cena is tapping!!! [Cena taps out and Venis lets go of the hold and rolls out of the ring. FINK quickly announces him as the winner, he raises his hand into the air and leaves the ring area quickly. Cena sits there holding the back of his neck in pain, and HBK leaves with a disgusted look on his face.]
Jim: Fans we're going to take you to the back so we can get word on Taz, is
Michael Cole standing by?
[Shot of Michael Cole by the damaged Taxi Cab. We see LAPD measuring the skid marks, writing reports, it's total chaos backstage and we see them pushing
Michael Cole back and forth trying to get information from the cement.]
Michael: Guys, as you can see it's a mad house down here. Taz has been taken to the hospital, he's been checked into the ER and hopefully we'll find out more before the end of the night. From what I overheard there was some type of internal bleeding, and he's got a broken arm..possibly a broken collar bone.
We'll get more as - [All of a sudden Triple H, Chris Jericho and Shawn Michaels run up...] Triple H: What the hell happened?? Shawn: Who was in this Taxi?? Michael: Like you guys don't know. Triple H: What the hell are you talking about?? We just got here. Michael: Do you mean to tell me that you didn't ram Taz's taxi? Triple H: Taz was in there?? DAMNIT!!!! Come on guys...we have to find out who did this!! [All three men take off and we cut to a commercial.]
