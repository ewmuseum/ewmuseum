XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 5)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans this next match should be more than interesting. It's for the XCW World Tag Team Titles, and you want to talk about a cluster bomb of a division. This is all out crazy. Don: My money tonight is the Outlaw Gangsta Party! [All of a sudden "Epic" by Faith No More starts to play and Chad Costello walks down to the ring. He smiles this huge cheesy smile, and grabs the microphone.] Chad: I figured since you all were happy to see me this past Monday, I might as well grace you with my presence right now. There are a few things I need to change on this card, and since I am the CEO...well I'm going to change them. First of all this 3 Way Tag Team match has to go. There's not heat involved in the damn thing, I'm sick and tired of seeing these matches go on and on. The bottom line is that I get bored, and I don't want to be bored on Christmas Eve. So that match has been scrapped. Second...Test is no longer with the XCW. Heh, first Chris Benoit and now the other Canadian Bastard...Test. So that leaves just Chucky Palumbo, and someone else. Well there are people DYING to get into this fed...so I am replacing Test with a man who I have known to love as a wrestler. The man absolutely should get the job done here tonight, he's a man who I respect, admire...ya and all that stuff. AJ STYLES!!!! Now now before I go...I just want to say we had a toy drive this month. You guys from around the country did your part, and I am so excited to say that _I_ got some really great toys from you all this year...I mean seriously you all went out of your way to make this my best Christmas. HA did you really think I was going to give it to some snot nosed little punk? I am n - #Join in with me, into the mind of a maniac. #Doomed to be a killa since I came out the nut-sack. #I'm in a murderous mind-state with a heart full of terror. #I see the devil in the mirror. #Buck, buck! Lights out. #'Cause when I grab my sawed-off niggaz get hauled off. #Barrel one! Tears your muthafuckin' flesh... #Barrel two! Tear your fuckin' heart out your chest. ["Natural Born Killa" brings the arena to their feet, as two men come running out from backstage.Chad is standing still inside the ring as he knows what about to take place. The crowd sings along, or just screams at the top of their lungs, as the song continues.] #You see, I'm quick to let the hammer go click on my Tech-9. #So, if you try to wreck mine - fool, it's your bed time. #Feel the blast from the chocolate bomber: #Infrared aimed at your head like your name was Sarah Connor. #Decapitation, I ain't hesitatin' to put you in a funeral home #With a bullet in your dome. [The garbage can goes flying into the ring, scattering the contents all across the canvas. Toys of all kinds can be seen. Into the ring rolls the ski-masked man, and he's followed close behind by the man in the cowboy hat. Chad tries to run away, but is caught by none other than New Jack. He throws him into Dusty and BIONIC ELBOW takes him down. Chad gets up and New Jack smashes him in the head with a Barbie Play House.] #I'm hot like lava. You got a problem? I got a problem solver, #And its name is revolver. #It's like a deadly game of freeze-tag. #I touch you with the 44 Mag, #And you frozen inside a body-bag. #Nobody iller than this graveyard filler - cap piller. #'Cause I'm a NATURAL BORN KILLA! [Another BIONIC ELBOW~! catches a rising Costello up in the head. Costello is back up, and charges at Mr. Cowboy. A flapjack onto a garbage can sends him down in a whole lot of pain.] #Terror, illustrates my era. #Now I can't hang around my mama 'cause I scare her. #I want the baddest muthafuck. #"Yeah! What's up?" #It feels like I'm bustin' a nut when I open you up. #'Cause your body is exposed to the midnight mist. #All weak muthafuckas give my wing a kiss. [Chad is back up...] _CLONK!_ [... and is back down as the Cowboy's cowbell smashes into his skull. Chad dazed and confused gets back up.] _CRRRRRRRACK!_ [... and back down as new Jack drills him with a Chia Pet. Yes... a Chia Pet.] #'Cause I'm givin' dirt naps. #Comin' with them dumb ass wax that make your lungs collapse. #Perhaps you never sleep 'cause everytime you doze #You catch blows to the muthafuckin' nose. #Ain't seen the sun in 66 days. #Let me kill the ways in a fuck-up maze. #I never ever, ever, made a hoe stay - #But I'm down with Dre like AC is down with OJ. #So fuck how you livin'. [...Abbdullah the Butcher comes down and he pulls out a sharp fork wrapped in a bow. Chad gets up and pleads not to be attacked anymore...his face covered in blood, and Abby just starts stabbing away. New Jack and Dusty have to pull him off of Chad...as they leave Abby takes Chad's Santa Hat and the crowd goes wild.] Jim: Merry Christmas...Mr. Costello!!! Don: HOLY SHIT. Jim: This trio is without a doubt the craziest, most dangerous, insane group of guys that have ever walked in the XCW!!!!
