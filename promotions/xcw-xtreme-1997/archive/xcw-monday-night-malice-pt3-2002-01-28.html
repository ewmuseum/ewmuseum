XCW Monday Night Malice (part 3)

January 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Folks lets take you to a ma... <All of a sudden Raven's music starts up, and he walks down to the ring dragging a lifeless body with him. He rolls the body into the ring, and then grabs the microphone...>
Raven: For weeks...I have tormented the name of Hulk Hogan. For weeks I have told you all that Hulkamania will die, and tonight...it does. I have destroyed your friends, and tonight I destroy your family. Hulk Hogan...you never cared about these people. You have made your money, and you don't need to comeback.
Well tonight I put an end to Hulk Hogan, and Hulkamania... <Raven takes the hood off of the lifeless body, and it's none other than Horace who is a bloody mess. Horace has on a bloody Hulkamania t-shirt...and Raven lights a match and is about to throw it onto him. When all of a sudden we have....
STING!!!!!> Jim: STING IS HERE!!!!! Sting is here, and he turns Raven around..back fist to the side of the head, and another back fist!!!
Jesse: What in the hell is he doing here? Jim: Sting whips Raven into the turnbuckle...STINGER SPLASH!!!!!! Raven rolls out of the ring...
<Sting walks over to Horace and checks on him...> Jim: Sting has helped out Horace...and where in the hell is Hulk Hogan? Jesse: He's an ego...he'll never show up again! Jim: Fans we just saw the return of Sting, and one has to wonder why he's out here and Hulk wasn't.
Jesse: I told you before...Hogan is nothing short of a coward. The man doesn't stand a chance against Raven, and nobody wants to see The Hulkster anymore.
Jim: Well you can bet that Sting isn't going to stop bugging Raven, and that
Raven will have some choice words for Sting. That's going to be war!!!
Jesse: Sting did look in tip top shape. Jim: Fans right now we're going to see the debut of Vamprio, and he's going to be taking on Randy Savage. It's going to be an interesting battle between the two, so lets take you to FINK for the intros.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from parts unknown...he weighs in at 234 pounds. Here is
Vamprio! <Vamprio comes down to no music at all, and the fans are quiet as he climbs into the ring. He's got his face painted all up, and is walking around all mysterious.>
FINK: And his opponent...from Sarasota, Florida. He weighs in at 245 pounds, and is led down to the ring by Miss Elizabeth. Here is..."Macho Man" Randy
Savage!!!
<"Pomp and Circumstance" starts up and the crowd is going wild. Savage walks down to the ring with Elizabeth, and he holds the ropes open for her and the hops in himself. He goes from turnbuckle to turnbuckle, and the fans don't stop their face response towards him.>
JR: There's the bell...and they lock up! Savage with a kneelift... and a side headlock... [Vampiro backs up against the ropes and tries to push Savage off but to no avail...] Ventura: Savage keepin' it simple early on, smart wrestling by the veteran... [Side-headlock takedown by Savage...] JR: Savage takes Vampiro over and to the mat, but Vampiro quickly gets back to his feet...belly-to-back suplex by Vampiro!
[Vampiro gets back up, elbow drop attempt...but Savage rolls out of the way!] Ventura: Once again Savage showing the intelligence that comes with only being in this business for decades.
JR: Both men back to their feet...Savage with a kick to the gut...Savage hooks
Vampiro...vertical suplex!
[This time Savage attempts an elbow drop and it connects...] JR: Savage with a quick cover...1...2...no! Vampiro kicks out... [Vampiro back to his feet and Savage is immediately all over him with a flurry of rights and lefts that send him reeling into the corner, Savage wastes no time in climbing up to the second turnbuckle and beginning to punch away at
Vampiro as the crowd chants along...]
Crowd: One! Two! Three! Four! Five! Six! Seven! Eight! Nine! Ten! [After the tenth, Savage hops off the turnbuckle and whips Vampiro to the opposite corner. Savage runs in...]
JR: Here comes Savage...but Vampiro explodes out of the corner with a diving clothesline that takes Savage to the mat! Vampiro lifts Savage to his feet, whip to the ropes...and a beautiful dropkick!
[Savage rolls out of the ring...] Ventura: The impact of that dropkick sent Savage right out of the ring! JR: Vampiro certainly does know how to throw a hell of a kick. [Savage gets back to his feet...] *CROWD POP!* [...only to get put right back down again with a beautiful crossbody block plancha from Vampiro!]
JR: Look out!!! What a move by Vampiro!! Ventura: Savage never saw it coming...I don't think anybody saw that one coming!!! [Vampiro wastes no time in rolling back into the ring, grabbing the ropes once more...] JR: WAIT A MINUTE!!! *THUD! HUGE CROWD POP!!* JR: MY GOD!!! MY GOD!!! VAMPIRO MIGHT HAVE BROKEN SAVAGE'S NECK!!! Ventura: Along with his own tailbone!!! [XCW Replay: Savage is laid out on the concrete. Vampiro slingshots himself over the top rope and to the concrete with an insane legdrop suicida.]
JR: It looks like a damn car wreck out here and both men might be seriously hurt! Ventura: Well Vampiro is moving so I think he's alright...and Savage seems to be hurt, but not paralyzed thank god!
JR: All I know is that Vampiro simply does not care about his own body! He might have ended his career in an instant with a stunt like that!!
Ventura: You know JR, Vampiro is a pretty cool lookin' dude...you ever think about painting your face like that? It might go good with the cowboy hat...
JR: I'll paint my face when pigs fly Jesse... [Vampiro gets back to his feet, clutching the small of his back and grimacing from the pain. Vampiro takes a moment to lean on the apron and regain his composure but that might not be the smartest move...]
JR: Savage!! Savage is getting back to his feet! Ventura: ...and Vampiro has no idea! [Savage spins Vampiro around and quickly scoops him up...] JR: Bodyslam on the concrete from Savage!! Ventura: Where's the Macho Man going now? [Savage climbs up onto the apron and walks over to the turnbuckle. He swings around to the other side so he is facing Vampiro and begins to climb the turnbuckle...]
Ventura: Uh-oh JR...I think we're about to see that patented Macho Man double ax-handle! JR: He is just waiting for Vampiro to get to his feet! [Vampiro gets back to his feet by pulling himself up using the guardrail and as he turns around, Savage takes flight...]
Ventura: There he goes!!! *CCCCLAAAANNGG!!!* Crowd: OOOOOHHHHH!!!! JR: SAVAGE COLLIDED WITH THE GUARDRAIL!!! HE JUMPED OFF THE DAMN TOP ROPE AND HIT NOTHING BUT STEEL!!! Ventura: He hit his chest too...we better get some help out here, he might have crushed his windpipe!!
[No time for EMTs Jesse, because this match ain't over, apparently to Vampiro it's not over anyways as he now has a steel chair in hand...]
JR: NO NO NO!!! [Vampiro brings the chair down across the back of the prone Slim Jim...] *CLANG!* JR: DAMN HIM!! Ventura: Shouldn't that be a DQ?! JR: The ref has been very lenient in this match...almost too lenient! *CLANG!* [Deja vu...] JR: DAMNIT! Somebody stop this!! Ventura: This is getting a little out of hand...what has happened to the rules?! [Vampiro slides the chair into the ring, lifts Macho up and rolls him into the ring also. Vampiro climbs back into the ring...]
JR: Vampiro now setting that chair up, and he must have some evil intentions. Ventura: I think we've already seen his evil intentions...he wants to hurt
Savage! [With the chair folded open in a sitting position, Vampiro lifts Savage to his feet, slaps him in a standing headscissors and...]
*CRRRUNNNCCHHH!* *HARDCORE CROWD POP!* JR: GOOD LORD!!! GOOD LORD!!! A SNAP POWERBOMB RIGHT THROUGH THAT CHAIR!! Savage has to be dead!!! Ventura: Vampiro covering and this has to be over...1...2... *Crowd pop!* JR: OH HELL YES!! Savage is still in this one!! Ventura: And Vampiro can't believe it! [Vampiro gets up, walks over to the corner and climbs to the top...] JR: If Vampiro hits this all bets are off...MOONSAULT! NO! SAVAGE MOVED!!! Ventura: But how he moved after the abuse his body has taken was beyond all logical explanation!
[With a surge of adrenaline, the hurting Macho Man powers himself to his feet. Using the ropes to help support himself he walks over to the turnbuckle and begins to slowly climb them...]
JR: Randy Savage is proving all his naysayers wrong...they've said he's over the hill, they've said he's through, but I dare anybody to take a look at that tough SOB in the ring right now and tell me he is over the hill!
Ventura: I agree JR...I'm amazed Savage is moving, let alone climbing the ropes after what his body has been put through...
[Savage finally makes it to the top, looks out at the audience with those crazed eyes, before slowly lifting his arms high above his head and taking flight...]
*THUD!* JR: HE HIT IT!!! THE FLYING ELBOW DROP!!! Ventura: And he covers...ONE! TWO! THREE!!! FINK: Here is your winner..."MACHO MAN" RANDY SAVAGE!!! *Crowd pop!* [Savage gets up on the turnbuckles and begins to play to the crowd, celebrating his win...]
Ventura: Wait a minute...Vampiro is up..and he does NOT look happy! [Vampiro rushs Savage and nails a forearm to the back. Vampiro climbs up to the second rope, grabs Savage by the neck and jumps off...]
JR: Inverted DDT off the turnbuckle!! Now come on...the match is over ya damn sore loser!! Ventura: And Savage is already hurt from the beating he took in the match! [Vampiro lifts Savage to his feet and in one swift motion, scoops him up and drops him in a split-legged Michinoku Driver...]
JR: The Final Nail In The Coffin! That's Vampiro's finisher and he just nailed
Savage with it.
Ventura: Savage is out COLD! [Elizabeth gets into the ring, puts herself between Savage and Vampiro and starts pleading with Vampiro to leave Savage alone...]
Ventura: Liz better watch hersel...too late! JR: Now come on! [Vampiro grabs Elizabeth by the wrist and starts to say what looks to be very mean things as we quickly see tears begin to stream down Elizabeth's face from her fear-filled eyes...]
Ventura: And now Elizabeth has to beg for her own safety... *CROWD POP!* JR: OH NO SHE WON'T!!! HERE COMES TOMMY DREAMER!!! [Dreamer rushs the ring and immediately spears Vampiro to the mat, breaking his hold on Elizabeth's wrist. Dreamer schoolboys Vampiro and starts to blast him with a flurry of rights and lefts...]
Ventura: It looks like Tommy Dreamer saved Elizabeth's life! [Dreamer picks Vampiro up and tosses him out of the ring. Dreamer rolls out and the two men fight all the way up the ramp and back behind the curtain...]
Jim: Fans when we comeback we'll announce the new commissioner!!!!! STAY WITH US!!!!!
