XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 3)

July 31, 2002

[When we comeback Arn, Ole, and Ric Flair are all talking.] Arn: War Games is right around the corner, and 3/5 guys involved on our team are masters of the game.
Ole: Damn right. Ric: And the nWo are in some serious trouble. I wonder if they think that they're going to be able to stop the Horsemen? We're alive and well, and - wait a minute. Where's Shawn Michaels?
Arn: I'm not sure. I thought he was with you. Ric: Eh..no. Ole: Maybe he's running late. Ric: That's gotta be it. [Camera fades.] INTRO [Ole and Jericho are about to lock up when all of a sudden we hear "N....W...O" and Hogan, Hall and Nash run down to the ring. All three men start attacking
Ole and Arn...]
Joey: And the damm match hasn't even begun, and they're all over the Andersons. Nash and Hall working over Arn, and Hogan has Ole in the corner. Joel: NWO is slowly but sure - Joey: RIC FLAIR!!! Flair flies down to the ring, and he unloads on everyone with chops. Nash goes down, Hall goes down, Oh but Hollywood drops Flair with a double ax-handle.
[NWO back up and they're all working over the 3 Horsemen in the ring. Hogan takes off his belt and starts to whip Flair with it...and then Ole and finally
Arn. Hogan then begins playing the "Air Guitar", and Rock comes in.]
Joey: ROCKY IS HERE!!! ROCKY!! [The Rock runs down to the ring and clears the ring of NWO. He throws his steel chair at Nash, and it clips his shoulder before he takes off. Hollywood flips off Rock, and Rock just smiles. The crowd goes into a huge "ROCKY ROCKY". Rock grabs the microphone...]
Rock: NWO... [The fact that is voice is fully healed sparks a huge pop, and the Rock just soaks it in..]
Rock: NWO...Hogan, Hall and Nash. The three stooges, you come out here you ruin the matches and then you get chased away. That's been going on for the past 2 weeks. Don't you three get it? Shouldn't you know by now that the fans think the NWO is OLD? The Rock says this...these men in the ring. Flair, Ole, Arn, myself, and where ever HBK is...we're going to climb into that War Games, we're going to lay the Smackdown on all your candy asses...and then..then...as The
Rock stands over the NWO...I'm going to take a spray paint can, I'm going to put a letter on each one of you. I'm going to paint...G...A....Y....and then
I'm going to take that Can and stick it stright up Kurt Angles ass. Next Friday night in Las Vegas...The Rock and the Four Horsemen together at last!!! IF YA
SMALALALAL WHAT THE ROCK IS COOKIN'!!!! [His music starts to play and all five men leave the ring area to a huge face response. We cut back to the locker room and we see Dynamite Kid walking. All of a sudden his attention is turned towards someone on the side...he stops, and then charges it. The camera turns and it's Chris Benoit. Dynamite attacked him from behind and starts kicking the hell out of him. Kid slams him into the cement wall, kicks him a few more times, and then walks away.]
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. -Intros- [Dynamite slides into the ring goes for a clothesline, but Booker ducks it. Booker spins him around and goes for a Superkick, but Dynamite quickly backs off and exits the ring.]
Joey: Whoa and that was a quick way to start off the match. Joel: I don't think Dynamite is going to be like the fans expect him to be. The man has bad blood in him, and he's not about to change himself for these people.
Joey: Dynamtie walking around the ring, and Booker is working the crowd. Pumping the fists. [While Booker T is distracted Dynamite slides in, spins him around and kicks him in the stomach. Dynamite with a HUGE SNAP SUPLEX!!!]
Joey: WOW the best suplex in the XCW today. Dynamite crackin' that back of
Booker. Joel: And I thought Booker was going to be on the offense right away in this match. Joey: Kid brings him back up, and hits him with a punch to the stomach. He's going for a piledriver. He goes for it once...and it doesn't happen. Here comes a second time, and no it doesn't work. Booker with a backdrop!
[Kid backpeddles on the floor over to the ring ropes. He leans up against it, with his back arched already. Dynamite begs off as Booker T walks over. Booker looks to the crowd, and Dynamite with a low blow.]
Joey: LOW BLOW!!!! Dynamite now with a uppercut to the chin, and another one. Russian Legsweep by Kid, and he follows up with a kneedrop. Joel: See what did I tell you? These fans aren't going to be to happy about this whole deal. They wanted to see him work the crowd, play around...they wanted him to be their friend. Well Dynamite didn't have any friends back in the day, and he's not about to have friends now.
[Dynamite applies a reverse chinlock. He's wrenching away, continuing to work over the head and neck area of Booker.]
Joey: Can you imagine what kind of damage Benoit is going to cause when he gets revenge on Dynamite Kid?
Joel: If they ever do wrestle it's going to be one brutal match. Joey: It's not like Chris Benoit has enough to worry about. [Several minutes passed and each time Booker T attempts to escape Dynamite thwarted his comeback. Slowly Booker raised Kid to his feet. Elbow to the ribs of Kid, but Dynamite fought it off and tightened his grip. Booker with another shot, this time it had more behind it. A third elbow forces Dynamite to release the hold.]
Joey: Booker charges - but Kid was ready and waiting. Powerslam!!!!! Joel: WHOA he burried him with that. Joey: Kid walks around working the fans into a frenzy, and who would've thought we'd see this side of Dynamite Kid.
Joel: Didn't you read his book? Joey: No. Joel: That's right you don't know how to read. [Dynamite brings Booker to his feet and whips him to the ropes. He plants a foot into his stomach, and he takes him down with a double-arm DDT. Kid rolls him over for another cover...]
Joey: ONE!! TWO!! Booker gets his shoulder up off the mat. Joel: Look and the look on Kid's face. The man isn't happy about that at all. Joey: Dynamite now talking ot the ref. Oh and now he's pushing the ref, and the ref pushes him back!!!
[Kid stands in the corner and snarls, before pushing him again. This time
Booker rolls him up...]
Joey: ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!!! Joel: NO!! NO!!! Joey: Kid escapes!!! Both men back to their feet, and Dynamite goes for a clothesline..and he nails it.
[He looks to the crowd and drags his thumb across his throat. Shades of Chris
Benoit...] Joey: WOW and he's almost taunting Chris Benoit. He's telling him to come out here, and he wants a piece of him.
Joel: Why waste tim Joey: Dynamite Kid lifts up Booker T, and takes him down with a bodyslam. Dynamite runs to the top turnbuckle. He flys off with a Swandive headbutt right on target. He hooks the leg...ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!! BOOKER KICKS OUT!!!
[Dynamite sits up, his expression one of disbelief. Fans pump their fists into the air, and Kid continued sitting on the mat, frustrated that Booker was able to kickout. Not only that, behind him Booker was slowly getting to his feet.]
Joey: Kid turns around, and look at his eyes. They're boiling...nearly popping out of his skull. Kid charges with a clothesline, and Booker ducks it. He turns around and a SUPERKICK!!!
Joel: That sends him against the turnbuckle. Joey: He follows Kid in, and he began punching away at Kid. [The fans chant along with each blow. "ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE!" Booker pauses and took in the fans reactions. This gives Dynamite a chance to escape. Kid grabs him and walks him out of the corner, before attempting an inverted atomic drop.]
Joey: Booker blocks it!!!! AND HE GETS THE TENTH PUNCH!!!! Joel: Wow...these people are going crazy!!! Joey: Kid falls to the canvas, and Booker with the cover. ONE!! TWO!! No...Kid got his foot on the bottom rope.
[Booker brings the former World tag champ up, and he kicks him in the stomach. Booker runs to the ropes, and drops him down with an ax kick.] Joey: SPINNEROINI!!! SPINNEROONI!!!!! Joel: This has been an amazing match!!! Joey: He brings him to his feet, and he whips him to the ropes. Dynamite reverses, and Booker with a Sunset Flip!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!!
[Both men rush back to their feet, and Booker charges again. This time Kid caught him with a face buster, and as Booker shot back up he bounces of the ropes and this time the Double Underhook DDT could not be stopped.]
Joey: Dynamite gasping for air, and he goes for the cover. ONE!!! TWO!! THR - NO!!! Joel: I can't freakin' believe it. Joey: Booker got his foot on the ropes this time. Dynamite stands up and he paces arounc the ring. Booker is up, and he slowly turns around....Dynamite goes for the Claw?? WOW...
[Booker counters with a kick to the stomach, followed by a Running Powerbomb, but right before he drops him...Dynamite turns it into a Head Scissors pin...]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!! THREE!!!!! [Dynamite quickly escapes outside of the ring, raising his arms in the air, the fans booing every move he makes. Booker laid on the mat in frustration, diebelief, and rage all rolled into one. He was left on his back, looking up at the lights.]
FINK: Here is your winner...Dynamite Kid!!!! Joey: That was a great match... Joel: Booker T loses his debut match here on Hardcore, and Dynamite wins. Joey: These two put on an incredible match here tonight, and I can't wait to see what they have in store for the XCW.
[We cut back to the parking lot, and we see Mikey Whipwreck talking to Sho's father. Both men are walking to a car, and it looks like they're going to get something to eat. All of a sudden from behind Sho Funaki and Brock Lesnar attack them from behind. Sho attacks Mikey, and Brock grabs Sho's father. Sho throws Mikey into the grill of the car, and then shouts something to
Brock...Brock lifts up Sho's father and F5's him onto the hood of the car....the car alarm goes off, and Sho climbs the roof of the car and nails his
OWN father with a SHO STOPPER. Brock and Sho run away laughing....camera cuts to a commercial.]
