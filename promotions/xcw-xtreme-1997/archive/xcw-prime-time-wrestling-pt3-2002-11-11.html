XCW Prime Time Wrestling (part 3)

November 11, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We come back to the studio.] Bobby: Hard to believe that the Bee's lost that one. Gorilla: I can sense your sarcasm. Bobby: Good because I'm laying it on pretty thick. Gorilla: Well fans it's time to run down the December 1st card. It's going to be a happening live from Madison Square Garden. Are you ready Brain?
Bobby: Of course I am. Gorilla: Okay... Hulk Hogan vs. Ric Flair (Steel Cage) Jake Roberts vs. Curt Hennig (US Title) Ted DiBiase vs. Shawn Michaels Owen Hart vs. Tony Anthony The Hart Foundation vs. Terry Gordy/Steve Williams (World Tag Team Titles) Hollywood Blondes vs. Chris Benoit/Dean Malenko Bobby: We have Shawn Michaels? Gorilla: He's on there isn't he? Bobby: I want him on the show next week. Gorilla: Well you're the host. See what you can do. Bobby: I will. Gorilla: Well fans it's been an incredible first night here on Prime Time. We've seen the debut of Owen Hart, Hulk Hogan, Ric Flair...now we have one more match to go and that's the return of Jake "The Snake" Roberts.
Bobby: Bleh...ugh. Gorilla: Well we're going to leave you with this match. Thanks everyone, and we'll see you next week!!!
[Camera cuts to the ring and Jake Roberts is taking on Brian Costello. The match is already in progress as Jake is working over the jobber.]
Jake with a series of jabs the jaw, and he whips him into the turnbuckle. Brian bounces out, and Jake follows up with a high knee across the chest. Jake brings him back to his feet and slams him down to the canvas. Roberts with a series of boots to the stomach, and he brings Brian back up once again. He grabs him by the arm and nails him with a short clothesline....
The crowd starts to pop as they know what time it is. Jake brings the man back to his feet, locks him in a front facelock, and hits the DDT. Jack slides over to his knocked out opponent, and gets the 1.2.3!
His music starts to play and he points over to the bag. The crowd rises to their feet as he grabs it and throws it open...Damien pops out and the ref jumps out of the ring. Jake grabs Damien and throws him onto his unconscious opponent. The camera man gets right in Jake's face and he says..."Perfect the time is counting down, tick tock, tick tock..." Jake gives that errie grin, and the camera fades.
