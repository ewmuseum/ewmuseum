XCW Monday Night Malice (part 5)

December 24, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(All of a sudden The Rock busts open the door...Mikey looks like he's pulling up his pants.)
Mikey: Don't you know how to knock? The Rock: The Rock doesn't need to knock...The Rock is the Rock. Therefore he can enter whenever he damn well pleases.
Mikey: Umm..okay. The Rock: Now The Rock is here to give you some advice about your match tonight. Misawa and Cho....
Mikey: Flair already beat you to it... The Rock: Excuse me? Mikey: Ric Flair already was in here and gave me some advice. WHOOO!! The Rock: Oh so because Ric Flai....wait a minute. Did The Rock just hear you go "Whoo"? Mikey: No I said "ah". The Rock: No The Rock heard a whoo, and the Rock didn't whoo...so that must mean that you whoo'd. Did you whoo?
Mikey: Yes. The Rock: Did Flair give you permission to use that Whooo? Mikey: Well sort of...we said it together. The Rock: Listen...the Rock doesn't want to know about your sex life. The Rock came in here to tell you that he wants to go back to Miami, Florida...he wants to be with his wife and kids on Christmas...and The Rock wants to be back in the USA. Can you make it happen?
Mikey: I dunno... The Rock: Heh...The Rock doesn't need to hear an I don't know. The Rock needs to hear yes...wait a second. The Rock is going to give you something to make you a little more confident.
(The Rock slowly pulls of his famous elbow pad and hands it to Mikey.) The Rock: Now if you lose this the Rock will Rock Bottom you all the way to The
Middle Earth where you can be with the rest of your Lord Of The Ring, Elf,
Friends.
Mikey: Wow...can you autograph this for me? The Rock: The Rock doesn't have time to play patty-cake with you. The Rock wants to know if you are going to bring the XCW back to the USA?
Mikey: I hope so. The Rock: You're trying the Rocks patience. Now he gave you the elbow pad, but since it's almost dman near Christmas the Rock is going to let you borrow something else.
Mikey: Your tights? The Rock: You'd like that wouldn't you? No, The Rock is going to let you borrow his catch phrases for the night. BUT only for tonight.
Mikey: WOW!!! The Rock: And as soon as the night is over...The Rock better never hear you use them again, because if you do...The Rock will come over to the Whipwreck House,
Rock Bottom Ma Whipwreck, Pa Whipwreck, Lil' sis with the saggy breasts, and your one eye'd dog Sparky.....then The Rock will look at you and Rock Bottom you off your damn roof.
Mikey: But I have.... The Rock: Sshhh...repeat after me. If YA SMELALALA..... Mikey: IF YOU SMELALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALA!!!! The Rock: This is not a damn song, N'Sync. You do not mess around with The
Rocks catch phrases.
Mikey: LALALA? The Rock: Better...now repeat after me. IF YA SMELALALALOW WHAT THE ROCK IS..... Mikey: If ya smell what the Rock is.... The Rock: IS WHAT? Mikey: Is what? The Rock: WHAT IS THE ROCK SMELLIN'? Mikey: I'm confused. The Rock: COOKIN'!!! Mikey: IF YA SMALLALALA WHAT THE ROCK IS COOKIN'!!! The Rock: Decent, but Mick Foleys little boy does a better job that you. Now go out there and bring the XCW back to the USA!!!!
Mikey: YAY!!! (Mikey runs out of the dressing room. The Rock sits down with his head in his hands knowing there is little to no hope.)
Jim: It's time for the Main Event. As you can see the cage has been lowered, and it's now or never. Lets hope it's now...in a few minutes we're going to know the fate of the XCW. Will my family have to move out here...or will I get to go home for Christmas?
Jesse: I'm feeling the same way. FINK: Ladies and gentlmen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, he is the special guest ref for tonights match. Here is
"Triple H"!!!! ("Are you ready to play the game?" is heard over the PA, and Triple H isn't showing up. The fans await the arrival of Triple H, and nobody shows up yet.
Finally he shows up, but he's dragging a drunk Mitsuharu Misawa. Chono is no where to be found. Triple H drags Misawa into the cage and is trying to revive him....)
FINK: And his opponent...from Detriot, Michigan. He weighs in at 224 pounds. Here is Mikey Whipwreck!!! ("Loser" by Beck plays and he comes out to a huge pop. You can see that he has
The Rocks elbow pad on, and a little bit of confidence that he doesn't usually have. He climbs into the ring and wonders why Misawa in on the floor.)
Jim: Whipwreck standing over Misawa, and Misawa is just laughing. Kurt Angle got Misawa drunk off of egg nog.
Jesse: I can't believe that!! Jim: There's the bell and Triple H is stepping in front of Misawa. He's not letting Whipwreck get to the World Champ, and Whipwreck pushes Triple H out of the way.
Jesse: Whoa!!! Jim: Whipwreck now stomping away on the drunk Misawa. He picks Misawa up and throws him into the cage, and another toss into the cage. Misawa is already on dream street, and Whipwreck with a clothesline.
Jesse: Here comes Triple H though.... Jim: Triple H gets right in Whipwrecks face, and it doesn't look like he's afraid of him at all. Whipwreck is telling him to move, and he pushes Triple H out of the way again!! Triple H can't believe it. Whipwreck brings Misawa to his feet and executes a bodyslam...elbow drop by Whipwreck, and another one.
Jesse: And once again Triple H comes over. Jim: OH Whipwreck just decked Triple H, and the fans are going wild. Misawa is up and he swings at Mikey but misses and hits nothing but air. Whipwreck with a dropkick and Misawa goes crashing into the steel cage.
Jesse: The Game is up now and he's not happy at all. Jim: Hunter whips Mikey around and nails him with a knee to the stomach. Another knee to the stomach takes Mikey down to the mat...Hunter now drives a series of punches into the forehead of Mikey.
Jesse: This isn't fair. Jim: This is TOTALLY not fair....luckly Chono isn't in the ring. Hunter brings
Mikey back up...boot to the stomach and he goes for the Pedigree. Mikey with a backdrop though, and he falls down to the ground.
Jesse: And look at Misawa he's just sitting back laughing. Jim: Mikey is making it back up to his feet and he throws Hunter into the cage. He goes over to Misawa and starts kicking away on his stomach...over and over. Come on Mikey!!! Yeah!!! Jesse: Uh oh! Jim: Hunter grabs Whipwreck from behind and throws him right into the steel cage!!! His whole body flew into the cage, and now Hunter grabs Misawa. He's trying to revive him, and Misawa isn't about to sober up.
Jesse: Whipwreck is busted open!! Jim: Mikey has been busted open, and maybe it was that wound from X-Treme. This doesn't look good for Mikey....Triple H comes over and starts stomping away on him now, and another huge stomp. Helmsley brings Mikey to his feet, but Mikey with a low blow. Mikey now with a chop, and another chop....
(Mikey lets out a huge WHOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!) Jesse: AHAHA!! Jim: Triple H grabs him and throws him into the turnbuckle, and Mikey does a
180!!!! Shades of Ric Flair!!! Mikey tries to crawl to his feet and Triple H stomps down onto his fingers, and again...
Jesse: That'll just snap them like twigs!!! Jim: Misawa is up and he pushes Triple H aside, and now he's going to work on
Mikey. He kicks Mikey in the face, and Misawa falls backwards.
Jesse: I don't think Triple H liked being pushed by Misawa. Jim: Well Misawa is drunk...he doesn't even know where he is. All he knows is that his goal is to beat up Whipwreck! Triple H walks over and starts to kick away on Mikey again, and Misawa comes over and helps him. Misawa choking Mikey with his boot now, and Hunter is kicking him.
Jesse: This isn't fair, and if I could I'd go in there and even up the odds. Jim: Well nobody can get into that ring....they both finally let up on Mikey, and the fans aren't happy about this at all. Mikey trying to get up and this kid isn't going to stop. He wants to make The Rock happy, he wants to make
Flair happy...he wants to make the fans happy!
Jesse: He wants to make me happy! Jim: I doubt that. Mikey is up and he double clotheslines both men out of the corner. COME ON!!! YEAH!!!!! Mikey with a standing legdrop over Misawa, and another one to Triple H. Mikey stands on Triple H's face and spins over it...OUCH!!!
Jesse: He needs to play dirty if he's going to win. Jim: Misawa is up now and he catches Whipwreck with a belly-to-belly suplex. I think Misawa is starting to sober up....Mikey is crawling back to his feet, and
Triple H comes over....
Jesse: Oh and Misawa pushed him again!! Triple H and Misawa are having words with each other now, and this doesn't look good for them. Oh and Misawa pushes him again....
Jim: Sonny Ono is down here now and he's trying to calm them down. He's screaming from the outside of the ring...Whipwreck is up and he spins Misawa around...WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!!!!!!!
Jesse: I can't believe it!!! Jim: Mikey goes for the pin, and Hunter isn't counting. DAMN YOU HUNTER!!! DAMN YOU STRIGHT TO HELL!!!!! I WANT TO GO HOME!!!!! Jesse: I'm about to go down there Jimbo.... Jim: Mikey is up and Triple H catches him with a boot to the stomach. PEDIGREE!!! GOD NO!!!! PLEASE NO!!!! Triple H brings Misawa over and drapes him across the chest of Mikey...
1.... 2.... 3!!!! Jesse: I can't believe it!!!!!! Jim: I can't either!!!!!!! Mikey Whipwreck has kickedout of the super fast count, and of the Pedigree nonetheless. The fans are going wild and Triple H can't believe his eyes....Misawa is a hurting still from that Whippersnapper.
Jesse: What a night!!! Jim: Hunter is slaming his hands down on the mat in frustration...he's angry!!! Whipwreck is trying to make it to his feet, and Hunter with a boot to the midsection. ANOTHER PEDIGREE!!!!!!!!! THAT'S TWO!!! DAMNIT!!!!! NO!!!!!
Jesse: Misawa makes the cover again.... 1....... 2.............. Jim: NO!!!! Mikey kicked out again, and if you never believed in miracles this would be a great time to start. Mikey Whipwreck has kicked out of the second
Pedigree, and this match isn't over yet. Hunter is crying he's so angry....
Jesse: Misawa is up and he brings Mikey to his feet...MIKEY WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!!! SMALL PACKAGE!!!! Jim: Is this it? 1. 2. 3!!!!!! Jesse: HE DID IT!!!! Jim: WE'RE GOING HOME!!!!! FANS THE XCW IS RETURNING TO THE USA!!! HUNTER MADE THE COUNT!!!!!! Jesse: I can't believe it.... FINK: Here is your winner and the NEW XCW World Champion.....Mikey Whipwreck!!!! Jim: After two years and 1 month the XCW is returning to the United States. We're coming home, and I get to see my wife and kids. What a night in XCW history!!!! It may not have been a classic wrestling battle, but it's on my top ten!!!
Jesse: Mikey is still dazed and confused....Hunter walks away, and Misawa doesn't know what's going on.
Jim: Mikey Whipwreck has done it for us...he's won the XCW World Title and brought it back to the USA!!! Of all the people...I'll be damned!! Of all the people...I'll be damned!!!
Jesse: Mikey Whipwreck pulled it off, and tonight he made history as the new
XCW World Champion.
Jim: So many people have come here and just left because they couldn't handle the pressure of being in Japan. So many people left because they needed to be with their families...but we stuck it out Jesse, and tonight it's all worth it.
I don't hate this place at all, and as a matter of fact I'm going to miss it. Jesse: Merry Christmas!!! Jim: This is one present that I was hoping I'd get!!! Fans the cage is raising and here comes Santa Claus!!! The ring crew is bringing in a HUGE box, and it looks like there could be someone in there.
Jesse: As long as it's not the Gobbledegooker..... Jim: The box is in the ring now and Santa is passing out Candy Canes to all the kids in the front row. Santa climbs into the ring, and has the XCW World Title in his hand. Santa Claus is going to award Mikey the title....but he's telling
Mikey to open the box first.
Jesse: What if it's King Kong Bundy? Jim: What? Jesse: Nevermind. Jim: Mikey is opening it...and...WHOA!!!! Look who it is.... Jim: Francine!!! What a present that is!!! Jesse: Does he get to go home and unwrap her? Jim: I would hope so...Mikey deserves it. Oh my...Mikey and Francine hug, and the fans are going crazy. Santa gives Mikey the title, and what a night this has been. Mikey holds the title up high in the air!!!
Jesse: Our new World Champion!!! Jim: Fans I'm Jim Ross saying....OH DEAR GOD!!!! SANTA JUST SINGLE ARM DDT'd
WHIPWRECK!!!!! That's no Santa Claus...OH MY GOD!!! That's...Shane Douglas!!!!!
Jesse: Shane Douglas is here??? Jim: Shane Douglas is here!!! He's back in the XCW, and he just planted our XCW
World Champion into the canvas. We're outta time!!!! MERRY CHRISTMAS!!!!!!
