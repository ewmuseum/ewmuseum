Hero"...Kurt Angle!!!

WHOOO...] FINK: And his opponent...from Miami, Florida. He weighs in at 256 pounds. Here is "The Rock"!!!!

[Rocky runs into the ring, and he ducks a clothesline. He spins Kurt around and nails him with a right, and another right...he spits on his right hand and punches Kurt Angle right over the top rope and to the outside.]

Rock here tonight.

[But the Rock counters with a back drop. Rocky grabs Angle by the head and slams it down onto the steel walkway.]
Joey: The Rock just waits there with his knees bent...he wants Angle to come at him. Kurt Angle looks like he's been hit by a truck, but he charges anyway.
Jesse: What's he thinking?? Joey: HIPTOSS BY THE ROCK ONTO THE STEEL!!!! [Angle gets right back up, Rock spins him around and plants him with a Spinebuster.] Joey: And the ref seems to letting these two just go at it. He wants to see them brawl all over the arena.
Jesse: Which is so unfair. Joey: Rock mounts Angle, and he's driving those lefts and rights into the face. Oh and Angle reaches up and drives a thumb to the eye. Rock gets off of him, and tries to clear the pain...Angle from behind with a Sleeper Drop!!!
Jesse: Nice move. Joey: And Angle is jawjacking with a fan in the crowd, and you know they're not talking about The Road To Perdition.
Jesse: What a movie. [Rock starts walking towards the ring, and Angle hits him with a double axhandle from behind. He grabs Rock and hits a reverse neckbreaker on the cement. Angle begins to stomp away on the back of Rocky. Angle slaps the fallen
Rock across the face, and then grabs the protective padding off the floor.]
Joey: What's Angle up to? What is he going to do? Jesse: He's got Rock up...Back Suplex onto the cement. Joey: No the Rock flips over him, and spins him around. Inverted Atomic Drop by
The Rock, and he takes him down with a clothesline. This place is going wild for The Rock here tonight.
["Rocky" chants are being heard all over the arena. Rock slams Angles head onto that unprotected piece outside on the floor, and then rolls him back into the ring. Rock comes in as Angle backs up, trying to escape to the turnbuckle.
Rocky looks at the crowd, and they roar.] Joey: Rocky isn't going to let up on him...he's not about to. Jesse: Give Angle a break, he deserves it. Joey: Rock heads over to the turnbuckle, and Angle with a fist to the midsection. That looked a little low to me. He grabs Rocky by the tights, and pulls him into the turnbuckle face first. Rock bounces out and falls to the canvas.
[Kurt starts stomping away on Rock. He quickly goes for the cover, and hooks the leg...] Joey: 1!!!! 2!!! NO!!! Rocky gets his shoulder up! Jesse: That was very close right there. [Kurt goes up to the second turnbuckle, and flies off...and he eats the Rock's boot. Rock kips-up, and whips Angle into the ropes. Rock goes for a Samoan
Drop, but Angle slides down from it and locks in a modified Dragon Sleeper.]
Joey: And Angle is going to try to put Rock away right now, but the Rock is staggering around and fighting it off. Rock grabs Angle by the waist, and sets him up onto a turnbuckle.
Jesse: This doesn't look good for Angle, he needs to get down from there pretty quick. Joey: Rock climbing up, and he fires a shot into the head of Angle, and now
Rock with a Back Suplex off the top rope. OH MY GOD!!!
Jesse: Angle's neck landed awkward. Joey: Rock goes for the cover, and he hooks the leg. 1!!! 2!!! 3!!! Jesse: WHAT?!? Joey: No, Angle got his foot on the ropes. Rock glares at Angle, before yelling something to him. Rock gets up and kicks Angle's right leg, and what's he doing now? He grabs the leg and slides out to the floor....
Jesse: Eye for an eye? Joey: He takes his leg and whacks it against the ringpost. Oh and he does it again, and the crowd is on their feet loving this.
Jesse: Yeah of course they would. They love it when someone gets injured so they can just complain about it later.
Joey: Rock listens to the crowd, and he repeats it! Again and again!!! The crowd is now begining to count along with each blow. Rock is just trying to payback Angle for what he did to his throat.
[One!! Two!! Three!! Four!!! Five!! Six!! Seven!! Eight!! Nine....] Joey: Rock stops, he grabs the other leg, and he wacks both of them into the ring post. Oh my God...Rock is in total control.
Jesse: This isn't fair. Hasn't the ref ever heard of a countout? Joey: He hurt the Rock's throat, and now it's time for him to get even. Jesse: Even is not cheating! The Rock is a cheater. [The Rock grabs Angle by the legs - and he goes for the figure four around the ring post.] Joey: And Angle is screaming in agony!! He's shaking his head violently back and forth, and Rock just applies more pressure.
Jesse: Finally the ref lays on the count. Joey: He's gotten to 5, but Rock hasn't let go yet. Jesse: That's not fair...see other people would be DQ'd. Joey: Rock finally lets go, and Angle crawls backwards out of the corner and
Rock climbs back in. He splits Angle's legs apart, and he is going for the
Sharpshooter!!!
Jesse: Oh come on!!! Joey: And Angle kicks Rock back against the turnbuckle, and the Rock almost hit the ref. Kurt makes it back up, and limps over to Rock...Angle with a right hand, and another right hand.
[Kurt lifts Rock up and plants him in the center of the ring with a quick snap suplex. Angle grabs his knee in pain though. He walks around trying to get rid of the pain, and Rock is sitting up. Angle comes from behind and locks in the
Cobra Clutch.]
Joey: Rock is trying to fight it off, and he reaches from behind Kurt......... JAWKBREAKER!!!! The turnbuckle is the only thing that's keep Kurt Angle standing now. He's been knocked silly...
Jesse: This is making me sick. Angle can't get any offense in at all. [Rock staggers back to his feet and stares at Angle from the oppotise turnbuckle. He wipes the sweat from his brow, and charges at him. Rock nails
Kurt with a clothesline against the turnbuckle. Angle staggers out of the turnbuckle, and Rock kicks him in the stomach...Rock with a jumping DDT, and then makes the cover.]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Angle barely got his shoulder up off the mat, and
Rock is going to the ropes.
Jesse: These people havenot stopped chanting the "Rocks" name since this match started. [Rock goes for a knee drop, but Angle rolls out of the way. Rock back to his feet quickly, and charges the dazed Angle. Kurt backdrops Rock over the top ropes, and onto the cement floor.]
Joey: Rock falls out over by us, and Angle is limping out after him. Jesse: This could get ugly. Joey: Rock catches Angle with a right hand, and another right hand. Rock puts
Angle on the table next to us. Now Rock is climbing onto the table....he brings
Angle to his feet.
Jesse: This could be it... Joey: ROCK BOTTOM!!!!! [As Rock lifts up Angle for the Rock Bottom the table goes out, and both men go crashing to the floor. Angle smashes his head onto the concrete, and Rock's knees take a bad beating from the suprise fold.]
Joey: OH MY GOD, and both men go down. The table couldn't take the weight, and
Rock is hurt...Angle is hurt.
Jesse: That might have saved Angle from losing the match tonight. Joey: And the ref begins the count. Nobody is moving at the moment. [1....2....3....4....5...6......7......8....9..] Joey: Kurt is up, barely up. He grabs Rock by the head, and rolls him back into the ring. Jesse: It looked like me like Kurt got the worst of the spill, but he's the first one up. Joey: He charges Rocky, and Rock with a 180 Spinbuster!!! He caught him with a
Spinebuster, and he hooks the leg. 1!!! 2!!! 3!!! NO!!!
Jesse: What a close call!!!! Joey: Kurt Angle got his shoulder up just in time. These fans are beside themselves, thinking that Rock had this match won.
[Rock sits there on the mat stunned. He gets back up and kicks Angle's leg into his body, and looks to the crowd. He grabs his elbow pad and throws it into the crowd, and runs to the ropes....]
Joey: PEOPLE'S ELBOW!!!!!! Jesse: He MISSED!!!! Joey: And Rock hits nothing but cold canvas. Kurt Angle is up now and he brings
Rock to his feet...OLYMPIC SLAM!!!!! OLYMPIC SLAM!!!!! He drapes his arm over the Rock...Angle with the cover. 1!!! 2!!!!

Joey: Rocky grabs Angle, and he whips him into the steel ste - NO!! Angle reverses it, and Rock goes smashing into the steps. He hits them with authority, with such impact it takes the top portion of the steps off.

[Rock rolls back into the ring, and Angle follows.] Joey: Angle swings at the dazed Rock, and he ducks it. Rock with a boot to the stomach, Angle drops the bell....Double Arm DDT into the bell.
