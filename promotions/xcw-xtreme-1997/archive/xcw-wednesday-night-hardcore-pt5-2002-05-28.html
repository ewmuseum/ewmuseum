XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 5)

May 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: We're back and during the break we saw Armstrong chase Hunter and Steiner all the way out of the arena. As soon as he turned around Cactus was there to attack him...
[A screen pops up and we see "Moments Ago". Cactus grabs Armstrong and throws him over the mech. table. Cactus gets onto the table and drops an elbow across the sternum. Security, Police, and XCW officials are all there to break it up.
Camera cuts back to Jim and Joel...] Jim: Steve doesn't want to be liked... Joel: Why makes enemies with a guy like Cactus Jack? Jim: I don't have any idea. Joel: Steve is an odd bird. Jim: Fans it's time for our Main Event, and what a match this is going to be tonight. Last week we saw Tommy Dreamer come out and save Stevie Richards from the hardcore attack by Raven.
Joel: That was awesome. Jim: So tonight we get Tommy Dreamer taking on Raven in a Hardcore match. Joel: This is going to be hot. We have two freakin' wackos willing to do anything to win here tonight.
Jim: Well let's take you down to How - Joel:...... Jim: Fans I'm being told that something is going on in the parking lot, and we've got a cameraman on the scene. Lets go to that right now.
Joel: YAY! [Camera cuts to Raven who is pulling up in his car....] Jim: Well it looks like Raven is running a little late!! Joel: WHOA!!! [All of a sudden from out of no where comes Tommy Dreamer with a bat, and he smashes the drivers side window. Dreamer drops the bat on the ground and grabs
Raven. He pulls him through the window and drops him out onto the floor.]
Jim: And Raven isn't even dressed for the match! You can see that the broken glass is cutting right though his clothes and slicing right into his skin!
Joel: Nice!!! Jim: Tommy gets Raven out of the car, and the white t-shirt that Raven was wearing is now dark red. He's got it covered in blood, and Tommy Dreamer isn't wasting any time at all.
Joel: Heh...ya think? [Dreamer grabs Raven and slams his head onto the hood of the car. Raven stumbles back and Tommy drops him with a clothesline to the throat.]
Jim: Well Dreamer wanted to go for the cover, but there's no ref out here yet. Joel: That's what he gets for not waiting. Jim: Dreamer brings Raven back up and whips him into the rental car!!!! Joel: That Hertz....hahah get it? Jim: Oh no. Dreamer charges, and Raven backdrops him onto the hood!!!!!!!! [Raven stares at Dreamer who is stunned on the car, and Raven takes his shirt off and jumps onto the hood. He begins to stomp away on Tommy, and he's not letting up for one second.]
Jim: And finally here comes Nick Patrick!!! Joel: It's about damn time. Jim: Raven brings Tommy to his feet and bodyslams him onto the ROOF OF THE
CAR!!!! Raven jumps up, and LEGDROP!!!!!
Joel: I don't think this is covered in the insurance. Jim: Raven makes the cover on the roof, and Nick Patrick makes the count. ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT BY DREAMER!!! Joel: You're going to have to hit Tommy with a lot more than that to take him out. Jim: Raven brings Dreamer to his feet and grabs him by the neck...OH GOOD GOD
ALMIGHTY!!! He just tossed Dreamer from the roof of the car down to the cement!!!
Joel: Splat!!!! Jim: Now what in the hell is Raven doing? [Raven leaps off the roof of the car and goes for an elbow....] Jim: AND DREAMER MOVES!!!! Joel: Ouch...that's not nice at all. Raven could've popped his elbow out. [Dreamer brings Raven to his feet and throws him into the arena door. Raven goes flying though, and Dreamer follows.]
Jim: They're right inside the loading dock now, and the workers don't seem to give a damn. They love a good fight just as much as the next person.
Joel: Yeah, but that's because they fight each other all the time. Jim: Dreamer stops, and he see's an idle semi...and you can almost see the wheels turning in his mind. You can see what's going on, and this isn't going to be good at all. Raven better get himself together before Tommy does something serious.
[Dreamer climbs into the cab of the truck, and magically the keys are there. Tommy smiles and starts the semi up. The sound of the horn can be heard, a huge cloud of smoke comes out of the smokestack...Dreamer puts his foot on the gas which is headed right for Raven.]
Joel: HOLY SHIT!!!!! Jim: Dreamer what in the hell are you thinking??? Joel: He's going to kill him!!! [Raven sees it coming and dives out of the way at the very last second. Dreamer crashes the semi into a steel garage door!]
Jim: Dreamer slowly pulls himself out of the cab, and Raven is over on the drives side step..and he grabs Dreamer by the head. Raven tosses him out of the cab!
Joel: Talk about fair play! [Raven climbs to the top of the cab, and he's yelling for Dreamer to follow. Tommy Dreamer collects his thoughts and makes his way to the top. Both men end up about 15 to 20 feet up on the top of that trailor.]
Jim: And Raven begins to unload with lefts and rights, and Dreamer fights back with some of his own. Raven is stunned, and Dreamer goes behind him and hits an atomic drop.
Joel: WHOA and Raven is about to fall off!!! He's about to fall 20 feet down to the concrete!!!
Jim: Don't push him off Dreamer!!! [Tommy spins Raven around and hits an inverted atomic drop this time, and that saves Raven from falling. Raven holds his crotch in pain, and Dreamer clotheslines him down ontot he hard trailor.]
Jim: Tommy makes the cover!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Raven kicks out!!! Joel: That was close...if Raven would've fallen it would've been all over for him. Jim: What the hell is Dreamer doing now? He's bringing Raven back up, and he wraps his hand around his throat.
Joel: OH NO!!! He's going to chokeslam him off the Trailor...20 feet down to the bottom!!! Jim: Dreamer has that crazy look in his eyes!!! [Raven is trying to break the hold with all his might, but Dreamer just isn't letting go.] Jim: Tommy lifts him.... Joel: Swift kick to the groin!!! [Dreamer backs off and Raven charges him....] Jim: OH GOD!!!! OH GOOD GOD!!! HOLY HELL!!!!!! Joel: RAVEN SPEARED HIM AND THEY BOTH FLEW OFF THE TRAILOR!!!!!! Jim: I can't believe what I just saw!!!! BOTH MEN 20 FEET!!!!!! GOOD GOD!!!!!! STOP THE DAMN MATCH!!!!! Joel: Did you see the air they caught? Jim: Oh my dear Lord!!!! One can only hope that these two men are okay...they took one hell of a bump right now, and things don't look good.
Joel: And the workers don't even seem to care. They're just working around them? How odd is that?
Jim: You know there comes a time when these high spots are way to much, and tonight that's what this is. Raven took it too far, and both of these young men are seriously hurt now.
Joel: Well I hope Raven is okay...I wish I could say the same for Tommy Dreamer, but he's been boinking Francine. So he deserves to die. Jim: It looks like the ref is starting the ten count, and one can only hope this is the end of the match tonight.
1 2 3 4 5 6 Jim: Nobody is moving yet....both men have not gotten up. 7 8 Jim: WHAT IN THE HELL!? Both men are moving, and they're making it back to their feet!!! I can't believe it. I can't believe it. How the hell are these two men doing it?
9 Jim: And they're up!!! Oh my God they're up!!! Joel: Amazing!!! Jim: Give credit where credit is due...these two survived a hellish fall, and now they're up. This match isn't over!
[Raven grabs Tommy and flings him through the door this time into the arena area. He drags Dreamer closer and closer to the ring stopping every now and then to smash his head into a cement wall or two.]
Jim: And now Raven goes behind Dreamer as they stand in front of the doorway to a locker room. AWE HELL!!!!!
Joel: Whoa!!! [Raven executes a Side Russian Legsweep which knock both men into the door and onto the locker room floor. Raven puts his arm over Dreamer in a sad pin attempt.]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! Joel: He's going to win!!! Jim: THR - NO!!!!! Dreamer just barely kicked out of that one, and Raven brings him to his feet. Raven with a thumb to the eye, and now he's looking through the locker room.
Joel: What's he going to find? Jim: OH NO...Raven's found a....a....vibator??????? Joel: Hey wait a minute...this isn't Mike Piazza's locker room!!! Jim: Fans I'm really sorry about this...I know this is a family show, and I don't know why someone has that in their locker room.
Joel: Well I didn't know we had Pat Patterson working for us. Jim: You can go all day with that can't you? Joel: No, but Pat Patterson can! Jim: OH!!! He smashes "it" into the head of Dreamer, and he falls down. Raven makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!!!!
Joel: How embarassing would that be for Dreamer to lose to a vibrator shot? Jim: Can we talk about something else? Joel: Sure...what happened to your face? [Raven brings Dreamer up once again and drags him out of the locker room, and you can see the curtain is getting closer.]
Jim: And there they are...the crowd is going wild as both men make their way down to the ring.
[Raven gives Dreamer a knee to the stomach, and sets up a powerbomb which he turns into a Hotshot!.]
Jim: OH!!! Dreamer's face just ate that raw steel of the enterance ramp!!! Raven isn't done yet, as he drags Dreamer to the ringside area. He tosses Tommy into the guardrail, and he follows up with a boot to the stomach.
Joel: This has been one brutal night. Lucky we only have one card a week! [Raven goes for another right hand, but Dreamer back drops him into the crowd. Tommy ignores it and walks over to the time keepers table. He grabs the ring bell and heads up onto the ring apron. Raven recovers, and climbs back to the ringside area and before he can even look up...Dreamer jumps off and hits him in the head with the ring bell.]
Joel: Talking about ringing someone's bell. HA!!! Jim: And he gets paid for these ladies and gentlemen. Tommy leaves Raven on the floor, and he's looking under the ring. He pulls out a TABLE!!!! Tommy slides it and himself into the ring.
Joel: He's setting it up, and here comes Raven. Raven is busted wide open from that bell shot...it's like that scene from Unfaithful.
Jim: Raven is on the apron, and Tommy gets a running start and knocks him off with a huge forearm!!! Raven goes crashing into the guardrail!!!!!
[Tommy finishes setting the table up, and Raven goes under the ring to find something.] Joel: And things are about to heat up!!!! Jim: Oh no....this isn't good. Raven has just pulled a canister of gasoline, and a lighter from under the ring.
Joel: What the hell is that doing under the ring anyway? [Tommy has just finished setting the table up, and as soon as he turns around
Raven shoots some of the gasoline into his eyes. Tommy drops to the floor in pain, trying to clean his eyes out....Raven simply walks past him and drenches the table in gasoline fluid.]
Jim: If I know what's next...this isn't good. Joel: No shit...you said that like for times. Jim: Raven steps back and flicks the lighter!!!! He tosses it onto the table!!! [The table goes up in flames with a flwoosh...] Jim: Raven walks over to Dreamer...LOW BLOW!!!! Tommy hits him with a low blow, and he whips him into the ropes. OH MY GOD!!!!
Joel: WHOA!!!!!! Jim: SPINEBUSTER ONTO THE FLAMING TABLE BY DREAMER!!!!!! SPINEBUSTER THROUGH THE FLAMING TABLE!!!!!! Joel: I can't believe what I am seeing tonight!!! Jim: And the flames are gone, and so is Raven! Joel: For that matter so is Dreamer. Jim: Cover by Tommy....ONE!!! TWO!!! Joel:.......... Jim:.......... Joel:.......... Jim: THREE!!!!! Joel: NO!!!! Jim: Tommy Dreamer wins the match here tonight!!!! FINK: Here is your winner....Tommy Dreamer!!! Jim: I can't believe this match here tonight. Both men gave it everything they had, and....My God is all I can say.
Joel: Yeah I'm at a loss too. Jim: My God...fans for Joel Gertner, I'm Jim Ross saying thank you for tuning in. [Camera fades, but then quickly fades back to the parking lot. Arn Anderson is walking to his car, and he gets jumped by Ric Flair and Scott Hall. Both men start punching away on him...and Hall grabs the keys. The trunk flies open, and
Arn begins to fight back. Anderson knocks Hall down to the ground, and then throws Ric Flair into the trunk. Once again Arn slams the trunk, and Ric Flair is locked in. Arn kicks Hall again, and runs to his car...]
Scott: Ugh...man...the old bastard got me again. You okay Ric? Ric? [All Hall hears is thumping. He gets up and he looks at the trunk...] Scott: Flair? Ric: DAMNIT!!!! Scott: Are you in there? Ric: DAMNIT!!!! ARN!!!! ARN!!!! You're a dead man!!!! Scott: Umm...buddy, I don't have the keys. Ric: They're in here. Scott: Awe...hell. [Camera fades.]
