XCW Monday Night Malice (part 5)

January 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans during the break Ric Flair was taken to the nearest Hospital, and he's being checked on as we speak. The whole damn thing was a set up, and Flair walked right into it.
Jesse: I wanna know where Arn Anderson was. I mean he was with him all night, and then all of a sudden the man just vanishes?
Jim: I don't know what to tell you. Jesse: I'm going to get my men on it. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Atlanta, GA. He weighs in at 245 pounds...here is "Wrestling Sensation" Buff Bagwell!!! ("I'm Buff and I'm The Stuff" is heard all over the arena, and he comes out to a huge mixed reaction. He does his Buff Skip, and climbs into the ring.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Amarillo, Texas. He weighs in at 230 pounds...here is "The Hardcore Legend" Terry Funk!!!
("Simple Man" by Lynard Skynard starts to play and he comes out to a huge ovation. He's got what looks to be a snake skin around his neck, and he walks around the ring slapping the fans hands before he climbs into the ring. Buff attacks him right away...)
Jim: And Buff isn't going to waste his time!!! Bagwell with a forearm shot to the back, and another one...
Jesse: Well this is his debut tonight, and he's not about to let this match get away. <Buff whips him into the ropes, and catches him with a HUGE powerslam. Bagwell gets next to him and does some push-ups.>
Jim: What an arrogant guy he is. Jesse: If ya got it then flaunt it. Jim: I guess your right. <Buff nails Fuck with a right hand, and Funk swings but misses. Bagwell with another right hand and then follows up with an elbow to the face. Funk falls down, and Buff steps on him and strikes a pose.>
Jim: And you thought Gunn was not focused. Jesse: Well I never said that Buff was. <Bagwell isn't paying attention, and Funk grabs his foot and pulls it out from under him. Bagwell hits the canvas face first.>
Jim: And what a counter by Funk. The Hardcore Legend now fires in with some lefts and rights.
Jesse: Too bad that's all he can do. Jim: The man has been around for more than 20 years...if that's all he could do he wouldn't be in the business still.
Jesse: Oh yeah...well Hogan is. Jim: Good point. <Funk lifts up Buff and executes a knee breaker. Funk goes for a suplex, and
Buff counters with a small package. He's got a hand full of tights...>
Jim: 1!!! 2!! 3!!! Buff had a handfull of tights!!! Jesse: So? FINK: Here is your winner....Buff Bagwell!!! Jim: That's not fair... Jesse: Nothing ever is. Jim: Funk can't believe it and he's talking to the ref. Buff got away with a quick, cheap win here tonight. He can't be proud of himself...
Jesse: Well a win is a win, and it'll go down as a win in the books. Not a win with a handfull of tights.
Jim: Terry Funk has other things to worry about anyway. He's headed to the back, and Buff should be leaving now too. What's he sticking around for?
(Buff gets on the microphone...) Buff: Heh...ya know since we still have some time right now...I'd like to take a minute and call a pretty little lady up from the crowd and give her the honor of touching my buff stuff.
(Crowd boo's.) Buff: What? You boo this? I don't think so. Ya see I'm Buff and I'm The Stuff....and I just beat Terry Funk. What do ya'll think of that? (Boooo...the ring officials bring a lady wearing a purple dress into the ring. She's rather tall, and extremely ugly. She's wearing a ton of make up, and some horrible fake pearl earrings.)
Buff: Ummm I know there are some finer chicks in the crowd. I don't want Chewbaca here touchin' this body. Get her out of here. Girl: I'm your biggest fan. Buff: Yeah you are my biggest fan. Now get out of here... <Buff turns around to talk to another part of the crowd, and the girl takes off her wig and reveals that it's none other than VAL VENIS!!!!!!>
Jim: VAL VENIS is here and the crowd is going crazy. Jesse: Buff thinks it's for him.... <Val grabs the mic and gets right behind Buff..> Val: (Girl voice) Excuse me...I forgot to tell you something. <Buff begins to speak as he turns around...> Buff: What the hell do you want you ugly piece of..... <Just then Venis knocks him down to the ground with a huge right hand. Buff gets back up and Venis knocks him down again. Venis goes to the top ropes...>
Jim: MONEY SHOT!!!!! Jesse: IN A DRESS!!!! Jim: Val Venis has tricked Buff Bagwell, and who looks like the idiot? Buff has lost his stuff. Venis walks away and the girls are going wild for him, and Buff can't believe it.
Jesse: What a war that's going to be!!! (Camera cuts to Mikey Whipwreck....all of a sudden we see Michael Cole walk into the room.)
Michael: Mikey...I just wanted to let you know that Austin is pla..... Mikey: Listen Coley...I don't need to hear anything. I've been sitting in this locker room all night not listening to anything but me. I don't need to ruin my moment.
Michael: But Chad.... Mikey: Chad isn't going to get his way tonight. Austin and I are going to open a can off 100% whoop ass, and there's not a damn thing anybody can do about it.
Michael: But.... Mikey: Now if you'll excuse me...I have a match!!! (Mikey leaves Cole in the locker room....)
