XCW Monday Night Malice (part 6)

December 8, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: So this is what it boils down to...Triple H, Chris Jericho, Tazz and
Booker T. All four men are going to battle it out in a no holds barred way for the XCW World Title.
Don: I have never been so damn excited in my whole life. Jim: That's pretty sad. Fans lets get to our Main Event!!! [Booker T makes his way down to the ring to a huge ovation. The fans are all on their feet as he slaps their hands...Booker walks over to one side of the ring and he begins looking underneath for weapons of some kind...
While he is doing that the next man is introduced, and it's the man they call
Jericho. He gets solidly boo'd here tonight, but he's no where to be found. So
Fink decides to continue on with the introductions...
He introduces Taz, and he wastes no time running down to the ring. Taz spins
Booker around, and Booker levels him with a stop sign. Taz falls down to the ground, and Booker continues to work him over.
Fink continues and introduces the final guy in the Main Event. Triple H...but just like Chris Jericho he's no where to be found. Both Hunter and Jericho haven't shown up, but that doesn't seem to bother Tazz and Booker T.]
Jim: Booker T laying in those soccer style kicks to the ribs of Taz, and wait a minute we're getting word that Chris Jericho and Hunter are duking it out over by the loading docks.
Don: Damn I thought that they might be plotting something together. Jim: Hunter clocks Chris Jericho with a right, and that knocks him back into a semi-truck... Hunter slams his head into the side of the semi! Chris Jericho stumbles off... Hunter grabs a wooden plank and charges at Jericho! Wooden plank clothesline!
Don: That's not pretty. Jim: Hunter goes for the cover, but there is no ref is sight. Well, I suppose we'll alternate doing commentary on the Taz/Booker and Hunter/Jericho fights until they meet up, assuming they do at some point...
[At ringside, Booker has brought Taz up to his feet, and he rams him face-first into the ringpost! Taz bounces off like a pinball, then crashes to the floor...Booker makes the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! And a kickout by Taz! Don: Close one there. Jim: Booker T brings the Human Suplex Machine up to his feet now - big kick to the face! To the stomach! To the jaw! To the side of the head! To the chest!
Taz stumbles back into the guardrail, and Booker T is really letting it all out. Don: Well he's fresh. He didn't have to fight anyone here tonight... Jim: Booker definitly has the advantage. [Booker gets a quick start and hits a hook kick which sends Taz into the crowd. Booker T gets a running start, and he dives over the guardrail and onto Taz...but Taz catches him and turns it into a spinning belly to belly suplex.] Jim: WHOA what a counter that was by Taz, and the man went toe to toe with the
Undertaker earlier on tonight and now he's not going to let Booker T by with anything either.
Don: What's going on with Jericho and Triple H? [When we cut back it looks like they're resting, and then all of a sudden they start fighting again. Hunter has a long lead pipe and he's smashing it into the leg of Chris Jericho...Chris is yelling "Please no more"...but Hunter continues.]
Don: Seems like he's being a little easy on Jericho with those shots. Jim: Doesn't sound like it though. We've now got a ref down there, and even he is suprised by the noise Jericho is making. Hunter brings him up and he goes for a right hand, but it's blocked by Jericho....Jericho fires back, but it's blocked, Hunter tries again and that is blocked.
Don: Um. Jim: Block, block, block. Don: You sound like a chicken. Jim: Both men are trying to hit each other, and they keep blocking the punches. [We find Booker T and Taz again, this time in a clearing in the fan area where a merchandise stand and a refreshment stand are conveniently located!]
Jim: Taz hammering away on the bent over Booker T. Don: WHAT!? Jim: Not like that you sick bastard. He's pounding forearms into his back...Taz wailing away at Booker, and now he straightens him up and drags him over to the merchandise stand, where a table awaits, covered in XCW merchandise...
Don: What did you expect it to be covered in, moose excrement? Of course it's covered in XCW merchandise!
Jim: Thank you for that delightful image, as Taz goes behind Booker and hooks him up... german suplex through the table which shatters!
Don: Shards of wood go everywhere, workers scatter, Brock Lesnar dolls are thrown miles in every direction!
Jim: Taz standing over the fallen Booker T , and what's he doing now? Digging through the rubble,Taz produces a... well, a foam finger.
Don: Unfortunately, not a foam middle finger. Jim: Taz shrugs, apparently it was the best he could find, and now he's balling it up and he starts to shove it down the throat of Booker T, choking him! And wait a minute! Running out from the backstage area with chairs is Triple H and
Chris Jericho!
Don: Huh? Jim: It was a set up!! It was a set up all along! The crowd is going nuts, but
Taz doesn't realize Hunter and Jericho are coming up behind him!
[Thunderous double chairshot to Taz...Hunter tosses the chair down to the ground and brings Taz up... spikes him with a DDT on the chair!]
Jim: There's a cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Don: Booker rolls up Jericho by suprise...ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!!!! NO!!! I can't believe it. Jim: Just like that Chris Jericho is go - OH COME ON!!! Booker just got smashed with a chair to the back of the head by Hunter.
[Jericho won't leave, but Edge comes from out of no where and starts to unload on him. Both men start fighting to the outside of the arena.]
Jim: That's one way to get rid of Chris Jericho. Don: Damnit. [Hunter just gets right back up and brings Taz to his feet... and from behind
Booker lays out Hunter with a stiff kick!]
Jim: Spinning Heel kick by Booker T... Don: Now what the hell is he doing? [Booker T now climbing a nearby set of built-in stairs up to the next level of fans... there's a little space between the safety guardrail and the end of the level, and Booker is going to try something off that.]
Jim: Booker T measures Taz...leaps off! HARLEM HANGOVER COMING UP!!! Don: HE MISSES!!!! Jim: And oh my God Booker misses! Taz rolls aside and the 180 legdrop fails to connect! Booker T's leg slams into the concrete, and he's obviously in deep pain. Taz struggles up to his feet, brings Booker up as well... there's the
T-Bone Taz Plex on the concrete floor! This has to be it! Cover!
Don: Ooooo Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Booker T has seriously injured himself here tonight, and now it's down to two men. Taz and Triple H...who is going to walk out the XCW World Champion?
Don: Only one man can walk out of Conseco with it...and my money is on Taz!!! Jim: I don't know...Hunter just charged at Taz as he got up and tackled him right into the refreshment stand, knocking it over! Coke and Pepsi are spilling on the floor, CODE RED! bottles dot the landscape, hot dogs are everywhere, beer is flowing freely, my God it's chaos!
Don: Did you say... beer? Mmm... beer. The cause of and solution to all of life's problems.
Jim: Hunter gets up first, and he brings Taz with him...They're approaching what looks to be a camera tower, and the cameraman smartly heading down the ladder and out of town...Hunter drops Taz off and starts climbing the ladder...Taz recovering as Hunter reaches the top and calls down to him...
[Taz snarls and begins to head up the ladder. Heading up to the top... it's high, maybe ten feet or so.]
Don: Watch out, Taz! He's got the camera! Jim: Indeed he does, Hunter has detached the camera itself and is hiding it behind his back... Taz gets up, climbs onto the top of the tower it's a solid floor up there, with a fair amount of room to move.
[Hunter swings the camera...But Taz knows it's coming and ducks. He had to know something was up because Hunter allowed him up there without a fight.]
Jim: Hunter caught off-guard, and Taz kicks him in the gut, causing him to drop the camera! Taz picks it up - camera shot!
Don: NICE!!!! Jim: Hunter down, and Taz makes the cover! The ref on the floor counts by banging his hand against the ladder! ONE!!! TWO!!! .......NO!!! Hunter just manages to kick out at two! Taz pissed, and he heaves the camera aside, it goes over the edge and shatters on the floor...
[He brings Hunter up...there's no safety guardrails up there...Grabs Hunter by the head and takes him to the edge of the tower! He's going to suplex him off the camera tower to the floor ten feet below...]
Don: DO IT!!! Jim: But Hunter counters! Knee to the gut! Taz stunned, doubled over, and
Hunter hooks his head! OH GOD NO!!!! NO NO NO NO!!!! VERTICAL SUPLEX OFF THE
TOWER!!!! Don: AHHHHHH!!!! Jim: Hunter got the worst of it!!! Taz twisted his body in mid-air, and he landed onto Triple H. He bounces off with impact though, and they're just an arms reach away from making the cover.
Don: Oh come on!!! Jim: WAIT HBK IS OUT!!! HBK IS OUT HERE!!!! Don: Jericho is chasing him!!!! Jim: Shawn rushes past the ref, and James Beard stops Jericho in his tracks. Both of them are having words... [Shawn Michaels starts to help Taz up...] Jim: He's going to screw Hunter if it's the last thing he does. Damnit do it!!! Don: DO IT!!! Jim: Taz is helped up, and Shawn tells him to cover that SONOVABITCH!!! [Taz goes to cover, but before he can Shawn taps him on the shoulder. Taz spins around...] Jim: SUPERKICK!!!! SUPERKICK WITH THE BAD KNEE!!!!! MY GOD WE'VE BEEN DUPED!!!! Don: ................. Jim: Taz falls down to the crowd, and Shawn drapes Hunter's arm over Taz. What in the hell is going on!?
Don: The ref turns around, and he see's this.... Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! GOOD GOD!!!!! HUNTER HAS SCREWED US ALL!!! ["Break It Down" is heard all over the arena, and HBK helps up Hunter with the assitance of Chris Jericho.]
Don: Wait a minute, this is the theme music for DX!!!! Jim: Don't tell me...DX has come to the XCW? [Hunter is almost dead from the fall he took, but he hanging on to HBK and Y2J. Both men celebrating with big smiles on their faces. The ref hands Hunter the title, but too weak to hold it..Shawn takes it and raises it into the air.]
Jim: What in the hell just happened? Don: Like you said...we've been duped. Jim: Fans we'll sort all this out, and see you next week. So long everyone!!!
