XCW Monday Night Malice (part 4)

January 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Generic nWo music begins to play and Scott Norton comes out, much to the chagrin of the audience. They even begin throwing a couple drinks but Norton responds with a violent expression. He rolls in the ring and warms up.]
Jim Ross: What a monster. Scott Norton, in his short stay, has already managed to shake things up. Last week, he came within moments of capturing the
XCW World title shot.
Jesse Ventura: Yeah, although I disagree with his actions, he's a pretty mean guy. I sure wouldn't wanna fight 'em.
[Sounds of a garbage truck and various other odd noises are heard, and through the curtains stumbles the Brooklyn Brawler. Dressed in the usual ratty clothes, he runs down to the ring and slides in.]
Jim Ross: There's the bell and we've got a match underway! Scott Norton now, pounding away at the Brawler with those powerful boots. He grabs the Brawler by the head, oh man! He manhandles the Brawler by tossing him into the corner!
What power displayed here by Scott Norton! Jesse Ventura: The Brawler better say his prayers. Jim Ross: Norton resorting to those big hook punches of his. Brawler now, responds with a couple chops... My goodness, Norton just laughs.. And delivers a headbutt!! That had to crack something!! Norton grabs Brawler, whip to the turnbuckle, Brooklyn Brawler stumbles back... Clothesline!! He decapitated him with that move!
Jesse Ventura: I doubt the Brawler has much else to show us tonight. I mean, he's not exactly off to a great start.
Jim Ross: Certainly not. Norton grabs Brawler, whips him into the ropes.. What a powerslam!! The power of this man is phenomenal! Cover! 1, 2, no, the
Brooklyn Brawler is able to get a shoulder up. Norton now leaning over the
Brawler, hammering his face with lefts and rights. Brawler can't do much else but cover up. He grabs the Brawler and oh man! He takes a nasty spill to the outside. What's he gonna do now?
Jesse Ventura: This don't look too good! Jim Ross: Norton grabs the Brawler... He gorilla presses the Brawler!! What's he... No, don't!! Oh my goodness, Brawler down throat first across the guard rail!! Norton is laughing, what a sick man. He stomps away now, Brawler basically sprawled lifeless on the mats.
Jesse Ventura: Ring the bell, this one is over. Jim Ross: Scott grabs the Brawler by the hair.. He can barely stand... Benchpress again by Norton!!! OH LORD!! This time he's dropped gut first on those steel ring steps!! Come on ref!!
Jesse Ventura: Oh wow, that's one strong son of a gun right there. Brawler's what, 2-somethin'? Yeah, he's a fairly hefty fellow.
Jim Ross: You got that right. Wait a minute, what the hell is Norton doing coming over here?
[Norton starts yelling at JR and points at him.] Jim Ross: I know, no one's saying you're a bum. Take it in the ring, will ya? Jesse Ventura: Don't worry Jim, I got your back. Jim Ross: Thank God for that. Norton rolls Brawler back into the ring... He walks over and lifts him up. What's this gonna be, a piledriver? Norton lifts him up, and.. OH!! A devastating powerbomb, right on the back of his neck!!
Jesse Ventura: Like I said a couple minutes ago, this one is over. Jim Ross: Brawler isn't moving, I think you're right Jesse. Wait, Norton's grabbing him again. What the hell is he doing now? He picks Brawler up and sets him on the turnbuckle, facing the crowd... Norton slides in front of him, he's got Brawler in powerbomb position.. He climbs to the bottom rope, now the middle.. He's on the top and he's got Brawler in powerbomb position!!! He jumps.... OH MY LORD!!!! Top rope powerbomb and Brawler is out!!!!!!
Jesse Ventura: My gosh, ring that damn bell. Brawler's neck was snapped, I swear it, JR! Jim Ross: Oh man, I wouldn't doubt that for a second. Norton with that sick smile again.. He covers the Brawler...
1, 2, 3! Ding, Ding, Ding. Jim Ross: Thank God, this one's over. Mark it a win for Scott Norton but poor
Brawler didn't have a chance to exhibit any offense.
Jesse Ventura: Look Jim, he ain't done! Jim Ross: What is he doing? Norton on the outside, looking under the ring.. Get him out of there.. He pulls out.. What is that?... A ladder?! No, a table!!! He's setting up a damn table!!!! Come on, get security out here.
Jesse Ventura: Forget protection, he needs paramedics. Jim Ross: Norton is setting the table up and he's looking under the ring again... What's going on? He's got another table!!!!!! Damn it!!! He sets it up, and he's placing it on top of the first. Good God almighty, will someone please stop this???
Jesse Ventura: Would you look at that! The Brooklyn Brawler showing signs of life. He's getting to his feet now.
Jim Ross: Norton slides that ladder inside the ring, and he slides in. Look at Brawler! He's going for some boots to the back, but Norton gets up...
Brawler with a low blow!!! Norton's on the mat, gasping for air!! He didn't see that coming!!
Jesse Ventura: Yeah! I mean, no.. Cheating's bad. Jim Ross: Brawler ascending that ladder. What is going through his head at this moment? He's playing up to the crowd... WAIT!! WAIT!! LOOK!!
Jesse Ventura: Norton's up!! He's laughing hysterically! Brawler looks down... Norton comes charging...
*SMASH!!!* J im Ross: OH MY GOOD GOD!!!!!!!!!!! Jesse Ventura: You can say that again!!!!! Jim Ross: Brawler was just sent off the ladder through two tables!!!!!! He's dead!!! He is dead, ladies and gentlemen, that despicable Scott Norton just killed the Brooklyn Brawler!!!!
Jesse Ventura: Finally, the paramedics are here. [Several paramedics rush to the scene, helping Brawler onto a stretcher. During this, Norton plays up the crowd as they boo intensely. He waves as if he telling them to "bring the heat" and they start throwing objects like beverages, food, toilet paper, even a beach ball! Norton laughs and walks off, shrugging away the middle fingers from the angry fans.]
Jim Ross: Well, I'm sure the fans didn't adore the Brawler but he sure as hell didn't deserve what he got tonight!!! I hope that Chad Costello suspends him.
That's uncalled for! Jesse Ventura: I doubt he will. Costello's probably laughing it up just like
Norton was. That's just the way the world works.
Jim Ross: Well, in any event, folks I hate to see someone get hurt like that tonight. Scott Norton should be fined, and suspended for what he did...I seriously doubt anything is going to happen though!
(Camera cuts to Ric Flair getting out of his limo and walking into a Old beat down gym. There's about 50 fans in there, and two unknowns are working a pathetic match....Flair walks right past security gets into the ring and throws both men out. He then rips his shirt off, and tells the commentator to come into the ring with a microphone...)
Ric: DO I LOOK LIKE A HAPPY MAN? (Crowd responds with a "WHOOOO") Ric: Shut up...I didn't come here to work the crowd, or this lousy gym. Why don't you punks pull out a couple extra bucks and see some real wrestlers down the street?
(Crowd starts to boo.) Ric: HUNTER!!!! HUNTER!!! BY GOD WHERE THE HELL IS HE? (Flair starts walking around the ring is a fit, looking in the crowd for him. He stomps up and down in rage and then gets back onto the mic...) Ric: What's the matter? Steph ain't here to stroke ya tonight? You need that extra bit of confidence from your whore of a wife to meet me face to face?
(Crowd Oooo's.) Ric: Come on....I know you're here. Come out here and face me...FACE WHOOOO The
Nature Boy!!
(Still nobody comes out...) Ric: Hunter I'm giving you to the count of three, and if you don't come out here I'll fly to your damn house and sit in your lawn and wait for your ass!!!
("Ready to play the game?" starts up and Ric Flair's eyes get huge as he waits for Hunter to make his return...all of a sudden out through the $10 curtain is
Stephine McMahon Helmsley. Flair can't believe it...she climbs into the ring and asks for a microphone...)
Steph: WHO DO YOU THINK YOU ARE? You're an old has been that needs to hang up the tights, and if there's one thing I hate more than anything in the world it's old men that need to hang it up.
Ric: Don't mess with me right now...where's your husband? Steph: He couldn't make it tonight. As a matter of fact he told me that he's not going to see you until the Pay Per View. You don't deserve to be in his presence. He's surpassed you, and he doesn't need to deal with your crap anymore.
Ric: CRAP!? CRAP!? You send a message to your husband...tell him that since he doesn't have any balls of his own...maybe he can borrow yours for the match at the Pay Per View, because you have to have pretty big ones to step into the ring with WHOOOOOO the Nature Boy!!
Steph: I'll tell him your as old and brittle as you look on TV. Ric: Steph you can tell him whatever you want, but you can also tell him this. Ric Flair has taken a lot of women on Space Mountain, WHOOOO....and I can honestly say that you will never be one of them. There's definitly a weight requirement!
(Steph slaps him, and Ric Flair grabs her by the hair...she starts to scream and out of the small crowd comes Hunter with a Sledge Hammer. He takes it and swings on the back of knee of Flair, and he falls to the ground. Hunter does it three more times and then walks away with Steph....we cut to a commercial.)
