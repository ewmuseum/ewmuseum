XCW Monday Night Malice (part 3)

December 23, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we comeback from the commercial break we see none other than Bill Goldberg standing in the ring. The crowd is giving him a HUGE face reaction and he's got an extremely tense look on his face.]
Goldberg: Brock...I know you're in the back. Get out here because you and I need to have a conversation.
[Brock's music starts to blare and he crowd stands up to give him a mixed reaction. He comes out bigger and badder than ever, and marches himself down to the ring. Brock climbs in and he and Goldberg go nose to nose. The crowd gets silent as both men stare each other down...Goldberg finally stops it by raising the mic to his mouth.]
Goldberg: I want to know what the hell you were thinking when you attacked me from behind the other night.
[Brock grabs the mic from him.] Brock: The question is...what the hell were you doing sticking your nose in my business? [Goldberg grabs the mic back from him, and a PA runs in and gives Brock his own.] Goldberg: I was here to sign up with the XCW, and it looked like you needed a little help. So I having respect for you and Taz came down to the ring and tried to help you out. Then you decided to take me out from behind...that's something only a (bleep) would do.
[The whole crowd "Oooooo's" at the same time. Brock looks like he's getting reder.] Brock: You want to talk about being a (bleep), then lets talk about how you don't show your face in pro-wrestling until the time is right for you. Not for anyone else but yourself. Now that I think...makes you a (bleep).
[Crowd "Oooooo's" once again.] Goldberg: Look I can see this is going to get us no where. We're either going to drop our microphones and settle this like men...
[Crowd pops.] Goldberg: Or...we can put aside our differences until DX is gone. [Crowd pops again.] Brock: Let me tell you something Bill...I hate you, but I hate DX more. Taz isn't going to be here tonight, so I'll take his place. I'll wrestle Eddie, and then go on to help you and Edge...
Goldberg: Smart man. Brock: But Goldberg...I would've been just as happy to take you out instead. So let than sit in your head for awhile. You (bleep)!
[Brock throws the microphone down and his music starts up. Bill Goldberg is left standing there as Brock leaves the ring area.]
Jim: Now that was a stand off...I think that was the biggest stand off in the history of the XCW.
Don: When these two men meet...ugh, I can't imagine what kind of match it's going to be. Jim: I have a feeling it'll turn into World War 3. Two big beasts wrestling... Don: It's your wet dream!! "New Era Has Arrived" is heard all over the arena, and then the music turns into "Underground" by Anti Flag. Mike Sanders comes down to the ring with his other three men and they climb in with a roar of boos. Mike grabs the microphone and begins to speak...]
Mike: Who are these people? Why did they attack our Flair? Are the Horsemen dead? These are all the questions I've been hearing throughout the week. These are all the questions that I have been thinking about as I bask in the glory that is New Era Of Wrestling...no we're not here to take over. No we're not here to rule the XCW, well in a way we are. We're here to take over, because this fed needs young blood running it...not some washed up old has-been that really never got over his own hype.
First of all let me introduce to you the members of New Era... Dave Bautista...a man who not only can destroy the Horsemen on his own, but his intesity rivals that of Goldberg, Brock Lesnar, Randy Savage...the man is a beast.
Sean O'Haire...a man who I like to think of as the new and improved Arn Anderson. He's an Enforcer in the ring, he knows how to get things done. You want someone to work on a bodypart and just rip the damn thing right of of it's socket...look no further.
Randy Orton...this man knows every wrestling move in the book. He's not only the hottest commodity in wrestling to date, but he's got the best damn smile too. I mean I don't just love him, the ladies adore him as well.
Then you have me... Mike S - ["2001" starts up and the fans rise to their feet. Ric Flair comes out to a huge ovation, and he just stands at the XCW ramp and smiles...]
Ric: Ya know something...if I had my boys with me right now, we'd storm that ring and make you eat your microphone. WHOOOO!!!
[Crowd WHOOOO's with him.] Ric: Mike Sanders you go on and on about how you're going to change things, how you're going to be the man...well you never did beat the man, so you sure as hell can't be one!
[Crowd responds with a huge pop.] Ric: So why don't we play a little game tomorrow night? Four of you against the four of us...in a War Games match. Only this time we make it elimination...
Mike: Hey that's fine Tit Flair, but your men are no where to be found. Ric: Heh...you aren't that bright are ya? [As Ric Flair asks that question we see Ole, Arn and Tully slide into the ring with chairs. All the members of the New Era turn around and all at the same time get smashed with them. Then fans go wild as the rookies hit the canvas hard. Mike Sanders is left in the ring, and Flair comes in. The crowd stands to their feet waiting, and Flair loosens his tie....]
Jim: Business is about to pick up here in Los Angeles...Mike Sanders should never try to trick a trickster.
Don: This isn't right...let the man go. Jim: Flair with a jab to the jaw, and another one. Mike backs into the corner, and Flair unloads with a series of chops to the chest. Listen to these people!!!
Don: And the rest of Sanders' men are down on the canvas...talk about now playing fair. Jim: The Horsemen are back, and they're stuck once again!!!! [All four men hold up the Horsemen sign and the crowd pops once again. The members of the New Era roll out of the ring holding their heads in pain and humiliation. We cut to a commercial for XCW Trading Cards...]
