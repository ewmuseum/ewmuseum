I've hated watching wrestling. JR is the kinda guy who can call a cow wrestling match and make it interesting...you, you on the other hand make me look forward to Joel Gertner...and that should say something.

Whipwreck, and Randy Savage.... Jesse: Is that all you're going to say? What about the torrid love affair that
Mikey and Liz have been having behind Macho's back? Aren't you going to mention that? What about mentioning the fact that Macho Man has been losing his mind recently, and that who knows what he's going to do when he gets Whipwreck into that ring.
Joey: Umm... Jesse: Do I have to do your job to tonight? I will you know! Joey: I know you will Jess...I know you will. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen WELCOME to Trust No One!!! This is the first match of the night...and is for the XCW Television Title. First coming down to the ring weighing in at 250 pounds. He is led down to the ring by Liz....here is
"The Macho Man" Randy Savage!!!
["Pomp and Circumstance" starts to play and Savage walks out to a huge heel response. Liz follows behind him, and he just waves the boo's into him. Whipwreck.]
FINK: And his opponent...he is the current XCW TV Champion, and a former XCW
World Champion. Here is Mikey Whipwreck!!!
["Loser" by Beck starts to play and as he walks out to the ring fireworks blast off behind him. It scares him a little, and he turns around to look at them...]
Joey: Savage is running down the asile and he meets Whipwreck with a clothesline. Wow he almost took his head off with that one.
Jesse: Well when ya got a women as pretty as Liz...you'll do anything you can to gain back that respect.
Joey: She is a very pretty lady. Jesse: Only in your dreams. [Savage grabs Whipwreck and throws him into the guardrail, and he dives onto him and lays in with lefts and rights. Savage brings him up and draps
Whipwreck's throat across the guardrail. Mikey falls down holding his throat.] Joey: And Liz almost looks like she's distressed. Jesse: No...she's just worried that the match isn't going to start. All she wants to see is Whipwreck get beat in the ring one, two, three.
Joey: Mikey is crawling on all fours to get to the ring, and Savage just stalks him from behind.
[Savage extends his foot right into Mikey's ass, and Mikey falls down. Randy brings him up and takes him by the hair back to the ring. Savage rolls Mikey into the ring, and he follows in. Mikey crawls quickly over to the turnbuckle to help himself up, and Savage turns him around.]
Joey: We knew Savage was going to be an animal, that is no suprise to us here tonight. We knew we wasn't going to let up, but did we know that he would have this much control from the very begining?
Jesse: Of course. He's Randy Savage! Joey: Savage drives those jabs into the face of Whipwreck, and he falls down to the turnbuckle once again. Whipwreck is seeing stars right about now...Oh and another huge jab. Randy now drives those elbows into the forehead, and the ref backs him out of the corner.
[Savage pushes the ref out of the way....] Jesse: Whoa, and that's something he doesn't want to do. Randy Savage doesn't want to mess with Tim White, because he'll DQ him without any question at all.
Joey: Randy whips Mikey into the other turnbuckle, and he hits chest first!!! Savage follows up with a high knee to the back, and Mikey hits the turnbuckle again. We see a shot of Liz on the outside, and it looks like she's worried.
Jesse: Well Savage did hit his knee pretty hard right there, maybe he should have her kiss it.
Joey: Mikey is a former XCW World Champion, Savage the current XCW Television
Champion. If Mikey wins tonight this will be only the 2nd title that he's held in the XCW...he went from rookie to World Champ in a night, and nobody will ever forget that day.
Jesse: What happened? Joey: Umm when Mikey brought us back to the US... Jesse: Oh yeah...I think I remember it. [Randy snapmares Mikey out of the turnbuckle, and locks in a chinlock. Mikey scrambled around to get to a rope, but Savage has it locked in pretty tight.
Randy now screaming and Mikey, and Mikey can't hear it.] Joey: He's trying to get all the air cut off...he's trying to cut his air off. Jesse: This could very well be the end of it. Savage has that locked in pretty tight, and it might even be a chokehold.
Joey: Tim White checking on that hold to see if it's a choke, and it is. He lays on the count....1....2....3....and Savage breaks it.
[Savage looks at the ref, and then applies a blatant chokehold.] Joey: Come on!! 1....2....3....4...and Savage breaks, and he goes for it again!! This time waiting right before the 5 count to stop.
Jesse: And Randy Savage has just had total control of Whipwreck. I don't think a guy like Whipwreck can be prepared for the Maddness of Savage.
Joey: A lot of people have felt that wrath, and tonight Mikey is. All because he had a little crush.
Jesse: A CRUSH? That's what you call a crush? No wonder you never date. Joey, that was an obession. Mikey was basically stalking the poor lady, and Randy is just trying to stick up for his girl. You know like that Peter Cetera song
"Glory Of Love", Savage is Liz's Knight in White Armor.
Joey: Savage is more like the guy in the shining. Jesse: And you're like white Urkel...so you shouldn't be talking. Joey: I'm glad you're working with me tonight. [Randy whips Mikey into the ropes, grabs him by the head as he's coming back and throws him to the outside. Mikey smacks the cement floor hard, and Randy goes to the outside. Randy grabs him and whips him into the guardrail. Savage sets Mikey up against it, and he climbs to the top turnbuckle....]
Joey: Savage from the top....FLYING DOUBLE AXHANDLE, but Mikey moves!!! Savage eats steel guardrail.
Jesse: He hit that hard too. Joey: Mikey is trying to collect himself, and Savage is too. Mikey is up first and he waits for Savage...Savage has his back turned, and Mikey with a HUGE bulldog onto the cement.
Jesse: And that'll give you a migrain. [Mikey keeps clearing the cob webs, as he brings Savage to his feet. Mikey drives an elbow into the side of the head, and another one. He scoops up Savage and slams his back into the ring post, and another slam into the ring post. He drops Savage back down, and slides into the ring.]
Joey: Randy is holding his back in pain, and Mikey is holding his head. Jesse: The ref is laying on the count...Liz runs over, and she's trying to help
Savage up. See!!! See!!! What did I tell you...what did I tell you about her liking Savage more than she does Mikey.
Joey: Well she did walk out with Randy. All I was saying is that she feels for
Mikey when he's getting beat up too.
Jesse: NO! Joey: Savage back up now, and he's holding his lower back. Mikey bounces off the ropes...he hits a baseball slide, and Savage goes crashing into the guardrail. Talk about being in control.
[Savage holds his jaw, and makes it back to his feet. He kicks the guardrail in anger, and then slides back into the ring. He charges at Mikey and goes for a clothesline, but Mikey ducks it. He spins Savage around, and goes for a suplex.
Savage puts on the breaks and turns it into a small package.] Joey: 1!!! 2!!! NO!!! Savage couldn't hold him, and Whipwreck kicks out. Both men back to their feet and Savage with a thumb to the eye.
Jesse: Smart thinking by Savage, you get that eye and dig your thumb into it as much as you can.
Joey: Randy with a nasty elbow to the forehead, and another elbow. He smashes
Mikey's head into the turnbuckle, and another turnbuckle smash. Back elbow to the side of Mikey's face!!!
[Mikey falls against the turnbuckle, and Randy digs his boot into his throat. The ref is counting, and Savage isn't listening. Randy finally lets go and brings Mikey out of the corner. He hits a jab, and another jab and then a snap mare. Savage straps a chinlock.]
Joey: Savage trying to wear down Mikey now. The ref better get in there and make sure that's not a choke.
Jesse: Now why would a guy like Savage choke a guy like Mikey? Joey: Well look at him, do you realize how much hate Savage has inside of him? Do you realize how much he wants to destroy Mikey Whipwreck? [The crowd starts to get behind him, and Mikey is starting to show some life. Mikey gets back to his feet, and Savage still has the chinlock on him. Mikey with an elbow to the gut, and another elbow to the gut...Savage grabs Mikey by the hair and takes him back down onto the canvas.]
Joey: Oh come on!!! That wasn't fair, that wasn't fair at all. Jesse: All is fair but love and war. Joey: Well this is a war...Mikey Whipwreck wants to prove himself to Liz. He wants to show her that he's all man, and that he can beat Randy Savage.
Jesse: Do you really think she's going to leave Randy if Mikey wins tonight? Joey: Yes....I think she digs Mikey, and I think that we're going to see Mikey step up to a whole new level with Liz on his side.
[The ref comes over and lifts Mikey's arm up....and it drops once, it drops twice....he lifts it up for the third time and it stays up. Savage lets go of the hold right away and drops a knee across the face. He hooks the leg....]
Joey: 1!!! 2!!! NO!!! Mikey kicks out again, and Savage slams his hands down in frustration.
Jesse: What Savage needs to do is hit a big move. In all my years in this business Savage has been known for hitting little moves, and then the elbow drop. He needs to hit a big move, because you're not going to take Mikey out with something small. Elbows, and bodyslams won't work on him.
[Savage brings Mikey up to his feet and takes him down with a backbreaker, but he keeps him there in the submission position. Mikey is crying out, and the ref runs over to check on him.]
Joey: Savage has Mikey bent over his knee, he's pushing on that chin and knee with all his strength.
Jesse: Great move right here, wear down his lower back. Joey: Look at Randy's eyes...this is not the look of a sane man. This is the look of a nutcase. He'd be happy right now if he bent Mikey into two pieces.
Jesse: Wouldn't that be a sight to see? [The crowd starts in with "Whipwreck Clap Clap Clap....Whipwreck Clap Clap
Clap", and he's starting to stir again. Before he can mount a comeback, Savage throws him to the floor. Randy looks to the crowd and is telling them to shut up, but they get even louder. Mikey makes it to his feet, holding the small of his back and charges Savage....Randy grabs him by the hair and tosses him to the outside with the momentum taking him over the top rope.]
Joey: And he lands right next to us.... Jesse: Savage leaps over the top rope...doesn't this man get tired? [Savage grabs Whipwreck and whips him into the edge of the commentators table, and Mikey drops to his knees. Randy grabs him again and whips him into the table once more. Randy throws Mikey onto the table, Jesse and Joey move out of the way.]
Jesse: This might be the big move I was talking about. Joey: Savage is climbing up to the turnbuckle. Oh no...he's going to do what I think he's going to do. He's goin -
[With that Randy "Macho Man" Savage leaps off the turnbuckle to the outside and he hits a HUGE Flying Elbow on Mikey Whipwreck. The table collapes into pieces,
Mikey is out...Savage is holding his arm, and everyone is on their feet.]
Joey: OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!!! Jesse: I can't believe it. That was the best flying elbow I've ever seen in my whole life, and he landed it right on Mikey's neck.
Joey: Both men are out, we don't have a table, and this is just the first match of the night.
Jesse: The ref has no clue what to do. [The ref comes out and he's checking on both men. Savage is moving, but you can tell he's in a serious amount of pain. Mikey Whipwreck on the other hand has yet to move. The ref starts talking to Savage, and Randy is responding. He walks over to Mikey who is out cold.]
Joey: Mikey Whipwreck has been knocked silly, and Savage may have seriously injured his elbow.
Jesse: And Liz is over to check on him. See she went to check on Savage, not
Whipwreck! Joey: She's looking over at Mikey though. She's with Savage, but looking at
Mikey. [Savage starts to move, and he gets to his knees. Mikey still isn't moving, and
Savage uses the ring apron to make it to his feet. He takes a huge breath, and holds his elbow in pain. Randy walks over to Mikey and brings him to his feet, and Mikey just slumps down again.]
Joey: Mikey is hurt, he's not getting up for the rest of the match. Jesse: Well that just makes it easier for Savage to win. Joey: Randy throws him back into the ring, and he's favoring that arm once again. [Savage tries to shake it off, and he climbs back up onto the top turnbuckle.] Jesse: He's going to finish it!!!! He's going to finish it!!! Joey: Randy Savage....from the top...FLYING ELBOW!!!!! [And it was like slow motion....Randy flies off the turnbuckle, and extends that bad elbow. He's just about to connect when....]

Savage in the stomach and hits the WHIPPERSNAPPER!]

"Loser" cuts off, and they're standing eye to eye. Liz smiles, and Whipwreck is confused...]
Joey: I WAS RIGHT!!!! Jesse: You've got to be kidding me. [Liz runs over and hugs the sweaty Whipwreck, and the crowd is going wild.] Joey: And little Mikey Whipwreck has a girlfriend! Jesse: I'm going to be sick, what a tramp! [Mikey goes to lift her like Savage did back at Wrestlemania IV, but he can't. So instead he just raises her arm....she pulls him towards her, and kisses him.] Joey: And that's got to be the lon - OH MY GOD!!!! Jesse: HAHAHA!!! [Liz just knee'd Whipwreck in the groin, and is biting his bottom lip as hard as she can. So hard that you can see the blood start to pour from Mikey's mouth. She finally lets go, and she slaps him across the face. Mikey falls to the floor, and Liz helps Savage up.]
Joey: Fans lets take you to comments from the one and only STING.... [Camera cuts back to the locker room, and Sting is standing there with Mene
Gene Okerland. The crowd pops like crazy as Sting stands in his wrestling gear for the first time in a long time...]
Mene Gene: Alright fans, standing next to me is none other than the Stinger!!!! Sting are you ready for your match against Scott Steiner? Sting: MY MAN GENE!!!!! Let me tell you something...Scott Steiner wanted the
Stinger, and look who is standing right here. Not Steve Borden, not Paul

Steiner.... [Steiner's music starts to play and he comes down to the ring wearing his chain mail head piece. He climbs in and flexes his biceps as fireworks shoot out the ring posts...he kisses his muscle, and then starts to stretch out.]
FINK: And his opponent...from Venice Beach, California. He weighs in at 256 pounds..here is "A Man Called" Sting!!!!!

Joey: And Sting is running down to the ring!! He's not going to waste anytime here tonight, and look him dart down there.
Jesse: Big mistake! [Sting slides into the ring and Steiner starts in with the forearms right away. He whips Sting into the ropes, and Sting ducks the clothesline. He bounces off and comes back with a huge leaping clothesline of his own, and both men go down.]
Joey: What impact by the Stinger. Jesse: Well when you mess with someone's family, when you mess with someone's little boy...there's a whole new level to your ability.
[Sting and Steiner both get up, and Steiner goes for a right hand. Sting blocks and comesback with a back fist, and another back fist. He drives a boot into the stomach, and sends Scott to the ropes. Scott puts on the breaks though, brings Sting towards him and hits a spinning belly-to-belly.]

[Quick shot of Stings family looking on.] Joey: There we can see his son, and his wife. They're looking on, and that poor boy has been the focus of Steiner. They hung him upside down from the second story of the mall, they even went as far as to make the kid pee his pants.

Scott lifts him up in a Gorilla press, and starts to bench him. Scott slams him down hard onto the canvas, and Sting holds his back.]
Joey: And you have to wonder how much ring rust Sting has. I mean the guy isn't even properly trained. He just came back a week or so, and they toss him into the ring with Scott.
Jesse: That will definitly be a factor in tonights match, and for the next few matches Sting wrestles.
Joey: The crowd is right behind Sting though, one of the biggest wrestling stars ever. [Scott grabs Sting again, and hits a forearm to the back. Sting falls to his knees, and Steiner gets a running start and drives a knee to the side of the head. Sting falls down to the canvas...]
Joey: He's just playing with him. Sting is just a toy to be played with right now, and where is Scott going?
Jesse: I think he's going to say hi to Sting's little boy. Joey: NO...don't do it. [Scott walks over and grabs the little boy by the arm. Scott points to the ring as to say "Look at your father, he's a loser"....he throws the boy back down, and he falls against the chairs...he starts to cry, and Steiner just laughs. Joey: What a brave move by Mrs. Borden.... Jesse: A dumb move...he's got her by the hair. Joey: STING WITH A TOPE!!!!!!!!!!! Jesse: Where did he come from? [Both men trying to recover from the move, and Sting gets up first. Steiner swings, and it's blocked by Sting...Sting grabs him and throws him into the ring steps. Joey: Sting checks on his family, and he see's that his kid is crying. Jesse: Steiner better watch it now. [Sting grabs Scott and whips him into the ring post, and Scott hits face first. Sting does it again, and another ring post smash. Steiner is wobbely, and Sting throws him back into the ring.]

Jesse: What's he going to.... Joey: OH MY GOD HE LAUNCHED HIM!!!! Belly to Belly Overhead Suplex, and Sting lands over by the opposite turnbuckle.

Sting and whips him into the turnbuckle again...Sting reverses, and Steiner hits hard. Sting runs in at him with the STINGER SPLASH, but Scott pulls the ref into him.]

Joey: Both men back up, and Sting catches the EMT's working on his son out of the corner of his eye. He's stopping right away, and he's heading to the outside.
[Steiner comes from behind him, and locks in a Full Nelson. Steiner hits a
Dragon Suplex, keeping the bridge.]
