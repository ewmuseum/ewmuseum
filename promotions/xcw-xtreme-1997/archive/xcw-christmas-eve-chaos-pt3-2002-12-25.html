XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 3)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Edge charges the ring, and both men are trading fists. Right cross and that takes Jericho down to the canvas, and almost out of his boots.
Don: Look at that fighting Canadian...he gets right back up. Jim: And he goes right back down with another right hand to the jaw. [Edge gets a running start and he spears him through the ropes and to the outside. Both men go down hard, but make a pretty quick recovery. Both men back to their feet and Edge grabs Jericho the face and throws him into the ring post.]
Jim: Jericho falls against the guardrail, and Edge charges. BACKDROP!!!! BACKDROP INTO THE CROWD!!! Don: Deja Vu? Jim: Edge gets up pretty quick, and Jericho slingshots himself over the guardrail and clotheslines Edge. Remember fans this is a falls count anywhere match, and I have a feeling this could go anywhere.
[Jericho grabs Edge and whips him into the guardrail, but Edge reverses. Jericho reverses once again, and Edge puts on the breaks. He catches the running Chris Jericho with a hip toss which sends him over the guardrail and back to the other side.]
Jim: Did you hear that impact? Don: Oh come on...Edge is lucky. The man has always been in the right place at the right time, and the fact is he's really nothing without his Canadian
Heritage. Aren't we all though? Jim: Please. [Edge rolls Jericho back into the ring, and he comes in as well. Jericho backs into the corner, and Edge charges in at him with a clothesline.]
Jim: Edge catches him walking out of the turnbuckle and nails a Cradle Suplex. Don: Cover... Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout there by Jericho, and as much as I want to see him lose that'll never put him away.
[Edge whips him into the ropes, reversal by Jericho. Edge ducks a short clothesline and hits a German Suplex.]
Jim: Nice counter there by Edge...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO...Jericho kicks out at two! Don: Damn that was a close one. Jim: Edge not wasting any time in trying to put him away. Edge brings Jericho to his feet...belly-to-belly suplex on Jericho, and Edge goes for the cover once again. ONE!!! TWO!!! Kick out....
[Edge brings him up and tries another go-behind...he hits a German Suplex, rolls him over and hits another German Suplex...he rolls him up again. This time he changes it and turns it into a Dragon Suplex.]
Jim: WHOA!!!! ONE!!! TWO!!!! NO!!!!! Don: And Jericho escapes at the last moment. Did Benoit enter Edge's body or something, because these are moves we have never really seen out of him.
Jim: Edge wants to win this match, and I don't blame him. [Edge lifts up Jericho for a vertical suplex...but Jericho falls back down and turns it into a small package.]
Jim: ONE!! TWO!!! Kickout by Edge. Both men up, and a right hand by Edge. Oh and you could here the fist connect to the face on that one.
Don: ALOHA!!! Jim: Edge sends Jericho to the ropes...he comes off, leapfrog. Jericho puts on the breaks and as Edge comes down...Chris kicks him between the legs.
Don: It's good!!!! Jim: Edge walks around, he's trying catch his breath. He walks right into a
Northern Lights Suplex bu Jericho...ONE!! TWO!!! Edge kicks out!!!
Don: That was a slow count. Jim: Jericho whips him into the ropes, Edge bounces off and Jericho hits him with a spinning heel kick.
[Jericho smiles and yells to the crowd. He brings Edge back to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle, Jericho charges and hits another spinning heel kick. This time it takes him out of the ring and onto his feet on the ring apron.]
Don: That's called the Canadian Jump. Jim: No it's not it's called luck. Jericho back into the ring now and hits a series of chops to the chest. He grabs Edge's leg and puts it behind the middle rope. Edge fights back though with forearm after forearm.
Don: Edge isn't dumb...he saw the last match. Jim: Edge pounces at Jericho with a series of rights. He's hammering away on
Jericho, and he's got him back against the ropes.
[Edge whips him to the ropes, but Jericho reverses. Jericho with a HOT SHOT against the ropes.]
Jim: Edge's throat has been draped across that ring rope, and he's in some pain right now. Don: Gee ya think? Jim: Jericho grabs Edge and throws him right through that middle rope. Edge scrambles back to his feet, and Jericho dives through the ropes with a clothesline.
Don: WHOO!! Touchdown baby!!! Jim: Jericho grabs Edge and whips him into the ring steps. Oh and he hits knees first, which makes him flip over the steps.
[Jericho grabs the dazed Edge and slams him head first into the guardrail. He then looks down and undoes the padding to reveal hard dirt and grass.]
Jim: Don't tell me he's going to do something on that. Don: Yeah!! It's about time. Jim: Jericho lifts him up...BRAINBUSTER ON THE HARD DIRT!!!!! Don: OH MY GOD!!!! Jim: Jericho just tried to make Edge's head apart of the Stadium. [Chris gets up and yells "WHOOO" and then throws Edge back into the ring. Jericho rolls in, gloats for a second and then drapes an arm across the chest of Edge.] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!! THR - NO!!! I can't believe that...Edge kicked out. I don't know how the hell he did it, but he kicked out at the last split second.
Don: Wow Edge has a lot of enudrance. I hope my wife isn't watching at hope. Jim: You have a wife? Don: Doesn't everyone? Jim: Edge on his back now - Don: You know Edge has been on his back longer than the two Hula girls were last night. Jim: Didn't you just say you have a wife? Don: Yeah...you really think she's watching this Pay Per View? She's out spending my money.
Jim: What if someone tells her? Don: About what? Jim: ARGH nevermind. Jericho with a jumping knee to the chest, and another one. [Chris whips him into the turnbuckle, and then sets him up on top.] Jim: What's he going for? [Edge's back is facing inside the ring, and Jericho grabs him by the back of the neck and hits a Super Neckbreaker. Edge falls to the canvas holding his neck in pain, and the fans around the ring don't seem to happy about that move either.]
Jim: Did you see that? Don: Will you stop asking me that? I'm right here, I saw everything. Jim: Jericho to the ropes...LIONSAULT!!!! He's calling for the Walls Of Jericho...and he locks it in. Edge is TAPPING!!!!! [The ref calls for the bell and Jericho raises his hands high into the air. Edge is holding that neck after the move Jericho executed on him, and the crowd erupts in a heel response as Chris leaves the ring area.]
Jim: Fans while you're at home you can go to XCW.com and check out the latest interviews, and the happenings of tonights event. Right now it looks as though
Crash Holly os backstage talking about his recent signing...
[Jericho is walking backstage to celebrate his win, and he sees Crash Holly talking on an XCW Webcast. Crash has this cardboard cut out of himself next to him, and he seems to be having a good time. Jericho pushes him out of the way and rips up his card board cut out...he begins to talk into the camera, and
Crash comes back with a forearm and both men start to brawl all over the back.
XCW officials rush to the site and break things up...]
