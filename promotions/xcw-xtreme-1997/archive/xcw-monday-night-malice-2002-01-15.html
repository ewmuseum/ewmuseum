XCW Monday Night Malice

January 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ric: Yeah...well I don't care if Costello doesn't want to see me. I'm coming back, and I'm going to make sure he reinstates Triple H. What? They're not letting me in? Well tell em' that the two founding members of the Four Horsemen are on their way, and there's no way they're going to stop the Nature Boy!!!
(Flair hangs up and the camera cuts...) [The camera cuts to a sold out crowd in the Pepsi Center, in Colorado. The crowd is chanting "USA, USA", and fireworks are blazing all over the arena. We pan across the fans to catch a few of signs..."This is MY sign", "Bring back
Goldust!", and "Vader who?"....are shown on camera. We then cut to Good Ol' JR sitting at the booth along with Jesse Ventura. Jim is wearing his usual, and
Jesse is wearing a tye-dye shirt, some jeans and a boa around his neck with a pair of pink sunglasses...]
Jim: What a night it's going to be. So many things lined up it's not even funny, Jesse. Jesse: Well first of all lets talk about that Battle Royal...how Mikey Whipwreck got passed everyone, and then eliminated Vader too...I don't get it. Jim: And how about Shane Douglas? Jesse: Shane Douglas is a man who can make things happen in the XCW. The man is set on winning that title, and nothing else. Is he going to win it? I don't have any idea, but I have a feeling that it's going to be a battle no matter what!
Jim: We just saw a few moments ago that Ric Flair will be here tonight, and I'm sure he's going to have a lot to say to President Costello.
Jesse: Ric Flair is not a man you want to cross. He will always win no matter how you try to get around him. He's the Nature Boy!!!
Jim: We've got some other great matches here tonight. Kurt Angle and Randy
Savage are both making returns to the XCW tonight, and what a match that will be. As a matter of fact lets take you to that match right now!!
FINK: Ladies and gentlement the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Pittsburgh, PA...he weighs in at 245 pounds. Here is
"Your Olympic Hero" Kurt Angle!!!
["Medal" starts to play and Kurt Angle comes out to fireworks, and a huge heel ovation. He gives the crowd a mean look, and climbs into the ring. Fireworks go off as he does his little "Olympic" skip and spin...]
FINK: And his opponent....from Sarasota, Florida. He weighs in at 250 pounds, and is led down to the ring by Miss Elizabeth. Here is "Macho Man" Randy
Savage!!! ["Pomp and Circumstance" is heard all over the arena and out walks Miss Elizabeth with a Pink Evening Gown, followed by Randy Savage who is wearing his usual wrestling gear. He's got on the Sun Glasses, and the sparkling robe, along with the "Macho" Bandanna". Savage climbs into the ring, points his finger in the air and spins around. Miss Elizabeth begins to disrobe him...]
Jim: This is going to be a great match tonight, and here's the bell. <Both men meet in the center of the ring, and there's a quick lock up.> Jim: Both men jocking for position, and nobody is moving. Jesse: Now Jimbo...who would've ever thought that we'd see Angle and Savage locking up against each other here tonight? I never would've imagined that in my wildest dreams..and tonight live on Malice...it's happening.
Jim: No doubt the XCW has some great talent, and they're going to show it off as much as they can. Tonight they're doing it with every single match...
<Angle quickly delivers a Firemans Carry, and Savage hits the mad hard. He gets right back up though and is a little suprised by it.>
Jim: Randy caught off guard a little there by Angle... Jesse: Well Randy has to be carefull tonight. Kurt Angle is one of the best wrestlers this sport has ever had, and Savage isn't going to be able to come in here and just drop an elbow across the throat. There's going to have to be a little more than that!
<Both men lock up again, and this time Savage hits an armdrag takedown. He keeps the armlock on, and whips his head back to get the hair out of his face.>
Jim: Quickness by Savage, and he's still got the speed like a cat. Randy Savage has the lock on, and Angle trying to make it to his feet.
Jesse: If Savage was smart he's frustrate Angle to the point where he's making mistakes. Angle is a talented wrestler Ross, but the bottom line is that he's still new. He's not used to the mind games...
Jim: Randy now lets go and Angle makes it to his feet, and Savage slaps him across the face!!!!! Angle charges, and Savage with a single leg takedown. What a move by Savage, and the place is going wild!!!
<Randy waits for Angle to get back up and he jabs him, and another jab. Savage follows up with an elbow the forehead, and Angle falls to the canvas.>
Jesse: Macho looks like his old self here tonight. Jim: He sure does....Randy Savage waits for for Angle to make it back up, and he whips him into the other turnbuckle.
<Angle bounces out and catches Savage in a spinning belly-to-belly suplex.> Jim: And Savage goes flying!!!! Jesse: Where did that move come from? Jim: Angle bounced right out of that turnbuckle, and he exploded with that belly to belly, and Savage is holding his back. That might have moved some discs, or injured those always bruised ribs of his.
Jesse: Can you believe how HOT Miss Elizabeth looks tonight? Jim: She does look very pretty. Jesse: It's a shame that Lex Luger gave some to her...she would be worth the trouble if he didn't touch her.
Jim: JESSE!!!! You can't say that!! Jesse: I just did. <Angle picks up Savage and drives a forearm into his back, and another forearm into the back of Savage. Kurt whips him into the ropes, and takes him down with a HUGE clothesline. Savage holds his neck in pain....>
Jim: That almost took his damn head off, and Kurt Angle wasn't a fan of Savage's earlier work obviously. Jesse: Can't say that I blame him... Jim: You didn't like Savage? Jesse: Well he was decent..he was no Jesse Ventura. Jim: Nobody ever is huh? Jesse: Well Gorilla Monsoon came pretty damn close. Jim: I'm sure he's rolling over in his grave now that you said that. Fans, Kurt
Angle looks like he's not going to let up. He's stomping away on the ribs of
Randy Savage, and that can't be a good thing.
Jesse: You know what I heard Jim? Jim: What? Jesse: Savage has "Pomp and Circumstance" play over the PA all the time because he never graduated. So this makes him feel like he did.
Jim: He did so graduate Jesse...don't start rumors like that. You'll get yourself in trouble.
Jesse: I'll just tell them you told me. <Kurt Angle brings Savage back up and executes a snap suplex, and then he follows up with a Northern Lights Suplex.>
Jim: He's pulling out all the suplxes he knows here tonight, and now he straps on a chinlock. Savage losing air....that move will cut off the oxygen to the brain, and knock you right out.
Jesse: And look at the concerned look on Mis Elizabeth. I bet the fans miss that look... Jim: She's a great woman..stop giving her a hard time. <The crowd is starting to get behind Savage, and he's using his right arm to get their support. Randy fights his way back up to his feet and nails Kurt with a jawbreaker. Kurt lets go fast...>
Jim: Whoa, and Kurt lets go of the hold quickly. Randy Savage back up to his feet and he nails Kurt with a back elbow, and that sends The Olympic Champion over the top ropes!!!
Jesse: And Savage is going out too!!! <Randy Savage grabs Kurt and places him across the guardrail.> Jim: Uh oh...I think I know what's coming!!! <Savage climbs to the top turnbuckle, and raises his hands high into the air. Savage takes off and hits Kurt with a double ax-handle across the back of the head.> Jim: This place is loving it, and that's a move from the Macho Handbook!!! Jesse: I didn't think he was going to make it, but he did. Kurt wasn't doing his homework, because if he did he would've known that Savage was going to do that move.
Jim: Savage now grabs Angle by the back of the head and throws him into the steel steps, and Savage follows up with boot after boot.
Jesse: Whoa, and Kurt hit his head hard on that. <Randy rolls into the ring to break the count, and then rolls right back out. Randy grabs Kurt by the head and whips him into the ring post, but Angle reverses the move.>
Jim: Kurt Angle reverses the whip into the turnbuckle, and Savage his his shoulder. Kurt charges at him and knocks him down with a clothesline to the back of the head, and Angle celebrates!!!
<Kurt lets out a huge "WHOOOO" and jaws with some of the crowd outside.> Jim: Kurt talks to the fans for a second before going after Savage again, and he goes to pull Randy up...but Savage with a jab to the stomach. Another jab to the stomach, and Savage is up now.
Jesse: Angle was wide open on those punches. Jim: Randy with a knee to the stomach, and he smashes Kurt's face into the ring steps...and he does it again. Randy throws Kurt back into the ring, and he goes up top once again.
<Angle makes it back up, and he's dazed. Savage comes off the top ropes and hits him with a Flying Double Ax-handle. Angle falls to the canvas like a giant redwood tree...>
Jim: Randy now brings Angle back up and hits him with a bodyslam. Savage follows up with a jumping knee to the face.
Jesse: That caught Angle right on the bridge of the nose, and he's holding it. That's going to make your eyes swell up, and you won't be able to see anything. Jim: Savage now puts on a chokehold....1,2,3...and he breaks it, and there he goes again. Randy Savage starting to get a little down and dirty here tonight, and that's what these people love to see.
<Savage goes to the top ropes again and waits for Kurt Angle to make it back up. Kurt is up and tries to wipe the cob webs off, and Savage comes flying off...Angle catches him though and executes a Belly-To-Belly Overhead Suplex.>
Jim: Angle throws Savage across the ring, and he's giving the sign for the
Olympic Slam!! Here is comes...NO Savage slides back down. Roll up...1!! 2!!
No...Angle reverses it, and he's got the pin...1!! 2!! NO....Savage kicks out. Both men up. <They run at each other and hit a double clothesline.> Jim: OH MAN!!! They almost took each others heads off tonight.... Jesse: Who's going to get up first? Is Savage going to get the win or will it be Kurt Angle?
Jim: What a match this has been here tonight. We're only on the first match, we have a whole night of action.
<Both men make it to their feet. Savage swings, Angle ducks and counters with his Olympic Slam!!!>
Jim: THERE IT IS!!!! He's going for the pin....1!!! 2!!! 3!!! FINK: Here is your winner....Kurt Angle!!!!! Jim: Kurt won the match here tonight, and what an amazing match it was. Both men had amazing debuts here tonight, and I'm looking forward to seeing what they each have in store in the XCW.
Jesse: Kurt ain't leavin'...he's telling Savage to get up, and he's still recovering from that slam.
Jim: Wait a minute...KURT LOCKS IN THE ANKLE LOCK!!!!! He's got the Ankle lock, and the crowd is upset about this.
Jesse: He's going to break Savages ankle.... Jim: Here comes the XCW officials, and they're telling Angle to let go. Kurt finally lets go...
<Kurt smiles and does his infamous "WHOOOO".> Jim: That's not fair...what a poor sport Kurt Angle is. They had a great match, and he had to add insult to injury. I'm sure Savage will want to say something about this!!! (All of a sudden the camera cuts to Ric Flair walking into the arena with his whole group of people.)
(All of a sudden Mikey Whipwreck steps into the picture, and Ric seems happy to see him.) Ric: Mikey...how's it going? Mikey: Good. I have a big match tonight, and all three guys don't like you. Ric: I knew that was going to be a problem for you...I hired a friend of mine to watch your back. So if you get into any trouble, don't worry because he'll be watching your back. I'm going to go deal with Costello right now.
Mikey: WHOOOO!! Ric: That was a good one!! (Mikey and Flair go their own way, and the camera cuts to a commercial.)
