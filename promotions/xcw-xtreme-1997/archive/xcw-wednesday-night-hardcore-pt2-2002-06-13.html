XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 2)

June 13, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When we comeback we see Al Snow and Chris Jericho smiling in their dressing room. Both men are content with the damage they causes...all of a sudden Sabu kicks open the door and shoots a fireball into the face of Jericho. Van Dam is in and he smashes a chair into the face of Snow. Both Sabu and RVD now start to stomp away on the chest of Al, and you can hear Bill's whistle in the background....back to the ring.]
FINK: The following match is set for one fall...introducing first, here is
Scott Steiner!! [Scott walks down to the ring, and the crowd gives him a ton of heel heat.] FINK: And his opponent...he weighs in at 267 pounds. Here is "The X-Treme
ICON", here is The Sandman!
[Sandman comes in through the crowd smoking, and drinking...] [Sandman rolls into the ring and meets Scott with a series of fists to the face. Scott returns with some crushing blows, and he sends Sandman to the ropes. He catches him in a overhead belly-to-belly suplex, and he crashes down to the canvas.]
Jim: Who can't Scott throw around like a rag doll? Joel: Me. Jim: He probably can throw your fat ass around the ring too. Joel: I guess. Jim: Scott brings the dazed Sandman to his feet, and he hits a lightning fast snap suplex. Scott goes for the cover, 1!! 2!! Sandman kicks out quickly...
[Scott grabs Sandman by the neck and executes a snap mare. Scott stands over
Sandman and drives a series of forearms to the chest each one looking like can kill. Scott brings him back up and Sandman hits a low blow.]
Jim: That's one way to stop Scott Steiner. Joel: Yeah, but would you want to try it? Jim: No. Sandman goes behind him and executes a back suplex. Sandman now perches himself up onto the second turnbuckle, and comes crashing down with a double ax-handle.
Joel: And Scott isn't down. [Sandman runs to the ropes and hits a poor high crossbody. Scott catches him and sets him down, Sandman kicks Scott between the legs and just smiles as he doubles over.]
Joel: Why is he trying to ruin Scott's baby maker? Jim: Why was Scott trying to ruin Armstrongs...er...Arm? Joel: Bah...he can do whatever he want. Sandman can't! [Sandman with a left hand, and a right hand, and another left. He backs Steiner into the turnbuckle, and drives a knee into the stomach. Another knee into the stomach, and Sandman headbutts him...this makes Sandman a little dizzy.]
Jim: OH!!!! Steiner explodes out of that turnbuckle, and knocks Sandmans head right off. Joel: How do ya like that? Jim: Scott brings him back to his feet....he drives a forearm into the side of the head, and another forearm. He lifts Sandman up....PILEPLEX!!!!!!!
Joel: WOW. Jim: There's the cover...1!!! 2!!! NO!!! He didn't get it. [Scott brings Sandman to his feet and hits a Dragon Release Suplex. Sandmand sits up after that and then goes back down. He grabs Sandman again, and locks him in the Steiner-Recliner.]
Jim: He's got it locked in, and is Sandman going to tap? He does!! Sandman taps out... Joel: Too much for him. FINK: Here is your winner...Scott Steiner. Jim: And Steiner isn't letting go. Joel: Stretch him! Jim: The bell is going off, and Steiner finally lets go. [Steiner raises his hand in victory, and the ref pushes his arm down. He walks over to Sandman and raises his hand.]
FINK: Your winner by a reverse desecion....The Sandman!!!! [Sandman is up now and he spins Steiner around and takes him down with a Singapore Cane shot. Everyone pops in approval, and he leaves the ring.] Jim: Sandman clocked Steiner, and Steiner can't believe it. Joel: That would've knocked out a normal man! Jim: And Steiner is not normal. [We cut backstage to Magnum TA who is whistling and minding his own business.] ????: Psssst... Magnum TA: Huh? ????: Pssst..... Magnum TA: Hey who is that? ???: Come over here dummy. Magnum TA: Hey...Shane Douglas, you're not supposed to be here. Shane: Really? Maybe you should get a fucking candy bar. Listen, I need to talk to you. I'm not allowed to be in the arena, but I need some help. I want you to help me get back in the XCW.
Magnum TA: Gee...I dunno man. Shane: Listen I just want to help Arn out. Make me the Special Guest ref in his match on Sunday.
Magnum TA: Why? Shane: So I can help him win! Magnum TA: Oh...well in that case sure. I'll fill the paper work out later today. Shane: Don't tell Arn...I want it to be a suprise. Magnum TA: Heh...it'll be our little secret. Shane: Thanks!!! HAHAHA!!! [Camera cuts to a commercial.]
