XCW Beach Blast '02 (part 5)

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VO: Over the past few weeks we have seen friends develop, and hatered begin. We've seen friends untied, and old foes become bitter rivals. Rivals like Tommy
Dreamer and Raven...the history that these two men have compares to nothing in the XCW. The battles that they had in the ECW were epic, the battles they've had in X-Treme are priceless...but what happens when you throw...
Stevie Richards into the mix? You get a three way fued that's about as wreckless as one can imagine. With
Stevie unsucessfully trying to play both sides of the fence, and Raven attacking Dreamer every chance he gets....this is bound to be an explosion. Put the US Title up for grabs, and this goes from the Gulf War...to the battle of
Gettysburg. Tonight these three men will dance, but the question is...who will lead?
[Camera cuts to a screen that is split up by Tommy Dreamer, Stevie Richards, and Raven...all of a sudden a HUGE question mark "?" stamps the middle of the screen and we fade back to Safeco Field.]
Jim: Well fans this match has hit it's boiling point. All three men have had enough of each other, and tonight it ends with the vacant United States title.
Stevie Richards, Raven, and Tommy Dreamer...this should be about as brutal as it gets. Jesse: Ross, it's still etched in my mind. The dive that Tommy and Raven took off of that huge semi, and the fact that they went on to wrestle an incredible match. I can't imagine what this is going to be like.
Jim: There's no need to wait anymore. Lets take you down for the intros... FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this match is a 3-Way Dance for the XCW United
States Title. First coming down to he ring, he hails from Yonkers, New York.
Here is Tommy Dreamer!!! ["Man In A Box" starts to play and Tommy walks out with an XCW T-shirt on. He works the crowd all the way down to the ring, and then climbs in.]
FINK: His opponent...from PA. He weighs in at 230 pounds. Here is "Dancin" Stevie Richards!!! ["Dancing Queen" by ABBA starts to play and he comes out to tremendous heel heat. He stays on the outside of the ring...]
FINK: And their opponent....he weighs in at 238 pounds. He's from Short Hills,
NJ here is Raven!!!
["Keep Em' Seperated" starts to play and the crowd gives him tremendous heel heat. Everyone is tossing things at him as he makes his way down to the ring.
Raven seems to feed on this misery though, and just climbs into the ring...] Jim: There's the bell and it looks like Stevie and Raven are going right after
Tommy. Jesse: And they need to get the resilient one out first. [Richards and Raven drive fists into the head of Dreamer, and they double whip him into the ropes. Double Clothesline, and they follow up with a double elbow drop.]
Jim: Teamwork on the part of Richards and Raven, and I find that very odd. Jesse: WHY? They have to get rid of Tommy Dreamer, and if order to do that they have to work together. Think about it.
Jim: Yeah, but you know as well as I do that Raven is going to turn around and attack Stevie in a matter of minutes.
[They bring Dreamer up to his feet...Stevie sets up in the corner, and Raven whips him into another corner where Tommy hits hard. Raven now walks over to
Stevie and whips him into Tommy...and Stevie with a huge splash.]
Jim: Stevie moves, and Raven follows in with a HUGE clothesline! Unfortunately this is totally legal, and there's not a damn thing that Tim White can do about it. Tommy stumbles out of the corner, and they plant a double kick to the stomach.
Jesse: WOW and a double snap suplex. I'm liking this duo more and more as the match goes on.
Jim: Raven makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Stevie rolls Raven off, and now he's making the cover!! ONE!!! TWO!!!!
Jesse: And Raven returns the favor, and here we go. Jim: Stevie and Raven in each others faces. Whoever gets the pin becomes the US
Champion, and so it's ever man for himself. Stevie is saying something, and
Raven is spouting right back.
Jesse: Hit him already!!! [Tommy Dreamer gets back to his feet and both men hit him with a punch to the head at the same time.]
Jim: AWE!!! You've got to be kidding me!!! Jesse: It looks like they're going to focus on Dreamer instead now. Jim: Raven brings Tommy back to his feet, and he hits a back suplex. At the same time Stevie hits a neckbreaker!!
Jesse: They're doing a number on Tommy tonight!! [Raven brings Tommy back to his feet and whips him into Stevie who hits him with a Steviekick! Raven grabs Tommy and throws him into the turnbuckle, and now he unloads with kicks and punches.]
Jim: Tommy needs to start mounting some offense or he's going to be in some serious trouble. Stevie just watching, and now what is he doing?
Jesse: Stevie goes behind Raven...SCHOOLBOY!!! What a smart move!!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!! Stevie almost had Raven right there, and we almost had a US Champion.
Jesse: I'm shocked that Stevie actually did something that requires the use of his brain. It's every man for himself, and he wants to make sure everyone knows that.
[Raven and Stevie back to their feet and Raven doesn't waste anytime with talking. Raven with a boot to the stomach, and Stevie comesback with a punch to the jaw.]
Jim: Who's going to get the upperhand? Raven whips Stevie in the ropes, and he goes for a flying forearm. Raven ducks out of the way and Stevie ends up hitting Tommy Dreamer!!!!
Jesse: He was just getting out of the corner too. Jim: And Dreamer falls to the outside of the ring. [Raven gets up and walks right into a Steviekick....] Jim: And Raven with the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Raven lifts his shoulder just in time. Jesse: He hit that one right under the jaw, and Raven is still feeling the effects of that move.
Jim: Raven back to his feet now and he swings and misses Stevie by a mile. Stevie with a boot to the midsection, and hits a high knee. Raven goes down, and now we get Stevie dancing.
Jesse: Ugh...someone please knock him out. Jim: Stevie now climbs to the top turnbuckle, and he waits for Raven to get back to his feet. Flying Clothesline....but Raven moves out of the way. Stevie hits nothing but canvas.
Jesse: And Raven dives over and makes the cover!!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! Dreamer reaches in and pulls Raven to the outside, and now
Dreamer rolls in and makes the cover!! ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!!! Stevie kicks out, and both men make it back to their feet.
[It looks like Raven has twisted his knee on the outside, and he's trying to walk it off. Stevie goes for a clothesline, and Dreamer ducks it. Tommy lifts him up and hits a backbreaker...]
Jim: Raven on the outside, and he's limping around. He needs to get into the ring before Tommy ends up pinning Stevie.
Jesse: Or vice-versa. Jim: Dreamer whips Stevie into the turnbuckle. Dreamer slowly walks over and unloads with punch after punch. Dreamer sets Stevie up onto the top turnbuckle, and what's he going to do?
Jesse: Super Snapmare!!!!! Jim: Wow that was an interesting move right there by Dreamer. [Raven slides back into the ring, and Dreamer hits him with a clothesline. Tommy brings up Raven and headbutts him, and tosses him into the ropes. Dreamer with Spinning Spinebuster, and he goes for the cover.]
Jim: Same move he used to beat him before!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! Jesse: And Stevie breaks it up!!! Jim: And Stevie has a chair in his hand. [He's telling Tommy to bring Raven up, and Dreamer is pissed he broke up the count. Tommy hits him in the face, and Stevie drops the chair. Dreamer drives a fist into the head, and another fist into the head....]
Jim: Tommy sends Stevie to the ropes, and OH!!!! Raven plants Stevie in the head with the chair, and he staggers back.
Jesse: Jawbreaker by Dreamer.... Jim: WOW and Stevie is in bad shape right now! Jesse: No kidding. [Dreamer gets back to his feet and Raven swings the chair, and Dreamer ducks. Tommy instead dropkicks the chair, and it hits Raven right in the face. Tommy rolls out of the ring and lifts up the ring apron.]
Jim: And Tommy looking for some sort of object...he's looking fo...oh, he's got a chain! Jeese: Oh this can't be good. [Tommy wraps the chain around his hand, and waits for Raven to get up. Tommy winds back and drives his fist into his forehead, and Raven goes down hard.
Stevie is up and spins Dreamer around only to catch a face full of chain.] Jim: Wow and Tommy Dreamer isn't about to cut any slack to either man. He drops the chain, and grabs Stevie by the hair...he tosses him to the outside.
Jesse: Now it's just he and Raven. Jim: And this place is about to come unglued. [Raven is busted wide open, and Dreamer runs over and forearms him in the jaw. Another forearm to the jaw, and he backs him into the corner. Tommy drives a series of shoulders into the stomach, and then follows up with kick after kick to the gut.]
Jim: Tommy whips Raven into the ropes, and he back body drops him over the ropes. Jesse: And talk about a hard spill onto the cement down below. Jim: We've really seen Tommy come alive here tonight. [Stevie Richards sneaks in from behind, and taps Dreamer on the shoulder...] Jim: STEVIE KICK!!!!! NO!!!! Dreamer ducks it, and hits him with an uppercut to the jaw. Jesse: He's on his game tonight. Jim: Dreamer brings Stevie up...he's going for a vertical suplex. Here comes
Raven from behind, and he clips the leg of Tommy. Stevie falls on top, and
Raven kicks him off quickly.
Jesse: Smart move there by Raven now. [Raven lays out the chains and brings Dreamer to his feet. He double under hooks the arms and hits the IMPALER onto the pile of chains. Tommy rolls off of them holding his forehead, and Stevie spins Raven around jumps up and hits a tornado DDT onto the same pile.]
Jim: WOW, and Stevie has a great chance to win the title right here. He hooks
Raven's leg....ONE!!! TWO!!! TH- NO!!! Raven got his shoulder up just in the nick of time.
Jesse: And Dreamer is busted open now. Jim: There you can see the puddle of blood that is being made next to Dreamer's face, but he's not down and out yet. He has a long way to go tonight.
[Stevie brings Raven up and plants a foot to the stomach, and an Irish Whip into the turnbuckle. Raven hits the turnbuckle so hard that he falls to his knees, and Stevie walks over and just starts slapping Raven in the face.]
Jim: Whoa..and Stevie doesn't know who he's messing with. Jesse: Stevie is just slapping Raven around, and that's humiliation if I've ever seen it. Especially from a guy like Stevie Richards.
Jim: Tommy is up now, and he's walking over to Stevie. Stevie is having a good ol' time slapping the hell out of Raven...and Dreamer lifts Richards up from behind. HUGE Back suplex!!!
[Both men make it back to their feet and Stevie walks right into a Pinning
Chokeslam...] Jim: Great move by Tommy Dreamer!!! There's the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! Raven breaks it up. Jesse: I don't know how this match is going to come to an end. All three of these men are dying, clawing, doing everything they possibly can.
[Dreamer barely makes it up to his feet before Raven can knock him down with a bulldog. Raven now climbs to the top turnbuckle, and flies off the top...he hits a flying kneedrop to the chest...and Tommy writhes around in pain.]
Jim: Raven goes for the cover....ONE!!! TWO!!! TH-NO!!! Jesse: WOW...that was close. Jim: And now Raven is going BACK up top!!! [Raven flies off the turnbuckle again, targeting Dreamer....but out of no where
Stevie hits the STEVIE KICK on Raven, on the way down. Raven snaps back, and
Stevie holds his knee in pain.]
Jim: My God, Raven's neck snapped back like a toy. He's out cold, and Stevie has jammed his knee.
Jesse: Well it was a great move, but it was a dumb move at the same time. Ya know Stevie doesn't think.
Jim: Tommy Dreamer gasping for air, and the blood comes out of his head like water and he gets back up. Nothing is going to keep him down tonight....
[Dreamer walks over to Stevie, and starts to kick away on the leg. Stevie screams out in pain, and Dreamer continues the assult. Dreamer "helps" Stevie get back to his feet, and then drives a fist into the head. Dreamer grabs the chain, and winds back...and whips him.]
Jesse: It's like a scene from Roots!!! Jim: Come on Jesse!! Stevie is being whipped with that chain, and you can see the welts appear automatically!! That has got to take any kind of will right out of your body...
Jesse: Ya think? Jim: Why are you getting smart all of a sudden? Jesse: Cause I can! [Dreamer wraps the chain around Stevies neck, and then tosses him over the top rope...and Stevie is hanging!!]
Jim: This is sick!!! Stevie Richards is hanging...he's hanging there, and his face is turning red!!!
Jesse: And Raven still hasn't gotten up. Jim: The ref is laying on the count, and Dreamer simply walks away. Stevie hits his backside onto the apron, and then falls foward to the floor.
[Dreamer turns his attention to Raven who is starting to move his body. Raven slowly makes it to his feet, and Tommy kicks him in the stomach. He lifts him up and hits an inverted brain buster..."Wet Dreamer".]
Jim: And he hits the "Wet Dreamer"....Tommy musters up all that strength to make the cover, he hooks the leg.
Jesse: Is this it? Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!! Jesse: I can't believe it!!! Jim: Raven has kicked out. Tommy with a face full of blood looks at the ref in disbelief, and now he's bringing Raven back up.
[Tommy goes for a boot to the stomach, but Raven moves out of the way. Raven with a back elbow to the side of the head, and a quick EVENFLOW.]
Jim: HE HITS THE EVENFLOW!!! Jesse: ON THE CHAINS!!!!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Raven won the match!!! FINK: Here is your winner and NEW UNITES STATES CHAMPION, Raven!!!!!! [Raven staggers to his feet, and grabs the belt. He raises his hands in the air, and then slumps against the ropes. The crowd is giving a HUGE heel reaction to him, and he doesn't care at all.]
Jim: Raven has done it, but I think the book hasn't been closed on this fued. We're going to see a lot more from Stevie Richards, and Tommy Dreamer. Jesse: Well Stevie is still out on the floor. Jim: Yeah he's seriously hurt. We've got the EMT's out clearing things up.
