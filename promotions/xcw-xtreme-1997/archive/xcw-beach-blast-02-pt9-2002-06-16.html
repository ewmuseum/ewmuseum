XCW Beach Blast '02 (part 9)

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: There you have it...the Rock is back in the XCW, and he's not going to be taking anything from anyone.
Jesse: I like the Rock...he reminds me of myself. Jim: Except he was in good movies Jess. Jesse: Wait a minute...I was in Predator, I was on Running Man...I was in No
Holds Bar - scratch that last one!
Jim: Folks..the next match is going to be one for the history books. Ric Flair and Arn Anderson have not gotten along for the past month or so. These two men hate each other, and tonight it all comes to a stop.
Jesse: Scott Hall and Ric Flair are teaming up, and Shane Douglas is the special guest ref. Now that will be interesting.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this is a special tag team match. First let me introduce to you the Special Guest ref for tonights match. Here is "The
Franchise" Shane Douglas!!!! ["Perfect Strangers" begins to play over the PA, and Douglas walks out in a ref shirt. He's getting a lot of heat from the crowd, but he loves that kind of thing. So he's just soaking it up.]
FINK: Now coming down to the ring...at a total combined weight of 515 pounds. Here is the team of Scott Hall, and Ric Flair!!!!! ["2001" starts to play, and the fans all get up to boo them out of the building. Scott Hall walks out first, and it looks like something is bothering him. That something walks out shortly after him, and he's wearing his fancy
"Nature Boy" robe. He "WHOOO's" the crowd all the way down to the ring.]
FINK: And their opponents....hailing from MINNESOTA. Here is the team of Arn
Anderson.....................
.......and OLE Anderson...The MINNESOTA WRECKING CREW!!!!! [With that everyone stands up and Ole and Arn walk out to the most electrifying ovation of the night. Even the sound system is having a hard time picking up the cheers for these two. Air horns are going off, people are screaming, waving posters and signs. Ole and Arn stand there for a minute and work the crowd...and begin to make their way down to the ring.]
Jim: OH MY GOD!!! OLE ANDERSON!!!! ARN ANDERSON!!! Jesse: It's the Minnesota Wrecking Crew....reunited!!! Jim: The crowd is on their feet, and Arn and Ole just smile. Flair can't believe his eyes, and nither can Scott Hall.
[Both men climb into the ring, and work the crowd into a frenzy.] Jim: Ole Anderson looks to be in great shape, and so does Arn. I can't believe we're seeing this. He said he had a suprise, but I didn't know it was going to be the return of the Minnesota Wrecking Crew!!!
Jesse: These two men are awesome. [It looks like Arn is going to start off against Scott Hall. Both men circle each other, and lock in a collar and elbow tie-up. Hall quickly with a knee to the stomach, and a fist to the side of the face. He whips Arn into the ropes, and Arn ducks a clothesline.]
Jim: Hall misses, and Arn spins him around and forearms him on the side of the face. Arn whips Hall into the ropes this time, and he hits a clothesline!
Jesse: Scott Hall is looking a little lugish tonight. Jim: When was the last time you saw him? Jesse: '97? Jim: That is when he was sober...ever since then he's been a slobering drunk. [Arn brings Hall back up and drags him over to his corner. Ole extends his foot, and Arn slams Hall's head into it. Arn tags out and Ole comes in to a huge response. Both men start kicking away on the stomach, and unloading with lefts and rights.]
Jim: Can you hear these people? This is amazing!! Jesse: Ole and Arn are a tag team that has always been thought of as amazing. They haven't missed a beat! [Ole drives a shoulder into the stomach of Hall, and another huge shoulder. Ole with a fist to the side of the face, and another fist. He tags out to Arn once again, and the place erupts.]
Jesse: Quick tags Jimbo...that's the way to get things done around here. Jim: A lot of teams now days use the amazing moves to get them out of trouble, and the Wrecking Crew is using an old school technique.
Jesse: That's why tag teams back in the day held onto those titles forever. Remember Demolition? Now that was a tag team that brutalized their opponents. [Arn whips Hall into the ropes, and hits a Stun Gun. Hall rolls around clutching his throat, and Arn just smiles. He looks over at Flair, and the
Nature Boy has a dark red face...]
Jim: And I think Flair wants in... Jesse: And I think Arn is going to let him come in. Jim: OH Arn throws Hall into his corner by the hair, and Flair makes the tag. [Everyone gets on their feet...] Jim: This has been a lifetime for them to climb into the ring, and Shane Douglas is going to let them go at it. Jesse: Douglas hasn't been the factor I thought he was going to be. Jim: He wants them to wrestle, he wants them to brawl. Ric and Arn meet in the center of the ring, and the fans are about to explode.
Jesse: This is louder than when the Mariners won their game to get to the playoffs. Jim: Flair is saying something, and I wish we could hear it. [Both men are jawing back and forth and Flair slaps Arn across the face, and
Arn comesback with a slap of his own. Flair hits a nasty chop to the chest, and another one...Arn comesback with a series of fists to the head.]
Jim: This place is on their feet here tonight!! Jesse: I love seeing old school matches like this. Arn Anderson, and Ric Flair wrestling like they should've so many years ago.
Jim: Arn whips him into the ropes and plants a boot into the stomach, and he tags Ole!!! [Arn holds Flair's arm, and Ole plants a fist into the shoulder. Ole whips
Flair into the ropes, and hits a HUGE Sidewalk Slam. The crowd pops huge for that, and Ole eats it right up.]
Jim: There's the cover...1!!! 2!!! Hall comes in and makes the save with a boot to the face.
Jesse: Smart thinking by Scott Hall. If they lose this match here tonight they will have no credability in the XCW.
Jim: You're right about that one! [Ole makes it up and brings Flair back up, and he hits a stiff backdrop suplex. Ole gets right up and makes the tag to Arn. Both men send Flair into the ropes and they hit a double Flapjack, and the place rises to their feet once again]
Jim: And every single time these two hit a double team move the crowd is on their feet. Jesse: Well this is history right here. They know, you know, I know, and the people watching on Pay Per View know it.
Jim: Arn now with Flair...and hits a snapmare. Arn with a huge kick to the small of the back, and another one.
[Flair screams out in pain, and holds his back. Arn whips Flair into the turnbuckle, and he follows in with a clotesline. Arn backs up, charges and hits another clothesline...and another clothesline. Arn charges one more time, and
Flair drops to his knees hitting a low blow.]
Jesse: And that's going to get you every single time. Jim: Flair's not called the "Dirtiest player in the game" for nothing. Jesse: And if he hopes to win this match at all, he'd better make the tag to
Scott right now. If Ole tags in..then it's over.
[Flair barely makes it up, and he kicks Arn between the legs. The ref talks to him about it, and Flair brushes him off. Ric walks over to Hall, and makes the tag. Flair falls against the turnbuckle, and Scott Hall comes in cocky as ever.]
Jim: Hall is slowly headed over to Arn, and he slaps him in the back of the head. Don't disrespect our commish like that!
Jesse: Scott Hall needs to focus on winning the match, not making a name for himself tonight.
Jim: He sends Arn into the ropes, and Arn ducks a clothesline. He comes back with a clothesline of his, but Hall ducks it too. Hall with a stright right to the side of the face, and another one sends Arn down to the canvas!
Jesse: And he's going for the cover!!! Jim: 1!!! 2!!! NO!!! Arn gets his shoulder up just before the 3 count, and that was close. [Arn crawls back to his feet and Hall locks in a Full Nelson.] Jim: Full Nelson, and if Hall keeps this locked in this could be it. Does he have those fingers locked in behind Arn Anderson?
Jesse: It looks like he does. This looks like a lock, and Hall is a taller man so it's easy for him to add pressure to that neck.
Jim: Arn trying to get all the air he can in, but Hall is putting a stop to it. He's wearing Arn down...and Anderson goes down to one knee. Jesse: This could be it. Jim: The ref is checking on Arn's arm....it drops once....it drops a second time... Jesse:............... Jim:...................... Jesse:................... Jim:....................it drops!!!! Jesse:............... Jim: NO!!! It stays up!! Arn kept his arm up, and he's trying to get back to his feet. He's trying to make it back up so he can break the hold, and he's up!!! Arn Anderson is back to his feet and he's trying to his all his strength to break the hold!
Jesse: And Hall can't believe his eyes. Jim: I don't think Hall has looked this suprised since his wife left him. [Hall quickly releases the hold and pushes Arn chest first into his turnbuckle. Hall distracts the ref, and Flair chokes him out with the ring rope. All the fans in Safeco are going booing, but the ref doesn't even notice. Hall walks over and Flair stops.]
Jesse: Nice cheating there going on by Ric Flair. Use the ring rope to choke the life out of your opponent. What a great move!
Jim: And Hall drives those back elbows into the jaw of Arn...and he drives a knee into the stomach.
[Hall with a short whip, and brings Anderson right into another knee. Anderson tumbles over onto the mat holding his stomach. Flair reaches out and yells
"WHOOO"...Hall makes the tag.]
Jim: And now that Arn Anderson is down, Ric Flair wants in. Flair with a series of boots to the side of the face, and he's down on the mat now driving those fists into his face.
Jesse: He wants to bust him open. Jim: And I think he's going a good job. He's opened up a spot on Arn's head, and there's blood gushing out. Flair has a look of anger on his face, and he's not stopping. He's continuing to drive those fists, and the ref is pulling
Flair off.
Jesse: OH AND FLAIR PUSHED THE REF!!! Jim: And the ref pushes him right back! Jesse: Flair brings Arn to his feet and backs him against the ropes. Wow look at the mark on Arn's head...that's nasty.
[Ric hits him with chop after chop. Arn clutches his chest in pain, and Flair whips him into the ropes. Ric goes for a backdrop, but Arn connects with a faceslam instead.]
Jim: The mat is covered in blood from where Arn is lying....Flair is using the ropes to get back to his feet. Will Arn be able to make the tag to Ole?
Jesse: Flair is up, and he's walking over...he tags to Hall! Jim: And Arn tags to OLE!!!!! [As soon as Ole gets into the ring Hall gets on his knees and begs him. Ole looks at the crowd, and kicks Hall in the gut. Ole with a series of fists to the head, and brings him back to his feet. He hits Hall with a lariat...]
Jim: Flair comes in and he gets backdroped for his troubles!!! Jesse: I have never heard this place be so loud!!! Jim: Ole with a double clothesline, and both men stagger up...Ole with yet another double clotesline.
[Arn comes in and grabs Flair...Ole grabs Hall and they send both men into the ropes.] Jim: STEREO SPINEBUSTERS!!!!!!!! Jesse: I CAN'T HEAR MYSELF THINK!!!!! Jim: Hall staggers back to his feet, Flair staggers back to his feet. [Arn kicks Flair in the stomach, and Ole kicks Hall in the stomach.] Jim: DOUBLE DDT!!!! Both men make the respective covers...1!!! 2!!! 3!!!! Jesse: They got em!!!! FINK: Here are your winners....Arn and Ole Anderson, the Minnesota Wrecking
Crew!!!! Jim: And they embrace...what a reunion!!! What an amazing event this has been so far, and tonight we just got our moneys worth right here.
Jesse: I'll tell ya what Jimbo...I'm not mark, I've been in this business for years. Tonight I think I got goosebumps watching these two teams go at it.
Although I would've liked to see Scott Hall not be involved, but that's just personal preference.
Jim: Arn and Ole are walking back to the dressing room, and Arn's face is covered in blood.
Jesse: Well it wouldn't have been old school unless SOMEONE walked away with a crimson mask!
Jim: WOW what a night so far, and we're not even done. [We cut back to the ring and Ric Flair is yelling at Scott Hall.] Jim: And it looks like we've got something brewing here. Jesse: Hall did a better job than Flair in that match, and Flair is yelling at Hall? Jim: Ric is mad...he's turning red, and we've all seen this before. [Flair without even thinking slaps Hall across the face, and this wakes Scott up. Scott grabs Flair by the hair, and the crowd is on their feet. Scott is yelling at him, and from the crowd comes a man in a black mask....]
Jim: What the hell? [The masked man levels Hall from behind with chair, and then leaves the ring quickly. Flair drops, and rolls out of the ring. The crowd is still kind of in awe after they saw the masked man come into the ring and take Hall out.]
Jim: Who was that? Jesse: I don't know why are you asking me? The man had a mask on. Jim: I wasn't asking you, I was thinking outloud. Jesse: Well don't... Jim: Fans, it's time for our Main Event!!! These two men have taken a long road to the finals here tonight...right now we're going to tell you what we know about these two men!!! Steve Armstrong, and Sho Funaki!
