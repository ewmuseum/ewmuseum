XCW New Years Brawl

January 21, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The XCW presents.... (Camera cuts to the crowd who is on fire, we've got signs everywhere. "Al
Snow's Future is Jobbing", "Whipwreck is a loser baby", "WHAT!?"....are all shown, and then we cut to Jim Ross and Paul Roma. Jim is wearing a suit and tie, with his black Stetson hat. Paul has on a Gucci Suit on, and he's got ice all over his wrists, and ears. Both men seem so happy that it's PPV time...)
Jim: WELCOME EVERYONE!!!! We're live here in a sold out Nassau Coliseum, and this place is as loud as hell tonight. The Brawl tonight is going to be a PPV for the history books...the first ever since the XCW came back.
Paul: Ya know...I've been to the Super Bowl, I've been to the World Series...hell I've been to the Rolling Stones, but nothing compares to an XCW
Pay Per View. I've been in them, and nothing is going to compare to this. The people are the loudest I've ever heard, and the line-up has some amazing talent!!!
Jim: No doubt about it. The XCW is bringing to you tonight the biggest and baddest Pay Per View that anyone could give you this month. You want to talk about hard hits, amazing high spots, and total drama...tonight is where's it's going to happen.
Paul: I'm looking forward to every single match here tonight. Jim: What's going to happen when Vader, Douglas, and Whipwreck lock up in the ring? Paul: People have been looking forward to that match since it was announced. I spoke with Shane Douglas earlier today, and he seems dead ready. Mikey
Whipwreck is ready, and Vader...well he's always ready! Jim: Then you have RVD taking on Steve Austin.... Paul: This match will come close to being the match of the night. Austin and
RVD in the ring finally to settle the score. The TV title will be on the line for this one too. It's going to be an amazing match!!!
Jim: Well lets not waste anymore time...lets take you to our first match! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Marietta, GA. He weighs in at 245 pounds...here is
"Buff" Bagwell!!!!
["I'm Buff and I'm the Stuff" is heard over the PA, and the fans start to boo automatically. He comes out from the curtain and has a huge smiles on his face as he gets in his knee to pose for the fans. He gets up and does he strut then climbs into the ring.]
A ONLINE 6.0\IDB\Diction.ind FINK: And his opponent....from Las Vegas, Nevada. He weighs in at 247 pounds...here is Val Venis!!!
A ONLINE 6.0\IDB\Diction.ind ["Hello Ladies" is heard on the PA, and Val Venis walks out to a huge ovation. He has the towel wrapped around his waist, and as he makes his way to the ring he undoes it and runs the rest of the way...Val slides into the ring and the bell sounds.]
Jim: And we're underway!!! Both men hitting each other with lefts and rights... Paul: Whao...nobody is wasting any time. Jim: Venis gets the upperhand, and he runs to the ropes... <Buff puts up a knee, but Venis rolls around it and goes for the roll up in...>
Jim: This could be a quick one....1!! 2!! No Buff kicks out, and both men rise to their feet again. Buff takes a wild swing at Venis, and Val grabs him from behind...
Paul: He's up high!!! Atomic Drop!!!! Jim: OH and that hit it's target!!!!! Buff holding his backside and Venis runs to the ropes, and knocks him down with a huge clothesline.
Paul: And this place is going crazy!!! Jim: Val has the control here, and these people definitly approve of that. <Venis waits for Buff to get back up and he nails him with a right hand to the face, followed by another right hand. Val with an Irish Whip, and Buff bounces out of the turnbuckle hard...>
Jim: And Val catches him with a HUGE Hip Toss!!!!! Paul: Buff needs to start showing some offense or this is going to be a damn quick match. Jim: Well Venis came in like a house on fire, and he isn't going to let up it doesn't look like.
<Buff charges again, and Venis with a single leg takedown. Buff hits the canvas with his nose first, and he screams out in pain. Venis gets up quickly and drops an elbow on the back of the neck, and another quick elbow to the back of the neck.>
Jim: I'm impressed with both of these men, and they're starting off this Pay
Per View with a bang.
Paul: You got that right...we can't even call most of the match because they're moving so damn fast!
Jim: Val Venis has comeback to the XCW, and if you remember the last time he was here he upset Goldberg pretty damn bad.
Paul: Oh yeah, but maybe Goldberg doesn't remember. Lets hope he doesn't. Jim: Venis locks in a chinlock, and Buff is trying to scramble to his feet. He finally does, and goes for an elbow to the back of the head. Venis ducks out of the way....
<Val spins Buff around and hits him in the face, and another hit. Val throws him into the ropes and goes for a backdrop, but Buff counters with a kick to the face.>
Jim: Oh he put his head down...big mistake right there, and Buff took advantage of it. Paul: Well when you're getting so much offense in like Val was, it's hard think of little things like that. You get excited and then you make mistakes, and then a guy like Buff comes in and beats you...and then your wife leaves you because you're a loser, and the dog dies because you don't have enough food to feed it...and the....
Jim: Umm okay...I think we get the idea. Paul: Sorry about that. Jim: Buff with a scoop slam, and Venis hits the mat hard. <Buff goes up to the 2nd Turnbuckle and drops a fist onto the head of Venis. He gets up again and drops another fist onto the head, and Venis tries to get away.>
Jim: Buff isn't about to let him walk away. He brings Venis to his feet, and
Venis tries to get the upperhand with a punch to the stomach, but Buff with a rake to the eyes.
Paul: That'll slow you down right away, and Buff is smart by doing that. <Buff Bagwell lifts up Venis and drops him down to the mat with a modified
Spine Buster. Venis holds his back as Buff smiles and takes a breather against the turnbuckle.>
A ONLINE 6.0\IDB\Diction.ind Jim: And Buff thinks it's a good idea to sit back and relax, and Venis recovers. That's why so many people don't take Bagwell seriously. Do you think that he's going to be able to carry a title with an attitude like that?
Paul: I did, and I was damn good. Jim: No you weren't...you and your wife got choked out by Taz. Paul: Umm yeah thanks for bringing up such sweet memories there Jimbo. Jim: Anytime Romeo. <Buff turns around and Venis is right there with a boot to the stomach. Val throws Buff into the opposite turnbuckle face first, and he drives him into the turnbuckle with a series of head smashes. Buff staggers out of the corner, and
Venis hits him with a back drop suplex!!!>
Jim: Beautiful Backdrop Suplex by Val Venis, and now he's going for the cover. He hooks the leg....and Buff kicks out before the 1 count. Paul: Well now I think Buff is going to get serious. Now he needs to focus, and take out Venis.
<Val brings him up again and he goes for a suplex, but Buff puts on the breaks. Venis tries again, and Buff counters with a knee to the stomach. Buff now runs to the ropes and hits a back elbow.>
Jim: Back Elbow and both men go down hard, but Bagwell is up quick. He runs to the top ropes...and he's waiting for Venis to make it back up.
Paul: I don't think going to the top is a good idea. He doesn't need to take a risk like that.
Jim: Well he obviously thinks he does. <Buff leaps off the top turnbuckle, and ends up dropkicking Val's knee....Val falls down and screams in serious pain.>
Jim: Oh my dear God...he might have messed up his knee badly. Buff I think was meaning to his Val's chest, but a miscalculation and he hit his knee. Right on target, and that might have knocked his patella right out of place.
Paul: Well Buff might have inadvertently won the match by doing that. What a smart mistake by Buff Bagwell!!
Jim: And Buff just smiles, because he knows he's hit pay dirt. Look at poor Val
Venis trying to make it up. He's using the ring ropes, and Buff clips the good leg!!! What a cheap shot!!!
Paul: I thoght Buff had nothing but mush in his brain, but he's showing me that he can be pretty damn smart!
Jim: Cheap, cheap move by Buff Bagwell!!! <Buff brings a limping Venis back to his feet and kicks the bad knee out from under him, and Venis falls to the canvas. Buff points and laughs at him, and the crowd gives him a whole lot of heat for that.>
Jim: Now that Venis can't get up, Buff is pretty cocky. That's not a true sign of a wrestler, that's a sign of a true cheater.
Paul: But a smart man. You have to give him some credit. Jim: I'm not giving him anything. <Buff poses right over Venis, and Venis all of a sudden pulls Buff down to the ground face first. >
Jim: Venis pulls Buff's legs out from under him, and the place is going wild. Paul: Well you can't always get an A! Jim: Val is trying to get back to his feet, and he's dragging himself over to the ropes. He's up now, and he's limping over to Buff.
<Buff swings at him, but Venis blocks it and hits him with a fist to the face. Another fist to the face, and Buff goes down hard. Venis drops to the mat holding his knee though, and the ref is checking on him.>
Paul: HAH!! Jim: Oh man things don't look good for Val Venis. His knee is seriously hurt, and it looks like Jimmy Korderas is going to have to stop the fight.
Paul: No keep it going. Jim: Buff is coming over now, and he grabs Venis by the hair. WAIT A MINUTE!!! Venis with a small package!!! 1!!! 2!!! 3!!! Paul: WHAT!? FINK: Here is your winner...Val Venis!!! Jim: Val has just played possum with Buff Bagwell, and he suprised even me. Paul: What a cheap move!!! I hate cheaters!!! Jim: Buff Bagwell wasn't expecting that at all, and Val came in and locked in that Small Package.
Paul: Venis is being helped back to the locker room, and Buff doesn't look to happy about this at all. He's storming back to the dressing room...I wouldn't want to mess with him.
Jim: Fans we have just scraped the tip of the ice berg here tonight. We have so many other things planned tonight, and this match was just a little of what you're going to get.
Paul: That was a pretty good match too. Jim: Lets take you to the locker room for words from Goldberg..... (Camera cuts to backstage where Goldberg is standing next to Mene Gene. Goldberg is shaking his neck and body out, and trying to loosen up a little before his match here tonight.)
Mene Gene: Ladies and gentlemen standing next to me is one of the phenoms of the XCW. A man who made his presence felt just a few days ago, and now has a big match with Scott Norton. Goldberg...your thoughts.
Goldberg: Gene, it's real easy. You see when I came into the XCW, I wasn't expecting anything bit disrespect. I wasn't expecting a loser like Scott Norton to understand my goals, and my ambition here. So I knew it was only a matter of time before some fat punk like Norton put his nose into my business. Well, you went looking in the wrong place...you came knocking on my door. You should've read the sign...the sign that said "Who ever enters...is NEXT!!!"
AAAARRGGRGH!!!! (Goldberg sticks out his tongue and walks away.)
