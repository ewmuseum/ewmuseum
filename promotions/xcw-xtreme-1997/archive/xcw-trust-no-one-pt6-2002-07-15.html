XCW Trust No One (part 6)

July 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: What an amazing Pay per View this has been tonight. I can't remember when
I've seen so much action. You can be sure we're going to top ourselves though when the XCW Presents....."Annihilation", live from The Sky Dome in Toronto,
Canada. August 11th....2002. 30 Men, every 2 minutes...1 man wins.
Jesse: And the winner goes on to face the XCW World Champion at the following
Pay Per View.
Joey: I'm telling you that this is going to be an amazing event....this gives everyone a chance at the XCW World Title. Who will walk away the number 1 contender?
Jesse: It might be someone like Brock Lensar.... Joey: Or it could be Chris Benoit...fans tonight as you all know Terry Funk won't be wrestling. This past Wednesday Funk was taken out of pro-wrestling all together by Chris Benoit.
Jesse: He should've never stuck his nose into Benoit's business. That's what happens when you mess with a man like that. He'll take you right out, just ask
Mick Foley.
Joey: Chad told Chris Benoit to show up, because there will be someone at the
PPV to face him.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first....from Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. He weighs in at 245 pounds. Here is "The Crippler" Chris Benoit!
[Benoit's music starts to play and the crowd gives him the biggest heat of the night. He climbs in at waits to find out who his opponent is...]
FINK: And his opponent...weighing in at 269 pounds. Here is Ron Simmons!!!!! [Ron comes out to his APA music, and he's got a pair of jeans on with no shirt. The crowd pops like crazy, and we get a shot of Chris Benoit just smirking.] Joey: Benoit and Simmons are staring each other down...and this is Ron's first match in the XCW ever. The man has never step foot in an XCW ring, and tonight he steps in with one of the most dangerous man in the sport.
Jesse: You think Ron Simmons is the kind of man who cares? No way. Joey: There's the bell, and both men are in each other's faces. Trading words. [Great shot of Benoit looking right up to Ron Simmons, and him looking down at
Chris Benoit.]
Joey: And Benoit with the first punch to the face, and Simmons takes it. He backs back with his own, and both men are going back and forth with lefts and rights. Simmons with a right, and another, and another...
Jesse: Chris is backed into the corner, and here comes Simmons...he blasts
Benoit with a clothesline.
Joey: Simmons goes back in and drives a series of boots into Benoit's stomach. [Simmons lifts his big boot and begins choking out Chris Benoit in the corner. The ref starts the 5 count, and he breaks it at four. Simmons brings him out, and gives him a shot to the ropes - HUGE POWERSLAM.]
Joey: Wow I felt the impact on that move!!! 1!!! 2!!! Just two at the moment. Ron brings Chris up, and takes him over with a hip toss. Jesse: Where is Simmons going? Joey: He's going up top...he's on the second rope, and he drops a leg across
Benoit's throat. Ron with the cover once again...1!!! 2!!! And another kickout by Chris. The fans love the way Ron Simmons is starting out..he's taking it to
Benoit here early.
[Ron whips Benoit into the ropes, and catches him with a jumping shoulder block. Benoit stumbles back, and he goes over the top ropes. Ron walks over and goes through the ropes to join Benoit.]
Joey: Ron grabs Benoit by the head and slams it into the ringpost! Ron grabs him by the arm and whips him into the guardrail - he follows him and dumps him over int the crowd.
Jesse: And Simmons is going to follow. The ref has lost total control of this match. Joey: Ron has Benoit by the head, and he's leading him into a clearing where what appears to be a merchandise stand set up. Ron drags Benoit over to the merchandise stand, and throws him head-first into the side!!!
Jesse: And look at the workers scatter. Joey: Simmons persuing items available for sale both here and at the XCW Online
Store..and he comes across the official XCW logo beer stein!
Jesse: Hey I bought my wife one of those. Joey: Ron has the stein....Benoit is getting up...and OH MY GOD!!!! Simmons shatters the beer stein across the face of Chris Benoit.
Jesse: Whhhhoooooaaa. [Benoit falls back to the floor, and Simmons is left with nothing but the handle which he tosses aside. Benoit is busted wide open.]
Joey: Look at the blood flow from Chris Benoit... Jesse: Well he only got a beer stein slammed into his face. Joey: Simmons brings Benoit up, and he grabs him by the throat. I think he's going to chokeslam him through the merchandize table.
Jesse: It's over if he does.... Joey: He lifts him up, but Benoit lashes out with a kick between the legs. Benoit hooks him, and....HE DROPS HIM ONTO THE TABLE!!! SNAP SUPLEX ONTO THE TABLE!!! Jesse: And Simmons goes right through it. Joey: Both men down at the moment, and Simmons is in the wreckage of the table. Benoit gets to his feet, and he's dragging Ron Simmons back to the ring. He tosses him over the guardrail into the ringside area, and he joins him.
[Benoit hooks Simmons by the head - DDT on the floor!!] Joey: Benoit has the total advantage now, he wipes the blood out of his eyes and rolls Simmons into the ring, and then follows him in.
Jesse: Benoit makes the cover...this is going to be it. Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout by Simmons!!! Jesse: Benoit brings him up...and whips him hard into the corner. Benoit follows in - leaping lariat strikes the neck directly. We've seen Benoit work over a body part before, and it looks like he's going to start with the neck.
Joey: Benoit climbs up to the second rope, and he has Simmons by the head. Chris leaps off the rope to the outside, snapping Ron's neck across the top rope!! What an amazing move by Chris Benoit!!!!
[Benoit slides back into the ring and makes the cover.] Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! And just two... Jesse: Ron is one tough guy there's no way he's going to go down that easy. Benoit runs to the ropes, and he drops an elbow across the chest. Joey: Kneedrop to the back of the neck by Chris Benoit, and another one. Third times a charm, and wow...Benoit in total control right now.
[Benoit grabs him and Snap mares him down, and locks in a Dragon Sleeper.] Joey: And Ron is quickly fading, his face is turning red...and now purple. Jesse: How can you tell? Joey: Here comes the ref to check his arm...he raises one...it drops, again it drops, and this could be it...
Jesse: Heh you think it's going to actually drop? Joey: IT DROPS!!!!! Jesse: Whoa! Joey: No...wait, Simmons is raising his arm. He's coming back. He's spinning himself around in the grip of Benoit. He's getting himself back to his feed, and now he lifts Benoit up...WHOA!!! He hulrs him to the mat!!!!
[Both men are down, and now Simmons crawls over to Benoit for the cover...] Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!!! Jesse: Ron Simmons almost beat Chris Benoit, that was the cloest pinfall of the night... Joey: Both men barely making it to their feet, and Benoit swings. Ron ducks, and he hooks Chris from behind...FULL NELSON!!!! Full Nelson Slam by Ron
Simmons, and Benoit hits hard!!! Jesse: Whoa! Joey: The cover...ONE!!! TWO!! TH - NO!!! NO!!! Ron Simmons didn't hook the leg, and that could've been the win right there.
[Simmons brings Benoit back to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Simmons hits a HUGE Sidewalk Slam, and goes for another cover...]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!!! Simmons not wasting any time brings Benoit up - another whip - and this time it's a tilt - a - whirl slam. Cover and a hook of the leg...ONE!! TWO!!! Another kickout!!
Jesse: Ron jus - Small Package by Benoit!! Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! Reversal by Simmons....ONE!!! TWO!!! Reversal by Chris!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout!!! [Both men back up - Simmons blocks a shot from Benoit, kicks him in the stomach and lifts him up....Tiger Bomb!!!]
Joey: Cover!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THRE - NO!!! OH MY GOD!!!! Jesse: These pinfalls are giving me an ulser. Joey: Who would've thought that this match was going to be a great one. I figured some big moves, but nothing...nothing like this ever.
[Simmons brings Benoit up again, and goes behind him. He goes for a pumphandle slam, he lifts him up but Benoit slides back down. Benoit hits a German Suplex, and another one...and another one...he keeps the bridge on the 3rd.]
Joey: Three German Suplexes in a row!!! Here's the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!! KICKOUT!!! Jesse: Simmons kicked out of three suplexes in a row, incredible. [Benoit shakes his head in disgust and brings Simmons back up, Simmons with a punch to the gut, and he catches Benoit off-guard. Simmons puts Benoit's head between his legs...he lifts him up, and drives him down to the mat with a HUGE powerbomb.]
Joey: Benoit is down, as is Simmons. He's got to still be feeling those 3
German Suplexes. Ron trying to shake the cob webs, and he's going to the top turnbuckle.
Jesse: This is a mistake right here. Joey: He's waiting for benoit to get to his feet... [Benoit staggers to his feet, and Simmons leaps off and connects with a flying shoulder tackle...]
Joey: THIS IS IT!!! He hooks the leg...ONE!!! TWO!!! [All of a sudden we see someone come down to the ring and pull the ref out.] Joey: BRET HART!!! BRET HART!!! He pulled the ref out of the ring, listen to these boos. The ref is threatening to disqualify Benoit as he shouts at Bret Hart.
Jesse: And look inside the ring...Christian is on the top ropes!!! Flying
Legdrop and he lands on the back of Ron's neck!!!!!!!!
Joey: He rolls Simmons off Benoit, and rolls Benoit onto Simmons. No it can't end like this!
[Bret leaves the ref alone, and he slides into the ring - makes the count...] Joey: ONE!!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Jesse: WOW!!!! Joey: I can't believe it!!!! RON SIMMONS HAS KICKED OUT!!!!! [The crowd explodes here, and Benoit rolls off of Simmons and shakes his head. He gets up and slowly brings Simmons to his feet...he lifts him up and drives his head and neck into the canvas with a TOMBSTONE.]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! THR- NO!!! Simmons kicks out again...I can't believe what
I'm seeing, Ron Simmons has kicked out again.
Jesse: You've gotta be kidding me. Ron Simmons has been hit with everything, and this man has not given up just yet.
Joey: Chris Benoit can't believe it, Bret Hart and Christian can't believe it. What is it going to take for Benoit to put out Ron Simmons? [Benoit brings him up, but Simmons with a shot to the face, and the blood and sweat go hurling through the air. Ron with a chop, and another chop...]
Joey: And where is Ron getting the energy? Simmons with a third, and he backs him into the corner. Ron is stepping to the outside, and he's climbing to the top.
Jesse: He's going to hit him woth a Super Powerbomb...here it comes. Joey: NO!!! Low Blow by Benoit,. and Simmons has doubled over. Benoit moving his position up on that top turnbuckl - OH MY GOD!!! OH MY GOD!!!! SUPER
TOMBSTONE!!!!!! Jesse: And if Ron Simmons kicks out of this, he's officially not human. Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! THREE!!! It's over!! Chris Benoit pulls out an incredible, hard-fought victory against Ron Simmons.
Jesse: That was an incredible win. Joey: And Hart and Christian come into the ring and help the bloodied Benoit up to his feet. Christian kicks the lifeless Ron Simmons from the ring, and man did Ron give it his all. He ended up just short...just a little short.
FINK: Here is your winner...Chris Benoit!!!!! Joey: And have Bret, Christian and Benoit formed some sort of alliance? How dangerous would that be?
Jesse: Well they're all from Canada...ya never know they like to run together. Joey: I think Simmons would've pulled off the victory if it hadn't been for
Bret and Christian at the end. He had the match won after that Flying
Shoulderblock. [Camera cuts to the dressing room of Steve Austin who is sitting having a beer. Next to him is a gagged, and tied Francine...] Steve: Ain't that a bitch? [Francine just stares at him....] Steve: Don't look at me like that...WHAT? Francine:........... Steve: I said stop staring at me, what? Francine:.............. Steve: Stop undressing me with your eyes. See tonight you're gonna get to go free if Deb ain't my ref for tonight. If she is, well...heh, you're gonna be severin' ol' Stone Cold a lot of beers for the time to come.
[Camera fades]
