XCW Beach Blast '02 (part 7)

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Mikey isn't paying attention, and without even looking bumps right into none other than The Rock...]
The Rock:............ Mikey: Hey, watch yourself little... Ahhh, Rock, I, uh, wow, hey, uh, Rock, wow, ummmm The Rock: Do the Rock's eyes besieve him? Is this Mikey Whipwreck? Mikey: That's me! Michael Whipwreck, you can always tell by either name tag or dragon print tee-shirt.
The Rock: Back from Middle Earth, and here to take the World title huh? Well the Rock has a few questions for you...you ready for Funaki?
Mikey: Of course I am! It's my turn, not his. The Rock: Is your momma here tonight? Mikey: My mom? Uh.. No.. The Rock: The Rock thinks that's a damn shame, because when the world title is on the line..that's when a mother is needed the most. Now the last question the
Rock has for you is did you see "The Scorpion King"??
Mikey: No, I did not, I saw Jason X though! It was bad ass! The Rock: Whoa whoa whoa WHOA....you're telling the Rock that you missed the
"Scorpion King" to see Jason X?
Mikey: Well, not on purpose... I also saw Spirit.. Not because I wanted to but ummm, because I walked into the wrong theater.. That's it. Yeah.
The Rock: If the Rock wasn't wearing this fancy $500 shirt, he'd Rock Bottom you right back to the center of there earth where your little fat elf ass came from. The Rock thinks that you need to skip this tournament...buy yourself a ticket, buy youself some popcorn...sit your monkey ass down, and watch The
Scorpion King!
Jason X....you make the Rock sick to his stomach. Mikey: Hey, it was a good movie! The Rock: The Rock see's that you haven't changed one damn bit since he left. You'd think that the XCW closed up while the Rock was gone and just opened back up! Mikey: I suppose.. But I'm on the road to a championship, Rock. Me! Mikey! The Rock: Haven't you won the title before? Mikey: Yeah... The Rock: THEN ya shouldn't have lost the damn thing in the first place you big pile of monkey crap!!! The Rock is back, he's headed to the ring...and after tonight he's gonna grab that XCW World Title from WHOEVER has it. IF YA
SMELL.... Mikey: What the Mikey is cooking!! The Rock: WHAT THE....Wait a minute...heh...did the Rock just hear you say his catch phrase?
Mikey: No.. I, uh.. Said Scorpion King RULES! The Rock: NEVER EVER use the Rock's catch phrase again. Now if the Rock see's your ass next week, and you don't have a ticket stub from the Scorpion
King...it's gonna be one hell of a smack down for Mikey Whipwreck! Got it? Mikey: Got it! Yes sir! [Mikey leaves quickly....] FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is the part of the 2nd Round in the XCW World Title Tournament. First coming down to the ring, hailing from
Osaka, Japan. Here is "The Whole Fn' Show" Sho Funaki!!!!
[Sho is wearing a new shirt, and niew tights from the last time he was out here. He comes out with the Japanese Flag in his hand, and he's waving it like there's no tomorrow. Suprisingly the fans are giving him a decent face response. He climbs into the ring and raises his arms, and that's when they give him the heel response.]
FINK: And his opponent....here is Mikey Whipwreck!!! ["Loser" by Beck plays again, and Mikey comes out in the same outfit. He looks a little tired, but not too much. Mikey slaps a few hands, and goes right over to Vader for the second time tonight...]
Jim: And once again Mikey is having words with Vader. Jesse: I like how Vader is just laughing it off. The guy is here to enjoy himself, Arn was nice enough to invite him here and he thinks it's funny.
Jim: Well Mikey doesn't think it's too funny. Jesse: Mikey's a big guy when he knows Vader can't do anything to save him. [Mikey climbs into the ring and charges Sho, and Sho ducks and hits a drop toe hold. Sho quickly applies a legbar on the left leg.]
Jim: Mikey quickly scrambles to the ropes, and Sho lets go. Jesse: And Sho doesn't seem to care. I like the fact that this guy has tons of confidence, and he's only to say one word in the English language.
Jim: Indeed. Sho whips Mikey into the turnbuckle, and he hits a dropkick to the knee!!! Sho now takes the leg and wraps it behind the ring rope, and now he's pulling on the leg.
[The ref start the five-count, and Sho breaks it at four. Bringing Mikey back in, whip into the ropes and a chop block by Sho. Mikey falls to his stomach, and Sho is headed for his legs.]
Jim: Who knows what is going through Sho's mind. He looks like he's going to lock in some sort of submission, and he does...he locks in a Bow and Arrow.
Jesse: Great submission move right there. This will wear your opponent down, and let you go in for other moves..such as his finisher.
Jim: Sho now rolling around on the mat with the hold still applied, and Mikey isn't feeling to good right now. Sho releases it, and he brings a limping Mikey back up. Sho hooks his leg and rolls through with a kneelock.
Jesse: But look, Mikey is right next to the ropes and he quickly grabs ahold of them. [The ref tells Sho to break the hold and he does. Sho makes it back to his feet and brings Mikey up. Sho with a dragon screw legwhip. He pull him back up and hits another. Sho holds on and pulls him back one more time...lifts him up...kneebreaker.]
Jim: And Sho still hasn't let go of that hold, and he straps on a figure four!!! Jesse: I haven't seen someone wrestle like this in ages. Mikey Whipwreck has been totally man handled this whole match, and Sho is doing everything he can to win.
Jim: And Mikey reaches the ropes. The ref is patting Sho on the shoulder, and he lets go. Jesse: I think Sho thinks he won. He thinks that Mikey gave up. Jim: Sho is jumping up and down, and he thinks he won the match. The ref is telling him now, and Mikey with a deperation dropkick to the back.
Jesse: That was a dumb move. He hurt himself too. [Both men make it back to their feet and Mikey ducks a superkick, and comes back with a clothesline. Sho is back up and Mikey takes him down again with a bodyslam this time.]
Jim: And Sho goes for the cover...ONE!!! Kickout by Mikey... Jesse: Well since those legs are taken out from under him he needs to use his arms. [Whipwreck with a short whip, and into a sit-down chokeslam!] Jim: And talk about a comeback move...that could turn the tide of this match right here. Jesse: Mikey brings Sho back up, and he hooks in a Fishermans suplex. Is he going to last on those knees?
Jim: Whipwreck tries, and he can't get him over on the first try. He's going for the second try, and he uses all his strength to get him over. ONE!!! TWO!!!
Kickout by Sho!! [Mikey gently gets back to his feet and executes another short whip this time putting Sho into a DVD position. Mikey's legs buckle from the weight, and he falls down to the canvas.]
Jim: Mikey fights his way back to his feet, and Sho with a kick to the back of the knee. Now he grabs him in a hammerlock on the right arm, and lifts him into the arm with the hammerlock still applied!!!
Jesse: WOW...I'm amazed at what this guy can do. He is the "Whole F'n SHO"!!! [Sho drops Mikey back down and lets him go. Mikey is limping and holding his arm in pain, and walks right into a slap to the face. Everyone in the crowd
"Oooo's" from that, and Mikey walks into another one.]
Jim: And Sho is just humiliating Mikey right now. He knows he's got him where he wants him, and Mikey can't do anything about it right now.
Jesse: A third slap!!! Jim: No!! Mikey blocks it and nails him with a right hand, and another right hand. Mikey sends Sho to the ropes, and he takes him down with Thez Press.
Jesse: But Mikey landed on those knees, and he topples right over. [Sho gets up and brings Mikey up again...Inverted Russian Legsweep, and Mikey hits the canvas like a rock. Sho goes to the top rope and he gives the sign for the "ShoStopper". Mikey on his knees lunges at the turnbuckle, and Sho gets crotched.]
Jim: And Sho will be singing a high song in the shower tomorrow. Jesse: Mikey is trying everything he can to gain some momentum, and every single time he does Sho is there to take it away.
[Mikey climbs the turnbuckle, and Sho punches him off. Mikey's back almost snaps in half from the impact, and Sho flies off....]
Jim: SHOSTOPPER!!!!!! Jesse: He hit it!! Jim: He's got a hand full of tights!! ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Sho Funaki!!! Jim: Sho won the match, and he goes onto the finals. What an upset!!! Jesse: This is an upset...I can't believe Sho Funaki won tonight. Jim: Mikey is up now and he's talking to the ref, and the ref isn't listening. The ref has already made his decesion, and that is that. [Mikey grabs the ref and throws him down to the canvas. He walks over to Sho and takes him down with a reverse DDT.]
Jim: Mikey has lost it!!!! Mikey has lost it!!! Jesse: And Sho is getting beat to a pulp. [Mikey gets on top of Sho and starts hitting him with lefts and rights, and the place is going wild. Sho gets his clock cleaned for about 2 more minutes, and all of a sudden the crowd stops cheering and in the ring is none other than
BROCK LESNAR.]
Jim: BROCK LESNAR IS HERE!!!!! Jesse: And what's he doing here is the real question of the night... Jim: The man is a damn monster. [Mikey continues to beat over Sho, and then all of a sudden Brock screams and
Mikey slowly turns around. Mikey's eyes are huge...]
Jim: Mikey looks like a deer in headlights. Jesse: Oh no...Brock is going to kill him! [Brock slowly makes his way over to Mikey, and all of a sudden we see another monster run into the ring and it's....]
Jim: VADER!!!!! VADER IS BLOCKING BROCK'S PATH!!!!! Jesse: I can't believe it Ross!!! I can't believe that Vader is saving Mikey
Whipwreck from getting killed.
Jim: Vader throws off his jacket, and he's ready to go. These two big red heads are ready to go, and we're ready for em'.
Jesse: Brock is taller, but Vader is bigger.... [Both men meet face to face in the ring. Mikey and Sho are just standing there in awe, and everyone at Safeco Field is going wild. All of a sudden about 5 refs slide into the ring, with security and Seattles finest.]
Jim: And they broke this up just in time...Brock is being held back, and Vader is too. Jesse: WOW...can you imagine what it would've been like had these two gotten into the ring with each other?
Jim: Why did Vader come to the aid of Mikey? Jesse: Why did Brock come to the aid of Sho? Jim: That's an even better question!!! Fans we need to clear this mess out of the ring, and we'll be right back. Folks, comming to you LIVE on July 14th from
The Palace Of Auburn Hills in Detriot, Michigan...it's going to be TRUST NO
ONE!!!!!!
