XCW Monday Night Malice (part 3)

January 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Raven has come to the XCW to talk Hogan out of retirement? Are we going to see Hulk Hogan comeback to respond to these words?
Jesse: Ugh don't get me started on Hogan. Jim: Well we'll see soon...if he doesn't comeback, Raven is still a great addition to the XCW!!
[Camera cuts to Chad's office again, and Steve Austin walks in with his 3:16 vest, and a 6 pack in his hand. He walks right up to Chad and has a very angry look on his face...]
Steve: What in the hell are you wastin' my time for son? Chad: Look I called you in here to ask a favor from you. You and I don't really know each other very well, but tonight we could become good friends. You're teaming up with Mikey tonight right?
Steve: Ya got that right. Me an' that sumbitch are fixin' to cause some hell. Chad: Well how about half way through the match you STUN him and walk away? Then you don't have to worry about getting hurt, you can just collect your money and leave.
Steve: Ya want me to sell out? WHAT? Ya want ole Stone Cold to turn his back on all his fans? WHAT? Ya want me... to tuck tail and run to the back?
Chad: Who cares about Mikey Whipwreck? I know you don't......I know someone who you do care about though, and that's RVD. You want to see RVD lose his title tonight right? WHAT? Heh...I like doing that. WHAT!?
Steve: Ah can't believe you're even askin' Stone Cold Steve Austin to walk away from a fight...Ah can't believe that ya have the audacity to suggest that. Ah can't believe that ya...
[Smirk] ...made a good point. Chad: Austin listen to me...you can stay there and fight if you want, but at least STUN Whipwreck for me, and if you do that...I PROMISE YOU RVD will lose the TV title tonight. WHAT!? WHAT!? WHAT do you say? WHAT!?
Steve: Ah don't want that sumbitch ta lose his belt. That belt belongs ta Steve
Austin... and at the pay per view, I'm fixin' ta swipe it. I'll give Whipwreck his due... and fer me... well, let's just say ya owe me one.
Chad: Hey Austin...umm...you wanna get a beer with me later? WHAT! Steve: A beer with you? Ya sorry sumbitch... Austin doesn't share. Chad: It's on me...WHAT! Actually we'll take it out of RVD's Paycheck. AHAHA! Steve: Sounds good, ya crazy sumbitch. Now if ya'll excuse me... ah got some ass ta whip. [Chad extends his hand to shake Austin's.Austin locks in on him with his icy blue eyes. With an intense smirk, the rattlesnake shakes his hand.]
Jim: What in the hell? Austin is going to turn on Whipwreck tonight, and then go out for a beer with Costello?
Jesse: Well Austin loves oppertunity. Jim: What does Chad have in store with RVD? Jesse: I don't know.... Jim: It's got to be something big. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall, and is for the XCW Television Title. First coming down the ring weighi....
(All of a sudden Chad Costello runs down to the ring waving his hands. He climbs in and whispers something into FINK's ear...Fink looks very suprised, and then begins to speak again.)
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the CEO of the XCW has just informed me that Marty
Jannetty will not be in the match tonight. His replacement....weighing in at
500 pounds. Here is RIKISHI!!!!
(Rikishi's music starts to play and the fans all rise to their feet to see him walk out. He comes out in his usual gear, and the fans erupt. He smacks his ass and makes his walk to the ring. He climbs in and raises his arms into the air, and the fans give him a mixed reaction.)
FINK: And his opponent....from Battlecreek, Michigan. Here is the XCW Television Champion...Rob Van Dam!!! ("Walk" by Pantera starts to play and the crowd jumps to their feet again, and
RVD walks out with his title. He smiles at Rikishi acting like it's no big deal. He hops into the ring, and works the crowd.)
Jim: This is going to be a great match...Rikishi is so agile for his size, and
RVD meshes well against anybody.
Jesse: Man I wouldn't want to be on his bad side. Jim: Or back side for that matter Jess. The ref holds up the belt, and the goes the bell. What a suprise by Chad Costello here tonight...I can't believe that he tossed Marty out of the match, and now he throws Rikishi into the match.
Jesse: Well I think that Rikishi would have a better chance of taking RVD out than Marty would.
<Both men lock up and Rikishi quickly throws him down to the canvas.> Jim: Great show of power by the big man, and RVD should know not to lock up with a man like that.
Jesse: Well his whole strategy has been thrown out the window. Jim: How is RVD going to overcome this mountain of a man? <Both men start circling each other, and RVD sneaks under Rikshi's legs. Rob bounces up and his Rikishi with a spinning back kick. Rikishi is stunned, and
RVD hits it again...>
Jim: Quickness is shown by RVD, and Rikishi is losing some balance!!! Jesse: RVD needs to use his quickness to win here tonight. Jim: What's going to happen when RVD gets stuck in a corner, and Rikishi locks in that Stink Face.
Jesse: Ugh... <Rob fires away with a series of right hands, and Rikishi falls against the ropes. Rob hits him with a superkick, and Rikishi falls over the top ropes. Rob gets a running start and jumps onto the top ring rope, and executes a Stardust
Press!!!>
Jim: OH GOOD GAWD!!! DID YOU SEE THAT? Jesse: I did!!! Jim: Rob Van Dam hit a Flying Stardust Press, and Rikishi is out. Rikishi doesn't even know what the hell hit em'. Rob is barely getting up after that move, and now he's jumping onto the apron...
Jesse: This guy is nuts! Jim: Rob with a running start.... <Rob jumps off and hits a jumping senton splash. Rikishi ROLLS out of the way though, and Van Dam's back makes a sick THUD!>
Jim: Oh man!!! RVD's kidneys have to be popped like damn water balloons after that.... Jesse: Don't make moves you don't need to. That's like Brad Pitt trying to spit game to a girl who already wants him!
Jim: "Spits Game"? Jesse: Booker T taught me that. <Rikishi is up now and lifts up RVD in a bodyslam position, and smashes his back intot he ring post. Another ring post smash, and he dumps him onto the cement.>
Jim: Rob is in some serious trouble now... Jesse: Yeah I'd say so. Jim: Rikishi is working on the back of Van Dam, and the fans are loving this match. <Rob tries to make it to his feet, but Rikishi throws him back into the ring. Rob gets a running start and hits a Baseball Slide...> Jim: NO!!! Rikishi moves out of the way and pulls RVD out of the ring and fires in with a serious right hand. Van Dam falls to the canvas...
Jesse: They need to bring this match back into the ring. Rikishi can't do much on the floor...unless he uses some chairs, or a garbage can.
Jim: Rikishi is talking to the fans, and RVD is making it back up. Rikishi with a running start!!! CLOTHESLINE!!!! Rob does a 360, and falls to the cement again. This doesn't look good at all....Rikishi is just a machine.
<Rikishi smiles, and throws RVD back into the ring. Rikishi climbs back in and picks up Rob by his pony tail, and executes a Belly-To-Belly suplex, and it totally squashes RVD.>
Jesse: Welcome to the house of Pancakes!!! Jim: You're not kidding Jess. Rikishi brings the TV Champ back to his feet and chops him into the corner.
<Rikishi starts in with fist after fist, and RVD is slowly slumping against the turnbuckle. Finally he falls to the canvas against the turnbuckle, and Rikishi knows what time it is...>
Jim: OH NO...it's time for the Stink Face!!! Jesse: I hate this move... Jim: Rikishi is backing that thing up, and OH!! RVD with a kick to the posterior, and another one. RVD isn't about to get that done to him tonight. He comes out of the corner with a spinning leg lariat, and Rikishi goes down.
<Rob gets on top of the fallen Rikishi and starts driving his fists into his face. The ref comes over and pulls RVD off...>
Jim: Whoa...and RVD doesn't look to happy. This man hates being disrespected, and that's just what Rikishi was about to do to him.
Jesse: And Rob is making it clear that he doesn't want that to happen. <Rikishi is back up and Rob hits him with a Springboard Sommersault Dropkick.>
Jim: Rikishi is down again, and now RVD goes to the top ropes.
Jesse: He'd better hurry. Rikishi is recovering quickly... Jim: Rob with a flying crossbody, and Rikishi catches him in mid-air!!!! He just caught him like a baby, and he hits RVD with a backbreaker, AND ANOTHER
ONE!!! <Rikishi tosses the injured Van Dam down to the mat like sack of potateos. He's giving the sign for the Rikishi-Driver, and waits for Van Dam to get up.>
Jim: This could be almost over....he lifts up Van Dam. WAIT!!! Here comes Steve
Austin, and he attacks Rikishi from behind.
<Rikishi drops RVD, and Austin spins him around for a KICK!!! WHAM!!! STUNNER!! Austin then walks over to RVD and executes a KICK!!! WHAM!!! STUNNER!!! Austin flips everyone off in the ring and walks away.>
Jim: Austin has just thrown the damn match out. Rikishi was close to winning that XCW TV Title, but Austin didn't want to see that happen.
Jesse: He wants that title at the Pay Per View. Austin isn't a dumb man!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest as been declared a No Contest. Therefore STILL the XCW Television Champion...Rob Van Dam!!! Jim: What a night it has been thus far, Jesse. Jesse: Any night is good when I'm around, Jim. Jim: I can only imagine what else is in store... [With that said suddenly the arena lights go out and the big screen flashes to life. Four words come across it, "THE FUTURE IS HERE"]
Jim: What in the world? We saw something like this last week. [Then just as quickly as they appeared the words explode as Silver Future by
Monster Magnet blares over the arena!]
Jesse: What's going on here, Ross? No one told me anything about this. Jim: Well that makes two of us.. Jesse: But I should know, I'm important! [The arena lights come back up as the camera stays fixed on the entranceway as a familiar man steps into view, but certain things about his appearance make everyone wonder if it's who they think it is...]
Jim: Is that...No, it couldn't be....Al Snow? Jesse: It looks like Al. [It is indeed Al Snow! The confusion is justified as Al is not dressed in his more familiar ring attire but is instead dressed in a pair of black tights with the word "THE FUTURE" up the outside of both of his legs. Snow begins to make his way down the aisle. Fans a bit surprised by his appearance still give him a positive response though he seemingly avoids them on his way down the aisle.]
Jim: Al Snow is here in the XCW? He doesn't seem like the Snow we all know and love though.
Jesse: I never loved him. [Snow gets into the ring and gets a mic. He steps to the middle of the ring and raises it to his lips as the music fades away...]
Al Snow: I sense everyone is surprised to see me... Jim: You got that right. Al Snow: For so many years I've been in the industry. For all those years I've sat back and watched guys with nowhere near the talent I have, or the ambition that I possess get free rides to the top because they had the right look or the right catch phrase..I've sat back and watched it happen time after time. What was I good for? According to the "boys" I was good for a laugh!
[Al pauses almost in disgust..] Al Snow: "Hey we need a good laugh, send out Snow." All these years I've been made to look like nothing but a big joke!
Jim: You have got to be kidding me... Jesse: He was a pretty big joke... [The fans go silent, not sure how to react to this..] Al Snow: But even they're not entirely to blame...A lot of the blame goes squarely on all of YOU!
[Snow points to the crowd...] Jim: He's blaming the fans for not making it now? Give me a break. Al Snow: You people cheer beer swilling brawlers like Steve Austin...Or talentless catch phrase spewing jackasses like The Rock! Because of you people cheering men like that I've had had to watch my career go down the toilet as the joke of the industry! I'd come out and did I get any cheers for my amazing talent?
[Snow shakes his head.] Al Snow: NO! It was always "We want head, we want head." WELL NO MORE! [Snow's expression turns to a combination of rage and extreme seriousness...] Al Snow: I've sat back long enough! From here on out I'm doing what's best for one person..
[Al Snow jams the thumb of his free hand square into his own chest..] Al Snow: ME! You got it? It's my time to shine! I will not be held back anymore! XCW I am The Future, and my time has come!
[Snow drops the mic and makes his way out of the ring as the once confused crowd now turns to boo's..]
