XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 6)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Guys it's time...Steve Austin will be taking on a mystey opponent. Remember fans this is a non-title match, no matter what happens in this match
Steve Austin will go on to face Brock Lesnar in the Main Event.
Don: I better get ready for my big debut tonight. Jim: What do you mean? Don: I mean I'm the mystery man. Jim: WHAT? Don: HAHA idiot. Jim: Fans lets take you to the match... [Fink stands in the ring, and the fans are eagerly awaiting the return of Steve
Austin. You can hear a faint chant of "Austin", and the bell rings. Fink introduces Austin as the sound of glass breaks through out the arena. Steve
Austin steps through with his black leather vest, tights, and boots. He has the title hanging in one hand, and his blue eyes are focused on that ring.]
Jim: Listen to this place go crazy!!!! Don: They love their Austin. Jim: 50,000 plus fans are rocking the stadium. [Austin goes from turnbuckle to turnbuckle and flips the bird. Everyone goes wild, and Austin holds his title high into the air.]
Jim: And now we're awaiting his mystery opponent. Don: Here I go. [The crowd draws to a hush as they await the announcement of the mystery man. All of a sudden the lights go out, and the spotlight shines on the XCW walkway. "Medal" starts to play and the fans recognize the song right away. Kurt Angle comes out to a huge mixed reaction, and fireworks blast off behind him. Angle makes his way into the ring and does his infamous skip.]
Jim: KURT ANGLE IS HERE IN THE XCW!!!!! Don: Our Olympic Hero!!!! Jim: Steve Austin is just smiling...these two have had some battles in the past, and tonight it's not going to be any different.
[Kurt Angle gets on the microphone.] Kurt: Wait a minute Austin, before you come charging at me with that beer breath of yours...I want to introduce you to our special guest ref here tonight. I was told that if I wrestled tonight there would be someone to watch over me and make sure that you didn't cheat. Well please welcome..."The
Franchise" SHANE DOUGLAS!!!! ["I am the Franchise" is heard all over the arena and then "Perfect Strangers" starts to play. Shane Douglas walks out from the curtain, and the fans boo. Steve Austin can't believe what he's seeing, but he just smirks. Shane Douglas has a HUGE smile on his face as he slides into the ring. Austin and Douglas meet face to face.]
Jim: The return of Shane Douglas!!!! Shane Douglas is back in the XCW!!!! Don: And Angle isn't about to wait for them to shake hands. [Angle grabs Austin and tosses him through the ropes. Kurt goes right after him, and Douglas follows.]
Jim: Kurt with a right hand, and another right hand. Don: This has the odds stacked a little against Steve Austin. Jim: Damn right. Angle with a boot to the midsection, and he executes a Butterfly Suplex on the XCW ramp. Oh wait a minute...Shane Douglas hands Kurt
Angle the World Title...
Don: Angle has never looked better. Jim: Angle has ten pounds of gold in his hand, and when Austin gets up he's in for a suprise. Austin gets up, and he goes right back down. Damnit...a shot to the head with the XCW World Title.
Don: Douglas is telling him to do it again. Jim: DAMNIT STOP THIS!!! Steve Austin gets up, and Angle swings. Austin ducks, plants a boot to the stomach. Austin goes for a piledriver, but Angle backdrops out of it.
[Austin hits the ramp hard, and Angle celebrates. He brings Austin back to his feet, and Austin counters with a low blow.]
Jim: Steve Austin now playing by their rules, and he brings the doubled over
Angle to his feet and whips him right into the ring apron!!!
Don: Douglas is letting this happen? Isn't that cheating? Jim: Austin charges and drops Angle down with a clothesline. Don: Oh man!! [Austin climbs up onto the apron, gets a running start and drops Angle down once again with a double ax-handle to the back. Angle falls down to the canvas, and Austin pulls one of the blue pads up.]
Jim: Austin is exposing those blue pads, and what's he going to do here? Douglas is telling him not to do anything, and Austin ignores him. Don: Never ignore Douglas, that'll just piss him off even more. Jim: Steve Austin grabs Angle...he puts him between his legs. Piledriver coming up, he's going to do it one way or another. Angle fights out of it though, and he slams Austin's head into the ring apron!
[Kurt rolls Austin into the ring, and he follows. Douglas comes in as well. Austin backs up against the turnbuckle, and Angle misses with a splash. Shane
Douglas calls for the bell, and the match is officially under way.]
Jim: Austin now with a series of boots to the head, and Austin is showing no mercy. Austin makes the cover...ONE!!! No Angle kicks out at the one count, and
Austin drives those fists into the head of Kurt.
Don: Wow...he's not holding up at all. Jim: Austin now hooks the leg this time, and ONE!!! TWO!!! NO...Angle kicks out. Steve Austin marches up to the second turnbuckle and comes off with a front elbow, but Angle lifts his boot up.
Don: Austin eats Red Boot!!! [Kurt gets up now, and Austin is walking off the boot he just recieved. Kurt catches him in a belly-to-belly suplex, and Austin goes flying across the ring.]
Jim: What a move by Kurt Angle, the exploding power that he has...man! Don: You're telling me. Jim: Angle goes behind Austin and locks in a Dragon Sleeper, and Austin is trying to fight it. He's moving around, and he walks towards the turnbuckle.
Angle grabs him by the side of the trunks...he lifts him onto the top turnbuckle... Don: What the hell is Angle thinking? Jim: INVERTED SUPERPLEX!!!!! Angle crawls over and hooks the leg...ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!! Austin kicks out, and that was a fast count. Whoa was that fast. Don: You're just seeing things. Jim: Kurt gets back up and brings Austin to his feet, and he unloads with a series on right hands to the head. He whips him into the turnbuckle, Austin bounces out and DAMN....
Don: WHOA!! Jim: Austin with a clothesline right out of the turnbuckle, and Angle does a
180! Steve Austin brings up Kurt and fires in with right after right, and a left after left. Austin gives him the double bird, kicks him in the stomach...he goes for the Stunner, but Angle pushes him!
Don: Nice. Jim: Austin bounces off the ropes chest first, comes back...Angle catches him in a German Release Suplex!!!!!
[Crowd pops for that move.] Jim: Kurt waits for Austin to get up, and Austin goes for another clothesline. It's ducked by Angle this time, and he HITS THE OLYMPIC SLAM!!!!!! Don: It's over!!! Jim: Angle goes for the cover...ONE!!! Don: TWO!!! Jim:....... Don:.......... Jim: KICKOUT!!!!! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!!! Steve Austin kicked out of the slam, and Angle can't believe it. Shane Douglas can't believe it.
[Angle is talking with Douglas, and Shane is telling him that's the best he can do. Angle walks over to Austin and locks in the Ankle Lock...]
Jim: Ankle Lock, and Angle wants to win this match badly tonight. Don: Ya think? Jim: Austin is hanging in there, but you can tell he's starting to fade. Steve
Austin is starting to fade, and Angle is grinning deeply as Austin tries to fight out of it.
Don: I have never seen Austin in this kind of situation. I mean he's been in some messes, but this is bad news for him.
Jim: Shane Douglas reaches for Austin's arm, and Austin flips him off. I guess he's not ready to go.
[Angle locks it in tighter, and Austin finally stops fighting it. He's in a serious amount of pain, and now Douglas checks the arm...
It drops once.... It drops twice....] Jim: Is it going to drop a third time? Is this going to be it for Steve Austin? Don: YES!! Jim: NO!!! He kept his arm up on the third try, and the crowd rises to their feet here. Austin keeps that arm up, and he extends his middle finger to
Douglas. Angle is in a state of shock, he thought he had it won. Don: Well he needs to think of something quick. Jim: Austin is trying to get back up. Austin is trying to get back to a vertical base, and Angle still has the lock on. ENZUGIRI by Austin...Angle has been knocked silly.
Don: You've gotta be kidding me. Jim: Austin grabs him and whips him into the ropes...THEZ PRESS!!! He unloads with a series of right hands to the temple. Douglas is trying to get him off of
Angle, but Austin just keeps firing away.
Don: COME ON!!! Jim: WHOA!!! Douglas pushes Austin off of Kurt Angle, and both men are face to face now. Both men are having words, and Angle from behind...he goes for a clothesline, and Austin with a backdrop.
[Angle goes crashing down to the outside.] Jim: He's right by us, and here comes Austin. Don: Uh oh. Jim: Austin fires in with a right hand, and another right hand. He throws him up onto the table next to us. Austin drives a knee into the stomach of Angle, and he's going to Piledrive him.
Don: Damnit that guy is persistant. Jim: Austin lifts him up, Angle kicks and fights his way back down. Austin goes for it again, and Angle does the same thing. Angle with a backdrop!!!!
[CRASH!!!!!!] Jim: Oh my God!!! Both men have been broken in half, the table has been broken in half...all that momentum carried both men through the table, and it looks like a car wreck.
Don: And the ref is starting the count...ONE!!! TWO!!!! [Both men are still out of it, and nobody is moving.] Jim: We're about up to the 8 count, and wait a minute. Kurt Angle is making it back up to his feet, and he is barely able to stand. He needs to use that guardrail.
Don: Do you blame him? Jim: Angle grabs Austin and throws him into the ring. Steve Austin gets up and is walking around like a drunken sailor.
Don: Umm... Jim: Angle rolls back in, and he charges...SPINEBUSTER!!!! Austin was waiting for him, and he caught him with a spinebuster. The XCW World Champion hooks the leg, and this might be it...ONE...................TWO...............NO!!! Angle kicks out.
Don: Now that was a fast count. Jim: No...Shane Douglas did that on purpose. Wait a minute...HERE COMES BROCK LESNAR!!!! Don: Oh what? Jim: Brock Lesnar is here, and he slides into the ring. Brock pushes Shane
Douglas aside and brings Austin to his feet. Brock...F5!!!! F5!!!!
Don: AWE SWEET!!!! Jim: Austin is down, and Brock smiles at Shane and walks away. Don: Hey Brock felt he needed to spice things up just a little bit. Jim: Shane and Angle have screwed us all. This is not right. Don: All if fair but love and war. Jim: Shane Douglas brings Angle to his feet...this is horrible. Angle hooks the leg, and Shane makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!
["Medal" starts up and Angle doesn't even realize he's won yet. Shane Douglas kicks Steve Austin out of the ring, and helps up Kurt. Shane raises his arm in the air, and Kurt gives us a huge "WHOOOO" as the fans start throwing trash into the ring.]
Jim: Did Brock have this planned with Douglas and Angle? Don: Why are you asking me? Jim: What the hell is going on? Don't tell me they've formed some kind of alliance between the three of them.
[Just as soon as Jim Ross says that Shane Douglas plants Angle with a Double
Arm DDT.] Jim: WHAT THE HELL!?!?! Don: Uhh...did that just happen? Jim: Douglas walks away, and he just made some kind of statement. Fans Steve
Austin walks into the Main Event with a loss, and Brock Lesnar walks in with a win. What in the world is going to happen next? We have so much more!!
