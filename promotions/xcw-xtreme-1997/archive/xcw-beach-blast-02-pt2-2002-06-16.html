XCW Beach Blast '02 (part 2)

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Thanks for that interview Tori. Jesse: Always nice when you get to see the other side of the wrestlers here in the XCW. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this is part of the 1st round for the XCW World
Title. First coming down to the ring, weighing in at 255 pounds. Here is
"Tatanka" Chris Chavis... ["Smack my bitch up" starts to play and Chavis walks out in a pair of jeans, and boots. He's got a shaved head, and extremely angry look on his face. People in the crowd are doing the Indian Call to piss him off. He climbs into the ring and simply ignores the fans.]
FINK: And his opponent...hailing from Osaka, Japan. He weighs in at 230 pounds, here is "The Whole F'N Sho" Sho Funaki!!!
["Indeed" is heard all over the arena, and the crowd pops. The slowly change their tune as he comes out waving a Japanese Flag. He walks down, slides in, and points right at Tatanka...]
Jim: And the boo's for Sho are shaking the ground here tonight. Jesse: It doesn't seem to bother him, and both of these men look to be in the best shape of their lives. I've never seen either man look any better than tonight.
[Collar and Elbow tie-up, and Sho takes the early advantage with a side headlock. Tatanka whips Sho into the ropes and goes for a clothesline, but Sho ducks it. He comes back and levels Tatanka with a flying forearm to the side of the head.]
Jim: Tatanka back up now, and he takes a hard kick to the stomach. Sho with a quick superkick, and Tatanka hits the ring hard. Sho runs to the ropes and drops a leg across the throat.
Jesse: Sho has turned into a great singles wrestler. He's a great tag wrestler as well, but he's the breakout star of Kaientai. I think he has a bright future ahead of him here in the XCW.
[Sho goes for another legdrop, but Tatanka rolls out of the way. He waits for
Sho to get up, and he hits him with a leaping clothesline. Both men make it up again and Tatanka with a huge right hand. Sho with some force pushes Tatanka back into the corner.]
Jesse: Nice desperation move. Jim: Irish Whip by Sho, and Tatanka hits hard. Sho follows in with a huge dropkick against the turnbuckle.
[Sho gets up and unloads with punches to the face which rock Tatanka's head back every single time. ]
Jim: WAIT A MINUTE...Tatanka is brushing those punches off. He's not feeling it, he's absorbing every hit...and it's just making him more mad.
Jesse: That's called a no-sell Ross. Jim: Sho goes for a desperate right, and Tatanka blocks it. He comes back with a right of his own, and another one. Tatanka whips Sho into the ropes and lifts him up in a huge GORILLA PRESS!!!
Jesse: Look at the power!! Jim: Tatanka just drops him down onto the canvas! And by the look on Tatanka's face, I don't think he's going to be a nice guy anymore.
Jesse: Yeah once you've pissed an Indian off...it's all out war. [Sho gets up and Tatanka locks in a Cobra Clutch, but Sho quickly with a go-behind and hits a belly-to-back suplex. Sho runs and jumps off the middle rope and hits a Springboard Bulldog on the dazed Tatanka.]
Jesse: Well maybe I spoke too soon. Jim: Sho with the cover...ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!! Tatanka kicks out with authority. Sho isn't too happy and he starts reigning down with right hands to the forehead of Tatanka.
Jesse: And now Sho with a chokehold. Jim: He waits for the 4 count. Jesse: Smart move! [Sho brings his opponent back to his feet and whips him into the ropes, but
Tatanka reverses. Tatanka hits him with a jumping back elbow. Tatanka throws the staggering Sho into the ropes again and hits a HUGE BACKBODY DROP!!!]
Jesse: Air Japan!!! One way ticket.... Jim: Tatanka launched him right off his back! [Inverted Atomic Drop by Tatanka, and then hits a lariat. Tatanka now with the cover...] Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! No way...Sho gets his shoulder up. Tatanka now picks up Sho, and he hits a fallaway slam! And Funaki helps himself up by using the ropes, and Tatanka charges him and knocks him down again.
Jesse: Don't let him get up. Keep him down on that canvas no matter what you have to do. [Tatanka lifts him up and goes for a pumphandle, but Sho falls behind and hits a HANGMANS NOOSE NECKBREAKER!!!]
Jim: Oh what a move, and there's the cover again...ONE!! TWO!!! Shoulder up by
Tatanka!!! Shoulder was up.
Jesse: That was a great counter by Sho, and even if you don't like him you have to give the man some credit here.
Jim: Sho waits for Tatanka to make it up, and he runs to the ropes. [Tatanka runs to the opposite side, and they both connect with a double clothesline.] Jim: OH AND BOTH MEN ARE DOWN!!!! Jesse: The impact on that was pretty damn hard. I don't think these two are going to be able to get up.
Jim: Folks if this is a draw then that means that Mikey will go onto the finals without wrestling. He'll be as fresh as a daisy.
Jesse: A daisy? Jim: Wait a minute I can see Tatanka start to move again, Sho has rolled over to the ropes. Tatanka is up now...Sho charges him, and SPINNING SPINEBUSTER
SLAM!!! Jesse: Tatanka planted him!!! Jim: Cover!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!!! Kickout at the last second by Sho. He gets up and walks right into a hiptoss. Sho is up again near the turnbuckle, and
Tatanka charges with a shoulder.
Jesse: Oh Funaki with a leapfrog, and Tatanka hits shoulder first!!! Jim: Sho from behind with a sunset flip roll-up!!! ONE!! TWO!!! Rollout by
Tatanka, and he has both of Sho's legs!
[Tatanka slingshots Sho 3/4 the distance across the ring, and Fuanki eats the turnbuckle. He struggles to make it back to his feet and Tatanka locks in a
Full Nelson.]
Jim: Dragon Suplex!!! NO SHO LANDS ON HIS FEET!!!!! Jesse: How did he do that? [Tatanka turns around and Sho with a HUGE SUPERKICK to the jaw, and Tatanka goes down. Sho goes for the cover, this time with a hand full of tights...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TATANKA POWERS OUT!!!! Jesse: This has been a great match!! [Sho sets up Tatanka again, but he misses the superkick. Tatanka kicks him in the stomach, and puts him between his legs. He's going for the Trio-Driver....]
Jim: Can he hit all three piledrivers?? Jesse: If he does we see him move onto Mikey! Jim: NO!!! Sho grabs the legs and flips over him for the cover!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! Tatanka with a bridge breaking the count, and with great power turns himself around. He's got Sho locked...Side Salto Suplex!!!
[Sho is down, and Tatanka goes to bring him up. Sho with a kick to the midsection, and another kick to the midsection. Sho kips up, runs to the ropes and takes Tatanka down with an axe-kick to the back of the neck.]
Jim: What quickness by Sho!!! Now he's going up top..."WHOLE F'n SHOW"!!!!! He rolls him over to make the cover...
Jesse: And TAKA is holding Tatanka's leg down by the apron! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Sho Funaki!!!! Jim: Wow!! Sho pulled that one off with a little help from TAKA. So now Sho will go on to face Mikey Whipwreck. That should be an amazing match.
Jesse: That's like Lakers vs. Kings...the real finals will be right there. Jim: Heh, yeah no doubt about that. Did the Nets even play in the finals or did they just not show up?
Jesse: Don't get me started. Jim: Folks right now we're going to take you to comments from Steve Armstrong.... [Camera cuts to Steve Armstrong who is standing in the back with Michael Cole.] Michael: Thank you Jim and Jess, right now standing next to me is a man that many think won't even get past the first round. Steve Armstrong what do you have to say about that?
Steve: Listen Cole, I don't care what you think. I don't care what the people out there think, because I know that I'm the best damn thing going in pro-wrestling today. I have yet to lose a match...I AM UNDEFEATED!!! You forget that I beat Ric Flair, you forget that I've beaten Raven....you forget that every single time I've been on TV here in the XCW...I have won. Tonight it's not going to be any damn different, and this XCW World Title is going back to
GA!!!! [Steve walks off leaving Michael looking like a total fool. Camera cuts back to the ring...]
