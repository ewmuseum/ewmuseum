XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 4)

July 24, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We comeback and Shane Douglas is in the back preparing for his match. All of a sudden there's a knock at the door.]
Man: Ya get a special delivery. Shane: AHAH you think I'm going to take this? This is Steve Austin inside here waiting for me to open it and kick my ass.
Man: Just pay me. Shane: You're joking...look. [Shane takes a chair and starts to beat the cardboard box like crazy. All of a sudden he starts to her screams of a women. He opens the cardboard box, and
Francine is in there...]
Shane: DAMNIT!!!! [Austin walks in pushes the delivery boy out of the way and attacks Shane
Douglas.] Steve: Happy Birthday you sonovabitch!!!! [Steve grabs him and throws him right into the mirror. It cracks in a thousand pieces.] Steve: Bad luck for you huh? [Austin starts stomping away on him. Steve Austin spits on him, and leaves...camera cuts back to Joey and Joel.]
Joey: I can't imagine Steve Austin being pissed at me. Joel: Me nither. The man is like Batman. He can do anything he wants to anyone, and not get caught.
Joey: You're right about that. [Eddie paces around the ring for awhile, before he and Cena lock up.] Joey: Eddie backs him into the corner, and a punch to the face brings him down. Joel: Eddie is going to cheat as much as he can to win this match. Joey: This man is without a doubt the dirtiest player in the game today. [Eddie starts to stomp away, and he's using the kicks more and more. He lifts
Cena's limp body and sets him in the tree of woe.]
Joey: Eddie walks to the other corner, and then runs at Cena. OH MAN!!! A dropkick to the face of John Cena. Cena holds his nose in pain, but this doesn't stop Eddie. Eddie unhooks him.
Joel: John Cena is showing his green tonight. The man is still a rookie, and everyone is expecting him to be awesome. He's just another new face...boring.
Joey: Eddie unhooks him, and he whips him into the ropes. Oh and Cena bounces out with drives a flying forearm into the face of Eddie. Eddie goes down, but gets right back up...and Cena with ANOTHER Flying Forearm.
Joel: Two times...if he does it a third he's a rookie. Joey: Eddie up once again holding his mouth, and Cena goes for another. Eddie ducks it though, and Cena hits the ropes.
[Cena bounces out, and Eddie catches him in a Tornado DDT. Eddie stands up and laughs before he covers...]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! No...somehow Cena gets a shouler up. Joel: And Eddie goes back to the drawing board, with an Irish Whip. Joey: Cena comesback, and Eddie with a dropkick to the knee. Joel: You know I was in the back watching Cena get ready for his match, and he listens to tapes of Richard Simmons...
Joey: He does not!! Joel: Does too!!!! I'm telling you the man likes Johnny Mathis!!! Joey: He does not!!! Eddie stomping on that leg, and now he's going to the outside. Wait a minute, he's getting a chair. Eddie climbs back in and sets the chair in the middle of the ring.
[Eddie goes back over to Cena, and perches him on the top turnbuckle.] Joey: WHAT IS EDDIE THINKING? Joel: He's going up top..and he gets behind Cena. LOOK AT CENA SMILE!!!! Joey: That's not a smile, that pain. Eddie jumps...OH MY GOD!!!! He hits a
Hurrcanarana...and the back of Cena's head hit the unfolded chair. This could be it......ONE!!! TWO!!!
Joel: HAHA Joey: And Eddie lifts up Cena before the 3 count. Why would you do that? The man has already proven that he can beat John Cena, but he wants to send a message?
[Eddie grabs Cena and throws him to the outside. Eddie then goes out after him.] Joey: Cena must not be feeling well tonight because he hasn't shown any offense at all. Joel: Maybe he has a crush on Eddie, and he doesn't want to hurt him. Joey: Eddie whips Cena into the guardrail, but Cena reverses it. He grabs him by the hair, and throws him back in.
Joel: The mullet. Joey: Huh? Joel: He tossed him in by the mullet. [Cena slides into the ring...he plants a boot into the stomach. SPINNING POWERBOMB!!!] Joey: NO WAY!!! ONE!!! TWO!! THREE!!!! Joel: What just happened? Joey: I can't believe it. He turned it around in a matter of seconds...Cena is amazing. FINK: Here is your winner...John Cena!!!! [Cena rolls out of the ring with his title, and the camera cuts to a man wearing a mask in the 1st row.]
Joey: AND JUSHIN LIGER IS HERE!!!! Joel: He's clapping for Cena. Joey: Jushin Liger is giving Cena a standing ovation. [Eddie rolls out of the ring, and he pushes Liger. Liger quickly responds with a palm thrust, and another palm thrust. Eddie is staggering, and Liger hops over the guardrail and nails him with a kick. Eddie falls down, and Liger kicks him a few times and walks away.]
Joel: What just happened? Joey: Don't you ever pay attention? Joel: I just can't believe what I just saw. Did Liger just come in and mame him? Joey: Yes. Joel: Whoa. [Camera cuts to the back and Chad Costello is walking to the ring.] Joey: I guess we're going to hear from Chad...stay with us! [Commercial.]
