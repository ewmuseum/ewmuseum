XCW New Years Brawl (part 6)

January 21, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans this next match is going to be so hot. Jake Roberts has been tormenting Terry Funk, and his family for days and weeks now. He's put Viki into a neckbrace, and Terry into a fit of rage...
Paul: I have never seen someone like Jake have such a disreguard for another humans life. Jim: Well Terry Funk is looking to get some revenge tonight!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall, and is a
Barbed Wire Match. First coming down to the ring...weighing in at 250 pounds.
He is from Amarillo, Texas...here is "The Hardcore Legend" Terry Funk!!! A ONLINE 6.0\IDB\Diction.ind ["Simple Man" by Lynard Skynard starts to play and the crowd goes wild. He comes out to a huge ovation, and he walks over and hugs his wife before climbing into the ring. He raises his arm in the air one more time, and the crowd gives him the biggest pop of the night.]
FINK: And his opponent....from Stone Mountain, GA. He weighs in at 253 pounds. Here is Jake "The Snake" Roberts!!! [Jake's music starts to play and he comes out with that famous sneer on his face. He doesn't have a snake in his bag due to the fact that Terry burned it this past Friday. He walks over to Terry's wife and just laughs, and then climbs into the ring.]
<Roberts stands outside the ring for a moment before diving in under the bottom rope, but before the bell can even sound, Terry Funk pushes past the referee and begins roughly putting his boot into the top of Jake's head and the back of his neck.>
Jim Ross: Well would ya look at this! Paul Roma: That wily old bastard, Funk will do anything to get ahead! Look at him! He didn't even give poor Jake Roberts a chance to get into the damned ring.
Jim Ross: After all that Jake's done to Terry and his poor wife Vikki, he deserves everything he gets in this match. Go get him, Funk!
<Terry bends down and grabs two big handfuls of Robert's hair and tugs him back to his feet. All the while spitting and spatting curses at Jake, Terry emphisizes each and every one with hard right hands to the head.>
Paul Roma: I'm surprised Funk even agreed to do this matchup, JR. This could very well be the end of his pathetic career.
Jim Ross: This one could very well be the end of both of these great competitors' careers! A barbwire rope match has got to be one of the most brutal and gruesome matches in the game.
Paul Roma: Yea, but look at Funk! That guys been hammered with so many steel chair shots I don't think he knows what the hell he signed up for.
Jim Ross: I'll tell you one thing Terry Funk does know, Paul.. he knows how to wrestle and he knows how to fight. And right now, he's really putting it to
Jake Roberts.
<Funk grabs another big handful of Robert's hair and tries to irish whip him into the deadly ropes, but Robert's puts on the breaks and tries to counter instead..>
Paul Roma: Look at this! Funk is about to taste some barbwire, baby! <Somehow Funk manages to gain the leverage back and again tries to send Roberts in instead, but Jake puts on the breaks and uses his power to pull Funk full speed into a vicious short arm clothesline instead.>
Jim Ross: Ooooh my! Paul Roma: Did you see that, Ross? Roberts just about took Funk's head off. Jim Ross: We've seen this before, Paul. Jake normally hits the clotheslines as a prelude to his DDT.. and look at this! Sure enough the Snake is signaling for it!
Paul Roma: I don't think we've even reached the two minutes mark and Jake is gonna end this thing!
<crowd pops> Paul Roma: WHAT?! Jim Ross: Terry Funk with a low blow! Paul Roma: Cheater! <Roberts reels over in pain after getting a vicious uppercut to his family jewels. Funk quickly gets back to his feet and whips the stunned Snake right into the barbwire ropes!>
Jim Ross: There it is! Roberts is the first one to taste that cold unforgiving barbwire and would you look at his face! Oooh man!
Paul Roma: Funk just impaled Jake on the ropes! Look at his back! <Roberts tries to no avail to pull himself off of the barbwire.. but he's stuck!> Jim Ross: I don't think Jake can get himself off the ropes! Paul Roma: Well that sore sport Terry Funk sure isn't helping any! After that
Jake has done for him, Funk's just pounding on him!
Jim Ross: What in the world are you talking about, Paul?! Terry's the one that helped Jake quit drinking! ..and for all of his troubles all he got was a
DDT. <With Jake still stuck to the barbwire, Terry gives him a few more kicks before rolling onto the outside of the ring and grabbing a steel chair!>
Jim Ross: Funk's got a chair and I think it's safe to say that thing has just picked up! Paul Roma: A chair?! Isn't it bad enough the whole damn ring is surrounded in barbwire?! Do we really need any more weapons?!
Jim Ross: Here comes Funk! <Funk charges in for a running chair shot, but no luck! Jake was playing opposum! Roberts leaps into the air and hits a picture perfect standing dropkick that sends the chair right back into Terry's face!>
Paul Roma: Genius! Pure genius! <Roberts winces and takes a look at his ripped up back for a moment.. he then growls and drives a few stomps of his own into the downed body of Terry Funk.>
Jim Ross: That was quite an impressive dropkick by the Snake, but I think the damage may have already been done. Roberts back looks like hamburger meat.
Paul Roma: Jake the Snake isn't done yet, Ross! Not by a long shot. Jim Ross: He's bringing Funk back up to his feet annnnnd Paul Roma: Snap suplex! <Roberts grabs the slightly dented steel chair and begins to wedge it into the corner turnbuckle.>
Jim Ross: Uh oh! Paul Roma: Hahah! This is great! Jim Ross: Jake now pulling Terry back up to his feet and here he goes! <Jake irish whips Terry right into the corner with the steel chair wedged into it. Funk hits hair and comes stumbling out in a total daze.>
Paul Roma: Kick to the gut.. swinging neckbreaker! Beautiful! Jim Ross: That was a vicious neckbreaker. Jake now hooks the leg. Paul Roma: One! Two! Jim Ross: Funk kicked out! Paul Roma: What?! Impossible. Jim Ross: I've been in this business a long time, Paul, and lemme tell you - there are few men in this sport as tough as Terry Funk. This guy is as hard as nails.
<Roberts hauls Terry back to his feet and scoop slams him down.> Paul Roma: Funk maybe a tough character, Ross, but lets face it. Nobody in this business is as methodical and calculated as Jake the Snake Roberts.
Jim Ross: I'm not taking anything away from Jake. Paul Roma: Roberts drives a hard knee right into Terry's chest! <Again Roberts hauls Terry back to his feet and again he sets him up for what looks like a suplex.>
Jim Ross: Look at that power! Paul Roma: Haha! Excellent! Roberts is just holding Funk up in that beautiful vertical suplex letting all Funk's blood rush right to his swollen old head of his.
Jim Ross: Wait a minute.. what the hell is Robert's doing?! Paul Roma: Whoa! Jim Ross: Oh God NO! <crowd winces> Jim Ross: NO! <Instead of dropping back into the suplex, Roberts drops Terry gut first onto the top barbwire rope!>
Paul Roma: Oh that was great! Look at Funk! Jim Ross: Poor Terry Funk has just been literally impaled stomach first onto the top barbwire rope!
Paul Roma: Roberts isn't done yet, JR. Not by a long shot.. look at this! <Jake comes in fast and drives a high knee lift right into the face of Terry
Funk. The force of the knee shot sends Funk sprawling off of the top rope and all the way onto the cold concrete floor below.>
Jim Ross: This is too much. I don't think anybody expected to see these two guys tear into one another like they have been.
<Roberts rolls onto the outside and stomps Terry for awhile before looking under the ring and pulling out a table.>
Paul Roma: I think it's pretty obvious Jake is looking to finally end the worthless career of Terry Funk.
Jim Ross: Roberts now slides one table into the ring, but he's not done! He's taking another one out!
Paul Roma: And look at this! He's bridging it between the outside ring apron and the steel guard railing!
Jim Ross: Come on Terry! Get out of there! <With the table setup, Terry reaches down to grab Terry Funk, but Funk fires back with a gut shot! And another.. and another.. and another! Jake stops his momentum with a quick headbutt though.>
Paul Roma: Jake is gonna powerbomb him through the table! Jim Ross: He's got Funk up! <crowd pops> Jim Ross: Hurricanrana! Paul Roma: WHAT?!? Jim Ross: That wily old bastard, Terry Funk just pulled a hurricanrana right out of his you know what, Paul!
Paul Roma: How in the hell did he do that?! <The crowd continues to cheer like crazy as Terry Funk slowly begins to pull himself back to his feet. Roberts is close behind though.>
Jim Ross: Come on, Terry! <Jake, using the table that he bridged earlier, hauls himself back up to his feet. But before Roberts can get his barrings straight, Funk comes in hard and fast and clotheslines him right on top of the table! Funk that holds Jake down by his hair and begins to pound vicious right hands right into his face!>
Paul Roma: Look at him go! Get in there!
Jim Ross: Incase you haven't noticed, Paul, anything goes. And right now Funk is pounding Roberts like all hell. Funk's not done yet, he's climbing back into the ring and.. oooh no! He's going up to the top!
Paul Roma: What in the hell does he think he's doing?! Jim Ross: Terry Funk is climbing up to the top turnbuckle! With Jake Roberts out cold on a table about ten feet below, I think Terry Funk is about to blow the roof off this place! These fans are going wild!
<Funk looks over the crowd for a long moment before lunging off the top rope with a beautiful senton splash! Funk comes down and nails Roberts - sending both men crashing through the bridged table much to the excitement of the crowd.>
Jim Ross: He did it! Paul Roma: I don't believe this! Jim Ross: Believe it? ..it just happened! <Both men lay motionless.> Jim Ross: But from the looks of the wreckage, I think both of these guys maybe done. Paul Roma: What in the hell would possess anyone to throw himself off the top rope through a table?
Jim Ross: These fans love him for it. <Finally Funk starts to drag himself to his feet. Using the outside ring apron, Funk drags himself up and crawls under the bottom barbwire rope and inside. Jake meanwhile sits up and then falls right back down.>
Paul Roma: Come on, Jake! Get the hell back up and finish that geezer off! Jim Ross: Funk is back inside the ring and it looks like he's setting that second table up against the turnbuckles.
<While Funk leans the table up against the corner turnbuckle, Roberts somehow manages to get himself back onto the outside ring apron.>
Jim Ross: Terry now charges back in at Jake and - <crowd winces> Paul Roma: YES! Jim Ross: Ouch! <Jake ducks a big right hand, grabs both of Funk's ears, and then drops down off of the ring apron - thus gagging Terry Funk throat first onto the top barbwire rope! Funk snaps back, holding his throat and coughing up blood.>
Paul Roma: Beautiful!
Jim Ross: Roberts now looking under the ring and.. ooh man! Here comes the toys! <Jake throws in a trash can, a few more chairs, and then pulls out a ladder!>
Paul Roma: This is gonna be great!
Jim Ross: This is gonna be bloody! <Roberts slides back into the ring and quickly knocks the stumbling Terry Funk right off his feet with a vicious trash can lid shot right between the eyes.>
Paul Roma: Roberts hook the leg again!
Jim Ross: ONE! TWO! Paul Roma: THREE! Jim Ross: Roberts did it?! Paul Roma: Of course he did! Jake Roberts has just - Jim Ross: No he didn't! Paul Roma: What the hell are you blabbering about, JR?! Jim Ross: Somehow, someway, Funk kicked out! Look at the referee! Paul Roma: I don't believe it! Jim Ross: Neither does Jake! <Roberts gets back to his feet and immediately starts arguing with the referee.> Paul Roma: This is a bunch of bullshit.. Roberts had him, Ross! Jim Ross: No matter what you or the Snake has to say, this thing continues! And if Jake wants to keep on going, he better turn himself around! Funk is back up! <The crowd starts to get louder and louder as Funk turns Jake around and - NO! Roberts turns, kicks Funk right in the gut, and nails him with his DDT!> <crowd winces> Paul Roma: There it is! Out of nowhere! Roberts hit the DDT! Jim Ross: Jake nailed Funk right out of the blue with it! Paul Roma: Well now you could count to a freakin' hundred.. come on and end this thing, Jake.
Jim Ross: Roberts makes the cover againd and I think this thing is pretty much academic. Paul Roma: ONE! Jim Ross: Two.. Paul Roma: TWO! Jim Ross: Funk kicked out! Paul Roma: (speechless) Jim Ross: FUNK KICKED OUT! <Roberts just stares in disbelief at the referee and then down at Terry Funk.>
Jim Ross: Funk just kicked out of Roberts' DDT!
<Jake throws Funk's shoulder back down to the mat and again makes the cover.>
Paul Roma: One.. two..
Jim Ross: Funk kicks out again! <Roberts snaps. He begins hammering fists of fury right between Funk's eyes. He then grabs a big handful of his hair and hauls Funk back up to his feet.>
Paul Roma: Roberts with an irish whip annnnd - YES! Eat that Funk!
Jim Ross: Terry again tasting cold barbwire! Paul Roma: And now Jake has got himself a steel chair, baby! <Terry screams out in pain as the barbwire sinks into his flesh. Roberts then comes in fast and crowns Funk right over the top of his head with the steel chair.. and again!>
Jim Ross: Oooh Man! Jake is literally trying to beat the body of Terry Funk off of that barbwire with a steel chair!
<CLaNG! CLaNG! Again and again Roberts sends the chair into Funk's head.>
Paul Roma: This is great!
Jim Ross: This is disgusting! <Funk finally falls off the barbwire and lands on his knees. His face, covered in his own blood, looks up right at Jake. The crowd goes crazy as Funk points at Roberts and then signals him to hit him again!>
Paul Roma: Stupid bastard.. do it, Jake! Do it! Jim Ross: Roberts is just looking down in amazement.. what can you do, Jake? What can you do to try and keep this old fighter down? Paul Roma: Hit him again, damnit! Hit him again! Jim Ross: Roberts hoists that chair up! <Funk suddenly lets out a vicious battle cry and slams his head right into
Jake's groin!> <crowd winces and then pops> Paul Roma: That bastard! That's the second time tonight he's hit a low blow! Jim Ross: Jake is keeling over and.. look at this! Funk's a bloody mess, but he's back to his feet and he's setting Jake up.. this could be.. YES!
Paul Roma: Ough! Jim Ross: PILE DRIVER! <Robert's body cripples onto the mat as he's driven head first onto a steel chair with a pile driver. Funk gets back up his feet and looks over the crowd while wiping blood off of his face.>
Paul Roma: This is insane.. Roberts should have already won this damn thing! Jim Ross: Funk's getting the ladder! ..and he's setting it up! Paul Roma: What the hell is this old fool gonna try this time? Jim Ross: It's setup! <With Roberts out cold, Terry begins to scale the ladder.> Paul Roma: I can't even watch! Jim Ross: Funk's signaling for it! He's gonna go for his moonsault off the top of that ladder!
Paul Roma: No way! NO WAY! <crowd gets louder> Jim Ross: Here he comes! <crowd suddenly winces> Jim Ross: Oooh no! Paul Roma: Oooh yes! Roberts just kicked that ladder and look at it! It's gonna topple for sure!
Jim Ross: Hold on Terry! <The ladder weebles back and forth, back and forth. Terry holds on for dear life, but finally.. once Roberts gets back to his feet, he again drills a big boot into the ladder! Funk flies off and lands stomach first onto the top rope!>
<crowd winces> Paul Roma: YES! Jim Ross: OH MY GOD! Paul Roma: That's it! Jake Roberts has just KILLED Terry Funk! Jim Ross: TERRY FUNK HAS JUST FALLEN OFF THE TOP OF THAT LADDER! ..AND THEN LANDED ONTO THE BARBWIRE ROPES! Paul Roma: Great, isn't it?! <With Funk dangling from the top rope, Roberts grabs the trash can and slams it right onto the back of his head! Roberts then grabs him by his blood soaked hair and rips him off the barbwire and throws him back down onto the mat.>
Jim Ross: This may have gone too far, Paul.. I think Funk may have serious internal bleeding after that fall.
Paul Roma: May have?! MAY HAVE?! Funk is dead, JR! Roberts just ended that pathetic bastard!
Jim Ross: Terry isn't moving and Jake meanwhile is just pacing around his body.. <Roberts smiles a sadistic grin before lifting Funk back up to his feet.>
Jim Ross: Ooh no!
<Roberts yanks Funk forward for his patented short arm clothesline, but, somehow, Funk ducks right under it!>
Jim Ross: What?! Paul Roma: No way! Jim Ross: DDT! <crowd goes INSANE> Jim Ross: Funk just hit Roberts with his own finisher! Paul Roma: IMPOSSIBLE! Jim Ross: This place is gonna explode! Terry Funk has just somehow, someway, found the strength to fight back!
Paul Roma: NO! <Funk gets back to his feet and looks over the jam pakced audience. He then signals for it again!>
Jim Ross: Wait a minute.. look at this! Funk pulls Jake back up and - <HUGE POP> Jim Ross: ANOTHER DDT! Paul Roma: STOP IT! These damn fans are cheering so loud I don't think Jake can hear me shout my words of encouragement! Come on, Snake!
Jim Ross: Funk is again pulling Roberts back up and.. YES! He hooks Jake up for another! <With Jake ready for yet another DDT, Funk drags him over to where the dented trash can is and - WHaM! Again he plants Roberts head first down with his own finisher!>
Jim Ross: That's three! That's three DDTs! Paul Roma: This can't be happening.. Jim Ross (crowd counts along): ONE ! ! ! Paul Roma: NO! GET UP, JAKE! GET UP! Jim Ross (crowd counts along): TWO ! ! ! Paul Roma: NOOOOOO! ! ! ! Jim Ross (crowd counts along): T H R E E ! ! ! ! Jim Ross: Terry Funk did it! Terry Funk has defeated Jake the Snake! <With his theme music blaring, Terry Funk rolls back to his knees and looks over the crowd. His wife Vikki dives over the guard railing and charges into the ring, the two quickly hug.>
Jim Ross: What a match! What a night! THE FINK: Here is your winner.. TERRRRRRRRRY FUUUUUUUNK! ! ! ! (crowd continues to go insane) <As Terry and his wife embrace inside the ring, the crowd suddenly pops even louder - Cactus Jack is making his way down to the square circle.>
A ONLINE 6.0\IDB\Diction.ind Jim Ross: WOW! Paul Roma: What in the hell is Jack doing here?! Jim Ross: The hardcore legend, Cactus Jack is in the house! <Jack makes his way into the ring and him and Funk look one eye to eye for a long moment. Suddenly the two men give a big smile and huge.. again the crowd is on their feet.>
Jim: Terry Funk has taken this match, and what a way to go out tonight. Paul: I'm shocked...
