Here's the Story...

May 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's the story... I'm sitting here collecting money from a commercial, I'm taking as many classes as I can...I'm even writing, but the fact is that I have this itch. Ya know the itch that won't freakin' go away. The itch that you want to go away, but it just won't?
Well it's gotten to me, and I have free time. I don't know how many of you are willing to jump back onto the XCW Train, but even if you're remotely interested let me know.
I got some critisim for the last XCW because I was a little pushy, and I have to agree that I was pretty annoying. The fact is though people were having fun in the XCW, and people were actually into Real Wrestling feds for a little bit.
If only for a brief moment. BigHort was, Blottie was, even Pillow was....you guys were all into it at one point, and now I'm not saying that I want to bring it back so it can rule all.
I want to bring it back so we can all have fun again. I've been sitting back looking at the RSPWF, and I've been watching wrestling...and man it's in the saddest case I've seen right now. The feds here are great, but there is something missing. WWE plain ol' sucks right now, and it's not fun. Wrestling is not fun, and I want to give the people an option to have that fun again.
I want you to MARK out... I want you to LOVE wrestling again.... So I'm going to start the XCW again. 1 card a week, no real pressure to Rp...just a chance for you to have fun. If you want to know more about it feel free to e-mail me, feel free answer on this board. Whatever you want to do.
The summer is coming up, and I know a lot of you are busy...but this could be your chance to enjoy wrestling again.
Sorry for the novel... Talk to you all soon. - Chad
