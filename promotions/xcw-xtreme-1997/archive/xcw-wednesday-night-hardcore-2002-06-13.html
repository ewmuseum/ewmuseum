XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore

June 13, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Loser" by Beck starts to play and Mikey Whipwreck comes out to a huge ovation. He's slapping hands, he's smiling, and he even does the Hogan Ear thing which makes everyone groan. He slides into the ring...]
Mikey: Fans...a lot of people have been wondering if my heart is still in wrestling. I'd have to admit that I've been distracted, I haven't been myself.
I haven't even been a shadow of myself, and then the other night I got a phone call. That phone call was not just any phone call, but a phone call from a man named "The Miz". I know you all know him...Mike from the Real World.
(Crowd boos) Mikey: Yes the Miz...he called me and told me to fight for all the little fella's out there, and to review the tapes of the matches I've been in. Sure enough something's been missing. I have been missing, and tonight...I comeback!!!
[All of a sudden from behind Mikey comes Roddy Piper. Piper limps into the ring and smashes a chair over the head of Whipwreck...Piper continues to beat him up until Dreamer makes the save.]
Jim: Fans Piper is taking it to Whipwreck a little early!!!! Joel: Well he doesn't want to wait for the PPV.... [Piper hobbles out of the ring, and Dreamer is checking on Whipwreck. We cut to the intro for Hardcore...]
Jim: Fans WELCOME TO HARDCORE!!!!! We are just 5 days away from the first XCW
Pay-Per View, and what an event it's going to be. Live from Safeco Field!!!
Joel: What the hell is Mikey Whipwreck talking to the "Miz" for? That's his damn problem. He's been hanging out with freakin' geeks!!
Jim: Maybe he should hang out with you? Joel: I'm too busy for losers like that. [Camera cuts to the locker room and Arn Anderson is on his cell phone. He's talking to some unknown, but seems to be very happy...]
Arn: Yeah...mmhmm...well as long as you're here before I make my announcement. Yeah, I don't care. Whatever you have to do, I mean it's your business. You have to do what you have to do, but so do I. So I'm expecting you here in a few...I don't care if Flair and Hall are after me. You just need to watch my back! See you soon!
[Arn hangs up the phone, and Hall walks up to him.] Scott: *Hiccup* Hey Arnie Arn! Arn: ??? Scott: You think fo - *hiccup* for one damn minute that you're going to stop me? The Bad *burp*...the bad guy? Razor Ramon?
(Hall reaches behind his ear....) Scott: Wait a minute...*hiccup* where's my toothpick? Arn: You're drunk again? You diry son of a bitch...get out of here. Scott: No no...I'm not drunk. I'm acting...yeah I'm acting. Heh!!! Arn: You do me a favor..you tell Flair that I want to meet him in the center of the ring tonight to tell him a few things. Got it?
Scott: Arnie...don't worry. I got everything under control. [Hall staggers off and Arn shakes his head is disgust. Camera cuts back to Jim and Joel...]
Jim: And Scott Hall doesn't look to good. Joel: Well what do you expect...he's preparing for an audtion. Jim: So he's not drunk? Joel: Nah..he's just a damn good actor. He's going to play Ben Affleck in a movie about his life.
Jim: Oh stop it. Sum Of All Fears has been a huge success for him! Joel: Don't go see that movie....it's horrible. I lay bigger farts than the explosion they showed in that movie.
Jim: I'm sure you do. FINK: Ladies and gentlement the following match is a tag team match set for one fall. Introducing first, here is the team of Sean O'Haire and Prototype!!!
[Both men walk out to huge heel heat.] FINK: And their opponents...at a total combined weight of 430 pounds. Here is the team of Rob Van Dam & Sabu!!!
[Huge pop as they enter the ring.] [RVD walks up to Prototype and tells him that they're going to be starting the match in no uncertain terms. RVD with a side kick to the stomach, and follows up with a jumping ax kick.]
Jim: And RVD wants to get this over ASAP, and he goes for the cover. Only a one count though, and this should be a great tag team match here tonight.
Joel: I hate RVD...the man just thinks he's so damn good. Makes me wanna puke all over you.
Jim: Please refrain. [Both men back to their feet, and RVD hits a kick to the side of the head, and then a spinning back kick. Van Dam runs to the ropes, leaps off the middle on and hits a Springboard Moonsault.]
Jim: What a great move there, and he hooks the leg. Prototype kicks out once again, this time by two.
Joel: RVD this, RVD that...Prototype is the future of wrestling. There is not a flaw on the man, and he can do much more damage than RVD can.
Jim: Van Dam whips him into the turnbuckle, and Rob charges in with a dropkick. [He pulls Prototype out of the turnbuckle, and goes for a suplex. Prototype slips down the back of Van Dam and hits a Death Drop DDT. Prototype brings Van
Dam to his feet and hits an inverted atomic drop, and he tags out to Sean
O'Haire.]
Jim: O'Haire comes in, this man is huge. The man is a monster...oh and he levels Van Dam with a kneeling right fist, and another one.
Joel: I love this man...not a lot of talent, but I love him nonetheless. Jim: Van Dam on his back, and he hits a spin kick to the stomach. Rob runs to the ropes, and Sabu slaps him on the back.
Joel: I don't think Van Dam noticed. Jim: Sabu is going up top...Rob with a nice sommersault legdrop. He's going for the cover...but the ref isn't counting.
[RVD see's Sabu at the top and looks a little confused. Sabu jumps down off the turnbuckle and starts yelling at Van Dam. O'Haire from behind with a double head smash, and then brings Sabu up...he whips Sabu into the ropes, and lifts him up in a Gorilla Press.]
Jim: Look at that raw power. Joel: Gotta love it! Jim: Sabu goes down to the canvas hard, and Sean just smiles. Sean brings Sabu back up and hits a Gutwrench Powerbomb. He's going for the cover now...and 1!!!
2!!! No, Sabu kicks out. Joel: That was close. Jim: Folks RVD and Sabu will be facing Al Snow and Chris Jericho at the PPV for the World Tag Titles. What a Ladder Match that is going to be.
Joel: I start to drool when I think about it. Jim: Sabu is up now and O'Haire leaps over him for a Sunset Flip! Joel: Beautiful! Jim: 1!!! 2!!! Sabu rolls out of it, pops back to his feet...OH MY GOD!!! Sean almost took Sabu's head off with that clothesline.
[O'Haire tags out to Prototype. O'Haire brings Sabu back up, and the send him to the ropes. Double clothesline, followed by a Double DDT....]
Jim: Great team work. Prototype lifts Sabu up, and he goes for a powerbomb. Sabu turns it into a hurrcanrana though, and Prototype goes down. Joel: But he gets right back up. How do ya like that? Jim: Prototype is up, and Sabu rolls over to his corner. Tag made, and RVD is in. [Van Dam leaps over the ropes and catches Prototype with a kick to the chest. Van Dam whips him into the ropes, and levels him with a jumping forearm smash to the side of the face. He jumps to the top ropes...]
Jim: Frog Spla....OH GOD!!!! Al Snow from the crowd pushes Van Dam off the turnbuckle, and he goes flying to the outside of the ring.
Joel: And the ref has called for the bell. Jim: Jericho is in the ring now, and he's got a ladder. He's swinging that ladder every which way he can, and he levels Prototype with it. Sabu comes in and he gets hit with the ladder!!!
Joel: NICE!!!! [Jericho sets the ladder up against the turnbuckle, and Al Snow sends Sabu into
Chris.] Jim: Jericho catches him...HOT SHOT ONTO THE LADDER!!!!! Joel: WOW!!! Jim: RVD is back in though, and he's got that chair. [Van Dam throws the chair to Snow...] Jim: VAN DAMINATOR!!!!!! Joel: Damnit! [RVD with a legsweep on the charging Chris Jericho, and Sabu grabs the ladder and throws in onto the stomach of Chris.]
Jim: Sabu and RVD are leaving now, and what a match that's going to be. Joel: Someone is going to get killed in the match!! [Camera cuts the office of Arn Anderson. Triple H and Scott Steiner walk in with very cocky looks on their faces. Arn is obviously very upset with the both of them....]
Arn: I'm going to advise the both of you so you'd better sit down, and shut the hell up. You'd better take what I say into consideration, got it?
(Both of them look at each other and laugh...) Triple H: Why did you call us in here again? Arn: I'll tell you why, I'm not going to stand for the kind of crap that you pulled last week. You damn near took out two men who are in the XCW World Title
Tournament. Cactus Jack is still hurting, and in the hosptial....you sure as hell aren't going to get away with this while I'm the commissioner.
Scott: Is that so? Who says we don't corner you right now, and send you to the hostpial with Cactus Jack, and then we go out and finish the jobs we started last week?
Arn: Because if you do, I'll have plenty of ground to fire both of your asses and send you to a place called the WWA. Damnit, you outta be polishing my shoes right now, thanking me that I just don't fire you after what you did last week.
Triple H: Well then, if you're so pissed off...why haven't you fired us yet? Arn: Cause I have something better in mind. I'd like to see you both get your asses handed to you...not tonight because I have other things to worry about, but next Wednesday....Scotty....I think putting you against Sandman and Tommy
Dreamer in a handi-cap match is fitting.
Scott: WHAT? Arn: And Triple H...if you happen to not win the World Title, I think you'll do a fine job of being my bitch for a night.
Triple H: You can't do that!!!! Arn: I'll do whatever I damn well please...as a matter of fact, if you two screw up again I'll just throw you both in the ring against each other. Get out of my office!!!!
[Scott and Hunter leave with very pissed off attitudes....camera cuts to a commercial.]
