XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 3)

July 24, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["OH MY GOD...IT'S...EVIL!!!" is heard all over the Compaq Center, and out walks Sho. He looks like he's a man on a mission. He climbs into the ring and grabs the mic...he hands it to Bob, and gives him a piece of paper. Bob begins to speak in his heavy Japanese accent.]
Bob: I am disgraced that Mikey Whipwreck dare goes to Japan. How dare he even think about going to my homeland, and disgracing my life. I love my country, and for him to go and mess around with things...is horrid. You people see me as
EVIL, and I am....I am INDEED EVIL. As XCW World Champion, I think Mikey
Whipwreck should be banned from the X..C..W due to his inconsistant ways of fouling things up for me, and my heritage. Mikey if you a -
[Mikey Whipwreck comes in through the crowd and stands behind both of them making faces. He squints his eyes to make himself look Japanese, and then motions that Sho has a small "Pee-Pee" the crowd starts to laugh, and Sho doesn't know why. He turns around, and Mikey takes a picture of him. Sho clears his eyes quickly, kicks Sho in the stomach and hits the Whippersnapper. Fans are going wild....he gets up and Bob Kurimoto is like a scared little boy.
Mikey pulls a piece of paper out of his pants, and hands it to Bob.] Bob: Umm..Mikey Whipwreck wants me to read this. Hello, I am Mikey Whipwreck. I like dogs, and kites. I am -
Mikey: Read it. Bob: (Gulp) I am...going to beat Sho Funaki within an inch of his life at
Annihilation. I wil -
Mikey: Read this loud. Bob: I WILL....PEE ON THE JAPANESE FLAG!!!! (Crowd laughs. Bob starts to cry.) Bob: I WILL POO POO ON THE KAIENTAI NAME!!!! (Bob can't read anymore and Mikey gives him a hug. Mikey puts his arm around him, and Bob starts to feel better. Mikey then kicks him in the stomach and nails him with a Whippersnapper. Mikey grabs the mic..)
Mikey: Tonight I win my right to go on to face...uh Steve Armstrong. That's beside the point though. I'm out here tonight to let everyone know that Japan isn't a horrible place...it just sucks a little. Sho Funaki...while I was in
Japan I did make a few friends though. They're in the crowd tonight...wave Mr.
and Mrs. Funaki. [Camera cuts to Mr and Mrs Funaki and they have "Whipwreck" shirts on. They snap a few photos and wave.]
Mikey: The more fans I have the better. See you later tonight!!! [Mikey pulls his pants down and moons the crowd with boxers that he has on. On the back of the boxers there's a picture of the Red Sun of Japan, with Sho's face on it...."Loser" starts up and Mikey leaves the ring area.]
Joey: Wow...can you believe that? Joel: Mr and Mrs. Funaki are traitors to their own country. Joey: Well how can you not like Mikey Whipwreck better than Sho Funaki? Joel: WHAT!? Their his flesh and blood. Joey: So what? [Camera cuts to Chad Costello talking to security. All of a sudden a limo pulls up, and Chad screams with joy.]
Chad: THEY'RE HERE!!! WHOOOO HOOOOOO!!! THEY'RE HERE!!!!!!! [Camera cuts back to the ring.] FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall, and is for the XCW World Tag Team Titles. First coming down to the ring are the challengers. They weigh in at a total combined weight of 578 pounds. Here is the team of Ken Shamrock, and Kensuke Sasaki!!!
[Both men walk down to the ring with a mixed reaction. Kensuke is wearing his black and green attire with the face paint. Shamrock is wearing his blue tights, and gloves. They climb into the ring, and work the crowd.]
FINK: And their opponents..they are the current XCW World Tag Team Champions. Here is the team of Steve Corino and Simon Diamond, they are Old School!!!! [Both men walk out to no music, and they're wearing the old school tights with the matching kneepads. They climb into the ring and hand their titles to Earl
Hebner. The bell sounds...]
Joey: And it looks like Shamrock and Simon will start this match up. They lock up, and Shamrock takes the man in a side headlock. Diamond shoots him off into the ropes, takes him down with a shoulderblock.
Joel: Whoa no..Shamrock doesn't he stays right up. Joey: Diamond bounces back again, looking for another shoulder block...and once again, Shamrock doesn't go down.
Joel: He's like running into a brick wall. The man doesn't go down easy. [Diamond going for one more, and Shamrock annoyed just grabs him and lifts him high above his head with a Press Slam!!!]
Joey: Diamond pops up quick only to run into a scissor kick by Shamrock. There's a cover...ONE!!! TWO!! NO!!...Diamond out at two. Quick action here to start, and Shamrock works over the arm..before tagging in Sasaki.
Joel: I love this man!! [Sasaki comes in with a barrage of fists to the head. They back Simon into the ropes, where Kensuke goes for an Irish Whip.]
Joey: Reverse by Dimond, Sasaki comes back, and he walks into a boot to the stomach by Simon. Swinging neckbreaker takes the man down, and Simon tags to
Steve Corino.
Joel: Saski against Corino? Gee...who's going to win this one? Joey: Corino with a few boots to the back of the head. A whip into the ropes, and he catches him in a Sleeper....and he drops. That was like a Sleep Buster.
Corino with the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Sasaki up at two. [Corino makes a quick tag to Diamond, and he goes back to work.] Joel: The teamwork by Old School is what we needed in the XCW. Quick tags mean fresher guys.
Joey: Smart way of doing things. Joel: Simon Diamond lifts up Saski...IMPLANT DDT!!!! Cover...ONE!! TWO!!! Joey: And once again Sasaki kicks out. Diamond brings The Power Warrior back to his feet, and he hooks him...Vertical Suplex, and he goes for another cover.
Joel: AND ANOTHER KICK OUT!!! [Diamond brings Saski up and holds him open...he tags to Corino, who slingshots himself over the top with a hard punch to the kidneys.]
Joey: And Sasaki is in pain. Steve scoops him up, big bodyslam! Corino now up to the second rope, and he leaps off with a legdrop to the throat. Once again another cover by Corino. ONE!! TWO!!
Joel: And Saski kicks out again. Don't you think they'd know this by now? Joey: Corino drops a knee on Sasaki, and tags out to Diamond again. He comes in and lifts Kensuke...he whips him into the ropes and drives a knee to the gut.
Joel: Saski gets flipped, and he crashes to the mat. [Diamond brings him up once more, hooks him up, and a stiff backdrop suplex. Simon bridges up...] Joey: ONE!!! And Sasaki is quick to break the count. Joel: Kensuke will not lose to a bunch of American punks. Joey: Fallen brings Sasaki up, and he whips him into the ropes. He lifts up into the air...FLAPJACK!!!!
Joel: Oh man!!! Joey: Simon is about to go for the cover, but he says forget it. He tags out to
Corino, and he comes in. Snapmare by Steve, and then a stiff kick to the back!!!
Joel: Look at Shamrock's face. Does that look like man who can't wait to get into the ring or what?
[Corino whips him into the turnbuckle, and he follows in with a clothesline. Corino backs up and starts in with a series of clotheslines against the turnbuckle. Corino tags out to Diamond...]
Joey: Diamond comes in and he begins to pepper Saski with lefts and rights to the body. The ref with the 5 count, and Corino lifts Saski up. We've got a double team...
Joel: Oh what are they going to do? Joey: BELLY TO BELLY NECKBREAKER COMBO!!!!! Corino makes the cover, and here comes Shamrock to make the save. He saves Kensuke before the count even starts.
Shamrock out of the ring, and Corino shakes off his attack and brings Saski to his feet. Joel: OH MY!!! Joel: Big Inverted Atomic Drop by Corino.... [Saski doubles over, and Corino hooks the arms. Butterfly Suplex....but Kensuke puts on the breaks.]
Joey: AND LOOK AT THE POWER!!! He's using all his strength, and Corino is holding on..Kensuke falls backwards slamming Corino onto the canvas hard.
Joel: WHOA!!! Joey: Both men are down...who's going to get up first? Both men are starting to move, and wait a minute...WHAT THE HELL IS STAN HANSEN DOING?
Joel: Stan Hansen is here???? Joey: Corino begins to crawl to his corner, and the ref stops the count. Kensuke making his way to his corner as well. Tag by Corino to Diamond!!!! Joel: TAG BY SASAKI!!!! Joey: Shamrock screams, huge forearm shot to Simon. Simon back up, and a savate kick by Shamrock.
[Diamond up, and walks right into a gorilla press slam from Shamrock. Diamond gets up and Shamrock hits a over the head belly to belly suplex.]
Joey: Shamrock with a flurry of moves. Joel: This place is cheering for him. Joey: Diamond is up...he swings, and Shamrock ducks. He catches him in a Full
Nelson...SITDOWN SLAM!!!!!
[Sasaki comes back in and they double whip Diamond to the ropes...Diamond comes off, and they hit a double back body drop.]
Joey: Diamond staggers up...Double CLOTHESLINE!!! Shamrock makes the cover...ONE!! TWO!!! And Diamond kicks out this time. Shamrock brings him up again as Sasaki walks back into the apron.
Joel: Does it look like he took to many to the head? He's walking kinda funny. Joey: Sasaki? Nah. Shamrock shoves Diamond into the corner, and charges with a back elbow!!! Now he places his back to Diamond, and strikes with a series of back elbows....
[Diamond staggers out of the turnbuckle, and Shamrock with a German Suplex.] Joey: COVER!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! Corino makes the last second save. The ref escourts him back to his corner as Shamrock lifts Diamond up...DEATH VALLEY
DRIVER!!! Joel: Shamrock makes the cover, and we're 3 seconds away from new Tag Team
Champions... Joey: The ref isn't counting here...I think Diamond may have made a blind tag as here's Corino coming from behind Shamrock. Ken is up, and Corino from behind...LOW BLOW!!!!! INVERTED DDT!!!!
Joel: This could be it!!! Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! Kensuke makes the tag!!!! [The ref now escourts Kensuke back.] Joey: Corino brings Shamrock to his feet and he goes behind him...low blow, no
Shamrock counters with a kick of his own between the legs of Corino. Double
Underhook DDT by Shamrock!!!!
Joel: Whoa and Shamrock dives for his corner, tags in Kensuke... Joey: Sasaki runs...and Corino catches him in a Japanese Fujiawa Arm Bar!!!! Joel: Sasaki is screaming out in pain. Joey: He's fighting it though...he's getting back to his feet. He's lifting
Corino, look at the strength. He slams Corino down with one arm, and he breaks the holds.
[Kensuke screams and shakes his arm out. Corino gets up and Sasaki hammers on him with a right, and another right. Corino goes for a right, but it doesn't have any effect.]
Joey: He doesn't feel it. Boot to the st - POWER SPECIAL!!!!!!! Joel: It's over!!! Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO - Simon Diamond dives in with a double ax-handle to break the pin. Shamrock is in and he grabs Diamond from behind...ANKLE LOCK!!!!
Joel: Diamond is hurt...he's screaming out. Joey: Shamrock lets go, and Diamond rolls out of the ring. [Shamrock grabs Corino and lifts him up in a Gorilla Press. Kensuke jumps up onto the top turnbuckle....Shamrock throws him up, Kensuke leaps off and grabs
Corino's head...]
Joey: WELCOME TO HELL!!!! FLYING SITDOWN FACEBUSTER!!!!!!! Joel: ONE!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Joey: We have new Tag Team Champions!!!! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the winners of this match, and NEW XCW World Tag
Team Champions...Kensuke Sasaki and Ken Shamrock!!!!!
[Shamrock grabs the titles and hands one to Kensuke.] Joey: What a match that was!!!! Joel: Hardcore has the best tag matches I've ever seen. Joey: They do. Old School will be back though.... Joel: Wait a minute - Stan Hansen slides into the ring. [Stan grabs the microphone.] Stan: Heh, that's fine and dandy that you won these titles tonight boys. How about you face a real old school team though? I'm dropping a challenge to you two fruitcakes for this Sunday Night on Sin...can you handle that?
[Stan throws down the mic and leaves the ring area.] Joey: Who is Stans partner??? Joel: I don't know..maybe he's going to come alone? [We cut to a car pulling up. Out walks Steve Armstrong and Kenta Kobashi, you can here the arena pop for them as they make their way into the arena.]
Joey: STEVE ARMSTRONG IS HERE!!!!! STAY WITH US!!!!! [Camera cuts to a commercial.]
