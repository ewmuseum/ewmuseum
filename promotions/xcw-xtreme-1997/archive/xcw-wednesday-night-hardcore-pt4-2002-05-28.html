XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 4)

May 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We come back and Chris Jericho is standing in the ring with a microphone in his hand. The fans are giving him heat for just standing there, but he doesn't seem to care at all. He just smiles and enjoys listening to the fans complain...]
Chris: Now...last week we all saw me take care of that loser Hawk. That slack jawed, bald headed, inbred, no good, dirty piece of trash. Now tonight I once again will take someone down in this very ring. I have searched high and low...far and wide...and tonight I give you a man who not only has been to wrestling school, but he's also been to Harvard!!! Please welcome...CHRIS
HARVARD!!!! [Chris comes out and he gets heat for just being him, but that quickly changes when he starts slapping the fans hands. Chris Havard climbs into the ring and goes to shake Jericho's hand...and Chris just laughs at him.]
Chris J: Now you think you can beat me? Chris H: I can sure as hell try. Chris J: You think you're better than Hawk? Chris H: Yes. Chris J: NO! [Jericho goes to hit him with the microphone, but Havard moves out of the way.] Jim: Whoa, and the match is underway!!! Jericho can't believe it, and he walks right into a bodyslam.
Joel: Please don't lose to this asshole Jericho....please. Jim: Jericho is up and Harvard with a nice dropkick!!! [Jericho holds his lip, and Havard whips him into the turnbuckle. He charges in, and Jericho hits him with a back elbow to the face. Jericho springs out of the turnbuckle and hits a beautiful clothesline.]
Jim: Rookie mistake by Harvard, and now Jericho is going to use this to capitlize. Joel: Yes!! I love it when he kicks the hell out of these rookie punks. [Jericho locks in the Liontamer, and Havard quickly taps out.] Jim: And just like that Jericho wins again tonight. Joel: He's on a rampage!!! Jim: Are you kidding me? He hasn't beaten anybody yet! Joel: Oh come on...Chris Havard is going to wrestling school. Hawk...well Hawk is just an oddball.
Jim: Jericho isn't letting go now. Come on... Joel: He can't hear the refs.... Jim: Chris can hear it...he just doesn't want to let up. This could seriously hurt him, and Jericho doesn't even give a damn.
[Finally Jericho lets go and parades around the ring like he just beat Bruno
Sanmartino. Jericho leaves, and the paramedics come running out to check on the poor rookie.]
Jim: He might have popped his knee out... Joel: Or worse, but he went to Harvard so who cares? Jim: I do. Joel: Well you're face is limp...so shut up. [Camera cuts to Arn Anderson on the phone once again, and he seems to be in a heated conversation. All of a sudden a big figure walks in and Arn shuts up real quick. All is quiet, and then we see Arn get a HUGE smile on his face. He shakes the persons hand, and it's none other than...]
Arn: MICK FOLEY!!! Damn good to see you!!! Mick: Hi Arn. Arn: What can I do for you? Mick: Well I was wondering if you were still hiring for the XCW? Arn: YES!!! YES WE ARE!!!! You want to work next week? Mick: Well...um...don't I need to fill something out? Arn: Don't worry about it...FOLEY IS IN THE XCW!!! MAN I COULD KISS YOU!!!! Mick: Arn, we've known each other for a long time...but that's where I draw the line. Touching another mans lips...is not my thing. No matter what the internet says!
Arn: Good to have you here!!! Mick: Good to be here... [Arn gives Mick a huge hug that would make any hetero male feel odd. Mick pats
Arn on the back and leaves...camera cuts back to the ring. We see Big Pappa
Pump who is already standing there...]
Jim: And during the return of Mick Foley...Scott Steiner decided that his match was going to happen next.
Joel: I like that. Who the hell is going to stop him? Jim: First we get Jericho demanding matches, and now we get Steiner. Who's next? Joel: Me. ["Time to play the Game!!" is heard through out the arena, and the fans all stand to their feet. Triple H walks out from the curtain and Steve Armstrong is following right behind. Both men climb into the ring, and everyone meets in the center.]
Jim: Scott doesn't seem to care. Joel: Would you if your arms were the size of my car? [Hunter and Scott are having words, and they don't seem to be nice ones. Hunter pushes his index finger into the chest of Scott, and Scott pushes him.]
Jim: Scott pushes Triple H...and he lays back. Joel: Here we go!!! [Triple H winds up for the punch and he hits Steve Armstrong right in the head.] Jim: WHAT!?? OH NO!!! Triple H has double crossed Steve Armstrong, and now he's stomping away on him.
Joel: I LOVE IT!!! Oh that is awesome! Jim: And Scott Steiner now kicking the stomach of Armstrong. The man had an amazing match earlier this evening...and now he's being brutally beaten.
Joel: Don't you just love it? [Hunter tells Scott to get a chair, and Hunter locks him in the position for the Pedigree. Steiner slides the chair into the ring, and Hunter Pedigrees
Steve onto the steel chair!]
Jim: OH MY!!!! Somebody stop this heinous act! Joel: No way...this is awesome. Jim: Hunter damn double crossed Steve, and now Armstrong is paying for it. Damnit! I should've known. I should've known Triple H wasn't going to do something out of the kindness of his damn heart. Damn you Triple H!!!
Joel: Steiner and Hunter aren't stopping. They want to send a message to everyone in the locker room.
[All of a sudden..."Born To Be Wild" starts to play. The crowd quickly turns their attention to the curtain, and Cactus Jack is running out from the back with a flaming 2X4.]
Jim: CACTUS IS HERE!!!! CACTUS JACK IS BACK!!!! Joel: What the hell is he doing here? Jim: Jack slides in and the two idiots slide out.... [Hunter and Scott give each other a high-five and just smile. Cactus goes to help Steve up, and Steve hits him with a low blow.]
Jim: WHAT!?!? Joel: WHAT!?!? Jim: Steve Armstrong just low blowed Cactus Jack! Joel: What the hell for? He saved him? [Armstrong grabs the flaming 2X4 and slams it down onto Jack.] Jim: What the hell is wrong with that guy? [Steve looks at Steiner and Hunter and he starts pointing at them with the 2X4. Armstrong takes chase, and leaves Cactus lying in the ring hurt and confused.] Joel: What the hell was that all about?!?!? Jim: I don't know...fans stay with us!!!
