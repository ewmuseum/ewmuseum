[When we comeback Shawn Michaels is sitting with the therapist, and he screams in pain as he moves it back and forth.]
Therapist: Well it looks like you're not going to be able to be in this tournament. It's a shame, I would've liked to see you kick Hunter's ass.
Shawn: DAMNIT!!! Therapist: I would say that you need to get this checked out at the hospital. Don't stick around here anymore, and get this checked out. [Camera cuts back the ring.] Jim: Well Shawn Michaels isn't going to be able to wrestle here tonight, and it looks like Booker T just got a free ride.
Don: Yeah that's all his kind look for. Jim: WHAT!? What did you mean by that? Don: What do you think I meant? Jim: Were you refering to his race? Don: NO! How dare you say something like that, I'll have you know I find what you said very offensive, and I will not tolerate any kind of racial slurs while
I'm commentating. I knew you were a hick, but I didn't know you were a redneck.
Jim: I ju - Don: No...I don't want to hear it. Jim: Fans our first match here tonight is The Undertaker taking on the Human
Suplex Machine Taz.
Don: Oh yeah! Jim: This should be a brutal, brutal match here tonight. [Both men lock up, and Undertaker right away pulls off and starts in with a series of shots to the side of the head. He backs Taz into the ropes, and whips him to the opposite side. Taz bounces off, he ducks a clothesline and spins
Undertaker around to nail him with a clothesline.]
Jim: The big man isn't down yet...he's not down. Don: No it's going to take a lot more than that...a whole lot more. Jim: Taz with another clothesline, and he whips Taker to the ropes this time. [Undertaker bounces off the ropes and takes Taz down with a leaping clothesline. Taker gets right back up, and starts to stomp away at Taz.]
Jim: Undertaker not wasting any time at all, and he wants Taz to know that he's not only in the ring with a wrestler..he's in the ring with the Undertaker!
Don: The American Dead Badass! [Undertaker whips him into the turnbuckle, Taz reverses quickly and Undertaker bounces out hard. Taz catches him in a Death Valley Driver.]
Don: DVD!!!!! Jim: Whoa! Did you see how quickly Taz executed that move right there? He's going for the pin, and this could be it!!! ONE!! TWO!!! TH - NO!!
Don: It's going to take a whole lot more for the Undertaker to go down. Jim: We're proud to announce that the Undertaker just had a baby girl. Don: He did? Jim: Well he didn't, but his wife did. Don: Ugh...I'd hate to be a fly on the wall during that love making session. She looks like more of a man than he does. Did you hear that she has his named tatted across her lips...
Jim: I never saw it. Don: Of course you haven't seen it, she always has her pants on around you! Jim: Oh I can't believe I walked into that one! [Taz brings Undertaker to his feet and he levels him with a right hand to the side of the head, and punched to the head once again. Taker staggers back, and
Taz hits him with a beautiful belly to belly suplex. He hooks the leg...]
Jim: Taz goes for the cover once again...ONE!! TWO!!! TH - Once again The
Undertaker kicks out. Remember tonight one of these men will go onto the Main
Event for the XCW World Title.
Don: I can't imagine Taz being the XCW World Champion...I mean that would just be a shame. I mean when the ref is taller than you are, you're going to have some problems with respect.
Jim: Would you disrespect him to his face? Don: No...but behind this desk I can. [Taz undaunted brings Undertaker back to his feet and unloads with a series of stinging chops to the chest. Undertaker comesback with a few of his own, and he winds up and drives a right hand into Taz's head.]
Don: Nice! Undertaker with a Gerco Roman Eye poke!!! Jim: Taz staggers away in pain, and Undertaker levels him with a clothesline to the back of the neck. Taz falls down hard, and Undertaker just stands there soaking in the heel heat that he's getting. Undertaker now brings Taz to his feet...
[Taker grabs Taz by one arm and whips him into the turnbuckle. The Dead Man follows in with a clothesline, and Taz falls to the last turnbuckle. Taker drives his boot into the throat of Taz until the ref reaches the 4 count. He repeats this for another 2 times, and then leaves the corner.]
Don: Undertaker isn't showing any kind of mercy here tonight. I know he has to have a lot of pent up anger, I mean he's been hanging out with his pregnant wife for the past 3 months.
Jim: It probably was a special time for the two. Don: Yeah...every time he'd get on her his ears would pop. Ugh...visual!!! [Taker brings Taz over to the turnbuckle, and Undertaker climbs up. He's got
Taz by the arm, and he hits his trademark forearm smash from the top rope. Taz crumbles to the canvas, and Undertaker hooks the leg.]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! Don: No...Taz got his shoulder up. Barely, but he got his shoulder up. Jim: Taz has gotten himself into a lot of trouble here tonight, Undertaker has all the momentum now after Taz came in like a house of fire. Undertaker brings
Taz up, and he lifts him high into the air for a suplex.
Don: Whoa and they come crashing down like Courtney Love after a 4 day coke binge. [Undertaker runs to the ropes and drops an elbow across the chest. He keeps his elbow draped across him for a few seconds, catches his breath and brings Taz back to his feet again. Undertaker with an Irish Whip, and he follows in with a
HUGE Splash!!!]
Don: What a move there by Undertaker...nice splash to crush the ribs. Jim: Taker to the ropes and he drops a leg across the throat. Undertaker hooks the leg once again, and this could be it...ONE!! TWO!!! NO!!! Taz kicks out again.
Don: This is a good match for both men, but the pacing is a little slow for my taste. Jim: That's because you're a high spot whore. Don: Aren't we all now days? Jim: Where is the Undertaker going? He's headed up top, and he's almost there. Taz is making it to his feet, and Undertaker from the top ropes....FLYING
CLOTHESLINE! Taz knocked down to the mat, and Undertaker has a chance to put it away here!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THRE -
Don: Barely a kickout...whoa. Jim: Barely doesn't cut it though, and it looks like he's a little upset. Maybe he's upset because he didn't hook the leg that time. Undertaker brings Taz back up to his feet, and he runs to the ropes...
[Undertaker leaps up into the air and takes Taz down with a nice leaping DDT. The impact ends up rolling Taz onto his back. Taker now heads to the outside and he slingshots himself over the top rope and legdrops Taz across the throat.]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO NO NO!!! TAZ KICKS OUT AGAIN!!!! Don: I can't believe it. Jim: I don't think The Undertaker does either. He hit a series of big moves, and it didn't seem to get the job done. Taz is a fighter, and damnit he's not going to give up that easy on his first match back with the XCW.
Don: Uh oh...Undertaker is giving the sign for the Last Ride. [Taz gets shoves between Taker's legs, and he lifts him up high....Taz starts to punch away on the head of Taker and he gets set down.]
Jim: Taz with a back elbow to the head, and another one. Head and Arm Tazplex!!!! Now he's going for the pin...ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - NO!! That little hesitation at the end might have cost him that pinfall.
Don: So damn close...what an opening match for a Malice. [Taz gets a running start at the dazed Undertaker, and Taker throws him over the top ropes and onto the outside. Taz smacks hard onto the cement floor, and
Taker slides out after him.]
Jim: Taker isn't about to let him rest on the outside...he grabs Taz and throws him over the guardrail into the crowd.
Don: This could get ugly. Jim: Taker to the crowd now, and Taz just grabbed a fan's alcoholic beverage and threw it in Taker's eyes! Taker is temporarily blinded! Taz doesn't take a drink of the beverage, because that would suck and be stupid, but he politely shoves the same fan off his chair and grabs it!
Don: WHOA!!! He clocked Taker in the back of the head with it!!! These two guys are doing whatever it takes to get a shot at the XCW World Title, and I can't say as I blame them! If it means hitting somebody with a chair, throwing them off the turnbuckles, blinding them with an alcoholic beverage, then you do it!
Jim: They're doing just that, and now Taz has the advantage as Taker gets up off the knee he was driven to by the chair shot and turns to face Taz - Taz tosses him the chair and Taker instinctually catches it!
[Taz drives a forearm into the chair which hits Taker in the face.] Jim: Taker knocked to the ground, and now Taz quickly brings him back up... doubles him over... he's not gonna? Don: I think he is! Jim: Lifts him up... piledriver on the concrete on the outside! My God! Somebody call a damn ambulence!!! Taz showing no mercy brings Taker right back up amongst the sea of fans now... walks him to the guardrail... hooks him for a hiptoss!
Don: He's gonna hiptoss him over! Jim: Taker somehow reverses it! And Taz reverses that and Taker goes flying to the ringside area! Taz now follows him back over, and he brings Taker up to his feet... now Taz climbing onto the Australian announce table...
Weebie: Ey' watch it mate! Dongy: Rip 'is bloody 'ead off mate!!! Don: Dongy is the heel. Jim: So I noticed. Taz hooks Taker's head... Don: This doesn't look good. Jim: Looks like he's going to DDT him off the table. [But Taker reaches forward and sweeps Taz's legs out from under him! Taker climbs onto the table...big left from Taker! Taz fires back with a right! Right by Taker! Left by Taz! Right by Taz! Another right by Taz and Undertaker is staggered!]
Jim: Taz with a go-behind, and he hooks him in a Full Nelson. He's going to nail him with a Dragon Suplex...Good God don't let him do it. It'll kill the
Undertaker.
Don: And Sarah will have a hard time trying to find a husband for her and that ugly beast! Jim: Taker quickly kicks him between the legs, and he revers - OH MY GOD!!! Reverse DDT through the table!!! Both men are down, both men are down!!!! Don: Taker hit hard, and so did Taz! Jim: And we have an Austrailian table that is in ruins. Don: Bumma. How to speak Austrailian...."GET THE HELL OUT OF THE WAY MATE!" AHAHAHA. Jim: You crack yourself up don't you? The ref has put a count on...lets hope it doesn't end like this tonight. Lets hope that these two men will be able to go on and continue with this match. 1...2...3...4...5...6...7...8...9...and Taker is back up!!!
[Taker rolls Taz into the ring to break the count.] Don: That was a stupid move. Why didn't he just roll into the ring and win the match that way? Ugh.
Jim: Maybe Taker wants to win this match with honor, and some respect. Don: Well that makes him an idiot. Jim: Undertaker brings Taz to his feet, and he lifts him up. Uh oh...here comes the Tombstone. He hit it!!!!
Don: Goodnight!! Jim: ONE!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Don: NO!!! Taz kicked out!!! The guy is a friggin' animal. He didn't get the win, and I have never seen The Undertaker's eyes so damn big.
Jim: I don't think anyone has ever kicked out of that...maybe Hulk Hogan, but I don't think anyone else has. Undertaker can't believe his eyes. He's got a little trickle of blood running down his forehead, and he's just stunned.
[Taker gets him up and whips him into the ropes, Taz reverses...he puts his head down though, and Undertaker double hooks him...Taz though counters with a backdrop.]
Jim: Taker holds onto the backdrop, Sunset Flip!!!! Don: Taz is about to go down!!! He's wobbling... Jim: WAIT!!! Taz drops to his knees...he hooks the legs and leans forward!!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!!! Don: Undertaker can't believe it...he's holding his head in disbelief. Jim: Taz rolls to the outside, as The Undertaker makes it back to his feet. Taz rolls back into the ring to get his arm rasied by the ref. Taker stares at Taz, and Taz stares right back.
Don: Round 2!!!! Jim: Undertaker finally break it off and rolls to the outside. Taz to the outside now, and he leaves through the crowd. What an opening match that was here tonight.
Don: You never fail to take something awesome, and turn it into mediocrity. That match was amazing. Jim: Taz now will go onto the Main Event...the Four Corners Final for the XCW
World Title. What's going to happen?
