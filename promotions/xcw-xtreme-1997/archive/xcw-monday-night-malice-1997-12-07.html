XCW Monday Night Malice, 7 December 1997

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday Night MALICE Jim: Well folks it's time to get this show started and this first match is gonna be a slobber knocker!!! Joey: You couldnt be more right Ross! We're gonna kick things off here tonight with Barry Horowitz taking on Eric Shelley!! This should be a great one! Jim: I totally agree so lets not waste anytime. Shelley is already in the ring and here comes Barry running down the aisle followed by his manager, Saphire! Joey: Horowitz slides into the ring with the chair and is met by a vicious kick by Eric Shelley!! He kicks him again. He lifts Horowitz up.........he's got him up in a military press!! Jim: Power. That's the only word to describe it. You wouldnt think Eric Shelley had much power by looking at that couch potato like frame of his. Joey: He drops Horowitz to the outside. Boy Shelley is fired up. Hey!! Saphire is up on the ropes arguing with both the official and Shelley now. Horowitz has a chair!! Barry is in the ring.....he BLASTS Shelley in the back of the head with the chair as both he and the official were distracted by Saphire!!! OH MY GOD!! BARRY HOROWITZ HAS TAKEN IT TO THE EXTREME!!! I dont believe it!!!! All thanks to a distraction by Saphire! Jim: Look at her. I've been distracted by Saphire ever since she made a booty call to me last night! Joey: What?? Now Horowitz has Eric Shelley on the canvas. He's punching him repeatedly. This isn't what Shelley expected and Horowitz is taking advantage of his opportunity. Horowitz has the man hurt and now he's going for the cover. 1!!! 2!!!!! NO!!!! Shelley kicks out!! Horowitz lifts him up.....throws him into the ropes....kicks him in the gut.......DDT!!! ITS OVER!!! 1!!!! 2!!!!! NO!!! SHELLEY KICKS OUT!!! Jim: Did you hear opportunity Joey? She just left town. Horowitz gave Shelley his best shot and he still didn't get it done. Joey: Horowitz is still working on injuring Shelley's cranium, trying to beat him into unconciousness. He's got him in the corner.....throwing his head into the top turnbuckle. Horowitz sends Shelley into the opposite corner and runs in. Shelley lifts a boot and Horowitz collapses!!!!!!! Wait a minute...we Jim: Oh great. We've got a fan in the ring! He's a short fella. Hey people! Yeah we encourage involvement but this is crazy. Stay out of the ring or your gonna get hurt. Joey: Wait a minute!!! The ref is trying to push the fan out of the ring but the fan grabs the ref and belly to belly suplexes him over!!!!! The ref crashes down on the back of his neck!!!!! THAT'S NO REF!!!! Jim and Joey: THAT'S TAZ!!!!!! Jim: oh no!!!!! Joey: Taz is BACK!!!!! Taz has returned to XCW!!!!! This is incredible!!!!! Shelley charges at Taz with a clothesline.......Taz ducks it......he grabs Eric and cinches him up.........T-BONE TAZPLEX!!!!!!!!! Jim: Horowitz is up! No he isnt!!!!! He sees Taz and falls back to the mat and tries to roll out. Joey: Smart!! But not quick!!!! Taz grabs Horowitz by the foot and drags him back in the ring........he wraps Horowitz up in some kind of anklelock!!!! Jim: This guy knows how to make you tap!! Joey: Horowitz is screaming and.........Saphire is running in the ring!!!!!! Taz catches her as she runs in......T-BONE TAZPLEX!!!!!!!! Jim: And Horowitz took the time to get out of the ring and he's limping toward the back!!!! Joey: What a shocker!!!! Hey!! Oh great. Taz has the mike!!!! I guess we have no choice...... Taz: That's right!!!! You run you pussy!!!! (The crowd gives Taz a semi-big pop. See they know he is a bad ass heel but yet he is so friggin cool they dont know if they can help but cheer him.) Taz: SHUT THE HELL UP!! (That answers their question. The crowd begins to boo.) Taz: Now I know everybody here in the XCW wanted to be the one to give Taz his big introduction but too bad. Taz dont like to fool around with red tape and BS. I do what I want when I want to do it, so Im out here now. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Taz: It's been a long time hasnt? A long time since Taz has stepped through these ropes. Because quite frankly Taz got sick of wrestling. Too many backstabbers in that damn lockerroom. I didnt need that kind of shit and I really didnt need to be in some stupid fucking fed ran by a man I despise. Paul Roma is a piece of shit and everybody knows it!!! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! Taz: But I got sick of sitting at home watching other less talented wrestlers steal my spotlight. Yeah I got offers from plenty of places. I coulda went to TWF, TCW, IEUW, WWF, WCW, APW. But all those feds SUCKED!!! And believe me... those guys were offering me a ton of dough to sell my soul and step back into the ring. But if there's one thing I want to get across is that Taz doesnt do this for money. Taz does this because this is what Taz does best. But what made me choose XCW? It's quite simple really. I look up and down this roster and every single name makes me sick. I look at the title situation and it makes me want to puke. You only got one champion thats worth a damn in Dean Malenko and all you've gave him is the US title. Once again another fed has succomb to pushing the "name" stars while the real talent resides in mediocrity. Well Taz has come back to change all that shit. I am the single most talented wrestler on the face of planet Earth and I'm gonna take that world belt. And after I beat your washed up champion, I'm gonna take that belt and shove it up Paul Roma's ass!! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! Taz: I mean just take a look at the names surrounding the world title situation. You got the Patriot surrounded by a has been and a never was. The Patriot is one of the most overrated wrestlers on planet Earth. The only reason this loser gets over is because he wears a stupid little mask, waves a flag and keeps blowing smoke up his own ass by bragging about how much he accomplished in Japan. Taz has been to Japan. Taz has dominated Japan. You dont see my bragging about it. The Patriot is nothing but a 3 or 4 move wrestler tops but he gets you people off your feet because he's so damn "Patriotic." Well people let me tell you. I've seen this guy in the locker room and this outside apperance is nothing but a facade. He couldnt give a rat's ass about America. All he cares about is making you people think he's a patriot, so you'll buy a ticket, so he can go buy his high priced FOREIGN cars then drive around thinking he's better than all of you. Then you got the his friend, the has been, Hogan. A man that claims to be the GOD of wrestling. Hey Hogan. Just because your as old as God doesnt mean you are God!! I've beaten your ass before Hogan and if you step in my path of rage, I'll do it again. Then you have Patriot's other little buddy, the never was......Ron Simmons. A even worse wrestler than the other two. The guy's only claim to fame is being the one and only black world's champion. Who cares??? I dont give a shit what color you are, if you suck you shouldnt be getting no world title and Simmons sucks!! The only reason he even got to smell that title was because he's black. And look at him now? The big black man didnt go far did he? Now he does nothing but carry the Patriot's bags and wipe his ass for him. Joey: This is going way too far.... Taz: Shut up Styles!! Who else? What about Bret Hart? Or should I say Vince McMahon's screw toy? Bret Hart is just another hypocrite in the world of professional wrestling. A man too good to do what's right for the company that made him a superstar so he can go get a multi-million dollar contract with the "other" guy and meanwhile say he isnt in it for the money. Yeah fucking right. Bret Hart is the biggest loser in the history of professional wrestling. On the outside he wants everybody to think that he's the loyal company man that keeps getting screwed but meanwhile he's the one that's busy screwing everybody!!! And the only reason Bret Hart is anywhere near the title in this fed is because his name begins with a Bret and ends in a Hart. With his deteriorated skills he wouldnt get a job cleaning toilets in Titan Tower if his name was Leroy Hart. Then there's the fag of wrestling, Shawn Michaels. The man that has a ego bigger than whole fucking planet. The man who's been between Pat Patterson's legs more times than Patterson's own hand. Shawn Michaels is a whining crybaby, backstabbing, manipulator that only wrestle's when his knee isnt in the middle of ending his career. A lousy career such that it is. And finally......you got your World Champ. That's right. STING!! What can you say about Sting that hasnt been said before? Well I could say he could wrestle. That's never been said before. Sting is a piece of trash. Another guy that lacks talent so he comes to the ring with spikey hair and stupid ass paint on his face so the marks will love him. Then when that get's old he just randomly picks a movie character and steals that as his gimmick. Sting is a loser in the worst way. Look a this fucking interview!! With Taz gives a interview I get straight and to the point. I dont waste time describing what kind of clothes I wear. I dont describe the whole fucking scene. I say what I gotta say and leave. I dont try to make my fucking interviews longer than the bible to try and cover up the fact that I couldnt spell to save my god damn life!!!! Unlike Sting. Reading a Sting interview is like trying to unscramble top secret coded CIA language. The only guy who gives a interview with every word in it typed liek TSHI. He makes me sick!!!! And it makes me wanna puke to know I was once aligned with this guy in another fed. But you know what's even worse? Beyond all that....Sting is a coward. Sting is afraid. Sting is a lowlife piece of trash and he's scared of one thing. One thing can make Sting run and hide under his fucking bed.......and that one thing is a challenge. He doesnt like to be challenged. He likes everything real neat and simple. If the president says to jump, Sting say how high! So right Im gonna scare the shit out of Sting. STING!!!!! GET YOUR ASS OUT HERE!!!! I know Sting is in the back somewhere. Somebody get his scared ass out here. That's it. Come on Sting!!! Get your ass out here!!!! (The crowd erupts as Sting walks out the curtain and down the aisle.) Taz: That's far enough bitch. You dont have to get in the ring. I just wanted you out here so I can see your face when I say this. You think your real special dont you? You carry that title around like its the holy grail. You think that your on top of the mountain here in XCW dont you? Nothing can stop the Stinger!!!!! Well all except for one thing. And that one thing just knocked on your fucking door and shattered this dream world you have created for yourself. Sting........I am your nightmare. I came to the XCW for one reason and one reason only. Once upon a time Taz was world champion. Only once!!! And that one time, I lost the title due to backstage manipulation. I never got to hold a world title ever again. Taz got pushed to the back while other less talented wrestlers got to hold the spotlight. THAT SHIT STOPS!!!! And it stop tonight. What I got......right here!!! You see this? You see this paper? This is a contract Sting. (Taz throws the contract at Sting) Taz: That's a contract that your gonna either sign or Im gonna kick your ass. That's a contract that says tonight, if your man enough, your gonna fight me with that World Title on the line. That's a contract that seals your doom. Are you man enough Sting? Will you sign that little piece of paper and give me my shot? Or will you back down and cower like the pussy I know you to be? (Sting begins to walk toward the back with the contract.) Taz: Yeah. You just walk on back. Go to the back and think about this real long and hard. But if you dont sign that contract, your signing away your life. Because if you dont sign your name to that little dotted line......I will hunt you down.....and break you like a twice. But either way Sting.....you will be......JUST ANOTHER VICTIM!!!!!! Joey: OH MY GOD!!!!!! Jim: Well Taz is finally leaving the ring and heading to the back. Joey: OH MY GOD!!!! Jim: What a night folks!!!! We didnt know THIS was gonna happen!! Will Sting sign the contract???? Could we see Taz vs Sting here tonight on free TV!!! That's a PPV type match!!!!! Joey: Anything can happen in the XCW!!!! Jim: Just like the debut of Bret Hart!!! He's going aganist a great wrestler...Brian Christopher. Lately Christpher's been trying to get into the Perfect Alliance...maybe we'll see them down here tonight!!!! FINK: Ladies and Gentlemen....the following contest is set for one fall. First comming down to the ring...weighing in at 230 pounds...here is "Too Sexy" Brian Christopher!!!!!!!! {Brian's music starts to play and he comes out kissing his muscles.} FINK: And his opponent....from Calgary, Alberta, Canada.....he is the "Excellence Of Execution"....here is Bret "Hitman" Hart!!!!!!! Jim: OH and Bret tackles Brian, but Brian is defending himself pretty well. Brian nails Bret with a right hand, and another right hand. Joey: Brian sends Bret to the ropes...Powerslam!!!!!!!! He's got a handfull of tights!!!! One...Two...THREE!!!!! OH MY GOD!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner....."Too Sexy" Brian Christopher!!!!!!!!! Joey: And what's Paul Roma doing out here? He's helping Bret Hart up...Oh and he slaps Bret in the face!!!! Jim: OH MY....Paul nails him with a dropkick, and Hart goes over the top rope. HAHAHA...that's what Hart gets for being a deadbeat!!!!! Roma grabs Hart and throws him into the crowd..and the crowd is getting some shots on him!!! HAHAHA!!!!!! {Some fat guy with a tight ass yellow shirt throws beer in Bret's face. Bret trips over a chair and the crowd dog piles him. Roma grabs the mic in the ring.} Paul Roma: Like I said before.....we don't keep trash in our fed!!!! Madchef you piece of crap I want you to listen up. You ever come around here again, and you ever plug your fed on my air time...you'll pay!!! I don't care who the hell you think you are....I'm the friggin Prez of this fed, and that's that!!! Hart.....I'll see your pink and black ass in hell!!!! {Roma throws down the mic. Camera fades.} The 822 Wrestling News Source
