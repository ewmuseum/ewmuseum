XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 3)

May 21, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Break The Walls Down" starts to play and Chris Jericho walks out with a huge smile on his face. He climbs into the ring and gets onto the microphone...]
Chris: Now ladies and gentlemen let me introduce to you my opponent for tonight. The man has had extensive training in the ring, he is known to many as a nutcase and he is truely hated by YOURS TRUELY. Will you please welcome...from Tough Enough 2....HAWK!!!!
[Hawk from Tough Enough 2 comes out, and the crowd pops big time for him. He's got on a pair of old dirty sweats, and a Tough Enough 2 shirt on. His head is still shaved, and he still has the same goofy look on his face. Hawk climbs into the ring and Jericho can't stop from laughing.]
Chris: Whoa...wait a minute. Kid...do you know what you're getting yourself into? [Hawk shakes his head yes...] Chris: Do you know you're going up against a "Living Legend"? [Hawk just smiles...] Chris: Do you realize I'm not Al Snow? I'm not Bob Holly...I am Chris Jericho? Heh, you're nothing but a punk. A slack jawed, no talent little punk...and tonight in front of all your little fans I'm going to kick your ass.
[Jericho hits Hawk in the head with the microphone...] Jim: Oh come on...he's just a boy. Joel: That's what I've been trying to tell Father Marshall. Jim: You're sick. Jericho with a series of boots to the head, and Hawk isn't going to stand a chance. The kid is no more trained that you or I....
[Chris brings Hawk to his feet and hits him in the face with a stiff forearm, and another forearm. He whips Hawk into the ropes and executes a flying forearm. Hawk goes down hard, and holds his face in pain.]
Jim: What a disgusting human being Jericho is. The kid barely knows how to run to the ring ropes, and he's beating him senseless.
Joel: Never get into the ring and mess with him. [Chris lifts up Hawk and goes behind him. Jericho with a snap German Suplex, and Hawks head bounces off the canvas like a basketball. Jericho brings him up again, and this time hits a Dragon Suplex...he bridges it, and the ref makes the count.]
Jim: Let this be over....1!!! 2!!! 3!!! Thank God!!! Joel: Awe man!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Chris Jericho!!! Jim: So from now on Chris Jericho gets to wrestle whenever he wants to on the card. Which means we get more Jericho...
Joel: YAY!!! Jim: Folks it's time for our Main Event, and this should turn out to be a great match. First it's the return of the former XCW World Champion...Mikey
Whipwreck, as he takes on TAKA and Sho Funaki. Arn Anderson booked this handicap match for a reason, but who knows why?
Joel: I'll tell you why...it's because he doesn't want Mikey to get any momentum. Last time he won a match he didn't stop winning until the fed closed, and WHY do you think the fed closed?
Jim: Not because of Mikey... Joel: HELL YEAH IT WAS BECAUSE OF MIKEY! [The familiar japanese music associated with Kaientai pumps over the loudspeakers causing the crowd to boo. ]
FINK: Coming down the aisle... at a combined weight of 427 lbs... from Japan
TAKA Michinoku and SHO Funaki...
K A I E N T A I ! ! ! ! ! [The duo emerge from the back, clad in matching blue "Evil: Indeed" tee-shirts and their ring attire. They both have Japanese flags which they frantically wave around as they jump on the ramp, to more jeers from the crowd. After boasting a bit, they make their way to the ring.]
FINK: And their opponent...weighing in at 229 pounds. He is hails from St. Paul, Minnesota. Here is Mikey Whipwreck!!!! ["Loser" by Beck starts up and the crowd rises to their feet. Mikey walks out and the roof almost blows off of the Staple Center. Mikey takes his time coming down to the ring, as he's soaking everything up. Mikey climbs in, and goes to each turnbuckle to work the crowd....]
Jim: And it looks like Sho is going to start off against Mikey tonight....both men circling each other, and Sho tries to pull the leg out from under him.
Joel: He's gonna have to try something different...Mikey has picked up a few extra pounds. [Both men lock up, and Sho executes a go-behind. Mikey looking for an escape, and he hits a few elbows to the side of the head. Sho lets up, and Mikey executes a go-behind this time.]
Joel: Quick moves for a fatty. Jim: You should talk. Mikey with a take down, and Sho hits his nose onto the canvas. Mikey hits the mat, and straps on a headlock!
Joel: I'm noticing something different about this Mikey....normally he'd stumble around the mat trying to figure out what to do. This kid is gaining some confidence...boring way of doing it, but doing it nonetheless.
Jim: Sho gets to his knees, and back to his feet. Sho goes for a back suplex, but Mikey flips over!!!
Joel: WOW!!! Jim: Mikey with a hard back suplex of his own, and he goes for the cover. TAKA comes in to break the count though.
Joel: HA!!! Not even a one count. [Mikey whips Sho into the ropes, and his a jumping back elbow. Sho staggers back up, and Mikey with a series of jumping kicks to the midsection. Sho backs up into the turnbuckle, and Mikey with a chop after chop...]
Jim: And those are making my ears ring. Joel: What has gotten into Mikey? Jim: Mikey finally stops the chopping, and brings Sho out of the corner. Textbook bodyslam by the former XCW World Champion. [Mikey gets onto the second turnbuckle, and flies off with an elbow to the throat.] Jim: Flesh on the throat, and I am suprised by this!!! There's the cover...1!!! 2!!! Once again TAKA comes flying into the ring, and breaks the count. Joel: Nice work!!! [Mikey gets up this time and walks over to TAKA....both men are having words, and Sho comes from behind and hits a Reverse DDT. Sho crawls over and makes the tag to TAKA.]
Jim: TAKA now in, and he climbs to the top rope. Missile Drop-kick, and Mikey goes down hard.
Joel: I like this kid...I like him better than Sho. Jim: TAKA brings Mikey back to his feet...whips him, and brings him back into a OH MY GOD!!!! Joel: Short-Arm Superkick??? WOW!!!! Jim: Mikey goes down! [TAKA claps to the fans, and they respond with some serious heel heat. TAKA brings Mikey up and hits him with some vicious forearms to the chin. He backs
Mikey into the corner...]
Jim: What's he going to do with him? Mikey is face first in the turnbuckle, and
TAKA jumps onto the top turnbuckle....front facelock by TAKA, and TORNADO
DDT!!! He plants him into the canvas with that move.
Joel: Cover by TAKA....ONE!!! TWO!!! THR-He kicked out!!! Jim: It's going to take a hell of a lot more to keep him down!!! [TAKA brings Mikey up again, and puts him over his shoulders....Death Valley
Driver, and Mikey's head smashes into the mat. TAKA gets right up again and smiles...he points to Mikey again, and lifts him up. He grabs Mikey and puts him on his shoulders again....]
Jim: This is going to be two DVD'S!!!! Joel: TAKA has him up...put him out of wrestling baby!!! [Mikey squrims out of the move...grabs TAKA and goes for the Whippersnapper.] Jim: WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!! WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!! Joel: Wait...NO!!! Taka lands on his feet.... [TAKA goes for another superkick, but it's ducked by Mikey. Mikey comesback with a HUGE clothesline.]
Jim: And listen to this place errupt....Mikey is a house of fire. TAKA back to his feet and Mikey hits a drop-kick, and another drop-kick. In comes Sho, and he gets hit with a drop-kick.
Joel: Who the hell lit this kid up? Jim: Mikey whips TAKA into the turnbuckle, and he bounces out hard. Mikey runs tot he ropes...SPRINGBOARD BULLDOG!!!!!
Joel: Listen to this place...you'd think Hulk Hogan was here or something. Jim: Mikey with the cover...hooks the leg. 1!!! 2!!! Sho with a legdrop across the back of the head, and the ref has to get this match back under control.
[Mikey gets back to his feet and so does TAKA. Both men are stunned, but hit each other in the face at the same time. Mikey gets the better of the two blows, and goes down. TAKA runs to the ropes and drops a quick leg across the throat.]
Jim: TAKA with that quick legdrop, and he tags out to Sho. Joel: In comes the "WHOLE F'N SHO"!!! Ya like that? I just made it up! Jim: Clever. [Both men grabs Mikey and execute a snap suplex. Sho climbs to the top as TAKA holds Mikey down...Sho hits a beautiful Senton Splash]
Jim: Did you see the elevation he got on that?? Joel: Of course...he's the "WHOLE F'N SHO"!!!! [Sho grabs Mikey by the arm, and locks in a hammerlock. Mikey screams out in pain, and Sho puts on the pressure.]
Jim: You can see the look on his face, and Sho is skilled in attack those pressure points. He can pop that arm out of it's socket whenever he wants to.
Joel: So why doesn't he do it? "POP" [All of a sudden we see a sudden jolt in Mikey's shoulder...and he screams like there's no tomorrow.]
Joel: Ugh... Jim: HE POPPED HIS DAMN ARM OUT OF HIS SOCKET!!!!! Joel: I think I'm going to be sick. Jim: Tim White is asking Mikey if he's had enough... Joel: And that idiot is saying no. Jim: Sho isn't letting go either. [Sho looks like he's trying to rip the arm out of the socket...Mikey looks like he's crying. Finally Sho lets go and walks over to TAKA who is already laughing. Both men start cracking up pointing the finger at Mikey...]
Jim: Poor Mikey...he's in such agony, and Kaientai is have a grand time laughing at him. Joel: HAHAH!!! Jim: You too.... Joel: You have to admit it's a funny sight. [Mikey helps himself up with one arm, and slams his bad shoulder onto the mat. Everyone in the crowd groans at the same time....Mikey does it again, and this time it looks like he popped his arm back into the socket.]
Jim: Mikey just popped his arm back into his socket!!! The man will do anything to win, and he's proving that here tonight.
Joel: He wouldn't have done that 3 months ago....he would've walked out. [Mikey with a serious look of determination on his face makes it back up to his feet. He points to Sho and tells him to "Bring It"....Sho yells...TAKA yells.]
Joel: This is like a throw back to a Kung-Fu film... Jim: It sure is! [Sho charges Mikey, and Mikey hits a backdrop which takes Sho to the outside. TAKA runs to Mikey, and he counters with a single leg takedown...TAKA hits the bottom rope with his mouth, and Mikey gets back up.]
Jim: This place is on their feet here tonight. Mikey, bad shoulder and all has taken Kaientai to the limit here.
Joel: All they have to do is hit a Single Arm DDT or something. Where's Al Snow when you need him to work down a bodypart?
[Mikey starts shaking that bad arm to get some blood flow in there. Mikey see's
Sho recovering on the outside, and he gets a running start....]
Jim: PLANCHA!!!!!! Mikey Whipwreck with a damn Plancha onto Sho Funaki, and what the hell has this kid been eating? What the hell has gotten into this kid?
Joel: I have no idea... Jim: Now TAKA is over and grabs Mikey...he throws him into the guardrail! TAKA follows up with another whip...this time into the ringpost, and Mikey hits shoulder first!!!!
Joel: WOW. Jim: This could be the end for Mikey here tonight. He drops to his knees, holding that arm. TAKA and Sho rolls Mikey back into the ring, and both men climb to the top of opposite turnbuckles...
[Sho and TAKA give each other the thumbs up and fly off the turnbuckles like
Superman. Flying headbutt by both men....]
Jim: MIKEY MOVES!!!!! MIKEY MOVES!!!!! Joel: They both wiped out hard!!!! Jim: Mikey crawls over to Sho and makes the cover....ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!!!! Joel: What a match!!!! FINK: Here is your winner....Mikey Whipwreck!!!!! Jim: Mikey is back, and he's better than ever. Joel: I can't believe it. [Mikey still on the ground holding his arm in pain...he knows he won, but he can't do anything to celebrate. He's using the ring ropes finally to make it to his feet, and he's barely making it.]
Jim: What a dangerous duo Sho and TAKA are. Joel: TAKA about a winning team!! Jim: Can we please get him fired? [Mikey gets his hand raised finally, but Sho and TAKA ruin the celebration with a double drop-kick to the back. Sho grabs one arm, and AKA grabs the other..and they play rowboat with them. Mikey is screaming out like someone just tossed comet in his ass.]
Joel: Ooo man...this isn't pretty. Jim: Someone please stop this. Mikey has had enough tonight. Joel: Never enough...this is nothing. Remember when Vader beat the living hell out of him? Now that was enough...this is like a party at the Williams house hold.
[TAKA stops and goes to the outside. He lifts up the apron and grabs a chair,
TAKA throws the chair back into the ring and Sho puts Mikey's bad arm in it.]
Jim: Now they're going to break his arm. Mikey you need to get out of there... Joel: Yeah I'm sure he can hear you Ross. [TAKA is about to jump onto Mikey's arm when all of a sudden we hear the sound of a Native American Voice cry out. The drum begins to play, and we see one man...a man who has not been around wrestling for many years. A man...who's name means BUFFALO!!!!]
Jim: TATANKA IS HERE?????? Joel: Oh my God....he's even got the stupid red stripe down his head! Jim: Tatanka slides into the ring, and he's giving out Tomahawk chops for free!!! [Tatanka with a series of Tomahawk chops, and Sho and TAKA roll out of the ring. TAKA and Sho are shocked to see the return of the Indian Brave...and as they walk to the back we hear Tatanka yell..."YA YA YA YEEEE!!!!!!".]
Jim: TATANKA is back!!!!! Joel: Great...this is going to boost the ratings!!! Jim: Fans thank you for tuning in..we'll see you next week!!! [Camera cuts to Arn Anderson who is hiding behind a car in the parking lot. We see Ric Flair and Chris Jericho walking to the Lexus. Flair opens the trunk to put his suitcase in...and Arn clotheslines him from behind and tosses him in the trunk. Arn slams the trunk closed and wipes his hands.]
Arn: You mess with me next time...it's going to be 10 times worse!!! [Arn walks away...] Ric: Let me out... Chris: I can't... Ric: WHY NOT? Chris: You have the keys!!! Ric: DAMNIT!!!!!! [Camera fades.]
