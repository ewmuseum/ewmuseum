XCW Year In Review (part 2)

December 29, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Michael: With the fun, comes a whole lot of action. Who can forget some of the classic matches we had in 2002? I mean like the classic Kurt Angle against
Shane Douglas match...these two had a classic battle, and it looks like they might be gearing up to do it once again. Lets take you to...JULY 31st, 2002.
CUT TO MATCH Joey: You're sick. Fans we head to the ring now for what is going to be described as an amazing match. These Hawaii fans are absolutely jacked, knowing that they will be witness to a match that, nobody ever thought they would see.
Kurt Angle.. Joel: And Shane Douglas. Joey: Kurt Angle, the Olympic Gold Medalist. Arguably the most talented wrestler in the XCW, maybe even in the history of the XCW. A man who can take any style of wrestling - hardcore, high flying, technical, brawling - and make it his own, and has done so to have tremendous sucess here in the XCW. A man who talks the biggest game in the XCW, and has proven time and time again that he can back it up. A man who when it's all said and done will undoubtedly go down as one of the XCW's true immortals.
Joel: Okay my turn....Shane Douglas, the man the myth the legend. Who asecension to the top has been faster than any other's. A man who has a shot on everyone in the ranks of the XCW. His no-crap style and his unbelievable chrisma. A man who has taken on all commers, a man who Kurt Angle wishes to be.
Joey: Mine was better. Joel: NO! [The crowd buzzes, waiting for the two wrestlers to arrive. "Perfect Strangers" starts to play and Shane Douglas arrives on the enterance ramp to a roar of boo's. Shane Douglas walks down the tamp, gesturing all the while that he wants the US Title, as fireworks shadow him. Finally, he climbs onto the ring apron, and turnbuckle, flashbulbs pop all over the area. Finally Douglas drops down and waits for his opponent.]
Joey: Shane Douglas looks more ready than ever. He looks like he's on a mission to win the XCW United States Title Once held by Curt Hennig, and vacated because he was going to join the nWo.
Joel: You know once one of these men get this title, it's going to be a long time before someone takes it off of them.
["Medal" starts to play over the PA system. Kurt walks on and stops for a moment, taking it all in, then points to Douglas. He walks to the ring, slides in, and then raises his hands into the air...fireworks blast off in the ring turning everyone a light shade of red, white and blue for a moment. ]
Joey: And Earl Hebner holds up that title...he passes it off to the ring attendant, who in turn passes it along to the timekeeper.
Joel: There's the bell. Joey: And it's on! [Both men circle each other, looking for an opening. Nither one find it. Finally Kurt steps forward, holding out his hand. Shane looks at it for a moment, and he shakes his hand. The crowd boos.]
Joel: Oh come on...can't these guys be fair to each other? Joey: I can't believe they trust one another. Joel: I would. [Both men lock up, and Douglas gains the advantage, twisting Angle into a hammerlock. Kurt reaches back, looking for an opening. Finally he reaches over his head and snapmares Douglas with his free hand, forcing him to let go of the hold as he foes down.]
Joey: Quick move by Kurt Angle, and Shane Douglas quick back to his feet, Angle with a dropkick to the back of the head!! He goes for the co - No Shane Douglas kicks out.
Joel: Wow..he's trying to go for the quick win. Joey: Or the mental advantage. [Douglas gets to his feet and runs at Angle, who side-steps and pushes him along into the ropes. Douglas comes off, and Angle leap frogs him. Shane comes off again, and this time he slides between Angle's legs. Douglas leaps into the air and dropkicks him right in the face.]
Joey: And Douglas with a cover this time...only a one count. Both men back to their feetm and Angle eyes Douglas warily and then feints toward him. Shane buys it, and Angle takes him over with an armdrag.
Joel: Shane Douglas has been put through some incredible matches. Shawn Michaels, Ric Flair, even Misawa...this is going to be his toughest one I think. Joey: Shane quickly gets to his feet, and he counters with a Mexican Armdrag. Angle kips up, and takes Douglas down with a Japanese armdrag - fittingly enough. Joel: Wow it's around the World with Armdrags!!! Joey: Both men are going to be pulling out moves we've never seen them pull out today. [Both men get up, Angle swings at Douglas with a clothesline. Douglas ducks and waistlocks Angle from behind. Angle counters with a go-behind, and a run into the ropes. Douglas hooks the ropes with his forearms, and Angle rolls back to his feet,]
Joey: Douglas runs at Angle, and Kurt catches him and drops him throat first across the top rope. Stun Gun!!! Angle brings Douglas back up and whips him into the corner, he follows in with a clothesline.
Joel: As much as this place hates these two men, they're loving this action. Joey: Oh and Angle with that nasty knife edge chops. Douglas reaches out and grabs Angle by the shoulders, and spins him into the corner. He starts ripping away with chop after chop.
[Douglas with a series of open hand chops, and then backhand chops...] Joey: And Kurt counters out of it by unloading on Douglas' head, wailing away on him with lefts to the face.
Joel: Get em' Angle. Joey: You like Angle in this match? Joel: I like both of them...I don't know who to choose. Joey: Angle hops onto the second rope, steps up one, and leaps off with a
FLYING CLOTHESLINE!!!!! First big move of the match, and he quickly makes the cover. ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout.
[Angle doesn't waste any time in bringing Douglas up and whips him into the ropes. He shurgs Shane onto his shoulders as he comes of, then prepares to spin him off into a Flapjack.]
Joey: Douglas spins around though and hits a DDT!!!!!!! Cover...ONE!! TWO!!! No... Joel: Which one of these men will get the advantage first? Joey: Douglas brings Angle to his feet and whips him into the corner, then whips him into the other corner. Shane backs up and runs across the ring, pasting Angle with a high knee.
Joel: Nice Douglas!!! [Angle stumbles out, and Douglas takes him down with a Bulldog.] Joey: He's going for the cover once again...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO...Kickout by Kurt
Angle. Joel: We almost had a new US Champ. Joey: We will have a new US champ. Joel: Really? [Douglas brings Angle up again and hooks him. He then drives him to the mat with a swinging neckbreaker. Rather than hold on for the win, Douglas elects to head to the top turnbuckle. He quickly reaches and measures Angle...he leaps off....]
Joey: Flying fistdrop....Angle moves!!!! Joel: Ouch! Joey: Shane is up, and he's shaking his fist off. Kurt grabs him and he tosses him through the ropes. Shane struggles to his feet, and Angle runs to the ropes..leaps through and hits a tope!!!! Both men crash into the guardrail...this is turning out to be a war!
[Both men take a few minutes to gather themselves, and then Angle brings Douglas up. He takes him back down with a double underhook suplex onto the guardrail...] Joey: OH MY GOD!!!! Douglas' back hit that steel hard, and Angle feels no remorse right now.
Joel: The rulebook has been tossed out. Joey: Earl Hebner is letting them brawl, letting them do whatever they want to win the XCW US Title.
[Angle throws him back into the ring. Kurt climbs in and takes Douglas down to the mat with an Exploder Suplex. Angle makes the cover...]
Joey: ONE!! TWO!!! NO... Joey: Angle is up and he looks to be gett - Joey: Roll up by Douglas...ONE!! TWO!!! NO... [Both men back to their feet and Shane sucks a clothesline by Angle. Shane lifts up Angle and takes him down with a back suplex. Shane brings Angle to his feet and sets up him...]
Joey: FRANCHISER!!!!! ONE!!!! TWO!!!! NO!!! NO!!! Angle kicks out!!! Joel: I can't believe it. Joey: Douglas brings him back up, and he goes for the Belly To Belly....Angle shits his weight. He comes down on his feet and he lifts Douglas into the air, and he drives him down to the canvas with the - Orange Crush!!!!!!
Joel: HE HOLDS FOR THE PIN!!!! Joey: ONE!!! TWO!! Douglas kicks out!!! [Angle can't believe it, but he brings Douglas right back up again. This time, he puts him between his legs. He lifts him up...POWERBOMB...he lifts him up again......POWERBOMB....he goes for a thrd one, and this time it's a Sitdown
POWERBOMB!!!!]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!! NO NO I CAN'T BELIEVE MY EYES!!!!!!! Joel: Nither can Angle. Joey: We have had to close matches so far today....what a night it's been. [The camera picks up Angle who is shouting out a curse, having been sure this was it. Being the pro that he is, he wastes no time in bringing Angle back up.
This time, he goes behind and hooks his head...Inverted DDT.] Joey: NO!! Douglas counters by spinning forward, he lifts him up...NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX!!!! ONE!! TWO!!! Joel: Foot on the ropes!!!! Joey: Angle is lucky enough to be close. If he was an inch further from there, the match would've been over.
[Douglas brings Angle up. He goes for the Single Arm DDT, but using all the raw skill that he has...he twists and turns out of the move, and hits the...]
Joey: OLYMPIC SLAM!!!!! Cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Joel: What the hell is going on? Joey: These two men aren't human...we're seeing Superman vs. Batman right now. [Angle slams his hands on the mat. He brings Douglas back up, he hooks him and goes for a Brainbuster, but douglas counters out by clubbing the side of Angles head a few times. Kurt releases the hold, and Douglas....with an inverted atomic drop.]
Joey: Shane grabs the dazed Angle and sets him down crotch first on the top rope!!! Joel: Right in the Gold Medals!! [Angle falls off, dazed in major pain. He's also in position for Shane to finish him off. Douglas heads up to the top ropes...]
Joey: This could cost him the match... Joel: What's he thinking? Joey: FLYING KNEEDROP!!!! Joel: ANGLE MOVES!!!!! ANGLE MOVES!!!! ANKLE LOCK!!!!! [Shane Douglas trying everything he can to get to the ropes...but he can't withstand the pain. He taps out...]
Joey: Angle wins the match!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner....and NEW US Champion, Kurt Angle!!!! [The crowd pops for him, and Angle stands up and has his arm raises - only to fall back down onto the mat in exhaustion. He gets right back up again, showing his tenacity, and accepts his belt. He raises it high inoto the air to the cheers of the fans.]
Joey: Incredible match here on Hardcore.... Joel: You're tellin' me!!! [All of a sudden Red, White, and Blue confetti starts to fall. "When You Wish
Upon A Star" plays, and out comes Mickey Mouse. The crowd erupts into a huge ovation for him. He climbs into the ring...and we see Kurt Angle say..."I'M
GOING TO DISNEYLAND" with tears running down his face.]
Joey: Micky Mouse has now come in and he's raising Angle's arm. Joel: Um...why is Kurt crying? Joey: It was an amazing match here tonight. [As this is going on, Douglas gets to his feet. Slowly, and obviously in pain. He looks at Angle, who looks right back at him. Shane walks over and raises
Angle's head, and Mikey raises Douglas' hand...]
Joey: WHAT A MOMENT IN XCW HISTORY!! I have a lump in my throat!!! Joel: Fag! Joey: DON'T RUIN THIS MOMENT FO - OH MY GOD!!! STUNNER!!! MICKEY MOUSE JUST STUNNED DOUGLAS!!!!! [After the Stunner Mickey Mouse flips off Douglas with those big white gloves. Angle turns around, and he gets a STUNNER.] Joey: What the hell has gotten into Mickey Mouse?!?!?!? Joel: WHAT!? Joey: Wai - that's not Mickey Mouse!!! That's STEVE AUSTIN!!!!!!! [Steve Austin throws down the Mickey head, and the fans are going wild. The ground is shaking because of all the comotion. Austin flips both men off some more, and then asks for some beers.]
Joey: Steve Austin has come into the XCW again...this time as Mickey Mouse. Joel: He said he's going to be back, and tonight...wow...tonight he showed
Douglas he can be anywhere anytime.
Joey: Austin downing those beers in the Mickey costume. Fans we're outta time!!!! [We fade as Austin is downing a beer in the Mickey Mouse costume....classic picture.]
