XCW Monday Night Malice (part 4)

December 17, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Well lets hope that Mikey is okay... Jesse: Did you see how serious that guy was? I mean seriously....Misawa looks like one mean dude, and as prepared as Triple H looks...it doesn't look like he's prepared enough.
Jim: Well the next match gets to see who will face Misawa or Triple H next week on Malice. If it's Misawa it'll be right here in miserable Japan....but if
Triple H can save the day then we'll be right back in the heart of America.
Jesse: Wouldn't that be nice? Jim: Mikey Whipwreck and Lance Storm will go toe-to-toe, and we'll see who comes out on top here tonight. I have a fe...
(All of a sudden "Cause I got high" starts up on the PA, and the crowd stares at the curtain waiting to see who walks through. Jake "The Snake" Roberts comes staggering out with a bottle Jack in his hand, and no shirt. He points to the commentators table and walks over...)
Jesse: Ugh...he hasn't even gotten all the way over here and I can smell him. Jim: One of the true legends of our sport is here tonight. Jesse: Is he drunk? Jim: Shhh....don't let the fans hear you say that. Jake Roberts the master of the DDT, and mind games.
Jesse: I'm sure he's the master of Free basing too!! (Jake Roberts staggers over and puts on a headset.) Jake: *Burp* Lets get this damn party started. Jake Roberts is here...and the only snake he brought is in his pants!!
Jim: Ummm.... Jake: Come on Jimbo...you know what it looks like. Hell I used to catch you lookin' at it in the locker room from time to time. Maybe if I whip the damn thing out it'll knock the nerves back into your face...
Jim: That was a rather rude comment... Jake: Well I'm a rather rude kinda guy, porkins. Hey I didn't see the Gov. sitting next to us...hey how many cigars have you stuck up an intern? Jesse: Jim might be afraid to hit you, but I'm not afraid to choke you out right here. So watch what you say to me.
Jake: I'm shaking. You know what Jesse...you think you're a good Gov, but let me tell you that Minnesota is the best place to get crack. I'll tell you what...hookers and crack is what I love about St. Paul. Maybe I should write a song?
Jim: Why the hell are you out here? Don't you have somewhere to go? Jake:......No. The XCW is my home now. Jim: Oh no...we're stuck in Japan, and with Jake Roberts. Jesse: I know how to shut him up...hey Jake why don't we invite your old man to sit next to us?
Jake:...........don't you ever talk about my father again. He's nothing but a bastard and a piece of white trash. I was a better wrestler than he ever was, and I'll tell you something else...the demons in me will get to him soon enough.
Jesse: Why are you here? Jake: I have to pay child support some how, and I was sick and tired of getting poked in the ass in jail.
Jim: Well can you call a match? Are you conherent enough to call a damn match? Jake: Sure thing Porkins... FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. The winner will face the XCW World Champion next week on this same show. First coming down to the ring...weighing in at 230 pounds. Here is Lance Storm!!!
(Stoms music starts up and he comes out with a very serious look on his face. The crowd gives him an extremely huge pop as he climbs into the ring. He walks around the ring ignoring all the fans...and not paying attention to any kind of applause they're giving him.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Philidelphia, PA. He weighs in at 225 pounds...here is Mikey Whipwreck!!!
("Loser" by Beck starts to play and Whipwreck comes out holding the side of his head. Obviously he was jumped by Misawa and his men. He makes his way to the apron and Lance Storm nails him with a baseball slide...)
Jim: OH and that's going to start the bell.... Jake: Ya think so? Jim: Storm goes to the outside and throws Whipwreck into the ring. Storm rolls back in and starts putting the boots to him right away.
Jake: You call those boots? I call that stroking a pussy with your foot!!! Try wrestling Dynamite Kid...he'll stiff the hell out of you.
Jesse: I'm going to stiff the hell out of you if you don't shut up. Jake: Ventura...come over here and I'll punch you in that bad heart of yours. Jim: Lance whips Mikey into the turnbuckle and follows in with a clothesline. Storm now fires away with somes lefts and rights to the side of the face, and another huge right hand almost sends Mikey over the top ropes.
Jake: Lance Storm is Canadian? Jim: Yes he is... Jake: Does his wife have hairy armpits? Jim: I don't know...why would you even ask? Jake: Well I did this one chick from Canada one time, and she has the hairiest arm pits. It was the dirtiest thing, and then I went down on her and it was like munching on a damn brown shag carpet!!
Jesse: I'm going to be sick. Jake: You know you like that thick brush...you were a Navy Seal. You can handle the hairy's.
Jesse: The hairy's? Jake: Yeah you know the bits of hair that gets stuck in the back of you..... Jim: ENOUGH!!!! If you're not going to call the damn match then shut the hell up. Jake: I'll just take a few swigs of JD....okay porkins? Happy? Jim: Lance with a scoop slam, and now he runs to the ropes. Quick legdrop by
Lance Storm, and another legdrop. Lance brings Whipwreck back up and executes a
Cradle Piledriver...beautiful move.
Jesse: That'll knock you out in a second. Jim: Lance goes for the cover...1!!! 2.... Jake: Why are you even bothering to count? You know he's going to get up. It says so in your little script right there.
Jim: CAN SOMEONE PLEASE GET HIM OUT OF HERE!?!?!?! WHO HIRED HIM?! Jake: I showed up on my own. Jim: Security? Someone!? OH MY LANCE STORM WITH A SUPLEX TO THE OUTSIDE OF THE
RING!!!! Lance suplexed Whipwreck right to the outside of the ring, and now
Storm is going out after him. Lance throws him right back into the ring, and
Whipwreck is crawling to cover.
Jesse: There's no where to hide.... Jim: Lance whips him into the ropes again....sidekick to the stomach, and Storm with a Side Russian Legsweep...he's going for the cover. 1!! 2!!!
Jake: Kickout. Jim: Kickout by Whipwreck... Jake & Jim: This guy has a lot of fi..... Jim: SHUT UP!!! Jake: Oooo I'm reading your lines. Jim: Storm with an armbreaker, and then takes him down with an armbar. Lance
Storm and Bret Hart have been spending a lot of time together recently, and with someone like Hart in your corner....
Jake: You don't need many enemies. Jim: You don't know what you're talking about...Bret Hart can be an amazing teacher for Lance Storm. Lets hope that he's helping him with his ethics too, because Lance Storm has them all wrong.
Jesse: I think Lance Storm is a legend in the making. The guy has so much untapped potiental it's not even funny.
Jim: Whipwreck is being sent to the ropes again, and Storm with a rolling clothesline...but Whipwreck ducks it. He spins Storm around and nails him with a right punch and another punch. Mikey now with a boot to the midsection and he takes Storm down with reverse neckbreaker drop.
Jesse: Great move by Whipwreck! Jim: Lance Storm slowly recovers from that move, and Whipwreck with a jumping
DDT!!! He goes for the cover, and Lance Storm seems to be seeing some stars right about now.
Jake: No DDT is as good as mine. Jim: 1!!! 2!!! NOO!!!! Storm kicks out...Whipwreck brings Storm back to his feet, and OH GOOD GOD!!! STORM WITH A LOW BLOW, and now he nails him with an uppercut to the chin. Whipwreck falls to the floor, and Lance runs to the ropes, dropkick to the face.
Jesse: That's going to knock some teeth out. Jim: Storm brings Mikey back to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle. Mikey hits chest first, and Storm catches him with a German Suplex. There's the pin...1!! 2!! 3!!!
FINK: Here is your winner...Lance Storm!!!! Jim: Lance Storm wins the match here tonig....wait the ref is telling Fink something. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen....Tiger Mahaagi has informed me that Mikey Whipwreck had his shoulder up before the 3 count, and Lance Storm did not. Therefore the winner of tonights match is Mikey Whipwreck!!!!!! Jake: I'm going to go now...there's a bar calling to me. Jesse: Tell Scott Hall I said HI!!! Jake: (Bleep) you.... (Jake throws off his headsets and walks off. Camera cuts to The Rock who is standing in his dressing room stretching, and then we get a shot of Triple H who is talking to Steph....camera the cuts over to Mitsuharu Misawa talking to all of his buddies.)
Jim: When we comeback...Misawa/Triple H everything is on the line. Don't go away!!!!!! (Camera fades.)
