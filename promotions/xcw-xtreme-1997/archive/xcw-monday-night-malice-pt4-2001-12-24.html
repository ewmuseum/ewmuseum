XCW Monday Night Malice (part 4)

December 24, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Ha! Can you believe that exchange? Jesse: Mikey should feel honored being able to gets chops like that from Ric
Flair!! Jim: Fans this next match should be an.... (All of a sudden Jake Roberts music starts to play and he comes staggering out to the ringside area. His boots aren't laced up, he's got a huge beer stain on his white t-shirt and comes over to sit next to JR and Jesse.)
Jake: Merry Christmas you big fat porkins! Jim: Just when I thought things couldn't get any worse here in Japan! Jesse: Didn't we learn from last week? Jake: You two need to shut up because Ol' Jake has a sack full of some tastey toys....if ya know what I mean, and I'm ready to share them with everyone here.
Even the little kiddies. Jim: God, can someone get him out of here. I'm going to throw up from his smell. Jake: Well I might smell like beer, but you smell like salt. All that fat melting away from the lights. That's disgusting Porkins...you ever think about not eating once in your life?
Jim: Have you ever thought about not drinking? Jake: Heh...nope. Jim: I thought so. Fans we have a US Title match coming up next. The Undertaker will put his title on the line against The Hurricane!!!
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall and is for the XCW US Title. First coming down to the ring and weighing in at 186 pounds.
He's from the Hurri-cave...here is "The Hurricane"!!! ("Stand Back"...is heard all over the arena, and Hurricane runs down to the ring. He does his famous spin in the middle of the ring and waits for The
Undertaker to arrive.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Texas. He weighs in 325 pounds. Here is The
Undertaker!! ("Dead Man Walking" is heard over the PA and then it goes into "Rollin" by Limp
Bizkit. He rides out on his motorcycle, and the crowd boo's him right away. He rides around the ring once and then gets into the ring.)
Jim: There's the bell and Hurricane with a dropkick, and another dropkick. Jake: The Undertaker is older than the boots I have on my feet. Jim: Hurricane with a right hand, and another one...Undertaker brushes them off and grabs him by the throat. Taker turns it around and throws him into the turnbuckle, and now Taker is unloading with lefts and rights to the stomach.
Jake: Just like Taker to not sell and start with offense. Jesse: You did the same thing after your WWF days. Jake: Was I talking to you? I don't think I was.... Jesse: I will knock you out you druken fool. Jim: Easy. Undertaker whips Hurricane into the ropes, and Hurricane comes back with a jumping spinning leg lariat, and both men go down. Hurricane makes it back up to his feet, as does Undertaker....
Jake: Have you ever seen Taker's wife Sarah? Hot body, but man does she look like a man close up. I'd have to put a bag over her head.
Jesse: I heard you were sharing some of your egg nog back stage. Jake: Well when Trish Stratus wants it.... Jesse: NO!! I mean with Kurt Angle.... Jake: You sick mother fuc.....oh wait. Yeah I gave him my special kind of Egg
Nog. HAHA...you know Egg Nog is just another word for elf cum right?
Jim: OH MY DEAR LORD!!! Get him out of here!!! Hurricane from the top rope with a Flying Sunset Flip!!!! 1!!! 2!!! 3!!! WE HAVE A NEW CHAMP!!!!
FINK: Here is your winner...and NEW XCW US Champion....Hurricane!!! Jim: Undertaker was taken by suprise, and Hurricane got the win with no problem. He's out of her...wait he's taking the Undertakers bike. He's running off with the bike, and Undertaker doesn't even realize!!
Jake: Well it's been nice talkin' to you two idiots, but I have to go throw up now. (Jake throws off his headset and starts to walk back to the dressing room. Terry Funk is shown hoping over the guardrail and getting on the microphone.) Terry: JAKE!!!! JAKE!!!! Get back here you old fool.....I'm sick and tired of seeing you like this.
(Jake turns around and just smiles.) Terry: Listen to me Jake...you used to be one of the best ever. You used to make guys like Hulk Hogan shake in their boots, people like Savage wish they were still playing ball. Hell, you still have the best damn DDT in wrestling.
You're throwin' it all away...you're tossin' it right down the crapper, and
Jake...I hate to see you do it, and these fans hate to see you do it. I want to help you....
(Jake starts to get a serious look on his face....) Terry: These fans still care about you...don't chase them away. (Jake starts to walk towards the ring, and he climbs in.) Terry: I am going to help you damnit. We'll beat this thing together, and you'll get the respect you deserve.
(Jake looks at him and then looks at the crowd who gives him a HUGE pop. Jake starts to cry and Terry extends his arms for a big hug. Jake takes the offer and the fans are going wild. Terry raises Jakes arms in the air, and they walk back to the dressing room.)
Jim: Anything can happen around Christmas time!! I can't believe it, but Terry
Funk reached out to Jake and Jake accepted the help. That is amazing!!!
Jesse: Jake just wanted some help... (Camera cuts to Kurt Angle, Misawa and Chono all sitting around the Christmas
Tree. Something is different about all three though. Triple H opens the door....)
Triple H: Okay you two re....WHAT THE HELL!?!? Misawa: BONZAI!!!! Chono: BONZAI!!!! Kurt: BONZAI!!! Misawa: Daniel-san...karate here, not here or here or here. BWAHAHAHA!!!!! Triple H: What the hell did you do to them Kurt? Kurt: Nothing..we're just having some foonies? Foonies? FUNNIES!!!! HAHAHAH!!! Triple H: Wait a damn minute this Egg Nog has alcohol in it!!! Misawa: OH YEAH!!!! Triple H: They're both drunk out of their mind. They can't wrestle... Misawa: We must. (Misawa and Chono stand up but fall down right away. Camera cuts to Mikey
Whipwreck.)
