XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 7)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We comeback and the camera is set on the middle of the ring with the ladder in the middle. The US Title hangs from a huge crane, and the fans know what match is about to take place. Fink announces the participants in the match and both
AJ and Chuck come out to a good reaction to the crowd.]
Ross: This here is set to be a slobberknocker! Cyrus: While your jargon might be a bit off, I do agree that this match will kick all kinds of buttocks. Even those not as dimply and perfect as my own.
Ross: Oh please. Cyrus: There's the bell! Ross: And here we go! Palumbo and Styles sizing each other up here. Lockup... and Palumbo just shoves AJ Styles away. The strength difference is going to play a key factor here in this match. Chuck Palumbo is a hoss.
Cyrus: Ah, but AJ Styles has the intellectual superiority that only the finest of purebred athletes possess.
Ross: What in the world did you just say? Cyrus: He's smart, JR. Ross: Ya coulda just said it like that. Cyrus: Not everyone is from Oklahoma, JR. Ross: That's true... wait a minute. Cyrus: Another lockup... and Styles shoved right back down. C'mon, AJ. Show this cretin your true psychological prowess.
Ross: OH! Wicked clothesline by Palumbo as Styles stands up, and I think AJ just showed us his lariat-recieving prowess there. AJ back up, ducks another clothesline by Palumbo.
Cyrus: OH HO HO! Discus clothesline by AJ Styles, and down goes Mr. Hoss,
Chuck Palumbo. Chucky is getting back up... right into a boot to the gut.
Styles off the ropes. OH YEAH! One-armed spinning neckbreaker, and Chuck
Palumbo is learning that AJ Styles isn't some small guy he can push around.
This kid is pure dynamite. Ross: I hate to agree with you, but Styles recovered nicely from Palumbo's early attacks, and now is firmly in control.
_SMACK!_ "WOOOOO!" Cyrus: Yikes. Now _that's_ a chop you can be proud of. _SMACK!_ "WOOOOO!" Cyrus: And another, AJ Styles really laying those babies in. _SMACK!_ "WOOOOO!" Ross: Those chops welting up the chest of Palumbo... right hand from Palumbo! Another right! And another drops AJ Styles! AJ up quick, but Chuck is waiting... pumphandle powerslam! Muh gawd, what impact.
Cyrus: Chuck smartly bringing the stompage, not letting AJ get much breathing room here. Styles, likewise, smartly scoots out of the ring post-haste.
Ross: What's Chuck doing now? MUH GAWD! Plancha down onto AJ Styles! Chuck
Palumbo with a HOSS-SIZED plancha!
Cyrus: That's going to take all of the air out of Styles, but it's also going to hurt Palumbo as well. That might not have been the best of moves there.
Ross: Chuck holding his ribs, but he seems better off than AJ Styles, who hasn't moved a muscle since getting almost 300 pounds of HOSS dropped on him from ten feet up! Chuck drags AJ up... locks his hands... OH! And rams AJ
Styles right into the ring apron. And again!
Cyrus: Very smart move by Palumbo, concentrating on AJ's back. You can't climb a ladder if you can't move off the mat. And with that US title hanging high overhead, neither of these men can afford to give the other any breaks.
Ross: Chuck with a single-arm lift... right into a backbreaker. Chuck now going over... and he's got himself STEEL!
Cyrus: A steel chair to be exact. He's measuring up AJ... _CRRRRRRRRACK!_ Ross: MUH GAWD! Hellacious chairshot to the back! Cyrus: That'll mess up your vertebrate. Ross: Chuck disposes of the chair, measures up AJ... and drops an elbow right into the base of the spine. Reposition... and a seated full nelson!
Cyrus: That move almost like a variation of a camel clutch, pulling AJ Styles backwards while shoving his head and neck forwards. It doesn't help AJ that
Chuck is sitting down right where he dropped that elbow just seconds ago, either. There are no submissions in this match, but Palumbo might be making a smart move here. Ground the kid, work on his back, then claim the title from the cripple at the end.
Ross: Relenqishing the hold, Chuck picks up AJ Styles and tosses him back into the ring. Chuck onto the apron... slingshot splash right down across AJ's back! I am impressed tonight by young Chuck. He's showing that he can go aerial just as well as a cruiserweight like AJ Styles.
Cyrus: I don't take anything away from Chuck Palumbo. He's a wonderfully put together athlete, and he knows how to work in that ring in multiple ways. But
AJ Styles is one of the most amazing talents to come around in the past five years, and no matter how much I'd love to say the bigger man always wins... I don't think that'll be the case here tonight.
Ross: Lifting AJ off the mat... vertical snap suplex puts AJ back down. Now
Chuck positions AJ onto his belly... Chuck going to the middle rope!
_THUD!_ Cyrus: No one home! Chuck Palumbo went for a flying kneedrop down into the back of AJ Styles, but hit nothing but mat as Styles moved out of the way. Oh, and Chuck is limping... AJ with a dropkick to the knee! Like a shark, JR, AJ is right there to capitalize on the smallest injury.
Ross: But AJ is still favoring that lower back, and landing that dropkick couldn't have helped matters. Regardless, AJ shoves Chuck back into the corner... backs up... MUH GAWD! Whatamanuever!
Cyrus: Dropkick, using the momentum to backflip, land on his feet, and then chopblock that knee of Chuck's as he stumbled out of the corner. It's moves like that that make AJ Styles one of the most exciting competitors today.
Ross: Chuck getting back to his feet, really favoring that right leg... MUH
GAWD! Dropkick by AJ Styles, and one of the best I think I've seen in my long career of calling matches.
Cyrus: AJ doesn't just hit you with that dropkick, he almost uses your own body as a springboard to flip off of, immediately putting himself right back in position to attack. It's very smart wrestling, JR. And AJ picks up Chuck, nailing him with a forearm shiver to back him into the corner... and a solid kick to the knee to keep him there. AJ sees the opportunity... leaping legdrop to the back of the... HOLY SHIT!
Ross: MUH GAWD! CHUCK PALUMBO JUST BACKDROPPED AJ STYLES OVER THE TOP ROPES TO THE FLOOR! Cyrus: With AJ Styles' leg draped across the back of Palumbo's neck nonetheless. That was an impressive display of power, and it gives Palumbo that much needed rest to massage that knee back into working shape.
Ross: I don't know if Styles is going to be able to continue this match. That was a nasty spill he took to the floor. But I don't think Chuck cares, as he's coming out to get him some! Grab... standing headscissors... POWERBOMB!
POWERBOMB! RIGHT ON THE DAMN CEMENT! Cyrus: Jesus. AJ Styles' back might have been hurting before, but I think it's just about broken now. This one might be over right here, and Chuck Palumbo can feel it. He's going under the ring... and he has the ladder!
Ross: Sliding it into the ring, Chuck follows suit, and now he's setting that ladder up. Having a bit of trouble, but I really don't think he needs to worry about time here. The ladder is setup! Chuck is climbing!
Cyrus: But look at how slow he's going, JR. That right leg of his is still hurt, and he's having difficulty getting himself up there.
Ross: But by Gawd he's getting up there, and I think we may have Chuck Palumbo retaining... MUH GAWD! NO! NOOOOOOOOOOOO!
_Creeeeaaakkkk... ... ... ... CRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRASH!!!!!!!!!_ "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" Cyrus: AJ Styles just barely shoved over that ladder, with what had to be his last ounce of strength... and Chuck Palumbo has crashed headlong into the ringside barrier outside the ring from fifteen feet up! He has to be out! AJ
Styles, with one push, may have just won himself the XCW United States
Championship! Ross: Both men down! Neither man moving! AJ clutching his back! Chuck clutching his jaw! MUH GAWD! It's a trainwreck!
Cyrus: Styles is pushing himself off the mat. AJ now grabs that ladder... and is setting it up!
Ross: But nowhere near the title. What in the world is this young man thinking about? Cyrus: AJ scaling that ladder, gingerly. His back is still hurting. Chuck
Palumbo on the outside... and he's bleeding profusely from the mouth! That has to be a broken jaw. But Chuck is muscling it up, and getting to his... OH SHIT
NO!
Ross: MUH GAWD! "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" Cyrus: Flying crossbody from the top of the ladder ALL THE WAY TO THE FLOOR! Chuck Palumbo, AJ Styles... neither man is moving! What was AJ Styles thinking?! What in the world can happen next?
Ross: We may have to get some paramedics out here for these two men. Yes... yes, here come the medics. I think we're going to stop this match right here, because quite frankly, neither of these men are going to be able to continue.
Cyrus: A pair of gurneys, and between Chuck's broken jaw, and what has to be several broken ribs on AJ's part, the hospital is going to have a long night ahead of them taking care of these two.
Ross: AJ Styles now loaded up onto that stretcher... Chuck Palumbo loaded onto another. Folks, we're sorry this match had to end this way, but when two men put their bodies on the line like that, something bad is bound to... what?
Cyrus: No way. There's no conceivable way. Ross: MUH GAWD! Chuck Palumbo has rolled off his gurney... AND HE TIPS OVER AJ STYLES! STYLES CRASHES CHEST FIRST INTO THE RING BARRIER! Cyrus: Now what? What can Palumbo be thinking here? He's grabbed AJ and has him in an inverted standing headscissors... lift!
_CRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRASH!_ Ross: MUH GAWD! THROUGH THE HOSPITAL GURNEY! CHUCK PALUMBO JUST INVERTED POWERBOMBED AJ STYLES THROUGH THAT DAMN HOSPITAL GURNEY! Cyrus: AJ Styles' ribs taking a beating, and then some. If he didn't have broken ribs before, he certainly does now!
Ross: Palumbo grabs up AJ Styles... and he's dragging him back to the ring! AJ
Styles is coughing... coughing up blood!
Cyrus: A sure sign of a punctured lung. But Chuck Palumbo is just relentless, as he rolls Styles into the ring, and follows in. Chuck dragging AJ Styles up again... standing headscissors... Sit-Out Powerbomb! Goodnight!
Ross: Thankfully, Chuck has grabbed that ladder, and despite whatever injuries he has recieved, he's leaving here with the XCW United States championship.
All he needs to do is climb that ladder, grab the belt, and we can get AJ
Styles some medical attention. Chuck has the ladder setup. And he's climbing it. This is academic folks.
Cyrus: This has been a wicked match, in more bad ways than good, but a fitting end here, as Chuck Palumbo uses AJ Styles' own offensive move against him to finish...
_Creeeeeeeeeeak... ... .... ...... "OHHHHHHHH!" Ross: AJ STYLES KICKED THE LADDER! CHUCK PALUMBO HAS BEEN CROTCHED ON THE TOP ROPE! Cyrus: And Chuck falls to the outside! AJ Styles, somehow, someway, has found the energy to keep going. He might be on his back, he might be throwing up blood for the next two weeks, but he's still going. He's still trying to become the US Champion.
Ross: AJ is up! He grabs that ladder! Do it, kid! Shock the world! The ladder is setup! AJ is making the climb!
Cyrus: Here comes Palumbo from the other side, still clutching his Little
Palumbo's, but he's climbing as well!
Ross: Both men struggling... but it looks like Chuck Palumbo will make it first. AJ Styles is too hurt to climb fast enough. Too hurt to make it up first. Palumbo is up...
Cyrus: But he's not going for the title, JR. He wants to do more damage... he's dropping down on the other side, and grabbing AJ Styles around the head. I think I know where he's going, but I don't like it. I think we're going to see a powerbomb from the very top of that ladder.
Ross: LOW BLOW! LOW BLOW BY STYLES! CHUCK PALUMBO FALLS OVER THE TOP OF THE LADDER! Cyrus: He's hung out to dry, Ross! And AJ Styles is following him up! He's... he's setting him up into the tree of woe on top of that ladder! Wait a minute... no way... he wouldn't.
Ross: MUH... _THHHHHHHHHHHUD!_ Ross: ... GAAAWWWWWWWD! MUH GAWD! MUH GAWD! Cyrus: STYLES CLASH! STYLES CLASH FROM THE TOP OF THE LADDER! "HOLY SHIT!" "HOLY SHIT!" "HOLY SHIT!" "HOLY SHIT!" "HOLY SHIT!" Cyrus: AJ Styles fell straight across his ribs, but Chuck Palumbo... Chuck
Palumbo may have a broken neck! His jaw may be broken in MULTIPLE places.
Hell, Chuck Palumbo may be DEAD. That was insane. Completely, and utterly insane. Ross: What now?! What happens now?! Both men are down! Both are completely spent! Cyrus: STYLES IS UP! Ross: How?! How does he do this?! Cyrus: HE'S CLIMBING, JR! HE'S CLIMBING! Ross: Chuck Palumbo is out cold! No one to stop him! Claim it, kid! You earned it! In front of all of these fans here tonight, and all of them watching at home, you proved you were the gutsiest kid alive!
Cyrus: YES! AJ STYLES HAS IT IN HIS GRASP... AND HE PULLS IT FREE! NEW US CHAMP! NEW US CHAMP! Ross: Muh GAWD what a hellacious encounter. How can we follow that with _anything_? Cyrus: AJ Styles falls from the top to the mat, clutching that US title to his chest. Folks, even I, Cyrus the Virus, have to say this; Oh... My... GOD!
Ross: What can happen next? What can happen to top this, on this night of shocking twists and turns?
