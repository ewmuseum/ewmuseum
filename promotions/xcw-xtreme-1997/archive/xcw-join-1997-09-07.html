Join X-Treme Championship Wrestling!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!, 7 September 1997

7 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you sick and tired of joining feds that close after three weeks or even three months? Are you sick of joining a fed that you think is really looks good and then when they write a card it reminds you of the days you were in 3rd grade? Well look no further. X-Treme Championship Wrestling is a fed that doesn't have any of that crap. We've got our stuff together and we're ready to give you the full product. We're here to make sure that you don't have to join boring feds anymore. We're here to make sure you don't have to join feds that close down the next day. We're here for YOU the Role Player. I know that hundereds of people come onto the NEWSGROUPS every day and say that they're different, and they're the best. Well that's a bunch of BULL SHIT.....X-Treme is a fed that can back up it's words, and you can tell that to your Mother, Father, and the rest of your family. We're the best Fed in all of Fantasy Wrestling. Let me just give you a little bit of what you get to look forward to in the most X-Treme fed!!!!! 1. Two cards a week full of action packed wrestling. A. Monday Night RAGE - Almost like a Nitro or a Raw, but way more Extreme. Angles galor on this card. Story Lines Develop and titles change. A promise from me to you.....THIS CARD WILL NEVER BE LATE OR SUCK!!!!!!!! B. Thursday X-Treme - It lives up to it's name. This is a totally Extreme Card with different matches, and some great interviews with Role Players. Another promise to you............THIS CARD WILL NEVER BE LATE OR SUCK!!!!!!! 2. A PPV Every Other Month - Why is this Every Other Month? Well that's simple to answer the only reason we're doing that is to make the angles and story lines HOT, HOT, HOT for the PPV's we do have. It's that much more worth it!!!!!!!!! Alright well that's just a little bit of what you're going to get if you join the most X-Treme fed on the planet. You'll get a whole lot more, but why tell you now? If you want to join let me know!!!! E-Mail.
