XCW Monday Night Malice, 15 September 2003

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The TV fades to black right after the episode of "18 Wheels Of Fury", and then we see the XCW logo...then we get the song "Bring It On" by Nick Cave. Shots of Ric Flair and Kurt Angle going toe-to-toe is shown, quick shot of the return of Ted DiBiase, Horsemen, and then we see a huge explosion...and we cut to a sold out auidence at the American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida. Fireworks are blasting off, fans are waving their signs, and we hear an air horn in the distance. We cut to the two men who will be bringing us all the action tonight...MIKE TENAY and DON CALLIS.] Mike: Welcome everyone to American Airlines Arena. What a show we have for you tonight, as Ric Flair and The Sandman go one on one for the XCW World Title. Don: That should be one hell of a match. It's the return of The Sandman, a guy who has been jerked around the XCW...well tonight he gets what he wants, and that's a title shot at the XCW World Champion. Mike: Fresh off his win for the US Title, Ricky Steamboat will battle it out with the former XCW World Champion. Kurt Angle. Don: Now Former World Champ just doesn't sound right...Kurt Angle was robbed last week, you know it and I know it. These people, they know it...but they probably forgot who the XCW World Champion was anyway. Mike: Fans the return of Vader tonight, all the Horsemen are on the show. It should be one hell of a night. Don: With the return of Vader, I'm sure that Ricky Steamboat is going to be watching his back very carefully. I mean when you run into a truck head on, and get run over...you don't do the same thing seven days later. In fact you never do it again. Mike: Well lets not wait any further for the show...take it away Howard Finkel. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen welcome to MONDAY NIGHT MALICE!!! The following contest is set for one fall, and is a tag team match up. First coming down to the ring at this time, at a total combined weight of 476 pounds...here is Triple H and Eric Angle!!! [Both Eric and Triple H walk down to Hunter's music. Eric doesn't seem to mind, and he doesn't even really seem into the match at all. Hunter on the other hand has something on his mind, something to the effect of revenge. He was embarassed last week by Barry Windham, and even though he doesn't face Barry....he gets to seek revenge another way.] FINK: And their opponents....weighing in at a total combined weight of 467 pounds. Here is the team of Tully Blanchard, and Arn Anderson....two memebers of the Four Horsemen!!! [The sound of horses galloping rings throughout the arena, and then the sound of the guitar is heard. The fans rise to their feet as this is the moment that they have been waiting for, for at least seven days. Arn and Tully walk out in their black trunks, and everyone goes crazy. They raise their four fingers right away, and they continue down the walk way. Fireworks shoot off behind them....and they both slide into the ring at the same time.] Mike: There's the bell and this one is underway...it looks like Tully is going to start off against Eric, and this should be a good match up between these two. Don: Well Tully isn't a spring chicken anymore, so you have to think about that. Eric Angle hasn't even reached his potiental at all yet...so I think it's going to be more one-sided that anything. Mike: Both men with a lock up... [Jocking for position, and Tully with a go-behind. Eric reaches for the foot, but Tully is too smart and he turns it into a side headlock. Eric shoots him to the ropes, Tully comes back but Eric with a drop down. Tully bounces back off, but tags Arn at the same time.] Mike: Tully ducks the clothesline by Eric. Blanchard hangs onto the ropes, and Arn taps Eric on the shoulder. Don: Run out of the ring. Don't even look. Mike: Eric turns around and he gets a slap to the face!!!! [Eric holds his jaw, turns around and walks right into a bodyslam by Tully. Arn heads to the ropes and drops a knee across the face. Eric pops up and holds his mouth, and he walks right into a back elbow from Arn Anderson. The fans all rise to their feet as Arn raises his arms in excitement. We get a shot of Triple H who is pissed...] Mike: Look at that mug, he doesn't look to happy does he? Don: Would you be? Triple H hasn't had the best of luck in the XCW...he has what we like to call "Mikey Whipwreck" disease. Mike: Oh please...Mikey Whipwreck didn't have any problem but himself. Don: True that. [Eric slowly gets back to his feet and Arn drives a knee into the midsection, and he executes a drop toe-hold and once again Eric is on his stomach holding his face. Arn doesn't wait one second though, and he brings Angle up again. He whips him into the ropes, goes for a backdrop but forgets to bring his head up. Eric counters with a kick to the chest, Arn pops up and Eric plants a boot into the stomach. He hesitates for a second, and then turns him over with a swinging neckbreaker.] Mike: Nice move to stop the offense of Arn Anderson, and Eric tags out to Triple H. Don: Now we're going to see a classic match. Arn Anderson and Triple H are two of the smartest wrestlers in the business. Mike: You're right about that...they both know how to work on a body part, they both know how to slow the match down. [Triple H waits for Arn to get on his hands and knees, and then he drives a boot into the stomach. Arn crumbles to the floor, and Hunter drives a high jumping knee into the chest. Hunter gets up again, and he executes a second one. The crowd by now is booing him, but he doesn't hear it. The only thing he hears is Arn screaming out in pain. Hunter brings Arn back to his feet and drives a fist into the forehead, another backs him into the turnbuckle.] Mike: Hunter not about to show any mercy towards Arn Anderson... Don: Well if Hunter and Eric beat the Horsemen in their debut match tonight, you can bet that it's going to be a huge boost for them. Mike: No doubt about that. [Hunter drives a knee into the stomach, and another knee. He executes an Irish Whip, and Arn bounces right into the turnbuckle and comes out into a huge clothesline by Hunter. Arn falls down onto the back of his neck, and he graps it in pain.] Mike: WOW vicious clothesline by Triple H, and he makes the tag back out to Eric Angle. Don: And now Eric can do it to you 5 million different ways, and how much do you want to bet that he's going to work over that neck? [Sure enough Eric walks over and kicks the back of Arn's neck. Another kick, and Arn rolls away from him. This isn't going to stop Eric Angle though as he brings Arn back to his feet and whips him into the ropes...Arn flies off and Eric catches him with a belly-to-belly suplex.] Mike: Arn goes crashing down to the canvas, and Eric Angle pops up to celebrate. Don: That was perfect, he executed that move almost as well as his brother. Mike: I'm sure he would love to hear you say that. [Eric brings Arn back to his feet, and he goes for a short clothesline. Arn ducks it though, and plants a boot into the stomach of Eric...and then a DDT. Eric's head snaps off the mat, and Arn lays there to regain himself.] Mike: Now this could be the chance for Arn to make the tag. The fans are on their feet trying to give him some support, and it looks like Eric is going to make the tag first. Eric Angle crawling over to Triple H, and Arn starts to move. Don: Eric's gonna make it there first. Mike: He makes the tag to Triple H....Arn dives....AND MAKES THE TAG!!! [Tully comes in and he blocks a right hand, and he comesback with his own. Another right hand, and another one. Hunter is wobbley, and he whips him into the ropes. Hunter bounces off, and Tully nails him with an inverted atomic drop. Eric comes in and he gets a backdrop for his troubles.] Mike: Tully is a man on fire here....he's taking care of business, and Eric and Triple H need to re-think things. Don: Yeah I would say so. Mike: Eric is up again, and Tully with a massive eye rake. [Eric staggers around the ring, and he bumps into Triple H. He executes the Olympic Slam on Hunter, and Hunter goes down hard. Eric still can't see anything as he makes it to his feet...and he walks right into a Stun Gun. Both men are on the canvas, and Tully makes it back to his feet. He crawls over and hooks the tights....] Mike: Here we go!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Don: He cheated!!! FINK: Here are your winners....Arn Anderson and Tully Blanchard!!!!! Mike: Tully came in there just ready for anything, and he got a little help from Eric Angle. Don: I don't even think that Eric knew he hit the Olympic Slam on Triple H. This is going to give Hunter just another reason to hate The Angles. [Tully and Arn leave, and you can tell that they have a lot of ring rust. Arn looks like he has run a marathon, and Tully is favoring his back. We cut back to the ring and Triple H is recovering from the Olympic Slam he just took. Eric is up and he looks a little worried.] Don: If that isn't the look of being scared I don't know what is. Mike: Eric Angle is about to leave the ring, he's gonna leave without even explai - oh you can forget that idea. Hunter dives for him, and he brings him back into the ring. Don: Uh oh. [Triple H starts yelling at him, and pointing the finger in his chest. He pushes Eric, and Eric pushes him back. Hunter is about to punch him, but Kurt Angle slides in from behind him and clips the back of his knee. He gets up and both Angles start putting the boots to Triple H.] Mike: Well that is one way to stop an angry Triple H. Don: Leave it up to the brother to help you out. Mike: The Angles leave the ring with a fallen Triple H, and fans we'll be right back after this!!! [Camera cuts to a commercial.] [When we comeback from the commercial break we see Triple H smashing things up in the locker room. We see Shane Helms walk by, and Triple H grabs him and attacks him. Hunter throws him into the cement wall, and then grabs him and throws him into one of the metal lockers. Hunter starts yelling at him...] Hunter: Tonight I finally get the respect!! If I can't earn respect, I'm going to take it from every last one of you! Tonight...this all comes to an end. Now get out of here! [Hunter pushes the camera away, and we cut back to the ring. Ted DiBiase is standing there inside the ring with a huge smile on his face. Virgil stands right behind him and gives the camera a cold hard stare. DiBiase begins to speak...] Ted: Last week I told you I would deliver Sting, and that is what I have done. In just a few minutes we're going to bring back The Stinger...the man, the myth, the living legend. But it doesn't stop there...oh no. It's not about to stop there. You see, next week....next week I bring back someone who needs no introduction. Someone who I brought into the WWF, and someone that has made people scream, shout, and drink some beer. [Crowd starts to pop.] Ted: That's right....I'm going to bring to you the one and only, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin. [Everyone jumps to their feet and starts to go crazy.] Ted: You see....like I have said before, everyone's got a price for the Million Dollar Man. Steve's price was just a couple of beers, and a new trailer to live in. HAHAHA, so don't doubt the Million Dollar Man....because he will always prove you, your mother, and your father wrong...and then he'll buy them! MUAHAHAHAHA!!! [Ted DiBiase leaves to a huge heel response, and the music starts to play.] Mike: Steve Austin...here next week? Don: I guess so. I mean how cool is that? You see we get Sting this week, and Steve Austin the following week. Mike: Ted DiBiase like him or hate him, he knows how to deliver. He knows how to make things happen, and I'm sorry to say but that's more than Chad has ever done for us. Don: Damn right about that. Mike: Fans before we go to this next match I just wanted to tell you that Shawn Michaels won't be wrestling for a long time. He has injured 3 disks in his back, and the doctor has let us all know that it won't be any time soon that he'll be back. Don: I heard that he's even having trouble walking. Mike: Fans Shawn Michaels wanted to comeback to be apart of the XCW again, for one more big run...but due to Vader...we're not going to see that. Tonight, Vader is here and he's taking on Barry Windham. I hope Barry is prepared, because Vader wants to make a name for himself. Don: Yeah, and if he can take out one of the Horsemen...heh, that's going to jump him right up there. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Sweetwater, Texas....weighing in at 273 pounds, here is Barry Windham!!! [The sound of horses galloping is heard, and out walks Barry Windham. The fans jump to their feet and he walks in his black tights, and his black leather vest. He climbs into the ring, and he waits for his opponent.] FINK: And his opponent...from Boulder, Colorado. He weighs in at 400 pounds even, here is Big Van Vader! ["What time, it's time, it's Vader....time, time time!!" rings through the American Airlines Arena, and the lights go dark. Out walks Vader, the huge beast. He doesn't waste much time dealing with the fans, he just storms down to the ring. He uses the ring steps to climb in, and the bell sounds.] Mike: Vader walking right up to Barry, and he starts in with those huge swinging punches. Vader with a shot to the stomach, and another shot. He backs Barry into the corner. Don: This isn't good. [Vader unloads with lefts and rights to the head. One right after the other, and Barry is putting is forearms up to cover his face but it's not working. Vader even works in a few jabs, and then goes to the ribs. Barry falls back against the turnbuckle, and Vader just continues to beat the living hell out of him.] Mike: I can't believe this....come on get in there ref. Get in there and take care of this. Vader should not have his opponent held up in the turnbuckle like that. Don: Well that's what he does, and that's what he's going to do until someone stops him. You think Barry can stop this beast? Not a chance...Shawn Michaels couldn't, and Ricky Steamboat sure as hell can't. Mike: Finally the ref gets in there. [The ref squeezes himself in between the two wrestlers, and Vader backs up for a second. He pushes the ref away, and he charges into the turnbuckle was a HUGE splash. He backs up, and Barry staggers out of the corner only to receive a massive clothesline which takes him down.] Mike: You've gotta be kidding me. Oh man, this guy isn't about to let up. Vader runs to the ropes, and he drops a splash onto Barry. He goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!! TH - NO...he didn't get him. Don: You're gonna have to do a lot more than that to keep someone like Windham down. Mike: Barry Windham is one hell of a fighter, and he's going to keep trying and fighting. [Vader brings Barry back to his feet and gives him a couple kidney punches. Barry tries to get away, and he drops to his knees holding his lower back. We get a nice shot of blood trickling out of the nose of Barry...] Mike: And that can't be good for one of the Horsemen. Don: Yeah that's not good. Mike: Vader might have broken Windhams nose, oh and what a massive right hand to the back of the head. Barry falls down to the canvas in a heap of nothing. [Vader yells for Barry to make it back to his feet, but you can bet that Barry hears nothing but the muffled crowd cheering for him. Barry starts crawling to the turnbuckle, and he's trying to help himself up. He grabs the first turnbuckle, and then reaches for the second rung...and Vader comes charging in with a splash. Vader hooks Barry from behind...] Mike: OVER HEAD GERMAN SUPLEX!!!!!!!!!! Don: WHOA!!!! Mike: He tossed Barry over his head like he was a lightweight. What an awesome display of power on the part of Vader. I mean this guy doesn't fear anything, and he's going to do whatever he can to make sure you stay down. Don: This makes me glad I never really persued wrestling as a career. Can you imagine a guy like me climbing into the ring with Vader? Mike: Ugh... Don: The guy wouldn't stand a chance! [Barry Windham hasn't moved since that brutal attack, and Vader just stands over him and smiles. He reaches down and brings Barry back to his knees, and Barry goes for a shot to the stomach. Another shot to the stomach, and Vader looks a little stunned. Barry back to his feet and he drives a knee into the stomach of Vader.] Mike: And Barry is fighting back! Don: Not for long. Mike: Barry has blood dripping out of his nose, his face has been pounded into just meat. He's fighting back though. [Barry Windham whips him into the ropes, Vader bounces off and Barry nails him with a huge boot to the stomach. Barry runs to the ropes, and Vader pops up catches him with a Vader Attack....Barry crumbles to the canvas.] Don: What did I tell you? Mike: Well Barry is trying everything he can, the man is at least trying. Don: UH OH. [Vader gives the sign for the match to end. He brings Barry to his feet, puts him between his legs...lefts him up...] Mike: POWERBOMB!!!! POWERBOMB!!!! POWERBOMB!!!!! Don: It's over. Mike: Vader hooks the leg....ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! [The bell rings and Vader's music starts up, and the crowd boos right away. Vader stands up and stalks around the ring, trash is being thrown in, and even a water bottle hits him in the face but it has no effect. Vader walks over, and it looks like he isn't done with Barry.] Mike: And the ref needs to get in there before he causes some real damage. Don: Would you get in front of him? [Vader reaches down and picks up Barry...but before he can do anything we see Arn, Tully, and Ric Flair race down to the ring. All of them have chairs, and the fans pop like mad. Vader backs off and Tully goes over to check on Barry.] Mike: The Horsemen are here.... Don: A little late? Mike: Well any later and Barry would be right next to Shawn Michaels. [Vader backs off and he points to Ric Flair. He leaves the ring area to huge heel heat, and we cut to a commercial.]
