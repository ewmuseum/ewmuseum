XCW Monday Night Malice (part 2)

December 23, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We comeback from a commercial and Ric Flair is sitting with all the other members of the Four Horsemen. Flair has a somber look on his face, but the fans who are watching on the monitor are still giving him the respect that he deserves.]
Ric: Last week, what happened to us was a disgrace. Something that I can't describe, something so bad that it almost made me quit wrestling all together.
Last week we were embarassed, humiliated, run down like a bunch of old folks...and we're here this week and we've lost a little bit of what the
Horsemen once were. I can't go out in front of these fans and be the man...Mike
Sanders ran a better game than us last week, and he beat the man at his own game. Hell, we used to be the ones who made people feel the way I do. We used to be the ones who u -
Arn: Quit your moaning...listen to yourself. Flair...Mike Sanders might have gotten the best of us one time, but how many times have we over come adversity?
How many times have we picked ourselves up and shown punks like him how things are done? Just because we got our asses handed to us last week, does that mean that we're going to tuck our tail between our legs and run? The Flair I knew was a man who didn't give up after being attacked, he was a man that come hell or high water got revenge. These four guys who attacked us are nothing more than punks who got lucky, and now they think they own the world. Well I'm not gonna take that, and you sure as hell aren't going to. So why don't we have them play our GAME...our WAR GAME!!!
[Everyone in the STAPLE center cheers like crazy, and Flair just stares off into nothing...]
Jim: War Games? Tomorrow...they've got to be kidding. Don: Yeah I think as soon as the camera cut out they all had a good laugh. Jim: I sense sarcasm! [The Hardy's music starts to play and the crowd boos right from the start. They come out hamming it up like crazy, and slide in. Before you know it..The Filthy
Animals music starts up, and they come out to a HUGE response.]
Jim: So it looks like Matt and Rey are going to start the match off here tonight. Matt charges at Rey, but Rey counters with a dropkick to the knee.
Matt gets right back up, but is taken down quickly by a dropkick to the chest. Don: That has to be annoying. [Rey brings Hardy back to his feet for an Irish whip but Hardy reverses it, Rey ducks a clothesline attempt by Matt and hits a hangman's neckbreaker. Rey runs to the ropes and springboards off with a legdrop across the throat.]
Jim: Great work by Rey Misterio trying to keep Matt Hardy down. He brings him right back up and whips him into his turnbuckle. Rey follows in with a huge splash for such a little guy.
Don: He's got him in what they like to call the Bario... Jim: Filthy Animals corner. Rey works over the midsection with a series of kicks to the stomach, and big slap across the face.
[Rey tags out to Juvi, and Juvi lifts Matt onto the top turnbuckle. Super
Frakensteiner!!] Jim: WOW and Matt Hardy just caught huge air. Don: He flew right to the center of the ring, and now what the hell is that
Mexican Jumping Bean doing?
Jim: Juvi is going to the top...he raises his arms into the air. 450 SPLASH!!!! WAIT!!! Matt moved out of the way!! Both men are down, and now it's a race for the tag. Matt tags out to Jeff, and Juvi tags out to Rey!
[Jeff meets Rey with an armdrag. Rey gets back to his feet and is met with a hiptoss this time. Jeff hits a series of lefts and rights to back Rey into the ropes, and he clotheslines him over the top to the outside.]
Jim: Rey to the outside!!! Jeff with a running start now...he runs...lands on the top rope, flying crossbody...REY MOVES!!!!!!
Don: Oh my God he's dead. Jim: Rey moved out of the way, and Jeff landed hard onto the cement floor. If this match is anything like the big six man tomorrow...it's going to be a match of the year candidate.
Don: This place hasn't been this excited since the Lakers actually got a win...last year. [Rey with some lefts and rights, but Matt comes over the top rope with a plancha and takes him out. All three men are trying to get up, and with that we see Juvi run to the ropes and take all three men out with a suicide dive over the top rope.]
Jim: The ring looks like a damn war movie. Bodies everywhere... Don: I haven't seen so many people that high since I hung out with RVD last month. Jim: Rey moved out of the way of that dive, and he's recovered by now. Rey throws Jeff back into the ring, and he locks in a Surfboard!!!!
[Juvi pounds away on Matt outside the ring, while the action takes place inside the ring as well. Jeff is trying to reach the ropes, and while the ref is distracted with that we see Matt in the background grab a chair and smash it over the head of Juvi.]
Jim: Did you hear that? Don: Hear what? Jim: Nevermind. Matt slides into the ring and drops a knee across the face of
Rey Misterio. Matt brings him up...TWIST OF FATE!!!!
Don: It's over!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Rey kicked out...I don't know how he did it, but he kicked out of the TOF.
[Juvi is angry now and he is trying to climb into the ring, but the ref won't allow it. Matt makes a "clap" sound, and climbs into the ring. He starts to stompt he upper body of Rey]
Jim: Tomorrow nights match should be an amazing one. Don: Well you add Eddie and RVD to something like that and you're going to get something special. I mean lets hope it lives up to it's expectations. All the hype, and if it doesn't go through...it'll be a damn shame. Just like Two
Towers.
Jim: You didn't like it? Don: Didn't like is to nice a word. Jim: Matt whips Rey into the ropes and takes him down with a back bodydrop. He brings him up once again, sends him to the ropes...and he takes him down with a knee to the midsection.
Don: That'll make you remember what you ate for lunch. Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TH- NO!!! Rey gets his shoulder up just in time. [Matt Hardy slowly climbs to the top ropes, by the time he is up there Rey is just getting to his feet. Rey turns his attention to Matt who flies off the turnbuckle and lands a beautiful clothesline.]
Jim: Matt's not done yet...he's not even close. He grabs Rey by his mask and brings him to his fe - OH LOW BLOW!!!
Don: Damn cheater. Jim: The ref didn't catch that, and Matt falls to the canvas clutching little
Hardy Boys. Rey crawls over to Juvi, and he's almost there. Man, Juvi is dying to get into the ring.
Don: I think it's just because he's on that super crack he likes so much. Jim: HE MAKES THE TAG!!! JUVI COMES IN!!!! [Matt gets up and walks right into a huge leaping clothesline by Juvi, and Matt gets up again only to go back down with a snap dropkick. As Juvi walks by Jeff though, Jeff pulls him by the hair and he falls down to the canvas.]
Jim: Oh come on!!! Juvi is back up and he knocked Jeff off the apron, but here comes Matt!!
Don: These two work together like they're brothers or something. Jim: Matt with a forearm to the back, he turns Juvi around. Boot to the midsection, and he lifts him up...piledriver!!!!
Don: If I were Matt I would've made the tag right there. Jim: Matt goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - No once again they can't get the job done here tonight. Juvi gets his shoulder up just in time, and Matt seems to be as cool and calm as ever.
[Matt brings Juvi up and casually tosses him into the turnbuckle. Matt Hardy charges in and nails him with a clothesline that rocks the ring. Matt yells
"Watch this" to the fans, as he climbs up onto the bottom rope...]
Jim: Jeff comes over and kicks through the ropes to Juvi's stomach...Matt lifts him up for a Piledriver...OH MY GOD!!! Off the 1st rope!!!!
Don: Juvi's neck looks like a road map! Jim: His neck has to be broken. Matt is going for the cover once again, and this time he hooks the leg. ONE!!! TWO!!! Here comes Rey to break things up...that was a close call.
Don: Well I think Rey is just hurting his partner. I mean the impact that Juvi took was not nice at all.
[Matt Hardy gets up and whips Juvi into the ropes once again, he pushes up Juvi into the air and Juvi turns it into a HUGE dropkick to the face. Matt falls down, and Juvi falls down hard.]
Jim: Juvi has got to make that tag here tonight...he's got to make the tag to
Rey. Matt tags out to Jeff, and Jeff helps up Matt.
Don: Both men are going after Juvi now... Jim: JUVI MAKES THE TAG!!!! Rey is in...and his fists are flying like crazy. Dropkick to Jeff, and an armdrag to Matt! [Jeff charges him again, and Rey hits him with a superkick. Matt charges him and Rey hits a Tornado DDT!!]
Jim: I've never seen Rey like this...the man is hitting everything but the kitchen sink. Don: Here comes Jeff - Jim: Jeff goes for a clothesline, Rey ducks and wraps himself around his arm. Come around DDT...Matt charges Rey and gets tossed over the top ropes. Don: And Juvi is going after him. Jim: Rey is going to the outside of the ring, he's going to hit his finisher. He's going to nail it... [Jeff staggers to his feet...] Jim: Wait...PAUL E!!! PAUL E!!!! [As Rey jumps onto the top rope, Paul E smashes the chair into his back. Rey falls forward into a Twist Of Fate...]
Jim: There's the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Don: What a comeback!!! Jim: The ref didn't see it...he was busy with the fight going on the outside. [Fink announces The Hardy Boys as the winners, and the fans disapprove. Matt and Jeff embrace with Paul E, and they all exit to the back. Rey on the other hand is holding his neck in pain. Juvi slides back into the ring to check on his partner...]
Jim: You can be sure that Rey, Juvi and Eddie aren't going to go down like that tomorrow. Don: Paul E is a slick slick man. He's not going to let some guys from Mexico pull one over on him. He knows how do deal with Cheech, and Chong...and umm
Ponch from CHiPs.
Jim: You really are a racist. Don: I hate all minorities equal. Jim: Fans I believe Michael Cole is at the hospital right now, we've got something set up over there so we can find out how Taz is doing. Michael are you there??
[Cut to Michael Cole standing in Cedars Sinai with a Doctor.] Michael: I'm standing here with Dr. Foonbaba...can you tell me what the status is of the XCW wrestler Taz?
Dr Foonbaba: Well Taz is in serious condition right now. He suffered trauma to the head, and he's recieved a broken arm and collar bone as well.
Michael: Anything long term? Dr Foonbaba: From what I can see so far no, but you never know. Taz was in great shape which helped him tremendously, and the fact that his body knows wear and tear is going to help a little more than most people in the recovery.
I don't seem him coming back for at least 6 months though. Michael: Well Doctor I wouldn't tell him that until after Christmas. He was supposed to wrestle Triple H for the World Title.
Dr Foonbaba: I'm talking about saving someones life, and you're talking about some little title. HAHA...please, I need to go to work.
[The Dr. walks away making Michael Cole feel like the little penis that he is. We cut to a commercial.]
