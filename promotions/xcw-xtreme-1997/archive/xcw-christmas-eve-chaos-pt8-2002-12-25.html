XCW Christmas Eve Chaos (part 8)

December 25, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We cut to the locker room with the Horsemen...] Ric: Over 25 years in this business, and my God how things have changed. You can go from never-was, to superstar, to has-been in a blink of an eye. The
Nature Boy, ridin' on top for so many years. Oh so many years, and how sweet it's been baby. We've had good times, bad times, times of craziness, and times of sadness. Through it all I've have one belief, and that belief is...no matter what the situation is, no matter how bad things get...you fight...you fight until the end. As I look at all three of you, I stare into your eyes..I see more excitement than any other day we've shared in the past. Tonight...heh, tonight fellas we're going to make history. Not only are we going to go into
War Games and turn the World on it's ear one more time...we're going to make the world stand up and take notice to a stable, that MADE stables. The Horsemen are going to ride in tonight, and win, lose or draw baby...we're going to walk out proud. WHOOO!!! Ya with me? WHOOOO!!!! ARE YA WITH ME!??
[Everyone "WHOOOO's" in approval, and we cut to Mike Sanders in the locker room area with his group of wrestlers.]
Mike: Men, we have a chance tonight...we have a damn good chance tonight to change the way wrestling is viewed. So many years the old guard has been in charge, and so many years we've sat back and watched. Well tonight the old guard changes, and we take over. They have experience on their side, but the fact is that they don't have the power, they don't have the speed, they don't even have to wit that we do. We're monsters, we're hungary, and we're going to show these guys that it's time for something new. Ric Flair...is nothing more than a has-been self proclaimed living legend. The man isn't everything he's cracked up to be, and I want you to save him for me. If you men get into the ring before me, please save me some of Ric Flair. I want to take him out of this sport more than anything in this world. I want to be the man that beat Ric
Flair, took him out of the game and showed the world that he isn't as great as the New Era...or Mike Sanders. Tonight men...we destroy the Four Horsemen, and we become the Guard. Let's do this!!!!
[Camera fades.] Jim: Fans the next match is for control of the XCW, and as the cage lowers there are many questions that pop up in my mind. Are the Horsemen going to be able to wrestle this new breed of atheletes? Even though the Horsemen are great, can they beat the power and the strength of New Era?
Don: I can answer that in one word...NO. How do you expect a bunch of old guys like the Horsemen to go up against this new talent? I mean the New Era has speed, power, wit...everything that the Horsemen used to have.
Jim: Well fans earlier we had the coin toss, and New Era won that so they'll have the advantage through the match here tonight.
[FINK announces the rules for War Games, and the crowd doesn't seem to pay any attention at all. They're waiting for the match to get underway, as they know they're about to see something that only the XCW can pull off. The first one to come out is Sean O'Haire, and the crowd doesn't give him much of a reaction.]
Jim: This man is a big boy... Don: Big boy doesn't describe this man. [FINK then introduces his opponent which is none other than Tully Blanchard. Tully comes down to the ring with a smile, and a cocky strut. He climbs into the cage, and does his strut right in front of Sean.]
Jim: There's the bell, and the cage is closed. Sean charges Tully, and he levels him with a forearm to the back of the neck. Another forearm.
Don: Every two minutes someone comes in right? Jim: Yes..and the next person will be from New Era. Tully gets whipped into the turnbuckle, and Sean goes downstairs with a right hand. Another right hand, and he throws Tully into the cage face first.
[Tully bounces off hard, and Sean continues his assult on him. Sean with a side kick to the ribs, and then executes a gutwrench suplex.]
Jim: In order to win your opponent must submit, or pass out. There is no pin in this match here tonight.
Don: Tully is getting his clock cleaned. Jim: Sean throws him to the ropes, and he takes him down with a clothesline. Things don't look good so far for the Horsemen. O'Haire now waits for Tully to get up, and Tully blocks a right hand. Tully now with a right hand of his own, and another one.
Don: The old man has some power. Jim: Tully with a thumb to the eye, and Sean backs off quickly. Tully Blanchard spins Sean around and executes an inverted atomic drop.
Don: Maybe I spoke too soon about the old man. Jim: Tully kicks Sean in the stomach, and hits a follow through DDT. Boot scrape by Blanchard, and he's all over this youngster here tonight.
Don: Not for very much longer. Jim: Tully pulls him up by the hair, and drives another right hand to the side of the face. Another one, and Sean blocks it. Tully with a thumb to the eye though, and that backs Sean against the cage.
[Tully grabs Sean's head and starts ramming the back of it into the steel cage. He finally lets go, and Sean drops to his knees. Tully hits a kick right to the teeth, and Sean falls holding his mouth.]
Jim: Brutal kick there by Tully...and we're winding down. [5...4...3...2...1....Randy Orton pops out.] Jim: Here comes Randy Orton...and 3rd Generation wrestler, a man who's father fought some hard battles with Ric Flair as well as Tully Blanchard. Randy climbs into the cage, and he gets a clothesline.
Don: Nice little welcome to the party there. Jim: Tully whips Randy right into Sean, and both men hit heads hard. Double noggin' knocker comin' up...and he nails them both.
Don: Ugh...I hate watching old men wrestle. Jim: Orton and O'Haire are both on dream street, and Tully takes them down with a double clothesline. The man is on fire here tonight, and this place is loving every minute of it.
Don: And in a minute we're going to see another Horsemen? Jim: We certainly are, and business will pick up for sure. [Tully goes to pick up Orton, and from behind Sean O'Haire drives a knee to the back. Sean turns Tully around and hits him with a right hand, Tully turns into the other direction and gets hit with a right hand by Orton.]
Jim: He's like a damn pinball. Don: 100 points, 200 points... Jim: Tully gets hoisted into the air by Sean...and he drops him across the knee of Randy Orton. Now both men start to stomp away at the head of Tully
Blanchard!! Damnit they're taking turns working over the head of Tully, and we're almost ready for a Horsemen to come in and make the save.
Don: You mean get beat up? Jim: Randy and Sean grab Tully by the head and throw him into the steel cage, and he goes down hard.
Don: You think Ric Flair is going to come out in a t-shirt or you think he'll come out with big tits and all?
Jim: You need to show some respect for Ric Flair. Don: I have respect for him, but it went away after he kept wrestling at the age of 88. [Orton and O'Haire bring Tully to his feet and they execute a double hip toss. Tully holds his lower lumbar in pain, and Orton gets a running start and kicks the same part.]
Jim: It's about that time once again... Don: Oh yeah baby! [5...4...3...2...1...and out comes Ole Anderson.] Jim: Ole is down here, and he's making his way down to the ring. Don: Yeah don't run whatever you do...you don't want to save Tully or anything. Jim: Ole climbs into the ring, and he goes to work on Randy Orton. [Ole with a few forearms to the chest, and then he sends Orton to the ropes. Orton gets hit with a spinning spinebuster, and the fans pop. Ole gets up and he charges O'Haire, but Sean catches him with a boot to the face.]
Jim: Whoa...Ole goes down hard. Don: AHAHA....of all the people in the Horsemen why did they ask Ole to comeback? I'd rather have Paul Roma comeback than have Ole wrestle. It's rather sad to see a man who thinks he still has it try to do something.
Jim: Sean whips Ole into the corner, and he follows in with a clothesline. He's choking him out with his boot now.
Don: Randy won't stop kicking the hell out of Tully. He think he's a soccer ball or something.
[Randy brings Tully to his feet and unleashes a nasty chop to the chest, and then hits a boot to the stomach. Randy gets a running start and takes Tully down with a high knee to the mouth.]
Don: How do you stop a charging Horsemen? Jim: How? Don: You hit him with his bed pan. Jim: You ever thought of stand-up? Don: Well... Jim: Don't. [Sean still has that boot choke, and Ole is turning blue. Finally he lets go, but he drives a series of knees into the ribs. Ole falls back against the turnbuckle, and too the canvas.]
Jim: This isn't looking to good for the Four Horsemen. Don: Randy isn't finished with Tully by a long shot. Jim: Sean walks over to help Randy...I guess he got bored with Ole. Both men lift up Tully...OH and they throw him head first into the cage. Tully falls in a heap, and he's holding his head.
Don: I think we're going to see some blood...YES there it is. Jim: Tully Blanchard has been split open like a melon. [Ole comes over and clotheslines Randy down to the canvas, and he turns Sean around. Sean hits him with a roundhouse kick though, and Ole goes down.]
Don: Nothing is keeping him down...nothing is keeping Sean O'Haire down. This kid is impressive.
Jim: Well you're about to see a monster enter this ring here tonight. [5...4...3...2...1...and Batista comes storming out.] Jim: Big Bad Batista is walking down to the ring, and the look on his face could kill someone.
Don: Yeah this guy doesn't mess around. Jim: Batista climbs into the ring, and he looks at his partners and then focuses on the two Horsemen in the ring.
[Both Tully and Ole are getting up at the same time, and Batista double clotheslines them back down to the canvas.]
Jim: Man this doesn't look good for The Horsemen. Don: Looks good to me. Jim: Batista brings Ole to his feet and throws him into the steel cage, and another shot to the steel.
[Tully staggers up, and Batista catches him in an Airplane Spin.] Jim: That move really doesn't work does it? [Batista tosses Tully off of him, and he slams down hard onto the mat.] Don: Well when you throw your opponent like a shot put, yeah it works. Jim: Ole runs up to Batista, and he just tosses him into the air with a Flapjack. [As Ole crashes back to Earth, Batista simply extends his knee and Ole hits it hard. Both Ole and Tully look like they've been in a car wreck. What seems like hours for them in the ring as only been a few minutes.]
Jim: The sad thing is that Batista is letting his team mates get a rest here tonight. Sean and Randy are resting in the corner, and he's doing all the work.
Don: That's called being smart. Jim: Ole is screaming out in pain, and I think he's seriously injured. Ole
Anderson is injured, and Batista picks up on it. Like a shark that smells blood.
[He grabs Ole and lifts him up and excutes a nasty rib breaker. He then pushes down on Ole's head and leg to add to the pressure. Ole screams out and Tully comes over to break it up.]
Jim: Tully with a series of forearms, and here comes Randy and Sean. Both men drives their fists into him, and that'll take care of that.
Don: You know it's a shame because the Old Horsemen would've taken care of these guys with no problem.
Jim: I can't imagine what the XCW would be like if Mike Sanders ran the joint. [5...4...3...2...1...Arn Anderson comes running out.] Jim: And here comes "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson. He climbs into the cage, and he ducks a clothesline by Randy Orton. SPINEBUSTER!!!!
Don: WHOA!!! Jim: Arn turns his attention to Sean O'Haire and he ducks a BIG BOOT. He spins
Sean around and throws him into the steel cage. Sean goes down...and that leaves Batista.
Don: And Arn isn't afraid of him. Arn walks right - OH MY!!! He slapped the taste right out of Batista's mouth.
Jim: Arn with a boot to his stomach, and he takes him down with a DDT. [Arn gets up quickly and makes the "He's Safe" gesture. The crowd goes wild, and Arn Anderson quickly goes over to check on his partners.]
Jim: Randy Orton is back up, and Arn Anderson uses Orton's momentum and throws him into the steel cage.
Don: Now this is a real Horsemen. Jim: Tully is back up now, and he whips Sean into Arn. Arn grabs him...SPINEBUSTER ON O'HAIRE!!!!
Don: He's mowing through them like a lawn mower. Jim: Batista is up, and he drives a right hand into the side of Tully's head. That takes him down, he spins Arn around and whips him into the ropes. Arn reverses, and he catches Batista...SPINNING SPINEBUSTER!!!!!
Don: He's passing em' out like Candy Canes on Christmas. Jim: Listen to these people!!! The Horsemen aren't dead...they're ALIVE AND WELL!!! [Arn brings Batista to his feet and Ole comes out. Both men back him into the corner and unload with a series of boots to the chest. Randy charges them, but out of no where comes Tully with a chop block.]
Jim: VINTAGE HORSEMEN WORK!!!! VINTAGE BY GOD!!!!! Don: Uh oh... Jim: The three members of the Horsemen turn their attention to Sean O'Haire, and Sean begs for mercy. The guy is begging for mercy here tonight...
Don: Wow. Jim: Sean charges Tully, but Ole and Arn catch him. DOUBLE SIDEWALK SLAM!!! Don: That'll change your smile. Jim: And just like that the Horsemen have turned the tide, but it's time for someone else to come in. Mike Sanders is the only one left...for the New Era.
[5...4...3...2...1...Mike Sanders comes out to the ring. He's wearing his street clothes.] Jim: Mike climbs into the cage, and Tully approaches him....WHAT THE HELL!? Don: What is that stuff? Jim: Mike just sprayed som - that's Pepper Spray!!! He pulled the damn stuff from his pocket, and Tully can't see a damn thing.
[Ole comes over and gets the same thing. Ole and Tully are scrambling around the ring, and he's about to do it to Arn but Arn grabs it away from him and throws it onto the canvas.]
Jim: And look at that little rat...he's a damn rat. Mike Sanders begging for mercy, and Arn won't show it to him. Damnit Arn...beat the hell out of him!
Don: HEY!!! Jim: I don't care...I don't want to see Mike Sanders take over the XCW. GOD!!!! Batista from behind with a knee to the back, and Arn walks into a savate kick by Sanders. Don: And here we go. Jim: Sanders with a roundhouse, and another roundhouse. Arn falls to one knee, and Mike Sanders with a dropkick to the face. Arn goes down.
Don: Randy and Sean are taking care of Ole and Tully. Jim: Batista is holding Arn Anderson for Mike...Mike with a slap to the face, and another slap to the face. He's humilating the Enforcer...damnit. COME ON!!!
[You can hear the fans start to chant "Ric", but there is still one more minute left.] Jim: These fans want to see Ric, I want to see Ric...hurry it up. Don: Look at Mike Sanders just toy with Arn Anderson. There's nothing Arn can do about it either.
Jim: Batista with a REVERSE DDT!!!! Now what the hell is Mike Sanders doing? He's pulling something out of his pants. Don: OH NO!!! Jim: No not that!! He's got...it's...it's a New Era t-shirt, and he's putting it on Arn. He's humiliating the man.
Don: Ole and Tully are out...these guys are seriously hurt. Jim: Arn has no idea what they're doing to him...they send him to the ropes. Batista catches him...with a Double Arm Choke Slam!!!! [Mike Sanders stands over him and holds up Four Fingers....one by one he puts each of them down. When only the middle finger is left, and then he sticks the middle finger right into the face of the knocked out Enforcer.]
Jim: Oh this is sick... Don: I never thought I'd see something like this. Jim: Me nither...this is horrible. [All of a sudden Mike goes cold, as he hears the count down....5....4...and before the count can get down to one we see Ric Flair racing to the ring. The crowd all stands to their feet. Ric is wearing is red tights, and boots. He gets into the ring, and all hell is about to break loose.]
Jim: Sean O'Haire goes after Flair, and Flair kicks him right between the legs. Flair with a chop, and another chop and a face rake. He slams his head into the steel cage, and he's telling them to bring another.
Don: LOOK AT THE RAGE!!!! Jim: Flair is red, and he's ready. Orton comes over, and he gets thrown into the steel cage. Orton bounces off, and Flair throws him into it again. He's telling Batista to bring some...
Don: OH MAN!!! Jim: Batista charges Flair, and Flair drops to his knees. He's beggi - NO!!! Low Blow by Ric Flair, and he hits a thumb to the eye. Flair with a chop, and another chop. Bastista is backed into the corner, and Flair unloads with jabs to the face.
Don: I have never seen Ric Flair so determined. Jim: Mike Sanders walks over and spins Flair around, and Flair gets hit with a right hand...no he blocks it. Mike Sanders runs away!!! THAT DAMN COWARD!!!!!
Don: Flair has taken out every memeber of New Era by himself. Jim: Ric Flair catches Sanders by the shirt...and he throws him down to the canvas. Flair mounts him, and he's unloading with fists to the face. He's not letting up, he's unloading...BEAT HIM!!!!
Don: I'm going to get you fired if you keep that up. Jim: SCREW IT!!! Flair needs some revenge. Flair not letting up, and Sanders' face is a bloody mess. There's blood all over the hand on Ric Flair, and the man has lost his mind. He's beating Mike Sanders silly.
Don: He could kill him. Jim: I think that's what he's trying to do. Here comes Sean O'Haire, and WAIT!!! Arn Anderson intercepts it with a clothesline!!!! [Arn walks over and pulls Flair off of Sanders...Flair turns around and sees
Arn with the New Era shirt. Flair grabs it and rips it off of him, and Flair has an extremely angry look on his eyes.]
Jim: Arn puts out his hand, and Flair shakes it. Don: I thought for a minute...ah nevermind. Jim: Flair takes that torn t-shirt, and he's choking Sanders out with it. That's totally legal, and if Sanders taps the match is over. Don: Arn is making sure the other members of New Era don't get up. Jim: Flair stops and he tells Arn to help him. Ole and Tully are still hurting from the attack...Flair and Arn whip Sanders to the ropes...DOUBLE BACKBODY
DROP!!!! [Sanders goes flying into the air, and falls down on the canvas hard.] Jim: WAIT A MINUTE!!!! Batista sprayed that mace into the eyes of Arn...Arn is staggering around. He can't see a damn thing...
Don: Ric Flair has no idea... [Ric turns to Arn, and Arn hits him with a SPINEBUSTER.] Jim: AWE WHAT THE HELL!!?!?! Don: Sitdown Powerbomb by Batista on Arn Anderson. Jim: Flair is out of it...and Sanders locks in the Figure Four. Flair isn't moving, and the ref is checking on him. He checks his arm....it drops once....twice...NO!!! THREE TIMES!!!!
Don: SANDERS DID IT!!!! Jim: I can't believe it. Mike Sanders has won the match here tonight, and he's in charge of the XCW now. You've gotta be kidding me. DAMNIT.
Don: He said he was going to beat the Horsemen and he did just that. Mike
Sanders and the New Era Of Wrestling are in charge now.
Jim: All the Horsemen laid out...it's a sad day for wrestling. [Sanders and the rest of his men celebrate as the cage lifts up. He calls to a ring attendant and he throws him four NEW t-shirts, and they drape them across the fallen Horsemen.]
Jim: This is disgusting. Don: Hey be nice...he's your boss. Jim: Oh I'm going to be sick. What is the XCW going to be like now that he's in charge? Don: Better. Jim: I doubt it. Fans our next Pay Per View is on January 19th, live from the
American Airlines Center in, Dallas Texas...it's "Ruination". Tickets go on sale next Monday at ticket master locations, and the box office. Not doubt should be a great one.
