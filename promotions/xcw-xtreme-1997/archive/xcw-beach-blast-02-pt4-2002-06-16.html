XCW Beach Blast '02 (part 4)

June 16, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is part of the 1st round of the XCW World Title Tournament. First coming down to the ring...from
Greenwhich, Connecticut....he weighs in at 275 pounds. Here is "The Game" Triple H!!! ["The Game" by Motorhead starts to play and the lights go out and a green spotlight hits the curtain. The crowd rise to their feet as Hunter walks out, he stands there for a moment pouring the water over his head. He continues to make his way down to the ring, and he's got a serious intense look on his face.
He climbs up to the apron and spits the water out.] FINK: And his opponent...from Truth Or Consequences, New Me - Jim: And Cactus is running into the ring!!!!!! Cactus slides in and Triple H meets him with a series of kicks to the back!!!!
Jesse: Cactus is brushing it off though, and he tackles Hunter down to the mat. He's working him with right after right. Jim: He's a madman in there!!!! [Cactus whips Hunter into the ropes, but holds onto his arm and hits a short arm clothesline. Cactus follows up with a huge elbowdrop across the chest. Jack with a "Bang Bang", and the fans are going wild.]
Jim: Cactus waits for Hunter to pull himself up using the ropes, and Cactus with the Cactus Clothesline and both men go to the outside of the ring.
Jesse: He's not holding back. The man wants to hold the XCW World Title, and he's going to do whatever it takes.
Jim: Both men got wiped out on that move, but it looks like Cactus is up first. He grabs Hunter by the hair, and Hunter oh my!! Hunter pushes Cactus into the ring post, and the back of Jack's head connects with a huge blow!!!
Jesse: This is why I love wrestling. [Cactus holds the back of his head, and Hunter drives an arm right into his chest.] Jim: Clothesline by Hunter, and now he's stomping away on Cactus. Triple H isn't going to be pushed around by Cactus. If you remember a few weeks back
Triple H and Steiner decided they were going to reak havoc on the XCW....and the only one who did anything about it was Cactus Jack.
Jesse: Well good for him. [Hunter brings Cactus up and lifts him torso up and drops his throat across the steel guardrail. Cactus falls to the ground holding his throat in pain. Hunter gets in the face of some of the fans, and they're yelling right back at him.
Hunter lifts Cactus up again and slams him onto the ground.] Jim: Mighty slam onto the ground. Jesse: And that really is the ground. We're out here in Shallow center field, of course, with a long entryway from each dugout and some extra seating set up in the outfield....all that's covering the hard natural grass of Safeco Field is some soft padding.
Jim: So you don't want to get hurt like that. [Hunter brings Cactus up again and drops him armpit-first across the guardrail!!!] Jim: It looks as if Hunter is going work on that arm tonight. Jesse: Hunter is great at picking a body part and focusing on it. One of three great men who do that.
Jim: Who are the other two? Jesse: Well Arn Anderson who focuses on anything, and me...and I focus on boobs. Jim: Boobs? Jesse: Listen I have to connect with the younger crowd some how tonight! Jim: Makes sense. [Cactus gets thrown into the ring post shoulder first. Hunter with a full armdrag twist into a hammerlock...and then bodyslams Cactus on the hammerlocked arm.]
Jesse: Whoa and you wanna talk about old school...there's a move from the pages of Greg "The Hammer" Valentine.
Jim: One of the true greats of our sport. Cactus came in like a hurricane, but
Hunter being the vet that he is has taken the advantage.
Jesse: Ya think? [Hunter throws Cactus back into the ring, and quickly rolls back in himself. Both men are up and Cactus is favoring that arm. Hunter grabs him by the arm, twists it and flips Cactus to the canvas. Triple H to the ropes now...]
Jim: Hunter jumps up and lands square on the bad arm of Cactus, and now he's going for the cover. ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!!
Jesse: Cactus has a whole lot more fight in him, and you're going to have to take both arms and legs out.
Jim: Hunter brings Cactus to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle. Triple
H charges in and Cactus with a boot to the head. Hunter staggers back and
Cactus with a HUGE clothesline!!!
[Hunter goes down and Cactus takes a few seconds to clear his mind. Cactus brings him back up and plants a boot to the stomach. Cactus runs to the ropes and takes him down with a diving DDT!!]
Jim: Cactus plants him with that!!! He goes for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! And barely a kickout by Hunter.
Jesse: He landed that DDT hard, and I'm suprised that Hunter had the will to kick out of that.
Jim: Cactus now brings Hunter up once again, and he whips him into the turnbuckle this time. Hunter comes stumbling out of the turnbuckle, and Cactus runs to the ropes and hits him with a HUGE Bulldog!!!!
[Cactus Jack brings Hunter back up and goes for a suplex, but Hunter shifts his weight and drops down to his feet. Hunter grabs Cactus by the arm and hits a cross armbreaker, and now he's cranking on that right arm.]
Jesse: And Cactus doesn't seem to happy about that move. He's in a lot of pain, and Hunter knows it.
Jim: Triple H is like a damn shark, and he smells that blood...and he's going to attack. Jesse: You don't have people like that in the XCW so much anymore. Hunter is one of a kind, and I think that he's going to win the whole thing tonight.
Jim: This match isn't even over yet. Jesse: I know, but I can still have a pick. Don't you have one? Jim: No...I am unbiased. [Hunter keeps cranking away on that arm, and the crowd starts to ge behind
Cactus. Everyone gets to their feet and starts to clap, and Cactus is feeling it. Cactus is tapping his boot to the rythm of the claps, and Hunter just adds more pressure.]
Jim: 46,000 plus fans are right behind Mick Foley tonight. They're all on their feet, and this place won't let Mick walk away the loser here.
Jesse: Well it's easier for them to cheer, they're not in the ring with Hunter. Jim: Cactus is starting to move, and Hunter adds some more pressure. Cactus is in some intense pain right now, and the ref has come over to check on the hand.
Cactus doesn't seem to be moving anymore. Jesse: And this is it...Hunter has own the match tonight. Jim: His arm is raised, and it falls!!! Two times...will it fall a third? Jesse:.................. Jim:..................... Jesse:........................ Jim:...................................NO!!! It stays up on the third drop and this place is going wild. Cactus is fighting it, and he's making his way to the ropes. He's trying everything to get that hold off of him.
Jesse: Is he going to make it? Jim: HE DOES!!!! Hunter lets go by the count of 5, and he brings Cactus back up. WAIT SMALL PACKAGE!!!!! ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!
Jesse: WHOA!!!! Jim: Hunter kicks out!!! Hunter kicks out...that was close. We almost saw
Cactus go onto the second round.
[Both men get back to their feet and Hunter with a right hand, but it's blocked and Cactus comesback with a left. Another left hand...and Hunter is dazed, and
MANDIBLE CLAW!!!!]
Jim: MANDIBLE CLAW!!!!!! He's got it locked in, and Hunter is trying to reach the ropes. Jesse: What? I can't hear you!!! Jim: Cactus Jack might have this match won right now. [Hunter drops to one knee, and Cactus smiles while adding more pressure. Hunter with a LOW BLOW!]
Jim: Oh damn you!!!! Jesse: Hey if it works use it. Jim: Cactus doubles over, and Hunter got out of that by cheating. [Hunter throws Cactus into the turnbuckle chest first, and sets him up onto the top turnbuckle. Hunter goes up and hits a Super Back Suplex, and he rolls over him for the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!!!! Cactus kicks out!!! Jesse: How did he do it? Jim: Cactus Jack's head bounced off that mat like a damn basketball, but he still kicked out. That is amazing!!!
Jesse: And Hunter is getting angry. [Triple H grabs Cactus and puts him between his legs. He gives the sign for the
Pedigree...]
Jim: This could be over now...he's going to hit it. NO!!!! Cactus with a backdrop, and Hunter hits the mat hard.
Jesse: And Cactus falls to his knees. Jim: Both men have gone through some hell here in the first round. [Cactus gets up and charges Hunter, and Hunter pulls the ref into his way. Cactus crashes into the ref, and both men go down. This gives Hunter some more time to catch his breath.]
Jim: Both men staring up into the sky, and who's going to get up first? [Cactus is up and he's checking on the ref, and the ref isn't moving. Hunter walks over to him, and Cactus stops him with a punch to the stomach. Another punch and Cactus back him into the turnbuckle. He unloads with a series of right hands to the side of the head...]
Jim: Cactus backs up now, and he gets a running start. He's going to drive those knees into the face of Hu - OH MY GOD!!!!!
Jesse: WHOA!!!!!! [As Cactus Jack was running to the turnbuckle, someone came in and tossed the chair right into his head. The man is wearing a hooded sweatshirt, and he's standing over the knocked out Cactus.]
Jim: Who the hell is that man? Jesse: He leveled Cactus with that chair, and now he's taking off his hood. That's CHRIS BENOIT!!!!! Jim: Chris Benoit...what a dirty son of a gun! He said he was going to take
Mick Foley out, but he never said when.
Jesse: Cactus is out cold. [Hunter walks over and brings Cactus up. He holds Cactus and Chris Benoit hits
Cactus in the head again with the chair. Cactus just crumbles to the floor, and both men stand over him and smile.]
Jim: The ref is still knocked out and both of these men should be damn near ashamed. Jesse: You think they care? Jim: Hunter wants Chris to hit him again. OH COME ON!!! Mick Foley has kids, he's got a wife...just get the damn match over with.
[Chris Benoit this time holds Foley and Hunter winds back and goes to hit
Cactus, but Cactus moves!! Benoit gets clocked in the head with the chair, and
Hunter can't believe it. Hunter drops the chair in disbelief....]
Jim: Cactus spins Hunter around and kicks him in the stomach. Double Arm DDT ONTO THE CHAIR!!!! Jesse: WHAT!? Jim: Cactus with the cover, and the ref is waking up. This is it!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!! Jesse: No!!! NO!!!! Jim: I can't believe it!!! Triple H got his shoulder up, he got his damn shoulder up. [Cactus drags himself back to his feet and brings Hunter back up. Cactus runs to the ropes, and Benoit in a desperation attempt clocks Cactus in the back of the head with another chair. The ref was busy with Hunter so he didn't see it...]
Jim: DAMNIT!!!! Hunter with a kick to the stomach...PEDIGREE!!!! PEDIGREE!!!! He makes the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Jesse: What a battle!!!! Jim: Cactus had the damn match won. How did Hunter kick out of that? Jesse: I don't have any idea. FINK: Here is your winner...Triple H! [Hunter leaves the ring area with his eyes half shut, and his lungs gasping for air. Meanwhile Cactus is lying in the ring knocked out. Chris Benoit climbs in and you can see a trickle of blood from the chair shot he took.]
Jim: What's he going to do? Jesse: Anything he wants. [Chris grabs the steel chair and puts Foleys neck in between it. Benoit runs to the ropes...]
Jim: OH HELL!!!! NO!!! DON'T!!!! GOD!!! Jesse: OH!!!!! Jim: DAMN YOU CHRIS BENOIT!!!!!! Jesse: He jumped onto that chair, and Mick Foleys neck has to be broken. He has to have broken the guys neck.
Jim: And here comes Foleys wife....she's such a loving wife, and mom. She never asked for any of this. This is disgusting.
Jesse: I'd have to admit this is sadistic. [Nicolette runs into the ring and covers her husbands lifeless body. She's crying huge tears, and won't move. Chris Benoit stands over her and smiles, and before he walks away he spits on him and her. Chris leaves the ring area to
HUGE heel heat.]
Jim: And here comes Magnum TA and the EMT's. Jesse: I know the Foley family, and they're like family to me. I love them, and
I hate to see something like this happen. When you decide to wrestle though, you take that risk.
Jim: Nobody ever expects something like this. Nobody, and it's a damn shame that people like Chris Benoit can legally ruin someone elses life.
Jesse: And Ross, Foley hasn't moved. I think there is something very seriously wrong righ now, and the EMT's are doing everything they can.
[All of a sudden we see Al Snow run out, as well as Tommy Dreamer. Both men climb into the ring and Al Snow comforts Mrs. Foley...]
Jim: Al Snow is down here now, and he's taking Mrs. Foley back to the dressing room, and Tommy Dreamer is checking on his friend. What a way to kill the mood here tonight. We had things so perfect, and Chris Benoit came in and ruined things.
Jesse: Well tonight is still going to be a great night, but this will just be in the back of our minds.
Jim: You know for me it's just odd to see Mick Foley not walk out of the ring, or even have some kind of motion. Not even a thumbs up...so you know this has to be bad.
Jesse: If I know Mick Foley, and I do...this isn't going to be the last we've heard of him. In one form or another.
Jim: Chris Benoit had better enjoy his days alive, because someone is going to go after him for this. Fans we need to get things cleared up in the ring, so lets take you to the history that has transpired between Tommy Dreamer, Stevie
Richards, and Raven.....
