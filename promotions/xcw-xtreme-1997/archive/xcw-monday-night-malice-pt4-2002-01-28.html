He begins to make his way down to the ring...>
<Goldberg climbs into the ring and Angle attacks him right away with a series of forearms to the back...>

Jesse: No way! Goldberg isn't a medalist...Angle wrestled with a broken neck! <Angle makes it to the ropes, and Goldberg lets go. He grabs Angle, and Kurt counters with a thumb to the eye...and another thumb to the eye. Angle scoop slams Goldberg down, and then follows up with an elbowdrop.>
Jim: Angle sits Goldberg up...and where is Angle going? <Kurt Angle hops up onto the second ropes and does a jumping neck snap on
Goldberg....> Jim: SHADES OF HENNIG!!!! That was from the 2nd ropes, and Goldberg doesn't look to be in the best shape right now.
