Closing =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 1. INTRODUCTION A. President/Owner -- Hello, my name is [name withheld]. I've been running e-feds for about 3 years now. I started out with a fake wrestler fed, and then went to Real Wrestlers. The EWWF was the last fed I ran, and people seemed very burned out on the real wrestler idea. So I decided to shut it down, take a short break, and comeback when I felt the desire. Well with the intro to the WOW chance, and the web page, and the fire inside of me...I'm ready once again to make this baby happen. I can't wait for this ride to start! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 2. STAFF A. Fictional: 1. Owner/President = [name withheld] 2. Commissioner = Tony Atlas 3. Head Commentator = Jim Ross, Magnum TA 4. Other Commentator = Paul Roma, Bobby Heenan 5. Ring Announcer = Howard Finkel 6. Head and 2nd Interviewer = "Mene" Gene Okerlund, Michael Cole 7. Head of Security = Brian Gallagher 8. Head Referee = Earl Hebner B. Real Staff 1. Owner/Prez/Founder/Writer/Booker = [name withheld] ([detail withheld]) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 3. RULES AND REGULATIONS A. Please, Please, Please do not attack other wrestlers or even use other wrestlers without there permission. B. Please, Please, Please RP at least RP twice a week. D. Please, Please, Please do not break kayfabe in roleplays saying jobber, email, roleplay, or spam. Unless your character does so...like Shane Douglas or maybe even Rocky Maivia. People like Undertaker and Test do not say things like that. E. Please, Please, Please send all roleplayers to me: [detail withheld] only. F. Have i said please enough. Well 1 last rule and well thats to have eff-yoo--en. (hint hint: FUN!!) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 4. CARDS A. Monday Night Malice Action packed every single Monday night. You wanna talk about action, and angles? This is the place to be when you're looking for the best. With 8 matches on the cards, and angles running like crazy you're going to think that you were right in the middle of it all. This is the highest rated show every to show up on your computer screen, and with the talent that we have now...it's only going to get better. Jim Ross and Paul Roma will be the commentators on this awesome show.
B. Thursday Night X-Treme This has a little bit of everything. With some of the Hardcore guys showing their stuff, some top talent battling out in the ring, and tag teams duking it out like crazy. This is the place to be....for get NBC, X-Treme is a MUST SEE
TV. A ton of angles and matches will take place on this card. Jerry Lawler and wrestling legend Magnum TA will be the commentators for this show.
C. [Flashpacket] "Trash Talkin'" Basically this is a piece a packet that will basically just show all the RPs together. Prolly will come out after every 5 Rp's I get from you guys.
Remember that there is not maximum of RP's you can do, but there is a minimum. It's two Rp's a week. If you choose not to do two Rp's...then you will be either jobbed or simply kicked out. I'm not asking for novels every single time, I'm not asking for poets...all I'm asking is that you get your face out in the spotlight twice a week. Remember it can be an interview, or it can take place anywhere you want it to. Be creative...on out XCW Webpage I have a link that leads to some Rping tips. Check it out, it'll help out a lot.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 5. TITLES A. XCW World Title This is the most prestigious title in X-Treme Championship Wrestling. If you have this title you are the best wrestler in this federation. Holding this title is hard because everyone is gunning for you. You will defend this title 1-2 times a month and at every supercard and PPV. Plus if you feel like defending it more, go ahead,
I'm not stopping you. Just let me know that you want to defend the title, and we'll book it the best we can.
B. XCW United States Title This is the 2nd best title to hold in the federation. It doesn't necessarily mean your 2nd best but it does mean that you getting up to the top.
With this title, you'll be defending it between 2-3 times a month and at every supercard and PPV. Plus if you wanna give out a title shot, go ahead.
C. XCW Television Title This is very self explanatory. This title is defended on every single
Monday show, all the supercards, and all the PPV's. You have a huge responsibility, and some consider this the hardest title to keep, while others don't even bother with it. If you choose not to defend your title then you will be stripped of it, and it will be given to the 2nd ranked.
E. XCW World Tag Teeam Titles This is for all the tag teams out there wanting a title in there favor. I probably won't put this title in effect til i get at least 5 or so solid tag teams in the division. Holding this title will make you defend this title between 1-2 times a month, at every supercard and PPV, and plus if you feel like giving out another shot.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 6. ROLEPLAYING A. Flashes aka Roleplays -- Please try to roleplay atleast twice a week whether you have a match or not. Try to roleplay consistently if possible. Most of the better roleplayers consistency will be 2-3 creative and well developed flashes per match. 85% of the match will be deteremined by roleplays and the other 15% is by angle. I judge roleplays on quality, not quantity. The more you focus on your gimmick, the better I like it. Although we have people who write long Rp's and they are creative. So if you want to join...be sure you can hang.
B. In rings -- In rings are when an interviewer gives a interview during a wrestling event in the ring or in the backstage area. I love this so use Scott
Jonson, the interviewer to give some good in rings for me to use in the card.
They don't count as RPs but they make your character more over as a heel or face. C. Strategies -- These are very useful in what you want your wrestler to perform during matches. This basically tells me or another match writer what you want to before your match, during your match, and after match, and also if you want to be involved in another match. Strats are useful in furthering an angle.
Strats and Inring will most likely be due around the Thursday or Friday before the card. Roleplays will most likely be due around Sunday''s and Wednesday's before the card. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 7. JOBBING Everyone and I mean, everyone will lose sometime in the XCW. Whether its to further an angle, because you got out RPed, or anything else, you will lose sometime.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 8. PICKING WRESTLERS Okay when picking a wrestler think about how well you can Rp this guy, and know that you're not going to get sick of them the next 24 hours. If you pick up a guy and decide that you don't want him that's fine...it happens to the best of us, but if you pick and choose every day then I will kick you out.
You can have up to 4 guys, and in those 4 guys if you decided to choose them all...you would have to pick up a tag team. Tag Teams are always low here in the XCW, but hopefully we can change that. I am looking forward to doing some booking for them, and it would be pretty damn sweet if we had enough.
Also remember that if you choose 4 guys....you're going to have to come out with 2 Rp's a week for each of them. It can be in the same packet, it can be in the same day...whatever you want, but as long as we hear from them at least 2 times a week...then it'll be fine.
9. CLOSING I hope you take the time to read everything and fill out the app and join XCW and soon it will become a greatly established e-fed. I hope to start this fed in the beginning of September. Hope to see you soon. The key for me is start small and not stress. We'll get the ball rolling soon enough, but PLEASE DO NOT join if you don't want to RP. I'm done.
