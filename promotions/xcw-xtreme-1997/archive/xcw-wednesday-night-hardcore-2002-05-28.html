XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore

May 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts to a sold out crowd in the Pepsi Center. Fireworks are blasting off, and the camera pans the crowd to see all the signs being waved. We cut to
Jim Ross and Joel Gertner who have huge smiles on their faces. Both men seem to be refreshed after their holiday in the sun on Memorial Day.]
Jim: WELCOME EVERYONE!!!! Welcome the Hardcore!!! Joel: Listen to these people! Jim: What a night it's going to be folks. We're going to have an amazing show for you, and I promise you'll walk away wanting more.
Joel: Yeah...heh. Jim: We've got that huge main event for you tonight. Raven will be taking on
Tommy Dreamer in a Hardcore Rules, No DQ match. Both men have a HUGE history with each other, and tonight is just going to make things deeper.
Joel: What about Mikey and Tatanka teaming up? That's a ratings booster right there if I do say so myself.
Jim: Well say wha - [All of a sudden the crowd is stunned and silent. Both Jim and Joel stop talking and the camera picks up the curtain. Who walks out, but "Big Pappa
Pump" Scott Steiner looking bigger and badder than ever. Scott climbs into the ring and grabs the microphone...]
Scott: Shut up. (Crowd boos) Scott: I didn't come out here to talk to all you freaks. What I came out here to talk about is the Big Bad Booty Daddy, and let everyone know that business has just picked up. Forget Tatanka, forget Mikey Whipwreck....from now on the
XCW belongs to me.
(Crowd responds with "YOU SUCK"....) Scott: So tonight...I challenge anyone from the back to come out here and just try to ruin my day. Come on!!!
[Camera cuts to the curtain and nobody comes out. The crowd is hoping for someone, but to their dissapointment...nobody shows up.]
Scott: That's what I (bleep) thought. You pe - ["Ready to play the game???" is heard all over the PA, and the lights go out and the camera cuts to the curtain. None other than Triple H walks out, and the fans are going balistic. He's got on his shirt and jean vest, along with his jeans. He makes it down half way and stops. He already has a microphone...]
Triple H: So...Big Pappa Pump is in the XCW. Well tonight...and for the next couple of years. So is Triple H!!!
(Crowd goes wild, and they cut to Scott Steiner who doesn't seem impressed.) Triple H: All I have heard is horror stories about you Scotty. How you can't control your temper, or how you work to stiff in the ring. Ya see...I get these stories, and yet I realize you've never faced someone like me.
(Crowd pops again.) Triple H: So...why don't you shut your pill popping ass up, and get into the ring with me tonight?
Scott: Hunter, I'd be more than happy to break that big nose of yours. This isn't the WWF...and I'm not that bitch of a wife Stephine McMahon. I'm your worst nightmare, and you're going to see why tonight. As a matter of fact lets step it up...you find yourself a partner, and you two can face me in a
Handi-Cap match! Triple H: You want it...then you got it. [Trips music starts up and he walks away. Scott leaves through the crowd...] Jim: How do you like that?!?!? Triple H and Scott Steiner in the XCW for not more than 10 minutes, and Triple H dropped a challenge to Scott, and Scott upped the ante.
Joel: Scott ain't playin' with a full deck. The guy is seriously messed up, and
I don't care how talented you are...you're not going to be able to beat him.
Trips doesn't know what he's getting himself into. Jim: What a night it's going to be!!! Joel: Oh yeah!!! Jim: All right fans our first match here tonight is Steve Armstrong taking on
Scott Hall. Now last week we saw two things, the first one being Steve beating
Hall's friend Ric Flair...and then Hall getting kicked out. What's going to happen tonight?
Joel: Well I can tell you that one thing is for damn sure...you're not going to see Steve Armstrong win again. The man is just not good enough to pull off two upsets. Ric Flair was a fluke, and Scott Hall isn't about to go down like that.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, from Minnestoa. He weighs in at 269 pounds...here is Scott
Hall!!! [Scott's NWO music starts to play and he comes down to the ring with a very arrogant swager. It might be because he's facing Steve Armstrong, or it could be because he's drunk. Who knows with him...Hall climbs into the ring and does the pointing to himself deal...]
FINK: And his opponent...from Atlanta, GA. He weighs in at 250 pounds...here is
Steve Armstrong!!!
[Steve comes out to no music, but gets a little reaction this time. He doesn't really care as he just wants to climb into the ring and take care of business.]
Jim: What an incredible match this is going to be. Joel: HAHA... [Starts off with a clean lock up between the two men. Armstrong presses Hall against the ropes. Quickly Scott Hall turns it around, and then again Steve turns it around once more. Hall is backed into the corner...]
Jim: And Hall is asking for a clean break... Joel: Do you blame him? Jim: Oh my, and Armstrong obliges. Hall with a boot to the stomach, and a cheap shot!! Joel: Nice!!! Jim: Hall with a huge right hand, and another right hand. Armstrong backs into the corner, and Hall with a series of kicks to the midsection.
[Armstrong slouches against the turnbuckle, and Hall hits him with a knee to the face!!] Jim: OH and that could've knocked some teeth out right there. Joel: Then Armstrong can look the rest of these fans here. [Hall brings Steve out of the turnbuckle and sends him into the ropes. Steve returns to duck the clothesline by Hall. Steve turns him around and drives a boot to the stomach, and executes a nice vertical suplex.]
Jim: And Steve isn't done yet...he picks him Hall by the right leg and slams his knee down to the mat. What a painful move that is!
Joel: It's painful because you know that's one of Steve's big moves! Jim: I'm sure he's got a lot of moves that he doesn't use. [Steve brings Hall to his feet and hits a double-arm DDT...and Steve makes the cover!!!] Jim: One!!! TWO!!!! Kickout by Scott Hall. Steve runs to the ropes, and drops a knee across the chest. Steve gets up again and he executes another kneedrop to the chest, and now he's laying in with some vicious stomps!!
Joel: This man has to be on something. Steve Armstrong has never ever been able to pull of this kind of offense. I mean he beat Ric Flair, and now he's beating
Scott Hall. What is this guy doing that hasn't caught on yet?
Jim: He's just sick of that damn curse. He doesn't want to have to hear people talk about his family anymore, and I don't blame him.
Joel: Why people talk about your family? Jim: No. Joel: They should...they're ugly. Jim: I take offense to that. Joel: Well at least I didn't say they were fat. Jim:..... [Steve runs to the ropes again and goes for a kneedrop for the third time, but
Hall rolls out of the way quickly. Steve quickly gets up and whips Scott into the turnbuckle, and Hall bounces out and hits a HUGE CLOTHESLINE!!!!]
Jim: Steve staggers back to a vertical base, and Hall clotheslines him over the top rope and to the outside!!!
Joel: And this is where the fun begins!! [Hall goes to the outside and whips Steve into the steel guardrail. Scott goes to slam Steve's head into the rail, but Steve puts on the breaks and elbows him in the stomach. Steve throws him into the ring steps....]
Jim: And Hall's head hit hard! Joel: That'll sober him up... Jim: Who said he was drunk? Joel: Not me. Jim: What do you know? Joel: Nothing. [Steve rolls Hall back into the ring, and Armstrong goes to the top ropes. He waits for Hall to make it to his feet, and he leaps off the turnbuckle...]
Jim: HIGH IN THE AIR..... Joel: WOW!!!! [Hall moves out of the way right before a Missile Dropkick...and Steve hits the ground hard. Scott hooks the leg, and there's the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THR....and Steve kicks out at the last second. That was damn close. Joel: Now is the time when Hall should bust out his secret stash of JD and hit
Steve over the head with it.
Jim: Unless he drank it all. Joel: Why are you pushing me to give you answers about Hall when you already know? Jim: So he is drunk!!! Joel: ............... [Scott brings Steve back up, and lays in with a hard fist. Steve comeback with one of his own though, and Scott returns the favor. Both men are going back and forth with lefts and rights.]
Jim: Hall goes for that Spinning Right Hand, and Steve ducks it. Steve from behind, hits a GERMAN SUPLEX!!!!!
Joel: WHOA!!!! Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!!! NO KICKOUT!!!!! [Steve yells at Earl Hebner about the count, and brings Hall back up. He drives a boot into the stomach, and puts Hall across his shoulders.]
Jim: Looks like he's going for a DVD....but Hall slips down!!! Scott reaches back and hits a three-quater Nelson Breaker!!!!
Joel: WHAT A MOVE!!!! Scott Hall wrestles best when he's drunk!!!! Jim: I knew it!!! Joel: Shit. [Scott brings him back up and sends him into the turnbuckle. Scott rushes in with a shoulder and Steve moves out of the way quickly.]
Jim: AND HALL HITS THE POST HARD!!!! Joel: That will take the momentum right out of you!!! Jim: Steve grabs Hall and puts him onto the top turnbuckle. Hall's back is facing the ring, and it looks like Steve is going to finish it right here.
Joel: He can't do anything from the top. Jim: Steve climbs the turnbuckle, and Hall hits him in the side of the head with an elbow. Another elbow to the side of the face, and Armstrong falls down to the canvas.
Joel: That was a close call there. Scott Hall needs to finish this ASAP, or
Steve is gonna upset him again. We can't have that. You know that's like the US
Soccer team winning, and then we'll have all these soccer fans running around.
Jim: What's wrong with that? Joel: Soccer sucks. So does Armstrong. [Hall jumps off the turnbuckle and locks in a Camel Clutch.] Jim: Wow and he's going for the submission. Steve Armstrong is screaming out in pain, and the ref is checking on him. Is Steve going to have enough to hold on or is he going to lose tonight?
Joel: Lose. Jim: Listen to these people...last week they were chanting "WHO IS THIS", and tonight they're rallying behind Steve Armstrong.
Joel: And Steve is reaching his arms around. NO!!! Jim: He is, and he's trying to pull Hall's arms off. Is he going to have enough strength? He's trying with everything he's got...these people are on their feet. He's pulling those arms off, and Hall can't believe his eyes.
[Hall breaks the hold and jumps and as he goes to land on Steve's back, Steve quickly turns around and holds his knees up. Halls crotch makes perfect contact with Steve's knees, and the crowd erupts.]
Joel: A Three Wise Men is the only thing that'll cure that pain for Hall!! Jim: Steve charges Scott, and SCOTT WITH A BACKDROP OVER THE TOP ROPES!!!!! Joel: Ouch, Steve makes a horrid thud onto the ground. Jim: Once again this match goes to the outside, and Hall limps out to the floor. [Hall lifts up Steve into a bearhug-like hold, gets a running start, and rams him into the ring post. Scott pushes him again into the ring post, and Steve lets out a huge scream. Hall grabs Scott by the back of the head and rolls him onto the Spainish Commentators Table...]
Jim: Hugo and Pepe better move out of the way. Joel: I don't think they understand you....hey...El Move-o Out-of the way-o. Jim: That didn't work. Joel: Maybe if we yell La Migra...they'll start running? [Hall gets up onto the table, and he gives the sign for the Outsider Edge. He brings Steve to his feet and puts him between his legs. Hall raises Steve high into the air and drops him down....
SMASH!!!!! both men go down through the table hard. Pieces of wood go flying, and the monitors are smashes to hell. The crowd responds with a "HOLY SHIT" chant.
Steve isn't moving but his eyes are open, and Hall is barely awake.] Jim: GOOD GOD!!!! HALL JUST KILLED HIMSELF AND ARMSTRONG!!!!!!! Joel: Well...if ya can't beat em', kill em'. Jim: Both men are down, and the impact from that move had to have seriously injured both men!!! Earl Hebner runs out of the ring to check on both men, and this isn't good. Both men aren't moving, and Hebner starts to lay on the count.
Joel: Don't let this match end in a double countout. That was be nothing but crap. Jim: He's up to 5....6....and Hall is using the guardrail to make it back up. He's barely making it back to his feet, and he beats the count. Scott Hall is up after that helacious move he executes through the table.
Joel: Steve ain't. [Hall moves pieces of the table, and brings Steve up...he tosses him back into the ring. Scott takes a little breather on the outside, before rolling in himself. Hall is in now, and he goes for the cover....]
Jim: Hooks the leg...One!!! Two!!! THREE!!!!! Joel: He got him!!! Jim: NO!!! THE REF IS SAYING NO!!!! Steve got his shoulder up just in the nick of time, and can you believe it? This is our first match of the night, and I feel like I'm watching a Pay Per View. ONLY IN THE XCW!!!!!!
Joel: This is an incredible match!!! [Hall gives the sign for the Outsider Edge again....] Jim: This is going to be it...Scott Hall gets the win right here. Joel: If he can get him up...he's about as tired as Steve is. [Hall puts Steve between his legs again and goes to lift him up...but Steve quickly turns it into a Small Package!!!!]
Jim: SMALL PACKAGE!!!! One!!! Two!!! THREE!!!!!! Joel: I can't believe it!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Steve Armstrong!!!! Jim: He did it again!!! What in the hell is he doing? He's incredible. Joel: The man won after being Edged through a damn table. [Steve is on the ground and the ref raises his hand up high, and the fans applaud him. Armstrong makes it to his feet and raises his own arm this time, and then holds the back of his head in pain. Hall rolls out of the ring and stumbles to the back.]
Jim: Steve Armstrong has beaten Ric Flair, and now Scott Hall. Who the hell is next? Joel: Who knows? Maybe he'll wrestle God next week and win....I mean it could be a fluke. Jim: Steve exits the r...OH GOOD GOD!!!! SCOTT STEINER!!!! SCOTT STEINER IS
DOWN HERE AND HE JUST HIT STEVE WITH A DAMN PIPE ACROSS THE NECK!!!!!
Joel: NICE!!!!!! Jim: Somebody get security!!!! Get him the hell out of here!!!!! Joel: He smashed that pipe right on the neck of Steve, and he's not letting up. Jim: He's got the Steiner-Recliner on the outside of the ring!!!! [All of a sudden the crowd pops as we see Triple H run down to the ring. Scott see's him and takes off into the crowd. Triple H stops at Steve and checks on him.]
Jim: Triple H is here!!! He said he wasn't going to put up with Scott's crap, and tonight he's showing us that!!!
Joel: Always gotta be the center of attention!!! How do you like that? ************************************************ [Cut to Arn Anderson's office where is talking on the phone. All of a sudden
Chris Jericho bursts in and starts yelling...]
Chris: Listen Arnie...I want a title match tonight!! Arn: I'm on the phone. Chris: Well hang up. Arn: Listen I'll call you back...yeah Chris Jericho is here. HAHAH yeah he certainly is. (Arn hangs up.) Chris: I'm certainly what? Arn: Nothing. Chris: Well I beat that slack jawed goof last week and tonight I want to wrestle in the main event for the XCW World Title.
Arn: Heh...you're one silly little Canadian aren't ya? Chris: ................ Arn: I said you can wrestle whenever you want....not who, and when on the card. I said you can wrestle on any card you want, and so you want a match tonight that's fine. Find yourself an opponent and you can wrestle.
Chris: You know I'm really sick and tired of not being booked...but that's fine. I'll find someone, and I'm going to carry them to a five star match. Not like that other piece of crap we just saw in the ring!!!
(Chris storms out, and the camera fades.) *************************************************
