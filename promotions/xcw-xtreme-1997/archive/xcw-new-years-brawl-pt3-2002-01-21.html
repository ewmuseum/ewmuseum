XCW New Years Brawl (part 3)

January 21, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Well... Paul: What the hell was that? Jim: Leave it up to Tom Green to annoy anyone. Paul: He shouldn't be bothering wrestlers before their match. I mean seriously,
I wouldn't want him bothering me before my big match. Even if it's against someone like Al Snow.
Jim: Yeah well I'm sure we're going to see a whole different Al Snow here tonight. The man is serious, and he isn't going to be a joke anymore. If you ask me though it's hard to take a man seriously when he walks around with a manquien head.
Paul: HAHA yeah! FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first...from Lima, Ohio. He weighs in at 245 pounds...here is "The
Future" Al Snow!!!
[Al Snow's music starts to play and he comes down to a huge heel response. Snow doesn't seem to care as he climbs into the ring and waits for Rikishi...]
FINK: And his opponent...from the Island of Samoa. He weighs in at 494 pounds. Here is Rikishi!!!! [Rikishi's music starts to play and the fans get to their feet. He comes out to a huge face response, and he's wearing his glasses and a big smile on his face.
He slaps the fans hands as he climbs into the ring.] Jim: Well this should be easy work for Rikishi tonight. I don't see Al Snow getting very far in this match, the man is just not a serious contender here in the XCW.
Paul: Maybe in like 3 years when he's retired and gone we can bring him back for the gimmick battle royal, and he can join like three times?
Jim: Heh that would be a good idea. This match is underway and Snow meets
Rikishi with a thumb to the eye, and another thumb to the eye. Snow with a kick to the midsection, and he follows up with a nice dropkick.
Paul: Huh...that was a nice series of moves by Al Snow. Jim: Snow waits for Rikishi to get back up, and Al with a superkick...and
Rikishi walks backwards.
Paul: What's going on? <Al runs and springboards off the ropes to hit Rikishi with a Swinging DDT.> Jim: That takes the big man down hard! Paul: Umm...that was a pretty impressive move there by Snow. Jim: Yes it was, and Snow stomping away on the head of Rikishi. <Snow gets in Rikishi's face and starts yelling at him. Al brings Rikishi to his feet and kicks him in the stomach, and then runs to the ropes. Al comes off the ropes and executes a swinging neckbreaker.>
Paul: Okay...I think Al Snow means business. Jim: He sure as hell does. Rikishi is like a little child in that ring tonight. Paul: I didn't think that Al Snow was going to come in here like this, and just destroy Rikishi.
Jim: Well he said it and nobody is taking him seriously. <Rikishi makes it to his feet and is holding his neck, and Al Snow goes for a back heel kick and misses.>
Jim: Rikishi moves out of the way just in time, and now he's going to take care of Al Snow. Rikishi drives a fist into the head, and follows up with a bodyslam!!!
Paul: Oh look out!!!! Jim: OH!!! LEGDROP!!!! He's just sitting there now with his leg draped over the throat of Snow, and the ref is making the cover. 1!! 2!! No...he doesn't get it. Al Snow kicks out...
<Both men make it back to their feet and Rikishi lifts him up for a Samoan
Drop, but Al flips over.>
Jim: Nice counter by Al, and he throws Rikishi chest first into the turnbuckle. Rikishi bounces out, and Al Snow catches him with an Enzugiri to the back of the head!!! Paul: And that'll take you down in a second. What a great by Al Snow...I'm starting to really respect this guy.
<Al jumps to the top ropes and waits for Rikishi to make it to his feet again. He finally does and Snow flies off with a Missle Dropkick. Rikishi goes down hard, and Snow goes for the cover.>
Jim: 1!! 2!!! NO!!! Rikishi kicks out... Paul: And Al Snow looks angry! <Al Snow brings Rikishi back up and lock in a full nelson, and yells to the crowd and then leg sweeps Rikishi forward causing him to fall face first.>
Jim: Full Nelson, Foward Russian Legsweep!!! Snow now stomps away again on the head of Rikishi!!!
Paul: The man has no remorse at all. He wants to prove a point tonight, and he's going to do it by beating Rikishi to death with his foot.
Jim: Rikishi has been totally taken by storm in this match tonight, and it's amazing how much Al Snow has shown us all here tonight.
Paul: Well he's definitly part of the Job Squad anymore. The guy has totally be given a chance to show off his skills, and that's what he's doing.
Jim: Al Snow debuted in 1982, and it's 20 years later and he's just starting to get the respect he wants.
Paul: 20 years of being a joke would send anyone over the edge. Jim: You speak from experience? Paul: No..I was talking about your wife. Jim: My wife is a lovely woman thank you very much. Paul: Heh...no comment. <Rikishi gets up and swings wildy at Al Snow, and Al catches the arm and turns it into an Arm Breaker. Rikishi hits the mat, and holds his arm in serious pain. Al gets right up and drives four consecutive knees on the bad arm.>
Jim: And Rikishi is holding that right arm, and Al Snow is working it over.
Paul: Smart move there by Al Snow, but I mean should I even bother saying that? Not very often does smart and Al Snow go together. Jim: Al locks in an armbar, and the ref is checking on Rikishi. You can hear him scream in pain. Look at the look on Rikishi's face...that man is in some serious pain here!!!
Paul: Well that arm breaker might have knocked the arm right out of the socket, and then if that happens you know you have some serious trouble.
Jim: Snow hasn't been able to do a whole lot with Rikishi because of his size, but what he's been doing has been extremely affective.
<Al brings Rikishi up and pushes him back against the turnbuckle. Al goes to the top ropes, and executes a Super Arm Breaker!>
Jim: That snapped his damn arm!!! My God...that snapped his damn arm!!! Paul: Listen to Rikishi scream!!! Jim: Al Snow locks in the armbar again, and the ref is calling for the bell. This match is seriously over... FINK: Here is your winner....Al Snow!!! Jim: Wait a minute...what's Al Snow doing? Paul: He's got a chair, Jim!!! <Al Snow brings in the chair and puts Rikishi's bad arm between the seat and the top of the chair. He kicks Rikishi a couple of times, and then jumps to the top ropes.>
Jim: AWE NO!!!! DON'T DO IT AL!!! DON'T...... SNAP!!!! Jim: OH HELL!!!! He just broke Rikishi's arm!!!! What poor sportsmanship by Al
Snow, and he's an ass!! He won the damn match, and now he's trying to just put the man out of wrestling.
Paul: Well I don't think we're going to see Rikishi for awhile. Jim: He jumped off that top turnbuckle and came down onto that chair, and that broke Rikishi's damn arm.
Paul: Al Snow has left, and he's left his mark on the XCW. The man is not to be taken lightly, and he showed us all that tonight.
Jim: He did it by costing someone their wrestling career. Paul: Well you have to get respect somehow, and you have to prove to everyone that you're not to be messed with and Al Snow did it by breaking that fat piece of goo's arm.
Jim: EMT's are in the ring now, and they're trying to work on Rikishi. ["Come Out And Play" begins to play over the arena sound system as Raven steps through the curtains. He makes his way down the aisle, dead eyed and unemotional. He slides under the bottom rope and makes his way to the nearest turnbuckle where he slumps down, requesting a mic. He shortly receives one and holds it to his mouth, waiting for the crowd's boos to lower before he begins to speak.]
Raven - "You know, I've tried to give Hulk Hogan the benefit of the doubt. I assumed that maybe the reason that he hasn't answered my challenge yet is because he's getting _old_ and hard of hearing. I also thought that perhaps he's not as intelligent as he used to be and it takes him longer to comprehend things."
[Raven pauses as the crowd boos.] Raven - "But in reality, your hero is nothing more than the color he represents... yellow! Hulk Hogan is afraid and won't accept my challenge! Hogan is willing to let Hulkamania die right here tonight in front of _each_ and _every_ one of his fans! All of the Hulkamaniacs here tonight will witness the death of
Hogan's career, and the rebirth of mine!"
[The crowd jeers loudly, chanting "RAVEN SUCKS!" Raven sits in the corner of the ring looking coldly out at the audience. He slowly raises his arm and points his finger at a kid in the crowd wearing a "Hulkamania" shirt.]
Raven - "You there, little boy, how do you feel now that Hulkamania is _dead_? How do you feel when you look in this ring and see the man that brought it all down? You have been lead down the wrong path, little boy. Follow me...follow the truth! Follow Raven and witness...."
[Raven is suddenly cut off as Brutus "The Barber" Beefcake's old WWF music begins to play over the arena sound system. The crowd pops as Beefcake and
Jimmy Hart appear on the entrance ramp. "The Barber" has a mic in hand and looks offended.]
Beefcake - "Raven, I am sick and tired of hearing you go on and on about how you are gonna end Hulkamania. You are a sick, delusional man and you seriously need your ass kicked!"
[Crowd pop!] Beefcake - "Everybody in this building knows as well as I that Hulkamania will
_never_ die! It will be here as long as there is wrestling!"
[Brutus hands the mic to Jimmy.] Hart - "I've spent the majority of my career against Hogan, but after many years I've come to accept that he's the man, and that nobody can beat that man as long as he's got his Hulkamaniacs behind him!"
[Another crowd pop. Crowd starts chanting "HOGAN!"] Hart - "The truth is that the Hulkster doesn't feel that you are even a remote threat to his legacy. Your words mean nothing to him, and you will have a
_very_ hard time getting him out of retirement!"
[Raven remains slumped in his corner, eerily calm.] Raven - "Hogan _will_ take me seriously. He may think that I'm all talk, but soon he'll realize just how much of a threat I really am! And if you two clowns think you can stop me, be my guest."
[Crowd cheers for Beefcake to accept.] Beefcake - "Raven, I'd come down there and whip your ass right now, but I have more important things to do. I'm not here to wrestle, just to bring Hogan's word."
[Cut back to Raven.] Raven - "I figured you would say that. You're just like your best buddy Hogan, scared and weak. You wouldn't know what to do once you got in this ring with me. The fact is that HULKAMANIA IS DEAD!"
A ONLINE 6.0\IDB\Diction.ind [Without notice, Brutus suddenly loses his temper and takes off toward the ring. Jimmy Hart is in hot pursuit to try and stop him. Meanwhile, Raven calmly rolls out of the ring and reaches under the apron, grabbing a chair. He then slides back into the ring as Beefcake enters, and decks him hard across the face with the chair. Jimmy, who has accidently followed too close, is also in the ring, and before he can even move, is also nailed. Raven picks up the mic.]
Raven - "Hogan, do you see this?! Do you see what's happening to Hulkamania?! It's DYING! Dying in this very ring!" [Raven drops the mic and picks up Beefcake, getting him in the DDT position. He then falls back and nails the Evenflow DDT right onto the chair, driving
"The Barber's" head into it. He reaches down and picks up the mic again.]
Raven - "HULKAMANIA IS DEAD! Follow ME! Follow the TRUTH!" [Raven drops the mic again and then picks up Jimmy Hart, who is just starting to come to. Raven quickly sets him up and Evenflows him onto the same chair as
Brutus, leaving them unconscious side by side. Raven picks up the mic one last time, breathing hard.]
Raven - "Quoth the Raven...................... Nevermore!" [Raven drops the mic a final time and rolls out of the ring, leaving Hogan's two friends battered and beaten. "Come Out And Play" blares over the arena sound system as Raven vanishes through the curtains.]
