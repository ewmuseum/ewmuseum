XCW Friday Night X-Treme (part 3)

December 29, 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: The two most unlikely to be friends are now becoming enemies....speaking of two people that are becoming friends. Terry Funk is up next, and I'm sure
Jake Roberts will be watching. Lets take you to that match...
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, from Texas. He weighs in at 245 pounds. Here is Terry
Funk!!! ("Simple Man" by Lynard Skynard starts to play and the crowd goes wild. Terry
Funk comes down to the ring to a huge ovation.)
FINK: And his opponent...from Minnesota. He weighs in at 269 pounds. Here is
Scott Hall!!! (Hall's music starts up and he comes down to the ring with a mixed reaction. He's very much over as a cool heel...) Joey: Nice collar and elbow and we're on our way! Bobby: Yeah, ya know Joey, it's good to see an old timer like Funk out there, mixin it up with the likes of Scott Hall and the other boys in XCW.
[Funk steps behind Hall, into a reverse waist lock.] Joey: Nice reversal by Hall! Russian Leg Sweep! [Funk stumbles to his feet, as Scott Hall taunts him. Funk throws a few misplaced haymakers, but Hall connects with a few of his own.]
Bobby: Funk's staggered ..... oooh and that last right took him right off his feet! What's Hall doing?
[Scott steps through the ring ropes, perching himself on the top ropes. He goes for the elbow drop.]
Joey: The Funker moved! [The referee begins his mandatory 10 count, and as he approaches 6, Scott Hall is on both feet, and Funk is on one knee ... Hall throws a right.]
Joey: Funk blocks! Right hand! And another! And another! Hall's reeling! [Hall catches the fourth hook, and catches Terry in an explosive DDT!] Bobby: And you can be sure that Funk's gonna feel that one in the mornin'. [ Scott Hall rolls out of the ring under the bottom rope, and reappears with ...] Joey: It's Terry Funk's branding iron!! Bobby: Now I wonder what he intends to do with that ... [Funk, still dazed from the DDT that planted him in the center of the ring, makes it to his hands and knees. Hall buries the branding iron into Funk's back, as he collapses back to the mat.
Joey: Jesus! What's hall thinking?! He was in complete control of this match! [The Ref calls for the bell, and slides out of the ring. Hall continues to smash the branding iron into the small of Terry Funk's aging back. He squirms around on the mat.]
Bobby: Now this is outta line. Somebody's gonna have to do something about this! Joey: It's Jake Roberts!! [The crowd lets out a cheer as Jake 'The Snake' Roberts comes running (well, it's running for him) down the entrance ramp, sliding into the ring, stopping
Hall's assault on the Wrestling Legend.]
Bobby: Now, I don't know if I'd be getting in Hall's face, with that branding iron in his hand ...
[Roberts stand toe to toe with Scott Hall, and they verbally jab back and forth at one another.]
Jake: Roberts has the branding iron! Bobby: Hall has butter fingers!!! [Funk begins to make his way to his feet ... clutching his ribs, which have undoubtedly been badly bruised by Hall's attack with the branding iron.]
Bobby: Looks like Hall's gonna get what he had coming to him here ... [Roberts pulls out a match and LIGHTS THE BRANDING IRON ON FIRE!!] JS: OH .. MY .. GOD!!! He's lit it on fire!! Watch out Scott Hall! [Jake Roberts holds the tire iron high in the air, to a loud cheer from the crowd in attendance. He points at Hall, asking the crowd for their approval, and approval is what he gets.]
Bobby: The people wanna see Hall get it! Give it to him Jake! Burn him! [Roberts nods his head and cocks back the flaming branding iron ...] Joey: OH MY GOD! He used it on Funk! He used it on Funk! Poor Terry Funk has just been branded yet again by his own damn branding iron! And these people hate it!!
[Hall laughs as Jake Roberts lays into Funk, who is on the ground agonizing, burns starting to form on his skin. Roberts holds up the iron to a chorus of boos, and drops it to the mat]
Bobby: And look at this, they're laying the boots into him now, as if that weren't enough. Joey: This is ridiculous! Somebody stop this! [XCW Security storms the ring, taking half a dozen to stop the assault on Funk. Quickly following is EMT's, who place an almost convulsing Terry Funk on a backboard, carrying him to the back.]
Joey: That was disgusting Brain, absolutely disgusting! Bobby: Texas Steak anyone? HAHA!!! FINK: Here is your winner as a result of a disqualifactation....Terry Funk!!! Joey: It's too late now...he doesn't care. (Camera cuts to Lance Storm preparing for his match...all of a sudden a man in a hooded sweatshirt comes in and lays into him. Woman is right behind him laughing like crazy, and then the guy takes a chair and smashes it over the ankle of Lance Storm...he does this until we hear a cracking sound.)
Woman: HAHAHA...lets go to the ring!
