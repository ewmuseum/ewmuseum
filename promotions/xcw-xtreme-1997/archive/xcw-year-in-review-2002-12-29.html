XCW Year In Review

December 29, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We fade onto studio covered in New Years decore. Michael Cole is standing there, and the producers are right behind him working on clips for the XCW's
Saturday Morning show.]
Michael: Over the past year the XCW has had many ups and downs. We've seen wrestlers taken to their limit, we've seen wrestlers that we didn't even know turn in some of the best matches of 2002. We've seen some great Heart Felt interviews, and tonight...we're going to review. Over the next couple of hours we're going to show you some matches, and some great moments from the
XCW...lets talk about this first little thing we're going to show you. One of the best things about the XCW was the confrontations between Mikey Whipwreck and The Rock. These two had the oddest love hate relationship...here's a couple examples of what I'm talking about....
(Camera fades onto Mikey Whipwreck who is entering the arena. He's whistling a familiar tune "Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck.".....he walks into his dressing room and The Rock is there. Mikey can't believe he's face to face with
The Rock. )
Mikey: Holy cow, can I have an autograph?? The Rock: A what? Mikey: An autograph, Mr... Uhhh.. Maivia? The Rock: The Rock comes into your dressing room to give you some advice about tonights match, and you want an autograph?
Mikey: Oh... Well, could you write the advice down, then autograph that? (The Rock puts his hand in Mikey's face) The Rock: Do not speak again until The Rock says you can... Mikey: Um... Ok.. The Rock: Now The Rock comes in here because he's damn sick and tired of being in the Land Of The Rising Sun. He's sick of all these little people, and he's damn sick and tired of the thick garlic smell...which reminds The Rock...do you like sushi?
Mikey: Well.... No. The Rock:You don't like sushi? Well then Little Mikey Whipwreck you must like eggrolls...do you like eggrolls?
Mikey: I used to. But ever since I came to Japan, every street vendor, every biker, every whatever has been stuffing their eggrolls in my mouth.
The Rock: You make The Rock sick...taking anyone eggrolls you can find. So does that make you a little cumsi-coomsa?
Mikey: Cumsi-coomWHAT? Uh, sure. The Rock: Maybe The Rock isn't speaking your language...do you like pie? Mikey: Oh definitely. My mom's was the best. The Rock: The Rock thinks that you are one messed up kid, and tonight if you don't listen to The Rocks advice we might all be stuck in this hell hole. Now
The Rock loves to make people bleed... The Rock has made Triple H bleed.. And if there's ONE place...that Triple H bleeds it's that big ol' nose of his.
Mikey: Ah, got'cha. The Rock: Now if you let the Rock down...he will have to Rock bottom you. Now
The Rock doesn't want to do that, but he will....
(Rock turns around to leave and before he leaves he says this...) The Rock: Why don't you grow some facial hair kid...it'll make you look like a man. (After Rock leaves, Mikey's lip trembles) Mikey: I SHAVE EVERY DAY FOR YOUR INFORMATION!!!!!!..........damn you...... (Camera fades) CUT TO ANOTHER SCENE [We cut to Mikey Whipwreck trying to have a conversation with the Rock. Even though the Rock can't speak due to a throat injury.]
Mikey: So...umm..you fought pretty hard at Trust No One. To bad you didn't win. [Rock gives him the People's Eyebrow.] Mickey: Cool. [Rock gives him a mean face.] Mikey: Cute. [Rock takes a deep breath.] Mikey: Are you sighing because of me? Don't worry buddy, I'm going to keep this
XCW TV Title around my waist if it's the last thing I do.
[Rock points to the door.] Mikey: You're right...I need to get out there for my match. [Mikey pats Rock on the shoulder, and leaves for his match.] CUT TO ANOTHER SCENE [Mikey isn't paying attention, and without even looking bumps right into none other than The Rock...]
The Rock:............ Mikey: Hey, watch yourself little... Ahhh, Rock, I, uh, wow, hey, uh, Rock, wow, ummmm The Rock: Do the Rock's eyes besieve him? Is this Mikey Whipwreck? Mikey: That's me! Michael Whipwreck, you can always tell by either name tag or dragon print tee-shirt.
The Rock: Back from Middle Earth, and here to take the World title huh? Well the Rock has a few questions for you...you ready for Funaki?
Mikey: Of course I am! It's my turn, not his. The Rock: Is your momma here tonight? Mikey: My mom? Uh.. No.. The Rock: The Rock thinks that's a damn shame, because when the world title is on the line..that's when a mother is needed the most. Now the last question the
Rock has for you is did you see "The Scorpion King"??
Mikey: No, I did not, I saw Jason X though! It was bad ass! The Rock: Whoa whoa whoa WHOA....you're telling the Rock that you missed the
"Scorpion King" to see Jason X?
Mikey: Well, not on purpose... I also saw Spirit.. Not because I wanted to but ummm, because I walked into the wrong theater.. That's it. Yeah.
The Rock: If the Rock wasn't wearing this fancy $500 shirt, he'd Rock Bottom you right back to the center of there earth where your little fat elf ass came from. The Rock thinks that you need to skip this tournament...buy yourself a ticket, buy youself some popcorn...sit your monkey ass down, and watch The
Scorpion King! Jason X....you make the Rock sick to his stomach.
Mikey: Hey, it was a good movie! The Rock: The Rock see's that you haven't changed one damn bit since he left. You'd think that the XCW closed up while the Rock was gone and just opened back up! Mikey: I suppose.. But I'm on the road to a championship, Rock. Me! Mikey! The Rock: Haven't you won the title before? Mikey: Yeah... The Rock: THEN ya shouldn't have lost the damn thing in the first place you big pile of monkey crap!!! The Rock is back, he's headed to the ring...and after tonight he's gonna grab that XCW World Title from WHOEVER has it. IF YA
SMELL.... Mikey: What the Mikey is cooking!! The Rock: WHAT THE....Wait a minute...heh...did the Rock just hear you say his catch phrase?
Mikey: No.. I, uh.. Said Scorpion King RULES! The Rock: NEVER EVER use the Rock's catch phrase again. Now if the Rock see's your ass next week, and you don't have a ticket stub from the Scorpion
King...it's gonna be one hell of a smack down for Mikey Whipwreck! Got it? Mikey: Got it! Yes sir! [Mikey leaves quickly....]
