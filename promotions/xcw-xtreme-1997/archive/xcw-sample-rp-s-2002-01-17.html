1. Randy Savage 2. Shane Douglas 3. Triple H 4. Kurt Angle 5. Steve Austin 6. Buff Bagwell [The scene opens up inside Macho Man's locker room where Randy and Michael Cole are sitting in two chairs infront of a TV monitor.]
Michael Cole: Well, Randy, I do have this video handy of my latest interview with - Randy Savage: (cutting Cole off) Hold it right there, Michael Cole. You know the Macho Man has had it up to here with having to watch that blabbering idiot come out and spew the same garbage each and every damn time. So as for this..
[The Macho Man takes Cole's tape of Angle's latest interview and drops it on the ground. He then removes his glasses and looks right into the camera with those intense eyes of his and lets a small smirk spread over his lips.]
Randy Savage: Just like your reign as a top star in the XCW, I'm also going to put an end to this tit for tat shit you seem to love playing. (Macho motions down to where the video tape of Angle's interview lays beneath his boot)
Michael Cole: But Randy! Don't think it'd be wise to see what your compitition will be doing to prepare for your big match this coming Sunday?
Randy Savage: Probably the exact same shit he's been doing and saying since I got here. ..nothing special.
[With that Savage stops down and smashes the video.] Michael Cole: But.. bu.. Randy Savage: Michael Cole.. get the hell out of here! [Savage stands tall and grabs a handful of Cole's shirt.. forcing him back up to his feet, Randy yanks him to the door and then forcefully sends him sprawling down the hallway. The door is then slammed shut. Savage turns to look back at the camera.]
Randy Savage: Listen up, Kurt. Enough's enough. Agreed? Now you can prance you silly ass back onto camera and give us that load of BS one more time - we've all heard it. Yea, you've got some shiney medals. Yea, you've got your three I's. Yea, you got the best of me last Monday night.. and yea, you damn near broke the Macho Man's ankle too. Well bravo, Kurt Angle.. bravo. But what I don't think you seem to be able to comprehend my friend is that everything you said.. everything you've done.. (chuckles) It's all not worth shit come Sunday night. Cause once that match starts, Angle, you're gonna be face to face with the Macho Man. Nose to nose with Randy Savage. Eye to eye
(pauses and smiles) with the man that isn't leaving that arena Sunday night without making you pay big time for your actions on Malice.
[Savage walks back over to where his chair was and reaches behind it.. he pulls out a 2x4 wrapped in barbwire.]
Randy Savage: A long time ago there was another superstar that liked to call himself a patriot, Angle. Infact, you and him.. you're about at the same intelect. He'd stumble on down to the ring with this puppy (Macho looks at the
2x4), and he'd wave his American flag, and he'd make a real ass out of himself.. just like you.
[Savage smirks, looks down, and then suddenly snaps.. his eyes again turn to the camera, only this time he hoists the 2x4 up into the air and then sends it crashing into a nearby wall..]
Randy Savage: Call it mind games, call it a threat, hell.. I'm gonna call it a big ol' favor, Kurt. But mark my words, boy, Sunday night I'm not going out there to win any match. I'm not coming down to that ring with any hops of pinning your shoulders to the mat.. I'm gonna do right, what you screwed up.
You tried to end my career, Kurt.. but you didn't get the damn job done. Now? ..it's my turn. [Savage drops the 2x4.] [The camera suddenly cuts out and then reopens outside Macho man's locker room where Michael Cole is still holding his head as he sturggles back to his feet.
Suddenly Savage's door swings open..] Randy Savage: Make yourself useful, Cole.. give this to Angle.. [Savage whips a video tape of HIS latest interview right at Cole before slamming the door.. this time the scene fades out.]
