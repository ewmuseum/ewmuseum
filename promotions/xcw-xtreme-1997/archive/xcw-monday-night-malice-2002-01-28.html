XCW Monday Night Malice

January 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Camera cuts to a sold out crowd in Los Angeles, in the XCW arena. The arena seats about 1,000 people, and they're all packed in here tonight for the huge event. Nothing has been said, but there's a certain feeling about SOMETHING big going down tonight....we cut to Jim Ross and Jesse Venutra who are seated at ringside.]
Jim: WELCOME everyone!!!! New Years Brawl was a night to remember, and tonight we're going to clear some things up, as well as get on with the XCW action.
Jesse: Well I'll tell ya what Jimbo...I sat home and watched the most pathetic display by a commentator ever. Paul Roma is nothing more than a loser who never was, and he isn't in wrestling anymore because he doesn't have enough class to sell for someone.
Jim: Well he's a former Horsemen. Jesse: Who the hell isn't? I mean the Horsemen had more dudes in it than Kobe
Tai, and that has nothing to do with what I'm saying Jim. I from now on until my last days here in the XCW will be next to you for the PPV's. Enough said about that!!!
Jim: Well...fans tonight we've got some great matches lined up for you. Al Snow will be taking on Steve Austin in what should be a great match for the TV title. As well as Terry Funk and Shane Douglas in the Main Event.
Jesse: Not only that but we get to see who the new commish is going to be. Jim: Fans took a vote throughout the week, and who do you think it's going to be? Jesse: I'm leaning towards Mr. Perfect. The man is one of my favorites, and I think he did a lot for the XCW when he was the commish.
Jim: Not Bob Backlund? Jesse: That nutcase...no way. Jim: Well lets not waste any time here tonight. Lets take you to our first match, and the debut of the Ultimate Warrior...
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first from Las Vegas, Nevada...weighing in at 247 pounds. Here is
Val Venis!!! ("Helllooooo Ladies" is heard all over the arena, and he walks out in his little towel and the crowd goes crazy for him. He doesn't seem to even care that the fans are cheering for him. He climbs into the ring and takes his towel off quickly and stretches a few times...)
FINK: And his opponent...from parts unknown. He weighs in at 256 pounds. Here is The Ultimate Warrior!!!!!
(Warriors music starts to play, and the lights go out and turn all different colors. Fans are all on their feet as they wait to see the return of The
Ultimate Warrior... <The bell sounds and both men stare across the ring at one another for a long moment. Finally they meet in the center of the ring and it looks like they're exchanging a few choice words while circling one another.>
Jim Ross: I don't think anyone in the world would have ever expected to see this matchup! Val Venis and the Warrior!
Jesse Ventura: Tell me about it, Ross. Who in the right mind would believe the Warrior could ever show his face in public again after his last stint.
Jim Ross: Jesse! Jesse Ventura: What?! <The Warrior suddenly raises his right hand high into the air in a test of strength.> Jim Ross: The Warrior is and always has been an amazing athelete! You're talking about a guy who's defeated such greats as Hulk Hogan and Rick Rude!
Jesse Ventura: He's still a freak in my book, JR. Jim Ross: Call the Warrior what you will, but right now he's asking Val Venis to put it all on the line here in a test of strength.
<Venis looks at the Warrior and then turns to look over the crowd. Smiling that vile smirk, he nods and hoists his hand up as well. The two men slowly interlock their fingers and then all at once let out vicious grunts and barrel into one another.>
Jim Ross: And here we go! Jesse Ventura: Come on, Val! Teach this guy a lesson. Jim Ross: Well right now I think it's pretty safe to say that the Warrior gaining some major control in their at the moment.
Jesse Ventura: Kick him, Venis! Jim Ross: The Warrior has got Val down to one knee! <The look of extreme pain is splayed all over Val's face as the Warrior brings him to both of his knees and continues still the apply even more pressure.>
Jesse Ventura: Look at that ape! He's foaming from the freakin' mouth!
<The Warrior growls and lets Venis' hands go. Val lets out a yelp before grabbing both his hands back and rubbing his now bruised knuckles. The Warrior doesn't waste anytime though, he quickly grabs a hand of Val's hair with one hand and continually smashes his other fist into the side of Venis' head.>
Jim Ross: Oh man! Look at the Warrior go! He's pounding the living daylights out of poor, Val Venis.
Jesse Ventura: I think it's about time the ref got in there.. those fists look pretty closed to me, JR.
Jim Ross: I don't think the referee wants to get too close to the Warrior, especially not at the moment.
Jesse Ventura: We should get the Rock down here! He did such a great damn job officialing at the pay per view! Did ya taste the sarcasm on that, Ross?
<The Warrior brings Val back up to his feet and sends him into the ropes.>
Jim Ross: Almost as thick as my BBQ sauce!
<The Warrior ducks low and catches Val with a huge back body drop.> Jesse Ventura: Whoa! Flying adult film star at twelve o'clock! Jim Ross: The Warrior just sent Venis for quite the ride and he's not done yet, folks. Both men are getting back to their feet, but again it's the
Warrior who comes in fast and hard with a big fore arm smash. Jesse Ventura: He's got Val backed up into the turnbuckles and he's just hammering him like crazy. Venis has got to really get on the ball in there.
Jim Ross: When you're in there with some of the intensity of the Warrior I think it's hard to find an opening.
<The Warrior irish whips Venis out of one of the corners and into the turnbuckle opposite of them. Venis hits hard and lets out a shrill scream of pain.>
Jesse Ventura: There ya go! Jim Ross: Warrior went for a corner splash, but Venis gets the boot up! <The Warrior stumbles back slightly, shakes it off, and then comes right back in - and again Venis gets a boot up! > Jesse Ventura: There ya go, Val! Now go finish him off, damnit! Jim Ross: Here comes Venis! <crowd winces> Jesse Ventura: Jeez! This guy is freakin' hopeless! Jim Ross: Well Val Venis gave it his all, but the Warrior catches him and sends him right back into the mat with a beautiful powerslam.
Jesse Ventura: Ooh great, now I've gotta look at this guy shake the damn ropes and stomp around again.
Jim Ross: The Warrior's bringing Venis slowly back up to his feet, and thus far
I must say, the Warrior has shown no signs of ring rust. He's dominating, Val!
Jesse Ventura: He's got Venis hooked up for what looks like a suplex.. <Sure enough the Warrior hauls Venis up into a beautiful hanging vertical suplex. He just holds him there for a long moment before dropping back and driving Venis into the mat.>
Jim Ross: Uh oh.. look at this, Jesse! The Warrior's getting a bounce off the ropes himself annnd -
Jesse Ventura: Move, Val! MOVE! Jim Ross: No such luck! The Warrior drops a BIG elbow right into the sternum! Jesse Ventura: He's hopeless. <The Warrior gets right back to his feet and drops another elbow, and another.>
Jim Ross: Looks like Val needs to get himself a breather! He's rolled right under the bottom rope and to the outside of the ring!
Jesse Ventura: Good move! It'll give him some time to think about how BAD he's been thus far.
Jim Ross: Well he better hurry up and regroup, because the Warrior isn't going to waste anytime!
<The Warrior climbs to the outside ring apron. Val stands right infront of the
Mexican anounce table, he turns just in time to get a flying clothesline off the ring apron! Both men topple right onto and then over the anounce table - much to the crowd's enjoyment.>
Jim Ross: Beautiful! That was an absolutely beautiful flying clothesline! And the Warrior is right back up and he's bringing Val with him!
Jesse Ventura: Uh oh.. look at this! He's got Val up in a gorilla press and.. ooh jeez! Jim Ross: What power! The Warrior is actually pumping Val up and down while holding up in that gorilla press slam!
<Warrior gets a running start..> Jim Ross: He just tossed Venis into the crowd! Jesse Ventura: I've seen enough. I think I'm gonna go get some nachos, Ross, you want anything?
Jim Ross: Jesse! Sit down! <With Venis out cold in the crowd, the Warrior rolls back into the ring and continues to pump up the crowd.>
Jesse Ventura: You're so uptight, Ross! Jim Ross: Give Val a shot, Jesse! Look! He's already getting back to his feet! Jesse Ventura: Great, so we can watch him get his ass tossed right back into the crowd? Jim Ross: Actually.. look at this! Val is climbing back over the guard railing and he's getting a chair!
<Venis grabs the folded steel chair and starts to head back into the ring, but the crowd boos so loudly that he turns before getting into the ring and starts to yell at them! Without wany warnign the Warrior, from inside the ring, reaches down over the top rope and grabs Venis by his hair and starts to yank him back into the ring!>
Jesse Ventura: Idiot! Hit him! Hit him with the damn chair! <Sure enough Val brings the chair straight up and winds up cracking the Warrior straight in the face with it! The Warrior gets sent backwards in a huff and winds up taking out the referee!>
Jim Ross: Oooh man! Jesse Ventura: YES! <The Warrior continues to stumble around holding his face in pain. Val meanwhile reaches under the bottom rope, grabs the Warrior by his ankles, takes his legs out from under him, and then drags him back outside the ring.>
Jim Ross: It took a steel chair, but I think Val may have just gotten things to sway into his favor.
Jesse Ventura: By any means necessary, Ross. Jim Ross: That chair shot busted the Warrior open and bit and Val is capitalizing big time! Look at hims pound fist after fist into the already lacerated forehead of the Warrior!
Jesse Ventura: The Warrior's squirting now, baby! That's my boy, Val! <Venis grabs the Warrior by his hair and sends him face first into the steel ring post! As he stumbles backwards, Val quickly grabs him - runs - and drives him face first onto the steel steps with a running bulldog!>
Jim Ross: Bulldog onto the steel steps! Jesse Ventura: And now Val's got that chair again! This is great! I'm glad I stayed! Jim Ross: Sure enough, Venis is measuring the Warrior up! Jesse Ventura: Take his head off! <CLaNG!> <Crowd winces.> Jim Ross: Ooooh man! Jesse Ventura: That was great! <Venis grabs a very limp and bloody Warrior and throws him back into the ring.>
Jim Ross: Venis is making the cover, but the referee is still down!
Jesse Ventura: What?! Oooh come on! One, two, three! This thing should be over! Jim Ross: Wait a minute.. he's stirring slightly. Oh I hope it doesn't end like this, Jesse! This is a chepa win, a REAL cheap win for Val Venis.
<Referee.> ONE ! ! ! Jesse Ventura: Two! Two you, moron! TWO ! ! ! Jesse Ventura: Come on! Put your damn hand back to the mat! Jim Ross: THREE?!?! T H R E - Jim Ross: The Warrior kicked out! Jesse Ventura: Well isn't that some shit! Come on ref! I could have counted to a hundred.
<Venis argues with the referee for awhile before hauling the Warrior back to his feet and drilling him into the mat with a beautiful DDT.>
Jim Ross: Wait a minute.. look at this! Venis is going to the top! I think we might see a Money Shot!
Jesse Ventura: I can't wait! Jim Ross: Val is up on top! <Venis looks over the crowd and does his usual little dance up on top before leaping off annnnd - >
<HUGE POP.> Jesse Ventura: WHAT?!? Jim Ross: The Warrior rolled out of the way! Venis just ate mat! <The Warrior suddenly rolls right back to his feet and starts to stomp around and shake the ropes. He then looks down at Val, who's still flat out on the mat in a bit of a daze. The Warrior gets a bounce off the ropes..>
Jim Ross: WARRIOR SPLASH! <HUGE POP> Jesse Ventura: DAMNIT! Jim Ross: WARRIOR SPLASH! WARRIOR SPLASH! <Referee> ONE ! ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! ! T H R E E ! ! ! ! Jim Ross: He did it! The Warrior wins! Val missed a big Money Shot attempt and the Warrior takes advantage and the win with a beautiful splash off the ropes of his own.
Jesse Ventura: It's enough to make you sick. Jim Ross: And look at these fans! They're on their feet! <With his music blaring over the PA system, the Warrior begins to celebrate in his usual fashion. He shakes the ropes and runs around the ring like a wild man. All of a sudden his music stops and who should appear but HBK. He walks down to the ring, and slides in right past The Warrior...he asks for the mic and begins to speak.>
Shawn: Does the Heartbreak Kid look good or what? Ya know I took a little vaction, and tonight I'm back. Tonight...I finish what I started, and Warrior if you'll excuse me you can leave the ring now.
<Warrior just stands there breathing deeply and snarling...> Shawn: Listen I know I don't speak your funky little language, but I know you know what LEAVE means. Get to steppin'....
<Warrior begins to leave the ring area....> Shawn: That's right Warrior...keep walking. You know if there was anyone in this sport who did something to ruin it..I'd say it has to be the Warrior.
Fans..that's exactally what you don't want to end up like. The man's ego is the size of New Jersey, and he can't wrestle for the life of him.
<Warrior stops, but doesn't turn around...> Shawn: It's pieces of (bleep) like him that made me comeback to wrestling. I couldn't stand by and watch guys like Warrior, Goldberg, Vader...stink up the ring anymore. It's time that we have a showstopper, a 5 star wrestler....The
Warrior is certainly not him.
<Warrior slowly turns around...> Shawn: Listen to me Hellwig...this is my time, and I suggest you leave. I don't care who you are, I don't care if the fans like you or not. I don't...not lea...
<All of a sudden the Warrior charges the ring and Shawn attacks him right away.> Jim: Oh and Shawn Michaels is beating up on the Warrior!!!! Jesse: Well Warrior made a dumb move and decided to run back to the ring. Jim: Shawn sends him to the ropes, btu Warrior reverses it....Warrior with a clothesline...NO!!!!
Jesse: SUPERKICK!!!!! Jim: Shawn superkicks Warrior, and he goes down hard!! Michael was quick like a cat on that exchange and Warrior goes down.
Jesse: HBK is taking off now, and Warrior is just now starting to recover. Jim: And there goes the Warrior going after Shawn Michaels. We'll be right back!!!!
