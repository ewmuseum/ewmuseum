XCW Prime Time Wrestling

November 11, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A familiar sax is heard as we open up to the set of Prime Time Wrestling. Bobby Heenan is sitting there in his blue suit, and Gorilla ia sitting next to him in his purple suit. Both men have already started talking even before the cameras are rolling. Camera cuts to Gorilla and he begins to speak...]
Gorilla: Welcome everyone to the very first Prime Time Wrestling. I'm your host
Gori - Bobby: I'm the host. Gorilla: You're not going to start already are you? Bobby: JJ told me I was the host. Gorilla: You want to be the host? We'll call you the host. Bobby: Good. Gorilla: Fans it's going to be a happening, December 1st is closer than you think and I've got the line up for the card right here. We'll be going over that a little bit later, and I think it's the best card I've seen in a very long time.
Bobby: Well anything that doesn't have Sgt. Slaughter or even Tito Santana is a winner. Gorilla: What do you have agains those two? Bobby: Haven't you ever wanted to tell Sgt. Slaughter to clear his throat before he speaks?
Gorilla: No. Bobby: Have you ever heard what he sounds like when he does? Gorilla: I can't say that I have, but I'm sure you're going to tell me. Bobby: He has an Arabic accent. Gorilla: Will you stop! Fans lets take you to Owen Hart in action against Iron
Mike Sharpe!!
Bobby: Sgt. Habibbi. [Both men turn to the tv which is in between them, and the match has already started. ] The two start to brawl as soon as Mike Sharpe gets inside, and Sharpe uses his slight size advantage to gain early dominance. But, it doesn't last long. Owen fires back with a couple of quick rights and a headbutt which sends Mike flying backward, nearly off his feet. Angrily, Mike attempts to clothesline Owen, but misses and goes flying off the ropes. On his way back, he's met with an elbow from Owen, which nearly takes off his head.
Now, laying on the ground, Mike manages to open his eyes just in time to be met, square in the nose, with Owen's knee. Yelping in pain, Sharpe manages to get back to his feet, where he's met with a superkick that sends him flying back to the mat.
The ref gets a long two, before Mike manages to get his shoulder up. A sadistic smile on his face, Owen picks up Mike by his hair. Having a complete advantage,
Owen hits an Enzugiri, which should put an end to the match quickly. The ref's arm hits the canvas another two times, before he notices that Mike's leg is on the ropes. He signals to Owen this, and Hart looks up rudely. Upset about not having the pinfall, he approaches the referee, and starts harassing him, insulting him, and viciously bumping the ref with his stomach. The ref keeps stepping back, afraid, until he sees a figure move out of the corner of his eye.
Mike is back on his feet. Seeing the small smile on the ref's face, Owen turns around quickly, just in time to be met by Mike. Iron Mike Sharpe nails him with a boot to the stomach and a suplex and quickly goes for the cover. Even with the ref's slightly quicker than normal count, Sharpe only manages to keep IOwen down for a two.
Picking Owen up, Mike swings wildly at him, almost taking off his head. Luckily, Owen has the presence of mind to duck, and Mike misses him by mere inches. With Sharpe off balance, Owen's quick kick to the midsection sends him sprawling to the mat. Another cover by Owen leads only to a one-and-a-half count by the referee. Once again, rising angrily, Owen targets his frustration toward the referee. Up to this point, he's dominated the match, but has been unable to put Mike away.
This time, knowing Sharpe is behind him, Owen hits a back elbow on him, and follows it up with another hard kick to the midsection. A hair-splitting kick to Mike's face sends him in the opposite direction. As Mike charges at him,
Owen moves his body out of the way, and smashes his opponent viciously with a headbutt. With Sharpe laid out on the mat, Owen decides to go high-risk. After climbing a turnbuckle, he launches himself off the top rope, and splashes the motionless Mike.
Owen finally decides to end the match quickly. Not finished with his opponent,
Owen sets him up, yet again, for his trademark move, his finisher – The Sharp
Shooter. Putting the icing on the cake, a cake that's been baked practically since the match started, Sharpe is locked in with the Sharp Shooter. With all the abuse he's taken so far, there's no way IMS going to kick out of this one.
The crowd dislikes the end result, and boo, while Sharpe is being helped out of the ring by the refs.
In the ring, Owen watches them with a smile on his face. The smile doesn't stay there for long. Tony Anthony comes from behind Owen and starts to pound away on him. Punch after punch sends Owen down to the mat. After he gets back to his feet, he backs up against the ropes. A bad move on his part. Dirty White Boy just runs forward and nails Owen, sending him flying over the top rope, and tumbling to the floor. Tony Anthony raises his arms in victory as Prime Time heads back up…
[Camera cuts back to Bobby Heenan and Gorilla Monsoon.] Gorilla: Well there's one match we're going to be seeing on December 1st. "Dirty White Boy" Tony Anthony, and Owen Hart will be facing each other. That should be an outstanding match.
Bobby: You know this Owen Hart, I like him. He's a talented guy, a far more talented guy than his brother. He's the kind of guy that will slap you in the face and then convince you that the guy next to him did it.
Gorilla: Kinda like you? Bobby: When I slap someone in the face I don't talk afterwards. I run. Gorilla: You would. [All of a sudden Bobby's Banana phone rings. Gorilla is shocked...] Bobby: Excuse me for a minute. [Bobby picks up.] Bobby: Yeah, did you get the tickets? December 1st, uh huh. Well don't get smart with me, I'll slap you in the mouth. Do you want me to knock you down? Uh huh, okay...I'll talk to you later mom.
[Bobby hangs up like it's nothing, and Gorilla just stares at him.] Bobby: What? Gorilla: Ugh, we'll be right back folks.
