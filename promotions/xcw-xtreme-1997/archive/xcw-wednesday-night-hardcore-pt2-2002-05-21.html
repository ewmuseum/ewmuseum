XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 2)

May 21, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As we fade back from commercial we saw the event that just transpired. Scott
Hall whacks Arn over the back of the head with the chair, and Arn goes crumbling to the floor. Cut to Arn talking with Magnum TA in his office...]
Arn: Ahhh damnit. Magnum: That looked pretty damn bad. Arn: Did it? Magnum: They both got you pretty good. You did what you were supposed to do, and that was save the match from turning into a big cluster bomb.
Arn: Yeah... Magnum: So what are you going to do with Flair and Hall? Arn: I don't know y.... (Chris Jericho walks in....) Chris: Here...I brought you some asprin, I heard you had a headache. Now I have my opponent...my match is going to be on right after Raven and Richards. Got it?
Arn: Yeah yeah whatever. Chris: Ha....loser. (Chris walks out and the camera cuts back to the ring.) Jim: Fans this next match should be one hell of a slobber knocker. When you get someone as demented and sick as Raven into the ring with a character like
Stevie Richards...you know something is going to go down.
Joel: Stevie Richards is a pussy plain and simple, and tonight I hope he gets his ass kicked by Raven. Maybe Raven will beat on him so hard that Richards will grow a sack...
Jim: You know there are kids watchin', why don't you watch your mouth? Joel: Well I've been watching yours, but only half of it moves. Jim: (Sigh) Fans lets get to the next match... FINK: Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is a RAVEN'S RULES match. First coming down to the ring, from Philly, PA....he weighs in at 230 pounds. Here is "Dancin'" Stevie Richards!!! [Richards comes down to "She's a maniac" by Hall and Oates, and the crowd is shocked at his choice of music. He enters from the curtain, and he's got the dumbest look on his face a man can possibly have. He climbs into the ring and gets little or no response.]
FINK: And his opponent...from Short Hills, NJ. He weighs in at 246...here is
Raven!!! ["Keep Em' Seperated" starts to play and Raven walks out with a sour look on his face. He pays no attention to the fans, and no attention to Stevie who is dancing in the ring. Raven rolls in, and slouches against the turnbuckle.]
Jim: Raven is just slouched against that turnbuckle, and Stevie doesn't know what to do. He's not sure if he should go over there, or just wait.
Joey: If I were Stevie I'd go over there and kick him right in the jaw. Jim: If you were Stevie...you'd be thin. [Stevie slowly approaches Raven, and once he gets to him Raven makes it back to his feet. Stevie turns around and runs to the outside. He flips Raven off, and starts to dance...Raven sees this and runs to the outside after him.]
Jim: And we've got a chase... Joel: This is sad. Jim: Stevie is refusing to lock up with Raven, and it looks like his frustration is working. Raven is looking a little winded...and Stevie slides back into the ring.
Joel: Look how tough he is when Raven is on the outside. [Raven slides back in, and Stevie hits him with a kick. Another kick to the ribs, and follows up with a kneedrop. Stevie brings Raven to his feet and whips him into the ropes...Raven ducks a clothesline, and comesback with a bulldog!]
Joel: I could feel the impact from here!!! Jim: Raven back up, and he stomps away on the hand of Richards. He's stomping away on it, and he's trying to break his damn hand.
Joel: Good. [Raven runs to the ropes, jumps up and stomps right down onto the left hand of
Richards. Stevie screams out in pain, and Raven just stands there and smiles.
Raven pulls Stevie up by the hair, and drives a right hand into the side of his face. Richards drops to one knee, and Raven gets a running start....]
Jim: MAFIA KICK!!!! Square on the jaw, and this doesn't look good for Stevie. Joel: Oh here we go!!! [Raven goes to the outside and brings in a chair. Stevie is rubbing his jaw, as he makes it back up...and Raven slams the chair onto his head. Stevie looks like he's seeing stars...and Raven doesn't let up, and hits him with another brutal chair shot.]
Jim: My God...the sickening thud of that chair is enough to make someone cringe. Joel: Nice!!! Jim: Raven said he was going to come in and dominate the match, and that is just what he's doing. He's sending a message to everyone in that locker room.
Joel: I hope Mikey Whipwreck is listening. [Raven unfolds the chair, and brings Stevie to his feet. Raven hits a back suplex, and the back of Stevies head hits the chair.]
Jim: GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY!!! HOLY HELL!!!!! Stevie's head hit the chair like a ton of bricks, and it busted him open like a damn melon.
Joel: I love it. That was awesome!!! Jim: Raven gets up and see's the pool of blood...look at the sick and twisted smile on his face. What a disgusting human being!!!!
Joel: WHOOO!!! More!! More!!! [Raven brings Stevie to his feet and the camera picks up the blood pouring out of his head...Raven drives a fist into the face and Richards drops to the floor. Raven grabs the chair jumps up for a double kneedrop to the face, and quickly puts the chair over his knees....crushing Stevie's face!]
Jim: Awe damnit!!! Joel: Holy shit! Jim: The impact had to have crushed every damn bone in his face!!! Joel: The man is bleeding from the front, the man is bleeding from the back...what an awesome display of violence. Could tonight be any better?
YES....could it be anymore violent...NO! Jim: I have never seen anything like this in my whole time working here in the
XCW. Maybe when Taz lost his finger, but this has got to top that. Raven is brutal!
Joel: WAIT A MINUTE.... [Camera cuts to the curtain, and out walks Tommy Dreamer. The crowd stands to their feet, and the he's saying something that the cameras can't pick up. He's got a mean look on his face, and he's headed stright to the ring...]
Jim: DREAMER!!!! He's seen enough!!! He's going to put a damn stop to Raven!!!! Joel: Get out of here Dreamer...why do you always have to ruin everything? Damnit! Jim: Dreamer slides into the ring, and Raven goes to hit him with a chair....he ducks!! [Dreamer spins Raven around and plants him in the head with the steel chair!] Jim: HOW DO YA LIKE THAT???? Joel: Oooo that can't be good. Jim: Dreamer just smashed Raven's neck right into his body. That had to have jolted the spinal cord.
[Dreamer grabs Raven and holds him for Stevie to kick....Stevie closes his eyes hoping to make some big time contact, and Raven quickly moves out of the way.]
Jim: STEVIE KICK!!!! STEVIE KICK onto Dreamer, and he goes down hard. Joel: What an idiot!!! Jim: Stevie kicked Dreamer, and Raven spins Stevie around. EVENFLOW!!!!!! Joel: GOODNIGHT!!!! Jim: Raven is still feeling the effects of that chairshot, and he rolls out of the ring. Where the hell is he going?
Joel: He's had enough... Jim: Not in this lifetime. FINK: Ladies and gentlemen....this match has been ruled a NO CONTEST!!!! Jim: And the ref has tossed this match out. Joel: Raven should've gotten the win here...Tommy was the one to climb in at hit him with the chair.
Jim: But this is Raven's Rules...anything goes. Joel: You think you're so smart....yeah well....you're just a hick. Jim: Are you done? Joel: NO! You're just a hick who shills his crappy BBQ sauce. Jim: Now are you done? Joel: NO! You're just a hick who shills his crappy BBQ, and has a life long numb face!!! Jim: Are you done now? Joel: Yes. [Camera cuts to Scott Hall in his locker room drinking a 40. It looks like he hasn't had anything to drink in ages the way he's downing it. All of a sudden
Arn Anderson busts open the door with a piece of paper. Hall scrambles to put away the alcohol.]
Arn: Ya see this? Scott: Umm...yeah. Arn: This is a contract that you signed when you joined the XCW. You signed here saying that you will not be drinking before or during an XCW event, and tonight well I guess I caught you red handed.
Scott: No.... Arn: Oh then what's that behind your back? Scott: What's what? [Scott puts his other hand behind his back, quickly switches hands...and brings the other one forward.]
Scott: See I don't have anything in my hand. Arn: Put both hands in front of me. [Hall quickly drops the 40 behind him and brings his hands forward....you can hear the loud thud as it falls to the ground.]
Scott: What the hell? Who's throwing shit at me? I don't find that funny at all chico! Arn: Listen...you're out of here. Get out. Scott: WHAT!? [Security grabs Hall by the arm, and escourts him out....] Arn: Now what am I going to do about Ric Flair? [Arn picks up the 40, smells it...and takes a sip.]
