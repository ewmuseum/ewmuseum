XCW Wednesday Night Hardcore (part 4)

June 13, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Steve slides in and he's a house of fire. He dropkicks Sho to the outside, and backdrops Hunter onto the canvas. Hunter gets up and Steve hits a monkey flip,
Hunter hits the turnbuckle.]
Jim: And I think Steve is going to be able to handle his own here tonight. Joel: Uhh I guess. Jim: Steve Armstrong might be facing Triple H in the second round, so we could be seeing a future match.
Joel: And Steve isn't to happy with Hunter. Last week he tried to break Armstrongs arm, and Hunter is begging off. [Steve is shouting at Hunter and he brings him to his feet. Steve drives a knee into the midsection, and another knee into the midsection and he whips him into the other turnbuckle. Steve charges in, and Sho Fuanki from the apron...hits a slingshot clothesline...]
Jim: And Steve goes down hard!!! WOW Sho came out of no where, and the ref is telling him to get into his corner.
Joel: Nice, put a stop to Steve's offense. Jim: Triple H shaking things off, and he brings Steve to his feet. Huge right hand takes Steve down to one knee...another huge right hand.
Joel: Look at those fists. He's a damn monster! [Hunter stomps onto the head of Steve, and he hits the mat. Triple H continues to stomp on Steve until the ref pushes him back. Triple H argues with the ref for a bit, and moves him out of the way.]
Jim: And Dreamer and Whipwreck should be here. You know as well as I do that they're fighting in the back.
Joel: That was like a scene from Duces Wild...the gang fight scene. Jim: It was a scene from something that's for sure. One can only hope that they get back here soon, because Hunter and Sho are going to rip apart Steve
Armstrong. Joel: Don't forget about Raven!!! Jim: Oh there's Raven too...I forgot. [Hunter brings Steve to his feet, and he takes him back down with a kneedrop facesmash. Hunter tags out to Sho Funaki who runs to the top ropes. Sho raises his arms high into the air and hits a reverse flying elbow drop!]
Jim: But Steve moved!!!! Joel: That was a close shave right there!! Jim: Sho is holding that elbow, and Steve crawls back to his feet. Oh Steve stomps on that right elbow, and another one.
[Steve grabs the arm, and executes a Firemans Carry with an armbreaker. Sho screams out in pain, and Steve goes for the cover...]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! TH-NO....Sho Funaki kicks out at the last second. Joel: That looked a little slow to me. Jim: Sho is up now, and Steve with a turnbuckle smash. I don't think Sho has been hit that hard ever.
[Steve grabs Sho by the arm, and he locks in an arm wringer. Sho yells, and
Steve squeezes tighter. Sho slaps that right arm with his left to keep the blood flowing, and Steve just cranks it harder.]
Jim: Great move here by Steve Armstrong, he's trying to stall for some time here. Mikey Whipwreck, and Dreamer have yet to show up, but Steve is holding his own here tonight.
Joel: I can't believe it either. Sho needs to hurry up and make the tag to
Hunter before this ends in a submission.
Jim: Steve lifts Sho off the ground!!!! Joel: WHOA!!! Jim: He's adding that pressure to the arm, and the fans are loving it. [Steve lifts Sho up against in the arm wringer hold, and crotches him on the top rope. Sho screams out in a high-pitch, and Armstrong dropkicks him off the rope and onto the floor. Steve walks over to Triple H and slaps the taste right out of his mouth.]
Jim: OH MY!!!! Joel: Whoa you don't do that to Triple H!! Jim: Steve just did, and Hunter is being held back by the ref. Steve Armstrong is really playing it smart right here.
Joel: That's an oxymoron if I ever heard one. Jim: You're an oxymoron...fat pinhead. Joel: JR you don't want to have a war of words with me. I'll kill you. [All of a sudden we see Raven walk down to the ring. His forehead is busted wide open, and the crowd boo's at his arrival. He walks over and hops into the ring, and Steve Armstrong wants a piece of him...]
Jim: Collar and elbow tie-up, and Raven with a rake to the eyes. Fist to the forehead, and look at the blood pour out of Levy's head.
Joel: You mean Raven! Kayfabe breaker...I hate you people! Jim:....... [Raven sends Armstrong to the turnbuckle, and executes a drop toe-hold. Steve hits the ground hard, and Raven comes up with a kneedrop to the back of the neck.]
Jim: Raven is one of the most hated men in our sport today. Everywhere we go
Raven is boo'd, not even in his hometown is he liked.
Joel: Well nobody likes you either. At least Raven has some talent...you're just fat, and numb.
Jim: I'm going to tell you to shut up one last time, and if you....OH!!! Joel: WHOA!!! [Triple H was talking to the ref, and Sho tossed a chair into the ring and
Raven caught it...and slamed it right into the forehead of Steve. Armstrong is walking around dazed, and Raven hits a HUGE neckbreaker. Steve flops to the floor, and Raven makes the cover....]
Jim: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Joel: NO!! He didn't get him...Steve put his damn foot on the ropes. Jim: That was close....wow that was close. Raven is up now and he makes the tag to Hunter. This isn't going to be pretty.
[Raven holds Steve, and Hunter drives a boot into the gut. Another boot, and
Hunter snap mares him down to the canvas. Hunter brings Steve right back to his feet, and he whips him into the ropes. Hunter lifts him up...and lets him falls right onto the canvas stomach first.]
Jim: And that'll take everything right out of you. Where is Mikey and Tommy?? Joel: Ummm maybe fighting??? Jim: Steve Armstrong is in there with 3 other men, and these are three of the most skilled wrestlers in the world. Hunter grabs Steve again, and he lifts him up...HOT SHOT ONTO THE TURNBUCKLE!!!!!
Joel: HAHA!!! What an awesome move there by Triple H! Jim: He rolls Steve up for the cover!!! Hooks the leg...ONE!!! TWO!!! Another kickout by Steve Armstrong.
Joel: Oh come on...this is dumb. Knock the damn guy out with another chair shot, and lets go home.
Jim: Well he's already been smashed in the face with a chair, and now this. Steve Armstrong is one hell of a fighter, and he's not down and out yet. [Hunter brings him over to his corner, and all three men are now attacking him. Everyone is driving lefts and rights, and boots into Armstrong. Steve slumps down against the turnbuckle, and all three men stomp on him.]
Jim: Come on!!! Joel: Can you imagine the hate Armstrong will have for Dreamer and Whipwreck after this match is over?
Jim: Well it's not like they don't want to come down... Joel: I don't care. Look at Steve! Poor bastard! [The ref is trying to pull them off of Steve, and Hunter decks the ref.] Jim: THE REF IS OUT!!!! THE REF IS OUT!!!! Joel: This is where the fun begins!! Jim: No telling what they're going to do now. Joel: I have a few ideas. [Hunter brings Steve into the center of the ring, and locks in a Camel Clutch. Sho runs to the ropes and dropkicks Steve right in the face. Sho rolls out of the way, and right after that Raven comes in and dropkicks him in the face. Sho follows up and hits another dropkick....]
Jim: This is horrible...this is sick. Joel: This is great! Jim: Steve Armstrong is being brutalized in the ring right now, and there's nothing anyone can do about it.
[Sho grabs the chair in the ring, and SLAMS it into the face of Steve. At the same time though he catches Hunter's fingers. Hunter lets go and is shaking his fingers up and down...]
Jim: Sho accid - Sho goes down!!! Hunter just punched Sho to the floor, and
Raven spins him around and he gets decked too.
Joel: Hunter is a mad man!!!! Jim: Triple H grabs Steve, and now he's going to hit the Pedigree. He's got him over the steel chair....WAIT!!! Mikey and Dreamer are here.
[Tommy and Mikey run down, and both men are busted open pretty bad. Tommy tackles Hunter, and Mikey double clotheslines Raven and Sho. Dreamer brings
Hunter to his feet and throws him over the top ropes.]
Jim: And they're here to save the day! Joel: Yeah little to late!! Jim: Hunter is down on the floor, Raven goes over the top after a clothesline. Dreamer's headed out after him. Joel: And that leaves Funaki and Mikey...both men have a history with each other. Sho goes for a right hand, but Mikey blocks it. Mikey with a boot to the stomach, and a Whippersnapper!!!!
Jim: Good call there Joel... Joel: I know!!! [Mikey goes for the cover, and the ref is still out. Mikey gets up to check on the ref, and we see Piper limping down to the ring again. He's totally busted open, and he's got a bat in his hand.]
Jim: Mikey doesn't see Piper...and Piper climbs into the ring. Joel: Doesn't this guy die????? Jim: Piper winds back, and he smashes that bat into the knee of Whipwreck!!! Joel: Tommy and Raven are going at it on the outside of the ring. Jim: We've got total mayhem!!!! The ref is out, Steve is out, Funaki and Piper are working over Whipwreck....Hunter hurt his knee on that fall.
[Stevie Richards is slowly walking down to the ring now, and he joins in the fight between Dreamer and Raven. Tommy clotheslines both men over the guardrail, and he hops over to take care of business.]
Jim: Hunter has slid back into the ring, and he takes Sho out with a clothesline. Mikey is working over Piper, and HERE COMES TATANKA!!!
Joel: What the hell is this a Royal Rumble??? Jim: Tatanka, Chris Chavis whatever you want to call him...he's here now, and he pushes Hunter off of Sho. Tatanka now starts kicking Sho, and Hunter pushes
Tatanka off.
[Both men meet in the center of the ring, and they trade punches.] Jim: We've got a ton of people down here....the ref is still knocked out!!!! Joel: I can't imagine what's going to happen at the Pay Per View now that I see this!!! Jim: Scott Steiner is running down to the ring now, and he's not even apart of the tournament. He climbs into the ring and he spins Tatanka around...HUGE
BELLY TO BELLY!!!! Joel: Whipwreck....Piper...WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!! [Mikey gets up turns around and gets a boot to the stomach by Hunter. Hunter hits the PEDIGREE!!!]
Jim: MY GOD IT'S HELL!!! HELL HAS BROKEN LOOSE HERE!!!!!! Joel: Scott and Hunter are the only two in the ring now, and everyone is out of it. Jim: Who the hell knows where Dreamer, Richards, and Raven went! Joel: I can't believe it. This match has turned into a free for all. [All of a sudden "Born to be wild" is heard on the PA, and Cactus Jack runs out with a flaming chair.]
Jim: CACTUS JACK!!! CACTUS JACK IS HERE!!!! Joel: He's chased Hunter and Scott out of the ring!!! Jim: Cactus Jack is here, and he's not one to miss a party!!! Joel: OH WHAT THE HELL!? Jim: Steve Armstrong hit Cactus with a low blow!!!! Fans we're out of time...see you at BEACH BLAST!!!!!!!
[Camera fades, but we quickly fade back. Arn Anderson and Tully Blanchard are walking to their car in the parking lot. With out any warning Ric Flair and
Scott Hall attack both men....]
Ric: Open the trunk.... Scott: Huh? Ric: Open the damn trunk! Scott: You have the keys. Ric: WHAT? NO...YOU HAVE THEM!!! Scott: Shit. Ric: WHAT? Scott: I left them in the locker room. Ric: DAMNIT!!!! [Flair kicks the tire, and as he does that Tully and Arn take full control over both men. Arn and Tully lay into each man with lefts and rights. Tully throws
Arn a set of keys, and he walks over to an old Impala. Anderson opens up the trunk and they throw both men into it....Tully and Arn slam it shut, and walk away.]
THUMP!!! THUMP!!! THUMP!!!! Ric: DAMNIT!!!!! NOOOOOOOO!!!!! Scott: You're hurting my damn ears!!! Ric: Shutup...you'll waste our oxygen! GET US OUT OF HERE!!!!!! ANDERSON YOU'RE DEAD!!!!!! [Camera fades]
