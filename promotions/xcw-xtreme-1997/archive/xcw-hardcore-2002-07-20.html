XCW — Card

XCW Hardcore

July 20, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: Fans what a week it's been in the sport of pro-wrestling. We've had what some would call a roller coaster of a week, with so many ups and downs. Well tonight the card is HUGE....we've got the US Title on the line, the TV title on the line...what an event it's going to be.
Joel: Talk about overkill. Your voice has been ringing in my ears over and over, all this week. Do you realize how annoying your voice is? I wa -
["OH MY GOD....IT'S.....EVIL!!!!!" is heard all over the arena, and out walks
Kaientai 2.0. Jim Ross is in the lead with his red cowboy hat on with the devil horns, Sho Funaki is right behind him with the XCW World Title around his shoulder and Brock and TAKA are walking slowly behind. Once they get into the ring, Sho lifts up his title and as he lifts it....a big red puff of smoke shoots off into the air. JR gets the microphone and begins to speak...]
JR: Hell yes. HELL YES!!! Sho Funaki is your XCW World Champion, he IS the man that will lead you all into the future. Steve Armstrong couldn't handle it, and that is why this man...THE WHOLE F'N SHO....is the XCW World Champion. Nobody as skilled, nobody as cunning...nobody in the World is better than this man. So lets start with a little celebration shall we?
[Tony Katai a famous Japanese Singer comes out and shakes all the mens hands. He gets the mic and starts to sing the Japanese National Anthem. Sho waves the flag in the air....]
Tony: E to chi mah shin jo...wa pee ting ba chee...ooo su li li ta - ["Loser" by Beck starts to play, and everyone stands to their feet. Mikey
Whipwreck walks out with his TV title over his shoulder. He stops on the XCW
Walkway, and begins to talk totally cutting off Tony Katai.]
Mikey: Ya know, I've been sitting in the back for the past 3 minutes and this is enough. I can't stand listening to this crap, and I can't stand the fact that we have another Japanese Guy wearing the XCW World Title!!!!!
[Crowd pops and starts a "Mikey" chant. He stops and listens to it for a second, and Kaientai don't seem to happy.]
Mikey: Listen folks, I'm not trying to rain on your parade. Hey congrats you won the title...it's a great thing isn't it? Now I'm not out here to just talk, what I'm out here involves that XCW World Title and Annihilation.
[Crowd pops because they know what's coming.] Mikey: Sho...you and me for the XCW World Title. What do you say? JR: HEY! Who the hell do you think you are coming in here and ruining such an event? You want a piece of Sho Funaki? You want his XCW World Title? Well, then
I suggest you listen up -
Mikey: Hey JR...what's with the horns? [Crowd laughs and JR gets this look on his face] JR: What horns? Mikey: ?????? Crowd: ?????? JR: Anyway....you want Sho, then you have to have a Handicap match next week on
Hardcore. You against TAKA and Brock!!!!
Mikey: Uhh...Brock? JR: HELL YES!!!! Mikey: Umm how about we just do it right now? [Mikey throws down the mic and runs into the ring.] Joey: Mikey being extremely ballsy right now. Mikey with a boot to the stomach on JR....WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!!
Joel: Ahhh man. Joey: Kick on TAKA....WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!!! Joel: WHAT THE HELL!?!?!? Joey: Sho charges him...Mikey tosses him over the top ropes!!! Mikey spins around, he kicks Tony Katai!!!!! Tony Katai!!! WELCOME TO THE XCW!!!!
WHIPPERSNAPPER!!!!! [Mikey gets up and works the crowd, but he forgot about big Brock who was just standing there. Mikey takes the fans advice and turns around, and Brock is walking towards him.]
Joey: Mikey with a kick...WHIPPERSNA - NO!!! NO!!! Brock's body didn't even go down...he broke out of the Whippersnapper using his strength.
Joel: WHOOO!!!! Would ya look at that! Joey: Mikey gets up and Brock grabs him..F5!!! F5!!! F5!!! Joel: OH YEAH!!!!! Joey: Brock stands over the knocked out Mikey, and Sho climbs back in and drapes the Japanese Flag over him. Kanetai leave Mikey in the ring, and I'm sure...I am so sure that Mikey isn't done just yet.
Joel: Did you see that though? Brock didn't even budge...the man is just a beast. He's not human.
Joey: Well fans as Mikey is being helped back to the locker room. Lets take you to our first match.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen , making his way to the ring, accompanied by Minister James Mitchell, he weighs in at 310 pounds, he is MALICE! [He comes down to a big amount of heat.] FINK: And his opponent for this evening, from the Red Hook section of Brooklyn, the "Master of the Suplex" TAZ!
[Taz walks down to the ring.] Joey: Malice is a big big man.... Joel: I agree. There's no way on Earth that Taz is going to be able to overcome this mountain. I mean the man's head is bigger than Taz's body, and I don't mean the one on his shoulder baby!
Joey: You're sick. [Both men lock up, and Taz with go-behind. He goes for a German Suplex, but he can't get the big man up. Malice grabs Taz's lock, and kicks him between the legs.]
Joel: Speaking of head. Joey: Taz doubles over, and Malice grabs him and throws him down to the ground hard. The man can't be messed with...he grabs him, Atomic Throw...and Taz lands right on his backside.
Joel: I think we should let everyone know that Malice used to be The Wall. A lot of people have been asking that question, and I don't like talking to people...so there's the answer.
Joey: Taz gets up again, and Malice drives a boot into his stomach. He whips him into the ropes, and Malice gets reversed. Taz goes for a clothesline, but it only staggers the big man.
Joel: WHOA and another one... Joey: Taz with a third clothesline, and he grabs him...T-Bone Ta - NO!!! Malice with a forearm to the side of the head, and another one. He backs Taz into the corner, and he's choking him out with a boot.
Joel: His leg is bigger than Taz's whole body. [Malice finally lets go after the 4 count, and then drives an elbow to the side of the head. Malice whips him into the turnbuckle, and Malice follows up with a big splash.]
Joey: Taz is a walking pancake now...and Malice lifts him up onto the top turnbuckle. Malice grabs him by the throat...SUPER CHOKESLAM!!!!!!
Joel: Did you see the air that Taz caught? Joey: Malice is going for the cover...ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...Malice!!!! Joel: What a beast!!! Joey: Malice is standing over Taz, and I have never seen anyone man handle him like that before. Look out for this man...
Joel: Malice is a pure monster. [Camera cuts to Steve Armstrong packing up his stuff. Kenta comes in and he's questioning...]
Steve: Listen...I just patched things up with Brad. The time is not right to comeback to the ring, and I'm leaving.
Kenta: WHAT!? Steve: The XCW is not as important as my family, and Brad and I need to go spend some time together. Kenta, you need to understand. You need to know that this is personal, all business aside. I've proved myself...and I'm out. Steve
Armstrong is leaving the XCW...and Brad is coming with me. When and if I come back...it's going to be something the XCW will never expect. So take care...and
I'll see you soon.
Kenta: WAIT...I'm leaving too. Steve:.......well if you want. [Brad walks in..] Brad: You ready buddy? Steve: Yeah lets go. [Steve grabs his stuff, and all three men walk out the door. So long Steve, thanks for the memories. Camera fades to a commercial.]
