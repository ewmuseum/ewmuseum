XCW Trust No One (part 5)

July 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joey: Fans, I'm being told that after tonight's event you can log onto XCW.com for interesting statistics on the match we just had, and in addition to a complete transcript of tonight's event, and the news on Wednesday Night
Hardcore. Jesse: I'm enjoying myself now... Joey: Good lets talk about the next match!! For that past four weeks, Vader and
Brock have been at each others throats. These two monsters, these two specimens will battle in a brutal match.
Jesse: Vader is the more experiences, but Brock is out to prove himself. He wants to show the world that he is the monster in this fed, and that he is going to be the man that will lead the XCW into the future.
Joey: No doubt about that....fans lets take you to the ring Introductions for this HUGE match.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen this is a special grudge match. First coming down to the ring, from Boulder, CO. He weighs in at 360 pounds...here is The Man They
Call....VADER!!!
[Vader walks down to no music, but a huge face pop.] FINK: And his opponent...led down to the ring by Jim Ross. He is known as "The
Red Headed Monster" here is Brock Lesnar!!!!
["Snap your fingers, Snap your neck" starts to play and Brock comes out to a tremendous amount of pyro. He climbs into the ring, and the fans boo him like mad.]
Joey: And JR is giving instructions to Brock as he stands there snarling. I'm sure he is not listening to what he's saying, he just wants to go at it.
Jesse: And Vader is on the ring now, and he's standing face to face with Brock. Joey: What's this? JR is sneaking up behind Vader, Vader turns and he's got Jim by the throat...Brock motions to take out Vader, but he relases Jim and nails
Brock with a right hand.
Jesse: There's the bell, and this one is underway. [Vader continues to unload on Brock, backing him up into the ropes and sending him across with an Irish Whip. Brock hits hard chest first, and comes out dazed, and Vader kicks him in the stomach...and goes for the Power Bomb, but
Brock escapes to the floor.]
Joey: Brock and JR regroup on the outside, and it appears they're forming another plan. Jesse: And Vader is going right after him. Joey: Vader to the outside, and he grabs Brock by the head. Brock puts on the breaks, and he throws Vader into the guardrail head first. Oh man!!! Vader is up and Brock clotheslines him out and over into the fans!!!!
Jesse: Vader looks to have hit his head pretty hard, he's already busted open. Joey: Blood is pouring out profusely from the forehead of Vader. Here comes
Earl Hebner to check on Vader.
Jesse: And look at the look on JR's face...the man is happy as pigs in shit. He wants to see Vader get busted open, and that's what he's getting.
Joey: JR giving Brock instructions again, but he stops him. Jim reaches underneath the ring...what's he looking for?
Jesse: A lead pipe!!! [JR hands the pipe to Brock, and he tells him to finish Vader off.] Joey: Brock leaps over the guardrail with amazing agility, and he pushes a fan aside!!!! Oh what is that all about???? He pushes Hebner out of the way, and he's about to unleash hell on Vader.
Jesse: LOW BLOW!!!! Low blow by Vader, and he grabs the lead pipe. Joey: He's about to whale on h....NO Hebner takes the pipe away. Oh and Vader doesn't look to happy.
Jesse: Earl had better run... [Despite being hurt, Brock rises to his feet again and locks in a Full Nelson from behind. Vader falls back though, and both men fall back onto the steel guardrail and it falls down.]
Joey: Both men crashed through the steel mesh that seperates the fans from the ringside area.
Jesse: I don't know who got the worse of it, but the blood continues to pour down the face of the man they call Vader.
Joey: Ross now heading into the crowd to get the lead pipe, while the ref exits the crowd to check on both men.
Jesse: I don't even think the bell has rung to start this match. Joey: I believe it has. Jim grabs the pipe from Earl Hebner, and he rushes over to Vader who is climbing the ring steps. He's trying to get back into the ring.
Brock is up now, and JR hands him the pipe....BUT HE DOESN'T WANT IT!!! Jesse: He's got something else in mind. [Brock climbs up behind Vader, and he locks in a sleeper hold. Vader won't have any of that though, and instead he flips Brock into the ring. Vader stands on the ring apron, and throws off his mask before playing to the crowd by raising his hands. Vader lets loose witha loud yell of fury.]
Joey: Vader shouts in anger, and this pl - OH MY GOD!!! Brock charges Vader, and he goes flying down onto the cement floor.
Jesse: Did you hear the sickening thud? Joey: Brock actually speared Vader through that middle rope and both men went crashing down to the canvas. Both men aren't moving, and Jim comes over to check on his man.
Jesse: And the ref begins to lay the count on.....1....2...3....4....5...6. Joey: And now signs of life being shown by Brock. JR helps him up to his feet to break the count at eight. Brock now grabs Vader's lifeless body and rolls it into the ring!
Jesse: Brock with the cover!!!!!! Joey: ONE!!! TWO!!! NO!!! Vader kicks out... Jesse: And JR is livid. He's telling Bro - oh he's going to piledrive him. Joey: Brock nods and brings Vader up, and he forearms him four times right on the side of the face. He hooks him up...PILEDRIVER. There's the cover once again, One!!! Two!!! Th - and a lift of the shoulder prevents a three count.
Jesse: With a bruised body, a bloody face, Vader is still in this thing. [Brock is up now and he brings Vader to his feet....Vader stands there in a haze daze. Brock lifts him up and hits a MASSIVE F5.]
Joey: This is it....this is all she wrote. ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NO!!! I can't believe it. Vader has just kicked out of the F5!!!!
Jesse: Listen to this place on their feet here tonight. Joey: Vader has kicked out of the F5, and JR is jumping up and down in anger. [Brock is confused, but now he and JR are talking, and Brock is nodding. Brock actually smiles a sick, disgusting smile...that has EVIL written all over it.]
Joey: Brock grabs Vader's limp body, and he brings him over to the corner. Brock unloads with several elbows to the side of the head, before resting the dazed Vader against the turnbuckle....
Jesse: And what's this??? Joey: JR is in the ring now, and he's keep Vader on that turnbucke so he doesn't fall down. He's pushing with all his might...
Jesse: Brock is on the top rope... Joey: Wait - Vader reaches back and elbows the turnbuckle, and Brock crotches himself!!!!! Vader has got JR!!!! He's got JR, and he's going to drop him right here, right now....
Jesse: I can't even hear myself think!!!! Joey: Brock is on the top rope though.....Vader turns, BROCK WITH A MOONSAULT BODYPRESS!!!!!!!!!! Jesse: WHOA!!!!!!! Joey: That huge man just jumped off the top turnbuckle like it was nothing. There's the cover....ONE!!! TWO!!!! NO!!! NO NO NO NO NO!!!!!! Vader has kicked out once again, and Brock is bright red.
Jesse: He's telling JR to get that pipe!!! [Brock brings Vader up and tosses him through the middle rope. Vader lands on his feet by JR, and Ross takes a swing with the pipe...Vader ducks and JR hits the ringpost instead.]
Joey: Brock is on the apron...he gets a running start....FLYING LARIAT OFF THE APRON!!!!! Jesse: OH!!! Vader pushes JR IN THE WAY THOUGH!!!!! Joey: Jim goes down hard!!!! I think Jim lost his neck on that one!!! [ Vader throws Brock back into the ring, and Vader clinbs in. ] Joey: Vader lifts him up...CROSSARM POWERBOMB!!!!! This could be the win for
Vader!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! NO - he got his foot on the ropes.
Jesse: And there we get a good shot off Jim Ross on the floor, he's out but he's not dead. So that still means trouble for Vader.
Joey: The blood has become a crimson mask for Vader now, and he's plotting his next move. [Vader whips him into the ropes, but Brock reverses it. vader returns, and
Brock goes for another sleeper, but Vader goes behind him - ]
Joey: RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX!!!!!!! Brock crashes down to the canvas, and Vader crawls over to make the cover....ONE!!! TWO!!! HE GOT HIM - no he didn't!
Jesse: JR pulled the ref to the floor, and they're arguing now. Joey: Here comes Vader!!! He grabs JR by the jacket again, and Ross is stunned. Hit him!!! Hit him!!!! Jesse: Brock is getting back up! [Vader pushes JR down, and Vader climbs back into the ring. As Brock gets up
Vader meets him with a series of swinging punches, and down goes Brock.]
Joey: Vader with the cover - wait he's stopped. He gets up and he stomps away on Brock, and now he's pointing to the top turnbuckle. Oh my God...is he going to execute the Vader-sault?
Jesse: He could....he's going to the top ropes. Joey: Vader from the top turnbuckle...MOONSAULT!!!!!!!! [As Vader flips over...you can see flashes from every camera in the arena go off. Vader lands it perfectly, and he hooks the leg.]
Joey: ONE!!! TWO!! NO!!! BROCK KICKS OUT!!!! Jesse: I can't believe it. Joey: Vader slams his hands down on the mat in anger, and he gets up. Vader runs to the rope - OH MY GOD!!!! Damnit Jim Ross....JR just smashed that led pipe into the head of Vader.
Jesse: A shot right to the back of the head. Joey: The ref didn't see it though, he must think Vader passed out from the loss of blood. Brock is up once again, and he brings Vader to his feet....he's got him in a Fireman's Carry....OH and he turns it into a Gutbuster!!!!!
Jesse: You realize the power he has to have in order to execute that move??? Joey: Brock goes for the cover once again....ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! Jesse: And Brock wins this match!!!! HE wins here tonight!!!! Joey: I don't believe this...Brock with the help of Jim Ross has won an incredible battle here tonight.
Jesse: JR is jumping for joy, and Brock stands over the knocked out Vader. Joey: Well a win like this certainly puts him on the path for XCW World Title contention. FINK: Here is your winner....BROCK LESNAR!!!!! [JR jumps into Brocks arms, and he catches him with one arm. Both men walk back to the ring as the EMT's check on Vader...]
