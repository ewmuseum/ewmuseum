XCW Trust No One (part 8)

July 15, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Epic" by Faith No More starts to play and Chad Costello walks out. He's rolling out a huge Birthday cake himself, and inside the ring the crew is setting up a table. Chad climbs into the ring, and the crew helps put the cake into the ring. As they set it up on the table, Chad grabs the microphone...]
Chad: Yes yes...I know. Wednesday I was supposed to have a Birthday but it was ruined, so I figured tonight...heh, tonight I celebrate with millions of people watching at home.
["Asshole" chant starts.] Chad: What a Pay Per View huh? Well can't say that I've been watching, I'm reading the new Kate Daniels novel. Oh yeah, I forgot...you people don't know how to read. Anyway Magnum TA has told me that the Pay Per View is going well, as it should because I pay big money for these losers to put on a good show.
[Crowd boos to that.] Chad: Now where was I? Oh yeah...HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO ME!!! HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO ME!!! HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO C - ["N....W.....O" is heard all over the arena, and the crowd pops like mad. The screen turns to black and white, and out walks Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, and
Hollywood Hogan. The place erupts with an ovation louder than anything we've seen or heard tonight. All three men slowly walk down to the ring, and climb in. We get back to a regular picture as Kevin Nash gets on the microphone.]
Kevin: Happy Birthday to Chad huh? Happy Birthday to Ch - DON'T CHEER US. [Crowd stops cheering...] Kevin: Before I go on any further let me get this out. We're not out here to make the fans happy, we're not out here to make money. Everyone in this ring has enough money to go around for 3 lifetimes. What we're out here for is to spice things up, to breathe a little life into the XCW...if ya know what I mean. So Costello...like or not the NWO is in the XCW.
Chad: Uhhh listen I don't want any trouble. Kevin: TROUBLE? HAHA...this isn't trouble. THIS IS HELL! [Chad looks scared out of his mind as Nash towers over him.] Chad: What do you want? [Kevin hands the microphone to Hollywood.] Hollywood Hogan: Ya know somethin' brother....I've watched you run this fed with an Iron fist for the past few years man. I've seen you strike down the best, and elevate the worst. You call Steve Armstrong a champion? You're looking at three men who could beat that guy in his sleep. All three of us came to you a week ago asking if you wanted our help with your sagging ratings.....and you deny us? You tell us no? Well it looks like that did a whole lot of good, because we're standing right in front of you. We're standing right here face to face....and we're walking out in one piece. You're not.
Chad: Wait...wait...what do you guys want? You want to be apart of the Main
Event? I can make it a three way dance Hogan, I have the power to do that.
[Hogan stops for a second, and rubs his beard.] Hollywood: Keep talking. Chad: Well...ah...I can...well you see I have the power to make any match here in the XCW. So why don't I just throw you in the Main Event?
[All three men look at each other....] Hollywood: NAH... [Kevin kicks Chad Costello in the stomach, and Jacknife Powerbombs him onto his own Birthday cake through the table. Costello is knocked out cold, but Hogan goes to the ropes and drops a leg across the throat. Kevin grabs the mic...]
Kevin: Enjoy this Pay Per View folks, because this will be the last one that the NWO isn't in for a looooong time.
[He throws the mic down, and the music starts up again. All three men walk away to a huge heel response.]
Joey: Can you believe what I just saw?? Jesse: I can't believe the NWO has stuck their nose into XCW business. Who the hell do they think they are?
Joey: Folks this can't be good for business. Hollywood Hulk Hogan, Kevin Nash, and Scott Hall....ugh. As we're talking about this we get a shot of Chad
Costello who is still lying knocked out on his Birthday Cake....fans while we get things up lets go to the history between Sho Funaki and Steve Armstrong....
[Camera fades to Beach Blast '02...] Jim: He's almost at the top, and Sho is starting to wake up. He's pushing the ladder off of him, and Steve it there.
[Steve grabs the loose chair and hovers over the hole in the roof. Sho look up and Steve...]
Jim: Suicide Dive with the chair!!!! Jesse: DID YOU HEAR THAT CRACK???? Jim: I have never heard anything so disgusting in my life. Steve Armstrong landed on that knee, and he's clutching it.
[Sho has a river of blood pouring out of his head, and Steve crawls over to make the cover....]
Jim: 1!!! 2!!!! 3!!!!! Jesse: HE GOT HIM!!!!! FINK: Here is your winner...and NEW XCW WORLD CHAMPION...STEVE ARMSTRONG!!!!! Voice Over: A man...an underdog. Someone many people would call a nothing, became something on June 16th 2002.
[We get a shot of Steve Armstrong sitting in a chair being interviewed...] Steve: Up until that day, all I ever heard about was the curse. Your family is cursed, you're a bunch of losers. Ya know, it was heart breaking. Then....then when I went through 3 other men, and I won that XCW World Title things changed for me. I became somebody that night...I became the XCW World Champion. Who would've thought that so many years ago, and I would be a World Champ? Not me...
[Camera cuts to the following Hardcore after the PPV 6/19/02] Chad: Now Steve, you're probably wondering where you go from here. Obviously you go onto "Trust No One", but who are you going to face? Well, since I thought it was such a great match at the Pay Per View...and since you two wrestled each other after 2 other wrestlers had a piece...why don't we throw you into the ring one more time against SHO FUNAKI??? "Last Man Standing"
match.... [The words "Last Man Standing" echos, and we see another shot of Sho and Steve lying on the ground...and then we get a shot of Steve Armstrong knocked out from last week...he's got the Japanese Flag over his body. Sho begins to be interviewed through an interpreter]
Sho: Haguchi Masao Tuka Ro Fookai Interpreter: Nothing phases me, nothing at all. Steve Armstrong can come at me as much as he wants, but I will win on Sunday. I fear him none...
Sho: Karurura sasaki toru koaki bukkake... Interpreter: Japan will be victorious, Kaientai will be taken seriously, and
EVIL will reign supreme. INDEEEEED!!!!
[Camera fades back to the sold out crowd at the Palace]
