XCW Monday Night Malice (part 6)

January 28, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Fans what a match up this is going to be. Shane Douglas has skipped out on the XCW, and tonight either Vader or Mikey Whipwreck will walk away with the
World Title.
Jesse: You can see the cage lowering, and man...I'd hate to be in Mikey's shoes here tonight.
FINK: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is the MAIN EVENT. This is for the XCW World Title. First coming down to the ring, from New York City, NY. He weighs in at 230 pounds. Here is Mikey Whipwreck!!!
<"Loser" by Beck starts up, and the fans are going wild. He comes out with a horrible scared look on his face. He looks at the cage, and then decides he's not going to go in, but he's going to climb the cage...he gets halfway up, but can't make it the rest of the way and falls back down. Although on the way down he twists he knee....he falls to the ground holding his knee in pain.>
[A consistent, haunting keyboard piece, something you might hear in a horror movie during the killing scenes, begins to repeat over and over again. This lasts several seconds, until the guitars chug in, and Mushroomhead's "Chancre
Sore" begins to blast over the arena speakers. With the guitars cut in, Paul
E. Dangerously emerges from backstage, and with him, the monster himself. A large metal mask, shaped like a Mastodon's head, covers the face of DA MAN~!, and billows smoke from either horn. His arms reached out, his fists clenched,
"Big" Van Vader begins to shuffle back and forth from side to side, intimidating the crowd and causing his opponent, already in the ring, to stare on in shock and fear.]
FINK: And now coming to the ring, representing the Dangerous Alliance Version
2.0, he weighs in tonight at FOUR HUNDRED and NINETEEN POUNDS. Hailing from
Boulder, Colorado. He is "THE MASTODON"! He is "THE MAN"! He is... "BIG"...
VAN... VAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAADER~! [Whether the fans love him or hate him, they damn sure respect him, and show him such by a hearty round of applause. He walks up to Mikey without taking a breath, and tosses him into the cage through the door.]
Jim: And poor Mikey took a nasty fall, and Vader is going to take advantage of that. Jesse: Why the hell did he try to climb up there in the first place? <Vader grabs Mikey, but Mikey wiggles out and runs to the ropes. He bounces off and Vader catches him with a HUGE lariat. Mikey falls helplessly down to the ground, and Vader lifts him up again for another short clothesline.>
Jim: Oh my God...Mikey is already damned near dead. Jesse: Vader might as well pin him now! <Vader with a turnbuckle smash, and another turnbuckle smash....Vader whips him around and goes for a chokeslam, but Mikey counters with a kick to the groin.>
Jim: And Mikey is fighting back....Mikey with a left hand, and another left hand. He sends Vader to the ropes, and Vader reverses it. Mikey slams into the turnbuckle hard, and Vader follows in with a HUGE Vader Splash!!!
Jesse: Pancake....he's a pancake now. Jim: What a move there by Vader, and Mikey was starting to get a LITTLE bit of offense going. Wouldn't that be an amazing upset if we saw Mikey walk away with the XCW World Title here tonight.
Jesse: Then I'd have to wake up and have breakfast, and live this card all over again! Jim: Oh Vader with a nasty headbutt, and Mikey falls down to his knees. Vader now with a series of lefts and rights...and Mikey goes down hard.
Jesse: Vader has those punches, and he can dent a Buick with those. Mikey needs to block those, get his hands up....or SOMETHING.
<Vader lifts him up and slams him down to the mat quickly. Vader then jumps up and hits a Vader Splash.....>
Jim: 1!!! 2!!! Only a two count there, and Mikey got his shoulder up. Vader lifts him up and throws him into the ropes. Mikey bounces off, and Vader with a SKY HIGH
BACKDROP, and Mikey hits hard!!! Jesse: Did you see how high he went? <Vader walks around with his fingers in the shape of a "V"....Mikey makes it up and Vader hits him once and he falls. Mikey gets up again, and Vader knocks him down once more. Mikey gets up again, and Vader hits him with a horrid jab to the face and Mikey falls down like a ton of bricks.>
Jim: He's gonna put Mikey out of wrestling tonight. Vader is going to put Mikey on the shelf for good, and then he's going to rule the XCW.
Jesse: Well Mikey signed the match with him.... Jim: Not Hell In The Cell damnit!!! <Vader grabs Mikey and throws him face first into the cage, and when Mikey bounces off...Vader hits him with a clothesline to the back of the head.>
Jim: OH MY GOD!!!! Jesse: HA man!!! Jim: I hope Mikey's momma isn't watching this at home. If you are turn it off, because I'm sure as hell it's going to get worse. It's going to get a whole lot worse, because Vader wants to tear him from limb to limb. He wants to take
Mikey out, and make sure we don't see him comeback!
Jesse: He's doing a damn good job of it. <Vader lifts Mikey up into a bearhug, and throws Mikey into the cage....again...and again...and again....and again....and again...and again.
Vader them drops him down to the canvas, and walks around once more.> Jim: Vader isn't about to stop this match. He's having too much fun here, and
Mikey isn't going to fight back. He's tried that and look where it got him.
Jesse: Mikeys back is blood red. You can see the cage imprints on his back...but HE'S GETTING UP!!!
Jim: That damn young kid!!!! <Vader turns around and see's Mikey getting back up. Vader goes for a swinging punch, but Mikey ducks it...Mikey comesback with a kick to the groin and another kick to the groin....he dropkicks Vader and Vader falls against the ropes and gets caught up in them!!!>
Jim: VADER IS CAUGHT IN THE ROPES!!!! Jesse: Yeah but Mikey is hurting from that dropkick he gave him. Jim: Mikey is trying to make it to his feet, and he walks over to Vader...and he's slapping him. He's bitch slapping Vader, and look at Vader....he is angry.
Mikey is flipping him off, and slapping him in the face!! Jesse: Oh man!! That's like sticking your hand in a bee hive...he's testing death! Jim: Mikey now fires in with right after right, but Vader breaks free and grabs
Mikey by the throat.....he has Mikey by the throat. CHOKESLAM!!!!!!
Jesse: That sounded like a bomb hitting the ground! <Vader brings Mikey back up and starts punching him in the nose as hard as he can, and Mikey's nose almost pops. Blood is pouring out every which way, and
Vader isn't stopping.>
Jim: STOP HIM!!! DAMNIT TO HELL STOP HIM!!!! Jesse: Yeah this isn't right...he can finish the match whenever he wants, and he needs to stop this.
Jim: God, someone get down there and do something about that. <Vader finally gets off of him and goes to the outside of the cage. He reaches under the ring and grabs a chair. Vader climbs back into the ring with the chair, and smashes it across Mikey's face....not once....not twice...not three times...but four times!!!>
Jesse: Okay...this is enough. You're not a man if you continue this kind of attack. You're a man if you stop it right now, and you let Mikey live.
Jim: You're damn right Jesse... Jesse: Where are the officials? Where is Jack Black? Jim: He's looking for another commish...he doesn't want the job. <Vader throws the chair down onto the ground and picks up Mikey for the Power
Bomb. Vader lifts Mikey up and POWER BOMBS him onto the chair. Vader smiles and he looks at Paul E...who is telling him to do it again.>
Jim: NO NO NO!!!! AWE GAWD!!!!! Jesse: Somebody better get down there. Jim: 2 Powerbombs from Vader, and now he's going for the cover. 1!!! 2!!!! 3!!!! FINK: Here is your winner, and NEW XCW World Champion. Vader!!!! Jim: Vader has won the title here tonight, and he is going to rule the XCW. Who's going to stop this man? Jesse: I don't know, but what a night it was. Jim: We had some classic battles here tonight, and this Friday is looking to be one of the best X-Treme's we've ever had. Fans...I'm Jim Ross saying SO LONG!!!
