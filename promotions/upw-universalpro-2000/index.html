A November 2000 newsgroup promotion whose only produced card, Christmas Chaos, built toward a December pay-per-view before its president closed the operation within the month. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Universal Pro Wrestling introduced itself to the newsgroup in early November 2000 with a run of recruitment posts seeking wrestlers and a web designer, followed on the twentieth by a staged press conference at a Los Angeles hotel where its president billed a Monday night broadcast for Fox Sports and unveiled a commissioner, two commentators, a head referee and a head of security.

Its own application form asked for original characters only, barring real-wrestler replicas outright, and its promoter described an e-mail based model built on members sending in material for him to compile and send out in batches rather than a message-board operation.

On the twenty-ninth of November the promotion aired its only surviving broadcast, Christmas Chaos, posted in four parts: an opening tag squash, a triple threat contested for an early berth in an eight-man battle royal, a first blood match that turned into a bloody worked shoot, and a video teasing a December pay-per-view under the name Jingle Brawl.

No further broadcast survives. A community reply posted less than two weeks later reported that the president had already closed the operation the previous week, leaving the teased pay-per-view unaired and the promotion's run at a single card.
