UPW Card Archive

Press Conference

20 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera fades onto the Universal Sheraton in Studio City, California. All different kinds of press are standing by waiting for the big announcement from
Joe the President and CEO of the UPW. On the stage is a podium, and a table on each side. Each table has 3 chairs on it, and behind the stage is a HUGE "UPW"
logo. We cut to Sam Ruben who is a reporter for Channel 5 news here in Southern
California.]
Sam: Okay I'm standing here with my fellow reporters and we're waiting on the appearance of the man they call Joe. This guy has been hyping his UPW for sometime now, and we're all looking forward to what he has to say. Some speculate that he's going to be talking about the upcoming event, and while others have mentioned that he might just talk about his staff. Either way it's going to be interesting. He should be here any minute now....and just as I say that here he is.
[Joe along with what looks to be other members of the UPW walk up onto the stage. All the staff take their seats and Joe steps up to address the crowd.
Joe's stands at about 6'0, and is medium build. The wrestlers and press know him for his wild hair which is different almost every time he's in front of the camera. Today due to the Thanksgiving Holiday...he's got dark brown hair, with light brown and yellow tips on his spikey hair. Although don't mistake him for being a punk...that's the only wild thing on him at the moment. Today he's wearing a Navy Blue suit, and a pair of $500 shoes. Joe smiles and begins to speak....]
Joe: First of all I want to thank each and every one of you for coming, and showing your support. Universal Pro-Wrestling is going to kick off today and it won't be touching the ground for a very long time. Today we embark on a journey that is going to last for many months, and years. Though those years we're going to have action, adventure, drama, and comedy. This isn't going to be like any other fed that you've been in, and that's a promise from me to you.
(Crowd applauds with approval.) Joe: Now to the left and right of me you've got the people who are going to make the UPW run. You've got the Commish, the commentators, the head ref, and we've even got the head of security up here. They're going to introduce themselves, and after they are done I'm going to talk about the huge event that will be taking place very soon. Now will you all please put your hands together for the Commish....Phillip Marteen.
(Phillip stands up and towers over Joe. This guy looks like he's straight out of the Hell's Angles. He's got a bandana around his head, dark sun glasses, a ripped up Harley T-shirt, tight jeans, and a pair of leather boots with silver buckles on them. He looks like a REAL American Bad Ass. )
Phillip: Yo! Heh...first of all I want to tell you's all a little sutin'. You people may tink that you've got it all figured out, but you ain't seen shit. Ya got all these otha commeeshes walkin' around these feds. Ya got your Mick
Foleys, you got your weak little white guys from WCW, and then ya even got
Spike Dudley from the ECW. What the (bleep) is dis? I mean come on...ya need someone who's going to be able to kick some ass in dat ring. I'm the guy to do it. Any questions?
Reporter #1: Are you sure that you're qualified to run the UPW? You don't seem to have the education to do so.
Phillip: Excuse me? What the hell did you just say? Do you's want me to knock ya into next week? The fact is this...I graduated from M.I.T. with a degree in
Mathematical Engineering. I can show you da diploma if you's like me to. I ain't stupid...and I'm not one to mess with. I made a damn good livin' befoa I took dis job right here's. I'm the real damn deal, and I have a lot of ideas to make dis fed one of the best. Joe hired me, and everyone who's workin' for us has faith in me. Ya got it?
Reporter #1: Yes. Phillip: Now if you'll excuse me...I gotta go for a smoke. [Phillip jumps off the stage and walks to the back of the room. As he does that he bumps right into the reporter and he falls down onto the floor. Phillip stops...looks and him and simply says "OOPS!" and gives a big laugh as he strolls out.]
Joe: Heh...he's a real character isn't he? Okay next up...I would like to introduce to you the commentators for Rage & Warfare. That's going to be our
Monday Night Show which is going to be aired on FOX Sports, and you can catch it at 9 pm....any time! Here's Mark Anderson, and Jake Davids....
[Mark steps up with a huge smile on his face. He looks like the boy next store with a cardigan sweater on, and a pair of khaki pants on. The guy is all of 25 years old, and he's got a huge smile on his almost model looking face. Jake
Davids on the other hand is a former pro wrestler. Davids stands about 6'3, and has long flowing golden locks. He's got on a UPW t-shirt, and a pair of jeans...]
Mark: Thank you all for coming. My name is Mark Anderson, and I'm going to be doing commentary for Rage and Warfare. I've previously done commentary for
Rollerball on the Hick Network, and I've also done Pop Warner Football for
Community Access Television here in LA. Umm..I guess I'll give it over to Jake now.
[Jake steps up and just smiles. All the reporters go crazy for him as they all know him from his past glory days. He works the crowd a little bit, and then he begins to speak in that deep and controlling voice that he has....]
Jake: So who brought the beer huh? You know when Joe came to me and asked me to be apart of his little tea party I was pretty reluctant. You see I've known Joe since he was just a little boy. Ya he used to come to my matches and cheer me on, he used to hold up signs...he was a real mark. Well, he still is a real mark, but who isn't a Mark of Jake Davids? So I thought about it long and hard, and then when he told me how much I would be makin'....I didn't have to think anymore. Mark and I are going to make these cards the most entertaining cards you've ever heard. I know everything there is to know about wrestling, and he knows absolutely nothing...so we're going to be a great team. Thanks! Peace!!!!
[Mark and Jake step down and they don't answer any questions going their way. Joe steps back up and this time he introduces the Head Ref...Jeff Anderson. Jeff looks totally nervous and doesn't even know where to begin...sweat drips down his forehead and down his nose onto the microphone. You can hear the drop all over the crowd....pretty disgusting.]
Jeff: Uhh...umm...well thank you. I really wasn't prepared to come up here and speak. I am never prepared to speak though. HAHAHA...heh....umm...okay well what I'm here today for...today for? Does that make any sense at all?
Wait...are we live? Oh my gosh...oh my gosh...this isn't good. I hope my mother isn't watching. Well I...umm just want to say that Mark and I aren't related at all. Thank you!
[Jeff steps down and gives Joe a HUGE hug. Joe waits as Jeff just won't let go of him...finally Brian Gallgher who is head of security breaks the whole thing up. Joe finally gets back onto the microphone, and begins to speak...]
Joe: Now as of right now we only have 5 MEMBERS. We're currently searching for more, and when we find them this fed is going to quick off a great beginning.
I'll get my Christmas gift early if I can get 5 members before next week. Until then I cannot speak on the Mega-Card, but I will tell you that's it's going to be one of the best you've ever witnessed. I can also tell you it's going to be free, and televised on FOX Sports. Thank you very much, and I'll answer questions at another time.
[Joe steps down from the podium and walks away to a million questions. Everyone with the exception of of Phillip walk away to the back. Phillip is no where to be found. Sam Ruben finishes up by saying...]
Sam: Well there you have it. This is going to be a wild, wild ride...and I will report more on the UPW as information becomes available. Thank you very much, and I'll see you all very soon!
[Camera fades.]
