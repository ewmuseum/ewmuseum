Time to Spread Christmas Fear

22 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[All is quiet on a cold winters night....the sound of soft Christmas music is in the background, snow is falling, the animals have all gone to sleep, kids are tucked in bed. Presents are nicely wrapped all around the Christmas tree,
Santa's cookies are laid out for him, and there's even a carrot for Rudolph.
Tonight is the night many kids have been waiting for all year so they have gone to bed early. Not a sound, not even a peep and then...all of a sudden the music stops...the earth starts to shake, the kids wake in a fit of rage, animals scurry for shelter....FLAMES start to emerge from the ground, the sky turns pitch black, and the wind rages like you've never seen before.]
Voice Over: The time has come....no more Yule tide cheer, no more Christmas joy, no more happiness....not even visions of Sugar Plum Ferries. Instead there are visions of torment, torture...pain and destruction. Blood, pain, defeat, and victory.
For this is Jingle Brawl....an event that will change the face of Wrestling as we all know it. Forget everything you know, forget everything you've heard, forget everything you are....for the time has come for an event so huge, so violent, so intense that even the wrestlers are having second thoughts.
Never again will you see 8 men battle it out so hard for one thing. The right to be called the Universal Pro Wrestling World Champion. The right to be called the best, and to have the respect they so rightfully deserve. Everything is out the window December 9th....no friends, just enimies....
[In a quick series of 1 second shots it shows Ivan Kage smile as blood trickles down his face, Steve Greedy screaming in pain and pleasure, Death Master powerbombing someone off the ring apron onto steel steps, Thirty Below and The
Enigma having a stare down like no other...and in the background of all this there is a picture of a shadow. Who is this mystery? In another series of rapid shots to the camera it shows random acts of violence and Natural Disasters such as the Hindenberg falling out of the sky, bombs going off, a man being shot in the head...and then it stops and this appears on the screen.......]
It's not just about winning...it's about surviving.
