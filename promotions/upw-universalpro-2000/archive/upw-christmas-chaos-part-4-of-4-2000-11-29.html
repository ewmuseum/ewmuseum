A few paces in he stops, lifts his head and stares at the crowd in attendance. The size of the crowd must shock him, as he takes a couple uneasy steps backwards but quickly regains his composure and continues his walk to the ring. About midway to his destination, he stops again to admire a fan standing to his left. The fan yells and screams and makes flailing arm motions all around him, just about nailing a smaller, younger and visibly more mature fan than himself. The Enigma turns to face him and stands motionless before him in awe of the wild action on the opposite side of the fence. Visibly amused, the Enigma leans back slightly, extends his arm, points at the fan and mimics a shooting motion with his hand.
The fan reacts by returning the motion and yelling something at the Enigma which causes him to laugh. Turning back towards the ring the Enigma finishes his walk, enters it and stands before his opponent ready for the match to begin."]
