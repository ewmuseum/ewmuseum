The Last Ad

24 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all I'd be an idiot if I didn't mention something. Why is Nikki Cox married to a fat guy on her new TV show? I mean come on now...I'm sure they could've found someone a MILLION times better than that guy. Did you know in real life she's married to Bobcat Goldwaith or whatever his name is. Ugh!!!
------------------------------------- Now onto the Ad. Most of you have seen the ads for UPW already. A lot of you are joining, and it's going to be one hell of a fed. You're going to get everything you want and more, and you're even going to get a card BEFORE the PPV. Oooo. Anyway look if you're gonna go hunting for a fed this holiday season don't make it some fed filled with marks, and elves.
Make it the UPW.... WHY JOE? Because the UPW isn't full of shit. We're not going to jerk you around with crappy angles, two bit gimmicks, and lame cards. We're not going to be late, and I can promise you that on Christmas morning you're not going to race downstairs to see the gifts....YOU will race down to see what happened December
25th with the UPW.
Okay well maybe not that exciting, but we're going to be that close. I have the enough, and all you have to do is E-Mail or IM me! - Joe
