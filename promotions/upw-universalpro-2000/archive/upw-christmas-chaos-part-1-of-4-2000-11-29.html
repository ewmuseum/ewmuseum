Christmas Chaos (Part 1 of 4)

29 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades onto a SOLD OUT crowd at the Staple Center. Fans are going wild as this is the very first UPW event to be held. Signs are being held up everywhere, and the camera shows what some of the signs say...."The Enigma
Rules All", "Thirty Below for President", and "Ivan Kage = Destruction".
Fireworks sounds off as soon as the camera shows the ring. Flames shoot out of the turnbuckles, big fireworks shoot into the sky, and they're so loud it muffles the sound for the camera. We then cut to Mark Anderson and Jake Davids who look like they're having the time of their lives. Mark is wearing a white turtleneck, and a dark purple vest over it, and like usual he's go so much gel in his hair he could play football without a helmet. Jake on the other hand is wearing a UPW shirt, and some jeans. Davids has his hair tied up in a pony tail, and he's got reading glasses on.]
Mark: Welcome everyone to a sold out, jam packed Staple Center!!! I'm Mark
Anderson and with me tonight is the one and only Jake Davids!
Jake: Ya know I went to the NBA finals, I saw the fans here....but ya know nothing beats these wrestling fans, and after tonight...they're going to be UPW fans. Plus ya got the greatest wrestler ever standing next to you.
Mark: I most certainly do. What a card we have for you tonight here. Talk about top of the line action. You want to talk about as good as it gets....what about the Triple Threat match we have here tonight between Enigma, Death Master, and
Steve Greedy?
Jake: Yeah I'm looking forward to that one, but I'll tell you what I've been looking to see Ivan Kage and Morgan De Sade climb in this ring since day one.
These are two of the baddest dudes in wrestling today, and we've got them here in the UPW. Mark: Well we sh.... ["Higher" by Creed starts to play and out from the curtain steps the President,
Phillip Marteen. He's covered in leather from head to toe, and has a black cowboy hat on. The man has dyed his beard and handlebar mustache all black...so he looks even more mean that he did before. As he walks down to the ring he totally ignores the fans, and bushes off a little boy at ringside. Phillip climbs into the ring and asks for the microphone....]
Phillip: YO! First of all I wants ta come out here and talk about how dis nasty ass music is bein' played for me to come down to. If there's ONE thing that I hate it's a band who tries to rip off another bands sound. Creed is nothing but a wannabe Pearl Jam, and I will beat the living (bleep) out of the audio person if he gives me that music again!
(Crowd gives a huge pop for that.) Phillip: SO we're finally here. After all the hype we finally have a card to present to you people. Well we've got a blockbuster show for you all tonight, and there's nothing more enjoyable than watching two buff men roll around and beat the hell out of each other.....
(Crowd seems puzzled.) Phillip: YOU KNOW what I meant...I ain't gay! (Crowd appluads) Phillip: Before I go, I want to change something on a match tonight. Ya see there's this guy out here by the name of Morgan De Sade. Yeah he's a real tough guy...whateva. He wants to talk about me on his microphone time, well that's just fine and dandy to me. Tonight...we change his match to a FIRST BLOOD match. I ain't talkin' about a little scratch either. I'm talkin' the first man to bleed like a (bleep) faucet loses. Now I'm outta here...I gotta go for a smoke.
[Phillip throws down the microphone and Creed starts to play again. Phillip stops right in his tracks and points up to the audio person. The music stops, and Phillip starts to shake his head in disbelief.]
Mark: Whoa...a first blood match here tonight! Jake: Who's gonna bleed like a faucet? Mark: Well I wonder how Valentine Riley is going to feel about this? Maybe we can get some words from him a little bit later. Lets go to our first match here tonight. All you UPW fans remember this because it's the very first match EVER in the UPW. So many years down the line when someone asks you in a Triva game what it was you can tell them that it's Thirty Below vs. Baddy McGuffin. Let's take it to Brian Gallagher....
****THIRTY BELOW vs. BADDY MCGUFFIN**** Brian: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the 1st match in Universal Pro Wrestling history. The time limit is 30 minutes, and it is scheduled for one fall.
Introducing first from the Land Of The Midnight Sun.....he weighs in at 269 pounds. Here is Thirty Below!!!
[No music plays as Thrity Below makes his way down to the ring. The crowd gives him a decent face pop, and he responds with a few slaps of hands. Below climbs into the ring and jumps onto the turnbuckle and raises his arms into the air.
The crowd gets a little louder as he works them a little harder.] Brian: And his opponent....from Dublin, Ireland. He weighs in at 250 pounds....here is Baddy McGuffin!!!
[The sounds of bag pipes fill the arena, flashes of men in kilts walking down a cobble rode blowing on bag pipes; interrupted by flashes of the words IRELAND, and BADDY MCGUFFIN. Then with a bam shoots up a flash of pyro and Baddy
McGuffin emerges. Dressed in a green kilt, and a bla suit top complimented with an old english style dress shirt with the buttom up plum. A look to the crowd then a small spin with his arms outstreched leads his way into the ring.]
DING!!!! DING!!!! DING!!!! Mark: This should be an outstanding match. Both men are tremendous wrestlers, and very skilled brawlers.
Jake: Can you be a skilled brawler? I mean how much skill does it take to punch someone in the nose?
Mark: Ummm...not much. There's the bell and these two bad boys lock up. Collar and elbow tie-up...and Baddy lets go and starts whaling on him with forearm after forearm.
Jake: It's like a damn windmill. Mark: He's not letting up and Thirty Below is backed into the corner. Listen to these fans erupt!! Baddy is ripping right through Thirty Bel...OH!!! I spoke to soon!! Thirty Below with a knee to the midsection, and he follows up with a clothesline.
Jake: And all the forearms in the world can't protect you from a knee to the stomach. Mark: Thirty Below with a series of stomps to the chest area, and he brings
Baddy back to his feet. Thirty with a Snap Suplex, and that was beautiful.
Jake: Perfect speed and elevation. Those are hard to do, and if you don't do it just right you'll pull your whole back out of place. Believe me when I tell you this!
Mark: Thirty brings Baddy back up to his feet and whips him into the ropes...Baddy ducks a clothesline, and spins Thirty Below back around. Boot to the midsection, and he goes for a DDT, but Thirty backs out from it. He barely got out of the one.
Jake: A move like that so quick in the match could put you out. I know that
Thirty Below is really a master of DDT's, and when he hits you with one...it's hard to get up. Then when he hits you with two, it's almost over.
Mark: Both of these men will compete in the BMF Battle Royal on December the
9th. Odd day to have a Pay-Per View on.
Jake: Hey everyone else has theirs on Sundays...why not be a little different? Mark: Baddy rakes the eyes, and he takes him down with a nice scoop slam. Baddy now with a kneedrop to the right shoulder, and another knee to that shoulder.
This man is a pure brawler... Jake: Well I wouldn't say that just yet. I've seen this guy wrestle...I know that he can execute some pretty decent technical moves as well.
Mark: Baddy brings Thirty back up...oh devistating short clothesline, but he didn't let go of the arm. ANOTHER short clothesline....ONE MORE!!!! Whoa!!!
Three short clotheslines in a row, and Thirty drops to the mat holding his neck in pain. Jake: It's bad enough he hit one, but he nailed him with three!!! Mark: Baddy now goes for an arm shoulderlock. Looks like he's going to soften up that right shoulder a little bit more.
Jake: If he's going to soften up anything it should be Thirty Below's back. I mean Baddy's finisher is a modified Lion Tamer anyway. Once you lock something like that in...you can go home. There's no getting out of that.
Mark: McGuffin lets go and he waits for Thirty to make it back up. He charges
TB, and OH OUT OF NO WHERE!!! Spinnnig DDT by Thirty, and that'll knock you for a loop. He's going for the cover....
1.... 2..... KICKOUT!!!! Jake: That was close. I told you once he hits a DDT like that you can count to 15. It didn't happen to Baddy because he's a red headed freak. Mark: Well I don't think that's the case. Thirty brings Baddy back to his feet, and he gives him a boot to the midsection. Double Underhook DDT by Thirty, and
Baddy's head snapped off that canvas like a basketball.
Jake: Ahh and this is what I like. Mark: Thirty is rubbing Baddys face into the mat. He's grinding it....that can't feel pleasent. Thirty now brings Baddy up and he nails him with a punch to the side of the head, and another one...Baddy swings back but only hits air.
Thirty grabs him and nails him with a suplex, but OHH!!!! He drapes him over that top rope!!!!
Jake: Ouch. Mark: Thirty just hung him out to dry, and now he turns that into a DDT!!! Front face DDT, and Thirty is going for the cover again. This tim he hooks the leg..... 1..... 2......... 3!!!!!!!!! Mark: He got him!!! Thirty is the winner of the first match here tonight. Jake: No, the ref is saying that Baddy got his shoulder up right before the three count. Pay attention damnit.
Mark: Thirty can't believe this and he's arguing with the ref, and that won't get him very far. He's got his index finger right int he chest of our head ref, and Baddy is recovering behind him.
Jake: Thirty better look out... Mark: Baddy with a reverse neckbreaker, and that caught Thirty by suprise. Baddy is going to the top rope?!?!?! He doesn't look to comfortable up there....and here comes Thirty. Both men are on the top rope now...they're jocking for position. Baddy with a forearm to the face, and Thirty responds with a poke to the eye....Baddy with a headbutt, and that nailed Thirty right between the eyes. He's reeling...he's gonna fall back.
Jake: Baddy pushes him...WAIT!!! OH THAT WAS BRILLIANT!!! Mark: Fans when Baddy pushed Thirty. We saw TB hold onto that arm and turn it into a Top Rope Single Arm DDT!!!! What a move by Thirty Below, and these fans are giving him the respect he deserves. Below gets up and he's calling for his finisher....
Jake: Tundra Fights Back!! This is an amazing finisher...it's a Powerbomb into a Falling DDT.
Mark: HE HITS IT!!!! This could be it... 1..... 2..... 3!!!!!! Mark: This one is history!!! Brian: Here is your winner....Thirty Below!!!! [Thirty Below makes it up to his feet and helps up Baddy McGuffin in the process. Both men shake hands, and the crowd appluads like crazy. Thirty Below raises Baddy's arm in the air...BUT with the other arm McGuffin nails him with a huge clothesline.]
Mark: OH NO!!! What the hell is wrong with him? Baddy just leveled Thirty Below with a lariat, and now he's stomping the hell out of him.
Jake: Well some people were born to win, some were born to lose, and then there are the ones that just want to hurt people. I would say that Baddy falls into that last one.
Mark: Baddy with a headbutt to the nose of Thirty Below, and that busts open the Angry Alaskan like a water balloon. There's blood all over his face, and now he falls to the floor. Baddy's manager is telling him to lock in the Pot O'
Gold. HERE IT IS!! He's got it locked in, and Thirty is screaming in pain. Jake: Isn't it about time we get someone out there to help? Mark: Yeah...can we get some people down there? Here comes the head of security, Jerry Corbett and he's telling Baddy to let go. You don't want to mess with Jerry..that guy can bench a Buick.
Jake: Baddy isn't letting go.... Mark: JERRY WITH A FOREARM!!!! JERRY CLOCKED BADDY!!! Jake: That's one way to get him off of Thirty. Mark: And Baddy is trying to get to Jerry, but his manager won't allow it. Look at the look on Baddy's face....he wants blood.
Jake: Oh and Thirty Below from behind Baddy....REVERSE Hangmans DDT!!!! Classic!!! Mark: Thirty rolls out of the ring and walks to the back. That doesn't look like a very upset man. I mean his nose is like a river of blood. Doesn't it stop?
Jake: Well Baddy must have gotten him pretty damn good. Mark: Fans lets take you to Willy Green who is standing by with comments from
Valentine Riley about tonights match. Willy...
-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Camera cuts to Willy who is standing in the locker room with the one and only
Valentine Riley. VR looks to be in top shape for tonights match, but he also looks like he has something very important to say...]
Willy: Thanks Mark and Jake..right now standing next to me is a man who in just a few moment will get into the ring with Morgan De Sade. Now we've all heard what Morgan has had to say, we've heard him make the acusations that you are upper class, you ride in limos....I mean what do you have to say about that?
Valentine: I don't know why he would go and spread these lies about me. My momma, she always told me, there ain't no reason to lie, the only people who lie are the people who are afraid. I don't ride no limos, I don't carry a briefcase, and most of all, I would never...let me repeat, NEVER be mean to a waiter. Someone waiting on you is a privaledge, now I might not be able to tip as well as a rich man, but I treat every human being with respect. Now Mr. De
Sade, I don't consider you to be much of a human being. Like my momma always said, you're lying...that must mean that you're afraid. Afraid of me? I don't really understand that, I'm nothing special, I'm just your average guy. But in the ring, you can't lie, you have to go head to head, toe to toe, and see who the better man is. Now Mr. De Sade, you can be mean to anybody you want...anybody, except for me. Cause I ain't just gonna lie down and let you win, cause my momma taught me to always stand up for what I believe in, and I believe in people telling the truth. And for the record...I drive a 1987 Red Ford Tempo, four door, Kansas plate WES 848, thank you very much...limo, ha.
Willy: Now tonight you have a First Blood match against that same man. This wasn't planned, this wasn't even a thought about 30 minutes ago and now it's going to be an all out war. Do you hold anything against the President Phillip
Marteen for this?
Valentine: Why would I possibly hold anything against this man? I don't even know him. My momma, she told me, never judge a man by what you hear, you can only judge a man on his heart. And President Marteen, he's doing what he thinks is right in his heart, he's giving the fans what they want, blood. Now whether it's my blood or Mr. De Sade's blood, that remains to be seen, but if the fans want blood, I'll bleed for them, cause as wrestlers, we are here to serve the people. Now Mr. De Sade, you asked me if I have what it takes. Well, I'm not sure if I do, but I'm gonna give it my damndest, and if I lose, then so be it,
I'll live to fight another day, but just remember, in the ring, you can't lie.
It's all going to come down to who wants it more, and I can tell by the way you hold yourself, and the lies that you spread, that I want it more.
Willy: In one word how would you sum up your match here tonight with Morgan De
Sade? Valentine: Truth. Mr. De Sade wants to go spreading his lies, well, dammit. THE LIES STOP HERE! Willy: Calm down Valentine. Valentine: Don't you 'calm down' to me...lies hurt people...but they don't hurt people as much as I do!
(Valentine storms out.) Willy: Okay well that was rather odd. Jake, Mark back to you!
