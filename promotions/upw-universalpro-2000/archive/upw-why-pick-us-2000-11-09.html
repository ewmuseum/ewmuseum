Why Pick Us?

9 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all before I go any further...if there is anyone to blame for this damn Presidential problem it's the OLD people from Palm Beach, Florida. Damn them! After you turn 80 you shouldn't vote at all...because more than likely you're not going to be around for the changes anyway. Take away your drivers license, and you're right to vote!
Now onto the real subject...why choose this so called E-Fed? You've seen em' all, you've heard em' all. Some say they're the best, some say that they're new and the ideas are awesome...whatever it is they end up folding in a month.
I know I'm sick and tired of joining this little crappy feds that think that they're going to change the world of E-Wrestling as we know it. All the hype and then nothing. It's like LUCKY CHARMS when it gets a new marshmellow. All the hype, and then you rush to the store to get it, you race home pour it in a bowl...and even though you get one extra marshmellow...you still are left with more dry cat food.
Universal Pro Wrestling isn't going to be like that. You see the fact is that we're all about giving you the marshmellows, we're about giving you the good stuff and forgetting about all the other crap that goes along with e-feds.
But Joe...how do you do that? Well below are ten things that separate us from the other NEW feds on the RSPWF. 10. We know that you have to start small to become a big factor here...we realize that we're not going to be the best right away, but in time we will be. 9. We're E-Mail based so the Message Boards aren't a problem. I hate Message Boards and there is nothing more annoying that having to Log-In, Read all the single Rp's, and then respond...with the UPW all you have to do is send us Rp's and we send them out in a batch.
8. We don't bite off of the WWF or WCW (Not that anyone would bite off of the WCW). You're not going to see a re-hashed Vince/Austin angle, and you're not going to see an angle where you say to yourself...GEE is Arn Anderson the father of the baby?
7. No late ANYTHING. I hate it more than anything when some lazy fed head says something will be out on time and low and behold...the bastard sends it out 15 days later. We're not even going to be 24 hours late.
6. NO MARKS HERE. Now when I say "Marks" I mean that we're not getting a "Nature Boy" Ric Austin, or "The Rock" Pablo Suckme or whatever. Now I love being a mark for my own stuff, as we all should be because that's what makes it fun...but we're not going to have some loser who RPS IN ALL CAPS AND SPIELS
HIS WRODS ALL WONG. 5. Play by Play cards. Now I know some people perfer this, and other don't...but the fact is that I think it makes things way more interesting. You can get a wrestler over, you can describe more and you can feel like you're right in the story.
4. We won't neglect the tag team ranks. They will have great angles just like the singles wrestlers. 3. Everyone will get a shot. If you're busting your damn hide to be a good E-Wrestler, and you're trying as hard as you can...and you're improving, you're getting heat, and you're involved with the fed we'll push you.
2. We will not fold for a long time. As soon as we get the ball rolling, IF we get the ball rolling we're not going to have the fed close by Thanksgiving. We'll have it close by Christmas! AHAH okay no...for real. We're going to keep this baby going until something big happens.
1. This is going to be a fun that you're going to have fun in. I want a fed we're you're going to look forward to getting online to participate. I want you to be able to look forward to the cards, PPV's, and Flashes.
These are ten GOOD reasons, and I think that they're pretty damn good. You guys really have nothing to lose by joining...this isn't another crappy e-fed that will fold. This will be a big fed, and some day it'll compete with the best.
Give it a try and I swear to you as I'm typing right now...you won't be sorry. - Joseph
