The Soap Dish (Tazzmission Into a dropping Camel Clutch) [detail withheld] - "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs - Call Me Crazy (A Hangsman DDT) TsC Rules & Regulations TOS Service - The TOS service and agreemants are quite Easy to follow. Just dont report Other TSC Members for sending in roleplays. If you will have Problems with this Agreement Then dont think twice about not joining The Squared Circle. Roleplay Guidlines - Roleplay Guidlines will be set right here. First is first and that means i will Expect a Roleplay Minimum Of 30 Lines a Roleplay. And The deadline will be explained all later. You Sha'll only use your character you Joined as Unless Your in a stable with another. Then your Freedom Serves you right to use him or her in your roleplay. Third and final. we will judge your Roleplays Off of Quality. If Your Roleplays Arent turned in before deadline Then they will not count and you will lose your match for that week. Their will be An Rp Limit which is 3 Full length And Quality Roleplays Per a match. Cards And Event Information - Roleplays are a big part of the events. The Quality and Realism Of the roleplay will be the outcome of your match. Their will be a deadline At the bottom of every card. Check that out and that is when your two or three roleplays a week are due in. If all goes as planned , I Will make great results which will Tingle the spines of many Joining Or Quitting - If you Join The Squared Circle Email your Application to [detail withheld] And Include a Sample Roleplay. without one you will not be Accepted. So If you Are gonna go and Ask , "Do i need a sample?" I will not even bother , So either send a sample or I will not accept your Character into the TSC. Only the Fittest will make it. So I would say send your best work in. And If your quitting then email The President [detail withheld] And he will take you off the List. Thank you. Titles And Rankings - The Orginality of your character will determine how well you do with The Championship(s) and rankings. If i think you are fit for whatever title i put you in then that means i like your rp's And think that you would fit in with that Gimmicked Championship. Angles And Stables - First of this column is the Angles. If you have any Angles you would like to Fullfill then write out how and what you would like to happen And send to [detail withheld] , If i agree with the Angle then i will make sure you notice it in The results. Other then that that is all i would like to say. Now Second Is Stables. The Stables will draw Fueds of many kinds. So we expect Many sorts of Them. The Stable Limit is 8 Members And 1 Chosen Manager Of Male or Female Gender. You are allowed to throw Members out of the stable as Feuds and Angles go along. Any other Questions. Email [detail withheld] For the answers. Racial And Religious Agreemants - Roleplays may contain Stuff that may Offend Your Race Or religion. NO! I will not tolerate it. You Will be abled to produce great Roleplays without Offending anyones Religion or race. If you have a problem with this. Then dont Join because this Is One thing i will never Put up with. first time its A warning , Second time you Know what happens. I throw you out in the cold. Others - Roleplays Cannot be written in a font Larger then 10 Arial. I dont want it any Bigger then 10 Arial Bold. Also One screename may have one character and One tag team with the Privelage of one manager.This Federation is also Just Fake. I Feel as if Their are reals in the fed then it will take most of the creativity of a made up fed away. And Last but not least. Have as much fun as possible. Roleplaying Is a Luxury. Not something to take for granted. And I add this in because it is Important , You may not delete TsC mail or you will be tooken off the mailing if so. TsC Title History tSc World Title History 1.
