Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

XWF ran out of a Philadelphia handler's GeoCities account under two names at once: the account title reads "XWA--Xtreme Wrestling Federation," the pages under it brand themselves "XWF" throughout, and its own fictional staff page describes referees "working in the XWA" without ever reconciling the two. Its flag banner explains itself in the operator's own voice — the American and Canadian flags because that is where the handlers were, the Pennsylvania flag because that is where he was, the UN flag for the autumn of 2001, and the Portuguese flag because he was Portuguese.

It was a roleplay fed run on a strict clock. Eight rules, sent out plainly: build every roleplay around one of the fed's own interviewers and never injure one, send roleplays and strategies in by noon Eastern, no slurs on pain of becoming a permanent jobber, two wrestlers to a handler, and victories awarded on creativity rather than length. Real places were welcome in a promo and real people were not — a line the fed drew for its writers and kept on its own roster, every name in the surviving record being its own invention.

What survives is a fed poised at its own starting line. Four championships — World, TV, Hardcore and Tag Team — are named on every one of its title pages and every one of them reads VACANT, with the title-histories page carrying the heading PREVIOUS HOLDERS and nothing under it. Five men are listed in the singles division and the tag division is an empty grid. One stable had been announced, the Executive Mafia, three men out of the front office, and giving it a logo was the last thing the site is recorded doing. The one card held is a Monday Night Massacre at the Birmingham BJCC, dated "Date TBD," promising a battle royal that would settle the number-one contendership and crown a Television Champion in the same fall — and its results page is one of six pages on the account that no archive ever captured.

The commissioner, Brawler, is the only wrestler here with a page of his own: seven feet, 280 pounds out of Phoenix, retired to the front office and billed on an entrance video reciting the championships he won in a federation called the IEA. NAME-GUARD: distinct from the held xwf (a Spanish-language GeoCities e-fed, geocities.com/xwf_pr) and the held xwf-uwow (a 2008 real-wrestler brand extension) — a comparison this wing's own roster now makes directly, and no name, belt or team is shared with either. Also distinct from several further leads on the frontier ledger built out of the same two acronyms, one of them sharing the full name outright and nothing else.

Storylines

The fed never got as far as a story Written by AI, 2026

Nothing in the surviving record stages an angle, a turn or a feud. The corpus is entirely the apparatus a fed builds before it runs: rules, an application form, a staff, two roster pages, a stables table, a rankings sheet, a title-history sheet, a champions page and one undated card. Every belt is vacant, every ranking slot is blank, every title history is empty, and the one card names no wrestler in the one match it bills. The roleplays that would have carried a story were e-mailed to the operator rather than posted, and none is held. There is no connective tissue here to recount — only the stage, set and never used.
