XWF (2001–02)

The rules

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1 - When writing a roleplay, use an interviewer. That's the whole point of me having that "Fictional Staff" section. Only the interviewers are to be used. Interviewers are NOT to be injured in any way, shape, or form in your roleplays. Failure to follow this rule will result in your wrestler jobbing for that week. If this happens twice in a row, you'll be on probation for two weeks. Third time, and you will be released. No questions asked, no chance to argue your case. 2 - Do not use another peson's wrestler in your roleplay unless you get permission from them. I must get an email from the handler of the wrestler you're going to use, letting me know that they have allowed it. 3 - All roleplays and strats are to be sent to me no later than 12 Noon Eastern Time (if you live in another timezone or country, do the calculations. I never was good at math). Anything received after the deadline will be ignored. If you won't be able to roleplay for any reason, or if you can't be put on a card, let me know AT LEAST one week ahead of time. 4 - There are to be NO ethnic, racial, or religious slurs in your roleplays. If it's done even once, you'll be fired right away, and your wrestler becomes a permanent jobber here in the fed. 5 - Here are the limits on wrestlers: either 2 singles or 1 singles and 1 tag team. Now I know you're probably thinking you'll be smart, take the latter, and have three wrestlers. That's true. BUT, if you break up the tag team into singles wrestlers, you have to give one up. 6 - I want quality roleplays. I don't want to open my mailbox and read something like this: Hey man. This Monday, Im gonna kick you're ass, because you suck. I'm better than you. And I'm gonna prove it Monday. Because you suck. If I get anything like that, it won't be included in the RP packet. Which leads me to... 7 - Be creative in your roleplays. Don't always have them in the ring during a house show, or in the locker room. The cards take place all over the world. If you've been there, use some place. Hell, if we have the card in San Francisco, for example, have your wrestler give an interview while he's fishing off Fisherman's Wharf. The only thing I don't want is actual names. Using San Francisco again, it's OK if you give the interview while watching a 49ers game, but don't have your wrestler down on the sidelines talking to Terrel Owens or something. Basically: Real places and events - OK; Real people - Not OK. Got it? Also, most, if not all, e-mail programs and services have a spell checker function. USE IT. Victories are based on creativity and quality, not quantity. 8 - Swearing will be kept to a minimum. I know sometimes the only way to express yourself is to curse, but there are limits. The only word you have to censor is the F-bomb (e.g. f---, f***, f**k, f*ck, f$!@, etc). And I dont' want every other word to be the F-bomb. That's unacceptable, and your roleplay won't be counted if you do. That's it. I'm not aking for your firstborn child. I'm not asking for blood from a stone. I feel that if these 8 simple rules are followed, everyone will be happy, and we'll all have fun. BACK TO MAIN PAGE
