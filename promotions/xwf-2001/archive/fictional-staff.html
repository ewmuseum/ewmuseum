XWF (2001–02)

Fictional Staff

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brawler - Commissioner. That's right. Everybody's favorite three-time IEA Tag Team champion and North Atlantic Champion is back in the business, only this time he's got more authority. While he is sticking to his guns about staying out of the ring, he will put his gear back on if somebody gets out of line and needs a "talking to". (NOTE - Brawler is NOT, under any circumstances, to be used in roleplays unless you get my permission to do so first. He is to be treated like a wrestler, even if he is the commissioner.) Kevin Taylor - Senior Interviewer. Kevin's been an interviewer in lots of different federations. He worked hard all his life, and now he's reached the big time. Fred Johnson - Interviewer. Kevin's stepbrother. He's out to prove that he's better than Kevin at everything, so he's takes lots of dangerous assignments, usually interviewing wrestlers that are just blurring the line marking "sanity" from "delusion". Doug Mathis - Senior Referee. Doug has worked all over the world. You name any promotion, chances are that Doug knows at least one person in there. Doesn't take shit from anyone, and won't hesitate to get in between the wrestlers if the rules aren't followed. Dennis Williams - Referee. One of the few African-Americans working in the XWA. Works his ass off to prove he wasn't just a token minority hired to avoid problems. Does get quite annoyed when people think he's the football player, meet him, then are disappointed when he's not. Zach Conway - Referee. Virtual rookie. He's still not too sure of what's right and wrong in the ring, so he usually let's everything slide unless it's very blatant. Back To Main Page
