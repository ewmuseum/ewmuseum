NAME: [name withheld] EMAIL ADDRESS: [detail withheld] AIM SCREENAME AND/OR ICQ #: [detail withheld] / [detail withheld] WRESTLER NAME: Brawler HOMETOWN: Phoenix, AZ WEIGHT: 280 lbs. HEIGHT: 7'0" STYLE(Brawler, Aerial, Mat Technician, etc.): Aerial Hardcore GIMMICK/BACKGROUND: Brawler was left on his own at the age of 10, so he had to learn how to fend for himself in the streets at a very early age. When he turned 16, he walked into a wrestling school owned by Sarkkhan, and he has been training ever since. While learning in the style of Sarkkhan, he has also developed a style all his own. He's the kind of person that will kick ass first, and ask questions later. His mother was African-American, and his father was half Irish, half Italian, which definitely accounts for his extremely short temper. APPEARANCE: Brawler has often been described as a black version of Kevin Nash, only with more talent and charisma. In the ring, he wears blue jeans, a grey sleeveless Nike t-shirt, and black combat boots. He wears his head in dreadlocks, and has several tattoos marking his body: a large tribal design covering his entire left upper arm, the shape of Italy on his right bicep, and a Celtic cross on his back. He wears contact lenses to make his eyes look white like Rey Mysterio's. When he's performing his regular XWF duties, you can find him dressed in a light grey Armani suit and silver mirrored sunglasses. When he's relaxing away from the office, he's usually wearing a yellow Reebok track suit and a pair of white Answer 6 sneaks. ALIGNMENT(Heel, Face, Neutral): Neutral DIVISION(Singles or Tag): N/A ENTRANCE SONG: "Party Up" by DMX ENTRANCE(Pyros, lasers, etc.): The lights go out and a blue light slowly comes up over the stage as the words "2 Time IEA Tag Team Champion" fade into "IEA North Atlantic Champion" fade into darkness again. "Party Up" by DMX starts pumping from the speakers and the blue light pulses in rythm with the bass. At the part where DMX goes "Whoo!" white pyros explode on the ramp, and Brawler comes out from the back bobbing his head to the music. He makes his way to the ring slapping hands with the fans as some boo him and throw things at him. He slides into the ring under the bottom rope and walks to the middle of the ring. He does a backflip and as he lands, blue pyros explode on the ring posts, bringing the lights back up.
