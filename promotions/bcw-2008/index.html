Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This BCW survives only where a member of the Extreme Wrestling Association's own board, posting under the handle "Commander D," cross-posted two of its own results cards to that board's "Other Shows" sub-forum — 25 April 2008 and 14 July 2008. The same handle wrestles on both cards, retaining the BCW World Championship both times. Five titles are attested across the two cards: World, International, Hardcore, Cruiserweight and Triple Crown — four of them held by an incumbent defending, without a recorded start; only the Triple Crown title actually changes hands in the surviving record, Total Destruction defeating Eric Hinz for it on 14 July.

No document anywhere in this holding expands the fed's own acronym, and no URL for it survives — nothing beyond "BCW" is ever written down. This is a different promotion from the held British Championship Wrestling (also billed BCW, a 1999 NeWA-hub territory in Great Britain): different era by nine years, no shared name on either roster, and a different hosting platform entirely — not merged, sharing nothing but the bare, unexpanded acronym.

No story survives to recount Written by AI, 2026

Neither card carries a promo, an interview, an authority-figure segment, or any line of in-fiction narration beyond the World Championship match's own named lumberjack lists. There is no angle, turn, or feud of any kind staged anywhere in the surviving record to recount without inventing the connective tissue the corpus doesn't supply.
