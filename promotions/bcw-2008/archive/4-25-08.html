4-25-08 Show -Xero defeated Ace to retain the BCW Cruiserweight Championship (11:12) -Xero pinned Ace after a spear -John Varkin defeated Fat Joe, James Starr and K-Stevens in a fatal Four Way match to become number 1 contender of BCW International title (19:51) -John Varkin pinned Starr after a Dublin Driver -Adam Young defeated Sir Jacob in a singles match (9:44) -Young pinned Jacob after a Rock Bottom -SafeCracker defeated Triple J to win the XWF Championship (13:57) -SafeCracker pinned Triple J after a Vault cracker -Commander D defeated Alex Austin in a team lumberjack match to retain the BCW World Championship (26:19) -Commander D pinned Austin after a frog splash -Lumberjacks were Commander D (King, Total Destruction, Killer K, Ryan Wilson), Alex Austin (Zamatros, Kai, StingRay, Blaze)
