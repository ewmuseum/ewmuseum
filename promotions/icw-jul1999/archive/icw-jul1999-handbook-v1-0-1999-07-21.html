Final Words =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-= I. Introduction =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Welcome to the Internet Championship Wrestling. A federation dedicated to bringing you Quality Cards,Quality Wrestlers and just flat out a Quality Federation. Hello, My name is [name withheld] but most of you know me as the handler of "Extreme" Eron the Relentless. I have been in the e- wrestling world for awhile and I always wanted to start up my own federation. So here I am, Giving it my best shot. Take a look over the handbook and if you like it fill out an application.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= II. About the ICW =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The ICW is a booked federation. There is no simulation involved at all. To win you must roleplay. The ICW is only for original wrestlers. No, Steve Austins or Undertakers here. We at the ICW are trying to bring you the best in e-wrestling today and I will try my hardest to try and bring you that.Currently, I am looking to pick up some writers,
So if you are interested in being a part of the ICW, Give me an e-mail at [detail withheld]
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= III. Rules & Regulations =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Well, Like every federation, There has to be rules. Here they are: ROLEPLAYING RULES: Here are the rules for roleplaying. There is NO limit on how many roleplays you can do a week. If you want to dish out 5 roleplays a week, I don't care. But remember we are playing by the QUALITYover
QUANTITY rule. One good flash is better than three bad ones. Also, To prevent some lack of roleplaying, You MUST roleplay at least ONCE every two weeks. The
REAL superstars of the ICW will obviously do more than one every two weeks But I am just making this rule. If I haven't gotten a roleplay from you in two week, I will give you a warning. After that if you continue not to roleplay,
You WILL be fired. I don't care who you are, Big name or little name. You WILL be fired. Also, If you really want to get into the thick of things in the ICW then you have to be consistant. My definition of CONSISTANT is at least ONE QUALITY RP a week.If you are consistant, You will be rewarded, If you are not then you will suffer the consequences.
If their is a reason why you cannot roleplay like Vacation, School, or anything else just let me know via e-mail. Just tell me that you can't roleplay and I will take care of it. If I don't get notice from you then you will be fired.
CHARACTER RULE: Each and every handler in the ICW can only handle ONE wrestler or ONE tag team within the federation.This just makes it a lot more fair and a lot easier for everything else. So if you handle more than one character, Pick which one you would like in the ICW.
OTHER THINGS: Do not expect to be undefeated in the ICW. EVERYONE and I mean EVERYONE will lose at some point. It may not be clean but EVERYONE will lose. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= IV. ICW Broadcasts =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Here at the ICW, We currently have one weekly card. ICW Friday Slamfest: The ICW'S biggest card of the week occurs on Friday Night from arena's all over the world. This card will feature
6-8 matches all play by play, Not to mention interveiws with ICW top stars. Titles will be defended, announcements will be made and much more. When Slamfest hits the airwaves you won't want to miss it. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-= IV. ICW Pay Per Veiws =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Yes, we do have monthly Pay Per Views. Fueds will be started here and some will end here. All titles will be defended here no matter what. PPVs will usally be on the first or second Friday of the month.
January: It’s Time February: Winter Warland March: Law and DisOrder April: Bloodbath May: Spring Break June: Summer Mayhem July: Beachbrawl Augest: Mid-Summer Mayhem September: Clash of the Titans October: Ghouls and Gold November: Unforgiven December: Beast Wars =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= VI. ICW Titles =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Here at theInternational Championship Wrestling. We currently have 4 singles titles. Here is a little description for each:
ICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: The grandaddy of em all. The main prize of the ICW. The most prestigious belt in the ICW.
The man who wears this belt has the right to call himself the best in the ICW. Do you have what it takes? Step into the ICW and find out. ICW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Hey, Second best isn't bad. This belt comes runner up to the world title. It is also a very prestigious belt and the man who carries this is right up there with the best of them.
ICW CANADIAN CHAMPIONSHIP: This belt is defended weekly on ICW Friday Slamfest against a worthy contender. Can you hang onto this belt for a long time? If you can, you have the right to have this belt wrapped around your waist. And the right to be called champion.
ICW HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP: Think your the best hardcore wrestler? If you hold this title, You are the best in the
ICW. * There could possibly be a Tag Team division in the SWA. After all the singles titles have been decided I will decide whether I want a tag team division in the ICW. If there is a REALLY big responce it could happen.
