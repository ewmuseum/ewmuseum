these
        days... You can CHALLENGE, like calling someone out what have
        you... another is asking for an OPEN CONTRACT, allowing anyone
out
        there a chance to fill your open contract and get a shot at

TO WIN: Roleplay - Interviews, In-Rings, Bad Connection, what have
you...
        MCW is a roleplay fed with ZERO quantity limit... The funnier
you
        are, the more you win, plain and simple. We're quality
booked...

can't
        always follow it to the letter, but we'll use everything we
can...
ROLEPLAYS/CHALLENGES/STRATS - All come to KLEE at HIS address... :P
