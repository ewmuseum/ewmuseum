Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The fWa introduced itself to the newsgroup on 29 June 2000 as a federation that had “just opened earlier today,” hosted at its own site, fwawrestlin.hypermart.net. Its president, in the post’s own words, had “run a few feds in the past which have all been extremely successful” and considered himself “a very good e-fed president” on that record.

The fed’s pitch to prospective wrestlers set itself against a kind of federation it never named directly: it promised storylines and cards that would be “nothing short of great, not like any of these angles that are ripped off Raw or Nitro and then switched around a bit,” and staff it called some of the most original people its president had met in the e-fed world. It described itself as freshly opened, with “a lot of spots left” on its roster.

No second document survives. Whether the fWa ever staged a card, signed a roster, or crowned a champion is not recorded — the one recruitment post is the whole of its known history.
