A self-styled ‘Hardcore Central's Official E-fed’ that introduced its own cruiserweight title in December 1997, independently corroborated by a community member's own contemporaneous federation directory. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Hardcore Wrestling Association states its own founding plainly — “We started in September of '97” — from a Tripod homepage. One of its own recruitment ads signs its president “President of the TRUE HWA,” a pointed claim against whichever rival was using the same initials that season.

Its recruitment ads that December sought tag teams and cruiserweights specifically, citing “a sudden drought of tag teams” and the “new arrival of the cruiserweight belt.”

A capture of its own homepage from 18 February 1998 shows a fuller title slate: a World Championship held by Ezekiel Rage, a North American Championship held by Astroman, a Cruiserweight Championship held by Waylon Cash, and a Trans World Title held by Jack “Hard Rock” Harmond, alongside membership in an alliance it calls N.I.W.C.

That same page announces an interfederation supercard against EAW for 22 March at the Cow Palace in San Francisco, headlined by Slade against Czar and undercarded by a “Prez Fight” billing each side's own president, LaDue against Stool.

No card, roster document, or result survives beyond that page; a community fed directory from September 1997 independently corroborates the promotion's own name and web address. Whether the March supercard was ever held isn't in the surviving record.
