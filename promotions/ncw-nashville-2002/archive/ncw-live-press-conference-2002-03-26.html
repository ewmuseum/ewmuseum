NCW (Nashville) — Card

[NCW] Live Press Conference 03.26.02

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=========================================================== NASHVILLE CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING =========================================================== [A peppy little instrumental song, similar to what you might hear at the opening of a news broadcast, begins to play as the scene slowly fades in to a dimly lit news studio. The only three things visible in the shot, are a small black director's chair, a potted plant of some kind and a young blonde man dressed in khaki pants, and a red golf shirt, that has a logo which reads NCW emblazened on it over his chest. The young man smiles at the camera and begins speaking] Terry Daniels: Hello ladies and gentlemen, my name is Terry Daniels and I am the newest employee of Nashville Championship Wrestling. We know a lot of you are wondering just what Nashville Championship Wrestling is, and well that's why I'm here tonight. This fine television station has allowed us some time tonight to take you live to the first ever Press Conference being held by the executives in charge of NCW. We hope that this will answer a few questions and if nothing else, at least let people know we're serious about bringing wrestling back to Nashville. I hope you'll all stay tuned, to meet some of the faces you'll soon be seeing week in and week out on our wrestling program right here on this channel. Now I understand they're about ready to get started down in the press room, so I'm going to turn things over to them. We'll go there live now, and again thank you for watching. ======================================================= [the scene slowly opens to a crowded press room, full of people of every race, creed and color. All running about trading stories, ideas, and rumors. At the front of this room, on the wall farthest from the camera is a small black stage, which holds an even smaller wooden podium, and two long tables extending out from the podium on each side. The tables are each covered with blue tablecloths, and various sized pitchers of water. To the left of the podium, four people are seated at the table, and to the right 3 more people can be seen. They are all talking amongst themselves, and looking at papers. Much like the rest of the people in the room, until finally a man in a black suit seated directly to the left of the podium stands. He walks up to the wooden podium and pulls one of the three microphones positioned on it down to his level, then clears his throat and adjusts his brown, mop top, hair with his fingers before speaking.] Man: Um, excuse me. [the room begins to settle down as everyone begins to take their seats and turn their attentions toward the stage] I'd like to thank you all for coming out tonight. Some of you know me, but for those who don't, allow me to take a few minutes to introduce myself, along with everyone else seated before you here. And then we'll get to the reason you're all here tonight. [clears his throat again] My name is Sam Malone, and I will soon be familiar to you all as one of the on air commentators of the upcoming Nashville Championship Wrestling TV show. However, some of you may remember me from my years as a sports anchor on the news here in Nashville. I'd like to take a moment to thank those in charge at NCW for giving me a chance to follow my true dream, and that's to be a part of professional wrestling. [he nods to the three men at the right of the podium and the everyone gives a brief bit of applause] Malone: Now, I'd like to introduce you to three of the people who will be joining me on air each week with NCW TV. First of all...[turns to the lady who was sitting next to him at the table]...my co-commentator each week. [the lady stands to reveal herself to be a 20-something, attractive, blonde, with flowing hair, an hour glass figure, and supermodel looks. She's wearing a very revealing business suit style skirt and white top and a huge smile] She's a former superstar of the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance, former manager and ex-wife of Classic Championship Wrestling superstar "The Natural One" Ricky Revolution, and former model for just about every fashion designer in the world. And believe me when I say, her looks are only surpassed by her knowledge of this great sport. If only her attitude were that good. [he chuckles as she smirks at him] I give you...'SEXY' Sally McClane. ['SEXY' Sal takes a slight curtsey, and then wiggles her fingers in the gesture of a wave as cameras all across the room flash. She then retakes her seat] Malone: Next to her down there is a man who was seen briefly on TV doing play by play for the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance's Cruiserweight Division. But has actually been involved in this sport in some way or another since age 15. He'll be digging up all the backstage dirt for us in NCW. He is David Jones. [the young, black haired, man next to 'SEXY' Sal stands. He is small in stature, but still attractive in a Michael J. Fox sort of way. He is dressed in a pair of brown khaki pants and a navy blue golf shirt with the NCW logo on the left side of his chest. He makes a gesture to the crowd and then retakes his seat.] Malone: And last, but certainly not least, the man who will be joining Sally and me for all of NCW's big TV shows and supercards. He's held so many titles in this area in his life, I won't even begin to name them off. Ladies and gentleman, one of the Nashville wrestling scene's true legends..."BIG" John Frazier!! ["Big" John stands revealing his 7'4" frame, covered in a black suit almost matching Sam Malone's. He appears to be in his mid to late 50's, and his thick black hair is greying at the roots. He gives the crowd a big toothy grin, and a HUGE wave, and then sits back down] Malone: Now...I'll get to the [clears his throat mockingly] important side of the stage. [the men to the right of the podium begin to chuckle] First off, at the far end down there. The man who will be acting as our senior referee in NCW, and a good friend of mine. Ladies and gentlemen...Nicholas Anderson. [the man in the grey suit at the end of the table stands and waves. His receiding brown hair doesn't move as he sits back down] Malone: And next to him, one of the men who is destined to be the most unpopular face in NCW. Due to the fact that every problem the boys have with the office has to go to him first. He's been involved in this sport for longer than most of us could ever dream of being, and like "Big" John down there is definately a LEGEND in this area. The acting Commisioner of Nashville Championship Wrestling..."Stonewall" Jackson Crockett. [Crockett stands, pulling the black Stetson hat from his head and gesturing to the crowd with it. His black hair is plastered to his head with gel, and his suit appears to be much higher priced than the others at the table. He stands a moment and then retakes his seat] Malone: And finally, after I introduce this next man I will turn the floor over to him. He is the one who's making all this possible today. One of the most prominent business men in the greater Nashville area. The Owner and President of Nashville Championship Wrestling...Mr. Richard M. Stallings. [Stallings stands and approaches the podium. He is a heavy set man in his mid 40s, with thick grey hair, and a thicker grey mustache. He stops to shake Sam Malone's hand before turning and readjusting the microphones] Stallings: Thank you, Sam. [Malone returns to his seat] I would like to also thank each of you for coming out tonight. We hope that this press conference will give everyone in the area a better understanding of what Nashville Championship Wrestling hopes to accomplish. First of all, it is an honor to be bringing wrestling action back to Nashville and the surrounding area. We want everyone to know that they can expect the same excitement from NCW as they've expected and received from all the promotions the great state of Tennessee has hosted over the years. We hope to present that same "classic" style, coupled with modern day competitive action and excitement. We are doing everything in our power to bring the best to NCW, and we hope to give the best product we're capable of giving each and every week. We're also going to start negotiations soon with some of the other top promotions in this industry in order to maybe bring in some outside talent now and then. We're very excited about Nashville Championship Wrestling, as we hope you all are as well. And hopefully this Press Conference will get the ball rolling fast enough that we will meet the goal of an early May start up date. [clears his throat and flips through some papers laying in front of him on the podium] Stallings: I would also like to take this time to say that the schedule originally posted by us has changed slightly. Our first event will be NCW's Night to Remember. We have a few things in the works, and now feel that we will be able to pull off a Supercard for our first event, and won't have to worry about jumping the gun. We want that first night to FEEL as big as it is, for all our fans. We are also rather confident that we will have access to the Tennessee State Fairgrounds' Sports Arena for the show, however this could change, depending on the date that's set. Now I'm sure you all have questions you'd like to have answered, so at this time I'm going to turn the floor over to Jackson Crockett, since he'll be the one you'll all be dealing with mostly once things get up and running. Once again, thank you all for being here, and we hope to see each of you when NCW gets going strong. [turns to Comm. Crockett] Jackson? [Jackson Crockett stands and approaches the podium. As he passes Stallings, who is on his way back to his seat, he pats him on the back, and then turns to the microphones] Crockett: [speaking with a thick southern draw] Thank you, Richard. You covered about everythang, so we can go ahead and get started with these fine people's questions, and then get them all home to their families. So go ahead the floor is open. [everyone in the room scrambles to get picked, like kids in a classroom. Comm. Crockett finally points out a young lady at the front and she stands] Reporter #1: Thank you, Mr. Crockett. I was just wondering how you can expect to have an early May start up date, when you currently only have one wrestler on your roster? Crockett: [chuckles] Well ma'am. In the wrestling business, May is a lifetime away. And keep in mind that we haven't really been looking for talent until this week, so we feel we have plenty of time. And if by some occurence, we don't have a full roster by May, we'll postpone the opening card. That's just our goal at the moment. We'll know more accurately as to when NCW will be kicking off, very soon. Next question. [everyone scrambles again, and this time Crockett points at an older gentleman at the back of the room] Reporter #2: Speaking of the one person on your roster. Isn't it true that this Tommy Murdoch is Richard Stallings' nephew from Texas? Crockett: Yessir, that is true. Reporter #2: Well, I've done some research on this kid and well, it seems he's only been wrestling professionally for a few months and so far he hasn't won a match. Crockett: [chuckles a bit] That too, is correct sir. Reporter #2: I'm just curious as to how you can say you plan on bringing in the best when as of right now the only man on your roster is about as green as they come. Crockett: Sir, let me just say that no where in any way did we say that Tommy Murdoch was going to be our Heavyweight Champion. We've never even mentioned the boy's name other than on our webpage. We're just giving the kid his start and we hope that he will give us his best. If he doesn't, he won't work it's as simple as that. But no one will be kicked out of NCW for losing, that's for sure. Reporter #2: So you're saying the kid's a fall-guy, a journeyman, a jobber, right? Crockett: I never said that, sir. It only takes 3 seconds to win a match, and well...anyone can win at any time. Depending on how focused their opponent is. Let's just say, we're not expecting much out of Mr. Murdoch until he gets some more experience under his belt. AND...furthermore, just because he's the first one on the roster, doesn't mean that everyone on the roster will be as..."green" as he is. Now, next question. [another scramble and Crockett picks out another man in the back] Reporter #3: Since we're on the subject of the roster, have you guys talked to any talent yet? Are there any plans to bring in any big names? Crockett: We have several things in the works at the moment, but unfortunately I'm not allowed to comment on them at this time. Next? [yet another scramble, and Crockett points out a young man near the front] Reporter #4: Thank you, sir. Um...I'm wondering what venues, aside from the Sports Arena at the Tennessee State Fairgrounds, have you guys thought about using? Crockett: Well, we've talked to the owners of the Outer Limits night club about doing some TV tapings there. We've also talked to many of the High Schools in the greater Nashville area, about using their gyms and such. And we are in the opening stages of negotiations with the Grand Ole Opry. Right now, our main goal is to find a building to tape our TV show in each week and then use other locations for house shows and Supercards. Reporter #4: I've heard some rumors that part of your deal with the local network that you'll be using here in Nashville is that you'll get to use their studio for TV tapings? Is that true? Crockett: We'd definately like to, but we're trying to make sure there are no legal issues, like fire hazzards and such. Next? [another scramble, and Crockett picks another young woman from the crowd] Reporter #5: Mr. Crockett, everyone knows you were once a highly respected wrestler in this area, and that you and Mr. Frazier there had quite a heated feud, many years ago. Is there any chance we'll see that rekindled here in NCW? Crockett: [chuckles] Well sweetie, I think Mr. Frazier and myself have long passed our days in the sun. And NCW hopes to focus on young talent, not old fogies like ourselves. Now if enough people want to see "Big" John and myself settle our differences with a game of checkers, I think we can probably handle that. [the crowd all laughs] Now, next question. [the scene suddenly cuts back to Terry Daniels in the TV studio] Daniels: Sorry folks, but that's all the time we could get tonight. I know there are still a lot of unanswered questions out there, but at least now, more people know that NCW is on it's way. And soon enough we will be bringing some of the most exciting, classic style wrestling the world has ever seen to Davidson County and the surrounding areas, and hopefully one day, to the world. Thank you for tuning in, and keep an eye on this channel, as well as on our webpage at http://www.geocities.com/nashvillewrestling for more details. Thanks again, and good night. [the peppy little instrumental song plays again as the scene slowly fades to black] -- "Doc" [name withheld] [detail withheld] http://www.geocities.com/nashvillewrestling
