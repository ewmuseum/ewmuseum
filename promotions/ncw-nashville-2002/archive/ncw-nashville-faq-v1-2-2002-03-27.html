WHAT IS NASHVILLE CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING? ========================================================== Nashville Championship Wrestling is an e-fed, or fantasy wrestling federation, or internet wrestling federation, or...well you get the point. I'm not going to go into any detail here as to just what an e-fed is, because well 99.9% of you all reading this already know, and for that one small .1% who don't...no offense, but NCW probably isn't the place for you. Now, first before I go any further into describring NCW, let me tell you a bit about ME. My name is [name withheld], and yes I do live in Tennessee, which is why I chose the region to base my fed in. I've been involved in e-wrestling in some form or fashion since late 1996, but most of my accomplishments came in Classic Championship Wrestling and the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance where I handled "The Natural One" Ricky Revolution and 'SEXY' Sally McClane (respectively). Other promotions that I have been involved with over the years include, WAR!, IWF/WoW, SJPW, and others. But anyway, enough about me, let's get on to the reason you're reading this post. My vision for NCW is kind of the same one that Curt Crane began with Classic Championship Wrestling back in 1997, and that's to project a "Classic" style of wrestling over the internet. Basically what that means is, you won't see a lot of hardcore, death matches in NCW, but will instead see good competitive contests between your characters along with exciting, riveting storylines and hopefully some of the best roleplaying around. Because it's my oppinion (and others') that a chair shot is much more devestating when it's not seen every match. Therefore, with all that being said, if you're still interested keep scrolling down to find out a whole lot more about what's in store for those involved with Nashville Championship Wrestling. GETTING STARTED: First of all you have to fill out one of the character applications which is attached to the bottom of this e-mail. Kind of goes without saying doesn't it. :) But before you do so, allow me a moment to explain what type of characters we're looking for here in NCW. As I said before, we are going for a "classic" feel, so more than likely characters with names like The Terminator, and The Extreme Alcoholic will not be accepted. We're looking for gimmicks that show the style of fed that we are. Now don't get me wrong, I'm not that picky, and any good RP'er has a helluva shot of getting on board, but well you get the point I'm trying to make (I hope). :) But anyway fill out the application and send it to [detail withheld] and hopefully we'll see you soon in Nashville. NCW AS A BOOKED FED: Now that you're thinking about filling out that app, let me remind you that NCW is booked/scripted. Which basically means we do not use simulators. Your characters push and ultimately win/loss record is based solely on your ability to roleplay and work strategies. A lot of people prefer simmed feds, but personally I feel so much more can be achieved when left up to the imaginations of everyone involved in the fed. So in my opinion, booked/scripted is without a doubt, the only way to go. HOW TO WIN IN NCW: Victory and push in NCW is based on two major factors. First of all, and most importantly, is roleplaying. Roleplays will be released once a week (may go to twice a week as the roster grows) in flash packets called NCW Showcase. At this time the Showcase will just be a copied and pasted listing of all flashes/roleplays received for the week, but eventually (I.E. when I get some more help:) I'd like to see Showcase become an actual interview/news type show. But regardless of how they're presented, always keep in mind that without roleplays you will NOT be able to succeed in NCW. Now another point to make about roleplaying is, that your ability to run good angles and feuds, and keep good storylines going will also affect your push in NCW. Since that's really what makes this whole game fun and interesting it should play a strong part. However, a good flash/roleplay will win you a match regardless of what kind of storylines might be going on, and not sending in a flash/roleplay can lose you a match just as easily. Another important factor in NCW is Strats (or strategies). In my opinion a Strat isn't all that important and the only reason I require them is so I can know just what your wrestler wants to accomplish out of a match, and to see just how much you've been paying attention to your opponents. The only thing a Strat HAS to include is your entrance, the rest is left totally up to you, but remember hte more detail the better. BUT, don't worry a good flash will beat a great strat any day of the week. Any questions, comments, or suggestions on all of this are more than welcome and can be directed to [detail withheld] NCW AS A REGIONAL FED: One last thing to consider before you send in that app. NCW is a regional fed, and I am a total sucker for realism. So don't come in expecting a lot of glitz and glamour. We won't be selling out the Municipal Auditorium in Nashville or anything, but will instead be holding shows in venues like the Sports Arena at the Tennessee State Fairgrounds, or the Outer Limits night club. I guess what I'm trying to say with this is, in the spirit of realism, a wrestler that's held numerous titles and gained a great reputation in the sport probably wouldn't work for NCW. Unless maybe they've fallen out of the spotlight or something. In no means am I saying I only want new characters, but well you get the picture. If we can't afford pyrotechnics and such, we couldn't afford the contract of a legend. =========================== II. RULES AND REGULATIONS =========================== There aren't really a lot of rules here, just the basics. Such as, don't say anything in a flash that might be found offensive to someone elses beliefs or culture. For instance no ethnical, racial, or religious slurs please. And please keep the profanity to a minimum. I of course won't say anything about using ass, damn, or hell or anything like that in a flash, but stay away from any words that seem more "severe" than those. Basically, if you don't think it could be said on regular cable, don't say it here. And really and truly that's about it, however any flash which breaks either of these rules blatantly will not be used. Also allow me to say that NCW does reserve the right to suspend or dismiss anyone who continues to blatantly break these rules or any others we might add later. And also reserves the same right toward anyone with any type of long periods of inactivity. Don't worry, everyone will get jobbed royally before they're suspended or dismissed, but jsut keep in mind that it could happen. ================================== III. NCW Shows and Events ================================== TV SHOWS ------------------ NCW TV: This will be the weekly card, which will feature 4-5 matches (this number will grow as I get matchwriters and the roster grows) along with interviews and angle progression and other things. The card will be in full format, which basically means the matches will be written out with full commentary. We won't just be posting results or anything. This show will be what leads up to all the promotions' supercards. Basically, this is our version of Monday Night Raw. The show is broadcast in the greater Nashville area every Thursday Night. (which means the card should be released on Thursdays, that may change though after I talk to my match writers, when I find them;) NCW Showcase: As I said above, this will be (at first) nothing more than a cut and paste listing of all flashes turned in for the week. But also once again, I'll remind you, if you don't have a flash on this show, you stand no chance of winning your match for the week. SUPERCARDS ------------------------ NCW's Night To Remember: Night To Remember is being slated as NCW's first Supercard. As of now a date has not been set for the event, but it will be held at the Sports Arena at the Tennessee State Fairgrounds. On the show the first ever NCW Southern Heavyweight Champion will be crowned in an 8 man single elimination tournment. Entrants will be determined in the weeks preceding the event, in qualifying matches on NCW TV. This card will be an annual event, and will hopefully become the coveted show to compete on in NCW. NCW's Wrestling Classic: Not a lot is known about Wrestling Classic at this time, however it has been announced that NCW will try to bring in workers from other regional feds around the world for this event and that it will unlike most supercards, be a 3 day event, with the main attraction being some type of large interpromotional tournament. More information will be released later. NCW Southern Impact: NCW is already planning to within their first year do a tour extending further into the South, and this event will cap off the tour. Again the date is not disclosed, nor is the location, however it is almost a definate that the event will be held outside of Nashville. NCW Fright Night: NCW's annual October Supercard which will throw the rulebook out the window. Every match of hte night will be a gimmick match of some kind. There is talk that all the matches gimmicks/stipulations will be determined randomly, but this was not a definate. =============== IV. NCW TITLES =============== Singles Titles -------------------- NCW Southern Heavyweight Title: This will the granddaddy of NCW's singles straps. The belt is open to everyone in the promotion and must be defended once every 30 days. NCW Southern Jr. Heavyweight Title: Same as the above title except the strap is only available to wrestlers 220lbs or less. Also must be defended once every 30 days. NCW Tennessee State Title: This belt is the secondary singles title in NCW. The title was created to show the heritage and tradition of wrestling in Tennessee, and thus is not taken lightly. The rules for this title are the most strictly enforced and only true WRESTLERS will ever hold it. The title must be defended once every 2 weeks. Tag Titles ---------------- NCW Southern Tag Team Titles: The Southern Tag Titles will be the goal of every tag team in NCW. The titles are open to every team on the roster and must be defended once every 30 days. NCW Six Man Tag Team Titles: These straps will be added to the roster in the distant future. Not a lot has been decided on them as of right now. Only that htey will be defended once every 60 days. =============================== V. NCW PERSONALITIES AND STAFF =============================== Front Office Staff ------------------------- President Richard M. Stallings: Mr. Stallings is the main governing force in NCW, personally appointed by the company's owners to basically run the show from a distance. Stallings does not get involved in angles or appear on TV all that often. He is simply put, the man behind the curtain. Basically, this is the guy that your characters complain about, not to. Commisioner "Stonewall" Jackson Crockett: "Stonewall" Jackson Crockett is, as his name implies, a hard nosed, southerner appointed by Stallings to keep physical order in NCW. Crockett, a former wrestling star in the Nashville area, is no stranger to life in and around wrestling, but as commisioner he shows a fury that not even his opponents back in the day saw. He is without a doubt the hand that guides the promotion, as he will be the man who comes out and makes decisions live on TV. And in fact could end up being hte most unpopular man in the company. Reporters, Commentators, Announcers, Etc. ----------------------------------------------------------------- "Wrestling's Encyclopedia" Sam Malone: One of the hosts of the weekly NCW TV show, Malone has dedicated his entire adult life to broadcasting, and his entire life "period" to wrestling. His exciting commentary style, combined with his seemingly endless knowledge of the wrestling world makes him one of the hottest commentating commodities (say that 3 times fast) on the market. WIthout question he's the play-by-play man of the group, whose style could best be described as a little Mike Tenay, a lot of Jim Ross, and some Joey Styles thrown in for effect. 'SEXY' Sally McClane: Former Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance superstar, former manager and ex-wife of Classic Championship Wrestling legend Ricky Revolution, former fashion model, and mother of one, 'SEXY' Sally McClane pulled no punches in the ring and she pulls none at the announce table either. The primary color commentator on NCW TV, Sally calls things like she sees them and as those who've seen her before know, she doesn't always see things just how they are. She's drop dead gorgeous, devious, and tough, a lethal combination and all three come through in her commentary. And she's not just a ditsy blonde who's there to look good. She knows her stuff in the ring and thus does a damn good job of calling the action. Sally was last seen in CCW with, her then husband, Ricky Revolution. However after a somewhat public divorce, Sally moved back to her hometown of Nashville and settled for living off of some fat alimony and child support checks. However she couldn't stay completely out of the business, but then again, who can. "Big" John Frazier: Frazier is another former superstar of the Nashville area, who competed in many matches with and against NCW Commisioner, Jackson Crockett. Frazier is the "baby face" color man for NCW TV, however he doesn't appear each week, due to other obligations. He's basically, more or less just a back up, and his skills behind the mic really don't compare to his skills in the ring. But at 7'4" tall and 398lbs, who's really gonna tell him that. David Jones: David Jones is the investigative reporter of NCW. He conducts most of the highly important interviews and usually digs up most of the talked about rumors and news. His monthly segment on NCW TV, known as "David Jones' Locker" spills some of the most inside of hte inside dirt in NCW. Terry Daniels: Terry is an employee of the television station that hosts NCW TV, and really doesn't know much about wrestling. He's slowly learning from the others around him, but pretty much he's just a young 20 yr old with little experience who's easy for the boys to push around. He does most of the random interviews for the fed. Gary Smiley: Gary Smiley is the ring announcer for NCW, a young man with a killer set of vocal cords and the right voice for his profession. He doesn't do anything other than introductions in the ring however. Referees/Officials --------------------------- Nicholas Anderson: Nicholas Anderson is the senior official of NCW, and is the hardest one to get anything over on. At times he will let the rules bend a little to prevent a screw job ending to a match, however you never want to push it with Nicholas because more than likely if he's conscious he's going to see it. He refs all title matches and main events, unless he appoints someone else to do so. Donald Morton: Donald Morton has been involved in the wrestling business in some way or another for the last 30 years. And now at age 64 he is an official in NCW. Unlike Anderson, it's very easy to get stuff past Donald however due to his "old school" mentality, any cheating or foul play that he notices will be punished. Donald refs only one or two matches per week. Jason "Squeaky" Walker: Jason is 19 years old and just breaking into the business, however he's still a fairly well rounded official. He let's some things get past him, but he's learning a lot from Nicholas and he catches on quick. His only real weak point is, it's rather easy for the boys to push him around. ================= VI.
