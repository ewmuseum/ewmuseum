UEWA - Join Today, 26 August 1997

26 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Visit our webpage at http://www.mindspring.com/~ryanz/uewa/ The Rules - You must roleplay - All wrestlers must be original - Try to use spellcheck - Never say or use the word jobber in a flash - Cursing is allowed in roleplays only and it must be masked - No complaining of any sorts about loses will be tolerated as we feel we are fair - Never put down another member outside of a roleplay - Please do not send mail to all the fed unless it is a flash - All gameplans must be approved by the President - No Out Of Character comments allowed - No Out of Character insult directed at holders - No in character insults directed at holders - You may advertise for another fed with the mailing list but the limit in one time only These rules are to be very strictly enforced. If any rule is broken then it may result in a suspension. If they are repeatedly broken then you will get the boot. UEWA Cards and Pay Per View Events The UEWA host weekly cards and monthly pay per view events. These matches are commentated by the following commentators: Saturday Broadcast Personnel: [name withheld] - Neutral Commentator and Wrestling Analyst "The Greatest" Don Jolly - Heel Commentator and "Wannabe" Movie Star Jim Jacobs - Face Commentator Thursday Broadcast Personnel: George "The Gorge" Sullivan - Neutral Commentator and Former Wrestler [name withheld] - Heel Commentator and Millionaire Jim Jacobs - Face Commentator The UEWA staff consists of the following: Jason King - Ring Announcer Jim Bragdon - Head Ref Danny Olson - Ref Alan Rammel - Ref Troy Smith - Ref Andy Bogar - Interviewer #1 and Face Mike Shaguassy - Interviewer #2 and Neutral UEWA Thursday Night Thrashings (1 hour or 500 lines) UEWA Saturday Night Slammasters (1 1/2 hours or 750 lines) Here's our yearly Pay-Per-View evenets UEWA New Year Knockout - January 1st UEWA Apocalypse '97 - Febuary 18th UEWA March Mayhem- March 15th UEWA Battle Bash - April 22nd UEWA Wrestle Wars - May 27th UEWA Blood Bash - June 15th UEWA Independance Day Brawl - July 4th UEWA Summer Showdown - August 19th UEWA Fall Brawl - September 15th UEWA Frightfest - October 9th UEWA Blastoff - November 1st UEWA Christmas Carnage - December 15th Note: Cards are subject to change UEWA Belts The UEWA hosts numerous belts. As always, you cna only win the belts via submission or pinfall. UEWA Universal Championship Belt UEWA World Tag Team Championship Belts UEWA International Championship Belt UEWA Cruiserweight Championship Belt ( 235 pounds or under only ) How Match Winners Are Decided We decide match winners via strageies sent in and roleplaying. You are required to send out two roleplays per week. If you do not, you will be notified. If it continues, you will be put under suspension. Roleplaying shows how much you care about your wrestler. A good average of roleplaying is between 4-6 QUALITY roleplays. If you send out some ludicrous amount of roleplays (like 10+), your wrestler will start to burn out and loose matches. Remember, quality over quantity. The Application Depending on where you are reading this you have an application down below. Please fill it out if you still intend on joining the UEWA. Also we do not allow wrestlers over 7'6" in height and 600 pounds in weight as they are very unrealistic. They would be very slow or very very fat. The Finisher, Combined Finisher area should not only have the name of the move, but also a description of the move. While the Other Notes area should describe their physical description, ring attire, and their personality which in turn will allow us to give your character more of a personallity in his matches. Please also remember that when filling out a tag team app to include name for both wrestlers and all their stats in that one app it will save us time. You can view how the stats will look at our roster area.
