UEWA Needs Wrestlers, 29 August 1997

29 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear Readers of RSPWF, The Universal Extreme Wrestling Federation needs more wrestlers! As many as possible! We're trying to get up to a total of 24 wrestlers, singles and tag team so we can start on the 6th of September with our Title Battle Royal. Tag teams will also participate, so if ONE part of as tag team wins the belts, they share it with their partner! We need members! This is the newest most up and coming league! We are looking for experienced, veteran, or even beginning e-Wrestlers. We use a booked system based on strategies, rank, and roleplays! Please, we need more wrestlers. We promise to give our best effort in provifing an enjoyable e-Wrestling experience. Below are some of the benefits you get of participating in the UEWA! You get your own personalized biography page, created by our top of the line webmasters. You may also provide a picture or midi of your entrance theme. If you join any other feds, you can just point them to your web address. You also get full access to our Challenge form, Sneak Attack form, and Gameplan form, as well as full access to our website. When you join UEWA, you get the most options availble in the business today, for free! We are the most in-depth federation, and we guarentee that your wrestler will do exeactly what you want! As well, you get full color commentated matches by our broadcast personnel. No more one line synopsis, you get full descriptions of each pulverizing move your wrestler executes. The additions don't stop there! Joining UEWA means you get the dedication no other e-fed can provide. We guarentee each and every card will be out on each and every specified day, produced by our expert card writers. You can tell how much we care, because we set a specific time for a show. You can expect it to happen right when it is supposed. Also, our staff is dedicated to help answer your questions. Our response time averages between thrity minutes to two hours, better than any other federation out there. We promise you that we will do our best to make this the best fed, if you can help us too. Currently we only have two staff members. We are looking for two card writers. Remember, you get the best, when you join the UEWA. We try our best to live up to our motto, "The best wrestling organization in the world." Below is a description of our services and details of our particular e-fed. If you are interested in joining, just email the application to [detail withheld] . We hope you have an ejoyable time in the UEWA. Sincerely, President [name withheld] UEWA Universal Championship Belt UEWA World Tag Team Championship Belts UEWA International Championship Belt UEWA Cruiserweight Championship Belt ( 235 pounds or under only ) Also, we have the following pay-per-views. There will be no Fall Brawl this year, as it has been scrapped for the Title battle Royal which will determine the title holders! UEWA New Year Knockout - January 1st UEWA Apocalypse '97 - Febuary 18th UEWA March Mayhem- March 15th UEWA Battle Bash - April 22nd UEWA Wrestle Wars - May 27th UEWA Blood Bash - June 15th UEWA Independance Day Brawl - July 4th UEWA Summer Showdown - August 19th UEWA Fall Brawl - September 15th UEWA Frightfest - October 9th UEWA Blastoff - November 1st UEWA Christmas Carnage - December 15th Please, join today!
