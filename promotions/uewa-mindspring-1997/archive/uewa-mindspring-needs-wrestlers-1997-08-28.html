New Fed needs wrestlers!, 28 August 1997

28 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Ultimate Extreme Wrestlign Association needs YOU! Yes, we need wrestlers to wrestle in our up and coming league. We serve as a farm league for the hit new fed, UIW or Ultimate Internet Wrestling. You can either stay in our fed, or move up to theirs if they come asking! We use a universal method that has worked for most feds! We base winners on writing and roleplaying, commitment, rank, gameplans, and strategies. There is also a certain amount of luck within it. We offer the following belts: UEWA Universal Championship Belt UEWA World Tag Team Championship Belts UEWA International Championship Belt UEWA Cruiserweight Championship Belt ( 235 pounds or under only ) Also, we have the following pay-per-views. There will be no Fall Brawl this year, as it has been scrapped for the Title battle Royal which will determine the title holders! UEWA New Year Knockout - January 1st UEWA Apocalypse '97 - Febuary 18th UEWA March Mayhem- March 15th UEWA Battle Bash - April 22nd UEWA Wrestle Wars - May 27th UEWA Blood Bash - June 15th UEWA Independance Day Brawl - July 4th UEWA Summer Showdown - August 19th UEWA Fall Brawl - September 15th UEWA Frightfest - October 9th UEWA Blastoff - November 1st UEWA Christmas Carnage - December 15th Please, join today! Below is the application! Copy/paste it to an email message and send it to [detail withheld] We offer complete play-by-play, move-by-move commentary on our two shows, Thursday Night Thrashings and Saturday Night Slam Masters! If you are an experienced e-wrestler, or just new to the sport, this is the league for you!
