NOTES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -Remember, there is a THREE RP cap limit on all Vortex shows. All RP's there after will not be counted and will count against you UNLESS you notify us ahead of time and are using them for angle purposes. We WILL
NOT EVEN READ THEM if we are not notified until after the Vortex show and then we will see what was so important. The reason being, even when people post after the deadlines and what not and say, oh it doesn't have to count, it still persuades the voter. The info is there, and we can't go back in time and not read it. Please remember this.
-Deadlines will typically three days prior to the card. This gives you five days to RP AND Strat. Strats will not be a huge emphasis. We would like to see AT LEAST a ring entrance, a few spots, and your reaction to winning/losing or idea on how to end the match in either occurrence. Anything else will be additional knowledge to help the writers. If you want to use a Universal Strat/Ring Entrance [cut and paste every week] that is fine, it's better then nothing.
-Universal Strat Template includes: Ring Entrance [please do], state of mind, several spots, and reaction to win or loss. You can use the same ring entrance every week, that is fine. For state of mind, just tell us how your wrestler is going to approach the match and opponent [1 or
2 paragraphs is all we ask]. Spots, just give us a couple of move sequences you''d like to see your wrestler use to help us portray your character how you see him. Win/Loss Reaction [please say this obvious.]
-For Ring Entrances and Strats. The "play by play" announcer is the "Straight Arrow" Sammy Brooks [SB}. He calls everything straight down the middle w/ a wide spand of vocabulary. The commentators are the "General" Stan Patton [GP], he is an intimidating face commentator. The other is Archie Rictor [SB], a young out spoken punk kid. Plays favoritism towards heels.
-If for some reason the link to the role-play board doesn't work on the page then you can find the ROLE PLAY BOARD at http://users3.cgiforme.com/pwp/cfmboard.html -All in ring work and strats go to [detail withheld] unless you are notified otherwise by Matt, Dave, or Corey. NEWS & RUMORS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -The Vortex title has been rumored to carry special stipulations. However, it is not a hard-core title but rather more of a television title to be defended weekly. If anyone remember the Ozark Mountain title from the MWA it is some what like that but not as insane.
-Rumor has it several former NWC World Champions will begin showing up in Paradise Wrestling. In fact, expect to see a big return this week on Vortex. Who will it be? Nobody is quite sure but PWP will never be the same.
-A four man mini tournament is being constructed to crown the first PWP Thunder champion. The participants have been randomly selected and the first match is on Vortex this week.
-The first PWP PPV will most likely occur in mid July. We have a ongoing PPV entitled Trouble in Paradise. It is our historical PPV that will occur roughly every eight weeks. Each one will carry a fitting sub title that will sum up the angles occurring at the time.
Expect this PPV to be announced officially in the upcoming weeks. -President David A. Gordon, Dexter Irons, and [name withheld] are, as expected, are all ready having conflicts with one another. Who will be the voice of PWP? Who will be the "behind the scenes" man? Who will be the enforcer? Only time will tell....
