Final Thoughts\Closing Statement 9. Application 1. What is Paradise Wrestling Promotions? Paradise Wrestling Promotions [PWP], is a regional fed based out of the state of Florida in the United States. It came about when three men put their money together to turn one of the most enjoyable states in the US into Hell. Yes, we're driving out the hold folks and bringing in some wrestling that will take the world by storm. Gators, hurricanes, and even seminoles! Florida has it all but now it finally will have something that it can brag about on a consistent basic with
Paradise Wrestling Promotions. 2. Who runs Bluegrass State Wrestling? Paradise Wrestling Promotions is run by three people. [name withheld], two time president of Sin City Championship Wrestling and handler of the cyberneticpenis God Ebola Zaire. [name withheld]. Former Rocky

All of the feds above have been a part of the National Wrestling Council in which is a future goal of ours to join. We fully support the National Wrestling Council as handlers and former presidents.
3. What can I do to be successful in PWP? a. Angles PWP prides themselves on creativity at all times. Angles are one of the keys to not only being successful but making your stay in PWP the most memorable. We are extremely open to ideas from you, the handlers, on where your characters go. Afterall, it's your character and you have the final say as to what your roll in PWP will be. However, we are more then willing to speak with you through email and AIM to keep your interest in PWP high and with fresh ideas throughout your career.
Wrestler X attacking Wrestler Y is not entertaining. We in PWP like to take it to the next level and we want to take you with us with interesting feuds, original characters, and most of all the key to the
PWP, that "c" word, creativity. b. Roleplays Just like all the NWC feds, we use a message board system. We feel it allows the handler to RP when it is convenient for them. Why? Well not everyone lives in one state or even one country for that matter.
Who wants a 12:00am for role-plays when that is 8:00am for some of you. However, spamming the boards is not recommended for we don't want you posting three RP's an hour before the deadline. As of this moment we are enforcing a THREE roleplay cap on all live events EXCEPT for
PPV's which will have a cap of FOUR [subject to change]. You don't have to RP three times for any match unless you choose too. We feel that anything surpassing three RP's turns into handlers stretching for ideas and we begin to see a lot of repetition which becomes dull. You will always have an adequate amount of time to RP for your match and deadlines will be posted when the card is released.
c. Strats I know some of you hate writing them. Yes, they can be a drag can't they? But, we -need- them. They don't have to be 45k in length. They can be as long or as short as you want them to be. They are not nearly as big of a factor as RPing but could make the difference in a -tight-
RP war. All we ask is you include information on your character like how you will approach a match, ring entrance, and some spots you'd like to see your character perform in their match. Why you ask? It honestly takes a load of those who help us write matches. Not everyone knows your character as well as you do. Infact, nobody does. We in
PWP feel that by sending the writer information on your wrestler other then your Roster Page that it gives us more to work with. We don't want every match to be the same, do you? In strats you may also include in-rings, backstage bits, all angle work, attacks, etc. We recommend that if you don't have a match you try and put together some sort of in ring/backstage bit to keep your character as active as possible. In the end, it can only help and be beneficiary to you. Use the strat template linked off of the main page for every strat.
d. Card Participation It isn't necessary but it's a big help. I hope you don't expect one man to write every single match. If that was the way, every card would be late hands down. If you can only write a match at PPV's, that's fine. Every other week, great. Every week, excellent. Not a good match writer? We can start you out with lower tier matches and you'll learn. Sure, it's a pain in the butt sometimes but it's always appreciated. Another part of card participation is in-rings or backstage segments you write for your wrestler. They always help to spice the card up, and to get your wrestler noticed.
4. PWP Cards a. Vortex Vortex is the only show within PWP at this time. In the future, once the roster fills up a bit and it's needed, we may create an additional show that will be either a house show, or depending on handler participation, a second live show. Here is where all of your angles will come into play and the superstars of PWP will put up or shut up after tarring each other apart on the message boards for the last week. Being a regional fed, Vortex will take place within the confines of Florida on a consistent basis unless we decided to take the fed on tour. There is NO SUCH THING has an automatic push in PWP so don't come looking for one. This is our main show and if you work hard, you will see yourself on this event. It could have anywhere from 4-11 matches depending on how many of you work hard. We reward those who work, it's one of our policies. PWP will take place roughly every 8 days.
b. Pay Per Views The biggest shows of the year. They will happen every 6-8 weeks as we like to have time to build up the hottest angles and sweetest feuds.
We will do our best to make sure everyone has a match on a PPV and EVERY title will be defended on almost all occasions. Here is where the main angles will play out and new ones will begin. The
d. Super Vortex Since PPV's are every 6-8 weeks PWP will host Super Vortex’s usually 3- 4 weeks after a PPV and prior to the next one. Basically, it's the midway point to the PPV and from this point on you will see A LOT of angle work leading up to the actual PPV. You'll probably see the main title defended as well as other hot feuds.
5. Titles a. Paradise Heavyweight Championship Championship The most prestigious title PWP has to offer. Every wrestler who steps foot into a BSW dreams of the day they will once hold this title. Any wrestler can compete for it and it is the highest honor we have to offer. ONLY the best will ever strap this piece of gold around their waist, and earning a shot will not be an easy task. We are all capable of achieving it, it's just a question of who wants it the most.
b. PWP Thunder Title The secondary title in PWP or for some of you, the IC strap we have to offer. This is the title that will be held for the top-carders in
PWP. Just like the Heavyweight title, all wrestlers qualify to hold it. For some of you, this will be as far as you go in PWP but that is nothing to cry about. This title is an honor in itself and is known to be a stepping stone to the Heavyweight Title for those of you who are ready to take it too the next level.
c.PWP Lightning Title The cruiserweight title in PWP. There is a 235 pound weight limit on this belt but it has been known to be one of the most competitive cruiserweight belts for PWP's three presidents to lay a focus on. We pride ourselves in always maintaining a competitive cruiserweight division at all times.
d. PWP Vortex Title The Television title of PWP. It will be defended EVERY Vortex show and is the hardest title to hold onto for a lengthy period of time. Some of you will never even reach this accolade and give up trying. We don't see the television title as something to have lesser known wrestlers hold. PWP recognizes the Vortex title as the champions being the future of this organization.
e. Tag Team Championship The main and only tag-team title in PWP. Tag-team style wrestling from time to time will die off in the world of e-wrestling. We will do our best to keep a strong tag-team division at all times. Only the best will find themselves with these titles and it is no easy task. Some people think by joining a tag-team division they will make themselves known quickly. Not in PWP. Just like all titles, the challenger MUST defeat the champion on the boards and it is highly implied for tag- teams.
7. General Rules 1.) Flash at least once a week when given matches... It's not asking too much. If we notice you continue to no show for matches without advance notice you will be given the boot. There is no such thing as a low card in PWP as any and all dead weight will be replaced immediately. We understand that from time to time something comes up in the "real world." Let us know, that's all we ask.
2.) If you have a match, please strat. It doesn't have to be anything big. Like I said it makes it easier on our writers.
Honestly, it doesn't really take that long. If you use the same strat for every match then that's fine. It's better then nothing at all.
3.) Swearing is tolerable in PWP as it is on WWF or WCW from time to time but don't go overboard. If your character feels the need to swear then edit it... F[bleep], s[bleep], etc... Racial slurs are not acceptable and WILL NOT be tolerated. Warnings will be given, but only once. After that it's the pink slip for you.
4.) No breaking kayfabe in flashes or no shooting on another handler. While backstage politics is fine, I don't want a grudge between handlers breaking out amongst their characters in the fed.
It's very unrealistic, and we will not tolerate it. 8. Final Thoughts\Closing Statement Let me just touch up on something’s we didn't cover or wish to imply one more time. The PWP isn't for everyone. Some of you may not like how we operate or our unique and creative style, that's fine. But for those of you who are ready to step threw the doors and into the PWP we welcome you with open arms. However, leave the bitching and whining at home, we don't want it here. 50% of people in life are winners, 50% are losers, it's a golden rule in e-wrestling. Losing happens to
EVERYONE. If you are looking to set some unbreakable winning streak go ahead. Yes, go ahead and do it somewhere else. There will be times where you feel you should have won, that's fine. If you have questions on a decision approach us in a professional manner and we will do our best to explain to you why we chose as we did. We like to think we are not bias in any form or fashion at anytime.
We are hear for fun and to creative an enjoyable time for everyone. We don't claim to be the best fed but we will do our best to try and create a place that everyone loves to be in. We encourage handlers to work together threw AIM and emails to create a friendly environment.
If you are stuck on angle ideas, talk to us, we are hear for you. Like I mentioned, dedication is the key. If you are willing to meet our list of requirements that are far from being far fetched and unattainable, then don't join. It's that simple. Below is our application. If PWP suits your needs, and we hope it does, please fill it out and we will get back to with our decision [either way] as soon as possible.
