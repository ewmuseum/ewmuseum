A short-lived regional federation whose own debut world title tournament also marked its close, its results reaching print only in a wrestler's own after-the-fact roleplay. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Rhode Island Professional Wrestling Federation opened on 7 August 1999 behind a president who introduced himself, in the promotion's own handbook, as the wrestler Alex Cote, promising a fairer alternative to what he called “a serious injustice in a fed of mine”: “You have a match and you work hard enough for it, you will win, fairly, plain and simple.”

Its handbook set two weekly cards — a full live show, Magnificent Monday Night, and a smaller, mostly-summarized Shakin' Da Foundation Tour — feeding six titles: World, New England, Rhode Island, Lightweight, Hardcore, and World Tag Team.

Its record ends on a World Heavyweight Championship tournament held 22 August 1999. “Fabulous” Freddy Floyd wrote a victory promo for the occasion, naming rivals Ethan Love, Tommy Lionheart, and Kenneth Morlock as men he had already beaten and taking aim at James “Simply The Worst” Pillowbag, a fighter Alex Cote had brought in specifically to stop him, and promising the cameras he would carry the belt “into the next millennium.”

The promotion folded before that promo ever aired. Its results still went out after the fold, and by the roleplay's own writer's account, Freddy Floyd came away from the tournament not with the World Championship he had promised the cameras, but with the RIPWF New England Championship.
