"Fabulous" Freddy Floyd RP for now Defunct RIPWF

25 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
. Hello all, This is an RP i did for RIPWF, it never got sent out casue the fed closed, the results were sent out and I did win the New England Championship. So please critique, let me know.
{ Scene - Outside of the RIPWF Arena } { Scene opens as the Rhode Island Professional Wrestling Federations camera crew pan through a crowd of fans out back of the arena. The fans carry signs and await the arrival of thier favorite superstars }
{ A Black Stretch Limosine makes it way around the corner and pulls up in front of the arena. The driver exits the limo quickly and makes his way to the back.
The fans hush with anticipation. } { The Driver opens the door to reveal "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd, who at the sight of all of the people puts a horrified look on his face. } { Freddy whispers something to the driver, who runs back to the drivers seat. The RIPWF camera pans into the limo and gets a shot of what appears to be a woman. She wears sunglasses and had a wrap over her head and face}
{ The Limosince quickly pulls away from the curb, as the furious Freddy Floyd makes his way up the steps, and into
the arena. The cameras follow him down the hall and into his dressing room where he allows them inside, and then slams the door. } "F" FF: "Alright listen up, and listen good. The Rhode Island Professional Wrestling Federation needs to understand two very important things. The first and foremost is that
I, like every human being, need some privacy once and and a while, and when i attempt to bring in an extremly important buisness associate, I get bombarded by a bunch of sign carrying freaks."
"And next on my agenda, is the Rhode Island Professional Wrestling Federation needs to understand that I don't take very kindly to surprises. The surprise I speak of is that the weak Alex Cote decided he couldn't handle the
RIPWF and its main event talent like myself, so he had to go out and get some help. And how convient that the it be the man I hate most in this business "Simply The
Wost" James Pillowbag. Pillow, I am going to make this real simple for your warped little mind to understand.
It goes like this, I am "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd. I am the single greatest talent that the Rhode Island Professional
Wrestling Federation has on its roster, and if you think for one second I'm gonna let you ruin this one for me like you have tried to do in the past, then your wrong
James. Your DEAD Wrong. And if it comes down to it, I will do whatever, and I do mean whatever it takes, to become the Champion of the World. Its my destiny, and quickly fuckin' frankly, its about time."
{ Freddy paces about the room for a second thinking over the things he has just said } "F" FF: "Now don't get me wrong. I understand that The Rhode Island Professional Wrestling Federation does have some talented wrestlers. And yes, guys like Ethan
Love, Tommy Lionheart, and Kenneth Morlock can go out there and get the job done inside the ring. But the fact of the matter is, I have already beaten them before, and proved my superior wrestling skills. So now, on this biggest night of my career I take it one step further and pin those same shoulders to the mat, except this time it means more, oh yes, so much more. It means that for the first time in over two years I will have a World
Heavyweight Championship around my waist."
"And I really do feel bad, becasue I know that this federation has a lot of great young guys. It just so happens that this great young guy, is better than all of the rest of the young guys, and that means one thing.
That when the night is over, I will be crowned Champion." "Can't you just see it, the ring clears, the smoke is settling, and the crowd comes to a slience of apreciation. A silence for they realize that standing before them is the greatest wrestler of all time. The man who above all else has proved his worth to this great sport in which we participate. And most of all a silence for the man in which all of the morons out there finally respect, and
love. Then the announcer takes his place center ring and officially announces it to the whole world that "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd has won the World Heavyweight Championship." { Freddy runs his hand through his hair, and then he sits down on the couch on the far wall }
"F" FF: "I don't mean to get all worked up and everything here people, but I don't think you understand the utter importance of this event. In one night I am going to complete my legacy, my secret weapon will be by my side, and James Pillow will be forced to watch me take home the gold that I so richly deserve. This one night will forever shape the rest of the Rhode Island
Professional Wrestling Federation's history. Becasue the man who walks out the winner on Sunday, is the man who is going to take this federation into the next millenium."
"Now before I kick you losers out of here, I want everyone out in telivision land to just close there eyes for a second. Think long and hard about the RIPWF's roster, think of all of the familiar faces you know. . .
Now picture "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd standing in the center of the ring with the belt hoisted high up into the air as the winner. Now go ahead and open your eyes, becasue this is exactly what your going to see on Sunday. Now get those camera's out of here!"
{ Freddy Floyd stands up as the camera man quickly turns around and leaves the dressing room area as the scene fades to black }
