The WAIWA: Join the Revolution

28 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings E-wrestlers, Allow me to introduce myself.I am 'Xtreme'Robbie Greer,the president of the WAIWA.I am offering an invitation to E-wrestlers who are ready and wanting to join the uprising known as the WAIWA.The WAIWA has been open since April of 1996,and it's been going strong.Many of the greats of the E-wrestling world are in the WAIWA,such as
Radiation,Rock Ramon and Laughing Matter,are in this federation.We have two weekly shows(Monday Massacre,Thursday Night Warriors),full of interviews,announcing and suprises you wouldn't expect ever.I do use
TNM6.2+,but roleplaying helps your chances of winning.We have had a bunch of PPV's(Survivors Slamboree,Summersplash,August Anarchy,Fall
Frenzy,Fright Night,The Great Thanksgiving Fest,Christmas Clash comes out tomorrow),and many of our wrestlers scored on the RSPWF awards(Jenny Shards 6th manager,Robbie Greer 8th highflyer,Dojo of the
Risng Sun,Hellraiser,Psycho).If you like what you hear,Mail me and I'll send you a card to look over,and if you like it,I'll send you the application.If your saying "I wanna join now,dammit!!!",go to our homepage at
http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/8311/WAIWA.html,and click the link to the interactive form.Thank you,and please consider joining the WAIWA. Wrestling Advantage CEO, Robbie Greer
