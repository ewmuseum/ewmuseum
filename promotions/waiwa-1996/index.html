Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WAIWA introduced itself on the newsgroup on 28 December 1996 with a recruitment post signed “Wrestling Advantage CEO, Robbie Greer,” who called himself “the president of the WAIWA” and dated the promotion's own opening to that April: “The WAIWA has been open since April of 1996, and it's been going strong.”

Its own account names two weekly shows, Monday Massacre and Thursday Night Warriors, run on the TNM6.2+ simulation engine — though, in Greer's own words, “roleplaying helps your chances of winning” — and a year's worth of named pay-per-views: Survivors Slamboree, Summersplash, August Anarchy, Fall Frenzy, Fright Night, The Great Thanksgiving Fest, and a Christmas Clash the post promised for the following day. It named Radiation, Rock Ramon and Laughing Matter among its roster's greats, and pointed to a fan-voted year-end awards ballot as proof of the company's own standing: Jenny Shards placed sixth among managers, Greer himself eighth among high-flyers, and Dojo of the Rising Sun, Hellraiser and Psycho all placed besides.

The post itself was an open call: mail Greer for a sample card, and a favorable look earned an application, with a homepage hosted on Geocities carrying, in its own words, “the link to the interactive form” for anyone ready to join outright. How WAIWA fared after that isn't in the surviving record — no card, no result, and no further word survives beyond this one recruitment post.
