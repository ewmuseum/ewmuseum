EWN Warzone Results, 28 July 1999

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://welcome.to/ewn for the main EWN page EWN Warzone results for July 28, 1999 1) Stan Grubb beat Chris Benoit by disqualification in 0:07:29 when Justin Credible came out of the crowd with a cane and began hitting
Grubb with it. The New Age Four Horsemen came out for the save, only to have Credible leave through the crowd.
2) Hunter Hearst Helmsley pinned Dean Malenko with the Pedigree in 0:06:04. 3) Scott Levy pinned Al Snow with the Cashflow DDT in 0:05:35. 4) Balls Grizzly beat Jizz and Gravedigger when Grizzly pinned Gravedigger after a chair shot at 0:06:08 in a Hardcore Triple Threat
UWA Match. 5) Hardy Boys beat Too Cool when Jeff Hardy pinned Scott Taylor in 0:07:38 to retain the North American Tag Team Titles after Mark Henry & D. Lo Brown interfered. -) Interview with Ric Flair, Roddy Piper, Arn Anderson, & Charles Robinson Flair says that tonight he's got a big surprise for Goldberg when they lockup that will ensure him victory. Brings out Asya. He says that that won't be all because earlier today he had his son David flown in for tonight's event. Calls him out as he comes down and enters the ring along with Torrie Wilson. Ric tells Goldberg that if he wants a piece of him, he'll have to go through his son first. Everybody looks at Ric in surprise as Steve Austin comes out. Austin tells Flair that a month ago he tried to weasel his way out of a match but failed, and tonight would be no different. Austin proceeds to tell him that if he doesn't wrestle tonight against Goldberg, he'll be suspended along with the five others with him.
6) Lance Storm pinned Spike Dudley with the Tornado DDT in 0:05:38. 7) Bart Gunn pinned Val Venis with a backslide in 0:06:03 to retain the International Title. 8) New Age Outlaws beat Dallas Page & Bam Bam Bigelow when Billy Gunn pinned Page with the Fame-ass-er in 0:07:05. Afterwards, Kanyon came out and along with Page & Bigelow ganged up on the Outlaws.
9) Vader pinned Mark Henry with the Vader Bomb in 0:05:48. 10) Randy Savage pinned Jeff Jarrett with the Big Elbow in 0:07:02. Afterwards, Debra McMichael distracted Savage as Jarrett attacked him from behind. Gorgeous George then pulled Debra off the apron as the two began fighting outside the ring as Jarrett tried to seperate them only to get slapped by George.
-) Interview with The New Age Four Horsemen Stan Grubb says that Justin Credible made the biggest mistake of his life tonight. Darion Collins says that to avoid any issues with
Credible, he'll settle the score tonight. Challenges him to a Hardcore match. Credible comes out and tells him the only way he'll accept the challenge is if Collins puts the North American Title on the line. Grubb interrupts and accepts the challenge saying Collins will defend the belt and win. Collins began arguing with Grubb, only to be reassured by Grubb that they'd have his back if it looked like he were in trouble at any point.
11) Test pinned Ken Shamrock with his own feet on the ropes in 0:08:24. 12) Dudley Boys beat Sabu & Rob Van Dam when Buh-Buh Ray Dudley pinned Van Dam after the Dudley Deathdrop in 0:06:49. 13) Madusa pinned Jenna Anderson with the German Suplex in 0:05:27 to capture the Women's Title. Afterwards Justine came out and punched
Madusa rendering her unconscious.
14) Bret Hart made Chris Candido submit to the Sharpshooter in 0:06:28. 15) Bill Goldberg pinned Ric Flair with the Jackhammer in 0:04:46. Afterwards, Roddy Piper, Arn Anderson, David Flair, Charles Robinson, &
Asya entered the ring and started beating on Goldberg. Ken Shamrock came out, but was quickly mauled down as well. Ric began taunting Goldberg as the others continued beating him down. He took out a pair of handcuffs, only to have Goldberg quickly outmaneuver him and handcuff Ric to the ropes. Goldberg & Shamrock then cleared the ring leaving Flair handcuffed to the ropes all alone. Ric unlocked them and tried to leave but was stopped by Shamrock. When he turned around to run away, Goldberg speared him.
-) Interview with Chris Jericho Says he's back in the EWN and with one purpose. Calls out Reckless
Youth. Youth comes down as Jericho attempts to shake his hand, but is denied. Says he holds no hard feelings against Youth for what happened earlier this year because the past is the past. Youth says he still hasn't forgotten all the crap that Jericho has put him through ever since he can remember. Jericho tells him that he shouldn't hold a grudge this time because he has something important to tell him. Announces that at the next PPV, Youth will defend the Light belt against Lance Storm, but that's not all. Storm then came in from behind as the two began doubleteaming Youth. After officials seperated them, Jericho announced he'd be the guest referee.
16) Justine pinned Perry Saturn in 0:07:37 to capture the Extreme Title after Jenna Anderson distracted the referee allowing Shawn Michaels to interfere and give Saturn Sweet Chin Music.
17) Justin Credible pinned Darion Collins with That's Incredible in 0:06:45 in a Hardcore match to capture the North American Title. 18) Scott Andrews beat Shawn Michaels & Christian Idol when Andrews pinned Michaels with the Deep Freeze Drop in 0:15:12 in a non-title
Triple Threat match after Hunter Hearst Helmsley came out and attacked
Idol outside the ring. As a result of a prematch stipulation, Michaels must now retire. Afterwards, Michaels, Stan Grubb, Justine, & Jenna
Anderson began beating on Andrews until Justin Credible & Perry Saturn came in with canes to make the save.
19) Steve Austin & Paul Wight beat Jack Victory & Rod Price when Wight pinned Victory with the Chokeslam in 0:08:43 to retain the World Tag
Team Titles. 20) Undertaker beat Kane in a Crucifixion match in 0:14:16 to retain the World Title after Undertaker tied the unconscious Kane to the cross after help from the Acolytes and hoisted it upright. Afterwards, the
Acolytes poured gasoline around the cross and Undertaker was about to light it on fire when Scott Andrews & Bart Gunn made the save. Kane was able to escape and along with Andrews & Gunn cleared the ring. The three had a brief staredown before Kane walked away as the show went off the air.
