Broadcast, Distribution of News & Notes NEWS --------- - Congratulations go to "Son of a Bitch" Sam Hill, the first ever PWC Puerto Rican Heavyweight Champion and first-ever PWC title-holder. However, apparently, Hill's reign as champion isn't going to be an easy one as he's already gained the attention of the giant "Colossal" Ron Walker as well as every other man without a title in PWC. Good luck, champ . . . you'll need it. - It's official - PWC has aligned with Southern Championship Wrestling [SCW]. SCW is one of the top leagues in the business today and it is PWC's distinct pleasure to be working with them. Details on the connection will be given as they are hammered out. - Oliver Santiago and the PWC Board of Directors have approved instatement of the PWC First Blood Heavyweight Championship. Santiago announced that the title will be filled in a four corners, no- disqualification match on the July 4th edition of Legacy!. Santiago went on to name three of the four participants - Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin, "Mr. Self-Destruct" Malcolm Lyle and "Tabloid" Trent Regan; the fourth participant will be named on the evening of the event. The dynamics of the match are simple - you bleed, you leave. - "Mr. Self-Destruct" Malcolm Lyle has confronted Oliver Santiago about the enforcement of a stipulation of his contract. According to Lyle's contract, ANY and ALL matches with him must have no-disqualification rules. Santiago's lawyers found this to, in fact, be the case and, as a result, ANY and ALL matches with Lyle WILL be no-disqualification. - After Lyle's heinous attack on him coupled with the armbreaker he received via "Britain's Nightmare" Alexander Lawson, "Big Time" Bobby Baker will be sidelined indefinitely. - The roster here at PWC continues to expand with the recent addition of three top notch singles competitors - Ezekiel Madras, a young cruiserweight from Washington state; "Mean" Mark Mease, an old style Texas brawler; and the giant "Colossal" Ron Walker. The experienced duo of Ian "The Highlander" Finley and Nightshade have also brought their talents to PWC in the form of a tag team known as The Underground.
