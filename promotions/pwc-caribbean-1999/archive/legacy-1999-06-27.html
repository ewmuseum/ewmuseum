[SANCHEZ]: "Nice gesture by these two stellar performers before things get started. A nice change of pace after all of the chaos and animosity that's been prevalent in this edition of Legacy! . . ."
<<The two lock up. Hill backs Lawson into the ropes. The referee calls for the break. Hill breaks cleanly.>>
[SANCHEZ]: "Clean break by Hill. This is going to be an old-fashioned traditonal wrestling match from the looks of things."
[RASLO]: "Yeah . . . until one of them thinks it's time to turn up the juice." <<Another collar-and-elbow tie-up. Lawson slips out and executes a standing waistlock. [SANCHEZ]: "Lawson wisely backing Hill into the ropes forcing him to break the hold . . . Another clean break between the two . . ."
[RASLO]: "I hope this nice guy act these two are putting on wins them both an Academy Award 'cause it's not doing anything for me. Of course, for them to get the award, they'd have to stand in line with
Sweger and Madras."

Otherwise, you can pretty much continue to hate him like I do."
[SANCHEZ]: "Both men apparently trying to immobilize the other man's right arm. That strategy was very successful for Lawson early in the night as he more than likely broke Bobby Baker's arm in the semi-final round."

<<Hill picks up Lawson again. Lawson shoots for Hill's legs, taking him down to the mat. Lawson quickly steps in and executes a figure four.>>
[SANCHEZ]: "FIGURE FOUR BY LAWSON IN THE CENTER OF THE RING!" <<Lawson wrenches back on the figure four, grabbing the toe Hill's boot to increase the pressure. Hill reaches out his hand in pain as the referee gets into position.>>

[SANCHEZ]: "Figure four locked on tightly on Hill . . . But from what
I've seen from Hill, he's not somebody you're going to get to quit.
