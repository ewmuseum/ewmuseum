[NAPWO] New Promotion Looking for Great Roleplayers

18 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The North American Professional Wrestling Organization is just about to start up it's weekly cards. However, first we need members. Good roleplayers only need apply. You probably want to know something about the NAPWO. We're based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada at the NAPWO Arena, a 7,300 seat building designed for the purpose of professional wrestling events. There is one show per week called NAPWO Stampede on Sunday Nights, and it will be broadcast over local Canadian cable TV. After a few months, the show may be carried by a major channel in the US, but for now, it will be shown on the Prime Networks and on MSG, live, instead of taped. (just a fictional set-up) There will be three titles to contend for in the NAPWO, the World Title, the North American Title, and the World Tag Team Titles. The staff of the NAPWO consists of three people - myself, [name withheld] (match writer), and my vice-president [detail withheld] . All three of us will write matches and myself a TPham123 will do the booking. Again, only good roleplayers need apply. You can visit our website, which is still under some construction, at http://www.angelfire.com/ny/napwo/index.html for more information. Adam Thompson ( [detail withheld] ) CEO of North American Sports (NAS, the fictional Parent Company of the NAPWO)
