IMWA Preview — 2 February 1999

3 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

["One" by Three Dog Night starts to play as the darkness fizzles into the IMWA logo. The logo then separates down the middle and opens away like doors. The camera shows the IMWA Studio. The studio is dark and the camera shows a shot from behind a camera, a man's fingers signaling "two" then "one." Illuminate.
The background is of brick walls. There is a jumbo screen on the back wall and it is showing the IMWA logo. Lastly, two men are sitting behind a large news desk. The man on the left of the screen is Dean Grass. He's a white male in his early thirties, but looks to be in his late twenties. He is wearing a black suit and black tie with a white shirt. His black hair is parted to the side. His partner, sitting to the right of the screen, is Sean Demos. He is a tall, slender white male with mid-length, blonde hair and sparkly blue eyes.
Hs is dressed in jeans, a black cowboy hat, a black T-shirt with the IMWA logo printed in the middle of it, and, of course, a brown suede jacket.]
[Camera moves in closer as they fiddle with some papers. The camera labels each man as who they are. "One" fades.]
DG: Good evening, everyone, and welcome to the first edition of IMWA Preview. I am the host with the most, Dean Grass, and I will be . . . [Sean clears his throat.] DG: [Correcting himself] . . . We will be bringing you news, updates and previews of what is to come in the premier week of the IMWA.
The purpose of this show is to keep you, the viewers, the fans, the people who make it possible for us to bring the world quality stars in quality matches.
SD: [Quickly starts to talk before Dean can continue.] Dean, you are so excited about premier week that you forgot to introduce me.
Folks, I am Sean Demos, CO-host of this show. The IMWA is here and I am part of the team that will bring this federation quickly through the rise of the ladder of success and keep it at the top.
DG: Well, you are right about being part of the team . . . SD: What was that? DG: Nothing. SD: Oh . . . Anyway, the IMWA brings to you, the public, a show that informs you on what is going on in the IMWA. We tell you about what matches are going to take place, what we think about the competitors, and then we give our predictions on them.
DG: However, Sean, you cannot forget that the matches and announcements on scheduled matches are only a part of what this show is about.
SD: You're right, Dean. We bring you up-to-the-minute news on what is going on in the IMWA. On IMWA Preview, we have such segments as:
Rewind, Schedule for the week's upcoming matches, and any other news that breaks in the IMWA.
DG: With the IMWA's inception, we hope to enter with a hard and heavy impact and we hope to show the world of wrestling that there is another big-time player that is rising quickly, that player, that force, that being . . . is IMWA. In the IMWA, we thrive on bringing you, the viewers, the best matches and best events that we possibly can. I have been associated with federations before and none of them have seemed half as big as the IMWA is and will be.
SD: Dean, what people need to realize is that, as much hype as there is, as much as the IMWA is talked about, as much as we talk about how great the IMWA is, we must face reality and realize that the
IMWA is young. Hell, it's brand new. Therefore, we need to establish ourselves quickly. We aren't like _other_ federations who open up and then close down almost as fast. No! We are a supreme legion of hard-working people in the wrestling business.
Think of the IMWA as a work horse. The IMWA will work well, work hard, and work constantly in order to bring the fans, the viewers what they want to see . . . action.
DG: Since the IMWA is yet to have their premier event, we have no "Rewind" segment for you this week. Next week, once the first event has taken place, we will debut our "Rewind" segment.
However, since we have some time, we can tell you about what goes on in the "Rewind" portion of IMWA Preview.
SD: In this portion of IMWA Preview, what we do is we look back at what went down the previous Saturday on Zero Hour. We reflect on any key events and speculate on what may come out of them as a result.
DG: That's a good way of putting it, Sean. SD: [Grins] Thank you. DG: Since this week is a special, shortened edition of IMWA Preview, we must keep the ball rolling and continue on with the news.
|砝|秤\ /秤||砝 __ |砝|秤秤砝 | || \/ || |/ \| || |砝 |......................................... | || |\__/| || /\ || |砝 | Zero Hour |_||_| |_||__/ \__||_| |_| Coming: Saturday, February 6 ....................................................................... 1. Main Event: Ethan de Sade vs. "Hurricane" Mark Winters 2. Tag Team Match: 187 vs. Dazed and Confused 3. Cruiserweight Match: Andrew "Flash" Tucker vs. "Homicidal" Jarrett Scott Other Matches: 4. "Just" Dave Hamrick vs. "The Lethal Injection" Darren Scott 5. "Brass Knux" Jason Williams vs. Justin McKagen 6. Scott "Skatin" Richards vs. "The God Damn Show" Tim Beck 7. Devon Case vs. Hugo Burns [J] 8. The Abortionist vs. Super Lazy [J] SD: As you can see, this is just the start of the IMWA. This is the line-up for the first event, the premier, the debut, the inception, the coronation, the genesis to all that is IMWA and this will certainly not be the apocalypse by any means. We plan on going straight to the top with only a few obstacles that stand in the way.
DG: Zero Hour is looking to be a fine event and I personally cannot wait to see these men all get into it and give the fans the action that they came to see.
SD: The main event is going to be a blockbuster match. DG: Sean, I couldn't agree with you more on this. Ethan de Sade, a star in many other federations, makes his way into the IMWA.
However, he has no easy task as he stars in the main event. When there is a main event, we don't have a star face somebody who just has no skills. Hell no! Ethan de Sade is dealt with the task of facing a man who has showed plenty of promise. He is facing a man who is oozing talent. He is facing the fury of a "Hurricane."
That's right, folks. I am talking about "Hurricane" Mark Winters. SD: Dean, you nailed that right on the head. Mark Winters is a promising young star and I am sure that he will put Ethan de Sade to the test in what I know will be a blockbuster of a match.
DG: The IMWA is proud to have the myriad of stars that it does. Ethan de Sade and Mark Winters are two of those many. Two other stars of the IMWA have to be the two men facing off this weekend.
First, we have "Just" Dave Hamrick. Second, we have "The Lethal Injection" Darren Scott. SD: Dave Hamrick, a plain man who just doesn't get all flashy and doesn't care what people think about it, will face off against a man who people seem to love as does trouble, Darren Scott.
DG: Both of these competitors have shown us a lot of promise during their stay in the IMWA Training Camp. In preliminary matches, at the camp, both men were most impressive and therefore, we signed them and now are bringing them to you as one of the greatest matches of the evening, Dave Hamrick against Darren Scott. Meat and Potatoes versus Flashy. It is going to be great.
SD: This match will definitely be one of many of what will be a great event. However, I can't seem to pick a winner at this juncture.
DG: I can't pick a winner either, Sean. But what an event this will be. Another match that is looking to be exciting is a match pitting the team 187 against Dazed and Confused.
SD: This match is one that gets the adrenaline rush going. This match will show what the IMWA has to offer in the form of tag team action. This match will, without a doubt, be one that will go down in history.
DG: Yes, it will, Sean. Let's start off by talking about 187. 187 is a team comprised of two thugs who both served time in the State
Penn in Florida. SD: It was just before that when the duo met, am I correct? DG: Yes, that's . . . SD: Good. Anyway, both of these guys were poor and troubled and still are. Basically, they lived gang lives and showed the world that gang life, no matter who gets entangled into it, is bad and leads to a disgraceful and pathetic life.
DG: Sean . . . SD: And furthermore, I think that their punk, thug attitudes are going to backfire on them and get them in trouble! [Stands] I am telling you punks, right off the bat, that I hate you and hope you guys lose this match on Zero Hour!
[Dean holds onto Sean and brings him back into his seat.] SD: What?! DG: Sit down and shut up! Jesus . . . Grow up and let whatever happens happen. It's not like they did anything to you. They can't help that you are a rich, high-class snob.
SD: What do you mean? I'm I snob? Well, partner, you should talk. DG: I am and I am telling you that you are a snob. Deal with it. You brought it upon yourself. I personally think that 187 is going to come off with the victory on Zero Hour and then come after you for the remarks that you furiously uttered a few minutes ago.
SD: Yeah, whatever . . . DG: Next up is a match from yet another devision of the IMWA. The Cruiserweight devision is a devision of lightweights, mostly comprising of high-fliers. However, from what I hear about Tim
Beck and Scott Richards and their past, this match will be nothing less than a suicidal, anti-gravity war that will shock the crowd and leave them amazed at the end.
[Sean seems calm.] SD: Dean, I hate to say it, but I agree with you. Beck and Richards are two accomplished atheletes. I have heard plenty about both, but I have not seen them in action. However, if this match is all that it is hyped up to be, them we are in for a treat . . . and a war.
DG: Good call. If this match is as good as it is speculated to be, and if it is any sign as to what we are to expect in the IMWA
Cruiserweight devision, then the IMWA is heading in the right path and is heading for success in this vast world and business known as wrestling.
SD: While we are on the subject of cruiserweights, there are two other cruiserweight matches on tap for Zero Hour. First, a man who has made a name for himself just about everywhere he has gone. He is none other than former EMWC star, former NEW star, INWI star and star of many other places that are just not the IMWA. [Grins] He is here, in the IMWA, and we can't wait to see how he stands as he faces a man who claims to be the most homicidal maniac in the world of professional wrestling. He is "Homicidal" Jarrett Scott!
DG: Fans, let's note that Jarrett Scott is of no relation to Darren Scott. SD: At least, that's to the best of our knowledge. [Both men smile.] DG: You never know. Anyway, this match is looking to be promising as two more stars go at it in a high-flying, all-out war that will be all over air and land. This is another match that I, Dean Grass, am looking forward to. I can't seem to predict the winner of this match. I guess I'll have to see how each competitor does in their debut matches to really tell what they are capable of.
SD: I'll tell you, I have seen Andrew Tucker wrestle before. He has wrestled many stars. We will have to see how he does against
Jarrett Scott. We will see how "Homicidal" does against "Flash." DG: That will definitely be a match to see. And that is not the last of the cruiserweight matches as another up and coming star named
Devon Case take on a man who has been known quite well in the IMWA Training Camp by the name of Hugo Burns. Let me tell you that this match is going to be wild.
SD: Well, Hugo Burns has trained the cruiserweights in the IMWA and adapted them to the expectations of the IMWA. Hugo Burns is a tough, tough competitor. I have never seen him compete. However, he has a reputation in the minor leagues as being a very respected wrestler and ring general. I am sure that his experience will play a big part in this match against Devon Case.
DG: Devon Case looks to be a star in the making. Devon Case versus Hugo Burns? I'd give the edge to Case. That's my pick for this match. SD: I'll have to agree with you again. However, only time will tell us who the winner will be.
DG: Let's move on and take a look at a match that was just announced before the show. Let's talk about the match with "Brass Knux"
Jason Williams facing off against Justin McKagen. SD: Yes, Dean, let's talk about this match. I have a feeling that people are going to be in for a treat as Jason Williams makes his way into the IMWA to face a man who comes from the IMWA Training
Camp. A man by the name of Justin McKagen will be the opponent for Jason Williams. I have a feeling that Jason Williams will be underestimating Justin since Justin hasn't been talked about as much as ole "Brass Knux."
DG: I have a feeling that "Brass Knux" knows exactly what he is getting himself into. I have seen this man go into fights, into matches without knowledge of his opponent. He has come out on top in most of those cases and I would not be surprised if he came out on top here. I predict that Jason Williams will come out on top in this one.
SD: Woah, woah, woah! I wouldn't go so fast there, Dean. DG: What? I was just . . . SD: Jumping to conclusions? Yes, I know. Y'see, you can't count Justin McKagen out this early because you haven't heard much about him. As a matter of fact, he happens to be a great talent. I won't be surprised if he pulls the victory out in this one.
DG: You are absolutely right. However, I am sticking with my prediction. I just feel that in order to gain my utmost respect, the wrestler must show himself and prove himself with the ability that he has.
SD: I'll agree with the reasoning, however, I am going with Justin McKagen on this one. DG: I guess everyone is entitled to have their own opinions. SD: First amendment. [Smiles] DG: And last of all of these matches is a match between a huge man named Super Lazy who is amazingly large and a man we know as The
Abortionist. SD: The Abortionist? We signed a wrestler by the name of "The Abortionist?" Who in their right mind would do that? DG: I guess he needed to find another profession after his license was revoked. I am not even going to get into the details of his attire.
SD: Thank you, Dean. We can only imagine . . . [Both men laugh.] DG: We'll just have to see what happens with this match. I can't predict this one. SD: To tell you the truth, I can't predict this one either. As a matter of fact, I don't want to be around to see this fat, buldging man face this sick, bloody bastard. Cancel this match!
DG: Sean, calm down. Just calm down. SD: Why should I calm down? I see a match like this signed for Zero Hour and it makes me want to not show up. DG: I am sure that it will be a great match and I am sure that it will be wild. SD: It'll be wild. However, I don't want to watch this. DG: How about if your job depends on it? SD: Hmm . . . I guess I'd have to grin and bear it. DG: There you go. Because if you don't do the show then I am sure that you will be looking for a job elsewhere. Heh . . . I remember when I was warned with that back in another federation.
Ah, yes. It was something. SD: Didn't Ian Walsh kick the crap out of you in NAW? DG: Well, I guess, if you want to put it that way. [Sean laughs] But I provoked him and a lot of tension was in the air. SD: Tension, right . . . [Dean gives a frustrated look.] DG: Anyway, this event is looking to be action-packed and that is what will launch the IMWA on its way to superstardom. It may take time, but the IMWA will get to the top and the IMWA will show all just who is the leader in sports today. Zero Hour will open a whole new horizon for the IMWA and it will showcase our talent in the way that only the IMWA knows how. Zero Hour . . . it'll be one hell of a show.
SD: You're right, Dean. IMWA will definitely show what they have when Zero Hour goes down. This will be the first edition of this weekly show and it will be the first event of the IMWA. Zero Hour
. . . it's the genesis, the inception, and the coronation. DG: The next arena event will be Wednesday, the tenth of February. No matches have been announced yet. However, I understand that the stars of the IMWA will be on hand for the IMWA Arena event as well as personel and the massive security guard known as Kodiak. Those guys and more as IMWA presents their second IMWA Arena event. It will be a great event.
SD: And for the report on what happens at the Wednesday IMWA Arena Shows, tune in on Wednesdays at nine PM Eastern Time. Check your local listings. IMWA Anarchy will premier and gives results of the IMWA Arena shows. It will be another weekly show for the
IMWA. Until then, tune in on Friday for Countdown to the Zero Hour and Saturday for the premier of Zero Hour. Both at nine PM Eastern Time on their respected days. DG: Folks, it looks as if we are out of time. Therefore, until next time, for Sean and the IMWA, I am Dean Grass saying . . .
SD: "Sean Demos cut me off again and I got my ass kicked by Ian Walsh!" DG: Argh! [Credits.
