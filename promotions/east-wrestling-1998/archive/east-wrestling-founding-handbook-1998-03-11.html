IWA Founding Notice

11 March 1998

_________ |___ ___| | | | | ___| |___ Internet |_________| ___ ___ ___ \ \/ \/ / \ \ / / \___/\____/ Wrestling ___ / \ / /_\ \ / __ \ Alliance /__/ \__ \ The IWA was formed on March 10th 1998. The IWA is DEDICATED to bringing the internet community the best e-wrestling action to date and blow the competiton away!
HOW DO I JOIN: Well, there are a couple requirements to be a part of the IWA. First off, the IWA is a booked fed, meaning no simulators. Matches are determined in the following fashion: 1/2: Roleplay content (what is written in the role-play) 1/4: Roleplay quality (How strongly and forcefully it was written) 1/8: Strategy (See below...) 1/8: Angles/Fueds/etc... ROLEPLAY'S ARE A MUST!!!! IF YOU CANNOT ROLEPLAY WITH QUALITY THEN DO NOT CONSIDER THE IWA FOR EMPLOYMENT!!!!!!! Strategys are usually sent in before the match and describe the characters actions. If no strategy is sent in to me (The Prez.), it is like your wrestler goes in unprepared. Here is a sample role-play:
Match: Mr X vs. Mr Y Mr. X comes to the ring in a cocky manner. Mr. X believes that this match is a waste of his talent and would rather face a champion. If Mr. Y get on the offensive, Mr. X will duck out of the ring and take a breather. Mr X's valey (Ms. Q) always has a loaded purse. Mr. X will only use it if in dire need. If Mr. X senses victory, he will attempt the "X-plex" for the win. Is that hard??? HOW DO I GET MATCHES?? two ways: 1) Create an Angle with another wrestler in a role-play. Don't be afraid to create your own angle!!! Write something: "Mr. X teamed with my father for years, and then turned on him! I'm here for revenge!". If your wrestler is sent something like this, just play along. It makes the fed more fun! 2) Send a request to the IWA Booking Committee! I like what I hear, and I WANT TO JOIN!!! The application is not yet ready, but it will be by this weekend! E-Mail me at [detail withheld] and request an app. I will get them out ASAP!!! Hope to hear from you soon IWA Prez, Sandman
