Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EAST Wrestling opened under the acronym IWA on 10 March 1998, “DEDICATED to bringing the internet community the best e-wrestling action to date and blow the competiton away.” It billed itself as a booked promotion rather than a simulator-run one, scoring each match by a fixed formula — half on roleplay content, a quarter on roleplay quality, and an eighth apiece on submitted strategy and ongoing angles.

Twelve days later it renamed itself EAST Wrestling and debuted under that name with Extreme Sunday Night, broadcast, in its own words, from the Omni in Atlanta before a crowd of 23,209. The card ran a string of matches that saw a wrestler billed as Mole beat Marc Alexander, then Czar, then The Swinger in succession to be crowned the first EAST World Heavyweight Champion by night's end.

The promotion's own president signed off each broadcast as the handler of a wrestler called Silver Bullet.

No document of EAST Wrestling later than that debut broadcast survives; how or when it closed is not known.
