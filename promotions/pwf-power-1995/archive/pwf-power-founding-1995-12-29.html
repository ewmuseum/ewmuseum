Hi all, this is HeathBar23 (Heath) asking you to join the newest of the wrestling leagues, Power Wrestling Federation (PWF) **********************CARDS***************************** There will AT LEAST one card a week. I hope to have more -Saturday Night Slamfest:This card will be out every week. It will be like WCW Saturday Night. -PWF Assault:This card will be sent out randomly. It will contain most info. on pay-per-views, and will be like WWF Mania w/ one main event -Power Explosion:Explosion will be like a mini pay-per-view. There will be no jobber matches and usually will have a title contest. I hope to have this out once or twice a month. -Pay-Per-Views:I hope to have one out every month. January's will be called Power WAR. *********************ANNOUNCERS************************* Bob Genlin-He will be the head announcer. A little partial toward face wrestlers Zeus-Yes, my wrestler Zeus will be the color man for Slamfest and Explosion. Loves heels. Larry Bowers-He will be the studio man and will be an announcer for Assault Skyhawk-After retiring once the NWWF fell apart, Skyhawk has signed on to be an interviewer Chad Smith-Ring Announcer and head referee *********************BELTS****************************** -PWF World Title:The most prestigious belt in the PWF. PWF World Title matches will only take place at Power Explosions and pay-per-views. -PWF American Title:This belt will truly be an American Title. No foreigners can win this belt. -PWF TV Title:The TV Title will be put up for grabs at every Slamfest, Power Explosion and pay-per-view event. Plus the champion gets to choose who he wrestles on every Slamfest -PWF World Tag Team Title:Goes to the top tag team -PWF Corporation Title:Every group in the PWF can fight this title. The whole group will represent this title. The title will be fought for in tag team matches *********************WRESTLERS************************** -You can have up to five singles and three tag teams -Two of your singles wrestlers may also form a tag team with another person's wrestler -Remember you must roleplay with ALL of your wrestlers -I will need an interview of some sort w\ your wrestlers stat sheet -Send all roleplays to ME FIRST not to the group *******************OTHER INFO*************************** -This federation will be run on WLS 5.1 so all matches will be fair -Only the finishes of most matches will be seen as to keep cards from getting too long -Roleplay will be mandatory your wrestler will lose stat points if you don't roleplay -Bringing in other people will raise your stat points but I have not decided how much -The first card will be a Power Explosion sent out Dec 30. It will decide the TV Champion ********************WHAT I NEED************************* -Name: -Weight: -Height: -Hometown: -Face or Heel: -Manager/Valet/Bodygaurd:(Optional) -Name of Finisher: -If it has a special name what move does it resemble: -How it is performed:(Where you are and where opponent is) -5 Specialty Moves: -Please divide 100 points between the following: -Strength: -Toughness: -Conditioning: -Intelligence: -Technical: -Aerial: -Cheating: -Mat Wrestling: -Brawling: -Quickness: -Teamwork: -Tell me your wrestlers personality: -What is he like: -How does he act in an interview: -Your Info: -Online name: -Real Name or Initials: -Age: -City, State: -Do you have any ideas that might improve the federation: **************************LAST THINGS******************* -There will be a tournament to decide the TV Champion -Size of TV tournament depends on number of entries -Deadline for entry is Dec. 29 at 3:30 -I would like to have a heel conduct an interview show on every Slamfest if you are interested in having your wrestler do this e-mail me -*****MY E-MAIL ADDRESS IS [detail withheld] ***** -Have Fun -[name withheld] Copyright 1995 PowerW Inc.
