PWF : The Addams Report

13 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Scene opens to a ruggedly handsome blonde haired man sitting at a desk with the Power Wrestling Federation logo behind him.** [Paul] Welcome PWF fans! I'm Paul Addams and this is the Addams Report. The purpose of this program is to help you keep up with the goings on in the Power Wrestling Federation and at the match I have the honor to host, Tuesday Night Fights. Lets start with some pre-recorded comments from Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal, The Machine! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ {The Machine stand in front of their WALL OF CONQUESTS} Paul: "The Modern-Day Goliath" David Graves has stuck by us when no one else would back us up. Now Robski, Drexler, and William "The Rich" Payne cost him the TV Title. David, if you want us either as partners or just to back you up in the corner, say the word. Simon: Payne also has those overgrown thugs, Mace and Dreggs, in his stable. I say we take them out, on the principle of removing any trash in the local area. And Payne, picture this: Paul here is slamming Mace through the mat. I'm twisting Dreggs' legs into a pretzel. And Graves is lifting you about eight feet into the air, ready to drop you on either your head or your back. Paul: You'll have to excuse Simon. Counting myself and Graves, he almost has two friends, and despite the fact that he's broken almost every rule and convention in the book... Simon: Almost? Which ones have I missed? Paul: ... he does stick by his friends. Simon: Payne, pray that Morton, Daniels, and the Giant run your men out of the league. You do not want to face us when we're mad. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Note to [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] )- The Machine are willing to watch David Graves' back in any match, especially against Robski, Drexler, and company. [name withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Paul] WOW! I'd hate to be on THEIR bad side! Let's checkout what Zeroburn had to say to "Crimson" Joe Reed... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The scene is an abandoned warehouse. Over in the corner, laying on a velvet coverlet, is Tracy, zeroburn's valet & mangaer. she looks lucious. ZEROBURN: CRIMSON JOE REED! LOOK AT THE BEAUTIFUL TRACY. THE LADY IN RED CANCOT HOLD A CANDLE TO HER. GAZE UPON HER, REED. ISN'T SHE RAVISHING? WELL GEUSS WHAT PUNK!!!!! YOU CAN'T HAVE HER!! IF I CATCH YOU LOOKIN AT HER AGAIN I'LL KILL YOU! I AM HEREBY CHALLENGING YOU AND YOUR SLUTTY HO TO A STEEL CAGE MATCH! PUT THAT PIECE A GOLD UP IF YOU WILL! DONT IF YOU ARE CHICKEN! BUT I WANT YOU IN THE RING! IF NO CAGE, THEN A NO DQ MATCH, OR A FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN THE BUILDING! OR IN THE COUNTY FOR THAT MATTER !!! DON'T WEASEL AWAY FROM ME EITHER. YOU ARE THE MAN FOR NOW!!!! THE PWF CHAMPION!!!! I WILL BE THE MAN !!!!!! FACE ME OR COWER IN FEAR !!!!!!!! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Paul] With The Lady in Red, I doubt the Champ is looking at Tracy! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The scene is an abandoned warehouse. Over in the corner, laying on a velvet coverlet, is Tracy, zeroburn's valet & mangaer. she looks lucious. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Paul] Then again... Now a word about Tuesday Night Fights. Here's my co-host Otis Peter Nandy. ** The scene changes to a portly man with thinning red hair, standing in front of a huge arena that has a banner over the doors that reads "PWF TUESDAY NIGHT FIGHTS!"** [OP] Hi there! This is the site of Power Rasslin's Tuesday Night Fights! This Tuesday were gonna have THREE matches in the Tag Team Tourny! Punishers take on The Backstreet Giant and John Daniels! Catdaddies lock up with The Cobra Corporation! And New Order go to war with Desert Storm! We have a PWF warrior saying goodby when Princeton Maxwell takes on Cameron "The Killer" Cod in Maxwells FINAL PWF bout! A new PWA superstar makes his debute! Newcomer "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer goes after his first PWF win against Roadkill! Ghostrider takes on Scorpio! Tower against Lightning! AND THREE BIG MAIN EVENTS! In the first one Redeemer gets another chance at David Graves! In the second, The Backstreet Giant goes for the gold when he takes on TV Champion "Master of Destuction" Zak Powell! AND if THAT wasn't enough! WORLD CHAMPION, "Crimson" Joe Reed defends his title against Redemption! Don't miss a second of the action! Get you tickets early! [Paul] Here's something we just got in from Damage Inc. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "IT AIN'T MY BITCH" BY METALLICA BEGINS TO PLAY THE SCENE IS OUTSIDE A BOWLING ALLEY. ZEROBURN AND BOZ, DAMAGE, INC. , ARE STANDING HOLDING BOWLING PINS. BETWEEN THEM IS THE LOVELY TRACY, ZEROBURN'S VALET. BOZ: PINBALL PAULY! YOU FAGGOT! PLEASE.... PLEASE EXCEPT OUR CHALLENGE! ZEROBURN AND I WILL ROLL A PERFECT GAME USING YOUR HEADS FOR BOWLING BALLS! MIKE THE STRIKE? WHAT A SISSY SOUNDING NAME! PERFECT FOR A MORON LIKE YOU!!!! WE WILL REIGN SUPREME!! TELL 'EM ZEROBURN! ZEROBURN: ALLEY KITTENS!!! LISTEN UP!!! YOU GUYS ARE A COUPLE OF WUSSIES! WHEN I DROP THE ZEROBURNER ON YOU, YOU WILL NEVER GET UP!!!!! YOUR CAREER MAY END!! IT IS THE MOST DEVASTATING HOLD IN THE PWF!!!!! IT HAS NO COUNTER MOVE!!! YOU WILL BE THE STEPPING STONES ON OUR WAY TO THE PWF TAG BELTS !!!! WE ARE COMING FOR YOU. BOZ AND ZEROBURN BOTH HOLD UP BOWLING PINS HIGH OVER THEIR HEADS. THEY THEN BRING THEM DOWN ON EACH OTHERS SKULLS. THEY EXPLODE AND THEY LAUGH MERCILESSLY, HIGH FIVE EACH OTHER AND WALK AWAY FADE TO BLACK ! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Paul] MAN! They should be called BAD ATTITUDE! They REALLY don't like The Alley Cats! And finally, a message sent from Power Wrestling Federations home office. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [PWF Official] The Power Wrestling Federation does not and will not recognize any matches held using the so called RAVE rules of the PERSON know as Hacker. Any PWF wrestler who participates in a RAVE match is doing so at there own risk and neither the PWF of it's subsideraries will take any responsibility for injuries sustained there. Also PWF will pay $1500 for information leading to the arrest and conviction of this Hacker person for the unauthorized use of PWF's satellite. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Paul] Well that's all for this edition of The Addams Report. I'm Paul Addams...See ya in the ring! **Fade Out**
