GWA UPDATE: TOURNAMENT OF BRUISERS COMING SOON!!

27 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
REMINDER: To join the GWA you have to be an AOL member as well as a GWA member. If you have AOL already checkout Keyword: GWA If you don't have AOL GET IT!!!! GWA's 1997 Tournament of Bruises This Wednesday, October 1st, marks the begining of the 1997 Tournament of Bruises. I hope you have all been working on your parade floats, because we will be kicking things off early Wednesday morning with the traditional Tournament of Bruises Parade. You'll have to work hard to top last year's winner: Sting's "Alphabet Soup" Float. Then Wednesday night at 8PM ET, the first round of the tournament begins and lasts all week. * The tournament involves 32 of the top ranked GWA wrestlers divided into 4 brackets: USA bracket, SWA bracket, CWA bracket, and AWA bracket. *The first round of each bracket happens during one night during the regularly scheduled cards. USA bracket: 10/1 AWA bracket: 10/4 CWA bracket: 10/5 SWA bracket: 10/8 *Your wrestler(s) will not necessarily be put into the bracket of the division they wrestle in. *4 matches will be scheduled for each card. Once matchups are announced, I highly suggest that you send a contract to your opponent for the date and time that the match is scheduled. *Matches do NOT have to be wrestled on their scheduled date, they may be wrestled before or after. However, ALL first round matches must be completed by the end of the October 8th card. Any unwrestled matches at that time will be considered double no-shows and both wrestlers will be eliminated. This will happen UNLESS one of the wrestlers was online and waiting for his match during the regularly scheduled match time. In this instance, the wrestler who did show at the regular time will advance (but will not receive a win or XP). Remember, if neither wrestler shows for the scheduled time, it is the responsibility of the wrestlers to re-schedule by October 8th. *Matches are officiated as standard GWA matches with no corners allowed. *20 minute time limits will be enforced, and time limit draws eliminate both wrestlers. Get your damage in early and often. *Tournament match winners will receive wins that count on their record (no-show wins excluded.) They will also receive XP or lose XP depending on whether they win or lose the match. However, the T.O.B. matches do NOT count toward your match limit. *All Final Four wrestlers will receive a 1xp bonus in addition to the XP they received for winning their matches. *The final round loser will receive an additional 2XP bonus. *The Tournament of Bruises final round winner will recieve an additional 3XP bonus and a shot at the GWA World Championship within 7 days. *First round matchups will be announced this Monday night with the release of 4 videos revealing the first round seedings and matches for each bracket. *If you do not want to be included in the tournament, please inform me (BlindReff) ASAP. Or if you would prefer one of your wrestlers over another to be included, also let me know ASAP. *Any questions should be directed to BlindReff or RoidRef. *Good luck in The 1997 Tournament of Bruises!
