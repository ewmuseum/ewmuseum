"GWA Glycerine"

19 March 1996

From [detail withheld] Sun Mar 17 20:09 EST 1996 The screen goes dark, and then we see the Glycerin logo burned through what looks like a pair of jeans. We go to Jim Floss, who's standing up and putting out the fire.
JF: Yeah, well I didn't think that was funny! Geez! Get another opening to this show! Anyway, (he sits down) as we go towards Spring
Fling '96, we have five big matches to report on, and,....where's
Michael??
Just then, Michael BS Crazed comes out in a white suit, clean shaven, and the dye all washed out of his hair.
JF: Michael, what's this?! PP: Excuse me, Jim Floss. Call me Pro-fessor Presley! JF: Oh, Lord. I thought you got out of that! PP: The contracts aren't up yet,...plus I make twice as much money,... JF: Let's go to the ring,... Gary Michael Amaretto: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, OUR FIRST MATCH IS SET FOR
ONE FALL WITH A 30-MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING, MAKING HIS GWA DEBUT,
FROM IRELAND, AT 275 POUNDS, HERE IS DANNY RADIGAN! JF: Well, here's a pleasant-looking young man,...what do ya know about him,...Pro-fessor?! PP: (sniffing) Smells like somebody was roastin' ham,....oh, well, he's from Ireland and he's a straight arrow, man. Plays by the rules,...whoop de do!
JF: Were you behind this? PP: No,...but your be-hind looks like it was on fire, Jimbo. Want some water? JF: Naaah, I about got it out. Well maybe just a little. PP: Don't touch my glass! JF: I didn't say mother may I? PP: (under his breath) It's got whiskey in it! JF: Oh,.... GA: AND INTRODUCING HIS OPPONENT, HE IS ORIGINALLY FROM GERMANY, NOW
LIVING IN LOS ANGELES,....HE IS A MEMBER OF THE FOUR STALLIONS (the crowd boos unmericfully) WEIGHING 330 POUNDS, HERE IS THE GERMAN BEHEMOTH,
HEINRICH HIMMLER! JF: Himmler's about as popular here as a dog at a cat show,.... PP: That wouldn't bother me,.... JF: Himmler looking awesome as always, and he no doubt feels good about himself after that unwarranted attack on Sam Hart last week.
PP: Yeah, my good friend Michael BS Crazed told me he may want back in the ring to get ahold of the Stallions,...(Floss looks at Prof, hard,...)
what? JF: There's the bell, and young Mr. Radigan is in for a test with one of the all-time greats in the history of the GWA.
PP: I'll agree with ya there. Heinrich Himmler is probably the most powerful man in the history of the GWA.
JF: Collar and elbow tieup, and Himmler turns into a side headlock. Really grinding it in there, Michael. PP: What'd you call me? JF: Sorry,....Prof! PP: That's better,.... JF: Himmler now picks up Radigan,...my gosh, what strength! PP: Oh, man! What a military press slam that was, Jimbo! JF: Yes sir, and the youngster is down,....Himmler throwin' Radigan to the outside. PP: Himmler's on the apron,...what's he gonna do now? He's comin' off,....spiked him, baby! JF: Oh, my! What a devastatin' shoulder block from the apron to the floor by Heinrich Himmler, and Radigan will not get up from that.
Himmler back in,...and the ref's puttin' the count on Radigan,...he's out! PP: Well that didn't take long. Do it to , big G! GA: YOUR WINNER OF THE MATCH IN ONE MINUTE AND SEVEN SECONDS ON A COUNTOUT, HEINRICH HIMMLER! PP: Suppose he's sendin' a message to everybody else? JF: No doubt about it, Prof. As we look at the monitor, what a slam there by Himmler, and there's the spike shoulder block from the apron.
PP: Well, I tellya, when somebody that big hits you with a shoulder block off the apron, you better just phone it in, baby!
JF: We'll be back after this,...(Jim still swats at his pants.)
