Looking for New Talent

25 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now, I know what you are saying, you're probably a crusier weight or a tag team or one of the other rarities in e-wrestling, and you're wondering, what does this fed actually have to offer me besides false promises about results, contracts and all those other E-wrestling goodies. What the GWA has to offer is nothing more than awesome results. Our last pay per view was over 200 kilobytes long, and although length does not always mean quality, this pay per view had quality. For 200 kb, we had 17 matches, and it was written all be me! The president of this fine company! Think about what we can do with the weekly cards, considering that we get multiple people to run them. We have two cards a week, a Sunday Night Slamfest, and a Midweek Mayhem:Hardcore Night. Don't let the names fool you, we have some great wrestling on these programs. MWMH is more of a Raw type program, as SNS is more of a Smackdown type. We also may be bringing in a third card, once every two weeks for the rookies to shine. Pay Per views are once a month, and we've been going on for over 2 years strong. We are in need of some new talent, some fresh blood to be brought in, mainly crusier weights to help strengthen our division, as well as some more tag teams. We would love to start a women's division, so if any of you want to help the GWA start it's women division, we'd be happy to ablige(Sp). We would also love to get some fresh heavyweight contenders, and we do not have weight restrictions on any GWA titles, except the Crusier weight championship, which is at 225. We also run by a system of contracts. We have you, the handler, sign a contract for each character you wish to run, and which time wise is based to you. We also like to go around the handler's time constraints. Therefore, if you need some time off, all you need to do is ask. We also have a very large constructed front office, that are equal partners. [name withheld] is more of the angle man, but I think of him as a commishiner. We have [name withheld], who is a co-president with myself, [name withheld]. [name withheld] is the vice president, as we may use our names in the e-fed itself as characters for storylines, we still are the people who run the federation. We also have a few card writers outside of the front office, so you can see we are very well structured. We are e-mail based rps, with more of an AOL style, but we are also integrating a net style. AOL means length, and Net is style, so with both, you can't really go wrong. We try to find your ability, and match you in the division/championship/feud that would best suit you. If anyone would care to hear more about the Grand Wrestling Association, please feel free to e-mail me at [detail withheld] . I'll talk with each of you personally, and tell you more about the GWA.I thank you for your time and consideration to the Grand Wrestling Association.
