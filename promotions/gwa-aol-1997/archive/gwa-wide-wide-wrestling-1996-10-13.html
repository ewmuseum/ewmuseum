"GWA Wide Wide Wrestling"

13 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The following is a sample of action from the Global Wrestling Alliance. We're an e-mail league that has been around for over four years. First on GEnie, now we're on the net.
Give our page a look at http://www.cam.org/~beau/gwa/gwaweb.htm If you're interested in some big time e-wrestling fun, give me a holler at [detail withheld] . ] The ringside area is dark as the fans' murmur fills the air. An announcer pierces the darkness.
YOU WANTED THE BEST! YOU GOT THE BEST! THIS...IS... KISS! Fireworks explode in the ring as the lights come up, revealing Gene Simmons, Paul Stanley, Ace Frehley, and Peter Criss at ringside, in full makeup, all wearing headsets.
Paul: We're commentatin' on the wrestling this week in the warm California sun of the GWA! Oh yeah!
Globaldome, let me hear ya! YEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRGGHHHHHHHH!!! Peter: Our first match is Terminator vs. Powerman. We're told they were partners in Savage Sweepstakes, and they're both members of the new Global Alliance.
Gene: Where's the girls? (A bouncer gives some nameless girl the thumbs up, and she immediately climbs over the ringside gate and sits in Gene's lap. )
Peter: Off the ropes, Powerman with a knee to Terminator's head! Big clothesline, and he tries to kick him in the ribs, but Terminator gets under him and backdrops him. Now he tries a belly to back suplex, but Powerman won't go over, and he drags him to the ropes for a sitdown splash. Now the POWER SURGE!
Ace: 1, 2, 3. Your winner, Powerman. Paul: Where'd you learn about wrestling? Peter: I wasn't really doing much the past 10 years...Powerman and Terminator walking to the back together. Look, Ace -- members of a group making up and staying together despite a big fight.
Paul: (ahem) Let's go to the next match! Globaldome! WAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Peter: Mr. Intellect and Nate "the Great" Martin in this one, and Mr. Intellect tries to start off with a hammerlock, but Nate whips him to the ropes and clotheslines him hard! Righteous!
Sends him to the buckle, now he's climbin' on top of him and drivin' punches to his head! Crowd: 1! 2! 3! 4! 5! Peter: Nate tries a bearhug, but Mr. I busts him in the mouth, then gives him a flying neck snap! Flying crossbody, but Nate rolls through and heads to the top rope. He's off the top -- oh crap!
Gene: Ha ha haaaa! He hit the referee! What's he doin' to him now, Catman? Peter: Nate Martin with his specialty, but the ref's out colder than Space Ace in 1978! Now Nate's tryin' to wake him up!
Hey, where's Mr. I goin'? Mr. Intellect: (climbs out of the ring) Gimme that! Gene: He's got my ****in' boot! Peter: He whacks Nate upside the head, and makes the cover! The ref is up! Ace: 1, 2, 3. Your winner, Mr. Intellect. Paul: Cool! Next match, Creepin' Death vs. Flyin' Kats! California! AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAIIIIYYYYY!! Peter: Flyin' Kat here with a sleeper! Takin' after me! I used a lot of sleepers! And some wakers, then some more sleepers.
Paul: Kat really thumpin' on Creepin' Death! Gene, where's that girlie? Gene: What girlie? The blond? Paul: I thought she was brunette? Gene: Look, a wrestling match. Paul looks to the ring, and a blond scampers away with Paul's jacket. Paul: (snaps his fingers) A sign from the bouncer, and a redhead leaps over the ring gate for Paul. Paul: Hey, Shay just pimp-slapped the Creep! Now he's...Peter! Peter: He's got my chair, and just laid it across Creep, and there's the bell! Ace: Your winner by DQ, Creeping Death. Peter: Big match for our main event, literally. Paul: I'm...uh...not exactly ready right now. Set the crowd up, Ace. Ace: SHOCK...ME. WWWWWWWWAAAAAAAAAEEEEEEEEEEE! Peter: 506 lb. King Kab vs. 300 lb. Tommy Thunder, and a big face rake by Tommy, he tries his Lightning Bolt brainbuster! Just barely got him! I don't believe it! But here comes Nate Martin from the back, and he's still got Gene's boot! Paul: Tommy's hollerin' at Nate, Kab grabs his drawers and rolls him up! Naw, he kicked out!
Gene: Just wanted to tell all the fans out there to come see us on our big reunion tour, all original, all makeup. But if you don't, only about a million others will, so what do
I care? You young punks can bite me. Peter: KISS really loves our fans! Tommy tries a fallaway slam, but can't pick Kab up, now Kab with a legdrop, and the Kaba Krunch! But Tommy powers out! Now HE gives a fallaway slam to Tommy, but manager JW Griffith is in there. Kab throws him out to the floor.
JW looks up to see KISS sitting over him. JW: AAAAAAHHH!! Peter: Into the turnbuckle, Tommy puts up both boots, right in Kab's big, makeup-less face! Running clothesline takes
Kab down! He throws Kab out to the floor. Ace, he's coming over here! Ace: What? Oh, it's cool, man. Peter: He's got your smoking guitar! BLAM! It's upside Kab's head, and he rolls him back in the ring! Ace: That was a nice guitar. Gene: These two guys are almost as tough as me. Right, Paul? Paul: Hey, I'm a lover, not a fighter. Peter: Hey, Tommy Thunder's got Kab up...POWERSLAM! I haven't seen someone get THAT high since...well, I don't remember. How about that. Paul: He's layin' on top of King Kab! 1, 2, 3! Oh yeah! Ace: Your winner by pinfall, Tommy Thunder. (Paul stands up on the table as a spotlight circles around him. The fireworks start going off in the ring)
Paul: IN AMERICA, AND ALL OVER THIS WORLD, WE ARE ALL ONE PEOPLE, ONE NATION, AND THAT IS THE KISS NATION! GOD GAVE ROCK AND ROLL TO *YOU!* (the spotlight shines on Peter as lighters shine all over the crowd) Peter: Beth, I hear you callin', but I can't come home right now...
