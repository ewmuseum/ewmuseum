A Roster Member's Unprompted Defence Of The Fedhead

6 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>We have 4 singles titles including a lightweight title and 2 tag team >titles. People who have held APW titles in the past include: American >Patriot, The Syndicate, Bruce MacDonald, the infamous Brandon Charm >thats right, the one and only Brandon Charm) and Robski (yes.. the >english sensation was in APW just before he retired.. and he called APW >one of the best feds that he had ever been in, no kidding around). > Gee thanx, it's about damn time that I was appreciated by you damn yanks. Here I have been, working my butt off, making you losers look good but mainly going undiscovered. And now, the world wants me. I told everybody that it would happen and it did. You're looking at the most talented, intense and all-round great guy that I am. And that's the truth Baby!! (In all seriousness Mike has been a great fed-head and doesn't deserve the bad-rep that the Patriot is trying to give him. While my wife was pregnent & ill, Mike gave me all the time I needed to get things sorted out and put up with irregular flashing from me during that period. APW has been a great fed to be a part of and when Mike passes over the reigns he will be missed.) This has been a public service announcement, by the one... the only... Brandon Charm!!! (Member of APW & NEF'n'W)
