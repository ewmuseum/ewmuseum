APCW: WrestleStock 96 Schedule, 20 October 1996

20 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On October 29-31st, APCW presents 3 days of action-packed, exciting wrestling like the world of e-wrestling has never seen before. WrestleStock 96: 3 Days of Violence and Hardcore Wrestling. *********************************************************** Day 1: Astrodome - Houston, Texas -Fisto Flash vs Dr. Melancholy in best 2-of-3 falls World Title Match -Rounds 1 & 2 of a 16-man $1,000,000 single-elimination tournament -Menonite Connection vs Dr. Death & Leviathon * World Tag Title Match -Surfer vs Richard Bloodstrike *Brass-knuckles Match for Bruiserweight Title -Destroyers vs Scary Nights *TV Tag Title Match -Gq Assassin vs Dublin Daredevil *TV Title Match -Bruce MacDonald vs Psycho *British Commonwealth Title Match -American Patriot vs Robski * Wheel Of Torture Match! - a wheel is at ringside with 10 matches on it. Wheel is spun every 5 minutes and new match stipulations are chosen by the wheel. This will be the last meeting of Patriot and Robski. Day 2: Alamo Dome - San Antonio, Texas -World Title defended * opponent is drawn randomly -World Tag Titles defended *opponents are drawn randomly -Semi-finals & Final of the $1,000,000 tournament -Rounds 1 & 2 of an 8-team single-elimination tag team tournament. -TV Singles & Tag Titles are defended -British Commonwealth title defended -Bruiserweight Title defended in a table match -Thunder/Darklord/Dr. Death/Leviathon vs Smasher/Apocalypse/Tremor/Overkill * Cage of Horrors Match - cage in the middle of arena on concrete floor. All 8 men are in there, to win all 4 men of the opposing team must be unconcious. Anything-goes, bring anything you want into the match. This will be the last match EVER where any members of Temple of Darkness face any members of Devastation Inc. Day 3: Cotton Bowl - Dallas, Texas -World Champion vs winner of the singles tournament in a ladder match. -World Tag Team Champions vs winner of the tag team tournament. -Tag Team Tournament Final -I-Quit match for the Bruiserweight title -British Commonwealth title defended -TV Singles & Tag Titles are defended -Lifesaver/Search/Destroy vs Dogs of War * six-man tag titles -31-man Battle Royal of all singles & tag tournament losers -more matches to be added to this last day depending on results of the first two days interested in joining APCW? send a message to [detail withheld] asking for APCW info and i'll send something out quickly. get your wrestler in by the end of this week and they'll be on the wrestlestock: Day 3 card.
