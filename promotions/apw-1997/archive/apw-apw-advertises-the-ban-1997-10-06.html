APW Advertises The Ban As A Badge Of Honour, 6 October 1997

6 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-Pro Wrestling is proud to announce that our titles are not recognized by the World Invitational Championships Council. Our next goal is to have APW totally banned from the WIC. What other feds are out there that are not recognized by the WICC? Nobody else. Send a message to mduda@uoguelph and check out our really bad web page at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5670 We have 4 singles titles including a lightweight title and 2 tag team titles. People who have held APW titles in the past include: American Patriot, The Syndicate, Bruce MacDonald, the infamous Brandon Charm ( thats right, the one and only Brandon Charm) and Robski (yes.. the english sensation was in APW just before he retired.. and he called APW one of the best feds that he had ever been in, no kidding around). What we want a quality roleplayers. You don't have to flash everyday, but that flashes that you do send out have to be good. What do you need to be in APW? Enthusiasm, and enjoyment of e-wrestling and creativity. Check out APW. A great fed, that just got better. A little non-recognition can go a long way.
