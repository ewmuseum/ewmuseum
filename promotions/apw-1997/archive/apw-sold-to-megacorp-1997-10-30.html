The Kayfabe Handover: "Sold To MegaCorp," 30 October 1997

30 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
: The fed was in good hands. Alex showed fairness to every non-American : handler in the fed. Much different from you Paul, if I remember rightly... : you begged me to join your fed after my success in another. And while you : wanted me, you treated me like a god. And then what happened? Your other : handlers didn't like the fact that people like me were being given pushes, : so they constantly complained. And you didn't want to risk losing your bread : and butter. But did you have to do it at the expense of every other : non-american? : So now, you've lost everything. At the last count about a dozen wrestler's : walked. So that leave you with a handful of guys that are illiterate or can : only flash once every two weeks. : I can offer you some advice, give up... it seems to be what you do best. : Oh... and I'll let you know where the International Alliance end up, just so : you can how a fed should be run. : Brandon Charm - Member of APW & NEF'n'W (No Longer) : & : The International Alliance This is [name withheld] here, the former owner of All-Pro Wrestling. I sold that fed for a tonne of money to MegaCorp and I have lots of money to play with. If the International Alliance needs any sort of funding whatsoever, please don't hesitate to call me, i would be more than happy to make some heft donations to your worthy cause. [name withheld] former owner of All-Pro Wrestling
