APW: Carnival '97 Tournament Schedule

4 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This Sunday, APW Presents its next great ppv event - Beantown Bedlam. Superstars appearing include former APW World Champions Brandon Charm, American Patriot, Dr. Melancholy and Smasher. Also appearing are the APW World Tag Champs the Destroyers, Dr. Doom & Masked Bulldog and the APW EXTREME Champion Supernova defends his title against Psycho in a Barbed-Wire-Scaffold-Fire-Thumbtack-Death Match. Check out the APW homepage ( http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5670/index.html ) this Sunday for all the results. If you want a complete writeup, become part of APW. Check the webpage on how to sign up. Here is the full lineup for the ppv: 'All-Canadian' Steve Carpenter vs 'Shooter' Ty Borger Pimp Dog vs El Bastardo TK Santiago 'The Franchise' Steve Scott vs Playboy Tony Flint 'The Heavenly Warrior' Angel vs Mr. X Vincenzo Galliano vs Carjack Hardcore 'Ironman' Jeff O'Connor vs 'The Dream' Scott Majors Best 3 of 5 Falls Brandon Charm vs Bruce MacDonald Champion Challenger Triangle Tag Match Scary Nights vs Night Breed vs Dream Boys TV Title Match Mondo Somatic vs Bruiser Terry Orton Champion Challenger EXTREME Title Match - Barbed-Wire-Scaffold-Fire-Thumbtack-Death Match Supernova vs Psycho Champion Challenger Bruiserweight Title Match - Modified Bullrope Match Smasher vs Jack Agoney Champion Challenger North American Tag Title Match Dr. Doom & vs Rocketman & Masked Bulldog Spaceman Champions Challengers Unified British Commonwealth-North American Title Match 62nd Assassin vs Apocalypse Champion Challenger World Tag Title Match - Barbed Wire-Cage Match Destroyers vs California Dreams Champions Challenger Main Event - War Games Match 62nd Assassin vs Smasher Snake Eyez Apocalypse California Dreams Destroyers ======= APW is also starting it's Carnival '97 tournament, with the winner being crowned the APW World Champion. The tournament starts on June 4th in Ottawa, Ontario and concludes on July 12th in Toronto, Ontario. Toronto, Ontario Mondo Somatic vs Jack Agoney American Patriot vs Krazze Larree Snake Eyez vs Psycho Bruce MacDonald vs Apocalypse Retro Danny Devo vs Smasher Supernova vs Dr. Melancholy Bye: Brandon Charm Also, if one of the entrants is involved in a tie-breaker match and has a bye in the final round, they will be placed in a match against suitable opposition to ensure that both parties are equally prepared for the match All matches will have a 20 minute time limit and will have no special stipulations attached to them. A victory by pinfall or submission will give a wrestler 3 points, a draw 1 point and a loss by dq or countout will be -1 points. A loss by pinfall or submission will get 0 points and a win by dq or countout will be 1 point. I expect that all entrants will strictly adhere to the match rules and anyone who is dq'd during the tournament will have that match reviewed by myself and a suspension may be considered for after the tournament to punish those who do not show respect for their fellow wrestlers and APW officials.
