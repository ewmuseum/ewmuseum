APCW: Sunday LIVE!, 10 August 1996

10 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-Pro Championship Wrestling ============================== presents ******** Sunday LIVE! ************ Mongoon:"Welcome to Sunday LIVE! folks from the UIC Pavillion in Chicago Illinois.. " Dentura:".. i think this is where they filmed Judgement Night, Mongoon.." Mongoon:"We have some great action for you tonight folks plus the complete card listing for this thursday's ppv, Anarchy In The UK. Lets go down to Howard Fickle."
Fickle:"This bout is schedule for one fall. Introducing first.. from Tokyo, Japan.. weighing 245lbs.. Dark Ninja! ( huge pop from crowd) His opponent.. from Florence, Alabama weighing 220lbs.. Bonzai Clemmons!" Mongoon:"What a match this is going to be.. two of APCW's best high flyers. They lock up.. Clemmons kicks Ninja in the midsection.. Clemmons whips Ninja into the ropes and hits him with a dropkick. Clemmons up and nails Ninja with a spinning headscissors.. Clemmons stomps on Ninja, picks him up and throws him outside the ring. Clemmons goes out after him but is met by a clothesline from Ninja.. Ninja throws Clemmons into the guardrail.. Ninja picks up Clemmons and puts him back into the ring..
Ninja on the apron and executes a springboard dropkick! Nice move by Ninja.. goes for the cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Ninja whips Clemmons into the ropes and goes for a dropkick but Clemmons moves out of the way, Ninja hits the mat and Clemmons grabs Ninja's arm and puts on an armbar submission."
Dentura:"I am surprised Ninja is wrestling this weekend.. he has a big triangle match with Darklord and Thunder this thursday for the
British Commonwealth Title and I would have thought he would be resting up for it. What an idiot for wrestling a guy like Bonzai Clemmons tonight."
Mongoon:"Ninja is not one to duck a challenge.. and he finally gets out of that armbar. Ninja hits Clemmons with a forearm to the head.. Ninja with a huge karate kick.. Ninja grabs Clemmons and nails him with a back suplex. Ninja to the top rope.. and he nails Clemmons with a moonsault!
1, 2, 3! Dark Ninja wins and is he ever ready for Anarchy In The UK!" Dentura:"Looks like Ninja lucked out here with a quick victory.. but at Anarchy In The UK, it's going to be over quick for him as he has to face two members of the dangerous Temple of Darkness.."
Fickle:"This match is a tag team match and is for one fall. Introducing first, with their manager Robo Stone.. from Olympia, Washington.. weighing a combined 660lbs.. Heavy Metal! Their opponents.. from Bird-in-Hand, Pennsylvania.. weighing a combined 475lbs.. The Menonite Connection!" Mongoon:"Both teams have big matches at Anarchy In The UK as The Menonite Connection has a chance at the World Tag Team Titles with a match against Dr. Death and Leviathon.. and Heavy Metal will be in a Barbed Wire match against Scary Nights... both of the matches are looking to be great.. that Barbed Wire match is going to be a war.."
Dentura:"It's going to be a bloody mess is what it's going to be as Atlas and Scwartzentreuber lock up and Atlas hits Adam with a punch.. whips Adam into the ropes and nails him with a backdrop. Atlas picks up Adam and bodyslams him.. goes into the ropes and drops a huge elbow..
cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout. " Mongoon:"It is going to be blood.. but that tag match should be great too. Atlas tries to whip Adam into the ropes.. Adam reverses it and clotheslines Atlas.. Atlas up and he gets bodyslammed by Schwartzentreuber.. Adam tags in Yoder and they whip Atlas into the ropes and hit him with a double fist to the stomach.. and then they double dropkick
Atlas. Yoder with a cover as Adam leaves the ring. 1.. 2.. kickout. Yoder whips Atlas into the ropes and nails him with an elbowsmash. Yoder bodyslams Atlas.. Menonite Connection look like they are ready for Anarchy, Jesse." Dentura:"They better be preparing themselves as not only is their match at Anarchy a world tag title opportunity but against the dangerous
Dr. Death & Leviathon. Yoder twising the right arm of Atlas.. Yoder doing the smart thing here, keeping Atlas in their corner of the ring so that
Atlas cannot tag in his partner.. Atlas pokes his thumb in Yoder's right eye breaking the armbar.. Atlas runs into the ropes and he kicks Yoder in the head. Atlas tags in Apollo.. they whip Yoder into the ropes and hit him with a double elbowsmash... Atlas & Apollo run into the ropes and hit Yoder with a double headbutt. Apollo with a cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout by Yoder."
Mongoon:"This match is back and forth.. Yoder whips Apollo into the ropes and nails him with a shoulderblock. Yoder with a cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout.
Yoder going up to the top rope.. but Atlas comes over and pushes the man they call Buttermaker off the top rope on the mat.. referee
Cyclone Brody has keep things like that from happening, that is his job and he is not doing it. Apollo nails Yoder with and elbowdrop to the back of the head.. Atlas comes into the ring and they start stomping on
Yoder.. Atlas & Apollo whip Yoder into the ropes and hit him with a double backdrop. Atlas with a fistdrop.. cover. 1.. 2.. kickout. Atlas picks up Yoder and throws him outside of the ring.. and both Atlas and
Apollo go out after him and start stomping on Yoder.. here comes Schwartzentreuber, the man they call Bomb. Bomb and Atlas are going at it as Apollo at Yoder are brawling.. Atlas bodyslams Bomb onto the concrete floor as Apollo bodyslams Yoder onto the floor.. Atlas and
Apollo back into the ring and both Bomb and Yoder are left laying outside the ring.." Dentura:"Menonite Connection are a joke.. they are outweighed by almost 200lbs, how do they expect to compete with the bigger teams like Heavy Metal and Temple of Darkness? Yoder back into the ring and he gets clotheslined by Atlas. Atlas nails Yoder with a vertical suplex..
Here come Thunder and Lightning.. the Scary Nights! They come down to ringside.. " Mongoon:"Brody has to get those guys out of here.. they are only down to cause trouble. Apollo is tagged in and he goes to the top rope..
and nails Yoder with a double axhandle. Atlas and Apollo pick up Yoder.. and nail him with a double powerslam... its over! 1, 2, 3!" Dentura:"As soon as the bell rings, Thunder and Lightning come into the ring and start brawling with Heavy Metal! I love it! Here come Temple of Darkness... they want in on this too!"
Mongoon:"All eight men brawling right now.. Bomb and Death are outside the ring.. Death picks up a chair and nails Bomb with it! Leviathon grabs a chair and both men of Temple of Darkness are nail Bomb with chairs!"
Dentura:"I love it! Temple of Darkness taking care of thursday's business before Anarchy In the UK! Here come Tremor and Overkill, The Destroyers!"
Mongoon:"10 men on this brawl now and all ten are brawling in the aisle! Thunder nails Apollo with a chair.. Atlas nails Lightning with a clothesline.. here come some security guards to break it up.. thank god this one is over.. it was getting pretty messy."
Dentura:"Just when a fight is getting good those damn APCW officials have to come into the ring and ruin things."
Fickle:"Introducing first... from Sicily, Italy weighing a combined 415lbs.. Arturro and Darius.. The Bungee Bros.! Their opponents.. from parts unknown.. weighing 535lbs.. here are Thunder and Lightning..
The Scary Nights!" Mongoon:"Another tag match here on Sunday LIVE!.. Arturro starting things off with Thunder.. Arturro with a dropkick.. another dropkick.. Arturro whips Thunder into the ropes and hits him with a shoulderblock..
cover..1.. 2.. kickout. Arturro tags in Darius and they whip Thunder into the ropes.. double dropkick! and then a double elbow drop! Darius with a cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Darius runs into the ropes and is met with a huge savate kick by Thunder. Thunder tags in Lightning and they whips Darius into the ropes and hit him with a back drop.
Lighting throws Darius outside of the ring.. goes out after him and nails him with a clothesline.. Thunder out there and they start stomping on Darius. Double bodyslam by Scary Nights.. Lightning throws
Darius into the ring and hits him with a knee drop. Cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Lightning tags in Thunder and they whip Darius into the ropes.. double clothesline. Thunder with a splash and a cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout.
Thunder picks up Darius and nails him with a big bodyslam.. Darius really needs to tag in Arturro."
Dentura:"That's right.. Bungee Bros. were doing okay in the match.. until Darius came into the match. " [ Scary Nights work on Darius for a little while.. a couple of tags a some double-team moves.. they can't quite pin Darius]
Mongoon:"Darius makes a big tag to Arturro! Arturro with a dropkick on Thunder.. another dropkick.. Lightning runs into the ring and he gets dropkicked! Arturro grabs both Lightning and Thunder and hits their heads together. Arturro throws Lightning outside the ring!
Arturro whips Thunder into the ropes and clotheslines him. Arturro going to the top rope.. and he hits Thunder with a flying bodypress.. 1..2..
kickout by Thunder. Arturro going back up to the top rope.. and he hits Thunder with a dropkick! Cover..1.. 2.. kickout. Arturro tags in Darius.. they whip Thunder into the ropes and nail him with a backdrop.. and then a double headbutt. Darius with a cover... 1.. 2. kickout. Darius going to the top rope.. and he nails Thunder with a clothesline! Cover.. 1..
2.. kickout by Thunder." Dentura:"Lightning comes into the ring and he nails Darius from behind with a knee to the back. Lightning bodyslams Darius and gets out of the ring."
Mongoon:"Thunder grabs the left arm of Darius and goes for an armlock submission.. Darius gives up! Thats the match! Scary Nights victorious."
Dentura:"Everything goes fine with the Bungee Bros. until Darius comes in.." Fickle:"From San Diego, California.. weighing 285 lbs.. American Patriot! and from parts unknown weighing 315lbs.. Darklord!" Mongoon:"At Anarchy In The UK, American Patriot will be wrestling.. but will also be making his managerial debut as he brings in his new team,
Red, White and Blue. Both guys are 7 feet tall!" Dentura:"Height means nothing Mongoon.. apparently they like the cheers of the crowd.. so they must suck."
Mongoon:"Would you stop.. Darklord attacking Patriot right away nailing him with a couple of kicks to the midsection.. Darklord whips
Patriot outside the ring and goes out after him.. Darklord has a big opportunity at Anarchy In The UK in that triangle match for the
British Commonwealth Title.. but if he wants it, he is going to have to beat his fellow Temple of Darkness, Thunder. Patriot takes control outside the ring as he clotheslines Thunder.. and another clothesline..
throws Thunder back into the ring.. Patriot goes to the top rope.. goes for a flying bodypress.. Darklord down but rolls right over and covers Patriot.. 1.. 2.. 3! Just like that another match is over."
Dentura:"Patriot should have waited until Darklord was worked over before going for a move like that.. Darklord caught him by surprise and was able to get the victory."
Fickle:"This match is for the APCW TV Title! Introducing first.. the challenger.. from Brooklyn, New York.. weighing 305lbs.. Fisto Flash! His opponent.. coming down the aisle.. from Detroit, Michagan.. weighing 287lbs.. the APCW TV Champion: Smasher!" Mongoon:"Big match here as Smasher makes his first TV Title defense.. the winner of this match has to defend the TV Title against 750lbs Aegis at Anarchy In The UK.."
Dentura:"Fisto Flash apparently has something to argue about with referee Rich Donaldson.. Smasher rolls up Fisto from behind! 1.. 2.. 3!
What a cheap victory.." Mongoon:"Flash was talking to the ref, Smasher took advantage and gets a quick victory." Fickle:"This match is a non-title match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first.. from the mental hospital, weighing 280lbs.. Phsyco! And his opponent.. coming down the aisle, from Death Valley, California weighing
325lbs.. the All-Pro Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion.. Dr. Melancholy!" Mongoon:"Melancholy making his first big World Title defense against the great all-rounder, Buck Snipe."
Dentura:"Snipe is a dangerous man.. he can hit you with the high flying moves, the scientific moves, power moves, you name it he can do it."
Mongoon:"You are right Jesse.. but Dr. Melancholy is not a bad wrestler either.. in fact, pretty damn good. Phsyco and Melancholy lock up and
Phsyco nails Melancholy with a couple of kicks.. and then a headbutt.. and then throws Melancholy outside the ring. Phsyco has a big Strait-Jacket match at Anarchy In The UK with Fist Flash.. to win, you have to put your opponent in a strait jacket. What a wild match that is going to be as anything-goes. Phsyco helps Melancholy into the ring and hits him with a vertical suplex. Cover.. 1.. kickout."
Dentura:"Melancholy is showing a lot of ring rust this week as he is having trouble with Phsyco. While he sits around on his butt, Buck Snipe has been wrestling all week getting ready to win the world title in
England." Mongoon:"Phsyco stomping on Melancholy.. runs into the ropes and hits him with a legdrop. In WCW, that means the match is over.. but in APCW, a legdrop just doesn't quite cut it as a finisher.. unless you weigh 750lbs like
Aegis does. Melancholy kicks outs of a Phsyco cover.. Phsyco bodyslams Melancholy.. Phsyco runs into the ropes goes for a big elbow drop but Melancholy rolls out of the way. Melancholy up and he whips Phsyco into the ropes... and hits him with a dropkick. Melancholy whips Phsyco into the ropes again and nails him with a shoulderblock."
Dentura:"Melancholy picks up Phsyco and bodyslams him.. Melancholy going to the top rope.. and nails Phsyco with a clothesline. Cover..
1.. 2.. kickout. Melancholy grabs Phsyco and rams his head into the turnbuckle.. but that has no affect on Phsyco and he grabs Melancholy's head and rams it into the turnbuckle. Physco with a bodyslam..
Phsyco with a cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Phsyco throws Melancholy outside the ring and goes out after him.. Phsyco clotheslines Melancholy.. Phsyco throws
Melancholy back into the ring, but Phsyco stays outside. Melancholy gets up and he goes to the top rope.. and nails Phsyco with a huge clothesline off the top rope onto the concrete outside! Wow!"
Mongoon:"What a move that was.. Melancholy throws Phsyco into the ring.. Melancholy whips Phsyco into the ropes.. and nails him with a frankensteiner! 1.. 2.. 3! Thats it!"
Dentura:"What a boring match and what a boring card this has been.. i am going to a bar Mongoon." Mongoon:"That is it for Sunday LIVE! folks.. stay tuned as Gene Oakland will be right here with your Anarchy In The UK report. I'll see you in Newcastle-Upon-Tyne, England. Goodnight folks."
Oakland:"Hello everyone and this is your Anarchy In The UK report. This thursday in Newcastle-Upon-Tyne, England it all goes down... the greatest ppv the world has seen yet and the best card England will ever see.
In a match for the World Title, Dr. Melancholy defends his title against the great Buck Snipe, the number 1 contender right now.
There will be a triangle match for the British Commonwealth Title as Thunder defends against Temple of Darkness-mate Darklord and Dark Ninja. The World Tag Titles are on the line when Menonite Connection, Adam 'Bomb' Scwartzentreuber and Reubanthal 'Buttermaker' Yoder meet the APCW World Tag Champions Dr. Death & Leviathon. Smasher defends the TV Title against the man he injured two weeks ago on LIVE!, Aegis. In a 10-man First Blood Battle Royal, Aegis, Smasher, Richard Bloodstrike, Phsyco, Fisto Flash, Reubanthal Yoder, Adam Schwartzentreuber, The Assassin, Gq Assassin and American Patriot will fight it out for the right to call themselves APCW's toughest man as APCW crowns it's first
Bruiserweight Champion. In a Strait Jacket match, Fisto Flash takes on Phsyco. There will be a Barbed-Wire match at Anarchy In The UK as Scary Nights take on Heavy Metal. In other matches, Bungee Bros. take on Night Breed, Bonzai Clemmons vs Tai Sabakki, Voo-Doo vs American Patriot, Red, White & Blue go up against the Destroyers, Richard Bloodstrike takes on Prisoner #109. What a card it will be, don't miss the best ppv so far this year..
