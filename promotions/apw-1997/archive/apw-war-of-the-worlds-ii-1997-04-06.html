APW: War Of The Worlds II Lineup, 6 April 1997

3 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lineup for War Of The Worlds II ppv on April 6th from Kingdome in Seattle, Washington War Of The Worlds Match: 12 teams in 1 cage the size of 8 rings on the concrete floor of the Kingdome. Only way to win is to have a member of your team to be the last man standing in the ring. Pinfalls and submissions do not count. Members of the winning team will all receive title shots at the next ppv, with their choice of stipulations! -Devastation Inc. (Smasher, Apocalypse, Tremor & Overkill) -Masters of Havoc (Psycho, Scary Nights & Jack Agoney) -Hells Kitchen ( Iron Sultan, Cosmic Connection & Supernova) -Team Hardcore (Ultimate, Extreme and General Hardcore & Mr. X) -Palace of Power (Alexis Windhelm, Playboy Tony Flint & Dallas Connection) -Stud Stable (Buck Snipe, Bonzai Clemmons & Night Breed) -These Charming Men (Brandon Charm, Titanticus, Masked Bulldog & Dr. Doom) -Mike Swanson, Johnny Swing, 62nd Assassin & Snake Eyez -Wild Card I (Dr. Melancholy, Bulldog Bruce MacDonald, American Patriot and Mondo Somatic) -Wild Card II (Krazzee Larree, Retro Danny Devo, Bruiser Terry Orton & Maniac) -Wild Card III (Dream Boys & Unforgiven) -Wild Card IV (Nightmare & Three Amigos) plus: APW World Title Match: Brandon Charm vs American Patriot World Champion Challenger - with Charm's bodyguard Titanticus inside a ringside cage! APW World Tag Team Title Match Smasher & Apocalypse vs Unforgiven World Tag Team Challengers Champions - Best 2 of 3 Falls! APW Bruiserweight Title Match: Mondo Somatic vs Krazzee Larree Bruiserweight TV Champion Champion - I Quit Match! APW Six-Man Tag Title Match: Iron Sultan & Cosmic Connection vs Psycho & Scary Nights Six-Man Tag Team Champions Challengers
