I have had my APW open since last year in > July and it is still going strong with two cards a week and a ppv every 3 > weeks. > It is all a matter of respect. If i were to open up a fed today and i > called it, oh lets say, NCW and the NCW head told me that the name was > already in use, I have enough respect for the other fed heads out there > to pick another name. > > It is all a matter of respect, and i think that the other APW guy is > showing that he doesn't have any class at all by keeping the name. > > Just my 2 cents worth. I do not believe anyone has said it better. One word, respect. Speaking of which, a respectful president would not use another's mailing list without permission <as you did Mr. Collins>, nor would it use MY guestbook to promo your fed <as you did again> without asking permission. Don't get me wrong, if you would have asked, you would have been given a space on any of my shows or flash packages. But, you had to be cheesy and cool, and, despite me recommendations, Mr. Collins, you went ahead and did it your way. E-feds do not need to be competitive like this, for Pete's sake. We should respect each other. We should help to promote the entire concept of e-wrestling. Also, as I prepare to face the flames, I want to plug myself and my work. I started my fed with work, hard work. We started small, and worked hard. We published regularly, improved the home page, picked up the pace of the angles, worked better match writers. In all, we owe only ourselves for our success. We did it on our own, and we still have the commitment to impprovement. Mr. Collins, you had to go out and make this personal, didn't you. <before flaming me, I received some negative email from this young man>  -- ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ And I ain't changed, but I know I ain't the same. -The Wallflowers ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ NEF'nW Homepage http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/2693/index.html
