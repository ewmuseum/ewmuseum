APW: Wednesday Night Chaos Announcement, 15 October 1997

15 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MegaCorp International and Ringleader Promotions present... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- WEDNESDAY NIGHT CHAOS! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- This week from Manchester, England ATT: 3,500 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Dark matches: Cyber Machine def. Hunter Hearst Menendez Spider Green def. TAKA Menendez The Great Toshida def. The DMV Clerk -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH 1: Psycho vs Mumonkan **Bruiserweight Title Match! [What a way to start the card! Mumonkan hit a number of awesome looking technical moves, but Psycho's power put a stop to it fairly quickly. After ten minutes of solid action, Apocalypse came down to ringside. Mumonkan left the ring to chase him away and the referee went to break it up. Meanwhile, Smasher came in from the crowd and laid out Psycho with the Bruiserweight Title Belt, allowing the unsuspecting Mumonkan to roll him up for the easy pinfall.] WINNER: Mumonkan ***NEW APW BRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH 2: Scary Nights v P&P Winners get title shot at the next PPV [solid but uninspired match... a few high spots, but the fans weren't very interested.. after around seven minutes, P&P planted Darkness with the Ped & Preakfast for the right to meet Syndicate (or whoever the champions are next month)] WINNERS: P&P -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Howard Fickle is standing in the ring.] Fickle - At this time, I'd like to introduce to you the new President of APW... Tony "Tough Guy" Pascoe! [warm applause from the crowd] Pascoe - Ladies and gentlemen, thank you very much for attending tonight. At this time, I'd like to brwak some red hot news about the APW International Title. Instead of the previously announced Battle Royal next week, the BrutalBowl on Sunday, November 2 will sort the men from the boys. The qualifying stages will happen over the next four weeks here on Chaos and on the House Show circuit, until 8 men remain. Those eight will compete in the Brutal Bowl finals at the PPV. Other BrutalBowl matches will include a World Title defense of some sort, with the number one contender yet to be announced, and of course P&P will get their title shot against the tag team champions. The show will emanate from the brand new APW Arena in suburban Pheonix, AZ. This site will be the home of the new APW weekend show, Saturday Sychosis, which begins on November 8. [Pascoe leaves to similar applause] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH 3: Mondo Somatic v Apocalypse Falls Count Anywhere, no time limit [What a brawl! This match will surely go down as one of the most violent in APW history! Anything they could find was used on one another, from trashcans to chairs via guard rails, sodas, camera cords and the ring bell. After a while, the ringside area was invaded by almost every APW wrestler, with Dr Melancholy charging out to get to Mondo, Creed and Chris Douglas going for Apocalypse, Devastation arriving to help out their man, and Psycho coming to take revenge on Smasher. In the confusion, with Brandon Charm making an appearance to punish Mondo for his comments before the show, an _enormous_ man appeared from nowhere with a woman by his side and planted Apocalypse with a DDT onto the ring bell, disappearing again just as fast. Mondo managed to make the cover before Dr Melancholy and Tremor could get to him and became TV champion once more] WINNER: Mondo Somatic **NEW APW TV CHAMPION! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH 4: Creed & Chris Douglas v The Destroyers [your commentators are Harry Nicholas and Paul Donald] Fickle - this match is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Coming down the aisle, accompanied by Roxanne, The Destroyers! [mixed reaction] Their opponents, with a total combined weight of 480 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Angel and Titanicus, APW World Champion Chris Douglas and Creeeeed! [same reaction - fans split 50/50] Nicholas - interesting that despite being a hugely patriotic Brit, Chris Douglas can still only receive a lukewarm response, Paul. Donald - not really. There are two of them - it's obvious that it's Creed they don't like. Nicholas - After Monday's PPV, I can't see how you can keep a straight face while saying that. Creed is more popular, and carries the team in my opinion. Here come the Destroyers now, living up to their names! The two teams are brawling in the aisle before the bell has even rung! The four men really beating one another, with no thought for the match! Donald - Titanicus is joining in to make it three on two now... as Angel and Roxanne come face to face! Roxanne slaps Angel hard! Nicholas - officials coming to break this up folks... looks like we won't see this tonight. Creed and Douglas are taken backstage by officials, and the Destroyers are ordered away also... but here comes Brandon Charm! Charm jumps Titanicus as he follows his buddies, and I guess this match is under way! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH 5: Brandon Charm v Titanicus *No DQ Donald - Brandon Charm really has a score to settle here with Titanicus. The big guy finally saw sense and kicked the ass of Charm. Now he's gonna do it again. Nicholas - Brandon Charm made Titanicus who he is today. Without Brandon, Titanicus would still be jockeying the register in some local convenience store. Charm is in full control of the match here, as he snap suplexes his former bodyguard. [Charm continued to dictate the pace for the next few minutes, really working over the neck and throat areas. We rejoin the bout at it's near conclusion...] Nicholas: Brandon lifts the big guy up for a Fisherman Suplex.. and gets a near three count. Donald - Titanicus'll take a lot more than that to put away... Nicholas - And here comes Creed.. Creed, Douglas and Titanicus seem to have formed an alliance recently.. not sure how long it'll last though. Creed tosses a chair to Titanicus, but Charm intercepts and nails Titanicus with it. He lays the chair out on the ring mat, and hits Titanicus with the Brandon Bomb II onto the steel chair! That'll be all... a win for Brandon Charm. WINNER: Brandon Charm Nicholas: Creed comes in to attack Charm, and is joined by Chris Douglas. The three of them are working over APW's returning hero, and he looks to be in a world of hurt. The Maniac Crew are here! I didn't know they were in the building! Dr Doom and the Masked Bulldog start to brawl with Creed and Douglas, and we are out of time! See you next week folks! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- An APW Production. -------------------------------
