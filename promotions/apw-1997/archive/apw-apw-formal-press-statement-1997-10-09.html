APW Formal Press Statement, 9 October 1997

9 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guelph, Ontario All-Pro Wrestling released the following statement earlier today regarding American Patriot and the World Invitational Championships Council "American Patriot was released earlier this year because of his poor roleplaying. His flashes were frequent, but were always brief and never showed any development or sophistication of his character. The flashes were always one-dimensional and rarely contained any creativity in them that are necessary in e-wrestling. American Patriot also had a severe problem with losing matches and could not handle losing more than one match. He always insisted that he was a first-class wrestler and held many, many world titles and that should have provided him with reason for winning. Instead, American Patriot is nothing special in the e-wrestling world and is no more talented than anyone else and it should be expected that he would lose some of his matches. APW heard several times from American Patriot when he lost to a clearly superior opponent and complained if he won a match thru non-clean results. As for the World Invitational Championships, APW asks that Mr. [name withheld] release who is on this WIC Council that he has formed. APW suspects that [name withheld] is the only member of this council. APW suggests that if Mr. Bungardner truly wants the WIC to have any special meaning in the world of e-wrestling, that he add other members to this council and not be the sole runner of these championships and disallow his own wrestler, American Patriot, from entering the tournament. Although Will has repeated several times that his wrestlers winning of the tournament was a pure coincedence, it still seems a small bit suspicious to others that his own wrestler won the tournament this year. This the final statement that All-Pro Wrestling will have concerning American Patriot and [name withheld] and consider the matter closed. Any and all communication with American Patriot that is not different from past comminication with him will be ignored. All-Pro Wrestling invites new wrestlers to join the federation and contact [detail withheld] to get information on joining APW and to look at our web page at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5670 "
