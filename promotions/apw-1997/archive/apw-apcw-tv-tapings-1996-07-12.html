APCW: TV Tapings, 12 July 1996

12 July 1996

All-Pro Championship Wrestling ****************************** TV Tapings Report "Hello wrestling fans and welcome to the first ever APCW TV Tapings Report! My name is [name withheld] and I am here right now in Cornwall, Ontario where the first APCW TV Tapings took place and what an event it was. We had 32 matches featuring the stars of All-Pro Championship Wrestling. There were no real surprises here, but all of the 600 fans that were in attendance were given a great display of the talent of APCW's roster.
Now, most feds use their TV Tapings for the television shows and don't give their fans the results of these tapings. But, these TV Tapings are for the APCW television shows shown outside of North America so don't worry about this report spoiling the upcoming shows. Right now fans, lets have a look at all the results of tonight's card:"
Dr. Melancholy pinned The D.M.V. Clerk after a frankensteiner Dark Ninja pinned Steve Strong after a moonsault Fisto Flash pinned Steve DiSalvoh Heavy Metal def Juan & Luis Menendez after a double power slam Gq Assassin pinned Barry Gordy Bonzai Clemmons pinned The D.M.V. Clerk after an asai moonsault The Menonite Connection def. Ultra Invaders I & IV Richard Bloodstrike pinned Steve Strong Buck Snipe def. The D.M.V. Clerk by submission Temple Of Darkness def. Jose & Tito Menendez The Assassin pinned Barry Gordy after a top-rope dropkick Darklord pinned Steve DiSalvoh Thunder pinned Ultra Invader III Night Breed def. Jorge & Juan Menendez The Scary Nights def. Ultra Invaders I & IV Psycho pinned Tony "Stair" Roid after a DDT Psycho pinned Terry Brunswick after a DDT Temple Of Darkness def. Jim Thomp & "Sugar" Ray Floyd Fisto Flash def. Barry Gordy Night Breed def. Ultra Invaders III & IV Gq Assassin pinned The D.M.V. Clerk Dr. Melancholy def. Terry Brunswick by countout The Scary Nights def. Juan & Tito Menendez Thunder pinned Jim Thomp The Assassin def. "Sugar" Ray Floyd The Menonite Connection def. Ultra Invaders I & III Buck Snipe pinned Tony "Stair" Roid Richard Bloodstrike def. The D.M.V. Clerk Dark Ninja pinned "Sugar" Ray Floyd Darklord pinned Terry Brunswick Bonzai Clemmons pinned Steve Strong Heavy Metal def. Jorge & Jose Menendez "Wow, what a night of action that was. All those in attendance were not dissapointed. This Sunday afternoon i'll be releasing the lineup for the first week of the Road To The Gold tour, and on Monday, This Week In APCW will have the entire setup for the World Title Tournament including when and where the tournament rounds will happen. I was going to save this little tidbit of information for monday's show, but instead I will release it now: the first APCW ppv will no longer will be held on August 1st in New
York City because were unable to get satellite time to broadcast it.
Instead, the first ppv will take place on July 25TH and now the Thursday Night Live! show scheduled for that day will now take place next thursday, thats July 18th! For more detail watch This Week In APCW on monday for complete details of the tournament and the Road To The Gold tour and pay attention to your mailbox on sunday for next week's tour lineup.
Thats all for now APCW fans, I'll see you this Monday." --
