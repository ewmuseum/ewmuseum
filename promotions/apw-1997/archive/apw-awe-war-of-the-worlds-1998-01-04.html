APW/AWE War of the Worlds, 4 January 1998

January 4, 1998

MegaCorp, in association with MegaSoda and MegaBurger, present... ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~ WAR OF THE WORLDS!!! ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~ LIVE From Washington DC ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~ Attendance: 15 200 ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~ Voice Over: Two promotions, different in outlook, style, and size, will be brought together tonight, right here at War of the Worlds.
The APW - All Pro Wrestling. Steeped in tradition, with many legendary competitors in their history. Men like Robski, Fisto Flash, Dr Melancholy, "Bulldog" Bruce McDonald, Mondo Somatic and Brandon Charm are all in the history books as APW competitors. Original promoter [name withheld] built a promotion with a high reputation, a promotion being taken to new heights by MegaCorp.
The AWE - American Wrestling Enterprises. A bright young star in the future of wrestling that perhaps attempted to achieve too much too soon. The ideas were first class; the budget was not. A sell out to MegaCorp shortly after the recording of Rocktober led inevitably to this event tonight.
One promotion will stand; the other will fall. This is War. The War of the Worlds! [Opening credits.] Harry Nicholas: Welcome everybody to APW and AWE's War of the Worlds Television Special! It's 8PM on New Year's Eve and for the next four hours we will bring you the hottest inter-promotional unification matches! Joining me tonight as ever is Paul Donald!
Paul Donald: Evening, all. Nicholas: Starting us off tonight, the Survivor Series tournament! Let's start the first match!

Donald: I thought the board guaranteed that one of the two feds would not exist in 1998? Nicholas: They did! This match has gone on too long! We go off the air at midnight, folks! Donald: What happened to the power? Nicholas: Did we just go off the air? What? There's still 90 seconds of air time to go! Damn Networks...
