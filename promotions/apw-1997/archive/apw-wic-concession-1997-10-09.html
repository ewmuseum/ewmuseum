The WIC Concession, 9 October 1997

9 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Like I said before, it was childish of me to do what I did this past weekend. There is no 'official' WIC council. It was the first thing that came to mind when I was thinking of this. Never did I think that this would be challenged. When I started the WIC two years ago (under a different name), I had other people help in the writing. However, as we got on through the tourney, I would request the cards out by a certain day, and we would still be waiting for two weeks after that deadline. This is why I decided to do it alone last year. I know that it looks suspicious that AP won last year, but like I've said in the past, I ran it with a simulator, and AP won. Plus, and this is something that I haven't stated yet, I had someone with me when I was rolling the matches so that I wouldn't cheat. I will use a suggestion that was brought up by APW. I will get some other people to help with the WIC. So, here is the first call. Anyone that wants to help out from an EXECUTIVE standpoint, please get back to me ASAP. I would like to see this tournament become the official championships to crown an undisputed champion of wrestling, but as long as we have doubters in the fray as to how the tourney is run, why someone won, etc. As for AP's dismissal from APW... it is true that my flashes were short. They still are. I don't believe in long flashes because the longer the flash gets, the more tendency there is to ramble and repeat. Some people can flash well and that's great. Of course, AP is the type that doesn't say much in person, but does his talking in the ring. As for complaining about matches: If AP lost cleanly, I respected it. IF, on the other hand, AP lost because of interference, especially for a title, I would become angered. Maybe it's because virtually every time AP went for a title, something happened that cost him the title other than losing cleanly. To this, I apologize to Mr. Duda and all of All-Pro Wrestling. I would like to thank Mr. Duda for bringing the information to my attention, and I hope that we can put that all behind us. Once again, if anyone is interested in taking on an executive position in the WIC, contact me as soon as possible. Thank you. ******************************************************************************* Actual bumper sticker seen on a car: Serving Time in San Clinton *******************************************************************************
