APW: Anarchy In The UK III Lineup, 29 June 1997

13 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Sunday June 29th, All-Pro Wrestling returns to St. James Park in Newcastle-Upon-Tyne, England for a day of incredible wrestling action with Anarchy In The UK III. All-Pro Wrestling will be going full out for this event and all of its superstars will be on the card. The All-Pro Wrestling World Title is currently vacant and unfortunatly not be available for defense in England but the double main event will more than make up for it. The lineup as of this writing is: o The Freshmen vs Scary Nights o Bruiser Terry Orton vs Gin N. Tonic o Dream Boys vs Night Breed o El Bastardo TK Santiago vs 'The Dream' Scott Majors o The Perfect Gentlemen vs Dallas Connection Six-Man Tag Title Match! o Scary Nights & Jack Agoney vs Mr. X, Carjack & General Hardcore Champions Strap Match For The APW TV Title! o Mondo Somatic vs Vincenzo Galliano Champion Best 2 of 3 Falls Tag Match! o Brandon Charm & Titanticus vs Smasher Apocalypse North American Tag Team Title Match - British Empire Rules! o Cosmic Connection vs Ultimate & Extreme Hardcore Champions Four-Way Table Death Match For APW Unified British Commonwealth North American Title! o Apocalypse vs Mr. X vs Psycho vs Brandon Charm Champion Four-Way UFC Rules Match For APW Bruiserweight Title! o Smasher vs Bulldog Bruce MacDonald vs Snake Eyez vs Carjack Hardcore Champion Four-Way-Two-Ring-Barbed-Wire-Cage-Glass-Chain-Death-Match For The APW EXTREME Title! o Supernova vs Dr. Melancholy vs Krazzee Larree vs 62nd Assassin Champion Four-Way-Anything-Goes-Falls-Count-Anywhere-Elimination-Tag-Match For The APW World Tag Team Titles! o California Dreams vs Destroyers vs Dr. Doom & Masked Bulldog vs Cosmic Connection Champions - for more info and the latest lineup changes, check out the APW Home Page at: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5670/index.html Thank you. [name withheld] APW Vice-President of Operations, Head Booker, Promoter & Writer --
