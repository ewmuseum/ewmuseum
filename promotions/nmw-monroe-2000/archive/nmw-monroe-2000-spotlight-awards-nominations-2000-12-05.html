NMW * You cannot vote for yourself. Put aside the greed and self-desires. Also, this isn't Florida - we have one set Ballot. So, if you lose - no excuses. And no cheating. * (}^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v{) BEST NMW ANNOUNCER - This Award is given to the Best Announcer the NMW has to offer. Whether it's because of their humor, wit, stand-up approach, or their knowledge of wrestling, these men are the Best-of-The-Best. And it's up to you, yes you, to decide who the "Best damn Voice in the Business" is.
Frankie Tha Pimp - Weekly Heel Announcer for Ground Zero who has been around many Promotions. Known for his role in the KAWF's but is now finding his niche in the NMW. Frankie is spontaneous, vulgar, and funny. Everything a Color
Commentator needs to be in the Millennium. Fred Hart - Weekly Face Announcer for Ground Zero noted for his deep hatred for Frankie Tha Pimp, also a Candidate. Fred Hart somehow finds his way to almost every [name withheld] Fed of the Late 90's and now Millennium. Not the wisest man with a Microphone but he gets the job done.
Prison - Weekly Heel Announcer for Shockzone and also the first man to break the "ganster" Barrier on TV. Four years ago, a man with Prisons record wouldn't even be allowed near the [name withheld] Offices but now, Prison is a commodity. He's interviewed some of the best in the Business and has an added dimension most Announcers don't. He can fight.
Brian Winslow - Weekly Neutral Announcer for Ground Zero. Brian is argueably one of the most knowledable men in all of Pro-Wrestling, possibly coming a close second to Mark Cleveland or The Knight. He's stepped up big time in the
NMW, taking the reigns of Announcing and bringing credibility to the Booth, something not many Promotions can say they have.
CO-President Outlaw - Many Special Appearances as Announcer of the NMW's Brief History, Outlaw never disappoints his fans. When he straps on a Headset, you can be guarenteed one of three things. He's got some big plans, The Cenors have a long night ahead of them, or someone in the ring is about to get their ass kicked.

The War in which you were on the edge of your seat and could never predict the winner correctly. It may have been a One Time Deal or a Month of Blood, anything is fair game in this World of Hate and more Hate which brings this Category to the forefront.
SL vs WOI - An Epic Battle between two great Tag-Teams and four great Men. At least three Battles have occured so far and who knows how many more to come.
Tommy Stylz vs ADB - An ongoing Feud that traces back to the prestigious MCWF. The Specialist and The Franchise never fail to delight. Mess Man vs Drifter - The DAD Program hit its pinnacle, bringing Drugs, Sex, and Violence to the NMW.
Brock vs Outlaw - Brock has somehow decided to challenge a Legend of our Sport and also the CO-President of our Promotion. The Manhunter has crossed the boss on more than one occasion so you have to wonder when he pays the Piper.
H2O vs Kris Klepan - Klepan had his fingers broke and H2O had his heart broke in this feud. Some speculate this Feud will hit its stride once more while others are adamant that the Candle these two lit ablaze is out for good.

Not lightweights like Taylor Threat who constantly fight Heavyweights but the Men who dominate their Division and continue to amaze us with their daredevilish antics that we never knew were possible, proving size doesn't matter!
Drakon Hughes - The most lethal cruserweight in the game who's found much success in his return. A recognizable name but does he still have the ability to gain power and maintain it? Time will tell.
Gryphon - Silent but deadily defines this man. Very little is known about him except the fact he is probably the most suicidal Cruiserweight of them all.
Regardless of the Match and what it stipulates, Gryphon continues to dive from Post-to-Post, taking it to his Opposition. Allan Jordan - The trickster of the NMW cruserweight division who uses words over actions. With a big win over Ricky Shame, Jordan is elevated to a level quicker then most. He's continuously proving himself and for that, many have to wonder how long it'll be until he wears Gold.
Chris Murtock - The dynamic one, who never seems to get shown up. But every man has their time. Murtock has been Swinging Chairs, Dropping Bombs, and
Moonsaulting all over the NMW for atleast three Months. No stranger to CW Gold, Murtock is definetly worthy of this Award. Mike Flash - The current cruserweight Champion who might not be the most worthy for this Award but he continues to make strides others just are not. Flash is improving himself in every aspect while his opposition has become complacent.
Find this man a strong Opponent and a good Angle and he'll fire out like a Bottle Rocket. The move in which makes you go "Ohhhh" and feel sorry for the men it's executed on
Fall of Fate - Sleep Stalker - [See Sleep Stalker vs Mike Flash and Skyler Fury] A DDT Variation Pro-Wrestling has never seen. With Stalker's size, any point of execution is deadly.
Total Elimination - Jim Fraser - [See Outlaw vs Randall Brock at Cold Blooded] Fraser has been laying out Opponents with this huge Elbow Drop for years. And looking at his current stance in the NMW, he'll continue doing so for a little while longer.
Talon Clutch - Gryphon - [See Gryphon Win the Cruserweight elimination match at Genocide] A Submission Hold that can be applied from nearly anywhere, Gryphon has mastered it. What appears to be just a complex Arm-Bar is a devistating
Wrench that even the most massive men will find themselves tapping out. The Bus from Nowhere - Drifter - [See Drifter vs Anthony Withers non title Ladder Match on Fever] Drifter's been riding The Bus for almost three years now and still men cannot seem to come up with a solid counter. Few have actually attempted to create a Reversal for this .. "Wrecking Ball" Maneuver to no avail. Drifter will continue to send Opponents drifting into Nowhere as they continue to step up.
The Showstoppa - Tommy Stylz - [See Tommy Stylz vs James Branchini vs Millionaire Mike at Genocide] Some say this Move is overused. There are many versions of the Shuffle Kick but none delivered with the potency like The
Franchise's. With one swift motion. Stylz can rock your jaw back, sending you into oblivion.
