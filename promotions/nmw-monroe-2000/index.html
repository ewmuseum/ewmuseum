Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A December 2000 note searching for a new webmaster described the promotion, headquartered in Monroe, Louisiana, as four months old, placing its own beginning around August of that year. A reply weeks later, written by the person who had created it, recalled running the promotion for about a month before losing an internet connection and handing control to its current president.

By December the promotion staged its own year-end Spotlight Awards, first as a nominee ballot and then as a results post naming winners across categories built for its own readership: best announcer, best feud, best finisher, most hardcore, best heel and face, best tag team, best entrance, and an overall award drawing fifty votes from its roster and its regulars.

The same month brought two internal notes. One looked for a new webmaster after the promotion's own site handler decided to strike out on a federation of his own; the other looked for a match writer and angle director to help move the promotion from summary results to full commentary starting the new year.

The name is a common one on the newsgroup. This promotion shares its initials and its full title, New Millennium Wrestling, with an unrelated 1999 promotion that relaunched at its own website, and with a second, brief promotion that had introduced itself under the same name earlier in the same year. Nothing beyond the coincidence links any of the three.
