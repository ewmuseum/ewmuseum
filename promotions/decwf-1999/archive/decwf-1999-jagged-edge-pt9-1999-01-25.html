DECWF Jagged Edge, Part 9 (Main Event)

25 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alexa: This is the big one.. Dante: You're used to 'big ones' arent you? Alexa: Hehe heh.....oh, but you arent. Like I was saying. Interrupt me again you obnoxious little bastard, and I'll see how far I can shove my knees in your crotch.
Dante: You're used to that also, hehe.... Alexa: Just keeeeeeeep on. Dante: Yeah yeah....Williams and Blood is the main event for the world title. Alexa: These two have outstanding long-term heat between them dating all the way back to the BsWo where they had a classic feud.
Dante: With Williams getting the upperhand... Alexa: Whatever.....not what I heard, but it doesnt matter. [The opening guitar riffs for "Sad but True" by Metallica blares over the PA system. In perfect synchronicity to the crescendo there's a huge explosion on either side of the entrance ramp. As the lyrics begin to start Williams explodes out of the curtain strutting like no other. He seemingly walks in slow motion as the cameras pan all around him. Flames of fire shoot up as
Williams walks down the isleway. the fans are booing to the top of their lungs, but Williams pays them no attention. He enters the ring then awaits the entrance of Blood with his eyes dead set on the aisleway.]
Dante: Hey Hextall, THAT was an entrance. Take notes. ["Temptation" by the Tea Party plays Once the music is playing, J.P. Blood walks slowly from the back, staring towards the ring. He walks down to ringside and rolls under the bottom rope, his attention focused totally on
Williams]
Alexa: Talking about people that need entrance help. Dante: This won't be a fancy match. These two will fight on pure hatred for one another after what happened to Jim Allen. Williams holds Blood responsible, and rightfully so.
Alexa: But Blood thinks Williams never did like Allen....which is probably right. Dante: I wouldnt own up to liking the idiot either. Anyways this match needs to get started. [AW spears JP Blood to the ground, and starts pounding the shit out of him. They roll around for a few minutes until it spills over to the outside.] Alexa: Well, that didnt last too long in the ring.... Dante: The intensity making itself evident. [AW picks up JP then attempts an irish whip into the steel steps....Blood reverses it sending Williams spilling over the steps and rolling to the guradrail.]
Alexa: Now THAT was some momentum. [JP takes a running leap off the steps then hits Williams face first landing on the opposite side of the railing. JP brings AW over with him into the fans.
They fist fight down the side of the railing near the entrance ramp. AW gets the upper hand and drops JP throat first over the railing.]
Dante: These two are full of 'piss and vinegar' [AW stands up JP then takes a few steps back. He runs forearm first over to
Blood, but Blood bends down and backdrops Williams over the rail to the entrance ramp. AW gets up then Blood launches himself at AW. AW swats Blood to the ramp then proceeds to stomp the shit out of him. Williams picks up JP then does a running Lygerbomb on the entrance ramp.]
Dante: Too bad this isn't falls count anywhere or Williams would have it won. [AW picks up JP then attempts an irish whip into the side of the ring. Blood reveres, but WILLIAMS REVERSES!! DOUBLE REVERSAL...AW picks up Blood the puts him back in the ring.]
Alexa: Well....we're back where we started now. Dante: The hatred really showing here. Alexa: Already said that... [Williams looks under the ring then he gets out a barb wire baseball bat.] Dante: Here we go. BLOOD BLOOD CARNAGE CARNAGE [AW gets back in the ring then goes after Blood with the bat. Blood punches AW in the gut then hits him with a double axehandle to the back sending him to the mat. Blood gets the bat then hits a home run with Williams ribs. You can see the blood start to rise to the skin of Williams as Blood hits AW in the neck with it.]
Alexa: Umm...ouch? [JP picks up AW and hits him with a hotshot over the top rope. He then picks him up, and whips him into the corner. Blood follows up with a reverse elbow hammer. JP sets Williams on the top rope then nails a superplex...the cover...ONE,...TWO,...TH! Two and a half]
Dante: That crybaby better NOT beat Williams. Alexa: Oooohhh so close. Nice combo, though. [JP goes to the outside, and sets a table up. He rolls back in then places
Williams back on the top rope.]
Alexa: He could be going for a belly-to-belly superplex which is a signature spot of his. [JP goes for a move, but Williams blocks....goes for it again...block. OH!! WILLIAMS POWERBOMBS BLOOD THROUGH THE TABLE!!] Dante: DAMN!! Hellfire he just powerbombed Blood from the top through a table.....shit that was bad.
[AW unable to capitalize slumps off the top rope onto the ground. The ref would usually begin to count, but knowing what is at stake he doesnt. Williams eventually gets up, and goes to the outside. He slings JP back in the ring.
AW goes over and gets a chair then follows Blood back in. He sets up the chair then tombstones Blood into it. the cover: ONE,...TWO,...TH!! KICKOUT]
Dante: Now THAT was close....damn. [Williams then goes over and ties Blood up in the ropes.] Alexa: He's eyeing the barb wire bat. [Williams goes over to the bat and picks it up. He then walks over to where
Blood is tied up. Williams takes the bat then WAMS Blood upside the head with it busting him open more than he was before. He then proceeds to beat the shit out of Blood with the bat until the ref stops it then lets Blood go....plus the bat was broken]
Dante: That was BRUTAL! He *BROKE* the bat. Blood is still crawling for god's sake! [Blood keeps crawling at Williams, and Williams laughs at him. Williams goes to the side, and sets down the dented chair that he used earlier. he picks up
Blood into a face-first crucifix position.]
Alexa: Finally it will be over when he hits the AnarchyDriver [Williams picks up Blood then attempts the move onto the chair. But with the last amount of energy that Blood has in his body he sends Williams face first through the chair.]
Alexa: HE'S STILL WITH IT!! Dante: I gotta give it up for both of these guys. [Williams and Blood lay motionless in the center of the ring......after about two minutes they begin to stir at the same time. AW and JP get up...slowely, and Blood nails AW with a fist to the face.]
Alexa: Blood seems to have a second wind. [Blood picks up Williams then hits him with a belly to back suplex....then hits him with a snap suplex. Blood repells off the ropes then hits a fist drop on
Williams. he then picks up the chair, and beats the total HELL out of AW with it until it bends beyond repair. then he slings it down and hits Williams with a takedown full nelson [face first full nelson]
Dante: NO NO NO!! Adam Williams is getting the shit beat out of him. Alexa: This is one of the most brutal matches I've ever seen. [JP Blood goes to the outside, and brings another chair inside the ring.] Alexa: This could be the extreme DDT which is a setup for the Bloodbath. [JP Blood picks up Williams bloody...bloooooody face and NAIL him with a DDT onto the chair.]
Alexa: IT'S NEARLY OVER!! Dante: NO NO NO NO!! [All of a sudden, his music fades, and the lights suddenly go completely black. Rob Zombie's "Return of the Phantom Stranger" blasts over the PA system. As the organ chords fade, a spotlight falls over the side of the ring where Blood is standing.]
Alexa: What the hell is this? [Blood looks somewhat surprised, but very calm. Then, a second spotlight falls over the ringside area, and there stands a strange apparition. The thing, whatever it is, has two legs, arms, and a head, and basically resembles a normal human. However, a huge bloody hole which goes completely through the center of his body. His arms are green with mold and riddled with small worm-sized holes, as are his legs. He is wearing no shirt and a torn pair of black jeans, in addition to black boots with the initials JA. His face is pale and decaying, with dead skin hanging off of some part. However, the identity of the figure is unmistakable...]
Dante: Oh.....my....God! Alexa: This is pretty fucked up cause that's Jim Allen. I thought he was dead. Dante: Umm...I ain't afraid of no ghost? Alexa: This is some pretty scary shit. Just imagine the horror JP Blood is going through right now.
[Blood's eyes are as wide as golfballs, and his skin is as white as a sheet. He points to the undead Allen in shock. The ghastly zombie Allen raises his right hand slowly, pointing towards the ring at Blood. He speaks with a voice from beyond the grave which fills the arena:]
Allen: Jason... why? [Blood seems to be trying to say, "It was an accident", but can't find the words.] You took my life... now, it will not be long before
I take yours...
[With this threat, the arena is then filled with a blinding light as the music suddenly cuts off. When the light is gone and visibility is possible, the usual lights are on... and the ghost of Allen is gone! Blood still stands in the ring with a look of pure horror.]
Alexa: WAIT!! Adam Williams is up! [Adam Williams rolls up JP Blood from behind..ONE...TWO...THREE!!!] Dante: YES!! Adam Williams wins the DECWF World Heavyweight belt. [JP Blood seemingly doesnt give a damn as he is still in shock. He then walks out of the ring.]
Alexa: What's this?....looks like Baxter Goodfunn is over there in the shadows. [All of a sudden as Williams is celebrating we see the president of DECWF,
Jason Cline, walks down the ring with a microphone in hand. Williams doesnt give a rat's ass as Cline enters.]
Jason Cline: First of all that was some pretty fucked up shit. What if all the dead DECWF people came back to haunt us? Yikes, I'd get my ass outa time
REAL quick! But onto other business. First of all, as much as you wont like this Williams I'm reversing the decision. JP Blood is STILL DECWF world champion via he didnt deserve to lose.
Alexa Castilla: WHAT?! Evidentaly JP Blood is still DECWF world heavyweight champion. Dante: Well that was a short title reign. Jason Cline: I know you're pissed Williams but... [Just then Williams hauls off, and knocks the shit out of Cline with the DECWF belt in his hand. He then tosses it on the ground. Williams picks up Cline then hits him with a face first crucific powerbomb on the belt shattering
Cline's trademark RayBans. Williams then proceeds to smash Cline's face into the belt over and over again.]
Alexa:[almost in hysterics] STOP IT!! Somebody needs to stop this. Dante: HAHAHA!! That little brat is getting his ass kicked! [Here comes Flamethrower Rob Dalton, the EWWF Alliance President. Dalton is carrying a chair. Dalton slides under the bottom rope and stands between Cline and Williams.]
Alexa: Thank GOD Rob is here to help Jason... [Dalton raises the chair and turns around and clobbers Cline.] Alexa:[total look of disbelief] Bu...bu..but....that's Cline and Dalton. [Dalton begins bashing Cline in the head with the chair. Williams climbs out of the ring and walks away. Dalton drops the chair and picks up Cline, who is bleeding profusely from the back of the head.]
Alexa:[confused] Why is Rob doing this? [a tear comes to her eye] Dante:[gitty] HAHAHAHA!!! THAT LITTLE PUNK IS GETTING HIS ASS KICKED!! Did I ever say how much I liked Dalton?
[With that Alexa hauls off and knocks the hell out of Hicks knocking him out] [Dalton picks Cline up into a powerbomb position. Dalton runs towards the ropes and hits a running Awesome bomb on Cline through a table.]
Alexa:[crying in hysterics] SOMEBODY PLEASE MAKE HIM STOP!! [Dalton climbs out of the ring and grabs the DECWF World Title. Dalton drives it into the head of Cline. Dalton reaches down and grabs the camera cables.
Dalton begins choking Cline with them. Cline is turning blue. Dalton throws
Cline back in the ring and grabs another chair. Dalton climbs into the ring and lays the chair flat in the corner. Dalton picks Cline up and puts him on the top rope. Dalton with a top rope falcon arrow onto the chair. Dalton calls for a mic.]
Alexa: I need to find some help [she then runs away from the broadcast booth] Rob Dalton : THAT is what the DECWF is about Jason. Violence. Plain and simple, you have missed the fuckin subject. You have TOTALLY fucked up the fed
I created. This is a mockery of the old school DECWF. You just don't get it do you Jason? Maybe you'd like some more?
{Cline crawls towards Rob. Dalton kicks him in the face. Cline slumps down into a pile. Dalton climbs up to the top rope with the chair. Dalton with a frogsplash using the chair onto Cline's back. Dalton reaches down and locks on the kataha-Jime. Dalton is choking out the already near unconscious Cline.
After about ten seconds Cline is completely out. Dalton releases the hold and grabs the mic again}
Rob: You are pathetic Jason. Look at you. Just look at you. Lying face down in your own blood. What's the matter, becoming Prez make you a pussy? THIS
Jason, THIS is how you get results. It's not bitching every other day. It's not about delaying cards for days on end. It's about violence. It's about carnage. It's about beating the holy hell out of somebody else.
I MADE you Jason. Without me where would you be? You wouldn't run the DECWF. You wouldn't be Alliance VP. You'd still be prank calling Shaw and snatching your nuts. You're NOTHING without me, you hear me NOTHING! I MADE you Jason.
This is how you repay me? By making the DECWF the butt of East jokes? Fuck you Jason. I'm out of here.
{Dalton throws the mic down in disgust and kicks Cline in the side of the head. Dalton reaches up and covers his right nostril and shoots snot all over Cline's battered body.....the camera fades with the crowd in utter disbelief.]
