Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

DECWF was already running by 25 January 1999, its earliest surviving broadcast referencing a previous show, “Ataxia Onslaught,” and pitching itself in its own recruitment copy as fun rather than serious: “If you want a fed where you wonder 'What will happen next,' 'Who will die next,' or 'I can't believe they did that' then this is the fed for you.” It counted itself a member of the EWWF Alliance, publicly at odds with a rival member it mocked in its own broadcast as “the god-awfulest piece of $h!t fed in the Alliance.”

Its January 1999 special, Jagged Edge, opened with a montage of the promotion's own past on-screen deaths set to Metallica, broadcast from the Prodigy Pavilion on a fictional Ataxia Isle, with color commentators Dante Hicks and Alexa Castilla filling in for its own president, who was conspicuously absent from the booth.

Its main event crowned a World Heavyweight Champion: Adam Williams against J. P. Blood, fought on real hatred over the death of a wrestler, Jim Allen, that Williams blamed on Blood. The two fought through tables, a barbed-wire bat, and broken chairs until the literal ghost of Jim Allen appeared ringside to confront Blood by name, freezing him long enough for Williams to steal the pin and the title.

The promotion's own president then reversed the decision on the spot, restoring Blood as champion — and was beaten unconscious for it, first by an enraged Williams with the title belt itself, then by the alliance's own president, who stormed the ring, declared on the house microphone that he had personally built the promotion and that its founding ethos was violence, not politics, and left the president bloodied in the ring as the broadcast ended.

No further document survives after that broadcast.
