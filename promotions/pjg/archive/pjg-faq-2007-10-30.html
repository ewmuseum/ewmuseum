KAYFABE STORY ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- On November 15th of 1997 a young independent wrestler, "Tombstone" Timothy Jeter, bought himself a birthday gift. A Powerball ticket. Three days later he was the world's newest millionaire. With the largest Powerball jackpot for many years to come, Timothy moved south from Oregon to Paradise, California. There he bought a large chunk of land in the hills. Now living in a mansion he decided to do something he'd always dreamed of. Start his own wrestling federation. Building an arena in his back yard the stage was set for BYCW. Back Yard Championship Wrestling had the capacity to handle 2,000 people at any one time, though their largest gate was 954 tickets sold. With a large roof supported by columns to protect from rain, the events were otherwise open air. BYCW closed and for two and a half years, Jeter dedicated himself to other business ventures. Once he learned to properly manage a business he reopened his wrestling dream under the banner of Paradise City/Championship Wrestling, or PCW for short. After a strong six month run, PCW was shut down by court order when Suter Kain sued for copyright infringement claiming Jeter was using Kain's intellectual property to make money and won damages in excess of PCW's liquidated value. To this day the tapes of their final event Pompeii have never been released to the public. Ranma Kurotsuki wrestled in high school amassing an undefeated record by graduation. Instead of joining his college team he began a career in pro-wrestling. He was very popular and very violent. Too much so for some promotions who began to black list him. The more violent he got, the more popular he got and it went to his head. He started having trouble in school, but never enough to get him kicked out or suspended. Trying to be more and more popular he banded together with a group of nine other indy workers to start bringing Jisatsu style elements to the various places they worked in. When one of them was left with a serious scar on his face, Ranma began wearing his trademark mask to protect his good looks. When Ranma was nearly out of places to wrestle he and the other nine "Jisatsu Senshi" as they were called on the internet formed the Sendai Jisatsu Senshi Group. This nearly got him kicked out of school due to the time he was spending on organizing the promotion, but in 2004 he graduated with honors. With a prearranged agreement to take over the printing company when his father retired should he graduate to fall back on if SJSG failed, Ranma dedicated himself 100% to the promotion. After meeting through a mutual contact, Timothy Jeter and Ranma Kurotsuki have started a joint venture. The sister promotion to Sendai Jisatsu Senshi Group located in California, the Paradise Jisatsu Group. The company plans to run it's first card before the end of the year, likely in mid November! The current plan is to have both a men's and a women's division. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- 2. I'm the handler of such characters as Kazuki "Tsunami" Shimizu, "Crazy" Kevin Tainer, Moira Faith, Mariko Shimada, Achika Akatani, Bruce "The Oni" Saotome of Lights, Camera, Action fame, and many others. Many, many, many others actually. I've kicked around the idea of another fed before, but never really wanted it enough to try again. Now I want to keep writing for a Jisatsu fed because it's so damn fun and I don't see any active ones around so it's up to me! If you want to know what to expect from me as a match writer check out Andy James vs. Jeff "Madfox" Matthews from Night 2 of TBNIT or the soon to be released Joshi Jisatsu match from same said event. You can also see my work back in NAWC, specifically the two super card main events involving Kevin Tainer. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- 3. PJG ARENA SET-UP ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- The PJG Arena where all our Bloody Paradise shows currently are held is actually the backyard of Timothy Jeter's sizable property in the hills of Paradise, California. There is a professional grade ring with all black turnbuckles, ring posts and canvas with white ropes and ring apron. The PJG logo is emblazoned in the center of the ring in Paradise blue. It's expected this will quickly become stained with blood. Old school guard rails surround the ring and end half way up the entrance ramp. Metal advertising placards will be locked over the front of these. Bleachers surround the ring on all four sides, with a capacity to hold 2,000 fans. The entrance ramp is level with the ring apron all the way down the aisle. A high grade entrance is a left over picked up on the cheap by Jeter when he first opened BYCW. It's attached to a large guest house on the side of Jeter's estate. The entrance itself is your typical steel platform. It has a large oval shaped doorway in it's center with huge steel hands wrapped around it and slightly holding it up. There is a metal sliding door inside it that can be used for a more elaborate entrance. 3 steps lead down from the opening to the actual stage itself. To the left is a small video screen (3x5 feet). ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- 5. RULES ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- I'm not gonna waste a lot of time here. Don't use PJG characters that aren't your own without getting permission and discussing it with me. Don't do anything that couldn't reasonably be done in real life. Dream sequences, sure, just describe it like you'd see in a movie. A whole RP done in your character's fantasies? Sure just act like it's a movie. Which still means you have limits. This isn't a big production. There will not be insane special effects in the promo for a wrestler in a little Jisatsu fed. Basically, just be polite and keep it mostly realistic. If you ever have a problem with a match ending don't come to me angry. Calm down, THEN come talk to me. I'll always be willing to discuss things in a manner that's fair to ALL the parties involved in a dispute. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- 6. ROLEPLAYS & STRATS ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- Roleplays will be sent to me. I will then compile them with commentary by Twist and send them out as the tri-weekly show, "The Bleeding Edge." There are to be NO in-ring segments sent in as material for The Bleeding Edge. However, there will on occasion be clips of matches either from SJSG or "non-taping" shows from PJG. If you are booked in one of these matches it will usually be as a reward for consistently RPing or to further an angle. Strats are highly encouraged! If you do not send in strats your character will behave as close to their characterization during application and RPs have shown them to act, however this might occasionally go against something you have planned. So, again, use strats! They can be extremely effective in furthering your development. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- 7. PJG EVENTS ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- BLOODY PARADISE Bloody Paradise is the PJG's main show. It is broadcast live for a fee over the internet or sold on DVD along with the most recent edition of The Bleeding Edge. This is where angle, inring and backstage segements and most matches take place. Scheduled tri-weekly, the show is usually about 2 hours long give or take. THE BLEEDING EDGE The Bleeding Edge is where you get to see everyone talking and doing what they do. It's basically our flash packet, only with a twist. In fact THE Twist. Twist hosts The Bleeding Edge, generally talking smack about everyone. He doesn't recognize face/heel lines as he favors people very randomly. The only for sure way to get on his good side is to feature a hot chick regularly in your promos. Matches will appear here occasionally in clipped format, as well as the lastest news and rumors. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- 8.
