NEW WORLD PRO WRESTLING [NWPW]
If anybody is looking to join a brand new E-Fed which uses only real wrestlers, than come join NWPW! I will be the President, I've had experiences running a federation before and know that committment is the key to success. My former E-Fed (RWWF) survived for 11 months! Anyway, onto NWPW, the cards will be FULLY written, so no summaries or 2 liner matches. This federation will be fully booked, which means I decide the winner based on roleplaying. Interviews are the most important thing in NWPW, there are a lot of feds that don't give a damm wether you roleplay or not, well NWPW ain't one of them! You MUST be a dedicated roleplayer and able to roleplay about 3-5 times a week. Just to start off, there will be two shows a week, one major show (like RAW and Nitro) and one Spotlight show which features only one Main Event Type Match. There will be a NWPW World Title, a NWPW International Title, and a NWPW World Tag Team Title. More titles will be annouced if there federation grows. You are allowed to have 4 wrestlers and out of those 4 wrestlers, you can make one tag team. So if you wanted to have The Steiner Brothers, you would have to pick Scott and Rick. I have a list of "Superstars" (Located at the end of this message), you can only have one superstar in your control, thus spreading the talent. But like I said, NWPW is based on roleplaying, so you could win the NWPW World Title with Barry Horowitz if you're a great roleplayer! If you would like to join NWPW, then please email me at [detail withheld] with a list of about 30 wrestlers. First come, first serve and remember, you gotta be a dedicated roleplayer! Thanks for your time!
