Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Wrestling Council is a promotion documented entirely by its own unused paperwork. Its rules page lays out a competitive-roleplay federation in detail — one original wrestler per handler, no real-wrestler clones, no simulator, hand-written cards judged on roleplay quality alone, two weekly shows (Tuesday Night Terror and Friday Night Fear) plus occasional Sunday pay-per-views — and its own application form asks for a full character sheet, down to entrance music and a sample roleplay. None of it was ever exercised: its own roster page reads "NONE AS OF YET," its own eight championships are each individually billed VACANT, and its own Hall of Fame names no one.

The site's own closing line dates its application page "© 1999," the only date anywhere in the surviving capture. The Wayback Machine holds the same eight unpopulated pages, byte-for-byte unchanged, from a 2 August 2001 crawl through a 29 August 2009 crawl — eight years in which nothing on the site was ever filled in, up to and past GeoCities' own 2009 shutdown. Because that earliest capture already shows the fed exactly as inert as its last one, its own close is dated circa 2001 rather than left unknown: nothing in the record shows it a going concern any later than the point its own earliest surviving snapshot already found it silent.

A full crawl of all 8 held documents, cold, confirms rather than adds to that reading — there is nothing further in the record to find. The eight championships the fed named for itself are registered as titles in their own right, each permanently vacant; no wrestler, tag team, stable, show, match, or Hall of Fame induction is minted, because none is depicted happening anywhere in the capture. The Staff page's own list of real-world announcers and referees is an aspirational wishlist with no card for any of them ever to have worked — the names stand in the served document, unminted.
