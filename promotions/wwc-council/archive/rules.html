WWC Rules

undated (the site as this pass captured it)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are the rules that govern the World Wrestling Council. Learn them well, as you'll be bound by them every time you access our site. In other words, when you're here, live by this rules, or you'll die by them. 1) HAVE FUN! Remember, that's the primary reason of why we're here! 2) You may have only one wrestler to start with, and you have to Roleplay consistantly with him. He may only be an ORIGINAL wrestler. We don't want to see no real wrestlers, no clones or any ripoffs of any current wrestler from WWF or WCW or ECW, nor a retired/past wrestler nor a hall of famer. In other words, the original wrestler must be totally created, no exceptions! 3) This is a ROLEPLAYING e-fed. The fed will not be using simulators to help us out with the cards and commentary, it will all be hand-written by the Staff members. This means that your roleplays are the ones that are going to set your wrestlers' pushes and ultimately get you the wins. If you roleplay, you'll win, if you don't, you'll loose. It's that simple! 4) When roleplaying, ALWAYS stay on character, and NEVER use other handler's wrestlers in your roleplays without the written authorization of both the handler and Prez Ivan or Prez Rafa. And NEVER EVER use any racial or denigrating comments towards anybody, including wrestlers, handlers and/or staff. 5) As stated before, we don't use simulators here in the WWC, so the QUALITY (not quantity) of your roleplays is what we use to determine the outcome of your matches. Prez Ivan and Prez Rafa, along with the other WWC Staff members have the ABSOLUTE FINAL WORD on the results here in the WWC. We don't wrestle ourselves, and we don't do "alliances" with wrestlers here, so the WWC can be considered pretty damn fair. In other words, I don't want any whining or crying if you loose! 6) These are the basic rules. They have been created for the well-being and for the sake of you and your fellow handlers, as well as for the World Wrestling Council as an entity. Believe me, folks, when I tell you that this rules are not flexible, and so, nor is the Staff that'll be exercising them. Break the rules, pay the price; and the price is either suspension or termination! 7) Ok, now to the fun part! We will start with two weekly cards: Tuesday Night Terror and Friday Night Fear. We will also have occasional Pay Per Views, which will take place on Sundays. These will be announced as they appear. 8) In order to request a match for any of the cards, you must challenge someone in the Roleplay Board. Whenever you feel like you need to have a special match, if you're going on vacation or will be absent for some other reason, if you're leaving the fed for any reason, or if you have any ideas, suggestions or comments, don't hesitate, contact the Staff. 9) If you agree with this rules, then by all means go to the Sign Up Page and join the action today!
