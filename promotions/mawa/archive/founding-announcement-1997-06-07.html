Announcement about a new fed

7 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes this is post about another 'NEW' fed but I'm not going to go the usual route by claiming to be 'The Best Fes Ever'. Right now I just want to get the fed off of the ground. The fed itself is called the Mid-Atlantic Wrestling Alliance. I'm not going to over extend myself by promising an insane number of matches a week, that way lies madness. The MAWA will be booked. Why? Simple, I like people who will roleplay and work angles with each other. As of now there is no webpage, whether or not there will be one remains to be seen. For now any who wish to join the MAWA can contact me, [name withheld] at: [detail withheld] You will then recieve the application. The fed will NOT post a card till i get at least 10 singles wrestlers and 4 tag teams. This way I at least can have some warm-up matches. I'm looking for a roster of about 16 singles (maybe 20) and 8-10 tag teams. I'm not promising the best fed, just one that will try damn hard to meet the requirements of its handlers. My background: I, [name withheld], have been E-wrestling since last September. In that time I have joined many e-feds and seen many of them fall. I have handled such wrestlers as Cruel Angel, Mike "The Mechanic" Malone, El Kondor Magnifico, Rekka Sakura, Nova Storm, The Mystic Beast Kings and Super Tengu. I was Vice-president for [name withheld]'s WICWF, a now defunct organization. It had one Major PPV posted on RSPW-F in Feb. titled Nuclear Winter. The main event for the card was a War Games match that was written by me. I also wrote the entire Cruiserweight Tournament for WICWF. Sadly the fed closed before our second PPV. I was quite honored when I read that Nova Storm had been included in the RSPW-F Top 200 Wrestlers list. i was even more suprised that he was included in the top 100. I also wrote 3 matches for [name withheld]' ICW. Needless to say I have a good background despite a relativly short time in e-wrestling. If you wish to see some of the matches I have written before deciding to apply I would be glad to send some to you. Please give the MAWA a chance. Thank You [name withheld]
