MAWA: Bell Time!! Part 1

1 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[camera opens on a small ring bell against a black backdrop. A giant fist gripping a hammer comes falling down on the bell. As the hammer connects the bell rings 3 times then explodes. After the smoke and Fire clear the MAWA logo with burnging bell appear with the words ‘BELL TIME’ scrolled across it] ----------------------------------------------------------------- m m aaaaaaa w w aaaaaaaa m m a a w w a a m m m m a a w w a a m m m aaaaaaa w w w aaaaaaaa m m a a w w w w a a m m a a w w w w a a m m a a w w a a ----------------------------------------------------------------- BELL TIME [camera fades and opens on a small studio. Standing behind a white podium are Jack Donner and Bob ‘The Bronto’ Bukowski. Both men are wearing suits with the MAWA logo over the breast pocket] Jack Donner: [excited] Welcome ladies and Gentlemen to the MAWA’s first edition of BELL TIME!! I’m Jack Donner and with me, as always, is the incomparable ‘Bronto’ Bukowski!! Are you excited about tonights BIG card ‘Bronto’? Bronto Bukowski: [rather unimpressed by the whole affair and looks at Donner like he wants to slap him] Do I look excited Donner? Are you retarded? The only reason why I’m here is because I’m being payed to!! Jack Donner: [looks shocked] Bronto that’s terrible!! C’mon you must be looking forward to seeing some people compete? Bronto Bukowski: Are you deaf? Read my lips [mouths ‘I’d rather be playing the slots’ to Donner] Jack Donner: [shakes his head] Regardless folks, we have one Heck of a show for tonight coming from the MAWA studios in Salem,North Carolina!! Tonight we’ll see the Demolitionist take on Stan Manson in what promises to be a wild match. Then Japanese legend Masashi ‘LIGHTNING’ Lanther faces the cagey veteran Dutch McNeely!! Bronto Bukowski: OK maybe it’ll be worth it to see Lanther get pinned by a great man like McNeely!! Jack Donner: Then we move onto the man known only as ‘The Unknown’ facing ‘Mr. Bad Example’ Randy Blaze!! Then to top it off, tag team war fare will erupt in our main event as The Nubian Princes, Billy Dee and Isaac Blayze, take on Extremely Portly Pat and Massively Obese Max the team called Larger Than Life!! Bronto Bukowski: [rolls his eyes] Great 2 mounds of blubber against 2 guys no one has seen yet!! Geez, this ain’t wrestling... it’s batch of nobodies!! NOBODIES!! Jack Donner: [looks nervously at Bronto] Ah... well that reamins to be seen... Let’s goto the ring and our announcer ‘BIG’ John Ochlis!! [camera cuts to ‘BIG’ John Ochlis. He’s about 5’7 and looks to be in his 50’s] ‘BIG’ John: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN... Welcome to the first MAWA Bell Time!! Our first match is a singles bout scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring, being led by his manager the Lovely Leah... He weighs in at 270 pounds... HERE IS THE DEMOLITIONIST!! [Demolitionist comes down the walkway with Lovely Leah in front of him. He stands at 6’4 with shoulder length blond hair and green/blue eyes. His face is one of almost manic proportions as he looks at the camera with a very disturbed look] Jack Donner: Well The Demolitionist is making his MAWA debut tonight, any comments Bronto? Bronto Bukowski: Yeah I do actually.. Are those things real? Jack Donner: [confused] Come again? Bronto Bukowski: Y’know her T- Jack Donner: [cutting Bronto off] Time for Stan Manson to make his way to the ring... ‘BIG’ John: And his opponent, being led to the ring by his ‘Supervisors’ Law and Order... [A man enters the runway in shackles around his wrists and ankles. All the chains lead to a collar around his neck. He is escorted by 2 men dressed in Police riot gear. The man wears gray prison fatigues and jack boots] from the San Quentiin State Facility for men, this is... STAN MANSON!! Jack Donner: a little scared] And big Stan Manson is being led to the ring, ‘Bronto’ I understand that you have a little background on this man? ‘Bronto’ Bukowski: Yeah, he’s a real nutcase, thinks he’s ‘The Last True member of The Family’, whatever that means. The California State parole board agreed to let him do ‘community service’ in the form of wrestling for free... I just don’t understand prison politics... Jack Donner: Both men are in the ring, Law and Order are removing Manson’s restraints. The referee looks very nervous, these are both BIG men!! Demolitionist turns to face The Lovely Leah for a pre-match kiss and... MANSON ATTACKS!! Leah is crushed between Demolitionist and the turnbuckle!! Demolitionst wasn’t ready for that and falls to the floor!! Manson now kicking Demolitionist out of the ring!! ‘Bronto’ Bukowski: See that’s what I call an aggressive start!! This Manson knows how to take it to someone!! Jack Donner: Manson rolls to the outside as Demolitionist gets to his feet. Manson charges and is met with a back drop by Demolitionist!! Manson slowly gets up and is met with a clothesline!! Demolitionist picks Manson up and rolls him in the ring and follows with an elbow drop and a cover... 1... Kick out by Manson!! ‘Bronto’ Bukowski: It to early for a cover, besides Leah is still on the matt crying like a cow!! Jack Donner: Demolitionist grabs Manson by the hair, but Manson meets him with a shoulder to the stomach and Demolitionist falls over like a bag of bricks. Manson now takes Demoltionist and bodyslams him down. Lovely Leah is still clutching her Stomach in pain, she must really be hurt!! ‘Bronto’ Bukowski: Heh, she’s just got a little heartburn and a case of nausea. I mean who wouldn’t be bauseous looking at the Demolitionist!! Jack Donner: Manson hits a snap suplex and follows with an elbow drop and a cover 1... 2... Kick out by the Demolitionist!! We need some EMT’s for Leah already!! Bronto Bukowski: That dame has no business being near the ring!! She should be at home bakin’ bread and siring the Demolitionist’s children!! Jack Donner: [rolling his eyes] Oh please not this again Bronto!! [looks to the action] Manson now whips Demo to the ropes. Rebound by Demo, clothesline by Manson, Demo ducks and rebounds again. Manson goes for another clothesline but Demo ducks again!! Demo rebounds... HIGH CROSS BODYBLOCK!! 1... NO!! Manson kicks out again!! Bronto Bukowski: Demolitionist is trying to rally but he don’t got no chance against Stan Manson... I mean Manson is nuts!! Jack Donner: Demo is on his feet, right to the head of Manson, now a left! Right, left, right, left... AND A BIG ELBOW!! MANSON IS DOWN!! Demo now tosses Manson to the turnbucle and follows-up with a clothesline!! Manson stumbles out of the corner and is now kissing the mat!! Bronto Bukowski: Hey look, the EMT’s finally arrived for Leah!! Ain’t it great to know that when someone dials 911 thay you’ll probably die before you see a doctor? Jack Donner: Demolitionist now is on the hunt!! He’s looking to demolish Stan Manson!! He drags Manson up and has him set for a piledriver. Wait, he’s hoisted him over his head, could this be? YES!! He’s going for the Doomsday Decapitator!! No, wait a second he’s stopped... He sees Leah being carried off by the EMT’s and runs to the ropes to see what’s wrong... Bronto Bukowski: He crushed her, that’s what happened!! He injured his own Valet!! Jack Donner: Demo is frantic and confussed, he’s trying to reach Leah but the ref is in the way... Manson is up and rebounds off of the ropes... MOTHER OF ALL CREATURES GREAT AND SMALL!! Manson just knocked The Demolitionist onto The Lovely Leah, who was on a gurney out of the arena!! Demo is injured and Leah is in convulsions!! Manson isn’t finished, he slides out of the ring and grabs Demo and throws him thru the ropes. Demo is struggling to his feet. Manson rolls bakci n and slapps on a sleeper... Bronto Bukowski: This is the set-up... he’s going to polish him off!! Jack Donner: Manson with a sleeper and... WHAT THE?!? He just executed a spinning neckbreaker from a sleepeer!! Demo is out cold!! Manson with a cover... 1 2 3!! Manson wins!! Bronto Bukowski: He calls that move the Helter Skelter!! Let’s get the word from ‘BIG’ John.... ‘BIG’ John: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match... STAN MANSON!! Jack Donner: Manson is leaving the ring as the Demolitionist is slowly getting to his feet. He’s looking for Lovely Leah, she’s been carted out of here already by the EMT’s!! Demo looks ANGRY!! He’s just sprinted out of the ring and charges after Manson!! Demo nails Manson with a fore armn shot to the head!! Manson replies with a right hand!! These men are trading blows back and forth up the runway!! SOMEBODY GET SECURITY OU HERE!! Bronto Bukowski: Heh, now this is wrestling... 2 big men beating the brains out of each other!! THAT’S PURE ENTERTAINMENT!! Jack Donner: Ladies and Gentlemen, We’ll be back after this word... [fade out]
