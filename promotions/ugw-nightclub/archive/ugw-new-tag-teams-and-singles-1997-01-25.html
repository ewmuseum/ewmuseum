[UGW] New Tag Teams and Singles Wrestlers still needed

25 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, I'm starting up a new fed called the UGW. It's scheduled to hold its first card on 2nd February. I want to keep the fed reasonably small to start off with, but I still have open spaces left and want to fill them up quickly. There are two rules of entry to the UGW: 1 Your wrestler must either be a new wrestler or have never held a title. 2. On entering the UGW he must be wrestling exclusively for the UGW for a couple of months before he enters another fed. Right, so what is the UGW? Quite simply the UGW is a small regional extreme fed. There are rules, but the refs are very leniant on letting things go. Each card will come out from an underground Nightclub. There shall be two cards a week. One on Wednesday containing dark matches. (ie a short description of the match and how it ended.) And a card on Sunday which shall be a commentated play by play card. We offer 2 singles belts and one tag team belt. No tournaments have been announced yet. But this is how I plan to do the main title: After two weeks of cards the top five ranked participants in the league shall be entered into a round robin tournament to decide our main champion. The Tag Title shall be done with a four team double elimination tournament. The secondary Singles title shall be decided when the other champions have been crowned. There is still plenty of time for a crack at entering the singles tournament and there is space for one team in the tag tournament. I want to get around seven more active teams for the league and around thirteen more singles competitors. So I IMPLORE you, SIGN UP! I don't care whether you're brand new to e-wrestling or a veteran. I want to get this underway ASAP and hope to have the league running fully by the end of February. If this interests you mail me at... [detail withheld] ...and I'll give you details on entering the fed and an application form. Adrian
