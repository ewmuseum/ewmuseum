and [detail withheld] and we'll be sure to add you. ==================================================== JJG House Show: Sunday, October 14, 2001. ==================================================== Hey, remember me? In 2000, I was the mysterious ACWA fanboy, and things haven't changed. I still love the crew that Strickland Sports has put together, even though a lot of the people have changed.
When I heard this house show was going to take place, I just about wet my pants. Something I can admit to because this is the internet, and I'm anonymous. Yes, this is a good thing. Finally being old enough to drive, I was the first in line for tickets. I got a pair, and brought a date. I won't say her name, because she might read this and figure out who I am, and the Mysterious JJG Fanboy must remain mysterious in order to function properly~!
Before the show, there was this fan thing in the JJG Arena's lobby area. It's really cool that JJG has a home arena now. I live in Atlantic
City now, which means I can see all the JJG I want! Anyway, JOE GONZO WAS
THERE!!! Yes, _the_ Joe Gonzo, who ruled the ACWA for how long? FOREVER!
Ron Strickland got up behind a podium thing, and made a short statement. Basically just saying that the big boys in the wrestling business were in trouble, because the JJG's brand name was going to steal their fanbase, or something. It was cool to see Strickland talking.
There were _so_ many wrestlers signing autographs. The crap jobbers were all there, and wading through them to get to the cool guys was hard. I got Gonzo's signature! He was really cool. Did a few kicks for the fans and everything.
Other guys from the ACWA were there too. Precious Moments. I'm still a little traumatized from last year when Binky hit me with his wand. That was weird, and a little gay. Still, they were cool, and posed with me while my date took a picture of us. We got a pic of her with them too. Boy, wait until the guys at the local photolab get a load of this!
And ... PRESTON MAYFIELD. The world's biggest pervert is back in
Atlantic City! He talked to me, but asked for money for an autograph. It caught me off guard, but I did pay. He offered to take a photo with my date, but ... not with me. She didn't wanna get near him, either, so we walked away.
Mental note: Take the Mayfield poster in my room down before having her back to my place. Some of the new guys in JJG are kinda weird. Julian Campbell has this whole ambiguously gay crossdresser thing going on. And he's _mean_.
Legitimately mean! He said I was judging him! The Great Dave claims to be from Japan, but his eyes aren't asian. What's up with that? Bret Lane IS
SMALLER THAN ME! And I'm in better shape! Hey, maybe I could be a wrestler.
Unfortunately, I didn't get a chance to talk to all the wrestlers. The lines sucked, and the event started before I could get a good look at some of the guys. I took my seat (FRONT ROW!) and took in the show.
"Emo(tional)" Eric Echo -vs- Crackhead. Can you say "One-sided"? I knew you could. This dude's from Trenton, so a lot of people cheered for him. I did too, just going with the crowd and all that. When he got to the ring, he shook hands with the ref, Freddie Jacobs, and slapped him on the back like they were friends. Seemed a little weird to me.
Crackhead, being a redheaded stepchild, an idiot, and out of touch with reality, came in screaming "HARDCORE" just like always. In the ring, he laughed at Echo, and poked him in the chest with his pipe (a foreign object), and Echo ... snapped.
It was a straight-up mugging as the supposed "hardcore badass" was whipped from pillar to post by Echo, who's smaller than he is, by the way. Eric ripped the Sandman T-Shirt off of Crackhead's back, choked him with it, rammed his head into the guardrail, the ring post, asked Freddie Jacobs (who was just letting this all happen) to take a shot at Crackhead, which he
_didn't_. I'm not sure why Echo's being so friendly to the ref. Just sucking up, maybe, but it did seem to affect the way Freddie was acting.
He's usually stricter, from what I remember. After a brutal beating, Echo grabbed Crackhead from behind, lifted him up vertical, then dropped him on his head in what looked like an implant DDT!
Only INVERTED! It was crazy. The crowd popped like mad. I get the impression that Echo was supposed to be the bad guy, but the crowd didn't care. Any reason to watch Crackhead get beat up is a good one, or something.
"Superfreak" Tony Blake -vs- "The Rush" E.J. Drivven. There is something really scary about Drivven. He's not _that_ big, he's just that intense. Coming out, he looked like he could kill a man, just by the look on his face. I thought he looked more like A UFC fighter or bar brawler than a wrestler, but he could wrestle. Boy, could he wrestle.
Blake is actually Superfreak, which is funny. Now his nickname's just a moniker, rather than, y'know, his name. He didn't seem nearly as stupid as before. A little more on the ball. Not scary, though. Not by a long shot.
At first, Blake stayed away from Drivven successfully. He backpedaled, dodged shots, rolled him up (pointlessly, as Drivven kicked at at like a .01 count each time) hit a few dropkicks, even got him up for a bodyslam! I never would've thought he could bodyslam someone that much bigger than himself.
After that, though, Drivven reversed an irish whip, and Blake hit the turnbuckle like a bullet, hit the mat like he'd been shot, and rolled around just to drive the point home. Picking him up again, he did the same thing, but followed him in with a clothesline. Superfreak spat blood.
From there, a gutwrench sitdown powerbomb put Blake on the mat, about ten snap suplexes (which hit the mat like cracking a whip), a running between-the-legs piledriver, and then a BACKDROP DRIVER! The announcer, whose name I guess is Jack Britain, howled "ADRENALIZED!" like ten times.
Drivven hooked both legs, and the back of Blake's head, just to make sure. You could've counted to ten, and Blake never could've kicked out. Hell, he might be dead. Corrupted Morals -vs- the Pain Brothers. The Pain Brothers rule. Just thought I'd say that. They come out, they dance, they stomp, they have these big oversized boots that I want a pair of. That being said, they need to work on their moves.
Corrupted Morals are a couple of drunk guys. One of them is actually named
"Drunkaholic". The other one's Lint. They're drunk. I'm not kidding.
We're talking ruddy cheeks, wobbling on their feet, smiling partiers. I could _not_ keep track of this match. Corrupted Morals kept cheating like mad, not making tags, and beating the snot out of Wayne Payne. Shane came in, and got sidelined by Lint, and they spilled outside. Then
Drunkaholic took Wayne outside. Then the Pain Brothers both reversed irish whips, tossing Lint and Drunkaholic into the guardrail, and followed that up with "mad idiot charge"s, getting BACKDROPPED almost into MY lap!
I slapped Wayne in the face to wake him up. Took a few slaps, but he woke up. I promptly ran away, my date in tow.
When I could see the action again (hard to watch when you're running),
Corrupted Morals had Wayne in the ring again. One guy did a giant swing, the other hopped in and did an inverted DDT, spiking Wayne hard! It was over, but Shane jumped off the top turnbuckle from nowhere to make the save.
Tariq Abdul Aziz, the referee, ordered Shane from the ring, and while his back was turned, Lint hit Wayne with a chair! As Aziz turned around to see what that noise was, Lint tossed the chair to Drunkaholic, WHO NAILED SHANE!
It was crazy! Drunkaholic shoved Wayne into the corner (Wayne being the legal guy, and held up only by turnbuckles), climbed to the second turnbuckle, got an inverted facelock, and FLIPPED into an Ace Crusher! It was unreal! Then, from nowhere, Lint came off the top WITH A STOMP TO THE FACE! As if Wayne weren't already injured, he had to do that.
From there, Drunkaholic, who I didn't even think was sober enough to stand when this match began, dragged Wayne to the center and pinned him. Lint pulled Shane, who was waking up, to the outside for more abuse. I had no idea these guys were so dangerous. They looked like jokes to me, but dammit, they annihiliated the Pain Brothers. Period.
After that, Bob Kyle came out to the ring. Oh, excuse me, "The Matchmaker" Robert Kyle. Kyle's gone from whitebread, middle-American boring announcer guy to a hipster cat. Who knew he had it in him? Howling about how the first "Big Bang" card would be in a week, and the JJG would be LIVE on RTN.
He can really talk now. The entire crowd was popping as he mentioned that there would be big announcements about the Tag Titles, the "Big Badass
Strap" (which seems to be the top title in the league), and other stuff.
Ending his monologue with "You think you know wrestling? You don't know
Jack! But you will..." Bob left the ring to much applause. I was impressed. Really impressed.
"Super Stud" Bret Lane -vs- "Hippo" Higgans. Uh, okay, there's Bret Lane again. He's small. He's puny. I mean, Christ, why'd they give him to Higgans? I thought all these matches were supposed to be lameasses getting stomped by badasses, but with Lane, it was just a really puny man being stomped by a lameass.
I gotta say, he is quick. Lane ran circles around Hippo, mostly in fear, I think. He begged after Higgans floored him with a lariat. Begged for his life. Higgans just yelled "CHOMP!" and went to box poor Bret's ears, but
Lane ducked between the bigger man's legs, turned around, HIT A LOW BLOW, and rolled the fat bastard up!
1. 2. 3. Everybody went insane, Minnie McDougal raised Lane's hand, Bret hugged her, got shoved away, then ran, because Hippo was up, and Hippo was chasing him.
Fleeing through the crowd, I'm sure he'd be dead if he got caught. Man,
Higgans can move when he's mad. I tell you.
"Kaotic Nightmare" Sid Savage -vs- El Gato. Sid Savage is, get this, a mailman. He came out with his mailbag, showed off a T-Shirt with his face on it, and actually got a good amount of crowd support. Me, I never heard of him, but I went along with it. Cheered, etc.
El Gato came out second, and everybody cheered for him too. Maybe this wasn't the best booking, Gato being an old-time crowd favorite, but nonetheless, people appreciated it.
Savage and Gato shook hands. I didn't care for this, but hey, sportsmanship is a virtue, or something, right? They went to their corners, and the bell rang.
Diving, dodging, ducking, Gato kept clear of Savage, who was much larger and more powerful. It was standard Gato gameplay. Keep out of range, then slip a pinfall in when the guy isn't looking.
Savage took some punishment, which surprised me. Gato had gotten better, but still, he's like 130 soaking wet. A spinning leg lariat, some counter wrestling to escape Sid as he applied an armbar, leglock, and he even countered a powerbomb with a sunset flip.
Finally, Sid got a grip on the little guy. A full nelson suplex folded
Gato's neck. He got up, amazingly, just long enough to catch a DVD. A second powerbomb was countered with a second sunset flip, but Sid pulled him up off his back, shoved him in the corner, went for a clothesline. Gato dodged, and started running. Both guys went off the ropes, Gato jumped over, ducked under, then put his head down for a backdrop.
Sid put on the brakes, hit a BRAINBUSTER, and BRIDGED for the pin! It was over, just like that, and nobody saw it coming. I always see it coming, too.
"Motown Man" -vs- Cameron Hawkins. Hawkins came out first, slapping hands with the fans, and acting all happy and crazy. The crowd was instantly on his side. Kinda odd, since I thought that nice guys were passe now in the age of the badass or whatever.
Suddenly, they're everywhere. Motown Man came out second ... and got booed, 'cause he's a moron. But he did some cool kicks and stuff like he's a martial arts master, but he looks like a refugee from the '70s, so how can you get behind him?
Anyway, early on, Motown Man had it in hand. He kicked and punched and bounced Hawkins around like he was a basketball. A top rope powerbomb (!!!)
put Cameron down, I thought, for good, but he kicked out at 2.5, and I was wondering if it was Motown's breakout time.
Hawkins kept taking bumps. It was insane. He didn't look like he could take that kind of punishment, but every time he got back up, people cheered for him louder. The match hit the 5 minute mark, and all Hawkins had done was a few reversals he couldn't capitalize on, and a quick hiptoss that lead nowhere...
Then, as Motown went for a northern lights suplex, Cameron hit a floating DDT. Everybody screamed! Me too! My date, especially, who had been clinging to me, calling Motown evil and stuff. It was cool. She didn't even notice where my hands were.
The ref, some guy named Brannon, got up to a 9-count when Hawkins finally pulled himself up on the ropes. He was using them to hold himself up, but he was standing. Pinning, he got a 1 count.
When Motown got back up, Cameron ducked a spinning back kick, and popped up in a spinning leg lariat. Motown slid to the outside, and got hit with a somersault plancha! Everybody was amazed that this kid could do something like that!
Both guys were hurt, and the 9 count occurred again. In the ring, some cheap brawling occurred shortly before Cameron hit a DVD out of nowhere, climbed the turnbuckles, hit a pose (massive ovation) and nailed a
SKYTWISTER PRESS that left Motown convulsing on the mat! From there, Cameron had to crawl (I guess he was really that hurt) over to
Motown in order to make the pin. When the 3 was counted, we all cheered bigger than ever. Maybe the JJG brand really will be superior. Or maybe
I'm biased. I dunno.
And that was the main event. Hawkins isn't exactly ME material, I don't think, and for some reason none of the old school ACWA guys were in action, but by god, this was a good show. It was great to see that "Uncle Ron" is still in the game, and I look forward to taking in show after great show from Jersey Jack Grappling.
--The Mysterious JJG Fanboy.
