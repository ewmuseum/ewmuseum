Coming on strong with a healthy dose of Badassitude, Jersey Jack Grappling is slated to begin active competition in the third week of October! A unique outlook on the Ewrestling community has allowed my partner, [name withheld], and I, [name withheld], to concoct a truly intense experience in textual Sports Entertainment.
In the posts that follow, I shall show the world of RSPWF just what [name withheld] is made of when it comes to being an Ewrestling booker, a matchwriter, and an editor.
Firstly: Writing samples. An ACWA house show will demonstrate the format of the upcoming JJG house shows. The first HUGE card will show what the "Aftershocks" clipshow format will look like. Finally, the very last weekly episode of ACWA Revolution to remind the world what happened just before Ron Strickland's first financial foray into Professional Wrestling ended.
Secondly: A press conference was handed down the newswire, detailing just what Ron Strickland, 18 months removed from the tragedy that was the death of the Atlantic Coast Wrestling Alliance, had to say. If you didn't read it the first time, you may want to now. You probably won't get another chance!
Finally, the JJG FAQ. Called a "handbook" by myself, because I prefer the word. This 30+K monstrosity details all the personalities you'll encounter on the JJG television programs, describes the weekly cards (3 of them, up to 6 matches each), and gives various other sundry details about
Jersey Jack. Again, if you didn't read it the first time, you might want to now. And to all the loyal handlers that I know frequent this board: I know a lot of you don't have time to join another fed, and I know I haven't invited you specifically, and I KNOW that maybe, just maybe, you're afraid...
Afraid that the JJG is too much for you to handle. Nevertheless, to those of you who are famliar with my work, or [name withheld]'s work, please let the world know, even if you can't join personally ... that we rule.
Yes. Yes we do. And if you join JJG, you WILL have fun! Guaranteed ... or your money back. Thank you all for your time. Feel free to read the following. -- [name withheld]. E-Wrestling's most polarized non-entity. "* TheHolyEyebrow raises. * TheSacredSpleen vents. * EnlightenedNipple tweaks. * BlessedToeNail claws. [TheHolyEyebrow] Excellent.
