Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PCWA introduced itself on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 20 March 1997 with a single recruitment post: an AOL-hosted, real-wrestler-only promotion, its winners settled by “TNM 6.2plus freshed-up version.” The operator's own words set the terms plainly — “This fed is only real wrestlers” — simulated by that engine but decided in part by how a submission was written: “You MUST flash to win. The more you flash the better your chances of winning.”

Its roster policy was capped from the outset: one tag team and two singles wrestlers per handler, plus one manager, joined through a homepage the same post pointed recruits to. No show, champion, or card survives from it; the post is a recruitment ad, not a record of anything staged.

The only trace of what came after is a repeat of the same recruitment ad, posted five days later, on 25 March 1997. Nothing beyond that survives — no closing notice, no result, no second document of any kind.
