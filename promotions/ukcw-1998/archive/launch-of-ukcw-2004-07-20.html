Launch of United Kingdom Championship Wrestling (UK*W)

Jul 20, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. This post is to announce the official (re)launch of United Kingdom
Championship Wrestling, now operating under the "UK*W" brand.
UK*W is a small, regional, independent league based in London,
England. You may remember UK*W from previous times it ran. Although never around for long, UK*W was always a fun deal and had a storied past featuring many recognisable names. And some not so recognisable ones.
Now it is coming back, hopefully as fun as ever! Only the name has been changed ... in order to protect the innocent :)
Broadcasting fortnightly, UK*W is looking for talented individuals to raise the excitement of a wrestling crowd. If you are a former UKCW handler, or even if you're not but you're still interested, we want you!
A number of people have already expressed some interest, but recruitment is still open. If you're interested you'll want the FAQ. As it's moderately large I've not posted it here. If you're tempted enough to want a peek, though, e-mail me at [detail withheld] and I'll send you a copy of the FAQ asap.
Alternatively, if you're interested sign up to the UKCW mailing list at http://sports.groups.yahoo.com/group/ukcw/ - it's a new announcement group with very little currently available, but the FAQ can be obtained from the Files section.
I hope I've piqued your interest. If so, sign up to the ukcw mailing list today! Or send me an e-mail. Or both, really. :)
'Official' launch date with the first show is expected to be Saturday
July 30th. Thank you very much indeed for your attention. (I'm English. Can you tell? :) ) [name withheld] [detail withheld]
