ANNOUNCEMENT: United Kingdom Championship Wrestling (UKCW)

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. This post is to announce the return of United Kingdom Championship Wrestling. It's been a while since UKCW was last "on air", but it will be returning sometime in Mid-June. June 16th is the provisional launch date. For those of you who know UKCW, I hope this will be considered good news - so sign up, damnit ;) For those of you who don't know about UKCW, UKCW is a booked, e-mail based fantasy wrestling league run by myself ([name withheld]) based in London, England. A provisional website exists at www.ukcw.org - at the moment it's a tad out of date, but I shall be updating it in the next few days. (I planned on doing that last night, but I'm on-call for my company at the moment and last night was unexpectedly busy, pager-wise :( ) The website contains a provisional FAQ (which needs updating, as Tag Teams - contrary to what it says - will be welcome). The website also contains some few example cards from UKCW's previous incarnations, so if you'd like to see what UKCW's all about by all means have a read. Some of the handlers from UKCW's previous runs are already onboard (thank you, guys, you know who you are) but there is always room for good new talent. If anyone would like further information, please feel free to e-mail me at the address shown below. Or, indeed, if you'd like an app.... <hint hint ;) > Thank you, and I hope to see you at ringside... [name withheld], UKCW President. [detail withheld]
