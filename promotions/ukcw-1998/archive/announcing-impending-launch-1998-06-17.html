Announcing Impending Launch: United Kingdom Championship Wrestling (UKCW)

Jun 17, 1998, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A moment of your time, please... This message is to announce the impending launch of United Kingdom Championship Wrestling, a booked fantasy wrestling federation run by [name withheld] (former handler of Requiem in IIWF, MLWO, NLWP) and [name withheld] (a.k.a. Osiris/Spunky Lobster. Yes, *that* Spunky Lobster ;) ) UKCW is a small federation based in London, England, and booked by genuine UK inhabitants! Accept no substitutes! :) Although we have some applications already we are currently looking to recruit a number of original single wrestlers and tag teams for active competition. Experienced and beginning handlers alike are welcomed, though priority goes to "new" talent, i.e., those who are not "famous name" or well known wrestlers. The reason for this? Whilst some federations seek out existing famous "names" in e-wrestling, we are more interested in *creating* famous names. Our motto is "Tomorrow's Stars Today" and we hope that, with your help, we can achieve this objective. Simply, whilst it may not be possible to make UKCW the best federation in e-wrestling today (which is awfully subjective anyway), we do hope to make UKCW the best it can be, which will hopefully be enough to make it one of the best of the best :) Originality, creative role-playing and characterisation, plus the ability to write well, is a priority. Please, no blatant Stone Cold clones. UKCW will produce two 'broadcasts' a week: _________________ | | | UKCW UNLEASHED! | |_________________| The primary action card, with limited roleplaying permitted. This will be written up in the form of a house show report from the perspective of a fan in the arena, such as that found on the various wrestling websites on the World Wide Web, as well as various newsgroups on Usenet. The main event will *always* be written fully however, as will other matches when writers become available. In WWF terms, think of this as WWF Raw Is War. _________________ | | | UKCW DRIVE TIME | |_________________| The primary roleplay card, with some recapping of matches carried out upon the previous Unleashed! In WWF terms, this might be considered to be a WWF Livewire, only without Tod Pettingill or Michael Cole :) _________________________________________________ | | | PRESENTERS, ROLEPLAY POLICY, VENUE DETAILS etc. | |_________________________________________________| Can be found in the UKCW FAQ, which goes into much greater detail on the nature of UKCW than space permits here. If you would like to join, or you would like to know more, you can obtain the UKCW FAQ and/or UKCW Application Form by e-mailing me. Alternatively, a UKCW website is at an early stage of development at http://users.powernet.co.uk/magus from which you can download the FAQ and/or application form. UKCW will launch upon sufficient numbers of handlers joining, or early July. Whichever comes earliest! Thank you very much indeed for your time. I hope to see you at ringside in the UKCW Arena soon :) [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) "Excessive belching can cause brain damage and social ostracism. Kids, please don't give in to peer pressure. Play it safe."
