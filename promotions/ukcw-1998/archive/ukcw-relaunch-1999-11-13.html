United Kingdom Championship Wrestling (UKCW) Relaunch

UKCW first launched in June of 1998, but closed down after a couple of months. Whilst running, however, quality was high with such excellent competitors as Jamie Kidd, Chris Douglas, Jack Frost, The Idol, "Krippler" Karl Kruger, Nick Demola, Buster Hymen (briefly), Andrew "Flash" Tucker, Style Over Substance, etc. We are currently looking to recruit a limited number of original wrestlers and tag teams for active competition. Experienced and beginning handlers alike are welcome, though character-wise priority goes to "new" wrestling talent, i.e., those who are not "famous name" or well known wrestlers. Highest priority of all, of course, go to those former UKCW handlers who would once more like to make the trip to London, England... The reason for this? Whilst some federations seek out existing famous
"names" in e-wrestling, we are more interested in helping to create famous names. Our motto is "Tomorrow's Stars Today" and we hope that, with your help, we can achieve this objective.
Simply, whilst it may not be possible to make UKCW the best federation in e-wrestling today (which is awfully subjective anyway), we do hope to make UKCW the best it can be, which will hopefully be enough to make it one of the best of the best :)
Originality, creative role-playing and characterization, plus the ability to write well, is a priority. Please, no blatant Stone Cold,
Rock or Goldberg clones.
UKCW will produce the following regular shows: 1) UKCW ACTION! The weekly live event, broadcast from the UKCW Arena in London. "Broadcast" days have yet to be determined, though Sunday/Monday is currently the most likely.
2) The Zone A weekly interview, news and roleplay show, The Zone will be broadcast from the UKCW studio complex adjacent to the Arena.
Commentators UKCW ACTION! will be presented by the old team of Rebecca Jamieson,
Barry ("don't call me BJ!") Jordan and, of course, the _infamous_ Lord
Sebastian Salisbury.
The Zone will be presented by David "Double" Cross. If you are interested in UKCW and would like further information, you can either e-mail me at [detail withheld] or visit the prototype website at http://www.[detail withheld].co.uk . As yet there is little on the website, but as UKCW grows it will be regularly updated. The UKCW FAQ can be obtained from this website.
