Looking for Former UKCW Handlers

13 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi! I'm looking for the former handlers of UKCW. Due to a disk problem many, many months back I lost the e-mail addresses of most of the handlers (and even, in some cases, the names of who handled who)... So, below is what I've managed to reconstruct of the list of characters and handlers who took part in UKCW over the years. If your name is on the list, or if you know somebody who is on the list, or if you know the e-mail address of somebody on the list, would you be kind enough to e-mail me (or post here, if you'd like)? Thanks very much! Martin oh, btw, the UKCW webpage has been updated to include some previous cards, so if you're interested take a look and let me know whether or not you'd want UKCW to return (or even if you'd want to join a relaunched UKCW. The site is www.ukcw.org .
