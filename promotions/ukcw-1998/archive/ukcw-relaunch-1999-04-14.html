Announcement: UKCW Relaunch

14 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If I could possibly have a moment of your time, please...? Thank you. This message is to announce the impending RELAUNCH of United Kingdom Championship Wrestling (UKCW), a booked fantasy wrestling federation run by [name withheld], assisted by [name withheld].
Hopefully some of you may remember UKCW. We are currently looking to recruit a number of original single wrestlers and tag teams for active competition. Experienced and beginning handlers alike are welcomed, though priority goes to "new" talent, i.e., those who are not "famous name" or well known wrestlers. Although each application will be judged on merit, it's hoped that some "veteran" UKCW handlers may return to the fold ;)
Our goal is simple. Whilst it may not be possible to make UKCW the best federation in e-wrestling today (which is awfully subjective anyway), we do hope to make UKCW the best it can be, which will hopefully be enough to make it one of the best of the best :)
Originality, creative role-playing and characterization, plus the ability to write well, is a priority. Please, no blatant Stone Cold clones.
UKCW will produce two 'broadcasts' a week: FURY! : The primary action card, with some roleplaying. In WWF terms, think of this as Raw Is War. Broadcast at the weekend. INSIGHT : The primary roleplay card, with additional news from around UKCW and possibly some recapping of key events from the previous weekend's FURY! event. In WWF terms, this is a Live
Wire. Further details can be found in the UKCW FAQ, which goes into much greater detail on the nature of UKCW than space permits here. The FAQ can be found on the UKCW website, which is in an early stage of development. At the moment it contains very little, other than the FAQ and applications form. The FAQ, however, should be informative enough.
If you want some info not currently provided on the web page, please feel free to e-mail me.
Thank you very much indeed for your time. I hope to see you at ringside in the UKCW Arena soon!
[name withheld] UKCW President [detail withheld] http://users.powernet.co.uk/magus/
