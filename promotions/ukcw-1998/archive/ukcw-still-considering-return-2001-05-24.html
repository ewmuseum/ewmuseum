UKCW (Re: UK Based Fed)

24 May 2001

Malacian sat before the keyboard on 23 May 2001 21:03:34 GMT and composed the following missive to the rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy newsgroup: Wow. First day "back" with rspwf and what do I see....? >I'm not familiar with any active UK leagues, but I do remember [name withheld] >contemplating about bringing back UKCW. I'd check into that if I were you. Hello... It's [name withheld]. I oughta know, it's my name ;) >He runs a great show. Thanks. You're very kind. Yes, I was giving very serious consideration to bringing UKCW back. I still am, sorta. I was somewhat dismayed at the response I got. I got quite a bit of e-mail, but most of it was the "Hey, you should bring it back. It was cool, and I'D READ IT, variety" rather than the rather more (to my mind at least) optimism-inducing "Hey, you should bring it back. I'D PARTICIPATE" variety. Add to that, I contacted a few old handlers. One or two were still interested, but alas [name withheld] (my former champion) had fallen off the face of the Earth, Matt was really really busy, and I just couldn't even find Steinar. So, three out of my "top five A-list stars" (oops, I'm channel ling Vince McMahon) were unavailable to me. I was understandably (hopefully - hey, I've a sensitive side too, ya know!) depressed there. For a while. And then work flared up with a big triple Oracle database migration which has kept me more or less busy for about the past month, so I've not really had much time to do anything with it. Apart from sulk, and stick little pins into effigies of "Mr Main Event" Chris Douglas, Titanicus, Jack Berg, Karl "Krippler" Kruger, Ferret Boy and "Razorblade" Zakk Wylder... ;) But I *am* still interested. Honestly. And I know that Archbishop Bob is still keen as anything. So, [name withheld], Steinar, if either of you guys are reading this... (And Jase, Mike B, and any of my old handlers, if you're still interested...) I *will* bring UKCW back. IF any of you guys tell me I should. OTOH, if you're so pissed off with all these promises of "Maybe I will" and all that crap (and I can't say I'd blame ya), just let me know and I'll go away :) Thanks. [name withheld], ex-UKCW president. [detail withheld] "A PC without Windows is like a chocolate cake without mustard" [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) - atheism is a non-prophet organisation.
