Hello. Inspired by the "Any old schoolers still around" thread - and my own dark and sinister thoughts over the last few weeks - I'm planning on returning to the fedheading world.
I'm currently plotting to launch a small, regional, independent league based in London, England, and absolutely probably certainly not called
UKCW* - alternate names are being considered carefully, though most of them suck.
You may remember UKCW* from the first time it ran. Or the second. Or the third. Possibly the fourth, though I doubt it since that one lasted 3 seconds :)
Although never around for long, UKCW* was always a fun deal and had a storied past - usually involving Jack Frost being hit with road signs
- including such luminaries as: The afore-mentioned Jack Frost. "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas (former champ). Nick Demola (former champ - will be returning) "Krippler" Karl Kruger Andrew "Flash" Tucker (former TV champ) "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian The Idol Jamie Kidd Pedro Kozaro "Everglades" Al E Gator Lord Sebastian Salisbury BOB (Especially BOB) Ferret Boy "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards Ryan Donahue "Reckless" Jack Berg The Anti-Gravity Kid Plus many others over the various iterations. Things are at a very early stage - I've got a great "venue" lined up,
Im currently in the process of finalising a FAQ, etc, so I just thought I'd post this to "test the waters" so to speak, and see if there's any interest ...
So, if there are any former UKCW* handlers out there, or anybody else, who'd be interested in once again being verbally assaulted by heel announcer extraordinaire and all-around git Lord Sebastian Salisbury, please let me know.
