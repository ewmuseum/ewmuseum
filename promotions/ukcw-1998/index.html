A London-based booked fed launched in 1998 under two co-presidents and the motto "Tomorrow's Stars Today," relaunched by the same operator under a near-identical motto across several further short-lived iterations through at least 2006. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

United Kingdom Championship Wrestling announced its own impending launch on 17 June 1998: a “small federation based in London, England, and booked by genuine UK inhabitants! Accept no substitutes!” Its own motto, “Tomorrow's Stars Today,” ran unchanged through every relaunch that followed, alongside a standing house rule against “blatant Stone Cold clones” and a stated preference for new talent over famous names.

Its own two weekly broadcasts changed names across the years — Unleashed! and Drive Time in 1998, Fury! and Insight in 1999, then Action! and The Zone from a November 1999 relaunch, presented by Rebecca Jamieson, Barry Jordan, and “the infamous” heel announcer Lord Sebastian Salisbury — but the format stayed the same: one action card, one interview and roleplay show.

The promotion relaunched under that same premise repeatedly across its own surviving record: within 1999 itself, again in 2004 under the “UK*W” brand, and twice more in 2006. Each relaunch called back to the same roster of returning names — Jamie Kidd, “Mr. Main Event” Chris Douglas, Nick Demola, “the Krippler” Karl Kruger, Andrew “Flash” Tucker — and its own 2004 relaunch post remembers Jack Frost by name as the wrestler “usually involving…being hit with road signs,” alongside a wrestler recalled only as “BOB (Especially BOB).”

The last surviving trace of the promotion runs to 1 March 2006, a recruitment post from its own president reporting nine wrestlers on the roster and a few spots still open. Nothing further survives.
