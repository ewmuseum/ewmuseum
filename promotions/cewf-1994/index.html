Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Cutting Edge Wrestling Federation opened on a bulletin board system on 1 June 1994 with about fifteen wrestlers, running a weekly Saturday house card plus five annual pay-per-views. Its own house style leaned on constant in-character interviews rather than results alone, its commissioner billing it as carrying “more role-play interviewing than ANY federation on the market, because any player can post whenever they want.”

Its first year turned on a feud between Bad Company, “Flashy” Freddy Massey and “Deadly” Dirk Venom, and the Horsemen, Al Andrewson and Roger DeCarnac; a retrospective column that October credited the CEWF's first Television, United States and Battle Royal champions to that same roster. Massey won the Television title through a loophole — his partner, not he, had fought his way into the deciding battle royal — and held it for months on interference alone before Andrewson finally beat him clean; Venom took the Battle Royal championship outright on a European tour; DeCarnac dethroned the masked Gravedigger, played on-air by the commissioner himself, for the United States title before putting it up against Lightcrest, the promotion's own World Champion, for a shot at the top belt.

The CEWF relaunched three times over about fifteen months under two different on-air commissioners. Its final surviving announcement, in December 1995, invited newcomers back under a new BBS host, promising the same weekly card and pay-per-view calendar under the same commissioner, posting under the on-air alias Keeper of Fate.

No closure document of the promotion's own survives; that December 1995 relaunch notice is the last dated document held.
