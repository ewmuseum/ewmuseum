"[CEWF] Intro 1/2"

13 October 1994
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Here's some more commentary from the peanut gallery. Vote Keeper of Fate for CEWF Booker!] MO>... and the TV Title saw MO>"Flashy" Freddy Massey control all in his brackets to win the gold. Well, not exactly. Actually, he slipped through the cracks to get the title. He didn't have the best win/loss ratio, but he *DID* have his partner, Dirk Venom, in the Battle Royal match that decided the champ. MO>... Even though the MO>Horsemen hold more gold, Bad Company is currently, in *MY* opinion MO>winning the dominace award. The feud has been highlight by many DQs, MO>torn out knees, bloody matches, and even Bad Company putting one of the MO>original Horsemen out of wrestling. You flatter me, sir! I've tried my best to petition for more exchange of dirty tricks between my Bad Company and the Horsemen, but so far, I've been fortunate to get the lion's share of breaks. Makes for some Drama, though! MO> Moe Puddin' - The Party Man extrodinaire in the CEWF. One of the most MO> popular wrestlers in the Federation. He has a high MO> energy style that makes him a dangerous opponent. His MO> only set back is that he is the most hard luck wrestler MO> today... Not to mention the fact that our SysOp host is the player. I hope he gets involved more once he gets all the "real life" computer and netware setup. MO>Walmart Stock Boy - Relatively new wrestler that made an impact as soon MO> as he entered. He carries a shopping cart full of MO> stuff to the ring and has used a mob to club people MO> in several of his matches. He quickly rose to the MO> number One contender spot but disappeared around the MO> time he was to recieve his shot. Rumor has it he is MO> back and VERY upset. You know, I had my doubts about this guy's "angle" (I'm sorry, but 'The Wal-Mart Stock Boy' seemed real stupid), but he ended up with a 'magic touch' and won several BIG matches. He just had the right ability scores and moves, and cut his way to the top. I think my man Dirk Venom is STILL upset about this guy beating him, and still wants to get him in a Chi-Town Rumble match! MO>TaZzEr - A short, fast wreslter that seems to go through streaks. MO> He'll be hot for a few matches then seem to fizzle MO> out. His unusual style makes him an interesting and somewhat MO> unknown opponent. I seem to recall that he was going to form a tag team with WMSB or somebody. Maybe his luck will improve. MO>"Flashy" Freddy Massey - One half of Bad Company, Freddy is a VERY MO> IMPRESSIVE singles wrestler. He not only MO> won the TV Title but successfully defended MO> it on numerous occasions. At one point in MO> time he held the record for the person MO> to longest hold a title. Freddy's one MO> weakness is a bad knee, given to him MO> courtesy of Roger DeCarnac. Well, again, his win/loss record really bites, but that didn't prevent him from becoming a champ. Of course, he cheated more than virtually any OTHER champ, but even so, he outlasted the BIGGEST cheater/champion. MO>"Deadly" Dirk Venom - Second half of Bad Company, Dirk is one of the MO> most awesome and most physical of all the CEWF MO> wrestlers. He height is only surpassed by his MO> strength was made apparent in his winning the MO> Battle Royal Belt during the European Tour. I dunno what it was about Dirk, but he just couldn't lose there for a while. He MUST have the best lifetime career record of the original wrestlers (even more than the CEWF Champ, Lightcrest). He's wrestled so often, so successfully, he was bound to get a title sooner or later. Especially when it comes to battle royal matches. He's the champ of that sort of match in every since. He came close to winning the TV Title in the initial battle royal, being one of the last 3 wrestlers in the ring. He's been in at least two battle royals since, and won both (I believe there's been two), including the second for the Battle Royal championship. I just got the magic combination with him. MO> Big Blag - A HUGE mountain of a man, over 500 pounds of dimwitted MO> wrestling machine, but however he *IS* very effective MO> in the ring and as fullfilling his role as muscle for MO> Bad Company. His manager, the CPA Lord, makes him MO> doubly dangerous in and out of the ring He has possibly the worst win/loss ratio (not entirely surprising), but great to have around for his character. There's nothing like seeing an interview with Blag slobbering and devouring sweets while the Benevolent CPA Lord Ned does the talking. Bad Company hired him for a reason that will become clear in time! MO> The Gravedigger - Truely one of the most awesome wrestlers in the MO> CEWF. Dressed totally in black, this man utterly MO> destroyed just about EVERYONE he encountered when MO> he first came to the CEWF. Since then he has been MO> a constant thron in the side of the Horsemen, mainly MO> in the side of Roger DeCarnac. They have the largest MO> personal feud in the CEWF to date. The GD has taken MO> everything dished out at him and nothing seems to MO> slow him for long. If he has a weakness, it may ne MO> his knee as discovered by Roger DeCarnac. Well, we ALL know whom he's based on, but hey, the Commish himself plays the 'Digger, so we'll forgive him, right? :) At least he doesn't borrow his model's lines too often. MO> Al Andrewson - "The Enforcer" is one of the orginal Horsemen. Al is a MO> complete wrestler. He can wrestle with the best of MO> them or get down and brawl with the best of them. Al MO> finally ended a month long quest for the TV Title by MO> finally beating Freddy Massey after Freddy beat him MO> several times in a row. Talk is he and another MO> Horseman are going to form a tag team to go for the MO> gold there. It's been a long, drawn-out conflict between him and Freddie, and if he goes into tag team wrestling, it's only going to get worse! Besides, the only reason he DIDN'T get the title sooner is because Freddie had Dirk interfere half the time. After being DDT'ed for the millionth time
(slight exaggeration), Freddie had to finally lose the title. You know, the funny thing is: Freddie has NEVER successfully used EITHER of his special finishing maneuvers on Al, or anyone ELSE for that matter (in a match that was detailed). MO> Roger DeCarnac - The Ironman, is kinda the "pretty boy" of the MO> Horsemen. A "pretty boy" who has beaten everyone MO> but Moe Puddin and Lightcrest in the CEWF at least MO> once. He is a tremendous techincal wrestler but MO> is noted for his long, marathon matches. He won MO> US Title from the original holder, GD, and held it MO> till he put it up against Lightcrest for a World MO> Title shot. He is currently in the process of MO> regaining his belt. It is true that he's been in more 'all-nighters' than any wrestler *I* can recall. His endurance is legendary, and he's also single-handedly defeated the Gravedigger on virtually every occasion.
