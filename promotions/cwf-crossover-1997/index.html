Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Crossover Wrestling Federation opened on 13 May 1997, run by the former president of the Parody e-Wrestling Federation on one deliberately simple premise: forty top stars of the real WWF and WCW, one member per wrestler, cards in summary format, “low maintenance for me and you alike.” Its own gimmick was timing rather than invention — it ran exactly one week behind the two real promotions, so that any title change on an actual Monday Night show or pay-per-view became the Crossover Wrestling Federation's own result too. Its own disclaimer was explicit that this was a not-for-profit rebooking sim, “not intended to infringe on any copyrights” of either company.

Its only surviving card, dated 26 May 1997, carried that mechanic through six title matches — The Undertaker defending the WWF Heavyweight belt against Bret “The Hitman” Hart, The Outsiders defending the WCW Tag Titles against Lex Luger and The Giant, Ultimo Dragon defending the WCW Television Title against Steven Regal among them — plus ten further non-title matches drawn the same way from that week's real results, several marked as taken directly from an actual televised show. A roster change accompanied it: Glacier dropped from the federation's lineup, Jeff Jarrett added in his place.

No document later than that 26 May card survives, and no closing notice does either — how long the federation kept pace with the real promotions it was rebooking isn't in the surviving record.
