Crossover Wrestling Federation (WWF vs. WCW)

13 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the former President of the Parody e-Wrestling Federation (PeWF)... comes the Crossover Wrestling Federation!! The Crossover Wrestling Federation (CWF) is comprised of 40 top superstars from the WWF and WCW (No ECW or other feds, since I don't follow them well enough to keep up the writing). Cards are summary format, and this is intended to be low maintenance for me and you alike. Cards will start on May 26th whether or not the CWF is full or not (extra players are controlled by me until they are taken). Here's the catch: The CWF runs one week behind the WWF and WCW, and all matches that happen on Monday Night shows or PPVs (of WWF or WCW) happen in the CWF as well. For example, if Shawn Michaels has managed to win the WCW Television Championship, but then on Monday Night RAW, Michaels loses to Bret Hart, in the CWF, Bret is now the WCW TV Champ. Sound fun? Mail to [detail withheld] to sign up. You may have ONE wrestler, WHETHER OR NOT HE IS A TAG TEAM COMPETITOR. (For example, you may be Hawk OR Animal, but not both). The reason for this is that all tag team competitors may also wrestle singles matches. When you sign up, indicate your top 3 choices: BTW, no profanity allowed. Flamers will be referred to their respective webmasters. WWF: The Undertaker, Owen Hart, The British Bulldog, Bret "Hitman" Hart, Shawn Michaels, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, Mankind, Vader, Road Warrior Hawk, Road Warrior Animal, Rocky Maivia, Doug Furnas, Phil LaFon, Goldust, Hunter Hearst Helmsley, Ahmed Johnson, Farooq, Crush, Savio Vega, Sycho Sid WCW: "Hollywood" Hulk Hogan, Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, Syxx, Macho Man Randy Savage, Diamond Dallas Page, Sting, Lex Luger, The Giant, Booker T (of Harlem Heat), Stevie Ray (of Harlem Heat), Chris Benoit, Ric Flair, Rowdy Roddy Piper, Steve "Mongo" McMichaels, Ultimo Dragon, Dean Malenko, Rey Mysterio Jr., Lord Steven Regal, Glacier These are the 40 people, who, in my opinion, are experiencing a "push" right now. If you want to be someone else, I am open to a MATURE AND RATIONAL argument of why that person should be on the roster, and why someone else from the same fed should not. (Like, you could argue that Kevin Sullivan should be on the roster, and Steve McMichaels, could not. I'm just not sure why) Expect rosters to change, and you have the opportunity to change "handled" wrestler.
