Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

BUWE surfaced in a 6 January 2002 update that already treated the promotion as running, its poster linking a flash packet and directing former National Extreme Wrestling and Fantasy Wrestling Group mailing-list members who hadn’t received it toward BUWE’s own new roster list on Yahoo Groups.

A second notice, 14 January, reports the promotion’s own mailer failing to reach some of that list and asks anyone from the National Extreme Wrestling or Fantasy Wrestling Group rosters, or anyone who had applied or been asked to join, to confirm their status against it directly.

No founding statement, roster, card or closure notice survives beyond those two administrative notices.
