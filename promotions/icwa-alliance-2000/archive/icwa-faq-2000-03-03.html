What is ICWA? 1.1 Fantasy Explanation ICWA, as you may have read, stands for International Championship Wrestling Alliance. ICWA was opened in March of 2000 by it's current President Joshua Blake. ICWA has been in the making for a _long_ period of time, and it is _completely_ ready for the big time! 1.2 Real Explanation ICWA was started in March of 2000 by [name withheld], a handler of wrestlers in feds like; MCW, MWA, ACWA and UWC. He has a hand working with feds such as;
DVWA and MWA. He knows what's going on and (hopefully) what you would like to see in an e-Wrestling federation. He can be contacted at
[detail withheld] , [detail withheld] on IRC and [detail withheld] on AIM. 2. Who runs ICWA? 2.1 Fantasy ICWA is run by Joshua Blake, a former wrestler who was forced into early retirement from repeated leg injures. He recently came into a large sum of money and decided to follow his true dream.. Open a world wide wrestling federation.
2.2 Real Life ICWA is ran by me, [name withheld]. As of right now, I am in it alone, card-writing would be _VERY_ much appreciated. I will soon be asking people to help me out with certain things, just about any help would be great! One note though, if you aren't going to write matches, don't say you will and back out at the last second :)
3. What you have to do? 3.1 Role-play Role-playing is the whole point of the ICWA, When you have a match, you should role-play, even when you don't it helps to further angles. Role-plays shouldn't be long, or short.. They should be a size that makes you comfortable and lets you get your point across. You cannot role-play more than 3 times a week, after that, they will not be "graded."
3.2 Strategies Strategies help me _ALOT_. A strategies (strat for short) is the way you tell me what you are going to do in your match. A _good_ strat will have your wrestler's written out entrance, some suggested holds or part of the match, and what will happen in each outcome, or perhaps, if a special angle is worked in.
3.3 Angle Ideas Angle ideas could sometimes be the most important part of the decision in who wins and loses, if an important angle is going down in the match, that would have more pull than role-plays.
3.4 Other Uhh, sucking up :). Actually, If I know you are dedicated to the ICWA, you will have a slight advantage over the others. Say you have been around for 3 months, and a guys comes in and debuts against you, and the role-plays are about even in quality, I will give the match to the won who cares more, usually the older guy. Dedication is a big thing here in ICWA, either you have it or you don't.
4. ICWA's shows 4.1 ICWA Presents "Psycho Saturdays" Psycho Saturdays (PS for short) is ICWA's weekly show, very comparable to WWF's Raw is War. PS will, you guessed it, by sent out on Saturdays around 10 o'clock p.m. eastern time. It will feature Play by Play matches and the finest stars that ICWA has to offer.
4.2 ICWA "In Your Face" In Your Face is ICWA's flashcard, sometimes called a role-play packet. This is what you role-plays will be sent out in. They do not have a specific date, and will come out whenever I feel the need.
4.3 Pay-Per-Views PPV's are the big show of every wrestling organization, it works the same here at ICWA, we will have one PPV every month, these will have the biggest matches, most suprises and most fun. :)
5. Names and Faces 5.1 Paddy Martins Good Ol' Paddy, Paddy is a big lovable teddy bear of a man, who happens to love the mindless violence of Wrestling. Paddy is the "good guy" commentator on
"Psycho Saturdays" plus he is the know it all of the team. He can name any move from the "Bodyslam" to the "Flying Twisting Mexican Back Destroyed." Ouch.
5.2 Simon Brooks Eww! The evil "bad" commentator. Simon isn't really that bad, so what if he thinks cheating is good as long as you don't get caught.
5.3 Melissa Edwards Melissa is _gorgeous_, it looks like she steps right off the model's runway and into ICWA headquarters. Her job is a backstage interviewer, she does her job very well, either that or she looks _great_ doing it.
5.4 Alex Stevens Alex is the rookie in ICWA, he has only about a year experience, and he still has trouble with wrestlers. He, also, is a backstage interviewer. He is a good interviewer, but usually pisses the wrestlers off. His dream is to one day be the host of "Psycho Saturday"
5.5 Joshua Blake Blake is the head honcho, he owns the place, and runs it. He gets involved in very few angles, but he usually makes a few appearances on "Psycho Saturdays."
6. Titles of the ICWA 6.1 ICWA World Title This title is the main title of the federation, if you have this you are _The Main Event_! There is no higher rank than this title and it will be defended often, if not weekly.
6.2 ICWA North American Title This title puts you at a close second to the World champ, lots of people use this title as a stepping stone to the World title to show that they are for real.
6.3 ICWA Lightweight Title This title is for wrestlers who are 230 pounds and lighter, this is the World Title for the lightweights and is held with high regard and respect. 6.4 ICWA Tag Team Titles This is the ICWA title that is given to a tag team, anyone can form a tag team, two singles wrestlers who have been cold lately or anything else. This title can usually jump-start you career at ICWA since Tag Teams are well liked there.
