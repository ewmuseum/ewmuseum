[ICWA] - Press Conference

2000-03-13
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*CLICK* *CLICK* *CLICK* [Dozens of cameras go off as the camera pans back in the small conference room, many sports reporters are shouting out questions. Everyone on the board in waiting for the TV signal so they know when to start the conference. A small hand from behind a camera is waved and a skinny man, clad in a black suit stand up and walks to the podium.]
Man: "Hello, and welcome to the inauguration of the ICWA. Tonight, President
Joshua Blake is going to go over a few thing about the ICWA, not to mention...
The first ICWA card!!" [The attendance begins to applaud as the man slowly sits down and a very confident Joshua Blake stand up. He walks to the podium and signals for the people to stop applauding.]
Joshua Blake: "Thank you, thank you. First I would like to give thanks to all the people who made this possible. While I can't thank everyone right now because of the time that it would take, I would like to put a heartfelt thank you out to everyone who helped.. You know who you are."
[Blake takes the mic out, and walks away from the podium.] Joshua Blake: "Heh, I guess old habits don't die. You see, I myself used to be a wrestler.. But that was then, this is now.. I realized I can't wrestle anymore.. My body just can't take it.. But I can't stay away from this great business.. I have to do something.. And ICWA is the something I did.. And folks.. [laughs.] It's gunna be one hell of a ride.."
[A reporter stand up with a question.] Reporter: "Mr. Blake, Can you name some of the talent that you have hired so far, and maybe a little bit about them?"
Joshua Blake: "Why, of course I can. First we have a couple people I knew who are in wrestling.. People like Pico Flush, in my opinion, the toughest, hardest worker in the business.. Kurt McCready, who you may know from MCW, is also working his way up the ranks, having fun the whole time.... Then we have others.. People like Mue, who is a pure fighting machine.. I can't wait to see
Pico Flush and Mue lock horns..."
Reporter: [Interrupting.] "Mr. Blake, it seems you act as if you are more of a fan then a part of the show.."
Joshua Blake: "Is that so bad.. It makes the whole thing better for everything.. I am a fan of wrestling.. I know what fans want to see.. Back to some of the talent.. We have "Special K" Jon Krieger, the only man to interview from a toilet stall.. Not to mention people like Michael Pearson, a complete metal head.. And Tyr, a man who's head is probably made from metal!!"
Reporter: "So, Mr. Blake, with all this talent, when could we see a first card, and where could we tune in to see it?"
Joshua Blake: "I thought you would never ask. ICWA bookers _have finished_ the first ICWA Psycho Saturday and I have inked a deal with ESPN."
Reporter: "So what are we going to see next Saturday?" Joshua Blake: [Looks toward a few sitting men.] "Think they are ready? [One of the men nods and smiles.] "Alright, the _first ever_ ICWA Psycho Saturday card will be opened with "Prime Cut" Shawn Fleming taking on Mue! Then we will see
Kurt McCready, in his first non tag-team match against none other than, the monster of a man.. Tyr!!! Oh, and that's not all.. Next we have "Special K" Jon
Kreiger taking it out back with "The MetaL" Michael Pearson!! With the main event being.. "The Hammer" Alex Cote... Pico Flush.. One on One.. Falls count anywhere!!!!!"
Reporter: "Wow, sounds like a great card.." Joshua Blake: "You can say that again.. Any other questions?" [The room is silent, except for a few whispers about the card.] Joshua Blake: "Well then, I hope to see you all there.." [The camera fades to black, with the last visable image being Blake and Co. walking off stage.] ------------------------------------------------------------ This message brought to you by [name withheld]. - ICWA President - Used to handle: "24 Karat" Kerry Powell - Proud member of the RSPWF Suikoden club! :)
