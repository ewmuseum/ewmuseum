[ICWA] Presents.. "IN YOUR FACE"

2000-04-03
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
====================================================== ICWA PRESENTS: "IN YOUR FACE" In today's edition - [1] "Prime Cut" Shawn Fleming [2] "Flying Hurricane" Elijiah Bennett [3] Mue ====================================================== <><><><><><><><><> FLEMING <><><><><><><><><> **VROOM** **VROOM** [A car, Shawn Fleming's 2000 Ford Expedition to be exact, flys by the camera going at least 80. A few seconds later, sirens are heard and blue and red lights are seen. The camera is right behind the cops and the cops are RIGHT
BEHIND the Expedition. Slowly, the truck pulls over to the side of the road, immediatly followed by the cop car. A few brief seconds of nothing go on, and then the cop slowly gets out of his car, followed by the cameras. The cop knocks on the window.]
Cop: Hey pal, do you have any idea how fast you were going in that 35 zone? Fleming: Uhh, I don't know, maybe around 40? Cop: Don't get smart with me, son. Fleming: Uhh, alright, how about 42? Cop: I'm going to have to ask you to step out of the car. Fleming: No way! I know my rights! As soon as I get out of the car your going to beat me with a club! And I don't want that!
Cop: Out of the car!! NOW!!!!!! [Shawn Fleming, very scared, slowly climbs out of the car. The cop doesn't do anything at first and just looks at the 300 pound Fleming.]
Cop: You a big fellow arn't you? Fleming: Uhh, your not gunna rape me... Are you? Cop: NO!!!! Geez, where were you going? Fleming: To the local whore house of course.... [Realizes what he just said] Uhh, I mean.. Uhh, the super market... Cop: Whore house, ehh? Fleming: Haha, no no silly, The supermarket! Cop: I don't think so buddy.. Fleming: But... But... Cop: Your going to have to show me some ID. Fleming: Fine.. [Shawn hands his lisence to the Cop, and the Cop looks it over for a few seconds.] Cop: Your Shawn Fleming from the ICWA!!! Your the man!!! Fleming: Uhh, uhh, yea.. Yea, I am the man!!! Cop: Well, do you want an escourt to the whore house? Fleming: ........................... Cop: Well? Fleming: Sure......... [They both climb into their cars and drive off into the sunset to get laid.] <><><><><><><><><> BENNETT <><><><><><><><><> -- Scene opens outside the vacent parking lot at what was the Seattle Kingdome's lot. Once filled with cars and signs of baseball and football now has pieces of rubble debris and litter aside. The camera focuses on where the
Kingdome was once standing now metal gates don't let you past halfway to the old Ticketmaster stand. The camera fades away to black and picks up to a young man no older than twenty five. He is sitting indian style looking at the rubble left behind studying the piecies of rock that lay aside. He gently and anxiously arrives to a standing position and arrives in front of the camera.
Few people are on the back for it is night around midnight. The city has a dark eerie smell of must in the air. The streets are silent and the cars are few and far in between. Now the kid begins to talk gets in front of the camera and begins to talk. --
Man - The world has come to such a dark eerie mischevious self today who wonders me make life appear on this hellasious life. I imagined this as how someone's world will come down during my reign of terror begins in the ICWA.
The name is Elijiah Bennett otherwise the Flying Hurricane. The walls break down as if I were desimating their future. Then they explode in agony and
twist to their final breaths. There is nothing left but little pieces of what could have been. You see everyone what all this means is I may not have to be seven foot nine inches to become a superstar and reach havoc upon the world. I am six foot less than two hundred and twenty pounds. The ultimate athlete who can give thunderous blows and deathdifying leaps. Someone like me only comes every one million years. I bring the ability to this universe to lead the extreme be the cruiser king of the world and supass the giants.
-- Camera quickly cuts to the implosion of the Kingdome -- -- Fades into previous action of Elijiah -- EB - When earlier this week it was announced I had a match against some Spanish champion named Paco Sanchez I laughed and called executives asking if this was true considering he isn't even on the ICWA roster. When they told me they hired him for one match on the theory that there are no other lightweights in the division I said dimly "Well give the damn title then." I still haven't gotten a response on the situation however I do think we will see the title around my waist shortly. If there is no competition in the class who the hell else will get the title. Anyways if not I have a better goal on someday becoming world champ.
--Elijiah starts to walk around with his hands clasped over his face trying to keep warm from the brisk breeze blowing in the the Puget Sound a couple miles away-- EB - This very Saturday at Psycho Saturday I have my first match in the ICWA against a entrepanuer from Spain. Paco Sanchez was the original El Torado. He was a four-time GSWL (Global Spanish Wrestling League) champion. However, with such a storied past the man's time at top the globe has finally with this match come to an abrupt halt.
--Voice gets louder-- EB - With this match comes a new king to the sport. One day I to will reside among the cornerstones of the legend we know of today. The future today lies in great younsters like me the critics say well I only have one response --Pause-- EB - They the dang stupidest journalist eva! First of all I --Pause-- EB - I am the future along. I carry with me the training since I was in first grade for training in professional wrestling. The great late Hiro Matsuda was my very first trainer and he taught me what was needed in the ring. The mindset needed to last in the business through the tops and the very low of points.
--EB bends over and grabs a piece of rubble and looks over it closely. Then clenches his hand making ashes that quickly spread with the cool brisk breeze--
EB - When injuries cripple you to beyond imaginabilty I will send my farewell to the wrestling gods and breath that final breath inside the ring. Here is my destiny and here my soul with strenghing then once fail and leave this
universe. My point here through all this rambling on is that here in the ring is where I belong til one day long down the road I decide to hang up the ole wrestling boots and call it quits. Don't expect that for a eternity for I will roam this path for years to come. Finally this Saturday Paco your old-time wrestling skills are long past my time and just wait for the final match of your life. The Hurricane has spoken and for now sweet dreams everybody!
--EB walks away from the picture kicking a stone as the picture fades to white-- <><><><><><><><><> MUE <><><><><><><><><> (Scene opens with the title, 'Tiger Hall' showing right in the middle. As the title fades away, a huge gym is shown with a lot of people trainning, and working out, led by few bald guys wearing black korean custom dress. Then camera zooms up the center of the gym, and we see the little boy, still wearing same outfit from the last time, looking around the gym with amazed eyes. After short while a guy with a tiger mask on his face comes into the gym. All the people in the gym suddenly stops what they were doing. and bow to him.)
Everyone:Good Morning Master Tiger! Master Tiger:Good, good. everyone continue your trainning. And,(he looks around the crowd and finds the boy) and you come with me.
(The boy walks out and joins Master Tiger, as everyone starts their trainning again. Master Tiger leads the boy out of the gym. When they walk out, camera shows the outside. It's korean traditional kingdom-style house, and they walk through the hallway, which connects each rooms like a bridge. It seems the tiger hall is on the top of mountain, as you see trees and rivers all around the hall way. They finally get to a room, and Master Tiger opens the door.)
(Inside, it's a room with full of weird pictures drawn all over the wall. the room is very unorganized, with a lot of stuffs flying around on the desk, on the floor, etc. There's a guy standing in the middle, right next to a big chair in the center of the room. He recognizes them and walks foward.)
?:Master Tiger! You are here. Master Tiger:It is time to give our 13th weapon his name, Jack. Jack:(looks at the boy) This is the boy? Master Tiger:Yes, this is it. Jack:But Master, why would you want to name someone our 13th weapon before he even passes the survival test?
Master:Don't you worry. You shall see, Jack. This boy is going to be the most fearful weapon of them all. and besides, we can't empty that M spot any longer.
Jack:They are saying that Mindless got killed because 13 is unlucky number. no one lived long enough as our 13th warrior. and..
Master Tiger:Nonsense! Mindless wasn't professional enough in that mission. That's why he failed. Nothing to do with him being the 13th warrior. Now, give him his sign.
Jack:Alright. Come here boy. (Jack makes him sit on the chair, and prepares tatto equipment. Tiger Master comes next to the chair.
Master Tiger:your name, your past, your family, and everything you've ever done in the world won't matter, boy. From this day, you're Mue. Tiger Hall's 13th weapon. And you're going to be the most dangerous of them all.
Jack:close your eyes (camera zooms up the boys face as he closes his eyes. Then the screen quickly changes to Mue's face. He's also closing his eyes. You see 'M' tattoo on his forhead. He slowly opens his eyes. He's lying on a bed in a hotel room. lights are off and it's dark. through the window you see some lights coming through.)
(Mue gets up, walks to the window and opens it. He looks up the sky, the camera shows the moon and the stars.)
Mue:Longer than 10 years... I've done it all.. And finally.. I came to ICWA in the name of Tiger Hall. On the first ever Psycho Saturday, I beat the guy. And this coming saturday, things aren't going to be different. The Metal, Your wrestling skills are great. It is obvious you pride yourself as such a great fighting machine, but in my eyes you're not good enough. I was taught to be tougher than even the toughest in the world. But you're like a rabbit in the food chain of animals. You may eat carrots, but tigers eat rabbits. On saturday night, you're my target. I'm a tiger searching for his dinner and Shwan, you will be the one to make this tiger advances in his quest to become the king of the jungle.
(You hear the sound of the door opening. Korean Angel ' comes in.) Korean Angel:Why aren't you sleeping? Mue:..Just looking up the sky. Korean Angel:Mue, you are not thinking of... Mue:The Metal will be cut in half when the sword named Mue go through his body and career once and for all. Tiger Hall ordered me that I finish off everyone in the ICWA. I got the job done on the first psycho saturday, and tomorrow won't be any different.
Korean Angel:Mue... Mue:(looks at the camera) The Metal, No talk will give you enough idea of what
I will do to you. I will see you tomorrow. Just remember, the tiger never fails to catch a rabbit.
(Camera fades away as Mue continues looking up the sky, and korean angel looking at him from behind, with concerned eyes.)
