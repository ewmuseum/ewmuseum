Tag-Team Application ===TSW============================ 1. WHAT IS TSW? ============================TSW=== IN CHARACTER TSW stands for Tri-State Wrestling. Tri-State Wrestling is run by millionaire internet mogul Ethan King, who, although young, saw a lot of promise in wrestling. TSW is a high-budget regional federation running out of the New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania tri-state area. So, why a high budget regional federation rather than a national or international federation? Quite simply, TSW CEO Ethan King felt that the smaller, regional feel was much more personal, but could do with a bit of a spice up. OUT OF CHARACTER TSW is a roleplay based, weekly flashcard, e-federation. E-wrestling is an online game in which players control a wrestler and move him/her through their career. This is one such federation. ===TSW============================ 2. WHO'S IN-CHARGE HERE? ============================TSW=== IN CHARACTER TSW is run by it's CEO, internet mogul and multi-millionaire, Ethan King. Ethan is in his early 30s and made his fortune through shrewd investments in e-commerce. A long time wrestling fan, Ethan felt that the time was right to fulfill his lifelong dream and open his own federation. Hence, TSW was born. OUT OF CHARACTER TSW is run by me, [name withheld]. You might remember me from federations such as NEO Wrestling, GWC/MPW, IWF/WOW, GYeah or CWO. Most prominently, I've handled Psicopa and Seth Draken (and others) and have been in the game since 1996. I've never had the time to open a federation before, but I do now, so here we are. ===TSW============================ 3. RULES ============================TSW=== As in all federations, we have our rules, here they are. - NO REAL WRESTLERS We run an original wrestler's only federation here. Meaning that all wrestlers are creations of individual handlers. This also means that we can't have wrestlers that are highly similar to real wrestlers. So please, no "Stone Cold" Brock Jerichos or anything like that. Your creations may resemble wrestlers either physically or gimmick wise, but no total rip offs. You may also use a real wrestler's finisher, but please, change the name. - LANGUAGE Please try to keep coarse language to what you might see on television. Swear if you need to, just keep it to a minimum. NOTE: racist, sexist or any prejudice remarks or slurs will not be tolerated or aired and wrestlers found offending will be suspended, warned and fired if they become multiple offenders. - OTHER WRESTLERS IN FLASHES If you must use another wrestler in your flash or oncard, make sure you clear it with them first. It's not a lot of fun to read a show and find that your creation has been beaten half to death with a baseball bat. If you're going to attack someone, ask their permission, you don't have to tell them EXACTLY what you're going to do, but at least they know something's up. - DON'T GO OVERBOARD Yeah, wrestling's becoming more hardcore, but there's always a line drawn. Don't cross it. Keep it to what you might see on television. Or, an ECW PPV at most. - BOOKINGS ARE FINAL Once the results are determined, there will be no dispute. If you lose a match you felt you should have won, don't write abusive emails or messages to everyone around complaining Write a civil email to management and we'll discuss it. ===TSW============================ 4. SHOWS ============================TSW=== Here at Tri-State Wrestling, we have two weekly shows. One flashcard, where roleplays will be displayed. And one show with matches and a few oncards and such. The shows are: TURMOIL Shown on Friday nights, Turmoil is our main card. This will be the show where matches and oncards are set. BACKCHAT Our flashcard. This'll be out Monday nights. If you want to win your match on Turmoil, make sure to get something in for that week's Backchat. Please note, these are all "pre-recorded" segments. Please don't set it at a jam-packed arena, because there is no jam-packed arena! As well as these two weekly shows, we will be holding bi-monthly pay-per-views. The reason they're bi-monthly? Every fedhead I've spoken to since 1996, is completely and totally drained after a PPV and doing them monthly can be suicide. PPV's TBA ===TSW============================ 5. DEADLINES ============================TSW=== Here is the weekly schedule. Late submissions will not be accepted so please try to live to them. BOOKING ============================TSW=== Booking decisions will, for now, be made solely by me. They will be based on a few factors. These factors are: ROLEPLAYING Writing flashes. Or, for those of you who are knew, interviews. Consider this like one of the interview/promo tapes you would see on TV maybe. This is often a wrestler talking about his/her upcoming match, a feud, an enemy, a friend or just developing a character. There are lots of things you can do with this. Our flashcards do NOT take place in an arena, so all segments are "pre-recorded". If you send something for Backchat set like this, it will NOT be included on the show, or counted. STRATS Strats (or strategies) are, quite simply, your wrestler's strategy for the match. This should include a ring entrance, their general mindset, goals for the match, spots and possible finishes. This is a basic strategy, you can make them more complex if you like. ONCARDS Oncards are the backstage, or in-ring segments you see at wrestling shows. They don't count towards the outcome of a match, but are excellent for promoting a feud or angle. Booking decisions are given the following weightings: Roleplay - 70% Strat - 20% Heat - 5% Effort - 5% As you can see, all things add up. So, put in a bit of effort, get things in on time and make them good QUALITY and presentable, and you'll go far. ===TSW============================ 7. TITLES ============================TSW=== The following are titles which are available for contention in TSW. [=TSW NEW YORK STATE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP=] The big daddy of them all around here. We're based in New York, so it's only fitting that the Heavyweight Title be our home state. This is the belt to gun for around here. It'll be defended AT LEAST once a month and at MOST three times a month. This keeps prestige in the belt. But note, once you have this title, you're a marked man. [=TSW TRI-STATE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP=] The tag team equivalent of the singles big cheese. It'll be defended at the same intervals as the New York State Heavyweight Championship. More titles will be added as the need arises. ===TSW============================ 8. JOINING ============================TSW=== Ok, so you still want to join?
