Roster and Rankings 4. Recent Results 5. Upcoming Schedule 6. Deadlines 7. Contact List =============================================TSW== 1. PRESIDENT'S NOTES =========================== Well, here we are, the first ever update for Tri-State Wrestling. How exciting, eh? Not much to say here, I've decided with the recent additions to the roster, to push back the card by a week and add a flash show before the actual card. No need to run exhibition matches, we can get straight to business.
Deadlines and the lineup for the card are below. Get me some oncards and stuff so we can make these first two shows as good as they can be! I'm going to try to do this weekly for a while, if it's too time demanding we'll consider going bi- weekly.

UPCOMING SCHEDULE ======================================== The first EVER Tri-State Wrestling Turmoil kick off on Friday,
May 10th, 2002 from Atlantic City, NJ and what a line-up it is.
In the opening bout of the night, "THE DUKE" GEORGE MARTINSON meets "THE OUTLAW" JOHN BLAKE. Two men with monikers, but two
VERY different personalities. Who'll be victorious? The blue blood or the outlaw?
Following that, a battle of the light will take centre stage as our man from Japan ENZO MAKO faces DREW BRODY. The combined weight of these two men comes to under 400 pounds, but it should be one hell of a match!
Then it'll be a battle of the big men as the two largest men in the company lock horns as AZRAEL ABADDON meets COUSIN
BUBBA! They're the same height and weight and the world will shift as these two bohemouths lock horns!
And in the main event of the evening, Germany's contribution to TSW, DECKER will meet the mysterious NOVEMBER! This will be another great battle of the light heavyweights and has "show steaker" written all over it!
Also expect comments from the wrestlers and of course, the
CEO of Tri-State Wrestling, Mr ETHAN KING!
