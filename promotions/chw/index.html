Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Canadian Hardcore Wrestling's entire surviving voice is three sentences, posted 31 January 1997: “Canadian Hardcore Wrestling has opened its doors. This federation is booked. All other info can be found in the player's guide on the home page,” pointing to a page hosted on a Manitoba freenet.

No card, roster, or copy of that player's guide survives to say anything more about what it actually ran.

Its ending is recorded by someone else entirely: a rival fed's own newsgroup roundup, dated 25 September 1997, calls Canadian Hardcore Wrestling “officially closed” — the only dated word on how it ended that survives anywhere.
