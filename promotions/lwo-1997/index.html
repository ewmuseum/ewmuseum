Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The LWO advertised itself on 5 August 1997 as “a great new Fantasy Federation,” run entirely by e-mail, offering each applicant a pick of three current WWF Superstars and promising a homepage still to come.

A follow-up post five days later names its own weekly shows after the WWF's own programming, Raw Is War and Shotgun Saturday Night, plus a monthly pay-per-view and the occasional special such as One Night Only. By then Goldust, Shawn Michaels and Mankind/Dude Love had already been claimed by other applicants; the post invited whoever signed up next to write their own interviews, angles, feuds and interference, promising the fed would run “the way you want it run.”

No document dated after 10 August 1997 survives — no card, no confirmed roster, and no closing notice of any kind.
