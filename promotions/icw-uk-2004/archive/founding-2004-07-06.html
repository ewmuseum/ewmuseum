ICW - The New Beginning, Founding Post

Hello to you all. Myself and a couple of other guys have set up our own e-fed for any and all wrestling fans to join. I have been involved in e-feds for around four years, with plenty of experience in running and playing in them. We wanted to get a new fed set up, where no matter your skill level, anyone can join in. You don't have to have any experience, as it's all pretty easy really. We are currently recruiting at the moment. At the time of writing this, the site has only just been completed and we are looking for more of you to join in. We will be having two weekly cards and a monthly supercard. We want to get started asap, and as soon as we have enough wrestlers involved, the first card will be announced. When you sign up, you can select any two wrestlers from anywhere in the world, and you can roleplay as them. To join up with ICW, visit our site by going to www.icw.me.uk Before applying to join, check out the roster section to make sure your selected wrestlers are available. You can make your application in the Join Now section. Again, I am very sorry if this is in the wrong place, but I wanted to post somewhere to see if any of you guys would like to join up. Thanks for your time. [name withheld].
