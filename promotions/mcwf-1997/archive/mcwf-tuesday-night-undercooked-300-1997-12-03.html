Tuesday Night Undercooked (#300)

3 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, here's a new and hopefully untried concept in e-wrestling from a couple of guys who really should know better... :) [name withheld] and myself, the former heads honcho of the late, great, CWF/WCWF empire (1993-1995, RIP) are starting a new e-fed called the MCWF, and here's what's so groovy about it: You get to write your own matches. I don't mean occasional special matches on PPV or stuff like that, I mean you get total and complete creative control of your guy. You do the role-playing, you write the matches for him. Steve and myself will control the "JTTS" portion of the federation, of which there's lots, and provide you with lots of cannon fodder for your up-and-coming wrestler. How good a match you write determines how far and fast you get pushed, so to speak. Plus you do the role-playing for your guy, to ensure good character development. I'm basically in charge of the character development stuff, and Steve will handle the booking aspects, but the nuts and bolts of the federation will be handled by the players. Don't kid yourself -- this is heavy stuff and this is not for the weak of heart. It's a lot of work and the success of the federation depends on everyone doing a good job of writing their matches. For those interested, a FAQ is available at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4693 , which goes into lots more detail on the hows and whys of this thing. What follows is the introductory show, Tuesday Night Undercooked (episode #300) which introduces the main characters and gives an example of what we'll expect for a match writing style. Read it over, and if you're interesting reply to this message or drop a line to [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] or preferably both. See you in the ring... Live! (on tape) from the Capital Centre in Landover, Maryland, it's MCWF Tuesday Night Undercooked! [Pyro goes off as hosts Dean Walker and former 3-time MCWF World champion Rexx Ryder introduce the show.] Dean: Good evening, fans, and welcome to this very special 300th episode of Tuesday Night Undercooked! We've been on the air live for almost six years now, every week, here on the TNA Network. And to start us out, we have a special interview with MCWF World champion [name withheld], back from making his latest movie, "Surburban Commando Assault on Santa's Nanny." To be shown on the TNA Network next week. And with Wrestlefest VI coming up very shortly, I'm sure Mr. Coley will be anxious to learn who he will be defending the prestigious MCWF World title against. [Dean's smile seems a bit forced at saying this.] Rexx: Dammit, I can't take this anymore! I'm sick of this facade! I'm sick of sucking up to that little rat-faced punk and his buddies. [Rexx' sudden outburst is cut off by Coley's theme music and fireworks display, as the champ, along with his manager/spirtual advisor Styx and the MCWF International champion Porno Inferno, come to the ring.] [Rexx Ryder grabs the mic to do the interview himself.] Rexx: Coley, I'm sure all these fans here know that if your uncle Brandon didn't run this federation, you'd be out on your movie-making ass by now, and your fat buddy Inferno there wouldn't have that worthless piece of tin he has strapped around his waist. Coley: You've been riding my ass for six weeks now, old man! Put up or shut up. You've been obsessing about the fact that I won the MCWF World title from you at Wrestlefest II back in 1993, and you can't deal with it like a normal human being! You're retired, remember? The rematch at Wrestlefest III a year later was "loser must retire" and YOU'RE the loser! Rexx: I never signed that contract! Everyone knows that you and your "Klubb" control the whole MCWF. You signed it for me. I wasn't even in the country! Porno: Yeah, you were probably at the tar pits visiting the rest of the dinosaurs! [Ryder suddenly snaps, lunging at Porno Inferno and knocking the huge rubber dildo hanging from his tights right into the crowd. Ryder gets Inferno on the mat and pounds a few right hands into him, before Coley wallops him in the back of the head with the title belt, knocking him out cold.] Coley: I'm Steve fuckin' Coley and YOU'RE NOT! [He and Inferno proceed to stomp on the retired ex-champion mercilessly, and no one comes out to help because everyone is either working for the Klubb or scared of retribution from them. Finally, Dean Walker pleads for mercy from them and they back off, heading back to the dressing room as Styx places a final curse on the fallen ex-champion.] Walker: Are you all right, Rexx? Rexx: [spits out blood] I've had worse than that. [Security guards, paid by the Klubb, come out to escort Ryder back to his announcing position and ensure that he doesn't interfere in any of the matches.] Walker: Hey, Rexx, forget about that loser, our first match tonight is an intriguing one. It features two tag teams both making their MCWF debut. Let's go right to the introductions! "Introducing first, weighing a combined 565 pounds, they hail from the San Quentin maximum security prison, accompanied by Warden Buford Jones, here are #112471 and #123174, The Chain Gang!!!!" Rexx: Holy moley! We've got convicted criminals in the MCWF?! I don't like the looks of this! Walker: It's OK. It looks like The Chain gang isn't just their name: they're actually being led to the ring in chains, and accompanied by a huge prison guard. I don't think we need to fear for our safety. Rexx: I do fear for the safety of their opponents, though! "And their opponents, weighing a combined 600 pounds, hailing from Parts Unknown, Davey and Goliath!!" Rexx: Parts Unknown. What a boring cliche. Why don't they just say where they're really from? Walker: I think they're really from Parts Unknown. It's the name of a town in South Dakota or something. Rexx: Oh. MY GOD! Goliath is huge! Walker: If this team is a combined 600 pounds, Davey must be under 200 because Goliath certainly fits his name! [The match gets underway with Davey and 112471 in the ring. They begin with a collar and elbow, and 112471 quickly overpowers the diminutive Davey, as Goliath looks on with concern. 112471 Irish whips Davey into the turnbuckle, watching with intensity as Davey bounces back toward the center of the ring. He grabs Davey by the hair and pulls him toward his corner and makes the tag to 123174.] Rexx: The Chain Gang is certainly impressive here in the early going. Walker: I'd say it looks more like Davey is completely out of his league. This match is less than a minute old, and he's already in sorry shape. [123174 and 112471 double team Davey in the corner, and this lures Goliath into the ring to protect his partner. He tosses 123174 over the top rope and to the floor, and checks on Davey's condition.] Walker: Goliath made the save just in time! [The referee orders Goliath out of the ring as 123174 re-enters. He continues to batter the seemingly defenseless Davey. He sets up Davey for a vertical suplex, but he underestimates his proximity to Goliath's corner, or perhaps closer to the truth he underestimates the length of Goliath's arm, as he reaches across the ring and slaps Davey's hand. 123174 didn't see it, and drops Davey to the mat and goes for the cover. Instead of the ref counting to three, Goliath counts to one, using his boot and 123174's head. The crowd is abuzz with excitement.] Rexx: This big man is really an intimidating presence in the ring. Walker: Yeah, but it doesn't look as if the Chain Gang is intimidated, as 112471 rushes into the ring to double team Goliath! [The double team is less than effective. Goliath quickly establishes control of the ring with a double headbutt to the Chain Gang. Warden Jones is distraught on the outside. Goliath knocks both men down with big boots. He turns to make the tag to Davey, but Davey is still in no condition to return to the ring. The delay, however, gives 123174 just enough time to clip Goliath's knee, dropping him to the floor.] Rexx: Now that's the way you beat a big man, you've got to find a way to knock him down to size. [123174 now lays into Goliath with vicious kicks and stomps to the back. He runs off the rope and connects with an elbow to the back of the head. He runs over to make the tag to 112471, then runs back to hold Goliath down. 112471 climbs to the top rope and connects with a moonsault on the prone big man.] Walker: Would you look at that, Davey is back to his feet, and he's trying to make a tag! That kid's got spunk! Rexx: But no brains. What business does he have getting in the ring? [Warden Jones, using typical tag team logic, nevertheless concludes that allowing a tag would be a bad thing, and sends the security guard over to stop Davey from making a tag. Bad move.] Walker: Goliath sees the guard heading toward Davey, and that really has given him a second wind. Rexx: Davey's useless in the ring, Goliath must have some strange fondness for him. Walker: Rexx, that's their personal business, and I'd rather not even speculate. [Goliath rises immediately to his feet, and casts and angry glare at the guard, who stops in his tracks in fear. 112471 and 123174 charge at Goliath, but he grabs them both, and, positioning them both in front headlocks under each arm, lifts them both up and down for a double powerbomb!] Rexx: That was a very impressive move. I don't recall ever seeing something like that before. Walker: He's tagging in Davey. Rexx: Sure, now that the Chain Gang is unconscious! [Davey covers 112471 for the easy 1..2..3! Goliath runs into the ring and lifts Davey up on his shoulders.] Walker: It's going to be a long night of solitary confinement for the Chain Gang, and we've seen the debut of two new tag teams who might challenge Octane and Slash for the belts in the near future. Rexx: Fat flaming chance! As if they'd ever condescend to defend their so-called championship! Walker: And you have to wonder how far Goliath could go as either a singles wrestler or with a more capable tag team partner. Rexx: I think that The Klubb should consider themselves lucky that for now, he's limiting himself to tag team action. But right now, it's time for everyone's favorite feature... >From the pages of the MCWF Magazine, here's Update... [Ross James sits in MCWF Studios in Hackensack, New Jersey.] Ross: Hello, folks. As you all probably know by now, [name withheld] is once again your MCWF World champion, as he defeated Vincent Ramses in Fort Wayne, Indiana under very suspicious circumstances. [Clip of Coley superkicking Ramses and missing by a good foot, but MacPherson falls like a stone anyway and Coley pins him to regain the title.] I think we can all see from that clip that the Klubb has gained yet another member in it's already bulbous ranks. But newer fans may not know just who this group is, and why they control so much of this once great federation heading into Wrestlefest VI, just weeks away. Many site the original source of all the problems as being MCWF Wrestlefest II in Miami, Florida, back in 1993. At that time, 13 year veteran Rexx Ryder was the reigning MCWF World champion, and was scheduled to defend against the automatic #1 contender, the mammoth International champion Octane. However, mere days before the event, Octane lost the title to rookie sensation Slash Maraud in the most shocking upset in MCWF history, and demanded a rematch on pay-per-view in lieu of his World title shot. MCWF owner Brandon Coley promised a big surprise for the hated Ryder to replace Octane, and delivered on the night of the show, as his nephew, and at that time World champion of rival wrestling organization the BWF, [name withheld] showed up out of nowhere and electrified the crowd, downing Ryder in a classic match to unify the BWF and MCWF World titles into one. [Clip of Coley superkicking his way into history shown.] But then ego and greed took over. Coley took nearly six months off to shoot a movie, never defending the title once during that period. His uncle was the owner, so no one could say anything. Rexx Ryder lobbied every week for a rematch, winning the International title from Octane in the process and thus making himself the automatic #1 contender. He formed a friendship with Octane's enemy, rookie sensation Slash Maraud along the way. [Clip of Maraud and Ryder celebrating Rexx' title win over Octane at MCWF Winterwar 1993] Finally, Coley agreed to a tag match at MCWF's "Nightmare Before Xmas" card in Madison Square Garden, with Coley and best friend Octane teaming up against Rexx Ryder and Slash Maraud. If Ryder could pin Coley or Octane, he would get a guaranteed title shot at Coley. We all know the fate of Ryder in that one. [The famous clip of Ryder turning around to tag Maraud, only to receive a fireball in the face from his partner, is played.] Ryder was crushed by the betrayal, but he was determined to beat Coley no matter what the odds. Finally, in desperation, he agreed to put $1,000,000 of his life savings and his own career on the line against Coley's World title at Wrestlefest III. Coley agreed, but had a plan. Coley forced Ryder to adhere to the 30-day rule as International champion. He had not defended the title due to his matches against Coley's Klubb. Brandon Coley ruled that Ryder had to defend against the monster Octane before his title shot. [Clip of Ryder battling Octane and eventually keeping the International title] Octane and Maraud delivered the beating of a lifetime to Ryder after the match, who was in no shape to wrestle by the end of the night. Coley easily defended against Ryder as over 50,000 fans and millions around the world watched in shock as the career of the greatest MCWF World championn ever ended after merely 5 minutes of action. [Clip of the bloodied Ryder falling victim to the superkick for the second year in a row and Coley getting the pin.] The next two years were a nightmare for the entire MCWF. Coley and his gang of thugs proceeded to move further and further into depravity every week as Coley himself took frequent sabbaticals to film movies. In the winter of 1994, they introduced Styx, their "spirtual advisor" who was rumored to be involved in Satanic rituals. They didn't care, they just wanted to see how far they could go. In 1995, they took lifetime MCWF loser "Titan" Turner Telford and re-dubbed him The Porno Inferno, complete with strap-on prosthetic penis and see-through tights. The fact that he was a 300 pound slob meant nothing to the Klubb. [name withheld] would vacate the MCWF World title at will before shooting a movie, then return months later to abuse, intimidate or buy the loyalty of whoever had won the tournament for the title. Usually the latter. Octane and Slash Maraud were awarded the MCWF World tag team titles as a "Christmas present" by owner Brandon Coley in a bizarre ceremony at Winterwar 1995, and they hold those titles to this day. The former champions, the Irish Express, were fired that day and have not been heard from since. The ranks of the "Klubb" swelled over the months, as everyone was either run out of the MCWF or joined their ranks, until there were only two or three wrestlers left who were not known members. Vincent Ramses actually beat [name withheld] for the title last week right here on Tuesday Night Undercooked, but of course only two nights ago turned his back on the MCWF and virtually handed the title back to Coley's greasy hands. Once regarded as the best wrestler on the planet, Coley has degenerated into a conniving, backstabbing coward who uses his over-inflated ego to buy his success in and out of the ring. Of course, that's just my opinion. Possibly the most digusting abuse of power occured when Brandon Coley was in the hosptial for hip surgery and interim president Clay Evans attempted to conduct an open tournament for the International title with wrestlers from around the world. The Klubb proceeded to beat each and every one up, then threaten their families with violence if they so much as showed up at the arena. In a total mockery of justice, they then had Porno Inferno substitute for every single competitor, essentially strapping the title around his bloated waist by default. I for one completely understand Rexx Ryder's actions this week against [name withheld], and I sincerely hope he's not the only one. [Suddenly, the camera spins around to see Octane's not-so-smiling face.] Octane: Yo! That's enough of that! Shut that camera off! [punches out the cameraman for good measure.] Cut to something worthwhile. [A commerical for "Suburban Commando Assault on Santa's Nanny," starring [name withheld], is shown.] [We cut back to the arena, where a sickened looking Rexx Ryder is watched by the Klubb's security force and Dean Walker shares his expression.] Dean: Fans, this situation is completely out of control, that's all I can say. Just keep cool, Rexx. Now is not the time to lose it. ["Stayin' Alive" blares over the PA, which means it's time for the Porno Inferno to "wrestle."] Rexx: This idiot is a disgrace to the belt he's wearing and to the sport in general. [Porno comes out to defend his International title against promising newcomer Angus Macbeth, already in the ring.] Angus jumps Inferno right from the start, hammering him in the corner with hard lefts. He whips him into the opposite turnbuckle and hits a flying lariat that stuns Porno completely. Suddenly, Octane comes stomping out of the dressing room and into the ring. The referee pretends to notice something else going on outside the ring, because everyone knows the deal, ya know? Octane yells something at Macbeth that sounds pretty nasty, then lets off with a vicious spinning heel kick to the head, which is impressive considering Octane is 6'10" and weighs more than 300 pounds. Macbeth drops like a stone and Porno covers for the easy win, as the referee decides now is the time to turn around again. Octane is still standing there, but that's neither here nor there. Rexx: I'm going to be sick. [Porno Inferno does a disco dance over the fallen Macbeth, shaking his rubber instrument of love the whole time. Octane keeps stomping on him to hammer home the point about knowing when to lose to the Klubb on purpose.] Rexx: FUCK IT! [He jumps onto the table, before the security guards can stop him, and launches himself into the ring, dropkicking a stunned Octane over the top rope and then grabbing the slobbish Inferno by the greasy hair before he can run away like usual. He levels the champ with a right hand that nearly dislocates his jaw, and Inferno is soon begging for mercy. Ryder tosses him into the corner and starts pounding on him like a punching bag as the crowd goes absolutely wild.] [MCWF owner Brandon Coley storms into the ring, mad as hell, and takes the ring mic.] Coley: Dammit, Ryder, I warned you! You're retired! Stay away from my wrestlers! I didn't spend millions of dollars turning the #3 organization into the #1 organization so you could ruin it for me! Rexx: You pathetic old man. Can't you see what your nephew has done? You're the laughingstock of the wrestling world. Steven and his friends control this entire federation and you let them do whatever they want as long as the ratings stay up. Your World champion never defends his belt because he's too busy making movies or meeting *actual* movie stars, or "finding his smile" or whatever. They're a bunch of talentless thugs who beat up anyone in their way, and they couldn't beat a real wrestler in a fair fight on their best day anymore. Coley: That's not true... Rexx: It is and you know it. I'll prove it. Give me a shot at your lazy nephew Steven at Wrestlefest VI, just the two of us, one-on-one, no dumb plans, no double-crosses. I don't even want the title! But if I win, and I will, then you step down from the running of this sinking ship and let me find some one *qualified* to run it for you. [[name withheld] comes on the Colossal-tron, fondling his title belt.] Steven: Hey, old man, I'll speak for my uncle Brandon right now and accept your challenge myself! Coley: Steven...WHAT? What are you doing? Steven: Don't worry, Uncle Brandon, I'll make sure that Tyrannosaurus Rexx Ryder here won't even walking by the time we get to Wrestlefest. I'll beat him and then stuff this special edition [name withheld] "Fan Klubb" t-shirt down his throat, and I won't even charge him the $29.95, plus shipping and handling, that it would usually cost for a fine garment such as this one. Half cotton, half silk, half nylon, how can you go wrong? [The address to order flashes on the screen below Coley.] Rexx: You sellout. You'd have "Hertz Rent-A-Car" tattooed on your ass for a buck. Steven: No, but I did have "Jimmy Dean Sausages" tattooed somewhere else for five hundred thousand bucks. Wanna see? [Crowd shrieks in horror as he prepares to pull down his tights for the world.] Rexx: Jesus! Put that thing away! I believe you, already. Steven: See ya at Wrestlefest. Wouldn't wanna be ya. [The screen blinks off, and suddenly new Klubb member Vincent Ramses runs out of the audience and tackles Ryder. Security pulls them apart after Ramses gets a few good shots in. Dean Walker comes into the ring to interview Ramses.] Dean: Why'd you join them? That's what's on everyone's minds right now. Ramses: The great Pharoh Ramses needs not explain his actions to mere mortals such as yourself! The gods spoke to me and commanded me to do the bidding of the holy one. My god came to me in my sleep and had me do his bidding. Dean: Was this god about 6'10" with "Octane" in big neon letters on his spandex tights by any chance? Ramses: Why yes. Did you have a similar epiphinic moment yourself? Dean: Not really. And what's with the "Pharoh" title? You're not Egyptian, last time I checked. Ramses: I am the reincarnation of the great Pharoh, little man, and be glad I do not crush you where you stand for such mockery of me. Dean: That chair to the head that you took at the hands of that little creep Styx last week must have messed you up worse than the doctors thought, Ramses. Ramses: I am in perfect mental health. And I demand to be addressed as "Pharoh Ramses" from now on. I am royalty. Dean: Sure. A royal pain in the ass. ["Pharoh" Ramses looks ready to backhand Walker for that comment, but thinks better of it and merely turns his back and walks out instead.] Walker: I'm afraid that about does it for this week. Hopefully we can make it through next week's show without any more ugly incidents. For the MCWF, I'm Dean Walker. [The credits start to roll, but suddenly the view cuts to the dressing room, where [name withheld] has stolen a camera and given it to Styx to man.] Steven: Okay, I'm the show stopper around here, and the show doesn't stop until I say it stops! [Coley, Octane, Slash Maraud, Styx, and Porno Inferno all stand around inoccuously, doing nothing in particular. Inferno occasionally picks his nose.] Steven: Okay, I think my point has been made. Show's over! [Octane yells "Cut!" and punches out the floor director, prodcuer and cameraman.] [Cut to black, roll credits, which have been doctored to feature [name withheld] in each and every position.] Next week: More proof of the greatness of the Klubb! I love [name withheld]! Buy his t-shirt, only $29.95! [Damn, they got to the "next week" guy, too.] [end] Scott Keith, Master of funk and evil, and a bad PPV's worst nightmare.
