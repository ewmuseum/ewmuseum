[Fade back into Jason Molina sitting down looking through his stack of papers.] [JM:] Hello, fans, and welcome back to TrashTalk! My next guest, has to be one of the greatest e-wrestlers, in this sport today. It'd take all night to tell the list of titles he won! But I'm not going to waste all that time. Fans across the world, without further adu, here is "The Franchise" Andru Edwards!!! [Edwards walks through the curtain, wearing a "Franchise" Andru Edwards APWA T-Shirt, and Black Shorts. He then sit's down in the red leather chair.] [JM:] Hello, Mr. Edwards, and welcome to APWA, Monday TrashTalk! ["The Franchise" Andru Edwards:] Good Evening. [JM:] First off, I'd like to welcome you to the APWA! [Andru Edwards:] Thanks a lot...It feels good to be back... [JM:] Same here, Andru. Now, let's get down to business here... Who do you think is going to win tonight's Monday Night Football Game between Da' Bears! and the Green Bay Packers? My money, on DA' BEARS! [Edwards:] I really don;t give a damn about Football! The bottom line is....E-Wrestling is where it's at... [Edwards:] I'm here in the APWA to WRESTLE. [JM:] Come on, Da' Bears, or the Packers! [Franchise:] Green Bay can go to hell. [JM:] AIGHT!!! [Franchise:] Of course! [JM:] Now... How about Da' Coach! Mike Ditka! What do you think of him, "Franchise"?!?! [Franchise:] Ditka can manage...but of course....no one can manage like Erotic Erin... [Franchise:] How about we move on to the Sport of Kings? [JM:] Fine, Fine, Fine... Do you think you can capture the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance National Championship ? ? ? [Yawn] [Franchise:] Well, the thing is..I was Screwed Molina!! I came out here, and I threw in my application...and they say Edwards....go into the National Title Tournament... [Franchise:] They won't let me into the World Title Tournament... [Franchise:] So here is how it's going to go, Jason... [Franchise:] I am going to take that belt...and I am going to come out here and take the national Championship...propelling myself to #1 contendership.. [JM:] Sounds like a good plan, Andru. Tell me, do you think there is any competition for you in the National Title Tournament? The wrestler's you MIGHT clash with are... "Mr. Hall of Fame" Tommy Van Dam, J.D. "The Bird" Raven, and some other person. [JM:] By The Way, that some other person is Shawn Diggs, who you face in the First Round of the National Title Tournament. [JM:] Apparently, Andru Edwards has fallen to sleep! Well, fans, due to the fact Andru never answered le question with in that LLLOOONNNGGG period of time, we've got to go! Um... Lady Person! Get Andru a blanket! Well, for me Jason Molina, goodnight everybody, and we'll see you at the re-kickoff of the APWA, Tourney Smash!!! ©APWA Inc.
