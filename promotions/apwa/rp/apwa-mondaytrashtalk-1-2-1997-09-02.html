[APWA] MondayTrashTalk (1/2)

2 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The APWA Logo followed by TrashTalk swirls onto the screen.] [Announcer Dude:] Coming up next on TeWN, The e-Wrestling Network, the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance returns with it's first EVER episode of TrashTalk! Live, and only on TeWN, the leader in e-Wrestling!!! [Fade out.] ["Full Moon Over Dallas" by The Crawl, begins to play loudly, as we fade into a U-Shaped Desk, where the APWA Main Correspondent sits, Jason Molina. He wears a "APWA Returns" T-Shirt, and Black Sunglasses. He brushes back his long blond hair, and picks up a stack of papers, and bounces them off the desk, so they are neat. The camera continue's to zoom onto Molina, as he begins to speak.] [Jason Molina:] Hello, there, fans! And welcome to the first ever edition of Monday TrashTalk, here on TeWN, The e-Wrestling Network! Well, it's my pleasure to say that the APWA has re-opened to the e-wrestling world, and has a lot of different wrestlers in it! In fact, all of them are different! And those wrestler's will be competing in Tourney Smash, our kickoff Pay-Per-View! [Molina:] Well, let's get TrashTalk rolling! My first guest, is a Former Three Time FCW Champion, Former XWF Tag Title Co-Holder, Former GWA Shoot Fighting Champion, Former WCWF Extreme Champion, and former XWF International Champion! [Takes a deep breath.] Whew! And he's finally made his way to the APWA! Ladies and Gentlemen... Rob Solo!!! ["Smack up my Bitch" by Prodigy plays, as Rob Solo, walks through the curtain, wearing a Black Leather Jacket, Black and Blue Shorts, and Black and Blue Shoes. He then walks over to one of the two red leather chair's, and sit's down. Then "Smack up my Bitch" by Prodigy stops playing.] [JM:] Hello, there, Mr. Solo, and welcome to the first ever edition of Monday TrashTalk, and I'm glad to say, you are my first ever guest, on this very show known to the world as Monday TrashTalk!!! [Rob Solo:] Yeah, so what, is everybody else scared to show their ugly mugs out here? Or are they just in back calling their mommies telling them that they are hurting? [JM:] Okay... [JM:] Um... Tell me! How did it feel signing here with the APWA? [Solo:] Let's just say that the money was good, and the competition, well let's just say the competition is somewhat of a challenge. I've come here with my lovely Valet Lucious Laura to win titles and break some bones in the process, I've got only one goal in mind and that's to be the very best here in the APWA. [JM:] The best? Well, you do have that chance, because you happen to be in the First Round of the World Title Tournament! And you are facing... [Shuffles through his stack of papers.] Taylor "The Godfather" Luchinni! Any words for "The Godfather"? [RS:] First of all, I'm the only Italian stud here in the APWA, secondly he can have all the Mafia Funding he wants cause I'll kick his spaghetti eating ass back to the old country. Who else is in this tournament? Hector "The Car Theif" Gonzalez? Come on, what kind of crap is this? Who is supposed to be the man in this fed, well, besides myself being the only man here, who claims to be the one to beat here? [JM:] Well... "ONE and ONLY" Eric Cooper, Brandon "The Hurricane" Honeyman, Mr. Hall of Fame" Tommy Van Dam, and "The Franchise" Andru Edwards are excellent wrestlers. [RS:] Oh Yeah, I'm familiar with those idiots. First off let me tell you this, in another federation these guys are trying to keep me from top billing cause they know I'm the most dangerous man alive, another point is that they are all pretty boys who think that a smile can take them places. Well I'm not one to brag but when I smile is when I'm on the verge of breaking somebodies neck, and I intend on breaking each and everyone of their necks with a SoloPlex. How does the APWA like me now? [JM:] Well, I don't know. Um... Any last words for anybody in the APWA, cause we've got to step out for a commerical break, fast! [RS:] I've come here to show the world that I deserve RESPECT and praise and I will stop at nothing to get what I want, so all you jackasses in the APWA, get ready cause the cards are being dealt and I've gat all the ACES. I've said enough for now. [JM:] Well, thanks Rob Solo for your time! [RS:] The pleasure was obviously all yours....... [JM:] HaHa. Well, time to take a break, folks! Will be right back with... [Shuffles through his papers.] I don't know!!! Wait a minute... "The Franchise" Andru Edwards!!! Okay, let's take that break now! ["Full Moon Over Dallas" by The Crawl plays, as we go to a Chicago Skyline Shot. APWA MondayTrashTalk is on the bottom left hand corner of the screen. Fade out.] [Screen Remains Black as a Voice Over Speaks.] [Voice Over:] It was the best... [VO:] Then it shutdown after six months of glory in the sun. [VO:] And now, fans of e-Wrestling, it's back... and IT'S BACK WITH A BANG!!! [Cut to a big explosion going off.] [VO:] The Action Packed Wrestling Alliance returns with Tourney Smash! Eight Live, Action Packed Matches only on Pay-Per-View! Including the Roofed Steel Cage, "I Quit" Match for the National Title, and the Main Event, for the grand daddy of them all, the Ladder Match for the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance World Heavyweight Championship! So, fans, make sure to make that call to your local cable company to order the return of the APWA, TOURNEY SMASH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Fade out.]
