[APWA Tourney Smash] (6/7)

^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^ !Roofed Steel Cage! !"I Quit" Match! !For the APWA National! !Title! ^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^ J.D. "The Bird" Raven vs. Shawn Diggs v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v Written By: [name withheld] v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v [Aerial Shot of the APWA Palace, as some of the Roofed Part of the Steel Cage is seen, as "Roofed Steel Cage, "I Quit" match for the APWA National Title", shoots across the screen. Then we go to a ringside shot, as the camera zooms into Joeboo standing in the middle of the great squared circle.] [[[DING, DING, DING!!!!!!!!!!!!]]] [Fans:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!! [Joeboo:] The following contest... here at APWA Tourney Smash... is the special... Roofed Steel Cage, "I QUIT" match, to decide the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance National Champion!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [WJ:] What a dork! [JS:] Um... Okay. [JS:] Joeboo, stop! [Joeboo:] Alrighty, John. What is it? [JS:] The fight has started in the back again! [Joeboo:] Aight! [Joeboo leaves the ring through the padlock door, as we cut to the back.] [JS:] This is the second time tonight, Shawn Diggs started a locker room brawl! [WJ:] And Wizard! [JS:] Well, Yeah. [WJ:] Well... [JS:] How about THIS SHOT TO THE HEAD WITH A CHAIR BY SHAWN DIGGS! [JS:] LOOK AT THIS! J.D RAVEN'S HEAD IS ALL RED! [WJ:] Well, duh! Shawn Diggs just nailed him over a head with a steel chair! What do you think is going to happen? Some Big Fat Guy will pop out of it make a weird laugh eating a hot dog, and three slices of pizza, and then fall to the ground?!?!?! [JS:] Um....................... [WJ:] YEAH! I THOUGHT SO! [JS:] Well, Shawn Diggs and Raven continue to brawl, AS WIZARD HAS TAKEN A TRASH CAN AND NAILED IT OVER J.D. RAVEN'S HEAD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [WJ:] DAMN! [JS:] Diggs and Wizard are now dragging J.D. Raven! Wait a minute!! Wizard and Shawn Diggs have dragged THEM THROUGH THE CURTAIN!! They are going to finish him off, I think! [WJ:] Well of course they are going to finish him off you big stinkyhead! It'll be easier for Raven to submit now cause Diggs nailed Raven over the head with a chair and Wizard nailed Raven with a Trash Can! [JS:] Well, put, Walker, as they are half way down the aisle. Wait a second! Diggs says stop dragging Raven! Diggs brings J.D. Raven to his feet, and half way down the aisle, HE... HE... WHIPS HIM TO THE STEEL STEPS! Whoa!! [WJ:] Damn!! [JS:] Diggs and Wizard are now laughing! The referee has opened the Padlock Door, as Diggs throws J.D. Raven into the Roofed Steel Cage! The other offical is in, as the outside referee shut's the door! [[[DING, DING, DING!!!]]] [WJ:] Come on, Diggs! Finish that punk bird off!!!!!! [JS:] Well, Diggs brings J.D. Raven back to his feet! And... And... HE THROWS HIM INTO THE STEEL!!!!!!!! [Fans:] WWWWWWWWWWWHHHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [WJ:] Damn! [JS:] Now Diggs is laughing even more! Diggs is now HOOKED ON THE FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK!!!!!!!!!!!! [All the fans stand up and look on excitedly.] [Special Referee:] Do you Quit, Raven?! [J.D. "The Bird" Raven:] Nnnnnnnnn... Ooooooooooo.... [Some fans cheer, as the special referee looks on holding the cordless mic.] [WJ:] COME ON SHAWN!! [JS:] Diggs still has the Figure Four locked on here but Raven still isn't giving! [Special Referee:] Do you give up?!?!!?!?!! [J.D. "The Bird" Raven:] Nnnnnnnnnn...... Ooooooooo..... [Some fans cheer, as the Special Referee slowly backs away.] [JS:] Wait a second!!!!!!!! [Fans:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!! [JS:] J.D RAVEN "THE BIRD" HAS TURNED OVER THE FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK!!!!!!!! [WJ:] NO WAY!! WHAT A CHEATER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [JS:] Unbelieveable!!!!!! [Special Referee:] Do you quit, Diggs?!?!?!?! [Shawn Diggs:] N... YEESSSS!!!! [Fans:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [WJ:] NO WAY!!!! [A Bird Call Roars out of the loudspeakers in the APWA Palace, as J.D. "The Bird" Raven, finally get's on his feet. He wobbles around the ring for a little bit, as the fans continue to cheer. Then the Special "I Quit" Referee, hands Raven the APWA National Championship as Joeboo speaks.] [Joeboo:] Here is your winner, and NEW, Action Packed Wrestling Alliance National Champion... J.D. "THE BBBIIIIIRRRDDDDDDDD" RRRAAAVVVEEEENNNN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [WJ:] NO WAY!! [Diggs rolls around on the mat in pain, as J.D. "The Bird" Raven holds up the APWA National Title.] [JS:] Just unbelieveable!! [The padlock door opens, as J.D. Raven walks out to the end of the aisleway. He slaps the fans hands, as he holds the APWA National Championship. He then disappears of to the back.] [Then a part of the cage is taken off by the APWA Crew.] [JS:] Well, after we've witnessed that action-packed, but fairly short match, it's time for are main event of the evening... The Ladder Match for the APWA Championship! [WJ:] It's not time NOW, John! They've still got to take off this frickin' cage! [JS:] Oh Yeah. Well... I guess Jason Molina is back with J.D. Raven! Take it away Jason. [Cut to the back, where Jason Molina stands with the newly crowned APWA National Champion, J.D. "The Bird" Raven.] [Jason Molina:] Thanks, John. Well, Mr. Raven, congratulations on being the new APWA National Champion! [J.D. "The Bird" Raven:] Thanks, Jason! But, you know, that chair to the head, and the trash can to the back of my head, slowed me down a little, but you know, I got what I was shooting for, the National Championship. [Molina:] Now, Mr. Raven. [J.D. Raven:] Call me J.D. [JM:] Okay, J.D... Now, in case you didn't know, you'll be defending your title, at APWA In Your Face, Da' Man of Da' Ring, against... Hold on, Simpson is telling me... That's against ROB SOLO! [J.D. Raven:] Solo, well at In Your Face, Da' Man of Da' Ring, I'll be walking in, and walking out the National Champion! [Raven walks away.] [JM:] Thanks for your time Mr. Raven! Back to you, John! [Fade in to a shot of the SOLD-OUT APWA PALACE.] [JS:] Well, take a look at this SOLD-OUT CROWD, as the APWA Crew, has taken away the last part of the Roofed Steel Cage! And now, the ladder has been put out! And now we can get to Joeboo for tonight's Main Event... Da' Ladder Match for the APWA Championship!! [WJ:] Whatever!
