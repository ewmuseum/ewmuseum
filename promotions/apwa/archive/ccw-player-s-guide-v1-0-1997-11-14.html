-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Chaotic Championship Wrestling Presents . . . CCW Player's Guide V1.0 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- I. -The CCW in General- CCW stands for Chaotic Championship Wrestling, and is one of many imaginary fantasy wrestling leagues on the internet. The CCW exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative, and a enjoyable gaming format. Ever dream of putting on tights, and walking down that long aisle to the squared circle? Well, in the CCW, you can create your own wrestling character, and guide him to the grand daddy of them all . . . the World Title! :) CCW is presided by [name withheld]. [name withheld], before stepping onto CCW, ran the APWA, the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance. Brett, created it on April 4, 1997, and it folded, due to the lack of help for the President on June 4th, 1997. And on August 9, 1997. Also, Brett has been in quite a few leagues, including, EWA, where he almost captured the North American Title, NOW, where he was the First and Former Midwest Champion, and SPWA, where he was a Superheavyweight Champion. Brett has also written for the EWA. So it's VERY, VERY, VERY, safe to say the front office is filled with experience. :) II. CCW Rules CCW will not allow any "Out of the World" gimmicks. Meaning, I will not tolerate wrestlers with gimmicks that are too far-fetched. For example I don't want any bionic plumbers or clowns in my league. NO comic book gimmicks, just keep it plain and simple. As a general rule of thumb, applicants whose wrestlers are similar characters the league already established in CCW, are not admitted, even if they are top roleplayers. I mean, would you want to have five undertakers running around? ALL wrestlers must be original and not taken off any 'real' wrestler. (i.e. no "Heartbreaker's", "Stone Cold's", or "Nature Boys".) In CCW, you can either submit ONE Singles Wrestler, or ONE Tag Team. People intend to enter four or five characters in a league, and only pay's attention to ONE of them. In order to ensure each player recieves sizable activity and participation in CCW, the roster must be kept relatively small. CCW allows 24 singles wrestlers and 6 tag teams. This allows each wrestler a match once a week and the attention the player deserves in a league. Upon being admitted to CCW the President will ask for a sample roleplay. Here is an idea of what is expected of you in your sample roleplay. III. CCW Participation Participation in CCW is essential. It is the key to success. Roleplaying (see Roleplaying Regulations below) takes up much of that participation. You are required to submit a minimum of one quality flash per week. Obviously, tihs flash, is your major flash. Meaning it is your all-around big time flash that developes your character. Read Roleplay Regulations below for full understanding of your roleplaying standards. Other ways of participation is sending in strats, sneak attacks, and getting involved in your current angles and storylines. We'll get into it in more detail as the league progresses. IV. CCW Programs CCW currently promotes one live weekly card a week: -CCW Chaotic Zone (Occurs Every Sunday. 4 Live Matches. 1-2 Darks.) In addition, there are three features that comes out during the week: -CCW Timelock -CCW Speaks Out -CCW In The Spotlight Pay-Per-Views occur, every month. V. CCW Roleplay Regulations CCW emphasis on, good writing=good push. A long roleplay doesn't neccessarily mean that is a good roleplay. A well written quailty 6K roleplay can beat out a long, dull, boring and dragging 12K roleplay any day of the week. Though, the longer the roleplay the better. Remember the three most important things when creating a roleplay. Content, Length and Detail. Your flashes will be sent out in CCW Speaks Out. In The Spotlight is a interview program that holds three live interviews with CCW Wrestlers. If you volunteer to get an interview, it'll give you a better chance of winning your upcoming matchup. Failure to produce roleplays of any sort, be they interviews, strats, or whatever, in a short period of time will result a cautionary warning from the President, and if the inactivity continues, your wrestler may be suspended or fired from the CCW altogether. -The Ground Rules- Nobody is stopping you from having fun, and that is the main reason you come to CCW is to have fun. But like all good things, there must be rules that each player must abide by, in consideration of the The CCW Council and your fellow players. Please follow these regulations: DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hosptialize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the President's. DON'T attack announcers or any of the CCW staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want. DON'T use excessive profanity. Often when you go into realization of your character, obscene language is all part of the game when it is hot and heavy that your wrestler is going through. However, dirty and very vulgar words will be edited out of your flashes. Words such as "ass", and "damn" will be allowed, though stronger words than it will not. You have to keep in mind that such language is not acceptable in television programmings. It is very possible to express your anger and extreme emotions without using a string of four letter words. VI. How does the CCW Operate? The CCW is a ''booked'' league. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the CCW Council. Results are not made up of, with that single match in mind. Generally, it accounts for the bigger picture, meaning the angles, storylines, and so forth. And your basis of roleplaying helps a tremendous way in helping the match results. However, for instance, two good roleplayers have a match against each other, both have been flashing all week, and now it comes to conclusion that the President's will take it upon himself to determine a results with a coherent storyline in mind. VII. Titles Chaotic Championship Wrestling currently sanctions two singles titles: CCW World Heavyweight Title CCW Trans-Continental Championship And CCW currently sanctions one tag team title: CCW World Tag Team Championship The addition of a Tri-State Tag Team Championship is currently being considered by the CCW Council. Titles can only change hand by pinfall or submission, unless specified before the match. VIII. Rankings CCW uses a point system for its rankings. Once getting admitted to CCW, the wrestler starts off with zero points and works his way up by getting victories. A wrestler is not deducted any points if he happens to lose. Point System are as follows: +10 Points for match if won by Pinfall or Submission + 5 Points for match win by Disqualification or Countout VIII.
