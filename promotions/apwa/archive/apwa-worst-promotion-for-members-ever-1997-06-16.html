[APWA] Worst Promotion for Members Ever. :)

16 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK, I'm gonna make this quick: Using three posts to advertise is a bit much, especially when you repeat a few things in all three. THis in mind, let me go over all three: From the first post: > Well, folks, I realize the E-Wrestling circuit is being flooded >with new leagues forming left and right. Some people see it is a bad >thing... I see it is a good thing. Why is that? Because it gives me the >chance to prove that we are one of the best E-Wrestling promoters on the >planet and if you give me a chance, I will prove it to you! Guranteed. The Anti-SHEF sees it as a good thing, as long as the fedheads are committed to running their fed. Most aren't. Calling yourself the best would normally get you an Anti-SHEF nasty-gram, but the wording shows that you are WILLING to prove your claim (plus the rest of this post shows that you put some thought into your fed. A definate plus)) However, you need to re-read your posts before sending them out. This first paragraph is painful to read, and much of the other two are similar. From the second, we get a sample card, a DEFINATE plus! It shows your writing style. While it may not appeal to everyone, at least thiose who it WILL appeal to will see it and want to apply. From the third, we get an idea on who's in the fed, so that fans of these wrestlers will want to tune in. Very nice. And we also see that the fedhead has a definate sense of humor (the title of the message calls the fed the worst on the net...a snub at the Anti-SHEF?)
