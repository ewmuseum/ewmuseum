[APWA Tourney Smash] (1/7)

12 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Screen is Black, the Announcer Dude speaks over.] [Announcer Dude:] Coming up next on Pay-Per-View... [A flash of white hits the screen quickly, as it returns to black.] [Announcer Dude:] It's... [Another quick flash of white nails the screen.] [Announcer Dude:] The Re-Kickoff of... [The APWA Logo, Followed by Tourney Smash fly onto the screen, do a flip, and nail smack dab in the center, with a loud noise heard at the same time.] [Announcer Dude:] the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance with TOURNEY SMASH!!!!!!!!!! [The timer clock then appears on the upper right hand corner of the screen, and it goes to 0:00. Fade out.] [A big explosion is heard as we go to a aerial shot of the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance Palace, in Chicago, Illinois. All the fans are on their feet, cheering in their excitement, as fireworks shoot off high in the arena. Fans wear APWA Merchandise, and wave their signs high. The camera zooms into a "Welcome back APWA to Chicago!", sign, as well as a "Eric Cooper can go to hell!". Then, Jason Molina, comes out through the curtain attired in a Customary APWA Suit, sharp Blue Pants, and a pair of Black Sunglasses, holding a mic.] [Jason Molina:] HELLO, CHICAGO, ILLINOIS!!! THE "WINDY CITY!!!". [Fans:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Jason Molina:] And welcome to the RE-Kickoff of the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance, TOURNEY SMASH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Fans:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Molina:] Well, we are counting down to start here of the RE-Kickoff of Tourney Smash, and boy, do we have a excellent card for you tonight!!! [Change camera angle to the aisleway, where Vince Beard, walks out wearing a Customary APWA Suit, and Blue Jeans.] [Vince Beard:] THAT'S RIGHT JASON!!! [Crowd POP!] What I can't wait to see is the Roofed Steel Cage, "I Quit" match, for the National Heavyweight Championship here in the APWA!!! [JM:] Oh, that match! That sure won't be one to miss! ALSO!!! The Main Event for tonight, The Ladder Matchup, for the APWA CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!!!!! [Beard:] That'll be one to match too! Also, we've got action from three other e-wrestling federations which are the FWF, Fantasy Wrestling Federation! ASW, Awesome Simulation Wrestling, and RDW, RICE DICE WRESTLING!!!!!!!!! [Little Crowd POP.] [JM:] Of course! And if you aren't here in the SSSSSSOOOOOLLLLLDDDDD OOOOOUUUUUTTTTTT APWA Palace in Chicago, Illinois, then you can get the second best seat in the house, by watching on Pay-Per-View, and how? Making that telephone call to your local cable company NOW! Now... fans... Let's ask of who do you think is going to win here tonight! Will the APOW Champion be Taylor "The Godfather" Luchinni?!?!?!?!?!?!??!!??!?!?!!?!?! [The Fans give a mixture of cheer's and boo's.] [JM:] How about Rob Solo??!!!!??!?!!?!?!?!?!?!!??!!?!?!?!?!? [Fans:] BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [JM:] Or, Or... Will it be Brandon "The Hurricane" HONEYMAN?!?!!??!?! [Fans:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!! [JM:] Or, will it be... "ONE... [Fans already get up and give all kinds of negative responses.] and ONLY" Eric COOPER?!?!?!?!?!? [Fans:] BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [JM:] Geez, LOUISE! [Fade out.] [APWA slides across the screen as Tourney Smash does too.] [Voice Over:] APWA Tourney Smash! [Split Four Screen where Taylor "The Godfather" Luchinni, Rob Solo, Brandon "The Hurricane" Honeyman, and "ONE and ONLY" Eric Cooper fill up those four spots. Then the APWA Championship shoot's across the screen.] [VO:] These four wrestler's, are destined for the Championship, but there will only be two survivors to battle it out in a Ladder Match for the title!!! [Then, Shawn Diggs, "Franchise" Andru Edwards, "Mr. Hall of Fame" Tommy Van Dam, and J.D. "The Bird" Raven, take over the four spots, as the APWA National Championship then shoots across the screen.] [VO:] Also, these four men promise to walk home for the gold, but only one of these four people, SHALL!!! [VO:] HAUS'S Health & Fitness presents APWA Tourney Smash! "Clash for the Titles", Live, and only on Pay-Per-View!!! Make sure to call your local cable, or satelite company to order now!!! [Fade out.] [Fade back into the APWA Palace in Chicago, Illinois.] [JM:] Folks, we are back here at Tourney Smash! And we have only Two More Minutes, until we kickoff this Pay-Per-View Spectaular!!! [HUUUGGGEEE CROWD POP!!!] That's right, fans! So, people at home, you have now , to call your local cable company, to order APWA TOURNEY SMASH!!! Live and only on Pay-Per-View!!! Your time is running out! Aight, Prevue Channel, we gotta go! And remember, the only way you can join us is on PAY-PER-VIEW!!! ©APWA Free-For-All Chicago, Illinois... 1997 All Rights Reserved® [Fade to Black.]
