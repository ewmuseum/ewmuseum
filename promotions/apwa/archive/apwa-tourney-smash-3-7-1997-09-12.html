^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^ !First Round National! !Title Tournament! !Match! ^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^=^ "Mr. Hall of Fame" Tommy Van Dam vs. J.D. "The Bird" Raven v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v Written By: [name withheld] v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v=v [[[DING, DING, DING!!!!!!]]] [Crowd POP!] [Joeboo:] The following contest, scheduled for one fall, and is action in the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance, National Title Tournament!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Small Crowd POP.] [A bird call is heard, as the fans begin to cheer like crazy.] [Joeboo:] Now coming down the aisle, hailing from St. Louis, Missouri, Standing Six-Foot-Five, and weighing in at Two-Hundred-Twenty-Five- Pounds, here is . . . J.D. "THE BIRD" RAVEN !!!! [J.D. Raven makes his way through the curtain, and begins walking down the aisle, looking sharp as always. He wears just plane Blue Tights, as he slaps the fans hands. He then makes his way up the steel steps, and grabs onto the top rope, jumps over, and lands in the squared circle perfectly. J.D. then goes to the top, and nails a flip perfectly, and right when he lands, a big BOOM of fireworks go off, as the fans get on their feet, and cheer even LOUDER.] [JS:] Wait a minute!!! [JS:] That's "Mr. Hall of Fame" Tommy Van Dam! He was under the ring! He's slides into the ring, and taps J.D. Raven on the shoulder!!! [Fans stand up excitedly!] Raven turns around and he's KNOCKED DOWN WITH A RIGHT!!! [[[DING, DING, DING!!!]]] [Joeboo rushes out of the ring as Raven, get's pounded by TVD.] [WJ:] Both of em' suck! [JS:] Ah, Well... Anyway's... TVD back to the ropes with "The Bird" J.D. Raven... Raven is released for the ride, and TVD with a leap frog over Raven. Van Dam lands, Raven back off the ropes and TVD dropkick's Raven to the mat! [Crowd POP!] [WJ:] That sucked too! [JS:] Okay... Tommy Van Dam, "Mr. Hall of Fame" now has got a arm bar applied onto J.D. "The Bird" Raven. [WJ:] And there's a problem with that arm bar! [JS:] What is that problem, Walker? [WJ:] I don't know. [JS:] Oh Boy... Raven back up and delivers a elbow to the midsection! And another! Van Dam is stunned after those devasting elbows to his midsection! Raven now off the ropes and nails Tommy Van Dam with a FLYING clothesline! [Crowd POP!] Raven with the quick cover! One!!! Kickout! [JS:] And J.D. Raven only got a one count on that one, but quickly J.D. Raven applies a Reverse Chinlock here, on TVD! [WJ:] Now this isn't even close to a perfect chinlock! He doesn't have enough pressure on Tommy Van Dam! I mean, TVD's already up!!! [JS:] And indeed he is! But, Raven takes TVD over down to the canvas, and quickly changes to a side headlock onto Tommy Van Dam!!! [Crowd POP!] [WJ:] Now this is applied perfectly, but Raven's weight is just so little compared to Tommy Van Dam, so TVD has the power advantage right now and that is coming into play, as TVD is back up and relases Raven for the ride. [JS:] Your calling this match pretty good as a shoulderblock takedown is nailed perfectly by J.D. "The Bird" Raven! TVD back up as Raven is off the ropes, again! [Deep Breath by John Simpson.] [WJ:] And a BBBIIIGGG DEEP BREATH BY JOHN SIMPSON!!! [Crowd POP!!!] [JS:] HaHa. J.D. Raven actually nailed one of his perfected maunevers, the Arm Drag Takedown!!! [WJ:] No, it was a BBBIIIGGG DEEP BREATH BY JOHN SIMPSON!!! AND HERE'S ANOTHER!!! [HUGE CROWD POP!!!] [JS:] Actually... TVD went outside the ring, and J.D. Raven did a suicide dive through the middle rope, onto TVD driving him back into the steel guard rail!!! [WJ:] HaHa! Look at these two fools!!! [Walker takes his headset off, and walks over to TVD and slaps him across the face.] [JS:] Here we go... [JS:] TVD comes back with rights and lefts! Walker is in pain!!! [CROWD POP!!!] TVD whips Walker Johnson to the guard rail!!! [Another Crowd POP!!!] TVD now even more angry just throws Walker Johnson into the steel steps!!! And now TVD climbs into the ring, and is met with a shoulder to the midsection on the ring apron!!! Raven now up and Fisherman Suplex!!! One!!! Two!!!!! THRRREEEEE!!!!!! [JS:] No way!!! [A Bird Call plays over the speakers in the APWA Palace as a huge mixed reaction is given in response.] [Joeboo:] Here is your winner, and advancing to the Action Packed Wrestling Alliance National Title Match... J.D. "The Bird" Raven!!! [JS:] Raven leaves the ring, as Walker comes back to the commentary table!!! [Walker sit's down in the chair, shaken up, and looking in poor condition. He then put's on his headset.] [WJ:] Nexxxxxxtttt.. Mmmmmmmmaaatttccchhhuuuppp... Pleeeaaassseeee. [JS:] Alrighty then! Let's see, to Joeboo for Eric Cooper vs. Brandon "The Hurricane" Honeyman!!!
