APWW Monday Night Asylum Recap

19 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
APWW Monday Night Asylum Recap Reno Pirelli, APWW/LOC The Inaugural Monday Night Asylum at the Left Of Center Arena was by all accounts a huge Standing Room only Success. At the beginning of the night, The Owner and Charwoman of the APWW, Queen Kaz, came down to the ring and told the audience that this new regeneration of the APWW was going to be bigger, badder, and bloodier than anything they've ever seen, and the crowd just ate it up. Either that, or they were all cheering as they were looking up her mini kilt, we couldn't really determine which. With Kaz in the ring were 2 very distinct personalities--two that really didn't want to be in the ring unless they were beating each other to a bloody pulp--IceNine, the former Champ, who took the opportunity to thank everyone for coming, and told everyone in a rare display of enthusiasm that they were about to see the kind of game he plays..which brought a growl from Wolfy on the other side of the ring. Wolfy never took his eyes off Nine the whole time in the ring, and for good reason. Nine and Wolfy have a date in the Cube at Hell Freezes Over on Feb 25, along with Bubba Lee Beauregard (formerly of LOC), and Algernon Q Locussbraith, the LOC Accountant. Locussbraith recently replaced Jordan Quinn in the match, after Quinn suffered a critical injury as the result of the helicopter crash that garnered international attention for the APWW's last PPV, Great Expectations. Wolfy, for his part, never said a word. He didn't need to. Our first matchup pitted two newcomers to the APWW: The Nullifyer versus Lady Lhia Rose. For newcomers, these two put on one heck of a show for the audience. Two very athletic high flyers with a lot of speed to their moves. At one point during this match, this reporter would swear to you that neither opponents feet hit the canvas for a good 10 seconds, ending that sequence in a double clothesline at midring from opposite turnbuckles! Truly amazing! OK, I might be exaggerating about the 10 seconds part, but you had to see it, folks. Nullifyer, for his part, played quite a bit to the crowd, while Lhia was very focused on her opponent, and that ended up paying off in the end. Lhia managed to counter a hurricanrana attempt by Nullyfier, and turn it into a modified sidewalk slam. A quick 3 count, and Lhia hits the pay window. Nullifyer and Lhia got a huge pop from the audience on the way up the entrance ramp. Our second match paired up two veterans from APWW, and some fan favorites: Phoenix and Brimstone. Phoenix and Brimmy were there to have some fun, and it showed. These two big men were really hamming it up for the crowd. Things got a little ugly after a headbutt that sent both men down to the canvas, Phoenix reached down to help Brimmy up, and Brimmy reached up and slapped him. Phoenix didn't take too kindly to that, and neither did the crowd. Phenix let loose with an all out assault on Brimmy, and was ready to plant the big man with the Egg Drop Bomb, when Brimmy overbalanced and toppled Phoenix backwards, bringing all 350 pounds of Brimstone squarely on his chest. Audible gasp from the crowd, as Brimmy dragged Phoenix over to the corner. One bellow of "Grab My Ass!" and a BrimBomb later, Brimmy was victorious. On the LOC Tron, Nine was shown backstage taking on his cell phone asking when someone could get here. Cut to Wolfy , Alley Katt, Bubba and Algernon watching this on the monitor with intense interest. Algernon then asked where the coffee machine was, which resulted in a smack on the back of the head by Bubba. Wolfy remarked that if Algernon made it through the Cube Match, it might restore his faith in miracles..just. Our third matchup of the night was a tag match between the two top teams in APWW in contention for the APWW Tag Team Championship: Karen Marie and Chooch, and Bluefusion and the Muted Beast. This Match only barely remained in control, as the current Tag Champs came down to ringside to comment along with myself and Alex Blaque. Chooch and the Beast were primarily in the ring, until Pork tried to lure Chooch away with another bag of cookies. This happened at Great Expectations, and Chooch wasn't about to fall for it again, Chooch executed a spinning heel kick on the Beast and popped right back up to clock Pork right off the apron. This brought all six combatants into the ring, and the ref had no choice to call it a no contest. Alley Katt addressed all of them in the ring at one time from over the LOC Tron, stating that since they can't get their act together, she was going to have to teach them how to. She then declared a match on Thursday Night Chaos between all three teams, with a twist. Pork and Chooch will be a team, Steve and Bluefusion comprising the second, and Karen Marie and the Muted Beast rounding out the trio. Cut to Kaz and Nine talking about how well the night is going.when Nine's phone rings.He tells Kaz he has to go meet one of his partners for the Cube Match shortly. Wolfy bursts out of his dressing room to go find a place to observe this. Stevey Wadds, our Hardcore Champion, comes down to ringside with his own personal shopping cart full of toys.Play Doh, Urinal cakes, Chainsaw, Sledgehammer, cookie tins, gingerbread men. Stevey says that after Great Expectations he demands respect as the APWW Hardcore Champion. He demanded that people cheer for him, the crowd did in fact start a cheer, but not the kind he was looking for. Stevey said that if he couldn't get respect from the audience by asking for it, then he would get it by beating up every single member of the APWW Roster. So he called out Bubba Lee Beauregard. Bubba, watching backstage was a bit surprised by this.but he came running out to the ring and straight into a garbage can lid, which knocked some sense into him. Bubba stood ramrod straight and stared at Stevey, who literally looked up at Bubba and pissed himself. Bubba literally chased Stevey all around the ring until Tino the Falcon came down to ringside to try to restore order by slapping Bubba in the face and yelling at him to get back in the ring. Bubba dragged Tino into the ring and put the Cobra Clutch on him, choking him out, when Stevey clocked him from beside with a stop sign. A three count later, and Stevey retains the APWW Hardcore Strap. Dazed, Bubba went back to the Dressing Room, only to find a note in handprint on the chair he had been sitting in that read "Bubba, help me. I am in the cl" Cut to Nine waiting out in the parking lot for his arrival, the camera focusing deep behind him, a pair of red eyes can be seen. The fifth match was our second pairing of newcomers to the APWW: Doj and Sandman. This, quite frankly, was a mismatch on the part of the booking committee. On oneside you had sandman at 160 pounds, and 6 feet tall, and then you have Doj at 4 foot 6, and in a wheelchair. Sandman never knew what hit him. Doj came down the entrance ramp and literally ran over Sandman. Doj managed to tow Sandman back into the ring, and despite his broken knees, put on a great match, even if you had to squint to see him in the ring from the rafters. Sandman was still dazed from his conference call with Doj's wheelchair that he didn't see Doj behind him when he stood up. LOW BLOW, LOW BLOW which sent Sandman back down into a fetal position. Doj somehow negotiated the climb up the turnbuckles and executed a beautiful frogsplash to get the victory, but somehow reinjured his knees on impact. Refusing help and sputtering obscenities that could only be reserved for members of the Armed Forces, Doj wheeled himself back up the entrance ramp to a standing ovation. In appreciation for the applause, Doj flipped everybody in the LOC arena the bird and wheeled off. Cut to Wolfy in the bushes as a limo pulls up and a figure steps out to greet Nine. Wolfy jumps out of the Bushes to ambush, but is caught in a chokehold, and lifted off the ground. The shadowed figure climbs up on the hood of the car and executes a spike piledriver, bouncing Wolfy's head off the pavement. Nine remarks that if he can do the job that well at Hell Freezes Over, we may have some talking to do. The Figure walks underneath one of the streetlamps, and Flashpoint says, "You're gonna have to talk to Dr. Patel about that, he says I'm not ready to be a people person yet. " Nine replies "That is EXACTLY what I'm counting on."
