APWW Thursday Night Chaos Recap

22 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday Night Chaos Recap Reno Pirelli-APWW/LOC The Inaugural Thursday Night Chaos proved about as popular as our journey to the Asylum on Monday Night. Standing room only in the LOC Arena, where we will be right up through Hell Freezes Over on 2/25, and then we'll take this show on the road! Lookout, America, the APWW is coming to your town, save your strength! Nine started everything off by coming down to the ring with FlashPoint. He stated that while he and his brother have a lot of issues that still need to be worked through, and that they really trusted each other about as far as they could spit an elephant, they one thing they found they could agree on is that neither of them liked Wolfy very much. Flashy really didn't say anything of value during this process, he was pacing the ring and mumbling to himself almost uncontrollably. Nine mentioned the Cube Match at HFO, and Flashy stopped and grinned at the crowd. Folks in the front row were visibly shaken by this. Wolfy came out to the ramp to a huge pop, and told Nine that while he had no idea how he managed to get Flashy on his side, he really didn't care much. He wanted a piece of Flashy, and he wanted it tonight. Nine denied his request, saying that without Flashy's medication to bring him down before a match, he wasn't gonna be responsible for that. But, he offered Wolfy a match with his yet to be named opponent tonight. Wolfy reluctantly agreed. Stevey Wadds came down to the ring, and started yelling to the crowd about how he thought beating Bubba Lee Beauregard would have brought him the respect he deserved, but it didn't. So, he said he was going to go through the APWW Roster until he got the people's respect-and then he called out Chooch. Chooch came ambling down to the ring eating cookies and throwing them to the crowd to some pretty good response. It was a pretty even match with both men having the upper hand at different points of the match. The ref really had a fit when Chooch went for his patented crotch bite, which in a usual match would have resulted in a fine ans suspension for Chooch, and a regimen of vaccinations for Stevey. Things got muffled though, when Midnight Express came down for the dressing room area and hit Chooch with his tag belt. Stevey took the cue and a three count later, Stevey retains the belt. Just after the win, though, Doj came down to the ringside area and challenged Stevey to a hardcore match at HFO. Stevey mocked the handicapable wrestler, saying that a guy with broken knees has absolutely no chance at being a serious contender for the HardCore Title. Doj climbed into the ring and swept Stevey of his feet, and began pummeling him with rights and lefts. Stevey managed to get away before real damage was inflicted, but he accepted Doj's challenge to meet at Hell Freezes Over for the HardCore Belt. Doj got a HUGE pop, which he returned with some choice obsenities and the obligatory middle finger before wheeling himself back to the backstage area. MiMiC was next down at the ring and he told the crowd that he wasn't interested in waiting for a PPV to get his shot at the InterContinental Belt, he wanted his shot tonight against Zowie. Zowie came down with Bubba Lee Beauregard in tow and took it to MiMiC like gangbusters. MiMiC, showing what was either ring rust or some really bad rookie tendencies, had a really hard time in the early going. Zow made a blunder when she went for a Lou Thesz Press, MiMic converted it to a Spinebuster Slam. MiMiC made the cover, and Bubba surprised a lot of people when he reached into the ring and held Zowie's foot down, guaranteeing a win for MiMiC, the new InterContinental Champion. Backstage, Alex Blaque asked about his selling Zowie down the river, he said that was one of the hardest things he's ever had to do. "But," Bubba continued, "seeing as MiMiC is a member of Predatory Intent, Wolfy woulda had my balls for breakfast if I had been out there and hadn't insured a win for MiMiC." Bubba then made it very clear that at some point MiMiC was gonna have to meet Bubba in the ring to straighten it out. MiMiC agreed. Backstage, Wolfy and Algernon were sitting at a cafeteria sized table with 17 cups of coffee, each pot named after a wrestling move. Wolfy made it clear that after Algernon made an acceptable attempt at a wrestling move, he could have that cup of coffee. Wolfy watched as Algernon tried a clothesline and ended up wrapped around a mannequin. Wolfy wondered how Algernon was able to count his nuts and get the same number twice. Barf Simspson made his APWW debut at Chaos, and he started off by saying how shocked and dismayed that someone of his sophmoric stature was not included at the inaugural APWW Monday Night Asylum. Nine spoke to him from the LOC Tron and asked if he would like to be the 2nd partner in the Cube Match at Hell Freezes over. Barf enthusiatically said he would love to be a part of that match, to which Nine laughed and replied "I'm sure you would." Nine then told Barf that he would be facing action tonight against another Newcomer to APWW, Cheeky Chappie. Chappie came down to ringside to get the match started, but it was eventually ruled a no contest by ref Rydell Lidocrantz, after a well placed can of Dinty Moore beef Stew was poured out over the ring, grossing the ref, Cheeky, and this commentator completely out. Nine then came to the ring and called Wolfy down to join him. Wolfy came down with Alley, Bubba, and Algernon in tow. Nine then revelaed that his 2nd Partner at HFO would be--Nightmare! This came as a shock to most everyone, as Nightmare had recently retired from wrestling from another fed. This match never got started because Chaos engulfed the ring. Everyone started taking shots at each other, and Tino the Falcon--recently appointed head of APWW security--stormed the ring with several referees to try to restore order. The refs were all knocked out in short order, and Tino himself was the victim of a High Times Slam, book ended by Nine and Wolfy! The two arch rivals stared a hole in each other as order was eventually restored. See you all at Hell Freezes Over on 2/25! Reno Pirelli
