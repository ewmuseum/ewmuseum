[STW] It's coming........

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Darkness, we see nothing. After a moment, a deep voice speaks to us.] V/O: They say everything is bigger in Texas... [The letter "S" written in stone bursts onto the screen, then shatters into pieces, leaving the screen black yet again.] V/O: ...even the appetite for wrestling, especially, in the Southeast... [The letter "T" written in stone bursts onto the screen, then shatters itself into pieces. We are black once again.] V/O: ...and now, the face of wrestling in Texas has been changed forever. A new chapter will be written... [The letter "W" written in stone bursts onto the screen, then shatters once again, leaving us with a black screen for the third time. The voice still speaks.] V/O: ...superstars _will_ be made, or, _broken_. The limits of any normal man will be challenged... [The three words "Southeast Texas Wrestling" now appear in stone on the screen. They shatter, the screen goes black, and the voice still speaks.] V/O: ...Southeast Texas Wrestling. Can you handle it? [With the screen still black, we fade in to see a man sitting behind a desk. He wears a nice black suit, has black hair slicked into a pony tail, and is none other than STW owner Lexington Reed. He speaks.] LR: Hello out there. My name is Lexington Reed, the head honcho around STW. I've put quite an amount of money into this commercial, even broadcasting it outside of Texas. Why? You ask. Because the STW is currently looking to assemble the best wresting talent there is today in the world, not just Texas. We want to provide the most diverse environment for our wrestlers and fans alike. We even implement a unique division which combines all style of wrestling. So, I urge whoever is watching this, come on down to the STW and give us a look. If you're a superstar, or, you want to be a superstar, we can use you. If you're a hardcore wrestler, or, a high flying wrestler. We can use you. Basically, if you wrestle and want the opportunity of a lifetime, come join us. You won't regret it. Thank you for your time. [Fade out.] To join the STW or for more information, contact [detail withheld] or read the STW FAQ on RSPWF.
