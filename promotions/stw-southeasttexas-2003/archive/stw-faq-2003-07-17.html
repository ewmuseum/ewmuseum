***************************************************** * [STW] SOUTHEAST TEXAS WRESTLING * ***************************************************** ----------> FAQ v.1.0 <------------ ---> What is the STW? ---> Where is the STW based? ---> What kind of fed is the STW? ---> What kind of shows does the STW put out? ---> Who are the STW personalities? ---> What are the titles in the STW? ---> Rules and Regulations ---> Application ************************* * WHAT IS THE STW? * ************************* --> The STW, which stands for Southeast Texas Wrestling, is a fantasy wrestling federation created solely by [name withheld] in 2003. The STW is dedicated to giving it's members a quality fantasy wrestling experience without making it feel like a job and giving overbearing work. Take a few minutes to read through this handbook and tell me what you think. *************************** * WHERE IS THE STW BASED? * *************************** --> Well, as you can see from the name, the STW is based in Southeast Texas. All the STW events will be held in the Southeast Texas Entertainment Complex, located in Beaumont, TX. The arena seats 9,500 max, and has been dubbbed the STW Arena by the higher ups. To get a look at the arena, go to the link below. http://www.setxevents.com/default.asp?lnopt=5&sn1opt=2&sn2opt=0 ********************************** * WHAT KIND OF FED IS THE STW? * ********************************** --> The STW tries to imply an open door policy. A wrestler may come in for a show and then later may be offered a contract if neccesary. The STW is looking for _all_ talent, established or not. If you're hardcore, we want you. If you're a cruiserweight, we want you. We are trying to put together a diverse group of athletes from all backgrounds and cultures. We are not marked by any name, be it hardcore or traditional. We are a hybrid of both. ******************************************** * WHAT KIND OF SHOWS DOES THE STW PUT OUT? * ******************************************** --> The STW is not a true indy fed, but it's also not nationally known either. We have 2 shows here, one flash and one live. Here is how the schedule will look. 1st Week: Flash Show comes out, this will include some summary matches for the people who don't get on the big show. 2nd Week: Big Show. This will include all the major happenings in the STW as well as 4 to 5 _huge_ matches. Update will follow 3rd Week: Same as 1st. 4th Week: Same as 2nd. ************************************* * WHO ARE THE STW PERSONALITIES? * ************************************* - PLAY BY PLAY - The STW implies a two person announce team. Both men are highly involved in the sport and know just about everything in the book. Well, maybe that's because the STW has a twin announce team! That's right, brother's Rick and Alex Marshall, make up the first twin announce team in the sport today. They look alike, but sometimes don't act alike. - BACKSTAGE - The STW has no main backstage correspondent. Random people will pop up to do interviews if need be. - MANAGEMENT - The IWE is run by business man Lexington Reed. Lexington has built the STW from the ground up and is looking to make it the top in the sport today. He is niether a fan favorite or a hated man. ******************************** * WHAT ARE THE TITLES IN STW? * ******************************** At the moment, the STW has 2 singles titles and no tag team titles. More may be added later. - STW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - Although we are only a regional based fed, we do have a World Title. The STW will travel to other places on rare occasions, where the holder of this belt will be recognized as our world champion. This is the number one title to have. - STW LONESTAR STATE CHAMPIONSHIP - And here it is, the most revolutionary title in the business today. While some other organizations have just a plain old singles title, the STW employs this hybrid title. It may be the number two title, but don't tell the man who holds it that. We are about diversity here, and so is this title. This title combines all styles from around the world into one. High flying, hardcore, whatever type of style you like, you'll see it in this division. It makes for some interesting matches to say the least. Hardcore may fight cruiserweight, it might be hardcore vs. hardcore, and it even might be cruiserweight vs. cruiserweight. Maybe a martial artist will step up and challenge for this title, or an old school mat wrestler, you just never know. **************************** * RULES AND REGULATIONS * **************************** Okay, I think you all pretty much know the basic rules. Don't swear in your RP's unrealistically, don't attack announcers, blah blah blah. My major concern is participation. We only have one big show, which means that all you need is one RP every two weeks and that's all you're allowed. That's not a lot at all. And if you're kind enough, throw me a strat. I really don't like writing matches for guys who haven't done a damn thing and sometimes I won't. I want to make every handler in the STW the best they can be. If you show me the effort, I will gladly return the favor. We don't have PPV's in the STW, we have Mega cards. It's the equivelent of a PPV. And nothing ticks me more than me giving somebody a match and then them not RPing for it. It's supposed to be the big end to the cycle so I expect everyone to turn in something. All in all, I think we can make the STW one of the best. I've had a lot of experience in e-w before and if you doubt my writing skills, e-mail me and I'll show you a sample of my work. C'mon guys, lets make history, sign up. **************** * APPLICATION * **************** SINGLES APPLICATION Handler Information ********************** Name: E-Mail: AIM: IRC: Wrestler Information *********************** Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Alignment: Appearance: Gimmick: Entrance: (Write out entire ring entrance, John Morten is the STW ring announcer.)
