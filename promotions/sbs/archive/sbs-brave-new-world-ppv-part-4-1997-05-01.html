Fine, you sign up a 6 on 1, yeah that's right 6 I said. We will all kick your no good slimy ass! Anyone who wants to cross my path, the AA will take care of you, and if you cross any of my brother's paths, I'm gonna take care of you. 1 for All, All for 1. BL: Thanks for the interview Zach, you can have Spawn, can I keep the hookers though? ZZ: Sure, when I get done with my freebies. Thanks for talking with me! { Fade } TJ: Lance, er...nice of you to join us. LM: Shaddup Johnson, you know I’m only here because the Championship Committee would let me ref tonights match. TJ: Hardly suprising knowing of your past associations with Zager and the AA. LM: Look Johnson, I probably know Zager as well as anyone here in SBS and I know what tactics he’ll be using. But I must admin I’ve now got a grudging respect for Dubois. BL: What you actually like the Cajun Flower and your turning you back on the AA! LM: I wondered when you’d pipe up, you know what Porky you make me sick, sucking upto whoever’s the current flavour of the month. You know what Porky Johnson may be a dork but at least he’s consistant. BL: { Hiding behind Ted } If you weren’t such a cripple I wup your ass right now Morrison. LM: Yeah right, save it for someone who cares. There’s only one person that Lance Morrison looks out for and that’s himself, I just want to see a fair fight. TJ: As do I, Lets go to Mike as I believe the match is about to begin. { Rockit by Def Leppard blasts over the P.A. and there is a big Heel pop as Zach Zager appears at the top of the aisle with his valet Samantha. } MF: This match is for the SBS World Title, annoucing first from Jacksonville, Florida and weighing in at 279lbs he is Zach ‘Movin’ Man Zager. { Fireworks explode as Zager struts his way down to the ring, he seems to be in a very confident mood, he pauses for a moment and looks up to the booth before finally climbing into the ring } { Some different music blares over the P.A. and the crowd start to cheer as they expect to see Rene Dubois appear at the top of the aisle. } MF: And his opponent weighing in at 248lbs and hailing from Baton Rouge Louisiana he is the current SBS World Champion, ‘Cajun Power’ Rene Dubois!!!! { The crowd pop wildly but still Rene does not appear. } BL: The wuss has chickened out, he’s going to have to forfeit the belt. LM: I wouldn’t be so sure of that Porky. { The crowd go really quiet but suddenly pop again as a figure appears at the top of the aisle, it’s Rene Dubios he is leaning on Lady Duval for support his head and shoulders are heavily bandaged, the crowd go wild shouting ‘Cajun, Cajun...’ as they make there way down to the ring closely followed by John Maverick. } BL: They should stop this match before it starts Dubois can’t wrestle in the condition he’s in, { turns to Lance } You know what I couldn’t have done a better job myself. TJ: I’m suprised that the match has gone ahead, but there’s only one man who knows whether Rene Dubois can wrestle and that’s Rene Dubois. { Zager looks well pleased with himself as Rene painfully climbs into the ring, he signals for a mic. } RD: The doctors told me I shouldn't wrestle tonight. They think I should forfeit thematch and give up the belt . (looks down at the belt) I came here to do just that. (Zagar laughs and slaps hands with his friends outside the ring). That's what a sane man would do - just give up the belt and go away to heal. (looks down at the belt then looks up and throws the beltdown behind him) THE HELL I WILL! { Rene tears off his bandages and attacks Zagar. The crowd go wild } LM: I told you that Dubois was one tough kid, he just don’t know when to give up. TJ: Ref calling for the bell as Dubois his Zager with a knee to the gut, he follow’s up with a couple of blows before taking Zager to the mat with a snap suplex. Zager looks stunned as he slowly gets to his feet but is soon taken down again by a clothesline by Dubois. He rubs his shoulder before quickly applying a figure four leglock, Zager screams out in pain, and Dubois really leans into the move. Zager manages to reach the ropes and the ref makes Dubois break the hold. BL: Blatant cheating there by Dubois, attacking Zager when his back was turned. LM: When fighting someone like Zager you gotta play on his terms, there ain’t a match gone by where he hasn’t cheated in one form or another. TJ: Both men get to there feet now, Dubois tries to whip Zager into the turnbuckle but Zager reverses and quickly follows up with a handspring elbow knocking all of the wind out of Dubois.{ Crowd pop } I thought it would be too good to be true here come Johnny Durante and the Immortal. Zager looks up and smiles as he see’s the two men comming down the aisle. Zager picks up Dubois and executes a shoulder breaker and Dubois screams out in pain. BL: Dubois is done for there’s no way he’s gonna win with that damaged shoulder, Zager’s got him licked. LM: There’s no way the kids gonna give up that easy, there’s plenty of fight left in him yet. Although Zager’s gonna work on that shoulder as much as possible. TJ: Zager stomps on Dubois a few times before moving over and picking up the Cajun Glove. { Crowd pop} Look it’s Mercy and the Mask looks like they’ve come down to ringside to even the scores up a bit. They move over and stand next to Lady Duval. Zager really seems to be struggling to get that glove on. Dubois comes round and quickly takes Zager down into a small package. 1...2..Kickout by Zager. BL: You can be sure wherever Mercy is there’s gonna be trouble. TJ: Both men to there feet, Dubois tries a belly-to-belly suplex but his shoulder gives way, Zager grabs him by his bad arm and once again whips him into the turnbuckle, this time he follows up with a splash but no-ones home. Zager stumbles back and turns round straight into Rene Dubois boot, what a savate kick by Dubois. He picks up the glove and slips it on. BL: What’s going on over there, there seems to be some dissension in Dubois ranks, Lady Duval and the Mask seem to be arguing. John Maverick tries to intervene but Mercy holds him back. Lady Duval starts to wag here finger at the Mask and he slaps her across the face...the coward that’s twice in one night he’s attacked a woman. TJ: Dubois quickly executes a DDT on Zager and see’s what is going on outside the ring, he leans over the ropes and tries to pull the Mask away, OH MY GAWD!!!! { The whole arena takes a deep breath. } BL: Dubois has just unmasked the Mask and I don’t believe it, the Mask is Eric Righteous, how could it be he’s such a goody goody. LM: I’m not suprised in the slightest there was always something funny about that Righteous character it always looked like he was hiding something. TJ: Zager takes advantage of the situation as the ref tries to break things up outside the ring and executes a neckbreaker on Dubois, he whips Dubois into the Turnbuckle and Durante grabs hold of his legs. Zager follows up with a big splash, Dubois slumps to the mat and Durante grabs hold of his arms as Zager continues to stomp on his bad shoulder. The ref climbs back in and Durante lets go. Maverick starts to make his way round to Durante. BL: Now what’s that god damned Texas cow poke doing. { The is a pop from the crowd } LM: I think I can guess what’s going to happen next. TJ: Machete makes his way down to ringside and goes over to where Mercy and Righteous and standing wondering what there next move should be now that Lady Duval has run out back. Maverick and Durante now going at it on the outside , the Immortal joins in and the both start to beat on Maverick. Zager picks up Dubois and throws him through the ropes to the outside. { Suddenly Scorpion emerges from the crowd and leaps over the barrier to where Zager is pounding on Dubois } LM: I feel it’s time for the mailman to make a special delivery. { Morrison gets up and hobbles out of the booth } BL: Now where’s he going, probably to help Zager finish Dubois off. TJ: Scorpion flashes the AA sign at Zager and tosses him a baseball bat, Zager grins, Scorpion notices that Righteous is across the otherside of the ring and he makes a beeline for him. Scorpion now going at it with Righteous and Mercy this is chaos there are fights go on all around the ring someone has to get control. BL: What’s Machete doing, he goes over to where Zager is stomping on Dubois, Zager lifts up Dubois and Daigle starts to shout at him something about friendship and a title shot, Daigle picks up the bat and hit’s Dubois round the head with it, that’s it Dubois is out cold. The Immortal and Durante have really given Maverick a beating and they go over to help Scorpion beat off Mercy and Righteous. TJ: Lady Duval helping Maverick back to the locker room, where the hell are security. Morrison makes his way down to the ring. He goes towards Zager and Zager flashes the AA sign at Morrison, Lance grins and OH MY GAWD!! he chokeslams Zager to the concrete floor broken leg and all. Morrison grabs hold of Dubois and starts to pull him back to the locker room area. { Big Crowd pop } BL: Here comes Spawn he’s got a big chain, Spawn now clearing house, he sends the members of the AA scattering into the crowd. Spawn now helping Mercy and Righteous to there feet. Security come from out back and help Morrison and Dubois to the locker room area. Things still ain’t over yet. { The members of the AA stand in the aisle looking at the ring, in it stand Spawn, Mercy and Righteous. S: Apocalytic Alliance...take a good look at what you see here in front of you. We are the SBS's response to the Tripple Threat, The Dangerous Alliance, and The Four Horsemen, all rolled up into one nasty stable which, from here on out, you will refer to only as...The Untouchables. ER: Hey, Durante! Swing that one, baby!! { 'Come and Get Sone of This', by House of Pain begins to blare over the PA. The members of the AA try to storm the ring but security holds them back. Especially with the Untouchables already being in possession of the TV Belt and Mercy having the next shot at the world title, I’m sure the AA will be looking for some payback. Anyway that’s all we’ve got time for tonight folks, remember to join us next week for more grappling action. { Picture fades with Bill still trying to come to terms with the fact that Dubois is still champ and that it was Morrison that made sure he was.....}
