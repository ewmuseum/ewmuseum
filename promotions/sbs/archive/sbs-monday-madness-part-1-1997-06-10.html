------------------------------------------------------------------ SBS eWrestling Monday Night Madness, 9th June 1997 Live from Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota. ------------------------------------------------------------------ TJ: Welcome to Monday Night Madness brought to you live from the Target Center here in Minneapolis, Minnesota. I’m Ted Johnson and as usual I’m joined by Bill Lane. What the… (Boogyman, by White Zombie blares over the PA. Righteous and Mercy walk out together to start the show. Righteous flaunts his belt to the crowd, while Mercy drags his on the ground behind him. They climb into the ring to a chorus of boos, but we can see Untouchable signs being waved proundly in a few places. Once in the ring Mercy grabs the mic from Falco and shoves him into the corner.) WM: Just like the prodigal sun, we've returned (crowd boos). Thanks to Marshall who sent us on a Euro wrestling tour, I can't tell you what a thrill it was to wrestle in front of those yellow toothed freaks every night (crowd laughs). ER: You said it Waylon, the funny thing is that even with their ugly teeth, their stinking bodies, and their tiny brains...(crowd boos)...they at least recognized true greatness when it walked amongst them...(garbage is thrown into the ring) WM: Well, it sure is good to be back in the good 'ole US of A, where we can finally take action against some of our problems. Lets start with the small ones. First up is you Eric, I recall you're facing some Masked Mabel chump... ER: Yeah, I'll do him just like Scorpions valet...reeeeaaallll easy! Damn, I hope Firestarter is in attendance, because I just might deliver two Penances so that he can see how its done. And if he comes close enough, well, he just might find himself on the receiving end of one. WM: And me, I got myself The Immortal tonight. He might not be AA material anymore, but when he was, he irritated me, and, well sir, when I get irritated I don't always think so clear. I suppose I'm gonna have to make a point of demonstrating that tonight. Immortal, (eyes seethe with hatred) even after I've snapped your ribs with a Titanium Splash, we're still not through, you're just the first mongrel I have to destroy before I get to the top dogs (giggles) this is gonna be so easy...I just might not stop!! ER: As for this four corners tag match...It doesn't make a lick of difference. I'm not done with my belt yet, and I'm sure your not done with yours, so who in the hell is good enough to come and take 'em from us? Nobody...we don't have a thing to worry about. The only other thing we have to address is this Survivor Series... WM: Let me take this. Zagar, like I warned you before, that man has no business here. He's nothing but a foreign stiff. Bringing him to the ring with you is gonna be your greatest error...he's not gonna be the trump card that wins it, he's gonna be the liability that seals your grave...You know what I mean? ER: Oh damn Zagar, maybe you really are as dumb as you look, cause you've awakened a giant, and I sure as hell wouldn't want to be in your position...but hey, you f*ck with a bull, you get the horns. Don't forget that we have some goodies for your entertainment as well. It looks like the Survivor Series is a pot luck, and we’ve cooked up a couple of real sweet dishes! (Both laugh and return to the locker room area. ) BL: They seem to be in confident mood tonight. TJ: That they do and Righteous is starting things out as he takes on the newcomer Masked Marvel. BL: Yeah and later we get to see Spawn and Goouch’s gals, well hopefully he’ll bring some with him this week. TJ: Also we get to see Scorpion battling against ‘Uptown’ Johnny Durante for the World Title as well as a four corners elimination bout to see who faces Righteous and Vengeance for the Tag Team belts. BL: Anyway looks like the first match is about to start. ------------------------------------------- Eric Righteous Vs Masked Marvel Writer: BF ------------------------------------------- ( The Masked Marvel is already standing in the ring. He is a tall, well built man dressed in a blue super hero outfit with red boots and a red cape. A big Red M is written on his chest.) BL: This ought to be very boring. C’mon! Righteous Vs. this buffoon? This league is really going down hill since Deacon left. TJ: Well we sure don’t know much about this guy except he is big. BL: Yeah and he has a bad taste in clothes. Where did he get this outfit? From a comic book convention of loser super heroes? TJ: Calm down, Bill here comes Righteous. BL: I wish I was younger I’d.... TJ: Lose with style!! Take it away Falco..... BL: HEY!!! (Devil’s Haircut plays and the crowd begins to boo loudly) MF: From Chicago, weighing 245 pounds, Eric Righteous ( The crowd boos louder as Righteous walks out in his traditional garb. Waylon Mercy, The Spawn and Goouch walk out with him. The new head of security walks out with a squad of jobbers and rent a cops who force the Untouchables to the dressing area. Righteous is upset but continues to the ring. ) TJ: I guess this new security officer takes his job seriously. BL: I wonder if Marshall will send the goon squad out so quick against other people? TJ: Are you implying that President Marshall is showing favoritism? BL: I’m not implying nothing. I was talking to Eric Righteous earlier and he says he has proof that Marshall is on the take but he wouldn’t give me any names. (Giant face pop) Oh My Gawd! Here comes that silly hill billy, Maverick. Where’s the security guy when you need him? He’s going into the ring! I hope both guys beat the snot out of him! (Maverick enters the ring and asks Falco for the microphone. He then places his cowboy hat on Falco’s head. Marvel starts to come towards Maverick, but Maverick stops him with a cautionary gesture ) JM: Easy down there, big boy. I got no problem with you. (turns and points at Righteous) But you, son, I got a big problem with. I been tryin' to get you in the ring ever since that Tag tournament and you been duckin' me. Well, now I'm callin' you out, you little coward. (turns to Marvel) Mr. Marvel, I'd count it as a personal favor if you'd just kindly leave enough of this punk for me to beat on next week. You can whomp on him all you want to, but leave me just a little bit so's I can have the satisfaction of messin' up that purty face of his. (back to Righteous) If you got any guts at all, boy, you just sign your name on that contract in ol' Marsh's office and git your puny carcass down to the ring next week. An' don't you worry none about how you're gonna get back, cause I'll have a stretcher waitin' for ya. (throws the mic down and walks out to a loud face pop from the crowd. ) TJ: A challenge has been made. The ball is in Righteous’ court now. BL: If I were Righteous I would have beat that cowpoke silly. TJ: Whether we like him or not, Righteous is a professional. He is signed to fight the Masked Marvel and I believe that is the only thing on his mind. BL: Maybe you are right (grumbles a little) Righteous looks ready for this one. I guess he pestered the committee to let him fight jobbers to pad his record. TJ: Maybe but that isn’t for us to decide. BL: Is this guy going to wrestle with that silly red cape? TJ: It sure looks like it. BL: They’ll let anyone wrestle here won’t they? I wish they would be more choosy. TJ: Paulie Anderson checks both men and calls for the bell. Righteous tugs on the ropes and circles the MM. MM just stands there and baits Righteous in. Righteous feints for a lock up but walks into the ropes instead. Anderson walks between MM and Righteous. The crowd boos as Righteous motions for the ref to back MM up. The Marvel is really upset now. He pushes Anderson out of the way and throws a clubbing right hand at Righteous but Righteous moves out of the way. BL: I wish someone would hit someone here. I mean the match has been going on and the only person hit has been the ref. TJ: The men are just feeling each other out...that’s all. BL: With Righteous you can’t be sure.... TJ: That was uncalled for. BL: You were thinking it. I just said it is all. TJ: The two men finally lock up. MM easily over powers Righteous and rakes him in the eyes. Righteous staggers back but the MM clamps on a choke hold. Anderson moves in for the break but MM doesn’t look concerned. Paulie counts...1,2,3....MM breaks the hold and starts stomping Righteous into the mat. BL: I think Eric is getting a little more than he bargained for. I take back what I said about MM. He is brutal!! I haven’t seen this type of fighting in a long time!! TJ: Anderson pushes MM back because Righteous is caught in the ropes. Righteous is a little reluctant here as he gets to his feet. The MM sits on the corner turnbuckle and folds his arms. I think he is laughing at Righteous. Righteous comes charging the corner but all he meets is the Marvel’s size 18. Bulldog off the top rope by MM. Quick cover..1,2......kick out by Righteous. BL: That was close. Oh geez....MM clamps on a choke hold again. Anderson is warning the Marvel and he breaks the hold. If Righteous wants to win this one I suggest he get busy!!! ER: Righteous is a victim of overconfidence. MM picks up Righteous and sends him flying into the ropes and Power slams him!! I’ve never seen Righteous take this much abuse before!!! Cover by Marvel 1,2...Marvel picks Righteous up before the count of three!!! I guess he isn’t finished yet. Marvel whips him into the far corner and follows him in with a big time splash. BL: Did you see the elevation on that one? I wonder what he calls that move? TJ: I am beginning to think that something fishy is going on here. BL: It’s just your wife, Ted. TJ: I’ll deal with you later. Righteous is basically out on his feet but MM drops him with a DDT. MM covers Righteous. Anderson in position......1,2.....MM picks Righteous up again. Anderson warns the Marvel, but he doesn’t care. Righteous whipped into the turnbuckle.....Oh MY!!! He nearly took his head off with that flying clothesline!!! MM doesn’t go for the cover instead he whips Righteous into the corner and hits him with another devastating splash!!! Righteous falls face first. MM hoists Righteous up in a vertical position and climbs to the second rope. What a display of balance and strength by the big man. OUCH!!! MM delivers a brainbuster from the second rope. BL: HEY!!! He stole that move!!! TJ: MM doesn’t cover Righteous again. He places him on top of the corner turnbuckle and climbs to the second rope....OH!!! Not Again!!! MM delivers another brainbuster. Righteous is hurt!! The human neck can’t take this much punishment!! MM climbs to the top rope and lands a leg drop on the prone Righteous. BL: Why doesn’t he just pin him? He has already proven he can beat him. TJ: It looks like this is personal. I don’t know who this guy is but he sure has Righteous’ number!! BL: I knew Righteous couldn’t back up what he was saying!!!! TJ: MM drops another leg across the throat of Righteous. Anderson begins to count but MM removes his leg. The crowd is cheering uncontrollably as MM places Righteous on the top rope. Here comes that move again!!! Righteous is planted head first with that brainbuster!! MM places 1 finger on the chest of Righteous. Anderson counts.....1,2,3.....(Ding, Ding) The Masked Marvel wins!!!! BL: I don’t know who that was but he sure is tough!!! TJ: I think you and I both know who it is. BL: Nah, it can’t be him. TJ: Fans we’ll be right back after these messages. ( Cut’s to a commercial ) -------------------------------- Crazedee Vs Hellfire Writer: NM -------------------------------- TJ: Well this should prove to be an interesting encounter. BL: ( Choking on a pork rind. ) Interesting, well that’s not a word that I would use! The worst wrestler in SBS Vs Hellfire the drug smuggler how about losers. TJ: You’re being a bit uncharitable there Bill, Hellfire was cleared of all charges and Crazedee, since his change in name has improved in leaps and bounds and he’s promising us a big surprise tonight. BL: Yeah, hopefully he will retire! TJ: There’s no taking to some people. BL: Up yours Johnson. ( Invisible touch by Genesis plays over the PA ) MF: Annoucing first from Calgary, Canada and weighing in at 244lbs, the self styled icon Hellfire. ( There is a small face pop as Hellfire appears at the head of the aisle, he is dressed in his usual ring attire, he slaps hands with a few of the fans before slowly climbing in to the ring. ) TJ: Hellfire looks to be a bit under the weather tonight. BL: Yeah, probably been sniffing something he shouldn’t (Whata Man by En Vouge comes blaring over the sound system. ) MF: And his opponent from Miami Beach, Florida and weighing in at 275lbs , KX…..Crazedee!!! ( The crowd are pretty quiet, but then they start to pop a little as Deadlock and Makaveli appear at the head of the aisle.) BL: Now I know what his surprise is, he’s not wrestling tonight he’s gone and chickened out! ( Suddenly there is a commotion from the crowd and Crazedee jumps into the ring from the other side. He has completely changed his ring attire he is wearing orange/yellow/pink tights with a wacky face on the side and he is no longer wearing a shirt. Deadlock and Makaveli disappear into the locker room area as he starts to beat on Hellfire. ) TJ: Crazedee has completely changed his image I wonder if it’s going to change his luck as well. TJ,BL: Naaaa BL: Peewee calling for the bell, I wonder how long it will be till Crazedee screws up again. Crazedee now sending Hellfire for the ride, he sets for a back body drop but no! Hellfire turns the move into a swinging neckbreaker and Crazedee hits the mat hard. Not long apparently. TJ: Hellfire going up top I think he wants to finish this one quickly, he’s a bit to slow Crazedee to his feet and he shakes the ropes. Ouch now that’s gotta hurt. Hellfire falls to the outside holding his package. Hellfire struggles to get up and Crazedee floors him with a double axe handle from the ring apron. Crazedee hauls Hellfire to his feet, Hellfire tries to whip Crazedee into the rail but he reverses and Hellfire crashes into the ring steps. Crazedee now grabbing Hellfire by the hair and he rams his head into the ring post. Your quiet Bill. BL: I can’t believe what I’m seeing, Crazedee is actually giving someone a pasting! TJ: He sure is, Crazedee rolls Hellfire back into the ring. Crazedee pulls Hellfire up, Hellfire with a rake to the eyes and Crazedee staggers back, Hellfire goes for the DDT but Crazedee grabs hold of the ropes and he falls to the mat. Hellfire staggers up, Front Face Slam by Crazedee and Hellfire hits hard, Crazedee going for the pin 1………2……..Shoulder up by Hellfire that was close. BL: Crazedee’s probably blown it now, here comes the Butcher. (Small crowd pop as he Bashers manager the Butcher comes to ringside and starts to taunt Crazedee ) TJ: Crazedee getting involved in a slanging match with the Butcher, look here come the rest of the Eastside Connection. ( Small heel pop Makaveli and Deadlock run down to ringside and start to beat on the butcher. ) BL: This is finally getting interesting! ( The is suddenly a big crowd pop as the Basher comes running from the Locker room area and he knocks Makaveli and Deadlock off there feet. ) TJ: Crazedee leaving his Eastside friends to fight it out with the Basher. Crazedee sends Hellfire for the ride, again he sets to early for the back body drop and Hellfire reverse’s it into a backslide. Crazedee holds firm! Hellfire can’t get him over , Crazedee climbing the ropes he’s going for the Crazy Slam and he hits it. He’s going for the pin! 1…….2…… BL: The Basher knocks Deadlock and Makaveli over and dives into the ring he tries to break up the hold but it’s too late,…3 Peewee calling for the bell! TJ: Crazedee gets the win!!, he’s still going at it with the Basher, Makaveli and Deadlock storming the ring now, the Basher is getting his butt whooped, the Butcher gets to his feet here come security. ( Eventually security clear the ring leaving only Crazedee. ) MF: The winner of this match by Pinfall, Crazedee! ( There is a surprising large pop as Crazedee celebrates his win. He signals for a mic. ) C: This is the new Crazedee your seeing now and he’s one hell of a mean SOB! Basher be warned there’s plenty more from where that came from and be rest assured after the PPV there gonna be scraping you off the canvas with a spatula. This goes out to all the other stables in SBS, the Eastside Connection is in the house and we’re gonna spoil all your parties! ( Crazedee throws down the mic and leaves the ring. ) BL: Who’s he trying to kid one win over a fading Icon, these Eastside boys are nothing but a bunch of chumps. TJ: I have to agree the Eastside connection have n’t made many waves so far but week in, week out the are definitely improving. BL: ( Grumpily ) Well that’s not too hard! TJ: Well this should cheer you up. BL: Yeah ? TJ: Yeah up next we have Spawn. BL: Yeah and Goouch’s gals!
