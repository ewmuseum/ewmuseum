Friday, March 23, 1997 Friday Night Fire SBS Live from the Summit Houston, Texas 8:00 PM ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Backstage at the Summit) The Untouchables are all gathered around Gouuch who appears to be handing envelopes to the event security staff. Eric Righteous looks to the camera and yells, “Get those cameras out of here!!” as he slams the door to the dressing room. (fade) The camera opens with a scene of The Dragons pinning the X-Boyz and SBS champ, Rene DoBois beating the snot out of the New Mexico champ. The scene shifts to a sold out arena. The fans go crazy as fireworks explode and the voice of Mike Falco yells over the PA, “LET’S GET READY FOR WAR.........SBS STYLE!!!!!! The crowd is worked to a fever pitch and the camera cuts to our good friends, Bill and Ted in the commentary booth. BL: What the hell was that all about? I’m seeing a side of Righteous I’ve never seen before. I am really getting impressed now. TJ: I hate to see a great athlete like Righteous throw away his career like that. Whatever happened to the Eric we all liked? BL: He got smart and joined a winning team. Haven’t you been keeping up with things in the SBS? TJ: A lot has happened since the mini event Monday Night. BL: Nobody wants to hear this...who cares if DuBois got his belt back. He’s only going to lose it to Machete later on. TJ: That may be possible...did you ever think of what that would do to the AA if Machete were to bring home the gold that Zager couldn’t? BL: (fumbles with the mic) what did you say (taps on microphone) Is this thing on???? TJ: (smiles and clears his throat) Lets take it down to ringside, where Mike Falco is ready to announce our first match of the night. BL: What’s the point? Do you think the 'Trashman' is actually gonna get a win over Firestarter? Look at the size of the guy...6'7"! You know what I heard? I heard that a recently released study has proven that a man's intelligence is inversely proportional to his height! (begins laughing) TJ: Well, if it was to the size of a man's stomach you wouldn't be able to put two words together! (Bill Lane stares at him with a look of confusion) Enough, lets go to Falco. (Mike Falco has already introduced Ted Rogers, who is jawing at some spectators who seem to have littered around their seats) MF: ...and his opponent, hailing from Seattle, at 6'7" and a weight of 297 pounds (Suddenly Firestarter comes blasting from the PA, as a giant Caucasian man wearing orange and red spandex, with short orange-red hair comes charging out from the locker room) he is the Firestarter! (The Firestarter stops halfway down the aisle raving to his entrance music, the crowd loves it! Some teenagers in the crowd begin copying his frenzied movements) BL: What is that garbage all about? Is he an epileptic or something? TJ: I don't know what this man is about, but he seems to have the crowd behind him. (Ding, Ding) There's the bell and...where had Ted Rogers gone? BL: He's bent over picking up some paper cups that some of these idiots threw on the ground! TJ: Now he's a little closer to them as Firestarter just kicked him face first into the guard rail. Wow, look at Firestarter work him over with those closed fists! BL: Get in there ref! That's a disqualification! I can't even watch this, Rogers didn't have a chance to start with, and now Firestarter is grinding Rogers' face against the ring post! TJ: Firestarter rolls Rogers into the ring, and now begins repeatedly booting him in the head. BL: He ain't gonna win many technical matches like this... TJ: Oh, really? Firestarter just delivered an impressive looking inverted bulldog. Rogers is completely senseless. Firestarter lifts Rogers back up, and sends him for the ride into the ropes, and CATCHES HIM WITH A HUMUNGOUS CHOKESLAM!! Wow! I haven't seen power like this in quite some time! BL: That was vintage Bill Lane right there, the greatest TV champion of all time! TJ: Do you ever get enough of yourself? BL: Haven't yet, don't think its possible. TJ: (sigh) Firestarter has just delivered a vicious powerbomb to the hapless Rogers, and now gestures to the crowd. I think this means he's setting up his finisher. He calls it The Pyro, but I have no idea what it is. Why not just pin him? Rogers is finished. BL: Its called making a statement, and here it comes. Firestarter climbs the ropes, leading Rogers with him. TJ: Oh, no...I can't watch this... BL: He's set Rogers up for a Tombstone, and there’s only one way to go from way up there and that’s TJ+BL: DOWN!! TJ: Firstarter has trouble hooking Rogers' leg, likely because its violently spasming from the impact of that maneuver. He finally just holds Rogers' shoulders down and gets the three count. MF: Your winner, in 5 minutes and 38 seconds...THE FIRESTARTER! (Again Firestarter, by The Prodigy plays as Firestarter returns to the locker room to a mixture of boos, and cheers from the crowd. He plays to both on his way back.) The Pyro is very similar to Eric Righteous' finisher, The Penance if I'm not mistaken. BL: The only difference is the version of the piledriver each uses. It'll be interesting to see if Righteous has anything to say about that. One things for certain though, Rogers'll have problems even making a fist after the beating he took tonight. It looks like garbage collection might be a little late in his neighborhood! (starts laughing) TJ: You're a very twisted man. You know that? Anyways lets move on with the action... --------------------------------------- Black Knight vs Little Louie --------------------------------------- TJ: Well next up we have newcomer the Black Knight going up against a returning star in the form of Little Louie. BL: Little Louie now that’s a much better name for him, I don’t know how you could describe him as a star he never made an impact here in SBS. TJ: He had a good start in the World Championship when he first joined. BL: Yeah and how the mighty have fallen finally getting kicked out of the league at the hands of the Basher! And what about this Black Knight he seems to be stuck in some sort of time warp. TJ: Well he comes here with a good reputation and a favorable record. BL: Yeah from some crummy European league wasn’t it. ( Beethoven's Ninth Symphony starts playing over the PA and the Black Knight appears at the head of the aisle. Small heel pop.) MF: Announcing first from London, England and weighing in at 285lbs he is accompanied to the ring by his manager Saigon ...The Black Knight! ( The Black Knight is completely covered in black with no flesh visible. There is a gray shield with a black cross printed on the left chest of his costume. Saigon follows behind he is a very small wizened old man he carries a cane. The Black Knight completely ignores the crowd walking to the ring as if he has a bad smell under his nose. ) MF: And his opponent Little Louie! ( There is barely a murmur from the crowd as Louie who was already in the ring holds up his hands. ) BL: The Black Knight certainly looks like he has the physical tools, lets just hope that he picks up where the Basher left off and gives Louie a beating. TJ: Whoa, Black Knight going to work fast and he starts to beat on Louie before the bell rings, Black Knight now whipping Louie to the ropes, he comes back and the Knight catches him on the return and executes a big backbreaker. Louie rolls around in pain and the Black Knight slips on a camel clutch Louie cries out as the ref checks him. BL: Look at him bleating he always was a wimp. Black Knight releases the hold, you know what I’m beginning to like this Knight guy it looks like he likes to cause pain. TJ: The Black Knight holds up his arms and the crowd start to boo. He pulls Louie to his feet and sends him for the ride, Louie bounces off the ropes ducks under a clothesline before flooring the Knight with one of his own the Knight tries to get up but Louie hits him squarely of the chin with a dropkick. Louie now going up top. BL: He’s too slow, the Black Knight getting to his feet, Louie jumps off the top rope, but the Knight catches him in mid air and sends him crashing to the mat with a huge powerslam. TJ: The Black Knight applying an abdominal stretch, Louie cries out again, the ref checks to see if he wants to give up and the Knight breaks the hold. The Knight now whipping Louie into the turnbuckle and he follows up with a running clothesline which nearly takes off Louie’s head. The Black Knight lays a few kicks into Louie before holding up his arms and walking round the ring much to the displeasure of the fans. BL: He’s just toying with Louie now, I wish he’d hurry up cause I’m getting a bit hungry now. TJ: What again Porky ? Louie gets to his feet and the Knight hits him with a textbook DDT. Louie is out of it. The Knight signals that he’s going for his finisher what’s it called. BL: I believe he calls it the Royal Deathlock it’s some variation on a full nelson. TJ: The Knight applies the hold and Louie almost immediately submits. The ref now calling for the bell. BL: He, He, the Knight isn’t letting go of the hold the ref warning him, the Knight still has the hold clamped on and he’s starting to shake Louie about, the ref warns him again and Saigon jumps into the ring and finally manages to get the Knight to release the hold. TJ: The Knight throws Louie into a corner and starts to get in the ref’s face, the crowd start to boo him, once again Saigon has to intervene to stop the Knight doing something stupid. ( The two men leave the ring and they are booed as they walk up the aisle, a couple of staff come running from the locker room area and help Little Louie out of the ring. ) BL: Well I must admit that was better than I thought it was going to be the Black Knight showing he has some real promise. TJ: I’m not sure that he’d do so well if he faced off against someone like Rene Dubois. BL: What the Cajun Flower, I can’t believe that he is still SBS World Champion., no, the Knight would easily kick his butt. TJ: Anyway next up we see two newcomers face off against each other. ------------------------------- Makaveli Vs Knuckles ------------------------------- ( One of the SBS staff comes to the booth and hands Bill a tray of pork ribs and coke.) TJ: How did you manage to get someone to bring you them ? BL: Well, it’s one of the perks of being SBS’s top commentator. TJ: What and not something to do with spilling food all over Lance Morrison last week. ( Bill grins innocently ) BL: Ahem, these guys sure do have strange names Makaveli is strange enough but what sort of name is Knuckles sounds like the name you give to some kinda kids toy. TJ: What does it matter what they are called, they both come highly recommended, Makaveli already has friends here in SBS including Deadlock and Crazedee. BL: ( Spurting out some of his drink ) Well with friends like those who needs enemy, Crazedee is hardly one of the top ranked wrestlers in SBS he’s yet to win a match. This is going to be boring. TJ: Well every match can’t be a Zager/Spawn match and who knows either of these two wrestlers could be a future World Champion. BL: Yeah, right well lets get on with it. ("Me Against Tha World" by Tupac Shakur comes blaring out over the PA ) MF : Announcing first from Westside, New York and weighing in at 265lbs, Makaveli !! ( Makaveli appears at the head of the aisle and there is a small mixed pop from the crowd ,he is wearing a black mask with Gold sparkles. Black and white Ninja uniform with yellow trim on both sides. Makaveli gets to the ring and starts to reach under the apron. ) BL: Well he seems very popular with the crowd and what’s he doing under the ring apron. TJ: Seems to be looking for something. ("Momma Said Knock You Out" L. L. Cool J comes blasting over the PA ) MF: And his opponent…. ( Knuckles comes tearing out from the locker room area and sprints down the aisle leaping straight onto Makaveli who is still looking under the ring. Big Crowd pop ) BL: Looks like chuckles can’t wait for the match to start. TJ: Knuckles is really laying into Makaveli hitting him with a series of blows to the ribs and head. The ref manages to separate the two men and he warns Knuckles. Makaveli now getting up and he’s got a chair, that must have been what he was looking for, Makaveli hits Knuckles on the head with the chair and he crashes into the guardrail. ( A guy dressed like a pimp comes down to ringside. ) BL: Who’s this? He looks like Goouch, no wait it’s not who the heck is he. TJ: I believe it’s Knuckles manager Willie Jackson. BL: Man I hope he’s got some girls as well. TJ: Makaveli drops the chair and rolls the stunned Knuckles into the ring. Makaveli climbs into the ring and the ref calls for the bell. Makaveli drops his leg across Knuckles a few times and then goes for the pin, 1….2….Kickout by Knuckles. Makaveli pulls Knuckles up before sending him crashing back down with a DDT. BL: Whoa, look at that Knuckles has just got straight to his feet that DDT has no effect, he seems to be grinning and he’s getting right in Makaveli’s face. TJ: He may be a bit shorter than Makaveli but he must nearly 50 pounds heavier. The two men lock up and Knuckles uses his strength advantage to drive Makaveli to the mat. Knuckles now with a chinlock on Makaveli he starts to pull back on his neck but Makaveli manages to grab hold of the ropes. Both men get to there feet and Makaveli tries to whip Knuckles into the ropes, Knuckles reverses and sends Makaveli for the ride, Makaveli ducks under a clothesline by Knuckles before flooring him with a flying clothesline of his own. BL: Man these pork ribs are fine, Makaveli is going for the pin 1…..2…..KICKOUT by Knuckles. TJ: I’m glad I don’t have to wash your clothes Bill you’ve spilled all that sauce down your front. Back to the action Knuckles whip’s Makaveli into the turnbuckle, he follows up with a splash but there’s no-one home. Makaveli is quick to take advantage and he starts to beat on Knuckles in the corner. Knuckles now fighting back. He hits Makaveli with a couple of big uppercuts which stagger the bigger man, he follows up with a superkick and Makaveli crumbles to the mat. Knuckles now going for the pin, 1….2….Shoulder up by Makaveli. BL: hmmm, mmmm, hooked the leg *burp*. TJ: Knuckles pulls Makaveli to his feet and tries to set him for a piledriver, Makaveli replies with a thumb to the eye and Knuckles staggers back, Makaveli follows this up with a standing cranium kick and Knuckles falls to the outside. Makaveli quickly follows up and drops an elbow to the back of Knuckles neck from the ring apron. Makaveli now picking up the chair again, he goes to hit Knuckles with it but he manages to roll out of the way. BL: *burp* this is what I like to see a good old street fight! TJ: Knuckles with a kick and he knocks the chair into Makaveli’s face and he staggers back. Knuckles is quick to take advantage and he whips Makaveli into the guardrail and then he drives his head into the ringpost. Knuckles rolls Makaveli back into the ring and pulls him to his feet, Knuckles now puts Makaveli’s head between his legs an executes a piledriver. He goes for the pin 1…2…Th, shoulder up at the last moment by Makaveli . BL: Tonight’s turning out better than I thought, those pork ribs were excellent! TJ: Knuckles hauls Makaveli up, Chokeslam! Makaveli goes crashing back down. Knuckles picks him up and places him on the turnbuckle, he’s going for the Knuckle Bomb! Knuckles set’s Makaveli like he is going for a superplex, he then lifts him into the powerbomb position and jumps back off the ropes, what a DEVESTATING Maneuver Makaveli is completely out of it. Knuckles lays his arm across Makaveli and the ref counts 1…2….3 he calls for the bell. BL: That wasn’t a bad match, I mean there not quite Spawn or Zager but not too bad none the less, all that action has made me hungry! TJ: Hungry! What about those ribs. Lets go to Mike for the official verdict. MF: The winner of this match in 9:25 by pinfall….. Knuckles! ( The ref lift’s up Knuckles arm and Willie Jackson hands him his robe, the two men then make there way up the aisle to a decent pop from the crowd. ) TJ: What a match up!!!
