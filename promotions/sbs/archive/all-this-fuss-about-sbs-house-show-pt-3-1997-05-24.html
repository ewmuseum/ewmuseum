BL: Let’s quit playing around and get to the match...I want to see Zager defend his title. Zach ZagerVs Eric Righteous ---------------------------------------------------------- Rockitt blares over the PA. The Arena goes dark and an impressive laser display delights the fans. On the Jumbotron above the arena highlights of Zager defeating Mercy and Righteous. The crowd goes wild as the Movin Man comes out styling and profiling with 2 ladies on each arm. The jumbotron goes blank and the scene changes to Zager’s hand dropping 3 times at the hands of Spawn’s scary dreams hold. Zager is upset and pushes his way to the ring and orders Falco out. ZZ: Is that all you got? You’re in trouble Righteous. Get your ass out here and quit screwing around...I’m gonna whip your ass....cuz the Movin Man says so!! (crowd cheers) Devil’s Haircut by Beck plays and Righteous walks from the back with Goouch, Mercy and Spawn. Howie Long walks out and the crowd cheers. BL: Where’s the rest of the AA? Is Zach going after it by himself? TJ: You never know with the Movin Man. It does look like the Untouchables have an unfair advantage. BL: The only member of the AA that is scheduled to wrestle tonight is Machete. I know the other guys are here. Where’s Scorpion? The Immortal? UJD? Zager better watch his back. Because we all know what the Untouchables can do. TJ: I’m sure the Movin Man has a trick or two for the Untouchables. BL: The bell rings and we are underway. How did Mercy and co. talk Howie Long into joining their gang? TJ: Everyone wants to be part of the SBS. Many top talents are jumping ship and signing up with us. I have heard a rumor that a minor organization’s Intercontinental champ has sent Mercy an open challenge. BL: Oh yeah, I heard about that guy. His name is Rayne and he has killed a few people in the ring. Just what this organization needs. TJ: Well from what I understand. The match is all but signed. They are just working out the legal details. BL: Zager and Righteous tie up in the middle of the ring. Zager breaks the hold and slaps Righteous across the face. Oh MY. Righteous is infuriated. He goes to slap the Movin Man but ZZ ducks out of they way and levels Righteous with a hard right cross. Zager goes for the figure four. But Righteous breaks it up. The men are fighting toe to toe in the middle of the ring. This isn’t a wrestling match. It’s a pier 6 brawl. TJ: Did you expect anything else? Righteous with the upper hand and he levels the Movin Man with a textbook suplex. Righteous definitely has the skills. I wish he had a better attitude.Righteous has the Movin Man up and brings him down hard with a belly to back suplex. Zager makes it to the corner. Righteous comes rushing in and meets ZZ’s right boot. The crowd is going wild!!! ZZ shakes and bakes and throws Righteous to the far corner and levels him with a handspring elbow. Righteous goes down and ZZ goes to the top. Zager looks posed and Righteous gets up wobbling. Here comes Zach!!!! He nails Righteous with a ddt from the top rope. The fans are really loving this!!! BL: Zager flashes the AA sign with his hands. And drops an elbow on the head of Righteous. Zach looks really good tonight. I guess he is showing the fans that he doesn’t need his boys to back him up. That Zach is the man!!! He can beat anyone at anytime. TJ: He does look like he has caught fire. After all the TV title matches are only ten minutes. And Zach doesn’t have to pin Eric to keep his belt. Zager just standing over Righteous and slapping him. I don’t think that is a smart move. Righteous fires back with a knee to the groin. He follows it up with a double armed DDT. Quick cover.....1,2....kickout by the Movin Man. Gut Buster by Righteous and another cover. 1, 2, kick out by the Movin Man. This is what Eric needs to do if he wants to win the title. A lot of power moves and quick covers. German suplex into a pinning predicament. 1,2, thr...kickout again by the Movin Man. Zach checks his watch and rolls out of the ring. BL: Be careful Zach there are a bunch of thugs out there. Mercy and Zager exchange words and Zach gives Spawn a not so nice hand gesture. Righteous breaks up the ref’s count. There seems to be some commotion from the back. Here they come......The crowd really loves this.....Scorpion, UJD, Machete and The Immortal come walking out to ringside. Zager climbs back in the ring. TJ: What’s this? Security comes out in force and suroounds the AA. They appear to be asking for ID...Now wait a minute! Since when do our stars need an ID badge? Look at the Untouchables. Thety are all wearing event security badges. What the hell is going on??? Security rushes the AA to the back. Zager is upset!! Righteous rolls Zager up into a small package......1,2,thr...Zager kicks out!! BL: That was really close!! Zager had better watch himself in there. Zager sent into the ropes. Flying cross body by Righteous. The ref is in position......1,2, kickout by Zager. How much can Zach take? Righteous pulls Zager up and sends him down with a gut buster. Righteous positions himself on the ropes and comes down with an elbow drop...but Zager rolls out of the way!!! He isn’t through yet folks. TJ: Zager to his feet first and picks up Righteous and delivers another DDT!! Roll up by Zager...1,2 kickout! Righteous still has some reserve left. Zager whips Righteous into the far corner and follows him with a splash! Zager starting to hit Righteous with a series of chops to the chest. Headbutt by Righteous sends Zager back. I think that hurt Righteous as much as it did Zager! Righteous drops a knee on the head of Zager and then levels him with the gut buster!!! OH NO!!! Righteous sets up Zager for the Penance. If he hits this it will be lights out for the Movin Man!!! BL: C’mon Zach!!! snap out of it!!! Righteous has ZZ in position....here it comes!!!!!! (ding, ding) The bell rings but Righteous still plants Zager with the Penance. Righteous didn’t hear the bell and starts yelling for the ref to count. Righteous is livid!!! He throws the ref out of the ring. Mercy and Spawn enter the ring. But wait what’s this??? Is that a fan pulling Zager from the ring? TJ: I don’t know but he sure is big!! I bet Zach is glad for the help whoever it is. BL: Here comes the jobber squad security. The Untouchables are taken to the back. Wow that was a wild one!!! Let’s go to Falco for the official word..... MF: Ladies and gentlemen this bout has ended in a time limit draw......Zach Zager is still the SBS TV title champion!!! Crowd goes wild as Rokit blares over the PA. BL: I’ll hand it to the Untouchables. They really tried to psyche out Zach tonight. TJ: It takes more than head games to defeat the Movin Man!!!! (Commercial Break) BL voice talks while scenes of SBS madness take place..... Do you like it extreme???? Do you want to see who is the most extreme of all??? Coming soon....The one and only Extreme championship where anything goes!!! More details when they come available.... (fade) --------------------------------------------------- Rene Dubois vs Machete Daigle --------------------------------------------------- TJ: Well, I don't expect this to be a classic textbook match. I've got a feeling the rulebook may go right out the window on this one. BL: I think even you can get one right now and then. I think Daigle's finally gonna show the world that Dubois is nothing but a cheese champion. Payback is gonna be real sweet for the Machete now. TJ: What are you babbling about, Lane? If anyone needs to give a little payback it's the champ. Look at the record... Daigle turned his back on Dubois, and hit him with a bat, then stole the belt. Why if anything, Rene's been more than patient with.... BL: Yeah, yeah. We'll see. (looks down to the aisle) Guess we won't have to wait long, Ted. Here comes Daigle now, and does he look confident. He's taking even longer than usual, posing for the fans. TJ: Too confident, I think. The fans sure aren't very impressed. (the sound of boos can be heard even in the broadcast booth) (Daigle continues to strut slowly to the ring) BL: Wonder where the Flower is. Hah! I bet he chickens out. TJ: Don't be too sure, Lane. Rene Dubois is.... OH MY GOD! HE'S HERE! (the cameras shift suddenly to the aisle, where Rene Dubois has attacked Daigle from behind before he can get to the ring. Dubois rams Daigle's head into the guard rail) Daigle's cut already. I've never seen Dubois this fierce. He's like a man possessed. BL: What's happening here? (stunned) The ref's standing and staring at all this like he doesn't believe it either. (Dubois grabs Daigle by the back of the neck and starts marching him to the ring. Instead of taking him into the ring, however, he runs his head into the ring steps.) TJ: Daigle's really cut open now. There's blood everywhere. Why doesn't the ref try to stop this? This is sickening! BL: (shaking his head) I don't know, Ted. Maybe he doesn't want to get in Dubois' way. I sure wouldn't. TJ: Now he's leading Daigle up the steps, holding him with a handful of hair. OH NO! He piledrove Daigle from the top step to the arena floor. Daigle is completely out now. He's not even twitching. BL: Can't anybody stop this? This is gruesome. I've never seen so much blood. Come on, ref. Call it off. TJ: The ref finally tries to stop Dubois, but he's pushed back. He tries again, and Rene knocks him down with a haymaker. Oh, this is madness. Now Dubois is taking off the championship belt. He seems to be saying something to Daigle as he holds it in front of his face. No. I don't believe it. I can't look any more. (he turns away) BL: Sissy. What Ted didn't want to say is that Dubois is now whipping Daigle with the belt. Welts are forming on Daigle's back and somebody'd better stop this while Daigle is still alive. The ref's back on his feet and he's calling for the bell to summon the jobber squad. (the jobber squad runs to ringside, but when they see what's happening they don't want to enter the ring) Wait. Here comes Maverick. If anybody can stop the carnage it's the Flower's best friend. (Maverick enters the ring and Lady Duval is close behind him. Maverick grabs Rene's arm as he is raising the belt for another blow. Dubois turns and cocks his fist before he sees who it is. Suddenly, he smiles and drops his fist. Maverick talks to him, quietly and Rene finally calms down and starts to follow Maverick from the ring. As Maverick jumps down from the ring apron, Dubois holds the belt above his head as if he's going to jump down on Maverick from behind, but Lady Duval grabs his arm. Rene looks at her, then jumps down to the floor and leaves the arena. The crowd is too stunned to cheer or boo. They stare in shock at the still form of Daigle in the ring) TJ: I don't know what to say. This is the most vicious display of brutality I've ever witnessed in all my years of announcing (sighs and shakes his head). I guess someone should try to get a word with Dubois. BL: Don't look at me. I'm not going anywhere near that madman. Let Mahone do it. TJ: Spoken like a true champion, Bill (he sneers). Over to you, Tex. (the scene shifts to Tex Mahone, standing outside Dubois' dressing room) TM: Okay, Ted and Bill, I'll see if I can get a word with the champ. (he knocks on the door but there's no answer. Suddenly, there's a noise like something crashing into a wall. Several screams and thumps are heard from the dressing room, then silence) Oh my. Well, here goes. (he opens the door. Inside, there is nothing but wreckage. The room is otherwise empty. The camera pans over the mess before fading back to the announcer's booth) TJ: Well there you have it. Whatever's eating Rene Dubois is still a mystery. BL: While you were speaking, Ted, the referee's decision was announced - as if he needed to. He's disqualified Rene Dubois and awarded the match to Machete, but I guess someone's going to have to tell him about it when he comes to. I sure hope President Marshall was watching tonight. If I was him I'd strip Dubois of the title for this little escapade. TJ: I'm afraid that I have to agree with you on that one, Bill. He should at least be fined heavily. Well, fans, that's all we've got time for tonight. Join us Monday for another great card. This is Ted Johnson for Bill Lane and Tex Mahone signing off. (the camera fades as Ted and Bill wearily take off their headphones and stand to leave)
