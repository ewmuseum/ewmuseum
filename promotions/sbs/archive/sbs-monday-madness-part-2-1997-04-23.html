--------------------------------------------- Patrick Daigle VS Waylon Mercy ------------------------------------------ MF: Ladies and gentleman........in this heavyweight elimination match....... From Quebec City, weighing 245 pounds...MACHETE....Patrick Daigle..... (Crowd booos as Daigle walks to the ring wearing blue tights with white stripes. Cuts Like a Knife plays over the PA.) Mercy’s voice plays over the PA......One of these days, lives are gonna be in Waylon Mercy’s hands...You know what I mean. A depressing melody plays over the sound system...the crowd begins to cheer.... MF: From deep in the bayous of Louisiana weighing 320 pounds.....Waylon Mercy!!!! (crowd pop as Mercy walks to the ring wearing a white t shirt white pants and white boots. The Mask accompanies him to ringside) BL: I am sure glad Goouch gave me a beer otherwise I wouldn’t be able to handle this match up. TJ: This is gonna be a good ‘un. Mercy is really upset over his poor showing against Scorpion Friday night and also about Daigle laying down for Zager. Let’s cut to some of his earlier comments:- ( Waylon Mercy stands alone at the end of a pier ) Hello everybody, Waylon Mercy here. I've received a lot of criticism for my performance on Friday night. I deserve it, my head wasn't in the match. I was too concerned with that goody two shoes Eric Righteous, and lost focus. I'll be the first to admit that I got my ass kicked from pillar to post, but I'll also be the first to tell you that that wasn't the last time you'll see Waylon Mercy fighting Scorpion. While I'm on the topic of Scorpion, I also question the presidents thinking when he decided that Scorpion earned a title shot. You spineless sack of sh*t! Your a stupid pathetic chump. If you think that I'm going to let Scorpion wear the title that I won at the battle royal out of the pay per view while I'm still drwing breath, your even stupider than Morrison looks. While on the topic of Morrison, I think he better shut his yap. You whine that there is no competition. Well, you never stepped into the ring with me, even after cheating me out of the belt, and , as much as I hate to admit it, I seem to remember a man by the name of Eric Righteous pinning your ass. So, Morrison, don't bore me with any more complaints, as far as I saw you weren't much of a champion anyways so suck it up, and get out of my face. Speaking of brain dead *ss holes, I see that I'm gonna be stepping into the ring with Patrick Daigle tonight. Sir, I might have helped pull your ass out of the fire last Monday, but I have quickly grown to dislike you. You're a pretender, you stabbed Dubois, and myself in the back during the three man tag, and then you tried to join the AA, only to be rejected. I can't blame them. I'll never trust you to take my back ever again either. It looks to me like you need to take some time off to evaluate your situation. Lucky for you that you have to face me. I'll fix it so that you have to take a real long vacation...You know what I mean? (Quietly chuckles as he turns and walks away) BL: That was probably the smartest thing he could have done. That way he only took a partial beating. TJ: Sometimes I wonder about you.....Bell rings and we are underway. Mercy goes to work right away. Low blow by Mercy sends Daigle to his knees. Clubbing rights and lefts to the back of Daigle and Mercy tops it off with a boot to the face. I haven’t seen Mercy this worked up since the TV title tourney. BL: He probably has a crawfish in his shorts or something. TJ: Mercy throws Daigle to the mat with a body slam and clamps on the leg scissors to wear Daigle down. Sometimes He can get a submission from this hold. Daigle works free from the hold after 2 minutes. Both men to their feet. Flying forearm sends Mercy reeling and Daigle follows it with a dropkick that sends Mercy to the mat. Daigle climbs the ropes and executes a moonsault....BUT MERCY COUNTERS by putting his knees up. Thumb to the eyes by Mercy and a blatant kick to the groin. That’s gotta hurt!!!Inverted atomic drop by Mercy has Daigle bent over in pain. Mercy Power bombs Daigle....What has gotten into Waylon tonight? BL: (burping) who cares...we all know that Mercy has ducked every top contender and only fights these mid card guys anyway. Look what happened to him against Scorpion. He got his ass handed to him. TJ: Mercy sends Daigle down with a brain buster.... he climbs the ropes and hits him with the titanium splash...this match is over.....take it away Mike..... MF: The winner in 6:38.....WAYLON MERCY ------------------------------------- Hellfire Vs Eric Righteous ------------------------------------- MF: This match is scheduled for one fall and is tonight’s main event, announcing first all the way from Calgary, Canada and weighing in at 244lbs….He is also an icon to his fans….Hellfire. { Invisible touch by Genesis blasts over the PA. There is a good face pop as Hellfire appears at the head of an aisle and makes his way to the ring. Hellfire slaps hands with lots of the crowd and hands out a couple of Hellfire T-Shirts to some of the younger fans as he makes his way down to ringside. A couple of Icon chants ripple through the crowd as he jumps in the ring and poses on the turnbuckles } BL: How can this match be tonight’s main event, these two are no more than mid-carders at best what did they do bribe President Marshall. TJ: They are a couple of young ambitious wrestlers, Hellfire has been having a lot more success of late after a somewhat shaky start to his career here in SBS. And Righteous has proved on many an occasion that he is a tough man to beat. BL: Now seems to be as good time as any to run some of Hellfire’s boring comments. I’m off to the concession stand *burp* { Hellfire is in a whirlpool at a health club. He still has his face paint on } Hellfire: WOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! Hellfire over the Barbados Brawler and soon to be Eric Righteous. Those punks couldn't hold a candle to this flame, baby! Woo! Anyway, thanks to the fans for cheering me on past the Brawler. I'll do anything for my amgios, my clique, my FANS! I will do anything for them! at that includes trash my opposition! FLAME ON!!! Now seriously, after I trash some more losers, I wanna be first in line for a title shot, and my fans want it! Oh, do they ever want it! How can an icon be complete without a title belt! You can't! Therefore I'm gonna win a title fair and square! Let's face facts, the fed has only gotten better cause of my antics! So let's give credit where credit is due and give me my title shot! WOOOOOOOOOO! All my fans, I thank you and I won't let you down! Noooooo Sir! Let's get a move on, because Hellfire can't be stopped! { Fade } { Devil’s Haircut by Beck blares over the P.A. and Eric Righteous appears at the top of the aisle to a big face pop } MF: And his opponent from the Streets of Chicago weighing in at 245lbs…. Eric Righteous!!! { Righteous slaps hands with a few of the fans as he purposefully makes his way down to the ring } TJ: Righteous looks to be in a determined mood tonight, at least we should get to see a good clean fight tonight. Let’s see what Righteous had to say earlier. { Righteous addresses the camera in SBS studios } I have a number of things to say, so I beg all of my loyal fans to hear me out. I know I've apologized to my fans for actions I've taken in the past. (role a clip of Righteous piledriving the referee after the Morrison match) But what happened on Friday was inexcusable. (Role clip of Righteous repeatedly hitting Scorpion, while he lay defenceless on the apron) I have come to realize the error of my ways, I've spent so much time trying to root the evil out of this league that I lost myself in the madness. When I step into the ring again your gonna see the side of Eric Righteous that made the kids proud to wear Eric Righteous t-shirts, and to carry my posters around with them. I've taken every cent of money that I've made from this promotion and put into helping inner city youths around this great country of ours, and I've doubled the amount of time I spend in volunteer placements at home. I've done all of this in order to force myself to focus on what is important. Its not titles, its not money, its about giving the kids someone to look up to, a good role model. In the next week or so everybody is gonna realize what Eric Righteous is all about, some might be surprised, but in the end everybody will understand. My biggest problem at the moment however is the fact that the brainless president has decided to give Scorpion a title shot before any of the other, more deserving wrestlers in the league. I don't know what the hell was going through your head when you made that decision, but I'm sure its sold on the streets, and I think they call it 'rock'. What kind of an example are you trying to set? Anybody who turns the lights off, clobbers somebody with a bat, before running back to a comfortable hiding spot in the locker room gets a title shot? I just don't get it. I can't worry about that right now either though. Hellfire, tonight we get it on. I know that in facing you I am assured a fair match. I respect your ability, but I fear you in no way. I'm afraid that I am going to have to use you to make a statement. I'm back, I'm focused, and I'm really pissed off. (Turns his back to the camera, and stalks away) TJ: Righteous seems to be really angry by the recent turn of events. Anyway both men in the ring and the ref calls for the bell. Hellfire holds out his hand and Righteous shakes it, nice gesture. The two men size each other up and they finally lock up. Neither man seems to be able to get much of an advantage. Righteous now with a headlock, Hellfire pushes him off and sends him for the ride, nice armdrag takedown by Hellfire. Righteous kips to his feet runs over, another armdrag takedown by Hellfire. Righteous nods and gets to his feet. Righteous to the ropes, Hellfire tries another armdrag take down but Righteous reverses and Hellfire hits the mat. Hellfire to his feet the two men lock up , Righteous with a low blow, he holds up his hands and backs away as the ref admonishes him. Righteous now with a textbook suplex and Hellfire hits the mat hard. Righteous goes for the pin 1 - 2 - KICKOUT by Hellfire. Both men get to there feet, Hellfire with a boot to the midsection and he follows up with a well executed neckbreaker which knock all the wind out of Righteous. Hellfire poses for the crowd and signals that he is going up top. Hellfire climbs the turnbuckle, elbow drop but no-ones home and Hellfire comes crashing down. Righteous gets Hellfire by the legs and slaps on a figure four and Hellfire screams out in pain. Hellfire won’t give up and he manages to grab the ropes, Righteous reluctantly breaks the hold. Both men to there feet and Righteous whips Hellfire into the turnbuckle and follows up with a splash, Hellfire moves out of the way at the last moment and Eric’s shoulder hit’s the ringpost hard. Hellfire picks up Righteous and executes a shoulderbreaker. BL: Now that had to hurt Mr Goody Goody, especially after his shoulder had just smacked into the ringpost like that. Come on Hellfire put him away. 1 - 2 -kickout by Righteous. *munch crunch* TJ: Glad to see you’ve decided to grace us with your presence again. Hellfire picks up Righteous and executes a backbreaker once again goes for the pin 1 - 2 - kickout by Righteous. Hellfire pulls Righteous to his feet again and sets him for a DDT but Righteous once again catches him with a low blow. He must have been taking lesson’s off the AA. BL: Ha! The AA has the creme of the wrestling talent in there ranks they don’t need to keep hitting low blows to win matches. TJ: Righteous now with a German suplex into a bridge 1 - 2 - kickout by Hellfire. Righteous to his feet, he rubs his shoulder. Hellfire starts to get up BIG clothesline from Righteous. Righteous pulls up Hellfire and slams him back down hard. Righteous once again goes for the pin 1 -2 - shoulder up. Righteous now pulling Hellfire to his feet, sends him for the ride, Hellfire ducks under a clothesline before flooring Righteous with a flying forearm. Hellfire quickly drops a leg across Righteous and signals that he’s once again going up top. BL: C’mon Hellfire see if you can make this one count. Hellfire now with a moonsault on to Righteous and he hits it. He’s going for the pin 1 - 2 - shoulder up. You should have hooked the leg Hellfire. TJ: Righteous’s now legendary endurance seem him get out of that one. Hellfire pulls up Righteous and sets him for the Hellfire Slam. He lifts Righteous up but he wriggles out of it at the last moment and he pushes Hellfire face first into the turnbuckle. Hellfire staggers back out and Righteous hits a belly-to-back suplex. Righteous pulls Hellfire up double-armed DDT! BL: Why isn’t he going for the pin. Righteous now puts Hellfire on the turnbuckle. He climbs the ropes himself, he’s going for the Penance!. He lifts Hellfire up and they come crashing back down, he rolls him up for the pin 1 - 2 - 3! The ref calls for the bell. MF: The winner of this bout by pinfall is Eric Righteous! { The ref lifts up Eric arm, big crowd pop. Eric goes over and checks that Hellfire isn’t too badly hurt before signalling for a mic. } ER: Scorpion, I’m talking to you coward, if you have any guts you’ll come out here and face me now. Just as I thought your too chicken you saw what I did to Hellfire well that’s nothing compared to what I’m going to do to you!. { Righteous throws down the mic and makes his way back up the aisle to the cheers of the crowd. } TJ: Righteous really seem to have a score to settle with Scorpion and he was willing to have another fight after that tiring match with Hellfire. He really is a wrestler the fans can look up to. BL: Naaa, he was only running his mouth off because he knew that Scorpion wasn’t in the building, I think he’s still recovering from that beating Righteous gave him with that baseball bat on Friday night. TJ: Hmm, Anyway that’s all we have time for this week, but don’t forget to join us for our first PPV Brave New World on Sunday 27th April. Contact your local cable operator for further details. Until next time…. { Fade }
