----------------------------------------------------------------- 4 - Corners Match X-boys Vs Medalists Vs Time Travellers Vs Dragons Writer: BF ----------------------------------------------------------------- TJ: Fans this next match could be a PPV main event match up but it is brought to you live on Monday Night Madness. This match will decide who fights Mercy and Righteous for the tag titles. BL: I think the whole thing is lousy. I mean Vengeance and Retribution won the titles last week. Now Marshall comes in and screws them out of it. I think the whole thing is a waste of time! TJ: Marshall thought that VP Deacon was showing favoritism towards Mercy and Righteous and stripped them of the titles. Remember the members of the Untouchables bribing security? Whose to say they didn’t bribe Deacon? BL: Well we will never know. Deacon has asked for air time but Marshall refuses to give it to him. I heard that the legal pressure has been turned up a notch and Marshall is going to be forced to let Deacon back in. TJ: Well who told you that? All the legal stuff is supposed to be confidential. You’re probably just making it up. BL: See even you don’t believe me! TJ: Let’s turn it over to Falco for the introductions. MF: Ladies and gentlemen. From Downtown NYC weighing a total combined weight of 546 lbs. The X-Boyz (loud Boos as Mace-X and Puff-X walk to the ring wearing their usual stuff. Can’t Nobody Hold Me Down by Puff Daddy plays.) MF: From China weighing a total of 490 lbs. Dragons! (No response as Yin and Yang walk out wearing black and white ninja outfits. Traditional Chinese music plays.) MF: Team 3 weighing 520 lbs. The Medalists (loud face pop as Mr. Bad Boy and Eric Froelich walk out wearing glittering vests and matching Olympic theme wrestling trunks. They come out with Lady Duval and ex champ Rene DuBois. John Maverick can be seen lurking in the hallway. An Olympic fanfare plays over the PA.) MF: Our final team from England weighing in at a total combined weight of 502 lbs. The Time Travelers. John and Justin Moody. (Gemini Dream plays over the PA as the TT walks out with their manager Ray Pinder. Loud face pop as the trio makes it to the ring.) TJ: So who do you like in this one? BL: No one. I think this is a good time to cash in some of those coupons that Goouch gave me. TJ: That explains it! The Untouchables are bribing you too. BL: Shutup! They are not! Look Anderson is flipping a coin to see who starts first. TJ: It looks like Yin and Puff-X are going to start this. BL: so let me clarify the rules a bit. Any man can tag in any man. That means the X-Boyz can tag in the Time Travelers or any combination. You don’t have to tag in your partner. You can tag in whomever you please. TJ: It would be stupid to tag in someone else. That would take your team out of contention. The team that wins this match will be the team that scores a pin fall. And you can’t get a pin from the ring apron. BL: Did I ask you to help me explain? TJ: Well everything is set. Yin ties up with Puff-X. Snap mare takeover by Yin. Puff to his feet only to hit the mat with an arm drag take over by Yin. Yin places him in a arm bar trying to wear down his arm. The faster Yin is really taking it to Puff-X. Puff reaches the ropes and Yin breaks the hold by doing a somersault. BL: He should save that stuff for the circus. Wrestle in the ring and do that daredevil stuff at home like you are supposed to. TJ: Puff tags in Mace and Yin stays in the ring. Jump spin back kick sends Yin to his back. Mace follows it up with a splash and goes for the cover. Bad Boy comes in and kicks Mace in the head and stops the ref’s count. The crowd goes wild as Mace and Bad Boy stare at each other. These two don’t really like each other and they don’t care who knows it. Yin struggles to his feet as Bad Boy steps out of the ring. Mace points to Yin and then points to the Medalists’ corner. Yin looks confused. The crowd is screaming and Yin tags in Eric Froelich Jr. of the Medalists. BL: Froelich hits Mace with a drop kick . Mace staggers back and Froelich nails him with a flying headbutt. I think that hurt Froelich more than it hurt Mace. Both wrestlers are down. Mace makes it to his feet first and nails Froelich with an elbow smash. He picks up Froelich and sends him for the ride. Froelich ducks and comes off the ropes with a clothesline. Mace ducks and bounces off the ropes. Froelich drops to the mat and Mace steps over him and comes off the ropes again. Drop toe hold by Froelich makes Mace hit face first. Arm bar by Froelich. I guess he is going to try to wear Mace down. TJ: Well with all this fast paced action between the two high fliers. I guess it is a good time to rest. BL: Speaking of resting, the Time Travelers have yet to enter the ring. I guess they are waiting until the other 3 teams wear each other down. TJ: Actually, Bill, no one has tagged them in yet. BL: (mocking) TJ: Froelich drags Mace over to his corner and tags in Mr. Bad Boy! The crowd is on their feet! BB comes off the ropes with a double ax handle. Here comes Puff-X! Froelich drops him with a drop kick. The ring begins to fill up as all 8 men come in the ring. Anderson has his hands full. Here comes Maravich from the back to help sort this out. BL: Now this is worth the price of admission. This looks more like a battle royal than a four corners match. There is too much action to describe. TJ: Well it was bound to happen. I mean some of these teams just don’t like each other. The Time Travelers are out of the ring as well as the Dragons. Maravich and Anderson have separated the Medalists and X-Boyz. Bad Boy and Mace resume their battle. European uppercuts by Bad Boy send Mace into the Time Travelers corner. He inadvertently runs into Justin. That’s all it takes to make a tag. Anderson signals that a tag has been made. Mace doesn’t know it, he complains to Anderson who is ordering him out of the ring.. Bad Boy grabs Mace and flings him in the Dragons corner. Yang slaps Bad Boy’s arm. Anderson signals for the tag. Wow a martial arts kick by Yang send Bad Boy and Mace out of the ring. Froelich and Puff-x come to the floor also and a battle ensues on the floor. We need to restore order here. BL: That’s dangerous. Don’t forget DuBois is out there and you know how much he likes to stick his fat nose in other people’s business. Here comes that idiot cowpoke, Mavarick. Where’s security when you need it? TJ: I think it is going to take more than a couple of people to break up this fight outside of the ring. The Medalists and X- Boyz are fighting each other back towards the dressing area. I wonder how you call this? BL: Well, the two legal men are in the ring and watch out (Justin nails Yang with a spine buster). He makes the signal to the crowd. The fans are on their feet. Justin tags in John and sets up Yang for the Razor’s edge. As Yang hits the ground John comes off the top rope with a plancha. Yin runs into the ring but Justin nails him with a clothesline. Anderson counts..1,2,3...Time Travelers win! Let’s go to Falco for the results.... (As Falco steps into the ring, the voice of Eric Righteous can be heard. All eyes shift to the Jumbotron) (Mercy and Righteous face the camera. They are relaxing in their private apartments) ER: Well, well, well. I guess the sun shines on every dogs ass once in a while. (Crowd boos) Shutup you idiots, I'm talkin' here! Now, as I was saying, Time Travellers you better get your asses back to the future, 'cause I just know you ain't gonna like what happens next week. WM: Congratulations Time Travellers, and I mean that sincerely (laughs). You've just stuck your heads into the Guillotine, and we're gonna be your executioners. Next Monday your not gonna be facing three jobber quality tag teams, your gonna be facing man's two greatest vices...Vengeance and Retribution, and we;re gonna beat the hell out of you. (Face goes very serious) After we're through with you, D-Day is gonna look like a summer vacation...You know what I mean. ER: Mr. Mercy here is right. Sure you can beat X-Puffy and X-Snowy, the Dragons, who made this match happen by running away from us in the first place, not to mention these Special Olympians, Bad Boy and The Kraught, but can you hold a candle to Vengeance and Retribution? No you can't. So, enjoy your moment in the spotlight, but move on down the line, cause the SBS ain't big enough for two good tag-teams. I gues that means your shit out of luck...Doesn't it? I'm Eric Righteous, and don't you ever forget it! ----------------------------------------------------- World Title Match ‘Uptown’ Johnny Durante Vs Scorpion Writer: NM ---------------------------------------------------- BL: ( quietly ) Come to dada my little beauty. TJ: What? Did you just say something Bill. BL: Er, I said I just found that burger that I lost during the Immortal, Mercy match-up it was hidden under a load of your papers. ( Bill wipes a bit of fluff off the burger.) TJ: Sorry Bill, your not going to eat it now are you ? BL: Waste not, want not mmmmmmmm TJ: Yuck, This should match should be an exciting encounter as two members of the AA Scorpion and ‘Uptown’ Johnny Durante face off for the world championship belt. BL: The AA can’t lose can they, whatever the outcome of this one they will still hold the world championship belt, it really does make you wonder about President Marshall doesn’t it. TJ: Not really, these two have a score to settle although I wouldn’t have thought that UJD ranking would have entitled him to a shot at the title. ( Uptown Girl by Bill Joel comes over the PA. ) MF: This match is for the SBS world Title. Announcing first hailing from Chicago, Illinois and weighing in at 263 lbs he is accompanied to the ring by Cheryl Guze, Uptown Johnny Durante!!! ( There is a fairly large heel pop as Uptown Johnny Durante appears at the head of the aisle , Cheryl guze is on his arm in her hand she holds the cattle prod. Durante confidently makes his way down to ring, pausing to jaw with a couple of little Cajuns and some Untouchables fans. ) BL: Durante has been pretty quiet of late both in and out of the ring, this could be his chance to grab some of the limelight. TJ: Yes but it would be at the expense of one of his AA colleagues. (Voodoo Child Jimi Hendrix blasts out over the PA. ) MF: And his opponent weigh 249 lbs from parts unknown and accompanied to the ring by Carrie, he is the current SBS World Champion, SCORPION!!! ( There is a big crowd pop as Scorpion makes his way down the aisle, he is dressed as normal but is not carrying the baseball bat. Carrie walks along behind waving away the hands of the eager fans. Scorpion gets in the ring and holds up the belt and receives another big pop from the fans. ) TJ: Anderson in charge again for this important match. He calls for the bell and this one is underway the two men nod at each other and lock up. Scorpion with the early advantage and he slaps on an armbar driving Durante to the mat. Durante powering his way out and he reverses the hold, Durante with a series of rights to Scorpions head and he drives him back to the corner. BL: Durante really showing that he means business, guess that incident during the tag matches really ticked him off. Durante is really going to town on Scorpion hitting him with a sucession of blows. TJ: Scorpion fighting back, he kicks Durante in the gut and he staggers back. Scorpion kick to take advantage and he send Durante to the mat. Scorpion quickly climbs the ropes and delivers a flying elbow smash. Scorpion going for the pin 1…..2…..Kickout by Durante. Cheryl shouting encouragement to Durante from the outside. BL: Scorpion gets up as Durante slowly staggers to his feet rubbing his neck. Scorpion with another kick to the gut and he takes Durante to the mat with a devastating piledriver. Scorpion going for the pin 1……2…..Scorpion pulls him up !! TJ: What’s he doing? Scorpion backs off as Durante gets to his feet. The two men move to lockup and Durante hits him with a forearm to the head, he follows up with a couple more and Scorpion falls to the mat. Durante really forcing his way back into this one and he drops a leg across Scorpions throat. Durante signals that he’s going up top and he climbs the ropes, Durante with a big Splash from the top rope! Scorpion lifts his knees and Durante hits them hard and rolls on the mat in obvious pain! BL: Big mistake by Durante, Scorp kips to his feet and he hits him with a couple of leg drops before he hauls Durante to his feet. Scorpion sending Durante for the ride, he comes back off the ropes and Scorpion floors him with a flying forearm. Scorpion signalling for the Paralyzer, he sets Durante on the turnbuckle and starts to climb the ropes. TJ: Cheryl Guze has climbed onto the ring apron Scorpion gets into position and OH MY GAWD! Cheryl has just hit Scorpion with the cattle prod and he and Durante fall to the mat. Scorpion is out cold he’s just lying on the mat twitching. BL: Anderson is calling for the bell, Durante staggers to his feet wondering quite what happened the he see’s Cheryl holding the cattle prod he don’t look two happy. He snatches the prod from her and throws it to the ground before grabbing her arm and leading here to the locker room area. TJ: What a bizarre ending to this match, Scorpion is still out cold, here comes the SBS Doctor there are a couple of paramedics as well this doesn’t look to good. Falcos in the ring now. MF: The winner of this match by disqualification, and still SBS World Champion Scorpion!!!! ( Somewhat muted pop from the crowd, they seem to be rather concerned about the condition of Scorpion who still lies on the mat. ) BL: Wait look Scorpions holding up his hand. ( Big pop from the crowd as Scorpion get’s to his feet. ) TJ: Here comes Zach Zager. ( Scorpion falls against the ropes and Carrie grabs hold of him to stop him falling. Zager and Carrie help Scorpion out of the ring and take him to the locker room area, there is a big pop from the fans. ) TJ: Well what a night it’s been. BL: Yeah you can say that again, I’ve had presents from Goouch, I’ve been abused by ugly female fans and to top it all off you go and hide my burger! TJ: I was talking about the in the ring action, Crazedee gets his first win, we now know that the Time Travellers will be facing Vengeance and Retribution for the Tag Belts and Scorpion retains the World Championship. BL: Yeah and you hide my burger. TJ: Hold on a minute Bill Lane you still owe me for all that food I had to pay for last week. ( Bill looks at his watch ) BL: Is that the time, I gotta go.. ( Bill hurriedly takes off his headset and leaves the booth. ) TJ: Bill Lane I’m gonna get you for this……. ( Fade )
