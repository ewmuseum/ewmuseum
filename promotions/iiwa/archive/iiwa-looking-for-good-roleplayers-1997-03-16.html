******************************************* IIWA ********************************************* The IIWA is a brand new fed made up of original wrestlers only. We have two cards a week, with a PPV once a month. A newsletter containing wrestlers' interviews is sent out daily. The homepage is located at http://www.angelfire.com/IIWA/index.html You can mail me at [detail withheld] for more information. Remember, to do well in my league, as well as any other, you must ROLEPLAY!! And in the IIWA, you must have a creative mind and roleplay well. This league is based on original wrestlers only, so there are NO RIPOFFS. Thank you for your time, President Jim International Internet Wrestling Association
