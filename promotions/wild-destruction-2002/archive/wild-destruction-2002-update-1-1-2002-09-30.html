PRESIDENTS NOTES =================== Quite an incredible 10 days. 10 days ago I decided to make a return to fedheading. If you've read
rspwf.com recently, you'll have seen why. 10 days later, and we're here, time for the first update already. I never thought I'd be able to have for the first card a roster of such incredible ability, top to bottom. It's almost like a dream roster for me in many ways. You've all shown a lot of faith in me, and I intend to return that faith, with interest.
I have no doubt that WILD is going to be a source of much fun and good eWrestling memories for everyone involved. The excitement, anticipation and interest in WILD has been magnificent, and even the response for match-writers has been marvellous, a rare-thing. Everything is in place for a great eWrestling experience to begin here.
Naturally, with so many great players on board, things'll be very competitive. That's why you're here though, to try to compete with the best. I know most of you, I know most of you are cool, so there isn't too much to worry about in terms of egos that can sometimes crop up in eWrestling. Politics, too, is something that can crop up. I'll tolerate neither. However, I want any of you, who has any problem with WILD or the people in it, to talk to me immediately.
That all said, let the games begin. With so many characters and handlers that I love working with and reading, this'll be top notch stuff, I know.
Some of you are already working on angle ideas. If you want an angle or a storyline, let me know and something will be sorted out for you.
A few other points I might as well make now I've begun. The first card is 9 matches long, with the roster of 18 split into 3 groups of 6. The groups you are in for the title tournament is not the groups will be staying in. It is just a starting point. The Rising Star section includes the rookie characters (even though most are very experienced handlers) while the Wild World title includes the more recognisable names from eWrestling on the roster - and top quality players at that.
If you want something to happen on a card, you must let me know at [detail withheld] Although I've got about 5 match writers already, with 9 matches on this card, I could always appreciate more help! If you'd like to show how kind you are with a bit of match writing, let me know.
Some of you are used to 'open-flash' feds for developing stories etc. I ask that you consider using segments on Fusion to carry stories across as an alternative.
Website. www.wildring.com Not much to look at currently, but plans are underway for a really great website in time for Cycle 2.
Some people worry that, as with a lot of feds, it'll be the same old main eventers going at each other time and time again. You'll be glad to hear that I intend to keep everything fresh.
And finally, I know it's going to be a lot of work to make this work, but trust me, it's going to work. And work well.
Okay, that wasn't much more to add [GRIN] I'll stop there and cover more during this Update at the relevant points. ======================= 2. NEWS & RUMOURS ======================= - The wrestling magazines and internet sites are all on the edge of their seats waiting to hear what they can about WILD. The mixture of young hot talent and experienced superstars with a big budget behind them is something everyone is looking forward to
- At the time of press, 18 have officially been signed on to the roster, with 2 more to come. The roster limit is suspected to be 24. - The level of cruiserweight talent is phenomenal and a cruiserweight title may be added if the current cruiserweight's live up to their potential - 'Juggernaut' William Bennett & Travis Lagrange - in the same fed!!! Word is that a lot of money was spent getting these two to sign up to WILD - but these arch enemies would gladly destroy each other for free.
- Sid Starr, the cruiserweight's cruiserweight, the cool cat, now in the peak of his career, but some are suggesting he's not up to the level of competition now facing him.
- Spikyjim & Travis Lagrange, the Suicide Kings, would like to keep their partnership the dominant one in WILD, but word is that someone seems keen to eclipse them. - Eric Scott has been receiving rave reviews - which is why WILD signed him. A lot is expected of this youngster. - Neil McDougall is said to be aiming to fly again - all the way to the WILD World championship - Kyohei Nakamura and Neige Thirteen are said to be two of the hottest high-flyers in the business. - Marcus Christian's good looks are already pulling in the female fans - shame he's such a jerk! - Not much has been heard about Jerome Marko, but word is he is truly main event material - Rick Hanson may be the Moosejaw Madman, but there's a Moosejaw Monster on the loose too.an un-named monster from Moosejaw managed by a Sir John A. McDonald. ======================= 3. RESULTS ======================= No results. ======================= 4. SCHEDULE ======================= FUSION 1.1 - October 10th From the Gund Arena, Clevland, Ohio Capacity: 20000 Fusion 1.1, the much anticipated debut WILD card, coming from the Gund Arena, Clevalnd, Ohio, in front of 20000 will see 9 men progress into next weeks finals. Six men will compete for the RISING STAR CHAMPIONSHIP, another six will compete for the WILDSIDE CHAMPIONSHIP and another six will compete for the WILD WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP. The 3 winners in each group progress to the 3-way, one-fall finals next week.
PART ONE The very first battle of WILD really will be a wild one, as 'UBERMODEL' MARCUS CHRISTIAN goes up against KYOHEI NAKAMURA in the first of the night's three RISING STAR QUALIFIER
WILDSIDE CHAMPIONSHIP ACTION sees two popular athletes go at it as RYAN STEVENS go up against the ninja snowman known as NEIGE THIRTEEN. A knock-down, drag-out affair is guaranteed between the hated TRAVIS LAGRANGE and current EWWF champion, the fan-loved SIMON SOLKOFF as both men battle for that much wanted place in the WILD WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP FINAL next week.
PART TWO 'MAIN EVENT' JEROME MARKO kicks off the second part of the show against a mystery wrestler brought in by the manager Sir John A. McDonald in the second of the night's RISING STAR QUALIFIER encounters.
OLLIE SAWYER is a no-nonsense kind of guy - how will he cope with the certifiable 'MOOSEJAW MADMAN' RICK HANSON in the second WILDSIDE
CHAMPIONSHIP QUALIFIER of the night. ADAM MITCHELL, one of wrestling's greats, has fought many men in his time. But can anything prepare him for going up against the rarely pinned 'JUGGERNAUT' WILLIAM BENNETT and take him through to the final of the WILD WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP? PART THREE The final RISING STAR CHAMPIONSHIP QUALIFIER sees the man a lot are seeing as the next big thing, 'ALL AMERICAN' ERIC SCOTT, go up against the man, the monster, who has been destroying everything in his path, NIKUI MONSUTAA
'FLYING SCOTSMAN' NEIL MCDOUGALL and JAIME ROBERTS are familiar with each other from their days in RAW together as two of the best cruiserweights around. Sometimes friends, more often enemies, one must defeat the other to progress to the final of the WILDSIDE CHAMPIONSHIP
The main event of part three sees the violent, twisted, notorious and hated SPIKYJIM try and make the future blue against the coolest cat in the neighbourhood, a man who truly deserves the title legend, SID STARR in the first of the night's WILD WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP QUALIFIERS.
PLUS. TOXIC SHOCK INTERVIEWS PRESIDENT NERO VALENTINO IN THE TOXIC PIT AND MUCH MUCH MORE.
