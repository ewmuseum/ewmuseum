Claire Maynard: a Hundred Thousand Dollar Challenge (roleplay)

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys, Anyone wanna take a look at this? Background is last card, after
Jaime Roberts was pinned in something of an upset during a 3 way match for the Wildside title, his manager Claire Maynard announced that she was so confident he wouldn't be pinned or submit again, she'd give
$100,000 to anyone who could prove her wrong before the end of the next PPV.
Thanks in advance, James [Claire Maynard is in the bath. However, it's a rather unusual sort of tub... mainly because, rather than having it's ultra-stylish interior actually get WET, Claire's filled it with a substance more essential to life than even water.
Money. LOTS of money.] CM: Yup, take a look at it, guys. Eddie, Eric, Big Bad Doug... why don't you all feast your eyes on this small portion of the Maynard family riches, and drool? I know I would in your position...
You see, I like money. A lot. In fact, behind Jaime, it's the one thing I could never bear to part with.
Oh, a little here and there's okay. For necessary essentials, like the odd sports car, the latest fashions, and my second favourite substance for filling the bath with...
[She pulls out a bottle of champagne, fires the cork at the camera, and takes a swig from the bottle.]
CM: But I don't like giving away money unless I'm getting something in return. Which is why I've had 'friends' calling me all week about my Hundred Thousand Dollar challenge. You know what they're saying?
'Why, Claire, why?' I'll tell you why. Because while it's generally acknowledged that those people who say money can't buy happiness are seriously bad at shopping, they sorta have a point. You see, money can buy LOTS of things to make you happy. Cars, booze, clothes, tickets to every major event in every town in the US... but there's one thing it can't buy.
The sweetest, most perfect thing of all. Despair. The look of longing on an idiot's face as he looks up at more money than he's ever seen before, and for a moment, can almost taste it. The look of complete misery when it's torn from his grasp.
Between now and WILD's first PPV, there'll be about four guys tasting that despair. Four guys whose dreams of riches will be dashed by the
Sex Pistol. Starting with Neil McDougall...
Hey Neil, how you doin'? Can you imagine what you could get back in your homeland with this kind of cash? Hell, you could probably GET your homeland with this kind of cash... six figures seems too much for
Scotland, if anything... maybe you'll get a discount for having to take Aberdeen.
Like that idea, Neil O? Like the idea of ruling over Scotland as the next Robert the Bruce? Or would you prefer to add to your guitar collection? Hell, a hundred thousand dollars might even be enough to get you a spot on a decent gig! If not, you could buy yourself an audience... I'm sure if you paid enough, even YOU could get a few hundred sad souls to listen to your drones.
Smiling, Neil? Enjoying those dreams? There's only one problem. Something that stands in the way of you and that money. Sex... Stable... Domination. [As she giggles, we fade to black.]
