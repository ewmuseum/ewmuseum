Fusion 1.3 - out now!

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WILD's latest card, FUSION 1.3, is out now. Links at the bottom. But first, a quick look at what to expect on the card.
PART 1 The show kicks into life with DOUG McCRAY debuting against OLLIE
SAWYER. Will McCray make the same impact on Sawyer that he did on
Mitchell or will the Empire Club's Ollie Sawyer find a way to deal with ‘The Deal'.
MARCUS CHRISTIAN just missed out on the Rising Star title but big things are expected of him. Even bigger things are expected of SIMON
SOLKOFF who returns from a mild injury. Christian joined The Sex
Stable at 1.2 and Simon Solkoff may find he is facing more than just one opponent.
‘ALL AMERICAN' ERIC SCOTT has been a marvel so far with his high impact wrestling. A true revelation and embodies everything about being a ‘RISING STAR' champion. He makes his first defence against a man who dwarfs him – JEAN-GUY TRAPPEUR. He may need to worry more about SIR JOHN outside the ring though, who would happily relieve him of the title.
Part 2 A shot at the Rising Star title is on the line as hot fan favourite
JEROME MARKO continues to make progress towards being ‘The Main
Event'. His opponent is DONOVAN ANARCHY who is beginning to catch people's attention in WILD. These really are two rising stars.
ARRON BRADLEY stormed the World title match at 1.2 and suddenly everyone knew who he was. Will his 15 minutes of fame last longer though? A victory against the man everyone loves, the man who should have been World champion EDDIE DRYSDALE would cement that. Easier said than done.
NEIGE THIRTEEN surprised everyone when he won the Wildside title. He promised his first defence against Ryan Stevens, but JUSTIN SLAYNE claimed it as his own when he beat Stevens. Stevens was due to be in the match as the third man, but currently he has a mild injury that means Valentino will not let him compete.
In Part 3 Claire Maynard, amazed at Robert's loss, made a bold claim that no one will pin JAIME ROBERTS or make him submit between now and the first
WildWorld. And she even put $100,000 to back up her words. NEIL
MCDOUGALL takes the first bite at that apple, happy to take on his bitter rival who tried to put him out of wrestling at 1.2 .
TRAVIS LAGRANGE is losing it. Fast. He's put into a match against
SID STARR, the man who always has his head together. Sid Starr pushes
Spikyjim beyond the limit. Will he push Travis Lagrange over the edge in this high-profile encounter?
And in the main event… SPIKYJIM makes his first title defence a man who embodies ‘champion' – the veteran ‘DIRTY' DOUG SIMPKINS. Could this be another shot in the war brewing between the Suicide Kings and The Empire Club as the two stables jostle for dominance? A win for Simpkins here, taking the
World title, would swing power firmly to the Empire Club.
