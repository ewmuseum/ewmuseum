Fusion 2.2 line-up

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LINEUP FUSION 2.2 Stabler Arena, Bethlehem, Pennylvania - December 22nd http://www.stablerarena.com/ In part 1... Fusion is back under the control of Nero Valentino. Ever keen to promote the Cruiserweight division, the show will open with the debut of an innovative daredevil, CK MARTIN as he goes up against perhaps
WILD's finest flyer, and a rising superstar, the creepy NAITOMEA.
This one will be fast, furious and fly! ARRON BRADLEY seeks vengeance on the man who injured him so badly at
WildWorld, 'THE AVENGER' JESSE EWIAK. But Bradley is teamed with newcomer MAGNUS ZAHN while Ewiak will be allied with a returning
SIMON SOLKOFF who will be seeking to shake off a little bit of ring rust and show his world class ability.
LARRY GIONET won the Rising Star championship, something many wouldn't have predicted when he lost to Neil McDougall so quickly early in the last cycle. He defends against one of the most promising new talents to enter a wrestling ring, NORTH FAIRVIEW. His performance against Neige Thirteen earns him this title shot while
Larry Gionet will be out to cement his title hold.
In part 2... One of the most sadistic men in the sport, STEVE LIERMANN joins the ever poisoning hardcore influence entering the WILD bloodstream.
This hated man will battle the Moosejaw Madman, the Cruiserweight champion RICK HANSON, one of the most loved stars in WILD, in this
non-title match. Two of WILD's most popular stars will battle, the All-American against the Canadian hero, ERIC SCOTT against NEIGE THIRTEEN...for a shot at the Wildside championship! These are two contrasting heroes, a serious and powerful phenom against the off-the-wall daredevil luchador ninja snowman.
The Empire Club is back in full operation. Last week Spikyjim tried to tear them apart by making them face each other in a first blood elimination match. All is not well in the Empire Club camp after that match, but this week, DOUG SIMPKINS, JOHN SMYTHE and OLLIE
SAWYER will go up against DESPAIR, the man Smythe attacked last week, DAMIEN BARNETT & hot newcomer JAKE DEMOLA.
In part 3... The tag-team champions will battle! Their alliance has never been easy but now, with the WILDSIDE CHAMPIONSHIP on the line, DONOVAN
ANARCHY & JUSTIN SLAYNE will see who the weakest link in the team is. Will their tag-partnership survive this battle?
Los Angeles is a long way from Scotland, but natives of both areas meet in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania as 'STRAY CAT' SID STARR and 'FLYING
SCOTSMAN' NEIL MCDOUGALL clash - what a contest they had at 2.1 - and this time Juggernaut will not be involved. The winner of this match could see themselves propelled back into contention for the World title.
The main event sees an unusual pairing of JEROME MARKO & ADAM MITCHELL go up against SPIYKJIM & TRAVIS LAGRANGE. Marko still isn't finished with Lagrange, even if Lagrange made things clear at 2.1, and it seems like Nero Valentino is having a bit of fun teasing Adam
Mitchell with a taste of what it would be like to go up against
Spikyjim. Valentino, remembering Mitchell's action from Cycle 1, is very happy to see Mitchell out of the main event at Wildworld 2.
