Enter The Phenomenal One

25 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enter The Phenomenal One 'The Phenomenal' Steve Owens 4WW Exposures #1189 Date: 1/25/05 Location: Toronto ( The scene fades to a bright sunny day we are outside a busy place we can see traffic for miles and miles waiting at some of gate with a security guard sitting there watching most of the stuff and cars as they go in one by one. We find the camera pointing to the sky with puffy beautiful clouds that clutter the sky. Just when that happens a dozen birds fly by in a nice pattern. They land on some electric cable in which they lay still the black crows just crow at everything under them. They face the camera and one fly’s towards us but when it looks like he’s about to fly into us he fly’s over as he does wind from his wings can be heard.) ( The crows sit for a minute then a sudden engine roar makes the cameraman jump big time the crows stay still. As the camera height changes we look right across the street from us there is a field with different color lights going down in one direction. The cameraman keeps the shout just then as he begins to walk forward a roar of an engine scares him again. He stops in his tracks before he hears a vehicle coming down at an incredible speed as he see’s this he drops to the ground . The black crows make noises and fly away wildly each one of them go in a different direction never getting in the others way.) ( The cameraman runs across the busy street looking on as cars pass him with incredible speed that knocks him over as each one pass. He finds a pattern which allows him to safely cross the street he falls to the ground from exhaustion. The camera drops right next to him as the grey haired 45 year old begins to pant and gasp to regain some air. At the fence he slowly gets to his feet then walks towards the front gate as he does security gives him no problem and he just walks toward the airport. He see’s a sign the says “Toronto Canada Air”. We see him waiting for a while in the front of the airport looking like he is waiting for a car. But nothing show’s at this point. ( The cameraman gets impatient turns around reaches his arm in front of the camera and walks in violently. He cuts some people in line. Then ask the lady at the front counter some questions. Cameraman: I need some information have you seen a man called Steve Owens. (The clerk smiles) Clerk: You mean the football playing Cowboys tight end. Cameraman: Yes. (She points towards a plane the camera man rushes towards it were the Phenomenal one is standing with bags in his hand.) (The Phenomenal one lets out a cocky grin he is wearing black warmup pants and a white T-shirt) Steve: Feast your eyes upon The Phenomenal one. (He Flexes) Steve: I am perhaps one of the greatest football player’s and unlike the others who so called claim it I have a suber bowl ring to prove it. (Steve show’s his suber bowl ring) Steve: Yeah I can pretty much back it up I mean I got my own private jet to fly me where ever I want to go. Right now I’m taking this plane to Rome because I have the first match there against a person who couldn’t hurt a fly. (Steve smiles) Steve: This is not a place were gang members thrive because you don’t brawl in wrestling. No you wrestle and honestly after the last time I saw you getting taken out personally everyone knows you can’t fight I mean you used sounds to try to scare the opponent. (Steve smiles) Steve: That’s almost to pathetic but when you think about it you should not be here and I’m going to show you why by beating the hell out of you again by then you should realize that 4WW is not your thing . (Steve smiles.) Steve: But it is my thing the Phenomenal one shall rise to greatness quickly without any problems and I won’t use any rap to do it mike. Mc mike is gang person who needs friends for him to beat someone up he needs guns to pin somebody so ask yourself how will I get in the ring with greatness. I know I would be asking myself that question if I knew I didn’t belong here and I’m better off getting rape album. (Steve spits a little bit as he gets on the plane. He lets out a huge cocky grin.) Steve: Remember the Phenomenal one is here to stay and he is not gonna let some wanna be rapper take that away from him. So you better come thinking that you want it more or face utter humiliation and destruction without a doubt. Well everything is packed up and I’m off to Rome remember if you thought there was trouble in Paradise. (Steve smiles) Steve: Then know it’s gonna be followed by a phenomenal disaster in that same Paradise. If you thought the gang member jumping you were bad just watch what I do to you come match time MC Mike. (Steve starts to walk on his private jet with a huge cocky grin on his face as the scene starts to fade to black.)
