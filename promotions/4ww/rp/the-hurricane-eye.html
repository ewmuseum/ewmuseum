The Hurricane: Eye

27 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Hurricane: Eye Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #982 Date: 4/27/04 Location: The office (The scene opens up in the familiar office of Luscious Lady Luck. Sitting behind the familiar desk is The Lucky One. In addition to the normal pictures in the office, there are two new pictures hanging there. The first one of Luscious Lady Luck pinning Chris Slade, and the second one from MSV with Luscious Lady Luck hanging from the belt 15 feet above the ring. LLL has her hair up in a bun and is wearing a purple business suit.) LLL: My first successful title defense. The first in many I do hope. The first in a long line of title defenses that the Savior of the 4WW will have on her way to establishing a true identity and establishing legitimacy to the belt they call the Tempest Title…… And over all, a very successful Maelstorm 5. Sure, not everything went my way, but what does one expect. Not everything does go one’s way. Sometimes when the unexpected hits, you just have to roll with the punches as they try and bring you down. Sometimes you just have to let things go as they go. No matter what you do, something always seems to happen. (The camera goes into flashback mode because this is my RP and I say it does, and honestly, it’s just a cool effect anyway. The camera refocuses in on Luscious Lady Luck and another woman, similar in physique but a quite a bit taller with brunette hair and green eyes. Lady Luck is wearing Daisy Dukes and a halter top, the other woman is wearing a pair of biking shorts and a sports bra. The are both going through boxes and placing things back up on the shelf.) Janet: It’s a good thing that we moved all of this stuff before the hurricane hit. LLL: What are you talking about? The building is just fine, nothing would have been hurt. We just wasted thousands of dollars on your unfounded concerns about this building. Janet: True, but could you have imagined what would have happened if the building had gone down and we hadn’t moved all of this stuff? LLL: Why do you worry so much? Don’t you understand that when I had this building built I made absolutely sure that nothing would happen to it? Don’t you understand that this building is so sturdy and so safe that nothing would have ever happened to it? Janet: Nothing is impossible. No matter what anyone says, nothing is impossible. LLL: That’s why I’m in charge around here, and your not. That’s why I am the owner of this business and you are working for me. Because I know what is impossible and what is not. Now get back to work. I’m scheduled to go to Australia tomorrow and I don’t want to be stuck here. (The two women work for a while in silence. Janet is building up the courage in order to continue to question her boss while trying to put her office back in order. In about 10 minutes, Janet looks right at Trips with such disdain.) Janet: You know, I don’t know what the hell you see in that girl. It’s not like she is anything special anyway. Red heads are a dime a dozen. And after looking at her photos and stuff there is absolutely nothing there that anyone is looking for. (Luscious Lady Luck glares at Janet, not believing what she has just heard.) LLL: You say that Patti is nothing. You’re telling me that there is nothing special in her? This coming from the woman who was working on a street corner when I brought you into this business and saw the potential you had to run an office for me. This coming from a woman who is without a doubt my right hand woman. Janet: Your damn right I’m telling you all of this. Your damn right to know where I lie. And you’re damn right to know that it’s time someone knocked you off of your throne. (Luscious Lady Luck laughs a bit.) LLL: Maybe it is time that someone knocks me off my throne, but I can guarantee you one thing. That person is sure as hell not going to be you. You know why. You’re fired. And since none of your things are here, you can just leave right now. I’m sure that there is someone else in this office that could probably do a better job. (Janet just looks at LLL. She humphs, throws down what she had in her hands, and stomps out of the building.) LLL: Good bye to you too hon. Maria, Steph, could y’all come in here please? I need some help in here to get this office in order so that I can take off tomorrow. And besides that, I need someone to take over Janet’s job. (The camera unflashbacks if that is the correct terminology to Luscious Lady Luck.) LLL: I never did make it to Australia. I never did expect what was going to happen. I never could have planned it much like the random people they throw at the Tempest Champion, I could never expect exactly what was going to happen….. My opponent this week, Zane Ozz, a tag specialist, and a man who is make a name for himself quickly. An unexpected competitor who I don’t know what to expect and am not really sure how things are going to go. Just some more of the unexpectedness that happens in life…… But like all things that happen in life, I will overcome. Like all things that happen in life, I will come out on top. And like all things in life, your Savior will rise to the top…… Control is something that I’m use to. I’ve always snatched control out of the jaws of Chaos. And Stormfront will be no different. Stormfront is going to be no difference than the rest of my life. Zane Ozz, you and your brother are both great competitors, I have no doubt, but when confronted against the Savior of the 4WW, you will fall like the rest, because as so many have found out here in 4WW, this place is nothing without me. (Scene fades out.)
