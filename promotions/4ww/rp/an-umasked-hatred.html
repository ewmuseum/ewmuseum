An Umasked Hatred

11 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An Umasked Hatred El Lobo de la Muerta 4WW Exposures #121 Date: 9-11-03 Location: Luchacore Training Grounds ¥ƒ¥Fed-up Tired Sick and twisted One-man army I'm enlisted Trust yourself trust no one else Fuck a hero be yourself I don't need your lousy hand-out Clinched fists I’ll fight my way out Fighting my way out Find my way out People wake up and sing along I trust no one My trust is gone Born with nothing Die with everything In a daze These days go by Faster and faster I speed through life Now I've got to take control Of my mental and my physical Never sheltered from life's hard storms I was cold but now I am warm Inside I'm warm Searching and finding the truth inside myself Inside myself My soul was starving I was born with nothing I'll die with everything¥ƒ¥ `~` Born With Nothing, Die With Everything; Papa Roach`~` ~ Speeding through life unprotected by life’s harsh storms are we only then capable of fulfillment. At that moment in time is when we truly can find peace with ourselves. We come into this life born with nothing except a soul to help guide us through our days here. Guardian angels keep eyes upon us as long as we prepare for them a great domain. Certain things only exist out of the choices that we make, the actions we take, and the states of our minds that we choose. There’s a time and place for everything. There’s a reason why certain people exist. There may be certain questions asked but no one will be there to answer them. Don’t be sorry, and never regret these years that we live through. There’s a destination that everything takes us to, where we can look back and remember all of our memories. The days, the places, and all the times where we’ve become young, restless, stupid and reckless; above all I am not sorry and do not regret all the things I have chose and done. I can honestly look back on all my years that have passed me never wondering what could have been. It’s strange how when people “start this gig” they forget who helped them get them where they are, and who guided them into the right direction. To get anywhere in this gig you need to have trust that everyone is leading you into the right direction; then again you don’t need to trust anyone. Only for the simple fact that they are after you themselves. When the time comes you will acknowledge the fact that you are in your December, willing to give it all up for that something that pulls us through day in and day out. And that one thing that drives you to do everything, to live, consumes your life and creates an ultimate open wound. When one gouges at it, deeper and deeper does that wound get. Within that time of healing will a scar grow to remind you of your failure. After this you will wonder what is truly worth fighting for, and what makes you want to scream. This question will never be answered, not even in death. So search all you want because in the end it doesn’t even matter.~ {The scene opens up within a small training facility where several sets of equipment are seen. Punching bags in one area along with speed bags and training dummies. Just around the corner in this white facility are several weight training machines and just beyond our view is a wrestling ring set up with blood and sweat stains on the canvas. A single bulb is set over the ring and lights the entire squared-circle as a reminder of where it came from. In another area there is nothing except tumbling mats where a Spanish cross is seen before the center of it. In the corner lies a giant bamboo stalk and at the opposite corner lies a bonsai tree with a pail of salt beside. According to Japanese beliefs bamboo and bonsai trees are powerful entities that ward off evil demons, and the salt is a way to pay respect to the ones whom have passed away. Out from the locker room walks a man wearing a baggy pair of white pants sported by a dark blue belt tied up in the front. He walks towards the cross kneels down with his blonde hair with black and red streaks fall down over his eyes. He kisses his finger and then places the fingers at the center of the cross. He stands up and walks towards the bonsai tree grabbing a handful of salt and flings it across the end of the canvas. He steps onto the new world and walks to the opposite corner where the large bamboo stalk sits. Beside it on a stool is a stereo, he turns the stereo on and places the dial on a hard rock station. The sounds of Ill Niño playing How Can I Live is heard as he turns the radio up. He starts to jinga to the beat of the music. He starts jumping and spinning with swift kicks into the air. He lands to his feet doing several spinning roundhouses followed by aerial flips. Just as the song ends he completes three back flip kicks before going back into the jinga position. He stops and turns towards the cross before kneeling back down and giving a silent prayer. He stands up and steps outside the mat and walks toward the punching bag. He hops around in a jinga type form again hitting with swift punches and kicks to the side of the bag. He stops and starts throwing hard rights and lefts to a white square taped onto the punching bag. The ruthless aggression begins to dent the surface of the punching bag with untamed speed and accuracy.} El Lobo: Chris…. *gasp* West… will not go *gasp* unpunished. {El Lobo hits the bag furiously with right and left jabs. He darts to the other side and connects with a couple of swift kicks leaving sweat marks on the bag. He stops and drops to a knee breathing heavily as he begins to look up through his colored hair.} El Lobo: Con el espiritu de Dios yo voy a luchar para revancha. Cristofer Oeste you really think that I got my revenge on you on Storm front? Well my friend, you are very mistaken. You are speaking to a man who chewed a mans flesh right off his garganta. You speak to a man who fought his menor amigo para chistes. You see Cris you think you are a God like figure. Le’ me tell you sumsin esse, you ain’t shit here in the real world. My revenge is yet to be seen to the likes of your eyes. Mine eyes have seen the brutality you are capable of. My body has felt the torture that you have given to me holmes. Believe me esse when I say that may revenge, mis revanchas will never end not even in your worst nightmares. I came here to 4WW to escape my past, mis diablos. But you’ve awakened that demon once again esse. This luchacore star found his groove and will show you the vibe from the demented wolf of death. El Lobo de la Muerta will get even with you esse. Just when you think it’s over… heh… that’s when it first begins chucha. {El Lobo steps over to the wrestling ring in the centre of the building. He hops to the canvas then does a front flip over the rope landing on his feet perfectly by the turnbuckle on the inside of the ring. He walks around for a second like he was circling an invisible opponent. He stands by a turnbuckle then sits down with his back to the bottom turnbuckle looking downward to a huge stain of blood and sweat.} El Lobo: Look down here Cris… {El Lobo rubs his hands across the blood stained canvas as he begins to lose himself in the past.} El Lobo: This is exactly where many of us have learned, have fought, and have bled for everything. We gave it all up for just a chance to fight for gold, a chance to become a great champion. But as you know there are some of us who never held a title instead we have great fans a powerful following and great respect amongst the boys. I’m sure los chicos have told you stories about my career, about mi vida primo. You have no idea at what I am capable of primo, so before you count your pesetas take a look at all the checks you ass wrote. Your mouth is writin’ checks that yo’ ass can’t cash. With all the blood I’ve lost, it made me a lil’ but stronger, a lil’ bit wiser, it makes me tougher in the end. And that is why I my fights mean the world to me. It’s from my ignorance and my mistakes that make me a fighter my friend. You think you’re a tough guy holmes. Le’ me tell you something about tough guys. Before you are considered a tough guy you need to get your ass kicked more times than you can count. You have to learn from your mistakes so that you won’t do them again. You must acknowledge the fact that there is always someone better than you. You have to go into a battle with no fear showing. You have to go out there with a purpose and a reason to fight. You need to pick your battles wisely and not jump at the moment. You see Cristofer Oeste, I chose my time to start this battle. Go ahead fuck me up esse, make me scream in pain, bust me wide open so that you can feel a little bit of satisfaction. Go ahead and make this a fight worth my while. Make my blood stain this canvas once more, make my eyes water and drop tears to this canvas once more. Go ahead prove to the world that you are a better man. Just remember its all in this game of mine, it doesn’t matter how you play because in the end you’re gonna lose my friend. Yes you will, you will get the short end of the stick my friend. Rest assured that you may have got the best of me last time esse, but as you will find out it’s a brand new day. Nothing is the same though nothing has changed my friend. You best not take this light holmes, the innovator of luchacore will bring ever little bit that I have to give. You best hold onto that Nightmare title while you still can. This world is cold, oh but my wrath will be much colder than you wish to bear. You can ignore my warnings now, or you can hold onto every little word watching your every little move. You, my friend, need to have eyes in the back of your head. ‘Cause here on the streets, you’ll get spotted out, jumped and punk’d the fuck out. Hold onto these words my friend, and I may just give you mercy. {El Lobo slides under the rope and begins to walk towards the training dummies. He stops for a moment and steps towards a black bag sitting beside the ring. He unzips the bag and pulls out a black mask with white dragons stitched on. He holds it up to the light examining its every aspect.} El Lobo: The Nightmare title, in ancient times it was believed that the dragons were evil creatures that haunted your every movement. In my mestizo heritage the wolf is said to be one of the most feared animals. They are like a family, they hunt together, they attack together, and they kill together. Everything is done in unity, and what I will bring to you Cris is the Unity in luchacore. You think you’re busted open now, just wait ‘till I get my hands on you. I will take the hubcaps off your cheap Nova, I will steal your kendo sticks, and I will stuff you inside the trashcans. Chiquito you will just not be prepared for what I have in store for you. You will wonder what I have become, what type of monster did you unleash. You will scream for help but none will come out. You will be left alone and I will make you hurt as you have made me hurt. You will be wearing a crown of thorns beneath that shell of a man you claim to be. What comes around goes around Cris. You get what you put in and people get what they deserve. It’s the price to pay to prove you are a big shot, a tough guy. It’s up to me, to keep you on your feet. I’ll just sit there indulging in your self-defeat. Heh I like that in this battle I’ll indulge in your defeat, don’t worry you can’t alter this fate Cris. No matter what you do it’s inevitable, ‘cause in the end it doesn’t even matter. Because this is the real mutha’ fuckin’ deal my friend, you will pay for what you did. So instead of running away like you did on Storm Front you bitch; sit there and take this ass whoopin’ like a man, like a real tough man would. {El Lobo places the mask over the training dummy and exits out of the training facility into the dressing room. The camera pans around the room recollecting on everything it has just witnessed. The sounds of the stations changing are heard on the station. It stops and suddenly the sound of Spyder from Powerman 5000 begins to Theme to a Fake Revolution. The camera pans in on the bamboo stalk, then moves toward the bonsai tree and then cuts back to the cross. We then turn our attention to the punching bag where we can still see the dent and the wet marks where El Lobo’s sweaty body connected with the leather mass. The scene cuts to the ring where we close in on blood and sweat stains on the old canvas. We continue to close in as the scene fades out from those blood and sweat stains.}
