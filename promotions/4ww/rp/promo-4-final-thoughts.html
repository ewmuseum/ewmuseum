Promo 4: Final Thoughts Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #395 Date: 14/12/93 Location: A Lounge Sometimes I hardly know where to begin, sometimes my mind is completely blank and at other times I can perceive the near future. The world used to be so clear to me, then one day, everything I knew died. When Xantos died in that bombing, I died. Honestly, though there was clarity then, now I realize how obscured my vision really was. It was as if something was pulled over my eyes; that was a world that will never again utter the name Dark Angel. The past has been so clear to me, through dreams and flashbacks. All of a sudden I have been bombarded with a lifetime of story and I cannot help but place my current thoughts there. I have often found myself thinking, thinking of home. Where is home? Some say home is where the heart is, and others, a place where you belong. To me home is the past, I thrive from knowing what I have done, then again, I thrive from never know just how far I will go. The Gulfsouth was a defining time in my life and career. Dark Angel had not always been a threat, it was first the GWA when I was a rookie. The world was young and I was blinded by the light of God, I had always loved his voice, his presence and his worth. After a short period in the Gulfsouth life and my career had become one, the tragic demise of Xantos Dimiscus hit me all to hard.Though these days, I realize his death was not in vain, he died to allow the Angel to be ressurected. I fondly remember being closed into a casket... The heat soon became so intense I had slipped into unconciousness, it was then I had changed. Reborn into the Gulfsouth, the Angel no longer sat and waiting for advisaries, instead he made them. It was almost as if I had no control of the evil within my soul. In one night, all the good within my heart was washed away, I realized then, God was not good enough, his words was not strength enough and he himself was not righteous enough, to have my servitude. The only being that now deserved my devotion was the angel within me, the Dark Angel. I had sucuumbed to the mind numbing voices and thoughts that had overcome me. My world was changing, my perception unfolding and reality was just so much more savage and brutal than it once was. The time of peace had passed and it was time for the world to revisit the Angel. I set forth to destroy and dismantle all those who stood in my path to glory. Friends, enemies, bosses, and even the fans, they all became names on the list of victims. For those who know the real Darek, many would not have expected me to offer a hand in partnership to James Slade. Though I had become so much more vile, so much more sadistic, I still thought James and I could rule on high. James turned his back blindly to my kind gesture, that was the day James Slade had been destined to feel No Mercy. James and I had fought before in some of the fastest, most brutal beatings I have ever given, but this time things were different, I had the Ladies Man in a cell. Much to my delight I could only begin to fathom the the brutality that the world would see that day. I remember Amanda giving me final words of encouragement. The rest happened all so fast, James had been brutalized for nearly the entire match. His body had become my toy and I had done with it as I pleased. I was in absolute bliss as I heard bones cracking. I could not help but shed a tear when I saw him spit up blood for the first time, I was so prud of him. James had become faceless to me, I no longer saw him as a man, only a victim. I remember that I showed him No Mercy, then I made my cover and last mistake... Billy Dork, Chris West, the rest of 'Special ED', you all violated the balance between life and death. I do not recall the second or third or fouth chair shots, it was the first, from the hands of West, that put me out. I slipped out of conciousness and the only thing that lingered in my mind was the though of revenge. In the end, I laid on my back for enough time as James made the cover and was victorious. The Ladies Man had won the battle, yet the war was just beginning. Dork, West, paid the price with your blood, fond memories I will never forget. Though Slade, he managed to get by without having to stand face to face with death and destiny. I moved onto bigger and better things leaving my vengence and vow to dismember James in the back of my mind. The SCWF was good enough to be called home and though it did not last long enough, I know I had made an impression. Four Winds Wrestling was my sick idea of the next stage of my career. Deep within this mind lies a reason for all actions and that reason brings me to Maelstrom II, that reason is vengence. James, Bullet, Dork, the other Gulfsouth 'boys' have now got to own up to death. I have half a mind to completely dismember them in a senseless blood bath, broken bodies, shattered bones and destroyed dreams. The other half od my mind though, is much more... excited, about this situation. I have only begun to marvel at the ways to torment Bullet and James. I hope that they will begin to feel the pain that I feel each day of life I wake up to. The East Wind titles are on the minds of all men competing in these tag matches, not I. My thoughts dwell in the fecinity of a strategy to fight a two on one match, hell not just one, but after I beat Slade and Bullet, another match. I know with all certainty that Slade and Bullet will finally have their names scratched from my list as I proceed to feed from their carcass like a sickly vulture. Nothing signifies death more than the thought of referal to a vutlure, it will always be there, watching, waiting, wanting more, just as the Angel will be. Sometimes I lose control... [Private Lounge - 18:32 - 13/12/03] 'Glad you could make it.' Eliza smiles. 'Could not imagine missing an opportunity to have you interview me.' Darek smiles back. Eliza stands up and offers Darek a seat, 'please sit. You want something to drink?' Darek shakes his head no and sits across from Eliza. 'Are you ready then?' Darek nods and Eliza places a small recorder in the middle of the table and begins taping. 'You've come to the 4ww, what are your reasons?' Darek leans forward and smiles, 'My reasons are more personal than most would care to know. Let us just say that the world has changed and the way I perceive things has become so much more different than it was before. There were some in my life who commited travesties so criminal that I could never forget about it.' 'How exactly do you get into the tag tournament before you've even set foot in a 4ww event?' Straight forward and to the point, Darek leans back and runs his index finger under his chin. 'Chaos had one thing on his mind, revenge, Chris Baker knows my reasons for being here, he knows whom I seek revenge upon and just how badly I want it. It was a golden opportunity to situate two monsterous beings into one ring and watch the ratings skyrocket as we would begin to dessimate the pathetic competition.' Darek pasuse for a second, 'Perfect Pleasure, Project; Apocalypse, Featured Attraction, they are all teams of standing calibur, but none of them would stand to the likes of the Angel and Chaos.' Eliza pushes her chair away from the table, giving her enough room to cross her legs. 'What about Chaos, It's seems as though he may not show for your match or matches. Care to comment?' 'Chaos, I am sure has his reasons for his actions. He however has left me in a predicament inwhich I must possibly face four men in one night alone.' Darek folds his hands in prayer and places them on the table between he and Eliza. 'Honestly, I could not be more thrilled. How else could one being make a bigger first impression. First, I must face James 'The Ladies Man' Slade and his bitch Bullet, that match should already be booked in the records, I win, clearly.' Eliza cuts in immediately, 'how can you be so sure, you all have a long and complicated history are you sure the past won't come back to bite you so to speak?' 'The time to contemplate is not the past, but much like Bullet says, the present and future. I find it so bleak that at one time in my life I cared what people had said or thought of me, that was the Angel the Gulfsouth had gotten to know. Welcome to Four Winds Wrestling gentleman, class is almost in session, I will instruct you in the ways of torture. You will both become graduates on the honour roll when I am finished with you. Slade and Bullet have never amounted to anything more than mid card performers and that is exactly where they will stay.' 'You sure think you've got it under cont-' Darek raises his hand into her face, 'shhhhhhh. Think? James Slade, Bullet, they are nothing more than a faint memory of a place I once called home. To me, they do not exist, instead a vile hatred for them holds a place in my heart and mind, a hatred so pure and evil that not even I can convince myself of a shortcoming in this match.' He pulls his hand away and sits back down, 'next question.' 'Okay...' She looks deep into his eyes and almost begins to feel the hurt, 'If you suc-' 'What did I just say? There are no 'ifs', I WILL defeat Perfect Pleasure.' 'When, you beat Slade and Bullet, who do you think you will face in the finals?' 'Moses and Lazerus would not assosiate with Psymon if they were not equally as sick as he is, though I do hope and believe it will be a 'Featured Attraction' main event.' Darek turns to the left and orders a drink from the waitress. 'Truth be told, Marvellous and Jerms are a great team, but you have to be more than great to defeat the Angel. Marvellous, Jerms, I only have one thing to say to you.' He pulls the money out of his wallet and pays the lady, telling her to keep the change. '...I will see you in the finals, you may aswell start preparing now, it will be doomsday kids.' 'Not as confident to comment on Featured Attraction unlike Perfect Pleasure?' 'Not as confident?' Darek leans forward and looks Eliza squarely in the eyes, 'I am VERY confident of my ability. Though since I have come to the 4ww noone has taken anything I have said seriously from day one. Instead, I intend on letting my in ring credibility do the talking, people like Dream say they don't give respect, well that is just too bad, I TAKE respect. Whether it is Todd Dream himself or his underlings, I will not let my feelings dull the pain they will feel.' 'Why Todd Dream?' Darek continues to stare Eliza in they eyes, 'no comment,' he fires at her. 'Going after what everyone else wants? The Northwind Championship?' She shoots back. 'It used to be about the glory, it used to be about championships. This matter is personal.' Darek returns to an upright position in his chair, 'are we finished?' 'If you want it to be.' Darek nods and excuses himself from the table, 'I have to be going.' Eliza gets up, 'but your drink.' 'It was for you.' Darek leaves the lounge. Anything can happen these days, the world has become a much larger stage than it was before. Thus demanding performances unlike never before. I know I am capable to live up to the expectations, where the question lies is, am I willing to live up to them. Simply put, yes. I have sacrificed alot in my past, friendships, championships and now is my opportunity to outshine even the brightest stars. The 4ww has managed to produce and hire some of the greatest and most sadistic humans there are; great and sadistic are were the simularities end between myself and them. Human, I am not, dead, I am not... There was a question that once burned my mind and heart, what am I? Xantos told me best when he told the truth. He told me I was the Angel of Death and there was nothing I could do or say that would change that. Some people would accept this, believe it and embrace it, the others... they have been less welcoming and in a far greater demonination. Each day that passes I am greatful for who I am, for there is no other on the face of the god ridden, filthy planet that has the power I retain. The 4ww has simply become the Gulfsouth, only with new faces and some of the same old ones. People like Slade, they know exactly who I am and they FEAR it. Fear is the only thing that they can grasp onto, it is the only reality they will know when you step into a ring and look into my eyes, face to face. What an Erruption it will be when some of the biggest names in the 4ww collide with the unknown soldier from a small federation. It will be a slaughter of mass proportions and the only one left standing will be the Angel, for he is far greater than any of the 4ww have ever imagined. The Angel and I are one in the same, though when I get to the point where my fascination and thirst for blood overcomes me, I know it's then I have lost control. I will be accountable for my actions and when they 4ww begins to put the parts of their fallen warriors back together, only then will they see the world as I do. There have been few who have held my shoulders to the canvass for that excrusiatingly long three count and I very well intend on keeping that number as low as it is. Welcome to the world of the 4ww, welcome to my sanctuary of pain... A place where you will Feel No Mercy. The world will learn that I am Forever, they will learn that my legacy will fly high and they will learn that in this dynasty, I am the ruler.
