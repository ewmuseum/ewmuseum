Reprobate Patti McClancy (nee Anderson) 4WW Exposures #1336 Date: 08/23/05 Location: The Arena reprobate • \REP-ruh-bayt\ a depraved person : scoundrel or to be condemn as unacceptable or evil I should be shaking in my Armani boots, but I'm not. I've faced bloody rookies before and I'll continue to face them as that seems to be all that the bloody staff of the 4WW seems to be able to throw at me. To be honest... I don't even know why Lorena is bothering pillaging and raping the roster of Divas Unleashed to pull talent from them. Her last great choice from DU seemed to have crashed and burned without ever really going anywhere wait, my beautiful and talented sister, Caitlin, took care of that one. Now with the recent addition to the roster of Keiko Yoshimoto, Hope Clarkson, and Faith Von Alman, they all seem to have decided to target me for their desperate climb up the ladder. Faith has yet to make her debut so I wont worry to much about her as she's nothing more then a blot on society and deserves no less then to remain flat on her back ass. Kawanaha you honestly need to wake up and realize denying someone sexual gratification isn't the thing now. You want your toys to play nice, then you have to do more then just deny them the pleasures of the flesh. You've got to take away what is most important to them. Hope Clarkson will find out soon enough. The newest rookie on the scene. A blond haired, blue eyed, misplaced cowgirl from Missouri, who gave up on love to move to Hawaii with some tanned goddess who thinks she can control her play toys with the denial of sexual gratification's. That one will backfire soon enough. To deny those whom you care about will only succeed in making sure that they find their pleasure with someone or someplace else. From the looks of things the last couple of weeks, your darling little Oriental, Keiko Yoshimoto is buying up copies left and right of anything dealing with that blond tart, Diana King. She and that bloody granny, Christine are probably having several bloody masturbation and squirting orgasms over seeing her naked. I know that one isn't setting well with you as your losing your control of her. It won't be long before Keiko realizes that she can't be controlled by the promise of sexual delight. Oh and let us not forget the reason why I came back. Madam Sekhet.... You still hold the belt I want and I will get it. I don't care if i have to go through Madam Min or your lackey in the form of Scarab. I will have that South Wind belt again. Your dodging the pulverable bullet is getting really old, and frankly my dear, I'm getting annoyed with it. A true champion doesn't dodge the challenge of challengers, nor do they hide behind those in their stable. So its time to be a woman (if you really ARE a woman) and step out to the challenge that has been laid down. Its time to meet your destiny in the ring. If I'm lucky the damn booking committee will quit sending the rookies after me. Time: A few days before the show Place: Backstage The words echoing backstage didn't bother Patti Anderson at all. She'd been called a lot of things in her career, Rookie killer was just another one. The various news agencies around the world were dubbing her that based on her actions in the ring against Keiko a couple weeks ago. Now it was just getting worse. One of Las Vegas' betting houses, the Mirage, had her at 8-1 odds of destroying Hope Clarkson, while two separate betting houses, William Hill and The Ladbrooks were at distant odds of 12-2 and 24-1. To which theses odds change on a daily basis. Patti wasn't upset about it. What was upsetting her was the fact that her husband and the booking staff were constantly setting her up against the rookies that kept coming through the revolving door. She began to think that she was being replaced with the top talent. Sean denied that they were looking to replace her as did Lorena. So that left only one person that would know what happened to her sister and everything else that was going on. Her black Gucci thigh high cut boots clicked on the cement floor of the building. Her black and purple outfit brushed up against the wall several times bringing the dust from the walls and various backstage cases that were in her way. As she walked along the wall leading to where personal offices, her suit jacket becomes caught on a nail sticking out of the crates. “Bloody fucking hell!!” Patti looked at the tear and proceeded to swear in a way that would make the ears bleed of anyone who could translate the Greek Language to English. She removes the purple jacket, and continues her walk down the hall way. She walked along the hallway checking over each door, looking for one in particular. She walked another few feet and came to the door marked with a gold star reading... TODD DREAM... COMMISSIONER OF THE 4WW. Patti crinkled her nose in disgust as she turns the knob on the door. She raises an eyebrow in concern that the door isn't locked. She pokes her head inside and notices that Todd isn't where he normally is. She smiles as she notices the bane of her existence wasn't around and that he was stupid enough to leave his office door unlocked. She steps in side and slams the door behind her. The door makes a resounding thud a second before Patti superkicks it off its hinges. Patti walks the few feet over to where Todd's leather chair rests facing away from the door, and proceeds to plop down in it to make herself at home. She props her long legs up on the stack of papers on desk, pulls out her nail file and prepares to wait out the return of the so called Commissioner of the 4WW. Time: Several Hours Later Place: Todd Dreams' Office Patti was growing impatient. Toddy Boy was making her wait and that wasn't the smartest thing to do. Especially when she's wanting answers to questions that she probably doesn't believe he knows the answers too. She spun around in his chair making sure to scatter all of his papers all over the desk as well as the floor. Her foot kicks the wedding picture of him and LLL from their marriage that has been nothing more then a scam. She grabs a pen and begins to doodle on his company stationary. After a while, Patti gets up from her seat and begins to pace, making sure that her heels are wearing a nice pathway into his carpet. At this moment she didn't care if it came out of her paycheck, all she wanted was answers to her questions. She and Todd had always had bad blood, but this was way to low for him to stoop or was it? After a few more paces, Patti noticed a shadow outside of the broken office door. A smile appears on her face as she leans back against the desk and prepares to face Todd head on. But after a few seconds she decides to take a step into the shadows. Better he view the mess of his office without him seeing her first hand. She giggled as she remembered a cornered Toddy Boy is much more fun to deal with. “What the hell happened here? I know when I left the office wasn't left in that way. Someone will most likely pay for this?” “If you'd bloody hell would learn how to lock yer damn door, nobody would get into yer office otherwise.” “Oh great. What do you want now? No wait, let me guess... you're turning in your resignation?” Todd said with his usual smirk. “You only bloody wish that would happen. I'll retire when you decide to be a man and return to the ring. Oh wait, you're still afraid to step back into the ring so I don't have to worry about retiring again.” Todd sighs as he pushes his gift from the West Virgina Mountaineers back on his head. He knew Patti was pushing for the usual response and he wasn't sure if he wanted to play the game again. “What do you want Patrica dear?” “I want some bloody answers that's what I want. And you can start by answering as to why your allowing your booking staff to book me two weeks in a row against bloody rookies? I can understand against Keiko as I did cost her the match. But Hope Clarkson? What the hell would I want with a bloody redneck from Missouri?” “Why is it every time something bad happens to you, you automatically blame me for it?” “Funny you should ask that there Toddy Boy. Because every time something happens you have your bloody hand in the cookie jar. So what's your excuse this time?” “Let me guess... your blaming me for the ransom note and the disappearance of your sister. Please. You should know me better then that. I have better things to do with my time then make Caitlin disappear.” “Then who bloody did it Toddy? Your wife? We know she's been snooping around Caitlin like a bloody bitch in heat.” “I wouldn't put it past her to do that, but I highly doubt that she did it. Try examining people in your family. Better yet... question her boyfriend. He's more then likely the one to do that to entice you back into a match with him.” “Whatever Todd. I see I'm not going to get anywhere with you on that one. How do you explain the ransom note? You would be the first person I suspect to attempt to stop me from climbing the ladder back to my success.” Todd just rolls his eyes. He can tell by the way Patti is acting she's looking for a fight and he's not about to give her one. “What else is supposedly my fault Patti?” “Hmm let me think!” Patti puts her manicured finger up to her mouth and bites down on her nail. “How about the fact that yer letting yer bloody booking staff book me in matches with rookies? My days of having to prove myself were taking care of when I sent yer ass into retirement.” “What Patti? Afraid to lose your crown as a dominate diva in the 4WW to some young upstart. You realize that's a sign that your time has come and gone.” “Unlike you Toddy. I don't give up. If this is all you have to throw at me thinking you're going to chase me into retirement, then just keep sending them in, and I'll keep knocking down your play toys. If Hope isn't any better then Keiko, then I guess I'll be spending my bloody time calling yer ass out from behind the desk again.” “If you think you can. Anything else?” Patti stood for a second going over her mental list of things to accuse Toddy Boy of doing and once again appearing frustrated over the fact that she wasn't getting the answers to the questions she just asked. She stamped her foot in frustration. “Dammit Toddy! Why can't for once you just give the answers without being an arrogant prick?” Todd smirked again as he plopped down in his chair. “Because Patti my dear... I am not behind any of these plots, plans, ideas, and shit that you constantly think I am. Now unless you have any more business with me, get out of my office.” Patti just glares at him. Her temper at almost full blown proportions. She begins to exit the room her mind filled with no answers and a temper that's about to bring a storm onto the northwest that they haven't seen in a while. “Oh Patti, you owe me for the damage that was done to the office while I was out. It will be taken from your paycheck this week.” Patti spins on her stylish heel, reaches into her top and throws a handful of bills at Todd. “Thank Kyo for it... he makes the same mistake as you do... Always leaves his door unlocked.” Patti spins back around and leaves Todd calling maintenance to come fix his door again.
