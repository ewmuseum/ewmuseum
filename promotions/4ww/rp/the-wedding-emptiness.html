The Wedding: Emptiness

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Wedding: Emptiness 4WW Exposures #1087 Date: 12/3/03 Location: The Office (The scene opens up inside of a room. In the room there is a desk, with a large leather swivel chair. The chair has its back turned toward the camera. However, there is someone sitting in the chair, and that person is Luscious Lady Luck. Her Blonde hair looking toward the wall. Toward what was a big part of the wall. The wall, a very special wall.) (On the wall, there are several pictures on the wall. Lady Luck in her final moments defeating Chris Sanchez. Her moments beating Slayer. Her victory over Ike Angel. The Trick or Treat match. All of them on the wall. All of them in a circular pattern.) (And in the middle of them all, a big empty spot. A very recognizable empty spot. The spot in the shape of a title belt. A belt that is all too familiar. A belt that LLL has held for almost 3 months now. A belt that she has held longer than anyone, and a belt that was cost to her.) (There was an empty spot there. There was an empty spot that is sitting there. Much like the hole in LLL’s heart, an empty spot that is needs to be filled, that should be filled. How does one fill that spot. How does one fill that emptiness.) (Much like the emptiness left by Patti, there is now an emptiness left by Patti. It was her who had cost her the Tempest Title. It was her that distracted her. LLL was unfocused. LLL knew she was unfocused, but she couldn’t get herself focused. But she couldn’t get herself focused.) (She had a big match this week. A big match for unStable. A huge match for unStable. A match that would truly prove the future of the stable. Would Patti interfere in that? Would Patti find a way to screw that up? Why was it that Patti kept interceding herself in the match?) (This week’s match is against the Dream Makers Cult. What a dream this would be. A dream to have Patti back. A dream to get back the woman that she loved. A dream to somehow become one again.) (But no, the Dream Makers Cult. A team that is as dangerous as its leader. They found a way to steal the East Wind Belts. And now they are trying to find ways to win this match. Find ways to establish themselves as a big part of the 4WW. They also left her empty. But not as empty as Patti.) (Yet does Patti truly know what is going on with LLL. Does Patti truly know what is going to happen at MSVI. Does Patti truly know what is going to happen. All she knows is the phrase. That one phrase.) (And then there is the KB. A group that wants to keep everyone empty. A group, not unlike what Patti has done, but continues to leave people empty. They are a great stable. A stable that knows what to do to win. But they do not know what is going to happen either.) (Patti has some good friends. She may need them. Especially after MSVI. Especially after what Patti experiences and sees what happens at MSVI. The KB, they are a series of great competitors. They are a group that knows everything they need to know, but they can not even stop what is going to happen.) (Get Out Alive, is that what it is? Yes, people will be getting out alive. LLL will be getting out alive. LLL will be at MSVI to become something that she has wanted to become for a long time. LLL will be at MSVI doing exactly what was suppose to happen several years ago, but has been put off till now. And Patti has her friends to help her make sense of it all. Yes, it is definitely something that Patti will need friend to help with.) (This week, unStable is going to out last 3 other stables. LLL knew that. LLL was going to do everything that she needs to do in order to survive. And after this week, is MSVI. A day that will be a happy experience for LLL. A day that will bring to LLL everything she has wanted for the past several years. A day of joy for Patti and LLL, as well as Melinda and Caitlin. Soon, shortly, it will all happen.) (LLL moves her hand, raising her hand up to her, looking at the diamond engagement ring that her soon to be husband had given her. Soon. Very soon, it would be.) LLL: Diana King Anderson. Soon, it will be. (Camera fades to black where there is a giant purple heart on the screen with the letters “LLL + PAA” inside the heart.)
