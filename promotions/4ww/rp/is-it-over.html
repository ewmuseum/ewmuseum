Is It Over? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #397 Date: 12/14/03 Location: The Rental House A coin appears in front of Destiny. One side says wrath, the other side lust. Two words that are describing the battle that lay ahead. A battle that Destiny had hoped to prevent Patti from facing the wrath that was coming from thousands of miles away. A battle that will put all on the line, with only one coming out the winner, the other to sadly walk away in defeat and letting go. Destiny sits back in her chair and flips the coin. She watches the coin twirl in the air, its silver and gold spinning brilliantly in the air. It lands face up on Wrath. Wrath - noun - 1) Strong vengeful anger or indignation. 2)retributory punishment for an offense or a crime: divine chastisement. Synonym see ANGER. Unfortunately this time it deals with someone that affects both of them. Your wrath and pettiness has led to this battle that you are wanting to wage upon the man you claim you want. Instead of being the good girl and gracefully admitting to losing what you never truly had in the first place. You attempt to find anything to use to get what you want. Destiny has watched Patti stand back and watched you attempt to destroy her from all sides, even going as far as trying to ruin the relationship with Sean, but all it succeeded to do was drive Sean and I closer together. When you first sprang your little surprise, of course both Destiny and Patti was upset, who wouldn’t be? But when Sean insisted on staying with her, because he loved her, and NOT you the relationship thrived even more. Since the wrath of hatred you have shown against Patti has gotten to the point that it has started to destroy you. And now you have decided to call upon your birthright of sending your wrath upon Sean as you see fit. Do you honestly think your blood lust will be enough to satisfy the lost of someone that is supposed to bring forth the new generation for you to survive. Destiny thinks not. Destiny once again flips the coin, the gold and silver sides shining in the light of the single candle lighting the room. The lust side of the coin came up. The side that pointed to the other battle being waged. A strong, lusty battle, that was bound to happen. Lust - noun - 1) Pleasure, Delight b: personal inclination: Wish 2) usually intense or unbridled sexual desire: Lasciviousness 3) a: an intense longing: Craving b: Enthusiasm, Eagerness. And the lust for Sean McClancy will be the downfall of one woman. They both love him in their own fashion. One enjoys the pleasures that he offers to her for the last six months, the other suffers the cravings of what her opponent has. An interesting mixture in this match up. One set up by the CEO of the company. A woman who lusts for someone to come out the winner in this match up will surely call it fair down the middle....or will she? Fey has never done anything really to help Patti out in the past, and Destiny is afraid that Fey will do something to cost Patti the match. This would be the perfect opportunity for Fey to get her wrath against Patti. If she were to unfairly officiate this match, she could get her wish of having Patti out of Sean’s life forever. This action would force Sean to leave the side of the woman he has loved for the past 6 months. A woman that he had so desperately wanted to start a family with. A woman that helped him achieve his last world title reign. A woman that he had battled his sister over several times. Sean had thought that Fey had come to terms with Patti being in his life. He had thought that Patti had proven to be worthy of whatever challenges that Fey had put forth. Obviously not. Fey must still be holding some sort of grudge against them for sneaking around behind her back. Oh well, but as of lately, Fey seemed to have softened a little towards Patti. She wasn’t sending Patti into hardcore matches. In fact, she made Patti the number one contender for the North Wind title. But with Fey, Destiny can never tell which way she will turn. So it is always best for Patti to watch both very closely. Now in Hawaii on the 14th of December, Patti Anderson, ‘The Darling of the 4WW’ takes on the woman calling herself Annie Leanan, ‘The Beauty of the Wisps’. This is the final battle. This is were wrath and lust will be decided. The question is which will win out? Stay tuned to find out. ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: Halekailani Beach House Time: Early Afternoon ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti sat at the dining room table, staring out the window at the beautiful scenery that Hawaii is known for. Sean had left about an hour ago to attend a meeting. Fey showed up in the limo, and took Sean with her. All he had said was something about a meeting with the Order, and that’s where it ended. She didn’t press for information, her intuition told her otherwise. She had a feeling that the whore was up to no good, and using a meeting like this to attempt to get her hands upon him before the match. Patti’s hand continued its normal spoon stirring motion in the cup sitting in front of her. The cup of tea had gone cold the minute Sean had left the house. Patti continued to stare out the window at the ocean. She couldn’t help but worry about this meeting. What if this was Annie’s way of setting Sean up for the kill seeing how he has rejected her. A cold chill danced down her spine at the thought that Annie had lured him to his death. Then again, Fey had been the one to show up at the house and informed Sean he had to attend with her, and unless Annie was a cold blooded killer..... Patti shook off the image of Annie going all psycho and killing both Fey and Sean. If that were to happen, Ian wouldn’t have already eliminated Annie from the face of the planet. Patti quickly pushed herself away from the table. Sean’s t-shirt clung to her frame. She pulled the collar up closer to her face and inhaled the scent of him. The earthy smell of him kept her calm. If by some twist of fate, she were to lose him, she would at least know that these past 5 months had been the best and most remarkable of her life. Even her meeting with Fey went well. Which was surprising enough, she was even more suprised when she received a faxed copy of the contract stating that Annie had agreed to put her engagement on the line. Patti should be planning her wedding to the man she loved, not trying to be thinking about losing the one thing that matters to her. She had hoped with the arrival of Jade into the federation would have helped cheer her up, or the fact that Eliza Murphy was free again, and making plans on recapturing the heart of Sean Summers again. Nothing mattered to her anymore. Patti walked away from the table and reached into her purse. She stared at the cell phone for a few minutes. Her first thought was to call Sean and see how things were going, but that was not what he needed. She rested the antenna against her chin and thought about who she could possibly call. She pulled Eliza’s number up on the phone and quickly dialed. A frown graced Patti’s beautiful face. Eliza wasn’t answering the phone, which probably meant that she was working on an interview with someone. The frustrations were really setting in as Patti slammed the phone closed. She thought about calling the Tale Smith production office to see if her worthless opponent had put out her promos trying to convince the fans of the federation that she, not Patti would walk out of the Neal S. Blaisdell Center with Sean McClancy at her side. A small smile came to her lips. When will that tramp realize that she was going into that match where Annie would not win. Patti had managed to con poor unsuspecting Annie into signing a match with Sean on the line. Who’s the smart one now missy? Patti opened up her phone again and speed dialed another number. Once again four rings and a voice mail message. The frustrations were beginning to mount within Patti. She was dying to know what was going on, but then again she was fearful of what could happen. She was extremely fearful of a life without Sean. An unsettling feeling began to appear in her stomach once again. The same unsettling feeling she got when Sean had briefly disappeared from her life. She knew her stress levels were building up slowly within her causing the nauseating feeling to return. The doctors had warned her that this will continue to happen when she was faced with a decision that could alter her life. Unfortunately this bout was a little more unsettling then the previous when Sean and her had separated over a simple misunderstanding. Then it returned when he showed up in her home and various other places to fulfill his quest of regaining her. This time the unsettling feeling was worse then before. Patti darted quickly into the bathroom, the tea and breakfast contents decided this wasn’t the morning to stay in place. She kept her head hung over the toilet for several moments. Her face flushed with the sudden act of vomiting. Patti sighed as she closed the lid, and rested her head on the cool lid. Tears streamed down her face as the stress continued to rush through her body. The sobs soon followed, each one racking her body as they came. The sudden realization that she could lose everything was too much for her mind and body to handle. After a few minutes, the nauseating feeling subsided enough that Patti was able to get up off the floor and attempt to do some cleaning up. She took a couple steps to the sing and cleaned her face of the tear stains and everything else. No sense in letting Sean know that this was working on her just as much as him. A sound from the other room alerted Patti to the fact that the fax machine was alive with an incoming message. Patti dropped the cloth and headed into other room. The feeling of impending doom resurfaced in her as she waited for the machine to finish spitting out the message. After a few moments, the machine released the message. Patti pulled it out and looked at it. A look of worry crossed her face as she read it. To: Patti Anderson From: Fey McClancy, CEO of 4WW Subject: Change In Match Due to the severity of the match at Maelstrom 2, I have made the match a NO INTERFERENCE match. As you know, this means that neither Sean nor Jade will be able to be at ringside. Jade and Sean are more then welcome to watch from the comfort of the locker room area, as I have asked Ian to watch over Sean to make sure he does not attempt to interfere with the match up. Annie has also been informed of this and knows that the members of Featured Attraction are not welcome at ringside either. This match will be a fair contest between the two of you, and to put an end to the battle for my brother. Patti’s face fell even more as the paper fell to the floor. The stress came rushing back through her body, as the tears started to fall. She had battled once before without Sean and it turned out disastrous for her. Patti slowly fell to the floor as a knock on the door breaks the silence in the house. Fade Out.
