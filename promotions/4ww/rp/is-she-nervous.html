Is She Nervous?

Is She Nervous? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #38 Date: July 12, 2003 Location: Phoenix, Arizona Brazen as defined by Websters as one who is shameless or impudent, bold, brassy, insolent, rude. A word that will define an opponent for this week. He has to be brazen enough to think he has what it takes to wrestle a woman. A woman who knows how it will be. A woman who was trained in the best gyms in Austrialla. A woman trained by the best in the business. A woman trained by the Hardcore Icon. A woman who has the skills and the talent to knock Bad Boi out of the qualifying match for the North Wind Title. Bad Boi?!?! What the hell kind of name is that for a wrestler? Did someone get all excited over watching XPac on the telly and decide to model your life after him? Or did you get inspired by watching Cops and InnerCircle? Or is it the influx of the rappers? Maybe you should pursue a career as a wigger instead of a wrestler. Might do ya some good. And as all good apoklada logos goes it has to come to the point. In other words Bad Boi, after all is said and done, the dust clears, the smoke leaves the building, YOU wont know what hit you. Friday July 11, 2003 Morning Hours A Hotel in Phoenix A knock is heard on the door. Patti, the first to hear it, rises quickly out of bed. She dresses in the first thing she locates. Sean's shirt will just have to do for now, and opens the door. She sees what looks to be a male agent of some sort standing before her. He is dressed in a gray suit with an annoying bloody red tie. His hair is gray and thinning on top. Patti smirks at the resemblence to Matt Cummings. Patti: What the bloody hell do you want? Matt Cummings Wannabe: Ms. Anderson, Mrs McClancy-Childress couldnt make the meeting with you this morning so she sent me over with the rest of the papers that she needs for your upcoming match. Patti: Is that so? And just what does dear Fey have in store for me now? Cummings Wannabe: Well, Mrs McClancy-Childress has placed you in a match for the 4 Winds North Wind Title. It's a Gauntlet Style Match. In order for you to advance, you must defeat your opponent. According to the paper work I have here, it says that if you advance you will be seeded number three, behind Shocker and Fagen Gilchrist. Then followed by Syxx Figures, the second match qualifier, Jay Phoenix, Nick Raynes, Johnny Beckett, and the the third qualifier. Patti: And how do I win this entire choris axia thing? Cummings Wannabe: Why survive it of course. You have to either pin your opponent, make them submit, or get them disqualified from the match. That is the only way to win. In other words my dear, you have to outlast and outwit to win. Patti: You just HAD to quote Survivor didnt you? That bloody American show isnt worth the one million they offer. Cummings Wannabe: Mrs McClancy-Childress has also named your opponent in the qualifying match. Patti: And whom would that be? Cummings Wannabe: A man by the name of Bad Boi. Patti bursts out in a fit of giggles. Patti: Bad Boi? What the fok? Is he looking for a mommy? Does he need someone to spank him? Change his diaper? Give him a patsy? The agent shakes his head. He had a feeling that his boss has had her hands full with this woman. After meeting the lovely Ms. Anderson face to face, he saw a woman that possessed great strength in outer beauty as well as inner beauty. He saw a charming woman who had an air of innocence and sweetness. He just wished he knew the reason as to why Mrs. McClancy-Childress didnt like her. Cummings Wannabe: Is there anything else you need to know? If there isn't, I will need you to sign these papers, and I will be gone from here. Patti: No, you seemed to answer all the bloody questions I have. Patti signed the forms and walked the agent to the door. She closed the door and walked back to the center of the room. She was lost in thought, and without paying any attention, she walked right into the bare chest of Sean McClancy. She steps back and admires the man that she had fallen in love with. His red hair mussed from sleep, his face still has the 'I'm sleepy' look to it. A two day stubble of hair on his chin. Sean: Wot the fok was that noise aboot? Patti giggled. He was always a bear when he gets woke up before he's good and ready. Patti: Your sister decided to cancel her meeting with me. She sent over the papers that needed to be signed for my first offical match. The lypimenos nothos could have passed for Cummings. I wonder how he's doing since the table incident back in Los Angeles? Sean shrugs. He's still not quite awake enough to understand what she just called Cummings, but knowing his girl, it wasnt something nice. Sean: Wot else was said? Patti: That in order for me to win this Gauntlet match I have to go through a few others. And from the list that I was given, looks like I've got some serious competition. A bunch of lypimenos, chorisaxia, arsenikos if you ask me. Sean: EXCUSE ME?! Patti: Not you, you oaf. Sean: Oh. lol Patti: Out of all those involved in the gauntlet match, Nick Raynes seems to be the biggest arsenikos pornis i've ever seen. The man thinks he's God's gift to women or something. Syxx Figures, I know from the OTHER federation. As the Jay Phoenix, I remember him too. As for Shocker, Gilchrist, Beckett, and the others, I dont know enough about them to care. I know I have the skill to take them all on, and I know I can walk away victorious. Sean smirks at Patti's sudden enthusiasm at the upcoming match took him a little by suprise. He knew he taught her well, he just wished the nagging feeling would go away. He shrugged it off and looked at Patti. He still felt his heart flutter and the sight of her in just a tshirt. She must have given the poor agent a thing or two to think about. Sean: Let's get a shower in, and then off to the gym for some training. Patti: You and your training. You trying to bulk me up? Sean wiggled his eyebrows at Patti. She caught the look he was giving. She stopped in the middle of her pout, stripped off her shirt, and darted for the shower. Patti: If ya can catch me, I'll wash your back first. Sean didnt need an invitation for that offer. He chased her into the bathroom. Fade out Private Balcony Suite Phoenix, AZ Patti Anderson's quiet world was spinning. Her life had changed. In just a few days she would be stepping into the ring for her first professional bout. Why? Because Fey McClancy-Childress thinks she's what the 4WW needs. Or maybe its a test? Patti rolled over on her side and stared at the hotel wall, a picture of a seaside landscape caught her attention. Patti tried to stop the sigh that had come from her lips. Sean briefly stirred in bed beside her, but didnt wake up, he rolled over onto his other side and went right back to sleep. No sense in disturbing him for this. This was something she needed to work out on her own. Patti crawled out from under the blanket. She slipped on Sean's shirt. The smell of him still clinging to it. She tipped toed quietly over to the picture and viewed for a few moments. The picture showed a house located near the beach. She closed her eyes and remembered a time of being on the beach. A moment of homesickness struck her and tears fell once more. She walked quietly to the sliding glass door that lead out to the balcony of their room. The sun was just starting to rise on the horizon. The sky a beautiful shade of reds, yellow, and oranges. She opened the door and stepped out. She closed her eyes as she felt the gentle mountain breeze hitting her face, blowing her already disshelved hair around her face. She wished she had grabbed a clip or something to keep the hair out of her face. She walked over to the railing and looked out over it, staring off into the distance. So much had changed in her life. She had always loved the world of wrestling. She remembered back to when an old friend had introduced her to it. The muscler bodies of the men, the looks of the women, the action and those silly storylines that kept bringing her back to it each week. And after spending time on the road chasing Sean from show to show and having one experience at ringside with him, that was just enough to sinch her desire to be in it. She admitted to herself that she felt sick when she heard Sean talking to someone outside of her door back home, and when he said he was leaving, she thought he had just said those things to get another shag out of her, and she had to admit she was feeling sick that again. Then she was told she was coming along with him. That suprised her. Why would the Order want to see her? She found out the reason why the Order wanted to see her. Ian felt the need to address her 'stalking' of Sean. Technically she wasnt stalking him, she was just following her heart. She had managed well enough to hook up with him each time, and she didn't regret it. She handled the suprise from Ian and Sean very well. A chance to be on the road with Sean more then just when he came back through to Austrialla or sent her a plane ticket to come see him. And now a big suprise, Sean's sister decided to sign her to a contract. A contract that would put her in the action instead of her sitting on the sidelines. Patti leaned up against the cement wall of the balcony. She continued to stare out over the landscape, what little grass there was on the hotel grounds, everything else was brown and mountain ranges as far as her eye could see. Nothing like home, but she continues to stare off over the plains. Meanwhile back in the bedroom of the hotel room, Sean McClancy rolls over in bed. He prepares to wrap his arms around what he thought is the sleeping form of Patti, but all he finds is an empty pillow. He looks around the room, a feeling of worry comes over him. He reaches over and grabs his pair of shorts and starts to search the room. He looks out the patio door and finds Patti standing outside. He quietly slides the door open and steps outside. He walks up behind Patti, runs his hands up and down her arms. He drops an innocent kiss on the top of her head. He feels Patti shudder. A smile appears on his face as he feels her melt against him. He notices her arms folded almost protectively across her chest. A sigh escapes her lips. Sean: Wot the matter love? Why didnt ye wake me up. I'd have gotten up with ye. Patti: Just thinking about the last few weeks. Cant believe everything that has happened. Just kinda worried about something. Sean: Wot? Patti: This whole contract deal. I mean WHY would yer sister decide to do something as parafronas as putting me inside the squared circle? Sean continued to rub her shoulders. Him listening to her is all she needed right now. She didnt need his advice nor his questions. She just needed him to listen to her. He knew her emotions were running at full steam. Patti turned around and wrapped her arms around him. She stood on her tip toes and kissed him on the lips. Patti: That's why I love ya. You always listen to what I have to say. Makes me feel that you really do understand and care. Sean: Come back to bed and 'll show ye how much I care. Besides ye have that match tomorrow night. Ye need to be fresh for that. The two return inside for the night. . FADE TO BLACK
