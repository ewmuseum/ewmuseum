The Hurricane: Preperations

7 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Hurricane: Preperations Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #934 Date: 9/7/04 Location: The office (The scene opens up within the office of Luscious Lady Luck. She is sitting there wearing a low cut deep red business suit. She is going over some papers. The Tempest Title is sitting up on her shelf, just staring everyone in the face. Her office has several different pictures of different models, actors and actresses, and other random famous people. The desk is made of a hard oak, and everything is extremely ritzy and nice looking.) LLL: One thing, that one may never understand, is how a life can be so turned upside down. How a world can be placed on its head in a moments notice. And what happens to that person when such devastation actually hits them. Sometimes, they just move on and work forward through everything. And other times, they become so involved in things they forget about everything else…… Much like how Mr. Chapman will feel when the Savior of the 4WW climbs that so called ladder of success and reclaims my title, my life has also been in disarray before. I’ve climbed that ladder of success several times, but I have also been knocked off it several times. And unfortunately, one of those times, I must admit, that it cost me something that was very near and dear to my heart. Isn’t that right hon.…… It was several years ago. Five to be exact. It was shortly after I promised my love that we would always be together. Shortly after I promised her that I would return to Australia. I had something to attend to in the states. Little did I know at the time how it would destroy everything and little did I know what I was getting into…… You see, shortly after I left Australia that first time, I was working out of the main offices of my modeling agency, when I got some of the worst news of my life. (Scene goes into flashback mode. We see Luscious Lady Luck sitting at a desk in a high rise office building. Many of the same furnishings are on this desk as is in the one before the cut back. Only the desk in this case is a steel one, and there are several books behind her labeled “Headshots.” Suddenly, the phone rings.) LLL: Diana King. (Silence) Yeah, I’ve been watching the news Janet. I’ve seen the Hurricane coming toward you. (Silence) I haven’t heard any reports recently, but last I heard it’s not supposed to be a very strong one. Like a 1 or a 2. (Silence) Really, it’s predicted to be that powerful? I don’t know, I think that you’re going to be ok hon. The building should be fine. (Silence) Your right, we do have a lot of stuff down there. I tell you what, I’ll come down there and we’ll get everything transported up here to the main office. All the headshots and videos and stuff. I’ll be there as soon as I can book a flight. (Silence) Just get stuff ready to be transported. It’d be even better if we could get all of our headshots on disks and CD’s even. They are much smaller and transport easier. (Silence) OK, thanks Janet. I’ll see you after a while. (LLL hangs up the phone, pick up her suitcase, and heads out of the scene.) LLL: Hey Gretchen, I’m going down to Florida for a few days. Take care of the office and everything while I’m gone. Gretchen: Yes ma’am. Everything is good in my hands. (Scene fades out of the flash back mode and back to the present.) LLL: Little did I know exactly what I was going to get into as I went down there. Little did I know what was going to happen. I knew that there was one Hurricane coming, but little was known about what else was coming at the time…… And now I have a hurricane with a mouth that thinks he has to take things easy on me. Never mind that I was strong enough for my own business. Never mind that my business was made up and run by women for the most part. Never mind that we did everything by ourselves without the assistance of men. I was tough at the time, and I still am tough…… You’re going to take it easy on me Chapman? Is that it? You’re going to take it easy on me. Please don’t. Please don’t make any exceptions for me. This is a wrestling. This is what I do for a living. I’m not our there for someone to take it easy on me. I’m not out there just because I can. I’m out there because this is what I love to do. This is what ‘m trained to do…… This is all about the Tempest Title. This is about more than just some regular match, this is about a title. This is about a title that can launch someone’s career faster than a hurricane can launch a piece of paper. This is a PPV Title Match. Take it easy on me? Please, don’t. I’d hate to think that you did. And besides, this body, while it may look small and fragile, has been through hell and back. This petite body has taken the best shot of many men, and has lived to tell about it. This small body has been trained to take moves of unbelievable pain and still make its way back to the top. And this small body could take an assault of the most devastating type and still come back…… I should know. I was there when it happened. (Scene fades out.)
