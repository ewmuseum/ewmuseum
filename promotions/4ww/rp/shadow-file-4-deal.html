Shadow File #4 - Deal Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1150 Date: 1/25/05 Location: |>> Scene opens in Kris’ restaurant, Kris Shultz in an expensive gray suit sit at a table with his lovely twins that wear matching short black dress. Kris deliciously eats a Russian dish…stop when a Russian man in a black suit approached him and whisper in his ear. Kris stands, throw his napkin on his plate and walk away…the man follow his boss. Twins continue their meal. > In Kris’ office that was above his restaurant, Kris storms in with three of his bodyguards. > Kris and his bodyguards look over to the man, see that it was Yosho Hiroshima in a black color suit with his bodyguard in a familiar red color attire and a bloody bandages over his right eye. Kris’ bodyguards draw out their guns and aim them at both men…Yosho’s bodyguard draw out two Desert Eagles and aim them at the Kris’ bodyguards. > In the interrogating room of a Germany Police Department, the Deathscythe Outlaw sit at metal table alone with his head lean back on the chair, relaxingly smoke a cigarette. The door opens, a chubby German man with dark brown color hair and green color eyes in a white shirt with black pants and black suspenders enter with a file in hand, read the file diligently. The detective closes the door, approach the table and throw the file on the table. He pulls up a chair and sit at the table with Kyo. > Kyo doesn’t reply. > Detective glare at Kyo…the door open and a slim German man with blonde color hair and blue eyes in a German police uniform step in the room halfway. > Faith or Victoria hands the detective her identification…the detective skim it and shock fill his face. > Kyo leans back in his seat have his eyes close, sigh out a cloud of nicotine smoke from the corner of his mouth. > Outside of the Police Department, Kyo Kushinaga and Faith walk down the concrete steps together. > Kyo lights up a cigarette with his silver lighter and breath out a cloud of nicotine through both nostrils. > Kyo makes an amuse sound…catch a silver pocket watch with a chain that have the Shadow Ninja emblem on one side and Deathscythe Outlaw emblem on the other. > Kyo stares at Faith. > Faith smirks and make an amuse sound. > In Kyo’s dark hotel room, the Deathscythe Outlaw lay shirtless on a Queen size bed, hold the pocket watch up and stare at it. He sits up with a sigh, move to the nearby dresser, laid the watch down and pick up the phone…dial a number and get a busy sound. Kyo frowns and hang up the phone, lay back down with his arms behind his head. > The night disappears…a new day appears. > Kyo soundly sleeps in his room with the TV…he suddenly jerk and grunt in disturbance. In his dream, a large figure with violet color orbs stand in the shadows…a dark violet color vortex appears on the ground and a dark shadow rise. > Twin crimson orbs appear on the dark shadow…Kyo sit up in bed in fright, pant heavily and feel cold sweat run down his face…the demonic tattoo on his back dimly glow a cerulean color. He calms down…the glow disappear. Kyo gets out of bed with a sigh, head to the bathroom and enter. He stands at a running sink with head down, splash cold water in his face…lift his head and stare at himself in the mirror. > Kyo washes up…in the bedroom, a recap of last week’s StormFront was shown on the twenty-six inch gray TV…it later talk about the condition of Claudio Turbine after his violent match against Slayer. > Seven blocks away from the hotel, Kyo Kushinaga in his work clothes walks down a sidewalk alone…stop when he notice a rusty black color GTO park in an alley, head for the car. He stands before the car, check it. > Kyo nods…catch the key of the car. > It’s midnight in downtown Munich, the Deathscythe Outlaw stand on the curb alone, stare at The Soviet Lounge on the other side of the road and see no one in the restaurant through the front window. He draws his guns out, check and see that both are fully loaded; holster them in his trench coat. Kyo heads to the restaurant…stop when he noticed its fuse box on an outside wall…he smirks. > Kyo enters the restaurant…twenty-something of Kris’ bodyguard arm with sub-machine guns and assault rifles come out of hiding from tables, the bar, and the kitchen...they aim their guns at Kyo. Kyo holds both hands up and glare at them. > Yosho nods…Yosho’s bodyguard punch Kyo in the face. Kris smirks. > Yosho’s bodyguard punch Kyo in the gut…Kyo grunt with his head lower, glare at the bodyguard and nods. > Twins glare at Kyo. Kris laughs…order his men to aim their guns at Kyo’s fatal areas. > Yosho gives Kyo a more serious look. > Yosho’s bodyguard pulls out both his Desert Eagles, aim them at Kris. Kris’ bodyguards turn their aim at Yosho and his bodyguard. > Kyo watches with a smirk. > Yosho’s bodyguard and Kris’ bodyguard have their fingers on the triggers. > Kyo’s eyes pulse a crimson color…an explosion from outside was heard and felt…the lights in the restaurant go out. Rapid gunshots are fire in the darkness. > The gunshots stop. > Yosho’s bodyguard and Kris’ bodyguards search through the darkness with their guns aim ready. > Twin cerulean orbs appear behind the bodyguard. > Rapid gunshots are fire…Kyo dodge the bullets and open fire on the bodyguards. Grunts and other sounds of death are heard underneath the fire fight. Kyo sneaks behind one of Kris’ bodyguards and use him like a human shield, fire his advance at the blind bodyguards. The bodyguards fire at Kyo…they hit their comrade. More yells and gunshots are heard…it all stop, the emergency lights come on and reveal that all of Kris’ bodyguards are dead. > Kyo notices that Kris, Yosho, and the man in red are not among the many corpses. He suddenly hears a sound above him, look up and see that Kris with Yosho run down a metal bridge that hang high in the rafters. Kyo runs to the stairs…stop when Kris’ twins flip out of nowhere and stand in Kyo’s way. > Twins grab hold of their matching green color dresses, pull them off and now wear matching black leather dominatrix attires complete with long whips. > Kyo aims his guns at both girls…the twins swing their whips and grab hold his guns, pull them away from him. > Twins viciously swing their whips at Kyo…Kyo slip through their assault. > The twins continue their attack on Kyo…Kyo move through slashes with use his great aerobatic skill. One twin’s whip wrap around Kyo’s wrist and pull Kyo over…Kyo counter and pull the twin close to him. The twin kicks at the groin…Kyo lock his legs together and hold the twin’s leg, knock her down with an elbow to the face. The other twin knocks Kyo down with a flying thrust kick to the chest from out of nowhere. > Kyo glare the twins…flip back on his feet with his head lower. > The whips mysteriously stop and levitate before Kyo. > The twins’ whips mysteriously glows a crimson color aura, they pull on them…the whips wouldn’t move. Kyo lifts his head with eyes close, open them and reveal that glow the crimson color. Fright fills the twins’ faces. He makes a serious sound…the whips go back to their masters and tie them up tight. Twins struggles free…Kyo lunge for them, knock them out with a double roundhouse kick. > Kyo looks up at the rafters, see that Kris and Yosho disappear in another room…one of Kris’ bodyguard sneak behind Kyo…Kyo kill the man with a knife to the head. He picks up his guns…run after Kris and Yosho. Kyo reaches the door…the man in red jump down from rafters, fire both his Desert Eagles at Kyo…Kyo flip over to a stack boxes and hug them, avoid the gunshots. Man stops…Kyo come out and fire both guns rapidly at the man…the man stop the bullets with a hand gesture, swing his hand and the bullets go back to Kyo…Kyo deflect the bullets with the Light Shield. > The man fires at Kyo…his guns glow a crimson color; the guns are thrown out of his hands. Surprise fills the man’s face. > Kyo charges and leap for the man…the man release a shockwave of power…Kyo plow through the shockwave and connect with a punch in the man’s injured eye…blood spray out of the man’s eye, he favor his eye. Kyo runs to the man and knock him back with a vicious combination of punches and kicks. The man couldn’t defend himself against the attack. Kyo yanks out a power cord nearby, wrap it around the man’s neck with a swing, pull him in and connect with multiple punches to the man’s injured. Kyo lets go…leap up and knock the man off the bridge with the Raising Outlaw…the power cord snap and a loud crack was heard…the man hang over the restaurant. > Kyo enters the restaurant’s helipad, look around and see that no helicopter. > Kyo senses someone and turn around with guns drawn…Drake knock his guns out…Kyo leap and strike Drake in the face with an elbow. Drake lunge for Kyo, pick him up and slam him down with the spinebuster. He stands over Kyo and stomp on his head…Kyo grab hold of Drake’s foot. > Kyo strikes Drake in the groin…Drake favors his area with a look of pain. Kyo flips up, strike Drake in the back of the leg…Drake drop to one knee…Kyo flip over Drake’s head and apply the Kneel and Pray submission on him. > A Russian military helicopter rises up. Kyo stare at the helicopter, watch it open fire on the roof…Kyo stand Drake and kick him over to the spray of bullets. Drake gets kill instantly multiple gunshots. > The helicopter continues it assault…Kyo grab his guns and avoid the bullets with a dive to the ventilation shaft. Helicopter stops…Kyo come out with guns drawn...surprise fill his face when he saw Kris stand in the helicopter with a Stinger Launcher aim at Kyo. Kris fires...the roof explode in form of a giant emerald cross. > Smoke clears…Kyo stand without his trench coat on in a dim cerulean color aura with arms cross over his face, lower his arms and glare at Kris. > Kyo’s Light Shield shorts out, he look down at his gauntlets and see them spark. > Kyo watches the missile…he lunge and leap for it…press his foot off its head and soar for the helicopter. > The pilot fires the guns…Kyo pull out his plasma sword that have a red blade and cut the copter’s whirling rotor clean off…the copter head straight down to the restaurant. > Kyo lands on the roof of a building nearby with his back turn on the helicopter and his black angel color wings spread out…the helicopter explode in impact. His wings disappear, turn around and watch the restaurant’s roof burn. > Kyo heads for the elevator…stop when he felt a familiar evil aura…a dim crimson aura shine through the back of his shirt. > Shock fills Kyo’s face. > A figure soars out of the helicopter wreckage and head straight for Kyo. Kyo snaps out of it and turn…get knock down with a strong punch to the chest. Kyo coughs and favor his chest, look up, see a mutilated Kris stand over him. > Kyo flips up and come at Kris with a stiff arm clothesline…Kris grab Kyo by the arm and throw him down on the concrete hard. Kris stands Kyo, apply a tight bear hug on him. Kyo dislocate his right shoulder with a yell of pain, slip out of Kris’ grip and fly up in the air. > Kyo brings Kris down to the ground with the hand to the face…Kris struggle to move the hot hand off his face. > Kyo stands and head over to a sidewall with his right arm limp…brace himself, ram the shoulder on the wall with a yell and laugh. He leans on the wall, go to a grab a smoke from his trench coat and remember that it was blown away. Kyo sighs softly and continue to rest on the wall…Kris’ headless corpse stand and face Kyo’s back. Thick black color liquid oozes out from the neck…it lunge for Kyo…it mysteriously stop and struggle to move. Kyo turns and face the ooze with a smirk. > Germany police and the corners are gathered outside of The Soviet Lounge. Corners speak to each other in Germany and say that they might need more trucks for the bodies. Two police officers put the tied up twins in a police car. Kyo Kushinaga was there too, tell the officer in charge what happened. Kyo stops…see a corner wheel Yosho’s bodyguard in a black bag with his face out. He place the man in the back of the truck, close the doors and enter the truck…a muffle scream was head. Kyo looks at the truck…the doors bust open with the corner laid out dead in blood…the man in red soar out with an fire axe in hand. The officers are in shock. > Kyo pulls the triggers…hear click sound from both guns. > The man swings the axe at Kyo…Kyo dodge the man’s assault. Kyo flips away from the man in a corkscrew maneuver, land on one knee and go to grab a clip…remember that the clips were in his trench coat. Disgust fills Kyo’s face. The man swings the axe down at Kyo…Kyo rolls out of the way. Kyo leaps up and strike the man in the throat with a jump kick…the man catch his bearings and continue his violent assault on Kyo…Kyo slip his attack. > Kyo look over at Kayin, see Kayin drive in the crime scene and throw a clip over to him. > Kyo grabs the clip in a flip, slide it in the magnum and land behind the man. The man turn and swing at Kyo’s head…Kyo lean back, spin around and knock him the air with a strong kick to the chest. Kyo aims the gun at the man. > The man’s back explodes many times in blood…Kyo continue to fire until the gun gone empty. The man spits up blood and lay on the ground, dead…blood ooze out. Police officers surround the corpse and their guns at him. > Kyo enters the huddle, glare down at the man…grab the officer’s handgun and fire a single shot to the man’s head. The other officers jump in fright. > Kyo stops…look over at the officer. > Kayin stops and stare at Kyo. > Kayin shakes his head with a laugh and drive away in the opposite direction. Kyo stops…look up at the night sky and think about that voice. > A black helicopter leaves the country of Germany, Yosho sit inside and look out the window with arms cross…there’s another figure in the helicopter who sit in the shadows. Twin crimson color orbs appear on the figure…the scene fade to black. > To be continued
