An Open Letter to Darek DeAngelo

13 September 2004

An Open Letter to Darek DeAngelo Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #966 Date: 09/13/04 Location: An Open Letter to Darek DeAngelo Just when I think I've watched the stupidest promo that Kyo had ever done, you go and do one that is twice as bad. Yours is an orgy of stultifying cacophonous verbal depravity; an exercise in impotence, and an offense to all of good taste and decency. Here's a tip: no one will ever know that you've had a lobotomy if you wear a wig to hide to the scars; stop talking! If ignorance were a disability, you'd get the full pension. A long period of you not being on the telly would be most welcome on your part. You bring to mind a quote from Josh Billing: 'Doesn't know much, but leads the league in nostril hair.' Calling everyone dull is a gross underestimation of just how tedious you are. You have the personality of a damp sponge and the appeal of a moldy sweat sock. Genius does what it must, talent does what it can, and you had best do what you're told, you dyslexic lobotomy patient. Maybe you wouldn't read like such a pathetic loser if you didn't eat all those paint chips and lead pencils when you were a kid; if the world didn't find you so annoying, then most of us would wish the Bungee cord would snap off of forcing you to land face first on a rock in the middle of the Sumida River, or if you didn't have a face so ugly that your Psychiatrist makes you lie face down. I think it would be best that you just go away Darek. Go find a monistary somewhere and hide from the world. You're better off serving your worthless God then trying to be a stable person in the world. Your instability is already costing you your granddaughter. No court in this world would put her with someone who can't seem to make up his mind if he's a dead soul or a man of the cloth. I know for a fact she'd be much happier curled up next to me and her future father in Sean McClancy. That gets to you doesn't it? The fact that Raven, yer own flesh and blood, would rather be us then with you. You know why that is? It's because she wants to be with someone who loves her and spends time with her. Something you've failed to do since that fateful day when you dropped her off in that lonely orphanage. If it hadn't been for me, she would have been in some home that is much worse then where she is. At least with me, she is loved and adored. Is it eating away at you, Darek? That your granddaughter is finding a loving home with the woman you can't stand to be around anymore? The woman you drove all the way to Phoenix, Arizona to beg her to take care of Raven...to protect her from trouble? It has got to be killing you. While your at it, think about this...Raven Amanda McClancy...has a nice ring to it doesn't it? Maybe, just maybe I'll use this as leverage when I'm forcing you and that weak pathetic full known as Kyo to your knees, making you both beg at the one you both should still be worshiping. I can't help it that the two of you are misguided individuals that need to be taught to respect the position of being world champion. In fact, I do believe I've waisted enough time on you and Kyo that I'm just going to simply ask this of you....In the future, please make sure that the hamster in your brain is running at full speed before you up and decide to talk. It saves the rest of us on the roster the head ache of trying to decipher your ramblings. Sincerely, Patti Anderson Your North Wind Champion *insults are the result of http://www.insultmonger.com
