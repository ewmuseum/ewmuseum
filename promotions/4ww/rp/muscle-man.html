Muscle Man Syxx Figures 4WW Exposures #73 Date: 8/3/03 Location: There .. The view begins with a free floating camera that is flying slowly through the front doors of the workout gym. As it passes by the lounge and Members counter, you can see several muscle bound giants walking by. The camera floats into the main facility where there are about 30 men and women along with several dozen large and small workout machines. It slowly floats past the many gym members and heads down a small hallway. The camera comes to a large door that turns out to be a freight elevator. It goes down without much of a sound and opens to a large empty warehouse section underneath the gym. At the far side of the large room are several lights and a few people moving about. The camera doesn't take long to move across and zoom in on the small group. The group is in a large semi-circle and their attention is focused on a single person. That single person is military pressing 350lbs. Sweat is dripping down his toned flesh as the small crowd cheers him on. The camera pulls back a second and reveals the man as none other then Syxx Figures! Syxx pumps the 350lbs up to his waist and then strains a few seconds just before he hammers it above his head. His muscles are exploding as a smile spreads across his face. 'This federation has been twirling it's collective finger in a glass of water for months and it's about time they started filling that glass with something more interesting and a lot less boring! No one cares about the current North Wind Champ and who is the South Wind Champ? Anyone interesting? That's right, can't even think of who they are can you? That's the first sign of the coming doom! It always starts in the passive mode, but soon it spreads and before you know it, you are the North Wind Champ! Wait, Patti is the South Wind champ too, that's right, no one cares. Fucking females.' Syxx pulls the bar back down to his waist as more sweat trickles across his chin. His face is contorted in anger and pain as he hammers the bar back up over his head! His posse cheers him on. Syxx drops the bar and walks over to a chair near the wall. He grabs a towel and wipes his face. He looks in the small wall mirror as he ignores the comments from his close friends. 'It shouldn't be too long now. I'll destroy Mr. Jay Phoenix and take care of business! A few more steps and everyone in Four Winds Wrestling will finally realize that I am the true Superstar! Not these other losers! No more Shocker‘s, No more Patti Anderson‘s, No more Pajaro De Pueblo ala chillidawg‘s, and especially no more Jay freaking Phoenix!' The cameras pull back again and zooms around the small crowd. Syxx smiles as he stares into the free floating camera and gives a wink. ***** Even though 'Syxx' was an extension of Cade Candler, with the volume turned way up, it was safe to say he was in 'Syxx' mode, completely. His hair was soaked, bright blue, and occasionally dripped all over his 'Improve Your Image, Be Seen With Syxx.' t-shirt. He wasn’t nervous he couldn’t afford to be. In Four Winds, the roster was made up of a bunch of unproven, young, wrestlers, who believed their own hype. To them Syxx Figures was 'it.' Sure, they’d never admit it, but, everybody stepped their game up when they had a match against Syxx, or felt they had something to prove to him, either by insulting him in front of a live audience, in totally unnecessary situations, or by trying to test him backstage. He couldn’t afford to be nervous; because if he was nervous, chances are he’d slip up and they would be waiting with pointed fingers, to laugh and capitalize. Such hypocrites, going around doing tedious little chores to make themselves look more angelic then everyone else, while we all live life like a human should, not really worrying about mundane activities that ruin the mind with new problems about everyday. But I go out, I do. I go out and I learn. Learn you say? How are you going to learn by going out? Simple, learning is something we all should do. Like Jay, he should learn, learn to live life without worries. Worries of impressing people, you see, I don’t worry. It’s genetically impossible for people NOT to be impressed by me. See, now whether I wrestle tonight, cut a promo, or just walk through the curtains and give the fans the bird, people are going to pay attention. People are going to notice. I made the mistake of checking out a wrestling forum board, where all the lowbrow smarks get together and bitch and how unfair it is that they’re not hanging out where true superstars, like myself, do. Anyhow, while sifting through all the self-pity and whining, I find out that people think the Four Winds Wrestling is in a slump. They feel that some people aren’t pulling their weight, and that they’re getting a free ride. I know what would make the smarks happy. Soon, very soon, I’ll give it to them. Syxx Figures - not only the best thing to grace the face of professional wrestling, but the Four Winds Wrestling North Wind Championship. It’s not a prediction. It’s not a promise. It’s a scientific fact. Evolution, if you will. I’ve always stayed on the cutting edge, physically as well as mentally. I’m in peak physical condition; in fact I was supposed to be on the cover of Flex Magazine, but someone pulled some strings and banana beak stole my spot. He’ll pay for that. Mentally, I’m always in the game. I study tapes, pick up new strategies, and even learn a trick or two in the process. I even check out talent in some of the smaller promotions and rip them off if I see something I can use. Hey, it’s not like people will ever see them do the move, will they? I’m always changing something. I never let things get stale or predictable. In short, I evolve. Jay Phoenix has had it easy so far. I’ve been splitting my time between beating him up and slapping some sense into the rest of the Four Winds Wrestling roster. But now that the cameras are starting to roll, I’m going to show the World that the North Wind Champion, Jay Phoenix, is up for extinction. He’s not in my league, and he knows it. Everybody on the roster is gunning for my spot, because Jay Phoenix is a very beatable champion. No one can deny the impact I’ve made in Four Winds Wrestling since I got here. People set records, I broke them. People set standards, I raised the bar. I am the measuring stick of the Four Winds Wrestling. Phoenix doesn’t measure up. The Four Winds trophy is going on my mantle, sooner or later. And there’s only two things Phoenix, Childress, or anyone can do about it: Nothing and Like it.
