Discovering the Truth Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #167 Date: 09/27/03 Location: Patti's Quiet Room Discover - verb - 1. To make known or visible. 2. To obtain sight or knowledge of for the first time. Moses will soon find out that. For Destiny is going to allow Moses to discover the truth. To take on the best in the league, you have to think somewhere in your brain that you deserve a chance to face them. A very rare tag team. One fueled by desire and destiny. And Moses thinks he has what it takes to defeat them. What in the world is he thinking of bringing in a mystery partner? Is he afraid that his secret will be discovered? Is he afraid that both Patti and Sean won’t take him seriously should he bring a surprise in as his tag partner. So the two are left facing someone that should be the number one contender for a title that Patti herself once held. Oh well, hopefully he’s smarter then Annie Leanan and Sean Summers. Oh wait, that’s not possible. These two need to discover what they really need. Each other would be nice, but destiny doesn’t quite think that Eliza would be happy sharing her man with Annie. Hmm, maybe Annie would be good with Moses. He spent time in jail for murder. Granted he thinks he’s a bigger bad ass then McClancy so it’s a possible match made in heaven. Moses can always use someone strong in his corner. Maybe a mother figure. Annie would be perfect for that. She’s got the desire to be a mother, let her take some misguided youth in under her wing. But it still leaves something to be said on it all though. Can Moses and the mystery partner co-exist? What will happen if the mystery partner is revealed to be Annie? How would Sean cope with having to face the woman his family expects him to marry? What will happen? Why does Fey feel the need to constantly put our heroine into matches that either will harm her in some way or attempt to knock her down a few notches. Is she really afraid that Patti will become someone that she was once was? Or is that she knows that her brother is really in love with this woman, and that her gaining her as a sister-in-law is a threat? Destiny thinks its just a plot. A way of testing just how strong Patti is. Her mental and physical abilities are being tested every day. Soon it will take its toll on the weary warrior from down under, and once all the blood, sweat, tears, pain, heartache, and happiness. She believes that Patti will walk away stronger then before, with a new found appreciation of what has happened in her life. For she knows must be done, and what the battle brings forward. Discoveries will be made. Questions will be answered, and Destiny will finally get her rest. ______________________________________________________________________________ Time: Less then 24 hours before Storm Front Place: Patti’s Quiet Place ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti Anderson sat in her quiet place. A tiny room, not much bigger then a closet, barely big enough to hold the small tv/VCR combo that is seen flickering, and the chair she’s sitting in. Her long legs dangling over the arms. She’s dressed in her comfort outfit for the night. Normally she borrows one of Sean’s shirts, but not tonight. This shirt is special to her. She snuck into his hotel room and stole it. Along with his favorite pair of sweat pants and a pair of his underwear. At the time she needed all she could of him. The infection had taken its toll on her from that first night they had met. A half empty carton of Ben and Jerry’s Chubby Hubby at her side. Her ‘shovel’ in it. She continued to stare at the tape replaying in the VCR, her mind not really focused. Just another tape from the people at TaleSmith Productions. Her request for a copy of last weeks show had been granted. She wanted a chance to study this Moses character. Her family back home had said that something was said that may help her with her conquest against her opponent. A quick phone call to Eliza, and the tape was in her hands rather quickly. Thank god for friend within the federation that can do that for her. Her mind was already in a whirlwind on what was going on. But she needed to keep her focus on what was at hand, and not on other things. She reached down and grabbed the container of ice cream. Her vision never really wavering from the picture she was looking at. The vision before her showed a tall black male, really masculine, and from the looks of it, really, really buff. A Lennox Lewis type wanna-be she guessed. She shook her head at the scene before. He stood in the ring, almost like he owned it. A microphone in his hand. TV MOSES: The one thing I have noticed about this fed, is the women seem to be running things around here. Well I won't be the one to step on toes, but I think I need to make it clear, once I make my way to this so called nightmare title, I plan to hover. I don't want shots at the feds gold yet. I don't want to be the one to face the wrath of all these damn crazy assed women around these parts. Patti: What the bloody hell? Crazy assed women? He better be talking about those dyo trelos Sheila’s in the form of Annie and Fey. If he did his homework, he’d realize that the South Wind Title became the ‘NIGHTMARE Title’ after they did a screw job on me. I held that belt with honor and pride for over a month, taking on everyone that wanted a shot. But if he thinks I’m going to be the crazy one this week, then he just needs to re-think his theory. Patti dug back into her half eaten pint of ice cream. When will these guys learn that you can’t just think that she’s some soft little thing that can be easily over taken. All of her hard work should have proven that she isn’t meek and mild. Her training by the ‘Irish Bastard’ didn’t leave much room for meek and mild. Patti’s cell phone, quietly started its chirping, bringing more sound to the already semi quiet room. The caller identification blinked that she had an incoming call. She squinted at it and looked, an unavailable call. It was either someone that mis dialed her number, or someone from home was trying to get her. She annoyingly flipped it open. Patti: Hello? A woman on the other end softly spoke up. Woman: Is this Ms. Patti Anderson? Patti: Yes it is. What can I do for you? Woman: Oh good, I was so afraid couldn’t go through with this, but there is something I really need to discuss with you. Patti sighed with a little less enthusiasm. It must be someone that is wanting to sell her something or another offer to do a public appearance. Woman: You don’t know me, but I have some information that pertains to your past. You see, Ms. Anderson, you are not who you believe who are you. Patti: Go on. She hears the woman take a deep, as if to pause to brace for the news. Woman: You may not believe me, but I am your mother. Patti gasps, as if the wind had been knocked out of her. Tears well up in her hazel eyes. Anger starts rising in her voice. Patti: I don’t know what sick joke you are trying to pull, but my mother is alive and well in a mental facility in Melbourne. She has been there for years. Woman: Please here me out. She is not your biological mother. Your father is Minos Anderson, correct? Patti: Yes he is. Woman: About 25 years ago, I met your father. I was a woman, who was down on her luck. I had a job working as a barmaid. I met him in a bar in Chicago, Illinois. Sparks flew, and the next thing I knew, he was gone and I was left pregnant with a child of his blood. Patti remains silent. Her world as she knew it was completely destroyed. Silent tears covering the anger she was feeling. Woman: Because of this, I signed my rights as your mother over to your father. I couldn’t afford to take care of you because of being sixteen at the time. My family frowned on it, and I did what was best for you. Patti: YOU LIE!! You are just after the fame that is attached to my name. This bloody well goes on in Australia all the time. Woman: Since you seem to not want to believe me. Call your father, he will confirm my story, But before I leave you, be nice to your brother, for he is not WHO you think he is. The line goes dead. Patti slams her cell phone down hard. The force of impact shatters the phone. Sean hears the outburst and comes into the room. He sees Patti crying. He rushes to her side, and quickly wraps his arms. Fade To Black
