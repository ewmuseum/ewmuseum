Retribution Is At Hand Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #382 Date: 12/11/03 Location: Halekailani Beach House Retribution - noun - 1) recompense, reward. 2) the dispensing or receiving of reward or punishment especially in the hereafter. 3) something given or exacted in recompense especially: PUNISHMENT. Retribution. The one thing that everyone desires. The one thing that everyone gets. The one thing that Patti Anderson deserves. It has been months since Annie Leanan has made her appearance in the federation that Patti calls home. Why after 5 years did she feel the need to attempt to retrieve something that she never really had in the first place? Annie had been out of the picture for long enough to know that Sean was done and over with her. It’s time to pack your bags Annie and move on down the road. Sean has told you to your face several times now that he doesn’t want you, nor does he need you. You no longer exist to him. A nameless face in the crowd of women that he has slept with on his path to finding the love of his life. Please just grow up and move on, its pathetic how you keep pining over someone. It’s time to wake up and realize that Sean McClancy doesn’t love you. He loves someone else. He loves Patti Anderson. Fate and Destiny both saw it, the gods foreseen it, and plotted the courses that would put both of them on to the right path. But somewhere along the pathway to finding the happiness they deserved, now you have felt the desire to make it impossible for two people so much in love to find the happiness they want. You saw fit to cost Patti her chance at the North Wind Title at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. You saw fit to inject yourself into a match that you had no reason to be a part of. You were to be paid to just stand at ringside and look pretty, and cheer on that chump, Todd Dream. You just couldn’t be happy with that role. You are so damn lucky the referee wasn’t paying any attention, for if he was, Todd would have lost the match via disqualification and you more then likely would have been tossed to the streets because of your evil manipulating ways. But, the highlight of it all is that you were officially stupid enough to go and talk to Fey about a match against me. Believe me you stupid bitch, I was content with facing Todd Dream once more at the pay-per-view for the title I should have won, but obviously, Miss Bitch, you had other plans. So now it finally come down to the retribution that I, as Sean McClancy’s girlfriend/valet, has been given. I finally get your worthless ass into the ring and show you WHY he fell in love with someone that his entire clan said couldn’t happen. I am more then willing to beat your worthless, pathetic, liposuction, badly in need of breast implants, scrawnier then hell body all over the ring. I’ve had it with your constant interference in everything I and Sean have attempted to do. Your petty jealousies and this “ME” attitude that has possessed you is just down right ugly, but then again, with a face like that, I’m suprised the mirrors in the locker rooms and restrooms have managed to stay in one piece. This is the end Annie, this is the end to your desire for Sean McClancy. This is the end for your career in the Four Winds. This is the end of everything that you have come to know and want, for it shall no longer be yours. For he and the career will be all mine. And you will be left with nothing. Place: Halekailani Beach House Time: Near Evening ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti Anderson stepped outside the new home that she and her boyfriend/manager had rented for the week. She stared out over the beautiful ocean view. The beautiful mint green sarong clung to her well built body. Her feet bare underneath it, her toes digging into the warm white sands. Her long red copper hair flowed lose for a change. A beautiful white flower rested over her right ear. Patti signed as she was reminded about her home back in Australia. She continued to stare out over the ocean. A little less than three thousand miles separated her and what was left of her family. Her father was still not happy with her decisions to continue with the lifestyle of a wrestler, but as long as she was happy, he was happy. She was pretty sure that he was less then sure that he was thrilled with the fact that she was now engaged to the same man that had broken her heart a few months prior to her coming stateside permanently. Now that’s just assuming that she manages to keep hold of him. Annie had was slowly becoming a furious hurricane that wasn’t buying the clues that the damage she was causing was causing problems to everyone around her. Patti knew subconsciously that Fey and Ian were getting to the point that they couldn’t stand being in the same room with Annie, let alone in the same building. Of course, Patti couldn’t stand Annie the minute she decided to stick her nose back into Sean’s life. Patti continued her walked out past the house and on down the beach, completely lost in thought as to the match up that she had signed for Maelstrom. She knew the deck was partially stacked against her with Fey being the special guest referee for the match. Fey had made it known that she didn’t like her, and would do anything to get her out of Sean’s life. Fey had never really had forgiven her brother for forgetting that he was engaged to another woman while he cavorted with other women. She just figured that it was his way of sowing the wild oats that most men did before they settled into the state of getting married. But then again it was Karma coming back to smack her in the face for her sneaking around behind Sean’s back with Ian after Xian had died. Patti walked until she came to a rocky incline and sat down. The skirt of her sarong flowing around her long legs. Her well manicured toes poking out from underneath. Her knees coming up to rest under her chin. Her lost in thought look crossing her face. She continued to stare out over the ocean, the waves crashing into the rocks just mere inches below her feet, the salty spray hitting her in the face, slightly dampening her hair that framed her angelic face. Patti just continued to stare, her mind not completely sure of what was going to happen at Maelstrom II in a few days. She prayed silently that she would find the strength to battle not only Annie in the ring, but the officiating of Fey. She continued to sit and watch the waves lapping at the rocks. Her mind full of every thought and every possible thing that could happen at the card and then some. Fade Out Fade Back In Several hours had passed and Patti still remained seated on her rock staring out at the ocean. A shadowy figure appears behind her. His red hair damp from the sweat of looking for her. Sean McClancy had practically torn up the beach looking for her. He never thought to realize that she may have found a quiet secluded spot to sit and think about this match. He knew that everything was riding on her winning this match. He put her through a serious training regime in the few days before the trip to Hawaii. He wanted her at top phsyical condition, the mental part of her conditioning was something she would have to deal with. Sean walked up behind and gently laid his hand on Patti’s shoulder. The sudden warm touch on her cool bare skin startled her. She turned her head slightly and stared at Sean. A smile came to her face as she stared at the man that the battle was about to be raged over. Sean’s other hand reached out and helped Patti up off the rock. Her sarong was now almost soaked from the incoming waves. He noticed that she seemed perfectly content as it was. He figured she must have made some sort of peace within herself. She had a glow around her that showed she had achieved the state of mental and physical tolerance. The two slowly started the walk back to the beach house. Somewhere along the way, the two stopped and embraced as the sun can be seen setting behind them, ending a rather emotional day for one.
