Chrysalis, Part 1 Blaise 4WW Exposures #1157 Date: 1/27/05 Location: Munich, Germany Chrysalis, part 1 Putting the pieces together. I know the pieces fit cuz I watched them fall away Mildewed and smoldering, fundamental differing, Pure intention juxtaposed will set two lovers souls in motion Disintegrating as it goes testing our communication The light that fueled our fire then has burned a hole between us so We cannot see to reach an end crippling our communication. -”Schism” by Tool Have you ever felt like you were a minor player in the world of people playing just a big game of chess? Have you ever felt like others were hiding things from you? Have you ever felt like you've just not even grasped the big scheme of things around you? If you think you've got those feelings, just imagine being a member of the Order of the Hammer. Yeah, the KnightMare Brigade is one thing, but when you toss in it's parent organization run by immorals.... eh, Immortals... you run into the situation of... how do you fit into it all. This was one of the things that scared Blaise McClancy so long ago when he was offered to join the first time. A young man, he had graduated from their training facility in Ryu Kyu, Japan (Okinawa) and was offered to go for broke and finish his training. He looked at the Order's means and it's greatness, and how it's little fingers were entwined in so much of the world and he had balked at it. And the knowledge that there was another organization almost just like it doing almost the same things... that made it even worse. The world's goverments don't even know who they're actually dealing with anymore. Is it a servant of Ishram Ableson, or a servant of Cain and his Methuselah children? And the rumors of a possible third party getting involved. it was almost too much to take. Especially when Blaise's big question in it all was: Where do I fit in? He'd seen it all when he was going around as Ishram's chosen assassin for the game between Tarrant and the Arch Angel Zadikiel. Since then he's really not felt to much in control. Yes, he's got his girl now, Ukyo, something that is wonderful and great, but there are still things that are a mystery to him. Still things that feel like their just not for him to grasp just yet. But he wants to. It's like that carrot dangling in front of the horse drawn carriage. It's there, he knows it is, but what is his real purpose. Between it all there are the blackouts while on missions for the Order as a Hunter. Ukyo's told him that she's guessed it comes from his grandfather, old William Slyfox the Paiute Elder. But what exactly was it? He'd heard the talk of others, but no one gave a clue as to what exactly was the reasoning. Childress and his damned secrets! But it always came down to the bouts of sleeplessness that Blaise had faced for many years. All to many for him to remember. It always started with a dream, a dream that starred him. A dream where he had to make a choice between himself and another, one that he couldn't see in the dream. He had to choose his life or theirs.... He always chose his own to be forfeit, but it's there that the dream would progress into redness all around, red, the color of blood. Usually it ended there, and then he'd wake up and not get to sleep again for a few days. But tonight was different. As he and Ukyo had arrived in Munich for the pre-show build up, after getting the last week off to recover from taking care of his twin daughters over the holidays, he had found himself not only on the receiving end of a fine from the run amock commissioner, Mr. Todd Roark, better known as Todd Dream, but also a bout with the dream again. But he fell back to sleep afterwards. And tonight he fell asleep again, but he had it a second night in a row. Last nights it had been Patti that had been the one he had to choose between himself and them. Tonight's though, was rough. For he was given the choice by the ghost like entity of Ukyo or him. It wasn't like Blaise would answer any differently then he always did. But the thought of someone ever wanting to take her from him was too much right now. She had been there for the entire Holiday break to help him take care of Kaylie and Kiley, his 18 month old daughters, twins, from his first lover. And the two girls had taken to Ukyo like they belonged together. It was almost disheartening to him to see it, for he knew what he wanted, but also knew it was too early. Blaise had woken from the dream and laid there in bed for a about an hour. Ukyo's head on his chest, her blue dyed hair cascading down across the blanket and his abdomen. He sometimes woke up and just watched her sleeping there, peaceful. While he wondered if he had madea mistake and possibly brought her into danger. For many years he had avoided the Order, the Courts and everything. Then again, Ukyo had made the move on him. He had not persued her. Although it was what he had wanted. She moved slightly and he saw his opportunity to slide out from under her and not wake her up. He had to move her slightly, but he managed to put her head gently onto the pillows and then climb out of bed. He slid on a pair of loose fitting shorts, almost cut off sweat pants before standing fully from his side of the bed. Walking over to the dresser he picked up a piece of paper, an official letter that had been delivered from 'Commissioner' Todd Dream. The same piece of paper that had informed Blaise that while he was on approved leave time last week, he was being fined $10,000 for not showing up to StormFront last week. He had been in Seattle last week, picking out a new home and spending time with his daughets and Ukyo. No, this didn't sit well with Blaise at all. “Bastard thinks he runs the place. He's only the damn commissioner. He's no one I need to listen to. That's for damn sure.” He tossed the papers back down and then opened the top drawer of the dresser, the one he had taken. He slid it out slowly so as to not make much more then a grating sound as wood slid on top of wood. Inside of it, he grabbed a bag of green velvet and pulled the small case out of it. Opening the case he pulls out it's contents... a ring, very old and very expensive. He rolls it in his hands for a few moments, and then puts the ring back into it's case, and slips it home into the drawer, in the bag. And the drawer shuts, he's startled by Ukyo speaking to him. “I take it, that tonight is another night that you're not going to end up coming back to bed?” He turned and looked over at his shoulder to her, thanking whatever for her timing and hopefully not seeing what he was just messing with. He didn't want to scare her, even though if, no when, he asked her, she'd not be the first to wear that ring. The time just wasn't right yet. “Sorry, just not my night for sleeping.” “You had the dream again? Or is it that horrible German food coming back at you?” “You know me too well, my dearest. No, it was something that happened when I was sleeping. I guess 3 hours will have to do.” She was laying there on her front, leaning over to his side of the bed. Her hair cascading down about her face, tussled from her slumber. “Damnit, going to need to look into getting you looked at for this.” “I don't think it's anything that a regular doctor can handle.” She sat up, pulling the blankets over her naked form as she did, to keep the warmth in. “What do you think it is?” He frowned... they'd only talked about it for a little bit since she told him that she had a guess as to what was going on with him, but the forthcoming days with his daughters and then their time with them, kept it from coming out fully. “I don't know. I really don't. All I know is that when I go out on a mission, sometimes I black out. I'm moving, I'm doing things, and we end up leaving with the mission successful, but I don't remember a damned thing of it.” “I told you who to contact though. Your grandfather, Billy. I think he knows more then he lets on.” “Kinda hard, I tried calling home and it seems he's taken a little trip up to Canada of all places in the Winter. He won't be back until March according to dad. Said he goes up there this time of the year, every year.” “That's strange, who in their right mind would go to Canada during the winer?” “A crazy old coot, that's who. You've not told me all of what you think it is though.” Ukyo sighed. “I've told you. I saw it that night in the alley. You look almost feral. You growl at people, especially your enemies. You appear to almost go animalistic with them, but still able to control your facilities. And, you appear to get stronger. And I might add, it's pretty much showing to be linked directly to when there is adrenaline at play, lots of it. Not just a normal rush, but downright full on adrenaline surge.” She smiled at him. He almost blushed. “Yes, you've even begun to go on the verge of this when other adrenaline surges are in play. Not as often, but it's right there, on the edge.” “Well if it's any consolation in that case, I've never got to black out at that time....” She just smiled at him more. “I don't know, I just would like some bloody answers for once. And the rest of the stress with Dream's little crusade and the other shit... I don't know if I can take much more before I just start to crack, even in the ring.” Ukyo's smile went to a slight frown. She'd heard stories from Charlene about some of things that he'd done in the ring before when pushed. Then again she'd witnessed his days as the enigmatic Shoudou. She knew what he was capable of. Ukyo reached over onto the side of the bed and pulled up one of Blaise's rock T-shirts she'd taken to wearing. (What is it with women and the desire to steal the man's clothing?) She slid it over her top and then climbed out of the bed. A pair of bikini cut panties were quickly covered by a pair of jeans. “What are you doing?” “Well, you know what. I heard that Mr. Childress was actually here in Munich. I know the Order is here. In fact the Black Lodge has a faction here. They're home is the Germanic countries. So you know what, how about we go down there and do something they won't expect.” “What's that?” “We ask them what's going on.” Blaise stumbled on the words... “They've never had anyone actually ask. They've always had soldiers. But I'm not here to be a mindless zombie of a soldier. I want to know know just what the....” “It won't work.” She stopped. “Why not?” “Look at Ian's title in the Order... The Keeper of Secrets. He doesn't just spout them off. You play their games and when it's done, you hope you undestand it.” “I think that's just a facade. I think if you actually ask, they'll have no options. Remember, Mr. Childress was groomed to take the position he's in, But remember, you showed me the tapes of his old wrestling days. His motto was that he was an honest and truthful man. 'The Truth Hurts', remember?” Blaise struck a bit of recognition to his mentor's old catch phrase. “Okay, not like we're going to get back to sleep anytime soon. Let's head down to the Order's Base of Operations here and go get this taken care of. I'm not going to get any rest unless I get one thing off my chest. And that would either be Dream or this crap.” Ukyo opened up the dresser drawer next to the one he kept the ring in, and pulled out some clothes for him quickly before heading to the bathroom herself. As he grabbed the clothes to get dressed to leave in the winter night, he just shook his head as he watched her intently from behind. *********** A few hours later, deep in the bowels of an old building outside of Munich. *********** We now find Blaise and Ukyo walking down a hallway, white walls covered in modern plaster. Something they'd not expect on the basement under an old stone catholic church that appears to have seen better days. But it's been a few hours since getting here, and the look on Blaise's face is one of malcontent. They've found no one to talk to, and for some reason they're personal ID codes aren't letting them get more then 3 levels down, even though there is at least one more listed on the elevator controls. That one needs a special access, and it's off a fingerprint to activate it. “I thought these places were supposed to be manned 24/7. This is ridiculous.” “That's what I thought too, but I think they knew you were coming.” “Thanks a lot.” **Crack** Blaise stopped and spun around, he reached behind his leather jacket to the holsters that he kept on the back of his belt for occasions like this. Ukyo turned the otherway, taking up a spot on his back... the teachings of being a Hunter coming through, she flipped her wrists and the two ASP batons she was carrying came out, only to be folded up under her forearms in a traditional martial arts method. “Who's there? Identify yourself!” “I wish I had dressed in my gear. The goggles would have been rather helpful right now.” The began to circle, back to back, so they could survey the area properly. “My, my, look at wha' we be' 'avin' 'ere. Ye put us on a scare, would 'ave been 'elpful if ye would 'ave called in tha' ye were comin'. Maybe we would 'a made ye a cake.” They knew who it was by her accent... the former girlfriend and manager of Sean McClancy, Aine “Annie” Leanan. “So, they send out a spider to see the flys?” Blaise gave her a smirk, but he kept his soul knives holstered, with his hands on their handles. “No, but I can tell you that The Keeper is not ready to see you tonight. Come back later. We weren't expecting visitors, so you put the whole compound on alert when you came in. These are getting to be dangerous times as of late.” Blaise remembered how many double agents he'd uncovered over the last few weeks now... dangerous times indeed. Ukyo spoke up though. “Well, I think Blaise has something he needs to ask Mr. Childress, and we're up late, and not enjoying ourselves, you'd figure he'd at least be hospitable.” “I said, no. Now go home, get ready fer yer matches. We'll take care of Mr. Dream for ye behind the scenes, ye do it in front of the Television so that it's looking good.” Annie went to walk by Blaise, her mistake... He brought his hand out, soul blade in grasp, and slipped it under her chin as she walked by. Annie came to a stop. “Blaise, ye don'na want to do that...” “Wrong, I do. Why? Because I'm tired of being jerked around. And as for you, I don't see a crown on your head bigger then mine in the courts, you'll show me some damn respect. I'm here to ask questions and get answers. You're a princess of the UnSeelie Court, and an intelligence officer with the Order, you can give me some.” “I can'na give ye anything, Blaise. I only follow Orders.” Well, how about you decide to interpret those orders to see what you can tell us. This is Blaise's wellbeing here. You guys know more then you're telling. It's not like you can really be trusted, you're one that tried to undermine someone else's relationship, for your own game.” Annie just turned her eyes, the knife still at her throat. “No, I was following orders.” “What?” “Blaise, you don't get it. It's all a big game. Jus' a big game, an' we all play a part. Usually there are only two players in th' game that we deal about, us and them. But now there's us, them an' them. Get it? This is not the time. We're still working on yer reassignments as it is.” “Reassignments?” “From what to what?” Blaise and then Ukyo asked. “From th' Hunters t' a different grouping.... now, go back t' yer 'otel room, an' do whatever it is you do.” Blaise turned the blade slightly, putting the cold iron against her lower chin. “Blaise...” A small tear ran down Annie's face as the cold iron came in contact with her skin. “I don't think you understand. I'm not here to play games. I'm here to find out why I keep blacking out while on missions, and why you guys won't tell me.” “You're not ready yet, now please, drop the knife.” Blaise held his arm steady. “Damnit... Please Blaise, don't do anything stupid.” Blaise just stood there, steady. **Creak** A door opened, Ukyo turned towards it, her ASP batons ready. Out walked an oriental man, wearing a very expensive silk suit. “Leanan-San...” Blaise turned his head, and the knife went about an inch from it's previous mark. He looked at the man and his face went pale and his jaw dropped a little. “I take it you know this guy, my love?” Ukyo asked him as she went to the ready. “Yeah, don't do anything. He'll kick both our asses. Ukyo, meet my instructor from the South Wind academy, Yoshahiro Kogagawa.” “Kogagawa, as in the Yakuza clan?” The older man smiled. “You've heard of us then? Very good. But we're not yakuza, we never did the racketeering. We were just the Emperor's Royal Assassins. Leanan-san, I bring word from the Keeper... He says that maybe it is time for these two to finally find out what their lot in life is. They have asked to know, you may fill them in on the basics. Especially the side that you know more about, like the politics of it all.” Annie stared at him for a few seconds and then shook her head to try to absorb what he had just said. “Go ahead.” Blaise looked a bit leery of it all. Ukyo though... “You heard him, go ahead.” Annie sighed loudly and then motioned for Blaise to move his blade further away. “Okay, where do I start? Blaise, do ye know the status of the courts and it's rulers?” “Oberon is in charge, all I know.” “Na good. Tomorrow remind me tha' ye'll need a full breifing as t' the players. Bu' tonigh' we'll give ye the fast version. Oberon is King, yes. Bu' 'e's been King fer years, Decades, Centuries in fact. There are some who think it's time fer 'im to give it up, an' go back t' sleep for a millenia 'er something. Well, that's got a few people thinking that they can be king.” “Okay?” “But there is one that we as the Order would support getting into that spot. A man who's proven he can be king, tha' 'e's a leader. A man who was willing t' give up 'is own glory an' position fer love.” “Sean.” “Yes, th' bearer o' the Mantle o' th' former dual king, Bres Mac Elatha, we are backing in' the political struggle. Him an' 'is queen.” “Patti. But she's not...” “Na, she is. She's jus' riogh'. I 'elped prove tha'. Let me fill ye in on something, I was followin' orders when I tried t' get in th' way. Ye se, many years ago, it was true tha' Sean an' I were t' be married, bu' we found it jus' would'na work between us. We did'na like th' idea o' the arranged part o' the marriage as it was. So Sean went off galavantin' around, an' screwin' everythin' that was female an' 'uman tha' 'e could find. Now, I won'na deny it, I loved 'im, an' still do. Bu' we're way different people. Well, something happened one day, the big oaf fell fer a girl. Hard, really hard. Now, as the Order, we decided t' check up on this Patricia Andonia Anderson, and we found out some interesting things...” Blaise got a quizzical look on his face. “Like what?” “Oh, ye ready fer this one? Ye see, we found a man by th' name o' Minos Anderson. A bloody whore o' a man if there is a righ' way t' say it. Well 'e's na' wha' 'e seems.” “What then?” “The bloke is one o' th' oldest fokin' immortals on th' planet. Jus' 'e does'na always play in th' games. Usually 'e sits up on Mt. Olympus an' jus' parties it up and plays around. Well, thing is, abou' 30 or so years ago, this guy comes down, makes himself a body and then falls for a woman hard. A few of them actually, an' starts spreadin' the seed around.” “Wait...” “What?” “Mt. Olympus?” “Ye don'na get it do ye? Minos Anderson is the current Alias of th' Olympian Sky Father. Patti, Caitlin, an' Melinda. All o' them 'ave the blood of th' Olypians goin' through them.” “Okay?” “So, we found Sean was bangin' a potential goddess, an' mettin' 'is own prophecy, Bres 'imself 'ad taken a wife outside th' fomorians. So, we talk t' Minos an' 'e agrees tha' we can test Patti t' see if she's loyal t' Sean, then test 'er t' take 'er mantle. Aphrodite 'ad been missin' fer years, so Ishram found a mantle for 'er tha was compatible, th' Goddess Astarte, someone who is considered t' be a offshoot o' Aphrodite.” “So you're saying that your little bit of time being a bitch to her, was just the first test of loyalty to Sean?” “Yes. I think sometimes Sean knew it was, an' tha's why 'e did'na figh' it. When we 'ad determined tha' she was willing t' fight fer 'im, Maeb made 'er 'decision' an' I went away for a bit.” Ukyo shook her head. “She wasn't kidding that it's all a game.” “No, I wasn'na. Now, we ga' Patti tested an' she's now a Seelie Princess, and bearing the mantle of Astarte. We've ga' the Olympian's now workin' with us. I mean, ye should 'ave guessed something was up when 'er daddy sent the God Mercury t' talk t' 'er when ye follwed 'er t' Greece as Shoudou.” “I thought stomething was up, but I wasn't exactly good to go for general converation with the man.” “Now, we're vying fer Sean an' Patti t' take up the position o' King an' Queen o' the Courts. We ga' both o' ye int' th' Hunter program fer the fastest training. Now ye know how the Assassin's an' killers work. Yer next job is goin' t' opefully be... Royal Guards.” “What?” “Blaise, yer 'older o' the Mantle o' Turenn, t' Shield Man. Yer destined t' be Bres' guardian in this life. An we think Patti will be 'avin' a personal guard in Ukyo. Things are just fallin' int' place.” Annie moved closer to Mr. Kogagawa. “What about me? What do you know about me? Other then this supposed predestiny.” Annie stopped... “Your grandfather's gift to you. But you're unique Blaise. There is a reason why you're the first McClancy t' pass th' Hunter Training, you're a natural hunter.” “Some more explanation would help here!” Mr. Kogagawa started in. “Blaise, I told you this when I gave you the name of Tetsuo Oni. You are the best I've taught, for a reason. Your grandfather and I knew each other. You have his blood, you have your mothers blood. But you are unique on a factor of your Seelie blood also.” Blaise gave him a stern look. Annie picked up again. “Blaise, have you ever noticed your grandfather avoiding dogs an' such?” “Yeah, always said he was allergic to them.” “Ever see 'im wear silver jewlery like most natives?” “No...wait a minute!” Blaise's face came up with a realization. “Yes, yer grandfather is an old Wendigo as they call themselves. Heavy on 'is native blood an' tha' o' the Garou.” Ukyo looked at Blaise as his face went long. “Blaise, doncha be worryin' abou' it. Bu' let me tell ye 'ow ye are unique. Ye see, th' Garou are of a race tha' 'as many tribes. Clans an' such. Two of them were from th' lands o' the Celts. T' give ye th' short version, both o' them from the Celtic lands were wha' they called, Fey Touched. They 'ad Seelie an' Unseelie blood flowin' through their veins as well. We embraced them, as th' Order does t' those who will work with us an' na' against us.” “Okay?” “Well, one o' those tribes was wiped out thousands o' years ago. Gone, no more. Their only remains are twisted an' down right demonic in form. Ye've faced some o' them befer. Bu' tha's neither 'ere nor there. Occasionally, a throw back appears, some other members o' the blood show signs of bein' associated with this lost tribe...” “Such as me?” Annie's face went to a smile, she looked happy about it. “Yes, yes. Like ye. Ye see, yer na' only of tha' blood, bu' yer Fey Touched as well by birth. Yer more then a throwback. If we could have 20 more like ye, we'd 'ave a shoe in t' win the damn war.” “But why the blackouts. It's not like it's the full moon.” “Actually you were born unde the Gibbious moon, and thus you're not in it's cycle.” Kogagawa interjected. “But, it's not like that' makes for the time of the change. You can learn to control it. Right now it's linked to your most primal emotions... lust and anger. And lord knows you McClancy's have some anger in you.” “You can say that again...” Ukyo filled that in, causing a look towards her from her lover. “There, now you know it Mr. Turenn, now you know why we need you. You're unique, as is your lady friend, as we don't have many Kami under us.” “And how do I know that this is all good?” Annie shrugged. “Ye can believe what ye want Blaise. I'm only tellin' ye because I've been ordered to.” “Well, maybe we can' save them the hassle of belief. By just taking them to the Keeper and going ahead and putting them through the trial.” “The Trial?” Ukyo asked. “Yes, the trial. You two are destined for more then this. You two are destined to shape the Seelie and Unseelie Politics, you two are destined to be very high in the Order... and to get there, you must pass a test that will show how much you two are for each other, and if you can learn to control all aspects of yourself. Particularly you, Blaise... as you must adopt the side of you that is of the Tautha De Dannan, the Garou an' your training. Three aspects that will make you a man t' be feared in the courts. Come with me, both of you, we will start the trial as soon as we're in the lower levels...” He turned and went back through the door he had come in at. Annie followed him. Blaise turned his head towards Ukyo. “You wanting to reconsider telling me to come and ask them what they knew?” “Too late now. Guess now we know the reason for the paint job on your truck?” Blaise smiled. “Yeah. You up to this?” “Never hurts until you try it. Let's go.” As Blaise and Ukyo follow the others down the corridor we fade out. To Be Continued....
