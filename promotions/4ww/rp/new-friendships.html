New Friendships Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #405 Date: 12/13/03 Location: The Rental House As the days pass it seems there is just one more thing in my way. I'm Jade, a strong competent matter, and a shell that can go out and fight everyday for hours. My body couldn't be much stronger, healing, my emotive magic's and regular battles to the death all feature in my daily fun routine. Yet I am still left here feeling weaker than ever, my health gets cut away like strands of cotton from a rope. Each tiny piece accounts for a vital part of my sanity, it's slipping away from my grasp like flour from a sieve, every movement I make another bit slides. Each grain of sand being the friend I lose, the fight I lose, a life that fails when there's something I can do to prevent it, when I feel I know longer want to prevent some body's life descending from this Earth, that's where my sanity goes. Another change is to be made in my life, I shall find Patti, and I shall be more than her mere bodyguard, I will be here for her to lean on. I'm going to be a confidant; she shall not be another Lacuna Debris or Angelus Sorrow. I shall start taking back the sands time so cruelly steals from me. Yes… Trust - noun 1 a : assured reliance on the character, ability, strength, or truth of someone or something b : one in which confidence is placed 2 a : dependence on something future or contingent : 1. HOPE b : reliance on future payment for property (as merchandise) delivered : CREDIT 3 a : a property interest held by one person for the benefit of another b : a combination of firms or corporations formed by a legal agreement; especially : one that reduces or threatens to reduce competition 4 archaic : TRUSTWORTHINESS 5 a (1) : a charge or duty imposed in faith or confidence or as a condition of some relationship Something that Patti doesn’t do or believe in much. She trusted before and got hurt. She walked out of Sean’s life at one point in time. A simple misunderstanding led to a reconciliation for the history books. Patti had once again learned to trust someone again. She felt the safety that she needed to be complete again. With the strong possibility of losing Sean to the she-witch that is Annie Leanan growing closer by the hour, Patti was in desperate need for someone she could turn to. Her best friend in the form of Eliza, was being kept busy with her duties at the arena and the tv station. Patti was allowing the stress to take over completely. After last weeks episode of Storm Front, and the appearance of Jade, Patti had someone she could turn to now...but the question is...where is Jade at? The phone call to the cell number she had was going to the voice mail and not a real person. This led Patti to believe that Jade may not be the one she can trust either. ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: The Rental Home Time: After Noon ______________________________________________________________________________ Fade In Patti had just read the fax for a third time since it had come in from Fey’s office at the arena. Sean and Jade both had been banned from ringside, which left Patti to battle not only against Annie, but battling Fey at the same time. The deck had been stacked against her and from the looks of things, Patti wasn’t going to come out the winner. She prayed that Destiny had a swift and serious plan. The stress became to much as Patti slowly slipped to the floor. All color completely drained from her face. The last sound Patti heard was a knock on the door. Jade: 'Hello?' A more insistent knock bounces off the door again as the spike covered fist scrapes down. Jade wasn't the most patient person at the best of times, when it's somebody that she is supposed to be guarding she is even more incessant that her charge, if you will, answers immediately. Jade taps the black toe of her stiletto heeled thigh high boots and grunts under her breath. Jade: 'Well, I don't care if you're in the shower Patti, I've come here to ensure your safety and that's exactly what I'm going to do.' Jades boot makes the floor creak beneath her as she takes a step back, her left leg flying into the air and shattering the door from its hinges, her legs immediately going back to work as she sees Patti laying unconscious on the floor, the only sign of damage is a large forming bruise on her head. Jade grabs Patti by the neck and relatively easily tosses Patti onto the white futon sofa that rests beneath the large extravagant window. Jade pulls out a small jar from the inside of her bra before opening it beneath her nose, Patti comes to quite violently by shoving the small jar out of her face. Jade reaches back into her black leather overcoat, hardly the attire to be wearing in Hawaii but she can look at buying something else in a while. When she releases her hands back into the air they hold a pair of pearly white porcelain pistols, expensive white leather guarding the hands from chaffing on the butt, Jade quickly has the whole building swept before she gets back down and rests next to her, judging the scene nimbly and questioningly looking down at Patti. Jade: 'Who did this to you?' Patti, still groggy from coming out of the faint, stared intently at Jade. Patti: 'That woman that we both work for now. She faxed over the change for the match.' Jade's eyes squint, trying to read just why this had affected her so severely. She begins twisting a raven strand of hair between her thumb and forefinger, almost nervously. Jade looks around for the fax, she was thinking that maybe that'd get the answer out of Patti quicker than her actually saying it. Alas there was not a spot of fax paper in the whole room. Jade twists onto the futon uncomfortably and keeps the gaze locked onto Patti as she kids her. Jade: 'So, what? Now this has changed to a 'knock your opponent clean out before your match' match?' Patti: No you bloody twit! You and Sean have both have been banned from ringside during the match. If either one of you attempts to interfere in the bloody match, Sean is lost to both of us. Patti's face crinkled in pain as she works to sit up. Her right hand flew up to her head as to try to still the throbbing feeling that always appears after a fainting spell. Patti slowly wobbled to her feet. She needed to walk it off, or the headache would still be around. The arm of the chair would suffice for the moment as a temporary fixture to help keep her balance. Jade was angry, she hated being talked to like a piece of shit and it was easily represented by Jade sitting uptight and her eyes burning a hole in Patti's back. Her right hand tenses up in the air, fighting to control the rage burning through her emotions. A medium sized flame pops up in the center of her palm, quickly introverted as Patti turns back. Jade growls and hisses out her words. Jade: 'Allow me to make one thing perfectly clear. I can be all nice and dandy, that's what I'm getting paid to do at the end of the day. But if you're going to start talkin' to me like a frickin' piece of shit, I'm gonna get nasty. I was only joking, is it really that hard to notice with your head stuck up your ass the way it is?' Patti turned and stared at Jade. Patti: Excuse me, but it wasn’t my bloody idea for you to be hired as my bodyguard now was it? Sean has decided that since Annie is close to going trelos , he’s afraid she may make an attempt at me life before the show on Sunday. That is why you are here. Patti slowly turned back around and continued her slow, painful movements back to the bathroom. She needed the medication that the Order had prescribed for her, and she needed a chance to breathe without being under the constant staring of Jade. Patti didn't need the attitude that she was giving. Patti shakily reached up, and opens the medicine cabinet. The bottles of pills stared back at her as she reached for the right one to take. She slowly filled the glass as she stared at the bruise that was centered over her right eye. This is just another thing for her to make Annie pay for. Patti wrinkled her nose as the after taste of chalk dust filled her mouth. She turned around slowly, only to be staring right back at Jade. A flash of her obstinate nature flows through her hazel eyes, as they suddenly become a whole lot more watery. Jade slams her fist into the wall, allowing her silver studded spikes to make an imprint before turning on her heel. Jade scoops up her coat in her hands and walks towards the door. Patti calls out as the raven tresses of Jade flow quickly behind her slashing at the air in the room like a vicious whip. Jade doesn't look back as for the second time that day, her hand angrily scrapes at a doorframe. Patti: 'Where are you going?' Jade: 'Fucked if I am going to tolerate this shit. I have problems of my own to deal with.' Patti began to wish Sean was here to deal with this. Her head was still throbbing from the fall, and the medication hadn’t kicked in yet. Jade being pissed wasn’t what she needed right now. With the small frame that Patti possessed, slipped between Jade and the badly banged up door. Patti’s anger was slowly coming to the surface as she blocked the door. Patti stared into the eyes of Jade. A coldness took form in her hazel eyes as she glared at her. A quick glance at them, and one could have sworn that lighting bolts were dancing in them. Patti’s hands began to ball up into fists as she fought the urge to crumple to the ground. Her will was being tested by this woman, and she was hell bent on making sure that she would not win. Patti: Just where in the bloody blue hell do you think you are going? Jade mumbles under her breath as she turns back around, slightly interrupted by Patti, but hell bent on making her way through the door with or without her standing there, a powerful flash of light shining directly into her eyes interrupts her eye line. Jade goes wide eyed and dives backwards, her hand shooting upwards in front of Patti's face as the crimson water of life blasts all over the room. Jade collapses on top of Patti and curses as she holds tightly a hole in her hand; a car screeches away from the area, no doubt half balding the tires in the process. Patti wipes the blood from beneath her eyes and looks down at Jade who is mumbling something beneath her breath. Jade: 'Stand back, it's a clean wound, won't take two minutes to sort out with the kind of pissed off I am right now…Inflame!' Her words set off some kind of mechanism inside her body. Her arm tenses from the very utmost part to her fingers as a flicker of raw energy dances upon her hands. Patti steps back, slightly taken aback by what exactly was happening. Jade hand bursts into a fit of flame, the blood that decorates her hand so liberally begins to fall back on itself between the flames. Jade looks up from her hand as her pupils fill with the red stuff. Blood from all over the room retreats back into place, as though evaporating back to a sky, Jades hand. Patti: 'You saved my life....' Fade Out ______________________________________________________________________________ Fade In Patti and Jade sat on the floor of the home, several empty beer cans scattered all over the room. Two more cases of beer sat next to each of them. The coffee table that was covered with magazines, now was hidden underneath several empty cases of beer. The attempted assassination of either Jade or Patti’s life brought a complete understanding between the two women that Patti did need Jade’s protection. The girls burst out in a fit of giggles as they attempt to pull themselves up to the couch. Patti: I still wish you could have seen the look on Jerms face when you bit off his nose. He didn't know whether to faint or pass out. Jade: 'I didn't bite it all off. Just a lil' bit. Isn't fainting and passing out the same? You know they just don't make beer like they used to. Give me a second…' Jade gets up from the futon sofa and clumsily walks over to the door, her hands tracing along the pine walls as she does so to help keep her balance. When she gets back into the room a good friend of hers is stuck in between her tightly strung cleavage by the neck of the bottle. Jade shuffles into the room and giggles as she falls down to her knees, protected by the fine padding of the high boots she sniggers and crawls over to the sofa yanking herself up on it. Jade: 'Patti, I'd…like you to meet a friend of…mine, Jack. Jack this is Patti, Patti…Mr. Daniels.' Patti: Nice to bloody well meet you Mr. Daniels. I’m pretty sure after tonight the two of us will be good friends. Both girls break out in fits of laughter. Jade scoops the bottle from her chest and chews off the top with her super strong teeth. Jade chugs down and then dozily offer the bottle up to Patti as she looks down unsure of what to make of the liquid. Patti takes the bottle from Jade, and swallows about half of the bottle. The liquid burning down her throat, forces a coughing fit in the process. Patti: I don’t see how you American’s can drink that stuff. The bloody Aussie beer is better, but I think the Germans have everyone beat on alcohol beverages. Jade smiles as she takes the bottle back from Patti and downs more of it. The two are to the point where fits of giggles are coming more and more and the speech was really starting to slur to the point that they can barely understand each other. Patti collapses on the couch next to Jade. Patti: You do realize that Sean is going to have a bloody fit when he sees the shape the house is in. He’ll be a little upset over not being invited to join the drinking fun himself. And you know what Jade? I’ve bloody well had it with Annie. I’m tired of her holier then though attitude about me stealing what was once hers. Look lady, he may have been hers to begin with, but it obviously wasn’t meant to be between the two of ye. Instead of being the good little troll, dwarf, elf, or what the hell ever her species is, and go away, she finds it to the point of having to make my life a bloody living hell. Sean got over her the minute he stepped out side of Ireland and started his travels around the world. As from what I have learned, she never shown no real bloody desire for him until he fell in love with me. Then she flies into the states on her broom stick, whining and crying about how much she has to have Sean back into her life because its all arranged and planned. Well, ya know what, this Sunday I have her plastic, synthetic looking hair, contact wearing, steak and kidney pie eating person in the middle of the ring. I’m going to humiliate her to the point that she will wish to god she never even bloody well bothered coming to the states. I’ll send her and her banshee wailing arse clear back to Ireland. Let the Unseelee’s deal with her. I’m pretty sure they can find someone for ‘Princess’ to be with. Somewhere in the course of Patti speaking about her true feelings for Annie, Jade had passed out. Her raven hair hanging down in her face, her grip loosens on the bottle. Patti picks it up and takes another drink from it. Patti holds on to the bottle and stares out the big window. A few minutes later, and Patti is passed out. Fade Out ______________________________________________________________________________ Fade Back In The outside of the house is dark as evening has started to set in. Sean notices the door to the house is knocked off its hinges. He steps cautiously through the door. He sees cases of beer all over the living room. Beer cans, beer bottles, and several hard liquor bottles lay empty all over the living room floor. He looks over on the couch and sees two woman passed out. He smiles as he notices Patti has a half empty Jack Daniels bottle in her had. The two girls must have decided to have a little party to celebrate a new found bond, and a new partnership. Sean walked over to the couch and gently picked up Patti, making sure he grabs and finishes off the bottle. Her arms wrapped themselves around his neck subconsciously and snuggles into his chest. She mutter something incoherently into his chest. He smiles more as he carries her off to bedroom. Sean reaches the room in a few strides, and places her gently on the bed, making sure that her head hits the pillow. He notices a nasty bruise over her right eye. He shakes his head, he warned her about crushing them beer cans on her head. He covers her up and kisses her lightly on the forehead. Sean heads to the closet and grabs another blanket. He walks quietly back into the living room and throws it over Jade. Sean sets about cleaning up the mess that the two ladies had caused in his absence. He knew at least one of the girls was going to be waking up with a really bad hangover. FADE OUT *thanks to Wes (Jade’s handler) for help with this RP.
