Bedside Confessions 2nd Edition

Bedside Confessions 2nd Edition Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1230 Date: 4/15/05 Location: Hospital in England [The scene opens in a hospital somewhere in England. The hospital is busy with almost every square foot of the halls and corridors covered with people either yelling at nurses to see their loved ones, or crowded around bedsides of the patients themselves. One room in particular, however, voids all of the previous description. The room of Rustin Slade is as lonely and empty as a ghost town in mid-December. Rustin lies in the bed that has been assigned to him and stares blankly up at the ceiling.] Rustin: It’s funny. I always seem to end up here. Not here in particular, but a hospital in general. Strange how it usually happens after a match with Draven Lox or some scuffle with Lincoln Frink. [Rustin rubs his bandaged head at the name of Lincoln Frink, the reason to why he’s in the hospital again. Three vicious piledrivers onto the broken glass is the reason for the seemingly infinite wraps of gauze around Rustin’s head.] Rustin: It sure is lonely around here, especially at nights. No one’s come to visit me since I’ve been admitted. No Rob, Kristian, Paul. Not even Darek. I think it’s safe to assume that the Legends have become null and void. Chastity hasn’t even stopped in to see how I’m doing yet. The only damn thing I got is a notice from Mr. Childress telling my ass better be at next week’s show for my match against Shawn Singleton. [Rustin produces the notice with a neat sleight of hand technique that gave the effect of the notice popping out of thin air. Rustin looks at it, scoffs, and tosses it onto the hospital nightstand.] Rustin: I don’t know much about Shawn Singleton, except from what I’ve gathered from a few tapes from the 4WW archives. He’s been a former Tempest Champion, and at one time a worthy protégé to the now extinct DMC. But what about now? What does he have going for him? Just like me, he’s a has-been. Shawn, our glory days are over. Sure we still have our fans and we still have our loved moves. You with the Next Wave. Me with the Riverside Rockabye. However, one of the biggest things we both lack, Shawn, is luck! When Stormfront finally rolls around, you and me will put on a match to remember, but at what price? Take a good look Shawn, one of us could end up again right back here. And by here I mean a general hospital. Or did I mean to say a hospital in general? [Rustin clutches his head again as a pain shoots through. He clenches his teeth to suppress a cry of anguish.] Rustin: I don’t even know what I’m saying half the time anymore. It seems like I’m just getting more and more depressed. Hell, the only thing that makes me smile anymore, or have a shred of what the old Rustin used to be is... [Before Rustin can finish, Chastity Spence, 4WW West Wind Division referee knocks on the door of Rustin’s room. Rustin looks over and gives her a half smile.] Rustin: Speak of the devil! Well in this case...Speak of the Angel! [Chastity comes over to Rustin’s bedside and pulls up a chair.] Chastity: Hey there. Kind of funny you were doing this for me only a few ago, huh? Rustin: Amazing how roles get reversed, isn’t it? [Chastity gives a weak smile and gives Rustin’s hand a squeeze.] Chastity: How’s your head? [Rustin gives a gentle touch to the gauze once again, causing another sharp pain to surge through his entire body. Rustin winces, causing Chastity to squeeze his hand tighter. A smile from Rustin puts her at ease.] Rustin: (In mock Irish Accent) Still a little tender there lass. (In Sexy voice) Maybe you could kiss it and make it better? [Rustin gives off another weak smile, showing hints of sexual intent. Chastity rolls her eyes in response and gives Rustin a playful slap on his face, causing Rustin to clench his teeth once again to hold back pain. Chastity then realizes what she has done, and becomes worried.] Chastity: Oh my God! I’m so sorry. Are you ok? Rustin: Yeah, I’m fine. Nothing a few thousand more stitches can’t heal. Just be careful there Ms. Tyson, I’m not a punching bag. Chastity: I’m sorry. [Chastityy breaks down into tears and begins sobbing. Rustin frowns at the sight of Chastity, and thinks it’s something he said.] Rustin: No, it’s all right really. I was just kidding around...you didn’t hit me that hard. [Chastity regains some composure, and gives a loud sniffle.] Chastity: No, I mean I’m sorry for everything. I feel that the reason you’re in here is because of me and my stupid ex-boyfriend issues. Maybe if I just give myself up... [Rustin realizing what Chastity is saying, quickly interrupts, shaking his head.] Rustin: No! Look, Chass, it’s that G.I. Jackass’s fault that I’m here. It’s not yours! This [Rustin points to his head.] is not as bad as the feeling I’d get if I got this [Rustin points to his heart.] hurt. [Chastity smiles at Rustin.] Rustin: We’ve come a long way , haven’t we Chass? A few months ago, you were gagging at the thought of me being close to you. Chastity: What are you talking about? I still gag at that thought. [Chastity gives Rustin a wink to let him know she’s kidding. Rustin smiles.] Chastity: But I have to admit you’ve become a really good friend to me in the past month, ever since Lincoln came back into my life. Rustin: He’s going to be out of your life again soon Chass, I promise. [Chastity smiles at Rustin, and then frowns again, as she thinks to herself.] Rustin: What’s the matter? Chastity: What if he isn’t? I mean what if he’s always there? Even if you win, or if you lose at Maelstrom VII? What if he’s still there? Rustin: I don’t want think about that right now. It makes my head hurt more than it already does. [Rustin clutches his head once again. Chastity looks sadly down at the room’s floor. Rustin sees this and tries to cheer her up.] Rustin: Chastity, I can’t promise you I’m going to win. Chastity: I know that... Rustin: But I can promise you this: If I do lose, I’ll still be there for you, regardless of what Lincoln says. We’ll always be friends. (To himself) Just not as close as I’d like to be. [Chastity smiles at Rustin and gives Rustin’s hand another squeeze.] Chastity: Thanks Rustin. [Rustin closes his eyes and mediates on his upcoming match and continues to think as the heartwarming scene comes to an end. The scene fades to black with one thought looming over Rustin.] Rustin: First, I gotta get through you Singleton...
