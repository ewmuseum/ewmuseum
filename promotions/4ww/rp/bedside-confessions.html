Bedside Confessions

10 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bedside Confessions Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1197 Date: 3/10/05 Location: Vio Pallaci Hospital: Rome, Italy [Darek moves out of the way at the last second and Kyo sandwiches Chassy into the corner. Chassy falls down in a slump and Kyo falls right to her side. All of a sudden, Kris Kanton runs out of the entrance carrying a steel chair in hand….Ukyo runs to Blaise to check on him as Chassy gets taken away on a stretcher.] Vio Pallacci Hospital: Rome, Italy [The camera fades into a hospital room. The room is that of Chastity Spence, the 4WW West Wind Referee. She is lying on her bed recovering from the crushing impact of Kyo Kushinaga’s blow. Fortunately, she had only suffered a few bruised ribs. Sitting next to her bedside, however, is the always faithful and unrelenting “friend” of the referee, current West Wind Champion, Rustin Slade.] [Friend is all Rustin could himself at this point. It had been a strange roller coaster between Chastity and Slade for the past two months. Slade was usually on the receiving end of Chastity’s dislike for him. However, it seems that she has warmed up considerably to him. Rustin had become the man she loved to hate.] [Rustin holds onto the small hand of Chastity, who is currently blissfully asleep. He gently strokes the top of her hand with his other hand. To any passerby, one would think that the two are married, or at least very romantically involved, due to the fact that Rustin has been by her side the whole day since she was registered to the Vio Pallacci hospital.] Rustin: Oh Chass, why did it have to be you? I guess it’s dedication to your job, but still…If you just stayed in the back…And damn Kyo to hell! I can’t help but think though that this wasn’t his entire fault. If I had been out there to protect you… [Rustin looks down at her hand and brings it to his lip and gives it a quick kiss.] Rustin: I still don’t know what the hell I am to you. Am I a friend? A burden? A lover? Your hastiness to accept Lincoln’s challenge would prove that you have some confidence in me. But do you really like me? It’s hard to tell. [Rustin pats Chastity’s hand once again as he listens to her peaceful breathing. The doctors said she should be fully healed and recovered by next week’s Stormfront in Greece. Rustin had begun to hear rumors about his first title defense against the “Innovator of Violence” Draven Lox. Too say the least he wasn’t too happy. He had heard it was to be a brutal Japanese death match with chains replacing the normal ring ropes.] Rustin: Next week, I’m going to go through hell. But I suppose I deserve it. This is the burden of a champion. Draven, the “Innovator of Violence” wants to step up and try to take away the belt I worked so damn hard for. He wants to show how “innovative” he is by calling for a hardcore chain rope match? I didn’t know innovative and sadistic had the same meaning. It’s fine though. The “Innovator of Offense” will show him how good old fashioned mat work and true innovation through maneuvers can help win a match. If I need to go hardcore, I will. There’s no doubt about it that I’ll take it to that level to retain my belt. I’ll spit my own blood right into Lox’s vindictive eyes and give it everything I have within me to hold onto the West Wind Championship. [Rustin realizing how worked up he is getting, looks back down in Chastity’s direction, and gently pats her hand once again.] Rustin: (to Chastity) I’m sorry Chass. I didn’t mean to get so loud. I hope I didn’t wake you up. [Chastity snores back in response, shifting ever so slightly in her hospital bed. Rustin releases her hand and brushes back a few strands of her long blond hair that have fallen over her eyelids. Rustin then rests a hand on her kneecap.] Rustin: Chastity, can you feel that? [No response. Rustin moves his hand to her stomach now.] Rustin: Chastity, can you feel that? [Again no response. Rustin, looks around to see if anyone is watching, and his hand now starts to creep closer and closer to her chest. As Rustin gets ready to rest his hand upon Chassy’s breast, Chastity’s arm flies up, knocking Rustin’s hand away. However, she still remains asleep. Rustin yanks his hand back.] Rustin: Damn, she’s good. [There is now someone who is standing outside the hospital room. The man is known to both Rustin and Chastity. He is wearing a dark black business suit, and army insignia. His name is Lincoln Frink, and old army friend of Chastity’s. He eyes Rustin wearily from afar, as he sees Rustin cradle Chastity’s hand in his once again.] Lincoln: Well isn’t this cute? [Rustin knows whose voice it is, but doesn’t turn to face him. He instead continues to console Chastity. Lincoln, annoyed that Rustin hasn’t acknowledged him walks closer towards Chastity’s bed, and closer to Rustin.] Lincoln: Hey squid! I was talking to you! Don’t you say hello to people you know? [Lincoln starts to laugh loudly. Rustin now faces him. Rustin is seen with a finger up to his lips.] Rustin: Shhh! Don’t you know she’s asleep? Don’t wake her up. She needs her rest. [Lincoln pulls up a chair and slides it to the other side of Chastity’s bed. He looks at Slade.] Lincoln: And you need your rest too you little creep! The match has been decided, and you are going to face me. [Rustin keeps his head geared towards Chastity.] Lincoln: Did you hear me you little shit? Rustin: Yeah. I heard you. [Rustin looks into the hateful eyes of Lincoln Frink, momentarily dejecting from Chastity.] Rustin: Now you hear me! The only reason that match is taking place is because Chastity spoke for me. I was going to give you quite the opposite of her answer. But if I can rid you from our lives as we know it in one match, than you can bet I will. Lincoln: Do you think you’re ready for me? [Rustin again turns his head towards Chastity, keeping his head down.] Lincoln: Look at you Slade! You’re not even ready to face Draven Lox next week. You got big words, but someone like Draven will wipe his ass with a puny punk like you. If you’re not ready for someone like him, how in the hell are you going to be prepared for me? Hmmm? Someone who could easily tear you limb from limb… [Rustin refuses to answer Lincoln.] Lincoln: Think about it you little bitch. Draven’s going to soften you up for me, and you’re going to be putty in my hands come Maelstrom VII. You have no idea how much I want Chastity, and the lengths I’ll go to get me and her back together. Think about it Slade! [Lincoln picks up Chastity’s hand and squeezes it a little.] Lincoln: She wants a real man! Not a waste! [Lincoln and Rustin lock each other’s eyes once again. After a moment of silent struggle, Lincoln gets up and starts towards the door.] Lincoln: I’ll see you real soon Rustin. [Lincoln exits the hospital room. Rustin looks back down at the sleeping beauty who is Chastity Spence. He wants nothing more than to be her Prince Charming, but realizes it’s going to take a hell of a lot. He leans down and gives her a kiss on the forehead.] Rustin: And he has no idea how much I want you Chass. [Chastity’s eyes slowly open. Rustin smiles as Chastity comes to. Chastity looks over to Rustin and sees his hand in hers. She squeezes his hand reassuringly and gives him a smile as the scene slowly fades to black.]
