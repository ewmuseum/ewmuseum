Stormfront RP #1 La Envidia Mata 4WW Exposures #657 Date: Location: 'Damn it, Metrenich,' God David shouted angrily. His voice echoed off the bare white walls of his office in Madison Square Garden. 'I thought I told you to take care of him last week!' Completely calm (as per the norm) and staring blankly, Metrenich responded in a voice devoid of emotion. 'Mr. David, I believe there was a variable you did not expect.' 'Variable,' David laughed, 'what f*cking variable?' The only change on Metrenich's face was that he blinked a few times before answering. He couldn't believe the XWF CEO was asking such a stupid question. 'A chainsaw comes to mind. And it nearly did, quite literally. If you thought that I was going to challenge a chainsaw wielding man, hell-bent on defending himself, with only a lead pipe, you, Mr. CEO, are out of your f*cking mind.' 'Nobody talks to me that way!' David's voice rose in pitch as he shouted, springing out of his chair. 'That's IT, Metrenich! If you don't get rid of La Envidia Mata, you are f*cking fired, you got it?' 'What,' Metrenich scoffed, 'and waste all that precious time you spent hiding my signing from Charles Stevenson?' Dismissing the comment with a raised middle finger, David suddenly snapped back into a calm tone. 'You've failed me too many times, Metrenich. I can't stand losers. With Stevenson away on personal leave, the future of your stay in the XWF is completely and totally in my hands.' 'I keep trying to tell you, Mr. David, that I will take care of La Envidia Mata, but on my own terms. Destroying him outright is not how it will be done.' 'It will be if I say it is!' Complete silence. The two stared at each other; Metrenich's piercing red eyes locked with God David's. Metrenich spat a curse in French, beginning to leave the room. 'Have it your way, God David. You ask, and so you shall receive.' 'F*ckin'-ay-right I will.' Grabbing a pack of smokes off his desk, David flipped one into his mouth. 'And if you don't do what I tell you to, and I don't get what I want tonight, then you Metrenich, are through here. Is that too much for you to handle, you whiny little b*tch?' Metrenich's only reaction was a thin, unpleasant smile. He turned towards the door. 'When my task has been completed, Mr. David, I will no longer be in your debt. You will be unprotected. Remember that.' David's eyebrow arched at that as Metrenich left the room, leaving the door wide open. All alone, David lit his cigarrette with a plain zippo and puffed out a breath of smoke. 'I make the rules, Metrenich. You'll be staying in my pocket for as long as you're of use to me.' [With a kendo stick resting against his shoulder, La Envidia Mata makes his way down the hallway to the entrance ramp. There's a hint of a smirk on his face, as if he's thinking his plan over in his head, anticipating the results. For the past month, La Envidia Mata has been terrorizing the 4WW ranks. It's the only thing he's been able to do thanks to 4WW Management, who has purposely kept him off of the cards. Maybe now La Envidia Mata will make his intended impact when he teams with Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling to face Featured Attraction for the East Wind Titles at Stormfront.] 'Where are you going, Mr. Mata?' [La Envidia Mata's eyes shift from green to gold in the light as he spins to face...] Envidia: Metrenich. [The words ooze off his tongue as if the name tasted foul in his mouth. Kendo stick in hand, La Envidia Mata moves towards Metrenich.] [Click.] Metrenich: I wouldn't do that, Mr. Mata. [Aimed at La Envidia Mata is a Beretta 9mm. Glinting in the bright light of the arena, the cold steel of the handgun flashes the blue of Metrenich's azure hinted hair.] Metrenich: There is a man holding a gun to your face, Mr. Mata. I'm interested to know what you're thinking right now. [He cocked back the hammer. Staring straight down the barrel of the firearm, La Envidia Mata locks on to the tattooed infinity symbol on Metrenich's bare forearm. He's calm, but La Envidia Mata's eyes search desperately for a hesitation or a flinch from Metrenich. His only option at this point is to fight back, but Metrenich is rock solid. Taking a deep breath, La Envidia Mata nudges his head at the tattoo.] Envidia: I'm wondering why you have infinity on your arm. Metrenich: Infinity? [Shaking his head with another thin smile spreading across his lips, Metrenich laughs.] Metrenich: Look again, Mr. Mata. [La Envidia Mata's eyes flick over to his arm again, and then right back, wary of Metrenich. Obviously. The man had a gun pointed to his head. La Envidia Mata winces and carefully studies Metrenich's arm. Frowning, La Envidia Mata mouths a few words and stares back up at Metrenich.] Metrenich: What's the matter? Envidia: It's an eight. Metrenich: Exactly, Mr. Mata. An eight. Envidia: Why is an eight on your arm? Metrenich: For identification. So that the others would know who I was. Envidia: What others? What are you talking about? Metrenich: Oh, La Envidia Mata. So young. So ignorant. I'm sure our friend Charles Stevenson would know what it meant. In fact... [He licked his lips, smiling thinly.] Metrenich: I'm sure even his sister would know. How is Gabriella doing, anyways? Last time I checked, she was still in the hosp-- [Forgetting that his life rested in the palm of Metrenich's hand, La Envidia Mata clenched his fists and stepped forwards to challenge him. Metrenich met La Envidia Mata halfway, pressing the barrel against his forehead, bringing La Envidia Mata to an immediate halt.] Metrenich: That's far enough! You don't know how tempting it is for me to kill you right now, La Envidia Mata... but I have more patience than that. I will destroy your life as you have destroyed mine. Envidia: I've never met you before. I don't even know who the f*ck you are! How could I have-- Metrenich: You killed him! You murdered him in cold blood! It's your fault it all ended, it's your fault he's gone, it's your fault I have no family! It's all your fault! [A lighting fast backhand cracks the side of La Envidia Mata's face. It knocks him backwards, but La Envidia Mata steadies himself against the wall. He starts moving forward, but the gun is again pressed against his head. He's helpless as Metrenich hits him again, and again, slamming the side of the Beretta into La Envidia Mata's skull as hard as he can until La Envidia Mata slumps to the floor, bleeding profusely from his nose and a gash along his forehead. His arms lay slack against the floor as his eyes close. Metrenich stands above him, still holding the weapon pointed at La Envidia Mata.] Metrenich: It would be so easy, La Envidia Mata, if I just pulled the trigger. It would be so easy for the both of us. You don't deserve the easy way out. I'll make you wish you were never born. [He lowers his head... smiling. Happily.] --fade
