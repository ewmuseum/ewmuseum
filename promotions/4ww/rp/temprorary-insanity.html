Temprorary Insanity

3 February 2004

Temprorary Insanity Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #496 Date: 03/02/04 Location: Hospital [how it begins...] 'Are you fucking crazy?' She snaps with vigor. You tell me. What do you think I am?' A slight curl smooths over his lips. 'You know exactly what I am. You've known it for nearly ten years now and it has always bothered you, deep inside. I can see that.' She taps her fingers one after another along the counter. Her red hair is tied back, she's wearing long sleaved red shirt and a small black skirt. She turns sharply toward Darek and a stern look has found its way onto her face. Darek pushes his hand across his face and waits for her response. 'I think it's a little more than that Darek, face it, you don't have that for an excuse.' She looks deep into his eyes, 'I know what you are, what you have always been and I have always loved each and every part of you. But now,' she pauses, '...it's different.' 'Oh Christ Amanda, you always bring love into this. You have known how I felt for years now.' Darek steps towards Amanda, but she turns slightly showing disapproval. He stops and steps back as she begins to speak. 'Last week you are bent that our fight...' She stuffs up and has a hard time continuing. 'That, incident.' 'You're dellusional.' He interupts. 'Excuse me?' 'I told you that last week at breakfast, it was a dream I had.' He tilts his head to try to meet her eye level, 'because I told you the dream I had, inwhich, yes I did temporarily lose my sanity.' A pause. 'You think it's real, you were probably still 'influenced' from the entertainment the previous night.' He looks at her and nods. 'Our Anniversery.' Her head turns towards him and their eyes meet. 'The night we went to the club and it happ-' She is interrupted briskly, 'It was a dream for fuck sakes.' Her head instantly turns away. 'We did go to the club and yes, we did get into an arguement, I believe you slapped me.' His hand massages his chin for a momment. 'Then what?' 'You passed out from having a little bit too much of a 'good time' and I took you home.' 'That's bullshit Darek!' She bites back, 'You are a master of mind games but I, unlike you, can tell the difference between reality and dreams.' 'Are you seriously implying that I am the one that has lost their mind here?' It comes to him like an epiphamy but she has been telling him this since last week. The question in the air to be debated was whether or not Darek had killed a man in violent rage. To her, his murderous rage was caused by his inability to seperate his hate for work and reality. A bit of a fetch to Darek, who was under the impression that he had carried her out of the facility and took her home. He had a dream, one fuelled by his anger from the fight at the club. When he awoke the next morning he explained the dream to Amanda as casual conversation at the kitchen table. Who had lost their mind? 'Excuse me.' She charges out of the room. Darek can only watch her leave and as the door is closing it, it springs open and in walks a familiar face. 'What's the news.' 'Referring to Amanda leaving? None of your business.' He steps forward and extends his hand, 'or if you're referring to the doctor? I'm not sure yet' 'Look, I'm not here as your friend or enemy, I'm here as your boss.' Ian grasps Darek's hand. 'And I need to know the results of this visit.' They let go and Ian continues, 'so when can we expect the news?' 'You honestly can't think that I am crazy?' 'I never said that.' He cuts in immediately. 'I just need to know, for the record and safety of individuals within my organization.' [...meanwhile...] The light flicks on and a faint buzzing noize can be heard from above. She turns back and locks the door, before falling into her arms which now rested on the counter sink of the bathroom. She lifts her head enough so her eyes can look forward and catch hold of her reflection. Her makeup is smeared down her face with the currentof the tears which relentlessly continue to fall. The whole scene plays through her mind a thousand times a day, Dark Angel had killed men, sinners, he was bound to for life; she knew Darek's 'demon' would always give him an excuse to do what he did, in the end, the excuses had always justified the means. Those whose lives were taken had indeed sinned, whether intentionally or unitententionally in life. This man, the man Amanda heard and saw Dark Angel refer to as 'Christopher', was a good man. Her eyes continue to glare at the the reflection she casts. She hoped that Darek was not insane, but then that would mean, she was... and the story unfolds again in her mind. 'We are about to see just how hardcore you are Christopher. You like barbwire, c4 and glass, well, I hope you know, I hate to see you in pain like this...' Without warning Dark Angel brings the blood stained point downward, piercing flesh, cartolage and the man's rib cage. The glass sinks deeper and deeper into the fresh wound until the Angel brings it upward and with a crazy look in his eyes brings it down again. 'You are nothing to me West. Nothing.' ...again. 'Get up, Hardcore Legend!' ..again... 'I SAID GET THE FUCK UP!' ...and again... 'Some Legend, myth is more like it.' ...until the man is no longer breathing. Dark Angel gets up and backs away from the onslaught before him. The whole time, that trademark smirk is plastered on his face just begging people to come forth. However, noone comes and the world grows cold and dark. Dark Angel turns his back to the mess before his eyes... 'Practice make perfect West...' He takes a few steps away from the bloody murder. [...in another room...] 'Doctor Jenson.' Darek steps towards the door and offers his hand. The doctor hesitates for a momment before taking his hand. 'Good evening,' he notices Ian standing in the room, 'gentlemen.' 'I'm sorry,' Ian steps forward and extends his hand. 'Ian Childress, I'm Darek's employer.' The doctor meets up with Ian and they exchange looks and a troubled look forms on Ian's face. The doctor turns around and looks at Darek, then at Mr. Childress. 'Advanced Dillusionary Scizophrenia.' Darek pause instantly, 'What?!' A look of amazement forms on the face of Ian Childress, 'how did you determine this?' The Doctor steps walks over to the TV mounted on the wall and pushes a tape into the vcr. A blue screen comes up with 'rew' flashing and numbers rolling back into the negative. Soon after a click sound is heard and the screen reads 'play' before flicker and then showing a small room with a desk. Darek is seated on one side and the doctor at the other. Darek swings his chair and gives the doctor a side profile as the speach is now heard. 'Darek, I want you to tell me the first things that come to your mind when I recite the following names,' he pauses, 'okay?' Darek simply nods and continues to relax. 'Apocalypse.' 'Love.' He responds quickly. 'What about, Ian Childress?' 'Good.' '...Draven Lox.' 'Leader.' '...Se7en' 'Destined.' '...Bullet.' 'Average.' '...Billy Dork.' 'Inferior.' '...James Slade.' 'Relentless.' '...Fnord.' 'Silhouette.' '...Mike Marvelous' 'Unrealistic.' '...Jerms.' 'Annoying.' '...Todd Dream.' Darek pauses, '...victim.' The doctor nods, 'and Chris West?' Instantly his arm shoots to the side and he grabs the doctor by the collar. 'I'M NOT CRAZY!' He stops. 'I know where you're going with this and I don't like it.' 'I'm not going anywhere,' he gasps for some air, 'I'm reading the employee listing of Four Win-' 'BULLSHIT!' Darek lifts the doctor into the air above his chair. 'I am a master of mind games, I know exactly what you are doing.' The screen turns black as the tape ejects from the vcr. The doctor puts the tape back into it's case places it on top of the TV. He looks at Darek who is shaking his head. 'I think we've seen enough, but there is also the catscans.' [...a bloody mess...] Amanda continues to stare at her reflection. Until she leaps forward driving her skull into the mirror. Shards of glass errupt from the wall and scatter all over the counter, sink and floor. The cracked pieces left hanging on the wall are speckled with red stains of blood. She reaches into the sink and pulls out a long, sharp edge of the mirror. Cautiously she places the sharpest tip onto the fleshy pink skin covering her wrists. 'I'm not insane.'
