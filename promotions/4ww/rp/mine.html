Mine... Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #627 Date: 5th of April Location: Unknown [The scene opens with Lee standing in a black room alone, not much is visible besides his head and upper abdomen, it’s clear he is wearing a plain white shirt.] Lee: Where do I start? [He shrugs, and then laughs.] Lee: I get a shot at the south wind title, oh and don’t get me wrong… That’s great, but at Maelstrom 3 I was robbed! I should have been in the finals, not Mike Marvelous, he stole that from me. I should have been there not him, not him, NOT HIM! [Lee clutches his head and begins to twist it until a loud crack is heard.] Lee: That’s much better… [He laughs again] Lee: I should be the 4WW champ, it should be me Lee Satima, not Jerms… But soon, soon enough it will be mine! It will belong to me and no one else. But now…now I have to look to the present not the future. Bullet… you, you’re my target, you’re the one I’m after, you and you’re south wind title… [Lee looks down and the camera follows to reveal a table with a white substance in lines across it and a single knife. Lee bends down to the table and sniffs in the powder. As he begins to talk he wipes his nose off.] Lee: I mean come on, you had 5 matches at Legacy, 5! 5 fucking matches, and ya you won some, ya sure you got skills --- But… --- But you’re nothing, nothing compared to me, NOTHING! I will win, I will become triumphant and I will take your title. [Lee bends down and picks up the knife and begins to repeatedly slam it down, blood begins splattering on him, his white shirt and the knife.] Lee: How dedicated are you Bullet? Huh? Would you do this? This is how confident I am that I’ll win. [Lee raises his left arm revealing bleeding slashes in the formation of SWT. The blood begins to trickle down the arm dropping onto the floor.] Lee: SWT, South Wind Title, IT’S MINE! MINE! MINE! MINE! [Lee begins to cradle his face and rock back and forth, the blood trickling onto his shirt. He begins to laugh. As a few drops of sweat begin to appear on his forehead he reaches down and picks up the knife. He stares at it for a few seconds, and then he licks it, once, twice, three times. I drop trickles down his cheek. He stares into the camera, his eyes a burning red, so much passion, so much hate, so much staring into the camera. He starts to laugh again] Lee: Do you know why I wear white? [He asks as if he wants a response] Lee: Huh? Do you? Do you? It’s because you can just bleach it, anything that stains you can just bleach and all it is, is a memory… Kind of like you and your title. It is soon just to be a memory, and nothing more. [A grin comes across his face.] Lee: You heard what happened to Starwind? Yep that prick got his, he’s dead Bullet and it’s your fault. You basically killed me, and you know what? You can’t get away from me that easily, you can’t just hope that there is a car accident, pray all you like but there is something you can never change and that is… That is… I’m already dead, and you’re just dying. It’s over, you’ve held that title too long, it’s mine and it’s mine to keep. [The scene fades out as Lee’s haunting laughs continue with the words mine, mine repeated.]
