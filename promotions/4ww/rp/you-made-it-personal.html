You Made it personal... 'Insane' Chris West 4WW Exposures #1008 Date: Oct 15th 2004 Location: Everywhere Locaction: Winnepeg Arena Date: Oct 12 Time: 8:30 (During Stormfront) [The Camera fades on backstage during Stormfront. You are Running down the Hallway of the Winnepeg Arena, as you get closer, and closer to the sounds of yelling] Insane: WHAT THE FUCK!!! [You get Closer and Closer to the Sence, as you see The Hardcore Legend “Insane” Chris West looking down. West forehead is taped up from his three Way match earlier in the evening. West's t-shirt is covered in blood. West is also wearing his Blue Jeans he wore in the match. You Also see one of the Road Agents using a Extinguisher. As you get Closer to the Sence, you see The Road Agent is shooting Bird with it. To take out the fire. Which D. M. C. Set. You See Anger on West face, like he's about to explode] Insane: Is he going to be all right?? [All of a Sudden, the Medicals rush into the picture, as they get down and check on Bird] Medical: He still has a Paluse. [West doesn't understand why they would be saying that. West has a confused look on his face] Insane: Well, is he going to be Ok?? [The Medicals just keep working on Bird. As the Paramedicals rush into the picture with a Stretcher, and a bunch of other Medical stuff.. They Hook Bird up on a IV, The Road Agent goes over to West] Road Agent: Dude, they are going to be taking Jayson to the Hospitals.. [West cracks his knuckles, as he grabs his on hair, and starts pulling it as he's trying to keep his Calm and not Snap. By looking at the hardcore Legend, you know its only a Matter of time, before West Snaps, and does something stupid.. The Road Agent slowly backs away. The Camera looks down at “Holy Shit” Jayson Bird.. As you see his clothes are burned.. as you see his skin is also in bad shape. Bird seems to out of it, just lays there. As the Medicals Work on him. All of a Sudden, they get the stretcher down, as they put him on the stretcher. The Camera turns back to West who face still have blood on it from his three Way match earlier in the evening, they start wheeling Jayson Bird off, as the Hardcore Legend fellows them.. You hear the sounds of the Wheels on the Stretcher, as the Camera fades to Black] Location: A Hospital in Winnipeg Date: Oct 14 Time: 3:20pm [The Camera fades on outside of the Hospital. As Rain falls down onto the leds of the Camera, as you look right at the Building. Cars, and trucks drive by the Hospital, as the Camera fades to Black. It Fades back on inside of the Hospital, looking outside of a window. The Rain pings off the Window. The Camera Zooms out, as you see “Insane” Chris West looking out the window. His face is still taped up. But, you can see he has cleaned up a bit, as he's wearing different clothes.. he has on a “Superman” Shirt, and Blue Jeans, West turns and does and sits down in a chair next to “Holy Shit” Jayson Bird, who is sleeping on his Hospital Bed. Bird, doesn't look all that good. He Looks like, he's been from hell and back. Some parts of his Body is wrapped up. Covering his Burns. Bird is connected to a IV Drip. All of a Sudden, Bird Opens up his eyes slowly.. as he looks over to the Hardcore Legend] Insane: What up Buddy?? [Bird Opens his mouth, and begins to speak] Holy Shit: I'm in alot of pain, and I feel like Shit.. [West see a Nurse Walk by the room door.. As West gets up and goes to her. The Nurse is a Blonde, about Five Foot Nothing] Insane: Hello.. My Buddy, is in some pain.. Nurse: Ahh ok, I'll be there in a min. Insane: Thanks! [West goes back to the Hospital Room, and Sits down] Insane: She'll be here in a min dude.. I hope with some fucking drugs!! [Bird looks at West] Holy Shit: Why would someone Burn me like This?? Insane: I don't know. I don't know why D. M. C. would do such a thing. Its above and Beyond. [A Tear comes down Bird's face] Holy Shit: What the Fuck did I do to get this?? I'm just a Manager.. [All of a Sudden, the Nurse walks into the room, and looks down at Bird] Nurse: I heard you in some pain.. [Bird turns to the Nurse] Holy Shit: Yes.. It hurts everywhere. [The Nurse Checks the IV Drip] Nurse: I see what the problem is here Jayson.. Don't Worry.. [The Nurse goes into her pocket and pulls out a Needle, and sticks into the IV. As she goes over to the Medical report and writes down what she just did. As she puts it back in its place, she looks back at Bird] Nurse: I just gave you some Drugs.. They will make you sleep, so you don't feel the pain.. Holy shit: Thank You.. [West gets up] Insane: Dude, I'll see you in a couple days. I have to go to Toronto and find the people who did this to you, and teach them a lession they will never forget.. Holy Shit: You Do that Dude.. show them, why you are the Hardcore Legend!! [West Fakes a Smile, as he leaves the The Room, the Camera fades to black, as the Camera looks back at Bird, who slowly passes out] Location: Inside of Skydome! Date: Oct 15 Time: 5:34pm [The Camera fades on backstage at the Skydome.. You are in a Hallway somewhere.. As you see “Insane” Chris West, Sitting down. He is sitting on a chair. You can see his anger and stress just coming off of him. He's wearing a “Jayson Bird” Shirt, and Blue Jeans. His forehead is still tapped up from his match on Stomfront. West takes a deep breath. As he keeps trying to calm himself down, he Opens up his mouth and begins to speak] Insane: Where the fuck do you get off burning someone like that?? [West and looks up at the lights, as he looks back down at the Camera] Insane: I can't believe you would Pull a Stunt like that. If you want to get to me, come to me.. You don't go after a defenseless Man, who can't wrestler anymore cause of his Back. You have no idea how much, you have pissed me off. You guys popously went out of your way, and Scared a Man for life.. For all know, Bird will never be the same again. You have taken a heated in the ring thing, and made it personal.. [West gets up and looks right into the Camera. You can and feel his anger coming from him, as he keeps speaking] Insane: You have made this more personal then you know.. You took my best friend and Burned him.. YOU FUCKING BURNED HIM... Eddie B.. I told you this before, and I'm sure I'll tell you again.. I'm going to thought everyone of your Fucking Mindless fucking goons, and I'm going to get to you, and when you do, who knows what I'll do.. You have taken this to a new level. New level of anger.. YOU MADE THIS PERSONAL. You went above and Beyond.. [West Grabs the Chair he was sitting on, and folds it up] Insane: Chairs, and Burning Stable Flags is NOTHING.. NOTHING, What I'm going to do to all of you when I get my chance. You don't Burn Bird. [West starts smashing the Chair upside of the Wall, as Bite and peices of the Drywall start to break, West stops and tosses the Chair down the hallway, as he turns back to the Camera.. He Grabs the Camera and forces it to show the drywall] Insane: Hardy, I want you to look at the Wall.. I want you to really look at it. [West forces the Camera to look back at him] Insane: Did you look at the Wall?? See the Broken Drywall?? After Stormfront, Hardy that is going to be YOUR FACE.. Hardy, Maybe you don't know, cause of all the rap music, and all the Bullshit you have been hearing from Eddie B.. But, right now, at any moment I could Snap, and hurt someone. But, you know what I'm doing Hardy?? [West Stops, and gives Hardy a time to answer West, while he watches the promo??] Insane: I'm going to do my best not to snap. I'm going to try my fucking best to hold in the anger as much, as I can. Hell, I'm going to even let the Anger Build up. [West Takes a deep breath, as he rubs his eyes.. As you can feel how stressed and pissed off he is thought the Camera. You Just know he isn't lieing... He's telling the truth] Insane: I'm going to take all the anger.. all the stress I've builded up over the past couple days, and take it all out on you. After this Match, then will talk about the Massacre from TO. About how Much Blood you lost. How Much you Burn as the D. M. C. Flag burns over your face. Eddie B wants to make this personal?? Hardy you are going to feel frist hand, what happens when people make things personal. [West turns around and walks off, as the Camera fades to black] Location: Outside of a Beer Store! Date: Oct 15 Time: 6:30pm [The Camera fades on inside of Beer Store in To. Its getting kinda dark, as Its Cloudly out, but its not raining. There is a few people out on the streets walking around. As you see “Insane” Chris West walk into the picture. As he walks into the beer store. He walks into the store, as the Camera follows him into the store, as West goes over to the Canadian Beer, and picks up a six pack, as he goes over to the counter and picks it up. You can see the anger, stress and sadness in West's eyes as he buys the beer. West Walks out of the store.. As West gets out of the story he rips off one of the Canadian beers, and pops it up. He looks down at the Beer. As he could be destorying a week a Bit of being clean... Cars drive by, as West lifts up the Beer. He puts it up to his mouth.. he Stops right before he drinks, as he tosses the Beer upside the Beer Store Wall] Insane: FUCK!! [West tosses the rest of the Cans into the Garbage, West walks off as the Camera fades to Black]
