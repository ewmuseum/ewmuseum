Shadow File #3 - Grudge Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1144 Date: 1/23/05 Location: |>> The scene opens in the abandon Safety Shelter, Kyo Kushinaga dressed in his work clothes was searching through the crime scene where Germany Police officers found Evan Frank’s corpse. He walks under some yellow tape, enter the small bedroom that have a flat bed at the corner of the room and a dresser with a mirror over it. Kyo approaches an open door, enter the small and dirty bathroom. He stops at the bathtub and see the tape outline of Frank’s body. Kyo kneels beside the bathtub, examine it carefully. > Kyo stands, leave the bathroom and re-enter the bedroom. > Kyo walks under the yellow tape again, enter the living room that have a small brown sofa in the center with a small old TV on the coffee in front of it. He looks around his surroundings, see an empty desk at the far corner of the living room. > In a local café in Munich, Germany, Kyo Kushinaga sit at a table alone with a gray color laptop, search the Internet for information. A young waitress with short blonde hair and blue eyes approaches Kyo’s table. > Kyo looks over to the voice and see his sister, Ukyo Kushinaga in a punk attire stand at his table. > Ukyo was cut off when she saw her brother have his 9mm aim in her face. > Kyo holsters the gun in his trench coat secretly. Ukyo sighs in relief and lean back in her seat. The waitress heads back to Kyo’s table with a cup of coffee in hand. > Waitress nods with a faint pink color blush on her cheeks. > Waitress looks at Kyo disappointingly with a weak smile. > Waitress nods, leave both siblings alone. Kyo takes another sip of his coffee and go back to what he was doing on the laptop. > Outside of the local café, Ukyo Kushinaga stands at the close door and think to herself. > Ukyo leaves the scene with a naughty look and pink color blush. > Back in the café, Kyo still hacking his way through the tough system. His expression turns to a serious one. > Kyo skims through Germany Intelligence’s highly classified files. He sharpens his eyes in a serious glare and smirks. > Waitress nods. Kyo finishes his special coffee…stop when he see a commercial for the upcoming StormFront on the small TV that hang up on the corner of the café, advertise the matches that include his match against Claudio Turbine. He finishes the coffee, sigh softly and look back at the distant TV seriously. > Waitress gives Kyo the check. Kyo pays for the coffee…stop when he see a commercial advertising the FMH Magazine with his girlfriend, Caitlin Fairchild in it, it show a few attractive shots of her. A couple of German man that sit at a table behind Kyo was talking in their native tongue, speak about how good she look and what they would to do if they met. Kyo understands the men’s words, his eyes pulse once in a crimson color…both men yell in fright and shot up from their seats from their pants being caught on a fire at the crouch. Men put out the fire in panic. Kyo smirks and put on his orange tint shades, he put down more money for the laptop he decided to swipe. He leaves the café with knapsack in hand...the waitress and the other workers help the men. > Outside of the café, a black Rolls Royce pulls up. Kyo Kushinaga stares at the car, watch two big Russian men in business suits exit from the backseat. > Kyo looks at the men seriously. > In the more luxury part of Munich, the Rolls Royce pulls up to a large mansion that’s being guarded by other Russian men in suits that are arm with sub-machine guns and assault rifles…the Russian men and the Deathscythe Outlaw exit. They disappear in the mansion…walk through the main hall and the giant living room, enter the large indoor swimming pool. Kyo and the Russian men stand at the edge of the pool. In the pool, a middle-age Russian man with very gray hair and brown eyes in swim trunks was swimming in the pool. Twin young German girls with long red hair and blue eyes in matching black color bikinis come out of a room with towel and robe, approach the pool. > One twin dries the middle-age Russian with the towel…the other twin put the robe on him. > Kris takes a sip of his drink…Drake lunge at Kyo for the attack. Kyo sidesteps Drake and strike him in the throat with a sharp chop. Drake favors his throat and gasp for air. Kyo strike Drake in the face with a strong right jab. Drake makes a big splash in the pool. > Kyo stops at the door. > Kris glare at Kyo. Kyo makes an amuse sound and leave the scene. Drake climbs out of the swimming pool and stand beside the other Russian man. Kris stare at his bodyguard with a serious look. > Russian men nod and leave the scene too. Kris finishes his drink, throw the glass down to the floor, see and listen to it shatter. The twins jump. Kris looks at his twins with a smile, approach them and lead them to the bedroom. > It’s nighttime in the heart of Munich, the Deathscythe Outlaw was staying in a hotel. A shirtless Kyo comes out of the bathroom in black color jeans with a towel on his head and rub his raven color hair with gray highlights to dry. There’s a knock on the door. Kyo approach the door…stop at the oak dresser and pick up his 9mm. He holds the gun up, look through the peep hole, make a curious sound and open the door after he unlock it…Faith in a violet color leather trench coat was outside the door. > Kyo moves aside…Faith enter the room. Kyo peeks out of the room, look side to side, see no one was in the hall…enter back in the room and close the door behind him. Faith take off her coat, reveal that she wear a violet color business dress with a long skirt and dark blue high heels. > Faith gasps. Kyo gives Faith a serious look when he heard her gasp…change his expression to a calm one and approach Faith with the beers. > Faith looks at Kyo…shock fill her face when she saw that Kyo have his gun aim at her face. > Imposter throws her beer bottle at Kyo…Kyo tilts his head and avoid the bottle, fire the gun at the imposter…the imposter flips out of the way, make Kyo shoot the chair instead. She lands behind Kyo and leave the room. Kyo follows the imposter…stop to grab his modified magnum and his trench coat, leave the room. People peek out from their rooms in confusion of the gunshots they heard. > She stands at the far end of the hallway, draw out an Ingram from within her skirt and fire it down the hallway. People scurries back in their rooms in panic. > She stops and load a new clip in the Ingram. Kyo hugs his back on the wall…come out of hiding and fire his gun at the imposter. She dives to the corner, roll back on her feet and run down the new hallway. Kyo follows her. She stops at the elevator, push the button frantically. A “ding” sound was heard…the elevator doors open, she enter the elevator. Kyo rushes down the hallway. He fire his gun rapidly at her…she hide at the side and avoid the bullets. The elevator doors closes…Kyo’s shots bounce off the metal doors. Kyo’s gun reach empty. > Kyo throws the knife between the doors…the knife enter the elevator before it closed. The knife graze her arm, she winces and favor the wound. > Kyo stands at the elevator, push the button repeatedly…see that the elevator wasn’t coming back up. > Kyo looks over to the right and see at the far end that a window was there…he smirks. > Down in the lobby, she stumbles out of the elevator and head to the exit. A young bellhop approach her. > The bellhop back away with his hands up. She aims the Ingram around her the area, make the people go in a panic. She runs to the exit…two security guard come out of nowhere and aim their handguns at her. Guards yell out “freeze” in their native tongue…she fire her Ingram at them and kill them instantly. She storms out of the scene…the bellhop outside approach her…she stop him with aim of the Ingram. The bellhop nearly stumbles over when he back away. She runs away from the hotel…gunshots was heard up on the seventh floor of the hotel, the bullets have broke through a window. Kyo jumps out of the weaken window…he flips on his back and bounce off the awning…perform a front flip and lands in front of the hotel’s entrance on one knee with his advance magnum drawn. She runs down the sidewalk, clear people away from her with a quick aim of the Ingram. Kyo aims carefully and fire rapid…a bullet stuck her on the back of the leg. > Kyo holds out his hands, a cerulean color aura appear around his body…the bullets bounce off him. She looks fright and run away. > Kyo follows her to an alley...walk further down and discover shatter clothes on the cold concrete. He gets down on one knee and examine the clothes, see that these are the same clothes that the imposter wore and he see lime color blood on the skirt. > Kyo hears loud music…look over in the direction of the music and see that there was a nightclub at the far end of the alley. He heads over to the club. > In the hoppin’ nightclub, the Deathscythe Outlaw makes his way through the dancers on the dance floor. He looks around for the changeling in the crowd. > Kyo heads to the bar on the other side of the club…a woman with short red color hair and blue eyes in a tight black and gold leather cat suit stand at a table alone, stare a hole through Kyo. She sneaks over to him. Kyo reach the bar. > Kyo nods his head to the loud music. The redhead continues to sneak her way to him. An attractive German woman with long pink hair and green eyes in a very skimpy blue outfit walks up to Kyo and whisper something in his ear. Kyo makes an amuse sound. > Kyo watch her leave. > Bartender returns with Kyo’s order. Kyo nods and drink the warm liquor from the short glass cup. The redhead stands behind Kyo, pull out the Ingram and aim at Kyo’s back…Kyo turn around with plasma sword in hand and sever the redhead’s hand with the Ingram. She screams and favor the stump that use to be her hand. The people hardly notice…they continue to have a good time in the club. > Kyo grabs her by the arm and lead her out of the club. > In an alley, Kyo pushes the hurt redhead against the wall. > The redhead stays silence and look away from him with sweat on her face. > The redhead still doesn’t answer. > Back of her head suddenly explodes in blood, brains, and skull…a hollow bullet from out of nowhere had pierced her forehead. Shock fills Kyo’s face. Up on a nearby building, a sniper in familiar red clothes stands on the rooftop, load another hollow bullet in the rifle…Kyo heard the click, spin in a 180 degree turn, draw out his magnum and fire up at the roof. The sniper falls. > Kyo lands on roof and discover a dark green color bloodspot on the ground with the sniper rifle in the middle of it. He hears a car drive with haste…he run to the edge and see a black car drive away. Kyo runs and hop from rooftop to rooftop…follow the black car. He stops and perform a back flip off the rooftop, grab hold of a drain pipe, slide down to an alley…perform a sideways corkscrew flip out of the alley and lands perfectly on both feet in the middle of the street. The driver stops the car when he saw that Kyo stand in the way. The driver and Kyo are at a standoff…the driver sound his engine a few time…Kyo stand with ease. Black car drives right for Kyo…Kyo drawn his 9mm and fire a single shot at the windshield, struck the driver in the head. The car swerves pass Kyo and crash into a park car; perform a nasty a 180 degree spin in the air…crash through the front window of a closed Sport’s Store and land upside down. Kyo stands with his back turn to the car and spin his 9mm around like a gunslinger from the Old West, holster it in his trench coat. Kyo runs to the damage store…open the door on the driver’s side, see the driver was dead from the headshot wound…he doesn’t see the bullet wound that he made when he shot him the first time on the roof. > Kyo makes a curious sound when he heard a “beep” sound in the car…look inside of the car and notice a timer count back. Kyo gasps…the car explode and level the entire store…Kyo stood on top on a light pole on the other side of the street, watch the flames dance wildly. > Kyo hops off the light pole and lands on one knee. The sirens of police cars and fire trucks were heard from the distant. Kyo sits on the curb, light a cigarette with his favorite silver lighter, smoke it relaxingly and wait for the law to come. > The familiar black Rolls Royce pulls out of an alley four blocks away from the crime scene and drives away…the scene fade to black. > To be continue
