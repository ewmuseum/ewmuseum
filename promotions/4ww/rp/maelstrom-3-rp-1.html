Maelstrom 3 RP 1 La Envidia Mata 4WW Exposures #575 Date: 3/17/2004 Location: We open up to Miami Airport. Our view is from the top floor window looking down over the runway's. The sun is shining as the crowds of people are being held back by security guards on the main runway. Barriers are being placed down. A plane that has just landed comes to a complete stop. The stairs are layed down and out come various people. A man with long black hair comes to the door of the plane and a roar comes from the crowds of people. Cheers of Envidia-Envidia can be heard even from right up here inside the airport. AS the camera focus's in we make out the face of XWF former World champ La Envidia Mata. He is wearing his sunglasses as usual with a blue La Envidia Mata 'God' t-shirt. He walks past the crowds of people taking photo's and slapping hands. He finally makes it into the airport where Banner's and posters from 4WW fans have been placed. From the look on his face Envidia didn't expect this. He seem's to be liking it. The camera switches to another on the ground floor around 10 feet away from Envidia. Different young fans are asking Envidia for autographs and aren't walking away dissapointed. Just then a man in a blue suit approaches Envidia. He has a badge reading 'WYNJ News' He stands with a mic trying to get a word with Envidia. News Man: Hey Envidia how does it feel to be a 4WW superstar? Envidia: It feels good. Its something I've wanted for a long time. News Man: Last we seen of you was in the XWF when you un-characteristically turned your back on the fans. Why did come back with re-formed love for them? Envidia: You see I've been out of this business a year and when I think about it I missed these guys. I mean the chanting of a name can make you feel good inside. Hearing grown up adults scream their heads off when you win a title is a great feeling. Like you just seen I signed an autograph and a kid walked off with a happy smile on his face. Envidia then attempts to walk off but is caught with one last question. News Man: As you probably know there's a South Wind Contenders Open Match. Where the winner faces Josh Starwind later that night for the championship. Envidia: Yeah I had noticed. Josh Starwind eh? I never thought I'd see him again but unluckily for me I turn up to have this sucker here too. What you going to do? Beat me? I dont think so. If I remember rightly you haven't beaten me before since we have not faced each other yet but at Maelstrom we will finally meet since I will win the contenders open and then Starwind you will tap out when I put you in La Envidia Tamer and I win my first championship in 4WW. News Man: Envidia thanks for your time and I hope your career in 4WW is a long one. Envidia: Thanks! Envidia heads up the stairs towards the 4WW cameras were he has scheduled a meeting. He takes a seat at the bar and orders himself a drink. After paying for and receiving his drink he walks over to the cameras and takes a seat. Taking his sunglasses off he looks into the camera. Envidia: For 1 year I've been training hard, I've been working I disagree off for a return to this business. I was sitting reading the newspaper the other day when I got a phonecall about a possible return to the ring. I didn't understand at first but surely enough 4WW wanted to sign me up. They offered me big money. The thing is this isn't about the money. It may be that way for some of the superstars but not La Envidia Mata. I'M here to win gold, kick ass and entertain. I know you have a guy by the name of Josh Starwind here and for those of you that don't know me, I'm the one man that Josh Starwind will not beat. I'm the man that Josh Starwind will stand to hear the mention of my name. Josh Starwind your still as pathetic as you always have been. But right now you don't interest me the only reason I'm after you is because I want the South Wind Title. I have other dealings to take care of. My debut match soon at Maelstrom 3. Hey what a debut I got right? I'm in the Contenders Open for a chance at the goddammed South Wind title. Josh Starwind I know that's killing you. While your competing with guys that can't win I'll be challenging for titles. I'm the newest guy around here and already I'm bigger than you. I guess its just what people want Josh. They don't want your ugly, yes I said ugly, face all over the TV sets. Hell no. They want to see the God of Wrestling La Envidia Mata kicking your ass. But sorry to dissapoint that I will only do it once at Maelstrom 3 as I've already said bigger fish to fry right now. Anyway my opponents in the Contenders Open. I don't know much about these guys but I sure as hell won't be taking them lightly. Which I'm sure they will be doing about me. I don't underestimate nobody unless their name is Josh Starwind, sh*t why can't I stop talking about the little green man. Anyway underestimating someone can be a huge mistake especially if you don't know them. I'll be striving to win this Contenders Open and I'll win that South Wind title from Josh Starwind. I won't back down from a challenge and I'll give it my all. Just then Envidia gets up and is about to head off when.... He turns around with one last message Envidia: Because in this ring I am God and I have no equal. Why? Because La Envidia Mata. The scene fades into blackness as Envidia smiles cheesily into the camera. Later... We open up to a shot of the entranceway to MP13 Gym in Miami. This is a place re-nound for its Wrestling superstars making daily stops there for tune ups and warm ups. In the window is a picture of 4WW's La Envidia Mata. A 50% owner of the gym as well as being a well respected member. Upon entering the gym we see tributes to Envidia with copies of his past title wins. Other superstars are also plastered around the walls. We walk through two double doors and into the actual gym part of the building where standing in the center is a wrestling ring. Punching bags and weight benches are also spread around the room. Standing in the ring is La Envidia Mata himself. He looks to be showing younger generation wrestlers-to-be a few moves. He notices the cameras and ushes them in. Envidia: Ok boys I have business to take care of. Go take five. Envidia's students listen and each walk to seperate parts of the room enjoying a heavy drink of water. The camera man climbs up on the apron and Envidia approaches the camera. Envidia: Not long now until La Envidia Mata makes his 4WW debut. Maelstrom 3 the event. Contenders Open the match. A battle Royal to determine the number 1 contender to the South Wind championship. Now in the past day since I spoke to 4WW fans a number of issues have been talked about and mentioned. The first thing people mentioned about me was the fact that I'm not focused on the matches and I'm focused on Josh Starwind. Obviously they aren't focused enough on what I said as I mentioned that Josh Starwind and I will have a history some day but for now more important things are happening for La Envidia Mata. Josh Starwind is future business. Josh may of been around here longer than myself but don't let my 4WW in-experience fool you. Josh Starwind you may think your the big tough dude around here. When its all said and done and all the other superstars have been eliminated from the Contenders Open it will be me and you left in that ring for the South Wind Title. La Envidia Mata and Josh Starwind one on one. My main task will be to eliminate and destroy everyone in my path and you my friend won't be any different. Envidia takes his sunglasses from his pocket and places them on top of his head. He takes a drink from the bottle that stands in the corner and then looks back at the camera. Envidia: Get in my face at the wrong time and you are falling victim to La Envidia Tamer. Say what you want about me. Watch as many tapes as you can get your hands on, Do your homework because you are going to need it. You my BOY know absolutely nothing about La Envidia Mata. You don't know how my mind works or how I think. You can learn my style all you wish Josh Starwind it won't make a difference. My style changes from match to match and nobody knows what to expect when it comes to me. Your damn right I'm prepared for 4WW. First is a battle royal. I have taken part in my fair share of rumbles and battle royals and right now I'm one hundred percent focused on this. Not only is it for a shot at the South Wind title its also my debut. My debuts in the past have not been dissapointing and I plan on keeping it that way. Learn the sentence Josh, in this ring I am God and I have no equal. Why? Because La Envidia Mata. Because in the near and distant future you are going to hear alot of it. I'm going to work you so bad that whenever you hear the name Envidia your blood will boil to the point of explosion. I normally make it a thing of not making enemies on the first day but I will make the exception this time. Look behind you at all times Josh Starwind as La Envidia Mata somewhere down the line is going to blow up in your face. Come Maelstrom 3 look up to the ramp when you put the South Wind Title on the line and see the arrival of the God of Wrestling and be ready for an ass whooping. Envidia rubs his chin before looking back at the camera. But before he can speak we get an interruption ???: Yo man, How have you been? Envidia: If it isn't DVD. What's up man? DVD: Chilling. I hear you signed up to the big 4WW. Envidia: Yeah I got a couple of matches coming up at Maelstrom 3. You never going to believe who else is there. DVD: Yeah I heard. Josh Starwind. Envidia: Yeah man. I may get him in my first night in 4WW. DVD: Yeah but don't forget..... Envidia: Man don't be going with this bullsh*t. I've already had this from the people. (in baby talk) Your too focused on Josh Starwind to focus on your matches at Maelstrom 3. (back to normal) It's pissing me off people keep talking about it. I mean Josh Starwind is nothing to me. Yeah I can't stand the guy and yeah I would love to kick his ass but like I keep f*cking saying I have BIGGER FISH to fry like the 4 Winds Tournament. DVD: Alright man Alright. I was just saying Envidia: Sorry man its just people keep getting on about the Josh Starwind thing. DVD: So who's your opponent at Maelstrom 3 was it? Envidia: Opponents. Its a battle royal for a shot at the South Wind champion, then after that win I face Starwind for the title and finally I get in the 4 Winds Tournament. DVD: Sh*t!. They have seen potential in you quick. Envidia: Yeah man. I don't see what they see in some of these other guys though. None of them impress me too much. I mean how hard will it be. I'll walk in there Envidia Kick all their asses, throw them over the top rope and head off to face Josh Starwind as the number 1 contender and then win the tournament. And you more than anybody know what I say, I do. DVD: Yeah well man I gotta be out. I got a date with this fine ass girl in an hour. I haven't even showered yet Envidia: You a date? YOU? Man she either blind or she's blind and ugly DVD: You may be bigger, a wrestler and muscular but I'll still kick your ass. Envidia: Yeah right man get the f*ck up outta here and I'll speak to you later. Pe@ce man DVD walks out of the gym leaving Envidia with the cameras. He clears his throat and... Envidia: Ok back to business. You know in every federation across the world we see freaks like Josh Starwind. You however suprised me with your attitude so to speak. Who do you think you are? Your the sort of guy that talks the talk but doesn't walk the walk. I however do both. You are currently at second focus in the mind of La Envidia Mata. I've beaten your sort before. You are no different from them and come Maelstrom 3 you too will be handed your ass on a plater. Infact a Silver plater. Take a Tarot reading, get your palm touched up and all the time you will see one thing. La Envidia Mata kicking your ass all over that ring and taking your South Wind Title because in this ring I am God and I have no equal. Why? Because La Envidia Mata. Envidia laughs sarcastically into the camera as we fade to commercial.
