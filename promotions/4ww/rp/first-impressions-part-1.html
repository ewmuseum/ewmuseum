First Impressions (Part 1) Calegero Vincenzo Macili Jr. (called Vincenzo) 4WW Exposures #1187 Date: 2/23/05 Location: He won and he was elated but he was not satisfied. Vincenzo’s satisfaction only lasted the night of the match. It only lasted until he remembered that it was only one match and in that one match what did he accomplish? He accomplished nothing. Nobody in the back respected him any more or any less because of that match. Vincenzo wasn’t feared because he hit multiple power bombs. Vincenzo still is not feared because he’s still in the opening matches in Four Winds Wrestling shows. The scene opens up with cars going by on a semi-busy street and others parked on the side. The sidewalk is lined with stores ranging from café’s to bookstores to radio stores to computer stores. People are walking by, men and women, some with coffee, some with newspapers, others empty handed. The camera follows one man, bigger than ordinary people. It is, of course, Vincenzo. He is clothed in light blue jeans, brown shoes, and a blue collared short sleeve shirt. The sun bounces off of his shiny, clean-shaven, tan head. Vincenzo seems like he has good hygiene because there are no signs of facial hair and his skin is as smooth as a baby’s bottom. He would blend in with all of the other citizens if not for his enormous size. As he continues to walk he makes a right into a café. Peaking into the windows of the café you can see a counter where food is ordered and in front of that are numerous tables, some occupied with computers and people. Most people are drinking coffee and a select few are eating while others work feverishly at their computers trying to finish up a class assignment that is probably due that night. Every time he walks into a store he garners attention. It is a combination of his size, the way he walks and his looks because he is a somewhat attractive young man. Women love his skin tone and his physic. Of course, he is not a cocky gentleman but he does not go out looking for attention but he does not shy away from it either. It was Vincenzo’s turn to order something. He peered up at the menu of drinks and food they had. After making a quick decision he looked at the cashier. Vincenzo: Water and a plain bagel. The cashier looked quickly at the keypad, pressed three buttons and the total came up. Cashier: Is that all sir? Vincenzo: Yes. The cashier took another look at the total, looked back at Vincenzo and gave him the total. Cashier: Out of 10 dollars. Your number is 3. The cashier put the ten-dollar bill Vincenzo had given him and exchanged it with some change. He gave the bills and change to Vincenzo and he quickly deposited it into his left pants pocket. Vincenzo turned around and walked to an empty table as the lunch crowd began to file in. He patiently awaited his number to be called. Other numbers were called and more patrons walked in to get something to eat on their lunch break. All tables had at least one occupant now and people were sitting at tables with people they did not know. A beautiful lady sat down right across from Vincenzo. She had long, shiny blonde hair with traces of brown in it. Her beautiful, tanned skin was accented by the white tank top she was wearing which matched perfectly with her jeans. Lady: Hello. Her voice was very soft but it was very confident. Vincenzo: Hi… Vincenzo was shy. He was impressed by her looks, very impressed. Lady: I hope this seat isn’t taken. She said it with a beautiful smile that showed her white, sparkly teeth. Vincenzo was taken back by how beautiful she was for he had never seen or spoken to a woman of her beauty. Vincenzo: Oh no, it’s not. Please, sit down. He had a huge smile on his face and he knew it. He wanted to stop smiling because he was a man who wanted to act tough and muscular. His smile would not go away. Finally, his number was called. Vincenzo: That’s my number, I’ll be right back, save the seat for me? Lady: Sure. Vincenzo got up from his chair, hurried over to the counter to get his food. He walked back trying to act cool. As he sat down he looked at her, she looked at him and they both smiled. Lady: So what’s your name? Vincenzo: Calegero. Lady: Calegero? Are you Italian? Vincenzo: Yes I am. Lady: Really? Me too. She had excitement in her voice. She must have found Vincenzo attractive and liked, even more, that they had at least one thing in common. They soon found out they had many things in common. Vincenzo exchanged numbers with her and, well, we’ll have to wait to see what happens.
