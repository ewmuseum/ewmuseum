Visit to a Hospital

23 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Visit to a Hospital Fnord 4WW Exposures #149 Date: 9/23/03 Location: Lutheran Home for the Mentally Unstable and Local Hospital (The scene opens in front of what looks like a hospital. It’s the middle of the night and everything is quiet. A little bit too quite, if you ask me. I drive up to the gate and see a sign that says “Lutheran Home for the Mentally Unstable” as the gate opens up. It squeaks a deafening, shrilling squeak as it opens. It sure as hell sends chills up my spine. I drive up the twisting road to the main entrance. I get out of the ambulance and walks towards the entrance of the place. The door is wide open. Even though I question myself, I still walk through the door. What I see next sickens me. There are orderlies’, doctors’ and nurses’ bodies lying all around on the floor. All of the patients’ doors are open and they are just roaming free around the hallways! I pass by a few of the crazies and go straight to the nurses’ station. No one is there. I turn back around and see a huge black man staring down at me. He giggles like a little schoolgirl as I run the hell away. I see one room where the door is closed and go straight there. It’s a patient’s room, but it’s the only chance I have. I struggle with the lock, but eventually get it unlocked and open. I rush in and slam the door closed behind me. I get down on my knees and pray that the huge black man didn’t see me come in here. A shadow passes by the window and keeps moving. I thank God that he didn’t open the door and see me here. I hear a small noise and a shuffle in one of the corners of this dark room. I find my lucky Zippo from my pocket and flick it on. There is a man huddled in the corner of the room wearing a straight jacket. He looks over to me and smiles.) Man: Hey, you want out of this place? I can help you out. Get this thing off me and I will lead you out. (I hesitate at first, but what choice do I have? I came here to pick up some psycho and transfer him to a different nut house, but it looks like plans have changed. I don’t care if I lose my job; I just need to get out of this place! I go over to the man and let him loose. He seems very muscular and quiet for a crazy man. He stretches as if he has been wearing that straight jacket for years. With this strange voice, he starts some small talk.) Man: So, what the hell are you here for? Don’t you know that the inmates have taken over? He he. This place isn’t safe for someone like you. (I decide that it might be safer to make a “friend” out of this guy.) Me: Well, I’m just a nightshift EMT. I was sent here to pick up some crazy guy that goes by the name of Fnord. (The man winces at the name Fnord.) Me: Is everything okay? Do you know this man? I heard he is totally insane and dangerous and I don’t even know what he looks like! Man: Yeah, I know him. He isn’t a pretty flower that’s for sure. Remind me what room he is in again. I gotta be sure I know who you are talking about. (I look down at the paperwork in my hands.) Me: Well, it says he is in room 133. Are we anywhere near that room? Man: Damn! That’s really close! We had better get lout of here in a hurry! Let’s go, open the door. (I open the door and the man and I start to walk away. I look back towards the man and he’s gone! I look towards the door and it’s shut tight. And then I see something that almost makes me shit my pants. I just walked out of room 133. I start to run full blast towards the front door, but I run right into that huge black man. The man from room 133, the man that is known as Fnord steps out from behind the huge black man.) Fnord: Willey Bubba, take him out back with the others. We might find some use for him later. (The scene quickly changes to a semi-private hospital room with the EMT in the bed and a few cops around him.) EMT: And that’s the last thing I saw until I woke up here. That man must have knocked me out or something. Cop1: That story sounds a little strange. Why did you go into the cell? Why didn’t you just call us, the police, when you first saw what was happening? EMT: I guess I was too scared and wasn’t thinking straight. I’m sorry; I must have lost my head. Cop2: Okay, we’ll let you get some rest. If you can get your roommate to turn down the TV, I’m sure you can get some sleep. It might help you with your memory. We’ll be back later to see if you have any more information for us. (The cops leave the room as the EMT talks through the curtain to his roommate.) EMT: Do you think you could turn that down or even off? I have a huge migraine and I need some rest. Roommate: Sure. You helped me once, why can’t I help you? I will help you get some rest. (The EMT gets a confused look on his face. He flings open the curtain and Fnord is laying in the bed with his feet up smiling.) Fnord: SURPRISE! (Fnord pounces on the EMT and knocks him out with a metal bedpan. He starts to look through the EMT’s belongings until he finds the EMT’s Zippo. Fnord smiles as he pockets the Zippo. He snickers as he stares at the knocked out EMT.) Fnord: FIRE! (The scene changes again, this time to the far future, which is still the past for us. Fnord gets a phone call from Chris West, a long time rival. Fnord comes to the 4WW with a contract in hand. He does damage to Psymon many times during the night of his first appearance. Finally, we catch up to current time. Fnord is standing outside a normal hospital. This one is quite busy as Fnord stands outside of the ER smoking a cigarette.) Fnord: You know what? I like 4WW. It’s a fun place and I think I’ll stick around for a long time. I can see my future being bright here. Along with that Canadian simpleton West, we are gonna rule 4WW. He claims to be the true “hardcore icon”, which I used to fight him about. He isn’t as hardcore as I am. Then I got to thinking that I’m not hardcore. I’m EXTREME! (With that last yelling of the word “extreme”, a few security guards come out of the ER. Fnord takes a puff of his cigarette and blows it in their faces as the come over.) Fnord: Can I help you boys? Security1: Yeah, what are you doing out here? Are you here to visit someone? And this is a smoke-free facility; you are going to have to put that out. (Fnord looks down at his cigarette. He takes one more puff before putting it out in the palm of his hand. He pops the butt in his mouth and swallows it.) Fnord: There, now leave me alone. I’m here to visit a dear friend of mine. See this cameraman here, I’m famous and now you’re on TV taking up my space and looking like an idiot. Get the hell out of my face. (The security guards realize that they are on TV and they smile and wave. One even says “Hi, mom”. They turn around grumbling as they go back into the hospital. Fnord smiles and spits in their direction.) Fnord: Now, where was I? Oh yeah, I’m gonna show 4WW, Syxx Figures, Ian Childress, and Fey McClancy-Childress just why they hired me. I’m gonna bring in ratings and all that crap! “DEEZ NUTZ” will be a household phrase! I even have in mind a few “insane” matches that would be great for ratings. But enough about that. I’m not here to kiss anyone’s asses. I’m here to cause CHAOS! (Fnord walks into the hospital and they point him towards the front desk. He walks up and there are two old lady volunteers sitting there answering the phone. Fnord walks up to the desk and just stares a hole into one of the old ladies.) Lady1: Can I help you sir? Fnord: Yeah, I’m looking for my “cousin”. Her name is Angel… maybe Angel de la Muerta? Hell, she just changed her name… She works for 4WW! Yeah! Look her up… Lady1: Well, I don’t think I can tell you any information since you don’t even know her last name, but I know whom you are talking about. (Fnord jumps over the desk and grabs the old lady by the collar of her shirt.) Fnord: LISTEN LADY… (The old lady points to the computer screen with all of the information and Fnord just smiles.) Fnord: Thanks. (Fnord lets the lady go and walks over to the elevator. He pushes the button as the scene fades to black.)
