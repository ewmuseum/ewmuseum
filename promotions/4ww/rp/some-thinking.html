4 December 2004

Some Thinking 'The Done Deal' Paul Williams 4WW Exposures #1092 Date: 12/4/2004 Location: Nationwide Arena, Cleveland, Ohio [The feed fades in on Paul Williams, who is standing outside the Nationwide Arena. It's roughly midday and Paul is conversing on his cell phone. Williams, wearing his traditional sports jerseys, stands outside in the Arena's parking with his Tempest Championship proudly over his shoulder.] Paul Williams - Yeah ma. .... Yeah I won. The belt's right here around my shoulder. .... Hey I didn't know she was out there for me. I just came to, saw LLL laying there, and went for the cover. ... No ma, I'm not affiliated with them. ... No I'm not. ... Ewwww, I just started here a few weeks ago, how can we be like that. .... Ma, what the hell have you been watching? ... Ok I didn't need to hear that. ... Ok I really didn't need to hear that. ... Ahh Ma, go to go... No I have to go. Bye mom... [Paul hands up the phone and stares back at the Nationwide Arena. He speaks as if he were speaking to Almighty himself.] Paul Williams - I knew I said it would be a comeback... but a comeback like that. My second night here ending one of the most lengthiest title reigns there was in the 4WW. I mean how much luckier can one guy get. First 4WW answers my audition tape. Then I'm booked to a #1 Contender's match on my first night for this title. Then after a week of panicking out and running around scared crapless out of my pants... a prayer came in the form of Apoc and Patti Anderson distracting LLL long enough for me to get the win. I must be some lucky guy. [Williams starts to walk through the parking lot. Empty for the most part except his own car which is now parked several yards behind him. Paul walks and continues to talk to himself aloud.] Paul Williams - Ok, then I get to face Rustin Slade again... A good guy, but now I'm the champion and he's the challenger. I mean I got him on the first night and I just about whooped his behind. And now he whooped someone else's behind to get his shot at me. I mean if I beat him once before, I should do it again, relatively easy. Then again, Slade gave me some problems that night... Nothing I can't handle. [Williams continues to walk a few more yards. He grabs a lamp post and leans against it for a short while.] Paul Williams - Hell why can't I be happy. Why can't I be happy the fact that I won this title from LLL's waist. Err, wait that came out wrong. I won this belt from LLL whether it be from outside interference of Apoc or not. But then again, Apoc scares a me a bit. I mean she just came and looked at me dead in my eyes and said words that I probably won't forget... Then again knowing me I already forgotten them. Heh... Where's that Gingko Biloba when you need it. I mean Apoc came over and whispered something to me in my ear that just scares me on what her purpose was that night. She didn't have to come out there, I mean she didn't have to come out there to help me. But that's what she did. If anything, Apoc could've came over and whooped my behind worse than LLL did to me that night. Then Patti coming to ringside was a complete mystery to me. I mean I know I submitted that promo tape for a chance to lay out with... I mean... Oh my god, got to get mind out of gutter! Especially since she's married for crying out loud. (Paul taps his head slightly with the belt.) Paul Williams - Come on Paul, get it together. You're the champ... Now it's time to do some thinking... You got people to pay back... You always hate it when you have people to pay back. Apoc came out to help you, now you got to help her out. Same goes for Patti, she got your back a little bit, now you have to repay the favor. But that's what's scaring me... What is it that they want from me? [Screams out loud at the top of his lungs....] Paul Williams - WHAT IS IT THAT THEY WANT FROM ME!? [Paul pants heavily after screaming.] Paul Williams - Why can't I be happy I did something good? Why do I have to torture myself. [Williams looks back at his car to see that it is quit far in the distance.] Paul Williams - AND WHY THE HELL DID I WALK SO DAMN FAR FROM MY CAR!? DAMN! [Williams walks back towards his car with his Tempest title in his hand as the camera fades to black.]
