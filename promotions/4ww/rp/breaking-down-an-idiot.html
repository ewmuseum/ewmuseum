Breaking down an idiot. Syxx Figures 4WW Exposures #47 Date: 7/16/03 Location: From the home. There are over one billion nerve cells, or neurons, in the human body. These receive, integrate, and transmit messages along the central nervous system, the brain and the spinal cord, and the peripheral nervous system, nerves and structures outside of the brain and spinal cord. The end of adolescence does not mean that developmental stages come to an end. Though some personality traits remain stable, the personality itself continues to evolve throughout adult life. As in the case of children, however, the rates f development differ from person to person. ***** The breaking down of a man is physically, mentally, and emotionally hard. Trust me I should know. I’ve been doing this for a while now. And I’m pretty good at what I do. I can make a grown man cry, get on his knees and beg for his mother, weep at his own insecurities, pathetic, puny, meek little excuse of a life. I can make people wish they were never born, and make some actually end their life. I can make the president of the United States step down and work at Burger king. Do you know what makes me so damn good? I studied Psychology. Do you know why studying psychology pays off? Well, first off. You will learn systematic approaches to tackling problems that are best suited to your personality and the problem at hand. Secondly, When you study the roots of behavior and techniques of self-control, you will be able to make better choices about your actions. You will be able to act, instead of react. And thirdly, by understanding how other people see the world, you will develop more empathy and patience interacting with others, and your communication skills will be enhanced. ***** Syxx is reading a newspaper, flipping through the pages, he stops on the horoscope section, and thinks back to his research he did on Sean Summers. After remember his birthday, Syxx checks the paper for “Libra”. Syxx, with reading glasses on the tip of his nose, nods and reads the article, the looks up with a ‘thinking’ look amongst his face. “Oh Sean, oh Sean. You know, you should look out for your tendency to gloss over your own shortcomings kid, I wouldn’t dwell on them. I would change them.” Syxx says to himself, but towards the camera. “If I was you, I wouldn’t worry yourself about any of the titles, or the 4 Winds Trophy. I would worry about your opponent this week, you know why? Because he’s a Leo. A Leo is someone that has great nerve! Someone that is gutsy, fearless, and nevertheless fun to be around.” Syxx smirks at the camera. “Fun to be around, unless you’re across the ring from that Leo. And guess what, you are!” Syxx scratches his cheek, then glances towards the camera. “You know, I’m not as stupid as you think. I talk to people backstage, you know. I hear the things you say, and you say I’m past my prime? You say that I impressed you in the other fed, but I’m lacking my ring ability in this fed? You couldn’t be more wrong, kid. You couldn’t be more jackassed to the idea then Jay Phoenix actually being good. I know he’s not, but you don’t. Know why? Because you, my son, must be on the best hallucinogen the world can offer. I was at the arena in Phoenix today, when I was watching a taping of the autograph session, I got sick to my stomach. You actually took money from a little fat girl! She could have eaten some cake or something for that, now she’ll starve. And you can’t write some little child’s name? Someone that looks up to you, someone that thinks you’re something! The only people that actually like you, the little kids. And you treat them worse then I’m going to treat you this Thursday at Tempest. I can’t believe you, it’s pathetic.” Syxx throws an evil look of disgust towards the camera and spits in the trash can aside his lounge chair. “And to say that losing to Patti Anderson should make me feel disgraced? Patti is damn good professional wrestler. You lost to Jaime, okay. Jaime, I’ve seen better wrestling on my Xbox.” Syxx makes a chuckle. “Just wait until its our time in the ring, son. You will see if I’m past my prime, I will take your ass down. Technical as hell.” Syxx stands up. “And I’ll leave you with this, if you can figure it out, you will beat me tomorrow, if you can’t. The only thing you’ll see is the lights as Chastity counts the one, two, three.” Syxx clears his throat. “After socialism, Fascism combats the whole complex system of democratic ideology, and repudiates it, whether in its theoretical premises or in its practical application.” Syxx sits down, reading his paper once again. ***** During waking hours there are varying levels of consciousness. When a person learns something new, attention is focused on the task at hand, excluding other stimuli; once learned, the task can be done automatically, even while a person is engaged in another activity. So no matter what, you are either, used to losing, or going to very accustom to losing. You see, I’m not used to losing, but I do admit it happens. But you know what I do about that? Work harder, I feel sorry for you, this week I prove I’m worthy of Jay Phoenix’s title, and he knows it. Summers, pray to the lord, for he is god. Pray to me, for I hold your life in my hands. During sleep, levels of consciousness also vary. Electroencephalograms demonstrate five stages of sleep. Rapid Eye Movement sleep is the dreaming stage, when brain activity resembles wakefulness and the eyes move rapidly under the eyelids. And you seem to be stuck in that state of dreaming Summers. People cycle through all five stages several times in the night, with longer and longer Rapid Eye Movement periods. And you shall remain in that state of nothingness as long as you’re across the ring from me. My job is to win, my job is to humiliate, and I love my job!
