Cultural Differences Aside Blaise 4WW Exposures #891 Date: 8/23/04 Location: Not in Yokohama Yokohama, Nagoya, Tokyo. What do they all have in common? They’re the ending of the 4WW’s Pacific Rim tour and three of Japan’s largest cities when it comes to cultural power. Yokohama is the location of this week’s show. The second largest city in Japan, and at one time its capital. Only 30 miles from the main city of Tokyo though, it’smarred by being a big hustle and bustle place just like it’s ‘sister city’. Next week takes the federation a bit south by a few hours as they go to another former capital in Nagoya, but a place that has more cultural signifigance then even Tokyo itself. A busy schedule that would drive most of the people nuts. Fortunatly on the 4WW roster, there are a few that find Japan to be an enjoyable place to be. Ukyo Kushinaga and her brother are natives of Tokyo. Blaise McClancy grew up going to school in Okinawa. There are a few others, but very few. Two of them are obvious. Chris West and Eddie B. Two members of a stable known as unStable. Once known as Impact. Two guys who are teammates with such wonderful members such as: Robb Hardy and Lucious Lady Luck Kris Kanton Those names sound familiar? I sure as hell hope they do. For those names are the current hit list of people who have fallen to either the grouping of Ukyo Kushinaga or Blaise McClancy over the last few weeks. Robb Hardy and Triple L got a shot at the East Wind Titles and failed. Kris Kanton faced off against Blaise last week and found himself on the receiving end of a beating even worse then he’d gotten from the man named Darek DeAngelo. Now, this week, it all comes to a head. The real force behind the KnightMare Brigade now, the team of Future Shock is about to take on the other two members of unStable and teach them their place in life. Below them. Nothing but bottom feeders, as Blaise has called them before. Two more to go. Two more are being raised to the main event status to try to remove the East Wind Titles from the waists of FutureShock. Two more will fail. They can’t have what they don’t deserve. They can’t have what they can’t get. The story of your lives, and even those of FutureShock’s. West and Eddie B are a small bump in the way of Blaise becoming what he once was, and what Ukyo wants to be. Welcome to our world. We now go to… Yokohama? No. Not Yokohama. For now that things are on the mainland of Japan, there is a much easier way of doing things. For Japan fortunatly has something called the bullet train, which has one of the best records on the world. The next few weeks of travelilng are something that needs to be done with a plan. That plan was presented by Ukyo. Being from the area, she suggested a place they could all get away from it all. Using a Japanese Ryokan, a type of inn that includes full on Traditional style Japanese living. Also including all that you would expect from a nice get away. Finding themselves saying in Hakone, a 75 minute train ride south of Tokyo. A nice half way point between Tokyo/Yokohama and Kyoto/Nagoya means that they can stay there over the next few weeks and not have to be changing constantly. They’re at a Ryokan in the Shinzouoka prefecture, in the eastern shadow of Mt. Fuji, right along the rail lines that serve the bullet trains of Japan. Staying now at the Arai Ryokan, a luxury level one. They’ve rented one of the of the “Momji” detatched houses, or a cabin like structure and “Yoshi” superior room for Blaise. This will allow them to be within a few hours train and auto ride from any of the events here, and serve as a centralized ‘base of operations’ for everything that needs to be done, including promotional stuff and just meet and greets. And it’ll give them some time for relaxation. And we now find Ukyo Kushinaga doing just that as she’s sitting in a pool of water. Only her head is above the water, her shoulder length blue hair flowing out from her. Steam can be seen rising from the surface as she leans her head back, using the hot mineral springs to soothe the hurts and aches common to proffesional wrestlers. As she lays there, she’s joined on the outside by Charlene Kwon. Charlene stands there in a two piece swimsuit in orange. IT’s got a modest cut. She’s already wet, but is carrying a small basket with a couple of towels and what looks to be a book. “Hey, Ukyo. Climbing in.” She taps the water with her foot to get Ukyo’s attention. Ukyo’s eyes open and she stands up slightly, the water still at her shoulder’s level. “Come on in, Charlene. Just trying to relax. Why aren’t you dressed for the baths?” “You think I’d come down her naked? I know these places come from India, to China and then to Japan, but this is stil weird. I passed by an old man with a lot of tattoos on the way in. He was in the area where you actually wash, and he was all wrinkled and that.” Chalrene starts to climb into the hot water, wincing as she does. She slides down to her neck and then starts to get used to the water temperature. Finally she moves to the side, where she ends up sitting on a submerged bench that raises her partially out of the water. She reaches over into the basket and pulls out the softcover book that she had brought with her, opening it up to the marked page. Ukyo moves over to one of the benches and sits down. However, at six feet tall, the woman comes much more out of the water then Charlene does. Fortunatly for Charlene, as Ukyo is dressed in the traditional manner for the Onsen hot springs baths, her long hair falls into place to cover her breasts. “What you reading Char?” Charlene turns towards her and notices her sitting there, and then quickly turns her face away. “Uh, just a book on Irish Mythology I found.” “Really, what’s got you interested in that?” “Oh, maybe your tag team partner. There were some things that Kyo said about Sean McClancy in one of his interviews that I found interesting so I decided to look them up.” Charlene looks back at Ukyo. Ukyo just grins at her, knowing all to well how embarassed Charlene could get. She rose a bit more out of the water, and went up onto the stone side of the tub. Charlene’s face went a bright red as the now naked Ukyo climbs out and heads over to a small pump handle of cold water next to the tub. The cold water is used to help control the body tempterature when you’ve been in the hot springs too long. She sits down onto a small stool and starts to pump cold water into a bucket there, and then pouring it onto her body. She keeps her positioning as modest as possible to not ‘scare’ Charlene off. “Damn…” Charlene exclaimed. “What?” “Never mind. Just commenting on some of your ‘genetics’. I’d prefer to not be seeing them, but you had this idea to do one of these types of hotels.” “We got a room with a private bath. You could have used it. On the other hand, I’ve used the Sentos in Tokyo and the Onsen out in the natural areas for years. There’s nothing to be worried about. The only ones who would oogle are the gaijin that think this is a voyeur sport.” “I know.” “So what did you find interesting that Kyo mentioned about Patti?” “It wasn’t about Patti.” At that Ukyo stopped pouring the colder water, and took a must more intested stance. “It was about her boyfriend… Sean McClancy.” “The big cousin of Blaise. What did you find intesting?” “Well remember when Kyo went to Australia and got into her apparement?” “Yeah.” “He called Sean McClancy a Dryad.” “So?” “Come on. You have seen the things that were after him as Shoudou. You saw that big thing in that campground and the glowing woman. You saw what he did to Kyo to help him when he was hurt. The entire McClancy family are dryads.” “I thought Dryads were only women?” “Only in Dungeons and Dragons. Here, let me: “Dryad: A term used for a very high ranking member of the Seelie courts. These are creatures that are bonded to a tree or grove of trees, but are not limited to be with them. Common belief is that there were only female dryads, but they are found of both genders. The female dryad is actually properly called a ‘Hammadryad’. These creatures are not only natural spirits, but also spirits of lust and love. Any mortal or immortal that becomes the target of a dryad will find themselves caught in a state of unnatural desire that can not be sated except by being with their dryad. For those that have sexual relations with a dryad, they would become the inheritor of what the Faery Courts call the infection. This ‘infection’ makes them only desire this creature and no others. Going long periods of time without intimate contact by their dryad can make someone almost resemble as if they were going through a very addictive withdrawl.” Ukyo looked on with interest but then cuts it off with an attempt at a joke. “That would explain the 5 months of PMS that Ms. Anderson had until Sean McClancy showed up the night Blaise was unmasked. But that can’t fully be true.” “Why do you say that?” “What about this woman you told me about, the one who gave birth to Blaise’s daughers. She’s not exactly breaking the door down to get back with him.” Charlene just shrugged. “I can’t answer that. But if you remember, this ‘game’ he kept talking about, he said it ‘awakened’ him. That means that right now he could be able to do this infection.” “Yeah, Right. And why is this important.” Charlene’s jaw dropped. “Are you in that big of denial?” “Denial?” “Nevermind. Are you going to be ready for this week?” “We got West and Eddie B., right?” “Yes. East Wind Ttiles are on the line.” “I’m ready…” Ukyo trailed off on the last part on that. She suddenly got up and slid right back into the hot water of the onsen that she was using with Charlene, looking off in one direction. “What is it Ukyo?” Charlene turned around to see the back, and backside, of a man who’s skin color was not white, but deffinatly not as dark as those from the area. A tattoo of a snake runs up his leg, encircling the thigh. Another tattoo visible, a Japanese kanji hidden in the shell of a turle on one shoulder, could be seen as well. While his skin was darker, his short red hair gave away who it was to her. She knew Blaise wasn’t a stranger to Japan. He wasn’t ‘Gaijin’ to its true extent of the word, barbarian. He was just an outsider who respected the culture. Both ladies sat there as Blaise went throught he proper steps to wash before he entered the onsen, ensuring no soap was left on his body before climing into the mineral water. “I can’t belive this.” Charlene was a bright red. She couldn’t belive it. Ukyo just stared. As he finished up he wrapped one of the small Sento towells around his waist and then started to walk into the hot springs area. Fortunatly for Charlene he had covered before he turned around. The other tattoos he had, not enough to make him look Yakuza, but enough to show how long he’d been in Japan, shone in the morning light. Ukyo started it off, still showing that she was a bit off with her partner. “So what are you doing?” “A little workout this morning, a little sweat, running, calestentics, and now it’s time to relax before we have to head to Yokohama tomorrow for another fan meet and greet at a Dojo. Or hadn’t Charlene told you about that yet?” Charlene just blushed as she looked up at him, quickly putting her book away. “I hadn’t gotten to that yet. It was a last minute thing and I wanted to get her after she had her morning tea.” “No big deal.” That was all Ukyo could rebuttle. While she was keeping her composure, she found herself staring. “Well, I see Char doesn’t really enjoy the Japanese bath house experience. I’ll head on over to one of the other pools.” Ukyo smiled, that is she smiled until Charlene spoke up. “No, please. We’re a team, right? We’re all covered right now, join us.” Charlene had opened her mouth and didn’t even realze what she had said until it was too late. Ukyo scowled at her. “I’d not want to interrupt.” “Well then...” Ukyo stopped as she was kicked in the shin under the water. Charlene had seen the outbursts and had been on the receiving end of the veritabl ass chewing for a week now. She was going to do something to get them to sit together and keep the team going. Otherwise people like West and Eddie B had already won. “Please, join us Blaise. Although before you climb in, warn Charlene.” At that, Charlene’s instict said to look up at Blaise. She did just as the small waist towell fell away from him. She instantly looked away as he then entered the tub without bringing up any form of splash. Once she knew he was in the water, she looked back to him. “I told you so.” Blaise looked over at Charlene, her face a beet red color. “Sorry. Guess I’m just too used to things like this. Between growing up on the reservation or over here, it’s not something I really worry about.” “I… uh… it’s okay…. I’m just…” “She’s a prude, even though she’d jump my brothers’ bones in a heart beat.” Ukyo leaned back, putting her hands up onto the side of the small tub. This pushes her torso back and once again she’s half way out of the water. “So, it’s more publicity stuff tomorrow and the final two members of unStable a few days later?” “Yeah. Like that is something really high on my list.” Charlene looks at the sneer on Blaise’s face and gets a concerned look on her face. “What’s wrong?” “Oh, nothing. Just pissed off that no one has taken the open challenge yet that’s worth a fuck. I mean, West? What’s he done? Oh, he’s the West Wind Champion? Why? Because he can do matches where it’s not how well you wrestle, but how well you swing a chair? Worthless in this sport. Truly worthless.” Ukyo speaks up, apparetnly to try to rile her partner. “Yes, but he is a champion. He beat Bullet.” “No he didn’t. He and Eddie B. beat Bullet. He and Eddie B. are again going to do it. So I wonder who will help them this time. Kanton’s got to be pissed off that he went down so easy. Hell, I didn’t even get a chance to make him take the risk. He just laid there and took the 3 count. Then again, he seems to be ready, willing and able to do that lately. For all we know, Kanton, Hardy and that slut Triple L will be there as a cheerleading squad and try to help them get the job done. Oberon knows it’ll take 5 of them to beat us.” Ukyo smiled. “No, I’ve seen both of their promos. Eddie B is so full of himself; it’ll be fun to pop his ego. And West, West… oh my fucking god. I swear he masterbates with that damn belt.” “That would make him a bit raw, wouldn’t it?” Blaise chuckles at Ukyo’s comment. Charlene returns to her red shade. “I think I’m going to go check on a few other things really quick.” Charlene climbs out of the tub and walks away. She leaves her basket, a sign of her planning to return. “She’s realy got to loosen up a bit.” “Well just think of what she’s going through. She wants something but can’t have it. Kyo doesn’t look at her sideways. Yet she’d give anything for him.” Blaise sneeks a glance at Ukyo for that comment, but doesn’t keep it long. He knows all too well. “So, you mind if I ask you a few personal things?” “No, go ahead.” “What was this Rebecca like?” Blaise turns his head, sideways to be exact, not expecting that type of question. “At first? Or now?” “Both.” “At first, she was perfect. She was Ian’s secretary in the SCWF. Everyting was going fine. She started coming down as my valet and everthing. When Sean broke my knee, we moved in together. A few months later, she was pregnant with my little girls.” “So what’s going on with that? I noticed you don’t talk about it much.” “She’s become a raging bitch to be exact. When the PWW deal fell through, and it folded before it started, she wanted us to move someplace else, get a huge house, and stuff, and then started talking about me doing this and that. I had money. It’s not all gone. Just I had kept a good portion of it in accounts she couldn’t get to. When it all fell out, she had drained me of a good ammount, and had left me.” “She has your daughers?” “Yeah… I didn’t even know where they had gone at first. At first things were really strange. Then I got word that she had filed for child support and was trying to garnish the accounts she didn’t have access to. At that time I knew I needed help and contacted Maeb, my cousin.” “That’s Fey, right?” “Yeah, her first name is actually Maeb. Named for the Queen Maeb of the legends and all.” Blaise stretches out a bit, moving to a position on his arms not unlike Ukyo. The two were only an inch off on height, and about the same on arm span. That’s when it happened. He stretched and their hands touched. She didn’t recoil either. She felt it though. Or more, she smelled it. It was almost as if she had entered into a room of flowers. All sickly sweet. Something that you can’t help but keep smelling, even though you know you’ll regret it afterwards. His hand rested on hers for what seemed like an hour, but to the two of them it hadn’t been more then really a few minutes, both got lost momentarily with the view they shared… Mt. Fuji and the lake here. Suddenly, Ukyo pulled her hand away. As Blaise snapped back from where his mind had gone, Charlene was just coming into view. “Hey, you two stop day dreaming and let’s get a move on it. I just got a call from the front offices; they want us in Tokyo tonight.” Ukyo was the first to answer. “What for?” “Well, how about some promo work for the whole fed, as we’ve all, that is all of the 4WW has been invited to the Tokyo previer of that new Jet Li Movie, Hero. It just happened. They said to dress nice. Which means you’re out of luck there, Ukyo?” “Is that so?” As Ukyo says that, she climbs up out of the water some and splashes Charlene with a good double arm load of water. Charlene goes from partially dry to drowed rat look rather quickly as the naked Ukyo goes for antoehr armload. Blaise just sits there, watching, a sly smirk forms on his face. Things were changing. West and Eddie B. didn’t stand a chance. Cut….. on dotted line……
