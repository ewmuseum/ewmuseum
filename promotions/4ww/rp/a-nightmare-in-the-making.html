The consistency of everything is nothing more than a repiticous nature in this game of life. The exalting of a skill manifested by growth and training are nothing except the expectations made in society today. In the time to itself, we only become that which we need to become out of necessity. The sound of crickets and owls are heard through out the air as the wind blows steadily keeping it semi cool. Taking time to slowly readjust to the vicinity paces are took slowly as we begin to hear the sound of water running. Turning around to a point we see a small bridge over a steady stream with rocks of all shapes and sizes below. The two of them sit side by side sharing a cigarette. El Lobo takes a puff and then passes to his love so she can take a hit as well. El Lobo lifts his knee up and unconsciously rests his hand over his knee while enjoying the solitude of the dusk.} Angél: Amor you did very well on Domingo. You survived the Hell Hole match, which not many people can say they have. This week you have a chance to become the number one contender for the Nightmare title… El Lobo: The Nightmare Title indeed. I am one known to make comebacks and win titles as the underdog. They may see me as a weak freak with no chance of winning. We see a luchador that will fight his hardest and give his all so that he can prove beyond any doubt… that I can master the four winds. Ye hold not thy knowledge that the Master had chose me to be the South to the North. The darkness to the light, the good to the evil. With the correct given time I will prove that this Nightmare title, is my dream come true. Heh… Heh… Heh {El Lobo takes a puff before the smoke filters into the screen fading it into a dark black.}
