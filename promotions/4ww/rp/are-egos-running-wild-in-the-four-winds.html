Are Egos Running Wild in the Four Winds

9 August 2003

Are Egos Running Wild in the Four Winds Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #89 Date: Saturday August 9, 2003 Location: The Ranch Ego - noun - The self thinking, feeling, and acting distinct from the external world. Egocentric - adjative- Thinking, observing, and regarding oneself as the object of all experiences. Two words, similar meaning. Destiny spins the arrow of fate, watching it land directly on, oh wow, look at this, its landed on almost everyone in the federation. They all have a problem with egos of some sort. Even the 'Darling of the 4 WW' has a problem with an ego from time to time, and unlike most her ego can be justified to a point. She worked hard to get where she is. And reguardless of what Fey says, she did not use her 'tits and ass' to get where she is. Sean trained her to be able to defend herself, how was she to know she would kick ass and take some names in the process. Now her ego was being tested severely this weekend, against TWO wrestlers who are good, but not good enough to take her title away from her. That honor, has been reserved for someone who deserves it, but Destiny won't reveal who she will drop that title to. That my friends, will be a suprise. Lets take a moment to exam Psymon and Pajaro. Both men have egos. Both man hide well behind their egos, they let there actions speak for themselves. They go into the ring, do their job and get out. They go back to their locker room, shower, change and head out. Psymon, with his girlfriend. Pajaro, he probably has a blow up doll, with a picture of Patti taped to its face. How about the other ego's on this list: Summers? Yup. The man has a huge ego. It's amazing that his ego hasn't chased poor Eliza Murphy away from him. I guess when your out to prove something to someone, you overlook the ego trips that they put on. Annie Leanne? A woman sent in by Fey to break up Patti's happy life. You guessed it, she's got an ego, too. What in the world was Sean thinking when he agreed to be fixed up with Dracula's daughter? My guess is that McClancy decided he wanted a warm woman, not an pagos kyros skyla. Of course, the list is rather long, and Madame Destiny doesn't have all day to analyze each and everyone of the 4 Wind's Roster. She has to many things to do, to many people's lives to mess with, and this really strong desire to just sit back and watch. Maybe let her cousin, Fate, play around for a while. _____________________________________________________________________________ Place: Front Door of the Ranch Time: Noon ______________________________________________________________________________ Eliza Murphy stands outside of the home belonging to the people she was sent to interview. She straightened her dark blue skirt and white shirt. She smoothed her hair back behind her ear. The camera catches a glimps of a bruise peaking out of her top. She went to draw a breathe to calm her nerves, and winced in pain. Her ribs were tender from where Patti's boyfriend had nailed her with a huge clothesline, instead of nailing the intended target of Sean Summers. The one man that Patti had promised to help her get. The one man that Eliza KNEW she needed to make her life complete, guess that would be her excuse for becoming a journalist. Eliza sighed and knocked on the door. She waited a few more moments. She knocked again. Eliza stood staring at the door, a rather annoyed look on her face. Finally, Eliza banged on the door. She was greated with a rather nasty Austriallan dialect. The door opens to find Patti standing there, dressed in a simple black tshirt and shorts. Her copper hair pulled back from her face in a single ponytail, so unlike her trademark braids. The butterfly bandage still above her eye, covering up the stitches she received earlier in the week. A peek inside the room, shows Sean McClancy, reclining on the couch. Patti: Would you bloody hell wait a damn minute? Eliza: Suga', I don't have all day. I do have another interview to conduct yet today. Patti: Summers will just have to wait about getting his choros stroked by you. He'll survive a few minutes without you. Patti steps away from the door and allows the 'Twit' to enter her home. Eliza took in the view of the home around her. A modest home, furninshed with only the best that money could buy. Patti pointed to a chair sitting facing the couch. Eliza and the camera crew moved into the spot. A camere crew memeber set up a single camera in front of the couch. A boom operator made sure that the microphone was in place. A makeup artist came out of nowhere and began to play with Patti's hair and face. Patti: Bloody hell get away from me bitch. The last Shelia that played with me make up, wound up making me looking a pin up girl. The makeup artist moved away from Patti, and went to work on Sean. Eliza: Patti, my darling, this interview is going to cover three things. Your thoughts on Sean Summers, Fey McClancy, and your opponets this week. Then you wrap it up anyway you wish. Patti smiled at Eliza. The woman may be Summers number one fan, just too bad she was stupid to realize that it wasn't her that had been using the blonde twit to cost his matches, but fate and destiny are. Soon, Eliza will have someone knew to help her in her quest to gain the affection of him. A memory flooded back to Patti of a time when she used her wits to catch what she wanted. She remembered chasing her Sean all over the state of California. Sneaking into his hotel room was always sweet, but the best part of it all was when she became his manager after winning that 'Number One Fan' contest. An idea, she thought, came from her. Fey still hadn't forgiven her brother for that one. Oh well, she'll have to get used to it. She had no plans on going anywhere anytime soon. Patti shrugged off the feelings of a major fight coming her way. The cameras finally in place, the makeup artists and other non essential personel removed themselves from the scene. Eliza adjusted her shirt once more and prepared to get down to business. The cameraman nods to Eliza, a sign that he is ready. Eliza: I'm sitting here with the current South Wind Champion, Patti Anderson. This woman has done amazing things since her debut in the 4 Winds. After your little rough up this past week, how are you feeling? Patti: I'm just relieved his pathetic chair shot didn't do anything more then giving me a couple of stitches. Eliza: So let us get down to business, Ms Anderson. You have a big match this week with Pajaro da Pasa and Psymon for your title this week, how do you plan on taking them out this week. Patti: I have a very well thought out plan this week, which I won't reveil. I have nothing against Psymon, the man hasn't done anything to cross me. He's good. I'm not worried about losing my title to him. He's claimed over and over again that he's not in it for the title. Pajaro, he's another story. Sean's sister is using him in someway. How? Not sure of, but I know it involves her placing the woman she claims is betrothed to him, into Pajaro's corner. If she done it to throw me off, she's sadly mistaken. Annie may prove to be the downfall of Pajaro. Eliza: What are you planning on doing if and when Annie tries to interfer in your matches? Patti: I'll put her out of action if I have to. I will fight for Sean if it takes the last breath in my body. Once the war is over with her. Then Sean and I can get back to living our lives as we have been doing. Eliza nods at that statement. Eliza: As everyone else very well knows, you are also involved in a big feud with 'Simply Stunning' Sean Summers. This man has made it known that he wants what he claims is his. A title that he claims you stole from him. Patti: He can just keep thinking that this title belongs to him, in truth it doesn't. I have given my blood, my sweat, and my tears to get to this title. I've beat the odds to get to where I am today. Summers is just a little pissed because he can't keep his temper in check. He has faced me twice, and both times I have gotten around him. I defend my title each and every week with pride and honor. Being a woman on an entire roster with men is hard enough. Summers doesn't see that the more he attacks me, the more he tries to hurt me, the more he helps me. He helps me prove week in and week out that I can take a beating and come back stronger then ever. A chair shot here, a beating there is nothing compared to the humilation each week that Summers has to deal with involving you Eliza. He loses to some he knows he can beat. I know it doesn't sit well with Fey that one of her stars can't get that hard earned win, but you know what? I don't care. Summers has made it known his mission is to derail the current South Wind Champion. If he wants a fight, then fine, i'll give it to him. And as of right now, he really doesn't have a prayer of winning a legit match with me. I think he's bloody well afraid of losing to a woman out right. Sean whispers something into Patti's ear. A smile can be seen breaking out on her face. Almost like an evil plan is forming. Eliza raises her eyebrow in questioning, but knows better to question a private moment like that. Patti: As Sean here, has so loving pointed out. It seems that word is floating around that you, Eliza my dear, is the source of all of the confusion involving Summer's losses. I am being blaimed as the master mind behind your 'affections' for him. I'm going to go on record and say that I, in no shape or form, have been involved in Eliza Murphy's quest to claim you Summers. She has come to me TWICE asking advice on what she can do to gain your attentions. Being her friend, I have told her what I did to claim the man I love. Nothing more then that. Eliza and I have had a long talk about the 'manipulations' you talk about. They are all formed within that shaven head of yours. I will admit I have assisted her in her quest to gain your attention. I have given her advice, guided her on what she can and cannot do. Most of my attacks on you, are in retaliation to yours. I guess you are just too stubborn to admit that your ego is enjoyed being stroked by having a rather attractive blonde vieing for your attentions. Eliza knows what she wants, and its you, why wouldn't I want to help my good friend out? Eliza: You have mention Fey a couple times. What are your thoughts? Patti: I don't have a real problem with that shelia. I admire her for everything she has done in this business. Her attitude and her look is one of those things that have drawn me to this sport. I watched Fey and her brothers for a few years on tv before I even made the trip to the US the first time. My hooking up with her brother wasn't planned by me. Destiny and Fate set that one up. Somewhere, along the lines, Sean and I fell in love. Our desires for everything in life matched up very well. After his stint in the SCWF, we knew I would be flying back home right after that show. Sean and I had a talk, we decided to part ways. I guess fate decided differently. He chased me all over Austrailla. I think Fey just doesn't want to see her brother happy with a woman who isn't cold. I think Fey should remember how it felt when her husband married another woman instead of her. Remember the pain and heartache you felt? Remember the feelings of betrayel that ran through your body. You somehow managed to survive that. You accepted Mr. Childress first wife as part of his life, why can't you think of Sean and me in that light? I have never done anything to personally hurt you other then fall in love with your brother. You making my life a living hell isn't going to help either one of us out. Eliza: What about her accusing you of using your assets to get into the business? Patti: I have never used my assets to get anywhere. She knew that Sean was training me. She knew that with my being his valet, I would not be able to defend myself should someone come after me. It was Sean's idea to train me to be able to defend myself. Before she points the blame at me, she should look at who sent me the contract to wrestle. I do believe it was her that sent the messenger with the contract for me to sign. I guess she can't handle the fact that for once in her life, she is wrong about something. As for my holding a title, she should be thrilled that I am helping the federation out by putting people in those seats. If I knew the real reason why she hates me, I think this whole issue would be resolved. Eliza: Very good Ms. Anderson. Word in the locker room areas is that Syxx Figures, the current champ, is saying your title doesn't matter, that you can't hang with the big dogs. Care to make a final comment? Patti: Syxx Figures says my title doesn't matter? He's bloody wrong. It does matter. Has the man developed a case of selective memory? I defeated him in the guantlet match. I was the one that pinned his shoulders to the mat. I was the one that was the under dog in my qualifier, and both of the gauntlet matches I was in. I went through Bad Boi. I went through Shocker, and I went through you. I did the job that needed to be done. I took YOU out. Eliza: How do you respond to the words 'egotistical romantic'? Patti laughs. Patti: Egotistical romantic huh? Lets take a look at this. I was born a romantic as most woman are. Yes, I do have an ego, like everyone else. I just don't go around bragging about who I beat. Yes beating a great man like Phoenix is an accomplishment, winning the world title is an accomplishment. But to call me egotistical is a little off the mark. I caught his latest little promotion on a commerical advertising our next show. I think he missed his calling. He claims his title is the only title that matters, and that he is a shoe in to win the trophy that is handed out at the PPV. I think he is the one that has a problem with an ego, not me. I am also respondsible for those people in the seats, too. He can claim he is, but when he's defending his title show after show, then and only then can he talk about how his title is important. I am not in any way wanting to start something with Mr. Figures, as I am sure he doesn't wish to start something with me. I just want to clear the record straight on one thing. Every title is of some matters in the 4 Winds. The heavyweight, the nightmare title, and the other two titles. They are all hard fought. They are earned by the people that fate deams worthy of holding. Lady Luck and good talent apply here also. It's just sad to see power go to the head of a man's head like that. Patti shrugs at her last statement. Eliza: Is there anything else you would like to say? Patti takes a moment to think about it. A thoughtful expression crosses her face as she looks at the camera. Patti then breaks out in a smile. Patti: Sure Eliza, there is something else that I would like to say. To all my fans, thanks for the support over these last few weeks. I promise I will get around to answering all of your emails and letters. To my family back home, keep up the phone calls and well wishes, they are always appreaciated. To Summers and Fey, this war isn't over between us. In fact, I sense it is starting. To Pajaro and Psymon, be prepared for the fight of your lives this weekend, for a power more destructive then a tornado is heading your way. And of course to you Syxx Figures........Why dont you just filo dikos gaidaros !!! I'll leave the translation up to you. The camera pans in on Patti smiling. Director: CUT!! Great interview Eliza. Nice comments. We'll get the equipment loaded up and back into the truck. Then we will head off to the next interview. Patti gets up off the couch,takes a good long stretch and walks over Eliza. She begins to whisper a few things into Eliza's ear. Both women break out in a smile as the scene fades off.
