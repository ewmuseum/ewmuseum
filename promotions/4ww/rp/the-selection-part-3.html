The Selection - Part#3 Apocalypse 4WW Exposures #1094 Date: December 4, 04 Location: N/A The Selection - Part 3 Apocalypse ...It was Xantos, The bitter smell of almonds soon began to displace the fresh air in the room, Xantos walked around the bed and tucked over it placing his moist lips upon my brow. Standing back upright, he started towards the door. The footsteps echoed again, but stopped as the door swung open. 'Go in peace mother.' Darek and William watched in awe as Darek's only son had sought to poison me before he could even think of pulling my plug. By all intensive purposes, Darek would have been freeing my mind from this prison by pulling the plug. Instead, that never happened. 'Go after him Darek, Do not kill him I'll do what I can for Apoc.' ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: That meant bringing the doctors back into my room. Removing the posion injected plant. William in the end, with Darek brought life back to me. Slowly my eyes had opened and I looked upon the world for the first time in over two months. William, was a man from my past and I found myself wondering how he had come to be involved in this matter. And Darek, had he been given another chance? A miracle opportunity to bring life to someone he loved but took forgranted under possession? I believe so. With all of my heart and soul, I believe Darek meant well, and now he and I are free. He is free from the demonic possession that changed him from a person I loved to the person who put me in this position. I felt as though things could only get better. And hopefully they would. ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: When Darek had come to the discovery that our son, the being we created had tried to do what a demonic possession of Darek also tried to do. The whole situation seemd to take me by storm at first. Subject to be murdered by both my husband and son. What left was there for me in the world. I know that God exists, Darek at one time spoke with him, before his lunacy inspired rebellion. All triggered by Payne and the demise of Father Xantos Dimiscus; a man of honour, integrety, the man we named our child after. When Darek knew that he would guide the soul of Dimiscus beyond life, when Dimiscus died the last face he would have wanted to see was Darek's and fatefully it, it was. Being the Angel of death had taken it's course on a weathered being. He had endured so much and still fought so hard, I loved him because he inspired me. His passion, the loving desire he went on to persue his vengeance. It all seemed to make me want him more and more. For Xantos, he had told me that I was the one to blame. And perhaps I am. I was supposed to be the child's guardian, the one that would love and nurture him. I did my best to provide for Xantos, he had what he wanted, he had protection, and I saw him frequently. Yet his burning desire, that very same desire Darek posses, brought him to believe that my love was not looking for his best interest, he felt it had betrayed him. That's where it all seemed to go wrong. Patti Anderson, Xantos, Darek, the entire world conspiring to bring an untimely misfortune upon me. I fought hard to keep myself sane, but the more I was lured into the reality of the situation, the more I regreted it. Slowly I came to understand in a deep depression that my taking my life would be the best way out of the reality I faced. Everything in my world seemed to collapse upon me, the burden of the world on my shoulders... and I crumbed. Sitting now, with humming flourescent lighting above to provide lumination. I gazed painfully forward starring off into nothingness. For some reason I was intrigued by something that wasn't there, the thought that everything was going to be alright. I had been waiting now, sitting here, under the white light. Snapping out of the daze like trance, as the door opened and Darek stepped into the dressing room. Ever since he had come to rescue me with my beloved brother William, there was something different in his eye. Stepping forward into the room, he reaches back and pushes the door closed behind him. Smiling at me, his aura seemed to fill the room, uplifting even my bleakest thoughts. I was just thinking how could he have wanted to take my life, but I could see now, there was something peculiar and yet vaguely familiar with him. Trying to put my finger on it as he moved in to sit next to me... That was it! I knew what was familiar, he was the same Darek that I fell in love with so many years ago. Wrapping his extended arm around my shoulder, I couldn't remember the last time I truly felt this warmth in his touch, this tenderness. Just being around him was intoxicating, almost as if I could never want to harm another being. There had indeed been someone, or something in control of the soul of Darek DeAngleo for more than seven years of his life. And now? Now he seemed to be free. 'I pray everyday to thank God for giving you back to me.' He said as he moved his arm around my shoulder. It wasn't long ago that I wondered if Darek truly loved me, now here he was, next to me, telling me the truth, the words I longed to hear. Turning to face him, I leaned my head on his shoulder. 'I really miss him Darek.' 'I know you do.' He said in a comforting tone, 'another pain I wish I hadn't had to have to burden. Taking my own son to the otherside before I went myself...' There was an awkward moment of silence, Darek knew I felt mostly responsible for Xantos' accident. He was after Patti and Darek for what they had done to the marriage, but he only went after them after spilling the truth to me in the hospital room. 'Tonight is the night I bring Patti Anderson to her early demise, tonight is the night I make Kyo Kushinaga suffer the fate of the Modern Messiah. Tonight is Maelstrom V Winds of War, the dawning of my era.' The words seemed to come from nowhere, obviously an intended subject change. I went along with him. Tonight was his big night, the chance to become the North Wind Champion. Smiling, I pulled my head from his shoulder and looked deep into his eyes. 'Tonight is your night. I have a feeling that everything is going to change after tonight.' 'I think you're right.' He stated, 'as per usual.' 'I know you have business to take care of, so why don't you go do your thing, become the champion.' I said with confidence. He rose after placing a soft kiss on my lips, then looked down into my eyes, I nearly melted. 'If I could be half the champion you were, then I would be a great champion.' Always one to place me on a pedistol before his possession, I felt wonderful being there again. Obviously the blood had rushed to my face in slight embarassment at his accolades as I blushed brightly. 'You'll be the best champion the world has seen, I know it.' And with that, Darek walked to the door and said one last goodbye before disappearing down the corridor. Slowly the door closed behind him and again I was alone to contemplate this second chance, this perfect second chance on life. I had hoped that from point forward we would have the perfect existence. ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: As the night went on, I found myself asking God a for an answer. One that I felt I had deserved to know. I folded my fingers together and closed my eyes. I asked God for the truth, did Darek truly change or was this just another of his games? I asked for a sign, anything... I felt that I had gone long enough living a tortured existence, in love and infatuated at one time with a maniacal monster. Wanting nothing more than peace of mind, safety and the love of my husband, I wanted for that sign. Reaching down to the side of the sofa I had been seated on for most of the night, I flicked on the television and managed to catch a glimpse of what was happening on in the ring. Sitting backstage I hoped that Darek could overcome the odds and finally bring Patti down. [Syckle calls for the ending bell of the match. Ice pulls Patti to the outside of the ring as Darek lets go of Kyo and climbs to his feet. Kyo uses the ropes to climb to his feet as well. Syckle rolls out of the ring and goes to talk to Tania Brown about the final decision. Todd Dream runs over to the announcers table and grabs a microphone.] Todd Dream: Your winner and the NEW NORTH WIND CHAMPION… DAREK DEANGELO!! [Dream jumps up into the ring, dropping the microphone down; he grabs the title belt and hands it to Darek. Senior Referee Tommy Yung suddenly comes down from the back and enters the ring. He walks over and takes the title belt from Darek and then walks over and grabs the microphone. The former Tokyo wrestling hero makes his announcement.] Tommy Yung: NO! I was watching in back. Kyo Kushinaga never left the pinning position as DeAngelo did to try to attack Kushinaga. Your NEW NORTH WIND CHAMPION IS KYO KUSHINAGA! [Tommy Yung tries to give the title belt to Kyo, but Syckle walks over and takes the belt from him, along with the microphone.] Syckle: I was the assigned official. I make the final decision. My Vision was obscurred trying to make the count. I think I was certainly upset at this point time, well upset and anxious. I hoped that Darek could cap this great victory, but what stood in my way was a split decision heading into a huge arguement. It wasn't long before Todd Dream, a long time enemy of Dark Angel and commissioner of the Four Winds came to the restore order. I watched in anticiapation, hanging off every word. Dream, the last man I had expected to ever back Darek, did. He made his decision and ruling in favour of Darek. Hearing that Darek was going to be the North Wind Champion, I nearly hit the roof. Jumping from my seat in excitement perhaps this was the sign I had asked for. This could be the one thing in our lives that would bring us purpose and meaning again. Then the unexpected happened. The official theme song for Ian Chlidress III had begun to play. Knowing full well he was coming out to sort the mess according to how he saw fit, I suddenly felt my hopes fade. A member of the Order and now sworn enemy of my Husband, also the CEO of the Four Winds, he held all the cards now. Childress: You know, the sad thing is, I was just up in my skybox, having me a little buffet with some fine inported greek wine and such, and I was on the phone with your father, Aaraon Roark, in that little shithole baby fed I gave him after the collapse of that one fed he was running… and I told him how I was finally seeing you as the commissioner of this federation. I mean, you handle Bullet and his malignancy very well. You fired that dipshit Jerms for being a putz. You put and end to that West Wind Title shit-fest that was being forced to these fans… All good decisions… until this. [Dream just looks up at him.] Childress: You know how I run my ship. You just tried to hand pick the new North Wind Champion. You can't do that. If there is one belt that I want no controversy with is the North Wind Title. And now you've gone and given it some. I see that you, as Commissioner have called Darek DeAngelo as the new champion. Tommy Yung, my respected and senoir referee saw the issues with that and came down and declared Kyo Kushinaga the new champion. The Referee of the match, Syckle is torn because he saw what I saw… both had the pin. So what do I do now? [The fans are silent.] Candler: He's going to hold it up, just like the West Wind Title! Moraless: The 4WW with two vacated titles? NO! Candler: We're about to see it right here! Childress: Well, just earlier today I was actually having a press conference where I had every North Wind Title ever made with me. Including… YOURS… [Junior Referee Billy Smith walks out, holding Todd Dream's personalized North Wind Title belt.] Candler: What's this? Childress: Now, I'm not going to hold this title up. We've got one vacated right now… But I see only one fix for this. Mr. Smith… [Billy Smith walks down and enters the ring, and walks over to Syckle. He hands him that title belt as well.] Childress: Mr. Syckle, you are the appointed referee for this match. Can you please hand the Todd Dream Title belt to Darek DeAngelo… Moraless: Darek wins it! Childress: … and the Patti Anderson title belt to Kyo Kushinaga! Candler: What? Childress: No, we will have a North Wind Champion… We'll have TWO NORTH WIND CHAMPIONS… Your NEW NORTH WIND CHAMPIONS… DAREK DEANGELO AND KYO KUSHINAGA! I couldn't believe what I had just heard. Despite the negative thoughts towards him, he never denied Darek the victory, only placed him on the same level as the Deathscythe Outlaw. Sighing at that thought, I knew Kyo Kushinaga was nothing more than a puppet being played by Childress. Walking the line as a champion I was certain he would stand no real opposition as long as Darek was his co-champion. Turning the television off, I think I had enough excitement for one night. I beleived in my heart now, more than ever that Darek was true to his words that he had said earlier. Whatever drove that demon away, whatever this transformation was, I knew for certain that I was going to like it. The Angel of Death had become a good being again, working in the light of the lord, he served a grander purpose than revenge. I felt the warmth inside my heart heat my entire body, I was overwhelmed with the whole scenerio. A second chance, a new Darek (or old one), and now the most coveted prize in the Four Winds was around the waist of my husband. It was at this precise moment that I was certain Darek was the most unstoppable force in the Winds and as long as he was here, there would be order and justice. ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: This brings us back to full swing, back to the beginning of my story, the beggining of my new life. The beginning of a rare second chance at love and life. This was my opportunity, an opportunity I was not going to waste. The time had come for me to learn the full truth. To see what had really happened, to see who Darek would become. There were so many things to learn and in such little time. The whole situation happened in a brief span of time, it was almost as if it never happened at all. But I remember, I know it happened, I know because every time I look at Darek I can see the difference. I can see that there was something that happened to us that night while lying in bed... ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: When the world spirals upside down and everything that you know is tossed out the window. Your entire perspective of life, love, hate, anger, it all seems to morph before your very eyes. There is nothing that is as it once was, the times have changed and now I am presented with an opportunity. As if someone had placed a large golden key into the door of life and unlocked it for me, I have been beckoned. Beckoned for what? By whom? The questions are swirling for you now, just as they were for me then. How do you handle the actual situation when it happens to you? The only thing that you can think at first is a million questions, but then almost as if your mind is wiped blank, you freeze. Taking in the situation, your body numbs out the pain, the joy, the fear, and you become impervious to any feeling. You freeze, and when you freeze in a situation like this, your emotions shine through. Fear, I was afraid... Darek, he didn't move. His face was accepting, his eyes filled with joy and his auro seemed to glow. I could see he had taken the situation in a much different manner than I had. The look on his face when the moment came defined his ability. He was like a rock, shelter, support, my love. The one man I loved through good and bad, and though what we have been through is near unparalleled by any others, we did not face this with same anticipation. The fear ran through my body as I froze, and he seemed to be at peace. A peace that was more tranquil than anything. The calm filled the room and even all my feelings of the past began to fade. There was a force present that was beyond my power, beyond even the power of Darek. A blinding light, a voice, and only an instant in time with only a few words uttered. I looked at Darek and he seemed to know what was going to happen, he had expected it all along. There was nothing to fear, the light and voice meant us no harm, infact it was what had given us the second chance. The Seraphim, the voice of God in our presence, had only one message to deliver from the lord to his now faithful servant. Everything seemed to happen at once as the Seraphim spoke. 'Angel of Death no more, you shall be the voice of God.' The brilliantly glowing figure seemed to fade into nothingness and Darek was left sitting next to me, a smile on his face. No longer was he doomed to bring death to the world, now instead he would serve a new purpose, his mission would be that to spread the word of the lord. To be the best concience, voice of reason, and final word of justice he could be. The Seraphim's power, his presence all seemed to be absorbed by Darek. He Was still the same person, only now he would serve a greater purpose than holding any championship. He would travel to the ends of the earth to spread the word of the Lord, but to do so he would have to relinquish his position within the Winds. A decision that I know wasn't easy for him. Looking into his face, I knew that he had already made that decision and there would be nothing that I could say to stop him from going. But suddenly I felt the responsibility to take up his burdens in the Four Winds. It was a thought that I had been tossing around with for sometime, but was only awaiting the perfect moment. It just so happened that fate had once again brought me another reward. 'I know you are leaving and I know what you have to do.' I said to him. He finally broke his trance and looked over to me, 'Thank you. And I know what you must do. I know what you've wanted to do for a long time.' Darek points out as if he could see into my soul. 'I can see alot of people's desires, their passions, their secrets, this is the life of the Seraphim...' I only wanted to know one thing,' Do you support that?' 'If you want to walk into the Four Winds and take control while I'm away, then by all means feel free.' He leaned over and kissed me passionately. Before long the both of us had dazed off. ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: Calgary, AB. December 2, 2004 14:23 Hours I walked through the expansive collection of titles, photo's and other memorabilia from both mine and Darek's past. The world was slowly piecing together for me and I was starting to see the bigger picture. With Darek away doing his thing, I had an aweful lot of time to think, think and prepare. The walls are scattered in several 8x10 glossy photo's of different pay tv events. Many of them with Dark Angel grasping various titles in different federations. Stopping suddenly, I came across a photo that more than sparked my interest. I had come to the GWA in a tournament entry, a tournament that would determine the Heavyweight champion. After a gruling opening match, everything fell into place, one by one the people fell and soon it was left to myself and the Franchise Ricky Borden (one of the greatest GWA champions of all time), and I had managed to pull the huge win. Cementing a place in the record books as the first ever female champion, fastest newcomer to become champion, and only undefeated heavyweight champion with the longest winning streak. These were days when I was perceived as indestructable. Now I was losing many battles, the war with Patti over Raven. I was fading away from the North Wind Title scene... I needed to make a point, make a statement. I did that with Paul Williams and LLL, I took the Tempest Champion off her game. I eleminated her from the picture and offered Paul something I would hope he cannot refuse. With LLL off her game that meant one less fiesty vixen to deal with and if my plan falls through as I hope, then Lady Luck herself will have something to say. Walking out of the room, I found myself wondering if Caitlin would fall for the plot I had aswell. To take care of two birds with one stone would mean smooth sailing for myself into the Maelstrom Tournament. It would be great to head into the Four Winds battle with a South Wind title, but it wasn't a priority on my mind. I felt myself more concered with repeating history. To become the Heavyweight Champion as I did in the Gulfsouth. Reaching into the room I flicked the light switch and sealed the vault to the memories of the past. Now I was walking towards a new future, on that would see the Apocalypse bring a justifiable end to Kyo Kushinaga's title reign, one that would tie up the two most dominate women in the league and leave the door open for myself to become the greates North Wind Champion in history. It was only a matter of time, maybe for luck, I'll lose this South Wind Title match like Darek before I go on to capture the glory at Maelstrom... _______________________________________________________________________ Credits/Sources ~ MSV: WOW AFTERMATH Writers _______________________________________________________________________ ::[+-+-+-+-+-+-+-]:: ...To Be Continued...
