Cram Session

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cram Session Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1047 Date: 11/11/04 Location: Wayfarer Inn, Somewhere off of Highway 690 12:35 AM (The camera fades into a quaint, lonely motel somewhere on a stretch off of the local highway 690. The motel's sign blinks vacancy, while advertising at the same time its cheap rates. The camera pans along the empty rooms but comes to a stop at a room that has its lights on. The camera zooms closer and we can see on the handle of the door there is a 'Do Not Disturb' notice.) (We hear a slam of the door and the camera pans and finds its soure. We are now focused on a man walking towards the room with the lights on with an object in his hand. Due to poor lighting conditions, we are unable to discern what exactly it is in his hand. The camera zooms in again to the room with the lights on and we see the man with the object knocking on the door.) (Rustin Slade answers the door, nods at the man with the object and takes it inside with him. The object is now in the camera's view and we realize it is a VCR.) (We are now brought inside the motel room as we see Rustin Slade hooking up the VCR to the room's TV. He pops in a tape to the VCR and sits back on his bed with remote in hand. Rustin now looks at the camera and speaks.) Rustin: What I have in the VCR is a tape outlining most of the common counters to the torture rack. Next Tuesday at Stormfront, you won't see the Kamehameha Wave performed on me. (We catch a blurb of the tape as we see an unknown wrestler escape a Torture Rack with a twisting Reverse DDT.) Rustin: Next Tuesday, I'll give everything I got. Me and Paul Williams in our debut matches. It doesn't get much more perfect than that. With a Tempest title shot on the line, there's nothing stopping good ol' Rusty from taking home a win. Not even the hottest woman in the world! (Rustin realizes what he just said.) (Another blurb of the tape plays as we see another unknown wrestler escape from a torture rack by grabbing an open arm and dropping down into a fujiwara armbar) Rustin: I'm sure everyone's wondering at home how I'll be able to focus on the match after that brutal round of 30 second dating. Well have no fear! I pumped up my vitamin dose, and gave myself a shot of 'Chicken Soup for the Constantly Rejected's Soul.' (We're about to see another blurb of the tape but the VCR turns to static. Rustin, annoyed, gets up and ejects the tape. Before he gets a chance to pop it back in, he catches a phone ad on TV advertising ladies.) Girl On TV: Hi my name's Julie. Rustin Slade: I'm completely focused on the match. Girl on TV: Call me up and talk to me or one of my girlfriend's. I promise you a good time. You won't be dissapointed. (Rustin seems a little persuaded and sets the tape down, but then quickly picks it back up.) Rustin Slade: Like I said....completely focused. Girl on TV: Call now and you'll get a chance to receive an actual live date with me. Let me be yours. (Rustin drops the tape now and walks quickly over the phone and we see him dialing.) Rustin Slade: One call won't hurt. Next Tuesday, it's as simple as ABC, I'm going to win that match. And that Paul Williams, just like this date with Julie, will be a 'done deal.' Rustin(to phone): Hey I'd like to talk to Julie. (Rustin waves off the cameraman and the scene exits as we see the door close and the Do Not Disturb notice come into view.)
