Torture Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #66 Date: 7/26/03 Location: Phoenix, AZ Hospital Torture used as a noun is the infliction of intense pain as punishment, something causing anguish or pain. When used as a verb, to subject or cause intense suffering; to wrench or twist out of shape. If destiny were a real live person, this is exactly what she would be doing. Torturing sounds like what Fey is doing to her brothers girlfriend. A woman that she cannot stand. A woman who's only crime is loving her brother. A woman who has stood up to her. A woman who has done better then her male counterparts. Oh yes, the gauntlet. Fey had placed Patti in the gauntlet as a amusement factor for herself. How was she to know that this woman would out perform her male counterparts. And the fact that she had done better then expected was a torture unlike anything Fey had seen or experienced. It ate away at her. It was as if the torture had been reveresed to make Patti look better. Fey even placed Patti into battle for the South Wind Title. A title that our heroine should have won. Skeptics believe that Fey was behind the Summers attack on Patti. And now Fey has come up with the greatest thing she could. She found her brother's intended. His betrothed. A woman he hadn't seen in four years. A woman, she hopes, will stop this love affair between her brother and the woman he has fallen in love with. And what about the betrayal that Patti is feeling at this moment? We just have to take a look into what is going to happen....Destiny, I'm sure, wasnt expecting this one. ____________________________________________________________________________________________________________ PHOENIX MEMORIAL HOSPITAL PRIVATE HOSPITAL ROOM THE NEXT MORNING ____________________________________________________________________________________________________________ Patti Anderson awoke from a tortured sleep. She dreamed that Sean stood over her broken body and laughing. A woman that she didnt recognize was standing by his side Both were pointing at her, laughing, arms wrapped loving around each other. The dream seemed real enough. Patti rolled over on her side and attempted to focus a little clearer on where she was. The room didnt look like her and Sean's hotel room. Her right arm was hooked up to an IV, her left, a blood pressure cuff attachted to it. Her ribs hurt and so did her lower back. She looked over the side of the bed. Sean was still asleep. His arm laying over his eyes. His snoring still sounding like someone forgot to shut off the buzz saw. Patti toyed with the idea of waking him up, but decided against it. She rolled over onto her other side and looked at the tv. She had forgotten she punched out the television after Fey's little announcement. A new batch of tears appeared. The feeling of nausiousness returned at the same time. Why had Sean not said anything about being engaged to this Annie person? They had been together for almost six months, why was nothing said about it. She knew there were answers to these questions. Answers that a certain person could answer. She reached over onto her table, grabbed the pitcher of ice water. One way to wake up a sleeping giant, pour cold water on him. Patti quietly slipped out of the bed, and walked the short distance to where Sean was sleeping. Patti removed the top of the pitcher, tested it for the coldness factor. Then she proceeded to pour the entire picture on Sean's sleeping form, making sure all the water hits him around the head. Sean awoke with a start. His temper already flaring from being rudely woken up. He sat up with his fists balled and ready for action. Patti stood back and giggled for a few minutes then stopped quickly. Her temper starting to flair up, along with the tears that were getting ready to fall. Sean: Wha’ the fok was tha’ fer? Patti: How could you??? How could you do this to me???? Patti threw the empty water pitcher at him. Sean: Alriogh’, calm down an’ tell me wha’ th’ fok is wrong? Patti picked up a few more things and threw them at him. She didn’t care if she was missing him or whatever. She needed to vent her anger at him. He had once again hurt her. This time it was nothing like the first time. The first time she had confessed she loved him, and because of his hurt for someone he cared about, couldn’t return it. Because of his love for her he managed to find her, and thus they began the life she was having now. She sat down in the center of the floor, crying. Tears streaming harder then before her little fit. Sean stood staring at her speechless. Not sure how to react over her outburst. He walked over to where she was sitting. His arms attempted to hold the woman he had fallen in love with. She removed his arms from around her neck, not wanting his touch or his scent anywhere near him. Patti continued to sob uncontrollably, visions of last night still strong of her mind. The reality finally sitting in that Sean was having a fling with her, nothing more. Patti: How could you bloody hell not tell me that you were betrothed to another? Sean's reaction was the immediate view of a deer caught in the headlights look. His face fell. Sean: You know about Annie, I take it? Patti threw an ice-cold look his way. Patti: I bloody hell know about her. I found out watching the replay of StormFront in the room last night. I fell asleep watching the matches. I woke up slightly and caught your sister and you having a discussion about your 'engagement'. And what does she do, she springs it upon the whole bloody nation and world that YOU are promised to another woman. A woman YOU could have told me about months ago. But, NO! you couldn’t do that. You kept a secret from me. I bloody hell want the truth, and I want it NOW!! Sean sat down on the floor across from her. His mind racing a mile a minute. How would he explain Annie to her. He sat quietly staring at her. His mind trying to form the words that would explain the relationship he has had with her. It had been sometime since he had thought of or even heard her name mentioned. Sean: Patti, its been more then a good four years since Annie ‘as been in me life. She is an arranged marriage set forth by me parents. ‘Er family an’ mine an a lit’tle peace treaty between th’ two courts. We grew up together. We dated. Once I decided I wanted to take th’ career of being a wrestler, she supported it. I ‘ad t’ prove meself t’ come back an’ claim ‘er. My chosen path was t’ become a champion. A real champion. I left Annie with the promise t’ come back. Tha’ promise was based on me provin’ meself t’ be a champion. After a couple of years I hadn’t heard from her. Bu’ me mind ‘ad been made up. I saw tha’ th’ way o’ the court was na’ the only one. It was bad enough I ‘ad taken up the same profession me Pop ‘ad. I guess somewhere along the lines I lost what I felt for her. She was a pretty girl, an’ all. We ‘ad been t’gether a few times. Bu’ when I never ‘eard from ‘er, I figured she ‘ad found someone a bit more worthy o’ one o’ ‘er type. Then you came along, you jus’ took me completely by surprise, an’ I fell in love with you. Yer one I chose, na’ me mum an’ Pop. Patti: Then why didn't you tell me about her before? Don’t you think that is something important? Sean: It never crossed my mind, until Maeb brough’ ‘er name up. She’s getting’ desperate. I’ll admit it meself. Patti: What is she like? Sean swallowed hard, not exactly sure how to explain the woman he had become so distant from over the years. Then it came to him. Sean: Ever heard the song 'Cold As Ice' by Foreigner? Tha's abou’ the best way t’ describe ‘er. She could be loving when she wanted to be, but most of the time she was someone that I didn'na like. She’s na’ of th’ Seelie Court like me an’ me family. She’s, well from th’ other court. I used the time on th’ road to decide wha’ I wanted. As for now, I have NO plans on going back t’ ‘er. You are the one that I'm living with, the one that I share my bed with. YOU are the one that I want to be with, raisin’ a family. Now ye know why I’ve na’ been wantin’ t’ go back ‘ome. If I openly reject ‘er, it could cause more problems then naugh’. Bu’ then again, I was plannin’ on filin’ fer me Australian citizenship if I needed t’. Sean turned on the puppy dog eyes. He knew she couldn’t resist it. He used it on several occasions to get her to forgive him. He tilted his head down and looked up at her from below. His emerald green eyes going soft and an adorable pout appearing on his face. He had to try his hardest to get her to forgive him for this and he knew it was going to take a while to get it to happen. The wound his sister had created caused was very deep. Just then Doctor Roberts chose this time to enter the room. He was carrying a chart with him. Must be time to dismiss her from the hospital. Patti: Don't even think we are done with this bloody conversation. We will continue it the minute I'm out of here. Dr. Roberts raised an eyebrow at the young woman. Lover’s spats were something he avoided at all costs. He walked over to where Patti is standing. He opens her file, and begins to speak. Dr. Roberts: Ms. Anderson, all your tests came back negative. You have no signs of a concussion; your ribs are still slightly bruised. Nothing that wont heal within a few days. As for your wrestling career, you should be able to wrestle on Sunday with no complications. Patti: Then where is the bloody papers to get me out of here. I've got my training to do. The doctor shook his head at the young woman's impatience. He's seen people anxious to get out of the hospitals, this one was no different. He quickly wrote a prescription for some pain relief medication. He handed the clipboard over to the young woman. Dr. Roberts: I need you to sign by the 'X' and after that you are free to go. Just be careful next time. You could have really been injured. Patti threw the doctor an annoyed look. She grabbed the pen, scribbled her name on it, and waited for the doctor to leave the room. Sean stood up first, and offered his hand to Patti. From past experience he knew that she was going to need some assistance on getting. Patti, still in a foul mood from the little surprise that Sean's sister had sprung on her, refused the acceptance of his hand. She grabbed her clothes and walked in to the bathroom. Sean followed with the hopes of watching his girl dress. As he neared the door, he was greeted with it slammed in his face. She still wasn’t in quite the mood to forgive him for his deceit. She quickly dressed. As she opened the door to the bathroom, she ran into the chest of Sean. Patti: Would you get out of my way? I'm not going to forgive you for not telling me the truth right now. I have other things I need to work on. Sean sighed. He knew the one way he would get her to forgive him, but that would have to wait until they got back to the hotel. Sean grabbed Patti's bag of stuff, opened the door for her, and let her out first. As the two walked down the hall, her arms remained crossed. Her body language showing her on the defense. The two exited the hospital and headed for the hotel. The drive back to the hospital was silent, both lost in their own thoughts. Sean's sideway glances towards Patti; the look on her face still showed she was upset over what was said. He had to get her refocused even if it was nothing more then on her upcoming match. He decided to give it a shot. Sean: Look, I know yer goin’ t’ ‘ave this mess with wha’ me sister ‘as put before you, but forget it. We will deal with that AFTER the match. I’ve na’ deceived ye, as I never accepted th’ engagement. I ‘ad t’ prove meself first as I was an unproven son o’ a publican. Bu’ nothin’ in tha’ really matters riogh now. Ye need to concentrate on defeating Summers and Pajaro. Ye ‘ad th’ Pajaro beat last week, ye ‘ad the title in your hands. Remember how you felt as you grabbed the belt? You've got to go with that feeling. Its the burning desire that keeps you going, and I know that feeling is inside of you. I can sense it, the crowd sensed it. Patti nodded her head in agreement, but kept staring forward at the section of highway that lead to the hotel. Sean: Yer desire fer th’ belt ga’ ye t’ th’ finals. Yer desire fer th’ belt ga’ ye t’ th’ top o’ th’ ladder. I know ye can do it. Th’ fans know ye can do it. Jus’ go in and do wha’ ye did last week. I mean Summers is going to be ou’ t’ ‘urt ye, bu’ you found that yer assets are wha’ keeps him off guard. Jus’ like they keep Pajaro off guard too. Ye need t’ utilize those when you can. Patti nodded again.She knew he was making sense. Her stubbornness was kicking in. Sean: Remember when ye did the Apollo's Rising off th’ top of the ladder? Ye took Pajaro ou’ o’ ‘is game. Yer going t’ ‘ave to do the same thing t’ Summers to take ‘im out of it. I'll still be in the corner during your match, giving ye directions and being yer personal cheerleader. Jus’ remember that when you win the title, you will be walking out of the ring knowing ye bested two of the best people ye could ever face. Sean reached over and patted her on the knee. He noticed she didn't make an effort to stop it. Sean: An’ ye’ll ‘ave pissed Maeb Fey McClancy off t’ no end. She cracked a slight smirk at that. He was getting through. He know that his sister had done this on purpose. Still, she didn’t speak to him. They arrived at the hotel, still in silence. Sean crawled out of the car first, quickly grabbing her bag from the hospital. He went to the other side of the rental car. He started to open the door, when he noticed Patti had tears falling. He reached in side and gently helped her leave the car. As she came out of the car, she wrapped her arms around his neck, and clung to him. Tears falling from her face soaking the shoulder of his shirt. His hand automatically going to the back of her head, stroking her hair. He let her cry out her pain, her angers, and her frustrations. After a few minutes of this, her tears stopped. The hug remained in place. He scooped her up into his arms. He walked the few steps to the hotel room, a quick unlock of the door, and they were inside. Without missing a step, he placed Patti on the bed. A quick kiss... FADE OUT
