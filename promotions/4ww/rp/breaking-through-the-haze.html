Breaking through the haze Blaise 4WW Exposures #1119 Date: 12/19/04 Location: New York For the second time ever, the 4WW will hold an event that will crown not only the highest champion that it can have in it's books, but also the best of the best. At least that's what people think it does. If you look back though, the first person to ever get this accolade was a conniving, talentless hack who managed to get his friend to help him and basically didn't do crap to get it, nor did he really do anything with the prize itself once he had it. “The Technical Spectacle” Jerms wasn't anything that was really worthy of calling himself the best there was in the 4WW. But he was the first ever 4 Winds Champion. There are many factors that you can pull into being with events like this happening. Look at the first round match that Blaise has pulled. Paul Williams, the Done Deal, and a man who's proven to be a good Tempest Champion, even if Apocalypse did help him get that title. He kept it in his first defense on his own, and yes, he's got the old double booking this week because he's got to defend that title again. So the question would be, which one does he truly want more? Slayer, a recent failure when you look upon his record. A member of the DMC and one that may or may not still be in good standing with the “Dream Maker”. You'd have to think that the Dream Maker will be hoping that Slayer makes it through this crucial first round so that he can hopefully pull all the way to the end and somehow use him to help secure a victory, taking a note out of Featured Attraction's books. And then you have Syckle, the Dark Horse, a man who's hoping that things go his way finally. First the tourney and then the East Wind Title match... all to hopefully finally get his hands on a man named Eddie B. He could potentially see Eddie B in the finals of the tourney itself, so in one night, could Syckle ride all over the DMC on his own? Slayer in this round, The Infidels as he's helped by Black Orchid, and then Eddie B in the finals? He could only wish he was that lucky. What does Blaise think of this first round? He doesn't. He's won tourneys before. He's gotten accolades. This isn't about the titles you have, otherwise he would have already slammed them down with a rematch for the South Wind Title and the East Wind Titles, both of which were stolen from him, and in the case of the East Wind belts, Ukyo also. They didn't loose them in an honorable fashion, as their opponents had no honor. If it was about personal glory and fame, then he'd be triple booked in this event yet again... he'd be joining Caitlin Fairchild in whatever match they end up in and taking that South Wind Title back, and probably pissing his new cousin-in-law off after what would happen to her little sister. Although he'd make up for it after embarrassing Triple L once again. Then he and Ukyo would turn around and make sure that the Infidels felt the heat of the competition for the East Wind Titles and force the triple threat for the East Wind Titles, and they would come out victorious again. What matters is the sake of the competition. What matters is the fact that you go in there, give it your all and don't give up. However, Blaise has come to the realization that no one here in the 4WW outside of the KB will even think of trying to go for a fair shot. He knows that Slayer will not abide by the rules in his first match... He's part of the DMC. He knows that if the likes of Eddie B, Triple L, Apocalypse, Rob Givens and the like make it to the finals, they're going to throw the rule book out the window all for one thing... Personal Glory. It's not about their stables, or the federation. It's about themselves. The other stables don't have a brotherhood. They others don't think of each other as a family. Triple L and Zane Ozz if they made it wouldn't see each other as a group that needed to work together until they were the last in the ring. No, it would be immediately about the prime factor. Me... Me... Me. It's ironic that phrase was the catch phrase for a character in movies recently, and the biggest symbolic user of that has been nicknamed after that character by Blaise's ally, Kyo Kushinaga. The KB is coming into this to uphold it's honor and the truth that is... good competition. No, Blaise may not win. But then again, when he's cheated, he may not get the check in the W column, but he had undeniably... won the battle. When it's all said and done though, the KB will reign supreme in some form. Either they'll take the 4 Winds Tourney as all the others try their damnedest to step up for themselves, or either Kyo or Wesley will leave with the North Wind Title. Or maybe Melinda herself will finally walk away with her own piece of gold with the Tempest Title. Some self satisfaction is allowed, but when it boils down to it, you do it in a style that the others are afraid to do... Somewhere in New York The smell says it's near the water. A large blue van sits next to a large building We go inside the van and we find 3 people wearing full combat style fatigues, in black and gray, the colors of the Order's nighttime hunters. Three people inside, two women and a man. One woman is a brunnette with a rather athletic build, her hair cropped short and her features kinda plain. The other is a blonde with an almost hollywood look to her with long hair. The man with them is none other then Blaise McClancy. “So, what are we expecting in there? Some leeches, a couple of of imports?” The brunette had spoken as she was sliding large bullets into an extended clip. The tips of the bullets shone in the light almost irridecent. They were silver, but they were more then that. The gun itself was almost bigger then her, a large caliber rifle with a scope and silencer on it. The Blonde was loading up some clips for some smaller sub-machine guns and had a medium size crossbow with her and that had about a score of small bolts mounted on an under rack for it. The blonde haired woman spoke with a strong Germanic accent. “I herad there would be a troll or two, as that's what they're tracking on the ship. If they open the cages up, we may be in for a good work out.” “That's why I'm here. Hey, booky, any words on what all has been scouted?” A small, almost book worm looking man in the drivers seat looked back at them. His glasses were thick, making coke bottles look thin. “I've heard of a couple of leeches and their golems. Not to mention rumor that they got a lychanthrope with them. Porobably a Garou. Other then that, we've got minimal intel on the whole operation. I just know that I'm not feeling good about this one.” “You're a pessimist”, the blonde woman answered. She then looked over at Blaise who was strapping on an extra harness holding many different styles of knives, his prefered weapon. “So what's up with you rookie? No guns?” “Guns are for people who need a crutch. I work better with things that cut. I'm here becausse of the garou if you really need to know. “ “So you know they have one?” “Yes, glass walker rogue, they're suspecting it was picked up and indentured. Just keep everything on target, this is mostly an intel gathering mission, not a break the bank mission.” The blonde smirked, the brunette chuckled. “So the rookie is telling us how to do a job? You're not from New York are you?” Her “New Yawker” accent was almost as thick as the German girl's. “No, I'm from out West.” “You're the half breed Irishman right?” “Excuse me?” 'Booky' broke in. “Turren Mac Elatha, aka Blaise McClancy. That's the Mistress of Secrets baby cousin, and the student of the Keeper himself from the academies. He's been working with Lord Malik for the last few weeks. He's a rookie but not a greenhorn. From what I've been able to glean, he knows what he's doing.” “That makes one of us, huh?” A radio in the front went off. “Booky, the game is set, offload is started. Two containers are confirmed.” All of them looked at the radio as if it was a real person. Booky glared at them. “The Clairivoyant says the games afoot, happy hunting.” The blonde slung her crossbow over her shoulder by a sling, the brunette did the same with her large fifle. All three pulled up their half masks and then Blaise popped the back door and they all ran out, heading right for the dockside warehouse. In the distance a large sea going freighter loomed. The reek of dead or dying clams and muscles from the pylons around was almost too strong to handle. The three headed right for the large outside loading doors that were partially open to keep ventilation in the building. Blaise was keeping the jaws of the garou away from his throat with all of the might he could muster. He looked as he saw the collar around it's neck, the gleaming fetish hanging off of it. The device that the leech was using to control his slave. A quick survey showed his two companions now in a stand off. The brunette was on the ground, her rifle next to her as she held her shoulder. A small crossbow bolt sticking out of it. The Blonde was standing over her and speaking. The entered quickly and silently, quickly taking their places. Blaise went behind some of the other storage containers and took a quick survey with his night vision goggles. He could see two people standing apart from the others. One a male, small and wiry, he held a leash going to the collar on another, a sicly looking woman who looked nothing more then a girl. Around a lit circle of flat area, a couple of scraggly looking men stood and looked to be ready to take the large shipping container with a cage front as it came off of the ship. Blaise signaled to his fellow soldiers and pulled out two small devices from his bandolier and then flipped a small cover on them and pushed a button at the top with his thumbs. Holding the button down he waited until the Brunette, the Liutenant of the mission nodded her approval. Blaise broke and ran towards another stack of shipping containers and spun as he threw both devices. They hit and then rolled right into the middle of the spotlit circle... 3.... 2.... 1.... A strobescopic flash suddenly went off as well as a loud bang that looked like it was almost as much physical as it was sonic. All of the dock workers dropped to their knees and held their ears or their eyes, depending on which one got them more. One looked almost to be having a seizure, having faced the stroboscope full force only a few feet in front of him. The man with the leash turned and hissed loudly as he saw Blaise's heels as the rest of him went back into hiding. He snapped the leash, letting it go and yelled something in a strong language at the sickly girl. She then lunged forward and landed almost near where Blaise was, but... she was no longer a human looking girl, but instead... something else entirely. Not quite person, not quite dog, but all something that would hunt the hunter. The ships crew appparently hadn't heard or seen the issues below and the cage continued to lower. A loud roar came from inside and the cage shook as something large inside of it moved. The Lieutenant on the mission had taken a spot higher up on a cat walk and had taken a kneeling position with her rifle, using the scope to look right into the cage. “I have confirmed quary, intel gathered, going to backup orders, second objective is to prevent the release of the cargo if confirmed.” She wasn't hiding it, she wasnt' going to let them unload these things to let loose in the backalleys and sewers in New York. With an aim that would have made Wild Bill Hickock envious in his day, the dark haired New Yorker took the shot and a dull thud was heard on the inside of the large cage like container. A hand fell through the bars, pasty white and the size of a large christmas turkey. She took aim again, and pulled the trigger and not the sound of metal hitting metal was heard in the cage, a viscious green fluid began to seep through the bars and dribble to the outside. Blaise watched and chalked up a victory for this operation, the other troll was still on the ship board and as soon as they got word that the Order was here, they'd not want to unload it. That's when Blaise saw the Blonde German woman come up right under the mission commander. She raised her crossbow and aimed it right... at her fellow member of the Order. “Lieutenant!” She yelled and the woman looked down at her. A whistle emitted from the crossbow as the bolt flew in her direction. He saw the woman jerk back and then fall from the catwalk to the concrete floor below. It's at that time that he smelled... yes, smelled something behind him. He spun around just in time to put his arms up and grab the garou by both the throat and it's obviuos 'on' arm. It pressed him up against the containers behind him though, it's hot breath right in his face. “Valkyrie, you have done well bringing the Order's forces here for their slaughter. We will show them that they can't police those who do not want their interferance. Your reward will be good. Now, my pet just needs to finish with the other one.” “He's a rookie, she won't take long.” Blaise tuned his head just enough to get the full situation in. The lieutenant was laying on the ground, he rifle not far from her. A bolt was sticking out from her shoulder. The blonde woman was standing above her, a smile on her face. He looked as best he could at the garou that he was trying to hold off, she growled and barked at him, but she wasn't very strong compared to him. He forced her claws and teeth away from him for a good bit. “You bitch!” He could hear the New York native now coming to and letting their German sister know what she thought. “You set this up?” “Of course, you can only do so much good. There is good money to be had in this trade. Sometimes we need to step aside and keep the balance a little out of whack. This way we have things to do. Just too bad that you and the rookie will not be there to give the report.” She was racketeering, using Order resources to do so. Not fully a traitor, but definatly one that doens't think about the Order, not about the big picture. But one that would need to be taken care of. He looked at the garou, and noticed that she wasn't fully pushing the issue. She was sickly and malnourished. He kind, the city based garou were normally nothing more then scrap eaters and that. Time to act. He started to push the wolf-woman away from him, her face contorted as she dealt with something that she wasnt' expecting. She lashed out, and tried to get a good bite at him, but he grabbed her head and pulled the chin down to a knee strike which threw her backwards to the ground. She sprung right back up and came right for him. Again he blocked her using her throat, her jaw was obviously broken now, but as a garou that wouldn't last long. He pushed at her, keeping her away. “Hold the shooter, I'm going to go check on her.” They could hear her yelp from the hit and her barks and growls as she still tried to get him. “I think she might be enjoying this, maybe she's gnawing on his thigh bone or something.” The vampire, leech as they called them, started to walk in that direction. Blaise knew he had to act quickly, he'd not be able to take on two, especially when this thing would just see him as scraps to eat. He could hear his foot falls coming up... and then looked the garou right in the eyes. Yellow... Yellow slitted eyes. Yes, Yellow eyes..... He heard what sounded like a growl, but at a different pitch from the she-wolf... deeper, darker.. .more feral. Then he heard what sounded like a yelp come from his current wrestling partner. And he looked her in the eyes again... brown, doe like eyes as he was no longer holding a beast, but the girl once again. He let her go and she slumped to the ground, he could hear her heart still beating, she was still alive... barley. But then he turned just as the vampire turned the corner. He saw him as he stopped and hesitated, his eyes getting bigger and a loud hiss coming from his fanged mouth. It was the last sound that he ever made as Blaise moved with a speed even he didn't know he'd be capable of, all he saw was the vertical cuts from his blades that formed across his chest. His blades must have been off target a bit as he could swear that there were multiple cuts forming in his chest before the enchantment of the spirit blades took over and turned him to that from which he came. Ashes to Ashes... Dust to Dust... The blonde haired woman listened as she heard the tussle from about 50 yards away. She turned to look in that direction and took a few hesitant steps. “You dumb bitch.” She turned to look at her still wounded captive. “And what makes you think that?” “Because I can tell you right now that your leech cash cow just got out of paying you shit.” “And what makes you say that?” She heard it land behind her, something large. She heard the growl come from it's location. Sweat formed on her forehead. She spun around, crossbow in hand.... **BANG** The crossbow just about disintegrated in her hands as the rifle went off. She turned and looked at the Brunette one last time... she knew the gig was up now. A growl again came from the dark, and then a scream.... A few moments later, outside one of the windows right at the heght of the catwalk exploded outward as something came through it. It landed on it's feet and carrying the woman's rifle in his hand and the wounded woman in a fireman's carry was Blaise McClancy. He came up from his landing and bolted carrying her towards the Order's van. The door opened on it's own and he dropped the woman in. “Where is the Valkyrie?” Blaise looked up at Booky as he slammed the door shut. “She didn't make it. They were ready for us.” “Shit. That's not good.” The van then sped off, leaving a good amount of it's tires rubber on the street. Blaise looked down at the Lieutenant who was just glaring at him. He fell back against the side of the van and did a mental check of everything... he didn't quite remember a lot. A problem he'd been having more and more regularly when out on a hunting mission. All he could remember was that he'd leave and then come back and Lord Malik would be singing his praises. What was worse was that occasionally when getting intimate with Ukyo, things would get hazy too. Almost like he wasnt' fully there. He started to take an inventory of everything. He's used the two flash bangs and his spirit blades... wait... His spirit blades weren't in their sheaths...What the...? “Holy shit, where are my blades?” “Back in the warehouse.” The woman was still with them, but they'd not cut the bolt out until they got back. You could do more damage pulling the barbed head out then it did going in. “When did I drop them? I remember having them pulled when the garou came around the corner.” “My guess was that you dropped them about that time.” “But the I took on the leech and that bitch after that.” She smiled at him. “Yup, and Lord Malik was right, I'm glad I had you along.” He looked down at his hands and tried to recollect. But it wasn't coming to him... it wouldn't come out of his mind. Haze, fuzziness was all he got. “Booky, call in the report. I'm going to need to take things up with Malik himself. We had a situation back there, we were had and I don't think it'll be the first time. We need intel and I think he's got it, just not telling us.” “Yes, ma'am.” At that Booky picked up the radio and started to call into their home base. Back at that base, a substation off of the subway 'R' train tunnel, a small group of people stood around, looking at the reports coming around from quite a few hunter patrols. Ukyo is also there, having just given her report from her mission earlier. “Your Dryad did well out there...” Ukyo turned around, still in her formfitting jumpsuit. “I'm glad. Maybe then we can go back to the hotel and get some rest. Tonight was hell, you had 15 teams out there.” “Yes, but one of them came up with what we needed to know. Blaise did well. Although I think he's getting closer to knowing his full potential. You know what I speak of?” She looked over her shoulder to Malik. “Yes. I've known for some time. He's more then he seems. I'm assuming that is one reason why you brought me into the fold.” Malik smiled at her. “What makes you say that my dear?” “Because I can control him if he gets out of hand.” He really smiled. “Yes, but tell me, how did you know?” “I just know. Let's just say that he takes after his grandfather more then stome would want him to admit. The sly old fox gave him more then his name. I've noticed it when he was parading around as your assissin under a mask.” “You're smart, but that didn't scare you off?” Her tired eyes didn't give him any emotion back. “Should it? I don't fear him. I know what we share. But it does make him more then one of the Tautha. That's why you keep over working him, you're tryin got get him used to using it?” Again he smirked at her. “One day, you'll be an officer. I can see it. You're smart, resourceful and you take in all the evidence and make your plan of attack. Keep him well. It's been almost 3 centuries since there was something like him on our side. A halfbreed such as him doens't come around often. He's part one thing and part another, but the sum of it all is a greater value then it should be.” “I'll keep him well for myself. If you benefit, that's just out of the kindness of my heart.” “You slay me my dear. I would never ask you to do anything that you'd not be inclined to do anyway.” Ukyo didn't give him a happy look, but one of someone who was just tired. Suddenly all of them turned and looked at the opening as Booky opened the door and in walked Blaise carrying the lieutenant in his arms. Someone took her from him as he stepped further inside and then walked right up to Lord Malik. “You've done well Prince Turren... You found our mole, and I would assume that the situation was rectified?” “Yes, I did, Yes it has been, but now that I've done the extra work for you apparently, you have some explaining to do.” “About?” “Well, you can start off with what you know about my little 'epileptic' fits that I seem to have, and why when I have them I can take out a leech and a valk, without my spirit blades.” “Ah, finally you ask... yet I'm surprised you've not been told yet, it was found by your cousin and his new bride a few weeks ago. I'd figure Miss Patti would have blabbed to you. Maybe she can hold a secret when told to. Well, I'd tell you what I can, but I think I'll let your little princess do that later on. After you've talked to her, you can come and ask me questions. Or better yet, ask your sensai, he knows more then I do. I just know that you're a tool to get my job done. And you're a good one.” At that he walked away and Blaise looked at Ukyo. “Come on, let's leave them to their paperwork bullshit. We've got better things to do then listen to a bunch of cackling hens trying to figure out why your mission went so wrong.” Blaise put his arm over Ukyo's shoulders, and pulled her in tight as they started to leave the group as the Lieutenant gave her verbal report. She watched as Blaise left with a smile on her face. ~~fini~~
