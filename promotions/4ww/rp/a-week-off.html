A week off. Syxx Figures 4WW Exposures #67 Date: 7/26/03 Location: Gym I’m a business man, ya know? Businesses are the backbone of the world’s economies. They transform a society’s resources into goods and services that fulfill the demands of that society. These resources are the factors of production and include land, labor, and capital. In today’s economy, information is also considered a factor of production. Entrepreneurs are the driving forces who bring these resources together and set businesses in motion. Businesses are made up of people, whether one or hundred of thousands, who work in an organized way to earn a profit. And you have Jaime, here. Bust’s his ass out there in the ring for the, compared to me, measly few bucks for pay, while I rake in the money. But do you know why I rake it in, so? It is because I have the mind power to bend my paycheck a little my way, you know Jaime, I could rip you apart, make you wish you were dead. Easily, I already did it to Sean Summers, ask him how he felt after the match, knowing he got his ass kicked by Syxx Figures, you know why you need to ask him? Not only because it is a funny story, but because after Sunday, you’ll need the same rehab skills that Sean went through. Because you till will fall to the almighty wrath of what you call Syxx Figures. Syxx is a man of all men, he has a body that make the men want to kill him, and a body that makes the female species of a Human Being scream in lust. But Syxx is a calm, tender hearted guy, who doesn’t go ‘fuck’ing around. But all in all, Syxx takes one thing serious, his job. His training, and his technical ability that makes promoters pay more. ***** Six AM, the annoying ‘Enh! Enh! Enh! Enh!’ of Syxx’s alarm clock rings through his head as he reaches over and hits the ‘snooze’ button. He puts his head underneath his pillow and continues to sleep. A small pug puppy lays at the end of the bed, scratching, sniffing, licking .. ya know, puppy stuff. [Flash] Nick stands at the gym, looking at his watch. He gives a sigh of disgust, and picks up his cell phone, after dialing and pressing send, he puts the phone to his ear. [Flash] “Bohemian Rhapsody” by Queen rings over and over as Syxx sleeps through the noise. The puppy pug stirs around and stands up. He heads towards the head area of Syxx as he snoozes through the cell phone. Then once again, the ‘Enh! Enh! Enh! Enh’ of the alarm comes on. The pug, obviously perturbed about the whole situation, starts to lick the face of Syxx. He immediately wakes up and smacks the alarm clock off of the side table. He sits up out of the bed, and scratches his head. Placing his pup on the floor, he bends down and picks up a pair of pants, he puts them on, taking his cell phone out of his pocket, noticing the “1 Voicemail” on the screen, he presses and holds ‘1’, dialing his voicemail. He puts the phone to his ear, listens to the directions and responds accordingly. “Syxx, it’s Nick. Man you were supposed to be at the gym at six o’clock. It’s a little after six, where the hell are you? Get your ass down here, man!” Syxx realizes what was said as he deletes the message, puts his phone in his pocket, grabs his gym bag off the floor, grabs his keys and heads out the door. Halfway to his car, he forgot to feed and water his pug. He turns around and fights with the key, trying to get it in the keyhole. After succeeding and turning the key the right way, he flies through the door and into the kitchen. He sets his stuff down and grabs the ‘Puppy Chow’ and pours it into a platinum bowl labeled “Pug Daddy”. He then grabs another bowl, this one labeled “Dizzle”, and fills it up with ‘Aquafina’ and ice. He whistles as Pug Daddy comes running from a different room. “There ya go Dizzle, there’s some food and water, I’ll be back later on, boy.” With that, Syxx bolts out the door, locking all three locks and setting the alarm. He flies into his 2003 Lincoln Navigator, tossing his gym bag into the back. As Syxx screeches out of his driveway and down the road all of the sudden .. [Flash] Nick is on a weight bench, doing repetition after repetition when in the background, Syxx comes violently into the room, running towards Nick. “Hey man, my bad.” Syxx says, extremely out of breath. “No excuses, you’re in training, you have been nonchalant about your whole career here in Four Winds. You have Jaime this week, and you haven’t been in the gym but once besides today, it sickens me. Not only as your personal trainer, but as your friend, and as your fucking manager. If you’re late one more time, I’m going to go off on you Syxx, I’m not playing. Now at the moment, do you think Jaime possesses any attributes that worry you in securing your rightful victory this week?” Nick, still doing repetition after repetition stares at Syxx. “Well, I don’t think Jaime possesses to much threat to anyone really, except for maybe Sean Summers, but they both are insecure. They both are very intimidated to be in such a fed with these athletes, I’ve seen their eyes, it’s like they are twelve years old at their first wrestling event, ya know? Pretty pathetic, being around superstars like me, Sean McClancy, Ian, Fey, hell, even that son of a bitch Jay Phoenix is better then Jaime, and Jay Phoenix fucking sucks.” Syxx looks a little angry as he starts doing jumping jacks to get his heart pumping. “Well don’t be so cocky, Jaime has some skill, and that skill could put you down for the three count.” Nick smirks at Syxx. “No, he doesn’t have any skill that bothers me. Maybe the skill to be insecure, to be beat, to be humiliated. I’m not terrified, hell I’m not worried in the slightest way. I know I can beat him, and I know I will. So I’m not bothered at him at all.” Syxx says as he lays on a bench next to Nick. “Syxx, you might want to start working out now. You don’t have long to get ready for Jaime.” Nick sets his barbell down and wipes up his sweat. “Man, I told you, I’m not worried about the guy, it’s in the bag. A check in the “W” column.” Syxx starts his repetition. ***** People that run a successful business can make a successful profit. Ian and Fey, now they run a hell of a business. And I bank one hell of a profit. And Jaime, he scrapes out of the bucket. This week is going to be an easy week. Just like a week off. My job is to win, and I love my job!!
