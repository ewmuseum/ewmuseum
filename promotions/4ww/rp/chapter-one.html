=+=Chapter One=+= Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1265 Date: 5/22/05 Location: The Chronicles of the Deathscythe Outlaw Volume 1:The Coming War Saga The Reunion =+=It’s evening time at a busy airport that is located in Manchester, England, as some people are waiting for their flights while others are either boarding or departing their planes. Suddenly, the people, mostly men are staring at a certain woman with burning lust in their eyes from being drawn by the woman’s great beauty, as this soon causes some of the men’s girlfriends or wives to glare at their companions furiously from the men disrespecting them by looking at another woman. It’s soon reveal that the men’s attention is on a young Japanese girl of the age of her late teens in a street clothes that consists of tight blue jeans, a tight sleeveless black top that fully shows her bust, and a sleeveless red jacket is walking through the active place with a gray backpack resting over her left shoulder, as she is heading to the airport’s exit.=+= =+=Outside of the airport, she stands on the curb among the other people who are trying to catch a taxi to get to their destination in this country. She looks up at the evening sky after moving a few strands of her light purple hair with azure highlights at the ends, as she sniffs the cold air and soon picks up a familiar scent with her keen sense of smell while the dark sunlight hides her gentle eyes.=+= “He’s nearby,” she softly said. =+=Hold out her hand and signals for a taxi, as one slowly pulls up to her. The taxi driver carefully watches the girl enter his cab and soon drives away from the airport once she gotten settle in his cab.=+= Taxi Driver looks at the girl from his overhead mirror and sees she’s looking out the window with her long hair hiding her eyes, “So…” Nervousness shows on his face in reaction to being intimidated by her lovely appearance. “Where are you heading?” =+=She keeps her conceal eyes out the window, as she comfortably watches the scenery passing by.=+= “To the nearest harbor, please,” she replied in a gentle tone. Taxi Driver curiously looks at her after he heard her words, “The nearest harbor?” He starts to think a bit about this. “The only nearest harbor I know of is the Manchester Harbor, but that harbor has been closed down for repairs after a drunken ship captain crashed his ship at the harbor three weeks ago, and since being in construction the harbor is off limits until the repairs are done. Maybe I can take you to the Blue Sea Harbor.” “I want to go to that harbor,” she kindly said. Taxi Driver sighs in a dull manner from her persistence of going to this harbor, “Alright, I’ll take you there even though the workers aren’t going to let you in.” Like all the taxi drivers in the world, great wonder of wanting to know his passenger builds. “So, what brings you here, business or pleasure?” =+=She looks at the taxi driver with her long hair shadowing over her eyes, as a pleasing grin forms on her soft pink lips while a faint blush appears underneath her conceal eyes in reaction to thinking of a certain someone from her past.=+= “I guess you can say a little of both,” she replied in dreamy tone. =+=Meanwhile, at the Manchester Harbor, the constructers are hard at work with repairing the damages that the drunken captain has caused, but while the workers work there is something going on inside of an abandon warehouse that is located on the far away from where all the constructing is happening, as this is the doing certain silver-haired person.=+= =+=The sound of loud banging is heard from the warehouse, as the former North Wind Champion and currently one half of the East Wind Champion, the Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga is punching viciously on a large chunk of old rusty metal that is hangs from the rafters by a strong steel chain that shares the same feature of the chunk of metal, as it’s apparent that he is keeping his skill sharp for adversaries that comes his way in or out the square ring. He’s not wearing a shirt, but is wearing black jeans, his trademark black gauntlets with metal plates that he never takes off due to his awesome power, and he has his hair tie in a long ponytail by a worn out black bandana. With him not wearing a shirt, the many scars on his upper body is reveal, as most of the scars appears to be from a whip while there are two scars that are much different than the others. One scar is a short wide one mark diagonally across his right chest plate where the heart is, as the other scar was a long wide and incredible brutal scar that travels from his collarbone to his waist.=+= =+=Sweats slowly runs down his body, as Kyo stands before the metal punching bag with hands up in a defensive pose, staring at the many dents that he has already man from two hours of punching it. Softly pants while keeping his defense up and his soulless eyes on the chunk of metal. He soon tightens his fist and goes back to punching the metal, but he soon changes up the combination with a knee thrusts along with strong sidekicks that will make anyone’s knees buckle from just one shot. While attacking the metal with great fury, he starts to have a flashback from long ago.=+= =+=In the flashback, a batter, bruise and bleeding Kyo Kushinaga who is chain down to the stone floor of the Council of the Superior Emperor of the Realm of the Light, as he stands trail for a murder that he didn’t commit.=+= Superior Emperor seriously glares down at the convicted from his throne with a few guards in silver armor around him with wooden staffs in their hands, “Vile demon of darkness, you are charged with the murder of my daughter. For this crime, you are sentence to death by decapitation.” =+=A guard in black armor that stands over seven feet tall steps out from the shadows with a giant axe in hand, as he stops beside Kyo with a focus gaze on the Superior Emperor. Kyo didn’t even bother to look at his executioner, as he silently stares down at the floor with his long hair concealing his eyes.=+= “How do you plead, demon,” the Superior Emperor asked. =+=Kyo stays silent with head lower.=+= Superior Emperor sharply stands from his throne and glares at him furiously, “Answer me, you vile demon!” =+=Kyo slowly lifts his head and glares at the Superior Emperor with great coldness in his eyes, as the Emperor’s furious look changes to a slight nervous in reaction to Kyo’s glare.=+= “I didn’t kill your daughter,” Kyo coldly replied. “The only thing that I am guilt for is that I loved your daughter unlike you who was too busy screwing every whore you saw on each street corner.” =+=Great fury forms on the Emperor’s face and quickly signals a guard to attack the convict, as the guard swiftly lunges at him and connects with a powerful swing to the face. Kyo stares down at the floor with his hair over his eyes for a moment before turning his cold gaze back on the Emperor, as he spits blood from his mouth in sign that the strike didn’t even hurt him.=+= “You typical mortals always resort to violence when their burden called the truth is revealed,” Kyo said in slight amusement. Superior Emperor simply sighs frustratingly and turns his back on Kyo in sign that he grew impatient with Kyo and his disrespect towards him, “Execute him.” =+=The executioner lifts his axe over his head and glares down at Kyo seriously with a hint of death in his eyes.=+= Superior Emperor sits back in his throne and amusingly looks at Kyo with a matching smirk as he anxiously waits to see Kyo get decapitation, “Any last words before you sent off to the World of Nothingness, demon.” “Yeah, I actually do,” Kyo softly replied in a cold tone. =+=Superior Emperor curiously stares at Kyo and wonders what he has to say that is so important.=+= Kyo turns and glares at the executioner seriously, “Don’t miss.” =+=Superior Emperor furiously growls, but he soon calms down and smirks in a cocky manner from knowing that he is about to die, as he signals the executioner to kill Kyo with a swing of the hand. Kyo lowers his head and closes his eyes in preparation for death, as the executioner swiftly swings the axe straight down at him, but the flashback ends before the sharp weapon reach his neck.=+= =+=Back in the warehouse, Kyo stands in a defense stance, as he seriously stares at the large chunk of metal that is stain with his sweat and his black blood, but he soon sharpens his eyes in great alert when sensing a presence nearby. With a quick spin, Kyo throws a cross-edge knife over at the warehouse’s open double doors and watches the knife strike the edge of the left door.=+= “It is rumored that the Deathscythe Outlaw is the sharpest and deadliest wrestler in 4 Winds Wrestling,” a woman said in great interest. “And I see that the rumors are in fact true.” =+=Kyo watches a mysterious woman with long emerald hair that has been tie into two pigtails that hangs on the both sides of her head who appears to be in her early 20’s steps out from the left door where the knife is sticking out from. She is wearing street clothes that consists of green pants, an oversize yellow shirt, and wears glasses over her twin azure eyes.=+= “Who are you,” he seriously questioned. Mysterious woman stops walking and straightens her glasses while looking at Kyo calmly, “Just one of your biggest fans. I’m Ami Toyota, a magazine journalist from my upcoming wrestling magazine called, The Wrestler Tribute.” She approaches him and soon forms a curious expression on her face when noticing the nasty scars on his sweaty chest. “Oh my, I never notice these before. Did you get these scars from wrestling?” =+=Ami is about to touch one of Kyo’s scars, but Kyo smacks her hand away, as she realizes that he doesn’t want his scars to be touch.=+= Kyo heads over to the rest of his clothes that rests on an old crate, “Those pity mortals who call themselves wrestlers don’t have the strength neither the guts to do this to me.” He slowly puts on his Punisher shirt to hide the scars and glares at the young journalist seriously. “So, what do you want?” “To do a coverage story about the Deathscythe Outlaw,” she contently replied. “No one really knows who you truly are, as the only thing they know about you is what they see on StormFront. I mean who is the Deathscythe Outlaw really.” “I’m just your typical Outlaw who wants to start a revolution,” he simply replied. Ami looks at him seriously when thinking up a certain thought, “Speaking of revolution there are a few certain people that they will try to prevent this from happening.” She pulls out a notebook and prepares to write notes in it. “I was wondering what your take on this is.” “People are afraid of changes and will do anything to avoid it,” he softly said in seriousness. “But they can’t avoid it forever, as they will have to face it sooner or later in their lives.” =+=Ami emits a soft curious sound from her lips when seeing Kyo walk pass her and heads to the warehouse’s exit after putting on his long black leather trench coat.=+= Ami blinks and stares at Kyo confusingly, “Hey, what about my coverage story?” =+=Kyo simply leaves her alone in the warehouse in response to her query, as Ami sighs dully in reaction to his rudeness.=+= =+=At one of the docks that hasn’t been damage by the crash, the silver-haired Outlaw stands and stares out at the sea through his orange-tinted sunglasses, as he thinks deeply about the upcoming event that will be happening soon in this city.=+= “Everything is going by schedule, as there’s only one more event left before the finale. The naïve wrestlers seem to believe that they can gather some major points to qualify to the final round where only one will have a shot at the vacant North Wind Title. It is already quite clear to me that the one’s efforts are going to be in vain because when one of these naïve wrestlers does reach the final round then that one will become the greatest causality of all, as this one will truly start the revolution.” =+=Kyo sharpens his eyes in great seriousness behind the orange-tinted lens of his sunglasses when realizing that he has a couple of obstacles to take care of before the revolution can begin.=+= “With this being the final show before entering the finale, the Superiorities known as my former stable, the KnightMare Brigade will do everything to stop me and I can’t allow that to happen not when I’m so close to achieving my greatest goal. Because of my actions last week, one of the commissioners or that incompetent Dryad of a President book me in a match this week to face off against the current 4 Winds Champion. This one hasn’t been much of a fighter to back up this title. I already know that something isn’t right with this match. Why risk the chance to ruin their main event by booking this match, unless…” =+=He lifts his head up and causes the burning light of the setting sun to gleam the lens of his shades, as he perhaps reveals that his match might have a hidden agenda that involves a third party.=+= “Hmph, the final show truly brings out the desperate side of people, as they’re too anxious to tie up loose ends to conquer their greatest rivals before entering their final battle with them. I guess I have to play along with their little game for now, but the one I am facing who was once known as Bullet will sample a taste of true Hell before tasting the fullest of it at the finale. I already know that HE and perhaps THE OTHER will be watching and waiting to make their presences felt.” =+=He soon lights a cigarette and begins smoking, as he resumes looking out at the sea that shines from the slow setting sun. When placing his smokes back in the left trench coat pocket, he soon feels something and pulls out from there, as it is reveal to be an ancient scroll that holds vital information about a certain warrior that has an interesting connection with him. He seriously glares down at the scroll and soon clutches it tightly in his right hand.=+= “Also, I must give a certain one a message. Guess I have my work cut out for me this week. This should be fun and interesting.” =+=Kyo suddenly sharpens his eyes in alert when sensing a strong presence behind, as he draws out his plasma sword that has been activated and faces the intruder, but soon looks with slight surprise when seeing that the strong presence is from no other then his old friend and apprentice, Kairi Silverwolf.=+= “Kairi-chan, what are you doing here,” he curiously asked. “I thought you were off modeling somewhere in the world.” Kairi smiles modestly and stands in distant from her master with her backpack resting over her shoulder, “Model life was getting boring, so I decided to leave that world behind and become your apprentice once more.” She slowly walks pass him and stands on the edge of the dock with her back facing Kyo’s. =+=Kyo closes his eyes behind his sunglasses, deactivating his weapon, as he attaches them on the hem on his pants and forms a look of seriousness on his face.=+= “You don’t have to lie to me, Kairi-chan,” he seriously said. =+=Kairi gasps softly when she heard that he seen through her act, as she lowers her head to make her light color hair fall over her dark pink eyes and changes her serene look to a serious one.=+= “You’ve been sensing the strong disturbances in this world, haven’t you,” he strictly asked. “Hai,” she respectfully replied. Kyo thinks about the disturbances while looking up at the darkening skies with great coldness behind his orange color shades, “The strange disturbances have been happening ever since that warrior has risen up from the darkness. They’re after this warrior and their untapped potential.” Kairi turns and looks at her master curiously of wanting to know who this certain warrior that her master spoke of, “Who is this warrior?” “Someone who’s past is linked to my own,” he seriously replied. =+=Kairi looks with great shock in reaction to Kyo’s words not expecting to hear such a reply, but soon watches him leave the dock, as she quickly follows him.=+= Kyo walks ahead of Kairi with a serious look on his face, “You are aware if you accompany me once more, the assassins will be after you too.” “It’s my duty to be by master’s side no matter what the risks are,” she said in a strict tone. “I’m here to aid and protect you.” “Then welcome back to the Graveyard,” he spoke in a cold tone. =+=They continue to walk together through the harbor, as the scene soon fades to darkness.=+= =+=End=+=
