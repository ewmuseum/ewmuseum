A Phone Call From Home Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #72 Date: July 31, 2003 Location: Home Wanna-be, a noun, a person who wants or aspires to be someone or something else or who tries to look or act like someone else. Sound familiar? It should. A wanna-be is what destiny is placing in front of our heroine this week. Patti Anderson, 'The Darling of the 4WW', is the current South Wind Champion. Her first title, her very own to defend against all that come her way. Destiny, who has had her hand in everything, is probably sitting back, with a big satisfied smile on her face. Her charge had finally done it. Granted it wasnt the world title, but hey its a belt, can't be too picky. Out of all this, some questions have been answered, others haven't. Yes, Sean and Patti are still together despite the fact that he is still betrothed to another. Yes, Sean is still planning a life with Patti. What about the one obstacle in the path. How about the irrate twin sister of Sean? The co-owner and CEO of the 4 Winds, Fey McClancy-Childress? How is this woman going to react when she finds out that the woman she hates the most is currently holding a title in her federation. And what about the latest addition to the puzzle? A mysterious man was shown to be inside our heroine's locker room, stealing her towel. What was that all about? That, Destiny is happy to say, is a story for another day. We must focus on what is coming upon us now. A title match. Our heroine must defend her newly accquired South Wind Title. Her Title. A title she gave blood, sweat, and tears for. One that she will make sure doesnt leave her possession anytime soon. Someone who is a wanna-be is after his shining moment. Another one of the trelos nothos, the crazy bastards luchadore's of the federation. One who thinks he is worthy of a shot at her title. A man who, along with his pregnant girlfriend, thinks he has what it takes to dethrone the champ. He believes his luchadore style will be enough to take out her out of the game. He believes it will be enough to dethrone her. He believes he will be able to pin her shoulders to the mat, and walk out of this Sunday Night Match up with the belt. Is he stupid? Is he desperate? Or is he just looking to get his ass whooped by a female? Or how about 'Simply Stunning' Sean Summers? Can he consider himself a wanna-be? Sure he can. The man has made it his personal mission to take out the one woman who seems to get the best of him each and everytime. Looking back at last week, hmmm, looks like this wanna-be has his hands full with his own little fan club. How about our own CEO? Fey you ask? Well, sure she can be considered as a member of the wanna-be class. Destiny believes the main reason Fey is the way is she towards Patti, is that Fey misses the glory that Patti is experiencing? Hmm, good questions. Can answer's be given? Not at the moment. Destiny and Patti both would love to say that any of these wanna-bes has a chance at it, but neither want to answer these questions until after Sunday's match. The forsight says that Patti Anderson, 'the Darling of the 4WW', will walk away with the title. ___________________________________________________________ Place: Minos Alexander Anderson's home on Alderwood Ridge Time: 4:54 pm ___________________________________________________________ Minos paced his living room several times. A large, attractive greek man, his long, black, curly hair, hanging around his chisled, handsome face. His temper at full range. The latest letter from his beloved daughter in his hand. His frustations at an all time high with her. He stopped in front of the fireplace where pictures of his one time whole family. His face growing angrier by the second. Patti Adonia Anderson. His daughter, the only memory he had of his beloved wife. You see, Minerva went insane after the death of her youngest child. Mellissa, had died on the beaches of Perth. The family had been there on vacation, when a massive storm hit the beaches. Lighting came out of nowhere and hit Mellissa. She was killed instantly. Minerva was devestated by the loss of her other daughter. Minerva became mentally unstable after the funeral for Mellissa. She became verbably abusive to Patti. Minerva blamed Patti for the death of her precious daughter. Minos, still feeling the sorrow of losing his daughter, and watching his wife's mental state dimminish as each day passed. He committed Minerva to a mental institution, where she is getting the treatment she needs. He stopped at the picture of Patti. She standing next to a big Irish man. A man that he himself had come to hate. The nothos, the bastard, who had hurt his daughter not once, but twice. He replaced the picture back on the mantle and continued to pace around the home. His thoughts continued to stir as he remembered the last letter that Patti had sent him. A letter that had told him, she was coming back home. The man she had traveled to meet turned out to be nothing more then an gaidaros, an ass. She was returning home to pick up her life where she had left it. He was happy to have his daughter home again. At least she could begin the healing. After just a few days of being away from the man she had went to claim. The two of them show up on her doorstep, and appeared to be very much in love. He wasn't happy with this turn of developements, but as long as his daughter was happy. That was what mattered the most. He remembered meeting the man that called himself the 'Irish Bastard'. He was impressed with McClancy's manners, his strength, and his overwhelming desire to do what was right by his daughter. So, Minos didnt say anything about it. As long as his princess was happy, so was he. Then, this letter came. Papa, I write this letter with a heavy heart again. Sean is stil being good, we bought our first house together. A nice little ranch style, an extra bedroom that we just finished painting. The wrestling organization is taking off at full speed. I've appeared in several matches and gotten myself into a feud with a man that is an adelfi malakas. Summers, has made it his business to prove to the world that I, a mere woman, can not compete with the big boys. Unfortunately for him, I have more wins then he does. As you are probably aware of, Papa, I became the South Wind Champion. This little action has made Summers more determined then ever to defeat me in the squared circle. Do not worry Papa,I won't let him win. Sean has taught me well. As for Sean's sister, Fey, I have not been able to work things out with her. The woman is more determined to end the relationship. She has already tried to break me in the ring, but I have managed to take on everything she has sent my way. Well, not everything Papa. This is the reason for a heavy heart. It seems that Sean has failed to mention to me that he already was involved with someone else. A woman that he is supposed to marry. Fey decided to inform the nation and the world about this during a previous show (A show that I am sure will be airing soon down there). This is the reason why I am writing Papa. Sean and I had been discussing the desire to start a family. A joy that I was looking forward too. I began to think that Aphrodite had decided to forgive Cupid for marrying a common princess in Psyche, but I guess she still won't forgive it. Is our family destined to be punished for that? As of right now, I have forgiven Sean for not being honest with me. It took a lot of faith on my part to believe that he does love me, and wants to make this work between us. Heaven forbid he should decide to chase me all over Melbourne again. I really could use your advice on this Papa, what should I do? Please write me soon with your answer. Love, Patti Minkos sighed a heavy sigh. The weight of the world had fallen on his shoulder once again. His daughter had been hurt and was asking for advice. Maybe this would be a good time to call her and convience her to come back home. FADE OUT ___________________________________________________________ Place: The Homestead Time: Three A.M. Tuesday Morning Event: The Phone Call ___________________________________________________________ Fade into Sean McClancy and Patti Anderson's home, looks to be the living room. A home like atmosphere fits the room. A nice looking couch sits in the middle of the floor, facing the wide sceen television. In between the couch, sits a beautiful coffee table. A gift to them from someone important. A completely hand carved item by the other man in Patti's life. A figure appears, a woman, her hair messed up, sweat drenched. Her face still showing a smile. She must have finished celebrating her recent title victory over Sean Summers and Pajaro. She smiled as she looked at the flowers her best friend had sent her. At least someone out there was thrilled. She walked over to the refridgerator, grabbed a bottle of water. Opening it up, she took a long healthy drink. After draining nearly half a bottle, she went and curled up on the couch. Patti mindless grabbed the remote control for the television. Her mind aimlessly wandering, not really paying attention to what was on. After a while, she began to dose off, her head and feet resting comfortable on the couch. A dream set in, a nice safe dream. A dream of her reliving her glory of winning the championship gold of the South Winds. Then it turned into a nightmare. A strange creature, a charti sakoula teras, a paper bag monster. She dreamed of him taking her towel, and then running out of her locker room. The dream continued into Pajaro coming on to her, while she reamins screaming for Sean. Somewhere in the dream, a phone is heard ringing. Patti slowly comes out of the dream. She squints at the clock that is on the VCR...3:15 am, who would be calling at this hour. She waits until the answering machine kicks in. Neither Sean nor me can is able to get to the phone IF ya feel its important then leave a message IF its not important then try to get us later Patti came instanly awake when she heard the Greek military voice of her father. Adonia, analambano artho tilefono aftos stigmiaios eite I am erchomos eis Ameriki! <english translation Adonia, Pick up the phone this instant or I am coming to America> Patti reached over and grabed the hand receiver that was laying on the coffee table. She made a mental note to remind Sean to put it back when he's finished with it. She hit the talk button just before her father could finish the threat he was about to make. Patti: Papa!! Good to hear your voice. Minos: Adonia, word is spreading fast that my little princess just won her title. Patti: Yes papa! I won it Sunday night. I won it Sunday night. Sean and I just got home from celebrating again. She knew it was a tiny lie, but her father didnt need to know know what she and Sean were really doing. After all they were a couple living together, doing everything that couples do. Minos: Adonia, I received your letter. What is that is troubling you? Patti swallowed hard. Patti: Papa, you know I'm a big, strong, girl and I can handle anything life throws at me. This is just to big for me. Minos: Tell Papa what is troubling you. Patti: Sean is betrothed to another. He says he doesn't love her, that the marriage was all arranged, to keep peace between their people. He doesn't want it, they haven't spoken in four years. Sean feels that his sister is pushing to keep it, because of her extreme distaste of me. Wonder how she would react to find out that her twin is considering filing for Austriallian citzenship? Minos could here the humor in the last statement. He also noted that his daughter was trying to not let it worry her. Patti: What about this Annie girl, Papa? Minos sighed a heavy sigh. He knew his daughter was torn by the desire to follow her heart and the desire to do what was right. These type of matters weren't always easy to deal with. Minos: If you love him as much as you say, and he loves you the same. Then FIGHT for him. Don't let the kargiola win. Patti sighed. She knew he was right. She giggled at the thought of treating Fey and Annie the way that her ancestor Psyche was treated by Aphrodite. An evil smile appeared on her sweet natured face. Minos: Always remember Adonia, revenge has its way of back firing on someone. Use your mind to defeat your enemies, that is your strongest asset. Everything else will fall into place. Now, get to bed, get some rest and keep making us very proud of you daughter. I agapo eseis, I love you. Patti: I apapo esis episis, mapmaps. I love you too, papa. Patti hung up the phone, feeling more confident about the upcoming battle. If Annie wanted Sean back, she was going to have a serious battle upon her hands. Annie was Sean's past, but Patti Adonia Anderson was most definately his present and his future. Patti crawled off the couch and ran smack dab into Sean's chest. He must have awoken, and not finding her in bed , went in search of her. Sean mumbled something not quite understandable. Patti: It's ok love. Papa called to congradulate me on my victory. Let's go back to bed and continue our celebration. Patti quickly undoes Sean's pants as the scene fades to black.
