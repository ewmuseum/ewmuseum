There are outside forces that question a faith as it was brought into view. Certain things hold us when we are scared and drives us to surpass our expectations. A certain drive factor that always pushes us to carry on and on and on. When those things have passed away then our hope and faith slowly begins to diminish with it. We will sit there and wonder what has happened to everything that we’ve ever known. We even sit there questioning our faith and why certain things have happened. ‘Tis then when you’ll realize that dreams, money, rankings don’t mean a damn thing. That everything is indeed over-rated and not worth the fight to survive. Even on the eve of insanity will you hold the knife against your wrists about to use your one and only ticket to the other realm? Whether it be in Tatarsus, Purgatory, cursed to roam this world for centuries to come, or a ticket to the heavens on high. Out of all these trials and tribulations in this life, there are too many things to avenge and we are indeed running out of days. Some of us the last day will be sooner than others and others will be cursed to spend more time here than that which we want. Recollecting on past times where such things have come to pass I have noticed two types of anger and depression. Implosive, and explosive; explosive is the type of person you see taking all his frustrations out by yelling and cursing even to an extent of uncalled behavior. The implosive type is the one that holds everything in, and at one single moment. An action triggers an ultimate implosion where everything within radius is destroyed in a chaotic fashion. With only one chance you can go on and get over this “hill” or you can let it get to you and destroy everything in your path. Whatever you do you must realize that it never ends. It is all one continuous test, which examines your capabilities and strengths and weaknesses. When the end truly is nigh, trust me at that moment all your efforts will be dust in the wind. It will not matter what you do to redeem yourself. After all we are all just sheep in the pasture lost and looking for our way home.~ {The scene opens up as a dark chills overcomes us. A certain coldness, not weather oriented, consumes the area around us. We can feel a hatred, a non-loving presence around us. With no light around the only visible thing is the crescent moon in the dark night sky. We continue on in the midnight streets in the Phoenix area, suddenly a rain begins to down pair over the restless city. The sounds of rain splashing on the pavement is heard as are the sounds of frantic feet scattering about trying to reach for cover. We continue on and hear a faint voice on a phone.} Voice: Look I don’t care anymore… Just get the shit here. Yea I really fuckin’ need it chucha. Look get it here man I’ll be at the Korner Kafe. Adios puta… {The man stands there at the corner where he sits on the stoop awaiting his friend. The man continues to sit there in the pouring rain. The scene switches to the inside the small café where we see the man outside sitting in the cold night. Continuing in the rain the man sits getting drenched by the sudden downpour in the quiet city. No sign of movement and so sign of emotion emitted from the being. He just sits there with his hands over his knees carelessly.} {The scene then cuts back outside where we look at the face of the man revealing several glistening piercing over his face and ears. Looking closer we can see tears falling very fast from deep inside his soul. At first it looked as if it were the rain cascading off his disturbing face. Out of nowhere the silence is broken as the man speaks.} El Lobo: Oh Sunday how you have destroyed me in the entirety. Mi vida, es en el fin por que fui estupido. What the fuck is goin’ on? I can’t believe his drunk ass crossed that line. Where there’s love there’s lust. Then there comes blood and guts. Cristofer Oeste you think this is some kind of partido de tennis? I’m about to become the John McEnroe here, its fifteen love so where the fuck you gonna go chucha? I’ll see you on Storm Front if not sooner puta. Mis revanchas esta viniendo Oeste. I’ll get my hands on you again West and mark my words, you are about to feel the pain from dia de los muertos. You will understand what it’s like to see the tequila sunrise. When all is said and done you will feel the pain from my ghetto life. A sensation will wash over me that I can’t describe. A sensation that will lead to your decimation. You will be holding onto every wrong thought and deed you have done. Cris, you crossed the line, I defeated you for the nightmare title. You couldn’t handle that I am superior than you so you had to try and attack me. We seen it coming and moved, but you… chucha you saw Angél there. You were flustered and needed to take your frustrations out so you decided to hit a defenseless woman, pregnant woman at that, in the stomach. You sent her to the hospital and you killed a baby. My unborn child Chris, you destroyed a life. You killed, and soon I will get my revenge and kill you even if it means me sacrificing my own life to destroy yours. {El Lobo takes his hands off his knees and runs his fingers through his hair forcing it back. He exhales deeply thinking about all that had happened during the week. There’s a bright light and the scene switches to memories left inside El Lobo’s mind.} {EL Lobo looks on with Psymon as they celebrate. He see’s Angél climb to the apron then hears the sound of someone charging. Psymon jumps out of West’s way as does Lobo. They hear a thud and look over only to see Angél slumped down holding her side. Lobo quickly jumps to his feet rushing to her aid. Psymon jumps up going after West in hatred. West pokes Psymon in the eyes and fleas to the back. El Lobo sits there looking at Angél holding her close to him.} {Scene switches to the back where we see El Lobo carrying Angél to the parking area where there’s an ambulance waiting. Psymon and Uriko following close behind him looking extremely concerned. The medics quickly and carefully try to get Angél onto the gurney as soon as possible. With few jerks El Lobo jumps up there.} El Lobo: Yo’ esse be gentle. I said be careful holmes… {The medics strap her in carefully and load her up. El Lobo is about to jump into the ambulance as Uriko pulls on his arm. El Lobo pulls away as Psymon pulls him back.} Uriko: Tomás she’ll be alright I’ll go… Psymon: It’s for the better man; really Uriko will take good care of her. She will make sure she’s alright. Uriko: Tom tell me, if you went what would stop you from attacking a Dr. or nurse with little provocation? I’ll go and I’ll make sure she’s alright. I’ll make sure they take good care of her. Psymon: Yea go ahead Uriko I’ll take care of Tom… Lobo we’ll head on over after the show. You’re not thinking straight right now just calm down and we’ll be there after the show. {They sit there watching the ambulance drive off heading toward the hospital. The scene then jumps to El Lobo wreaking havoc in the back. Breaking car windows, denting doors, shattering door windows, punching car doors and then sliding down crying in fear and frustration. A familiar females hand is placed on his shoulder. He starts to look up about to expose who it was when suddenly a set of bright lights cover the view as the sound of a horn honking.} {The scene cuts back outside where El Lobo looks out to see a White Lexus with a black cloth top. He gets up with his hoody soaked and jogs to the driver side window.} El Lobo: Ok you got I take it??? Voice: Yes I have it. It’s been so long since you’ve been around Tom what happened? El Lobo: I became clean thought I had things to live for… Voice: But??? El Lobo: But something came up that changed everything and this is the only thing that can help me. Voice: I know, I seen it last week that’s why I went to see you. I told you that in the end Lady Heroin will be back to help you. You are my child Tom, and this is the only ting that can save you. El Lobo: How much do I owe you Lady Heroin??? Voice: This one is on me Tom; it’s good to have you back. {The driver rolls the tinted windows up, backs up and drives off. El Lobo quickly places the black box into his wet hoody and starts walking off. We catch up with him as he speaks.} El Lobo: I see that Sunday I defend my title against Mike Marvelous. Look Mike I don’t wanna fight ya. I don’t even wanna be here. I’m gonna be there at Strom Front because I have to. Look Mike, you’re new here and I’m willing to give you the chance of not showing up. If you show up then that means a pissed off Mexican will get very, very violent. And you don’t want that Marvelous do you? DO you want to see me get violent or angry? {El Lobo grabs the trash can and throws it at the car. He begins punching the car windows breaking them. He jumps on the hood and begins kicking the windshield out.} El Lobo: Fuck this Mike, Fuck you Cris, fuck all of it. I don’t care anymore; go ahead beat me half to death. Go ahead make me yell and scream, make me bleed. Make me a lesson Mike, show the world just how fucking marvelous you really are. You wanna fuck me up Mike; you wanna destroy me and take away my Nightmare? FUCK THAT MIKE AND FUCK YOU TOO! VETE A LA MIERDA BANDEJO! I really don’t give a fuck anymore. I think I’ll just kick your ass so I can release some of this anger that’s pent up inside of me. I think I’ll picture Chris West’ face on you. That’ll be motivation enough wouldn’t it? Or maybe I’ll sit there and hang myself on live TV. You guys wanna kill me well go ahead and do it. Shit I’ll bring the gun, the noose, the knife whatever you want vato. Oh you don’t wanna kill me, you wanna attack pregnant women? I see how it is then let me see what I can do. Yes I know exactly what to do. I’ll go after you with a bat, beat your fuckin brains out. I will smash your face in with my bare fists. You’ll get what you deserve and damn it will feel so damn good doin’ it myself esse. Oh you wanna change me? Am I not good enough for your pathetic culos? Why can’t you understand that this pain is consuming me? I feel you beating me down but I want you to stay? What the fuck do you want from me? I cant take this abuse you give me. It’s never good enough for you. I do things I don’t wanna do, and I hate the things you put me through. You ripped my heart and broke it into tiny pieces. Why cant you just fuckin’ leave me be? No I won’t drop this fuckin attitude, I won’t stop. What is it you want from me? Why won’t you leave me be damn it? What do I have to do to make you understand? What the fuck did I do to deserve this fuckin punishment? You took every damn thing I ever wanted in life away. You destroyed my fuckin dreams vato. I don’t give a damn anymore, just like you don’t give a damn about what you put me through. I can’t take this abuse holmes, what do you want me to be? What do you want from me? WHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAATTTT? WHAT IS IT YOU WANT? WHY WONT YOU FUCKIN LET ME BE???? AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH {El Lobo starts grabbing everything he can wreaking havoc in the city around. He throws trashcans in the middle of the street; he punches through windows out of anger. He stands in the middle of the street looking onward. A police car approaches and flashes his lights. He turns the spotlight on El Lobo and slowly approaches him.} Officer: What are you doing??? {El Lobo ignores him and kicks the vehicle and breaks a headlight.} Officer: Take off your hood and answer me son! {The officer approaches with his hand on his nightstick. He starts to pull it out when El Lobo kicks him in the stomach. He then grabs his arm and twists it behind his back. The officer’s hand still attached to the strap on the nightstick, El Lobo grabs the night stick and starts choking the officer. He swings him against the car pounding his head onto the roof. He continues doing that until blood is seen dripping from the officers head. El Lobo grabs his hat and throws it behind him and pulls the officers head back. He looks at his head and then throws him head first into the side window shattering it. El Lobo spits on him and walks off.} El Lobo: Fuck you vato! Vete a la mierda bandejo. Cristofer Oeste I’ll meet you in hell and Mike I’ll send you to hell bitch. {El Lobo walks off in the rain with no emotion as you can see him exhaling. The scene pans out to view the chaos and then fades to black.}
