Episode Two Ukyo 4WW Exposures #716 Date: 5/29/04 Location: Location: At Joe’s All-Night Bar in Boise, Idaho Time: Late Night = [At the small and almost empty bar, Ukyo, who was wearing a tight pair of blue jeans with a long baggy and saggy sleeve violet shirt that was slightly hanging down from her left shoulder and black leather boot, was sitting at the bar drinking a small glass of whiskey though you noticed that there was a dark pink blush across her cheeks signaling that she had been drinking for a quite while now. She finished the light brown beverage from the small glass cup, placed the empty cup down on the counter, staring over to the bartender with a slight drunken gaze.] = Ukyo: Yo, bartender… = [The middle age bartender looked over to Ukyo curiously as he approached her.] = Ukyo: Another round of whiskey. = [The bartender looked down at the sitting Ukyo with concern in his green eyes.] = Bartender: Uh, Miss, I think you had enough. = [Just then, Ukyo had sat up from her seat causing her barstool to fall on the floor, glaring over at the bartender furiously. The bartender was startled by her actions as she was now looking at her nervously with a couple sweat drops trickling down the side of long face.] = Ukyo: I’ll tell you when I had enough! Now give me another damn whiskey or you will have to be eating through a straw! = [The bartender looked at her with great fear in his eyes.] = Bartender: Yes, yes, ma’am! = [He ran away from her and quickly began to do his order while Ukyo was staring at him furiously.] = Ukyo: Damn idiots thinking they know everything… = [She bent down and picked up the stool from the floor and set it at the counter once more, taking a seat there once more. The bartender quickly rushed over to her and placed a small glass cup that is full of whiskey on the bar counter.] = Bartender: Here you go, ma’am… = [He moved away from the furious though slight drunken Ukyo. Ukyo held the small glass cup, looking down at the glass, seeing her own reflection in the whiskey. She sharpened her eyes in a more furious glare.] = Ukyo: …thinking they know everything = [Her mind began to wonder as she was thinking about what happened at the hospital a couple hours ago.] = Location: In a hallway at Hope’s Hospital in Boise, Idaho Time: 5 Hours Ago = [Ukyo was storming through the long hallway, having her long hair hiding her eyes though you still could see that she looked serious along furious about hearing her brother’s condition from the doctor. Charlene was quickly running after her.] = Charlene: Ukyo wait! = [Ukyo stopped walking through the hallway, having her back facing Charlene. Charlene stopped walking and looked at Ukyo’s back with concern in her brown eyes.] = Charlene: The doctor is right this is not the way to support your brother. Vengeances will not… = [Ukyo snapped her head over to Charlene, glaring at her seriously though slight hint of sadness was in her eyes as tears was slowly forming in her eyes but she was trying not to cry since she figured that crying was a weakness.] = Ukyo: Will not make my brother any better! I know that, Charlene. But it will sure as hell make things even. = [Charlene looked at her curiously when hearing Ukyo’s words. Ukyo lifted her hand up, wiping away from her tears both eyes with her fingers, smirking thrilling as she lowered her hand down and looked at Charlene seriously.] = Ukyo: You see, while we were waiting in the Waiting Room, I called the 4WW offices demanding a match… = [Charlene looked at her with great worry from hearing that startling news.] = Charlene: Ukyo, what have done? = [Ukyo turned her back on Charlene, looking down causing her long hair to hide her serious almost scary eyes, lifted her head a bit though her eyes was still hidden, having the same smirk on her lips.] = Ukyo: Since I could not get the main sadistic fucker of Shadow Ascension I decided to get one of his lap dogs and that lap dog is Judgment. = [Charlene surprisingly widened her eyes once hearing that she booked herself in a match against Judgment.] = Charlene: Are you crazy, Judgment is like one of Tarrant’s strongest second to Bullet of course but still. I do not think you are ready to face hi--... = [Suddenly, a large crashing sound was heard causing Charlene to be startled by the loud sound as Ukyo had punched the wall so hard that her fist was within the solid wall. Ukyo kept her arm held out while looking down at the floor through her hidden eyes.] = Ukyo: Damn, I never thought you would doubt my abilities even think I would lose. Tell me something, did you believe the doctor’s bullshit about my brother not being able to wrestle every again. = [Charlene was about to reply though she stopped and looked down at the floor with sadness in her eyes. Ukyo emitted a soft amusing sound pass her lips knowing that Charlene did believe the doctor’s every word about her brother’s condition, pulled her fist out of the wall to cause debris and paint to fall from the now shattered wall as she began to walk down the hallway leaving Charlene in the hallway. Charlene looked up and watched Ukyo leave curiously.] = Charlene: Where are you going, Ukyo? Ukyo: None of your fucking business… = [Charlene just stood there and watched Ukyo go further and further down the long hallway.] = Location: At Joe’s Bar in Boise, Idaho Time: Present Time; Late Night = [Ukyo was drank the whiskey down with one gulp from the small glass cup, slammed the empty glass down on the wooden bar counter, looking in frustration though there was a slight hint of daze in her eyes from being drunk as the blush across her cheeks became a bit darker than before. While Ukyo was sitting alone at the counter, a man in his mid-20’s with messy dirty blonde hair and blue eyes, who was wearing black jeans and an open multi-color silk shirt revealing his chest a bit with green tennis shoes, approached her while holding a small glass cup that contain gin and tonic, looking at Ukyo like some eye candy. He lifted his hand up to his lips as he lightly licked his fingers wetting them up a bit and he used him to straighten his eyebrows wanting to look presenting for her making obvious that he was a womanizer.] = Womanizer: Hey good looking, want some company? = [Ukyo glanced over to the man for a brief moment before looking back down at the countertop in a frustrating gaze, deciding not to pay the man any mind.] = Womanizer: C’mon, a beautiful lady like you shouldn’t be sitting here all alone. Ukyo: I am not in the mood… = [She kept her gaze down at the countertop, having a more frustrating gaze than before as the man’s advances was irritating her.] = Womanizer: C’mon, don’t want some loving… = [He lowered his hand down to Ukyo’s behind and began to rub her there which caused Ukyo to gasp surprisingly though sharpening her eyes in furious glare. In a blink of an eye, Ukyo placed her hand on the back of the man’s head and slammed his face on the hard bar counter causing the man’s face to explode in blood. The man briefly yelled out in pain as he fell straight down on the floor. Ukyo got up from her seat, as she picked up the barstool and sat it down on the man’s neck though she sat down on the stool causing to choke from feeling the stool lodged against his throat. Ukyo still sat down on the stool, angrily glaring down at the man whose face is extremely bloody after she rammed his face on the hard counter.] = Ukyo: Here is a little lesson to learn about women… If a woman says that they are not in the mood then they are not in fucking mood! Got it, you stupid prick. = [The man slowly nodded his head while looking in great pain from feeling his nose being broken.] = Ukyo: That’s a good idiot… = [She got off the stool and moved the stool off the man’s throat. The man rolled to the side, favoring his throat while gasping out for air to breathe. Ukyo placed the seat back at the counter, glaring down at the hurting man seriously.] = Ukyo: Now get out of here… = [The man stood up, favoring his injured face as he quickly ran away from her and exit out of the bar in the same manner. Ukyo take a seat on the stool, looking around her to notice that the few customers that was there was staring at her frightening from her actions and then glanced over to the bartender to see that he was looking at her in the same way. She closed her eyes and chuckled for a brief moment, opening her eyes halfway looking at the bartender amusingly as she held the empty glass up, acting like nothing just happened.] = Ukyo: Hey, another round here. Location: At Joe’s All-Night Bar in Boise, Idaho Time: Next Morning = [In the bar, Ukyo still sitting at the counter though she was resting her head there, having both arms underneath her chin as she was resting her arms as a soft cushion while she slept. You noticed there was a small pyramid made out of seven small glass cups signaling that she did a whole lot of drinking the other night to ease the pain she was feeling about her brother laying in the hospital. The bartender was cleaning the cups while another worker was vacuuming the carpet floor. Suddenly, the soft ringing sound was coming from the sleeping Ukyo. She slowly stirred once and opened her eyes halfway, having a slight grazed daze in her eyes along with faint blush was across her cheeks signaling that she was still a bit drunk after the other night, listening to the soft ringing sound coming from her as she lowered one hand down to her pocket taking out her small cell phone from there, opening it and placing it at her ear while she still rested her head on the counter.] = Ukyo: Hello…? = [Ukyo lifted her up sharply, looking in great shock from hearing the words on the other end of her cell phone.] = Ukyo: What? = [She listened to the panicking words on the other end.] = Ukyo: Ok, I am on my way! = [She hung up her cell phone, placing it back in her pocket, hopped off the stool as she placed money on the counter for all the whiskey she drank, glaring at the bartender seriously.] = Ukyo: Keep the change! = [She ran out of the bar in hurry causing both, the bartender and the worker, to look confusion wondering why she left the bar like that.] = Location: Kyo’s Hospital Room in Hope’s Hospital at Boise, Idaho Time: Mid-Morning = [In the hospital room, you noticed a couple of the police officers are investigating the room for any clues while the others are questioning the nurses and doctors about the whereabouts of Kyo Kushinaga. You noticed that a worried Charlene was in the room too being questioned about Kyo Kushinaga. Suddenly, Ukyo quickly entered the room but two police came and blocking her way inside of the crime scene, staring at her seriously.] = Policeman: Sorry, no civilians can enter the crime scene. = [Ukyo glared at the officers furiously.] = Ukyo: You idiots! The one who is missing is my brother so let me through or I will go through you! = [Charlene glanced in the direction of Ukyo’s loud voice, she glanced over to the police officer she was talking to.] = Charlene: Excuse me. = [She ran over to where Ukyo and the officers were.] = Charlene: Its ok, she is really his sister. = [The officers stepped aside as Ukyo walked in between them though he glared at them seriously before changing her gaze to a curious one when looking at Charlene.] = Ukyo: What the hell is going on? Charlene: The police believe that Kyo might have been kidnapped from his room. = [Ukyo looked in confusion though sharpened her eyes seriously.] = Ukyo: That doesn’t make sense? Who kidnap my brother in his condition unless… = [She glanced over to the open window and began to approach it, looking out the window seriously noticing that it was beautiful day though she had a eerie feeling about her brother.] = “He escaped on his own…” = [Ukyo looked around the room though changed her expression over to the doctor who mentioned that her brother wouldn’t be able to wrestle again after the punishment he took in his match against Tarrant. She curled her lips in amusing smirk and began to approach the doctor who was being questioned by an officer. Once she reached the doctor, she drilled him with a damaging punch in his face, glaring down at the fallen doctor. The doctor favoring his face as he was looking up at Ukyo curiously as the officer looked in surprise from her actions. Charlene surprisingly gasped out and looked in the same manner.] = Charlene: Ukyo! = [Ukyo kept an amusing smirk on her lips while staring down at the hurting doctor.] = Ukyo: Idiot, I told you my brother wouldn’t be stop! = [The doctor slowly stood up and looked at Ukyo seriously.] = Doctor: Do not be ridiculous your brother was kidnapped by a madman I mean you saw that the kidnapper yank off the instrument off your brother’s body and ran away in the night with your brother. = [Ukyo closed her eyes and amusingly smirks while looking in amusement.] = Ukyo: Whatever helps you sleep at night but you were still wrong. = [She turned her back on him and began to leave the crime scene as Charlene quickly followed her.] = Charlene: Ukyo, you think that Mr. Kyo escaped? Ukyo: He must have unconsciously heard us talking to that doctor who was full of shit which caused him to wake up and train his body to prove everyone wrong. Charlene: If that is the case then why hasn’t he called us telling us that he is ok? = [Ukyo stopped walking when hearing Charlene’s query, beginning to think of an answer for that query but she could not think of one.] = Ukyo: I dunno… = [Charlene looked in concern.] = Charlene: I think we should allow the police to handle this case for now I mean we do have an event to get to… = [Ukyo slowly nodded her head once.] = Ukyo: You’re right I have a lap dog’s ass to kick. = [They began to walk down the hallway together.] = Ukyo: Listen, about yesterday I am sorry I was just pissed and sad about my brother being that condition I mean after losing one brother I did not want to lose another one. Charlene: I understand you are just being his big sister… Ukyo: Yeah... I’m his big sister… = […Fade…] =
