Disgraced Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #585 Date: 03/20/04 Location: The Hotel Disgrace - transitive verb 1. 1 archaic : to humiliate by a superior showing2 : to be a source of shame to <your actions disgraced the family> 3 : to cause to lose favor or standing <was disgraced by the hint of scandal> or used as a noun 1 a : the condition of one fallen from grace or honor b : loss of grace, favor, or honor Welcome once again to the spot that holds your attention. I, Patti Anderson, have decided that it is not worth playing games anymore to try to catch the North Wind Title. It’s more of a desire to bring honor to the title that is being held by Toddy Boy. Now it seems that Fey is entrusting a heated rival in the form of Jay Phoenix is just outright disgraceful. What in the bloody hell was Fey thinking to even suggest that? Even worse it would appear that she is attempting to stack the deck against someone. The obvious finger points to me, but it could also be pointing to you too, Toddy Boy. Even though Fey doesn’t like me much, she has helped me out in the past to gain what she wants. Maybe, just maybe, she thinks that you, Toddy Boy have disgraced the title to the point to where only a female can truly bring some kind of grace to the title. As from the looks of it, I am just the woman who will strip that title off of you. You see, Toddy, you have claimed that I am not worthy of being your competition well look at it this way...I earned this spot by taking on everyone that came within my path, including your pathetic poofter ass, and it seems that you are still strung up on morphine that you have no bloody clue as just what is going on around you. It looks like the self proclaimed 'Savior' is afraid of the 'Darling of the 4WW'. I think maybe you need to be the one to resign your title reign. Your constantdelusions of thinking that everyone needs saving is just completely anal. Why don't you take a long glorious look in the mirror. The only one that needs saving is you. The evil demons have possessed you into believing that you are the second coming. I'll agree alright, you're the second coming of a fake, a fraud and a phony. You see, the last two times I met you in the ring, I was inexperienced and frightful. Fear and failure owned my thoughts at all times. Confidence just did not exist. This time though, I feel the confidence. I feel the desire. I feel that the disgrace comes to an end this time around for I will leave you lying in a pool of your own blood, with myself standing over the top of you making you wish you hadn’t stepped into the ring with me. Place: California Hotel Time: Night Time Patti Anderson tossed and turned in her sleep. Her hands moving as if she’s acting something she is dreaming. Her hands stop in mid motion as she turned over again. This time her closed eyes began to flutter as if her eyes are attempting to follow her around whatever she was dreaming about. She rolls from her left side to her right side, the white lace gown starting to stretch over her firm body, showing off the figure that makes men go wild when they see her. The beads of perspiration starting appearing on her forehead, slowly running down her face and down to the front of her gown. Just as the last bead hit the top of her gown, Patti flew up in bed, the blankets tumbling off the bed and onto the floor. Her sudden movements didn’t disturb the man sleeping beside her. Sean subconsciously grabbed the blankets and rolled over facing the wall, snoring loudly like he always does when he’s fast asleep. Patti sighed as she realized how much she missed that sound. It was always a comfort when he snored like that, at least she knew he was alive that way. She reached over into the chair beside the bed, grabbed the matching robe, and slipped it on. Her bare feet shuffled across the dark green carpet. Patti looked around at the shadows that encased the room. Her imagination showing her what she thought was all around her. To Patti, it almost seemed as if she was still walking in the nightmare she had just attempted to awaken from. Her hands touched the furniture as if she were afraid something would jump out at her, and force her back to where she awoke from. Her bandaged hand grabbed hold of the chair tightly as she made her way across the room, spots of blood starting to show through the gauze. She stumbled into the bathroom, thinking that a warm splash of water would help her relax and maybe fall back to sleep. She turned on the light and stared calmly at the sink. Her hands shakily turned on the faucet and watched the water start to fill the sink. She sighed as she rested her head against the door frame. “ Mate-o! Faça-o bleed!!” Patti blinked at first not certain if she heard a voice or not. “ Mate-o! Faça-o bleed!!” Patti spoke out, a slight note of fear in her voice. “What? Kill who? Make whom bleed?” Patti looked around to see if someone was in the room. She checked behind the shower curtain, to find the normal shampoo, conditioner, soaps, sponges in there. She checked around the toilet to make sure Sean wasn’t playing jokes, only thing that seemed to be around it was a roll of toilet paper. She grabbed the roll off its hooks and checked it again. Patti put the roll back on the hooks and turned the water off of the nearly overflowing sink. Patti grabbed one of the wash clothes out of nearby closet and threw it into the water. She ringed the water out of it, and careful of her bandage, started to cool herself off. Patti stopped in mid stroke as she heard a voice again. “Você deve matá-lo para ganhar a vitória” A look of horror crossed Patti’s face as the voice again made its presence felt in the small room. Patti looked up and caught a quick vision before her eyes of Todd Dream standing before her. Patti saw herself wrestling him, taking all he was giving her, and returning it with much more passion and raw aggression. Patti smiled as she watched the vision with joy and wonder. She saw herself capturing her dream. The dream of every wrestler. She watch as she pinned him for the title, but then the vision took on a darker turn. She watched herself pull out an axe and proceeded to slam the edge into Todd’s chest. The blade drove deeply into Todd’s chest, as blood shot out from the wound. Todd’s mouth opened to scream out, but all that managed to come forth was a raging river of blood. Patti turned her head towards the closet to avoid the image. Patti quickly reached inside and pulled out a cloth, dropping the cloth into the water as she looked up into the mirror. The steam from the faucet had fogged it up. Patti quickly grabbed a dry cloth, and set to work of getting rid of the moisture that had gathered. She folded the towel over as it appeared that more was gathering faster then she could remove it. Suddenly Patti’s image appeared through the fog. Patti stared at her reflection for a while. She noticed her hair was a mess. She decided while she was awake that she would attempt to brush it and make it look somewhat workable. Adrian would have a total fit if he saw her hair. The long smooth strokes of the brush did little to remove the tangles from her hair. She put the brush down, pretty much resigned to the fact that she’ll get scolded by Adrian in the morning over the shape of her hair. She stood and stared at her reflection in the mirror for a few more moments. She slowly started to believe that the vision she had seen earlier was just an overactive imagination, or some really bad shellfish. Patti reached over the sink and grabbed one of the many hair ties that Adrian had started to keep with him. Patti separated her hair into her trademark pony tails. It would at least keep the tangles down a little more should she start having more negative dreams again. Patti yawned and stretched. She shut the light off in the bathroom and walked the few feet it took to get back to the bed. The white robe dropped to the floor, landing all bunched up at her feet. She placed the vision out of her mind as she slides back into the bed. As Patti settles into bed, the shadows from around the room seem to settle into the center of the room. A dark figure proceeds to grow up and out of it as the scene fades out to black...... To Be Continued
