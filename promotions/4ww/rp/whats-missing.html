What's Missing? Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #888 Date: 8/23/04 Location: The Trophy Room (The scene opens up inside of a trophy room. Sitting on the shelves within the room are several trophies, tiaras, and several other awards along with pictures, programs, and other mementos. The camera pans around the room, slowly scanning by all the awards. As the camera pans, Luscious Lady Luck is seen standing in the doorway of the room. She is wearing blue t-shirt with the words “Your Savior Has Arrived” on the front with daisy dukes.) LLL: Impressive isn’t it? Very few people can claim the awards that I have claimed, and even fewer at my ripe young age. But then again, what would one expect from your savior. What would one expect from the greatest wrestler to ever lace up a pair of boots here in the 4WW…..? There are trophies from everywhere up here. Here is a trophy from cheerleading when I was in High School. We had a great team. Well, actually, everyone else sucks and I carried the team, but what does one expect. Sure, these are team trophies, but there are several individual trophies from cheerleading just for myself…. Then we have all of my tiara’s and crowns that I’ve one from being the most beautiful woman to ever grace the face of God’s green earth. I mean everyone wants to see me and be with me, men and women alike. Those other girls at these pageants, they were so damn ugly I hardly had to do anything except show up and I’d win…… We can move over a little farther here, and here are all the trophies I’ve won for being such a great gambler. Poker, Blackjack, Euchre, Bridge, Roulette, you name it, I’ve played it and I’ve won it. Sure, some times I’ve had to have a partner, but they were pretty useless. They wouldn’t have the trophy without me being there…… Even for my videos I have trophies. Trophies for everything that you could get in the industry. Best Female Performance, Best Amateur Video. Video of the Year. These ones I couldn’t have done without the love of my life, but she already knows that. Why she is holding back from me and why she is stopping our award winning performances from being out in the public and being viewed by those who deserve it I don’t know, but it is something she should be proud of…… But out of all these trophies, there is still an award and an achievement I have yet to win. That achievement that I have yet to conquer and make my own is a wrestling title. Granted, I haven’t been around the sport for that long, but I have yet to actually claim one for myself. And I have a spot set out just for it…… (LLL points to a spot on the wall wear there is an empty shelf. It is dusty and there is very little there.) But that will all change shortly. That will all change starting this week. That will all change on Stormfront when I win the Tempest Title and achieve my first title in wrestling ever. The first of many I’m sure. And the first that will become my legacy in my hall of fame career. It’s not going to be easy, and I know that, but they always say the first is the hardest to get…… My opponent calls himself a Ladies Man. Chris Slade, a champion who is among champions. And unfortunately, the person who will fall victim as I start my Hall of Fame Career as far as titles are concerned…… Do not get me wrong, you have been a good champion, but you have just been given a bad break. Let’s face it…Lady Luck is just not on your side for this match. You’ll have other chances, you’ll have other opportunities, but this one is not yours. This title has been given its spot, and that is right here on my wall….. It’s time for the Tempest Title to experience a real champion. It’s time for the Tempest Title to experience its glory days and its true place on the top. And it’s time for the 4WW to experience a real champion, much like 4WW, the Tempest Title is nothing without me. (LLL flips the light switch, turning off the lights in the room as it fades to black.)
