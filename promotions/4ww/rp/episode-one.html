Episode One Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #556 Date: 3-3-04 Location: Abandon Subway Station = [The scene opens in an old and abandon subway and by the appearance of the dusty and rusty environment, you can tell that this station has not been in use for many years.] = “Hero…a hero known as a person who do good deeds for himself and not the glory of being recognize or famous for the deeds he do to others…” = [The view begins to move down the dark tunnel of the old subway line slowly.] = = [Listens to the sound of water dripping down from the leaking iron pipes that stretches across the concrete ceiling like blood vessels.] = “Four Winds Wrestling is in the need of a hero. The kind of hero who can defend the good citizens of this well-establish wrestling league from the ones who find amusement of committing immoral deeds. A hero who will make sure that justice will get serve when an unfair crime happens in the square ring or outside of the ring. A hero who will not show fear to the vile villains who want to disgrace this league with their vigor behavior and disrespect to rules of this dominating league…” “Well…” = [The view still moving slowly down the long pathway.] = = [In the depth of the darkness, you see a man clad in all black standing in the middle of the non-operational steel tracks that trains use to zoom down the tunnels like rockets.] = = [The mysterious man raises his head though his black hat hides his face.] = “I think that hero is I...” = [He smirks contently.] = First Step To Justice = [He lifts his hand and raises his black hat to reveal his black metal mask, and looks down the tunnel seriously.] = = [You notice that there is about thirty tall thin rusty stands that holds unlit candles line up on both once electrical steel train tracks.] = “For the ones who have not been paying attention to StormFront from the last two weeks, I am the Masked Man.” = [He amusingly chuckles once and closes his eyes behind his mask, as he looks seriously though amusingly.] = “No, I go by the name of the Masked Avenger.” = [Opens his eyes though still has the same expression on his face.] = “I figure that this name sounded a bit better than the Masked Man I mean if I am going to be the superhero I’m going to need a catchy name.” = [He nods his head and smirks while looking down the tunnel.] = “That’s right. This is no joke I am nobody but your resident superhero here to save the day from evil.” “Wondering what kind of superpowers I have?” “Well…” = [He sharpens his eyes behind his mask, and you can small flames take form within the brown eyes.] = = [With his powers, he cause mysterious flames lit up the wits of all thirty candles, and cause the dark tunnel to dimly light up.] = = [The small soothing cerulean flames within his eyes soon disappears after using his powers to light up all the candles.] = “You can say it is something that will help me burn through the dark corruption that taints my league, Four Winds Wrestling.” = [He begins to walk down the dimly lit tunnel and watches the blue flame dancing slightly from the wind he is causing by his walking.] = “As some of you know, I entered myself in the Insane Battle Royal that West Wind Master, Fnord, is holding to declare a contender to fight him at the upcoming Pay-Per-View event, Maelstrom 3. Why enter a match that chaos and mayhem is probably going happen in the square ring?” = [He looks seriously with an amusing smirk while walking down the long pathway.] = “Because of what your West Wind Master said not long ago… He called himself the super-villain of Four Winds Wrestling and of course being the superhero I am I cannot not allow an evil doer like himself to wear this second highest title of the league.” “And if it means that I have to go through four other people to get my chance then so be it… I will play his game and claim victory to take down this so-call super-villain.” = [He begins to pace back and forth down lit part of the dark tunnel.] = “Citizen Bullet, I believe that you are part of the stable with an adulterous name, Porn Inc. I should get on your case for the name I mean the young citizens who watches StormFront or Tempest does not need to hear about the kind of stuff you do to your love one, Rachel. There is a time and place to have this kind of discussion with Rachel, so please try not talk about it while young citizens are watching, or better yet can you control your hormones. Is that so hard to ask, jeez…” = [He shakes his head in disappointment about how the way Bullet and Rachel act.] = = [Stops walking and has a gentle look behind his mask.] = “Citizen Coricidon, one of the most beautiful young ladies to wrestle today in Four Winds Wrestling. It is truly a crime for her to look this lovely.” = [Closes his eyes and looks modestly through his trademark black metal mask, as he modestly chuckles once. He raises his hand, and scratches his cheek lightly once.] = “I know that sounded lame but it is the truth.' = [His modest gaze turns to a serious one.] = “Citizen Coricidon, do not take my gentle words kindly, because I am going to be all seriousness when our match come.” = [He nods his head in agreement to his words to let her know that he means it.] = = [Now looks with a curious expression as he resumes pacing back and forth in the dimly lit area of the tunnel.] = “Citizen Jaime, I do not really know anything about you nor do not know if you have committed any immoral deeds to your fellow wrestlers so I just have to say that you need to refrain from doing something that you are going to regret or you will get a visit from me, ok?” = [Looks with a more curious gaze than before.] = “Citizen Chris Wrestling, the same goes for you. I do not know anything about you or if you did anything wrong in my league. Just keep yourself out of trouble or you will have to answer to me.” = [Stop pacing and stands in the once operational train tracks, as he looks in the camera with seriousness for a brief moment but soon raises his hand to the top of his black hat, and lowers it down a bit to hide his face.] = “Heed my words, you citizens I have to face on Tempest… I am not going to hold back nor show any mercy to you all, so I hope you citizens will not take it personally when I will become the #1 contender to face the West Wind Master or his newest title as the super-villain of Four Winds Wrestling, Fnord.” = [He keeps his hand on his black hat while covering his face still.] = “Good luck to you all…” = [Quickly does a quick spin to cause him to explode into his trademark fast spinning flaming tornado on the tracks.] = = [The great wind from the rapid moving cyclone is blowing out the candles one by one.] = = [It swiftly disperses into tiny droplets of fire.] = = [You notice that a single flame remains on a candle to reveal that the Masked Avenger had disappeared without a trace like always] = = […Fade…] =
