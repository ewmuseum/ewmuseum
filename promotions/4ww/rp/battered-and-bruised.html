Even tho’ we are hallow and alone, there are many others watching us taking note to every wrong that we’ve done. Many focus on only the bad that we have come to pass. Even if we’ve done many a good thing, it does not matter in their eyes. Even if we’ve done far more good than bad they will not accept that in their feeble minds. When it shall come to pass nothing will keep the truth from being made known. The days of learning are nigh; those days of learning will never be over. In the end we’ll be left in the wake of the mistake… it’s then we’ll taste that taste of hypocrisy. I’m trapped in these memories of pain and suffering and kept everything inside. It was then I asked the question will you be there tomorrow? Will you stay with me today? So will you love me tomorrow? Keep that question in mind as I break down. I am now at that point where I must make a decision. Will I plummet and perish? Or will I use my wings to fly up to new heights? I dare not attempt to fight my destiny because I know that in the end it doesn’t even matter.~ {The scene picks up where we see El Lobo laying in bed all covered up. The room has a nice blue look about it as we pan around. We notice incense burning beside a prism, a bamboo tree, and a bonsai tree. On the table beside the bed we see a black glass, of water perhaps, there at arm level. Beside the glass we see a silver looking phone plugged into a charger, charging while the man rests. From around the corner we see Angél holding a black bowl carrying a white rag as she rings the excess water into that bowl. She sits beside Lobo and wipes his forehead, neck, and chest down. She then places the cloth rag into the bowl as she removes his hair from his eyes. She bends over and gives him a kiss on the forehead as the bedroom door creeps open. The sound of a knock is heard as Angél answers.} Angél: Who is it??? Man: It’s Dr. Woodward. I came to check up on Tom… Angél: Come in… {The man opens the door and walks in. He’s wearing a pair of blue jeans with his white button up coat. In his hand we see the black Dr’s bag. He opens the bag and pulls out several items. Angél gets off the bed and steps toward the Dr. as she looks at him with concern.] Angél: Thanks for coming again doctor. Maybe I’m just over protective but that fall really has me stressed. Dr. Woodward: Now Ms. Sanchez, I’ve told you when you were released that you need not be stressed. It will make everything worse. Angél: Comprendo lo. But when I saw Tom fall through the ramp I could only think about losing him too. Dr. Woodward: Tomás is a strong individual. I’ve seen him bounce back from many injuries. Angél: Ya sé. I’ve been there through all of it. I just couldn’t handle losing him after the child. Dr. Woodward: I understand that but you need to know that those wounds will heal in due time. Angél: So did you get the results in yet? Dr. Woodward: Yes I did… Angél: How is he??? Dr. Woodward: He will be fine. However I highly suggest that he not participate for a while. He’s still recovering from major injuries to the neck and back. Angél: What do you suggest? Dr. Woodward: I highly suggest that you send him to a drug abuse counselor. Angél: Excuse me??? Dr. Woodward: After that bump Sunday night I was told that he went out and fought by Mr. Kreed’s side. So I took the liberty of testing his blood to see if there were any drugs in his system. Angél: And???? Dr. Woodward: We’ve found large amounts of liquid heroin in his system. Angél: Aye buen Dios! Will he be ok? Dr. Woodward: Yes, his system has gotten rid of the drug… however I’m concerned for his, and your, wellbeing. Is there anyone you could call? Angél: I could call his friends… Dr. Woodward: Do that and make sure they watch him constantly. So that he cannot come into any contact with any drugs. Angél: Thank you… I’ll call Psymon as soon as I can. Dr. Woodward: That would be good. Angél: How would I pay the house visit?? Credit, cash, check? Dr. Woodward: Oh, no charge… I just want you two to get back on the right track. The two of you have had quite an ordeal and I hope that everything works out. Angél: Muchisimas gracias. {Angél gives him a hug for all of the help that he’s provided for them. He takes his things and steps outside the door closing it behind him. Angél walks back towards the bed and sits beside her fallen Lobo. She grabs the phone and flips it before she says a name aloud.} Angél: Psymon… {She sits there waiting for someone to pick up. After several seconds her head lifts right up.} Angél: Psymon it’s me Angél. The Dr says that Tom’s system was flooded with heroin. I want you and the rest of the guys to keep an eye on him. Usually I wouldn’t discuss something like this through a voicemail but I’m definitely worried. Please keep an eye on Tom. I won’t be able to be at the show for a while. Please I’m extremely worried about Tom. If you wanna call me I’ll be at Suzi’s house. Make sure Tom stays clean Psy. Adios. {She presses a button and flips the phone back. She then lies beside Lobo curling up next to him. She holds onto his arm as she kisses his cheek and starts to cry. The scene fades to black as the muffled sounds of tears fill the air then cuts.}
