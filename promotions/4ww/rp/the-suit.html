The Suit

12 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Suit 4WW Exposures #1228 Date: 4/12/05 Location: Lawyers Office (The scene opens up in back in a familiar place. We are back in the states, quite obviously. And, even more obviously, we are in the office of a prominent lawyer. One who is fairly familiar to 4WW fans, as this is the personal lawyer of Luscious Lady Luck. Sitting on one side of the table is the blonde vixen of the 4WW wearing a low cut blue tank top. Across from her is sitting the diminutive lawyer of LLL, Vincent Vegas. There are papers spread out in front of him, His black hair is combed to the side. He wears a grey suit with a red tie, and his glasses are a nice thin type. A newer look for him, but still the same man that LLL has always associated with.) LLL: I’m going to kill that bitch damn it. Do you understand me? Vegas: Yes, you’ve said that already, but I don’t think that is the best way to go. LLL: Damn it, your damn right it is. (LLL grabs one of the pads of papers that is in front of Vegas and throws it across the room in complete disgust.) LLL: She just doesn’t get it, does she? She just doesn’t understand that not only is she ruining my life, she is ruining the 4WW. Vegas: Relax. There are better ways to deal with this. LLL: Like what? (Vegas looks down at his papers trying to find the correct one.) Vegas: Well, I do have friends in just about all places. And I have informants in just about all places as well, as I’m sure you are well aware. LLL: Yeah, so? Vegas: Well, one of my friends has provided me information that will give us the advantage against her. LLL: Really? You’ve got my attention. Vegas; Well, my friend has provided me with photos of Patti Anderson on the phone. LLL: Isn’t that illegal? Vegas: Well, that’s totally up to interpretation. Most places, not illegal if you are on public property, or if its part of security. LLL: Security? Vegas: That’s right. Part of your detail you asked me to arrange for. LLL: DETAIL? I DON”T REMEMBER YOU TELLING ME ANYTHING ABOUT THIS!!! WHAT KIND OF FREAKING DETAIL? (LLL stands up, leaning over the table, fire blasting in her eyes.) Vegas: Relax. Yes, you asked me to provide you a detail. It’s all right here in this contract. I didn’t tell you about it because if you knew who your detail was, then it wouldn’t be as effective. (Vegas hands a piece of paper across the table to LLL. LLL examines the paper, and sits down, recalling back to the day.) LLL: Then why would you let me do that contract with the Ozzes? I mean, if you already had security set up, why would you let me hire them? Vegas: Well, two reasons. First, nothing wrong with having extra security. Secondly, you’re the one who wanted it, and I work for you, so why shouldn’t I let you protect yourself to your contentment. LLL: Is there anything else I should know that you’ve set up for me that I’ve requested? Vegas: Other than all the upgraded hotel room and flights for your events which you already know about…. LLL: Yeah, the ones the 4WW puts us in absolutely suck. I really hate flying coach and all. And the private plane has been down a lot recently. Plus those beds in those hotels suck too. Vegas: Right, exactly. LLL: So anyway, back to your idea. Vegas: Right. So yeah, there are the photos, and he traced the number and got some information for me. I have it on good authority that you are totally correct. It was Patti who set you up. And it was Patti who had you arrested. LLL: (sarcastically) I already knew that. Vegas: Right, of course. So I have been drawing up papers for you to sign. I think we have a case on several levels here. Wrongful Arrest, Breech of Contract, Slander, and Libel. LLL: OK, so what is that going to get me? I don’t need the money. All I want is her. Vegas: That’s part of the big effect here. We have enough on her that her and her husband Sean McClancy will have no choice but to sell the 4WW, which, of course you have enough money to buy outright if you so wish. LLL: I could probably do that now, but how is that going to get me here. Vegas: It’s all in the conference committees and all in the settlement conferences. LLL: Interesting, very interesting. Vegas: I thought you’d say so. Now, if you will just sign here. (Vegas hands the paper over to LLL. LLL smiles as she signs the paper and the camera fades to black.)
