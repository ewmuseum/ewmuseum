Another Room Another Show Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #461 Date: 01/13/04 Location: T.R.H. Smith Mansion Bed and Breakfast Revenge - transitive verb 1 : to avenge (as oneself) usually by retaliating in kind or degree 2 : to inflict injury in return for or used in a noun sense... 1 : a desire for revenge 2 : an act or instance of retaliating in order to get even 3 : an opportunity for getting satisfaction No matter how it is used, revenge will be severed this time around. Patti has been blessed with facing the likes of Tyler Blaze and Todd Dream. Patti has the ultimate revenge of facing two men that owe her. Tyler Blaze for ripping up her Sean McClancy sign in the old federation, and Todd Dream for just being a member of Featured Attraction. Destiny weaves her magic at what is in store for this round. Todd Dream is still holding on to the title that should be around Patti’s waist. You see, if it wasn’t for Annie, Patti would be sitting on top of the 4WW with the gold around her waist, and Todd would be off shining Patti’s boots. He’s held that belt for far to long and its time for him to release his hold on it. It’s time Todd. It’s time for you to meet your lord and mistress in the form of Patti Anderson. Your time line grows thin as the champion. For you will lose this time around. The grounds have been set for revenge to come into play, you do not have Annie this time to take care of you. Marvelous is off nursing his petty little pride over being dubbed a queer in a match, Jerms is off creating mass destruction of the roster to sooth his wounded pride, and Summers is just being the same wanker he always is. As for Patti, she now has back up. She has back up in the form of Jade and Lacuna Debris. She now has no reason to fear being jumped. Destiny may go as far as to whisper into the commish’s ear about making the title battle between these to a match that they will not forget. Now with Tyler Blaze being thrown into the equation makes it an even better. Patti owes Tyler Blaze. She owes him for the destruction of her sign from an old federation that she used to follow. A sign that she spent a long time working on. Mix that anger with the fact that he is attempting to date her best friend within the fed just makes it worse. Destiny has a feeling that Lacuna won’t be seeing much action in this match. Patti just may attempt to take them on herself, and let Lacuna watch for trouble. All in all revenge may be a dish best served cold, but it will be a dish that Patti will surely enjoy serving up a healthy portion to Todd Dream and Tyler Blaze. Both men better be prepared for the this league of extraordinary females to come in ready to do some business, and leave the bodies of their opponents in the ring. For these women are coming to the ring ready to do battle, and it will be a hell storm like none other that the Four Winds has ever seen. ________________________________________________________ Place: T.R.H. Smith Mansion Bed and Breakfast Time: Sometimes during the day ________________________________________________________ Fade into Patti Anderson parking her car. Her midnight blue Mazda Miata stood out against the backdrop of a 1914 style home. Patti opted to spend a quite week from society then be stuffed inside another hotel where the fans were more then likely to spot her. Sean was still over seas in Ireland finishing up business over there. He still wasn’t sure when he was coming back, but was still very supportive of her. He would be watching very closely to see how things were going for her. She could still sense that he would prefer to be with her then over there under constant questioning and everything else, but they both knew that they would be reunited soon. Patti grabbed hold of Princess’ leash, and crawled out of the car. She took in the surroundings of the quaint little place that she herself decided to book. Princess pounced around at her feet, barking. Patti bent down and started petting her. Patti: Feels good to be out of the car huh? I know traveling is going to be hard on you, but I can’t really leave you in a kennel, just not right now. Princess sits back on her hind legs as she licks Patti’s chin. Her little bottom wags back and forth as she continues to lick Patti’s chin. Patti stands back, slings her purse over her shoulder and heads quietly to the door to the building. Patti walks up the door, Princess still running around her feet barking at a squirrel she sees darting up a tree. Princess’ natural instinct kicks in as she wants to chase it, but Patti stops her and drags her inside the building. The puppy and her enter through a 1914 century door and are immediately greeted by a room with walls painted gold with English wood panels along the walls. A cheery fire burned in the fire place, directly in front of it, two upholstered chairs and matching sofa surrounded a cherry coffee table. Patti stepped into the room, her sandals making perfect timed steps on a varnished hard wood floor. Patti took in the blue rug used in the center of the room. She decided she needed to find out where that rug came from, for it would go perfect in her bedroom. Princess seeing the comfy chair, makes a mad dash for the sofa, nearly pulling poor Patti’s arm out of the socket. Patti continued to look around the room. Her eyes drawn to the painting over the fire place to the quaint little piano off to her left side seemed to just fit the layout of the house very well. One of the staff members appeared out of nowhere and directed Patti towards her rooms. Patti watched as the staff member unlocked the solid oak door and stepped to the side to allow the overly hyper puppy to bounce into the room. Patti readjusted her purse over her shoulder and prepared to walk into the room after the dog. Patti stopped mid entrance as she was taken by surprise. Her purse slipped off the shoulder and hit the floor with a thud. Her eyes grew to the size of silver dollars. The room was breath-taking beautiful. The floral drapes and bedspread just completely made the room come alive. Patti noticed he king size bed with the huge upholstered headboard just seemed to match the writing desk, the wood and upholstered chairs just took her back to her old room at home. She even noticed the rug carried the same matching design. She wiped a tear away as she realized it was more like home then the brochure let on. Princess wasted no time in making herself comfortable on the bed. Her bottom wiggling happily and finding the comfy spot on the bed. Her soft little head resting comfortably on her paws as she watched her mistress continue her walk around the room. Patti walked over to a matching oak door, that she assumed open to a small closet. Patti was totally amazed to find her entire wardrobe had been hung up and waiting patiently for her to decide on what she wanted to wear. Patti wished Sean was here to see all of this. Damn that court in Ireland needing him. She stepped inside the closet and realized it could rather easily pass for a separate room then a closet. Patti continued to walk around the room, marveling at the beauty of the place. Patti squealed in delight when she walked into the bathroom and spotted the vintage pink tile bathroom. She nearly swooned in delight at the bathroom. A old fashion claw footed tub and sick stared at her, her toiletries placed on the rack beside the tub, along with plenty of fluffy pink towels that matched the bath mat. She just could not believe her luck with everything to day. She fell in love with this place all over again. She knew she would be staying here again when the federation came through this area again. Patti walked back into the bedroom and sat down on the bed. She noticed a tv and a VCR were set up along the far wall. A quick glance at the note on the top tape stated they were from Eliza. Something about this had something that would get her into the mood to take on Todd Dream and Tyler Blaze this week. Patti cocked an eyebrow as she stared at the note. Patti (to herself): Sometimes I swear Eliza makes no sense at all. I guess she wanted to make sure I was good and psyched. Patti popped the first tape into the VCR and curled up on the bed Patti sat down on the bed, curled up at the head. Princess roused up enough to see her mistress on the bed, and scooted up closer to her. Patti’s hand reached out and began to stroke her head. Her hand stroking and playing with her ear. Princess let out a yawn and rested her head on Patti’s knee. Patti laid back and prepared to fast forward through whatever it was that was on this first tape. At the beginning of the tape was Tyler Blaze’s promotion for the upcoming match. Patti: BLAH! BLAH !BLAH! Can these men ever do anything other then how bad they’re life has been. I need something to totally prepare me for this match up. Patti hit the fast forward button as she continued to watch the promo speed by. Then suddenly the tape parted from Tyler talking about his life being empty to a simple scene. A scene that causes Patti stop the tape. Patti sat up, her hazel eyes never leaving the scene before her. Her mind suddenly raced back to the scene showing. [I][Tyler stands there over Syxx and poses for the crowd. He then looks over and sees something that sets him off though. A female fan at ringside, not one of those little things, but a full blown woman, with long blonde hair and a tight fitting KnightMare Brigade Babydoll T-shirt.. She's standing there with a sign for Sean McClancy. Tyler walks over and gets into her face. She yells at him. Tyler grabs her sign and then rips it up in front of her. He throws the sign's pieces back at her. He gets back in her face and she slaps the taste out of his mouth!] [/I] Patti: Bloody fucking hell!! Where did she find that from? Patti threw the pillows off the bed and screamed at the top of her lungs. A moment in her life had been caught on tape and displayed for the world to see. A moment in her life that she wanted to remember with such joy and passion turned up in a Tyler Blaze promo. Someone was playing mind games and it wasn’t going over to well. Patti slid off the bed and began throwing things. If it wasn’t nailed down, then it was on the other side of the room. Her Greek temper flaring at everything going on around her. Several small unimportant items went crashing against the wall, one barely missing the now terrorized Princess. Patti continued to destroy whatever she could get her hands on. As far as she was concerned, it no longer mattered that Tyler Blaze was attempting to date her best friend, it was now more along the lines of war for reminding her of it all. Patti grabbed a pillow and carelessly threw it onto the bed. A soft yelp comes from behind it. Patti turns and stares at the lump of a pillow. Her anger completely gone as she hears the whimper coming from behind the oversized pillow. Patti: Oh, Princess! I’m sorry. I’m not used to being around here just yet. Patti moves the pillows out of the way and finds the poor thing whimpering and shaking, her brown eyes reflecting the fear she feels toward her mistress. Patti quickly picks up her puppy and strokes her head. Patti: It’s ok baby, I’m just upset over all of this. Hopefully Lacuna and I can pull off this victory, cause if we do, we will prove once again that women can out battle men. Don’t you be worrying about anything, I won’t watch the Toddy boy tape, I won’t bore you with this useless dribble about once again having to face me. It will all come to an end, when both Lacuna and I stand victorious over you both, for this time, I’m coming in with revenge on my mind, and revenge is what I will get. Fade out.
