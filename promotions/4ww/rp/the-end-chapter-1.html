The End, Chapter 1 Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #684 Date: 05-10-04 Location: N/A INTIAL THOUGHTS _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ After being away from the sport for sometime, you begin to feel as though you can no longer play with the best. The preasure can be immense and the oppertunity can be somewhat overwhelming. It's almost as if you are being left breathless and you just know it's your time to die. I had considered running and never coming back, I thought if I were to escape, maybe just maybe I could try to live a normal life... Then I came to the harsh realization, I could never be normal and no one that knew the truth could accept me for that. No one, except Amanda and Dimiscus... Alot has happened since Dimiscus passed. I've become so much more, open... Though, I cannot help but wonder what life would have been like had I made other choices. I wonder where I would be if I had just walked away at times. There are so many questions, so many thoughts that ravish my mind and all I can do is attempt to comprehend what I want... what I need... what I desire. THE END, CHAPTER ONE _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ ...After a few quick paces forward they find themselves standing before an automated door which opens for them. Instantly after another automated door opens infront of them. Xantos nods and heads up to the receptionist to inquire about her condition. A small elderly man in a white jacket turns around and lifts a clipboard to his face. He looks over the counter and waves to Darek. Darek appraoches the doctor who doesn't look too pleased. 'Mr. Angle, I had hoped I would see you today.' He pauses, 'This is never easy, your wife's condition does not seem to be improving. Infact over the last ocuple days it seems as though she may be ready to give up hope.' He pushses the bridge of his glasses up to the top of his nose, 'I want you to know that we are doing everything in our power to keep her here with us.' Without saying a word, Darek brushes past the doctor and begins heading towards Amanda's door, her lucky number, 13. The hollow sound of his foodsteps is heard down the empty hall as the doctor turns back to Darek and calls for him to come back. Instead the footsteps continue and Darek stops for a second peering down either way of a crossing hallway. He sees her room and quickly heads in the direction. Xantos walks up to the doctor and shakes his hand before calling out to his father. 'Everything is going to be fine, trust me.' As Xantos finishes his sentence a loud beeping noise is heard and the intercom flips on: 'Code red room 13, Code Red.' Everything in Darek's world stops , his body, his mind, his heart. The glass vase begins to slip and fall from his hands. The doctor begins to sprint down the hallway towards Darek. As the doctor comes coser the vase falls from Darek's hands and crashes into the floor shattering into hundreds of pieces. Instantaniously the doctor speeds by avoiding the glass chariging into the room of Amanda. Darek rushes towards the door to her room but is met by security and nurses as the doctors hurry into the room. 'She's flatlining.' And that was the last thing Darek heard before being taken away from the room. His eyes never leave the door but he doesn't struggle, in the end he knows it is best for the doctors to handle the situation... but the pain of losing is one the Angel has not been to familiar with and recently, it has become reality... HEARTS DESIRE _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ Even after all the work that had put in, the hours of undying attention, a figure of beauty lay in a coma and has for the last two months. The world as Darek knew it had begun to change before his very eyes. He could remember sitting with Amanada for most of the days, in this great time of need. With his undying love, he would grasp onto her hand and squeeze it tightly, letting her know she was not alone. Somehow, through all of this, through the pain, the agony, the waiting, Darek's feelings had begun to change for Amanada. He would go to the hospital each day, more out of a routine than love. He felt he could no longer stand to wait for his love to be returned to him. Her life was in the hands of a unruly God. Darek had sensed that she was not ready to die yet, but he also knew things would not be the same in the end. 'Amanda,' he pause and gets no response, not a twitch, not a wiggle, 'Amanda, I cannot keep going on like this and knowing, I know you wouldn't want to go on like this.' With a cloud of blackness rolling in over head, Darek pushes himself out of the chair and moves gingerly toward the vines and peers outside. The sky has been consumed by the demonic blackness of the storm clouds. Flashes of bright yellow light flicker high above the world and sounds of crashing thunder shake the streets. A smile forms around the mouth of the former East Wind Champion. 'I wish we didn't have to live like this. I wish everything could have been different.' He turns back to Amanda, his smile vannishes. 'If I wasn't cursed to be me, you and I could have been truly happy. I want you to know, I would not change thse past years for anything in the world.' His head cautiously swings back towads the wind and his eyes immediately to the split in the blinds made by his fingers. Overhead the lightning crashes downward, drawing closer and closer to earth. The rain has begun to pour and like billions of sharp needles they pierce from the heavens down. The faint flicker of his grin seems to resurface. Slowly his eyes begin to wander away from the blinds and towards the large machine which now breathes and beats the loving heart of his wife. 'I-I-...' A tear begins to fall down the cheek of Darek. His eyes appear gloomy and dim, his smiling face ceases to exist and in this momment only trhe pain of heartache can be felt. Love had been a forbidden feeling for a demonic being like him, yet still in his life of sin he found someone who saw him for more than he was and something more than he ever thought he could be. Yet he had begun to desire something more. His passion, his fire, it had begun to burn brighter than ever before. The thought of being with another seemed alomost forbidden. '...love you.' He spins away from the glass and walks slowly towards the door not once making eye contact with Amanda. His thoughts could do what his heart could not. He knew his life had become overtaken by the thought of another. His pending love, no something more, had made his lust for blood stronger than ever before. Thoughts of mere carnage had run through his mind as he imagined himself with the sleek beauty he could only dream about right now. Soon, he had hoped his dreams would become reality... A SENSE OF REALITY _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ 'Come on Darek,' She coo's at him, 'come with me. I'll make you free.' Her beautiful hair flows gentley as the breaze picks up.Turning towards her objective, she makes the world seem to stop, all focus, attantion and eyes are poised on this woman. Everything about her is captivating, her beauty cannot be described and yet for such an innocent face the attitude could hardly match. 'I emplore you, please be with me forever.' Her luscious lips press together only enticing her victim more. Slowly she stalks her man like a great preditor; just as she does in the ring. It's almost as if her beauty could charm any man, any being, charm them into doing anything and everything. Flaunt what you've got to get what you need... 'Forget the past, forget her.' A smile curls around her ruby red mouth, 'She couldn't love you like I do.' A distant noise is heard. In an instant all focus surrounds the thick voice heard creeping into the darkness. ' ...I leave for two months and come back to find the future of the Four Winds in jeopardy. We have the FAW coming into our federation, challenging for our championships and taking names of our superstars. I have watched this boil into the affair that it has and could no longer stand back and watch this imminent take over. ' Familiar... yet, the vision of the perfect woman entices the attention more so. fighting her way back into the scene, she comes out revealing more. A thick grin highlights the definition of her beauty, subtly her lips curve perfectly, small lines wrinkle beside her nose and her smile seems to provide something to hold onto. '... I do love you, more than anyone and I can give you anything.' Her voice floats through the nothingness, 'anything at all.' She runs her index finger from her sweet lips painfully slow down her chin, neck, cheast and inbetween her breasts, a look of allurement draws all the attention. The appeal of such a fine specimen could attract a man to do anything... some have, and some will. Intruding yet, faintly and ever so distant. The thick, familiar voice seems to break through, destroying all that is pleasant... ' It's nothing personal Edgecrusher, it's only business. And speaking of which, I do have some I wish to take care of. I want the world to know and fear the name Dark Angel. To do this I have to beat the best and the best is certainly NOT Patti Anderson. Patti, you may be the North Wind Champion, but as long as you hold that title, it has absolutely no value or meaning to me. Tonight was a random occurance, better a bitch for a champion than an immigrant from another promotion. ' Louder, clearer, the voice speaks; almost as if it was beckoning... ' Face it, the only hope you had was in your 'Saviour' and now he cannot even help you. He happened to hear that Dark Angel was returning and ran away with his tale tucked between his legs. Some saviour, some champion. Todd Dream, you forgot about me after the SCWF and I never forgot about you, remember this, no matter where you go, no matter how far you travel, I will not be happy until my vengence is satisfied. I want your blood, I want your dignity, I want you... Apparently though, you can only fear me as much I as I thought you had. Therefor I would like to announce myself your SAVIOUR. ' Though louder and clearer, the voice cannot manage to uphold the attention. Instead, it is the arousing scent of a woman seems to take hold once again. Her gentle voice sings a chorus in the hearts of her endearing fans and obsessive stalkers... 'You could never forget me, you could never hurt me...' she pauses, 'I know this, because you lov--' She seems to fade into the distance as the familiar voice takes hold once again. ' And as your Saviour, I promise to walk the Four Winds out of this period of damnation and towards the light of a better tomorrow. As of this point forward, the Knightmare has just become reality and an imminent war between the Brigade and the world is brewing. The Knightmare Brigade shall free the 4ww from the oppression of the FAW and PRIMETIME. Fnord andI will dominate the wrestling industry with our intimidation, guile, intellegence, resilliancey and raw power. Welcome to my knightmare. ' DEEP THOUGHT _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ His eyes roll open , the blur seems overwhelming at first. Still, like each day, he fights through the blurriness and stares off into the darkness. His senses adjust and in the distance the all too familiar sounds of StormFront unfold. Bringing his hand to his eyes, he rubs away the sleep. Shaking his head only a picture of the sultry woman remains in his thoughts. He rolls to the side and glances at the bright red lights of his alarm clock. 9:21. Reality begins to set in and the pain of living continues to eat away at his mind, body and soul. He falls back into the bed and closes his eyes. There is no desire to be, there is no longer a will to win, there is only a broken man. Slowly, the events had unfolded before his eyes and all that he had planned for was lost. He had hoped that he could be saved, and at one point in his life, he thought he was. The darkness consumes his mind and slowly he allows himself to be taken into thought... 'What the hell have I been thinking? What in God's name have I done...?' His thoughts echo through his mind, constantly eating through his head and into his soul. His deepest desires had been the driving force for a ghastly deed. He fought in vain to rid his body of the temptation and will that fueled his actions. The most he could figure was possibly that he was indeed in love, only his love had changed. The prize had gotten much larger, his goals had become clearer and then he was given the perfect momment. One instant to change everything. 'I did what had to be done...' 'I did what was right by me...' ' I did what my heart desired.' Ravished by the overwhelming impulse to take what should be his, sends a cold chill down his spine. Small, round lumps form rapidly over his body like a plague. He could only hope that his actions were going to help him suceed in this endeavor. He had hoped that a triumphant return under the right pretenses would entice his 'desire', that it would open the eyes of his 'destiny'... 'Whomever claimed, actions speak louder than words, was clearly mistaken.' 'My actions showed my loyalty, my deepest wish to obtain one thing...' 'Words could not describe the feelings of betrayal, the pain...' His mind is continually ravished by the thought of Patti Anderson. For long he had planned, waited and then finally executed a masterful plot at exactly the right time; a plan to obtain the services of the moset renowed athlete in the Four Winds, Patti Anderson. Instead, after watching and counting Patti Anderson to a victory over Edgecrusher he came out to make his words heard and feared. He never wanted the world to see him as one who could even care for this... so called champion. '...rejection....' '....humiliation....' 'She made me feel this way...' '...and in my dreams, she's so different...' However he did feel this way and he could only describe the intense desire as something more than lover, more than an obsession. From her, he wanted more, he wanted all that she had to offer. Those luscious red lips, those perfect eyes and that god killing body. He could hardly take his mind off of her. Rarely he found himself imagining life with her rather than Amanda... '...what about Amanda?' 'What, about her?!' 'I... I don't know...' The conflict of reality and his wonderous dreamland had become hard on Darek. His sleeping habits had been irregular and he found little time to rest. Between a hectic schedule, a dying wife, the return of his son and all the weight of the Four Winds on his shoulder. The weight to elevate the federation beyond any status it could have reached before his inception. He opens his eyes and glances to the clock once again. 10:32. His eyes close and in that instant he finishes his thoughts... '...if I still love her...' FORSEEN FUTURES _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ Peering through a crack in the blinds, a beam of golden yellow manages to find the still closed eyes of Darek. As the light beats down upon his face he begins to shake slightly and then rolls over. His arm slides the sheet just high enough to cover his eyes before he lets out something of a snorting sound. Suddenly the sound of the shades raising up in a swift fasion is heard and the sun just beams into the once black hole containing a sleeping giant. Darek shoots his head upward in more of a nerve reaction than anything else. Slowly his eyelids begin to roll up and the chalky white has become noticable. He turns his head inspecting the source of such a travesty and finds the culprit. Standing smugly in the corner of the room is Xantos. He has his arms crossed and is tapping his foot. 'While you were sleeping last night, I took the liberty of taping Jerm's Promo for you.' Darek drops back to his bed and once again pulls the sheets up and then this time follows with the covers, hoping for more darkness. 'You woke me up for that?' he slurs out, 'I can beat Jerms on my worst day. Now fuck off.' 'Unbelievable. Here you lay, motionless and uncarring a couple nights before the biggest fight of your life.' 'It's not going to be a fight.' He stops before pulling his head out from under the covers, 'it's going to be a war. This is not about the East Wind titles like I had preoccupied with before, it's not about the North Wind title or even that figurhead-like title that Jerms holds.' Interupting his father, 'then waht is it about?' Darek pushes the sheets away from his body before kicking his feet off the bed and onto the floor. He turns his head away from his son. 'it's about obtaining something they said I could never have.' Xantos lets his shoulder fall against the wall and uses it for support as a look of confussion crosses his face. He squints his eyes and tilts his head to the side; peering harshly onto his father Xanto's eyes question the previous statment. His hand moves under his chin and scratches it for a momment. '...I said it wasn't about the North Wind title...' Darek repeats as if he knew exactly what Xantos was thinking. 'I don't think you could understand the driving force in this match, I am a desperate being right now and desperate times do call for desperate measures.' He turns towards his flesh and blood, 'I want you to remember that. In this world, the fallen have died as casualties of a greater war...' Xantos steps off the wall and turns away from his father, he toses the tape onto Darek's bed and makes his way towards the door. 'Why do I get the feeling you've done something terrible?' 'I am who I am, Xantos.' Darek stands up, his body is covered in gothic art tatoo's. The word 'Immortal' is written down his spine in indian black ink. Along his chiseled left arm is a free flowing blood red gothic cross. His name, 'Dark Angel' arches above his belly button in a gloss blood tinge. He turns towards his son with a blank face, 'we can never change who I am...' 'That's truly what I am afraid of.' Xantos walks out of the door. 'THE MESSENGER' _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ 'I declared war within the Four Winds Federation and the Primetime community. I said that all would learn to fear the name, Dark Angel. I stand by those words. I have taken many people to the brink of their lives and even sanity, I will not cease to do so. Strong as ever, more capable than ever and even more so now, I long to have my name remembered. I will build my dynasty this week, I will bring to their knees, the most dominant in the 4ww today...' He stops for a momment. '...Jerms, The Technical Spectacle, the Four WInds Champion, the rightful number one contender; you've had my number twice, two nights I will never forget. I want you to understand that I never forgive and I rarely forget. Malestrom III saw you corinated as the Four Winds Champion, I could not be there to attend your cerimony, however this StormFront, I have a special celebration planned just for you. You've embarrassed and disgraced me before, I know EXACTLY what you are capable of and I am more than ready to compete with it. All I can think about at this given momment is the taste of bitter-sweet revenge, I thirst for your blood in return for you tarnishing my record. You and the Featured Attraction may have been the big thing , and now this take over you're planning you believe is the new big thing, but I want you to understand... NEVER, NOT EVER... will I stand down to you. You can fight and fight but you will never hold me down. I refuse to be beatten by worthless trash such as yourself again. You and the world need to realize that the Immort Angel is the future of the Four winds.' After a momment he continues on. 'Sweet, dear Patti Anderson... There's so much I can say about you, but nothing I wish to share with the world. Words roll off my tongue without discretion, I say what needs to be said to get the job done... Last week WE got the job done... The best the 4ww has to offer and yet you walked away on the arm of Tarrant. Revenge is a dish best served cold, I know the cliche is old, but it seems to make so much sense to me. Patti you should not fear the wrath of the reaper, for he does not come for your soul, he only comes for you... Have pleasant Knightmares...'
