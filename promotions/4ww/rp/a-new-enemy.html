A new enemy

4 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A new enemy Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #435 Date: 4th of jan 04 Location: Tucson, Arizona [Fade in] [The camera pans into a small TV flickering in the dark, a small line runs up and down the screen as it rewinds. Play. The tape begins to play it shows what happened at the holiday show in-between Lee Satima and Jerms. Jerms rushes over and drives the front of the chair into the back of Satima's leg. Satima falls to the mat as Jerms begins to stomp away at the leg of Satima. The ref rushes back into the ring but is pushed directly out by Jerms. Jerms takes the chair and slams it down onto Satima’s leg. The faint sound of Morales and Candler are heard before the tape rewinds. Rewind Lights turn on and camera pans out to Lee sitting on a bed with a meager smile in a nice hotel room, the sheets on the bed are made up, they haven’t been used yet, they are a pale yellow which matches the colour of the wall. Lee’s right leg is propped up on a chair a few inches between the bed and an old wooden cabinet where the TV rests on top. To his left side there is a wall which most of its space is taken up by a large window, the window is shut and the yellow curtains are closed. A pair of metal crutches are leaning against the wall. In the left corner of the camera the same footage as before is being shown; Jerms rushes over and drives the front of the chair into the back of Satima's leg. Satima falls to the mat as Jerms begins to stomp away at the leg of Satima. Lee puts down the remote for the VCR. The camera pans closer to Lee who begins to straighten up. He clears his throat. With a grunt and a smirk he begins.] Lee: Good evening, as you know I was assaulted by Jerms at the holiday show. It was a Santa Clause suit match and despite my reluctance to be a part of the match I went out there and did it any way. [Lee shakes his head slowly.] Lee: But as I went out there I was booed, and that’s not even what I care about, what I care about is that Jerms; mister-too-good-to-wear-a-suit-he-thinks-is-dumb, not only doesn’t wear the suit but he attacks me from behind. I won that match I was the first and I was the last person in that suit and for no reason he took the cowards way out and assaults me! [Lee takes a deep breath and calms himself down.] Lee: Hah let me tell you Jerms, anytime, anywhere, I will face you and I will beat, whether it’s a hardcore match or whether it’s a submission match I will win. Your nothing more then a coward. A little child afraid to face a real man. Tell me are you gonna weasel your way through life or are you gonna be a man? Are you gonna bark all day or bite? [He lifts his leg off the chair with assistance from his hands he gets it onto the ground, he sits up and looks into the camera with a sadistic smile upon his face.] Lee: Jerms, everywhere you go, everywhere you look, everywhere and anywhere I will be watching you. I’m not out for a pitiful revenge. I don’t want redemption. I want blood. I want gore and I want you. [Lee smiles with his mouth open, the dim light in the room reflects off his pointed teeth.] Lee: You better sleep with one I open my friend, ‘cuz when you least expect it I’ll be there waiting, I’ll be there smiling. Because I’ll know that that’s when I’ll get my revenge, that’s when I’ll get my gore. [He leans back] Lee: You’re just a small bump in the road, and Jerms I will flatten you. I will have my time, and that time is soon. [He begins to laugh] [Fade out]
