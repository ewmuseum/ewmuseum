Did he change his spots?

31 August 2004

Did he change his spots? Blaise 4WW Exposures #916 Date: 8/31/04 Location: Arai Ryokan A leopard can’t change its spots. No they can’t. Call a spade a spade if you will. Or call Darek DeAngelo… Dark Angel. It doesn’t matter. People give you a façade in this sport, it’s true. But deep down below a true artist puts a little bit of themselves into their work. Darek DeAngelo would have you think he’s a changed man. He’d have you think that he’s suddenly found some god and that he’s now out to save people and be their messiah. But is he really that? Is he really here to save you and me? Just a few weeks ago he was trying to steal another man’s woman. A sin that is even in the 10 commandments. Sean and Patti are all but officially married. And he tries to take her from him. He’s supposedly killed. He’s supposedly done very evil things. And he’d like you to belive that he’d just up and change in the course of one week and then suddenly become your favorite? What a crock of shit. He can’t change what he is at the genetic level. He’s a half breed: Half angel, half human. There hasn’t been one of them come out that has not actually been diabolical. He’s evil. They say that the false messiah in the last days will sway those to thinking that he’s the way of the world, and that he speaks the truth. They say that evil is beautiful to look upon. Guess he’s just living proof of that. I mean, why pretend to be what you aren’t, Darek? Do you really think you’re going to save this federation? From what? From people like Patti Anderson and Kyo Kushinaga? From people like me? You walk a path that is thankless then. I did that in the SCWF for almost a year. The one that they all cheered. The one that they all rooted for. Kissing the babies, selling the t-shirts, hawking the merchandise, doing the commericals. Yeah, the pays good. But in the end, you get shit. All that matters is if you can put the asses in the seats. They don’t want to be saved, they want to see certain people. And those that are nothing but a paper doll don’t interest them. C’mon Darek… you needed 3 people to help you last time in a match that was nothing but a gimmick and a means of you satisfying your dark side. Now it’s my turn. Now it’s my match. Now it’s for real. You won last time. You beat me. Well you, Bullet, Patti and Kanton beat me. But I went into the casket and it closed and I got burned. Are you happy? Just remember one thing… I never got pinned. I never submitted to you. In fact, you were the one laying on the mat when that match was over. Now it’s between you and me for that piece of gold you parade around with. I’ve got my own, but adding yours would just be a slap in the fact to people like Jerms and others that are nothing but flounders compared to me. Those like your friends Bullet, Kanton and the others. All of unStable have fallen to me. Now it’s your turn. People feared me when I wore the mask as Shoudou. Why did they stop? They shouldn’t have. Who will be in your corner Darek? Can you trust anyone enough to serve as your second? Can you ensure that the person you choose to trust won’t hurt you? I know who I trust. And she’s proven that to me. No, I see how Darek will do this. He knows now that Ukyo would be a weakness. He’ll go after her. Then again, he seems to not have a clear grasp on how things go anyway. He thinks he can play the game with a McClancy? Yeah right. Not even an honorary McClancy like Patti. The rules are simple. Out wrestle your opponent. Pin or be pinned. Make them submit or submit. Or run like a scared rabbit. It doesn’t matter. I’m a better wrestler then you. I’m a better fighter. In the end, you will know you’ve been in a match. Remember that, get used to it. When you bet against the house, you’re bound to loose. Look at you Darek. You’re no different. You’re still the man who needed help. You know nothing about change. You know nothing about embracing a legacy, a culture that you knew nothing about. You know not about another thing… love. You have gone so long without it, you’re just a bitter old man. Your son didn’t want you, your granddaughter is on the path to either taking a McClancy or an Anderson name. And for all we know, your woman didn’t love you either. You’ve not changed. But some things have. Sometimes change is good. It’s time for one change in the federation, for almost 3 months you’ve held the South Wind Title. It’s time to put it on someone else. But now on to other things as we pick up after where we left off. Act IX: Later on that day. Charlene stood in her room, looking at her watch. She was dressed to go out. But for her that meant a nice blouse and skirt. She was always the one to look the role of the adult. Even though it betrayed her true age, the soon to be 18 year old was a business genius, but was still a child in many people’s books. Especially Kyo Kushinaga’s, her one love that she pined for. But he showed no interest. But she’d not be the one to give up hope. But now she was recovering from what she had found this morning… Just last night her two charges had quite a night. First they wrestled Chris West and Eddie B in a match where they defended the 4WW’s version of the tag team titles, the East Wind Championship. But as they celebrated, Ukyo Kushniaga, the big sister of her desired one, punched Blaise McClancy, her other charge… and then kissed him for what ended up beign a few minutes in the ring. Ukyo had then run off early and made it back to the hotel room first. After talking to Charlene, a little bit heated, she’d gone to apologize to her partner. And then this morning, Charlene found them naked and sleeping wit each other. It had been bound to happen. Charlene had detected the tension between them. She’d talked to Blasie about it. He was open enough to her, but he never pushed it upon Ukyo. She probably hadn’t even noticed the things he did. But apparenly last night she did. And two hours ago, she had woken them up. And she still wasn’t back to their room. Today was supposed to be a day of relaxation for them, but it was going to disappear quickly. She could guess what they were doing. Charlene had studied up upon what Blaise was. He wans’t too far off from what Ukyo really way. She was a Kami, connected to the spirits of earth and fire. Her powers were there, but she wasn’t as open about using them as her brother was. Blaise was a dryad, a nature spirit on his half Irish side. But the book she had been given by a member of the Order, had detailed the studies that had been done on Dryads as if they were something to be disected. It detailed the full accounts of what was called getting ‘infected’ by one. When ever alone with Blaise, Ukyo would feel lust. Charlene understood why Patti Anderson had fallen for Sean McClancy so bad. She hoped Ukyo had made the right choice. The door knob rattled as it opened. Charlene looked at her watch again. Ukyo stepped in wearing what she had the night before, the silk robe and her underwear back in place. Both had been originally found discarded by her. Ukyo shut the door and leaned back against it as she looked at her younger friend. “It’s about time. I figured our shopping trip was canceled if you two were going to take all day.” Ukyo blushed slightly. “I’m sorry. But it was…” “Don’t say it. But I must say, you might have more guys hoping for apologies from you if this gets out.” Ukyo’s face went to a scowl. “What? I… I…” “So was it a one night thing, or are you two going to give it a try?” Charlene crossed her arms, and sat there looking like the good old mother hen. “Well let’s put it this way. He’s going with us today, and he’s taking us both to dinner at a steak house in the area that serves the best Kobe Beef. And when we get back, I’m thinking Blaise and I will be reserving a private Onsen to relax from the day. We have that photo op shoot tomorrow.” Charlene smirked at her. “So I’ve got to go out with you two, when you probably won’t be able to keep your hands off each other then? Maybe I should stay here.” Charlene walked away from the door abit and kicked one of her pillows up from the floor to her hands and then threw it right at Charlene. “Hey!” “That’s what you get.” “So, I take it he’s forgiven you? And you him?” “Uh, many times over?” “I didn’t need to hear that, thank you.” “Oh, really? That’s coming from the girl that used to sit up watching TV with me rating the guys on the late night TV shows? The same one I used to get into the R movies to see them with me?” Charlene blushed a bit, but for a different reason. “You’ll both make a lovely couple I’m sure.” “Yeah, and before you try to get it out of me. Based on that video that Kyo found, yes he and his cousin apparently have some things in common.” Charlene threw the pillow back at her. “EWWW!” Ukyo smiled and then walked over to where her dresser was. She reached in and pulled out some clothes from the drawers. They had decided to stay at this secluded Ryokan and take the trains to all of the events in a means of having a settled appearance over the many weeks they would be in Japan. So most of her clothese were here in the dresser. “I already showered over there. So just let me get dressed and we can head out. He’ll be knocking on the door soon.” “You showered over there?” Ukyo just smiled at her and then disappeared into the small bathroom. Charlene looked at the door and shook her head. “She’s almost giddy.” And then a knock on the door. “Hold on!” Charlene knew who it would be and as she walked over and opened the door, her suspicions were confirmed. It was Blaise, standing there wearing a pair of black slacks and a collared shirt that looked like it was silk. She had watched a few weeks ago as he had some tailored work done in a business suit and a few other things. This fit him so well it had to be one of those that he had specially done. “Hi, Ukyo said she wasn’t going to take long to get ready. So I tried to hurry up. I’m going to assume we’re still waiting.” “Yeah, you can assume that. How’s the nose?” “Doin’t better.” Fortunatly, there hadn’t been any brusing or blackening from his encounter last night. “I’m almost ready, just have to get my shoes!” Ukyo yelled as she came flying out of the bathroom. Charlene turned around to see her as Ukyo was bending over to put on a pair of heels. A pair of shoes that she normally did not bother with wearing. And then Charlene almost dropped to the ground. For the second time in a few hours, Ukyo had shocked the hell out of Charlene Kwan. Ukyo stood up after putting on the heels, and then turned around, straightening out her skirt. Yes, Ukyo Kushnaga stood there in front of them in a skirt and a blouse. Her athletic sculpted legs actually seeing the light of day. “Uh, Ukyo….” “Yeah?” “You’re still missing part of your outfit.” “No I’m not” Charlene looked at her with an inquisitive look on her face. “Blaise said that we’re going to an expensive dinner after we go shopping. I’m not going to wear jeans and a cut up t-shirt when you two are dressed nicely.” “Yeah, besides, I like how her legs look.” Charlene turned her head and looked at Blaise. He was a strange man when it came to things. He was always so open about how he felt. Turning to look at Ukyo, a slight tinge of red had returned to her cheeks at Blaise’s comment. Ukyo grabbed what would be her purse for the day and threw her usual wallet in it. Normally that wallet would be in her jeans back pocket, but that luxury wasn’t available today. The three turned and headed out, Charlene taking up the rear and ensuring the door was locked… it was time for some rest and relaxation. Then tomorrow the work would begin again. But now Charlene had something else to worry about…. The damned reporters. As she watched the new couple hold hands on their way down the walk, she knew that rumors would spread soon. Not that they really were rumors anymore. Again, she hoped that Ukyo and Blaise had both made the right choice. End.
