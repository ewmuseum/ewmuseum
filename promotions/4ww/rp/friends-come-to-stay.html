Friends Come To Stay Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #443 Date: 01/05/03 Location: The Hotel Faith - noun 1 a : allegiance to duty or a person : 1. LOYALTY b (1) : fidelity to one's promises (2) : sincerity of intentions 2 a (1) : belief and trust in and loyalty to God (2) : belief in the traditional doctrines of a religion b (1) : firm belief in something for which there is no proof (2) : complete trust 3 : something that is believed especially with strong conviction; especially : a system of religious beliefs synonym see BELIEF Something that this pathetic excuse of a mystery opponent obviously lacks. Faith is something one always depends on, and you my masked friend, do not have what it takes to take step into the ring with me and do battle. For you see, I walk into the ring every week with it. I take on my opponents with the strong belief that I can and will conquer my opponents. I stand proud to battle all that come my way. I face the tests and challenges that Fey, Ian, and the entire booking staff throws my way. Faith is all it takes, and a strong belief in ones self. And from the look of your little promos there Mr. Mystery Man, I sat in the state of fear and doubt for long enough. Your surprise will be a welcome on that’s for certain. I hope you wrestle far better then you are at trying to talk the talk. You’ve failed to impress me so far. That little promos makes Chris West seem intelligent, and makes Fnord seem like a god among men. I guess you are going to need the faith factor more then I am. I see nothing of what is going to make me afraid of you. I’ve done my cowering in fear and still came out smelling like a rose, I will NOT let the fear you are so working on creating within me make lose this match, for the unknown just maybe what I need to defeat you in the long rung. So bring your masked covered face and your ‘holier then thou’ attitude and give me some real competition for a change. I am willing, ready, and ABLE to prove to the great fans of the 4WW what a perfect phoney you are. That is all you are. A phoney, a fake, and someone that lacks a lot of faith within themselves to be able to defeat the ‘Darling of the 4WW’. And on that note...its time to see what the ‘Darling’ is up too.... ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: The Hotel Suite of Patti Anderson Time: A Few Hours Before Storm Front ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti and her brand new gift from the “Irish Bastard” laid curled up on the couch, mindless flipping through the channels desperately trying to find something to cure the boredom that was setting in. If Sean had been here, they’d be testing out the springs on the bed, and probably doing the Karma Sutra in every room of the suit. Patti looked down and saw that Princess was falling asleep, her soft brown eyes following everything in the room, as she struggled to keep her eyes awake. Patti slowly started to stroke the pups head, playing with her ear every once in a while. Suddenly Princess bounced off of her lap and scampered to the door, her bark alerting her to the fact that there is someone at the door. Patti follows close behind, hearing the pounding on the door. Patti: Shhhhh Princess! Your going to wake up everyone in the hotel, and I’ll have to deal with a pissed off desk clerk. Now we don’t want that. Patti stands on tip toe to peer out the little eye whole to see who could be showing up on her door step at this time of night. All she manages is to catch a glimpse of two dark haired beauties. One barely hidden from view while the continues the pounding. The sound of another puppy barking on the other side could mean only one thing....Jade and Lacuna Debris had come to Arizona. Patti: Bloody hell is up with this? I was expecting Jade but not Lacuna. What do you say Princess, should we let them in. The little Australian Sheepdog tilted her puppy head from side to side, pretending to understand her mistress. She yelped in response as a foot kicked the door open.. Lacuna carried a half passed out Jade into the room and sat her down on the floor, making sure she was propped up against the wall. Princess, happy to see someone other then her mistress, pounced on Jade licking her face. Jade started sputtering as both puppies attacked her with loving. Patti and Lacuna couldn’t help but smile as the two puppies kept pouncing on Jade. Jade would push them away, but they both would keep coming back at her. After a while, Patti called off Princess, leaving the St. Bernard puppy to torment his mistress. Patti: What in the bloody hell happened to the two of you? You look like the red kangaroos got a hold of you in the wild outback? Lacuna: Jade was attacked in her apartment. She got hit shortly after she got off the phone. I arrived just in time to kick some serious ass. Unfortunately Jade lost someone close to her. I needed to get her out of New York, she said something about you invited her here. Patti stared down at Jade, a look of concern on her face as she watched the tears slowly falling down her face. From the looks of things, Patti knew more then just Jade had been attacked in that apartment, she’d question it, but her common sense told her to wait until later. Patti: What kind of affect will this have on hers and your match this week? Lacuna looked over at Jade. Lacuna: With the mind set that Jade is in right now, Fnord doesn’t have a chance of getting out of either match alive. I’ll handle Coricidon. What about you? How are you handling not knowing what you are facing? Patti: Oh, don’t you be worrying about me. Eliza sent over his promos. I have been worried over nothing. I have a feeling Mr. Black Mask boy is going to turn out to be nothing more then a joke. I should have realized it was just someone trying to make a name for myself. He’s got it figured that I’m just a simple girl that will cower over having a surprise thrown at her. I hate to be the one to tell him this, but it won’t happen. Suddenly a voice came from out of nowhere. Jade: Don’t fucking worry about a thing. The worst that could happen is that freak that stole your towel. Patti flushed as those memories came back. A time in Patti’s life that she had tried to forget. Patti: Bloody well not be him. I don’t have Sean here to protect me this time around. I’m afraid he may try to do something this time. Jade and Lacuna: We’ve got your back. Patti smiled for the first time since Sean had left. This is what she needed. She needed to know her friends were going to be there for her, and with this week’s match, she was more then thankful to know that her two new friends would and will be watching her back. Fade Out.
