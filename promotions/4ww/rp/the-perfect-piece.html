The Perfect Piece Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #191 Date: 10/4/03 Location: The Mall Broken - adjective- 1. Violently separated into parts: SHATTERED 2. Damaged or altered by breaking; as a: having undergone or been subjected to fracture. Broken, just like the two others that will be in the triple threat match this weekend at the first ever Maelstrom Pay Per View. Fey has decided she needs to protect her ‘investment’ from the anger that Patti is holding against her. It must be one of those deals where if Fey thinks having a has-been in the ring with her ‘choice’ will stop Patti from beating the hell out of Annie. Then Fey may just need to rethink that strategy. Because its one that is surely to blow up in her face. Fey puts her precious Annie in harms way. She knows that Patti has been itching to get her hands on the one woman who stands in the way of her happiness. A woman, who still after all these years, claims Patti’s boyfriend as her own. A woman who has used lies and deceit to separate Patti from Sean. She’s tried tying her up, creating a scene as to where it looks as if Patti has gone to the hospital to get a pregnancy test. Annie just doesn’t get it. Here let Destiny make it simple.....GIVE UP! You can’t and won’t win. Sean no longer wants you, he found the one thing you were incapable of. He found love, and its just killing you. Just go back to Ireland, and find yourself someone new, settle down and keep your little world going. And what about Rose Astral. A woman who retired years ago. One to many broken necks killed her career. Now she thinks she has what it takes to take on the ‘Darling of the 4WW’. Someone please explain to Destiny WHY Patti is always the target of the new ones that join the federation? Does she think she can honestly compete with someone trained by the True Hardcore Icon? Or is it because she thinks that after a two year stint of retirement, she can come right back and claim what she believes is her spotlight? Or could it because she has to prove to her daughter that mommy still has what it takes to be the best in the business? The questions go on and on, but the answers point to the same answer. NO. Rose can NOT beat the ‘Darling of the 4WW’. Her neck might as well have a bull’s-eye target painted on it, because that is what the intended target will be.. And what about Mr. Sean Summers? The Simply Stunning One? The man who has made it his personal mission to attack Patti on every occasion? He should be obsessed with taking on Patti’s boyfriend, instead of attacking her. Maybe he should concentrate more on patching up his relationship with Eliza. Destiny knows that without love, the world isn’t really a nice place to live. You can wind up cold and cruel like Annie. All Destiny can say is that a few bodies will be broken. Someone will more then likely get hurt, and someone will come out the winner. We all know that the ‘Darling of the 4WW’ will be the one that will have her hands raised high in the air. ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: LAS VEGAS Time: AFTERNOON ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti couldn’t believe what she had learned in just a short time. Sean’s own uncle had been exiled from the clan because he fell in love with a commoner. Someone almost like her, except that Patti was of royal blood herself. Her ancestry was as old as Sean’s if not older. Her father told her of the days of old when men and women worshiped the Gods and Goddess of Greece. Her father always spoke proudly of it. Up until a few years ago, her father had been her strength. Minos told his little girl that no matter what it is, everything is worth fighting for. From the last conversation that she had with him, she told him she intended to fight for the man she had been living with for the last few months. She sighed as she continued to walk along the halls of the mall. None of the shops seemed to catch her interested. After her little appearance at the bar where Sean had gone, and was told that she could be in trouble should they come after Sean, made her worry. She had decided to leave after a while. She needed to get away to breathe. A feeling of uneasiness was settling in her stomach. The same feeling she always got when she was upset. It was a defense mechanism that set in. Last time it happened was when she and Sean had separated after his last reign as champion. She looked at her watch. An hour had passed and she still had to use her credit cards. She just couldn’t decide on what she wanted today. She’d seen a couple outfits at Victoria Secrets that Sean would love to see her in, but she just wasn’t in the mood to shop for lingerie. She wanted something but she didnt know what it was. For some odd reason she would find it if she just kept looking. She ventured into one of the many jewelry stores. Her mind still dealing with the news that her life could be in danger should the Order decide to come out of her. Her mind not really focused on what she wanted, other then something about the store kept calling her. A friendly sales associate notices Patti out of the corner of her eye, and from the looks of things she was loaded. Price wouldn’t be to big of a deal to her. She watched Patti. Patti wasn’t looking over the diamond jewelry, she was focused the emerald pendents that were in the window. One in particular. Sales Associate: My name is Sorsha. May I be of assistance to you? The key to the window that Patti was looking in was dangling from her fingers. Patti: I want that necklace that is in there. The one that is in the back. The necklace in question was a dazzling sparkling sapphire and diamond pendent. The beauty and sparkle of it drove Patti to distraction. She had to have it, it was something that would keep the attention focused on her ample bosom on more then one occasion while she was standing at ringside. Sorsha: You do realize that is extremely expensive and a very rare design. It does not come cheap. Patti: Does it look like I care what the price is? Sorsha looked again at the young woman standing before her. She was about her height and full of fire. The look of determination in this woman’s eyes made Sorsha recoil a little bit. She wasn’t sure if the woman was serious or not. She went to the back of the display and unlocked it. She carefully removed the item from its display and showed it to Patti. Patti knew instantly that this is what had been drawing her to this particular end of the mall. Patti reached into her purse and pulled out her personal credit card. Patti: Charge it, wrap it up, and I’ll be back to pick it up. Sorsha looked at Patti with a look of surprise. Sorsha: Do you realize that this item costs more then what you make? Patti looked at the sales girl with an impatient look. She looked around the store for the manager. This girl needed a lesson in manners. She must not have been taught that the customer is always right, and you’d think she would want the commission off of it too. The manager saw that there was a problem and worked his way over to see what the problem was. He noticed his sales associate was trying to explain to the girl at the counter she couldn’t afford the item of choice. Manager: What seems to be the problem here? Patti stared at the elderly gentleman. His beady little eyes looking over Patti several times. He licked his lips in anticipation. This girl was either stupid or she had a sugar daddy that didnt care how much money she spent. Just as long as she was happy. Patti sighed and prepared to give them both a solid tongue lashing. Patti: The bloody problem here is that your girl here won’t sell me what I want, and she’s bloody well pissing me off with her refusal. Now if your not going to sell me this trinket, then I'm bloody going to go somewhere else and spend my bloody money. The manager looked at her again. A smirk coming to his lips. The girl had spunk he had to give her that. But there was something strangely familiar about her. He knew those hazel eyes from somewhere. The accent was a good clue too. Finally it dawned on him, she was that girl from the 4WW. She was in town for that show at the Orleans Casino this weekend. Manger: Oh I’m terribly sorry Ms. Anderson. I didn’t recognize you. It’s not ever day we have a star of your popularity in our store. You must forgive us. Sorsha, sell her the necklace. She can afford it. Sorsha blinked at her boss. Then stared at the redhead that was getting more furious by the minute. The commission off of this sale alone would pay for her to go on to college and not have to work for a while. She smiled then at her boss, then took Patti’s credit card. Sorsha took the credit card, and happily rung up the sale. At the store managers request, Patti was given free sets of jewelry for her to wear to the show on Sunday night, along with some basic jewelry to wear at anytime, along with several personal pieces for her and Sean to wear. Patti left with a smile on her face. She took her purchases and left the store. She decided a trip to Victoria Secrets was defiantly in the works now. She was going to need something to go with her brand new jewelry, and with the mood she was in at this very moment, Sean wasn’t going to see much of Vegas until the show Sunday night. She tucked the jewelry bags into her purse and headed on down the mall. Fade Out
