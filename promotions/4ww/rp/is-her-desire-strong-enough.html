Is Her Desire Strong Enough? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #154 Date: 09/26/03 Location: Home Desire - noun -1 : conscious impulse toward something that promises enjoyment or satisfaction in its attainment 2 a : 1. LONGING, CRAVING b : sexual urge or appetite 3 : a usually formal request or petition for some action ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti sat curled up on the couch, dressed as usual when she’s home, one of Sean’s shirts and a black lacy thong. Her arms and fingers covered in bandages. Some pregnancy test, she thought. She thought only one blood test was required not several. She stared down at her bruised arms and fingers, but obviously Annie had something to do with it. Of course, Annie would pay for this ordeal. But then again, Fey could have just been doing what the Arizona Athletic Commission was requesting. Another miscarriage by reason of wrestling could mean lawsuit by the injured part. And neither Ian nor Fey co8uld afford the damages from such a lawsuit. So Patti was unsure of what the truth was. All she knew there was a conspiracy among Fey and Annie. They desperately wanted Patti out of Sean’s life and they would do anything to get her out of Sean’s life. Sean walked into the living room. A butterfly bandage over his eye. The battle between him and West was extremely brutal. At least he had walked away with less. He noticed from the way Patti was sitting. She was still upset with being suspended and then her close friend psymon gets beat up in a match that she should have been involved in. It just didn’t make sense to him at all. Patti noticed Sean had come into the room. Two pieces of paper in his hands. From the looks of things, paperwork from the Four Winds offices. She couldnt tell from the looks of things whether it was good news or bad news. Patti: What did your loving sister send this time? Sean handed her the papers, and from the looks of things he wasn’t to happy. She took the papers, from him. The tears began to fall. She wasn’t pregnant. All their hard work had failed. Along with an updated contract with the pregnancy clause in it. Patti: Ti perissotero dynamai emeis symbaino tora? Patti layed the papers on the table, the desire to look at the paperwork was way past her. She walked away from the couch, and staright into the bedroom. She curled up on her side of the bed, knees to her chest. The comfort position. Her way of dealing with the pain of everything that has happened. She felt as if her entire world was slipping away. She was living her dream and slowly that dream was being wripped away from her. Patti just couldn't take anymore of it. She felt the desire to slip away from it all. Her love for Sean would always be there as it was those months ago. But his duty to be with Annie were causing more problems with her then she let on. Maybe she needed to go back home for a while, take a personal leave from this world and return to the same world where she came from. A chance to deal with everything, then come back fully strong. She proceeded to bury her head into her pillow and let the silent tears fall. Sean stepped into the bedroom. The sight of Patti curled up in a ball, her makeup streaking down her face, brought a pain to his chest. He wished she would talk to him more about what was troubling her. He had noticed that she hadnt been the same since Fey brought Annie in. He walked over to the bed and sat down by Patti's legs. His hands slowly rubbing up and down them. A gentle massage was all that was being offered. Patti raised her head and looked at Sean. The man was so good to her, he treated her like she was the one, but what about his duties? What about Annie? What about the Seelee court? What about the life they had talked about together? So many questions that needed to be answered. And right now, neither one of them could give the answers required. Patti looked at Sean through her tears. She saw the look of concern on his face and the pain that she knew he dealing with, and cried even harder. Her tears soaking the pillow she had her arms wrapped around. Patti forced herself to stop her tears. Her crying was only going to make her sick. She whiped the tears away and sat up, pulling her knees up under her chin. Her copper hair curling around her face. A small smile appeared on her face. Sean caught a look that caught him a little off guard, it was like a spark of an idea had come to life. The more Patti sat with that grin, the brighter her eyes got. An evil plot had come to mind within in a few moments. Patti: I have an idea that may work, love. It will throw a nice little suprise at both your sister and that bitch that won't let you go. Sean looked at Patti. He caught the glimmer of mischief in her eyes. Patti moved closer and whispered what she was thinking into his ear. Sean's eyes grew wide at what Patti was whispering in his ear. Sean: Ye sure that be what ye want? Patti: What do you think? Sean: If its whot ye want, then ye know I'll support ye. Fade out.
