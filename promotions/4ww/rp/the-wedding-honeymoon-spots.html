The Wedding: Honeymoon Spots

15 December 2004

The Wedding: Honeymoon Spots 4WW Exposures #1105 Date: 12/15/04 Location: Some tropical Location (The scene opens up with a view of an ocean. The sun is setting, and the horizon has entered that blueish reddish orangish shade that it enters at this time of night. The camera pans out from the ocean. There are several rocks, jagged, flat, all shapes. On one rock in particular is the shape of the blonde bombshell Luscious Lady Luck. She is wearing an aqua bikini with a sarong wrapped around her waist. She is staring off into the distance.) LLL: Soon, oh so soon it will happen. (LLL flips her hair back.) LLL: Many things will soon happen. Many things that will be more than what everyone expects. And soon, this shall all be reality. (LLL let’s the sarong fall down from her waist. She sits on the flat rock and puts her feet in.) LLL: Yeah, there is a lot going on this week. A lot and more. unStable, we’ve been in a little bit of a funk lately. The two brightest spots was Zane acquiring the West Wind Championship and Ike joining us and becoming part of what will soon become the most dominant stable in 4WW…. Of course, we have had our downs. After all, I did lose my title, through no fault of my own of course. But I was the longest running Tempest Champion ever and there is no one who will beat that record…. And we did lose the get out alive match technically, albeit a match that seemed to be more a feud between DMC and KB. unStable, well, we were like the innocent by standards who get caught in the middle of a gang war. Of course, then again, since this is a gang war, you might as well let the two big stable beat the living hell out of each other, And then come in and pick up the pieces…. But let me ask you all. What do you think of this spot? It’s a beautiful little spot. A wonderful view, a private condo. Everything a pair of newlyweds would want. And in just a few short days that will be me. Me and my new husband. It will have such a beautiful ring to it….. Sure there are other things I’ll have to attend to. But nothing can spoil this night. Nothing at all. This night is going to be perfect….. Of course I have Caitlin. A match that I don’t even know what to expect. The fans are deciding this one. Will it be inside of the Iron Maiden, or will it be an Atlantic City Street Fight? The fans are deciding that… Either way, it won’t matter. I’m going to do what I always do. Walk into the match the underdog, and walk out of the match with another victory. But this time, the victory will be that much sweeter. The victory will be that much better…. This will be after all my second title that I have ever held. The second major title that I have ever held in my career. The Savior of the 4WW is making his way up the ranks. Slowly but surely, and I will do everything that I said that I can do…. But not only that, but I can prove that I belong with them. I belong as part of the Anderson family. What better way to prove you belong, than by actually showing that you are just as good if not better then they. And at MSVI, I will do that…. But the South Wind Title, that’s not all I have. I have the Four Winds Championship Tournament as well. What a tournament that is going to be. And look who I have in the first round. Very well the future of this company. No doubt this is the future of this company. “Shooter” Josh Cameron, don’t know much about you. You seem to be a shoot wrestler. Well duh, your name would imply that. The thing is it would seem that shoot wrestlers don’t have the success one would think they would, but you do undoubtedly have a bright future…. And then there is my friend and stablemate Ike Angel. No doubt, with myself and Zane Ozz, the soon to be most dominant force in the 4WW. You all know it is going to happen. But there is no reason to be hasty about it. Someday my friend, I can just see it now. Me and you are going to be highlighting one of theses PPV’s. Yes, someday the Marquee will read “IKE ANGEL vs. LUSCIOUS LADY LUCK for the NORTH WINDS CHAMPIONSHIP” A day that I can not wait for…. And then we come to another odd one. Luna Storm. It would seem that you are trying to get something. It would seem that you would be after something. Well, hon, I can offer you lots of some things. Just let me know. I have what you need, it’s just a matter of whether you or willing to come for it…. (The Lucky One stands back up, showing off the thong bikini.) And then after ward, I and my husband will come back here; sit on these rocks under the hot sun. Naked, tanning, and having lots of other fun. It’ll be a honeymoon to remember. Oh, and Patti, don’t worry, I’ll send you the tape. (LLL dives into the water, after a few seconds, she emerges, just leaning against the rock.) Maelstorm VI. My second PPV. And the second time that I will show that I can answer the call and will answer the call. Big game player, your right, I am a big game player; I’m a small game player. I’ll play whatever game you want to play and I’ll win. After all, someone needs to win, and these games would be nothing….Without me. (Camera fades to black where there is a giant purple heart on the screen with the letters “LLL + PA” inside the heart.)
