[IC] Fan's Questions for the Most Dominant 4WW Female

1 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1 March 2006 I know you all want to know what makes me tick, so ask me. I'd love to answer the questions of the fans. And I know that all of my fans are wanting to hear from the most beautiful and most dominant female of Four Winds Wrestling, it's savior Luscious Lady Luck. ---- 1 March 2006 Hrmm. Okay, here's one. Did you get any air time during all that girl/girl action that went on? ---- 1 March 2006 Me, action? I always get all the action I want. Whether its girl/girl or boy/girl. You know that I'm going to be in the middle of the action or somehow involved. In fact, I think you would be most interested in some of my most famous girl/girl action starring myself and Patti Anderson-McClancy. You should check out our videos, Double Your Pleasures and Re-Double Your Pleasures. I think you'd enjoy them. ---- 10 April 2006 Hello, this is Sid Torvald from the PTC Global News. You call yourself the most dominant female in 4WW? Have you forgotten about Patti Anderson? Before you came along, Patti Anderson dominated everyone in the business. What are your major accomplishments in the 4WW? Have you won the South, East and North Wind titles? Did you win the North Wind your second year in the business? On top of that, what makes you think you're so dominant? Everyone knows that Patti Anderson would make you her bitch any day of the week. Thank you for your time. ---- 18 April 2006 To measure the measueres of accomplishment by titles alone would be like trying to measure the accomplishments of a intelligence by their monetary status. It isn't a reliable measuring stick of dominance. As far as Patti Anderson is concerned however, she is just a footnote. As I'm sure your well aware in all of your reporting duties and research, you would realize that I, Luscious Lady Luck was the one who made Patti Anderson. If it wasn't for me, Patti Anderson would still be serving as a bar wench in Australlia trying to suck up and make ends meat. I made her the woman she is today, and I made her realize the star potential that she has. If it wasn't for me, she wouldn't have all those titles to which you seem is so important. Also, on a side note, I am unbeaten against Patti Anderson. For those of you out there who don't understand that, it means that Patti Anderson, whom you are trying to say is more dominant than me, has never beaten me. In fact, everytime we have faced, I have beaten "The Darling of the 4WW." For that matter, I have beaten every member of the Anderson family, whom many consider the best family in 4WW What else is there when considering dominance.
