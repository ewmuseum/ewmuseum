Nights of Darkness... Night 1 Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #942 Date: 9-9-04 Location: Somewhere outside Tokyo (ooc: This is a 3 part series... you should only read if you intend on reading the next two. All togethar they make sense as you must read everything. You don't want to miss details... part two should be up in the early AM of tonight) My friend, Things are a little rocky around here at the moment, but they are still going. Rachael has made things a little… interesting. She keeps us on our toes. She seems to really be getting along with Angel though. I think she almost looks up to her. At least she has someone like that. There is something that worries me though. When Angel isn’t around, she’s very… I don’t really know how to put it. She likes to show her appreciation I guess. The other day at the hotel, Angel had volunteered to get breakfast for all of us. I was watching TV and Rachael was in the shower. She came out and seemed… to forget her towel apparently. She just walked out, naked. She changed right in front of me like it was no big deal. I acted like I didn’t notice. Sometimes the way she acts when we’re alone worries me. She’s almost too nice to Angel it seems. All is well though, she did suggest a good idea. Tomorrow we are going on a picnic. It’s a nice little park, very quiet. It’s near a forest with what seems to be great trails for hiking and walking. Maybe it’s just the right thing to get my mind off of Maelstrom. I know I must face Blaise McClancy. It’s been the only thing on my mind. As long as I can shake it off for just one day I will be fine. On a side note, I received a strange message today. It basically said to stay away from the jade statue. I don’t really understand what it was or who it was from… it’s probably nothing. Your Friend, Kanton 9-9-04 ~ Journal Entry +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ Kanton woke up that next day feeling rested and refreshed. He had slept in though. This was odd though as Rachael, Angel, and he had been forced to share one king size bed due to rooming restraints and all rooms being booked. They had barely gotten a room as it was. Angel and Rachael were both almost finished getting ready, but Kanton figured he still had time. They were women and although he loved his Angel dearly, she was still female and as the staple of female culture… she took forever to get dressed and ready. The same seemed to be true for Rachael also. Kanton looked across the room and there was a picnic basket on the table with a blanket. Rachael and Angel must have already left and come back once. “So what did you get us?” Kanton asked as he eyed the large basket of food. “We didn’t get it. It was on the door with a note from the hotel. Compliments for our staying in this room with only ONE bed.” Angel chuckled as she finished speaking. She took the whole situation in stride with humor as it seemed. It had been an interesting fit, but they all got through the night. “Sleep well?” Rachael asked softly. “Actually… yeah! I’m surprised you guys didn’t wake me up.” Kanton scratched his head as he sat up in the bed. He slipped the covers off as he swung his legs over the side of the bed. He scratched at his T-shirt as he wasn’t used to wearing one to bed. He wasn’t sure he was all that comfortable sharing a bed with Rachael but he would just get through it. He looked over at Angel as she was brushing her hair. She seemed to almost glow to him. She was the personification of perfection to him. He loved her with all his heart. He looked her up and down and smiled. She was dressed well as she was wearing a white halter top with khaki shorts. It was then that he noticed Rachael was basically wearing the same thing. She was wearing a black halter top with black shorts though. It was just different enough he guessed. Rachael and Angel both continued to get ready in front of the mirror which hung above the TV across the room from the bed. He made a straight b-line for the bathroom with his clothes in hand. It was simple enough, his suitcase was basically right next to the bed. Time passed on, Kanton got ready and was finished with all by the time Rachael and Angel were finished. He didn’t take long. He had a sort of routine and stuck to it. It had always worked for him. They grabbed the wallets, purses, basket, blanket, and other belongings. Kanton had decided to dress casually like Angel and Rachael had. He had just thrown on a grey Old Navy T-shirt with a pair of jean shorts. It was enough for him. They took the elevator down to the lobby and passed into the car park. They would be at the park shortly. As Kanton drove to the park an ominous feeling passed over Kanton. He didn’t know why or what it was, but he just couldn’t shake the feeling. Something seemed wrong, but he just couldn’t place his finger on it. That note he had received yesterday still seemed to bother him also. It was so strange and vague yet so direct at the same time. What jade statue though? It had never said. He had so many things on his mind at the moment. He was being pulled in every direction. Also, at all times it seemed as if Blaise McClancy was on his mind. He had been on a winning streak again it seemed, but his loss at the hands of McClancy still weighed heavily on him. Maybe this was just what he needed after all. They arrived at the park after a while. It was semi secluded and sat right in front of what seemed to be a large and dense forest. There were a few paths cut in that could be seen, but nothing could be seen much farther in. They stepped out of the car and a strong cold breeze blew in. It sent shivers up Kris’s spine. Something was troubling him still… though not McClancy. This was something else. He could feel it deep into his bones. They picked a spot close to the trees. It was semi shaded and pretty comfortable. There they sat and had their lunch. They talked on and on about this and that. Nothing really important though. Time passed on though and they had finished. They gathered everything up and took it back to the car. It was then that Rachael had an idea. “Let’s go for a walk!” she had exclaimed. Angel quickly nodded and looked at Kris for approval. He thought it over a bit as he looked at the forest. It seemed innocent enough to him. That feeling came back to him though as he looked on at the forest, that uneasy feeling in the bottom of his stomach. He watched as the trees swayed from side to side. There seemed to be no wind though. Kanton took no notice of this though and just looked past it. The forest seemed to be calling him almost. It felt like he was supposed to go… like he was meant to enter. “I don’t see why not… it would be nice to get a walk in.” Kanton answered back to Angel and Rachael. They both jumped and giggled like little school girls. Angel started off quickly on one of the trails. Kanton had still been placing the basket in the car when he noticed that Rachael was just standing there, staring at him. Kanton stood up and looked at Rachael. She was stone faced, but had a bit of a grin on her. She looked on at Kanton with cold dead eyes. “Are you coming?” She asked as Kanton’s stomach flopped. He nodded and Rachael’s expression quickly changed as she ran off after Angel. What had just happened? Did he really see Rachael as he thought he did? Was it just in his head? Of course it was… it had to be. Kanton shook off his uneasy feeling and ran towards the edge of the forest where Angel and Rachael were waiting. Angel stepped in a bit and looked around. “It’s kind of creepy now that I look at it,” she said, but Rachael quickly shot back. “I think it looks fun and inviting! Let’s go in!” Rachael rushed forward and Angel and Kris stood at the edge looking in. It seemed as if Angel had the same feeling as Kanton now. Rachael noticed and came back grabbing both Kanton and Angel by the hand. “C’mon! Let’s go!” Rachael had said as she pulled them in. So they followed the trail for what seemed to be no more than twenty minutes or so. It was then that Kanton began to notice something… it was getting darker. Was the sun setting already? No way… it couldn’t be any later than two o’clock. They walked on a bit more before Kanton stoped and looked around. “Haven’t we passed this spot already?” “Yeah… I think so!” Angel replied to Kanton. “Where’s the exit? Shouldn’t we have come back to it yet?” “It’s probably just a bit further. Don’t worry,” a weird look passed over the face of Rachael. She almost seemed irritated. So they continued on a bit farther, but it continued to get darker. Angel even began to worry because they didn’t have any flashlights. It was then that Rachael noticed what seemed to be fire up ahead, maybe a campfire. “I see something. That’s probably the exit right there,” Rachael had remarked assuringly. They sped up their pace and continued to where they saw the light. They stopped at the edge of the shadows though as something caught them by surprise. The light wasn’t a campfire, it was torches, they surround a center area in a circle. In this circle stood about ten men as it seemed all clothed in black from head to toe. They were veiled priests. They chanted as they seemed to walk around a large statue of sorts. It could not be seemed though as a large black cloth was draped over it. “Aaawwwwweeeyyyyyyyyoooooooooooaaaaaayyyyyeeeeeeeeeeaaaaaaaaaaadooooooo,” the seemed to chant over and over. Angel and Rachael both stood behind Kris as he looked in at the men. Apparently they had not seen the three as there had been no reaction at all to their being there. The men then lined in front and behind the large object and reaches to the ground. They then all at once lifted poles which rested under the large statue. Kanton looked on and marveled as ten men as old looking as they seemed, were able to lift such a large and seemingly heavy object. The men then took the statue down hill on a winding path. Kanton, Angel, Rachael all waited for a minute until Kris thought that the coast would be cleared. They stepped out into the light and across the circle to what was a cliff. They kneeled at the cliff as to not bring attention to themselves. They looked down and saw the priests were carrying the statue in what seemed to be a small run down rural town. Something about it seemed ancient though. There was nobody outside and Kanton had notice that it was completely dark. Night had fallen. He did not understand this though as it could not have been any later than five o’clock. The small town contained a central town square of sorts, but it was completely empty. There were small houses here and they but, there were three main large buildings. One was close to them and there was a steep path down the cliff that led to an alley of sorts for the house. Ahead of that to the top left of the main area was another large house, and at the far end of the village was a house as big as the first to combined. It was blocked off though by high stone walls and a large wooden gate. Numerous stone pillars about five feet tall dotted the streets of the town, they were adorned with fire pits though as they almost worked like street lights. “What are we going to do?” Angel had whispered the question into Kanton’s ear. He paused a moment as he continued to watch the priests begin to walk onto a large street. He watched them more closely now. They seemed to be heading for the “town square”. Angel nudged him again, but it was to no avail. Kanton watched as the priests set the statue down in the middle of the square. “C’mon, we need to get closer,” Kanton said to Angel and Rachael. “No way!” Rachael exclaimed but Angel quickly put her hand over Rachael’s mouth. “Be quiet!” Angel whispered intensely to Rachael. She nodded as Angel removed her hand. “Maybe we can find someone that will help us find our way back. We need to go,” Kanton motioned for Angel and Rachael to follow as they headed down the steep path that lead to beside the first of the big houses. Kanton had lost sight of the happening of the square, but he would soon be close. They walked carefully down. Kris had helped Angel and Rachael in a few places, but they seemed to be able to handle it. They walked forward along side the house between the house and what was a large wooden wall. They came upon the street and there was no light where they were standing. Kanton could look ahead though. The priests were gone, but the statue was still there. Kanton crept out into the square and up to the statue. He looked it over and then looked around, but no one was to be seen. He motioned for Angel and Rachael and they quickly ran to him. All three surveyed the surrounding area, but nothing could be seen. There was only the statue, covered. Kanton ran his hands across the cover, fine black silk. He couldn’t help wonder what was under it. “What is it?” Rachael had asked. “I don’t know…” Kanton responded as he looked on. Rachael couldn’t wait though. She reached up and grabbed the silk. She pulled and it came flying off. It was a statue alright, it was of a crying maiden. She was bound with ropes and on her knees as she seemed to be crying. It was made out of jade. Kanton looked on as he it all clicked in his head. “Oh shit…” Kanton realized this had something to do with that note. A wave of fright washed over Kanton. They all marveled at the bright green jade. It was an amazing work. It was unlike anything Kanton had ever seemed. It was almost lifelike. Kanton and Angel backed off a bit, but Rachael was unmoved. She was almost in a trance. She slowly reached out and placed her hand on the statue. Her head dipped, but she was breathing heavily. She slowly removed her hand and Angel stepped forward. Angel softly placed her hand on Rachael’s shoulder trying to figure out what was going on. Rachael spun around and grabbed Angel’s hand though. Angel screamed loudly. Angel fell to the floor then. She didn’t move. Kanton looked at Rachael… and he couldn’t move. An evil smile slid across her face. Her eyes… her eyes, they were green. They were as green as the jade of the statue. They glowed and almost pulsated. Kanton was too frightened to move. What was going on? Rachael walked forward slowly. She came within inches of Kanton. It was then he blacked out. And so it would begin… a series of nights and events that Kanton would never forget so long as he lived. There began the first night. +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ NIGHT ONE +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ And Kanton woke up, but where was he? He had no clue. He sat up on what seemed to be a sort of bed mat on the ground. He had even had a blanket layed on him. What had happened? His head was swimming and he was in a daze. Angel and Rachael were nowhere to be seen. Rachael’s eyes… they were so strange… so… evil. Angel had fallen. What had happened to the two of them? Kanton looked around the room. It seemed fairly dilapidated, but still used. A lantern was lit in one corner lighting the whole of the room. Kanton looked to his right and there he saw it. It wasn’t very noticeable at first, but the glimmer caught his eye. Kanton stood up slowly and walked over what seemed to be a family shrine of sorts. There rested a book, on top of it was the silver cross necklace that Kanton had bought recently for Angel. It rested softly atop the book almost calling for him. He took the necklace up and looked at it for a minunete he kissed the cross softly and then he blanked out. He began to see something else. All was in black and white. He was running. Ahead of him he could see Angel. She was running down a hallway, but before she reached the end a figure stepped around the corner. She had green eyes. Angel screamed as she turned into a door to her right. It was then that Kanton flashed back into reality. Kanton stood there for a moment trying to register what he had just seen. Kanton looked down again and took more notice of the book. It was what seemed to be a simple black leather personal journal. Red letters stained the front cover. They were in Japanese, but Kanton could read it. It said, “The Trial of Tears”. We worked the buckle off of the journal and began to read. The writing too was in Japanese. He thanked all the years he had stayed at his uncle’s in Nagamura. The Japanese lessons were really paying off now. Kanton flipped through a few pages before he stopped on one entry. ---Entry 74--- And so it was that on the third night the temptress was bound. She cried tears of blood as she knew her fate was sealed. We cast her into the pit. May the dark gods have their way with her. --Entry 78— Why? Why have the gods deemed us worthy of such punishment? What have we done? The Jade woman has returned and now I fear what may come. I fear my own death, but I know now that I am deserving. We did wrong. Mercy shall not be dealt to us for our sins. ------ Kanton looked at the book, a few pages had been torn out. ---Entry 97--- I no longer fear death. I welcome it. I have suffered as no other should. My dreams have become my reality. My nightmares have become my fate. The Jade woman comes for me soon. I know it. I am the last one left now. Fate is cruel it seems. ---Entry 98--- I hear her cries at night. Why will she not come for me? END THE MADNESS! I fear I must take my own life soon. I cannot take much more. ---Entry 99--- The screams… the screams… I cried tears of blood tonight. ---Entry 100--- She has come at last. The glow of her eyes brings me mercy. Finally I may take my eternal rest. ---Entry101--- NO! Please help… beware the Jade woman. She is the temptress. She brings no mercy for she was given none. ---Entry 102--- The walls are bleeding! I have lost all grip of reality. The glow… it calls for me. My rest has come at last. ---------- Kanton continued to flip the pages trying to make sense of what he had just read. The entries had stopped, there were only blank pages. Kanton set the book down and backed away a bit trying to take everything in. The pages had just ended without any notice. Kanton thought… jade woman… could that be the statue he saw? And if so… what was Rachael? Kanton shook his head and looked around. Was it just him or was the room getting colder. Kanton saw an open screen window. He walked over and looked down. It was Angel! He saw her! The window overlooked what seemed to be the main room of the house. It was also obvious he was on the second floor. Angel was crouched in a corner, hidden behind a few folding screens. “ANGEL!” Kanton yelled out, but it garnered no response. He turned around and saw a door on the other side of the room. He rushed over to the door. He had to get to Angel. He would then find Rachael, fuck finding out what’s going on. He just wanted out. Kanton slid the door out of the way. **FLASH** Kanton looked forward in utter horror. He was unable to move. **FLASH** Kanton looked ahead again and there was nothing there. He couldn’t believe what he had seen. It was the Jade woman… bound in pain. She was not jade though… she was almost like the negative for a shadow. She stood there, looking down the hallway in front of him, but not really noticing him. She had a cloth over her eyes, but it was soaked in black… black tears flew down her face. It was obviously blood. Kanton shook his head trying to get the image out, but in his mind he could see in plain as day. She was pointing down the hallway, but to what? Kanton stepped through the doorway, there was a doorway at the end of the hall that led to some stairs. “AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!” Kanton heard the scream, he rushed back into the room and looked down through the window again. Angel was running, but from what? She had left her hiding spot and down a hallway across the room. Kanton hurried out of the room, down the hallway, and down the stairs. He went to enter the main first floor room, but couldn’t. He couldn’t walk through the doorway. He pounded on the open air and his fists stop dead center of the doorway, almost as if there was an invisible wall. It was then that Rachael turned into the doorway from the right. Her eyes were glowing green and there was an evil smile on her face. **FLASH** There above Rachael Kanton saw her again. **FLASH** She was floating in the air behind Rachael… looking the same as before, but this time her face mimicked Rachael’s. **FLASH** Kanton saw her floating once more as she shook her head no. Rachael was doing the same, but also waving her finger back and forth. **FLASH** Rachael waved softly at Kanton as she turned around and began to walk towards the hallway in which Angel had ran down. “YOU BITCH! STAY AWAY FROM HER! I’LL FUCKING KILL YOU! I’LL FUCKING KILL YOU!” Kanton had yelled, but it garnished no response. What was Rachael doing? Was she being controlled by the bound woman? The Jade woman… What had happened that had made the bound woman so evil… so powerful? How had this happened to Kanton? Who did this? Who was responsible? Kanton then heard voices behind him whispering. “Yes Kris… we are here… to help you,” the voices had said, but when Kanton turned around… all he saw was darkness. The voices were coming from a pitch black dark area of the room. Kanton couldn’t see them, but he knew they were very familiar. Could they be… had the essences of the Four Winds returned to guide him once again? “We… are here… to help… you,” the voices had answered, “But you have much… to learn still. This spirit is the incarnation of your fears… and the fears of those who oppose you. There are those who would see fit to have you… not succeed. You must… not let them stop… you. You must destroy… this spirit… your fears… your opponent’s power. Only you can do this.” “Why is this happening to me? Why is this happening now?” Kris had asked still trying to make sense of things. “You are one of the chosen,” the spirits spoke softly, “All those who are chose must be tested. This is the first of many tests. Now go, save your love. Lay this evil spirit to it’s final rest.” And with that a strong warm breeze blew through Kanton and through the doorway. Kanton turned around… and walked through that same doorway. It was no longer blocked it seemed. Kanton saw the direction Angel and Rachael had gone. He ran through the main room, ignoring the side hallways. He ran straight for the hallway they had went through. He stopped at the entry way to the hallway… it was dark… and cold. Kanton entered slowly trying to make no noise. He continued down the hallway for a bit until he came to another door. He listened and heard footsteps. With a deep breath he opened the door. It was then that Kanton saw something he did not expect. Angel was bound in ropes, with a gag in her mouth, and blindfold tied around her head. She was latched onto a hook which hung from a chain in the center of the room. She was dangling in mid air. There in front of her stood Rachael. There was blood on the ground, and both Angel’s and Rachael’s clothes were somewhat torn. Rachael was holding a large ceremonial sacrificial knife in her hand. Kanton knew she wanted to use it. The room itself almost looked like a slaughter room. The walls and floor were bloodstained. Kanton noticed something… there was a hole in the ground underneath Angel. Another thing… she was silent. And she didn’t move… Kanton had to react. He wasn’t going to just stand there. He lunged forward, but Rachael just moved out of the way. Kanton fell into the hole, and entered darkness once again. Angel was hanging at Rachael’s mercy, and he was unable to stop it. With that the beginning of his trial had ended… the first night was over. Kanton would soon awake again, but where? What happens to Angel? What about the spirit that controls Rachael? How was he going to stop it all? Only time would tell. To Be Continued…
