A Womans Scorn

15 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Womans Scorn Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #870 Date: 8/15/04 Location: Outside (It’s the middle of a beautiful day and Triple L is sitting outside with a target and a bunch of balls. She is whipping the balls at the target as hard as she can, connecting with everyone with a large pop. She is wearing sweat pants and a sweat shirt. She looks absolutely pissed, and her actions are showing it too. This isn’t a PMS pissed though. This is a pissed where somebody did something they shouldn’t have.) LLL: GOD DAMNIT!!!! (POP!!! POP!!!!) WHO THE HELL WOULD TAKE MY DAMNED BEAR? WHO THE HELL WOULD DO SOMETHING LIKE THAT? (POP!!! POP!!!) AND WHAT THE HELL IS KYO DOING TAKING MY DAMNED DVD’S? FOR THAT MATTER HOW THE HELL DID HE GET INTO MY FREAKING HOUSE? (POP!!! POP!!!) I CAN’T FUCKING BELIEVE THIS IS HAPPENING!!!! I CAN’T FUCKING BELIEVE THAT THERE ARE ALL OF THESE THEIVES RUNNING AROUND IN THIS DAMN FEDERATION!!! AND I CAN’T BELIEVE THAT NO ONE IS WILLING TO DO ANYTHING ABOUT IT!!! NOT THE 4WW OFFICIALS!!! NOT THE GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS!!! I CAN’T BELIEVE THIS IS HAPPENING!!! (POP!!! POP!!! POP!!! POP!!!) That bear was all I had left. That bear was the last thing that I had from Patti. And now it’s gone. Gone. (LLL breaks down into tears.) Everything I ever wanted in this world. (Sniff) Everything I ever wanted to have. (Sniff) Everything I needed was represented by that bear. And now, it is all gone. (Sniff, sniff) Stolen, by some lowlife, who wouldn’t understand what love is if it bit him in the ass…… I just don’t understand what these people have against love. I just don’t understand what these people have against two women expressing their true desire for each other. I just don’t understand why people are trying to hold it all back. And in the mean time taking everything that I own that means anything to me…… (The Lucky One wipes her eyes and gives one last sniff before looking into the camera.) The DVD’s, those don’t bother me so much. I mean, yeah, they bother me, but I have tons of those. I have tons of the DVD’s. See even though my darling tried to have all the copy’s destroyed; there is no way a judge would force me to give over my stash and what I own in DVDs. They are hard to come by, but it doesn’t matter. I have plenty. All he would’ve had to do was ask and I could’ve gotten him a copy……. But the bear. That bear meant everything to me. It was all I had left from what I had with Patti. Sure, I have all of her other stuff she has ever put out, but that bear was given to me by Patti. That bear was given to me out last night together before I left to go back to America. That bear was the last thing she gave me and the last memento from our relationship….. (Triple L gets a determined look on her face.) And if the officials aren’t going to help me, I’ll have to take matters into my own hands. I’ll have to go and find who took it myself. And I’ll get my own justice. I’ll punish those who had taken it. I’ll make sure they pay for what they’ve done to me…… Kyle Silverman, it’s not a good day to be you. It’s not a good week to be you. You are dealing with someone who’s anger and determination are driving her to something that you will never understand. You would do yourself good to just get out of the Saviors way…… Hell hath no furry as a woman’s scorn is the old saying. Well, this woman’s scorn is going to be coming at everyone full force until I find out who took my damned teddy bear. It doesn’t matter who you are. It doesn’t matter where you work. People are going to pay. People are going to get hurt. And people are going to feel my wrath until I find out who took my damned teddy bear….. You thought that I was on a mission before, you haven’t seen anything yet. You haven’t experienced anything yet. I will have what is rightfully mine. And I will take it out of people until I do get it. Do not stand in my way. Do not think about lying to me. People are going to pay. And you are truly going to wish you were nothing with me around. (The Lucky One yanks down the target and throws it at the camera.)
