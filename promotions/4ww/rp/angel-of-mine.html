Angel of Mine

3 December 2004

Angel of Mine Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #1085 Date: 12/3/04 Location: Santa Barbara, Los Angelis [The 4WW logo blows across the screen as the sound of Monica singing Angel of Mine plays. Our current South Wind Champion sits in front of her white porcelain vanity mirror set in just a simple white summer dress, with the straps hanging off her shoulders coming her long red hair with a soft smile on her face. She was obviously in a very good mood considering losing Elimination Chamber match for the West Wind title.] When I first saw you I already knew There was something inside of you Something I thought that I would never find Angel of mine I look at you, lookin' at me Now I know why they say the best things are free I'm gonna love you boy you are so fine Angel of Mine [Who would have thought the new champ would walk out not pinning anyone. A really messed up outcome to a very important title match if one looked at it, but Caitlin wasn’t thinking about that as she slowly combed her hair, while admiring herself a bit in the mirror…if one were too look at it, they’d say she was in love…but Caitlin would probably say she was in a state of like…and the person she liked…the person that was on her mind…was Kyo Kushinaga.] How you changed my world you'll never know I'm different now, you helped me grow You came into my life sent from above When I lost all hope you showed me love I'm checkin' for ya boy you're right on time Angel of Mine Nothing means more to me than what we share No one in this whole world can ever compare Last night the way you moved is still on my mind Angel of Mine [It had been awhile since Caitlin was interested anyone, anyone smaller than her to say the least. Not that Kyo was a runt or anything; the current North Wind Champion was a respectable 6 foot 5, but Fairchild was use to liking guys over the 7-foot marker…mainly because she felt more like a real girl with guys bigger than her…she could be the “little woman” for a change. But with Kyo it was different…he was nice…and exotic in a mysterious way, not to mention he was drop dead cute...a little weird…but still cute. As Caitlin sighed as she looked at herself in the mirror; she wondered if he felt the same way about her, she felt like she was in high school all over again. All she needed was the few scraps of papers used for note passing that said “Do you think he likes me?”, “I like him too”, or “Yes, I like you a lot.”] What you mean to me you'll never know Deep inside I need to show You came into my life sent from above (Sent from above) When I lost all hope, you showed me love (Boy you showed me love) I'm checkin' for ya, boy you're right on time (Right on Time) Angel of Mine (Angel of mine) [As she evened out the last strands of hair with her fingers, Caitlin’s ears propped up and the hairs stood up at the back of her neck as she could feel someone entering her room creeping up behind her.] Caitlin Fairchild – Who’s there? Rox… [Before she could finish her sentence, a pair of long slender arms wrapped around her neck forming a sleeper chokehold. Caitlin was shocked for a second, as the arms began to tighten trying to strangle her…mind you…only for a second.] Free Fall – Go to sleep bitch…go to sleep… Caitlin Fairchild (Slightly struggling to breath) – Roxy…what the hell…are you…doing? [Roxanna Spaulding a.k.a Free Fall, Caitlin’s best friend/manager/client (wrestler in training) decked out in a white sports bra, with matching Lycra Nike 1 shorts, and blue Nike sneakers had Caitlin locked in a sleeper hold trying to choke her out…Caitlin however was not amused.] I never knew I could feel each moment As if it were new, Every breath that I take, the love that we make I only share it with you (you, you, you,you) When I first saw you I already knew There was something inside of you Something I thought that I would never find Angel of Mine Free Fall – Trying out my patented Sleeper Hold, you about to take an early nap. Caitlin Fairchild (Now irritated.) – Roxy…you’re messin up my hair…I just combed it…let go. Free Fall – Uh-uh! This is locked like a pitbull; the only way out is to tap or go to sleep. Caitlin Fairchild (Now really irritated.) – Roxanna…let go…you’re messing up my hair… Free Fall – I guess the loss of oxygen to your brain is makin you deaf…I said…no. You came into my life sent from above (Came into my life, yeah yeah yeah) When I lost all hope you showed me love (Boy You showed me love, uh huh) I'm checkin' for ya, boy you're right on time (But boy your right on time) Angel of Mine (Angel of mine, oh mine) [Caitlin sighed as she slowly put down her comb on the dresser, the made a low growl as she raised her longer and stronger arms behind her grabbing Roxy by her neck!] Free Fall (gagging) – Hey! No fair! [Caitlin got up standing to her full height lifting Roxy into the air, then squeezed harder choking her while pulling her upward…it took like a minute, but Free Fall finally released her choke hold as Caitlin hoisted her with ease another good foot up.] Free Fall – Okay…okay…I give…I give… [Caitlin walks calmly over to her bed, and with one motion judo flips Roxy over her driving her into the bed hard! Roxanna bounces twice stunned almost coming off the bed.] How you changed my world you'll never know I'm different now, you helped me grow I look at you lookin' at me Now I know why they say the best things are free I'm checkin' for ya, boy you're right on time Angel of Mine [Caitlin turns to check her hair in the mirror trying to fix it.] Caitlin Fairchild – See what your bloody rough housing did? You messed up my hair! I was coming it to get it just right. Free Fall (Turning over onto her stomach) – Since when do you SIT combing your hair for hours? And since when do you use the word “Bloody”? [Caitlin rolled her eyes as she softened her hair up again, apparently the longer Caitlin hung around her older sister the more she began to pick up her “bad habits.] Caitlin Fairchild – Since when do you listen to every word that I say? And did you finish you run? Free Fall – (Smiles.) Why else do you think I’m dressed like a retard from the Eights, and now soaking your bed with my perspiration? [Caitlin smiles sarcastically as she walks up to Roxy smiling back at her kicking her legs into the air. In one motion she grabs the sheets, and yanks them pulling Roxy off her bed sending her crashing hard to the floor!] Free Fall – OW! SHIT! Caitlin Fairchild – Nasty bitch… Free Fall – HEY! Since when do you use the B word? Caitlin Fairchild (Tosses the sheets on her.) - Since I’m NOT a little girl anymore… Free Fall – That’s obvious…what the FUCK is up with you? [Roxy narrows her eyes as she looks Caitlin up and down narrowing her eyes in a second they light up.] Free Fall – OOOOOOOOOOOOOH! You’re in looooooooooooove! Caitlin Fairchild – What? NO!! Free Fall – Who is it? I bet it’s that Lucifer dude! You and him were rolling around in the elimination chamber getting all sweaty! He’s obviously your size…so you can get your freak on! Oh course you’re gonna have to reinforce this bed… Caitlin Fairchild (Turns with her eyes wide in shock) – Okay…it’s time for YOU to leave! Free Fall – OOOOOOOOOOH! I know!! Kyo Kushinaga! [Caitlin was about to say something…but became stuck.] Caitlin Fairchild – I’m…not…in love…with him. Free Fall – But you DO like him right? Caitlin Fairchild (Smiles.) – Maybe…a little… Free Fall – Oooooooooooooooh! Did you TELL him? Caitlin Fairchild – No… Free Fall – Why not? Caitlin Fairchild – BECAUSE…I got other things to think about then! Like defending my South Wind Championship against Apocalypse this week! [Roxy sits up in an Indian position with an “Are you stupid” look on her face.] Caitlin Fairchild (Looking down at the floor.) – And…I’m afraid he might not…like…me…back. Free Fall - What? Are you retarded? What dude do YOU know buys out a bar, and then takes you to dinner? Caitlin Fairchild – I know…but still…he’s like sooooooo cute, with those dreamy eyes, and dark raven hair…and he’s like so mysterious…he’s…he’s… Free Fall - Perfect for you? Caitlin Fairchild – Might not be into me like that… Free Fall (Gets irritated.) – Shit Kat, you always do this shit…it ain’t like you never had a boyfriend before… Caitlin Fairchild – I’m NOT starting anything…I just question guys motives sometimes… Free Fall - You need to take that ring confident, and focus that into your love life. Caitlin Fairchild – Yeah…well I need to stop with this fairy tale…and get focused on the ring…I’m going against Apoc. Free Fall - And? Caitlin Fairchild – Roxy…you’ve seen her record…she’s got the LONGEST winning streak I’ve ever seen for a female wrestler…even I wasn’t that good. Free Fall - AND? Don’t think the bitch didn’t have help! Not to mention I bet half the guys she went up against were getting boners while wrestling the scrawny bitch, no wonder she was kickin dey asses…plus she’s never went up against an opponent like you before…you’re gonna mop the floor with her yo. Caitlin Fairchild (Sighs.) – Yeah…but I’m not gonna do that living a fairy tale life… [Caitlin walks over to her stereo shutting off the melodies, and pulls out her training gear lying next to her South Wind Championship on her desk. Her soft expressions turn stone cold as she begins to remove her summer dress slowly with her back turned to Roxy preparing to get dressed to get some a work out and some last minute ring training herself. Roxy gets up shaking her head, as she prepares to leave, but she stops and turns to her friend.] Free Fall - You know…this is the good life…it’s exciting and fun…but it ends sometime…the glory fades…the flair gets dull…and the championships all get locked up in some trophy chest for past memories…you don’t want to be alone when all that happens Kat. Caitlin Fairchild – Roxy…you’re gonna learn soon…that sometimes…that’s the sacrifice it takes to get where you want to go in this business…sometimes…Legends stand alone… [The 4WW logo blows across the screen as Roxy shakes her head as she as she walks out of Caitlin’s room to allow her to get dressed. Once again, Fairchild’s love life would be put on the back burner…it was back to business…and number #1 on her agenda was walking out with her South Wind Championship…at Storm Front the female unstoppable force would run straight into the female immovable object, and it wasn’t going to be pretty. The scene fades with a final shot of Caitlin in her pink G-string with her back turned putting on her red and black sports bra.]
