A Rude Awakening 4WW Exposures #1162 Date: February 9, 2005 Location: N/A +-+-+-+-+-+-+ RUDE AWAKENING Saturday February 5, 2005 Calgary Alberta 17:32 -+-+-+-+-+-+- You would think I would know where to begin. The planning, the precaution and all that time you'd think I should have put in. The truth? I'd almost quote a famous line, 'You can't handle the truth.' What would someone say to that, I mean, if I just jumped up and got in their face screaming. Priceless, abso-fucking-lutely priceless. They also say, 'The truth shall set you free', which I whole-heartedly agree with. The truth shall indeed set you free, but today it shall set me free. This would bring me back to my original thought, 'you'd think I would know where to begin.' In fact, come to think of it, I haven't known anything to do with this world for some time. So the question that begs to be asked is: why didn't I get to know what I've got myself into? I have walked back into the Four Winds, my head held high, perhaps higher than ever. My confidence budding, my adrenaline pumping, and every instinct aware. But I haven't had the respect to study the competition; to understand the inner workings. Yet, I'd bet my dollars I had nothing to worry about... Ding dong. The bell rang through the empty house. A slight, well timed pause followed the loud bell. Ding dong... The world closed its doors to the conscious mind of a pale skinned man. Sitting in the comfort of a large black chair, the foot of the chair extended outward along with the man's long legs. Snug, cozy, and sound asleep, the echoing ring of the doorbell made its way into his subconscious. Still ringing in his mind after a moments silence, the bell eventually brings his eyes open. Gaping into the light of his surroundings as if a newborn just entered the world. He stretches his arms to the sides and growls out a loud yawn. The instant of awakening when the mind is totally unaware and almost insecure. Knock-Knock-Knock! Persistant thumping follows the high pitched bell sound. Eyes open, ears perked and now almost fully alert, he springs forward from his relaxed position. The spiked red locks of his hair sway with the motion of his rise as he marches towards the door. Reaching down for the bronze colored latch, a click of the lock can be heard as his free hand twists the knob. Pushing down on the latch with his free hand the mammoth sized man pulls the door open. Standing on his stoop is a familiar face, someone he had been looking forward to seeing. Perhaps now there could be a beginning. Dressed in a black Armani suit, with a silk white shirt accented with a sleek black leather tie, was a face that reminded him of the past. To the man's side he held a silver handle leading up to a large black umbrella. The rain seemed to strike this evening with more ferocity than any previous night. Making a thumping sound as it ricocheted off the black material covering the dome wire frame. A moment of silence passes the kind of silence that makes the small bumps on your arms rise and then run across your body, eventually down your spine. An awkward moment to say the least, until the silence was broken. 'Are you going to invite me in?' With a grimacing smile he points to the inside, the Armani suit almost getting wet in the down pour. As he finishes the question a booming roll of thunder stretches across the plane. Looking at him with an almost gleeful child look, Darek extends his right arm onto the man's shoulder and brings him in. Bringing the large oak door to a tight close after him, Darek ushers his guest into the home. 'What brings you here?' The question had to be asked, so putting it aside early, the man in the suit smiles. 'You did.' Cracking a smile at Darek he starts to unbutton his jacket. 'Well at least your decision to return to the Four Wind Wrestling.' Turning into the den just up the hallway from the entrance, Darek looks into the eyes of his guest. A look of curiosity etches itself on his face as he watches the man remove his jacket and turn full center to him. Resting the jacket on the back of the black leather recliner in which Darek had been resting, the man places his right arm on the side of the chair. He leans against the chair, and reaches into his pocket. The jingling of coins and keys emits from behind the fabric pocket until the hand stops moving and wrestles out a silver Zippo lighter. Reaching back to his jacket, he brushes through the material then slides his hand into the inside pocket. A long brown, rolled tobacco cigar is pulled from the pocket with the plastic wrapper still attached. Pulling the wrap off the rum smelling cigar, he stuffs the garbage into his pocket. 'What do you mean my decision to return?' Darek asks, 'not that I'm not grateful to see you.' 'Don't worry Darkie, you and I go back. If anything I shouldn't be welcome in your home, but bygones are bygones.' He replies and then the spark of the flame finishes his sentence. Taking a deep draw of breath, he brings the smoke from his cigar into his body then releases it. 'You're a smart man Darek, let's cut the chit chat and get to the reasons.' Darek walks over to a leather sofa and drops into the fluff of absolute comfort, sinking into the material. Smiling, he looks at his guest. 'It's been awhile Matthew. I can't seem to figure out why you would show up now.' Matthew Hyde, the hidden talent behind the Four Winds staff. Former acting Vice President, former runner of the West Wind Division, and a man who held many prestigious offices within the Gulfsouth Wrestling Alliance as well. But perhaps the most remarkable thing about Matthew Hyde was the fact that he could back up his cocky and rather brash attitude. Training for years in independent based federations and working through grueling conditions in Japan, Matthew had earned the respect of those he confronted; if he didn't, he beat it out of them. It was that mentality that Darek liked. Despite a rough introduction to each other in the GWA, Darek and Hyde soon developed a business relationship. One built on respect more than anything, Darek recognized that Matthew had over taken many great Gulfsouth Legends in his short career and Matthew knew that The Dark Angel was not to be fucked with. 'It began with a phone call I got.' Hyde started. +-+-+-+-+-+-+ AN EYE OPENER Thursday February 3, 2005 Los Angeles California 24:12 -+-+-+-+-+-+- Ring... Ring....... Ring............ Rushing through the black void, a shadow blurs through the scarcely lit area. Moving at a high speed, the blur of shadow comes to a stop and as it does so, so to does the ringing of the phone. A faint sound of shuffling and fumbling about is heard through the blackness, but before long the snap of a flicked light switch brings illumination to the situation. The darkness disperses with no trace at the instant the light is beckoned. In a muffled voice, the man standing beside the light switch inside a large room begins to speak. Holding the cordless phone close to his ear, the man stretches and lets out a yawn just before answering. 'Hello?' he says with sleep dripping from the tone of his voice. A pause, one long enough to bring some tension into the atmosphere. 'Hello!?' The mans asks, but almost demands. Finally a response. 'Hi. Kristian?' More of a greeting than a response, but it dignifies the same purpose. Nodding as if the person on the other end of the phone could see his response, Kristian responds. 'Yes. Who's this?' Quid Pro Quo, you ask something then I ask something, or at least that's how this felt. The voice from the other side of the phone waited just a moment before responding again. But at least that tension, the eerie tone that had been established earlier was now lifted. Kristian twisted in a stretch as he waited for a response. 'I don't know if you remember me, but I was the President of the Gulfsouth Wrestling Alliance during your stay. My name is Hyde, Matthew Hyde.' The faint recollection came rolling back into his mind, but nothing concrete. The man also known as Severed (or Kristian by birth name) placed his pointer finger under his chin and massaged it through the short patch of hair there. Looking hard into the recess of his mind, Severed tries to recall everything he can about the name Matthew Hyde, but only a faint association with the GWA (as Hyde pointed out anyway) came to mind. A look of bewilderment now crosses on Kristian's puzzled face. 'Remind me again, I've taken one too many shots to the head.' Severed responds half joking and half serious. 'Let's play a game, I'll say a name and you tell me the first thing that comes to your mind.' Severed looks confused as he pulls the phone away from his ear and gives it a glare. Who was this 'Matthew Hyde' to be calling him and wanting to play juvenile mind games? 'You know what, fuck you. Tell me what you want and maybe then I'll play your little game.' 'Billy Brahma.' Hyde says, totally dismissing the threat from Severed. 'It's nice to see you still have that temper.' He does add. After a few seconds of careful deliberation Kristian brings a face to the name, to both names; Billy Brahma was the co-owner of the GWA at the time Severed had begun his reign of sadistic dominance in the Hardcore division. Then there was Hyde, the take no shit, cocky, loud mouth that would never shut up no matter how deep of a grave he was digging himself. However, the thing Kristian remembered the most, was Hyde's ability in the ring. An all around athlete, one who's mouth was as large as his muscle. 'Billy Brahma...' Kristian begins, 'egotistical. It's a wonder how you two worked as a cohesive unit.' The silence on the phone indicates Matthew is taking in what Kristian had just responded with, 'So you do remember me then?' 'I do now.' 'Excellent, now let's continue the game if you don't mind.' Severed felt an uneasy swelling in his stomach cause it to turn and give him that butterfly nausea feeling. The Gulfsouth was a time in his career when he and the likes of Chainsaw, 'The Insane One' Chris West, and ‘Superstar’ Eddie B had taken the GWA Hardcore championship to a new level. A time when the World Championship (which he also soon came to hold) played a minor role to the death defying antics of the crowd pleasers in the hardcore genre. Severed knew his days in the Gulfsouth were valued, but they did nothing for his reputation, his name was not known for his wrestling ability. Instead he was known for his own dark, sinister past. A tainted time in his life when he really did fight for survival and the right to live. A few seconds pass and the dead silence acts as a trigger for Matthew Hyde to continue his game, 'Chris West.' There was a million and one things Kristian could say about Chris West, but only one in particular came to his mind at this point of time. 'Legend in his own right and time.' 'Very good,' Hyde replies without hesitation. 'How about William Syckle?' What could Severed recall of the man known as the Crimson Blade. He was a being that lived by his own accord; he played his own tune and instilled his own brand of justice. But to Kristian, he was just a big name in the World Title hunt and eventually became known as the last Gulfsouth Champion. Stumbling in his mind for what seems like forever, it doesn't take long for his reply to really come out. 'Champion.' Kristian turns a half circle and begins to walk out of the den and down a corridor. Still holding the phone to his ear with his shoulder, he pushes the door open to a dark room. 'What's the point?' 'Dark Angel?' Now there was a name he hadn't heard in awhile. Maybe it was because he had initially given up on the wrestling scene or maybe it was because Dark Angel was the shadow that loomed over the Gulfsouth at all times. His ominous presence seemed almost belittling to those in the federation, probably one of the most dominate names to be born from the southern federation, Kristian at one time feared stepping into the ring with this malevolent force. Now his mind was being asked to think of the first thing, just one thing that comes up with association to that monster, to that name. 'Weaver of Shadows, master of the mind game.' Severed trails off slightly before continuing, 'the one who brought the dark ages upon us.' Hyde takes the answer in and keeps Severed in suspense. 'Perfect, just perfect.' 'What's perfect?' 'Are you still interested in fighting Kristian?' Matthew asked, as if it were rhetorical question. Without hesitation, 'extreme is a way of life. I chose to live this way and I chose to fight,' he replies. 'What do you have in mind?' 'Well, it started with a call.' Hyde began... +-+-+-+-+-+-+ A CALL Monday February 1, 2005 New York, New York 7:46 -+-+-+-+-+-+- The sun had just begun to rise; rays of light were just beginning to peer through the cracks of the vertical blinds covering the far window. The room was dimly lit with just the lighting of the rising sunlight. The night had been brushed away by the dawning of a new day, the same that had happened yesterday and the same that would happen tomorrow. Only today, unlike yesterday, there was a slight disturbance. Drifted off in a deep sleep, recovering from the previous days indiscretions. Matthew Hyde could hardly begin to separate the dream world from the harsh reality of the real world. The fact of the matter is he hadn't slept well in ages. His dreams were haunted with visions of horror, he spent countless hours rolling, tossing, turning and staring at the red LED lights of the clock beside his bed. But today, he was sleeping and he was sleeping well. That is until he was disturbed. '...not home right now or am unavailable to take your call. Please leave your name number and message; I'll get back to you as soon as possible.' Beeeeeeeeeeeeep! The long, high pitched beep from the answering machine began to ring through the mind of Matthew. Still away in a dream world, Hyde refused to let this intrusion wake his only decent nights rest in several months. Then the voice of the caller could be heard, a message was being recorded. It was a voice that Matthew Hyde had known all too well and one that he knew he must break his deep sleep to answer. 'It's Ian; we've got some issues that should be discussed concerning the East Wind Championships. Get a hold of me as soon as you get this message.' Beeeeeeeeeeeeep! Snapping forward sending the blankets shooting forward, Hyde turns his head to the desk at the far left corner of the room. His eyes glance at the black digital answering machine whose number used to read 00 and now read 01. One message, one important message, so important in fact Matthew had risen almost immediately just to try to get to the phone. Coming to a full realization that he had undoubtedly 'It's Ian; we've got some issues that should be discussed concerning the East Wind Championships. Get a hold of me as soon as you get this message.' missing the call, he stared blankly forward. 'Shit.' Taking in a deep breath, he tries to crack a morning smile. Rising from his comfortable bed, Matthew reaches up to the ceiling of his bedroom, extending his arms as far as they can go while he lets out a yawn of monstrous proportions. Forcing his heavy legs to move towards the door, he twists the handle and pulls the door open. Almost stumbling in his half aware state, he fumbles his way through the dark corridor and into the lavatory. Flicking the switch, forty watts of glowing white light fill the once dark room and Matthew steps in closing the door behind him. His reflection in the mirror seems almost appeasing to him, looking as though he were a narcissist. Reaching out, he grabs a hold of the side of the mirror and gives it a gentle tug, bringing the mirror swinging forward on a hinge. From behind the reflection of himself, Hyde grabs a thin white plastic handles, soft back bristled tooth brush and some Crest Toothpaste. Making haste out o a normal daily routine, Matthew finished up his duties. As he walked out of the bathroom, he reached back and snapped the light switch back into the off position. Walking back towards his room, dressed only in a pair of black silk boxer shorts with Homer Simpson's head all over them and the quote 'D'oh' beside each picture. As he steps closer towards his room he flexes his muscles out of instinct and then turns the handle while pushing the door forward. Walking over to the desk which holds his cordless phone receiver and the sleek black answering machine, he pushes the play button and steps back. 'Matthew are you there? If you are wake up and answer the phone. Well, look, it’s Ian; we've got some issues that should be discussed concerning the East Wind Championships. Get a hold of me as soon as you get this message.' Beeeeeeeeeep! The machine then replied with the date and time of the call its feminine robotic style voice. 'Monday February First at 7:53.' Another beep emits from the machine as Matthew reaches down and pushes the erase button. The machine then asks: 'Do you wish to erase your messages?' and he follows up by pushing erase again. 'Message erased.' Picking up the cordless phone, Matthew makes his way towards his closet while dialing the number to the Four Winds Head Office. Briskly flipping through his collection of high priced suits and shirts, Matthew comes a stop as the secretary answers the call. 'Four Winds Wrestling, a subsidiary of Talesmith Productions. Lynn speaking how may I help you?' Nodding at the sound of Lynn's voice, Matthew has a momentary lapse where he visions the fair green eyed brunette beauty sitting at the other end. He imagines that he could be the one that wakes up to her voice each morning for the rest of his life, and then he quickly snaps back to reality. 'Hello?' she asks. Understanding that he's allowed himself to doze into a brief daydream, Hyde chuckles and then begins. 'It's Matthew; I got a call from Ian. He said it was urgent.' 'Yes, he's in a meeting right now and will not be available until some time this afternoon. But he did leave a message for you.' She explains with a sexy, silky smooth voice. Waiting for the woman to finish her sentence, Matthew is anticipating what kind of message the co-owner of the Four Winds had left for him. Though he had been named the Vice President of the Four Winds, Matthew Hyde had left the business for a period of grieving when a family member of his had passed. This was his first official call to return to work. Or at least he hoped. 'What's the scoop Hon?' he asks with his confident voice flirting without even seeing his subject. After a brief giddish school girl-esque giggle, Lynn continues. 'He's left a memo for you regarding the Infidels and the East Wind titles. It seems the Federations tag division may be in jeopardy. It says here...' she pauses for a bit while reading ahead in the memo. Hyde waits patiently to hear what she has to say but at the same time can hardly wait, though it had begun to sound like bad news. '...says here that the DMC, Eddie B., Lucifer Priest, Claudio Turbine and Slayer are no more. The DMC tendered their resignation to the federation.' 'Interesting,' Hyde cuts in accidentally. 'It also says that, the East Wind Division needs to be holstered up. We already have Kyo Kushinaga and Paul Williams named as number one contenders but we need a real battle for the titles with the Infidels leaving after this match.' She takes a minute to let the news sink in for Matthew. 'He wants you to bring in a team, a tag team. One worthy of competing in an East Wind Title match Next StormFront.' 'Are you shitting me?' He blurts out without thinking, 'I'm sorry, Lynn. I just can't believe he wants something like that done in this short of a time frame.' 'Don't shoot the messenger sweetie.' She replies with a hint of flirting back at Hyde. Matthew pulls a suit from the closet and turns to lay it across his bed. Scratching his head in amazement, he begins to contemplate the possibilities. What could be a dominating force? But to pick the perfect team he would first need to find the definition of dominating by the Four Winds Standards. The division had to be in shambles if the current North Wind Champion was challenging the throne. Kyo Kushinaga. An interesting thought on its own. Matthew dazed away from the conversation for a moment while following a train of thought on the North Wind Champion. How was he supposed to make a team that could stand in the same ring as a legend in the making like the Outlaw? Actually, the real question was: Who could he find that Kushinaga, the Champion, could not or has not beaten? It was this thought that ravished his mind for the time being. Taken away into a world of his own rules, Matthew went through lists of names in his mind. Names of people, competitors, and fighters that he had met in his journeys. Coming to a blank each time, Matthew began to feel the frustration of the task setting in. How in the hell was he going to assemble a team that could survive the onslaught of the North Wind Champion and former Tempest Champion, let alone stand a chance of winning the battle. 'Are you still there?' Lynn asked. 'Oh, sorry. Just thinking.' Hyde replies instantly. 'I wish you luck Matty, and I'll see you when you get in. I scheduled you an appointment with Mr. Childress at two-thirty. I hope that's reasonable.' Hyde thinks it through as he pushes the thought of forming a great tag team out of his mind just for a moment. 'Give me a meeting at three-thirty, I've got to jump a plane but I will be there.' If Hyde could see her, he would know that she was smiling at him as she said her goodbyes. 'Done. And I'll see you when you get in.' 'Thanks dear.' Click. +-+-+-+-+-+-+ PUTTING THE PIECES TOGETHER Saturday February 5, 2005 Calgary Alberta 17:43 -+-+-+-+-+-+- Matthew took another deep inhale of his wine dipped cigar. The potent flavor taken all the way into his lungs before he exhales the toxins into the air. The bluish-grey smoke pours out of his nostrils and he bites down on the end of the cigar smiling at Darek. Standing in the window frame, Darek has both arms extended down and he leans forward on them staring out into the darkening sky. Lowering his head, Matthew had given him the whole story. The call, the careful contemplation and now the master plan was to be released. Darek could understand Matthew for making this approach out of the blue, but he felt himself wondering if he still had what it takes to stand before great warriors such as Wesley Williams, Kyo Kushinaga and Paul Williams. In fact, he even had doubts about standing before the former Tempest Champion. Paul Williams to him was a protégé of sorts. Amanda had chosen Paul to align himself with YTO Rob Givens and herself as a new force within the Four Winds: The Legends. But through a random chance of fate, or perhaps not so random... Paul now had been teamed up with a sworn enemy of the Dark Angel, a sworn enemy of the Immortal, an enemy that walked triumphantly with the unified North Wind Title. The Championship Darek had to leave behind. The one thing Darek had in his life that really symbolized his commitment and dedication. For an instant in time, Darek was the best of the best. Tragedy, a wretched turn of events forced Darek to relinquish the most prized title in our industry. But his feelings of contempt never gave up; he refused to relinquish his hatred for the man who could withstand all the warriors in the Four Winds. Turning away from the window, Darek looks deep into the bright blue eyes of his colleague. 'Who did you have in mind as my partner?' 'Well,' Matthew begins, 'you may or may not be familiar with the name. But he's mastered. His lifestyle should contrast well with yours. Each of your perceptions on life and reality should form a cohesive unit that undoubtedly could become the East Wind Champions.' 'Quit beating around the bush and just tell me who it is?' Darek adds in an abrupt manner. 'Is it Kanton?' 'No.' 'Givens? Rustin Slade? William Syckle?' 'Again, no.' Hyde moves towards the coffee table and ashes the cigar before continuing. 'But you've gotten towards the right track. It is someone from the past.' Darek looks right in Hyde's face and squints slightly as if he can see right through the one time Legend Killer, the one time Icon. 'Dammit Matthew, don't play games with me.' Ding dong. Darek's head turns in shock as he's cut off by the sound of the bell. 'Who the hell could that be?' He asks himself aloud, and then glances at Hyde. Matthew just smiles and nods. 'The questions you had asked before and now will be answered when you open that door.' Hyde nods in the direction of the door. 'Go ahead.' Making his way out of the living room, Darek rushes through the corridor and comes to the large oak doors with bronze handles. Then the as he approaches the door, the bell sounds again. Ding dong! The sound beckons him to open the door, but anticipation, the fear of not knowing now made him reach forward to unlatch the deadbolt. Pulling the door open, the thunder echoes overhead as a bright flash of lightning flickers across the nightline. The brightness causes Darek to step back a bit in awe while still pulling the door fully open. Standing on his step was a face he had never stood before in a ring, it was a face he did recognize. One that he could never really forget. A man (if you wanted to call him that), most called a monster because of his past, but Darek could look past the man's sins of before and see the improvements made to his everyday life. The being known as the Dark Angel extends his right hand forward reaching for the man's extended hand. 'I see you're still practicing self mutilation?' Darek asks as a greeting. 'Aren't each of us?' He replies. 'I mean, look at what we put our bodies through willingly. I just take pain to a new level.' 'Agreed.' Darek nods in approval. The man takes a step into the house as Darek backs away from the door inviting him in. Faint smiles roll across the face of Darek and his new guest as Matthew Hyde steps into the hallway to observe the meeting of his two soldiers. The meeting of his destined tag team. 'After all, what kind of masochist would I be if I didn't enjoy the pain?' Kristian Kross asks with a smug tone in his voice. The man known as Severed, the Modern Masochist was chosen to ally with the one and only Dark Angel. They were to form a team that could resculpt a crumbling division, a team that could oppose the Outlaw and all that he stood for. A team that was now going to have to face the likes of Wesley Williams and a Knightmare Brigade loaded match. Darek cracks a firm smile as he turns to Hyde for a moment then back to Kristian. 'Pain is only weakness leaving the body.' Severed smiles back. 'Pain will be our ally in this war.' Matthew Hyde brings his hands together to make a loud clap that sounds through the corridor. He walks slowly up to both men as they walk towards the den. Meeting in the middle, the tripod of men just begin to smile uncontrollably. Finally, after a period of silence, Matthew Hyde speaks up. 'So it's a team then?' Darek looks back at Severed, then to Hyde and nods. 'It's a team.' 'Just as the Dark Ages fell upon the world, so too will they fall upon the Four Winds.' Kristian walks along side of Matthew, who both follow Darek into the comfort of his living room. And so the night went, the pieces of the puzzle were meshed together nicely as one team in the four way match for the disputed East Wind Titles had gotten themselves on the same page. The rest of the night was spent sharing stories about the past, the harsh realities, the truths and the secrets that each man had. For a team of this caliber to be successful there had to be trust and for there to be trust, if one man fell the other would have to be there to catch him. Kristian went into detail about his tainted history. From the moment he felt warm blood spill across his hands, to his first intimate night with manager Brianna Smith. He explained his lifestyle, jail and the reasons he chose to be the sadistic fuck he was. While Darek shed light on the truth behind religion. He spoke of prophecies that have yet to be seen, but swore one day they would be fulfilled. The hours of the evening soon dissipated and turned into the cold blackness of the dark night. Into the wee hours of the morning, the team had begun to know each other. But it was then that sleep had finally caught up with them. The next couple of days were going to be as exhausting. Training day in and out in Derek’s gym, the preparation to face Main Event caliber contenders. Nothing to worry about. In fact, I felt more confident about stepping into the ring with Kyo and any other member of the Four Winds or even the PrimeTime. With Kristian Kross standing in my corner, I knew the world was ripe for the picking. And beginning with the East Wind Titles, I now made a vow to begin to take away as much from the Outlaw as he had taken from me. Only my pride is worth more than any title and that's exactly what I was looking to target. His dignity, his pride, the world knew he was supreme as long as I was not around. Now that the time for The Dark Angel of death to return to the winds hath come, he shall fall. Triumphant through so much, Kyo Kushinaga would now be seen for what he really is: a fraud. I respected the fact that my old colleague Wesley Williams had been awarded a shot at the very title I had to give up. However it blew my mind to see him unsuccessful in obtaining the gold from the Outlaw. Perhaps he had held onto it for this long for only one reason, to give it back to its rightful owner. No, that was it. I control my destiny and fate, with that, I know that the gold Kyo holds so coveted towards his heart was soon going to be mine once again. When that day came, the Four Winds would be guided away from his imbecilic reign as the North Wind Champion. The Dark Ages are coming.
