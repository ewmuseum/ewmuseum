The pain of loss... Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #867 Date: 8-10-04 Location: Kanton's hotel room (OOC: Sorry, I know this is late, but I just couldn't get it out... last week drained me. So, if I lose I lose... not a big deal as I will understand. I just didn't have enough of my stuff this week. Also, yeah, last week's rp was ALOT better, but like I said.. I just didn't seem to have my stuff this week.) +!+!+!+++!++!+!+!+!+!+!+ My friend, What is going on? I can’t believe this is all happening! I don’t know what to do… I lost Angel tonight and I failed to win. It all happened so quickly. It’s all still a blur. My mind is tearing in so many directions. I feel like I’m dying inside. So much has been taken from me. I have lost almost all hope, but like I said… almost all hope. I still have some left as I must for Angel’s sake. I can not stop… I must get her back… Bullet will pay. Angel my love… I miss you dearly. I cannot lose hope . I will with you again shortly. Your love, Kanton Journal Entry, August 3, 2004 +!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!++!+!+!+!+! Then scene slowly faded in this week. And we find ourselves in the company of a man is by his lonesomeness as it would seem. Kanton had gone through much in the short span of less than an hour. His whole world had come crashing down. He had been given everything in the world, but now… it had had all been taken away. Kanton was alone in his hotel room that day. Storm Front and come and gone so quickly. Angel was gone and Kanton knew one man had her… Bullet. Why had he taken Angel through? Did call her Rachael? Kanton didn’t care… He just knew he had to have Angel back. Kanton looked around the room in which he was calling haven that day. His things had been everywhere. Clothes were strewn around the room and take out food cartons were all over the place. There were also many empty bottles nightstand besides Kanton. He had been drinking, heavily. It was one of Kanton’s ways out from this nightmare he was living. The sun shone into the room between the cracks in the curtains. Kanton had them closed tightly though… Sunlight was not a great thing when one has a hangover… or in the process of getting drunk again at one o’clock in the afternoon. Kanton ran his hand over his grizzly, unshaven face and then through his unkempt hair. He had let himself fall apart. He didn’t care though… he lost his Angel. There was a knock at the door just then… Kanton yelled. “Come in!” Kanton struggled to reply through his alcohol stained voice. Hiccups interrupted him every now and then. The door creaked open and a man stepped in. He was in his early forties and dressed fairly well. He was one of Kanton’s closest and dearest friends. He was John Bass. John looked around in horror though as he waded through the mess of cartons and clothes. He could not believe how badly Kanton had let himself go. It struck great sadness into John’s heart. He looked at Kanton… even trough Kanton’s alcohol influenced guise John could tell what was wrong. He knew his friend. Kanton had almost been a son to him over the years. Bass knew something was wrong. It was then that he worked his way into the room and near Kanton. He waded through the trash and filth and he was standing next to Kris. Kanton looked up to him with alcohol stained tears. Kris had been looking for a way to forget the pain… this hadn’t worked. Bass sat on the bed next to a man who was obviously in pain. The only thing is though, Kanton needed help badly, but unfortunately… Bass didn’t know exactly what to do. It was then he broke the silence. “How are you doing Kris?”, asked Bass softly. How was he doing? What kind of FUCKING QUESTION IS THAT?!!?! Kris was almost shocked at Bass’s words. Maybe it was the liquor, but Kanton truly felt John should have known better. How the fuck was he doing? Jesus, John… c’mon. “How the fuck do you think?’ “I’m sorry…” “Why the fuck are you sorry? HUH? You aren’t the goat fucking ass hole who stole Angel are you? “No…” “Exactly… Shouldn’t you be asking the shit like why are you drinkin n shit? HUH? C’mon John! Tell me this is wrong! Tell me I’m throwing everything away!” Kris ended his tirade. John said nothing as a few moments past. Kanton just glared at John and then sighed. What was the point? Kanton didn’t even know anymore. “It’s not too late Kris… you can still drop this… you can still get her back,” Bass went on for a moment. Kanton looked at him with a semi shocked look. How dare him he felt inside. Bass had no right to say anything! He had no idea what was going on! “WHO THE FUCK TURNED YOU INTO AN AFTER SCHOOL SPECIAL?!?!?!” Kanton was outraged it seemed. That was an understatement though. It probably had more to do with the liquor though. Kanton never acted like this… it had been a long time since Kanton had drank like this also. This scared Bass very much. “I don’t even see why I fucking bother. Is this what you came here for? My god John… that’s pathetic. Just leave me the fuck alone! I don’t fucking need this. I only need to be thinking about one thing. How the fuck I’m going to find Angel and then how much pain I’m going to put Bullet through,” Kanton was enraged it seemed and John was in no way about to interject. Kanton had mentioned two things… Bass got the idea though. “I’ve been all over this fucking city and the surrounding area! I’ve looked at every hotel! NOWHERE CAN THEY BE FOUND! How is that? HOW THE FUCK IS THAT?! What the hell is he doing with my Angel? If he fucking touches a hair on her head, he is SO FUCKING DEAD!” Kanton kept going on about Bullet and Angel, but John knew there was something else… He knew that Kanton would be taking on Darek DeAngleo again. Bass knew that Kanton respected Darek very much, but at the same time he was concerned that Kanton was walking into this match unprepared. Kanton wasn’t though. Kanton knew about this two, and it didn’t help the situation. “What about Darek, Kris?” asked Bass looking at Kanton who had calmed down a bit from his tirade. “What the fuck about him?” “Have you put any thought into your guy’s match? “Of course I have! What the fuck?” “Well, it’s just that you’ve been going on about Angel and Bulle…” before John could even finish his sentence, Kanton had exploded once again. Kanton couldn’t believe that John could downplay Angel being taken. That hurt Kanton deeply… it was almost as if John didn’t care. That wasn’t the case, but it was what it seemed like. “OF FUCKING COURSE I’M GOING TO GO ON ABOUT ANGEL! I FUCKING LOVE HER YOU MORON!” ”I’m sorry Kris I was just sayin…” “YOU WEREN’T SAYING SHIT YOU LITTLE MONKEY FUCKER! Angel was fucking taken from me! She is my heart… my soul! Without her I’m nothing. I’m only emptiness.” Tears began to flow softly from Kanton’s eyes. He loved Angel deeply even though they had only known each other for a few months. It was love at first sight. Kanton would give his life for her… he knew that. Now he felt empty… she was gone. “It’s not like you were married though…” “We weren’t… yet.” Kanton slowly reached into the pocket of his blue jeans and pulled out a little black box. He slowly opened it and showed John what was inside. It was an engagement ring. “I was going to ask her to marry me this week,” Kanton was fighting hard against the tears now. John looked on at his friend who now seemed to be a broken man. Kanton slowly slid the ring back into his jeans. “I know you love her Kris, but you have to put this behind you a bit. You have to worry about Darek! You could be South Wind Champion!” Bass spoke with great vigor, but Kanton was truly shocked. He couldn’t believe that John was acting this way. It was like a dagger in the heart. Kanton had just shown and told Bass how he was feeling. He had poured out all his emotions to the man. It had changed nothing in Bass’s eyes as it seemed though. Kanton lowered his head. Did his friend even care? He couldn’t tell. Sure, he knew he had to deal with Darek, but that wasn’t even the most important thing to Kanton. It was an afterthought. Kanton just wanted his Angel back. It was then that Kanton pointed to the door. “Get out…” Kanton told John softly. Bass couldn’t seem to understand though. What had he done? He just wanted to make sure that Kanton’s priorities were in the right order. He didn’t realize that he and Kanton had different ideas about the order of Kanton’s priorities. “I said… get… out!” Kanton repeated himself again, his anger was beginning to grow yet again it seemed. “Kris c’mon… I just wanted to make sure you were up to what’s going on!” Bass tried to explain himself, but all it did was anger Kanton more. Kanton scowled and then shoved Bass off of the bed and away from Kanton. Bass caught himself and did not land on the floor, but he stood up very quickly and looked on at Kris. Bass had enough. He was just going to say it to Kanton. He didn’t care how it made him feel. “Dammit to hell Kanton, listen to me! Forget about Angel for now! You could be a champion!” Kanton couldn’t believe what was coming out of John’s mouth. Surely this was not the man he looked up to so much. The anger had exploded with Kanton. He picked up a bottle and took aim for John as Bass quickly hopped and evaded the objects and trash that stood in his path. “GET THE FUCK OUT! GET OUT NOW DAMMIT! FUCK YOU! FUCK YOU GOD DAMMIT FUCK YOU!!!!! GET THE FUCK OUT!” Kanton was enraged now. Tears of anger and frustration cascaded down his cheeks. Bass opened the door, but he turned around to say something to Kanton. “If you know what’s good for you… you’ll forget about her,” Kanton threw the bottle, but John quickly closed the door behind him as the bottle shattered against the door. Kanton would be calling room service soon. He needed to get away. He had so much on his mind now. He just needed to get away from it all. It came across Kanton’s mind then. He would write… writing had been his way out lately. It let him get away somewhat from his worries as he was free to express them however he pleased. Kanton stood up and walked over to his suitcase. He grabbed his notebook and saw something underneath it. It was a picture of Kanton and Angel in Sydney at the zoo. They had such a great time that day. She had loved the koalas. Kanton shut the suitcase as to avert his eyes. It pained him to think of Angel, but he knew what he was going to do now. He was going to win for Angel… The he would take her back. Kanton opened the notebook and grabbed a pen from the nightstand. It was then that he put his thoughts down on the paper. +!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+ My Friend, It has been a few days since I have written. I don’t know what to say really. I am just as torn as I was. I spoke with John just now… he seemed so strange to me. He was not the man I knew him to be. The John I knew would not have been like that. Why would he want to hurt me like that? I know he wanted me to focus on my match with Darek, but I have thought of that. He did not need to be the way he was. He was almost attacking me. There was no compassion it seemed. He was so cold. He was almost emotionless. Why? I don’t know. I’ve been having trouble sleeping again. It’s just like it was before I met Angel. The nightmares have returned. I am back to five hours of sleep or less a night again. With her, I had been able to be calm. I had been able to control it. Angel has changed so much in my life for the better. Distance makes the heart grow fonder they say. I love her with all my heart, and I miss her. I will have her back. It’s only a matter of time. It’s only a few more days till Storm Front. I will be reunited with Angel as I said, but I will cross paths with a man I have much respect for once more. I met Darek DeAngleo in the ring once and he was the victor. That would not be happening again though… I believe that it is finally my time. I can wait for the North Wind. I was not surprised that I lost. I knew something would happen, but I knew I gave it my all. I am satisfied by that thought. Only a few more days now. I am finally ready. Angel… I wonder what you are thinking now… I pray for your safety. I love you Yours, Kanton Journal Entry, Saturday August 8th, 2004 +!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!+!!+!+!+!+!+!+! Kanton slowly closed the notebook with a sigh. He set in down on the nightstand with a sigh. He picked up the phone then to call house keeping. The room should be cleaned he thought… the scene then slowly faded out.
