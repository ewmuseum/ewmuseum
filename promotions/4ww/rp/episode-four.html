Wrestling event, the Masked Avenger, whom wears all black that consists of black jeans with a black tank top that reveals his slight muscular arms and the black demonic tattoo on his shoulder and his trademark black hat along with trademark black mask over his twin brown eyes, is walking forward with a large wooden door that is attach to a doorframe over his back.] = = [You can see that he is not struggling with the great weight over his back, as he is using his great strength to carry the large structure in this way.] = “Anarchy…” “Chaos…” “And shedding of blood…” = [He sits the door structure down in the middle of nowhere.] = “All this happened last week on Tempest at the Insane Battle.” = [At the glass part of the door that is in the center of the oak wooden door, you can see a piece of paper tape on the glass surface.] = = [The piece of paper reads “Masked Avenger Headquarters” and has a flaming emblem with a black masked symbol along with twin different Japanese characters that reads “Burning Justice Prevails”.] = = [He stands in front of the door and looks at the door with a slight please expression on his face.] = = [Raises his hand up to the edge of the hat and lifts it up a bit to reveal a thin band-air over across the side of his forehead, as he wipes the slight hint of sweat off his forehead.] = “I have the cut to prove it…” The Challenge = [The view moves with him, and you can see him heading over to a large circular table that lies on the floor on its desktop side, as he sets up the metal legs to make the table stand once it is right side up.] = “I won Fnord’s Insane Battle Royal and won it fairly without resorting to cheating or using mere weapons to defeat my opponents that match.” = [He sits up the circular table and strokes the smooth surface palms of his hands against the smooth surface of the desktop while he looks down at the clean and shiny surface with a content expression and smile on his face. He chuckles softly to himself, and begins to think how his career has been going ever since making his debut not too long ago at StormFront.] = “It is quite funny…” “I never expect myself to get to this level so quickly at these early times of being a superhero at my home league, 4 Winds Wrestling.” “It just proves that a person who has justice on their side can go far in this league than a person who does not have justice on their side.
