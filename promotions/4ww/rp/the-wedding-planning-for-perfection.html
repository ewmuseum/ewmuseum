The Wedding: Planning for Perfection

31 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Wedding: Planning for Perfection 4WW Exposures #1042 Date: 10/31/04 Location: The Corporate Meeting Room (The camera fades into a conference room looking area. Sitting there is Luscious Lady Luck. There are a couple of other women there with her, as well as a speaker phone. All of the women are dressed in business suits. LLL is wearing a dark blue one, with the other two wearing gray and black respectively. LLL is also looking at something in front of her. It is a long black box; she keeps opening and shutting the lid.) LLL: Ok guys, I want this to be absolutely perfect. This has got to be perfect. I won’t have anything less. Do you all understand that? Others: Yes ma’am. LLL: Great, now, Kim, how are you doing with the attire? Kim: I have several different models of attire worked out for you to look at. All of them are from top designers, as you had requested. As soon as you pick the design, the designer has agreed to come in, custom design it, and make the attire themselves. LLL: Great. Great. That is exactly what I needed to hear. It’s all going to be so perfect. Now you got them my specifications, right? Low cut, blinding white, and enough pearls and jewels to put even Bill Gates to shame, right? Kim: Yes ma’am. They understand that and the designers worked hard with these designs. I’m sure you’ll be pleased. LLL: Excellent. Now, Stacy, facilities? Stacy: Not knowing exactly what you were looking for in this setting, we have a couple of different locations ready for your word to be reserved. LLL: I thought I mentioned what I wanted. Stacy: You did, and you didn’t. The locations we have picked out for you meet what you gave us, but that’s all we could do. There are three places in mind right now. First of all is a location on the shores of Greece. The second place is in the Florida Keys, and the final location is in Australia. LLL: Australia, Greece, or Florida? All supreme locations. Stacy: Fit your style, your direction, and each one has different reasons for the location that we felt would best suit the event. LLL: Wonderful: Now, Vincent hon, how are the legalities coming. Vincent (on speaker phone): The legalities are coming along fine. This is one area where nothing will be messed up. LLL: Of course not. You’ve taken care of my family in legal issues before. No reason you wouldn’t do it now. Vincent: Yes, but I do have a couple of concerns here. First, this is not affecting your wrestling career, is it? I mean if it is than there is the possibility of a breach of contract suit. LLL: Absolutely not. I have the Tempest Title tightly secured around my waist, and it isn’t leaving anytime soon. We’ve got this 6 person scramble coming up on Stormfront, but there is nothing that will say I won’t retain the title. They have 15 minutes, and they’ll all be too concerned with each other to go after the belt or the chest, plus they won’t have the opportunity if everything goes according to plan. Vincent: That brings me to another matter. Not everyone is going to be happy with this. Do you have a contingency plan? LLL: Of course I do. And I know exactly who is not going to be happy with this. No reason to make any rash moves, but it’s about time to put some sort of plan on the burner to be put into action. Vincent, I want you to prepare to put Contingency Plan K into action. I will be faxing over the information that you’ll need later, but be prepared. Vincent: Very well. LLL: Great, anything else you all have for me? Group: No ma’am. LLL: We all have our assignments then. Let’s make this as perfect as possible. That’s the only way this can be. You all have a great couple of days, and we’ll see you all on Monday. (LLL reaches out and presses a button on the phone. Stacy and Kim both stand up and leave the table and out of the room. At this point, she opens the box once again, and pulls out a gold ring with a large diamond mounted on it. She stares at the ring, and is mesmerized by it.) LLL: This has to be perfect, and this will be perfect. (The camera pans up from LLL admiring the ring. As it pans up, over her head and her chair, there is a poster size picture of Patti Anderson. The picture itself is a couple of years old, but it is no doubt her, wearing a low cut sparkling white wedding dress, with a train the hangs down to the floor, and a bouquet of roses in her hand. The scene fades out to the purple heart on a black background with “LLL+PA” written in the middle.)
