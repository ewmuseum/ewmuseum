Brass Knuckles

August 2004

Brass Knuckles Chris Sanchez 4WW Exposures #864 Date: 8th Location: Seoul = = = INSIGHT = = = [So here it is a couple of days away. An event the entertainment industry firmly believed that would never happen due to Chris Sanchez’s tendency to give up unless he’s ahead. In Hollywood, Chris quit a television show after three years due to sliding ratings rather than trying to help the ratings rise again. Also for a film, he backed out of an adaptation of a Stephen King film due to delayed shooting thanks to the director being ill. Chris Sanchez knows what he wants. If things aren’t moving fast and if things aren’t going well for him, he will drop it in an instance. So everybody’s still trying to figure out why Sanchez has stuck around to see in what will be his tenth match in 4WW on Monday!] [Why has Chris stuck around for so long? Why has Chris actually survived approximately one-hundred days in the federation? The answer is simple; Sanchez has finally accepted that in the stage of growth to become the best at what he does, he must progress through stages towards the top. This is rather than just being shoved straight into a high position and failing miserably. Chris started at the bottom and progressed to an unfortunate one week reign of the South Wind title. He had a shot at the West Wind title; however Bullet winning signified that Sanchez wasn’t ready for that particular title yet. Now, five weeks after Maelstrom IV, Chris is undefeated in four matches and is the Tempest champion.] [But what does the future hold for Chris Sanchez after everybody in the world celebrates his tenth match anniversary? The answer to that is unknown. It is unknown due to the fact that despite Chris winning four straight matches, including one against a former North Wind champion, he has gone nowhere unlike the people he has defeated. Bullet lost to Sanchez when Sanchez’s career was extremely early. While Sanchez has stayed where he is, Bullet has progressed to the West Wind title and now main events this week’s StormFront. After Chris beat Jamie, Jamie went ahead and faced Jerms in a highly rated match. Jamie didn’t do well but he still got the match. Then the icing on the cake, Robb Hardy lost to Chris and the very next week, he was in a main event! Is this indication that Chris’ career is going nowhere? Chris is avoiding this subject.] [Chris is trying to look on the bright side though before his match against Jeremy Page, his tenth match. When Chris defeats Page on StormFront, Chris will have five straight wins. Then the following week it will be six straight wins. Then seven… Eight… Nine… Ten… Eleven… Chris should be higher and everybody will appreciate him right? The answer: no. If Chris has got anything to learn, that if he wins eleven times then he will be at the beginning of a show as everybody who he defeats seem to go on bigger and better things.] [It makes you wonder, what really is the point of Chris hosting this party on Monday? He has got nowhere with his career. Nobody knows his name as they’d all rather remember names like Patti “Adonia Does The Twist” Anderson, Mike “Don’t Call Me Marvelous You Little Bollocks” Biddle and Darek “DA Is Dead… Until Sweeps” DeAngelo. And nobody really respects Chris Sanchez as he hasn’t had a real alliance with anyone minus the one night where Jerms tried to use Chris as the sacrificial lamb. While everybody else has a lot of them, Chris has never been in a tag team match. Also, a few have difficulties with learning his name… But maybe it’s a gag! Maybe they will surprise him at the anniversary party and tell him that all along, they were playing a prank. Yeah… That’d be great!] [So off to Seoul, South Korea, the location of the 1982 Olympics for Chris’ tenth match against “The Immortal” Jeremy Page. Jeremy Page must be one of these no hopers that give it a go anywhere because their rent and/or payment to Patti is due. Page obviously won a match last week in order to get the shot, which is nice and all. Because “The Immortal” Page’s opponent last week Kyle Silvermoon is a name that a sugar or spoon company would consider for a line of products. And who the hell calls themselves immortal anyway? Supernatural phenomena away from Kyo’s wet dreams do not exist so immortality is merely false advertising. Although a name like that will probably draw more than Maelstrom V’s main event: Patti vs. Kyo. That main event is about as exciting as buttered toast.] = = = SCENE: HOTEL ROOM IN SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA = = = [The scene opens in Chris Sanchez’s hotel room in Seoul, South Korea. Sanchez is getting ready for his big night at StormFront. However it was nothing like what the Playgirl magazine cover wannabes called the other wrestlers would be doing. They would be in the gym, pumping some weight, trying to outdo each other in weightlifting competition and drinking fucking god awful protein shake. Chris is doing the opposite. The only weight Sanchez is lifting is a cheeseburger into his mouth. He is sat on the hotel settee with the telephone at the side taking down notes in his posh business executive notepad. He was, of course, preparing for his big upcoming anniversary party.] SANCHEZ: Hey Bill, how’s the party planning going at the moment…? Yeah… That’s great! Listen I need to know about decoration of the party. I don’t want to come into my own party and see an array of fucking bright balloons now do I… Uh, yeah, you better put away those balloons you moron. I am a twenty-nine year professional, not an eight year old Yu-Gi-Oh fanatic whose other hobby is sticking his finger up his nose… Okay… How about refreshments…? Yes I think beer is essential, and lots of it too, duh! So I think you better get as much beer as you… Oh… You have… That wasn’t a funny prank was it? [Chris noted down in his notepad: “Bill = Moron!” while he was still talking. Suddenly, ketchup from the burger leaked out of the side and fell right onto the notepad and a bit onto his leap. He leapt up. ‘I don’t want to wreck the present my wife got me for this tour’ he thought and he made his made to the kitchen, still keeping grasp to the phone. He went into the kitchen, tore off some kitchen paper from a big roll of it on the side, went back into the room and began to wipe the ketchup away while talking on the phone.] SANCHEZ: How about snack things…? Yeah I guess you’re right, who needs snacks when you have beer. But what about… The noise is me wiping ketchup off my notepad, what is it to you? So anyway back to the party, how about some music…? Why did you do that? The music selection is dedicated to me, not for the rest of the damn roster so you can take that AC/DC album and shove it. I’m thinking of maybe playing Limp Bizkit, Muse, White Stripes, Nickelback, Linkin Park. Then I will end it off with a bit of Mozart remixed with Christine Aguilera songs… Yes, congratulations, you’ve finally established what sarcasm is… [Suddenly there was a knock on the hotel door.] SANCHEZ: Ah crap… Bill, I’ll talk to you later I got someone at the door. [Chris put the phone down. He was about to walk to the door when he noticed the ketchup dripping down his jeans. Sanchez quickly grabbed the kitchen towel and wiped away the tomato sauce before making his way to the door. He quickly opened it to reveal two guys in suits and sunglasses; you could call them Men In Black rejects actually. The two guys pulled out two wallets with medals and identification in them. They were from the FBI. Chris got a quick glance of the names, one was called Agent Jackson and another was called Agent Harrison. Strangely despite the different names, they almost looked like identical twins!] SANCHEZ: Hello! Please do come in. I’m glad you could finally catch up with the 4 Wind Wrestling glorified circus. [The two agents did come in and Chris closed the door behind him.] SANCHEZ: So what’s the news about that phone call that I got while getting a video in Perth? A nice video… About underdogs succeeding in a corporate world! HARRISON: We traced the phone call to a mobile phone in the States to a man called Kyle Westwood, a man who’s 6”4, 491 pounds, currently unemployed and homeless. We arrested him for the threat, but as you can tell, a mobile phone and homeless do not go together so he’s also been charged for theft and intent to harm the person he took the phone off. SANCHEZ: Kyle Westwood? Why do I recognize that damn name? JACKSON: It’s the actual name of Knuckles, the bodyguard you have on 4WW television a month or so back. If it wasn’t for the fact that agent Harrison and I here weren’t 4 Winds Wrestling, we wouldn’t have caught him in the dozens of homeless people there! SANCHEZ: Knuckles is 491lbs? Fucking hell, he’s grown since 4WW. So anyway, you’re fans of 4WW? JACKSON: Definitely. I just bought the Jeremy Page T-Shirt yesterday. SANCHEZ: Page has merchandise? I haven’t even got merchandise yet. Fucking wankers. I’ve been told that I have to wait six months from June 28th on a decision whether to make a: “You’ve Just Been Sanchized!” shirt, damn nation… Uh, so I guess the case with me is over then? HARRISON: Not necessarily. I think you may have gotten yourself into a lot of trouble this past Monday night on StormFront. I’ve got a tape to show what happened. [Harrison revealed a tape from his jacket pocket. He then walked towards the living room where the video player was located. Chris and Agent Jackson followed closely and when they reached the room, Harrison already had the tape slotted in and just pressed played.] HARRISON: Watch closely. [The tape plays that of Chris Sanchez’s controversial ending to his match this past Monday night on StormFront. It is right at the point where Chris puts out a mysterious gold object and placing it on his fist, having his back facing the ref and Curtis as he was pretending that he was still hurting after being hurled across the ring.] MORALES: Did he put something around his fist? CANDLER: Or was he playing with himself? [Suddenly in the living room, Chris grabbed the remote control and put the video on mute. The two agents turned around to him looking suspicious.] SANCHEZ: Sorry… I can’t stand Mary Candler’s voice though. She’s a cow who likes to take it up the backdoor! [The video continues. Curtis nods his head to the ref, letting the ref know he can continue on as Curtis limps back on his feet and begins to approach over to the cornered Chris. Curtis places his hand on Chris' shoulder which causes Chris to sharply turn around and face Curtis. CRACK!!! Chris connects with a swift right hook on Curtis using the hand with the object in. The force causes Sanchez to go down too. While Chris was laid on his belly, he uses this opportunity to secretly take off the blunt object from his hand and place it down his pants. The ref was about to make the count but he stopped when noticing Chris roll over to Curtis and covers him with an inside pin. The ref begins the count. 1… 2… Chris tightly grabs hold Curtis' tights!!! 3!!! The ref signals for the bell. Chris wins yet another match. Sanchez pauses the tape.] SANCHEZ: I’m so great! JACKSON: Chris, you warned about using illegal weapons. You clearly have a pair of brass knuckles there! SANCHEZ: Who’s cares? Nobody was hurt except for Curtis. It’s not like anyone was influenced by it. HARRISON: That’s where you’re wrong. There was an incident last Wednesday where a regular 4WW fan saw you use the brass knuckles, so he got a pair of his own and attacked someone with it who has been giving him grief for the past year. SANCHEZ: People following my footsteps, about damn time! JACKSON: Chris! You must realise that you were the influence behind a rare disruption! Luckily you are not getting the finger of blame this time due to several reasons. However if you pull a stunt like that again, you will be prosecuted. That includes if you use your trusty baseball bat in front of an influential audience. SANCHEZ: Aww come on! HARRISON: We better be getting onto a case in northern Japan. Just be aware of what we have said to you today! SANCHEZ: Okay I understand. And thanks for slamming that bastard Knuckles into jail, I truly appreciate that! [The two agents then slowly made their way to the door and let themselves out. Chris Sanchez stood there looking a bit annoyed. Why the fuck should he be careful of wht he does and says? Kyo stabs people! Patti acts like a whore! Everyone else is going to hell so why should Chris be any different? Chris stood there thinking about it while relishing in the fact that StormFront will see his fifth straight win and him retain his championship for another week… Hopefully.] [Suddenly the phone rang. Chris gasped. The last time the phone rang on him, it was that evil fat bastard Knuckles threatening Sanchez that he is dead when Chris gets back into America. He was in the slammer though. Surely that couldn’t be him. But a man that big could run into his cell wall and escape, never mind the hard way. Sanchez slowly walked to the phone, slowly picked it up and answered.] SANCHEZ: H-H-Hello…? [Chris looked relieved.] SANCHEZ: Oh hey Bill… [False alarm!] END!
