Working with the enemy. Blaise 4WW Exposures #1205 Date: 3/12/05 Location: Rome, Italy Before Reading this one, please read Ukyo's rp located here http://www.aogames.net/4ww/roleplays/messages/1204.shtml The day after last StormFront. Still in Rome. A long limosine pulls up the small street made of cobblestone. The Colliseum of old Rome itself off in the distance, as well as many other places that have been written down in antiquity. The limo takes up most of the small road, for some reason devoid of any other cars, except for a small sedan that's sitting about 20 yards up the street. A driver, female and itallian looking, gets out of the car and then opens the back door. Out steps a redhaired man, with a bit of stubble on his face. His suit jacket, unbuttoned, flaps in the wind a bit as he takes two steps and then truns slowly to survey the area. He looks back into the car as a man who looks not unlike him, but quite a bit heavier, about 40 lbs or more, steps out. The smaller, first, man, Blaise McClancy, stands in a business suit, Italian cut, with a light gray color. The other man, his cousin, Sean McClancy, stands in a similar suit, in a deep Russian blue. “Looks clear, so far. You sure you want to do this?” “This one's fer Ian an' The Order, na' fer me. Bu' they're offerin' information tha' we need. Be prepared though.Jus' be prepared.” Blaise reaches to the small of his back, under the unbottoned sports jacket and checks the presence of his special weapons for this. And then also checks on his spirt blades, tucked inside two spring loaded writst sheaths. “I'm good.” Sean stops him before he enters in what looks to be an old 'bar' type place. “Ye focused? Ye ready? Ready fer anythin'?” Blaise stares into Sean's eyes. “I'm ready. I worked out some frustrations last night. Don't worry about it.” Sean smiles at his younger cousin. “Good thing we're family. Cause if we weren't, she'd kill ye fer tha' comment.” “What? Not like Patti is quiet.” Sean hits his cousin in the shoulder at that comment, and then looks at the door to the place their going. “This isn'na goin' t' be easy. Let's 'ope tha' don'na dawdle around.” Blaise reaches out and pulls the door open for the elder McClancy. Sean steps in, with Blaise moving to come in right behind him. As they enter they see that the room is full of large amounts of second hand smoke, a couple of guys stand next to a billards table, cigars in their mouths. They look at Blaise and Sean as they walk in and take long drags off their cigars and blow them out. The sound of something Euro-poppish, no... new age... Enigma from their first album, MCMXC A.D. plays from a jukebox. “Nasty stuff.” “They're infernals, the smoke makes them seem more at 'ome.” The bartender nods at them as they approach into the main part of the bar, and then pours a couple of glasses with something in them. He has some hot little number in a miniskirt and babydoll tee shirt that surprisingly enough has the 4WW logo on it, bring it over to a table, and put them down in front of an older looking man, also smoking from a cigar. Sean looks around and then acknowledges the man who sits in front of them. “Asmoday, yer lookin' good fer yer age.” “Ah, PRINCE Bres, your looking good yourself. Come, sit with me and we can talk of conspiracies and war. And of your bid for kingship of the Courts. I wish to hear all that you know.” He emphasised the title that Sean currently holds, that of Prince of the Unseelie Courts. What? He's trying to become king of both. But Bres is a Fomorian, he's no angelic creature if you've not noticed. Why else would he have had someone like Annie Leanan betrothed to him as a child? “Only if ye repay in kind.” “Yes, that was the deal I discussed with your brother-in-law. But I requested you come, as I can't have The Wrath that close to me. There is... bad blood... if you can understand.” Blaise pulls out the chair that his elder cousin then takes. Blasie remains standing behind him and a bit off to the side. The man addressed as Asmoday looks up at Blaise. “Please, Prince Turren, you may sit to. This place is a haven. No conflict would be allowed by either angelic or demonic forces. And your kind? You're alone here in this part of the country. We have none of the courts down this far south.” “I'll stand, thank you.” The olderman takes a sip from a cup, holding what appears to be wine, as the sounds of “Callas Went Away” begins to come from the jukebox, obviously an entire CD put into play. “Ah, Prince Turren, the rumors are true that you have become the future King's shield man. A good pick, I have watched him for some time. Since your uncle left the courts to raise him as a whelp. He was always an honorable man to a fault.” “Blaise, his human name, is a loyal man. And he's been trained by Lord Malik himself as a hunter before he took on his new role.” The older Italian man looks up at Blaise. “He has something else about him. An aura, an aire if you will. Yes... his testing for his new job entailed doing something that brings a pain to my dark, black heart as well as convinces me that he has done well to my kind. For I can see the ghost of Zadikiels Sword slung across his back, for he has claimed it as his own, and merged it with his spirit. He was one of my brothers.” Blaise looks down at him. “I'm sorry then. He was a good opponent.” “I'm not sorry, he was jealous of me and tried to manipulate things for his own to try to get what I have done over the last few millenia. So, Prince Bres, what shall we discuss first?” Sean grabs the glass that had been provided for him and takes a drink from it. He then puts it at arms length and smiles. “Ah, good Irish made. Ye flatter me.” “I'm glad you approve. Now, let's talk of conspiracies, first. I like them best. I have been paid in good gold from the Order. So let's get to business. There are many who are moving to stop this. We have some of the Olypians pissed off over your lovely wife. Some in your own kind are not to happy with you potentially getting back on the throne, and we'll not forget that there are a few in the Black Lodge who are so against your brother-in-law now, that they'll sell their souls to beings like... me, that they'll try to get things. It's all falling into place. War will come soon.” Sean takes another sip and then starts to ask questions. “The Keeper wishes to know what you have on the Third Primal... you are an ally of Cain. Where is the stance on the Spear Bearer?” “Longinus is a threat now. He's no longer a weak being. He's made deals that we are still trying to figure out. Rumor has it that he's the one who found the essence of Aphrodite and made away with it. He's also taken out his own fair share of angels and demons. He's not unlike either of the other two now. His power grows, and he's making war, not conspiracy. He wants open war, all around the world. There are some that even speculate that he's been behind many of the worlds issues, spreading money, hatred and discontent in the world. The day someplace like the United States uses it's first nuke, is the day that the three Primals will finally see who will be the one to make paradise.” As the two talk, Blaise feels the hair on the back of his neck suddenly stand up on end. He starts to look around, starting to get wary. “Who is he in league with?” “Everyone who is against either of the other two. He has whole worlds under him. Whole countries. Parts of the Vatican venerate him as a Saint. Some in my old home have begun to fall for his money, and others have come to seek him out because both of the other sides are distasteful to them. Most have a problem working for either a Vampire or a Vampyl. I've heard that his ilk have even been in contact with the brother of your former betrothed. He's still bitter that he'll never be the brother to a queen.” “Aine is fully in support of the Order, and she and I had our agreement. That's why I didn't fight too much. I had to allow them to test Patti. She passed, and now I'm happy and the other prophesies are coming true. The Olypians are now allies of the Order. Those that aren't jealous.” “So you know about Hepheastus.” “I've guessed. The old legends hold that he took his forced marriage to Aphrodite serious, even though she'd refuse to touch him. She was given to him by Zues as punishment to all the others who pined for her. Minos didn't want to repeat his mistakes.” “No, but instead he let an outsider take her. But one that the Greeks would have seen as a means of treaty. Something they almost invented. But treaties have been broken by idiots before. Look at what happened in that walled city in Turkey.” “Troy...” Blaise whispers that last comment as his hair really begins to stand up. “Yes, Troy...” Asmoday looks up at Blaise and then hesitates. “What's wrong?” Blaise reacts before he can speak to answer the question. He grabs Sean by his collar and pulls the man up and out of his chair. He sends him sailing backwards and to the side, towards a different grouping of tables. Blaise falls backwards and then rolls as the front door and wall comes flying into the building. The two at the billards table move in on the lone figure that comes in, but in a speed that's not visible, they're both dispatched, you only see them fall. Blaise loooks on as the figure, dark, forboding... the space around him blurred as reality tries to decide what it's doing where he is. An effect he's only seen limited around the likes of Korwyn Malik, an ancient man. This man's form tried to decide if it was wearing an expensive suit or that of the armor of a roman legionaire. Space rippled about him as he stepped, what appearned to be purple lightling occasionally arced from him, striking things as he walked by. Blaise reaches to his lower back area and brings forth two matching pistols. He capps off a few rounds in the direction of the disturbance. The man just raises his hand and the bullets stop in mid air, not unlike the hokey stuff that was in the Matrix. Only Blaise is watching it right now. Although Blaise then gets back a big surprise as the ripples of the veil that surround this man then shift, and the bullets he just sent int hat direction, suddenly go whizzing past him, one of them grazing his shoulder as reality decided it was going to work against him at this time. Blaise drops the pistols and grabs the now bleeding shoulder. The disturbance walks over to Asmoday, who appears to be just sitting there, drinking his wine. “You're not welcome here. Go away. You're mere presence will attract things that should not be.” A deep, booming voice answers back in what sounds like Latin, English, hell all launguages at the same time. “You have spoken enough of your lies to all. It's time for you to join your brothers and sisters in their eternal slumber. Only through your new salvation can you be puified. I offer even you, one who fell with the Morning Star, your absolution and entrerance into Heaven once I'm done.” “I like it down here now. I've made myself my own little kingdome that spreads from Tehran to Rome. As I said, you're not welcome here. You're not one of the Primals, you're a joke of an Immortal from a God who learned his lesson on the first two.” Blaise watches as the legionaire side of this being pulls his sword. The image shifts as he comes back to the more modern look, then shifts back as the sword is pulled back. He pulls back, swings and the sword is stopped as the wooden table suddenly grows to stop it cold, roots start to form and branches, taking a grip around the sword. Blaise hears his cousin chanting slightly, recognizing the magic of those from Tir Na Nog, the Isle of Ireland at play. The being appears to be fristrated as he rips his sword out of it's holder, the wall of plant material that now blocks him from his prey. “Blaise, get 'im ou' o' there.” Blaise reaches in and pulls Asmoday out by the chair he was sitting in, just in time as the legionaire's sword crashes down and breaks the weak wood to splinters that his cousin was using. The magic of the Tir could only grow the wood, but not make it more then it was normally. The being swings his arm, and the rest of the makeshift wall is shattered. He steps in, pulling the sword into a blind preparation, just ready to swing. Blaise reacts and pulls the sword that he keeps hidden inside his own version of a Veil Rift.... The legionaire's sword is stopped cold in the air, by the sword of the now former Archangel Zadikiel, the trophy Blaise claimed when he took him out in his trials. The being leans in, to took Blaise in the eyes as they both try to over power the other one in strength. As he does, the air around Blaise also begins to ripple as the veil is pulled back... The being receeds some when he's greeted by not the face that he thought was there.. The ruddy cheeked, flame red haired Irish-Amerindian man... but that of a beast with white fur, vertical slit in it's green eyes and a maw on it that could tear a man to shreds... holding onto the large great sword of an archangel in all of it's flaming glory. “What is this? You're... no... it can't be.” Asmoday looks on in shock as the New Primal pulls away. The veil around Blaise recedes back to normal, showing him at the guard with the metal, real world variant of the prize sword. “You are unexpected... the half breed. If you're here, then Ishram's men are.... very unexpected. I don't like changing my plans. I don't like it when new variables are thrown into my equations. Maybe next time Asmoday, maybe next time False King, Prince of Demons. Tell my surrogate brother Cain that I said that I'm looking for the day when he and his nephew can meet, so that I can be there.....” He pulls back some, sheathing his sword, and pulling the disturbance around him in some so that he appears to look normal. Sean steps out from where he had landed. “Ah yes, the Fomorian King. Prove to me that my enemies are allies why don't you? Good, gives me fuel for the fight. But No, I cam prepared for the Demon Prince, not two princes of Tir Na Nog. Next time, I'll be must more ready for surprises such as this.” And he truns around, towards the door... “Halfbreed, I'll give your regards to the mother of your children for you...” And with that, he then and walks out... Blaise turns to go after him, but Sean stops him. “No... no' one on one, na' riogh' now. Ye may 'ave surprised 'im, 'ell ye surpised me, an' I even saw yer trials meself. Bu' next time, 'ell be ready.” Asmoday stands up from his chair and grabs Blaise by both shoulders. “While I'm allied with your enemy, you Irish Prince and bearer of the spirit of the lost Tribe, you have my regard and my word with you. You are owed a big favor for me, a life debt.” “Who was that?” Asmoday smiles at the apparent ignorance of Blaise in this matter. “Ignorance truly is bliss, you might hot have pulled your sword had you have known. That was the man once known as Cassius Longinus... the Roman Solider who gave us the Spear of Destiny, which I stole from him almost two millenia ago and have used to make rulers and kings in this day and age... Constantine, Charlemange, Napoleon, Hitler... all have aquired his old artefact, and used it for greatness... that is, until one of the holy knights of your Order took it and hid it away from all. The sad thing is, while I know where it is, he'll not get it. But he knows that it's the secret to getting what he wants. You though, you saved me and your cousin from him. Thank you, young prince.” “No problem. Just doing my job.” At that, we fade out here.
