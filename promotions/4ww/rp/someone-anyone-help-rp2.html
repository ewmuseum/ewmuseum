Someone, Anyone.. HELP!!! (Rp2) 'Insane' Chris West 4WW Exposures #129 Date: Sept 13th 2003 Location: 4WW Arena [Everyone had a common Goal in this Sport, its hard to make friends, yet keep the friends that are hard to Make. I’ve done nothing but been a Asshole, and kicked everyone’s ass here in the 4WW.. I haven’t had time to make a friend.. Someone to help me out when I’m down. El Lobo has a Friend. His Name is Psymon, the Man, who wants to fuck me up. Without Backup, I’m fucked. I need to find backup] [The Camera Turns on, as you see “Insane” Chris West. Who’s Forehead is still taped up for the Match with Psymon. He is wearing a “El Lobo” T-Shirt, and his Trademarked blood Stained Blue Jeans. He has the South Wind Championship around his Waist. He’s Walking down the Hall ways of the 4WW Arena Looking for someone.. Someone to help him out.. He Stops and turns to the Camera, and Starts to talk] Insane: There is only two things the problem could be. One, I Haven’t been here long enough to earn the respect from anyone on the roster. No One, Here wants to Work with me. I’m Unstable, No One trusts me. The Other Reason is, no One Likes me. they don’t like the fact, I can Kick there asses. Who knows, and Who cares what the reason is. I’ve made the Phone Calls, I’m emailed people.. No one is answering. I’ve Left Messages.. Nothing.. El Lobo, Psymon. I’m All alone, you are getting the chance of a Lifetime to Finnish me off once and for all.. FUCK. [West looks down at the South Wind Championship.. As its coming into his Mind, that he could be Losing the title on Stormfront. Losing it to El Lobo.. West Starts to walk down more Hallways and Look into more doors looking for someone, Anyone to Help him out.. As he Walks into the Parking Lot, as he see’s “Holy Shit” Jason Bird on the Cell Phone Talking to someone. A Big Smile comes across West’s Face.. Bird gets off the Phone] West: BUDDY.. BIRD.. DID THE ORDER GO THROW?? Bird: He Said, if he can get the truck here by Show Time, He’ll Show up. If, the truck doesn’t make it. Then he doesn’t make it.. But, he said he’s going to try. [West Stops and Thinks, as he Looks bird right in the Eyes. As the Disparate West Starts to talk] West: Damm, You used to be a Great Lightweight Wrestler. You Didn’t do Much Hardcore, hell it was Hardcore that Ended you’re Career, but you used to be a Great Worker. Bird: Hmm, Thanks.. What are you thinking about?? West: Dude, It was Hardcore that Ended You’re Career.. Hell, I ended You’re Career, Now, I need You Back.. I need you to Suit up one last time, and Help this Old Hardcore Legend Out. [Bird gets A Worried Look on his Face. He Doesn’t Want to do it, and he doesn’t know how to Answer West, without getting his Asskicked] West: Jayson.. Look at me.. You Know Me. If, You where in my shoes I would do the same thing, I Would risk it Just one time, to Help out a Old Friend. Bird: But.. West: NO FUCKING BUTS!! El LOBO is a Lightweight.. Lucha fucker just like You... He’s going to have his Friend Psymon involed in this Match.. I NEED YOU TO Come out of Retirement for one last Time.. PLEASE.. DUDE.. Don’t Make me Beg.. I NEED YOU TO DO MORE THEN JUST STAND THERE.. I NEED you to get involed.. I need you to Bump and Hurt people.. Don’t Make my Beg. Bird: FINE... West: THANKS.. You’re A Great Friend.. Don’t Worry.. I’ve been Making Phone Calls, Leaving Messages, and Emailing Everyone I know.. I need to Save this title, I can’t lose it. BIRD, YOU WON’T LET THEM TAKE MY TITLE WILL YOU.. Bird: Dude, Don’t Worry, I’ll do the Best I can.. [West Walks Off, as The Camers Stays on Bird, who has a Worried Look on his Face.. He doesn’t know what is in Store for Them. Bird Looks into the Camera] Bird: When this Man gets Desperate.. Who knows WHAT THE FUCK Could Happen. He doesn’t get this way Offen. I Just Pray to god, I don’t fuck up my Neck Again.. [The Camera Fades to Black] [I Can’t Lose this title. Its my One Way ticket to Superstardom. Its my Ticket to being someone here in 4WW. I can’t Lose this title.. I Can’t Lose it El Lobo, Cause I was cheated out of the title by Psymon. I Got to do everything I can to Counter act Psymon, and El Lobo. At this Point, I would Even Make a Deal.. With Anyone....]
