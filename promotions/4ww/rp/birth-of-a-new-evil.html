Birth of a New Evil Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #356 Date: 01/12/03 Location: The Past & A Car [the past ~ 19/10/66 ~ alberta, canada] At first, it's so dark. Nothing except that bleakness, though his eyes are open, they do not see the light. Completely dumbfounded as to the situation at hand he finds himself embarking upon a life altering mission. No longer will he see only darkness, but soon if all goes well, he will see the light. Hurridly he attempts to breakhimself free of the constraint that he has found himself. His arms push against the walls in an effort to push himself down, nearer to the exit. For in his mind, there is but one goal, to be free. Desperately he fights with every breath in his body, he clenches his fists as he is unable to get any closer to his objective. As far as he could remember he had been confined for several months. Knowing that his struggle has only been in vain he remains consumed by the darkness. 'Siefer honey, come here.' The voice of heaven itself could not be sweeter. 'What is it that attracts the dirt to you?' She moistens a cloth with her perfect lips and presses it gentley against a boys face. The boy, Siefer, had only known life for seven years when this day came to pass. Though he loved his mother, he knew something terrible. His eyes would never allow him to forget what they had seen. Everything he loved about this woman, her smile, her touch, her voice, he knew he was not the only one to love those things. Of course there was his father. 'Boy, I've never seen someone as messy as you.' Siefer turned to the door to see his father standing with his jacket on his arm and a small case in his hand. The man smiled and opened his arms as he embraced his only son. Placing his hands on the boy's shoulders he smiled and kissed him on the cheek. It seemed that nothing could go wrong with this existence. The world was flawless in the eyes of a child. 'Hello sweetheart, how was your day?' Siefer remembered the look in her eyes, that day he would never forget that look, for it was that look that taught him the meaning of guilt. His father set his case down and moved to the table where his beloved wife sat. The struggle, the urge to be free, all of it in vain. For the confined walls that imprisioned this being could not be penetrated. However, he knew a day would come when he would be free and that day would bring a fierce reckoning with him. Hell on earth would be an understatement, but imagine the most powerful weapon in the world being placed into the hands of the unexpected. Of course a person in general can be smart, but humanity as a whole is easily threatened and quick to make decisions. When he got free, he knew the world would change, after all, it was in his destiny. He could remember spending countless hours kicking, hoping he'd find a place of penetration, instead all he found was darkness and another wall. To him, hell was this place, solitary confinement. A small, dark hole that allowed no entrance and no exit, or atleast not to his knowledge. Now, he could remember spending the worst time of his life in this place, but that was all he could remember. As his father sat down he to noticed the lack of confidence in his wife's eyes. He leaned towards the woman he loved and asked her one question: 'What's wrong?' From that point, Siefer could only watch as the outcome slowly unfolded before his eyes. Earlier that day he had spent alot of time in a small room, normally a child of his age would be screaming for attention, only this room was filled with toys, books and other children. As he mingled amoungst his peers Siefer remembered seeing his mother talking to a man. Rather rugid, but in a handsome way the man placed his hand on her shoulder and smiled as he spoke. At first Siefer's mother responded with joy, then her joy quickly faded as she caught glimpse of her son's eye. The same eyes that she now found herself staring into as his father tried to find out what was happening to his wife. 'Look, I do have something to tell you. Before you say anything, I want you to know, I will always love you.' Bewilderment etched itself upon the face of Siefer's father as he leaned back embracing himself for only the worst news imaginable. Siefer tried to interject into the conversation, though his attempt was exploited as he was sent out of the room. Siefer peered around the corner only to see his mother with her face in her hands and his father with his back turned to her, 'I don't know how this could have happened, but it did.' Her husband spun around and looked directly at her, his eyes were that of comfort rather than anger, his face showed sympathy and compassion. Siefer was confused, all he could understand, was that simply something earth shattering had happened and his father embraced it with all the love in the world. Time passed, as it has a tendancy of doing and with each passing day, each passing minute, he knew had to get free from this isolation. He knew there would be no way he could take anymore of this punnishment, he longed to be free. Not only that, he felt as though he was changing, like a catepillar going into metamorphesis and becoming a butterfly. He knew when he finally got out he would not know what to expect, but he did know that he was not what he used to be and it was all because of this time of inprisonment. 'Siefer, son...' His father started as he looked into Siefer's eyes. 'Sometimes in life things happen that we don't expect. Sometimes its a bad thing and sometimes it's a good thing.' Siefer could remember his mother's loving touch at one side and his father's encouraging voice on the other. He looked deep into his facer's face and there he saw a reflection of the man he wanted to become. 'Son, your mother and I wanted to tell you that things are going to be changing around here. Lord knows that everything was fine before, but sometimes we are blessed and this is one of those times.' He swore that one day, when he was free he would change the world. He knew deep inside of him that he was meant to be special, if only he could be free of this torment. All he knew was the months that he had spent in this hellish prison. Hope kept him alive, hope of living up to what he knew he would become. Sometimes things happened for a reason, be it good or bad and this was one of those times. He knew he had to get free, in his freedom he would become something no other would... [4ww Headoffices ~ 23:04] 'There it is...' The lights illuminate the darkness. Like a first born opening his eyes for the first time, a new world is seen for the first time. His eyes widen with anticipation and for some reason there is a will to cry. Instead, he holds it all back, the world is full of babies. It had been a long time since he felt this new to a place. He groggily wipes his eyes and stares at the massive building before him. Everything, his past, his present and even his future will lie within these confines. There would always be the familiar faces in the crowd, Chris West, Slade, Fnord, Todd Dream and on. These people had all encountered the Angel in one manner or another at one point in their lives, to them, deep inside their souls, they believe the myth. In Chris West there has always been an unstable relationship. At best, as rookies in the SCWF, West and the Angel had managed to upset the recently dethroned Superior Champion Todd Dream and the current Network Champion Heretic and the worst? The days of the GWA promoted a match which carried more than a championship, it carried and held the career of Dark Angel. West would challenge the undefeated US champion Dark Angel. Darek was then forced to put his career on the line in this hardcore United States Championship match. Then there is Slade, The Ladies Man, there has always been a connection between the Angel and Slade. There have been many speculations as to the extent of the depth and complication in the history of these two men. Fnord had always been a loose canon, firing his mouth at whomever he chose, whenever he chose to do so. Darek could remember the days when he visioned Fnord at the top of his game and those days only brought two words to mind: Lynch Mob. Today, though still a formidable opponent Fnord seems to have lost his edge. Finally, the Dream... Darek had always dreamed of becoming a great and proving leader. His style, his attitude, his desire, these attributes fought endlessly in the SCWF to help him become someone more than 'the rookie'. He could remember battling to be something more than nothing, the SCWF was a dog eat dog federation. It all began to change when the four warriors stood face to face, Darek, West, Dream and Heretic, the world... and now the Dream is just a nightmare away! As it was said, the past does reside here, but the past can be forgotten and some things will never change. Once upon a time, a long time ago, fate had given the world the destructor. It was at this time that a family found themselves staring down possible hardship that became joy. A woman had become pregnant with a second child. As she found herself talking to the doctor, her son caught a look of her joy and discouragement. She knew that it would be hard to tell the boys father. When she had gotten home, could only recall sitting at the table with guilt written in her eyes. Eventually she explained everything, and not knowing what to expect from her husband, she did not expect him to welcome the news with such happiness. That day, the world accepted the truth as something more than human was about to be brought into the world. Long ago, this being, the Angel was confined and now he is free to roam the earth. In his travels he found many places and even love, but there was only one place he called home and in this home he was truly the king, he was something more than human. He was a monster who sat atop a throne built on the bodies of fallen warriors. A being who built his legacy with the souls of his victims. In this world, there was only pain and suffering. It's a pity that the same fate lingers in for this new world. Little does it know that a demon is about to be born into their world, a demon that noone will ever forget. '4ww, I have come to this place in search of revenge, a home and a future. I have hopes that this place will give me all of the affirmentioned. I have long awaited a return to the wrestling world. Over the past year I have found myself thinking of what I really am without pain and punnishment as a part of my life. I have spent the best years of my life breaking bodies and damning people to the outter reaches of hell. I have been burned, beatten, attacked but never will I be destroyed. You can say what you will about me, you can call me washed up and even old. But the truth is, people who know me, they are the ones staying back holding their feces from leaving their bladders. I am the only incarnation of death, I am the only thing in this federation close to a God and when I bring forth my righteous hand, each of you will feel the love of a true ruler and king. One by one, your warriors will fall, all this will be written as my legacy. There are many in this world who will jump at the opportunity to teach me a lesson, this I know. In that lesson I can only offer you one promise... PAIN! Afterall, I am the one and only Idol, I am the Angel and I am the word of Final Testament.'
