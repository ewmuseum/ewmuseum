A New Patti Anderson?

27 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A New Patti Anderson? 4WW Exposures #1155 Date: 1/27/05 Location: A German Restaraunt (The scene opens up inside of a diner. Sitting there, with the camera locked square on her face is Luscious Lady Luck. She is wearing a pink halter top. Sitting in front of her is a half eaten salad and a clear stein of a dark German beer, kind isn’t important. There is also another stein, and probably another person, but the camera doesn’t reveal it. LLL looks straight ahead, at whoever this other person is, but not toward the camera at all.) LLL: Wow, I could’ve never imagined there was someone out there who had that much of that it factor. I couldn’t imagine why you haven’t been anywhere important yet. What have you been doing all of these years? Girls Voice: (with an English accent) I’ve been working in London, a ritzier part of London. I work at an upscale winery. We had so many celebs and famous people coming through there. I have been the lead bartender there now for a few years. (LLL couldn’t help but think that she had heard all of this before. It was so familiar. So much like someone else she had discovered and made huge.) LLL: So why did you decide to come to the tryouts? Girls Voice: I’ve seen what your agency has done with so many people. I’ve seen how your agency has made so many people famous. From Patti Anderson-McClancy to Kawanaha Kuno. And modeling has always been a part of my dream. I always knew that I could be so much more. (Names that LLL remembers, some fondly, some not so fondly, but they all had what this girl had. They all had something special. They all had that it factor.) LLL: How long have you been modeling hon? Girls Voice: This is my first time giving it a try. This is my first time that I’ve ever actually gotten up the courage to do something like this. When I heard the 4WW was coming here, I knew I had to meet you. And when I heard you were having open auditions, I knew that I had to get here for it. I spent most of my life savings just to get here for the auditions, and the rest for the bloomers. (Penniless, desperate, attractive, and a star in the making. No modeling experience could be a problem, but then again, Patti had very little.) LLL: Well, I have to ask, what all are you willing to do? Girls Voice: Anything that I need to do. (That’s a good sign.) LLL: Are you going to be loyal to us, or are you out for yourself? Girls Voice: Of course I’ll be loyal to you, as long as you keep me around. (That answer was a little worrisome to LLL, but then again, she had more things to worry about. Like Ukyo.) LLL: So what do you know about me? Girls Voice: What do you mean? LLL: Well, you said you follow 4WW, so what do you know about me? Girls Voice: Well, you’re only the fastest up and coming star in the 4WW. You’re the longest reigning Tempest Champion, and currently the South Winds Champion. In fact, you have a match this week against Ukyo. (Yes, yes LLL did have that match. Can’t mix business with business too much though) LLL: Yeah, I do have that match, but I’m not too worried about it. Ukyo is walking into my match, and I’ll retain my title. But onto other things. I think that you have everything that I am looking for. We are going to have to work on your modeling and give you some classes, but I see no reason we can’t make you a star. In fact, I think you could be a bigger star than Patti and Kawanaha Kuno. What’s your name hon? (The camera starts to pan out and show the girls sitting across from LLL. The girl has red hair. As the camera focuses in on the girl, she has much the same figure as Patti McClancy. The camera continues to focus in on her face, and she shows a striking resemblance to “The Darling of the 4WW.”) Girls Voice: The name, we may have to change, I’d hate for there to be any confusion. My name is Patty Andersen. (LLL was taken aback when she heard the name, but she quickly regains her composure.) LLL: We can easily change the name. (LLL reaches into her purse and pulls out her check book. She starts writing in it. After she is finished writing in it, she tear out a check and hands it to Patty.) LLL: Here is a check for $200,000 American. That should more than cover any expenses you’ll have missed from missing work and travel and such. This will also help pay for your modeling lessons and all. I’d like to officially welcome you to the modeling world. (With that, LLL reaches across the table to shake Patty’s hand, and then kisses her on each cheek. This was going to be the beginning of a beautiful friendship.)
