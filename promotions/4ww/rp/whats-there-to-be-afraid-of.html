What's There to Be Afraid of?

27 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What's There to Be Afraid of? Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #901 Date: 8/27/04 Location: Some night club (The scene opens up inside of a huge party. There is music, there is dancing and there are lights going everywhere. People are drinking, most of them are wearing 4WW paraphernalia, and most of them are having a hell of a time. Sometimes mixed in with everyone you’ll see a random 4WW staff member or wrestler. This is one huge party. The camera pans along to where Luscious Lady Luck is sitting. She is wearing a short pink leather miniskirt and a black see through sweater with a white bra. Her hair is pulled back. She is sitting there with her newly won Tempest Title, the face plate facing toward her, and she is just admiring the craftsmanship of this fine belt.) LLL: I finally have something to complete my award room. I have finally done something worth while here in the major leagues of the 4WW. I have finally made a difference in the realm of professional wrestling. And my name will be up there with the names of the great. My name will now be on the record books as one of the few to have actually held a title……. Granted, lots of people have held this belt. People say this is the bottom feeder’s belt. People say this is where the rookies start. People say this belt means nothing. It’s had more men than Annie has had in a 24 hour period. But it doesn’t matter. It’s mine, and it is going to stay mine for a very long time……. And my title win. It was all for the one person who means more to me in this world than any other person. My title win was for the person that everyone wants, but someday, my love, we shall be together again. My title victory goes to you, darling Patti Anderson…… (Trips picks the belt up, kisses it, and then places it around her tiny little waist, fastening it in the back. She then stand up and starts walking toward the camera.) And what a way to celebrate. Lot’s of fun. Lot’s of dancing. And lot’s of endless entertainment. I wish you were here Patti. I wish you could’ve been here for the celebration. We have all of your fans here. We have several members from the crew here. We have some of the talent here. This is a helluva a party. You’re missing my shining moment my love…. (LLL looks toward the bar tender, and motions him over.) LLL: I want to spare no expense on this party. This is supposed to be the hugest party ever. Drinks and everything are on me. Bartender: Yes ma’am. (The Bartender quickly rushes back to his post as LLL stands up on the bar.) LLL: HEY GUYS!!!! THIS PARTY IS GOING TO BE ONE HAPPENING PARTY!!! DON”T BE SHY, EVERYTHING IS ON ME!!!! (With that, there is a huge whoop and hollering from the crowd. LLL gets down off the bar and makes her way out of the bar.) LLL: Gotta do all this celebrating now. I mean as the Tempest Champion, I don’t get to relax very long. I’m on every card. I’m defending my title every week, no breaks, no rest. Just me and my next opponent. Of course, I do get a break from defending the title. I don’t have to defend it this week. So I will have the belt for at least 14 days…… This week, I have a match with Kyo in my corner, and against my love, Patti Anderson who is teaming with Ice. What an odd occurrence. Instead of being on the same side, we are opposing each other. This doesn’t have to happen dear. This truly can be done differently. Me and you could just go back to the locker room after the match starts and let those too big men fight it out on their own…… I mean let’s be honest here for a moment. Everyone knows that you are just hiding your feelings. You are afraid of what people may think of you if you were to ever truly declare your love for me. You are afraid of what people are going to say and how they would react. But it doesn’t matter. What they think is their own problem. Love is the answer, and you and I both know that love is something that we have each other…… And I know you are worried about Sean. You’re worried about hurting him. You’re worried about how he is going to react if you were to bring me into the relationship. You know that I have never had a problem with having another person in our relationship. We use to bring men in all the time. Just because we use to have so much fun teasing them. And Sean, he doesn’t have to be just about being teased. He is always more than welcome to join us. I’m sure I could teach him a thing or two about he type of things that you enjoy…… Maybe you are worried about being let go by the 4WW. Well, you and I both no that is never going to happen. If it were, they would’ve done it already. They would’ve said goodbye a long time ago, as soon as I came in and let them all know what happened. So that is not going to happen. And besides, I think Mr. Childress has a little bit of a kinky side to him anyway, so he could only be as happy as to see the two of us get together……. (LLL sits on a bench outside the bar. The neon lights are the only thing keeping the scene lit.) Much like the night, my dear, there is very little to be afraid of with us. You are always going to have those bigots and hypocrites who try to tell you how to live your life. You’re always going to have those people who are going to make things tough for you no matter what you do. So forget about them. Forget about what they might say, what they might do. Just plain old forget about them…… We’ll leave Kyo and Ice in the ring. Me and you can go back to the locker room, get ready for a night on the town, I’ll buy a plain ticket, we can have Sean fly in, I’ll treat you all to a night on the town, and then after word, we can go back to my hotel room and you can relive two of the greatest nights in your life at once. The one with Sean and the one with me. (Lady Luck kisses her hand and blows the kiss at the camera as it fades to black.)
