A Lesson in Flowers

13 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Lesson in Flowers Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1102 Date: 12/13/04 Location: Chiao's Flower Shop: New York, NY 11:37 PM [The camera fades into a New York City business. The building looks quite dilapidated, as if the only customers who frequented the store were those of the Middle Ages. The broken neon sign is only halfway lit, leaving the only recognizable word to be Flowers. The store is only about 10 yards long and the only flowers in the window sill seem to be long since dead. Rustin Slade is seen peering into the windows. He walks over to the door. Noticing the “CLOSED” sign, Rustin slowly shakes his head. He raps on the door window. The camera catches some unknown movement in the store and soon an old chinese man, who looks even more ancient than the store comes to the window and gives Rustin a smile. A minute later the door opens.] Old Man: Oh! Mistah Slade! I trust gift has... Rustin: Save it Mr. Chiao. It didn’t work.. She still doesn’t notice me. Old Man: Oh Mistah Slade. You can’t expect flower to do all work. Rustin: Look Mr. Chiao. I appreciate your help with the selection of the flowers, but I don’t think Chastity is going to go for it. Old Man: Mistah Slade. What happen exactly with referee lady? Rustin: That’s just the thing, nothing happened. I mean I left the rose for her, but I didn’t say hello to her. I didn’t introduce myself. She refereed my match, and I told her I was going to win the match for her. I was going to be the new Tempest champion and beat Paul Williams. I lost! I failed her! Old Man: Mistah Slade, she is still in dark. She does not know you are admirer. You must tell her. [Rustin shakes his head. It seems whenever he opens his mouth to talk to a girl, that’s when the trouble starts. Although Chiao had a point. Rustin never did tell her he was her secret admirer. Rustin looks back into his head and remembers the smile on Chastity’s face. Then Rustin slowly brings a smile to his own face.] Rustin (to himself): She was happy. Maybe I have a chance. I can’t screw it up. [Mr. Chiao hearing the last bit of what Rustin said nods his head. He then heads towards the back of the store. Mr. Chiao stops at a certain potted plant and then waves Rustin over. Rustin slowly follows. He also stops at the plant. The camera zooms in on the flower that is in the pot. The orange petals shine brightly due to the beams giving off by the moon. The old man picks up the container and holds the plant in his hand.] Old Man: You see...fine woman like flower here. [Rustin eyes the plant suspiciously. He tries to make an attempt to grab the plant from Mr. Chiao but Chiao is quick to see this coming and holds it away from Rustin. Rustin seems to get a little annoyed.] Old Man: You see? You grab quickly. Flower not ready for you! Rustin: [Gives Chiao a quizzical look] Huh? Old Man: Flower blooms best when care given to it. Flower do not take roughness. Rustin: So what you’re saying is... Old Man: Give to get. Flower needs nurture. [Rustin begins to think about what Chiao has said. He suddenly smiles and thinks it has all come together. He gives Chiao a quick pat on the back, which almost knocks him over. Shaken and stirred, Mr. Chiao offers a near toothless grin. Rustin reaches into his back pocket and takes out his billfold.] Rustin: How much do I owe you? Old Man: Advice free. Rustin: I couldn’t. Old Man: Get out of here. You remember advice. Rustin: Oh I’ll remember it. Thanks Mr. Chiao. For everything. [Rustin goes for another pat, but Mr. Chiao throws his hands up causing Rustin to stop. Mr. Chiao offers another near toothless grin. Chiao strokes his Fu Man Chu] Old Man: Chiao knows he appreciated. No thanks necessary. Rustin: All right then. [Rustin smiles and heads for the door] Thanks. Old Man: [Unintelligible Chinese curses as he rubs his back where Rustin patted him earlier. Chiao now looks at the plant in his hand.] Well old friend, we do well tonight, hmmm? [Chiao puts the flower back down on the counter and as soon as he does notices that it is wilting.] He really does have bad luck. He doomed. [Chiao picks up the potted plant and throws it into the garbage. Chiao shakes his head and goes back into the rear of the shop as the camera fades to black.]
