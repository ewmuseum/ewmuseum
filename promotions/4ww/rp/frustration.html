Frustration Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #890 Date: 8-23-04 Location: Okinawa, Japan **{sorry this is so shity, i got burned out on Jen and TOny... lol, next week my rps will be back to normal the way they were against Tony an Jen... this is just a journal entry rp to give insight into Kanton's thoughts)** My friend, Three losses... three losses in a row. Why? What am I doing wrong? I was so close... I was almost Northwind Champion. I was almost Southwind Champion. Hell, I was even close to beating one of those bastard McClanceys. How could I come so close yet lose it all? I need guidance... With that an idea has come to me... I am in Okinawa already... and I haven't paid my old master a visit. I don't have to head to the next city yet either. Fujiwa-san would be a great sight for sore eyes. I haven't seen my master in a few years. Maybe I'll even run into Jin. Those memories of meditation and practice are flooding my mind. Maybe that is what I need. Maybe I need some insight and guidance. Maybe I just need to return to my old home. I cannot keep coming up short... this next match is a turning point. Should I lose, then maybe it's the beginning of the end. Should I win, finally I will be turning around. I cannot be known as the one who comes close and does not succeed. I need to turn things around... I just hope Fujiwa-San can give me the guidance I need. My old master... I am returning. Sincerely, Kanton
