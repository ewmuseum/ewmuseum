That's Amore

24 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's Amore 4WW Exposures #1184 Date: 2/24/05 Location: Venice (The scene opens up inside of a Gondola in the streams of Venice. Sitting in the boat are Luscious Lady Luck and Patty Andersen. LLL is wearing a pink dress and Patty is wearing a purple dress. They are both sitting in the Gondola and looking off to the sides of the more picturesque areas of Venice. Patty has a down look on her face however.) LLL: What’s wrong hon? Patty: I don’t know. LLL: Something has got to be wrong. (Patty turns her head toward LLL.) Patty: I feel like I’ve become a distraction to you. LLL: How do you mean? Patty: Well, you lost your title after you hired me, and now you seem to be a little unfocused on your wrestling. LLL: Nonsense. I lost my title because of that bastard Blaise. It isn’t his brother who should be nicknamed the Irish Bastard. It should be him. Patty: I know but still, I think that I should leave. Besides, I want to get a chance to see my family. LLL: I understand wanting to see your family. I understand how you feel about missing your family. Patty: Do you/ LLL: Of course I do. And I’m going to give you a chance to see your family. (Patty gets a quizzical look on her face.) LLL: Ever since your first photo shoot last week, I’ve got nothing but great responses from the people back in America. I’ve gotten great responses from everyone. They are singing that Dean Martin song, if you know what I mean. You know the one. (Singing) When the moon hits your eyes like a big pizza pie that’s amore. Patty: That’s great, but how is that going to allow me to see my family? LLL: Well, I’m going to pay for you to go back to your family for a few weeks while the 4WW is finishing up its European Tour. I’m going to give you the money you need to go back, and enough to treat your family really nice and really good for a few weeks while we finish up here, and while we get your visas in order to come back to America. Patty: America? LLL: Yes, America. I’ve got my people working with some different agencies. Your hot over there right now. Everyone wants you to model for them. Everyone wants you for everything over there. Patty: Really? They really want me? LLL: I told you hon, your going to be a big star. And it’s just now starting. You are going to be such a huge star. You are going to have so much popularity, fame, money; you won’t know what to do with yourself. Your going to make it big. Patty: Wow, I can’t believe this. I can’t believe that I am actually going to become something. LLL: Did you ever doubt it? I told you you would, and now you are. Everything is so perfect. You’ll get a few weeks off; you won’t have to worry about distracting me. You’ll be with your family. And you’ll be making enough that you’ll probably never have to worry about money again. I’ve talked to my people back in the states. They are getting a place all ready for you. (LLL hands Patty a slip of paper.) LLL: Here is the number to my people back in the states. Don’t worry about costs. I’ll take care of long distance. Just call them if you need them to make sure there is anything in the apartment for you or anything else that you may need. Patty: Thank you so much. I can’t believe this is happening. (Patty throws her arms around LLL, who returns the hug.) LLL: Don’t worry about it hon. You deserve it. You definitely deserve it. Now I’ve got to get ready for my match with Luke this week, and figure out exactly how to deal with the commissioner situation. I hope you have a great time in England with your family. (The Gondola meets the port and Patty and LLL both disembark.) Patty: I will. Don’t worry I will. (Scene fades to black)
