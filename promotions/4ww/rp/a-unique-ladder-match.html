A Unique Ladder Match Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #57 Date: Saturday, July 19, 2003 Location: Spare Bedroom at the Branch Crazy- noun - one who is or act crazy, mad, insane; being out of the ordinary. Outside of our resident psycho Psymon we have a man who is competing in a match for the other qualifying spot in the tournment this week. Pajaro de Presa, the bird of pray. A man who has been impressive in the past. He's competed in tables and ladders match against two of the best in the business. Psymon and El Lobo de la Muerta. Destiny has dubbed him with a new name, trelos nothos, the crazy bastard. How about his opponent? Jamie? Hmmm don't think Destiny could come up with something to call this one. Jamie seems to have this weak stomach disorder that keeps him throwing up. Is he a wanna be bulimic? Or is it just the stress getting to him each and every time that he has to compete. Destiny is praying that Jamie doesn't decided to throw up all over our current heroine in the ring should he be the one that advances. What's this all for you may ask? Why its for the gimmick title, the 'NightMare' title, the South Wind Championship. A title that must be defended each and every show. The match? A Ladder Match. The illustrious championship belt is suspended high above the ring. The only way to claim ownership of the belt is to 'climb the ladder to success'. Match over. Who does Destiny want? Silly question.....Patti. Who doesn't want Patti to win? Another silly question. Fey. This is just another match that Fey has come up with to humiliate Patti, make her suffer the torment of her hatred. A match that Fey is hoping her rival gets hurt. Patti could suprise her and pull out a suprise in the head. Who knows where this one will go. But we will know soon enough. ___________________________________________________________ Saturday Evening July 19, 2003 Spare Bedroom at the Ranch ___________________________________________________________ Fade into Patti and Sean working on painting the room. Patti is on top of a ladder painting a moon and stars type design on the ceiling. Sean is paiting a skyline scene along the walls. Both are dressed in clothing decorated with splotches of blues and blacks. A little streak of yellow paint is seen on the white gauze wrapping on Patti's hand. Her fingers still a little bruised from the encounter with the brick wall after Thursday's Tempest show. The room has taken on an almost romantic feel to it, a cozy 'honeymoon feel' to it. Patti stops long enough to roll her neck a little to the right and then back to the left. The muscles in her neck and upper back causing a minor discomfort. She should have taken Sean up on the offer of having two ladders and a board set between them. At least she could have been on her back to finish up the painting. Sean steps away from the wall and looks at his work. The skyline looked perfect, but the trees, forget it. They looked more like something he had drawn as a child. He looked up above him and saw the sky that Patti was working on. He noticed her tongue was peeking out the side of her mouth. She must be in deep concentration for her to have that look. A moment of mischief seemed to set in on him. She had just pelted him the day before with a water balloon. Now it was her turn. He needed to get her prepared for her upcoming match and ladder match on Sunday. He knew from past experience the ladder match would be brutal, and he knew that her back may get hurt. He walked slowly over to the ladder, checking to make sure that Patti didn't notice what he was doing. He took his shoulder and rammed into it. Patti wobbled on the top run of the ladder. Patti: What the bloody hell do ya think yer doin'? The yellow she was using to fill in the middle of the star ran into the dark blue of the sky. She quickly dropped the paint pallet and brush she was using became discarded to the side. She took her sneaker covered foot and kicked at Sean. His experience in these matches came full forward. He moved out of the way as Patti aimed a second time. He hit the ladder again. This time using his hands to shake it. Patti started to lose her balance again. She waited until the ladder steaded itself. She took a step down, removed the straps to her overhalls. Her baby doll tshirt came off in one simple movement. Her zipper front bra became the weapon of choice. She unzipped it, worked it to where her cleavage could be viewed. She stepped down another couple steps, her ample bosom, calling out for Sean to take a sneaky peak. Like a fool, Sean goes in for the close up look of what is being offered. His eyes stare at the offered bounty. Patti waits til he's within a few more inches. She waits until his attention is fully upon her bosom. Her knee comes up and squarely hits Sean in the solarplexes. He falls overbackwords, the wind briefly knocked out of him. He watches from his prone position on the floor. He notices Patti climbs to the second rung from the top. She doesnt turn around to see where her opponent is. He knew she was going for her Apollo's rising. The moonsault that she perfected after a few tries. He stood up and waited. Patti did a perfect flip in the air. Her form and grace just perfect. He waited until she was within arms reach. She flipped right into his waiting arms. Her upper body hanging to the south, her lower body facing north. Sean's boyish grin appeared. Patti: Put me down ya bloody oaf. Patti starts trying to kick her way out of it. Sean, being more experienced, held her tighter. Patti balled up her fist and hit him with a low blow. Sean grunted and dropped to his knees, taking Patti with him. Patti angled her self to where she landed on top. her bra coming almost off. Sean: Now if ya do that, ye will have no trouble becomin' the new champ. Patti flipped herself around, her head resting on his shoulder. Patti: What ya wanna do now, love? Sean: Oh, I've got a few ideas. Patti: Ye know your trelos, love? His boyish smile appeared. His hands began there familiar roaming over her body. Her hands returned the favor. And as the camera pans in for a closer look, Patti's sports bra lands covering the lens of the camera. ___________________________________________________________ Fade to Black
