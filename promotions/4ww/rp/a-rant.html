A Rant Fnord 4WW Exposures #1090 Date: 12/01/04 Location: ? What happens when the world as you know it comes to an end? What happens when the one thing you have total faith in is found out to be nothing but lies? This is the predicament that Reverend Wesley Williams had just about a month ago. He had lost all of his faith in what was then known as God. He thought that nothing was worth believing in someone that would make his life so fucked up. The funny thing is, in a funny way that was true. He had a right to lose that faith. What he learned, after being possessed by a half-angel/half-demon by the name of Genesis and giving God-like powers, is that God is no longer above us. That’s right; God himself gave up on all of us and fled Heaven, apparently afraid of someone or something. So, not it’s up to Wesley to search for God to find out what the hell is wrong with him. If he has to, with his new God-like powers, Wesley will deal with God with the iron fist he has used all these years with Earth. So, Wesley found out the truth. God is real. Heaven is real. There are angels and demons. It’s a real mind-fuck isn’t it? On this mission or quest, Wesley has a few companions. Cassidy, an honest-to-God real-life vampire has grown to be a great friend to Wesley. Dracula ain’t got shit on him. Who would ever think that there was such a thing as goddamn vampires? Wesley’s seen it and has no excuse to not to believe him. He burns in the sunlight. He drinks human blood. He has no faux-television fangs. He doesn’t sleep in a coffin. He doesn’t have mesmerizing powers making people go against their wills. He’s just one bad motherfucker. And on his right is a lady that’s been with him from before his wrestling career ever started. He’s known her as Dis Cordia, but her memory’s finally been jogged. When her and Wesley met, they were in a mental institution owned by Wesley’s now deceased father. Neither Wesley, then Fnord, nor Dis Cordia new much about their pasts. They had confusing and crazy tactics and they got away with anything they desired. Now Melinda has had her eyes open, shortly after Wesley had his own opened. Melinda, though not completely, has her mind back. She is a sister to Patti and Caitlin from 4WW. Go figure. It must have been fate that brought both her and Wesley to 4WW. Now she is using much of her time in search of opening all the once-locked doors deep within her head. Before long, she will know everything that she needs to know. The question is: will she know too much? Cassidy has gone away on his own for right now, but Melinda is by his side, as always. She is still pissed off about Wesley leaving her way back when, but that is a tale for another time. Just imagine if Fnord and Dis Cordia never came to 4WW. Is that even a possibility? Was it fate that Chris West, a name hated throughout 4WW, went to his most hated enemy from past federations to bring in help? Fnord and Chris West never got along, but something inside of West had told him to bring Fnord to 4WW. And where Fnord was, Dis Cordia was always by his side. Fnord, upon coming to 4WW, was nothing. He got no push. He got no fan appreciation. Not until he started to snap in the ring and hurt people. He used to hurt people purposely just to be liked by the fans. The fans did love him for this. They cheered him over and over as he permanently damaged his opponents over and over. Even other 4WW superstars saw potential in the man known as The Craziest Bastard in 4WW. Fnord was sought out to join the fast growing KnightMare Brigade. Along with Se7en, Draven Lox, and Dark Angel they were sure to be the most dominant team in 4WW. But Fnord decided he didn’t just want to join up. He wanted to really stab Chris West in the back one last time, for old time’s sake. So, Fnord decided to make a group of his own. 4 Random Guy must have seemed like a great idea to the other three superstars that came in contact with him. And on that very night that 4 Random Guys started, it ended. Chris West got the knife twisted at the end of Stormfront when Fnord joined up with the KnightMare Brigade. He has stayed in the KnightMare Brigade the longest in 4WW. Se7en disappeared shortly after Fnord joined. Draven Lox got injured and shortly retired. Dark Angel was brutally kicked out because of actions not like him. Since then, new members have joined. Blaise McClancy, a high member of The Order along with his tag team partner Ukyo Kushinaga have joined. Blaise joined shortly after unmasking himself from his Shoudou persona. Ukyo was his tag team partner, so it was a give-in that she would join. What was a slight surprise, but barely was Kyo Kushinaga joining up with the KnightMare Brigade. I mean it’s understandable that he joined up. His sister is a member… His enemies are our enemies… He holds the North Wind Title, which I’m gunning for… Wait, that don’t make any damn sense! And we can’t forget the new Commissioner of 4WW. After losing to both Kyo and Darek in a match with her North Wind Title, she fled. Well, she didn’t really run away. She left for other things. She got married and all that stuff. Then, when she came back, she took up the mantle of Commissioner. She also joined the KnightMare Brigade because of her new husband, a leading member of the KnightMare Brigade and The Order. It’s funny because the only time I ever got a shot at the North Wind Title was against Patti Anderson and Jerms… and I was screwed out of that match! But that’s the past. The past is just that, the past. But I am a member of the KnightMare Brigade and I will live by the rules. Bell-to-Bell is the way that the KB rolls. We don’t interfere in matches unless cheating is occurring on the other side of the ring. That is a huge change for me. I used to be Fnord. I used to be the man that cheats every single chance he had. When the ref isn’t looking, I would knock out my opponent using a foreign object, anything I could get my hand on. But as I found myself, I found my values. I found that I should be using the Bell-to-Bell rule. That is the right thing to do. So, other than the KnightMare Brigade, there are others that have joined up with one another to make so called ‘stables’ but in reality, they are nothing but jokes. DMC, otherwise known as Diseased Monkey Carcasses are top on that list that is really pissing me off right now. Lucifer and Claudio, with the help of my treacherous uncle Garrett, stole the East Wind Titles from Future Shock and the KnightMare Brigade. Not that they can’t be taken back with great ease… I can see that Lucifer and Claudio are like two different currents. For some reason, right now they are flowing together. Their basic instinct is to flow opposite of each other, yet they flow in the same direction. It doesn’t make sense. Lucifer is a monster and Claudio is a beauty queen, in more ways than one. People look at those two and know that they make about a good a team as Zane Ozz and LLL… Oh wait… That IS another team! First you got LLL who’s greatest achievement is being in a porn video with Patti Anderson and holding the lowest Title in 4WW. That’s right, LLL is proud to hold the Tempest Title FOREVER! You know what that means? That means she’s the best of the losers! Yes! Oh wait, she WAS the best of the losers. Some other loser beat her. Yeah, I know what you’re going to say, she beat me. Well, guess what? I won that match because of a disqualification. I’m not going to make excuses, the Ozz brothers interfered and cost me the Tempest Title. But as I tell everyone that tries to mock me for that match: Sometimes I get paid to ‘do the right thing’ and take the fall. Her partner is another thing. He’s a big guy. He’s a monster. He’s a fuckin’ retard. Without his brother to make it a real team, he’s got nothing. He probably thinks that his brother was holding him down, but in fact, his brother was the talent of the team. I understand that there’s a time in someone’s life that they have to move up in the world. They have to break off from their ‘team’ and try something different. Well, teaming up with LLL, that is a LOT worse than teaming up with your brother. Your brother and you knew each other well enough to play off each other. You and LLL, you two don’t know shit about each other. Which is good. Because that means you will lose. Now, on to what I’m calling as the ‘third wheel’ of each other team. First, you got Slayer. Hmmm… Slayer. Who is this guy again? I heard that some guy named Slayer joined 4WW. I hear he might’ve won like one match or something. Then he disappeared. Wow. Good move recruiting that guy DMC. You did real good getting someone on your team who goes to shows and just stands there like a statue and lets his ass get handed to him. So, DMC has Slayer on his team. Yes! Just more cannon fodder! This does make me quite excited. I mean, why not bring in someone that could get his ass handed to him. Now, lets get to Kanton. He cost me the North Wind Title. He handed Kyo the win in our casket match. He HANDED it to him. I didn’t lose. Remember that. Kris Kanton as far as I knew retired from the wrestling world. Then all of a sudden, he comes back to ruin my match? What the fuck! When did I ever piss that little bastard off? Kris Kanton thinks he’s tough. Kris Kanton thinks he’s North Wind Title material. Hell, Kris Kanton could get beaten by Slayer! What does that say about him!? Kris, you are too far below my league to even begin to THINK about getting in the ring with me. Stay down there with the low-carders where you belong. So, let’s look at the teams over all. Team DMC: Slayer, Lucifer, and Claudio. Sure, they achieved the East Wind Titles by cheating. Sure, Slayer is the worst wrestler in the history of wrestlers. Can they pull of a win in this match? No. Lucifer has size, but not brains behind the size. Claudio has some talent, but again no brains behind what he can produce. Slayer has nothing. It’s plain and simple. This team loses. Team unStable: Kanton, LLL, and Zane. Kris Kanton—full of himself when he has no right to. He has no talent. His finishing maneuver is so simple, Slayer could hit it perfect. Kanton tries to run with the big dogs, but he can’t even get close. LLL is the only woman in this match. Lucky for her. She’s going to get demolished. Who will be there to save her? No one. Who will be there when she gets pummeled? No one. She signed up to be in this match, so she signed up for pain. Last and certainly least, Zane Ozz. The big and stupid Ozz brother. Rumor has it that Landen Ozz ran away because he say too much competition. Maybe his brother should follow in his footsteps. Run Zane Run! Run like the big bitch you are. Team KnightMare Brigade: Wow. That’s all you need is that one word, but I will embellish. Blaise McClancy is not only of the McClancy fame, he has taken down almost everyone that enters in his path. Do you see Dark Angel running around here anywhere? Blaise McClancy took him down. Kyo Kushinaga is our 4WW North Wind Champion. He is a fighting champion and even though some deviances happened, he has won every match that he has entered ever since he won the North Wind Title. And then you have me. Rev. Wesley Williams IV. I am the Number One contender to the North Wind Title. I am in the exact spot that everyone else wants to be in. I am also a wild card. What happens if Fnord emerges in this match? What happens if I let the past creep up and revert back to the wild animal I once was? That’s not the question that should frighten you. The question you should all be afraid of is: What if I don’t?
