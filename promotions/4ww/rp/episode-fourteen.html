Episode Fourteen Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #730 Date: 6/3/04 Location: Location: Above the Sea of Japan Time: Night = [The view was quickly moving above a blanket of white clouds that are floating in the night sky though you noticed a dark figure clad in black flying in the cold night air like a speeding bullet, the figure begin to rise up in the air until reaching the full moon’s light as the light revealed that the figure was no other than the Demon Slayer, Kyo Kushinaga. Kyo just floated in front of the moon with his back turned to it, looking down through the white clouds as he was seeing the Japan continent through his bright orange tint shades.] = “If you all joining the show just now then I should properly introduce myself, I am Kyo Kushinaga but in the wrestling world I use to be known as the 4 Winds Wrestling’s superhero, the Masked Avenger but as you can see I am not wearing a mask anymore even since two StormFronts ago when a sadistic bastard named Tarrant ripped it off my blood cover face and revealed everyone my true identity.” = [Kyo lowers his head a bit causing the light that shines on the lens of his shades to move off them as you now noticed his burning crimson eyes behind them, smirking amusingly.] = “My true identity… Actually, what Tarrant and everyone saw was an incomplete version of me.” “What you see before you is my true identity…” “You see, I used that black metal mask to fit in with you normal people but the only thing I was doing was making myself weaker and weaker with each day I wore that stupid mask, allowing people to walk all over me as well take their shit like I was their goddamn obedient dog!” = [Kyo sharpened his eyes in seriously though cold glare.] = “Well, that is no more... I am now going to do things my own way with a satisfying smirk and not having a single thought of remorse. If they do not like it then they need to get out of my way or avoid me all together and they will not have to feel my fury…” = [Kyo still looked down at the Japan continent through the white clouds, searching for a certain location in the continent. His long burning crimson hair gently moved by the gentle cold breeze.] = “What? You want to know why I am flying around like Neo well after my last encounter with Tarrant my Spiritual Power has increased 10 folds. Actually, that sadistic bastard cannot take all the credit since someone else gave me the power I need to become the person you are seeing right now…” “You asked how you acquire the same power as me…” = [He smirked amusingly while having a sadistic expression on his face as the lens of his shades was shining from the bright moonlight.] = “You must die…” = [Quickly dived down through the clouds in great speeds causing the clouds to swirl around in a slight cyclone for a brief moment from him flying down to the large continent of Japan in great speeds, and loses sight of him within the continent.] = The Visit Location: On the rooftop of Shinjuku Plaza in Shinjuku, Japan Time: Night = [On the large rooftop, all seemed quiet and normal though you quickly noticed Kyo was diving straight down in great speeds before he reached the surface, he did a swift flip and landed on the ground in a kneeling position with his head lowered. Kyo lifted his head up slowly and began to look around his surroundings though his gaze stopped on a pure black crow that was his Spiritual Guardian, Iori, who was sitting on the edge of the rooftop looking at Kyo with its cold brown eyes, emitting a loud crow sound. Kyo closed his eyes behind his shades and chuckled softly once, as he slowly stood up from the ground and made his way over to a door that led to the stairs. The scenery changed to a long hallway with many bright orange color doors on both sides, Kyo stood in front of a door, looking at it cautiously though he glanced over to his Spiritual Guardian that sat on his shoulder for a brief moment before looking at the door tranquilly through his shades.] = Kyo: Welcome home, Kyo Kushinaga… = [He lowered a hand down to the doorknob and slowly turned it, entered inside of the apartment room, closing the door behind him and began to walk further in the room. Iori flew off his shoulder and glide over to the large living room though gently landed on top of the sofa.] = Kyo: Mother? = [Kyo’s vision changed to black and white as he was now seeing what Iori seeing, and Iori was looking into the kitchen while sitting on the sofa. Iori saw a short older woman walking out of the kitchen as Kyo saw the same thing. Kyo blinked once causing his vision to return back to normal, watching the older woman with shoulder length blue hair with traces of gray in it and brown eyes, who was wearing a dark violet kimono, holding a wooden tray in her hands that have two cups and a tea kittle.] = Kyo’s Mother: You’re just in time for some hot herbal tea. = [Kyo smirked when hearing his mother’s words.] = “This is my mother, Ayane Kushinaga, the well-known psychic of Japan, so you can say she has been expecting me…” = [Kyo’s mother, Ayane, placed the wooden tray down on the flat coffee table that was made out of glass, picked up the kittle and began to pour tea into each cup. Kyo just stood there and watched his mother pour tea into the cups, though his gaze stopped upon a bookshelf was full of some books and many family pictures, began to walk over to the shelf as a certain picture caught his eye and moved that picture from the shelf, looking down at it through his shades seriously. Ayane finished pouring the tea into the second cup, placed the kittle down on the tray once more and turned her gaze over to her son, looking at him contently for a moment until noticing him looking down at the picture as she began to walk over to her son, wanting to see which picture grabbed his attention. She stood beside him, looking down at the picture curiously though sharpened her eyes in a slight seriousness when seeing what he was looking at. In the family photo, you could see a younger Ayane with her three children, Ukyo, Josh and Kyo, who were only children when the photo was taken. Ayane looked over to Kyo seriously though slight hint of concern was in her eyes.] = Ayane: Are you mad? = [Kyo gently placed the picture from where he got it from, looking down at the floor through his shades.] = Kyo: You had your reasons for hiding Josh’s true identity to me… = [He walked away from the tall bookshelf and headed over to the sofa where Iori still sat on the top of it, he took a seat on the comfortable sofa and guided his hand over to a cup, picked it up and began to drink the warm tea. Ayane was still standing in front of the bookshelf, looking at a single picture of Josh Starwind when he was a teenager.] = Ayane: When I took you to that Shrine, I looked down at your infant body watching you crying in great pain from feeling the overwhelming power surging through your tiny baby as you wanted me to stop the pain but I could not. I did not want to see you die in my arms from this. = [Kyo stopped drinking the tea for a moment and looked over to her mother, placing the cup down on the tray, standing up from the sofa as he took off his shades, folded them up and placed them in the trench coat’s pocket, walking over to his mother and stood behind her as he placed his hand on her shoulder for comfort, looking down at her with a tranquilly in his burning crimson eyes though it was uncommon for demons to show human emotions although Kyo was a half-demon which made it possible for him to show those type of feelings.] = Kyo: You were just being a mother. Mother, is the word for God to hearts of all children around the world, and that was what you were then and now… = [Ayane closed her eyes as tears slipped pass her closed her eyes, she turned around and faced Kyo, embraced him tightly with her arms while crying in his chest.] = Ayane (crying): I am sorry…Kyo… = [Kyo just looked down at his mother, placed his hand on her back and began to rub her there while embracing her with one arm, looking tranquilly though there was a slight hint of sadness. After a moment, he placed his hands on his mother’s shoulders and gently moved her away from his chest, looking down at her seriously.] = Kyo: You shouldn’t be crying for me, mother, since you are crying for a son who is not your “mitsukai inosento” (innocent angel) anymore. = [He turned his back on his mother and placed his hand in his pocket, taking out his shades and put them on over his eyes, began to head over to the front door. Iori flew off the sofa and passed Ayane, heading over to his leaving master and reaching him it rested on his shoulder.] = Kyo: I am more like a fallen angel better yet an “oujou mitsukai” (death angel)… = [Stepping into the darkness that was in the small hallway that led to the front door of his mother apartment room.] = Kyo: Thanks for the tea. = [Ayane heard his words as she soon listened to the door opened and then closed as she still stood at the same spot, looking forward in great sadness after hearing her son’s words though she looked over to the dark hallway with the same expression.] = Ayane: You’re my “mitsukai inosento”, Kyo… = [More tears began to fall down from her eyes, looking with more sadness than before.] = Ayane: You always be my “mitsukai inosento”… = [She began to cry softly alone in her home.] = Location: Tokyo Tower in Tokyo, Japan Time: Late Night = [At the top of the large tower, a serious almost tranquil Kyo was crouching there, looking down at the large and bright metropolis of Tokyo through his shades while slowly drinking a small canister of sake.] = “You know the sad thing is that she saw this happen in one of her visions. She knew that I was going to come there today and tell her goodbye.” = [Kyo stopped drinking the canister for a moment and emitted a soft sigh pass his lips.] = “I never did envy a person who has the power of sight because I always knew that having this power will cause great pain to that person of course depending on the kind of vision the person has but most of the time it is something very painful that hurts their soul…” = [He began to drink from the small canister once more while his Spirit Guardian, Iori, was sitting below him, looking around its surroundings with its cold brown eyes.] = “You shouldn’t feel sad for my mother. Like I said, she already knew this was going to happen way before my visit this night.” “You all probably see me an inhumane person for making my own mother cry but you must understand. No, what am I saying. How can you incompetent fools even try to comprehend my reason for doing this? But if you really want to know my reason then I shall tell you even though I still think you all won’t understand…” “I did this because…” = [A gentle cold breeze blew causing his long and smooth burning crimson hair and his long leather trench coat to move lightly in the soothing night wind. He lowered his head down a bit causing his bright orange tint shades to move down from a single eye slightly as you noticed that serious burning crimson eye.] = “…of her own protection” = [Kyo smirked amusingly and lifted his head up causing his eye to hide underneath the bright orange tint lens once more as he gone back to looking down at the large city.] = “I knew you all wouldn’t understand which proves why I call you all incompetent fools and I do not feel like explaining myself I mean why should I? It isn’t like I am part of your kind…” = [Kyo stopped drinking his warm sake, screwed the cap on the canister as he put it back within his leather trench coat and slowly stood up from his crouching position, looking down at the city more seriously through his shades as he was getting ready to fly back to the states but before he could he felt a brief vibration coming from one of his trench coat pocket which caused him to make a curious sound while looking down at that pocket. The vibration came to stop as he lowered a hand down in that pocket, taking out his small black cell phone, looking down at it curiously as he pushed a few buttons causing it to reveal that someone left him a couple of in-voice messages on it.] = Kyo: Well, aren’t I the popular guy. It is probably Charlene or Ukyo telling me that I have a match. = [He tilted his head to the side causing the moonlight shine to disappear from the lens of his shades, revealing his seriously though slight curious eyes.] = Kyo: I wonder who will be my first victim… = [Kyo turned on the cell phone, put it near his ear and began to listen to the messages with a straight face while standing on top of the tower. Just then, Kyo lowered his head down causing his long crimson hair to hide his face a bit as his body began to tremble but not fear instead utter thrill as he tilted his head back and began to laugh out a few times in amusement. Iori crowed out from being startled by hearing his laughter as it looked over to him with its brown eyes.] = Kyo: Oh shit! I must’ve done something right in my past life! = [He lowered his head a bit and revealed an excited face about hearing a certain message on his phone.] = Kyo: I get to kill two birds…no more like two vultures with one stone but it is unfortunate that partnered me up with someone who is clearly going to get in my way. = [He sharpened his eyes seriously as an idea had come to mind, smirking amusingly as he closed up his cell phone and put it back in his pocket.] = Kyo: Or will he…? = [He began plotting for something as he just found out that he was in tag match involving the new West Wind Champion, Tarrant, and his North Wind Champion, Patti Anderson as he will be partnered up with the 4 Winds Champion, Jerms, though there was unusually thrilled about having this match. Kyo removed the smirk from his face and looked down at Iori seriously.] = Kyo: Let’s go… = [Kyo slowly began to levitate from the top of the tower as Iori began to fly ahead while Kyo still levitated as he glanced over in the direction where Shinjuku was. He began to think about his mother but turned his gaze back over to Iori who was quite far away from him as he slowly began to follow Iori, flying above the large metropolis.] = “…Goodbye mother…” = […Fade…] =
