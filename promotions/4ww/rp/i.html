I Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1264 Date: Location: IS IT OPEN YET! Rustin: Hey! Rex, keep your voice down. Rex: Rust, dude. Why don’t you just go into the library when it’s open. You know, in the morning? [Rustin and Rex Slade are seen outside the Manchester Public Library. It is sometime near midnight and Rustin is seen trying to open the door with some type of unknown lockpick. Rex is on the lookout waiting for any signs of the police that may be coming to keep out trespassers.] Rustin: (Scratching his chin) Why won’t it open? Rex: Rustin, it’ll be open at nine in the morning! Rustin: Rex! That’s too late. Rex: Rustin, this is nuts! What can’t possibly wait until the morning? Rustin: I told you, this is important. I need to find out more about Henry Voodoo tonight! Rex: Rusty, this is like criminal trespassing or something. You should know all about that with taking some law school courses. Rustin: Rex, it’s a library for Christ sakes. Besides, I’m not taking anything, I’m just trying to find out some stuff. Just keep a lookout, will you? [Rex shakes his head and continues to keep look out. There is suddenly a dual beam of lights that rounds the corner. Rex starts to tap Rustin on the shoulder.] Rustin: (Annoyed) Rex! What is it! [Rustin turns around just in time to see the lights and immediately him and Rex dive into a nearby bush. Rustin and Rex watch the car pull in and park. A young teen steps out with a skull and crossbones t-shirt and leather coat. He walks up to the library door looks around and starts to work on the door. Rustin starts to say something, but Rex tries to hush him up, Rex knowing very well that the man has the same idea of getting inside that Rustin does.] [The man hears Rustin and Rex and shouts into the bush.] Teen: Hey get out of there! [Rustin and Rex step slowly out of the bushes and look the teen in the eyes.] Teen: You two spying on me? [Rex gulps.] Rustin: No, not at all. Thank god you’re here. You’re the night janitor, right? I just need to look some stuff up in the library. Can you let us in? [The teen looks at Rustin in disbelief. Rex shakes his head and tries to tell Rustin that he’s obviously a thief.] Teen: Yeah, right. Night Janitor. Sure. Rustin: Thank you so much. [The teen reaches in his pocket and pops open a switchblade. He starts to work on the lock and within seconds it’s open.] Teen: The keys are kinda rusty, my knife works better. [Rustin nods in agreement and enters with the teen. Rex stays outside. Rustin motions for him to come in. Rex is still in awe and does not want to enter because he thinks the whole situation is bad. Rex finally enters closing the door behind him. The teen goes off in a direction while Rustin and Rex go into the computer room and start their information search.] Rex: Rustin, I can’t believe you can’t see it. Rustin: See what? Rex: That kid’s a thief? Rustin: Who? The janitor? Rex: Never mind, you’re hopeless. [Rustin begins his search, scanning every database that is known searching terms from Voodoo, Magic, and Witchcraft to Carribean Folklore. Rex looked around the library sadly as the thief ransacked everything he thought to be of value. The thief walked by Rustin and started to take something from a shelf. Rustin looked up from an article he was reading and seemed to be surprised.] Teen: Sorry, I like to get everything off the shelves before I dust. [Rustin nods, understanding the noisy method perfectly now, goes back to work. The thief goes on to another room to continue his pillage. Rex stares with his mouth open at Rustin’s stupidity.] Rex: Rustin, you have gotta be kidding me! Rustin: Rex! Quiet! I think I got something. [Rustin points at the screen. Rex quickly scans over it.] Rustin: I knew something was up with that guy. [The headline sticks out. “Dr. Henry Domingo sued for unorthodox medical practices.” There is a photo of the strange Carribean man that Rustin has ran into the past couple of weeks. The article tells the tale of the strange doctor. April 24, 1995 Haitian National News Dr. Henry Domingo, local respected doctor of Port-Au-Prince, recently was brought under accusations of a former patient. The patient, Renee Crispus, states that Dr. Domingo has treated her for a form of depression with black magic, recently outlawed in Haiti. Although the treatment was successful, according to the patient, there was some extreme “side effects.” Dr. Domingo denies all allegations, and denies his use of the elder art of voodoo in his practice. Thorough police search of his office turned up magical relics, gris-gris, and various books outlining voodoo spells and rituals. Trial date is set in two weeks. [Rustin shakes his head. The thief now is walking around with a mop. Rustin looks up and acknowledges him with a smile.] Rustin: I told you something was wrong with this guy Rex. This guy’s a witch doctor! [Rustin continues to read the article, looking for more information. The thief smiles at Rex and uses the mop handle to smash a case holding some items. Rustin looks up. The thief looks apologetic.] Thief: I’m sorry! This is my first week. I haven’t quite got the hang of this mop yet. [The thief mops a little to show Rustin he’s working. Rustin goes back to another article.] May 8, 1995 Haitian National News Accused Witch Doctor Nowhere to Be Found! Dr. Henry Domingo, accused voodoo practitioner, did not show up on his court date. A nationwide search has been performed. Police continue to look for the man responsible for “emotional distress” of a patient, but it seems that the good Doctor has vanished into thin air. The magical relics which were at the doctor’s office have been removed for closer inspection, except for one missing item, an animal skull that was found in one of the Doctor’s closets. [Rustin smiles happily about attaining some more information on the mystery man who has been appearing before him the past weeks.] Rustin: This is the guy Rex! The skull proves it. It was really weird. An animal or something, like they said. [Rustin turns off the computer and gets up. The thief walks by with a garbage bag full of the items he had just taken. Thief: Well time to lock up. Just gotta take out the trash! Rustin: All right, I’m ready [Rex continues shaking his head.] Rex: (Quietly) Rustin! That’s the stuff he stole. Do something! Rustin: Rex, just be quiet. You need to calm down. [Rex and Rustin walk to the entrance. The thief opens the door for Rex and Rustin and the two brothers walk out to their car. Rustin climbs in, starts the engine.] Rustin: Well that was an informative night. I got some dirt on the ol’ Doc. Something I can use against him. We should do it again some time. Next week’s bad for me though, I’m facing Syckle in a pretty important match. Rex: I know. I heard. Aren’t you a little worried? Rustin: Rex...you should know your big bro’s got things covered. Me and Syckle have fought each other before and I know what to expect. It will be a hard battle, that I have no doubt of, but one I think I can overcome. After all, the stakes are high. The tourney’s coming to an end and me and Syckle are in the lead for the North Wind Title Shot. Rex: You think you have a chance against Givens and Kyo if you get the shot? Rustin: Why so little confidence bro? You know your big brother has worn gold before. Rex: Rustin a lot’s going to happen in the next couple of weeks, you need to keep your eyes open. Rustin: Believe me Rex....they’re wide and live! [Rustin exits the parking lot as the police go after the “janitor” who just ransacked the library. Rex looks in the mirror and tries to get Rustin to see the thief being apprehended.] Rex: Look, Rustin! I told you he was a thief. Rustin: (angrily) Rex! Please I’m driving. [The scene slowly fades to black as the car of Rustin Slade is speeding away into the distance.]
