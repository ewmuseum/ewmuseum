Internal Reflections Blaise 4WW Exposures #1091 Date: 12/03/04 Location: Pittsburgh, PA The more things change, the more they stay the same. The DMC won, yup, they got the belts. They're holding the titles. The Infidels are the new East Wind Title holders. But are they champions? “Negative, Ghost Rider, the pattern is full, no champions in sight on the East Wind Scene.” Then again, when has Blaise McClancy ever lost a title without someone resorting to a cop out? Oh, yeah, when he lost the SCWF Superior Title to his own cousin and a member of the KnightMare Brigade. Other then that, it's either been dumb luck, or in this case, outright and blatant disregard for any integrity of what it means to be a champion in wrestling. For his trouble, I hope that Mr. Garrett enjoyed his little treat. Bloody bastard needs to learn that if he's man enough to get into a match that he's not invited into, then h e's basically on his way to being man enough to face someone like Blaise. But he's a weasel, one who setup that abomination of a match in the first place. Then he tried to prevent Patti from playing sheriff during the match to keep the wrongs from happening in the first place. But no, Mr. Garrett not only got involved and pulled Blaise out of the ring, after the illegal actions of the Infidels using the table during a fucking ladder match, but then he manhandled a woman who's much smaller then him. Notice that since the subject is one Diana King, aka Luscious Lady Luck, I didn't say Lady. But still, Garrett proved to be nothing more then a conniving, lecherous weasel of a man whose only goal in life is to suck off Eddie B. Just like the rest of the DMC. But it's time. It's time to show these others how it's done. They think because they have a SINGLE victory, in anywhere, they are now big time players. They think that they're in control. But look at it this way, they have the confidence now to really screw things up. Not surprising that Kanton couldn't beat Blaise without help, and now the Infidels also needed help. So, unStable has lost the Tempest Title, the feeder title of the fed, but picked up the West Wind Title. Big freaking deal, you got the belt that doesn't mean that you can wrestle. It means that you can do gimmick matches to feed the people their want for blood and guts. It's not a wrestling title, it's a circus act title. And as for The Infidels, they got the East Wind Titles. OOOOH, they stole them. Yeah, they're so much better now. They couldn't' do it without illegal tactics. Yeah a win is a win? Or is it? Will the hallowed halls of PTC sing the praises of the Infidels? When they write the annuls of the year of 2004 in the wrestling community, will they say that the Infidels ended the reign of FutureShock by pinfall or submission? No, they used a table on Ukyo, in a match where tables weren't legal and then they used Eddie B's little man-slave, Daniel Garrett to get the rest of the job done. And Mr. Garrett got to feel up Triple L at the same time. So how do you fix this? How do you make things right? No, FutureShock isn't going to demand a rematch right away. There are bigger and better fish to fry. But we know that it's always an option. Just like Blaise has been sitting on his South Wind Title rematch for some time now. No, you'll wait. For in the mean time, there are lessons to be taught and learned. The KnightMare Brigade is still on top. They have the North Wind Champion now into their fold, and the number one contender for that. If Kris Kanton was the best man in unStable, and he's all but done for in the business, then what can they hope to obtain in this match? What do they have to gain? Nothing. But one simple thing... Humility. A small word, something that Blaise himself was taught a long time ago by his cousin. Something that will now be the first lesson for both unStable and especially the DMC. Slayer? A man who at one time almost had the Tempest Title, but fell to the charms of one Triple L. The Infidels, Priest and Turbine, they're useless. They couldn't get the job done, and get it done right. But they like matches like this, right? This is a contest of who is best, and who is a real stable. A real team. Triple L, a woman who tries to use her body to put her opponents off. But one who finally lost the Tempest Title after many weeks. Zane Ozz, big man who now has claimed the Circus Ring title. And Kris Kanton, worthless piece of trash that really shouldn't be in this federation, let alone in this match. But you see, the last match was on their terms. This one is on Blaise's. He's the only man on the 4WW Roster who has ever been in one of these matches. He's the only man who's done a Get Out Alive Match here. Almost 2 years ago in the SCWF. He aligned with his cousin, Sean McClancy, and Draven Lox to defeat a group that didn't like them. He also hurt someone who had cheated him in that match. Guess, what, the more things change, the more they stay the same. That's the plan this time. Blaise is set to ensure that he's avenged for what was done to him. Garrett's punishment will come at the legacy he tried to create like some sort of diety, is destroyed. Only thing is, a real diety would have at least used mud to make something. Instead of complete and utter SHIT! Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania Morning Glory Inn Charlene Kwon, the manager and valet for the now former East Wind Champions, stands looking out off of the porch on the back of the Inn. A good winter coat with a wool lining wraps around her small frame. The twenty some odd degree weather, a cold snap that's come in lately has laid down the beginnings of a real winter. No snow yet, just cold and wind. She watches as a car pulls up, a truck to be exact. A dark colored Chevy Avalanche with Nevada plates. It's setup as a pickup truck right now, completely sealing the cap from the outside air. The truck stops and Blaise McClancy climbs out and starts to walk up towards the door down below her. She's got a small hand held PDA type computer in her hands, tapping on the screen with her stylus. Blaise looks up at her and then heads into the building. A few minutes later while she's working, a knock can be heard at her door. “Come on in!” Blaise opens the door and walks into her room. She's still on the porch, standing there. “Hey, whatcha doing up so late? Ukyo probably won't be back for a few hours.” “Nothing, just couldn't sleep, so I figured I'd try to look at a few things. I've been checking the archives and found your old Get Out Alive Match. Can I ask why this was booked?” “Lorena had talked to me about it when we first started fighting the other two stables. She used to be a lower manager position within the SCWF, and she wanted some information about it. I guess when she saw Garrett get into it all and how he played the contract against Patti, she figured it was time to do a big match like that.” Charlene looked up from her PDA at him. “Are you worried about this? I mean, things are not looking good.” “I've got Wesley and Kyo on my team. Even though those two obviously don't fully get along quite yet, their talent is phenomenal. This match is very important. And they both know how to produce when the chips are down. As long as both of them don't decide to go cliché and start to suddenly hate each other as life long enemies.” “But Fnord and Discordia didn't get along with us just as much as a few months ago. I'm not too damn happy with this as it is. But I'm no longer managing...” She stopped for a moment and then moved to change the subject. “So, this match can be pretty brutal though. This can't be too good for anyone in the match. How do we know that you'll all be safe?” Blaise moved up towards the younger girl. “You're worried about Kyo?” She blushed. “Yes, I can see it. You don't want him to get hurt. You're probably not to happy with them making him a hunter either.” “Blaise, stop.” “I'm sorry.” She puts the PDA down onto a table that sits out on the porch, a plant sits dying from the cold snap in a pot upon it. “No, don't be. It's me. I guess I'm just moody. I even snapped at you last week over Ukyo. I've been waiting for the right time, I think this is it. I'm sorry for all that I've said to the negative about you and Ukyo. You're a good man, and you deserve her and her, you. I guess I'm just a little, envious of you two when I have been denied the same thing.” Blaise could see it in her eyes. He took a few more steps towards her, taking a finger to her chin to push her chin up. “What's wrong?” “Last week, I ran into Kyo at the arena. He apologized to me for everything. I saw the man I had loved then and there. I saw that he had purged the evil from him. But I still feel a rift between us. I know that I can't be with him the way that I want to be. It's not some school girl crush on the bad guy, the outlaw, the street tough. I know it's not. I don't know what it is fully. I just can't place my fingers on it. I just know that I... never mind.” Blaise smiled at her. “No, you're a part of their family. I can't never mind. You're Ukyo's friend. And while I hope that I was part of Kyo's change this last few weeks, and not fully Eddie B. While I hope that it was my words in that tavern that struck a cord, or even Ukyo's to him... You're a friend. I have to support you just like I have to support Melinda, Wesley, and Kyo.” A lone tear fell from Charlene's eye. “But there is nothing you can do. He's blind when it comes to me as anything else but the girl who grew up around him. That he protected. I don't know if he sees me as a little sister, or what.” “Look, I've got first hand account, he cares. He cares a lot.” “Then why did he take Patti's sister out on that date?” She knew... that wasn't going to be good. But it was something that Blaise didn't have a full answer for. “I'm sorry, but I don't have an answer for that. Maybe to get a little extra shot at Patti. Maybe just to try to make a new friend. But you yourself said that you don't know fully what it is. It's a path you'll have to take, and find yourself. But Ukyo and I are here for support. Don't forget that.” “Yeah, again, I apologize for my words over these last few weeks. Just chalk it up to moodiness I guess.” “No problems, Char. That and loosing 50 games in a row to a young girl at Yu Gi Oh.” “That's not fair.” “Just remember, eventually all winters end. Things make their way right again. You just have to give it time. Why don't you try getting some sleep, hanging around out here will only get you a cold or something. Bad enough we can't even get flu shots on the fed's medical right now. Don't need to tempt fate. You're not exactly immortal, you know?” At that Blaise turns to walk away from her and leave her alone. As the door shuts, she reaches down to get her PDA back from the table. She grabs it and then double takes on the flowers on the table. What was once bare and dead due to the harsh cold, was not vibrant and in full bloom. She smiled, knowing that the dryad had done it for her... Blaise went up to his room, and walked into the dark place cautiously. A flash of metal and then the sound of something grating against the wood of the wall until the light switch is clicked on. Blaise stands there, holding a knife in his hand, touching the switch itself. He looks around, surveying his surroundings. Straining both eyes and ears to ensure nothing was there that didn't need to be. A note on the table... He walks over and picks it up. “Blaise, got sent out on a training mission, going to be a little late, but they said this was the last one for a few weeks. Your lawyer called and said that everything is ready for the 19th. I'll see you when I get home... Ukyo” He put the note back down and then slid his jacket off and just dropped it onto the chair that sits there next to the large iron framed canopy bed. He grabs his guitar and walks over to the ottoman and sits down upon it. He plugs it into his portable amplifier and starts to tune it to the opening of A Perfect Circle's “3 Libras” when he's interrupted. “I understand you've been looking for me?” Blaise looked over at his porch, the door slightly open. “Yes, Lord Malik, I have been.” “Well, it's cold out here, you know the rules for me. You going to invite me in?” “Yes, you may enter.” The older Middle-Eastern man walked into his room. “What is your query for me? I don't make house calls that often.” Blaise looked up to him, a smirk on his face. “So I'm special, huh?” The man pulled out a cigarette and brought it up to his lips. The tip of it ignited with a small flame and then died down into a typical smoldering tip. “You could say that. Your training is done, and you're special in many ways to me and the Hunters. Not only are you the only Tautha De Dannan to ever become a hunter and complete the full training of the 4 Winds, but you're also the one who brought me the Kushinaga siblings. Two very powerful and potentially great hunters. Kyo is special in many ways that he doesn't understand yet. Some discipline in him and we would have a hunter who could possibly take on Mephisto himself. Ukyo, she's unique. As I'm sure you already know. She has potential that I can't even see being tapped just yet.” “What about me? You keep reminding me in our meetings about the other Tautha never being able to be a Hunter. You've even mentioned that my cousin couldn't make it. But I know Sean. He's ruthless, I mean he broke my knee in the ring when we had our little spat...” Lord Korwyn Malik took a drag on the cigarette and the tip flared up as he did. He stops to savor the smoke and then lets it go as he talks. “Prince Bres was ruthless, until it came time to deliver the killing blow. He couldn't do it. The Tautha side of him wouldn't' allow it. Even though he's a Fomorian Prince, he's a half breed. And that one side couldn't' take a life, even one of such vileness.” “But I can? I've taken out two so far. The Angel Zadikiel, and the Bean Shihde. I'm fully Tautha... if the dryad side is to preserve life, why is it that I can do it?” “Because Blaise, you're even more special then that. You're unique. Just like Ukyo, just like Kyo. One day, you'll be my right hand. And what a right hand it'll be.” “I don't get it.” “You don't have to...” “Look, I know what I've done, but that fight in Columbus, where Ukyo showed up... I barely remember it. Can you fill me in?” “Not yet.. Not yet. Just know that you have done rather well. And you're well above your current age. You need to just keep things flowing and the brotherhood strong.” “What's Wesley's play in this?” Malik chuckled. “Williams is special too. You'd not be in the KnightMare Brigade and being watched over by my Nephew if you weren't. Everyone is playing a part in the big game here.” “I got into this by playing as a pawn in a game.” Malik took another drag on the cigarette. “We all play games, Prince Turren. Every thing I've done for the last 467 years has been a game. Sometimes we play for keeps, sometimes we play for amusement. I know my role in the game, only because I've forged it myself. This is a game that we can't win unless we know what our role in it is. You must first discover your role. You must know what it is that you're after and what you're going to do. Then you'll understand.” Blaise just shook his head. “We all have a role?” “Yes, even the Chinese girl. We all have a role to play in it. You're role right now, if you want a hint is to keep the peace and to make sure that the others don't crack under the pressure. Everything is a game, everything is a test. A challenge, a risk... something you should understand quite well.” “Yeah, I do. It's what I live for.” “Be ready, Turren. Your cousin, Prince Bres is soon to make his power play. He's going to challenge for the position of Crown Prince for when the elf king decides it's time to sleep again. You need to be ready, for that time will be a time of challenge for anyone who is his sworn ally. You must be prepared, and you must fully understand by then.” “That's why the enhanced training and multiple back to back missions?” “Yes, now get some rest. I'd assume you'll need to be in top form for when Ukyo gets back. I'm available for questions, but try to direct them through the more secure channels from now on.” “Yes, Lord Malik.” Malik turns and leaves the room... ~Fini~ .
