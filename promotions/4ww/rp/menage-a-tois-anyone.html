Menage A Tois Anyone? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #85 Date: August 7, 2003 Location: Locker Room / the Ranch Menage a tois - threesome, more then two, less then four. Who's brilliant idea was it to put a woman in a match with two men? Two men who have stated that they want no part of her title. Who's the culprit? Why the CEO of course. Her opponents in this match dubbed a 'menage a tois' --- Psymon and Pajarao de Presa. Why would Fey pick these two men? Let's examine the facts of these two men. On the one hand is Psymon. A man who has no quarrel with Patti. The two have a mutual respect for another. No feud, no desire to tangle with another. Psymon seems more intrested in making Jay Phoenix and Impulse's lives a living hell, then being concerned with the 'Nightmare Title'. Then again, Destiny has something special planned for him. On the other hand is Pajaro de Pasa. The thorn in Patti's perverable backside. Dstiny doesnt believe that Pajaro is after the belt, but more after getting a chance to tap Patt's ass. Guess this leads him to believe that she wants him. Doesn't help the man has sex on the brain since the first meeting between the two. After the little meeting a little over a week ago. Patti has no plans of allowing Pajaro to see her nekkid form again. The gift of the nekkid Patti made out of jalapinos, should one laugh or feel insulted? Now for the real insult to Patti. In an evil attempt to throw her off her track of being a champion. Fey has done the unspeakable, she has put Sean's betrothed in the corner of Pajaro. Why? Hmmm, survey says....To keep Patti's mind off of the matches, to cost her the title. An evil attempt to cost Patti the two things she finds most dear. Her love for McClancy, and her desire to remain the champion. Two things that Patti will battle harder then ever before. Some questions are about to be answered in the coming weeks. Especially those involving Eliza, Annie, and Summers. These three are causing all sorts of problems within Patti's life, and looks like they will be continue to do so. Will Destiny step in and let Patti achieve her dream of keeping the belt? Or will the forces working with Fey cause her to lose it? What are the plans involving Annie and Pajaro? What about Summers? This week, will find out if Patti can continue her winning streak or will that streak be broken? Tune into Storm Front to find out. ___________________________________________________________ Place: Locker Room Time: After Championship match ___________________________________________________________ Blackness, is all she saw for a few minutes. Her head throbbed, and she felt something sticky on her face. She heard a voices from time to time. Some she recognized, others she didn't. She could hear Sean's familiar Irish temper and worry through his accent. She sensed that he was somewhere near her, but not close enough to reach out for her. Trainer #1: Mr. McClancy, Ms. Anderson is going to be ok. Just a couple simple stitches is all she needs to close that spot. She's lucky he didn't hit her any harder then that. Sean seemed to relax at that news. Trainer #2: Now as for your hand, you'll need to keep that wrapped for a couple days. You deciding to knock out a glass water bottle with your fist wasn't a smart move. Sean glared at the trainer. He watched closely as he wrapped guaze and bandage around the cut. He looked over at the woman he loved, laying there, her eyes still closed, a little fluttering here and there. He waited impatiently as the trainer bandaged him up. His desire to dismantle Summers was meeting the desire to be by Patti's side. When she woke up, he wanted to be the one by her side, not some faceless baffoon. A small moan came from the direction of where Patti was laying. She came to with a start, took a swing at the trainer who was placing the butterfly bandage over her right eye. Patti glared at the trainer. Patti: Get the bloody hell away from me. Sean shoved the trainer off oh him, and moved the short distance to where Patti was laying. He placed his hand on her shoulder, to reassure her that he was there beside her and to calm her. He knew once she came to she would be in a mood to kick someone's ass, and he had a feeling that Summers is gonna wish he hadn't pissed her off. Patti: Where the bloody hell is he at. He's gama stavros sou, he's pissed me off. When I get done with that adelfi malakas, he'll wish he was off wrestling Eliza in the backseat of his car. Patti attempted to stand up, but the dizzyness from the chair shot, knocked her right back down to her knees. Trainer #1: I would suggest she not do to much moving around for the next couple days. That chair shot was rather nasty. From the looks of things, she's got a slight concussion. So my advice is little to no movent at least until tomorrow. Other then that, she's free to go home. The trainers and medical technicians cleaned up their stuff and moved out of the room, giving the two the room. Sean set to work packing up her stuff. He began picking up the discarded clothing, towels, and light workout equipment. Patti attempted to help him clean up her mess, but she stopped in midmovement as a quick, pain hit her. She fell backward, clenching her head. Tears of frustrations falling down her cheeks. Patti: Why does he bloody hell feel the need to hit me every time? Is it that bad losing to a woman? I mean none of the bloody others have bothered to complain about my beating them. Pajaro, Shocker, Bad Boi, and even Syxx Figures hasn't complained about losing to me. They all have taken the loss with grace. Even my losing to Phoenix in the Gauntlet match, I didnt cry whine and cry about it. But, on two different occassions, this wanker has managed to smack me in the face with a chair. Sean: Donn'cha be worrin about it. I will handle him. Patti smiled up at him. Her heart overflowing with the love that she pocessed for him. He came over to her, and placed a gentle kiss on her forhead. Sean: Why don't we forget about this stuff and we go home? His eyebrows wiggle, his boyish smile appearing on his face. He knew of one way to get her mind off of Summers, his sister, and Annie. A little rest and relaxation is all she needs right now. He picks Patti up gently and heads out the door. Fade Out ___________________________________________________________ Fade Back In Sean McClancy pulls up in front of the modest style ranch house that he and the woman sitting curled up asleep next to him call home. He stared at the house for a few minutes. This was the life he had imagined for himself, a loving home, a woman who loved him beyond everything else, the promise of children to fill any empty rooms, and above all a woman who was as passionate as he is. He looked at Patti. Her head resting comfortably on his shoulder. A gentle smile appearing on his face. This was the one he loved. The one that had helped him get his title back when he was in the other federation. The one he chased 3000 miles to get back. The woman he spent a glorious two weeks with. He just couldnt see his life without her. He had to figure out what do with Annie. Yes, at one time he loved her, but after being away from her for those four years. She just didn't fit in his world anymore. She seemed fake compared to Patti. Her look, her smile, her voice, just everything about her seemed fake. What about the sex? Hmm, last time he slepted with her those four years ago, was like he was pounding a dead fish. He shrugged off the thoughts. The simple movement awoken Patti from her sleep. She struggles to wake up, but the pain medication still is in affect. She notices her surroundings, notices she is home and falls back to sleep on Sean's shoulder. Sean gently crawls out of the car, careful not to wake Patti up. Between the chair shot, the stitches, and now having to deal with Annie. The poor girl is just emotionally exhausted from all the extra stress. He carefully picks her up. Her head lays on his shoulder. A tiny wince of pain crinkles her forehead, but it doesnt wake her. Sean takes the few strides into the house, leaving the front door standing wide open, he lays Patti down on the couch. Her head coming to rest comfortably on the pillow, her knees curling up around her mid-section. A position Sean knew as her way of dealing with the pain. After about a half an hour, Patti begins to stir. She opens an eye. The first thing she sees is Sean asleep in the recliner. His head laying off to the side, the sound of the buzzsaw going at full speed filling the room. Patti smiles at the image. An almost angle expression on his face as he sleeps, a five o'clock shadow gracing his handsome face. Patti slowly sits up, allowing her pounding head to adjust to the spinning room. The blanket that was covering her up slips to the ground. She places her feet on the ground, attempting to stand up. She stumbles a little, her knee crashing into the coffee table. Patti: Bloody fucking hell, this damn thing needs to be removed. Her little outburst wakes up the sleeping giant. Sean comes to quickly. His fists in fighting position. Patti giggles at the sight of him awake and ready for a fight. Her giggling causes her head to hurt again, forcing her to sit back down on the couch. Sean smiles and walks over to where she is sitting. He bends down and gives her a kiss, a kiss full of love, a kiss full of promises, of dreams and wishes. He pulls away and gives her a smile. Patti reads the instand desire in his eyes. As the two begin to head into a heavy makeout session, heavy petting and groping can be seen but nothing more then that The two decided which would be better re-christening the couch or winding up on the floor. Just as the decision is being made, the door bell rings. Patti silently pleads with Sean not to answer the door, but better judgement forces him to answer. It could be Ian checking on Patti's condition or even his sister. Patti struggles to get up from the couch and slowly walks to the door. She opens the door and greated by a Federal Express agent. Fed Ex: I'm looking for a Ms. Patti Anderson? Patti: That would be me. A package from my father? Fed Ex: No it's not. I have a letter for you that I need for you to sign. Patti takes the pen from him, and quickly scrawls her name at the bottom. Patti walks back into the living room. She stares at the letter, her eyebrow raised as if trying to question the letter. The camera zooms in on the letter in her hands. All that can be seen is the return address. ILLINOIS STATE DEPARTMENT OF CHILDREN AND FAMILY SERVICES 406 EAST MONROE SPRINGFIELD, IL 62701-1498 Patti looks again questionally at the letter. Patti: What the bloody hell does the Illinois DCFS want with me? Unless they be wanting the 'Darling of the 4WW' and the 'Irish Bastard' to come and greet fans and promote adoptions or something. Sean shrugs his shoulders. Patti curls up in the crook of his arm. She flips the letter over and opens it. Two letters fall in her lap. One being an offical form letter from the director, the other sealed in an envelope. Patti scans the letter..... Her eyes growing wide as she reads it, tears starting to fall from her eyes. A confused look appearing on Sean's face. Fade Out
