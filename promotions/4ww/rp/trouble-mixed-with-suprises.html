Trouble mixed with suprises Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #432 Date: 01/04/03 Location: Tucson, Arizona Trouble - verb - to agitate mentally or spiritually. 2) to put into confused motion 3) to become mentally agitated 4) to make an effort: be at pains Trouble, brings its evil face into the Four Winds this week. Someone somewhere has decided that ‘The Darling of the 4WW’ is on a high horse, and needs to be knocked down a few notches. Her constant claims at being the number one contender for the North Wind Title has rubbed someone the wrong way. Or maybe it has nothing to do with being the #1 contender, maybe its someone that is jealous of all the attention that Patti is receiving. Destiny realizes that there are some in the federation that can’t seem to handle the attention the ‘Darling’ has gained. Everything from her title reign as the first South Wind Champion to her relationship with Sean McClancy, throw the idea of a stable in with the newest woman to come to the fed in the form of Lacuna Debris. Maybe it’s the possibility of its someone hoping to use Patti as a stepping stone to fame and fortune? Or is it someone hoping to use Patti to get to Jade? Or is it someone from Patti’s past that is out for revenge? It could be all of it, or it could be none of it. Far worse it could be another test laid out by the “Mistress of the Secrets” Fey McClancy to test Patti once again. Destiny sighs. The test will never be done for Patti in the eyes of Fey. Especially now that Patti is sporting a rather gorgeous diamond ring. An engagement ring to make everything perfect for them. Destiny remembered watching over Sean pick it out and quickly had it wrapped. But unfortunately for Patti, the engagement came with a little surprise with it. Sean had been called home by the courts to answer for the actions that had transpired at Maelstrom. With Annie no longer holding claim to him, Sean was needed for questioning and the planning of his plight for the rest of his life. Destiny somehow knows that Patti and Sean being separated will affect them both, but she knows that with Jade being in the fed, someone will be watching Patti’s back. Which of course makes it a little easier for him to leave her at this time. Destiny hopes the business over in Ireland goes swiftly and allows him to return to Patti in good time. For the distance will more then likely bother the both of them. Destiny turns again and stares into the mirror where she can view Patti Anderson. She knows that this match that is forthcoming is bringing an unknown trouble to her. But is it something that Patti will be able to take on her own or will it require Patti to take a long, deep, look within herself to see if she has the strength to battle it? Only this week’s Storm Front will have the answer. ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: Sheraton Tucson Hotel & Suites Time: Check In ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti Anderson’s midnight blue Mazda Miata pulled up in front of the hotel. Her long red hair completely hidden under a blonde wig. The baggy clothes that she kept in the trunk were now on her frame. Being a celebrity in the business had its moments when she had to hide herself, and with this recent tour, she was going to feel the need to hide herself from the public. With Sean being over in Ireland, and Jade back in New York, she knew she was going to be needing her wits about her to keep from being seen and mobbed by the screaming fans. Patti adjusted her sunglasses and reached over to grab her travel bag and garment bag. The rest of her luggage had been delivered earlier in the week by the company. The less that the stars had to move on their own, the easier it was for them to get to places they needed to be. Patti sighed heavily as she crawled out of her car and handed the valet the keys. Patti: Scratch the car, and you’ll bloody be working at McDonald’s for the rest of yer life. Patti handed the poor valet a small tip and walked into the hotel. She stared at her surroundings. A rather rich looking hotel stood before her. At least being engaged to a member of the Knighmare Brigade, they went all out for them. Not like it mattered much to her. She’d be just as content as staying in a two star hotel or even a three, but with the ending of Annie’s presence, Fey had seemed to accept her into the fold. Patti quietly walks over to the doorman. Her high heels clicking loudly as she walks. The doorman smiles as he opens the door for her. A chipper smile on his face. Doorman: Good morning ma’am. The Front Desk is straight ahead, and we hope you enjoy your stay here at our fine establishment. Patti nods her head at him and walks through the open door. Her hazel eyes grow wide as she walks into the lobby of the hotel. The gold colored walls take her breath away as she feels having stepped back into her old apartment building. Her eyes got a little misty as she realized that she would be staying in a hotel that reminded her of her old home. After a quick scan of the room and the people in the lobby. She made her way cautiously to the front desk to get checked in, and escape to the safety of her hotel suite. Patti arrives at the front desk. A huge counter that is stashed away in the corner. A pleasant looking older woman stands at the computer, finishing up the last transaction. She turns and stares at the woman now standing at the counter. She notices all the woman seems to be carrying just a couple of things. Desk Clerk: Hi! Welcome to the Sheraton Tucson Hotel and Suites. Do you have a reservation? Patti looks around to make sure no one is with in earshot. She can’t afford to be discovered by a wayward fan hanging out in the hotel lobby. Patti removes her sunglasses long enough to stare into the brown eyes of the desk clerk. Patti: I have a reservation under the name of Murphy. The company said you would know what that meant. Patti quickly replaces her glasses on her face and stands still while the clerk looks as if the woman standing before her lost her mind. The clerk punches a few keys on the keyboard, making an attempt to humor the woman standing before her. The first several attempts on the computer fail nothing but a search. The clerk nervously looked at the blonde standing before her, she could here her foot tapping impatiently on the floor in front of the desk. Desk Clerk: I’m sorry it’s taking so long, but this computer only decides to only do what it wants when its wants. Patti cracks a nervous smile and stares down at her watch. This would be so much easier if Sean were here to take care of this. At least she’d have something to hide behind or lean up against while everything was being taken care of. She signed as she stared down at her watch again. If she didn’t get the room soon, she’d miss the call, and her night would be filled with tortured dreams of Sean doing something else. The sudden voice of the desk clerk startled her. Desk Clerk: Here we are, Ms Murphy. It seems that you have one of our suits reserved for yourself and a guest that will arrive at a later time. It also says that you are not to be disturbed unless these names call. The clerk spun the monitor around and Patti nodded that she agreed. The clerk smiled again at her and finished up the check in procedure. She ran a couple card keys through the machine to activate them. She hands Patti the keys along with a few extras that the hotel offers. Desk Clerk: Oh, and these phone message came in about twenty minutes before you arrived. The clerk smiles as she hands a stack of about ten messages, a few from Sean, a few from Jade, as well as one from her dad. Patti smiled as she scanned them. . Patti started the slow walk toward the elevator, her purse slung over her right shoulder, the garment bag bent over her left arm. She stared at the messages as she pressed the button to take her up. Several from Sean stated he had made it safely to Ireland, and that he’d be staying with his parents until the courts would see him. Annie had returned also from what one of the other notes had said, but she was in hiding, refusing to come out do to the damage done to her face. Patti suppressed the chuckle that was trying to come through. She knew that if it got out, her disguise would do her no good the at the next hotel she stayed at, but then again, from the looks of Jade’s phone call messages, she’d be in town before the next Storm Front. Patti continued to look at the messages as she walked into the elevator. Just pretty much the same thing from both of them. But one from Sean caught her eye. A phone number was all it said and to call it when she got the chance. From the looks of it, she guessed that Sean had another surprise for her outside of the nice diamond ring she wore proudly now. Patti continued to read the phone messages that she had received as she walked to the hotel room that was hers until after the Storm Front. Without looking away from the messages, she slid the key into the lock and walked into a sight that took her breath away. Her senses were assaulted by the sent of roses. Every shade known to the world, in everything from a single red one to bouquets of multi colored ones. Patti squealed in delight as she saw them. She squealed even more when she caught a glimpse of a puppy out of the corner of her eye. She dropped both her purse and garment bag on the floor as the puppy came to her. She bent down and her up. The puppy happily licked her face as she walked around the rooms. Housekeeping was on the ball when she spotted a food dish and water bowl for the puppy. Patti noticed the puppy had decided to scamper over to the bowl and pillow and crashed out sleeping, making cute little puppy snores. Patti walked back into the bedroom and started to work on her luggage. She hated her clothes being wrinkled and stuffed into suitcases, it annoyed her sometimes that Sean left his stuff in the bags, but the last time he helped her unpack, they wound up putting another notch on the bedpost. Patti turned her back to her luggage just as she heard the melodious tones of her cell phone kicking in. Patti grabs her purse off the floor, the puppy waking up with the cell call, comes bouncing over to her feet. Patti picks her up and flips it open. Patti: Hello? And fade out til next time.
