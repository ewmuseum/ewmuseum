From Life to Death El Lobo de la Muerta 4WW Exposures #126 Date: 9-13-03 Location: doesnt matter You call me a loser you call me a faggot this is it I'm tired and I'm not gonna have it yellin' and screamin', dissin' my name the words you say are all the same I never did shit bitch now what? I tried to be nice and ignore that stuff Now I'm gonna let out all I gotta say all things I've kept inside day after day You say I'm a pussy, that I'm a chump het at least I found a thing called love all I do is cry, I shed a lot of tears You know what all happened over the years? I'm just a baby, I can't win a fight I tried to take my life every damn night Life is a bitch that I can live with It was all this shit that I couldn't lift I know living this way isnt the way to be I'm tired of ignorin' and trying to sing I did a lot of things I regret parents said things that I can never forget You sit there and I'll let you talk your shit now it's time to break, there's something to admit I don't give a damn anymore, I don't give a fuck you fucked me over now your shit out of luck You sit in disbeliefe I can see you're scared shut the fuck up I told you I didn't care you've cost me friendships that I held so dear those friends helped me live the life I had feared they could calm me sayin' give it a chance now is the time this is gonna be your second chance Life is a bitch that I can live with it's all this shit that I can't lift living that way isn't the way to be I'm tired of ignoring and trying to sing I'm doin' something I'm gonna regret Hopefully in my death they won't forget ^&^ From Life to Death ; Kevin Southern ^&^ ~Painful memories and trying tribulations bring forth an upcoming choice that NONE can hault. Bitter-sweet reign is about to fall and a darker, evil, and more sinister reign will come to power. In the words of a modern philosopher, 'would you cry if I died to today or would you go about your merry way? You would indeed cling to every memory of mine, but lest you will forget and continue on during time. Would you cut me out of every memory we shared or would you cling close to all those feelings of love and care? I cannot and will not say I'm sorry nor make amends because once the picture has been crumpled up it can NEVER be perfect again.' This philosopher knew all about life and death, he even knew what you would do if such things were to happen. Kevin knew that there is more to life than pain and agony. He knew that love is in all things, even inside his cold hateful heart, but he wouldn't accept it until it was too late.~ {The scene cuts to a white piece of paper where we see a hand with tattoos and scars holding a pen to the paper. We pan out a bit more to see a long gash through his wrist leading to his the lower part of his forearm. We then carry back to the paper where he speaks aloud what he is writing.} El Lobo: Yo amigo I have to get this off mi chest. I'm tellin' you that things are really messed up here man. It's really got me way beyond stressed and I'm not sure how much I can take. I can hear all of them still whispering behind my back. And the thing I can't handle is Angél, things between us are going to hell man. I really need help getting through this because it is all new to me man. You know that I'm the one to fight everything head on, but this one I need a lot of help man. Things have never gotten this bad and I'm not sure if I can handle everything going on this way. The stress has got me to the point where I'm totally burnt out mentally and physically. I'm at the point of giving in, going back to the demons before. I'm asking for your helpd before I get too far gone, please help me get through this. Hopefully you'll get this before all hell breaks loose and I'm left in ruins. In peace love unity and respect Tom Viva para ahora {El lobo takes the letter and sets it down on a stand infront of a chair. He begins to walk away as the scene cuts to black.}
