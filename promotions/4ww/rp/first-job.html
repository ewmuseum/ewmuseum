First Job

11 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First Job 4WW Exposures #1170 Date: 2/11/05 Location: Eiffel Tower (The scene opens up with a distance shot of the Eiffel Tower. The structure soars above the different buildings in France as there is a beautiful sunrise that can be seen in the background. The camera pans towards the top of the Eiffel Tower. As it continues to get closer and closer, two figures can be seen at the top of the tower. The closer the camera gets, the forms turn into those of Luscious Lady Luck and Patty Andersen. LLL is wearing casual clothes with a light jacket. Patty Andersen on the other hand is wearing a long red evening gown. There is one other figure there, that of a man with a camera.) Man: That’s it. Keep going. The camera loves you hon. Just feel it, just feel it. Your doing wonderful. (Patty just starts taking different poses, striking it in several different ways, trying her best to make the dress look good while working on her own look.) Man: Great. Great job. (A few more clicks are heard, and then the man puts his camera down.) Man: Outta film. Gotta go change. While I’m at it, why don’t you go change into the other dress? (Patty and LLL walk off toward a small little room. The room is open on the top.) Patty: How am I doing? LLL: Your doing wonderful. Absolutely wonderful. You’ve caught on so fast. So much faster than the others that I’ve worked with. Patty: Do you really think so? LLL: Of course I really think so. You get ready for the next photo. (Patty puts her red dress so it hangs over the top of the little room as LLL walks off back towards the photo area.) (Things had been going so well for LLL. She was making her way to the top. She was with one of the most powerful people in the fed. She was married to the Commissioner, and she had one of the biggest and best men as her bodyguard. Things couldn’t have gone much more perfect. LLL was on her way to the top.) (But then it all came crashing down. Crashing down farther than one could’ve ever imagined. Blaise destroyed everything that she had been working for. Blaise destroyed it all. The adulterous pair destroyed everything that she had worked for, and everything that she had built up. But soon, they would get theirs. Todd would make sure of that.) (And what was up with Zane this past week? Why was he acting so odd? He just stood at the top of the ramp watching, waiting. For what? Was there something going on the LLL should know about? Did she need to get a new body guard? Was she losing hers/) (But there were other things to look at. Other matters that were more important outside of the events of the past week. Things that were happening this week. Like her match with Bloo. A lingerie match. Something for the fans in France to see. It was going to be fun though. LLL enjoyed these types of matches. LLL enjoyed what she was doing. And LLL would rebound from this to make it to the top.) (And of course there was Valentine’s Day coming up too. The day of lovers. Todd was going to love what LLL had planned for it. Especially after such a bad experience that he had in Munich. Todd would get something that he would deserve and that would bring his day to such a brighter level.) Man: OK, I’ve got my film changed. Are we ready yet? LLL: I’ll go check. (LLL walks briskly back toward the small room, and knocks on the door.) LLL: You ready hon? The photo guy is ready. Your doing such a great job on your first job. You can only get better. Patti: Yeah, I’m ready. Just a second. (Patty Andersen comes walking out of the little room wearing a strapless blue dress with a white shall. She starts making her way toward the photographer to get ready for the next series of pictures.) (Things were going to turn around for LLL. Patty was going to be the new star of her modeling agency, and LLL herself was going to find a way to rise in 4WW. After all, there is no where to go but up from here. Scene fades to black.)
