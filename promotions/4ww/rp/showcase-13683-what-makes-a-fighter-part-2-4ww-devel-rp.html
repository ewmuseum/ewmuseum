What makes a fighter part 2 [4WW Devel RP]

26 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sight of ones Devil Standing directly in front of Rylinroth was Clefaire. The girl who had sent her the letter telling her that she didn’t want to be friends anymore. After the initial shock had wore off Rylinroth her hand quickly swiped across the face of Clefaire which sent her to the ground. Clefaire looked up with a saddened looked upon her face. She stood up again and walked towards Rylinroth once more. Once more she was struck down by the angry female. This time she didn’t stand up though but remained on the floor, tears swelling up in her eyes and rage building in her mind. Without movie she shouted up at Rylinroth… “What the hell is wrong with you Ry!” Rylinroth’s eyes glazed over as she stared a whole directly through Clefaire’s face. Slowly her hand came up and pointed towards her former best friend. She then shook her head and turned her back on Clefaire before crossing her arms over in put distain. Clefaire stood up again at this point and dusted herself off, she tried to control her anger as she spoke once more… “I don’t see what your problem is with me Ry, I’ve done nothing wrong. I saw you that night in my house all those years ago then you just disappeared. I’ve looked all over for you and now I’ve finally found you. Why did you leave?” Rylinroth’s head snapped round to catch a glimpse of Clefaire, a frown now across her face. She shook her head once more as her train of thought went away from the real word for a number of seconds. In this lapse she imagined herself jumping at Clefaire, pushing her to the floor and strangling her until the life passed away from her. Slowly as she thought this a grin started to appear on her face, only to be broken by more talk from Clefaire… “Ry! What have I done, if you can show me, then show me! I need to know!” Tears were now streaming down Clefaire’s face. In her mind she truly believed that she had done nothing wrong, she was confused beyond her own belief. Rylinroth however was not convinced, she thought back to the day when she got the letter from Clefaire, that thought would always stay in her mind. She decided not to go with the strangling option but to instead to show Clefaire why she was a part of Rylinroth’s hate. She turned on her heel and stormed past Clefaire, bumping into her shoulder on the way past which knock Clefaire sideways into the wall. Frantically, moved to her room where she searched the drawers beside her bed, ripping everything out and throwing it onto the bed. Eventually she got to the final drawer at the bottom and lifted out a single envelope and held it above her head. At this point, Clefaire was standing at the entrance way to the room, her eyes read from the river of tears she had been producing. Rylinroth turned to see her standing there, the letter still held up high. She walked with a purpose towards Clefaire and grabbed her hand. Once Clefaire’s hand was outstretch with her palm open, Rylinroth placed the envelope into her hand and stood back a number of steps. Clefaire looked down at the envelope and then back up to Rylinroth who nodded once as if to tell Clefaire to get on with it. Clefaire slowly opened the envelope while still looking Rylinroth and speaking softly… “What is this?” Rylinroth did not answer, her eyes firmly set upon the letter which was emerging from the envelope. Clefaire opened it out and read the contents. It was the letter which Rylinroth received when she was younger from Clefaire. A look of shock came across the face of Clefaire and she dropped the letter from her now numb hands. Her whole body had become numb from what she had read, she knew it was her writing, her way of ending a letter…everything. The one thing that shocked her though was that she believed that she didn’t write it. Before Clefaire could even speak again which she was planning to do Rylinroth pushed past her and out to the main entrance way where she pointed towards the door. Clefaire knew exactly what Rylinroth wanted and did not want to disturb her anymore. Promptly she moved towards the front door and exited. Once more Rylinroth was alone. Packing is fun To get her mind off things Rylinroth decided to pack. She had a long journey to go from the house she rented in Wales to the new once she rented specially for the Edinburgh event. She placed all the items that lay on her bed from the drawers neatly into her suitcase followed by the small amount of clothes she had with her. She then got her wrestling gear from another room in the house and placed it in the suitcase. Just as she was about to close the case over she tapped herself on the head before looking under the pillow on the bed. There lay a framed picture which she then placed on top of all the clothes in the case. It was a picture of Sekhet, her opponent from the previous week. A smile came across her face before she closed the suitcase over. One thought ran through her mind until her transport came to take her to Edinburgh… “Sekhet…” -To Be Continued-
