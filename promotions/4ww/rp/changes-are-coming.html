Changes Are Coming Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #898 Date: 08/27/04 Location: Shirakabaso Hotel Changes are coming once again to the world that is around us. Presidential Elections cover the media in all parts of the world. The voters of the American nation are battling it out to determine who will carry their country for the next four years. Something that we're all pretty sure will be either a change for the best or a change for the worst. Another change blows long on the horizons of Japan as this week in Nagoya, Japan at the Rainbow Hall, a ill wind of change comes blowing in as Patti is forced to team with ICE against the team of Kyo Kushinaga and Luscious Lady Luck. Three people that Patti has no desire to be around. All three of them have something against Patti going into this match. She knew she couldn't trust anyone in that match, for she knew that each one of them were after to make a change in her life. A permant change if they are given the chance. Let us take a look at the three this week....starting with Kyo Kushinaga. Kyo...the current co-holder of the number one contendership for the North Wind Title. He's getting a chance to climb the ladder of the 4WW to possible success and fame that he has wanted for the longest time. He would stand a chance at creating a change so large for him that he would develope an ego worse then West. We all know one more of those is more then enough. Kyo has what it takes to become the new North Wind Champion in less then a week but How about LLL? Yes its true that Patti has admitted that they were involved in the past. A mistake that happened five years ago when a struggling young woman in search of a break that would lead her to the stardom that she needed. A decison that has come back to haunt her. LLL got a taste of something and she's hell bent on bringing it back into her a life or share in a small part of it. She has gone as far as to dedicate her recent title victory to her, like she did it all to impress her. A fact that won't go undetected by Patti and will have a serious response to come this monday night when the next show happens.LLL may want another night, but all she will receive is a beating that she will remember for a lifetime. And now being forced to team with ICE. Hmm, Toddy Boy's attempt to bring in someone to put Patti in her place. Something that Toddy has been wanting to do since Patti laid him out with a loaded boot, and a walked out with his title. Toddy did his homework by finding someone bigger and stronger to try to remove the 'Darling from Down Under.' A 6'4' monster from another fed is getting a shot to team with the person he was brought in to destroy. Talk about major conflict of interest, tag with a woman whom you were sent to destroy or take her out and make her the sole loser of the match, could be a wild and fun night for him to have the bragging rights that he helped LLL and Kyo get what they wanted...a win over Patti. Or will the fact that Patt knows something that will bring ICE to her side. It will be interesting to see how all parties that are involved will deal with them. Changes are coming. Changes to Patti. Changes to Kyo. Changes to Ice. Changes to LLL. All four of these will experience the change of some sort in the next few weeks. Changes for the good or changes for the bad, the experience will be something that all four parties will remember and hopefully grow and learn from. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Place: Shirakabaso Hotel Time: Downtime for the North Wind Champion ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Patti Anderson curled up in the quiet seclusion of her hotel. The perks of having fears of crowds is what made this mountain retreat in Hida Mountain range perfect for her. Her fiancee, Sean McClancy had managed to join her for a brief time before he got called into Tokyo for their sister fed's ppv. Patti had volunteered to go with him for moral support, but he didn't want her there should their be a reason that he didnt succeed. Patti sensed that it had to do with Annie being in his corner. The past between them was still filled with alot of anger and hatered. Ian's great grandfather had assured her that Annie had no more romantic interests in him, but somehow it didn't seem right. He had become secretive, hiding things from her. Plus he was spending more time with Annie then with her. This burned Patti. It ate at her. It feuld her fire. It had made her want to forget her commitments and return to his side as his valet, and send that Irish whore back to the side of her husband. Unless this was just another test set forth by one of the annoying courts that either Sean or her was tied too. She survived her trials as best as she could. Her senses told her that there was more to come, but nothing that she couldnt handle. It was all a test to see if she was truely the woman destened to be with the Prince of the Seelie Courts. So far she had proven it. The pieces of information she was getting out of Blaise and the Twins had been almost nothing at all. They wouldn't confirm to her that something was going on, other then Sean was worried about his big match. If she went to Fey or Ian with her questions, she knew that neither one of them would tell her anything. Plus with Fey expecting the birth of the twins at any time now. A visit from the future sister in law wouldn't be quite welcome. Patti sighed in almost disgust as she paced around nervously in her room. She was never one to snoop on Sean, and she wasn't about to start. Her mind kepted going back to knowing that he was not trusting her with the truths. She shrugged it off as she walked into the room off from where she slepted and changed into her custom made purple and black bathing suit. She smiled at how the suit fit her body. The material molded to her body almost like her wrestling gear did. The two simple spaghetti straps rested gently on her shoulders as the cups held her bust up higher and pushed them closer together. The thong bottom rode up high on her hips and clung tightly. She smiled as she twirled around in front of the mirror. She giggled as she got dizzy from the spins. 'LLL is going to die when she sees this suit. Just to bad there was only one made. Now to just find a way to keep that greedy shelia from stealing anymore of my stuff.' Patti sighed as she grabbed her custom made I-pod as well as her towel and made the walk downstairs and to the outside area where the hot springs awaited her. Fade out only to fade back in.... Patti sat soaking in the springs. The steam rising up off the water The mosture from the heat forced the strands of hair to stick to her face. The ipod laid within a few feet of the edge of the pool. A pair of black headphones graced her ears as she tilted her head back upon her towel. Her hazel eyes remained closed as she drifted off to the song that came through her headphones. Josh Groban's voice slid into her subconscious taking a stroll down a memory lane that she hadn't been down since that faitful day a little over a year ago. Like the sound of silence calling, I hear your voice and suddenly I'm falling, lost in a dream. Like the echoes of our souls are meeting, You say those words, my heart stops beating. I wonder what it means. What could it be that comes over me? At times I can't move. At times I can hardly breathe. Her mind wandered back to when she and Sean had first met. A bar in California. A place he went to find his date for the week, and drink the stress of travel away. Hazel eyes met green eyes and sparks flew between them. A few drinks later, light conversation and they left together to have an all night orgy in his hotel room. Her mind remembered sneaking out of his room that first night...making sure that his memory was where it should have been. Her mind remembered how he sounded when he talked, the sound of his laugh and the way he held her all night long. When morning came, a trademark rose left on his pillow as a sign of that she was there. She tipped toed quietly to the door and slipped out into the night. Her memory flipped to another night with him. The night that she filmed the video of their sexual escape with him. A treasured memory of hers. She felt and remembered it all as just yesterday. A smile graced her features as the memories flooded over her. When you say you love me The world goes still, so still inside and When you say you love me, For a moment, there's no one else alive. The memories faded into their last night in the states together. The night they walked along the boardwalk hand in hand...silence beign the comfort between them. Each person lost in their own thoughts as they dealt with their own personal demons. Patti remembered saying those three little words and watching them settle in on Sean. And for that one single moment in time, there was no one else alive other then then those two. But it all came crashing down when he couldn't return the words to her. His mind wanted to be somewhere else and not where it should have been. With that little event...Patti was prepared to fly back home. But a change was coming. A change that would be the best for both parties. You're the one I've always thought of. I don't know how, but I feel sheltered in your love. You're where I belong. And when you're with me if I close my eyes, There are times I swear I feel like I can fly, For a moment in time, Somewhere between The Heavens and Earth. I'm frozen in time Oh, when you say those words. The memories rewinded and showed Sean chasing Patti all over Melbourne. Attempting time and time again to tell her that he loved her, and would go to the ends of the earth to chase her and prove it all to her. She fought the desire and the urges to be with him until she couldn't fight him anymore and let the moments in heaven and earth freeze them in place. Patti felt like she could fly when he said he loved her too. As if those words were enough to make up for the error of his ways. Time was frozen for a brief minute when he repeated the words over again. And this journey that we're on. How far we've come and I Celebrate every moment. And when you say you love me, That's all you have to say. I'll always feel this way. The memories brought along the changes of Patti going from waitress to valet to a highly trained wrestler. Victory after victory bounced around her memories as she relieved every match she had within the 4WW. Every win. Every loss. Every draw. They all came rushing back to her. The changes her life had gone through now seemed fullfilled and she was ready to move on with whatever was in her path. The battle with Annie brought a flinch to Patti as she saw the evil woman who tried to destroy her happiness. When you say you love me The world goes still, so still inside and. When you say you love me In that moment I know why I'm alive. Patti flinched more causing ripples in the water as her body shuttered as memories of fighting that woman over took her. Each time she battled her, she took al little more of Sean with her, and gave Annie less of him each time that they faced. The more they faced, the more the battle over him came stronger and stronger. Til one would be declared the winner We all know Patti walked away with the heart of Sean McClancy. A few months have passed and they were still the blissfull happy couple. But just as quickly it all changed. Pleasent happy memories turned to dark and forbidden things. Patti was replaced by Annie. Annie was the one by Sean. Not her. Annie was leading Sean to the upmost hights. Not her. Annie was doing everything that Patti should be doing. And it was more then a dream....it was becoming reality. When you say you love me. When you say you love me. Do you know how I love you? Patti woke up in a fright. The song in her headphones was finally coming to an end. The feeling in her chest told her otherwise. The change was taking place. Annie was ready to make her move and Patti had become powerless to stop her. Patti shut her I-pod off and slowly climbed out of the water. She grabbed her towel and rapped it tightly around her fame and walked the few feet back into the hotel. Her hazel eyes caught the brown eyes of the hotel's desk clerk. He was holding up two manila envelopes of some sort. Patti's name was scrawled across the front of it. Patti nodded at the little man and took the mail upstairs to her room. Patti slid the door open on her room and stepped inside. Her ever curious eyes bouncing from one envelope post marked 'Phoenix, Arizona'...the other had nothing on it other then strange writing and her name. Patti opted to open the one she didnt recognize first. She reached inside and slid out what appeared to be the backs of two pictures. A note was stuck to the one. Patti's hazel eyes scanned the note: Snapped these shots not more then three hours ago. Thought of you and immediately sent them to you. An Adoring Fan Patti raised an eyebrow as she flipped the shots over and saw her worst nightmare coming true. The shots dropped to the floor as Patti curled up in a ball. Tears streaming down her face as the pictures come into view.....
