Artemis and Fey related? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #272 Date: 11/02/03 Location: Home palpable • \PAL-puh-bul\ - adjective 1 : capable of being touched or felt : tangible *2 : easily perceptible : noticeable, manifest I do believe that is what Mr. Black Jack thinks he is. A palpable. Something that is touched or perceived as being the next big savior of the Four Winds. His promise of eliminating Featured Attraction from the federation have gone unanswered as of this week. With the looks of things, Featured Attraction has gone about making Mr. Slap Jack their own personal bitch. Destiny guesses that Annie must not be that satisfying as she claims to be. He may call himself the ‘Five Card Stud’, but Destiny would have to say he’s the ‘Five Card DUD’, that seems to be all he can do. He’s a dud like no other. His win/loss record isn’t that impressive. He lost to a girl with outside interference on her behalf. Plus he got his ass kicked by Draven Lox, and Patti’s own boyfriend. The boy sure does have this knack of making more enemies then friends. When will he learn? Destiny shakes her head as she moves on to examine Jamie. Jamie, on the other hand, we have no conflict with. Then why is Patti being forced to face him?: The man had the nerve to say Patti is nothing more then a T&A attraction for Fey McClancy. Fey in her own right is a T&A attraction but her actions in the ring made her more then just that, but if that is all that Jamie is thinking of, then Destiny knows that Patti has done her job in gaining his attention. That means the feminine wiles just may have to come out of the closet to keep the ‘Asian Sensation’ off his guard., because for in his shallow little mind, he believes that is all Patti is. But pairing Jaime and Slap Jack together is just a powder keg waiting to explode. The two won’t be able to co-exist in the same arena because they will want to concentrate on the possibly of getting jumped by the trio, so that should make it a rather easy victory for Destiny’s favorite couple. But what about Annie? She made it known last week that McClancy has until this weekend to make up his mind on what to do about his engagement to her. Patti knows that Sean is torn between the duties of his family and the feelings he has for her. She knows he’s never spoken the words of love to anyone but her. That evening in the garden in Australia will forever be in the mind of Patti and Destiny because it was written that way. Unfortunately, Annie can’t see beyond that point. The only thing driving her is the bitterness to have what she can’t have. Destiny knows Patti wont let her go. Destiny also sees the burning questions of the fans as to what side Patti and Sean are on. Its simple. They prefer to be what they are, neutral. True that it can and will probably hurt Sean’s sister that her very own brother won’t take sides, but why take sides, when its fun to watch everyone question your motives. And as long as Patti stays right on the line, then it makes it much easier to not have to chose sides when it comes time too. So you can just might as well guess that Patti will stay right there, and from the looks of things Mr. McClancy is staying right there too. So how about we just drop the attitudes and move on to something more important like the birthday of Fey and Sean McClancy. The CEO and the brother. Let us take a look as to what Patti is preparing. Place: The Kitchen Time: Halloween Eve Patti stands in the kitchen. Her long red hair pulled back in a ponytail, giving her face the appearance of being more a teenager then the 26 year old that she is. In two days time, her love, Sean McClancy and his sister, Fey would be celebrating their 28th birthday. The dilemma at hand was what to do for their birthday. Patti was faced with a difference. Most of her twin friends were born on the same day. So it was rather easy to do something for them. Sean and his sister had to be the difficult ones. Fey, being born on Halloween, and Sean being born on All Saints Day, gave a little clue into their personalities as a whole. Fey’s personality moved towards the odd and macabre, while Sean was more the saint and savor in Patti’s eyes. She smiled to herself and remembering his battle with Sean Summers just recently, as his way of showing to Patti that he is her knight. Patti smiled as she pulled laid the book she was reading on the counter. Her attempts at trying to find something special to do for Fey was proving to be a little unnerving. Patti was looking forward to prove to her future sister-in-law that she wasn’t out to ruin her brother, but to show the family that his love for her was real, and not just a way to get out of his engagement to the raven haired bitch that had tried unsuccessfully to come between them. Patti continued to flip through the book of traditions that she got off the internet for a few bucks. With as people in the fed coming from all over the place, this book was sure to give her a clue on how to do something special for someone. The learning of Sean and Fey’s ancestry had proved to be a little more confusing then anything. The Gaelic mythology was so different then her own. Sean was the decent of Bres himself, a man that was considered the God in charge of it all. From her vast reading of it, she had found that Bres married the woman, Bridgid. Both were the God and Goddess of Fertility in their respected line of mythology. A moment of fear overcame Patti as she realized that she may not get what she wants. Her love for Sean kept growing by the day, and the desire to start a family was getting bigger too. She continued to flip through the book she had before her. She was looking for something special to do for Fey. She had to find a way to bring a little bit of peace between them, and maybe this Sunday at Stormfront, Patti would sure like to be the first one to bury the hatchet. Even though the idle place would be in the middle of the shoulder blades of Annie. Patti continued to flip through the book, looking for the right gift for her precocious soon-to-be sister in law. The gift she had for Sean would be something that she would give to him later. The local lingerie store had come through at the last minute with that special birthday outfit that she had sent away for about a month ago. She knew that he would love it. She couldn’t wait to celebrate his. She shrugged off the thinking of Sean’s. She needed to concentrate on Fey’s. She needed something from her own traditions, something that she wouldn’t expect. She continued to look through her book. She had to find something of tradition. She continued to flip mindless through the book. Then something caught her eye. A Greek tradition involving the Goddess of the Hunt. She read quietly about the tradition involving Artemis. The goddess Artemis played an intriguing role in Greek mythology and religion. She was known as the 'Mistress of Animals' and the protectress of children, but she was also a huntress and the goddess who could bring death with her arrows. Myths such as the one about Niobe show Artemis as a strong willed and powerful goddess, a female who could punish injustices against the gods with ferocious and deadly accuracy. Artemis was the daughter of Leto and Zeus (the ruler of the Greek gods). Together with her twin brother Apollo she enjoyed the status and privileges of an Olympian. And as an Olympian goddess, Artemis was free to pursue her interests, and was often found frolicking in the forests, accompanied by a band of Nymphs. Myths and legends show that the goddess Artemis was aloof and free-spirited, and not constrained by husband or hearth. Her independent nature is further reinforced in a very important way, for in mythology and religion, the goddess remained eternally a virgin. Indeed, those who in some way compromised her strict requirements for chastity were severely punished by the maiden goddess. There are several tales that describe the swift and terrible retribution of Artemis. One of the most revealing of these stories involves the youth Actaeon.. In addition, Artemis was also responsible for punishing the nymph, Callisto. In myth, Callisto was at one point a follower of the virgin goddess, but when she became involved in an affair with the god Zeus, Artemis had her revenge on the unfortunate nymph . Patti smiled at what she just read. Another set of Greek Twins, which of course were related to her somehow. Artemis seemed to fit Fey with her jealousy and her distrust. Maybe this tradition would be something Fey would except from Patti with a smile, and maybe put her on the path of having a better relationship. If not, it sure was worth a try. Patti quickly set to work to make the bread. She knew Sean wouldn’t be home for hours. Gave her just enough time to make a loaf or two for them. And Fade Out.
