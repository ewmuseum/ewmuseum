Contemplation Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #459 Date: 12/01/04 Location: N/A Contemplation - RP#1 Role Play for Final Testament - 01/12/04 Each and every story worth telling has a beginning. A significant time when nothingness becomes something, a time when someone can begin to shape the person that they will become for the years to come. The story of a being, so powerful, so immaculant, begins with the real beginning... the creation: ...and God said, 'Let there be light.' Then there was light. ...and in seven days, the world inwhich we love dear, was born. ...and in this world, we were born. Born out of God's creativity and love. The world was not always cold and dark for Darek. There were days when his life was glorified by God's will, Darek had always wanted nothing more than a simple life, a family, a good job, and a place in God's heaven. As fate would have it, thigs were not as simple as they seemed. Through all the good in his life, it was not until Darek was touched by darkness that he had begun to scratch the surface of his mass potential... The day had started off relatively well. The sun rose, there was heat, light, and even before the crack of dawn, the streets were still hardly empty. Another day had passed and a new beginning had come, Darek stretched upwards, folding his hands. Crack. The sound of his knuckles echoed through the darkness. In an instant a small lamp flickers on and Darek kicks sideways setting his feet onto the cold wooden floor. He glances slightly at he clock as the minute hand rolls to the 12 and the hour hand sticks firmly on the 6. Bringing a fist to his face, he begins to wipe away the dreams of last night and attempt to bring himself into the new day. He wiggles his toes before standing up and heading towards the mirror on the wall. With a grin etched on his face he glares at the picture shown before him. Swirling with imperfections, Darek had known that his new job would bring out the best in him. It would be a chance to be himself and not have to hide the truth. He could be free to pass judgement on the sinners of the world and still mercifully find himself accepted into the pearly gates above. A loud sound is heard in the near distance. Averting his attention from the mirror, Dark reaches over and grasps a black cylindrical object. He pushes the object towards his head and it's not until Darek pushes 'talk' that the loud ringing noise ceases to exist. Groggily, 'hello?' 'Darek? Darek Angle?' a quaintly familiar voice questions. 'Yes. Who is this?' 'Ian, Ian Childress.' A momment passes by before Darek begins to realize full well just what exactly was going on. He takes a seat on his bed only capable of nodding. His thoughts begin to swirl in anticipation as Mr. Childress begins to make his proposition. Darek eventually agrees and sets the phone down; his face finds confort in his hands. He roughly rubs his eyes, and murmmers his thoughts loud enough to wake his partner. Amanda reaches over and runs her fingers down Darek's should to the small of his back, small bumps begin to flare from benieth his skin. In an instantanious motion he turns his head to meet the eyes of the holder of his heart (though many have come to believe he never had a heart). She pulls herself towards him with nothing more than a towel wrapped around her body. Darek smiles and for that instant the world had become his for the taking. He place the phone on the night table, then turns completely facing Amanda. 'Who was that?' She asks. 'Noone. Wrong number.' 'Go back to sleep then babe.' He kicks his legs upwards onto the bed. Amanda wraps her arms around his neck and plants a kiss onto his cheek. Still smiling she releases her grip and lays back down in the bed. Darek brushes his finger past her face, carefully pushes the thin blonde streaks from her eyes. His hand gentley slides down the side of her face and under her chin until he leans forward and kisses her. He closes his eyes and slowly is whisked away into thought and memory. The world as they know had only just begun to change into what it is today. It was one phone call that will forever remain a prominant day in history, a day when good was sent to cleanse the world of evil. Darek had grown up knowing full well what his destiny was, he was to bring to judgement, those who sinned, it was his duty to punnish the wicked and fully purify God's world. The Gulfsouth provided ample camoflauge as he went discreetly about his business, yet claimed it all to be a gimmick. Some people, people who would come to know the truth, included Darek's first ever tag team partner... James 'The Ladies Man' Slade. James Slade had always had his way with the ladies, a young, talented roster member of Global Championship Wrestling, he had once lived the magic with Darek. It was then, truth became too real for James, Darek would tell him stories, stories that would be enough to turn the pigmants in a person's hair white... It was not long until the undefeated Global Tag Champions, 'The Ladies Man' and Dark Angel, parted ways with their respective signings with competitor and rival federation, GWA. Darek enjoyed sucess and near instantanious federation gold in the Gulfsouth, just as he had in the GCW. The newely equipped Television Champion extended his hand forward and hoped that James would once again wish to relive the past. Instead, James had hoped an alliance with a female member of the roster would elevate him to a status, that he felt Darek could never reach. It was then, that James had turned on the Dark Angel... After his only friend and ally rejected his offer in partnership, Darek found himself contemplating the true meaning of life. As he walked slowly down the street, he could only remember wanting to hear the words of Xantos Dimiscus, a father figure. Scratching his head, he could not begin to understand the complexities of life, he had only just begun to open his eyes when the wailing sound of sirens filled his mind rather than his malcontent thoughts of life. Glancing down the roadway he could see the lights flashing and the noise only got louder and louder until he was at the scene. It was then, Darek first cried... 'My world had changed so quickly, everything and everyone ceased to matter at that momment. The world had given me Xantos as a father and they took him from me. I shed a tear that day, not because I was sad and not because I did not understand, but because I knew what exactly I was going to have to do. I sought retrobution for Dimiscus' murderer, I found him right where I last expected it, in the GWA. A radicallist and member of The Confederate Army, Major Payne, he was the one to blame. I was put through this pain, simply because I had something he wanted, he wanted to be called champion, the Gulfsouth Hardcore Champion. Our war, a brutal battle ended with the stench of burning flesh. I had gone nearly a year without feeling the lows of defeat, but it was Payne, who made me the being I am. I can remember the first two shots to my skull with the fire extinguisher, I could hardly keep my eyes open at the time, everything became so blurry. As I collapsed, I can remember that this would be the end of my reign. I remember thinking and believing that, this was the end of the Dark Angel. The lights faded as the casket was closed shut. If it had not been for the lack of oxygen, which numbed me enough to render me unconcious, I would have felt much more pain. The casket was covered in gasoline, then ignited. I burned slowly in that box of death until Payne allowed Gulfsouth staff to put out the flames... however, by then it was too late.' Some were surprised that Darek survived the match, and most were shocked when he returned the following week to face the woman that James had chosen over his offer of partnership. The Dark Knight stood before this woman, towering monsterously over her small body. It was when she swung, that her story really began to unfold. Dark Angel proceeded to continue a relentless assault on the woman until she could no longer move, and in three seconds, his memories the loss last week had faded. Somehow, at sometime, Darek had changed. His perception of life, his knowledge of pain and suffering, it all became to much for him. He embraced the Gulfsouth and in his touch, the Gulfsouth found a year of terror. For Darek to establish himself, his new attitude, his new outlook, caled for drastic actions. With on swift crushing blow Dark Angel retalliated against The Confederate Army, beatting all four members single handedly. Hardly satisfied, his thirst for blood was nearly quenched when he viciously turned on his stablemates and allies of the Lynch Mob. Darek knew he could be something much bigger, and because of James, because of one turning point in his life, he has steadily proved to the world and it's procrastinators just exactly how real his threat is. Within no time Darek found himself standing cautiously on the top of a cage. He looked down to the carnage on the surrounding floor below, bodies were broken and aflame. The Dark Angel had lost all control when he saw the tables being ignited, he knew he could not Fall From Grace again, instead, he knew it was time for the others to share in his pain. Warrior after warrior vanquished when they attempted to toss Darek from his heaven. In the end, it was the Angel who stood on the top, surveying his work, watching the expressions on the children's faces as blood fell, flesh burned, and all their hopes died with their fallen heroes. Dark Angel had become the Gulfsouth Champion. '...Under this killing moon, under this burning sky, the fire shining through. Hold my breath and I close my eyes...' The music begins to taunt. '...It is none of your concer, you want to crawl above? You gotta wait your turn. We'll watch the withces burn...' Darek rolls over to his right and begins to search for the clock. 'We'll watch the wiiiiiiiiiiiiiiitches buuuuuuuuuuuuurn!' He pushes the button and rolls onto his back. His eyes opened wide, glaring at the ceiling. 'Must have dozed off.' He turns to Amanda, she's sound asleep. He glances at the clock as it taunts him, reading, seven a.m. Finally gathering the motivation to get up, he pulls himself from the bed, the blankets fall and through the moonlight not much is visible. Through the darkness he reaches into his drawer and slides a pair of pants over his mostly nude body. Cautiously and quietly, he makes his way to the closet where he finds a shirt. He pulls it over his head and turns a half circle. His cheast seems much broader, unclothed. He pulls the shirt down the rest of the way and walks to the night table beside the bed. He takes a seat and begins to write: Amanda, I have only enough love in this world for one person. You are the one that I have chosen to be with for the rest of eternity. You have been here with me through the good, the bad and I know you would follow me to the depths of hell if I asked you to. You are more than I ever imagined that I would end up with. Sometimes because of who I am and how I feel, I take my work more personally than most, you've been the most understanding person in those regards. I hope that you will understand me now, I must go to StormFront alone. If last week was any indication to the extent of what could happen Tuesday, I do not want you to be present. I know that you can handle yourself, but this is my battle, this is the END of my war that started so long ago. Though, I can see their reasons in their eyes, they want what I have and wish to put me through pain to take it. I have felt that pain before and last time it caused the loss of one of my loved ones. Knowing him, his friends will not be far behind him either. As you know and have seen, Chris West and I have an extensive and long history, that is where my concern lies. You know, I know, he will do anything, anytime to get what he wants, this time... he has to get through me... Darek slips away into thought. His mind begins to dwell on the numerous times that he has had encounters with 'Insane' Chris West. He would always remember the hellish battle in which Darek nearly lost his career and United States Championship, but then he had the comfort in knowing that he vacated the US Championship undefeated after he was crowned the Gulfsouth Champion. West and Darek had only fought one other time, long before then, when Dark Angel first became the GWA Hardcore Champion. Recently, Darek had found himself aligned with Chris West in the late Superior Championship Wrestling Federation. It was there that West and Dark Angel would begin to really make names for themselves. Darek closes his eyes and begins to recall the fond memories... 'Todd Dream should kill himself!'
