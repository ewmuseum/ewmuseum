Getting Ready El Lobo de la Muerta 4WW Exposures #163 Date: 9-27-03 Location: 4 Winds Arena No one knows what it's like To be the bad man To be the sad man Behind blue eyes No one knows what it's like To be hated To be fated To telling only lies But my dreams They aren't as empty As my conscience seems to be I have hours, only lonely My love is vengeance That's never free No one knows what it's like To feel these feelings Like I do And I blame you No one bites back as hard On their anger None of my pain and woe Can show through But my dreams They aren't as empty As my conscience seems to be I have hours, only lonely My love is vengeance That's never free No one knows what it's like To be mistreated To be defeated Behind blue eyes And no one know how to say Their sorry And don't worry I'm not telling lies But my dreams Are as empty As my conscience seems to be I have hours only lonely My love is vengeance That is never free No one know what it's like To be the bad man To the sad man Behind blue eyes ªƒª Behind Blue Eyes; Limp Bizkit ªƒª ~Beneath all the false coverings within every single one of us. We all try to hide who and what we truly are. Times, actions, and memories mold us all into the very creatures that we are now. There is absolutely no substitution for anything that has come our way. There is always something that stretches deep within our soul keeping us holding onto every little thing that comes our way. In the words of a great lyrical composer, Kevin says “I’ve tried hard enough, I’ve got high enough, and I just don’t cry enough. Grown men don’t cry enough it’s time that I cry myself to sleep tonight.” We all lose control where we believe that we’ve wasted our time on nothing of value. The will and strength to go on is much stronger than anyone can ever imagine. There is a blue flame that burns on the inside all of us tormenting every last one until we succeed in our ultimate goal. Here we are again in the same familiar place, on the same familiar path as traveled down before. Change is inevitable, but change cannot be forced. No matter how many times we go down the same path, no matter how many times we make the same mistakes. It is always the same thing when people try to change each other. What is it really you want? And why don’t you just let them be when they have chosen their own paths? Who are we to judge when we ourselves are at the bottom of the bottle? Perhaps there’s something wrong with me, or just maybe it’s not me. Maybe it is you and your issues of perfection. We are already falling and the pace that we plummet is faster than anything that we can come to terms with. We can either use our wings to fly or we can fall to our deaths. So what will your choice be? Will you use your wings and fly to new heights or will you plummet and perish? Open up your eyes and make your choice now. ~ {The scene comes to us as we see El Lobo pacing around the locker room in the back trying to get ready for his match later that evening. He sits in his chair and pulls his duffel bag in close as he pulls out several objects. First being a roll of grip tape as he sets that on the table before him, the second being a jewelry case, a shirt and then finally a picture. He sets the picture in front of him as he grabs the grip tape and begins taping his fist.} El Lobo: Why am I still here? Why can’t I give it up and try and move on? Instead of trying to gain new heights I’m forcing myself to stay here and avenge something. Why can’t I just pull away and go about my day? Why do I have to sit here and get angry and go out there? Where am I going? What’s my destination? Can anyone answer my questions and give an explanation? Or must I sit here as always not knowing anything, not even knowing what’s goin’ on inside my head? I think it’s really fucked up that no one understands what’s goin’ on in my life. Hell I’m not sure if I even understand what’s goin’ on. {He wraps his hands for the last time before biting the tape and pressing it down making sure all the loose ends are securely fastened. He opens the jewelry case and takes a couple of things out. The first thing being a small compact disc player as he places the head phones over his head. He reaches inside his bag and pulls out some body paint and a small foam brush. He begins painting an intriguing design on his arm and around his neck.} El Lobo: Man I’m not ready for anything coming before me. There are so many things I need to conquer before I can move on. I can’t move on I just can’t find strength in this moment of weakness. I’m burdened with all these blames and regret that just killing me. It’s so fucked up because nothing’s changed but nothing’s the same. I can’t move on or figure out why it should be like this. {Suddenly from around the corner we hear the sounds of a mellow type of song using the piano as its main source of melody. El Lobo lifts his head up earnestly looking for the source of the sound. Being intrigued by it he turns off the portable compact disc player and slowly follows the sound around the hall. He goes to a hallway where the music gets louder and louder and words become clearer and more meaningful. El Lobo steps to the door to see Patti Anderson stretching out in her black leather outfit. Right by her left foot is a stereo that has been turned up as she listens to the lyrics taking in the meaning of the song. She looks underneath herself between her legs and sees an upside down El Lobo. She stands up and looks over at him as if he were some fan. She walks over and looks at his bare chest noticing that his wounds were finally healing.} Patti: Hey Tommy boy I see that you’re healing better. You can barely recognize the scars. Well except that one right here. The color is fading from the cuts. El Lobo: Ehh it’s not as bad as it looks. I’m used to all the abuse, I’ll heal and get better soon… yo creo. But the reason I came here Patti… is… ummm… {Patti looks on at him hoping he will get this done with little confusion.} Patti: Yes… what is it Tom? El Lobo: Well I was getting ready and thinking when I heard your radio on… {Patti reaches over about to turn off the radio before El Lobo stops her.} Patti: Oh was it too loud? I’m sorry I’ll turn it down… El Lobo: No it’s not too loud at all. I was just wondering what song that is and who sings it? Patti: Oh them? That’s a band called Rascall Flatts. That song you heard was Movin’ On. It’s about how a man sits there and looks back on everything in life. How his friend helps him find strength to carry on. You see the man had a lot of demons per say. He acknowledged those demons fought them and still wasn’t happy. He was stuck on all the mistakes he made in life. He then gets the courage and strength to move on, like he found strength in his moment of weakness. El Lobo: You think it’s possible that I could borrow it? Patti: Yea it’s no problem just make sure I get it back because they’re one of my favorite bands. El Lobo: Don’t worry you’ll get back just as you gave it to me. Muchisimas gracias Patti: De nada Tommy. {El Lobo takes the disc and walks back to his dressing area. He pops in the disc and places the headphones back on his head. He takes a seat and starts to listen to the song as he continues to paint his body. He stops for a moment listening to the words of the song as we start to see tears stream down his face. His lips begin to quiver as he lifts his arms up and wipes the tears away from his eyes with his taped fists. He grabs the picture sitting on the table and looks at it examining its every aspect. We focus in on the picture. We see El Lobo and Angél in a room full of hearts and a love motif smiling and happy. We see tears fall down onto the picture as El Lobo places it back into the bag. He scrummages around and pulls out a vile of liquid. He looks at it and stares.} El Lobo: Angél que bueno fui a ti? Me falta ti Angél. It’s all my fault and I apologize for everything I put you through. Please come back to me can’t you see that I’m sorry? I swear on mi vida that I’m sorry I should’ve been there. I should’ve protected you better. I’ve failed you mi amour and I desperately wish for your forgiveness and love. Please come back to me mi Angél de vida. Bring me back to life and I will never fail you again my love. Please come back and grant me peace. All I want is you please come back. Please forgive me, please take me back… {The scene then zooms in on the vile as we notice what it is. He clenches it tight and begins to cry a little bit louder. Suddenly a familiar hand rests upon his shoulder. It’s the same hand that was on his shoulder several weeks ago when Angél suffered her terrible fate. The scene cuts to black as be begins to look up. Who did that hand belong to? Was it Sweet Lady Heroin once again or was it someone else? Whatever the case we will find out within the next few weeks.}
