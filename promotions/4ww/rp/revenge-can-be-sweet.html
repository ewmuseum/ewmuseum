Revenge Can Be Sweet Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #714 Date: 28/05/04 Location: N/A IT BEGINS, WITH A CHALLENGE _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ Darkness is an epidemic, it spreads like a great plague vasting consuming more and more. People have dedicated their lives to good and some to evil, as corny as it sound the truth is known. A war has been setting for quite some time now and it is about time the first shot was fired. An epic struggle between good and evil will determine the fate of mankind. The standard had been set, the Saviour could ill afford to forfeit another battle; not with a storm brewing. The savious had proclaimed that his legacy would be left for future generations to see; all built upon the sweat, blood, tears, bodies of the fallen, the Saviour will sit upon a throne and mark his dynasty... Could the soldiers of the Four Winds unite and stand before the darkness that seemed to beckon warrior after warrior at any odds. The establishment sent in more fighters only to be vanquished by a stronger evil. However, greed is a basic symbol of evil and evil will seduce anyone at any cost. There is no such thing as betrayal when one evil doer deceives another. Perhaps the most devious and treacherous villian of all had managed to pull out of the last encounter with the Saviour unscathed and victorious. This heyena would prove to be more of a thron than anyone ever before. The darkness has now completely consumed Darek. His mind, his body, his soul all rotten with vile thoughts and feelings. He could only begin to comprehend the matters of love as he pull the cord from the hospital wall. As the tears fell he knew all that was good in him had died with her and that noone would ever trust or love him again. He knew, for Patti, it was worth a shot and to prove to the world that he could be the Saviour of the Four Winds were his only thoughts now. Through desperate manipulation he had conceived a plot to become the Saviour and Patti's knight in one fell swoop while taken the innocent with him. He would be dedicated to the darkness and the darkness to him. His mind could only dwell upon the thought of being completey consumed and for the time being he had that to hold onto. He had sacrificed all, his wife, his son, and perhaps his career to prove his worth to Patti, the world and even himself. He knew this weeks battle would not be easy but he also knew he could not lose again. Another defeat would humilate him and as the Saviour he would not bear to hold that feeling. He could remember standing out in the cold rain with his arms extended to the horizons. He remembered because it was the first time he had prayed... in 6 years. Only this time, he prayed to the darkness; prayed for it to take him. I] _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ Even through the bleakest of days, the dawning hope of vengence is the only means of survival for those who bleed the Immortal blood. His mind was squared on the retribution for Darek's treacherous crime. Xantos reaches down and adjust the volume of the stereo in his vehicle. His eyes seem cold, almost lifeless as they glare into the streets. The car seems to be empty, except a single, cromne passenger. After fidgeting with the controls on his deck, Xantos finds himself running his index finger around the curvature of his new found 'friend'. It seems as though his eyes do not even blink as his concentration remains apt and able, ready to snap at anytime. His blank, expressionless features provide a great sanctuary for his feelings, the only thing certain was he was going to get his revenge. The sun beats down through the windshield and Xantos quickly reaches for a pair of pearl blue Oakly shades. With a snap of his wrist the arms unfold and he slides them behind his ears, and over the bridge of his nose. His right hand begins to tap the steering wheel as it guides it steadily forward. The black tinted side and back windows provide shade and confidentiality, he glances over his shoulder quickly and then makes a turn. Stepping firmly on the gas, his Mercedez accelerates quickly and fires down the strip. The bass vibrates the windows as he begins to nod his head... 'Tell me exactly what am I supposed to do, now that I allowed you to beat me?' ...His hand stops tapping and grabs the wheel firmly. His vision is fixed straight ahead and the music seems to have gotten him into a better mood. He glances down to his right and next to the gun is a small black case of compact discs. He stops and continues to sing for a moment. The ironic pleasure of planning revenge while singing his father's former theme and yet favorite song. Xantos leans a little deeper into his seat and a slight smile begins to form around his lips. Again and again the thought of a bullet piercing the flesh of Patti Anderson consumed his mind, eatting it away. He couldn't help but be in a sadistic trance that would soon bring him to the city of this weeks StormFront. What did Patti offer to his father that Amanda could not? He had often wondered just what in God's name was going through his father's head. But that became meaningless the second he looked down at he gun again. He new what his mission was and he knew that he had to suceed, if not for himself, but for his mother. He had loved her and did not regret her decision to hide him from Darek until he was old enough to understand just who his father was. Xantos didn't mind growing up in an Catholic Orphanage, he was treated well and he clearly excelleed in not only athletetics, but in scholarly duties aswell. He was never know to make an irrational decision and now, on the brink of his sanity he travels like the wind to exact his revenge. 'Vile treacherous bitch,' he mutters aloud. 'When she looks into my eyes she will wish that my father was her knight.' He gingerly plucks the disc from the tray and slides it back into the black case. He flips through quickly glancing over to see his spot. He stops and pulls out the Evanesence CD. he pushes it in and pulls his fingers away searching for the small remote. As he finds it he begins to flip through the songs and somes to an instanious halt at a song... My Immortal... It's symbolic reference in title and the fact that he had dedicated this the song to explain his parents relationship. He could only wish his mother was alive to share his feelings, instead he was left cold and alone, capable of doing the unmentionable. I DO -past- [pt. He could never remember feeling so alive and despite being who he was, he had never imagined this day in his life would come. He glances down at the watched wrapped around his wrist. He nods solemnly and continues to look. 'What time do you have?' Darek asks the driver. Turning his head towards the back, 'Three Ten, sir.' 'Me too.' A strange silence fills the air as Darek seems to tense up slightly. His finger nudges it's way inbetween his neck and colar and begins to try to loosen it. A slight sign of perceipitation begins to form above his brow as he reaches into his jacket pocket with a shakey hand. Pulling out a white cloth he carefully pats it against his forhead taking the dampness into the cloth. 'It's ok to be nervous sir,' the driver speaks up, 'It's a special day in your life, everything will be fine.' Darek places the cloth into his jacket pocket, 'God I hope so. I would do anything for her.' 'Well if that's the truth, then you will be just fine.' The driver turns back once again, 'We're nearly there.' Darek sits back and finds some comfort in a glass of wine. He carefully raises the glass to his lips and takes a good drink. Fourty minutes from now he would be standing before the eyes of God and standing with what he considered to be the most perfect creature on the planet, Amanda Raine Johnson. When the time had come he hoped that she would say those two words that would make them soul mates for eternity. More importantly, as he sat the glass down and pulled out a cigarette, he began to wonder if he was ready to say those words. He knew the step he was about to take could define him as a person for the rest of his life, it was a thought that had come to hid mind and stayed there plaguing his judgement for the duration of the trip. 'I don't know if I'm ready to give it up yet...' 'Give what up?' The driver questions. Darek's eyes find those of the driver in the reflection of the rearview mirror, '..my freedom.' 'Union is beyond on individuals freedom, it's about becoming one with someone you love and sharing a free future together.' Darek struggles to pull his eyes away from the hired driver. He manages to find the window switch and rolls it down. His head turns to the outside and the fresh air begins to circulate through his body, almost rejuvinating him, reassuring him everything would be alright. Yet, his mind had never allowed him to be free, it knew there may be strings attatched and it ate away at him constantly. '...I- I- think... gonna be... sick.' Darek jams his head out of the window. The house lights had begun to buzz and illumination was only a few moments away. The shadow of a tall creature seems to slither upward into a ring as the sound of a motor running echos throughout the empty arena. Slowly above head the ominous cage begins to lower towards the ring. The shadow doesn't move, it's head tilted upwards watching the cage draw ever so close to the canvass. As the lights begin to shine a little brighter, the shadow has dispersed and a being dressed in black slacks, a silver dress shirt covered by a black jacket, and a red tie, stands eagerly in the ring. The cage lowers slowly and almost comes to a complete hault as it begins to embrace for contact with the ring. The being's face is blank, his spiked red hair stands on end like soldiers on guard. His eyes are cold and blue, looking deep into them is as if you were drowning in the abyss. Darek cracks a devious smile as the cage and ring become one. He walks briskly towards the steel and begins to examine it cautiously. His hands reach out as if it had called to him, his fingers trace the steel square outlines and his smile fades into nothingness. Grasping tightly onto the cage, Darek steps back and extends his arms fully away from the steel. With a sudden burst of movement, Darek begins to shake the cage violently. The sound of cold steel rubbing fills the void and Darek just continues to shake for the time being. Coming to a sudden hault he releases his grip from the cage and his arms fall to his sides. Again the grin has come back onto his face and a joyous look overtakes his eyes. For the first time in several days, Darek could remember being truly happy. 'Have you ever needed something so bad?' He begins to speak and the words come out of his mouth like a masterpiece escaping it's owner. 'I have done waht it takes to prove my loyalty to Patti Anderson. I aided her in a time of need when the FAW had attempted to take our coveted North Wind Title. I was there for her against Jerms... Although we suffered that terrible loss, she was kept safe by these hands. Last week, another attack and the plotting of an action that makes even me sick had occured.' His smile fades and the joy seems to escape his eyes. 'Xantos, my own flesh and blood seems hell bent on exacting his revenge. What I did that day had nothing to do with love and everything to do with saving your mother from becoming something I should never have asked her to become, a monster. She gave up her innocence, her life, her everything to be with me, she had changed,her thirst for blood had become more apparent than mine and I had to save her from that.' His hands find comfort in the cool steel. 'Then there is the one they call Shoudou. Warrior of the night, being of unconventional power, let me explain something to you in terms that even you may comprehend. Should you lay another finger on Patti Anderson, I will take that as a direct assault upon myself. You have been marked and in due time you too will become just another victim. You see, if it's No Mercy that you want, then it will be No Mercy that you receive. My lust for carange has grown and since the last weeks, I can only hope to get the chance to bring you to your knees once again.' Slowly he presses his face against the cage. 'It's meant to keep the men in the ring, or is it meant to keep the others out. Xantos, Shoudou, Tarrant, Avenger, it doesn't matter who, should any of you set foot into my ring, should any of you attempt to take my throne, you will be my definition for the words pain and suffering. StormFront draws ever so close and the impending doom for our beloved Four Winds Champion begins to become more and more apparent. I stand here, waiting, wanting, just one chance to tear Jerms limb from limb.' And now on the brink of his very own sanity he drives ever so close to his final destination a place inwhich his path could perhaps cross with his destiny. Pushing the shades further up onto his nose, Xantos free hand begins to wiggle through his glove box looking for a tissue. As his fingers feel the rough texture of a napkin he pulls it out and puts it immediately under his nose and blows. Crumpling the napkin into a ball he tosses it out of his semi-open window. His index finger finds comfort in turning the stereo off completely. A thousand thoughts per minute seem to haunt the mind of Xantos as he begins to wonder why, why his life had to be so different... WHY?! Was it punnishment, had he done something wrong in the past. He often wondered what things would have been like had he had a real father. A man that would teach, discipline and be there for him. Most importantly, he had always wondered what it would feel like to be loved by his father. The tears had begun to flow more freely now and he could no longer maintain his composure. With a fierce anger harnessing within his body he tries to release some of his feelings through his automobile as he presses even harder onto the gas. The car accelerates and begins to pick more and more speed. As he races faster down the highway the tears seem to slow. He tries to even out his breathing hoping to regain himself and come back to the land of the sane. It had become apparent that he was allowing himself to be taken deep into regression by his own thoughts... As calm begins to set in, Xantos reaches down to the system and is about to flick the switch on when from behind him, a faint red tinge can be seen. The red sson turns to blue and then in unision the two colors begin to blend together. The lights become brighter and brighter, soon the sound of a siren can be heard as Xantos looks back over his shoulder. 'Fuck.' Xantos peers down to the gun sitting in the passenger seat, he looks at the speckles or red blood that stain the cromne piece. His face becomes blank once again as he fixes his cold gaze onto the shifter. His left foot squeezes the clutch inward and he shifts from fourth gear to fifth. The Mercedez speeds up even faster, after a few seconds the flashing lights disappear out of his rear sight. Xantos looks right over his shoulder for a moment checking to see if the police were still behind him. The faint flashes of the lights are still seen as he turns forward again with a smug grin plastered on his face. 'Persistant bastards.' For a split second Xantos begins to understand his fate. His destiny had now been revealled to him at this time of deep desperation. His finger pushes play and 'Going Under' begins to play. Like a means to forshadow his new path, Xantos finally smiles through the tears. Though his smile was not one of happiness, but one of closure. Soon after, the two had sealed their union with a kiss blessed under the eye's of God. Darek had known that he would eventually find love, but never had he expected it to find him. Amanda was beautiful, she was an amazing person and Darek considered himself lucky to be with her. He had worshiped the very ground she walked upon. While their home life was at it's peak, Darek had sent the ominous warrior of God in to judge another sinner, his career was taking a turn for the worse... or better? The Gulfsouth was an era inwhich the Angel had defined himself. Learning through his losses, suffering from the pain of the loses. His loses consisted of being betrayed by ones he had thought were his friends, being taken down in an epic battle that claimed the good man that once existed within the soul of Darek. When the casket was closed and Dark Angel felt the flames consume his surroundings, all that was good in him were burned alive and so to was his heart. Amanda was no longer his primary love, Darek had become possessed by trinkets of gold and yearned to be crowned as the ultimate ruler of the Gulfsouth. He sacrificed nearly everything he and Amanada had worked so hard for to make a name for himself when he rebelled against his own God. His blackened heart was no longer capable of the kind of love it once felt, Amanada had known this and yet she stayed with him. She vowed to love him through good and bad, she vowed that she would love him for eternity. Giving herself to him, she slowly was consumed by the hatred that plagued Darek's soul for some time now. Blind to the very changes in a woman he once worshiped, Darek couldn't even notice that Amanda in her own right had become more than capable of handling herself. She had become so much like him, so close of a mirror image that even though he did still love, her new state, her absolute hatred for all things living, worried even him beyond belief. Darek knew he had corrupted this delicate flower, forever changing the person she was... that was when Amanda had truly died... Yet he continue to love his creation, it was not until she took shards of broken mirrors to her wrists that Darek had felt compelled to free her from his evil grip... He prayed to be nothing like his father, he prayed for his mother, he prayed for his freedom. He quickly shifts up another gear. He stares directly forward, straight into the sun and instantly closes his eyes... He had always known he would end up like his father, but since had had never met him while growing up, he could only hope his father was a good man with good intentions. He realised now in this moment that his father never was really a man, he was indeed a monster, one consumed by hatred and Xantos could never understand how he would allow himself to be that man. As he prayed, he prayed for an end to the Angel... 'God as my wittness, Jerms will defeat Darek...' he mutters, nearly incoherently to himself. His eyes open once agian and he tries to find comfort in his vision, but he soon remembers the circumstances of his current velocity. He sits upright and takes a long hard look over his right shoulder. The sirens have faded and the lights seem to have disappeared. A sigh of relief escapes tfrom Xantos slightl open mouth. He takes a deep breath in and turns backforward. The end of 'Going Under' just finishes playing and Xantos' eye's begin to tell the fear of another story nearly instantly. His car flies down the highway and across a long metal belt sprawled across the road. Sharp spike puncture all of the tires on Xantos balck Mercedez. He pulls the steering wheel desperately towards the right as his car continues to spin left. His eyes close and soon his life begins to flash before his eyes. Forcing his eyes open Xantos refuses to allow this to be his final moment. He knew his mission could not fail and that he would rid the world of the poison referred to as Patti Anderson and her pathetic wanabe knight, the Dark Angel. Finally he brings the car to a straight path and he maintains a well over one hundred mile per hour speed on the rims of his tires. Sparks begin to fly everywhere in a mad frenzy as the rubber tires eventually catch flame. Xantos tucks his head out of the window as he sees the black smoke, he cannot believe his eyes or luck when he sees the flames consuming what's left of his tires. *HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK!* The unforgetable sound of an eighteen wheeler's horn begins to signify the begining of the end.
