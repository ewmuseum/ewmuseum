The Hurricane: Dénouement

11 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Hurricane: Dénouement Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #1004 Date: 10/11/04 Location: LLL's Office. (The scene opens up within the familiar office of Luscious Lady Luck. She is sitting there at here desk, just looking out at the camera. She is wearing a low cut lilac shirt.) Lady Luck: Yes Yes, I’ve heard this all before. It’s my fault. I’m the one who did it. If it weren’t for me then someone wouldn’t be the way they were. Yeah yeah. It’s all familiar. It’s not like people don’t always blame me because I am who I am and the fact that I have confidence that brims over to the point of being cockiness…… Slayer, you are saying nothing new at all. You really aren’t. I’ve been there, done that. Oh poor me. Oh poor me. Whatever. Get over yourself. It’s a dog eat dog world and someone has to end up wearing milk bone underwear……. Five years ago, my dear, I’ve dealt with this exact same thing. Five years ago I dealt with someone who wasn’t confident in themselves and their abilities. I took someone who didn’t know what she had and I made something out of them. I made them realize exactly what they were worth. I made them realize who they were and why they were…….. All this bitching and moaning is nothing new to me. All of this is something that I’m not unfamiliar with. To me, it’s a challenge. A project of sorts. Not too much unlike what I had to deal with several years ago. And what a job I did so many years ago. What a job. I should be the envy of motivational speakers. I don’t know anyone who has ever done as well as I have……. Unfortunately, it may have been too good. I took someone who was toiling around, serving as a bar wench, and made her into something she never dreamed. I made her successful. I gave her the confidence she needed to be successful. Too successful almost. It’s almost like it was yesterday. It’s almost like it just happened. (Trips rest her chin on her fist, and leans forward, thinking. A flashback scene is happening again. After all, that is what this series of RP’s is all about. In case you hadn’t noticed that part and are just noticing now, this is a flashback RP. Thanks to Jen/Patti for helping me with this flashback.) (The scene opens up inside of a lavish hotel room. Lying on her bed wearing next to nothing is Luscious Lady Luck. She is staring up at the ceiling, with an upset look upon her face and a pile of papers all around here. She is reading some of them, before throwing them back on the bed. She sits up on the bed, revealing her red lace bra and panties that she is wearing and barely containing her. She reaches to the phone, starts pressing buttons. Over the phone’s speaker you can here the phone on the other end ringing.) Voice: 'Ello? (LLL looks a little relieved hearing the familiar voice on the phone. She lies back down on her bed, hands behind her head.) LLL: Hello beautiful. How is down under? Voice: Bloody lonely and empty without you here, but I'm dealing with it. LLL: I’m sorry to hear that. Trust me the feeling is quite mutual Patti. And the news just keeps getting worse. Patti: Wha? The puppy you just got was killed in those bloody annoyances of hurricanes was she? LLL: (giggle softly) No, the pups fine. I left her back home when I came down here. Unfortunately, I wish I could say the same for the Florida operation. Totally wiped out. Patti: Wiped out? What do you mean wiped out? What about our plans? LLL: Wiped out as in destroyed. I went to the building after the second Hurricane we had and there was nothing left. Just a pile of rubble and some random pictures. And I, not thinking, didn’t realize there was a second wave coming, so nothing is backed up except in our main database. So the weekend has become just as much a disaster as it looks like I’ll be working. (Silence fills the room.) LLL: But I’m sure that we can figure something out. I’m just going to be required here in the States for a while. (The screen splits to allow a shot of a younger version of Patti Anderson standing in her kitchen, a rather pissed off look crosses her face.) Patti: Yer required? Bloody fucking nonsense!! You said that you would be here, I've already taken the vacation from work, and all you can seem to think about is yer fucking work.... LLL: Honey, it’s not like I have anyone here I can trust to rebuild this operation. The only person I had I fired because she was badmouthing you, and I wasn’t going to put up with that. There was no way I was going to let anyone say anything bad about you. But unfortunately now I’m up the creek without a paddle. If you’d like I’ll fly you in. We can still have time together and you can even help me start rebuilding. Patti: Why should I fucking help you? The rumors down under are pointing to the fact that you just used me love. You used me to make money for yourself. You have no one else to blame but yourself for firing someone. Just do me a favor, Diana, and forget that I exist. Your business has and always will come first, that is why you wanted me from that first night we met. Just... [Tears begin to fall down Patti's face as she fights to withhold the emotion in.] Patti: Just leave me the bloody hell alone. LLL: That’s not true. None of that is true. I do love you. I love you more than anything. Why would I just randomly fire one of my most trusted assistants for bad mouthing you if I didn’t love you? I didn’t use you to make money. That was the last thing on my mine. You have that factor that would make you a star wherever you went. I just brought it out of you. I made you realize what you had. But you or more to me on a more personal level. You will always be more to me on a personal level. Don’t do this Patti. Please don’t do this. Patti: Don't do what? End a relationship that you created to satisfy yer craving for the female flesh? Who do you think yer talking to Diana? LLL: (sounding desperate, almost begging.) The love of my life. The woman who has taken me from hello and made my every belief in a soul mate real. That is who I am talking to. Let me make this up to you. Let me fly you in. Let me buy you your own business. Anything. Just let me know what. [Patti wipes the tears from her eyes as she fights to remain strong.] Patti: You can just bloody forget I ever existed, Diana. I told you that you would not get another chance should you break yer word. I don't want a business, I don't want yer money, and I just want you to leave me the bloody hell alone. [Patti slams down the phone and drops down onto the chair in front of the island. She lets the tears fall as the screen returns to a full view of LLL.] LLL: I never meant to hurt you. It wasn’t supposed to happen like this. This isn’t the way things are suppose to happen. (LLL flips over on the bed burying her head in the pillows.) LLL: I’ll never forget you. I’ll never let you go. Someday, somewhere, my love, we will be together again. (The phone starts the familiar tones of the operator and shortly there after the buzzing. The flashback ends returning to the original scene of LLL. Tears are slowly falling down her cheeks, ruining her makeup.) LLL: Such a strong woman she had become. She knew exactly what she wanted and was able to run with it. Such an admirable trait. I built my love up to where she needed to be, to where she could reject me. Disappointing, yes. Encouraging, most definitely….. You can not deny what I have done for you Patti. Imagine where you would’ve been if I wouldn’t have helped you. Imagine where you would’ve been if I didn’t give you the confidence to make your life better, to make a difference in your life. Imagine where you would’ve been if I didn’t give you your big break, or if I hadn’t made you realize what you had……. Yes, it still hurts, deeply. But I never did forget. I never did let you go. Sure, I’ve been with other people, but you will always be the one. And we will be back together again. Somewhere, somehow. It will happen…… You tried to run, my sweet, but I still found you. You didn’t think I would forget, did you? I hope not. Changing your number, moving, all that, it was just minor inconveniences. I knew what I had given you. And I knew it would grow within you. I gave you the confidence to make more of yourself. I gave you the confidence to make yourself into a star from what would be perceived as a bar wench. And I knew it was a matter of time that you would become a star and I could once again find you….. You did, and I have…… And Slayer, much like Patti, I can build you up to. I can make you the star. I can give you the confidence you need to pull yourself out of the doldrums and make your self more than you ever imagined. Sure, you’ve held titles. You’ve held people in your heart and visa versa, but that confidence, it’s never been there, has it? Of course not. The true confidence in yourself has never been there……. Slayer, I can help so much. I can give you all of this and more. Yes, we are wrestling at Stormfront. But you, you are more than just an opponent to me at this point. You are a project. You are someone who needs that extra dose of go get’em. You’re someone who needs that extra dose of confidence to go out and take things. I’ve done it before, and my results are proven. As everyone in 4WW will know the name, Patti Anderson is the results I can tout as someone I gave that confidence to. You, I can do the same for you. Just say the word……. You can call me cocky. It may be true. But cocky and confidence those are two words that really can go hand in hand. Much like madness and brilliance, it is impossible to know where one ends and the other begins. But, I do know where they are and where confidence ends and cockiness starts. It is important to know these things if you truly are the Savior of the 4WW. Think about it Slayer. Think about where you will be without me. (LLL blows a kiss at the camera as it fades out.)
