It's Just A Matter of Trust Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #335 Date: 11/23/03 Location: Somerwhere in My Mind Trust 1 a : to place confidence : 1. DEPEND b : to be confident : HOPE 2 : to sell or deliver on credit transitive senses 1 a : to commit or place in one's care or keeping : ENTRUST b : to permit to stay or go or to do something without fear or misgiving 2 a : to rely on the truthfulness or accuracy of : BELIEVE b : to place confidence in : rely on c : to hope or expect confidently 3 : to extend credit to Trust, which is something I’m going to have to do this week. I have to trust two people that will probably more then likely turn around and stab me in the back. Blackjack has become an ego maniac because he wants to bloody well get into Fey’s pants. Wait, maybe im mistaken he wants to get into Ian’s pants. Nah, I’m thinking it’s the option of getting into the pants of both. He has yet to prove to me that he is the best, and its probably nothing more then riding on Jaime and my coattails to get a victory in that column. Personally, I hold more victories in my little pinky then he could ever dream of having. So I’m entrusting my record to a man that spends more time insulting my future sister-in-law and her husband, I have to be totally bloody insane to do that. I swear, maybe Slap happy jack there needs to wake up and realize that he’s in a survivor series style match. I want and desperately need to win this match against Todd Dream, will give me some strong power going into the title match at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. And of course on the other side of Slap happy Jack is Jamie. A man that will probably have another beef to pick with me because of my costing him the title during that lumber jack match. It wasn’t my fault, but when neither gave me a good reason to be on their side, I did what was best for mei. I wanted a champion that would give me competition. And after doing that, I’m pretty sure that Mr. Jamie will want to avenge the loss he suffered at the hands of me. So I’m pretty much going into this match a marked woman. With Sean at ringside, he’ll be watching my back to make sure that nobody will interfere in the match from the outside, and with the bell to bell rule. Now on the other side of the ring, is the unlikely crew of Mr. Dream, Mr. Marvelous, and Mr. West. The tricky trio. Personally, I don’t have any problem with two of the. As with the third one, that is were the problem lies. You see. Mr. Dream, has something that I want, and come this Sunday I will have it. I will own that title that is around your waist, and if I do say so myself, will look so much better. Mr. Marvelous, can have at Slap Jack all he wants, same with Jamie. The only thing I ask of the two of you is that you leave Dream to me. One extra chance to get my hands on him before the Cup is all I want. I don’t care if it comes down to just me and him in the ring, for that is the only thing I care about. Well, not the only thing I care about. I need to remember that little Irish bitch is at ringside too. Her mind will be trying to attempt to take back the one thing that I will not let go of. Annie, you will learn your place in all of this. Sean McClancy has told you twice now. He does not want to honor the commitment that was from once upon a time ago. He has moved on. He found warmth and love with someone. So now not only do I have to worry about someone wanting to jump me from behind, I have to worry about you trying to put your disgusting fingers all over my man, I just may have to take your bloody head off as well. But, I think the worse thing of it all is, the booking staff of the Four Winds decided to make it a 6-man elimination match. What the bloody hell is this? The Survivor Series? I don’t think so. Six of us in a bloody ring, tagging in and out to trying to take the other side out, sounds like a bloody high school game should I have ever heard of it. Oh well, when it all comes down to it I will be the only one left standing in the ring. I am the only one who can seem to carry this team of misfits I am on, so it will be all left to me to carry this team to victory. I’m sorry if my ego shows, but that is how I feel about it. I can’t really find it to trust these two to work with me to get the victory, so it’s better to be weary of whom I’m partnered with. Black Jack will more then likely turn on me because of my ‘relationship’ with the McClancy clan, and Jamie will turn because I cost him the world title match a few weeks ago, so it’s safer for me to trust really no one. Either way, it will be one hell of a match up. And one that will go down in the history of the Four Winds as one hell of a bloody match up.
