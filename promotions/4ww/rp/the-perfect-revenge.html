The Perfect Revenge? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #107 Date: 09/04/03 Location: The Beach Revenge: 1 : to avenge (as oneself) usually by retaliating in kind or degree 2 : to inflict injury in return for. Or as destiny likes to think of it, A dish best served cold. A strong chance to get even with things that have happened. The past week, Destiny had been giving the time off. A bad move on the part of her charge. Patti lost her title. She lost the title to someone she should have defeated easily. What happened you ask? Simple. An unlikely alliance between two people. Two people out to destroy Patti. Annie Leanan and the 'Simply Stunning' Sean Summers. One wants to end 'The Darling of the 4WW's' career. The other wants what is claimed to have been stolen. Sean Summers, claimed he wanted the South Wind Title. He claimed he wanted to take it from Patti. Well, he managed to do that, but he also cost his chance to face Patti one on one too. But that's ok Patti will stop him from ever getting that belt. Oh wait, no that would be his ditz of a girlfriend stopping him from getting that title. Eliza's enthusiasm will pretty much put an end to those chances, or is it his undying affection for the twit that will keep him from doing his job right. Who knows, but for all Destiny knows it is a match made in heaven. But then again, how can her best friend be attracted to THAT?!?! The man has no hair, just two big dumbo ears, guess it helps to hang on to something. He was cocky, claimed he was the best in the business, and has gone down to Patti more times then Monica on Bill. Destiny knew. Fate knew. In fact, the Greek ancestors of Patti knew. Just like they knew that Patti belonged with McClancy. Mr Summers is looking for a fight of a lifetime in this battle. A grudge match. A match that will no doubt go bloody. Someone is bound to get hurt, injured, or even worse. Someone is probably going to get 'knocked the fuck out' or even worse, 'knocked the fuck up'. And hopefully the chair shots, baseball bat, golf club shots land right on that ugly smug face of his, Summers. Something sure in the hell has got to make him look better. It could be a vast improvement. Maybe Annie will fall for him. I mean she is his type. The walking dead are in fashion right now. Destiny is sure that Eliza won't mind sharing him with someone new. Every man's fantasy will come true on that one. A raven haired beauty and a blonde with a bald walking ego trip. Hmm...sounds like a porno movie in the making. And what about the newest member to this mess? How about Annie Leannen? The skyla apo Iplavdia . A woman that was brought in by the CEO. Why? Because Fey still hates Patti. Even though the reasons as to why, still aren't fully known, and probably won't be known for some time. As everyone saw last week, Annie made her self known, after her blatent attack on Patti, costing her the title. Destiny fears that once Patti recovers from her bruises, Annie may wind up in the hosptial. Remember the attack on Lobo by Chris West? Annie just may need an oxygen tank after all is said and done. This one will be a bloody war. ___________________________________________________________ Place: The Beach Time: Hours before Sunset ___________________________________________________________ Fade into a view of a sandy beach. The sun just beginning to set upon the horizon, lighting up the sky with its perfect shades of yellow, orange, and red. The sand on the beach was still warm from the afternoon sun beating down on it. Families with small children are seen playing in the sand, a few others playing in the surf. Further on down the beach, laying on a pretty purple towel is the resident 'Darling of the 4WW' Patti Anderson. The straps to her purple bikini top are down around her sides, allowing the rest of the sun to warm her skin. She reaches behind her and undoes the top, allowing the sun to hit the area's covered by the string. Next to her, is laying the 'Irish Bastard' . His back to the sun, from the way he's laying, he's enjoying the day off as much as her. His eyes stare at the curve of Patti's breast. His memory tracing it over and over in his mind. He knew that she was in pain by the way she way laying on her side. Her body was still sore from that damn move that the current witch of the 4 Winds had done to her. Her stomach was still rather bruised from the hit she took. Just too bad Chris West doesnt realize his victory is a tainted one. It took THREE of them to take her out of action. The warmth of the sand coming from under the towel followed by the rays of the sun, made her feel relaxed. The calming rush of the waves upon the shore soon lulled her into a sleep that her body needed. Her mind entered a dream state. She saw herself a few years down the road. She saw a couple small children running around. Both of them looking identical. Same red hair, chubby cheeks, and bright twinkle in their eyes. She saw herself bend down and pick up the twins in her arms, giving them hugs and a kiss on each cheek. Patti continued to watch the images that appeared before her. Everything seemed so perfect. House, family, husband. Everything. She smiled at the happiness she was seeing. She smiled even brighter when she saw the man she was married to. Patti saw her married to the one that mattered most to her. Sean Michael McClancy.. Viewing everything she was seeing in the future made her smile. The thought of giving him the family he always wanted made her smile. Even the sight of her slight rounded belly in the dream made her smile more. A perfect life with a perfect future is all that mattered to Patti. Then a dark cloud appeared over her door. The couple who she wrestled for. The couple of Ian and Fey Childress. The sister and brother-in-law of the love of her life. She watched the older version of herself stand in a defiant pose, ready to fight. She noticed Ian soften when he saw the children. She watched him bend down and start to play with them. She watched as her now sister-in-law walk towards her. A smile starting to appear on her normal serious face. She watched as Fey moved in closer, her arms stretched out as to giving a hug. Patti came instantly awake, as if she felt someone touch her. She noticed the sky had taken on an almost angry color. Dark clouds were forming over where she was laying. Something within the dream had upset her. Patti: What the bloody hell was that all about?? Fey giving me a hug?? That will bloody never happen in this life time. Patti shrugged the dream off, the terrible feeling that, somewhere down the road, the dream would become reality. She looked overhead and watched the dark clouds return to fluffy white ones, and the sun returned to its setting in the distance. Patti sighed and prepared to wake up the sleeping giant, in that special way that he loved best. Fade to black.
