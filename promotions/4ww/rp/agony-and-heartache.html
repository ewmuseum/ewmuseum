Agony and Heartache Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #61 Date: July 24, 2003 Location: Phoenix, AZ Agony, an intesne mental distress or physical pain, anguish. Agony, yet another word for what our heroine is facing. Sundy Night Storm Front tested her mental and physical ablities. Her mind never gave into the mind games. that were being played, but her body did. Her opponent and most hated rival, the adelfi malakas, the homosexual male, Sean Summers, tried to break her body and her spirit. The only thing he succeeded in doing was unleashing her anger. An anger that will cause him to pay, cause him undo pain, to avenge the damage done to her body. What is this woman capable of? Only destiny and time know what that is. Oh yes, our friend the adelfi malakas. The man that has more arhidis then expected. Takes a sick twisted individual to knock a woman off of a 15 foot ladder. Then think he's all that by taking out the opposition. Maybe instead of whining and crying about his getting beat by a girl, he needs to see an anger management therapist. Oh wait, psychos need committed, not re admitted to society. Maybe someone should place a call to the Federal Mental Institution and have Summers slapped into a straight jacket. Oh yeah, the man who is also wearing MY BELT. Someone got jealous in a hurry that a woman can compete with the big boys. And what about the other one. One of the members of the trelos nothos, the crazy bastards of the 4 Winds, Pajaro de Presa. The man took her to the limits and still hung in there. Maybe there is something with those chilidawgs he eats that gives him that ability, or is it those jalapenos? Guess the world won't know the answer to that one. Pajaro hung tough last week, and will probably put through a good showing this week. But the bigger question is, will he be able to survive the sex appeal of a woman he wants? A woman that he thought it was cute to spank. A woman he thought it was cute to bronco bust. Just too bad he spent himself on that one. Oh well, silly Pajaro, how is he going to feel when Patti decideds to turn the tables on you. Both these men will pay Sunday, but as to which one will feel her wrath the most, that will remain to be seen. For what we dont know is that destiny has a plan. ___________________________________________________________ AFTER STORM FRONT SUNDAY JULY 20, 2003 4 WINDS ARENA ___________________________________________________________ The paramedics, trainers, security and a worried 'Irish Bastard' surrounded the crumpled form of the woman laying among the splintered, broken announcer's table. Mary Ellen Krug and Joseph Morallas stand off to the side. A pained expression on their faces. Normally a wrestler of good weight will be helped back to the dressing room after taking a bump such as this. Unfortunately the woman lying there isnt moving. Her eyes have yet to flutter, her fingers have yet to move, her body yet to respond. The voice of her beloved McClancy isnt even making her respond. The woman laying in that mess, Patti Anderson. The Darling of the 4 Winds. How did she wind up here? Simple, Sean Summers. A jealous, evil man who didnt care about the title, but more about hurting the woman going after the title. He couldnt handle the fact that once again this mere woman cost him a match for the second week, oh wait, he should have thought about that before he used a steel chair on her. Thank god it didnt do much more damage then putting a scratch on her nose. But this time, instead of being a man, and dealing with it the adult way, he did the unthinkable. He interfered in the match, rammed a shoulder into the ladder, send the Darling of the 4 Winds decending 15 feet into a table. And now the once proud woman lays battered, bruised, and unconscious. The paramedics on hand, carefully put Patti on a board. Sean desperately keeps trying to get Patti to respond. The longer she remains unconscious the worse things can get for her. A second paramedic places her neck in a neckbrace. Patti's eyes still do not flutter, causing alarm among the trainers at ringside. The paramedics roll her to the back of the building and into one of the training rooms. Paramedic 1: Should we notify the nearest hosptial that we have a trauma victim? Paramedic 2: Go ahead and notify them that possible trauma victim is on their way. Sean, still at the side of Patti, continues to try to get ther to respond. He keeps her one hand in his, trying to get her to come back to life. His fingers run across her ring. The intertwinded hearts. A sign of love and committment between two people, a promise that they belonged to each other. At this very moment he felt her slipping away from him. He looked over her face, her dark eyelashes and eyelids closed, no color to her cheeks. The spot where that idiot Summers had hit her in the case looked nasty. He'd have seen worse. In fact, he'd had worse. Finally, after 20 or so minutes the paramedics send Patti off to the hospital. Sean hops in the ambulance with Patti. He resumes holding her hand. If she does come to, he wanted her to know that he is right there with her. His fingers begin to twittle with the ring. Fade to Black ___________________________________________________________ PHOENIX MEMORIAL HOSPTIAL PRIVATE HOSPITAL EXAM/ROOM THAT SAME EVENING ___________________________________________________________ The world was dark. The pain and aches the body were starting to feel didnt seem right. She felt as she'd been hit with a sledgehammer or something. Her lungs felt as if they were on fire. Her ribs hurt as she'd breathe in and out. Her back felt as if someone had kicked the living shit out of her. Oh wait, someone did. Her eyes attempted to flutter open but the pain in the back of her head wouldnt let them open. Her other senses told her that Sean was next to her. She could feel him playing with the ring on her finger. She attempted a strong squeeze on her part to let him know she was coming around. Sean jumped slightly. He stopped playing with her ring to double check that she was coming around and wasnt an involuntery reaction. She squeezed the hand again, waiting for him to respond. Sean squeezed her hand back, to let her know he was there. Sean: Don'ncha be tryin to talk. Save yer strength. Patti nodded her head a little and winced in pain. Her head starting to throb. She licked her lips in an attempt to bring some mostiure to them. A nice failed attempt there. Mouth tasted like cotton. She used her eyes to plead with Sean to get her something. Sean: Once the docs are through looking at ye, then we'll get ye something to drink. Sean debated leaving the room, seeing the love his life laying in bed, brings back bad memories of his sister being in the hosptial. He runs his fngers over Patti's forehead. He noticed that she had fallen back to sleep. The drugs must be taking affect. He kissed her gently on the forhead and steps outside the room, and walks towards the nurses station. On his way down the hall, the attending doctor stops him. Dr. Roberts: Mr. McClancy, I'm the attending physician for Ms. Anderson. I wanted to be the one to update you on her condition. From the catscan and the x-rays that were taken, Ms. Anderson has a few bruised ribs and a mild concussion. She's very lucky she knew how to land when she fell. We're going to keep her in overnight for observations. You are more then welcome to sleep in her room with her. We'll just have the nurse bring in a cot for you. Sean: Will she be able to wrestle on Sunday? Dr. Roberts: I don't see why she shouldn't. With that news, Sean heads off to a place to call his sister to inform her that Patti will be able to wrestle on sunday's card. A nurse on duty comes into check on the status of the patient. Patti opens her eyes to stare back at the woman. She raises her hand to get the nurses attention. The nurse being quite new to the third shift floor didnt realize the severity of her mistake until to late. She leans over to place her ear close to Patti. Patti grabs hold of the woman's smock. Patti: Where in the bloody hell is the doc? And where is Sean? The nurse, who is now shaken by this, undoes the grip on her smock. She grabbed hold of the hand, and starts taking her pulse. After a few seconds, the nurse releases the grip on the hand. Nurse: Now just calm down. The doctor will be in to see you in a while, and im sure your young man will be in shortly. He stepped outside to catch some air. He hasnt left your side since you came in here. Patti: Can I bloody hell get something for the pain? My head is bloody well killing me. The nurse shook her head as she continued with the examination. Bruises were forming around her ribs and back area. She clicked her tongue in disgust at the marks. If the paperwork hadn't said that this woman was a wrestler, she would have been tempted to call the police and file a formal report on a domestic violence case. The nurse handed the woman the remote to the tv. She noticed that Patti wouldnt be awake for much longer. Nurse: Miss, why dont you just relax and try to get some sleep. We'll be monitoring you during the night. So just relax and try to get some rest. I'm sure the doctor will be in to see you soon, and if not first thing tomorrow. Patti grumbled at the nurse and killed the light that the nurse had rudely left on. She grabbed the remote that was on the bedside table and flicked on the tv. Patti mindlessly surfs through the channels. News? No. Weather Channel? No. ESPN? No. Patti begins to get frustrated. She keeps surfing until she spots the opening segment of tongiths Storm Front. Good she thinks to herself. I'll watch it and get a general idea on what Summer's wickness it. Patti watched the opening and saw the bit with the Pajaro. Sleep and the affects of the drugs that were slipped into her IV were taking affect. Patti left the tv on, maybe something would slip in while she slepted. Patti must have fallen asleep during the early portion of the card. She roused up sleepily and looked around the room. She heard the melodic buzz saw of Sean sleeping on the floor. The tv was still flashing the Storm Front showing. The chair shot could be heard from the tv as they replayed that shot several times. Patti stared in disbelief at the tv. When she found who the sick twisted individual that wanted to replay that, they'd pay for it. The television continued on with the dribbles of what else happened. Patti layed back and decided to watch it until she fell back to sleep. She became tempted to throw a pillow at Sean to get him to roll over. She shrugged that idea off. She layed back and rolled over onto her side. Her head and ribs throbbing at the same time. She groaned in pain at the movement. She layed staring at the box. Suddenly she flew straight up in bed. Her head really pounding. Her ribs hurting from the deep breaths that are coming in Tears pouring down her face. A close up view catches this : Fey: Sean Michael McClancy, I could'na care less which groupie yer screwin' this week, next week, a few weeks ago. I could'na care less if ye went ou' an' bedded a different one each week. Bu' this rumor tha' she's started abou' ye two getting' married, it's a bit much fer me. [The big man appears to back down a bit and get defensive.] McClancy: It's only a rumor. Besides, wha' does it mean t' ye? Yer na' Mum 'er Dad. [Fey's look goes from stern to almost fury.] Fey: Damnit' Sean. I don'na 'ave t' be. I'm th' 'ead o' the family now, as ye well know. An' migh' I add tha' there is more t' it then tha'. Wha' abou' the simple fact tha' ye've already met th' requirements fer yer betrothal! BETHOTHED?!?! The love of her life had been lying to her? WHO? WHY? Patti smashed the tv set with her fist, glass landing all over her bed. She curled up in a ball, more tears running down her face. She knew that in the morning Sean would have some serious explaining to do about this, this kargiola. ___________________________________________________________ FADE TO BLACK ___________________________________________________________
