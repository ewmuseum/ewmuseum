Random Ramblings Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #201 Date: 10/04/03 Location: Hotel Room Has-Been - noun - one that has passed the peak of effectiveness or popularity ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: The Palms, Sean and Patti’s room Time: Two Hours After the Shopping Spree ______________________________________________________________________________ Two hours had passed since Patti had returned from the shopping center. Two hours had passed since Patti met up with her love’s exiled uncle and cousin. Two hours had passed since Patti found out that her life could be in possible danger. The Order was serious on these matters. And if What Connor McClancy had said was true, that Annie could give the word for the final strike. She could be the target, and from what she knew about Annie, she wouldn’t put it past her. Patti stepped back into the room that was the bedroom. Her hair freshly washed, her body relaxed. She should have used the hot tub a lot sooner. It would make the unpleasant task of looking over the videos that Eliza had sent over. If that woman wasn’t her best friend, she would have strangled her by now. The slightest mention of her name in an interview and Eliza was sending over the tape And from the looks of the package on the bed it, instead of the expected six videos, only two laid there. Both clearly marked in Eliza’s handwriting. And her standard little note attacked, informing Patti that she needs to watch them, may find something in one of them that would cause her to get more fired up then normal. She walked barefooted over to the closet and grabbed one of Sean’s shirts, and the black lacy thong, that she knew wouldn’t have either one on for very long. She quickly reached into her purse and pulled out the newest piece to the outfit. The necklace she had bought at the store. She clasped it on, and tucked it underneath the shirt. Sean would see it when he took off the shirt at bedtime. She grabbed the tape marked ‘Rose’ and walked over to the VCR. With Sean still being gone, she’d have time to get her research in and study her opponents. Plus she knew he’d be home sometime after the bar closed, the sleep she would get before that would help some. She slammed the tape home, pounced on the bed and pulled her knees up under her chin. Her thinking position. She could stare at the television without losing focus or thought, and still walk away with knowing what she was going to have to take on. She waited for it to being. Her impatience starting to show through. Patti: Come On. We know this woman is bloody old, maybe she should pull out the walker and go from there. Finally, the looks of an old, battered, and very well worn out woman appeared on her screen. She appeared to be dressed as cheaply as Annie does. Hmm, they must have the same fashion designer. Patti made a mental note to stay away from him. TV ROSE: Let's look at Patti Anderson, the self-proclaimed 'Darling of the 4WW.' That moniker is nothing more than a mere mask, a persona, a lie. She's not a darling, she's nothing more than a petty bitch. Who gives a rat's ass if she was trained by Sean McClancy? I respect McClancy and his skills in the ring, but Patti...she has no skills. She uses her 'sexploitations' to win her matches. Dressing skimpily and revealing her cleavage. Shit, woman, have some dignity, grow some balls and wrestle for god sakes. This is a wrestling business, not a porno movie. Patti: Oh you think I’m fake do ye now? Kinda funny, seeing how I bust my tail off week after week to give these people a good show and you just think you can walk right into the federation and take a spotlight that you think belongs to you? I don’t bloody think so. As for me using my ‘sexploitations’ to win a match, honey, at least I got something to show, yours are so flabby from breast-feeding your child, that I’m suprised your not mistaken for a cow yourself. You may make Annie homesick, or she’ll get this urge to milk a cow. Now that would be funny to see. Patti got discussed with looking at the old wench and shut the tv off. She needed something a little something more stimulating then the crap that was coming from the old bag. Patti crawled off the bed, and walked over to the VCR, traded tapes and resumed her position on the bed. The vision of Annie appeared bouncing around the room. Attempting to sway the viewer into feeling sorry for her for god knows what reason. It was just sad. Her constant moaning over Sean not being hers anymore was getting down right irritating. Just get over it please. Patti: Hmm, your bloody damn well right I blame you for all of this. I know you’ll do anything to try to reclaim Sean. You’d be bloody well wailing like the banshee you are if my tests came back positive. You knew that would cause you to lose him to me, and that bothers you. As for me claiming that the federation is against me, where in the blue hell did you get that idea? Not once have I EVER screamed that the Four Winds was against me? Not once. I take my lumps and bumps like everyone else. I go about my business like everyone else does. I go out and win, even those unfair matches where I get my ass handed to me on a platter. Do you know what the best part of it is? I get to go home with Sean every night, change into one of his shirts, curl up next to him on the couch and just spend a quiet night with the man who loves me. So I guess you could say, I’m living the dream that you want so badly, and you know what. I’m not giving him up. You’ll just have to live with it. Patti didnt bother to even finish watching that tape either. Both were just having to much fun yelling at each other, so she decided to not bother anymore. It was all the same bull that they spew constantly. Patti got the feeling that Annie and Rose were after each other, and more forgot about her. Oh, well. She shrugged it all off. Patti decided that fretting or worrying or about it. She knew she would walk away the winner. She always did. Patti glanced up at the clock just as Sean walked in the door. Fade out.
