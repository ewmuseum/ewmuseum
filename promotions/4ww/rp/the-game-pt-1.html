The Game, Pt. 1 Shoudou 4WW Exposures #715 Date: 5/29/04 Location: Somewhere, not here. What have I gotten myself into? How did this all happen? It was pretty much going downhill from here, it had to be. I’ve gone from someone trying to make a name for themselves to being a hitman for an angel? A fucking real, honest to god angel? Yeah, you can find it hard to belive. But then again wrestling has become a strange place as of late. There are some in the 4WW even that claim to be part angels, or even to be demi gods or worse. But what part was I to play in this? Zadikiel kept talking about this ‘Destroyer’ dude, and that Patti, chic. Yeah, a looker in her own right, but my job wasn’t to pawn all over her. No, the halfbreed, as ‘Zad’ called him, was doing enough of that. Let’s not forget the wannabe that was walking around with the two twins, and almost everyone else on the roster who had a swinging member between their legs. Zad’s said it was something about her. Then again, he’d told me a lot that I have had to fully take in about this whole situation. My job is to basically be the biggest thorn in her side. To basically beat the holy living shit out of her if I get a chance. She’s good. She’s got to be, she’s got two titles right now. But then again, I’m supposed to be a nuisiance so that she doesn’t forget just what is going on. Zads calls it a game. A game he’s playing with the other one, the one known as Elexhander Tarrant. He’s a bastard that one. But I’ve been told to leave him alone, and told that from him I’m safe. I wonder if this is the way it was played in the ancient days when the Gods would sit there and deiced to play games with the meager mortals about. As they wheeled and dealed their way into legend. It’s too bad the movie Troy doesn’t touch on too much of the actual legend behind it. Such as Paris being picked by a couple of goddesses to see which one was the most desirable. He picks Aphrodite, and she gives him whatever he wants for it. He chose Helen of course, leading to that war. We play games like that even today, only when they happen, you don’t know it apparently. Unless you’re in the game. A sick game of chess, and when someone looses, who knows what goes on. “Boy, what are you doing?” Boy? Boy? I’m sorry, I must have wandered off in my thoughts. Then again hanging around with some of these likes that Zads does leads me to do that. I find some of his followers to be utterly and completely boring as all hell. “Boy, what are you doing? Zadikiel has summoned you. He feels it’s time for you to be explained what is fully going on.” Heh? Finally? I get to find out what my real job is other then having to beat the shit out of some chic? You should have heard him the other day when he had me go after her. “Bust her teeth in” is what he said. I hope I came close. “Yeah, I’ll be there, give me a moment, damnit.” I’d been sitting in this kneeling position for hours. No matter what I was, I was still a man of the art. My style was built around it. It took a bit to stretch out my calfs as I stood up. Sitting like that for that long was a good conditioning exercise, but having to get moving quickly took measures that made you limp for some time. “Yeah, yeah. Whatever he wants. He’d just better remember my part of the damn deal. I expect to get paid and paid well. Based on the danger, damnit.” What, are you surprised to find out I’m nothing more then a mercenary? Aren’t we all? Will not those in this business fight not unlike mercenaries or gladiators? You flight for the glory, the paycheck, the titles. All in all, you’re still fighting for a personal reward. Mine I just prefer to be a bit more tangible and useful for me. I’ve got expenses to pay. Ah, the hallway isn’t long, and doesn’t really take me much to make it to where Zads was hanging out. This underground, basement thing he had going was a bit much, but damn, he had the finest stuff. His little ‘hall’ was nothing unlike what you’d see in the pictures of all of those old persian drawings and that. He had pillows about, tables full of food and drinks, and surprisingly enough, women about. Even though he never did anything with them from what I could tell. But his traveling party, they took them on a regular basis. Hmm, he barely notices as I walk in as he’s biting into some large piece of meat, looks like a chicken breast cooked with a good amount of spice. His cup was full of a thick liquid that sloshed about as he drank it and rolled down his chin. Finally as he chewed he answered my presence. “Ay, yes, my little warrior.” “What do you need Zads?” He hated that name, but Zadikiel was too damn long to say on a regular basis. Supposedly he told me he was the Angel of Memory, and one of the Angels of the rituals that were called by the mystics. All horse shit if you asked me. But then again in the last few weeks I’d seen quite a bit that shocked the hell out of me. As he swallowed the large bite of avian flesh, he frowned at the name. “Please, Shoudou, have a seat, eat your fill. And I will tell you the rules o fthe game for you.” The rules of the game? His way with words was quite a bit strange. “I’ll grab some Jack in the Box later. You can fill me in now. I’ll not take from your banquet.” “The banquet is for all of my servants. Please, have some. By the way, you did very well last week. I think Patti knows that I mean business. She will decide between me and the Destroyer soon. She’ll have no choice.” That was funny. What was it with this girl? “And why should she choose between you or him? Right now she’s got everything she wants. She’s got the North Wind Title, the East Wind Titles and men pawning all over her. Most women would want what she has. I head she’s even got a new contract that’s paying more then that Jerms fellows.” He chuckled. “Ah, but she doesn’t have everything she wants. You see, what she wants more then anything is to prover herself to her sister in law. To prove that she’s the proper mate for a strange family of half Fomorian and half Dryad Seelie royalty. She wants Sean McClancy. “ Now he had my interest. “And why can’t she have him yet? Why not?” “That’s a funny thing there my young assassin. You see, he’s a prince of a race of beings that is an offshoot of your own. A race that is for all intents and purposes... immortal.” “Immortal? Yeah, right?” “You’ve seen me. Do you doubt my actual race in this world?” “No, you’ve got an impressive wingspan. But mind you, there are no such things as immortals.” “But there are angels, right?” “Okay, where are you going?” “Well if your books are right about Angels, as you’ve found out, are they right about immortals? The Christian bible itself mentions two. And some of the books of the Order that McClancy follows bring up more. The one who attacked you after you kicked Ms. Anderson’s teeth in the other night is one himself.” “Oh, and what’s he? A troll?” He didn’t like my tone, I could tell. “No, he’s a half breed. One of his parents was either Infernal or Angelic. I don’t know which at this time. That makes him a Gibborim, as immortal as me, in that he can’t die of natural causes, but his soul is still mortal. Thus he can die, you just have to do it right. It’s not an easy task.” This was not good news. The ‘halfbreed’ was somehow infactuated with the buxom red head. “You told me that this Tarrant fellow can’t touch me, right?” “Not unless he wants to conceed his reward from the Master. We both serve the same one on this game. The thing is, right now Tarrant has the biggest piece of the puzzle. I need it.” “What is that?” “Ms. Anderson.” “And why is this. Isn’t she the one you want me to break a bone in or something?” “No, no breaking of bones, then again you’ll be hard pressed to do that anyway. You see, You see, Elexhander Tarrant is a user of souls. He uses them like you do your air and food. Like I do the Piety of individuals about whom belive in things that go bump in the night. It’s how he survives. I’ll not tell you how he got that way.” “What makes Ms. Anderson special over his other minions?” “Patti is not unlike her fiance. She’s not fully human. We know this. You see, when Tarrant feeds, he takes a little of their soul. Contrary to popular belive, the soul is not immortal. It’s a permanent entity, then again so is any actual energy. But if you consume it, it will die. It will go away. But there are some, like the McClancy man, and Patti that have a touch of immortal in them. Even such as the halfbreed, Darek. They’re souls regenerate at a good rate. He consumes some, then lets them heal, and then consumes some more. In doing this, he gains power. The thing is, with an immortal blooded one in his grasp, he’s got a perpetual power supply. I want that. I need that. I get that and I win.” “You’re going to use her for the same thing?” “No. I can’t eat that. But if Tarrant doesn’t have it, he looses his queen. Very few chess games have been won without a queen. I want his most powerful piece to fall. Any way we can make that happen.” Power by denying him his. It sure was a good tactic. “So, what does she get if she chooses you over him?” “She’s not choosing me. I couldn’t care less which she chooses, only as long as she does. The saving or destruction of things does nothing to me. I have a purpose and it’s to play the side against Tarrant. To make his job not easy. I know to do this I have to take his pieces. He’s already started a chain reaction in this place. And the Childresses will do nothing to stop it. The have no desire to. The destruction of the world is something that The Keeper wants more then anyhing in his little group. They would serve by Tarrant winning. Then again, I have something to offer for them also if I’m to win this game. So they will not stop it.” He took a long drink from his goblet, the thick red wine splashed over his shirt as he did. “If she goes with you, does she get McClancy?” “I can’t tell you that. I don’t know. I know that one way will drive her to the path of that of the Destoryer and his ilk. One way will coral her back to what she once was. One way will make her able to take the Fomorian Prince as her own. One way will separate her from him for eternity. And in the end, she will be looking at an eternity on this earth. It’s up to the Master to determine which path wins her prize.” How fucking cold. I liked it. “ I like how your own Master keeps you in the dark.” “Yes, I figured you’d like the irony of that. You see, things are going to get interesting. I’ve found out that you will have a match this week against someone named Johnny Page. He’s a lower class fighter. Someone who will be an easy prey, such as that washed up has been known as the Britsh Bomber was.” He paid attention, this was good. “Mind you, be careful. You are safe from Tarrant, but there are others who now know you’re there. The one known as Fnord will be wanting to take you down for what you did to his woman. And the halfbreed is his aqauintence and ally. And he knows you’re after the one he desires now. You must be wary.” Concern? Well he is paying me a lot. He just doesn’t fully know it yet. “I’m no amatuer.” I had to reply. “I’ll take care of business. This page is in for a wake up call. And if the Half Breed or the Crazy one get invovled, I’ll just have to drop them also.” His face went to a scowl. “Don’t get cocky! You will need to be watchful. I have no other allies for you at this time. Although there are those who form against the Destroyer. We can not rely on them to get the job done. I am taking precausions against things, but I am limited on my abilities at this time. Tarrant will need to loose a few ‘meals’ before he can be taken off the treat department. He and his direct minions can’t touch you outright, but mind you this... He has ways of using others to get his work done. Now, join us. Eat, drink, take a woman. Live the life that will be so short for you… human.” “Sorry, no thank you. I’m going out for some sushi or something.” “Have it your way.” He said that as he lifted his cup, then stuffed another bite of the meat in his mouth. He chewed with his mouth open. You’d think they didn’t’ get that type of stuff where he was from. It was no matter though. I couldn’t stay with him. I needed my space to get ready for the fight. Although I find it funny that he does not know the full true nature of his hired guns. He knows not what he deals with or whom. Only that I can fight and that I’ve done work for this ‘Master’ before. When the time is right, I may be able to use him just as he thinks he is using me. Patti Anderson is something that is needed for something else… And that is not for him to know, or this Elexhander Tarrant. “Boy, please, before you go. Take at least a drink, you will need it. You know what I offer.” Yes, I did. The very stuff this being had aquired, the drink of the gods themselves. And he was offering it to me to ensure I was going to be able to handle business. Oh, yes. I would take it. “Just a cup. That’s all I’ll need.” I watched as a beautiful woman wearing only what could be called a loin cloth came over and handed me a silver goblet. She poured the thick red wine from a pitcher. Yes, I would drink of it. And then I would be ready for the fight ahead.
