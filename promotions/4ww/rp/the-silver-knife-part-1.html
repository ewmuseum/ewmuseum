The Silver Knife part 1 Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #959 Date: Sept 13 04 Location: Tokyo Japan “Sunday September 12 2004” People often ask what the life of a vampire is like, it must be exciting and fun being up all night and sleeping all day… Well that’s a miss conception, I don’t sleep during the day nor do most vampires, most of us have evolved since “the time of Dracula”, I don’t like the sunlight very much, I don’t think many vampires do, but we endure it, our eyes are very sensitive, especially to light, almost like a natural night vision, that’s why I wear sun glasses during the day… Still I get people telling me that I’m a poser, that I’m a fake, that I don’t know shit. I have a simple response to people like that ; FUCK OFF!… Hell if I even feel like it I’ll prove to ‘m, suck their blood, once they finally believe me they get a sliver of hope that they might be lucky enough to turn into one of us, to have that privilege and that curse. But most of them are dumb, don’t realize they have to drink from me, the ones who are smart enough to know that are too weak to do it, it’s kind of sad leaving them their wanting something, letting their dreams go unfulfilled, but hey they asked for it and I’m just doing what god tells me to do… Recently I haven’t done it though, haven’t drank from a body in weeks. I haven’t felt like it. I’ve robbed the blood bank numerous times, hell I’ve even contemplated going back to rats, but I’ll never go back to rats, it’s so degrading eating a something so far under you in the food chain… If vampires can gain clinical depression then I’m sure I have it, I’ve barely moved for weeks, I went to the studio to cut a promo, called my dealer for some info in case I needed some “medication” for my depression while over here in Japan I haven’t needed any though, been trying to lay off the stuff, I lose lots of money and I’m starting to think that they’re watering it down somehow. Either way I have my mind set on the biggest match of my career, well biggest matches… Lee sits back in his chair, he’s wearing a pair of black jeans and sitting in the dark, he looks at the book he’s been writing in and closes it. He sighs as he rummages through an open suit case on the floor of his hotel room. After a few seconds he pulls out what looks like a small metal box with a lock on it. He reaches over onto the desk and picks up a small silver key which he inserts into the box and twists. As he open’s the lid of the box it’s obvious it’s pretty full with random papers and small books similar to the one on the desk, presumably journals. He fumbles around a bit within the box to find a cigarette; it’s dusty and has no filter probably a good forty years old. He rummages around the box some more finding a match booklet with ‘Studio 54’ written on it. He lights up the match illuminating the dark room. He shakes the match extinguishing the flame. ‘What am I doing here?’ he thinks as he takes a puff of his cigarette. ‘Wrestling? Yeah I guess…’ He takes the cigarette out of his mouth for a second, savoring the flavor. He looks down at his left arm, examining the various scars and marks left from two centuries of wars, both physical and mental. He takes a puff of his cigarette and looks back down at his arm momentarily before driving the cigarette butt into it. “Argggg…” he exclaims as he winces in pain. ‘Is that why I’m here?’ he asks himself standing up. “Pain?” he mutters as he puts the cigarette in the ashtray. He grabs the box and heads over to the bed. It hasn’t been cleaned in days and it’s obvious, the sheets are in a tatter and have sweat and various other stains on them. He places the box in the middle of the bed and rummages through the papers and books to pull out a something wrapped in a dusty cloth, he opens it up revealing a silver dagger, he stares at it, mesmerized. It emits a faint glow; it would be unnoticeable if he was not in a dark room. Lee examines it for a moment before the creek of the door is heard and the sudden rush of light into the room which is quickly stopped as the door closes and Fable walks in. Lee quickly wraps the dagger back up and slips it underneath the covers. “Hey Babe” “Hey…” “Writing in your journals again?” “Yeah…” “What’s wrong?” “I don’t know. Does everything count?” He looks down at his arm where he burnt himself, it’s gone. He sighs. “Hmmm” “Why am I here?” “To win the West Wind Title, right?” “I don’t know, maybe…” Fable sits down on the bed next to Lee. She kisses him on the lips and lies down. “Do you want t- Arg, what’s this?” She pulls out the cloth from under her, she unwraps it and looks at it for a second before touching the point of the blade and pricking herself. “Fuck!” “Huh?” Lee looks over, not noticing what happened. “You have a silver knife in our room?” Her face turns from a look of anger to a look of sympathy. “I’ve had it forever, it was Damon’s…” “How come you never told me about it?” “Dunno, I never liked telling that story.” He reaches into the box and pulls out an old book and hands it to her. “November 16th”
