A new beginning... Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #721 Date: 6-1-04 Location: Anaheim, CA I've felt the air rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander over where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... “A new turn on an old story is about to begin…….” I've felt the air rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander out where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... Goodbye! “Wait…. Or should it be hello? Am I the same person anymore? No… I’m not that person anymore. Then, what am I? Who am I? I guess that’s why you’re here… We’re going to have to figure this out together.” I wipe it off on tile, the light is brighter this time Everything is 3D blasphemy {The scene slowly fades into an empty warehouse. Dust covers the floor of this large abandoned building. Old rusted workout equipment haunts the open air arena. The lone source of light, a simple sky light, shines over the one thing it seems that time has forgotten to touch, a simple makeshift wrestling ring.} My eyes are red and gold, the hair is standing straight up This is not the way I pictured me {In this ring stands a man scarred by demons, fear, but not age. He is a tall and well built man of a yet still young age. He could be no more than twenty-five years in age. The man is also scarred on his face across his right eye. He has short black hair in a style close to a fade. To match this is a black goatee that marks his face. His eyes though are mismatched in accordance with his scar. His right eye instead of a deep gazing brown is nothing but white with a small black pupil. He also, can be seen wearing a black muscle shirt with a pair of dark blue jeans and white shoes. His T-shirt has a white tribal logo of some design on the front of his shirt, and on the back of this shirt is a simple saying, “Survival of the Fittest”.} I can't control my shakes How the hell did I get here? Something about this, so very wrong... Kanton: Yes, how did I get here… Yet, as it seems I have come here and some thing is truly wrong after all. What could be awry in this old world that I have been reborn into? Is it my placement? Is it where I have ended up? How could it be wrong though? I have been reborn as I said… Should I not start from the beginning once again? I have to laugh out loud, I wish I didn't like this Is it a dream or a memory? {Kanton walks around the ring slowly. He slowly takes in a breath of the dry stale air. He then comes to rest in one of the corners. He then laughs to himself for a few moments.} Kanton: A memory of a dream maybe? My fame… My glory… My pride… Was it all for nothing, or did it cease to ever exist? Is my past but a figment of my imagination? Am I now who I have been always, or is it all still a dream in which I cannot awake? I've felt the air rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander over where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... {Kanton again moves to the center of the ring. There he pauses a moment only to look up at the open air and wonder.} I've felt the air rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander over where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... Kanton: Yes inside I wait… I wait for the truth. I wait for opportunity. My time has now come. I can no longer wait for my destiny as now I seek my destiny out. I force my demons to run as I meet them head on. I have cleared the leaves from my eyes. I have uncovered myself to find truth. Get outta my head cuz I don't need this Why I didn't I see this? Kanton: Why didn’t I see this… my fate? I have lived in an emotionless purgatory as it seems. All that I once knew is gone… like a thought on the edge of a dream. I'm a victim - Manchurian candidate Kanton: I was laid to waste by those I held dear… I was the scapegoat of their failure’s. No longer will I hold up the downfalls of others… I have sinned by just Makin' my mind up and takin' your breath away Kanton: I have made up my mind. As I said, I will take my destiny into my own hands. It will take your breathe away… I've felt the air rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander over where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... (Kanton stalks and does a bit of shadow boxing around the ring.} I've felt the air rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander over where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... {Kanton returns to one of the turnbuckles and rests his body upon it.} Kanton: Hmm… Who will be first to taste my new wrath? Goodbye! Kanton: Yes… goodbye… a fitting message to give to him. You haven't learned a thing I haven't changed a thing My flesh was in my bones The pain was always free Kanton: They may have not learned anything, but I have learned much. I have woken from my ignorant slumber of false hopes and fake dreams. You haven't learned a thing I haven't changed a thing My flesh was in my bones The pain was always free Kanton: This flesh… This blood… This hunger… all of this exists into this world I have been reborn into. Yet with this does come the pain. I've felt the hate rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander out where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... Kanton: Anger… Hate… Violence… All of this exists within the real. Raw emotion in it’s purest state is but priceless. I've felt the hate rise up in me... Kneel down and clear the stone of leaves... I wander out where you can't see... Inside my shell, I wait and bleed... Kanton: I have gone beyond where you can’t see and have returned. I have seen what cannot be seen. I have been where all cannot exist. I am beyond… And it waits for you! Kanton: I await you… I am your destiny… I am your pain… {Kanton then returns to the center of the ring where he once again looks to the sky. He then slowly exits the ring as the scene slowly… fades… out…} (breaking ooc: Yes, this was my sample rp on the PTC boards, but many people and I myself like this... So it's a fitting intro for Kanton)
