A familiar voice is heard as uncertainty is replaced with clarity. The room is filled with people, mingling, talking and dancing, loud musice ehoes through the building. A man dressed in a long black leather jacket turns half circle and reveals himself. Darek is holding a glass of dark brown liquid topped with ice and a slim blue straw. In his other hand he carries a tall glass, bright red with a rim of salty flavor. He weaves himself through the crowd and makes his way to a table where Amanda sits. 'One for you.' He moves his eyes so that they are paralell to hers, a slick grin etches itself upon his face. 'And one for me.' He pulls a chair out from the table and pops himself square in the middle of it. 'Here's to the night off, atleast.' She reaches forward and take a hold of the glass, raising it slightly from the table and tilting it towards Darek. She deviously returns his grin with one of a more sultry standing. In that instant the two galsses clink into each other and both of them quickly bring their drinks to their lips. 'You're not very good with the love scene, stick to being vile and evil, it's a much more enduring quality.' 'I don't always find it necessary to bring work home with me, sometimes I do actually give a rats ass about what exactly is going on in the world.' He stabs back confidently, yet slightly obscured. His look of frustration is met with hostility, 'and I was only kidding.' The world seems to freeze at that very instant. A cold, uncaring, killing look chisels itself onto the respective faces of the bickering lovers. Neither of them moving, neither of them breathing, the only thing that exists in this momment is hatred, pure and simple. After all the years they had spent together, they had learned exactly what buttons to push and often, Amanda would push the right ones, just for, as they say, 'shits and giggles'. 'Don't you have something to say to me?' She finally breaks the tension and all that once was, returns to life. '...' '...and that's all.' She stares coldly, 'you're content with sitting there with a dumbfounded look on your face and watch me get up and leave.' '...' She does exactly that. Pushing herself away from the table she reaches behind the chair and grabs her purse and swings it over her shoulder. She looks on last time deep into the eyes of the man she loves. 'Do you care that I'm leaving?' The question fires straight into his cheast, piercing his heart. She lifts her eyebrows and gives him his final seconds to respond, 'well? Do you?' Her eyes fall to the same level as his and her anger is disipated in a blue sea of sadness. 'what do you think? I love you, I was about to tell you. Now all I can do is sit here and watch you leave. I tried to tell you once, I will not tell you again. My way, my lifestyle, it's all funny to you? Funny enough for you to make a joke about? Well joke about this, don't let the door hit on the ass on the way out. Truthfully, I never want you to go, but if they I live, the person I am is too much for you then so be it, leav-' His thoughts are hardly complete when he begins to feel the sting. A searing pain spreads across his face as Amanda steps forward and unleashes all hell into one vicious slap. The echoing high pitched sound turns the heads of many as Amanda turns her back on Darek and walks towards the door. Darek raises his hand to his face and rubs gentley, his eyes never leave Amanda as she disappears into the mass of people. With his right hand still messaging his cheek he finds comfort as his left brings forth his glass of 151 and Coke. 'God, what am I becoming? I find comfort in drinking, and I watch the woman I love walk away...' he pauses, 'and now I am talking to God.' Quickly he pulls the straw from his drink and proceeds to down the rest of the glass. He closes his eyes and finds himself immediately wisked away into a near lifetime of memories with Amanda. From the first time they met, kissed, and made love, to the time when she kept the birth of his child from him, the time that she had been unfaithful. The was a world of good within their love, but also, benieth the surface lay a hatred so deep that it burned a place perminately into the hearts, minds and souls of each other. Of course Darek was no different, he had indulged into many indescretions of his own, not to mention, bloodshed was a daily feature in his life. In some way, shape or form, Darek was the responsible for the lives of everyone in the world, and as if it were a sick game, it would be his face that people would see before they passed. Or so it was rumoured. Darek could only attempt to cover the truth, many people were skeptical and most were just didn't care, though there had been a few, the ones who for what seems like forever, must stand before the wrath of an angry angel. His fate, his destiny, it has become the fate and destiny for the people of the Four Winds. His pain, his suffering, has become our their plague. He opens his eyes for a momment and finds himself glancing forward towards the dance floor. He pulls another cigarette from the pack and places it between his lips. Soon after the soothing comfort is taken into his body and then released. Hoping that his stress would exit his system with the smoke, he opens his mouth and blows a few smoke rings. His fingers move around the table and find another glass before his tips. He turns his attention to the waitress, in one smooth fluent motion his hand makes his way to his pocket and produces enough money to cover the drink, tab and tip. She smiles gratiously and steps away. His hand grasps firmly onto the drink and brings it upward and is met halfway by the lips. As he takes in a cold drink his eyes begin to survey the area, all seemed normal... ...except the man fondling Amanda on the dance floor... Immediately his eyes are drown by his rising temper and the cold blueness that was once there is displaced by a flamming red. Instead of putting the glass down on the table, he tilts it back slightly more, swallowing more and more. Before long he sets the glass onto the table, empty and with a horrid smile burning on his face. He brings his cigarette up to his mouth and takes in a deep breath. Just as he is about to get up and rightfully correct the situation he is interupted. The sound of 'Immortal' can be heard, Darek finds his hand reaching into his pocket pulling out a small black metallic object. He flips it open, 'Yeah?' A pause. 'Really?' His eyes never leave that dance floor. 'Well thats, fan-fucking-tastic.' Another pause. 'Christopher West, Billy Dork and Bullet?' Darek sighs. 'Hasn't Bullet suffered enough?' Again. 'Alright, thanks Lox.' He closes the phone and places it back into his pocket. His mind takes him away for a momment before he realises just what exactly is happening, His pupils shrink right down to nothingness as he reaches to the table. Goddamn Chris West, I am sick and tired of seeing his face. I am sick and tired of hearing him sing his tune about being the greatest Hardcore Wrestler alive. And qutie frankly, I am sick with his existence. He wants to beat Fnord, he wants to beat me, well he can have one but he will never beat me. I will show Chris and the world just exactly what it is like to look into the eyes of a god, just before I proceed to disembow him. What a beautiful day it will be when the skies rain red with the blood of Christopher West, it is day the world has seen all before, only this time, it will be my hands tearing him apart. As I try to wash the blood from my hands, I know it will never ever come clean. My slate is filled with righteous sin and corrected wrongs, why not have the blood of West fall from my lips as I feed from his fear. If that fat fool was here now, I would show him just exactly what fear is. Darek pulls his arm back and launches the empty glass across the room. SMASH! Blood immediately begins to squirt from the open wound on the forhead of the man dancing with Amanda. Her eyes quickly find the answer to her question as the monsterous being known as Dark Angel begins to make a pathway to the dance floor. Her face is nearly marked in fear itself, if only she hadn't known that Darek would never lay a finger on her. Instead she manages to form a look of bitting confidence as The Ominous One finally finds himself standing in her presence. He looks down at her and smiles sickly before turning just in time to be met with a massive right hook from the bleeding man. Darek turns to Amanda and nods, she instead lowers her eyes to the ground. Using his periferal vision, Darek never lets the man out of his sight, now after Amanda lowers her eyes to the floor, Darek turns back to him. 'C'mon cocksucker.' Darek raises his hand to his jaw and rubs it carefully for a momment. He finds himself now looking deep into the man's eyes. As the Man raises his fists and begins to step around, Darek only stands still, staring, meniacly into the man's eyes. Darek turns his head back to Amanda, he places his hand under her chin and pulls her head upward. There are tears falling from her eyes. With his rugged thumbs he carefully wipes away the drops of despair. He looks deeply at her and smiles caringly. Again, almost as instantanious as the first time, the world seems to turn. Everything that exists, exists only for this momment when Darek and Amanda realise that they do love each other, no matter the pain, the suffering that either endure with each other. They had begun to realise that their world was one and forever they would rule it together.
