2 wins one night

11 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 wins one night Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #1006 Date: Oct 11 04 Location: White room/ Dark alley [Fade in] [We see Lee in a faded black shirt and black jeans, it’s raining moderately, he is walking down a dark street at night, the walls are grungy and the ones not tattered or broken have graffiti and numerous gang tags overlapping each other. The faster he walks the harder the cameraman has following him, dodging discarded waste and trash cans that haven’t been emptied in weeks, the garbage seeping out of them is so sickening you can almost smell the stench while watching them on TV.] [Lee Stops. He turns around, his face is blank, but suddenly he smiles. He reaches into his right pocket and pulls out a cigarette and a lighter. He sticks the cigarette inside his mouth and tries to light it, the light fails, the fire extinguishes from the pouring rain. He tries again sheltering the top of it so the rain doesn’t touch it, it ignites.] Lee (voice slightly muffled by cigarette): There we go… nothing like a good smoke to ease your troubles, eh? [The pitter-patter of the rain echoes off the trash cans and ground, reflecting the nearby light and illuminating the surfaces before each drop bursts.] [Lee’s smile turns from to frown as he glares at the camera.] Lee: I’m talking to you… [The camera looks around, showing more tattered and dirty walls and trash cans.] [The camera turns back to Lee, he is still glaring at the camera, annoyed he puffs a large puff of smoke.] Cameraman: Me?!?! [Lee blinks.] Lee: Who the fuck else would I be talking to?!?! Cameraman: I dunno… maybe som’buddys all see through an’… Lee: Invisible, you imbecile. Cameraman: Yuh… Yuh… maybe he’s all imbiciled up… Lee: NO, you dipshit, invisible… Cameraman: Yuh… Tha’z what I meant, maybe he invisible or maybe you just crazy… Lee: You think I’m crazy?!?! [Lee drops his cigarette onto the wet asphalt, even though it has been sufficiently extinguished by the small puddle, he steps on it. He then begins his short journey to the cameraman.] Lee: You think that me, Lee Satima, Lord of the Night, Vampire Prince, is crazy? Think again… [Lee walks up close to the camera, his breath fogs up the lens, he wipes it off.] Lee: I’m Fucking Nuts! [He steps back and begins to laugh.] [Fade Out]… [Fade In] [Lee is now sitting in a computer chair, he is still wearing the same clothes as earlier, they are still damp but not soaking, he is in a nice white room, everything is bright and clean, so bright and clean that it seems almost holy. Two of the walls are covered in shelves full of DVDs and VHS tapes. The wall in the background has numerous computers and electronic on a white table.] [Lee wheels over to the right wall and grabs a DVD entitled ‘Maelstrom 5: Winds of War’] Lee: Winds of War, the night Lee Satima had his first match in 4WW in 2 months…The night Lee Satima defeated Chris West and Caitlin Fairchild along with many others, in one night. The night Tarrant made his un-welcomed return… [Lee places the DVD in the computer and presses a few buttons.] Lee: Now let me refresh your memory of just what happened on that night. [The camera zooms onto the computer screen.] Lee: Miss Fairchild had just eliminated Jaime… [Caitlin kicks Jamie out of the ring. Jamie falls and hits the ground hard. Out of nowhere, Lee Satima drops down and delivers a massive leg chop behind Caitlin's leg! Caitlin winces and drops down to one knee.] Lee: Oh… there she goes down on her knees…as usual… Morales: Lee is out here and hoping to get an early advantage on Caitlin! Candler: Good luck is all I have to say I mean Caitlin is huge bitch who is not going to take any prisoners! [Lee pummels away on the kneeling Caitlin with punches and elbows on the top of her head. Lee stops for a moment and takes a couple of steps back. Caitlin starts to stand up and shakes her head a couple of times to regain her senses. Lee bounces off the ropes and lunging straight for the daze Caitlin…NOOO!!! Lee is stop by Caitlin tightly grabbing hold of his throat. Caitlin attempting to connect with the chokeslam…NOOO!!! BLOOD SPIT…LEE SPEWS BLOOD IN CAITLIN'S EYES!!! Caitlin drops Lee down as she frantically begins to rub her eyes wanting to get the blood out.] Morales: Blood Spit! Candler: Another wrestler who must resort to spitting! [Lee makes his way over to the top turnbuckle and climbs up it quickly. Caitlin still frantically rubbing her eyes out as she begins to turn around. ENGURZI…LEE CONNECTS WITH A TOP TURNBUCKLE ENGURZI!!! Caitlin falls down on the mat hard. Lee quickly rolls Caitlin up in a pin. The ref drops down and goes for the count.] …1 …2 [Lee lifts his legs up and places them on the second rope for the leverage without the ref looking.] …3!!! Lee: And now that one’s over, let’s see what happened later… [He presses a few buttons on the keyboard and after a minute we get a picture of the already taking place West Wind Contenders Battle Dome Match.] Lee: I’m just gonna let you guys watch this on your own… Candler: Three men left... Moraless: Hardy... Satima... West....Three men who hate each other... [West stands in the middle of the ring trying to recoup when all of a sudden the lights in the arena flicker a bloody red color and a blood curdling scream takes over the P.A. system with static taking over the 4WWtron. Chris West looks all around wondering what the hell is going on when SMASH a man in a black cloak comes from behind West smashing him in the back of the head with a lead pipe. West drops down to his knee and the man takes the lead pipe back and cracks him in his spinal cord knocking him flat on his stomach. The man tosses the lead pipe aside and pulls off his hood revealing it to be the Dream Maker ] Candler: What the hell is he doing?! Moraless: Looks like Eddie B. ruined this match for West... Candler: IT's the Dream Maker now... don't let him hear you say that... [The Dream Maker lifts West up by his throat and tosses him into the turnbuckle. He starts to dive his shoulder into West's stomach causing some internal bleeding... West start's to bleed from his mouth as he swings at the Dream Maker, but DM ducks and comes up with an uppercut knocking West back. DM kicks West between his legs and sets him up for the Nightmare Effect....He lifts West up into a suplex like move and tosses West forward into a brainbuster] Moraless: The Superstardom!! Candler: No fool it's the Nightmare Effect now! Moraless: Whatever, Dream Maker just screwed West out of a rematch against Bullet... [DM slides out of the ring and grabs Robb Hardy tossing him underneath the bottom rope... Hardy crawls over to West and places his arm over his chest....] 1...! Candler: NO!! THAT'S MY PICK!! 2...! Moraless: Come on not like this! 3....!!!! Moraless: Your pick is eliminated! Two men remain... [DM stands outside of the dome looking on inside at the carnage... when Satima gets up to his feet and rolls into the ring with all he's got left... Hardy looks at Satima and they keep their stares at one another... Hardy runs at Satima and the two lock up shoulder to shoulder... Hardy pushes Satima back towards the turnbuckle and then starts to bring his knee up into Satima's stomach. Shot after shot connects with Satima, before Hardy runs backwards and then forwards grabbing Satima and nailing him with a monkey flip. Hardy gets up and rolls up Satima.] 1... 2.... [Satima shoves his shoulder up and Hardy smacks the mat. Hardy looks at DM outside of the dome and points at him, but DM simply smirks and puts his hands up shaking his head. Hardy turns back towards Satima knowing if he wants to win this one he's gonna have to remain focused. The camera pans in on Chris West who lays lifeless outside of the ring.] Moraless: I don't know what happened to Eddie B. but since he got that necklace he's been a lot more sadistic. Candler: You're talking about the same man who kidnapped Rachael and tore down a church. [Hardy lifts Satima up and goes to whip him into the turnbuckle post, but Satima reverses it and whips Hardy so fast into the turnbuckle post that he falls and Hardy's body smashes against the post and slingshots him down to the canvas. Satima crawls over to Hardy and rolls him onto his back putting his arm over him.] 1.. 2.... [Hardy gets his foot on the ropes breaking the count. Satima stands up and drags Hardy by his arm towards the center of the ring, pinning him again.] 1... 2..... [Hardy kicks out again and Satima slams his hands against the mat. Satima lifts Hardy up and grabs him by the back of his neck tossing him outside the ring. Satima rolls outside of the ring and picks up what seems to be a bamboo stick from the remains of West's shopping cart. Satima slams the stick against the ring apron calling Hardy. Hardy gets up and Satima swings with all his strength, but Hardy ducks and the force sends Satima in a circle. Satima comes back facing Hardy and Hardy nails him with a DDT. Hardy points down at Satima and then up at the dome similar to West earlier on and Hardy starts to make his way up... Satima gets up quickly and climbs up near Hardy. The two hold on to the dome and they start to exchange punch for punch with one another.] Candler: This doesn't look too good.. Moraless: Now that West is gone who you going with? Candler: Chris Sanchez has been gone who you going with? Moraless: Satima... he's experienced and he wants it... Candler: I'm going with Hardy... just for the fact that you're not... [Hardy gets the upper hand and grabs Satima hooking his head up. Hardy screams and leaps off the top of the dome to the floor below with the Fate of a Gangsta!!] Moraless: OH MY GOD! Candler: MY PICK IS GOING GREAT! [The two men lay there without any signs of life as DM continues to look on....] Candler: What the hell does he want?!?! Moraless: This is an exciting match maybe he wants a front row seat... [Hardy gets up and stumbles backwards falling into the dome wall. Hardy looks around as if he doesn't know where he is. Hardy goes over to Satima lifting him up and rolling him into the ring. He slides into the ring and goes for the pin fall...] Candler: This is it.. 1... Moraless: Come on Satima! 2... Candler: My pick! 3...!! [BUT NO! Satima gets his foot on the ropes!] Candler: What the hell is it gonna take!? [Hardy crawls to the top rope and signals for the Five Star Gangsta Splash... he leaps off, but Satima rolls out of the way and Hardy slams chest first into the canvas. Hardy holds his chest and rolls around the ring in pain as the camera pans to Eddie B. showing him laughing and clapping his hands together. Back in the ring Satima grabs Hardy and nails him with the Cut Throat Driver!! Satima pins Hardy...] 1... 2..... 3.......!!!!!! Tania Brown: You're winner....LEE SATIMA!! [The camera zooms out back to Lee.] Lee: And there you have it ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, bitches and bastards alike, the night I defeated 90% of the competition in this tournament... [Lee smirks.] Cameraman: But you never actually pinned West… [Lee glares at the camera once more.] Lee: I know that, but I still beat everyone in that Dome and triumphed over Caitlin in one night, which is more than you can ever say. [Lee smacks the camera.] [Fade Out.]
