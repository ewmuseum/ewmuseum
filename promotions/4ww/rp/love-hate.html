Love/Hate Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #552 Date: Feb 23rd Location: ... Words. They are everything that our language depends on. We couldn’t survive without them, they are the foundation of our lives and our feelings. It’s amazing how these almost random constructs of letters being mashed together can define something like love, or hate for that matter. I mean here I am using words to convey my emotions towards words, it’s ironic… love (l v) n. 1. A deep, tender, ineffable feeling of affection and solicitude toward a person, such as that arising from kinship, recognition of attractive qualities, or a sense of underlying oneness. 2. A feeling of intense desire and attraction toward a person with whom one is disposed to make a pair; the emotion of sex and romance. 3. a. Sexual passion. b. Sexual intercourse. c. A love affair. 4. An intense emotional attachment, as for a pet or treasured object. 5. A person who is the object of deep or intense affection or attraction; beloved. Often used as a term of endearment. 6. An expression of one's affection: Send him my love. 7. a. A strong predilection or enthusiasm: a love of language. b. The object of such an enthusiasm: The outdoors is her greatest love. So many different meanings for a four letter word. So many meanings for one of the most meaningful things that you can feel. But everything has an opposite. hate (h t) v. hat•ed, hat•ing, hates v. tr. 1. a. To feel hostility or animosity toward. b. To detest. 2. To feel dislike or distaste for: hates washing dishes. Now why is it that hate only has a few definitions but love has so many more. Is it because people like to feel loved and like to have a broader range of what it means so they can feel good about themselves. Is it because no one wants to be hated? No one wants to hate? Well I do, I love to hate and hate to love. I love to be hated and hate to be loved. There is only one person in my life who has the proverbial keys to my heart and that’s Fable. She showed me this world. This world of misery, this world of hate, and darkness. She showed me this world, the real world. Now I ask you can you see this world or are you blinded by what they tell you? Do I care? No I really don’t. Why do I ask? ‘Cuz I wanna know how much of an idiot you are. And you are obviously dumb enough to believe that people care, that people love you and that people respect you. Well they don’t. Who am I talking to you ask… All of you. All of you idiots, you morons, you meatheads whatever you’re called I don’t care. You are all weak and pathetic and that’s why I am here, I’m here to rid this world of lower forms of life, clear a path for the true people who deserve to live, the very few people who actually understand what this life is about. But don’t worry, you’ll understand soon enough… When you die and believe me that’s soon enough.
