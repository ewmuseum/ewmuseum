Shadow File #1 - Pilot Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1129 Date: 1/12/05 Location: |>> It’s a late Monday night in the “City of Angels” where a man dressed in someone else’s blood was running down a back alley, as if something or…someone has given him quite the fright. The man reaches a dead end, slams both his blood soak hands on the solid brick wall in frustration. > Footsteps was heard. > The frighten man snaps his head in the direction of the noise, glaring angrily and frighteningly, awaiting for a certain someone to come out from the shadows. > Man frustratingly growls. > Billy’s eyes flare a crimson color and roars out, as his face takes a monstrous turn. He lunges at Kyo and goes for a straight punch, but Billy’s fist goes straight through Kyo’s face as he nothing but Kyo’s after-image. Billy watches Kyo’s after-image disappear, growls in frustration, trying to sense Kyo’s whereabouts. Billy sharpens his glowing eyes and snaps his head in the left direction, but his serious expression takes a frighten turn when seeing the barrel of Kyo’s modified black colored 9mm aimed right at his face. > Kyo have his head lowered making his raven colored hair conceal his piercing cold brown colored eyes. He smirks in delight, pulls the trigger and fires a single plasma round. Billy’s head explodes in blood, as Billy falls dead on the ground with blood and brains oozing from the back of his shattered head. Kyo kept the smoking drawn out for a moment before holstering it inside of his long black colored leather trench coat. > Suddenly, a strange thick black colored slime seeps out from Billy’s open skull. > Kyo holds out a right hand and chants an incantation causes his entire hand to glow a cerulean colored aura. > The strange ooze springs off the concrete ground and attempts to attack Kyo. > Kyo’s eyes flare in cerulean color and finishes the incantation by tightly grabbing hold of the ooze, watches it squirm but it quickly stops when Kyo absorbs the evil essence of the ooze. After absorbing all its’ evil essence, the slime turns to a brittle piece of white stone. He crushes the stone, sprinkles the stone’s dust over the Billy’s corpse. Kyo returns to normal and quietly walks away from the body. > Billy’s body mysteriously ignites in a crimson colored fire, burning away. > Meanwhile, at the far continent of an early morning Germany, German police surrounds a crime scene. A young homicide officer kneels before a dead body, examines it carefully and notices that the dead victim was extremely old. > An old homicide officer enters the crime scene. > Old homicide officer stares down at the odd corpse seriously mix with curiously. > Back at Outlaw Dynasty in Santa Monica, California, the Deathscythe Outlaw enters inside, takes off his trench coat and throws it on a chair. He was wearing a black holster that holds his modified 9mm. Kyo walks through his dark home, but stops when seeing his German shepherd companion, Sephiroth come and greet him. > Sephiroth pants softly and gives his master a gentle lick to his cheek. >>| “That’s good you’re doing ok,” Kyo contently told his companion with a smile. |>> Kyo stands and walks over to the living room, pushes a button on the answer machine. > Kyo opens the fridge door and closes the door after taking out a cold bottle beer. He flicks the bottle cap off the beer, drinks it. > The answer machine beeps. > Dullness fills Kyo’s face and sweat drop trickles down from side of his face when he heard his sister call him that. > Kyo leaves the kitchen and makes his way back to the living room. Sephiroth enters the living room too, hops up on the couch and comfortably rests there. > The answer machine beeps again. > Kyo shakes his head in disapproval when he heard Charlene call him Mr. > Kyo looks at the answer machine with concern, knows what was bothering the young Chinese girl, sighs softly and takes another sip from the bottle. > The answer machine beeps once more. > Anger fills Kyo’s face for some reason. He reaches over to the nearby phone, picks it up and dials a number, listens to the ringing for a moment until someone picked up. Kyo speaks in the Greek language to the person on the other end who was no other then his girlfriend’s grandfather. He listens to her grandfather reply to him in the same language. Kyo closes his eyes and tries to keep his temper in check, calmly tells her grandfather a message in Greek. After a moment, Kyo hangs up the phone and lies back in his chair. > Kyo goes in his pocket, takes out a picture of Caitlin and stares at the pic with a weak smirk. > Kyo sighs and favors his heart, looks with a bit of sadness. > Kyo finishes up the beer and places the empty beer bottle on a nearby coffee table. He grabs the TV remote, turns on the TV, and watches it. > The night fades away as a new day appears over the horizon. > In the living room of Kyo’s detective agency, Kyo laid comfortably on the couch asleep with his shirt off but he kept his pants on. He stirs a bit and makes a soft grunt from something bothering him in sleep. > Kyo’s dream takes place in an open grass field at night. > A gentle wind blows that makes the tall grass lean to the side. With the gently breeze it cause white petals from a Japanese Cherry Blossom to rain down from the dark colored night. > In the mix of the raining Cherry Blossom petals, a woman with long brown colored hair that wears an attire of that of a Shinto Priestess have her back turned to the world. She slowly turns around and looks out at Kyo with her hair over her blue compassionate eyes. > Suddenly, the Shinto Priestess’ chest explodes in blood, an arrow have pierced through her heart from the behind. The falling petals changes to the same shade of color as the Priestess’ blood. She slowly falls. > Shinto Priestess hits the ground and lies on the scattered petals. > Another arrow comes out of nowhere and heads right where Kyo was. > Everything fades to black, but a bright crimson flash appears with the sound of a splash. > Back in the living room of Kyo’s agency, Kyo quickly sat up with his 9mm drawn, looks around his surroundings disturbingly. He pants heavily while feeling cold sweat run down his face. Kyo calms down, sits the couch and rests the gun on his forehead, his eyes are closed, reflecting on the dream he had. > Kyo favors a nasty scar on his chest that reside a few inches away from the heart. > Kyo opens his eyes a bit, looks over in the direction to the woman’s voice and sees a woman in an attractive brown colored business dress with a pair black high heels that have long blonde hair and blue eyes that are concealed behind black sunglasses stands beside the couch. > Sephiroth enjoys Faith’s warm touch. > Kyo laughs amusingly from hearing Faith’s comment. He stands and walks over to the kitchen with gun still in hand. > Kyo stops drinking the beer and looks over to Faith seriously. > Kyo looks over in the direction to the man and notices an old German man in a black colored suit with a pair of expensive grey shoes that have grayish-brown colored hair with brown eyes stand at the front door with a dark colored brown folder in hand. > Old German man approaches Faith and stands beside Faith. > Kyo stares down at Evan’s hand, but turns his attention back at Faith and looks at her seriously. > Evan clears his throat and lowers his hand, looks with embarrassment from Kyo ignoring him like that. > Kyo laughs when he heard Evan’s words. > Kyo take the folder and reads the contents inside, sees that a file talks about the recent incident that happen in Germany the other night with the extremely old corpse. > Kyo turns the page and sees a black and white picture of a young Japanese man in a military uniform. > Evan stares at Kyo seriously but yet there was some doubt for some reason. Kyo looks with seriousness when noticing this. > Kyo heads back to the kitchen. > Faith whistles in reaction to Evan’s words. > Evan softly gasps in surprise when he heard Kyo’s comment. > Evan changes his surprise expression to a calm one. > Kyo sharpens his eyes in serious glare, sees a single trail of sweat running down the side of Evan’s face. > Evan curiously stares at Kyo when he heard his reply. > Evan nods and leaves Kyo’s agency. > Outside of the agency, a black colored car rides away with the German Intelligence, Evan inside. > Inside of the car, Evan sat on side of the car and a mysterious figure sat in the shadows on the side of the car. > The black car drives into a back alley and stops. > Figure diligently thinks. > Evan looks with great fear. > A gunshot is fired in the car, blood and brains splatter on the back window. > The back car door opens, a Japanese man in all red that have green colored hair and piercing gold eyes that are concealed behind a pair of blue tinted shades steps out with a smoking Desert Eagle in hand, closes the door and walks over to the driver’s side of the car. > The driver’s window lowers, reveals a man in a black colored suit with a pair shades of his eyes sat at the wheel. > Man nods and raise the window, drives away with Evan’s corpse inside. > Man in red disappears in the alley. > Back in Kyo’s living room, Kyo practices his swordsmanship with the wooden sword. Faith sits on the couch with Sephiroth, watches Kyo practice. > Sephiroth enjoys Faith’s soft touch. > Kyo spins in an 180 degree turn and follows through a strong side swing, kneels down on one knee. He stands, spins the sword around his hand, rests it behind his back and looks at Faith seriously. > Kyo lights a cigarette with his silver lighter and smokes it. > Faith thinks when she heard Kyo’s comment. > Kyo puts out the cigarette in an ashtray. > Faith makes a sound and looks at Kyo curiously. > Kyo nods to Faith’s words. He looks out the nearby window, sees his neighborhood shine under the bright morning sun and thinks about the case he just accepted. > To be continued View Masked Avenger 's Biography
