Shadow File #7 - Transformation Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1192 Date: 3/7/05 Location: |>> Walks down the long prison hall, the attractive Faith in a dark violet business dress with black high heels, friend and business associate to Kyo Kushinaga, was being escorted by an Italian police officer, listening to the loud whistles and cat calls that the convicts were giving her from behind their cages as it has been a long time since they seen a woman. > Sweats and pants heavily, a shirtless Kyo Kushinaga was hanging upside from the ceiling of his cell doing sits up and spins his body side to side with each time he lifted himself up, as he had his back on the metal door. > Flips down, Kyo stands before the cell’s lumpy and worn out bed with his head lowered causes his hair fall over his eyes, as his sweat runs down the demonic markings on his back that pulses a crimson color. > Holds his hand out, Kyo stares down at the palm of his hand through damp strains of his hair, having a fragmented flashback about Todd Dream interfering and nailing him with the Dreamscape DDT, then sees himself destroying Dark Angel with the use of chair, a loud crash halts the flashback, as Kyo with a dark eerie glow around him has thrown the entire bed on wall. Kyo stands straight, staring at the hole that he made in the hole with calmness but yet there was great coldness in his eyes. > Walks over to the wall, Kyo takes a seat on the flipped over bed under the large hole, leans his head back having his eyes closed. Applauds, Kyo reopens his eyes when he heard the applauding in his head, sees that he was not in his cell anymore, as he was back in the Timeless Realm, a realm that can either freeze time or accelerated it to show one’s future. Looks around his surroundings, Kyo notices that he was seeing the future of Tokyo, the future of him making the large metropolis burn within the flames of his rage and hatred. Applauds continues, Kyo looks in the direction of the slow and steady applauding, sees that his very long hair counterpart was the one applauding, as he causally sitting on top of a worn out billboard that stood above a short ruin building, staring down at his short hair counterpart. > Silent, Kyo intently watches his counterpart hop down from the billboard, landing on edge of the building, then hops off the building and lands on the ground with ease, staring right at him with a sly grin. > Kyo nods. > Ground violently shakes, Kyo stands his ground, staring at what’s left of the building with great calmness, as he watches crimson color beams pierce through the fallen debris. Bursts out of the rubble, Kyo’s counterpart floats above what’s left of the building with his long hair flaring up from the dark aura that surrounded him, glaring down at Kyo. > Silent, Kyo stared down at the ground, having his hair fall over his eyes. > Spews out blood, Kyo’s long hair counterpart was bent over in pain, as Kyo have soared up in the air and struck him in the gut viciously with a punch, then connects with a damaging spin kick to the gut again, knocking the counterpart through three buildings which collapses in impact. Coughs up more blood, the counterpart was on fours, staring down at the street, but he looks up, and sees Kyo taking his time approaching him. > Leaps high in the air, the long hair counterpart holds out both hands, gathering intense dark energy, and forms a large emerald orb in his hands, then fires the great attack down at Kyo with a mighty yell. Kyo stands there, staring up at his counterpart with an intense bloodlust in his eyes, as the orb strikes Kyo, exploding greatly in a wide and dark and electrifying dome. Pants heavily, the counterpart sees smoke surrounding the entire area. > Looks with shock, the counterpart watches Kyo with miasma and all tear right through the thick smoke, as Kyo reaches out, grabbing hold of the counterpart’s face, and dives straight down, both crashes through the rooftop of a tall leaning building, a great explosion occurs at the entrance of the building. Gasping for air, Kyo have his counterpart up on the wall, as he kept a tight grip around his face, staring up into the counterpart’s fearful eyes. > Kyo’s entire right arm glows a dim emerald color, making the counterpart’s face to fill up with great fright, but the counterpart’s expression changes to a curious one when seeing essences of his own soul leave his body, and going into Kyo’s glowing arm. > Kyo’s explodes in black miasma that was giving off strong currents of electricity, as his short hair grows long like his counterpart, and his eyes fumes a crimson mist, staring down at his helpless counterpart. Weakly and shakily holds out his hand, the counterpart glares at him, panting heavily, as he attempts to fire an energy attack at Kyo, but nothing then a speck was fired, it pops and disappears a few inches away from him, gasping with a terrified look. Kneels down, Kyo holds his hand right in the counterpart’s face, staring at him without a though of hesitation. > Stares with fear, the counterpart watches Kyo shoot out a strong bright emerald orb of shadow at him, everything brightly lights up in a great explosion. Door unlocks, Kyo was back in his cell, sitting on the flipped over bed with his head lowered, having his hair fall over his eyes, as the demonic markings on his back pulse a crimson color through his very long strands of his midnight hair with grey highlights. Door opens, an Italian police officer stands in the door, examining the cell, as he looks with shock from seeing what Kyo did to the wall, but turns his attention back over to Kyo. > Faith glares at Kyo, sensing that Kyo has changed into a much stronger and darker being than before. Groans, an extremely old Japanese man sits alone in a dark room underneath a demonic magic crest, leaning down in his life support wheelchair, favoring his head, as a young Japanese woman with long dark blue hair and brown eyes in all black leather attire quickly approaches him. > Leans back in the wheelchair; master sighs out, opens his eyes, and stares up at the dark ceiling. > Walks down the prison hall, Kyo and Faith were being escorted by the same police officer, as Faith notices that the convicts are silent, and backing away from their cells, realizing that Kyo’s mere presence are the result of their strange behavior, looks back over to Kyo to see that he was looking forward with a deadly calmness in his eyes. Leaving the police station, Kyo straightens his trench coat, and then puts on his orange tint shades over his eyes, avoiding the bright sunlight. > Goes in pocket, Kyo pulls out his Shadow Ninjas’ pocket watch, throws it at Faith, as Faith catches it with ease in her left hand, watching him turn his back on her and leave. > Walks through a grand hotel lobby, Kyo lights a cigarette, takes a couple of puffs, before he sighs out a long flowing cloud of nicotine pass his lips. > Blinks, the clerk stares at him with confusion, wondering what he meant by that. Exits the hotel’s cafeteria, Charlene in warm clothes, holding a suitcase in her hand, and heads to the hotel’s exit to catch a taxi to make her flight to Greece with Blaise and Ukyo, but stops when she notices Kyo waiting for an elevator. > Stands outside of the grand hotel, Charlene holds her bag before her knees, staring down at the red carpet with concern in her eyes, thinking about Kyo, and wondering why he reverted back to the person she hoped wouldn’t return. > Opening the package, Kyo sits at the foot of the bed in his dark hotel room, and through his shades he sees a very familiar attire inside the package, the very attire that he wore in the match that won him the best match of 2004, that has also been deemed as the most bloodiest match of 4WW’s history. > Holds up an old gauntlet, Kyo sniffs his old gauntlet, smelling what’s left of Tarrant’s scent on it, traces his fingers on the metal plates on the back of the gauntlet. > Stands up from the bed, Kyo gets out of the clothes that reminded of him being the soft Outlaw, and changes into his old attire that reeks the scent of blood and death, but stops and compares both trench coats, decides to go with the new trench coat since it resembled more like a cape with it’s long length, snaps the coat close with a strap that rests across his shirt with the old white ghostly Punisher skull. > Stares down in the package, Kyo sees that the black hat was all that remained to complete the attire, picks it up, puts it on, lowers it down over his face. > Sunlight seeps through the closed blinds, Kyo stands in the light, it gives his dark appearance a gentle glow, lifts his head, revealing his calm frightening eyes behind his orange tint shades, as he disappears in the thick black miasma. Stands at the docks, the Deathscythe Outlaw was back in Los Angeles, staring up at the biggest and brightest full moon in the clear night sky, but turns his attention down at the black abyss, holds out his right hand, an emerald glow shines through his sleeve, and watches the water bubbling wildly, as a crimson light appears at the bubbling area. Slowly rises from the ocean, a familiar four foot object that was wrapped in a thick cloth floats above the now calming waters, as Kyo calls it over with his dark power, grabbing hold of the soak and wet object. > Glides through the night, a black crow lands on the rooftop of a warehouse, it crows a haunting note, causing Kyo to gasp from sensing the crow’s presence nearby, as he was now seeing through the eyes of the crow like he did once before. > Blinks, Kyo’s vision returns to normal, drops the wrapped object, as he swings the trench coat’s long end to the side quickly, pulls out his guns from behind, fires his guns at the rooftops of two warehouses, watches two suited men fall off the rooftops, and hitting the concrete ground. Machine gun fires, Kyo turn his body in a 90 degree angle, fires, killing a suit that hid halfway in a warehouse. Two suits comes out of hiding, Kyo quickly leaps in the dark heavens, disappearing in the miasma, reappears behind both men, having the hot barrels of his guns resting on the back of their heads, as he listens to suits panicking with his head lowered, but their panicking ends when he blew their brains away, lifts his head, revealing the distant look in his eyes behind his shades, that was known as the look of untamed beast who craves for the blood and death. Suit steps out from between two warehouses, Kyo kicks up the wrapped object, and does a sharp spin kick, throwing the object right through the suit’s arm, the suit was nailed up against the far wall, Kyo fires the guns at the suit while approaching him, watching the suit’s body exploding in blood with each, but Kyo continues firing, instead of seeing the suit squirm, he was playing the flashback with the sibling prick, Todd Dream screwing him over on repeat in his mind, Kyo reaches the blood soak suit, shoves the hot barrel of his 9mm in the suit’s mouth, now he sees the sellout, Dark Angel holding his title, causing to Kyo eyes flare up his eyes and releasing a sizzling mist from his eyes, fires the gun, as the suit’s blood splatter on his face. Suit leaps out of nowhere, Kyo releases the 9mm clip halfway, and thrusts the gun to the side, throwing the clip right in suit’s face, Kyo swiftly lunges at the suit when distracted, pulls back his right arm, and strikes the suit hard in the face, watching the suit’s entire face explode in blood, then follows through with a strong mid-air spin kick to the head, not only breaking the suit’s neck on impact, but throwing him right through the warehouse’s wall. Shoots gun out of hand, Kyo sees a suit shooting while charging at him in complete panic, but Kyo stands there with not a hint of fear in his eyes, feeling and listening the hot bullets pass him, the suit reaches Kyo, as Kyo leaps up, knocking the suit down with a strong knee shot to the ear. Two suits steps out of opposite warehouses, Kyo hops on the back of the nearby suit, kicks off, and rides the suit like a skateboard, picks up his fallen piece, reloading both his guns, fires, killing both men when he rode up to both warehouse. Vision changes, Kyo sees two men setting up on two rooftops of nearby warehouses through the eyes of the black bird, blinks, changing his vision back to normal, and notices a pair of rusty thick pipes, having an idea in mind, kicks off a couple of more times, rides the suit up the pipes like a ramp, catching some serious air, crosses the guns over his chest, kills the two men on the rooftop with quick shoots to the heads. Machine guns fire, hot bullets are tearing the skateboard suit apart, the torn off limbs and torso hits the hard concrete ground, blood oozing out, but the suit with smoking double machine guns notices no sign of the Deathscythe Outlaw. > Crow squawks, Kyo senses a presence, and looks over to it, but gets frozen in a ice block, as a Chinese man with long blonde hair and golden eyes in a blue suit appears in the scene, staring at Kyo within the ice with a smirk. > Ice cracks and leaks, black miasma seeps out, the man looks with shock from seeing his ice melting by the miasma. > Holds up arm, the suits’ corpses shiver up to ashes, as their souls flies out from the ashes, and soars over from the beacon that was calling them, all the souls goes into Kyo’s right arm, forming a dark emerald-onyx aura. > Pulls back arm, Kyo sharpens his eyes, staring at the suit with great calmness. > Drifts in the ocean, the suit’s burnt and mutilated corpse stares blankly at the night sky, as Kayin was at the docks on his motor scooter, looking down at the corpse, but turns his attention to a large impact crater that was right beside him, seeing first hand what Kyo’s new dark power is enabling him to do. > Holds out the wrapped object, Kyo stands on top of a cathedral’s statue cross, that stood outside on the rooftop, strokes his fingers lightly across the covered object, sensing and feeling the raw hatred it contained through the cloth. > Crow squawks, it lands on Kyo’s right shoulder, looking around the area, but it stops, and looks at his new master, it feels his master rub his throat lightly, as it revealed that Kyo has gone through one more transformation, his midnight color long hair changed to beautiful, but yet deadly silver color, completing his transformation as the Shadow of Death, Kage. > Walks across the rooftop, Kyo stares out at the bright city of Los Angeles through his orange tint shades with the crow still on his shoulder. > Kyo disappears in the black miasma. > To be continued
