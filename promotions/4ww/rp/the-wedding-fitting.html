The Wedding: Fitting

22 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Wedding: Fitting 4WW Exposures #1067 Date: 11/22/04 Location: Wendy's Dress Shop (The scene opens in what looks to be a tailors shop. There is material and pins and measuring tape all over the place. As the camera pans around, there is someone standing upon the stand. The white fabric can bee seen hanging over her feet and there is a black-haired woman standing there, pinning and measuring, trying to get the measurements just right. The camera pans up over the dress. As we get higher and higher, we can see that the dress is extremely low cut, and we can start to see the long full bodied blond hair of the one and only Tempest Champion. The camera continues to pan up over the spaghetti straps of the dress.) LLL: The dress has to be perfect. I know you know that, and that’s why I came to you, Wendy. Wendy: Yes Diana, I know. But you of all people should know the high quality of my designs. You’ve seen them now for years. You’ve put my business on the map. LLL: Yes, I know. You’ll have to excuse me hon, I’m just a little more nervous now than I have ever been. Wendy. We’ll, don’t be. There is no reason to. This dress will be as lovely as any dress you have ever seen. Now just hush down so that I can get this exactly right. (Luscious Lady Luck respects the wishes, and starts to think introspectively. The thoughts flash all over the place. Weddings, Patti, Caitlin, Paul, Future Shock, Zane. Everyone and everything goes running through her head. This week was going to be a hectic week. But what could she do. She knew what she was getting into, and she was ready for the challenge. And Patti looked so beautiful in the dress she was wearing for her wedding. This had to be even more perfect than that.) (The camera pans down again, over the dress that Triple L is currently wearing. The dress itself is a tight well fitting dress. Made of lots of jewels and fine silk. We get to the bottom of the dress, where we see Wendy still working hard on it, but there is something a bit different this time, Wendy looks younger than she had before. She also looks a lot more nervous.) LLL: I don’t know why you’re so nervous Wendy. Wendy: This is the first chance I’ve ever had to show off my skills. And especially with such a high class modeling agent as your Ms. King. LLL: Don’t worry about it; I’m sure it’ll be fine. Wouldn’t you agree hon? (The camera pans up. The dress looks very similar to the one that was being worn before, only it is quite a bit taller. As the camera pans up, the hair this time is red, not blonde. As we reach the top of the dress and the face, we recognize it’s a younger version of Patti Anderson. The camera pans out to show Luscious Lady Luck standing off to the side, looking at Patti with a twinkle in her eye.) Patti: Wendy, This dress is absolutely bloody gorgeous. I don't deserve the honor of wearing your crowning glory in this show. Wendy: Do you really think so? I’ve tried so hard to get everything perfect. LLL: She’s right Wendy. I have no doubt that this show is going to put you on the map bigger than you could ever expect. And of course the crowning glory of the show should be worn by the brightest star in the show. (Triple L slowly walks around Patti, admiring the dress from every angle. Patti just follows LLL with her eyes. LLL grabs a hold of the fabric, lifting it up, and letting it fall from her hand as she walks around. She kneels down next to Wendy as she is working on the gown, looking up at Patti.) LLL: I know that you’ve had plenty of customers already Wendy, but I might just have to become your biggest? You look great in this dress hon. And I could think of other uses for you in it other than just modeling. (LLL smiles slyly up at Patti.) Patti: Other uses? What are you talking about? LLL: There is only one other use for a wedding dress that I can think of hon. But that will come in due time. (LLL walks out of the room, leaving Wendy and Patti alone together. Wendy seems too pre-occupied with her work to notice anything, and Patti is just standing there in slight confusion, but doing what she can to help the process along. Wendy masterfully measures and pins the dress with air tight precision. (Shortly, thereafter, The Lucky One walks back into the room, wearing a similar wedding dress. She makes her way over, stealthily toward Patti, making her way up onto the stand where Patti is standing.) LLL: Well, what do you think? Patti: Wow! Yer planning on going out there with me aren’t you? LLL: Of course I am. The more exposure and the more beautiful designs Wendy can get out there the better. (Wendy looks up, not noticing the LLL had made her way back into the room. She smiles warmly.) Wendy: Oh look, a pair of blushing brides. (Trips and Patti both look down at Wendy, smiling. They then look at each other. LLL embraces Patti around the neck and kisses her on the lips. The camera pans back on down the pair in the dresses to Wendy. Wendy has returned to her original age, and there is now only one dress there.) Wendy: Ms. King? Are there any other alterations you want to the dress? LLL: What? What did you say hon? Wendy: I was just wondering if you wanted any more alterations to the dress. Are you ok? LLL: Yeah, just have my mind on other things. Wendy: Well, if you don’t need anymore alterations, you can slip out of the dress and it’ll be ready for you. LLL: Thanks hon. (Luscious Lady Luck slips out of the dress carefully. The camera is careful to keep LLL in just a head shot. Other things on her mind. You bet there were other things on her mind. What was Patti doing? What are her plans in all of this?) (And beyond that, she had the Tempest Title to worry about. Paul Williams, a great competitor, and an even sexier man. But she was the longest running Tempest Champion ever. She knew what she was getting into, and no one was going to beat her anytime soon for that. That was, unless, she didn’t keep her mind on what she was doing. And Patti was doing a hell of a job distracting her.) (Why was Patti doing what she was doing? She’s married. She wouldn’t cheat on her husband, would she? She just wouldn’t do that. And it was Patti who was keeping her distracted from what she must do.) (And then there was the whole Tag Team Ladder match with Zane. Zane and LLL were great friends. They formed a wonderful team after the departure of Landen. And the shocked a lot of people. The ladder match, right up the alley of LLL, and with Zane at her back, it was going to be hard to beat her. The Infidels were a great team to face, but they were both big, both not exactly cut out for a ladder match. This match just wasn’t up there alley. But Future Shock. They both could do a lot of damage. And both of them have the ability and the grace to climb the ladder. Even though Blaise McClancy hadn’t ever won a title ladder match, that didn’t mean anything. The McClancys were on a roll. Sean just recently married Patti after all.) (And yet Patti was putting moves on her. Why would Patti be doing that? Especially so recently after being married. Was she so unhappy already? Or did she want to offer her husband more? What was it? LLL had to get her mind off of Patti. She had to get her mind back on her match. She had to do what she needed to do, and leave Patti out of it.) (At least she didn’t have to worry about Patti’s sister Caitlin anymore. With the restraining order on her, she wouldn’t be able to come close to her. And that just left things more open for LLL to get to Patti. But it was Patti who was getting to LLL. It was Patti’s mind games that were winning. Or were they really mind games? Did Patti really want her again.) (Two matches this week. She had the Tempest Title to defend. She had the East Wind Championship to go after and attempt to win. And she had to have her mind clear from all other distractions, who ever they were.) (But she couldn’t, she just couldn’t do it.) Wendy: Ms. King? LLL: (startled) Yes? Wendy: Are you ok? You seem overly distracted. More than I’ve ever known you to be. LLL: Just got a ton on my mind. Thanks for everything your doing hon. I’ll be back in a few weeks to pick up the dress. (With that, LLL walks out of the door, forgetting that she had forgotten to put her clothes back on.) Wendy: MS. KING!!! MS. KING!!!! (It was too late. LLL was out of the shop and into her limo. Distracted and all. This just wasn’t doing any good. LLL needed to stop being distracted. Especially with such huge matches coming up. But would she do it? That’s what everyone wanted to know.) (Whatever Patti’s plan, she had accomplished, she was in LLL’s head. Would it cost LLL her belt and stop her as the longest reigning Tempest Champion ever? Would it cost her and Zane the East Wind Belts? Or would it not become a factor? It looks like a factor now.) (The scene fades out to the purple heart on a black background with “LLL+PA” written in the middle.)
