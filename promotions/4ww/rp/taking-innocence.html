His actions speak volumes of his character, but how can one portray the hatred, the pain and suffering? They say a picture can express a thousand words... [Atlanta, Georgia ~ 17:36 ~ 01/12/03] ...The grandfather clock chimes as the large hand reaches twelve and the small, six. There is a pleasant odor aloft, the air is sweet. A light can be seen in the near distance, slowly we creep closer to the brightness. The world is once again illuminated and the sense seem to collaborate creating the perfect setting. A beautiful woman stands near the kitchen sink washing her hands. At first you are not sure what captures your attention, the amazing light blue sky trapped in her eyes or the sweet as chocolate yet sultry smile. Nonetheless, her look, her aroma, it is pleasing. She is dressed in a breath-taking dress, and her slim feet are covered in tan nylons. She pops her head around the corner and she begins to speak. 'Dinner's ready, come and get it.' A rush comes to you at once as the entire family is revealed. From the slim young lad wearing a red Yankees cap, to the medium built, tall, dark and stereotypically handsome man. Last but not least the princess of the group, her beauty is inexplicable, though she did not smile. Infact, her eyes rarely managed to bring themselves from the cold floor. As dinner had been placed, a conversation had managed to strike a chord. 'Billy, how was school?' The man asked as he nods his thanks to his wife for the plate of food. Bill brushes his blonde hair back under his cap and shoots a slight grin. 'That good?' 'Could be better, could be worse, it's school. All of sudden she felt so heavy, the eyes all moved to her and their attention was exactly where she did not want it to be, on her. She manages to raise her eyes level with the rest of the field and forces out a convincing grin. Her mother moves in beside her and takes a seat. 'How did rehersal go?' Again the attention moves from the others and plants itself squarely on the young lady. 'Jessica, you ok honey?' If only she could tell, but she was told not to... not now, not ever. Instead she shakes her head no and excuses herself from the table. She quickly escapes the vision of the others and finds herself pulling herself upstairs towards her room. Closing the door, she could only wish there was a lock... or even something else, maybe a weapon. Taking the opportunity to say the wrong thing at the wrong time, Billy opens his mouth. 'Anyone else think she's 'el poyo loco'?' 'Billy!' His mother shouts, and his father's look says enough to make him shrink to nothingness. 'John, maybe I should go talk to her.' With only number one in mind John stands up and pushes his plate forward, 'I'll do it.' John moves out of the light and into the shadows of the corridor, he runs his finger along the wall as he comes to the banister and stairway. Taking a firm hold of the rail, he steps slowly onto the first step. His mind whirls in thoughts of what he may do or say. Before the thoughts can finish dancing upon his mind, he reaches the top. A pale light eminates from benieth the crack of a door, 'pink lights' he thought to himself. He finds himself reaching towards the rustic looking handle and opening the door slightly before peaking his head in. 'Honey?' Jessica is seen sitting with her head between her knees. Her father stalkingly moves into position. He sits next to her on the bed. 'Is everything okay?' Silence. He looks over to her, obviously she is an emotional wreck and John does his best. He carefully places his hand on her thigh. She slowly raises her head, rain falls from her face and there are markings upon her eyes. She looks directly into her fathers eyes and stares. A few moments pass and her eyes are still locked in position. John has moved his hand slowly into her inner thigh, a slight grin flickers upon his face. The pain, the suffering, her world, her life and most importantly her innocence, all robbed from her. With each passing second he has moved his hand closer and closer... His face drawing so much closer than it was before. She could smell the stench of his breath, she hated this man, most of all she hated herself for not being capable of stopping him. With his right arm he pushes Jessica down to the bed as he works the button on her jeans with his free hand. As she falls back to the bed everything came rushing back. Her whole life flowed through her memories... she would do anything to numb this feeling, Though, there could really be no escape. Even as a child Jessica was often visited in the creeping hours of the morning, often the putrid odor of alcohol could be smelt lingering through the door. While most children were concerned with nightmares and visions of ghosts and the boogie man, Jessica had already become a woman... her dreams collaborated thoughts of hatred and escape. Her nightmares were drenched in seamen, she could only recall being in excrusiating pain as she was entered for the first time. As she grew and time passed, the only thing that changed was the frequency of the visits... This had become a nightly routine. A man who created life, nurtured and cared for it, had decided to use it for his own personal reasons. He stole everything from her, she had remembered her visit with the doctor a couple months ago. Her life and her ability to give life was taken, taken by the very man who waited for her in the lobby. Taken by the same man who drove her home, kissed her goodnight and then proceeded to indulge into forbidden pleasures. The memories fade as she comes to... the room is dark, her body is cold, naked and sticky. She reaches onto the floor and pulls on her underwear. As she lifts her head she catches her reflection in the mirror. Her body was no longer hers, it had become the playground of a greater evil. She carefully studies the reflection in the darkness and for what seems like an eternity, she stares into her own eyes.
