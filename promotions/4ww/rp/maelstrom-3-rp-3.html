Maelstrom 3 RP 3 La Envidia Mata 4WW Exposures #605 Date: 3/24/2004 Location: The scene opens up with La Envidia Mata sitting on a leather sofa. He is wearing a La Envidia Mata t-shirt and as usual his sunglasses. He is watching a tape of the unification title match from JICWF Xtreme between him and Canadian Perfect for the TV and Intercontinental Titles. He takes a quick look up at the camera before returning his eyes to the tape. Looks of anger come across his face, eventually he looks back at the camera. La Envidia Mata: Ok Ok, Chris I know your sitting at home or wherever you are enjoying this like never before. Chris I know this is the biggest pin fall win you ever made. This win was what Canadian Perfect was all about. Winning world titles or any other title means nothing to this win for you. You know what Chris I'm giving you this one. You beat me, No excuses. No nothing. You won and kept your I-C title. Maybe I am losing my skill. Maybe I am not good enough to remain in the actual ring. How about I just retire and hang up the black boots. NO f**kING WAY. Chris you had this win and that makes it all square. That means some where down the line we will meet again. Until then this is over. No more Canadian Perfect vs La Envidia Mata until the time is right which should probably be in the 4 Winds Tournament. La Envidia Mata's anger seems to be dissapearing rapidly that is until La Envidia Mata's friend DVD shows up in the room. In his hand he is carrying a tape. He takes out the Canadian Perfect tape and slips in the tape. After pressing play La Envidia Mata looks at the TV screen to see a shot of Fnord. La Envidia Mata: What the hell is this. Why does this son of a b!tch interest me. DVD: Well answer me this. What would a 4WW PPV be without La Envidia Mata? La Envidia Mata: The way things are right now, I'd say it would be a better show then it would be with me on the team. DVD: Forget it man. You got yourself matches at Maelstrom and in the 4 Winds Tournament you may take on that jackass right there (pointing at Fnord on the TV) La Envidia Mata: What? Ah man this is bad. I've done nothing since arriving here man. NOTHING. Then they give me this piece of sh*t Fnord in a possible Tournament match at the PPV. DVD: Man I swear to god you either shut up or I'm gonna slap you upside the head. La Envidia Mata : Try it and you won't be having any more matches in any federation. Anyway watch the tape. La Envidia Mata and DVD begin watching the tape. After a long wait La Envidia Mata bursts out into laughter causing DVD and the camera crew to laugh too. La Envidia Mata: Man this guy is still rambling on about nothing. What is he talking about? There is nothing between him and myself. One match, a tournament match and afterwards everything will be forgotten. This guy needs serious help. On your way to Superstardom? Man shut the hell up. You aren't going no-where. You have the West Wind championship...Hold on, DVD, is this a title match. DVD: No, unless you meet in the finals of the tournament. La Envidia Mata : When I get my chance you won't have that belt. So what if I win 2 titles at Maelstrom. I will be given my chances and I won't blow them. You Fnord are on a lucky streak right now. That's exactly what it is. Luck. You see right now I'm having no luck. Excuse me, but to me your sounding like a winy 13 year old kid that just can't lose his virginity to the girl next door. Rambling on about nothing. Fnord I'm 100% sure you need help. I mean I should of quit while I was ahead in the game. For your stinking mind I'm only 22 years old. Which means I got atleast another 20 years in me to show people like you what I'm made of. Fnord call me what you will but names don't hurt me bro. Show me what your capable of one on one if we meet at Maelstrom. Lets see what you can do one on one b!tch. I don't like you and I don't care about you in anyway. So watch your mouth when talking about the God of Wrestling boy. I hope that you come near death on this match so I can prove to you that my start here in 4WW is the best one in wrestling history. La Envidia Mata's run of un-fortune is about to be changed and I'm about to BLOW UP one more f*cking time because in this ring I am God and I have no equal. Why? Because La Envidia Mata. I'll be seeing you around West Wind champ. La Envidia Mata turns his head away from the camera as we cut to commercial.
