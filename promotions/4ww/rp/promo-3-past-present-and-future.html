Promo 3: Past, Present and Future Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #383 Date: 11/12/03 Location: Read to Find out The truth is often hidden from our sight. There was a time, long ago when God had sent forth a weapon, a tool of destruction that would bring death to even the most pungent of sinners. This weapon was used to bring forth justice and truth, this weapon was a man. Though the bloodline had remained a secret, every centenial a new child would be born unto the world. This child would grow, die and be ressurected, upon the return the child would be awarded with imense power and invunerability. Legend has it, when people die they are visited by an angel, one who gives them their life story before taking their life itself. Leaving an impression, it has been believed that the face of this Angel, was the last thing man would see before they die. Then again, hersay could be exactly that and the world could never knoe what really lies out there, waiting for our ending days... [The Vatican - 21:36 - 31/10/66] At first there is only nothingness. Just as you are sure the sun will rise in the morning it shall set in the evening, darkness would have it's time to consume the world. Though, man as convential and indivually wise as they are, had managed to fid a way through the darkness. At the instant touch of a swith, darkness had depleted and light had been restored. The light found it's way to the deepest and farthest corners of the room, taking it's time to shine brightly of the gold trim along the bas of the floor and the top of edge of the ceiling. To the left was a large window with red flowing, silk drapes enticing the floor. The other side had a rustic antique oval table, furnished in the early 19th century, restored and home to the most powerful bodies of the Catholic empire. Though, ahead of the table only a few steps stood the most valliant of seats. A tall oak chair, lined in gold, trimmed in jewels, carved with religious depictions and seat to the pope himself. Soon after the room is illuminated the squeaking sound of the large wood and brass door is heard. The familiar sound of hollow steps tapping off the hardwood floor could only signify that someone was on their way. Though empty now, this room had been home to many meetings of the secretive type. Within the confines of these walls the world was kept from the most desperate and even greatest of church wisdom. If the walls could breathe, with their life giving breath they would begin to share a millenia of desires and secrets. Could it be time for another of those meetings, would we find out first hand the kind of knowldege that slips into this room and then stays burried there for another millenia. The church had often made the decision to keep this coming knowledge from the public for their own safety. This room would be filled with men of the upmost importance and they would detail beliefs such as judgement day and the second coming of Christ. Though they could be certain of the knowledge they possessed these men felt it would be in the best interest of the general public not to know, to avoid a mass histeria. Yet, now the door swings wide open and the footsteps have gotten much louder. 'It's been here all this time,' the voice is soon after matched with a face, Cardinal Delucia. 'Under our very noses.' The Cardinal enters the room and place a rectangular object onto the table. He cautiously steps back from the box and looks back over his shoulder. His eyes shed his years of knowledge, his face though wrinkled still preserved and dignified. Solemnly approaching the table from Delucia's left is another man. Drapped in a long white robe, complete with a tall white hat and all lined with gold material, a young man stands there confidently. His eyes shed impatience and a story ambition, he turns to Delucia and smiles. To most a smile as this would suffice, but Deluica had always know Cardinal Angelini to be deceiving. His return look responding to Angelini's trademark grin was met with some hostility. Delucia runs his finger down the grooves of the cheast, he pauses at the place that can only be assumed to be a lock. After a momment of thought he steps back and just looks at the box. 'Are you going to open it?' Cardinal Angelini finally asked in a smug tone. Delucia turns to his colleague and shakes his head, 'it wouldn't be my place, how do we know it's safe?' 'It's nothing more than paper old friend, no harm can come from that.' Angelini passes a small brass metal jagged object. 'The key, it unlocks a world of unknown, it brings light to things we could only speculate, open the cheast Cardinal.' Spinning away from Angelini, Delucia places his finger on the box. 'No harm can come from the paper itself, but what of the knowledge held within this cheasts confines? Is knowledge harmful?' Delucia turns sharply back to Angelini who is now within a foot of the box. 'In the wrong hands, yes.' Angelini must have caught the look eminated from the elder Cardinal's eyes because he took another step back, 'that is why we mustn't open it without proper documentation and representation present.' Delucia hoists the box under his arm and paces quickly towards a small picture on the wall, he pushes it to the left and reveals a small safe. Within a momment the cheast is locked into the small vault. Angelini walks towards Delucia, his smiled faded. 'Fair enough, you are as wise as you are holy.' Before Angelini is capable of finishing his remarks, that hollow sound taps in the air. Delucia returns the picture to it's proper alignment and staggers back with a bewildered look on his face. Anticipating only the worst Angilini moves towards his 'old friend' and whispers discreetly. Within a momment there is yet another figure standing below the great arches of the room. Though less startled by the person than they originally had expected, Cardinal Angelini brushes past Delucia and makes a hastey exit. He whisks by the figure waiting patiently at the door. His black robe, white collar and lack of importance gave Angelini his justification to simply push the man away before taking his leave. Delucia extends his hand ans smiles. 'Pleasure to see you Father Dimiscus.' Dimiscus, a young priest fresh out of the studies of his life work had sought the attention of Delucia. Dimiscus himself had prided himsefl into the work of the unknown scriptures. There had been rumours circulating that when the bible was written many people had torn out pages or stole works that belonged in it, these scriptures are what had lured Dimiscus into the priesthood. His education was not cheap and his attempt to be the best begain here, and now, in this room filled with secret. As he had first stepped into the room, he taste the thickness of the air, hot and damp. Dimiscus himself had only just begun the journey he was about to undertake and soon his life would change forever. For now, his mind was rested squarely of learning the truth. His studies had lead him to belief that one of the nine missing scriptures taken from the bible was within the confines of the church walls, here in the Vatican. Dimiscus had begun to take a curiousity in the missing scriptures when a large controversy had errupted in class on their realness. Many people denied the existence of such scrolls, even Dimiscus' very own teacher, Cardinal Delucia. 'Cardinal, is it true?' Dimiscus peers into the eyes of the old mentor, 'Had I located the cheast which may contain a long lost biblical scripture?' Cardinal Delucia lets out a deep sigh and begins, 'It is true young lad, your knowledge has helped isolate and safeguard the deadly knowledge that lies within that cheast.' Delucia smiles at Dimiscus, 'I know you have begun to devote yourself to the church and these scriptures only intrigue, that you will grow out of. I want you to understand, these words were removed from the book of God for a reason, information like this could mean the unravelling of mankind. Can you keep this secret?' Put instantly on the spot, Dimiscus raises his eyes to meet those of the Cardinal, 'Yes your holiness.' 'Very well.' Dellucia begins to explain what kind of speculations and secrets that could be inside that box. Knowledge from the day of judgement to God's own word stating that church is a home in your heart, Dimiscus could only be intrigued by these stories. He listened intentively as Delucia laid it all out for him. After several moments the two had found themselves not alone and they had been honoured with the pressence of his Holiness, the Pope. Delucian and Dimiscus both fall to a knee, and each take the right hand and kiss the large ring on his finger. The pope places his hand on each of the men's heads and spouts a short prayer. 'We've found it your Holiness.' Delucia speaks out. The pope doesn't speak, instead he takes a seat on the large chair and his right hand man leans in to hear his words. 'His Holiness applauds you for your efforts and hard work. however, he believes power such as this was sealed for a reason and that reason will not be breached. Where is the cheast?' Delucia points to the picture on the wall, 'In safe keeping just as I knew you would want it.' The right hand man of the pope turns his attention to Dimiscus, 'what of you? What is it that you have to do with this issue? It is a sin to be in this room unless summoned by one who sits at these seats.' The man points ominously at the oval table. 'I summoned him,' Delucia says instantly, though the truth is, he hadn't, 'it was Dimiscus' interest and wisdom that brought us to the location of the box.' 'Your holiness, in my studies I happened to learn the location of this scripture and I am lead to believe that it was actually stollen by the holy church to keep it's contents a secret from the world. I believe that information of such importance as being missing pages from our holy book, belongs to the world, it belongs to the people.' Both the Pope and Delucia shoot an are you crazy look directly at the young priest and Delucia speaks, 'You have to understand, these pages, if that's what they truly are, could contain information so vital yet so alarming that the world would not know how to handle it. People as individuals are smart however, humankind itself is panicy and alot less intellegent. Forget you ever knew the truth, go back to your studies and become a fine priest.' [Honolulu, Hawaii - 18:47 - 10/12/03] 'Is there something bothering you Darek?' 'Nothing really, just that dream I have been having, I cannot seem to understand the message it wants me to spread.' Darek stops and looks at Amanda, 'all I can think about is Father Dimiscus.' 'How could you not think about the man? He treated you as if you were his own son, he loved you Darek.' She runs her fingers through Darek's spiked hair, 'I know his death still haunts you to this day, but try to let it go. It is for the best.' Darek looks out of the window to his left, everything seems so small, the world seems so insignificatn. He turns abruptly back to Amanda, the one person he knows he could love, and the one person he had become a part of. When Dimiscus was killed in a tragic church bombing, Darek knew his lif was going to change forever. He longed to feel the touch of Dimiscus elderly face, he wanted to hear again the words of utter wisdom, he lived only to serve the memory of his fallen mentor. Darek had placed Dimiscus' death squarely onto his shoulders, an ongoing fued with a man of the Confederate Army, a man the world called Major Payne, had hoped to destroy the home of Dark Angel and instead he took the life Dark Angel's only mentor. This would forever be the secret Darek kept locked in this heart, he could have stoppe Payne, had he only known. As Dimiscus lay on his death bed, Darek knew it would be his face that Dimiscus saw shortly after his life flashed before his eyes and Darek's face was indeed the last thing Dimiscus saw, it left the biggest grin on his face. Dimiscus had raised Darek since finding out the truth and Darek had become quite the passionate follower of God until the day that Dimiscus' life ended in Darek's arms. No matter what he tried to do, Darek could never place his love into a false God again, never would he allow someone to manipulate his moves for their will, instead he rebelled and proved to many, that beings created and put on earth could become as powerful as God himself. Darek leans back into his chair and closes his eyes. Soon after darkness takes over his dreams and were are whisked away into a world that only can be found in the Dark Angel's mind... [The Vatican - 02:16 - 01/11/66] All is still and the world is at peace. Yet, something can be heard. The quickening pace of a faint thud can be drawn into our minds. Darkness only covers the land and it is difficult to place a finger on the location of the ever quickening thud, thud, thud. Through the darkness we begin to seek out the source of the dull sound, it beats in perfect rhythm and never fails to continue in the pattern it had begun. Through the blackness there is a thin line of white light illuminating a cheast on a large table. A hand reaches towards the cheast and places a small brass key into the carved slot. The thudding had become so much louder as we draw closer and closer to the impending doom. Ba-Thump, pause, ba-thump, pause and continuing in an endless pattern it is the fear that resides within the heart of the man who turns the brass key to the right unlocking the large gold clasp on the jewelled box. Soon after another light flickers on and shines right into the unexpecting eyes of Cardinal Angelini who drops the key and steps back from the box in shock. 'Is that you?' He calls out. No response, instead the light leaves the face of Angelini and turns to the opened cheast on the table. A few hollow steps can be heard as the light is fixated on the contents of the case. The man holding the light walks quickly over to the table and places his light down. 'Yes, it's me.' 'Good, you're just in time.' Angelini reaches into the cheast and pulls out several old papers with ancient arabaic writing on them. 'I've been waiting for this, we all deserve to know the truth,' Cardinal Angelini points his light into the eyes of Father Dimiscus. 'Are you sure you want to know the truth?' Angelini studies the inscriptions on the papers and a sick grin flickers across his face. 'My God, so it is true.' 'What is?' Dimiscus tries to catch a glimpse of the papers. 'The myth, the legend, the folklore, it's all true according to this and...' Angelini stops with a lock of shock smoldered on his face. '...it's already begun to happen again.' 'What has?' 'The return of God's only weapon, Dimiscus, take this information as you want, but there IS an Angel of Death.' Both men pause after those words are uttered. Angelini continues read, 'it says here, a boy born to a woman who has not known the touch of man, oh, this sounds vaguly familiar,' he continues on, 'will live to be nine years of age when an act of God shall take his life. It also says the child will make a return on Easter just as Jesus was ressurected. The Angel was sent forth in the 17th century, the year 1666 to be exact, a year rumoured to be called the year of witches. God wanted to purify the world and placed a being of extraordinary power onto earth to govern the deaths of the wicked.' Dimiscus can hardly begin to formulate the words he wanted to say, 'what else?' 'To ensure the Angel of Death cannot bring forth mass destruction he can only be given the duty of Angel of Death for one hundred years.' Angelini looks at Dimiscus, 'meaning, if this is true, this year a child will become the Angel of Death. Dimiscus, we cannot let anyone know this truth. If this information was in the wrong hands, the world would face a hundred years of absolute darkness and terror.' 'What do you suggest we do?' Dimiscus asks. 'We need to find this child, I will search this area, keeping the others away from this truth. You, you must go to North America and begin a search there. Noone can know this truth.' '...if we find him?' 'Kill him.' Dimiscus looks into the eyes of Angelini, 'you cannot be serious.' 'Imagine mass death, destruction and slaughter unleashed into an unsuspected world, what would you do to stop it?' '...teach him the ways of God, teach him to love...' [Honolulu, Hawaii - 18:55 - 10/12/03] The roaring sound of the engine is enough to keep anyone awake, but Darek had already had problems sleeping. Tossing to the right this time, he bumps into Amanda. She places her hand on his shoulder and shakes Darek for a moment before his eye lids shoot upward revealling his ice blue eyes. Darek take this as an opportunity to regain awareness of his surroundings. Glancing to his right, his eyes are treated with the sight of true beauty and upon peering to the left, the tiny world begins to become much bigger. His thoughts are interupted when a 'bing' sound is heard followed by the crimson red seatbelt light illuminating overhead. The engine sound is soon dulled by the static of the intercom. 'May I have your attention please, this is your captain speaking. Honolulu is approximately five minutes away, please fasten your seat belts and prepare for landing. It's 32 degrees celcius and an absolutley beautiful evening, thank you for flying Air Canada flight 1966 to Hawaii.' Darek reaches over and clasps the seat belt of his love, closed. He then turns his attention to the loving smile planted on her face. He instinctively clasps the belt around his own waist closed and leans back lost in her eyes. 'We're almost there.' 'Do you think you can handle this?' 'Handle what?' 'The new federation, the thousands, no millions of people who have no faith in what you say you are. Are you prepared to be the unknown soldier in the 4ww?' He turns to Amanda and kisses her gentley on the cheek, 'I fear for any being who is foolish enough to stand in my... no our way. You have seen what happens to those who challenge my throne. It is the fallen who make my dynasty so much stronger, it is their bones and blood that will define my legacy. I may win and I may lose, but in the end, all will be judged by my righteous hand.' Amanda rests her head on Darek's shoulder, 'you know what?' The doors open and people collect their belongings and exit the plane. [St. Basil's Church, Calgary Alberta - 15:22 - 05/11/66] 'Father Dimiscus?' 'Yes' 'It's a pleasure, sir.' 'Thank you.' 'Are you ready to see your new church?' Dimiscus steps under the larch archways of the holy catholic church, his slate wiped morally clean as he does so. The church is noticabley smaller and not nearly as filled with riches. Dimiscus walks into the church, standing in the lobby he examines the crystal bowls containing holy water. However his eyes are dragged to the large crucifix hanging above the altar. He studies the cross and then the large stain glass windows lining both the left and right sides of the ceiling.
