When in Rome...

23 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When in Rome... Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1182 Date: 2/23/05 Location: Rome, Italy [The camera fades into the cheap motel room Rustin Slade rented in Italy. Cheap is an understatement, however, and it would be an improvement from what Rustin has chosen to hole up in. The crudely arranged bed seems to be nothing more than a gym mat covered with a bed sheet on top of a coffee table. There is no TV in the room, and the camera picks up slight movement on the floor of something scurrying underneath the “bed.” A cockroach, no doubt.] [It’s been two days since Rustin has won the 4WW West Wind Title and he couldn’t be happier. He should be happy, right? As the camera zooms in on Rustin we see, however, anything but a smile spreading across his lips. The 4WW title is in view also, sitting next to Rustin on top of the “bed.” He gently strokes the gold with one hand as he ruffles his hair with his other. As his hand comes down from his head, Rustin grabs something from behind him. As it becomes more visible, we see it’s a dart. Rustin throws the dart against the wall with such force that the only light fixture in the room seems to be ready to fall down in the middle of the floor. The camera falls to where the dart has stuck and we see that there’s a calendar on the wall. The camera zooms onto the date. February 14th. Valentine’s Day.] Rustin: (sarcastically) Well isn’t this great? Two weeks in Rome, and I don’t have anyone to spend it with. Sure I could call up the other guys, but hell they got their own lives. Darek, Paul, Severed, and Rob all got stuff going on. [Rustin picks up another dart behind him and throws it again, hitting February 14th again.] Rustin: Rome, Italy, the city of love. Even the city name itself seems to be mocking me. Rome- Romance. Coincidence? I think not. Love. I’m beginning to think it’s more and more of a joke everyday. Hey do you know what love spelled backwards is? E-V-O-L. Evil. Pure evil. Granted they’re spelled differently, but you get the idea. [Rustin looks back down at his title, gives it another loving pat and looks forward at the all again.] Rustin: (Talking to the belt) Has it been two days already little guy? It still all seems so surreal. Everyone was so happy for me. Mom, Dad, Rex, all my fans, and even Chastity. [Rustin smiles thinking back to the hug he received from Chastity.] Rustin: She willingly hugged me! I didn’t have to hold her at gunpoint, tell her the boogeyman was behind her, or trick her with an arm raise. [Rustin shakes his head, getting serious again and throws another dart he produces into the calendar wall with February 14th circled.] Rustin: But damn it! She’s given me more trouble than benefits lately. This Lincoln Frink guy is bad news. I don’t know what this guy’s deal is, but he’s got some major issues. When I was trying to get a girl to notice me, I didn’t beat the hell out of someone she liked. Call me crazy, but that wasn’t the best way to get her to fall head and heels in love with you. But Christ, what do I know? I haven’t had a girlfriend. EVER! But I’m sure if I was able to get one, giving one of her friends a black eye and a split lip wouldn’t have gotten me a romp around in her underwear. [Rustin looks back down at the West Wind Title, smiling at it.] Rustin: That was a hell of a match though. Who knew that we’d go that long. Zane Ozz surprised me, and I hoped I surprised him a little also. The Legends are here to stay fellas. Take note. “The Dark Angel” Darek DeAngelo, YTO Rob Givens, Paul “Done Deal” Williams, Severed, and myself. Who could ask for a greater group of guys? Darek’s guidance, Rob’s tutelage, Paul’s crowd skills, and Severed’s....well Severed’s mutilation I suppose is a little strange, but I’m impressed with what I saw from the main event. [Rustin gets up off his bed for a moment and walks over to the motel room’s mirror, half shattered.] Rustin: And what about me? What do I bring to the Legends? [Rustin looks back at the title, making sure it’s still there.] Rustin: Well I hope I bring more than my own misfortunes and troubles. At least now I can show for my ring work. I’ve got that little trinket over there to show proof of my abilities. [Rustin thinks back again to the raising of the title and the hug from Chastity, and then the aftermath brought on by Private First Class Lincoln Frink.] Rustin: Damn him! We were fine. Me and Chastity were having a great time. We actually celebrated! TOGETHER! Then he had to offer a match with her as the bait. I only have two weeks. It’s tempting. [Rustin seems to contemplate the ideas a little further.] But I can’t! I don’t even know what Chastity thinks. It’s wrong to gamble with her. To rid myself and her of Lincoln Frink though is one hell of a reward. [Rustin looks back into the mirror. From where Rustin is standing now, the cracked view of the mirror only reflects half of Rustin.] Rustin: I’m torn between my own morals and my desires. The thought of those two together though makes me sicker than looking underneath my bed in there to see what ungodly insects may be there. [Rustin shakes his head again and walks over to where he has the calendar and tears it down. He rips out the month of February and crumples it onto the floor.] Rustin: She’s probably hanging out with him tonight. It is Valentine’s Day. Only once did I ever get a Valentine. It was one of those “everyone gets one” sort of grade school games. Well can you imagine what mine was? I received it from one of the girls I had a monster crush on. If my anatomy was capable of working at that age, you can surely bet that every time that girl entered the room, a certain part would always show it’s appreciation. I would of gladly skipped Mrs. Trumbull’s lectures about the Civil War if I could skip out into the hallway with Trudy Baxter. [Rustin thinks back trying to get a visual of Trudy. He clicks his tongue as his eyes move about. He then pauses and smiles.] Rustin: Ah there she is! [Rustin’s hand instinctively moves down towards his groin region, but he catches himself and gives himself a hard slap on the wrist.] Rustin: (Imitating an old woman’s voice) Mr. Slade! I asked you a question. What do you think you’re doing? Rustin: I was just giving my Billy a Yank Mrs. Trumbull. You get it? Billy Yank was a name for a Union soldier. Rustin: (Imitating Mrs. Trumbull) You filthy little boy. Down to the principal’s office Mr. Slade! [Rustin scratches his head, and chuckles a little bit to himself.] Rustin: I was so happy when I got that valentine from Trudy. That is, until I opened it. Dear Rustin, Roses are Red, Violets are Blue, You’ll never get me into bed, So keep your monkey in the zoo! Damn that was mean! Clever, but mean. Oh well, Trudy isn’t half of what Chastity is anyway. [Rustin chides himself for thinking about Chastity again.] Rustin: Damn you Rustin! You gotta focus! You’ll be facing Syckle next week. It’s going to be a tough match! [Rustin picks up the calendar page and puts it back up onto the wall. Rustin goes back to sit down onto his bed and picks up a dart, and holds it between his fingers and twirls it a little, allowing the point to spin just a tad bit into his finger. He aims and lets the dart fly, this time the date being hit is February 26, 2005.] Rustin: Bullseye! That’s right Syckle. You and me. This will be the first time I have the pleasure of meeting you inside the squared circle, but let me tell you this, that title match opened my eyes. I went into that match more focused than I ever was before. The support of the crowd, my parents watching me, Chastity being in the ring. It fueled my energy. It may not have been my best match, but it got me out of a slump. I’m not prepared to go back into a slump. You want to take the competition to a higher level, I’m all for it. Competition is what this is all about. Respect is what I hope to gain. Blindfolded (under breath) pardon the pun, by glory and the title is not going to be an issue next week. I’m ready for you Willy ol’ boy. Just be thanking your lucky charms that this is a non-title match and we’re not taking it to that level yet. You bring the Dark Horse, Syckle, and I’ll bring the bridle! Saddle up Will, and enjoy the ride! [There’s a knock on the door of the cheap motel room. Rustin sees a card slip underneath the door. Rustin walks over, watching his step as two more cockroaches scurry underneath the bed. He kneels down at picks it up. There is a heart with a doily attached around it. Rustin turns it over, smiling, but his smile quickly fades when he starts to read it.] Occupant of #13, Roses are Red, Violets are Blue, Stiff us the room’s rent again, And the police will be coming for you! P.S. Happy Valentine’s Day! [Rustin crumples up the Valentine’s Day card and throws it on the floor.] Rustin: I hate Valentine’s Day! [The scene fades to black as we see two cockroaches pushing the crumpled up card on the floor underneath the bed.]
