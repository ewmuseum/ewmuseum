The proverbial darkness falls we are left alone to reminisce all things that have come and all things that will come in the future. There are paths in our past that we wish we could derail and change all together; however that is not the case. We must accept those wasted years and live on trying to be their dreams. When we challenge reality and live for the moment, living for the dreams that keep that fire burning inside of them. This fire can be used for the bettering of themselves, or that fire can go on unharnessed ready to destroy all that comes in contact with it. Before we continue on let us look back to El Lobo and expose his true reality.~ {The scene opens up many years before as we see a young man running across a deserted road with a brown sack over his back full of belongings. He continues to run fast as he crosses the road. He stops and takes a long hard look back.} Kid: (translated) Good bye Mexico, you have taught me well. But it is time that I do things my way. {The kid begins to walk towards a new beginning as the scene fast forwards a few years. We see that same young man grown up a bit, he walks in the back of an alley preparing for something. As he steps into the light we see his fists taped ready to fight.} Kid: Esse this will be your last chance to walk away holmes… {The two begin to fight as the young man is beaten unmercifully, however he makes a huge comeback to gain the victory. As he slides across a brick bulding with blood dripping we fast forward a few more years as we see the same young man a bit older and a bit wiser walking through a white hallway. As he continues to walk we hear a familiar wolf howl followed by Break Stuff by Limp Bizkit. The man runs out to the ring and right before our eyes we see a match. As usual the young man is being beaten unmercifully, the opponent drags him to the back by his hair. He signals for a powerbomb onto the table, when he lifts him in the air the young man reverses it with a hurricanrana through the table. He rolls over for the pin and gaining an upset victory over the heavyweight champion.} {The scene fast forwards a few more years as we see the man grown up in the bright lights of stardom. He is inside another ring with another large man. The man charges and he leaps up and dropkicks him to the back of the head. He turns around and roll him up for an upset victory. Every one storms the ring as he holds the World Title high above his head. Tears stream down his face as a dream is starting to become a reality.}
