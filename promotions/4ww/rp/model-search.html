Model Search

24 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Model Search 4WW Exposures #1148 Date: 1/24/05 Location: German Auditorium (The scene opens up in an auditorium type of a place. In the front row there is a table set up. Sitting at the table is Luscious Lady Luck and some unknown man. The man is standing behind a camera, filming whatever is going on. LLL is wearing a red dress, a fairly expensive one. She is looking over some applications and pictures of different women. She lets them go, resting her head in her hands.) LLL: This is ridiculous. No one we’ve seen this far has the ability to work within the modeling world, let alone work for me. Oh well, I guess we’ll keep going. NEXT!!!! (Out walks a petite brunette. She is wearing a white pushup bra and bikini panties. She steps up to the middle of the stage, twirls, and then stands there.) LLL: Name? Girl: (In a German accent) Wanda LLL: How long have you been modeling? Wanda: About 4 years. LLL: What types of things have you modeled? Wanda: Everything, but mostly dresses. LLL: What makes you think you have what it takes to work for my modeling agency? Wanda: Because I have the package every guy wants. LLL: Yeah, you and every other woman. You’re really common amongst women. Like a cardboard cutout. Wanda: Just give me a shot. LLL: We’ll let you know. NEXT!!!!! (Wanda walks off the stage, head down, and a look of disappointment. Out walks a blonde. A relatively busty blonde wearing a baby blue bra and thong. She strolls out to the center of the stage, twirls, and stands there.) LLL: Name? Girl: (In a French accent) FiFi. LLL: Interesting name, come from anywhere? FiFi: Yeah, my parents. Duh. LLL: Yeah, OK, um, how long have you been modeling? FiFi: 10 years. LLL: What types of things do you model? FiFi: I do a lot of nude modeling. LLL: And how will that help you with a modeling agency? This isn’t Playboy. FiFi: Then why am I standing here in panties and bra? LLL: Cause I asked you too. FiFi: And who are you? LLL: You know what, never mind. I’m done with this interview. You go away now. The door is over there. Just open it, and walk out. Or even better, just bash your head into it so that maybe you’ll have some intelligence. (The blonde, looking quizzically, walks away.) LLL: You know I can’t handle this right now. I’ve got Ukyo to worry about on Stormfront. Patti and Caitlin are off trying to model, even though they both know they are nothing without me. At least I have a wonderful husband, even if I didn’t ask for it. Damn it. How did that all go wrong? Whatever…… I’ve got to find some new talent. I can’t worry about Ukyo or Patti or anyone. My clients are looking for someone new. I’ve got to find that someone new. NEXT!!!!! (The camera pans in on LLL’s face. Looking at the curtain, just watching for the next applicant. All of a sudden, LLL’s eyes light up and she jaw drops.) LLL: Raul, cancel auditions. I’ve found my next model. Call all the girls back, or hell, if you want, finish interviewing them, but I have my next star right here. (LLL stands up and walks toward the stage, after throwing all the papers down. The scene fades to black.)
