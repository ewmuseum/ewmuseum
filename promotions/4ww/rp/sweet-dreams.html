Sweet Dreams Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #729 Date: Location: SWEET DREAMS SWEET DREAMS 'Immortal' Dark Angel - 03/06/04 MIND GAMES?! _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ Slowly her body pressed up against his and in that instant his body went numb. He didn't know if he had loved her or if he just wanted to save her. Somehow, in this moment, when they touched his mind made him believe it was love. The warmth of her touch, the strength yet delicate clutching of his hair, the soft, luscious lips running carefully over his. Ravished at the thought of this touch becoming his, his mind plays the scene back and forth, over and over again. He could only invision such an encounter in a dream, but there they were, in the ring together with the spotlights shining down heavily... The stares that are seen on their faces send the fans into choruses of boos and cheers, as they aren't sure exactly what goes on. Patti's hazel eyes never leaving the eyes of Dark Angel as if trying to prove to him that she is not afraid or intimidated by him. It was almost as if they were drawn together for that moment, for this one point in time, an incident that will stand as the ground for a new future. Patti's actions, her movements, her touch, it all indicated that the game had either gone too far or there was really a connection between the two. Darek had hoped that Patti was extending her hand, pleading for help; one is desperate need of a knight. He knew that given the chance he could be the one to destroy the evil that had begun to spread through her like a great plague, just as it had taken the others. When he reached down and pulled the plug from the wall and Amanda had been released from her suffering, Darek vowed to make his life worthy once again. He would set out to use his guile, strength, and ability amoung other attributes, to suceed in his mission, his passion. The zipper slowly works its way down revealing an ample view of her cleavage, stuck neatly in her cleavage rested a single purple rose that hadn't bloomed yet. Her hand gently reaches inward and pulls out the rose. A guesture, a gift, or perhaps the plot of a maniacal and yet insanely brilliant villian, Tarrant. Darek had known that the mind of Patti was not true at the present time, nor did he care. He, just as she, was entirely wrapped up in the moment. The instant she revealled a rose, Darek had begun to feel slightly queezy. His body losing it's entire feelig. As if a flood gate had been opened, his blood preasure began to rise through the roof. Beatting away, hard, fast and strong, the object many thought he was incapable of holding, a heart. Small shivers quickly swirl and spread across his body. He longed for the rose to be a guesture of gratitude, of acceptance but deep inside his mind, he had wondered just exactly Patti's intent was. She brings it up to her lips as if giving it a soft kiss. The hand holding the rose slowly moves from her lips, along the side of her face, almost as if she's allowing it to caress her face. Her eyes never leaving his face, a slow smile appears on Patti's face as her other hand reaches up and softly traces the exact same path along DA's face, bringing a half smile to his face. He must have watched the video a hundred times over by now. His mind simply played it all back to him as if it had just happened. He was captivated, taken away when the warmth of her touch had pressed against his skin in peace rather than war. As she pulled her fingers along his face, he could remember closing his eyes and wanting the world to stop. He wanted to hold onto this moment forever, there would always be an offer to be a champion, somewhere, but the genuine touch of our champion was enough to make all the sacrifices worth while. The obsession with defining the best by claiming as many championships as possible had once taken hold of Darek. He too was a blood thirsty tyrant that had whatever it took and by any means necessary to be victorious. The winds had changed in his life, priorities had taken shape, a goal had been set and previous dellusions of false worth seemed to disappear. He finally believed he had matured from the careless powerhouse he once was... He wouldn't let it all on the line, or would he? Suddenly Patti grabs DA by his hair and forces his face closer to hers. The rosebud that is in her other hand magically opens to reveal a purple rose, which she places behind his ear. After toying with it for a brief moment, she has it in place. Intoxicated by her presence, Darek had frozen completely. When death draws near, your life flashes before your eyes, as a reminder of what had happened to you, up to that point in life. Darek knew this would be one of those memories that would come back to either haunt him or put a smile on his face... Her hand returns to her side, as she leans in, whispers something into his ear, then brushes her lips quickly over his. She steps back from him, and without taking her eyes from his, slowly walks up the ramp, quickly disappearing from sight. When her lips had touched his, Darek's life did flash before his eyes. He did not understand why, but for what seemed like an eternity as the warmth of her lip covered his, he remembered his life. His head had been filled with words from the mouth of his dear Patti. Echoing through his mind, Darek could hardly begin to move. Finally, when Patti's eyes had released their tormenting hold upon him, he snapped to and realised just exactly what she had said to him. After StormFront had come and gone, Darek had done what he said he would do, he defeated Jerms. He defeated Jerms with the help of Patti Anderson... Infact, had she not been present, would the outcome had been different? Perhaps Jerms would be four over Darek rather than two. Darek tried not to concern his mind in the trivial thoughts of mere wins and loses, yet he seemed particularly troubled by his outcome. THE SOUTH WIND TITLE _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ 'Why?' 'I had to.' 'Why?' 'I cannot explain.' 'Why?' 'I had my reasons, you have to understand...' 'Why?' 'You weren't you!' 'Why?' 'GOD DAMMIT! PLEASE STOP!' 'Why?' 'Please?' 'Why?' Darek's head snaps forward and a hard cold sweat pours down his forhead. He is breathing rather heavily and manages to turn his head to the phone, which had brought him from his deep trance. He could remember Amanda and Xantos standing togetherm hand and hand, wondering, asking, they had died to know... Why? Darek sends his right hand over to the nightstand at the side of his bed and grabs the phone. He lifts it slowly and brings it near his ear. He tried to explain himself, he thought they would understand now that they had gone on. Darek had prayed the day Amanda died and again the day Xantos died. He felt his connection to God would never be the same, but he also knew that he owed it to them, he had to have them welcomed into a 'better' place. After a few seconds of fumbling the phone, Darek places it against the side of his head. 'Hello?' He asks groggily. A couple seconds pass before Darek begins to nod his head. 'Yeah,' he switches the phone quickly to his left ear as he begins to write down some information, 'no worries.' He nods again and a slight smile cracks around the corner of his lips. 'Chris Sanchz?! You've got to be fucking kidding me,' a pause, 'Right?' Silence consumes the darkness and Darek sits there listening, not hearing what he had wanted. 'Look, I beat Jerms, 'The Technical Spectacle' that should put me at the top of the list.' Again silence sweeps away the voice of the Angel. 'This is like a goddamn joke,' he takes a second to absorb the information, 'What you're saying is this-' He gets interupted for the time being. 'Chris Sanchez the South Wind Champion, versus the Angel?' He smiles brighter and stops, 'let me ask you something. Do you not value the life of your roster?' Returning the blcack handset to his right ear, he tilts his head slightly using it and his shoulder to hold the phone up. 'The South Wind Championship.' Darek's smile fades and a look of intrigue crosses his brow. 'Well they do call it the Nightmare title for a reason-' Again Darek is quickly interrupted. 'Perhaps it would make sense to call it the KnightMare Brigade title after this weeks StormFront.' The smile returns to Darek's face and this time his eyes fill with some excitement. 'After what he did to me in that Cage match,' he pauses rubbing his chin with the side of his index finger, 'he'll be lucky to survive this onslaught.' The voice on the other end coaxes Darek on. 'As I said, if that's what you want, that's what you've got. I do not run from fights and I can guarantee that Chris Sanchez is going to be in the need of a plastic surgeon after our match is said and done.' A few seconds of nothingness pass. 'Sounds good. Alright Ian, I'll catch you later.' Darek brings his right hand up to his shoulder and he guides the the phone back onto the receiver. With his left hand he begins to rub the sleep from his eyes. Once the hand moves and his vision is cleared, his eyes begin to wander in the direction of his clock. He stares almost painfully at the red LED lights that tell him the time. With his head slowly turning away from the clock he finds hemself starring forward, wondering at the possible intereference into this match. Not only had his dreams been riddled with the images of Xantos and Amanda, but they had become overtaken by those who wished harm or to stop the Angel. Demons like Shoudou, Tarrant, they seemed to call to him in his slumber, yet, ever resilliant he had always tried to free himself from the voices. They had called to him and he did not call back, instead he tactfully has been waiting for his opportunity to unleash his masterpiece into action. His eyes roll closed and he drops his head back once again. It soon finds comfort and relief in the white feathered pillow, behind him. Within a few seconds his body becomes deeply relaxed and his world seems to drift away into nothingness. His fears, his hopes and even his dreams, all that he had thought about became nothing. Here, in sleep the deepest desires of the heart and mind are shown. Darek had always believed that finding the symbolic references in dreams and defining them would help him take the next days steps. He had always believed that dreams were his version of forsight. Though, recently his dreams had become overtaken, perhaps the remoarse for his actions had finally become apparent. He did feel guilty for the actions he had commited yet he also realised that they had to be done. LORD OF ILLUSIONS _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- 'Darek?' The soft voice calls through the darkness. Darek opens his eyes and from his laying position on the bed he tilts his head slightly forward. His ears seems to stand on end, as if they had expected to hear the call again. 'Darek?' The faintly familiar voice becomes slightly louder. 'Please.' Turning his head from side to side, Darek's eyes quicky adapt to the darkness and he can easily see through it. The room is empty except for him, the voic he had heard seemed to have gotten louderm perhaps he was simply losing his mind. The secrets shared, the feelings exposed and the games that have been played would have been more than enough to bring any other being to insanity. Yet, he remained aware, knowing that his mind was simply confused, not crazy. 'Are you there?' She calls again, this time her shadow leans up aganst the doorframe of Darek's room. 'I need your help Darek.' His ears seem to shoot up, picking up every last word. He reaches over to the side of his bed and flips a switch. A tinge of red begins to faintly illuminate the dark room. The figure standing at the door presses inside as the light flips on. Darek now pushes himself into a sitting position. The blanket falls off his bare, muscular cheast and into his lap. Squinting slightly and tilting his head, Darek can clearly see whom his visiter is. 'How'd you get in here? Darek asks. 'Or better yet, how'd you know where I lived.' The woman simply takes a few steps closer and then stops, 'I need your help, will you help me?' Bringing his left hand to his face Darek rubs the sleep away from his eyes, his face cracks a half grin. He turns back to where the woman stands, his smile grows a little wider and he stops moving for a second. 'I am in danager,' she spills, 'I need you, I need your help.' Pleading she half turns away from Darek. 'Please?!' Darek pushes himself up off the matress of his bed, the covers fall onto the floor. Darek now standing beside his bed, reaches down and throws the blanket back onto the bed. He is almost entirely nude, except for red silk boxers. He turns gingerly back towards the woman and takes a couple steps in her direction. With desperation writen in her eyes, she half turns back and sees Darek standing just behind her. She crosses her arms across her midsection. The red tinge shines of her face and Darek can only find himself reaching out and placing his thumb under her eye. With a soft, gentle sweeping motion, he soaks up a tear and pulls it off her face. 'I'd do anything you needed me to,' he rubs the tear between his thumb and index finger, turning it into nothing. 'Why now do you need my help?' 'I'm afraid,' she cries. 'Of what?' An eerie moment of silence consumes the dark room. Darek takes another step towards the woman, she does the same. Standing only a two feet apart, Darek extends his arms to the horizon and the woman stares blindly into his eyes as he does so. She takes a half step forward, Darek wiggles his fingers, waiting to once again embrace the one he... loved? obsessed? yearned to protect? He couldn't tell what the feeling was. All he knew was that Patti Anderson was standing before him, tears and all. 'Whatever it is that you're afraid of,' pausing for a second to look into her eyes, 'I'll take care of it.' 'Would you?' she questions, 'really?' Darek just nods his head and smiles. Patti steps forward and then falls into his arms. As her entire body weight comes crashing into him, he doesn't even budge. His feet stay planting firmly to the floor as he closes his arms around Patti. He gentley leans his head up against hers and asks, 'Why are you afraid?' Patti pulls her head off of Darek's shoulder and uses a free hand to wipe away the tears. She smiles brightly at Darek, her lush crimson red lips seem only to entice Darek. He looks deep into her eyes, straight into her soul. He finds a truly remarkable being inside and out, he couldn't begin to comprehend what could make her so afraid, after all, she had stood before some of the greatest of all. Perhaps it was her way of showing that she cared back, it was her way of saying free me? 'I need you to-' She sniffs, 'take care of Chris Sanchez next week.' Of all the unlikely suggestions, this was the one he had least expected to here. 'Why?' 'He's going to hurt me. To get to you. To hurt you.' Darek drops his head onto Patti's shoulder, 'son of a bitch, I'll make him regret ever wanting to become a fighter. This will be one fight he cannot win.' He runs his fingers through her hair and as he does so he knocks down a small purple hair band. Finding his words ultimately comforting, Patt pulls away from Darek and in that instant their eyes meet again. A sparkle flashes off of Patti's eyes and Darek can only smile at her. Their heads tilt closer and closer together, Patti slightly leans to the side just as the two are about to touch lips... '...seven thirty two, a slight overcast with a high of twenty two and an overnight low of six degrees. The weekend is shaping up nicely as well...' Darek's eyes roll open and he stares blankly at his alarm clock: 7:30. He closes his eyes again and shakes his head slightly. Sitting upright he glares around the room looking for any trace of what had happened. He reaches over to his nightstand he flips the switch and the red tinge further illuminates the room as the sun shines brightly between the cracks of the black blinds. Pushing a switch on the side of his alarm clock, he turns out of his bed and plants his feet onto the floor. Rubbing his eyes he tries to adjust to just waking up. He reaches over and opens the blinds slightly, letting some more light into the room. Pushing himself out of bed, Darek walks over to his dresser, as he does so, he stops for a moment. Bending down, Darek grabs something lying on the floor. Closing the object tight into his hand, he continues to the dresser. Dropping a small purple hair elastic onto his dresser, Darek steps back in awe... he had thought it was just a dream. Perhaps it was, maybe it wasn't, he only knew the message was simple: Destroy Chris Sanchez. That was something Darek was more than happy to oblige to. THE FACTS _-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_ 'Excuse me, Dark Angel?' Darek stops and takes a half turn to his left. He looks down and sees Eliza Murphy and a camera man. He smiles brightly and responds, 'what can I do for you?' 'I was wondering, do you have a moment for a few questions?' Darek nods. 'You made your return a few weeks ago, what kind of impact were you looking to create?' A faint smile curls up around his lips. 'Impact, that perhaps could be the most obvious question of all Eliza. Most of these scavengers here flock for one reason, to destroy the champion and take their coveted gold. The impact inwhich I expect to make, keeps that title around Patti Andersons waist for a long time.' Eliza smile back and asks again. 'After failing to protect Patti Anderson from defeat and yourself also losing in the same match up, how did you expect to match up with Jerms in the steel cage?' Darek's smile fades and a look of near anger sweeps across his face. 'How did I expect to match up to Jerms? had beatten me once in singles competition. He and Marvelous had beatten myself and Fnord. Then a couple weeks ago he pinned Patti and I failed to save her from defeat. The truth is, Jerms cannot match up to me. Each of his wins have seen him take any opportunity that may have presented itself... He tried that again last week.' Eliza sesning the anger, tries to divert the conversation away from Jerms. 'Patti Anderson came to your match last week and may have saved you in more ways than one, how do you feel about that?' 'Firstly, I would have beatten Jerms. Though I am grateful for her assistance and evening of the battlefield, she had her own reasons for her actions against Jerms. Secondly, Patti's mind has been consumed by that vile trash that Tarrant seeps from his never shut mouth. He poisons her and this makes her his slave. I find myself torn between wanting to believe she was genuine and wanting to go directly to Tarrant to find out just exactly what, if there are any plans against me.' '...and on last question, This week you are scheduled to face Chris Sanchez the new South Wind Champion, what do you think of this match?' Darek stops for a second. 'Chris Sanchez signed his own death certificate last week when he agreed to accompany Jerms to the ring. Chris Sanchez had gone to far by just showing up with Jerms in that match, but he crossed the line when he and that miscreant friend of his stopped me from scalling that cage and winning the match. Let me tell you something about Chris Sanchez, last week he defeated Bullet for the South Wind Title, then he chose to come to the ring and help Jerms, Chris Sanchez had obviously pissed the Angel off and now Chris Sanchez HAS to pay for his actions. Have you ever seen the South Wind Title have an opportunity to belong to the most dominant being in the Four Winds today? Not once, and now that I will have the opportunity, I will seek my vengence on Chris Sanchez, tarnish his beautiful record, end his streak and show him why they call the South Wind Title, the 'K'nightmare title. If you see that Hollywood reject, you tell him something for me. The Angel never stops hunting until his prey is killed.' Darek turns away from Eliza and the camera man, he walks briskly away and out the doors down the corridor. The sunlight shines briefly into the building before the door closes tightly behind him. ~END~
