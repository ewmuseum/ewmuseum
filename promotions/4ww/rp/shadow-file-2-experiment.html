Shadow File #2 - Experiment Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1142 Date: 1/14/05 Location: |>> The scene opens in the secret Shadow Ninja’s headquarters that’s underneath a local Japanese herbal shop in Kyo’s neighborhood of Santa Monica, the Deathscythe Outlaw walks through a long corridor…he enter the armory. > In the armory, a young Japanese man in a rock punk attire that have wild red hair and blue eyes stand at a table, nod his head to the beat of the music he was listening to through a pair of earphones from a portal CD Player. He works on a special project. > Spud didn’t reply, continue to listen to loud music. > Kyo makes a sound and notice the reason why Spud didn’t reply to his words. He softly sighs and shakes his head in disapproval, approach Spud. Kyo reaches down to the CD Player and cranks up the volume to the maximum level. > Spud approaches a locker, open it and take out a black magnum. He throws it over to Kyo. Kyo catches the gun, examine it carefully. > Kyo looks at Spud curiously. > Kyo holster the gun in his long trench coat, head to the exit. > Kyo looks at Spud curiously. Spud takes out a pair of gauntlets that look exactly like Kyo’s…they have been modified for special use. He places the gauntlets on the nearby table. Kyo approach the table, pick up a gauntlet and examine it. > Kyo takes off his old gauntlets, reveal his right forearm was marked with a black colored dragon and nasty scars. He puts on the new ones. > Kyo puts his trench coat back on after he put on his new gauntlets. > Kyo nods with a look of curiosity. > Spud throws the handle at Kyo. Kyo catches the handle, curiously examines it from all angles. > Kyo shrugs and do what Spud told him, a crimson color beam shoot up from the handle, take form of a blade that can pass for a katana’s. He blinks and looks at the energy beam surprisingly. > Kyo looks at Spud more dully than before. > The energy blade disappears back in the handle. > Kyo looks down at his right forearm, favor it. > Kyo stands at the exit. > Kyo leaves the armory…disappear in a shadowy blur. > In the once warring country of Germany, it’s evening, the Deathscythe Outlaw reappears in front of a rundown apartment in the same way he disappeared. He has a dark green color knapsack over his left shoulder. > Kyo looks at the apartment with smirk. > Kyo walks up the stairs, enter the apartment…there’s a mysterious black car parked a four blocks away from the apartment. > In the apartment, Kyo walks up a flight of an old staircase. He reach the top of the stairs, walk down the long hallway. > Kyo stand before a door with 2B marked on it. > Kyo approaches a nearby dusty wall light, unscrew the glass casing and tilt it, an old key fall onto his hand. He approaches the door after he put casing back on, unlocks the door and enters inside. > In the room, Kyo see that the room has a decent appearance not that dusty. > Kyo walk to a fridge, push it easily to the side, reveals a secret compartment. He kneels down, open it with a hard push and take out a tall bottle of sake. Kyo blows the dust off the bottle, see that it was six years old. He smirks in delight. > The golden sun has set…the dark night plagues the country. > Outside the safe shelter, the Deathscythe Outlaw exits the shelter. He heads to the nearby city by foot…he stops and look behind him, see the same black car from before is parked three blocks away from the shelter now. Kyo looks at the car suspiciously…shrugs and continue to his trip to the city. > In the car, a man in a black suit was at the driver’s seat, watch Kyo leave the shelter carefully…turn his attention over to the shelter and look serious. > In the bright city, Kyo stands at a newsstand. He purchases a pack cigarette, take the charge from the clerk and leave. Kyo takes out a cig after he open the pack, light it with his silver lighter. He sighs out a long cloud smoke pass his lips. > Kyo looks over to the voice, see the young Charlene in warm clothes stand behind. > In the far part of the city, Charlene and Kyo walk together in the city. > Kyo have stop. > Charlene walks a bit…stop and look over to confusion when she saw him hang back. > Kyo stares at Charlene silently and walks pass Charlene. Charlene looks angry, hit herself on the head. > Charlene catches up with Kyo. Kyo continue to walk…he stop when he felt a bad vibe, seriousness fill Kyo’s face. > Kyo shifts his eyes over to the right, see a man in a tan color overcoat and the same color hat with eyes that glow a dark crimson aura stand at the street corner five blocks behind him. The Shadow Drone smirks at Kyo…run away. Charlene approaches Kyo, look at him curiously. > Kyo looks at Charlene seriously. > In the outskirts of the city, Kyo Kushinaga flies down and land before a building that was under-construction. He looks at the building seriously. > In the indoor parking area, the Deathscythe Outlaw enters with caution. He makes a sound and notice a black van parked beside an elevator. Kyo approaches the van, examines it…a “ding” sound was heard cause Kyo to draw out his modified 9mm and aim it at the direction of the sound, watch the elevator doors mysterious open. He notices an active camera that hung high in the far left corner. > In the nearby building, in a dark room that was surrounded by many TV screens, Yosho Hiroshima in a slick black color suit sit at a computer. The screens show the footage from the parking area that Kyo was in. > Meanwhile, back in the parking area, Kyo kept his gaze on the camera. Kyo smirks and holster the gun back in his trench coat. > Kyo enters the elevator. > The elevator stops on the nineteenth floor, Kyo Kushinaga exit the elevator, walk through a room that is one giant aquarium without the fishes. He explores further in room…stop when he saw a German man with his head lowered in torn pants and no shirt that have brown hair and dull green eyes. > Back in the room that Yosho was in, Yosho smirk in delight when he saw Kyo reach the man. > In the aquarium room, Kyo stares at the man seriously. > German man holds out his arms. The aquarium’s glass mysteriously cracks around Kyo. Kyo looks with surprise when saw this. The man makes a sound and the glass blows apart with his power. The water pours out with great haste…the man stop the water makes it stand beside him. Kyo looks seriously. > Kyo draws out his 9mm…the German man thrust both hands, throw the great amount of water at Kyo. The water swallows Kyo whole…it take form of a water cyclone by the man’s great Psy-power. Kyo floats in the center of the cyclone with both eyes closed. > Kyo open his eyes and reveals that glow a cerulean color aura. He uses his power to burst out of the cyclone, soar right at the man for the attack…the man blows Kyo away with the release of his strong Psy pulse. Kyo flies back, burst out of the building and head straight to another. German man goes after Kyo. Kyo catches his bearing, land hard on the sidewall of the building…sideways cause a deep dent. German man lands on the sidewall on all fours, glare at Kyo blankly. > German man takes a deep breath cause the sheets of glass behind him to shatter, the shards levitates…he sighs out and the shards darts right at Kyo. Kyo runs up the building, avoid the shards. Many sheets of glass explode Kyo. > On the top of the building, the Deathscythe Outlaw hops over the edge and lands with ease. German man floats down…Kyo lunge for the man, attack the man with punches and kicks and finish the combination off with the “Raising Outlaw” (flip kick). German man flies back and hits hard on metal railing…the man stands up like nothing happen. > German man grunts and undergoes a bizarre transformation, his body grows larger, the skin turn gray with black scales, the eyes turn blood-shot red...the transformation make chunks of concrete raise from ground from man’s surging Psy-power, levitate around the changing man. Kyo gets in defensive stance, prepare for the unexpected. The man finishes the monstrous transformation…something happens that cause the man to go pain, he cries out and favors his head. The concrete falls to the ground. > In the room with the TV screens, Yosho wasn’t there anymore. > On the rooftop, the man was still in pain. Kyo glares at the man seriously. > Kyo lunges for the man, draws out his Plasma Sword. A red energy beam shoots out from the handle. Kyo swings hard…he mysterious stops and holds the blade near the man. > The energy blade changes to a cerulean color. Kyo looks at the man surprisingly > A gunshot is fire out of nowhere…the German man’s chest explode in a green color blood, fall to the ground, dead. Kyo looks with shock and snaps his head in the direction from where the gunshot was fire, sees a helicopter fly beside the rooftop. Seriousness fills Kyo’s face when he saw Yosho with a smoking gun stand in the helicopter. > Kyo growls furiously when he heard Yosho’s words. > The helicopter flies away from the rooftop. Kyo leaps up…something pulls him down, throw him through a concrete wall. > Kyo makes a sound when he saw the Japanese man in red stand with the decease German man on his shoulder. He glares at Kyo through his shades with a smirk. Kyo glares back at the man. The man takes a step back and fall off the roof. Kyo gets out of the wreckage, approach the edge with his 9mm drawn, aim the gun over the edge. > Kyo lowers the gun to the side. > Kyo takes out a cigarette, lights it and smokes. He looks up at the dark color sky seriously, smell the nicotine scent. > To be continued
