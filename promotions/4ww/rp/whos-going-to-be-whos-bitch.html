Who's Going To Be Who's Bitch? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #533 Date: 02/16/04 Location: The Jay Leno Show Alpha - noun - something that is first adjective - socially dominant especially in a group of animals Omega - noun - last, last ending, the lesser of two evils. Bitch - noun - the female of the dog or some other carnivorous mammals; a lewd or immoral woman b: a malicious, spiteful, or domineering woman. Either way you look at it. Coricidon thinks she’s the ‘alpha’ bitch in the 4WW, but she seems to have forgotten that she isn’t the dominating female in the federation. I, Patti Anderson, the ‘Darling of the 4WW’, has held that honor longer then any female that has come and gone. Jade and Lacuna were poised on being great replacements for me when I was ready to step down and take my rightful place by my loving man’s side and create the family he wants. But as of right now, you, Coricidon have decided to threaten my reign as the “Alpha bitch”. If you had a brain in that bi-sexual wannabe body then you would know your place in the 4WW as waiting patiently for your time to shine in the spotlight, but nope you had to make it personal by stating that you were becoming the ‘alpha’ female. I have news for you missy, it won’t happen as long as I breathe air here in this world. You’re loss of the South Wind Title is only the start of it. That, my dear, was just a lesson to teach who the REAL ALPHA female is within the federation, but I’ll leave it up to the actions in the ring this week that will speak for me instead. As for this week, I plan on taking that chain that we are connected with and using it to do the nice little physical damage to you that will more then be do you during this match. I plan on making you look bloody fucking awful, so that the women you so illegally pick up and keep bound in your home, car, hotel room, they’ll be afraid to look at your face. Or is this what you are looking for, to become so physically scared that no man nor woman will want to play with you ever. Corky my dear (you don’t mind if I call you that?), you will never EVER be the dominate female in the 4WW. I am the only woman who has and deserves the title. When you have battled Fey McClancy and walked away unscarred then you can claim the title of most dominate female, until that day my dear Corky you can continue to dream your little bi sexual dreams and desire the day to be “Queen of the 4WW”. Either way Corky you will lose to the ‘Darling of the 4WW’, and once and for all prove that I, Patti Anderson am the dominate female in the federation. ____________________________________________ Place: Jay Leno’s green room Time: A few minutes before Patti is set to be on the show (Patti sits in the lush green room that is behind the scenes of the Tonight Show. Her copper hair shining brightly against the black leather couch she is sitting on. Her outfit is something she’s not used to wearing. A black leather dress clings tightly to her curves, showing off her dazzling body that has managed to make the men in the back stage area drool. Her impatience of having to wait forces her to get up, pacing the room, her high heel boots giving her 5'10' frame extra height, making her look like an amazon compared to every one else in the room. After a few impatient pacing, a runner comes to the door.) Runner: Patti, you’re about on. Mr. Leno is finishing up his monologue and you’ll come out after the break. So if you’ll follow me, I’ll take you to where you need to be for your introduction. (Patti nods and follows the runner to the backstage area where the stars come out. She hears the crowd laughing at one of Jay’s jokes and the banter between Jay and Kevin Eubanks seemed to keep the crowd extremely warmed up. Her nerves were kicking in, as she gets the cue to get ready to enter the stage area. Jay: Our first guest tonight is . Overwhelming favorite #1...beautiful and talented wrestler for 4WW...fighting in the first ever female dog collar match....Please welcome ‘The Darling of the 4WW’ Patti Anderson. (Patti enters the stage area, as the Tonight Show band plays Creed’s “Beautiful”. The male population lets loose with loud yells and screams, a few stand, giving off the “We’re not worthy” bow to her, showing their appreciation for the fastest rising star of the federation. Patti walks over to a few of the fans and shakes there hands, before she gets a hug from Jay.) Jay: And what a great deal! You guys get hands shakes and I get to hug Patti. What a great country this is! Yea! (The male population continue there wolf whistles and yells) Patti: Wow! Jay: They're happy you're here. (Audience member yells something) Patti:: I....I.....What?! AUDIENCE MEMBER: Give me a hug! Patti: Hold on.....what was that?! AUDIENCE MEMBER: Give me a hug! (Audience starts rooting) (Patti runs off stage to the audience and gives the male fan a big hug) AUDIENCE MEMBER: Thank you. Patti: You're welcome. (Patti runs back on stage and bump right into the camera man, giving the man a slight thrill in the process) Patti: Oh my God! I almost knocked that poor camera man unconscious. (Another camera pans onto the man as he’s wiping sweat from his brow, a goofy smile on his face.) Jay: How was it for you? Good? Patti: I need a cigarette! (laughs) I.....I.....I feel....umm.....I'm really happy to be here with you, guys. I’m just itching to get into the ring this week with Coricidon. Jay: We need to cut away for a commercial break, but when we come back, we’ll pick up with Patti Anderson. (The producer drops her arm as the show goes to commercial. Patti sits staring nervously as she waits patiently for the return. Several make up people come out and touch up Jay’s face with powder and dab a little on her to make her feel a little more comfortable. After about thirty seconds, the show slowly begins to return to its status. Patti’s hazel eyes catch the sight of a young man in uniform and makes a promise to sign him an autograph as soon as she can. Jay notices it and decides to open up with that.) Jay: During the break, I couldn’t help but notice you staring at the young man over there in the uniform, your family has some kind of military background, right? Patti: Yea, you need to when you have .... well .... me as a child. You need to have a little..... No, my Dad was in the Greek Army and so he was there and you know, as a kid, you don't care about Army stories .... I was a girl .... I didn't know if you noticed that - I am female. (Audience cheers on.) And ... um ... but, yea, he used to tell us the story when he fell ..... he has a chipped tooth and apparently, he jumped into a fox hole and someone was in his fox hole. Apparently, you're not supposed to go into other people's fox holes ..... I don't know the rules .... but the guy's gun was sticking up and he chipped his tooth and he got some sort of medal for that. Years later we found out that wasn't the story. Umm .... it was actually a lot braver and amazing of what he did. But I loved it as a kid, I was going around saying 'My Dad got a medal for chipping his tooth and kicking a guy out of a fox hole .... which I thought was a little rude. I mean .... Can't you share? You know, I don't know the rules .... Jay: Yea, yea... That was a pretty brave thing that your Dad did. (The crowd applauds Patti as she beams with joy over the memory of the stories her dad told her as a child.) Jay: Now what's the bravest thing you've ever done? Can you think of .....? Patti: Aside from doing this match this week against Coricidon? I’d say it was stalking my now fiancé, Sean McClancy, or taking on his sister, Fey McClancy-Childress. Jay: Stalking? Well, that's it. I didn't say scary. I said brave. Patti: That's true, that's true. You know I don't do any brave things. I....I...do a lot of stupid things. (Audience members says something.) Patti: That was not stupid - that was brave! You're right, you're absolutely right. No..... Jay: Well, what was the most stupid thing you did? Patti: I fell in love with my boss’s brother a few months ago when he wrestled in the federation SCWF, living in Australia I would catch the video feeds of the show and somehow I felt drawn to him. I saved my money up to come stateside to visit a few friends and take in the sites of California. While there, I ran into him in a local bar and we just hit it off. Before I knew it, I’m chasing him all over the place like a damn groupie and annoying the bloody hell out of his sister. When Sean decided to keep me around, that really irritated his sister. Jay: His sister didn’t like you?. What was the problem? Patti: She thought I was a gold digger.... Jay: A gold digger? Patti: Yes she did, until she found herself assisting me in chasing the woman that claimed she was the one for Sean right out of his bloody life.. Jay: I heard something .... you know, all this internet stuff going around .... that you are running scared of Coricidon this week, and that is why you agreed to this matchup? Patti: Oh good God! Thank you ..... No, there's some kind of confusion. Apparently, Miss Corky is spouting some bloody nonsense that since I cost her the title at Storm Front, that my status is being threatened. Unfortunately, she has no bloody clue as to what she’s done.(Audience members follows her lead). Thank you. Jay: We have a clip of the show. Now this is where you came out of the back area and cost her the match. Patti: From what Fey told me when I came to the show, that is what would it. Are we going to it now? Jay: I do believe we are. Patti: Yea, yea. Jay: Let’s go to it [Hearing the crowd’s reaction, Coricidon quickly lets go of Satima and comes to her feet. She turns and ends up face to face with Patti Anderson as she stands there. The two women, only a few inches in height apart, stare each other down. Coricidon just smiles and spits in Patti’s face. She turns to go back to work and Patti spins her back around. Coricidon pulls her hand back for a punch or slap, but Patti pulls back in her own way and nails Coricidon with a shin kick up between the legs, the move she calls: Venus’ Revenge. Coricidon drops down to the mat, holding herself.] Candler: Oh, damn, what was that about? Patti feeling her spot as top female threatened? Coricidon has already pretty much taken out every other top female in the 4WW. Morales: I don’t know, I really don’t! [Patti hits the ropes and comes off with a baseball slide drop kick that takes her and Coricidon out of the ring. On the other side, Tyler Blaze and the referee come to an agreement and Blaze climbs back into the ring. Blaze stands there and notices Lee Satima is coming to his feet. He doesn’t see Coricidon anywhere in the ring. As soon as Satima is to his feet fully Blaze just runs in and just about takes his head off with a superkick, Insanity’s Embrace. Satima drops like a sack of potatoes. Blaze quickly jumps down for the pin. One…. Two… .Three!] Jay: OW!! That move had to have hurt? Patti: I don't know (makes a funny face). Jay: ? Patti: I don't know ...... where it came from. We'll just leave it at that. Jay: It never happened? Patti: It never happened. Jay: Well ..... a lot of our men would like to believe that it happened, but it’s on the tape clearly that you nailed her with that move. (Audience members agreeing) Jay: So .....This is what is causing this match between the two of you? Patti: My guess would be she went and cried to Ian and Fey that she got screwed and wanted a chance to prove herself. I more then gladly accepted. It’s not every day I can get a work out in against another female. Fey is pretty content sitting up in her office looking around and wishing she could perform again, but it’s ok. Jay: So what are the rules for this match? Two women in a Dog Collar Match is usually reserved for men who need to settle a score. Outside of the clip, why would Coricidon want a piece of you? Patti: It’s really simple. Corky and I will be wearing dog collars. Strapped to the collars is a 10 foot chain. We use the chain to beat each other into a submission. In order for there to be a winner, One of us has to touch all four ring posts first. And believe me Jay, Corky will NOT know what hit her. Jay: Aren’t you worried about getting all bloodied up or even injured from the match? Patti: Of course I am. There are always risks in this type of match up. Those chains can and will probably make us bloody. But I’m not afraid to shed a little blood on my path to making her my little bitch. She definitely came in ‘barking up the wrong tree’. Jay: We'll be right back.... Jay: The DVD, 'Darling of the Ring.' The DVD just came out this week, right? Patti: It’s out now, and from what I’ve been told, is that it is doing very well. I picked out my best material for this. Sean has some really sweet things to say about me and our relationship. Jay: Sometime soon. Now Sean is Irish? Patti: Yea, yea. He's Irish.. Jay: He is Irish, isn't he? Patti: I didn't know how many people knew that .... but he is. Jay: No, he's what we call a coalition partner. Patti: Yes, yes .... sure is. Jay: You're really doing your duty as an Australian to hook up with him. Patti: Yes, I guess so. Yea. Jay: Now you two are so cute together. Is his sister still hell bent on the two of you not being together even though you took care of his then fiance in the ring? Patti: It seems like it, wouldn't it. You know, I'm sure if you ask Fey, she would give you a lot of reasons why he should not date me. Jay: Oh really? Patti: You know, he's a wonderful man. He's incredible and he's looking to start a family. He says he's ready to settle.... Jay: You said Fey is still uncertain about your relationship with her brother what’s going on there? Patti: She just is just a sister that is too over protective of her brother. In my opinion she needs to focus on her relationship to her husband, not what her brothers are doing. They’re grown boys, let them live their lives. Did I mention that her twin brothers are single? (The women in the audience begin to twitter and yell at the thought of two McClancy brothers that are still single.) Patti: You know, they are wonderful men. They are just incredible . For all you women out there and I know there are a few Jay: There's a lot over there. Patti: Thank you. Thank you. If you're single, I have there number on my cell phone in the back. We should call them. I think we should call them. They make a good living, dress real nice, they are clean and very talented. I think ... you let me know, I have the phone in the back and we'll do a quick ..... Oh, right there!( Patti points to a couple women in the audience that are just about to drool over the picture that Jay’s crew puts up) Those two ladies want them in the worst way! Jay: That's the lady with the vibrating cell phone. She doesn't need him! And when she's on the phone, she's on the phone. Patti: Was that in good taste? Was that? It was funny.... Jay: There's another joke, but I'm not going to touch it. You know, I'm looking at the DVD and you know I enjoyed watching it. Patti: You watched it all? Jay: Yes, I did see it. I saw the whole thing. (In Aussie accent) 'I live like a Monopoly life.' I remember all the lines from this video: But the thing I enjoy .... I don't want to say the outtakes are funnier than the show itself.... Patti: They are. Jay: They are cause we know the DVD and then you watch what happens in the scene.... Patti: Apparently, I had some problems learning the lines and this is what Fey says and what she lists as one of the reasons not to date me. Im a gold digger .... I had a giggling problem... Jay: Let's take a look. Here are some outtakes from the DVD. (A few outtakes are shown) Jay: What's with the ice? Patti: Well, every time he dropped the ice cube .... it's .... ah ... it would be bad taste for me to say what it sounds like. It just sounded like ..... just the sound of the ice cube made when it hit the water ..... (Patti busts out in laughter) Jay: What's so funny about the sound? Yes? Patti: You know when you're in the bathroom? Jay: Alright. Thank you.. Thank you very much. Patti: Thank you very much. Jay: Now I know why Fey doesn’t want her brother going out with you. Okay.... AUDIENCE MEMBER: I will! Patti: Thank you. AUDIENCE MEMBER: I second! Patti: It's all about toilet humor. Jay: Well, 'Darling of the Ring'..... it's a terrific DVD and check out the first ever Ladies Dog Collar Match Up this week on Storm Front. Patti: Thank you for inviting me. (Patti slides down the couch to make room for the next guest that is scheduled, as the camera fades to black.)
