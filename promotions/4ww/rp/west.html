West...

8 September 2004

West... Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #935 Date: 8/9/04 Location: A room [Fade In] [Lee is sitting in a dark room with a brick wall behind him and two cement ones on each side. He is sitting backwards on a blue steel chair, his arms are crossed and are resting on the top of the back rest; he’s looking at the ground shaking his head.] Lee: West, West, West… [Lee looks up from the ground.] Lee: You wanna talk shit West? Ok let’s talk some shit. Did you stop to think for a second that you were never meant to hold that West Wind Title? Did you stop to think that maybe I was a messenger of god; that I still am? Well did you? I didn’t think so. [Lee smirks] Lee: Karma, West. Karma. You see, it wasn’t my choice to interfere in your match with Bullet, and it won’t be my choice to beat your ass up at Maelstrom Five, not to say I wouldn’t do either of them if it was up to me. I’m just doing what I’m supposed to be doing, and so are you. Our paths crossed, as they will again and just as I did last time, I will fulfill my destiny and so will you. [He adjusts himself in the chair, we know see he is wearing a buttoned up leather jacket and jeans.] Lee: Chris West, the West Wind Champion. Isn’t that predictable? Isn’t that almost redundant? Well it would be if you could actually wrestle, as you learned last week, the West Wind Division isn’t a hardcore division, it’s a gimmick division and some times those gimmicks require more than basic wrestling. Face it West, if I didn’t take Jayson Bird last week and distract you, someone else would have or Bullet would have just have out wrestled you, not that it would be very hard to do that at all. [Lee laughs] Lee: As I said West, it was an act of god, divine intervention if you will. I’m just that, a hand of god, doing his bidding. Call me a Sadist, call me the Anti-Christ but come on, if I was really the Anti-Christ would god not have killed me two hundreds years ago, before I was a vampire. The “devil” and God work hand-in-hand West, if God posses all the power in the world and the devil was his enemy, wouldn’t he be dead? Wouldn’t I be dead? Well more dead then I am now… [Lee stands up] Lee: You’re a good Christian right West? Why? Because your scared, scared that you might go to hell when you die. Isn’t that what suckers most people into Christianity the fact that if you aren’t a good Christian then you’re a “sinner”, and if you’re a sinner you go to hell, I’m a sinner West, I have been for two hundred fucking years! I’M NOT GOING ANYWHERE! Unless this is hell, but if this is hell then what are we so scared about and who’s the “devil”. I’ll tell you West, we all are, we’re our own personal devils… [Lee unbuttons his top button.] Lee: Now usually when people talk about the devil they are speaking of Lucifer, who in fact isn’t really all that bad, he’s kinda like you, a little fat, pretty ugly, single, and a lot of hype! But the big difference here is that he has power, you don’t West, you look to weapons to gain power. You break the rules West, the rules of wrestling! [Lee unbuttons his second button] Lee: Tell me, are you a wrestler so you can fight? So you can legally beat people up for a living? Because that’s the only thing you’re good at? You don’t do the fighting, you hold the weapon, the steel chair does the fighting, the tables do the fighting, the thumbtacks do the fighting, not you, them! [Lee unbuttons his third button revealing a black shirt with some lettering.] Lee: Come Maelstrom Five, I’ll only have one thing to say to you. [Lee unbuttons the rest of his jacket revealing a ‘Chris West Hardcore Legend’ shirt. He rips the shirt down the middle revealing a black shirt with flames and words on it. As he tears down he points to each one of the words on the shirt reading them out loud.] WELCOME… TO… HELL!!!
