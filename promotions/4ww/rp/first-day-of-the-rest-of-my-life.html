maybe this kid just wanted to be hugged Whatever it was I know it's because We are, We are, the youth of the nation We are, We are, the youth of the nation Little Suzy, she was only twelve She was the nation We are, We are, the youth of the nation ¥ ╜╗╜ Youth of a Nation ; P.O.D. ╝╗╜ ~ Although trials come and trials go they still test us to our abilities. They take us to our limits where we can either surpass our boundaries or we can fall short from our personal heights. Whatsoever be our destiny is set in stone; however just because it is a predetermined destiny we can alter that destiny by surpassing our expectations. Does it matter what we become or how far we are to succeed? In the end isn’t it the only reason we continue on, just to see the ending of our life stories? Let us sit here and recall the previous times that we reminisced about earlier times in our life. Like many others do we sit there wishing we had done more or do we sit there satisfied in our attempts knowing we did our best? All of the efforts that we put in we will receive back. In all reality you get what you put in and people get what they deserve. Just because you haven’t received yours yet doesn’t mean that it’s not coming. After all in the end it doesn’t even matter.~ {The scene opens up within a dark vicinity known as “The Hell Hole”. Nothing is seen but a great heat is felt as we continue to walk in this blinding darkness. With every step the eerie quietness becomes deafening as we continue walking through the area. Continuing on our journey we come to a chain link fence where we notice a white sign sporting black bold lettering. We can’t make out exactly what it says but there is a rip in the fence where it’s obvious someone has entered before. We carefully make our way through where we continue in an area of forestation. As we make our way through sounds of flames erupting and night critters are heard through the deep pass. We walk towards the sounds where the eruptions become louder and it begins to get hotter and hotter. We finally get clear from the brush and see red tints all around. We cut through bushes to see miniature volcanoes with fire all around. We look closely to see a wrestling ring set up surrounded by the volcanoes. Encircling the volcanoes looks to be a dirt-bike track. Off over to the far left corner we see a half-pipe with skaters skating all over. We continue to make our way forward but are suddenly stopped by guardian komodo dragons. Out from the blue we see a dirt-bike fly past a volcano sliding the back tire causing dirt to fly in the face of the camera men. He extends the kick stand and walks toward the dragons. He grabs the chains and attempts to pull them back. His efforts being dust in the wind, he howls out loud and suddenly several men come out holding a beef short loin. They throw it towards the dragons and watch them tear it apart.} Man: Come before they make you desert… {They all walk towards the half-pipe where they all commute for the Friday Night usual. The guys are standing there waiting in line paying their dues. As the man on the dirt bike gets off he climbs up the ladder and stands next to the guys and gives them all hand shakes of sorts. He grabs a skateboard with the face of Venom. He begins to skate the pipe and hits a picture perfect back tail slide. He comes back across leaps up into the air steps onto the platform jumping onto the rail and then running it before doing a half cab flare to mute. He lands and tries to regain composure and barely keeps his balance to hit another backslide before getting back to the group.} Kid: Shit Tom ‘at shit was off da hinges esse. Other Kid: Dayum howz come you’z ain’t in da X-Games an’ doin’ ya thang. Kid: Lobo man you aughta get ya culo back in dat ring and prov’em you’z still gots it. El Lobo: Esse I haven’t been back ‘cause of tha thing that put me out. I am so wantin back in the ring though. It takes time to heal yo, and what happened to me will take a lot o’ tiempo to heal esse. Kid: It’s a shame for ya being out man. You really did do a lot and proved a lot of people wrong. You was out there doin ya thing and I was sittin here thinkin’ damn that’s Tom showin’ off. Other Kid: Yea holmes you was turnin’ heads there esse. People was lookin at you like you iz da new shit. El Lobo: Kid I am the shit so don’t doubt that holmes. Like we all say in this biz you gotta be careful but bring it all. Today you won’t get anywhere playin’ it safe man. You’ve got to go out there and give it your all. Like those one dudes say it’s all or nothing at all. You have got to go out there and give more than you are capable of. That’s why the people are in love with my luchacore style. I combined the classic luchador style with the mentality of street fighting hardcore death matches. But now that I’ve got a little one on the way I need to be more careful so that I can provide him with a good life and a good future. I mean who really wants their Toto in the hospital all the time because he went too extreme. You know me guys and you know about my life yo. Kid: Yea I know esse, you lived in los barrios all tu vida. You immigrated here as a kid looking for a way to survive and get it together. I know all about it man so don’t think I forgot all about it esse. Hey I was just thinkin’ what’s up with Angél? How’s she doin’? El Lobo: She’s a lot better now that I’m out of the hospital. Kid: Yea she was there the entire time while you were in the hospital. She neva left ya side man. You gots a good one there man. Don’t let her go or I might get my wildest dreams come true. {All of them begin laughing hard as a female figure begins to walk towards the half-pipe. The female walking is wearing a tight pair of jeans with her stomach protruding out.. She is sporting a white top that halts after the bust so that it won’t stretch over the pregnant woman. She walks slowly towards the half-pipe as El Lobo slides down running to her side making sure she’s ok.} El Lobo: Angél are you alright? Angél: Si, estoy bien ahora. I just wanted to see how everything was going out here amor. It’s been so long since we’ve had a chance truly to ourselves Tom I was just hoping that since you’re awake and well I could spend some time with you. I’ve missed you Tom, please can we just have some together time now before you go back to wrestling. El Lobo: Now mamacita you know I don’t have a match and that I won’t be cleared to wrestle till who knows when. Angél: I don’t care Tomás Lobo! I wanna spend some time with you now. You were there in the hospital all sick and really hurt. I stood there day in and day out by your side not leaving you alone for very long. I was there when Simon, Patti, Fey, Pajaro, Garrett, even Jay and Chris stopped by to see how you were doing. I wanna spend some time with you awake where we can just sit together and enjoy each others presence alright? {Angél then walks to El Lobo and hugs him close kissing him gently in the fire light around them. El Lobo whistles at the kids on the half-pipe and motions for them to do something. Kid grabs his Venom skateboard and throws it to El Lobo as he walks off with Angél back towards the Wolf’s Den.}
