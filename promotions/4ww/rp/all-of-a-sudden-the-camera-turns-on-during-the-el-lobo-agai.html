"Insane" Chris West — 4WW Roleplay, September 2003

September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Insane' Chris West 4WW Exposures #118 Date: Location: [All of a Sudden The Camera Turns on during the El Lobo against West Match. West, is Dragging the El Lobo around the Ring ontop of Broken glass. Destroying, and Ripping open El Lobos Back. All of a Sudden, El Lobo Starts Screaming in Pain, Yelling he Quits, he’s done] [This Way My Debut Match. Over a Month Ago, I walked into the 4WW Arena, Full of Steam waiting for someone to Unload all the Years of Anger, out on Someone, that someone just happened to Be El Lobo. I Took Someone everyone respected and Loved. And Fucking Destroyed him.. Making him scream out for me to Stop.. I Left him in a Pool of his Own blood. But, like they Say, What comes around.. Goes Around] ['Price To Play' by Staind Starts up, as Clips from the Psymon Against 'Insane' Chris West Start up. You See the Match go, as Psymon gets the upper hand, and beats up on the Insane One. With a DQ Finnish.. A Masked Man, Ran out from the Back, and Started to attack the Insane One. The Man, Takes off his Mask, as the Camera Zooms in for a Close up.. its None Other then El Lobo, Looking for Revange against the Insane One for his Actions a Month Ago. El Lobo, and Psymon Double Team the Insane One, Leaving West to Roll out of the Ring, While A Shitload of Blood Pourss down his Forhead. the Song Stops Playing, as you hear West’s Voice Again] [Revange Was Sweet?? Was it?? You got to Bust me Wide Open and Busted me While Open.. Do you think over my Seven Years in this Sport you are the first man, to do that to me?? I Doubt It, You got Revange.. You Did What you set out to do. But, it was shortlived. This Week, on Stormfront, I’m going to Prove it wasn’t a Fluke.. I Beat you Cause I’m Better then you] [The Camera Turns on, Backstage after Stormfront, Where Psymon, and El Lobo Double Teamed 'Insane' Chris West. All of a Sudden a Door Opens, as the Camera Walks into the Room, the 4WW Doctors Room.. As you See 'Insane' Chris West Layin down on the Table, as the Doctor Works on him. West is Wearing a Blood 'Insane' T-Shirt, and Blue Jeans.. Stiching up his Cuts. West is Also Holding onto his South Wind Championship like its a Baby. 'Holy Shit' Jason Bird Walks into the Picture. He’s Wearing a 'Can I fuck Patti Also??' T-Shirt, and Joggers. Jayson Looks into the Camera, and Starts talking] Holy Shit: This Man, has Been throw Alot in his Career. El Lobo, As someone who has Witnesses some of the Most Brutal Matches in the History of this Match, You have nothing, but Pissed him Off. You have Opened a Can of Shaked Beer.. You are not going to Drink it down, you are getting all the Beer all over you’re self.. West is going to be all over you like the Beer, Beating the Ever living Fuck out of you.. [The Camera Turns Back to West.. As you See the Doctor put one the Stitches into West Head.. You can See the pain on West’s Face. As he Didn’t take any of the Pain Meds.. He Didn’t take the Shots he would Normally be given. He’s Taking the Pain. The Pain from the Doctor Putting a Niddle in and out of his Head, Putting the Stiches into his Head to close up the Woons, that Psymon, and El Lobo Opened, the Camera Turns Back to Bird] Holy Shit: You think you put him throw a shitload of Pain. Look at the Man, Look at the Doctor put the Stiches in his Head, without putting the shit into his Forhead so he doesn’t feel the Pain of the Stiches. El Lobo. You Mexican Mother Fucker. You are in for a Shitload of Pain. But, Can you handle this type of Pain.. From the Past, the Truth has already been told.. You Can’t. When things get too Bad, you Scream out in Pain, Yelling for the Ref.. Yelling for the Insane One, to Stop doing what he’s doing to you’re Body.. El Lobo. [Bird Shakes his head, while Rolling his Eyes] Holy Shit: You Might think you got Revanage.. You think West is going to be a Diffrent Person. You got to be Shitting Me. West is going to be the Same Hardcore Son of a Bitch like he always his, but this time. You and Psymon are imprinted in his Head. All He Sees is Psymon, and You El Lobo Double Teaming Him. It Took Both of you, and a Mallet, to do what you did to him. You got you’re Revanage El Lobo.. But it won’t last forever.. Keep Partying with Psymon.. The Party won’t last forever. [The Doctor Finnishes up.. As West, Sits up and Looks into the Camera, you see all the Blood still on his face, the Doctor puts a Bandage onto His Face to cover the Stiches, as the Doctor Goes away.. West Starts to talk] Insane: El Lobo, you think what I did to you last month.. Where, I Made you Scream like the Bitch you are. You think that was Insane?? Bitch, you got another thing coming. Its time to take everything up a Step on the Invisble Ladder of Brutalness. Its time to go Fall Back into my Past, and Bring out everything I have, and Make you feel even more pain then before. You Better be ready to hold in the Screams, and not Yell I Quit, at the first sign of Horror. You Will my Friend. Be going throw even more Brutal Shit then BEFORE!! [West Grabs his Title, and gets up and leaves the Office.. Bird Follows him, as the Camera Fades to Black] [This Could have been Simply Avoided. It didn’t have to come to this. El Lobo, you are the one who started this, and I’m the one who is going to Finnish this. But, I want you to do me a Favor, I want you to get You’re Special Friend Psymon to watch this Match. I Want him to know what, is coming for him. Can You do that for me, Thanks You]
