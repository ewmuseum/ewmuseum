Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #339 Date: Location: Place: The Office Time: A Few Hours Into the Evening Patti is sitting behind the desk, her email program open. Several spam emails are shown, a simple mouse click and they are gone from the screen. Patti: Why the bloody hell does Sean have to use my account for all these sign ups? He has his own account for that. Patti continues to delete the unwanted crap that seems to filter into the account. A sigh of annoyance slips out between her lips. She finally gets rid of the garbage and settles into reading the important emails. A couple from her dad, one from her best friend back home, and a few from other family that have managed to keep in contact. She notices that one email address seems pretty unfamiliar. Something from someone from the email address of FiveCard@4WW.com. Patti: Bolloks. What in the bloody hell does he want? Patti runs the virus scan over the email to make sure that he didn't try do something to her computer. She full knew well that her sister-in-law and husband aren't seeing to well with each other, and she did NOT want to get caught in the middle of it. She took a deep breath and decided to see what he wanted. To: Sean'sLady@4WW.com From: FaceCard@4WW.com Subject: FA and West This came from my latest interview with that blonde twit of a best friend of yours. She says that she and that loser are done, think I'd have a chance with her? We know all the ladies love the Five Card Stud. Take a look and tell me what you think. Mike, you think you are so cute mocking The Five Card Stud don't you? Sure, saying that I just didn't get the job done. Well, I went to finish the job and put your out of your misery. However, you on the other hand would just walk away back to your locker room and play Super Mario Bros. with the Get Along Gang until you shoot chocolate milk out your nose. Chris West is as useless without a chair in his hand during a wrestling match as a hockey player is without any balls. Do you really think the 4WW is encouraging you spitting out your bleeped words on TV every week? Take your Ritalin, prepare to get your ass kicked, and move your ass down the card where you belong. I have to hand it to Todd Dream for beating me one - two - three in the ring last week. Well done. You should be proud of yourself. But no, you come out and ask me to leave you alone. Are you some sort of a wuss? A real champion waits for his victim to get back up so he can beat him down again. Who would have thought our champion would run away from a fight? The whole damn federation knows that. Patti cracked a smile, then burst out laughing. Patti: Uh BlackJack, I don't bloody well think that you are Eliza's type of man. Besides she's still got feelings for that bloke Summers. So I don't quite think you'd make her any happier. She thought about throwing in a reply to it, but it would just have to wait until later. Her little icon on her screen notifies her that another. From the looks of it, it looks to be something from her other tag team partner, Jamie. Patti: Wonder what is on his mind. To: Sean’sLady@4WW.com From: AsianSensation@4WW.com Subject: My thoughts The blonde woman hits again. I sent you the comments for you to see exactly what kind of friend you have. By the way is she single? :) We're facing Todd Dream and Mike Marvelous??? There aren't enough words to describe the fear that I am going through right now. I mean, any time in the match, 'ole Chrome Dome Summers could lose control of himself and rush down to the ring to bum rush us. I had better arm myself with a tranquilizer gun just in case he breaks out of the cage and the zoo guys don't sedate him in time. Of course, that won't stop FA. They'll probably bring in some other equally smelly bald dude to take Summers' place. Or maybe Mikey'll shave Hillary's hair off and have her masquerade as Summers. Oh, the possibilites... Oh, the HORROR! Chris West? Who the hell is Chris West? Memo to self: Get someone to tell me who Chris West is. Seriously, though. I have never heard of the guy in my life. Is this some sort of Ian Childress conspiracy? Add in someone that I have never heard of into a match to confuse me? This is all a set-up. First they give me the Mother of All Bitches and a supposed 'Las Vegas card shark' as partners, and the next, they give me an opponent that I have never heard of. Memo to self: Question FeyRobot and find out why she felt the need to book me in such a horrible match. Patti shook her head in disbelief. Her tag partners have gone crazy. Both drooling over Eliza. She wasn’t going to assist Eliza this time. She figured that decision would be hers to make. She looked over both of the emails and figured out how to go about replying. To: FiveCard@4WW.com; AsianSensation@4WW.com From: Sean’sLady@4WW.com Do you two bloody have any clue as to what is going on here in this match? We are all in an elimination tag team match, which means at any time one of us can be pinned, submitted, or disqualified for breaking the rules. How much assistance are the two of you going to be? Or am I going to be forced to carry this team by myself? I realize that we all have our issues with the opposing team. West, Dream, and Marvelous are going to be more solid of a team then what we are expecting, especially knowing that Dream and Marvelous are a team all own their own. They will everything to their advantage, including any discord the sense among the three of us. So what’s it going to be? Are we a team or are we individuals going into this match up? With a smile, Patti hits the send. She makes a note to offer up a prayer to the gods that her team becomes victorious at this weeks show.
