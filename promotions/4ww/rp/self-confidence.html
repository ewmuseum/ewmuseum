Self Confidence

20 October 2003

Self Confidence Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #237 Date: 10/20/03 Location: Hotel Room in Phoenix Self-confidence- noun - confidence in oneself and in one’s powers and abilities. That is something that Patti is desperately going to need to defeat her opponent, and right now it is something she is seriously lacking in. She lost it one time before. That one time right after that bitch Annie cost her the South Wind Title. A title that she and defended with pride and honor. She had thought the training Sean had put her through had paid off. And it did for a short while. Her confidence began to build with every title defense, each pin fall victory she earned. But as of right now, her confidence is taking the blow of facing Todd Dream. Once more she is being forced to deal with something a little stronger and more powerful then herself. Todd Dream possess the skills and desire that it takes to be the champion. His record speaks for itself. He has title reigns under his belt, been in some of the best stables in the feds he’s been in. The arrows all lead to the fact that Todd Dream should be able to ‘own’ Patti Anderson. Don’t get Destiny wrong, Todd Dream may seem impressive to the fans of wrestling, but how is he going to react going into a match with a woman trained by the his own opposition in the form of Sean McClancy? Todd got off pretty easy when he didn’t have to face Fey McClancy in the old federation. A restraining order kept him out of the ring and away from her. Even Fey’s own brother and husband made sure that he wouldn’t get close to her. But this time it’s a little different. Todd isn’t set to face against Fey McClancy. In fact, Destiny believes that Fey chose Patti to face Todd as a test. A test of her self confidence. It will also be the test for you Mr. Dream to see if your little dream world will come into a reality. It’s time for you to wake up, take a shower and come back to reality. Patti won’t be an easy conquest for you, she’ll teach you what it means to be in the ring with a real woman. A woman who has taken on the top names in this fed. In fact, if you take a really, really close look at who she has defeated, you’ll see that a former North Wind Champion is on that list of defeats. In fact, Patti knocked him out of tournament for the title. Destiny is pretty sure she can go on with the trash talk, but why bother. Tempers are already flying from the videos that the both camps have sent out. And thanks to Eliza Murphy, Patti got first hand vision of what Todd Dream thinks of her. So lets take a look at Patti and see what has been said. Place: Hotel in Phoenix Time: Sunday October 19, 2003, A few hours before Storm Front Patti sat curled up next to McClancy. Her red hair hanging down, messed up from a heated make out session with her love. She knew it would just get it to Annie to find out that she and Sean were still together. Since his and her victory at Maelstrom, they’ve been closer then ever before. Patti reached over and grabbed the remote for the VCR. Eliza Murphy had sent over the latest tapes from the studio. Seems that her opponent. The good old ‘Wet Dream’ Todd Dream had cut a pretty vicious promos directed at her. And once again, Eliza got a little over excited. Patti waited for the tape to begin playing. She wasn’t quite prepared for what the ‘Wet Dream’ had to say about her. A blonde surfer wanna-be appeared on the screen of the television. The world title hanging over his shoulder. Eliza Murphy stood to the side of him. Her arm still in the sling, from her attack last week by Chaos. Her red dress clung to her body as she waited for him to let go with what was to be said. Patti sat up on the bed. Her feet pulled up under her chin. She was preparing for her battle this weekend, and she knew that Todd was going to say something to rub her the wrong way. Her temper would probably be unleashed this weekend over the latest adlephi malakas TV DREAM: You just cannot deny my success. I've been on a roll as of late. One by one men have gone down at the hands of Todd Dream. One being Issue and two was Jaime. Although, some might say it was cause of my new group of bro’s, but a win is a win. The North Wind Champion in just two matches, how do you like that, people? I'm on fire right now and let's be honest, is there anyone that can put that fire out? I don't think it's possible. Once I get the ball moving, it cannot stop. I am going to keep on rolling over the competition. No matter how big in numbers they come or how impossible they seem. I am going to continue to dominate the competition. This includes the upcoming show this week, where I'll be going up against ANOTHER female wrestler, Patti. Patti blinked. Patti: The only role you’ve been on lately is a jelly donut roll there Todd. You see your victory over Jamie was a tainted one. You needed the assistance of your two little jokers. You weren’t man enough to take on Jamie by yourself so your two hired goons came in and saved your hide. Guess each federation has its joke for a champion. In fact, we all know that Syxx Figures and Jay Phoenix where better champions then you. As for facing another female wrestler. Take it up with the booking staff. They were the ones that placed us in the same ring. But at least this time, you can’t attack me before the match, without getting fired. Patti watched Eliza ask Dream another question. Must have been something about her. TV DREAM: Even though she is a female, and not in the same league as Todd Dream she is without a shadow of a doubt, the toughest challenge I have had to date. Still that being said, she’s just not ready for this challenge. The challenge of facing the biggest member of Featured attraction. Sure big names have come together before, but no names have been bigger than Todd Dream, Mike Marvelous, and Sean Summers. This is top of the line competition put together in one single group. Three men that have the drive and determination to go all the way in this business. Can we be stopped, heck no. We are going to make our team debut soon enough and we are going to be walking out with the gold then too, much like I did this past pay per view. Patti shook her head again as she changed her sitting position on the bed. Patti: Well of course I’m going to be your bloody biggest challenge, who else would bother stepping into the ring with you? Do you not do your bloody homework? I’ve faced Sean Summers. In fact, I’ve owned his bloody lazy ass in the ring several times. Even that woman you are standing next to, has owned his ass. Mike Marvelous? The man who claims he’s the greatest South Wind Champion, oh bloody well get over yourself. You have no clue as to what I’ve done for that title. You know he got lucky with it. Just the same as you got lucky with yours. Even McClancy has owned his ass at the Pay Per View, but what would you know about that? Drive? Determination? You don’t think I don’t possess those? Must be awfully lonely living in that world of your Dream. Patti waited until Eliza had asked the next important question. She’d been pretty sharp with the questions this week, which was unusual for her. TV DREAM: In a way I hope you all do, for I relish the chance to put people like Patti in their place. See Patti in the grand scheme of things you're a meaningless superstar whom will be going no where in this federation as long as you continue to stick your nose into people's business whom are better than you. As you can see, I am obviously better than you Patti and your chances of being victorious this week are slim. Your chances are about as good as one of your interviews, which is poor. You bring a quality like no other to the 4 winds. A quality that can only be found in a chick like yourself. You've got poor work ethic, putting forth mediocre material that resembles that of a preschool child quality. Patti brings nothing to the table, has no talent to offer. She's nothing more than a meaningless threat. All bark but no bite. Patti began to choke on the drink from the bottle that she had grabbed from the table. Dream was becoming more a California Nightmare then a California Dream. He had a larger ego problem then Patti: Mediocre material that resembles preschool child quality? When did you learn them big words surfer boy? Surfer College? You may have been in the game longer then I have, but you must realize that those with big ego’s and those who underestimate their opponents wind up losing along the way. Sticking my nose in everyone’s business? Who’s business? Yours? Oh please. The only business I have with you is that title belt that is around your waist at this minute. And that is the only business I have with you. That is where the business ends. I could care less that there is a new faction in the Four Winds. It’s just all a constant BLAH BLAH BLAH to me. Your mouth just keeps moving Todd, and nothing but dribble comes out of it. You spew crap that nobody cares about. You were good ONCE before, but now you think that I’m going to be an easy match. Need I remind you that I’m graduate of the ‘School of Hard Knocks’? Don’t you remember I was in McClancy’s corner during your stint in that other fed? I saw first hand how you wrestle, so you could say I saw your ‘lack of style’ up close and personal. And I know the people of my homeland where happy to see McClancy use you for a mop. I knew that blonde mop of yours was good for something. I’ll think I’ll let it rest right there, because I will return the favor and use that mop to clean up the ring. The Fates and Destiny have given me the self confidence to know that I can defeat you in the ring, I’ll just be glad when I send you and your munga back to California. With that Patti shuts off the tv. She looks back on the bed and sees Sean has disappeared. She hears the shower running. Hmm, he must have snuck off while she was watching Todd spew his filth. She winks at the camera as her baby doll t-shirt goes flying at the camera.
