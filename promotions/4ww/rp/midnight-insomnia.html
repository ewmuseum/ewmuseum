Midnight Insomnia

23 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Midnight Insomnia Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1243 Date: 4/23/05 Location: Some Motel In British Isles [The scene comes to an open inside a motel room somewhere on the British Isles. It is obviously day outside, but the motel room has the blinds shut so tightly it seems to be the dead of night. The camera grazes over to Rustin Slade, who is in the lack of light, seemingly one big mass of body on the bed.] [The light on the clock radio blinks 11:55 over and over again. Rustin’s eyes watch intently as he counts silently (22...23...24...25), keeping tally of the number of blinks.] Rustin: Dammit, can’t sleep. (45...46...47...48) Rustin: That weird Jamaican dude’s got me all worried. (11:57) Rustin: What the hell did he mean? I’m in grave danger? (11...12...13...14) Rustin: I mean, the only thing that seems to be in grave danger right now is my career. I haven’t won in a dog’s age. (34...35...36) Rustin: Something bad is going to happen? I mean, is he talking about my match with Lincoln at Maelstrom? I think that’s the biggest bunch of junk. I mean sure, I’ve had my ass handed to me by him a few times by him, but now I’m ready. (57...58....59....11:57) [Rustin rolls over, tired of looking at the clock and counting it’s blinks. He reminded himself he would have to set it before he went to sleep. Time is all I have after all, or was it time was something I have nothing of? It seemed time was running out. Time to Maelstrom VII, time to get Chastity (Damn, can’t think like that. Overconfidence always lays room for waste.)] Rustin: If I didn’t have enough pressure with the upcoming match at Maelstrom VII, I now got another match that has high stakes. Not to mention I’ll be on the opposite side of the ring Paul Williams this week. [Rustin rolls over again and begins counting again as the clock blinks 11:58] (3...4...5...6) Rustin: Paul and me have quite a history. He was the first man I faced in my 4WW entrance match, and then became a friend and a stablemate in the Legends. Now it seems he’s back on the opposing side, and siding with the man who sent Chastity Spence to the hospital, Kyo Kushinaga. So it seems you and me are enemies again Paul. (28..29...30) Rustin: Then there’s Shawn Singleton. The smug little son of a bitch who bested me last week, when I had the match in the bag. Shawn, take note that there’s going to be not only me this week who are going to be knocking you around the ring, but two others also. You may have your eyes set on South Wind gold, but I assure you, your eyes should be focused on all three of us first. This match could take you right back down to where you started, and you and your little buddy will be “the hunted” rather than “the hunters.” (59...11:59....2....3) Rustin: And finally, there’s TJ Anderson. I won’t lie TJ, you’ve been on my mind since your first match. There’s only room for one true innovator in the 4WW, and you my friend, are not that one. You being the protégé of Draven Lox, or Pogo, or whoever the hell that clown, no pun intended, is this week, just puts the icing on the cake. I can make my misfortunes of losing against Draven corrected by taking you out in the match. [Rustin brushes back his hair, and buries his face into his pillow once again. After a moment, he resurfaces from the depths of his pillow and again looks at the clock. The clock stops blinking as it pronounces 12:00] Rustin: Time’s running out. I don’t know why I’m thinking like this. That creepy little Jamaican’s got me on edge. Gotta be extra cautious. [Rustin looks again at the alarm clock as it stays locked on 12:00. Rustin watches it for a good few minutes, but the clock remains constant. He then counts aloud, waiting for it to change. Rustin gets angry now.] Rustin: Damn it! Move! Damn you! Change! [The clock refuses to budge.] Rustin: Isn’t it funny? First I don’t have time to wipe my own ass. Now it’s standing still. [Rustin shakes the clock, trying desperately to change what the digital face is saying. Rustin then breaks down, and tears begin to form.] Rustin: Why? Why am I like this now? Easily irritable...unhappy? Has my quest for Chastity really been this depressing? I mean I have everything I want. She likes me. But something’s missing. The win! That’s it, the wins are missing. I haven’t won in so long, I forgot the feel of how it really is to be appreciated for my wrestling ability, other than my hopeless quest for Chastity. Is that what makes Rustin Slade, Rustin Slade? [Rustin shakes his head and buries his face back into his pillow.] Rustin: Is that what that crazy Jamaican guy meant when he said, “who are you Mr. Slade?” He’s right. Who am I? I don’t even know anymore. Am I just a lovesick struggling wrestler, or am I the spectacle of innovation of 4WW? Dammit, stop it Rustin! [Rustin again looks at the clock. Still 12:00.] Rustin: You need to get back in the game Rustin. Time will wait for no one. But it will stand still for you. Get your career back into shape. Get the girl. Just be you! [Rustin begins to drift off to sleep as the scene fades to black.] Rustin: (Mumbling) Whoever that is...
