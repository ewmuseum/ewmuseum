Midnight Rendezvous Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1128 Date: 1/11/05 Location: New York, NY Suburbia Disclaimer: Warning, there is some strong profanity and a depiction of violence in this roleplay, from the introduction of a new character into the world of Rustin Slade and Chastity Spence. [The camera fades in to a stretch of a lonely road somewhere just outside of New York City. The camera catches some off screen scuffling of feet and shouts. The camera follows the scuffling and zooms over to a suburban nightclub. The name of the nightclub is not seen, however the over abundance of neon signs tell what kind of club it is. What is seen, however, is a burly nightclub owner and Rustin Slade, who has seemingly just been thrown out. Rustin is face down on the pavement.] Club Owner: AND STAY OUT! I swear to you Slade if I see you in here one more time trying to pitch some woo to my dancers, you’re going to be sorry! [The club owner goes back into the nightclub, closing the door. The only vital signs from Rustin are an occasional twitch here and there. The camera zooms in closer on the bronze highlights of Rustin’s hair trying to get a glimpse of his face. A light from the camera shines down on Rustin, stirring him back into consciousness. Rustin blinks a few times and looks at the camera with his disheveled face] Rustin (Barely audible): What the fu... [Rustin picks himself up off the ground, and brushes his shoulders off. He is confused, glancing around at the sights of the lonely suburban road. The only noticeable spectacle is his strip club behind him.] Rustin (Louder): What the hell! I remember the tournament. I remember losing in the finals. And I remember Chastity. [Rustin smiles remembering the brief but painful embrace they shared.] Rustin: I was that close to heaven. What the hell happened after New York Nightmare? [A voice from somewhere not noticeable comes to life.] Voice: I can answer that for you. [Rustin spins around and back to his original position. He is even more confused now, not knowing where the voice is coming from.] Voice: Hey stupid! Over here! [Rustin looks back again and now comes face to face with the speaker of the mysterious voice. The camera focuses on the speaker. The speaker is a man who is about 6'5 and 190 lbs. He is dressed in a business suit with some noticeable pins and such. The pins are army insignia.] Rustin: Who in the hell.. Man: Shut up! You want to know what happened to you after New York Nightmare? [Rustin nods in response, not wanting to get in to another verbal argument.] Man: Here’s what happened. You got all bent out of shape that you lost the tournament and Chastity “rejected” you that you gorged yourself at this friggin’ joint. [The man points back at the strip club.] Man: Must’ve been going back to your old ways that you eventually got thrown out of the club. Rustin (obviously taking offense): Hey listen here pal.. Man (cutting off Rustin): No you listen pal! You stay the hell away from Chastity. She doesn’t like you and she never will. I’m sick of your damn crazy and demented attempts to get her to like you. Rustin: NO! I like Chastity. Me and her are going to be together... Man: Bullshit! You’re a fucking sicko. Stick to your roots Slade and pay for your women. The only pussy you’ll be getting is the ones on the corner of 5th and Grider. [The man starts turning away, but Rustin runs at him and spins him around and goes for a punch. The militant man blocks the punch and connects with a punch of his own, sending Rustin sprawling back down to the pavement.] Man: Your professional wrestling bullshit doesn’t work on a first class army private asshole. Stick to your roots Slade, stick to your roots. [Rustin starts to get back up but is met with a kick to his side. There is a sickening crack heard as Rustin is sent back on the ground. Rustin clutches his ribs and sends up a cough which hurls up a ball of blood and bile.] Man: Like I said Slade....Stay the hell away from Chastity. [The man gives one more kick to the midsection of Rustin, causing Rustin to again roll around on the ground, and send up another bunch of blood. The man adjusts his tie and then turns around and leaves the scene. A couple walking by sees Rustin on the ground in immense pain and knocks wildly on the strip club door. The manager opens the door. The couple points at Rustin, and tell the manager to get help. The scene fades out as we see the ambulances arrive on the scene minutes later.]
