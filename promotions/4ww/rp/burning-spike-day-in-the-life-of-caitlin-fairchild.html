BURNING SPIKE - Day in the life of Caitlin Fairchild... Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #1003 Date: 10/11/04 Location: Kemper Arena - Kansas City [The 4WW logo blows across the screen as Caitlin Fairchild stands in her ring attire, with her VSE World title belt pacing back and forth trying to warm up before her match in a couple of minutes. She was in Kemper Arena - Kansas City, Mo doing a the Value Sports Entertainment show called SIN on Friday October 8th, from there she would have the Saturday off, and Sunday to prepare to travel to do the Monday show FUBAR at Rupp Arena - Lexington, KY October 11, 2004, where she would compete in a tag team battle royal elimination match for another shot at the VSE World Tag title at the following SIN, only to leave that same night to head to Winnipeg, Manitoba October 12th, 2004 at the Winnipeg Arena at the 4WW show Storm Front to compete in a 3 way match against the man who SPIT blood in her face Lee Satima, and the Hardcore Legend of the 4WW himself Chris West. The life of a wrestler involved a LOT of travel, very little private time, but right now Caitlin wasn’t thinking about that…right now she was concentrating getting ready to go out there an show the thousands in attendance, and the millions watching world-wide, why she was the World Champion. In the 4WW she knew she was considered a mid-card, but here…in the VSE, she was the World Champ, the queen, and she knew that soon enough, just like here, she would be the queen of the 4WW. Caitlin walked around holding her World title belt by the strap, gripping it hard; she combed her slicked back hair with her fingers, while taking deep breathes trying to overcome the panic attacks building up within her. Almost two and a half years of doing this, and she still got them; it would change the minute she hit the ring, and the bell rung, that was the only time she would be able to breath again. As Caitlin looked up at the lights taking another deep breath, Roxy runs up to her decked out in her purple body short suit, brown trademark jacket, and black combat boots.] Free Fall – Game time champ, ready? Caitlin Fairchild – Yeah…who’s going out first? Free Fall – You are tonight. Caitlin Fairchild – Ok…(Places the main plate of the World title around her stomach.) Could you strap this around me please? [Roxy goes behind Caitlin, taking the straps, and begins to snap them together locking the belt tightly around Caitlin’s curvy waist for a snug fit. Fairchild takes her long pink short manicured fingers grazing the main plate of the belt. It was still real, it was still hers, and tonight, she was still walking out with it, now it was time to make a statement.] Caitlin Fairchild – Let’s go. [Caitlin and Roxy walked side-by-side heading to the entrance of the ramp way as they get nods and thumbs up from ring attendance and staff all around. They stop standing in the darkness just before they hit the light. Waiting for her music, waiting for her announcement, waiting to hear the fans.] Rebecca Winters - This match is a Standard Match. On her way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 245 pounds, from Santa Barbara, California. She IS the Undisputed VSE World Heavyweight Champion, Caitlin Fairchild!!! (Crowd cheers *) [Beautiful by Creed blasts on the PA system sending shock waves all over the arena, and especially down Fairchild’s spine as she walks out into the light as the crowd goes ballistic. She walked to the ring with an a soft look of concentration, she was still a woman, and proud to be one, but she was the VSE World Champion, and her demeanor while heading to the ring showed that she deserved the respect of a World Champion, and the fans gladly gave it to her. Caitlin hopped on the apron, and dipped between the ropes getting in, as Roxy front baseball slid into the ring. Caitlin removed the World title from her waist walking over to a turnbuckle, and climbed the second rope holding the World title high as the crowd’s cheers nearly tore the roof off the arena. Free Fall jumped on the second ropes hyping the crowd as Fairchild went from turnbuckle to turnbuckle making it known that the World Champion was in the building. As her music died down, Roxy catapult leaped over the top rope to the Apron as Caitlin turned waiting for her opponent to come to the ring.] Rebecca Winters - and her opponent, weighing in at 265 pounds, from Boston, MA accompanied by Cheyenne Silver, Laface!!! [Deceit by Suffocation blares over the PA system, Laface comes out from behind the curtain with short blond hair, dark reflective sunglasses, black wrestling trucks with red lightning bolts down the side, by his side is his ring tag partner female VSE female wrestler Cheyenne Silver. Laface was veteran to the sport, never reaching the big time, out of the ring Caitlin respected his years of ring dedication, but the minute he stepped into the ring that would end; she knew by beating her he would make an impact, but she was looking to make an impact as well, one of the World Champion not being fucked with. Laface dived into the ring leaping to his feet removing his glasses tossing them to the side. His ice blue eyes locked with hers as she slowly reached over the top rope handing the VSE World Heavyweight title to Roxy. Caitlin stretched a bit, psyching herself up…waiting for the bell to ring. L.W. gives an autographed t-shirt to a lucky fan. (Ding, ding, ding) With out warning Caitlin rushes Laface grabbing him, bounces L.W. off the ropes; she runs hitting the opposite ropes, comes in as Laface came in for the rebound and power clotheslines him sending him crashing hard to the mat. Caitlin had the upper hand in the beginning of a now dazed Laface as she grabs him by the hair pulling him to his feet throws him into the corner, and stretches her long muscular leg boot choking him into corner. The referee begins the five count, at four Caitlin releases him. Laface slumps on the ropes catching his breath. ] The Crowd - OHHH OHH!! [Fairchild grabs LaFace’s arm, and irishwhips him to the ropes, but counters with an irishwhips on his won throwing her off the ropes and hitting her with a diving spear. Caitlin hit the mat with a hard impact clutching the back of her skull, which was now stinging, and her stomach (now without any air in it) felt like it got hit with a baseball bat. She cursed herself in her mind for being so stupid, going for the same move twice as Laface already up grabbed her by her long red mane pulling her to her feet, crouches low grabbing her right left, hoists her 245 pound frame into the air, and flapjacks her hard driving her face first into the mat. Roxy winces at the hard impact move Caitlin, which has her friend clutching her face proving how unforgiving the mat actually was. Now Laface back on his feet now confident he has the match under control L.W. grabs Caitlin by hair again pulling her to her vertical base. Caitlin looks as if she about to drop to the mat again as Laface spins around her catching her around her waist only to nail a belly-to-back suplex sending her up and over crashing onto the back of her head. Fairchild could feel the cobwebs forming as she flipped face first onto the mat. It was apparent the World Champion was in serious trouble, and the challenger was in control, not that this was a title match, but Fairchild knew a loss like this would send shockwaves throughout the federation that she was slipping, giving challengers to her throne the extra edged they needed to knock her off the mountain. All eyes were on her and this match as a confident Laface grabbed her by her hair again yanking her up. Her scalp was on fire from the constant pulling as her forced her to her feet backing her up against the ropes, before nailing her across the chest with a hard knife chop, which was not only very painful but a total violation on her person in her mind. Laface irishwhips Caitlin to the opposite ropes, while bouncing off the opposite ropes coming in for a gore, but Caitlin Fairchild not looking to be a victim wraps her long muscular arms around the tope and second ropes ending her momentum, and while Laface comes in charging like a freight train, she releases the ropes executing a hop, then clocks Laface with a super sidekick right in the mush! The impact sends a shockwave from Fairchild’s leg causing her teeth to rattle as the crowd erupts from the counter move, while Laface stands fully erect with a now bloodied nose looking up at the ceiling lights, slowly he teeters ready to fall like an oak, but not before a now determined and pissed off rushes him again nailing him with a vicious kick to the gut, cinches him in between her legs, grabs him by the waist, and hoists him into the air across her right shoulder allowing him to slide down a bit as she locks his arms in a crucifix position. Caitlin kicks her legs out from underneath her finishing off the Black Tiger Bomb, driving the back of Laface’s skull onto the mat, folding him in half this time. The devastating move made famous by Razor Ramon shakes the ring getting the fans to their feet ripping the roof off the arena with their cheers.] Bob Taylor – One hell of a counter by the World Champion followed by a VICIOUS Black Tiger Bomb!! Fairchild has regained control of the match! [Caitlin gets to her feet first as limping a bit from the shock waves of the impact counter kick she executed as she grabs Laface’s head with two hands bringing him to his feet. She goes to nail him with another kick to the gut, but Laface still with some gas left in the tank catches her leg, what he didn’t count on was the unbelievable agility of the 7 foot Amazon as she jumps up but hitting a hard enzuigiri to the back of Laface's head sending the sending the big man into a 360 spin crashing on his back!] The Crowd - OHHH OHH!! [Caitlin scrambles to her feet crashing against the top rope almost falling to the outside, as Laface roll over getting to his knees. The big man now in trouble runs stumbling trying to go for a clothesline, but Caitlin sees that his speed not cut in ½ from the damage she’s done charges meeting him in the center of the ring, with a shoulder charge stopping his charge doubling him over. Caitlin hoists Laface up and over hitting a belly-to-belly releasing suplex sending into a somersault crashing on the back of his head, and neck again. The crowd erupts again at the Kurt Angle type maneuver, as Laface moving on instinct stumbles to his feet, Fairchild walks over to him nailing him again with another kick to the gut taking the wind out of him as she cinches him into a front headlock with her right arm, while reaching grabbing his right left with her left arm locking him into a cradle. Caitlin digs in showing the world that she is the Queen of the Suplexes as she snaps him up, and drives him onto the spine of his back with a cradle suplex. The crowd erupts as again as Caitlin holds it for the bridge as the referee Sarah Robbins counts. ...1 ...2…KICK OUT at ½. Caitlin rolls to her feet feeling the momentum and cheer of the crowd, as a now dazed Laface also rolls to his feet with the look of a zombie, Fairchild knew from then it was time to go for the kill as she ducked behind him catching his neck with her right arm, and his left leg with her left arm. It was cocky to do, but Caitlin Fairchild winked to the crowd as she spun Laface out of Argentinean over the shoulder position while sitting out hitting the neckbreaker a.k.a the BURNING SPIKE, but as the World Champion, you learn that you can be a little cocky especially when you have everything under control. Caitlin sending that Laface was out cold on the mat, turns and dives rolling him over, and cinches his leg for the pin. Sarah Robbins counts. ...1 ...2 ...3] Joey Spray - What a great contest! The World Champion comes out victorious! Rebecca Winters - The winner of this match, Caitlin Fairchild!!! [Beautiful by Creed blasts on the PA system sending shock waves all over the arena once again, as Roxy cheering Caitlin on hands her VSE World title, she walks over to a nearby turnbuckle climbing the second rope holding the title high as the fans cheer her on, after this she would venture to other shows other venues to do this all over again, to prove that she deserved to be in that right, but tonight she had indeed proven, that she was the World Champion, and that it was her ring.] Bob Taylor - OH NO!!!! Joey Spray - HE'S HERE!!!! CAITLIN!!!! [A Spotlight shines up into the top section of the seats as her long time rival, now in another federation Scott “The Game” Smith is stood amongst the fans with the Platinum Edition of the UWN World title over his shoulder he had captured a couple of days ago in the promotion he had been competing in. Caitlin locks eyes with him as the spotlight causes the Platinum title to gleam as the diamonds sparkle. The arena goes dark as a spotlight now focuses on Caitlin, while one focuses on Smith. Caitlin looked down at a now nervous Free Fall, then back at Smith with a defiant look on her face as she holds up the VSE Gold World title and the Ruby's sparkle. They both lock eyes on each other As Smith raises the Platinum UWN World title in the air. The camera switches to a split screen close up of Caitlin's face as her eyes narrow and Smith's face as his eyes narrow. Scott Smith had to leave the VSE to get his first World title, because when he tried to come for Fairchild’s she stopped him in his tracks. Seeing him, as champion didn’t faze Caitlin, it only solidified that no one was going to knock her off her throne, and it didn’t matter where she went, VSE or 4WW…champion or no champion…she had proven that she was one of the best of the best, and one step closer to becoming a legend. The 4WW logo blows across the screen as a final shot of Caitlin holding her championship World title high as she continues to stare the other World Champion down as the scene fades to black. ]
