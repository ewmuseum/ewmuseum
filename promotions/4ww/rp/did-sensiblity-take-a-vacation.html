Did Sensiblity Take A Vacation? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #95 Date: Sunday, August 17, 2003 Location: The Ranch sensibility • noun 1 : ability to receive sensations : sensitiveness 2 : the emotion or feeling of which a person is capable 3 : refined or excessive sensitiveness in emotion and taste. From the looks of things, it looks like sensiblity left the entire group of bookers this week. This little action is causing Destiny to bang her head on the wall several times. Fate took a step back, a fit of giggles taking over her form. Why would Fey put the 'Darling of the 4WW' in a match with someone who pretty much protected her from the 'Psycho-stalker' in the form of Pajaro de Pasa. Maybe Patti should have done something more then just sit on the top turnbuckle and file her nails. Maybe she should have hopped off that turnbuckle, got into the action, then went back and sat down. Just kepted it up throughout the match. Destiny guesses that pissed off the boss. My guess is Fey didnt like it and offered Psymon the chance to redeem himself. Oh wait, Fey must not have gotten the memo. Psymon doesn't want the title. Destiny sits back and looks over her notes on Psymon. His bio states he murdered his parents and sister, did drugs, wound up in the psycho ward of the hospital. To her and to Fate, it looks like Mr. Kreed lost his sensibility somewhere along the way. In Destiny's opinion, this week won't be any different. She'll send her darling girl into battle, defend the title, and let her walk away with it once again. Fate, will sit quietly on the outside of it all, watching and waiting for her suprise to continue to work its magic. _______________________________________________________________________________________________ Place: The Ranch Room: In the Kitchen, at the table Time: 11:00 am Saturday, August 16, 2003 _______________________________________________________________________________________________ Fade into Patti sitting at the kitchen table, papers spread out all over the top of the table. She's dressed in a plain tshirt and white socks cover her feet. A cup of breakfast tea sits to her right, a half eaten bagel sits to her left. Patt's left foot swung in frustration as she stared at the piles of papers that were upon the kitchen table. All requiring her to review, sign, or needed forwarded to her attorney. One pile contained requests for product endorsements or personal appearances. Someone somewhere wanted the 'Darling of the 4WW' to be in appearance, or wanted both 'The Irish Bastard' and her to appear. She sighed as she set another one on top of that ever growing list. The pile next to that one, held the papers she had already signed or reviewed. She figured anything that came as a request from her homeland were good enough to go ahead and endorse. She had a sneaky feeling Fey might be happy over huge revenue from the Austriallian public. The more fans she generated in Austrilla, the better chance that Fey would start liking her. Nah, Ian would be more pleased with the financial popularity of Patti then Fey would. Patti sighed as her hazel gaze landed on the third pile. A pile full of opened letters. Letters from a woman claiming to be her biological mother. A woma, who until a week ago, failed to exist. A woman, Patti intended to keep out of her life. Why was this woman claiming that she, not Melinda Anderson was her mother? And to claim that a member of the Four Winds Roster was her half sibling? Even better, why would the DCFS send a letter to her about this matter and not to the person she is supposedly related to on the roster? If all of this was true, then why did this woman wait almost 26 years to come want contact? Is the feelings of guilt that strong? What about this person that she clains is her sibling? Does he or she know that I exist? Patti shrugs it off and moves the letters. After a few minutes, she opts to put the letters in the pile to be sent to the attorney. Let him figure out this mess. She had more important things to deal with then a 'psycho stalker'. Her attorney was better equipped to deal with matters like this, then her. Sean won't worry so much about it this way. Patti sighed again, as her favorite song came on the television. Patti had forgotten Sean had turned it on for background noise. She got up from the table, grabbing the remote in the process. She walks over to the couch and plops down, her back a little sore from having the added stress of this confusion to her circle. Her sensiblity was about to take a walk out the door, when the television flipped into her favorite song. Patti: Matchbox Twenty couldn't have said it any better about someone being unwell, because this woman is bloody well nuts. Hmm, maybe Psymon should change that to his theme too. As the end of the video ran, Patti decided to do some channel surfing. Nothing wrong with music she thought, but she needed something to make her laugh. It was saturday, there had to be something else on. She pulled up the guide to get a better idea. She noticed a couple shows, she used to watch were being broadcasted in the states, a girlish smile appearing on her face. Patti starts to giggle as the theme music to Pokemon comes blaring out of the surround sound speakers. Sean McClancy enters the room as he hears music related to a child's cartoon show. Sean: Wot the fok are ye laughing at? Patti: Pokemon. Used to watch it in me room before I went to work. Patti burts out laughing as a yellow pikachu comes bouncing across the screen. Patti: Oh bloody hell, never realized they used Psymon as a model for Pikachu. Oh and look, they have Jamie and Syxx Figures too. Sean looks at Patti all confused. His first thought was that the love of his life was loosing her sensiblity over things. He noticed the way she wa curled up on the couch. Her smooth long legs curled up under her as she layed with her head on the pillow. Her long red hair wrapped around her face, framing it in an angel type view. Patti layed there continuing to watch the cartoon show on the television. Every once in a while she would smile at something Pika was doing or something that the bad guys were doing. Sean walked over and sat down on the couch next to Patti. He pulled her legs onto his lap, rubbing them softly. Patti rolled over onto her back and stared at the man she loved. A smile reappered. He was always around when she was feeling down or upset about something. The last person who was like that now lived over 3000 miles away from her, and she missed him terribly. The phone call the week she won the South Wind title made her miss him that much more, she just wished she could see him, or even her mother for that matter. Patti rolled her head back to the television and continued to watch the episode of Pokemon. Her smile returning to her face. She started to giggle. Between the antics of Team Rocket and the antics of Pikachu, she had reason to smile. Patti: You know, love, I think the rest of the roster is right about Psymon. He does look like a pokemon type character, except he's not cute enough to be pikachu. You know though it would be a very successful gimmick for him should he decide to do it. Sean smiled at Patti's sense of humor. It always showed through when she was upset or trying to deal with something bad. He knew the last few weeks had been rough. Her constant title defenses, personal appearances, radio/television intervies, and her training in the gym was beginning to wear thin on her. He knew something beyond all of this was bothering her, and it had something to do with the letters that kept coming in the mail every day. He noticed Patti had fallen asleep on the couch. Her head resting comfortably on the pillow. She must have fallen asleep from the simple leg rub he was giving her. He reached over onto the back of the couch, pulled the blanket down and let her sleep. He noticed a lone tear had started to fall; she must have started to cry as she fell asleep. Sean walked over to the kitchen table where the papers were that she had been working on. He looked for the pile that had the letters from the woman claiming to be Patti's mom. He pulled out the first one, looked at it, amd replaced it. He shrugged his shoulders. Maybe the attorney would know what to do about it. Sean walks over to the refridgerator and grabs a bottle of water from inside. He removes the top and takes a long healthy swig. He just wished he knew what to do with it all. Patti stirred a little on the couch but didnt wake up. He walked over to the couch and looked down at her sleeping form. The remote control was now laying on the floor, the hand that held it was now curled up along side her face, her lips slightly parted almost like she was getting kissed within a dream. He didn't have the heard to distrub her. The more rest she got, the better her mental stablity would be when she takes on Psymon on Sunday. Sean started to head off to the basement no sense in wasting some good work out time. Just as he passed the front door, a light knock was heard. Sean grumbled as he opened the door. A petite form greated him. A woman with long blonde hair greeted him with a perfect smile. Her eyes a pretty shade of blue stares back into his. Woman: May I please speak to Patti please? Fade out.
