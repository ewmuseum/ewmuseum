For sin was a the face of every person, there was not one being who lived a life completely free of sin and Darek knew this. His mission was impossible to complete, but it was something he was willing to do for the love of his lord and for the pride of his actions. He felt the same for the Four Winds, he was now destined to meet with Patti Anderson and Kyo Kushinaga for the coveted North Wind title. He would pride himself in his work, but the mission seemed endlessly impossible... But he had to try, after all there was no honour in not trying. If he had suceeded in making it as though Amanda had never died in the first place, he knew that anything was possible. The hatred he once felt no longer dwelled in his troubled soul, instead he only felt joy as he had the love of his lost wife back. She was troubled by darkness and Darek himself had changed more than she could ever imagine. With the butterfly effect, saving Amanda from certain Danger, prevented Xantos from ever robbing the gun dealership and ceased his death from ever happening as well. When the truth had been discovered, Xantos was realised as the true muderous monster in the family. His blood, his anger, it all flowed freely in the blood of his daughter Raven. Everything was piecing together as the larger puzzle known as the future came together for both Darek and William. Life as Darek knew it had already begun to change. He had Amanda back, he had Raven, he had passed the second of the eight trials. A feeling as though he was indestructable.
