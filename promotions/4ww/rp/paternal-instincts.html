Paternal Instincts

19 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paternal Instincts Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1117 Date: 12/19/04 Location: New York City, NY [The camera slowly fades into an empty arena, somewhere in NY. A wrestling ring is perched in the center of the arena, with about fifteen chairs circled around it. The only light in the arena is one that is dangling above the ring. Silence fills the arena. The camera then picks up some barely audible footsteps coming from somewhere nearby. The camera pans the arena, until we find the source of the footsteps. Rustin Slade is slowly walking down the aisle and towards the ring. He comes up to the ring and slides in underneath the bottom rope. Rustin bounds off the ropes doing a somersault on his way back. Rustin runs towards the turnbuckle and extends a leg on his way into it making a mafia kick motion.] [Minutes later there is another set of footsteps coming down the aisle. The camera pans along to get a back shot of a man wearing some sort of business suit making his way to the ring. Rustin, hearing the footsteps turns around and his eyes go wide. The man in the business suit steps up on the ring apron and then ducks through the ropes. The camera’s angle shifts so we now get a better view of the man’s face. We see that the man is none other than Edward Slade, the father of Rustin. He slowly walks towards his son who is walking towards his father also. They meet in the center of the ring face to face] Rustin: Pops! You came! [Edward Slade fires a left hand at his son, connecting right on top of Rustin’s head. Rustin hits the mat hard. Rustin, shocked rolls towards the ropes, but Edward grabs his son by the head and picks him up. He quickly follows up with a body slam. Edward bounds off the ropes and attempts to drop a leg across Rustin’s throat, but Rustin sees it coming and rolls out of the way, making Ed fall hard on his leg. Rustin gets up.] Rustin: Dad, what are you doing? [Edward gets back up and attempts a running lariat, which is ducked by Rustin. Rustin then gets into a collar-elbow tie up with his father. While they’re locked up, Rustin tries to understand what his father is doing.] Edward: Nothing like some Father-Son bonding, eh champ? Rustin: Dad, I don’t get it. Edward: You never did Rustin. [Edward whips Rustin into the ropes and catches Rustin on the rebound with a spinning back elbow smash. Edward then grabs his son and ties him up in a sleeperhold] Rustin: (Gasping for air) Dad, this is a little extreme don’t you think. Edward: And what you did to me and your mother wasn’t? Rustin: [Starts standing up with his father, as he still has the sleeperhold applied. Rustin tries to wriggle free but his father keeps the hold applied strong] You don’t understand either Dad. [Rustin hits some elbows in his father’s stomach, making him release the sleeper. Rustin runs into the ropes and comes off hitting a flipping lariat on his back knocking his father to the mat] I never wanted to hurt you or Mom. [Rustin attempts to drop the elbow but Edward moves out of the way. They both stand up again and once again start exchanging left and rights.] Edward: [Edward hits a right.] Failure! Rustin: [Rustin hits a left] Tyrant! Edward: [Edward hits a left] Traitor! Rustin: [Rustin hits a right] Controller! [Edward tries to hit another right but Rustin blocks it and kicks Edward in the midsection] Rustin: Wrestling is my life now Dad! [Rustin ties his father left arm in between his legs and holds it with his hand.] Rustin: You’re just gonna have to accept it. [Rustin hooks his father’s other arm underneath his shoulder, so he now is in pumphandle position.] Rustin: I [Rustin now lifts up his father a little into the air] Rustin: LOVE [Rustin now switches his pumphandle into a sitdown uranage as he sits down he screams his last word.] Rustin: WRESTLING! [Rustin goes over and quickly hooks his father’s leg. Rustin slams hs hand down onto the amt for the three count. Rustin rolls off and his father, dazed but now no longer confused slowly comes to and stares his son into the eye.] Rustin: There I said it. I’m sorry Dad... Edward: No! I’m sorry Rustin. This is obviously your dream. I can’t say it’s mine, but I’ve a better understanding of it now. I had a dream when I was your age too. Your grandpa didn’t let me follow it. Instead I did what he wanted me too. Who would have thought that me and your grandfather would end up being exactly alike. [Edward gets up and exits the ring. Edward starts for the door. Rustin runs to the edge of the ring and calls out to him] Rustin: Hey Pops! [Edward turns around and looks back at his son in the ring] Rustin: Thanks for one hell of a match! Edward: [winks] Make me proud at Maelstrom son. [Edward walks out of the arena leaving Rustin in the ring, alone, with a whole new respect for his father. Rustin sits back against a nearby turnbuckle, catching his breath.] Rustin: Damn where’d he learn to wrestle like that? [Shrugs] Must be in the blood! [The light of the arena goes out as the camera slowly fades to black.]
