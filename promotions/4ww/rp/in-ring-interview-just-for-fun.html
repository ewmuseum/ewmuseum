In Ring Interview *just for fun*

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In Ring Interview *just for fun* El Lobo de la Muerta 4WW Exposures #130 Date: 9-13-03 Location: House Show {The scene opens up as we witness the ending of a fantastic match. As they sit there and wait for the next match the lights go out and the familiar wolf howl is heard as Praise by Sevendust hits the speakers. El Lobo makes his way out from the back with Psymon right by his side. El Lobo looks out to the crowd making a suicide mocking sign with his hands as barbed wire dangles from his hands. Psymon holds his hands up with a croquet mallet high above his head. Psymon wearing a pair of baggy jeans with an old black shirt with a Darksider shirt with a few holes in it. El Lobo wearing his classic black, white and red army pants with an old black hoody with the Darksider insignia on the back and Darksiders name in blood red on the front. They slide into the ring and walk around for a minute. Before El Lobo pulls a mic out from his hoody pocket.} El Lobo: Let's see tomorrow I take on the likes of this Insane Chris West in a luchacore match for the Nightmare title. Psymon: Yea Nightmare is sooo your bag. Trust me I fought you a few times in those luchacore style matches. El Lobo: Yea... this is gonna be so much fun hombre. Cristofer Oeste I seen your pequeño promo esse. You look terrefied about sumsing esse... Psymon: I think it's a good thing too man. He's fearin you man, and we all know where there's fear there's respect. El Lobo: Yea he's gonna respect this after I tear him a new hole to shit out of. I see how he's looking para amigos, and how he is terrefied of us man. Psymon: As well he should be, he thinks he can get away with what he did. Revenge is gonna be sweet Tom it's gonna be sweet indeed. He best be watchin' his back because like a wolf pack we'll hunt him down just like we'll take care of Summers tomorrow. And trust me Lobo I'll be using this croquet mallet a hell of a lot. El Lobo: Just as I plan on using my secret weapon to dispense of the so called insane one. Thats why I've come out here to show you fans a few pictures that Psymon and I have dug up. Now I'm asking that if you are under the age of dies y siete... Psymon: That means seventeen... El Lobo: ... Please turn your head because these pictures are very disturbing. Psymon: I found this one in the garbage outside the 4WW arena so lets take a look... {We go to the movie tron where we see an altered picture of a female body with her hair being pulled back as she is beeing butt fucked. The faces have been replace by none other than Chris West and Sean Summers. The crowd erupts in chears and laughter as they look at the picture.} El Lobo: That is really disturbing but I found another one inside National Geographic... {The picture then switches to two elephants mating in the middle of the African Desert. Their heads replaced by those of none other than Chris West and Sean Summers. It looks as if Sean is screaming in pain as he rams it into the posteriour of West who seams to be in pleasure. Theres another loud rush of chears and laughter as the picture is set in freeze.} Psymon: Good God and I thought my mind was disturbing but this sure does break the ice. But Lobo I've found one thats rather intruiging. I can actually say that our opponets have trained hard for their fights... {The picture then switches to two sumo wrestlers in the middle of a match. Obviously doctered up seeing that the faces of Sean and Chris on the large men. Once again a large pop is heard from the crowd.} Psymon: But wait, you ask they're fat in that picture... El Lobo: This is how they lost all that bad fat and made their homosexual energy go through the roof... {The picture changes to two large females working on Tae Bo. Their faces once again replaced by Summers and West. The crowd going begins going nuts as they look at the picture.} Psymon: But the real motivational kicker of them is this right here... {The picture changes once again. This time we see two nude males inside a gym. One standing up with an erect penis while the other is doing situps about to reach the erect penis with his mouth open. Another uproar of laughter is heard.} El Lobo: Last week I heard that Sean called you a poufter... Psymon: That is correct but as we've seen all night it looks that Sean and West are the, how you say, AFEMMINATORS. El Lobo: But with all jokes aside we have one last picture to show the loving happy side of those two love birds. {The picture changes to see Sean and West's faces doctored onto that of happy parents. Their child replaced by the leprechaun from the movie The Leprechaun. They crowd erupts in chears and laughter once again.} Psymon: Awwww isn't that just precious. Look at that happy family. El Lobo: I know it almost makes me wanna cry. {The two begin laughing hard as they continue to get a huge pop from the crowd. They exit the ring and walk to the back as they laugh the entire way back. Almost to the point where they fall down. We take a look at the crowd and see them crying from laughing so hard.}
