Destiny puts her plan in motion Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #23 Date: July 5, 2003 Location: Websters dictionary defines destiny as a) something to which a person or thing is destined: fortune b) a predetermined course of events often held to be an irresistible power or agency...also see fate. And that is exactly what has happened to Patti Anderson and Sean McClancy. Destiny had brought these two together. She, nothing more than a fan of the 'Irish Bastard'. Him, a man of intense good looks and passion, wanted by women everywhere. And as in all cases, destiny had something else planned. Destiny had felt that these two people needed to be brought together, as destiny has always done. Always having its hand in whatever it can find. But Destiny can be a cruel mistress also. Look at what she has done. She fated that the god Eros would fall in love with the Princess Psyche. They did, and her love and fear of him almost caused her to kill him. Now Destiny was working on new fates for people. Destiny brought Ian Childress and Fey McClancy together after years of being apart, and now she was weaving her magic spell around Sean McClancy and Patti Anderson. Destiny was also working on bringing two people close together that couldnt stand to be around each other. Things are going to get complicated. Ian Childress takes a wild stab at opening his own federation. A federation based out of his own schools The Four Winds Academy, the 4WW. A place to see new faces and old favorites. Syxx Figures, Ritoru Joufu, Fey McClancey-Childress. The Irish Bastard, Sean McClancy will appear but not the way his fans are used to seeing him.. That's right fans, the man is going to be working as a manager.....his client...his own girlfriend has chosen to step into the ring and battle along side her champion. Should make for some interesting fireworks among other things. A move that has caused more fuel to be thrown on the fire. Oh yes, Fey and Patti...destiny has alot in store for the two of them. A plot so evil and so twisted that it could lead the two enemies down the path to becoming something that could possibly be good, or possibly be wrong. Destiny was playing a game now. Ever since Patti had won the Number One Fan Contest, Fey had disliked Patti, especially when she found out that her brother and this woman had already had a relationship that was secretly going on underneath her very nose. But now the real truth would soon be coming to the surface and destiny would once again have her hands in it. ..........AND SO IT BEGINS La Cruses, New Mexico....... Inside the new home of Patti Anderson and Sean McClancy Morning Hours Its a nice house, a love nest for two people. Sean McClancy had chosen well when he picked up this house for the love of his life. Patti fell in love with it the minute she had seen it, and practically begged Sean to buy it for her. She wanted normalicy; she wanted to feel as if she belonged somewhere once again. The sun comes up over the mountains and shines in on the living room. Boxes are scene stacked all over the place. Some are opened items thrown over the side of the boxes. Next to the boxes is a pile of clothes, looks like a combination of his/hers. Pictures of a couple in love are on the fireplace mantel. Items belonging to both can be found all over the place. Entering the building, we hear sounds of a shower running in the background. A silhouette shows a male form in the shower. Camera pans around the room and spies the woman the man is in love with, Patti Anderson. Patti is seen sitting at a table. She is wearing one of Sean's shirts and a pair of sweatpants. Her shoulder length copper hair ruffled and untamed. Another wild night from the looks of it. She sits on the stool, one leg tucked underneath her, the other dangling off to the side. To the side of her is a cup of english breakfast tea, in her hand a spoon in a constant stirring motion. In the other looks to be a letter. Her eyes scan the pages over and over again. A single lonely tear rolls down her cheek. Seam steps out of the shower and hears a sniffle coming from the other room. He quickly throws on a towel, ties it around his waist. He steps out of the bathroom and stares at Patti's back. He still couldn’t believe his good fortune. The fates had been smiling down on him when he brought this loving and caring woman into his life. He walks over as to where she is sitting, standing behind her, he places a hand gently on her shoulder. He saw she was reading a letter but couldn’t make out the gibberish that was in the letter. He started to massage her shoulders when he noticed her hand wiping a tear away. Sean: Wha's wrong? Patti: Nothing love, just a letter from my father, and feeling homesick.. Sean: ‘E still na’ thrilled with yer choice now? Patti: No he's not, he's bloody irate that I would chase after what I want. Now he thinks I’m insane because I chose to stay with you and lead this trelos life style as he calls it Sean: So wha’ is new a’ ‘ome? Anythin’ o’ interest? He knew Patti was having a difficult time handling the change from being in one place to constant movement from place to place. The letters from home helped her out, at least she was able to keep in touch with her friends and her family. Especially her family. He knew she didn’t get a chance to say goodbye. The Order was very strict on being ready to leave when they came knocking. Sean remembered the tears Patti shed the second night after they left her homeland. He felt his heart break as the tears soaked the front of his shirt, he knew she was missing her family, he felt the same way about his. He didn’t think he would have survived the years on the road without his sister and younger brothers. He knew enough about Patti's past to know she was very close to her father. He knew she had a younger sibling that was killed tragically during a family vacation in Perth. The accident have left her mother mentally unstable and unable to care for her family. Her father had no choice but to place her in an institution. Patti put the letter down and got up off her stool, and turned into the arms of the one man that truely loved her. She wrapped her arms around him, sighed, and took a big nip at his chest. Sean: Hey...watch wha’ yer doin’. Yer gonna take something off o’ value. He proceeded to tug on her hair a little to return the favor. Patti goes for revenge and sweeps his leg out from under him. Sean lands with a thud on the floor. Patti falls on top of him, her hair hangs down over her face. Sean reaches up and pushes it back behind her ears. Sean: I have a feelin’ tha’ I’m gunna regret teachin’ ye ‘ow t’ wrestle. Patti: But it makes the set up for sex that much more fun. Can ya say grudge match, love? Both start to giggle. Sean's free hand reaches down and starts tugging at the waistband of her sweat pants. Sean: Ye know those are me clothes yer wearin’? Patti: Ya want them love, then ya'll just have to take them off of me. They both lock in a passionate embrace. They break the embrace, looking into each other’s eyes. Silent words pass between the two of them. They continue to stare at each other, believing the connection that was felt the moment they met. Fade out Fade back in Patti's outfit is seen lying on the floor along to what looks like to be Sean's towel. Their bodies seem to be content curled up on the couch. Patti lets out a sigh and runs her fingers in lazy circles on Sean's chest. Sean plays with a lock of her hair. Sean: We should get up and do more training? Sean untangles himself from around the girl he loves, and bends down to pick up his towel. Patti pouts and follows him. She slips back on the clothes that are lying on the floor, and reaches for her hairbrush. Patti: Love, ya just got to be a pain in the arse on wanting to get this training done don’t ya? Sean cocks an eyebrow and breaks out in his boyish grin. Sean: I'll show ye a pain in th’ arse later. As the two continue getting dressed the doorbell rings. Sean looks out the window and sees his sister Fey standing at the door, looks to be an envelope in her hand. Sean opens the door. Sean: Wha's up Maeb? Patti and I were just abou’ t’ ‘ead down to the basement t’ work out. Fey looks at Sean and then over to Patti. From the looks of things the two had been working out, but from the looks of the couch and Patti's hair, they weren’t exercising. Fey: I've come t’ drop off some papers. They just need a signature on ‘em an’ then I can file ‘em. Sean assuming the papers are for him, takes them from his sister, and hands them off to Patti. She proceeds to place them on the counter top. Fey looks at Patti then at her brother. Fey: I suggest ye don’na take to long abou’ looking at them. I need them back soon. Patti: Don’cha be worrin’, ye'll get them back after Sean and I do our workout. Fey threw an annoyed look at a Patti. Fey knew that her revenge was coming soon. And it would be sweet. Sean gave his sister a hug, then quickly gets a pair of shorts on. He takes the opportunity to walk her out to the car. After a brief exchange, he returned to the house. Sean walks over to the papers, opens the envelope. The papers inside are official. A form letter from the desk of Fey McClancy-Childress at the top. Sean scans the letter, the papers slip from his hands and drop to the floor. Sean: FOK!!!! Patti looks at Sean with a look of concern Patti: What love? Sean: The paperwork tha’ Fey sent over.... its a contract fer you......she is offerin’ ye t’ sign ye t’ a contract with th’ 4 Winds. Patti's face drops. First a look of shock then pure outrage.... Patti: WHAT?!?! Bloody hell will I wrestle for your sister. FADE TO BLACK
