Someone Gets Hurt

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Someone Gets Hurt Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #475 Date: 01/25/04 Location: House Show in Vegas Place: House Show in Las Vegas Time: After a Summers match [Fade in to Eliza is standing just inside the current waiting for Sean Summers to return from his match. She paces a little nervously. She hasn't seen him since the first Maelstrom and she's still a little afraid of him. Summers steps through the current, his cocky attitude very apparent to her.] Eliza: Nice match out there Sean. You still look good. [Summers stops dead in his tracks as he inwardly cringes at the sound of Eliza Murphy's voice. He stares right at her.] Sean: When have I ever cared what you thought? Eliza feels the sting of his remark, but manages to shake it off.] Eliza: You used to care, but that's not the point. I just wanted to let you know that I've moved on and you won't have to worry about me interfering in your matches. Sean: Whoop Dee Do. Have you ever thought for even once second that your interfering in my matches was your true downfall? Sticking your nose where it didn't belong Eliza: Is it my fault that you gave me confusing directions? You told me that you wanted me at ringside at the crucial moments of your match. Sean: What? Are you delusional little girl...I've never once asked, nor would I ever ask for help from you. [Eliza crosses her arms and stares at the man she had once loved] Eliza: No delusions here. I should have known you would still deny everything to this day. [Sean strokes at his goatee as he chuckles] Sean: Have you ever heard the term, 'Thinking With Your Dick'? Of course you do I mean why else would any other human being accused themselves with you. Being on the road for that long deprives people, I needed a little something. [Without thinking, Eliza reaches out and slaps Summers across the face. A slap that barely even stung the arrogance of Summers. Eliza steps back as she sees the lighting bolts appear in Summers eyes.] Eliza: You know, I at one time thought the world of you, but I guess Patti and Tyler both were right. You are nothing but a pathetic loser and deserve everything that happens to you. Sean: Did you just lay your hands on me? Your lucky I don't just end every little thing you have right here and now. Open up YOUR eyes...fuck what Patti says. She cares for you about as much as I do, she used you as her little pawn in her game to get at me, she failed, as did you. Any comment coming from your lips is worth about as much as the dirt on the bottom of my boots, you’re the Pathetic Loser who is blinded by the world around her due to your personality being that of a five year old. [Eliza stands her ground. Her southern temper beginning to flair a little.] Eliza: No! You need to open your eyes. You are so full of yourself that you can't even see the real truth. You're a loser. Nobody will every want you the way that I did. I highly doubt even the prostitutes here in Vegas would lower themselves to even think of entertaining a night with you. Just grow the hell up and realize that you just used me to get closer to Patti. [All Sean can do is laugh at the ridiculous comments from Eliza.] Sean: Is that all you got? Loser? I know that your vocabulary isn't as sophisticated as others due to you being such a twit and all, but come on, tell me something I haven't heard before. Unlike you I don't care what others think about me, I don't let petty words from petty people get the best of me. I may not be where I want to be here in the 4WW but it's all coming to me now. Without you being the brainwashed little whore that you are I would have wiped the floor with every damn wrestler I stepped into the ring with. You’re the reason I'm down here, fighting no names like James Slade. But to actually think I want to get closer to that little red head is just obscured. Without her 'Boy' McClancy she isn't much more than an annoyance around here, I'm sick of hearing her talk, sick of watching her walk, hell she's about to fall to hell and back. As for the prostitutes here in Vegas, they only wish they could entertain me for a night. [Eliza refuses the urge to slap him again, for he seems to enjoy the abuse. Meanwhile, Tyler Blaze hears the voices of his love and Sean Summers arguing, he peeks around the corner and watches. He doesn’t notice Patti Anderson standing behind him. A smirk on her face.] Patti: Bloody Hell!! What does she think she’s doing? [Tyler slaps a hand over Patti’s mouth, he wants to hear what is being said.] Eliza: Oh whatever Sean. Care to explain the times you called out Patti's name in the heat of passion? Or the countless hours you sat in the backstage watching her and Sean interacting? Oh wait, there is no explainsion for it at all Summers. The only thing you care about is waiting for Sean to leave Patti alone long enough so that you could have your chance with her. Every wrestler and staff member here in the back KNOW that you are hot for Patti. She is the one that consumes you. You are as stubborn as a mule caught in the mud to realize that. Sean: See that's what's wrong with the world today, they always go deep into someones emotions and feelings. Well sorry to burst everyone's bubble but I don't have to many feelings to go around...I watch out for my boys in Featured Attraction, and myself. So many men are caught up in 'Love' that it makes me want to vomit, the world went to shit the day women decided they could enjoy sex to. Women are here to keep us occupied, to cook us dinner, and to straight up shut the hell up and look good. Stop living in your fantasy world of Love and Feelings. [Eliza shakes her head at the stupidly of Sean Summers and wonders why she ever fell for him.] Eliza: Sean, you are just pathetic. I'm so glad I ended things with you when I did. The male population would be much better off without you in it. Your ego and attitude will be your downfall. I am just glad that I cost you all those damn matches. You don't deserve to ever hold a title in this fed. [Sean applauds the comments he had just heard from Eliza, his face forming his traditional cocky smile. Sean: Ego? Attitude? No you see those are two things in my life which will project me to the top so damn quickly it will make your head spin. You didn't end a damn thing, forth and foremost, it never started to begin with. That's what happens when your deprived of a good man all your life and when one does come around that actually speaks to you, you feel you must latch on forever. Why don't you go hold Tyler in a corner and tell him about all your problems, he seems to enjoy listening to your pointless jabbering, for whatever reason. [Eliza slaps Sean again.] Eliza: At least Tyler's more of a man than you'll ever be. [Sean puts his hand on his cheek, but he is still smiling.] Sean: No, you see he isn't. You say that because your probably able to pussy whip that bitch, well I don't give into a womans word, because all woman lie, and never have anything interesting to say. [Eliza starts to laugh, a almost musical sound coming from her.] Eliza: Sure we Sean. At least with him, I have yet to fake it. Sean: Fake it? Sure, you say now, now that I want nothing to do with you. So you actually think I believe that, no woman on this planet can fake it when I'm in the room. [Eliza smirks at Sean.] Eliza: Sure I did. I faked it every damn time you and I went at it. Now I know why West has an affliction for sheep. They have to be a better lay then you. [Sean stands there with a dumbfounded look on his face.] Sean: You’re the dumbest individual I have ever met, you can't accept the fact that I want nothing to do with you so you'll go around spreading these lies. But go ahead, I don't really care if you were or not, I got off, and that's all that matters to me. I don't give a flying fuck if you enjoy it, as long as I do...oh and West affiliates himself with Sheep because even they look better than the woman around here. [Eliza just continues to laugh at Sean] Eliza: I'm pretty sure the sheep don’t fake it with him either. I don't even know why I wasted my time talking to you. Sean: Holy shit! Something we agree on...I mean I've given you more than enough time. Truth is your so distraught about not having me that your lies and accusations are just meaningless. So why don't you take your little problems, cry on the shoulder of Tyler, and waste away in your little fantasy world. [Sean then puts his hand over Eliza's face, drags her in for a breathtaking kiss then shoves her back, causing her to trip over the TV and Video cables, her heel of her shoe catches between them, causing her to fall down hard, her neck and back hitting the cement hard. Sean stands over her fallen frame as he laughs. Emergency and Staff personal rush to attend her, Tyler Blaze comes rushing out from the corner where he and Patti were hidden from sight. Tyler not too happy with the scene he just witnessed. He stares off down the hallway more then ready to settle the score with Sean Summers.] [ Tyler looks at Summers, with a furious look in his eyes. Tyler begins to walk towards Summers with a constant speed, he reached Summers and shoves him into the wall. Sean flies back with a punch, but it stopped by staff personal. Tyler just laughs at him while he is being pulled away, he looks over and see Eliza in the sitting position with EMT's around her. Patti is kneeled down beside her, Tyler walks over and sits down behind Eliza and lets her lean back on his chest., and puts his arms around her. ] Tyler: It will be alright, he will get what is coming for him. How bad do you feel? [Eliza continues to stifle the cry that is trying to come through. She is in too much pain to nod.] Patti: No worries Eliza. Summers will bloody pay for hurting you. He did it purposely to get to you. [Patti stand up and punches the wall hard enough that it cracks from the force of the hit] [ Tyler starts gently rubbing the side of Eliza's face and looks up to Patti. ] Tyler: How about you stop hating me for awhile and work with me to get Summers. [Patti stands staring at Tyler. She doesn’t know what to do. Her mind is set on hurting the enemy for what he has done. An alliance may hurt or help Patti. She isn’t exactly sure what to do.] Patti: Tyler have you gone bloody mad? This isn't your fight. Summers did this to get to me. It has nothing to do with you. [ Tyler gets up and lays Eliza back down, and walks over to Patti and looks her straight in the eyes. ] Tyler: The fuck it has nothing to do with me. He took this to far, and what he did is the last thing he will do if I get my hands on him again. So saying this has nothing to do with me is bullshit. [Patti stays right in his face. Tyler sees the lighting bolts dancing in her eyes, but he refuses to back down.] Patti: Bloody hell!! Sean was right, your ego is larger then Summers on his best day. You only want at Summers because he hurt your play toy for the moment, and once you get your revenge, then you'll move on to someone else. I'll still be the one fighting for Eliza when you break her heart. [Tyler laughs.] Tyler: Is that right? Well fuck you Patti for doubting me. I care for her more then anything in my life, but not that you give a shit. So why don't you get out of my way so I can go deal with the problem. [Patti turns away for a second to collect her thoughts. Then in the blink of an eye she nails Blaze with a right hook that sends him down to the ground next to Eliza. Eliza screams as she sees Tyler laying on the ground. ] Eliza: Patti!! You have got to calm down. Tyler is trying to help you out. He means it. And if anyone is to blame, it's me. I was the one that confronted him, not you. I had to make sure it was over before I could tell Tyler the truth. [ Tyler gets back to his feet. And gives his sadistic smile. ] Tyler: That almost hurt, but not quite..nice try though. [ Tyler looks down at Eliza. ] Tyler: What truth? [Eliza blinks the tears back as she stares up at the two people that matter the most to her. Her best friend and the man she has some how managed to love. But before she can comment the EMT's quickly place Eliza on the stretcher and prepare to wheel her out of the building. ] EMT #1: Sir. Ma'am. You'll have to move out of the way. We need to get Ms. Murphy here to the hospital. You are more then welcome to see her after your work is done. [ Tyler shakes his head. ] Tyler: Fuck this shit. [ Tyler shoves Patti out of the way sending her to the wall, as he go's to look for Summers. Patti sits against the wall for a few minutes, as she watches Tyler walk away.] Patti( to herself): It's about bloody time that boy start showing what's he's made of. Fade Out. *this was originally intended to be an angle, but it turned into a really great role play. A huge applause for Summers and Tyler for their contribution to it. Thanks guys, you are the greatest.
