Breaking the Facade Pt. 1: Realizations Blaise 4WW Exposures #984 Date: 9/27/04 Location: Getting ready to leave Tokyo. Betrayal? No. Vindictiveness? Yes. The Messiah you say? Messiah: One who is anticipated as, regarded as, or professes to be a savior or liberator. A pious man you say? Pious: Devotional. Professing or exhibiting a strict, traditional sense of virtue and morality; high-minded. Commendable; worthy. A good man you say? Godly; devout; religious; righteous? No… Why do these people cheer him? Why do they adore him? Why do they belive the shit that rolls from his mouth? But there were also false prophets among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you. They will secretly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the sovereign Lord who bought them–bringing swift destruction on themselves. - 2 Pete 2:1 Dear friends, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world. - 1 John 4:1 Why? Why would they follow a man who outright claims to be the Messiah of wrestling, of 4WW? And I saw a beast coming out of the sea. He had ten horns and seven heads, with ten crowns on his horns, and on each head a blasphemous name. The beast I saw resembled a leopard, but had feet like those of a bear and a mouth like that of a lion. The dragon gave the beast his power and his throne and great authority. One of the heads of the beast seemed to have had a fatal wound, but the fatal wound had been healed. The whole world was astonished and followed the beast. Men worshiped the dragon because he had given authority to the beast, and they also worshiped the beast and asked, 'Who is like the beast? Who can make war against him?' The beast was given a mouth to utter proud words and blasphemies and to exercise his authority for forty-two months. -- Revelations, 13:2-5 Him and his false prophet, Todd Dream. Proclaiming him champion when he had not rightfully won the match. Then I saw another beast, coming out of the earth. He had two horns like a lamb, but he spoke like a dragon. He exercised all the authority of the first beast on his behalf, and made the earth and its inhabitants worship the first beast, whose fatal wound had been healed. - Revelations, 13:11-12 Just like sheep to the slaughter, they all fall for him. Why would a ritcheous man use a chair, or a title belt? Why would he need Todd Dream’s help? Why would he attack a woman who was not in a match? Revenge? Vicdication? Is not Wrath one of the seven deadly sins that this man is speaking out against? Kanton, West, unStable, the fans, all of them have fallen for the façade, for the Dark Angel. We won’t say his other name, because he’s not proven to be worth it. He’s a false man. A wicked man. And now he’s brought the Whore of Babylon to join him. The crusades have begun. The crusade to save the 4WW from this prophecy has started. Unfortunatly, the masses don’t know better yet. But they will… if I have my way… they will. Tokyo, Japan Blaise’s eyes opened as he was laying in the bed. The sheets covered what little needed to be covered as he shot up. Looking over at the other side he could see where Ukyo had been laying not an hour… look at the clock… fuck! 2 hours before. He’d dozed back off to sleep, can’t belive he dozed back off after she’d woken him up. He climbed out of bed and grabbed a pair of shorts and slid them on. Walking out into the common area, he saw Charlene sitting on the couch. On the TV was playing a replay of last night’s show, Maelstrom V: Winds of War. It was just beginning the build up portion before the West Wind Title Match. Standing in a pair of tight shorts and a small half cut shirt was Ukyo. She was looking in a mirror at her side, where a large blue and yellow blotch was. From hitting the ring steps when Dark Angel pushed her down. As he paid Blasie back for something that Patti Anderson had done, Blaise walked up behind her and wrapped his arms around her abdomen, above the bruise, but below her breasts. He then kissed her on her neck. “You didn’t make sure I stayed awake.” She turned slightly a not so happy look on her face. “Charlene told me the replay was on. I missed a few things last night. Plus, you looked like you needed the rest. You did have two matches, and need your rest. We’re flying out of here later on tonight. We’ve got to start to adjust to a stateside schedule again.” “What’s wrong?” He didn’t like the look she was giving him. She still showed her tenderness that she had always shown him since that night. But something was bothering her. Charlene decided to answer for her. “How about you explain your actions from the tag match?” “What actions?” “The face spit, Blaise.” Ukyo didn’t yell, she just said the words. “That was justifiable paybacks for those putzes. Especially the one who blabbed off and called you names. You heard Sanchez. The man called you worse then a whore. So he got what was coming to him.” Charlene was the one to continue. “What about fighting with honor? Not using weapons and that.” “I didn’t bring anything extra into the ring. It started the match with it, and ended it with it. It’s in my repitoire. You should know, you’re the one who looked up my past. I used to use a blue mist to retaliate against those who used those tactics. It’s no more Mr. Nice guy. I’m through with it.” “So you’d do it again?” “In a heart beat. You could say that I’m going to use all of my natural abilities if I can. Look at what the idiot did after the match was over? He didn’t come after me. He went after her. I then took it to him again. He’s one of the false idols that this sports entertainment crap is shoving down people’s throats. Zane Ozz…. Bad mouths the honor of a woman he doesn’t know, then after the match is over, goes to attack her. Yet these fans cheer them. Look, at the screen. Chris West! Listen to the crowd. Not a few months ago they all wanted him dead. He kisses Kanton’s ass a bit, bad mouths me, and suddenly the sheep cheer him. Fucking lambs to the slaughter. Almost as bad as Dark Angel.” “Darek DeAngelo, remember he goes by his real name now.” “No… he’s still Dark Angel. He proved it. He payed me back for Patti’s misdoings. Why? Because I had beaten him? Because I had Kanton beat? No, and then the way he won his half of the North Wind Title.” Ukyo smiles. “Yeah, watching you backstage with that sabre strapped on, I almost thought you were going to run out there and scewer him right there.” “I almost wanted to. The man is a disgrace to this sport. Him, Dream… all of them. Anyone who kisses his ass to. Anyone preaching the coming of this wrestling messiah is just falling out of it all. They don’t realize that he’s still the same man. He’s still Dark Angel. He’s just saying the sweet words they think they want to hear. Just like West, only he’s slicker at it. It’s all a façade. It’s a hollywood backlot. You can see the fronts of the buildings, but when you get close enough to see inside, all you see is emptiness.” Ukyo turns to face him. “So, these tactics, they’re legal in the Order?” “The honor system we use is this. You use what you bring to the ring with you. If you try to grab something like a chair or a title belt and use it, you’d better not get caught. But as soon as you get help, physical help… you’re worthless. However, I did not bring anything extra to the ring. I can guarantee that. Did I spit in his face? Yeah… it was a pretty good lougie too. I think I hit him right in the eye too. I drank a glass of orange juice before going out.” “Don’t lie to be Blaise William McClancy. It looked a lot like water, but water doesn’t sting the eyes.” “No, but there is no other way to prove anything else. We’ll leave it at that. If you really want to see fully, we’ll do it at another time. In the meantime, we’ve got a little vacation.” Charlene interrupts again. “No you don’t. You’re on your way to Calgary, Alberta, Canada.” Blaise looked down at Charlene after she said that. “What? Ukyo and I are going to Lost Vegas, the place of lost money, and then meet my grandfather with my truck, and I’m driving her to my home and there we’ll meet my parents. And we’re spending a few days there. Right?” “No… got a fax from 4WW offices right early this morning. You’re facing Chris West this week. Regular rules. Regular match. But it’s the secondary main event. Former West Wind Champion, and former South Wind Champion…”: “Speaking of which.” “I’m working on that too. Kanton just got the title. He’s not going to want to sign for a rematch any time soon. Damn, he’ll probably sit on his laurels for the full defense time as it is.” “Yeah, hand picked by a champion who still has his rematch coming up too. Imagine that…. Dark Angel… South Wind and North Wind Champion, all because he made sure that some FLOUNDER like Kris Kanton won that match. What a waste.” Ukyo kisses him on the cheek. “It’s only the South Wind Title. Don’t worry about it. Kanton will choke his first defense.” “Yeah, and I just have to make sure that I’m the one getting that first shot. I have a rematch clause too. I just have to figure out when, and how. And that’s where Charlene comes into play. In the meantime, who do I have again this week?” “Christopher West, the Insane One, the Hardcore Legend?” “Oh, yeah, that talentless hack. The one that I’ve already beaten with Ukyo against him and a tag partner. The one that tries to turn every match into a damn hardcore spot fest that bores the fans to tears. The one who doesn’t know a hip toss from an arm drag.” “Yeah, that one.” “Oh fucking joy. He’s Canadian right?” “Yeah, that too.” “And we’ll be in Canada? That’s just shitty.” “Yeah, he’s almost from that area. He’ll be a home town hero.” “No, it’ll be shitty because Canada is shitty. I’ve hated that place since I wrestled in the SCWF. Hell, even Cade Candler doesn’t claim it anymore. Hell, do they even watch wrestling up there? They have more fights in hockey then they allow us to do.” Ukyo giggled. “Hockey is striking this year. Looks like that whole league is going down?” “Going down? Like Kanton in his first title defence? Like Dark Angel with Todd Dream making sure he got the North Wind Title?” “Like Triple L and one of those hockey teams.” “OOOH, that’ll have West’s panties in a bunch. So the place we’re fighting in, USED to be a hockey arena, huh? That’s just ripe. Well, I guess I’ll just have to use Christopher West to make an example out of to emphasize a point with Mr. Kanton. Any ideas as to whom the next Tag Contenders will be?” “Don’t know. I’m expect someoen to crawl out of the woord work.” Phone ringing…. “Oh, let me get that.” Charlene got up and headed into the foyer. Ukyo looks into Blaise’s eyes. “Well, no matter what happened, I’ll tell you that I’m proud of you. You wrestled both matches and we had them both won. Although I’m tempted to ask you for more of an explanation as to the tag match, but I think you’d tell me if you wanted to.” “I’m telling you, I didn’t bring anything extra into the ring. I swear it. But yes, I did spit into his face, stopping him from coming into the ring to make the save. And then, I didn’t use it on Kanton. But then again, that just shows that Zane Ozz is that much better then Kanton. As I had Kanton down and out… Dark Angel will pay for that.” “We don’t need to start this visious cycle, Blaise. He’ll say he did it in retaliation for what Patti did to you. You’ll pay him back, he’ll do something else. You’re right. He’s a big fake. He’s an asshole, and a general all around side show fake. I hate Patti Anderson, even though she’s going to be your inlaw here soon, but even she went out there and tried. But he just cheated and cheated, until he won. You know he’ll try to keep you from getting back at him legally. But if you do anything, he’ll just do something else right back.” “Then I’ll just have to deliver a message to him. I’ll just have to take care of it. You’re brother done well though.” Ukyo smiles. “Yes, he did. He did the best out of them. I just hope it doesn’t overwhelm him.” “I hope so too.” Charlene walks back into the room, a wide smile on her face and a fax paper in her hand. Ukyo acknowledges her first. “What? Did Chris West drop dead or something?” “Nope, better then that.” Blaise chimed in. “How?” “Congradulations Mr. McClancy… You’re a second cousin!” “What?” “The twins! They were born last night while TS1 was en route from Tokyo to Seattle. Your cousins and Ms. Anderson where in attendance. Emergency leave has been granted to both Ian and Fey Childress.” “Shit!” Blaise didn’t look happy. “What’s wrong?” “That means that Todd Dream is in chage.” “Nope, says that the acting presidency and CEO positions were granted to one person… VP of Talent Relations, Lorena Lovecraft.” “SWEET!” “What?” “That means that Dream is still playing second fiddle. Where he needs to be. Dark Angel is still under check. But there is still much that Dream can try to do to ensure that things go his way. Who’s booked this week?” “Ice, You, West, Kanton, Kyo…” “Kyo? Against whom?” “Jaime, oh, that Asian guy who’s a former North Wind Champ. It’s non title though.” “What about Biddle, Tarrant or Dark Angel?” “No… none of them.” “He’s already started it? Jaime isn’t a big threat, except that he’s also from that area. He’s actually Canadian. They’re going to start trying to wear down Kyo to get that belt off of him.” “Shit.” Ukyo crossed her arms on the realization.” “Well, we’ll need to try to take care of things here, right?” “Yeah. I’ll concetrate on West this week. In the meantime, we need to get my rematch with Kanton, and I need to take care of a few other things. First thing though, is this damn bruise on you. I saw you whince when you woke me up… let’s go let the doc see it. It’s low on your ribcage.” “I’m fine.” “No, you’re not. Let’s go get it checked really quick, then we can come back, pack and make the plane. If everything goes right, we can still head off to see my parents for a few days. I’m not worried about West. Not in the slightest, since he’s not a wrestler, and he’s going to be in a wrestling match. Come on.” Blasie hooks Ukyo’s arm, as she gives him a look of “no way in hell” as we fade out.
