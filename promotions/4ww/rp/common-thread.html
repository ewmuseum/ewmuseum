Common Thread Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1164 Date: 2/9/05 Location: Somewhere in France [The camera fades in to a classic French scene. With the Eiffel Tower in the background, we see French natives sipping their wine and popping bits of cheese in their mouths at corner bistros. One person, however, at one of the bistros, looks completely out of place. He is wearing a New York Yankees t-shirt and an I Heart New York baseball cap. The man seems to be arguing with the waiter. The camera zooms closer to pick up some more of the argument.] Rustin Slade: What do you mean ‘you don’t serve french fries here?’ This is France, right? Waiter: Monsieur, you must order something off the menu. (The waiter points at the menu to make his point.) Something we have. [Rustin looks over the menu again. Souffle...Escargot...Terrine de Saumon. Rustin shakes his head not knowing what any of these dishes are. The waiter takes the menu from Rustin, leaving Rustin a little annoyed.] Waiter: May I suggest you order a nice escargot? Rustin: (Not knowing what it is) Ok. That sounds good. Thanks buddy! [Rustin gives the waiter a small slap on his back. The waiter shakes his head in disgust of Rustin’s behavior and leaves Rustin’s table. Rustin looks towards the Eiffel Tower and sees some over native French men at another table. Rustin tries to break the ice.] Rustin: So that’s the Awful Tower, eh? [The French men look over and give Rustin a disgusted look. They get up throwing some money down on table and leave. Rustin shrugs his shoulders, annoyed by their rudeness. He calls out to them, nevertheless.] Rustin: It was a joke guys. Oh, come on! [Slight pause.] Rustin: I know I could sure use a good joke after the couple of weeks I’ve been having. (Rustin looks off into the distance) So much has happened. First I get my ass handed to me by Chastity’s old army buddy or new lover or whatever he is, and end up in the hospital. Then the next day I’m in Berlin, joining a bunch of great guys to form “The Legends,” and fighting for my life again in a cage match with Eddie B. and Jackson Outcast. Now I’m here in France, and still in the dark about Chastity’s true feelings, and waiting to face off against the West Wind Champ, Zane Ozz. [Rustin looks down at his fingers, which were keeping tally of the events. Noticing only 4 things, he jumps back a little wide-eyed.] Rustin: Yikes! Too much for my liking. [The waiter comes back and puts the escargot in front of Rustin. Rustin jumps up from his seat as he notices what his dish is really made up of.] Waiter: Something the matter, monsieur? Rustin: Those are snails, pal! [Waiter nods his head, but doesn’t seem to see why Rustin is so upset.] Rustin: Where I’m from, that’s a health code violation buckeroo. Waiter: Sacre bleu! You are a very strange man. [Rustin’s waiter, nevertheless takes his plate away and leaves Rustin to himself once again. Rustin looks over at another man at another table.] Rustin: They tried to serve me snails! Can you believe that? [The man dips a fork into his plate, which Rustin notices to be escargot and takes a bite. He smiles at Rustin with a bit of antennae sticking out of his teeth. Rustin motions at his teeth.] Rustin: You got a little something here. [The man cleans it and goes back to eating his luncheon. Rustin shudders at how fast the man is gobbling up his garden variety lunch, {no pun intended}.] Rustin: I can’t say I’m not excited though. A West Wind Title match! You know everyone’s been asking me, why a Lumberjack Coal Miner’s Glove Match? Well my first answer is this. I want the Lumberjacks because they’ll act as a safe haven for both me and Zane Ozz. It’s going to keep Zane and I from leaving the ring and ensures our match will be won inside. It also helps me and Zane by keeping out any backstage interference that may come into play, or another run-in with that nut job Lincoln Frink. [Rustin clears his throat, takes a sip of his pepsi, and starts again.] Rustin: At least they have Pepsi in France. The Coal Miner’s Glove is a little bit more complicated. Everyone usually thinks that these types of matches can end up bloody and should be reserved for the most bitter of enemies. The problem is, I have no problem with Zane. I think he’s a great athlete. Hell, he saved me from a total beatdown a few weeks ago at Cage Match Hell. He also scored some brownie points with me by helping up Chastity after the match. So why the Coal Miner’s Glove? Why, it’s the West Wind Title on the line. That title is almost everything to me. I say almost because we all know what’s most important. (Throws his voice and says Chastity) Who said that? (Rustin looks around in mock bewilderment) The title will allow me to prove myself to the 4WW, but more importantly Miss Spence. And Zane, I’m prepared to take it by any means necessary. [The waiter comes back and sets down a plate full of french fries and a slice of pizza.] Waiter: Compliments from the chef, monsieur. He says he likes your shirt. Rustin: All right! A slice of pie with some taters! [Rustin looks into the chef’s quarters through the bistro window, which is half open and notices that the chef is drinking from a I Heart NY mug. The chef slips his head out of the window and yells to Rustin.] Chef: (In a New York accent) GO YANKS! I hope you like the pie! [Rustin waves at the chef and sits back and smiles to himself.] Rustin: I love French culture! [The camera fades to black as it centers on the Eiffel Tower.]
