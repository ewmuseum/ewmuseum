To be or not to be

April 2004

To be or not to be 'Superstar' Eddie B. 4WW Exposures #626 Date: Location: To be... or not to be... that is the question The Scene begins inside of an old folks home... we see an old lady walk by on a walker and two old men playing chess by a table when an over sized woman enters the room, and from behind her comes the Superstar Eddie B. ][Eddie B.][ Woah woah there big fella... I'll take it from here. The large lady simply grunts and takes her leave as Eddie B. smiles to himself rubbing his hands together, but... why are we here? ][Eddie B.][ Now Seven... I truly believe this is a big joke. Now I've used that term loosely as of late, but I'm finding it pretty damn hard to believe the things that have been going on lately, and quite frankly I don't think I'd have it any other way... of course I'd have the gold, but that'll come in time. Good things come to those who wait, but is this statement true you might ask. Sometimes... Sometimes I believe and it then there are times like this when I've waited for you to talk about me, say something about me, hurt my feelings... damnit say something, but no... this big bad Seven is nothing more then a big bad bitch! Now, I've found out some things about you without you opening your pie hole... and thats that gay men find that you're a powerhouse and you drink shirely temples... and not the cute little girl kind singing on the good ship lollipop... So now I've come to another last resort. Eddie B. extends his arms out and the camera pans back from him showing the large folks home once again. Eddie B. makes his way over to the chest game and takes a seat. ][Eddie B.][ This is cute America isn't it? Two nice old men playing chess to pass the time while they wait here to die. One of the men gasp at the Superstar's rude remarks. ][Eddie B.][ Oh shut your trap before I brake your damn hip. You two are gonna tell me what I want to know or there will be problems, got it? They simply nod. ][Eddie B.][ I'm looking for information on a man named Seven, you know him? The men both remain quiet when Eddie B. smacks one of them in the back of the head. ][Eddie B.][ I asked you a damn question, now be poliet and answer it! ][Old man][ I know him... he usually comes in here on the weekends and plays some chess with us, and takes all the fine ladies from us. Camera pans on Eddie B's look of disgust. ][Old Man][ I mean... I never liked the guy to be honest with you... He comes here with his little strut and he steals my viagra that son of a bitch! The man slams his fist on the chess board knocking over some of the pieces, which makes the other man angry as he picks them up. ][Eddie B.][ So... Seven is an equal oppertunist you'd say? I mean he loves the men... loves the old ladies... loves the shirley temples... and loves the viagra...Man going into this match I guess I have to be prepared for anything. Hell i think one of you would pose a greater threat then this Seven guy. ][Old Man][ I never understood the name to be honest with you... it's a number... ][Eddie B.][ Yea his real names probably embarassing like...Alfred or something. ][Old Man #2][ HEY! That's my name you baby boomer! Eddie B. laughs to himself and stands up. ][Eddie B.][ Well gentlemen... it's be real and it's been fun, but it hasn't been real fun you get what I'm saying? Come Stormfront the era of the Superstar will be upon 4ww... and there's not a damn thing Seven can do about it... time for the big leagues boys... and trust me, seven isn't his lucky number. Checkmate... Eddie B. flips over the table the chess games on and laughs out loud before taking his leave and the scene fades to black.
