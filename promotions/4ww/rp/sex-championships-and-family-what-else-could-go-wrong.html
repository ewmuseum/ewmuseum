Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #1064 Date: 11/22/04 Location: Santa Barbara, California [The 4WW logo blows across the screen to the sight of Caitlin Fairchild in a white wifebeater and Lakers basketball shorts sitting quietly at the dinner table eating her cereal while her partner in crime/manager/best friend wearing rollers, and a “Draw Together” XXL large white T-shirt covering her 5’7 frame hangs off her shoulders, as she indulges in her bowl of cereal while give Caitlin looks at the corner of her eyes. It had been almost two weeks since Caitlin had her little experience, and every time she tried to talk to her about it, Caitlin chooses to change the subject, or leave the room either walking out of the house, or locking herself away. Besides that a lot was currently going on in her life, she was not only the South Wind Champion for 4WW, but once again ½ of the VSE World Tag Team Champions, and they had a big title defense later that evening which she had to get ready for, not to mention later that week Caitlin would take part in a tournament to crown a new West Wind Champion for the 4WW, mixed feelings began to spread around a lot of people, for though Caitlin was capable, many couldn’t see her winning much less holding two singles championships at once, especially with the high caliber and aggressive talent that would be hunting a piece of 4WW gold opposite her in the ring. Add Kyo Kushinaga the current North Wind Champion’s infatuation with Caitlin, as well as the restraining order that bitch Luscious Lady Luck put on Caitlin; it was enough pressure to put anyone over the edge. Roxy was use to getting restraining orders, She had about six of them under her belt now against ex-boyfriends, ex-boyfriend’s girlfriends, a Trucker, and the guy who would bring his little Cocker Spaniel along, and allow it to relieve itself on their law, Caitlin however wasn’t receptive to it at all.] FLASHBACK TO LAST STORM FRONT SHOW: CLEVELAND, OHIO (Caitlin’s locker room) Caitlin Fairchild – A restraining order!! She gave me a restraining order!! I can’t believe this!! How could she do this to me? Free Fall – C…it’s not that bad… Caitlin Fairchild – Not that bad?? Not that bad?? This is horrible!! I have a record now!! Free Fall – Dawg…what the hell are you talking about, you ain’t got a record. Caitlin Fairchild – What if I get injured huh? What if I have to get a regular job like everyone else huh? What if I go to get a job as a lawyer, or in a PR firm, or some high profile company? I’ll be ruined!! RUINED!! Free Fall – C…you’re over reacting Dawg… Caitlin Fairchild (Hyper-ventilating) - I’ll…I’ll have to get a job flipping burgers…or worst yet…appear on the “Surreal Life” to make ends meet…I’ll have to go to those sports memorabilia shows and do appearances for 150 bucks a night, where you take pictures next to nasty little kids…who drool…and drop food on you…and try to cop a feel…and tell you…how…how good you “use” to be… Free Fall – C…you are bugging the FUCK out…here…sit down and breathe into this bag… [Caitlin does as Roxy says, places the bag to her mouth, and breathes in and out rapidly trying to catch her breath as her best friend rubs her back (Mind you this was all BEFORE she regained her composure, and went to meet up with Kyo Kushinaga on their “date”.] Free Fall – Now chill yo…one you don’t have a record…unless you go and choke the bitch with the restraining order, and two…it’s not that bad! Hell Stone Cold Steve Austin has gotten restraining orders, and look where he… [Caitlin gives her an irritated look.] Free Fall – Ok…wrong choice…ok…I’ve gotten restraining orders before, and look… [Caitlin gives her another irritated look.] Free Fall – Aight yo…Aight…my point is, this ain’t the end of the world…so she put a restraining order on you…it only shows the world what a punk bitch she really is, besides…I thought you wanted to be a “BAD GIRL”? Caitlin Fairchild – (Close to tears.) But not one with a criminal record…my parents are gonna KILL me… Free Fall – DAMN girl, how old are you? [About an hour or so after her rant, Caitlin went out to think that night, and to Roxy’s knowledge ended up hanging out with Kushinaga-san for majority of the night, she briefly spoke about it saying that they went out for a bite to eat, and some conversation, but refused to go any further into their time together, which was ticking Roxy off because she told Caitlin everything. But as of late her friend has become more withdrawn, keeping to herself. Free Fall could no longer stand the silent tension building in the house. So she got up from the table, and went to her room. About a minute later she came back with something in her hand, and tossed on the table causing it to hit with a thick solid thud. Caitlin in mid chew looked at the object that resembled a Billy club or blackjack, only it was made of pure rubber, much longer and thicker, and it had a weird shaped head to it. Caitlin’s eyes widened as she spit out her cereal and began to choke.] Caitlin Fairchild – (Chokes.) Wait…the…hell….Roxanne… Free Fall – I thought it was time we had a little conversation… Caitlin Fairchild – SO YOU THROW A BLACK RUBBER DILDO ON THE DINNER TABLE? WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH YOU? IS THAT THING EVEN STERILIZED??? Free Fall – Don’t worry about that, my point is what’s up with you? As of late you don’t wanna talk about anything…all you want to do is stay to yourself…you have a date with possibly one of the hottest and weirdest dudes I’ve ever seen, you don’t wanna talk about it. Two big title matches this week, you don’t wanna talk about it. You pop your own cherry a week ago, you don’t wanna talk about it, so what DO you want to talk about? Caitlin Fairchild – JEEZ! I didn’t know I had to tell you EVERYTHING that went on in my life! For the last time nothing happened by between Kyo and I…just dinner between two professionals in the same sport and that’s it. As for my title matches, what can I say, I’m under a lot of stress and pressure, this is like crunch time for me, I got a World Tag Title match in a couple of hours; this is the second time Charon and I have held the belt, and I want to make a better run then the last run we had, not to mention the West Wind title final tournament is this week…that’s me against Bullet, a mystery opponent (who could be anyone), Zane Ozz (The man I defeated with some help to retain my South Wind title), Slayer, and Lucifer Priest, and each of those guys would like to take me apart piece by piece to insure I don’t have a shot at holding another title in the 4WW. [Caitlin pauses for a minute playing with her cereal.] Caitlin Fairchild – And as for what happened to me last week…I really don’t feel comfortable talking about that, I do not want to talk about it, and for all I care it happened and is not going to happen again. Free Fall - You decided to join a nunnery? Caitlin Fairchild – No…it’s just not going to happen again… Free Fall – Kat, you can’t just STOP yourself from becoming horny yo… Caitlin Fairchild – MUST you be so crass? Free Fall – I’m just sayin yo, when that time comes…a shortie gonna get wet, she’s gonna get wet… Caitlin Fairchild – LOOK! I got enough on my mind then worrying about my freaking HORMONES going APE on me! I have a wrestling career I’m trying to build, you know, the one that’s paying for THIS house, THOSE cars outside, the clothes that we wear, the food that we eat, and allows us to do the things that we want to do, so just drop it, take your toy up, and leave me alone! [Caitlin puts her head down, now furiously mixing her soggy cereal as Roxy sits there narrowing her eyes at her, with a look of “Oh no she did not” on her face. She grabbed her rubber adult toy, and stormed off knocking over the chair she was sitting on back to her room, as Caitlin sat there stirring her cereal. It had been awhile since she had ever truly went off of Roxy, and deep down she didn’t mean it, but at times she felt Free Fall couldn’t understand the almost inhuman amount of weight and pressure put on her at times, it was a little bit easier when she was an only child, but now that she has other siblings, a very successful older female sibling who’s in the same profession, though for now retired. Whether they had been together as children or not, because they came from the same gene pool people expect Caitlin to deliver ten-fold, and that’s easier said than done at times. Not to mention, Caitlin was still at odds with the main contributor to her gene pool. At the first sight of seeing her real father, Fairchild walked out not even saying a word to him. She wanted him to feel what it was like not to matter in one’s life or even exist, to be cast away as if nothing, but in the end only she was hurting, because deep down in reality she wanted to run to him, hold him tight, and cry on his shoulder while he comforted her. She wanted to ask him why he abandoned her, and if he ever loved her or thought about her. Questions that made the pain in her heart swell just thinking about it. In the end fear would guide her more than pride, she couldn’t risk being hurt again, beginning rejected by him, or abandoned. In her mind, he threw her away, and now that he finally realized what a precious stone she was, if he wanted her back…he would have to earn his way to get her back every once of blood, sweat, and tears; she was worth that, she deserved that. As Caitlin continued to stir her cereal, she was interrupted by another object dashed on the dinner table. Several sheets of papers held together by a stapler. Caitlin looked up with an annoyed look meeting the eyes of a pissed of Roxy.] Caitlin Fairchild – What’s this? A dirty story or novel? [Roxy didn’t say a word, as her eyes narrowed. For the first time not even a peep, as an unfazed Fairchild picked up the papers sighing to see what new form of perversion her roommate brought to the dinner table.] Caitlin Fairchild – Unified Wrestling Alliance/Machine Contract for new hire wrestler…Roxanne Spaulding…one-year contract effective November 19th 2004… [Caitlin swallowed hard as her entire face turned white, as she nervously turned to look a stone face Roxanne square in the eyes.] Caitlin Fairchild – This…this is a joke right? Free Fall – You see anyone laughing? Caitlin Fairchild – Roxanne…this is a UWA contract…a WRESTLING CONTRACT… Free Fall – Signed and sealed…that’s my copy… Caitlin Fairchild – YOU SIGNED THIS?? WITHOUT EVEN TALKING TO ME? HOW COULD YOU? Free Fall – Gee, I was trying to find the time to, but…oops…I forgot…someone didn’t HAVE the time to talk to me. [Caitlin paused and looked down for a minute. She deserved that.] Caitlin Fairchild – Okay…okay…well we’re talking now…and I don’t think you should do this… Free Fall – Why not? Caitlin Fairchild – Let’s count the reasons, you’re 5 foot 7 inches, and 100 pounds soaking wet while majority of the guys range from about 6 to 8 feet around the 220 to 550 pound range, this is also the UWA, one of the TOP veteran federations out there; WWE and NWA caliber, the one I use to be, where I completely bombed at my first début… not to mention…YOU DON’T HAVE A SINGLE SHRED OF WRESTLING EXPERIENCE! Free Fall – That’s what the Machine is about, a rookie fed under the UWA umbrella used to develop your skills and prepare you for the big time. Proving grounds yo, the ones who make it get a shot to roll with the big dogs in the big house. Caitlin Fairchild – But…Roxy…why? Free Fall – Why do you think? I’m just a valet Caitlin…that’s it…I make $40,000 a year just to walk your ass to the ring…I’m a friggin Hype-Girl…I want to know what it’s like…to hold it down like you do…to walk around with some gold over my shoulder, and most importantly…to pay for more than my car insurance, Con- Ed, and some food around this house…we were equals once Kat…and as of late we haven’t been…I don’t want that to effect our friendship yo…so in order to prevent dat…I gotta pull more weight, and the only way I can figure to do that…is to step into the ring. Caitlin Fairchild – Roxy…don’t do this…I don’t want you too… Free Fall – If you’re worried about me getting hurt…then train me…show me more than Hurricane’s neckbreaker…I can do it yo…I wanna do it…but I need you to believe in me…and to help me…please… Caitlin Fairchild – (Smiling close to tears.) Sure, sweetie…I’ll help you… Free Fall – (Close to tears herself.) Thanks…yo…thanks… [Caitlin opened her arms as she pulled her friend in giving her a hug; it wasn’t just a show of support, or an apologetic type embrace. It was to keep her from seeing Caitlin’s real look of sadness and fear. She knew all too well the brutality of this sport was all about, and not only what it could do to you physically, but how it could change you mentally. Roxanne despite all the times she’s gotten on Caitlin’s nerves was still her family despite finding her biological family. The first and closest thing she had to a sister. A sour feeling of guilt formed in Fairchild’s stomach thinking her actions of late of had pushed her, to step into a sport she tried so desperately to shield her from, and was willing to take all the bruises and beatings in the world so they could both make it and be happy. Now…Roxanne was in the war zone right with her, now she couldn’t neglect her, not for a second…because she knew if she did…she couldn’t forgive herself if anything happened to her. Caitlin wiped away her tears quickly as she held her friend close as the 4WW logo blew across the screen fading the scene to black.]
