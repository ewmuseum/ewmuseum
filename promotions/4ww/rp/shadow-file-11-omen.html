Shadow File #11 - Omen Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1242 Date: 4/23/05 Location: A cold cloudy night in a small unknown city of Scotland, as the staggering drunks and the loosely clad street walkers were out further corrupting this world. The scent of rain filled the chilly air, signaling that anytime now the GOD that the slumbering inhabitants of this plain favor was about to shed his sorrowful tears in response to all this corruption. But again, a flash dimly lit a portion of the dark skies, as the rumbling thunder was heard throughout the city in sign that their GOD wasn’t in sorrow instead he was enraged from this sight. Or perhaps, this was another’s doing, but the question was what being had this kind of power to angry the vast skies. The iron skeleton of a slow developing building that would possibly reach the heavens at completion was the place where the silver-haired Outlaw clad in black has taken shelter at…for now. Silently sits on the edge of a high beam, and through his orange-tinted shades he looks down at the vague city like he was GOD himself, though he would never consider himself a GOD even with his knowledge and great power. The silent Kyo turns back to his journal before the thunder interrupted him, thinking up the words as he writes. What makes a person who they are? Could it be their appearance? Could it be their personality? Could it be their behavior? Or can it be something in their past to make who they are? No, it’s none of these. So, what makes a person who they are? The Grave Keepers are the ones that make a person who they are. They’ve programmed the slumbering fools in their chambers in the cursed land. Then here in their twisted play, the naïve portrays their given roles, thinking they control their fate, though in actuality the Grave Keepers are the ones that are pulling the strings. The theory was proven last week when my associate and I faced the incompetent pair of Body Count. I can see that the Grave Keepers have programmed them for their own amusement, pinning them in matches they don’t have a chance of winning and laughing greatly at their defeats. Body Count is the Grave Keepers’ personal jesters. They’ll always be the laughing stock of this deceitful plain. And they’ll always be the victims of cruelty like they were the week of yesterday. The week of the present should be intriguing. Plan still goes on by schedule. Though this isn’t why the week intrigues me. More flashes dimly lit the shaded heavens, as the soft sound of thunder follow, breaking the silver-haired Outlaw of his thoughts again, though his senses were heightened from detecting a squad of individuals have breeched his silent haven. Ravenous individuals that have fill the silver-haired Outlaw with weariness, as he didn’t want to waste his time on these weaklings, but had no choice then to play their game that they forced him to play, and was going to make them pay for that with their lives for it. These individuals were professional mercenaries turn bounty hunters who were after the reward for the Outlaw’s death. Heavily armed, they stealthily move through the levels of the iron skeleton, searching for their target’s whereabouts within the darkness, but they had their night vision goggles on to aid them in the pursuit. Bounty hunters suddenly stop when they heard a dreary tune whispering in the shadows. Slowly head to the upper level from where the mysterious music originated like they were lure to this hypotonic tune. Lure to their prize, or perhaps lure to their own demise. The silver-haired Outlaw stand on the iron beam, playing this lonesome song, a lonesome song that he made and called “The Ballad of the Truth”, though the song didn’t have an ending to it yet, as he search for the final chords that he needed to finish the ballad, and plan on finding them during his pilgrimage of truth through the Grave Keepers’ deceitful world. There was a reason for playing this incomplete song, as he did want them to find him, but he also wanted to see how many of these individuals that he had to deal with. Before another note was played, the man clad in black look out at the grim city with an emotionless gaze, as the tedious game players have arrive with guns arm and lock on him for the instant kill. “Kyo Kushinaga!” Behind the orange-tinted lens, Kyo shift his piercing eyes over to the voice and see it was a short blonde-haired bounty hunter with serious blue eyes in military attire with a long white scarf have shouted his name in a threatening tone. “We’re here for your precious bounty. It’s said if we bring you to the Shadow King alive, he’ll grant us a wish. Any fucking wish we want. Now, be cooperative with us, and we won’t have to send you back to him in little fucking zip lock bags.” Kyo strap the guitar on his firm back in response, as the intense calmness that he was known for have taken over. Push the orange-tinted shades closer to both eyes, Kyo swing open one side of his long leather trench coat, and expose the plasma sword that was attach on the hem of his dark colored pants in sign that wasn’t going to be cooperative with the greedy bunch. The squad cocked their weapons in sync. Blonde-haired bounty hunter smirked in amusement when he saw his comrades intensify their defense, as he also enjoyed having the silent Outlaw in this predicament. “I don’t know if you haven’t notice, but we have your ass surrounded. In lamest terms, we will fill you with holes before you can even fucking make a move. Don’t think we won’t do it. We will simply tell the Big Boss that we had no choice but to kill you.” Kyo have counted eight bounty hunters during the blonde-haired bounty hunter’s empty threat. “So, what is going to be your choice, Deathscythe Outlaw?” Choice, which was word that the silver-haired Outlaw was all too familiar with in the plain of reveries. He knew that choice was the one thing that can truly control the fates of the slumber, though the Grave Keepers made it that they were only ones who can make the choices for them. He believe that your choice make your fate. He made his choice, the choice to control on his own fate. Kyo stand in silence not threatened by them, as the bounty hunters including the blonde-haired one keep weapons lock on him. Multiple flashes dimly lit various spots in the cloudy night skies, as rumbling thunder was heard, though soon the heavens were going to be enraged from the battle that was about to happen between the silent Outlaw and the ravenous hunters. A chilling wind blown upon them in a grim sign that death was on the way. Some of the bounty hunters nervously stare at Kyo, as they sensed a great dark presence from the man that they were staring at. Indeed, they had something to fear about Kyo, as they stare at the Grim Reaper in the flesh. A massive streak of lightning suddenly lit the entire sky, and great thunder that made the earth tremble in fear follow. Choking on blood, two of the bounty hunters have cross-edge knives in the throats, as the hot crimson spew through the fatal wounds while falling to the ground. The remaining bounty hunters including the blonde-haired one watch their comrades soak the cold concrete floor with puddles of their own blood. Blonde-haired bounty hunter turned his firearm over to where Kyo was, and notice that he wasn’t there, realizing that he have disappeared when that massive streak of lightning struck but not before he took both his men out. As this was the Outlaw’s choice, the choice to silence those that seek to tame him, and didn’t have desire to return to the enslave population. He’s free, and wasn’t going to allow anyone to ruin that not even those pitiful hunters. Great frustration build within the blonde-haired bounty hunter not enjoying being made the fool by the rebellious Kyo. He angrily looked over to the remaining hunters, and see that they were examining the corpses of their fallen comrades. Though the blonde-haired bounty hunter didn’t care that his men were mourning for the lost instead he was consume by great arrogance, wanting to kill the man clad in black no matter what. “Ok, you goddamn maggots! We have a wish to be granted. So, get off your asses! We got a stupid fuck to find and kill! He couldn’t have gotten far. You know what, I bet he still here. He’s scared shitless about the repercussions of murdering our own. And he should be, because he’s death is going to be messy one.” Blonde-haired bounty hunter licked his lips like he could already taste Kyo’s blood. One of the hunters stare the blonde-haired one with great fear, as he was traumatize after witnessing the brutal murders of their fellow men. “I think we should retreat.” Anger filled the blonde-haired man’s azure eyes when he heard that the one have submitted to his fear. “Retreat? Why the fuck should we do that?” The fearful hunter stood in front of the other hunters in defense for their angered leader, and stare at the leader with little determination he had left to show in his scare eyes. “This is no ordinary target. When I looked into that man’s eyes…it felt like I was looking in the soulless eyes of Death himself. From all the missions we took, we have never encountered someone of his kind. It’s like he has been born to give Death.” Scoffs, arrogance continue to consume the blonde-haired bounty hunter not being scared of the silver-haired Outlaw. “He’s trying to psyche you out.” The fearful hunter held his hand over to two corpses of their comrades while keeping his scare eyes on the arrogant leader. “I bet these two thought the same thing too, and look what happened to them.” The blonde-haired bounty hunter scoffs again. “It’s no surprise to me that they allowed themselves to be killed. They were the weakest of the group.” The fearful hunter’s emotion take a sudden turn, as great anger filled his once scare eyes, and glare at the leader. “You son of a bitch! These two were already our friends!” Close eyes, the blonde-haired man amusingly smirk in response to the comment. “Causalities of wars.” The anger hunter’s emotion took another turn, as shock mark his face after he heard that reply from the leader. He furiously threw his firearm down on the solid ground hard nearly breaking it, and put an end to the discussion. “You want to kill him, so badly. Then do it your damn self.” Blonde-haired bounty hunter stare down the hunter’s weapon, then stare up at the hunter for awhile before smirking oddly at the hunter’s furious resignation. This single smirk caused the other hunters including the surrendering hunter to look uneasy not the reaction that they were expecting from the leader. The wrathful skies unleash another furious streak of lightning, as the crashing thunder follow, though a cold gunshot soon went after it. Falling, the once determine hunter felt hot crimson blood flow out from the front of the head and the back of the shatter skull, as the leader have slain his own for showing fear before him. Surviving four hunters see their own lie limp beside the other two corpses that the Outlaw have slain with the fresh corpse’s blood mixing in the others’ puddle. “Now…” The hunters frighteningly look over to their blonde-haired leader who had been fully consumed by great greed. “Does anyone else want to quit?” Blonde-haired bounty hunter rest the warm weapon over his left shoulder, and glare at them with a smirk from seeing that they were now afraid of him than Kyo. Dead silence was among the four, as they have responded to the threatening inquiry. “Good. It wouldn’t make sense for us to quit now. We started this and we’re going to finish it. Let’s find this fucker and bring his head to the Shadow King, then we can get our wish. Let’s move!” The hunters including the blonde-haired leader leave the scene in pursuit of the silver-haired Outlaw within the iron skeleton. Though the Outlaw was closer than they thought, as he sat on a beam below the death scene, and stared down at the decease in deep thought after he witnessed the leader slain his own. He saw what made the blonde-haired bounty hunter what he was. He saw not only did the Grave Keepers rule him, though the poison of greed ruled him too. As the man’s ravenousness further disgusted the Outlaw not wanting to deal with a maggot who allowed their emotions to rule him, but it was too late…the greed that consumed the blonde-haired man had sealed his fate, and the Outlaw going to make sure that he and the others will meet it. A great streak of lightning lit the furious heavens, as Kyo disappeared within the dreary miasma of death before the roaring thunder made a peep, and was off to silence them in a manner that will haunt them beyond their deaths. Through the silent darkness, the armed bounty hunters move in stealth in the lower level with the aid of their radiating night vision goggles. They try to search for their target, but to no avail, as they continue to sneak through the eerie level to find him. Bone chilling scream cut through the abyss, as the hunters including the blonde-haired one sharply look over to the yell, and see that one of their own have disappeared. It was like the darkness itself have come to life and swallowed the hunter, as the other hunters have fear mark on their face including the blonde-haired one, though his arrogance and greed wasn’t going to allow him to show that. “He’s gone! There’s nothing we can do for him now! Keep moving!” The blonde-haired bounty hunter move ahead while the others hesitate about continuing on with this hunt, though soon decided to follow their leader from knowing that he will kill them if they didn’t. But again, they should be more frightened of the Deathscythe Outlaw than the leader since they were in his world…The Darkness. A hunter suddenly turned in the opposite direction of the patrolling hunters when he heard a mysterious noise, and aimed the weapon at the shadows, though see it was nothing through his bright vision. As the hunter face the distant hunters, the man clad in black silently appear upside down behind the hunter with silver hair hanging down and eyes glowing a dreary emerald shade through the orange-tinted sunglasses. Another shriek of horror pierced through the darkness, the remaining three hunters look over in that direction, and see that one more hunter have disappeared, as the hunter’s gun covered in blood only remain. Frustratingly growl, the blonde-haired bounty hunter grown tired of the Outlaw’s game, as he cock the gun, and started firing in the shadows in great fury, hoping that he rid of the bothersome Outlaw. “Show yourself, you damn fucking coward!” Blonde-haired bounty hunter angrily glared at the two hunters. “What the fuck! Why the hell are you two just standing around here for! Open fire! Open fucking fire, you stupid fucks!” Without hesitation, the two hunters fire the guns in the shadows with their leader. Stop firing, the two hunters have two cross-edge knives in their foreheads, and hot crimson blood spew out from the hunters’ fatal wounds. Blonde-haired bounty hunter stopped his gun assault when he saw his two remaining hunters lie motionless on the cold concrete ground, and look with fear, as his arrogance and greed was wearing off from the sight of death. Heavily pants, he loaded a new clip in the weapon, and look around the dreary surroundings through the goggles. Shockingly gasped, the blonde-haired hunter when he saw the corpses of the two hunters who supposedly vanish without a trace hanging upside down, as the corpses had their eyes snatched out of their skull with blood oozing out from their empty eye sockets and open mouths. As this was the Outlaw’s way of saying that everyone was blind to see the truth, or to see him. “Show yourself, you fucking coward!” Before another word said, the silver-haired Outlaw swiftly dropped down from the dark rafters arm with his plasma sword, and severed the blonde-haired bounty hunter’s gun with a slash, as a massive streak of lightning struck that lit up the dark skies once more in response to Kyo’s great attack. Painfully yell, the blonde-haired bounty hunter favor his bleeding left hand from his trigger finger and middle finger being severed by the Outlaw’s brutal attack. While in pain, Kyo lunge at the blonde-haired bounty hunter, and struck with a great roundhouse kick on his neck. As the blonde-haired bounty hunter spun around on his feet from the sheer force of the kick, and this was the last time he ever breathe this plain’s air. Out of in an opening, Kyo soar out with a tight grip on a long white scarf, though soon come to an abrupt stop, as a chilling crack echo through the shadows of the iron skeleton signaling that the Outlaw had broken the bounty hunter’s neck in a cruel way. Loosen grip, the silver-haired man drop three stories down, and land on the ground with ease, as he causally leave the construction site like nothing happened. Slow applauding broke the site’s silence. Stopped walking, Kyo seriously look in the direction of the praise, and see that a smirking Mei-Ling stand outside of her Rolls Royce with her bodyguard, Chan beside her. She dressed in a short red colored Chinese dress with matching flat heel shoes, and stopped her praise, as she cunningly stare at the Outlaw. “I can see that your skills are sharp as ever.” Push shades, Kyo seriously glare at the woman that he thought he lost in China. He did wonder what her reason for coming out of the blue like this to visit him even though they had separated their ties long ago in Kowloon. Dim flashes of lightning lit various places of the darken heaven, and the soft thunder come after, as cold raindrops gently fall. Open an umbrella, Chan held it over his Mistress’ head not wanting her to get wet, as Mei-Ling have seriousness in her eyes. “We need to talk.” A great streak of lightning lit up the entire sky, and the roaring thunder soon follow. Not being fazed by the loud thunder, Kyo return Mei-Ling’s glare with seriousness in his own eyes. ..:: † ::.. Café Occult, an under construction nightclub that China’s Mistress of the Underworld was having made in Scotland, and soon will have one in each every country in this world, as she was wanted her hand in the nightclub game. The silver-haired Outlaw lean back on the wall with a lit cigarette in his mouth while Mei-Ling sat on a barstool before a metal bar counter with her serious eyes on the Outlaw. “As you already know, the so gracious Shadow King upped your bounty to a wish. It appears that Shadow King has the ability to grant any wish that the one that brings your corpse to him.” Sighs out smoke, Kyo dully glare at the young Chinese woman, as he already knew that from his previous encounter with the hunters. Mei-Ling crossed her leg, and keep her serious eyes at the silver-haired Japanese man. “Well…since the reward had gotten more intriguing, the hunt will attract more of the deadliest of foes for you to face. The kind of foes that your butter knives will not being able to take out.” Sharpened eyes, as the comment have spark Kyo’s attention, and wonder what she meant. “The rulers of this world have taken off the gloves. You have become a great threat to them, and will do anything to rid you off this plain.” Kyo knew all this. Kyo understood the reason why the Grave Keepers was after him, as they wanted to silence him before he can show the slumber more of the truth. He also understood that the Shadow Ninjas was after him, as they wanted to reclaim their prize weapon. Though he didn’t understood the reason why the Shadow King was after him, as wondered what he have done to this one to issue out a bounty for his head. Thoughts broken, Kyo shift his eyes over to the backdoor, and curiously watch Chan enter the scene with a black metal suitcase with a crimson colored cross marked on it. Chan rested the suitcase on the bar counter near his Mistress, as he open it after entering the combination on the lock, and inside were two large black handguns with twin crosses marked on them, one being a crimson cross while the other being a silver cross. Kyo stare at the handguns with interest, as his right hand began to tremble in response since he hadn’t held a gun since he was with the Shadow Ninja, though soon gained control of the nostalgic feeling that he was having from the sight of his old friends. Mei-Ling gaze at the guns in admiration of the workmanship of the pair that will grant anyone instant death who was at the receiving end of them. She looked back at the silent Kyo with a smirk. “These are something that my people have been working on to help you contend with the approaching threat that waits for you in a near future. Each fire twenty-one strong and effective Soul Rounds.” Kyo curiously stare at Mei-Ling, wondering what Soul Rounds were since this the first time ever hearing these kind of rounds. Close eyes, an amusing smirk mark Mei-Ling’s face, as she found the Outlaw’s curiosity refreshing. “Soul Rounds are the mixture of Dark and Light Magic. These rounds pack a powerful punch. They’ll kill off any of the rulers’ soldiers. If you run out of rounds, you can come visit any of the Café Occults in the world to pick up more rounds.” Mei-Ling suddenly take out one handgun from the suitcase, and sharply throw it over at Kyo, though he responded by easily grabbing hold of the handgun. The Outlaw twirled the gun around his finger professionally, as he acted on impulse from holding a gun again, though stop the trick, and aim the gun in front with serious eyes. Mei-Ling looked more impress by Kyo’s skills, though soon lean over to Chan, and whisper in his ear, as Chan nodded in response. Chan oddly disappeared behind the backdoor once more, leaving his Mistress alone with the always focus Outlaw. “You’re going to plow through all the deceit of this world, and make room for the truth.” Kyo seriously glared at Mei-Ling in wonder about why she was helping him the tips and the weapons to aid him in the upcoming. He wondered what this Chinese girl’s true motive on the guidance was. Mei-Ling made a sound in an amuse tone when realizing that the Outlaw was cautious about her generosity, as her face was marked with a smirk that had hidden agenda written all over it. “I understand your mistrust for me after all I am the biggest Crime Mistress of all China, which sorta makes it hard for people to trust me. To put you at ease, you can just consider me as a spectator of the war between you and the three rulers of this plain. Honestly, I could careless about what happens to this world. Though I am a bit curious to see what would happen if you did succeed in your goal.” Kyo kept his serious eyes on the young Mei-Ling. Backdoor slowly creek open, Chan enter the scene once more, but with a big surprise for the Outlaw. As Kyo look with awe from the surprise that Chan lugged in, though the surprise disappeared from his face, and was replaced by the trademark serious expression. ..:: † ::.. The angry skies continue to unleash it’s fury upon this country, though one person was sleeping through it, as the Greek Amazon with smooth golden skin and burning red colored hair was in the comfort of her soft Queen-sized bed underneath the cool covers in the hotel room that the wrestling league, 4WW paid for her to stay in during her stay here in the country. Far outside of her room, the silver-haired Outlaw sat on the edge of the top level of a nearby building in the storm, though he was shaded from the rain by the building’s roofing, as he calmly stared at the window to make sure nothing tried to get her, and if something did try to get her, then he give them a torturous punishment before terminating their existence here from this plain. Look away from the window, Kyo pull out his leather jacket journal, and began to finish what he started to write at the now iron grave of the frighten and naïve hunters. Fourth time… This is now the fourth time I had to engage in combat with someone from a group that claimed to be my allies in the war of corruption. First two times I faced off the Judas Priest, who I easily defeated. Most recently was the night travesty, the Dryad that my female sibling longs for, never got the chance to grant him defeat, and it would’ve been fun to grant him this like I did the another McClancy. Fourth time, I’m facing another actor from the group that Grave Keepers created to keep order on their stage. Though this is no ordinary faceless actor, as this one have supposedly fought the good fight with the group at the beginning of the many shows that the Grave Keepers put on. He’s the current holder of a trinket that I held two times while performing on the stage. It is also rumored that he trained Blaise McClancy and the now absent Judas Priest to fight. For his tendencies of violence, he or the Grave Keepers have named him the Innovator of Violence. But...what makes him who is? Is it his reputation? Is it the many trinkets that he has held? Or is it the way that the supposedly allies rule him just like the Grave Keepers do? I shall get the chance to see what makes him who is. But again, it won’t be any surprise if he turns out to be like all the others that I've conquered on the stage of Fallen Souls. Stopped writing, Kyo intently stare down at his writing through his orange-tinted shades, as he had another serious thought enter his mind. A swift cold wind suddenly blow passed the silver-haired Outlaw. As Kyo thought was broken from the mysterious wind. He turned in the direction from where the gust originated, and see that it was over where the stage where the actors will be soon heading to entertain the fans and the Grave Keepers with their colorful performances. Sharpen his eyes, Kyo glare at the direction in seriousness from realizing the wind was a sign. The sign that a certain someone had made the important choice that determine their fate. Kyo slowly nodded in approval of the sign, though soon went back to his journal to finish the latest entry. An omen… When he wrote that, his mind trail back in a memory of what happened in Café Occult after Chan came back in the room with the big surprise for the silver-haired Outlaw. Mei-Ling curiously stared at Kyo while watching him strap his new weapons inside his long leather trench coat. “What was that?” Kyo looked at the surprise with slight pleasantry in his eyes. “An omen…an omen to those who think they can silence me.” Beside the metal counter, a large metal coffin with a long chain strapped to stood with a skull embedded at the front where the corpse’s head would be, as the skull have wires coming out from one empty eye socket while a single small crimson orb rest in the other socket. The coffin have been modified by Mei-Ling’s power to act as an weapon with hidden goodies in it that can take care of anyone that Kyo come across of. The memory of the past end, Kyo still sat on the edge with journal out, as he resumed writing in it. His omen shall be wicked like all the others before him. Draven Lox… In Death, the blind shall see the truth. Quote the Outlaw Nevermore. Great streak of lightning lit the rainy heavens, and the Outlaw vanished within the flash of light, as the earth-shattering thunder echo throughout the city. [Fin.]
