Brotherly Heart to Heart

Brotherly Heart to Heart Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1115 Date: 12/19/04 Location: Madison Square Garden, NYC [The scene opens outside of Madison Square Garden. The camera zooms into the magnificent structure that’s been host to many great wrestling matches. The camera pans and focuses on someone who is looking at the building from afar. The someone is quickly identified as Rustin Slade. Standing beside Rustin is a smaller version of himself, who is also staring at the Garden. It is the brother of Rustin, Rex Slade.] Rex Slade: It’s hard to believe bro. Rustin Slade: What’s that? Your big brother’s actually going to have a match in Madison Square Garden? Rex: No! That you still don’t have a girlfriend, after God knows how many tries! Rustin: Shut up Rex! Rex: Hey, I’m just messing with you man. It’s pretty exciting. “My brother the professional wrestler.” I could write a book on that. [Rustin laughs and gives his brother a noogie. Rex pushes him off and gets into a mock shootfight stance.] Rustin: Easy bro. You stay with the academics. Mom would flip if you turned pro wrestler too. [Rex laughs at his brother’s joke. Rustin slightly frowns however, remembering his mother’s face when he showed up a week and a half ago to surprise them for Christmas. The day he revealed that he had turned professional wrestler instead of his mom and dad’s hope of him becoming a lawyer. Rex, almost telepathically knows what his brother’s thinking and puts a comforting arm on his shoulder.] Rex: They just don’t know how much wrestling means to you yet Rusty. They’ll come around. They always do. [Rustin nods simply, still unconvinced] Rex: Hey remember that time that Dad came to my junior high’s science fair? Rustin: Yeah. Rex: He was so pissed at me when he got home, remember? He wanted me to do a project so bad on the solar system. The week before the science fair, he got all the stuff to make a candy solar system. He didn’t know I really wanted to make a volcano. I went and made the volcano. I don’t know what was quicker to erupt...Dad or my volcano. He got over it though Rust. He supported me when I got the A for my volcano. Rustin: Thanks Rex. I think I know what you’re getting at. Dad’s just worried that I won’t make anything of myself. I have no “A” to bring home to him. Rex: [Nods his head] Now you’re getting it bro. Rustin: After Maelstrom VI, I’ll finally have something to bring home to him though. The 4 Winds Title! Rex: I think that’d make Mom and Dad real proud man. You just gotta focus Rustin. I saw your matches the week, and it seems like your main problem is that you’re not focusing. Rustin: What do you mean? Rex: You know what I mean. You’re letting your woman problems get the best of you and you’re bringing them into the ring with you. [Rustin looks at Rex and shakes his head. Rex gives him an odd look. Rustin catches the look, and gives a chuckle] Rustin: Rex, sometimes I wonder who the bigger brother really is. Rex: [Chuckles] Nah, I’m just giving some sound advice. Rustin: I guess you’re right though, I have been letting the women get the best of me the past couple of weeks. I’m not hunting anymore though Rex. Rex: Huh? Rustin: I mean I’m done looking. I think I finally found the one. Rex: [Pause] That Chastity girl, right? Rustin: Yeah. Rex: She’s out of your league Rust. Rustin: [Locks Rex into a tight side headlock] Hey! Let me give you some sound advice now! Never abandon fate bro. If true love comes knocking don’t go flocking. Rex: [Rex wriggles out of the side headlock] Look Rustin, all I’m saying is that you shouldn’t get your hopes up so damn high man. You’ll just be let down. [Rustin hangs his head down. Rustin is shaking his head slowly] Rustin: There won’t be any more let downs. Not for mom. Not for Dad! Not for you! And not for Chastity! This is the big one Rex, and there’s only seven people standing in my way for me to get that title and all the glory that comes with it. [Rustin walks off camera as Rex looks back up at Madison Square Garden. Clouds slowly pass over Madison Square Garden, and it starts to rain. Rex is seen leaving the camera as it fades to back as he runs for shelter.] Storm clouds....harbingers of doom for some...however, the Native American rain makers would consider them a blessing. Things always change with rain. Unfertilized becomes fertilized. The old gets washed away, the new is born. Get ready for a flash flood at Maelstrom VI!
