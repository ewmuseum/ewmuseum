Episode Two Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #562 Date: 3-4-04 Location: Abandon Subway Station = [The scene opens in the familiar subway station.] = “Confidence…” = [The view slowly moves through the dusty and abandon station, as you can hear something moving quickly throughout the quiet place.] = “It is something that helps people overcome their fears… To stand up to someone who has tormented you in past life or current. Or it can even increase one’s abilities to limits that the person did not even know about…” = [Dim flashes of lights appear through a darkness area of station.] = “However, there is a bad side to this reassuring feeling of faith in one’s self…” = [The view moves closer and closer to the area where the light is radiating from.] = “Over-confidence…” = [Once coming to the view of rapid flashing of lights, the lights soon disappears though you can see a familiar masked man holding his close hand out as if he held an object in his hand. He has his black hat lower over his mask, but you can still tell that he has a straight look on his face.] = “Too much confidence can lead to one’s defeat… Too much of this feeling can also cause one to become corrupted and thinking that they are above all the hardworking citizens and everything else.” “Especially the law…” “That is why I came here to my new home league, Four Winds Wrestling.” = [He lifts his free hand to the top of his black hat, and moves it up from his face to reveal a serious glare through his metal mask.] = “To defend the law-biting citizens from the vile-villains who think they are above the law.” “That why I am here…” Purpose = [The Masked Avenger turns his hand face up, and opens his hand as you can see a small soothing cerulean color fireball that floats slightly over the palm of his hand.] = “From the shadows I have been listening to the many rumors and promos from only two wrestlers from the Insane Battle Royal to declare the number one contender of the West Wind Title…” “Well…” = [You can see about ten empty soda cans each lining up in two rows on the dirty and hard ground.] = “I should say only one wrestler.” = [He sharpens his eyes in a serious glare, and closes his hand a bit over the strange color fireball as he quickly winds up and then hurls forward like a professional baseball pitcher.] = = [The blazing fireball soars the soda cans in great speeds, as it begins to knock one by one down with great force and speed.] = = [He moves his hand side to side as he is mentally guiding the ball of fire towards the cans to knock them down.] = = [Listens to the loud sounds of the cans hitting the solid ground.] = “Rumors have it that Citizen Coricidon has left Four Winds Wrestling for some personal problems…” = [After knocking the last can down, the soothing cerulean color fireball launches straight back its master in great speeds.] = = [He holds out his hand, and catches the ball of fire with great ease.] = = [Looks down at the fireball he is holding in the palm of his hand with focus in his eyes.] = “It is quite a shame that the lovely flower, Citizen Coricidon, will not be competing in my home league anymore.” = [He emits a soft sigh and closes his eyes halfway, as he looks at the damage that the burning fireball caused.] = “However, people have problems that this superhero cannot resolve with his burning powers or his justice smarts. She will have to resolve her problems on her own and hopefully she will land back on her feet.” “Good luck and goodbye, Citizen Coricidon…” = [Holds the fireball in his close hand, as he slowly makes his way over to the fallen cans.] = “So that only leaves three wrestlers that stands in my way of getting my shot at the super-villain of Four Winds Wrestling…” “Citizen Jaime…” “Citizen Bullet…” “And Citizen Chris Wrestling…” = [He kneels down in front of an empty tall and thin beer can, as he picks it up and examines the can and his fireball with thinking gaze.] = “I have even heard a single peep out of both, Citizen Jaime and Citizen Bullet. I guess they do not find this match to be an important one.” “Honestly, I’ve expected Citizen Bullet to shoot a promo saying that he will please his friends in that stable with the adulterous name. As of late, his friends have not been doing too well when it comes to winning gold.” = [Still examines the silver beer can and now the pulsing ball of fire.] = = [He emits a soft curious sound and looks with seriousness behind his mask, as an idea just popped into his head.] = = [Stands the tall and thin beer can on the dirty floor, as he soon stands from the floor and begins to walk away from the can to give him space between him and the can.] = = [He stops walking and has his back facing the single beer can standing tall in the subway.] = “It is a shame that I am going to have to continue this bad streak of theirs’ by defeating Citizen Bullet in the Battle Royal…” “The same goes for Citizen Jaime and Citizen Chris Wrestling…” “Speaking of Citizen Chris Wrestling, I saw your latest promo and quite frankly I disappointed at what I saw. Being rude to that makeup woman like that…” = [You can see him go in his trench coat pocket, as he takes something out from there and begins to fiddle with it.] = = [Hears faint clicking sounds coming from him as he is fixing a new weapon of his.] = “I know your kind… The kind who thinks he is above all, as you do not have regard for no one except for number one. It would seem that I am going to have to teach this vile villain how to be nice to others along with showing respect for what they do.” = [A faint crimson glow appears over his body while having his back facing the silver beer can.] = “It is not going to be a lesson that I will use my words instead it I will be using my…” = [He turns around and faces the beer can, as he is in slight stance and reveals that he is holding a beyblade launcher with a black and white beyblade attach to it.] = “…fists of justice to make you learn this lesson!” = [You see him quickly pull the strip out of the launcher to cause his beyblade to soar quickly over towards the white beer can. His beyblade is gliding over the dirty floor in great speeds.] = = [His beyblade begins to glow a crimson aura.] = = [The glowing beyblade hits the can to cause it to be launch up in the air. His weapon blurs away swiftly, though you can see bright slashes appear on the airborne beverage can.] = = [His beyblade reappears, as it is coming straight down towards his owner.] = = [He catches his beyblade with great ease, and watches five severed parts of the beer fall from air and listens to the clinging sound of the clean cut parts lightly hitting the surface.] = = [Looks down at his beyblade with an intriguing gaze and nods his head in a pleasing way, as he likes what his new weapon did to that once tall beer can.] = “That’s right… I am going to cut through the competition in this Insane Battle Royal like a hot knife cutting through butter.” “And Citizen Chris Wrestling, I heard that the only reason why you enter this Insane Battle Royal is because to impress this girl named Karen. Here’s a word of advice, if you want to impress your flower then take her out to make her romantic dinner, give her flowers just because of it is Thursday, and tell her sweet nothings I mean do special things with this girl. If she really wants to you want this title more than nothing, then it means that this girl is only after greed and is not worth your time.” = [He places his beyblade in his trench coat pocket and looks down at clean cut parts with the same intriguing gaze.] = = [Raises his hand to the top of his black hat, and lowers it down to hide his eyes though he smirks amusingly.] = “As long as I have justice on my side I will succeed on defeating you all in the honorable way and get the opportunity to face the vile-villain, Fnord…” “See you all in the ring…” = [He quickly spins his body and does his trademark fire tornado.] = = [The tornado quickly disperses into tiny sparks of fire, and sees that the Masked Avenger has disappeared during the rapid spinning of the cyclone.] = = […Fade…] =
