Combined Training 4WW Exposures #1036 Date: 10/30/04 Location: LLL's Training Facility *Note - This is a combined RP for both Luscious Lady Luck and Zane Ozz. Both handlers had equal contributions to this RP and would greatly appreciate it if it was judged for both competitors. Thank you. * (Open Scene) [As the camera slowly fades in the form of Zane Ozz can be seen sitting in a hotel room. Nothing special in the room sticks out. Same design as the past hotels...TV, nice view of the city....king size bed. Zane is seen sitting on the bed, his head in his hands. He rests there for a few moments...not moving....then slowly he raises his head up and runs his hands though his hair. Zane then pulls his hair out of his face and pins it back while his hands rest on top of his head. Slowly he exhales and lets his hair fall back in his face.] Zane: I still can't believe that I had to fight my brother....my own flesh and blood. [Zane slams his fist down on the bed.] Zane: MY OWN FLESH AND BLOOD! [Zane sighs once again.] Zane: But it was gonna have to happen. I should have known better...I should have known. [Zane throws himself backwards so he is lying on the bed. He sports his baggy jeans and a Nirvana shirt.] Zane: I've still gotta worry about the West Wind Title Tournament....not to mention a match with Caitlin Fairchild this week. I honestly can't believe they expect me a six foot ten behemoth and her, a seven foot Amazon to compete in 'Black Catwalk' match. Why couldn't we have the graveyard match? But hey....another chance to put gold around my waist so can I really complain? God...that's what this wrestling sport is all about....looking out for yourself first and foremost. But then again it's not in just wrestling....it's in everyday life. Everyday people pass by the needy only with thoughts of doing something to benefit themselves. So I really shouldn't beat myself up over it....it's human nature, to be the greedy bastards we are. And besides I've gotta get ready for that friggin scaffolding match. [Zane arises from his spot on the bed and stands up. He sticks his knuckles into his mid-back along his spinal cord and pops his back.] Zane: Ahhhh...thats better. Still can't believe they expect two of the biggest people in the fed to get up on a scaffold. [Zane walks over to a corner and grabs a gym bag. He lifts it up on his shoulder and exits the room still voicing his complaints.] (End Scene) (Open Scene) [Zane is seen sitting on a blue painted bench as the camera fades in. Instead of his jeans he is now wearing black shorts that reach his knees, but the Nirvana shirt remains on. He is currently wrapping up his hands with tape.] Zane: Gotta keep in shape. Caitlin won't go easy on me...so I shouldn't hold back on her. She looks like she doesn't really want to lose her title in the matter of a week....so she will probably bring her A-Game. [Zane looks around the empty training facility and sighs.] Zane: God I miss the days when me and Landen had time to train to together always.....before we started getting accepted into feds we had so much free time on our hands....but maybe that’s the benefit of the training.....we got jobs...we got money..... [Zane sighs once again.] Zane: Oh well....guess I can do one day by myself. So long as I don't pull something...I'll be fine. (LLL walks into the state of the art training facility where Zane is sitting. She looks around at the different areas. She is wearing a black sports bra which reads “Patti Anderson” across the front of it, and a paid of smooth silk blue gym shorts. She walks through the facility, thinking that it had been a while since she had been in this area. With 4WW gallivanting all over the world, she hadn’t had the opportunity to do as much training as she had wanted to in her facility. Her second home.) (So much had happened here. It was here that she brought all of her models to train and get in the shape they needed to be. The desired shape of what every photographer and ad person wanted. It was here where her training for here trip to Australia had begun. The fateful trip to Australia where she met the woman of her dreams and everything she had ever wanted.) (And Patti, as of recent seems to have a different plan of action. The kiss. What was that kiss about? Has Patti had a change of heart? Of course she had. She knew what was good for her. She knew exactly what LLL could offer her and Patti knew that she would be with the one person who would treat her like the goddess she was.) (Or was it mind games? Was it all a game to Patti. Patti is known for her mind games. Patti is known for catching people off guard when they least expected it. Is that what Patti was doing? The confusion on the face of LLL was quite evident. The confusion on The Lucky One’s face was almost overwhelming.) (And then she saw him. Sitting on the bench. Himself, staring off into space, a look of confusion, a look of pain, but yet a look of confidence. The young man whom had volunteered not so long ago, with his brother, to protect the beauty while giving himself that extra advantage that only Triple L could offer a wrestler. LLL grinned slightly, as Zane had not noticed her yet. She snuck up behind him, as graceful and quiet as a cat. As she got up behind him, LLL wrapped her arms around Zanes strong shoulders, leaning into him, and giving him a kiss on the cheek.) LLL: Glad to see you here, hon. [Zane's eyes widen. Shaken from his trance to be brought back to reality by a very intimate greeting took the big man by surprise. Zane knew the voice but had to be sure it was whom he thought it was. As Zane slowly turned his head his suspicions were confirmed when the face of the lovely young Diana King , known to the wrestling world as Luscious Lady Luck, was seen within inches of Zane's face.] Zane: Glad to see you too....wish I got greeted by women like this more often. So..... is this how you greet everyone or is it just me? LLL: For those who have a special place in my heart, I greet this way and sometimes better. (LLL winks at him) Zane: Hmmm... [Zane had thought to himself why LLL would be there, at the exact same facility he was at. Maybe it was coincidence....maybe she knew about him being here. Whatever the reason Zane couldn't help but ask...] Zane: ...so any reason for you being here? LLL: Well, with the match I’ve got coming up, this entire pumpkin patch scramble or whatever it is, I thought I’d do some training here where I know the equipment and the people who would be here. Zane: Ahhh.... [Zane gently lifts LLL's arms off of his shoulders and then stands up and faces her.] Zane: Well I'd love to stay and chat....but I've got other plans here. LLL: Other plans, eh? Well, I know you have that scaffold match coming up. You ready for that? I mean if I can be of any help, please let me know. (LLL starts to walk over to one of the balance beams on the floor. It’s an average height, probably about the same as Olympic height and width, only quite a bit longer. The length probably averages about 40 yards. LLL gracefully mounts it, and starts walking back and forth down the beam. She feels as Zane’s eyes watch her making her way up and down the beam.) (What were the point of Patti’s actions? LLL kept asking herself this question as she continued along the beam. She quickens her pace, breaking out into sprints up and down the beam. She does about 10 of these sprints on this specialized beam. Shortly, she begins cartwheels and other tumbling along the team. She makes a few passes at this before hopping off the beam.) (It was a balancing act that Patti was playing. Or was it? The thought just kept playing in LLL’s head. What was Patti doing? Why the kiss? She had to have gotten it. That had to be it. But Caitlin wouldn’t allow that. There had to be more. There had to be a plan. There had to be something there.) (LLL stepped up into the ring. This ring was slightly different from the 4WW ring. The ropes where much narrower in this ring. And the ring was filled with thicker mats. LLL began her exercise of springboard moves. Landing on the mats each time, graceful, perfect, just like Patti. There was that thought again. Patti. What is it about Patti.) (She had to get her mind off of Patti. She just had to. She had other things to think about right now. The 5 people who would be trying to get her title. They were all accomplished, and they all deserved it. Ike Angel and Luke Chadbourne, both men new to the fed and new to the title scene. Slayer, a man Trips had defeated before. Robb Hardy a veteran who is always a tough match. And possibly her only ally, the brother of the man she knew was watching her, Landen Ozz. LLL’s chances were good, and she knew that, but still, if Patti could get in her head, she was ruined.) (Triple L ran and springboarded off the ropes again. This time, she landed with a thud and the rush of air could be heard escaping her lungs, quite audibly. LLL flips over on her back and starts looking up, motionless.) Zane: Whoa... [Zane quickly runs over to where his employer lay on the ground. She looked as though she was shaken up a bit from her fall but it was nothing serious.] Zane: You ok? LLL: Yeah...please help me up.... [Zane starts to lift LLL up off the ground...his left arm lifting her upper body and his right the lower half. However as his right hand slides over the underside of Trips his hand gently caresses her 'assets'. Zane looks just in time to see L's reaction...a slight smirk. Zane turns his face away which is starting to turn beat red with embarrassment...he swallows a lump in his throat and then continues to help LLL up to her feet. Zane starts to speak but his voice cracks...he clears his throat and tries again.] Zane: Ok then....glad to see your not hurt. LLL: Great lesson for you to remember during your scaffold match hon. You can’t have the balance and grace of a cat without having the fear and hesitation of a cat. Know the danger of what may happen, and you’ll perform even better. (LLL walks over to the balance beam again. She breathes in deeply, regaining her breath, and making sure that she is back to her full status. She needed to be at full status for the match this week. If she wasn’t then it was over. Her run to make the Tempest Title a legitimate title and not just a title for bottom feeders and newbies as some like to call it. She had held it now for 2 months. She couldn’t let it go now.) LLL: I think it’s a good thing that we are both here at the same time. I think we may be able to help each other train a bit for our matches’ hon. Me giving you a little bit more in the ways of balance and being sure footed, you giving me some training with high impact wrestling. What you say we get started. (LLL knew this could be a beneficial session. She knew that they both needed this. She looked at Zane whom contemplated a bit, then started walking toward her.) (Patti aside, problems aside, the outside world aside, this would be a very beneficial training session for both of them.) [End scene]
