All the wrong things

All the wrong things Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1225 Date: 4/2/05 Location: London, England [The scene opens up outside in a park somewhere in London. Rustin Slade is seen walking slowly through the dimly lit park. It is near dusk and all daytime visitors of the park seem to have already gone home. Rustin continues walking, dragging his feet along the paved walkways loudly. He seems to be a little upset. He kicks a stone to emphasize his mood. It then begins to rain heavily. Fitting, Rustin thinks. Something to match his mood. He then looks up and sees a large black dog in his path.] [Rustin walks up to the black dog who seems to be frightened. The dog quickly becomes soaking wet due to the torrential rains that now are sweeping the park. The dog begins shaking off, soaking Rustin even more than he already is. As Rustin reaches out his hand to pet the dog, the dog snarls loudly and makes a lunge at Rustin’s hand, which Rustin quickly draws back. The dog looks at Rustin sadly and trods off.] Rustin: Jeez, I guess it’s true what they say. I really don’t have animal attraction. [Rustin smiles weakly at his horrible pun. There has been too much on his mind lately. Number one of course is Chastity Spence, the love of his life. Chastity and Rustin seem to have finally established a deep friendship, and if Rustin didn’t know better, he’d say that Chastity and him were even becoming an “item.” Rustin shakes his head at the thought.] Rustin: I guess we’re not actually an item until after Maelstrom when I beat Lincoln for the rights to Chastity. [Rustin shakes his head again.] Rustin: Rights! Damn it, I hate that word. Nobody should have rights to anybody. Christ, what are we, back in the slave days? [A man of African Ancestry is walking by just at that moment, and stops and gives Rustin an angry glare.] Rustin: Sorry man, no offense! [The man starts walking again, shaking his head.] Rustin: I need to keep my mouth shut. Seems whenever I open my big mouth, I get in trouble. [Rustin takes a seat down at a nearby park bench that is drenched. Rustin, not minding the rain and wetness, brushes his hair back and continues thinking aloud.] Rustin: And what about this mess with the Legends? The only time we get to see each other is at the shows, and every time we do, each one of us seem to be at each other’s throats. It’s like Paul and Darek haven’t said more than two words to each other since the whole Kyo team started, and Rob and Darek are hushed too, with Rob being in the running for the North Wind Title. Then there’s Kristian. I have no idea where his allegiances lie. And here I am, the man in the middle. Friend to all of them, but unable to form anything that can bridge us back together. [There’s a big rumble of thunder overhead, as the rain ceases slightly. Rustin looks up at the storm clouds brewing.] Rustin: It just seems like lightning’s about to strike any minute with everything going on lately. [As Rustin says this, there’s a bolt of lightning that strikes down and nails a tree behind Rustin. The tree begins to fall. Rustin jumps out of the bench as the tree comes down and shatters the bench in half.] Rustin: Yikes! That was close! [Rustin shakes his head. He begins walking again. He walks slowly down another paved pathway.] Rustin: This week, I received a rematch at my former glory, the West Wind Title. Needless to say, this is one thing that is looking good for ol’ Rusty. The only problem is, I’m going up against Draven Lox. “The Innovator of Violence.” I lost to him once, and I’m afraid I might do it again. I’ve got no idea what kind of match I can have with this guy to get my title back. It’s got to be something sick. I figure the only way to beat a sicko like Draven, is to one up him in the hardcore department and fight him in match that’s as sick as him. There’s just nothing I can come up with. [As Rustin walks down the pathway, he passes a child of about twelve. The child runs by Rustin but in doing so drops some things that were in his hands. The boy runs as Rustin calls to him.] Rustin: Hey kid! You dropped something! [Rustin bends over the things the boy dropped. One item is a bottle of glue, another is a roll of tape, and lastly there are several glass tiles that have shattered. Rustin picks up one of the glass tiles and in doing so, accidentally cuts his finger open on the shard. Rustin drops the tile face up, showing a chinese character for “water.”] Rustin: Damn it! That thing was sharp. Hey isn’t that a Taipei piece? [Rustin looks down at the tile. He remembers a matching game when he was a kid called Taipei that used glass tiles like the one that had cut him. Rustin smiles at the Taipei piece.] Rustin: I think I know exactly what kind of match we’re going to have Draven! You have something I want. That title around your waist belongs to me and I will reclaim it. Even if it means crawling through broken glass to get it back. [Rustin starts crawling through the broken tiles to emphasize his point. He yells in response as he cuts himself deeper.] Rustin: Damn it, that’s sharp! [The rain falls down heavily washing away the blood on Rustin’s hand. Rustin tapes up his fists as a makeshift band-aid. The scene fades to black as Rustin nurses his wounds in the park.]
