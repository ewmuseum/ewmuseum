[IC] Sitting down with the McClancy twins

1 March 2006

1 March 2006 Ok so it is One Childress one McClancy Questions for Fey or Sean? Sean is currently the President of the company. And Fey along with her husband Ian, run the parent company. ---- 1 March 2006 For Fey... Who was your favorite person that you ever managed? Who was your least favorite person that you ever managed? ---- 1 March 2006
For Fey... Who was your favorite person that you ever managed? Who was your least favorite person that you ever managed?
Well, 'ow abou' I up tha' t' two fer each one fer yet. Because it's 'ard t' make decisions sometimes. Fer me favorites it would be pretty obvious. "Th' Fallen One" Nephilim, also known as me 'usband Geoffrey Ian Childress III. I managed 'im while in th' SCWF righ' befer the circumstances tha' lead t' us finally gettin' married. "The Irish Bastard" Sean McClancy. Yes, me younger twin brother. But 'e an I 'ave 'ad a long career t'gether. I was 'is manager durin' th' biggest 'ighlight's o' 'is career an' would always support 'im. Fer me least favorites? From me days in the GIWF, when the KB 'ad just formed there is one tha' comes t' mind. The Prodigy. Th' man who turned on us fer 'is own good. The KB has little tolerance t' those who use it as a launchin' point fer their own gain. The two factions tha' it 'as t'day are showin' tha' team work an' brother/sisterhood is wha' it's all abou'. The Prodigy was abou' 'imself an nothing else. The worst one of all though was in th' SCWF. Tha' was a man tha' I not only managed, but dated a' the time: Mack Johnson. The man, just like the Prodigy was only usin' the KB, and meself as a stepping stone fer 'is own career. 'E knew 'e'd na be able t' beat me cousin Blaise, who was na' very family like a' th' time withou' 'elp. So 'e suduced me, ga' in with me brothers, and used us t' further 'is career. 'Ow 'e treated all of us when 'e still failed, with all o' our 'elp, t' capture th' Superior Title from Blaise makes 'im the one member of th' Brigade tha' I'd rather jus' write off as a loss. Either tha' 'er cut 'is little boys from their hidin' place in 'is pants and feed them t' me big mastiffs. ---- 1 March 2006 Hello, Fey? Remember me? This is Psymon, the same man you fired a couple of years back. I have a question for you: if I faced your husband in a match, would I be able to kick his ass? My answer is yes. How would you feel about that? I'm not asking for a challenge, mind you, just asking for your opinion about it. See you later, hon ;) ---- 1 March 2006 Ye know there Psymon, if I didn't know you, I'd claim ye were more stupid then arrogant. In yer case it comes close. Ye make a few assumptions in yer question tha' are completely invalid. First yer assumin' that because Ian sits on the board o' TaleSmith Productions, tha' 'e's na' fightin' 'er trainin' anymore. 'E's the 'eadmaster of 5 fightin' academies an' is active in teachin' the next generation o' wrestlers an' fighters. 'E's a long standin' martial arts champion also, an' thus it's in 'is blood t' never stop. 'E an' I are both just now goin' over th' age o' thirty. I think in all honesty tha' either of us could take ye from post t' post. Most seem t' ferget tha' between th' two o' us, I was the last one t' wrestle an' tha' was in the last Chris Williams Memorial Cup, an' I made it t' the final 8 in' th' big event. An' I know tha' when we practice t'gether 'e can woop me arse pretty well. Wha'? We both still practice? 'Ow do ye be thinkin' I lost th' fifty pounds i gained while carryin' twins, there? I personally think tha' if 'e was in the mind t' do it, Ian would more then likely beat anyone in PTC, let alone a reject such as yerself. ---- 2 March 2006 *keeps from spitting out her coffee* Gee Fey, didn't hold back did ya? Well then the truth needs to be bloody well told.... What are your feelings towards me? ---- 2 March 2006 Me 'old things back? Ye of all people should know better then t' be thinkin' tha'. Trust me, Patricia, darling. If I did'na like yet, then ye an me brother would 'ave been th' second ones t' know be'ind me own 'usband. Although I'll admit 'e did get in trouble fer 'idin' yer affair from me in th' first place. It's interestin' 'ow things change. I was one o' them who agreed t' yet testing t' see if ye were good enough t' be with a Prince o' the Seelie Courts. So ye know, I did basically go fer 'aitin' ye, t' toleratin' ye, t' actually likin' ye. Now if only ye'd do somethin' t' beat Blaise in keepin' the family name alive. 'E's ga' a 'ead start on yet with two girls. We need a boy in tha' generation
