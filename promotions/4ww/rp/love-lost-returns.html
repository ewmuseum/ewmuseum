Love lost returns... Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #875 Date: 8-17-04 Location: Plane trip to Japan (ooc: i appologize to all those who read and like my work as i realize this is a let down... I am in no way satisfied with this work, but this is the best I could do this week... I dunno... I just suck ass this week.) My friend, All is right now as it seems… I have my Angel back. I may have lost to Darek, but at this point… it doesn’t matter. To think… Patti caused this to happen. It makes me sick. She is such a twisted and vile person. She is going to pay. She has caused too much pain to too many people. It’s not right… she’s hurt Darek, Angel, myself, and so many others… apparently Bullet too. The poor man didn’t even know. What a sick bitch I am growing tired of these late night plane trips. This allows me to travel unbothered for the most part though. I am able to think and clear my head. Angel is sleeping next to me… I didn’t even truly understand how much she meant to me until she was gone. I love her… I truly understand that for sure now. We will be in Japan in a short while as it seems. Now I am up againstBlaise McClancey… or is it Shoudou? It’s all the same. It was the same man. I have no respect for this man. He’s gotten in my way before, but it will not happen again. Hmm, I think Angel is waking up… I’ll go now Till next time, Kanton Journal Entry 8-15-04 +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~ Kanton closed his journal then and turned to his side. Angel was beginning to stir. Kanton looked on fondly at his heavenly creature. She slowly opened her dark gazing eyes and looked up at Kanton who was smiling. She softly reached her hand towards Kanton’s head and ran her fingers through his hair. “You’re still awake?” Angel asked softly. She was still coming to from her short bit of rest. Kanton nodded. It seemed he had many things on his mind. He had a match with Blaise of course. It also would be giving him another shot at the South Wind Title… something he has come up short for twice now… both times he has lost to Darek DeAngleo. That did not bother Kris though, Darek was a great champion. It was an honor for Kris just to get another chance against the man he had much respect for. There were other things on Kanton’s mind though. “You’re strangly quiet… why are you looking at me like that anyway?” an embarrassed smile crossed Angel’s face as she spoke. “I’m just glad to have you back. Yes also, I do have many things on my mind. I have a bad feeling about this match… something tells me I won’t be able to succeed.” Kanton spoke and it seemed as if the life just wasn’t in him. He had come up short two times in a row now. It seemed as if he was not ready. Doubt had begun to fill his mind. On top of this, he had other worries. “I know there’s something else. What aren’t you telling me?” Angel couldn’t figure out what Kanton was keeping from her. He seemed so clouded… so closed off. This was odd as it seemed that Kris told her everything. “Kris… talk to me…” “Alright, it’s just everything going on with me… from that bitch Patti playing games to me losing… I just don’t know if I have it in me anymore. I’m starting to have doubts. The only positive thing I have going for me is the fact that I have you back,” Kanton spoke as he looked deeply into Angel’s eyes. She noticed something in him. It was almost as if he lost a spark. She didn’t know what to do for him. Kanton though, was a mix of emotions and thoughts. He was being pulled in too many directions… Kanton sighed deeply as he slumped in his chair. “Don’t worry… I’ll be fine,” Kanton flashed a loving smile at Angel and did his best to look confident. She didn’t buy it, but she still played along. She had to be supportive. It was then that she laid her head on his shoulder. She fell asleep in the arms of the man she loved. Kanton stroked her hair fondly and thought to himself. He had to be strong for her. He couldn’t let her down. He sighed in deeply. It almost felt as if his world was crumbling down all around him. Even if it did though… it wouldn’t matter… he still had his Angel
