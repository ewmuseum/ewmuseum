Maelstrom 3 RP 2 La Envidia Mata 4WW Exposures #597 Date: 3/23/2004 Location: We open up to a shot of a pair of sunglasses sitting ontop of a wooden stool. Noise can be heard in the background. As we look up we see we are in a bar. The lady behind the bar is all dressed up with the makeup and a wig that makes you cringe when you look. The camera's realising this quickly take a turn away and are now looking straight at the pool table. On the pool table is none other than 4WW new superstar La Envidia Mata. After sinking the black ball he walks to the table at the side and picks up 2 rolls of money. Obviously gambling. An old looking man wearing one of those army hat's approaches Envidia at the table. Man: Oh so you think your good do you? Well lets see you beat a seasoned bar pro. Envidia: Bring it on old man. Envidia sets up the table and places one roll of the money back down on the table. The old man breaks. When it comes to Envidia's turn he doesn't miss and in 7 shots he wins the match taking yet another roll of money. Envidia: I guess I just took your championship. Now get the f*ck outta my face. Envidia slams down the pool cue and takes a seat on the stool in which his sunglasses were resting. He orders a drink and realizes the cameras are here so he turns face to face with them. Envidia: I asked the cameras to show up today because I have business to take care of. Mainly the jack@ss Josh Starwind but first off I need to talk about Sean Summers. Now Sean if I didn't hear Sean's name in Chris Wrestling's promo I wouldn't of even knew you were in this company. Your name to me isn't big enough to be in the 4 Winds Tournament and basically you will also feel the wrath of Envidia. I had to take a second look at your promos and I must say it made little or no sense. You don't know me and I see you also are probably going to take the tactic of trying to piss me off. Like I said days ago Josh Starwind business is my business and I don't have any plans to go into a feud with Josh Starwind until the future in other words until he gets to my level. I told everyone that right now the only thing on my mind is Maelstrom 3 and no other distractions will get in my way. Your quite confident Sean that you are going to beat Canadian Perfect. You need to think things over before deciding your going to win. First off you have easily looked past Chris Wrestling. You have looked past the other superstars taking part in the 4 Winds Tournament especially me. You Sean are nothing to La Envidia Mata and I will take pride in embarrassing you and beating you if you get in the South Wind Contenders Open or in the Tournament against me. Infact no scratch that, you mean so little to me that I wouldn't even notice if I beat you. You are just another jack@ss that needs to be put in his place. You can say what you want and do what you want but when it comes down to it, like I've said I'm going to Maelstrom 3 to win the South Wind Contenders Open and face the champ and I'm winning the South Wind title and then I will win the 4 WInds Tournament. You seem to think that you really are going to be left standing in the ring. YOUR WRONG boy. I'll prove it to you and when I do you will realize not to mess with La Envidia Mata. I signed the contract the other day and they have entered me in a South Wind Contenders Open and the 4 Winds Tournament. Envidia: Ok Josh Starwind you are beginning to seriously get on my last nerve. I can put up with sh*t for a while but you have went over board. I would say you have crossed the line but you have smashed the f*cking line. You make me laugh Josh. You just like everyone else think you are un-beatable and can take on all comers. This Contenders Open will go to me. Not many other people have much to say about it. Josh Starwind you aren't no tough son of a b*tch and neither are you immortal. You have weaknesses just like everyone else. The task of every match outcome is find your opponent's weakness, work on them then put the final nail in the coffin and secure the win. Battle Royal's however work in a different way. The objective of the game is throw your opponent over the top rope and his or her in your case, anyway their feet have to touch the floor. Now anybody can come into the ring and throw someone out. Its not that hard. Alot of people have been saying I don't deserve to be the number 1 contender just yet as I've just arrived. They may be right but in this Contenders Open I'll show them that the perfect number 1 contender has to be La Envidia Mata. No other man can hold the honor, the pride and the skill to be a South Wind champion like La Envidia Mata can.
