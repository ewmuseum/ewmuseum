The Fight... Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #586 Date: 21/03/04 Location: An Airport Maelstrom III: Legacy… So many matches, so many competitors, everyone looking to prove themselves, show themselves to the world, let everybody know who’s the best. But there is only one best, only one… Four Winds Champion… Only one who can spit in the face of the others and say “I am the best” and actually mean it. I guess that’s why we’re having this tournament to see who is truly… The Best… But is it really necessary? I mean do we really need to see who’s best or do we know it already? Chances are we probably already do know who is going to win, who is going all the way to the top, deep down we all know who is destined for greatness… des•ti•ny... 1. The inevitable or necessary fate to which a particular person or thing is destined; one's lot. 2. A predetermined course of events considered as something beyond human power or control: “Marriage and hanging go by destiny” (Robert Burton). 3. The power or agency thought to predetermine events: Destiny brought them together Now I don’t care what you call it, I don’t care what you think. When it comes down to it everything is written, everything has been planned and there is no delaying the inevitable. No matter how small or large the decision is it has been written and it is going to cause an equal reaction… Life… It’s worthless, unless…unless you have a purpose. Do you have a purpose? Maybe, maybe not. All I know is that this is mine; it is my destiny to become the Four Winds Champion. I don’t care if you’re Mike Marvelous or Fnord or Jerms. If you’re a member of Porn Inc. or a member of the KnightMare Brigade it doesn’t matter I will beat you, I will win… It’s already been written… ---------------------------------------------------- Lee is standing in an airport waiting for his plane to arrive. He is wearing a large black leather coat, a black shirt under it and some white khakis. It is dark outside probably about 2 or 3 in the morning. Not many people are in the waiting area and the ones that are, are sleeping or close to it. Lee looks at his watch. “Two thirty three the plane should be here soon” he mumbles under his breath. He wanders over to the flight display screen. He runs his hand down the screen until he finds his flight. “Looks like it’s on time” says a voice. “Huh?” Lee turns around. A young man with short black hair wearing a black turtle neck and sweat pants is standing there. “And who the hell are you?” asks Lee. “What you don’t remember me?” questions the man. “Should I” replies Lee in a sarcastic tone. “Does this ring a bell” says the man as he pulls down the shirts neck to reveal a massive scar. “Sorry but I have given a lot of people scars…” Lee laughs. “How bout these?” the man rolls up right sleeve to reveal a burn, but not just any burn, a burn in the shape of a pentagram. Lee’s eyes widen. “Didn’t think so…” says the man only to be interrupted by Lee “I have had enough out of you”. With that Lee grabs the man by hair and slams his head into the screen repeatedly. The man finally counters by elbowing Lee in the jaw; Lee wanders back a few paces and looks back to reveal a trickle of blood down the right side of his lip. The man on the other hand is much worse off with a blood pouring down his forehead. He lunges at Lee only to be flipped over in a Judo style trip. “Come On! You must be able to do something more then that.” Taunts Lee. “Arrrgg” yells the man getting up and tackling Lee down trying to punch his but only to be blocked and over powered by Lee. Lee twists the man’s arm and stands up holding his arm in almost an arm wench position. With all the commotion and yelling many of the people who were sleeping had awoken and are watching intently. “Is that all you got?” questions Lee. “Not quite” replies the man reaching into his pocket with his other hand and pulling out a pocket knife. He swings at Lee, Lee lets go and dodges backwards. The man continues swinging it until at Lee vigorously. By now Lee is backed against the screen. “Now you die” says the man attempting to stab Lee but to only be caught and kicked in the gut. The man moans as he drops the knife and coughs out blood. With this opportunity Lee throws the man into the screen shattering the glass. As the mans limp body hangs in the broken glass Lee bends over and picks up the knife and places it in the mans back. He heads back to the waiting area just as the plane arrives. He boards without hesitation and acts as nothing is wrong. The few people who saw were speechless and didn’t believe it, he was off the hook or was he?
