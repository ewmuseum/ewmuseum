Original Sin Dark Angel 4WW Exposures #860 Date: 08/07/04 Location: Darek's Home - Calgary, AB Original Sin Darek DeAngleo ::[[Introduction]]:: The transition of life is vastly caused by the wrong doings of mankind's sins. Whether these actions had been taken for personal gain or as an act of malice against you, sin changes who we are. From the very fabrics of our beginning, the origin of sin even predates man's greatest sin of crucifying God's only beloved son. The rebellion first began with the a decision and then another, two people had changed humanity from paradise into this corrupt, vile, disease infested world of suffering. Adam and Eve rebelled, they would no longer remain subject to their creator. With her vainly decision to listen to lucifer, Eve had brought sin into the world. Lying to her, Lucifer lead Eve to believe that her creator had been withholding something even better from the two. To God he saw this act of Eve as a move for independence, a decision to run her own life and not live under the light of God. This decision had her labeled in an attempt to know right from wrong as one who tried to be like God. The first sin had been brought upon the world and their paradise, however Adam could have kept the dream alive... By his will he could have fought the temptation, but instead he too rebelled. Their decisions, their fate had determined the way the world would evolve. Proving themselves unworthy of their immortality, the two suffered the wretched results of sin. Eventually Adam and Eve did die, and now it has become speculated that the death and sins of this one man and woman, wrote the entire damning fate of our world and time. It was the decision of mankind to live outside the shelter and love of God, it was their desire to be free willed and independent, to fullfill their lustful desire of greed. The forbiden fruit had been taken and now the world was doomed to pay the price. Evil has evolved into an everyday part of life, killings, crimes, daily people break one or two of the commandments, and even wars being carried out in the name of God. The world has become hell, when we stand before the judgement of God, will he accept us into heaven or will we be damned to live in this hell we created for ourselves. The reason things have gone wrong, the origin of sin. Mankind shall now and forever more until it's final demise, feel the suffering caused by the decisions made in a place long ago and so far away. The world was what we were to create for ourselves, the absolute corruption and greed of the human soul can now be attributed for the decline of moder society. It was the sins of our ancestors, the original sin, followed by the murder of God's only son, that hd already begun to shape the fate for the future. Mankind would be damned to live the rest of their pathetic existence in this modern hell. ________ ::[[Darek's Thoughts]]:: In a sense, we are our own God. He gave us the decision to live in the light of his life and instead, humanity chose independence, their sins have amassed and now the world cries for God to redeem them. We have have been left to fed for ourselves, but that's not to say the lord isn't watching. We may have prechosen our fate, we may have walked away from the wisdom of our creator but he would never stop loving us. ilived the last five years of my life through corruption, anger and greed. I was allowed to be consumed by evil and now, through the actions of Patricia Anderson, her sins opened my eyes to this hell I've been living in. My world has transformed into something that barely even ressembles that of what each of you can see. My world is tainted, painted red and black, the demons show me those who 'deserve' to be punished, the demons guided me through this red and black haze. Here I stand, capable of knowing my actions, capable of understanding the consequence and willing to right the wrongs I had created. Possession is the based for desire. The lost soul must have a reason to reamin in our world, and it must have a contract to consume the will and body of a person. The demon or demons that possess Patricia have sent her on a collision course, eventually her high ride will crash and all that she knows will crumble around her. She is possessed by greed, she only wants the best, to be the best and to have the best. Her unfathomable setup of the Shadow's Ascension, her allies by her and her fiancee, Sean McClancy, that was the lone thought upon my mind now. She left Bullet and Dark Angel out for a massacre while she was in the back having, I'm sure, what was a terrific time. Yet, I cannot help but wonder if perhaps Dark Angel did get through to her heart, because I know she got through to his. I constantly feel the pain and ache, the trauma. Dark Angel devoted himself to the well-being of Patti Anderson, and ultimately it was his well-being that he should have been concerned with. Of course, The KnightMare Brigade were going to pay for their actions. I have vowed vengeance upon the stable that once called Dark Angel brother. Well Dark Angel is no more, he was nothing more than an alter persona that seemed to control the fate of my life. Instead, I am in control and while Dominic lays dormant in the dark recess of my soul, I know that the KnightMare Brigade will own up to their sins. If I don't judge them her in the Four Winds, God will in Heaven. Even more important than the KnightMare Brigade was the biggot they once referred to as Shoudou. The reasons for my pain, the cause of this damnation, the now known as Blaise McClancy and family of the notorious leader of the KnightMare Brigade. After all the time Dark Angel spent protecting Patricia, she could not even learn once that her new enemy, the assasain was basically her family... and yet she still walked away from the war, instead of fighting like the champion I thought she was. The world has just begun to wittness the rebirth of a new era. The legacy and Dynasty that the Dark Knight had built for me was perfect, but all it needed was the touch of God. I have changed myself, things in life are going to be different now, the outcome of this war will soon come as what I ultimately want for the Winds, is a time, an era of peace. I, Darek DeAngleo, will subject myself to the same torture that Dark Angel did to define his career, only I will suceed where he failed. Though he was possessed and destined to bring forth the downfall of the Winds, Dark Angel was weak... he fell in love with Patti Anderson. A mistake, that I will not make. There is still a place in my heart for this woman, though she subjected Dark Angel and Bullet to the brutal onslaught of the KnightMare Brigade. For some reason, I am compelled to forgive, but forgiveness comes with a price. Her demon must be slain, her lust, that longing desire to be better than those around her, this all must suffer a poetic demise. It was Patricia Anderson's fiancee's KnightMare that killed the demon known as Dark Angel and soon it will be Darek DeAngleo that slays the demons that possess the soul of our coveted North Wind Champion. Kyo Kushinaga, the Outlaw, I wouldn't dare call him a man after what I have wittnessed; this being stands to rid Patricia of her throne, to take her crown and become the ruler of the Winds himself. This is forseeable and perhaps I could even live under his rule but the question Kyo must ask himself, the one that burns inside his heart, can he truly defeat Patti Anderson? And if so, can he defeat The Modern Messiah? Questions, inwhich the answers remain unknown, but hopeful in the eyes of the Outlaw. I can see the anticiaption in his eyes each time he looks at the North Wind Title. He wants it, he hungers for it, it will be the crowning achievement of his career... And again man falls to his desire for greed, their lust to be the best. I stand to battle Patricia not for her championship, but for the wrongs she had writen in the past. Dark Angel had given her his heart, his mind and even his body for protection and the vile, wretched betrayal that she commited warrants my attention. Dark Angel was a monster, I am the first to acknowledge this, but he wanted to be free and thought Patti Anderson would lead him to that freedom. Kyo wishes to battle for his will to dominate, just as Dark Angel had, it is a viscious cycle I know all too well. Make your choices wisely Kyo, for Maelstrom Five approaches with a ghastly fate for certain members of the Four Winds community. A community that is to be much like a family, but instead because of our individual freedom, we have created hatred, pain, suffering, kidnappings, theft and much more, even within our small escape from reality we created hell. Ian Childress stepped back long ago to watch his creation slowly fall to the hands of mankind and their sinful decisions. Truthfuly, I look forward to cleasning this wretched land free of it's skellitons. When the purification is said and done, the Four Winds may not only have a new ruler, but a new era for the ages. That era could have come much earlier than anticipated had it not have been for Kyo... The decisions that Kyo makes now will mark his life forever. Kris Kanton... Oh God, where to begin. The man befriended a misguided, mistreated monster. He found the goodness that Dark Angel wanted to free and became friends with it. Kris has been more of a brother to Dark Angel since his arrival in the Winds than Dark Angel's entire stay in the KnightMare Brigade. There is a difference between being genuine and wanted to satisfy the desire to win. Kyo, Patti, Fnord, they would all stop at nothing to be the best and in order to suceed in their missions as they attempted to remove their prospective threats. Fnord and his band of miscreants attempted to take me out... they half suceeded and Kyo, last week prevented Kris Kanton, who fought in my honor, from becoming the North Wind Champion. Another vile decision on behalf of the Outlaw, he disrespected me, Kris, and he cemented his position to his shot at the North Wind Championship. I will deal with the Outlaw personally whether by words or by actions, the two of us will come to an understanding. But now, the most important matter at hand was the battle inwhich I was soon to embark upon. Kris Kanton, Darek DeAngleo, a matter of pride, honor and business, two beings with exceptional presence but only one can win, only one can remain standing in the end. It's not personal, this fight between Kanton and myself will be a battle of skill, wits and ability, not a bloody massacre that people will be hoping for. There is a bond of respect between two individuals that cannot be broken and that bond will stand the test of time and war... ________ ::[[The Story]]:: Darek smiled at the being standing before him. His eyes lit up with anticipation as the man extended his hand forward and accepted the gift Darek had presented. Closing his hand, Darek nods in approval and steps back for a second. 'That will symbolize our undying memory, bond and sacrifice to a past. If we are to forget the past, how could we ever learn from our mistakes?' Darek looks the man in the eyes and then turns around, busying himself amoungst papers on a desk. 'You and I are bound together by fate, I can see this now and I know that you have yet to play your entire part in this epic tale of good and evil.' The man turns away from Darek and holds out his hand, examining a large golden band in his hand. he rolls it around through his fingers, almost ignoring Darek. Finally taking his eyes away from the gold, the man turns his head back to Darek to make eye contact. 'It's amazing, the things you come up with, vampire.' Syckle turns full circle to Darek and smiles deviously, 'I see you have embarked upon a mission, one to change who you've become, perhaps these rings are just a trinket value of waht you once accomplished?' 'It's not like that Jack. The rings are our past, our way to preserve what we know and have learned. I would never want to become that monster again, this 'trinket' as you call it, will help me remember what it was I did and who I had become.' Syckle removes a chain from around his neck and slides it through the ring, wrapping it around his neck, he closes the chain and allows the ring to dangle across his chest. 'Those memories have become my downfall, I will take this for now, but soon you will know where I stand. As you said, I've only just begun to play my role in the scheme of things, you vampire, you are about to be tested...' Darek's ears perk up at those words and he steps in towards Syckle, with a look of concern upon his face. 'Tested?' He asked mmediately, 'what do you mean?' 'I can't reveal everything to you, you will learn and see things just as I did. They came to me in visions and dreams, you and I are destined to each other, but our fate is going to be what you make of it.' He raises his eyes to Darek, who seems more than just concerned at this news, 'God is willing to forgive, but what you had done is inexcusable... to return to the Angelhood, to regain your worth you must suceed in the-' Darek cuts Jack off, 'the Trials of Redemption.' He runs his finger under his chin, knowing full well what the trials entailed, 'that means I have to find my mentor, a guide to lead me through the trials... Who the hell is capable of doing such a thing? The last Guide between Angel and God for the trials was sentenced of witchery and burned on the stake, that was so long ago...' Darek stops and a tear begins to fall, 'I have disrespected my kind, my way of life with Dark Angel's actions over the last five years. If I had been stronger, I wouldn't be in this position.' It was like a tidal wave had swept Darek into an undercurrent of hatred and suffering, he became the one and only Dark Angel. But now, he felt compelled to rid himself of the monster that had famed his career, he wanted to be free from the pain, free from the tyrany, the lies and the mindgames. Darek wanted nothing more than to live for himself, to be normal. Yet even though he fought to be glorified in the eyes of God, the trials would make Darek stronger and he knew this. Lifting his heavy eyes from the floor, Darek glances at Syckle for a moment. Syckle nods before making his way towards the exit. 'I have seen it Jack, the visions that haunt you. I see them aswell, you know very well that we share a similar fate. I know that you and I will need each other. I cannot stand alone, you know what your purpose is to be, you've seen it just as I have.' Darek motions for jack to stop and listen before reaching the door. 'You must give me an answer, are you, no will you be the one to guide me through the trails? Can you destroy Dark Angel?' Jack smiles while giving a small salute to Darek and turning the handle of the door, 'I don't have to do anything vampire. I do what is necessary, I vanquish demons, the ones I chose to, not the ones I am demanded to. If you want the help of this outlaw, you'll need to find a new tact, friend.' Syckle pulls the door open and steps to the outside closing it slowly behind him. Darek watches his one time enemy and now needed friend, leave. He steps back and leans up against the wall, shaking his head he tries to get a grip on the emotional roller coaster of his life. So much had changed from three weeks ago, the world now dawned an entirely new perspective to Darek. He felt good in helping others, he felt strong in guiding the weak, no longer did he feel the urge to maime, and dismember. Yet inside him, he still felt that blind anger, the swell of hatred build up within his soul. He knew Dark Angel and Dominic had to be destroyed, he also knew that Syckle had the means to do so... but how? Darek found himself drifting away into though, curiously trying to discover how Syckle could suceed where so many others had failed. Dark Angel had to die, Dominic had to be vanquished and sent back to hell, there would be no more demons to control the life of this being. Darek knew that within weeks, his whole life would begin to change, if only he could get Syckle to help him. Opening his eyes, he stares at the clock hanging on the wall, turning towards the staircase, Darek disappears to the upper half of his house. Swept away by the confusion and uncertainty that the future would hold, Darek found himself able to sleep, for the first time in a two weeks. ________ ::[[The Moral]]:: Sins have been around since the dawning of time, people would be punished for their sins by the creation of their own hell. This paradise that was built for mankind has been transformed into the definition of hades, plague, famine, disease, murder, all of it contributed to the overall appearance of our own prison. The world has been changed by our choices, society has built a set of laws and structures just as God had, their attempt to play God has failed now, just as God had with Adam and Eve. The Four Winds has become a distinct community within the world and even our bond would not be strong enough to stop the hatred, pain and suffering to consume this small corner of the world as well. The sins of our family members have brought a premature downfall to a once great home, now, the world stands it's best chance placing it's hopes and dreams into the hands of the one true Saviour. A being that once stood to bring death and destruction, an Angel that now stands to take the Trails of Redemption, Darek DeAngleo, the one who would now challenge the scriptures in Catholic dogma to ressume his pride and dignity... to watch over the Winds as an Elder Angel. This choir of Angels, specialized in seeing and knowing just about all. They wasn't much kept from these wise individuals, yet, not everyting could be revealled to them either or perhaps greed would consume them just as it had to Darek. Lucifer fell subject to the same greed and was vanquished to the underworld, where he created his own Heaven, on that bred warriors. God would not make the same mistake twice, banishing another of his creations would only create insurmountable odds. The power of sin was more than could be expected, in this day and age when lies and misfortune is common, the work of God is never done. In order to see the light of God's love, Darek knew he had to pass the trials and when that day came, he would watch over this community, keeping it from evil and harm. For some reason, Darek had always felt best when he was needed, in this time of peril and sin, a new Angel would be reborn. One to watch over the masses, one to guide people through misfortune, an Angel that would stand for truth and justice. The Modern Messiah, Darek DeAngleo.
