broken. Shawn Singleton 4WW Exposures #1222 Date: 4/2/05 Location: the airport. 'What is it you want, Shawn?' The words had broken the eerie silence that existed between the two of them for quite some time. The flight to Ireland had been delayed due to persistent and heavy rain and winds, diminishing the noise in the area. The few passengers that were still awake at this midnight hour actually outnumbered the overwhelmingly miniscule amount of passengers there were as a whole. 'Shawn...what is it that you want?' Shawn turned his head sideways, his brow raising as if he had just heard the question. He looked at The Hunter, a quizzical look on his face. 'What do you mean?' A voice on an intercom came on, announcing something about departures from gate 16 being on time. The Hunter checked his watch, then looked up at the arrival/departure screen to confirm. 'I mean, in this business, in your career, in 4WW, what is it that you want?' Hunter looked Shawn in the face, sitting across from him in his black wheelchair. Shawn shifted in his place, attempting to alleviate the awkwardness in his body from the painfully cheap red chairs the airport passed off as waiting seats. 'Success,' Shawn replied. Hunter rolled his eyes, gliding his fingers across the traction of his wheelchair's wheels. He was wearing a freshly pressed white collared shirt and black jeans. 'Of course you want success, Shawn, no one enters into any venture attempting failure. I mean, with specificity, what do you hope to accomplish, what is your goal?' 'To be the best.' 'And how will you go about that, Shawn?' 'Listening to you.' Hunter chuckled, nodding his head knowingly. 'Right, but is that truly what you believe?' 'Yes. I was with you every step of the way, Hunter, I know what you could do, what you were to the industry. And since...' Shawn stopped himself, looking away from The Hunter, glancing towards the ticket taking desk. He pulled his eyes back to Hunter. '...I owe it to you.' Oddly, The Hunter smiled after watching Shawn's uncomfortable movements. 'So, where do we go from here?' 'Belfast...in fifteen minutes,' Shawn said. 'You ready, you focused, you prepared?' 'Aren't I always?' Hunter shot him a cold glance, boring a hole right into his forehead. 'You want an answer to that?' Hunter asked. 'Did I or did I not make up for it, last week?' 'Shawn, regardless, you weren't supposed to lose. I told you that, I told you I don't accept failure. It wasn't a part of the plan, you dropping a match, and definitely not you losing a title. That's not the way I wanted.' Shawn stopped looking at him. 'What do you want this week?' 'Shiek Nasty...I want you to break him. Crush his mind, his body, his spirit. Punish him.' 'How bad?' 'Like I would have,' Hunter says, staring Shawn in the face. Shawn turned from him, unable to meet his gaze. He knew what Hunter meant, to what degree the brutality would need to go. He also knew that he had to do it, had to make someone else pay for his mistake. Shiek Nasty's fate was sealed right then. In the airport.
