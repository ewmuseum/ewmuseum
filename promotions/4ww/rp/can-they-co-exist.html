Can They Co-Exist Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #543 Date: 02/23/04 Location: Here, There, Everywhere Despise - transitive verb Etymology: Middle English, from Old French despis-, stem of despire, from Latin despicere, from de- + specere to look -- more at 1 : to look down on with contempt or aversion <despised the weak> 2 : to regard as negligible, worthless, or distasteful Despise, is not how I honestly feel about Corky, its more on how IT IS. Ever since Corky decided to grace the federation at the request of our illustrious Vice President of Talent, Lorena Lovecraft, she has been nothing but a pain in my bloody ass, and she’s made no small words about how much she wants to be the alpha dog in the federation. After last week, I showed her that I will not go away easy. I took her to the limits in the dog collar match up, but yet she still doesn’t understand that I am still the bigger dog in the fed. She is still living in this fantasy world that she will be the owner of me, and not the other way around. She will just have to get over it, and of course the darling, future, sister-in-law that is Fey McClancy isn’t helping matters much either. Fey decided that since Corky and I had so much going for us, why not do the one thing that would really set me off is team me with the bi-curious she-devil that needs to be put to sleep soon. But with the offer to go after the tag team titles against not one, but two teams I could not resist the urge to do at least make Fey happy and team with the self proclaimed “Dark Diva” out weighs the disgust and the despisement of being with that woman. I’ll do it for the sake of Fey, and the glory of gold, but for no other reason. For should we lose the gold for any reason other then legitimate reasons, I will not hesitate to turn on Corky. It will be her fault should the tag team titles go beyond our grasps. I will make sure that her days in 4WW will be the end of her career. But due to the “love” of my future sister-in-law, I and her will have to compete in a tag team lottery match to determine who will go onto the final match of the night to face two members of Featured Attraction and two members of the Knightmare Brigade. A match that spilled over once again into this week. Ian and Fey are looking at all the best teams that they can possibly create to offer some sort of challenge to the champs and the number one contenders. So to give them some real competition, they need a lethal lottery tag team match to make the odds more difficult, and like always the odds look to be stacked against me. But never fear, for I will go on to the next round to win the shot and take the tag team titles. The teams that are in this match, are made up of some of the finest talent that the federation has to offer. A fine battle for the fans of the great federation, but a lousy one for those teams that have come together. Teams from the already established stables of Porn, Inc., Knightmare Brigade, Featured Attraction, and other hastily put together teams that think they can and will hold dominance over the quest for gold. For like everyone else.....they will fail. Place: Somewhere Corky likes to be alone Time: Evening? Stalking along the night, Coricidon holds her hands deep into the black pockets of her coat while walking along next to the metallic fence. Barbed wire runs along the top as WARNING signs grace it every few feet. She pays no attention to the people on the other side although it would be oblivious for her not to realize them staring at her. The men stand around on the other side of the fence all positioning themselves on different parts of the picnic table while keeping their eyes locked along the black, skin-tight pants of the self-proclaimed Dark Diva. She might have a small piece of her that recognizes the stares but much of her brain continues to concentrate on the upcoming match where she must team with a woman who she has looked down on since stepping into the federation. Whistles begin to come from over the fence yet she still doesn’t care because reliving the moments of the Dog Collar match makes the new scars on her body ache and pulse. She stops and bends down so that her hands hold onto her knees for strength as the men at the table all hop off of it and move towards where Coricidon is as she lets out moans of pain. “What the fuck is up with this bitch?”, asks one of the men as they point towards Coricidon breathing quite hard. Coricidon looks up to notice the men as she backs away from them with a confused look in her eyes. They stand staring at her as she notices they all adorn the same outfits with numbers above the shirt pockets. She grows a small smile as she remembers hearing them whistle and retort the bad remarks towards her as she steps forward and looks at a slightly chubby white man with a bald head who is beginning to lick his chops at her slim body. She steps forward towards him, as she makes sure not to touch the fence although she begins to think that the other men do not happen to know that the fence is actually dangerous. With that, she gets on her knees and looks up at the man nodding her head while he unbuttons the pieces to his jumpsuit and has his hands on his penis. Coricidon begins to nod with her smile growing widely at the crucifixion that is about to take place as he pulls out his ‘whopper’ while she begins to wave her hands forward, waiting for him to stick it through the fence. “Let me take it all big boy”, she says while waiting for the dumbfounded man to actually do it. He begins to narrowly slip it through as she waits until it is long enough to actually grab. It gets far enough and she grabs hold of it and pulls forward as the fence lights up with electrical currents as the man’s pain comes through his mouth in the form of screams while the other men stand back and look in shock. She makes sure to get her hand away in time for the current not to come back through her as she hops back even more gleeful then ever as she turns and continues on while burnt flesh begins to become the scent moving through the street. But not even that aggression was enough to rid Coricidon of what she is feeling right now. For the problem of her and Patti having to participate as a tag team continues to tread on every piece of her mind, leaving it as the only thing that she can think about. Finding herself now in front of the hotel in which she is staying in, she walks over to the sign that reads ‘Vacancy’ and takes a seat on the brick montage that goes around it while continuing to think about her and Patti’s tag team relationship. “I cannot believe it”, Coricidon says. “I have actually been paired with Patti and for a shot at the East Wind Titles nonetheless. While I want the gold so much, can I truly trust Patti? She wants gold just as much as me but she also cost me the gold and that makes me not want to trust her.” Coricidon shakes her head as the drama overtakes her. She puts her head in her hands and begins to shake why this has all happened to her. Then again, she also remembers much about Patti and their match as the two were grappling around and touching their bodies together. It gets her a little excited as she looks back up and takes her hands away from her body, the excitement taking over. “But then again”, she says getting even happier. “Me and Patti, the same side of the ring. Oooh, this could be good. I could take the gold… And get a new friend on the side. Patti, if you need me, no matter how much we hate each other, I will give you what you need.” She grows a smile on her face as the night air blows harder against her body. Place: Patti’s own thoughtful world Time: Who knows.... Patti sits on another rented suite in a fabulous hotel room that the company had paid for. Her mind still irritated with the thought of having to team with the “Dark Diva” to win the tag team gold. But whom else could she tag with now that Jade was off attempting to find herself once again. The only thing that Jade left was her dog Lucifer, with a simple note asking her to take care of him until she could send for him. Patti chuckled at the check that came enclosed to have him fixed so that he wouldn’t be tempted to try to mate with Princess. Patti laughed at the thought of what an Australian Sheep hearding dog and a St. Bernard mix would look like. She guessed that the world would never know for since she painfully had the vet fix Lucifer. Patti sighed as she got of up off the bed and paced annoying around the room. Her foot steps falling softly on the ugly green carpet that graced the room. Princess and Lucifer watched her pace like a mad woman. Her stride going faster and faster, almost as if she were waiting on something. Her mind kept wondering what her future sister-in-law was up too. She knew Fey well enough to know that her teaming with Corky was a plan of some sort. Patti wasn’t sure exactly what it was, but she knew with Fey there was always was a reason. Patti continued to pace. The local news channel was airing a show talking to some of the 4WW stars that were entering into the lotto for what they believed was their chance for gold and glory. Patti’s inner voice told her that there would be only one winner standing tall at the end of both battles. Patti just could not shake the feeling that a set up was coming her way. A way of keeping her down from achieving the goal and dreams of hers since she was a child. But nonetheless she would be content to climb the ladder of success the way she had been taught by Fey’s own brother, still something didn’t seem right with this entire seen. A week ago she battled a woman that took her to the extremes. A dog collar match was harder to contain her anger that was raging inside of her. She saw that Coricidon was a great threat to her. A threat to her dominance over the federation. Her dominance over being what she had become was threatened, but yet......she still sensed something within the one that called herself the ‘Dark Diva’ that she couldn’t quite place. Could it be that she was like herself and that she was brought into the federation as a guide to Patti’s quest to be the best? Is it possible that the gods were sending her a sign that Coricidon was Athena in human form? Is it possible that Aphrodite had sent her own sister to aid her in her quest to become the best? Patti could not shrug the feeling that there was more to this woman known as Coricidon. And she had a feeling that this woman was her path to claiming glory. A glory that started with the South Wind Title and now a glory that is leading to the East Wind Title. The tag team title, a piece of gold that will once again be around her waist. A title that she will claim with the use of Coricidon to capture the gold that she needs to secure her place in the wrestling world. Patti began to pace more and more. Her mind swirling once more with the frustrations of the upcoming battle. Her mind and body battling to keep the other in line. Her mind itching to play games, her body itching to do the dance of battle that needs to be done within the squared circle. Patti stopped pacing, and stared at the musical tones that came from with the confines of her purse. A musical tone that told her a call was coming in. Patti walked the few steps to her purse and grabbed the phone and slammed it open to see who would rudely call out to her in her time of figuring out the things in the world. The phone kept ringing as Patti stared at it. Once it stopped, it would ring again. Patti picked up on its third cycle of ringing. She knew that if she didn’t answer it soon, Coricidon may start getting impatient. Patti pulled the phone up to her ear and put on her best performance to date. “Corky, love. It’s sooooo good to hear from you. I’m ready for this week, too. Let’s show everyone what Fey has done. Ok, talk to you later.” Patti slammed the phone closed, tossed back into the purse. She sighed as she grabbed her training gear and headed to the nearest gym to work out her frustrations.
