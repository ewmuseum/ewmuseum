When in Rome

24 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When in Rome Luke Chadbourne 4WW Exposures #1188 Date: 2/24/05 Location: Rome, Italy The 4 Winds blow through the seven hills of Rome as t our scene fades in to clips of the great structures built by the ancient Roman empire. Clips of grand churches and momumental buildings fill the screen. Then our scene fixates on one building in particular. A building where warriors tested themselves and battled to the death: Blood of early christians spilled out onto the soil, And all for the amusement of on lookers. Today the Collesium is nothing more then a shell of what it use to be, yet millions come every year to marvel at it's sight. The broken stone of a once great building is all that remains. Then our scene cuts to the streets of Rome, not far from the Collesium. A long black limo jets down the street, the license plate reading 'Cash Money'. The limo pulls up right in front of the camera crew and stops. The camera crew moves toward the back of the limo, to the door waiting to see who will emerge. Just then the door swings open, blocking the camera's view inside. The camera crew is a bit discouraged as a young man with a bright red mo hawk emerges, wearing a black button down shirt and nice black dress pants, all set off by the red tie. The mo hawk clad young man takes a deep breath of the cool crisp air and then takes a few steps away from the limo door as the camera tries to see if there is anyone else in the limo. Mo hawk Clad Young Man: Come on man. Lets go. Then seconds later, Luke Chadbourne stands up out of the limo. His hair is done perfectly, his bangs standign straight up and the rest laying neatly on his head. Luke is looking sharp as he slams the limo door and signals for it to leave. His black suit, accented with a baby blue under shirt is perfectly pressed and hangs off his body very becomingly. Unlike his companion, Luke has opted against the tie and has the top two buttons of his under shirt open. Luke takes in a deep breath of the Roman atmosphere before speaking. Luke: This is it Dave. This was the center of the Roman empire for over 2 millenia. Everywhere you look there is history. Look up there. Luke point up past the camera as Dave and the camera man both look to what he is pointing at. The camera is again locked on the Colliesium, or should I say the ruins of the Colliesium. Then the camera pans back over the horizon and locks on Luke and Dave standing on the side of the road admiring the Colliesium's grander. Luke: In anceint times, that would be equal to our modern day wrestling arena. There millions of men would square off to see who the better man was. But the only way out of there is death. Lucky for my opponents, the wrestling ring is a little more forgiving. Shit, I don't want to kill anybody, I just want to beat the hell out of them. Dave flashes Luke a sinical look as he pulls out his pack of cigerates and pulls one out. Dave: You cocky son of a bitch. You think that you are God's gift to wrestling don't you?? Luke's arrogance take over now as his smile widens, showing off his perfectly straight teeth. His body language beams with confidence as he shrugs his shoulder. Dave slides his pack of cigs back his his pocket and pulls out a red lighter. He sparks the lighter and lights his cigerate that is now hanging from his lips. Luke: Hey, when your right, your right. I am God's gift, but not only to the wrestling world. Why should I hide his gift fron the rest of the world? Dave: Because your not God's gift to anyone. Your more like one of the plagues. Luke: Dave,.so sinical. You just have yet to realize how great I really am. Luke and Dave start walking down the streets of Rome as they contiune to chit chat. Dave: If your so great, then where is your title? You haven't done anything to prove your greatness. Luke: Ohh really. Have you been under a rock for the past week or something?? Didn't you see the way I man handled the Tempest Champion, and the challenger for the belt. I destroyed them. Dave: *Sarcasticly* Wow, that's impressive. *Normal conversation* Look who the Tempest Champion is. Shit it's only Shawn Singleton. Who is the hell is Shawn Singleton any ways. He's just a jobber, not like he really matters anyways.And besides, it's not like you beat him, you just jumped him after his match. Luke: You act like I jumped the guy from behind while he was in the middle of a match or something. I cam right out to the ring, stood toe to toe with him and then destoryed him. And if I get a chance to I will do it again. I got that kid's number and he knows it. That Tempest title is as good as mine. Dave starts to chuckle as Luke starts to get fired up. Dave just casually puffs on his cigerette as he utters his next words. Dave: That is if you can beat the former Tempest and South Wind Champion, Lusious Lady Luck this week. Luke: If?? If?!? If I can beat Lady Luck?? Come on Dave. You know that there isn't on person in that locker room who can beat me far and square. And besides the way I see it Triple L has run out of Luck lately. Dave seems a little puzzled now as confusion takes over his facial expression. Dave: What the hell are you talking about?? Within the past two or three months she has been Tempest Champion and most recently South Wind Champion. Seems to me that she is having some pretty damn good Luck. Luke: Well, to a simpleton like you it would look like she is on one hell of a roll. But look at it from the other side. In the past couple of months or so she has been Tempest and South Wind Champion. And within the past couple months she has lost both of those titles. Tempest title to 'The Done Deal' Paul Williams and the South Wind title to Uyko. It looks to me like she is in a bit of a slump. Dave: Ok, say she is in a little bit of a slump.That is just going to make her all that more focused on beating you. Luke: That's just it old friend. As hard as she may try, She just can't get it done. That Lusious Lady is all out of Luck. And *Pointing to himself* 'The Cash Money Kid', I am on the top of my game. And after this Saturday at Stormfront, After I beat Triple L, I am offically the Number one contender to the Tempest title. Then it is just a techicality of beating Singleton, and I am the Tempest Champion, as I should be. But that is just the begining. Just Like the emperors of this great anceint empire, I will conquer not only the Tempest Title, but 4WW as a whole. Dave: Now what the hell are you babbling about? A devious smile covers Luke's face as he lets out an evil little chuckle. Luke: HA..HA..HA. Dave, your my best friend, but not even you are at liberty to know all my secrets. Just know that soon enough 4WW will bow at our feet, as we stand at top the mountain looking down on all the beaten and battered bodies we have left in our path. And as we stand upon that mountain, only one classic phrase fits so perfectly. Vinne, vinne ,vechie. I came, I Saw and I conquered. Luke continues walking and chuckling to himself as Dave stops and tries to comperhend what Luke just uttered. He is completly dumbfounded, and that is really weird cause Luke is his best friend. The camera focuses in close on Dave's face, racked with confusion, looking at Luke as he continues down the streets of Rome. Then our scene slowly fade to nothing.
