Meet and Greet Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #78 Date: Saturday August 2, 2003 Location: 4 Winds Arena Target - noun - an object of ridicule, attack, or abuse. From the looks of things, the target is painted on the back of our champion. Patti has become the target of more then one on the Four Winds Wrestling roster. Let us take a look at who all is targeting Patti. First on the list is Fey McClancy-Childress. The mighty CEO. This feud has been going on since the days in the other federation. It all started when Patti came stateside to see the world of wrestling up close and personal. She came to California for a mini-vacation. While in California, she ran into the 'Irish Bastard' at a local pub. The two hit it off spectacularlly and made a trip back to his hotel room. After a spectacular night of one that both wouldn't Sean moved on to the next city, leaving a besotted girl in his wake. Patti continued to follow Sean from city to city. She was already enthralled with him. He had to be apart of her life. And it finally happened when Patti snuck into his hotel room, where once again an idea was formed. She told Sean she was his Number One Fan. A radio show contest, a picture perfect appearance, a nod of approval from Sean, his sister Fey, and Ian Childress, put our heroine at ringside alongside 'The Irish Bastard'. Even with the full disapproval of Patti being at ringside with her brother, Fey remained professional about it. And as we know, Sean chased Patti all the way back to her native land, captured her heart, and the rest is history. Next, on our list of people is 'Simply Stunning' Sean Summers. The resident adelfi malakas, the homosexual wanker. Oh wait, he cant be that now, can he? He has a woman chasing him. A woman that thinks he's all that, and no its not Patti. Patti is smarter then that. She's using the 'blonde twit' to keep Summers off balance. As to the reason why Summers has targetted Patti, is simple. Patti is a woman, a woman that learned how to wrestle from the best in the business, Summers reasons still aren't all that clear, other then he intends to show the whole world that Patti belongs in a coma, not in the wrestling ring. Scarey thought, huh? 'The Darling of the 4WW' in the hospital again? Nah, don't worry? The first time they faced each other, Summers went and got himself disqualified for using a foreign object. Then in a fatal three way for the South Wind Title, Patti won that match, with a huge assist to Eliza Murphy. Third, hmmm, Big Scarey Guy Who Doesn't Talk. A man from an organization that Patti knew of, but had not interacted with in anyway.In other words, she avoided him. As to why she's targeted? Good question. No real reason. It could be a minor case of wrong locker room. As to looking at the facts involving him, he was in her locker room, went through her stuff, and kinda got to see something that only McClancy gets to see. Patti isn't taking this threat seriously, yet. And fourth on the list, good old Pajaro. The trelos nothos, the crazy bastard, that has developed an obsession with Patti rather ample body parts. My guess would be that he has had one to many bad chillidawgs and flat soda to know what is going on. Another possiblity is he, like Summers, is a tool being used by the CEO to get rid of Patti from Sean's side. This feud should bring plenty of laughs for thte audiences in the near future. Four people, four possible motives to target her. Is the champ worried? She doesn't seem to be. Is she concerned about everything that Fey is going to throw at her? Nah, no reason to be, sooner or later, Fey will come to accept Patti as her future sister-in-law. What about Summers, a match down the road will sooner or later bring that little feud to an end. BSG, not sure where that one will lead, only time will tell. Pajaro? If rumors are true, Patti may have her hands full with this trelos nothos for a while. ___________________________________________________________ Place: Outside Four Winds Arena Event: Ticket Sales/Mett and Greet Patti Anderson and Sean McClancy Date 24 Hours Prior to Storm Front ___________________________________________________________ Fade into a line in front of the ticket window of the Four Winds Arena. People of all ages are awaiting the favorite couple of the Wour Winds ogranization. That couple you may ask? Why the South Wind champion and her valet, 'The Darling of the 4WW' Patti Anderson and 'The Irish Bastard', Sean McClancy. Also noted to be in the area is Matt Cummings. Should be interesing since Patti and Matt have a history. Cummings stepped out of the van, surveying the crowd out front of the arena. He rememberd the last time he saw a crowd this large. A day back in August when the current South Wind champion put him through a table for asking a personal question about her chest size. He looks over to where he will be. He notices that his spot is close to where the woman he didn't want to be around. He shrugged, a job is a job. He heads over to where his assistant is setting upt the cameras and microphones. He was just not looking forward to sharing center stage with either Anderson or that man of hers. He noticed that one of his assistance was busy putting up some of the equipment. He wanders over to him and proceedes to help him with it. The two lift a couple of monitors up onto the tables. That way, the fans can see what kinda programing they have missed and some of the promos that have been cut leading up to today's show. After a few minutes, Cummings looked at his assistant. Matt grabbed the hankerchief outta his back pocket. Then proceeded to wipe the sweat from his face. Cummings: Why did Fey McClancy-Childress send me here? She knows Ms. Anderson and I don't get along? Why couldnt' she have sent Murphy or MacLooney? Assistant: Because Murphy is busy trying to make amends with Summers. MacLonney wants no part of either Anderson or McClancy. And seeing how you are the senior interviewer, she thought you would be better qualified for the job. Cummings lets lose with a sight. He guessed he had to choose his words a little more carefully this time. He wouldn't want to a repeat of what happened before. Cummings took a look at hist watch and realizing it was almost time to kick off the Meet and Greet. The champ would soon be entering the area for the Meet and Greet, maybe the interview would go very smoothly. Fade out ___________________________________________________________ Fade In To offsite waiting area for Sean and Patti ___________________________________________________________ Patti and Sean are together, she sitting on his lap, head resting on his shoulder. Her title belt sitting across her knee. Patti was still amazed that she got the belt. She must remember to thank Eliza for her help in that. Maybe a good solid chair shot to Summers head might make him fall for the blonde twit. Patti: Is it about time for this bloody thing to start? Why in the bloody world did your sister send up here and not the World Champ? Phoenix would be better at this then me. Patti began to pout. She looked down at the title belt and then remembered why she had to do this. It was her duty as champion to represent the federation at whatever even they sponsored. She sighed again. She hopped off of Sean's lap, wandering over to the dark curtain to where she would be coming out to great the fans. Her title belt, she placed over his shoulder. Patti: Well isn't this just bloody well grand. What's he doing here? Sean: Wot? Patti: The man with more 'Mattitude' then the Hardy Boy himself is here. Is your sister out to timoro, punish me or him? Sean shook his head as he is racked with laughter. Sean: Be nice. Dont'cha be lettin him get to ye. Patti looked out at the crowd forming around the box office and around the meet and greet area. She sighed and looked down at her outift. Her standard wrestling outfit had been replaced by the desire to wear something more fan friendly. She had decided to promote her newest shirt, one that was selling rather well. Her 'Patti Anderson: 100% Darling, 100% Psycho Bitch, Twice the Woman You'll EVER Be' was selling very well. They just couldn't keep them in stock. She oppted to wear a tight pair of leather pants, something to show off her legs. Her trademark low cut boots completed the outfit. Her hair, in her trademark pig tails and her purple/black baret completing the outfit. Patti sighed again. She began to pace the floor, being made to wait was something she didnt like doing. She'd rather be off doing something more entertaining, and one thing came to mind as she stole a glance at the man who just stepped up to her side. Sean walks up behind her, rubbing her shoulders. Relaxtion always works for her. She turns to jelly and can handle anything that is thrown at her. He feels her tensions leaving her body. Her stress disappearing. Maybe tonight he'd change her training, maybe something more relaxing. After a few minutes, a security guard pokes his head into the back. Guard: Its time. Ready to make a few thousand fans happy? Patti nods and prepares to follow Sean through the curtain. Fade Out ___________________________________________________________ Fade Back In ___________________________________________________________ Cummings stood standing by the curtain. Thousands upon Thousands of fan's were screaming, chanting PATTI! PATTI! PATTI!! Others were patiently waiting for the 'Darling' and 'The Bastard' to appear. Cummings: Alright, Ladies and Gentlemen. In just a second Patti Anderson and Sean McClancy will be coming through this curtain. Make sure you scream loud and long. And, as a big treat, Patti will be answering the remarks that her competition has said about her. So get ready. No sooner then Cummings finishes his little speach, Patti's theme 'All The Things She Said' hits, Out through the curtain comes McClancy, Patti holding tightly on to his hand. You can see the nervousness showing. Her title belt slung over her right shoulder. She timidly takes the mike that has been offered her. Patti Efprosdektos anenusturas! Welcome Fans! I'd like to thank you all for coming out today to see your South Wind Champion. It was a tough battle, but with your support and cheers, I manged to survive the challenge of Sean Summers and Pajaro to win this title. I will do my best to keep this title for as long as I can handle the challenges. And of course, cannot forget to say a HUGE thank you to Eliza Murphy, cause we all know without her assistance, I wouldn't be here as your champion. And what about Cummings here? How about a huge round of applause for him? Didn't he do an awesome job of introducing myself and McClancy? The crowd comes to life. Yelling, screaming, wolf whistles, and jeers follow. Cummings: Alright, just calm down. Patti, how would you feel about answering the comments that your opponents have stated about you? Patti: Let's do it. The sooner I answer these silly comments, the sooner I can get on to interacting with my fans. Cummings: Alrighty then. Let's go to our first set of comments. Several monitors in the room begin to flicker to life, but nothing appears. Cummings shovels his feet nervously as he looks sideways at Patti. Hmm, damn, she's looking better then Fey every day. Wonder if that's the reason Fey hates her. After a few seconds, the monitors still won't work. Patti begins to look bored or frustrated. Kinda hard to tell with the body language she is showing. Patti: Bloody Hell!! I'll just tell you all what I think. Shouldn't take to long. These good fans have waited long enough. The crowd erupts. Patti: Let's start with......Pajaro! Pajaro, you think your machismo, your latin heat, is enough to tempt me away from McClancy? You are sadly mistaken, my friend. How are you expected to concentrate on any match when I am on your mind constantly? One cannot wrestle when someone is on there mind. The focus is gone and we all know it does not work that way,. Pajaro. You aren't even in the same league as McClancy. How about our wonder adefli malakas? Yes, You Summers. I do NOT use Eliza for my own personal gain. Eliza came to me for help, she wanted advice on how to gain your attentions and affections. All I have done, is given her ideas on what to do, as any female would to another. How can I be the one behind your downfall? How can I, a mere female, be the champion? It's simple Summers, the desire, the heart, and the want to be the champion got me here. I haven't done anything that you yourself wouldn't do if you were in my place. Oh, and Summers, it's not all that bad dating someone in the business. You have someone to talk to, to bounce ideas off of, someone to just out and out talk to. Maybe that's the reason you are so bitter, Summers. You're just lonely. Maybe you should take an offer up on hooking up with Eliza. Outside of that ditzy appearance, she really is a sweet girl. Lobo believes he can take this belt from me? Why is it that what you all believe that? Sure you can try. You can try like everyone else to go after the title. I don't duck challenges. I wasn't trained that way. Now why would you think that holding this title is a nightmare for me? I hold this title with honor. I battled hard for it. I gave my blood, my sweat, and my tears for it. I didn't even think about the name of the belt. It came into my sights, so I took it. I do have to ask you one question though, Lobo? Where did you get the brillance to announce to the whole bloody world that your manager and soon to be wife is pregnant? Where is the brains in that? Don't you realize you just made it simple, very simple in fact, for any enemies to hurt her. That my friend was not a smart move. You just showed a weakness. Your mind won't be on me, your mind will be constantly on Angel, and that small amount of worry can grow. I would just hate to see anything happen to her. Angel, my female friend. You think you are going to attack me during the match should I try to seduce your man? I'm sure Lobo wouldn't appreciate you costing him the match for your outside interference. Don't forget that outside the ring is McClancy, he'll watch my back. As he has done in the past, and he will continue to do as my career goes on. Just be very careful Angel. Costing the challenger a match over something as silly as jealousy isn't a smart move. How's that for taking care of all who want me? I know I left a couple of people off, and I did that for a reason. Fey is my boss, and since what involves her and me is a family matter, I will keep it private. As for this Annie person? Sean and I will deal with that when the time comes. BSG? Probably just an accidental appearance in the locker room. So nothing really bad, there. Patti moved the microphone away from her mouth, taking in the crowd. She watched the thousands of fans on hand going crazy. She stared out at them for a while. Taking it all in. She would have never dreamed that she would be standing here, wearing championship gold, being loved by one of the best in the business. Cummings uses this moment of silence to take control of the scene. Cummings: Thanks Patti!! Doesn't she look great? Now that I've had the chance to look her over, I would have to agree with Pajaro from his last interview. I now KNOW I would love, how do you say it, TAP THAT ASS?!?! The crowd roars to life. Sean glares at Cummings. The desire to beat the living hell outta him comes to life in him. Patti, quickly turns to Sean. She notices the look on his face. She steps away from the table, and goes over to whisper in his ear. A smile breaks out on Sean's face. Somehow he knew Patti would have a plan in mind. Patti: Hey Cummings. How would you like to see that ass up close? Patti hands her belt off to one of the security guards standing close by. After making sure the belt is safe, Patti prepares to give Cummings, and the crowd a show. The crowd goes wild again. Cummings breaks out in a huge grin. Patti turns around and bends over and wiggles her ass at Cummings. McClancy comes over and gives Patti a light spanking. The crowd is loving every moment of it. Patti wiggles her ass a little more, tempting Cummings to come closer. Against better judgement, Cummings moves in close. Patti sees him out of the corner, waiting for him to bend down. Cummings, not wanting to miss a beat or this oppertunity, bends over. Patti spins around, and places Cummings into a headlock. Without a thought, Patti drives Cummings face first into the stage. The crowd lets loose with huge applauds, whistles, and screams. Patti steps back and grabs the microphone. Patti: Sean, I thought he learned his lesson from the first time he crossed my path back in Los Angeles almost 6 months ago. I drove him through a table for drooling over my breasts, now he has to drool over my ass. When will the man learn? The crowd shouts out NEVER!! Patti steps over the fallen body of Matt Cummings, making room for the emergeny medical technicians to check out the now unconscious Cummings. Cummings assistant, unsure of what to do, grabs the spare microphone. Assistant: And on that note, the box office is open. Patti, Sean, if you wouldn't mind taking your seats behind the table that has been set up for you. We'll get this event rolling. FADE OUT
