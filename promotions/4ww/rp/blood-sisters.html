Blood Sisters... Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #929 Date: 9/6/04 Location: Tokyo, Japan TWO WEEKS AGO: [The Divas Unleashed logo flashes across the screen followed by the 4WW logo blowing across the screen revealing the city of Tokyo Japan. As the scene dives into the streets of the extremely busy city, it comes up to Tipness Fitness Club, one of the biggest chains of gym within Tokyo located at 16-25 Nampeidaicho. Inside the gym on this typical day very few people are working out, or concentrating on they work out as their attention is draw to the site of a 7 foot red head by the weight lifting section executing squats, while her purple haired companion decked out in a light blue Nike sports bra, and tight blue matching spandex shorts counts.] Free Fall’s voice – 6…7…8…come on you can do it…push. [Cameras show Caitlin in a black Nike Sports bra, which skimpy red female biker shorts on. Her knees are wrapped as she stands barefooted going down in a squat position with a bar full or weights across her shoulders, the total weight is 400 lbs even, and this is her third set. Free Fall can be seen standing off to the side counting as Caitlin going down tries to hold her form while pushing the inhuman amount of weight up with all her strength. People all around some of them bodybuilders stand there watching and whispering amongst themselves.] Free Fall – 9…one more…one more…go strong! [Caitlin breathes hard taking in breath as she drops down for the 10th time, definition in her legs can be see popping from her calves, and thighs as they tremble to support the tremendous weight, while sweat drips all over Fairchild’s olive skin body causing her to glisten and drenching her gear; her long red hair frizzed and soaked from the massive amount of water it has accumulated. Caitlin screams as she pushes with every ounce of her strength to elevate the bar of weight. They clang as they go higher and higher until she’s finally back up to her vertical based. Caitlin wearily backs up placing the massive amount of weights on the rack behind her. As she gets out from underneath them to rest, she stretches her defined long legs getting the blood to circulate freely again.] Caitlin Fairchild – How much was that? Free Fall – Four sets. 40 reps. Not bad at all. Caitlin Fairchild – Could be better…next time I’ll see if I can hit 420. Free Fall – You’re gonna be a butch if you keep it up. Caitlin Fairchild – Hey…I don’t do bench presses…so lay off. Free Fall – Good…nothing worst than a big girl with rock hard titties… Caitlin Fairchild – For once…I agree with you… Free Fall – Come on…time to do some Sevens. [Caitlin nods as she follows Free Fall the a bar bell racked with two huge weights totaling a 110lbs. Caitlin runs her hand through her wet hair slicking it back, then stoops down grabbing weights readying for a curling position, she then raises to an upright position lifting the bar to her pelvis area. Fairchild raises the bar to her chest, and then slowly lowers the bar till her arm is into an “L” shaped position; Caitlin reps out seven straight reps, then reverse it where she lowers the bar to her pelvis position, and pulls it up into an “L” position, she reps out seven of these as well. Fairchild then executes seven slow full curls, which reveal the cuts of her slim shredded forearms, biceps, and traps.] Free Fall – Big night this Exile…four. Caitlin Fairchild – Yeah (grunts.) this will decided whether I get into that Unleashed title match at Massacre of the Orient. I have to get in there…one…(grunts.) because of the Divas Unleashed title…and two…(grunts.) Lilith will be there. I HAVE to be in that match…and I plan on tearing through Mya and Razor to do it. Free Fall – So what’s the plan…one more… [Caitlin grits her teeth as she elevates the weight one more time to her chest forcing her arms to failure. Upon completing her final rep she lowers the bar slowly back to her pelvis, and then kneels down slightly dropping it to the mat. Caitlin steps back wiping her forehead of sweat, and stands there breathing hard with her hands at her hips before looking at her manager and friend.] Caitlin Fairchild – Take Mya out of commission, and probably get the pin on Razor…I know they’re BOTH going to be coming for me, mainly because I have the size and strength advantage, if I were them that’s what I’d be doing…So that means the minute I step into that ring I have to take them down fast and hard…no mistakes can be made at Exile…none. Free Fall – Leg Press time. [Caitlin walks over to where she places her slip on Nike workout shoes, and slips them on before heading to the leg press. On the machine on each side are 6 sets of 45 pound weights totaling 540 lbs. Caitlin begins to grab more 45 pound weights off the rack and add them on as the crowd begins to form around the leg press, the whispering intensifies for when Caitlin is done she’s racked a full set of twenty-two 45 pound weights on EACH side of the leg press machine…total weight is 990 lbs. Caitlin then rewraps her knee caps tightly with knee support wraps, and takes a seat on the leg press positioning her legs. She takes a deep breath before pushing the unbelievable amount of weight up, and releasing the locking mechanism. Fairchild lowers the weight to her chest, and then braces herself as she pushes the weight back up remembering to keep her legs slightly bent. Free Fall watches keeping count as her wrestler reps out 4 times; each time her legs trembling under the pressure but holding firm.] Free Fall – Come on…that’s five…we’re going to eight…six…COME ON Caitlin, this is nothing for you! [Caitlin screams as she holds the side of her legs for support as she elevated the near thousand pounds of weight up again for the seventh time. Caitlin brings the weight down to her chest, and slowly pushes it upwards as her legs begin to shake violently as she pushed it up for the 8th and final time.] Free Fall – Come on Caitlin…one more! ONE MORE!! [Caitlin lets out a final scream pushing all the air out of her chest as she finally gets the weight up into the stratosphere. She pulls of the locking mechanism, and lowers the weight until it’s secure, then drops her long legs down out of exhaustion. Natives to the country clap and cheer impressed with the performance of the show of strength and power Caitlin as portrayed. Caitlin gives a faint wave and smile as the crowd disperses talking amongst themselves while Free Fall stands there smiling with pride.] Free Fall – I guess you’re their red head version of Godzilla. Caitlin Fairchild – (Pants.) Not…funny… [Fairchild slowly pulls herself up to her feet, and stretches to knock out the soreness.] Caitlin Fairchild – What’s next? Free Fall – Military presses for your back, then we hit the arms again, with some seated curls, followed by a 10 mile run around the track. Caitlin Fairchild – Works for me. [Before Caitlin and Free Fall can walk off a Japanese man with circle rimmed glasses wearing what appears to be a mailman type of outfit walks up to them with a mailbag over his shoulder, and a letter in his hand.] Free Fall – Hold up son…no autographs till AFTER my girl finishes her work out. Mail Man – Excuse please…I deliver letter for…Ms. Caitlin…Fairchil… Caitlin Fairchild – Caitlin Fairchild? That’s me… [The mailman bows before handing her a big brown envelope and walking away. They watch as he leaves before examining it.] Caitlin Fairchild – A letter…who would send me a letter? Free Fall – Who the hell would know that you were here? Caitlin Fairchild – There’s no return address… it’s post marked Phoenix Arizona. Free Fall – Who the hell do you know in Arizona? Caitlin Fairchild – Probably a fan… Free Fall – That knew you were here? Caitlin Fairchild – I don’t know Free Fall, I’m just guessing… Free Fall – Well OPEN it! Caitlin Fairchild – All right! All right! [Caitlin rips the top off the envelope, and reaches in to pull out a picture. Her eyes brows knit with bewilderment along with Free Fall as they look upon a promotional picture of a young woman with long dark copper red hair, hazel eyes, with a golden glow to her skin, she’s dressed in a black two piece cat suit style outfit, and has a gold and silver championship belt over she right shoulder which reads “ North Wind Champion” that she holds proudly. Underneath it says “4 Winds Wrestling: Patti Anderson.”] Caitlin Fairchild – Pattie…Anderson… Free Fall – You know her? Caitlin Fairchild – Personally no…but I’ve seen her name in wrestling magazines… She’s the first woman to hold The South Wind and North Wind titles in that promotion. Free Fall – What the hell would someone send you a promotion picture of her? Caitlin Fairchild – How should I know… Free Fall – What else is in the envelope? Caitlin Fairchild – Just this… [Caitlin turns the picture over…her eyes widen as she covers her mouth in shock. Free Fall gulps hard as she too ready what’s on the back of the promotional picture.] Caitlin Fairchild – She’s my…my… [The 4WW logo blows across the screen followed by Divas Unleashed logo flashing across it following to reveal what’s on the back of the picture in big bold black marker letters. “THIS IS YOUR SISTER.” Free Fall tries to hold her Amazon friend up as it appears as if she’s about to collapse from the shock as the scene fades to black.] TWO DAYS AGO: [The 4WW logo blows across the screen as an Amazon with slicked back red hair, sporting a skimpy ripped white wifebeater cut low to the cleave, and tied low at the bottom to create a make shift bikini top showing off her eight pack abs, with a pink push-up bra underneath, and tight blue jeans which hang low showing her matching G-string; tan Timberland boots turn her 7 foot height to that of 7’4” as she strolls out of the Rainbow Hall through the parking lot looking for her rental car. Caitlin Fairchild came to the Storm Front show of 4WW to see the sister she never knew she had in action, in the end she ended up being apart of the action, as she couldn’t stomach her sister being molested anymore. So she did what she did best, put people through tables, the unlucky person being Luscious Lady Luck…obviously she wasn’t lucky that night. As Caitlin searched for her Suzuki Jeep rental, her mind wandered to her first missing her custom red and black Humvee 2, these damn little Japanese cars were like a sardine can to her, and there were a couple of times she had to pull up the convertible roof. Her second thought which was more important was that two weeks ago, when she thought she was adopted and technically alone in this world she found out in the middle of a Japanese gym she had a biological sister…after two weeks of trying to process it all, and figuring out her next move, she just happened to run into her sister at the Divas Unleashed PPV Massacre of the Orient, apparently 4WW was doing it’s own tour of Japan, and Patti was tipped off about her having a sister in Divas Unleashed, only she didn’t know who so she came searching, which lead into the most awkward reunion ever. All of this was the explanation as to why Caitlin hadn’t left Japan yet, for twenty years of her life she really figured other than a father that she knew was alive, but didn’t want her, she was the only one in this world. Now that wasn’t true, she figured she could spend an extra week or two to try and get to know, and build a relationship with her long lost older sister. It seemed like it was a good idea…except…she wasn’t alone. Sitting on top of the hood of the Suzuki Jeep rental in a shoulder less white gypsy shirt, with a tight blue jeans tennis skirt, and purple Timberland boots, was her very irritated purple and lavender haired best friend and manager Roxanne Spaulding preferably known as Free Fall.] Free Fall – That was a stupid thing to do. Caitlin Fairchild – Excuse me? Free Fall – Don’t think I wasn’t watching the portable television…what do you think you were trying to prove putting the carpet muncher through that announcer’s table? Caitlin Fairchild – That you don’t mess with my sister. Free Fall – Caitlin…don’t you think you’re taking this sister thing a LITTLE bit too far? Caitlin Fairchild (Irritated.) – No I don’t…that’s my SISTER Roxy…MY sister, don’t you understand what this means for me? Can’t you be happy for me? [Free Fall gave Caitlin a concerned look, she was happy for her, and she was in a sense happy for her; apart of her couldn’t hide the fact that she was getting a little jealous, from the beginning when they first met it was just Caitlin and Free Fall, now with Caitlin’s sister in the mix she was afraid that would destroy the family they already built, not to mention something about Patti gave her a bad vibe…she just wasn’t sure what, but something to her stunk worst than a really bad cheesy yeast infection.] Free Fall – Look C…I’m happy for you…you know I am…I just think you should take things a little slow you know…you two just met like a week ago…you barely know anything about her… Caitlin Fairchild – Well, soon I’m going to have all the opportunity in the world to know everything about her. Free Fall – Sure…when we go back to the states… Caitlin Fairchild – Well… [Caitlin gave Free Fall the look as if she did something she KNEW she wasn’t going to approve off, which cause Roxy to jump off the top of the Jeep, and look her Amazon best friend in the face, with a semi-bewildered and irritated expression.] Free Fall – Caitlin…we’re going back to the United States TOMMORROW…right? Caitlin Fairchild – Not exactly… Free Fall – Caitlin Fairchild…what…did…you…do? Caitlin Fairchild – I…kind of signed a contract…I’m officially an employee of 4 Winds Wrestling…and I’m competing in the PPV coming up…Maelstrom V: Winds of War…we’re going back to the Tokyo Dome. [Free Fall stepped back in shock as if she was going to pass out, her heart beat began to quicken as she grabbed her chest, her face the turned an expression of pure rage.] Free Fall – Caitlin…what…the…FUCK! Caitlin Fairchild – Roxy! Free Fall – No fuck that! First of all you work of Value Sport Entertainment, you’re the FUCKING WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, not to mention you also work for Divas Unleashed! How could you make a decision like that without even SPEAKING to me your manager? Caitlin Fairchild – I haven’t wrestled for the VSE in months due to their shut down, we even talked about going to Underground Wrestling Network remember? What’s the difference in competing in a company where my sister works? Free Fall (Walks away shaking her head.) – This is just FUCKED UP all around yo…my word this is just fucked up. [Caitlin walks up behind her friend resting her on Roxy’s shoulder.] Free Fall (Shifting.) Don’t touch me yo… Caitlin Fairchild – Look…I’m sorry for not telling you…that was wrong of me…but I needed to do this…everything is going to workout…you’ll see. One more week, we do the Pay-Per-View, and we can go home…. not to mention this match is a big opportunity for me. Free Fall – How’s that? Caitlin Fairchild – Winner gets a shot at any title in the 4WW…ANY title. Free Fall (scoffs.) – Well you better win…because I’m tired of bein in a country will HALF the dudes are my height or shorter… [Caitlin places her arms around her best friend giving her a sisterly hug.] Caitlin Fairchild – Everything is going to work out…you’ll see. Free Fall – I thought I told you not to touch me…I don’t swing that way… [Caitlin smirks before kissing Free Fall on the cheek, and ruffling her hair.] Free Fall – HEY! Dammit heffa you know how long it takes me to get my hair dis good? [Fairchild come on, we got to go and get ready; we’re going out to dinner tonight.] Free Fall (Knits her eyebrows.) – Since when we got to get dressed for dinner? Caitlin Fairchild – Since we’re going to have dinner with my sister tonight. Free Fall (Stops for a minute.) – Thanks for not telling me. Caitlin Fairchild – Roxy come on… [Reluctantly Free Fall hopped into the jeep with her best friend, but she wasn’t happy one bit. They’re relationship was changing, she could feel it; whether it was for the best or worst she wasn’t sure yet, but it was changing, and she didn’t like it at all. The 4WW logo blows by as Let’s Get it Started by Black Eyed Peas pumps from the system as they drive out of the arena heading to their hotel.]
