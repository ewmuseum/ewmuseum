Being signed. Syxx Figures 4WW Exposures #34 Date: 7/11/03 Location: Home Why do human beings behave the way they do? Throughout human history, people have tried to answer that question. Those answers were the subject matter of literary works, philosophic debates, and medical prognoses. But one hundred and fifty years ago, the answers grew more precise when psychology, the scientific study of behavior, was born. Today there is a great diversity of interests within the field of psychology, placing psychologists in clinical, laboratory, educational, and business settings. The field continues to evolve because new information is always generating new questions about human and animal behavior. Though the activities and interests are varied, all subfields of psychology are concerned with the prediction and control of behavior. Businesses come in countless varieties. They range from a single person working out of the home a few hours a week to a multinational firm with hundreds of thousands of employees based in dozens of countries. Despite differences in size and scope, all businesses share common challenges. Each must decide what form to take and how to raise funds. The firm must have goals, and plans to achieve those goals. Workers need to be hired and compensated, even in the case of a one-person operation. The product bust me produced and brought to the market. An account of the firm’s transactions must be maintained. The business operates in an environment governed by laws and regulations. The science of psychology has grown out of many different disciplines. two of the driving forces in the early development of psychology were philosophy, study of wisdom, and anatomy/physiology, study of the structure and functions of the human body. Wilhelm Wundt, Sigmund Freud, Syxx Figures. The three have something in common. Knowledge? Power? Facial hair? Nope. What is it, you ask? The ability to break a human being down, make them cry. Find out the things that make them cringe. Their weakness’, and their strengths. What gives Syxx the ability to do so? Money? Strength? Good looks? Huh-uh. It was the eight plus years of college he went through. As a kid, he wanted to be a wrestler, but was too puny to do so, so he started working out, at the age of 9. Only standing a mere 3 foot 11 inches, Syxx began pumping weights in hopes to be a professional wrestler. At 13 he stood just over six foot, he towered over kids in his grade, after being told by a teacher, that no matter how big he is, he will never be successful in anything he does. Now 17, Syxx has graduated from High School and 6 foot 3 inches, and is working small independent shows. After the independent shows ran dry, and he ran his name into the ground in his area, so he went off to college. University Of Arkansas, a great University it is. Syxx went to the University of Arkansas for a little over eight years, eight frickin years. Now, 26, Syxx has two PH D’s. One in Business Administration, and One in Psychology. After working in a hospital for 2 months on the dreaded ‘6th Floor’, or the ‘Psych Ward’, Syxx receives another Job Offer. Let me tell you how this happened. You see ... Nick Van Nakia looked up from the bench as he tightened his grip on the bar. “Ready?” Syxx Figures looked down and nodded. He held the bar in between Nick’s hands and helped him lift it out of the rack. Six large metal plates rested on either side of the bar, about five hundred forty pounds in total. Nick slowly lowered the bar to his chest and exhaled hard as he pushed it back up. Down, back up. Down, back up. At about the eighth repetition, Nick started to struggle to raise the bar. “Little help,” he grunted. Syxx stared at his longtime friend and wanted nothing more than to drop the bar across his throat. “Pussy,” Syxx smirked as he stuck two fingers under the bar and aided Nick with his lift. It hadn’t all been bad. Syxx’s schedule was packed with personal appearances, TV and radio spots, not to mention his training and wrestling matches. To be a success as World champion, you had to give your full devotion to it. You couldn’t share your time with family, friends, and relationships. That didn’t help the small piece of himself that felt dead and empty. But there were plenty of other things to fill the emptiness with – booze and broads were working so far. “Syxx.” Nick picked up Syxx’s gym bag. “Your bag’s ringing.” The big Canadian unzipped the side pocket on his bag and pulled out his cellular phone. He flipped it open and held it to his ear. Normally he wouldn’t take calls during his workout, but he was waiting to hear about his new line of sporting wear. “Speak.” “Syxx? Hey, it’s Geoffrey, you know. Childress.” “Oh, well look who it is. What can I do for you, kid?” “I would like to you be here tomorrow morning, bright and early 7am. Your plane leaves in 10 minutes. They’ll know what to do, flight 783 .. coach.” “Coach? Shit man, come on. And 10 minutes? Me and Nick are at the gym still.” “Well they’re not going to wait, so go.” With that. Syxx hears a click. ***** 6:30 am, Phoenix, Arizona. A little 83, red Toyota Corolla screams into view as Syxx is apparently holding a map, driving, eating, and fixing his hair. The little hatchback flies into a parking lot, wasting no time, Syxx and Nick step out of the little car and stretch. “Childress has it out for us, I swear.” Syxx says stuffing the directions and map into the car. “No joke!” Nick cracks his neck and knuckles with a sigh of relief that follows. The two walk into the building, searching for the office labeled “G. Ian Childress.” After the room was spotted, Syxx gives a knock. A confirming “Enter” is heard. Syxx and Nick walk in seeing Fey McClancy-Childress sitting in a chair next to G. Ian Childress. Ian looks at Syxx, nods, and slides an envelope towards him. Fey looks at Nick, nods, and slides an envelope towards him. “Before you open that, Syxx. Glad to see you came, sorry about the Coach Class flight, the 10 minute warning, and the Toyota Corolla, but I figured if you would go through all this, then you would definitely be a man to sign.” Ian motions a ’go ahead’ towards Syxx with his hands. “A man to sign? So are you saying .. ?” Ian nods a confirming nod towards Syxx. Nick looks at the envelope. “Oh sir, a wrestling contract?” Ian nods again. “No sir, sorry. Don’t think I’m ready for it.” Fey stands up and walks around Nick, hand on his shoulder, circling him. “How about .. a managerial position among Four Winds Wrestling?” Nick looks at Syxx, who has already signed the contract, shook hands with Ian, and has been talking for a while. “Sure.” Nick pulls out a pen from his pocket and signs the document encased in the letter. “Glad to see you two with us, first show, Sunday. You’re in a gauntlet Syxx. Good luck.” Ian shakes Syxx’s hand. “Thanks, can’t wait to work with the likes of “The Fallen One” again.” Syxx gives a chuckles and motions a ‘Tip-Of-The-Hat’ towards Fey, who winks. “Nick, I’ll see you around.” Ian then shakes the hand of the obviously shaken up Nick Van Nakia. “Th-Th-Th-Th-Thanks sir.” Nick said nervously, and he motions as well a ‘Tip-Of-The-Hat’ towards Fey, who gives a giggle. “We’ll see you around.” Syxx says as he grabs the nervous Nick Van Nakia, who is in shock and awe at being offered his first Professional Wrestling contract. ***** Every human being is unique, having been created and molded through a combination of genetics and life experiences. But as different as people are from each other, everyone passes the same developmental mile-stones. Human Development begins at the moment of conception and progresses rapidly during the nine months of gestation. But it ends with one thing, a “Brute Force.” My job is to win, I do my job well, and ... I LOVE MY JOB!
