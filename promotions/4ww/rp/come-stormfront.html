Come Stormfront... Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #996 Date: Oct 9, 04 Location: A dark room [Fade in] [Lee is in a dark room, we can see that the walls are old and grungy red brick and the floor is dirty, he is sitting in a steal blue chair. He is wearing a black ‘Welcome to Hell’ Shirt and black jeans. He has a cigarette in his mouth. He takes a puff of his cigarette before he begins.] Lee: People… So many people… So many types of people… We all live in this world… Yet… We still discriminate against each other… We point out their differences… Rather than celebrating our differences… Oh how we’re different from our ancestors… That was sarcasm… [Lee laughs rather loudly.] Lee: We’re still primitive… We haven’t changed in 200 years… We haven’t changed in 2000 years… They… You… You booed Christ… And now… And now… They… You… Boo me… But I have something that Christ never had… Power Sure he has it now… But he’s gone now… So the power… It’s mine… What I do with it is my choice… And I chose to wrestle… To beat the shit out of people… Legally… And Stormfront will no different… [Lee takes another puff and drops the cigarette on the ground.] Lee: Caitlin Fairchild… Patti’s Anderson’s sister… I don’t know where to start… 7 feet… 245 Pounds of cold hard bitch… ‘The Perfect Woman’… So you call yourself… Who the fuck would want to be with you… You out weigh half the roster… And believe me… That’s NOT a turn-on… You think you can beat me… Hell beat me and Chris West… At our game? I pinned you… I pinned you 1, 2, 3… In fact I beat both of you… In one night… [A smile grows across his face as he stands up and begins to pace.] Lee: Which brings me to Chris West… I don’t think there’s much I can say about you that hasn’t been said… Except… Except… Except, I can’t believe you think you can actually go sober… I give it two weeks… Before you are once again nothing more than a drunken bitch… [Lee stops and looks into the camera.] Lee: Come Stormfront… I will beat both of you till you beg… Beg that I stop… Beg for your lives… [Lee grabs the camera, and picks it up, holding it up to his face.] Lee: This is my time and no one… NO ONE will stop me! [Fade out]
