Self Doubt? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #226 Date: 10/18/03 Location: Phoenix Hotel Doubt - verb - to lack confidence in: DISTRUST Doubt, a word that is the downfall of many. It has caused winners to question themselves. Losers to feel the shame for believing it could happen. It has been the token battle cry for everyone. Past, present, and the future will always be plagued by doubt, especially self doubt. Doubt is working its magic on someone. Destiny sees it. Her charge, Patti Anderson, the Darling of the 4WW is starting to doubt herself. Her fear of meeting her match in the ring has finally come forward. The person in question? Todd Dream. The reigning Four Winds North Wind Champion. A man whom Destiny had hoped Patti wouldn’t have to face this early in her career, but it looks as if the gods had other plans. The Four Winds North Wind Championship. The one belt, Patti had her eyes set on, had trained extra hard for, didnt quite happen that way. A loss to Jay Phoenix caused the first stages of doubt within her, but she had managed to bounce back. The help of Sean McClancy got her to refocus. She fought the doubt and proved to not only to herself, but to the doubters and critics that she could do it, she set her sights on the South Wind Championship. A step below the North Wind Championship, but it was a belt. She won it, and proved that doubt had no business in her life. Now the doubt was back. This time whispering like the harpies of old, trying to convince Patti that she doesn’t have what it takes to defeat the champion. A doubt that Patti is starting to feel. She needs to defeat Todd Dream to prove to herself that she can do it. Lets take a moment to look at Todd Dream. . He’s a bleach blonde wanna-be from California. The very man that makes Eliza Murphy look intelligent. A man who wouldn’t understand a blonde joke if it was made into a full length movie. A surfer dude who must have had the surf board hit him in the head one to many times. Don’t get Destiny wrong. He and the other members of Featured Attraction are good-looking people (Summers is debatable, but you’d have to check with Eliza for confirmation), with the density and intelligence of a brick. With Todd Dream as the leader, you know the intelleigence factor left him a long time ago. Destiny chuckled at her own sense of humor. The surfer dude of the Four Winds, the bleach blonde himbo. The man who thinks he has a chance to get an easy pin over the ‘Darling of the Four Winds.’ It almost sounds like someone has been listening to the bald headed cue ball to much. Summers must be filling his head with all sorts of bull on that one. Destiny’s bell like laughter can be heard echoing through out the world she lives in.. But then the sobriety of the moment stops her. Does he have a chance to beat her? Maybe. Patti has proven to be a strong competitor in the past. Taking on all who have come her way, but now the doubts are setting in. She isn’t sure of the path that lays before her. Todd is a seasoned veteran that knows his way around the ring, and won’t be afraid to capitalize on a simple mistake made by Patti. And with the other members of Featured Attraction being around, Patti would be in serious trouble. Marvelous really has nothing to deal with Patti. He’s left her to her own devices. Summers has made it personal by first attacking Patti whenever the opportunity presented it self. His attacks on Patti alone, became a personal war for Patti’s own boyfriend. McClancy took him to school for that. He then sunk lower then ever by attacking his own girlfriend, Eliza Murphy. He attacked her when he thought she was seeing someone else behind his back. The boy just has a problem that will be taken care of my McClancy later on down the road. Todd Dream has a history of beating women. He was involved in the violent attack on Fey McClancy in the old federation. His attack earned him a nice little restraining order to keep him away from her, and a severe beating from one Ian Childress too. Could the same thing happen to Patti? Could Todd and his cronies be capable of another attack on the ‘Darling of the 4WW’? Would he be willing to risk the violence of Sean McClancy if harm comes to Patti? And what does the psycho cue ball have planned to do to the ‘Irish Bastard’? Even more so what does the woman who thinks McClancy still belongs to her have something to say about it. Only these questions will be answered this wee on StormFront we shall just have to see what the results will be. ______________________________________________________________________________ Place: Hotel In Phoenix Time: Sometime During The Day ______________________________________________________________________________ Patti Anderson sat quietly in the hotel room. Sean had left earlier in the day, something about doing some shopping for a special night out and he wanted to get her a gift for the upcoming Sweetest Day. He thought it would be nice to get her something special. He told her to stay put, and to be ready to leave when he came back. She didn’t argue. Sean McClancy didn’t do surprises, but when he did they were sweet. So in the time he was gone, she washed her hair. The length always made it hard for the hair dryer to get it dried the way she wanted it. So most of the time she just let it air dry. She couldn’t wait to see what he was planning. She crawled out of the chair she was sitting and walked over to the window. This week, they had opted to stay in a hotel in downtown Phoenix. A little closer to the arena, but still far enough away that the fans wouldn’t bother them. Lately, she valued her and Sean’s privacy. The press had been hounding them for a while. The little announcement at Maelstrom had the wrestling world going crazy. Patti didn’t mind though, kinda shocked the hell out of everyone, but the buy rates for the encore presentations were causing a lot of revenue for the Childress. Patti walked back away from the window. She stared at the hotel room. She noted nothing had really changed from the last one they were in. She was beginning to notice that all the hotel rooms looked the same, just items in different places. Patti looked around for the usual stuff the hotel supplies for them. She just wasn’t in the mood for the normal magazine reading about her being one of the hottest females in the wrestling, or that the wrestling world was thrilled with the return of Sean McClancy. She wanted something different. She wanted stuff from home. The news and the gossip from there always cheered her up. Patti heard a soft knock at door. She almost bounced to the door. Sean must have forgotten the key to get in, or his hands were full. She opened the door and was greeted by a smiling and familiar face. Her best friend in the fed stared back at her. Eliza Murphy stood before her. Her shoulder still in a sling. She was dressed for comfort instead of style this time. Her blonde hair was hanging lose around her shoulders, wearing just a blue overhauls and a white t-shirt. She winced a little when her arm hit the door jamb my accident. Patti: Eliza!! I wondered when I’d be seeing you. Since you’ve been with Fate, I hardly see you. Eliza: Well, you know how it is. He treats me a lot better then Sean ever did. Patti nods in agreement, and from the way Eliza is acting; she came here with more then just a friendly visit on her mind. Patti: So what do I owe this visit to this time. I know it has nothing to do with Todd Dream this week, the man hasn’t sent out his verbal apoklada already? Eliza laughed as she sat down. Eliza: Not to my knowledge anything has come in from him, but you know, if something does, I’ll bring it right over to you. The new intern has been foaming at the mouth to see what all the fuss is about over you though. Patti: Why is it you bloody get stuck with the stargazers? That seems to be all that my future sister-in-law sticks you with? Eliza giggled. Eliza: I just do not know. An uneasiness settled into the room. The chit chat had stopped. The tension filling up the room was just too much for both to handle. Patti: So what’s up that you came to see me? Eliza stared at Patti for a second not sure how to breach the subject. She had been blissfully happy in the weeks she’d been with Fate, and she was falling in love with him. But she still longed for the one the one that didnt want her. Eliza sighed again as she tried to figure out how to deal with it. Eliza: I’m still dealing with missing my Sean. Its not getting any easier. But I came over here to spend time with my friend, and not discuss my problems. Patti walked over and picked up the phone, placed an order with room services. She curled back up on the bed and prepared for a little girl talk time. Fade Out
