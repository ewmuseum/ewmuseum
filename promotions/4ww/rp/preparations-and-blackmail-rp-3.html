preparations and blackmail? (RP #3) Future Shock 4WW Exposures #965 Date: 8/12/04 Location: Tokyo, Japan Is this all they got? Is this all the fight that the many opponents of Future Shock can put out? Is this it? Well it’s not really about them now is it? Are they the hottest thing to hit the 4WW since Jay Phoenix? Well Future Shock doen’st flame as much as he does, but still… there has been nothing hotter. Not Todd Dream… Not Ritoru Joufu… Not Psymon… Not Jerms… No, no one else… And DAMN SURE not KRIS KANTON! It doesn’t matter what anyone else says. People are afraid of FutureShock. People don’t want to face them. They intimidate their opponents. But how do they intimidate them? Their talent… Their skill… Their abilities… Their ring presence… The fact that they are the most solid team that the 4WW has ever seen. The Ozz brothrs don’t have it. They’re the typical sports entertainment tag team. Take one big hoss and the little guy, one good on the stick the other not. And then you put them in the ring and hope that the crowd cheers. But sometimes you have something, that special something that makes you a real team. Ukyo and Blaise are about 70 lbs and 1 inch apart, with Blaise being the larger of the two. But their styles compliment, not clash. They’re a true team. No, they’re not brothers or siblings. They are partners though. Some may speculate on how much in and out of the ring that they are. But that’s neither here nor there. There is one reason why Blaise would allow it to get out that he and Ukyo have taken their partnership to a new level. Because people like Zane Ozz would dwell on it. He’s contantly brought it up that they appear to have good chemistry in and OUT of the ring. Yes, Zane, that means that Blaise has managed to get closer to a woman then you’re big doofus ass has. Dropped out of the ninth grade, huh? Yeah, it shows. It shows all to well. And guess what… guess being dropped on your head on a regular basis is a good job for someone that couldn’t’ do it. You admit to not being able to succeed in something as simple as the United States Education System, not even managing to get through the 4 years of chasing girls and trying to figure out what the hell you’re going to do with your life, called High School? Was it too hard Zane? Did they pick on you? Did the women scorn you and call you mean names? Or did one just laugh at you when you dropped your pant the first time? Or did your wrestlin coach decide a little ‘one on one’ was good for him? No matter the reason, you want the fans to think that you have what it takes to be a champion? You want them to think you have the determination, guts, balls, know how, and general apptitude to get into the ring with a team such as Future Shock and take the East Wind Titles away from them? You couldn’t even finish High School, what the hell makes you think you’re going to finish this match? It’ll be much harder a learning experience then reading, writing and arithmetic. This week’s lesson for you, school boy, is going to be humility. If I may coin a phrase off of my cousin, the now retired “Irish Bastard” Sean McClancy… school is in session, and it’s School of Hard Knocks 101 for you. No, I can’t say it like he can, but the effect will be the same. First someone will go through a table, and the titles will leave where they are at now. And then Blaise will come back out to the ring and face off against a man who is on his third take on the South Wind Title. For Kris Kanton it’s make or break time. It’s final exams. And if he fails it this time, he’ll be repeating a grade…. Or he might have to take the Zane Ozz path of the short cut… drop out. Give up… Toss in the towell… Oh, wait, that brings up a point there… That’s how he’s going to loose, now isn’t it? Bring your pad and paper, we’ll find you a desk. It’s flashback time for Zane, Landen and Kris. They’re little clique in school is about to go to the principal’s office and get their punishment for not paying attention.
