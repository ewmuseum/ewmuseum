TJ talk numba 3 TJ Anderson 4WW Exposures #1226 Date: April 3rd Location: Studio Blog entry number 3 Just got back from Ireland, it’s some ungodly hour in the morning but I’m not tired enough to go to sleep. I defended my title, successfully I might add, we were in Sean (and Fey’s) hometown of Dublin, it was cool, we had a nice dinner afterwards, it’s cool, I’ve never traveled this much in this little time, even though we don’t spend as much time in each place as I’d like to, it’s still awesome! Yeah so Kris Kanton decided to be a bastard and attack me, and since I have to defend my title I don’t get to wrestle him… but Patti promised he would get his, and I believe her, Blaise and Ukyo will kick his ass. I got the “Phenomenal” Steve Owens to deal with… Don’t know much about him other than the only time I was in the ring with him, I won. Anyways, that’s it for now… Don’t forget to watch TJ Talk… [TJ walks into the studio, he is wearing a leather jacket with a gray Linkin Park shirt underneath and some baggy washed jeans, he is holding the Tempest title over his right sholder. He walks over to the single stool set up in front of the camera, there is a blue backdrop behind him. He looks towards the director who is situated behind the camera.] TJ: Ready? Director: One Second… [TJ sits down.] Director: Ready… [Fade in] [TJ smiles and takes off his sun glasses. He begins talking in a long deep voice.] TJ: Hello and welcome to another addition, the second in fact, of TJ talk, I’m your host the wonderfully charming TJ Anderson and boy do I have a show in store for you. Today our guests are ME, ME, and oh yes ME, the Tempest champion. [He pats the belt with his left hand. He begins speaking normally.] TJ: No I’m gonna jump into it, not dilly dattle with all the bullshit… Steve Owens, I don’t know you, we’ve wrestled in the same ring once, my debut… I won, that’s the bottom line, you got eliminated and your truly didn’t, in fact that’s why we’re having this match, cuz I have the title. Now I’m not saying your bad, I’m not saying you suck, but you’re not phenomenal… I’m phenomenal, I’m phenomenal, you’re just a big man… I can do things you wouldn’t dream of doing. The Show Stealing Spot, blam your out, the Ku Long Drop, you aint getting up for weeks, Ku Long Driver, better get a neckbrace. I can beat you so many ways… I was trained by the best, and now I am the best. This title proves it, I haven’t been beat, I haven’t been pinned and I haven’t submitted, I’m a fighting champion and all I can say is BRING IT ON! [TJ takes a deep breath.] TJ: Now you’re not much older than me… we’re the future, and you got potential, if you didn’t well then you wouldn’t be in 4WW. The difference is I got wrestling in my blood, my family was bread to wrestle; Patti Anderson, Caitlin Anderson, Syckle… the list goes on, I am destined for greatness, I am destined for power and glory, are you? Come Stormfront we’ll see, if you can beat me, I’ll gladly shake your hand but the fact of the matter is, if you hope to break your losing streak, expect to break your bones. Steve Owens… See you in the ring. [Fade out.]
