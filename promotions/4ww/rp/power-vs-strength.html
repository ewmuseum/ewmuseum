Power vs Strength

10 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Power vs Strength Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #940 Date: 9/10/04 Location: Tokyo, Japan [4WW logo blows across the screen to the Imperial Hotel Tokyo in Tokyo, Japan located 1-1, Uchisaiwai-cho 1-chome, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-8558 at 10 AM (Asia Pacific Time) in the Thursday morning, 4 days away from Maelstrom V: War of the Winds, on the 16th floor room 16E Caitlin Fairchild sits Indian style in the middle of the living room of her suite hotel dressed in just a white wife beater (one of the straps hanging down her shoulders), and a pair of Muy Thai red, green, gold, and blue boxer shorts munching on a blue bowl of frosted mini Wheats in front of the television studying. From 6AM Japan time Caitlin has been up in front of the television watching video after video of 4WW shows and matches. For 5 hours she’s sat through half a year of 4WW shows, and is on her third bowl of Wheats, which will be burnt off at about 12 noon when she hits the gym, and does ring work for about a good 8 to 9 hours straight, then she’ll get some sleep for 8 hours, and get up about the same time to watch the other 5 months coming up to the Anniversary which she will be apart of, where she plans to make a lasting mark, and impression left by the strongest tornado or hurricane…namely walking into the Gauntlet match, running through anyone that gets in her way, and winning it on her first debut. But to do that, she had to know her targets, study them like building structures, find the weakest point, and hit with all she had, and she had to do it quick and fast conserving her tank to go the limit. She had four days to do it, lucky for her she came out with a 4.0 and a knack for cramming. Caitlin put another spoonful of cereal in her mouth wiping the excess milk, which dripped down her chin with her forearm, as she continued to watch the match before her. Maelstrom II: Eruption Gauntlet Match: Winner goes to Main Event to battle for the North Wind Title Emerald Dragon vs. The Rebel vs. 2 Little vs. Aiden DeLaCroix vs. Se7en vs. Lee Satima vs. Scott Thomas vs. “The Legacy” Scott White vs. Billy Dork [Billy Dork hits the stage and bounces around a few times. He makes a gesture of a belt around his waist and then points at Thomas. He then sprints into the ring and attacks Thomas before the bell] Morales: And we are underway here at Maelstrom 2! These two are excellent high flyers, which should make this a great opening for this Gauntlet! Krug: You can tell both these guys want the North Wind Title! Not that they stand a chance against Dream though! [Billy Dork continues to pummel Thomas' head that lays flat on the ground. Billy Dork lifts him up and whips him off the ropes. He waits and then delivers a standing dropkick to the face. Thomas stumbles and falls to the outside. Billy Dork climbs the top turnbuckle and signals for a move] Morales: What's he gonna do? [He leaps off the turnbuckle and hits a flying spear which drives Thomas back onto the guardrail. Fans begin smacking the dazed Scott Thomas on the head as Billy Dork gets up slowly after the suicidal spear] Krug: It took the wind out of that Dork! [Dork lifts Thomas and rolls him back into the ring; he then rolls back in with him only to be met with a desperation punch to the midsection. Billy Dork seems to be agitated as he kicks the fallen Thomas square in the head. He lifts him up and begins to whip him into the rope when Thomas reverses it. Billy Dork bounces of the ropes and then charges hard clothes lining Thomas out again] Krug: Thomas Sucks! [Billy Dork grabs the ropes, leans back, and then springboards over to hit a flying hurricarana] Morales: That's it, he's dead! [He rolls him back in, lifts him to the feet, signals, and then hits the Flippity Fluck and goes for the pin] 1…. 2… 3!!!......... Morales: Thomas is eliminated. Good start for Billy Dork! Kerrie Douglas: Scott Thomas has been eliminated, the winner of this bout… Billy Dork! [Music hits as the fans boo] Morales: Uh OH! Krug: Here comes Bakers Boy! [The Rebel charges to the ring, before Rebel gets to the ring Billy Dork bounces off the ropes and nails him in the face with a baseball slide. Rebel gets up quickly and the two begin exchanging blows on the outside. The Rebel gets the upper hand and slams Billy Dork into the ring steps] Morales: Billy Dork is looking a bit tired; I don't think he'll last! [The Rebel rolls Billy Dork into the ring; he stomps on his head and lifts him up in position for a suplex. Billy Dork reverses and suplexes the Rebel to the mat. However, a tired Billy Dork is slow to get up leaving the fresh Rebel to capitalize. He quickly DDT's Dork to the mat, and then picks him up, sets him on his shoulder and then hits a running powerslam] Morales: The Rebel is dominating this match up! Krug: I wonder what Chris Baker thinks about this? [The Rebel begins to wear down Dork even more by applying a facelock. The fans begin to cheer for Billy Dork who slowly works his way to his feet and then elbows The Rebel in the gut, and quickly hits a swinging neckbreaker] Morales: A second wind for Dork!! [Dork heads to the top rope, waits for the Rebel to get up and then leaps for a hurricarana before being caught in the process and is nailed with a sit-down powerbomb from the Rebel] 1… 2… 3!!!... Morales: Billy Dork gave it a good effort! But The Rebel is just too much to handle after already being in a match! Kerrie Douglas: Billy Dork has been eliminated, the winner of this bout… THE REBEL!! Krug: Who's next? [On the entrance way appears 2 Little. No music. No pyros. Nothing. He cautiously looks around the arena and slowly strides towards the ring. He glances over towards the fans that are at ringside and then at the ring itself. Slowly he makes his way towards the steps and up into the ring. The Rebel tells 2 Little to bring it] Krug: What's up with 2 Little? Morales: This is 2 Little's debut; I was hoping it would have been a little bit flashier! [The Rebel charges and nails 2 Little with a hard clothesline. 2 Little's head bangs hard onto the mat. The Rebel has seemed to have snapped as he lifts 2 Little up and repeatedly bangs his head onto the turnbuckle. 2 Little elbows The Rebel in the face and tries to clothesline him down, but The Rebel doesn't move. Instead, the Rebel grabs 2 Little and rams his shoulder in the steel post] Morales: Ouch! 2 Little's in a world of hurt! [The Rebel lifts him up. And goes for a float over DDT, and connects with it but in the process 2 Little's feet nails the ref knocking him out] Krug: The ref is down! Look at the eyes of The Rebel! [Realizing the ref is down, The Rebels rolls under the ropes and grabs a table from underneath the ring, he sets it up on the outside and rolls back into the ring picking up an unconscious 2 Little! He sets him up on the top turnbuckle, lifts him up and delivers a jumping powerbomb onto the table on the outside] Morales: OH MY GOD! HE WAS JUST BROKEN IN HALF! [The Rebel rolls 2 little back into the ring and leans against the ropes for a bit for a breather! He then gets the ref up and cockily steps on 2 Little for the pin] 1… 2… 3!!!!... Krug: The Rebel is moving on, that's two matches he's won already! [Caitlin continues to watch as if she was in front of Saturday Morning cartoons, the bedroom door creaked open followed by an over exaggerated yawn. Free Fall stumbled out wearing a Spiderman 2 XL shirt with matching boxer shorts, and a black G-Unit bandanna covering her purple and lavender hair with furry green dinosaur that make roaring sounds when she walks. She let loose a belch which almost made Caitlin choke on her cereal, and began scratching her rear, while wiping the cold out of her eye as she stumbles into the living room trying to refocus her eyes into the morning sunlight. ] Free Fall (Mutters.) – Mornin… Caitlin Fairchild (With cereal in her mouth.) – Morphfin… Free Fall – Whatcha watchin… Caitlin Fairchild (With cereal in her mouth.) – Bfhtso 4WW Vidifios…. Free Fall – What? Caitlin Fairchild (With cereal in her mouth.) – Pfhtso 4WW Vidifios!!! Free Fall (Irritated.) – HEFFA DON’T TALK WITH YA MOUTH FULL!! Caitlin Fairchild – (Chews rest of cereal then speaks) I said…I was watching the Best of 4 Winds Wrestling. Free Fall – Know ya mama taught ya better than dat. [Caitlin rolls her eyes as she munches on her cereal while Free Fall goes into the kitchen, and pulls out some milk from the frig, and some Fruit Loops they smuggled over from the States, along with a huge yellow mixing bowl. Free Fall dumps HALF the box, with HALF the milk in the bowl, grabs a huge serving spoon, and carries the bowl with it contents to the tan sofa, plops down sitting Indian style with the bowl on her lap, and begins munching away as well.] Caitlin Fairchild – You know that’s going to make you fat. Free Fall – (Between bites.) Kiss my fat white ass when it does… [The two munch on as they continue to watch the action in the ring.] Free Fall – How long you’ve been watchin dis stuff? Caitlin Fairchild – From 6… Free Fall – Damn girl…don’t ya get tired of this…put on some Anime, or one of them Extreme competition type money shows. Caitlin Fairchild – I’m studying…. incase you didn’t realize I got one tough PPV match to prepare for. Free Fall – What’s so tough about it…it’s a Gauntlet match…you just go in, and bang em out one at a time. [Caitlin turns slightly looking at her in disbelief.] Caitlin Fairchild – You must think I’m Super Girl or something…you know how difficult this match is going to be, not to mention it’s been like months since I stepped into the ring with men. [It had been actually months since Caitlin stepped into the ring, with someone from the opposite sex. Her last match was defending her World Heavyweight title in other federation against the Unsanctioned World Heavyweight Champion known as Dante DeAngelis in a title defense. The Anti was upped when the CEO put BOTH belts on the line, with one person coming out the Undisputed World Champion…in one of the most grueling matches in her life…in the end Fairchild walked out with BOTH belts…the first ever female Undisputed World Champion, and at the age of 19, two months before her birthday the youngest her federation ever had. Due to political reasons the VSE would go on hiatus, Caitlin would continue to compete at Divas Unleashed, which is solely an all women federation. This would be 4 months, stepping back into a league mainly dominated by men again, even though she was still active, Caitlin wasn’t about to take it lightly, nor go unprepared.] Free Fall – So what’s the problem, it’s not like you haven’t been in the ring with guys before. Caitlin Fairchild – No, but considering that male athletes are more superior to female athletes, stepping into the ring with a man is not something you take lightly. [Free Fall chokes on her Fruit Loops upon hearing Caitlin’s comments. She pounds twice on her chest making sure her food goes down the right pipe, as she looks wide-eyed at her friend.] Free Fall – WHOA! I can’t believe that came out of your mouth. Caitlin Fairchild – What the truth? I was like that until I took the study in human mechanics when I double majored in Biology…funny…us as women pretend that we don’t have egos when we actually do…always trying to compete with the men trying to prove that we’re the superior species, when realistically if we examine the facts neither species is OVERALL better than the other…you take a seasoned 245 lb. Male boxer, and put him in the ring with a season 200 lb female boxer…9 times out of 10 unless she got a LOT of power behind her punch, and gets in a lucky shot, the male boxer is going to put her on her rear, not because she’s not as skilled as the male, but if you ACTUALLY look at the male body it’s basically BUILT to take a LOT of punishment as well as give a lot of punishment. Male muscles grow bigger and stronger than females, and they tend to heal quicker…not to mention the Testosterone mixed with Adrenaline running through their system is like a super-charged fuel, and finally they don’t go through the physical changes our bodies go through, periods…cramps…menopause…PMS…pregnancy reek havoc on a woman’s body…women’s bodies hate to say it were meant for two things…to be appealing to the male eye, and for child baring…while men were meant for the task of labor. Free Fall – HEFFA, what the HELL are you trying to DO? Set the woman’s movement BACK 2 million years? Caitlin Fairchild (Turns smirking.) – Actually the opposite…you see you’re missing my point…JUST because PHYSICALLY males are the superior species, doesn’t mean that MENTALLY they are the superior. Free Fall (Snorts.) – Everybody know dat. Caitlin Fairchild – The typical Alpha Male reacts to what he can see and touch, he’s cocky…aggressive, and throws caution to the wind. They tend to overlook the weaker gender of they own male species, and they especially overlook the female viewing us as something to wet their sexual appetite, now…a smart female armed with knowledge can use that to grab them by the short and hairy. Free Fall – (Knits her eyebrows.) Since when do YOU know about exploitation on THAT level Miss only been to FIRST base? Caitlin Fairchild – (Gives Free Fall and irritated look.) Ya think I wear that get up to the ring for COMFORT VALUE? Free Fall – (Smirks.) I thought it was because you were just a tease… Caitlin Fairchild – You wish…(although I do like how it fits snuggly on my shape.) It’s primarily an outfit with a FUNCTIONAL purpose. Free Fall – (Mutters.) Should be…the way you got your entire ass hangin out in the back. Caitlin Fairchild – (Looks at her in disbelief.) YOU’RE THE ONE THAT HAD THE OUTFIT MADE!! YOU’RE THE ONE WHO SAID IT LOOKED CUTE!! Free Fall – I say a LOT of shit that comes out of my mouth…don’t you know me by now? Caitlin Fairchild – (Gives her a sarcastic look.) Yeah…wonder why you don’t walk around with a diaper. Free Fall – (Pauses the smirks.) Oh you got jokes now…aight… Caitlin Fairchild – Anyway…back to the what we were talking about, the purpose of this is to study key opponents that will be in that match, take for instance… James Slade and Jaime are highflyer/luchadors…I have a feeling I’m a LOT stronger than them, so they’re going to chop me down with quick highflying maneuvers which means I’m going to have to just overpower them…lots of clotheslines, strikes, and quick hard counters to keep them off their vertical base, and because most Luchadores are known for slipping out of big power moves and countering I’m going to have to make sure I either lock onto them, or follow up with a faster counter of my own, more effectively strap on a submission maneuver. Then you have Luke Chadbourne, and all American type brawler…very clean cut type of wrestler…no big flashy moves, high impact and to the point…with him I’ll have to be more versatile…he as well as Eddie B. whose an all-rounder will be looking to muscle me…in situations like that I’ll have to meet them power for strength. Free Fall – (Knits her eyebrows.) What’s the difference? Caitlin Fairchild – There’s a HUGE difference…strength comes from your body…power is an EXTENSION of your body…anyone who hits the gym 24/7 can build strength…very few focus however on building their power. Lifting a man twice your bodyweight means your strong…hitting a man twice your bodyweight with a power clothesline that makes him STAY down tells the tale of how powerful you are. Free Fall – (Smirks and half bows.) All right there master Fairchild. Caitlin Fairchild – Stu Hart, God rest his soul once told me…wrestling is 95% mental and 5% physical…it starts when you make your entrance, to when you enter that ring, to when the bell rings…to when the ref hits that three count each move your opponent has to his arsenal has about 3 counters to it, and visa versa…the key is watching for them to make the mistake…know it…and be fast enough to capitalize off of it. Funny…wish I remembered that when I was in the FCW…I would have done a lot better. [Free Fall leaned back in the sofa smiling down at her Amazon friend, as her Fruit Loops soaked in the sea of milk. At the age of 20 Caitlin had become battle hardened, and probably more seasoned that most veterans in the sport for years. She just prayed that it wouldn’t age her beyond her time where she wouldn’t be able to find the true happiness which she deserved, because she knew being as extraordinary as her friend was almost meant times walking a road where she’s mostly alone, and sometimes people don’t see underneath the super woman exterior is a simple young girl looking for love and acceptance…even Roxy at times has to catch herself and remember.] Caitlin Fairchild – The final key is going to be the endurance factor…other than the match I had with Dante, the most enduring match I’ve EVER been in was when I competed in that Elimination Ladder match for the VSE World title, THIS match is going to push me to the limit, and I’m have to be 100 percent on point PHYSICALLY and MENTALLY if I’m going to walk away with the huge win on this one. Anyway…we got two hours to hang out, before we have to leave. Free Fall – (Snaps out of her trance.) Two hours? Caitlin Fairchild – Yeah, we’re meeting my sister to go work out at Top Fitness Club. Free Fall – (Sarcastic Smile.) Great…whoopee… Caitlin Fairchild – What have you got against my sister? Free Fall – (Puts her head down.) Nuthin yo…I just think you all up her ass and what not…and you tossin me to the side. [Caitlin gets up seeing the sad look on her friends face, puts her bowl side, gets to her feet, and bends over putting her forehead against Roxy’s.] Caitlin Fairchild – Come on Rox…It’s my sister…I’m just trying to get to know her, and I’m not up her ass…besides…you know no one can replace you… Free Fall – Yeah? Caitlin Fairchild – (Jokingly.) Of course…you’re my lover… [Free Fall looks up in shock as Caitlin plants a long kiss on her lips! Roxy screams underneath the kiss, as Caitlin releases her laughing! Free Fall jumps on her dropping the ENTIRE bowl of Fruit Loops on the carpet floor as BOTH of them go over the coffee table!] Free Fall – BEEEOTCH! I told you I don’t swing that way!! Caitlin Fairchild – Ya could have fooled me! HAHAHAHAHAHA! Stop I’m ticklish there!! Free Fall – Die rug muncher!! Die!! [The two friends roll around on the carpet laughing, tickling, and pretend wrestling each other. Free Fall quickly jumps on the coffee table, does a Jimmy Snuka Fly pose, the dives off onto Caitlin who dies laughing as they continue to frolic on the floor as the 4WW logo blows by fading the scene out all together.]
