Remeber, If the Baby dies its you're fault Psymon (Rp1)

14 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Remeber, If the Baby dies its you're fault Psymon (Rp1) 'Insane' Chris West 4WW Exposures #134 Date: Sept 14th 2003 Location: Backstage After Stormfront [The Camera turns on right after Stormfront.. Loud Sounds, and Bangs are Hard in the Background, as the Camera Slowly Turns around. Watching “Insane” Chris West who is wearing a “FUCK YOU PSYMON” T-Shirt, and his trademarked Blue Jeans.. He’s Smashing Everything in Sight with his Barbwire Baseball Bat. Tables, Chairs, Windows. You name it, West is Destroying it. Out of Anger from Losing the South Wind Championship] Insane: FUCK YOU, YOU MOTHER FUCKER!!! [He Keeps Smashing shit up, as he stops and Looks around at all the Mess he has Caused, as he Drops the Barbwire Bat, and Starts Laughing. He Downs down to his Knees, as he Starts Bounding the Ground with his Fist, as the Camera Man Walks over to West.. Doing Close ups of his Face.. As you Can Hear West Speak] Insane: Psymon.. You Want to Fuck with me. You Want to do Whatever you can to fucking screw me.. You Took my Title, you are Working on taking my rep.. Something I’ve earned over the past Seven years in this FUCKING SPORT.. [West Gets up and Grabs the Camera and Looks right into the Camera.. You see the Anger on his Face and in his Eyes.. His Forehead is Taped up from the Battle with El Lobo] Insane: Everyone has a Line you don’t cross. You Can Bend it, but you don’t fucking cross it.. Psymon, you have crossed it.. You Took the South Wind Championship from me, and most importantly, you made me Look like Shit. You Just don’t do that.. How do the Expect me to Ref You’re Match right down the Middle, when I want to grab you and Rip you’re fucking head off. I Know You Want to fucking Kill me.. Just look at El Lobo’s Whore who I mistakenly Hit. the Knocked up Mexican WHORE. She was in the wrong place at the ring time. [West Laughs, as all of a Sudden his Face turns to a Serious Look] Insane: BRING IT. Just Remember, You are on the Losing Streak, you can’t aford another Lose, you fucking touch me, I’ll fucking DQ you.. And I’ll get you fucking finded.. I’m the Ref, Bitch, you don’t get to touch me.. You think I’m in the wrong place at the right time, Psymon.. Simply.. YOU’RE FUCKED.. And Once I’m finished you.. YOU’RE FUCKING DEAD!!!! DEAD JUST LIKE EL LOBO’s UNBORN BABY!!!! [West Turns around, like he’s going to Walk away from the Mess, and put all this Behind him, as he turns around and Looks into the Camera] Insane: Psymon. When, You Crossed that line, you set forth a Chain Reaction. Events are going to unfold I’m not going to be Proud about. People are going to get Hurt, and it won’t be by a Mistake. Just Keep in Mind. I didn’t cross this Line, I wasn’t the one who set forth this Chain Reaction. It was You, and ITS YOU’RE FAULT if EL LOBO’s BASTARD CHILD DIES!!!! [The Camera Fades to Black, as it fades back on.. You See “Holy Shit” Jason Bird who is wearing a “I’m a Dirty Dirty Man” T-Shirt, and Blue Jeans. He has on a Set of Gloves as he grabbing the used Barbwire from the West against El Lobo Match, and Rolling it up.. West it Cleaning up all the Broken Glass, and putting it into a Box. West Stops and Pops open a Canadian Beer.. He Turns to the Camera. Bird Keeps Working in the Background as West Speaks his Mind] Insane: I don’t know if the 4WW did me a Favor or wants to punish me for what I did last Week. Where Mistakenly Hit Angel in the gut. They Put me Against “The Irish Fucker” Sean McClancy. Holy Shit: Isn’t He Married or Dating Patti?? Insane: Truthfully that shit doesn’t Matter.. Maybe, it could be why I’m facing him. it could be why the 4WW Saw it fit to put me against him.. He Knows and Respects Women, You see Bird right now I’m what they call a Women Beater. Though, It was a Mistake. But, you know what Bird. [Bird Stops Working as he Walks over to West and Looks at him] Insane: Its not going to Work, this isn’t Punishment, this is a Chance for me to Shine to Showoff my Skills, and Show Psymon, Just what he’s in Store for. [West Hands Bird the Broom, and Dust Pan, and tells him to clean up the rest of the Broken Glass, as West Walks off, Holding his Canadian Beer Can in his Hands.. He Stops and Leans up against a Wall] Insane: Sean.. Around here that call you the Hardcore Icon. From Where I’m From, I’m called the Hardcore Legend. For Years I would be do anything to win, Anything. Just like I’m going to do here, Sean, you’re no Hardcore Icon, You Just a Wannbe. You are Irish, You no Hardcore Icon, You are Just Drunk and Stupid. though, people could say the same thing about me.. Hell, I’m Drinking a Beer right now. [West Laughs a Little] Insane: Truthfully Sean.. I’m not looking past you or anything, But this Match too me, is threapt. a Chance to get out all the anger from Losing my South Wind Championship.. And I’m Sure you’re going to use this Match to get you’re shot at summers. We Both have Common Goals, You Have to win this Match to face Summers. Someone who I’ve happen grown to Respect. And, I need to win this Match to Show, Psymon I’m not fucking around. Bring All the Weapons You Can, I know I will be. BRING IT ALL. After this Match we are going to have another thing in Common.. We’re going to look like Patti’s Rags after this Match, You know a Fucking Blood Mess. [The Camera Fades to Black, as West finishes the Can, and tosses it at Bird] [Almost Eight years in the Sport. Everywhere I go, I almost have someone, I want to Hurt someone I want to take out, But I’ve never had this Much Anger at one guy, Psymon, You have done it, you have crossed the line, you are making me snap.. trying to make me do something I never though I would ever do. Something so Harsh, that I could be a Targeted Man. Psymon, Just Remember.. ITS ALL YOU’RE FAULT]
