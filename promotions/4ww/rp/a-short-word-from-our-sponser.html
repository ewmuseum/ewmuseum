A Short Word from Our Sponser

11 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Short Word from Our Sponser 'The Archangel' Lucifer Preist 4WW Exposures #1126 Date: 1.11.05. Location: Static takes over the air waves as the screen flickers back and forth between the static picture and a room with a single chair in the middle of it. We finally stop flickering and stop on the chair. Lucifer Priest enters the scene and takes a seat looking into the camera. ][Priest][ I know I don’t have a match this week and that’s fine. Better for the roster as they don’t get a chance to feel my pain and agony. But, there are a few words being exchanged that are getting to me. Claudio Turbine and his ideal threats on how he’s going to take out the DMC. Claudio with all due respect you’ve got a bad awakening in store for yourself when you t ry. Claudio, it’s time for you to shape up don’t you think so? I mean instead of holding these East Wind titles for three more weeks we have to defend them in two. I told you people don’t like us... They’re looking to screw us over any way they can and you need to realize that. Don’t you think it’s time you came home? To the participants in the Battle Royal. I’ll be watching... it doesn’t matter to me who wins christ it can be Kyo and Wesley Williams that enter that royal and walk out with title shots. I don’t care... I’m always looking for a challenge. No one on this roster strikes fear in my heart. Knowing I can crush each and every one of you with my bare hands gives me enough comfort to sleep at night. I’m predicting there’ll be some fierce competition in that match come Stormfront. Hell, there’s probably gonna be some names that come back into the business. Lee Satima, I know they checked that vat of blood after I dumped you in it and you weren’t there, but if you’re watching this right now know that I’m anticipating your arrival in the battle royal.. Anyone from the DMC’s past can enter that damn royal even fellow members can join if they’d like. I’ve been on the side lines for a long time now. A match every here and there, but know this.... the Archangel doesn’t get ring rust. He just get’s angrier ... more... aggressive. So Participants whoever you may be... step up to the plate and take a swing, because with Lucifer Priest holding half of the East Wind titles... it’s gonna take a damn miracle for you to... Lucifer looks down and runs his glove covered hand through his hair before snapping his head back up and looking into the camera once more.. ][Priest][ The Boss tries to reassure me that everythings ok... everythings fine. Garrett would never screw the DMC over, but I have my eyes open. Garrett makes this match and it’s opened to anyone on the roster, and he knows that me and the other champion aren’t on the best of sides right now so we’ll have a problem stacking up to the competition. Garrett, I’m watching you buddy don’t fuck us over and maybe I’ll let you live. The Boss is pretty high up about you, says you’re something great... Says you even hate family and deny your own child. Well, I was a child once and you all know what my father did to me and my mother, so Garrett... I don’t like fathers. You get the picture? I don’t like what you’re doing. The Archangel is open to any challenge, but what is your motive behind this? To ignite a spark within your stupid division? The division is on fire with the Infidels as tag team champions. It was dull when Future Shock was... we fixed that for you, we made the East Wind Division what it used to be. We’re making it the best division on the damn roster. You think people actually care about Kyo Kushinaga vs. Rob Givens? Really... what crowd will that draw? The people come to see the Infidels and you’re taking that away from them. Lucifer gets up and grabs the chair smashing it against the wall.. ][Priest][ Whoever is in the Tag Team Battle Royal will know the exact meaning of pain once I’m done with them. Bring it on... the Archangel plans to lose this title when he’s good and ready. The DMC is here and will reign supreme.... you nonbelievers will see..... Lucifer walks away and the screen returns to it’s static like state once more...
