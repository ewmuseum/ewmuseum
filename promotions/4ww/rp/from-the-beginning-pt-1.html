From the Beginning - PT.1 4WW Exposures #918 Date: 09/02/04 Location: From the Beginning - PT. 1 Darek DeAngleo _______________ ::[Introduction]:: _______________ Everything in the world had begun to change, nothing was as it used to be. There was a world of confusion and frustration that had sunk into the mind of Darek DeAngleo. He felt confined within a prison of his own troubled thoughts. Darek walked into a war with his pride, dignity, riding on the outcome and he was defeated. It the towel was tossed over the ropes and the match was stopped. Knowing full well that Patricia Anderson was the one that had levelled Syckle with a loaded boot and throw his towel in, Darek was now in search for retrobution. He had been defeated by Blaise McClancy, he had lost his South Wind Title the week before Malestrom V. Blaise McClancy was to be the first stepping stone on a long path to success, Darek was destined to beat Blaise, move to maelstrom and capture the North Wind Championship. But his influence and touch of purification was not going to end in the grounds of the Four Winds. Darek had spread his word into the PrimeTime, he had been training long and hard, ready to stand before anything Mount St. Hells had to offer. Depending upon his performance and the ability of the warriors from the Four Winds to do battle, Darek could very well be named as a competitor to the coveted Global Title aswell. But the plan was not going as it had been forseen, Darek knew he could out wrestle Blaise and his opportunity was taken from him. He could not help but wonder what the future of Winds of War had to offer, would Darek fall subject to a clean loss, would he be fooled somehow by someone and have the title stolen from under him or would he emerge as the victor. The options were laid before him, and all his training, his focus had been on larger promotions in the PrimeTime, there was no honor greater than representing the Four Winds. An opportunity had presented itself to Darek, after suffering a devistating onslaught from the KnightMare Brigade of the UWF and 4ww, he knew the demon within his body was gone. No longer would he fall subject to the seduction and witchery of Patricia Anderson, now he walked with a purpose, the mission to cleanse her soul and reunite himself with Raven. Darek felt he could trust Patricia with Raven when he believed Kyo was going to use her as leverage to take advantage of Darek. Learning that Syckle, Darek's mentor, had approached Kyo and warned him to leave Raven be, Darek knew he had made a mistake with taking Raven to Patti. Everything fell into place, his whole world changed before his eyes. The soul surviving family member of the DeAngleo family was about to become an Anderson, the sister of Patti. This thought ravished the Modern Messiah's mind day and night, tormenting the very fabric of his existence. There was not a second that passed when he did not think about the harm, danger and evil that Patti would put Raven trhough to get what she wanted. Though, another part of him believed that Patti really did love Raven and wouldn't let any harm come of her. Yet Darek was not willing to compromise, he wanted nothing more than Raven at his side and the opportunity to cleanse Patricia of her sins. It had been a long hard road to get here, first being drawn from the KnightMare Brigade by Patricia the fiancee of the leader of the Order. It was soon shown that the KnightMare Brigade's strings were not being pulled by McClancy, but by Patricia. She led Darek and Bullet into a slaughter then disappeared into the back to have a wonderful time with Sean McClancy. The beat down lead to the destruction of Dark Angel, the combination of a demon named Dominic and Darek; a vile, wretched persona that claimed the lives of the innocent to satisify his insatiable lust for blood. The monster was destroyed and Darek was left to journey the land with out his strength of Dominic. He had irresponsibley used his abilities and strengths as the angel of death to meet his desires. This soon settled into his mind and soul, free of the darkness, Darek knew had had to change his life. Devoting his life to the reform of his beliefs, Darek decided to walk into the light of the lord. He now chose to spread the will and word of the lord and in doing so had hoped to gain the knowledge and power to pass the Trails of Redemption. His very salvation rested squarely upon his ability, his will to fight. He trained day and night, with tenacious ferocity that made it clear to those around him, that he was dedicated to defining his career, to leaving his legacy. Dark Angel had fought long and hard to build a dynasty in which he could rule, but his existence was cut drastically short and Darek's chances at Survival too have been cut short. Falling subject to Patti Anderson's devious scheme, Darek had stumbled in his first attempt to glorify the Four Winds and purify the world. Blaise now held his South Wind Title, Patti held the North Wind Title and Raven; Kyo was a demented, tormented soul that could hardly realise the perfect opportunity when he sees it. He turned his back to Darek, he chose to stand before a path that leads to the purification of an Outlaw. Sin will no longer run rampart over the Four Winds. The destiny of Darek DeAngleo now began to unfold, perhaps Blaise was meant to even the series in a manner inwhich Darek had took the lead. The destined third meeting would bring the two titans together in a battle that could not be paralleled. One and One, the two would look to solidify their positions in the dynasty of wrestling history. Blaise McClancy, Darek DeAngleo, the two seemed fated to have such a war, to be taintedly destroyed systematically, but their final encounter would bring resolution. This was now clear to Darek, should he pass the obsticle of Kyo Kushinga, the Deathscythe Outlaw and the Diva from Down Under, Pattti Anderson, he would stand atop one mountain already. One bound, one leap away from Survival, the will of the Lord now rested in the hands of Darek's mission to cleanse the land of sin. To become the North Wind Champion, would be to stand before God's loyal subjects and proclaim to those who wish to walk the valley of the shadow of death, the path leads to Darek DeAngleo and purification. To defeat Darek DeAngleo would be to defy the will and testament of the lord, that chosen soul shall be deemed worthy. The eyes of the weary Modern Messiah were now set squarely ahead on Maelstrom. his endurace, stamina, all boosted from intense training to Survive. Bringing the Outlaw and the treacherous Patti Anderson before the judgement of our God Almighty would be the duty of the Angel and he swore to do so. Feeling emotions that he had long forgotton, compassion; Darek longed to understand the inner workings of his one time ally and brother, Wesley Williams. Trust: He had even learned to trust again, since Patricia betrayed him and left him for the wolves of the KnightMare Brigade. Darek now placed his faith and trust into the hands of a one time enemy, William. Together, the two had begun to fight for the right to redeem Darek from the sins of Dark Angel. The Trials of Redemption would offer him the chance to have one meanful, life altering expirience. Darek wanted to become an Elder, to sit above the Four Winds with uncanny ability and watch over it with a guiding and loving hand. Fair to those who have earned a place and opportunity to stand before his presence and swift to damn those who have treacherously worked their way before him. It all really began by losing that South Wind Championship, everything that Darek found good in his reign as the champion would now be magnified, his touch and graps would spread farther into the Four Winds than ever before. Now he stood destined to fullfill the destiny he and God had made for him. Walking the line of redemption, the Angel soon comes to the realisation that to hold the North Wind Championship comes with a dutiful responsibility to stand a test of time. To be a proven warrior, one who will not fall when the time comes. Darek had already fallen to Blaise, would he fall from the grace of God as well? Ironman hell, sixty minutes of torture as three individuals make an attempt to be procalimed the leader of the Four Winds. There would always be someone tere to challenge the champion never again two like Darek and Kyo. This was shaping up to be a war and with the mind games between Patti and Darek, this battle was going to be a painful lesson for those who stand before the Messiah. Darek felt the warmth of the comfort in knowing that God would be there to guide him through this enduring situation. The Mountain of the Winds must be climbd before Darek could begin to climb Mount St. Hells. Losing to Blaise only proved to make Darek much more focused on the tasks at hand. No longer would he underestimate the abilities of his opponents or his enemies. Patti Anderson had taken another possession from Darek and now Darek was set fixed on returning the favour by taking it all back and so much more. This would be his opportunity to stand before the Outlaw and the Diva from Down Under and morally cleanse their souls of any wrong, all with his graceful judgement handed from God. Striking down the likes of the current North Wind Champion and the True Number One Contender, would cement Darek's place within the Four Winds and PrimeTime. This would be the calm before the storm. No one had any idea whom Darek DeAngleo was until the Survival came along, saying alot of things Darek now hopes to back those words up, but why not show up as the North Wind Champion? People would be amazed to realise that Patti Anderson had been toppled, a new champion crowned. The leader of the winds would be the very being they were destined to stand before in the climb of the Mountain. Darek knew he had a golden chance to redeem himself and take the added benefits within. Following the Survival lead Darek to a glorious chance to confront the legendary Seymour Almasy, a match that has been months in the making and one that Darek has long awaited since Almasy spewed the challenge. After dispossing the weak and feeble Almasy, Darek then proclaimed it his destiny to purify the world. He had entered his name into a war that brought well over one hundred fifty mean and women to battle. This was the best position for Darek, standing in the tournament with a Global Championship, a North Wind Championship and the respect of all those who looked in awe. ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ Only God knows why things are as they are.
