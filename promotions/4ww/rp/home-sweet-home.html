Home Sweet Home

25 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Home Sweet Home Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1075 Date: 11/25/04 Location: Rustin Slade's Apartment: Manhattan, NY (The camera zooms into a small studio apartment, somewhere in Upper Manhattan. The camera pans over the apartment, and we see a very clean area. The only mess seems to be among the coffee table where endless amounts of self help and dating books are piled randomly over the surface. The camera catches a few of them. Dating for Dummies. Building Self-Confidence. Charisma Crafter. How to Make Anyone Fall in Love With You.) (The camera now moves over to the refridgerator where we see Rustin Slade, the 4WW wrestler getting himself a glass of orange juice. He’s holding a remote in his hand. He walks towards his couch, which seems to double as his bed and sits down in it. He flicks on the TV. As he’s channel surfing we see that most of the themes on the stations are about dating. Rustin gets fed up and hurls the remote at the TV.) Rustin: Ah what do you know! Stupid TV. All they show is how all these guys can get chicks like it’s no problem. Well, they’re false advertising. It’s hard ass work. (Rustin gets up and pops in a tape. We see that the tape is a highlight spot reel of his match last week against Paul Williams. Rustin sits back down, sipping his orange juice.) Rustin: Where did I go wrong? Damn, he was tough. (Rustin jots down a few notes on a pad with a pencil. He takes another sip of his orange juice, when we hear the phone ring. Rustin puts the VCR on pause and picks up the phone.) Rustin: Rustin here. Woman on phone: Hey! Ready for a good time? Rustin: Ummmm, I think you got the wrong number. Woman on phone: No, I don’t think so. Is this Rustin Slade? (Rustin smiles and sits back, sipping his orange juice. He now talks in a more seductive tone.) Rustin: You know it honey, one of the hottest assets in 4WW. What you going to do to me? Woman on phone: Oh, well we’re going to get it on real hard tonight. Rustin: Yeah that’s it baby, tell me what you want. Woman on phone: I just want to get it done with you Mr. Slade. Rustin: Oh we’ll get it done there pumpkin. Woman on phone: Good, let’s take it nice and slow. Rustin: Now you’re talking. I like it like that. Woman on phone: Good. Because you’re in debt $4000 to the IRS sir. This is Madeline Freeman speaking, of IRS auditing services. Rustin: Son of a bitch! I can’t win! (Rustin gets fed up and hurls the phone across the room. He unpauses the VCR and Rustin goes back to writing down notes.) Rustin: I’ll be ready for you this week Cameron. You and me pal, I’ve got to get noticed in 4WW. It’s the only way. I need to start making a name rather than working harder to defamate it. (Rustin watches his tape and suddenly jumps up in surprise, knocking over his orange juice!) Rustin: That’s it! I see it now. Gosh, was I out of it last week. No worries, I’ll just head over to the gym, and work on my weaknesses. (However, at that moment, Rustin’s biggest weakness comes onto the TV. He sits back down onto the couch as he fast forwards the tape a little and gets a good look at some of the woman wrestlers in 4WW. Caitlyn Fairchild is on the TV now giving an interview, and later we see a glimpse of Luscious Lady Luck in her match against Ike Angel.) Rustin: Well, I’ll go to the gym, just as soon as I analyze this match, and Caitlyn’s interview. A good rounded wrestler watches other matches than his own, you know. (He pours himself another glass of orange juice and sits down oogling the images of the finer of the 4WW competition.) Rustin: One day Rustin. Women will flock to you, you devil you. You’ll need a damn fire hose to keep them all at bay. (Rustin stops the VCR and hits another button on his remote and a CD Player turns on and “You Sexy Thing” by Hot Chocolate fills Rustin’s apartment. Rustin gets up and starts walking around Full-Monty-esque, making a fool out of himself. Noticing the camera, he quickly stops and speaks to it.) Rustin: Dancing’s just as important as pushups in a workout routine you know. (Rustin goes back to dancing, and getting his groove on. The camera pans away from Rustin as we leave his apartment, we notice a little picture frame with Rustin’s mantra inside it. “Cheer up the worst is yet to come.” The camera fades to black as we leave Rustin’s apartment and Hot Chocolate drowns out all rational thought.)
