What a girl wants

11 October 2004

What a girl wants Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #1002 Date: 10/11/04 Location: Santa Barbara, California [The 4WW logo blows across the screen as we hit down town Santa Barbara, California in the heart of the city. The Fall air can be sensed blowing by, but not enough to overtake the dominating heat of the sun, which was ever present. In the city streets where people around majority of the United States are putting on thick sweaters, and jackets, the natives here still dawn shorts, and tank tops. In the middle of the many shops is Bob and Son’s tattoo parlor where our story begins, here among the walls of colorful pictures, and graphics (some disturbing) among the shots of past satisfied clients, and the buzz of the tattoo machine being worked on a customer sits out heroines Caitlin and Free Fall with in the parlor room waiting. Caitlin decked out in a white midriff baby Supergirl T-shirt, and ripped up dazey duke blue jeans shorts with platform sandals with her long red mane tied back sits fidgeting shaking her shiny pink toe nailed feet next to Free Fall wearing a Batgirl baby T-Shirt, with matching dazey duke blue jeans shorts with purple flip flops, her lavender and purple hair banged out as she calmly flips through the Tattoo magazine looking at the various forms of body art people dared to don on their bodies. How they ended up there is kind of perplexed to say the least as early in the morning out of nowhere in the middle of breakfast Caitlin decided she wanted to get a tattoo, Free Fall scoffed knowing how squeaky clean her room mate/ best friend was (also the fact that Caitlin is severely squeamish around needles) Fairchild became more persistent as the morning drew on about getting some form of body art, so Roxy eager to call her Amazon friend’s bluff hopped on the net, found the nearest top tattoo parlor in Santa Barbara, made an appointment, and PERSONALLY drove Caitlin. Caitlin was fine for the first two minutes, but as the time passed she became for uppity.] Caitlin Fairchild – Um…did…did I take my clothes out of the wash? Free Fall – No…I did, they’re in the dryer. Caitlin Fairchild – Ok. [Another minute passes.] Caitlin Fairchild – Um…did…did you remember to shut off the computer. Free Fall – Yes…and the television. Caitlin Fairchild – Oh…Ok. [Another minute passes.] Caitlin Fairchild – Um…did…did you check to make sure this guy was legit…I heard people catch stuff from un-sanitized tattoo needles. Free Fall – Kat…this guys will WELL known…he did the Rock’s tattoos…look. [Free Fall pointed to an autographed picture on the wall of the Rock against the wall posing shirtless with his new tribal tattoos, giving the trademark eyebrow. The bottom says, “THE ROCK SAYS…THANK YOU BOB and ERIC!!” Caitlin nods as Free Fall goes back to looking through her magazine. Another minute passes.] Caitlin Fairchild – Did…did you remember to turn off the lights in the Humvee? Free Fall (Gives her an irritated look.) – Kat…it’s 2 p.m. in the friggin afternoon, why the HELL would I have the lights on for? Caitlin Fairchild – Oh…right. [Another minute passes.] Caitlin Fairchild – Did you… Free Fall – YES…YES…and the answer to your next question is YES…damn girl, instead of driving my crazy with questions, why don’t you go look around, and see what kind of tattoo you want? Caitlin Fairchild – I don’t really like what I see around here… Free Fall – Do you have an idea, of what you want? Caitlin Fairchild – Not really…maybe I should go home and think about it. Free Fall (Smirks while muttering as she flips the pages in the magazine.) – I knew it… Caitlin Fairchild – Knew what? Free Fall (Turns to her.) – I knew you were gonna chicken out. Caitlin Fairchild – I’m not chickening out…I’m just going home to… Free Fall (Cuts her off.) – Please Kat, as soon as you leave this place you’re not coming back, you know it, and I know it. We BOTH know you’re terrified of needles, not to mention you’re a GOOD GIRL, so why heck would you waste our Saturday persisting on coming here? [Caitlin leans forward putting her head down with a sad look on her face.] Free Fall – What Kat? Caitlin Fairchild (Sighs.) – Because…I don’t want to be a good girl anymore… Free Fall (Knits her eyebrows.) - Come again? Caitlin Fairchild – I’m sick of the good girl persona…I want to be known as a bad girl for a change… Free Fall – Kat…you’re a wrestler…a female Amazon…you choke slam GUYS for a living…you’re a WORLD FRIGGIN CHAMPION, how BAD do you want to be? Caitlin Fairchild – You see that’s just it…I don’t want to be know as JUST a World Champion female wrestler…aside from that everyone knows I have this squeaky clean image…I’m like a 7 foot nun… Free Fall – Who happens to wear tight clothing that causes traffic accidents…jeez Caitlin, what more do you want? Caitlin Fairchild – I don’t know… Free Fall (Scoffs.) – Well there’s always Play Boy… Caitlin Fairchild (Scowls.) – Not an option. Free Fall – Maxima? Caitlin Fairchild (Leans back in her seat and sighs.) – Forget it… Free Fall – This has something more to do that trying to get a bag girl image Kat…what is it? Caitlin Fairchild – I keep getting this feeling that something is missing in my life, and I don’t know what that is…I have a solid wrestling career with all the perks of fame, family, friends…but I just feel that something is missing…I feel empty inside, and I don’t know why. Free Fall – You need a man. Caitlin Fairchild – Why is that EVERYONE’S advice when it comes to me? My “mother” says that, Patti, you! I don’t need a man to make me complete! Free Fall – No…but you still want one, even if it’s to take you to the movies. [Caitlin sighs as Roxy flips through the pages, and then glances at the magazine. Her eyes knit as something catches her eyes. Caitlin leans closer to get a view of a well toned female buxom red head in a orange top, and white G-string striking a one arm muscle pose. She has a tribal tattoo on her right bicep, and a tiny heart with an arrow in it right by her crotch area.] Caitlin Fairchild – Who’s that? Free Fall – Who? Caitlin Fairchild – The red head. Free Fall – April Hunter…you know…the chick that was a WCW Nitro girl, she wrestlers in Indy feds, and does porn magazine spreads…I think she was in Playboy too. Caitlin Fairchild – I like her tattoos. Free Fall – Yeah right… Caitlin Fairchild – No seriously…I do…they’re perfect for me…I want them. Free Fall – No you don’t. You don’t know what you want. [The tattoo artist Eric (Bob's son) walks out, a young man in his mid-twenties with long dark hair, and various tattoos running down both arms decked out in a black “Black Sabbath” sleeveless T-shirt with ripped up jeans, and dirty construction boots. His latest work, a bald headed Caucasian male with a scar over his dead left eye in a blue wifebeater, with military jeans, and combat boots walks out with a new tattoo on his on his left arm, or a Eagle head with a tear in it’s eye, behind it was a mural of the Twin Towers, with writing underneath with read, “WE WILL NEVER FORGET”. It matched his right arm, which hard a chopper with American colors, underneath that one read “MADE IN THE U.S.A”. The man shook the artists hand, and handed him a wad of 100 dollar bills before saluting, and walking out giving the girls a brief glance.] Eric – Later champ, catch you on TV…. NEXT VICTIM!! [Caitlin out of nowhere snatches the magazine out of Roxy’s hand, gets up and walks over to the artist.] Free Fall – HEY! Caitlin Fairchild – Can you give me the same tattoos like hers? Eric – Sure…if that’s what you want. [Caitlin turns to look Free Fall in eyes with a confident and determined look on her face.] Caitlin Fairchild – Yeah…that is what I want. Eric – All right…follow me. [Caitlin gave a stunned Roxy a brief smile, and took a deep breath as she followed the tattoo artist into the back. Truth was, she didn’t know what she wanted, she wasn’t even know if she really wanted the tattoos, but what she did know was she didn’t want to be scared, or uncertain of herself anymore. Getting the tattoos may not be the right decision, but it was her decision, one that she wanted to make, and in her minded the right step to finding what she truly wanted in life. The 4WW logo blew across the screen as Caitlin disappeared behind the curtains, unsure of what she was stepping into, but confident that she’d be alright when she came back out.]
