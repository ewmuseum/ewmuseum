Situations, part I Blaise 4WW Exposures #1031 Date: 10/28/04 Location: Stil in Ottawa To recap: what was supposed to be a challenge match for the East Wind Titles by the new team of the Infidels of the DMC... turned into a shit fest. Todd Dream, the commissioner of the 4WW, who has taken it upon himself over the last few weeks to really drop the 4WW in to some shit. First he tries to choose the new North Wind Champion in Darek DeAngelo, but he ended up having Kyo Kushinaga outsmart him. And in the end when we had two North Wind Champions, Darek DeAngelo... Dark Angel... figured out that he had failed in all accounts, and ran with his tail between his legs and turned his half of his title over to the man who had fought with the only true honor he had. Todd Dream must have shit golden twinkies when that happened. So now he has a new target... Future Shock, Blaise McClancy to be exact. It's well known that Todd Dream does'nt like Blaise, even though the two have no history. Todd Dream only hates the KnightMare Brigade and Blasie's family and his teacher. The Infidels vs. FutureShock... East Wind Titles was set to happen. First Todd Dream restarted the match with a no disqualification clause. Why did he restart the match? Because FutureShock had taken the game to the Infidels and had trounced them like the amateurs they truly are. He restarted it and then the Infidels, knowing that Blaise hates gimmick matches, proved that they didn't need talent or skill to do anything, but only some luck and a table... spiking Ukyo through it for their own win with their own underhanded tactics going to 'good use' as far as they were concerned. Sorry, but the contract signed was for a one fall standard match. Todd Dream overstepped his bounds and one has to wonder if the Infidels were in on it. But Todd Dream was finally countered with a new co-commissioner... Patti Anderson. Dream's old nemisis was there and she restarted the match again when the DMC's representatives thought they had it won. Sorry guys, but you can see what happened there. Apocalypse, Amanda, The Whore of Babylon herself and her new found 'friend' in Fable came out and cost the match again. They handed the win to the Infidels, but they used a chair. Now, you have to wonder, if Todd Dream had given his backing to Dark Angel, is he also behind this new rendition of Natural selection? And you also have to wonder if the failure that is Darek DeAngelo has pushed his woman into some night time vampire antics with Fable? Figures, he didn't have the balls to face Blaise in another one on one contest with no guaranteed interferance. Now, these two ladies will claim that they owned Future Shock... They'll try to spin you worse then an Election Campaign manager for the US Presidency. But truth... which as Blaise's own teacher always said... hurts more then anything, will set this straight. Fact: Future Shock had already been wrestling in not one, but for all intents and purposes, two matches, going on a third full one, even though it was an extended match. They were wrestling with another team and were concentrating on what was going on in the match. Fact: Apocalypse, even though she comes in with a great Resume... 2 time GWA World Champion... 32-0 wrestling record.... She sure seems to have needed HELP in a simple beat down. Chairs, attempted use at a cattle prod/stun gun? Come on Ladies, yeah, that's how you make an impact. Maybe that half rate stable, unStable can get you two in. That's about the level you're competing at now. But you think that evened up the score? I don't bloody well think so! First was Darek DeAngelo paying Blaise back for something that Patti Anderson did. In doing so he put his hands on Ukyo, when she wasn't even involved in the match other then as his second in a pure wrestling rules match. That put your side up by one. Darek has touched the significant other of Blaise. And he had no reason other then malice in doing so. So for revenge, yes, revenge... Blaise bounced Darek's returned bitch of a wife off a table. Hell he didn't even put her through it. But he still paid back Darek's actions. But he did it with his own skills and abilities. Now for some reason, Amanda and her new gal pal decide that they're going to get revenge. And they use chairs, stun guns, while powder from who knows where, probably Darek's own stash.. to try to get their revenge. Got news for you, geniuses, but your math doens't add up. That's 2+1 doesn't equal 2... Did you not listen Amanda? Blaise said that if he wanted a challenge, a shot, he could come and find him and LOOK HIM IN THE EYES and say it. You took the cowards way... even Blaise smiled at you, right to your face before he did what he did to you. But we should know not to ask too much more from those who would hang out with the likes of Todd Dream and try to get the crowd to cheer them. 32-0? 2 Time GWA World Champion? Not like Fable has ever done anything worth a damn... so why talk about her... But if this is how Apocalypse does her business, well it just goes to show that the GWA would give that title to anyone. Just goes to show why some place like the GWA had died a slow and painful death... and why someone with honor, like Syckle, who had called Blaise out on his own... had gone about to make sure it was laid to rest. Some memories are best made to rest and be long forgotten. As far as Blaise is concerned now, it's obvious that the GWA and it's champions are nothing but charlatans. A big black mark in the history of Professional Wrestling. The Arena, StormFront that aired on 10/20/04 Backstage, immediately after the EWT Title Match. We find Blaise as he's carrying Ukyo to the back after the malicious attack at the end if their match. Charlene is coming up right behind him. “Bloody move out of my way!” Blaise yells as some of the gaggle of backstage workers are trying to see what's going on with everything. The crew parts like the red sea and allows him to gain access to his locker room. Charlene moves to get in front of him and open the door. Inside a couple of EMT's are getting ready to look at Ukyo, as she's still out of it. Blaise has blood still covering his face in a couple of streams and the remnants of the white powder he had thrown on his face. Blaise puts Ukyo down, gingerly A couple of the boys there with the orange T-shirts with the three initials on their back, EMT start to check on her after the two muggings that she got in the ring. As Charlene leans next to her, Patti Anderson walks into the room, still in her smart cut business suit. “Blaise, Fey just called me on the cell, we've got special EMT's coming in. Just give them a few moments.” Blaise just looks up at his soon to be cousin-in-law and then walks past her to the other side of the locker room. Patti walks over and stands next to Charlene. “This woman is under the protection of the other EMT unit, as you guys know. Stabilize but turn over to them when they get here. She's one of them considered to be special.” Patti pulls up her left arm and looks at her watch. Charlene looks up at her from her seated position. “What are you doing?” “5... 4... 3... 2... 1!” CRASH!! Charlene jumps away from the locker she was seated next to, as from the back room,t he other side, the sound of something beating on the lockers begins to ring out. CRASH... BUMP... SLAM... KARASH!! Charlene gets a worried look on her face as she looks once again to Patti. “Remember, love. I'm marrying one of them soon. The McClancy temper is one that, if anything, is predictable.” Some more loud noises as fist and booted foot to metal continues. Charlene looks over at Ukyo and then to the back again where Blaise is. “Don't fret. He'd never hurt her. However, those who would harm her have what would ammount to a berzerker if they don't watch it.” A couple of people wearing suits come in, along with a female lady dressed as a doctor. They open some breifcases and the suits beging to push the others out of the room. The Order's doctor was there. The Lady Doctor, while looking oriental was definatly a local with her accent. “What happened?” “She got put through a table, spiked vertical. And then a little electric stun gun action and the use of a chair. Nothing most of the guys take into account, but her frame? She's smaller then I am, while taller.” “What is she?” The Oriental doctor just looked up at Patti with that request, but Charlene answered. “I've heard Blaise call her a Kami before. One of the earth. Although if she's like her brother, we don't fully know.” “Kami are myriad. Almost as much as the damn faeries and the angels. But it gives us a point to start at.” Suddenly Blaise comes around the corner and walks over to his locker. His knuckles on his hands have begun to bleed, the tape on them torn and tattered. He reaches into the locker and pulls out a long case. He rips the cloth open, discarding it as the sabre that denotes his rank in the Order is revealed. Chalrene jumps out of his way as the sword is unsheathed with a lound sound of metal grating along metal. As Blaise heads for the door, Patti steps in his way. “I'm not letting ya through that door, Blaise.” “Move, Andonia, this doens't concern you.” “It doesn't? You know damn well Blaise, this isn't how we do things.” “We?” At that, Patti unfolded one of her lapels that had been overlooked. It looked like it was tucked in as a by product of her running to the back. But it was tucked in for a reason. As she did, two pins were blatantly visible there. One was cast into the symbol that adorened the hilt of Blaise's sabre, an anvil with a crossed set of hammers above it. The sysmbol of the Order of the Hammer's field agents themselves. The other was that of a rampant unicorn, it appeared to be in a black enamal or onyx stone carving. “Yes, I said 'WE' as in those of us of the Order. I can't have you marching off with your sabre and scewering a couple of nobodies who will get their just rewards in due time. You're not thinking with your head right now, but a bit below that.” “Always going to sexual?” “No, above that Mr. McClancy. I'm talking about the middle of your chest. To be blunt, you're doing exactly what Sean would do, but just like I'd not be able to allow him to do it, I'll not allow you. Now as the ranking officer here, hand me your sabre.” “Ranking... Officer?” “I've been given a warrant from Fey herself to act as the head of the KB. You're only a sergeant. Nothing against you, but I'm in the desk position now, and you are not trained as a field operative. You're more along a... special line.” Blaise sighed, loud and audible. His shoulders slumped a bit as he handed the 3 foot long silver colored sword to his soon be cousin-in-law, pommel first. “I still can't friggin' belive this.” He turned and grabbed one of the two East Wind Title belts from next to Charlene. “Todd Dream did this to try to get to you. He sees you as the one thing left of what he wants to remove. But I know you, you're probably one of the more stubborn McClancys, and they can be a handful, but I've seen you and you can take the cake.” “Yeah, but why? To take this from us? To take these little pieces of metal? He'd go out of his way to ensure someone got hurt?” “That's his motive. I mean, as much as these fans cheer him, he is one of them that committed one of the heinous beatings I've ever seen a tape of. And it was on your cousin, Fey. He's asked for forgivness. But from what I've seen, he's about as false as can be.” “No shit. Well, I'm not leaving here without doing something. You've got my sabre... now move.” Patti lets him pass by and he walks out of the room. But he stops at the door and turns back inside, looking at his soon to be family member. “You're now in the commissioner spot with him, right?” “Yes, I am Blaise.” “Good, first warning for you. As you can guess. I'm not happy. I'm sick of this shit. Next time... I'm not going to be nice.” “I don't expect you to be, Blaise.” “Next time, someone is riding the Death Dragon..” He then leaves, slamming the door, still carrying the East Wind Title belt in his hands. Patti shakes her head as Charlene comments. “This is going to be bad? Isn't it?” “Never good to challenge someone who'll not only accept it, but ram your own challenge down your throat. There's a few men by the name of Danko and King who can give you more information on that if you want to. And one in particular named Zsasz has done just as he just said, he's ridden the Death Dargon. But that's neither here nor there. Miss, are we going to need to take her to the compound or what?” The doctor looks up at Patti and seeing her pins out in the open, refrains from her intended comment. “Yes, I'll need her transported. The car is waiting. I'll need a cat scan and some natural cures. I could use one of the dryads right now.” “He just walked out after beating the shit out of the lockers.” “I know. But an angry Dryad is of no use to me. Your Prince has already confirmed on the 'net' that he's waiting to help out. While the younger one has the adrenaline flowing he'll only be more trouble. I'd like him to come in by another vehicle as it is.” “I'll take him. Charlene, stick with them, please. Sean will be waiting for you when you get there. Anyone does anything wrong, tell him and he'll take care of it. He's a Lieutenant with them, and the brother of their leaders. So they'll do as he says without thought.” “Ok...Okay...” Charlene gets a slightly blank look on her face as they start to 'pack' Ukyo up to take her out of the building. Fast forward about 15 minutes or so. The main event is starting out in the ring. Kyo Kushinaga, Ukyo's brother, is defending against the likes of Mike Biddle. However, Blaise is just returning from his altercation with Todd Dream in Dream's office. Blaise walks into the locker room and finds it.. empty. A kick to one of the open, and dented locker doors and he turns around. Opening the door again, he finds Patti Anderson standing right there. “Where is she?” “The doctor wanted her to be looked at. She's been taken to the local compound. Due to the Bigfoot sightings and that around here, they've got a full medical lab under the building for it. Charlene went with her. I stayed to take you there. You'll need to get looked also. You're still leaking blood from that head wound.” “Why did they take her?” Blaise yelled into her face. Patti didn't hesitate. He had no such protective thoughts over her. If he went on her, she'd have to fight him. Something she didn't relish doing right now. “Well you taking me there or not?” “Uh, you're not exactly ready, and you're just standing there instead of listening to me. My car is out..” He tried to push past her. She resisted and he brought his right fist up... Fist.... SMACK! Patti smacked him hard across the cheek. She called upon strengths that she didn't fully control yet, and was a bit surprised to see him stumble a bit with the impact. She then heard something that she didn't expect from him. It was almost guttural, a growl. He came at her, she pulled back to slap him again, but his hand came up and grabbed her wrist as it came across. Both came nose to nose as he held her wrist. “I don't care who you're marrying. Don't you ever do that again.” “Do unto others. Now are you going with me or do I have to make you walk?” He released her wrist, allowing her to back away a bit. “Lead the way, I'll be right behind you. Let me grab my bag first.” “Charlene already grabbed it. She didn't think you'd want to hesitate. She has that and the contract for your match next week that Lorena wants. So get to steppin'. My car is right out the nearest entrance to here.” Blaise motions his arms in that direction. “After you.” Patti turns and heads for the door, Blaise not to far behind her. Fast forward again.... a few hours later. Order of the Hammer's Ottawa, Ontario compound. Labeled as a Paranormal Research Center Specializing in Sasquatch research. Underground level 2, Infirmary. Observation room 1 Blaise, now in a pair of jeans and a muscle shirt walks in. He's been cleaned up and a couple of stitches are obvious on the top of his forehead where he was hit with the chair. Ukyo lays in the bed, appearing to be sleeping. Blaise pulls a chair up to her side and then sits down. “Hey.” His head snaps over as Ukyo's eyes open. “Hey, Baby.” “I'm sorry. I messed up. I heard we almost got shafted in it.” “You didn't mess up. There was no honor in that team or that match once Todd Roark got a sick up his ass. He's no better then his old man. If not worse.” He takes Ukyo's hand. “You didn't mess up.” “I wasn't prepared. Although I heard that after I went through the table there were a few more that got into the ring.” He sighs, audibly, and then continues. “We had won the match. Roark didn't like it. So he came out and changed the rules on us. He knows I'm not like Sean. He knows I hate that. You were a victim of his vendetta against my family. Both blood and soon by marriage. Again, I'm the fucking scape goat while someone wants to get back at others.” “They're just jealous.” “Yeah.” He holds her hand up to his lips and kisses it. “I'll be prepared next time. I've gotten complacent. Your cousin was in here earlier. He helped them get the healing done. He told me that he'd add me to the Order's training schedule if I wanted it. I'm not a hardcore wrestler like Kyo is.” “You don't have to be. I'm not.” “I've heard otherwise. I've heard you just don't like them. You know why too.” “Yes.” “I heard that Fey had to cut a pretty good check to the arena to pay for some lockers too.” Ukyo smiles at him. “That's the reason. Unfortunately I did inherit one really bad trait from my Irish side.” “You scared the crap out of Char. “ “Sorry.” “Don't worry about it. Patti put her to work taking care of some of the paperwork needed for a few things. She likes tasks like that. Keep her occupied.” “Sounds like a plan. They're going to give you some of the medicine soon. I'll be sleeping in here tonight, if you don't mind.” “Not at all. I'd offer you a spot, but they said I have to sleep on my back. No room to cuddle in this bed.” “Chair is fine. It's fine.” At that a male nurse walks in. His features appear to be just a little off to not be fully human. “Prince Turren, I've come to administer the medicine.” Blaise looks, still holding her hand. “I have permission from the Keeper himself to stay. I'll be sleeping either in this chair or on the floor. A few will be brining me down some blankets and a pillow.” The man just stares for a moment, and then nods. He walks over to the other side of the bed. Outside, Patti is standing in the hallway, signing something on a clipboard with another male nurse there. As she finishes, he takes the clip board. “Thank you, Mrs. McClancy.” “Not yet. Not yet. Soon. Tell me, there is another door over there, what's in there?” The nurse smiles at her. “That's the actual observation area. The room is one thing, but there is a two way mirror. That's where we do most of the studying.” “Studying?” “We're a paranormal retrieval and research center run by the Order and the Black Lodge. We have our ways of doing things.” “Is there anyone in there?” He nods to her. “Yeah, the Order sent a psychologist in. She's in there with one of her dark things.” “To study Ukyo?” “No. She's just here to heal. She needs it. The cat scan showed a good stinger and some sprains and some bad bumps after that match. He's not doing too good either. They're trying to find out some things about him.” “Like what?” “Well remember, he was just recently awakened. From what I heard, there have been a few things that they're wanting to look into.” “Yeah. I think I'm going to take a peek.” Patti walks over and opens the extra door. She walks inside and sitting there in front of a panel is a woman she recognizes. Annie Leanan, the woman who she fought with over her now fiance. Sitting next to Annie is something that she doesn't quite recognize. A creature that looks almost to be something from Star Wars or someone's nightmare. Annie looks over her shoulder as the door slides shut, softwly. “Aye, figured ye'd take a peek in 'ere.” “You're the psychologist?” “Yes, I 'ave been fer some time. As a Leanan Shihde, me traits make me prime fer a psychologist. 'Ow do ye be thinkin' I figured ye ou' really quick.” “Yeah, right.” The creature didn't move. He looked to be concentrating on something. Patti looked over both of their heads, and saw Blaise sitting in the chair as Ukyo started to doze off. “Yeah, he's bloody well in love.” “'E's also bloody well proving t' be dangerous. I'm wonderin' jus' wha' in th' 'ell Ian was thinkin' when 'e put 'im through 'is traning.” Patti looked a bit dumbfounded. “Ah, how abou' we show you. Since 'e's yer fokin' problem now, Princess.” Annie tapped her cohort on the shoulder and then right in front of Patti, on the 'table top' of the panel a scene suddenly played out for her. Her altercation with Blaise when she slapped him about 5 hours before. “What about that? I slapped him. Not a big deal. I know he's not someone I'd want to fight without bloody well having the upperhand. Remember, he came after ME as Shoudou.” “Yes, and he did as he was asked to do. He got ye t' recognize yer true self.” “Well a possession wasn't exactly planned.” “Ga' news fer ye. Ye weren't possessed. Ye 'ad it in ye all th' time. I was phase one on tha'. 'Ope tha' wasn't t' 'ard fer ye. Now, replay it with their aura's showin'.” The scene replayed again, only this time a slight glow came around both of them. However at the point right after where Patti slapped him until where they moved back from each other, Blaise's Aura increased and changed from his normal blue-green color to almost a deep red. “So what was that?” Patti had seen some strange things since coming under the care of Sean and his Order. And now as a fully sworn in member, it wasn't getting any less weird. She had to really ask Fey and Sean to fill her in on some of the things around here. But for this, she did show some genuine interest. “That's wha' we're tryin' t' find ou'. I've never seen tha' befer. Tha' was pretty weird. No Tautha does tha'. Na' even a Tautha/Fomorian 'ybrid like Sean. I just wish I had more then visual. But we were only doing a small surface monitoring.” “How were you?” “He was. We'll just put it that way. Do ye remember anythin' from tha' time?” Patti had to strain her memory a bit. To many things in her head... her... Sean.... Finally it came up. “Yeah, it was almost like he growled at me. Either that or he had his teeth clenched as he inhaled. Both would be close.” “I jus' don'na know.” The scene replays a few times as Annie looks in at the couple as they settle down for some sleep. “Mistress.” The creature spoke. “Yeah, wha' ye ga'?” “The' Prince approaches.” Just as the hissing noise from the creature's mouth warns them, the door opens. In walks Sean McClancy, Patti's finance, and the current field commander of the Order of the Hammer's Special Forces Unit, the KnightMare Brigade. As he comes in, Patti turns and puts her arms around his neck. The two kiss deeply as Annie makes it obvious that she's not going to look. “So how's me cousin doin'?” “He's doing fine. But Ms. Leanan 'ere is 'avin' a problem with somethin'.” “Wha' is it?” Annie turns around in her chair to address him. “A surge. A spike if ye will in 'is Aura. Na' only tha', bu'a color shift. Nothin' like I've ever seen. Even when observin' th' likes o' a Caern Ritual, I've never seen anythin' like this.” Sean's lips pursed a bit. “Let me see it” The scene replayed for Sean as it had for Patti. “Ooh, yer fiesty t'nigh'. Maybe I need t' get ye t' bed earlier.” “Promise? My first nigh' at work was a bit rough with this whole situation. I can'na jus' wait fer th' staff meetin' t'morrow. Toddy boy is goin' t' be fumin'.” “Yeah.” Sean just stared at the image as it replayed over and over again. “Oh, I see where yer interest goes. It went from me t' tha'.” Sean bent down onto one knee to watch it a bit closer. “I don'na bloody well believe it.” “What?” “Wha'?” Both Patti and Annie asked at the same time. “Annie, yer goin' t' be doin' some research. Ol' Conner an' Slyfox have pulled one over on us.” “Can ye be explainin' it a bit easier fer me, me Prince?” “Yeah, Mr. William Slyfox, Blaise's grandfather. Old Paiute Indian with a history that's still pretty much out to lunch as far as we're concerned. Conner is me uncle, Blaise's father.” “Yeah?” “I'll write up a report fer ye tomorrow. Get ye workin'. In th' meantime. I think some rest and bed time is in order fer us all. Bu' it looks like we 'ave a rather unique 'alf breed 'ere in me lit'tle cousin.” “A 'alfbreed?” “A very special one. Somethin' tells me tha' Ian an' Fey 'ave known this. I'll need some time in the mornin' befer I get with ye. Now, let's leave it be right now.” All take a look around to each other, except for Annie's assistant. To Be Continued....
