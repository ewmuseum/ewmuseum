Following fate... Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #749 Date: 6-12-04 Location: Seattle, WA “GET OUT OF MY HEAD!” {The scene slowly fades into a very dingy, dirty, and dim lit archway to a cathedral in downtown Seattle, WA. The light from the streets cannot reach within the hallowed archway. Shadows caress the objects which reside with its hallowed embrace. Among these objects exists a man torn with anger and hostility. In him also though exists uncertainty and self rejection. He is a man who has cast off his past in an effort to be reborn. He wished to become a pure soul trough his efforts. Now he lives on, scarred by his immoral past. Kanton merely rests his body against the wet brick of the cathedral archway walls. His clothes… although fresh and crisp… show signs of depression. Something is wrong… a look on resentment crosses his face.} Kanton: Why have you been torturing me? {A gravely voice rings through the air. It is very familiar yet still very similar.} Voice: I am torturing you? How curious… Kanton: What do you mean? You are driving me insane! GO AWAY! Voice: Oh… You know better than that. You need me! Where would you be without me? Kanton: I would be just fine. I don’t need you… Voice: Oh shut up… don’t be so melodramatic Kris… Kanton: What are you talking about? Voice: You know what I’m talking about… Think about Erika… think about the accident… {Kanton grimaces as he holds his head.} Kanton: NO! NO! NO! Voice: That’s what I thought… Let me take away the pain… {Kanton calms down and slowly lowers his hands.} Kanton: What happened? Voice: Nothing my child… I merely cleared the pain from your mind. {Kanton sighs and lowers his head as his face is still hidden by the shadows.} Kanton: I am sorry I spoke up. Voice: It is ok. You see… You should not apologize. You should be proud and hold your head high. You see… I cannot exist on my own. I need you just as much as you need me. Kanton: I see… I understand. Voice: Do you? Kanton: Yes I do… We are two sides to a coin. We are the Yin and Yang. Voice: Yes… and together we are bringing renewed strength to the Four Winds. Let us go… {Kanton then stands up and the scene shifts to his back as he makes his way out of the foyer and into the open night air. The scene follows Kanton as he begins to make his way down the street.} Voice: Look around you… It’s a simple world. What do you see? Kanton: I see darkness… Voice: No! Look under the lights… Look where you beyond where you can see. Kanton: I still see nothing… {The scene shifts to the right as it is focused on a park. Under a lone park light stand a boy and a girl, still in their teens, embraced in a kiss of life.} Voice: See there… See the boy and girl? What do you see? Kanton: I see two lost souls… walking a path that leads nowhere. Voice: But what do they have at the moment? Kanton: I do not understand. Voice: When you look… you only see past and future. Because of what has happened… you see not what exists in the now. Kanton: I still do not understand. Voice: Let us continue. {Kanton continues walking down the side of the road until he comes to an abandoned shop.} Voice: Stop… What do you see here? Kanton: Here exists a place that once had many happy times. It was a candy store for the neighborhood children… Voice: But what do you see now? Kanton: Now… the happiness no longer exists… Voice: Ah… now you are beginning to see. Now… look at yourself… {Kanton moves in closer to a shard of glass from the store front window. He then looks at his reflection in the glass.} Kanton: I see myself… but something is different. Something is missing or changed… {Kanton’s face twists into am evil grimace.} Voice: Because I could not be seen… {Kanton’s face twists into complete horror as he realizes that the voice is coming from his own being.} Kanton: No… It’s not possible! Voice: Why not? I thought we were two sides to one coin. {Kanton’s face twists as he laughs loudly to himself. Suddenly he regains composure and stares on. } Kanton: No… stay away from me! Voice: There is no… Kanton: YES there is… I don’t need you! {Kanton then turns and begins running down the street. He continues until he find himself in the middle of a park near downtown Seattle. He stops near a large water fountain and kneels down on the ground.} Kanton: WHY? {Kanton continues to shout as he pounds his fist on the ground. Tears flow down his face from his closed strained eyes.} Kanton: Must you torture me! Around… all around… the dark memories gather. My dread grows as the dagger of your words falls against my heart. It slays me, and darkly my essence drips to the fallen despairing leaves. In agony I hang my head while nothingness surrounds me. Now alone, my love falls upon dead eyes… This is because of you… {A light begins to shine behind Kanton. An angelic voice rings through the air.} Voice: You don’t really mean that. {Kanton’s face opens wide in shock. He slowly turns his head to look at the light behind him. The scene changes as we look on at a woman bathed in light. She is a very beautiful woman, standing about five feet seven inches. Her hair shines a bright redish blong. Her facial features are highlighted by the magnificent light to give her the look of an angel. She is a very fit woman also, yet well endowed. She wears a long form fitting white dress. Her arms are resting down at her sides as she tilts her head to the side as she looks at Kanton. A smile crosses her face. This woman is the embodiment of Kanton’s one true love… his ex fiancé… his dead fiancé… Erika Hastings. Kanton merely looks on shocked as the love of his life… a dead woman… stands before him…} Erika: Kris… do not be sad. Kanton: Why? Erika: Why did I leave you? {Kanton nods as tears flow down his face.} Erika: I have not left you. I am always here… with you my love. Kanton: But I cannot see you… but I cannot touch you… Erika: But you can feel me… inside here. {Erika reaches out her hand and touches Kanton’s chest.} Erika: I am always in your heart. Now I must go… Kanton: No… please… don’t leave me again. Erika: Kris… I told you… I am always with you. Now, before I go… I must tell you. You must continue with what you are doing. There is a purpose behind all of this. Kanton: What do you mean? Erika: Follow your heart… where I will lead you. The Four Winds needs you… it calls for you. Their essence strives for life within you. Kanton: Why me? Erika: I cannot tell you this my love. Soon all will become clear to you though. My time is over. I must go… Kanton: Erika… no! {Kanton begins to sob loudly as the tears flow down his cheeks.} Kanton: I love you! Erika: And I love you Kris. {Erika then leans down and raises Kanton’s face to her own. She places a soft kiss on his lips as he closes his eyes to receive. Before he even opens his eyes she fades away from existence. Kanton slumps over onto the ground.} Kanton: No… not again… {A wind blows in the air.} Wind: You must fight Kris… fight for me… the Four Winds calls for you… you must follow your true destiny. I am with you… always… {Kanton stands and wipes the tears from his eyes. His eyes burn red with emotion.} Kanton: I will then. I will become the South Wind and bring renewed life to the Four Winds… for you… my love. {Kanton turns and walks away down the path to the street as the scene slowly… fades… out…}
