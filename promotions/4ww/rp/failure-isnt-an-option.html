Failure Isn't An Option

Failure Isn't An Option Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #577 Date: 03/18/04 Location: A Happy, Happy, Place Failure noun Etymology: alteration of earlier failer, from Anglo-French, from Old French faillir to fail 1 a : omission of occurrence or performance; specifically : a 1. failing to perform a duty or expected action b : a state of inability to perform a normal function <kidney failure> -- compare HEART FAILURE c : a fracturing or giving way under stress <structural failure> 2 a : lack of success b : a failing in business : BANKRUPTCY 3 a : a falling short : DEFICIENCY <a crop failure> b : DETERIORATION, DECAY 4 : one that has failed That is what I have suffered. I have suffered failure in my first quest for the North Wind belt. I suffered a defeat that was more of a two-on-one match then a singles match. Todd became afraid and had to have assistance from outside to get the victory. Even though it didn’t happen at that particular moment in time, the feelings of being a failure did set in. I felt as if I had betrayed everything that Sean McClancy had taught me. I felt as if I had let my family down. I felt that I had even let Fey down. She had put me in that match to see what I was made of, and I still let her down. I was prepared to book a flight back home and hide, or even worse, retire and take on the lifestyle of being Sean’s wife and mother to the next generation. This time, it won’t be an option. Failure will fail itself. Todd Dream will be the one that will suffer the disappointments of failure. He’s held onto that title for far to long, that he’s developing a tan line. Trust me, it does not look good to be parading around the cities with a huge ass belt shaped white line on your stomach. It just isn’t appealing to the female fans of the 4WW. Oh, wait...it wouldn’t attract the male population either, for the rumors strongly suggest in the locker room area is that Toddy Boy straddles the fence when it comes to sexual relations. Unfortunately, Toddy Boy’s sexual preference isn’t what is at question here. The big question is...Does he have what it takes to walk out of Maelstrom 3 with the title? Destiny and I both agree...he has no bloody chance of walking out of it with it. He’s deserving of everything that will happen to him. Toddy Boy is owed for the unfair loss of the North Wind Title back at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. Toddy Boy is owed for making me lose the tag team titles to the likes of Jerms and Mike Marvelous at Storm Front. This time Toddy Boy will taste the bitterness of failure at the hands of me. For this time I refuse to allow him to walk out of Maelstrom 3 with the North Wind title. The belt will be coming home with me, failure is not an option. Failure is only the solution for you Toddy Boy. Place: Somewhere in California Time: A Happy Happy Moment Patti Anderson stood staring at the reflection in the mirror. The image that stared back at her was not the same one that was there nine months ago. Nine months... Nine months ago, Patti was a terrified girl entering a sport she had only seen on television. Nine months ago, Patti would have never dreamed that Sean McClancy’s training would lead her to the top of the ladder in the 4WW. Sure, she had won the South Wind Championship with Sean standing by her side and cheering her on. She faced his ex-fiancee and still won his heart, but without him by her side during her first attempt at the title, she failed miserably,. Nine months ago, she was terrified of going after the North Wind title. This time it is completely different. This time, Patti had the confidence to battle Toddy Boy for it. This time, she knew she would take it. A knock on the door disturbed her thoughts. “Come In.” The door opened slightly and Patti’s fiancee poked his head in the door. Patti caught his reflection in the mirror, a smile broke out upon her face. Patti stepped away from the mirror and pounced on him. “Morning, love. I didn’t think you would be getting up this early after last night.” Sean McClancy smiled at seeing the woman he loved. The distance between them had only managed to make the passion burn brighter. Patti was thrilled to have him home, even if it was for a while. She looked loving up at him. “Sean love, I wish you hadn’t cut your hair short. I loved it the way it was, but I know you were looking to change. I just miss the long hair is all.” Patti twirled the hair at the base of Sean’s skull. The hair still felt soft and silky like it always did. The goatee, on the other hand, was going to take a while to get used too. In fact, everything about him was the same except for those two tiny details. Patti sighed as she laid her head against his chest, and listened to the solid, calming beat of his heart. The rhythmic beat of his heart lulling her to fall gently asleep. Sean stared down at Patti, and noticed her eyes had gently drifted into a peaceful rest. He took the moment to notice that Patti had developed dark rings under her eyes from having to stay awake to travel, plus probably the lonely nights of not having him next to her didn’t help much either. His hand reached up and gently stroked her hair for a few seconds before he attempted to wake her up. Sean touched Patti’s shoulder, forcing her hazel eyes to open up and stare blankly at the wall.. Her eyes drifted around the room, stopping for a brief second to stare at the mirror. She saw the reflection of Sean and hers first, but quickly was replaced by a more startling image. An image that almost frightened her. Her hazel eyes blinked as she saw something that disturbed her. The vision continued to get stronger and brighter. Patti watched as the images got larger and clearer of Toddy Boy attacking her during the tag match, dropping her form to the mat with the Dreamscape DDT. The image quickly flashed to Toddy saving her from the hands of Apoc last week. Patti cringed with the visions that were dancing before her eyes in the mirror. She shuddered at the scenes of her failing. Patti pulled away from Sean and walked towards the mirror. Her fist making a solid connection with the glass, shattering it into small pieces. She pulls her hand back, small cuts appearing on her fingers, from where the shards of glass cut her. Patti stared down as her hand became a bloody mess, making no effort to clean it up. Sean blinked several times as to figure out what caused her to do that, but he knew better then to ask the questions. Sean grabbed Patti’s hand and looked it over, and from the look of it, she cut herself severely. Patti looked up at Sean as a lone tear began to fall down her face. Sean lifted the hand to his lips and kissed a clean spot softly and headed off to the bathroom to find something to clean her hand with. Patti stared at the blood that continued to flow out of her hands. The glossy look slowly appeared in her face as a sick, sadistic, smile appeared. Her mind began to form a plan that would bring the title finally home to her. Patti bent down and picked up a shard of glass, the reflection that was caught, showed her now hazel eyes going dark. The smile became more evil, as if something or someone from Patti’s past was speaking to her. The door behind Patti, slowly opened and Patti’s trusted friend and companion, Adrian came bouncing into the room a huge smile on his face. “Patti my dear! I just had the most wonderful night with the man I do believe I’m in lo.....OH JESUS CHRIST!!!’ Adrian saw the blood dripping from Patti’s hand. He let out a girly type girl scream and fainted at the feet of Patti. Patti glared at her friend, the hazel gone completely from her eyes, an evil dark possessed look was in its place. Out of know where Patti speaks. “Oh, Toddy Boy, you will not know what has hit you come this pay per view. The only think I can tell you is, call your personal physician, because blood will be shed, and it will be your failure that you are facing. It is time for a new champion to arise, to shine, to bring honor to the title that you have successfully made a joke of. This time the strengths of all of my ancestors will be here to help hand you the loss that is due you.” Just as quickly as it came, it disappeared as Sean re-entered the room with the first aid kit from the bathroom. He looked from Patti to the lump of Adrian laying on the floor. “Wot ‘append ‘ere?” “Adrian fainted at the sight of my bloody hand. Wimp!” Patti held out her hand to Sean, letting him administer the ointment and gauze to seal the cuts on her hand, as the camera fades to black.
