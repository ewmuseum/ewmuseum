Training Day Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #1074 Date: 11/25/04 Location: LA [The 4WW logo flashes across the screen revealing a huge arena where a wrestling ring can be seen, high above in the rafters, one can see two figures in the ring. One on the top turnbuckle ropes balancing, facing the millions of empty seats in front of them, while the other stands leaning against the ropes with arms folded watching, the individual on the turnbuckle extends their arms, hesitates, the springs off into a less graceful moonsault landing hard half way in the center of the ring…upon impact the following can be heard.] Free Fall – (Gets to her knees.) OW! MUTHAFUCKIN OW! MUTHAFUCKIN OW! MUTHAFUCKIN OW! [Leaning against the ropes in best friend Caitlin Fairchild the current 4WW South Wind Champion who she manages, who now has become her trainer/manager, decked out in a blue and white training version of her ring attire with black boots. In about two days from now she’ll step into the ring battling in a tournament to possibly hold a second championship in the 4WW the West Wind Championship, but upon hearing the news that her best friend has too decided to step into the ring herself, Caitlin has made her it mission to incorporate her into her training, with the objective that she’d be a well rounded rookie stepping into the Machine, a rookie training federation of the Unified Wrestling Alliance…if not a well rounded rookie to at least show her enough so she doesn’t get murdered her first match going in, but as of now training is going a little…slow.] Caitlin Fairchild – You know…you can’t be using that language on national television you know…not in the UWA anyways… Free Fall – Well we ain’t in the fuckin UWA now…what the fuck is this shit made out of? [Caitlin smiles as she gets off the ropes; walk over to the edge of the mat, and pull it up showing her what’s underneath.] Caitlin Fairchild – This covering…3 inches of padding…hardwood, and springs. Free Fall – That’s it? No wonder ya’ll keep gettin fucked up! Caitlin Fairchild – After awhile of hitting this for a couple of months it’ll feel like nothing to you. Now let’s try it again… [Roxy decked out in a red female wrestling body shorts outfit with black boots gets to her feet waving her arms trying to get the sting out as she turns to her friend.] Free Fall – Listen…why I got learn to do some stupid moonsault yo? Why don’t you teach me how to do the Burning Spike, or the Death Valley Driver a bitch into the mat? That’s some hardcore shit. Caitlin Fairchild – Really…and who do you think you’re gonna LIFT in order to pull any of those moves off? You’re weight only 100 pounds…when I first stepped into the UWA I weighted 220 pounds, and could barely muscle half the guys there…I had to pack on and extra 25 pounds of muscle to be able to even go toe to toe with the 300 pounds and up. No…you’re not going to learn any power moves…we have to work on strengths and defenses you can use, and High Flying Maneuvers are the ticket. Free Fall – (Folds her arms pouting.) This sucks yo! How the hell is I suppose to be World Champion material, and get respect flying around like some Masked Mexican? [Caitlin narrows her eyes walking up to Free Fall with her arms folded.] Caitlin Fairchild – Shut up. Free Fall – What? Caitlin Fairchild – You heard me…I don’t want to EVER hear that talk come from your lips again…there’s a lot of history and respect that goes with Lucho Libre wrestling, La Parka, Rey Mysterio Jr., Chavo Guerrero, Billy Kidman, and the list goes on and on, not to mention Eddie Guerrero WAS a World Champion…now you wanted to get into this side of the business…then you’re gonna pay your dues like everyone else who had the guts to step into this ring did…do you hear me? (Grabs her arm.) DO YOU HEAR ME? Free Fall – Aight yo! Aight! Chill! I’m goin…I’m goin… [Caitlin releases her, and watches sternly as Roxanna muttering under her breath took to the turnbuckle again. She didn’t mean to act like a task master, but she had been the few women to walk out of the Dungeon of the late Stu Hart, and even though she hated every minute of it, she learned all too well to love and respect this business as well as what they did in the ring, not to mention she had to prepare Roxy to face rookies just like herself, who would be just as hungry, maybe even more looking to climb that ladder to glory, and was willing to throw anyone else off that ladder who got in their path, and Caitlin did not want her to be one of those casualties. Roxy climbed awkwardly to the top turnbuckle trying to hold her balance again, she then extended her arms slowly, took a deep breath, and sprung off into a more graceful moonsault landing hard half way in the center of the ring, but she still gets to her knees clutching her left arm and gut with the same reaction.] Free Fall – OW! FUCK YO! That shit hurt! Caitlin Fairchild – A little better, but you can’t hesitate on a move like that…you got to get up there as quickly as possible, and come off even quicker if you plan to nail your target, and the pain will become less in time…it should also be an incentive for you to make sure you hit your target. [Caitlin heard the doorway to the arena open, and the sound of foots steps; she quickly walked over to her step watch she had hanging on one of the ring posts, and checked the time. A smile grew on her face as she turned to Roxy.] Caitlin Fairchild – Great…they’re here… Free Fall – Who’s here…and why you look like you had a dude eat your… Caitlin Fairchild – Knock it off…I just pulled a HUGE favor for you…I got one of the top wrestlers on the scene today to come by and give you some pointers… Free Fall – (Smiles.) You got Randy Orton??? Caitlin Fairchild (Perplexed look.) No… Free Fall – Shelton Benjamin! Caitlin Fairchild – Uh…no… Free Fall – OH SHIT!! You got my boy John Cena… Caitlin Fairchild – No. [Roxy contorts her face, looking Caitlin up and down with an irritated look.] Free Fall – Shit…then what OTHER top class wrestlers are there? Woman’s voice – Well…I could name a few…me for one. [Free Fall turned around with a “Who’s the fucks this bitch ?” look, which quickly changed to a “Holy Shit!” look as her eyes widened, for in front of her was a well-toned dark-tanned Japanese woman wearing dark shades in a white wifebeater tied in the front to show her washboard abs, with some tight fitting ripped jeans, and biker boots. Over her shoulders was a championship belt with read “ DIVAS UNLEASHED CHAMPION”, the nameplate underneath it read…”YUKU SHIRO”. And that’s who was staring back at her…all 6 foot 150 pounds of her. At the age of 21 with over 14 championships to her credit including the one she held over her shoulder, and majority were championships originally held by just men. Yuku was one of the few women who pushed the envelope of what women wrestles could and could not do in the ring…proving without a shadow of a doubt that they could become more than just eye candy…they could be serious competition. [IMG]http://img.photobucket.com/albums/v419/Yuku1/YukuShiroMini.jpg[/IMG] Roxy for the first time was both silent and nervous as Yuku hopped on the apron and entered the ring walking up to the duo. The commercials were all over the place of Divas Unleashed hosting the DU PPV “Dead to the World” in Ontario, Canada on Sunday November 28th, where she would defend the championship over her shoulder against her what many would consider her most deadliest arch-rival Star…so for a champion of Yuku’s caliber to take the time to step into the ring to see Free Fall, was a lot. Free Fall swallowed hard as Yuku walked up to the two of them, and slowly removed her shades showing an ice cold demeanor underneath as she hung her shades on her shirt…it quickly changed into a smile as she extended her hand to shake Caitlin’s, who quickly reciprocated.] Yuku Shiro – How’s it going girl? I see your still going strong…2-time World Tag Champ…South Wind Champ…and you got a tournament match this week? Caitlin Fairchild – For the West Wind title…looking to add it to my resume…I’m looking to make a big impact in 4WW, as I did in the VSE. Yuku Shiro – Well we miss you in the DU. Caitlin Fairchild – I miss it too, but you know what it’s like…when you feel like you’re not getting the push you want…gotta go where you’ll know you’ll be a star, anyway I got fam in the 4WW…that’s my home. Yuku Shiro – I hear you, and glad to see you’re doing well (turns to Roxy) so…you wanna be a wrestler? Free Fall – Well…uh…you know… Yuku Shiro – Rule number 1…confidence it key to this business, so don’t stutter. Number 2…you better learn the WWE is NOT where all the talent in the wrestling business is…you feel me? Free Fall – Uh…yes ma’am… Yuku Shiro – Ma’am? Jeez…I know I have a kid, but do I look like 50 to you? Free Fall – Uh…no ma’am…I mean…n…no Yuku Shiro (Turns to Caitlin) – You better head out champ, I’ll take it from here. Caitlin Fairchild – Thanks Yuku, I really appreciate this! Free Fall – Hold up! Where you goin??? Caitlin Fairchild – I have to get ready for my West Wind tournament match Roxy…this is huge! I asked Yuku to come by and school you some. Free Fall – Wa…wait…you’re leaving me…with her? Caitlin Fairchild – (Exits the ring.) Sure, you’ll be fine! I gotta run, I’ll be back in an hour or so! Free Fall – Caitlin! [Roxy gulfs hard as she turns to meet Yuku’s smile turned into a stern look again, she walks up to Roxy standing toe to toe with her. Free Fall leans back a bit wanting to back up as she feels off balance, but fears pissing Shiro off if she does.] Yuku Shiro – I don’t have to introduce myself…do I? Free Fall – Uh…no…you don’t…. Yuku Shiro – Good, now as you already know…I come from a large family…all men…all wrestlers…all of them have held a championship of some caliber in their career, and the most valuable lesson I learned in my career is that…when the end of the comes…I am still a woman. [Roxy’s eyebrows knit with confusion from Yuku’s remarks.] Yuku Shiro – That’s right…I am still a woman, and being a wrestler doesn’t change the fact that I am still a woman…if fact just because your partner Caitlin is seven foot and over 200 pounds, and can out muscle most men does not change the fact…that at the end of the day…she is still a woman. [Yuku backs up a bit giving Free Fall some room to breath.] Yuku Shiro – Now that we have that understanding, the question is…armed with that knowledge…how was I able to meet with the success I met over the years? By adapting to the game, playing by the men’s rules…and then playing a dirtier game then they play, and to it AS a woman…that is the key to success for us females in this sport. Now…let’s face facts…you’re 100 pounds soaking wet…quite possibly the smallest female wrestler I’ve seen willing to step into the UWA, which I was also originally a part of at some point, which means you’re going to have to play a dirty game…so dirty it’ll make Rick Flair looks like an old choir boy. No moves must be wasted…every one of them must be effective to do one thing…enough damage for you to get that three count or submission. You take it one match at a time, that’s how we climb the ladder in this business, and it’s very easy to fall off, and it’s jagged rocks at the bottom girlie. [Free Fall swallowed hard again, as Yuku looked her up and down, before walking away tossing her championship into a corner, and leans against the ropes with her arms folded looking back at her.] Yuku Shiro – Now… I want you to show me a moonsault… Free Fall – Again? Yuku Shiro –Again, because the last two I saw sucked, and if you come up cursing, you will feel my boot in the back of your head…this is a family business we are in, so you’re going to learn to curve that potty mouth out of respect for our younger fans…you feelin me? Free Fall – Uh…yes…ma’…I mean…yes. Yuku Shiro – Well what the hell are you standing there for? Get up there and show me what you can do. [Free Fall sighed softly as she walked back over to the turnbuckle climbing it realizing that maybe she was biting off more than she could chew when she took this venture, but she also knew that once on this road there was no backing out. So be it, she would climb that ladder, and feel the splinters like everyone else…just for a chance to feel what it’s like to be at the top, and to have Caitlin as proud of her, as she was of Caitlin. The 4WW logo blew across the screen as Roxanna perched on the top rope set up, and dove off for a third moonsault, the shot froze of her in mid-flight, which would prove to be quite symbolic…for where she would land…no one but she would know.]
