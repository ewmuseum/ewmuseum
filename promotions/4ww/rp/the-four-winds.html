The Four Winds... Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #726 Date: 6-2-04 Location: Seattle, WA (ooc: sorry but its way short) “The Fates are with me as it seems… The south wind… It will breathe new life into an old world.” {The scene slowly fades into a dark and stormy night. The scene pans in and we find ourselves looking out from a high vantage point. This point is actually the top of the Space Needle in Seattle, WA. On this dark and stormy night Seattle seems even more depressing than usual. The dim lights and empty streets add to the feeling of loneliness on this forsaken spot. As the scene shifts to the right we find a man sitting by himself in the rain. This man is one who exists of the edge of reality for he no longer knows his true purpose in life. He plainly stares off into space his eyes show nothing but signs of emptiness. His only smile seems to come when he leans over the edge almost if he’s going to fall. This man is the newest addition to 4WW, Kris Kanton.} Kanton: The South Wind… glory will soon be mine. {Kanton pauses for a moment as lighting streaks across the sky in the distance.} Kanton: It’s beautiful isn’t it? The way the lighting dances across the clouded night sky… {He pauses a moment to reflect and then picks something up in his right hand.} Kanton: Like gravity all things must fall. {He then tosses the object which is a rock. It floats in the air and then crashes down into the dark streets.} Kanton: And soon… The South Wind Champion will fall… You see, the wind does not always blow. There are times where the wind is still. Like the wind, your time to be still has come. Like the wind, your time to flow has ended. Now it is time for a new wind to rise… {Kanton then stands up as he teeters on the edge of the space needle. He then bends over and looks down into the darkness.} Kanton: You see… Each wind carries with itself a unique spirit. The South Wind has a very special spirit. {Kanton raises out his arms to his side as the rain continues to pour down.} Kanton: The South Wind is like the rain. It is so… refreshing. You see the South Wind represents all that is new and fresh. With the South Wind comes new blood, new sweat, new tears… and renewed life which courses the soul of the Four Winds. {He then stops an quickly lowers his arms. After standing there for a moment he raises his arm in the air. His arm then sways towards the north along with his body.} Kanton: Do you feel that? It is the South Wind. It grows old and weary. It is no longer fresh or strong. It grows old and weary. Soon… It will stop. {Just as he finishes speaking Kanton’s hand stops swaying.} Kanton: All things must come to and end… Then there is a rebirth. When the South Wind is reborn it will take shape in another form. With this the wind changes form again. {Kanton then crouches down and retakes his seat on the edge of the roof.} Kanton: This new form must be powerful. This new form must be different. This cannot be a renewed form. It cannot be one who has existed only to become the wind once again. For this wind to have true strength… It must come from within a new soul… a reborn soul… {Kanton then lies down on the cool wet metal. Rain beats down on his scarred face.} Kanton: I have a soul like the one needed. I have been reborn into this world with a fresh spirit. I have the strength to carry the spirit of the South Wind. {Kanton pauses again and sits up. He pauses and then stands up once again except for that he has his back to the edge now.} Kanton: You see… I am the Alpha and Omega. I am the East, West, North… and South wind. I am the true embodiment of what the Four Winds are. Come StormFront I will be the number one contender. Then… I soon will be the South Wind Champion. It is only a mater of time… {A wicked grin appears on Kanton’s face.} Kanton: Soon you will see… Soon all of you will see… the true face of fear. Survive… If you can! {Kanton then begins to laugh to himself. Just then lightning rips across the sky. Kanton stops and then looks at the camera one last time. He waves and falls backwards as the scene slowly… fades… out…}
