INTRO FOR FNORD!

20 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INTRO FOR FNORD! Fnord 4WW Exposures #146 Date: 9/20/03 Location: Fnord's house... (We open up in a dark room. We can’t see much in the room, because it’s dark silly! But there is a dim lamp sitting on an old wooden table in the corner of the room. Next to the table is an old beat-up recliner. We aren’t staring at this table, lamp, and recliner combo for nothing. Sitting in the recliner is a very serious looking person. He is wearing a suit, looking very “Blues Brother” like. He even has those dark sunglasses on! All of a sudden, “Ode to Joy” starts to beep out of nowhere. By beep, I am talking about like a cell phone. AND THAT’S WHAT IT IS! The Blues Brother wannabe takes the cell phone out of his coat pocket and answers it.) Wannabe: Yo. (There is a pause like someone else is talking. You didn’t think that he was talking to himself already did you?) Wannabe: 4WW? No, I never heard of them…. Dude, West, you bastard, if you say you need help, I’ll be there. (The man stands up and rips off his suit like one of those sports pants only connected by snaps. He rips it all off in a quick snap and throws it to the floor. He takes the sunglasses off too. When doing this, he knocked over the lamp, which is now shining a spotlight onto him. He is wearing a cut-off pair of blue jean shorts and a t-shirt that says “DEEZ NUTZ”. His hair is in what some people call a military flat top. He smiles really big as he hangs up the phone.) Wannabe: Dude, Fnord IS BACK! And I am so gonna rule! That bastard West is gonna be my partner. I don’t like that Canadian piece of crap, but I gotta respect him. Fnord and West, who ever thought! (Fnord looks at the lamp and screams like a little girl. He stomps on the light bulb and you can hear him running around the room like a crazy man! And oh he is crazy! Then you hear something smack into a wall.) Fnord: Ouch. I love pain. I love to feel something. Boy am I gonna hurt people. So, someone gets a miscarriage. Do I care? HELL NO! Ha ha, that woman probably deserved it. I give West a pat on the back for that one. It probably wasn’t that other idiots kid anyway. It’s probably the MAILMAN’S! HAHAHA! (I would say that we fade to black then, but we are already in darkness. So, we just fade out!)
