Fakeness Comes to the Four Winds Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #109 Date: 09/06/03 Location: Four Winds Arena Fake - noun - one that is not what it purports to be: as a : a worthless imitation passed off as genuine b : IMPOSTOR, CHARLATAN A simple word. One with many meanings, but Destiny liked this one. Describes both her opponents. Both Fake! Annie is a fake for the simple reason that she thinks she can have back what she thought was hers. Now come on Annie, who do you think yoru fooling? IF Sean still was interested in you, then can you possibly explain to the entire world why he chased Ms Anderson all over the city of Melbourne? Why he spent countless hours dialing her home phone number? Why did Sean risk getting her fired from her job as a bartender? The answers to these questions is simple. Because HE LOVES HER!! He has stated time and time again that he does love her. That he wants a family with her. You, Annie, are just upset that he found out that you are nothing more then a cold harded charlatan, a cheap fluzzie that doesnt know when to give up. You do know that there are therapists that will help you deal with this rejection issue. Just stand aside and let destiny keep these two together. Find yourself someone more worthy of you, I'm sure the 4 Winds is full of men that would love to be with you. But enough about you Annie, how about the person that person that must be dealt with. Destiny knows this man is fake. A man who thinks that the 'Daring of the 4WW' is lusting after him. Destiny's opinion believes that Summers is confusing the looks of disgust and pure hatered, for the looks of desire and lust. Try taking a look at your girlfriend, Eliza Murphy. She's the one that lets your name roll off her tongue, her eyes all dreamy over the mere thought of you. Destiny should know, she saw the two of you together. So she did her job. If you honestly think you have what it takes to once again take out the 'Darling of the 4 Winds', think again Summers. It took YOU and that bitch Annie to cost Patti the title. Destiny best guess is that there is a major conspiracy there. And you FAILED in your mission to make sure that Patti kept the title. Destiny's best guess is that your desire for the title is shadowed by your desire for Patti, or is it shadowed by your desire for the man who loves her, or is it the desire for BOTH of them that keeps you going? Destiny would love to slip inside your mind one day to see which one holds your attention the most. Destiny returns to staring at the field before her. There is something about all of this that just doesnt make sense to her. maybe things should start looking towards our leader. Fey knows more then she is letting on, and hopefully her plan isnt as evil as it looks. ___________________________________________________________ Place: Four Winds Arena Time: Training and Relaxtion time ___________________________________________________________ Patti Anderson stood outside her locker room door. A video tape in her hand. One that had caused her to come to the arena earlier then normal. She normally prefered to be in the arena later in the evening when nobody was around, gave her a chance to do her workout and run throughs. When word got out that the stars where in the arena, the fans always appeared. She had some business to take care of. There was only one person within the organization that could help her out. Simon Kreed, better known as Psymon. The resident psycho child of the Four Winds. She'd be suprised if he would talk to her after what she did to him last week. The make up job, torn clothes, and the tears would of course be more then enough to get him to do what she wanted. She had a feeling that this wouldn't be the last time she would be of need of his services. As long as she found a way to use him the right way. Patti made sure the door was locked tightly. She reached up and removed her name plate from the door. No need for someone else thinking it would be cute to try to do something in her locker room. She wondered how the clean up went in Summers locker room. She giggled at that thought. She's sure she'll hear about it when Summer's decides to try his alpha male routine again. She sighed as she headed down the hallway. She didn't know why she bothered somedays. She felt the desire to go back home to her homeland and stay. The call from across the water was becoming stronger. Just too bad that bitch of a boss wouldnt give her time off to find out. We can't have the big money draw take time off. Patti made a mental note to make sure she called her father after this weeks card. She continued her stroll down to the hall and stopped in front of Psymon's door. Her first impression was to burst right in, but she decided against it. She figured she'd be polite just this once and knock on the door. She knocked a few more times on the door. Finally the door was opened. Psymon's valet and girlfriend Uriko stood in front of her. Her five foot ten inch frame covered in denim hot pants and a white Aqua Teen Hunger Force babydoll tshirt, her feet completely bare. Patti looked at Urkio. Patti: I'm looking for Psymon. Do you know where I can find him? Uriko gave Patti the once over. Her eyes taking in the woman that tricked her man into doing her dirty work. She figured it was safe to assume that this was apology related. Uriko: He's in the weight room working out. I'll take you to him. Patti stepped out of the way to let Uriko lead the way. She noticed that Uriko didn't bother to put on shoes either. Patti shrugged it off and followed behind her. The sooner she talked to Psymon on how to handle someone who is unhinged the faster she can get in her training. ___________________________________________________________ Place: Weight Room* Time: 5 minutes later ­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­___________________________________________________________ Patti walked into the weight room and spotted him working his upper body. She noticed the tattoos on his body. Her nose curled in disgust at the perverted ones that were visible. Uriko noticed the disgust on Patti's face and walked over to Psymon. She whispered in his ear. Psymon looked over at Patti, reached over, grabbed his shirt, and put it on. Psymon: What can I do for you my dear? Patti looked at Psymon and took a big sigh. She hated to ask him to assist her, but what other choice did she have. Patti: I know after last week, I'm probably the last person you want to deal with, but I need yoru help. Talesmith Productions set me over this tape of that bitch Annie. I need your help in figuring out what her malfunction is. I can't go to Sean about it, because they are still betrothed, and I can't go to either Fey or Ian about it. So you are my last hope. Psymon stared at Patti, trying to figure out if this was another of her games or a general plea for help. He noticed by Patti's body language that she seemed rather upset over the whole deal. Psymon: What are you waiting for, go put it in the VCR over there and we'll have a look at it. Patti stepped further into the room, and placed the tape into the old looking VCR. An imagine appeared on the screen. A raven haired, blue eyed woman appeared. Her five foot, nine inch frame covered up in a robe. Sitting across from her on the couch, the current 'Lettch of the Four Winds, Matt Cummings. Patti: I don't see HOW Sean would think she's all that. Patti noticed a golf club leaing up against the wall. Ian had a putters green installed for those who wanted to practice their golf swing while waiting for either a piece of equipment to come free, or wait for their charge to finish. Patti decided to use this as a time to practice her swing. It would kill time while Psymon watched the video. Psymon stared at the image on the screen. He could see the reason why Cummings had a hard on. One could tell by the way he was stting that Mistress Annie was turning him on with just a single look. Psymon: DAMN, she's hot Patti: Hotter then me???? Psymon: How can something look so good...hurt so bad? Ha Ha! Patti: Hey now...that's my quote Psymon: Now, Patricia, you know that no one is hotter than you. Patti stopped in mid swing. She unconsciously turled the golf club in her hand. Out of the corner of her eye, she watched the replay. A cruel look coming over her face. Her mind ignoring the fact that Psymon just called her Patricia. Patti: OOOO she called me Patricia.....someone is gonna get a golf club up her ass Psymon paused the tape. No sense in trying to watch it if someone is going to make comments during it. Psymon: I don't see why you flip a bitch when I or anyone else calls you Patricia. Patricia is such a pretty name for a pretty girl such as yourself. Patti: My name is PATTI...NOT Patricia....I left Patricia back in Austrailia. Psymon: Don't you mean you left your mother back in Aussieland? She always called you 'Patricia' and you've always hated that. Patti began to look get an annoyed look upon her face. She was wondering what Psymon was leading too. Psymon: You despised her, didn't you? You wanted the worst to happen to her. Patti: My mother?? No, I loved my mother...no matter how mean she was to me. Psymon: Psh! I thought I loved mine, too, until she turned on me. Patti: My mother didn't hold the grief of the loss of my sister very well. Psymon: You and I are a lot alike, Patti...Search yourself. You using me to find the beast in me is delaying your discovery of yours. I never asked to become this way again, but you had to use me for your own personal gain. You need to be using yourself, not others. Patti: What the bloody hell you talking about Psymon? Psymon: It'll make sense if you open your eyes. Patti: My own eyes are already open. It's not my fault you forgot the actress in me. Psymon: You're missing the point, like you usually do. Think about it...think about it real hard. It'll come to you. Patti: And just what bloody point are you trying to make Psymon??? You honestly think there is a connection between us??? I think your bloody nuts. Psymon: Been there, done that, and you're making it worse. All I have to say is don't prolong your beast. Mine has already awakened and it'll be on your head if I can't be stopped. Patti: MY head??? What the bloody hell??? Psymon walks away. Psymon: Think about it... Patti stares back at Psymon. Whatever he was driving at, he wasn't being all that clear. Patti: Okay someone needs a trip the funny farm Sighing, Psymon comes back. He knows that Patti doesn't get it. Psymon: Okay, Patti, let's put it this way. Patti: Oh this ought to be good Psymon: Live and see of how destructive you can be. You've taken without giving back 'til the damage can be seen. Patti: Destructive??? .... PAHLEASE...I am the decent of the mighty Greek Gods...i know the damange..... Psymon: It'll all make sense. Oh, and don't go to sleep, he'll kill you in your dreams. Patti: Umm Psymon, love, do I need to call the boys with the pretty white coats? Psymon: They won't take me back. Patti: Maybe not the ones here in the states...but the ones in me homeland may. Psymon: They can't take an illegal alien. Gotcha there, sweetheart. Oh, if you want a complete win over Annie, I'll teach you a move that almost ended my career. Patti: Psymon, I think you've bloody been hit in the head too many times. Psymon: Sticks and stones may break me bones, but whips and chains excite me! Patti shakes her head at Psymon. Patti: You're about as looney as me own mother. Psymon: Maybe we can exchange mums, Patricia. Patti: Quit calling me Patricia! Psymon: You know you love it! Accept it. Patti: I hate being called Patricia...it stayed in Australla with me mother and that is where it will remain. Psymon: Wherever I may ROAM! HA HA HA! Patti: alright Simon.....either play nice or leave. Suddenly the voice inside Psymon's head spoke Voice: What should we do, psymon? Psymon: I don't know...what should we do? Let's see if she is a Goddess...If she is, she's immortal. Patti waits with impatientence Patti: And what do you mean by that crack? Psymon examines Patti then scoffs. Psymon: Goddess, my ass. Descents and full-breeds are worlds apart. Patti stares out the window, subconsciously the clouds coming to her command. Lightening strikes the tree outside the Four Winds arena. Psymon goes back to watching the tape. Figures he'd go back to it now since she was distracted. Patti's peripheral vison alloud her to see what was going on the video tape. She saw Annine going on and on about how Patti stole her betrothed and how Sean was just using her until he was tired of her. Patti: Appeared to be courting him??? Excuse me...HE came 3000 miles to find me. What the FUCK?......she's mental! Patti continues to watch the video, getting more upset by the minute. Patti: Who the bloody hell does she thinks she is? She's no fucking blood, she's a fucking banshee. Tainted Aussie Greek Blood? WHAT THE BLOODY HELL!!! Patti takes a subconscious swing at Psymon. Psymon does a matrix type move to avoid being hit by Patti's swing. A look of shock and outrage appears on his face. Psymon: What the hell, Patti? Patti: Who the bloody hell does she thinks she is! Psymon: And taking my head off would make it better? And you say I'm crazy. Patti: I am not bloody crazy Psymon. I am NOT my mother...and I am NO bloody relation to you....no matter what you think you know. Psymon: Well, Patti...Has anyone ever told you you have my mother's tea-colored hazel eyes? They're identical. She looks like me, but you have her eyes...And her hair color...And her lips. Patti: I think you are just wishing I was your sister Patti stared at Psymon. She knew he was insane, but there was NO way in the world he would even dream that she was related to him. Psymon: AND...her nose. I'm not lying to ya. Patti: Are you looking in a mirror Psymon? Psymon: No, I look like her, with the same facial structure. But you, on the other hand, have everything else. Patti: No I look like my relations...I look like Psyche did all those years ago! Psymon: Sure, ya do... Patti: My father even says so! Psymon: But the nose, lips, hair and eyes don't lie. He told you what you wanted to hear. Patti stared in disbelief at the man standing in front of the television. Did he just call her father a liar? Patti: YOU are just making this stuff up....You are trying to curb those impure thoughts that all you bloody men have about me. Psymon: You may think that...but I see it. Patti: YOU see nothing! Psymon: Besides, I respect you. Fucking you isn't on my agenda...I have Uriko for that. Patti: Then why make all these bloody observations if you dont want me? Psymon: Why not? You remind me of my mother. If you didn't have that accent, you would sound just like her. She's a low alto/high tenor...just like you. Patti: I think its just your bloody imagination...I am the daughter of Minos and Minerva Anderson Psymon: Maybe it is, but it's just what I see. There's no harm in seeing the invevitable. Patti: I have lived in Oceania and Greece all my life... Psymon: Doesn't mean you have mixed heritage. I see an American side of you. I feel it coming off you like heat. Patti: Oh whatever Psymon....I do have mixed heritage...I am Greek and Aussie. and the heat...its coming from your desire for me. Psymon: You are a desireable woman, my dear, Patricia, but I have my woman. Sean McClancy has you...at least...Annie can prove he belongs to her. Patti takes another golf style swing at Psymon's head... Patti: Bloody mention her name again, and I use your groin for target practice.I And you will learn, my dear psycho, McClancy is MINE! Psymon ducks out of the way to avoid the swing from Patti. He saw the same look upon her face that has been seen by friends and foes of Psymon. Psymon: Damn, woman, what is your problem? Patti drops the golf club. A few moments worth of confusion settle in on her pretty face. Patti: I don't know what you're talking about, Psymon. Psymon: You swung that golf club at my head. Why did you do that? Patti: I swung this golf club at your head???? Patti confused look remains on her face. As to trying to make some sense as to what she had done. Patti: I dont know Psymon...Did I connect? Psymon: No. Did I do something that made you upset? Patti: Not that I know of Psymon. Why were we discussing something? Psymon: Come to think of it, I made a comment about you having my mother's eyes, nose, lips and hair. The rest I don't remember. Patti takes another unconscious swing at Psymon's head. Psymon grabs the club in his and and pulls the club out of Patti's grasp. Psymon: Use it on Summers, not me. Psymon breaks the club over his knee. Patti: I'm sorry Psymon, something came over me. I'm not violent. Truely I'm not. Normally storm clouds and thunder and lightening appear Psymon: It happens to the best of us, Patti. Look at me. Patti raises her eyes shyly at Psymon. Psymon: I meant, look at me. Look what I've done in the past. Patti: Psymon I barely even know you. You have been a good friend to me when I've needed it Psymon: True, even though you used me two weeks ago, but I've learned to forgive you for it. Don't do it again, okay? Patti: You didnt have to attack West because of the little joke I pulled on you Psymon: Well, I was going to attack him anyway. Patti: But how can you blame the Darling of the Four Winds? It was all a joke. Psymon: Don't worry about it. Think about what I said. Listen to what is on the inside of you. That will guide you on what you need to do. Now as to take care of Annie, there is ONE move that will help you, but you have to do what I tell you. And you have to trust me. Patti once again looked confused about what had just happened. And as of right now, she would do anything to keep Sean out of the clutches of the evil banshee, and if it ment making an alliance with someone then she would do it. Desperate times called for desperate measures. Patti sat down across from Psymon and prepared to learn how to take down the one threat to her happiness.
