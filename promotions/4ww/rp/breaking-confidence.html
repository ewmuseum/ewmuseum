Breaking Confidence... Future Shock 4WW Exposures #1079 Date: 11/26/04 Location: Outskirts of Columbus Con-fi-dence ( P ) Pronunciation Key (knf-dns) n. 1. Trust or faith in a person or thing. 2. A feeling of assurance, especially of self-assurance. 3. The state or quality of being certain: I have every confidence in your ability to succeed There are things in life that are certain. Paying Taxes, eventually dying, and that someone will come up from the depths of a wrestling business and screw someone over. Guess which one is happening here now? Yup, the later one. Yes, it’s true that Future Shock has to defend their titles. But only one team has earned that shot, the new team from unStable known as Zane Ozz and Luscious Lady Luck. Admittedly, both of these jokers have fallen to Future Shock before. So have the other team that has weaseled their way into this. What the bloody hell have the Infidels done to deserve another shot? Oh, yeah, they have cheated and swindled their way to try to prove that they’re the stable to contend with. Yet so far all that we’ve seen the ‘Dream Maker’ do is try to make his wet dreams come true. He’ll never amount to anything in real life, so he can keep living in his dream world. FutureShock, Blaise McClancy and Ukyo Kushinaga would neither be worried about the status of the East Wind Titles, nor would they be worried about anything else. Except that not only did the Infidels finally find their way around the backstage to get a hold of the open contract that FutureShock keeps for anyone who finds themselves delusional enough to think they have a chance, but the DMC has succeeded in changing the rules of the game. Most people know that Blaise McClancy utterly hates the ‘gimmick matches’ that those of lesser talents rely upon. Most also know that he holds no secrets from people. The one gimmick match that he hates the most if the ‘Ladder Match’. A match where you don’t even have to wrestle your opponent(s). All you need to do is somehow use a ladder, metal ones being the most common used, and then eventually when the crowd has been bored to tears finally, climb the ladder and claim the belts that you didn't even have to wrestle for. This is not a match of skills. It's not a match of anything but if you can somehow use a ladder as a weapon, of which is falls under the most basic of weapons, a club. And can you climb a ladder. There is no pinfall. No submission. Nothing of value. But most also know one small non-secret about one Blaise McClancy.... He's also never won a ladder match in his career timespan. Never is a harsh word, but truth is truth. From his first ladder match for the SCWF Television Title where he lost it by someone elses sheer dumb luck... He had his oppoent on the ladder, then grabbed him for a head and arm suplex from the top to the ring. But as he took that man over, his leg hooked the title. When they hit the mat, the man who had almost had his neck broken on impact... was the champion. Since that day, Blaise has just never really concerned himself whenever someone books a ladder match. They're stupid. They don't ever allow the real wrestler to win. And that's his stance. He'd just blow this one off, and face reality. He's 0-4 in ladder matches now. But one thing has him concered with if that were to be the case. If he were to just disregard the match and let fate take it's toll... Ukyo, his lover and partner, is also defending these titles with him. He can't let her down. But then again, his own personal jinx when it comes to these types of matches could let her down. A big disappointment could be on the horizon. There are few things that can bring Blaise to question the future outcome of a match. The uncertainty of what some rougue staffer who has no honor system, and is out to either win the rights to some chicks bedsheets or to win the influence of some wannabe Charles Manson or Anton Levey. The signs of a desperate few men who have learned their place on the wrestling food chain. Their the food, unless they can pull something out of their ass and try to play as the big fish. We have to concede, they're in control for this match. They found a chink in the armor of FutureShock. They know this, and they'll follow the suit they have given. They'll get cocky, overconfident. The basic things here though, only one person can grab those title belts, only one person can win this for their team. And that one team leaves as the East Wind Champions. Will unStable win, solidifying their path to greatness? Triple L has held the Tempest Title for almost as long as FutureShock has held the East Wind Titles already. Zane Ozz, the future is the one half of the now former team of him and his brother that showed promise of some sort. Or will The DMC's Infidels win, making their promise of fed wide domination that much closer to fruitation? Or will FutureShock drop the big one in their laps, and not only break Blaise's streak in this type of 'match', and take the path of going on to be legends in the 4WW in hodling those East Wind tiltes longer then anyone has, and probably will? But the other two teams would try to sell the defending champions short. They wouldn't give someone else credit if they were paid to. No matter what DMC or unStable says, they'd discredit their grandmother if you paid them enough. 4WW knows which team is their cash cow. PTC knows which team is the superior force. You're a champion in the ring, but there are some like Triple L who sit there and think their gods gift to this world in femininity. I'll tell you this right now, I think my goal would be to get you onto the cover, instead of me. As long as I'm still centerfold, I don't care about covers.” Ukyo laughed. “Me, on the cover? I don't think so.” “Thousand bucks on it?” Ukyo suddenly slapped out of her laughter. “What did you say?” “Two thousand... dollars... bet... gambling... Doing a 'hazard'...” Ukyo smirked at her. “You'd have to go there.” “Oooh, I think I got a gambler here. One grand, I get you into the shoot with the photographers completely done in by you. Two grand I get you onto the cover instead of one of the obvious ones.” Ukyo looks to be pondering the deal. “Ah, she's goin' t' back ou'. She's na one t' take a risk. I mean wha' she ga' t' loose. She does it, an' don'na get picked, she gets some 'ard 'earned money. She does get it, she sticks an ice pick int' th' ones 'opin' t' get tha' top billin' in th' shoot.” “I don't...” “I'll toss in a shot at the PTC Tag Title contendership for it.” With that, Ukyo really stares at her. “What?” “Right now, you take the bet, I sign the papers that puts your team, YOU specifically into the PTC Tag Team contenders match. It's a team match. Two teams from 4WW are going. I want FutureShock, but I can put someone else there. But it's one rep from each team going into the ring to try to get a shot at the PTC Tag Team Champions. Say yes, and that's yours right now.” “Like I said, wha' 'ave ye ga' t' loose? Ye know Blaise would tell ye t' take a risk on this.” “You don't play fair.” “Never claimed t' be one t' play fair all th' time. When it's t' me advantage, ye'll find tha' we can be quite th' takers o' opportunity.” Ukyo takes another drink of the strong brew of coffee. She looks to be pondering things for a good minute or so. Patti and Sean just stay quiet as she does. Patti leans again onto the table, staring at Ukyo as she's deciding.... Finaly.... “Alright, I'll do it.” “YES! You wont' be disappointed. Now, while you guys are getting your practice in tomorrow, I'll be calling around to get everything ready... you won't be disappointed.” “I sure as hell hope not. Now, what about this dinner, you have a menu?” “Yeah, right here.” Patti hands Ukyo a folding paper menu for the local Chinese place as we pan up to the upper floor hallway, one side an open railing looking down into the dining area... As Blaise is looking down at them... smiling. A small jewelery box in his hands... ~The End... for now~
