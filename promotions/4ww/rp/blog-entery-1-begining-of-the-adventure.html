Blog Entery 1 - Begining of the Adventure TJ Anderson 4WW Exposures #1198 Date: March 10/05 Location: Seattle, Washington I guess this is where my journey begins, I’m sitting here on the airplane from Sydney to Seattle… glad I could get a direct flight, I hate having to wait in airports… I hate airports in general but I guess it comes with the business. I’m lucky, I guess, being born into a family of wrestlers, I get a chance at 18, not even out of high school yet, to travel around the world and do what I love, with some of my family. Speaking of airports chances are I’m not even gonna get enough time to unpack when I get to Patti and Sean’s home. Well it’s my home now… I guess, I’ll have pretty much just sleep then have to get back on a plane and head to Athens, us Anderson’s have our history in Greece. I haven’t seen them since they’ve been married, it should be interesting, I’d rather stay with a brand new marriage then live in a house with a marriage that’s falling apart… I love Australia but I’m really glad to leave, all the shit that’s been happening, my parents fighting, but this is a happy day in the life of TJ. In only a few days I will become a superstar, a legend, and hopefully a champion. I’m taking on 5 other men for a shot at the Tempest title later on in the night. I’m not really sure who any of these other guys are, only a few of them have had matches so I’m going in blind, but luckily I’ve been trained well enough to adapt a strategy in the middle of the match. Plus none of them know the power and the impact of the Ku Long move series, or the “Show Stealing Spot”, so I have a nice advantage going in… at least I think I do, lol. So this is TJ Anderson signing off. Adios “Excuse me sir…” “Huh?!?!” “I’m sorry to wake you but we’ve arrived. “Oh thanks…” TJ wipes his face and stands up. He adjusts his baggy jeans and his black t-shirt as he grabs his backpack from under the seat in front of him. He makes his way out of his isle and out of the plane, as he leaves the pilot and flight attendants thank him for flying and wish him well, he brushes them off and walks up the ramp into the terminal. He yawns again as he adjusts his backpack and looks both ways, searching for a sign. He turns to his left and follows the arrows directing him to the baggage claim. He walks quickly towards the escalator and notices a women a few steps in front of him. ‘She’s kinda hot’ he thinks to himself. ‘Should I pick her up?’ he questions and imagines what would happen if he did. ‘Nah, better not, the driver Patti sent is waiting for me outside.’ He heads off the escalator and walks past the woman quickly giving her a glance and a smile. He walks towards the conveyer belt and waits for a minute, looking around at his new city, well the airport of his new city. He quickly grabs a large green duffle bag and a wheeled black suitcase. He begins walking towards the door, adjusting his load by placing the duffle bag on his right shoulder and wheeling the suitcase with his left. As he walks towards the door he notices a man with a sign that says ‘Anderson’ on it. “Anderson, would be me” TJ comments “May I help you with your bags, sir?” asks the man “Sure” TJ tosses him the duffle bag The man struggles to catch it but manages. “Right this way sir” replies the man walking towards the exit. “The name’s TJ” says the young Anderson “you?” “James, sir” He states awkwardly “Well James, this is an adventure… and it’s gonna be fuckin’ awesome!”
