The truth be told Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #495 Date: 02/03/04 Location: Las Vegas Memorial Hospital Idiot - noun Etymology: Middle English, from Anglo-French ydiote, from Latin idiota ignorant person, from Greek idiOtEs one in a private station, layman, ignorant person, from idios one's own, private; akin to Latin suus one's own -- more at SUICIDE 1 usually offensive : a person affected with idiocy 2 : a foolish or stupid person - idiot adjective Who’s bloody brilliant idea was it to pit the ‘Darling of the 4WW’ in a match with the ‘Asian Sensation’? The bookers must have lost their minds over that one. Patti has never had a quarrel with Jamie. Unless this is the revenge for Jamie over Patti costing him the world title from good old Toddy boy. He’s probably still stinging from that little loss, oh well, like Patti has plans on giving him an edge in the match. It just won’t happen. Better yet, what idiot would put Chris West in a match with Todd Dream anyway. Maybe Jamie should be happy he’s facing someone that will give him a real competition then anyone else. At least maybe Jamie will get a good sturdy workout over it. Speaking of Toddy Boy, did you manage to catch that ridiculous request for an apology and a step down from the number one contender ship? Has Toddy gone from being the ‘Savior’ to the next idiot of the federation. Does he honestly think that Patti will give up her hard earned shot at the World Title because someone has developed a case of the ‘limber tail’? It would appear he has because of the simple fact that he’s asked for the resignation of his toughest opponent.. What a bloody idiot if you ask me. One simple super kick to his chin followed by a deadly Athena’s Entanglement, and he’s practically begging for Patti resign. Poor Toddy, guess he’s losing his ‘Savior’ status on that one. What about Sean Summers? The man is a born idiot. Week after week he comes out and attempts to think he’s the big man on campus, which of course is a lie anyway. With the recent injury to Eliza he’s going to be extremely difficult. His ego will definitely make him look like an ego. Especially seeing how not only will he have to worry about having Patti gunning for him, but he’ll also have Tyler Blaze going after him, too. There will be no rest until Sean Summers is crowned the reigning idiot of the 4WW, or is eliminated from the federation period. Either way, all three are idiots this week. And just like all idiots...they all will lose. Place: Las Vegas Memorial Time: A few hours before Storm Front Eliza Murphy sat in her wheelchair staring out the window. Her tear stained cheeks glistened in the afternoon sun. She dropped her cell phone into her lap and fought the tears that were threatening to fall. Her heart cried out for Tyler in a painful way, but Eliza was just not ready to admit her feelings for him. If she did, she would wind up back in the same mess that she was in now, except maybe worse. Tyler Blaze had just called her again. It was the fifth time he had contacted her to make sure she was ok. After the vicious attack by Sean Summers, Tyler was more worried about her then ever. She quickly wiped the tears away as she heard the door to her room opening. She spun the wheelchair around, her frown turning into a smile as she saw who had entered the room. Her best friend, Patti Anderson stood in the doorway, a bouquet of wild flowers in her hands. Patti: Hey love! How are you feeling? Eliza smiled as she wheeled the chair over to the doorway. Her neck still in the neck brace. Her hair hanging all around her heart shaped face. Patti noticed she had been crying over something. Patti attempted to hide her dismay at her friend’s constant worry about Tyler and Sean. With all of this drama, Patti was sure that her Sean was going to come back from Ireland to find her a basket case. Eliza: I’m great now. Doc said I only have to wear this brace for a few more weeks, but as for the wheel chair im in it for a month or better. When I fell, I came extremely close to fracturing my spine, but luckily for me I had the common sense to keep from landing wrong. Patti clicked her tongue at the latest news. She clenched her fist behind her back. Sean Summers would pay for the attack on Eliza. She just prayed that Tyler didn’t get their first. This was Patti’s fight, had been from the start, and Tyler was just doing what he does best, putting his nose in right where it doesn’t belong. Eliza: Are those flowers for me? Patti smiled at her friend. She would be in for a huge surprise when she found out who they came from. Patti handed them over with a smile on her face. Eliza reached up with shaking hands. Eliza inhaled the strong aroma of the daisies, thistle flowers, dandy lions and other wild flowers. Eliza: They are beautiful!! Where did you find spring flowers at this time of year? Patti: Sweetie, they aren’t from me and they aren’t from Summers either. There is a card. Why don’t you have a look. Eliza reached inside and pulled out the card. A small beautiful heart shaped card greeted her. Eliza opened the card. Patti watched the emotions of joy and sorrow dance across Eliza’s face. Patti knew what the card had said, for she had carefully helped Tyler say what he had always said to her. Eliza was his world and he would make sure nothing like this would happen again. Eliza: They are from Tyler! How did he know? Patti smiled at Eliza. Patti: I told him. He has been worried sick about you. He’s been driving me insane, with the constant phone calls about your condition. So I told him that if he was bloody worried about you, he should just send you some flowers. After I told him what your favorite were, he went on the hunt of them Patti stopped for a second as she noticed that what she had suspected. Eliza was truly in love and it wasn’t like when she had been dating Summers either. Maybe Jamie would have been a better choice for Eliza then Tyler, but Aphrodite and Cupid worked in mysterious ways. Patti shook her head at the thought of once again dealing with Mr. Jamie in the ring. Even though she hadn’t had a problem in the past with him, she wondered what she did to deserve this. Eliza blushed as she looked at her best friend so lost in thought. She knew that Patti’s mind was on her match this week, and that’s where it needed to be. Eliza: Don’t you think you need to be going soon? You need to get warmed up for your match and stuff. Just promise me if you see Tyler you’ll tell him to visit me as soon as he’s done with his match. I kinda want to see him. Patti nodded as her mind continued to plan her match strategy against Jamie. Patti: What are you going to tell him? Eliza smiled her dazzling smile at Patti. Eliza: Why sugah, I’m going to tell him ...... Fade out.
