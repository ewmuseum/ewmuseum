Wedding: Dual Obsession

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wedding: Dual Obsession 4WW Exposures #1048 Date: 11/11/04 Location: Ohio Lawyers Office (The scene opens up in an office setting. The office is very lavish, leather furniture, and a big oak desk. The walls are a very light blue coloring, and there are fake plants all over the place. Sitting at the desk, involved in some sort of office work or work of some kind of another is a good looking red-head wearing a grey business like suit. She has a computer next to here and several pictures on the book shelf behind her. As she buries herself back into her business the woman known as Luscious Lady Luck walks into the office.) (The woman at the desk looks up at Lady Luck, trying to give her the undue attention that she deserves. LLL is wearing a flower print sundress and is carrying a bag with her. The woman at the desk looks at the computer screen quickly, and then back at Luscious Lady Luck, smiling.) Woman: May I help you? LLL: Yeah, I have an appointment with Vincent Vegas. Woman: Ok, name? LLL: Name is Diana King. Woman: OK, please have a seat. (Luscious Lady Luck sits on one of the lavish couches in the office. She admires all of the different plants and furnishings of the office. As she is admiring the furnishings, the woman at the desk gets on the phone and informs the person on the other line that their appointment is here. Luscious Lady Luck looks at some of the different pictures and personalization of the office. She notices something in particular however which catches her eye.) (Exactly like the one she had. The face, the eyes, the position, the dress. It was the exact same one. Of course it isn’t surprising to see something like it around. After all, they are mass marketed, and quite a few people would have them. But this is one place she didn’t expect to see one. Especially not here. But why be surprised. Unlike herself, people like Patti Anderson. And people are likely to by her teddy bears. And there one was, staring at her straight away in the face.) (Luscious Lady Luck walks over to where the teddy bear is placed and picks it up. She examines it carefully, turning it around. It was about as perfect of a likeness as there could be, and it looked so much like the one that Patti had given LLL that last night in Australia. It was one of those things that people couldn’t forget. One of those things they’ll always remember. LLL turned it from side to side, as the woman continued her work.) LLL: Say, can I ask your name hon? Woman: Yeah, I suppose. My name is Jen. LLL: You knew here working for Vincent? Jen: Yeah, I started just a few months ago. LLL: And I see you have great taste in wrestlers. Jen: Of course, Patti is so dreamy. But of course you would know that. I just wish it could’ve been me in those videos instead of you. (What an odd statement, especially from someone you had just met, but the dream is one that LLL couldn’t deny. Thousands of men and women wishes it was them in that place instead of her.) Jen: So are you still going after her? I mean with her marriage and all? LLL: I’ve got plans. Marriages don’t last anymore. They are temporary. And besides, Sean is but a man. And how could any man refuse two beautiful women. Jen: And I’m sure that Caitlin is going to love all of that too. LLL: Yeah, Caitlin. She’s a hot little number. Feisty too. Wouldn’t mind having both of them in the same bed at the same time. But that’s another matter all together. Jen: Well, good luck with that. I don’t think it’ll happen though. LLL: Hon, I’m all about luck. I’m all about skill, and I’m all about being the best I can be. How else do you think I could’ve held onto the Tempest title for this long? Ike Angel is coming back for seconds. Of course we know it’s because no one could resist seconds of this. But the Tempest Title, it’s in the bag hon. And soon, so will Patti. Jen: So who are you marrying then? LLL: That’s for me to know, and you to find out. (All of a sudden, the phone rings. Jen picks up the phone, and then hangs it up.) Jen: Mr. Vegas will see you now. LLL: Thanks. And here is a little something to put next to that Patti Anderson Teddy Bear. (Trips reaches into her bag, pulls out a second teddy bear, this one the Luscious Lady Luck teddy bear, and hands it to Jen, along with the Patti Anderson one.) LLL: They make a cute couple, don’t they? (The scene fades out to the purple heart on a black background with “LLL+PA” written in the middle.)
