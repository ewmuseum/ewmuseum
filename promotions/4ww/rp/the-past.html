The Past Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #957 Date: 09/12/04 Location: Past - ADJECTIVE: 1. No longer current; gone by; over The past actions of Patti Anderson are coming back to haunt her. Her triast with Diana King, sparked major resales of a DVD that was promised to never be published again somehow managed to get re released for the general public. Patti was forced to acknowledge that she had a relationship with the woman. Soon, though she would deal with LLL, but right now. Her past actions against Kyo and Darek DeAngelo are more her focus. They both want to prove to the 'Darling' that their challenges were not to be taken lightly. The desires of these two men have driven them to do what they feel is important to take the title that belongs to her. A title she gave her blood, her sweat, and her tears to earn. She even sold her soul out to the likes of Tarrant to keep her title. Patti had even gone as low as to use anyone and everyone she could to keep it. But the sins of the past have come forward to be answered in the form of an Iron Man Challenge. The longest sixty minutes in a competitors career. These type of matches will make or break your career. But unfortunately there is a little curve to this one. Instead of it being one...there is two men. Kyo and Darek DeAngelo. Only one of those men have fallen into the trap that Patti laid out. Only one of them, she used her charm and her sexuality to claim him. She charmed him into leaving her fiance's faction to join her. As her protector, with the promise that only he and he alone could save her. She made him believe that he could do it, and according to him, all he got was a beat down. It's not Patti's fault that you carelessly fell in love with her and then tried to move in on someone else's territory. As for Kyo...You have decided to take it upon yourself to destroy what Patti has built up. He believes that the best way to destroy Shadows Ascension is to destroy those who stand before him. True he took out Bullet and Tarrent. He took both to the limits and even managed to walk away with the victories. Unfortunately, Patti's two strong men failed her, leaving her open and unguarded. Now she bares the target of Kyo. Kyo doesn't realize that Patti fights harder when her back is against the wall. In fact, you both will find out just how vicious the 'Darling' can be and just exactly what Sean McClancy has taught her. Just be prepared for a lesson that the two of you will never forget. This belt isn't going anywhere. ---------------------------------------------- Place: A prominent attorney's office Time: does it matter? ----------------------------------------------- The scene fades into Patti Anderson. A magazine rests in her lap as the camera pans back to show her dressed to the nines in a black Versa chi gown with her trademark calf high heeled boots. In her laps sits a manila envelope, her hazel eyes cast downward as she stares at her fingers. She begins to gnaw at her bottom lip, worrying that her next set of plans won't go through. If not only what someone sent her was true, then she was going to need the love and support of Raven to get her through the life she was going to be living without her McClancy. The girls giggles and smiles always made her feel that her life could be full filed without him. Patti looked around the waiting area of the office. She hated being kept waiting, especially when she's told that she can be seen that day. The cheap decor in the office wasn't helping her mood any. The cheap artwork, the tacky wallpaper and everything about the room in general annoyed her. Patti laied the manila envelope beside her and stood up. The dress slid down the curves of her body hugging it tightly like a lover wrapped around her for comfort. Her specially made boots wove up her calf muscles gave her an extra inch to her height, giving the poor receptionist a heart attack every time Patti stood up or moved around. Patti began to slowly pacing around the office, her nerves and her temper slowly rising with each pace of the floor. Patti's boots began to tap impatiently on the tacky carpeted that separated her from the desk of his secretary. Patti walked over to the desk and slammed her fist down on it. 'Now bloody look. I've been in this office for over a bloody half hour. You either find out what's holding this damn wanker up or I'm busting into this office and firing him before he's even hired.' The young woman quickly buzzed the inner office. She repeated the information again to the attorney and paused as she she listened to his voice. She nodded her head as if she listened to the explanation. She spoke again and hung up the phone. 'I'm sorry Ms. Anderson, but he just isn't ready to see you just yet. He said he will be ready for you in a few minutes.' The girl tried to give Patti a smile, but she knew the young woman was frustrated and she was beginning to fear that Ms. Anderson was going to lose her temper. She shook her head as her eyes watched her pace the room like a caged animal. Patti walked away from the desk and stared at the door. Her hazel eyes surveying it, almost as if she's looking for a weak spot in it. Without flinching or blinking an eye, Patti round house kicks the door, splintering the oak and leaving a huge hole in the door. Patti reaches a long, purple manicured nail in and unlatches the door from inside. Jon O'Neal looked up startled from his desk. His gray hair laied neatly combed over his balding spots, his gray eyes staring angerly at the person who pretty much just committed a felony breaking and entering. 'What the hell do you think your doing? That door cost more then what you make in months!' His gray eyes stared rudely at the young woman standing in front of the now busted up door. 'I told you I would be with you in a while, now kindly go back to the waiting room.' Patti hazel eyes danced with a mix of anger and annoyance as this pompous ass has the audacity to put her off again. Patti reached around to the side and grabbed her cell phone and prepared to dial a number. 'Well then, maybe we should call me father and have him remove his funding from this case and find a better firm to handle this job. I'm bloody certain that the senior partners will wonder why you just cost them a fortune for refusing to see a very important client.' Patti smiles slowly as she begins to dial her father's number. She notices through her have closed eyelids. The beads of perspiration began to form on his forehead as his gray eyes watched Patti slowly and deliberately punched a few buttons. Jon reaches down and grabs his handkerchief out of his pocket and dabs at his forehead. The senior partners would have his head if they knew he cost them yet again. 'NO! NO! NO! Please Ms. Anderson, the firm needs this account. I was just being slow and was more worried about the work then about taking care of who fills our paychecks. Please come in and sit down.' Jon gets up slowly from his desk and walks the few feet from where Patti stands, her trademark purple and black cell phone resting against her ear, the almost forgotten manila envelope under arm. Jon's pudgy footsteps fell loudly on the carpeted floor. His sweaty fingers grabbed hold of Patti's wrist and worked to lower the phone from her ear, while gently guiding her to the chair that faced his overly cluttered desk. Patti's hazel eyes darted over the mass confusion and wondered why her father would decide to hire this person instead of hiring someone like this. 'So, Ms. Anderson...Tell me what brings you and your father into this court battle?' 'We're after the custody of this little girl.' Patti reached into the envelope and produced a picture of a little girl with long dark hair. A smile covers her porcelain face. Her blue eyes sparkle showing the photographer just how happy she is. Jon flipped through the various pictures of Raven with Patti and her family. He couldn't help to notice how the little girl seemed to be glowing with happiness. He could tell that the girl was happy to be with this family. He noticed another set of pictures of the same little girl. 'Who are these pictures with?' He questioned as he looked over the shots of Raven with someone else. 'Those are shots of Raven and her sole living family member. He placed her in my care to protect her from those who have sworn to hurt him.' 'Does she have any other living family that could claim custody of him?' 'Just her grandfather whom I happen to work with, and he's the one that came to me to ask me to protect her.' 'Then why do you and your father wish to adopt her? She has family that is still alive.' 'When his wife and son died, Mr. DeAngelo placed Raven in an orphanage. He told the father there that he couldn't take care of her or supply her with the stability that she so deserves. So I and my father agreed to be foster parents to this little girl. We, unlike him, have been able to supply her with the love, care and support that she deserves. When I'm traveling to wrestle, she stays in the care of my father. As of late, though, Raven has been traveling with me so she can spend some time with Mr. DeAngelo. I'm taking a couple weeks leave from my job to help with the transition of her becoming my daughter and my father's granddaughter.' Jon places the pictures back into the envelope, grabs his black fountain, and legal notepad and began to jot down notes that were coming across his mind as well as what Patti was telling him. He knew that this kind of high profile case would make the firm into one of those high profile type ones. He continued to jot down everything that Patti had said. 'Now Ms. Anderson, you do know that in most cases the courts won't let you adopt a child without proof that you are married or are planning on it. What exactly your marital status?' 'Well, I'm 'engaged' to my bosses brother. He and I have been dating for a little over a year. We've been toying around with getting married. But no real date or plans have been made. My father is still married to my mother, but she is currently in a mental institution in Melbourne. After the death of my sister, she became unstable and we thought that this would help get her well. Papa just moved to the United States, where he's living in Arizona. He, as well as I, are financially stable and can provide everything that Raven would need to live.' 'Then why take this little girl away from her real family?' 'Mr. DeAngelo has proven he is unstable and has placed Raven in danger several items by having her come to ringside to deliver messages to his opponents. I know for a fact that ringside is not a safe place for an eight year old girl should be. When she travels with me, she stays in my dressing room, only once she came down to ringside with me, and I kept her out of danger.' 'But yet you accuse her grandfather of putting her in harms way. Isn't that a double standard?' Patti glared at Jon. She hated attorneys that tried to be smug. It annoyed her, even more so when she could sense what he was trying to do. He was trying to feel out both Patti and Darek DeAngelo. 'Now Ms. Anderson, how are you planning on protecting Raven this week while you are in the ring? She is traveling with you right?' 'Yes, Raven is traveling with me. She'll be with me up until my match. Then either my sister or Bullet will be taking care of her while I do my thing in the ring. If neither my sister or Bullet is available then Ian or Fey will watch her for me.' 'Aren't you afraid that Mr. DeAngelo or Mr. Kushinaga would try to do something to this little girl? How do you plan on protecting her should one of them or someone on the roster attempts to harm her.' 'You are bloody clueless. Raven is under the protection of the Order. Everyone on the roster knows that they harm her, they risk the hell of Fey and Ian Childress.' Jon readjusted himself in his chair. He leans back and stares at the redhead sitting before him. He crosses his hands across his chest and stares at her. 'Now Ms. Anderson, you know its not going to be easy to win this case to get custody of Raven from her family, but I promise you...' he cuts himself short as he's trying to take a breath. 'You will come home with Raven.' Patti sits back in her chair and smiles. 'I just need to know what name you would like Raven to take should the adoption go through.' 'Well, I'm thinking...Raven Amanda Anderson. Or Raven Amanda Anderson McClancy. Or my favorite...Raven Amanda McClancy. Which do you like?' The scene cuts away just before Jon can tell her which one he likes the best.
