Silence is deafening El Lobo de la Muerta 4WW Exposures #235 Date: 10-18-03 Location: Somwhere out there ƒºƒSee you sittin’ next to The window in the bedroom She breaks down (breaks down) Cryin’ over something And starin’ into nothing Afraid now (hey now) Wanting, needing, haunting Its killing me Thinking what has happened To live the life like that, man I break down It’s fake now Will you Will you love me tomorrow? So will you Will you stay with me today? Afraid and out of reasons To fight the way she’s feeling She breaks down (breaks down) Goin’ through the motions And holdin’ onto hopes And a dream now (somehow) Shaken (shaken), mistaken (mistaken), forsaken (forsaken) Its killing me Wishing you can change But he’s always been the same If you leave now I’ll drown Will you Will you love me tomorrow? So will you Will you stay with me today? Will you Will you be here tomorrow? So will you You’ll remember yesterday. Yesterday This time I’m sorry This time I’m sorry This time, this time I’m sorry for this time This time, this time I’m sorry This time I’m sorry. Will you Will you love me tomorrow? So will you Will you stay with me today? Will you Will you be here tomorrow? So will you You’ll remember yesterday.ƒºƒ Will You ; P.O.D. ~Breaking down from everything around; its there where we rebuild a new society. This new society is where new rules, new morals, and new days come into view. Breaking the former norm and burying it in a fire so it can never return. But unlike many things this life is like a phoenix and will rise from its ashes to create a new life. It is what we need at this crucial moment in time. We need a rebirth of a past life, and the time for change is upon us. It is now where we must infiltrate, destroy, and most importantly we must rebuild. What good is it for a man to conquer the world and make it into nothing? How sweet will the sound be when ye conquer the world and make it your own? In this war you must take NO prisoners. The more feeling you show the more weakness you show. Everyone feels this way, if given the chance they will take no prisoners. Quit trying to convince yourself that mercy is the answer. Why grant them mercy when they grant you none? Take heed to this now, show no mercy and give no mercy. When the time comes you will see that there will be no mercy. Fight not what the morrow will bring because in the end it doesn’t even matter.~ {The scene opens up once again in a small dead cornfield. The stalks, yellow and dead from the decline in temperature as of late. The night is very cold with the crisp air blowing all around bring an eerie motif to us. Down to the ground the crushed stalks carry that cold crisp fall air to parts uncharted to the naked eye. Walking around this forsaken field we stick close to the river. Walking on the rocky shore we stumble across a tree that has fallen, making a bridge over the wild water below it. Slowly stepping on the tree and making it across the fallen tree, we see a hallowed hole where something had made its home. Making sure to carefully step over the hole, continuing on we reach the end of the tree and jump off onto the dirt covered bank. There is only one path to take now, and that path is down stream about a quarter mile. They look around to see if there are any hidden paths when suddenly a small area catches their eyes. They walk to it and before the step through the forest they run into a cold dead corpse hanging lifelessly from the tree. Dangling from the rope and twirling in the wind we quickly walk past the dead to get on a small trail. They follow the trail for a whiles when they come across a pool of blood in the trail. Right beside the puddle is a trail where something has been drug for a distance. Following that trail and small droplets of blood off in the distance they come to a nook in the woods where they see a skeleton hanging upon a cross made out of two trees tied together. The continue walking following the small trail of blood, after a quarter mile the trail of blood stops and vanishes. They look around to see what has happened, looking off into the left behind some discrete forestation. There is a man tied around a stake and was left to die. Surrounding the man on each side are shrunken heads hanging from the trees. They walk on as they come to a close on the path. Within the area they notice a barbed wire fence where bodies are entangled in the wiry death. Just beyond the fence there is a small wooden platform, which holds something not visible yet. Continuing on mist begins to infiltrate the area surrounding the wooden platform. As we sit there staring at the platform an eerie sound comes from the edge of the platform ringing out over the deadly night. Suddenly a blue and red light flares onto the stage as the smoke reveal several people on the stage holding several items. A bright white light shines down in the deep forestation revealing a crowd of many people. Out in the distance we see a great light shine down upon a wrestling ring. A figure in a pair of dark black jeans and a dark black hooded sweatshirt, with the hood over his head and face concealing his identity walks around the ring. A band starts to play loud heavy metelish type music. Words fill the airwaves as the figure in the ring walks around perching himself on the top turnbuckle. The band goes into the ending of the song and set their instruments down as the man pulls out a microphone.] Man: Welcome here, to the meeting of The Brotherhood. The Master has called us all here for the furtherance of his plans. Like before we need four members, the four winds, and the four riders of apocalypse. Now fellow darksiders I must take heed to the Master’s warning. He is not pleased with the mockery Mr. Kreed has made with his alliance of powerful forces. There are to be only four, and those four will fulfill the prophecy. We shall infiltrate, destroy and rebuild. Then the seed shall be planted and a new race will come to control all that surrounds. Remember all of you, silence is deafening to us. We have to come together and make examples out of everything. The Master has chosen me to represent the Brotherhood at this moment. Soon he will add unto us as he desires. His team was brutally destroyed months ago. The Brotherhood faded away into the darkness where it was born and destroyed. {He jumps down and removes his hood to reveal himself as El Lobo de la Muerta. His hair matted up with a tired look on his face.} El Lobo: Now I’m telling you all now. The Brotherhood is about to be reborn. The Master was impressed by my actions and my skills. At Maelstrom I showed the final decision maker when I brutally destroyed Pepe. When I sacrificed my body to destroy his. That is what the Brotherhood is all about, no care about our wellbeing only getting the job done and fulfilling the destiny. While with the Master nothing can stop us. We will make an example out of Emerald Dragon on Storm front. Disgracing the power of the dragon in such a manner is sickening. I will show to him why I am the Wolf Dragon. Why I am the dragon in the flesh. He will be sorrowful the day he crossed the brotherhood and the day he disgraced the name of the dragon in my presence. Darksiders be by my side as I decimate the inexcusable Emerald Dragon. {El Lobo throws the microphone down as he puts his hood back on. He slides out of the ring and makes his way to the trees and disappears into the forest. The lights dim then turn off as we pan in on the moon. Scene fades in black.}
