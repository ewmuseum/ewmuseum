Blaise takes Ukyo by her hand and starts to walk with her. “I drove my car here.” “Figured that out when I saw you fighting with the Bean-Shihde.” “The what?” “In English it would be a Banshee. Relatives of the Lean Shihde, like Annie.” Blaise let her lead, since she knew where her car was. “So, what is it that he has actually asked you to do?” Blaise smiles. “Come on, tell me what I've gotten into here. Who's his Nephew?” “It's actually his great-great-great Nephew....” “Okay?” “His Nephew on his sister's side is none other then... Ian Childress.” “What?” “The Keeper of Secrets himself.” Ukyo looks down at the silver blade that she holds and can feel its warmth through it's sheath. “I think you're getting an idea as to just what you got offered there.” “Yeah, I think I do. And I think I know who your new partner will be.” She smiles and leans over to kiss him. ~End~ For now...
