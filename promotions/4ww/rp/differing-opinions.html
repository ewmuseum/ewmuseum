Give you the ability to relive the greatest nights in your life over and over again. It was like magic. It was a night that I shall never forget, and I’m sure that you won’t either. After all, in the real world, both you and I know that this was the greatest night of both of our lives. Scene fades into one of those cool flash back modes that you always see on TV shows and such. I was just getting ready to relax from a long night of shooting. I had been doing a bikini ad, and the photographer had been barking out orders at me all day. I needed sometime to relax. Of course, I couldn’t get away from the entourage that they always seem to send with me. Don’t get me wrong, those 4 guys were extremely hot, and I would’ve done any of them, but I was so tired of being around them and anyone else from that company that I could’ve just screamed. I had this quaint little place referred to me. A place called the Victorian Wine Precinct. I heard that it was a hopping place, and also a place where all sorts of Hollywood types like myself hung out. This was the place to be. Much like Studio 54 was at one in the United States, this was where anyone who was anybody was. It was a nice place. Not exactly the glitz of Hollywood, but it would do, we were in Australia after all. So after putting on my short red dress with knee high heels, I went down to this place. I didn’t expect much. I just needed to have something to drink, and have something to do to get my mind off of everything that was happening. So with my entourage in toe, I walked into the Precinct and looked around. There were a few people in here that I knew, but none that I wanted to hang out with at the time. There were also those “starlets” who were waiting to be discovered by someone who could actually make their lives worth something. And then something, or should I say someone caught my eye. A young woman, behind the bar, who demanded respect. She had hair as red as fire, and the low cut black dress she was wearing could turn all the guys' heads. She was obviously the one in charge; she was obviously the one who everyone listened to back there. But there was something more to her. I can’t describe what that something more was. You ever hear people describe those who are successful in entertainment as having “it? “ What that “it” is, nobody can describe. Whether it be the way they move, the way they act, or the way they talk. It is just something that draws you to them. It is just something that draws everyone to them. Well, whatever this “it” was, she had it. I scanned a little bit more over the bar. I found a table that was empty, and signaled to my unwanted entourage where we were going. They quickly made their way over there as I meandered over to my awaiting table. I quickly signaled over one of the waitresses who was standing around. She made her way over to me. ''May I help you ma’am?' 'Yes, I would like to have a bottle of your most expensive White Wine.' I calmly stated 'Yes ma’am, but I’ll need the money up front. It’s well over $4,000 American.' I handed her a $10,000 bill, smiling softly at her. 'I’ll take two, and keep the change.' The waitress quickly made her way up to the bar were the woman was barking out orders. She talked to the woman, who looked briefly at me. Our eyes met, and it was at that instant, that I knew I had to meet this woman. It was at that moment that nothing else mattered except for meeting her. I watched as she talked to one of the other young men behind the counter. He was telling him something. The way her lips moved, the way her body worked. Everything about her just screamed out for attention and to draw people to her. The man quickly moved off. It was several minutes till the wine arrived, but to me, it was only a few moments. I was absolutely mesmerized. I was absolutely taken aback by her. Everything there was what I wanted. Sure I had been with women before. It was part of my job, but something about her just seemed different. I had to meet her. As the waitress dropped off my order, I asked her to wait for a moment. I grabbed a napkin and wrote out “Your big break just arrived. Please join me.” I folded it up and handed it to the waitress and asked her to take it quickly to the woman who had so mesmerized me. The waitress quickly made her way over to the woman giving her the napkin. The woman and the waitress quickly had a discussion, with the waitress pointing me out to her. The woman talked to one of her assistants quickly, and started making her way over to my table. I watched her every move as she made her way toward me. I watched as her curves moved so with each and every motion. Her dress flowing as she moved made her seem in my mind as if she was floating on air, Her eyes seemed so determined, and so confident. Everything about here spewed confidence. She gracefully made her way to my table, where my entourage grabbed a chair and pulled it up. I caught her name. Patti. What a name for such a beauty. It reminded me of the sweet taste of a peppermint. She approached the table and looked down at me. 'What’s this about my big break?' 'Well, now that depends on you. My name is Diana King. I’ve been working here in Australia for a while as a model. But I also own my own modeling agency.' Patti kept her eyes locked upon mine. She just stared intently at me and listened to every word that I was saying. 'Sometimes we see people who have that special something, that “it” factor, and Patti, in my opinion, you are oozing with it.' 'Well, thanks, I think. So what do you want me to do?' 'I want you to join my modeling agency. I want you to work with me. I want you to assist me. Together we can make a fortune. We can make things incredibly huge for you. I mean look at this place. It’s a nice place, but think of what all you could have.' 'What kind of modeling?' I could tell Patti was intrigued with the offer. 'We do just about any kind of modeling you could imagine. We do magazines, videos, movies, commercials, you name it, we do it. And I think that you would be doing yourself a favor working here with me. In fact, I’m so sure of it, that I’m prepared to offer you a check right now. How much do you usually make a year?' 'Around $4000.' It nearly took the air out of my lungs when this creature made far below anything I could ever imagine. I took out my checkbook and started writing in it. Patti looked over my shoulder and her eyes bugged out as she saw what I was writing. Her jaw dropped as I finished writing the check and offered it to her. '$100,000 says that I am so sure of you being a huge talent and being able to make it in this business that you should come with me tomorrow morning. We can spend all day tomorrow, you can accompany me to the photo shoot, and I’ll make sure that you are totally taken care off.' We’ll cash this check together, and you can see how real that I am. You are going to be a star. You are going to be bigger than you could ever imagine. I wasn’t lying. This is your big break.' I knew more and more that I needed to make sure she didn’t pass up this opportunity. I needed to make sure more and more that everything went her way. I could open her up to so much more. I could open her eyes and her heart to new experiences that she wouldn’t ever be able to fathom. Patti talked to one of the assistants quickly. He nodded his head and understood. She then proceeded to take off her name tag. She then started walking back toward me and my table. She pulls out the chair and sits down next to me, her eyes wide open and her mouth still showing a bit of the shock. 'So let’s talk more about this. You’ve got my attention.'
