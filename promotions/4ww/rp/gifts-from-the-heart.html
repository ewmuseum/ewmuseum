Gifts From The Heart Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #425 Date: 12/30/03 Location: Homestead in Arizona Surprise - noun 1 a : an attack made without warning b : a taking unawares 2 : something that 1. surprises 3 : the state of being surprised : ASTONISHMENT And that is what I, Patti Anderson is facing. A surprise. I volunteered to sign up for a match for the Christmas show. A chance to have some fun against someone in the federation other then the current people that I’m feuding with. Or it could be worse...I could be facing the one person, outside of Sean McClancy that I care for. But its just one of those twisted things that Fey came up with for the holidays. A chance for all the 4WWers to have fun with each other for a change. For one night only the stars of the Four Winds will celebrate the holidays in a blind card where no one has any knowledge of whom they are facing. They could be facing a friend, someone they are feuding with, or someone they have no problems with. All the stars that signed up agreed to participate in matches that Fey deemed happy to make up. It could be anywhere from a present at the top of a ladder, to building a Christmas tree, to even beating your opponent senseless with a blow up candy cane. Sounds like fun for the entire roster. Just a shame more didnt sign up for it. It could have been a really big fun show. Oh well, most probably decided to head back to their respected homes to see their families and friends. Either way, Fey McClancy-Childress will NOT let the fans down. And now onto the story..... ___________________________________________________________ Place: The Homestead in Arizona Time: Christmas Morning ___________________________________________________________ The scene fades into two people sleeping peacefully on the couch. Their travel outfits lay all over the living room floor. From the looks of things a rush had been made into undressing. A celebration of some sort was on their minds. Their arms and bodies intertwined among the sole blanket that covered them. The contrast of the pair was striking, but still made them beautiful for the eyes to see. The morning sun slowly made its presence known to the world as it shown in on the couple. A stray ray from the sun blasts the sleeping redheads in the face. A hazel eye opens quickly as the blanket comes up over the faces of the sleeping faces. A soft kiss from the emerald eyed one starters her awake. Patti: Morning Love Sean pulls her in closer for a hug and another quick nuzzle and another quick grope. His massive hands cupping her face in another flaming kiss that could ignite the sheet that is covering them up. Patti flipped the sheet off of them so she could breathe a little fresh air. She sat up staring at Sean, the smile of a woman still very much on a high cloud. Patti grabs Sean’s discarded t-shirt that is currently laying on the back fo the couch. She loved the way his clothes fit her. It made night wear so much easier. She walked over to the closet and pulled out a couple of things. From the looks of things it appeared to be two rather neatly wrapped gifts. She walked over to where Sean was still sprawled out on the couch. Patti slowly started humming a silly little Christmas tune that she remembered from childhood. Patti: Jade and I had a chance to do a little Christmas shopping in between training and taking care of things while you finished things up with Fey. So I got you a couple of gifts. Sean smiles at Patti as he takes the first gift from her. His emerald eyes shining brightly with the love he felt for the woman sitting before him. He quickly tears the wrapping way to reveal a beautiful dark green bath robe. Sean slips it on over his shoulders and stands up from under the blanket watching it hang down low over his legs. He does a quick twirl about showing it off to Patti. A quick kiss on the lips is all the approval that Patti needs. Patti debated as she handed the second gift to him. This gift had special meaning. It was a gift that her mother had given her father. A special gift that a woman gave to the man she planned on marrying. A gift that had been passed down among the women of her family. Sean carefully took the package from Patti’s small hands and placed on his lap. He caught the look of concern as it flickered in her eyes, almost as if she was afraid to give him this particular gift. Sean carefully removed the wrapping from the paper from around the gift, his eagerness only being slowed by the desire to prove to Patti that a gift from her is always special. His eyes grew wide as a beautiful jeweled box stared back at him. The mahogany carvings were of the Greek Goddess Aphrodite. His eyes grew wider as Patti assisted with the opening of the box. Inside the box was what totally took his breath away. His hand reached in shakily as he pulled out a jeweled dagger. Patti: This box and dagger have been handed down within my family since the day my great-great-great grandfather gave it to my great-great-great-great grandmother. He gave it to her as a way to protect herself from harm while he was away from her. She only used it once to kill, and that my love, was a wild animal that was stalking her farm animals. Ever since then, the men in the family have passed it down to the women. Sean looked at Patti, more love flowing from his eyes as he watched Patti tell her story. He noticed a few tears falling from her eyes. Sean reached up and quickly wiped the tears away from her face as she continued. Patti: Now if I had been born a son, I would be handing it over to the girl I would be marrying, but since you are the one I have chosen to live with, I am giving you the dagger as a way of protecting yourself from the harm of the world. Patti grabbed hold of Sean’s right hand. Patti: With this dagger, I give you the strength to protect yourself, and the honor to know that what you do is above all, the right thing within a world full of unjust and untruth. With this dagger, I give you the right to protect and defend me from all evils, and above all, I give you my heart to protect. Patti’s hazel eyes showed with tears as she finished reciting the words she had carefully practiced and translated from the old text. Sean still sat mystified over the priceless gift that Patti had given him. Something that meant so much to her now belonged to him. Sean set the box carefully down on the coffee table and pulled Patti in for long and drawn out kiss. His lack of words flowing into the kiss for the gift that she had just bestowed upon him. Sean got up off the couch and walked over to the desk where the gift he had for Patti lay hidden for weeks. He hoped that his gift held the same meaning as the gift she just gave to him. He slowly turned and walked back over to Patti. A mischievous look appeared on his face as he walked back over to where Patti sat. He reaches out to Patti as he takes her left hand in his as he slowly brings forth a small crushed velvet black box. Sean slowly goes down to one knee as the scene fades to black.
