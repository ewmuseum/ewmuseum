An Open Letter to Kyo

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An Open Letter to Kyo Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #960 Date: 09/13/04 Location: An Open Letter to Kyo, I spent my day laying around the pool with my sister. You know. The one that you saw up close and personal when she power bombed your pathetic partner into the announce table. She and I were blessed to watch your little interview on the local television show. From what I saw...All I have to say is.... Thank you! We're all refreshed and challenged by your unique point of view, you ridiculous little carnival freak. In future, kindly study your opponents a little more before assaulting unsuspecting fans with a litany of empty promises that will just lay around and draw dust from being told over and over again. Trying to get something of value out of you is like trying to squeeze orange juice out of an apple. Why is it that the people with the smallest minds always have the biggest mouths? If you knew what you're talking about, you'd be dangerous. To quote Martin Luther King, Jr.: 'Nothing in the world is more dangerous than a sincere ignorance and conscientious stupidity.' I'm sorry to be so blunt, but you and Darek both bore me to tears now. I can't even stand watching the two of you babble. You're both overdue for the Prozac treatments that the wonderful doctors of the 4WW have offered. Kyo, your attitude is just horrible. I can't even understand how you can wake up one day and just not know who you are. Did you like eat some bad Shitaki mushrooms or bad dog? Do you honestly think that not knowing who the bloody hell you are is going to help you in this match with me? I mean even those who are half brain dead at least have a clue as to who they are. I mean even poor misguided Darek knew who he was the entire time he was under my enchanted spell. I mean when god was handing out personalities, you must have been holding the door. You're so boring, even a boomerang wouldn't come back to you. I'd get more pleasure from running my nostrils down a cactus, than watching another contribution from you. Maybe you wouldn't come across as such a jellyfish-sucking mental midget if you weren't intellectually outclassed by dead sheep; if your weren't so fat that your local 'All-You-Can-Eat' buffet had to install speed bumps, or if you didn't have a face that could scare a hungry wolf off a meat truck. Who am I kidding? You would. Just do me a favor and lay down for the full sixty minutes. It would save me and the rest of the fans of the 4WW the disappointment of watching a pathetic loser such as yourself, become a champ. I mean you claim I'm a disgrace to the title, but what about you? You're the one that danced around for weeks backstage freaking out over having to take me on, and it's gotten worse since Darek was added. Maybe it would be better off for you to scurry away into the shadows and remain hidden there. Let the rats, mice, and other creatures of the dark be your friends, because after all is said and done, the dark is where you are going to want to be. I know I certainly won't miss you, and neither will the fans of the 4WW. Sincerely, Patti Anderson Your North Wind Champion. *credit to http://www.insultmonger.com/ for assistance with the verbal insults.
