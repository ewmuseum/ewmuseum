A Rushed Convalescence

13 January 2004

A Rushed Convalescence Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1135 Date: 1/13/04 Location: Tarrytown General Hospital, NY [We are brought inside a hospital wing to a hospital room. Sitting in a hospital bed is Rustin Slade, who only hours earlier was beaten to a bloody pulp by an unknown assailant, who could only be recognized again by the army insignia he was wearing on the breast of his dinner jacket. Rustin is relaxing on his small hospital bed and he looks over to a vase with flowers sent by the 4WW Staff and a note from Patti Anderson-McClancy. “On behalf of the Commishes of the 4WW, Toddy Boy and I would like to wish you a speedy recovery and look forward to your return to the ring in the future.'] [Rustin smiles at the card and puts it back with the flowers] Rustin: Hope it’s real fast Mrs. Anderson-McClancy. I’m looking to win that battle royal this upcoming Saturday. I need a good partner in the business. Tag team gold would suit me just fine. [Rustin looks back at his nightstand. Hidden within a vase of yellow and white roses is a small white card. The card is gold foiled on the edges. The card is written in calligraphy and says 'Dear Rustin, Hope those nasty bruises are healing. If you need someone to kiss them and make them better, you know where to find me. Love, Diana King-Dream.] [Rustin smiles again at the card and inhales a bit of the smell of the flowers. A nurse passes by Rustin’s room with something in her hands. Rustin sighs, replaying the doctor’s warning back in his head. “You lost a lot of blood, and your ribs are bruised badly. I advise you to stay here for another four of five more days.” Rustin shook his head, knowing that Stormfront was only two days away. He tried to get up from the bed but fell back clutching his ribs. And he’s been sitting in the bed ever since. The nurse who walked by comes back and reaches to hit the light switch.] Nurse: Lights out Mr. Slade. Sleep well. [The room is flooded in darkness, and Rustin’s eyelids slowly become very heavy. Soon we hear some light snoring.] ..................zzzzzzzzz........zzzzzzzz....... [We are now inside a cage. Rustin is still in his hospital bed. Rustin is shaken by the sudden change of atmosphere. He looks out to the jam packed crowded arena in Berlin, Germany, who begin to chant RUSTIN...RUSTIN. Rustin then sees Chastity Spence appear. She is beckoning him towards her. Rustin gets out of the bed without much effort. He surprises himself, and realizes that his ribs are no longer hurting him. Chastity seems so far away to Rustin. He comes closer. There is a sudden rumble in the middle of the ring and Eddie B. bursts through the canvas, blocking Chastity from Rustin’s view. Eddie swings at Rustin, but Rustin quickly takes Eddie down with an armbar takedown and applies the Blindfold, and Eddie taps almost immediately.] [The ring bell rings. Rustin gets back up and starts walking towards Chastity again, who has her eyes wide open. As Rustin nears Chastity once again, from the top of the cage drops down the man in a business suit who beat Rustin up in the first place, the reason why he was in the hospital bed. Rustin catches his insignia again. “Private: first class,” Rustin remembers from a book he read on army pins. Rustin catches a flash of a dog tag that says “FRINK.” The man again hits Rustin. Rustin does not seem hurt, however, and delivers a punch back. The force of the blow knocks the man in the suit backwards hard into the cage which causes him to buckle face first into the mat, a clean knockout.] [Chastity Spence now walks towards Rustin and raises his arm in victory. Rustin moves in for a hug. He cups his testicles, hoping the same result won’t happen from Maelstrom VI, where he received a knee from Ms. Spence. To his surprise she accepts the hug, and hugs back. Rustin rubs his hands over Chastity’s back. Chastity then takes Rustin’s face gently into her small, soft hands. Rustin watches as Chastity’s velvet lips begin to sprout out words] Chastity: Rustin, you need to come to Stormfront. Do it for me! Rustin: Chassy, I can’t. Chastity: Please Rustin! If you love me you’ll come. Rustin: I do love you...it’s just that..I’m not allowed to.. [Before he can finish, Rustin falls to the mat with a thud. Rustin barely turns himself over, unable to get back up, his whole body seemingly paralyzed. He looks up and sees that Private Frink is now back up. He is holding Chastity in his arms. He gives her a quick kiss, making Rustin writhe on the mat, causing more pain than the axhandle blow he delivered moments earlier.] Frink: Like I said Slade. Stick to your roots! And stay away from Chastity! [Chastity and Frink leave the ring, leaving Rustin broken-hearted and bruised.] CRASH [Rustin jerks upright in his bead, and pays for it feeling a surge of pain travel up his ribs. He clutches them and bites back the pain. The crash he heard was a nurse who had dropped something. Another nurse comes down the hall, disgusted. Nurse 1: What the hell Lilly! This is the second time in two weeks. Lilly: I’m sorry I really am! Nurse 1: Oh god. All right, go back to your patients. [Lilly the nurse runs down the hall as the other nurse grabs a phone at one of the receptionist tables.] Nurse 1 [into phone]: Ricardo, bring a mop, we got another bedpan spill. [The nurse goes down the hallway, leaving the spilled mess in the middle of the hallway.] [Rustin, realizing he’s back in the hospital room, suddenly comes to the realization that he was just part of one really weird dream. Rustin remembers only one thing. “If you love me you’ll come.”] Rustin: I love you. And I am coming. You can count on that. Even if I can’t talk to you or hold you, being near to you is good enough. [Rustin steps out of his bed, holding his ribs carefully and begins to walk out of his room. He moves slowly but surely and begins walking in the hallway. No matter what, Rustin Slade is not going to miss this week’s stormfront. Not for the world. Before the scene fades out Rustin looks down at his slippers and shakes his head.] Rustin: Good luck cleaning up that crap Ricardo.
