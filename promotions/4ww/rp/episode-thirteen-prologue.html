Episode Thirteen (Prologue) Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #712 Date: 5/28/04 Location: Location: Unknown Time: Unknown = [At the mysterious location, you noticed Kyo Kushinaga formerly known as the Masked Avenger, who was wearing not his mask instead he was wearing a pair black jeans and no shirt revealing his slight muscular upper body that was covered with demonic markings with no shoes on his feet, was laying motionless in soft green-bluish grass with both arms spread out and his legs together as he was in a crucifix position.] = “…Kyo” = [Kyo still motionless.] = “Kyo…” …Wake up, Kyo… = [Kyo opens his eyes, looking in a daze for a moment as he changed his gaze into a curious one when noticing that he was looking up at a bright white sky without any clouds though he instantly knew that something was not right because the sky does not get white like that.] = “How long are you going to keep laying there?” = [Kyo slowly sits up from the grass, closes his eyes as he was favoring his head, suffering from a painful headache, looking confusingly.] = Kyo: What… What happened…and why am I not wearing my mask…? = [He made a soft painful sound, placed both hands on the sides of his head, seeing brief and disturbing images of his recent match against Tarrant in his head. He actually killed the Masked Avenger I guess this means that this is the end for the Avenger… A New Defender Location: Unknown Time: Unknown = [Still at the mysterious place, Kyo sat in the soft strange color grass, looking in disappointment as he could not believe that he lost so badly to Tarrant. Josh glaring at his brother seriously though angrily in his cold brown eyes.] = Josh: Sometimes I cannot believe that I came from you… = [Kyo just stood there in silence. Josh looked at Kyo disappointingly.] = Josh: However, I must remember that you are only half the man you are… You are not complete yet since our good mother decided to go to a Shinto Priest and have that priest split us up into two different beings. And what standing before you is your true devil self or should “Demonic” self that is not afraid of anything and goes by the code honor, striking down any those cheap imitations, goddamn punks who put the Demonic breed to shame and whoever does this shall be sent to the pits of Hell with no fucking remorse! = [Kyo sharpened his furiously when hearing his loud words.] = Kyo: Shut up! I am nothing like you!
