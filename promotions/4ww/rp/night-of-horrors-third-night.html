Night of Horrors- Third Night Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #964 Date: 9-12-04 Location: Japan (ooc: sorry if this seems rushed, i waited too long to get this series finished... ARG! oh well, i hope you have enjoyed the journy) +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ NIGHT THREE +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ And so the second night had ended… Kanton had survived the war of the mind. He still had a final test though. He had to defeat the demon he knew existed. He must plunge the sword into the heart of evil. With that… the third night began. Kanton felt warmth all around him as he began to stir. He slowly opened his eyes and looked around himself. He was in the courtyard of a large house… he thought to himself, it had to be the third home. He looked around the courtyard and it seemed normal enough as most things, but one thing stood out. In the center of the courtyard there was a large pit, about fifteen feet by fifteen feet. It glowed an eerie red like fire, but it wasn’t. An eerie fog began to roll out from the pit. It was then that Kanton saw his Angel. She was bound with her hands behind her back. A smile trace of blood fell from her forehead… she seemed unconscious. She seemed so peaceful… It was then that Kanton noticed something else… Rachael was standing only a few feet away, and there she stood, the dark goddess, the Jade woman. Rachael’s eyes no longer glowed the eerie green. They were dull, lifeless. The Jade woman seemed completely normal now, her tattered white kimono was stained with blood. The ropes about her body hung tightly around her form. The blindfold held tight to her face with the gag in her mouth in place. It was then that Rachael spoke. “Welcome Kris, this is the final test it seems. I really have to need for this husk to speak it seems.” Rachael slowly raised a ritualistic dagger in her left hand. She walked over to the Jade woman who remained motionless. Rachael used the dagger and cut the gag and blindfold from the face of the bound woman. When all fell Kanton understood why so many had come to desire her. She was beautiful… purely evil beauty. Rachael cut the ropes from bound woman who was now smiling. The woman’s eyes began to glow a jade green. She was free now… completely free. She had used Rachael to free her. “I have no more use for you” spoke an angelic voice from the lips of the demoness. Rachael nodded as she turned and walked to Angel. She slowly picked up Angel who was beginning to stir. Angel’s eyes opened wide in shock as she realized she couldn’t move her arms and legs. Rachael and pulled Angel over her shoulder. “You may take your leave… and join me forever,” the evil woman had spoke. With that, Rachael took a few steps and walked straight into the pit, and fell… only a few words echoed out. “Kris, I love you…” Kanton sat there in shock. He had lost his love twice now. His life was once again in shambles. Kanton sat on the cold ground and it all hit him. Tears began to flow from his eyes. His Angel was gone… It was then his emotions began to change. His tears turned from sadness to anger. His face grew enraged. It was then that Kanton remembered… **FLASH** “Yes… to save Angel and Rachael, you must destroy the demon,” the spirit spoke. “How am I supposed to do that? She’s a freakin DEMON!” Kanton sighed at the end of his words. What was he going to do, wrestle it? The man in white reached into his robes and retrieved a sword. He lifted it and handed it to Kanton who then took it and weighed it in his hands. “You must drive that sword into the heart of the beast… only when she shows her true form though. You will understand in due time. Now you must rest. Your test is almost over. You can succeed,” the man in white spoke. He then stood and looked at Kanton. Kris could tell he was much bigger and taller. The immense man placed his hand softly on the shoulder of Kanton. **FLASH** Kanton looked to his side and there it sat… the sword that Kanton had been given. He slowly reached his shaking hand to it and pulled it close to his anger filled persona. He slowly unsheathed the blade and was mesmerized by the cold harsh steel. He would have his vengeance it seemed after all. Tonight it would all end. He fully took the blade from it’s protective case and threw the sheath away. He stood up and anger began to brew once again in the being of Kanton. More that anger existed, there was hate, vengeance, and the taste for blood. “This ends now… you’re going to fucking DIE!” Kanton shouted in rage, but the demoness merely smiled at him. “And that little thing is supposed to help defeat me? You pathetic fool,” she finished as she laughed loudly in the dark night, “I gave you a chance… now you will join the ones you loved.” Kanton raised the sword to his side and stalked the vile demon of a woman. She remained unmoved though. With one fell swoop of the blade Kanton brought the blade across the neck of the woman, severing her head from her body. As the head flopped to the ground laughter could still be heard. The body still stood and the voice spoke again… “You fool…” The ground began to shake violently. Kanton slowly backed away and watched as the demoness’s body began to glow jade green. Bright light and wind exploded from her as Kanton shielded his eyes. As the light dimmed Kanton looked forward. There she stood in all her glory… he face was no longer that of beauty, but of horror. Her body glowed jade green as she hovered about a foot above the ground. “You foul human… you shall perish,” she spoke with her voice no more angelic, but more harsh and horse. She slowly floated towards Kanton, but he did not move. He was frozen in shock… in fear… his mind told him to move, but he didn’t. He was lifted in to the air by her hand. Her touch burned him through to his soul. She was pure evil. It was then she held him over the deep pit. He looked down and words cannot describe what he saw, but it would be something he would carry with him to the end of his days. It was hell… pure hell… the world of the demons… eternal fire, pain, and suffering. With that sight though Kanton’s body snapped to. He quickly moved his hand and drove the sword into the heart of the she demon. The glow left her evil body. She fell, and Kanton fell with her… straight into the eternal fire… straight to death. All of Kanton’s life flashed before his eyes then… all events and actions that caused him to be the man he was. With that it ended and Kanton was no more… **SNAP** With that Kanton opened his eyes as he sat up in bed. What had happened… how was he back in his hotel? All was still in darkness. Kanton looked to his right… there stood Rachael above him… and with one fell swoops she moved her hand. A dagger flew across the throat of Kanton. Blood flowed from his body and down the sheets as Rachael laughed evily. Her eyes glowed green… **CRACK** Kanton sat up in a cold sweat. He was sitting up in his bed, but when he looked around he realized he had seen it all before. Was this deja-vu? Kanton scratched his head as he sat up in the bed. He slipped the covers off as he swung his legs over the side of the bed. He scratched at his T-shirt as he wasn’t used to wearing one to bed. He looked over at Angel as she was brushing her hair. Had he not done and seen all of this before? She was dressed well as she was wearing a white halter top with khaki shorts. It was then that he noticed Rachael was basically wearing the same thing. She was wearing a black halter top with black shorts though. Rachael and Angel both continued to get ready in front of the mirror which hung above the TV across the room from the bed. CRACK~~~!!! The sound that had awoken Kanton was heard again. He looked outside the windows as the sky grew dark. Heavy rain began to fall. Angel looked outside as so did Rachael. “Rain? Look’s like our picnic plans are cancelled,” Angel spoke a bit upset, but Kanton’s stomach flipped as he realized everything around him. He stared at the picnic basket across the room on the table. Angel looked over at Kris and smiled. “Mornin sleepy head, you ok? You almost sounded like you were having a nightmare.” “You have no idea,” Kanton trailed off a bit. Had it all been a dream? It was so real… He had been there. He knew it. A knock was heard at the door. Angel walked across the room to the hotel door and opened it. A man stood there with a note. “This is for a Mr. Kanton.” “I’ll take that… thanks,” Angel walked back across the room and handed what seemed to be a little note to Kris, “This is for you I guess.” Kanton opened the note and inside was a simple sentence written in pen, “You’re destiny awaits you… you have passed”. Kanton looked up at his Angel and smiled. He took her into his arms and held her tightly before embracing her lovingly with a kiss. “Should I leave?” Rachael asked humorously. “No, I’m just feeling really confident now. I think I can be ready for Maelstrom after all,” Kanton smiled as he placed the note on the nightstand next to the bed. The rain was falling even heavier now. So he passed it seemed… it had all been a dream, or was it? What about the note? He was satisfied though, he was at peace. He had overcome the challenge of the mind and the physical and passed. Maelstrom would be coming soon. Kanton knew he was ready now… he was one of the chosen. His destiny was waiting for him… ~FIN~
