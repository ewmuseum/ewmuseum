Destiny... part two of the Four Winds

5 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Destiny... part two of the Four Winds Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #733 Date: 05-06-04 Location: Seattle, WA “You only truly live once… Am I already dead then?” “No…” {The scene cuts in as we continue from last time. We come in just as we find Kanton leaning backwards over the edge of the Space Needle in Seattle, WA. The rain continues to pour down as lighting strikes down nearby to the Space Needle in a bright white flash. As it flashes, Kanton’s soaked body crumples downward almost as if he were a puppet and the lightning cut his strings. As Kanton falls off the edge it’s almost as if time has slowed down to a crawl. Lighting shoots down again. This time it hits the tip of the Space Needle. Lightning courses through the metal of the Space Needle as the electricity seems to hop, skip, and jump towards the direction of Kanton. The electricity then strikes a light which charges an explosion sending Kanton reeling backwards end over end. Just as it seems all is lost Kanton grabs the lip of the Space Needle and holds on for his life. Tears escape his eyes as blood flows down his body from the cuts and incisions caused by the flying glass of the explosion.} Kanton: I don’t want to die… {Just then as Kanton has almost lost hope, a voice echoes from his side.} Voice: Don’t worry… It’s not your time yet… You must let go. Kanton: But I will fall… Voice: You must let go of your fears and worries… Trust me… Let go! {Lightning crashes down violently from the sky.} Kanton: The Gods must be angry tonight… {With a smirk Kanton removes his left hand from the ledge. He then begins to fall. As if in recall to before, everything moves at what seems to be the speed of light. Before Kanton even realizes it he is about to hit the ground.} Voice: Like I said… It’s not your time yet. {Suddenly the wind changes and it’s almost as if time has stopped. Basically, it has. Kanton has stopped in mid air only a foot as it seems off of the ground. Then almost as if he were lying on a bed he stands up to look around. Everything has changed now… The once busy streets of Seattle are now empty. The rain has stopped falling, and the sky is clear. The only light comes from the full moon in the sky which casts an eerie glow on the area nearby Kanton. No noise can be heard except for the heavy breathing of Kanton.} Kanton: What happened? {Just then the wind blows in. First it’s from the South… then the North… then the East… then the West…} Voice: I told you… It’s not your time yet. {Four shapeless forms then make their way forward in the night. As they approach Kanton nothing is known of them. The mere abilty to “see” them comes from the light of the full moon which seems to almost shine a light on them. Kanton inches back a bit only for a second before he finds himself unable to move. Just then a new voice speaks.} New Voice: Don’t worry… We are not here to hurt you. We are here to show you the way… The way of your destiny… {Kanton then looks on shocked as it seems.} New Voice: We are… the Four Winds… {Kanton merely looks on speechless.} North Wind: I am the North Wind… I bring strength… East Wind: I am the East Wind… I bring faith… South Wind: I am the South Wind… I bring life… West Wind: I am the West Wind… I bring passion… All Winds: Together we are one… We bring the spirit of the Gaia… {Kanton as before is still merely looking on. He is too shocked to speak.} North Wind: There is… East Wind: nothing to be… South Wind: afraid of… West Wind: We merely… North Wind: wished to speak to East Wind: you about something… South Wind: Your… West Wind: destiny… {As each wind speaks their traits are felt. With these traits though comes another feeling, a feeling of color almost. Each wind has unto itself an essence of light it would seem. Together they are white and pure. It seems that the strength of the North flows red while the faith of the East flows yellow. Then, the life that springs up from the South flows green as the passion of the west rings blue. As the winds stop talking Kanton finally is able to speak.} Kanton: My destiny? What is my destiny? North Wind: Can’t you tell? East Wind: You are the one! South: You are to bring balance. West Wind: You are the Four Winds. All Winds: We live on through you… Kanton: Through me? But I don’t understand… Why me? North Wind: Why anybody? East Wind: What does it matter? South Wind: All that matters… West Wind: Is that you are the one. North Wind: Some answers you must find yourself. East Wind: But you know the truth. South Wind: You have always known. West Wind: Now you must wake up. Kanton: What must I do though? North Wind: You must bring balance. East Wind: You must bring discipline. South Wind: You must bring life. West Wind: You must bring love. All Winds: The Four Winds are in trouble. You have come to restore balance as it is needed. You know what you must do. Kanton: I still don’t understand. All Winds: You soon will… {Just then all four winds erupts into a bring light as they then surround Kanton.} All Winds: Strength, faith, life, and passion… In you now exists the spirit of the Gaia. ((BOOM)) {Suddenly in a flash of bright light we find ourselves again on the top of the Space Needle. Kanton then sits up right upon the edge as he sucks in air quickly as if he were just suffocating. He looks on wide eyed and panicked only to find that he is alone once again.} Kanton: Was it real? {The wind then picks up and almost whispers to Kanton.} Wind: Remember… Your destiny… {The scene then slowly… fades… out…}
