Night of Horrors- Night 2 Kris Kanton 4WW Exposures #956 Date: 9-11-04 Location: A village in Japan +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ NIGHT 2 +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ So much has happened in what seems to be such a short time. What happened to Angel? What happened to Rachael? Was he really seeing the ghost of the Jade woman? Had the essence of the Four Winds really returned to him? What would this “test” prove? There were many questions that he had needed answeres… but first he needed to awaken. Then enter the second night, where things could happen that he could never be ready for… **SHNINK** “Aaaaawwwwweeeeeeeyyyyyooooooooowwwwaaaaaaaaa” **SHNINK** “Aaaaawwwwweeeeeeeyyyyyooooooooowwwwaaaaaaaaa” **SHNINK** “Aaaaawwwwweeeeeeeyyyyyooooooooowwwwaaaaaaaaa” **SHNINK** “Wake up… wake up… EEEEAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!” Kanton sat up suddenly in a cold sweat. What was that? Where was he? “Aaaaawwwwweeeeeeeyyyyyooooooooowwwwaaaaaaaaa” **SHNINK** Kanton listened closely to the chanting, and the other sounds. Suddenly down ahead of him there began a series of lights. They were torches. They lit up all that was around up until the feet of Kanton, even though they were a good fifty feet away. They were people… wearing all black with veils. They were the veiled priests Kanton had seen the previous night that carried the Jade woman statue into the town square. Each one carried a gold staff on their right side as they seemed to march left to right in the hallway ahead of him. On these gold staffs were black steel rings, they were causing the noise as the priests slammed their staffs into the ground. Kanton worked his way to his feet and looked around. He was what seemed to be in an underground cave or tunnel. It may have even been a series of tunnels for all Kanton knew. He backed up a bit and felt his back against the cool damp earth that made the tunnel. The procession of priests seemed to be long. It was then that the procession ended and all returned to darkness. The torches and priests vanished sending the tunnels into darkness again. A cold win blew about Kanton, but he could not tell from where. Slowly the hairs on his neck began to stand as he walked forward in the darkness. It was then that he remembered something as he reached into his pocket. A lighter, he always kept one for luck as it seemed. It was a black Zippo lighter with a silver dragon on it. He pulled it out and held it in front of him as he flicked it a few times. It caught fire. It was then that Kanton saw something he never expected. “PAAAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNNNNNNNNN,” the seemingly dead body had screamed. Kanton dropped the lighter as began to cry. It couldn’t have been real. His mind was playing tricks on him. He had seen the dead body of Angel, hanging from a rope with it tied around her neck. Her face was dark and bloated. Her skin was wet and almost purple it seemed. She couldn’t be dead. Kanton repeated it to himself over and over out loud. “She’s not dead… she’s not dead,” Kanton said as he searched around on the ground and found the lighter once again. He flicked it, it started up again. Angel’s body was gone… it was as if it was never there. Kanton walked slow and edgily along the dark earthen corridor. He came to a T and turned to his left, the direction where the priests came from. He walked a bit, but he came to a wall. He had seen many priests coming from this direction though. How had so many come this way… there was no corridor… what was going on. As Kanton tried to make sense of things he turned around to head in the other direction. He hoped there was a way out. How else was he going to get out of this earthly prison? He walked slowly as he held the lighter in front of himself shakily. The image of Angel in such a state was still burned into his mind. He had reached the intersection again as in front of him he saw a square light. At the top of the tunnel he saw an opening, a hatch of sorts. Coming through it was a ladder, it was Kanton’s way out. It was then that he heard it… “HAAAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA” Evil cold laughter filled the heart of Kanton as he looked to his right. A single set of jade green glowing eyes could be seen. Kanton’s blood turned cold as all the hair on his body stood up. He quickly capped off his Zippo and ran for the ladder. He quickly climbed up the ladder as he heard the source of the eyes getting closer… and closer. He quickly hopped through the opening and looked for something to close it with. There was a hatch lid for it with a latch. Kanton took the lid and slammed it close. He the hooked the latch closed. Then there was nothing. There was not a single sound. Kanton breathed in heavily as nervous sweat slowly poured down his head. He looked around the room he was in, and it seemed to be a storage room of sorts. There were old dusty trunks and boxes everywhere, and the whole room was lit by two paper lamps at the far corners of the room. He stood up and looked around for a bit before approaching the door at the end of the storage room. He slowly slid the door open. Now he seemed to be looking in what seemed to be a long corridor, almost like an entry hallway to the house. There were many doors. Kanton knew his answer rested somewhere near by. He began to search the rooms one by one. He wasn’t sure really of what he was looking for. He just knew he would know it when he saw it. It was then that Kanton found it, in the third room. It was another journal or book of sorts. It had a black cover and it had a sort of white paint wording on it. It actually said, “Trial by Fire”. Kanton opened up the book and there was no writing… until about mid way. It was pretty close to where the first book he found had cut off. Kanton sat down on a bench with the book. +~+~+~+~+ENTRY 103+~+~+~+~+ Everyone who passes the gate will die for the Dark Gods have spoken so. They power is great. They’re mind is all knowing. But they have no mercy. They take pleasure in our pain for we are mere bugs to their great power. The Jade woman was granted power from them. She is now our living god. The bound woman… She controls our darkest dreams and makes them reality. +~+~+~+~+ENTRY 104+~+~+~+~+ My dreams have become my reality. I live only to serve the Jade goddess. Isn’t it ironic… I loved my wife. Now, I love the goddess. She is desire. She is my dream. She wanted me, and now she has me. She has changed my world. She has opened my eyes to the truth. I can have anything I want… so long as I desire her and I will do so always. She came to me in my dreams last night… to speak to me… only me. She truly does desire me. I am happy. +~+~+~+~+Entry 105+~+~+~+~+~+ I have become one of the chosen… I am a priest in the order of her name. We are the dark monks for we serve the dark goddess. We will be her servants for eternity. As we are damned at the same time we are now enlightened. We are loved for we love her. All one must do is open your heart to her… she wants in. +~+~+~+~+Entry 106+~+~+~+~+~+ We shall serve her until the end of days… it is our duty for her love. We shall protect her always… always… Never shall she feel the wrath of another… we shall not allow it. +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ Kanton closed the book and shuddered, talk about a bunch of freaks. He thought to himself for a moment and remembered the priests when he had first arrived… then the priests in the cave. What did it all mean? Why had this supposed “god” chosen to fuck over his life along with Angel and use Rachael to do it? Where was the connection? Then it clicked. Kanton stood up slowly and brushes the dirt and dust from his shorts. As he walked to the sliding door he thought about one of the passages from the book, “Isn’t it ironic… I loved my wife. Now, I love the goddess. She is desire.” That passage rung Kanton as he paused for a moment… Rachael, had Rachael desired Kanton? When Rachael came to Kanton… who had told her to and why? Kanton began to wonder if this wasn’t just the part of an even bigger plot. Someone was out to destroy his life… but why? He didn’t know. All he knew is that this was a test of sorts for him. If he could pass… was worthy of the South Wind. Kanton slid open the door and looked at what had become not a hallway inside a house… it was like the inside hallway of an apartment complex… a very familiar complex. It looked like the inside of the apartments Kanton had lived in up until he was a teenager. This was all so strange, but everything so far had been strange. Kanton slowly stalked down the corridor as he read the numbers on the door… 110… 111… 112... Kanton continued walking until he came to door 120. It was the number of the apartment he had lived in as a child. Kanton reached for the doorknob and turned it. The door slowly swung open. **FLASH** Kanton looked forward and his mind tried to register the horrors it saw. All was in black and white. Kanton was looking in on the living room of the apartment. Lying on the floor in a circle were Angel, Rachael, Kanton, Kanton’s dead mother, Kanton’s dead brother, and even Kanton’s dead fiancé Erika. Each had their hands tied to the next person’s feet. Each had been sacrificially murdered. They eyeballs were removed with gruesome precision, dried blood surrounded their empty eye sockets. Each one’s face was the perfect expression of pain and agony. He looked around the circle as each person’s clothes were torn and partially missing. Each person had been gutted. Their entrails stuck out from their empty cask bodies, they were rotting and putrid. Kanton focused on himself as he saw his naked body. He had also been castrated… his lower regions were completely befouled as dried blood was spread over his dead and grotesque body. Kanton closed his eyes tightly and tried to erase the images from his head. It was then that he heard a sweet soft voice in his ear. “I can make it all go away. Love me… not them. I can make you happy for all eternity.” Kanton shook his head violently as he spoke, “NO! GO AWAY! I WON’T BELIEVE IT! IT’S NOT REAL!” Kanton began to cry as he opened his eyes again. He saw Angel standing in front of him. She was smiling… for a moment. It was then that Kanton noticed the knife in her right hand. She brought it up and slashed her own throat. Tears shook the hardened man as he watched the crimson fluid of life flow from her open neck. He closed his eyes once again. “You don’t want this… I can make it end. It’s painful isn’t it? You don’t have to feel the pain. I can end it.” Kanton opened his eyes. He saw Rachael and Angel… Rachael had bent over and was drinking the blood from the neck of Angel. Kanton closed his eyes… he wanted it to end. “I can end this… come to me. Be with me.” Kanton opened his eyes once more. He saw Rachael there in front of him. Angel’s blood was flowing down from her mouth to her chin. There was a sweet angelic smile on her face that caused Kanton’s stomach to turn. He closed his eyes once more. “COME TO ME! I WILL HAVE YOU!” Kanton screamed in fury, “NO! IT’S NOT REAL! IT’S NOT REAL! I CAN END IT! IT’S NOT REAL!” There was a cold wind before Kanton opened his eyes. He looked forward… and all was gone. The apartment… the bodies… everything. Kanton was standing there, right at the entrance to what seemed to be the main “town square” of the village. He looked around as the wind blew coldly in the night. The full moon shone it’s silver light on the village. There was a man in the center of the circle. He was sitting where the statue had once been. He wore an all while cloak about him and his head was covered with a hood. A lone hand emerged from the sleeve of the cloak. It waved Kanton towards itself. The mysterious figure beckoned for Kanton and he followed. The figure seemed safe… familiar… to Kanton. He walked towards the person. Kanton came within a few feet and it signaled to stop. Kanton followed the order and waited a few feet away. “You know not who I am, but I must feel familiar to you,” the man spoke with a strong familiar voice. It was calm and reassuring yet firm at the same time. He continued, “You have survived the trial of the mind. She has tested you, and you have passed… well. She is a powerful demon… she will not give up. She wants you… she wants Angel… she wants Rachael. You defeated her with your mind… now you must defeat her with your power.” “With power?” Kanton asked. “Yes… to save Angel and Rachael, you must destroy the demon,” the spirit spoke. “How am I supposed to do that? She’s a freakin DEMON!” Kanton sighed at the end of his words. What was he going to do, wrestle it? The man in white reached into his robes and retrieved a sword. He lifted it and handed it to Kanton who then took it and weighed it in his hands. “You must drive that sword into the heart of the beast… only when she shows her true form though. You will understand in due time. Now you must rest. Your test is almost over. You can succeed,” the man in white spoke. He then stood and looked at Kanton. Kris could tell he was much bigger and taller. The immense man placed his hand softly on the shoulder of Kanton. It was then that Kanton blacked out. With that the second night had ended and only one trial remained in the way of Kris. He had withstood the mental, now he would have to surpass the physical. Soon the third night would begin… soon the final test would come. To be continued…
