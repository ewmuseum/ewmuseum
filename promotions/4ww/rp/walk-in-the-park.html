We are surrounded by beautiful trees that are being brushed by passing light winds. We here nothing but birds singing in the nearby tree to are left as if chirping out a song with another bird in pure harmony. We take a look at the sky and observe it closely we see birds flying a T-shape formation and a Airplane slightly above them. We then look at the beautiful white and puffy clouds as each one shifts with each slight blow from the wind. We then pan down only to find a brown wooden bench with bushes surrounding the area. The cameraman keeps on this for a while and then goes back to birds as he does he watches to skinny looking birds come out there egg shells.
