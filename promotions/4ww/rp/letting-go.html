Letting go... Caitlin Fairchild 4WW Exposures #1024 Date: 10/26/04 Location: Santa Barbra, California [The 4WW logo blows by the screen as the new 4WW South Wind Champion sits in the middle of her living room in a simple white summer dress with the straps hanging off the sides of her shoulders, and her hands on her chin staring at her trophy case. In front of her neatly folded was the South Wind title, within her trophy case where the actual ½ of the SCW, ICW, and ECWF Aggression Tag titles she unified with her tag partner Charon (who has the other halves) as well as a trophy belt version of the VSE World Tag title she received after winning the titles, and above that was a trophy version of the VSE World Heavyweight title belt…both belts are given to former champions who were no longer champions. It had been a couple of days since Caitlin had lost her VSE World Heavyweight Championship, what was more humiliating was the way she lost her title…forced to tap out to the #1 Contender Dino Casino…now she was X’ed out of the possible chance to make history at the up and coming VSE PPV, to walk out as the first woman to capture 4 championships in the history of the business…now she was just the 4WW South Wind Champion…starting from the bottom all over again. Caitlin signed as she picked up her title belt…it wasn’t a bad looking championship belt, fashioned with a silver and gold design, with a huge diamond like star in the middle. It was still another great accomplishment for her in the career nonetheless, but she still missed being at the top of her game, not to mention she felt she didn’t have the chance to represent herself as a true world champion as she had liked too…her title reign had a huge gap due to the promotion being shut down…to come back after such a long time, and lose the title at the first defense was not how she wanted to go out as champion…not that way. Caitlin bit her bottom lip, and played with her long red hair as she held the belt in one hand. Roxy walked through the door with her purse over her shoulder in a pair of shorts with a 4WW tank top with two boxes underneath her small arms.] Free Fall – Hey champ. Caitlin Fairchild (Sighs.) – Hey… Free Fall – Still bummed? Caitlin Fairchild – A little… Free Fall – Got something that will cheer you right up. [Roxy drops both boxes in front of Caitlin startling her, as she tosses her purse on the plush white suede couch behind her.] Caitlin Fairchild – What the heck are these? Free Fall – Our costumes for the 4WW’s show Diva match, I got them right off of the actual Hollywood studio lot, although I had to get yours altered. Caitlin Fairchild – I’m not doing that… Free Fall – Come on! Why not? Caitlin Fairchild – Let me think…oh…I remember! I got a title match at the 4WW show against Mr. 6 foot 10 inch Zane Ozz…in a Black Catwalk match. Duh! Free Fall – What the hell is a black catwalk match? Caitlin Fairchild – It’s like a scaffold match…only…they use a catwalk. Free Fall – Who the hell thinks up these matches? ANYWAY, as your manager I think you should do this, because one it’s good PR work, and two you’ll have a lot of fun which will pick you up out of that stank mood your in? Caitlin Fairchild – What did you get me? [Roxy smiles as she opened one of the boxes, and pulled out a shiny long sleeved blue shirt with the Superman logo on it, as well as a short red frilly skirt, a red cape with the same Superman logo, underwear, yellow belt, and boots hanging from out the box.] Caitlin Fairchild – You’re kidding right? Free Fall – It’s an exact rep of the one that Slater chick wore for Supergirl… altered to fit you of course. Caitlin Fairchild – You…do know…Supergirl bombed at the movies right? Free Fall – True, but she’s making a comeback in the Justice League cartoons, not to mention, with the passing of Christopher Reeve, it would be a nice homage to him you know. Caitlin Fairchild – I see, and what are you gonna be? [Roxy opens the other box, and pulls out a very tight looking latex rubber out fit with a bat symbol.] Caitlin Fairchild – No…you did not… Free Fall – BOOYAH! Bat Girl! The same one out of the Batman and Robin movie! Caitlin Fairchild – Another movie that bombed… Free Fall – Yeah, but the outfits were tight, and I’d look better in this than that Silverstone trick any day. Caitlin Fairchild – I liked the old Batgirl costume…the one from the 1970’s show. Free Fall – Yeah, but I’m not too cool with the skullcap she had to wear. Caitlin Fairchild (shakes her head.) – What the heck am I talking about…I got a match with a near 7 footer in the upper 200 pound range, who’s last name is Ozz to Pete sakes. I don’t have time to play dress up. [As Caitlin tries to get up, Roxy now irritated pushes her back down.] Caitlin Fairchild – Hey! Free Fall – You’re not training today. Caitlin Fairchild – Why not? Free Fall – Because, you’re not in the right state of mind to train… Caitlin Fairchild – How do you…. Free Fall (Cuts her off.) – Because, I know you…you’re still upset because you’re not the VSE World Heavyweight Champion anymore, no matter how much you hit the weights, or run drills…you’re not going to be a 100 percent for your title match. Caitlin Fairchild – And putting on a costume will? Free Fall – It will ease your mind off of the burden and the pressure you’ve been putting ON yourself for almost a year…for almost a year you help a position very few women has ever had a chance to hold in a wrestling career beating off the BEST in the business, no it didn’t end the way you wanted it to end, no you don’t go on to make history, but it’s over now…you have to accept that…you are NOT the VSE World Heavyweight Champion any more…you’re a former World Champion and the current 4WW South Wind Champion…accept it and move on…until… Caitlin Fairchild – Until? Free Fall – Until your chance comes…once again. [Caitlin sighs, the smirks as she looks up at her partner.] Caitlin Fairchild – I don’t have to dye my hair blonde do I? Free Fall (Smiles back.) – Wasn’t even gonna ask you to. [Caitlin extends her arm, taking Roxy’s as her little friend helps her up to her 7-foot stance.] Caitlin Fairchild – Come on…let’s go see what these get ups look like. [Roxy beams as she grabs her costume and runs in the back, while Caitlin picks up her Supergirl costume, she goes to walk off, but pauses; turns back and picks up her South Wind Champion smiling. She was no longer the VSE World Heavyweight Champion, she was the South Wind Champion, but she would hold the championship in her hand in the same value as the World Championship she once held, which means Ozz would in one hell of a fight. Caitlin slings the belt proudly over her shoulder as she went into the back with her costume.] Free Fall’s voice – You know what we should do when we get dressed up? Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – What? Free Fall’s voice – Head down to Michael Keaton’s house and get an autograph. Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – Does he even live in LA? Free Fall’s voice – Not sure…but I think Val Kilmer does… Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – Better him the Clooney… Free Fall’s voice – Hell yeah…Clooney sucked as Batman. Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – So did Kilmer, but he did play a very good Bruce Wayne. He will forever be ICE MAN in Top Gun to me though. Free Fall’s voice – Hell yeah! If you could do either Batman or Superman…who would you do? Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – Not gonna answer that. Free Fall’s voice – You know what we should do? Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – What? Free Fall’s voice – Run down that Hollywood Boulevard dressed like this. Caitlin Fairchild’s voice – You don’t want us to make it to the show do you? [The 4WW blows by showing a final shot of Caitlin and Roxy’s beach house as the scene fades to black.]
