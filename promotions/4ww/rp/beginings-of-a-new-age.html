Beginings of a new age. Blaise 4WW Exposures #1191 Date: Location: Set the scene. Marsilles Airport A plane, a jet exactly Tail call letters: TSP-003 A Bombadier Global Class GXR Jet. One of there owned by TaleSmith Productions for use by it's higher ranking executives and the family of the owners. This family using it? Patti Anderson-McClancy and her 'family'. Blaise McClancy and Ukyo Kushinaga. As the plane is actually just about half an hour after taking off, over the waters of the Mediteranian Sea, we find Ukyo as she's pulling a blanket over the sleeping form of Blaise McClancy. He's laying in an actual bed on the plane, it having been setup for use as a mobile hotel if needed, and a mobile board room, also iff needed. Ukyo leans down and kisses him softly on the lips before opening the thin door and walking out into the main hold. In front of her are chairs and tables, all airplane type, but setup to make the plane be what it needs to be. She looks on as she sees Patti and Charlene going over some papers for this coming week's show, so that all the legalities are taken care of. She also notices that the in flight cooks have left out the buffet for Patti to get some food if she'd like. Due to Patti's own diet likes, a salad bar is what's predominantly setup. Although she's seen it before, it still amazes her at the efficientcy of the workers in the company who are also part of the Organization. Ukyo looks at what is on the buffet, and a small woman, blonde, with her hair pulled back, wearing what would almost be a french maid's outfit if one would really care to dress like that, tries to get her attention. “Miss? Miss? If you desire something else, I'm sure we could get the chef to prepare it. Mrs. McClancy has a special diet that she ensures is followed. But we do know about Mr. McClancy in the other room and the fact that he's a 'meat and potatoes' type person. So we did come prepared with a full kitchen staff.” Ukyo smiles at the young girl. “Actually the Mr. McClancy in the other room is not the meat and potatoes one. He's the sushi and teriaykki one.” She chuckles. “You did bring the sushi chef right?” The girl looks worried. “Yes, we did. However, I'm planning on someone else joining us, thus why MY Mr. McClancy's menu was also prepared.” Patti had looked up from the papers about using the Palazzo dello Sport for the show, one of the many tasks that she does... not a regular commissioner's job, but one that needs someone who can take care of it assigned to it. “I'm sorry Madame.” The girl responded to Patti. “No, the Salad Bar looks delicious, and there's more then rabbit food here. So I'll have some of this. No need for this short flight.” She grabs a plate and starts to go through the various things on the buffet table. “Ukyo, how's he doing?” She stops as Patti asks the question that she was waiting for. “The medicine is working, he's out like a light. I never realized why we drove or took the train every where in Japan until we had to fly over here. He just hates planes, huh?” “As much as he refuses to admit it, he's highly superstitious.” “What?” Patti chuckles. “When he was 8, a Natvie wise woman in his tribe told him he'd die in a plane crash. Since then he's not been able to get into one without something to put him out since then. He utterly hates them.” Ukyo smiles knowning that the big and bad fiery redhaired man she's come to be with... a man who's first name is Gaelic for “Firebrand”... is afraid of flying. She finishes off her late dinner and then goes over to a single chair with a foldout table in front of it. She sits down, and spins around to look at Patti and Charlene. “You're laughing at him?” Ukyo was taking a bite of her food when Patti asked her that. “Yeah. You'd not think that someone like HIM would be so out of it when it came to flying.” “He's also Tautha and a few other things. Staying on the ground is what he prefers.” Ukyo starts to laugh more. “What?” “I could just imagine his grandfather... He'd be one of those types that would say that if man was meant to fly, some sort of great spirit would have given us wings!” Patti chuckled. “So, how does it feel?” Ukyo suddenly stopped... Patti asked again. “How does it feel?” Finally Ukyo answered. “How does what feel?” Patti sighed slightly as she pushed the papers over towards Charlene, apparently after signing the last one. “A little over two weeks ago you and Blaise went through the Trials of the Shildman. You're no longer hunters, although you had both excelled at that. I've not gotten to talk to you much after I got back. But I have been briefed as to one of your new duties. That's why I called you to the ring when I was going to confront Toddy Boy.” Ukyo just stared at her. “I know, that's why I was ready for him to enter. I'm your protector, I took that duty willingly. But as for how it feels? For me? Not much different. I mean, I figure out how to do a few extra things with the powers I already knew I had. It's nice knowing that I can do it, and all. The big difference was him.” She nodded towards the door, behind which, Blaise was sleeping. “I can guess. I heard that both of you did very well, but he's gone through the biggest change. How are you holding upt to that change? I mean, for all intents and purposes, you've slept with...” “I'm doing fine with it. I kinda-sorta knew before that. I can tell you that after he took out that one bastard, Zadikiel, then rose up not only in his other self-form, but holding Zadikiel's sword, I knew it was over for the trials. He's different, yes, but only to you guys. I need to be there for him, and support him while he's accepting it. Can you imagine though, if he did flip out on the plane and we didn't have him sedated? He could change right in here... not something I'd want.” Patti nodded, knowning all to well from her reports as to just what had happened. “So, have you been able to attune yet?” Ukyo paused and then smiled wide at her. “Yes... I know where you are at all times. To a point. I don't know what you're doing. But I've been told that if you're in trouble, I'll know it. Neither Mr. Or Mrs. Roark will get near you at any of these shows without me being there.” Patti smiled. “What about?” “Yes, he's attuned also. He was the first to do it. As soon as he took the oath in the proper language, they said that he knew where Sean was. He's just not been able to get to where he's at.” “Sean's been busy doing some dealings with the Courts. I've not seen him myself in a few weeks. And yes, it's been a long few weeks. He said he might be coming out to Rome to join us for a few days, but I'm pretty much not counting on it. He's been in Ireland for weeks now, arguing with the others who think they'll make a better king then him.” Ukyo stops eating. “He really shouldn't be messing with the courts without Blaise there.” Patti's face softens a bit. “Yeah, I know. That's why you two went through the trial, to bloody well be there. Eventually someone is going to try to take it physical, and we need to be ready for it.” Suddenly the door to the back compartment opens. “Blaise!” The redhaired man walked out, his eyes locked on a map of the Mediteranian that was hung up on the wall next to the buffet table. He walked over to it, and pulled out one of his spirit knives, a tool that he was allowed to keep after leaving the Hunters of the Order... He reached out and slammed the knife right into a spot in Northern Italy, near Milan. Patti reacted first. “What's up Blaise?” He turned and looked at the women as they stood there. “TSP-002 just landed on an airstrip there. We're landing there soon. Make it happen.” “What?” “Sean's there, and I need to be there too. Get me there, it's a small detour away from Rome, actually a bit closer by a few miles. Just have the Pilot ask where TSP-002 is.” Patti started to look a bit worried, and she got up and headed for the front of the cabin. “Blaise, what's wrong?” “I lost Sean...” “What?” “I lost him when he entered there. He's in a nexus. A rift in the Veil. He's not protected anymore.” At that, Blasie turned around and headed back to the rear part of the cabin, to lay back down while the medication worked on him. Patti returned to the main cabin. “You'll excuse me.” She disappeared to the lavatory, Ukyo noticed that she was messing with her wedding set of antique rings that Sean had given her. Ukyo knew what they could do. Patti was going to go borrow the mirror in the bathroom and use the ring to look in on Sean, something that she had learned was called 'scrying'.
