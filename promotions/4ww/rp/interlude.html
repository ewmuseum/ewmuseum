Interlude Blaise 4WW Exposures #876 Date: 8/15/04 Location: Okinawa Japan Okikawa, Japan, RyuKyu Kingdom as they call it, Japan calls it a Prefectorate Naha City, The capital A lonely cab pulls up to the Okinawa Washington Motel and stops. The door opens and out steps our anti-hero, Blaise McCalncy. Anti-hero? What not like he’s a fan favorite right now. Gone are the days of girls screaming and throwing their panties to him. Then again, he’s not exactly the flamboyant fan favorite who was put up onto a mantle and worshipped anymore. Now he’s just a warrior. What he was meant to be. But the saga of Blaise McClancy continues. As we find ourselves in a place he knows all to well. Okinawa. The city near where he spent the first few years of his training. Just a hop, skip and jump as far as a map goes, from here, is the famed South Wind Academy. One of the 4 acadamies that would start your training on the path of the 4 winds. A path that Blaise has now finished. And the beginning of a new path for him to follow. The cab driver gets out and walks around to the back while Blaise looks up at the tall hotel. One of the best in Naha City, but not quite the best. The stars of the 4WW will be staying here when they all arrive from the show in Korea. But Blaise and his tag team partner were sent ahead to get ready, do some promo work, and also, to keep Ukyo out of Korea due to the current political situations, with her being a Japanese national. But it doesn’t matter to Blaise. Not like he’d want to see StormFront this week anyway with a double main event like it’s got. The cabby pulls out his various bags and puts them onto the luggage cart for him. Two large bags, and a box. Blaise picks up his large duffle bag, not unlike a Naval Sea Bag, and a guitar case, one on each shoulder. A bellhop comes out and takes the luggage cart, pushing it in as Blaise hands the cabby some of the colored bills of yen to him. Then he turns and follows his luggage into the hotel. Walking up to the concierge, he cracks a half smile. “Blaise McClancy, checking in?” The young Japanese man on the other side of the counter looks at him and smiles wide. “Oh, yes Mr. McClancy. We’ve been expecting you. Your room is ready for you, everything is arranged. Here is your key card.” He hands him the flat ‘credit card’ like key that the hotel uses. “And here are some other things that were delieverd for you from your lawyers. Your cousin sends her love and says when you are ready, just sign them and return them to her. The return has already been arranged through the hotel. Just drop them off here.” Blaise looks at the stack of papers and envelopes and sighs. More of his personal life catching up with him. But his ex fiance was after things that she didn’t deserve. Especially after cheating on him. All Blaise wanted was at least partial custody of his twin daughters and for her to go away otherwise. But when a woman thinks she has you over the coals, they don’t let go. Not unlike a shark they are. Escpecially one that smells money. “Oh, and Mr. McClancy, we’ve also got confirmation that you and Ms. Kushinaga will need to be at the Budokkan arena tomorrow at 1400 hours for the publicity and promo shoots for next week’s event. There will be a fan meet and greet and reporters and such.” “Is Ms. Kushinaga here yet?” “Yes sir, she checked in last night with Ms. Kwan. I do belive their out in the shopping district by now. We did get word that if you can handle it, to not hide from the public here.” “I know that. I went to school here and started my wrestling in Southern Japan league.” “Yes, I know that. Tetsuo Oni III if I remember correctly. Don’t worry, I have my tickets for next week already.” “No problems, and thank you.” Blaise just takes the stack and then turns to the bellhop. The bellhop bows to him in the traditional manner and then grabs the luggage cart and starts to push it along. Blaise follows him to the elevator. A few moments later they’re entering his home for the next week and a half, a small hotel room in the middle of the hotels rise. No, he’s not a penthouse kind of guy. Well magazine yes, but staying in one? Why? Not like he’s going to be throwing any parties or gatherings of any sort. He drops a few thousand yen in a tip to the bellhop after his bags are brought in, bows to him and finalizes it with a “Domo Arigato” after that to finish with a proper thank you. The guitar case gets hung form a chair near the window, the duffle bag just flopped down upon the bed. His suitcases and that put on top of the dresser. He quickly opens the duffle bag and starts to pull out a few things and put them on top of the nightstand next to the single bed. His alarm clock, a pair of glasses, a couple of books, tassles from bookmarks coming from their tops, a picture frame of a woman with two young redhaired little girls, the woman’s head has been cut out of the picture…. He looks at the picture longingly and sighs audibly as he tosses the stack of papers down onto the small desk they give their patrons here. “I’m really beginning to hate this shit. Why are you being such a bitch?” He just sighs again and turns away from them, closing the drop door on the desk with the papers inside, sealing them away until he can find time to sit down and look at them. Quickly he takes off his jacket and shirt, appearing to be wanting to get comfortable. He kicks his shoes off towards the hotel door, they land non-chalantly next to the door leaving him in his belted jeans and socks. His barechest, adorned with the red and black oriental dragon that he got 4 years ago while training just a short distance from here, almost devoid of all hair, sets him apart in a large way here. His other tattoos on his shoulders and back glisten as he’s been sweating a little. He pulls up a book, and old, one and leans back onto the bed, moving the pillows with a hand behind him, to give him an upright seating. He grabs the glasses and puts them on, opening the book up. A few seconds later, he takes the glasses off and looks at them. “Damn habits, I don’t even needs these anymore.” He drops the reading glasses into the waste basket that sits between the bed and the nightstand. A dull thud comes from it, as well as the rustling of the plastic bag. He looks at the book again, and then just folds it up and puts it back onto the nightstand. He grabs the remote control and clicks the tv on. Quickly he finds the channel he wants as the Japanese edition of StormFront from this last week plays, coming on at the end of the Kris Kanton and Dark Angel match. From the TV he watches as the final moments of that match play… {Video from StormFront of 8/10/04 Morales: Anything can happen now! Both men are even! It's anyone's match now! Candler: Nope, truly see a new champion! [Kris looks around his surroundings and begins to think a bit though smirks devilishly as an idea pops in mind. He leans back a bit while holding the top rope though soon hops up and was on the top rope for a brief second as the out of breath Derek was turning around to face Kris. Kris leaps off of the top rope.SUDDEN.NOOO.DEREK AVOIDS KRIS' MOVE BY DUCKING!!!] Morales: Derek avoided it! Candler: What! Damn it! [Kris lands on his feet with his back facing Derek and begins to turn around.DIVINE MERCY.OUT OF NOWHERE DEREK CONNECTS WITH THE DIVINE MERCY ON KRIS!!! KRIS HITS THE MAT HARD AS DEREK DROPS DOWN AND MAKES THE COVER!!! The ref drops down and begins the count.] .1 .2 Candler: Kris kick out! Kris kick out, damn it! .3!!! [The ref signals for the bell.] [DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!] Tania Brown: Your winner and still your South Wind Champion.DEREK DEANGELO!!! Morales: What a great match! Candler: What are you talking about! Derek still won!} “Kanton, what a fucking ‘tard. He telegraphs more moves then Steve Bruce ever did. Prick is still probably steaming after my little surprise for him last week.” He watches as the match ends and Darek DeAngelo plays to the crowd just a little bit as he proclaims himself still South Wind Champion. Kanton just stares up at him from the mat as the announcers take over. “Shit, he’s been thrown to the wolves twice, and he doesn’t even realize it. First Patti now Darek. Next thing you know he’ll be facing Kyo. He’s become a new bitch.” He half laughs with the last statement as there is a knock on his door. He gets up and cracks the door open. A small Japanese woman wearing the uniform of the bellhops in the hotel. She appears to not speak too much Enlish. “Excruse me, but rady with long red hair.” Blaise pulls the door open some more, and the woman gets all wide eyed with the half naked man standing there. Blaise responds speaking the Okinawan dialect of Japanese like he was a pro at it. {“Fey Childress sent you with what?”} The woman looks at him speaking her language and then holds out a large manilla envelope. {“She said to give you this for signing for next week, then to return it.”} {“Thank you”} He takes the envelope and opens it, pulling the papers contined within it out. He looks at the first page and laughs out loud. “Kris Kanton, winner will be declared the number one contender for the South Wind Title? Damn. Guess me and Darek are going to dance again one more time before Maelstrom V.” He looks at the girl and she pulls out a pen. He takes it from her, uncapps it with his teeth and then signs the contract on the appropriate line. He quickly recaps the pen and then hands it back to the girl. He repackages the paperwork into the envelope and hands it back to her also, along with a piece of colored money from his front jeans pocket. “Kanton…. Shit, Maeb must really have him in the dog house. First Patti almost kills him, and I drop him on his face outside the ring, now Darek murders him in the ring, and now he’ll get me? Shit… he won’t even make Tokyo. What’s he going to do to me? Oh, that’s right bore me to death. Him and the rest of those damn unStable morons are about as entertaining as new york mimes.” He winces a bit as on the TV, the announcemts of Chris West’s victory over Bullet is announced. Looking up he sees Eddie B standing there with him. “Damn, talk about career suicide. You should have stayed retired instead of joining with those idiot. Sure as hell would have allowed you to have gone out with your head held high.” As he shuts the door, he just looks up at the TV and then turns it off, just as the in ring celebration is still on it. “Well another StormFront down, and to think, it could have been worse. It could have had Jerms appear on it…” The TV goes off even before the final segment where Jerms and Mike Biddle put out their plans for Maelstron V. Blaise looks around the hotel room and then at the desk that currently houses a reminder of his real life problems right now. Another sigh escapes his slightly parted lips. “I need to clear my head. Hell, this is almost a homecoming for me. Time to get the hell out and stop worrying about everything else. I wonder if Charlie’s is having a slow night or not?” At that he opens one of his suitcases and grabs a t-shirt. As he unfolds it and slides it on, the flying dagger logo of the band, Queensryche is evident in a silver color on top of black. He reaches back into the suitcase and grabs a case, about 2 by 4 by 30 inches in size with a small shoulder strap. He puts it over his shoulder and then slides his feet into his white gym shoes, pocketing his wallet and room key quickly from the top of the desk near the door on the way out. The door shuts softly, and the scene shifts to that of the outside of a building. The building, advertising American brands of alcoholic beverages in it’s windows, appears to be some sort of public house or bar establishment. The sound of music comes from it’s door as someone steps out, well more like staggers, and heads down the street. The man is a caucasion, and his short haircut coupled with the sound of loose dog tags mark him as one of the local members of the United States Marine Corp that is stationed on the island. As the man stumbles down the road, appearing to be heading in the general direction he thinks he needs to go, two women walk up. One of them is Japanese with blue hair. She’s wearing a pair of bellbottom blue jeans with chains forming almost a ladder down the sides. Along with two shirts, the one under being tighter fit then the one over. The under shirt, a white sleeveless number, and the over shirt a black ‘babydoll’ with the straps falling down off her shoulders. The black babydoll has a typical graphic for skull and crossbones design on it, and it actually flashes in a strobe of lights every couple minutes. The other girl is dressed similarly, but shes doing the miniskirt and neon colored shirts type thing. Her red-orange hair marks her a close compatriot of the other, except she’s obviously not Japanese, but definatly asian… Chinese to be exact. It would look like two ‘ravers’ out for a night on the town, with the excetion that these two are two of the 4WW’s stars, Ukyo Kushinaga and Charlene Kwon. Charlene looks to be about ready to run away, not normally dressing like this, but the slightly older Ukyo appears to be keeping her under control, and more then likely the cause of her current genre of dress. “Come on Ukyo. You’ve not been able to get into any bars tonight, I don’t even know why you took me out with you.” Ukyo looks at the younger girl and smirks. “Because you look like you need a drink as much as I do with the current situation with my brother. So you’re out for a night before all the work we have to do tomorrow. Hell it appears Blaise is late. We’ll need something to relax while we’ll take care of all the promo shots tomorrow.” “Well you and your brother are the ‘local’ stars by the grace of being Japanese. So you two should be the main one’s advertised. I got the fax from before we left as to who he’d be facing this next week. If he wins, it’ll solidify his spot in the top contendership. I need to be helping him get prepared for this.” Ukyo keeps looking forward down the road at the bar with the American Music and signs. “Well, I plan on getting a drink, and if I don’t have my ‘adult supervision there’ I might get in trouble. You want to deal with that? I mean, not like Kyo even bothered to get you when he signed that contract to face Patti Anderson at the Tokyo Dome.” Chrarlene sighs at that, Kyo had gone behind her back when she was the business major and was technically his manager. But he had gone behind her back and signed to face the top dog of the 4WW in Patti Anderson is his continued quest for redemption against the Shadows Ascension. Tarrant was now gone, he'd left shortly after Sean McClancy showed up the night that Ukyo had shown her loyalty to her tag team partner, Blaise. Nothing had much been the same since then. “Oh, my. This is the plance there Charlene.” Charlene looked up and saw what Ukyo had been looking for. Even for being Japanese herself, Ukyo had a taste for American beer. The blinking neon Budweiser sign with two styleized old world chameleons and some furry weasel or ferret shown in the window of the bar with the music playing. “Ukyo, you know I can't go in there.” “You just follow my lead, okay?” Ukyo grabbed Charlene by the arm and almost drug her over to the front door. Upon approaching it a large man, bald headed and with a full beard, steps out. He's wearing a sleeveless t-shirt with a “USMC: Uncle Sam's Misguided Children” logo upon it. His obvious former affiliation showing there. He stands there and crosses his arms looking at the 6' tall Ukyo and the much smaller Charlene. “Ukyo, well are you the welcoming committee?” “Yeah, I'm the guy who plays it safe around here. The locals aren't too hot on a Gaijen bar being located here. I keep the boys inside playing the way their supposed to, or I bust their heads and then put them into custody of the SP's. Especially some of the squidlys when we have a ship come in for a visit.” “Cool. Well, we're here for a little bit of R&R ourselves. How about you just let us head on in. I'm sure the guys wouldn't mind something other then other guys to look at.” “I'm sure they wouldn't mind, but I need to make sure we'd not have problems with the local authorities.” Charlene's face goes flush, knowing this means the guy wants to check their ID's. Ukyo cuts the guy off. “I take it you don't even recognize us then?” “Why should I?” “I take it you're not a wrestling fan then?” At that point the guy's eyes go narrow as he looks both girls over. Then suddenly a smile of recognition comes upon his face. “I'll be damned.” Ukyo smiles at him, a wide smile of white teeth. Chrarlene continues to look nervous. “Well, Ms. Kushinaga, didn’t expect you people to be in town yet. All the boys from the bases have been given special deals on the event this week. I’ve picked up a few seats myself. “ “Well, seeing as how now you recognize us, how about you let us come on in and hide from the masses. I’m looking for a place that has a more American taste then the usual fair around.” “By all means, as long as you autograph Charly’s bar top. He tries to get as many people as possible to do that, who come in here. Well the celebs.But isn’t she only…” Ukyo chimes in. “Please, do you think the 4WW, a fed that has it’s roots with the Southern Japan leagues, have a real 17 year old girl traveling to other countries and doing the things we do?” The man looks at her. “Nah, just like some of those actresses. She just looks young. Come on in.” The big man opens the door and steps in, holding the door open for the ladies. They walk in to a bar that’s filled with smoke, and the smell of beer all over the place. Quite a few men are here, mostly Americans. Air Force, Marines, all types of the American service men who live on the island, all in their best ‘civies’ for going out on the town. Off in the corner are two pool tables, and in another a dart board. A jukebox, reletivly new sits near the far end of the bar. A middle aged, and half balding Japanese man stands behind the bar cleaning some of his glasses. The bouncer walks up behind the bar and whispers something into the bartender’s ear. The man then turns and points at Ukyo and Charlene. The bartender smiles at them and then waves them over. “Good evening ladies. Welcome to Charly’s. I’m Charly Wang, your host. I understand you’re out for a more descrete outing here. What can I get you ladies to drink? It’s on the house.” The man has a heavy… American Accent…Charlene chimes in. “No need to do that, we can pay.” “I’ll take a Budweiser. From the tap.” With that, Charley pulls out a frosted mug and fills it up for Ukyo. “I’ll take the same thing.” Comes from Charlene, and another mug is taken out and filled. “Now, would you ladies mind?” Charley reaches out with a large marker looking pen. He shakes it to aggitate the white paint inside it and hands it, uncapped to Ukyo. “Right there on the bar. You might as well put it right here.” He points to a spot and Ukyo quickly starts to write her name. She finishes and hands it to Charlene who adds her name to it. As Charlene finshes, she notices something. “Hey, who’s autograph is that?” She points to a small signature, written ina black paint marker, instead of white. It’s a single name: Blaise. “Oh, that’s your tag partner’s. He trained at the academy about 10 miles from here. His Irish blood brought him here almost every night to listen to some music, play some pool, rest his body from being beaten up all day and suckle on some beer to help him forget it.” Ukyo looks at the small signature, almost hidden, and starts to get a slight far away look in her eyes. “Alright, you’re doing that more and more lately.” Charlene turns around and looks more at the guys in the bar. As Charley walks away to cover another patron, Ukyo continues to look at the signature. “You’re spacing on me, Ukyo. Come on, you got your drink. Get done and then let’s go back to the hotel. You’ve got all that promotional stuff to do tomorrow.” “Sorry, Char. Just kinda strange that this is the bar we’d end up in.” “It’s the only American Bar that’s far away from the bases. Where would you end up at? You hate Saporo and Ki-Rin beer.” At that, the door opens. Ukyo turns her head a bit and looks over to find her tag team partner arriving at his old ‘watering hole’. He walks up and shakes the bartender’s hand, and reaches up and pats him on the shoulder. Charley reaches over and grabs a mug, fills it with one of the other taps and then hands it right into Blaise’s hands. She sits there watching him as he walks over to one of the pool tables. “Well I’ll be damned.” “When did he get here?” “I don’t know. But he apparently didn’t see us.” Blaise walks over and sets his glass down onto one of the coasters on the side of the open pool table. He then places the black case down and opens it, revealing the two pieces of a rather nice looking pool cue. He works the two parts together and then starts to work on setting up the table when a couple of the Service men walk over. “Hey, you play?” Blaise looks up at the guy. “Yeah. A little. When I get the chance.” The man is wearing a t-shirt for one of the airwings in the area. His friend looks to be there for more then moral support. “You play for money?” Blaise looks up at the man a bit more sternly. As he does, he gets a glimpse over the man’s shoulder and sees Charlene and Ukyo off in their little corner. “How much?” The man just looks at him. “Hundred. American.” “You got it?” The man seems to be taken slightly aback from Blaise’s assertiveness there. “Yeah, I got it.” He pulls out his wallet and takes out a crisp, fresh from the base credit union, 100 dollar bill and slaps it down on the table under one of the chalks. “Done.” Blaise pulls out his wallet and making it look a little better, does it as five 20 dollar bills. “Rack ‘em up.” As Blaise then starts to get the balls ready for play, Ukyo gets up from her seat and starts to walk over to the pool table. “Well, didn’t expect to find you here.” Blaise tries to look her in the eyes, but decides it’s best to just watch what he’s doing. “This is my home away from home here. I went to school just a little down the road, towards the outskirts of the city. I tended to stand out in other bars around here.” Ukyo walks over and grabs another stool next to the table. She puts her own mug down and watches Blaise as he sorts and sets up for the break. “You breaking or am I?” “I’ll break.” Blaise backs off, taking the rack off the balls. He watches as the man breaks and sinks the #5 ball. He then goes after the #3 ball, but fails to sink it. “Good Break.” Blaise says it as he’s eyeing an easy setup on the #14. As Blaise lines his cue up, he notices that Ukyo has gone to leaning on the pool table, her elbows on the edge, her hands up on her cheeks. Needless to say, the loose shirts leave little for the imagination if you’re trying to look. Blaise shoots and sinks the #14 and then starts to look around for his second shot. As he does, he looks over at his opponents. One has taken a position not too far from Ukyo. The other is on the other side, taking the look right down her shirt. Blaise looks at him, then her, then back to him and lines up a shot at the cue ball that looks to be targeting the #6 ball. He pulls back and looks to be trying to line it up and then… **whack** He jabs the cue right back into the guy’s thigh. The cue ball gets stuck and sinks the #6 ball with a clunk. The guy bends over, grabbing his thigh. “What the fuck? You stupid!” “Oh, sorry. Lining up my shot and guess it was a little close there.” The guy glares at him as he walks over to stand next to his friend. Blaise starts to look at the table top and try to pick out his third shot. Charlene clears he throat, loudly. Blaise looks up and sees that the two guys are whispering between each other. One is making motions to the backside of Ukyo. The way she’s leaning, from an angle, Blaise can see that he is making obvious remarks about the dark blue bikini cut panties that are showing out the back of her pants, from where she’s bending. Blaise looks at them as they start to make facial expression in Ukyo’s direction. Apparently Ukyo was oblivious to Charlene’s throat clearing. Blaise lines up on the cue again, apparently not even worrying about where he’s aiming it at… he strikes, and the ball hops from the pool table… and…. Slams right into the side of the head of the main player! “What the fuck? I’m going to kick your ass boy!” Ukyo gets up as she looks at the guy, the ball is on the ground now, but there is a red mark on the side of his face. As he stands there holding the side of his face, looking to be checking for any bleeding, Charlene interjects… in her native Cantonese. “{I don’t believe he did that. He is being so damn blatant. Is she not seeing it?}” Charlene stops when she notices Blaise giving her a knowing look. Quickly she steps away from the guys, as they start to curse at Blaise. Blaise pulls his cue up, gripping it in the middle. “How about we just say, you two take your money and leave? Before Chrarley calls the MP’s?” “What? You assaulted me man. I’m going to…” Charlene comes back, this time with the large bouncer and door guard in tow. “Hey, what’s going on around here Blaise? Got a couple of flyboys who need to get grounded?” The two look at the larger man and then apparently decide that it’s best if they don’t proceed. “You had better consider yourself lucky.” They reach over and take the 6 bills from under the chalk. The bouncer turns to follow them out. “Actually boys, you’re lucky I’m here to protect you. That’s one mean motherfucker over there, and you’re trying to mess with his lady. I’d advise going back to base or one of the bars closer to the main gate for a few days.” Charlene looks over at Ukyo, who now has a “What the fuck” look on her face, overhearing the statement from the bouncer. Ukyo gets up and grabs Charlene by the arm. “C’mon Char, let’s go back to the hotel.” Charlene looks back at Blaise, as she’s pulled from the bar by Ukyo. Blaise just stands there with the pool cue in his hands. As Ukyo and Charlene walk out of the bar… “What the fuck was that all about?” Charlene just doesn’t answer her….She just keeps walking along.
