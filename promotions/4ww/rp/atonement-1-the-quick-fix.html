Atonement 1 : The Quick Fix

Atonement 1 : The Quick Fix Slayer 4WW Exposures #1007 Date: 10/15/04 Location: Somewhere in Toronto [People in general are all the same. We breath the same air. We eat the same things. We drink the same things...and when something needs to be done, we all tend to want it done quickly and correctly. Most of the time, we're willing to ignore quality, so long as it's just done fast. It holds true for most everything, people want money quick and easy...they rob someone or something...people want to lose weight quick and easy...and we have a billion dollar a year diet industry. So and so forth. We like people like results as quickly as possible, I guess there isn't anything wrong with it, but sometimes the road less traveled is the only one you're going to find answers on.] [But perhaps we should slow down before entering all of that.] [Am I Slayer?] [No] [Am I Cy Quiñones?] [No] [Rather I am the narrator. Plain and simple. My association with Slayer is about as great as my friendship with any of you, non-existent. I am merely an unbiased view A voice with a clear perspective on things. I do not root for Cy Quiñones to win, and I do not look at him as anything more then a human being. Consider me an omnipotent being, one that happens to know all, see all, and experience all. I am not hear to humanize the former monster, anymore then I am to turn him into more of a madman then he considers himself.] [I am the voice of his story...and only that] [And his Story] [Begins now.] Atonement 1: The Quick Fix ----------------------------------------------------------- [Remember what that spiel about quick fixes? Well, for those of you a step below the average intelligence bar, or those of you who happen to be blind. Slayer is in fact a human being, and like most humans, he to desires a quick fix with his current problem. Granted, his problem isn't as cut and paste as someone with a broken bone, or someone whose car just died. Those can be solved with a quick visit to a doctors office or a car dealership...maybe even a mechanic. Slayer on the other hand, is not so sure his problem can even be solved. It's a rather touchy subject, how does one seek redemption for his past crimes...how does one seek atonement for a life like Slayer has lived? Send money? Apologies? Stop Wrestling? Suicide? Maybe the first two, but never the third and fourth. So like most people, he's going for the quick fix. Rather then focus on those that he once hurt, he's trying to make himself feel better...trying to find a way that he'll be more at peace with himself. The way he sees it, he has two choices. The first is the first one that came to mind. It's probably pretty apparent if you think about it for a moment. Go ahead, take a second or ten. Yes, that's right, a psychiatrist. Where else would one go to deal with mental anguish problems? Who better to visit to then to a guy who charges seventy-eight dollars a hour to talk to? Granted he won't do much talking back, occasionally quips here and there. But it holds a certain degree of comfort of the second choices head. Upon graduation, most psychiatrists are handed a diploma, and a leather plush couch. When you're a guy like Slayer? You've seen you're fair share of psychiatrists, you've sat on your fair share of couches, and you've had to handle more then enough ink-blot tests...And above all else, you've dropped a fair amount of coin onto the problem. The interesting thing is, that while most of the time. Granted, he just can't walk into an office and demand an appointment. But being in Ontario means that he's only a hop skip and a jump away from his old 'stomping grounds' in the EWA....Toronto. And then there is the second. Where would most people without the money go to absolve themselves? This one...should be a bit easier to figure out then the shrink. Well, that is if you are of faith. Church. Is Slayer religious? No. Not at all. In fact, he can't even remember what religion his parents claim to be. Catholic, Christian...one of those 'big ones'. Once as a child Slayer went to church...once before a match with Chris Davison he went, and a third time he went right before a match with a man named Power. Three times in his life....twice have been to talk to someone in confessional. After all, what better way to live a guilt free to be a clean man in the eyes of god(s). So though they aren't even close to each other, or even on the same highway off ramp. Same town. Same city....hardly even in the same state...still though, Slayer finds himself standing at a metaphysical cross-roads, trying to decide what to do. In his mind. Slayer thinks that perhaps a well placed word or two might cleanse his dirty soul, and relieve him of some of the stress he's been going through lately. His mind has been working overtime to realize why he feels guilty for things that are so old. Maturity? Nah. The World Title? That would be childish.....perhaps. But to him, it feels like more then that. Lately he's been having trouble putting it into words just how bad he feels. That's why he feels the need to have someone else examine him...step into his head (figuratively)....and tells him what's wrong. A Priest or a psychologist. Science or Church. He rations that he should go with the psychologist, because a Priest probably won't give him the answers that he's looking for. That and....Right now, the eyes of 'god ' are a bit much for him to handle What happens next is a three hour road trip by bus. Nothing much, I won't bore you with the details. But for those interested, he sat near the back of the bus..a little old lady wouldn't stop talking in the seat ahead of him, and a twelve year old boy got motion sickness..twice. The lives some of the 4WW wrestlers lead, eh? So....now we're hours later...and miles away from when we originally started. Some of you might be going...why is walking around Toronto looking for answers to a question that can't be defined? When he has a match against the Ozz borthers in the West Title tournament? Well, if you were to ask Slayer what he thought about that? Forced all those questions upon the former King of Xtreme...he'd probably answer something very similar to this 'shut the fuck up'. It's the Ozz brothers...not Kyo Kushinaga or even the Future Shock....it's the god damn Ozz brothers. So there is definitely time to stop and smell the flowers today. It's outside a small brick building...occasionally the bottom half is brick, and the top half is plastic side paneling, blue in color, but bleached light blue due to all the sun hitting it. It's been a three mile walk since Slayer got off the bus. But regardless, most of that is in the past him now. He's at the house of Nicholas D. Shanning, PHD. Slayer stares at the building, and then removes a hand from it's pocket. In it is a somewhat damp business card. The numbers on the house match up with the address of Nicholas home office. Slayer pockets it again, and slowly approaches the door, praying that Shanning doesn't own a gun...You have no idea how many people will draw a gun on you when you walk up to the door looking the way he does. He approaches quickly, and gives a few loud knocks on the door, on a glass part, but manages to refrain from putting his hand through it. Seconds pass by, before a tiny man in green sweater vest and dockers comes to the door. He looks timidly up at Slayer, and does something Slayer hasn't seen anyone do towards him in awhile... Shanning...smiles at him. Unlatching the door lock, Nicholas opens the door, and extends a hand. 'Always got to lock the door' Nicholas says as he finishes up the handshake with Slayer and hurries him inside 'You just never know what kind of crazy person could be out walking around'. Under his breath, Slayer mutters the word 'cute', but decides that the joke is just that...a joke, all in good fun. The larger man follows behind Shanning, eyeing the house...looking at all the little knick knacks and such a semi-wealthy man has amassed in a life time, next to most of them are pictures of nephews and nieces, of grandchildren and other family members fill shelves...the stuff you'd expect to find in a normal home. Cy doesn't have much time to look at them though, as Shanning quickly hurries him into the living room. While the size difference between the two men is great, Nicholas orders around the large wrestler without so much as giving it a second thought. Pointing to the couch, Shanning motions for Slayer to sit down. The large frame of Cy Quiñones covers up most of the couch, and the old metal frame of it creaks under his massive weight. Shanning takes a seat directly across from him, on a small rocking chair, and just looks at the man in his living room for a moment 'So how long has it been Cy?' 'Three years..maybe four' 'And how are you doing today?' Slayer leans forward, places his elbows on his knees and rests his jaw in his hands. He lets loose a small sigh, not of annoyance, or anger, but almost of indifference. “Good, though it's debatable....' 'Ah yes, and why is that?' Why is that? The question just flows through Slayer' head with all the finesse of an atomic bomb. Why is it debatable...that he feels good? How about because a few years ago, he took a girl that had nothing to do with the business, and dropped her on her head for a rise out of someone. How he had to watch as she laid there with blood just pouring from the back of her head. Or better yet, how about how he lead a group of fellow wrestlers into the EWA, and let them all be slaughtered, but somehow he remained untouched. He was the Judas to their group, he felt like he'd betrayed them without so much as lifting a finger against them. Or how about the wrestler simply known as Ian...whose total EWA career lasted only about three weeks....why? Because Slayer felt that he was no longer needed in the EWA, and simply ended him. Dropped him from a height of twenty feet, and shattered the man's knees....'good lord' Slayer thinks...'He's only twenty-five, twenty-six, he should have the rest of his life ahead of' 'Who did' 'Ah...was I thinking out loud?' Shanning leans back in his chair...the grin that has covered his face since Slayer arrived now slowly fades away into a stern look, realizing that Slayer most likely ISN'T here for a social visit. Instead of trying to be Nicholas Shanning the friend, he quickly turns into Doctor Shanning, P.H.D. Stopping the chairs rocking motion, Shanning looks at Slayer 'Yes...you were....now who had the rest of their life ahead of him?' Slayer cringes slightly...and the look in his eye quickly becomes distant, zoned out....as if he's no longer staring at anything in the room, just off into space. Quiñones opens his mouth, but quickly closes it, like he was about to stop a word that was about to escape...that he just knew wouldn't sound right. 'Well...it's a funny story' 'is it really?' '.....no.....but I suppose I should tell it anyway.' Slayer leans back in his seat again, and looks up at the ceiling...it's that spackled type deal that you often look at, and if it was close enough you'd probably try and touch it. He takes a second to think about the past, recollect everything he can about Ian....and as he does he begins to tell the tale. 'It goes back a long way actually...the EWA. The two of us were some of the first guys to sign contracts with the Extreme Wrestling Alliance, and I think that's why the two of us were put into the first ever main event on the show. A television title battle royal. He wasn't the kind of guy I wanted anything to do with, you know the type...a polar opposite, but not in the sort of 'opposites attract sort of way'. Everything about him bugged the hell about me, the way he'd stumble through words, the way he'd cockily walk around the arena's because his ego went unchecked by anyone for a few weeks. I was really looking forward to embarrassing him in the ring, one of the few times I've ever stepped into the ring with the goal to just flat out embarrass a wrestler, make him look as piss poor as possible. Could I beat him? Yeah, I know I could. I've seen him before, warm up matches, exhibition stuff before the federation even started. He's one of those guys that strive for greatness, but no matter what they do, they always sit in mediocrity, a self placed glass ceiling on success if you will... so that first week, I sat there and took everything he had to say, I sat there and listened to every single piece of garbage he could feed me. How bad he was going to beat me, how horrible I was, how great he was....all that typical bullshit that I'd expect someone in third grade to say, not a full grown adult. I would say a few things to him hear and there, but for the most part, I just wanted to ignore him...I knew the truth, how things sized up...boxers don't pick fights with people in the stand when they shout 'I could kick your ass'...and this was about the equivalent. Could I beat him? Yeah...did I? No.....Other people got involved, some guy named Bill Carns, and a steel chair...Ian won the television championship, and I went on to win the Heavyweight Title...Honestly, in the end, everything did turn out okay for me. But he just wouldn't shut up about that battle royal, he just wouldn't stop talking about how he beat me, kept poking me and poking me until I'd finally give him a shot at my belt. I did, and I beat the hell out of him. I proved finally that I was the better man..and I finally felt at piece about things.. Ian feel off the face of the earth after that, I don't even remember what happened, because so long as I beat him...he finally shut up. He was gone, I was happy, all was right with the world... Then he joined the SCW. He tried to before, I'd been told by numerous people that he'd attempted to get into the federation...Shawn Collins told me once back when he was in charge that he looked at the contract Ian sent, laughed, and tore it into pieces. He kept trying till he finally made it in...and he went right back to his old game..just poking me and poking me, trying to use my name for a stepping stone to make himself something in the federation. I felt the pressure, but I just bent, decided to leave him alone, and maybe he'd just go away....maybe he'd just shut the fuck up for a change, and accept the fact tht he isn't anywhere near my league....but instead he just started getting worse, until he finally flat out threatened to kill me. Of course...I'd heard bullshit like that before, but for some reason when he said it..I snapped...I just went nuts. I didn't want to do what I did...I didn't even mean to do what I did. It's like someone else just control of me, and I couldn't do anything about it. I found him walking into the arena, and broke a few windows with his face, until the blood was so thick on his face you couldn't even tell who he was...I tried to stop after that, I wanted to stop after that, but no matter what I did...I just kept telling myself 'it's not enough. more, more more...' I walked around with him on my shoulder for about a half hour, occasionally just ramming him into stuff, hoping that I'd cool down and just drop him off somewhere. instead I wound up on top of the SCW-tron' For a moment Shanning interrupts, never one to be able to sit down and talked about 'the business' he finds one little set of words puzzling' The SCW tron' 'Eh..that's right, you don't watch...It's a big screen that lets people in the nose bleed seats see what's happening on tv...So I wound up there, and with a really long chain. It's an old trick I learned in the EWA from another wrestler, you lop a note around his arms and then loop another around the back of his neck, and when you hang them, they look like they've been crucified...what with the name and all, he thought it'd make for a neat visual...good for 'match heat'....I hooked Ian in, but never hooked the other end to anything else...and without so much as a second thought, I kicked him off the tron...he didn't fall, not at first....I managed to step on the end of the chain and hold him there for a few moments...I let him dangle there...just over the metal ramp...and not for one second did I think I was doing something I shouldn’t. the idea of wrong never passed through my head....so I dropped him to the ramp...a steel ramp with no give at all. his legs buckled out right away and he laid there as I looked down, like some vengeful god. It took a few weeks for me to find out what happened to the poor son of a bitch. If I wanted to know, I could have found out the next day, but eventually word traveled through the grapevine enough for me to find out. Both his knees shattered, tendons and muscles just peeled away from the bone and such He was in crutches for a bit, but he can walk now.....but wrestler? Probably never again, and even if he was a stupid bastard, he still loved the business and such, he would have been one of those guys that just couldn't walk away from it...he's the one that would have stuck around forever, and eventually..he probably would have amounted to something...I didn't want to do it...but I just couldn't help it, no matter what I tried that night, I just couldn't stop myself.... In the end, I tried to cripple someone who loved to wrestle....because he annoyed me, he didn't do anything to me but say stupid shit...and I took away from him what he loved the most....' '....And how does that make you feel' '......' 'Cy?' 'Truthfully?' 'Yeah' 'Like shit....up until a week or two ago, I didn't care, I couldn't even muster a damn tear for the guy, but now? It feels like someone has stomped on my heart and kicked my soul right in the nuts.' 'Well then...has anything new happened lately...something big, that you could think of that would cause this change?' 'Just a bit' Slayer doesn't really say that, he just thinks that...and this time he's sure he didn't Instead of Stop. Have things changed. Yes. More then Slayer can even understand. Cheers. Relationships. Feelings. All of that in more. Slayer finds himself struggling to climb to the top of a hole...but every time he grasps onto something that will pull him out...he just winds up falling further down....he thinks of one person, and how he owes them an apology, and it makes him think of someone else. Therese...Ian...Michael...the list goes on and on for Slayer.... For most of his career he has been the monster...the villian..that which much be hated...and now? Now it's time for him to pay for his sins. Did the conversation go on? Yeah, it did. Nothing to important, they talked family, current events, business for awhile..silly idle chit chat that you have with someone when you want to end the conversation, but you feel rude doing so. But the point is, even Slayer' 'quick fix' isn't what he was looking for. Essentially all the trip managed to do was bring up another memory that Slayer would have liked better left buried...and instead he dug up more self loathing. In the end, Slayer knew that he probably wouldn't feel any better about things, short of having himself hypnotized. But there's always the chance of a long shot..but like most things, it was just a waste of time. Two hours later, Slayer did leave, albeit without really accomplishing anything more then working up a sweat with a long walk. Oh yeah, and opening pandora's box in his own head. A total waste of a day off. Slayer left a few hours later, Shanning did give him a ride back to the bus stop...and so life went on for both. But perhaps, the day wasn't a total waste, Slayer did manage to discover that his problems weren't going to be solved with a few medical terms in a book. Yet...there was one thing, he did say that struck a chord with Cy One little sentence 'Sometimes Cy...there just isn't anyway to forgive or forget' ...and the truth goes...bang
