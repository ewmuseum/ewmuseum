Break In Reality El Lobo de la Muerta 4WW Exposures #156 Date: 9-26-03 Location: Streets of Pheonix how can you see into my eyes like open doors leading you down into my core where I’ve become so numb without a soul my spirit sleeping somewhere cold until you find it there and lead it back home (Wake me up) Wake me up inside (I can’t wake up) Wake me up inside (Save me) call my name and save me from the dark (Wake me up) bid my blood to run (I can’t wake up) before I come undone (Save me) save me from the nothing I’ve become now that I know what I’m without you can't just leave me breathe into me and make me real bring me to life (Wake me up) Wake me up inside (I can’t wake up) Wake me up inside (Save me) call my name and save me from the dark (Wake me up) bid my blood to run (I can’t wake up) before I come undone (Save me) save me from the nothing I’ve become frozen inside without your touch without your love darling only you are the life among the dead all this time I can't believe I couldn't see kept in the dark but you were there in front of me I’ve been sleeping a thousand years it seems got to open my eyes to everything without a thought without a voice without a soul don't let me die here there must be something more bring me to life (Wake me up) Wake me up inside (I can’t wake up) Wake me up inside (Save me) call my name and save me from the dark (Wake me up) bid my blood to run (I can’t wake up) before I come undone (Save me) save me from the nothing I’ve become (Bring me to life) I’ve been living a lie, there’s nothing inside (Bring me to life) Ñª¥ Bring Me to Life; Evanescence Ñª¥ ~Why do we carry our heads down low when the battle becomes unbearable? The source comes over and over again making sure that we do not become soft. We tend not to look at the gruesome facts that exist only because we fear that they, in time, will consume us. We cannot let these fears overcome our drive and ability to fight and survive. We have to fight all of our ghosts and battle our demons. We need to be at peace with ourselves before we can find peace and solitude in anything else. If you look closely life is patiently waiting for you to realize your path and take it. There are no guarantees in this game; you have to continue on being strong in life. You need to move on and get passed this little hill that stands before you. We cannot be trapped in the past, we need to get by and carry on. You want to go home, but it’s a strange thing. Because you don’t realize that home isn’t where you belong. ~ {The scene opens up as the sounds of rain and thunder surrounding the area. With the thunder rolling loudly off in the distance a bright light flashes around as we see a man wearing black baggy jeans and a black hoody skating down the streets. With water splashing out from the wheels as he continues on carelessly into the night. In the sky we see that the moon is blocked by the dark clouds overhead; however we can see that the moon is starting to go into its last cycle of the month. Coming back down to earth the man on the skate board slowly comes to a stop as he slides at the edge of the sidewalk. He stops on the foot of the skateboard forcing the head to come to his hands. He lifts the board up to his side and walks across the street as cars fly past him spraying water in his face. El Lobo stops and wipes his face and walks on across the street.} El Lobo: Aye como fue mi cabeza? Donde esta mi amour? Donde esta yo? So many things are going so wrong right now that I can’t take move on anymore. I just need one thing to hold on to that is all I need to pull me through this. Mi bebe esta muerto, mi novia esta en el hospital, y mi titulo de parillos no esta mio. Mi amigo menor, mi hermano, mi primo creen que no tengo su espalda. And I’m going to the doctors to figure out what the hell is wrong with me. Maybe they can help me figure out what to do with my life since I fucked up every little thing that I held close. I killed all of them, I killed my dreams. I killed my unborn child, I sent Angél to the hospital. It’s all my fault, I know it is. I don’t need anyone to tell me that I did it. I know I did why do they keep staring. They won’t leave me alone I wish they would all go away. Why don’t you people just leave me alone don’t you understand that I’m sorry for what I’ve done? Can’t you see that I regret every thing I’ve done can’t you see just look at me. I SAID LOOK AT ME DAMMIT!!! {El Lobo starts screaming very loudly in Spanish too fast to actually realize what is being said. He charges across the street and starts punching a door furiously. He stops and puts his back on the door screaming and crying all in one action. The other door beside him opens as an old man motions for him to come inside out of the pouring rain. El Lobo looks up and flicks his thumb up and looks back out to the street watching the rain fall down. We then see a hand grab the hood and pull franticly inward. El Lobo falls back as he is drug into the building. The man pulls him in dripping wet onto an oriental carpet as he slams the door shut locking it. El Lobo stands up looking at the man as if he were crazy.} El Lobo: Hey holmes wha’s the prolem? Why you’z dissin’ me vato? I ain’t do shit to you bandejo. Man: I am Dr. Adam Marche, and I am your psychiatrist. You’re a bit early but its ok we have a lot to discuss according to the Childress’. Let’s see the first thing on the list… {He walks over to the desk sporting a pair of slacks and a button up shirt with a tie and looks through some papers. As he looks through them he makes several notes before speaking once more.} Man: So Sr. Lobo I see you have recently lost an unborn child and then a prestigious title in 4WW. So I take it you’ve been under a lot of stress lately??? El Lobo: Vato are you serious esse? I won’t even answer a tonta question like that. Man: Mmm hmm. A lot of anger and hostility in there, so tell me have you been under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs? El Lobo: I’ve been drinkin’ tequilas and other stuff… yea I’m back on heroin. It takes tha pain away. After all I’ve been through it’s the ONLY thing that gets me through besides the site of killing Oeste and Fnord. I mean I can’t take it the way I hear they whisper in the back. I know what they’re sayin’ no soy ‘stupido vato. Oy iss Lobo ‘e a pierdo. He doesn’t try enough, he’s just too high and canno’ take rejection. Man: Well how do you respond to their words Tom??? El Lobo: How the hell you tink I Deal with it gringo. Tell me who you know handles rejection like it ain’t nothin’. Don’t be a punk thinkin’ you’z a soldier doc. You think you got room to talk kid?? Lemme think, lemme roll and let me ease ya pain like a rhyme from Kurt Cobain. Look don’t worry, I’m not tellin’ lies. My dreams have died as my love learned vengeance. I wish that everything would just end holmes. You think you might know me but esse, you don’t know shit about me. I tried enough, damn I got high enough, now its time that this grown man cried himself to sleep. I don’t cry enough, I always thought that I couldn’t be hurt. You know something? My dreams are descending and I’m failing everything. Man: You need to discover something. L-I-F-E now say it… El Lobo: You don’t know what it’s like to be mistreated; hell I’m sure you’ve never been defeated. You don’t know what it’s like to be hated and demonstrated against. DO you know what it’s like to be the sad man? To be the man that lost everything he built and hoped that one day he would have and become? Now I live these hours all lonely because no one knows what its like to feel these feelings that I do. And No one fights back as hard as I do and it’s not fair. Everyone is out to get me and no one wants me alive. Hell no one knows how to say I’m sorry or not to worry that it’ll be ok. What the fuck vato, now one knows and none of you’z understands me holmes. I’m hated and I’m starting to fade and become empty on the inside just like everyone wants vato. I can see my dreams breaking and falling apart in front of my eyes. Man: Now my friend I’m sure… El Lobo: my friend???? VETE A LA MIERDA CHUCHA! TU SABES NADA! NADA DIGOTE!!! No one knows what its like to feel these feelings and I BLAME YOU! I have to retard my anger, none of my feelings can show through because I am who I am and people expect better of me vato. I’m tired of being this esse. I’m tired of being deceived. None of my pain can show through, and you now that my dreams are dying. Why do you have to sit there and antagonize me about this shit? Fuck you… fuck this… fuck me... I wanna fuckin’ die already. I wanna fail for once; I wanna lose and get it over with. Why do I have to sit here and fight it all the time? DIGAME AHORA VATO! Man: Well I must say that our time is over and I need to rite a report and give my diagnosis. Now I must say that from what I’ve gathered so far tonight is by far one of the most interesting cases I’ve had. You have an acute bipolarity. By that I mean you suffer from paranoid schizophrenia, chronic depression, and delusional schizophrenia. I must say that you are not very mentally healthy. At any given moment you may explode/implode depending on how you take out your frustrations. Whatever the case may be I suggest that you are not placed with any sharp objects. To tell you the truth you are very dangerous if provoked when you are at your worst; much like you are now, if not worse. {The man walks back to his desk and begins starting paperwork ready to give it to the 4WW. Could the diagnosis be correct or does it hold no expertise whatsoever? The man loosens his tie and turns on the radio only to hear soft soothing music. El Lobo just sits with his back to the door holding his knees in an upright position. The camera cuts around as it slowly fades to black with the sounds of the easy listening music and rain splattering on the ground outside.}
