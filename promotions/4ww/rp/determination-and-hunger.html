(Steve smiles.) Steve: But I shall never question my skills . I know I have the drive to keep going even after a loss are two , now it’s true I’m on a losing streak but TJ it ends with you. You’re the person who is going to start up my career in this sport. (Steve smiles) Steve: How you ask you see I’m gonna use you to break my losing streak as of late and gain gold. I know you’re a member of the Anderson family. But they can’t protect you from me. You see I’m at a turning point in my career where I will do anything to win I don’t care if the fans approve of my actions are not. When it comes to it the fans will turn on you. But you have the final say on determining if you will keep chasing on for that win or will you just sit there and let everyone walk all over you. (Steve gives a serious look.) Steve: You can damn sure believe that I don’t give up and you can believe that you shall never walk on me. Through my determination and my ever lasting push and drive to be the best you can call me. “The Phenomenal” Steve Owens the holder of the tempest title the man who is full of determination nothing will stand in my way everything starts with you TJ. My Legacy will come to at Storm front. Regardless I shall walk out Steve Owens tempest champion.. (Steve Owens gives a determined look as the scene fades to black slowly.)
