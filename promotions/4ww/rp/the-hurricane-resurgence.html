The Hurricane: Resurgence

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Hurricane: Resurgence Lucious Lady Luck 4WW Exposures #993 Date: 10/7/04 Location: LLL's Office. (The scene opens up inside the familiar office of Luscious Lady Luck. The Tempest Champion is sitting there, at her desk, shuffling papers, reading different things, and just in general trying to look busy. Behind her is the Tempest Championship Belt along with photos of her triumphs in order to be where she is today. Also behind her, off to one side, is a large color photo of Patti Anderson, along with a pair of purple delicates placed where they belong, and they look like they would fit Patti Anderson very well.) LLL: Respect is one of those odd things that very little will ever understand. It is something that we all strive for, but yet we may never reach. Be it because of an unexpected incident, the fact that no matter what we do, it is never good enough for someone else or the fact that sometimes, people just don’t know what respect is…… (Trips looks up from her paper and placing them on the ground.) And no matter how much we respect someone or something, it may always try to tear us down. It may never show you the respect that you think you deserve. No matter what, best laid plans, best intentions, best whatever, there are always going to be those people who don’t respect you…… And one of those people who will never respect you, one of those people who will always tear you down and rip you apart thanks to their lack of respect, one of those people who has no trouble chewing you up and spitting you up just because they feel like it. This person just doesn’t understand the meaning of respect, nor will this person ever show the respect that we all believe that we deserve as human beings…… And five years ago, that person showed me just how little respect they had for me and my plans. That person showed me just how little respect she would continue to have for me and will always have for me. That person took any respect for me I thought that I had from them and tore it up and burned it up faster than a propane fire. That person is Mother Nature…… That’s right. She threw the biggest wrench into my plans. She threw the biggest wrench into everything that I wanted to do. And she didn’t care. Mother Nature disrespected me that weekend. She disrespected me more than anyone could have ever thought. (Flashbacks are good, aren’t they? We flashback to a scene where we see Luscious Lady Luck walking upon a pile of rubble. The pile of rubble is a big pile of rubble. And all Luscious Lady Luck can do is stare on in disbelief.) LLL: How could this happen? This isn’t supposed to happen. (The Lucky One walks up to the pile of rubble and gets down on her knees. She grabs a pile of the rubble and looks at it. She just continues to stare at it in disbelief.) LLL: This building was supposed to be hurricane proof. This building was supposed to stand up against anything. This building was a rock in this organization. And now it’s all gone. (Luscious Lady Luck carefully starts walking through the pile of rubble. She sees several things as she walks through, mostly the remnants of the office which she worked so hard to restore after the first hurricane.) LLL: Who the hell didn’t tell me there was another hurricane coming through? Who the hell didn’t tell me that more disaster was coming in here. I could’ve been prepared. If only…. (Luscious Lady Luck picks up a stone from the diminished building and throws it out. As she continues to walk through the rubble, she looks at the pictures and the files of the different models she had worked for and with. Blonds, brunettes, redheads, male, female. There were all kinds. And they all had helped Trips make this business what it is. She just couldn’t believe it. She falls to her knees weeping.) LLL: How could you do this to me? How could you be so cruel to me? (She stares up to the heavens.) LLL: You knew my plans this week to be with Patti. You know how much I wanted to get back to Australia to be with the most wonderful person ever. Why do you torture me? Why do you hate me so? Why can you not just leave me alone? Now I’m stuck here at your will cleaning up this mess. And you, the Matriarch of Moss, Gaia, are laughing at me, knowing what you cost me. (Luscious Lady Luck takes one last look around, weeping, her head in her hands and the wind whipping up around her almost on cue, almost as if Mother Nature herself was laughing at her. The scene unflashesback, if that’s a word, to LLL looking at the camera.) LLL: And my opponent Slayer wonders why the world is perfect? Why is society trying to be perfect? It isn’t, my dear. Society is trying to be what society is, strive and live for itself. You talk about people who have to pay for a flat stomach, a big chest, and full lips. Honey, I am all natural. And that’s the way I will always be….. Sure, I’ll admit it; I did help some of my clients with their looks. I helped them because I believe in people’s dreams coming true. And I will always help people to get their dreams come true. And now, my dream has come true. You are upset because they put you in a title match for a title which doesn’t get any respect? Baby, respect is all I get around here, and respect is one thing that I am bringing to this title…… Please, bring your doom and gloom however. Please tell us all how you feel. And please, feel sorry for yourself all you want. Here in the real world, we don’t have the sympathy for you. We aren’t going to feel sorry for you. And I sure as hell am not going to take it easy on you. Because this belt deserves more than that, and I am going to bring this belt more than that. If you can’t accept that, then maybe you need to go back home…… Or you could just come to the ring, with all your self pity. All of your self loathing, all of your depression, and get added to my wall of title victories. It makes no difference to me. Life’s a bitch, but that’s life. Sometimes you just got to take the lemon of life and make lemonade. I wouldn’t be where I am today if I let the hurricane destroy my life. But I didn’t, I rebuilt, and I made my life a success, a greater success than I could ever imagine. And now, everyone wonders what would have happen to the 4WW and the Tempest Title without me. (Luscious Lady Luck picks up a rag and some spray, turns around, stand up, and starts to wipe the Tempest title to give it that shine that only a special human touch can give it. The camera fades out.)
