Home I Jade 4WW Exposures #371 Date: 9/12/2003 Location: here there, everywhere Escape again, it seems dangles in front of my eyes, gliding side to side like a pendulum ball. My blood boils within these petty rope shackles as the racking pain within my stomach increases to a shattering scream. The darkness clings to my skin like a wet cloth, uncomfortable and almost chaffing; if I’m left here long enough my will shall break like my skin. Blood shall fall and heads shall roll, I cannot allow this damned bondage to conceal my shell for eternity. I must break free; snap these bonds like I’ll snap their necks. This message should be of hope yet all I’m left with is desperation; the unremitting need to be free of this room overshadowed only by my want to better myself, my body. My body and my will shall break me from this cell. I am no animal, left caged to wander endlessly, I am no animal who shall hunt down defenseless weak individuals and pick them off as my next dinner, I am no animal who shall cower beneath his masters wrath and wait for the next in a line of beatings. If I’m going to be treated like an animal however, I shall be all of those things a thousand times over, I shall be the dark nights you fear, I shall be the shadows you can’t escape in a cold alley, I shall be the physical reincarnation of every childish fear your mind can concoct, and then some. ~Serene darkness~ “Are you guys going to just dance around in the dark outside this room or come in and play? I can tell you it isn’t going to be a pretty picture when you do. I’m going to smash your dick faces in with my bare feet and ride your Asses ‘til they bleed. Come on boys, come and play! I’m getting antsy dudes and it’s hurting me not to hurt you two right now. How about you come in here and untie my so I can play Dom and penetrate your rears. What? You still don’t want to come and play? OK, ok I know what’s the matter with you, I’ll give reach arounds but you have to promise not to bleed on me because that is just nasty! Oh come on men!” There’s another laugh that stifles through the air even though she seems to be in the most dangerous of situations. The scraping of skin against dusty floor rattles the room and no doubt the rats that inhabit the room. Jades eyes are barely visible by the beacon of moonlight that scatters through the boards on the windows. The hazel orbs angrily cover her eyelids as her eyebrows furiously furrow into the bridge above her nose. The shuffling from the tiny wooden chair is interrupted by silence until a small creak forms and a louder one follows as a snap pursues. As though she has been holding her breath her lungs release everything that’s been inside. Jade: “Oh and while I’m here mommy’s coming for you boys. There’s nothing quite like a challenge before I go on hunting mode. I won’t be sat around here all day. I have things to do! I have preparations…to prepare! DO YOU HEAR ME?” Her last words escaped as a growl and spittle helped her words over her violet lips and into the darkness that enfolds her like black soft satin sheets, comfortable to her touch. ~Nathaniel~ “What? Yes, this is indeed Nathaniel.” After the phone had rang the scruffy looking blonde man placed the bottle of malt whiskey on the desk he sat at and stared at the blue ballpoint pen in his hand. A smile broke through his bearded chin as he scratched the blue contains of the pen down against the yellow lined notepad. The pine desk that the pad is sat on shakes violently as he happily scribbles away whatever the person on the other side of the line is saying. He nods and runs his hands through his greasy looking hair and taps nervously with the pen against the desk. Nathaniel: “She’s confused huh? Yeah, that makes sense because she hasn’t been around for months on end now. This is crazy…my head just feels like it’s about to explode right now. After such a long time I thought she was dead. I wonder if she is even going to be the same person when I get her back here. Yeah, indeed I’m going to go get ready, there’s no way I’m taking on him and his goons without…what? Oh, sorry. Yes I’ll be waiting there on the rooftop…it is the only way I’m going to get inside there again without being seen. They have people there? That figures…Ok I’ll sort something out, you’re going to have to wait until I get there aren’t you?” Nathaniel wastes no time in forcing the phone down and looking dazedly into the dark room that surrounds him. He takes a large swig of the whiskey and rubs his dirty face in his hands and shakes his head. As though he has been hit in the head with a brick he suddenly snaps out of his trance and forces out a large raking of wood against wood as the chair flies backwards. He rushes towards the light filled hallway, pushing it out of his way and almost knocking it off from its hinges in the process. Nathaniel: “I’m coming Jade!” ~Back to Jade~ Damian: “You’re always the one to drag things out. You just never seem to get the point to all of this. If you’d have accepted that we weren’t here to harm you and just helped with our enquiries you could have been long gone by now. Instead you’re sat here on a cold steel chair which has been buckled to the ground, after the…err slight mishap with the wooden one we had before. You’ve barely eaten a thing for two weeks now, that’s got to be killing you by now…right?” Jades head bobs up and down woozily. She is barely managing to hold onto the contents of her stomach as it was, then she had to deal with his nauseating breath too. Jade’s head briefly leans back but is forced forwards as she once again battles the screaming urges to fall into slumber. Damian’s rough ebon hand lifts Jades chin so that her deep hazel eyes pierce into his, a flash of insanity passes her as she stares right through his shades. Damian: “So what’ya say you help with our ‘investigations’ and you can disappear from all this mess and keep well away from our boss?” Jades head drops back down into her coal rag covered chest, mumbling softly and deeply into it, it still pains her to speak yet she feels she must. Damian’s ears strain to catch the slithers of words that penetrate her pale, dry lips, the actual breaking of the skin on them clearer than her words. Damian leans closer, a big mistake made as Jade bites through the top of his ear and one of his dreadlocks. The man falls back onto his ass as Jade spits the piece of cartilage and blood against the limestone wall of this prison like room. Jade: “I said FUCK YOU!” The screams of the man vibrate through the walls, the hinges of the door shake as the people who guard the room on the other side try to break their way into the room after fiddling with, and losing their set of keys. Jade’s eyes catch the moonlight which shines through the window left slightly ajar yet encased with the solid steel bars. Jades head shoots upwards to gaze at the sky; her struggles in the metal become a more audible representation of an animal snagged within a bear trap. Two larger men ran through the door, sending the huge, heavy piece of metal collapsing down to the ground in a fit of dirt and smoke. The two men split as one tackles Jade with a huge balled fist that could have easily knocked the restrained chair over with a tiny little bit more pressure in the back handed slap. “Bitch” Jades body loses the fight with slumber as her eyes once again roll into the back of her skull. The other man who had barged into the room limps over to the heavily bleeding Damian and lifts him over his shoulder. “I told you to be careful around her you arrogant prick.” ~The Alley~ Three men stand guard in front of a large metallic door, probably dead bolted in about three different places, two of the men turn on the scuffed heels of their shoes to inspect a creepy rattle that had emanated from the darkness. The man we know as Luther, one of the previous kidnappers of Jade doesn’t even take a glimpse as to what is going on behind him as though he already knows what is about to happen. One of the scruffier men is the first to make their way towards the sound and peers over at the side of the huge trash container after taking a huge breath of the midnight air and pulling out his revolver. The last thing him and his hobo like accomplice had in common was the silenced PPK that was pointed to their brows before half of their face was blown off. The blonde haired guy pushes a strand of it; almost shoulder length, back behind his ear as he looks towards the Mediterranean bald man with his golden-toothed smile. The two walk over to each other and embrace briefly. Luther: “It’s good to see you again brother.” Nathaniel: “You too Luther. Does Mani suspect anything of you?” Luther grins and shakes his head, offering a hanky to his smaller brother to wipe the blood from his hands and arms. Luther: “That inbred idiot has even less of a clue than 4WW do allowing her to face Jerms in her debut inside their ring. I mean the first opportunity that she got she managed to bite off part of her future competitors nose and spit it away.” Nathaniel: “Well, if we don’t hurry up she isn’t going to be able to finish off what she started, Emmanuelle is a god damned animal, it’s taken me almost a year to find her again after her last disappearance and there’s no way in hell I’m going to lose her again.” ~Jade~ The light flickers back on in the room. It looks amazingly like a meat locker, with blood daubed upon every wall as thick as icing on a cake. The smell that fills the room could only be compared to cheap aftershave and viscera, its substantial depth lays heavy on the air, which with a severe lack of air conditioning is at least three days old. Jades head is in her chest; which is shallowly beating being a depiction of her lifeless siesta. Her arms are restrained to the side of the reinforced chair and her bare legs nailed to the floor, covered in only in a sickly mixture of still fresh and coagulated blood. The only strips of clothing left on her body now is a once white underwear, now drenched with sweat, urine, blood and bile, and her black hot pants, pulled down quickly around her knees. The one noticeable fixture in the room apart from the door is a scratched steel high cabinet. “Ungh.” Her lifeless body was awaked in an awkward position when her head dropped over slightly to the side. A steel shutter on the door closes to indicate that she was being watched. A heavily blinged Mediterranean man walks into the room and tosses the long black overcoat that adorned his shoulders to a man who stays waiting on the other side of the door. Jades hazel eyes try to gain a proper picture of the man who stands in front of her but it is blocked by her matted, sweaty raven hair and blood that’s dribbled from the open wound on her head, down into her eyes. The man removes his under jacket revealing some kind of teal dress shirt as he gets ever closer to the beaten Jade with a slight limp, the limp Nathaniel himself had given to the man when he shot him in the shin. Jades busted lips suck in the blood in an attempt to clean a part of her wounded face, the man draws closer still and she releases it in a vile amount of spit, flying down against his expensive black shoes. “Always a fighter, eh Jade? You can never just give in and let it be known that you are weaker; you appear to find it even harder to accept a loss against the male of our species.” Jade: “Fuck off Mani. I’m not going to trade bitchy comments back and forth with you about myself. You know exactly what kind of woman I am; I’ll fight you in my worst condition. Do you know what else? I’ll still be out here on time to break a little neck come Maelstrom. Nobody can stop me from doing that, you don’t stand a chance in hell.” Mani chuckles as he looks down at his blood cousin. Angry yet repudiating the opportunity to show this to a member of his family, knowing full well that she can get off on that. Mani: “You must be enjoying your little stay in this cold and dank room then?” Jade: “You’ve got to be kidding. The smell in here could eat the stitching out of Levi’s. Then again that doesn’t matter, for you shall get your comeuppance if I want it badly enough you shall fall at my knees.” Mani: “Why don’t you save yourself a lot of pain and trouble and just tell me what you have been plotting in your time out in the last 12 months? I’m quite anxious to hear about your little plans to fell me. Let me reassure you that right now there are thirteen unmarked passages out of this building. Thirteen reasons why you will never, NEVER be able to lay your hands upon me. I’m above you Jade, that’s the way it has always been, that’s the way it will be until the day I get to see you die. Fortunately for you, however you’re more use to me alive than dead.” Jades head falls down again her eyes closed, Her soft voice broken by the repetitive beatings, yet still it exists defiant and strong staring him in the face an angering him until his face fills with the crimson color associated with hatred. Her voice speaks out, not in English but apparently an ancient tongue. Jade: “Jinse…Fira…Ifrit…Jinse…Fira…Ifrit.” Mani: “I understand.” Mani raises a black gloved hand into the air and brings it back down against Jades head, blood pouring from a wound freshly made, still Jade lifts back her head and mumbles into the thin air that soars around her face. Mani pulls apart the silken shirt from his upper body with the greatest of ease and tears it into two strips to gag and blindfold the twenty four year old Jade who whispers insolently those three words constantly until her words are bound with the teal fabric. Mani seems to very comfortably ball his right fist again and slam it back against the neck of Jade, a wound opening above the welt that lies on her neck. Mani rears back again and his fist meets the temple of Jade, a blow so hard the bolts which tie the chair down to the floor struggle to maintain her position, the teal colored material of the blindfold staining crimson as it flows from a sickening gash on her head, her neck pops as it straightens back up to look through the cotton into the eyes of whom she believed to be the devil. Mani: “It could have worked out so much easier on you.” Mani rears back his black finely woven trouser covered leg and slams his knee into the chest of Jade ripping the chair straight from the concrete of the floor and sends her flying back against the wall, the holes in her feet larger than ever as the nails that kept her in place stay in the solid ground, blood flying through the air. Mani’s chest leaped up and down with adrenaline, preparing for the final charge before three of his henchmen make their way into the room side by side, their pistols cocked in their hands. Mani: “What is it Jonah?” The larger of the men steps forward with his hand on his hips and grabs Mani by the arm in an attempt to emphasize the situation. Jonah: “It’s become necessary that you make your way from here immediately. 9 of our men are dead, littered all the way around the building, closer and closer to this wing. It isn’t safe. You know the procedures by now I presume?” Jades cousin lets out a grunt as he makes his way towards the scratched cupboard that rests at the opposite end of the room. A loud screech accompanies the black door opening as the large man squeezes himself into the box. Mani: “Of course.” The door closes and the sound of a lock rattling escorts it. Jonah manages to actually get into the hole and close it before the two brothers Jade knows so well charge into the room and blow the brains out of whoever was visible in the room. Nathaniel grabs the black sleeve of his brother drawing his attention to blood soaked body of a woman who lies in the corner. Immediately they both run to take the gag and blindfold away from her respectively. Jade still mumbles under her breath, though each word dribbles another amount of blood from her innards. Jade: “Inflame!” Her tongue spits out the words with the last bits of energy that she can draw form her body, her words sound angry and hurt. Filled with everything she feels right now for the world, selfish, malicious and brutal anger for whomever will dare stand in her way. That all directed at the one man who waited inside the box, crying inaudibly for his life. Mercy was not something Jade has the energy for and a fireball of a man flies through the door. Her emotive magic at it’s strongest when her body is at its weakest. Nathaniel: “Let’s get you to a Hospital.” Jade: “OK, baby.” Nathaniel dismissed the comment as her dizziness of pain. Her pain and lifeblood dripping into his shirt as he carried her towards the first place she has actually been safe in twelve months. Home. ~End~
