Who Will Go Into MS3 With The Advantage

Who Will Go Into MS3 With The Advantage Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #570 Date: 03/08/04 Location: In The Car underload syndrome (UN.dur.lohd sin.drum, -drohm) n. Ill health or depression caused by a lack of challenges or stimulation at work. Todd’s worst fear. He’s been the champion for so long that he is slowly losing all types of face among his fellow competitors because of the simple fact there is only ONE person in the entire federation that can take him on. A person that he has faced before. A few months ago he faced me in a match for the title, a title that I was favored to win. Since Toddy Boy couldn’t handle the pressure of having to face some stiff competition, he relied on the use of his then manager Annie Leanne to keep the title around his waist so he wouldn’t look bad in the eyes of his “Savior” complex. Toddy Boy is nothing more then a pathetic champion that demands the best that can be found, but sooner or later the depression of only having a female as being his top competition has got to be wearing thin. Plus knowing that costing me the tag team title has lit the fire under me to prove once again that I deserve the gold that he is clutching so dearly to his chest. Now having to deal with the threat of me in a non-title match I’m pretty sure that the underload syndrome isn’t as bad as it will be in a couple weeks then the gold finally on the line and Toddy’s underload syndrome finally comes to a head and he gets the real competition he deserves. Now I know I’m going to have to watch out for the evilness of his new/old manager, Betty coming back into the picture. My best guess is that Toddy can’t wrestle a match without having someone in his corner. Are you scared Toddy? Are you afraid that your belt will finally leave your beer belly waist and wind up around mine that you would be driven to have someone in your corner to make sure that you retain just isn’t going to help out. But I will deal with her when the time comes, right now I should be focusing on the fact that we are set to dance again, this time in a non-title match up that is being declared the warm up for the North Wind Title Shot. A match that is supposed to give us a warm up to Maelstrom 3. Oh what the bloody hell ever. It’s just a nice little exhibition match to give the fans an idea of exactly what Toddy Boy and myself are capable of doing, but we all know that the tempers will be flying and someone’s desire, mainly mine, will be flying extremely high to prove that the other one is the best. But now that I look out over the entire past few months and realize that even though this non title (though stupid as it may seem) sets up the entire tone for the Maelstrom 3 pay per view, one has to ask who has the most to lose in the match. Is it me or is it good old Toddy boy? As being the one involved in the match and seeing how it is my point of view that counts here, Toddy boy forgot to mention in last weeks little rant on Storm Front that the victory over me was not a clean victory as he tried to portray it as. You see, as I have stated previously, Toddy boy completely forgot that he had help in that title match up. Sean McClancy’s former fiancé involved herself in the match up too, giving him the pinfall victory this time. Guess it wasn’t my time to shine, unlike this time. This time, Toddy, I’m coming at full aim at you. If I’m lucky this time around, I’ll just work to injury you a little so that the odds smile in my favor. ______________________________________________________________ Place: Highway leading into Anaheim, CA Time: Daytime ______________________________________________________________ Anaheim. Next Right. The sign seemed to scream at Patti as she drove her Mazda Miata along the interstate. A city she didn’t mind traveling to when she was in the mood to relive events of the past. Her mind briefly wandered back to when she came state side to pursue the man she had fallen in love with when he was in the other promotion. Unfortunately that was also where she caught a glimpse of the man that she would be fighting this time around. She glanced over at the passenger seat where Adrian was sleeping. She wondered how he managed to sleep on these drives with her. At least the dogs seemed to like him. Patti glanced up in the mirror and saw Princess and Luciefer sleeping comfortably in the back seat. Adrian stirred in his seat as if some sort of dream was attempting to disturb what little bit of sleep he was allowing himself to receive. Patti redirected her attention to the stretch of highway that was stretching out for miles before her. “Todd Dream”, her brain screamed over again and again. Something about this equation just didn’t seem right. A non title match against him was always a good thing, but when it’s the week before the major battle made Patti wonder what her future sister-in-law was up too. Patti made the sharp turn off of the highway and onto the ramp as her mind continued to struggle with the thoughts. A horn from the person in the next lane snapped her out as she forced her car back into her lane. Adrian woke up just enough to stare out the windshield, stretched his tanned legs, and smiled at Patti before he went back to sleep. “Bloody Damn Americans” she spit out in anger and having to pull back over into her lane. She couldn’t understand what joy the Americans got out of driving this way, but she sighed and turned on the radio. “Just my bloody luck, a bleeding commercial.” Patti banged her fist on the steering wheel in frustration as she changed the channel, desperately looking for music. Her eyes bounced from the radio to the road before her. Finally she hit a station that had music going. Her mind didn’t care that it was something soft and smooth, it was music. Patti started humming along with the music. Anything would work at the moment to keep Toddy boy out of her mind. Her humming was broken by the annoyance of her cell phone ringing. Her heart hoped that it was Sean calling to let her know when his flight was coming in. She stared down at the Caller ID on the phone and saw it was Fey calling her. “What the bloody hell? If it’s not her bloody inapt secretary calling, it’s her wanting to know when im going to be at the arena. Can I not get there without being on some sort of time frame?” Patti sighed annoyingly as she picked up the earpiece for her cell phone. No need to get into an accident over the stuff. She placed the earpiece in her ear and hit the talk button. “Yes, Fey love...I’m on the bloody outskirts of Anaheim... I know I should have left earlier, but with two bloody dogs to take care of...No I’m not leaving them in a kennel, Adrian takes care of them for me when I’m wrestling...Yes I know you suspect something about him...He’s a good kid, and just loves to help me out...I’ve got to go..I’m bloody hungry and so is Adrian...I’ll call you when I get close....” “What was that all about?” Adrian said, keeping his eyes closed. “Fey called again. Since I’ve been declared the number one contender for the North Wind Title, she’s afraid I’m going to bloody get lost or go running to wherever Sean is because I’m going to bloody freaking choke like I did the last time that I faced him.” Adrian shook his head. Patti took drama to a new level on some days, and today was no exception to that rule. Ever since she had gotten word that she would be facing Todd Dream in a non title match, the girl had been almost impossible to live with. Her constant complaining was enough to drive anyone mad, but as true and loyal friend to her, he knew she needed someone just to listen. “What seems to be bothering you with facing Todd again? Other then the fact that you know if you choke...your darling sister-in-law will go back to thinking of you as a fraud.” Adrian chuckled for a second under the joke he had made. Patti not finding the joke quite as funny, used her free hand that she was using to shift the gears to smack Adrian in the head. He playfully giggled as he watched a smile appear on Patti’s face from his comments. “Adrian, my little love, you just don’t understand. Toddy boy already has that one victory over me. He’s going to go into this match semi-cockier then normal. His bloody ego will be spilling out into the ring. He knows he’s owned me from the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. If it wasn’t that bloody harlot’s fault, I would have won that title.” “Patti, my dear...It’s a non-title match up. It doesn’t matter who wins. Todd isn’t going to care whether or not he wins the match. He has nothing to lose. He is just going into this match to collect his paycheck and that’s it.” Patti pursed her lips together a little tighter, getting more and more agitated with each mile that she drove. Her vision to the side of her let her see that Adrian was trying his hardest to be right about the entire situation. “It’s not that, Adrian. My first real good close up of Toddy Boy was when I was following Sean all over California. Toddy and I never met outside of Sean and Fey introducing me as Sean’s new valet. That was where it pretty much ended. Other then that, I’ve stayed out of is way and he out of mine...until recently....” “Until recently...what?” “Until I became the number one contender for the North Wind Championship. Todd and I stayed out of each others way until I started showing promise. A promise that I could be the ‘Leader of the New Generation’. Do you think Toddy is feeling threatened over the possibilty of being old news?” Adrian reaches over and pats Patti loving on the head. “Good girl! I knew it would come to you. You spend too damn much time trying to come up with a reason why instead of trusting your instincts....” Patti tossed a glare at Adrian as she turned the car into the parking lot of Anaheim Pond. She slams the car into a parking spot, tossing the two pups in the back seat into each other. A yelp lets Patti know that she scared them with the force of the turn. Adrian slams his shoulder up against the window with a sickening thud. “Watch it girl, you’ll damage my dancing tonight.” Patti just grins as she gets out of the car and makes the journey into the arena. Her mind full of ideas and thoughts that will keep good old Toddy boy guessing. Fade To Black
