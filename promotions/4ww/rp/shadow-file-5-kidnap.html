Shadow File #5 - Kidnap Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #1168 Date: 2/10/05 Location: |>> Scene opens in a dark bedroom, a little girl with long red color hair that was tie in pigtails was sound asleep in bed with her seven month old Golden Retriever at the foot of the bed sleep too. Meanwhile, downstairs in the kitchen, the mother with short red color hair and green eyes in house clothes quietly wash dishes in the sink. Back in the daughter’s room, the Golden Retriever wakes up and see a dark figure crotch in the girl’s open window. Instantly, the dog gets in an offensive stance and growl at the figure…the figure hold up its’ hands near a mysterious medallion with a black color leaf on it that glow a dark crimson aura, a wicked tree vine come out of the medallion and knock the dog against a dresser. The dog emits yelp and fall to the floor, unconscious. The little girl stirs and woke up from her peaceful slumber. > She moves out of bed…the vines wrap around her body and mouth tightly. Golden Retriever regains consciousness and saw his master in trouble. He lunges at the figure and strike the figure in the face with a scratch attack. The figure winces with a grunt, glare at the dog angrily and knock the dog out again with a backhand fist. In the kitchen, the mother stops when she heard the thud from her daughter’s room, she exited the kitchen and jogged up the stairs. > In the lobby of a grand hotel in Marseille, France, Kyo Kushinaga in his black Outlaw jersey and blue jeans with a pair of black color sneakers minus the trench coat steps out of the elevator, light a cigarette with his silver lighter and sigh out a cloud of nicotine pass his lips. He walks further in the lobby…stop when he see his sister, Ukyo in punk attire with a bandage the forehead sit at a table alone in the hotel’s restaurant. She drinks a hot cup of coffee, sigh softly. > Kyo doesn’t reply and glare at his sister. > Ukyo blinks and stare at an empty space where her brother once sat at, look around the restaurant, watch her brother leave. > Kyo enters the lobby, light a cigarette and release a long cloud of smoke with a gently sigh. > Kyo stops and stare at the young clerk. > In main hall of the France Police Department in downtown Marseille, a press conference was being held between the department’s chief and many journalists from different newspapers. > The lieutenant shook his head. In the far back of the main hall, the Deathscythe Outlaw lean back on the wall with his arm cross and lit cigarette in mouth, watch the press conference through his orange-tint shades. > In the home of Dr. Luc Sang, the French scientist with short brown color hair and brown eyes behind his eyeglasses in house clothes sit with his redhead wife that’s dress in house clothes too on a brown color sofa. On another sofa in front of the married couple, Kyo Kushinaga and Jacques Besson sat. Jacques quietly drinks a hot cup of tea that wife prepared. > Mrs. Sang nods with an anxiety look. > Jacques stares at Kyo…turn his attention back on Luc. > The phone rings. Luc, his wife, and Jacques stare at the phone. > Luc approaches the ringing phone…Kyo stopped him. > Luc, his wife, and Jacques watch Kyo. > Mrs. Sang’s face goes pale. > Jacques leaves the living room. Luc remains and comfort his wife. Upstairs, in the daughter’s room, Kyo examines the room. Jacques leans on the wall and watch Kyo. > A puppy whimpers. Kyo looks down and see the little girl’s Golden Retriever sit down at his feet. > Puppy whimpers in a reply. > Kyo goes to grab the blood-stain paw…the puppy wince with a whimper. > Jacques takes out a clean white color handkerchief from his pants’ pocket and hand it to Kyo. Kyo press the puppy’s blood-stain against the handkerchief and remove it, reveal a dried blood paw print on the cloth. > Kyo stares at him. > Kyo glares at him and blur away. > It’s night time in the city of Marseille…Kyo Kushinaga stand in a phone booth, stare down at the blue color phone and think about Caitlin. > A French man stops and blink with surprise on his face when he saw Kyo do that. Kyo walks down the sidewalk…stop when he notice a Goth store with a cool-looking black leather trench coat in the front window. He disappears inside the store. In the dresser room, Kyo wears the trench coat and check himself out in the mirror. He makes a curious sound when he noticed his pocket watch glow a cerulean aura. Kyo exits the store after he paid for the trench coat and noticed Kayin sit on his motor scooter. Kayin hands Kyo a file. Kyo reads the file. > Surprise takes over Kyo’s face. > Kayin holds out black color sword handle at Kyo in one hand and two gun clips in the other. > Kayin makes an amuse sound and drive away. > It was midnight at Hell’s Gate; a couple of bikers smoke and drink alcohol outside. They are accompanied by hookers at the nearby street corner. A French man with scratches on his right cheek, Jean with short blonde hair and blue eyes in a wrinkle French police uniform steps out of the hangout...he stop and grab hold of a hooker, plant a deep kiss on her then he push her away. He approaches his yellow color motorcycle, turn it on and drive off…he didn’t move. > Jean goes silent…he reply with a back kick at Kyo…Kyo block and release his grip on his bike at the process, watch Jean ride away. Kyo runs over to a motorcycle that a biker was bordering…he knocked the biker off with a strong elbow in the face. > Jean rides through the rough neighborhood in great speeds, look over his shoulder and see that Kyo was behind him. > Wicked trees spout out behind Jean from the concrete ground in form of a roadblock. Surprise fills Kyo’s face…sharply stop at the trees. > Kyo glares at the trees…a smirk appear on his face. > Kyo turns the bike around and drive off in the opposite direction. > In the heart of Marseille, Jean ride down the brightly lit part of the city, look over his shoulder and see that Kyo wasn’t there anymore. He looks back at front and stopped when he saw there was a traffic jam. Jean makes a curious sound when he heard loud honks from the cars. > Jean holds up the medallion and summon his plants…Kyo press off the airborne bike and dive right at him, shatter Jean’s medallion with the release of a strong pulse of his Shadow Power. > Kyo lands on the hood of a car…he flip off in a corkscrew motion and hops on Jean’s bike, turn it to face Jean. Jean favors his ribs with a grunt…fright take over his face when he saw Kyo’s motorcycle fall toward him, he scurried out of the way and hear the motorcycle crash on the hood of the car. The driver of that car exit the car and cruse at both, Kyo and Jean in French about ruin his car. Kyo gives the driver a glare…the driver gulp and apologize for what he said and go back in his car. > An onyx color glow surrounds Jean from neck to toe…he levitate off the ground. > Kyo have his hand held out with his eyes glow a cerulean aura, turn his on its back and watch Jean’s belly up. Jean falls on his back when Kyo brought his hand down…Kyo drive close to Jean. > Civilians watch on with shock. > A door opens in a dark room, Dr. Luc Sang’s daughter, Marie Sang sat in the corner with tears in her blue eyes, watch French police approach her. > In the main lobby of the French Police Department in downtown Marseille, a couple of French police officers bring in a handcuffed and traumatized Jean Russo. > A French police officer with his head stands in the far side of the lobby, watch the officers escort Jean Russo to his jail cell. He lifts his head…his eyes flash a crimson aura and heads in the direction of the officer. > Kyo Kushinaga stands outside of the police department, light a cigarette and smoked in relaxation, stare up at the night sky. > Kyo takes the cigarette out and sigh out a cloud of smoke. > Kyo look over to Jacques, watch Jacques walk down the stairs and approach him. > Jacques laughs. > Kyo holds up his middle finger in reply. Jacques shakes his head with a laugh…the scene fades to black with Jacques disappear in the police department. > To be continued
