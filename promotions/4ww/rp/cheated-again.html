Cheated Again

24 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cheated Again Rustin Slade 4WW Exposures #1072 Date: 11/24/04 Location: Various/Grotto Nightclub Somewhere outside a high profile nightclub (The camera fades into a prominent nightclub in the downtown New York City area. The camera pans across the street. We see Rustin Slade, the 4WW newcomer, standing in a shadowed alleyway.) Rustin: Any minute now. (The camera pans back to the nightclub and we see a man coming out with women flocking towards him. The man we see is a well known celebrity around New York known as Peter “Casanova” O’Poole. He is rarely seen without a woman next to him. He reeks of charisma. Peter hails a taxi as he signs a few autographs for some women. We see Rustin across the street, jumping into a taxi he has apparently stolen. Rustin stops and Peter steps into his taxi.) Rustin: Where to pal? Peter: 5th and Garber and make it snappy. Rustin: You got it. (The taxi takes off away from the nightclub and is hurtling through the streets of New York. The taxi stops at the intersection of 5th Street and Garber Ave. Peter tries to open the cab door but to his horror, it seems to be locked. The light changes and the taxi goes further into New York City.) Peter: Hey man! What gives? Rustin: Just sit back O’Toole. Enjoy the ride. (The taxi is now slowing down and we see that they have stopped at another hot spot. This spot is known for the women that show up there, and the men who try to get them. The club is known as the Grotto.) Peter: Hey what are we doing here? Rustin: These are my hunting grounds O’Toole. I want you to show me how you do it. Peter: Do what? Rustin: I want to learn how to pick up women. (Peter and Rustin step out of the taxi and head into the Grotto. Peter, still a little shaken by his kidnapping, looks at the man who has done it. A desperate soul. He’s seen them before. Guys who were hard up in the romance department. This guy was no exception. He looked like he hadn’t been with a woman in years. Peter, taking mercy continued to follow Rustin farther into the club. They come to a stop and Rustin points to a girl who is dancing by herself in the middle of the dance floor.) Rustin: Show me the light Mr. O’Toole. Peter: Okay, watch and learn. (O’Toole walks up to the girl and starts dancing with her. We zoom in closer and we hear the two talking. The girl’s laughing and then slips O’Toole a piece of paper. O’Toole finishes the song with the girl and walks away. He walks back over to Rustin and shows him the piece of paper, which has the girl’s phone number on it. O’Toole smiles at Rustin.) Peter: Now you try, ok? (Rustin looks around the club and spies another girl dancing alone. Rustin slides in and starts dancing. We hear the two talking. The girl hands Rustin a piece of paper too, and slides away when the song is done. Rustin comes back, not smiling.) Peter: Well? (Rustin slips the piece of paper to Peter. Peter unfolds it and the camera zooms in on what it says. The note is not numerical. It says “FUCK OFF!”) Rustin: I’m hopeless. Peter: Don’t say that, let’s try a more personal approach. I’ll help you on this one. (Peter finds a table where two bachelorettes are sitting by themselves sitting and talking. Peter taps Rustin on the shoulder and points to the table. They make their way over to the table and Peter and Rustin sit down to each lady, respectively) Peter: Hello ladies. My name’s Peter and this is my friend... Rustin: Rustin Slade. Lady 1: I’m Tammy. Lady 2: Rena. Peter: We were just remarking how beautiful you two looked. Isn’t that right Rustin? Rustin: Yeah. You two are very pretty. (The ladies start giggling. Tammy is all over Peter. Rena seems to be getting close to Rustin. Rustin puts her arms around Rena, and amazingly, she allows it. Peter smiling, gets up with Tammy.) Peter: Well Rustin, I’m going to get going. My work is done. (Rustin is too busy enjoying himself talking to Rena. Peter leaves the club with Tammy clutching his arm. After Peter leaves the club, Rena grabs Rustin’s arm and delicately takes it off of her) Rustin: What was that for? Rena: Who was your hottie friend? Rustin: That was Peter O’Toole. Rena: Do you have his number baby? Rustin: (Shakes his head in disgust, and hands her a piece of paper, with Peter’s number on it.) There you go. Rena: Thanks. (She gets up, leaving Rustin alone. Once again, he has been cheated in the woman department. The camera zooms on his face, and he begins to tear up as the camera fades to black)
