Snap Boom Lee Satima 4WW Exposures #479 Date: Jan 26th Location: Unknown OOC: First off the RP is written as two poems and is a new approch to RPing for me. Any feedback would be great. I lie here in my bed when I should be asleep I look towards my girlfriend makes no sound, not even a peep I stand up; see nothing, nothing but the black I look around the nothingness and wonder what’s the point? I ask my god, I plead him but I get no such luck I wonder why I’m here and what has become of me I think of all the things I’ve done and all the sins consume I ask myself what I am. Nothing but a meaningless dot inside this lonely world I sit down and ask myself how can I deal with that I know the solution, I know it well, my destiny I can see For what I am, and what I be, I shall overcome thee. The pain I'm feeling can't even begin to compare to the anger, Hateful, hellish anger brewing within me Every breath I take, Every time I move, I'm always burning with hate and anger I'm running out of time, I can’t control the pain Something is going to break, something is going to explode Is that something me? Everyone has ups, everyone has downs I'm lost in a void of nothing but searing pain Where are my ups? Where are my downs? Why Can't I do anything about the torment in my body, my mind and my soul? Is it my destiny to live in so much pain, always hating what's around me? Lashing out at those people who love me, is there nothing I can do to change? I think it's my turn to break; it's my turn to explode Snap Boom *The second poem entitled “Snap Boom” was written by Joshua Green, and the first entitled “I” was written by me.
