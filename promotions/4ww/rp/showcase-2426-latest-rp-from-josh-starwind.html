Latest RP from Josh Starwind

Visions In the far desert, the burning orange and golden sun rises from the horizon slowly, giving the dark cerulean color sky some light. Soft rays of sunlight shined down the endless sand in the desert that caused the sand to warm up a bit. The innocent birds was greeting the new day with their soft, peaceful chirps while they glide through the warm morning sky with grace and speed. Just as the day was beginning, someone else’s day was coming to productive start… At the deep desert, a brown and white tinted mobile home that was the size for at least three people to live in. In front of the mobile home parked two vehicles, one being an all black Ford F-350 and the other an unusual looking dark blue motorcycle that appears that owner customized it. Grunting from a man and pounding sounds faintly heard behind the large mobile home. There a wolf with grey fur and white coming down middle of the front was lying on the ground, looking forward intently with its crystal blue eyes. It opens its mouth wide in a soft yawn to reveal its sharp fangs though closes it mouth and lowers it head where its chin rests on his front legs. The wolf closes both its blue eyes as it was about to fall asleep but both its still twitch a bit, as it was still listening to the man’s grunts along with the pounding. Two dark moving shadows seen on the sand covered ground, one belonging to a person and the other a punching bag that was swinging back and forth. The person’s shadow was no other than Josh Starwind who did not wear a shirt only a pair of black sweatpants and black tennis shoes. He was delivering fierce and powerful punches that cause slight dents with each impact. Josh stops punching for a brief moment, panting softly and watches the punching bag swing back and forth in that way. Josh feels his hot sweat tickling down his warm chest. He gets in a fighting stance and clutches both hands that were bandage up with white tape into tight fist and watches the swinging punching bag that hung down from a nearby tree. Josh slowly close his twin brown eyes and stops panting, starts to listening to the soft clinging sound of a chain that was attach to the moving punching bag. He continues to listen to the sound intently with both ears, waiting for the right moment to strike the moving bag. Now tries to sense the sudden changes of wind so he could strike the moving punching bag with a finishing move. “Now,” he tells himself in thought as he sharply opens both his eyes and quickly spreads both his legs apart, clutches his right fist into a tighter fist that cause his muscles in his right arm to tighten. He moves his body forward along with his closed fist towards the moving punching bag. His swift punch reaches the moving punching bag right when the bag swings dead center. Josh hits the bag hard in the middle of the punching bag that causes the bag to stop in its track. Soon the back of the bag swells up and explodes where the sand that was within the punching bag pours out from the now hole in the ruin punching bag. He has his fist embedded within of what was left of the bag, glaring forward with seriousness in his twin brown eyes. Soft pants part his lips, feeling his hot sweat tickling down from face. “Oops,” he says in embarrassment when realizing he used too much strength to do that last move. Removes his fist from the deflated punching bag, rise that hand up to his face where he lightly scratches his sweaty cheek. “I think I over did it.” Josh closes his eyes and chuckles softly in a modest way at the comment. Stops his laughing, opens his eyes and looks over to the napping wolf with a content gaze. “Hayato,” he calls the wolf while he keeps his brown gaze on the furred beast. “It is time for our jog.” Hayato opens it eyes and rises its head up as he looks at Josh with his crystal blue eyes. Josh looks at his companion with a curious gaze, wondering what Hayato was going to do after he heard his words. There is a moment of silence as both, Hayato and Josh, look at one another... Hayato opens his mouth wide in a soft yawn to end the moment of silence. He squirts both his eyes in reaction from the yawn, lowers his head back down on both his front legs and rests once more. “Gah,” he yelled out and falls down in surprise when seeing Hayato do that. Josh quickly stands up and glares at Hayato with anger in his brown eyes. “Hayato, get your lazy butt up! We are going jogging and you’re going to like it!” Hayato breathes out heavily as if it told him “humph” while keeping both his eyes closed, trying to fall back to sleep. “You lazy wolf,” he scolds and approaches Hayato where he stands now stands in front of him. Josh glares down at resting Hayato with anger. “If you don’t jog with me I am not going to give you a steak tonight!” Quickly Hayato sat up, looking up at Josh with a content gaze as he has his tongue hanging from side. He starts to pant, wagging his furry tail out in excitement. “Good grief,” he says and looks at Hayato with dullness. “Some wolf I have… I have to bribe you so he would do exercise with me, how pathetic.” Turns his back on Hayato and starts to walk towards the back door of his mobile home. “Come on, you bum.” Josh opens up the door and enters his mobile home. Hayato follows his master inside of his home. Josh closes the door behind him and Hayato. After a moment, the front door opens up where Hayato runs out of the door. Josh follows and closes the door behind him. His attire has change a bit, he wore black jeans, a single black vest and with the same tennis shoes. Josh closes the door and locks it behind him. “Ok.” He turns around and looks at Hayato with a content gaze. “Let’s go, Hayato.” Josh runs away from his home and out to the desert. Hayato follows Josh behind him quickly. Unaware that a mysterious figure lurking in distant, watching Josh for some unknown reason though it seem to be an amusing one since the figure forms a smirk on their small lips that was covered with pink lipstick. It would seem that this person has something in mind for Josh Starwind though the question was what… Out in the wasteland, Josh and Hayato are running through the desert of the hot red sand that covers the land. Both of them feels the bright, burning rays of sunlight from the hanging sun though they continue with their jogging exercise. After a while, Josh and Hayato has stop jogging, resting at the cave mouth where they were keeping out of the sun. Josh sits on a bit large rock, looking out of the cave while feeling his hot sweat streaming down his face, hair and even within his black vest. Hayato lays on the cool cave surface on his stomach with his head resting on both front legs, having both eyes close, relaxes in the cool area with his master. Josh lowers his head down, placing elbows on his knees, closes his twin brown eyes and starts to reflect. “Joshy,” a ghostly female voice calls with Josh’s mind. Josh does not answer the voice nor make a sudden movement in response, as he continues to reflect to himself while resting in the shady cave with Hayato. “Joshy boy,” she calls again though soon giggles softly inside his head. This time Josh hears the ghostly voice calling him through telepathy, opens his eyes and raises his head up, looking with curiosity. “What,” he queries in thought. “Joshy boy,” she calls through telepathy once more. “Rain,” he questions in mind. Josh looks behind him in the deep darkness of the cave with his twin brown in wonder. “Where are you,” she asks through telepathy. “I’m getting lonely.” Josh stands up from the rock and starts to walk further within the cave with hesitation though wonder. Both Hayato’s ears twitch at the sound of Josh walking further in the cave. He rises his head up and turns to face the deep darkness of the cave with his crystal blue eyes, curious to know why his master went further in the cave without him. In deep cave, the soft sounds of water dripping down from the cave peaks that hangs down from the stone ceiling echoes out in the darkness. Underneath the dripping sound, the sounds of heavy footsteps echo out as a dim blue glow faintly pierces through the darkness. The blue glow came from Josh’s entire right hand that he was holding up in closed fist, lighting up the cave with the use of his mysterious Spiritual Powers. Josh looks around his surroundings with curiosity. “Rain was that you or am I going insane.” He still walks further in the dark cave. “Joshy boy,” the ghostly female voice continues calls out. “Rain,” he queries, looks side to side of the cave with both his brown eyes. “Where are you?” She giggles in amusement. “I’m over here, Joshy boy.” Josh guides his glowing fist over in the right direction from where Rain’s voice coming from. The light reveals another cave mouth, looking with hesitation. He was getting a bad vibe from that new part of the cave though his curiosity was too strong for him to just head back instead he wanted to explore further in the mysterious cave. Josh proceeds forward wanting to find out what lies within the darkness of the new entrance. Rain giggle echoes out. “You’re getting warm, Joshy boy, very warm.” Josh walks further in the cave and listens to the sound of water dripping down from the many sharp peaks that hangs from the stone ceiling. He moves the glowing light side to side, revealing the moist cave walls. “You’re getting hot,” Rain’s ghostly voice, echoes through the moist air. He still walks further in the new part of the cave, guiding his glowing fist in the directions he was looking. “Hotter,” she says within the thick darkness. Josh looks with curiosity while walking through the darkness. “Come on, Rain. It is dangerous to play this kind of game in this environment.” Utter silence soon reigns through the darkness… “Rain,” he queries as he stops and stands in the cave. Josh looks around the cave with curiosity at the sudden silence from Rain. Rain still does not say a word… Josh looks around his surroundings with worry. “Rain, are you ok!” The dripping sound gets louder… “Rain,” he cries out, looking around his surroundings with a worry gaze still. Josh stops looking at the sudden feel of something on his left side of his face. He looks with curiosity, raises his left hand up to his face and feels moisture there. “What the hell?” Josh rubs his hand against the moisture and feels the warmth and thickness of it. He looks with confusion in reaction. Removes his hand from his face, holds them in front of his face. Josh guides the glowing light over to his right hand to reveal someone’s blood was on his hand. “Blood,” he queries, looking with surprise and realizes that blood was the moisture that is on his face. He raises his head up slowly, looking up at the dark ceiling with confusion, trying to see where the blood was dripping. “I thought you love to play games, boy,” a cold male’s voice yells out through the darkness. When Josh heard that voice, he starts to look around the dark cave with seriousness though confusion. “What!” A loud chilling scream from Rain echoes through the darkness… “Rain,” Josh cries out, feeling his heart racing from that scream, looking around in the cave with fright in his brown eyes. The dripping gets louder and louder through the limitless darkness… Josh looks around the cave, guiding his glowing fist to see what was happening within the cave. He widens both his eyes in shock though fear, seeing rain of blood falling from the dark stone ceiling quickly. Feels the rain of blood getting all over his body, staining his clothing, hair, and skin that causes him to look with overwhelming fear in his eyes. Josh starts to back up, his fear was too much for him to respond at a quick pace instead he was moving slowly. After a few steps back, he feels something underneath the sole of his left shoe. Josh stops and looks behind him to see that something large was behind in the darkness. He looks with hesitation though fear about the large thing. Josh removes his foot from the object, he turns around and looks down at the mysterious object that lies on the moist, stone ground in the darkness. A deadly chill comes… Josh listens to the sound of the blood rain echoing through the darkness. Feels more of the blood getting all over him though at this point he did not care anymore instead he was concern to what lies in front of him. He guides the glowing light towards the mysterious object, his eyes widens in great shock from what the bright glow of his Spiritual Powers was revealing. The smooth cerulean light reveals…. The blood soaked corpse of a young woman with long brown hair, wearing a ripped and torn white gown, lying in her own pool of blood… Josh starts to breathe heavily while looking down at the girl’s corpse with fearing gaze. “R…Ra…Rain…” His body trembles in reaction to the great fear he is feeling. Falls down on both his knees, the light goes away from this distraction. Josh lifts Rain’s corpse up a bit, as he embraces her even though he knew that she could not respond back to him. “Rain,” he sadly says, closing both his eyes and presses his face against her cold, moist face. Josh starts to rub the back of her head in a slow, gentle motion. “Rain... No…” Tears from his soul seeping pass his closed eyes, mourning for her as he holds her near him in comfort. Josh ignores the feel of the warm blood rain pouring down on him, as he continues to hold her in his arms. A presence of a mysterious dark force chills the air… “You can have her,” a dark voice says. Josh opens his eyes, as he raises his head up and looks towards the direction of the voice with a tearful though curious gaze. The presence reveals itself in the darkness, showing a greatly large demonic figure with a dark red aura radiating from its being. “She has served her purpose…” The evil figure glaring down at both, Josh and the decease Rain, with its glowing crimson eyes. Josh looks up at the glowing figure with a fearful though anger gaze. He still clinging to Rain’s corpse tightly, trying to protect her even though she was dead. “And so have you,” the dark being says, raising its large right arm in the air, forming a large light green energy sword in his grasp. “Die!” Demonic figure brings down the sharp, burning sword quickly to smite down on both, Josh and the decease Rain. Josh closes his eyes, embraces Rain even tighter than before, as he prepares to die with her in his arms. “No,” he yells out, opens both his eyes quickly, looking around his surroundings, seeing that was back at the cave entrance with Hayato. Realizes all that he saw was a horrible dream of his past. He places a hand on his face, feeling cold sweat tickling under his palm, shaking up from the visions he saw in his mind. “Rain…” Josh feels something nudging against him. He looks over there, seeing a concern looking Hayato staring up at him. Josh smiles as he removes his hand from his face and lowers it down to the top of Hayato’s head. “It is ok, partner. I just had a bad nightmare.” He pets Hayato in a gentle gesture, looking down at him with content. Hayato closes his eyes, he makes a soft sound, savors the feel of his master’s touch. “Must have been the Mexican food I eaten earlier,” he jokes, continues to stroke Hayato on his head. Hayato opens his eyes, looking out of the cave mouth. He stands up on all four legs, looking with a serious glare, as he starts to growl with a firm tone. “Hmm,” he curiously says when seeing Hayato acting that, removing his hand from his growling wolf. “What, what is it?” Josh soon follows Hayato’s gaze, as he looks with surprise though curious gaze from what he saw in the distant. “What?” A nun stands in slight distance from the cave… “A nun,” he queries himself, looking at the distant nun with confusion. Josh instantly knows that this nun was different from the normal ones by the unique way her nun attire was. The nun’s color was not the normal black and white instead it was dark blue and light purple, her eyes was cover from a piece the hood, only revealing her small lips that was cover by pink lipstick. “Ok, I must have stay in the heat too long because I am now seeing mirages,” he says with disbelief, looks at the nun with a surprise gaze. Josh glances over to Hayato, as he sees his wolf still growling in that way. “If Hayato sees her, then I have not lost my mind yet.” He faces the nun once more with slight curiosity. Hayato lowers himself down a bit, as he gets in a striking stance. His growls becomes louder than before, revealing his many sharp fangs, glaring at the mysterious nun with seriousness. Josh sharpens his eyes in a serious glare, as he senses a strong force of Spiritual Power and an unknown force coming from the mysterious nun. “What an incredible power… This is truly not one of those normal nuns.” “Rain misses you,” the nun softly says in pure innocence. Josh looks with surprise when hearing her words. “What?” “Rain misses you, Josh Starwind,” the nun says softly. Josh lowers his hand down to Hayato, places it on top of his head, as he starts to stroke his companion in a gentle touch, tries to calm Hayato down. Hayato continues to growl though comes to a stop. He makes a soft sound, sits down on the solid ground, licks his lips and looks towards the nun with a now curious gaze. Josh looks back at the nun with curiosity. “How do you know about us? Who are you?” The nun does not reply to him, as she just stands there in the hot desert… “Answer me,” he yells out, glaring at her with anger, wanting to know how the mysterious nun knows about him and Rain. “That is for another time,” the nun says with innocence. Josh’s body trembles in anger, as he looks with an angry gaze. “Oh no you don’t, you are going to give me answers now!” He runs out of the cave and out in the desert, heading towards the mysterious nun, wanting answers to his questions. Before Josh approaches the nun, a small dust tornado appears right in between them… “Gah,” he yells out, raising both his arms up to his face, shielding his eyes from the sand. “Where the hell did this come from?” He feels his short, messy jet-black hair moving wildly from the fast winds. “For another time indeed,” the nun’s voice says within the rapid spinning cyclone. The dust tornado disappears, as the nun was gone… Josh removes his arms from his face, sees that the nun was there anymore, as he looks with slight anger from that. “Who was that…?” He looks forward at the place where the nun once stood. Hayato approaches the confused Josh. He now sits beside him, glances up to his master then over to where Josh was looking. Josh stands there in silence, thinking about that mysterious nun he met, wondering how she knew about his relationship with Rain… A beeping sound comes from Josh’s sport watch, breaking the moment of silence. “Hmm,” he queries softly, holds the watch up near his face, seeing that was late. “Oh damn, I am going to be late.” Josh looks over to Hayato with a slight worried gaze. “Come on, Hayato!” He begins to run forward quickly. Hayato stands up on all four legs, as he starts to follow his master through the desert. Josh is unaware that the nun is standing at an edge of one of the random cliffs that surrounds the large wasteland… She watches Josh intently, as she has something in mind for him… What could this mysterious nun have in mind for Josh? Is this nun friend or foe, and how does she know about the young woman he was once close to? Hopefully Josh can find the answers to all these questions before it could be too late…
