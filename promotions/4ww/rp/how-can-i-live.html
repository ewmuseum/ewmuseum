What if I did the impossible, and survived to tell the story over and over. I will remember the places we’ve been you and I. Our lives are slipping away and I don’t want time to pass us by. I have made my final choice so now it’s your turn. Will you plummet and perish? Or will you use your wings to fly up to new heights?~ {The scene comes to us at a small bar like area. Small lights filter the area as wee see billiard tables, tables where we see men passed out. Off to the left we see a table full of people calling for rounds as the waitress brings the table two sets of rounds. We pan over to the corner and see the ever famous El Lobo de la Muerta slumped down. We make our way over to see the man sitting in the floor with two or three empty bottles of tequila all around him. He has not changed for days as we can see the dirt all over his clothes and face. His blonde hair is all matted together as his hand moves toward the bottle. He grabs it and lifts it to his mouth trying to take a big gulp but nothing comes out. He holds it infront of him upside down with nothing coming out. He shakes it before throwing it down on the ground. He scrummages through the bottles trying to find one that has something inside it. After searching frantically he whistles and the waitress brings him a beer. He looks at it and takes a big gulp and kills it immediately. He looks at it and tosses it away from him. The bottle soars through the air and hits one of the men at the table. The man gets up as some beer is seen on his maroon hoody. He walks over and looks at El lobo laughing. He then picks Lobo up by the neck holding him against the wall by the neck. Immediately Lobo throws his head forward hitting the man in the nose with his forehead. The man lets go when Lobo throws a punch to the stomach and then rams the mans head into the wall before throwing him down. Lobo goes to the bar and takes a shot of tequila before he steps on stage. He looks at the band and makes a motion of some sort and they begin to play what sounds to be a hardcore song. Lobo steps to the mic and looks down.
