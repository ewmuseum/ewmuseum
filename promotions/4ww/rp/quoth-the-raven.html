Quoth the Raven 4WW Exposures #908 Date: 08/30/04 Location: ODE Quoth The Raven Darek DeAngleo _______________ ::[Introduction]:: _______________ The destiny of a warrior now hung within the trust and fate in his guide. Their mission would be to endure the trails, to redeem themselves. For William, this was an opportunity to do what his guide could not, he was beckoned to this fate by a higher power. The trials were salvation for Darek, they would free his tortured soul, raptured by sin. He would take his rightful place amoung the Elders, watching the people with God's willfull eyes. Together they were to endure, to survive the trails. Failure, hardly an option, if they failed, Darek would be damned for the sins of his demons and William, he would give his life. Burned alive, as an example of what happens to the weak. Their trust and faith had to be in each other, if it wasn't they would both fall to the excrusiating challenges that lie ahead. Darek had everything riding on this mission, he was destined to return to the glory of God. To sit next to his creator, the one who made him who he is. But Darek wasn't sure who he was, at one point in his life he was sure he was Dark Angel. A horrid monster that did what it wanted, when it wanted. The Dark Angel was maniacal, manipulative endbringer, he toyed with the emotions of people and fed from their fear. Darek knew the way Dark Angel thought, the way it felt... for he lived it's sins. It was easier then, when he was angery he would take his frustrations out on whomever or whatever stood in his path. Living as the Angel, Darek was free to destroy, pillage, he would never have let the Brigade get away with what had transpired after the Flaming Casket match. This monster was out of control and surely would have never stopped until the entire Brigade and those involved had payed for their mistakes. However, that feeling was gone. Instead Darek felt almost compelled to understand the nature of these beings. If he had been possessed to do such evil, why would those not possessed then comit such travesties. He felt the desire to forgive, to understand their mistakes. Darek felt nothing but hatred for Fnord, but somehow as he has seen Wesley Williams struggle, he's longed to understand the man behind the monster. Their encounter had left a mark in Darek's mind and soul, together the two of them would attempt to topple the Mountain of St. Hells. The ignorance of Darek's hatred perhaps blinded him from knowing that Wesley had nothing to do with the dasterly plot. But then that had to change his entire vision, was Patti really behind the whole thing as he had thought and assumed? Could he forgive Welsey and not Patricia? There was nothing left but confusion and frustration in the mind of the fallen angel. Destined to now follow the light of the lord, to walk the path of redemption, Darek knew had had to forgive these people for their sins. If only it was that easy, their souls were still morally filthy with their indescretions. It was Darek's mission to stand before these beings and cleanse their souls. To bring them free of the darkness, give them a fresh start on life. If Darek was destined to forgive Wesley, or Patti, that would mean he would have to face them in combat. The purification would come from his judgement, shoud he prevail, their souls would be cleansed and if he failed... he would never give up. Again and again, Darek would attempt to bring purification to the land. Following the path laid for him, Darek finds himself being outdone by one Outlaw, Kyu Kushinga. The Outlaw had claimed the rightful matchup against Patricia, his will bent on destroying what remained of Shadows Ascension. To cleanse Patti's soul, Darek had to stand before her, face to face. This was a mission that he once thought he would enjoy, however the more and more he looked into Raven's eyes he could see the love she had for Patricia. Darek was confused, left to wonder if this was right path to walk. When he looked Patti in the eyes in Pheonix, he knew there was good within her but her defences were so high even the bravest may not attempt to get through to her. She was strong in her ties with the Order, having Sean McClancy as her fiancee, this prevented alot of people from bringing her down. yet Kyo seemed hell bent on his mission to do so, and so did Darek. Then, when Darek closed his eyes, he remembered the face of Raven, he remembered how she fit into the scenario. Residing within her was someone else's soul. The soul of a tortured child, one destined to die at the hands of Nagata. William and Darek were bound together, not only within the trials, but how their past and present entwined together. _____________ ::[Flashback]:: _____________ Darek: I hear your words William. I can see how you and I differ but I can see how we are the same. Our future and the future of the Winds relies upon our shoulders... this I know. Come with me and well will discus matter that need attention. Syckle: There is just one more thing that I need to say in this tale before we leave. One night she changed into the wolf and someone saw her. They blamed her for witchcraft and burned her at the stake. I had only come to find out later that it was her brother that stired up the accusations of witchcraft. I then felt there was no reason to take the last trial and there is my failure. But God did take pity and transformed me into the wolf you know today. For the mere chance that I will find Nagata and serve him his justice. [Syckle shrugs Angel's hand off his shoulder and then looks him dead in the eye.] Syckle: Angel and I have found him...I have found Nagata. [Syckle's eyes grow a brilliant blue and a light glows from his hand.] Darek: Where? Syckle: Nagata is Nathaniel...Nathaniel Essex. I have told you this for one reason and one reason only Angel. You may lose something or someone very valuable to you and if you do you must not stop the trials. Darek: If you're referring to Raven, I will not stand to lose the LAST member of my family. That cannot and will not happen as long as I have a breathe in my body. Syckle: Because you are going to have to finish something that I started a century ago. That something is the one reason that I left Essex alive. Darek: And would it be worth it? My final blood for your lost mission, for my salvation? I'd rather Die. [Syckle lets his eyes grow brighter and he slams Darek against a wall. Dareks cold blue eyes glow to match the same brightness as Syckle's, He lifts his hands up to Syckles and breaks the grip.] Syckle: Darek one of the three girls that Nagata would have killed was Raven...or the soul that resides in Raven now! ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ To what means must a being fight, and keep fighting? I am destined to stand before Munson, Epic, Smiley and Nilsson in a Submissions and Pinfalls only matchup. Once the team of the Wind pulverises the immediate threat, they shall climb atop the foot of the mountain, then never look back. We will become known as the greatest nation in the PrimeTime, the strongest, smartest, most physically dominant species alive today. But aside from the Survival, I have known for a couple weeks now that I would soon be facing Blaise McClancy in a return match up from the horrid Flaming Casket match. Albeit, I had some help in that match, but none of it was asked for and none was needed. Truthfully, the match itself was just a set up to what was going to happen afterwards. Shoudou... I put my life out for Patti against a masked assassain, the cousin of Sean and a dryad. The match ended in my favor, but the end of the match ended the reign of Dark Angel. Something I should owe Blaise and company thanks and an beatting for. Yet, I find myself wondering what motivates this man to do what he does, what makes him a monster. The nature of the dryad is typically a peaceful spirit unless otherwise threatened, I can understand Dark Angel opposed him but what had Patti done? And now, why would he not continue to persue her? Under the strings of the order, Blaise is commanded by the Brigade. His mission is set before him and he does as is expected, just as I once did. Now, Blaise and I are destined to stand before each other again, this time there will be no excuses, everything rests on the ability to endure this battle. To me, Blaise was a pinnacle of athletic ability, he had dominated many in fast, painful matches. But he is also a stepping stone, a path to Patricia Anderson and Kyo Kushinaga. Maelstrom V draws precariously near and here I am to fight in two, perhaps three of the biggest battles of my life. The last time I met Blaise, he took Dark Angel to hell and back, the dryad has an uncanny ability to dominate. This match, this war, it would be more physically draining than the last. If I should suceed, there would be no reason to hold me back. Ian would have to see that I am destined to purify Patti and Kyo for their sins. First I must get through Blaise, not an easy task at all or one that excites me. This will be a painful encounter for the both of us, however Blaise must be cleansed for his role in the downfall of Dark Angel. Once I have taken him down, he will truly understand what Sean has rid the KnightMare Brigade of, the most dominant being in the Four Winds and soon the PrimeTime. The Sruvival, Blaise and if I can walk the path of redemption and cleanse Blaise's soul, just perhaps I'd have the opprtunity to do so to Patti. I will stand before Blaise and in a match based on pure wrestling rules and fight, knowing that I have nothing left to lose and everything in the world to gain. My success depends on my faith, I shall walk through the valley of darkness to prove my faith to the lord. He will guide me through this war and when it's all said and done, maybe I can understand the man behind Blaise McClancy. Though the task over the next few week will be grueling, I promised not to let the Winds down. Our team will take the PrimeTime by storm and I will cleanse the Winds of it's sins. Standing atop the Moutain, I shall watch the Four Winds as master of the wind, true champion. My legacy will not stop here, spreading to the PrimeTime, the world will know the name Darek DeAngleo and the word that he spreads. The word of God. I find myself contemplating what Syckle has done for Raven. He had gone out of his way to ensure the safety of a girl who means the world to me, yet is someone entirely different. Raven's soul is taken by the spirit of one of Nagata's victims. My life has been full of misfortune and suffering, now I learn my last surviving family member is consumed by the spirit of another. What the hell has my life become? One tragedy after another, it seems as though everything has set before me to try my patience, my understanding. I know that when I walk the path of redemption all will be right in the end, but for now the sitaution keeps getting more dismal. _____________ ::[The Story]:: _____________ The night sky casts an enchanting spell over the city. the hazy black and midnight blue, accentuated by the twinkle of the golden sparlkes. Everything seemed so peacefull when you lost yourself in the night sky. However, that was not the case, below in the city the world was booming. The night had brough out all different sorts of people, prowlers, deviants, party goers the list went on. Tonight was no different, these people were out and about. The dark haze in the sky blocked the moon from shining it's light down. Though the light was not needed, the world had it's own light for the night time, and until all hours of the morning they shone. People mingled amougst each other, while others fraternized with the opposite sex, some even began to cause an uproar. The scene was intense, hot and full of excitement, it lured all types of people, but mostly it brought the highest class of appetites. These people were hungry, but not for food, for a good time, willing to do anything and anyone to get it. The lights to the O.D.E. sign hummed as the line up had extended well down the city street. People had been dressed in skirts, suits, dresses, casual wear, hair and make up done, for what? To wait in line, in the rain, William was fair to all, but first he catered to the needs of his clientel, colleagues and friends. Large men, dressed in black suits and shades watched the door with a list in hand. The club had just recently opened, but it's unique cuture and style appealled to the senses of all sorts of people. The water had begun to fall more fiercely now than before, pulling into a reserve parking stall, a red Hummer comes to a stop. The door opens up and Darek steps out onto the street, just missing a puddle. Dressed in a black suit, with a baby blue silk shirt, he sports a gold crucifix around his neck and blue wire frame Oakly shades. Closing the door to his Hummer, he begins to walk towards the club, reaching back he pushes the button on a small black remote arming the alarm and locking the doors. The horn sounds to signify the alarm has been set and Darek continues towards O.D.E. Standing before the two large men, Darek nods and the two seperate allowing him into the club. The people in line watch as the mammoth near seven foot man enters the club. Darek marched through the dimly lit, sanctuary for night people to a back office. Stopping before the door, Darek reaches out and knocks three times and then steps back waiting for a response. Within a few seconds, the door swings open and Darek walks in. Coming to a stop just inside the room, Darek reaches back and pushes the door closed. He turns his head back and looks William in the eyes. 'We need to sit down and discus some things,' Darek turns full bodied towards his comrade and walks into the center of the room. Syckle, sitting at a desk in a high black, leather chair, doesn't move, 'then by all means, take a seat vampire.' Sighing, Darek nods and moves over to the small leather sofa at the side wall. Standing before it, he reaches down and braces himself as he takes a seat on the low couch. An awkward moment of silence falls over the room as Darek glances around the office taking in the surroundings. Various works of art, mostly dark, and with a hidden undertone message are scattered across the walls. Darek brings his hands underneath his chin as he leans forward, looking at the back of William's chair. 'Talk.' Syckle demands. 'That is after all what you came to do, is it not?' 'It is. I guess we'll skip the unpleasantries and just get straight to the point.' Darek says almost with disdain coming from his mouth. Syckle notting the bitting tone to Darek's voice, spins the chair around and comes to face him. Raising an eyebrow William smirks at Darek. Darek lifts his head off his hands, nodding at William he continues. 'What exactly are your intentions William, I think you know more than you're letting on to. There are things you know, reasons that we are all tied together and you aren't telling me.' Laughing a little, William shakes his head in disbelief and turns back to his work at the desk. 'I've told you all that I know Darek. I have gone out and searched for answers to your questions and mine aswell. So many questions and so few answers. That is the boat we lie in.' Darek shakes his head, 'look William, you live a double standard, I know this. You dedicate your life, your time to the Four Winds, to the cause we have begun to fight for. But secretly, I know you fight for something else.' William's ears seem to stand on end, knowing that Darek could may have well uncovered some form of the truth. Do you have a video player in here?' The leather chair spins around once again and William points up to a small black cupboard. 'In there.' Reaching over to the side of his desk, he grabs a long black remote and pushes a button at the top of the control. A large flat screen television unfolds from the ceiling and the picture turns on. Static is seen at first, then the word: VCR written in green in the top right side. Darek rises from his spot on the sofa and moves over towards the cupboard pulling a cassete from the inside of his jacket pocket. 'What's this all about?' 'You'll see, I just have a couple questions for you.' Darek opens the door and slides his tape into the player. The screen flickers and then begins to play the tape. Play glows, written on the screen then disappears as the static is cleared and the tracking finds a clear picture. 'Based on what I saw on this tape.' Darek turns to the screen, but glances in William's eyes first. ------------- ::[Footage]:: ------------- Syckle laughs again. Syckle: Patricia is as good as dead in my eyes Kyo. I would love to plant a dagger straight into her heart if she had one. I have one stake in this and that is Raven, the little girl as long as Raven walks away from this unfractured more than she is then I will be a happy man. There is one thing that I can't stand in this world and that is someone that uses a child as a pawn and that is the only thing that Patti keeps Raven around for. The Vampire that is of Raven's blood is still too much infatuated with Patricia for his own good. Kyo intently listens to Syckle's words and takes a couple of puffs from his cigarette and breathes the smoke pass both nostril. He stares Syckle with a carefree still since this very conversation began. Kyo Kushinaga: So you're after Patricia because of Raven. Interesting... Kyo tilts his head to the side and glares at Syckle. Kyo Kushinaga: However, you still haven't answered my question... What the hell do you want from me...? Syckle walks around Kyo and goes to sit on the hood of his car. Syckle: Kyo there are prevailing forces coming from each realm and I believe you know what I am talking about. I don't ask for a friendship, alliance, but I ask for an understanding Kyo. Syckle gets off the hood of his car and stares Kyo in the face. His eyes now glowing azure. Syckle: An understanding that Patricia my die, the Vampire may fade as well along all the other demons that hide in this form of a federation. But the girl never gets harmed. Kyo takes a single step closer to Syckle not even being intimidated by his glowing the same color of his as he simply smirks and blows a cloud toxic smoke pass from the corner of his lips. Kyo Kushinaga: You know, people have called me names ever since I have awakened. Monster... Hell Spawn... And even the common, 'Freak of Nature'... Honestly, I don't give a damn about Raven I mean if she doesn't cross my path or doesn't grow up to be like Patricia then we're cool but if that girl turns just like her then you will get a visit from me. Kyo seriously glares at him. Kyo Kushinaga: You want an understand and you got one... Are we through here or do you talk about sports? He sarcastically grins, Syckle matches Kyo's grin and takes the cigarette out from between his lips he takes a drag from the cigarette and blows a puff of smoke into Kyo's face. Syckle: If Raven grows into either a being like Patti or Darek then she will have a day of reckoning with me. Syckle takes the cigarette and puts it out on his open palm. Syckle: Kyo you shouldn't smoke these things they'll kill you. ----------------- ::[End Footage]:: ----------------- William stops the tape and sighs, as he turns his chair to face Darek. 'I think I know what you're thinking. Why Kyo? Right?' Darek nods, 'he can't be trusted. He works according to his own agenda. I offered him the opportunity to bring Patti down, then decide the better man of the two of us. Instead he insulted me, and while playing smug and coy the incompetant fool decided to stand before my wrath.' 'An outcome that has yet to be determined, trust me when I tell you this and don't get angry.' William pauses for a moment, 'only the strongest will survive that match. Between Patricia being encompased by her great ancestors, the original Goddess of Love and Kyo a savage, unkillable Outlaw, you must be on the top of your game. And in my opinion you have been more focused about climbing Mount St.Hells and completing your trials to stand before them.' Darek glares at William over at the desk, he nods. Perhaps William was just telling him what he needed to hear. But if it was the truth, it could become a painful reality. Darek ejects the tape and returns it to the inside of his jacket. 'You may be right, perhaps I'm not ready. But atleast I can be there, I can try to change the future. I have to save the world William, even if it is one person at a time. Patricia Anderson, Kyo Kushinaga, they are both the primary targets in my sight.' Darek strides past William and moves back to the sofa, before sitting, William swings his chair to face Darek. Darek sits and smiles, 'I wanted to thank you for looking into the intrests of Raven. But we cannot trust Kyo. Not until he has been cleansed.' William rises up from his chair and moves over to a two way mirror, glancing down at the people out in the club. 'I do what I have to do, to ensure I get what I am looking for. If he fails to hold up to his word, he deals with me, not you.' William spreads his arms across the window mirror and sighs. 'Everything has to walk their own line vampire. You walk yours and I walk mine. My line tells me that Raven will play a significant role we're yet to see.' The lights in the club fade and the heavy drumline and bass of the next song begins to pick up. Soon the strobes begin to pulsate, multicolored screens change the appearance of the darkened room. The people all appear to be having a good time as most people are dancing or conversing. William nods as he watches the people peruse around. 'This building is filled with sin, I can imagine how you feel standing in here. It is now your duty to right the wrongs of sin and I too have my duties. I hope you can understand this.' Darek gets up from the sofa and moves over about five paces away from William. William glances over his shoulder and nods at Darek. 'Are you ready?' 'Haven't heard you say that in years.' Darek adds. 'I've never meant it as much as I do now.' William turns back to watching the people. Walking towards the door of Syckle's office, Darek reaches down for the handle and pulls it wide open. Stepping halfway through the door, Darek stops and looks back at William. 'I hope it's not as eerie as you make it sound. I've got so many things o my mind, I can hardly begin to fathom taking a lose in these trials. You are putting yourself on the line for me. I don't want to fail you or our cause.' 'Faith Angel. It's a marvelous concept.' Darek walks out and closes the door behind him. Making his way through the crowd, he gets to the fron enterance and passes by the security once again. They wish him a good night and he moves over to his reserved stall, pushing the unarm button on his alarm remote. He opens the door and moves into his Hummer. Pulling the tape from his jacket, he places the tape into a VCR in his glove box and pulls down a 22 inch plasmascreen Television. Darek rewinds the tape to the very begining. Soon, it stops rewinding and begins to play as he pulls out of the parking lot. Featured is Shoudou taking on Dark Angel, two alter ego's meeting in a flaming casket match. One that saw the Angel win with the assistance of his allies, this time, Darek was prepared to face Blaise and win. He had studied their previous match on end and spent countless hours training for the trails. A test of endurance, heart, will, strength and other qualities that now honed Darek's skills for battle against Blaise. The battle loomed on the horizon and Darek knew that he had to overcome the monster before he would be worthy of facing Kyo Kushinagan and Patti Anderson. Coming to a stop at the traffic lights, Darek was set up in a fancy hotel downtown, just a few blocks away from the club. It came with recommendations from William. Well worth the three thousand a night, Darek was treated well and trained in a private gym daily to prepare. The light turned green and Darek accellerated forward, it was a quick travel to the hotel where Darek pulled in and retired for the night. _____________ ::[The Moral]:: _____________ There comes a time in one's life when making the decision to fight for what they believe in, becomes the only course of action. Syckle and Darek walk seperate paths leading to different destinies, but both are intertwined together, both willingly and unwillingly. The truth is, we all rely on one another, without a loser, there can be no winner, without Blaise, Darek could not earn the right to face Kyo and Patti. Blaise had become a sequential factor in the equation of Darek's life, it was fate to stand before this once masked assassain and fight in the heat of war. Clashing together, a winner would be determined and said person would walk out holding the South Wind Championship. A title that Darek had famed recently with a legthy run, defeating some great opponents and building a name for himself and others since doing so. It all comes down to the battle that lingers at StormFront, two titans shall collide and when they do, the very foundation of our great land will shake. When the war is done and a victor has emerged, the land will know whom the great warrior of the land is. Would it be Blaise McClancy, member of the Order, cousin of the famed Sean McClancy, a dryad... or would it be the former angel of death, the powerless but determined Darek DeAngleo. Time would answer this burning question and when it does, a destiny will unfold...
