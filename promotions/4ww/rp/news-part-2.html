News... Part 2 Future Shock 4WW Exposures #954 Date: 9/11/04 Location: Yokosuka, Japan Please. Please end it now. Just tell me that this is not what I think it is? Kanton’s going nuts and having dreams of spirits and stuff. What spirit in their right mind would have to deal with his innane backside? He knows nothing about reality or the veil. But then again, how is he actually going to be ready to face someone who downright owned him the first time? He can’t. He knows this. He’ll choke again. He always seems to do that. But he’s got confidence now. He’s got two wins, one of them against two guys under his belt in a row. Yeah, two whole wins. And where are those that he beat? Well let’s take a look at it… they’re gone. They lost to Kanton and that made them so bad in the ring that they were let go. Either that or they decided that the low part of their career had just happened, and if the master of the career-choke could get a 3 count on them, then they had better head to the new West Wind Academy to train under Blaise’s cousin. You can’t get much lower, now can you? Loose to someone in unStable, other then Lucious Lady Luck, without someone interfereing. Yeah, I said other then Triple L. Most of the guys she’s pinned wanted to lay down with her anyway. But then again, where was Triple L when she faced Future Shock… not winning that’s for sure. All of unStable have fallen, and been proven to be at their rightful spot under the KnightMare Brigade. Yes, the KnightMare Brigade, the KB, and the stable that Blaise is actually a member of. In fact, he’s assigned as the leader of the 4WW faction by virtue of the rank he holds within its parent organization, the Order of the Hammer. But right now, most don’t know that. Most don’t realize just what it is that the KB is about. Unfortunatly it’s been stuck in mire for some time. After the falling of Fnord at the hands of Jerms and Patti Anderson, and then the betrayal of Dark Angel, it’s been not a good time. Yet Shoudou ended up being the one McClancy that could take it by the horns and steer it further. But Ukyo is not a member, at least not yet. But still, this is something that the Ozz Brothers and Kris Kanton need to finally take into account. The KnightMare Brgiade has been one of the biggest and best stables of all of wrestling. Spanning over 6 federations now. Each time it’s worked it’s way to the top and done things that no other stable could do. It’s outlasted many other stables, and has shown to be an almost living entity in it’s own right. But each time it’s had a McClancy at it’s help. Whether it be Blaise’s cousin Fey McClancy, now Fey Childress… or his other cousin, Fey’s twin brother, Sean McClancy… or now, under Blaise himself. The Ozz brothers can’t handle this. Blaise can’t just go in there and even think he has a chance to loose. Kris Kanton can’t handle this. With the retirment and the dissolution of the KnightMare Brigade in the 4WW’s brother federation, the UWF… now the weight of it all, the future of the KB is on Blaise’s shoulders. And this is something that he can’t fail on. He’s the last. Fey is out with her heavy pregnancy. Sean is out, to take part in his last match and then slip off into retirement. It’s all on Blaise now. Honor… Tradition… And to some extent the glory. The news has already rocked him, but fortunatly for him, he’s got Ukyo to rely upon, and let’s also remember Charlene in there. But they’re not with the Order or the KB. Zane asked how you can deal with something that has been around since they were both born. How Future Shock can compete with a couple of brothers. But that is siblings, usually more rivals then actual friends, and it would be illegal for them to be lovers. But there is one thing that is worth more then a simple sibling status: that of a brotherhood. That of a family that extends generations and through more then the Ozz’s can ever comprehend. Then again, they don’t even have full comprehension of the English Language let alone the concept of facing a force that is not going in to a match to loose. Ozz says that there is no way for Future Shock to compete with them. They say that there is no way for them to loose because they’re going in to win. Damnit, Blasie and Ukyo wouldn’t have it any other way. But what do you think they’re doing with this? You think they’re going in there to play tiddly winks with you? You think it’s fun and games for them? You think that because you’re bigger then both of them, and that your brother can do some moves off the top turnbuckle that you’re going to just walk in and walk all over a team that has steamrolled through all of the rest of the tag team competition that has creeped up in the 4WW, only to be slapped down again? Do you really think that your ability to pick someone up and slam them is all that it takes to be a champion? So you really think that because you say you’re ready, that the fates themselves will deem Future Shock to be unworthy of their championship? They are champions by virtue of what they have done. They are champions because everytime they get into that ring, they give it their all and do anything within their power, skills and abilities to win the match and take the win home. They’ve not won their matches by virtue of the illegal use of tactics and weapons. No, they’ve done it by getting a 3 count on their opponents. This isn’t the East Wind Chumps, it’s the East Wind Champions. But don’t worry, they won’t underestimate you. You should be hungry. You should be wanting to do anything to win those belts in your debut match. You should want them ore then breating and eating itself, hell more then living. This is the 4WW. This is the pinnacle of Wrestling Entertainment. This is the abode of Future Shock you’re heading into though. You gamble with things that you should not gamble with. You’re on a high stakes table and and things are getting hot. Bring your ‘A’ game. You’re going to need it. Yokosuka, Japan 30 Miles down the coast from Tokyo. Yokosuka Seaside Park The place of many an independent Wrestling Event We find ourselves backstage at the event. The trio that you know as FutureShock were asked to come to this event to do a cross promotional thing. The crowd, mostly military from the Yokosuka military base were hot and were really into the match. But we won’t bore you with the details. To hype the fact that this fed was selected as the dark matches for the Pay Per View day’s supply to get the crowd hated and ready for the bigger matches of the 4WW later on during the night for Maelstrom V, the 4WW Tag Team or East Wind Champions came and gave the boys what they wanted. And that was the 4WW stomping the proverbial mud hole into the local indy boys. However, it was the perfect way for taking on the Ozz brothers and Kanton this coming week You face a couple of sets of people who are all wet behind the ears, and the effects should always be the same. But again, backstage, just as the trio walk though the doors that lead back to the gymnasium lockeroom area. Blaise is shirtless, as he just left the ring. His wrestling tights are done in a gray and black with blue lighting bolts going down the sides. He’s wearing his high black mocosin style boots and carrying all three of his belts. The dragon tattooed on his chest glistens with sweat as his breathing still shows that he’s coming down off the adrenaline. Ukyo is in her babydoll shirt, and silver pants that have become her trademark. As usual the waistband of the loose fitting pants has drooped during the match, and a slight hint of her underpants can be seen. Her long, dyed blue, hair is matted slightly to her face, even after she brushes it aside. Charlene is wearing her usual business sharp skirt, blouse and jacket to contradict both of the other two and their usual ways. “Well that was actually a pretty good match there. You two are getting closer and closer to almost reading each other’s minds each time you get into the ring.” Charlene trying to make small talk as they head to the far back. Blaise’s demeanour is not one of wanting to do small talk from the looks of it. Ukyo picks it up. “Good, we need to be. We’ve got a big night coming up in a few. I for one feel that I’m not going to want to have to put up with these Ozz brothers too much. They’re going to be trying to get these off of us. It would just not look right if a couple of new guys waltzed in and just up and…” Blaise stopped and turned around. “They’re not going to. I won’t… I can’t let it happen. Don’t worry about it. We’re better. We know this.” He turned back around and kept walking. Charlene whispered to Ukyo. “He’s not taking the news about his cousin well, is he?” “No. He’s acting like he’s the last saviour of his stable now.” “Well, have you talked to him about joining? His face grim knowing what he must do in the coming weeks. This time he’s got himself a camera operator and a few grips and other people to help him. Blaise looks to be about to just keep walking past him. “Wait, wait. Mr. McClancy, Blaise!” Matt breaks the proper honorable thing to do and stops doing the interview while some bald headed Japanese hoss was spouting off into the microphone. The big man glares at Matt as Blaise stops. “What the hell do you want?” “Well, since we’re here, and you’re the team that came to represent the 4WW here.
