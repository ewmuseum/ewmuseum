Arrogance Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #55 Date: July 19, 2003 Location: Home Arrogance is a noun described as an overbearing manner, intolerable presumption; an insolent pride. A word that describes this weeks opponent, 'Simply Stunning' Sean Summers. Hmmm. You might wonder why Destiny chose this word this week instead of the normal insults. Well, its simple. This week, destiny has placed a rather annoying opponent in front of her. A man that is a adelfi malakas. A homosexual wanker. A man who's arrogance can be smelled like its a cheap cologne. Maybe instead of him trying to talk the talk, walk the walk deal, maybe he needs to study his opponents a little better. Instead of poking fun at the ones that have lost to her, maybe he should review the tapes a little closer. Patti Anderson learned from the best and took on not one, not two, but THREE different competitors. Destiny must really be laughing at this turn of developments. Why is that you ask? Lets take a candid look at the past. Destiny brought Sean and Patti together, which in turn she brought in a feud with Fey McClancy-Childress. Destiny sat back and watched as Fey sent Patti into a gauntlet match, hoping to humiliate and crush the spirit of her brother's new girlfriend, and guess what, destiny thought otherwise, instead of crushing that spirit, it strenghthend the bond between the two of them. And now destiny sends this woman into battle against a man who should be the poster child for arrogance. Now what will destiny bring into play? We will just have to wait and see what this round brings after the debut of the Thursday Temptest showing. ___________________________________________________________ July 18, 2003 Friday Night Home ___________________________________________________________ Fade in to Patti sitting in her favorite chair. Her long tan legs hanging over the arms of the chair. Her eyes are fixated on the show that is on. A bag of ice is seen on her hand. A remote in the other. On the tv screen looks like a match. From the looks of it, looks to be 'Simply Stunning' Sean Summers. Patti: Why in the bloody hell am I wasting my time watching this bugger wrestle? She stops the tape and rewinds it back a few times. Her hazel eyes not missing a thing. She shuts the tape off. Patti's hand had began to throb a little. She stared down at the bruise that had formed. She knew she shouldnt have punched the wall, but the airhead Eliza just rubbed her the wrong way. Even to assume that she and Sean were getting 'hitched', well that was just bad information being thrown out. Summers must have made that one up himself. Maybe she'll pretend that Summers was Eliza Murphy and just beat him senseless. Without thinking, Patti flexes her bruised fingers. She winces in pain as the numbness wears off. She hit the wall dead center when that 'chippie' had said that. Her attention returned briefly to the tv where a Summers interview was on.. Thank god the people at TaleSmith productions were always willing to help out when needed. Patti sits back and watches. TV Sean: 'Your going to pay for this Patti. If it weren't for you parading your breasts around the ring like you did, I would have drove Syxx's damn head right into the canvas and pinned his ass for the 1..2..3, and none of this would have came about.' Patti: Hmm, parading me breasts around the ring? Your always looking at them, so I just thought I'd give you an up close and personal show of them. How was I supposed to know that it would take away your attention form the match. Besides nobody said you had to look that CLOSELY at them.. Patti giggles, as she continues to watch the interview. Her smile fades to one of anger. TV Sean: 'Sunday the real anger will be vented, to bad for you Patti, that you are the one in my way. Woman or not, I will not hesitate to knock your teeth down your damn throat. And don't look at that as a threat, look at it as a damn promise...your demise comes soon.' Patti giggles as she throws the now thawed ice bag at the tv. She couldnt believe what she just heard. Patti: OOOOOOO!! Big mean strong Sean threatening to kick my wittle arse. That will be the day. Didnt he pay close attention to the gauntlet match? I took out Bad Boi, Shocker, AND Syxx Figures. What did he do??? He lost to someone that he should have been able to take. Instead he spends all his time crying that he got beat. Maybe he should go back to downtown Phoenix and find himself a fellow adelfi malakas, go back to his place or the nearest cheap hotel. He can put that wanker of his to some good use. I don't think so Summers. You think the little peep show you got at Tempest was enough to lose the match? How are you going to handle it when you are in the ring with the Darling of the 4 Winds? How are you going to handle being in the ring with a REAL woman? Oh wait, you dont know what its like to be around a woman. Guess this Sunday will be the FIRST real encounter with a woman, and one I hope you dont soon forget. Patti removes herself from her chair, bend down, picks up the bag of water and heads to the kitchen. She undoes the knot that was in the top. She steps into the kitchen where she finds Sean McClancy sitting. His back is to her. She gets a wicked smile on her face. She walks cautiously up behind Sean then quickly dumps water on him. The water still very cold soaks Sean from the head to about the middle of his chest. His muscles showing through Patti's xtra large 'Darling of the 4 Winds' shirt. Patti: HEY!! That's me shirt yer wearing. Ya bloody better well give it back to me if ya know what's good fer ya. Sean winks at Patti, and takes off for the bedroom, Patti in hot pursuit. Clothes making a trail as they go. ___________________________________________________________ Fade to Black
