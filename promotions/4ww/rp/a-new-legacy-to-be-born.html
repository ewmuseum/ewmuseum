A New Legacy To Be Born?

A New Legacy To Be Born? Patti Anderson 4WW Exposures #590 Date: 03/22/04 Location: The Hotel Room Legacy: 1 : a gift by will especially of money or other personal property : 1. BEQUEST 2 : something transmitted by or received from an ancestor or predecessor or from the past <the legacy of the ancient philosophers> Why is it that I have this strong hatred going on involving Toddy Boy? I think it would have something to do with the fact that he is holding my precious North Wind Title hostage in his camp of losers and freaks of nature. As his last two promos have pointed out, he’s getting cocky over facing me for a second time, which is all find and fun for me. It took me a second try to win the South Wind Title, and I have a feeling that it will take me a second try to gain the North Wind Title, then it will happen at Maelstrom 3: Legacy. You see Toddy, Sean Summers tried to end any chance of me getting the South Wind Title, and he failed when I claimed it the following week. With you, Toddy Boy, it’s the same way. This is my second chance, a chance that I will not waste like I did on the first time around. I have the thirst for gold. A thirst that you tried to kill when you stole my chances of the East Wind Title. Now you think you will stop it and continue your legacy of being the North Wind champion by calling me weak, pathetic names. It is time for action Toddy. I want what you have. I will take what you have, I will take your title and make it what it should have been months ago. You’re reign is coming to an end. What will the “mighty” Savior do when he is left in the middle of the ring, a broken bloody mess? He will do nothing because he will be strapped to the gurney and be taken to the hospital. I’m pretty sure the nurses will remember you, and be more then willing to bring you the morphine drip for you, cause you will more then likely need it. You see Toddy Boy, I am not afraid to deal with you. You can spat all the stuff about how much better you are, or how much stronger you are, and all this other crap about what you can do. It’s pathetic. You see, Toddy Boy...I have come to terms with what isn’t right anymore and have learned to believe in myself that I can accomplish anything beyond the dreams that I once had. You accuse me of attempting to be something I’m not. Maybe you are right. Maybe I am not the greatest wrestler the world has seen. Maybe I’m not the one who sets on top of the world every night knowing that I can be taken down at a moments notice. I may not be a lot of things, but I am someone who has a heart big enough to attempt to make the dreams come true. I have never EVER once pretended to be something I am not. Yes I was chosen to be in this federation by Fey McClancy. All of it happened to me because I was involved with her little brother. What was your excuse? Oh, that’s right, you had to send your little buddy, Ritoru, in to beg Fey to let you back in. She still hasn’t forgiven you for that nasty little attack you pulled on her. But its ok, we all know how you have to sweat it out over not being able to attack me like you have attacked so many others in the past. Face it Toddy Boy, a new legacy is about to break upon the 4WW. A legacy that will put me up on the ladder, and knock you down to the bottom rung, forcing you to climb it once again. The hold that you and Featured Attraction have, are just a few days away from becoming non-existent. The legacy of Todd “The Savior” Dream is coming to an end at Maelstrom. A new one will be reborn when Jay Phoenix watches me pin you’re shoulders to the mat 1-2-3, then proceeds to lift my hand as he declares me the winner of the match. Place: The Hotel Room Time: That Same Night The shadows from around the room began to pull together in the center of the floor. They rose up from the center taking on the female form. The form began to develop long black hair that reached down to the middle of its back. The demon’s front began to fill out with high perky breasts. The skin took on a pale, gothic look, the eyes came in black as midnight. The hands, pale and white with long black nails. The demon stretched and adjusted to its “human form”. She stepped cautiously to the bed where the form of Patti Anderson laid sleeping. A cruel, evil smile appeared on her face as she ran a long black nail down the cheek. An evil smirk covered the face of the Patti clone. She began to breathe a little harder as if trying to adjust to the oxygen levels. Suddenly, a green like mist slowly starts to admit from its nostrils. The mist slowly encircles the sleeping form of Patti Anderson, levitating her body up and into the air, a quick wave of the hands and both of them are transported to someplace different. A dimension that only be reached by those who are not human. The demon and Patti appear together in the room The room is completely lit by candles, giving it an eerie glow to the room. Mirrors cover all the walls, reflecting the candle light to all around them. The demon and Patti appear together in the room. Patti still sleeping, the demon still smiling cruelly down on her. A soft chant begins that forces Patti to awaken from her safe slumber. “What the bloody hell?? Where the hell am I, and who are you? Patti stares at the almost carbon copy of herself. A moment of fear settles in as she looks at where she is. The room, even though completely based in candle light, freaks her out even more. Patti quickly gets up and backs as far away from herself as possible. The fear mixed with lighting bolts dancing in her eyes forces the demon to take a step back, but only for a second as it begins to speak. “Você deve matá-lo para obter a vitória! Não há nenhuma outra maneira.” “I do not bloody need to kill him. I just need to defeat him.” Patti walked around the room and stared into the various mirrors, seeing reflections of herself and Toddy Boy battling it out for the North Wind title the first time around. The Dreamscape DDT, various punches and suplexes that took her out of the game. Patti shuddered as she stares into each one of the mirrors. “Você deve matá-lo para obter a vitória! Não há nenhuma outra maneira.” “No it is not, now stop bloody saying that. I can defeat him! Now please take me back. I don’t bloody need these tricks and mirrors.” Patti glared at the demonic version of herself. “I can and will take Toddy to the extreme. I do not have to make someone bleed to get the point across. I can defeat Toddy Boy with the fans behind me.” “Usted no entiende. Para derrotarlo, usted debe hacer cuáles usted ha sido destinado ser. ¡cMal!!!!” Patti gasped in surprise as she stared into one of the other mirrors and saw what she didn’t want to see. Her subconscious mind showed her hoisting Todd Dream up in a suplex and dropping him full force across the back of the steel ring posts, snapping his back in half. Patti stepped away in shock as she ran to the next mirror. This new mirror showed her snapping Todd’s jaw in a chin lock that went horribly wrong. A sick sadistic smile on her face as she watched him rolling around on the ground. Patti closed her eyes to the sight. She opened her eyes again, seeing her mounting the turnbuckle with the time keeper’s hammer in her hands. She flips over in her moonsault and nails Todd square in the throat with it, crushing his wind pipe. “NO!! NO!! NO!! I refuse to do it. I will not become evil to win.” Patti ran to the demon and attempted to strike it with all her might. The demon laughed as it reappeared to her. Patti screamed as she quickly kicked the mirror, shattering them into tiny shards of glass. Patti grabs a few of the shards and throws them at the demon, trying to force it away. “Take me back, please. I do not need these bloody visions of you trying to change me. I am what I am, and if you can’t handle it. Then disappear. The confidence is there, you will never get me to change over to what I am not.” A strong flash of lighting hits the mirror as Patti flies up from her bed in the state of shock. Her mind a whirlwind of horror at what she just saw. Patti looks over to see Sean McClancy staring at her. A look of concern donning his face as he stares at her. “Ye okay?” Is all he says has he pulls Patti back into the safety of his arms. He plays with the braids, attempting to find a way to soothe her enough to calm her heart beat. He noticed a look of fear for a brief second, but it was quickly replaced by something that he wasn’t quite sure of. “Yes, just another nightmare of doing bodily harm to Toddy Boy.” Patti cuddled back down as she snuggled up against him. After a few minutes, Patti heard the rhythmic breathing of his, followed by his snores. Patti gently untangled herself from him as she slipped out of the bed, this time to check her reflection in the mirror. The scene fades out as the reflection of Patti’s eyes start to glow red and an evil smile appears on her face.
