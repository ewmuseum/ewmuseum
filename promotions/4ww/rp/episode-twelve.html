Episode Twelve Masked Avenger 4WW Exposures #708 Date: 5/23/04 Location: Location: Charlene and Ukyo’s Hotel Room in Golden Gate at San Francisco, California Time: Noon = [Their room’s lamps are on giving the room great light since a nasty thunderstorm was happening throughout the entire California state as the storm was making the many metropolises of the “Golden State” stand in complete darkness. Charlene, who was wearing a long sleeve black shirt and yellow mini-skirt with a pair of knee-high socks, stood beside one of the room’s bed, packing her suitcase up for the long trip to this week’s StormFront. She stopped packing for a moment and looked out the large window slight surprisingly, seeing as well listening to the rain pouring outside.] = Charlene: I cannot believe that it has been raining like this three days straight now. = [A flash of lightning was seen outside of the window which startled Charlene, listened to the loud booming thunder that echoed throughout the city. She began packing once more though a little bit frightened since she feared lightning and thunder. Meanwhile, near one of the large windows that was on the left side of the room, Ukyo, who was wearing a long baggy and saggy sleeve dark blue shirt and tight black jeans with gray tennis shoes, was sitting at a oak table with her feet propped up there, leaning back in her seat while drinking a can of beer, you noticed a faint pink blush over her cheeks as this was a sign of her becoming drunk. She moved the can from her lips and lightly placed the can on the oak surface of the table, looking out of the window, watching the rain pouring in slight daze.] = Ukyo: You know it is said that when it rains like this it often means that there is now an opening in either Heaven or Hell… = [She glanced over to Charlene amusingly with the same kind of smirk while sitting in the wooden chair in a relaxing way.] = Ukyo: Wouldn’t you say that this is quite ironic since this is the night that Tarrant and my brother fighting tonight. = [Charlene stopped packing and looked at Ukyo angrily though there was slight hint of fear in her eyes when hearing Ukyo’s words.] = Charlene: Don’t say that! Mr. Kyo will be coming out of this match alive! = [Ukyo was now drinking from the open can of beer, though she stopped once more and faced the table with both eyes closed as she looked seriously.] = Ukyo: You heard what Tarrant said, didn’t you? = [Charlene stood in silence while looking in concern as that was a “yes”.] = Ukyo: Tarrant is not after no championship neither glory. He only cares about inflecting pain and torturing his opponents until he finally gets bored or his opponent admits that he is afraid of him. This one is nothing more than a sadistic fucker who finds pleasure in death and misery of others. I know this as does Kyo… = [Charlene made a soft curious sound when hearing her words. Ukyo opened her eyes halfway, facing the table surface seriously though there was slight daze in her eyes.] = Ukyo: Why do you think Kyo was pushing his body to the limits this week? Why do you think that he was not even complaining when you apply all that weight onto his body and made him do all those intense exercises with all that weight on his body? = [Charlene glanced over to Ukyo with concern.] = Charlene: Aren’t you worried about him? = [Ukyo held her beer up a bit and glanced over to Charlene with the same gaze as before.] = Ukyo: Why do you think I am drinking this? = [Charlene closed her eyes and looked modestly with a sweat drop resting on the side of her face.] = Charlene: Uh…getting drunk… = [You noticed there are about ten empty beer cans that are placed in a pyramid standing on the oak table surface near her propped up feet. Ukyo amusingly chuckled once when hearing her comment though she kept his serious gaze on her.] = Ukyo: This is my way to claim my nerves. Charlene: Uh… You do know there are other ways of doing that. = [Ukyo drank the remainder of the beer left in the can, holding the can in front of her while staring at the pyramid seriously.] = Ukyo: Yeah…but this method is better. Anyways, like I was saying, of course I am worried about Kyo. When he was in that Flaming Hell in a Cell match my heart was racing every single I watched that match, thinking that Kyo was either going to be paralyze from head to toe or die that night from all those severe injuries he gotten from that brutal match. = [She now looked at Charlene contently with a playful smirk as the blush across her cheeks took a darker shade of pink.] = Ukyo: But at the end, he beat the odds and proved everyone that he is a tough bastard. That is one thing about my brother is that he does not quit until he gets the job done either it is devil hunting or going up against someone on the 4WW roster. = [Glanced over to the pyramid while holding the empty beer can in her hand, waving it around in a circular motion.] = Ukyo: Even if it is the bottom of the ninth, Kyo will pull something out of his sleeve and surprise all with something insane especially the way he has been acting of late. So everything will end up fine, no? = [Charlene looked confident when hearing Ukyo’s comforting words, nodded her head once in agreement.] = Charlene: Right, I have no doubt that Kyo will beat Tarrant in his own match like he did that fiend, Fnord. = [Ukyo smiled contently when hearing her words. Charlene smiled at Ukyo too but once more began packing while being now put to ease after talking to Ukyo. Ukyo changed her gaze to a concern one as she looked out of the window, staring at the nasty weather.] = “Better come out of this one okay, baby brother… I do not want to visit you in another hospital again I do not want to see you in the pitiful state you would be in if you lose, I do not think I could bare it.” “Or worst…I do not want to say goodbye to another…” “…another family member” Seeking the Truth Pt. He folded the paper up and placed inside his trench coat, glaring down at the busy street seriously while feeling the back of his trench coat floating a bit from the cold wind of the thunderstorm.] = “What is awaiting me at that address…?” = [He looked in deep thought about this while feeling the cold rain pouring down upon him.] = “Could this be another trap planned out by the Blood Brother’s assassin…?” = [He sharpened his eyes seriously.] = “Or could this be for real…?” = [Kyo clutched his hand into a tight fist, feeling the cold droplets of rain trickling down from his fist while he still stood at the ledge of the building.] = “Only one way to find out…” = [He looked to the gargoyles at turns amusingly as he smirked in the same manner.] = Kyo: Sorry guys, I must be going now. He made a soft curious sound when noticing a large metal sign hanging above the twin rusty doors, though he was having trouble reading it because rust have consume parts of the metal sign. Kyo tilted his head to the side, trying to read the writing on the side.] = Kyo: Cr...Crimson… Ra…Rain… = [Kyo sharpened his eyes seriously.] = Kyo: Crimson Rain Agencies… = [Suddenly, the sky erupted in a bright blinding streak of lightning and the loud sound of thunder followed though it sounded like it was reacting to Kyo’s words. Kyo walked over to the doors, standing in front of them as he was searching for the keyhole, though he made a curious sound when feeling the keyhole on the right door of the twin doors. He guided the key over to the keyhole, holding there key there as he was having a look of caution about what could be behind the doors.] = “The truth will set me free…” = [Kyo slipped the key inside of the keyhole, as the scene switched to the inside of the keyhole where you saw the teeth attach to the inside of the keyhole’s gears. He opened the unlock door, revealing a bright blinding light that swallowed Kyo within it as it was hard to see him. Everything was now pitch black as you truly cannot see anything now.
