With the question rephrased, sir, I am happy to answer you. I cannot answer what makes me different from the other vampires that you've seen because, simply, I have not seen through your eyes, nor can I ever. As far as what makes me tick, a difficult question, which I will attempt to answer in brief by telling you about myself, as I presume that is what you're asking. As Scarab said, I am originally from Thebes, and proprietor of an organization of people there... what said organization does, I suppose, is fairly inconsequential, because my life there is happy, my work fulfilling. You might ask, then, what motivated me to begin wrestling, of all things? A question that, at times, I cannot answer myself. I have been uncertain of my origins for some time now, and went to Europe at the behest of a friend who believed I might find some answers there. There, I happened to come across a 4WW flier tacked to a light pole. Ordinary enough, but there was something about it that intrigued me. I had no clue what all this was about, mind you, simply followed an instinct, and ended up starting in the company. There, I discovered that I had essentially followed my own senses to my half-brother, Kyo Kushinaga. Though the story, I suppose, isn't particularly important, he taught me many things about our origins, where our family bloodline comes from... things that I wished to know. He is a part of Global Wrestling now which means that, unfortunately, I have next to no contact with him, as the two companies really have no reason to interact- not anymore, at least. An unfortunate circumstance, really, to lose one's brother in such a manner, but these things happen. As far as what keeps me here... surely you have experienced a time in your life when you've been bored, have you not? And whether you believe in my vampiric nature or not, allow me to simply say that my life has been filled with many long periods of it. Wrestling started out as a simple diversion to fill time for me. It became more than that, however, when I won my first title. I brought glory home with me to Thebes, and the people who support me there, as well as the people who support me in wrestling arenas alike convinced me that it was a worthwhile pursuit. Perhaps you might call it an addiction to the adrenaline rush one receives when stepping out onto a stage in front of one's fans- really no different than what many other wrestlers describe as reasons to continue doing what they do. A diversion, some might say, but a pleasant one. One I enjoy. One I will continue doing until I cease to do so. I suppose that's really no different than any wrestler wrestling these days, no matter what he or she is or claims to be. Vampire is just one label, sir- who I am does not begin or end there, nor should it. Were I simply prancing about on television drinking red wine from a cup and spraying it about all over the fans, I might see your disgust at the idea of 'another' vampire wrestling. I am not, and I do not, which you might perhaps notice if you spent some time investigating my history. And... who is Dawn? Why would you allude to someone else, when it is clear that only Min and Scarab speak here in my defense?
