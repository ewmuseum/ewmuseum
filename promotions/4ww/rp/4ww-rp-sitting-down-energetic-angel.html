[IC] Sitting down with the Energetic Angel

27 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
27 February 2006 So, I know that basically everyone and their dog has already done this, but I just couldn't resist the chance to come out here and meet all you neat people in PTC. Sooooo.... If anyone has any questions they'd like to ask me, or things they'd like to tell me, I'd be MORE then happy to chat! oh yeah...in case people don't know, I'm Min Takahashi, the Energetic Angel! ---- 27 February 2006 When attempting to achieve your usual oh so bouncy state, is it more expedient to drink coffee with lots of sugar, or just eat lots of PixiStix? ---- 27 February 2006 I'm not entirely sure I understand the question, but I'll try my best to answer it. Actually I try to steer clear of coffee entirely, it stunts your growth and at my size that's not really something I can risk. As for sugar, I try not to have too much when i'm going to get into the ring, since it makes it hard to concentrate with all the pretty lights that start popping up. ---- 27 February 2006 Is it true that you are facing a series of lawsuits from such luminaries as the Jumping Bomb Angels and the Energizer Bunny for gimmick infringement? ---- 28 February 2006 The nice people at my legal firm have instructed me not to answer this question... ...but it'd be rude to ignore you right? Sure they don't like what I'm doing in the ring, but it's not like I'm making up a gimmick right? This is just me...well me in a mask, but you get what I mean. ---- 28 February 2006 – ---- 28 February 2006 Min, you don't have to answer some of these questions. Especially from people who've dropped more balls then the Bills have lost superbowls. To attempt to answer for her, since Kanton can't even give the same respect that he asks for in his own over in Global's federation boards... and Min Takahashi by far deserves more respect in her rookie time in 4WW then Kanton EVER earned during his over a year in the fed (well 4 months total time if you include all the time he took off cause he couldn't hack it)... There are Rumors as to WHY she wears the mask, the most notorious is that she once DATED Kris Kanton while he was wrestling in Japan. To save face when coming to the 4WW, she doned the mask so that the Japanese populous wouldn't recognize her. Let alone the members in the 4WW locker room who have ever seen a picture from Kanton during one of his times backstage. However, 4WW's official responce is that this is something completely made up by Kanton's PR team, whom everyone is surprised are still working for him since he owes them millions of dollars in trying to make crap look like a 7 course meal. It's well known that Ms. Takahashi has much better taste then that. ---- 28 February 2006 Min, You would think a man who obviously hung himself in 4WW wouldn't come back over to our side of the fence to make immature little jokes that he can't even back up. He was swept out with the dirt quite some time ago, and any comment otherwise can just be chalked up to sour grapes. You know it, I know it... I think we all know it. In any case, we don't take this sort of thing lightly in my neck of the woods. My question is, what do you plan to do, publicly, to embarrass Mr. Kanton for his lack of tact, his rudeness, his presumptiousness? (Oh pretty please, tell me it includes a ritual disembowelling!) Your Tag Team Partner, Sekhet P.S.- We really should come up with a name for the team, don't you think? ---- 28 February 2006 – ---- 28 February 2006 As the owner of these here boards in question (4WW's) I think I can post in any thread I feel like here. Mr. Kanton, it's publically known that Mr. McClancy was booked under you to try to push you, and you dropped the ball. Mr. McClancy was the by far favorite to take anything you could dish out. But due to his genetic relationship with the owners, was never booked to his potential. Case in point, before leaving Global he was booked for the title that Kyo now holds in only his second match there. And was offered a higher shot to stay there. Now in GCW he's considered a high contender for the Rampage Tournement and his name is mentioned with the likes of Shadow and Dawn McFarlane. Also note that while you got a SHOT at Patti Anderson, unlike Min Takahashi you've never had the fans vote for you for anything, let alone for #1 contendership spot that Min shared with 3 other people recently in the Rising Star match that crowned a new North Wind Champion. Also, note that Min Takahashi is known to be a top contender in the 4 Winds Tourney, something that you could never get passed the first round in. Oh, wait... you have to have something to even get there. Let's not forget that Mr. Kanton is also the only man that ever in public has scared his fiance, well girlfriend she had agreed to marry him out of desperation, into being a lesbian with another man's wife. Not only did he scare the woman away, but to the arms of another woman. ---- 28 February 2006 Well since Mr. Kanton has withdrawn his comments it seems, I don't really have much to go on here....but I can try to answer what I *think* the questions were. First for Sekhet, because she rawks. Well I don’t really know what was said exactly Bosslady, so it's kind of hard to plan some kind of DEVIOUS revenge when I'm sitting in the dark. Well it's daytime so it's not dark, but you know what I mean. And yes! We should come up with a name! But not Grave Revoloution, that's so grim. What about...Foxy Angels? Flying Foxes? Uhhhhmmm....Soldiers of Roses? Aaaaand for what I *think* Mr. Kanton's question was: I wear a mask because it's tradition where I come from. The school I trained in isn't super prestigious or anything, and I didn't learn a venerable old master...but like a lot of Puroresu schools there's a long tradition of having a mask to build personality and weight behind. The build of the mask itself is a salute to the Late, Great Bruce Lee for his work as Kato in the Green Hornet. I just prefer silver to black because I'm trying to do him homage, not imitate his mad sk1llz. And a small point to add: Isn't it lovely to have friends? It's so nice to have people defend me before I even know I'm being bashed. <3 <3 ---- 28 February 2006 Heh, Foxy Angels.... Tenshi Seiteki? (Tenshi Seiteki Nin Ni would be the full wording but it can be shortened for the sake of not looking like I masacre grammar or anything like that. Japanese plurals are some of the hardest to understand and translate to English) Or if you were to play off the Vampire and the Energentic Angel... Tenshi no Chishio (Blood Angels. The last word can be shortened to just Chi, but we know what that is, spirit/blood/soul) ---- 28 February 2006 Mou... Blood Angels might get me sued again, I think their a Space Marine chapter for some game where you play with painted little men. They're terribly cute...but probably coyrighted...sadness. ---- 28 February 2006 Bleh, for copyright infringement it has to either 1) use their likeness 2) denote the SAME thing (you're not a space marine are you?) and 3) openly challenge their copyright. In most instances, copyrights don't transcend languages either. Their boxes don’t say “Tenshi no Chishio” even in Japan. :stuck_out_tongue: ---- 28 February 2006 *wink* Does this call for a poll yet? ---- 28 February 2006 Oh! I like Polls! We should get some more suggestions though, I'd *love* to see what suggestions PTC has for some tag names ^_^ ---- 28 February 2006 *sighs* I suppose "Teh Hawtness" is out of the question? ---- 28 February 2006 Dawn who are a couple of the female competitors who've frequented PTC federations that you've never gotten the chance to face but would love to? ---- 28 February 2006 *ahem* For the last time, I'm Min Takahashi, I don't even LOOK like Dawn McFarlane. Sheesh. I'll asnwer your questions though you forgetful pants. Hmmm, that's a tough question though, because there are a lot of really talented women in PTC I think... I can't say as I want to face any one woman more then any other, so I guess I'll just ryhm off the list of people who catch my eye: Tessa Windsor - Duh! How could I not want to get into the ring with her? I feel we share a alot in common really, and I'd love a chance to see if she can bring it. Danielle Northsky - Another woman that really speaks to me with the way she wrestles. We don't really share much in common, but I think one of the things that can make a wrestling match interesting is when you have two totally conflicting personalities. Eva VL - Another big trendsetter for women in wrestling, how could I not want to go against her? Vivica Valentine - She's like me! Well okay maybe not SUPER like me, but enough that I want to give her a go y'know? Karina Wolfenden - She flies like I do, and she has YEARS of experience, I think a match with her could only mean good things win or lose. Shadow - Brrrr, scary. She's a great wrestler so I have to list her here for comparison...but I'll be honest, she scares the pants off me! Aimz - I'm not sure if she retired or not, but I'd really like a chance to fly with her in the ring. She needs to lay off the caffeine though, makes you hyper. ---- 28 February 2006 She is in AWC currently. Doing well from what I understand. *Damn this name switch. Has me completely confused.* ---- 28 February 2006 Keen! I'm very glad to hear that, I've always been a big fan of her work. ((For future, assume anything on the GCW Section from me is Dawn, and anything in 4WW is Min.)) ---- 1 March 2006 Are you a fan of the Grievous Angel? Isn't using the term angel a bit of blasphemy? ---- 1 March 2006 I'm afraid I'm not familiar with the Greivous Angle, so I can't say one way or the other. Sorry :( But my monniker isn't intended to be blasphemy. In my religion an Angel is a messenger of sorts, and that's how I see myself. I bring my message to the fans and wrestlers I come across ^_^ ---- 1 March 2006 Min, do you model yourself after anyone, as far as being a wrestler or otherwise? Also, do you have a favorite idol singer/TV star from back home? ---- 1 March 2006 Min, even though it seems our conversation was edited for television from the 4WW Christmas Show, have you ever considered going out to the club with the Big Dawg once again for a little dancing and…well, whatever happens after. Always an admirer of you in a Santa hat, Jake Hix ---- 1 March 2006
Min, do you model yourself after anyone, as far as being a wrestler or otherwise? Also, do you have a favorite idol singer/TV star from back home?
As far as being a wrestler goes, I don't really try to model myself after anyone in particular. Usually I'll study a particular move or style that I like and incorporate it into the way I wrestle. As for a person though...there was a young man I met once who was just an incredible person in everyway. Not a day goes by that I don't try to follow his example and make myself a better person because of it. I LOVE to dance Mr. Hix, so I'm always interested in going out for it ^_^ Maybe this time we could get together for a little.... KARAOKE!! ---- 5 March 2006 Ahhh... the lovely Dawn McFarlane. My respect for you and your abilities goes far beyond words. I will therefore, keep my question quick. Who has posed you're greatest in-ring challenge (yourself excluded) and/or the most talented person you've stepped in the ring with, be it as an opponent, partner, or both? ---- 5 March 2006 [OOC] This is actually the spot for my other character, Min Takahasi :stuck_out_tongue: I can answer your questions for her if you like, or I can start up my own thread in the GCW area, whichever's easier. ---- 5 March 2006 I'm a dum dum
