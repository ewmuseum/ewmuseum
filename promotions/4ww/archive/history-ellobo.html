El Lobo de la Muerta — 4WW career history

History Description: Hair color: long dirty blonde with streaks of red, blue, black and green Eye Color: he wears dragon eye contacts Fill in the rest of the deatails: He wears baggy black cargo jeans with a glow in the dark shirt of his own creation. He wears a barbed wire necklace and a bicycle chain around his neck. He has several tatoos and piercings all over his body. He has a dragon on his right pectoral the swings over his shoulder whipping its tail around his neck. He has the chinese symbol for love on the back of his neck and the chinse symbols for wolf and dragon on his right abdomen. He has a small scar on his forehead and neck from previous battles. On his left arm he has a tattoo of a wolf bearing its fangs, and around his belly button he has the cross tattooed. He has a lip piercing an eye brow piercing his ears pierced several times, he even has that little area between your lower lip and chin pierced with a spike stud. Even noticeable at times is his tongue piercing. He often has his arms and face painted in a wide array of ways much like war paint. Attire: baggy black cargo jeans, taped fists, streaks of white paint on his face left arm painted in an array of designs, black glow in the dark El Lobo shirt *e.g. Feel The Dragons Bite; I Smell Blood; El Lobo; Wolf of Death; Wolf Dragon; Dragon In Flight* and black sneakers Resembles: Jeff Hardy when released Gimmick: Dirty gothic taco bell eating punk rocker mexican thug luchador *signals include a head shake taunt a rock on taunt two finger gun taunt and when doing the DIF off a cage or ladder he shrugs his shoulders, any other DIF he spreads his arms out like wings Back
