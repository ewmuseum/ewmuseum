History Description: Patti Anderson's hair color is a dark copper red, her eye color a simple hazel. Other details about her....if she is wrestling hair is loose around her, kinda covers her face from time to time. If it gets in her way, a rubber band worn losely around her wrist she can quickly pull it up and out of her face. Attire: Patti wears a black two piece cat suit style outfit. The top is a zipper front style that when unzipped half way down can offer a pleasing distraction for the male opponents. The pants are tight fitting, showing off the slimness of her legs. Black low cut wrestling boots complete the outfit Resembles: April Hunter looks meets Victoria's mind Gimmick: Patti Anderson is of a Greek descent, dating back to the mythological period. Before she wrestles, she's a shy and innocent girl, not wanting any trouble. She hides behind McClancy as they head to the ring. He uses his body to shield her from the crowd, but when it comes to being in the ring, it's like a beast has been awakened and will stop at nothing to win. History: Brief History with actual life story to follow at a later date... Patti grew up on professional wrestling. Her love of the acting aspect of the sport lead her to a career as a part time actress on Austrialian soaps. When she wasnt working on the Aussie soaps, she tended bar at Victorian Wine Precint. Her desire to see the world of wrestling up close and personal came true when she managed to save enough tip money to take a vacaton to the states to see her 'dream man' The Irish Bastard, Sean McClancy wrestle. On April 13, 2003, at the Arrowhead Pond in California, Patti came close to achieving her dream of meeting her idol, when she stupidly got thrown out of the arena when another wrestler tore up her sign, and she slapped the taste outta his mouth. She finally hooked up with him a week or so later while staying in the States. She managed to get him at a local bar one night, a night which lead to an incredible night of passion in his hotel room. Her desire for him turned into a stalker personality. Patti spent money on flowers and a small gift after the first night. The next meeting wound up with a perminant keep sake, a now infamous video tape that almost got her and him in hot water. Finally, her dream became a reality while staying in Los Angeles she heard he was offering a contest that aloud a female fan to be his personal valet for one night. Patti won that competition rather easily when she sent Matt Cummings through a table full of refreshments. This won her the admiration of McClancy. After a weekends worth of memories to last her a lifetime, Patti was thrilled to have one last night with him. After McClancy regained his title at the Apocolypse PPV, the two took a long walk along the famed board walk in LA. Patti went out on a limb and confessed that she had fallen in love with him, but do to a personal loss within McClancy's life, he was unable to return the same feelings for her. Patti feeling rejected by his silence, boarded a plane back to Austraila, to the life she knew well. She hoped that she would be able to move past him. But this was not to be. The wrestling organization that McClancy wrestled for was coming to Aussieland. McClancy used this oppertunity to take a vacation, and work to reclaim the woman he loved. He would track her down and succeed in getting her back. While his twin sister was away on a honeymoon with the man she was intended to be with, Sean used this as an oppertunity to hunt Patti down the same way she had hunted him down. Sean finally manged to find his beloved at her apartment, but after a negative exchange, and a solid refusal, Patti stormed out in tears. Sean would not back down and followed her to her place of work. Once again she refused him, this time she got the upper hand and had the manager of her workplace remove him from the bar. Patti finally gave in to his request to meet her at a garden that was special to her, and once she did, the magic of the night and the confession of his love for her, brought these two people together. After a couple weeks of paradise in her small apartment, trouble came a knocking in the form of one of Ian Childress people. They had come to re-claim their lost member. The member gave the request that Patti herself was to come along. After Sean explained his new relationship to not only Ian, but to his sister, Fey as well, Sean did the one thing that has made this relationship work, he made Patti his valet. Patti is now living her dream of being a manager and now is prepared to go the distance. Sean has taught her how to wrestle. In staying true to her Greek ancestory, Patti has learned a deadly combination of technical and submissive moves that alone could make the strongest of male competitors crippled. To keep the nerves down, Sean has agreed to be her manager. A position that he would trust no one else with. Back
