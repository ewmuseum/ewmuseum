Maybe it's something I can take into account and work on. ---------------------------- It had been almost one year since the era of the Oblivion Wrestling Company had fallen to it’s knee’s. Chairman; Sir. Harold Carter was still nowhere to be found. After divorcing his beloved wife; losing the respect of his son then watching the only possession he had left crumble; it was no surprise he didn’t want to be seen anytime soon. With all the paparazzi that would regularly surround the Carter Mansion in search of answers to the downfall of Oblivion Wrestling; it seemed only a matter of time before something drastic were to happen. The biggest shame was that after thirty-two years in the business for Harold, it would come to his. Back in his day he was a legend in the eyes of the fans; and a super hero in the eyes of Michael. Days; even sometimes weeks would pass without Michael hearing from his father; and even though angry at him a lot of the time he would always manage to watch his father wrestle on Saturday morning television. Michael was to be a second generation superstar; no matter what anybody thought. Destined to follow in his fathers footsteps…he began a new chapter in his life. Four Winds Wrestling. MC Mike : “Oh My God! I don’t believe this dawg!” Mike’s slang accent filled the blank screen. His distinctive ‘Gangster’ way of speaking could be easily spotted from miles away. MC Mike : “This can’t be happening!” What exactly was going on? Was Mike in trouble? Was someone hurt? Thankfully the screen begins to appear to a more clear vision. We open in this beautiful apartment. A sky blue and silver theme accompany a bright-white marble floor. Glass-made ornaments fill the large apartment in several spots; a crystal fountain poses itself as the room centre piece, and a jet-black leather four-piece suite surrounds the fountain; accompanied by a colour co-ordinated lazyboy. To the far of the apartment sets to doorways. Neither are filled with enough light to see exactly what’s inside. Flashdance Freddy : “What? What’s the matter cat?” Freddy runs out of the right-hand side door wearing a towel around the waist; a pink shower cap and drips soaking wet. This room; the bathroom. Secondly, out of the left-hand side door slides MC Mike. Mike, sporting his usual black Nike cap, white t-shirt and black jeans; panting slightly our of breath. Flashdance Freddy : “…WHAT?” Smiling massively, and pointing with both arms through the doorway of the room he had previously exited, he begins to bobble up and down in excitement. MC Mike : “Did you see the size of my wardrobe in there?!” Mike continues to spring excitedly for a few seconds; until Flashdance Freddy breaks from staring questionably at him, then heads toward the camera…and into the kitchen. Both the kitchen and living room are conjoined with nothing but a small glass statue of a horse rested on a table separating the two. Freddy reaches into a fridge; which has a glass see-through front and reaches for a bottle of water. As he begins to twist the top off, Mike follows him over. Flashdance Freddy : “So you excited to be in Seattle Mikey?” Freddy throws MC Mike an unopened bottle of Coca-Cola. MC Mike : “Yeah man, real psyched!” Mike opens the coke and knocks back a mouthful as Flashdance Freddy removes his pink shower cap. Flashdance Freddy : “It’s a shame Big Joe couldn’t be here cat, but when your mother won’t let you cross the street…flying across the country is gonna be impossible to the big bucket of dope!” MC Mike : “(Laughing) Yeah, I guess yo’ right man.” Spilling a few droplets of Coca-Cola around the outside of his mouth and jaw; Mike wipes it clean with his t-shirt leaving a pretty noticeable stain. Looking at it in despair, and grabbing Freddy’s attention by sighing heavily…he drops his coke bottle to the table and quickly whips his shirt of over his head. After throwing it onto the table and rubbing his head of to decrease the static…Flashdance Freddy whistles the Woo-Hoo attraction sound. MC Mike : “Don’t start homeboy; I’ll have to give ya a beat down.” Mike points at Freddy as; in retaliation just laughs at him. Freddy takes another swig of his water as Mike picks up his bottle again. Flashdance Freddy : “So Mikey, you gots your first match in a couple of days I see. How do ya think ya will do?” MC Mike : “Aw dawg this is gonna be a breeze. I ain’t even heard of this dude before.” Flashdance Freddy : “What was his name again cat?” MC Mike : “Calegro Vincenzo Maccilli Junior, or senior, or the third…or something; I ain’t really sure man.” Flashdance Freddy : “Sounds Italian.” MC Mike : “I think he is, I ain’t really followed him much; that’s something I’ll have to do.” Flashdance Freddy : “You mean like stalk him?” MC Mike : “Stalk?...man lay off that weed?! Dawg; I ain’t you!” Flashdance Freddy :”Hey cat, that was just the once. And I’ll tell you man, John Travolta’s body guards are real tough. They show no mercy! I’ll never forget that wedgie!” Mike grabs a navy blue 4WW t-shirt from a radiator near the right hand side of the room. He slings it over his head, reaches in his pocket and grabs a mobile phone out…he begins to fiddle with the buttons on it. Flashdance Freddy : “What happened to your Technology sucks way of life there Mike?” MC Mike : “Time’s are changing dawg, need to keep up ‘em!” With a sudden loud thump on a large double-glazed window, Mike sprints over to spot the commotion; and within seconds; Freddy follows. Flashdance Freddy : “What the Hell was that cat?” MC Mike : “Dang pigeons! Nearly broke my damn window! Guess that’s the disadvantage of being on the twenty-eight floor of one of Seattle’s most exclusive apartments!” Flashdance Freddy : “Now you’re just boasting cat.” MC Mike: “Well as the latest addition to the 4WW roster; I have every reason to be!” Flashdance Freddy : “Don’t let it go to your head now Mike!” MC Mike : “DAWG! Quiet! This ain’t gonna go to my head, I know where I’m from; the projects dawg. That’s gonna stay with me.” Flashdance Freddy : “Yeah cat, keep it that way.” MC Mike : “Told you homeboy; shut up. Now come on, I’m hungry; let’s get something to eat man.” Final Testament Feb 11 2005, 06:18 PM Post #2 'The Seraphim' Darek DeAngleo Group: 4WW Staff Posts: 935 Joined: 27-November 03 From: Lethbridge, AB Member No.: 51 A good character. I can say that it's been done before but you have a way of bringing a refreshing new view to that style of character. First off, the words you are using, if you want to speak Ebonics you need to use more slang. For example. "Tol' yous homeboy; shuddup. Now c'm'on, I’m hungry; let’s get somethin' to eat man.” I enjoy the character development you've done here, I'm hoping that you are going to elaborate on some of your past. I'd like to see why MC mike is who he is. What significant things happened to him in the past to make him the man we see today. This is a really good start and I thoroughly enjoyed it. Keep up the good work. Final Testament Feb 13 2005, 09:12 PM Post #4 'The Seraphim' Darek DeAngleo Group: 4WW Staff Posts: 935 Joined: 27-November 03 From: Lethbridge, AB Member No.: 51 The best advice I can give you on this character is find a path or direction you want to go and go there. Usually having a good fued helps to get you there. The difference between RPing and efedding is RPing is just writing and hoping people like your work, efedding is becoming activly involved in how your character is portrayed in angles and fueds. I think MC Mike will fit in nicely, we had the 'Gangsta' Robb Hardy here before and just when he started getting into the good stories and back history, even good fueds, he left. I think MC Mike will be a good addition as long as you are willing to give some and take some. For your writing style in general, it's a script format whereas I use a novelist approach. Some people find my approach lengthy, tiresome and over descriptive and prefer the script style. This is true in most feds because script gets the point across with no garbage filler (extra detail, such as in mine).
