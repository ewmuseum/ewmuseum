"The Modern Messiah" Darek DeAngelo — 4WW career history

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Description: Hair color: Blonde Eye Color: Blue Fill in the rest of the deatails: Entrance atire is A long leather trench coat complete with black wings on the back. He IS the angel of death but sustains mans form, except for a few occasions when he's angry, he is usually calm and collected. He wears a Blue spandex tanktop tucked under black leather pants. He has several tatoos on his arms and is well built. He has a very slender appearance to him but under the attire Darek is really a monster. Attire: see above Resembles: Kane/Undertaker - size, Vince McMahon - Attitude, Glodberg - Fitting Style Gimmick: Darek is the Angel of Death, most people perceive it as a gimmick instituted by the federation, however few know the truth, History: Born to a poor family Darek Angle had grown up as a child in the early 60's. In 1966 when Darek had turned 9, fate it seems had caught up with time. Darek passed away during a lightning storm on the family farm. Darek was survived by his Mother, Father, and 3 Brothers. Christmas 1966 the year of Dareks death was particularly hard on the family. Darek's mother had turned to the love of another man and had 2 of Dareks brothers (Roland Xavier and Brian Roberts) with him. His father had turned to the bottle, he treated his family with an onslaught of physical and emotional abuse. Christmas eve, Dareks mother, father and Brother (Siefer) had sat down to dinner, little did they know what would happen. Dareks father had lost all control and poisoned the meal, after dinner as his wife and son lie face down on the table he pulled the trigger on a gun that he put in his mouth. Miraculously Siefer survived and moved on with his life repenting the death of his brother. Brothers! Brian and Roland grew up with their uncle Joe Scull. So the story ends? The following Easter sunday a boy was found wandering the wintery hills of western Alberta. The boy was later identifed as Darek Angle. Baffled by the mystery in his apparent death and miraculous reappearence the authorites held the boy for questioning. With no apparent memory, the boy was placed into the care of the Catholic Church under the watch of Father Dimiscus. While stumped as to the true identity of the boy, it became rumoured of an old scripture detailing the return of death. It was said that in the year 1666, the year of the witches, the year of darkness, God sent an Angel of Death. A human born but Godly strengthed being to condemn the evil. It was also said that the being would live the life of a human and would be reborn each One Hundred years. The bloodline stayed the same but these rumours were never substaintiated and noone searched the bloodline out. Three Centuries have passed and now the Catholic Church stands as close to the prophecy as any person could.! Eventually they learned this to be true when the boy began to display extreme strength and ability with proper training. The priesthood began to tell stories of his coming and soon the entire church knew they held a weapon, God's weapon. Many of the priests filled Darek's head with lies. They built his confidence as though he were indestructable. It was said that Darek was the last face anyone saw before they died, they had been touched by death. When Darek was old enough, he left the church. While looking for money to live off of he found a job with a local wrestling federation. He worked the indy circles with the federation and grew more and more attached to the infliction of pain he caused. He longed to hurt those who sinned, he urged to punnish the wicked. Darek was Brought up into the GCW where he continued his winning streak becoming one half of the tag team champions in his first national show. When GCW closed it's doors Darek moved to rival company the GWA. He start! ed small in this huge company and continued to tear through t! he competition. At this time he met Amanda, a young woman he saved from certain death. Amanda became orphaned and Darek placed her into the care of Father Dimiscus. As Darek continued his wrestling cover to appear normal he used the 'truth' of being the Angel of Death as his gimmick. When She was old enough Amanda became engaged to Darek. His life was going well and his profession, just as good. He soon became the Television Champion, he relinquished the title when he won the GWA Hardcore Championship. For just over three months Dark Angel held the Hardcore title. The world came crashing down and reality caught up with the dreamer. Father Dimiscus had died in an explosion caused by a man who wanted Dark Angel's title. Knowing that his face would be the last thing Dimiscus saw drove Darek to the edge. He walked into the GWA arena and fought Major Payne and lost. He was placed into a casket and set on fire. Being bron human, there was pain and Darek knew he was not immortal. This was a turning point in his life, Darek blammed God for his loss of a father figure and for the pain of defeat and the fire that scorched his body. Darek used his strength to completely destroy the competition in GWA. As he rose to the top he began to think himself more and more as a God. His strength was unmatched, and the mind games he weaved could not be penetrated. He used his power to benefit himself and inflict pain on others. He became infatuated with the destruction of life. After completing his first of several World Title reigns, Darek returned to the place it all began, the GCW. After what was a revolutionizeing yet remarkably short stint in his return to Global Championship Wrestling, Dark Angel found himself once again the Global Champion and an unmarked record of 13 straight weeks of victory. Feeling the tension burn between longtime competitors GCW and GWA, Jerry Burkhart, CEO of the GWA purchased the rights to Darek's contract and once again Darek found himself on the move. After leaving the GCW as the reigning Global Champion the GCW was sold and moved to new location. Darek fulfilled his 2-year contract with GWA with Hall of Fame worthy records and numbers. The stats, the glory the numbers, they meant nothing to this blood thirsty tyrant. After nearly 4 years of solid competition and rivalry between GWA and GCW, Darek turned his back to the companies which had launched his career. After missing a year of action, Darek received a phone call from long time aquaintence 'Insane' Chris West. Chris had gotten himself into Superior Championship Wrestling Federation and was making a name for himself aswell as making many new enemies. After a few weeks of negotiations Dark Angel was born into the SCWF, little did he know he would be on the roster when this great fed closed. After just 3 weeks in the SCWF Darek had retired Rivers Pollack, Beatten Todd Dream and Heretic with West (the former world champion and a current champion), and been named the Number one contenders to the Tag Team Titles. Darek Had accomplished great ratings and was starting to draw big numbers with the interfed when the SCWF closed its doors. Darek found the opportunity to retire and the once feared and reveered Angel of Darkness lay waiting to this day... Most people who have met Dark Angel, believe this is just a gimmick, some think it may be real and we know the truth... Back
