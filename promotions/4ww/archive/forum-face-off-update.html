Signups will close at either 32 or 64 people (definately no more than that!) also dependant on interest. RP limits will be 1 RP for round one and two, 2 RPs for round three-five and 3 RPs for the final round. The site itself is coming along (thanks to PRIME's Peter Vetra) and may have a backstage RPing facility (if not then RPing will take place on the forums instead). To (hopefully) stop people posting on the boards with guesses about idenities there will be an extra element to the tournament, and that will be a 'league of deduction' deal. People will (after the first round of RPing) email me with their guess as to who may possibly be handling which character, and at the end of everything I will post the table with the most correct guesses ... as well as the most 'out there' suggestions Currently there the following confrimed characters (and apologies if your name is not on the list, if you don't see it here it doesn't mean that you are not accepted it just simply means that I haven't updated you yet ) : Maddux Anthony Berkeley Christian St. Pierre Dylan Wright Candy Concannon Billy Skippy, the disgruntled backstage techie out for revenge Sophie Svelte Meathooks Thimble Tiberius Thatcher Blockbuster Nathaniel Manhattan Ramblin' Rebel Johnny Fox The Infamous Mr. Villain Tyler O'Neill Suki 'Five Buki' Fellatio Jimmy Red Eagle Killa Kween All the bios can be found at http://forum.rpcommunity.com under the RPC Tournament section in the Face Off forum. There are about five or six more that I have to get through today and hopefully will edit this as I go along ... but if you haven't signed up yet then do so, as this is sure to be a fun experience!
