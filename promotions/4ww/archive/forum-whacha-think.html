Often in life we are met by decisions and options that have the opportunity to change our whole lives. Today for one man such a thing has happened. This story takes us to a small community beyond the city limits of Tokyo, Japan. It’s a place much slower from the frantic and often hectic pace that is the true incarnation of Tokyo. We are taken beyond the sky scrapers and the crowds of people, past the cramped and condensed suburbs, past the open hills and roads, and finally resting on this one spot in particular. Surrounded by nothing but a picket fence to set itself off away from the surrounding farmlands and hills. In this community is a house, not too large bearing in mind the subject we are focusing on. Rather, it’s one of the smallest, but it’s just the right size for someone who wants to be away and not draw attention from themselves. Perfect for someone that just finally wants to get away and lead a different life having their debts paid. Being simple enough, the center of the house is an open Zen garden, so popular among homes in such areas throughout the history of Japan. The garden itself is nothing special, but rather it’s current inhabitant, a man, draws us to this place. A darkened soul only made bright by it’s new beginnings in such a peaceful and serene place. This man is known by many as kage or in English as shadow. He is the lighting in the night storm. He is the peace during strife and toil. He is the blade of vengeance in time of peace and tranquility. He is a man caught between two different worlds, his past, and his future… both drastically different. This man is the one Jinsuke Asawa Sakuya or known to most merely Jin Sakuya. Jin, wearing a plain black kimono with a hood that swoops over his head and hangs low, meditates quietly in front of a large banzai tree hoping to find answers to many of life’s most secret questions. It was then that the rice paper door behind him slid open as a young woman in a red and white kimono stepped out. She is the picture of perfect beauty. She is attractive yet mysterious at the same time. Her skin is that of a soft honey cream color. Her dark almond eyes flash passion and intensity and her dark black hair done up almost acts as a way for her to try and hide her most inner desire… to have the man which stands in front of her. Aside from all of this she has a purpose here, her love for the man in unquestionable as it does not even have to be spoken. He eternal devotion to his cause can be felt in the air between them. She approaches softly with a scroll in her left hand. It isn’t until Jin raises his hand with two fingers raised that she stops behind him. Soft words flow from underneath the dark hood. “Do you hear that?” he asked with an air of curiosity. She quickly responded to him. “I hear many things my koibito(sweetheart).” “It is the winds, they have delivered a message my aikou(love),” A long deep sigh escaped from the man, “it is not good…” “Yes my dear, but I do not know it’s contents. It only just came for you,” she spoke soothingly as she handed the parchment over to Jin’s outstretched hand. “I thank you… you may return inside now my dear,” the girl nodded with a smile as Jin finished speaking. She leaned over and gently kissed the top of his head before returning inside the home. Jin took the parchment in both hands and slowly unraveled it to read it’s contents. +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ 影の一族の親愛なる立派なメンバー、 あなたの評判は私達があなたのサービスを要求しなければならないように先行する。すべての多くの源から敬意の下に来、私達は私達の代表として選んだ。 UWF のランクを浸透させるために明日アメリカに飛ぶべきである。これの理由は今知られている必要はない。今のところ情報は基礎を知る必要性で与えられる。今の ところ私達のためのこの努力に加わる私達は単に要求する。計画は言われて既に整理されて、受け取るためにリムジンがあなたの住宅で明日着くので持ってい る。あなたの妻に関しては、彼女は後ろにとどまるべきで、計画は彼女のためにあなたの一族、あなたの祖父の頭部によってとどまるためになされた。私達はあ なたの一族の名誉のためにこの提供を受け入れることを信頼する。 Kenichi Nakagura (Translation: Dear honorable member of the shadow clan, Your reputation precedes you as we must request your services. You come under high regard from many sources and of all we have chosen you as our representative. Tomorrow you are to fly to America to infiltrate the ranks of the UWF. The reason for this does not need to be known at the moment. For the time being information will be given on a need to know basis. For now we simply request that take part in this endeavor for us. Plans have as said already been arranged, a limo shall arrive at your residence tomorrow to receive you. As for your wife, she is to stay behind and plans have been made for her to stay with the head of your clan, your grandfather. We do trust you will accept this offer for the sake of your clan's honor. Kenichi Nakagura) +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ Such a letter was expected to arrive sooner or later. Jin could not hide forever, and now it was already out of his hands. He would of course accept the offer. In such a situation on does not turn down Yakuza for it surely means death… if even a descendant from a long honorable line of ninja. Jin rolled the paper up and set it upon the ground in front of him. “So this is the path that is now given to me. I shall change as the winds do. I will return to America once more,” Jin spoke to himself with calm. He would be returning to America once more. And so our story ends for now. A man caught at a crossroads as it would seem. He will join with the UWF, but what for? Why has he been sent and why is he not told the reasons? In such a situation Jin expected to not be told. Only time will the answers to this mystery. The legend of the Jade Dragon Champion lives on… ~FIN~ :P Kris Kanton Feb 15 2005, 08:27 AM Post #2 LEGEND Group: Members Posts: 346 Joined: 5-June 04 Member No.: 93 yeah i know its short, but its just the intro Final Testament Feb 15 2005, 02:50 PM Post #3 'The Seraphim' Darek DeAngleo Group: 4WW Staff Posts: 937 Joined: 27-November 03 From: Lethbridge, AB Member No.: 51 I thought it was pretty sweet. I personally believe that lately there has been an overabundance of Japanese based characters, but you put a different spin on things mixing the Japanese characture and english paragraphing. For an introduction, I'd say its worth in the 80-84% range. There are some things about the characters that you will undoubtedly be unveiling later and those are the stories and tales that should make this character interesting. The Japanese are a proud people with a long, strong history, what are his ties to this history? Is he a brave cunning warrior? Or is he a wrestler through and through? Answering these questions will help your audience place themselves in your characters shoes.
