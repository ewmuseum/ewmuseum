'The Technical Spectacle' Jerms — 4WW career history

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Description:He is a Caucassian Male, with tan skin. Brown hair neatly parted in the middle, with green eyes. He has No Scars or Tattoos. His hair is always neatly combed and he is usually always smiling showing off his cocky/arrogant attitude. Attire: He wears a black shirt (Usually promoting the Federation, or himself), blue cutoff jeans, and black boots. Wrestler(s) he Resembles: A mix of Billy Kidman/Rob Van Dam Gimmick: His current gimmick is that of a 'Technical Spectacle'. He proclaims to be the greatest Technical Wrestler in the history of wrestling. He is cocky and arrogant, and you can easily relate that to him just by the way he carrys himself. The fans despise him for his attitude but his merchandies sells and he occasionally gets a loud pop (especially near his hometown of Baltimore). He describes it as 'The Fans dislike me so much that they love me!' He also has a weekly show called Technical Talk which is done as a Roleplay. He will promote himself, the fed, and will always have one guest from the fed(Wrestler/Management/Referee, etc.) (This is done as a joint roleplay with another handler in which I ask them questions, they answer them and then I write it as a roleplay!) History: Born and raised in Baltimore City. At the age of 21 he decided to take up a career in wrestling. His career started in The UWAC Amateur Division, which was located in Cleveland Ohio, where he competed in his first few matches as an E-Wrestler in 1998. He was signed into the NWA (Northern Wrestling Alliance). His success was limited, and fans lost interest in the NWA causing it to close. In need of a job he then signed with the WWA (World Wrestling Alliance). His success started in the WWA winning the first of many titles, 2 WWA World Heavyweight Titles, and 2 United States Titles. The WWA closed after a long run leaving Jerms un-employed. He decided to wrestle in Canada, where he joined the CIWA (Canadian International Wrestling Alliance) and the CWA (Canadian Wrestling Alliance). He won numerous Tag Titles and Junior Heavyweight Titles. He had another brief stint in the CEW (Canadian Extreme Wrestling). Jerms decided to take a break and start up his on Wrestling Federation, The ACWF. It lasted a good 6 months and was very popular. But Jerms still yearned for wrestling and decided to give it another go in The EWL (EDGE Wrestling League), where he won two titles, The EWL Triple Jeopardy Championship defeating Cliff Knight, and the EWL Intercontinental Championship defeating The Misfit! Jerms had a very succesful gimmick as The Inspector! The EWL closed down a few weeks after Jerms won the Intercontinental title causing Jerms to look for a new home. Jerms' latest project was wrestling in the PTC Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, he made it to the thrid round but lost to 'Renegade' Rich Rollins in a close match! Jerms latest federation was the WIA! He was thrown into a World Title Tournament but the fed quickly lost its funding and became bankrupt. Jerms is looking for a fed that can 'contain his ego, and his high-level, veteran stature' as he put it in the world of Proffesional Wrestling. He believes he has found that fed in the likes of the 4WW! Back
