History Wrestler most resembles in real life (WCW, WWF & ECW Wrestlers only): Nova What does your wrestler look like: Hair Color: Short Black hair with bronze highlights Eye Color: Brown What ring attire does your wrestler wear: Entrance Attire (Only for entrance) Head: Gray Bandana with gold sunglasses with red lenses Ring/Wrestling Attire: Head: Black makeup over right eye, giving a black eye effect. Upper Body: Blue short sleeve shirt. Over the shirt is a black vest unbuttoned. Lower Body: Pair of Black Khaki pants with raindrops going down both pant legs. Boots: Black boots with Blue Lining. Does your wrestler carry any special kind of gimmick: Rustin Slade wants to desperately please the crowd. He looks to their support when he executes a well thought out maneuver, or an innovative one. What he wants most though is to please the women, as he has had not a girlfriend in his life, and whenever he tries to get one fails miserably. History of your wrestler: Rustin Slade was born in Boston, NY on July 24, 1976. His childhood was more or less average. He excelled in high school although in mathematics and science, which led to his acceptance at the University of Rhode Island. His favorite hobby, should be noted, was to watch professional wrestling as often as possible. One day when he was attending a lecture in New York City, Rustin saw that there was an open tryout for the 4WW and he tried out. To his pleasure he received a callback later in the day letting him know he was accepted. He was no longer just a watcher of 4WW, but now he was to become a player. Back
