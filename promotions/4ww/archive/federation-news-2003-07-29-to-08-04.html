4WW Federation News Monday, August 04, 2003 StormFront - Mr. Childress @ 15:25 PST Results are posted. I know a few hours after I said they should be. Real life with my move got in the way. But a good show all the same. Thanks for everyone for sending in their things. Although a couple of the angles and segments could not be used! Remember, even if it's not used, it counts for your heat. I should ahve the card booked for this week in a few moments or so, and I'm going to try to get the heat updated by tonight also. As of tomorrow, Jay Pheonix's handler is basically Head Honcho (Pheonix on the boards), along with Aaron (raorkisgod on the boards). And a few others. Angles and that need to be submitted the normal way, but also if they'll post their contacts for everyone (or just use the links at the bottom of their profile and that). Make sure they get a copy also. Just in case. I should be online during and after the move. But I'd prefer to be better safe then sorry. Hotel rooms do have phones, and my ISP is nationwide. Update: Card Preview for the 10th is posted now! Sunday, August 03, 2003 StormFront Update. - Mr. Childress @ 21:45 PST Sorry guys and gals. I know you're waiting. I had one match writer this week other then me. So it's coming along. Just like last week, I'd expect it early int he morning my time. I wanted to let you all know that it's being worked on. Guess after seeing the ratings today, I'm just wanting it to be as perfect as it can be. I'll be online, but might not answer for most of the night. We're Number 1, We're Number 1!! - Mr. Childress @ 08:55 PST RWN (Revolution Wrestling Network, formerly the Revolution Studios Interfed) released their first set of ratings this morning since the re-build, and guess what guys and gals.....Who had the #1 spot? Dat's right...the little fed that want's to grow up!! On the boards will be the full report, check out the forums! But the rating break down was like this. 1. 4 Winds "StormFront" - 4.65 2. Crime Time "Lockdown" - 4.56 3. RaGE "Fearless" - 4.51 4. Animosity "Tantrum" - 3.88 5. Next Generation "Extreme" - 3.42 6. CHZ "Wild" - Unavailable 7. Overall Production: 4.7 Spelling/Grammar: 0.8 Introduction: 0.8 Main Event: 0.8 Ad Power: 1.0 Commentary: 0.5 Workload Bonus: 0.8 Saturday, August 02, 2003 Rules and LImits changes! - Mr. Childress @ 16:29 PST Okay, some of you know what happened to another fed earlier this week. And a few have applied here already. But due to me not updating the rules page with the recent changes (like no Tempest for right now and such) I'm going to post the changes to the rping and other limits here. 1. Shows. Currenly the 4WW is only doing one show a week. Sunday StormFront. Mid week didn't work out well with other's schedules. If we get up a big enough roster and if people can commit to rping and writing for it, we will bring back Tempest. 2. RP Limit Currently 2 rp's a week seems to work well. This limit will be imposed for all normal weekly matches. PPV's will have a 2 rp limit for undercard matches and all title matches can be 3 rp limit matches. 3. Deadlines: RP; 11:59:59 on Saturday Nights Prior to a show Angle: Noon the Saturday prior to a show Strats: Midnight Friday/Saturday night prior to (this way Match writers can write their matches with your input into them) A few big things planned for this week. As some of you know, I'm going to be moving this coming week also. Jay (phoenix), Pat (Joufu), Aaron (some know him as Todd Dream) and Jes (BSGWDT, to a point) will be in full control of the fed. All apps that are recieved by Monday will be put into the backstage area. After that, Jay will prolly have to make you use the boards for rping if you're not in the scripts. We'll get word out fully. Jay has guarenteed me that he and the others will make sure everything is up and running for that week/week and a half that I'll not be online fully (I'll be online. Got to get my fix. Even if it's from a hotel room. My ISP is nation wide) Tuesday, July 29, 2003 Who's Hot and Roster updated. - Mr. Childress @ 11:17 PST Who's Hot? Roster updates? Yep they're done. And I'd like to congradulate Patti Anderson with being the first person to break that 9.0 mark on her heat for the week. A couple snapping at her heels though, and they're ready to knock her from the top. Also some good stuff coming from Jaime and Pajaro. Some good storylines and angles coming in and heating up.
