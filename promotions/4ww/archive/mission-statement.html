Mission Statement

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Law of the Land! Mission Statement 4 Winds Wrestling is an experiment within the E-Wrestling community. It's an experiment to try to get the best of both worlds. The roleplaying based federation and the angle based federation. We don't want this to become a writing contest. We don't want this to be about the win-loss record. We don't want this to become who has what title and who doesn't. Take the WWE® for example. Does their current champion deserve the title? Or does he have an angle as to being an unbeatable monster? Or a huge technical wrestler? Are there wrestlers around who are selling more T-Shirts and foam hands? Who gets the biggest pop when they come out , or the biggest heel heat? Who do you and others really 'mark out' for? Yes, we have tiltes. They're the staple of anything based around Professional Wrestling. But it's the storyline that we want to be compelling to you and for you to get involved in. Too many times the staff of the 4WW had heard it from the guys who participate in this sport/hobby. "I didn't read the card. Only checked to see who won and who didn't." Well we want to change that. The Staff of federations around the internet has promoted this by only having your roleplaying or your angle alone be the determining factor. But now, with both being taken into account, in the 4WW, it will be up to you to see how far your career will take you. To see how far you will go. And most of your work will go into the card. The most of everyone's work will go into the card. And as for that ambiguous win/loss record? Why? Do they really worry about that in Proffesional Wrestling? No. They don't. If someone has to do the 'job' to further an angle or storyline, they don't jump up and down and cry about it. Unless that's their angle and gimmick right now. But then again, those that 'do the job' and keep the crowd entertained are usually rewarded in the end. We won't worry about that either, when it comes to teh wins/losses. And we will care about who is doing what for the storyline. It's that simple. But we do have a way of measuring how you're doing in the federation. A simple formula that will be used to measure your HEAT. All three aspects of this federations' scoring is scored on a range of 1-10. Angles, Match Strategies and Roleplays. And then they're put into a weighted formula that produces an average of the scores to show how you're doing. Angles account for 50% of this score, with the other two aspects coming in at 25% a piece. All in all, we want the fun, not the wins and losses to be paramount to the 4WW 's mission, with the storyline and the show coming in a very close second. Take a look around, and of course, consider giving us a shot. Copyright © 2003 by the 4WW and TaleSmith Productions. All Rights Reserved.
