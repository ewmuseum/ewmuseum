StormFront, 8 June 2004: Show Opening

8 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Show Opening Date: 6-8-04 Show: StormFront [The camera pans towards the announcers, Joseph Moraless and Mary-Anne Candler as they're sitting at the announcer's table to start this week's Storm Front.] Moraless: Welcome all once again to Storm Front! I am of course Joseph Moraless Candler: And I am Mary-Anne Candler… *****BUZZ****** [All the lights in the arena cut out at the same time as the main screen cuts to static. All of a sudden the picture changes to show a dark room with only one source of white light coming down in what could only seem to be the middle of the room.] “A man once said that there is nothing to fear… except for fear itself.” [A bit of shadow and the outline of a person walks just beyond the reach of the light.] “Well… he was part correct. You see, there are TWO things you must fear in this existence…” [The mysterious man then steps out from the shadows to reveal himself in the white light. The man is a great physical specimen. He stands at an impressive 6'5” and is well built. The man is also scarred lightly. He bears a long scar across his right eye from his hair line to his jaw to match an almost all white right eye with the exception of his black pupil offset by his left eye which is a deep brown. To go with this the man has dark brownish black hair in a form of styled fade. In the same color of his hair he has a simple goatee marking his chin. He is also seen wearing a pair of black tights with white trim and white tribal. To go with this he wears black elbow pads and black gloves with white trim. He seems to have a remorseless or empty look in his eyes as he looks on at the camera.] Man: I am that other thing… Kris Kanton [The man pauses for a moment still staring emotionless into the camera.] Kanton: I'll be seeing all of you soon… ******BUZZ****** [The lights in the arena come back on with a flash and the crowd roars with excitement just as the camera pans back to Moraless and Candler.] Moraless: Who the hell was that? Candler: … He's in the South Wind number one contender's match tonight! Moraless: Something tells me this is going to be an interesting night. View Kris Kanton 's Biography Back
