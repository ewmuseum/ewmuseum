Todd Dream — 4WW career history

History Descriptions: He’s the typical well built, tall blond haired, blue eyed wrestler. In his early wrestling days he used the classic surfer, gimmick, but that has basically gone by the way side, as he has basically adopted a more normal look. He still comes to the ring wearing his shades though, removing them after he gets to the ring. Strength 1) Unbelievable perseverance.. The guy does not give up… 2) Can wrestle excellent, in multiple styles from technical wrestling, to power wrestling, to hardcore tactics thus he adapts well.. Weakness 1) Sometimes he has a tendency to be to aggressive.. “not looking before he leaps” 2) Selfish.. looks out for #1, himself.. Bio: Began wrestling in the PWW under the moniker Todd 'The California' Dream. His obvious ring talent, and work ethic lead him up the ranks in the PWW having many a rememerable fued on the way e, eventually holding multiple titles along the way. Next to rival Ed Skorn, Todd was the last to hold onto the PWW world title taking the belt home at Wrestlefest 3 in the 10 man Pain Cubed main event. Featuring stars like Aquilia De Flagaro (aka Jay Phoenix), Ruff Ryder, THE HIGH FLYER, Ed Skorn, The Assassin, Nephilim (aka Ian Childress), Jeff Johns, Showtime, and Johnny Blaze. After the PWW closed Todd made a brief run in a minor fed and seemed content with the world. That was until a call from his father Aaron Roark brought him back to the wrestling world in the EWA, which never really got off the ground... So once again Todd looks for a home.. could SCWF be just what the doctor ordered?... Dreams do come true after all... Back
