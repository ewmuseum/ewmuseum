Group: Admin Posts: 2941 Joined: 13-June 03 From: Puget Sound Area, WA State Member No.: 2 Okay, Josh has stepped down as the head of the EWT Division. Will has opted to move up to that spot, and he had some ideas. Our Tag Division right now is this: Future Shock Lucha Soccer Kyo and Williams have the next shot guaranteed, but they're not a regular tag team and are in two different stables. We could add them if I knew what was the plans for that. But we have some options here.... And Jen suggested hearing from you. There are good and bad to all 5 options up there. Let me lay those out here. 1) Just move on and keep them as the tag titles and try to make it work. We've been doing this, but this does toss into the effect of only a few people gunning for the titles. 2) Suspend them vacate them and then build the division. I don't think this would work honestly, as without a possible immediate reward, who would form a tag team to compete in a dead division? 3) Ladies Title... if we did this then NO FEMALE CHARACTERS would be allowed in the SWT or NWT divisions. Period. NO reason to have a Ladies title unless it's their top belt. 4) Pure Wrestling title... this would counter the WWT's gimmick stuff. The issue here is while I'd support this one, you kill a wrestling side called a tag team. We'd have no use for tag teams or tag team matches, ever. 5) Cruiser Weight Title... if we did this then no cruisers (under 230 lbs) would be allowed to compete in the SWT or NWT divisions. Just like the ladies, they can't have a title to themselves and be allowed free reign of the others. This would put all of our female characters into that division also.
