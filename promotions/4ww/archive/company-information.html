Company Information for the 4WW

2003

The Law of the Land! Company Information for the 4WW The Company: The 4WW, or 4 Winds Wrestling, is by definition, a small federation. It's just starting right now. It has been formed by a man who has seen a need to get into the business for himself, fully. That man is Geoffrey Ian Childress III. He runs the 4 Winds Academy, located in Texas, along with a few others around the world. Now he has stepped into the wrestling leagues that he has wrestled in for so long. The 4WW does have connections to other federations, and even though it's starting out small, for a reason, it's a recognized full time federation, albeit a young one. It has other connections to a media company, based out of Asia, called TaleSmith Productions, and a few small schools around the world. These connections help the 4WW keep that big federation feel, while keeping it's head as to jsut how big it is, and wants to be. Hometomw Area: The 4WW is based in Phoenix, Arizona. A current hub of growth activity in the United States. Arenas: The 4WW uses it's own arena, if you could call it that. An old warehouse has been bought and converted by the company. It's a good location, not unlike a certain bingo hall, but a bit larger. The Warehouse was purchased due to it's ability to field such things as a cage around a ring and to have enough room to get a slightly larger federation feeling. It plans on one day growing up.. The 4WW will use the 4 Winds Arena for every card, with the exception of the occasionall PPV down the road that may be run either at the local college's arena, or maybe a small field trip to either Las Vegas, NV or San Diego CA. Currently the extent will be in the U.S. Southwest areas. The 4WW does have connections in a few other cities, such as Houston, TX area, the Chicago, IL area and overseas in Asia. Sometime down the road, the 4WW may verywell do a tour of the Oceana and Asian areas. In Arena amenities for use by the talent. The 4 Winds Arena currently can seat about 2500 people with a full setup, and getting pretty close to each other. It does have a full snackbar service and such in the front 'concourse' area. It also has a walkway coming from the area that was turned into the lockerroom. Some talent may find themselves sharing lockerooms. The entrance area has been built up wtih a facade, and it's been cleared out to give a walking entrance area. The arena has been certified for the use of small fireworks and other pyrotechnics. Although large and elaborate fireworks displays may not be possible. There is also a series of small, 15 foot diameter, plasma screens that are in select locations around the arena. These are used for the backstage interviews to be shown to the crowd and to play the entance video of the wrestles coming out. The sound system is up to par for the area, with the acoustics converted for use as an arena, so that it doesn't 'kill the experience' of being there.
