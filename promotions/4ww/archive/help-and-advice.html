Help&advice Application help To join the 4WW you need to fill out the application form as fully as possible, if you are having any trouble with the application process or don't understand any of the terms then you've come to the right place. First off all i must state that NO real wrestlers will be accepted into this federation, which means there are no "Stone Cold" Steve Austins or "Hollywood" Hulk Hogan's. I would also like you to be original with your wrestler names, i don't want any ripp off names like Stone Cold Adam Shaw, or Hollywood Bobby Jones if you can't think of an original name then just use your real name. Below is an application form with comments written in red to help you with that question. Personal Information: Your REAL name: just enter your real name e.g. Alan Miller Your email address (very important make sure it is spelled correctly and also AOL users don't forget to add @aol.com): Your email address e.g.4WWwrestler@someispl.com. Please note, if you do not fill in the email address correctly, you will not get your acceptance letter that will have your user id and password for the members area. Due to the want to keep this site secure, a confirmation email will not be sent. You will only see the confirmation page. Wrestler Information (no real wrestlers, e.g. stonecold steve austin, hollywood hogan): Wrestler's name: The name of your wrestler e.g. Nails Miller Height: The height of your wrestler e.g. 6'0" Weight: Weight of your wrestler e.g. 254lbs Heel, face or neutral: Heel = Bad Guy, Face = Good Guy, Neutral = Neutral Hometown: Where is he from e.g. New York, New York Wrestler Theme Music (must be a real song): Song your wrestler comes out to e.g. Marilyn Manson "The Beautiful People" Ring Entrance Description: Here you can either fill out the blanks on the application form or you can create your own style of ring entrance by sunig fireworks and ligh effects and stuff, just make sure all the necessary information is down. if you want to be boring, just fill in the blanks. But please feel free to get creative here. Use fire works, or whatever you want. I personally hate rewriting these week after week, so thats why you get to do it once, and I don't have to write them again. You can change this if you want, just make sure the info i need gets put in there somewhere. Kerrie Douglas:......Heading to the ring at this time weighing ___ and standing ____ he hails from____ this is none other than ____ ["____" by _____ blasts over the PA system as ______ steps out onto the entrance way to _________ from the crowd] Style Of Wrestler (technical, brawler, high flyer, submission , hardcore etc..): see examples Set Up Move: What move does your wrestler execute before going for his finisher, e.g a big boot, a DDT, chokeslam, or does the finisher come out of nowhere. Name Of Finisher: The name you wish to give your finisher e.g. The Tombstone What move does your finisher most resemble in real wrestling (e.g. stonecold stunner): see examples Wrestler most resembles in real life (WCW, WWF & ECW Wrestlers only): Bret Hart, Steve Austin etc.. What does your wrestler look like: descibtion of what your wrestler looks like, is he muscular, what colour eyes does he have, what colour hair does he have, does he have any facial hair, any scars anywhere on his body etc. What ring attire does your wrestler wear, also add any additional information if your wrestler wears anything different while making their way to the ring like a jacket or hat, also does your wrestler bring any foreign objects to the ring: what does he wear in the ring e.g. HBK style tights, a Bret Hart style outfit, Goldberg style trunks. Does he wear anything else on the way to the ring like a jacket or a hat, do they have anything written on them, does he bring an object to the ring like a cattle prod or a night stick. Does your wrestler carry any special kind of gimmick: Does he have a special gimmick e.g. a wrestling tradionalist, a country western singer, a movie star, a pornstar man from the darkside although try to be original and not rip anything off from WCW & WWE or other feds. We undestand they've done almost everything. But try to be unique here if possible. Also include if the wrestler is cocky or arrogant, and such as that. List 15 Fave moves of your wrestler: Quite obvious really, just fill out ten moves that your wrestler will execute often, don't include your sub finisher or finisher in this list, and wrestling move can be included, but remember try and keep it to the style of your wrestler, if he is brawler, he's hardly going to execute a spinning heel kick from the top rope. History of your wrestler: has he been in any other feds, what did he do before he became a wrestler, what titles in previous feds has he won etc. Manager/ Valet/ Bodyguard Information (optional, no real managers e.g. no sunny or sable you can't have real wrestlers for managers either): Manager's Name: Name of your Manager e.g. Kathy Lloyd Manager's gender: female or male Does your manager interfere: will your manager get involved in the matches or will he/she just watch What does your manager look like, what does he/she wear, do they bring anything to the ring with them: what do they wear in the ring e.g. A suit and tie, a rather revelaving outfit,. Does he wear anything else on the way to the ring like a jacket or a hat, do they have anything written on them, does he bring an object to the ring like a cattle prod or a police stick. Sample Roleplay (all applications will be deemed void if this is not compiled, must be at least 20 lines, you can use a recent roleplay from one of your current or former federations, which i would prefer as roleplaying on the spot with nothing to really rant on about can be very difficult): Angle ideas or entrance angle. *Note: As noted in the 4WW's Rules, the 4WW is a very angle driven environment. What are you plans to introduce your character to the federation. Give us your initial ideas here. Check out the cards and that. Who do you want to challenge? Let us know. This is where you would detail to us the course you're thinking you want to take in the 4WW. How do yo want to enter? Want it a secret? Want to do a few shows of promo's first? It's in your hands. Any other additional information or comments: Anything else that you feel would help your application Roleplay help So you're not happy with the fact that your character is languishing in the middle of the ranks of the 4WW, but can't seem to make it any higher? You want that little edge that will lift you to the upper echelons of competition in the 4WW. Follow some of these tips, and it's almost a guaranteed thing that you'll see your status in the 4WW rise. Suggest angle ideas, or send your wrestler's roleplay for a certain match. Give us an idea of what your wrestler should do. Want to interfere in somebody else's match to get them mad at you? Let us know what you want to do. Want to do something special during a certain match? Let us know, we'll see what we can do. Roleplay! This is the single-biggest way you can develop your character, build heat, and improve your status in the 4WW. Roleplaying means submitting a 'flash' or interview for the others to read on the site. Use proper spelling and grammar as much as possible. We know this isn't always possible. There are probably many typos in this very page. That's just the way it goes. But interviews where every second word is incorrect are nearly impossible to read. Do the best you can. Use proper paragraphs. This makes your roleplaying easier to read, and it just plain looks better. Try to make your wrestler sound like an adult. Remember that your character is likely between 20 and 40, and make him sound like he has the intellect and maturity of someone who is in that age range. (ed. note: I've always thought that it would be fun to have a wrestler whose gimmick is that he's a mental child, or alternately, a real child, say between 15 and 18...) Don't talk like a "smart". Okay. We know you know what "mark" and "jobber" mean. But do you hear wrestlers use these terms in their interviews? No! Neither should you. (In certain circumstances, this may be okay if it's part of an angle. Please take it up with the Booking Committee before doing it.) Otherwise, the members of the Booking Committee will pull their hair out, and you'll likely take a big downwards step. Don't refer to "e-wrestling". The 4WW is a fantasy league. The fantasy aspect is that we all pretend that this was real wrestling. Don't refer to yourself as "the most dangerous man in e-wrestling", refer to yourself as "the most dangerous man in wrestling" or "the most dangerous man in the 4WW." All references to "e-wrestling" or "fantasy wrestling" in roleplays will likely be ignored, or never sent out to the group. Be as descriptive as you can. Tell us where your wrestler is. Tell us what he's doing. Describe his actions, expressions, and tone of voice. Tell us what he's wearing. Give us as much information as you can to allow the readers to create a mental image of what's going on in the interview. Try to be consistent. Stay within the bounds of your character, or change those bounds once and for all. If your wrestler's a witty smart alec one week, it doesn't make much sense for him to appear as a raging monster the next, unless something major happened to get him mad. Take your wrestler's emotions into consideration when writing. Try to get into his mind. Don't write roleplays that just beg to win. The Booking Committee is fully aware that most of you would just love to win every match. Unfortunately, that can't happen. Be patient. It's traditional that those who happily "do the job" despite having roleplayed well will be rewarded handsomely in the end for their patience. If you want to "quote" another wrestler's roleplay in your roleplays... find an excuse for doing it. Have your wrestler watching videotapes, and responding to what he sees the other wrestler saying on the tape. Remember... treat the messages and e-mail which contain 4WW programs as Television programs. If you want to quote them, you have to find a way to work that in. Either go with the "watching TV/videotape" route, or simply avoid quoting. You can always elude to what the other wrestler had to say. "You might think you're gonna beat me in that ring this Thursday. You might say you're oh-so-much better than me... but boy, do I have news for you!" Learn the right size for a roleplay. Try not to make it too small, try not to make it too big. Making roleplays too small means you don't get any heat, unless they are truly masterfully crafted. You also coming across to the Booking Committee as if you don't really care very much. On the flipside, don't pad your roleplays with useless stuff just to make them look more impressive. These kinds of roleplays are frankly boring, and any heat they may produce dies as the boredom sets in. Say what you gotta say. Get in, get the job done, and get out. If you end up with a really long roleplay because you've got a lot to do, or a lot to say, don't worry about it! Just don't add filler to make it longer! Don't hesitate to contact a member of the Booking Committee to ask what areas you could improve on, or to make any comments or suggestions about your character! We always want to improve the 4WW, and the best way to do that is to improve the skills of you, our talent. We will do our utmost to help you advance yourself. Our contact with you, and your roleplaying, are the only two ways we know you're still out there and still participating! Sneak attack help If your new to e-fed wrestling you'll need to know how to use sneak attacks, now these are slightly more complicated than roleplays. For example you have an enemy in the 4WW, you want to get revenge or you basically want to give someone a good beating when your not supposed to. You see these on your WWF and WCW shows, people coming to the ring, whacking someone over the head, distracting someone, hitting them with a foreign object, attacking them in the locker room area, the parking lot, etc the list is endless.You may only use one sneak attack per show and sneak attacks are not allowed on house shows. Do not attempt to finish the match with your sneak attack, myself and only me will decide if it is going to cost the wrestler the match or not, and your sneak attack may even backfire, especially if your using the sneak attack option just a tad to much. Also please take into account that i recieve a large number of sneak attacks for each card, and it is literally impossible for me to action every sneak attack i get. Therefore i will only use the sneak attacks which i feel fit in best with the card or will help continue a feud that is currently going on or has the potential to start a brand new feud that could be good. Below is how i would like you to write your sneak attacks for the cards, take into account you must be detailed with what you want to do to the wrestler in question. Also you need to tell me WHY you want to attack that wrestler. The sneak attack must be a decent length, a simple two line sneak attack will not be accepted. During the match between, The Butcher and The Carpenter and i would like it for me (The baker) to come down to the ringside area and interfere in the match, i want to attack the Butcher, i will approach the ringside area but i will make it look as if i am just watching the match. When i get the chance or if the ref is distracted i would like to enter the ring and put the butcher in the Fairy Cake Slam, and then run out of the ring and escape through the crowd. This is the part where the wrestler, in this case The baker is telling the pres what he wants to do in the match, notice he tells the pres exactly what he intends to do and how he intends to get around doing it. If you could do this it would be great. obviously the pres has the right to change the sneak attack so it will fit into the card he is writing better, the pres can also reject sneak attacks and even make them backfire, so being nice to the pres rather than demanding a sneak attack will help it work in your favour. I'm sending in this sneak attack but basically i do not like the butcher and have been trying to get attention in the roleplays but he seems to be taking no notice of me, i think we have the potential of a great feud that could escalate really well and be one of the top feuds in the fed. Thanks. Here the person is telling the pres WHY he wants to attack the wrestler, basically because he is annoying him and he wants to get in the ring with this wrestler and prove he is better than him because at the moment this wrestler is refusing to take any notice of him and he feels a sneak attack is the only way to do it. There are some things that you will want to take into note when writing your sneak attacks. * They are broadcast on shows that is aired around the country. WWF RAW, WCW Nitro all use in their cards, for example when the nWo comes to ring and beats down a person, when Steve Austin came down the aisle and helped Mankind win the WWF Title. * I want only ONE sneak attack per wrestler per card that i sent out, take into account i recieve a lot of sneak attacks and only the ones which i feel fit in best will actually be written into the card. * Take into account i don't want every match to end in a screw job especially the bigger matches that take place and especially matches on PPV's notice most of the sneak attacks take place on weekly show in those 'real wrestling shows', while a PPV event is more than likely to have a clean match. That will work the same way here and we may just blatantly refuse sneak attacks for some matches. Copyright © 2003 by the 4WW and TaleSmith Productions. All Rights Reserved.
