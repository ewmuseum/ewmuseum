La Envidia Mata — 4WW career history

XWF: XWF was an organization based in Puerto Rico where everything was in Spanish and was the biggest fed in Puerto Rico since it was formed from 4 different E-Feds those been JICWF, UXWF, CWF and HWA. Making it almost a 50 men roster which at the beginning was divided in 2 divisions. Envidia was in Division 1 and started as a top contender for the title but decided to reform The Corporation which was a drastic mistake for him. Week by week the faction was getting back together as first Envidia and DVD united, then Rey and DJ united, then Asesino and Limp united, then Canadian Perfect united with Rey and DJ, then Asesino and Limp united with Envidia and DVD, then all 7 men united but God David owner of XWF knew the past history of The Corporation and didn't want them to take over his company so he took a big risk which hurt the XWF for a while. God David ordered DVD to defend the Intercontinental Title against Gargola and La Envidia Mata in a Triple Threat Match with the hopes of making the 2 Corporation members face each other and prevent them from getting full power of XWF. DVD and Envidia destroyed Gargola putting him out of action for almost a year. But when they got the job doned they had to fight each other for the I-C Title and in what was at the time an upset DVD defeated La Envidia Mata to retain the title. But after the match was over was when all The Corporation members entered the ring and beat up La Envidia Mata putting him out of action for a couple of months. DVD took the leadership role and The Corporation started their domination. God David somehow survived The Corporation by getting rid of The Corporation one at a time. First, DVD got injured in a Skywalker Match with Jersey Devil where he lost the Corporate Title, then DJ lost the XWF Title to Yadi Entertainment in a match where DJ's career was on the line. Canadian Perfect then won the title from Yadi and just when he thought he was on top of the world he had a title match against Jersey Devil in a PPV but Devil was injured a week before and God David told Perfect he had a mystery opponent for Perfect that knew him better than anyone. God David suckered Perfect into putting his career on the line and at the PPV, La Envidia Mata made his return and won the XWF Title from Canadian Perfect ending his career. Envidia kept the title for 3 weeks but lost it to Koko B. Because of the bad blood that was forming between Yadi Entertainment and La Envidia Mata they decided to go at it in a Hell in a Cell Match which was going to be La Envidia Mata's last match. Envidia won the match but in his last match which was a Royal Rumble that same night to decide the number one contender, Envidia came in second place when he was eliminated by Night Wolverine who was hidden behind the ring and from behind eliminated Envidia who thought he had won. On November 2002, God David then changed XWF bringing it to the United States and changing everything from Spanish to English. Another risky move which not many people thought he would get success but he did. La Envidia Mata returned to XWF at that time. Then at the first XWF PPV in America, God David made an interesting tournament to decide the first XWF Champion. He made 2 gimmick matches with all the wrestlers divided in each one so the 2 winners would meet in the main event to decide the first XWF Champion. La Envidia Mata won a Royal Rumble match to advance to the Title Match in which he defeated El Locon to become the first XWF Champion. La Envidia Mata lost the title a week later to Chivo in which he got injured and left the company. On the XWF Anniversary show, La Envidia Mata was inducted to the XWF Hall of Fame. IWF La Envidia Mata made his IWF debut along with his Corporation stablemates Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling, Rey and DJ in the World Heavyweight Title Tournament which was a title that was vacant for some time. After 2 impressive victories for the rookie he lost in the third round to Turok who went on to become the IWF World Champion. La Envidia Mata regrouped as well as The Corporation and they wanted nothing else but to win the IWF World Title so they went after the IWF's owner/wrestler Elo. A month later La Envidia Mata won the Royal Rumble to become the number one contender for the IWF Title after been the first man to enter when he eliminated Elo at the end who had entered number two. During that match a heated feud continued to develop. La Envidia Mata went to Elo's hometown in Salinas and disrespected the people of Salinas and he went as far as to spit on a little kid who had an Elo shirt. Finally, they wrestled again in Elo's hometown of Salinas, Puerto Rico and Elo got the victory over La Envidia Mata. As furious as La Envidia Mata was he had to swallow the match because his next opponent was going to be Turok in the big anticipated rematch. Weeks before the Rumble La Envidia Mata had brought DVD, Limp and Asesino to join The Corporation to have 7 men in the group so they could watch his back in the Rumble. The first thing they did was send 3 wrestlers to the hospital including Turok who suffered the devastating Envidia Kick. Since La Envidia Mata had a win in an interfed match over Turok and Turok had one over Envidia in the World Title Tournament people were anxious to see who was the better man. They collided and what a match it was as Turok retained the IWF World Title against a very determined challenger. After that La Envidia Mata started a feud with PDR an IWF legend. While that was happening Acroctic Tiger had won the IWF Title from Turok and weeks later Tiger had to put the title on the line against Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling. At the end of that controversial match Elo double crossed Acroctic Tiger helping Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling become the new IWF Champion. La Envidia Mata came to the ring and congratulated Perfect but Elo attacked Envidia from behind. Envidia started to make a comeback but schockingly Perfect attacked La Envidia Mata. Then the rest of The Corporation except for DJ came to the ring and joined in the attack of their former leader. Elo then renamed The Corporation to Elo's Corporation. A week later Perfect had to give Tiger a return match for the title but the new IWF CEO hand picked La Envidia Mata to be the special referee. After Acroctic Tiger won the match problems backstage started to happen. Canadian Perfect was upset with Elo because Elo was acting as the leader when Perfect explained that he double crossed Envidia because he didn't want to receive orders from nobody. The discussion became so heated that Canadian Perfect walked out of IWF. La Envidia Mata and DJ recruited Mr. Raytings to The Corporation to fight Elo's Corporation. Both factions had memorable matches. One of those was when La Envidia Mata and Mr. Raytings won the IWF Tag Team Titles from DVD and Limp who had a long title reign begore that night. La Envidia Mata and Mr. Raytings never lost the Tag Team Titles. A month later La Envidia Mata challenged PDR for the Intercontinental Title. Both men had a big feud before but this would become their first one on one meeting. Even though both were fan favorites PDR would never forget Envidia and his former stablemates for hospitalizing him, Turok and James Storm. La Envidia Mata went on to win the Intercontinental Title from PDR in another classic match. La Envidia Mata defended the title a couple of times but never lost it as well as the Tag Team Titles with Mr. Raytings until IWF closed their doors.
