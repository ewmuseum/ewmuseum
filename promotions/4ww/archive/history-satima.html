Eye Color: Red Fill in the rest of the deatails: He has slightly pale skin, he is considered to be good looking. He has a tattoo of a black spider on his right bicep (it looks like the one on the cover of colds album year of the spider.). He has well toned muscles all around, but not overly muscular. He has a face similar to Chris Jericho s (without the beard); he has the body structure of Matt or Jeff Hardy with a little more muscle. He often wears face paint of some sort but not all the time. Attire: What ring attire does your wrestler wear (be as descriptive as possible) : A black mesh shirt Matt Hardy style but with no sleeves, he has black and white arm bands that curve around his arms. He wears black kikwear pants (like Jeff Hardy.) and black boots. Resembles: A mix of Jeff Hardy and Chris Jericho. Gimmick: Gimmick: A vampire one along side with his girl friend Fable. Signals: Gives the devil horns sign and yells before his finishers History: He was born in Toronto Ontario Canada and is said to have died there as well. He lived in a nice neighbourhood with nice people When he was 10 he found a passion for wrestling and by the age of 14 was on his highschool wrestling team. When he was 16 he was in a car crash with his girl friend (who was never found after the accident). He died shortly after the accident, but came back from the dead as a vampire to finish his business in this world. He later found out that his girlfriend Fable (as she calls herself) was a vampire and bit him to save his life. He worships her. They have come to the 4WW to claim the highest of prizes and prove that vampires are superior then humans, and to make their vampire army bigger. Though he is a currently a face he (and Fable) is known to make quick changes but still keeps his Minion what he/Fable call their loyal fans. He will most likely be going after the South Wind Title or the West Wind Title. He and Fable desire to have all the tittles. Though he is ruthless in the ring he is usually nicer than Fable outside the ring. Back
