Track this topic | Email this topic | Print this topic Mr. Childress Feb 17 2005, 05:23 AM Post #1 Lord and Master Group: Admin Posts: 2983 Joined: 13-June 03 From: Puget Sound Area, WA State Member No.: 2 4 Winds Wrestling has the following Staff Positions Open for someone who wants to get behind and become a backbone for a hybrid rping and angle federation. Vice President of Operations (Match Writers Guild) – This position is to be in charge of co-ordinating the match writing process to produce the weekly shows, StormFront and the bi-montly Pay Per Views. They will be in charge of ensuring that the team of match writers are ready and the matches written on time to be assimilated into the show(s). Additionally, this person will be an integral member of the staff and will help with booking, deicision making and helping others with their storylines and angles. Another responsibility will be to train new match writers who become interested in joining the match writers guild of the 4WW. Idealy this person would not be a competing part of the 4WW roster, but this will not be made as a restriction. Match Writers – Must possess good writing skills and the ability to follow instructions. Can be a competing member of the 4WW Roster with no restrictions other then you will not be allowed to write your own matches unless special arrangments are made between you, your opponent and the VP of Ops who is in charge of the guild.
