Ref: Chastity Spence Grudge Tag Team Match Kid Dragon & Jaime vs. Syxx Figures & Impulse Ref: Tommy Yung Main Event: Triple Threat Match for the South Wind Title Patti Anderson (c) vs. Pajaro de Presa vs. Psymon Ref: Tommy Yung Monday, July 28, 2003 StormFront, August 3rd, 2003 - Mr. Childress @ 19:59 PST Opening Match Triple Threat Match Fagen Gilchrist vs. The Issue vs. Impulse Assigned Ref: Billing Smith Regular Match: Jaime vs. "Simply Stunning" Sean Summers Assigned Ref: Billy Smith South Wind Contendership Match Winner has next weeks South Wind Title Match Pajaro de Presa vs. Psymon Assigned Ref: Chastity Spence South Wind Title Match Patti Anderson (c) vs. El Lobo de la Muerta Assigned Ref: Chastity Spence Main Event North Wind Title Match Jay Phoenix (C) vs. "The Brute Force" Syxx Figures Assigned Ref: Tommy Yung Monday, July 21, 2003 StormFront: July 27th, 2003 - Mr. Childress @ 17:54 PST Live from the 4 Winds Arena Phoenix, AZ Opening Match: *If Sean Summers wins he goes to the main event for the South Wind Title Bad Boi vs. Ben Jericho vs. Sean Summers Assigned Referee: Billy Smith **Oh no, Sean Summers making waves around, but he's already accosted 2 referees, and stolen the South Wind Title. He gets a chance to redeem himself here, as he takes on two others. If he picks up his first win in the 4WW he'll go on to the main event to face off for the South Wind Title against two others who would like to see him beaten and bruised right now. But Billy Smith took a shot from him last week. Will Summers have his big day? Or see his dreams dashed by a 'six shooter'? South Wind Contender Match: *Winner gets shot at next StormFront for South Wind Title Johnny Beckett vs. El Lobo de La Muerta Assigned Referee: Chastity Spence **Zoning and Permits kept the Hell Hole Match from being aired this last week, but there are two potential champions here in the 4WW and after the South Wind Fiasco is decided, one of them will get a shot at the 'Nightmare' title next week. This one should be a barn burner. Handicap Match: No Holds Barred Jay Phoenix vs. Psymon vs. Impulse Assigned Refere: Billy Smith **Card Change: Due to Mr. Phoenix's injuries sustained in last weeks match, the doctors have not cleared him to wrestle. Therefore, Mr. Childress has decided that Psymon will come off his victory over the North Wind Champion last week, and face....him? errr..Impulse in a match with no holds barred? Psymon did show signs of rebellian against Childress when he at first took Phoenix's words to heart in last weeks match. Is this a way of showing someone that they don't NOT listen to the boss? #1 Contender for North Wind Title Syxx Figures vs. Jaime Assigned Refere: Tommy Yung **Speaking of which, it's time to see just who will be taking on Mr. Phoenix for that North Wind Title. It's got to be defended soon, and we'll see who will get that shot right here. South Wind Title Match Patti Anderson vs. Pajaro de Presa *Becomes Triple Threat if Sean Summers wins opening match. Assigned Refere: Tommy Yung ** Resolutions. Need we say more? Will Summers make it to this match and turn it around and legally claim the South Wind Title? Tune in and see.
