Summer 'The Storm' Andrews — 4WW career history

History Description: Hair color: Long Black Hair Eye Color: Brown Fill in the rest of the details: White Female with long black hair, that occautionally changes to blonde and red, blue eyes, she's 5'9' and is really insane looking. Basically the saying 'If looks could kill' applies to her. She always wears black or red shirts with either black leather or wide leg jeans. Her shirts always have a saying on them like 'ME SO CRAZY' or 'Freak' or 'HAVE YOU SCREAMED TODAY?' Attire: Black pants and black spagetti strap shirt Resembles: Daffney Gimmick: She is really cocky(like Stephanie McMahon) Before she does her finisher she screams really loudly. History: Summer Andrews is the middle child of Leigha and Eric Underwood. Summer choose to keep my mother's maiden name until she found her real father. Kira is not only her sister but also her best friend. But if she has to get away from Kira the first place she go is to Saitra Dudley's. Yeah Dudley. The two have so much in comman niether of them know who their father's are so they kinda hang together. Summer grew up in her grandparents' Charleston home after a tragic car accident took her mother and father. Along with her sister, Kira, she raised her two younger siblings, Jay and Kara. She then went into the TWF at age 12. Summer is a multi-title holder in TWF, including the Woman's title and the Light Heavyweight title. She married her life long love, David Martin, at age 17, and the two of them had a beautiful baby girl named Alexandrea Marie Andrews, whom they call Alley. She is now five years old. One night at a show, Summer and her family were forced to watch as her husband's life was taking in a non-scripted stunt at the hands of three pissed off superstars. She is the grandaughter of TWF owner, Bryan Andrews, and has held the TWF tag team titles on eight occations with Saitra Dudley. She has also held both the TWF World and US Heavyweight titles. She turned on her sister and brother in early 1998, to form one of the most dominating forces in TWF History, Team No Mercy. By the age of sixteen Summer had become the most dominate wrestler in the TWF. She has since went on to capture the Hardcore title, the IC title, & the European title. Summer was engaged to David's couin, and former TWF superstar, Alexz 'Kaos' Martin, but that engagement was brought to a end after her younger sister, Kara reasoned with her older sister, though it could be said that the relationship between the two has not totally been terminated. Summer was put in charge of TWF by her grandfather, Bryan, as Commissioner of TWF in early 2000. She stepped down as Commissioner in April of 2000 to let her grandfather return to stop the marriage of Siren and her younger brother, Dr. Jay Andrews. In early 2001, Summer returned to the ring as CEO of TWF. Summer's fued with her brother and sister continued until Jay cost Kira the women's title. In July of 2001 Kira and Summer joined forces at TWF pay-per-view Massacre, to 'rid' TWF of the 'Trash' Siren brings to the ring. After the match, Summer once again left TWF to presue a more stable career. Summer left Charleston and moved her and her daughter to New York City. Summer worked as a PR for a small indy movie production company. But the city life was to hard on Summer and her daughter, so in early 2003, Summer once again moved her and her daughter back to the familiar Charleston setting. Summer bought a beautiful house circa 1708 and openned it as a bed and breakfast. Summer swore that she would never step back into the squared circle again. I think we all know how that turned out! Summer has wrestled with countless injuries including, a sprang ankle, 3 broken ribs, a dislocated shoulder, and a broken arm Not to mention the countless head injuries due to chair shots to the head. Yet in the end, the Storm keeps coming back for more. Back
