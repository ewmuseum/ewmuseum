4WW — Match report

StormFront, 8 June 2004: match report

8 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kanton in Four Way for Southwind Date: 6-8-04 Show: StormFront Tania Brown - The following match is a fatal four way for the number one contenders spot for the South Wind Championship! [The lights dim as the crowd goes quiet. Suddenly, You Shook Me All Night Long by AC/DC hits the PA system as James The Ladies Man Slade struts into the arena. As all the women in attendance scream his name, yellow Pyro's go off...] Tania Brown - Making his way to the ring first weighing in at 204 pounds he is the Ladies Man... James Slade! Candler: This ones cocky. Morales: He has the right to be after all he is the Ladies Man! 'Push my fingers into my.... [Duality by Slipknot begins to play as the lights dim. As the song kicks up red pyro goes off on the stage. The lights come back on in the arena, but they have a strange red glow to them. Kris Kanton slowly steps out from behind the curtain. He pauses a moment to twist his neck and then continues down to the ring.] Tania Brown: Heading to the ring at this time standing 6'5 and weighing in at 283 lbs he hails from Louisville, KY. This is none other than KRIS.... KANTON! Candler: Two more to go! [Kris Kanton, and James Slade are already in the ring, looking out to the fans who are chanting 'WE WANT WEST'. Slade has the look of shock, and suprise. As Kanton doesn't know what to think, he just stands in his corner] Moraless: Listen to these Fans.. You would think we where in Canada.. Candler: In Three Weeks we will be, and MS4 is going to be in home town. These Fans will be nothing like the Fans in Vancouver.. Moraless: As Much as they hate him, they Respect him.. Candler: Its Been Over two months since they saw.... [The We Want West chants get louder, as all of a Sudden 'FULL NELSON' by Limp Bizkit Hits the Loud Speakers as the Fans let out a BIG RETURN POP, as out from the back Steps out 'Holy Shit' Jayson Bird.. The Fans Boo a Little.. He has a Six Pack of Canadian Beer with him, he is also wearing a'Billy Dork' Shirt, and his Sweat Pants, as he stops half way down the ramp, and turns around point up the ramp.. As out from the back Steps out the Hardcore legend.. 'INSANE' CHRIS WEST. West is Wearing a White Hardcore Legend Shirt, and his Blood Stained Blue Jeans, as he lifts up he points his Barbwire Bat out into the crowd as the Fans Pop again, West stops before walking down the ramp, as you can see his mouth repeatly saying Thank You, over and over again, as he starts to walk down the ramp] Tania Brown: COME DOWN TO THE RING, and MAKE HIS RETURN.. THE INSANE ONE.. 'INSANE' CHRIS WEST!!!! [West Walks down to Where Bird is and hands him the barbwire Bat, as he grabs the six pack from Bird, and breaks off one of the beers. He Walks around ringside with the Six Pack, as he goes over to the play by play table. West, breaks off two more Beers, and puts them on the play by play table. Both Moraless, Candler take the beers, as they don't want to piss off the Hardcore Legend. West, cracks open his Can as he Downs the Beer, as tosses the Can into the crowd. he gives the other two Can to Bird, as West starts to walk up the steps up, as he climbs into the ropes, he walks over to his corner, and waits for the bell] Moraless: You going to drink yours?? Candler: Maybe Later... I can't belive this crowd, how can they cheer for this sick freak. Moraless: Respect!! [The Music Stops as Tania continues] Candler: Billy Dork is back! Tania Brown:......Heading to the ring at this time weighing 282 pounds and standing 6'6' he hails from Charlotte, North Carolina this is none other than Syckle! [The lights go out and two fire trails blaze the way down to the ring. 'The End Has Come' by Ben Moody, Jason Miller, and Jason 'Gong' Jones blasts over the PA system as Syckle steps out onto the entrance and walks down to the ring. Syckle steps over the top rope and the lights come back on.] Candler: That doesn't look like Dork to me! Morales: just goes to show you that anything can happen! [Slade...West...Kanton...and now Syckle all pace around each other in the ring not knowing who should make the first move... the referee calls for the bell starting the match, but still not one makes his move...] Candler: Sometime today boys.. [West stares down Kanton while from out of the corner Slade comes flying with a punch connecting to the side of West's jaw. West stumbles back and Kanton grabs Slade by the back of his shirt tossing him over the top ropes. Slade hits the outside mat hard, but Kanton takes his focus off of Slade and lunges forward at West with a hard right foot causing West to fall against the turnbuckle. Kanton looks at Syckle from the corner of his eye and moves just in time for Syckle to connect with a body splash on West. Syckle comes back and Kanton bounces off the ropes connecting with a hard clothesline on Syckle.] Morales: This new comer Kanton is making a name for himself! [Kanton goes back to West, but from the outside Slade reaches into the ring grabbing Kanton by his feet. He yanks Kanton down to the canvas hard and West drops down on Kanton with an elbow drop. Syckle starts to get up to his feet as he runs and slams his forearm into West's back. West turns towards Syckle and the two begin to exchange blows with one another before finally be interrupted by the returning Slade who clotheslines both men back down. Slade reaches down and picks up West tossing him over the ropes. Slade slides out of the ring following West while back inside the ring Syckle and Kanton are going at it. Kanton grabs Syckle by the back of his head and goes to slam him fact first into the turnbuckle, but Syckle manages to elbow Kanton in his mid-section. Kanton stumbles back and Syckle turns around and goes for the six gun serenade, but Kanton catches his foot and nails him with a capture suplex. Meanwhile out side the ring the cameras cut to Slade as he irish whips West into the guard rail. The fans go nuts slapping their hands against West as Slade comes and connects with a running knee shot to West's face.] Candler: These four men are going nuts Morales! Morales: It shocked me to see Syckle go for one of his signature moves so early in the game. Candler: Rookie mistakes Morales... Rookie mistakes. [West connects with a few punches to Slade knocking him back as West gets up to his feet and runs at Slade catching him with a tornado DDT on the outside! The fans begin to chant “Holy Shit” as West gets up and laughs down at Slade. He turns, but not in time as Kanton comes down from the top turnbuckle to the outside with a cross body catching West off guard!] Candler: I didn't' even seen Kanton get up there! Morales: Taking all the shots in this one. [All three men lay lifeless on the outside while inside the ring Syckle starts to stir. Syckles rolls outside the ring and walks over the to men picking his target... sure enough Slade is the first to stumble up to his feet... his posture a little off from the DDT he took moments ago. He falls a bit, but holds himself up on the guard rail with Syckle brings his foot up into Slade's chest. Slade falls back and Syckle grabs him going to toss him into the steel post, but Slade counters and it is Syckle who goes face first into the post landing on the other side of the ring. Slade goes over to Syckle, but once again in this four way match there are no friends and Kanton comes from out of nowhere with a hard clothesline to the back of Slade. Slade stumbles forward and Kanton grabs Slade by the back of his tights and tosses him underneath the bottom rope and back into the ring. Kanton goes to get back inside the ring himself, but West comes with a hard drop kick to Kanton's side knocking him back down. West gets up and slides back into the ring going over to Slade and hooking one of his legs.] 1! 2! [Slade gets his other foot on the ropes breaking the pin. West gets up and stalks Slade as he starts to get up to his feet as well. West kicks Slade in his mid-section and sets him up for a powebomb. West lifts Slade up and Slade manages to counter and hit West with a hurricanrana! Kanton slides into the ring and quickly grabs Slade before he has a chance to get up, but Slade over powers Kanton and shoves his arms to the side before leaping up and going for a hurricanrana on Kanton, but Kanton out smarts the Ladies Man and slams him down to the ring with a hard powerbomb. Slade winces in pain holding his lower back as he moves around on the ring trying to get some feeling back into himself. Kanton laughs in approval as the fallen Syckle makes his way back into the ring.] Candler: All four men are back in the ring again! Morales: Poor Slade, couldn't dodge that bullet twice. Candler: It wasn't a bullet... it was a powerbomb. [Kanton turns towards Syckle, but Syckle catches him with a snapmare takeover before kicking him straight in his back. Kanton now like Slade holds his lower back as Syckle grabs Kanton's legs and hooks him in the Texas Clover Leaf! Kanton screams out in pain trying to get to the bottom ropes to grab them with all his might, but he's too far as it seems Syckle's got this one in the bag...Kanton raises his arm high into the air as if he's about to tap out when West connects with a big boot right to Syckle's face knocking him off of Kanton and releasing the hold. West quickly covers Kanton in his hurt state.] Candler: This is it Morales... 1! Morales: The returning West is gonna do it! 2! [Slade quickly slams his arms down into West's back breaking up the pin.] Candler: So close, but yet so far. Morales: Slade is competing for a shot at the title that his best friend lost last week. Candler: No friendships when it comes to gold. Those two men also fought for it one time. [Slade grabs West by the back of his head dragging him up to his feet, but West gets the better of him shoving him backwards. Slade comes back with a kick, but West catches his foot and smiles. Slade smiles too, but why? He brings his other foot up and connects with an Enziguri. Slade gets up to his feet and turns right into another hard clothesline from Kanton knocking Slade back down. Kanton starts to walk around, but he seems to be walking with more of a limp after the attack by Syckle. Syckle gets up to his feet and Kanton grabs him sending him flying into the ropes, Syckle bounces back and Kanton goes for a clothesline, but Syckle ducks under it and bounces off the other side of the ropes coming back with a shoulder dive to the back of Kanton's knee. Kanton yells out in pain as he falls to the ring and favors his left knee.] Candler: Oh no Kanton... you're not supposed to show pain... Morales: He has a bulls eye on him now Candler. [Syckle grabs Kanton's legs and goes for another Cloverleaf, but Kanton uses his leg power to shove Syckle backwards. West grabs Syckle and rolls him up in a school boy pin.] 1! 2! [Syckle kicks out with authority as he gets up to his feet and kicks West in his rib cage knocking him back down. Syckle quickly drags West up to his feet and sets him up for the Iron Cross. He holds West in a cross like position in the air, but before he has a chance to slam West down, Slade comes from the top ropes and connects with a missile drop kick to Syckles chest knocking him backwards and sending West over the top rope back to the outside. Slade covers Syckle.] 1! 2! [Kanton just breaks up the pin.] Candler: Two close falls! Morales: You amaze me each week with your counting skills. Candler: Don't push me Morales. [Kanton drags Slade up to his feet and hooks his head, spinning and nailing him with a neckbreaker. Kanton gets up and West slides back into the ring, West quickly runs at Kanton, but Kanton crouches down and connects with a firemen carry on West. Syckle gets up and runs at Kanton with a clothesline, but once again Kanton dodges it and grabs Syckle by his back lifting him up and nailing him with a hard german suplex.] Candler: There's no stopping this man now Morales! Morales: It looks like we're gonna have a new number one contender for the South Wind title! [Kanton takes this time to take a breather in the corner catching his breath. Kanton rests against the turnbuckle thinking he's got this one made when all of a sudden Slade gets up and runs at Kanton. Kanton not quick to react stands there as Slade runs up Kantons body and connects with the Love Tap!] Candler: OH MY GOD! IT'S OVER! [Slade goes to pin Kanton, but West quickly runs at Slade slamming him back into the turnbuckle, but the referee gets in the way causing himself to be squashed between Slade's back and the turnbuckle post. The ref falls to the canvas as West grabs Slade and nails him with the Westitis! West looks down at the referee and he starts to shake him trying to get him to wake up. West aggravated picks the referee up and tosses him outside the ring.] Candler: That's no way to win this match. [West turns his attention back towards the ring and Syckle leaps at him connecting with the Six Gun Serenade! West falls to the rings floor holding his chest in pain as Syckle covers West.... but nothing no referee has come out to call this match yet...Syckle slams his hands down against the mat as Slade is shown sliding out side the ring. He grabs a steel chair and slides back inside. Syckle turns towards Slade and Slade tosses the steel chair at Syckle. Syckle catches the chair and Slade connects with a spinning heel kick to the chair causing it to smash against Syckles face busting him wide open. Slade goes for the pin and another referee runs down to the ringside sliding inside the ring.] 1! Candler: Not like this he cheated! 2! Morales: Chris Sanchez take a good look at your next opponent! 3! [...BUT NO! A man leaps the guard rail and grabs the referee by his leg dragging him out the ring. The man backs up and leaps forward superkicking the referee right in his face] Candler: WHAT THE HELL?! WHO THE HELL IS THAT!? [The camera focuses in on the mans face revealing it to be “Superstar” Eddie B.'s!] Morales: What is he doing?! [Eddie B. grabs Slade's leg and drags him outside the ring just like he did with the ref. Slade goes to punch Eddie B., but Eddie B. puts his arm up blocking the punch. Eddie B. brings his foot up right between Slades leg with a hard low blow. Slade leans forward and Eddie B. grabs his head and drops to the canvas with a hard DDT.] Candler: You don't think the sinner is.... Morales: I think it is! Candler: This is great! Eddie B's laying it into James Slade and there's nothing Bullet could do about it, because Bullet must think what we thought. He's probably back there guarding his wife! Morales: So much for friendship... [Eddie B. picks up Slade and walks him around the ring till there right in front of the announcers table. Eddie B. clears off the table tossing TV monitors away and microphones before he kicks Slade in his stomach. Slade leans forward and Eddie B. Quickly grabs him lifting him up into the air. Eddie B. Holds Slade up for a few moments before he turns around and tosses Slade through the table with the Superstardom!!!] Candler: OH MY GOD!!! HE DESTROYED OUR TABLE!! [Eddie B. laughs as Slade seems to go into a seizure his whole body shaking uncontrollably on the floor. Eddie B. picks Slade up obviously not finished with him and tosses him into the ring. Eddie B. Slides into the ring and puts Slade on the top turnbuckle in a sitting position facing the crowd and not the ring. Eddie B. leaves the ring and lifts up the ring apron pulling out a wooden table. He sets it up outside of the ring and smirks.] Morales: What could he possibly do now?! [Eddie B. leaps up onto the ring apron and grabs Slade by his head falling forward and crashing through the table with his version of the Diamond Cutter. Both Eddie B. And Slade lay on the outside out of it.] Candler: Poor...poor James Slade. [Meanwhile back in the ring West looks down at the carnage caused by the Superstar...he turns towards Kanton and Syckle and runs at Kanton, but Kanton sends West up into the air into a flap jack and catches him in a stunner nailing him with the Fear Effect! Kanton covers West.... another referee comes running down the ramp way and slides into the ring.] 1...! 2...! 3...! [The ref calls for the bell...raising Kantons arm high into the air.] Tania Brown: Your winner and the number one contender for the South Wind Championship...Kris Kanton! [Outside the ring Eddie B. gets to his feet and grabs a microphone pulling Slade's head up...Slade is bleeding profusely as Eddie B. laughs into the mic...] Eddie B.: I told you a sinner would pay tonight didn't I!?!?! Bullet! More people will pay with their lives if you don't accept my offer! I'm telling you right now Rachael is coming home with me one way or another and until she does more of your friends are gonna end up like this chump... a piece of (BLEEP) on my boots! And as for you Slade where's your best friend now?! He didn't even come out to help you! I told you to trust the Superstar! I TOLD YOU! [“The Crowing” by Coheed and Cambria hit's as Eddie B. Laughs to himself rubbing his hands in Slades blood... EMT's rush down the ramp way to help Slade out as Eddie B. Leaves ringside walking up the ramp way with a sadistic look in his eyes... Shortly after when Slade's being escorted out the building the music of Kris Kanton finally hits and he steps up on the turnbuckle raising his arms high in victory.] View Kris Kanton 's Biography Back
