History Description: Hair color: Blond Hair Eye Color: Blue eyes Fill in the rest of the details: The perfect women. 36c-24-36, blond hair, blue eyes, skinny. everything a person would want in a blond hair blue eyed centerfold. Her hair is a little lower then shoulder, more than likely shoulder blade area Attire: Bikini. Usually a blue bikini with some sort of an instrument of gambling on it, be it dice, cards, or whatever. The bottom of the bikini is a thong, and there is not a lot left to the imagination. She wears no boots, pads, or anything like that. Her hair is usually left down, never tied back. She will never pay attention to the fans because she is too good for them, but she doesn't have a problem barrating her opponent and trash talking the entire time she is wrestling. She is also the HBIC and she will act like it. History: Has wrestled somewhat for a short period of time but never very extensively. She left her brother because she was given every break she ever needed. She won everything in high school, beautfy pageants, you name it, she won it.
