So, you want to see if you could be the Master of the 4 Winds? So you want to join the 4WW? First of all you need to know the following information: The 4WW is based around a product model that would be similar to the 'attitude era' of the WWE®, the "Golden Era" of the WCW®, and a good healthy touch of Puroresu (Japanese Pro-Wrestling) thrown in. The 4WW promotes a mature product and may not be suitable for people under the age of 14, due to sexual comments or situations and descriptions of violent situations, If you feel you would be easily offended please do not join. However, we're not going to stop you from joining, the choice is yours. The 4WW is a roleplay run federation, with a large concentration on Angles and Storylines also. if you do not roleplay you will not win and you will be fired. The 4WW also does not use simulators of any kind, the writers knowledge of wrestling as well as your input is used to determine matches. The 4WW staff wishes to welcome you if you decide to join us, and we hope your stay with us will be an enjoyable one. Now the important stuff: This application form needs to be filled out as fully as possible, any applications with not enough information will be made void. All applications MUST have the sample roleplay section filled in and it must be at least 20 lines long. NO real wrestlers such as Stone Cold Steve Austin or Hollywood Hogan will be accepted as we pretend we are in an environment in which these federations do not exist and that the 4WW is a real life federation. If you can't think of anything original use your own name. and try not to use rip off names such as Stone Cold Adam Jones or Hollywood Bobby Young, that's just lame. We do allow hardcore and exreme wrestling styles, but they are not our concentration. However, if that is your characters gimmick and angle, we will be happy to oblidge you. Please fill out the folowing form, as COMPLETELY as possible. Note: By applying to 4Winds WrestLing, you are agreeing to the following: If you are accepted you are giving permission for you information to be put on our contact list for handlers of this federation. This contact list is in the passworded area of the website and can only be accessed by other members of the federation. This list is controlled and is only to be used for the community of the 4WW to communicate within, whether it be for co-ordinating angles and such, or just getting to know each other. Because of the nature of this federation, and the way that it 'encourages' closeness and working together, this is not an option. But the staff, the president in his own, does see to it that this list is controlled, and those that abuse the priviledge of having access to this list will be reprimanded.
