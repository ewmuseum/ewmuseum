Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Royal Championship Wrestling billed itself "Est. 1997", an application-driven fed run by an in-fiction Executive Board, staging six PPV brands and two weekly TV shows (RCW Rage, Friday Night Fury) across its own run.

Its own World Championship title-history page runs a fully dated chain of 17 reigns. K.C. Colossus won the belt's founding tournament final on 8 November 1997 and held it three separate times before his own retirement; The Executive held it four times, the most of any champion. The ledger records the title vacant for a full year before Rob Holiday won a second tournament final on 21 October 2001, the belt's own last attested reign.

Its own flagship annual event, Royal Rampage, is billed "the Big Dance" — Royal Rampage V (18 June 2000) survives as a full nine-match recap. The Executive retains the World Championship over Jack McGriff in a nine-fall main event decided by three consecutive pinfalls, and three further titles — Cruiserweight, United States, and North American — are defended on the same card. Afterward, Jake Rockwell attacks ally The Executive with a crowbar and dissolves their own alliance, SyndiCorp, on air.
