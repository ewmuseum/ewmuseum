GIWF recruitment advertisement

3 Jun 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I operate a federation (GIWF) that has recently undergone a review by the E Wrestling Zone ... I'm sure you all know where to find those reviews.  Anyway, I've pasted some of the more positive comments below.   "...The GIWF does however imitate the atmosphere of a wrestling event very well. The commentary when present is done very well, and almost gives you the feeling of watching RAW or Nitro. Members and non-members alike will be put in the wrestling "mood" after reading one of these cards, which is a definite plus for the fed....."   "...The color commentary before and after the matches is some of the best on the net...."   "...All members play the role of their characters very well, with little or no break of character. The GIWF could compete with the "big boys" of the e-wrestling world as far as roleplaying is concerned...."   "...The talent of the GIWF makes it a very good choice for free agents...."   "...Story lines run in the GIWF are not your conventional, "Jonny causes Joe to loes, Joe vs. Jonny on the next card." This is one thing that many handlers on the net are looking for. Both the handlers and the staff are willing to put it all on the line to keep the attention of the members..."   "...The Global Internet Wrestling Federation is certainly headed into the right direction. By this time next year it is safe to say that both, The GIWF will still be around, and that it will receive a much higher score than this time. It is a good fed with a lot of potential and should definitely be taken serious as far as its talent goes. Anyone who joins the GIWF is sure to have a pleasant and enjoyable stay. This fed is recommended to anyone looking to have fun, and lots of it...."   On the negative side E Wrestling Zone did critise the way in which matches are written ... I am therefore looking for the following people to join the GIWF: Comitted Roleplayers to join what is already an excellent foundation of good quality roleplayers who are willing to sacrifice their characters for the sake of angles.  You too could be joining stars stars such as Curt Raines, Lew Spectre, The Cockney Rebel, Hamish McGrubber, Akira and dare I say it ... the Bum Boozler. Anyone that would be interested in writing matches.  The GIWF can be found at : http://www.giwf.freeserve.co.uk
