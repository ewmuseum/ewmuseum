Rosso:" Your a dead man Crud ... a dead man." Al Crud opens the door to his car and gets in. Joseph Rosso rushes to his limousine and tries the drivers door ... someone has filled the lock with bubble gum. Joseph Rosso smashes the window, cutting his hand in the process. The lock has been forced into the door. Joseph Rosso rushes round and opens the passenger door. Joseph Rosso reaches inside and drags the bound and gagged figure of Kitten out leaving her to fall hard onto the concrete. Kitten appears to be unconscious. Al Cruds car screeches away in a squeal of burning rubber. Joseph Rosso starts the engine and shifts the gear to drive. A cattle prod appears through the broken window and jams into the side of Rosso's neck. Joseph Rosso collapses behind the wheel. Cpl. Punishment looks at the cattle prod and smiles before returning it to his belt. Cpl. Punishment turns off the engine. Cpl. Punishment walks round the car and picks up the unconscious form of Kitten. Cpl. Punishment walks back into the arena carrying Kitten. Jenkins:"HE WAS THE SHADOW ... JOSEPH ROSSO WAS THE SHADOW ... AND TAKE A LOOK AT THAT CPL PUNISHMENT TAKING KITTEN BACK TO THE RING ...
