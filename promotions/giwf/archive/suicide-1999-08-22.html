Foster:"Good evening everyone and welcome to the brand new Sunday Afternoon Suicide ... a brand new time for one of the best two events to hit the e-wrestling scene." Jenkins:"We're here in a new time slot folks but we're still gonna deliver non stop action like only the GIWF mark down the 22nd August 1999 in your diary because the lords day just became Suicide." Foster:"Ladies and Gentleman ... we've got an amazing card for you tonight and to kick off the first of many future Sunday Afternoon Suicides we have something of a special event for you, for the first time in the GIWF we're gonna witness an Ice Rink weapons match." Jenkins:"The Stylemeister Lyle Wile squaring off against Superstar Stevie May ... two wrestlers who since their debuts have made quite a name for themselves and this match tonight represents the culmination of a mini-feud between the two, Brett." Foster:"A mini-feud? I'd describe it as more than that Tobe, it all started a long time ago when Wile made his return match in the GIWF kicking off his second spell with the company, that match of course against none other than his opponent tonight Stevie May. The very same Stevie May that had defeated Daneel McCord in the previous card." Jenkins:"And folks if your wondering what Daneel McCord has to do with this match , well it's this ... his defeat at the hands of Stevie May came after he was assaulted by a masked man wielding a shovel of all things. That assailant was later revealed to be none other than McCord's former tag-team partner the Golden Drifter, but McCord at the time felt that he'd been set-up by may." Foster:"It was with that in mind that McCord sought to assert his authority in the situation and returned the compliment to May by hitting him in the head with a shovel, that action costing May the match and handing Wile his first victory." Jenkins:"And the unusual thing about that scenario Brett was that it was  that Wile was most aggrieved by McCord's actions, feeling that McCord had tainted what was an inevitable victory in any event, and so from there McCord/Wile was booked." Foster:"That match was of course supposed to be triple threat between May, Wile and McCord but May withdrew himself from that match and after McCord and Wile fought it out we found out why that was." Jenkins:"We sure did Brett, Wile turned out the eventual winner of that match but the cork on the celebration champagne had barely been popped before May appeared from the back and set about the task of dismantling Wile with a piece of wood Brett, he really went to work on Wile and that was about all Wile could take Brett, that assault was the catalyst that finally woke Wile up." Foster:"And he reacted exactly how you'd expect anyone in that situation to react, he said that okay May, you want to use weapons then I'll bring along my trusty hockey stick ... in fact let's make it in the hockey rink." Jenkins:"Full credit to may for stepping up and taking on this match Brett, it's not something May's used to at all, May is a wrestler not a brawler, he does his work in the ring not on the ice but he's still there to give it a go and that's a testament to May's fighting spirit Brett." Foster:"We said it's unusual, we said it's first and we can say now that it ain't gonna be pretty let's get to the ring." Jenkins:"No, let's hit the ice .. roll the Global-Tron."
