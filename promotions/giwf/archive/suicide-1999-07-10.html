GIWF Suicide — 10 Jul 1999

10 Jul 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A long black stretch limo pulls up.  When the limo stops, a large man in a tight black SECURITY shirt rushes to the back door and opens it.  With the door wide open, it's obvious who the limo riders are:  Kitten and Ecstasy. The two gorgeous ladies step out of the car.  Kitten, complete with black fishnet stockings and matching black miniskirt, as well as her "Easy" shirt, is holding tightly onto Ecstasy's hand, leading her away from the paparazzi.  Ecstasy is looking extraordinarily gorgeous in her new attire, which consists of a green PVC dress, bright red stockings (complete with strategically placed rips and tears), and black stiletto heels.  The two look delightfully tacky. But, something is wrong as the camera zooms back to the back seat of the limo.  Ace is no where to be found. Jenkins:"From what I understand Rosso has already declared that the title holders coming out of Global Warming will all advance to the ASA tournament and from there he will hand pick one more tag team and two more singles wrestlers." Foster:"There should be plenty of GIWF wrestlers vying for those wildcard positions and that should really turn up the heat on the competition in the GIWF but for now we have a card to get through and let's get down to it." Jenkins:"Kevin Skinz versus Alexi the trainer." Foster:"This is an interesting match Tobe, Kevin Skinz has for a long time being try to get his hands on Coldfire Zach Shook but has so far been frustrated in his efforts, those frustrations could well boil over tonight but it's not that, that interests me Tobe, its the difference in styles that should make this an intriguing bout." Jenkins:"The Trainer is equally something of an enigma Brett, we've seen him working with the Cossack Warriors over the last few weeks and whilst we don't know a whole lot about him personally what is clear is that this guy knows his wrestling." Foster:"He's really refined the Cossack Warriors, added a lot more technical moves to their arsenal and many within the GIWF feel that he was directly responsible for the Cossacks getting their title shot at Highland Games." Jenkins:"Skinz on the other hand ... well, he's had a mixed bag of results in the GIWF, a loss to Ueshiba Kancho, a draw to White Wolf but there was that impressive win over Daneel McCord, he isn't pretty but he is effective." Foster:"When you get big fat guys like Kevin Skinz you tend to find that nearly all of them are very strong too, Skinz isn't ever going to be involved in an Iron Man Match Tobe but over the five to ten minute matches he's gonna look to dominate his much smaller opponents." Jenkins:"I was talking to Kevin before this match and I asked him how he'd prepared for a match like this with such an unknown opponent." Foster:"What did he say." Jenkins:"He'd eaten Russian Beef !!!" Foster:"Why not? If it works then you can't knock it." Jenkins:"We'll soon find out if out does work Brett, lets get to the ring."
