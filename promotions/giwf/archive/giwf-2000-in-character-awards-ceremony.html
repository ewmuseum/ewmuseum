Late 2000 (exact date not recoverable; the Second Annual GIWF Awards Ceremony, aired at the close of the calendar year)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hall of Fame inducteesThe year-2000 GIWF Hall of Fame class, announced at the close of this ceremony: Al Crud and Lyle Wile (singles), the Phenomenons and Tropical Storm (tag).
GIWF Second Annual Awards Ceremony The scene opens with a lavishly decorated GIWF entrance lobby. The usually drab building exterior has been decorated to reflect the grandeur of the evenings proceedings, a large canopy covers the main entrance under which uniformed concierges assist the arriving superstars and dignitaries from their vehicles and keeping back the throng of reporters whose flash bulbs would mesmerize even the most fame hardy of individual. Leading from the sidewalk to the entrance to the building is a deep piled red carpet looking a little drab from the light drizzle that has fallen on it over the last couple of hours. A motorbike pulls up onto the red carpet, leading into the awards ceremony Millions of camera's are flashing Off steps a casual looking Benito Cavalera, to the delight of the crowd on the other side of the barriers Benito Cavalera: "(Flips his keys to the valet) You be careful parking that! Valet: "Actually Mr. Cavalera, the GIWF Committee don't exactly want you attending this years awards ceremony." Benito Cavalera: "(Puzzled) Excuse me? Why the hell not?" Valet: "Safety reasons. Celest and Pete Steele are in attendance, and after various security measures where carefully thought through, we decided it was better if you were not allowed in." Benito Cavalera: "So some schmuck who joined a few weeks ago from the PWW is allowed in, and I'm not. I mean fair do to Pete Steele, that guy sucked a lot (makes obvious gestures) to get where he is today, but to let that piece of crap Celest enter over me?" Benito Cavalera drives away The scene cuts back inside the arena which, as it had been the previous year, has been converted to form a fan shape leading out from the central point that is the solid oak lectern that has featured so many times at different GIWF and GIWF related events. Stood behind the lectern are Brett Foster and Toby Jenkins dressed immaculately again and with a little festive decoration in their suit pockets. A quick camera sweep around the building shows the various past and present GIWF Superstars all enjoying a steak luncheon and glutting themselves on the free alcohol be it wine or beer that has been laid on by their employers for the event. In a stark contrast to the previous year the GIWF Wrestlers appear remarkably well behaved. Pete Steele and Al Crud appear to be having a civil conversation with Gurgles and even Luca remains seated rather than dancing on top of any tables, perhaps the only sight out of the ordinary is that of a virtual mountain of Red Bull cans that rests in the seat next to the tiny imp. As the red lights on the camera's slowly begin flipping on in succession the waiting audience begin to quieten and await the start of the evenings proceedings.
