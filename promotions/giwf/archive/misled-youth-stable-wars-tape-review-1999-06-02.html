Misled Youth, the Stable Wars tape review

2 Jun 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--=First off this was right after a HUGE PPV..Stable Wars(<--it was named three months aog os no it wasn't a Star Wars rip-off)Not usually so long but I recape at the end for the short readers..anyway PLEASE tell me what you think I can improve on --=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--=--= [The scene opens in the Misled Youth warehouse.Everyone is present except Ace Thunder.Pyro is on the couch along side Mad Jack Manson.Pyro has a black t-shirt on and black 3/4 shorts on.Mad Jack has the original Misled Youth t-shirt on and long black baggy jeans.Flammable is going over a tape of the PPV and is sitting in the recliner.Iron Preist is sitting in a folding steel chair beside the couch.He has his 'Jesus and Pals t-shirt on and blue shorts that hang halfway down his shin.Freak is sitting behind the bass drum with his spray piant,paint brush,paint cans and airbrush.He is smoking a cigarette.Next to the drum is a ash tray and a pack of Newports.He is just looking at the drum..staring at the blue head with a white circle around the top.Finally he picks up his airbrush.He does a test spot on a little peice of cloth to make sure it is black and then starts painting.] Flammable:Ok Freak and Jack come over here. (Freak is still painting) Freak:Umm...yeah...what now? (He finally cut the brush off and sets it down.He walks over and stands behind Flammable) Flammable:I want to point out some things in the match Freak(mumbling):Go damit.. Flammable:Hey I heard that now just watch this.You know you guys are the number 2 contenders for the titles. Freak:Oh yeah..so the fuckin Dreamwarriors are number 1 and we tryed to beat them twice already that aren'tlooking for another match with us. Flammable:True BUT..they had a title shot tonight..and you know what.. Mad Jack Manson:Your right they lost..so now they need to make way for someone else to get a shot. Flammable:I will contact the G.I.W.F. offices tommorrow morning but right now we have to go over this tape.The first thing I want to point out is pretty soon in the match.Jack you just taged out. (the scene changes to the telivision where the following clip comes up) Jack Manson tags out to Freak. Freak takes Hamish McGrubber down with an eye gouge. Freak locks Hamish McGrubber in a chokehold. Keith Burge warns Freak to let go. Keith Burge counts: One, two, three, four, five. Keith Burge warns Freak. (tape pauses but still same view) Flammable:Ok Freak you had the big man down.You should have broken the hold at the count of 4.Then you hook it right back on for another 4. Freak:Ok I guess you are right Flammable:Of course I am.Then we have this clip. (The tapes starts back up) Cockney Rebel locks Jack Manson in a full nelson. Jack Manson is inching his way towards the ropes. Jack Manson tries to fight the pain. Jack Manson breaks the hold after 19 seconds. Keith Burge removes the chair from the ring. Jack Manson tags out to Freak. Hamish McGrubber enters the ring and lays out Jack Manson. (Still same view but you hear the voices) Flammable:Ok right here it should be enter Freak and nail McTuby.Where were you?Nevermind..back it up a bit though.Jack you should have kicked backwards..I know you didn't think of that.I have ben in a full nelson before and a good way to get out is to kick behind you and hop you connect with a kneecap..next clip (Tape starts up) Eric Cameron and Shane Stone hit Freak with the Phenomenal Ending. Eric Cameron goes for the pin. Eric Cameron has the crowd going wild. Keith Burge counts: One, two, Jack Manson makes the save. Eric Cameron tags out to Shane Stone. Eric Cameron places Freak on the turnbuckle. Eric Cameron nails Freak with a double underhook superplex. Shane Stone places Freak on the turnbuckle. Shane Stone hits Phenomenal DDT on Freak. The crowd erupts. Eric Cameron leaves the ring. Shane Stone uses a short lariat on Freak. Keith Burge counts: One, two, three. Freak has been eliminated. The crowd is on its feet cheering for Shane Stone. Flammable:You made one good save but then where were you for the second one?Anyway you should have been there but that is it.Preist Pyro your turn. (Freak Mad Jack go back where they were and the Iron Preist and Pyro take there place) Flammable:You know you are the number 2 contender for the TV Title? Pyro:But who is number 1 contender? Flammable:The jail bird E-Razor Pyro:So how am I supposed to beat him for the spot? Flammable:You can't so you have to challenge Ice J one more time instead. Pyro:Do what? Flammable:He is the next guy up from you. Pyro:Damn well who is after him? Flammable:Al Crud but you have to beat Ice J again.Iron Preist you are the number 1 contender for the Intercontinentel title but now on with the tape. (tape comes on) Al Crud tags out to Pyro. Pyro throws Lew Spectre out of the ring. Pyro goes for Asai moonsault, but Lew Spectre moves out of the way. Lew Spectre knocks Pyro into the ringpost. Lew Spectre is being booed out of the building. Lew Spectre goes for Reverse Cradle Piledriver, but Pyro counters it with an arm bite. (Still frame but hear the voices) Flamamble:Stop number one.That was a beutiful way to stop him in his tracks..remember to use that move more often (Tape Back on) Pyro whips Lew Spectre into the guardrail. Dermott Gallagher counts: 1. Pyro executes a dropkick on Lew Spectre. Dermott Gallagher counts: 2. Pyro whips Lew Spectre into the guardrail. Pyro goes for an enzuigiri, but Lew Spectre ducks out of the way. Lew Spectre goes for the Figure-Four Sleeper, but Pyro blocks it. Dermott Gallagher counts: 3. Lew Spectre knocks Pyro into the ringsteps. Curt Raines comes over to make it two-on-one. The Iron Priest comes over and lays out Curt Raines. The Iron Priest executes a football tackle on Lew Spectre. Flammable:Nice move Preist.That is what team work is all about. Pyro uses an enzuigiri on Lew Spectre. Pyro whips Lew Spectre into the guardrail. Pyro executes a roundhouse right on Lew Spectre. Dermott Gallagher counts: 4. Pyro reenters the ring. Lew Spectre follows him back in. Pyro leaves the ring. He returns with a chair. Pyro runs into the ropes and springs off the chair. Pyro uses a legdrop on Lew Spectre. Pyro executes a flying cross body press on Lew Spectre. Flammable:You should have used the sross body block off of the chair. Dermott Gallagher counts: One, two, kickout. (back to the old shot of the couch and the recliner.) Flammable:off the chair you get better leverage.You go on to hit the Inferno and then you git put in the figure-four-sleeper thingy.Anyway I thought that might help you. Pyro:So what is going down on SAS...anything yet? Flammable:Not yet but we are laying down some challenges right now.Pyro-vs-Ice J falls count anywhere....Mighty Isrealites-vs-Misled Youth for the World Tag-Titles..Preist anyone you want to challenge Iron Preist:Let me sleep on it..well I have to split. (Iron Preist exits the room.) Pyro:So you will get all that to Darren Goldthorpe right? Flammable:yeah..hey Freak you finished the drum yet? Freak:I am working on it still.It is very detailed work and all this paint is making me light headed so I am going to have to wok on it later. Mad Jack Manson:Then the one and 2 right. Freak:Yes then the turntables and then I have to do the name on the front of it then I have to redo the cherry sunburst on the bass..then the Explorer then I am thinking the snare drum.It will only take maybe 2 days. Mad jack Manson:That's it? Freak:Yeah I have been doing it for a while.The turntables are small..the bass just neds some touching up but I am thinking a new design.The Explorer won't take long and the snare head is small. Pyro:Just as long as it doesn't take to long. Freak:Hey I said 2 days tops. (The scene fades to black) Recap- Matches for SAS- Pyro-vs-Ice J(yeah one more time)falls count anywhere Misled Youth-vs-Mighty Isrealites for the World Tag-Titles.
