GIWF In Character Awards Ceremony (1999)

1999 (exact date not recoverable; the first-ever GIWF Annual Awards Ceremony, aired at the close of the calendar year)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hall of Fame inducteesThe first-ever GIWF Hall of Fame class, announced at the close of this ceremony: Hamish McGrubber and Lew Spectre (singles), the Dark Lords and the Dream Warriors (tag).
GIWF - "IN CHARACTER" AWARDS CEREMONY -1999 [The scene opens in the lavishly furnished GIWF Arena. Gone are the rows of prefabricated plastic seats and the ring, in their place are groups of round tables each seating approximately ten persons and fronted by an elaborate lectern of solid Oak. The room itself is humming with the sound of low volume chatter and the occasional clink of champagne glasses. Everyone who is anyone (and a lot more) is gathered with the confines of the arena for the finale to the GIWF's wrestling calendar. Standing nervously behind the lectern GIWF Lynch-pin commentator and chief talent scout Toby Jenkins is dressed immaculately in a black Tuxedo, white shirt and bow tie, as in fact, are most of the 200 strong crowd. The front tables have been reserved for the active GIWF roster rather than the customary dignitaries as is often the case. The Order of the Hammer look to be having a great time at Table number two are the virtual pyramid of champagne bottles betrays, of course there are rumors that, as happened at the Order's Christmas party J.Harold Christ is turning his alcoholic beverages into water, but of-course proving something like that is anyone's nightmare. Off to the left sit the "Stable with No-Name", although conspicuous by their absence are The Masked Man, The Masked Bastard and Pete Steele, in fact the only people actually sat there are Al Crud and Ole Gus ... and there's no way that Gus has put away that much alcohol ... Al ... that's another story. The Stable with no-name are clearly uncomfortable with their attire as can be seen by Al Crud waving away the attention of Ole Gus as he tries in vain to tie the bow tie of Al. Al ain't one for formal evening wear that's for sure. Oddly enough the table behind Al Crud, occupied by former GIWF employee Luca has been pulled quite a distance away from Al, it would appear that perhaps Al's 'Problem' is rearing it's ugly head again. Sat behind Al, Luca seems to having a bit of a problem understanding just why he has been sat alone. The peg across his nose would suggest that he certainly isn't exactly non-plussed by his location and perhaps his incessant peanut flicking in the direction of White Wolf would go someway to explaining the presence of the two large security guards who seem to be constantly warning the mischievous Imp about his behavior. A few more former GIWF stars have received invitations to the Awards Ceremony ... Off to the far left 'Fatty' Kevin Skinz seems to have polished off his third whole chicken of the day and The Thompson Brothers don't seems to have quite grasped the concept of the evening as they repeatedly hit their manager Dankind with chair shots. Enjoying the show from a few rows back accompanied only by Physio Dave and Gimp is former GIWF Champion The Bum Boozler who for some reason is surrounded by a troop of security ... ahh ... there's Pete Steele. Despite the few brief scuffles generally speaking a good time is being had by all under the watchful eye of GIWF President Darren Goldthorpe who is making his only appearance on GIWF Television since February of 1999. Sat in a large skybox above the main hall the camera notices that he is accompanied by a much larger figure, as is zooms in the shot is suddenly changed to Toby Jenkins who, it appears, has got the go ahead to begin the evenings proceedings. Adjusting his tie, checking his cards and clearing his throat he begins.] Jenkins: "Good Evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to the first ever GIWF Annual Awards Ceremony. Tonight we celebrate the inaugural year of the GIWF and give thanks to the people who, in the short space of ten months turned the GIWF from a small promotion to one of the largest in the world ... the GIWF Superstars. When you look at the World of Wrestling there are a whole lot of people that it takes to make a federation great, from the lowly presenters such as myself and Brett Foster to the President himself. It goes without saying however that without the brave men that risk their life and limb by stepping into that squared circle we just couldn't achieve what we already have, particularly in such a short space of time. And it is those very men that we honor today. Throughout the course of tonight, over 12 different Awards we will acknowledge the achievements of these people voted for by the other members of the GIWF roster. Without any further a do let's get started with our first award of the evening, to read the nominations may I welcome my GIWF broadcast colleague Mr Brett Foster." [A polite round of applause greats the announcement as an equally immaculately dressed Brett Foster joins Toby Jenkins behind the lectern." Foster: "Thank you Toby. Ladies and Gentlemen our first award this evening is perhaps the less prestigious of them all. The 'Jerry Wight' sponsored Jobber of the Year Award. Naturally Jerry Wight was excluded from the voting for fear of a landslide but for those of you at home of our four nominations ... They are ... For his sporadic interviews and often varied style ... The wHiTe WoLf ... For his ability to talk up a storm and then be quieter than a mouse when he was signed to a match ... Stevie May ... For his unparalleled losing streak ... Daneel McCord ... And for his downright abysmal interviews, a name from the past and bookies favorite ... The Assassin." Jenkins: "And the results please Brett." Foster: "Thank you Tobe. In third place with 23% of the vote ... The Assassin. In second place with 36% of the vote ... Daneel McCord. And therefore the 1999 GIWF Jobber of the year with 41% of the vote ... 'Superstar' Stevie May !!!" Jenkins: "Naturally folks, thanks to his recent firing from the GIWF Stevie May is not here to collect his award, in any event he probably wouldn't have much to say anyway so we'll get things going with the second of tonight's awards and certainly and award that is a lot more sought after than our last one. I am of course talking about the GIWF: Best Newcomer of 1999 Award. Now before Brett gives the nominations can I remind everyone at home that this award is for the newcomer that rose through the ranks the quickest ... the man who arrived on the shores of the GIWF and really went on to create an immediate impact within a relatively short space of time. With that in mind I'll stand aside again and let Brett give you a run down of the four nominations for this great accolade." Foster:" Thank you Tobe. Yes ladies and gentlemen, no matter what these four people have gone on to achieve I'm sure that they would all like to know that when they first arrived in the GIWF they really made people sit up and take notice of them, that when they took those first tentative steps people looked at them and realised that they were destined for better things. Here are the nominations: For his rapid ascension up the hierarchy of the GIWF ... Nephilim. For his triumphant return leading all the way to a World Title shot ... Lyle Wile. For living proof that hard work and determination can take you anywhere you want to go ... Ben Jackson. And for his capture of the Television Title within six weeks of his entry into the GIWF ... Tremor." Jenkins: "And before Brett reads the results to you, could I just say that on a personal note I think that all of those nominated tonight for this award are all excellent competitors and should all regard it as an honor that they have made it this far. There can be only be one winner but the fact that your peers have recognised your achievements and nominated for this award is a testament to your ability." Foster: "Well I'd sure echo those sentiments Tobe, so here we go with the results. Tied in third place with 15% of the vote each ... Lyle Wile and Tremor. In second place with 23% of the vote ... Ben Jackson. And the winner of the 1999 GIWF: Newcomer of the Year, with a huge 46% of the vote ... Nephilim." Jenkins: "Our esteemed Commissioner and of course former North American Champion, lifting a well deserved award and the first of the evening for his stable The Order of the Hammer, I'm sure he'll have plenty to say about that later Brett." Foster: "He sure does like to talk Tobe." Jenkins: "He sure does and rightfully so as the first winner tonight of a 'real' award, and he's up for some more so we could be seeing his name on more than occasion tonight, possibly as part of the next award which goes to the best Stable of 1999. Now ladies and gentlemen as you all know the 1999 Stable Wars decided that the Youngbloods were the official 1999 Stable of the Year, however tonight's award is the Stable of the Year as decided by the GIWF Roster, a subtle albeit vital difference." Foster: "And a hard fought field Tobe. There are some great Stables in the GIWF at the moment." Jenkins: "There sure are and what we also know is that there's not a lot of love lost between any of our four main nominees." Foster: "No there isn't Tobe, it's not only the Official Award that's at stake in this one, you just know that the winners of this next award are going to enjoy the luxury of unfettered bragging rights." Jenkins: "And can you imagine just what the Youngbloods would be like if they lifted this award today?" Foster: "I don't imagine any less braggart than the Order would Tobe, or for that matter the Warrior Clan or the Mutha Buckers. They all want the title of the Award Winning Stable of 1999 Tobe." Jenkins: "The nominations Brett." Foster: "The Nominations for the best Stable of 1999 go to: The Mutha Buckers, consisting of: Studog Escobar, E Razor and Team Esco ... The Warrior Clan, consisting of: Akira and The Dream Warriors ... The Order of the Hammer, consisting of: Twiztid, Nephilim, Tropical Storm and Ace Thunder ... And finally the Youngbloods, consisting of: Lew Spectre, Cpl Punishment and The Phenomenons." Jenkins: "Get that envelope open Brett .. I can't wait for the results of this one ..." Foster: "Here we go Tobe ... good luck everyone." [Brett tears open the envelope and stares at the results before looking slightly disappointed.] Foster: "Oh boy ... In third place with 7% of the vote ... The Warrior Clan. In joint second place with 28% of the vote each ... The Mutha Buckers and ... ... The Order of the Hammer meaning that your winners with 37% of the vote and now the Official and Award winning Stable of 1999 ... The Youngbloods." Jenkins: "I can't say that I'm looking forward to hearing from Shane Stone and Eric Cameron after that announcement Brett." Foster: "I'm sure that they'll have plenty to say when they're given the opportunity Tobe, thankfully that won't be tonight." Jenkins: "Ladies and Gentlemen by the looks of things over there with Pete Steele trying to get at the Bum Boozler I'd say that time is running short. We're going to move on now with our next award which is the award for the best feud of the year. Let me say that this has got to be one of the hardest categories we have tonight. Over the ten months that the GIWF has been up and running we have seen some top notch action and in my opinion some of the most entertaining conflicts in the world of Sports Entertainment. I don't think that any will forget the day that The Bum Boozler ... err ..." [Jenkins visibly shakes as the Boozler, hearing his name mentioned takes a break from dipping gimps ... well, you don't need to know all the details, but nonetheless he takes a break from it to wave to Tobe.] Jenkins: " ... Climbed the ladder and retrieved the key to the Steel Chastity belt that had been around his waist for the last month ... and ladies and gentlemen to give you some idea of the quality on offer in this particular category the example I've just given you didn't even receive a nomination." Foster: "Could I just say Tobe that this award ought to be considered a special honor, perhaps more so than many of the other awards. You know that if you receive this award you have played a large part in something which has captured the attention of your peers and entertained them as well as the public at large." Jenkins: "Very true Brett, the nominations please." Foster: "The nominations for the best feud of 1999 go to: For the [shakes his head] 'Gay Wedding and pursuit': The Bum Boozler and Shane Stone. [Shane Stone is seen burying his head in his hands as that is read out on national television ... Eric Cameron appears to be doing victory laps with the best Stable Award.] For what has to be one of the most intense Stable Rivalries in living memory: The Order of the Hammer and the Youngbloods. For systematically destroying each other in more ways that was humanly thought possible: Twiztid and Ace Thunder. And for Al's visit to the Proctologist: The Bum Boozler and Al Crud." Jenkins: " And can I just remind everyone that we do have riot police standing by. The results please Brett." Foster: "Sure thing. In third place with 12% of the vote ... Shane Stone and the Bum Boozler for the 'Gay Wedding'. In second place with 25% of the vote ... Al Crud and the Bum Boozler for Al's visit to the Proctologist. And the winners of the 1999 Feud of the Year Award with a huge 56% of the vote are ... Twiztid and Ace Thunder. !!!" [Announcement met by hearty cheer from the TOOTH table where another bottle of champagne is being opened.] Jenkins: "With any luck perhaps with Ace now enjoying a well earned break those two brothers may just get through the next year without physically destroying each other another year." Foster: "That must be quite a reunion at thanksgiving Tobe." Jenkins: "Perhaps we should be there to call it !!! Seriously though folks we still have eight more awards to get through and our next award is for the greatest match of 1999. This category is even more diverse than the greatest feud of 1999, there have been some amazing one on one encounters in the last ten months and to pick just one of over 500 that we have held is a mammoth task in anyone's books, but pick one we have. Brett, the nominations." Foster: "Thank you Tobe, the nominations for the greatest match of 1999 go to: Studog Escobar and Twiztid for the Naked Roseanne Barr match. The Dark Lords and Team Esco for the Crucifix Match that marked the end of the Dark Lords reign in the GIWF. Curt Raines and Hamish McGrubber for the World Title Cage Match at Fear the Raine. And finally, to Nephilim and Ace Thunder for their 45 minute epic at Halloween Hell." Jenkins: "Four absolute classics Brett and four matches that have all in one way or another played a part in GIWF History. Would you announce the results please Brett." Foster: "Good luck everyone ... [Foster opens the envelope and his eyes widen with surprise] We have quite an unusual result here folks ... In a three way tie for third place with 23% of the vote each we have ... Studog Escobar, Twiztid, The Dark Lords, Team Esco, Nephilim and Ace Thunder. And that means that the winners of the 1999: Match of the Year Award with 31% of the vote goes to Curt Raines and Hamish McGrubber for the World Title Cage Match." Jenkins: "And can I just say that I remember that match well and in my view that's a very worthy winner." Foster: "I think it had us both on the edge of our seats Tobe." Jenkins: "Ladies and gentlemen when you use the words 'edge of my seat' you describe the action that the GIWF delivers twice a week no matter which of it's superstars is in the squared circle, however there's a lot more to the GIWF than that ... whole lot more. Our next award tonight is designed to acknowledge those wrestlers a little more 'unconventional' than their counter parts. It is the GIWF's Award for the best Comedy Wrestler of 1999." Foster: "And when we say comedy we don't mean a fool or circus clown, we mea the sort of person that lays it on the line not just to get titles and beat people up but to entertain in other ways too." Jenkins: "It certainly has to be regarded as one of the most difficult tasks to achieve in the GIWF ... the ability to provide that comic relief, the ability to move up the rankings yet at the same time provide a source of amusement for the fans at home and their fellow superstars. So for me this award holds with it the special knowledge that the winner has achieved something that not many others could." Foster: "Thank you Tobe. The nominations for the best Comedy Wrestler of 1999 go to: Al Crud for his hours of flatulence. The Phenomenons for the mad-cap antics that only they could get away with. Pete Steele for his 'Homo War' and finally, The Bum Boozler for his ... errrm ... actions." Jenkins: "Not exactly the man I'd like to see up ... forget it ... just give us the results Brett." Foster: "Come on Pete. Come on Al ... even Shane and Eric ... just please not the Boozler." [Brett opens up the envelope and spends a few seconds reading it before dropping it to the ground and walking off stage. A stunned looking Toby Jenkins retrieves the card before proffering a nervous smile to the camera.] Jenkins: "It appears that Brett's obligations for the night have just ended ladies and gentlemen, and let me tell you that we all expected this award to be particularly close run ... just not this close. Here are the results: In a three way tie for second place with 21% of the vote each ... The Phenomenons ... Pete Steele and ... ... Al Crud ... [A collective sign echo's around the arena] which means the winner of the 1999 Best Comedy Wrestler Award goes to none other than the Bum Boozler." Jenkins: "Not really wishing to dwell on that topic can I quickly move on and introduce my guest for the second half of tonight's awards ceremony the former Commissioner of the GIWF Mr Jae O'Connor." [A rejuvenated looking Jae O'Connor joins Toby Jenkins at the lectern] Jenkins: "Jae, welcome to the GIWF Awards Ceremony it's good to see you back." O'Connor: "Thanks Tobe, it's a pleasure to be back and especially on tonight of all nights." Jenkins: "Jae you have the honor of announcing the award for the GIWF's best 'Non-Wrestling' Character of 1999, before you give us the nominations could I just say that it is the nominees that Jae is about to read out should all be recognised for the contribution they make to the GIWF. We take for granted that people like Lew Spectre and Al Crud will be here to entertain us day after day but there's no doubting that without these people who operate behind the scenes the GIWF would not be the great spectacle that it is." O'Connor: "That's right Tobe, the nominees for the best Non-Wrestling Character of 1999 go to: Jumbo MacLooney for his substitute work during Toby's extended break to the Carribean. Bob the Robot for ... for ... having the longest extension cord in the GIWF. [Bob, dressed in an unbelievably well fitting Tux, nods his approval] Ole Gus for his ... erm ... motivation of Pete Steel. [Ole Gus grabs the ass of a waitress as she walks by an gets slapped] And finally Luca the Imp [Luca jumps onto the table and begins thrusting his pelvis in the direction of all the other diners] for his astute management of the Dark Lords and invaluable contribution to the formation of the Death Cult. And can I just echo Tobe's words in saying that the GIWF would not be the industry giant it is without the contribution of the Jumbo MacLooney's, the Ole Gus' and dare I say it, the Bob the Robots and the Luca's. And therefore the recipient of tonight's award can rest assured that their place in the GIWF is valued every bit as much as the Lew Spectre's and Pete Steele's. Now, here are the results: In third place with 19% of the Vote ... Bob the Robot. In a two way tie for the second place with 25% of the vote each Ole Gus and ... ... Luca, which means that the winner of the 1999 Non-Wrestling Personality of the Year goes to Jumbo MacLooney. It's a good job Brett still isn't out here Tobe." Jenkins: "Is that a gun-shot I just heard ?" O'Connor: "I sure hope not." Jenkins: "Well ladies and gentlemen this ... is where we get serious. The next five awards are arguably the biggest of the evening, from this point on we acknowledge those in the GIWF that are regarded as the best at what they do. We will shortly find out just who the GIWF considers to be the best there was and the best there is in both the Tag and Singles divisions, before that however we get a look at who the GIWF considers to be the best of the rest. The final award before we deicide the best tag-team and Singles wrestlers is to determine the best wrestler never to hold a major title. By major title we mean either the World Title and the North American Title and when you look at the caliber of wrestler that has received nominations it really brings home to you just how difficult it is to sit at the top of the GIWF's mountain." O'Connor: "It sure does Tobe, this award more than any other is a sign of the people to watch in the next year, everyone that has been nominated here has been nominated because their fellow wrestlers recognize them as people with the ability to go on and achieve greater things. All they need do is apply themselves rigorously and they could find themselves nominated for a greater award this time next year." Jenkins: "The nominations Jae." O'Connor: "The nominees for the best wrestler to hold a major title are: Former television Title holder and a man widely regarded by many to be the best submissions wrestler in the GIWF ... E Razor. Former television Title holder ... Tremor. The only man to hold the Television Title on three separate occasions and a former Intercontinental Champion ... Ben Jackson. And finally a former three time Intercontinental Champion and a man that since the polls closed has in fact gone on to capture the North American Title ... Cpl Punishment." Jenkins: "A tough field if ever there was one Jae ... I'm glad that I didn't have to vote in that one." O'Connor: "Well, many did and we did get a clear winner." Jenkins: "Would you care to enlighten us Jae?" O'Connor: "I sure would Tobe. In third place with 13% of the vote ... Tremor. In second place with 38% of the vote ... Ben Jackson. And the winner of the 1999: Best Wrestler to Never hold a major title is the man who went on to end that trend at Christmas Clash ... Cpl Punishment ... picking up his second award of the night." Jenkins: "The Youngblood table looking a little fuller after that one and they have chance at some more tonight Jae." O'Connor: "Well I don't think I'm spoiling the surprise if I say that the Phenomenons and Lew Spectre have received nominations so they do have a shout, not in our next one though." Jenkins: "That's right. Ladies and gentlemen we decide now the best there was in the Tag-Team division. All of these teams nominated are no longer with us but have made their impact on the Tag-Division. It's because of the impact they have had that we honor them today. Jae ... the nominations if you will." O'Connor: "Before I do Tobe can I remind the folks at home that all of today's nominations were put forward by the GIWF roster, it appears that the tag-teams of the past didn't quite create the impression we expected as we have only three nominations for this award. They are as follows: The only three time tag-team champions in the GIWF ... The Dark Lords. The only team to score a clean pin over the Dark Lords ... The Dream Warriors. And those Lucozade warriors the Mighty Israelites." Jenkins: "All three teams helped to construct the highly competitive tag-team division that we have today and we must thank them all for that. However, that's not what we're here to talk about, we're here to talk about the best Team of 1999 No longer to compete in the GIWF, the results please Jae." O'Connor: "It was emphatic Tobe ... In third place with 13% of the vote, The Mighty Israelites. In second place with 20% of the vote, The Dream Warriors. And you run away winners with an astonishing 67% of the vote, The Dark Lords." Jenkins: "And folks, we were to hear a few words on behalf of the Dark Lords from Luca but unfortunately he stormed out of the arena after losing the non-wrestling personality to Jumbo MacLooney so we'll move straight on to the award that decides who, of the present tag-team roster is regarded as the best team of 1999. And Jae, to the tag-division this award is the top of the tree. Whomever it is that lifts this award tonight can rest in the knowledge that until this time next year they, in terms of recognition, are the top of the tree. We have some excellent teams competing for this and the award here is a real sign that you have arrived." O'Connor: "Well Tobe I know who it is that you'll be routing for, so without further a do, here are the nominations: The inaugural North American Tag-Team Champions, Tropical Storm. The longest running GIWF Champions ever, Team Esco. Arguably the most annoying or entertaining, depending on your perspective, team in the GIWF, The Phenomenons. And finally the current GIWF Tag-Team Champions the Fomorians." Jenkins: "Fingers crossed Jim, Midge." O'Connor: "Try and stay impartial Tobe. The results are: In third place with 7% of the vote ... Team Esco. In a tie for second place with 29% of the vote each ... The Fomorians and .... .... The Phenomenons, meaning that the winners of the Best Active Tag-Team of 1999 are Tropical Storm with 35% of the vote." Jenkins: [Arms aloft] "I never had a doubt about it. There you have it folks, Tropical Storm are the best there is in the Tag-Team division, at least for 1999." O'Connor: "And perhaps we ought to move on before the celebrations get out of hand Tobe." Jenkins: "I'll remain as professional as ever Jae as we move to what are very possibly the two most important awards of the night. There can be no denying it folks, the singles division is, has been and probably always will be the highlight of the GIWF. In the ten months that we have been running the GIWF has seen some incredible talent come through it's doors and that is a word that I don't use lightly. During my three years in this business I have seen a number of wrestlers come and go, not just with the GIWF but with other federations such as the CEWF, XEWF, IBF, IWF and EIWL, I can honestly say that the talent we honor tonight could have made World Champion in any of those federations, these are the people that set the standard for the newcomers to the GIWF, they are as much a part of the foundation of the GIWF as the bricks and mortar that hold this very building. It is a distinguished filed that reads like a who's who of the wrestling world. Jae, if you will." O'Connor: "It would be my pleasure Tobe. This really is a who's who of the wrestling world, all of these guys are legends and it's a testament to the GIWF that they have all graced our floors. Here are the nominees: Ladies and Gentlemen, the ego has landed ... despite only wrestling three matches in the GIWF, former Commissioner 'The Demi-God' Joseph Rosso. The only man to hold the GIWF World Title on more than one occasion and the recently returning ... 'The Highlander' Hamish McGrubber. Perhaps the most controversial wrestler to ever set foot in the GIWF and bidding for his second award of the evening ... The Bum Boozler. And finally, the Hardcore Icon and a man also bidding for his second award of the night ... Ace Thunder." Jenkins: "Unbelievable to think the talent that is available to receive this award Jae." O'Connor: "It sure is, it almost seems unfair to try and distinguish between four greats like that but that's the task that was set to the GIWF roster and that's exactly what they have done. It was one of the closest run of the night but here are the results: In a tie for third place with 20% of the votes each ... 'The Highlander' Hamish McGrubber and 'The Demi-God' Joseph Rosso. In second place with 27% of the votes ... 'The Hardcore Icon' Ace Thunder, which means that the winner of the best GIWF Wrestler of 1999 no longer on the active roster goes to none other than The Bum Boozler with 33% of the vote." Jenkins: "Only 13% separating third and first place and the Bum Boozler picking up his third award of the night, who'd have called that one." O'Connor: "Not me, but then again, as we've said all night, all of these awards have been decided by the current GIWF roster and now Toby." Jenkins: "Now we get to the big one Jae. Ladies and gentlemen, the final award of the evening is without doubt not only the most prestigious if tonight's ceremony, it is also the most prestigious that this industry has to offer. The recipient of this award has the privilege of knowing that his peers consider him to be the best that the GIWF has to offer at this moment in time. That, is a privilege that no-one should take lightly Jae, I don't think that I could accurately put into words what an accolade like this would mean Jae ... the recipient of this award knows that he is not only sitting on top of the tree but that everyone else knows it too." O'Connor: "It is without a doubt the greatest accolade that I can think of Tobe and to be the one here announcing it is quite an honor it's self." Jenkins: "Don't go away ladies and Gentlemen because right after this award we will announce the first four inductees into the GIWF Hall of Fame, but before that ... Jae the floor is yours." O'Connor: "Ladies and Gentlemen, the nominations for the Best Active Wrestler of 1999 are: The flatulent Maestro himself ... 'The Bastard' Al Crud. The former GIWF Champion and quite possibly the most insane man to set foot in the GIWF ... Twiztid. Nominated for his second award of the night, possibly the most enthusiastic wrestler in the GIWF ... Ben Jackson. And finally the foundation on which the Youngbloods are built ... Lew Spectre." Jenkins: "We don't need to tell you that the tension is unbearable ... Jae ... now if you please. O'Connor: "It really is a great achievement just to be nominated for an award but here we go: In third place with 22% of the vote ... Ben Jackson. In second place with 28% of the vote ... Lew Spectre. And the winner of the 1999 GIWF Active Wrestler of the year goes to ... Twiztid" [As pandemonium breaks out on the Order table Toby Jenkins struggles to be heard above the noise." Jenkins: "Folks, here's a run down of those results, and remember don't go away because we'll be back with the Hall of Fame inductees.
