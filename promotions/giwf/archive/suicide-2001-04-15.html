Connolly: Where’s Prodigy? Curtis: He isn’t coming. Connolly: That coward! Curtis: With the BOD down there and Evil on the loose can you blame him? Luca: "Hah, ha, ha, ha… I knew there was nothing to worry about with Prodigy! I knew, that just like he was when he was a wrestler, he would be a silent partner in his daddies Business!
