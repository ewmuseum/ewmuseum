Joshua Dillinger, GIWF TV interview

Sep 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lost Soul wrote: > That Role Play needs some more qaulity and lenght, but for a begginer it's > good. Go to FWLnet.com and read up on their RP lessons! I thought so. Honestly, I wrote this about a year ago, one of my earliest RP that I could find. The sad thing was, I won with that RP.
The other sad thing? Things haven't improved much cos a month or so later, I came up with this nugget-o-fun.
-- (The scene opens in the arena parking lot. The door leading to the parking lot creaks open slowly. Joshua Dillinger's head pops out from behind the door, scanning the area for any signs of the other wrestlers. We see that he is wearing a Ben Jackson "Unforgiven" T-shirt. He heaves a sigh of relief when he sees no one around and makes a run for his Ford F150. Before he can jump into the driver's seat, Tobe Jenkins runs up with a camera crew.)
Jenkins: Josh! (Joshua jumps with a start then reaches towards the passenger's seat and pulls out a baseball bat.)
Jenkins: Whoa there. It's just me, Tobe. Remember? Dillinger: What do you want? And is anybody watching this recording right now? Jenkins: Boy, you're jumpy. Don't worry, we just want to get a few words from you for GIWF TV. Maybe you'd like to comment on tonight, followed by a few words on your match at the coming PPV.
Dillinger: What's there to comment about tonight? The North-South Express lost to RIMJOB and Titan got his a$$ deported back North. Titan, like the Titanic, you stank, I mean sank. It's nothing personal, my friend, it's business.
Titan, the Canadian Flyboy, Deke Aurora, Lyle "In Denial" Wile, see the similarities, Tobe.
Jenkins: If that comment means what I think it means, it could get you into trouble with the Canadian fans.
Dillinger: (Grinning.) Well, to my Canadian fans, it's nothing personal, it's just business. Now with Titan gone, I can finally go after what I deserve, starting with the TV Title. But Titan's departure leaves me with quite a disadvantage.
(Tobe raises his eyebrow in wonder.) Dillinger: Let's look at it this way, Nephilim has Deke and the Tropical Storm watching his back, and vice versa. The IWX have each other. Lyle Wile has the
Fan-tastics. Hell, even Ben Jackson has the Fomorians and, it looks to me,
Angel on his side. But I am all alone, so all alone, what can I do, what can I do?
Jenkins: Well, you have the Youngbloods. Dillinger: What do you mean by that, Tobe? Jenkins: You saved Lew Spectre. And last week, handing the baseball bats to the Phenomenons. It's obvious isn't it.
Dillinger: I was just doing my duty as a good samaritan. I wish I could have saved the Phenomenons as well but there's only one me and there were like gazillions of those IWX thugs. Just because I idolise them does not mean I'm with them. Everyone knows only the best join Youngbloods, and I'm not worthy of that.
Jenkins: That's not what Nephilim thinks. Dillinger: Well, he can think what he likes, he can even think that he's the
Intercontinental champion if he wants to.
Jenkins: But he is. Dillinger: My point exactly. Jenkins: Huh? Dillinger: Screw you, Tobe. You've been hanging out with them too much. Hell, you even gave the Storm lots of help. If I were you, I'd go into hiding once this interview is over because some people aren't too happy with you.
Jenkins: Good advice. So you're not with the Youngbloods? Dillinger: Oh, I am, every single second of every single hour of every single day. They're the ones who are not with me. And Corporal Punishment, hope you heal fast. Maybe you'd like to go see the Baptist Health Systems people at the Baptist Medical Centre. Remember when Lyle Vile destroyed my knees, they fixed 'em up real quick and real good.
Jenkins: Yeah right. Anyway, you are scheduled to face three men at Quake. Two of them are new around here while the other one, well, you may have met him before.
Dillinger: (Glares at Tobe.) It's plain and simple. It took me this long to finally get a contendership for the TV title. And in comes these two rookies, both who I have never seen wrestle before but I'm not worried about that, come in through the back door and get a shot to be the number one contender. OK, maybe Titan held me back a bit and my knee injury prevented me from competing for such a long time, but man, what have these two done to prove themselves, besides Angel hitting the Masked Retard over the head with a metal bar.
Speaking of which, Angel, you say you fight for good? Then why the Heaven did you help out Ben Jackson. For Christ's sake, he admitted that his veins are filled with darkness, that he is haunted by demons. Oh, I see it now, you're trying to play the exorcist aren't you? (Rolls back his eyes.) Argh... grrr...
I'm Ben Jackson, I've got demons haunting me, you have been dubbed Incoherent. (Stops rolling his eyes.) Then along comes an Angel from some Victoria's
Secrets catalogue. (Changes the tone of his voice to one more gentle.) The
Lord saveth your soul, child. Away, ye demons, I command you in the name of the Lord. (Changes his tone back to normal.) And then when Ben Jackson has stopped raving like a maniac, after they've gone through saying Hallelujah over and over again and they're butt-buddies once more, Jackson slips into a relapse because everyone knows the only demons haunting Jackson is that ego of his. And then we have Jack Leer. The Smutman? What kind of a nickname is that.
I know, you must be planning to take over from the Bumboozler. Well then, the
Lord save us all. And finally, we have Stevie May. Been a long time since we heard from the guy.
Jenkins: He's competing overseas, or is he on holiday, I forgot. Dillinger: You're getting old Tobe, retire now before something bad happens to you. So Stevie is probably out of the country somewhere. If he's competing, it just goes to show how lousy he is. He can't cut it here so he decided to cut his losses and go somewhere else and wrestle until he thinks he's good enough to face me. He's probably in Japan or something because those are the only places where he has fans which goes to show the standard of the fans there.
Jenkins: You really shouldn't be saying things like that. Dillinger: Why? Ueshiba Kancho might flare up and start calling me unpure and stuff like that? Last I heard, he was planning to change the name of the title which the great Lew Spectre so graciously dropped, so that thrash like Ben
Jackson and Daneel McCord can search through... erm... thrash and claim it.
Hell, everyone knows I deserve a shot at that belt too. I faced a former champion, the Cockney Rebel and he had a hard time defeating me. Four Last
Orders and he still had to win via countout. Let me state for the record that throughout my tenure in the GIWF, never once have I been pinned. OK, maybe just now I was, but that was Titan's fault. But all in due time, Tobe. Right now, I'm going for the TV title. Then, it'll be the North
American title, the Intercontinental title and finally, the World title. Call it ambition, call it fat hope, I'd rather call it my rise to superstardom. Now
Tobe, before any of the wrestler's I talked about come out looking for me, I have to get going. One piece of advice Tobe, carry a baseball bat with you, you never know when you're going to need it.
(Joshua steps into the truck, closes the door, starts his engine and drives off leaving one very confused Tobe Jenkins.)
Jenkins: You heard it, folks, Joshua Dillinger has got some very big plans. I don't know if those plans will come through. What I do know is that Joshua
Dillinger sure is crossing many people in this federation. Not a wise choice for the youngster. I'm Tobe Jenkins for GIWF TV and I'm out of here.
(Scene fades to black as we see Tobe giving instructions to the camera crew.)  -- Ridzal -Retired from e-wrestling. Remove 'clothes' to e-mail.
