GIWF Suicide — 21 Nov 1999

21 Nov 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cameras burst into existence, showing the ever-beautiful backstage area of the GIWF arena, where the ‘Hardcore Icon’ Ace Thunder walks past a row of vending machines and water fountains. The cameras follow Thunder for a few moments before swinging around and showing Lew Spectre and Corporal Punishment tip toeing behind the God of Hardcore, whispering to themselves with grins stretching across their entire face. Spectre and the Corporal creep up to just behind Thunder before stopping. Lew Spectre: "Hey, Acey!" Thunder turns around with incredible speed to see where the voice came from, and is met with a vicious clothesline from Corporal Punishment, sending Thunder down to the tile floor fast. Lew Spectre: "Now Ace, why so jumpy? You really need to calm down those nerves, you act like someone was… gasp… trying to make an example out of you!" Ace Thunder lunges at Lew Spectre, who brings his boot up into Thunder’s face, sending him skidding a few short feet across the floor, where Corporal Punishment drags him up to his feet, pinning his arms behind his back. Spectre moves in and begins driving boots into Thunder’s midsection, kicking him ten times before the Corporal drops Thunder to the floor. Lew Spectre: "Come on, Hardcore Icon, where’s those amazing hardcore talents at? Can’t even stand on your own two feet. Pretty sad, Acey." Thunder makes one last charge at Spectre, only to get cut off by a double axehandle over the nape of the neck from Corporal Punishment, putting down Ace one more time. Lew Spectre begins bringing up several kicks into Thunder’s sternum, sending him across the hallway and slamming against a vending machine. Lew Spectre: "Come on, Ace, get up! What’s the matter, the Hardcore Icon lose his footing? Can’t fight like a man and face me? Hahaha… you wanna be a hardcore freak, well, I’ll give you a little bit of ‘hardcore.’" Spectre lands one more kick across Thunder’s face, before the Corporal scoops him up. Corporal Punishment and Lew Spectre both grab a hold of Ace Thunder on the collar of the shirt and the belt loops on his jeans, get a running start, and heave Ace face first through the glass of the vending machine, shattering the glass and busting Thunder wide open. Lew Spectre: "This hardcore stuff always seems so messy. I really hate being degraded to the old prop wrestling. Just look at yourself, Ace, busted open, glass everywhere, and this is how you make a living? Pathetic." Spectre drags Thunder out of the broken glass, kicks him across his busted open face a few times, and then drags him in front of a soda machine. Lew Spectre: "You know, Ace, as much as I hate doing this, well, it’s all in the spirit of the game. You want to be ‘hardcore’? Fine by me… live by the weapon, die by the weapon. The Corporal holds Thunder’s squirming body down as Lew Spectre moves over to the soda machine… and pushes it over onto Ace Thunder’s legs and injured knees, Ace screaming at Spectre in pain all the while, before the YB duo begin walking away from the Hardcore Icon as security finally arrive to help out Ace. Lew Spectre: "Hope you don’t lose consciousness, Ace, we’ve still got a show to shake up, and I’m sure you won’t want to miss it!" Spectre and the Corporal head off towards the office area of the GIWF building as security try to pull the soda machine off of Thunder.
