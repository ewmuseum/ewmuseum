As the music begins to play, the crowd in Madison Square Gardens leap to their feet and begin to raise the room in a commendably enthusiastic display.  The bright overhead lighting zooms from one area of the arena to the next leaving a trail of dancing spotlights in its wake.  As well as the crowd, the deafening explosions of an elaborate display of pyrotechnics reverberates through the stalls. The almost tangible air of anticipation is clouded only by the thick smog of the spent pyro as it drifts from spectator to spectator.   In the thick of it sit the two diminutive and almost overwhelmed figures of the GIWF's senior announcing team, namely Brett Foster and Toby Jenkins. Stood behind their announcers table each of the men are immaculately dressed in their respective GIWF Tuxedo's with the Teal and Black of the GIWF logo standing proud over their breast pockets.  They are both visibly nervous at the magnitude of the evenings proceedings but are both also struggling to hold in the infectious adrenalin rush that passes over the crowd like a tidal wave.
