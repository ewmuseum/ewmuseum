Jay Phoenix can be seen balancing high above Jenkins: " What is he doing up there?" Foster: " He must have been watching the match ... he said that he would be remember!" Jenkins: " ... what the Hell is he doing?!?!?" With an almost graceful poise Phoenix leans forward, arms outstretched on either side of him, as he allows himself to fall from the rafter, and towards the ring, a long thick black cable streaming out behind him, slowing his fall, so that when he reaches the ring he lands with little to no impact at all. He motions out to the shocked pair of Gurgles and Adam Finn, who reach out to him, and as Phoenix holds onto both men tightly, the cable starts to pull him back up towards the rafters ... Gurgles and Finn along for the ride. Jenkins: " If I wasn't seeing that I would not believe it ... Phoenix has just saved Gurgles and Adam Finn .. two men he shares no love for ... from that fire!" Phoenix reaches the rafter again, and as Gurgles and Adam Finn cling on to it, many feet above the floor below, Phoenix allows himself once more to fall to the ring below, and as the flames are starting to reach higher, he grabs the GIWF World title belt before starting his journey back up towards to the roof along the wire.  Hanging about twenty feet above the ring, a few feet above the flames which almost seem to surround him, Phoenix pulls a microphone out of his pocket, and with the title in one hand speaks. Phoenix : " ... I told you that there would be a Guardian Angel for this match ... and now it looks like it is time that the GIWF itself had one!!"
