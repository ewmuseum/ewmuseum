IWWL, a singles-entry e-wrestling league launched as 'IWWF' in March 1996 and renamed eight days later, publishing a five-champion roster across five titles within a week and still cited by other feds as late as October 1996. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This league opened on 21 March 1996 under its founding name, IWWF, promising seven championships across singles and tag divisions and an open-door roster policy: “unlike most federations, [it] will not run out of room for wrestlers.” Matches were to run twice weekly, Saturdays and Wednesdays, in the newsgroup itself, opening with a title tournament on 27 March.

A week later, on 28 March, it renamed itself: “The IWWF has had to change its name to the IWWL. NOTHING ELSE HAS CHANGED IN THIS LEAGUE EXCEPT THE NAME!!!!!!!!!!!!”

By 2 April, under its new name, it reported more than seventy wrestlers and published its first title roster: Big Buster Johnson as World Heavyweight Champion, “The Head Patriot” Desert Storm holding the U.S. Heavyweight Title, War Hawk the Intercontinental Title, Bullywug the Television Title, and Edgar Figaro billed simply as its King, with the tag titles still to be decided.

No closure notice survives from this window. A rival federation's own broadcast recalled a wrestler's past reign as its World Heavyweight Champion, in the past tense, as late as 14 October 1996 -- evidence the name outlasted this surviving record without itself being a closure receipt.
