The fans outside of the 96 Arena in Las Vegas are full of life as they scream their appreciation for the first 96 show in months. Cameras flashing continuously as they wait eagerly for the next arrival. Many expect to see a nice, flashy stretch limo but instead up rolls a rather busted looking taxi cab. But even so, the fans scream at its arrival, seemingly not caring who will get out. A moment later the door flies open and a fresh pair of Timberland boots hit the pavement, followed by the full body of well known Jayson Smith. As usual, there is no smile on his face as he looks out at the crowd. And from the other side of the vehicle emerges none other than Eric Dickey the new GM of 96 wrestling. The crowd goes absolutely berserk.
The look on the face of the Handsome Owner does not seem to be one of adoration or enjoyment. Instead, he looks absolutely frazzled. He quickly speeds around the car, holding his hands up to shield his eyes from the…flashing lights. No signing autographs, no small talk, nothing. Jay darts through the people and into the arena as quickly as he’d exited the vehicle.
Jayson Smith: God, this is ridiculous, E. Is anything gonna go right tonight? Eric Dickey: Ummm…maybe if you weren’t ditchin’ fans like you just did. That’s not cool home slice
Jayson Smith: Look, I’m sorry but its bad enough I’m late like this. I should’ve been here hours ago. I go to my car to arrive for this show and the damn tires are slashed?! I just drove it to your mom’s house not too long before that! They didn’t just bust out of nowhere, Eric. Someone SLASHED them! In the garage of my apartment complex at that.
Eric Dickey: Relax, Jay. Like for real…chill. This is supposed to be a good night for everybody. Did you see all those people out there?! 96 is BACK, baby! So stop frettin’ over some damn tires and enjoy this show like everyone else.
Jayson Smith: Not just one car…THREE! The two I already had and the one Marissa just got me for my birthday! And it’s not just that. You KNOW it’s not just that. Brandon Maddox and that goddamn delayed flight. He’s not gonna be here tonight and my main event is ruined already
Eric Dickey: You got other people who would love a chance to face SBK for the belt. It ain’t ruined ‘cause some newbie couldn’t show up.
Jayson Smith: That’s not the point! And that newbie has potential! It’s always the same. If the night starts off badly then the rest always follows. That’s just the way things go for me. I mean look at the re-launch of WU. I swear if…
Eric suddenly stops in his tracks, grabs Jay by the arm and spins him around. Eirc Dickey: Look, Jay. Shut the fuck up. You put this show together. You’re making this night happen when you have a bunch of people who didn’t have an ounce of faith in you to get this company back off the ground after what happened last time. You already proved all them wrong. Now you just need to let this thing run its course and calm the hell down…understand?
Sighing deeply, Jay nods his head. Jayson Smith: Yeah, I understand. Eric Dickey: Good. Lets get this show on the road, then... The camera pans around the darkened 96 Spectrum as the faithful fans roar with pride. [Single Match] October Doom vs. Carmine Vestieri The match begins with Doom charging at CV, hitting him with big punches and kicks to the gut, but CV simply pokes Doom in the eye, sending him staggering away. Carmine then grabs Doom and sends him into the ropes. When Doom comes charging back, Carmine effortlessly knees him in the stomach, causing Doom to do a full front flip and land on his back in the middle of the ring. Carmine puts one boot on Doom and the ref makes the count.
1. 2.. Doom actually was almost pinned because of that. Carmine gets Doom up and sends him into the ropes again. When he comes back, Carmine levels him with a clothesline, sending Doom down to the mat. He gets Doom up again, and it seems like Carmine has somewhere better to be judging by the look in his eyes. He sends Doom into the ropes but Doom comes running back at him, attempting a cross body! Carmine simply catches him, steps forward, and then falls to his back with a big fallaway slam, sending Doom crashing into the mat.
Carmine gets Carmine up again and whips him into the ropes for a fourth time. This time, CV runs at him and levels him with another clothesline. CV is looking to end this contest as soon as possible so he whips Doom into the corner and follows that up with a corner clothesline! Doom is dazed in the corner so Carmine starts to work him over with rights and lefts, head and body shots. Doom is trying to cover up but Carmine's MMA striking is too much for him. Doom is getting hit with massive shots and looks to be in big trouble until Carmine backs up, granting Doom some time to recover. Or so he thought. Doom immediately charges back into the corner and nails Doom with an elbow to the head. Doom staggers out of the corner, Carmine boots him in the mid-section. Doom is doubled over and Carmine plants him into the mat with a big ddt. Carmine then climbs to the top rope and waits, perched on the top turnbuckle. The crowd boos the lion, as most of them were pulling for Doom
As soon as Doom rises, Carmine comes off the top rope with a big clothesline, turning Doom inside out. CV stands and points at Doom. Doom slowly starts to rise and when he does, CV hits him with a combo of big kicks to the gut. With Doom doubled over, Carmine quickly grounds him with a big snap suplex. Doom is down and could be pinned for the three but Carmine is turning up the heat. He gets Doom up and hits him with a giant t-bone suplex. Doom is sent crashing into the mat and Carmine knows that this match is over. He gets Doom up, who can now barely stand and sticks his head between his legs. CV then lifts Doom high in the air and drops him, planting Doom into the mat with a powerbomb! Doom is laying limp in the center of the ring and the crowd is not pleased. Carmine gets down on his knees beside Doom and mockingly begs him to stand up. As Doom starts to rise, Carmine laughs and waits for him to stand all the way up.
When he does, Carmine strikes and locks in a sleeper hold from behind. He immediately falls backwards, locks in the leg scissors to make it a rear naked choke that he calls the Fade to Black. Doom has no choice to submit. Ref calls for the bell and instructs Carmine to break the hold but he refuses. After a few moments of pleading from the ref, Carmine breaks the hold and lets Doom's limp body lay on the mat in a heap.
Winner: Carmine Vestieri (6:52) The camera opens up backstage and everything seems quiet... too quiet. As the camera man makes his way down the hall, we can see various words spary painted on the wall. The camera looks at each one as we move down the hall.
