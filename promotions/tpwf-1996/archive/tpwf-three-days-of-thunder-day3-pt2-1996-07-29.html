Three Days of Thunder - Day 3, Part 2

29 July 1996

Part 2 of 2 ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- JR: Let's get the thoughts of the Royal Rumble winner of the TPWF's first ever PPV. Austin: No holds barred battle royal. It don't matter what you punks bring into the ring because tryin' to beat Stone Cold will be like tryin' to take down a battle ship with a pea shooter. It just can't be done. See you losers in the ring.
============================================================================== 20 Man No Holds Barred Battle Royal A very interesting event, that promises to be a violent and exciting affair, all 20 men entered the ring, and waited patiently for the announcer to giver everybody's name. The last man to come to the ring was Brian Pillman. Shane
Douglas was helping him as Brian came out on a crutch, claiming he was hurt.
Rey Misterio was a gentlemen and sat on the middle rope for Brian to get into the ring, but instead Brian broke the crutch over him. Everyone was shocked and Shane had a bat and tossed it to Pillman, who was laughing at
Rey.
After the bell rang, all hell broke loose. Owen Hart and Razor Ramon quickly sized up the situation by choosing who they wanted, teamed up by knocking the crowbar out of Andre's hand, and pushing him back into the corner. The crowbar fell (luckily) out of the ring, and both men used kicks, chops, and punches to soften up the Huge Andre. Meanwhile, Arn went straight for Brian
Pillman, who took a swing with his bat, that the enforcer ducked. Rey
Misterio Jr. was accidentally hit, and Arn punched Pillman with some Brass
Knuckles. Pillman dropped the bat, which rolled across the ring. The Giant was tied up, trading blows with Yokozuna in the corner. Mysterio staggereds a bit, still feeling the effects of the bat, when Justin Bradshaw leveled him with a running clothesline. Konnan came to the aid of his fellow Latino by dropkicking Bradshaw, and then smashing him with his martial arts staff.
Sid had The Undertaker on one knee, and was hitting the man from the darkside with a branding Iron, before Terry Gordy hit Sid in the back with his chain. Benoit and Sting were rolling around on the mat, while Rick
Martel sprayed Rick Steiner in the eyes with arrogance. The Giant leveled
Yokozuna with a kick to the stomach, and grabbed Rey Misterio Jr out of no where. The Giant lifted the diminutive masked man by his throat, and tossed him over the Top Rope!! Mysterio is eliminated.
Double J has Rick Steiner held from behind while Rick Martel delivered some stiff chops and punches. Martel went for another blast with ARROGANCE, but
Steiner ducks it and Double J is blinded instead!! All of the sudden, Terry
Gordy nails JJ with a clothesline wrapped in chain!!! JJ stumbles over the top rope and is eliminated!!
The Undertaker and The Giant, are going it it hard, while Andre has escaped the corner after headbutting both Ramon and Owen. Andre and Yokozuna lock up, and are going at it as furiously as they can. Anderson is still dishing out punishment on Pillman, before Chris Benoit pulls the enforcer off, and delivers some chops. Benoit goes for a big punch with the Coal Miner's glove, but Anderson ducks it and counters with an Atomic Drop. Pillman grabs Arn from behind, and Benoit returns to settler the score. Owen Hart waffled Konnan with his Slammy Award, and the Mexican legend is down. Marc
Merro and Steve Austin are in the corner, where Austin swings his bat violently, and Mero just barely manages to duck out of the way. Mero just hit Austin in the face with his whip!! Sting just attacked Benoit, hitting him in the face with brass knuckles. Sting pulled Pillman off of Anderson, and hit Pillman with the knuckles as well. Anderson just DDT's Pillman!!
Sid and Bradshaw are locking horns, as Sid uses the branding iron to jerk the bull rope out of Bradshaws hands. Anderson has Pillman up for an atomic drop, and drops him crotch first across the ring rope!! Pillman is stunned, but tumbles to the floor after Anderson blasts him in the head with the knuckles. Pillman is eliminated!
Benoit, Marc Mero, Konnan, and Ramon are all trying unsuccessfully to lift
Yokozuna over the top rope. Rick Steiner and Terry Gordy are butting heads, and Arn Anderson is on the mat, feeling the effects of the Pillman/Benoit double team. Yokozuna didn't have to hold on long, as Sid came over and unloaded on all the men trying to put him out. Sid delivers a big boot to
Konnan's face, and rick Martel quickly throws him over the top rope! Konnan is eliminated!
Rick Martel took a shot to the face with a bucket by Yokozuna!! Benoit just tossed Martel over. Martel is eliminated. Martel landed on his atomizer, and smashes it!! Martel just emptied all of his ARROGANCE onto himself, and the entire auditorium stinks of it! Martel is jumping up and down, and looks ready to fight. He tries to get back in the ring, but is stomped into an oblivion by Benoit!!
Rick Steiner is choking Razor ramon with a dog leash! Steve Austin just hit
Andre in the leg with his ball bat!! Anderson is up, and is being whipped by Marc Merro! Owen Hart dropped his Slammy Award, and Steve Austin bodyslammed him onto it!! Owen jumps up and looks at his broken trophy, and
Austin seizes the moment by grabbing Own by the seat of his pants and pitching him over the top rope!! Owen Hart is eliminated!!
Razor Ramon ran over and attacked Austin for eliminating a stable-mate. Bradshaw and terry Gordy are using there rope and chain to work over the
Giant. Sid and Sting have Andre, and they Irish Whip him into Gordy,
Bradshaw, and the Giant!! All four men look worse for wear, as Sid levels his partner in crime, Sting, with a big time clothesline! Sid charges out of the corner, and hits Ramon with a clothesline as well. Steve Austin ducks down, and back drops the bad guy out of the ring while he stumbles back! Razor Ramon has been eliminated!!!
Anderson has Benoit on the mat, and is rubbing his face in the broken Slammy
Award...Benoit is bleeding bad!! Rick Steiner and the Giant just teamed up to double bodyslam Andre the Giant!!! The Giant grabs up Steiner and chokeslams him into the mat! Benoit goes for a running heart-punch (with the miner's glove) on Mero who is tied up in the ropes. Mero gets free, pulls down the top rope, and Benoit goes sailing over!!!! Benoit is eliminated!!
The Giant and Andre the Giant locked up to see who the real Giant was. It did't take long for them to hit the ropes which began to sag under their weight. Sensing an opportunity, Arn Anderson, Rick Steiner, Terry Gordy, and Sid all pulled down on the top rope. Andre and The Giant both spill over the top!! Hardly aware, the two men continue to trade blows on the floor, while the action continues in the ring. The Giant and Andre have been eliminated!!
Anderson is on the mat again, and Yokozuna drops a Huge Legdrop on the enforcer. Sting and the Undertaker are going at it in a corner, trading chops and kicks left and right. Sid is blasting Steve Austin with some forearm smashes, but quits when Rick Steiner spins the big man around, and executes a Belly to Belly Suplex!! Bradshaw is being whipped by Mero, while
Gordy attacks Yokozuna who is getting to his feet. Steve Austin just picked up Arn Anderson, and executes the Stun Gun, dropping Anderson throat first across the top rope. Yokozuna irish whipped Gordy, who ended up blasting
Justin Hawk Bradshaw with a forearm wrapped in chain!! Bradshaw is split wide open, and Gordy quickly dumps Bradshaw over the top rope! Bradshaw is quite angry, and trips Gordy in the ring with his bull rope! Ring officials quickly ran to the ring, and escorted the enfuriated Bradshaw back to the locker room.
Sid hit Terry Gordy with the branding Iron, and staggered the big man. Anderson and Austin are still going at it, while Mero and Sting are working over Yokozuna. The Undertaker and Sid are staring down one another, and then lock up in a clash of the Titans!! Austin jabbed Anderson in the ribs with his bat, but Anderson kicked the bat out of Austin Hand. Anderson winds up a big punch, which Austin ducked. Austin is close to the ropes, and quickly picks the Enforcer up for a Belly to Back. Anderson is held high, but levels a brass knuckle punch on the bridge of Austins nose!! Both men fall backward's over the top rope, with Austin bleeding out the nose, and Anderson taking a nasty fall on the floor. Both men are slow to get to their feet, and begin to exchange blows on the floor. Anderson rubs
Austin's face in a puddle of ARROGANCE, before hitting Austin across the back with a steel chair shot. Anderson DDT's Austin onto the chair, leaving him bleeding and unconscious as the Enforcer angrily goes to the locker room.
Gordy has Rick Steiner down on the mat, and is beating him black and blue with a chain!! Yokozuna just splashed both men, before being pulled off by
Sid and Sting. Mero and the Undertaker are in one corner, and the
Undertaker is chopping the Wildman in the throat. Mero falls, and Sting runs over to help him. All of the sudden, Rick Steiner scoops Gordy up, and body slams him over the top rope!! Gordy is eliminated!! Gordy is walking back to the locker rooms, when Bradshaw rushes back through the curtain, and levels Gordy in the aisle with a clothesline. Bradshaw has the bull rope, and chokes Terry with it. Gordy is stunned, and powerless to halt the attack, until ring officials break the two up. Gordy carried out by a handful of attendant, who also help Steve Austin back to the locker room area as well.
Steiner no sooner turned around from throwing out Gordy, when Yokozuna executed a devastating Belly to Belly Suplex on Michigan's very own.
Steiner is out of it, just laying unflinching in the center of the ring. Sting and Mero are teaming up against The Undertaker and Sid, with the bigger men coming out on top. Sid and The Undertaker execute simultaneous big boots, before fighting each other. With no one to help, Steiner was scraped off the mat by Yokozuna, who easily tossed Steiner over the top rope.
Mero and Sting teamed up against Sid, while the Undertaker and yokozuna tangled. The Undertaker blasted Yokozuna back into the ropes with 5 blows of his urn, and finally sent the sumo man over the top rope after a flying clothesline!! Yokozuna was eliminated!!
Sting saw the Undertaker put an end to Yoko, and hit Undertaker from behind with a dropkick, while Merro continued to work over Sid. The Undertaker crumbled into the corner, and Sting quickly sprinted to the opposite corner.
A huge Stinger Splash on the Undertaker!! Sting did two more Stinger Splashes of varying degrees of effectiveness, but in the end they all struck home. The Undertaker wobbled out of the corner, and Sting hit a big drop kick that sent the Undertaker spilling over the top!! The Undertaker was eliminated, but landed on his feet.
Sting quickly turned to see Merro being worked over by the Huge Psycho Sid, but was unable to help. The Undertaker had climbed onto the apron, grabbed
Sting by the head, and dragged Sting over the top rope!! Sting was eliminated, and none to happy about it. The two fought violently on the floor, before The Undertaker grabbed Sting, and tried to do the Tombstone on th floor!! Sting managed to slip behind the Undertaker, and threw the big man into the guardrail. It had no effect as the Undertaker turned around, and violently choke-slammed Sting on the arena floor!! Sting hardly moved as the Undertaker solemnly left the arena, and eventually he had to be helped back by security.
Sid was easily having his way with the much smaller Marc Merro. Merro was the victim of a powerslam, a choke-slam, and a boot to the head. Sid left the man, to climb the ropes and declare to the crowd that "He Ruled the
World!" Sid signaled for the powerbomb, and the fans went crazy. Sid methodically dragged Merro over to the ropes, and it became apparent that he intended to Powerbomb Merro over the top rope and onto the floor!! After show boating a bit longer, Sid set Merro up for the hold. Sid flipped Mero up, but Mero Slid to the side a bit. Sid was stunned, and powerless to stop
Mero, as Marc turned Sid's Powerbomb into a Frankensteiner. Both men went over the top rope, with Mero managing to grab the top rope and hang on!!!
Sid hits the floor and is eliminated!!! Marc Merro wins the battle Royal!!!!!!!!!!!!! The cheering has just begun when Sid pulls Mero off the ropes, and in a fit of rage Powerbomb's the Battle Royal winner on the arena floor, not once but twice. Mero isn't moving when security arrives, and the fans are silent as Sid is escorted out, and Mero who hardly appears to be a winner is stretchered out by an Emergency Medical Team.

The first team to grab the belts and get down from the scaffold wins the match and the belts. There is no Time Limit, and obviously no Disqualification.
Everybody was in awe to see the 40 ft scaffold above the ring. Tim White, the ref for this match, safely went ot the top of the scaffold and hung the fabric bag the held the TPWF Tag Team Titles. The belts hung at about 20 ft above the ring. Neither Team were going ot be able to reach them from the mat so they would have to climb the scaffold. The bell rang, and the match started. There is no normal tag rules so each team went at each others throats. The Undertaker, who was jumped before in his match with Rick Steamboat, went right after Animal with avengence. Picking him up like he weighed nothing and threw him around the ring. Diesel and Hawk went blow for blow, for a while, Until the
Undertaker came over to help Diesel. They both picked Hawk up and drove him down to the mat with a double suplex. Animal, saw this as an opportunity to try to get up the scaffold. Diesel saw him, start to climb up the left side so he followed him up and pulled him down all the way to the floor. Animal hit the concrete hard. Diesel then whipped
Animal into the post. He picked him up and slammed him into the concrete. On the other side of the ring, The Undertaker whipped Hawk into the ropes and gave him a vicious clothesline. He picked him up and choke-slammed him down into the mat. The Undertaker then tried to take advantage of thi, and started to climb the scaffold. Diesel then went over to Hawk and gave him a jack-knife. Hawk was out cold. Animal, though seeing The Undertaker start to climb, and Animal started to climb again. The Undertaker was at the top. Boy, this is the first time ever that he has been scared of something, Heights. He looked down and it seemed so high, he went down on all fours and tried to crawl over to the
Belts. Animal got up to where the Undertaker was. he also crawled over to where the belts were, hoping not to fall off of that 10 foot wide scaffold. They both got to the area where the belts were at the same time. They started trading blows up on the scaffold. Meanwhile, Diesel picked up Hawk and whipped him into the ropes, He went for a clothesline but missed. Hawk came back witha Flying one of his own, taking Big
Daddy Cool, down to the mat. Hawk then climbed to the second rope and landed a big double ax-handle right on top of Diesel's head. Knocking him again to the mat. Hawk then went to climb the scaffold. Diesle got up, albeit a little groggy, and also started to climb up the other side where hawk was climbing. Back up top, The Undertaker then stood up, picked Animal up. Everybody started to scream in danger thinking that he was going to send him all the way down to the mat, but instead slammed him on the scaffold. Just then Hawk reached the top of the scaffold. The Undertaker then went for the belts. Hawk got over to where the Undertaker was and hit him back. Then Hawk went for the belts, but slipped and was hanging on for dear life. At the same time
Diesel went for the bag as did Hawk while he was still hanging there. Hawk was hanging on to the bag with one hand and he had one hand on the scaffold. Animal came out from nowhere and clotheslined both the
UNdertaker and Diesel dropping. Diesel also slipped and was hanging on. Animal then gave Hawk a hand and pulled him up. The Road Warriors have the belts. All they have to do is get down. Al of the sudden, The
Undertaker, hits Hawk from behind and the belts go flying to the middle of the ring. All four guys looked straight down, looked at the belts and then both went to the ladder to climb down. Who would be first to get the belts. Hawk was still lying on the scaffold from that hit from the Undertaker, so he wasn't climbing down.
