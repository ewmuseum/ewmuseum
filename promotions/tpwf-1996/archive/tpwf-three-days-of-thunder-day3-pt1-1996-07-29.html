Champ: Sid Vicious vs Diesel This match was the battle of the Powerbomb. Sid unloaded with a flurry of blows early on! The crowd went nuts. But Diesel blocked after a while and cameback with a series of his own and had Sid reeling. Diesel clotheslines
Sid over the ropes! Sid is up and pulls Diesel outside where they go at it! Diesel uses a chair and dented it over Sid's head. Sid reverses a whip into the ringpost and Diesel hurts his shoulder. Sid rammed Diesel's head into the annoncers table for a wake up call. Back in the ring, Diesel catches Sid napping and executes a powerslam! 1,,2,,Kickout!
This match was all and more than expected! Diesel gives Sid a big boot, but
Sid avoids it and onehand chokeslams Diesel! Sid calls for the Powerbomb, but Diesel backdrops his way out of it. Diesel locks on a chinlock so both get a breather. It finally ends with Sid breaking free of the hold and taking Diesel down with a slam. A legdrop, but Diesel moves! Diesel locks on a bear hug, but Sid gives him a bell-ringer, a kick to the gut and executes the Powerbomb! The cover!

Winner: And Still TPWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMP, SID VICIOUS!!! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- TPWF TV Title Match Champ: Goldust vs. Jushin 'Thunder' Liger The next match was going to be a true battle for the TPWF TV Championship. The two men involved were Goldust, the champion, and Jushin Thunder Liger. Liger charged the ring before he was announced and immediately went after
Goldust. He slid under the bottom rope and then ran at Goldust and nailed him with a spin kick from behind, sending Goldust to the mat. Liger started pounding away at Goldust on the mat. Goldust soon got out of the beating of
Liger with an eyegauge and then wrapped Liger in a sleeperhold. Liger tried to get free from the grip but Goldust hammered Liger in the back and then he kicked out the leg or Liger. He let go of the sleeper and continued working on the leg with kicks, kneebreakers, splashes, and a knee-to-knee slam. He then clamped on and kept reclamping on a spinning-toehold. This was severe punishment to the limb. Goldust got tired of the toehold so, instead of just leaving the hold at a toehold, he went all the way into the figure-four leglock. "Moonsault now you punk!" yelled Goldust. Goldust had a hold of the bottom rope. Liger was pounding on the mat looking like he was trying to stop the match, but he was too proud to submit. Goldust yelled to Liger,
"Beg! You beg me and I'll let you go!" Liger had a few choice words for
Goldust which were far from begging. He was too proud to beg. Liger had been in the hold for four minutes already at this point. During the sixth minute of the hold, Liger went unconscious and was pinned. However, after the referee stopped the match, another official came down to ringside and told the initial official that Goldust was using the ropes. The official saw the replay and disqualified Goldust, giving Liger the victory but not the championship.
Winner: By DQ, Jushin Liger (however the belt doesn't change hands) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Sunday King of the Tag Teams (KotTT) Convoy ---------- Wild Warriors ------------ Steiners ---------- Texas Outlaws -------------------- Harlem Heat KotTT ---------- Frequent Flyers ------------ D&D Express ---------- Taz/Douglas <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
Convoy vs Wild Warriors This pitted UW and Undertaker at the start. Taker applied a choke on UW and pushed him in the corner. Diesel got the tag and Convoy roughed up the
Warrior badly. UW came back and made a tag to Mero, who used some nice moves on Diesel and got a near pin. However UW was unable to help and Mero got clotheslined over the ropes. Diesel distracted the ref as UT tombstoned Mero on the floor, rolled him back in and Diesel covered him for the win!
Winners: Convoy! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Steiners vs Texas Outlaws These two teams have run amuck before and this was an all out battle. The tide turned many times in the match, as each team had near falls. Eventually Ric was caught by a vicious knee from outside interference of Funk. Windham bulldogged Ric and covered him. Scott made the save, but while the ref pushed him back out, Ric was clobbered with the branding iron, that went undetected by the ref. Again a cover and this time was enough was the win!
Winners: Texas Outlaws! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Harlem Heat vs Frequent Flyers Heat attacked before the bell and dominated this match and it was over quick, as they used the Harlem Hangover to polish off Liger and get the win! Quick wins like this help in a tournament like this tonight, keeping you relatively fresh!
Winners: Harlem Heat! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Taz/Douglas vs D&D Express Taz and Douglas worked on the back of Gordy, using backbreakers and various suplexes to soften him up. Gordy toughed it out and eventually tagged
Dr Death who was dying to get in. And he took his chance and was unstoppable, as he cleaned house and used a piledriver on Douglas to get the win to advance!
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Round 2 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Convoy vs Texas Outlaws This match was a brutal affair, seeing Funk getting busted open via a headsmash into the ringsteps, courtesy of UT. This match was outside for the most part, seeing the rules broken frequently and each team using illeagal tactics to gain an advantage. Windham and Funk worked on the UT later on and Funk DDTed him. Funk turned to jeer the crowd, as the UT did a famous situp. He spun Funk around and chokeslammed him! UT tagged Diesel who swatted Windham off the ring apron and Jacknifed the dazed Funk, the scored the pin!
Winners: Convoy! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ D&D Express vs Harlem Heat Each team had their moments in this bout, with each team nearly winning it. The ref was knocked out for a good part of the bout and Gordy had Ray pinned for at least a 5 count, but the ref was too groggy to make a count. Sunny used this to her advantage and showed off her sex appeal on the ring apron.
Gordy enjoyed the view, but was rolled up from behind. This time the ref was aware of the situation and made the count, despite Ray had a hand full of tights!
Winners: Harlem Heat! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Sunday King of the Tag Teams (KotTT) Convoy Convoy ---------- Wild Warriors Convoy ------------ Steiners TO ---------- Texas Outlaws -------------------- Harlem Heat Heat KotTT ---------- Frequent Flyers Heat ------------ D&D Express D&D ---------- Taz/Douglas ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ TPWF KotTT Finals ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Winner get to challenge Roadwarriors in a scaffold match! Convoy vs Harlem Heat These two teams met on Day 2, outside of the tourney with the Heat taking it. Could they do it again? This was for a shot at the champs and Diesel was in control. But Heat is a more solid team in the teamwork department and made frequent tags. Diesel sidewalk slammed T and tagged UT, who came in, only to be double-teamed, despite the ref warning the Heat to put a man on the outside. UT showed signs of a comeback, but fell victim of eye gouges and face rakes. Heat executed the Harlem Hangover and made a pin. However, UT kicked out and Heat were stunned. They couldn't put him away. T tried a sunset flip on the UT, who turned the tide and ended grabbing T by the throat and chokeslammed him. Diesel got the tag and gave a big boot to Ray who came charging in. Then Diesel Jacknifed T and UT gave Ray a flying clothesline to boot, as Diesel made the pin and the crowd was on their feet with the suspense of the match!
Winners: And King of the Tag Teams: Convoy! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Arn Anderson vs. Barry Windham in an I QUIT Cage Match Both men looked determined to win, with not just the match, but their pride on the line. Neither men were showing the effects of their last match, thankfully. Both men locked up, with Arn countering a collar/elbow tie up with a high knee to the midsection. Arn went to ram Windham's head in the cage, but Barry blocked with a foot to the cage, a punch to Arn's midsection, and then bounced Arn's head off the cage!! Arn staggered back, and Windham leveled the Enforcer with a Huge Lariatt!!! Windham went to pick Arn up, but Arn countered with a greco-roman punch to the groin. The fans booed loudly at Arn's underhanded tactics, but he simply pushed Barry into the corner, and unleashed with a half-dozen shoulder blocks to the midsection. Windham gouged Anderson's eyes, and then smashed Anderson's head into the cage for the second tim tonight. Windham irish whipped Arn, who reversed the hold. Windham came off the ropes, and Anderson executes a beautiful Spinebuster!! Arn is signaling for the DDT!! Arn sets up the front face lock, but Windham countered by picking the Enforcer up!! Windham puts Anderson on the top rope, and climbs to the second!! Windham is going for the Superplex. Windham lifts the Enforcer high, and drops him hard.
Anderson bounces, and then lies prone!! Windham goes for the cover, but the ref refuses to count!! Windham realizes that he must make Arn say I quit.
By this time, Arn has recovered, and after Windham is finished arguing with the ref, he falls victim to the DDT courtesy of Anderson. Anderson covers, but is likewise denied. Windham stumbles to his feet, and dropkicks
Anderson from behind. Anderson hit the cage with a sickening thud, and is split wide open!! Windham quickly applies the Iron Claw, and within moments, Anderson is screaming for the microphone!! The announcer hands the mike to the ref through the cage, and Arn Anderson says I QUIT to the whole viewing audience. Your winner is Barry Windham!!!
Winner: Barry Windham! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ************************************************************ ***IRONMAN MATCH*** Rickey "The Dragon" Steamboat vs. The Undertaker After Steamboat was introduced to the huge pop of the fans, the lights went completely dark for the entrance of the Undertaker. As his music started, smoke came from the dressing room entrance. But there was no
Undertaker. When the lights came on, The Undertaker was in the ring. The bell sounded, he hit Steamboat in the stomach, Then he choked-slamed him down to the mat. got the cover, 1, 2, 3! First fall to the
Undertaker. The match went on with the Undertaker completely dominating. Steamboast did find a weak spot in the Undertaker, his legs. Steamboat went to work on them, using various submission manuevers. He had a boston crab on the Undertaker for about 45 seconds, then the Undertaker got to the ropes. Steamboat released the hold and went flying to the top rope to maybe catch the Undertaker with a cross body block. The Undertaker caught Steamboat in mid air and turned it into the Tombstone. a cover and a pin, and the Undertaker was up 2-0.
The bell rang for the next fall to start and all of the sudden, Hawk and
Animal flew down and attacked the Undertaker. They drug him outside, beat him down until he was hurting real bad. Animal then put him on his shoulders and Hawk got up on the apron and delivered a Dooms Day Device on the UNdertaker. Diesel then flew down to the ring to help his partner but was to no avail. The Road Warriors did their damage and left. The ref called for the bell and gave a DQ win to the UNdertaker.
This made the Undertaker very mad and he took it out on the Dragon. He picked Rickey up and powerslammed him to the mat. Rickey looked like a rag doll the way that The Undertaker was manhandling him. Undertaker did another chokeslam adn got another pin to go up 4-0. By this time,
Rickey was hurt. The undertaker threw him outside the ring. and then he went out there and pounded him into the post and the guard rail. THe undertaker entered the ring. The ref counted to 10 and Rickey was counted out. 5-0 in the UNdertakers favor. Rickey was hurt very bad and it showed. The ref called for the bell and Rickey was not even in the ring yet. He counted again to 10 and gave another fall to the
UNdertaker. Steamboats whole stable just flew down to ringside and threw in the towel. Giving the UNdertaker a 7-0 win in this Ironman match.
WINNER: The Undertaker, 7 - 0!!! ********************************************************************** TPWF INTERNET TAG TITLE MATCH!!! Internet Tag Champs Public Enemy vs Menace 2 Society Johnny Grunge and Ahmed were in the ring first, and Grunge actually took it to Ahmed with a roundhouse right. But then he got cocky, and started to wave his hands in the air, forgetting about his opponent. Ahmed made him pay by hammering on him with a bodyslam, an elbowsmash, and a bulldog headlock. PE double teamed him as usual, then Rock attempts the Drive By.
Ahmed rolls away and tags out to Yokozuna. Yoko and Ahmed dominate PE with power moves, Yoko hitting Rock with a waistlock suplex, and Ahmed with a flying shoulderblock. Rock avoided Yoko entirely by tagging out, leaving
Grunge to face the behemoth. Grunge thumbed Yoko in the eyes, then executed a ropeburn. Later in the match, Grunge had Ahmed on a table outside the ring, and tried to drive him through it with a flying legdrop. Ahmed moved, but the table didn't break. Yoko had Rock in the ring, and threw him over the top rope, onto Grunge and the table. A loud CRACK was heard, and the table broke in half. All that was left was for Ahmed to throw Rock back in the ring, and Yoko to Bonzai Drop him. 1,2,3, and Menace 2 Society were champions again.
WINNERS: NEW INTERNET TAG CHAMPS, MENACE 2 SOCIETY ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ End of Part 1 of 2
