Three Pistols Wrestling Fed (TPWF)

29 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The TPWF is open to new handlers. We are a fed based on the wrestlers from the WCW/WWF circuits from the 1980's to the present. The fed has been running since April and this is run by an experienced Fedhead. We have a homepage that is down and moving to a new location.
We have a few wrestlers left. TPWF is looking for active roleplayer type players who actively do interviews and flashes. The TPWF uses WLS 5.3 and
TNM to determine the outcomes. Basically you submit the challenges thru e-mail to other wrestlers. When confirmed, the matches are booked for the soonest possible card.
TPWF is not a fed which is caught up in claiming we are the best or should we be in the Hall of Fame. We really don't care about stuff like that. We just put out solid wrestling. TPWF is always on time and is going to stick for a long time. We have 20 handlers currently and each has 4 wrestlers in their stable. We have 2 tag belts and 4 singles belt and weekly cards. We are known best for great handlers and their interviews and participation. The wrestling is solid and realistic, plus with our own angles and fueds.
Do you think you would want to join? TPWF rarely shows up here. If you are interested, drop a note to TPWF Prez [name withheld] at this address:
[detail withheld] We just had the Mega PPV event. It was TPWF Three Days of Thunder. It was a PPV that was huge and spanned over three whole days! The report is posted on RSPWF and you can read it to see what we are all about and the style of the Fed! Looking forward to hearing from you! See you in the ring!
