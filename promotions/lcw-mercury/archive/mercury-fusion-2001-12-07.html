The inside of the Civic Center is seen, as the thousands of fan erupt, and a fireworks display engulfs the entrance area, and golden sparks shoot from the word FUSION, hanging above. As the smoke from the pyro clear, the Fusion entrance stage becomes visible. From the top of the building the word "Fusion" hangs, spelt out in metal girders. A circular metal girder with swirling lights dangling from it hangs below this Fusion, and two fair sized video screens, called the MercuTrons, hang on opposite sides of the Fusion. Below Fusion is the entrance area, which consists of two large metal girders that lead from the top of the ramp up to the U and O in Fusion. For their entrances, the wrestlers will walk out between the girders, and in front of the Mercury Fusion logo, which is built into the stage, and in between the large steel entrance girders. The elevated ramp way, which is covered in dull gray carpet, leads us right to the ring! As the smoke from the pyro goes away, the camera scans the crowd, and the signs come out! We happen to catch some of the better ones, which range from "Tim Hall Is Better Than Me" to "$ellout" and finally "Stay out of the bathroom...I just took a Blaze". We then cut to the announce desk where KC Steele, clad in a black "Fusion" polo shirt, sits next to John Hughes, wearing the new Barabbas LTD shirt. KC Steele: Emotions are running high as we steam barrel to Silent Nightmare. But tonight, we have one hell of a show lined up for you fans! "Iron" John Hughes: That's right, because we're Mercury, we've been around longer, and we've had more shows! So..yeah! Steele: Anyways, tonight, Gravity looks to get his third world title as he faces Harbinger. Suddenly, Metallica's "Struggle Within" heralds the arrival of Dave Beaumont and Harbinger! The World champion strolls to the ring with the belt firmly around his waist, and Dave smiles and chats with Herb all the way to the ring. As Dave gets in the ring, he asks for a mic, and receives. Dave Beaumont: Well, to start off this evening, I have some bad and good news. Well, it's pretty much all good news. Dave: And now, for my second order of business.... Suddenly, "Rumble Fish" by Sevendust blares through the arena and out walks Keith Summers to a nice reception, and mic in hand. The crowd boos, and Harbinger applauds. But Keith smiles. Keith: I'm cool with that. Very cool with that. But I don't know if I feel like waiting for Nightmare. You better beef up Security, Davey. See you later. Keith spikes the mic and smiles as "Rumble Fish" plays him to the back. Steele: Well next week is do or die for Keith Summers, but tonight, right now, we've got our opening match to kick off Fusion! Match 1, Tag Match--Entropy Crusade vs Blaze McCoy and The Dominator Victor Williams "Raining Blood" by Slayer heralds the introduction of Victor "The Dominator" Williams who appears at the top of the ramp, ready for battle. A traditional video package plays on the MercuTron as The Dominator stomps down to the ring, a determined look on his face. Once Williams is in the ring, he looks around, and then raises his arms up before retreating to his corner. The arena goes dark and the letters OutKast are shown one by one on the screen. Blaze McCoy is shown close up in slow motion from the soles of his boots to the spiked blonde hair on his head. Golden pyro explodes at the center of the ramp and "Outkast" by POD begins to blast throughout the arena. Blaze McCoy steps out from behind the curtain with his wife, the lovely Cherry Stacked. They both ignore the crowd’s boos as they head to the ring. Blaze lifts Cherry over the top rope then leaps over the ropes and heads for the nearest turnbuckle, steps up to the top rope and stares at the crowd as he slowly takes off his sunglasses and gives the "thumbs up" with a smile, and then the "thumbs down" with a frown and another blast of flames are shot out of the other three ring posts. Blaze hops down off the turnbuckle and hands Cherry his glasses, gets a kiss on the cheek and is ready for battle. Hughes: I’m not feeling to hot Steele..I think I need to take a Blaze. AH--HAHAHA! Entropy Crusade enters to "Angels Holocaust" after the words "I WILL KILL HIM!" echo throughout the arena. Finally, the letters "E" and "C" in cryptic lettering shoot out of the background, and the video shatters and crumbles into nothingness. Match 2, Singles Match--"Devestating" Geoffrey Slate vs Memosarks to determine the #1 contender to the North American Championship From the stage a large blast of white pyro shoots just above the heads of the fans. Disturbed's "Violence Fetish" heralds the entrance of Geoffrey Slate; on a pitch-black MercuTron appears the word "Devastation", which quickly fades into a standard entrance video featuring a splicing of stills and clips which is made special primarily by the ring of fire encircling it. Slate walks coolly and calmly to the ring, barely recognizing the fans on the way. Steele: Geoffrey Slate is coming off a rough loss to Harbinger in last Fusion's Mercury Title match. Hughes: Fortunately for him, he's facing someone who happens to be anything but rough. Steele: I'm not so sure of that. Confetti starts to fall from the rafters over the crowd and big top music fills the arena, prompting Memosarks to appear in the entranceway, a big grin on his face. He slowly starts to walk to the ring, smiling at all the cameras as he goes. Steele: The former North American champion is looking to get a shot at the title at Nightmare, and he's vert intent about winning this match. Hughes: I bet he's more intent about making balloon animals. Memo stalks forward and immediately scoops Slate up for a slam. Slate blocks and turns it into a small package, but Memo blocks that and turns it into a small package of his own, one, Slate kicks out and both men move to their feet rapidly. They tie up, with Memo powering through Slate's arm to grab a headlock and plant him with a DDT! Not content with that, Memosarks stands up while still holding on and delivers a second, harder DDT! Still not content, Memo stands up one last time and whips himself back and down, giving Slate a DDT that causes Slate to remain vertical on the mat for several sickening seconds before crumpling into a heap! Memo rolls him over and hooks the far leg, one, two, Slate gets a shoulder up! Steele: Memosarks really dropped him on his head there. A little more aggression just might be what he needs. Hughes: A little ? He needs to get rabies. That'll do him some real good. Steele: You're kidding, right? Silence. Memosarks positions Slate 'just so' in the center of the ring and runs to the ropes, then jumps and springboards back with an asai moonsault. Slate rolls out of the way, so Memo flips all the way out to his feet, and is able to surprise Slate with a spinning heel kick! Memosarks runs to the ropes and jumps into another asai moonsault, and this time Slate doesn't move. No, he just lifts his knees. No worry, Memo sees this coming and again lands on his feet! Slate has an "uh-oh" moment as Memo stands over his prone body, segued into an "ow" moment when Memo drops a knee across his face! Memosarks runs to the ropes one last time and jumps into an asai moonsault, connecting cleanly for one, two, Slate kicks out! Memosarks snarls and pulls Slate up for an Irish whip. Memo then runs and jumps into a hurricanrana, but Slate counters with a sit-out powerbomb! After a moment's rest, Slate gets up and pulls Memo with him. Slate cinches Memo up in a full nelson, Memo bends himself forward and slowly walks to the ropes for a break. Slate tries to pound on his back to get him to stop resisting, Memo pops him in the mouth with a back elbow to retaliate. Before he can follow it up, however, Memosarks' own music comes on the PA. From the entrance (which he happens to be facing) comes Dirge, on the world's eleventh smallest tricycle. He's wearing a huge rainbow afro, suspenders, and a tie-dye shirt. Memo's eyes go wide from the shock of this, while Slate does everything he can to block out the image from his mind while reapplying the full nelson in order to deliver a dragon suplex with a bridge, one, two, three! Winner by Pinfall, and #1 Contender to the North American Title: Geoffrey Slate Steele: Dirge deciding to play mind games with Memosarks, but I can only wonder why. Hughes: Maybe he, like most rational people, hates clowns. Steele: Fans, I hate to interrupt the broadcast again, but I understand there is a backstage disturbance! We quickly cut to the back where we see a cameraman running to the scene, which is Keith Summers smashing a bat against the dressing room door of Harbinger! Keith kicks away but not before we get an internal shot, revealing Harbinger and Dave hiding behind the leather couch! Harbinger: Dave, you have to stop this! I’ve got a big match tonight! Dave: I already called security! A few more thuds are heard before we cut to the outside, and a swarm of men in black Mercury T-shirts refrain Keith from hitting the door anymore. As the brothers reach ringside, their video ends with a clip of Mikel powerbombing Josh through an opponent and a table. Steele: The Trents are still the dominant team in Mercury. Gravity turns around, Harbinger buries a foot in his belly, then nails the Prelude ace crusher/ forward legsweep combo to send Gravity to his knees! With Gravity three-quarters of the way from the ropes in front of him, Harbinger has no choice but to put him in the Dreadlock dragon sleeper and push for all he's worth until Gravity taps out! Winner by Submission: Harbinger Steele: Big win for Harbinger, and give another assist to David Beaumont for causing Gravity to drop his guard, allowing Harbinger to get the Prelude and then the Dreadlock for the win. Hughes: I don't know what to say. I mean, Gravity's a great, violent guy and all, but he did lose fair and square within the rules established twenty seconds before the match. Steele: ... Harbinger goes outside to check on Beaumont, who is still plenty woozy from Gravity's lariat. The crowd pops, but not for Dave standing up. No, it's for Keith Summers, who runs out through the crowd, broken handcuffs dangling from his hand and security chasing him. After leaping over the guard rail he snatches the Mercury title and slides in the ring! Before any reaction can be made by the two comrades, Keith charges Beaumont and Harbinger, aiming a shot at the owner's head! Dave ducks and runs, and for the second week in a row, Keith Summers leaves Harbinger out cold! Steele: Summers, who again got set up in someone else's specialty match by Beaumont, isn't going to let bygones be bygones! Hughes: This won't go unpunished, no-siree. Beaumont watches on in horror from behind the announce desk as Keith tosses the belt in Harbinger face and sprints up the ramp with security hot on his trail. The camera closes in on Beaumont tending to Harbinger, surrounded by beer cups and popcorn boxes as "This broadcast is Copyright © 2001, Mercury Entertainment, Inc., and may not be rebroadcast or reproduced without express written consent of LCWE" appears at the bottom of the screen, and we fade to black.
