Regulations

April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition LCW Regulations Joining LCW: 1) Lethal Championship Wrestling is a fantasy e-wrestling federation, which means that you must apply only with a wrestler that you yourself have created. Any applications for real wrestlers such as the Rock, Stone Cold Steve Austin, Booker T, or Big Poppa Pump will be summarily deleted. 2) You may have up to three wrestlers in any combination, which means that you may have up to three singles wrestlers or one singles wrestler and a tag team. One thing to take into consideration before you apply for three characters is roleplay quality. From experience, we know that each additional character that you add to your own personal stable will stretch your roleplaying resources thinner, which may in turn hurt your roleplay quality. 3) When it comes to forming a tag team or a stable, you should clear it with the booking committee before going through with any plans. By that same token, a stable should in its initial form contain at least three wrestlers and a tag team must contain at least two. On the other hand, a stable may not contain any more than one-quarter the total number of active wrestlers on the LCW roster, so that no one can "take over" LCW by virtue of sheer numbers. A tag team may have no more than four wrestlers, and no more than three may wrestle in any regulation tag team match. 4) When you sign up and fill out your application, you will automatically agree to the rules and regulations set forth both here and in the Member Agreement page that precedes the actual application forms. We review our applications weekly, usually every Saturday. The voting process on applications takes no less than 48 hours, so expect to know whether or not you're accepted to LCW on the Monday following the date in which you entered your application. 5) From the time you apply in LCW until you receive a formal acceptance or rejection letter, you may post only on our fan board, not on our Roleplay, Out-Of-Character, or Complaint Boards, which are for members only. If you would happen to post on any of the non-Fan Boards, there is a 95% likelihood that you will be denied application to LCW, regardless of how well-done your application is. 6) Upon receiving your acceptance letter (should you be accepted), you will begin your two-week trial period. If at any point within those two weeks, either party (you or LCW) decide that the relationship is not mutually beneficial, the business relationship can be terminated by either party without further circumstances or repercussions. Should you join LCW and then simply quit roleplaying at some point after the two-week trial period, you will be removed from the fed in an on-air manner that will preclude your re-entrance into this fed. Roleplaying: 1) Lethal Championship Wrestling is a pre-dominantly roleplay-based federation, meaning that 90% of our matches are determined by the better roleplayer, and only 10% are based on preconceived angles. What this means is that you may always have the best roleplays (as judged by us - see next rule), but that you may not win every match. You will win a majority of your matches, however, and you will also be placed into better storylines and more important belt divisions should you maintain high-quality work. 2) Roleplays are judged by a panel of six bookers to ensure fairness, so if you lose a match that you should have won, please don't complain on the OOC Board or the Complaint Board. Instead, you should send an email with the nature of your complaint to the fed mail, and the booking committee will do our best to send a rapid response back to you. 3) Roleplays are judged based on two criteria: quality of content and quantity of roleplays. We put more focus on quality and would rather see two excellent 20-line roleplays a week than four horrible 2000-line ones. What we look for in terms of quality: a roleplay must be well-written, entertaining, and in line with what your character would say or do (in other words, consistent character development is a must ). 4) We expect a minimum of two roleplays per week, because it's extremely difficult to evaluate talent and create storylines when we have nothing to work with in terms of roleplays. If you fail to meet that minimum and do not notify us ahead of time, 99% of the time, you will be fired from LCW. (Exceptions will be made in the case of extreme circumstances). The roleplay deadline for Tempest is now Monday night at midnight, eastern standard time, and for PPV's, the deadline is the Friday before the PPV, also at midnight, est. 5) In the situation of an attack within a roleplay (either your wrestler attacking another LCW member or your wrestler being attacked by another LCW member), you must clear the attack with an attack form from the Members section of the LCW site. Upon approval (which will be granted within 24 hours of receiving the request), you may carry out the attack in your roleplay with a note at the end that you did receive approval from the Booking Committee to carry out the attack. 6) In the situation of issuing a challenge for a match in a roleplay, we also ask that you submit an attack form and receive approval before immediately assuming that the match will take place. Although we do try to take all of these match requests into consideration, it is impossible to fit them into a show two days after the challenge has been made. In general, we prepare our cards anywhere from 4-8 days before the show, and although they can be changed slightly, don't expect to request a match every week and receive it. Also, do not expect to receive a title shot just because you've requested it. 7) Concerning profanity in roleplays, we look at it as being juvenile and unprofessional if it goes beyond the three words that you would hear on broadcast television ("ass", "damn", and "bitch"). Though the roleplay board will automatically censor the vast majority of profanity in roleplays, we ask that you act responsibly and censor the rest on your own. 8) Should you fail to roleplay once within a seven-day period, you will be emailed and given a warning. Should three more days lapse without you roleplaying, you will be automatically fired unless you notify the booking committee of a problem or circumstance that prevents you from doing so. The LCW regulations, like those of any business or service-based industry, can be bent, modified, and adjusted in light of extenuating circumstances. For the most part, these rules are final; as mentioned above, we reserve the right to terminate you from LCW at any time should these rules be consistently violated.
