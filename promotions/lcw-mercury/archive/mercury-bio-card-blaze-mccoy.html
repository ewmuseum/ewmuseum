Blood type: B Eye color: Blue Team: none Manager: none Trainer: Jacob Paxton Wrestler Style Wrestling style: All Around Signature moves: POWERSLAM CLOTHESLINE SPINEBUSTER BRAINBUSTER FLYING BODY PRESS FLYING DROP KICK DDT ROLL THE DICE BRAINBUSTER SCORPION DEATH DROP FISHERMAN'S SUPLEX SIX PACK SLAM (PRESS INTO BELLY SLAM) BAIT AND TACKLE (SPEAR) BLAZE OF GLORY (HEADLOCK DROP) CORPORATE LADDER (MAT TARANTUALA) FOOD STAMP (GARVIN STOMP) CHOKEBOMB (CHOKESLAM POWERBOMB) HOOK, LINE, SINKER (PUMPHANDLE SLAM FROM SECOND ROPE) Finisher: SLINGSHOT SUPLEX OFF ROPES INTO A JACKHAMMER SLAM Finisher name: OUTKAST Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: MACW, EHW, LCW, GXWA, XWF, PSW, FCW Other/Previous teams: The Boyz, SCC, OutKasts Other/Previous managers: Cherry Stacked Other Wrestling titles won: MACW Tag Titles, LCW Hardcore Titles, LCW Tag Titles (2), GXWA Tag Title, GXWA Hardcore Title, GXWA World Title, XWF World Title, XFW Hardcore Title, PSW World Title, PSW Tag Titles, PSW North American Title Biography Blaise McCoy was born and raised in the "sticks" of Southwestern Pennsylvania. His family was not very wealthy and Blaise was sometimes labeled an "Outkast". He was an avid fisher and hunted ever since he was a child. He was involved in many sports in high school. He was the star receiver in football, played shooting guard on the basketball team and was on the wrestling team as well. Among his teammates on the football team in high school were his best friend, the african american boxing star Hype Norris the star runnning back, the monster mountainboy Schmuu who was basically the offensive line by himself, and Jacob Paxton the richboy who was the quarterback. Cherry was a cheerleader of course. Blaise went on to be all-conference and played for the University of Pittsburgh before playing three years for the Pittsburgh Steelers. Schmuu, Hype, Paxton, Cherry and Blaise have been friends since high school and have always been "Boyz". After pro football, Blaise used his Business degree to start up his own Business known as the Steel City Committee. This Business was in the Business of taking over Businesses and making them the SCC's Business. The highly successful Fortune 500 company has it's headquarters in downtown Pittsburgh. Blaise was influenced by high school all-american wrestler Paxton to try Pro Wrestling. After a few years in indy feds and doing it for fun, Blaise joined the MACW. Blaise's wrestling style is a perfect mix of hardcore, technical, power and highflying making him a very well rounded performer. Soon after, Hype and Schmuu also became wrestlers and the "Boyz" were reunited again. All of them then gained executive positions in the SCC Business and other members were recruited as well. The SCC is about taking care of Business in and out of the ring. Blaise took his act to the GXWA, which later became known as the XFW and won the Hardcore title twice and was the last one to hold both the GXWA and XFW World titles. Blaise pulled double duty as he left the SCC back in Pittsburgh and began to fight in both XFW and PSW. After the XFW closed down, he signed a contract with PSW and changed his name to BLAZE in favor of a more hard-edged and outrageous character. Blaze’s alter ego took over and he later teamed up with the man who tempted Blaze into coming over to PSW for a fight. This man, or woman, was the infamous Fazel. The two had many differences but one thing in common. They were both OutKasts. After teaming up with in weeks they had won the coveted PSW World Tag titles. After trying to get others to join the “OutKasts” and being ignored by the top carders in the fed, the OutKasts have vowed to fight together for respect without any help at all and prove to the world that THEY are the BEST DAMN TEAM IN THE BUSINESS once again. Blaze’s crazy side took over and the Lunatic Hick was born. The split personality hick took PSW by storm and with the OutKasts, Blaze finally completed his mission in PSW and defeated each member of the Elite and won the PSW World Championship off the legend Aimz. Blaze left PSW at the top of his game and has joined FCW at his peek along with his cousin Luke “Puke McCoy” at his side. He was then asked by Dave Beaumont to return to LCW in the Mercury Division.
