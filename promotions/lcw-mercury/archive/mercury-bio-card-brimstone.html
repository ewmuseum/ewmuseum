LCW Mercury LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Brimstone Real Name: N/A Nickname: "The Minister of Entropy , Bone Daddy" Born: Unknown Place of Birth: Hell, Michigan Hometown: Height: 6' 11 Weight: 295 lbs Blood type: Type O Negative Eye color: Obsidian Black Team: The Entropy Crusade with Dirge Manager: None Trainer: None Theme song: Angels Holocaust by Iced Earth Wrestler Style Wrestling style: All around/Brawler Signature moves: Clothslines, Assorted powerbombs, suplex's, and other painful things. He also does any painful thing he can come up with and get away with or not get away with. The "Whos your momma Ankle Lock" Tm Kenzer and CO, and the "bankhandwhich". Finisher: A Ball and Chain Powerbomb, inverted into the mat, or turnbuckle or other painful things,. Finisher name: The Mourning Star Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: TEWO, SMWL, EIWF, EWF Other/Previous teams: EEI and the Entropy Crusade Other/Previous managers: Dirge Other Wrestling titles won: World Champion Tag Team Champion Other Wrestling awards won: Stable Champion Biography Brimstone started his life of vileness early. Being named Flagg Brimstone isn’t the easiest way to grow up. Brimstone met another young man who claimed to be from another planet, whom shared his thoughts on life. They grew up together, doing very shady business deals with sketchy associates. Discussing life and came up with their philosophy of Entropy. Legally changing his name to Brimstone at the age of 18 along with his friend Dirge. They became ordained Ministers of Absolute Zero the church of Entropy, . They entered pit-fighting tournaments in the Middle East and Asia. This was barley sated their thirst for blood, or their thirst for cash. After being spotted by Rick Thorensen he invited them into his league Smash Mouth Wrestling League . Dirge debuted followed By Brimstone. They were hated but respected by most. Though Dirge was nuts and dangerous, Brimstone was methodical and calculating. They started taking apart opponents and gathering a following. After fighting a rival Stable the Yakuza for the Stable Titles, during one match against each other and another stable they joined forces and obliterated the lone face stable. They formed EEI . EEI was not only a stable, but a corporation. They built a huge foundation of business in and out of wrestling that is still in business today. Brimstone moved from different wrestling leagues. Mainly TEWO where a formed the Entropy Crusade again, this time cause the Main Face to turn on the other Main Face Rush. During a tag match against the Crusade, Rush was beaten with in an inch of his life when the turn happened. Rush unfortunately was the only man who tried to stop this Crusade, and did so and his own cost of blood. Making sure he learned, Brimstone had his goons again and again Beat Rush down. Ever resilient the man kept coming back. Brimstone left wrestling in early 99’, due to various reasons. Mainly due to Dirge becoming a true child psychologist because of amnesia.. Brimstone had to find him and return him to his normal state . Their Return marks a amazing feat considering the wealth these two have acquired. They are returning to their roots. LCW is a perfect place to start.
