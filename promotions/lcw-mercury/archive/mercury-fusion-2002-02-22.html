LCW Mercury Card Archive

Fusion, 22 February 2002

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mercury Fusion-2.2.22-Live from the Skyreach Arena in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada A dim light flickers in a dark room, swinging back and forth as a hand fiddles with the bulb. After giving it a few screws, the light suddenly shines forth with great luminescence as "It Ain’t Like That" by Alice In Chains plays in the background. The hand comes into view again, tinkering with the bulb before it explodes in his hand, morphing the shot into a video package for Mercury Fusion! Harbinger celebrates with his world title (There I was, laid out on the table) Geoffrey Slate tosses Magnum Wolf off a cage at Silent Nightmare (Screamin’ Sweat and bare feet to the floor) Pariah lets out a scream with a crimson mask on (In my life, I’d not soften) The Trents raise the tag titles high above their heads (Things that cut, and burn so often) Magnum Wolf pummels on Rush (But I sit, think of somethin’) TR Saxton tosses Gravity around (Scared to face, the dyin’ nothin’) Gol superkicks Pariah at Silent Nightmare (See the cycle I’ve waited for) And EEI poses as a group in the ring (It ain’t like that anymore) Blaze McCoy punishes Memosarks with a ladder (Where I go is when I feel I’m able) Glamour takes it to Mikel Trent (How I fight is why I’m feelin’ sore) Memosarks frolics about ringside with a wicked grin on his face (In my mind, not forgotten) Pariah spits out a tooth at Nightmare (Feel as though a tooth were rotten) Dave Beaumont sits on Edward Olmstead (Behind the smile a tongue that’s slippin) Picasso works over Josh Trent (Buzzards cry, when flesh is rippin’) Harbinger dreadlocks Blaze McCoy into submission (See the cycle I’ve waited for) Magnum Wolf tosses Slate into the cage (It ain’t like that anymore) TR Saxton walks around, tangled in barbed wire (Here I sit writing on the paper) Pariah Nausea’s Apex (Trying to make the words you can’t ignore) Blaze McCoy delivers the Hook, Line, and Sinker! ( In my eyes, what I’m lacking) Dirge and Brimstone create havoc in a promo (Score at face, a ten for slacking) Gol trash talks in a promo (Sign the deal, set in motion) Slate hugs the North American Title (Smaller fish, so huge the ocean) Pariah kicks and curses at Security Guards (See the cycle I’ve waited for) The Trents deliver some brotherly love (It ain’t like that anymore) Magnum Wolf and Slate trade blows in a cage (See the cycle I’ve waited for) And Harbinger makes Gravity play taps before the scene crumbles (It ain’t like that anymore) As red and silver pyrotechnics burst throughout the arena, "It Ain’t Like That Anymore" by Alice in Chains plays. As soon as the residue from the pyro clears, the entrance stage becomes more visible. Two large video screens in the shape of an octagon hang on both sides of the entrance tunnel, with swirling lights on all sides of the screens. In the middle, directly above the tunnel hangs a circular video screen that changes from the Fusion logo to the Mercury logo every other 10 seconds. And below that is the entrance tunnel, a long, round tunnel enclosed by Plexiglas that stretches out to where the fan barricades begin. And for a change, there is no ramp to this stage! The Mercury camera crew makes their scan of the fans, prompting the sings to jump out and high in the air. They range this week from "I (heart) Man Boobs" to "Whatcha gunna do, Harbinger?" and finally, "Pro Wrestling: More real than Olympic figure skating!". Puddle of Mudd’s "Blurry" strikes up over the PA, heralding the arrival of KC Steele and "The Iron Man" John Hughes! The two men bicker down the ramp before taking a seat at ringside, and placing their headsets on. KC Steele: Fans, welcome to another edition of Mercury Fusion! Tonight promises to be a barn burner, as we are only one week away from our big Pay-Per-View, Critical Mass on March 3rd, live from Calgary, Alberta, Canada! But tonight we have a lot to do as well, as Dirge challenges.... Just as Steele utters the name Dirge, "Angels Holocaust" kicks in over the PA as clips of Dirge running all around the world (actually Dirge running against a blue screen) are shown on the MercuTrons. As multicolored lights pulsate in the arena, Dirge comes sprinting from the back, green and pink tassels on his arms and boots, a big D painted on his chest. As Dirge finishes his sprint to the ring, he hops on the apron, and shakes the ropes before getting in the ring and pumping his fists in the air, breathing heavily before getting on the mic. Dirge: HAR-BIN-GUR, By the power of Miles Davis in his bikini briefs, I summon you to this circle of squared so we can do battle in what promises to be a match of ULTIMATE proportions! Soon, The Chemical Brothers "Playground For A Wedgeless Firm" strikes on the PA as Harbinger reluctantly makes his way from the back, Dave Beaumont shoving him down the aisle-way. About halfway down, Harbinger tries to make a break for the back, but Dave stops him and practically drags Harb to the ring. Once in the ring, Harbinger takes a deep breath before unsnapping his belt and bowing to one knee, his head lowered and the belt placed in front of Dirge. Harbinger: Dirge, you are by far the most powerful superstar LCW, Mercury, nevo, or LTD has ever seen. Your mere presence sends chills up the spins of everyone in that locker room, and me...I’m just another victim on your list, oh Ultimate Dirgior. So now, without further hesitation, I demand you take this title from me and save me the physical pain. As the fans boo, Dirge grabs the belt and looks at it, throwing it over his shoulder before snapping the mic out of Harby’s hands. Dirge: The Immortal Harbinger, you are a wise man, for by the power of Hank the hot dog vendor, I would have beaten you, and I would have beaten you to the ninety-third power of the mega stars. But I will accept this title, and regrant you a rematch when the third star in the fifth dimension falls in line with the ULTIMATE plans of Ghandi! Harbinger quickly stands up, a cockeyed look on his face. Harbinger: Are you f**king kidding me? Give me that damn belt back! Dirge is quickly leveled by a right hand before Harbinger tosses his belt to the side and picks up Dirge, throwing him into the ropes and then dropping him square on top of his head, knocking Dirge silly with a very high backdrop. Hughes: What the hell was that, AND THIS, all about? Steele: I don’t know, but why is Dirge puffing his cheeks and pounding his chest? Indeed Dirge is doing just that, getting up and taking every Harbinger blow like it was a feather hitting him. As Dirge shakes them off, Harbinger shakes his head too before kicking Dirge in the stomach and then dropping him with a michinoku driver! Harbinger then rolls Dirgior over before snagging him into the Dreadlock, nearly snapping Dirge in half before he taps. Dave Beaumont calls for the bell, and Harbinger holds up his title belt as Dave gets in the ring. Winner by Submission: Harbinger Dave Beaumont: Harbinger, just how did you do it? How did you manage to beat Dirge? Harbinger: I’ll tell you what, brother. It wasn’t easy. Dirge did take me to Painsville tonight, but I never gave up, and I guess I just got lucky. Beaumont: It must have been luck, because I have never seen anybody defeat The Ultimate Dirgior before in such convincing fashion. Harbinger: Ya know something, brother, I guess I owe it all to my little Harbingeriacs. Those little people saw me through it, and if I train, say my curse words, and eat my wings, someday I may be just like The Ultimate Dirgior! Beaumont: Okay, can the crap. Harb, you kicked Dirge’s ass, and now you get to kick Magnum Wolf’s ass at Critical Mass. Harbinger: Wolf, listen to me, and listen to me good. There is nothing you can or will do to strip this title out of my hands. I will have to be lying dead on that mat, limp, worms rotting on my carcass if you beat me at Critical Mass. Be prepared, Wolf, because now...now you face some real competition. Steele: Well, of all the Mercury title matches I've ever seen... um... Hughes: Yes? Steele: That was one of them. Unannounced, with no entrance and in his civvies, Magnum Wolf walks down with the house mic in hand. Dirge takes his leave, and Harbinger fits the Mercury title back around his waist. Magnum: Nice job beating Dirge. It's about time you won a match without interference or a grossly biased stipulation. I mean, it has been three months since that happened. A "Bin-Ger Sucks" chant breaks out. Harbinger squints, looking 'through' the noise and at his upcoming opponent. Magnum: This is my big shot, and I don't want you to take it from me in anything less than a fair fight. So, Bruce DeAula suggested I look over the big book of match stipulations. Lo and behold, he came up with something that makes perfect sense. A match which has never before been done in LCW. A match whose title makes no sense, but whose purpose will be clear to anyone. I'm talking about a lumberjack match. Everyone at ringside, present and accounted for. Nobody comes inside, nobody breaks the pin, and we're assured that for once , the better man- Harbinger: NO YOU DON'T! The fans boo the champion's stealing of the mic. Harbinger: I know damn well that everyone but Gol hates my guts, and if I happen to fall out, I'll get gang-raped. Meanwhile, I do the same to you, and they'll just dust you off and throw you back. That's not fair! I'm not dropping my title to a clearly biased stipulat- Magnum: If you'll close your mouth, maybe you'll learn why David Beaumont has already signed the stipulation. Pop for the match having been signed. Magnum: Beaumont voiced those concerns already, and he forced one addition to the rules. Anyone who fails to do their job as lumberjacks, and smack me around on the outside, will be suspended without pay for six months. Harbinger's eyebrows lift. Magnum: That's right. I'll get it just as bad as you will if I get tossed. I just don't plan on leaving the ring until I've got that belt around my waist. See you at Critical Mass, Harby. Magnum tosses the mic, points at the belt, and exits stage... um, back. Harbinger simply seems dumbfounded before taking his leave as well. Steele: Harbinger versus Magnum Wolf in a lumberjack match! Can you believe it? Hughes: Not really, I suddenly have a hankering for pancakes. Steele: Get help, John. Match 2 – Blaze McCoy v. Gol Hughes: Time for me to take a Blaze! Steele: That is so played out, John. Hughes: And yet, I don’t care. The arena goes dark and the letters OutKast are shown one by one on the screen. Blaze McCoy is shown close up in slow motion from the soles of his boots to the spiked blonde hair on his head. Golden pyro explodes at the center of the ramp and "Outkast" by POD begins to blast throughout the arena. Blaze McCoy steps out from behind the curtain. Blaze ignores the crowd's boos as they head to the ring. Blaze leaps over the ropes and heads for the nearest turnbuckle, steps up to the top rope and stares at the crowd as he slowly takes off his sunglasses and gives the "thumbs up" with a smile, and then the "thumbs down" with a frown and another blast of flames are shot out of the other three ring posts. Blaze hops down off the turnbuckle and tosses his shades into the crowd, ready for battle. Steele: Blaze had better be ready. He’s running up against one of the greats. The Smashing Pumpkin's "XYU" plays, and the LethalVision lights up with a hundred-dollar bill on it. As the bill burns from left to right, it shows a close-up of the Gol's face, his best evil grin on his face. On the ramp, Gol comes out. Green pyro explodes from the ramp, and Gol goes down in a half-split and flexes his biceps, demonstrating his musculature. The two men size each other up before charging hard and locking up. Immediately, Gol gains the upper hand, twisting Blaze’s arm around his back. Blaze drops to the mat and rolls out of the predicament, and while still holding Gol’s arm, clotheslines the Aussie superstar! Steele: TECHNICAL Blaze! Hughes: More like Crappy Blaze. Gol bounces right back up… into a bodyslam from Blaze! Blaze then drops to the mat alongside Gol and applies a headlock, with his knee digging hard into Gol’s back. Hughes: Hey, I hear a foghorn! BOOOOOOOOOOOOORING… Steele: You know, you aren’t even creative at all. After a few seconds, Gol fires off elbows at Blaze’s midsection, causing McCoy to break the hold. McCoy stays right on top of his man while he allows Gol time to get up. As soon as he does, McCoy boots him in the gut and DDTs him! He covers, and Chris Guzman counts to… 2. Gol kicks out. Steele: McCoy relentless in these early stages. Hughes: Oh, for a phone line to the bar. Steele: Stop it. McCoy stomps away at the downed Gol for a bit before hauling him up and whipping him to the ropes. On the way back, McCoy caught him, looking to hit a spinebuster! But Gol had other plans. He grabbed McCoy’s arm and reversed the move into an armdrag that sent McCoy skidding across the mat! Steele: Gol looking to turn the tide! Hughes: KC looking at the beef in the third row! Gol went to work on the “Lunatic Hick”, pounding away with hard lefts. With McCoy staggered, Gol picked him up in a bodyslam position, and threw him over his head with a fallaway slam that sent McCoy skidding back to the other side of the ring! Enraged, McCoy charges, but Gol casually picks him up and drops him with a hard Samoan Drop! With Blaze down, Gol one-hops the turnbuckle to a chorus of boos from the fans. He flies off, connecting with an elbow to Blaze’s chest! Gol leaves the elbow on top for a cover, but Guzman only reaches two before McCoy gets a shoulder up! Steele: Momentum’s on Gol’s side now. Hughes: Damn! She went to go get a beer… Steele: I guess that means you’ll be forced to pay attention. Hughes: Not bloody likely. Gol offers McCoy a hand. McCoy accepts, and begins to rise to his feet. However, his progress is impeded suddenly. Blaze finds himself in a worse predicament than before: in a standing headlock, courtesy Gol. One sit-out powerbomb later, and Blaze is back on the mat! Guzman slides over and counts 1… 2… no! McCoy kicks out! Steele: What a mo… Hughes: Onetwohegothim… nohedidn’t! Steele: Now that’s just annoying. Hughes: Would you rather me not pay attention again? Steele: Yes. Hughes: OK. Look, she’s back. Oh, man… Gol stomps his foot on the mat, signaling for the Sydney Superkick. Blaze stumbles up, and turns around. Somehow, he reads Gol’s lethal boot headed straight for his head, and ducks in the nick of time! Shocked, Gol turns around just in time to eat a Bait and Tackle spear slam from McCoy! McCoy covers, Guzman counts: 1, 2, … 2 and a half! Steele: McCoy almost had him there! A disgusted McCoy pounds on the mat in frustration. He attacks Gol hard, hitting a Lawler-esque fistdrop! He adds a legdrop, then goes to a corner to take a breather. As soon as Gol is standing, McCoy charges again. He grabs Gol’s head and hits a running spinning neckbreaker! He covers again, and Guzman counts again… and Gol kicks out again at 2! Again, McCoy outwardly shows his frustration. Again, he waits for Gol to get up. Again, as Gol stands, McCoy charges. This time, one thing is different. Steele: Ouch. Hughes: What? What’d I miss? Gol’s right foot meets a hard-charging Blaze right in the chin. The Sydney Superkick this time has the desired effect, ko-ing Blaze. Gol covers, and Guzman counts an easy three. Winner by Pinfall: Gol Backstage Harbinger is in his dressing room when David Beaumont opens the door. Only, he isn't alone. Sickness, decked out in a designer leather dress with matching gloves, is removing the Mercury title from Harbinger's waist from behind him. Dave: Oh, hello there Sick. I wasn't expecting to see you. Sickness: Hello, yoursel- Dave: Ever. Sickness gives a bemused expression and places her hands decadently across Harbinger's chest. The champ gives a little smirk. Harbinger: Anything else you signed behind my back? Dave: It was that, or Hell In A Cell. We both know which was the right choice. Harbinger: So, what brings you here? Dave: We need to talk business. I need someone who isn't a yes-man. Sickness: He certainly says 'yes' a lot to me. Dave: I didn't ask for your input. Sickness gives a cute little pout at Beaumont's snipe... but the look in her eyes shows an entirely different emotion at play. Dave: Come on. Harbinger disengages his companions' hands and exits his personal locker. Sickness holds the Mercury title in front of herself and runs her right index finger along the nameplate. The Hallway Dave: ...don't think you want anything to do with her, Herb. I really don't. Harbinger: That's for me to decide. Along the way to Beaumont's makeshift base of operations, they pass TR Saxton, who is chatting it up with Headbuster. Beaumont reaches into a pocket and tosses TR a quarter. Dave: Here, I heard you need cab fare home. He and Harbinger share a grin, before TR grabs Beaumont by the collar and slams him back into the wall. Headbuster senses correctly that now is a good time to get ready for the tag title match. TR: That's f**king it. You change the stips on my title shot at the last minute? Shove me into a tag title match I have no hope of winning? And now, you're mocking me because I want to earn my own living? I've been here for a year and a quarter, but it's only now that I see you for what you really are. He lets go before Harbinger gets physically involved. TR turns to him. TR: You're pathetic. Get rid of him before you lose what's left of your legend. As for me, I'm getting out before Beaumont ruins mine any further. David Beaumont's eyes go wide, and he lets out a squeak of fear. TR: I quit, you soulless bastards. See you in Philly. Beaumont's mouth goes slack as he tries (and fails) to come up with words. The shot zooms in on Harby, who grins. Harbinger: Cool. Arena Steele: TR Saxton has quit Mercury! Hughes: And was that a reference to him jumping to the other guys? Why would he want to do that? Being around Philadelphians that much is like ingesting dishwasher soap every day... just not good for you. Steele: Ever the politician. Match 3, Singles Match-Pariah vs Josh Trent The Trent Brothers' video package begins with a close-up of Mikel Trent's face, his eyes closed, as the beginning of Drowning Pool's "Bodies" fills the arena. Mikel's eyes then open, and his face fills with anger and rage before the camera pulls back to show his elder brother Josh with a smile on his face. The feed then switches to a split screen as pyro erupts from the ramp and the brothers appear from behind the curtain. On the left of the MercuTron, Josh hits several high-flying moves and attacks enemies with weapons, while on the right, Mikel spears and pummels other opponents. As Josh reaches ringside, their video ends with a clip of Mikel powerbombing Josh through an opponent and a table. The lights in the arena die as Machine Head’s "Nausea" blares over the pa and a green eye is shown on the video screens. The eye blinks, and then begins to crumble away as bright white and green lights shine throughout the arena and Pariah...doesn’t stomp out of the fog filled entrance area. The music dies after a few more seconds before kicking in again, the entrance starting all over. But still, no Pariah, until... Backstage As we go backstage from the arena, we see the frame of one Keith Summers lying face down on the cold cement, a bent lead pipe next to his head. As the fans gasp in shock, the camera pulls out to reveal a hand wearing one black leather glove before we go back to the arena, where we see Josh Trent leaving the ring, a look of befuddlement on his face. Arena Hughes: What the... Steele: You know, that is the second week in a row somebody has been laid out, and we have no idea who it is or was! Hughes: First Blaze, then Pariah....you know, I think Josh Trent is behind all this! Steele: What Hughes? Hughes: Look at it! Last week his match was after the Blaze attack, and this week it was DURING the Pariah attack! Look out people in the next match! It was Trent! Steele: Honestly, stop talking before you get even stupider. Backstage, AGAIN We cut to the lockerroom of Gol, where we see him shoving a pair of leather gloves into his gym bag before turning around to adress Leah Grant standing next to him. Gol: What do you want, Leah? Leah: Well, Gol, in case you didn't notice, your opponent for Critical Mass, Pariah, was laid out earlier tonight. Gol: Yeah, I saw it, and your point is....? Leah: My point is that many people are pointing the blame towards you. Gol: Listen Leah, I've got better things to do then keep knocking out that little bloke, okay? I hope he has a speedy recovery, because at Critical Mass he gets attacked from the front, full throttle. Leah: Well, thanks. Back to the arena, KC and John. Gol gives Leah a pinch, and Leah slaps his hand. Arena Hughes: Do I sense something more than wrestler-interviewer going on there? Steele: For those of you just tuning in, John can't remember anything past last week. Match 4, North American Title Match: Geoffrey Slate (c) vs ??? From the stage a large blast of white pyro shoots just above the heads of the fans. Disturbed's "Violence Fetish" heralds the entrance of Geoffrey Slate; on a pitch-black MercuTron appears the word "Devastation", which quickly fades into a standard entrance video featuring a splicing of stills and clips which is made special primarily by the ring of fire encircling it. Slate walks coolly and calmly to the ring, barely recognizing the fans on the way. He slides into the ring and hands his North American title to the referee, then awaits his opponent. Hughes: Well? Who's it gonna be? Steele: ...Beaumont? David Beaumont comes out of the entrance. Dave: Jeff, there was a guy who came up to me and said that in his last match ever, he beat you. I figured, he's kidding. But, there it was on the Wedding tape, sure as anything. So, without further ado, getting his third and final chance at regaining employment... Big Pee-On! Pee-On power walks down, gaining a huge pop from those who have been following LCW from the start. Slate shrugs and takes a fighting stance, as Pee-On suddenly starts sprinting and jumps onto the apron before doing a springboard crossbody into the ring! No, literally; he misses without Slate even moving, and hits nothing but ring. Pee-On is up fast, though, and Slate connects with a knee lift into a flowing DDT in a single motion. Slate covers, 1, 2, Pee-On kicks out... and in the process, kicks Slate low! Pee-On is up first, naturally, and he drops an elbow on Slate. Then another. Then eight more. Cover, Slate kicks out at one hard enough to send Pee-On flying! Steele: Big Pee-On can put you away with a number of moves, but I don't think that's one of them. Hughes: Great deduction to have after Slate kicked out. What's next, you'll say that you expect Harbinger to beat Dirge? Pee-On is a MIDGET OF FIRE, pounding away on Slate for kicking out so nonchalantly. Slate belts him to stop that, and makes his way to his feet. Pee-On punts him in the abdomen and sets him up for a powerbomb, but that doesn't work, so Pee-On pounds at Slate's back with well over a dozen rapid-fire forearm shots. Slate stands up, taking Pee-On over with a back-bodydrop. Pee-On fights through the pain (the fact that he's drunk helping that along), and he grabs Slate's head from behind while inserting his own head into Slate's neck. Pee-On sits out, connecting with the Drunk Driver! He covers, one, two, no! Another cover, one, two, no! Pee-On is incensed, and he tries another strategy. Wait, no he doesn't he covers again for two! Hughes: What strategy! Steele: Pee-On has used a low blow and the Drunk Driver, so if I'm not mistaken he's run through his entire moveset. Pee-On pulls Slate up and again tries a powerbomb, but this time Slate simply shoves Pee-On back. Pee-On charges in with a clothesline, which Slate merely absorbs before grabbing a full nelson and bridging into a dragon suplex, one, two, three! Winner by Pinfall: Geoffrey Slate Steele: Well, that was... um... Hughes: The phrase you're looking for, I believe, is "the fastest two minutes this side of your mom." KC smacks John upside the head. Match 5 – The Elite (c) v Headbuster & Inferno, for the LCW Mercury Tag Team Championships Sevendust’s “Praise” blares through the arena, and the two newest LCW Mercury stars, Headbuster and Inferno, head down to the ring. Steele: Quite an opportunity for these rookies to show what they’re made of. Hughes: Quite an opportunity to go to the bathroom. Steele: Oh Lord… Hughes: But I really gotta go… "Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap" by AC/DC blares up over the PA as the lights go out, and purple and blue lights swirl around the entrance stage as the words "The Elite" fill the video screens and images of people doing high class things plays. As The Elite enter the ring, they unhook their belts and hand them to the timekeeper, ready for action. The LTD graduates play rock-paper-scissors to see who goes first. Headbuster wins, and Inferno retreats to the apron. On the other side, Vincent Picasso defers to Johnny Glamour, and exits the ring himself. Steele: It’s go time! Hughes: (groaning) Don’t mock me like that! Steele: But it’s fun. Glamour and ‘Buster lock up. Glamour applies a headlock and knees Headbuster in the gut, keeling the newcomer over. This leaves open the window of opportunity for Glamour, and he hits a picture-perfect fishermans suplex, holding on for the bridge. Headbuster has little trouble kicking out at one, but has some difficulty getting to his feet, largely because of the repeated rakes to the back from Glamour. Glamour finally lets up. Big mistake, as the eager rookie jumps all over him with a spinning heel kick! He remains standing, his back turned to Glamour, and leaps in the air with a standing moonsault! But nobody’s home, as Glamour kips up just before Headbuster connects. With Headbuster down, Glamour looks to take advantage, leaping in the air with a leg drop. But, again, no one home, as Headbuster is up rather quickly. With Glamour sitting butt-first on the mat, Headbuster unleashes a vicious kick right to his face, putting him down hard! Steele: Headbuster looking to show the fans that he’s Number 1. Hughes: (groaning) Stop it! Steele: Make me. (singing) I… am, Number One… Hughes: The agony… Satisfied, Headbuster tags in his partner, Inferno. The former LTD Lethal Champion stomps away at a downed Glamour, before hauling him to his feet. He picks up Glamour as he might a sack of potatoes, and drapes him over his knee back-first, sending Glamour back down again! Inferno covers, but referee Chris Guzman’s count only reaches 2 before Glamour kicks out. As Inferno taunts the crowd, Glamour scrambles for his corner. This does not go unnoticed, however, and Inferno stops him in his tracks by applying a full nelson. Keeping the hold, Inferno slams Glamour down hard on his neck, and Glamour goes bouncy-bouncy, then limp on the mat! Inferno covers again, but this time Picasso comes to the rescue, dropkicking Inferno out of the pinfall. Steele: What does it take, to, makemyannouncepartnergo… Number One… Hughes: Take this, sit down, and write it… I will kill you. Steele: Sorry. Anyway, P… Hughes: You sonuva… Steele: Ahem! PICASSO bailed his partner out there. Picasso gets a few more stomps in before Chris Guzman escorts him back to his corner. While Guzman’s back is turned, Headbuster gets into the ring, and he and Inferno double-team Glamour, suplexing him down hard! Headbuster then hops back onto the apron, and waits for Guzman’s attention. When Guzman is back facing the ring, Inferno tags Headbuster in. Inferno approaches Glamour, looking to inflict more damage, but he is instead met with an EEI staple: the low blow kick. Guzman misses the violation, and Glamour scrambles to his corner, tagging in Vincent Picasso! Hughes: Vinny’s the residence in flames! Steele: You know what puts out flames? Water. Hughes: Damn you to hell, KC. Picasso charges hard, clotheslining Inferno down. Headbuster leaps the ropes, looking to join the fun, but his movement is resisted by a dropkick from Picasso! Inferno gets up and gets the same treatment as his partner! Headbuster gets up again and eats a knee to the gut, followed by a lightning-fast cradle piledriver! Hughes: Headbuster just got his head busted! Steele: You cold, brother. Hughes: Thanks. Steele: Cold as… ice? Hughes. Eat me, brother. Glamour rolls an invalid Headbuster out of the ring while Picasso works over Inferno. Picasso applies a headlock, and delivers a snap suplex! Picasso hauls Inferno up with him, and hits a Northern Lights suplex on the second try! He holds on for the bridge, and Guzman counts 1… 2… 2 and a half… no! Hughes: He’s a resilient little bugger. Steele: Could a get a Dasani over here? John, you want one? Hughes: I’d prefer to strangle you, actually. Steele: Bwa ha ha. Picasso picks up Inferno and powerbombs him to the mat in short order! Picasso thinks about dealing out more punishment, until he sees how close Inferno is to the turnbuckle. Picasso decides, “What the hell, I’ll end it now,” and mounts the turnbuckle. He leaps off with a beautiful shooting star press, and connects! He covers, and Guzman counts 1… 2… Inferno kicks out. Unfortunately, he’s about a half a second late on that, and the Elite celebrate as AC/DC plays. Winners by Pinfall: The Elite Hughes: Can I go now? Steele: Whatever. "Iron" John Hughes sprints off.
