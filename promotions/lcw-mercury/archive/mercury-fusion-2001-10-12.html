LCW Mercury Card Archive

Fusion, 12 October 2001

12 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Mercury Fusion- Live From The Charleston, West Virginia Civic Center- October 12, 2001. Disturbed's "Shout 2000" plays as the entrance video starts with a shot of the Mercury Fusion logo. (SHOUT!) Pariah makes Apex scream in a submission (SHOUT!) Harbinger celebrates with his Universal Title (Let it all out) Clips from various athletes in pain are seen (These are the things I can do without) And Toh lariat's somebody right out of his boots (Come on) G Slate bitches somebody out in a interview (I'm talking to you) And Jerry Cane drops a former LCW performer with the T for Texas (So come on) The Trents perform some Brotherly Love (In violent times...) Dave Beaumont flashes a sinister grin (You shouldn't have to sell your soul) The goofy Memosarks throws confetti at the crowd (In black and white...) TR Saxton mangles some unseen person with a cheese grater (They really, really ought to know) Gravity drops Brian Shotgunn through some tables (Just don't know) Apex looks out into space after a win. (Those wandering minds...) Harbinger lashes out at anyone and everyone in a promo (Who took you for a working whore) a shot of the newcomers Entropy Crusade talking is seen (Kiss them goodbye...) And The New School destroys The Birds at Torn (They shouldn't have to jump for joy) a table is broken by Gravity (Jump!) Pariah dents a chair over Rook Black's head (Jump!) The Trents beat up unsuspecting people (Jump!) Memosarks and Harbinger do battle (Jump!) And TR Saxton hits the D.O.I (Jump!) Harbinger wrestles in Japan (You shouldn't have to shout for joy) Pariah screams (SHOUT!) Beaumont addresses the workers of LCW Mercury (They give you life...) Gravity clutches a World Title (...and in return you gave them hell!) G Slate smiles under a crimson mask (As cold as ice) Pariah throws Pestilence off of the scaffold at Suicide (I hope we live to tell the tale) and the final clips of various wrestlers hitting their finishers are seen (I hope we live to shout the tale!) The inside of the Civic Center is seen, as the thousands of fan erupt, and a fireworks display engulfs the entrance area, and golden sparks shoot from the word FUSION, hanging above. As the smoke from the pyro clear, the Fusion entrance stage becomes visible. From the top of the building the word "Fusion" hangs, spelt out in metal girders. A circular metal girder with swirling lights dangling from it hangs below this Fusion, and two fair sized video screens, called the MercuTrons, hang on opposite sides of the Fusion. Below Fusion is the entrance area, which consists of two large metal girders that lead from the top of the ramp up to the U and O in Fusion. For their entrances, the wrestlers will walk out between the girders, and in front of the Mercury Fusion logo, which is built into the stage, and in between the large steel entrance girders. The elevated ramp way, which is covered in dull gray carpet, leads us right to the ring! The cameras make the traditional scan of the fans, who are proudly showing off their signs. They range from "The Greatest Fusion Of All Time!" to "Sloan’s Mamma Is Soooo Fat!" and finally we spot a group of young men holding up numerous signs that read "Getcha Some Herb". We then cut to the announce desk, where KC Steele, in a fleece "Mercury" pull over on, sits next to John Hughes, who is wearing a black button down "Fusion" shirt. KC Steele: Hello fans, and welcome to Mercury Fusion! We plan to deliver another action packed show to you tonight, which will feature round two of the World Title Tourney, and the Tag Titles on the line again as The Trents take on Sloan and Matt Godsey! John Hughes: And don’t forget the return of Blaze McCoy and Harbinger to LCW rings tonight! Before John can get another word off, Metallica’s "Struggle Within" plays out Dave Beaumont. He confidently strides down the rampway as his video package plays behind. Dave gets in the ring, and looks over the arena with microphone in hand. Dave Beaumont: Hello, Charleston. You should all feel special. You should all feel privileged. Because not only do you get to watch Mercury action, and round two of the World Title Tournament...you get to see me! The boo’s nearly shake the roof off the Civic Center. Beaumont: Hey, why the hostility? I mean, you people have nothing to cheer for. No major pro sports teams, nobody famous from this state. I mean, you should feel lucky to get to see us! The best you used to get was WCW. And how great was that? I know you people weren’t the ones making sounds during those Thunder broadcasts. Laughter. Yet still many boo’s. Beaumont: But that’s besides the point. I’m out here tonight to talk about my world title tourney. You know, the one full of wrestlers who can’t sell a t-shirt... Before Dave can get another word off, "Children of the Grave" by Rob Zombie plays out Dirge and Brimstone, better known as Entropy Crusade. They promptly walk into the ring, and motion for Dave to stand still. Dirge grabs him by the shoulders, and Brimstone walks behind him. Dave: Hey, hey, let go of me! Let go, or I’ll fire you so fas.... With that word said, Brimstone grabs ahold of Dave’s whitey tideys and yanks up with an atomic wedgie! The white underwear of Dave Beaumont is nearly pulled up to his head, leaving Beaumont in serious pain! Hughes: The atomic wedgie of doom! A move so fierce, so painful it has been outlawed in 5 states, and banned in Peru! Dave struggles to pick his wedgie, but Dirge hasn’t let go yet. But he soon does, spinning Beaumont around, and into a Brimstone boot! Dave and his stretched underwear fall to the mat, and Entropy Crusade simply walk out of the ring. Steele: Entropy Crusade, as popular as they will ever be, I can assure you of that fans. Match 1- Memosarks vs Jerry Cane Confetti starts to fall from the rafters over the crowd and big top music fills the arena, prompting Memosarks to appear in the entranceway, a big grin on his face. He slowly starts to walk to the ring, smiling at all the cameras as he goes. Hughes: I don't like that guy. Steele: Because he spreads joy? Hughes: Anyone who smiles *that much* can't be right in the head. An outline of the state of Texas is branded on the video screen, and Toby Keith's "How Do You Like Me Now" bursts into the main chorus of the song, skipping the intro of it. Bright white lights scan the entrance area, as Jerry Cane and Scott Tibor walk out from the back, and stand side by side at the beginning of the entrance ramp. They look at each other, nod, and then walk down to the ring. Steele: Cane has to be wanting to make a big comeback here, after the Graduates lost a close one to the Trents in last week's main event. Hughes: And Cane is going to DESTROY clown-boy! Memosarks gets it started fast and furious by bodyslamming Cane before he so much has a chance to get all the way through the ropes! Memo then does a falling dropkick to eject Cane to the side of the ring. With that done, he runs across the ring to build up momentum for a between-the-top-and-middle-rope suicida! Cane stops his momentum simply by planting his feet, however, and he tosses him over towards the elevated rampway side of the ring. He picks up Memosarks and slowly turns him around in his grasp so that he can toss him into the elevated ramp’s side with an exploder suplex! Memosarks body hangs half on, half off the entrance ramp before Cane picks him up and slides him in the ring. Hughes: Jerry Cane again demonstrates a his growing aggressiveness by tossing Memosarks without concern for his safety. If he'd shown more of this last week they might still be champions, and if they keep it up, they'll have the belts back in no time. Steele: I don't know about that. The Trents seem poised for a long run on top of the tag division. Memosarks manages to get up in record time, but he's on dream street and Cane has no problem reclaiming the advantage. One kidney punch later, Memo is softened up enough for Cane to put him in a fireman's carry, then drop him hard with a DVD! Tibor gives Cane a hi-sign, and Cane nods while moving to a corner to climb up top. Cane looks at Memosarks, steadies himself, and leaps off in a breathtaking big elbow from the third turnbuckle! Cane smiles and nods while hooking a leg for an elementary 1, 2, Memosarks gets a shoulder up! Cane's eyes bulge in disbelief, so he hooks the leg again, 1, 2, Memosarks rolls through into a pin of his own, 1, Cane kicks out with authority! Steele: Memosarks demonstrating a toughness that one wouldn't normally associate with him. Hughes: 'One' being the entire planet. Cane, the fresher by far, gets Memosarks up for a series of punches to the chest and sides. Memo falls to one knee from the onslaught, and is soon shoved between Cane's legs. Cane uses his powerful upper body to lift Memosarks for a snap powerbomb, and Memosarks uses his quickness to turn it into a snap hurricanrana with a cradle, one, two, Cane kicks out in the nick of time! Memo and Jerry get up simultaneously, and Memo goes kick-wham-DDT in short order. Memosarks whips Cane into a corner, then starts to clap his hands and run in place. The crowd claps as well, and they cheer when Memosarks takes off at a sprint for the Memosplash on Joe Black's trainee! Cane does the easiest counter- the sidestep of doom- and leaves Memosarks to leap chest-first into the top buckle! Steele: I think the smiley face rubbed off on the buckle.... Hughes: I hope Cane wipes his smile off for good. Cane shakes out the cobwebs with a jerk of his head, and then sets upon finishing Memosarks off by placing him in a seated position on the top rope. Cane climbs up after him, and pulls Memosarks to his feet for the T for Texas exploder suplex! At least, in theory. In practice, Memo blocks by hooking his right foot on the top rope, then by coming to life with a series of punches to Cane's face! Memosarks puts Cane in a side headlock, punches him a few more times from there, and lifts him into the air. Using every muscle fiber, and the energy of the thousands in attendance, Memosarks manages to fall forward while spinning Cane around in order to plant him with a falcon arrow slam off the top! Memo leans forward and pulls a leg back, one, two, three! Winner by Pinfall after Super Lobotomy Time: Memosarks Steele: What an incredible move to end the match! Hughes: What a stupid way to end the match! I can't believe Memosarks won. Tibor slides in from ringside to tend to his fallen partner, while Memosarks has his hand raised. Steele: Next up is a match I'm sure you'll enjoy, John. Hughes: You're damn right I will! Match 2- Barabbas vs Brimstone The MercuTron video package shows a swirling black hole tinted blue as light dies. Silence as the image is shown, then blue flames errupt keying the intro to "Angels Holocaust" as Brimstone makes his way to the ring. Hughes: Normally, I'd root heavily for ANYONE who participates in a Beaumont wedgie, but he's fighting Barabbas, so... After a roll of thunder, the completely dark arena is filled with Black Sabbath's "War Pigs", and the LethalVision lights up with Barabbas' name on it in large letters with a cross in the background. Because of the darkness of his entrance, the only thing that is highly visible is the enormous cross he brings to the ring carried on his back. Steele: Two really big guys going at it here. Don't expect a rave review if Clyde Thibault ever watches this. Hughes: That hack writes one review a month and still gets a paycheck. There's no justice, I tells ya. Barabbas and Brimstone start off by doing what comes naturally to them; that is, beating the living daylights out of whatever is directly in front of them. Barabbas, thanks to his raw girth and muscle mass, wins the contest of wills and kicks Brimstone in the chest. Barabbas lifts Brimstone onto his shoulders for a big powerbomb, and in a moment that can best be described as 'surreal' (or perhaps 'incredibly sloppy'), Brimstone turns it into a hurricanrana! Hughes: ... Steele: ... Hughes & Steele: What the hell was that?! They look, then point at each other. Hughes & Steele: Jinx! Brimstone pulls Barabbas up by the right arm, and uses it as leverage for a short-arm clothesline. Barabbas does the ol' "I'm really big" routine, and laces into Brimstone with knife-edged chops. Brimstone replies with chops of his own, loud enough to be heard in the cheap seats. Barabbas headbutts him, Brimstone headbutts him back... then stumbles backwards in a daze due to the thickness of Barabbas' cranium. Barabbas steamrolls him with a lariat, then does a standing splash into a lateral press cover, one, two, Brimstone somehow kicks out from beneath the big(ger) man! Steele: Not exactly a technical masterpiece so far. Hughes: They pulled off a frigging HURRICANRANA! What more do you want from them? Steele: Point well taken. Barabbas pulls Brimstone up by his Zandigian black hair, then slaps on a double-choke in preparation for a chokebomb. Brimstone lashes out with a backhand punch to cause him to break the choke, followed by a spinning 'backhandwich' to the nose that stuns the mighty Barabbas. Brimstone, with all the speed and grace God gave him (that is, none) gets behind Barabbas, puts him in an inverted facelock, and drops down for a reverse DDT! Brimstone covers, one, two, Barabbas kicks out... right after three! Winner by Pinfall after a reverse DDT: Brimstone Steele: I think the main positive to be taken from this match is that neither man caused the other grievous bodily harm by accident. Hughes: Aren't they trying to do that in the match? Steele: Sometimes messing up can be worse than pulling a move off properly. Hughes: If that was true then Bob Yobbo would have paralyzed half the LCW roster during his last stint. Match 3- Mercury Title Tournament, Round 2, Dirge vs Blaze McCoy The MercuTron video package shows a swirling black hole tinted blue as light dies. Silence as the image is shown, and then "Children of the Grave" by Rob Zombie erupts, keying the entrance of Dirge, seconded by his partner Brimstone. Both men slowly make their ways to the ring, with Brimstone remaining at ringside. The arena goes dark and the letters OutKast are shown one by one on the screen. Then the words scroll across the screen. The corners of the stage are set on fire and enormous flames run from both sides of the stage around to the front and then meet as Golden pyro explodes at the center of the ramp and “Outkast” by POD begins to blast throughout the arena. Shots of Blaze are shown in the ring executing the "Outkast" and the "Hook, Line and Sinker" moves, as well as many others. Blaze McCoy steps out from behind the curtain with his wife, the lovely Cherry Stacked. A mix of cheers and a few boos fill the arena. Blaze’s long brownish blonde hair is about shoulder length now and he is wearing a pair of dark sunglasses. Blaze has on a black singlet with gold flames on it with black wrestling boots with BLAZE and OUTKAST written down the sides. Cherry has long brown hair up in a pony tail and is wearing a red string bikini top, which her large breasts are bursting out of, and very short daisy dukes shorts with red high heels on. She is holding Blaze's fishing rod which is wrapped in barbed wire. They give the Steel City Salute on the ramp and walk to the ring. Blaze lifts Cherry over the top rope then leaps over the ropes and heads for the nearest turnbuckle, steps up to the top rope and stares at the crowd as he slowly takes off his sunglasses and gives the “thumbs up” with a smile, and then the “thumbs down” with a frown and another blast of flames are shot out of the other three ring posts. The crowd reacts once again with a mix of cheers and boos. Blaze hops down off the turnbuckle and hands Cherry his glasses, gets a kiss on the cheek and is ready for battle. Blaze does a little hop and prance around the ring, telling Dirge "You ain't nothing boy!". Dirge calmly walks to the middle of the ring, and awaits for Blaze to do the same. Blaze does, and the two men lock up. They struggle back and forth, but Blaze finally gets the advantage, locking a headlock in on Dirge. Dirge struggles to get free, and finally does, pushing Blaze off into the ropes. Blaze ducks a clothesline attempt, and rebounds with a spinning elbow! Dirge is floored by the impact, and Blaze picks up a 1, quick kickout. Hughes: See, Dirge is um..picking his spot again. Yeah. He's really good at it. Steele: Speaking of picking things, I wonder how Dave is doing back there. Blaze is quick to pickup Dirge, and tosses him into the corner. Blaze sprints in after him, but Dirge catapults himself out of the corner and tackling Blaze onto the mat! Dirge rolls off, Blaze kips up, and quickly the two men go back to trading blows, back and forth. Dirge gets the upper hand, knees Blaze in the stomach, and puts him in position for a suplex. Dirge kind of squats, and explodes with a snap suplex that surely rocks Blaze! Dirge rolls over onto Blaze's chest for a 1, 2, kickout by McCoy. Steele: A see-saw battle to kick things off between these two amazing athletes here. Hughes: This should be a good match, with both of them bringing speed and agility. Blaze rolls over to the ropes, and gets up, collecting his breath. Dirge does the same, but in the middle of the ring. Dirge tries to go for a lead by charging at Blaze, but Blaze is able to leapfrog over him, allowing Dirge to sail out between the ropes and in front of the announce table! Hughes: Get him away! I hate when this happens. Steele: Just part of the job John, just part of the job. Blaze awaits his opportunity, and lets Dirge get to his feet. Blaze then sprints to the corner, leaps onto the top turnbuckle, and springoards to the outside, performing a plancha on Dirge! Both men roll backwards into the table, shoving it into Hughes and Steele. Hughes: Oh crap, the pelvis! The burning is coming back! Steele: Alright, that one hurt. It shoved the wireless headphone box into my you know where... Hughes: So you got a pelvis shot too? Blaze backs up, and lets Dirge get to his feet. Blaze then charges again, but Dirge ducks, and backbody drops Blaze way into the air, and then hard onto the mats! Blaze squirms in pain, and Dirge chuckles at the fallen "OutKast". Dirge then picks up Blaze, and whips him towards the steps. But with the two men being so close, Blaze doesn't have time to take the impact with his shoulder, and his knees connect, flipping him up and over the steps! Steele: Well, that was ugly. Hughes: Wonder how the kneecaps are. SCC approved? Steele: SHHH! Dirty word! Blaze limps onto the apron, and checks his knees over. Dirge however grabs Blaze by the head, and pulls him up on the apron, and over the top rope. Blaze hobbles up, but almost gets his head taken off by Dirge and a vicious right! Blaze is able to duck his head, and quickly knees Dirge in the gut. He then puts Dirge's arm over his head, and his arm over Dirge's head. Then he grabs ahold of Dirge's tights around the waist, and lifts him up into a vertical suplex! Hughes: It's the setup for the Outkast! Steele: There's Brimstone though! Brimstone is indeed down at ringside now, and hiding right by Blaze and Dirge, ducking. Blaze goes for the slingshot part now by dropping Dirge's legs across the top rope, but Brimstone grabs ahold of Blaze's feet, causing Dirge to topple down on top of Blaze! Dirge stays on top, and gets 1, 2, 3! Winner via Pinfall: Dirge Steele: Brimstone and Dirge are nothing but dirty, dirty cheats. Bastards. Hughes: Come on, Steele! That's nothing but great, great teamwork. Match 4- Apex vs TR Saxton The ominous tones of Diamond Head's "Am I Evil?" echo through the arena. Three seconds in, every house light goes completely black, and a spotlight shines on the entrance ramp. A man in a black leather jacket walks out as the long instrumental opening of the song continues, revealing himself as TR Saxton. TR stares at the ring and walks with a measured gait in a perfectly straight line. Behind him, the MercuTron plays highlights of his thus-far incredibly violent career, with several close-up shots of the sickening drive of Dead On Impact interspersed throughout. TR enters the ring and pulls his hands out of his jacket pocket as the lights come on. The spotlight and the jacket go off together. Steele: TR Saxton and Apex have never fought before, which is odd considering how long they've been in LCW together. Hughes: I say that this is a 'money' match. Apex's brute force against TR's brutality. Ministry's "Psalm 69" fills the arena, and the name "Apex" flashes on the MercuTron, followed by shots of mountain climbers ascending the great peaks of the world. As they reach the tops of the mountains, the feed cuts to a shot of the Jonestown mass suicide and as the cameraman makes his way along the haphazard bodies, the camera looks up and there is Apex. He smiles, and then clips of him ending careers are shown. As he walks down the ramp, red streams of pyro shoot up from the ramp all around him. Steele: Apex doesn't look well. Hughes: But he's still f'n big. You've gotta give him that. Steele: For whatever it's worth anymore... TR assaults Apex with stomps before, at, and after the bell. Apex shrugs them all off and stands up before decking TR with a palm thrust to the face. TR gets right back up and begins pounding at Apex's face with alternating lefts and rights, none of which seem to have any effect. Frustrated, TR boots Apex in the gut and Irish whips him. Halfway across the ring, Apex simply collapses. TR gets a 'what the hell was that?' look on his face. Steele: TR is a hard puncher, but I've never seen anyone collapse like that after a few hits. Hughes: I don't like it. TR walks over and picks up Apex by the hair, only to startle and jerk his hand away in revulsion when a spider crawls out and onto his arm. TR flicks the spider away and wipes the hand off on his tights while Apex gets up the rest of the way and wraps his left hand around TR's throat. TR pounds on Apex's left elbow to get a break, only to have Apex grab him with his right hand and put him through a massive chokeslam! Apex keeps the choke on while TR is down, and uses his left hand in what appears to be an attempt to unscrew TR's head from his neck. Referee Bo Smirnow tries to pull Apex off before TR is seriously injured, but Apex headbutts him and continues the highly illegal hold. Smirnow calls for the bell. Steele: Apex warned that he'd kill whoever he fought, but I didn't think he meant it! Winner by Disqualification: TR Saxton Dealer, who has the night off, runs from the back to the ring in record time (for him). Apex releases TR when Dealer climbs over the top rope, and exits the ring without a fuss. Dealer goes to the side of TR, who is gagging from the hard choke. Steele: You don't see TR Saxton get his ass handed to him often but- Hughes: Don't speak so soon there KC. He's calling for the mic. TR (coughing and raspy): Apex! I'm not done with you! Get your big, Mexican ass back here so I can teach you not to screw with a Saxton's life! Apex stops on the ramp and turns around. TR: Smirnow, restart the match. I'm not taking a win by DQ. Bo looks at TR, then at Dealer, who glares menacingly between Bo and Apex. Smirnow calls for the bell while directing Dealer to leave the ring. Apex reenters the ring, and TR charges in with the hardest lariat he can manage. Apex absorbs the blow and grabs TR for another chokeslam, but TR fights it off in short order and drives his knee hard into Apex's abdomen. Apex doubles over, winded, and TR delivers an elbow and knee to his head at the same time, sandwiching Apex's skull in a highly painful manner! Apex falls to the mat in an expedient manner. TR signals for a finish and walks to a corner for the purpose of climbing to the top turnbuckle. Apex cooperates by staying down, and TR is able to jump off, twist in midair, and deliver the Glass Ceiling double-axehandle blow to the head! TR hooks a leg, one, two, Apex kicks out! Steele: And old Saxton standby doesn’t get the job done there. Hughes: Nothing but your best can beat Apex. The man is a machine! TR pulls Apex up by the neck, which takes some time due to Apex's much larger mass. That done, TR doubles him over with a boot, and places him in a standing headscissors. TR yells something about 'ending careers', lifts Apex into a vertical base, and drops him with a piledriver! Not content with that, TR keeps his hold on Apex's waist such that when he stands up, Apex is in the headscissors again. TR lifts, and again dumps Apex on his head Lawler-style. But clearly that can't be it. TR stands up, screams "CARNAGE!", and piledrives Apex a third consecutive time! Apex appears to be a 7'2" mound of gelatin, but when TR covers him with a lateral press, he somehow gets a shoulder up at 2 and 9/10ths! Steele: Unbelievable. TR Saxton comes back from nearly having his head removed, and now Apex kicks out after rolling piledrivers! We haven't even had ten minutes of match time and both men have seemingly cheated death. Hughes: You want to talk about cheating death? I bet on the Bills last Sunday. Steele: ... Hughes: It was the JETS for Pete's sake! Steele: Hey, it's your paycheck you're flushing. TR makes a second 'time for the finish' motion, and pulls Apex into another standing headscissors. TR lifts, but Apex isn't budged. TR lifts again, no dice. TR starts to pound on Apex's back with forearm shots, and Apex simply stands up, draping TR over his back. TR flails for dear life, and is promptly dropped on his head with a high-angle snap spinebuster! Apex raises his right hand into the air and plunges it down onto TR's neck. Apex then lifts TR all the way from the canvas into a gorilla press! TR comes to life before the Pinnacle can be hit, and he powers his way out of the press while grabbing Apex's arms for a swinging DOI triangle driver! TR holds onto the arms and turns such that Apex is sitting, and he then pulls forward on Apex's arms, pushing Apex's face between his legs while putting immense pressure on both shoulder sockets! Apex screams at the top of his lungs in pain, then signals his submission. Steele: I believe that's the move he calls the Saxton Stretch, which is a move he developed during his time off. Hughes: That's a pretty solid win for TR, but it's all going to pot once Gravity fights him again. Winner by Submission to the Saxton Stretch: TR Saxton Steele: And now, we go back to the tournament for some more big quarterfinals action! Match 5- Mercury Title Tournament Round 2, Pariah vs Mastadon An atomic bomb goes off on the MercuTron, and in the wake of the bomb, the arena blacks out completely for several seconds, just as if a real EMP had hit. As the lights come back on, Big L and Fat Joe's "Way of Life" plays, and Mastadon makes his way to ringside. The intro of Disturbed's "Down With The Sickness" plays as a green and white spotlight scan the staging area. A blue one now joins as it trails up the ramp, and a huge flame explodes as the lead singers scream erupts over the sound system. Pariah slowly paces out to the top of the ramp, and looks over the arena as blue and green spotlights engulf the arena. He makes his way to the ring as the MercuTron shows some of the more famous spots from his career, such as his destroying of Pestilence in a Scaffold Match, pinning Joe and Rook Black, fighting Pheonyx, and beating Apex with a chair. As he gets in the ring from the elevated walkway, he hops on the second turnbuckle, and looks out at the crowd as blue and white lights swirl around the ring. Steele: One of the more talked about matches this evening, as there really is a bit of history behind it. These two have battled in the past, and will do so today as well. Pariah stretches in his corner, and looks over Mastadon before coming to the middle of the ring. He motions for Domenic to bring it, which he does, clubbing Pariah in the back of the neck with a forearm. Pariah tries to shake off the pain, but Mastadon quickly responds with a knee to the jaw. With Pariah on his knees, Mastadon hits the ropes, and goes for a running knee. But Pariah spots it and rolls out of the way, letting Domenic sail on past. Mastadon quickly turns around, but into a boot from Pariah, quickly followed up by a free willed tornado DDT! Steele: The self powered Tornado DDT is a move that requires the right amount of skill and speed, because you have to make the whole spin on your own. Hughes: Whatever, just wait for Mastadon to step up and kick the hell out of Pariah. Pariah brings up Mastadon to a standing base, and quickly whips him into the ropes. He goes for an elbow on the rebound, but Mastadon ducks and continues running. Pariah turns around, but gets tackled to the mat! Mastadon explodes with rights and lefts to the unprotected face of Pariah. Pariah is able to fight back though, and now has Mastadon on his back! Pariah goes to town with elbows more than fists, and ends the series with one crushing straight elbow to Mastadon’s eye! Pariah gets up, and then spits on Domenic before collecting his breath. Hughes: If I was Pariah I'd watch what I'm doing to Mastadon. Domenic is a very volatile man. Steele: Pariah knows what he's doing out there. He has a great sense of the ring around him. Mastadon gets up with death in his eyes. Well, in the one eye, as his left eye is now bloodshot, and covered in the crimson from a cut above his eye. He wipes the spit off of his forehead, and tangles up with Pariah. Before Pariah can even get a move off, Mastadon leans back, and then shoves Pariah down to the mat, causing the smaller man to actually roll into the corner! Mastadon charges over, and delivers rapid fire punches to the ribcage of Keith Summers. Pariah’s smaller frame shakes with each blow, and he is eventually left lying in the corner.
