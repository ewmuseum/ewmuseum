LCW Mercury Card Archive

Mercury Rising, 13 November 2001

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Mercury Rising - November 13th, 2001 - Coming Live from the Palace of Auburn Hills Fog is covering the screen, and sounds of a man grunting are heard. We zoom out, and it’s a person climbing a mountain. We zoom out further, and he’s actually climbing a modified Rising logo that looks like a mountain. He reaches the top, stakes his flag, and we zoom into the flag which turns into a video package set to "#1" by Nelly. After the package ends, we cut to the inside of the Palace, and pyro begins to shoot wildly from the large video screen hanging over circular entrance area, which leads to an elevated rampway. Two large light banks hang on the side of the entrance area, and multicolored beam lights fly wildly about the arena. And of course, the signs pop out! They range from "I’m The Dark Mistress!" to a large banner reading "The Harbinger Fan Club!" held up by a group of overweight very white men, and "Blaze is gonna roll you up and SMMMOKE you!". We go to the announce desk, where John Hughes and KC Steele are sitting, dressed in very formal attire. KC Steele: Welcome to Mercury Rising! The first Pay Per View put out by Dave Beaumont’s brain child promises to be a thriller! Tonight, we crown not only our first World Champion, but also our first North American Champion. John Hughes: Two men. One road. A very similar goal. Who will it be, Herbert Ingles, the man who has nothing to lose, or Pariah, one that seems to be destined for the gold, and has given everything he’s ever known in his life to this business that both men love and respect so much? Remember this date. Remember where you were. Because tonight, a legend will be made, and a dream will be shattered, and possibly, a career. Steele: And don’t forget the TSC feud boiling over into Cage Carnage tonight. That is why we have 2 rings here fan, as you can see on the screen. If our camera crew will look up, you will see the massive roofed cage that will cover these two rings later on tonight. Hughes: As well as The Trents taking on Entropy Crusade for the Tag Team Titles, and Kill Switch facing Legion to determine the number one contender for the Tag Team Titles! Match 1, Tag Team Match--Kill Switch vs Legion to determine #1 Contenders for the Mercury Tag Team Titles The arena goes completely dark, and a black light with a greenish tint to it fills the arena, giving it an unhealthy glow. KMFDM's "Mercy" plays, as rats scurry across the MercuTron, leaving the word "Legion" behind. As the arena lights come halfway up, a black light spot comes on, showing a crouched figure at the top of the entrance ramp, and a standing one. The crouched figure smiles, and the black light gives his teeth an eerie glow. He rises to his full height and the two men make their way to the ring as the arena lights come back up. Myrddin makes his way to ringside, slingshotting himself into a somersault that carries him over the top rope and into the ring, as Barabbas slowly steps in. We quickly cut over to the announce desk, where John Hughes is giving the men a standing ovation. Hughes: Gonna miss these guys. Steele: If they win, then they'll get a title shot in their first match back from LTD. That is, if they return. Hughes: Oh, they'll be back before you know it! The intense picking and palm muting of the "drop D" cord was roaring over the P.A. Everyone was left to comprehend the six note flurry in a "D" power cord that lasted a good two beats. The hard-hitting drum lines accompanied the speed picking the second time and it was known that Megadeth's "Black Curtains" was blaring. A live feed of Sloan making his way through a crowd is displayed on the MercuTron. He wears his usual black leather pants. A black short-sleeved shirt is tucked in and bound by is black, leather belt. He wears his black, jean jacket with his "So Far .. So Good .. So WHAT!?" logo. Slingshot storms down the ramp, and he hits the ring after steeping right over the top rope. He stands inside with his hands raised, and a massive bellow is released. He wears similar attire to Sloan's; black pants, black shirt. Steele: Former champions, whose only successful title defense came against Legion. Hughes: Lightning never strikes twice, and nobody beats Legion twice! Steele: You watched Pariah's promos, I see. Sloan mixes it up with Myrddin, throwing a lariat at the smaller man's head. Myrddin ducks and runs to the ropes, then comes back and ducks down suddenly with a spinning sweep kick. Sloan jumps over and throws an elbowdrop, but Myrddin rolls out of the way. Myrddin kips up and throws a short dropkick at Sloan's head when he gets up, but Sloan falls to his chest and Myrddin misses. Sloan gets up, Myrddin kips up, Sloan grabs Myrddin by the throat and tosses him to the Legion corner, motioning for Barabbas to come in! Barabbas doesn't hesitate an instant, charging at Sloan with a shoulderblock. Sloan ducks down, lifts Barabbas off the ground, and delivers an inverted atomic drop! Barabbas does the 'ow, my tailbone' dance, leaving him wide open for an exploder suplex! Barabbas gets right back up, so Sloan gives him another! Sloan boots Myrddin off the apron before diving down for the cover, one, two, Barabbas kicks out! Hughes: What tenacity! Steele: I'm more surprised by Sloan pulling off one of the harder suplexes out there on a man the size of Barabbas. He's not exactly Jason Bird. More like if Gravity ate Jason Bird, in fact. Hughes: Yeah! And that means that Barabbas has as much talent as Jason and Gravity combined! Steele: Sometimes I wonder about how your mind works. And I get very scared. Sloan pulls Barabbas into a standing headscissors and signals for a powerbomb. Barabbas blocks by grabbing Sloan's legs, then he shoves his way out. Sloan uses the shove to his advantage and runs to the ropes and back for a lariat, but Barabbas doesn't go down. Ropes, back, lariat, no dice. Ropes, back, lariat... ducked, and Barabbas fires off the Pilate side salto suplex, causing Sloan to do a cartwheel on his head! Barabbas tags in Myrddin, who by now has recovered from his earlier bump and is able to climb up the turnbuckle. Steele: Myrddin looking for a high-risk move. Hughes: Myrddin, about to beat Sloan just like he did at the first Fusion! Sloan is woozy when he gets up, and Myrddin has no problem in planting him with the Sodomizer swinging bulldog off the top! Myrrdin knocks Slingshot off the apron with the Silencer spinning wheel kick, and calls for Barabbas to come in. Despite Bo Smirnow's protests, Barabbas steps over the top rope and picks Myrddin up for a powerbomb. Myrddin then stands on Barabbas' shoulders as Sloan lies motionless on the canvas. Myrddin backflips into the Conjure frogsplash with dramatic results! Dramatic, as in, he missed. Smirnow forces Barabbas to get out, and he starts up a standing 10-count on the downed men. Steele: Slingshot hasn't been in this match yet, and even after taking the Silencer he's the fresh man. Hughes: But Barabbas is Barabbas, and he's sure to get the job done! Unable to stand, both Myrddin and Sloan crawl over to their respective partners for tags! Slingshot is a house of, oh wait, no he isn't; after five steps, Barabbas grabs him in a double-choke and plants him with the Crucifixion chokebomb! Barabbas covers, 1, 2, Sloan crawls over to break the tag, 3! Winners by Pinfall: Legion Steele: Wow. That was different. Hughes: The pain ain't over yet! Legion is gonna leave a lasting impression on Mercury! Barabbas stomps at Sloan until he's out of the ring. He then scoops Myrddin up and places him on a turnbuckle, telling him to 'get ready' while he slides out and produces... a table! Hughes: Oh, you just know that this will rule. Steele: Can we get a stretcher ready in the back? Barabbas sets the table up kitty corner a third of the way into the ring after dragging Slingshot close to the corner. Barabbas gets Slingshot up on his shoulders, and finally Myrddin is able to stand up. He looks at Slingshot, then the table, then Barabbas, and both men grin huge. Myrddin jumps off, catches Slingshot's head as Barabbas turns to help the move, and Myrddin pulls off a tornado DDT to take Slingshot off Barabbas and put him headfirst through the table! A "HOLY SH*T!" chant starts up, and sure enough, a stretcher is brought out to collect the remains of Slingshot. Hughes (wiping away a tear): That was so beautiful. Steele: And I don't think they're done! Barabbas is sending Sloan in for more of the same! Sloan is sent into the ring, but there's still fight left in him and he begins trading blows with Myrddin. Barabbas waffles him from behind with a steel chair, and out of nowhere he (Barabbas) takes a Quiet Riot courtesy of Mikel Trent! Josh takes Myrddin out with a slingshot missile dropkick! Sloan gets up in a daze, fists clenched. He looks angrily between Josh and Mikel, both of whom have their hands out in the 'don't hit us' sign. Mikel furthers this by extending his right hand towards Sloan. Hughes: Don't do it! They're the ones who took the titles from you! Steele: Why would the Trents reach out to Sloan? Is there something we don't see? Sloan's eyes narrow. He slowly reaches his hand out, and shakes Mikel's firmly. Very firmly. The handshake ends, and the Trents leave while Sloan is left in the ring with a lot on his mind. Steele: As the table is cleared away by the ring crew, let's take a look at the upcoming Ladder Match! We cut to a video package, and it starts with a shot of the North American title and inspirational, soft music set to it. But then, a ladder crashes onto the screen, and the song changes to "Sugar" by System Of a Down. The battle in promos is shown between Blaze and Memosarks, and how both men got to the ladder match. The package ends with a blurred shot of an unseen man grabbing a title off of a ladder, and then falling to the mat as we cut back to the arena. Hughes: The clown and the man whose name, in some circles, means to light up a doobie. Steele: Blaze McCoy is no stranger to gold, having been a LCW Hardcore champion before. But Memosarks has been waiting since Torn to get this shot, and he may just capitalize. Match 2, Ladder Match--Blaze McCoy vs Memosarks for the North American Championship The arena goes dark and the letters OutKast are shown one by one on the screen. Then the words scroll across the screen. The corners of the stage are set on fire and enormous flames run from both sides of the stage around to the front and then meet as Golden pyro explodes at the center of the ramp and "Outkast" by POD begins to blast throughout the arena. Shots of Blaze are shown in the ring executing the "Outkast" and the "Hook, Line and Sinker" moves, as well as many others. Blaze McCoy steps out from behind the curtain with his wife, the lovely Cherry Stacked. A mix of cheers and a few boos fill the arena. Blaze’s long brownish blonde hair is about shoulder length now and he is wearing a pair of dark sunglasses. Blaze has on a black singlet with gold flames on it with black wrestling boots with BLAZE and OUTKAST written down the sides. Cherry has long brown hair up in a pony tail and is wearing a red string bikini top, which her large breasts are bursting out of, and very short daisy dukes shorts with red high heels on. She is holding Blaze's fishing rod which is wrapped in barbed wire. They give the Steel City Salute on the ramp and walk to the ring. Blaze lifts Cherry over the top rope then leaps over the ropes and heads for the nearest turnbuckle, steps up to the top rope and stares at the crowd as he slowly takes off his sunglasses and gives the "thumbs up" with a smile, and then the "thumbs down" with a frown and another blast of flames are shot out of the other three ring posts. The crowd reacts once again with a mix of cheers and boos. Blaze hops down off the turnbuckle and hands Cherry his glasses, gets a kiss on the cheek and is ready for battle. Confetti starts to fall from the rafters over the crowd and big top music fills the arena, prompting Memosarks to appear in the entranceway, a big grin on his face. He slowly starts to walk to the ring, smiling at all the cameras as he goes. Hughes: Doink! Doink! Over here! Look here Doink! Steele: Shut up Hughes! That guy still tours the indies. With ladders laying at ringside, Blaze looks to get out there quickly. But Memosarks stops him by grabbing him around the waist, and jerking him down to the mat. Memosarks picks up Blaze and tosses him into the ropes, and catches him on the rebound with an elbow that dazes Blaze, but leaves him on his feet. Memosarks measures Blaze up quickly, and then performs a picture perfect flipping dropkick that sends Blaze to the mat. Memosarks picks Blaze back up, but Blaze knees Memo in the stomach, and then gives him a toss over the top rope! Blaze slicks his hair back, and then hops to the outside, along with Memosarks. Steele: A confident Blaze here. Hughes: A dorky Blaze. This guy is so cheesy. But I like him! Blaze picks up Memosarks by the hair, and brings his head down against the security rail, bouncing it off and sending Memosarks to the protective mat. Blaze stomps away at Memosarks, but then notices the ladder laying down. He looks at it with googly eyes, and then picks it up, raising it high above his head for all to see! Blaze slides the ladder in the ring, but is quickly rammed into the apron by Memosarks. Memo gets in a few shots, and then rolls Blaze into the ring. Memo props the ladder up in the corner, but is hit in the back of the head out of nowhere by Blaze, sending Memo face down into the ladder. Blaze backs up into the opposite corner, and gets a full head of steam before leaping into the air and delivering a flying hip splash that crushes Memosarks into the ladder! Blaze yells in joy, and then starts to smash Memo’s face into the ladder repeatedly. Blaze finishes by rubbing Memo's face in, and then peeling him off. Hughes: Great use of the ladder by McCoy! Steele: I’d expect the ladder to get used in a number of ways in this match. Blaze grabs the ladder out of the corner, and sets it up right below the North American Title high above the ring. Blaze gets about 4 rungs up, but Memosarks uses his feet to shake the ladder, and send it toppling over. Blaze leaps off just in time, saving himself from pain. Memosarks kips up, and intercepts a charging Blaze into a tilt-a-whirl side slam! Blaze bounces off the mat, and writhes around in pain as Memosarks sets the ladder up. Blaze gets up as Memosarks finally sturdies the ladder, but Memosarks does not stay sturdied as Blaze punches Memo in the stomach. Memo bends over for air as Blaze hops onto a shaky ladder’s second rung, then jumps down backwards, giving Memosarks a somewhat sloppy rocker dropper! Blaze rolls up, pumped up and ready to fight. Steele: A different McCoy then we’ve been seeing on TV lately. Blaze really wants that title, and boy can you tell. Memosarks gets up, holding his head, and narrowly ducks a Blaze superkick. Memosarks finishes his 360 turn, and then dives for a clothesline that sends Blaze back on his butt. But Blaze is right back up, and Irish whips Memosarks into the ropes. Memosarks leapfrogs as Blaze rebounds, but right behind Memo was the ladder, which Blaze collides into! Blaze staggers around, and then turns around into the open arms of ‘sarks. Memo kicks Blaze in the stomach, and pulls him up for a piledriver! Memosarks turns around, so the fallen ladder is behind him, and then drops down, piledriving Blaze’s head on top of the ladder! Blaze’s head bounces off, and he rolls around in pain as Memosarks begins to set the ladder up by the elevated rampway. With Blaze lying flat on his back, nearly out cold, Memosarks begins to scurry up the ladder, with his back to Blaze. Memo gets to the top, and raises his arms. But out of no where, Blaze kips up, and then shoves the ladder with Memo facing towards the ramp! The ladder plummets, flashbulbs pop, and the clown smacks the ramp face first! A loud "thud" is heard throughout the arena as Memosarks lays still on the ramp. Blaze goes to the elevated rampway, and picks up the ladder, which is teetering on the top rope, with him. He throws it down in front of the still motionless Memosarks, and then picks up the downed clown. Memo’s eyes are glared over, and blood trickles out of his nose as Blaze slaps him in the face. Memo doesn’t fight back though, and just gets another slap to the face. Blaze decides to lay off though as he lets Memo drop down to the ramp again so he can prop the ladder up against the ropes. Hughes: That fall...was just brutal. Steele: I wouldn’t be suprised if Memosarks has a broken nose from that. And that could affect his breathing here, hurting him in the long run. Memosarks is up as Blaze walks back to where he was laying, and is met by a fist. But Blaze shrugs it off though as he throws Memo towards the ladder. But Memo is able to actually run up the ladder, and then when he gets to the top of it, he drops down to his but, catapulting it up into the air, right into Blaze McCoy’s face! Blaze flops around in pain as Memo pulls the ladder into the ring, and sets it up. He begins his ascent up the ladder, and is within a fingertip of the title belt. But out of nowhere, Blaze McCoy comes sailing off the top rope and dropkicking the ladder over! Memosarks jumps just as the ladder goes flying, attempting to grab the belt. But he only plummets down, bouncing off the mat. Steele: Just when it looks like Memosarks has it, Blaze rips it away from him. Blaze gets up from the seated position, and begins to set the ladder up. But Memosarks wraps his arms around Blazes legs, preventing him from moving anywhere. Blaze tries to get away, but Memosarks takes Blaze down, and then kips up. He throws the ladder to the side, and starts violently stomping away at Blaze! Boots find their mark as Blaze rolls out of the way, and in front of the announce table. Blaze gets up and grabs a chair, and folds it up before sliding in the ring with it. But once he is in the ring, Memosarks is already up the ladder again, and reaching for the belt. Blaze drops the chair and quickly scurries up the ladder, looking to stop Memo. Blaze gets a fist in on Memo’s abdomen, doubling him over. Blaze gets up to the top rung, and pulls Memo partially up before bringing Memo’s hand between his own legs, and then jerking up for the Hook, Line, and Sinker! Blaze twists in midair as he jumps and slams Memo in the completion of Hook, Line, and Sinker, creating a "HOLY SH*T!" (part 2) chant all throughout the arena. Steele: My God, holy sh*t indeed! Hughes: I think the clown... is down. Blaze rolls off of Memosarks, and basks in the chant, looking all throughout the Palace of Auburn Hills. He turns around, and sets his eyes on the ladder. After it’s set up right below the belt, Blaze starts the slow climb, holding his ribs while blood drips down his face. He gets to the top, but Memosarks is stirring at the bottom. Blaze gets a hand on the belt, but a flying chair meets his face at the last moment, sending Blaze on a violent plummet to the mat! Memosarks looks on in glee as he now climbs up the ladder, slowly but surely. With the red face paint replaced with red blood, Memosarks reaches up with all energy left in his body and actually grabs hold of the belt, becoming the first North American Champion! Winner and NEW North American Champion: Memosarks Hughes: WHAT! NO! This is more bullcrap! This wasn’t supposed to happen! Balls! Steele: Memosarks, waiting for this match since August, makes it count, becoming our first North American champion! Memosarks scales down the ladder and holds the belt high in the air as he parades around the ring. He hops onto the middle buckle and raises the belt up high as fans throw confetti towards him. Memosarks hops down, and turns around right into a Blaze McCoy chair shot! Blaze spits on Memosarks, and then begins to pummel him on the mat. Cherry hops into the ring, hoping to stop him. She does manage to get him off of Memosarks, but not before he is able to grab the North American title and raise it up in the air. Cherry yells at Blaze, and he yells back as she storms out of the ring and to the back. Blaze looks on confused, but decides to just follow her, without the North American title, as big top music fills the arena and Chris Guzman attempts to wake up Memosarks. Hughes: God bless after match violence. And Blaze McCoy. Steele: Sore loser is what Blaze is. Hughes: But will The Trents be sore losers? Steele: I don’t see them losing, John. They’ve got the gold, and now it is their time to work with it, and show us all about their toughness. Match 3, Tag Team Match--The Trents vs Entropy Crusade for the Mercury Tag Team Titles The MercuTron video package shows a swirling black hole tinted blue as light dies. Silence as the image is shown, then blue flames erupt keying the intro to "Angels Holocaust" as Entropy Crusade make their way to the ring. Hughes: No way the goody-goody Trents overcome these two powerhouses. Steele: They beat Kill Switch, who are bigger than that. And I don't think Mikel is in a very pleasant mood. The Trent Brothers' video package begins with a close-up of Mikel Trent's face, his eyes closed, as the beginning of Drowning Pool's "Bodies" fills the arena. Mikel's eyes then open, and his face fills with anger and rage before the camera pulls back to show his elder brother Josh with a smile on his face. The feed then switches to a split screen as pyro erupts from the ramp and the brothers appear from behind the curtain. On the left of the MercuTron, Josh hits several high-flying moves and attacks enemies with weapons, while on the right, Mikel spears and pummels other opponents. As the brothers reach ringside, their video ends with a clip of Mikel powerbombing Josh through an opponent and a table. Steele: Mikel is going through a lot right now, and if he can sustain the effort that won the Trents the titles last week he just might be able to overpower both Dirge and Brimstone. Hughes: Fat chance. Entropy Crusade overcame huge odds against Chub School to get to this match, and they won't be deterred by the likes of Mikel Trent. Plus, informed sources tell me that EC has some tricks up their sleeves. And they have some surprises, too. The Trents slide in under the bottom rope, and immediately the challengers go to work with stomps to the back. Josh stays down, but Mikel gets up despite them and pounds away on Dirge with rights and lefts, then a high-angle released belly-to-belly suplex! Brimstone kicks Josh out of the ring, and Mikel and he face off at the sound of the bell. Now they exchange blows, ending when Mikel gives Brimstone a belly-to-belly to match the one he gave Dirge! Mikel covers, 1, Dirge dives over and breaks it. Dirge and Mikel get up at the same time, and Mikel takes a Greco-Roman eye rake before he can return to his punching. Dirge shoves Mikel over Brimstone, who is on all fours behind him, and the Entropy Crusade exchanges hi-signs. Steele: Josh Trent is just now stirring on the outside. If he doesn't stop this double-teaming soon, we could have new champions! Hughes: You mean, will have new champions more like. Mikel is pulled to his feet, and Dirge gives him a few more eye rakes much to referee Jason Sanders' disproval. Brimstone is set behind Mikel, and he tenses his right arm. In an instant, Brimstone spins and Dirge ducks down, and they connect with a simultaneous European Uppernut and Backhandwhich on Mikel! Hughes: Kiss 'em Goodbye, Mikel! Brimstone covers, and Sanders calls for the mic. Sanders: Mercury lets me call DQs off of low-blows at my disgression. Well, I say, you're DQed when you reach 3. That's one right there. Two more strikes and you're out! For the first time in his career, Sanders receives a pop. Brimstone stays with the cover, and now Sanders counts, one, two, Josh pulls Brimstone from the ring and dropkicks him down! Josh slides into the ring and dropkicks Dirge, then tosses him out as well! Josh tends to Mikel while the Crusade confers on the outside of the ring. Steele: Looks like the match is going to get a fresh start. Hughes: But can any man really recover from the effects of Kiss 'em Goodbye, the recently created Entropy Crusade team finisher? Steele: If anyone can, it's Mikel. Hughes: Is that a good thing, or a bad thing? KC shrugs. Josh and Dirge start us off the proper way, with Mikel and Brimstone finally going to their corners. Dirge throws a clothesline, but it's ducked and Josh nails him with a standing enziguri kick to the side of the head! Dirge wobbles on the verge of falling down, so Josh hooks him up for a snap suplex and takes him down before floating over into a cover, one, two, Dirge gets a shoulder up... and, accidentally, his arm shoots into Josh's groin in the process. Sanders yells at Dirge for the low-blow, but Dirge maintains that he didn't mean it while pulling a doubled-over Josh up to his feet. Dirge then puts him through the Henry David Thoreau cradle, getting one, two, Josh kicks out! Hughes: Ohhhh, and to think, he could have gotten the tag team titles with a trip to Walden Pond. Steele: Dirge does more in a match to ruin the sport than Skillz could in a year. Hughes: Hey, he gets the job done. More than I can say about your boys, New School. Dirge pulls Josh over in a doubled-over position to Brimstone, and tags out. Brimstone continues to hold Josh's head down, then begins to smack Josh in the face repeatedly with the top of his boot, followed by him pulling the leg back as far as he can in order to punt Josh's cranium as hard as he can to finish the Kicks of Intestinal Discomfort series. Josh flops backwards clutching his head, and Brimstone takes the opportunity to roll him over and slap on the Who's Yo Momma ankle lock! Josh grabs the ropes in a hurry, so Brimstone decides that it's a good time to chat with Sanders while Dirge pulls Josh's head over the bottom rope and chokes him out with it! Hughes: This is classic! Steele: It's a mockery. Hughes: You have no taste. Brimstone concludes his recipe swapping, and puts Josh through a vertical suplex, followed by a belly-to-back. Brimstone walks over to Mikel and flicks him in the right eye, then signals for Dirge to come inside the ring when Mikel attempts to enter (and is subsequently held back by Sanders). Brimstone drops an elbow on Josh, Dirge drops an elbow on Josh. Brimstone drops an elbow on Josh, Dirge drops an elbow on Josh. Brimstone gives Josh a body splash, Dirge stomps Josh on the throat, and by the time Dirge steps back through the ropes the crowd is calling for the heads of both members of Entropy Crusade. Mikel gets back on the apron and Brimstone hooks a leg, one, two, Josh kicks out! Steele: Josh showing great toughness. It looks like Mikel is almost foaming at the mouth waiting for that tag! Hughes: Hmmm. I feel like I've seen this before. Brimstone places Josh in a pumphandle, and lifts the smaller man up high for the Mourning Star inverted powerbomb. Josh escapes, however, and manages to take Brimstone down by swinging into an angled bulldog that sends Brimstone's face into Josh's knee! Dirge senses what's coming next, and he carefully steps between the ropes. Dirge then trips over a wrinkle in the space-time continuum, accidentally sending his left fist into the back of referee Sanders' head seven straight times. And if you don't believe me, just ask Dirge. Steele: Great, just what this match needed. Hughes: Textbook teamwork, KC. It's an artform, and these men are the masters of it! Mikel, seeing that Sanders is down, explodes out of the corner before Josh even reaches him for the tag. Mikel lifts Dirge over a shoulder, and plants him with a Quiet Riot spear slam! Mikel turns around, and Brimstone gives him a boot to the gut, followed by a powerbomb! Mikel rolls out to the apron, and Josh fires off a hurricanrana on Brimstone, but Brimstone blocks it and throws him down to his feet. Brimstone puts Josh in a choke, then lets go so that he can spin around and connect with a Backhandwhich on Josh just as Dirge gives him a low-blow! Josh falls like so much stone. Hughes: It's... ovah! Steele: Maybe not! Look who's coming! Rush, whom we have all been introduced to by the medium of promos, makes his way down the entrance ramp. Brimstone turns to yell at him, and Rush yells back... until two men run up and clock him from behind with a double-clothesline! Hughes: Yes! The Crusade is complete! Steele: Glamour and Picasso, two members of the stable who have yet to debut in Mercury, have taken Rush out of the equation! This could spell disaster for the Trents! Brimstone grins huge as Rush is stomped into oblivion, just as Josh sneaks two low-blows in on Dirge! Dirge falls down and goes 'boom', and Sanders finally wakes up just in time to see Josh tag in Mikel! Brimstone looks to see what the fans are making so much noise over, and he's promptly taken down with a Quiet Riot! Mikel yells at the top of his lungs, and pulls Dirge into a standing headscissors. Mikel lifts and drops Dirge with a powerbomb... on top of Brimstone! Rush takes a double-team brainbuster on the ramp as Mikel covers Brimstone for one, two, three! Winner by Pinfall with a modified Brotherly Love: The Trents Hughes: And here comes the cavalry! Picasso and Glamour run to the ring and hop over the top rope to begin a pounding on Mikel. They make progress in a hurry, but before they can drop Mikel with the double-brainbuster, both men are taken down by a steel chair, wielded by none other than Sloan! Steele: Sloan, apparently paying back the Trents for their save earlier tonight! Hughes: I'm having a hard time keeping up. Steele: You're not the only one. The crowd goes wild, many chanting "Pa-Ri-Ah! Pa-Ri-Ah!", and others just cheering the obvious finish. Steele: I don't believe this! The crowd is climbing the walls! I'm climbing the walls! Keith Summers has nowhere to go! Hughes: But what will give first, the hold, or Harbinger's patience? Pariah shakes his head repeatedly at Borashi's promptings, and Harbinger yells in frustration. The effort of maintaining the complex hold causes Harbinger's bleeding to act up again, and after a full minute Harbinger's grasp weakens, causing him to let go of Pariah's wrists. Harbinger stands up, walks to the ropes, and slumps over the top to rest while Pariah pants from complete exhaustion. Thirty seconds later, Harbinger goes back to work. Harbinger pulls Pariah up, but is surprised by a sudden burst of energy from his foe. Pariah laces into Harbinger's chest with right chops, alternating between knife-edge and open-handed. Steadily, the blows grow slower and weaker, to the point where Harbinger catches Pariah's wrist out of one, then floats over behind, wrapping the arm around Pariah's neck. Harbinger begins to stomp at the back of Pariah's knees, and once more the crowd gets into it. Steele: Harbinger is going for the wrist-clutch Dreadlock! At this point in the match there's no way Pariah could survive it! Hughes: *Ahem* Now sure would be a good time for Barabbas to run in, yessir. Steele: Give it up. Pariah goes to one knee, Harbinger kicks at the other, Pariah stands up before he's on both knees, refusing to give in to the hold. Harbinger snarls, and upon the third time of this happening he grabs Pariah's left arm in a chicken wing, keeps Pariah's right arm around his throat, and bridges back with a suplex that drops Pariah flush on his head! Steele: This is beyond unbelievable! A half-halo, half-tiger suplex, and Pariah will be lucky to not be paralyzed let alone win the match! Hughes: I wouldn't mind seeing him in a wheelchair after what he did to Apex. Steele: How dare you say that! Pariah's out there showing just how valuable the Mercury title is, and you're insulting him! Hughes: He- Steele: Shut up! Those two are giving everything they have, and you have no right to say anything. None. Hughes: Screw you. I've got my opinions, and just because Pariah wants to win it doesn't make me like him. Steele: Ass. Hughes: Jerk. Harbinger methodically walks over to where Pariah lays in a crumpled heap, and slowly pulls him into a standing position. Harbinger laces his left leg with Pariah's right and puts him in an ace crusher headlock, then falls forward to his knees with the Prelude. Now the entire arena stands on its feet, anticipating. Harbinger puts Pariah in an inverted front facelock, grabs his right arm, and cinches in... the Dreadlock! Steele: Listen to the explosion from this capacity crowd! Listen to the agony that Keith Summers is letting out! Can you even imagine the pain that he's enduring in his entire body? I hope he has the sense to tap; it's not worth it just for one match. Harbinger screams the word 'tap' at Pariah, but Pariah screams back 'never!' with all the breath he can manage. Harbinger pushes harder on Pariah's neck, the effort causing Harbinger's blood to stream over his face and redden his clenched, bristling teeth. Borashi checks Pariah by lifting up his free left arm, but Pariah's not out and he jerks it away, then starts to tug at Harbinger's left arm! The fans coax him on, but to no avail; Pariah's arm goes limp... seconds before Harbinger falls back, releasing the hold due to the strain it puts on him. Steele: No way! There's no way! Pariah just became the first wrestler to ever outlast the Dreadlock! Hughes: Pariah just won't tap, at least not to what Harbinger has. Steele: Don't say that just yet. Harbinger's doing the throat slash. Harbinger clears his face yet again of blood, and while visibly worn out he's in much better shape than Pariah, whom he takes and places facing the ring on the nearest corner. Harbinger climbs up, puts Pariah in a headlock, and pulls him into a standing position. A hush comes over the crowd. Steele: No, Herb! Don't do it! Hughes: Do what? Steele: Punk-Out 5.0! It's what won him the Y-1 tournament, and after all that Keith's gone through it just might kill him! Hughes: Cool. KC gives him a backhand to the nose, which causes John to yelp in pain. Harbinger lifts, but Pariah blocks the superplex. Harbinger does a palm thrust to the left kidney and lifts again, but Pariah still blocks it. Harbinger releases the headlock and throws a hard palm thrust to Pariah's face, but Pariah blocks it and begins to rain punches on Harbinger! The crowd cheers the action, and then those cheers are tripled when Pariah knees Harbinger in the gut, turns around, and hooks his arms! Steele: Nausea off the top! This move almost single-handedly turned Aaron Antioch from an Apostle into a Lost man back in April! Pariah tries to turn Harbinger over, but the effort stretches his back just enough to make him stop. Pariah twists again, and this time Harbinger sneaks his left arm out and elbows Pariah in the lower back, causing him to shudder from the pain. Harbinger pulls out his other arm and wraps both around Pariah's chest, and then he jumps back and nearly commits homicide by delivering a spine-shattering super backdrop driver! Harbinger very, very slowly stands up, and looks down at the twitching, gelatinous Keith Summers. Steele: It's gone too far. Please let it end quickly. We really don't need to see a career end like this. Hughes: Pariah's a cockroach. As much as you'd want to, you can't kill him. KC throws a backhand. John ducks. Harbinger rolls Pariah onto his back, then puts him in an inverted front facelock and pulls him to a kneeling position. Harbinger carefully puts his feet together, between Pariah's legs, and grabs Pariah's right arm. Harbinger leans back, bending Pariah's body over his knees such that Harbinger literally hangs from Pariah's head and arm! Pariah lets out a bloodcurdling cry of unimaginable torment, and he taps his left shoulder to signal his submission. Winner by Submission to the Master Dreadlock, and NEW Mercury Champion: Harbinger Steele: Herbert Ingles has done it! The pasty white boy from Rochester, New York has won the big one! Harbinger lets go, falling flat on his back. This causes Pariah to whiplash forward on his face, leaving him gasping for air. Referee Bobby Borashi checks on both men, prodding them to look for signs of life. Harbinger stirs, and Borashi hands him the title before turning his full attention to Pariah. Harbinger gets up on his knees and hugs the championship to his chest while looking at the man he forced into unconsciousness. Hughes: I can’t believe it! What a match, what a give and take match, and how it ended! I salute those two men. And I don’t do that often. Tears wash over Harbinger's bloodied face as he raises the belt up high, and falls to his knees, crying. Golden sparks blast from the rooftop, and "Playground for a Wedgeless Firm" starts up again as Harbinger makes his way to the back, belt firmly around his waist. He turns around, and points at Pariah, who is now up and leaning on the ropes. Pariah looks on as Harbinger stops pointing, and then points at the belt as fans in "Da’ Palace" give the two men a standing ovation.
