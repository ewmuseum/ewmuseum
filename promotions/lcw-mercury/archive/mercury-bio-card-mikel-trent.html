LCW Mercury LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Mikel Trent Real Name: Mikel Trent Born: Feb. 3, 1983 Place of Birth: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Hometown: Height: 6'6 Weight: 280 Blood type: AB+ Eye color: Blue (Changes with moods.) Team: The Trent Brothers Trainer: Josh Trent Theme song: "Cold" by Static X Wrestler Style Wrestling style: Power Brawler Signature moves: "The Quiet Riot"- a Spear slam. "Immanent Failure"- Bear hug w/headbutts Double Handed Choke Powerbomb. Gorilla Press to Front Powerslam. Belly-to-belly flip slam. High Angle German-suplex. Gutwrench fall forward powerbomb. Tag Finisher: Brotherly Love (Josh is powerbombed onto opponent by Mikel.) Finisher: Reverse Brainbuster DDT Finisher name: Inner Fury Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: None Other/Previous teams: None Other/Previous managers: None Other Wrestling titles won: None Other Wrestling awards won: None Biography Mikel was born on February 3, 1983. He was the second child born to a seemingly happily married couple. The couple was young, and seemed warm on the exterior. However after a year or two, things changed. The loving husband and father, that the Trent family once knew, changed to a belligerent drunk. Joshua Trent sr. began beating his wife. When Mikel was three years of age, Josh sr. caught his wife cheating on him with another man, the other man had blue eyes, blonde hair, and pale skin. After several blood tests were performed it was evident that Mikel was not Joshua's son. Because Mikel was not his son, Joshua sr. began to beat him. When Mikel's mother would try to protect him, she got beat as well. Mikel grew up with a chip on his shoulder, along with a high threshold for pain. The only other person in which Mikel connected with besides his mother, was his older brother Josh. The pair stuck up for each other every chance they got. If someone was picking on Josh in school, they had to deal with Mikel. Everytime Joshua sr. would take his anger out on Mikel, Josh would speak up, and take a verbal beating from his father for Mikel. In High School Mikel was obviously not the poster child for the honor student. He got into fights almost every other day. He would have gotten expelled, had it not been for the fact that Mikel was very respectful to adults. Mikel seldom raised his hand, or answered questions verbally. The beatings continued, although they were less severe, because Mikel was growing at a fast pace, and was bigger than his "father" by the time he was a Freshman. One fateful night something happened which would change the course of The Trent brother's lives forever. The events of this night are known only by Josh jr. Josh sr did not live to tell about it. Mrs. Trent disappeared shortly afterwards, around the time she took Josh jr, and Mikel out of school. Mikel blocked the entire events out of his mind. To this day Mikel is continuing to try and find out the truth behind his past.
