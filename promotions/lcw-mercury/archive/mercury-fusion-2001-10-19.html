Fusion, 19 October 2001

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Mercury Fusion - Live, From the Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio - October 19, 2001 Disturbed's "Shout 2000" plays as the entrance video starts with a shot of the Mercury Fusion logo. (SHOUT!) Pariah makes Apex scream in a submission (SHOUT!) Harbinger celebrates with his Universal Title (Let it all out) Clips from various athletes in pain are seen (These are the things I can do without) And Toh lariats somebody right out of his boots (Come on) G Slate bitches somebody out in a interview (I'm talking to you) And Jerry Cane drops a former LCW performer with the T for Texas (So come on) The Trents perform some Brotherly Love (In violent times...) Dave Beaumont flashes a sinister grin (You shouldn't have to sell your soul) The goofy Memosarks throws confetti at the crowd (In black and white...) TR Saxton mangles some unseen person with a cheese grater (They really, really ought to know) Gravity drops Brian Shotgunn through some tables (Just don't know) Apex looks out into space after a win. (Those wandering minds...) Harbinger lashes out at anyone and everyone in a promo (Who took you for a working whore) a shot of the newcomers Entropy Crusade talking is seen (Kiss them goodbye...) And The New School destroys The Birds at Torn (They shouldn't have to jump for joy) a table is broken by Gravity (Jump!) Pariah dents a chair over Rook Black's head (Jump!) The Trents beat up unsuspecting people (Jump!) Memosarks and Harbinger do battle (Jump!) And TR Saxton hits the D.O.I (Jump!) Harbinger wrestles in Japan (You shouldn't have to shout for joy) Pariah screams (SHOUT!) Beaumont addresses the workers of LCW Mercury (They give you life...) Gravity clutches a World Title (...and in return you gave them hell!) G Slate smiles under a crimson mask (As cold as ice) Pariah throws Pestilence off of the scaffold at Suicide (I hope we live to tell the tale) and the final clips of various wrestlers hitting their finishers are seen (I hope we live to shout the tale!) The inside of the Civic Center is seen, as the thousands of fan erupt, and a fireworks display engulfs the entrance area, and golden sparks shoot from the word FUSION, hanging above. As the smoke from the pyro clear, the Fusion entrance stage becomes visible. From the top of the building the word "Fusion" hangs, spelt out in metal girders. A circular metal girder with swirling lights dangling from it hangs below this Fusion, and two fair sized video screens, called the MercuTrons, hang on opposite sides of the Fusion. Below Fusion is the entrance area, which consists of two large metal girders that lead from the top of the ramp up to the U and O in Fusion. For their entrances, the wrestlers will walk out between the girders, and in front of the Mercury Fusion logo, which is built into the stage, and in between the large steel entrance girders. The elevated ramp way, which is covered in dull gray carpet, leads us right to the ring! The cameras make the traditional scan of the fans, who are proudly showing off their signs. They range from "I Like Nachos" to "Entropy: Resistance is Futile" and finally "Where's Johnny Calzone?". We then cut to the announce desk, where KC Steele, in a fleece "Mercury" pull over on, sits next to John Hughes, who is wearing a black button down "Fusion" shirt. KC Steele: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Gund Arena in Cleveland Ohio! Tonight, the finalists of the tournament will be decided, and- "Iron" John Hughes: And Legion will become the first-ever 3-time champions when they beat down Sloan and a loser of his choice! Guns 'n Roses' "Out ta Get Me" signals the entrance of Mercury's Vice President, and Commissioner, the big guy himself, Bruce DeAula. He gets a mixed reaction, though sadly much of it is due to his lack of airtime. Bruce steps over the top rope and calls for the house mic. Bruce: Hello everyone. I wish my first appearance on Fusion could have been under happier circumstances, but, it's not. You see, there's someone backstage who I need to talk to, and I want to make sure the world sees it. Beaumont, I know you want to go easy on him because he didn't sink LCW when he headlined twice, but I couldn't care less about drawing power when it comes to Apex. Get out here, now! "Psalm 69" playing is all the intro that Apex gets. He walks down the ramp, but does not enter the ring. Bruce: Ignacio, I'm tired of writing out the form that fines you for testing positive for steroids. I'm also tired of having a wrestler who seems more like a vagabond than a fighter in Mercury. I'm telling you right here, in front of millions of people around the world, that you're about 'this' far for getting fired altogether! DeAula makes the "two fingers very close together" motion for the 'this' part of his dialogue. 'Fired' gets a decent pop. Bad things happening to Apex = ratings. Bruce: Someone get him a mic. Apex takes the quickly produced mic, but doesn't speak. Bruce: Look at you. You don't even want to defend your actions. How can you stand to allow yourself to deteriorate like this? Apex sneers at the almost-as-tall-as-him DeAula. Apex: I do what I want. This is a free country, right? The crowd heckles Apex for his snide remark. That segues into a sizable pop when "Playground for a Wedgeless Firm" blasts for the entrance of Harbinger, why stands at the mouth of the entrance. Bruce: Maybe you can talk some sense into him. I've got better ways to use my time. Harbinger: Hold on there big man, there's something you can do to help. See, the way I figure it, you and Beaumont owe me for being Mercury's cheerleader while I was winning Y-1 over in Japan. So, how's about this. You give me a match with Ignacio next week, and we'll see if I can't persuade him to shape up or ship out. Bruce: That's fine by me. Apex: Hold. Bruce: Oh, now you want to add something constructive to the conversation? Apex turns to face Harbinger. Apex: If you all want me gone, and if the only person who wants me to wrestle is God, then I'll leave if you beat me... on one condition. If I win, then not only will I stay, but Harbinger will return my mask to me. Harbinger shifts from side to side, thinking. Harbinger: You're on. It's your mask against you getting the hell out of Mercury. The crowd pops huge for the now-set match. Steele: That could be tremendous! Not only do Apex and Harbinger have a storied history, and there's pay-per-view level stuff on the line! Hughes: *coughshillcough* Steele: What was that, John? Hughes: I said...Brimstone will face Memosarsk! Steele: Well, no matter what you said, he will indeed face Memosarks here tonight as we kick the evening off to start another great Fusion! Match 1, Brimstone vs Memosarks The MercuTron video package shows a swirling black hole tinted blue as light dies. Silence as the image is shown, then blue flames errupt keying the intro to "Angels Holocaust" as Brimstone makes his way to the ring. Hughes: Go big man, go big man, go big man! Confetti starts to fall from the rafters over the crowd and big top music fills the arena, prompting Memosarks to appear in the entranceway, a big grin on his face. He slowly starts to walk to the ring, smiling at all the cameras as he goes. Steele: Memosarks better watch it out there! As Memosarks throws confetti at the fans from the rampway, Brimstone suddenly attacks from behind with an axehandle smash that sends Memosarks down to the ramp. Brimstone laughs at the fallen man, but Memosarks kicks him in the shin, and gets up. Memosarks goes to town with fists, but Brimstone absorbs the punishment, and places his hand on Memosarks face, shoving him off the 5 foot high ramp and into the crowd! Memosarks lays hurt among the fans, in a crumpled pile of chairs. Brimstone hops off the side, in between the ramp and the rail, and pulls Memosarks over with him. He drags him between the ramp and rail to the ring, and finally throws Memosarks into the ring. Hughes: That was ugly. Steele: Better the fans than you and me though, huh? Brimstone rolls in, and pounces on the hurting Memosarks. Brimstone delivers a massive blow to Memosarks ribs, and then finishes it off with a punishing elbow to the back of his neck. Memosarks falls to a knee, and tries to get his breath back. But Brimstone picks him up, whips him into the ropes, and turns him inside out with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Brimstone goes for a pin and gets 1, 2, kick out, just barely, from Memosarks. Hughes: See KC, it’s obvious here that Brimstone is a better athlete than Boink The Clown. Steele: Memosarks is a great athlete John. He just prefers to make us all laugh. Hughes: And I prefer to gamble. What's your point? Brimstone gently nudges Memosarks with his foot, and laughs at the smaller man. But out of nowhere Memosarks kips up, and delivers rapid fire punches to Brimstones stomach, backing the big guy to the ropes. Memosarks backs up, and shuffle sidekicks Brimstone right in the jaw. Brimstone teeters on the rope, almost going out, but barely staying in. Hughes: That one could have been nasty. Memosarks throws Brimstone into the corner, and charges at him. But when he leaps for the splash, Brimstone extends his hand, and catches Memosarks around the throat! He hoists him into the air with one hand, and drops him with a chokeslam! Brimstone makes the cover and gets 1, 2, kick out by Memosarks! Steele: Love him or hate him.... Hughes: Hate him. Steele: ....Brimstone is pure talent in that ring. Memosarks though showing his grit by not getting pinned. Hughes: Whoops. Memo rolls over onto his back as Brimstone mouths off at Guzman for what he considered a slow count. Brimstone grabs Memosarks by the hair, and pulls him up. But Memo elbows him in the stomach, bounces off the ropes, and goes for a spinning wheel kick! Instead, in mid-kick, Brimstone is able to catch him, hoist him up for a powerbomb, and drop him down to the mat! Brimstone covers and this time gets the 3! Hughes: Memosarks showing his stupidness by getting pinned. All hail Brimstone! Winner via Pinfall: Brimstone Steele: Brimstone with another decisive win there. Up next though Josh Trent looks to beat Scott Tibor in a series of matches that has been going on since August, when the Trents and Graduates did battle for the first time. Hughes: Scott Tibor? Josh Trent? Who are they. Steele: Well, they are... Hughes: It Doesn’t Matter Who They Are! (A clang is heard, and John Hughes looks under the desk) Damn...I dropped the flask. Steele: AGAIN! Match 2, Scott Tibor vs Josh Trent An outline of the state of Texas is branded on the video screen, and Toby Keith's "How Do You Like Me Now" bursts into the main chorus of the song, skipping the intro of it. Bright white lights scan the entrance area, as Jerry Cane and Scott Tibor walk out from the back, and stand side by side at the beginning of the entrance ramp. Scott adjusts his knee brace, and the two men nod at each other before coming down to the ring. Steele: Fans, I’ve just gotten word over my headset that the winners of this matchup will recieve a tag team title shot with his partner next week on Fusion! The Trent Brothers' video package begins with a close-up of Mikel Trent's face, his eyes closed, as the beginning of Drowning Pool's "Bodies" fills the arena. Mikel's eyes then open, and his face fills with anger and rage before the camera pulls back to show his elder brother Josh with a smile on his face.The feed then switches to a split screen as pyro erupts from the ramp and the brothers appear from behind the curtain. On the left of the MercuTron, Josh hits several high-flying moves and attacks enemies with weapons, while on the right, Mikel spears and pummels other opponents. As Josh reachs ringside, the video ends with a clip of Mikel powerbombing Josh through an opponent and a table. Hughes: Oh, these two. Steele: Why do I put up with him.... Josh and Scott start the match by circling each other, and the locking up in the dead middle of the ring. They jockey for position, and Scott wins by pushing Josh down to the mat, and on his back. But Josh kips up, and they circle again. Josh quickly goes for his spot this time though, and gets it by doing a leg sweep on Scott. Scott falls to his back, Josh rolls up, and sprints to the ropes. But Scott is up, leapfrogs over the running Josh, and catches him on the rebound with a floatover armdrag. Scott gets right up after the release, as does Josh with another kip up! The two men stare each other in the face, and get a standing ovation for the lightning opener. Hughes: Well that was...wow. Steele: A quick sequence of events to get this one underway. You can tell the competition is thick here in Mercury with every man wanting to show us his all. They lock up again, but Scott pulls out a knee that doubles over Josh. Scott grabs a headlock, and cinches in. Josh wriggle to get free, and he does, pushing Scott into the ropes. Scott runs off, but Josh puts him down with a shoulderblock. Scott rolls up and hits the ropes again, and Josh goes for another shoulderblock. But this time Scott gets a hold of one of Josh’s arms, and wraps around for a crucifix DDT! He connects, and goes for 1, 2, kick out by Trent. Hughes: Nice wrestling there by the one from Texas. Steele: *ahem* SCOTT TIBOR *ahem* is a gifted athlete, as is *ahem* JOSH TRENT *ahem*. Josh gets to his feet, a bit groggy. But he walks into Scott Tibor, who hops onto his shoulders out of nowhere, and spins under for a snap release hurricanrana! Trent nearly slides out of the ring, but stops right by the ropes. He gets up holding his head, and almost gets decapitated by a standing spin kick from Scott Tibor. But Josh ducks, Scott makes a full rotation, and is met with a boot to the gut once he stops. Josh puts Scott's head into a headscissors, and then lifts him up for a piledriver! Josh holds him there for a bit, and then drops down, bouncing Scott's head off the mat. Josh goes for a pin and gets 1, 2, and...a kick out by Tibor. Hughes: Tibor kicks out! Steele: Wow. You got it right. I’ll have to get your flask filled during a commercial break. Tibor and Trent get up, with Scott holding his head. He leans on the ropes for support, but only gets dropkicked over the top rope by Josh Trent! Scott flies over and smacks his head off the apron, and bounces back into the guardrail. Josh picks his spot here, and hits the ropes, leaps onto the top one, and performs a springboard cross body block that sends him and Scott right into the rail! Josh gets off of Scott, and pulls the fellow former tag team champion up. Josh then whips Scott down to the rail, right by the elevated rampway. Steele: The men mixing it up on the outside here. Hughes: I love when they do this. Josh runs down, and tries for a splash. But Scott catches him in midair, and runs towards the edge of the ramp, ramming Josh’s lower lumbar into it. Josh lies on the mat, holding his back, but Scott hops onto the apron, and up to the top rope. He looks at Josh, who is now standing, and then leaps off for a flipping senton splash! Josh turns around, only to find Scott flying at him. So Josh sidesteps, and lets Scott sail towards the fans, who have now moved out of the way, not wanting the same thing that happened earlier with Brimstone and Memosarks to happen again. But Scott stops just short of the fans, his back bouncing off the edge of the rampway, and his knee, the injured one, sickeningly cracking on the rail. Josh picks up Scott, and shoves him into the ring. Steele: That reminds me so much of the devastating injury Pheonyx had suffered last year. If Scott Tibor isn’t careful, he could wind up with a fake knee as well. Hughes: But they are PAID to entertain us like that. Besides, who cares about Andy Griffith up there anyways? With Scott laying prone on the mat, Josh hops onto the apron, and climbs up top. But Scott rolls backwards, to his feet, and shakes the top rope! Josh loses his balance, and falls off the top rope, and onto the apron! Josh’s arms and legs hang off the edge, and he barely moves as Scott Tibor places his hands on the top rope, and springboards over, turns in midair, and performs a sloppy looking leg drop to the back of Josh’s neck and upper back. Scott falls back to the mats, holding his braced knee in pain. He does manage to get up, and nudge Josh into the ring. Hughes: What a dodo! He just injured his knee on the outside, so he does a legdrop? Scott rolls in also, and picks up Josh. He whips him into the ropes, but Josh doesn’t let go of the said arm, and brings Scott to him. He kicks Josh in the stomach, and slams him into the corner. He hoists Scott up onto the top rope, and gets onto the second rope as well. He looks to superplex Scott, and he does. But Scott is able to fall on top of Josh in midair, and land for a pinfall! But the momentum carries, and Josh is able to roll over onto Scott for the 1, 2, 3! Winner via Pinfall: Josh Trent Hughes: That came out of nowhere. Steele: Yeah...it did. Hughes: Uh... Steele: Uh..... Hughes: *Psst* Next match Ken! Next match! Steele: Oh, ah, uh, yeah! Geoffrey Slate looks to gain some more respect as he takes on the fallen Apex. Hughes: Fallen? Well, yeah, I suppose. Damn, Apex sucks now. Steele: Agreed. Match 3, Apex vs Slate Ministry's "Psalm 69" fills the arena, and the name "Apex" flashes on the MercuTron, followed by shots of mountain climbers ascending the great peaks of the world. As they reach the tops of the mountains, the feed cuts to a shot of the Jonestown mass suicide and as the cameraman makes his way along the haphazard bodies, the camera looks up and there is Apex. He smiles, and then clips of him ending careers are shown. As he walks down the ramp, red streams of pyro shoot up from the ramp all around him. Steele: Apex, fresh off a tongue-lashing from Mercury's VP and Commissioner Bruce DeAula, seems to be on his way to making a losing steak just as long as his unbeaten streak from September to February upon his debut. Hughes: Yeah, like he's losing to this loser. Fromthe stage a large blast of white pyro shoots just above the heads of the fans. Disturbed's "Violence Fetish" heralds the entrance of Geoffrey Slate; on a pitch-black MercuTron appears the word "Devastation", which quickly fades into a standard entrance video featuring a splicing of stills and clips which is made special primarily by the ring of fire encircling it. Slate walks coolly and calmly to the ring, barely recognizing the fans on the way. Steele: This is Slate's debut in Mercury, and it's been a while since we've seen him. Hughes: Is he still in TSC 2 or what? Steele: I have a feeling we'll find out before too long. Apex stares Slate down, showing no emotion at all. Slate shifts around, looking for an opening somewhere on Apex's sizable frame. Without warning, Slate catches Apex with a standing sidekick to the gut to double him over, followed by repeated rights and lefts to the face. Apex slowly stands back up, his stare unchanged. Slate has a 'damn' moment, then gives Apex two kicks to the chest. Slate runs to the ropes and charges in with a jumping clothesline, only to be met by a stiff rolling elbow smash to the head that knocks him out of the air! Apex throws a standing senton splash on the now-down Slate and hooks a leg, one, two, Slate gets a shoulder up with a little room to spare. Hughes: At least Apex is trying here.Steele: But his hunger is gone. I just cannot understand how a former world champion can lose his love for this business.Hughes: I blame Pariah and his traitor ways. Apex starts to do a naked choke on Slate, which is half broken by referee Jason Sanders and half by Slate curling up and kicking Apex repeatedly in the side of the head. Apex skulks off by order of Sanders, so that Slate can catch his breath. Geoffrey slowly gets up, only to have Apex run over and crack him with a knee to the side of the head! Apex jumps into a standing senton, but Slate gets his knees up and Apex howls in pain! Steele: Almost exactly a year ago, I can remember Lex Laww doing that and having Apex shrug it off. How times have changed. Hughes: Well, Lex is no Geoffrey Slate. Slate gets up gingerly, since the force of Apex crashing down on his knees hurt a bit; still, Slate got the best of it, and he's able to pull Apex up while standing behind him for a textbook hangman's neckbreaker. Slate gets up, Apex gets up... and falls flat on his face. Slate grins in triumph, sensing that victory is near. He pulls Apex up in a side headlock and lifts for a vertical suplex, but Apex plants his feet. Slate sends a few shots to his abdomen and tries again, no dice. Slate tries jumping into it, but the action of jumping only helps Apex heft Slate into a delayed vertical suplex of his own! Steele: This may be the end of the losing streak for Apex! Apex ceases all movement, and Slate has a hard time rolling him onto his back for a 1, a 2, and a 3! TR: Blaze, get out here right now so I can finish what got started back when I kicked your ass out of this fed!
