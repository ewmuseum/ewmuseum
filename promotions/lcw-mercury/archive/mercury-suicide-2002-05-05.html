Suicide, 5 May 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Mercury Pay-Per-View LCW Mercury Suicide - 5.5.02. - Live from the Delta Center in Salt Lake City, Utah The show begins on a shot of one man’s hand, bullets lying inside the palm. The Suicide logo flashes, and then explodes onto the screen as Creed’s "Bullets" begins to play! Pariah holds the Mercury World Title aloft, and is destroyed by Oblivion in their first television appearance (I think they shoot cause they want it) A montage of the entire Merc roster hitting their signature moves is shown. (Walking around I hear the earth seeking relief) Blaze McCoy and Inferno shake their heads after another tag team loss (I'm trying to find a reason to live) The Trent Brothers destroy Vincent Picasso (But the mindless clutter my path) Oblivion stands united (Oh these thorns in my side) Dirge raises the North American Title (I know I have something free) The Elite hoist the Tag Team straps... (I have something so alive) Which are destroyed by Mikel Trent (I think they shoot cause they want it) Harbinger and Jin Akujin squre off (I feel forces all around me) Magnum Wolf and Gol make their presence felt (Come on raise your head) Hollow crushes Dave Beaumont (Those who hide behind the shadows) Robert Young locks in the Hangover 2002 (Live with all that's dead) A five on five brawl between EEI and Oblivion is shown (Look at me! Look at me!) Pariah takes the Fall from Grace from Gol (At least look at me when you shoot a bullet through my head) Magnum Wolf hits the Alpha Driver on Geoffrey Slate (Through my head!) Slate scores with Penance on Wolf (Through my head!) Pariah hits Gol with Nausea (Through my head!) Blaze McCoy and Inferno do battle (In my lifetime when I'm disgraced) Akujin reverses the Punk Out 5.0 (By jealousy and lies) Brimstone and Johnny Glamour make their return (I laugh aloud 'cause my life) Sickness reveals herself as the leader of Oblivion as Dave Beaumont looks on (Has gotten inside someone else's mind) The Trents and The Elite brawl (Look at me!) Akujin crushes Harbinger with a Ryuujin Driver (Look at me!) Gol nails Picasso with a Sydney Superkick (At least look at me when you shoot a bullet through my head) Pariah lands Society's Problem on Gol (Through my head!) Mikel Trent kills Pariah with the Quiet Riot (Through my head!) The Suicide logo bursts through the screen, framed by the Oblivion and EEI logos (Through my head!) Red pyro explodes throughout the arena, as red showers reign down from the heavens above the ring! Once the residue from the pyro clears away, the entrance stage becomes visible. At the top of the ramp are two larger than life right triangles constructed by steel girders, and inside the triangles is the Suicide logo. In between the right triangles is the entrance area where the wrestlers walk out of. The Suicide logo is of course built into the staging area between the triangles, and above all this sits a huge trapezoid shaped video screen. The camera makes the scan of the fans, spotting some creative signs. They range from "Pariah = Suicide Solution" to "We’ll Miss You Blaze!"and finally "Where’s Felony?" held up by a middle aged man holding a cheese steak. As the cameres pull away from the fans, "Bullets" hits again, bringing out "Iron" John Hughes and KC Steele to the announce area! KC Steele: Welcome one and all to beautiful Salt Lake City, Utah, for LCW Mercury Suicide! Every title is on the line, and this promises to be one spectacular show! "Iron" John Hughes: What’s going to happen in the main event? Can Dirge hold off a spunky Robert Young? Will The Elite manage to keep a very long and healthy reign going? Will Blaze McCoy leave Mercury on his own terms? Steele: That and many more questions answered tonight! But right now, I understand we’re going to kick off our show with the special Mixed singles match, as Dave Beaumont takes on Sicknes! It’s Mercury’s highest man of power versus Oblivion’s highest...thing of power. Match 1, Mixed Singles Match: David Beaumont vs Sickness "Struggle Within" hits, and a video plays on the MercuTron with "David Beaumont" doing Matrix-style kicks with Matrix-style camera tricks. Beaumont himself walks out in a sleeveless tux, to more cheers than he's ever gotten in his entire life. Hughes: The boss is over? Wow, they hate Sickness even more than I thought. "Giving In", by Adema plays across the sound system. Sickness enters, wearing a well supported black sports bra, and shiny silver colored tights, and sturdy looking black boots. Her long red hair is tightly braided into two small buns at the back of her head. She stands at the ramp and holds out her arms, palms up. Pyrotechnical bursts explode on either side of her. Sickness saunters towards the ring. She climbs up the steps and walks along the apron, blows a kiss to the ramp, then steps through the ropes. Steele: We all saw what Sickness pulled off last week on Fusion, and back in the early days of LCW. Let's see if she picked up any new tricks hanging around Harbinger and the Oblivion. Beaumont and Sickness slowly start to circle each other. After some hesitation, they go into a collar and elbow tie-up. Sickness spins into a twisting arm wrench, bending Dave over, then drops an elbow onto Dave’s shoulder. Sickness has a good laugh, as Dave favors the shoulder slightly and gets a ticked expression. Beaumont shouts at her to “bring it on” and they lock up again. Steele: Sickness looks to have about three inches in height. That could prove a big advantage. Hughes: I bet it's the heels. Beaumont should have countered by wearing pumps. Dave gets the advantage as he shifts the tie up into a side headlock, he grinds it in with some satisfaction, but then looks as if he’s not sure what’s happening as Sickness backs up against the ropes and uses the moment to throw off the hold. Dave manages to get his arm up in time to bounce off the ropes, then runs right into a high monkey flip. Dave clutches his back as he rises. Sickness slides right up to him, patting his back asking if he’s okay. He gives a nod and before he realizes what’s happening, Sickness licks his ear, then takes him right back down with a side Russian leg sweep. Steele: Solid technical wrestling from Sickness, much as we expected. Dave howls as he holds his back, but he rises regardless. He makes a throat slash motion towards Sick and charges with a violent lariat, Sickness ducks and launches a boot into Dave’s gut as he spins around. She puts him in a standing headscissors and grabs onto the back of Dave’s pants, yanking him over into a pulling piledriver. Sickness flexes with some pride. Hughes: Wow, Sickness is lookin' GOOD. Steele: That rose has a lot of thorns, John. And the thorns have thorns. Sickness mounts Dave and raises her right hand high, then unloads with a wicked slap across his face. She then gives him another hard backhand across the temple. She grabs a hold of Dave’s head and slams his head hard against the mat. Sickness stands up over Dave, looks down him, then jumps and drops a knee right into his chest. She starts to taunt, and Dave finally responds by shoving her knee of his chest. He gets to his feet slowly, and his face is a bright red. Hughes: He's bossing up! Steele: He paid you to say that. I have proof. Sickness smiles at Dave and begins circling for another tie-up, but Dave has none of it, and with surprising quickness, steps up and clocks her upside the head with a hard right. Sickness looks at him with some measure of surprise as she holds her left temple. She backs away from Dave, but not quite fast enough as he moves in and gives her a hard left body blow. Sickness doubles over and Dave yells, “You like that, bitch?” He grabs onto her arm and whips her hard into the turnbuckle. Dave charges in and Sickness narrowly rolls out of the way as he slams into the turnbuckle hard. Warily, Sickness delivers a hard high kick to the back of Dave’s head as he backs out of the turnbuckle. Dave leans forward as he clutches the back of his head. Sickness steps up on the bottom rope, bounces, then brings her heel down on the back of Dave’s head in a brutal axe kick, sending him face first into the mat. Steele: Sickness has improved a lot since she last wrestled. I think she might actually have a career in the ring. Sick grabs onto Dave’s legs and drags him toward to center of the ring. With some effort, she pulls down his pants, exposing his bottom, and begins delivering several hard smacks to ass. Dave kicks away after the seventh smack to his bottom, and stands while trying to pull his pants up. While he’s struggling Sickness moves in and lifts Dave up in a fireman’s carry. She carries him to the center of the ring, and drops him hard with a death valley driver. Hughes: It's ovah! It's ovah! Sickness rises and places a foot over Dave’s chest. 1, 2, and Dave shoves her foot away. He rolls away and gets to his feet. Sickness gets him in her sights and does a pumping axe kick, but Dave quickly pulls the ref into the path of her heel. Sickness blinks at this turn of events while a very furious Dave Beaumont rushes her and takes her off her feet with a hard lariat. Dave doesn’t let up as he grabs onto Sickness’ hair, and begins pounding her head with hard rights. Sickness struggles to protect her face but Dave keeps on pounding. Sickness finally wrenches Dave’s hand from her hair, and begins crawling away, but Dave is quick to apply a hard kick to her ribs that flips her over. Hughes: Man, the boss is more pissed than that time I cost Mercury a sponsor because of that joke I made about the priest going to a kindergarten! Steele: I'll never look at a teeter-totter the same way... Dave grabs Sickness by the hair and drags her to the turnbuckle. He throws her violently against it and begins kicking her in the chest and head over and over again. The ref rises to his feet and tries to gets Beaumont’s attention, but Beaumont just shoves him away. He moves to continue the kicking assault, but his foot meets Sick’s hands, she rises to her feet holding Dave’s leg. Steele: Sickness has only taken surface damage after all that! Incredible! Sickness’s hair is undone on one side of her head, and her eye make-up is badly smudged from tears and kicks. She moves in, trapping Dave’s leg against her body and throw him over in a nice capture suplex. She picks Dave up and grabs a hold of his head in a front headlock with her left arm. She underhooks with her right arm, clasps her hands, and launches back in a nasty head and arm DDT. Hughes: Ewww, I don't think that Beaumont thought through the decision to take this match. Sickness rises and shakes her long red hair out. Her shoulders rise and fall as she breaths, a distinctive trembling can easily be discerned. She reaches down, and drives her index and middle fingers into Dave’s nostrils, and begins pulling him up. Dave starts wildly screaming at the excruciating pain. Steele: This is getting downright disturbing. Dave is on his tip toes trying to relieve the pressure from his schnoz, Sickness leans back and smashes her forehead right into Dave’s nose. She pulls her hand away and shakes her fingers, blood spraying on the mat. Dave falls to his knees holding his nose, the blood already flowing down his face. Sickness stares at Dave a moment, then grabs a hold of him and hoists him up into a fireman’s carry. She carries Beaumont toward the turnbuckle and sits him on the top rope. Sickness climbs up after him, grabbing a hold of his right leg, and his head in a front headlock. She cinches his body, and clasps her hands underneath. Steele: She's setting up the Agape Syndrome! Hughes: This is just a wild guess, but the boss is screwed. Sickness bounces once to get the leverage to hoist Beaumont into the air, then slowly falls over backward, driving his head directly into the mat with the fisherman’s DDT. Sickness rises as the ref checks on Beaumont, and perhaps moves to inflict more punishment, but the ref grabs her shoulder. He signals for the bell and tires to raise her hand but Sickness brushes him off going after Beaumont once again. The ref gets between her and Beaumont and insists that it’s over. Sickness blinks as Adema’s "Giving In" plays again. She stands for a moment not really knowing what to do. Then she slides out of the ring and begins walking up the ramp, covering her face as she begins to sob on her way out. Winner: Sickness Steele: Well, Yish might have called for the bell a little early to protect David, but it's clear that Sickness has some impressive moves. Hughes: And something impressive that kinda rhymes with moves. Only with more "oo". Steele: That's just sad. Match 2, Singles Match-Hollow vs Brimstone The MercuTron video package shows a swirling black hole tinted blue as light dies. Silence as the image is shown, then blue flames errupt keying the intro to "Angels Holocaust" as Brimstone makes his way to the ring. Steele: The first singles match for him since all the way back on December 14th. It's going to be a real struggle for him to not let ring rust hamper him here. Hughes: Oh, and 'Hollow' is gonna be like Jin Akujin out there or something. Please. Absolute silence and perfect darkness covers the arena. A pale light emerges as Andrea Boccelli's "Nessun Dorma" is cued. A video plays of Hollow stretching and striking his opponents into submission, with very detailed, close-up shots of the pain on their faces. Interspersed is a shot of Hollow looking at the camera, without a hint of emotion on his face. The victims seem to hurt more and more, until Andrea's voice reaches a crescendo, at which point Hollow offers a chilling, genuine smile. By this point, Hollow has reached the ring, and the house lights return to normal as the Hispanic giant steps over the top rope. Steele: Hollow is a former LCW Champion as Apex, but he's claimed to have adopted a new style since that part of his career was ended in November. Hughes: Yeah, it's called "I lost forty pounds of muscle." Brimstone takes it right to Hollow with right hands, right boots and a hard knife-edge chop across the pecs, backing the big(ger) man against the ropes. Hollow does a standing switch and does a bell clap, then a fingertip jab to the throat and several forearm shivers. Brimstone no-sells ALL, recalling some of the more off-color remarks Hollow made, and he shoves Hollow back to give room for half a dozen right hands, then a short-arm lariat which nearly knocks Hollow down! Nearly, that is; Hollow staggers, but comes roaring back with a hard kick to the face, sending Brimstone down! For about half a second, that is; Brimstone stands right back up and decks Hollow with a lariat to assert his dominance! Steele: Wow, this is a brand new Brimstone! Hughes: Punches and a clothesline? Steele: It's not what he did, John, it's how he did it. Brimstone only lets Hollow up part of the way, forcing him to double over by grabbing the back of his head. Brimstone begins the Kicks of Intestinal Discomfort, repeatedly jabbing the top of his boot into Hollow's face, but Hollow stands up with a non-amused look on his face. Fine by Brimstone, who gives him a loud chop to the chest, and a louder punch to the face! Hollow kicks him in the face to stop the onslaught, then gives him a knee lift, a running knee lift to knock him down, and finally a kneedrop to complete the Chain. Hollow stands up, Brimstone stands up... and slowly falls to his hands and knees. Steele: Hollow isn't hopped up on steroids and painkillers, but he's still going to be able to win that battle with pretty much anybody in the industry. Hughes: Brimstone can act tough all he likes, but it's going to take something else to go anywhere here. Hollow gets Brimstone up and whips him hard into a corner, then charges in and boots him in the face as hard as he can, leaving a red impression on his face. Brimstone slumps into the corner, which leaves him open to a series of stiff knees to the head. Brimstone is eventually pummeled into a seated position, so Hollow scrapes the side of his right boot repeatedly across his face! The fans boo the disrespectful tactic, not that Hollow cares. Brimstone blocks the fifth scrape and stands up holding onto Hollow's foot; Hollow jumps into an enzugiri, which Brimstone ducks en route to the Who's Yo Momma ankle lock! Hollow scrambles to the ropes, and referee Bo Smirnow informs Brimstone that he must break. Brimstone does so, but only after a friendly tweak on the ankle. Hughes: That wasn't very nice. Steele: Neither was scraping a boot across the other guy's face. Hughes: ...still doesn't make it nice... Brimstone gives Hollow a few stomps, then whips him to the ropes. Hollow grabs them to stop his momentum, so Brimstone charges in, which leaves him open to the Mute forearm lariat to the neck! Brimstone goes down hard, coughing and clutching his throat. Hollow thus has no opposition when he scoops Brimstone up for a big ol' bodyslam, then starts kicking him as hard as he can in the back, followed by one to the back of the head that's loud enough to be picked up by the ring mic! The crowd gives Brimstone a sympathy wince, while Brimstone growls and stands up under his own power! Hollow... kicks him in the face. Steele: Hollow calls that series of moves the Machine. Geez, this isn't fun to watch. Hughes: Says who? This has way more redeeming value than, say, a Brandon Devola match. Steele: And you'll be served with the lawsuit in five, four, three... Hollow puts Brimstone in a sleeper, but rather than go to the ground as is the norm, he stands up. Brimstone throws a couple elbows, but gets cut off when Hollow begins kneeing him in the small of the back! Hollow then tosses Brimstone down like a rag roll and covers, one, kick out! Hollow stands up, and the crowd cheers when Brimstone gets up with him! Both men smirk, and Brimstone kicks him in the jimmies to another pop! Hollow is humbled by the power of the nut punt, giving Brimstone all the time he needs to nail two straight Backhandwiches, put him in a standing headscissors, and plant him with a piledriver! Brimstone gets up, Hollow gets up... and slowly falls to his knees! Hughes: Hmmmm. Eerie similarities between how these guys react to getting hit. Steele: They're both known for being able to take a lot of abuse. Hughes: Must... not... make... comment... about... KC's... relationships. Steele: Yeah, or I'll hurt you. Hughes: You don't scare me. KC glares at him. Hughes: Yes you do. Brimstone runs to the ropes and shoulderblocks Hollow the rest of the way down before covering, one, and a half, kick out! Brimstone gives an 'aww, shucks' to the count and a 'I'll just punch you in the head some more' to Hollow. Thirty seconds later, Brimstone hooks a leg, one, and a half, and a quarter, Hollow throws a shoulder up! Brimstone gives up on that tactic and stands up, then goes over to a corner and climbs up to the second rope. Hollow slowly stands up (though it's only slow relative to normal people) and faces him, so Brimstone jumps off with a perfectly aimed lariat! Hollow's aim is just a little better, however, and he swats Brimstone down with another Mute forearm lariat to the neck! Steele: Look at the velocity behind that! Brimstone whiplashed when he hit the mat! Hollow rolls Brimstone onto his chest, sits on him, and pulls Brimstone's left leg over his (Hollow's) left shoulder. With his right arm, Hollow grabs Brimstone's head with what appears to be a basic chinlock. 'Basic' becomes 'really painful', as Hollow attemps to pull his arms together, in essense trying to touch the top of Brimstone's head with the bottom of his own foot! The crowd's mood darkens as the inevitable draws near. Steele: He calls that the Ecstacy of the Agony, and from what I've been told it's the centerpiece of his new moves. Hughes: Easy to see why. I mean, OUCH! Brimstone pounds on the canvas, but not to tap out. Using his hands and his right leg, he actually moves forward a little bit! With the EEI-ites cheering him on, he inches his way little by little, despite Hollow's entire weight being pressed into him and despite the incredible pain, towards the ropes! Bo Smirnow calls for the break, and when Hollow only increases the torque at five, Smirnow is forced to interject himself and pull Hollow away from Brimstone. Hollow glares at Smirnow, and without hesitation wipes him out with a Mute! Steele: Yup, there's the bell. Winner by Disqualification: Brimstone A swarm of 'generic, chubby Mercury officials' run down to the ring, both to tend to Smirnow and to yell at Hollow. Unnoticed, Brimstone pulls himself to his feet, and tries to lunge through them at Hollow! The officials restrain him, and force Hollow not to try and get in any post-match shots either. Hughes: I smell a rematch! And I also smell this... John pulls out a small bottle of clear liquid. Hughes: Eau de Hughes! Only ten bucks at IronJohn-dot-com! Steele: *coughworthlessshillcough* Anyways, I understand Jason Schatz is standing backstage with Blaze McCoy! Backstage We go backstage to the interview area, where Jason Schatz, in a red Suicide shirt, stands next to Blaze McCoy, in full ring gear, Cherry Stacked by his side. Schatz: Blaze, just minutes away from what is probably your last match in LCW Mercury, what is going through your head? Blaze: Well, well, well.....what do we have here? A new interview man, asking me what I think? Lemme tell you something, Jason my boy. Inferno is good, yes he is...but nobody, I mean nobody...can stop the Lunatic Hick! Cherry: That’s right. Inferno, prepare to go down to a real man.... Schatz: Thanks, Blaze and Cherry! Back to you, KC and John. Back In The Arena Kid Rock’s "Forever" plays over the video package, highlighting Blaze and Inferno’s feud. Match 3, Loser Leaves Town Singles Match-Blaze McCoy vs Inferno Steele: This is the last match one of these two men will wrestle in Mercury, John. The stakes are high, former partners now going alone, one a seasoned veteran, the other a rookie who graduated from the former LCW development federation, LTD. I don't know who to pick John, this one could go either way. Hughes: Or both of them could lose and be thrown out. Steele: It's happened before, but I don't see it happening again, there will be a decisive winner! "So Whatcha Want?" by the Beastie Boys plays and gold pyro explodes on both sides of the ramp. Out walks Blaze McCoy with a sleeveless flannel, cut off jean shorts and his black OutKast hat and sunglasses on. His wife, the lovely and top-heavy Cherry Stacked, follows him down the ramp. She is wearing a red dress with red high heels and is holding his fishing pole wrapped in barbed wire. The walk down the ramp as the fans are split with a chorus of boos as well as cheers, while they enter the ring. Blaze opens the ropes for Cherry, then hops over the top rope and climbs the nearest turnbuckle. He turns his hat around backwards, slowly takes off his sunglasses and gives the old thumbs up, with a smile, then the thumbs down, with a frown. Steele: Remember Blaze has a part ownership of LTD now, so if Inferno does lose this match, he has nowhere to fall back to. Of course, if Blaze loses, he's got LTD to keep him going, that motivation could be the difference between victory and defeat, Inferno has to be more desperate for the win. The lights begin to flicker a little bit, but nothing happens. Once again, it starts, but then, the lights finally dim down into a red color. The crowd stands up to see who is coming out. Just then two huge flame balls come up from the top of the ramp and travel down it to the bottom. Smoke appears in the entrance way, as "Stupify" by Disturbed hits the PA system. Inferno emerges from the smoke with his long dark coat and bandana and sunglasses, all of which he leaves on the ramp as he runs down to the ring! Steele: Inferno Kid not wasting any time, he's running out to meet Blaze head on! There Blaze pummels Inferno a little more, throwing him face first into the ring steps, then deciding that isn't enough, bodyslamming him over the steel. Hughes: I have a feeling the ref wont be too willing to allow a DQ here. Steele: I've got word from backstage that Dave Beaumont has directed the referee to disqualify either man only if there is outside interference, a direct failure to obey his orders, or if either man lays a hand on him. Other than that, I'm guessing anything goes. Blaze thrust kicks Inferno into the barrier, then charges for a clothesline, but Inferno picks him up and powerslams him straight onto the barrier, leaving Blaze balanced delicately between ringside and the fans. Not for long, however, as Inferno leaps to the apron, then leaps off with a splash, landing straight on top of McCoy, and then both men topple into the crowd. Inferno is up first and hiptosses McCoy back into the ringside area, then slingshots himself over the barrier with a legdrop straight across McCoy's throat. Inferno picks Blaze and rolls him into the ring, then slides in himself and covers, one, kick out by McCoy. Steele: Blaze McCoy is not risking anything here, that was a powerful kick out, especially after taking some punishment on the outside, that splash wouldn't have tickled. Inferno helps Blaze to his feet and grabs him in a headlock, Blaze grabs a hold of Inferno's waist and tries to pick him up, but Inferno runs into the ropes and releases himself from McCoy, but gives Blaze enough time to charge back into him with the Bait & Tackle spear as Inferno turns around. Blaze hooks the leg, one, two, kick out by Inferno, who pushes Blaze aside while holding his stomach, trying to shake off the move. Steele: Blaze pulls out one of his trademarks there, he'll need all the tricks he has to win this one. Hughes: Damn straight he will, do you think he has any friends waiting backstage to help him out, you know, maybe an SCC revival? Steele: Somehow I don't think you'll be seeing Real Deal or Hype Norris running out here to help Blaze, and definitely not Pariah. Blaze keeps the pressure on Inferno, hitting a kneelift then whips Inferno into the ropes for a powerslam, holding on after the move, picking Inferno up again, and in a nice show of strength, throws Inferno across the ring in a fallaway slam. The fans pop for Blaze's effort as he slides across the ring to cover, but the referee is out of position, having dramatically dived out of the way of Inferno, and thus, Inferno kicks out just as the count reaches one. Steele: Inferno isn't leaving on just that. Inferno struggles to his feet using the ropes, and walks out straight into a Blaze kick, then a double underhook leads to an overhead belly to belly from Blaze. Inferno quickly slides back to his feet, but is dazed from the relentless attack of McCoy, and is easy pickings for the Food Stamp, which hits Inferno square in the face, and he stumbles backwards and out of the ring. Blaze looks at Inferno, takes a deep breath, and goes upstairs, takes another deep breath, looks to the skies and flies, hitting a huge flying cross body press to the fallen Inferno. Hughes: Holy heck! Was that Blaze McCoy just flying like that? He must really want to stay in Mercury. Steele: I haven't seen McCoy this dedicated and in such great shape since his return to Mercury, or perhaps even before then in his hardcore days! Blaze takes a second to get to his feet, then shakes his head in disbelief at what he just did. Remembering he has a match to win, Blaze rolls Inferno into the ring and hooks the leg, one, two, the referee sees Blaze's foot is between the ropes and points to it, saying Blaze must be entirely in the ring for the pinfall to count. Blaze gives the referee a mean glare, and makes a gesture as if to say 'what the hell is that all about?' but then picks up Inferno by the head and starts to deliver some stiff knife-edge chops, driving Inferno into the corner. Steele: Well, I've never heard of that ruling before, I suppose they just want to make sure there is no way either man could dispute the ruling should they lose. Hughes: Inferno is getting an ass kicking here, come on Inferno, this is Blaze McCoy! Blaze sets Inferno up on the top rope, but gets pushed back to the mat as he makes his own way up there. Inferno quickly gets to a crouching position on the turnbuckle, and as Blaze stands up, leaps off, grabbing Blaze's head along the way in a flying bulldog. Blaze bounces off the mat, but is to his knees quickly, just in time to see a flying dropkick meet his jaw as Inferno again goes upstairs and flies. Steele: Inferno likes that top rope a lot for a bigger guy, he has the agility and speed as well as the size to make those moves work. Inferno gets a groggy Blaze to his feet and hooks his arms behind his back for a straightjacket suplex, bouncing the back of Blaze's head off the canvas. Inferno then kicks Blaze to his feet, then throws him into the ropes, and on his return leaps up, grabbing Blaze in a hurricanrana, and holds on, hooking Blaze's legs one, two, thr and that's all, as Blaze powers out in a nick of time. Inferno picks Blaze up and hits a roundhouse kick, then another, and a third jumping kick ends the combination. Blaze staggers into the corner, and bounces out slowly, leaving enough time for Inferno to jump the ropes behind him, get onto his shoulders and roll through in a victory roll, one, two, Blaze again powers out.
