LCW Mercury LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Josh Trent Real Name: Joshua Trent Born: Aug. 30, 1981 Place of Birth: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Hometown: Height: 6'1 Weight: 210 Blood type: A+ Eye color: Brown Team: The Trent Brothers Manager: None Trainer: Mikel Trent Wrestler Style Wrestling style: High Risk Signature moves: The Jokers Wild - Ax Kick. Sorrow - Tornado DDT Melancholy - High Angle DDT (The Implant DDT). The Last Laugh-Super kick 450 Splash. Enziguri. Moonsalt. Hurricanarana Ace Crusher Tag Finisher: Brotherly Love (Josh is powerbombed onto opponent by Mikel.) Finisher: Flipping top-rope Leg drop Finisher name: The Homicidal Tendency Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: None Other/Previous teams: None Other/Previous managers: None Other Wrestling titles won: None Other Wrestling awards won: None Biography Joshua Trent was born on August 30th 1981. The first view of his life was of his mother's smiling face, a view which would soon later change forever. Josh was year old when his brother Mikel was born. Josh was a smart ass from the start, as soon as he turned 3 years old he began voicing his oppinion on subjects. Josh's mother was proud of her son's ability to stand out in a crowd. Josh's father was proud of his son as well, for a three year old Josh jr. was an identical image of his father. The happiness that Josh felt in his heart drastically changed forever, at his four year old birthday party, Josh jr. watched with tearing eyes as his father punched his mother. Josh was a witness to this often, and after a few years went by, he watched his father hit Mikel as well. The young Josh Trent saw the evilness that was being portrayed before his eyes, and decided to speak up. Josh received several mentally scarring verbal attacks, from his father, causing his eventual dark humor. One night all of the mental pain and anguish was at a stand still...a night that present day Josh will never forget, a night where the abuser became the abused, a night that he might never share with anyone.
