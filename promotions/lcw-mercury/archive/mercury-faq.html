Frequently Asked Questions

June 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Frequently Asked Questions General Q: What does the phrase "fantasy wrestlers only" mean? A: It means you can't be The Rock, Big Poppa Pump, Jerry Lynn, or any other real wrestler, active or retired. You have to create your own wrestler and your own gimmick. Also, don't use a real wrestler's theme song. Q: Will I be fired if I complain? A: In a word: no. However, if you make a habit of constantly complaining, especially why you lost this match or that match, then yes, you will be punished in some way, possibly even termination. Rather than simply complaining about why you lost, put that effort into your next roleplay, and make it that much better, thus decreasing your chance of losing your next match. You can come to us with problems, and we will do our best to resolve them as soon as possible. Q: How do I get to be a ranked contender for a title? A: Having solid roleplays is the first step. This will get you involved in angles, which will lead you to being put into matches with other ranked competitors. Should you win your matches against these competitors, you will move up in the rankings and eventually get a title shot. Q: I think that I'm a good roleplayer, but not quite good enough to be accepted into your fed. How do I go about improving? A: Your first step is to apply to the fed, as most people are there own harshest critic. If you have been rejecting in the past, then you could always study our roleplay guide and work from there. Shows Q: How many shows does LCW Mercury run per week? A: We have one weekly show - Friday Fusion. Q: When will the results be up? A: Shows will be up by midnight on the day that the event is scheduled. On rare occasions, we may have to push shows back, but they will be up within a day of the original date (this is a rarity, so don't let the likelihood of this event happening concern you). Roleplays Q: What are you looking for in a roleplay? A: Long roleplays are fine, but only if they are entertaining. We are constantly busy with the site and shows and we don't have time to read long pointless roleplays. The main area that we assess in roleplays, and the main thing that will move you to the top of this fed, is quality. What you say is more important than how long it takes you to say it. We also look for significant character development and creativity, something that many other feds don't seem to hold in particularly high value. Q: When is the deadline for roleplays? A: Midnight Eastern Standard Time, two days before the event.
