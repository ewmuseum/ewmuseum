A brief history of Mercury

June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A brief history of Mercury: LCW Torn PPV- Rook Black wins the LCW title, and then cuts a promo against the owner of LCW, David Beaumont, who proceeds to fire him, along with Rook's opponent and previous LCW champion Pheonyx. Used and Duel back up Rook, and decide to quit. This leads to many more in LCW leaving to join Neo, formed by LCW's commissioner Edward Olmstead. September- Beaumont forms Mercury from what remains. Neo takes the Hardcore title and renames it the Madhouse title; in addition, Rook takes the LCW title with him. Mercury takes the tag titles (stripped from then-champions Wallace and Heimdal), along with the National and Universal titles, both of which are retired so that new belts can be founded. The Mercury title will be inaugurated with a 14-man single-elimination tournament. Hector Carreras and Harbinger receive byes for giving up their singles titles. 10/5/01- The first Fusion contains the first round of the tournament, the crowning of new tag team champions, and the debuts of Dirge and Brimstone. Toh, Myrddin, Pariah, Mastadon and Dirge all advance, with TR Saxton and Gravity fighting to a time limit draw. The Trents beat The Graduates to become the new tag team champions. 10/12/01- Blaze McCoy replaces Hector Carreras (who had apparently left the country) in the second round of the tournament, losing to Dirge. Pariah and Harbinger also advance, with Toh receiving a bye. Meanwhile, the team of Sloan and Godsey upset the Trents for the tag team titles. After the match, Sloan assaults Godsey, injuring him. 10/19/01- Harbinger and Pariah reach the finals of the Mercury title tournament. Sloan and his new partner Slingshot successfully defend the tag team titles against Legion. 11/4/01- Harbinger defeats Apex, and due to a stipulation in the match, Apex retires. Entropy Crusade become the top contenders for the tag team titles. Blaze McCoy sneaks into a shot at becoming the first North American champion. The Trents regain the tag team titles. Mercury Rising PPV- Memosarks defeats Blaze McCoy in a ladder match to become the first ever North American champion. The Trents best Entropy Crusade and retain the tag team titles, despite the emergence of Johnny Glamour and Vincent Picasso. TSC Omega (Gravity, Slate and Chillz) outlasts TSC Alpha (TR Saxton and New School) when Gravity gets Saxton to submit. In the main event, Harbinger beats Pariah in a grueling "I Quit" match to become the first Mercury champion. 11/25/01- Pariah interrupts the celebration of Harbinger's win at Rising, arousing the ire of David Beaumont. Pariah defeats Blaze McCoy in the LCW Anniversiary match. The returning Magnum Wolf takes the North American title from Memosarks. With an assist to David Beaumont, Harbinger retains against Geoffrey Slate. 12/7/01- Pariah asks for a rematch against Harbinger, based on the nature of Harbinger's win over Slate. Geoffrey Slate beats Memosarks to get a shot at the North American title at Nightmare. The Trents have an incredible time limit draw with Picasso and Glamour. TR Saxton wins a hardcore match over Pariah, which was Pariah's punishment for his actions the week before. Harbinger retains again, against Gravity, and again Beaumont is involved in the outcome. 12/14/01- Bruce DeAula, Vice President/Commissioner of Mercury, lays down for Pariah in a match to decide whether or not Pariah gets a rematch against Harbinger. Pariah also names the stipulation: pinfalls only. In the main event, Harbinger retains against Blaze McCoy and Dominator, without the aid of Beaumont. Silent Nightmare 2001 PPV- Gravity attacks David Beaumont during a match between Beaumont and Olmstead of Neo. The Trents retain in a doomsday-style 8 man tag team match. In one of the most gruesome battles in LCW history, TR Saxton ends Gravity's career in a 2 out of 3 falls hardcore match; thumbtacks, exploding barbed wire, a staple gun, fire, a paring knife, brass knuckles, a chair and a table all come into play. Geoffrey Slate narrowly escapes the confines of a steel cage to become the third North American champion. In a rematch even more draining than the original, Harbinger gets help from the returning Gol to defeat Pariah a second time. In the course of the match, Pariah has a tooth knocked out. At the end of the show, The Leader gives Beaumont a phone call. 1/29/02- After a month's vacation, Mercury shakes the rust off and has a rather chaotic finish to the show, with Gol, Pariah, Magnum Wolf, Harbinger, the Trents, Picasso and Glamour all battling it out by the time the copyright notice comes up. 2/6/02- The LTD tryout match is set up, Picasso and Glamour win the tag team titles, Harbinger retains over TR Saxton in a rather biased "I Quit" match, and Magnum Wolf beats Gol in a falls count anywhere match with Pariah as the referee. 2/15/02- Blaze McCoy is blindsided, Inferno joins Mercury, Memosarks wins a shot at the North American title, and Magnum Wolf gets help from Gol to get a Mercury title shot over Pariah. 2/22/02- TR Saxton quits following an insult from David Beaumont, Pariah is beaten up by a mystery assailant, and Geoffrey Slate overcomes the challenge of Big Pee-On. Critical Mass PPV- The Heatseekers form, the Elite retain against both the Trents and the debuting Akujin and Young, Geoffrey slate retains over Memosarks, Pariah gains revenge on Gol in a last man standing match, and Harbinger squeaks by Magnum Wolf in a lumberjack match thanks to some interesting interference from Pariah. 3/11/02- Five months after the start of Mercury, its greatest rivalry finally reached a climax. With David Beaumont as special referee, Pariah defeated Harbinger in a shootfight rules match. Once Pariah held the title in the air, he felt as if his dreams had come true... only to have those dreams be shattered when he was attacked from behind by Magnum Wolf and Gol! The pair then turned their sights to David Beaumont, and Gol gave him a superbomb. They announced themselves as the Oblivion, and nothing would be the same. 3/21/02- Gol and Magnum beat down Memosarks, Dirge and Harbinger before the main event, in which they and Picasso fight Pariah and the Trents. As the match wound down, the Trents abruptly turned on Pariah! They annouced that they, too, were a part of Oblivion! Picasso also took a pounding in the process, thus solidifying his opposition to the group. 3/29/02- Gol wins the North American title from Geoffrey Slate, as a referee distraction allows Magnum Wolf to wipe Slate out. Pariah retains the Mercury title in a war with Robert Young. 4/5/02- Dirge wins the North American title from Gol thanks to a distraction from Geoffrey Slate. Pariah retains the Mercury title over Jin Akujin in a No-DQ match. The main event sees one of the most shocking twists in Mercury history, when Magnum Wolf and the Trents fight Slate, Harbinger and Picasso in a #1 contendership elimination match. When the last two men are Slate and Wolf, Gol comes out holding Sickness (the girlfriend of Harbinger) hostage. Slate lays down for Wolf, and Sickness is left unharmed. However, seconds later she reveals herself to be the creator of Oblivion, much to the horror of Slate and Harbinger. 4/12/02- Magnum Wolf and Gol end Memosarks' career with a series of powerbombs onto a table that refuses to break. Due to the screwjob ending in the last week's main event, two more contendership matches are held. Gol defeats Robert Young, and Slate defeats a distraught Harbinger. In the main event, Pariah defends against both members of the former tag team champion Graduates. After the match, Oblivion comes out, but beats on the Graduates instead of Pariah, much to the champion's dismay. 4/19/02- David Beaumont and Sickness sign a match for Suicide. The Trents win a tag title shot at Suicide. Robert Young beats Harbinger for a North American title shot at Suicide. Jin Akujin challenges Harbinger for next week's show. The Trents steal and maim the tag team titles. Geoffrey Slate beats Magnum Wolf. In the main event, Dirge and Pariah put both of Mercury's titles on the line against each other. Before either man can win, the Oblivion comes out and assaults Dirge. Pariah, having seen enough, tries to help but is overwhelmed. This leads to the return of Brimstone and Johnny Glamour, and the formation of a 5-man EEI stable, including Pariah, which holds all the titles in Mercury. 4/26/02- Blaze McCoy and Inferno set up a loser leaves town match at Suicide. Harbinger and Jin Akujin tear the house down with a fantastic time limit draw. The Elites make their match with the Trents a streetfight. Entropy Crusade falls to the Trents when the lights go out, and come back on to reveal the Crusaders laid out. In the main event, Pariah and Geoffrey Slate beat Magnum Wolf and Gol. Post-match, EEI and Oblivion have a battle, and much like earlier in the show the lights go out. This time, they come back to reveal Hollow, the man formerly known as Apex and the newest member of Oblivion. Suicide PPV- Sickness beats Beaumont to a pulp. Inferno ends Blaze McCoy's LCW career. Hollow and Brimstone beat the hell out of each other. Harbinger outlasts Jin Akujin in a match that somehow topped their initial encounter; post-match, Harbinger gives Akujin the dreaded Punk-Out 5.0. Dirge and the Elites both retain their titles. In the main event elimination match for the Mercury title, Gol defeats Pariah with the Fall from Grace superbomb. The order of elimination was: Magnum Wolf, Geoffrey Slate, Pariah.
