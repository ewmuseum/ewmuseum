LCW Mercury Card Archive

Fusion, 25 November 2001

25 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Mercury Fusion--11-25-01--Live from the MCI Center in Washington, DC Disturbed's "Shout 2000" plays as the entrance video starts with a shot of the Mercury Fusion logo. (SHOUT!) Pariah makes Apex scream in a submission (SHOUT!) Harbinger celebrates with his Universal Title (Let it all out) Clips from various athletes in pain are seen (These are the things I can do without) And Toh lariat's somebody right out of his boots (Come on) G Slate bitches somebody out in a interview (I'm talking to you) And Jerry Cane drops a former LCW performer with the T for Texas (So come on) The Trents perform some Brotherly Love (In violent times...) Dave Beaumont flashes a sinister grin (You shouldn't have to sell your soul) The goofy Memosarks throws confetti at the crowd (In black and white...) TR Saxton mangles some unseen person with a cheese grater (They really, really ought to know) Gravity drops Brian Shotgunn through some tables (Just don't know) Apex looks out into space after a win. (Those wandering minds...) Harbinger lashes out at anyone and everyone in a promo (Who took you for a working whore) a shot of the newcomers Entropy Crusade talking is seen (Kiss them goodbye...) And The New School destroys The Birds at Torn (They shouldn't have to jump for joy) a table is broken by Gravity (Jump!) Pariah dents a chair over Rook Black's head (Jump!) The Trents beat up unsuspecting people (Jump!) Memosarks and Harbinger do battle (Jump!) And TR Saxton hits the D.O.I (Jump!) Harbinger wrestles in Japan (You shouldn't have to shout for joy) Pariah screams (SHOUT!) Beaumont addresses the workers of LCW Mercury (They give you life...) Gravity clutches a World Title (...and in return you gave them hell!) G Slate smiles under a crimson mask (As cold as ice) Pariah throws Pestilence off of the scaffold at Suicide (I hope we live to tell the tale) and the final clips of various wrestlers hitting their finishers are seen (I hope we live to shout the tale!) The inside of the Civic Center is seen, as the thousands of fan erupt, and a fireworks display engulfs the entrance area, and golden sparks shoot from the word FUSION, hanging above. As the smoke from the pyro clear, the Fusion entrance stage becomes visible. From the top of the building the word "Fusion" hangs, spelt out in metal girders. A circular metal girder with swirling lights dangling from it hangs below this Fusion, and two fair sized video screens, called the MercuTrons, hang on opposite sides of the Fusion. Below Fusion is the entrance area, which consists of two large metal girders that lead from the top of the ramp up to the U and O in Fusion. For their entrances, the wrestlers will walk out between the girders, and in front of the Mercury Fusion logo, which is built into the stage, and in between the large steel entrance girders. The elevated ramp way, which is covered in dull gray carpet, leads us right to the ring! The camera scans the arena, and signs fill the arena. We pick up the more creative ones, which range from "I paid to see Yish!" to "Mrs. Ingles baby boy has done it! Oh mah gawd!" and finally, "EEI 3:15". We then cut to the announce desk where KC Steele, dressed in a black Mercury fleece pullover, sits next to John Hughes, who is wearing a button down Fusion denim shirt. KC Steele: Welcome to the first Fusion after the stellar Rising show! "Iron" John Hughes: You have a funny definition of 'stellar'. What I watched was Legion leaving, Entropy Crusade losing, and the boringest man alive winning the Mercury title! Steele: You wanted Pariah? Hughes: I didn't say that. "Struggle Within" hits, and David Beaumont struts down the entrance ramp in a full tuxedo. He waves to all the wonderful money marks in the audience, especially those who bought the Entropy Crusade 'random foam wad of chaos' for $20. Dave: Well, it seems to me that there's only one half of LCW that held its own pay-per-view, eh? Hey Ed, tell me again how none of my boys can draw? Beaumont beams from ear to ear. Dave: Oh, I could gloat all night, but there's bigger things to do. First, I'd like to announce that Silent Nightmare 2001 will take place at the Metrodome in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Tickets go on sale at midnight, tonight! Those of you lucky enough to afford front row seats not only get to take the chair with you, but you also get to hit Yish with it after the show! Pop. Dave: Next up, I'd like to call the greatest champion in the sport today to the ring. Harbinger, get your kiester out here! "Playground for a Wedgeless Firm" fires up. Harbinger walks down to the ring, with the Mercury title around his waist. He receives a warm reception. Dave: Herb, I'm so glad that you finally won the world title. You represent so much that's sorely missing from the sport. Harbinger: Th- Dave: I mean, you've got dedication, invention, four star matches as often as Dirge commits misdemeanors, and your T-shirts are finally outselling the leftover Carlos Rodriguez ones. Am I gushing? Harbinger: Only a little. Dave: Then it's not enough. I'd like to welcome out... the rest of the Mercury roster! C'mon out guys! TR Saxton, Dirge, Brimstone, Geoffrey Slate, Gravity, Chillz, Johnny Glamour, Sloan, Picasso, Josh and Mikel Trent, Rush, Blaze McCoy, Magnum Wolf, Memosarks, Deek and Dominator all walk out to the ring. Dave: We've got our champion at long last! Let the party begin! "Playground for a Wedgeless Firm" starts up again, as streamers and confetti fall from the ceiling and pyro goes off across the arena. At Dave's request, Harbinger is hoisted up on several wrestlers' shoulders. Dave: For he's a jolly good fe-llow, for he's a jolly good fe-llow, for he's a jolly good fe-he-looooowwww... which- Pariah: Which I'm about to deny! Pariah now makes his way to the ring. The music and pyro both stop, but the paper products keep falling. Dave: What's that, Keith? Feeling a little green? Pariah: Hardy-har-f**k, Beaumont. You know damn well that I wouldn't be getting the royal treatment if I'd won. And what about Memosarks? He's a new champion too. He deserves something. Harbinger is set down. Memosarks and a few others start to nod. Dave: Don't let your envy rain on Herb's parade! He earned all this, and you have no right to- Pariah: No, YOU have no right to make him out to be some goddamn messiah! Hell, he might not even <i>be</i> the champion at the end of the night, think about that? Dave: - Pariah: You know what? I agreed to a match on Herb's terms. Now, I want mine. I want a rematch for the world title, no-DQ, anything goes, against the winner of Harbinger and Slate! Pop. The fans liked the first match, why not the rematch? Dave: I've got an even better idea... Beaumont grabs TR Saxton by the wrist and tugs him over. Dave: You're fighting him in a no-DQ anything goes match next week! Saxton smirks at the prospect. Pariah does not. Pariah: You can't hold me back. I've gone through too much not to get another shot! Dave: You lost, now you can go to the back of the line. I mean, how long could it take to give everyone else a shot? Three, four months tops. Pariah screams something (which is bleeped entirely) and lunges at his employer. Before he can get there, however, he's tackled and beaten on by all four members of EEI! This brings the Trents, Sloan and Rush into the mix, followed by Saxton, Gravity, Chillz and Slate, followed by the other five men in the ring who aren't Beaumont or Harbinger! Security runs out, and they do their best to separate the throng of brawling wrestlers! Steele: What chaos! What anarchy! And we haven't even gotten to the first match! Hughes: The nerve of that Pariah. Getting Saxton next week serves him right for talking trash at his boss! Steele: Pariah against TR Saxton, no disqualification? I can only assume that'll turn into a hardcore match, and with those two involved it could very easily be pay-per-view level brutality! Hughes: I can't wait to see those two kill each otherSteele: Well, before Saxton faces Pariah, he has newcomer Deek "The Geek" Elvenstein here tonight. Hughes: Deek "The Geek"....what the f-- Steele: Watch it! Match 1, Singles Match--TR Saxton vs Deek "The Geek" Elvenstein "All About the Pentiums" plays, but it gets no pyro because that might irritate Deek's nose. The MercuTron shows various computer peripherals, like keyboards and monitors and such, and then an exploding monitor as Deek comes through into the view of the crowd. Hughes: BWAAAAAAAHAHAHAHA! Steele: What's so funny? Hughes: He's the only guy on the roster who'd actually be able to put ME over! The ominous tones of Diamond Head's "Am I Evil?" echo through the arena. Three seconds in, every house light goes completely black, and a spotlight shines on the entrance ramp. A man in a black leather jacket walks out as the long instrumental opening of the song continues, revealing himself as TR Saxton. TR stares at the ring and walks with a measured gait in a perfectly straight line. Behind him, the MercuTron plays highlights of his thus-far incredibly violent career, with several close-up shots of the sickening drive of Dead On Impact interspersed throughout. TR enters the ring and pulls his hands out of hisjacket pocket as the lights come on. The spotlight and the jacket go off together. Hughes: Yeah, this'll be long... Saxton crosses his arms and glares at Deek, who bounds around the ring thanks to his speed+2 ability. Deek then launches a dropkick at TR's face. TR sidesteps,and Deek gets nothing for his efforts. Deek kips up immediately and throwsanother dropkick, same result. Deek is not deterred, and he throws a third dropkick, this time aimed at the left knee. It connects, sending Saxton sprawling to the mat in quite a lot of pain. Steele: That's the same knee that Slate and Gravity have gotten wins with at the last two shows! I don't know if Deek knew that coming in, but still, it's about the only way I can think of for him to win. Hughes: Say what you will, according to my earpiece the line is still 43 to 1 in Saxton's favor. Steele: Your earpiece gets the Vegas odds? Hughes: It also gets Spice. Steele: I'm not even gonna start. Deek slaps on a figure four leglock, which further pains the LCW vet! Saxton gets his hands under him, and slowly crawls to the ropes for a break. Deek obeys referee Bo Smirnow immediately, and patiently waits for Saxton to get up. When he does, Deek yells at the top of his lungs, "by the power of Horonius, I challenge you to a test of strength!" Steele: John? Hughes: It just went to 65 to 1. Deek holds his hands up in the international signal for 'test of strength’.Saxton holds up his left hand and confirms that it's indeed what Deek wants, just before decking him with a right hand to the face! Deek falls down, clutching his face until TR grabs his left arm to drag him up for an Irish whip. Deek manages to duck the ensuing Saxton lariat, and he grabs a rear waistlock to set up the Hardrive suplex! Saxton snorts while Deek struggles to get him off the ground, then blasts him with a back elbow to make him let go. TR turns around, cocks his right arm, and blasts Deek with a lariat that flips Deek onto his face! Hughes: May I? Steele: By all means. Hughes: Lariatoooooooo! Saxton pulls Deek into a standing headscissors, then lifts him over a shoulder before driving him hard to the canvas with a falling, high-angle thunderfire powerbomb! Saxton pushes down on Deek's face for the cover, one, two, three! Winner by Pinfall: TR Saxton Steele: Well, congrats John. You actually won a bet. Hughes: Um... I put all my money on Legion's next match instead. Steele: *sigh* Hughes: Hey, what's Tiara doing? Saxton nabs the house mic. TR: Now that that's out of the way, I want to get this off my chest. Tim, I've sent you a contract in the mail for a match between us at Silent Nightmare. You and me, one-on-one, in a two out of three falls ultimate hardcore match. On one side of the ring, a bed of thumbtacks. On the other side of the ring, a bed of exploding barbed wire. The winner not only gets any titles we might get between now and then... but also, the loser's career! The litany of stips gets a pop when Saxton pauses for breath. TR: And, just to make sure the mistakes of the past aren't revisited, I propose that there be a ten million dollar bond against the loser ever competing again! I'm willing to put my entire legacy on the line to wipe you out, Tim. Let's see if you have the sac to sign the dotted line and do the same. TR spikes the mic and exits. Steele: Whoa! That could be a doozy! TR Saxton versus Gravity in... well,there's a lot of stipulations, but the final result could very well be the most violent match of 2001! Hughes: Gravity pounding on TR for two whole falls? Count me in. Steele: The night of newcomers continues as "The Dominator" Victor Williams faces Gravity. Here’s hoping Dominator has better luck than Da' Geek. Match 2, Singles Match--Gravity vs Victor "The Dominator" Williams "Raining Blood" by Slayer heralds the introduction of Victor "The Dominator Williams" who appears at the top of the ramp, ready for battle. A traditional video package plays on the MercuTron as The Dominator stomps down to the ring, a determined look on his face. Once Williams is in the ring, he looks around, and then raises his arms up before retreating to his corner. Hughes: I don’t think that guy could Dominate all that much... The arena goes dark, making the MercuTron the sole light source. On it, footage of Gravity putting Brian Shotgunn in the Metamorphosis is shown, and a voice says, "What comes up, must go down," coinciding with Shotgunn's sickening crash through the table. On this contact, the opening chords of "Sober" by Tool plays, and Gravity makes his way out, methodically marching to the ring in black jeans. Gravity circles the ring, looking over the newcomer. The two men lockup, and jockey for position. After a few tense moments, Gravity is able to get a hold of Williams’ head, and drop him to his back. Gravity hits the ropes, and Dominator is up, ready to shoulderblock Gravity. But Gravity wins again, knocking Dominator flat on his back. Gravity picks up Dominator, but Victor surprises him by sucker punching Gravity in the gut, and then tossing him down with a hip toss throw! With Gravity on his back, Dominator wastes no time and hops atop Gravity, going to town with punches. But after a few odd seconds, Gravity is just mad, and tosses Williams off. Dominator though is up first, and attempts to toss Gravity into the ropes. But Gravity holds on, and then jerks Dominator towards him before dumping the newcomer with an overhead belly to belly release suplex! Steele: The Dominator showing what he is worth here, holding his own with Gravity. Hughes: Just wait, Gravity will turn it all around in due time. Gravity picks up Dominator quickly, looking to work him over some more. Gravity slaps Dominator in the face a little bit, and then kicks him in the stomach, and locks in a standing grapevine on Dominators head. Gravity locks his arms around Victor’s waist, and then hoists up for a powerbomb! But Dominator won’t go down that quickly, and starts pummeling away on Gravity’s head, weakening the big man. Finally Gravity lets go, and Dominator lands on his feet, kicks Gravity in the stomach and drops him with a flowing DDT! Dominator goes for 1, 2, but a kick out from Gravity stops the 3. Steele: This kid really has what it takes I think! Hughes: You think. Exactly. That's all you do, you think. No real train of thought Steele. Just...thinking. Gravity gets to his knees, a bit worse for the wear. Dominator is scooping him from behind though, ready to attack as Gravity stands up. And when he does, Williams goes in for a standing side kick! But Gravity saw it coming, and snatches Williams’ leg in midair. With the leg in hand, Gravity takes out Dominator’s other one, and then drops an elbow on the leg of Victor. Gravity doesn’t stop there though, as he now kicks away at Dominator’s knee and then locks in a figure four! Gravity rocks back, and yells out "Remember this Saxton? Remember?" as Dominator writhes around in pain. Gravity sits up, and starts jumping up and down on his posterior, attempting to shatter the kneecap of the rookie. But Dominator is double tough as well, and starts to roll the hold over! Gravity screams in pain now as the roles are reversed. But this doesn’t last long as Gravity uses his bigger frame to roll it back over into his advantage! But on the last roll, Gravity took it near the ropes, so Dominator grabs a hold of the bottom rope, breaking it up. Hughes: The figure four, the very move Gravity used to beat Saxton with at Rising! And he’ll do it again at Silent Nightmare. Gravity gets up first, kicking his leg around to get some feeling back in it. Dominator then gets up via help from the ropes, but with a bit of a limp. Dominator turns around, but gets smacked by Gravity right in the jaw! Gravity gives another right, then a left, and a right, but Dominator wants no more, so he gives an elbow to the jaw of Gravity. Gravity is shocked, but angered as well. With blood streaming from his lip, Gravity backs up Dominator into the corner and then goes to town with a series of stomps to the midsection. Finally, after a good 30 seconds of stomps, Gravity lets up, and backs to the far corner as Dominator sits in the corner, head propped against the middle buckle. Gravity scopes Dominator, and then jumps up and down as he chops at his crotch and yells something about "Uck It" before sprinting towards the corner. But Dominator is recovered, and gets two feet up, which plant in the bread basket of Gravity! Gravity falls backwards, holding his stomach. So Dominator gets up, limping, and heads to the top rope. Steele: The Dominator pulls it out somehow, and now he heads to the top rope, possibly for his finisher, The Dominator. Hughes: Boy, original name for his finisher. Pshht. Gravity gets up slowly, and turns around towards Dominator on the corner. But Gravity is able to scout this and leaps for the top rope, which he hits and shakes, causing Dominator to fall off the top buckle and crotch himself on the top rope near Gravity! Gravity goes behind the somewhat sitting Dominator, and hoists him atop his shoulders for the Metamorphosis! Gravity leaps, and drops him down to secure the one, two, and three! Winner via pinfall after the Metamorphosis: Gravity Steele: Well Gravity just eked that one out if you ask me John. Hughes: What! He had him the whole time through. He just didn’t wanna make the new kid look bad. Gravity motions for a mic in the ring, and receives one. Gravity: Hey Ted, you want the Ultimate Hardcore Match at Silent Nightmare 2K1? You’re dumber than I thought. You wanna be hardcore? Well...at Nightmare...I’ll show you what real hardcore wrestling is. Bring the band-aids, Saxton. You’re gonna need ‘em. Gravity tosses the mic down, and walks to the back as "Sober" plays. But when he gets to the top of the ramp, Saxton leaps from behind the curtains and tackles Gravity down to the mat! The two men roll around on the top of the ramp, tussling. But Saxton gets off and waits for Gravity to get up. Saxton charges at the standing Gravity, but Gravity sidesteps and chucks Saxton off the ramp, down through a production crew table! Gravity smirks at the destruction, but stops when Saxton gets right back up and shoots him a hard glare. Gravity goes back to smirking and holds his hands in the air in victory. Steele: Gravity wins that confrontation, but Saxton shows that it's going to take a lot to keep him down. I shudder to think what it will require to get to a second fall on either man. Match 3, 8-Man Tag Match--EEI vs The Trents, Rush, and Sloan The Trents, Rush and Sloan make their way to the ring together, to the little ditty called "Bodies" by Drowning Pool. A video package of career highlights and their banding together against EEI plays on the MercuTron while they do a quadruple turnbuckle pose. Dirge, Brimstone, Glamour and Picasso enter to "Angels Holocaust". A gritty, black-and-white shot of each member is shown on the MercuTron, accompanied by the man's name. The feed changes to full-color highlights from various federations, including those in Mercury. Finally, the letters "E", "E" and "I" shoot out of the background, and the video shatters and crumbles into nothingness. Rush calls out Brimstone while EEI does a huddle to discuss the strategy of how they'll kick their opponents in the groin. Brimstone obliges Rush and steps forward, so Rush attacks with a series of punches hard enough to overcome the size differential! Dirge then spears Rush, and the other members of EEI go to the apron while Dirge pounds away! Steele: Dirty pool. Hughes: Hey, they're smart. As long as the other guy hasn't lifted a finger to attack, the partner can go at it to start the match. Little known rule. Steele: Doesn't make it right. Dirge sends Rush to the ropes and powers up his right arm, then unloads a European uppernut... which Rush blocks, to a pop. Dirge goes for an eye poke, Rush does the Curley block. Dirge pulls out the Moe counter (double-handed poke), Rush ducks, then jumps out of the crouch with a crisp hurricanrana into a rollup, one, two, Picasso breaks it with a missile dropkick to Rush's face! The crowd gets on Picasso's case, but the boo's slide off him like... um... well, let's just say they slide off him. Dirge tags Picasso in, but not before dragging Rush to the EEI corner. Dirge and Picasso take advantage of the '5 second rule' and stomp Rush into a seated position in the corner. Brimstone and Glamour hold Rush's arms, allowing Picasso to go to the other side of the ring and climb on the apron. Picasso slingshots into a springboard, then leaps the entire length of the ring to hit a tremendous missile dropkick right to the face of Rush! Picasso drags Rush to the middle of the ring and hooks a leg, one, two, Josh pulls Picasso off. Before Josh can return to his corner, however, Brimstone runs out and pops Josh in the back of the head, sending him over the top to the outside by the announce table! Bo Smirnow gives Brimstone a stern warning, but is then diverted by Sloan and Mikel trying to get into the ring. This allows Dirge and Glamour to drop roughly a dozen elbows on Rush, each accompanied by a taunt directed towards the opposing corner. Picasso, meanwhile, climbs a neutral corner. By the time the nonlegal men leave the ring, he's in perfect position to launch himself halfway across the ring with the Starry Night shooting star press! He covers, one, two, Josh Trent comes out of nowhere with a 270 Homicidal Tendency legdrop to break it up! This brings in Dirge, Brimstone and Glamour, who take a triple-double clothesline from Mikel and Sloan to a big pop! Five men roll to the floor and brawl, leaving Picasso and Rush in the ring, and Josh alone on the apron waiting for the tag! Picasso and Rush both crawl to their corners for tags, but only one actually touches a partner's hand. Josh sprints across the ring as Picasso pulls himself up, and he nails an incredibly stiff dropkick to the back! Picasso slumps down in a heap, and Josh pulls him to a neutral corner to take advantage. Josh heads up, even as Sloan takes a double-team from Entropy Crusade on the outside, thus freeing Dirge up. Josh turns around, measures the motionless Picasso... and gets his left leg tugged hard by Dirge, causing Josh to fall crotchfirst onto the turnbuckle! Hughes: Dirge, as always, a master off nut psychology! Steele: I'm sure his parents are proud. Dirge gets in the ring to drag Picasso back to the EEI corner, then goes to the apron in order to receive the elementary tag. Dirge bounds over to the hapless Josh, grinning from ear to ear at the potential for pain. Dirge climbs to the very top, and pulls Josh to his feet in a front side headlock. Dirge hooks the near leg and readies a super fisherman's buster! He hefts once, hefts twice, then lets go and punches Josh between the legs. Josh doubles over, then falls face-first to the mat thanks to a slight nudge in the butt from Dirge's boot. Hughes: Screw Harbinger. THAT is psychology you can wrap your mind around! Dirge turns to face the ring on the turnbuckle, and waits for Josh to get up. Dirge extends the index and middle fingers of his right and, and cocks the arm in preparation for the Absolute Zero eye pole. Josh is up, Dirge jumps, and Josh fires off a standing enziguri that connects with Dirge's head in midair! Mikel and Glamour brawl alongside the entrance ramp, Sloan and Brimstone return to the apron. Dirge and Josh crawl incredibly slowly towards freedom, and Sloan and Brimstone enter the fray simultaneously! Brimstone throws a lariat, but that's ducked. Sloan throws a lariat, same deal. Brimstone kicks him in the gut and puts him down for a pumphandle, then lifts him up. Sloan floats over a shoulder and rapidly applies a cobra clutch, then drops Brimstone directly on his head with the Foreclosure of a Dream cobra cluch suplex! Rush comes in and takes a charging Picasso down with an inverted DDT, Sloan makes a cover, one, two, Brimstone gets a shoulder up! The crowd has a collective gasp at the closeness of it, and Sloan is less than happy about having his finisher fail to do the job. Picasso grabs Rush's legs and pulls him to the floor, even as Sloan pulls Brimstone up in a second cobra clutch! Steele: Brimstone is tough, but no man can survive that move twice! Hughes: It ain't ovah 'til I say it is. Steele: Would that make you the fat lady? Hughes: Grrrrrr. Sloan lifts, Brimstone plants his feet. Lift, plant, repeat until Brimstone uses his bodyweight to throw Sloan off judo-style. Sloan is up quickly, but taken down a notch by a hard boot to the abdomen. Brimstone puts Sloan in a pumphandle as Mikel and Glamour return to ringside. Brimstone lifts Sloan, and plants him hard with a stuff inverted powerbomb! Brimstone rolls Sloan onto his back and covers; Glamour holds Mikel back, Dirge holds Josh back, and Picasso holds Rush back, as Smirnow bangs out a one, a two, and a three! Winners via Pinfall: EEI EEI raises their arms in the air, and parade around the ring. But this doesn’t last for long as The Trents and Rush attack! Sloan gets back in the mix, but is taken outside quickly by The Artiste, who pummels away on the rampway. But in the ring The Trents and Rush take over, dumping Brimstone on the outside and powerbombing Dirge onto the hard, cold canvas! Glamour gets his too, with a back-bodydrop onto the rampway! EEI retreats, and high-tail it backstage as "Bodies" by Drowning Pool plays. Steele: Well, that one is far from over. Just like our next match, which has roots that extend back to one year ago! Hughes: Yeah, yeah, yeah. Pariah this, Pariah that. I say poop on Pariah. Steele: You know what? He probably doesn't think two craps of you either! So how about when Pariah comes out, you tell him just how much you care for him, mmm'kay? Hughes: Erm....he knows where to find me! Match 4, LCW 1 Year Anniversary Match -- Blaze McCoy vs Pariah The arena goes dark and the letters OutKast are shown one by one on the screen. Blaze McCoy is shown close up in slow motion from the soles of his boots to the spiked blonde hair on his head. Golden pyro explodes at the center of the ramp and “Outkast” by POD begins to blast throughout the arena. Blaze McCoy steps out from behind the curtain with his wife, the lovely Cherry Stacked. They both ignore the crowd’s boos as they head to the ring. Blaze lifts Cherry over the top rope then leaps over the ropes and heads for the nearest turnbuckle, steps up to the top rope and stares at the crowd as he slowly takes off his sunglasses and gives the “thumbs up” with a smile, and then the “thumbs down” with a frown and another blast of flames are shot out of the other three ring posts. Blaze hops down off the turnbuckle and hands Cherry his glasses, gets a kiss on the cheek and is ready for battle. The intro of Disturbed’s "Down With The Sickness" begins to play as a green spotlight scans the staging area, and fog rises. The lead singer lets out his scream, and bright white lights blind the arena as Pariah appears at from the fog, stomping to the ring. He keeps marching away as green and blue lights swirl all around the arena, and a video package shows some of Pariah’s career highlights on the MercuTron. Pariah hops in the ring, and gets onto the second buckle before throwing his arms outward, slightly bent at the elbows, and screaming as blue, green, and white lights flash all around him. Steele: There is a man that was so close he could taste it. And now, with Harbinger getting some sort of royal treatment, he has every right to get in Beaumont’s face, JOHN, and ask for his rematch. Hughes: Beaumont is doing nothing wrong! He is treating his champion like a champion. Pariah and Blaze are quickly in each other’s face, cursing away. Blaze shoves Pariah back, but Pariah responds by tossing Blaze into the ropes. Pariah leapfrogs, and turns around just in time to give Blaze a Japanese armdrag. Blaze hops up, and locks up with Pariah. Blaze gets the upper hand, shoving Pariah into the corner. But Guzman asks them to break it up, and Blaze does, backing away. But just as Blaze backs away, Pariah grabs him around the head and pulls him into the buckle. With Blaze cornered, Pariah goes to town with chops and boots before backing away, allowing Blaze room to breathe. Blaze comes out, a little worse for the wear, and goes to kick Pariah. But Pariah catches it and spins Blaze around. So Pariah locks his arms around Blaze’s waist, and goes for a German suplex.
