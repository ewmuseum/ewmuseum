Fusion, 29 March 2002

Mercury Fusion - 3.29.02. - Live From The Rose Garden in Portland, Oregon A dim light flickers in a dark room, swinging back and forth as a hand fiddles with the bulb. After giving it a few screws, the light suddenly shines forth with great luminescence as "It Ain’t Like That" by Alice In Chains plays in the background. The hand comes into view again, tinkering with the bulb before it explodes in his hand, morphing the shot into a video package for Mercury Fusion! Pariah celebrates with his world title (There I was, laid out on the table) Geoffrey Slate tosses Magnum Wolf off a cage at Silent Nightmare (Screamin’ Sweat and bare feet to the floor) Harbinger stretches out Pariah (In my life, I’d not soften) The Trents raise the tag titles high above their heads (Things that cut, and burn so often) Magnum Wolf pummels on Rush (But I sit, think of somethin’) Robert Young tosses Gol around (Scared to face, the dyin’ nothin’) Gol superkicks Pariah at Silent Nightmare (See the cycle I’ve waited for) And Oblivion poses in the ring (It ain’t like that anymore) Blaze McCoy punishes Memosarks with a ladder (Where I go is when I feel I’m able) Glamour takes it to Mikel Trent (How I fight is why I’m feelin’ sore) Memosarks frolics about ringside with a wicked grin on his face (In my mind, not forgotten) Pariah spits out a tooth at Nightmare (Feel as though a tooth were rotten) Dave Beaumont sits on Edward Olmstead (Behind the smile a tongue that’s slippin) Picasso works over Josh Trent (Buzzards cry, when flesh is rippin’) Harbinger dreadlocks Blaze McCoy into submission (See the cycle I’ve waited for) Magnum Wolf tosses Slate into the cage (It ain’t like that anymore) Jin Akujin is piledriven from the top rope by Picasso (Here I sit writing on the paper) Pariah Nausea’s Apex (Trying to make the words you can’t ignore) Blaze McCoy delivers the Hook, Line, and Sinker! ( In my eyes, what I’m lacking) Dirge and Brimstone create havoc in a promo (Score at face, a ten for slacking) Gol trash talks in a promo (Sign the deal, set in motion) Slate hugs the North American Title (Smaller fish, so huge the ocean) Harbinger makes Slate feel his wrath(See the cycle I’ve waited for) The Trents deliver some brotherly love (It ain’t like that anymore) Magnum Wolf and Slate trade blows in a cage (See the cycle I’ve waited for) And Pariah drops Gol with a Nausea as the scene crumbles (It ain’t like that anymore) As red and silver pyrotechnics burst throughout the arena, "It Ain’t Like That Anymore" by Alice in Chains plays. As soon as the residue from the pyro clears, the entrance stage becomes more visible. Two large video screens in the shape of an octagon hang on both sides of the entrance tunnel, with swirling lights on all sides of the screens. In the middle, directly above the tunnel hangs a circular video screen that changes from the Fusion logo to the Mercury logo every other 10 seconds. And below that is the entrance tunnel, a long, round tunnel enclosed by Plexiglas that stretches out to where the fan barricades begin. And for a change, there is no ramp to this stage! The Mercury camera crew makes their scan of the fans, prompting the sings to jump out and high in the air. They range this week from "Jin Is Cute!" to "Where is Barry Manilow?" and finally "Pariahamania is Gunna Run Wild!". Puddle of Mudd’s "Blurry" strikes up over the PA, heralding the arrival of KC Steele and "The Iron Man" John Hughes! The two men bicker down the ramp before taking a seat at ringside, and placing their headsets on. KC Steele: Welcome one and all to Mercury Fusion! Tonight is going to be a huge one, as all the titles are on the line! "Iron" John Hughes: I do believe though, that Dave Beaumont wants to kick the show off properly, so we best be giving him the proper time! Metallica’s "Struggle Within" hits, and Dave Beaumont stomps to the ring, a few cheers this week. He hops in, mic in hand. Dave: All right, let's cut to the chase. I want the Oblivion out here right now! The arena goes pitch black and the word Oblivion forms in ice on the MercuTron as Static X's "Cold" hits. The arena lights pulsate in blue to the music, with various spotlights scanning the entrance area as the iced word begins to crack and soon shatter revealing all four members of Oblivion standing in an undisclosed location. The video flashes to the name Magnum Wolf, presenting his name in ice as a half shot of Magnum's face is shown on the screen by his name, soon turning into clips of his most famous matches before shattering. The video then switches over to The Gol as his name is also presented in ice with him posing next to his name, and shots of his career are flashed before crumbling away as well. The video flashes over revealing The Trents with their name also in ice and the two men standing side by side, a menacing look on their faces and clips of their tag career interspersed with various things being blown up, broken, etc. The video feed freezes over before crumbling away again, and revealing the Oblivion once again standing amidst rubble and other broken things before switching to the ring Dave: Nice to see you four gentlemen. I should point out that while I'm willing to let you go about your business, anyone who lays a finger on me gets suspended for a month without pay. Gol takes the 'group mic'. Gol: Why hit you, when you'll be feeling the Fall from Grace until the day you die? They all share a laugh. Beaumont absently rubs his lower back. Dave: Be that as it may, I'm afraid that there's one more rule I'm laying down. See, I'll tolerate beatings and interference. That's how the game is played these days. But, it's the secrecy that I find so distasteful. So, until I find out two things, all of you will each be fined one thousand dollars a day! Crowd pop. The Oblivion members no longer seem happy. Dave: Number one. Which one of you hoodlums is the ringleader? And number two, who else- if anyone- is in the stable? I'm not gonna tolerate any more show endings like we had last week. I want the other wrestlers to know what they're going into. Josh Trent takes the Oblivion mic. Josh: We'll let you know who else is with us when we damn sure feel like it! He passes it to his brother. Mikel: And as for the first thing, the Leader will be revealed when the Leader chooses to be revealed. All I'm gonna say is, it's none of us. Mikel passes it to Magnum Wolf. Magnum: It's going to be sad to get a pay cut, but we won't let that force us to bow to your demands. In case you didn't get the message, we're the only ones making demands around here. Right now, we demand the following. First, I get a title shot against Pariah or Rob Young, and second, that the Trents get a shot at the tag champs, both next week. Dave: Whoa, simmer down nah. I'd love to do that, but I'm afraid that I have brain cells. I don't want to shove my champs into a situation like that all at once. I'm letting Gol and Slate test the 'Oblivion title shot' waters, and if I like what happens, then maybe I'll consider it. However, for the moment I'll give you the following alternative match. It's gonna be you. Points to Magnum. Dave: You. Points to Josh Trent. Dave: And you. Points to Mikel Trent Dave: In a six-man elimination match against Harbinger, Picasso and Slate! The winner will automatically get a Mercury title shot at the next pay-per-view, Suicide! Crowd pop. Hey, what do you know, Oblivion pop too. Dave: I'm glad you all agree. Now get the hell out of the ring you worthless sacks of crap! Gol makes the "scary stuff" fingers movement, and they all walk out of the ring, Beaumont after them as "Struggle Within" hits up again. Steele: Big news from Dave Beaumont, laying down some minor groundwork for our next big PPV, Suicide! Hughes: Speaking of suicide, I think Jin Akujin has a death threat. And you’ll see why here in a second. Match 1, Singles Match-Magnum Wolf vs Jin Akujin Master of the Shadows" begins playing as out comes Jin Akujin. Jin rubs his wrists as he makes his way down to the ring wearing his leather jacket with the easily recognizable "flame circle" stitched to the back. The "Kamikaze Tiger" leaps over the ropes then makes his way to the turnbuckle, where he removes his jacket, and hangs it on that very turnbuckle. Jin smiles as he leans against the turnbuckle, waiting for the match to start. Steele: I’d be a fool to say Jin isn’t one of the better athletes here in Mercury... Hughes: Also one with a perfect complexion....*sigh*. The lights in the arena die. Darkness and silence fall... when suddenly, the entranceway is filled with flashes of blue and red. "Courage" by Alien Ant Farm blasts the crowd, but then the lights die again and all you can hear is the guitar. As the chorus hits, the lights come back on in blue and red, and standing at the end of the entrance tunnel is Magnum Wolf. He hops in the ring as "Courage" fades and the lights return to normal. Hughes: Eek, he looks pissed. The bell rings, and Akujin circles Magnum, who has a cold as stone look cast upon his face. Jin is first to strike with a blunt kick to the back of Magnum’s right thigh, followed up by another one to the calf, and one to the back of the knee, dropping Magnum Wolf! But Magnum gets up, kicks air a few times with his right leg, and slicks his hair back before snarling and rushing after Jin! But Jin sidesteps, spins, hits the ropes, and nails Magnum with a flying forearm! Magnum is dazed, so Jin hits the ropes again, and fires off with a short dropkick to the back of Magnum’s knees, dropping the big man again! Steele: Jin is just blinding with that speed! Magnum gets up, gingerly, but is soon met with firm toe kick from Jin right in the mush! Magnum bends over, holding his jaw and mouth, so Jin hits the ropes behind Magnum, leaps in the air, grabs Magnum’s head, and hits a bulldog! Jin pushes Magnum over like he is a log, and covers for one, t..Magnum Wolf bench presses Jin off of him and onto the mat for the kickoff! Hughes: Magnum’s raw strength and power paying off again. Steele: That is true, but Jin is putting Magnum in a place he does not want to be this early in the match, on his back! Jin pops up from the mat, but Magnum is up first, and catches Akujin with a hard jab that sends Jin backwards and into the corner. Magnum backs up, rushes in, and hits a knee to the midsection of Jin, nearly collapsing the newcomers lungs! Wolf backs away, and fires off with multiple kicks to the midsection of Akujin, dropping the young sensation down on the mat, in the corner, where Magnum stomps a mudhole and walks it dry! Steele: Now, this isn’t even wrestling, this is just a beating Magnum is attempting to deliver to young Akujin. Hughes: That is the point of wrestling! Beat the hell out of one guy and get the pin, get the money, get the belt! With Jin fairly well weathered, Magnum picks him up by the hair, and tosses him out of the corner nearly half way across the ring! With a bit of a limp, Magnum sits Jin up on the mat, backs up, takes a fake bow while making various sounds like Jackie Chan on crack, rearing back, and going for a kick to the head. But Jin rolls backwards, pops up, and delivers a sickening roundhouse kick to the side of Wolf’s head, dropping the big man like a red wood tree. Jin picks up Magnum, tosses him in the ropes, spins around, and nails a picture perfect roaring elbow! Jin covers and picks up 1, 2, kick out from Magnum Wolf! Steele: When it looked like Jin was down, he got up, and keeps going at it! Hughes: I have to admit he is pretty amazing. With Jin very much so on the offensive, Magnum rolls out of the ring, and kicks the ring steps in frustration. But little does Magnum know that Jin is on the top rope, looking down at him. As Magnum turns around, Jin leaps off, and forms his body into a beautiful cross body block that connects, and sends him and Magnum tumbling into the announce table! Jin gets up, the crowd firmly behind him, and full of energy! Hughes: KC, are we witnessing one of the biggest upsets in Mercury- and perhaps LCW- history? Steele: You’re g** damn right we are! Jin picks up the groggy Magnum, but Magnum fires off with a very low blow, and shoves Jin backwards, into the ring post! Holding his back, Jin staggers right into Magnum Wolf, who cleans his clock, leveling Jin to the floor where Magnum hops on and begins to punch in rapid succession! After numerous punches, Magnum picks Jin up, and smashes his head 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 times on the announce table! Hughes: Stop Magnum! His face, his beautiful face! Magnum doesn’t adhere to John’s words though, as he soon removes the top two stairs on the ring steps, places Jin face down on the steps, and grabs a steel chair, a sick look on his face. Steele: Oh no...he wouldn’t! He couldn’t! *THWAP* A steel chair crashes across the skull of Jin Akujin, sandwiching it between the chair and the steel steps as the bell rings. Hughes: He did... Winner via DQ: Jin Akujin But the carnage doesn’t stop there, as Magnum picks up the now very bloody Jin, and rams his headfirst into the announce table again, blood pouring like wine from a bottle all over the announce table. With Jin lying on the floor, a bloody mess, Magnum leans over the rookie, shouting, "You want to play in the big leagues? Well welcome to the big leagues." before walking off. Steele: Now that was just uncalled for! I can’t wait for the day when Magnum Wolf gets his! Hughes: I can’t guarantee you anything, Steele. Steele: Well, I can guarantee you that Harbinger and Memosarks will show The Trents who their daddy is here next. Hughes: Did you just say "their daddy", KC? Match 2, Tag Match-Harbinger and Memosarks vs The Trents Confetti starts to fall from the rafters over the entranceway and Mr. Bungle's "Golem II The Bionic Vapor Boy" fills the arena, signaling the arrival of Memosarks, who appears at the top of the entrance ramp, a big grin on his face. He slowly walks to the ring, smiling at all the cameras as he goes, and stopping at one to give it a big smile and a wave. The eerie first seconds "Playground for a Wedgeless Firm" lead to the word 'Harbinger' slowly fading in on the MercuTron. It flashes at the sound of the thunderclap, and a picture of Harbinger's face takes its place while he walks out to the top of the entrance ramp, suprisingly to some cheers. At the first electronic pulse, the picture of his face is slowly replaced by clips of Harbinger wrestling, including him doing the Purge, Dreadlock, and many variations of the Punk-Out. Harbinger walks down the ramp to the drumbeat, rolls into the ring, and stretches out before the start of the match. Hughes: That's about as odd a pairing as I've ever seen. Steele: It's like Tom Green and Drew Barrymore in the wrestling world. Hughes: So you're saying Memosarks is Drew Barrymore? Steele: *sigh* The Trent Brothers' video package begins with a close-up of Mikel Trent's face, his eyes closed, as the beginning of Drowning Pool's "Bodies" fills the arena. Mikel's eyes then open, and his face fills with anger and rage before the camera pulls back to show his elder brother Josh with a smile on his face. The feed then switches to a split screen as pyro erupts from the ramp and the brothers appear from behind the curtain. On the left of the MercuTron, Josh hits several high-flying moves and attacks enemies with weapons, while on the right, Mikel spears and pummels other opponents. As the brothers reach ringside, their video ends with a clip of Mikel powerbombing Josh through an opponent and a table. Steele: The Trents are the newest members of Oblivion, and Josh in particular seems to have gotten a lot more aggressive in how they're approaching wrestling. Hughes: ...meaning they might stop being meek little fan-favorites? Steele: I think the fan-favorites part speaks for itself. Josh plants himself in the middle of the ring and calls for Harbinger. Once Memosarks and Mikel are on the apron, Josh sprints at his now-hated rival with abandon. Harbinger halts the charge with a sharp palm strike, then he puts Josh in a front headlock. Josh tries to turn it into a northern lights suplex, but Harbinger starts roughly kneeing him in the chest. Harbinger releases the headlock once Josh stops struggling, and he pushes Josh on the chest while sweeping his near leg out in a single fast motion. The speed of the move creates enough impact to wind Josh and allow Harbinger to straddle his chest for a series of stiff palm strikes, until Josh is able to grab Harbinger by the shoulders and throw him off. Hughes: Wow, someone sure lit a fire under Harbinger's ass. Josh is gonna be feeling those shots tomorrow. And tonight for that matter. Josh rolls back to get some distance, only to charge at Harbinger a second time. He throws a punch halfway, then when Harbinger goes to block the throws a kick instead, settling up a hasty snap suplex. Josh hurriedly fires off a somersault legdrop and covers, getting all of a one count before Harbinger kicks out at one and pops up to his feet. Parts of the crowd cheer, the first time such has happened for Harbinger in the states this year. Josh, not appreciating Harbinger's lax selling, walks up to him and starts to say some things that wouldn't make it onto television at full volume. Harbinger slaps him hard on the right temple, but Josh doesn't budge and keeps right on talking. Harbinger does it a second time, with more force, so Josh responds by biting him on the left eyebrow! Hughes: Josh is a cannibal, and now Harby is bleeding! Those two will only feed each other! Harbinger is stunned from the unexpectedness of the bite, allowing Josh to run the ropes and clobberize him with a haymaker. Harbinger gets his wits and stays on his feet, but Josh goes to the well for another running punch. Harbinger avoids this by stepping forward and launching an upwards palm thrust to the chin that knocks Josh on his backside hard! Josh now pops right up, and he sends Harbinger down with a dropkick! Once the adrenaline rush slows, both men take some time to reach their feet again. With a smirk exchanged between the two of them, they tag out at the same time. Steele: What hard-hitting action from these two, though I'm surprised Harbinger would use so many strikes. Hughes: Harbinger wants revenge for getting humiliated last week. Nothing feels better in cases like that than smacking someone in the head. Mikel Trent goes for much the same strategy as his brother, charging straight at Memosarks, who cartwheels out of the way of a shoulderblock en route to nailing a spinning heel kick. With Mikel down, Memo runs to the ropes and springs off with an asai moonsault, only to see Mikel roll out of the way; Memosarks adjusts in time and lands on his feet, and which point Mikel gives him an Irish whip. Memo is pulled into a tilt-a-whirl, but he uses his agility to reverse that with a crisp armdrag takeover! Both men up, Memo runs in and takes a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Steele: Interesting. Mikel was patient and used the move until it worked. Mikel slaps palms with Josh, who comes in and takes advantage of a rising Memosarks with the Joker's Wild jumping axe kick! Cover, one, two, Harbinger pulls Josh off. Josh flips Harbinger off, then drags Memosarks up and ducks down for a released northern lights suplex. Josh tells Harbinger to "watch this, (censored)!" as he goes out to the apron. With Memosarks in the middle of the ring, Josh springboards for all he's worth in order to hit a spectacular swandive 450 splash! The crowd forgets that they hate him and give the move a standing ovation while Josh hooks a leg, one, two, two and a half, Harbinger runs in and gives Josh a loud kick to the head such that it's heard in the cheap seats! Hughes: I don't think someone just lit a fire under Harby's ass; with a shot like that, I'm tempted to say the fire is IN his ass! Steele: What insightful commentary. Josh dropkicks Harbinger out of the ring for breaking the cover, then tags Mikel in (but doesn't exit himself). After a brief moment of position, Mikel grabs Josh and lifts him up for the Brotherly Love powerbomb! With Josh on Mikel's shoulders, Memosarks sits up and gives a double kick to Mikel's left shin, causing Mikel to fall back while Josh awkwardly lands on top of his chest! Josh rolls out of the ring to recover, Memosarks rolls over to his corner and tags in Harbinger, who heads right over to the confused Mikel Trent and plants him on his head with a backdrop driver! Satisfied that Mikel's brains are scrambled, Harbinger pulls him up to his knees and clamps down hard with the Dreadlock! Mikel's free arm wavers, then hurriedly flails in a signal for submission just as Josh reenters the ring and slams a steel chair down on Harbinger's head! Winners by Submission: Harbinger and Memosarks Memosarks comes in to make the save, only to be blasted by the chair as well! The crowd roars, as Memosarks clenches his arms and stays on his feet... only to be felled by a second shot. Hughes: Memosarks no-sold a chairshot?! Steele: More than that, Josh spent time getting a chair when he could have been saving Mikel from the Dreadlock. That tells me he was more concerned with hurting Harbinger than winning the match! As Josh is poised to rain the metal down some more on his opponents, Dirge and Picasso run out of the tunnel with pipes in hand, causing Josh to head for the hills! Mikel isn't so nimble, however, and he takes a nasty whack from Picasso along the way. Steele: Dirge and Picasso were both beaten up by Oblivion last week, and it looks like they've gotten a measure of that back this week. Hughes: Just one battle in the war, KC. Steele: Well, that is the truth, 100%, as it continues on in our next match, a North American Title match where Gol takes on Slate! Match 3, Singles Match For The North American Title-"Devastating" Geoffrey Slate (c) vs The Gol Steele: This is one I've been waiting a long time to see, John. Geoffrey Slate, current North American champion, defending against the man many believe made the belt that Slate now holds, Gol. Hughes: I'm liking Gol as part of the Oblivion, what he did to Headbuster two weeks ago was right up my alley. From the stage a large blast of white pyro shoots just above the heads of the fans. Disturbed's "Violence Fetish" heralds the entrance of Geoffrey Slate; on a pitch-black MercuTron appears the word "Devastation", which quickly fades into a standard entrance video featuring a splicing of stills and clips which is made special primarily by the ring of fire encircling it. Slate walks coolly and calmly to the ring, barely recognizing the fans on the way. He slides into the ring and hands his North American title to the referee, then awaits his opponent. 'Prick' by Something For Kate, plays, as the arena lights dim and smoke covers the entranceway. On the Mercutron, the letters G-O-L each flash onto the screen, in bright green, interspersed with rapid clips of Gol doing various things, such as parading with the National Title and walking to the ring. As the song hits the heavier riff, Gol walks out under the entranceway and into the aisle, looking up at the Mercutron, which now shows clips of him in action, delivering the Sydney Superkick to various LCW superstars past and present. Gol turns around as golden pyro fires down the aisle and to the ring, and he proceeds down to the ring, sliding under the ropes and posing on all four corners to huge jeers and boos from the crowd. Slate and Gol circle each other, Gol stepping in occasionally to put Slate on the back foot, then locks up with him, driving the smaller man into the corner. The ref breaks the lock, and both men return to center ring. Slate this time initiates the lockup, and forces Gol back, then knees him in the gut to get things underway. Gol doubles over, leaving himself open to several axehandle blows to the back of the neck, then a whip and a clothesline from Slate which sends the big man to the canvas and rolling out of the ring. Gol rests for a moment on the outside as Slate stares him down. The ref begins his count, which hurries Gol back into the ring, and takes a kick at the approaching Slate, connecting in the stomach. Gol then locks in a side headlock, wrenching down on Slate, trash talking him as he throws punches with his free hand. Slate backs Gol into the ropes and pushes him across the ring, and attempts a backdrop on Gol as he comes back, but meets a high knee for his troubles, the force of which sends him back into the ropes, and as he tries to regain his balance, a powerful Gol clothesline knocks him over to the floor. Gol taunts Slate from the ring, flexing and posing for the fans, who don't seem to appreciate it. Slate slides in under the ring and Gol charges in, going for a flying clothesline which Slate counters by simply grabbing his arm and turning it into an armdrag. Slate holds onto the now extended limb, and drops a leg over it, then yanks it back two different ways. Gol groans in pain, but manages to swing a leg at the ropes, eventually getting his toes over the bottom rope by the slimmest of margins. Slate releases at the end of the refs 5 count, and stands back, letting Gol get to his feet, which he does while shaking out his arm. Steele: Both men just feeling each other out in these early stages, both men know how important this match is. Gol locks up with Slate once more and attempts a headlock takeover, but Slate lands on his feet and pushes back up, then hits Gol with a jawbreaker. Gol grabs his jaw and Slate charges, flies, and hits a running neckbreaker, after which he covers for one, two and not a whole lot more. Slate helps Gol to his feet, then puts on a front facelock, attempting a vertical suplex, but Gol uses his weight as he's pulled up in the air to release himself from Slate's grasp, and as Slate turns around Gol fires a no-look Sydney Superkick out of nowhere, which just clips Slate's shoulder and spins him around, straight into a Gol overhead belly to belly suplex. Slate gets up gingerly holding his shoulder, which Gol capitalizes on by kicking Slate in the gut then hitting a shoulderbreaker, which sees Slate roll to the corner in pain. Gol closes in and stomps away at Slate, eventually forcing him out of the ring, which gives Gol some much needed posing time. Slate walks around the outside of the ring, swinging his arm around to get some feeling into his shoulder. Gol stalks him from in the ring, but gets a bit too close and Slate lunges, grabbing his foot and tripping the big man. Slate slides into the ring and grabs Gol's head from behind as the Oblivion member gets to his knees, driving it into the mat with a mini-bulldog. Slate covers, but Gol kicks out with authority before the referee can even slap the mat once. Slate helps Gol up, throws a few roundhouse rights, then a knee to the gut leaves Gol open for Slate to jump up, hooks his head and hit a high power DDT. Gol's head bounces up off the mat with the impact, and Slate rolls him over, cover one, two, kick out. Gol is in daydream land as he gets to his feet, leaving him easy pickings for Slate to punch away at, circling the former National champion and throwing jabs at the side of his head. Gol eventually falls to his knees under the blows, leaving him open for Slate to pick him up and drive him onto his head again, this time with a swinging DDT. Gol's head hits the mat violently and Slate covers, but Gol groggily gets his shoulder up at around two. Steele: Gol in big trouble here, Slate is wrestling smart, keeping Gol dazed and no man can take too many high impact blows to the skull, it could be a matter of time. Hughes: Gol would have to have a plan here, Steele, he's one of the smartest men in LCW! Steele: Yeah yeah, sure, I bet the best plan he has is for Oblivion to run down here and save his backside. Hughes: Hey, sometimes the simplest plans work best. Slate kicks Gol as he gets to his feet, and locks on another facelock for seemingly another DDT. Gol fights weakly to escape, punching Slate in the chest, so Slate instead hooks Gol's leg and lifts him high in a fisherman suplex, but unfortunately for him, Gol is just too damn big for him to hold onto, and he loses his grip on him at the two count. Slate stays on the offensive, grabbing Gol's head and throwing a few jabs into his temple, then hooking him up for a double underhook piledriver, but Gol sees what could be coming and desperately shifts his weight, falling back to the ground on his feet and tripping Slate, then slingshotting him through the ropes and straight into the barrier outside. Gol rubs his head and rests on the corner, shaking out the cobwebs and what is probably a pretty wicked headaches. Slate gets up slowly and rolls into the ring, where Gol meets him and the two men, the North American champion and his challenger, go toe to toe, slugging it out with big roundhouse rights. Gol throws a big right that puts Slate to one knee, then Slate fires back with an uppercut that pushes Gol backwards to the ropes, but Gol bounces off them and swings another wild roundhouse to Slate's jaw, which knocks him backwards and to the canvas. Gol nods and taunts Slate to get up, which he does, albeit slowly, and Gol locks up with him, attempting to get Slate onto his shoulders, which fails as Slate slides down Gol's back and pushes him into the ropes, Gol comes back, shoulder down and charging in for a spear, which Slate jumps, but Gol is going so quickly that as Slate lands and turns around he's just in time to see Gol's elbow flying towards him at great pace, with Gol behind it, landing a powerful flying forearm. Slate sits up, but falls back to the mat again, and Gol perches himself on the second rope, yelling at Slate to get up. Slate eventually gets to a knee, which Gol sees as good enough, flying from the corner with a dropkick to Slate's face, which bends him over back on his knees. Gol hoists Slate up in a front facelock, and pauses for a moment, to decide what to hit the champion with, decides on a jackhammer suplex and hits it, staying on top of Slate for the one, two, and just not three. Gol gets up quickly and pulls Slate up with him, punching him into the corner then whipping him across the ring with enough force to send Slate into the tenth row if the opposite corner didn't abruptly stop his momentum. He stumbles out of the corner with the impact, straight into Gol's clutches, where a sitout powerbomb is waiting, as Gol throws him high in the air then follows through, the referee is caught out of position and slides across the ring to count one, two, thr...no, not three, as Slate kicks weakly at Gol's face and breaks the pin.
