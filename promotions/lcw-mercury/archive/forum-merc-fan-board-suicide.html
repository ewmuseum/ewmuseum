Alright, let's set things straight right now: First off, this post has been cleaned up, and any and all incendiary statements (except for Tim's initial post, which I let slide because it was only mildly offensive and was posted in a joking tone) have been removed. This post was intended for Suicide feedback, and from this point on, any and all posts like this, regardless of which board they are on, will be treated with respect and not turn into bashfests. I had a big part in this one going downhill, and for that, I apologize. Secondly, any posts asking for feedback should be met with respect and not ridicule. That means if you're going to criticize, at least make it constructive. Third, regardless of what board this is, these are still the LCW forums, so if you've got venom for other members of LCW or for either fed, don't bring it here. If you absolutely feel like you can't contain yourself any longer, then take it to the OOC board, that way, it's contained in and amongst the LCW family, and it doesn't reflect poorly upon us as a whole. Finally, as I said in another post on the main LCW forum, if we're going to be competitive because of PTC, let's keep it a friendly competition that acts as a motivator for everyone involved, and not a point of dissension to that point that people get upset. And, in an unrelated note - Evo and Merc are two completely different products. Merc follows the more traditional e-wrestling model with the majority of the focus on several matches and minimal emphasis on individual characters outside of the ring (I should know; it's a formula that I put into place in the old MMWF that has carried over to this day). Evo, on the other hand, tries to put as much focus on the characters as it does the matches, which means that we're essentially shooting for two different audiences. Does that mean one is better than the other? Not in the least. It does mean that each has its own pros and cons, and each should be viewed with an open mind rather than trying to pidgeonhole the other fed's show into the model that your fed prefers/produces. This isn't the first time we've had strife between the two feds when the competition became too serious, but I'd very much like for it to be the last time. Let's show some tolerance, people; I know we're all mature enough here, especially since the two members of LCW who happen to be 14 have refrained from getting involved in this debate.
