LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Please all Read Please all Read Posted Thursday, May 30, 2002 by Jin Akujin OOC: Sorry about the last post, I am using the keyboard to operate the computer since my mouse doesn't work. About the late post, it's because my computer messed up over the weekend; M-Dos anyway, so I'm waiting for new parts to build one with my dad and brother.This temporary modem can't even read a mouse, much less go to very many sites. I was lucky to get into this won without it shutting down on me, so to everyone; I'm sorry. ------------------------------ *It was around the mid night time that Jin Akujin was in a ring in training gear including black sweats, a white tanktop, and black sneakers to match. The rookie was running into the ropes to test out his speed, he needed to be fast enough to overwhelm Slate in what could be the biggest match of his career since it was for the biggest prize in Mercury, but could he do it? Could he, a rookie, beat Slate for the title? Jin stopped in the middle of the ring and bended down while rubbing his neck.* "You need to slow down and take it easy." Jin: Can't and won't, this match is much too important for me to not train my hardest for. "But your neck is straining just from bouncing against the ropes." Jin: Heh, that means that it's starting to toughen up. Young was what he said he was; he is the master at holding down someone's potential like he did Harbinger and myself. Enough about him, he's not my concern right now. The person or should I say champion that I get to face this week is none other than Geoffery Slate, the man that defeated Gol for what has to be the most sought after title in Mercury. A belt with more history behind it's early life than most titles have in an intire life. *Jin stood up fully keeping a hand on his neck.* Jin: And I get to be his first challenger, should I be honored or excited. No, a veteran never ever gets excited before something huge is about to happen, they play it calmly and that's what I have to do. Slate is also taking this calmly from my perspective, it would also give him the advantage if I was excited. My mind must be cleared and focused going into this match, I must not let anything stand in my way as I try to make myself grow stronger in both mind and body. That's the mistake that I did with Harbinger, my mind wasn't strong enough because I let my emotions over rule my judgement and it gave him the win. The mistake must not happen again and I am going to just that. "Heh, Slate worked hard to get this title. You and I both know that he is going to do his hardest to keep it around his waist, afterall.. He doesn't want to have the shortest title reign in the history of Mercury. Something that Gol currently has the record of." Jin: I know that as well, Slate doesn't want to lose the Mercury title so early. He wants to keep it in his hands as long as possible, just like any other Mercury wrestler would want to do. Take in mind that he did defeat the most experienced wrestler next to Robert Young, something that I haven't done. Experience, as they say, is everything. Another thing that he has an edge over me is the fact that he does have more experience as a Mercury wrestler than I do. Afterall, he was brought straight in while I went through LTD, fighting opponents such as Headbuster. It saddens me that after going through all that; that the best LTD wrestlers such as Headbuster and Inferno are beaten here. It's not surprising, Mercury is the step up from the "farm league". But does that make the Mercury wrestlers better than those of the former farm league, LTD? Yes, it's because the best that LTD had to offer was put to shame here. Inferno started that ridiculous tag team with Blaze McCoy that didn't even make much success, Headbuster went back to LTD, and I am on a losing streak as of late. I was one half of the first ever LTD Tag Team champions, but my skills have been pushed past the limit here in Mercury. For instance, my self now could easily defeat the Jin Akujin that was in LTD quite soundly. My technical skills have increased such much more than before because those skills had to be pushed to the limit and beyond to go against someone with the technical mastery of Harbinger. Not to mention that come from the land where technical wrestling is the main thing in the ring, I grew up watching it. You could say that I have been doing it all my life. *Jin rubbed his neck once again trying to message the pain out of it.* "Point made and taken. Slate is still more dangerous at striking than anything else." Jin: He can brawl with the best of them, that's his strong point. He could dominate me at Power wrestling because it's not my style just like I could dominate at technical wrestling or high flying. It all depends on a person's personal style, Slate prefers brawling and power wrestling compared to my technical and high flying styles. Martial arts is good to have though, it teaches dicipline and control. My style is flashy like Tae Kwon Do, it's straight to the point, something that I am going to demonstrate to Mr.Slate. I must focus the pain out of my system, it's how Harbinger and Young took advantage of me. The pain in my neck grows which each blow or submission put onto it, so I must figure out a way to keep Slate away from it. He isn't a idiot or even stupid, he will go for the body part; like my neck for instance, to win the match quickly. I am also guessing that he will hold down my full potential as I will do to him, that Penance move of his could come out of no where as some of my moves.. *Jin rolled out the ring.* Jin: I will keep my mind focused and my body strong..
