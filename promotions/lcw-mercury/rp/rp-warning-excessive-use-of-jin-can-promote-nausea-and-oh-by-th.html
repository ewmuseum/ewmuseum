Posted Sunday, April 7, 2002 by MagnumWolf ***The scene bursts to life with the Oblivion symbol. A crowd can be heard in the background. Not cheering, or booing. Moreover, just being a crowd. Loud yells every so often... sort of like they're waiting for something big to happen. The scene shows Fusion... it shows Sickness in a headlock. Now it pulses back and forth from Magnum Wolf to Sickness along with the beat of "Courage". Magnum's eyes are once again very sullen... his mind seemingly blank. Abruptly, the video package cuts off. The song continues. A new scene fades onto the screen. It is of Magnum Wolf's face. His eyes are deeply sunken in... he seems very tired... but you can still sense cunning. He breathes deeply... and then begins to laugh. A haunting laugh. It ends only when he begins to speak, and even then, you can hear chuckles in his voice...*** ((Magnum Wolf)) To give yourself so fully to one person whom you barely know. To accept everything that that person says to you just because she says it. To admit to everyone you meet that you love someone... love them so much that you would do anything for them... it just shows you weakness. Herbert... you were so much better than everyone in the world. You were the best there is in professional wrestling. And everyone knew it. But that whole sentence I just said was in past tense. Just like you. The Oblivion took your life, and twisted it. Twisted it to fit into our perspective. CONTROLLED you, Herbert. CONTROLLED... you. How does that even feel? Does it feel like love is supposed to? Does it feel like flowers, and warm sunshine like everyone says it does? I have a feeling it doesn't. Not for you, anyway. Sickness brings baggage with her that could fill more airplane terminals than you could ever imagine... but yet, you believed that deep down in there somewhere, you had done the one thing no man had ever done to her. Caused her to love you. The Oblivion is as close to love as she will come. We feed off of her, and her off of us. She wants things done... she wants to see things happen... she wonders how people will react when treated a certain way, and Oblivion gives that to her. We cause her dreams to come true. Did you see the way her eyes lit up when Harbinger's heart broke into a million pieces? That was a look of pure ectsacy. Did you take a look around, Harby? With all of your holier than thou art s[Bleep!] you managed to alienate everyone in the LCW who would help you now. You thought you had Sickness, and that was all you needed. You're nothing now, Ingles. You're less than nothing. No Dave, no Sickness, no LCW Title, no one to help you, and now no pride. With you out of our way, the LCW will finally begin to take shape. Finally begin to become the federation we would all like to see. ***Magnum's eye twitch slightly, and you can see a blood vessel above his right eye begin to pulse very quickly. His wicked smile has managed to stay on his face throughout this interview thus far. As he looks at the camera with narrow eyes, his smile grows even a bit more...*** ((Magnum Wolf)) Geoffrey Slate. You weak-minded punk. How DARE you try to make a mockery of The Oblivion? Staying out of the match until the very end? Trying to get the upper hand by... CHEATING? No, no, no... we don't play those games. That was my match to win all night regardless of you. I was on my A-game to no end, Geoffrey. Is that why you decided to go ahead with that plan you came up with? I dropped Harby on his head... CLEAN PIN... One of the first men in a very long time to do that. As you watched him fall from about 9 feet up, and land crooked on his neck, you just couldn't bear to step into the ring with me, right? Yea. I know. It's called fear. And fear has taken over your mind, Slate. When you saw me coming after you, you got scared. When you saw Sickness in a chicken wing... you got even more scared. When you heard Harbinger screaming like a little baby over his darling being choked out, you GOT SCARED... it's ok, G. We expect you to be scared. That's the whole frickin point. And it seems to be working to perfection... because now, you're beyond scared. Now, you're terrified. You don't know what to do, who to talk to, or how to act. You never know who's standing behind you with a knife. You don't even know if you can trust ole Harbinger. Maybe he is behind all of this... damn, wouldn't it be perfect? Let Magnum Wolf go into Suicide and destroy Pariah... then the next week, schedule a title match at Fusion, and have Magnum pull the old Hogan/Nash routine! WHAM! Harby's back on top. Can we really believe that love won't conquer a little ripping out of one's heart? I'm not sure. Well. I am. But you aren't. Immoral peices of s[Bleeep!]? That really hurts me, Geoffrey. It really does. Think back to that title match we had at a few PPVs ago... think really hard about what I said to you before that match. Think about my promos against Gol... it's all about emotions. You HAVE to have them. I have emotions... I have morals... but right now, those emotions and morals have been pushed to the wayside. There are more important things to be done, and I can't let my own feelings stand in the way. Bringing the LCW back to the point it should be at is of far greater consequence than whether or not I think it's right to use Harbinger to get where I want. The greater good is so much greater... that things that have to do with morals are no longer dilemmas. They're ignored. Things are happening right now, Slate. Things that you could never understand... things you could never do... because you are far too naive. You have always said that you'll simply win by out-wrestling us. It's not going to happen. Because everytime you think you have us beaten, there will be another Sickness in a headlock waiting around the corner to end a match... or your career. This is not a game anymore, Slate. But you're treating it like one. This has become so much more, and has left you standing by the wayside wondering where the world went. It went with us, Geoffrey. The world has shifted to rotate around us. Titles don't matter... what matters is the trophies we carry. Harbinger's heart... your soul... Dave's respect... every body that we've beaten since our arrival... that's what matters right now. We're cleansing a very dirty wound. And only now... only with Harbinger being shut out of the picture... is that wound beginning to heal. And it won't finish healing until we damn well say it will. ***Magnum's eyes shift from the right to the left...*** ((Magnum Wolf)) I appreciate your words of encouragement, Slate. But I have to tell you that I don't NEED Oblivion. You're right about that. I ENJOY Oblivion. I LOVE to stand by and watch men's hearts and minds and souls fall apart. It makes me feel just a little bit better about myself each and every time it happens. Why, you ask? Why the hell not? I'm so sick of being treated like a second class citizen no matter where I go, or what I do. Every LCW fan knew my name... but none of them cared. Because of guys like Harbinger, Gravity, Lex Laww, "Superstar" Steven Ryle... you name any damn one of them... they all overshadowed everything the federation had to offer. And for what reason other than Dave Beaumont and Edward Olmstead wanting them to shine? What reason? Charisma? Are you kidding? Talent? You MUST be kidding. No... it's because they would do whatever the hell was asked of them by the president of their federation. Steven Ryle changed his whole f[Bleeep!]ng persona just so that he could be a champion. How can you respect a federation that has KAMIKAZE... the most hardcore man I have ever met in my life... with his hair cut short, and slicked back in some chode haircut? All for the almighty dollar? Not me, that's for damn sure. And that's why I LIKE Oblivion, Slate. We don't do crap like that. We are who we are... and we ruin those people's lives who stand against us, and who don't deserve what they are getting. It's really a rather noble cause... one I think that you are undertaking as well. But, you don't realize who is on the right side here. You see us taking these steps that you believe to be evil, and immediately assume that we're the bad guy. Keep looking around, Slate. Keep your eyes open, and I think that the world will begin to take shape soon enough. You'll start to see the big picture. ***Magnum stops talking, somewhat abruptly... and his eyes now sink a little deeper... he looks that much more sullen...*** ((Magnum Wolf)) And what does Magnum Wolf recieve after defeating the three top contenders in LCW who aren't members of Oblivion? What reward does Dave give me for destroying his precious Harbinger? A chance to kill some rookie. Again. Jin Akujin... you have stepped into my path on more than one occasion. The first time I had something to do with you, you ended up with a steel chair wrapped around your head. Do you even remember that night, Jin? Do you recall what you saw just before you blacked out? That's called rage, my friend. Pure and unadulterated. That night, I welcomed you to the game. I ushered you into the world of LCW Mercury, and then cracked your skull for daring to think you could beat me. The next time I had anything to do with you was just the other day. At Fusion. Oh wait... you don't recall me having anything to do with you that night. Think back. Who suggested that match between yourself and Pariah being a No DQ match? That was me. Because you didn't deserve that match, Jin. You didn't deserve to have a fighting chance to win the title that belongs to me. so I took that chance away from you. I made it a match you could NOT POSSIBLY win. Are you able to recall any of THAT? He hit you with a chair, too... remember? Do you recall that Nausea on the chair? Probably not. Jin, you didn't know what you were getting into when you walked into the ring with me at last week's Fusion. Your torture didn't end that night when I left you for dead. It didn't end at Fusion when I fed you to that idiot Pariah and his hardcore ways... it won't end this week when you step into the ring with me AGAIN. Mr. Akujin... you have many years of wrestling ahead of you. I think it's about time you started thinking about the future, and staying the HELL out of my way. ***The scene slowly fades to black as Magnum closes his eyes, and takes a deep breath...***
