An open letter to Sickness

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition An open letter to Sickness An open letter to Sickness Posted Monday, May 27, 2002 by Harbinger The bags are packed. Herb is ready to leave Las Vegas and never look back, but before he does, there's one thing he must do. With a somber expression on his face and a wearied look in his eyes, Herb speaks into the camera from his suite at the MGM Grand. "Well, Sickness, I lost. After your whole intricate game designed to manipulate me into the man you wanted me to be, I lost the first big title match. "Does that make you happy? Does that make you sad? I can't tell anymore, Sickness, because you've got so many masks in the way of your actual emotions that nobody knows what you truly feel, or what you truly want out of life. "But I do know how I feel, and I do know what I want. I loved you, Sick, and to an extent I probably always will. At the same time, I find you to be one of the most repulsive human beings I've ever come across, and the thought of ever tasting you on my lips again makes me nauseous. "No, I'm not going to try and smack you around like an atypical wrestler would. I'm not going to attempt to interfere in Oblivion's fight with EEI. I won't even go out of my way to ask for a singles match with Gol and ruin what little reputation he has left. No, instead I'm going to become stronger; not through an extension of my anger over your betrayal, like what happened at Suicide, but rather through becoming a better wrestler and a better man. I'm going to reach heights that none of the men in your stable will ever manage to attain, and in so doing prove that your 'influence' is nothing more than smoke and mirrors covering for the fact that you have emptiness where a soul is supposed to be. You only manipulate because you're incapable of actually helping people through the building of relationships. "That's why the emotion I feel most for you is neither love, nor jealousy, nor hatred. It's pity, because you'll never be able to feel love the way I did for you." Herb chews on the inside of his left cheek, and his eyes shimmer with moisture as the video/letter comes to an end.
