Due(d) for a lesson

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Due(d) for a lesson Due(d) for a lesson Posted Monday, June 3, 2002 by Inferno Inferno: Patience is a concept in which everyone needs to get a grip of… (The scene opens with an image of mountain. The clouds are covering the top; the mountain seems to be a few miles away. The chilled breeze takes a few leaves across the floor, and all seems silent and motionless…) Inferno: I have it. I used it. It worked well. You see people, having Patience is a plus. Being able to use it is more than a plus. When? How? Why? Did I use some Patience? Bah… Subdued. You attacked me when I was down; you attacked me after Pariah finished me off. You seem to think that by kicking me when I was down would help you gain glory in Mercury – Subdued, you are sorely wrong. (Inferno takes a step into the view of the image. He has, as usual. A lather jacket, over a tattered shirt, jeans, boots and of course, his bandana. His hair is brushed back, and has sun glasses on, due to him facing into the sun…) Inferno: When you took me out, I waited for revenge. I waited for the perfect moment. I wasn’t booked for Fusion for a reason. I hid in the back and waited, being patient and using my mind. It is quite simple, Subdued. You are actually quite Naïve, huh? Now, I’ll get one thing straight before I go on… The more you come to me, the more you’ll get back. Understand that, please. I would also like to fire one question at you – Why? Why in the hell did you find it enjoyable to attack me? I am not going to trip over it; I’m just rendering my mind up. Is it jealousy? Due to the fact that of all of us here, it was me who came up from LTD to Mercury first, and that I was the first LTD Lethal champion? Is it hate? For something I did in a past life? Maybe you find me ugly? Or was it that punk Blaze McCoy who sent you up to do so? Whatever the reason, Subdued, I can tell you this; bad move! Inferno: I am not going to ponder why exactly you did start a fight with me, now, heh… Now, I’m going to ponder how our little ‘fight’ will end. I am not one to hold a grudge for too long, in fact, I’m not one to make things like this personal, I don’t like doing that, and I don’t generally do that. But Subdued, you obviously think that, in your half dead mind, attacking me will solve your worries. Well, although that is wrong, you remind me of someone…. You remind me of myself… (Inferno chuckles and looks down at his feet. He still has a small smile on his face, some hair now covering his face up.) Inferno: Well, you were a LTD Lethal champion – like me. You moved up from LTD to LCW Mercury – like me. You want to make an impression – like me. It all seems to add up for me. But there is one thing that separates us; our way of going about looking for recognition. You think you have made a mark? But then get your ass handed to you by Harbinger? I didn’t do that. I turned on Headbuster, now the LTD champion. I formed a tag team with a low life, now owner of LTD; Blaze McCoy. My ass has been beaten down by the likes of Pariah, Young and the Trent’s, yet I still feel alive and well. Inferno: After all I have been through so far here in Mercury, it seems no one understands who I am. People still look at me like I’m some sort of freak. They talk about me behind my backs, asking questions, pointing the finger. But the truth is, they will never know who exactly I really am. Subdued, you are the same; you will never know who I am behind Inferno. People continuously underestimate me. No one ever gives me a real chance. Now, is the time that Inferno will show the world, fans and people who I am and what I’m made of. I know what my abilities are, no else does, I think it’s time to show not only the fans and the bookers, but also Subdued. No matter how you feel about me, be prepared at Climax, you never know what will hit you. (Inferno walks around a bit, head down, and sunglasses on. He pauses for a few seconds and carries on…) Inferno: But what about you, Subdued? Watching your fight against Harbinger wasn’t the greatest thing in life, but I’ll live. You need to make an impression in Mercury, but, I hate to break it to you, kid, but by the performance at Fusion, no one will take you seriously. So, what can you do? Don’t get me wrong Subdued, I do not doubt you, merely… questioning your abilities – so to speak. Inferno: Right now, here in Mercury, there are so many things going down. That Australian punk, Young, joins Oblivion. Jin Akujin is constantly getting World Title shots. Hell, everyone in the roster will be getting a chance at gold this or next week, at Climax or Fusion. Apart from me and Subdued. Maybe it does figure. By pinning you, Subdued wont cause much of a stir. No one will see it or recognise it. I have to show everyone in the Mercury world what I can do. The one, two, three isn’t bad, but I want to beat you by going a step further. I want to beat you and make a mark by not only beating you, but leaving a statement written all over your body; Inferno is here, and ready to burst. Simple stuff – right? (Inferno has a confused look about him, but then shrugs!) Inferno: I guess! June 1st was your debut. June 13th will be your first real experience of loosing to Inferno… (Inferno grins) Inferno: Have fun…. (Inferno chuckles. Scene fades.)
