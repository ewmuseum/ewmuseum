Who wants it?

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Who wants it? Who wants it? Posted Monday, April 8, 2002 by Pariah The air is crisp, the sun shining, trees casting dark, imposing shadows on the sidewalk. With the camera slowly making a panoramic scan, we notice a house, and in the front yard of said estate is a patio chair. Sitting in said patio chair is Keith Summers, clad in nothing more than a white pair of Adidas gym shorts. After taking a sip from an ice cold Long Island Ice Tea, Pariah laces his fingers togethere, resting his hands on his abdomen before staring in the sky and addressing the camera. Pariah: Clouds really are magical. They can assume any shape,any feature that they chose. One minute they may bring shade from the sun, next a horrible torando that can wreak havoc. Pariah: Now, being the World Champion is also quiet magical. I can take on any contender, any kind of match, and show to them and you, the fans, why I am the world champion. One minute I may be showing respect to my competitor, the next, ripping through them. Pariah sighs, and grins broadly. Pariah: It really is great to be the king. At Fusion, I've got a mystery opponent. Who could it be? I don't know, and I really don't are. My motto is I'll take on anyone, anytime, any place. Do I question the meaning behind this match at all? No, because it's another defense, another chance to show people why I am the mark of excellence in this sport, why I am the champion, and why ham and eggers like Magnum Wolf aren't. Pariah: Speaking of Magnum, it looks like it's me and him again, at Suicide, with two more being thrown into the mix. Magnum, I hope you come ready, because last time we met, you may have beat me, but you needed your cronie Gol to help you out. I hoped you paid attention to what I did to Gol at Critical Mass, because people who mess with me and my business.....well get paid back, in full. Pariah: But I have to admit you did give me a bit of a favor in asking Dave that my match win Jin be a No DQ one. I know what you were trying to do, Mags. You thought that by making the match one that I excel in, and one that uses methods that aren't legal in a normal match, I'd look like a cheap champion.I think you failed horribly. Number one, I showed the world that I will do anything to become the champion, and number two, I will do anything to hold onto this belt. If it means repeatedly driving a chair across the skull of the victim, so be it.If it means dropping some fresh faced kid on the top of his head, so be it. Because I've worked too hard, and came too far to let some upset or some fluke toss me back down now. Pariah: But I find it kind of funny, you trying to make me look like a cheap champion when you rely on your little buds in Oblivion for some help. Geoffrey Slate, the far more superior wrestler than yourself, was poised to get his rightful title shot when you had to use your cheating ways, and reveal that wh*re Sickness as the leader of your little group of talent-less f*cks. Pariah sits up in his lawn chair, his eyes cold as ice, shooting daggers through the camera. Pariah: I've got a news flash for you, Magnum, Gol, Mikel, Josh, and Sickness. If that was me, I would have let the wh*re get her neck broken. I have no compassion, no remorse, and generally, I don't give a flying sh*t about anyone when I'm in the ring. Nobody can be trusted in this business, especially people who can't accomplish jack squat on their own, i.e. The Oblivion. Pariah: I don't know if this mystery opponent is a little Oblivion trick, or a little Dave trick. But like I said, I don't care. Because in that ring, defending my title, I've got one thing on my mind...leaving with my world title. Wheeled out, walking out, crawling out, by hook or by crook, clean, or tooth and nail, none of it matters as long as I leave with the belt that I deserve, the belt I own, the belt that nobody will ever pry from my fingers as long as their is one heartbeat left in me. Pariah lets up the whole intense look, and sits back in the chair. Pariah: Man, woman, child, adult, it doesn't matter one bit. You put them in that ring with me, I will force them to compete, and I will force them to rise to a level of excellence never reached before. Because I'm Pariah, I'm the world champion, and you...you're not. Get over it, because that's how things will be for a long time to come. Pariah closes his eyes and rests as the camera pulls back, goes to the sky, and we fade out...
