LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Contagion: Exposure Contagion: Exposure Posted Saturday, April 6, 2002 by Harbinger Sickness and Herb arrive in his nice shiny car in front of Sushi Rock, downtown Rochester. Herb: I've never even heard of this place. Sick: It's amazing what one can find when one searches in the right places. Herb parks the car. He exits and moves around to Sickness' side of the vehicle. He opens her door and offers his arm. It's become a sort of ritual. And the smile she produces when she takes his arm has consistently made him feel like he owned the world. Sick: So have you had Sushi before? Herb: I think I did ages ago when I was ten. I'm sure I threw up. Sick: Well let me reassure you. You had bad Sushi. Sushi, prepared well, is ungodly. Herb: Well, I'm here. Sick smiles. Sick: Yes, you are. They enter the foyer, a completely black clad hostess with an irritated expression awaits behind a podium. She rises with a well practiced snotty glance at Herb and Sick. Then her poise shatters. Hostess: Sickness? Herb smiles. This has almost become routine. In her particular underground circles, Sickness captures as much adoration as he does among wrestling fans. Perhaps in better ways, since Sickness gets favors as opposed to autograph requests. Hostess: Oh my God! Sickness! It's been forever. Herb rolls his eyes as the hostess rushes away from her post to hug and kiss Sick. Sickness disentangles herself. Sick: Judy, you look great. Judy: Oh, you think so? Sickness, I would so like to get back together with you sometime. I promise that it won't be like the time before, I'm sure you understand- Sick: Judy, this is Herb. Judy is crushed as Sick takes a hold of Herb's hand and with her smallest possible voice. Judy: Nice to meet you. Herb takes pity and doesn't rub it in. No gloating over the fact that people are dying for lack of Sickness in this world; unless it's another guy, at which point it's all fair. Herb: The pleasure is mine. Sick: I believe our reservation is under "Herb Ingles, two." Judy: Oh, of course. Judy seems positively dead to the world. Judy leads them like a tortured soul escorting the king and queen of hell into the dining room. The interior is chic. It's black with paintings, and elegant tables and chairs. Multileveled, steps and railings. Stained glass windows illuminate an array of colors. Lighting over tables illuminates the table surface but leaves the seats in a comfortable twilight. If they were royalty of the damned, they might as well dine as such. Judy lets them sit then vanishes. Herb thinks that she may be crying. Sickness is unmoved. He wonders how she can stand the constant unwanted attention. But she seems to put up with it without complaining. The waiter arrives and is positively charming as well as flaming as all hell. Sick: We won't be needing the menus. My friend here is a born again Sushi Virgin. We're looking at having 4 squid, 4 eel, 4 mackerel, 4 octopus, 2 sea urchin, 2 halibut, 4 swordfish. Sushi. We'll also have a caterpillar roll. The waiter smiles. Waiter: You know your stuff. To drink? Sick: We'll do Asian Pear Sake. Bottle. Waiter: Very good. I'll get right on it. The waiter turns to Herb. Waiter: You're in good hands. Herb: Don't I know it. The waiter departs. Herb: I noticed you said, sushi at the end. Isn't that redundant? Sick: Sushi is the fish over rice. Sashimi is it straight up fish. Herb: Ahh. How does caterpillar enter into it? Sick: It's a roll. You've heard of the California roll I'm sure. Herb: Ahhhhh. Sick: It's a kind of Sushi roll. Caterpillar is far better. It's a mixed variety with veggies topped with caviar sprinkled on top.. asparagus, cream cheese, kelp, tuna, halibut, rice, cucumber, wrapped in seaweed. I prefer the Sushi to the rolls, but I thought you might like trying it. To each their own. Herb: It's all pretty intimidating. Sick: Do you know why we're here actually? Herb: Because you love Sushi. Sick: Well, it's true that I do love Sushi, but this is about you tonight. Herb: Really? Sick: What were doing is feeding you. But more significantly, we're reawakening you to being fed. Herb: I can stomach whatever you want me to. After all, I... No, not yet. Sick: It's not just about stomaching. You like wings, right? Tell why you love them so much. Herb: Well, they're... wingy? It's hard to say. Like, asking me why I like sex, or wrestling. It fills a craving. Sick laughs. Sick: They fill a routine. They satisfy a craving and satisfy hunger. They taste good. This is about a new eating. This is about eating something that's new, raw, and awaiting to be devoured. Food that's cooked tastes like it's cooking. The raw fish and other such sea creatures taste just like they do. It's a sensation like no other that you've had. Very primal. Very conquering. Part of the joy of Sushi is that people are scared of it. Like it'll present something that they don't want to think about. You don't think of the chicken much when you devour wings. Here, you know the animal is the animal before you. It adds a new sensation of delight. Herb doesn't give a verbal response. Instead, he looks off into the distance and visualizes how that would translate with Sickness involved. It very much engrosses him. The waiter brings the sake with a flourish. Waiter: Enjoy, darlings. The sake is a very subtle taste. It's filtered sake, so no milkiness. It's clear, strong, and hardly sweet. Very liquid, not at all like hard liquors. Herb: This is incredible. The food arrives. It's a LOT of sushi. The plate is a dark blue lacquered square and the presentation of the food looks like art. Sickness takes her chopsticks. Sick: Can you do chopsticks? Herb: I can try. Sick: It's easy. Tuck the lower one at the base of your thumb. Here let me show you. Sickness leans across the table and puts the stick in Herb's hand. Sick: Here. Control it with your ring finger. Sick: And this one, put it at the base of your index finger, hold it with the tip of your thumb, good. And direct it with your middle finger. Use your index finger for balance and control with the tips of your fingers, use your middle finger to move it... play with it... there! The sticks come alive in Herb's hand. Sick: Very good. I'm impressed. Sick: Now. Squid first. Herb eyes the food. Sick: We'll do it together. Sick picks up the squid on a rice ball, and lifts it up. Sick: Now you. Herb gets his chopsticks around the squid sushi. With some shakiness, he lifts it up. Sick: 1, 2, 3, go! Herb and Sick eat the sushi. The squid is chewy. He pulverizes it in his mouth as the rice begins to digest. He chews. He bites down hard into the squid and it resists than gives. Almost alive. The idea isn't unpleasant. The taste is like nothing he's ever had before. I'm eating a raw squid. With some rice. He savors it's uniqueness and swallows. Sickness smiles. Sick: Now for some real delight, we'll do the mackerel. Time passes. The plate is mostly cleared. The sushi is almost all gone, except for the eel, which Sick says wasn't the best she's had. About half the roll is gone. The sake has been refilled and refilled. Herb is slightly buzzing. Sick: Had enough? Herb: Well, this certainly isn't the torture I was dreading, but I've had as much as my gut will allow. Sick: That's good. I've someplace special to take you. Herb: Really? Another plane of existence? Sick: No, where we're going is very earthly. Herb: I'm not sure about driving. Sick: It's not so cold out. We can walk there easily. It isn't. So cold that is. They walk a few blocks than arrive at a nondescript building sandwiched between two offices. Sickness knocks on a security door. Sick: Sickness. Open up, it's cold. It isn't that cold out. The door opens. A very very fat black man opens the door with an impeccable tux. The man beckons them inside. Man: Sickness, if I may. Sickness offers her hand and the man's lips part and he bends down to lightly kiss her hand. Herb nearly flinches. The gracefulness of the move is absolutely disturbing. Man: Thank you, your grace. You are always welcome here. May I inquire if you are playing tonight? Sick: No, we're just here to voyeur. Man: Very good. You have complete access. The man opens a side door into a dimly lit hallway. Herb and Sick enter. Herb takes Sickness' arm, but with a little more force than the usual 'escorting my lady' routine. Herb puts his far arm on her shoulder as they walk down the hallway. Sick: Ever really consider "fetish", Herb? Herb wasn't saying anything, but somehow that makes him quiet even further. Sick: It's hardly sex, but linked to sex. Some people have this strange attachment. They cannot explain it or define it. It's as undeniable as sexual orientation. Fetish is actually originally a mystical term, used to establish an item that his a spirit bound to it. And it's no different from these people. They are otherwise normal functional people. But with an obsession linked to sexual drive. But it's independent of sexual drive as well. Give a fetishist a lot of sex and they'll still obsess about their attachment. It's a deep drive. Psychologists have been frustrated and disturbed by it in ages. Most honest fetishists discover their attachment rather than develop it. It's hard to explain. But it's undeniably real. Herb is imminently more nervous than he was about the raw squid. Sick: Look here. Sick opens a panel on the wall and beckons Herb to look within. Herb reluctantly bends and watches as a well dress man kneels before a tall hussy. He removes her high heel shoe and regards it. With almost some sign of resistance, as his hands begin to shake, he lurches over and begins to run his tongue along her foot. Herb jerks back away from the peephole, breathing heavy. He regards Sickness with a "what in the world is a place like this doing in Rochester?" look. Sick: You're looking at a normal, highly successful individual, in every respect, except for this. Sick: He cannot help himself. If he doesn't indulge he begins to unbalance and cannot think of anything but ladies' feet. He loses focus and his work, whatever it may be, starts slipping. People are like this everywhere, not just California. The more upright the community, the more likely a place like this exists, a haven for these people and their terrible, undeniable attachment. South Dakota, Maine, Ohio, everywhere people feel this. Herb, in an odd moment of curiosity, inserts... Herb: There's more, isn't there? Sick nods. Sick: This is lightweight, Herb. People have it bad. This guy is very lucky considering some people's obsessions. They border on the illegal sometimes. Sometimes criminal. Sometimes mortal. But fortunately, everyone here is very human and very strict about the boundaries of the law. In here, at worse, it's high class prostitution. Some things are disgusting beyond belief. And I want you to see all of it. For the life of him, Herb can't fathom why she would want him to endure this. But, she must have a good reason; after all, she's been good so far in the relationship. Sick: Because all this, is an aspect of the nature of Sickness. It's part of understanding me and where I've come from. They treat me like royalty in this realm, because I am it's archetype in a lot of ways. Do you think you might have a fetish, Herb? He nibbles on his lower lip, and does what he cannot to look her in the face. Herb: Not to my knowledge, no. Sick smiles, and it almost looks like pity. Sick: It could be very possible that you don't. A blessedly unencumbered life. However, your life is hardly "unburdened." And you may have an obsession that is blessedly not linked to your sex drive. Your obsession with your old pal, Ignatio comes to mind. As well as your lust for the pop icons. Sick: Do you regret the fact that I'm not as pretty as Brittany or Jessica? Red alert! All hands to battle stations! Herb: No! I mean, you're beautiful in your own way... you're... you're... real. He bites his lip again, hoping that answer passed muster. Sick laughs lightly. Sick: I suppose it's good to be real. But the pop icons are a fantasy, and a fantasy will always have a special place in your mind and lust. It's almost easier to obsess about people that are less than real. Less dangerous. Someone with no substance can hardly hurt you, can they? Unrequited love/lust/obsession is a very safe thing. It's at rock bottom, and can never betray you. Herb: But all that was just something empty while I was waiting for something with substance. I've got that now. Sick walks down the hallway some before turning to Herb. Sick: Are you happy with me? Are you okay with me? In spite of this filthy disgusting world which I come from? Am I okay having previously a slut of great renown? Sick almost looks like crying. Sick: Am I good enough for you, Herb? Herb: I love you, Sickness. Sickness walks over to Herb and hugs him. She holds onto him tightly. Sickness: And I love you, Herb. And I'll be the best thing that's ever happened to you. This I promise. And I always keep my promises.
