LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition F L A T T E R E D F L A T T E R E D Posted Wednesday, April 3, 2002 by Dirge A beautifully decorated room glows with the flickering of candlelight. The shadows cast on the white marble floor seem to take on a life of their own, vaguely resembling hungry demons reaching out to grab something, anything. in the middle of the room, is an antuque chess table made out of black marble to look like the top of a roman column. A platimum and black gold chess set is atop it, with pieces matching the material of the board. Dirge is lounging across a backless couch carved of ivory. Dirge looks bored, and a little disappointed. He moves to touch a chesspiece, then withdraws his hand as if resisting temptation. He looks around expectantly as a tiny noise is heard....but no one's there. ------------------ DIRGE: Come now, Gol. Surely you have some move in mind? I've been patiently waiting here, and I haven't touched a piece, I promise. It seems you want nothing to do with me. That's a shame, but I don't blame you. (Dirge looks around lazily, then turns his attention fully to the chessboard.) DIRGE: You must have SOMETHING to say to me? Or are you too busy training? Do you have things in your life happening right now that take so much of your time that you have none left for me? Your associates in Oblivion seem to have plenty of time for me. Why, I feel like the most popular kid at the dance thanks to them. They can't seem to tire of talking about how important and interesting I am. Oh, I know they don't word it like that, but if I wasn't these things, would they be bothering to take so much of their valuable time speaking to, and about me? Everyone knows they have their own challenges to face, yet they take time away from giving them their full attentin to try and talk to me FOR you. How nice. (DIRGE stands abruptly, and the candles around the room seem to flare up in anger, making the shadows longer, and ...darker. DIRGE starts pacing a bit) DIRGE: Look Gol, so far every munchkin in your "lollipop guild" seems to want to get a piece of me but YOU. If my skin weren't so thick, that migh hurt my feelings. It seems EVERONE IN OBLIVION IS WILLING TO FIGHT, BUT NEVER THEIR OWN BATTLES. Oh, I know you're a group and thatyou use group tactics to garner wins. But I didn't know that the reason Oblivion did this is because they were so afraid of their individual battles, that they get each other to fight FOR them in every case. Not one of you is paying proper attention to your individual battles. Not one. And I'm honored, because you're all so WORRIED ABOUT ME. (DIRGE's pacing stops and the lights flare once more, each candle seems to be affected by the emotional intensity of the room's sole resident.) DIRGE: Gol, you've had my attention since the attacks on me, Vincent, and Memosarks. You had my attention when I beat my way past Blaze and Inferno, when I tagged myself in so that I could get a little blood on my hands before the match was through. You've been my inspiration, GOl. You actually got me to take my mind off having fun for a millisecond and to notice you. Since that MOMENT I have turned all my energy to you. Breaking you breaks the will of Oblivion, because you're the only one who's shown any kind of backbone, any initiative. And I don't mean in your matches, because that would be funny indeed and I'm not in the mood for jokes right now. I mean in your interviews. The others shut up when you talk. You could interrupt Magnum, and he'd just tuck his wolf tail between his legs and wait for you to finish. No, Gol, you had my attention. The brazen way you insulted every other wrestler, the way you blamed your losses on "that was then and this is now" and how you took credit for your wins with a "those are the matches in my past that counted" attitude......that was some shit. And as ridiculous as it was, it took balls to say it to people's faces and BELIEVE it. But apparently the fact that you're facing me has removed your testicles. Apparently, you don't do the talking now. (The candlelight seems to throb now, independent of any physical source. Its almost hypnotic in its rythmic flashing. DIRGE's eyes reflect every ember of flame in the room.) DIRGE: Come on GOl, have I cowed you THAT MUCH. Have I shut up the human propoganda machine that I singled out as my target because he seemed like the only worthy apple in a barrel of worms? How is it that you can be so silent, and your idiot associates so irritatingly loud? At least you had things to say. The fact remains that you are afraid of me. All of you are. I know you'll deny this. You have to, if you want to seem like men at all, but your actions speak louder than words. All in LCW can hear you, GOL. Your silence is deafening. It SCREAMS your fear. And the shrill cries of the babies known as Oblivion? Fear not, I will silence them as well. I would like to humbly THANK YOU, members of Oblivion. The actions of all of you have done for me something that no amount of blathering by me could ever do. By GOl running from me like a child too scared to even whimper, by the others immediately coming to his defense, as if to protect someone who they know can't protect himself, you have just made me OBLIVION'S NEMESIS. THAT'S RIGHT, JUST ME. If I had decided to declare myself as the one man whom you all fear, as your worst nightmare since soap and water, no one in LCW would have given a damn. Hell, even I'M not deluded enough to think otherwise. But I sincerely hope that all of you are not deluded enough to think that reacting to me the way you have, you show anything but what I have said. So now, I have your attention. I have your hate, and your fear. Go and ready yourselves to deny this as soon as possible in your interviews, go and talk about how crazy and self-important I am, how this is all a lie and you're not all a pack of cowards worrying about one man. Please. It will only prove the point that you already drove home with your actions, in big neon letters with vegas arrows pointing to them. You 4 are afraid of me. Nothing you say now can undo the actions that everyone has already witnessed. They saw you scramble to cover the hasty retreat of the lamb who was going to be sacrificed to me. Run, little sheep, run. (DIRGE looks down at the chess set, we now see how the pieces are laid out. Defending the black King is one KNIGHT, with the symbol of entropy carved on the base. The black King seems to be holding a canvas and a paintbrush. On the other side, 4 queens.) END TRANSMISSION
