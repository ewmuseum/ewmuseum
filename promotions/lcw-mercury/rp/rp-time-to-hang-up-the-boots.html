Time to hang up the boots?

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Time to hang up the boots? Time to hang up the boots? Posted Wednesday, April 10, 2002 by BlazeMcCoy Blaze sits motionless, staring out the window of his office over the city of Pittsburgh. He runs his hands through his hair and takes a deep breath and mumbles to himself. Blaze: I don’t know what to think anymore. Could it be time to hang up the boots? I’m not sure? Inferno still thinks we can do it, but do I? He does have some good points and we have a great opportunity here. I have the LTD now and with winning part of the Presidency, I have my own show, Lethal LTD. The Hardcore show with blood, guts, pain, agony and high risk matches. That’s what I like. Henstock and Paxton have the other two shows and I can here LTD calling me. I’m afraid Mercury is a lost cause. I tried my best and I want to give it another shot but I’m not sure if I can. Maybe I just don’t have it anymore? Heather Leather busts in the office door and approaches Blaze. Heather: Blaze, you can do it. I have a plan! Blaze: What the hell are you doing busting in here? You have done nothing for me ever since I met you! Heather: But what about… Blaze: That was fun and all, but this is Business. In the ring, you have done nothing. Your plans haven’t worked. Your help is worthless. You are nothing but a rebound whore! Get the hell out of my face, bitch. I don’t need this right now. Heather: You can’t talk to me like that! Suddenly, the door busts open again and Cherry Stacked walks in. Blaze stands amazed. Cherry: You better step away from my man you bitch! Blaze: Cherry, what are you doing here? Cherry: Saving your ass from this beast! Heather: You are nothing Cherry. He is mine! Cherry quickly grabs Heather by the hair, turns her around, and delivers “The Stacked”, placing Heather’s head under Cherry’s breasts and dropping straight down and knocking Ms. Leather out. Blaze watches stunned and then buzzes his secretary. Blaze: Um, can you send in security please? Seconds later security comes in and takes Heather Leather away. As she is leaving, Matt Henstock and Jacob Paxton walk in. Blaze: What is this? Did I instate an open door policy while I was asleep? Paxton: Come to LTD man! Leave Mercury and come home where you belong. Henstock: We can do this. You don’t need this anymore. You don’t deserve it. But do whatever you want. Blaze: Don’t worry guys. I know what I must do now. I will help run LTD, but I won’t leave Mercury without a fight! Inferno was right. We have another shot at gold and I can’t just sit around and let it pass by and accept defeat. It’s not in my nature. I plan on making the most of this match. Those fools think I don’t have it anymore? Well they will see first hand. Paxton: But you know they will screw you and make you look like a fool again. Blaze: They haven’t done that. When I left LCW the first time, I was screwed, but this is different. This is me not living up to my name, my potential, Beaumont hasn’t done anything but let me back in Mercury and give me title shots. I have done this to myself! If I leave under this losing streak, I will leave with no dignity and no respect from those in Mercury. Sure, I may not be in Mercury much longer, but I will not just walk away. My contract is up soon and I have LTD to work on so, if I’m going out, I’m going out swinging! I can run LTD while I finish out my contract. My first Lethal LTD show isn’t until May anyway, so I have time. Tag team wrestling is how I made my name in LCW, and how fitting that I show everyone in a tag match, exactly what I am made of. Win or lose, I will prove that Blaze McCoy is one of the best in the Business, Hook, Line and Sinker! Blaze looks at cherry for a second and they embrace. Matt and Jacob just look at each other as Blaze and Cherry leave the office hand in hand. Henstock and Paxton just look at each other and shrug their shoulders as the camera fades out.
