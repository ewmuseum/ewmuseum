State of the Model Address

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition State of the Model Address State of the Model Address Posted Wednesday, May 22, 2002 by Glamour Fade in...we see a vast, sunlit kitchen. In the center is a huge Islad with a finished oak top. The only thing on it is an open bottle of Wine and a half-full glass. as the camera pans right we see The Model leaning, arms crossed, against the large, stainless-steel fridge. He has a bandage over his right eye, a painful reminder of the chair-shot he took from Oblivion... Three drop and two more show up. True to form they enter like every other Oblivion member...(he grins)...from behind. So Rush is back, once again let me extend my warmest welcome to you, our old nemesis. I look forward to renewing our little feud in the days to come...sooner rather than later it appears. Beaumont, in all his idiotic glory has given you and your buddy Killjoy a title shot. A title shot?!? What have the two of you EVER done in this fed to warrant a title shot? You run in after your Oblivion buddies lost to us again and hit us with chairs?!? Oh, well I guess that DOES make you two worthy of a title shot. After all, that's the most action any LCW fan has seen out of Oblivion in a while. However, despite Wolf's bluster, neither Vincent nor I are lacking in either drive or ambition, you and Killjoy will earn your match--not through any previous deeds for you have none to speak of, but there in the ring, We will make you earn it. Make no mistake, if I even thought there was the merest possibility of dropping the belts to you I would protest this at the highest level, not through fear, but because despite how worthless the Trents were, they earned their stripes...you...have yet to prove anything to me except that you can hide behind the curtains and annoy us at inopportune moments...although your timing is a bit off, we still won. So, come Fusion you two will have an early shot at the gold, and to paraphrase Magnum,"EEI will have another victory under our belts." Johnny pushes off the fridge, grabs his wine and moves into his living room. He stops and takes in the hardwood flooring, the grand piano, the leather couches, and the antique lamps. He walks over to the window and stares out at the ocean for a minute before speaking... I never get tired of this view. But there are a few things that I am tired of. Firstly, I've heard the mutterings, and I've read the gossip columns so I know what people think. EEI's popularity is skyrocketing, but to the fans I'm still an unknown quantity. What about The Model? They say, what has he done? I'll tell you. I've given LCW some of the most exciting matches on record. I've pulled off moves that people still talk about and not just off the mat but off of ladders, through tables, and just about everywhere else. Time and time again I'm in the ring, giving my all, pulling the weight and trying my damn best to be the champion that LCW fans deserve! And yet this is how I am repayed. Listen to this: Johnny grabs a mangled copy of "On The Mat" a fanzine devoted to several feds around the world. he flips it to a particularly dog-eared and beat-up page and begins to read, although it appears that The Model has long since memorized the article... "Where have all the tag teams gone? by F. Michael Hall. Are we witnessing an end to an era? Or is it already gone? These are the questions that most wrestling fans are asking on forums, emails, and in editorials regarding the dying art of tag-team competition. Unfortunately it appears that they are right. "As wrestling progresses we see the bookers favoring singles competition and gimmick matches, with the tag-team competition being used to fill dead-spots in the card and to allow for the time honored tradition of heel turns. Tag-team matches are fast becoming a shell of its former self with fewer and fewer new wrestlers becoming dedicated tag teams. Most tag-teams these days are slap-dash ad-hoc assemblages of wrestlers, thrown together like OPEC--alliances of either convenience or to provide a quick shot in the arm for wrestlers whose popularity appears to be failing, and some, who would obviously prefer not to be in a team, are forced there by overzealous bookers who want to provide a 'dream team' match. In most cases these tag-teams are short lived. "In most federations the tag-team titles are being regarded as near worthless rating slightly on par with, or even below a federation's TV-title equivalent. So what was once the pinnacle of teamwork in this sport is now at best a title of slight regard and at worst, a booby prize to be kicked around. This sorry state of affairs is not entirely the fault of bookers, in fact the lack of prestige from tag teams can also be directly related to the quality of the teams them selves...." Johnny polishes off his wine as he continues... Blah, blah, blah...ok here we go, "In LCW, of the five registered tag teams only two, EEI's The Elite and The Trent Brothers, are currently active. Is it any wonder that LCW fans are growing a bit weary of seeing The Elites and or Dirge versus Trents XXIII? With a lack of competition comes a lack of interest, a symptom of what tag teams have become. After last week's Fusion, it appears that the Trents are now defunct leaving only The Artiste and Glamor. If the Elite are to survive beyond a few weeks then they need to find a way of generating interest for themselves rather than relying on their stablemates and a generous booking staff. This will more than likely prove more difficult than it appears. The Artiste has proven he can hold his own both in singles and tag team competition, no Vincent will doubtless stand the test of time in LCW. Glamor, is another story however. "The proverbial 'weak link' in EEI, The Model is billed as a 'technician deluxe' and while he boasts a deep arsenal of moves, locks, and holds as well as uncanny in-ring awareness, these gifts are overshadowed by his apparent lackadasical demeanor, lack of drive, and heart. While he did help earn the tag straps he seems more a temporary inheritor of the titles rather than a bona-fide champion. This image has been reinforced in LCW fan's minds by his six-week hiatus ostensibly as a 'good will' tour of Japan. His return to LCW was lackluster at best and lacked punch in many fan's minds. Despite an amazing match at LCW's monthly Pay-per-View entitled 'Suicide', next week's gimmick match showcased his ability to get beaten up and allow Vincent to hang in a match much longer than necessary. Is it age, laziness, or has he simply lost it? Only time will tell, unfortunately time is the one thing they don't have. The Elite have the dubious honor of being the survivors of the tag-team cancer, but they no longer have any opponents. The Model, a self-described moderate in the EEI crusade, has yet to truly buy into The anti-Oblivion sentiment of his brothers-in-arms, and seemed content to carry on the tired feud against the Trents, without them, by all outward appearances Johnny Glamour will simply ride out the end of his title reign and then fade away. "The fate of the tag team seems questionable at best and may soon become nothing more than a curiosity or worse yet a footnote in the history of this sport. However one thing is clear, the tag-teams themselves have a direct hand in the fate of their matches. Fan approval is a must and exciting and electrifying matches are a necessity. However if The Model is any indication, tag teams the world over are in serious trouble." Johnny finishes the article and slowly crushes the magazine in his hand. He is furious, gritting his teeth he throws the magazine across the room, followed by the wine glass which shatters against the far wall. Lazy?!? Lacks heart?!? I've dedicated my life to this sport, I give every match my all. I may not win them all, but I still have one of the more enviable win-loss records in this fed! You selfish bastards want entertainment? Fine! I'll start pulling sh*t off that will Harby's vaunted punk-out 5.0 look like a friggin' camel clutch! I'm going to make every match like a PPV! I'm starting with Rush and Killjoy--Fusion will a be a rude awakening for you two. After that I have a pretty good idea where I'm headed next. That's right Magsy, you and me. I'll show you just how 'lazy' I am. I'm tired? I'm cliche'?!? I'll tear you up son! I'm done taking this crap! And all your piss-ant Tony Shivani "human game of chess" bullsh*t won't save you from me. So clear your dance-card kid, I'm not taking "no" for an answer on this one. Johnny stalks off, pausing to kick over a coffee table
