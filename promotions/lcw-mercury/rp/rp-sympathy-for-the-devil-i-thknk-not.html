LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition sympathy for the devil? I thknk not! sympathy for the devil? I thknk not! Posted Wednesday, May 22, 2002 by rush (The scene opens with Rush standing in richly decorated apartment. His appearance a drastic change from what the fans have become accustomed to. His hair is gelled and tied back, his usually t-shirt and jeans have been replaced by a dress shirt, pants, and sport's jacket. Behind him is a set of doors opening up onto a beautiful balcony with an extravagant view. With a grin Rush looks around.) "Take a look around. I finally see where I went wrong. I now see why the fans have abandoned all things decent and began to follow such perverted role models as EEI. You see, as person is only as good as the life they live. So now I have chosen a life better suiting to my status. Like the apartment? Between it and my new wardrobe I have probably spent more than most of brain-dead masses with see in a year, hell maybe even their lives. Of course more me it is nothing, I mean with the money I have made and will make these are only the start of some major changes. You see I realize that earning the respect of the fans is a useless as most of their pathetic lives. If I am to show them just how wrong they are I must force them to respect me, and even fear me. Now I understand that most of you think I have changed and that I am no longer the man I was. But you could not be further from the truth. The fact is I am the same man I always was I have just grown as a person and realized that people are like sheep. They will follow anyone who has what it takes to lead them, and believe me Oblivion has what it takes. It is very simple actually strength rules and the Oblivion in the end will prove much stronger than the EEI has ever been. Now I know many of you probably think that this is a barbaric idea, but think about it. If I husband hits his wife for cheating, or harming their children, or hell even just getting too bitchy, why the majority of the time does the wife not call the cops? The answer is simple when you think about it. She doesn't because she knows she deserves it. Just like the EEI deserves everything The Oblivion does to them. Face it EEI your days of glory are gone. You and I both know it. You slime realize that you are out classed and over powered so now you try to play on the sympathy of masses who you have trained to believe anything you say. Dirge destroys his family by fire, killing his parents and running his brother's life, and want sympathy for it? He wants people to believe he is the victim and they buy it!! He thinks that I NEED TO BE SAVED! I NEED NOTHING FROM YOU AND YOUR KIND YOU FREAK!" (Rush's face is an explosion of fury and rage, his appearance a grave contrast to the primordial rage in his eyes. He takes a deep breath and tries to regain his composure, fixing his hair before he goes on.) "But Dirge is not the only worm twisting logic to manipulate to stupidity of the masses. Brimstone and Picasso are also trying to twist their pasts to suit their own means. I mean really Brimstone do you think anyone with half a brain would blame your mother's people for tossing her out. I mean for a whore to have a son like you she must have been a real piece of work. I think the only tragedy in your story is that she didn't take you with her when she left this life. As for Picasso, well honestly Artsie do you really see me as I flunky? But I guess someone who blames his life's problems on dead man must be the biggest coward of all. Tell me what did this man truly do to you Picasso. Did he truly torment you? Or was he simply a friend looking out for you? Let me guess you are so starved for attention that you have turned a man who was perhaps your best friend, who did nothing to you other than succeed in making you stronger, into a monster now that he can not defend himself. You are a pitiful example of a human being Vinnie, and I am going to enjoy watching you bleed in the ring, and then taking you tag titles away." (Rush laughs manically) "When we meet Picasso I will make what ever your friend in high school did to you look like a mere paper cut. And then when it is over and Killjoy and I are the tag team champions all you will have is the truth that you are a weak pathetic fool, who doesn't even have enough decency to respect the dead. You’re through in Mercury Picasso, you and the EEI. So get use to it!" (Rush then turns and walks out onto the balcony as the camera fades to black.)
