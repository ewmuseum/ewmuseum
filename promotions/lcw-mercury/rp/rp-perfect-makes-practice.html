Perfect makes practice

31 May 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Perfect makes practice Perfect makes practice Posted Friday, May 31, 2002 by Picasso The Dirgedome is oddly quiet, as everyone seems to be doing their own things. A Lamborghini pulls up in the parking lot, and The Artiste pulls himself out and walks in the front door. No one's there. He walks through the rec room and sees a pile of cards sitting in or near a hat, but no one is there either. "Hello? Anyone home? Johnny? Dirge?" He asks as he walks into the gym. Brimstone's assistant is on her way our, and she waves him over. "Vincent! Brimstone's been looking for you. He took off not too long ago. Johnny was here, too, but I guess he left. I haven't seen Dirge or Pariah today. Um, are you alright?" "Fine, Why?" Then Picasso looks over at a mirror and sees his reflection. His normally clean-shaven face has a few day's growth of beard on it. His long blond hair is stuffed under a baseball cap, and his eyes are bloodshot. "Oh, that's why. I'm fine. Been under the weather, that's all. I called earlier, but no one answered. Are there any trainers around? Thought I might get in a workout before the match at Fusion." The asistant looks around. "I think Johnny injured them all. I'll see if Bruno is still here. I think he's healthy enough to spar." "That's fine. I'll be laying in the ring." The assistant departs, and Picasso crawls into the ring and slumps in a corner. "You don't know how much a person means to you until they're gone. I guess Gol is feeling that way about Sickness now. What happened, mate. Couldn't handle the leadership thing, could you. Your belt's gone. All your old friends are gone, too. Heard from the Trents at all? Has Mags left you any messages? Is he pissed off that you've destroyed Oblivion after the two of you started it up." "Look at what your once mighty organization has become. You gained the Mercury championship, but you couldn't last even one title defense. Hollow has made an almost negligible impact on Mercury. And your constant threats about impending violence and pain are already tiresome. Rush and Killjoy have been even less useful, if that's possible. And you all think things are going to turn around now that Sickness is back?" He laughs at that. "Is she going to wrestle? She may be smarter than the rest of your four combined, but unless she's going to get in the ring, she won't help you one damn bit when the bell sounds. This is a WRESTLING federation, boys. Words only get you so far; actions are what gets things done. And the four of you have failed, absolutely, completely, in every way, shape and form, FAILED, in accomplishing anything." "This match at Fusion is more a mercy killing than anything. You've tried to take over, it didn't work. Go split up and try something else to get noticed, because Oblivion is about to die." As Picasso finishes speaking, a tall, muscular man with a bandage around his ribs enters the gym. Picasso recognizes him immediately. "Bruno, my good man. How about a little sparring, to keep my reflexes sharp." Picasso tries to pull himself to his feet gracefully, but he stumbles a little. Bruno shakes his head. "Vince, I can small the whiskey from here. Go sleep it off, because you're in no shape to train." "I am a perfect specimen of fitness, I'll have you know. Get in here, or I'll go out there and drag you in." Bruno sighs and enters the ring. He has a 50-pound weight advantage and is completely sober, but he is under orders to never let up during these sparing matches. He walks to the center and awaits his opponent. Picasso, stumbles out of the corner but stays on his feet. They lock up, and Bruno pushes Picasso away and throws a right hook. "Let's end this quickly," he thinks to himself. But Picasso steps back, avoiding the punch. Bruno tries another punch and a roundhouse kick, but Picasso ducks them both. He in turn plants a boot into Bruno's midsection, doubling the big man over. He bounds off the ropes with surprising speed and agility, landing a spinning neckbreaker. He scales the nearest turnbuckle and lands a picture perfect moonsault. He quickly grabs hold of a leg and locks in the Chiaroscuro. After a few seconds, Bruno taps out. Picasso collapses to the mat, breathing heavily. Both men pick themselves up, looking at each other. Bruno's face has a look of pain, but Picasso is grinning. "That was fun. Try again?" "Shouldn't you be throwing up or something?" "It takes more than a bender to get me off my game, my good man. Come on, and don't go easy this time." (fade out)
