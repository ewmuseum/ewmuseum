Back to Business: Part 2

2 April 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Back to Business: Part 2 Back to Business: Part 2 Posted Tuesday, April 2, 2002 by BlazeMcCoy Back To Business: Part Two Blaze hangs up the phone, after pleading to the person on the other end to let him call them back. Blaze: Ok, where were we? Leah: You were about to get “Back to Business”? Exactly how do plan on getting, “Back to Business”? Blaze: I plan on just being me. I plan on being the man, the wrestler, and the performer that made me successful in the first place. It’s good for me but not good for Mr. Young. Yobbo, you can suggest that you have superior skill all you want. The fact remains that you have never proven yourself to me one on one in the ring. We have both been around for a while, and just recently met each other in tag team action, but now it’s just you and me mate! Blaze laughs just a bit. Blaze: But first, before I beat the living hell out of you, I must thank you. Thank you for waking me up and reminding me that I happen to be the most well-rounded and diverse wrestler in the Business. I can go from old school to hardcore. I can go from brawling to technical wrestling. I can show you power, or torture you into submission. My arsenal has it all, but I have failed to use it lately. I plan on reintroducing the wrestling world to Blaze McCoy, and you happen to be the first victim. Leah: But Blaze you have claimed to be reinvented and starting over and changing over and over. How is this time any different? Blaze: All those other changes were stabs in the dark. I ignored that fact that I wasn’t being me. I let the Business I had in the past fade away and so did my ability to perform at the top of my game, like I used to. Leah: You mentioned not being able to close the deal. Explain that a little more and elaborate on that. Blaze: Well, it’s like when I was about to take over a Business. I had the deal so close and the owners ready to sell. Then, I went in for the kill. I used to wrestle just like that. I saw a soft spot and attacked and used my strengths to win matches. It’s like having it slip right through your hands. For a second you think you have it. Then, it’s all a flash in the pan. I was haven’t been like that in the ring. My desire to close the deal had gone and in exchange I made excuses. Leah: So, once again, you are going to say that you are “due” for a win? Blaze: To say that I am due would be the understatement of the decade! I’m not due. I’m not crazy, and maybe there isn’t a conspiracy against me. However, I plan on showing everyone who I am at Fusion against you Robert Young. Leah: Mr. Young has also mentioned that he has some new moves or variations of moves to debut against you. How do you feel about that? Is that a threat? Blaze: A threat? No way. I mentioned before how devastating my arsenal can be and I will show him at Fusion. With not being able to close out a match lately, I haven’t been able to showcase some of the most incredible moves on the planet! Take the OutKast, a quick snap suplex bouncing your legs off the ropes, then jackhammering your ass straight into the mat. No one expects that when it comes, and when it does, its over. Bob wants to talk about submission moves? Well, I don’t know if he remembers when the current and soon to be dethroned LTD President Jacob Paxton wrestled here in LCW, but he has a move called the corporate ladder. I have perfected that upside-down surfboard submission move executed directly in the center of the ring that will virtually stretch your ass almost in two. You will be the one screaming and tapping out if I get you in that hold. Then, finally there is the Hook, Line and Sinker. I haven’t used that move in such a long time. Even I forget how destructive it can be. Sure it’s a very difficult maneuver to pull off, but if I get you on the second rope and have the chance….well, you know how it will end up. Above and beyond that, I have adopted and perfected some of the moves from my old friends in LCW such as Hype Norris’ Food Stamp and even Schmuu’s Hungry Man chokebomb. Oh, how could I forget the Blaze of Glory? Your face is ugly but I think I can rearrange it a bit more with the famous headlock drop that you felt the need to mention. Leah: Looking at the tale of the tape for this match, I see many similarities. Can you touch on those a bit? Blaze: You are right. There are many similarities between both of us. Actually, I have a graph of that right here behind me. Blaze Pulls an easel stand close to him from behind and pulls off the top sheet to reveal a “Tale of the Tape” comparison between himself and Robert Young. One the left side is Blaze’s name, physical qualities and some moves. On the other side, you see Young’s information. Blaze pulls out a laser pointer and starts to show it off. Blaze: First of all, we are physically very similar. Oh, by the way, I had to dig up your information from the LTD file,s since your bio is nowhere to be found in any of the Mercury records. What’s that all about? Anyway, you have a 5 pound and one inch advantage on me, which may or may not be to your favor. We are both listed as All-Around wrestlers. I even see some similar moves in our arsenals. We both share the DDT, the Spinebuster and even one of my signature moves, the fisherman’s suplex. But of course, my versions are better. We both like to drink alcohol. We both enjoy sports. We both wear sunglasses and hats on occasion. Oh, let me state my hats aren’t as silly looking as his. We have a lot of similarities, but we are very, very different. Blaze puts down the pointer and stares into the camera. Blaze: I will use every little detail I have researched to defeat you Bob, Hook, Line… Leah interrupts…. Leah: Wait Blaze. Before you spit out your so-played out catch phrase that never seemed to make sense, what about your partner Inferno? You haven’t mentioned anything about him or your run at the tag division. Blaze: Well, as much as I wish it didn’t, I’m afraid our team has failed. We trained and thought we had it down pat. We can’t fool ourselves into thinking we are a great team. Three strikes and we are out. We tried and tried and tried, but in the end we lost! I couldn’t close the deal and I don’t want to hold Inferno down anymore. I know he wants a shot in the singles division and he is right. He deserves it. Blaze looks into the camera. Blaze: Inferno, you are a great wrestler. Don’t let anyone tell you otherwise. I thought we could duplicate the OutKasts, but that it impossible. I thought we could bring back the glory to the tag division, but I guess we couldn’t. I wish the best of luck to you and if you ever need any help, this OutKast will be glad to help out. Now, if you don’t mind, I have Business to take care of. Leah: Well Blaze, I have to admit, this has been quite a surprise. No catch phrases and no excuses. Leah turns to the camera. Leah: There you have it. Blaze McCoy owes up to his failures and his weaknesses and claims to have found that missing link to get him back to where he once was no longer than a year ago. We will see at Fusion is he can get his long awaited win. Blaze is seen going back to tossing papers around and typing furiously as the scenes fades out.
