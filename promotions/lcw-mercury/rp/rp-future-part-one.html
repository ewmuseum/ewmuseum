LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Future... (Part One) Future... (Part One) Posted Wednesday, April 3, 2002 by Inferno The scene starts with one of Inferno sitting on a bench in the middle of a park. The sun high yet again, the soft sound of birds singing and people talking, as well as the gentle noise of water flowing in the back ground. Inferno seems relaxed and laid back, enjoying the peace and quite of the area… Inferno: Life can be so simple some times… He has now turned his head to face the camera. His arms are folded, and has his eyes screwed up, as he is looking into the sun. Inferno: But so damn confusing the rest of the time! He then looks down and takes a deep sigh, and rests his head on the top of the bench as if he is very tired, and the closes his eyes… Inferno: But it equals out…. And becomes, very complex. You see, I was watching the TV the other day, and watching a news program and all I saw was bad news. So many stories, so much terror, so much hurt, pain and tears. It makes me think what I actually have….. Inferno shakes his head, still, his eyes are closed shut and taking deep breathes in and out. Inferno: What do I have though? A career. A home. Money. Pride. Integrity. A Future? Hah… A Future! That’s a good question. If I stop and think, where will I be, this time five years time, I would hope to be saying ‘Wrestling of course, what else?’ But I would only be saying that if I straighten out my ideas and get down to business, something that I haven’t been doing lately! Inferno: The past is a harsh thing for me. All I see is hurt, pain and tears. Since I started to wrestle, it has gone even further down hill. I will be the first to admit, I ain’t the best man in the squared circle, and I have lost, oh so many matches, but I am a man who can hold my own. Is it enough? From what I have been experiencing, no! Since I walked into the LCW scene, late 2001, into LTD, I didn’t do very well! My only memory that I took away from LTD is the Lethal title! Now I am here in Mercury… I haven’t won a single match, got the one, two, three on any one or made some punk submit… That sucks! Inferno opens his eyes, frowns at the camera and moves to the end of the wooden bench. Inferno puts his sunglasses on which were on his head before hand to stop the sun, and looks as though he carries on… Inferno: So… when people ask me what I will be doing in five years…. Will I say Wrestling? ‘Cause at the rate I am going now, lose, after lose and lose, constantly, wont get me far, any time soon! But Fusion, has myself pitted against Memosarks. To him, our little match on Fusion, could be only that… Little! But to me, this fight we will have is big for me. I am looking at our fight a way I have never looked at one before…. You know, this match, for me, could mean the ‘make or break’ time for Inferno! Finally, Inferno raises up from the bench and takes short, quick glances around the park and the sky above. Inferno then turns to his right and starts walking, only takes short, slow steps, his head down, facing the concrete floor. Inferno: So, Memosarks, what you think of me, and our match, wont bother me, I know who I am, and what I can do, but I will keep that to myself, what I know of you is you’re a man, who thinks he is funny, thinks he is a legend, walks around the back, as if he is ‘Mr. Big Shot’ and maybe even thinking I am his light work? I am not saying you do, merely questioning the fact that you do! Inferno: Now, we have never met inside the ring before, hell, we have never come face to face before and had a real conversation together, that has never happened, will it in the future? Who knows, but if you see, the future always seems to get in my words and proving to me that my ‘Future’ is one hell of an important factor in mine, well, everyone’s life. Inferno keeps walking, but now approaching a stand next to the side walk, a man is stood there handing out pieces of paper to passers by, he ahs a huge smile on his face and has a cheesy a outfit on. Inferno just looks up, groans and shrugs his shoulders as he gets closer… Inferno: Just look at him. I wonder where he was five years ago, huh? Backyard wrestling? In another indy’ federation? High School? McDonalds training facility? God knows where, but I don’t want to think about it, he obviously didn’t think about what he was going to do…. Or didn’t succeed in what he was doing before. I don’t want that to happen to me! I couldn’t live with myself, or forgive myself that I didn’t put in the hard work, and extra effort to accomplish something I wanted! Inferno: So, at Fusion, Memosarks and I, Inferno, will go at it, one on one, and finally, I will get my first real chance in LCW Mercury, if I don’t reach out and push myself to make the best of this chance, then all I will be doing is opening up cards, filling roster numbers up, being dummies for young, up and comers. I want to make it into the big time here, I know I can, I have the abilities, I have the dreams, I just need to work for it… As Inferno walks by the stand, which has a large banner on it saying; ’20 Questions’ , the man behind it shouts out… Man: Excuse me Sir, could I please give you this paper of questions….. inferno: Ha, no, I, erm, need to wash my hair… Man: What the… ohh, your just trying to get me off your back, hah! Please, just take one and read it… Inferno: Listen punk…. Man: Calm down there Sir, just read, its very interesting and makes you think about you and other things… Inferno: You know, you’re lucky that you are a good, whatever you are, ‘cause you were annoying me, so, I’ll take one…. The man at the stand gives Inferno one, as he smiles and looks proud of himself, Inferno walks away with his head down reading the paper… On it, has the title as ’20 Questions’ with a list of (you guessed it) 20 questions!
