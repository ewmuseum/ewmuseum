LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition E F F E C T E F F E C T Posted Thursday, May 23, 2002 by Dirge The scene starts out with no image, only the sound of running water and steam. A few moments later and your eyes adjust to see the dark interior of what looks like a cave. Flows of steam pass back and forth in front of the camera, obscuring vision. Through the haze, it is apparent that DIRGE is leaning against the wall of a cave in an underground hot spring. He is covered in steam and sweat, and is up to his chest in frothing water. Should have seen this place before I had the pumps installed. The water didn't move around as much. DIRGE runs a massive hand through his spiky, yet drooping blond hair. The moisture is heavy enough to counter the effects of his gel. Nothing relaxes a person like the wonders of nature combined with the wonders of man uniting to create a truly soothing environment. One perfect for thinking. Something I have been doing much of lately. Apex has also been thinking. But his thoughts are impotent. They bear no fruit. They are a single conclusion with no possibility for growth. Apex thinks that because he has hurt others, he can hurt me. He thinks that what he did to Brimstone means that he is from this moment on, capable of doing the same to everyone. He thinks that because he survived his initial battle with addiction, that he is from this moment on immune to it. And he thinks that because he has turned his back on God, and on his beliefs, that they no longer apply to him. The logic is not bad, merely the information he used to draw his conclusions is incomplete. First, I can assure you that after watching his match with my friend, and spending long hours training with said friend, I am amply prepared to counter anything Apex throws at me. But I would be a fool to have only studied one of his matches, or even all of his past matches. I also have to consider what new tricks he might have up his gigantic sleeve. And consider I have. I have been training extensively with Vincent, Johnny, and Pariah lately as well. With one purpose at the forfront of my list of purposes. To be a thorn. Every time you try to grab me, throw a punch at me, use your size against me, I will make sure that you suffer. There is not move in even Harbinger's massive repertoire that I have not practiced and analyzed to a certain point. The point where I can hurt you no matter what you're doing to me. In every hold, I will twist myself despite agony or injury to hurt you. Every punch you throw at me I will make sure hurts you more than me. Like a thorn, the tighter you grip me the more you will hurt yourself. Apex thinks that he can escape the wrath of the Almighty by choosing to ignore the reality of his situation. You said He once gave you strenth. At one point, it was reality to you. You FELT it happen to you. You also felt abandoned. So do all the damned. Turning your back will only provide a better place to stick the knife. Apex thinks I am remorseful. Rest assured I am not. Remorse would mean that I admit to making a mistake, when what I did was calculated even to the point of shifting the blame for my sins onto my brother. Apex doesn't understand why I am a crusader for Entropy. Because I don't feel like explaining the whole thing again, here's a reader's digest version of how it applies to my current situation: Entropy is the scientifically proven death sentence of the universe, the point where the massive engine of reality runs out of gas. The universe responds by evolving itself to become more efficient, thus entropy is the catalyst for change, the disorder that forces order to exist as a counterbalance. By championing Entropy on a micro level, one can induce this positive change, with the ultimate goal of forcing perfection to result from evolution. This is basically my mission, to create a world so difficult and harsh, that only the strongest may survive. These "strongest" will no longer need a catalyst for change. Consider us quality control. So far, no one has been able to wipe out the crusade, so the crusade must continue until someone can. When that day comes, we will no longer be necessary. So to make sure that I am the catalyst for the greatest possible enemy, I am forced to become as strong as possible. I am forced to put business first, pleasure second. I have been doing this since I have had the capacity for rational thought. My self discipline has borne the fruit of mastery. My will is unbreakable. My devotion to my cause has never wavered, my determination has never fallen short. Even past defeats have only strengthened my already formidable self control. You can't break me, Hollow. Nothing can. Even in hell, I will know it is all part of what I accepted. One more torment to endure. Its not me I feel sorry for. Its the ones that don't have the fortitude to accept their fate. The ones who lie to themselves to get throught the tough spots, the ones who let God carry them when there is only one set of footprints in the sand. The ones who think their shit doesn't stink. The ones like Hollow. You may think that the improvements you've made on yourself are significant, and that your will is stronger since facing the challenges that forced you to leave. And you are right. But I have a head start on you, Ignacio. A long lead. I have never run from a problem. No matter how big they seemed, no matter how heavy, no matter how bad the odds were against me. My will has prevailed. To use one of your own arguments, ask Gol, Young, Pariah, Blaze, Glamour, or even Rush and my Brother. Ask all the ones who should have beaten me. All the bigger, stronger ones. No one remembers an easy battle with DIRGE. No one can say my will ever failed to bring my body past the point it should have gone. Even those that beat me knew that my body shouldn't have been able to take as much punishment as it did everytime. It always surprises them. You, however, would seem to have the advantage. You seem to be the favored one. Its practically impossible for you to surprise someone by over-perfoming, by being more than they expected. The only surprised statements made about you after the match will be "Hollow---he's not as big as I thought." But enough talk. Soon we will see who's will is stronger. The one who recently grew out of adversity, or the one that has always been growing out of adversity. Goliaths fall far more frequently than people would think. Regardless of the era. DIRGE finishes speaking, his curt, precise tones now silent. He stares at the camera intently for a moment. Then looks down, cups a handful of water and washes his sweaty face with it. He then leans back and closes his eyes, enjoying the steam and warm water. END TRANSMISSION
