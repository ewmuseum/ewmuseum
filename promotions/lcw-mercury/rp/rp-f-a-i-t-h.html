LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition F A I T H F A I T H Posted Tuesday, April 9, 2002 by Dirge DIRGE is sitting at his black oak desk. Both his championship belts are mounted on the wall behind him, encased in glass. The room is dark except for a dim light directly above DIRGE and the belts. He looks down at a thick piece of paper. Its so quiet that the sound of DIRGE scratching something out can be heard loud and clear. He looks up at the camera, and his familiar deep voice resonates in the quiet room as his first word is uttered. ----------- Faith. Everyone has faith in something. Gods, demons, themselves.....sometimes in the concept of nothing. Faith is a condition not reserved for those who have imaginations. Every night you fall asleep, you have some measure of faith that you will wake the next morning. When you eat, you have faith that you will be nourished. A life devoid of faith is a life devoid of intelligence. That is not an insult, it is reality. (He breathes a sigh, somewhat disappointed) Sadly, intelligence is a rare commodity among human beings. Thanks to our overprotective society which coddles the weak and helpless, it doesn't take much cleverness to survive. When was the last time you had to kill your food, or even worry for your life? These things occur, obviously, but less so in America than in the rest of the world. It takes very little to make sure you have a warm bed and a full belly in this country. Even those we hold as prisoners for crimes of war against our country are well fed and allowed to practice their religion. Just ask the Taliban fighters being held in Guantanamo bay, on average they've gained 30 lbs a piece. Some would call it a reward. On average, americans are just plain fatter and more spoiled than anyone else in the world besides royalty. But I don't have a problem with that, because it shows that Americans ARE fit to survive. In a recession that began Clinton was in office, Americans still eat, still live, still dominate the world. The intelligence of the American people has evolved our nation. America survives despite the sloth of its inhabitants because we're too wily to be anything less than number one. Those physically evolved in this country are those rare few who face hardship, or those who purposely evolve their bodies, such as professional athletes. Why do women want to attach themselves to athletes, or to the wealthy more than any other? Because nature dictates to them that these are the most fit, the most likely and able to survive. For the good of the species, this must happen. But as natural law dictates, evolution only occurs when it is forced. A man must claw his way through peers and superiors to get to the top of a corporation. A man must spend hours a day in a gym or engaged in physical activity to force his body into becoming a machine, or spend hours in practice daily so that he can beat another competitor and earn a better chance of survival, a better livelihood. Intellectual, physical, financial superiority....The evolution of America. Or a select few at any rate. All it takes is a minority of people to achieve these pursuits and the entire nation can lay back and enjoy the benefits. America IS a great nation, the GREATEST nation because a small number of men each generation rise above their peers and have more of an impact than any ten thousand men. This is what I have faith in. The small percentage of men that stop at nothing to ensure survival for themselves, and their way of life. This is what I am. Professional Wrestlers must be superior to the common man in strength, and the ability to take injury that would incapacitate or kill the average person. Owners of corporations are ruthless, and use more wit and strategy to achieve and maintain their station than any other job. Philosophers must consider possibilities that others refuse to acknowledge so that mysteries may be known, that this world becomes a tool rather than a predator. In all of these, I am a success. I am a professional athlete who runs his successful corporation, who bases every aspect of his life on his governing philosophy, and who is the representing champion of the greatest nation and continent on Earth. Faith in Entropy made my life choices easy. Faith in myself made me a success. Faith in my chosen, not biological family made me happy. The way of life I have grown accustomed to in LCW Mercury is being challenged by men who have faith that they will destroy it. The challenge of Oblivion, although costly in blood, has forced me to evolve further. The only valid point I have heard any member of Oblivion make is that I don't deserve my tag team championship belt, because Johhny Glamour earned my half, and Vincent "The Artiste" Picasso earned the other. Absolutely right. I didn't earn it, but Dave Beaumont awarded me this belt. He let me defend it for Johnny, and I can honestly say that he would have done a better job. Picasso has carried me through practically every match because he IS the greatest tag team wrestler in the sport today. Just think, Dave Beaumont GAVE me the belt with complete confidence rather than putting it up for grabs. It seems to me that he is a smart business man because when he thought of the good of the company, he had no faith in the Trent brothers. It would have made the tag team title a singles belt, and that would have made the whole division a joke when no team proved capable of beating Vincent for the straps. He's already shown he can fight most of you at the same time and give a better accounting of himself. Blaze, Inferno, you two are the wild cards in this match. It will be interesting to see who you hate more, us, or Oblivion. No matter, just come ready for something ugly to happen in that ring. I look forward to witnessing your evolution through all this. Putting the best 3 tag teams in LCW in the same match should drive the buyrates through the roof. Before you think I'm kissing anyone's ass, rest assured I have faith that Picasso and I will stand together as champions after the match once again. (Dirge smiles broadly, but it doesn't reach his eyes which have not blinked once at any challenge he has faced.) Before I finish up, I would just like to say to Johhny Glamour and Brimstone, we had a good run. I know you guys are happy for us. But alas, the Entropy crusade is just me and Vincent now, and its not the same without you guys. Ultimate refinement is now the current state of things. I raise a glass to the completion of one of the greatest eras in history, but there's only one group in LCW now, and it is my quest to destroy them. In my time of bittersweet joy, I must also extend my sympathy to others who have tragedies to mourn as well. Harbinger, you had faith. It was in something false. Time for you to evolve it. Slate, you had faith, and were also betrayed. Don't change a thing. Being chivalrous and generous was never my thing, but its been yours. Evolve as well, but don't change. My condolences to the crowds who have been deprived of Memosarks lately, hopefully nothing to terrible is keeping him from them. My condolences to whoever has the misfortune of facing Pariah next. And of course, my condolences to Oblivion. Vincent and I are not through with you yet. Having Gol's belt around my waist certainly feels good, but I still want to take more from you. Don't worry, I'll find something. ----------------------- DIRGE looks down and crosses out another item on his agenda. He stands to leave, somewhat slow and ponderous. The lights are shut off. The sound of the door closing signals the camera to view DIRGE's desk and he paper on it. With the moon's reflected light, the final word on the page can be seen: E E I crossed out. ----------------------- End Transmission
