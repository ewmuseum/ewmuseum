LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition All good things... All good things... Posted Wednesday, April 10, 2002 by Picasso While the gallery section of the Artiste's studio is busy with employees and potential customers, the room where Vincent Picasso paints is completely silent. The walls are all soundproofed to keep out distractions. Near one corner sits a canvas on an easel. It's completely blank. On the other end of the room, Picasso stares at it intently. Several broken brushes lay scattered at his feet. It's been a week since he was able to pick up a brush and paint. Now, his hands start shaking uncontrollably whenever he tries to paint. Taking his mind off his current troubles, he looks at the camera and focuses on his upcoming match. "Well, I guess it's official, now that Dirge has spoken out. EEI, down to two members, is gone. It was grand while it lasted, though. The last few years are something I will never forget. Fortunately, two of us are still more than a match for any other group that would dare show its face in this fed, Oblivion included." "Dirge, I owe you a great deal. I've gotten to where I am in wrestling because of you, brimstone and Johnny pushing me to be great. I came into Mercury only because you invited me. And when I had no where else to turn, you put aside your singles career and agreed to be my tag team partner. That North American championship is richly deserved, my friend. It's an honor to stand by your side in combat." "And what a war we face at Fusion, against the Trents and the Heatseekers. The Seekers, in my eyes, at least know where they stand in this match. They're the underdogs, the wild cards, the team no one expects to win. I can admire their honesty. I still don't particularly like them, and they still don't have a hope in hell of winning, but at least I can take them seriously now." "The Trents, however, are still living in their little fantasy world, where they think being part of Oblivion will make them champions. I've tried, I've really tried to make them see reason, but it's no use. You know you're not any better wrestlers than you were a month ago. If anything, you've gotten even dumber and lazier. You've been attacking in groups; I don't even think you know how to fight when the numbers are even anymore. Between Gol, Mags and now Sickness leading you around, I'm not even sure you can think for yourselves, either." "I couldn't help but laugh at something Gol said when he was talking about your little group. He said you all want to create a Mercury when you all are the standard that all other wrestlers must measure up to. It was funny, because the two of you are far beneath my standards, and I don't ever see you improving. This team, with either Johnny or Dirge as my partner, towers over you, in terms of intelligence, ability and charisma. I thought, once, that given some time and some training, the two of you might be good wrestlers some day. But if you're content to take shortcuts, then you're both utterly hopeless." "EEI took over more federations than you'll ever know. And we did all the trashtalking you're doing now, and more. But when it came time to back up our talk, any of us could outwrestle the best competition there was to offer. All the eye-pokes and ballshots were just for fun. But I haven't seen any of you win a match by sheer talent. It's been through cheating, it's been by having strength in numbers, but it's never been by talent. It's just so...unaesthetic." "Ultimate Refinement is THE standard-bearer for the tag team division. The Heatseekers have already figured out where they stand here; it's past time for you to learn your place on the food chain too." "Now, get out. I have work to do." With that, Picasso turns his attention back to his canvas, determined to overcome this affliction. The camera is already forgotten. (fade out)
