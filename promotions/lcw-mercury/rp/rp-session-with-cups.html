Session with.... Cups?

15 April 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Session with.... Cups? Session with.... Cups? Posted Monday, April 15, 2002 by Inferno Inferno: A situation, one that could cause problems… Inferno is sitting down on a chair, with a table in front of him. He has six plastic cups in front on the table, laid out next to each other. Inferno has his head down facing the cups… Inferno: One that, well, makes me laugh…. Inferno turns to face the camera, and grins…. Inferno: One that will make life a whole lot easier…. Inferno looks back down and rests his head on his hands, as he stops moving and breathes heavily… Inferno: What is the problem? The mighty EEI… splitting up! Hah, you know, when I entered Mercury, over a month ago, I would never have imagined that EEI splitting would have been so…. Great. I mean, what will go down with the Tag Titles now, eh? Inferno: But now Dirge, has gone to concentrate on his singles career, leaving Mr. Picasso…. On his own! Ha! Now, I personally don’t know how this will affect me in the long run, but all I know now, is Picasso, is like I said, on his own! Inferno picks up one of the plastic cups and holds it just in front of his face, he shakes his head and then a small grin appears… Inferno: See, there were six men in the ring last night, and all of us tried to do what we had to…. Bring back the gold. Of course, EEI did it, but looking back, there is now only one….. Inferno crushes the can in his hand and drops it onto the floor, smiling at this…. Inferno: Bye bye Dirge! Now that man is out of the picture…. That leaves Picasso, but right now, I am not interested in him…. Inferno then flicks another cup away, falling off the side of the table, now leaving four cups… Inferno: And then there was four! Josh Trent, Mikel Trent, Blaze McCoy and Inferno! The men who will team and then face off for the prize we all want, the LCW Mercury Tag Team Titles. Inferno: But who will come out on top and head to Suicide to face, well, only god knows! It will be the third time myself and Blaze will go into battle against you both, and our history against you two, well, isn’t all that flattering! I went into Fusion last night, wanting to win, my spirits high, really ready…. Only to have it all come crashing down. But next week, well its different. Next week, I will go into the match thinking I can do it. I know Blaze and I are on par with you both, we are all on equal levels of abilities, and we all know it, you, me, Blaze and those people who watch us every week. Inferno: But how will it be different this week? The difference this time over, is that HeatSeekers, well, we will come out victorious. Yeah, y’all going to laugh that off, ain’t you? Do it! Do whatever the hell you want, in the back of our minds, we know what our limits are, and we have been in the ring with you twice before, this time, Blaze and I, we are ready and ready to kick half of Oblivions ass! Inferno picks two cups up, one in each hand and stares at them both. He shrugs and shakes his head, and turns once again to the camera… Inferno: Josh, Mikel Trent. I know you think you are both superior to us, well, everyone else in LCW who isn’t in your little ‘crew’, Oblivion. In my view on this… well, laughter! Watching y’all kicking peoples ass here, there and every where has to make you laugh, there will have to be something wrong if you think it isn’t funny! You do it to make a point? What is the point your trying to imply? That you can attack from no where, with out them knowing? To show power in numbers really does work? Inferno: If so…. Well done, point proven! But Josh and Mikel, I know you have won twice against us already and no doubt you will point that out on numerous occasions and I’m sure I will be asleep when you have finished. Maybe you will compare us both to lamps? Mock our Tag name, just like you have 100 times before? And call us your ‘light work’ just like you have said…. 100 times before! Infernos faces turns a slight red color as he crushes one of the cups in his hand and grins… Inferno: You want your shot, of course you do, but Blaze and I, we sure as hell want it, just as badly as you two. Run around talking your trash aimed at us, for the next few days, do what you want, come Fusion, our two man machine will be in the right gear, ready to attack… Inferno crushes the other cup in his other hand, releases the grip and both land on the floor. Inferno then turns to the two remaining on the table, and places them together… Inferno: And then, Blaze and Inferno, were left…. Ready for Suicide? Well, we just need to get past our first wall on the road to success, the Trents…. Yeah, tough job, but a ‘doable’ one also…. We will see as time passes on, I guess… only time will tell! Inferno lifts the remaining two cups up and looks at them as the scene fades to black…
