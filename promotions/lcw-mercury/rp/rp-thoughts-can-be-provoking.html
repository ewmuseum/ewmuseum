Thoughts can be provoking.

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Thoughts can be provoking. Thoughts can be provoking. Posted Thursday, May 23, 2002 by Jin Akujin Jin sat down in a small diner of sorts with many truckers dining on their food before having to head back out onto the road, Jin let out a sigh before laying his head onto a window in a bored manner. It had been such long days after what happened at Suicide, the betrayel of Harbinger, someone he considered a friend on a professional level. Herbert Ingles was not one to be trusted however, it was because of him that Harbinger had to raise his abilities up a notch, something that is going to untimely help him in the future. He shouldn't really be complaining however as his matches with Habra have also increased his ability in the ring, something that will definitly be good in his future with Mercury. Now he has to face another LCW legend being Robert Young, formerly known as Bob Yobbo Jr.. Jin: Is it all worth it..? I ask myself time and time again on why do I put up with the things that I do and the reason is; that I can't get enough of it. The ability of my opponents, the feeling of a part of my body being dropped onto that mat, the adrenaline running rampid throughout my intire body. "You get to fight Robbie.." Jin: I realized that, Mr. Young.. Mr. Young.. a legend in LCW, a man that many wrestlers did not really take too seriously in the past, but seemed to have turned over a new leave. He seems to hae became more of a technical wrestler than your average brawler like before, which is a good thing because I don't want to spend my time in the match trading blows for blows with him. "True, but you have to worry about him all the more." Jin: I'm currently thinking that over, him now being a technical wrestler makes him more dangerous than ever before. You see not very many people know what a technical wrestler is actually CAPABLE of, let's say, a brawler goes in with a technical wrestler and that brawler will be beaten in a matter of minutes. Technical wrestlers concentrate on joints, the most sensitive part of the human body, afterall.. what can a person do if they can't move their arms and legs? Technical wrestling also involves insane head drops like Habra's Punk Out variations, or my Raiden X Perfect Driver. *Jin moves a hand through his hair slowly before continuing.* Jin: Many other classes of wrestling such as power, brawling, and hardcore to name a few, completly underestimate what a technical wrestler can actually do to them. Imagine a power wrestler not able to use his power moves because his joints are in too much agony from the submission holds and work put on them, or a brawler not able to punch or kick because it hurts so much to raise that body part. Let's not forget that a hardcore wrestler couldn't use their precious weapons since their joints would be too weak to lift a weapon for use. Technical wrestling is, in my opinion, the most effective type of wrestling, there are almost no flaws in it at all. That's why I incorporated it into my mixed style of highflying, lucha, and martial arts. "Point well made.." Jin: I know who Bob Yobbo Jr is, but not Robert Young. He seemed to develop quite a meanstreak in him that he carried over to Mercury, he is definatly the master of his chosen profession. He has forgotten that I have faced Bob Yobbo Jr before and have actually defeated him in a match, sadly it was a tag match and not a singles match as I would have liked. This time however is different, there is no Kensuke in my corner, and no Headbuster in his, it's man to man in this confrontation. I'm not dealing with the happy-go-lucky Bob Yobbo Jr, but the sinister and cold Robert Young that many people seemed to not cheer for anymore. I also know that I'm no where near the size or power that Brimstone possesses or have as much experience as Dirge does in wrestling wars, but I can make up for that. Robert has forgotten some things, I am much faster than he is and have a little bit more experience in the technical field, sure, he can try to hit me with his power moves.. it wouldn't matter since I could work over his legs with my kicks. *Jin glanced around before continuing with his little "chat".* Jin: Sure, I'm fancy in the ring because I know what I'm doing in the ring, does he think that it's easy pulling off a 450 splash, let alone a Hell Crasher? He seems to have forgotten that I have taken and reversed some of the most dangerous moves in wrestling, I survived Harbinger's Punk Outs and the Dreadlock, not very many can claim to do that. I am also the only person to reverse his Punk Out 5.0 in mid air, so what I don't have in power or strength, I make up for it in my fighting spirit and quick thinking. "He is going to be surprised.." Jin: He also forgets that he is a veteran and I am still a rookie in LCW, what does that make me? It makes me hungry to get wins, it drives me to do better and better each match. He can do whatever he wants to my body, but I will get right back up and continue to fight him until I have nothing left. That's the same thing that I did with Harbinger or my other opponents, every time that I face them in the ring.. I get to know them a bit better, as they do with me. *With a simple glance outside the window, Jin continues to speak.* Jin: I have tagged with Young, I respect Young for what he has done for this sport, but one thing I am is afraid of Young. He can try to attempt to hold me down from my true potential, because he knows that I can defeat him with it. I am defeated a world class athlete with my true potential, and that makes Young afraid of what I could truly do to him when I put my mind to it. "Every Mercury wrestler has a great potential, but people like Harbinger and yourself can go even past that." Jin: That's true, Habra can get even better and so could I, we're both still young and with me still being early in my career here in Mercury while Young has established his legend here, he barely has anything more to do except win the Mercury title. Me? I still have a legend to build with my own two hands like great legends even if I do not really admire them, Gol and Rook Black have done... I want to establish it just like they have done, through hard work and dedication to this sport. "That's it..?" Jin: For now.. *Jin closed his eyes as he leaned his head back.* (Fade Out)
