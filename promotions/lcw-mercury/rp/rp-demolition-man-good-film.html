Demolition Man- good film

8 April 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Demolition Man- good film Demolition Man- good film Posted Monday, April 8, 2002 by Robert Young Robert Young is sitting down at a table in an independent coffee shop. He is having his Mocha and reading the local daily paper. Young looks up at the camera and gives a slight smile, then he frowns. Young: Gol and I have an elaborate history, one that does not need to be explained and extended because it would take a damn long time. I haven’t really spoken to him since I have returned to this top echelon of wrestling. His new friends occupy him way too much. Anyway I am not coming out here like Slate and trashing a truly great wrestler. A blind man can see Gol has a heck load of achievements in his resume and deserves the utmost respect for it. In the last year he has fought and defeated great wrestlers like Magnum, Rook, Felony and all those ones he has listed lately. But now he is speaking of how serious a threat his group is, and how I am a joke. Well I have been a joke in the past, but I don’t plan on being one ever again, that’s why I dropped the whole Yobbo thing. I hope in my matches so far I have proven I am not a joke. I am yet to live up to Gol’s current status to be sure but I would like to think I am no longer a joke. Gol does try to make a fairly logical argument with far too many flaws. He seems to brush aside me taking Pariah to the limit. He seems to brush aside me nearly defeating his stable mate Magnum. He seems to brush aside Blaze being humbled before me. See all those men came out here with the same words as you, how I am a joke, how I have had it, well look here I am standing, and I am standing with honor. Young sips at his Coffee and reads the paper for 20 seconds or so before continuing. Young: Yeah Blaze came out here and ignored everything I said, he ran around in circles trying to discredit me as a threat because he was being so businesslike. He saw me as the same old Yobbo. Well the same old Yobbo kicked his ass. Gol you come out here and finish by saying I am the same old man. Well like Blaze I plan to prove you wrong. But I would like to think you already know that. I mean I have proven it time and time again. Of course your strength has been your confidence, but your weakness was always your arrogance mate. You seem to really low a low opinion of my in ring skills and a grossly overstatement on your performance after rooting Jessica for a few months. Gol you were just beaten by Dirge, no insult but he isn’t exactly Felony or Rook he isn’t Harbinger or Pariah. Gol this man took this North American title that you so highly regard. Meanwhile I am someone who knows you better than anyone. I am a man that knows every intimate detail of your wrestling and personal life. I am someone who has fought you before both of us have taken wrestling, fighting, brawling, to new levels. If Dirge can beat you I would like to think my chances are up there. You can downgrade my achievements in the past month. I am not the Mercury or EVO Champ. But neither are you. Indeed you had an easy path back in here, straight into there top card, but you have done little to nothing since that return. Meanwhile I have earned every step. I have defeated Myrddin and Venom, Hell I did that to win the LTD title. So maybe you should get your facts straight. Again your arrogance seems to cloud facts. I teamed with Headbuster, I have never fought him, you seem to distort the truth. I earned my spot in Mercury the hard way mate. Since my return I have proved every doubter wrong. People doubted I could compete on Magnum’s level. People doubted Jin and I could defeat Blaze and Inferno, people doubted I deserved to fight Pariah, let alone did they think I could come so close to taking him out, lastly people doubted I could beat Blaze. Gol, you’re doubting me, just like those guys. Like those guys you have your reasons. You but stated in your promos I used to have it. Well I am going about showing that old persona had nothing on what I am now. I am much fitter, much tougher, a more well rounded, basically much better a man and a wrestler than I have ever been. See Gol every doubter has been proven wrong. I am not going to arrogantly pronounce my victory but I intend on making it far from easy for you to fulfill your prophecy. See I do intend on having another chance with Pariah, I think I proved I had what it takes to take that belt from him. I intend on getting a second chance at him. Gol I am far from intimidated against you. I don’t disrespect you like Slate. I believe in myself and in our match that’s going to help me far more than anyone understands. Gol my only guarantee is you better earn your shot, because otherwise it’s going to be my shot. Once more I am going into that ring to prove the Robert Young doubters wrong. Once again Gol I plan to succeed. Young takes another sip of his coffee and then grabs the paper and leaves. The scene fades.
