Less talk, more chalk

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Less talk, more chalk Less talk, more chalk Posted Tuesday, May 21, 2002 by Hollow Ignacio poses in front of a mirror, in jeans with no shirt on. He's not looking at himself with pride; rather, he's inspecting himself. "I have destroyed myself, have I? I doubt your friends, Brimstone and Picasso, would share that sentiment. A 'destroyed' man could never cause such injury, nor could he relish it. Certainly, while I might lack much of the strength I once had, I still have more than enough to deal with the likes of you." He turns to the right and continues the inspection. "You attempt to compare apples and oranges; or, rather, apples and homicide. While I caused injury to myself... injury which has since been repaired to a large extent... you wiped out your family. There is a difference. Ah, but what does it all matter if one is not concerned about sin in the first place?" Ignacio turns to the camera and gives us a look that's as smug as he is big. He's going to inform poor, dumb Dirge about somethin'. "God did give me this body. God told me to wrestle, and so I did. God told me to change wrestling as a whole, starting with LCW. Despite my best efforts, God refused to allow me to succeed, and he spurned me. The concept of 'sin' never enters into my decision process, and as such I give no credence to those like you who call me a sinner, nor to those who claim that my sin will lead me to damnation. I give you even less credence because you hide yourself behind some vague sense of moral superiority rather than address the physical reality of our upcoming confrontation." Ignacio makes a sweeping gesture towards his chest. "Here before you stands a man who is larger, stronger, is far more proficient in the craft of fighting, and has no compunctions about violating rules sent from a so-called higher being. Where you spent the majority of your career as a clown, I have always been capable of producing damage on a level far above anything you've ever managed. "We both know your current title reign is a facade; deep down, you'd rather atone for your crimes through antics rather than meaningful victories. You'd rather champion disorder than North America. Coming into the match, I was more than willing to gain my second singles title, but after hearing your words, I have something much more... fun... in mind." A smirk. On most men it would be subtle, but sadly for Ignacio, subtlety is nearly impossible in the way of facial expressions. "You reference 'hell' and 'burning' over and over, perhaps because of your childhood arson. Tell me, do you understand the nature of death by fire? The fire may burn flesh, but it is smoke which brings the demise of victims. First choking, then unconsciousness, then the embrace of nothingness. "You will not be so lucky, Dirge. At Fusion, I will show you something much more terrifying than a hazy, clouded end to existence. When I'm hurting you, you won't be able to fall asleep and let it all pass away. You'll be conscious, uncomfortably so, as each blow places you deeper into a pit of agony. You'll be able to feel each specific pain in clear detail, until either I'm defeated or you are even more broken than I ever was. Given the nature of your average match, a betting man would place his money on the latter. "Truely, Dirge, you seem to long for your own eternal punishment. Hopefully what you go through in our match will satisfy you until the day that finally comes." Ignacio waves goodbye. We fade to black.
