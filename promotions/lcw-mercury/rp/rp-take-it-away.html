LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Take it away... Take it away... Posted Thursday, May 23, 2002 by Inferno (As soon at the scene opens up, the image has a picture of some blinds down over the a window. The area is quite dark, not much light is able to filter through the screen over the window. Until a pair of hands come into view and pull the blind up to reveal a busy road. Cars shooting past, people walking buy, the sounds of cars, cell phones and people talking is heard faintly through the window. It is early morning, everyone making their way to work…) Inferno: It’s a busy world out there, an ever changing one, at that… (Inferno was the one who pulled the blind up. He has jeans on, a tattered shirt on, and seems to be in a motel room. He is rushing around the room, trying to find things, a bad on the bed open with some gear in it…) Inferno: … it seems to be a mirror image of my life…. (Inferno grabs a shirt and tosses it into the bag, now heading over to a corner of the room…) Inferno: Pariah; stop your laughing. Start your game; Fusion is closing in on us. (Inferno stops, takes a deep breath and heads over to the bath room. After a few seconds, we can hear, well… you know what we can hear! Then the flush is heard and then he returns into the main room. He grins at the camera and goes over to the bed) Inferno: I woke up a half an hour ago, and when I opened my eyes, I realised that I couldn’t care less what people thought of me now. A Joke? Bah…a rookie? Hah, a looser? Meh… I will prove everyone wrong, including you, Pariah. Inferno: You seem to think I will be a push over. You think I can’t talk the talk. I’ll leave you to judge that, but aren’t you the one who has pointed out, so many times, that actions speak louder than words?! So, whether I can talk the talk matters, not! You so rightfully put it that actions are louder, so, I will show you, that I can walk the walk, using my actions to make a point, a bold statement. (Inferno hops over the table and slips on his large black boots. He gets down and starts to tie the laces…) Inferno: I don’t think you understand just yet. Pariah, you seem to be blinded for your love of yourself. Yeah, I know damn well you are a LCW Legend. I know exactly why and I also know why you are a former champion. But you have to know why I was moved up to Mercury. I know you all think LTD was, maybe still is a load of Bull. No competition and so on, but it was Beaumont who wanted me up here to have the chance, so know that. Now I have the chance to become someone, and prove myself. Against you, yes; against a fellow wrestler. Not a champion, not a legend, a wrestler, a like. (Inferno comes up from his shoes and comes to the camera, hair in his eyes and holding his bandana) Inferno: I’m getting on your nerves, eh? Heh… is that a problem? Don’t you like it? Is it bothering poor littler Pariah? Tough, I can be very annoying out, and in the ring. I will cause you problems, headaches, new experiences. You are obviously not ready for me. You will get the unexpected and live the expected. (Pause.) Inferno: Indeed the truth does hurt… (Inferno sighs and ties his bandana on over his head, using it to hold back his long hair. He gets a bit closer to the camera, and shrugs…) Inferno: …Truth tells all, and the truth? Heh, the truth is that no matter how you see me, I will give you a run for your money. Maybe you will beat me, but boy, humiliation? Hah, stop dreaming, the problem with you is that you are up your ass too much to care! Its true, you knot it, we all know it. Come down from your partial dreamland, and live life how it is. Realise that I am more than nothing. Understand that I can accomplish more that just earning my place here on the roster. Take the cover away from your eyes and look a bit further inside of me than just my outline – you will find more. Abilities, a mind, body, a deep sole. Try it. I’m sure you’ll be surprised. (Pause. Wink) Inferno: Ready or not Pariah, I guess you could say, here I come! (Inferno zips up the bag, hops over a sock he left and leaves in a rush through the door, leaving it wide-open, scene fades.)
