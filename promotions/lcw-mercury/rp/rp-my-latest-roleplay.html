LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition My latest roleplay My latest roleplay Posted Wednesday, May 29, 2002 by Slate “No time to rest.” That’s what Geoffrey Slate said to himself as his plane landed at the Tulsa International Airport. His late-night flight from Las Vegas had been cancelled because a passenger on the plane had ragged shoes, “an obvious sign of potential terrorism.” At least, that’s what he mumbled when he asked the woman at the check-in desk when the next flight would be and she told him that he’d have to wait until the next day. That puts him in Tulsa just soon enough to pick up his luggage, drive home, make sure his apartment hadn’t burned down, and begin driving to Fusion. Luckily, also had time to schedule an LCW cameraman to meet him at a rest stop on the way, giving him at least a half-assed chance to address his opponent for this week. “Ever since the announcement was made that he is to wrestle for the Mercury Championship, and even beforehand when only the chance of it made itself present, Jin Akujin has remained quiet. More than likely off meditating to improve himself through an ancient Asian secret that I will ultimately not understand. “This would usually be where the current champion would say that butterflies have formed in the stomach of the relative rookie of an opponent, and that said opponent has been training instead of talking because he knows that unless he is at his absolute best, his skull will be caved in. However, I know better than to say that. He is a man that has made his mark early in his LCW career, earning the right to be recognized as being among the quickest and hardest workers this company and it’s incarnations have ever seen. Twice Jin has taken Harbinger to the limits of even his ability. I have more than enough sense to realize that he’s doing everything in his power to prepare himself to do the same to me. “I only have one warning for you, Mr. Akujin: prepare yourself for everything. You’ve risen to the level you are at because you’ve overwhelmed your competition with your outstanding technical ability and your uncanny agility. That’s worked well enough to bring you to the Mercury Championship, but it will not be enough to win it. “In order to take this title from me, you’ll need more than what you’ve shown to date. Can I go toe-to-toe with you in a technical exhibition? For a short while, possibly. Can I do it in an aerial assault? For a time, yes. But what separates us is that I can also hold my own in a slugfest, I can fend off the martial arts, and I can utilize so-called ‘power’ wrestling. You see Jin, the reason you are at the level you are, and I am at the level I am, is because I am a sort of jack-of-all-trades. I am able to wrestle in every style. “But, as much as physical ability matters, it is nothing without mental ability. “Jin, you may not know it right now but you’re about to take part in one of the most important matches you’ve ever wrestled to date. But you will. When the first bell rings, it will hit your mind like a semi-truck. It’s how you manage to deal with the realization that will determine if you’re able to succeed. “What is the realization that will hit you? It’s that you’ll know at that time that your entire career, the quest to be better than those you face, all comes down to that match. One match on one night will let you know if you’re ready to be considered one of the best, or just someone who happens to be good at what he does. What you think of yourself and what everyone else thinks of you comes down to just one night. If you win, you and everyone else will know that your time has come; that you’re one of the best at what you do. Lose, and all will know that you’re just not ready. “Train your body all you wish. But take my advice, the mental battle that night will be much, much harder. Deal with it however you want, you can be like Pariah and feed off the notion, or you can be like me and let it sweep over you. No matter how it’s done, you must find a way to deal with it or you have lost before the match ever starts. “But don’t be discouraged. You’re not the only one who has to make that battle. After all, the person who stands to lose the most in the match also has to deal with a similar notion. They have to fight the idea of knowing that despite all their training and devotion to becoming the best, there is someone better. “The title I hold tells me that I am the best. It tells all that see Mercury that I am the best. Come Jin, come prove that wrong. Come impress the world by beating the best.” Cut. Fade. That’s all folks. Where’d the bagels go?
