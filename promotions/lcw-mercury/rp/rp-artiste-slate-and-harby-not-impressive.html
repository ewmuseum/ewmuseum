LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Artiste, Slate, and Harby... not impressive... Artiste, Slate, and Harby... not impressive... Posted Tuesday, April 2, 2002 by MagnumWolf ***The scene opens with Magnum wolf. And that's it. His face dominates the screen. It has a slight worry to it, and a strange look of anger. He clears his throat...** ((Magnum Wolf)) You know, when I was becconed into this group of men, I figured that people would be forced to step up. Men who believed what they thought was right... would step up into the role of the instigator. Would be able to show me something. To prove to me that these shills of champions AREN'T shills. That this CRAP that has been going on in LCW Mercury IS the way it should be. But so far, my friends, no one has shown me anything. Geoffrey Slate gets beaten by The Gol for that North American Title. And what does Slate do to prove that it wasn't just a fluke? What does Slate do to make things interesting? He has a schizo moment, and then fades from the face of the earth. So, Dave Beaumont, who is the better of the two champions? Geoffrey Slate who shuts his trap the second he loses his beloved belt? Or is it Gol, who steps up to a challenge and takes away what means most to one man? If you say Slate, I'm afraid you have officially gone insane. And what of Memosarks? Another of the "CRUSADERS"... this bunch of men who were seeming to put together some sort of group... to vanquish us... where is he now? After a few semi-entertaining matches with Dirge, he's disappeared again as well. Where are your performers, Dave? Where is your big ticket? Your draws? They're non-existant. You lost them all to The Oblivion. Your tag team champions have scarcely seen each other for about three weeks now, let alone defend their titles. And Slate? He loses his title. Do you see it yet, Davey? Do you see the mistakes you have made in booking? Your champion was driven to his limits by Robert Young. ROBERT YOUNG... the man whom I manhandled from this end of Tucson to that end of Jersey City. I dropped him on his fat head for a quick 1,2,3. Where is your star power, David? I have a feeling that you can feel the grip tightening. It's only a matter of time before you allow Magnum Wolf to get his hand on Pariah's title. You can't deny me forever. ((Magnum Wolf)) How about Harbinger? Where's Golden Boy been lately? Well, no one's really sure, because we were all watching Boston Public during his match. You know, cause it's on nice and early in the night these days. L'Artiste? Is that maybe your savior? That guy's a joke. He's going to some party where he pays Steven Spielberg to look like he cares what he's saying. THAT'S the man who's going to save you and the LCW from what you see as a mere... invasion...? You just haven't gotten it yet, Dave. You haven't figured anything out. Even with all the clues we've given you. Nothing. You've lost your star power, and now your wits. The time is drawing near, LCW. The time is drawing very near. Magnum Wolf and the Trents... we have so much to gain from this fusion. And you people? You've got nothing to gain but a few black eyes and broken ribs. This is our promotion, our show, our roster... you all belong... to... us. ***The scene fades to black as Wolf's eyes close.***
