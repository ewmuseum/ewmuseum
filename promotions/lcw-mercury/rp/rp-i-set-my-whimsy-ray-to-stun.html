I set my whimsy ray to stun!

30 May 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition I set my whimsy ray to stun! I set my whimsy ray to stun! Posted Thursday, May 30, 2002 by Hollow "Nessun Dorma", as always, sets the house show crowd against whatever is about to happen. Ignacio meanders towards the ring, taking his time since he's not rushed by the constraints of the show. Taking keen interest in his detractors, Ignacio does his best not to lump them all together as 'white trash'. Sadly, his efforts are largely wasted; even in Mercury there's a large portion of fans who would find no irony in the phrase 'luxury trailer park'. After a leisurely step over the top rope, Ignacio goes into the act of cutting a promo, after waiting for a full thirty seconds of quiet from the crowd. "It strikes me as appropriate that the completed, final version of the Oblivion will not only debut, but also crush its opposition in the same night. Oh, some will scoff at our having the advantage of surprise, but that is trivial when ten men are involved in the match. The better side will win, and the better side is that of Oblivion. Shall we look at what constitutes both sides? "Gol is the best wrestler in the match. He proved such by defeating Pariah, Geoffrey Slate and Robert Young within the span of a month and a half. He negates Pariah. Then, we go to myself. While I lost to a clever ploy on the part of Dirge, I will be safe from fluke pins with my consorts behind me. I have physically dominated every man I have faced since returning, and Dirge shows no signs of learning how to stop that, so he is negated. Rush and Killjoy will fight to the last to make sure the Elites are a non-factor. Brimstone will find that the last member of team Oblivion knows quite well how to deal with him. In one fell swoop, I describe the long-awaited demise of EEI... but that is only the beginning." Ignacio's lips curl up, as he gleefully envisions a fantasy scenario. "As more and more of the EEI team falls, whatever fool is the last man standing will have to endure a cruel fate. Five men, all bonded to Sickness, picking him down to the bone, until nothing remains. When that man is pulped like so much produce, then we shall see EEI collapse. They have placed so much into loyalty and camaraderie to the individuals of the group that they will be unable to cope with a long-term loss. Even Glamour would make them heartbroken. And oh, wouldn't that be a pity." Ignacio barely manages to get off the last line with a mere hint of sarcasm, as opposed to an overpowering stench. Sickness has been working with him on his 'irony'. "Pariah, you have changed so little since our last fight. What a waste. You are so blinded by emotions that you are unable to see the flaws in yourself, and the flaws in your companions. Mark my words, EEI will be your downfall. "Picasso, I apologize for boredom during the mistress' vacation. I assure you that your own suffering will be quite occupying. "And Brimstone, dearest Brimstone, you continue to be thoroughly fascinating." Ignacio gives a wink and a nod to his Suicide foe. "Where most men would be busy planning for survival against great odds, you choose to seek out new enemies in order to justify your fears. Beaumont is conspiring against EEI? Why, that is positively Slatian* of you. The truth is, he wants to know the fifth member of the group sooner rather than later, and he's counting on the five of you to minimize the damage. Unfortunately for Evil Enterprises Incorporated, the match will lead to destruction. However, it is not due to ill-will from Beaumont, but rather, incompetence. The same incompetence that lead him to challenge Sickness for a match. Keep that in mind while you or one of your friends becomes a martyr on a globally televised program." Fade to black. ------------------- *Slatian = Slate-ian = reference to Geoffrey Slate
