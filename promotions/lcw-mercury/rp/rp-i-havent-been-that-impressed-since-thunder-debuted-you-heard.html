I haven't been that impressed since Thunder debuted. You heard me. I haven't been that impressed since Thunder debuted. You heard me. [ LCW Mercury Roleplay Board ] Posted by Owner Dave on December 04, 2001 at 19:47:17: Dave is at his desk. In front of the desk, and to his right (our left), is Harbinger. Dave: Nice little speeches you gave to open and close the shows there, Ed. First off, let me say that if you don't know who your partner is before you start again, you're a complete and utter moron. Hell, it might be good ol' Eric Russo for all you know, jackass! Harby chuckles at the name Beaumont chose to drop. He hasn't heard it in a while. Dave: You see this man, Ed? He doesn't take kindly to you saying that your roster is better 'where it counts', and that what you're doing will blow away what we're doing. Static? That's your big shocker? Hell, I've got talent lined up myself, but I'm not about to go blowing it on the first show. Dave: News flash, Olmstead. We're the best game in town. We've got a mix of the new blood in EEI, Dominator, and Rush; returns in Blaze and Magnum Wolf; and talented, dedicated vets from Saxton to Gravity to this man right here. The best wrestler of all time. Harbinger grins and pats the belt. Dave: MY boys have been working their asses off for the last month while you're busy figuring out how to work an editing device, or whatever the f**k excuse you had. MY boys have been creating a product that's entertaining from any standpoint, and their workrate is right up there with any incarnation of LCW. MY boys can beat the pants off of you and the same-old-same-old crew. Rook beats Pheonyx? Lost going through yet another repackaging? And, last but not least, Laww versus Static round 97. Harbinger: Mind if I step in here? Dave: Go ahead. Zoom in on Harby. Harbinger: You take time on your show to talk smack at us over here in Mercury, who have actually gone so far as to put on shows with more than three matches, AND a pay-per-view whose quality you'll never touch. Dave here spends show time promoting us, rather than his own political agenda. Talent? I've got more talent than all the barely working slouches in your main event. I can't even REMEMBER the last time ol' Rook cut a promo about wrestling. When one of your boys can pull a match out of Pariah like I did at Rising, that doesn't involve lots of random heavy objects and vehicles, let me know. Dave: Thanks. So, to sum up. We rule, you suck. I ain't scared of sh*t, Herb here isn't scared, Memosarks isn't scared, Deek's scared but then he's scared of everything, and last but not least, I'm not scared. Harbinger shoots him an odd look. Dave: It was worth repeating. Harbinger shrugs it off and looks back at the camera. Dave: Fusion isn't on time every week, but at least it's there. This week's show alone will make yours seem like the shoddy, not-worth-the-wait production that it is. Because we're Mercury, and you're not. Harbinger: Word. Fin
