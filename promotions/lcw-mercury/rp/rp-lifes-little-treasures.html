Life's Little Treasures

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Life's Little Treasures Life's Little Treasures Posted Friday, May 31, 2002 by Subdued (It’s a beautiful spring afternoon and Subdued has decided to take in the great outdoors. A swing set and slide can be seen beyond him as he sits at a park bench, his arms sprawled out across the backrest. Subdued is wearing an 'Iron Maiden - Best of the Beast' tee with his usual jeans and unkempt hair. His shoes are sitting to the right of his feet with his socks balled up inside. The full body shot is gradually zoomed to his upper torso as he starts to speak.) Subdued: Ahhhh, the simple pleasures in life. Just sitting outside, relaxing, running your feet through the cool green grass of spring. (He inhales deeply the odors of his surrounding, and a sigh is let out afterward.) It’s too bad a guy couldn’t lie around like this all the time. Lucky for me, I guess, that I am fortunate enough to partake in another of life’s pleasures. Yeah, that’s right; I think wrestling is one of life’s pleasures. Why? Because I love to do it. It’s just like I love to play baseball or sit in a park like this. I take more of a beating in this felicity than any other, but that’s just part of the game. All you can do is learn from it. Now I’d rather not take too much of a thrashing, but sometimes it happens. Maybe it will at my first Fusion. I don’t know. Harbinger, you seem to think this fate will befall me, and you seem to be a pretty intelligent guy, so maybe it will come to pass. You’re also probably correct in thinking that I came over as showing no humbleness in my first words to the LCW world. It’s not that I tried to show any disrespect, it’s just that I’m confident in my abilities. You have to be if you want to succeed. You should know that. What would be the point of showing up if you thought or believed you didn’t have a chance? I thought I had a chance when I faced a former LCW star in Barabbas, and I went out and beat him. That should say something because according to LCW’s own John Hughes, Barabbas is THE greatest wrestler to ever set foot inside the squared-circle. (He raises his eyebrows and shrugs his shoulders as if to say, “He said it, not me.” Subdued then stands up and starts to slowly pace back and forth in front of the bench.) Subdued: Don’t get me wrong, I have all the respect in the world for you, and what LCW brings to the table. That’s why I came here, to challenge myself, to face the best wrestlers in the business. You should keep in mind that I was accepted into LCW. They don’t let just anyone walk in here. But hey, if you want to underestimate me, go right ahead. Just don’t be too surprised if you’re lying face down on the mat being Silenced. Who knows, maybe it won’t end up being a match of submissions, but chances are one of us is going to tap. Hopefully my flexibility will help buy me some time, or even help me escape from some moves or something on Saturday. I know I’ll take all the help I can get. I’m no fool. Every advantage one has should try to be exploited as much as possible. Maybe that PTC title match is leaving you near empty. It wouldn’t bother me, but I won’t depend on it either. (He quits pacing and turns to face the camera.) Subdued: Harbinger, I’m planning on making a name for myself right away. I want my first LCW match to be a memorable one. If I win it will obviously be memorable. If I lose, I want people to go away saying, “Wow, that was one helluva match. That Subdued is tough, he’s going to be a star.” Either way, everyone in the back will know I’m no pushover. So, Harby, think this match is going to be a squash all you want, but be prepared to see a young star taking off. Like you said, on June 1st, 2002, I will be making my LCW debut. So I say you and everyone else should go ahead and mark that date on your calendars right now because you will not want to forget the day Subdued came into LCW and started his amazing rise to the top. (Subdued turns around and goes back to the bench to sit down. He resumes his relaxed position and gazes at the surrounding environment as the scene fades out.)
