Blow Me!

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Blow Me! Blow Me! Posted Wednesday, May 22, 2002 by Pariah Pariah sits in his garage a lot. He sits there, listens to music, and drinks the occasional brewski. Right now, Dream Theater's greatest work ever, in the mind of Keith Summers, "Images and Words" is playing. Keith, sitting in a lawn chair, throws a can to his side before turning down the music, and addressing the camera. Pariah: You run your mouth, you talk about nothing, and yet you still wonder how I'm going to walk all over you? He laughs, loud. Pariah: Inferno, make all the asinine comments you want, but in the end, our match takes place in the ring, a place you can't even touch me in terms of skill. And guess what, fire man? You keep talking, you keep running your mouth, and you keep getting on my nerves, I'm going to drop you on your head like your mother probably did when you were a baby. Pariah: Inferno, you have to prove something. You have to prove to me that you are not the joke I take you to be, and you have to do it one on one, with me. Pariah: Their are certain things I do in life better than almost anybody. At Fusion, I will prove to you and everyone else that I simply, outclass you in the ring. Call me egotistical, call me bull-headed, but the truth hurts, Inferno. I'm going to not only beat you, I'm going to humilate you, just to prove to you what I'm all about. Pariah: I'm considered one of the greatest in LCW history for a reason. And until you reach the same level I have, until you step up and prove to me, and everyone else, that you aren't some LTD punk that made it into the big time on some fluke shot, you will never be able to beat me, and you will never be able to make your stake at fame. Pariah: You've got talent, no doubt about it. But you don't know how to access it, you don't even know where to begin tapping into it. It's not just there, you have to work at getting it out of you. Pariah: At Fusion, I'm going to help you, Inferno. I'm going to beat the talent out of you, and I'm going to start at the head. Head drops--who doesn't like them? Pariah grabs a beer before turning back up his music, and chilling for the remainder of the day.
