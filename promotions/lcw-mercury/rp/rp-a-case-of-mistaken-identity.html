A case of mistaken identity

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition A case of mistaken identity A case of mistaken identity Posted Thursday, May 23, 2002 by Picasso The Artiste stands by a baggage claim conveyor belt at the LA International Airport. He has a sullen look on his face, but there is a spark in his eyes. "I think you've got things wrong Rush. I know you haven't changed. You're still the same deluded fool you've always been. You're still seeing EEI as this force that needs to be destroyed, even though you've proven time and time again that you just don't have it in you to do so. Your new friends won't help you. Hell, Oblivion is dismantling themselves faster than we ever could. They won't be around much longer." "And you are absolutely wrong about me. You don't know a damn thing about me or where I came from, so you have no right to make up stories about my past. I am a self-made man. It's true that my psychological state of mind has been made by...outside forces. But the wrestling ability, the desire, the success, that's been all my doing. And that's what this match is really all about, isn't it? Wrestling ability against wrestling ability, and we call ourselves The Elite for a very good reason. We are the best LCW has to offer." "The one thing that hasn't changed, is that EEI is composed of a group of very bad men. We may get the cheers now, but we're still as dangerous as we ever were. You and Killjoy are going to learn that the hard way at Fusion. And who knows, Rush? By the time I get done with you, some of those old familiar boos may come back," he says, smiling for the first time in this promo. "I might inflict some violence that this crowd has never seen before. It hasn't been a good week, and you're a convenient target." Picasso grabs his bag from the conveyor and turns to leave, when a couple of young children stops him, pens and paper in hand. Picasso obliges him by signing his autograph for each of them. "Good luck against Oblivion, Mr. Picasso," one says. "I hope you beat up Rush. You guys are our heroes!" says the other one. Picasso look at each one of them before handing the autographs back. "You all should get a new set of heroes, then." Then he departs from the airport, where his limo awaits.
