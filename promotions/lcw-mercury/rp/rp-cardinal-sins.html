LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Cardinal sins Cardinal sins Posted Tuesday, May 28, 2002 by Picasso Vincent Picasso sits in his usual spot in his favorite pub, O'Mallys. He has a few empty bottles in front of him, and it looks like there will be a few more by the end of the evening. "Do you know what your greatest fault was, Oblivionites?" he asks, laughing to himself. "It wasn't your violence, or your attitude. Hell, you came in prepared to kick ass. I almost admire that. Me and my friends, we've done that very same thing a time or two." "No, your sin was that you got boring. Quickly. Oooh, we have a lot of people on our side, we're talking over. Oooh, we couldn't win titles on our own, so we'll take it by force. Christ, show me a stable that HASN'T done that same thing. And you couldn't even back up your words. All your boasting and bragging, and what did you get? One title reign that lasted about a month. Your hired guns of a tag team didn't get the job done. Neither did your replacements. Your superstar got kicked out last week." "What the hell are you left with? A former champion and a host of unknowns. That's what the mighty Oblivion has come to?" The Artiste snorts, accidentally knocking over a glass of beer. He doesn't notice. "You're going to be defeated, just like every other half-assed stable that couldn't back up their words. You're defeat's going to be even more ignomin--, ignomi--, pathetic, because you talked so much and got so little in return." Picasso reaches for his glass, only to see it spilled. "What son of a bitch did that?" he shouts out, before the bartender quickly gives him a full glass. He holds the glass up to the camera. "Eat, drink and be merry, kiddies. For at Fusion, you're time is up." (fade out)
