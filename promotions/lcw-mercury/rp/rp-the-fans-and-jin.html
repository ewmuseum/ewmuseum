LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition The Fans and Jin The Fans and Jin Posted Wednesday, May 22, 2002 by Robert Young Voice: They cheer him, oh yes they cheer him. Darkness turns to light the old gym is dusty and unkept. In the middle of the gym a small wrestling ring appears. Inside Robert Young sits cross-legged. He is dressed in his wrestling attire. Young: Jin, last Fusion you got in the ring and rebelled in the fans sudden liking of you, how the fans have gotten behind you, how that annoyed Herb. So the fans have gotten behind the little guy? You think this is something to be happy with. Well Jin I have been there and done that… Young pauses thoughtfully for a while. Young: Mate, I have been to the top of that mountain and for all the fame, for all the admiration, for all the love it is not worthwhile. In the end it does not make a slight difference, in the end I learnt there is one man to rely on, your own self. Being the most popular man in Australia, touring the arenas, bars, and fighting dens. Being the man who kicked ass, being the man who represented the Aussie Battler, who overcame all obstacles, yes I felt there love, there admiration, there cheers. Being in Japan fighting in the sickest most barbaric matches ever I received there cheers, when I came to this country and main evented they loved me. When I won the MMWF World Title off Lex Laww otherwise known as Duel I had the fans behind me. I know only too well what the fans give, they give you a nice fuzzy feeling inside, yes you’re a happy chappy right now mate, but come Fusion it does not help you one bit. After my fall from grace did the fans help me? No they did not. Jin they support you, they love you but in the end they don’t help you. Maybe you might feel you owe the fans? Maybe that motivates you but it does not help you. All the fans in the world didn’t save me on my last LCW run. They won’t save you against me. Young stands up in the ring. Young: You live and die in this ring and no fan can stop the process, every moment I am in this ring only one man controls my destiny myself, the moment I allow another man to control my destiny I lose. If I allow you to control me I lose. You are a great fighter, you can control matches, but as I said last time I plan on not letting you get control, I plan on domination so you don’t have a chance against me. I will control this match. Not you and not the fans. The minute I dumped the fans, the minute I stopped caring I recovered, I got out of that crap farm league we called home. When you didn’t care you got promoted to Mercury too. Now the fans care about you where are you? Jobbing mate. I only am concerned by one-man and that is myself. When I only care about myself I win matches. While you attempt to get the fans to side with you I will kick your ass by any means I deem necessary because I only care about the result. Shaking his head Young continues. Young: Last promo I talked a lot about you and your weaknesses. I shared my thoughts but just be wary Jin. I do respect your abilities in the ring but you don’t have that edge, that edge you need to come out on top. I have found that killer instinct, that vital ingredient. Against you it will be enough to prove your downfall. Young signals to cut the promo. Darkness reigns. Voice: Fans who needs them?
