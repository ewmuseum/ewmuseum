Killing Time

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Killing Time Killing Time Posted Thursday, May 30, 2002 by Glamour Johnny is sitting in the darkened ring at the Dirge dome. Silently flipping cards into a top hat. A few bounce off of the brim and fall to the mat, others meet with more success. Ace, Queen, King, all fly into the hat but Johnny stops when he pulls up a joker. He runs his index finger along the edge of the card thoughtfully before speaking… You know, tossing cards into this hat is stupid, just me killing time. I do consider it a bit analogous to our upcoming stable war against Oblivion. Think about it: each card has a roughly equal chance of making it into the hat-these chances are modified by factors such as airflow, trajectory, velocity, etc. By studying patterns and improving muscle memory, you can increase your chances of success, but always there is the elusive factor, that element you didn’t count on that interferes at the last moment to rob you of that perfect shot. That element is me. You see, I know what everybody thinks, especially Hollow, it appears that I rank slightly higher than your average garden slug in his estimation. That’s fine by me Ignacio—and since you haven’t quite grasped sarcasm yet allow me to try sincerity: Go take a blind fuck at a rolling doughnut. But keep working on that irony with Sickness, I feel a man of your unique intellect can grasp that concept, maybe then you’ll understand why people are calling you “champ” around the office. Do you seriously think that your latest incarnation of Oblivion is any different than the rest of your other “less-than-perfect” Oblivion revisions? I swear you people are worse than Microsoft. Your initial release was, in essence, a sub-standard and essentially flawed creation, doomed to reside in the garbage cans of history. So, you do what? In an effort to break your long losing streak you fire the Trents and bring in Rush and Killjoy. Not much of an improvement there. Thus far we’ve seen them throw their return match because we were handing them their asses on silver platters. Scared boys? You should be. I didn’t even try to pull off a move on you, I just beat you out of the ring with my fists…no finesse, just brute force. Next time you two won’t get so lucky. And the latest revision: Sickness…everything old is new again. Hmmm, your mysterious fifth? Immaterial. Your little heel Pez dispenser is rapidly running out of villains for us to slap. Johnny looks at the card again, and starts rolling it through his knuckles The joker…generally considered the most worthless card in the deck, but also the most common wild card. Which am I? I guess time will tell. I know I’m not as Flamboyant as Vincent, nor as outrageous as Dirge, I lack Brimstone’s sheer power…but I do have endurance, technical depth and most importantly, the desire for EEI to emerge victorious. You see this has never been “just about me”, I’ve never really been a show-boat or a glory hound like so many others out there. For me it has always been about the brotherhood, about EEI. Gol may say he “chooses us” like some high-school kickball brawl, and Oblivion has attempted time and time again to destroy us. As ever they have failed. If we lose this next match it will not destroy us. Nor will it cause any one of us to fall into Wagnerian histrionics ala Mikel Trent. If anything it will fill us with a greater resolve, we will come back stronger than before. But keep thinking I’m just the joker, keep discounting me, scoffing at me, and running off at the mouth. At fusion you all will see what we can do…because THAT’s what this is about. Johnny turns and flips the card over his shoulder as he leaves the ring. It bounces off of the brim, teeters, and falls into the hat.
