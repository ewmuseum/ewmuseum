Oh yeah one more time at least

3 April 2002

LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Oh yeah one more time at least Oh yeah one more time at least Posted Wednesday, April 3, 2002 by Robert Young Robert Young is shaving. His razor slides across his skin as the Mercury Camera watches from behind, seeing his reflection in the mirror. Young slowly continues. Only when he finishes after a few more minutes does he turn, shirtless. Young: Mate, ain’t I a damn sex symbol. But as Blaze would say let’s get straight to business. Last promo I discussed McCoy’s little reality check I suppose you would say. Blaze thanks to my earlier remarks had suddenly realized he… well he sucked quite frankly. He was mouthing off how he was a changed man. Last Promo I discussed how this was a great discovery on his part, but would not contribute to his victory. It is all well to say I have changed, I know what I have done wrong, it’s just “words, word, words” as Shakespeare said. You need time to change, yes you may start on this path against me at Fusion but you are not far up the road. You have to travel down that road for quite a way before you make progress. You claim is that you are the most well rounded guy in Mercury, yet you have failed to use this skill. I pose a simply question for you. Why will you suddenly be able to find this arsenal? The simple answer is you can’t click your fingers and expect yourself to return to your old glory, not that it was that glorious. I will get to that later. I had to change over a long time and your no magician, you’re going to have to work hard. In this business it isn’t pure luck. I won’t and will never believe you can suddenly become Bret Hart or Rook Black over night. Blaze you have failed to answer how you can expect to gain this victory against me, a new attitude won’t change that win loss record in the near future, a month down the track the results of this new attitude may begin to show. But not now mate. Young slips out of the bathroom and puts on a shirt. Young: Oh yes you have some more claims, you have never wrestled me and you have the superior all round wrestling skill. Yeah you have never wrestled me, so bloody what. I am not some kid off the street. I am a guy who built the groundwork for the older guys in this fed. You owe me, Gol owes me, Harbinger owes me even Pariah and Magnum owe me. Those EVO superstars owe me too. Without me they would have ever been seen and never been heard. They would still be wrestling in a medium size fed or back in Japan. I am straying. Blaze put simply I don’t care about not fighting you one on one. You got a taste of you in the tag match. I got a taste of victory over you in that match and I plan on doing so again. I spent the last few months in LTD against new wrestlers I have never seen before. Did that mean I treated them differently hell no. I kicked their ass just like a Used or a Lex Laww. You want me to prove myself against you. I feel I have nothing to prove apart from I will beat you one on one. That is what will happen. The only thing you have to prove is you can beat me one on one. On your recent form verse mine I don’t rate your chances highly at all. But that’s right you are an awesome all round athlete. You can be old school, new school, hardcore, softcore. Do I give a damn? I have fought Big Pee On to Rook Black. Nothing surprises me. I have fought La Mosca to Apex. Do I care what style you wrestle, not really. I’ll stick to my game plan, my superior wrestling skills and my obviously superior brawling skills. Young is getting rather intense. Young: It doesn’t matter about your past achievements, if it did then I would win being an ex World Champion. But as I said it doesn’t matter because as I have already said you stink at this moment. The problem is you can talk about turning it around, and you can go about turning around the slump mate but it is going to take time for you to do so. You don’t have enough time before Fusion. What more is despite that running mouth of yours I am far from impressed about your past achievements in LCW and even Mike’s old breakaway. In LCW you were a multiple Hardcore Champion. That impresses me as much as a Multiple Champion of the Year 4 Arm Wrestle. Yes I am the King of the Bar Room Brawl, or so I have been called. But the Hardcore title always was a joke until Lex Laww grabbed it before the split. Anyway being Hardcore doesn’t impress me, being the King of the Bar Room Brawl I’ve seen every hardcore freak in Australia, Japan and North America, every single man went down to me apart from Gol. If you think a hardcore regime sends me trembling then you better wake up son. Hardcore Wrestling is a normal office day for me even against Mercury talent. Robert has entered a sitting area and sat down by now. He is drinking a milkshake. Young: I see Blaze picked up on more new moves, too bad I didn’t tell them what they were exactly. Blaze has come out here with the shopping list of his current move arsenal that he hasn’t used since he has sucked so hard he has lost every single match so easily. When you lose you don’t get to use your good moves do you now? Now every move he named are old school Blaze moves. While I may be a distant memory to him I have watched Wrestling over the past two years and trust me LCW has quite a few videos in the stall right now. I’ve researched your moves, hell I could pull a few of them on you. Ask Pariah about Robert Young giving him a taste of his own move. While he doesn’t know what is about to come I have thoroughly acquainted myself with Blaze. I know what to expect. His lightening OutKast isn’t that hard to miss. It’s all in the set up and when in the match he will use it. Furthermore he is using a Paxton move. Let me tell the Mercury fans about Dr Paxton, every week for two months in LTD his ‘boyz’ tried to take me out. Including Blaze’s friend Inferno Kid. Did they succeed hell no I kicked all their asses. Now if your suggesting copying Paxton will scare me your far from it. I suggest just like Paxton your going to end up on the losing end of this bargain. Now you can copy Paxton’s, Schmuu’s and Hype’s moves. Their all lackeys and deserve to be headlining in a Memphis Backwater town vs Bill Dundee. Now maybe I should copy Magman, Clinton and the Sex Machine. How ridiculous. Young sighs. Young: I know everything you have got Blaze and that really gives me an advantage. But the thing is you don’t know what I have got. You said you researched my Bio. The one that says I am Bob Yobbo Junior entering LTD ready to kick ass and drink beer. Hey you may notice I don ‘t exactly wear that Akubra hat and crazy a six-pack on me. That was an old gimmick. Now If I was basing my knowledge of you on your conspiracy theory and Business metaphors I would be in serious trouble against you. I base it on your in ring ability. I hope you have done some research or have some knowledge of me. If you best bit of into is in my LTD bio then your in serious trouble. Yeah you saw my ten moves on that list. Do you think I’m restricted to ten moves? Are you that stupid? I am a veteran and I will do whatever it takes to win. Did my match against Pariah have any of that move list? I think not, I am much better well rounded than that list. You’re basing your view of me on old information, irrelevant crap. Blaze what I am now is what you should worry about, a rejuvenated and meaner Robert Young, a man who nearly took the Mercury Belt off Pariah, a man that took Magnum to the edge, a man who defeated you in a tag match. Of course I can’t blame you for overlooking this fact. I do notice your double booked with Paxton on Sunday. You can’t even be focused on me, yet another mistake. You can’t afford all these errors Blaze. You’re the same old Blaze on a losing streak. Meanwhile you’re trying to get back to the old modestly successful Business like McCoy. You have everything going against you and hell with that much on my plate I wouldn’t be in Mercury I’d be in LTD or in my hometown sparring trying to rediscover myself. Hell that’s what I did. But not you, you continue on your losing ways here and your facing a good thing, meanwhile you have another match in close proximity with Paxton. It’s an unfocused Blaze who is a shadow of his former self against me, someone refocused and performing can I say quite well. Young is smiling quite happily and smugly. Young: Hey Blaze best of luck on this path to rediscovering how to close the deal. Good luck on returning to the original business demeanor. But this match has nothing to do with what you will become. It is what you are at this moment. At the moment your not impressive, at the moment you are not ready to defeat me. I look forward to beating you at Fusion. As the scene fades Young is smiling, not the happy Yobbo smile but something a little more twisted and wicked.
