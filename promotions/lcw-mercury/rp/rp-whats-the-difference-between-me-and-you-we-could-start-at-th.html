LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition What's The Difference Between Me And You? We could start at the peni...*ahem*..... What's The Difference Between Me And You? We could start at the peni...*ahem*..... Posted Thursday, May 23, 2002 by Pariah You remember the "Trophy Room"? We're back there again, Pariah sitting in a pretty dull wooden chair, clad in a pair of green gym shorts and a white Adidas tee. He looks at the walls of the room, buried deeply in trophys and titles from around the world. Pariah: So I'm "Just another wrestler"? My friend, the differences between me and you are numerous. Almost too many to talk about in the time alloted to me. But, I can tell you one major, glaring flaw. Pariah: I'm a better wrestler than you. Pariah sits in silence for a moment before nodding his head. Pariah: Pretty simple, huh? You know why it's that simple? Because in every area of ring skill, every little facet of fighting, I out match you, and to put it quite simply...I humiliate you. Now, I've never stepped foot in the ring with you, but I've seen your work. I've seen what you do. Granted, it isn't much, but I've seen it. Pariah: But it's not that simple. No, you see, I've got something you lack, pure skill and talent. I've been gifted with a skill that only a select few acquire, weather it be through hard work, or you just simply have it. Pariah: People who are deserving, people who bust ass and attempt to become better have that skill. You lack it, because you refuse to evolve. You've been doing the same crap since you got here, Inferno. You don't add any great skills, or take away defects that harm your performance. You refuse to let yourself evolve and rise to the occasion by doing that, and will never be able to say that you can beat me in that ring. Pariah: If you want to admit it or not, that's not up to me. Keep living in your fantasy world, keep talking trash that you can't back up. But at Fusion, where I prove to you that I am the better athlete, that I am the better wrestler, you're going to be humiliated, and reduced to a shell of your former self. You retired Blaze McCoy...so did Dave Beaumont. You came from LTD, where you had to "work for your spot". I wrestled under the "old-guard" of LCW, and had to give a mean blowjob just to make it on TV. Pariah: The facts are in front of you, fire boy. At Fusion, I show the world that you most definately don't have it . And on that note, Pariah stands up to look at the Mercury World Heavyweight Championship replica that was given to him. He stares, and stares as we fade out...
