LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Another Chance.. Another Chance.. Posted Wednesday, June 5, 2002 by Jin Akujin It was just after Fusion that Jin Akujin was in his locker room getting done changing from his wrestling attire. His hopes at the Mercury title and to establish himself had been dashed away by Slate's variation of his Penance manuver, but hope was still there because he had another shot to get the title, and something else. He had a shot to get back Herb after what he did to him after their match at Suicide, it was time to finally settle the score that had nestled itself in Jin's brain. "You've been getting lucky lately.." Jin: Yes, I know that I've been getting a few title shots when others deserve them more than I do. I almost had Slate, the win was just mere inches from my fingers, but unfortunatly I slipped up. He was holding back in the match, he wasn't hungry for the win like I was, he didn't have the intensity of his past matches. I am thinking that once he won the title that he felt that he had nothing more to prove to anyone except maybe himself, to prove that he can defend that he worked so hard to earn. I can't help but wonder how good he is going to do against both Harbinger and myself at the same time. Slate did what he had to do to make sure he retained his title, I was giving him quite a run for his money as he was giving it to me. The only real energy that I had was going for my big moves early while he had energy to continually kick out of each and every pin attempt that I put him through. Just two factors was missing from the match, I wasn't 100 percent and Slate wasn't really trying as hard as he would have wanted to. Maybe at this Climax card..We can change that. "There's one more person added to the equation.." Jin: Habra, I still have to finish our old score. I finally found the reason as to why he beat me at Suicide, he didn't let his emotions get in the way of his decisions and I did. I should have known better to not make unbalanced decisions on emotions alone, it was a rookie mistake on my part to actually do something so idiotic. Not this time however, this time I will not let my emotions get the better of me when facing someone like Herbert Ingles. This time..This time I will not make rookie mistakes. I figured out Harbinger's simple yet very effective strategy to winning a match, first he will use small moves to wear the opponent down, then he will work over the opponent's neck, and finally he will use one of his finishers such as his Dreadlock or Punk Out variations that I have become used to taking already. My strategy however is to counter all of Habra's, which has already proven to be successful in our past two encounters. I know him better than most other wrestlers from just two encounters like he knows me, Harbinger cannot possibly think of walking away with an easy victory. Jin stood up and checked himself out in a mirror, finding everything to his liking, the rookie sensation left the locker room while heading down the hall..Well...He would have until he saw all the girls on both sides of the hallway. This in turn made Jin turn on his heels and go back into his locker room before he could get mobbed by a bunch of female fans. Jin: This is just great, now that I'm so popular with the females that I have to avoid getting mobbed by them. This is going to have to take some speed and thinking on my part, I just hope none of them are good runners. "Why can't you just go out there like a man and get mobbed?" Jin: Well..That is just stupid since they could put more damage on my neck by running all over me. I don't really want to take more punishment in my neck area until this Climax Card is happening, I'll be ready then. Training is going to also require a lot of my physical and mental willpower just to get me through, this isn't some unimportant match, this is an oppertunity to prove my worth to everyone here. To show that I'm not another rookie that thinks he is hot stuff, I want to prove my prowless in the ring against two of the best in the whole wide world. Young obviously thought that I was arrogant when I went against him, I guess he thinks that because he beat Harbinger and myself that he deserves respect. Wow, it's a real accomplishment to beat two men that Young feared of their potential because he knew that he would have been blown away by it. It must also be an accomplishment to beat a man that was in a deep depression that I had to pull out of by myself, and to beat me, someone that isn't even 100 percent physically. I hope EEI tears those Oblivion punks apart because they need to have those egos shot down. "I'm sure, so how are you going to get out of this?" Jin, instead of saying something, ran straight out the door and down the hallway before any of those female fans had even noticed, but they slowly looked at each other and screamed before running after poor Jin who was already outside. Damn those feet of his were fast; fast enough to get him out of there before a female mob probably would have stripped him of every article that he had. Jin: Just like that. "Whoa, that was fast." Jin: Yes, I'll need to be that fast when dealing with Slate and Harbinger, that's one of my advantages over them. I need to keep on myu toes before Slate can catch me, and I need to be fast when going head to head with Harbinger in the trading moves catergory.. (End)
