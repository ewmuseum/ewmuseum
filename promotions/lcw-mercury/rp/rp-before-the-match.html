LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Before the match Before the match Posted Thursday, May 23, 2002 by rush (Rush sits in the locker room waiting for the start of Mercury. His ring attire sits folded next to him. He is still dressed in a nice expensive suit, his tie loosened and his hair hanging down from where it was tied. He stands and stretches before he begins to speak.) “It is almost time. Picasso, Glamour…the two of you think that because you have been able to bully and intimidate your way to the top that you actually have something that resembles talent? That you can actually beat two athletes with the calibre of Killjoy and myself. Well I think it is time that the two of you wake up and look at things the way they truly are. You are two second rate bullies, who try to make up for you lack of success in life by winning matches the only way you possible can cheat and luck. The two of you along with the rest of the EEI are tricksters, you have fooled a lot of mindless drones in the audience into thinking that you’re “cool” that your twisted views are a “modern way of thinking”. Well the time has come for your lies to be revealed for what they truly are nothing but crap! Just like the members who make up EEI. Now you may have had hard lives…. well boo hoo for you. Life is hard and no matter what has been done to you it is no excuse. The truth is that you are all nothing but pathetic cowards who no nothing about real honour or decency. But soon I will not be the only person who sees this. After Killjoy and I win the tag titles the whole world will know just how talent less the Elites and the rest of the EEI are.” (Rush stands and now removes his tie. He begins o unbutton his shirt and removes his gold watch.) “Only a matter of time now. My return match to LCW Mercury, and the beginning of the end of the EEI.” (Rush begins to laugh to himself) “It’s so funny really. You are all nothing but second rate villains from bad B movies. But come Fusion the final credits will role on the Elites.” (As Rush starts preparing for his match the camera fades to black.)
