Slate does a rant

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Mercury - Continuing the Classic LCW Tradition Slate does a rant Slate does a rant Posted Saturday, April 6, 2002 by Slate There come times in everyone’s life where important decisions must be made. There come times when you must make a decision between doing what is helpful, and what is right. Until last night, Geoffrey Slate was firm in the knowledge that, when a decision like that came along, he would do what was moral. He would do what was “right.” Now, that is no more. Slate’s plane landed quite a while ago. He should be home by now, but he’s not. He knows good and well that if he drove home, every bridge he drove over would provide just a little too much temptation. Instead, he drove a few miles out of town. Now, he sits on the hood of his car staring into a river. Usually, the sound of flowing water soothes Geoffrey, but not tonight. Tonight the anger is too much. The small light from the camera shinning on him shows that his eyes are bloodshot, the veins in his temples pulse and every muscle in his body seem stiff. Despite his visible anger, his speech is slow, his voice eerily calm. “Have you ever been so sure of something, you’d place your life on it being true? So sure, that you could literally feel it inside of you? “That’s how I felt last night. I knew that Oblivion was going to try to screw me out of my shot. I knew Harbinger had something to do with it. I knew that as long as I was the last person on my team left, I’d have a chance at getting through the match. I knew that I’d be able to make it to Suicide. “Last night, I knew that I had it all figured out. “Do you know what it’s like to be so sure of everything, then have it all taken away? Do you understand the hole it puts in you? Can you understand it when I say that in one night, I’ve been pushed towards near insanity? “Oblivion, you are scum. You formed because you believe that you can fix all that is wrong with Mercury. Bulls***! You’re right to say that you aren’t what’s wrong with wrestling, because you’re more than that. You’re what’s wrong with the world. Immoral pieces of s*** willing to take advantage of anything you can to get ahead, no matter how low or despicable. No dignity, no pride, no respect, at your heart is what is at the heart of every petty thief, every hardened criminal, every terrorist this world has ever seen. You’re willing to do whatever you can to help yourself, no matter what the cost is to anyone else. “I will be the end of you. I hate you, not only because of what you are, but also because of what you’re going to force me to become. Because of you, I can’t trust anyone since they may be working with you. Because of you I can’t do what I think is right, because it might be part of your plan. And because I can’t do that, you’ve made it so I can’t respect myself. You’ve finally brought me down to your level, because you’ve made me realize that it’s where I need to be to beat all of you. “Gol, you’re a piece of s***. You just can’t accept the fact that I am better than you are. You couldn’t stand the fact that I had the belt that you claim to have made famous. So you decided to take it from me. But you knew that you couldn’t do it on your own, didn’t you? The legendary Gol couldn’t beat the lowly Geoffrey Slate on his own. You had to cheat to get it done. “Fine, you beat me. I guess you proved how much more superior Oblivion members are than any Mercury member. But wait, what happened the first time that you defend the belt? You lost it to the man you were supposed to destroy. And you can’t claim that he cheated to beat you, because when I came down to the ring, I never laid a hand on you. “Like I said, Gol, you’re not what you used to be. You’re a has-been, a pathetic looser trying to grasp on to what used to be. I won’t deny that you’re doing a good job of covering up your inadequacies as a wrestler. But sooner or later, I’m going to get you in the ring without Oblivion around to protect you, and that will be the day that I expose you for who you really are. “Magnum Wolf, you’re even more pathetic than your little buddy. Why? Because unlike him, you shouldn’t need the help of Oblivion. You should be able to get things done on your own. The key word is ‘should.’ You should be able to compete with the best in our company, but you can’t. You can’t, because you’re scared that maybe you might not be good enough, or maybe you just have a phobia about loosing. Either way, you take matches against people you know you can beat. The only way you can get a win over someone who can actually compete with you is by manipulating them into giving you a win. The only way you can manage a title shot is by stealing it from those who deserve it. “Trents, I don’t have much to say about you. You’re losers. I hate Gol and Wolf because they can stand on their own, but choose not to. You, on the other hand, simply can’t do anything. You compete in the tag division because you’re not able to walk into the ring by yourselves and accomplish anything. But you figured out that when you rely on each other, you can a few things done. And when Oblivion came by, you jumped on board, knowing it would give you even more people to use to cover up your lack of ability. Make no mistake, I despise the both of you. But I’m not even going to bother fighting the two of you unless I have to. When the rest of Oblivion is picked apart, you two will simply sink back down to the bottom of the wrestling ladder where you belong. “Sickness, if you ever come near me in the span of your life, it will promptly come to an end, because your neck really will wind up getting broken.” Slate stands. “And then there is the leader of Oblivion. The person I will destroy next week. Herbert Ingles. That’s right, Herb, I’m still convinced that you’re the master behind Oblivion. It’s the only possible way. How the hell could Sickness really be the leader without you knowing? How could you spend so much time with her without realizing that she was just using you? No, you’re far to smart for that. It leaves too many loose ends. You have to be behind it all. “So, why did you form Oblivion, Herbert? They debuted the night we were supposed to wrestle, and they attacked you right before our match. Did you start all of this so you wouldn’t have to fight me? Were you scared of me? Was all of this done just so you could claim that the only person you’ve lost to in Mercury was the company’s champion? “Or was this all done so you could get the Mercury title back? If they really planned on breaking the freak’s neck so that a member could get a title shot, why wouldn’t Gol have come out at the beginning of the match to make all of us quit, then settle it on their own next week? Instead, they wait until the end of the match. “But everyone knows that Beaumont is in your back pocket. Wouldn’t they have known that if something at absurd as what they did happened, that he’d find some way to give you another shot at being in the title match? Wouldn’t they have known that doing what they did would screw up the plan for it to be Magnum Wolf and Pariah for the title? “Doing what they did got you another chance at wrestling for the title. It works out too well for you not to be involved, Harbinger. “Next week we wrestle, Herbert. Next week, I prove that you’re nothing compared to me. I don’t care what the reasoning Beaumont has for putting me against you; I will have no pity for you. I don’t give a rat’s ass what you have planned for Oblivion to do to me so you can win. I won’t allow it to work. “Do you expect me to take it easy because your poor little heart was broken? Do you expect me to be phased by it? I’ve had my heart ripped out of my chest three times; do you think I care if that whore did the same to you? Or do you want me to feel sorry for you because you’re alone now? I’ve been alone for the majority of my adult life. If you think that you can claim that you have some driving force behind you I can’t understand, don’t bother. First, you’re behind it all, it’s fake, it means nothing. Second, if by some strange aberrance of reason, it is true that Sickness just used you to further Oblivion, I know what you claim to be going through much too well. If you think you’re going to trick your way into a win by playing up that we have some common bond, don’t bother. I don’t believe it, and I won’t take it easy on you because of it. “There are one of two possibilities for next week, Herbert. The first is that you’re linked to Oblivion. You’re one of the ringmasters behind all of this. So, in our match, you’ll try to find some way to screw me over. It won’t work. I’m already expecting it. Run-ins won’t get the job done, I’m already anticipating it, and I’m prepared for it. Tricks won’t get the job done either; I couldn’t care less if you had God himself come down and threaten me with eternal damnation unless I lost to you. It won’t work; nothing will keep me from devoting every aspect of my being to defeating you. “The second possibility is that you really are just a victim in all of this. You’re emotionally devastated, and nothing like the person you were just 24 hours ago. In which case, beating you will be the single easiest thing I’ve ever done, because there’s no way you’ll be able to concentrate on the match. If you’re really the shell of who you were personally that you looked to be at the end of Fusion, you’ll be a shell of who you were professionally. If you’re really as effected by what happened with Sickness as you act, then there’s no way you’ll be over in a week, picking you apart in the ring will be child’s play. “I thought I was doing the right thing yesterday. I thought I was doing a favor for you by saving your girlfriend. Now, next time we meet, you’ll be doing a favor for me. Whether you want to or not, you’ll be the person that earns me a shot at the Mercury Championship. “So help me God, every chance I get, I’ll make every member of Oblivion regret what they’ve done. I’ll make them regret taking my title from me; I’ll make them regret stealing the number-one contendership from me; I’ll make them regret forcing me to abandon my morals and making me a person I don’t want to be. Whatever it takes, I’ll prove they don’t deserve to be in my company.” Slate continues staring off into the river. The scene fades.
