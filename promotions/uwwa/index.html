Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The UWWA opened on 18 May 1997 as an e-wrestling federation “where role-playing is NOT REQUIRED” — an inactive member's wrestlers would simply be booked for matches anyway — deciding results on the TNM 6.2 Plus simulator and portraying only real professional wrestlers, three per member for the time being. It ran one weekly card, the “UWWA Saturday Super Main Event,” and sanctioned two titles, a World Title and a Tag Team Title, with its first eight members set to enter a World Title tournament. Its first taken roster was a single stable, the Mega-Powers — Hollywood Hogan, Macho Man Randy Savage and Mr. Perfect, managed by Miss Elizabeth.

Four days later, on 22 May, it had grown to four titles, adding a U.S. Title and a Regional Title; two weekly cards, adding “UWWA Fight Night Thursday”; a six-wrestler cap per member; and its own dedicated web address. Its roster had grown to six stables: the Mega-Powers, now also fielding Zeus and the Legion of Doom; Triple Threat (Chris Benoit, Shane Douglas and Dean Malenko, managed by Francine); Broken Bones Inc. (Vader and the Eliminators, managed by Jim Cornette); NAFTA (Bret Hart, Steve Austin and Eddie Guerrero, managed by Bobby Heenan); the Dark Side (the Undertaker, Sting and the Giant, managed by Sable); and the Diamond Boys Stable (Diamond Dallas Page, Vinnie Vegas and Diamond Stud, managed by Kimberly).

No card, match, or title change of its own survives. A different federation's own later rulebook has its self-signed operator state that he had “held titles in the now defunct UWWA,” placing its close sometime within the following month.
