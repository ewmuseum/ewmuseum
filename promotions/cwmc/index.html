Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Chris Williams Memorial Cup is an interfederation tournament held in memory of Chris Williams, a community handler and a former PWW executive and TV Title champion killed in an automobile accident on 4 May 1999. It staged no roster and kept no home, running instead on the calendars of the federations that hosted it in turn.

PWW's own rulebook names it an annual fixture on its pay-per-view calendar. The tournament's roll of past champions — the organiser's “CWMC ‘Hall of Fame’” posting of 16 May 2007 — opens at PWW with Jay Phoenix, naming him first champion of the Chris Williams Television Title, the defended belt PWW carried in the same man's name; the record does not settle whether that first entry was a Cup win or a reign of the belt. Three further champions follow in order, undated: TimeBomb, Cutthroat Chris Hatchett, and Gurgles. None of the four carries a year.

The tournament's only staging fully held is PTC's 2007 running: six rounds and a Second Chance Battle Royal wildcard across thirty-four entrants, run in Flagstaff, Arizona as “the first ever PTC Indy Event,” decided for The Illustrious Face-Eater over Digital Mortality, three judges to none. No later staging or closing notice survives; PTC's 2007 Final is the last the record shows.

Affiliations

Professional World Wrestling (PWW)

Crossover booking with Chris Williams Memorial Cup (1999)
PWW's own rulebook names the Cup an annual fixture on its own calendar, and the Cup's own roll of past champions opens there, undated: Jay Phoenix, its first-ever champion, of what the roll calls the Chris Williams Television Title, followed in order by three further champions the record leaves undated.

Global Internet Wrestling Federation (GIWF, 1999–2001)

Crossover booking with Chris Williams Memorial Cup (1999) (2001)
GIWF staged a running of the Cup in 2001, its own broadcasts billing the same night inconsistently on air as both the tournament's second and third annual. No winner survives.

Primetime Central (PTC)

Crossover booking with Chris Williams Memorial Cup (1999) (2003; 2007)
PTC hosted the tournament's only fully-held staging, 2007, run in Flagstaff, Arizona as "the first ever PTC Indy Event" across six of its own show rows, decided for The Illustrious Face-Eater over Digital Mortality. PTC's own news separately reports the Cup's one other dated winner, Saij, in 2003.
