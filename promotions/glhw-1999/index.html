Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Great Lakes Heritage Wrestling dated its own beginning to April 1999, and its own retrospective broadcast of 5 October 1999 looked back on four matches it framed as its formative run.

The first pitted an outsider, Harley Phiresteiner, against the promotion's own president, Ron Van Clam, in a South Bass Island Brawl for a contract and a place in an upcoming War Games match, settled with no rules but one — the winner had to put the other man in an ambulance and drive him to the hospital — and won when Phiresteiner did exactly that. The second, a Hell in a Cell “Prison Match,” crowned Dave Death the promotion's first Extreme Champion over Hell's Angel. The third, a weapons brawl between debuting wrestlers PSiKNYTE and Derick Craven, ended with Craven leaping off a balcony through a table to finish him. The broadcast closed with a four-way King of Hardcore battle among Phiresteiner, Craven, Legacy Advisor, and reigning Middleweight Champion PSiKNYTE that spilled out of the arena, through a backstage elevator, and onto the roof, which gave way beneath two of the four men before Phiresteiner was declared the winner.

Its Thursday Night Warfare card of 21 October 1999, run mid-way through an Extreme Title tournament, saw Johnson beat Gannon Knight after an interfering run-in backfired on its own instigator, Vortex defeat Tony Jones, and the team of Cee Wooden and Drake Stevens beat the Helltown Greasers in a brawling tag match, only to be attacked afterward by the losing team.

Through October 1999 the promotion ran a public television-ratings rivalry against another newsgroup federation, Extreme Internet Wrestling, posting the two shows' competing overall and half-hour numbers head to head each week — a 1.5 to .9 loss the first week recorded, narrowed to 1.06 against .94 the next.

No further card survives past that 21 October 1999 broadcast.
