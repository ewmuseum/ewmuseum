Thursday Night Warfare

21 October 1999

TNW Preface - I recived word fromt eh Handler fo Chris Cage, who says school is taking too much out of him, and asked to be removed from GLHW for the time being. Consider 'Dynamite' Jeff Lawrence gone as well for not RP'ing. Therefore, there match was skipped, and a bye was placed in the Extreme Title tourney. You guys have been doing a great job so far, keep up the good work. Gannon Knight vs. Rob Johnson Knight came out first, Johnson second. Each man recieved lots of heel heat upon entering the ring. Johnson came out fast, hitting a GutwrenchSuplex as well as a Tilt-a-whirl slam on the smaller Gannon Knight at first. Knight mounted a comeback, and went to the airhitting a picture-perfect moonsault, as well as a corkscrew plancha, which drew a great pop from the crowd. Johnson tried to behead Knight with a stiff clothesline, but Knight ducked and connected with a stiff flying forearm, drawing a two count. Then GLHW Fan Favorite Cash Kencade made his way out, with a chair and Sammy Goodrich. While Distracted the ref, Cash went to kabosh Johnson with a chair -- but miss and connected with Knight. Johnson knocked Cash down as the ref turned around too see Knight covered by Rob. 3 count later, and Johnson walked away with the victory. Winner : Johnson Tony Jones vs. Vortex Vortex took control early and kept it the whole way. Vortex showed a great arsenal of moves. He took it to Jones with a variety of Power Moves, connecting with a huge Oklahoma Stampede as well as Double Arm DDT. Vortex took the win with a INverted Tornado DDT form the second rope. Winner : Vortex Cee Wodden/Drake Stevens vs. Helltown Greasers This match was a brawl inside and outside of the ring from the start. Lots of stiff shots. WOO-DY! Chants were deafining for the fan favorite Cee Wooden. By the time order was restores, Cee and Vice Proxy stodd off in the ring. The duo of Wooden and Stevens seemed to mesh well together, but the seasoned veterans in the Helltown Greasers seemed to have been one step ahead. They showed great teamwork and worked over Cee, keeping him on their half of the ring, away from Roberts. Proxy hit a vicious Spiccoli Driver on Cee, and Pail followed itup with a splash form the top rope. Cee hit a great Inverted DDT which turned the tide of the match. Cee tagged out to Roberts, who came in and cleaned house. Out of nowhere, Drake connected with Undeniable End on Proxy while Woody and Pail brawled ont he outside. Drake covered, and the Stevens/Wooden duo took the win. Drake and Cee are celebrating, when Dan Denison [Drake's Bodygaurd] comes out and calls Drake to the back. As Wooden draws mroe reactions formt he crowd, the Greasers attack from behind. Pail grabs a chair from the outside, and nails Cee right on the head. Cee's face is busted open now, he has blood covering his face. Vice Proxy forcefully grabs Alicia, and is about to strike her across the face. Cee sees this and gets outraged. Before VP can hit her Cee runs up to him and tackle and punches him. Pail comes over and gets Cee off Vice Proxy. Pail and Vice regain the advantage Pail goes outside of the ring and sets up a table. Pail gets up and climbs the top rope while Vice is laying Cee down on the table on the outside. When Cee is set up, Pail does a Moonsault off the Top-Rope onto Cee through the table. Vice Proxy grabs a mic and climbs in the ring. He goes over by Alicia, who is crying in the corner. Vice Proxy[angrily]: "This is your man?! Look at him over there, all limp and bloodied. Get it through your head, he is nothing but a fat, old, and out of shape piece of s[beep]." [Vice Proxy walks to the center of the ring looking towards the entrance way] Vice Proxy: "Now Drake Stevens, you should've stuck around and got your ass kicked along with your partner over there. But don't worry, you'll have your chance to get the beating of a lifetime, don't worry." "Bled For Days" by Static-X starts to play throughout the arena as Vice Proxy drops the microphone. EMT's and other trainers come out to help Cee Wooden. Pail and Vice walk to the back as the show comes to it's end.
