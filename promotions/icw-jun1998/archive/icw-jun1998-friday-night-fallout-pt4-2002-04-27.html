ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Card

Friday Night Fallout, 27 April 2002 (part 4 of 4)

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [MAIN EVENT] Non-Title Match Tommy Lionheart V/S Brian J. Blottie WRITTEN BY: [name withheld] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: This next match is scheduled for one fall, and is our non-title MAIN EVENT! [Crowd pop!] LANCE JOHNSON: Introducing first... ["Head Like A Hole" by Nine Inch Nails cranks up, and the crowd does to, booing incessently.]
LANCE JOHNSON: He weighs in tonight at two hundred and thirty-two pounds, and hails from Grosse
Pointe Farms, Michigan...
[The second he peeks his head out of the curtain, the boos grow in intensity, as in two weeks, this man has worked his way into the disdane and hatred of every man, woman, and child in the audience.]
LANCE JOHNSON: ... here is "The True Living Legend"... [MONSTER HEEL HEAT!] LANE JOHNSON: Brian J. Blottie! [The long black tights, accentuated with gold letters spelling "Living" down one leg, "Legend"
down the other, cling to the body of Brian J. Blottie as he sprints to the ring. The reason he's sprinting? To avoid the crowd's outreaching fists, all of them looking to do _bad things_ to Michigan's finest. Brian slides into the ring, springs to his feet, and tosses his arms into the air, the heel heat so overwhelming that if it were real heat, the
Earth would melt.]
LANCE JOHNSON: His opponent ... [As "Head Like A Hole" fades out, Hoobastank's smash hit "Crawling in the Dark" begins to play over the PA system.] LANCE JOHNSON: Hailing from London, England ... Weighing TWO hundred and THIRTY-FIVE pounds ... [Every fan in the arena turn their heads to the entrance way, awaiting their champion, as the vocals kick in.]
# I will dedicate # # And sacrifice my everything for just a second's worth # # Of how my story's ending # # And I wish I could know if the directions that I take # # And all the choices that I make won't end up all for nothing # [The lights dim down, as a bright white spotlight flashes down just before the entrance.] # Show me it's worth # # Make me understand it # # I've been crawling in the dark, looking for the answer # LANCE JOHNSON: He is the ICW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION ... Here is ... TTTOOMMMMYYY LLLIOONNHHEAARRRTT!!!! [The International champion, right on cue, appears from behind the curtain into the arena to a huge face pop from the sold out crowd in Washington, D.C. The champion, looking happy and confident, begins his stroll down the aisle. Like always, slapping as many fans' hands as he can along the way.
He jogs up the steel ring steps, the spotlight still shining down on him, and enters the ring via through the top and middle ropes. Without wasting any time, the champ leaps up onto the top turnbuckle and raises his title belt high above his head to another great reaction. He leaps back down, and grants the referee with the belt as the music fades out and the lights return to their normal brightness.]
[Tommy eyes Brian J. Blottie across the ring, who is grinning like a hyena. Both men come in, looking like they're going to lock-up... and Brian nails a thumb to the eye, blinding Tommy.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Cheap shot to start things off then from Brian Blottie. What would you expect? SHANE PROPONE: Hey, this guy is a damn good wrestler. Hell, tonight he'll run rings round our pathetic International champion.
[Tommy stammers away from Blottie, leaving himself wide open to a boot to the ass. "JACKASS!"
is all Brian can say for his actions, as Tommy stumbles forward, turns around, and looks at
Brian with anger in his eyes.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: I'm getting a little bored with that catchphrase, and it seems
Lionheart is
too!
[DA SMIRK~! from Blottie, as he waves Tommy on, trying to get him to lock-up again. They actually get to lockup this time, and Tommy lets his frustration guide him, pushing Brian J.
Blottie back to the ropes. The referee is in there, to break it up...] SHANE PROPONE: Get that 'JACKASS' off the 'True Living Legend', ref, do your damn job! [The referee does, but like before, Blottie gains an advantage with the thumb to the eye on the champ.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Another blatant thumb to the eye! Is that the only move in this guys' repetroie? SHANE PROPONE: Oh, stop whining. You wait, he's gonna make an example of
Lionheart tonight.
[This time, with Tommy holding his eye, Brian nails an actual wrestling move, bounding off the ropes, grabbing Tommy's face, and burying into the mat.]
SHANE PROPONE: There you go! Beautiful manuever! Give the guy credit. JOHN WILLIAMS: Give me a break. [Blottie is to his feet, "dusting" his hands off, then slams a boot into Tommy's back. He actually laughs, backs up, and goads Tommy as the International champ makes his way to his feet.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Blottie now, just mocking the champion. I can't wait to see
Tommy wipe that
smirk off his face.
SHANE PROPONE: Hold it right there! Nobody gives a damn what you think, just call the match, and do your job. Come on Blottie!
[Tommy's up, and Brian is quick to move in... but not quick enough, as Tommy buries a shoulder into Brian's gut. Tommy's cruiserweight past comes to the forefront, as Tommy hits the ropes, bounds back, and leaps over Brian's back, grabbing him and pulling him down into the pin.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Sunset flip by the champion ... ONE ... TTWOOO ... Kickout. Two count only. SHANE PROPONE: He doesn't honestly believe he can win like that? God, he's even more pathetic than I thought.
[Both men scramble to their feet, but Blottie is up first, and takes advantage by pushing
Tommy back into the ropes, and slinging him out. Reversal right before Brian lets go, Brian now hits the far ropes, bounces back, and ducks a hard clothesline attempt.
Off the other ropes, Tommy leapfrogs over Brian, rolls onto his back, and as Brian bounces back again, Tommy buries both his feet into Blottie's gut, then brings him forward, sending him flying and crashing to the mat.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a move by the champ! A great sequence there between both men. The tide has changed.
SHANE PROPONE: For now. [Tommy is up, and doesn't sit around and brag, but instead goes right to the attack, pulling
Brian up by his shoulders, and laying in a _wicked_ chop to the chest.]
_WOO!_ [Another chop...] _WOO!_ [And one more sends Blottie back into the ropes, clutching his reddened chest.] _WOO!_ JOHN WILLIAMS: Hard knife-edge chops by Lionheart who now has Blottie where he wants him. SHANE PROPONE: What a fag. [A push, and Tommy Irish whips Brian across the ring, waits for Brian to come back, then nails a nasty forearm shot to the face. A second... a third. Tommy spins, then nails one last forearm to the mouth, sending Brian crashing to the mat.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Tommy dominating now, as he points to the top turnbuckle. It's time to fly!
SHANE PROPONE: Time to fly? Who is he - Superman? [Oozing confidence, Tommy starts to ascend the ropes, looking like he's going for a moonsault.
Unfortunately for him, because his back is turned, he doesn't see Brian recover and scramble to his feet. The shove and inevitable crotching the follows shocks Tommy as much as it injures him.]
SHANE PROPONE: That's better. JOHN WILLIAMS: Blottie, with one simple shove, turns this match once again, causing Lionheart a great deal of pain.
[Grabbing the shoulders of Lionheart, Brian pulls him down into the tree of woe, hanging upside down. One... two... three boots to the gut, and the ref pushes Brian back, trying to keep him off Tommy. Brian gladly backs up, as the referee tries pulling Tommy down...
and doesn't see Brian's real reason for backing up, as Brian rushes forward and baseball slides across the mat, driving both his feet into Tommy's face. Normally, a man would fall after this move. But
Tommy is still stuck upside down.]
SHANE PROPONE: BWAHAHA! Look at the chump. This is wrestling at its best. [The referee is trying his damnedest to get Tommy out of the corner, but is pushed aside by
Brian, who climbs the turnbuckles even with Tommy stuck in them... then salutes the crowd while standing directly on Tommy's family jewels. The referee tries getting him down, which just results in Brian grinding his heel in nice and tight, soaking in all the fans' hatred.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: This is uncalled for, dammit! Disqualify him, ref. [And just as the referee seems to be on his way to doing just that, Brian leaps down, DA SMIRK~! greeting the referee's warnings. Finally, the referee gets Tommy out of the tree of woe, letting him fall to the mat, clutching his testicles.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Finally, Tommy has been let free, but can he come back from the pain he just endured?
SHANE PROPONE: Can pigs fly? JOHN WILLIAMS: Dumbo can. SHANE PROPONE: You sad little man. [Tommy is pulled up off the mat by Brian J. Blottie, a handful of hair in his grasp. A few shouts by Blottie, directly into Tommy's ear, and a kneelift to the gut to make sure Tommy doesn't try anything funny. Brian grabs a headlock, and grinds his arm in tight.]
SHANE PROPONE: Brian J. Blottie - what a professional. This guy is damn impressive. Look at those biceps bulge as he applies that headlock.
JOHN WILLIAMS: He has certainly inflicted a lot of pain onto the champ so far in this matchup.
[A _very_ cocky "True Living Legend" starts mocking the fans, all the while keeping the headlock applied. Tommy starts to shake his arms, getting the crowd riled up.
Brian doesn't notice this, as he's too busy soaking in the crowds response, which he thinks is for him. He notices it when Tommy locks his arms around his waist and drops backwards, dumping Brian on his back, though.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: SIDE SUPLEX BY THE CHAMP! Desperation move there, as the referee begins his count ...
ONE ... TWO... THREE... FOUR... FIVE... [Both men begin to move.] SIX... [At an almost identical pace, both men crawl to their knees.] SEVEN... [Both men get to their feet.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men up ... Tommy is fired up! Listen to these fans! [Right hand from Tommy! Another! Brian is rocked on his heels, and backpeddles, but not fast enough to stop the onslaught. Three more right hands follow Brian into the corner, as Tommy grabs Blottie's arm and sends him clear across the ring, crashing into the turnbuckles _hard_.
Tommy raises one fist into the air, charging up the crowd, then runs into the corner, leaps up, shoves his legs into Brian's gut while clinging to his head, then rolls backwards, sending
Brian soaring once more, and once more crashing to the mat.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Tommy building some momentum. This doesn't look too good for your man now,
Shane.
SHANE PROPONE: Whatever. [Brian is sprawled out on the mat, but Tommy sure isn't, as he mounts the ropes, steadies himself, and waits for Brian to get to his feet.]
SHANE PROPONE: Look up, Brian! [And as Blottie gets to his feet, Tommy soars through the air, driving both feet into Brian's face and sending him careening to the mat.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: MISSILE DROPKICK! What execution! Tommy goes for the cover
... ONE ... TTTWWOO ... THHHRR ... Shoulder up _just_ in the nick of time by Blottie. [Tommy wastes no time, pushing Brian back into the corner, then lifting him up onto the top.
Tommy follows him up, but gets punched in the face for his troubles. A second punch... and a third is blocked, and followed by a knee to the skull. That dazes Brian enough for Tommy to leap up, wrap his legs around Brian's head, then flip backwards, driving
Blottie skull-first to the mat.] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY GOOD GOD! HURRANCANRANA OFF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! WHAT AN AMAZING MOVE! [The fans are on their feet, the entire arena has come alive, and Tommy is jacked up. He pumps his fist to the crowd, then motions once more to the ropes. The crowd goes BANANA! A leapfrog to the top, along with a spin, and Tommy is ready. The crowd is on the edge of their seat... and then _EXPLODES_ as Tommy leaps off the top and hits a picture perfect pumping splash.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: FROG SPLASH!! THAT MUST DO IT! THE COVER! ONE ... TTTWOOO ... THHHHRRREE ... SHANE PROPONE: NOOOO!! FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE! BLOTTIE HAS HIS FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE!! [Tommy can't believe it, and starts questioning the referee on it, making sure he heard right.
Not one to give the referee hell, Tommy grabs Brian by the neck and pulls him up. Back to the ropes, slingshot, and Tommy meets Brian coming back with a _beautiful_ dropkick.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: A picture perfect dropkick by the champion sends Brian J. Blottie to the outside. Tommy's going up top again, what now? [You'd think at this point Tommy would wait for Brian to get up. You'd think wrong, as Tommy isn't a waiter, and instead sentons off the top, slamming the entire weight of his body down onto Brian J. Blottie's. Yes, there is a _lot_ of heat here between these two men, and
Tommy's willing to put his body on the line to finish it.] JOHN WILLIAMS: A BIG SPLASH TO A GROUNDED BRIAN J. BLOTTIE! What a match we are witnessing here, folks, this is phenomenal!
SHANE PROPONE: Come on, Brian! [Both men are down, and both men seem to be hurt, as the referee starts the mandatory 10 count, and really, it seems to be just a matter of time before both men are counted out here.]
ONE ... TWO ... THREE ... JOHN WILLIAMS: Neither men moving, this is not a pretty sight. FOUR ... FIVE ... SHANE PROPONE: Brian's moving! Yes! SIX ... [With the assistance of the ring apron, Blottie, somehow, manages to pull himself up.] SEVEN ... JOHN WILLIAMS: Wait a minute ... [Out of nowhere, playing posseum perhaps, Tommy kips up to his feet, much to the delight of the fans.]
EIGHT ... [Tommy pushes Blottie back into the ring, and follows him back in.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Tommy's calling for the end ... It's time for the Tommy Drop! [Brian tries getting up, but gets kicked back down by Tommy, who's not to be deterred. Tommy drags Brian into position, then bounds to the top. The crowd is waiting with baited breath, wanting to see Tommy Lionheart finish this chump off. He signals... he leaps... the somersault... but the legdrop hits nothing but mat.]
SHANE PROPONE: WOOO! The champ has had his fun, now it's time for Brian to finish him off and send him back to jolly ol' England for good!
[Blottie is quickly up, revealing that he can play possum to, and takes advantage of the fact that Tommy is still seated, his ass and leg hurting, by flipping over the front of him, grabbing his neck on the way down, and slamming his head into the mat the hard way.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Flipping neckbreaker by Blottie. You could be right, Shane, this could be the beginning of the end.
SHANE PROPONE: There's no 'could', it _IS_! [Now it's Brian pulling Tommy into position, and scaling the ropes. He slides his thumb across his throat, letting the fans know that this is it, and recieves a hearty boo in response. A middle finger in the air, and then _he's_ in the air, somersaults, and _nails_ his own version of the somersault legdrop;Simply Legendary.]
SHANE PROPONE: HE NAILED IT! GOODNIGHT TOMMY LIONHEART! JOHN WILLIAMS: The referee counts ... ONE ... TTTWWOOO ... THHHHHRRRREEEEE???? THHRRRE ... NOOOO!! SHANE PROPONE: WHAT?!! [Huge face pop~!] JOHN WILLIAMS: TOMMY KICKED OUT! HOW ON EARTH DID HE KICK OUT FROM THAT?! SHANE PROPONE: THAT WAS THREE!!! [Brian is just as shocked, but that shock turns to anger pretty damn fast, as
Brian drags
Tommy up to his feet, drags him into the front chancery, and lifts. He holds him up for a second... and drops with a piledriver. But he doesn't let go! Roll through, lift, and another! Roll through, lift, and a third piledriver!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: PARADIGM SHIFT! NO ONE CAN KICK OUT OF THAT! SHANE PROPONE: That is all she wrote! JOHN WILLIAMS: The pin ... ONE ... TTTTTTTTTWWWWOOOOO ... THHHHHHHHHRRRRRRRRREEE ... THE LEFT SHOULDER'S UP! THE LEFT SHOULDER'S UP! TWO AND THREE QUARTERS! THIS MATCH IS _STILL_ GOING! [Anger turns to rage, as Brian stomps around the ring, screaming at the referee, throwing a temper tantrum. He slides out of the ring, pushes the ring attendant out of his chair, and folds it up. Tommy isn't getting up fast, but he's _trying_ to get up. Brian slides back into the ring, chair in hand, as the referee screams at him to drop it. Pure and utter hate seems to be coarsing through Brian's veins, as Tommy slowly gets to his knees. Brian raises the chair high overhead... and has it pilfered at the last second by the referee.
Brian spins and screams at him, the referee dumping the chair out of the ring.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Good call, referee! What a match this has been, the last thing we want is for it to end like that.
SHANE PROPONE: True. But once Brian's won, then we'll see him knock the chump into oblivion.
[Unfortunately, Brian hasn't won, and with Tommy on his feet, and Brian's back turned, it certainly doesn't seem like Brian is focused on defeating Tommy. When he finally _does_ regain his focus, it's not because he's smartened up. It's because Tommy wrapped his arms around his waist, then dropped him backwards, with the bridge.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: GERMAN SUPLEX! IS THAT ENOUGH? ONE ... TTTTWWOOOOOOO ... THHHHRRRREEE????!!! THHHHRRREE ... NOOOOOO!!! BLOTTIE KICKS OUT!! I CANNOT BELIEVE THIS!! SHANE PROPONE: HE IS THE TRUE LIVING LEGEND ... HE IS _THE_ MAN!!! [Tommy is flabbergasted, but doesn't let up at all, instead bringing Brian back to his feet, and pulling him into a front chancery. Lift, and it looks like Tommy is going for the piledriver... but Brian fights it. Lift again, and again, Brian fights it.
Tommy, smart, doesn't fall for the ploy and pulls away, just as Brian goes to backbody drop him. Brian shoots up, loses his balance, and falls to the mat, ass first.]
SHANE PROPONE: HEY! That's not fair. JOHN WILLIAMS: I think we just found out who the real jackass really is, huh? SHANE PROPONE: That's not funny. [Embarrassed, Brian scrambles to his feet, but Tommy is waiting for him, and slams a boot into
Brian's gut. Off the ropes, and Tommy comes back with what looks like a leaping legdrop across the back of Brian's head... but instead wraps his legs around, spins, and on the third revolution, fires Brian out, all the way through the ropes and to the floor.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: THE TRIPLE REVOLUTION HEADSCISSORS TAKEDOWN! That's one of
Tommy's trademark
moves and, by the sound of it, one of the fans' favorite moves to watch, too.
SHANE PROPONE: He still sucks. [Tommy isn't done yet, either, as he gets up, waits for the dazed and dizzy
Blottie to get to
his feet, then hits the ropes, coming back... and does the most amazing move
Tommy Lionheart
has _ever_ done. Cartwheel into a backflip over the top ropes, moonsaulting onto Brian J.
Blottie.] [HOLY SHIT POP~!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SPACE TIGER DROP!! SHANE PROPONE: WHO?! JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT A MOVE! I HAVEN'T SEEN ANYONE DO THAT FOR A VERY, VERY LONG TIME! WOW!! [The fans go absolutely BANANA! as a "TOMMY, TOMMY" chant begins to erupt around the arena.
The referee begins his count.] ONE ... TWO ... [Tommy, surprisingly fast after such a dare-devil move, gets back to his feet.] THREE ... [And rolls back into the ring. The referee continues to count out Blottie.] FOUR... FIVE... [Tommy isn't waiting any longer, as he moves forward and grabs onto the top rope. The referee tries stopping him...]
SHANE PROPONE: NOOO! [... but is unsuccessful, as Tommy flips over the top, and delivers a legdrop across the throat of Blottie.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: TOMMY DROP! ROPE-ASSISTED TOMMY DROP! SHANE PROPONE: Will God have no mercy?! [Tommy pumps his fists, riling up the crowd, then rolls Brian back into the ring, and goes for the pin.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: SURELY THIS IS ALL ... THE COVER ... ONE ... TTTTWWWWWOOOOOOO ... THHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHRRRRRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEE ... IT'S OVER!!! SHANE PROPONE: NO, IT'S NOT!!! THE REFEREE SAYS NO! BLOTTIE GOT HIS SHOULDER UP ... HE WONT STAY DOWN!! HE IS GOD! JOHN WILLIAMS: I don't believe it! How close was that?! I can't take much more of this! [And neither can Tommy, who slams his hands down onto the mat in frustration. He has to be asking himself what it will take to put "The True Living Legend" away. He decides that it's about damn time to finish this thing once and for all, picks up Brian, and drags him into the front chancery once more. Lift... he hooks his arm between Brian's legs... then drops with the piledriver.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: CRADLE PILEDRIVER! I'm not gonna say a word ... ONE ... TTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTWWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO ... THHHHHHHHHRRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE ... [Tommy leaps up, arms raised in the air. What he doesn't realise, however, is that Blottie's foot is on the bottom rope.]
SHANE PROPONE: BWAHAHAHA! Not so fast Tommy! JOHN WILLIAMS: That _HAD_ to be pure instinct. This match will be remembered for quite some time, and it's still going.
[Tommy is still celebrating, and the referee is trying to get him to calm down by grabbing his arm. Tommy thinks the ref is raising his hand, and holds his hand up... which is then unceremoniously pulled down as the referee tells Tommy that this match is _not_ over. A few motions later, and Tommy is shaking his head, still not believing what he's being told.]
SHANE PROPONE: Believe it, choader boy. You're _never_ gonna beat Blottie. JOHN WILLIAMS: That remains to be seen. [Once more, Tommy lifts Brian off the mat, but whether it be out of instinct or gut reaction,
Brian J. Blottie drives his forearm upwards as he's being pulled up,
low-blowing Tommy Lionheart in the process. The referee yells at Blottie, Tommy stammers then falls, and Brian slowly... but... surely gets to his feet.]
SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH! Time to die, Tommy! JOHN WILLIAMS: Somehow, after all this punishment, Brian J. Blottie is up. This is amazing, folks. Absolutely amazing. [Still holding his crotch, Tommy forces himself up, crouched over. A kick to the face, and
Tommy is shot upwards, now hurting in more ways than one. A _hard_ lariat, and
Brian J.
Blottie falls on top for the pin.] SHANE PROPONE: That's it, folks! JOHN WILLIAMS: The count ... ONE ... TTTWWOOOO ... Tommy kicks out with ease! SHANE PROPONE: Huh?! [There's no shock on Brian's face, only determination now. No smirking, no playing up to the fans. Tommy is pulled up to his feet, and lays in a right hand to the gut.
Brian no-sells it, drives a knee into the chest of Tommy, hooks him, leaps, spins... and
_destroys_ him with
a DDT.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Tornado DDT! That could do it! SHANE PROPONE: He's not done, baby! He's not done! [The stomp to the mat, and the fans know what's coming.] SHANE PROPONE: Load that boot up! Load it up tight! [Another stomp, as Tommy slowly gets to his feet, disoriented.] JOHN WILLIAMS: If Brian J. Blottie hits this, this match is over! [One more stomp, and Tommy is up. Brian is on the move, coming forward, throwing out his leg into the superkick... and missing as Tommy ducks. Brian is unbalanced, Tommy grabs him, and delivers a spinning DDT of his own!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: SUPERKICK MISSED! TORNADO DDT~! MY GOD! SHANE PROPONE: NO! [But Tommy can't follow up, still feeling the effects of Blottie's DDT. The referee has no choice but to start the standing ten count.]
ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! [Tommy shows some signs of movement.] FIVE! [Now Brian grabs the bottom rope, trying to pull himself up.] SIX! SEVEN! [Tommy is on one knee, gasping for air.] EIGHT! [Blottie is holding on for dear life, dragging with all his might.] NINE! [Brian is up! Tommy is up! Brian goes for the larait...] SHANE PROPONE: LARIAT-O! [But Tommy ducks! Brian spins, Tommy with the boot...off the ropes... HUGE kneelift, sending
Brian sprawling to the mat.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: My God! Tommy Lionheart with that kneelift! Blottie is down! AND TOMMY IS GOING TO THE TOP! [Slowly, Tommy ascends the ropes, gasping for air.] SHANE PROPONE: There's no way he'll hit whatever he's planning! He's too exhausted. [Tommy closes his eyes, crosses himself, takes a deep breath... and leaps.] JOHN WILLIAMS: MY... [Moonsault turned over into a 450 press. Yes folks...] JOHN WILLIAMS: ... GOD! STARDUST PRESS~! STARDUST PRESS~! HE HIT IT! SHANE PROPONE: WHAT IN THE BLUE HELL WAS THAT?! JOHN WILLIAMS: COVER! ONE!!!! TWWWWWOOOOO!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!! SHANE PROPONE: SHOULDER UP! SHOULDER UP! JOHN WILLIAMS: NOOOOO! THIS IS OVER! MY GOD, WHAT A MATCH! [The fans are going crazy, as Tommy can't believe it. He's holding his ribs, hurting after all the punishment he has taken, but his adrenaline has to be coarsing through his veins, eliminating the worst of the pain. He is handed the title, and as he puts his head against it, in tears, the crowd reacts with the loudest chant in ICW history...]
_TOMMY!_ _TOMMY!_ _TOMMY!_ _TOMMY!_ JOHN WILLIAMS: Listen to these fans! It is _deafening_ in here! SHANE PROPONE: WHAT?! I CAN'T HEAR YOU OVER THESE STUPID NEANDERTHALS! LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner of this match, and _STILL_ ICW International
Champion...
[Lance has to stop, as the crowd is just _way_ too loud for him to continue. Finally, he finishes...] LANCE JOHNSON: TOMMY!!!! _TOMMY!_ _TOMMY!_ _TOMMY!_ _TOMMY!_ LANCE JOHNSON: LIONHEARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRT! [Monsterous pop!] [Slowly, "The True Living Legend" peels himself off the mat, sweat dripping off of his body, his eyes cloudy, and as he sees Tommy on his knees, title in his hands, raising it for all the world to see, Brian shakes his head in disbelief, then _demands_ a microphone.]
BJB: Tommy, you son of a bitch! [HEEL POP!] SHANE PROPONE: Tell him, Brian! Tell that son of a bitch that he _NEVER_ beat you! You had your shoulder up!
[Slowly, Tommy pulls himself to his feet, ready for the attack.] BJB: If anyone is going to raise your damn hand tonight... it's going to be _ME_. JOHN WILLIAMS: YES! SHANE PROPONE: NOOOOOOO! [Brian walks over to Tommy, grabs his arm, and raises it into the air. Tommy is in shock, but then it dawns on him what is happening, and he pulls his arm down. He shakes his head, then raises _Brian's_ arm. The two men shake hands in the middle of the ring.]
SHANE PROPONE: Damn you Brian J. Blottie! Damn you for ruining my night! JOHN WILLIAMS: Sore spot? SHANE PROPONE: SHUT UP! [Tommy starts to show off his title belt, letting the cheers soak in. He points at it, points at himself, then raises his arms into the air.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Folks, this has been one of the greatest shows I've ever had the pleasure of calling, and after that match? I have to say that if anything can top that, I can't _wait_ to see it.
SHANE PROPONE: Stupid crap. JOHN WILLIAMS: Until next time... [But he's cut off. By what, you ask? Why, by Brian J. Blottie SUPERKICKING
Tommy Lionheart
as he turns around, sending Tommy crashing to the mat for the _THIRD_ time with the best damn superkick in the bidness.]
SHANE PROPONE: YES! YES! YES! BJB: Tommy, you really think I'm letting you get away that easy? I half-assed it tonight. Next time? I'm going full [BLEEP]ing throttle. And I'm going to shove my boot right through your skull... JACKASS!
JOHN WILLIAMS: My God... My... God. [Credits.]
