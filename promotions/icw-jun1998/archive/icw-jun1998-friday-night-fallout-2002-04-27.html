258 pounds... DAAAAVE FREEESSSSSSCOOOOOO~!! [The crowd continues to throw that mixed reaction up in the air as the song continues. Out form the smoke, steps out the tall dark Hawaiian Giant, Dave Fresco.]
[Clad in navy blue denim-looking sweatpants, a black Tee-shirt that reads "I am..." on the front in blood red lettering, and a pair of black Tims, he walks down the aisle with his head held up and a smug grin on his face. His eyes a narrowed, showing focus and energy flowing through his whole body.]
[As he makes it down to ringside, Fresco pulls himself onto the apron, via the middle rope.

He puts himself onto the second rope and extends his arms out, then clenches his fists and curls his arms down, flexing for the crowd. His urban voice with a hint of a tropical accent is heard in the form of a deep scream to the crowd. His face goes back to it's normal grinning as the song fades out. He climbs down and bounces off the ropes, awaiting the opening bell.]

JOHN WILLIAMS: Tough luck here tonight for Sprinkle, he can't even get in the ring to fight.

Fresco up and both

ONE... TWO... THREE... [Ashe begins to raise to one knee. As does Sprinkle.] FOUR... FIVE... [Both Ashe and Sprinkle now to their feet but Fresco is still down. Sprinkle and Ashe go at it with rights and lefts back and fourth... this time Sprinkle comes out on top.

INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING RETURNS TO PAY-PER-VIEW! CANADIAN CONQUEST 2002 Sunday, May 19th 2002 LIVE! From The Molson Centre in Montreal, Canada [Fades out and back to the MCI Center...] [Without any warning, "Chop Suey" by System of a Down begins to blare through the PA. Half of the crowd starts cheering like hell, the other have rains down a shower of heel love. Then, without warning again, "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka bursts out of the entrance.
Wearing a "HEAVY MENTAL ~ Just Another Nickname" T-shirt and some jeans, his arms extended, a
Cheshire Cat grin
on his face, and more importantly, the ICW North American Championship around his waist. A few moments later, "Double D" Devin DeMasters, red vinyl halter top and miniskirt and all, along with "Marz" Candress appear next to him.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Well, here he is! The new ICW North American Champion, Dave
Pietka. SHANE PROPONE: And DEVIN DeMASTERS! How could we forget her! JOHN WILLIAMS: Calm down, Shane. [Pietka struts down to the ring, as if he's his charming and psychoholic self again... as if he was never mad at anyone. He dances to the Serj's quick tongue and the fast guitar stylings to match. DeMasters smiles as she watches her boyfriend act as if nothing happened, except for winning the North American strap. Candress just... walks down. What did you expect him to do?
Pietka rolls into the ring, and jumps onto the turnbuckle, giving his psychoholic grin to the crowd again, pointing to the ICW North American strap.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: He seems happy! SHANE PROPONE: With a woman like Devin? I'd be happy, too! JOHN WILLIAMS: Don't you ever stop? SHANE PROPONE: Not really. [DeMasters and Candress enter the ring, and the music quiets down and stops. Pietka reaches out for a microphone. When he's handed one, he doesn't leave the turnbuckles. He turns around and sits on the top rope. He nods his head, and then looks around to the people within his line of sight.]
HEAVY MENTAL: It's been a good time, hasn't it? I mean, I think so. Things have really gotten a lot better since International Incident. I mean... let's roll down my first five matches back here in ICW, yeah? First off, Higor Rosa... a shame I had to beat the hell out of my biggest fan, yah? PFFT! I steamrolled over his ass as if he were a marshmallow... who knows, maybe he was! Then there was Mike Wise, that pathetic brit mob-boss wannabe who can't seem to stop talking about how his WIFE is holding him back. What a yutz, yeah? I mean, c'mon Wise...
I'm not buying. But after all, unless your last name is Gambino, Gotti, or to a lesser extent... Soprano, I'm not impressed.
SHANE PROPONE: Neither am I, sometimes. I mean, you shouldn't cross the Sopranos! JOHN WILLIAMS: Tell me you're just not that stupid, Shane! HEAVY MENTAL: So after shoving his ass into his wife's, I claimed my dominion over him. Then, there was the unfortunate incident at the PPV, to where my chance to be
INTERNATIONAL Champion was stolen, along with Devin's shirt, by the Masked Superstar Jobber, and I lost to Lionheart... but I made the Masked Superstar Jobber my BITCH at the next Fallout... only to be told that I had a match against "Sadistic" Shane Alexander for the ICW North
American Championship. SHANE ALEXANDER... and that's when I knew it would be a good moment to shine.
And I remember how I went on and on saying a Belt wouldn't make me happy and a belt would make me not mad anymore. Well... what can I say... I lied.

Champion.
Following in the footsteps of people Alex Cote and Mike Masters... but when I look down at those footprints, I decide to pull a Robert Frost, and make my own path.

To send a message to all the people who held this title and to all the people who want to hold this title that wrestling's most psychotic figure now possesses it. JOHN WILLIAMS: Well Dave Pietka putting out an open challenge, now to see if anyone responds.
