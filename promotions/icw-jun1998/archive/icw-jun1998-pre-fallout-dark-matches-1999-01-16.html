ICW (Jun 1998) — Card

Pre-Fallout Dark Matches

16 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH #1 - "Crippler" Chris Diorio V/S "The ALL-STAR" Brad Collingsworth -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Collingsworth and Diorio lock up at the bell. Diorio shoots him into the ropes and bounces off the other side nailing him with a flying clothesline taking him to the mat. Diorio to the middle turnbuckle and comes off with a leg drop but
Collingsworth moves out of the way and Diorio hit the mat. Collingsworth back up and he takes Diorio, lifts him up and into a tombstone piledriver!
Collingsworth now goes for the pin but Diorio kicks out of it at two. Collingsworth pulls him back up and shoots him into the ropes catching him off with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Collingsworth goes for another pin but Diorio kicks out again. Collingsworth pulls Diorio back up and shoots him into the ropes but Diorio revearses and nails Collingsworth with a boot to the gut and nails him with an elevated DDT. Diorio went for the pin and got the 1-2-3!
WINNER - "Crippler" Chris Diorio -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH #2 - Stryker V/S "Indispensible" Scotty Angel -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Angel jumped the gun on Stryker nailing him with a dropkick as he had his back turned. Angel sent Stryker into the ropes catching him with a big back bodydrop as he came off. Angel dropped a leg across his chest and went for the pin but only got a one count. Angel pulled Stryker back up and nailed a series of lefts and rights backing him into the corner. Angel whipped him out and ran across but Stryker got the boot up in his face. Stryker ran out of the corner nailing him with a huge bulldog. Stryker pulled Angel up, scoop and a slam. Stryker climbed up to the top rope and came off with a big elbow on Angel and went for another pin but only got a two. Stryker lifted Angel back up, went off the ropes nailing him with a big boot to the gut, hooked him and nailed a powerbomb. Stryker went right down hooking the leg for the pin and got the easy
1-2-3! WINNER - Stryker -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH #3 - Night V/S "The Feline" Caitlin Lynx -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Night wasted no time jumping all over Lynx. Night sent her into the ropes nailing him with a big dropkick as she came off. Night then pulled Lynx up nailed a scoop and a slam then came off the ropes with a big elbow, she went for the pin but only got a two count. Night pulled Lynx up, hooked her and nailed a huge brainbuster. Night went for another pin but pulled her up at two.
Night hooked Lynx up and nailed her with a piledriver, but again no pin. Night lifted Lynx up hooked her and nailed a craddle DDT. Lynx rolled out of the ring. Night went off the ropes and went for a suicide dive but Lynx moved and she hit the floor. The ref then counted both of them out.
RESULT - Double Countout -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH #4 - The Gruesome Twosome V/S The Twins -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The Gruesome Twosome hit the ring and went right at it. Brad took Twin #1 and nailed him with a dropkick sending him up and out of the ring. Josh grabed Twin
#2 and swent him into the ropes catching him coming off with a huge powerslam as Brad then came off the top rope with a leg drop. Brad hooked the leg for the pin but Twin #1 got back in in time to make the save. The ref was then able to get some order as he got Twin #1 and Josh out of the ring. Brad took Twin #2 and nailed him with a big samoan drop hooking the leg for another pin but pulled him up at two. Brad then tagged out to Josh who came off the top rope with a huge clothesline knocking Twin #2 all the way to his corner and Twin #1 tagged in. Twin #1 came in a house of fire nailing Josh with big dropkick. Twin
#1 then sent Josh into the ropes and went for a hurracarana but Josh revearsed it into a powerbomb. Josh then tagged out to Brad. Josh nailed a scoop and a slam and Brad came off the top rope with a big splash. Brad then pulled Twin #1 up and hooked him up and nailed The Passing Fad. Brad went for the pin and Josh made sure Twin #2 didn't come in and they got the 1-2-3!
WINNERS - The Gruesome Twosome
