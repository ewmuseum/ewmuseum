Friday Night Fallout, 13 April 2002 (part 3 of 5)

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Stand Alone" by Godsmack plays over the PA System.] [The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. A thick smoke begins to fill the wrestlers entrance, and the fans begin to cheer with a newly found appreciation for the man about to step out from the back.
The curtains at the wrestler's entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the smoke and light display is "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife. The crowd pops, but Van Strife just stands there, a rather sullen look upon his face. Without a cocky smirk, he makes his way to ringside.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Setzer Van Strife comes out without a flashy entrance... I wonder what's wrong?
SHANE PROPONE: I think we all know. He was SCREWED out of the title! JOHN WILLIAMS: It was a double countout, there's no screwjob involved. SHANE PROPONE: That's what someone in the conspiracy would say! [Van Strife is dressed in an old "Lynch Mob" t-shirt, a baggy pair of blue jeans, and a pair of wrestling boots. Van Strife is without that trademark pair of black Oakley sunglasses, but he still holds onto his Singapore Cane, which he clutches tightly.
The crowd continues to cheer, but Van Strife continues to look upset, walking straight to ringside. He reaches the ringside area, then slides underneath the bottom rope.
He then calls for a microphone, which he receives. He then waits for the crowd to hush before speaking.] SVS: The dream... was shattered. At International Incident, I didn't live up to the expectations I set for myself. All of the thoughts, all of the goals... are gone. With one ten-count, I eliminated myself from competition. The favorite to win the ICW International Title was ousted by a double countout.
[A look of disgust on Van Strife's face as he continues.] SVS: I now have to look at Tommy Lionheart as the champion of this federation. The one man that couldn't beat me unless he had the entire [BLEEP]ing roster behind him... now holds the top title. A man of _my_ talent now has to chase after a man of _his_. From my viewpoint, the tides have turned. No longer am I at the top, looking down. I'm just another vulture in the flock, trying to inch just a little further to the front. I have to sit and watch as Tommy Lionheart holds the one thing that right now, I cannot have.
That doesn't sit well with me, folks... not at all. [Van Strife paces back and forth as he continues.] SHANE PROPONE: I don't blame him, I was ready to hurl as well! JOHN WILLIAMS: Like it or not, Lionheart is the ICW International Champion! SHANE PROPONE: Don't remind me... SVS: To top it all off, I'm going into the ring tonight to fight for a title that is second- rate. Though I normally would scoff at the idea, tonight I have the chance to get a bit of vindication. To see what would have happened had there been a clear-cut winner.
Morgan Ashe... your fifteen minutes of fame ends _tonight_. You were the man who decided my fate at International Incident. You were the man that took all my hard work and crushed it. Tonight, I will be the one to break you down, and make you join my world. The darkness you live in... it pales in comparison to what you will feel tonight.
Tonight, I'm not doing it for the fans. [The crowd begins to boo, and for the first time, Van Strife smirks.] SVS: How did I know that was going to happen? So hard to judge... you people will change your feelings faster than Tommy Lee and Pamela Anderson. We all know how that one ended up. I don't know what I was thinking when I decided that I cared about a cheap pop from you all.
You're just as much to blame for my loss as Ashe is. [A _monster_ heel pop for Van Strife.] SVS: Go right ahead and boo. You honestly think that I care anymore? We all see where that went. From here on in, I do what _I_ want. I could give a rat's ass whether or not you boo. I was never regretful for anything I've done... until I decided to do what you all wanted.
Now, it's the biggest regret I've ever had to deal with. I'm glad that I've come to my senses.
Because tonight, I have nobody to be my conscience. What I do to Morgan Ashe tonight, that's for me to love... and for you all to _loathe_. Deal with it.
[Van Strife looks into the camera.] SVS: Morgan Ashe... I hope you're ready. You might have managed to squeak by with a double countout at International Incident, but tonight... I finish the job! And when I raise the ICW
Television Title in the air... you will all see that I'm back.
And Tommy Lionheart... I'll be gunning for you, sunshine. [With one last laugh, "Stand Alone" by Godsmack begins to play once again. Van
Strife raises
the Singapore Cane into the air, getting a heel pop from the crowd. He then walks to the back, and the camera cuts back to the commentators.]
SHANE PROPONE: WOOOOO! S-V-S, S-V-S... JOHN WILLIAMS: Well there goes a bitter man! He blames everyone but himself for not becomming the International Champion...
SHANE PROPONE: It wasn't his fault! It was the fault of all these idiots, they skewed his perception and made him forget what was importantm WINNING!
JOHN WILLIAMS: If it weren't for these fans he wouldn't even be here! But that's a story for another time. Right now we have a match-up to get to, so let's go to the ring...
