The Return of Friday Night Fallout, 15 March 2002 (part 2 of 4)

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fades back in to the Fleet Center...] JOHN WILLIAMS: What a night this has been thus far. We still have our Main
Event coming up in just a little while. But first it seems like we're going to have a few words from an ICW legend. "The Hammer" is in the back and has a few things to say! Good to see this superstar come out of retirement and back in the ICW!
SHANE PROPONE: What are you talking about? You mean that big ape who thinks walks around thinking he built the ICW?! He was nothing, barely a midcarder!
JOHN WILLIAMS: What are you talking about? He held multiple titles in here! SHANE PROPONE: Did he have the International title? JOHN WILLIAMS: Well, no but -! SHANE PROPONE: EXACTLY! He's a no talent bum who should fade back into obscurity! JOHN WILLIAMS: How long did it take you to learn that word? SHANE PROPONE: What -! JOHN WILLIAMS: EXACTLY! Let's go to the back for a few words from Alex Cote. SHANE PROPONE[grumbling]: Making me look......[mumble mumble] JOHN WILLIAMS: What was that Shane? [The STBtron lights up to a black screen, then switching to a a shiny black screen. The camera fades out to a boot being tied. When it is laced the camera zoom back further to see "The Hammer" Alex Cote sitting in his wrestling attire with a large grin on his face.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: [takes a deep breath] Friday.....March 15th ..... second year of the new millennium in the year two thousand and two, Anno Domini, "Our Year of our Lord" for you uneducated, or to put it in lament terms, tonight, the night an empire raised from the ashes like the mighty phoenix. The Roman Empire? Nah. The Mongolian Empire?
Don't think so! Communist Russia? Not today friends! International Championship Wrestling, also known as the I.....C.....W. A federation heard around the world. Loved by the fans, hated by other promotions. If you know wrestling, you know the ICW.
[Alex stands straight up, looking out into space rubbing his goatee.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: The time has changed, the fans have changed, and some of the wrestlers have changed, but one this is the same, one thing will ALWAYS be the same when the name "ICW" is said, and that's top notch wrestling. Tonight is the return card. Tonight it all begins anew. Friday nights, PPV's, and life's will be changed forever, starting now.
[Alex stares directly into the camera with a cold stare.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I have taken it upon myself, I have made a solemn vow to bring the ICW back to the prosperity, the reputation it once had. This is not a one night deal or is a quick business venture, this is for real. This is the big time and this will live on. Tonight I start with Alex Hawkins.
[Alex's faces changed to a big grin as be backs up and leans again the wall with his arm's crossed.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: It's not often that I'm serious. Hell, I'm a clown to say the least, but the ICW is my home. The ICW was the birth of the man called "The Hammer" Without the ICW, Alexander Monatgue Cote would be nobody expect some two bit, second class citizen who probably delivered mail or worked in a KFC with two guys named Biff and Jack. James Pillow gave me that chance. He put up with all my bullshit, my big mouth, and my stubbornness because he saw potential, he saw a man who could rule the wrestling world, and I owe him, and tonight I start to give back, I start to pay him for what he has done.
[Alex reaches into his duffle bag and pulls out a handful of picture.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: So many memories. Like this one. [Alex hold a picture up to the camera. It's a picture of Alex with the ICW
Television championship over his head with a beaten and battered
Westside Walavan holding himself up in the corner.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: My first title. Where it all started. After only a few weeks in the ICW, Pillow gave me a chance and look at the result.
[Alex pulls the picture back and puts it back in the pile. He pulls out another one and puts it in front of the camera. It's a picture of Alex handing the ICW Hardcore title over to Kuba Rico.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Boy, did that piss off Rico. Kuba was always pissed that he could never beat me. I can't lie, Kuba Rico was on of the meanest and best Hardcore champions the ICW ever had. He took me to the limit and in the end, even though I beat him, I handed the title back to Kuba right after pinning him.
[Alex puts the picture back and pulls out another. It's a picture of Alex on his knees, covered with blood and the ICW Continental title draped over his shoulder.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: What a match. I came, I saw, I conquered. I held that title 'til the end of the ICW.
[Alex puts the pictures back into his duffle bag] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: And it all has to end sometime and since that time
I've been skipping from federation to federation trying to find another home, a place where I can stay and say "This is where I want to be", and I never did find that place, nothing can replace the ICW!
But now, it's back. It has risen from the grave to take it's place at the top once again. [Alex stops and stares into the camera. His smile fades.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: There is a time for laughter, a time for fun, and a time for jokes, tonight is not one of them. Tonight I am all
business, completely serious, a machine, an iceman.......focused. I owe that to the ICW, I owe that to Pillow, and I owe that to myself. Hawkins, I haven't talked about you at all tonight, but I'm sure when you look into these eyes you see the future, you see the intensity.
Tonight, Alex Hawkins, you better prepare to be hammered because I TAKE NO PRISONERS!! [Screen fades.] [Cuts back to ringside..] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well Cote looks confident..... SHANE PROPONE: He's without a doubt THE biggest loser I have ever seen... JOHN WILLIAMS: Right, if you say so. Well let's get to our next match as Dave
Pietka takes on Higor Rosa...
