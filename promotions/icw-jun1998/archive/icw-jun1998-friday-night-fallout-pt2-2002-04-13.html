Friday Night Fallout, 13 April 2002 (part 2 of 5)

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...] [DING,DING,DING!!] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, 'Quicksilver' Jeff KEENAN! JOHN WILLIAMS: Keenan picks up a big win in his first match-up! SHANE PROPONE: Blah blah...let's just get to Van Strife's match, everything else is crap.. [Cuts to commercial...] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...] [Fades back in to the Providence Civic Center..] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Sid Starr V/S "The Dentist" Mike Wise =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 10 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... (A loud *MEOW* sounds from the PA system, then a rockabilly/swing bass kicks in, followed by a guitar and some drums... The opening strains to "Stray Cat Strut" by the Stray Cats. The instruments are soon followed by the crooning voice of Brian Setzer.)
Ooo-ooo-ooo-ooo Ooo-ooo-ooo-ooo Ooo-ooo-ooo-ooo Ooo-ooo-ooo-ooo (At this point a blonde, caucasian man in his early thirties steps through the curtains and onto the entrance aisle. No pyrotechnics or explosions go off. In fact, a simple spotlight focused on him is the only concession to "glitz". He has an athletic build with decent muscle definition and is dressed in a pair of black tights, black boots with silver detailing, a black leather jacket with "Baby, I'm a STARR"
scripted across the back with silver studs and finally a pair of black Rayban shades. He stops in the circle of the spotlight and looks around with a smirk on his face.)
Black n' orange stray cat sittin' on a fence Ain't got enough dough to pay the rent I'm flat broke but I don't care I strut right by with my tail in the air Stray Cat Strut - I'm a (Ladies' cat) I'm a feline Casanova hey man that's that Get a shoe thrown at me from a mean, old man Get my dinner from a garbage can Oh, don't cross my path (Sid looks over at the nearest camera, lowering the shades on his nose and looking over their rim with that smirk still on his face. He winks, turns up the collar on his jacket and walks slowly towards the ring, arms wide and head held high with an arrogant grin on his his face. One might even say that he's strutting.)
I don't bother chasing mice around, oh-no I slink down the alley, lookin' for a fight Howlin' to the moonlight on a hot summer night Singin' the blues while the lady cats cry Wild stray cat you're a real gone guy I wish I could be as carefree and wild But I got cat class, and I got cat style. (At this point Starr has made it down to the ring, the spotlight having followed him all the way. He leaps up onto the apron, then without missing a beat vaults over the top rope with ease and lands on his feet, doing a little strut as the music fades out.)
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... [Mike Wise, his brothers and some other men in smart suits are seen storming down the backstage hall, one brother especially close as he rubs Mike's shoulders and pep talks him a little. A bottle of water from an anonymous hand is poured over Mike's head as he parts the curtains.]
["Hundred Mile City" by Ocean Colour Scene plays as Mike Wise steps through the curtains. Mike stands at the top of the aisle staring at the ring. He then runs towards the ring, slides under the bottom rope, runs across the ring to one of the opposite corners, jumps up onto the second rope and slowly raises his hands above his shoulders as if to show off his great body to the crowd while looking out at that crowd.]
[Mike Wise jumps down from the turnbuckle and demans a mic...] Mike Wise: At International Incident you all finally seen what "The Dentist" is really capable of. Before International Incident you saw a couple of false starts. You saw
Mike Wise come to
this ring on Fallout and do ..... OK..... but I was far better than OK at the
Pay Per View.
[pause] Mike Wise: People around here have been saying how I’m not a threat, how they’ve seen me in the ring and I’m not that good. Well I proved all those people wrong. For the first time in my
ICW career I looked like a threat, I came this close....
[holds up his finger and his thumb up to show how close] Mike Wise: .....to beating Alex Cote. It was the best match of my career and whether he’ll admit it or not Alex Cote was again pushed to his limit. So I still lost but
I’m just learning
my way in this game, it was only my fourth match, "The Hammer" on the other hand has well over a hundred matches under his belt. Alex, I was just one move from putting you away.
[He stops to listen to the booing crowd.] Mike Wise: Instead though I lost my third match in a row in ICW. Yeah I’m on a losing streak but you know what? After International Incident I thought to myself "who do I blame for this?".
Do I blame myself? NO, Do I blame my opponents? NO. Instead the finger of blame is pointing directly at...... MY WIFE. You see she just keeps getting in my way. She wouldn’t keep getting in my way though if I never married the slut. If she would’ve done what she should’ve done in the first place, _HAD_ _AN_ _ABORTION_....
[YOU C**T POP] Mike Wise: ....I WOULD NEVER OF HAD TO MARRY HER. I would have done it with a metal coat hanger myself if it weren’t for her dad sticking his nose in.
[YOU EVIL BASTARD POP] Mike Wise: SHUT IT. In my mind I know that if she would’ve just aborted Jessica, walked away and forgot about me, I could’ve come out here and I wouldn’t be 0-3. I’ve only been losing because of Bunny and that seven year old mistake of ours. If it weren’t for them there wouldn’t be anything to keep ruining my focus, to keep putting me off of my wrestling.
[Looks over to Sid Starr.] Mike Wise: Tonight, my family aren’t around to get in my way. As soon as the bell rings I’m not even going to be thinking about my family and that is terrible news for you
Mr. Starr.
Tonight Starr, my losing streak ends now is that what you want to happen? BECAUSE THAT’S WHAT WILL HAPPEN. [He drops the mic] JOHN WILLIAMS: My God...Mike Wise is sick in the head! Can you believe what he just said? SHANE PROPONE: What? He's saddled with some whore who can't keep her legs closed, taking his mind off of what it should be on, winning!
[DING,DING,DING!] [Wise and Starr move in towards each other and go to lock up but Starr with a poke to the eye of Wise stops him short. The referee warns Starr who basically ignores him as he takes Wise and drags him over to the ropes. Starr leans Wise throat first over the ropes and pushes down choking him. The referee lays the count on Starr...
1... 2... 3... 4...Starr lets up as the referee gets in his face. Starr again ignores him as he takes Wise and slams him face first into the turnbuckles. Starr takes Wise and goes to shoot him across to the other side but Wise Irish whips it. Starr hits the corner and rebounds out right into a clothesline from Wise. Starr hits the canvas and quickly rolls to the floor..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr to the floor, a smart move as he breaks any momentum Wise was beginning to build...
[Starr on the floor walking around the ring. Wise goes out after him. Wise giving chase to
Starr who quickly slides into the ring. Wise follows in and Starr stomps on the back of Wise's head before he can get to his feet. Starr now pulls Wise to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Wise comes off and Starr hits a perfectly placed dropkick right on the butt of the jaw of Wise. Wise hits the canvas and Starr off the ropes and hits a big leg drop then goes for a pin...
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO....] JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise kicks out of it with POWER! [Starr thrown to the canvas but pops right back up and jumps back onto Wise who wraps his hands around the throat of Starr. Wise slowly getting up, pulling Starr up with him by the neck...]
[CROWD: OWWWWW...] JOHN WILLIAMS: A hard low blow from Starr stops Wise's attempts once again... SHANE PROPONE: Ohhh...After that I don't think Wise will have to worry about any more kids...
[Starr hooks Wise up and locks Wise in a hammerlock. Starr now from the hammerlock nails Wise with a Russian legsweep then quickly hops to his feet and drops an elbow to the back of Wise's neck! Starr rolls Wise over and goes for the pin....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRR.....Wise kicks out of it. Starr right back to his feet as he pulls Wise up. Starr takes
Wise and sends him into the ropes. Wise comes off Starr goes for a headscissor takedown but
Wise holds on and just SLAMS Starr to the canvas! Starr slowly back to his feet and Wise off the ropes...]
[CROWD: OH MY GOD POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SAY ARGH! MY GOD! WHAT A CLOTHESLINE BY WISE! [Wise hits a MASSIVE clothesline sending Starr flipping around in the air and lands face down on the canvas...]
SHANE PROPONE: Starr is flapping around on the mat like a fish out of water here.. JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise nearly took Starr's head off with that clothesline. [Starr still down as Wise pulls him up to his feet. Wise hooks Starr up and lifts him up and then just HURLS him back to the canvas with a massive Powerbomb! Wise going for the cover....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOULDER UP! Starr just barely got out of it there! Wise is dominating with power moves here....
[Wise back up and begins to pull Starr up but he punches Wise in the gut stopping him for a second. Starr now backing away from him on his hands and knees and right into the corner...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr on his knees practically begging Wise to give him a break here... [Wise moves in and goes to grab Starr but Starr grabs the tights of Wise and pulls him into the corner. Wise's crashes into the corner, his head slamming into the steel post. Starr now rolls Wise up and puts his feet on the ropes for leverage...
ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRR...The referee sees Starr's feet on the rope and gets up. Starr up and thinks he has the match won but the referee says no and points to the ropes..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr saying he had it won there but the referee caught him with his feet on the ropes..
SHANE PROPONE: I'm sure it was an accident, he was just to close to the ropes, there was no where else to put them...
[Starr turns away from the ref and gets a thumb to the eye from Wise who then lifts Starr up into a gorilla press but Starr responds in kind with a rake to the eyes of his own and drops to the mat. Starr now quickly over to the ropes and ascends to the top turnbuckle but Wise quickly over and nails Starr dazing him. Starr now sitting on the top turnbuckle as Wise steps through the ropes to the ring apron. Wise quickly up the ropes to his Starr.
Wise wrap his arms around Sid and nails a Belly to Belly Suplex all the way out to the arena floor!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! What a move by Wise! Both men hit the floor hard though! [Wise slowly starting to get to his feet and pulls Starr up along with him. Wise takes Starr and throws him back into the ring. Wise follows in and pulls Starr to his feet. Mike Wise wraps his massive arms around the waist of Starr and lifts him up and NAILS a
German Suplex!
Wise holds on and gets to his feet, ANOTHER GERMAN SUPLEX. Again Wise holds on and gets to his feet. The crowds reaction gets louder as they anticipate what he's going to do next. Some boo and others just make noise, what's Wise going to do? Wise releases his hold then pulls Sids head back and hits a reverse DDT!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a string of moves! Wise could have it won here! [Wise goes for a cover..... ONNNNNEEEEE..... TWWWWWWOOOOO...... THRRRRREEEE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: NOT YET! Starr manages to get out of it! Oh my! [Wise a bit frustrated as he pulls Starr to his feet only to drive Starr’s head back into the canvas with a Double Arm DDT. Wise again goes for the cover.
ONNNNNNEEEEE..... TWWWWWWOOOOOO..... THRRRREEEEE?] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! NO! STARR OUT OF IT AGAIN! [Wise really looking frustrated now, he gets to his feet and waits for Starr to get up on his own. Starr slowly getting up, barely able to stand. Wise off the ropes..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: SAY AR...NO! Wise sees him coming and hits a drop toe hold sending Wise throat first across the top rope!
[Starr still a little out of it as he grabs Wise by the back of his head and nails a hangman's neckbreaker! Starr now out to the apron and up to the top turnbuckle once again. Starr perched up top and leaps off..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: BIG ELBOW DROP! HE HIT THAT DEAD ON! [Starr goes for the pin.... ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRREE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: FOOT ON THE ROPE! WoW! I thought it was over right there! SHANE PROPONE: And you were wrong as usual... [Starr pulls Wise back to his feet and sends him into the corner. Starr shoots
Wise across then
follows in but Wise out of the way. Starr in the corner and turns around, Wise coming in and
Starr grabs the referee and pulls him in the way and Wise runs right into him...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr just pulled the referee in the way and got him taken out! SHANE PROPONE: That's what he gets for.. JOHN WILLIAMS: Doing his job? SHANE PROPONE: Shut up... [The referee crashes to the canvas as Starr tries to rush Wise as he is distracted but Wise sees him coming and sidesteps him. Starr turns and gets a boot to the gut. Wise hooks Starr up....]
JOHN WILLIAMS: ANAESTHESIA! HE HIT IT! [Wise goes for the pin but the referee is still down...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise has it won but the referee is down.... [Wise gets up off of Starr and checks on the ref. Wise slaps the back of the referee's head trying to wake him up. Starr meanwhile slowly starting to stir and reaches into his tights.
The referee beginning to stir as Wise turns back to Starr who throws a handful of powder into the eyes of Wise who drops to one knee blinded...The referee suddenly calls for the bell..]
[DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH! I don't think Starr realized the referee was conscious! He got caught in the act!
LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner as a result of a Disqualification, "The
Dentist" Mike WISE!
JOHN WILLIAMS: And Wise picks up his first win, it was a Disqualification win but a win nonetheless...We'll be right back!
[Cuts to commercial..] INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING RETURNS TO PAY-PER-VIEW! CANADIAN CONQUEST 2002 Sunday, May 19th 2002 LIVE! From The Molson Centre in Montreal, Canada [Fades out and back to the Civic Center..]
