Friday Night Fallout, 13 April 2002 (part 5 of 5)

13 April 2002

[Fades back in to the Providence Civic Center as we see ICW President Shaun
Riche making his
way down the aisle...]
[The president enters the squared circle, grin on his face, clutching the beautifully designed International championship against his chest. Upon entering the ring, he is granted a microphone from announcer Lance
Johnson. He speaks.]
SHAUN RICHE: Ladies and gentlemen, tonight marks the start of a new era! [He nods; the fans cheer.] SHAUN RICHE: From this day forth, the ICW has a champion – an International champion. Last week, at International Incident, eight men were given a chance, an opportunity, to make their mark and cement their name in the history books.
[Pause.] SHAUN RICHE: On a night when guts, intestinal fortitude and dedication were necessary and required, one man succeeded in his quest for the title. One man went that extra step … he gave it _that_ much more effort and determination. That one man went through three men, all of whom worthy enough to be acknowledged as _THE_ man of
ICW, and proved he had what it took to lead this great wrestling organization into the future.
[Riche paces around.] SHAUN RICHE: That one man … [He stops still.] SHAUN RICHE: … Was Tommy Lionheart! [At the sound of his name, the fans cheer. Over the PA system, “Crawling in the Dark” by Hoobastank begins.]
# I will dedicate # # And sacrifice my everything for just a second's worth # # Of how my story's ending # # And I wish I could know if the directions that I take # # And all the choices that I make won't end up all for nothing # [The lights dim as the fans rise to their feet and turn their attentions’ to the entrance way.]
# Show me it's worth # # Make me understand it # # I've been crawling in the dark, looking for the answer # [From behind the curtain steps out, to a huge ovation, the new ICW International champion, Tommy Lionheart. Tommy looks around the sold out Providence Civic Center with a grin from ear to ear; this has got to be one of proudest moments in his wrestling career.
After a few seconds of taking it all in, Tommy hobbles slowly down the aisle, sporting a tightly strapped bandage around his left knee, slapping all the out-stretched hands of the fans before walking up the ring steps and stepping into the ring. Not knowing what to do Tommy slowly climbs up onto the second turnbuckle and raises his arms in the air as a spotlight shines brightly down on him. He leaps back down, as the lights come back up, and the music fades out. The deafening “LION-HEART” chants continue, however.]
SHAUN RICHE: Ladies and Gentlemen … THE ICW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION … TOMMY LLLIOOONNHHEAAARRTT!!! [The president, delightfully, hands over the ICW’s most coveted possession to the champion. Tommy just stares directly down at the belt he has just been awarded … before raising it high above his head to a thunderous ovation!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a moment this is, folks! Tommy Lionheart – the International champion! SHANE PROPONE: Bah! He’ll make a lousy champion, no doubt about it. JOHN WILLIAMS: Give the guy a break; he deserves that title. [Tommy places his newly crowned title belt over his right shoulder before taking the microphone.]
TL: Rhode Island … your champion has arrived! [The chants start up again, Tommy continues.] TL: You know, for as long as I can remember, it has been my dream to win a world title or the equivalent of a world title belt. For years it seemed impossible. I mean, who wanted to give a young guy from
London, England a chance to shine? What was the point of seeing whether or not he could make it to the very top of the ladder?
To them, allowing me an opportunity to succeed at the highest level was unthinkable … my dream seemed like it would never come true.
[Turns to Shaun Riche.] TL: Shaun … for allowing me the opportunity, and for having the balls to let me showcase my true ability, I’d just like to say thank you.
JOHN WILLIAMS: That’s classy. [Shaun nods.] TL: When I returned to the ICW, I constantly stated how this is my home … the place I loved and the place where I have my fondest memories. It was here, in the ICW, that I became the first person, man or woman, to end Miss Jasmine’s undefeated streak, capturing the Juniorweight title at the same time. A few moments later, it was here, in the ICW, that I captured the
Continental title. And it was here, in the ICW, just last week, that I won the big one … the biggest prize in all of the ICW … the
International title. Finally, my dream has become reality! SHANE PROPONE: Woo-hoo, big deal! TL: International Incident, ICW’s comeback pay-per-view, is a show I will always remember, not just for capturing the International title, but also for defeating three superstars in one night, with an injured knee. Injured knee aside, I’d just like to say that Dave
Pietka, Alex Cote and yes, even Alex Hawkins, you all gained my respect that night – you all proved that you are some of the toughest performers in the business and, if ever you want a shot at this baby…
[Pats the belt.] TL: … You’re more than welcome to give it your best shot. [The president applauds the fighting spirit of his International champion.] TL: You know, I still can’t believe I’m the champion. It’s unbelievable. I have worked so damn hard during my career, suffering serious injuries, shedding bucket loads of sweat, losing gallons of blood, and for nothing. Sure, I’ve won a few Cruiserweight and
Juniorweight titles down the line, but nothing this prestigious … this important. [Tommy, now looking very emotional and proud continues, a little choked up.] TL: Looking back now, I wonder how I carried on for so many years – being held back, not being able to reach my full potential, and
I realize the one main factor that kept me going. It was you guys … [Tommy holds his hand out and waves it around at the fans.] TL: … The fans! Without you, there’d be no me. You’re the people that keep me strong, alive and committed. It was _YOU_ guys that won me the ICW International championship! Thank you.
[Tommy applauds the fans, who in return, show their appreciation with even more chants for the champ. Those chants permiate the arena except for one section; directly behind the announcers.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Fans, this is truly a breathtaking... SHANE PROPONE: There's some commotion going on behind us... [Suddenly, a man leaps over the barrier off to the left side of the announce booth, slides into the ring with ease, and pops up to his feet. The crowd gasps, and the man in the ring is heard screaming at Tommy.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Who is that in the ring?! SHANE PROPONE: My God! I recognize him! [The man screams at Tommy, who turns around... and ends up hitting the mat like a ton of bricks, a picture perfect superkick catching the International champion right in the jaw. Tommy drops the microphone with a thud as he hits the mat.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: MY GOD, SUPERKICK! This bastard, whomever he is, has just ruined Tommy Lionheart's moment in the sunshine!
SHANE PROPONE: That's not just any bastard! That's "The True Living Legend"! That's BRIAN J. BLOTTIE~! [A deep, satisfied grin is etched on Brian J. Blottie's face, as he reaches down, picks up the microphone, and shakes his head towards the fallen ICW champion.]
BJB: Jackass. [With a flick of the wrist, Brian sends the microphone spiraling to the mat, and as quick as he came in, he's gone, back through the crowd.]
SHANE PROPONE: My hero! JOHN WILLIAMS: Hero! He's a bastard through and through! There was no reason for him to come out here and _assault_ Tommy
Lionheart like this! There was no reason for him to even _be_ here. He's not employed by this company, dammit!
SHANE PROPONE: For some reason, I'm guessing that won't be true for long! WOO
HOO! Goodnight Tommy Lionheart! Your moment, your shining glee, has collapsed just like your body with one...
single... SUPERKICK! JOHN WILLIAMS: You're sick. Absolutely _detestable_. [Shaun Riche, over the shock of seeing the proceedings in the ring, quickly hustles to Tommy Lionheart's side, helping him to his feet. Tommy gets up, nods to Shaun while rubbing his jaw, then picks up his
International title. Without a word to the fans,
Tommy slides out of the ring, obviously disheartened. His slow ascent up the ramp to the back is met with cheers, which he acknowledges, but you can tell he's seething inside.]
[Cuts to commercial...] [Cuts to commercial..] INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING RETURNS TO PAY-PER-VIEW! CANADIAN CONQUEST 2002 Sunday, May 19th 2002 LIVE! From The Molson Centre in Montreal, Canada [Fades out and back to the Civic Center..] JOHN WILLIAMS: My god, Brian J.
