Friday Night Fallout, 3 June 2002 (part 2 of 4)

3 June 2002

[We fade into a small, dark corner, somewhere in the basement of the Air Canada
Centre in Toronto. Sitting on the floor with his head on his knees and hair dangling over appears to be Shane
Alexander. The most SADISTIC man in the ICW. Shane is wearing his usual black ring attire, but his hands are hidden behind his hair, on his chest. Shane speaks in a low and almost raspy tone.]
SA: Blottie and Pietka versus Lionheart and myself? [Shane lets out a muffled grunt.] SA: A match that _on paper_, seems like a DREAM TAG EVENT. Almost like an old superhero story. The two most popular stars against the most hated villains... sounds good right?
[Shane lifts his head and snaps his hair back, he lifts the piece of paper he must've been holding with one hand, and raises a zippo lighter with the other.]
SA: Remember... I said.. [Shane flicks the top open to the lighter.] SA: _ON PAPER_!! [Shane rolls his thumb back, igniting the lighter and sets the paper ablaze. Shane sets the flaming sheet to his side and shoots a small grin.] SA: This match is nothing more than four guys that all really don't like each other, thrown into one ring. Only to rip each other apart...
[Pauses.] SA: But my concerns are not on whether my partner is going to stab me in the back... Or if Blottie is gonna land that
SUPERKICK! No. My concerns... my emotions... my focus... all on one man. "Heavy
Mental" Dave Pietka.
[Another pause.] SA: But Dave, remember, this match looks good on paper... and we all just seen what happened to the paper.
[Shane smirks.] SA: Pietka... I suggest you don't get burned yourself tonite. [We fade out as Shane plays with that lighter, flicking it open and closed, open and closed.]

A few seconds pass over the loud speaker as the HARSH chimes and loud THUMPING of "King Nothing" by Metallica starts to blare through the P/A. Fans jump up and blurt out a thunderous, rave of screaming, encouraging chants. On the screen behind him, many pictures of Shawn
Viper holding a few of his illustrious titles. Pictures of him capturing the
OWF World title back when he had long hair, another of him holding up the AWA World title, another of one of him laying motionless across the body of a beaten
Revelations as the ref has raised Viper's hand with as he hands him the belt,
Viper busted open as he captured the IWC
World title. A shot of Viper pinning Spikyjim for the IWC International title and a famous shot of him nailing Chris
Courtade across the chin with a Stinger Kick. A few more famous shots of Viper in elegant Armani attire, a few beautiful women with Viper and a few shots of Viper rolling with his title. The music stops and a V/O is heard ...]
.......................... S ............................ S ................................V!!! .... BOOM!!! .... [A HUGE explosion is heard as "King Nothing" starts to wale over the P/A and a
EARTHSHATTERING FACE POP is given out by the fans. A blood red spotlight pans down as it flashes on the middle of the ring
... the fans are all going ballistic all over the arena, "King Nothing" continues to play and a small red spotlights continue to flash one by one all the way up the ramp towards the entrance. "King Nothing" can barely be heard.]
[... *POP* ... *POP* ... *POP* ... *POP* ... *POP* ...] [An explosion of golden fireworks begins to shoot off around the ring. The fireworks continue to shoot around the ring and the fans are going absolutely nuts with screams and shouts at the
International Icon of wrestling. The fireworks keep blasting on both sides of the ring as they meet at the foot of the ramp and continue to shoot up the ramp toward the entrance. The fireworks reach the top of the ramp and spread in a "V" shape as they hit the back wall and ...]
............. BOOM!!! ..........................BOOM!!! ......................................BOOM!!! [..... AND SHAWN VIPER ENTERS THE BUILDING!!! Shawn comes out to an incredible hero's welcome as he looks to be in tremendous shape once again. The International Icon is wearing his a new attire. Viper comes out wearing a black and red
RVD style singlet with the word "SCORPION" in red lettering, wrapping around his right side, and a picture of a red scorpion wrapping around the left side of the singlet. On the back of the singlet near the neck, in jagged lettering the initials "SSV" are printed. On his feet, black boots with the word "Scorpion"
in red lettering down the side, along with black knee pads to follow it up. And as always, The Scorpion has his $450 Ray
Ban sunglasses perched upon his head, and a smug grin on his face. Viper comes strutting out with his arrogant and flamboyant walk, Viper seems a lot more cockier and confident in himself. Viper stops at the head of the ramp and throws his arms in the air like a Greek God. Viper still has his arrogant grin on his face and an ample amount of confidence as the ladies in the crowd are noticeably heard.
Viper's entrance music is almost downed out by an UNGODLY FACE POP REACTION by the fans.] .... BOOM ... ... BOOM ... .... BOOM !!! [...... Red and black fireworks go off behind him as "King Nothing" by Metallica's harsh chimes are heard as the song begins to wind down. Viper finally heads to the ring as he graces the aisle with a playful and flamboyant strut. As Viper walks down the aisle he points to himself and flexes to the fans a bit. Viper reaches the ring and just prances up the stairs and across the apron and shoots through the ropes and explodes into a strut as he stretches into an HBK like pose in the middle of the ring, looking OOOHH-SOOO DAMN GOOD!!! Viper pulls out of his stretch and stands in the middle of the ring and gets ready for his match.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Stand Alone" by Godsmack plays over the PA System.] [The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. A thick smoke begins to fill the wrestlers entrance, as the fans in the arena that already know who is about to come out begin to boo loudly.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way down the aisle, hailing from Chicago, Illinois... standing at six feet, one inch tall and weighing in at 213 pounds... here is... "2 SMOOTH" SETZER VAAAAAAAAAN STRIIIIIIIIIIFE!!!!!!!!!! [The curtains at the wrestler's entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the smoke and light display is Van Strife himself, the crowd booing even more loudly. Van Strife stands there, his trademark cocky smirk shining through the flashy show as he raises the ICW Television Title high into the air.
Van Strife dresses in a silk anime shirt, unbuttoned with no shirt underneath. He also wears a baggy pair of black jean shorts and a pair of black wrestling boots. Accompanying the ensemble is his trademark pair of black Oakley sunglasses and his Singapore Cane.
With a grin upon his face he makes his way down to the ring, walking down to ringside ignoring the fans that wave signs in his face and threaten him. Van Strife then makes his way to the ringside area, where he jumps onto the ring apron. He then vaults over the top rope.
Van Strife then moves to his corner to prepare for his match.] JOHN WILLIAMS: This should be a great match, these two men know each other very well....And to say they dislike each other would be an understatement.
[Van Strife and Viper now in the center of the ring looking each other right in the eyes. Viper with a determined look on his face, Van Strife with a familiar grin on his face...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife may be taking this match lightly, not a smart thing to do... SHANE PROPONE: Oh please, Van Strife just knows that Viper doesn't have what it takes to beat a man of his caliber.
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife now saying something to Viper who obviously does not like what he is hearing...
SHANE PROPONE: Must be telling him what he did with his wife last night.. JOHN WILLIAMS: Oh grow up.. [Van Strife laughing in the face of Viper then slaps him right across the side of his face. Viper becomes enraged and grabs
Van Strife by the throat and drives him back into the turnbuckles. Viper with
Setzer by the throat as the referee tries to separate them from the corner but Viper ignores him. He lets go of the throat of Van Strife then lays into him with a hard open hand chop. Van Strife held up by the ropes as Viper lays in with another hard chop, the sound rings out across the arena. Viper now takes Van Strife and shoots him out across to the other side.
Van Strife hits the turnbuckles and Viper follows in but gets a boot in the face. Van Strife runs out of the corner and goes for a clothesline but Viper catches him and nails a hard powerslam! Van Strife driven into the mat as Viper hangs on for the pin....
ONNNNNNEEEEEE..... TWWWWWOOOOOO...] JOHN WILLIAMS And the TV Champ kicks out of it. Viper dominating the match in the early goings here...
SHANE PROPONE: Van Strife can turn it around whenever he wants to.. [Viper back up to his feet and pulls Van Strife up with him. Viper lifts Setzer up and hits a hard European uppercut to the jaw of the champ. Viper now takes Van Strife and shoots him into the ropes.
Setzer comes off and Viper nails a standing drop-kick. Van Strife goes down to the canvas then quickly rolls out to the arena floor to catch his breathe...]
SHANE PROPONE: That's it Setz! You control the pace of the match, you're the champ! [Van Strife moving around the ring, keeping his eye on Viper in the ring. Viper now going out of the ring after Van Strife and Setzer quickly rolls back into the ring. Viper follows in and Van Strife with a boot to the back of his head stops him short. Van Strife pulls Viper to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Viper comes off and ducks a clothesline attempt from Van Strife. Viper off the other side and Van Strife turns just in time to see Viper go to the air and nail him with a flying forearm. Van Strife goes down and Viper feeling it. Viper poised as Van
Strife gets to his feet. Van Strife turns and Viper launches in...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: STINGER KIC....NO! Van Strife ducks under it and dropkicks the back of Viper's knee!
SHANE PROPONE: YEAH BABY! I told you, anytime he wants! [Viper goes down to the canvas as he caught that dropkick as he was standing on only one leg compacting the damage even further. Van Strife back to his feet as he grabs the leg of Viper and lays a hard boot into the back of his knee. Van
Strife rolls Viper onto his stomach. The champ now off the ropes and nails a rolling knee drop across the back of the knee of Viper!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife has found his opening and is now doing the damage! [Van Strife seemingly with things in hand as he takes Viper up to his feet and backs him into the corner. Van Strife lifts the leg of Viper up over the middle rope then begins to pull down on it, wrenching it against the rope. The referee warning
Van Strife and lays the count on him but Setzer lets go before five. Van Strife now up and boots the tied up leg of
Viper...and a second time!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife just trying to knot up that leg of Viper, it's hard to wrestle if you can't even stand up...'
SHANE PROPONE: Well aren't you a master of the obvious... [Van Strife now lifts Viper up onto the top turnbuckle and climbs up after him. Van Strife lifts Viper up and hooks him up into a front face lock. Van Strife now cradles the leg and leans back....]
[CROWD: MEGA SPOT POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SUPER FISHERMANS-BUSTER! OH MY! WHAT A MOVE BY VAN STRIFE! [Viper down on the canvas from the impact of that move but Setzer also down as it took a lot out of him as well. Van Strife making his way over and onto Viper for a pinfall, he hooks the leg as the referee goes down for the count....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRRRE...] [CROWD: MONSTER FACE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: VIPER KICKED OUT OF IT! OH MY! SHANE PROPONE: Stop making it sound like such a big thing, all he's doing is compounding the punishment Van Strife is going to lay on him...
[Van Strife a little surprised that Viper kicked out but gets right back on him as he lifts Viper to his feet. Van Strife takes Viper and sends him into the turnbuckles. Van Strife follows in with a dropkick to the jaw of Viper snapping his neck back. Van Strife takes Viper now and Viper fighting back with a couple shots to
Van Strife who stops the offense of Viper with a hard kick to the knee of Viper. Van Strife now takes Viper and sends him into the ropes. Viper comes off and ducks under a clothesline attempt from Van Strife. Setzer turns around...]
<<<<WHAP~!>>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: STINGER KICK! HE NAILED IT! SHANE PROPONE: COME ON SETZ! GET UP! [Viper and Van Strife both down on the canvas as the ref looks over them...] JOHN WILLIAMS: That move was out of utter desperation, Viper unable to capitalize on it... [Viper slowly making his way up to his feet. Shawn Viper trying to shake the cobwebs loose as he makes his way over and drops a leg onto the champion then goes for a pinfall....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRREEE...] [CROWD: MASSIVE FACE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: HE GOT HIM! [The referee quickly up and giving the shoulder up gesture as the crowd quiets down in disappointment...]
SHANE PROPONE: NO HE DIDN'T! HA! JOHN WILLIAMS: Oh my! That was as close as it comes! [Viper up to his feet now and wants to finally end this. He measures Van Strife as he pulls himself up. Van Strife up and turns as Viper throws another Stinger Kick but Van Strife pulls the referee in the way who takes the kick straight on the jaw and drops to the mat in a heap!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: COME ON! Van Strife just saved his ass there... SHANE PROPONE: Not his fault that dumb referee got in the way. JOHN WILLIAMS: You truly are a moron... [Viper grabs Van Strife but Setzer with a rake to the eyes then sends Viper into the ropes. Viper comes off and both men with the same idea as they clothesline each other and both go down! The crowd begins to stir as a figure is coming down the aisle, clad in a three piece suit and a mask over his face...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: A masked man? Talk about a cliche! We all know who's under the damn mask, just show yourself!
[The masked man hits the ring and goes to Van Strife checking on him then turns and pulls Viper to his feet and hooks him up. The masked man lifts him and nails a Fisherman's Buster!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: A Fisherman's Buster! Or should I say a Simply-Best Plex..Well the 'mystery' has been solved, if he wanted to hide his identity he shouldn't have hit that move!
SHANE PROPONE: Oh please, stop spreading wild rumors... [The masked figure pulls Van Strife on top of Viper then slips out of the ring. He grabs a cup from a fan at ringside then goes over and throws the liquid in it over the head of the referee who quickly stirs. The masked man points over to Van
Strife with the pin. The referee makes his way over and makes the count... ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRREEEE...] [DING,DING,DING!] [CROWD: <Massive heel heat> BOOOOOO!!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: I can't believe this! Van Strife had to pull trick after trick to get the win here, unbelievable..
SHANE PROPONE: Bottom line is he's still the champ! That's all I need to say! [Van Strife slips out of the ring as the masked man hands him his TV Title and the two slowly make there way up the aisle as we cut to a commercial...]
[A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...] [Fades back to the arena as we see Shawn Viper being helped to his feet but in a rage he knocks away the officials trying to help him...]
[CROWD: HEEL POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: What now? SHANE PROPONE: Morlock is back! [Kenneth Morlock slips in the ring from behind Viper, he runs at Viper who turns around in time and ducks a clothesline attempt from Morlock. Morlock turns and gets floored with a Stinger Kick!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT A KICK! Morlock just dropped like a ton of bricks! [Viper now pulls Morlock to the center of the ring then goes to the ring apron and climbs up to the top turnbuckle...]
[CROWD: MASSIVE FACE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: There's Viper's wife at the top of the ramp! [Viper looks back and sees her as she smiles and cheers him on. Viper nods and turns back to the ring and leaps...]
[CROWD: MEGA SPOT POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: 4.5 STAR SCORP SPLASH! WHAT IMPACT! [Viper a little hurt from the impact of the move but makes it to his feet as he stands over the body of Morlock as his wife stands at the top of the ramp cheering along with the entire crowd as we once again cut to a commercial...]
INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING RETURNS TO PAY-PER-VIEW! CANADIAN CONQUEST 2002 Sunday June 30th LIVE! From The Molson Centre in Montreal, Canada [Fades back to ringside...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well the "masked man" as if we don't know who it is made his impact felt as he cost Shawn Viper the
Television title moments ago... SHANE PROPONE: Give me a break! Van Strife had Viper beat, he is the greatest wrestler in the world and Viper was nothing more than another notch on his belt...
JOHN WILLIAMS: I don't know why I bother...Well right now we have another match to get to as Alex Hawkins takes on Mike
Wise. Let's go to the ring for that one...
