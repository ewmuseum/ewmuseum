Friday Night Fallout, 22 March 2002 (part 5 of 5)

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= [MAIN EVENT] Alex Hawkins V/S Tommy Lionheart -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= LANCE JOHNSON: THIS NEXT MATCH IS OUR MAIN EVENT!! [CROWD: POP!!] LANCE JOHNSON: IT IS SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 60 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... # I AM! # [The pounding riffs of "R.P.M." by Sugar Ray begins blaring over the PA to a moderate face pop as the cocky Alex Hawkins emerges from the entrance portal. Hawkins is decked out in his usual ring attire: navy blue tights with a kalaidoscope-type abstract design down the outside of each leg; white boots, and white tape around his wrists. His dark brown hair is spiked up and he's
clean-shaven, as usual.] # You're beautiful # # It's all or me # # You're beautiful # # It's me! # # I will let you # # I will let you down # [Hawkins walks towards the ring, barely giving the fans any high-fives and the like. He slides underneath the bottom rope and into the ring. Alex doesn't showboat or anything, but rather, he just stands there with that cocky smirk plastered all over his face.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Crawling In The Dark" by Hoobastank begins to play over the PA system as the fans turn their heads towards the entrance way]
LANCE JOHNSON: And now introducing... hailing from London, England ... [A puff of smokes erupts as a silhouette of a man appears from the backstage area] LANCE JOHNSON: Standing SIX feet THREE inches tall ... weighing TWO hundred and THIRTY-FIVE pounds...
[The individual steps out into the arena as the fans cheer on...] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is ... TTTTOOOOOOMMMMMMMYYYYYYY LLLLLIIIIOOOONNNHHHHHEEEAAARRRTTTTT!!!!! [Tommy, sporting a grin from ear to ear, strolls down the aisleway slapping as many fans' hands along the way as he can. He paces up the steel ring steps and into the ring, where a spotlight shines down on him. He raises his arms in the air, as the music fades out]
JOHN WILLIAMS: This should be a great match-up between hot shot rookie Alex
Hawkins and
veteran Tommy Lionheart..
[DING,DING,DING!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Here we go, our Main Event is underway. Lionheart and Hawkins both looking across the ring at each other. These two had some interesting words for each other this week, and now it's time to see who can live up to them...
[Hawkins and Lionheart move towards each other. The two lock up and Lionheart with a quick go behind on Hawkins. Hawkins quickly breaks free and moves as far away from
Lionheart as he
can...]
SHANE PROPONE: What was Lionheart trying to do there? JOHN WILLIAMS: He was wrestling! SHANE PROPONE: Is that what you call it? [Hawkins waves a finger at Lionheart...] SHANE PROPONE: See Tommy, Hawkins isn't like that. Try your little come-ons on someone else.
[Lionheart not happy with Hawkins little joke as he moves in after him. Hawkins sidesteps
Lionheart and nails a drop toe hold sending Tommy crashing face first into the turnbuckles.
Hawkins right back up and drives the point of the elbow down into the spine of
Lionheart. Hawkins now taking Lionheart back up to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Lionheart comes off and Hawkins nails a standing high angle dropkick right on the jaw of Lionheart! Hawkins over and goes for the pin... ONNNNNNEEEEEE... TWWWWWOOOOOO....Lionheart kicks out of it with ease..] JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins trying to put this one away early here but Lionheart has to much left in him.
[Hawkins right back on Lionheart as he takes him to his feet and shoots him into the ropes once again. Lionheart comes off and Hawkins catches him for a powerslam but
Lionheart hooks
Hawkins up into a small package!
ONNNNNNEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRR...] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Hawkins kicks out of it! WoW! Lionheart came close to beating Hawkins after a nice reversal there!
[Hawkins quickly back up and drops an elbow to the back of Lionheart's head! Hawkins now takes Lionheart up but Lionheart with a jawbreaker on Hawkins stops him in his tracks. Lionheart now takes Hawkins and lifts him up and hits a shoulder breaker. Lionheart holds on driving his knee into the shoulder of Hawkins trying to separate it. Lionheart then lifts
Hawkins back up
and spins around hitting a powerslam! Lionheart up to his feet and hooks the arm of Hawkins and locks on a Fujiwara Armbar!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart trying to tear Hawkins arm right off at the shoulder! [Lionheart cranking back on the hold, Hawkins in tremendous pain! The referee down checking on
Hawkins. The ref asks Hawkins if he wants to give it up but Alex shakes him off. Hawkins trying to fight out of it. He manages to roll onto his knees but Lionheart still with the hold locked on. Hawkins with his free hand lays in a couple shots to Lionheart but
Lionheart uses
his leg strength to roll Hawkins back over and drives his leg down into the chest of Hawkins then releases the hold. Lionheart back to his feet and lifts Hawkins up with him. Lionheart lifts Hawkins up and rams Hawkins shoulder first into the turnbuckle. Lionheart now lifts
Hawkins back up and drops him down face first across the top turnbuckle!
Hawkins down to the canvas in the corner. Hawkins sitting in the corner and Lionheart take a few steps back then runs in and nails a seated dropkick to the face of Hawkins. Hawkins down and
Lionheart goes
for the pin....
ONNNNNNNEEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: KICKOUT! Lionheart taking it to Hawkins here, wearing him down like the ring veteran he is. Hawkins needs to get something going here or it will be over for him...
[Lionheart pulls Hawkins back to his feet and locks on an arm bar. Lionheart now with a couple hard shoulder blocks to the shoulder of Hawkins. Lionheart now runs over hanging onto the arm of Hawkins and leaps over the top rope and drops down to the floor pulling
Hawkins arm down
across the top rope! Lionheart now reaches in and pulls Hawkins out to the floor. Lionheart takes Hawkins and goes to whip him into the steel steps but Hawkins able to reverse it and pulls Lionheart in and hits a hard back elbow to the jaw of Lionheart. Hawkins quickly capitalizes on that as he hooks up the head of Lionheart then jumps up and springboards off the apron and spins around drilling Lionheart's head into the arena floor with a Tornado DDT!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins with a big move there and that could change the tempo here... SHANE PROPONE: Elton is being shown the ropes by the rookie now. [Hawkins now pulls Lionheart up and drags him over to the railing. Hawkins hooks Lionheart up and nails a Russian Legsweep driving the back of Lionheart's skull into the steel railing!
Hawkins now pulls Lionheart back up and throws him into the ring. Hawkins follows in and goes for the pin....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Lionheart kicks out of it! Tommy is still in this one,
Hawkins needs to
come up with a big move here if he wants to put it away...
[Hawkins pulls Lionheart up to his feet and whips him into the corner. Hawkins follows in with a clothesline then lifts Tommy up to the top turnbuckle. Hawkins climbing up after Lionheart and pulls him up. Hawkins with Lionheart by the waist...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What is he going for here? [Hawkins leaps off with Lionheart by the waist and drives him throat first across the top rope with a modified Hot Shot!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: MY GOD! HE MAY HAVE CRUSHED LIONHEART'S WINDPIPE WITH THAT MOVE! SHANE PROPONE: It's called the Necksplitter for a reason...See, you're not the only one who knows move names.
[Lionheart down on the mat clutching his throat, gasping for air as Hawkins goes for the pin, hooking the leg of Lionheart....
ONNNNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRRREEE.....] [CROWD: MASSIVE POP!!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! MY GOD! LIONHEART GOT THE SHOULDER UP! That had to be out of pure instinct!
SHANE PROPONE: He's just delaying the inevitable, hard to wrestle when you can't breathe. [Hawkins pulls Lionheart back to his feet. Hawkins hooks Lionheart up and goes for a reverse
DDT but Lionheart somehow able to fight free. Lionheart breaks away and jumps up quickly nailing an enzuguri to the back of Hawkins head! Hawkins down to the canvas and
Lionheart down
as well...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart bought himself some time. He needs to get his wind back now and see if he can make a run at it.
[Lionheart and Hawkins down. Lionheart still trying to catch his breath. Hawkins already starting to move. He makes his way to the corner and pulls himself up. Hawkins over and pulls
Lionheart up as he was beginning to get up. Hawkins sends Lionheart into the ropes and goes for a clothesline as he comes off but Lionheart ducks it and hits a reverse neck breaker on
Hawkins. Lionheart with an arm across Hawkins for the pin....
ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRRREEE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOULDER UP! HAWKINS GOT THE RIGHT SHOULDER OFF THE CANVAS JUST IN TIME! WOW! What a match this has been! Hawkins is showing the world what he is made of! [Lionheart looks up at the referee in utter disbelief. Tommy slowly back up and pulls Hawkins up to his feet but Hawkins with a low blow on Lionheart. Hawkins to his feet and hooks
Lionheart up and hits a reverse underhook DDT!] JOHN WILLIAMS: HALO BREAKER! He may have it won! [Hawkins up and says that is it! Hawkins over and out to the ring apron...] SHANE PROPONE: He's gonna finish this now! [Hawkins up on the top turnbuckle. He looks down at Lionheart and measures him. Hawkins leaps....] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOOTING STAR PRE...NO! LIONHEART GOT THE KNEES UP! [Hawkins comes crashing down across the knees of Lionheart then rolls off to the canvas trying to catch his breath now as Lionheart slowly gets to his feet. Lionheart sees
Hawkins down and
goes up to the top turnbuckle. Lionheart measures Hawkins and leaps...]
SHANE PROPONE: NOOOOO!! [CROWD: MEGA POP~!] JOHN WILLIAMS: LION'S DEN!!! HE HIT IT! [Lionheart hits the Lion's Den on Hawkins and hooks the leg for the pin.... ONNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRRRRREEEE....] [DING,DING,DING!!!] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, Tommy LIONHEART!!! JOHN WILLIAMS And Lionheart picks a big win! He shows that he is a contender for that International Title! SHANE PROPONE: Yeah, he's a contender all right...still not a winner though. JOHN WILLIAMS: We could see a big rematch between Lionheart and Van Strife in the finals, what a match that would be! But there is plenty of competition on the way to the finals, including this man, Alex Hawkins!
[Lionheart up with his hands raised by the referee. Hawkins slowly to his feet and drills
Lionheart with a superkick to the back of his head!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! HAWKINS WITH A PEARL HARBOR JOB ON LIONHEART! SHANE PROPONE: Get that nancy boy! [Hawkins stomping away on Lionheart! Hawkins pulls Lionheart up and hits another Halo Breaker!
Hawkins now rolls out to the floor and reaches under the ring...] JOHN WILLIAMS: What the? Where did he get those? Did he rob Elton John? SHANE PROPONE: They must be Tommy's! [Hawkins with a pair of very eccentric sunglasses in hand as he rolls in the ring. Lionheart starting to get up but Hawkins with a leg drop to Tommy driving him back to the canvas!
Hawkins now putting those sunglasses on the face of Lionheart. Hawkins now standing over Lionheart looking down at him..] SHANE PROPONE: Those sunglasses work on Tommy! HA! [CROWD: MASSIVE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: What the? Is that.... SHANE PROPONE: Oh dear God no...Not HIM! JOHN WILLIAMS: It's The Masked Superstar JOBBER! Former International Champion! [A masked figure runs down the aisle and slides into the ring. Hawkins tries to nail a clothesline but TMSJ ducks under it. Hawkins turns around and gets drilled with a savat kick right on the jaw! Hawkins hits the mat and rolls out of the ring. Hawkins looking into the ring as he holds his jaw. Jobber checking on Lionheart...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: This must be Lionheart's big surprise! SHANE PROPONE: Why!? Why this freak!? JOHN WILLIAMS: What a night this has been! We are out of time! We'll see you at
Madison Square
Garden when we crown an International Champion! GOODNIGHT!
[Camera slowly pans back as Hawkins backs up the ramp as Jobber helps Lionheart up to his feet...]
[Fades out..]
