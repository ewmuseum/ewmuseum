Friday Night Fallout, 3 June 2002 (part 3 of 4)

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- "The Dentist" Mike Wise V/S Alex Hawkins =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 10 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... [Mike Wise, his brothers and some other men in smart suits are seen storming down the backstage hall, one brother especially close as he rubs Mike's shoulders and pep talks him a little. A bottle of water from an anonymous hand is poured over Mike's head as he parts the curtains.]
["Hundred Mile City" by Ocean Colour Scene plays as Mike Wise steps through the curtains. Mike stands at the top of the aisle staring at the ring. He then runs towards the ring, slides under the bottom rope, runs across the ring to one of the opposite corners, jumps up onto the second rope and slowly raises his hands above his shoulders as if to show off his great body to the crowd while looking out at that crowd.]
[Wise hops off the turnbuckle and stands in his corner as he waits for the match to start...]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... # MY DANCE HAS PASSED! # [As soon as Daryl Palumbo's opening screams to "Motel of the White Locust" by
Glassjaw shriek over the PA, the crowd bursts into a good-sized heel pop.]
# Combine the throb within the head # # With the rhythm of my fucking feet # # Say a novena for all those lost # # And read the bloodstains on the sheets # # I've whored myself for less than this # # And I've prayed to appear to fed # # As I knelt on my pillow God # # I clenched my fists and banged my head # [Out from behind the curtains emerges Alex Hawkins, sporting a mini-smirk on his face as he walks towards the ringside area. Hawkins looks ready as usual, hair all spiked with the gear all set.]
# Who could ever... # # Who the fuck could ever take the place of me? # LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way to the ring, from Sacramento, California...weighing in at 229 pounds...here is... ALLLLLEX HAWWWWWWWKINNNNNNS! [Hawkins makes his way towards the ring, paying no mind to the taunts and boos of the fans. However, as he goes up the steps on the ring, he looks out at a single fan with an obnoxious smirk. After uttering some kind of obscenity at said fan, Hawkins enters the ring.]
[DING,DING,DING!] [Hawkins and Wise move to the middle of the ring. Both men eyeing each other as they lock up and Hawkins pulls back and thumbs Wise in the eyes. Hawkins then nails a hard knee to the gut of Wise then drags him over and rakes his eyes across the top rope. Hawkins now lays Wise's throat over the top rope and leans on him as the referee lays the count on him but
Hawkins lets go before he is disqualified. Hawkins now takes Wise and sends him into the ropes. Wise comes off and ducks a clothesline from Hawkins. Hawkins turns and Wise boots him rather low then hooks Hawkins and hits a reverse neckbreaker!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: I think the winner of this match will be who can cheat better...I wonder who the crowd dislikes the most.
SHANE PROPONE: Who cares what those morons think? [Wise now on the chest of Hawkins and begins to lay into him with a series of hard right hands to his forehead. Wise now choking Hawkins with his forearm across the windpipe of Hawkins as the referee now counts him...
1... 2... 3... 4... 5...The referee warning Wise who finally lets the hold go and pulls Hawkins to his feet.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well Hawkins seemingly the smarter of the two, Wise seems to be going on pure emotion and that could cause him to get himself disqualified...
[Wise now hooks Hawkins and hits an arm drag. Hawkins down but he quickly kips up to his feet, a cocky smirk on his face as Wise on the other hand does not look pleased. The two lock back up and Wise with another arm drag only this time he holds on and applies an arm bar. Wise wrenching on the hold with all he has as
Hawkins tries to fight out of it. Hawkins fights to his feet and throws a back elbow breaking the hold. Hawkins now off the ropes and goes for a cross body but Wise catches him then falls forward landing on Hawkins crushing him between himself and the canvas. Wise hooks the leg for the pin...
ONNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRR...Hawkins lifts the right shoulder off the canvas before the three...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise so far able to keep on the offense using his superior size and strength, but the longer this match goes the more it will slide in the favor of Hawkins...
[Wise pulls Hawkins up and sends him into the ropes. Hawkins comes off and Wise goes for a powerslam but Hawkins able to slip out of his grasp and onto his feet. Wise turns and Hawkins nails a jumping front kick right on the jaw of Wise knocking him to the canvas. Wise up to one knee and Hawkins off the ropes and hits a seated dropkick to the face of Wise sending him back down to the canvas. Hawkins now going for the pin...
ONNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRREE...Wise kicks out of it with some power as Hawkins gets right back up. Hawkins lifts Wise up who nails a hard shot to the gut of Hawkins then locks on a side headlock. Hawkins fighting out of it but Wise with takeover takes Hawkins down to the canvas putting more pressure on the hold...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise with some good technical wrestling here as he leans on
Hawkins with this hold...
[Hawkins again looking for an escape but no luck. Hawkins now able to wrap his arms around Wise and leans back using Wise's own hold against him for a pin...
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRREE...] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! Wise able to roll out of the pin but it was almost put away right there...
[Wise now switches over to a waistlock on Hawkins. Wise rolls over and bridges
Hawkins into a pin...
ONNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRREEE...] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! Hawkins out of it once again! Wise getting frustrated now, that temper we have seen many times showing through..
[Wise lifts Hawkins up into the corner and gets in his face yelling at him. Wise then brings his hand back and slaps Hawkins hard across the jaw!] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! Wise just slapped the taste out of Hawkins mouth. [Wise now pulls Hawkins out of the corner and shoots him across to the other side. Wise follows in with a clothesline but
Hawkins out of the way and Wise hits the corner. Hawkins from behind rolls Wise up and hooks the tights...
ONNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRREEE...] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOULDER UP! Oh my, Hawkins using the tights but still unable to put it away..
[Hawkins to his feet and goes out to the apron and up to the top turnbuckle. Hawkins up top as he waits for Wise to get up. Wise up and turns, Hawkins leaps for a cross body block but Wise hangs on and nails a HUGE Powerslam! Wise with the cover....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRREEE...Hawkins gets the shoulder up before the three. Mike gets up and brings Hawkins to his feet as well. Mike
Wise hoists Hawkins up onto his shoulder and walks to one of the four corners. Mike charges, leaps and NAILS A RUNNING POWERSLAM. Another Cover.... ONNNNNEEEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRREEE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! Hawkins out of it again! Oh my! [Wise now obviously frustrated. Mike on top of Hawkins and starts laying punches in on him. Wise pulls Hawkins up and sends him into the ropes but Hawkins grabs the top rope and stops himself. Wise charges after him but Hawkins drops his shoulder and back body drops Wise to the arena floor! Hawkins following out to the ring apron. He looks down at Wise then turns his back to him. Wise slowly pulls himself up by the ring apron and
Hawkins with a hard kick knocks him back a bit.
Hawkins now springs off the middle turnbuckle and goes for an Asai moonsault and hits it but his left leg hits the guard rail..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a move by Hawkins but he is down as well and favoring his left ankle....
[Hawkins slowly up to his feet, he steps down on his left foot but a shock of pain crosses his face as he drops to the floor. Wise now getting up and pulls Hawkins up and rolls him into the ring.
Wise follows in and pulls Hawkins to his feet who hobbles on one leg. Wise now goes off the ropes...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: SAY ARGH! OH MY! HE NEARLY TOOK HAWKINS HEAD OFF! [Wise goes for the cover... ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOO..... THHHHHRRRRRE....Hawkins gets his foot on the bottom rope just in time as the referee sees it and stops the count...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins out of instinct stops the count with his foot on the rope but I don't know if he can continue here.
[Wise with a look of utter frustration on his face as he exits the ring and tosses Lance Johnson out of his seat and grabs the chair. Wise slides into the ring, chair in hand. The referee tries to stop him but Wise just shoves him down to the canvas. Hawkins on all fours trying to get up as Wise lifts the chair up and...]
<<<<WHACK!!!!>>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! WHAT A CHAIR SHOT BY WISE! [The referee calls for the bell obviously disqualifying Wise as he throws the chair down on top of Hawkins then spits on him before exiting the ring...]
LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner as a result of a Disqualification, Alex HAWKINS! JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins picks up the win by Disqualification here and Wise...snapped to say the least. Well up next we have our big cage match as Jeff Keenan looks for revenge against Sid Starr and a shot at the TV Title. We'll be right back...
[Cuts to commercial..] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...] [Cuts back to ringside as we see the Steel Cage being lowered down over the ring..] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well it is time, Sid Starr and Jeff Keenan will now step into the steel cage! This will be brutal to say the least! Let's go to the ring and watch the carnage unfold...
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Cage Re-Match] For a shot at the ICW Television Title at Canadian Conquest Sid Starr V/S 'Quicksilver' Jeff Keenan WRITTEN BY - [name withheld] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL AND IS A STEEL CAGE MATCH! [CROWD: EXTREME POP!!] LANCE JOHNSON: TO WIN YOU MUST PIN YOUR OPPONENT, MAKE HIM SUBMIT OR ESCAPE THE CAGE! INTRODUCING FIRST.. [A loud *MEOW* sounds from the PA system, then a rockabilly/swing bass kicks in, followed by a guitar and some drums...
The opening strains to "Stray Cat Strut" by the Stray Cats.] LANCE JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen. Introducing, from the City of Angels, Los
Angeles, California... He stands six feet, two inches tall and weighs in tonight at 224 lbs. He is the self-proclaimed coolest thing in the ring. He is...
... SID STAAAAAAAARR!!! [At this point a blonde, caucasian man in his early thirties steps through the curtains and onto the entrance aisle. No pyrotechnics or explosions go off. In fact, a simple spotlight focused on him is the only concession to "glitz". He has an athletic build with decent muscle definition and is dressed in a pair of black tights, black boots with silver detailing, a black leather jacket with "Baby, I'm a STARR" scripted across the back with silver studs and finally a pair of black Rayban shades. He stops in the circle of the spotlight and looks around with a smirk on his face.
Sid looks over at the nearest camera, lowering the shades on his nose and looks over the rim, that smirk still on his face.
Giving the camera a wink he turns up the collar on his jacket and walks slowly towards the ring, arms wide and head held high with an arrogant grin on his his face. One might even say that he's strutting. Meanwhile the fans' booing is competing with the music in an attempt to drown each other out.]
#I don't bother chasin' mice around, oh-no #I slink down the alley, lookin' for a fight #Howlin' to the moonlight on a hot summer night #Singin' the blues while the lady cats cry #Wild stray cat you're a real gone guy #I wish I could be as carefree and wild #But I got cat class, and I got cat style. [At this point Starr has made it down to the ring, the spotlight having followed him all the way. He leaps up onto the apron, then without missing a beat vaults over the top rope with ease and lands on his feet, doing a little strut as the music fades out.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... [The lights in the arena suddenly go out, as a faint swooshing noise is repeated over the speakers. A recording of an orgasming girl saying, 'Ooh baby' is then played, as blue spotlights shine down from the rafters, and begin doing figure-eights in the spot directly under the video-wall. The wall suddenly erupts in a plethora of color, as
'Sober' by Tool
blares throughout the arena.]
[The videowall suddenly changes to a black and blue ying-yang, with the letters
'JK' written across it is silver cursive, that is engulfed by flames. The song pauses for a moment as the word
"Quicksilver" is whispered. The song kicks back on as the word echoes in the background. Silver pyros suddenly explode off. When they die down, we see a familiar individual standing there.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Hailing from Beverly Hills,California...Weighing in at 266 pounds here is, 'Quicksilver' Jeff KEENAN!!
[The crowd erupts!!! Blue pyros then explode, as he makes his way down the aisle and into the ring. Keenan eyes Starr as he waits for the bell to ring...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What the hell is this? SHANE PROPANE: What, the champ can't look on? *[BIG TIME HEEL POP!]* [Setzer Van Strife then steps from behind the curtain wearing regular street clothes. He makes his way down to the ring.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: The odds are already stacked in Starr's favor. This is ridiculous! [Setzer stands at ringside watching as the two men in the ring are about to begin their war. Keenan shoots a quick look at
Setzer, followed by some very choice words.] *[DING, DING, DING]* JOHN WILLIAMS: Here we go, this match is going to be a good one. SHANE PROPANE: Yeah... And it's in the CAGE!! I can't wait to see Keenan get his ass kicked once again.
[The two men lock up in the center of the ring. Starr powers Keenan to the corner where the ref is asking for a break.
Starr gives it to him, but shoots a devlish smirk towards Keenan.] JOHN WILLIAMS: I don't know what that means, but Starr has to have something up his sleeves.
SHANE PROPANE: WHAT?! He is just a good sportsman, that's all. [Keenan comes out of the corner and attempts to lock up again but is met with a kick to the gut. Followed by a hard forearm to the back of the neck. And another, forcing Keenan to one knee.]
SHANE PROPANE: WOOHOO! Get 'em Starr, make him bleed. JOHN WILLIAMS: How about some biased commentating? [Starr now lifts up Keenan and whips him into the ropes with an irish whip. He follows in with a clothesline... but Jeff ducks it and hits the other side. He comes off the ropes and makes a leaping jump... AND HITS A CROSS BODY SPLASH!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Nice counter to the missed clothesline. [Keenan now with Starr on the mat and begins to lay some hard boots to the back of the head of Starr. Keenan grabs Starr by the legs and appears to be going for...]
SHANE PROPANE: Could be a Boston Crab here, trying to take out those legs early. [CRAASH!] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO!! KEENAN JUST SLINGSHOTTED STARR INTO THE CAGE... HEAD FIRST!!! [Starr hits the cage and immediately drops, but Keenan is still on the offensive. He lifts Starr to his feet and sends him into the ropes and connects with a beautiful dropkick.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Textbook dropkick there Shane... SHANE PROPANE: Shut up John, just shut up. [Keenan seems to be taunting Setzer Van Strife on the outside, saying he is next. Starr now to his feet and dropkicks
Keenan in the back of the knee.] SHANE PROPANE: Jeff Keenan just got a taste of his own medicine. He shoulda been paying attention to Starr in the cage, not Setzer outside.
[Starr lays some hard boots to the back of Keenan's knee. And now hits an elbow, and another, and ANOTHER! Three quick elbows to the back of that knee.]
SHANE PROPANE: See that... knows how to work the bad parts! JOHN WILLIAMS: Is that what you call it? Thanks for your input Shane. [Sid now drags Keenan towards the cage and lifts his throat onto the bottom rope. He begins to choke the life out of
Keenan by pushing his boot onto Keenan's head.] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH NO... NOT THAT! SHANE PROPANE: OH YES... _THAT_!!! *[HARDCORE HEEL POP!]* [Starr now grabs Keenan by the back of the head and rakes it across the cage from side to side. Keenan's face is almost immediately busted open, looks like above the right eye.]
SHANE PROPANE: YES! We got blood! And it's Keenan that's we have. JOHN WILLIAMS: Sid Starr is really taking it to Jeff Keenan here.. all on Van
Strife's command from OUTSIDE the cage.
[Starr lifts Keenan to his feet but not in the ring, Keenan is positioned on the outside of the ropes... AGAINST THE CAGE!
Starr hits three forearms to the back of Jeff's head, then hits the ropes on the other side and come in with a head of steam...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY GOD!! SHANE PROPANE: YES.. YES.. AND I DO MEAN _YEEESSS_!!! <CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH!!> [STARR DROPKICKS THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF KEENAN! SLAMMING HIS FACE INTO THE
CAGE!! And Starr motions to the crowd that he is going out. He then ascends the cage...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Here goes Starr, the first attempt at trying to win this thing here. SHANE PROPANE: Yes he's gonna do it, he is going to bury Keenan's hopes and dr.. JOHN WILLIAMS: NO!! Look in the cage Shane. [As Starr makes it 3/4 of the way up, some how Keenan grabs his leg out of nowhere. Starr is trying to kick him off but can't Keenan manages to pull Starr down... and his lands crotch first on that top rope.]
*[BIG TIME FACE POP!]* [Keenan seems to be getting his second wind now as he pulls himself towards the crotched Sid Starr, he grabs Starr by the head and...]
SHANE PROPANE: DISQUALIFY HIM NOW!! JOHN WILLIAMS: Sorry Shane, payback! <CRRAAASSSHHH! CRRRAAAASSSSHHH! CRRAAAASSSHHH! CRRAAAASSSHHH!> *[HARDCORE PAYBACK FACE POP!]* [KEENAN SLAMS STARR'S HEAD OFF THE CAGE ONCE, TWICE, TRICE, AND A FINAL FOURTH
TIME!!! Starr falls off the rope and he is also BUSTED OPEN NOW!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: And there goes Keenan, making HIS first attempt at a win. [Keenan is ascending the cage now... he makes it to the top but...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Now... he is NOT in this match, he has no business up there. SHANE PROPANE: He's just checking the errrr... lights?!?! [Setzer Van Strife is atop the cage as well, and he begins to lay some right hands to the head of Keenan. Van Strife now with a quick elbow and...]
SHANE PROPANE: TIMBEEEER! [Jeff Keenan goes crashing to the mat all the way from the top of the cage. Van
Strife descends the cage and seems to pull out a pair of brass knuckles from his pants pockets.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: This is getting out of hand. What business does he have out here anyways? SHANE PROPANE: There you go Setzer, do as you wish. *[HUGE HEEL POP]* [Van Strife now tosses those knucks in the ring and Sid Starr who is now to his feet sees what has just happened. Keenan is still down.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: And Starr just picked up those knucks. This can NOT vote well for Jeff Keenan!
SHANE PROPANE: Na na na na, na na na na, hey hey hey... JOHN WILLIAMS: NOT SO FAST!! [DAVE FRESCO JUST JUMPED OUT OF THE CROWD AND STARR SEES HIM! Starr is jolting with Fresco on the outside, telling Dave that he is next.]
SHANE PROPANE: Sid, BEHIND YOU! [Keenan is up and lays a boot to the mid-section of Starr, followed by a skull crushing DDT! The brass knuckles come loose and hit the ring's floor.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: I think Keenan just noticed those brass knuckles. Why yes he did, and now he has them on his fist.

SHANE PROPANE: NOOOOOO!!! *[HUGE EAR SHATTERING FACE POP!]* <WHAAAAAACK!> [... AND KEENAN NAILS STARR RIGHT INBETWEEN THE EYES WITH THOSE BRASS KNUCKLES!!! Starr drops and looks to be unconciouss. Keenan drops the knuckles and looks towards the top of the cage.] JOHN WILLIAMS: He is gonna do it, all he's gotta do is climb the cage. [Keenan begins to climb the cage, but on the outside... so is Van Strife! The two men meet at the top of the cage. They begin to exchange rights and lefts. Neither man wants to budge.]
SHANE PROPANE: Keenan is going to drop and when he does.. JOHN WILLIAMS: Oh Shaaaane... look up again. [Somehow Keenan has got Van Strife reeling, righthand.. right hand.. left.. AND A HUGE ELBOW OF HIS OWN! SENDING SETZER CRASHING TO THE MAT BELOW!! AND SID STARR IS STILL OUT COLD FROM THE KNUX!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Just climb down Jeff, you got the win. SHANE PROPANE: I hope he slips! [Keenan is still sitting on the cage, making no effort to climb down. Instead he seems to be telling Fresco to do something, and Fresco gladly abliges, going under the ring for something...]
SHANE PROPANE: What the hell is he getting? *[HARDCORE GOT SOME WOOD FACE POP!]* JOHN WILLIAMS: There is a table... DAVE FRESCO HAS A TABLE! [And Dave sets the table up at about mid ring where Keenan is still perched atop. Fresco now picks up Setzer and lays him on the table. Fresco holding Setzer's legs... KEENAN'S GONNA FLY!!!]
<CRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAASSSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHHHHH!!!> [HO-LY SHIT! HO-LY SHIT!] [HO-LY SHIT! HO-LY SHIT!] [HO-LY SHIT! HO-LY SHIT!] [HO-LY SHIT! HO-LY SHIT!] [A 20 FOOT FROG SPLASH DROP THROUGH THE TABLE... AND SETZER VAN STRIFE!!!! THE

[The camera zooms to where Setzer Van Strife and Jeff Keenan lay, in shattered wood. Keenan slowly gets to his feet and goes to the timekeeper and grabs the ICW Television Title belt. INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING RETURNS TO PAY-PER-VIEW! CANADIAN CONQUEST 2002 Sunday June 30th LIVE! From The Molson Centre in Montreal, Canada [The shot cuts to the backstage area.] ***** CRASH~! ***** [The sound of objects crashing to the ground are heard somewhere outside the shot. The camera pans around to view the source of the noise... and finds it as standing over broken camera equipment is the ICW Television Champion, "2 Smooth"
Setzer Van Strife. Van Strife looks angrily into the camera, walking with a hobble and holding his midsection in pain. Looking directly into the camera, he begins to speak with a tone of complete and utter frustration and anger.]
SVS: Congratulations, mother [BLEEP~!]er. You've just won a Television Title shot at Canadian Conquest. Not only that, but you've also crossed the line. This is now about more than just terminating you in ICW...
This is about terminating your career for _good_. [A slight smirk emerges from the winces of pain.] SVS: You think you're a big shot now, Keenan? You think that by knocking me off the cage, you've got a hightened shot at winning _my_ title? You want to up the ante in this little game? Then ante up...
Canadian Conquest. You... me... and a leather strap. Before you get any sexual fantasies in your head, let me clarify. You want a shot at _this_? Then you're going to do this by _my_ rules. A Strap Match. Bound by the hand, you'll truly earn the title if you beat me.
Do you think you can knock me out long enough to drag me across the ring to touch the turnbuckles? Do you think you have the experience to be able to wear me down? Now's you're chance to shine, kiddo.
[Van Strife grabs his midsection again.] SVS: You might have bruised me, but I'm not broken. At Canadian Conquest, your time will be up. There will be no more talking, no more attacks, and no more obstacles to go through. It's you... and me... once and for all.
We'll see you in Montreal, bitch. [With that, Van Strife kicks some loose equipment out of his way, then walks off as we cut back to the ring.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! A LEATHER STRAP MATCH FOR THE TV TITLE! SHANE PROPONE: Van Strife is finally gonna rid the ICW of Keenan once and for all! I LOVE IT!
JOHN WILLIAMS: That's two title matches already lined up for the Pay-Per-View. And now we will see the two men who will fight for the North American title go at it but in a Tag Team Match! This is our Main Event, let's go to the ring!
