Introduction Ok, obviously this is a FAQ for an E-Fed that goes by the initials of ICW. So read it over, if interested fill out the app and send it over. A. What is ICW? The ICW is a promotion based out of Rhode Island but it runs all across the US and even other countries on occasion, hence the name *International* Championship Wrestling. B. Who runs ICW? That'd be me, [name withheld]. The one and only....Not saying it's a good thing. I've done the fed-head thing several times in the past...Some were good, some were bad, and some were even great. Hopefully this try will fall under either the first or the last on the list... =================================================== 2. Shows A. Weekly Shows * The Lowdown I think the name speaks for itself, this will be the flash show where everyone will have the chance to say what's on there mind. * Friday Night Fallout This will be the weekly showcase where titles will be up for grabs, and famous/infamous and even maybe even legendary feuds will be kindled. As the name states this show will take place on Friday's except for the week prior to a Supercard where it will take place on Monday. B. Supercards These will take place once ever 6 weeks. This will be where champions are made and legends are born. Friday Night Fallout will have plenty of action but they will pale in comparison to what will happen on these nights.
=================================================== 3. Titles A. International Championship This is the big one, the title everyone strives to hold. Only the best of the best will have the honor to be the Champion, and he will have his work cut out for him.
This title will be defended at least once a month, not counting Supercards where it will defended...no if's and's or but's about it.
B. ICW North American Championship This is one step away from the big one. The North American Champion by rights in the number 1 contendor to the International Title, but don't let that cloud your mind bcause if you hold this title you will have plenty of people waiting to take it away from you.
C. ICW Television Championship This is the lesser of the three championships, but it shouldn't be looked as that as to hold it for any length of time is not an easy task as you are required to defend it on a weekly basis.
Those who hold and defend this title well usually go on to become stars. =================================================== 4. Characters A. Owner/CEO - James Pillow Pillow is known throughout the wrestling world both in the ring and out. James ran two major promotions in the past including the original inception of the ICW as well as the LVPWA. Though known to run things on and off camera this time around he is staying off camera, unless absolutely neccesary.
B. President - Shaun Riche A close friend of James' he's been designated to handle the day to day running of the ICW. He's a rather cocky type but he can get down to business when need be. He's no push over, that's for sure.
C. Backstage Interviewers * Max Power He's cool, he's cocky...he got his name off of a blow dryer, he's Max Power! He asks the tough questions and looks good doing it.
D. Ring Announcer * Lance Johnson He stands in the ring with a mic in his hand and announcers the wrestlers...Do we really need any more information?
F. Commentators * John Williams Williams is a vet at the commentary gig. He spent many years bouncing around the world of wrestling before finding his way into the original inception of ICW and later the LVPWA and now he's back in the ICW. Williams is the play by play man, but after much teasing by commentary partners he grew out of his shell and will speak his mind.
* Shane Propone Another ICW oldie, he sat by Williams side from the start...pokeing fun at him and anything and anyone else he felt like. Propone is your basic brash, arrogant guy that can talk a great game but doesn't have much to back it up. He's also a rather charming, and good looking...which he likes to use to his advantage if it means getting his butt out of trouble.
=================================================== 5. Role-playing/Angles This is a big part in the league, you don't flahs you don't win...Simple enough. But it is not the only measure of success. Angles will play a large role in outcomes of many matches.
So if you lose a match one week and feel you out-roleplayed your opponent keep in mind that the lose may have been neccesary to further an angle and you may have lost that week but it's what happens in the long run that makes the difference.
Don't go out of character in your role-plays. By that, I mean, don't call someone by their handler's name. Don't say stuff like "I'm a better role-player." Don't mention real promotions, or better yet, don't mention any fantasy promotions either. I mean, you could refer to things that have happened in passed feds that your character was in, but other than to revive a feud, or start one with a wrestler of that same league, there's no need to mention another fed.
Oh yeah, and don't mention real wrestlers either. I mean, obviously, if you can't mention real feds, you can't mention real wrestlers, but just to be safe.
Don't ever take anything personal and put it into your role-plays or in-rings. That, to me, is a shoot. I will drop you from the roster immediately if that happens. =================================================== 6. Strategies Strats help alot...if you have some things in mind you would like to occur in a match then a strat is needed...Writers can't read minds, so send in a strat. You can list some spots that you'd like for your character to do during the match. Give a description of what your character will do after he wins the match, or how will he react if he loses. If you don't strat then you can't complain about not liking how your match was written.
