Friday Night Fallout, 13 April 2002 (part 4 of 5)

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- "The Hammer" Alex Cote V/S "Sadistic" Shane Alexander =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... VOICE OVER: ARE YOU PREPARED TO BE HAMMERED?! [Crowd begins to cheer loudly.] ["What comes around" by Ill Nino begins to play over the PA system.] #I HATE YOU..... #I HATE YOU..... #I HATE YOU..... #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! ["The Hammer" Alex Cote steps out from behind the curtain. The cheers because almost mind numbing.]
[APPEARANCE: Black jeans with a pair of black steel toed army style boots. Also, a midnight blue tang top with the words "PREPARE TO BE HAMMERED" on the front. Around his neck hangs a silver chain with a silver dagger on it, a pair of black leather gloves cut at the knuckles on his hands, and a black leather trenchcoat covers him from shoulders to ankles.]
#YOU THINK THAT I'M THE ONE TO BLAME #EVERYTHING I LOSE IS JUST A PIECE OF WHAT THERE IS #TO GAIN #YOU THINK IT'S COOL AND ALL IS FINE #NOW IS THE DAY WHEN YOU PAY, THIS IS MY TIME #I FEEL NOTHING FOR BUT PAIN #I AM WHAT YOU WILL BE, YOU ARE DYING IN ME #I LOVE YOU.....I HATE YOU.....I MISS YOU..... LANCE JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, coming down the aisle, hailing from "The
City of Brotherly Love", Philidelphia Pennsylvania, he is three hundred and twenty eight pounds of twisted steel and sex appeal, he is your lord and savior, and the most devastating wrestler in sports entertainment today, here is.....
!!!!!_"THE HAMMER"_!!!!!_ALEX_!!!!!_COTE_!!!!! #YOU ALWAYS THINK THAT YOU'RE SO PERFECT #THOSE THOUGHTS DROVE ME AWAY FROM HOME #BUT IF YOU PUT ME THROUGH YOUR TEST #THEN I WILL FADE..... #I HAVE NOTHING TO SAY #BUT I FEEL LIKE MY MOUTH IS WIDE OPEN #EVERYTHING THAT IS REAL #COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! [Alex stands at the top of the entrance ramp. He raises his hands high slowly and then slams then down as spark gush from both sides of him virtually enveloping him. The sparks settle down and Alex turns around and looks up at the big screen where he watches scene of him pounding on his opponents. He laughs and turns back around. He walks down the aisle slapping the hand of his fans as the music plays.....]
#YOUR STUPID FACE JUST MAKES ME SICK #I SEE YOU CHANGING EVERYDAY #TO FIT INTO THE NEWEST CLIQUE #I KNOW YOU, BUT EVERYTHING YOU DO #IS JUST A PART OF YOU, YOU'LL NEVER SEE THE TRUTH #I HATE THE WAY YOU MAKE ME FEEL #I HATE THE WAY YOU THINK YOU'RE REAL #YOU VOICE.....IT DESTROYS.....MY ONE CHOICE..... [Alex walks up the steps and climbs into the ring over the top rope. He gets in the ring and does a little spin and them stops and flexes.....]
#YOU ALWAYS THINK THAT YOU'RE SO PERFECT #THOSE THOUGHTS DROVE ME AWAY FROM HOME #BUT IF YOU PUT ME THROUGH YOUR TEST #THEN I WILL FADE..... #I HAVE NOTHING TO SAY #BUT I FEEL LIKE MY MOUTH IS WIDE OPEN #EVERYTHING THAT IS REAL #COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! [Stands in the corner and removes his sunglasses, silver chain, and his trenchcoat revealing black elbow pads and "CUZ' I TAKE NOT PRISONERS" on the back of his tang top, and waits for the bell as the music fades....]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Sad But True" by Metallica starts to blare over the arena.] LANCE JOHNSON: "About to make his way down to the ring weighing in at 263 pounds and standing in at 6 feet 3 inches, he hails from Boston, Massachusetts. He is...
... "SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER!!!" [As the announcer finishes the name, the curtains split and out walks Shane
Alexander wearing
a pair of old black ripped jeans and a black leather jacket that is stained with blood and dirt. The jacket hangs down to about his mid-thigh. Shane has his head down, his long dark hair filled with water hangs in front of his face. As the song hits it's first acoustic break
Shane snaps his head back and the fans react with a large cheer.]
# Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who takes you there # Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who cares [Shane begins to make his way to the ring staring at the ring, almost as if it is sacred. His eyes have not come off of the ring since he has shown his face. His eyes have massive black bags under them.]
# They # They betray # I'm your only true friend now # They # They'll betray # I'm forever there [Shane makes it into the ring stepping through the middle rope. Once inside
Shane opens his
jacket and exposes a very gruesome scar across his stomach. Shane now takes off his jacket and waits in the corner.]
# I'm your dream, make you real # I'm your eyes when you must steal # I'm your pain when you can't feel # Sad but true [The music begins to fade out as Shane stretches in the corner.] [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Here we go! [Cote and Alexander move in towards each other. Cote towers over Shane but he stands his ground. The two move in and lock up. Cote tries to overpower Shane like usual but Alexander uses all his strength to stand his ground and not budge...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Shane is powerful! Not many can match power with Cote but Shane is doing it right now...
[The two break away from each other. Shane taunting Cote a bit and Cote bites and throws a hard right hand. Shane catches the punch square on the jaw and gets rocked but he stays up.
Alexander looks back and smiles then throws a punch of his own and connects, the two now trading shots!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Look at these two throw punch after punch here. Cote getting the upperhand though with those massive hands...
SHANE PROPONE: Yeah, all those knuckle-draggers have hands like that... [Cote getting the upperhand with the punches but Alexander with a quick knee lift to the gut of Cote then a hard forearm to the back of Cote's neck stops him short.
Alexander now with a few more shots to the back of Cote's neck then takes him and whips him into the turnbuckles!
Cote hits and Shane follows in with a clothesline! Shane pulls Cote out of the corner and hooks him up for a Russian legsweep but Cote hangs onto the ropes and swats
Shane to the
canvas. Cote now with a hard shot to Shane as he was getting to his feet. Cote takes Alexander and hooks him up and hits a quick gutwrench powerbomb and goes for the pin....
ONNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWOOOOO....And Alexander kicks out with force! Cote quickly up and pulls
Alexander up with
him. Cote takes Shane and sends him into the ropes. Shane comes off and Cote catches him with a stiff clothesline! Shane hits the canvas and Cote drops an elbow and covers once again...
ONNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHRR...] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Alexander again kicks out with relative ease! Cote trying to end it early but no luck so far as Shane keeps kicking out..
SHANE PROPONE: Cote is a failure, deal with it... [Cote pulls Shane back to his feet once again. Alex sends Shane into the ropes once more. Alexander comes off and Cote goes for another clothesline but Shane leaps and hooks around
Cote nailing a float over into a DDT! Cote planted into the canvas as Alexander back to his feet and pulls Cote up and hooks him up and nails a reverse DDT then goes for a pinfall attempt....
ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRE....] [CROWD: POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: KICKOUT! Cote kicks out of it! Alexander with a couple big moves there but it wasn't quite enough to put Cote away!
[Alexander pulls Cote back up to his feet once again and just throws him through the ropes and out to the floor. Alexander follows out and takes Cote to his feet. Shane sends
Alex HARD into
the steel ring steps. Cote hits hard knocked them loose and goes down to the floor. Shane now runs in after him but out of nowhere Cote just leaps up and nearly takes
Shane's head off with a MASSIVE lariat! Alexander goes down and Cote back on the offensive. Cote pulls Shane up and slams him face first into the ring apron. Cote now rolls Shane back into the ring. Alexander quickly to his feet and goes off the ropes, Cote on the ring apron as Shane comes towards him and Cote ducks and sends Shane flying to the arena floor!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! Shane just hit that floor HARD! His back bounced right off the concrete!
[Cote back down to the floor and pulls Shane back up. Cote rams Alexander into the barricade.
Cote now over and grabs a chair from nearby..] SHANE PROPONE: CHEATER! CHEATER! JOHN WILLIAMS: Oh do shut up... [Cote with chair in hand as he waits for Alexander to get to his feet. Shane slowly up and turns as Cote swings but Shane side steps then boots Cote hard in the gut making him drop the chair to the floor! Shane now with a second boot to the gut then hooks Cote up...]
[CROWD: EXTREME POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: POWERBOMB RIGHT ONTO THE CHAIR! SHANE PROPONE: Night night time... [Alexander pulls Cote back and barely able to get him up. Alexander rolls Cote into the ring and follows in. Shane hooks the leg for the pin....
ONNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRRE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOULDER UP! OH MY! I don't know how Cote is even conscious after that powerbomb but he is!
SHANE PROPONE: When you have as little brain capacity as Cote you're never really conscious to begin with...
[Alexander looking a bit frustrated as he pulls Cote up and hooks him up then quickly hits a quick Double arm DDT! Shane now rolls to the floor and reaches under the ring pulling two tables out...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: How many tables are we going to see tonight? [Shane sets both tables up. Cote meanwhile slowly gets back to his feet in the ring. Shane climbs to the ring apron only to get DRILLED with a big boot from Cote. Shane falls back but manages to grab onto the top rope. Cote though off the ropes and...]
<<<<CRRRRAAASSSHHHH!!>>>> [CROWD: MEGA EXTREME POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT A CLOTHESLINE! ALEXANDER SENT CRASHING DOWN THROUGH ONE OF HIS OWN TABLES! [Shane lays in the rubble out what was once a table as Cote slowly rolls to the floor and pulls him to his feet. Cote throws Alexander into the ring and follows in going for the pin....
ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRREE....] [CROWD: MEGA POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOULDER UP! Alexander got the shoulder up...Weather it was intentional or not is up to debate though.
[Cote slowly to his feet as he pulls Alexander up. Cote with a scoop and a slam then says it's over. Cote now out to the apron and slowly up to the top turnbuckle but
Alexander up and pushes Cote's feet dropping him on the top turnbuckle. Alexander now quickly ascends the turnbuckles and hooks Cote up then comes off..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: NO REMORSE! HE HIT IT! [Alexander goes for the pin.... ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOO.... THHHHRRRRREEE....] [DING,DING,DING!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: HE GOT HIM! What a win for Shane Alexander! SHANE PROPONE: Beating a loser like Cote is a big win? News to me... LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, "Sadistic" Shane ALEXANDER!! [Shane Alexander gets his hand raised then makes his way out of the ring now and making his way up the aisle...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Well what a match that was, our Main Event is coming up. We'll be right...What the? Alexander is coming back!
[Shane making his way back to the ring and slides in. Cote getting up and
Alexander nails him
from behind knocking Cote through the ropes to the ring apron. Shane now with
Cote hunched over
on the top rope then leaps over the top rope hitting a sunset flip..]
<<<<CRRRASSSSHHH!!>>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: POWERBOMB THROUGH THE OTHER TABLE! OH MY! SHANE PROPONE: THAT WAS GREAT! [Shane on his feet looking down at Cote, pointing his finger at him as we cut to commercial...]
[A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...]
