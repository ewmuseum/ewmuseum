Friday Night Fallout, 22 March 2002 (part 3 of 5)

22 March 2002

-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= [FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE] "The Dentist" Mike Wise V/S "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL AND IS A FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH! INTRODUCING FIRST... ["Hundred Mile City" by Ocean Colour Scene begins to play..] LANCE JOHNSON: Hailing from Solo, London. Weighing in at 240 pounds here is
"The Dentist" Mike
WISE! ["Hundred Mile City" by Ocean Colour Scene plays as Mike Wise steps through the curtains. Mike stands at the top of the ramp staring down at the ring. He then runs towards the ring, slides under the bottom rope, runs across the ring to one of the opposite corners, jumps up onto the second rope and slowly raises his hands above his shoulders as if to show off his great body to the crowd while looking out at that crowd. Wise jumps down from the turnbuckle as he looks over at his opponent...]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Chop Suey" by System of a Down fades in. A scream echoes through the PA, and is sounds a lot like the "Psychoholic" scream of the Harbinger of Madness, the Hardcore
Spokesperson of the 21st Century, and your Idol, Rolemodel, and H-E-R-O! The spotlight hits the end of the entryway, and there stand three people. The largest one, wearing a black
"SECURITY, PUNK!" T-shirt, jeans, and unremoveable Ray-bans is immediately Identified as "Marz" Candress. The woman dressed in see-though leopard print blouse and pants with a black sports bra and Rio-cut
Bikini Bottoms with Holographic Oakleys turns out to be "Double D" Devin
DeMasters. And the one in the middle, wearing the "BOBISM! ~ www.gotbob.net " T-shirt and black jeans and boots is...] LANCE JOHNOSN: Accompanied to the ring by "Double D" Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress... From Coral Springs, Florida. Weighing 170 pounds... The Great American Anti-Hero... "HEAVY MENTAL" DAAAAAAAAAAVE PIIIIIIEEEEEETKAAAAAAAAA! #WAKE UP! {Wake up!} #Grab a brush and put a little makeup! #Hide the scars to fade away the shakeup! {hide the scars to fade away the...} #Why'd you leave the keys upon the table? #Here you go, create another fable! [Pietka and his entourage walk down the aisle as the quick tounge of SoaD starts screaming.
The Heavy Mental One shows himself off to their crowd, a mixture of love and hate and hateful love from his "fans". He does a little spin around... before reverting to a more serious look.
Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress follow from behind, Candress standing close to DeMasters.
Pietka and DeMasters stand in front of the ring, and Candress steps up and opens the ring ropes for them. DeMasters steps through the ropes, while Pietka climbs over the turnbuckle and
Moonsaults himself onto the canvas. Candress releases the ropes and steps over them. After getting up from his flip, Pietka points to the signs in the crowd, some reading
"HEAVY MENTAL MANIA", "Dave Pietka = Crowd Pleaser", and "BRING BACK DEVIN'S DOUBLE Ds".] @YOU WANTED TO! #Grab a brush and put a little makeup! @YOU WANTED TO! #Hide the scars to fade away the shakeup! @YOU WANTED TO! #Why'd you leave the keys upon the table? @YOU WANTED TO! [Pietka spastically jumps around the ring, spouting out God knows what, slapping himself in the head as the song becomes louder and harder... and then he calms down when he hears...]
#I don't think you trust... #In... my... Self-Righteous suicide. #I... cry... when angels deserve to... #DIE! [Pietka stands in the middle of the ring, his arms spread out and his psychotic grin on his face. DeMasters lays herself down in front of Pietka, and Marz stands behind him. Pietka's smile seems to grow bigger as he looks up and shoves his hand into the air and...NOTHING YOU
IDIOT! This is Dave Pietka... He don't need no stinkin' pyro! The three move from their pose.
Marz steps over the ring ropes again. Pietka and DeMasters exchange a quick kiss, and then she steps through the open ropes provided by Marz. Pietka just trots around the ring a bit, waiting for that opening bell... a crazed look on his face as "Chop Suey" fades out.]
SHANE PROPONE: My faovrite pair! JOHN WILLIAMS: Pietka and DeMasters? SHANE PROPONE: No, those Double D's! OH YEAH! JOHN WILLIAMS: And Pietka going right to work as he nails Wise from behind! [DING,DING,DING!] [Pietka now with Wise in the corner. He takes Mike and goes to send him across to the other side but Wise uses his size advantage to reverse it. Pietka goes in and hits the turnbuckles.
Dave rebounds out and Wise catches him and drills him with a sidewalk slam! Wise staying on Pietka as he locks him in a reverse hammerlock!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise trying to keep the pace slow here. Pietka excels in these kinds of matches..
SHANE PROPONE: Yeah, matches with no rules tend to favor the insane... [Wise pulls Pietka up by his wrist and floats over into a side headlock. Wise grinding down on the head and neck of Pietka as Dave tries to fight out of it. Pietka able to wriggle out of the hold gut Wise with a pull of the hair locks him right back up. Pietka now backs Wise into the ropes and shoots him across. Wise comes off and Pietka goes for a dropkick but Wise sidesteps it and swats Pietka to the canvas then drops an elbow to the back of
Pietka's neck.
Wise now pulls Pietka up and hooks him up. Wise lifts Dave and drills him with a sitout powerbomb into a pin!
ONNNNNNNEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Pietka kicks out of it with relative ease. Wise trying to end things early, a long match would certainly favor Pietka...
[Wise pulls Pietka back up and goes to lock him back up but Pietka with a hard knee to the groin!]
SHANE PROPONE: ROCHAMBEAU! He kicked him Squaw in the nuts! [Wise goes down to the canvas as Pietka steps right on his head! Pietka walks across the head of Wise then pulls him up to his feet. Pietka takes Wise and throws him out to the ring apron.
Wise pulling himself up as Pietka over and knees him hard in the gut. Wise doubled over the ropes. Pietka now runs over and springboards over he ropes and locks Wise into a sunset flip...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise tries to hang on but no luck and Pietka pulls him down and POWERBOMBS HIM ON THE ARENA FLOOR! [The back of Wise's head bounces off the floor as Pietka gets back to his feet. Pietka over and throws Lance Johnson off of his seat and grabs the chair. Pietka climbs up onto the ring apron. Dave runs across and leaps off placing the chair under his legs and comes down onto the face of Wise with an Arabian facebuster!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Pietka dominating the match now. He busted Wise's nose with that move! SHANE PROPONE: BLOOD!! [Pietka hooks Wise's leg as the ref now out of the ring goes down for the count.... ONNNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRRR....Wise kicks out of it as the ref's hand was coming down for the three. Pietka right back up and grabs the chair off the floor. Pietka takes the chair and drives the edge of it down into the chest of Wise. Pietka now tosses the chair to the side then pulls Wise back up to his feet. Pietka takes Wise and slams him face first into the ring apron!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise going face first into the canvas, that can't help his already busted nose.
I wouldn't be surprised if it was broken... [Pietka now takes Wise and whips him hard into the steel railing! Pietka follows in after Wise with a clothesline taking both men up and over the railing into the crowd.
Security quickly rushes to hold the fans back as Pietka gets up and stomps on the face of Wise before pulling him back to his feet. Pietka now dragging Wise through the crowd. Wise trying to fight him off but Pietka with a shot to the nose stops him immediately. Pietka now throws
Wise into the side
railing. Wise with his arms over the top holding himself up as Pietka runs in after him but
Wise drops down and back body drops Pietka over the railing and Pietka lands on the steel rampway!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise able to send Pietka over and buy himself some time to recover... [Wise now going over the railing after Pietka who is slowly getting up. Pietka on all fours as
Wise steps in and boots him hard in the ribs. Wise now pulls Pietka up to his hands and knees again then drops a leg down across the back of his head driving him face first into the steel ramp! Pietka rolls over as we see he is now busted open as well.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men bleeding, Pietka from his forehead and Wise from his nose. SHANE PROPONE: What were you expecting out of this match? Milk and cookies? [Wise pulls Pietka up and hooks him up into a full nelson! Pietka trying to get out but Wise pulls back on the hold stopping him short. Wise now lifts Pietka up and...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: FULL NELSON SUPLEX ON THE STEEL RAMP! WHAT A MOVE! [Wise hangs onto Pietka and rolls back to his feet. Wise lifts Pietka again and hits a second
Full nelson suplex onto the ramp!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Two of those devastating suplexes in a row! Pietka has to be out of it! [Wise now going for the pinfall attempt.... ONNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRREE.....] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! SO CLOSE BUT PIETKA KICKS OUT OF IT! SHANE PROPONE: Calm down already... [Wise now pulls Pietka up and whips him up the ramp. Pietka goes up a bit before he stumbles and falls to the ground. Wise follows up and drops a leg across his chest before pulling him back up. Wise now dragging Pietka through the backstage curtain. The camera follows through into the backstage area as we see Wise throw Pietka face first into the wall.
Wise now pulls Pietka over and throws him over a nearby table flipping it over and ontop of
Pietka. Wise over
and pulls the table off of Pietka and sets it back up. Wise pulls Pietka up and hooks him up for a powerbomb. Wise lifts Pietka and...]
<<<<CRRRRAASSSHHH!!>>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: POWERBOMB THROUGH THE TABLE! Pietka has to be out of it! [Wise goes for the pinfall.... ONNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: "Marz" out of nowhere comes in and pulls Wise off of Pietka! ["Marz" with Wise by the hair, he takes him up and just THROWS him face first into the wall.
The ref now over trying to get "Marz" to leave. "Marz" looks at him and grins but walks away..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Candress walking away but the damage is done... [Now suddenly Bunny, the wife of Mike Wise comes onto scene and checks on her husband. She helps Wise back to his feet. Wise a bit groggy but suddenly realizes who is helping him get up...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise getting right into his wife's face here. Good God, she was just trying to help.
SHANE PROPONE: She needs to go back to the kitchen where she belongs.. JOHN WILLIAMS: Is that where DeMaster's belongs too? SHANE PROPONE: No, she belongs in a condo a few blocks from my house. [Pietka up and nails Wise from behind with a dropkick driving Wise into his wife crushing her against the wall. Dave now pulls Wise up and nails a boot to the gut then hooks him up...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: HEAVY MENTALITY ON THE BROKEN TABLE! SHANE PROPONE: Goodnight... [Pietka rolls Wise over and goes for the pin.... ONNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRREEE....] [DING,DING,DING!!] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, "Heavy Mental" Dave PIETKA!! JOHN WILLIAMS: Pietka gets the win! He has a lot of momentum going into the PPV next week, he may not be a heavy favorite but he could surprise everyone..We need to take a quick break, but don't go away...
[Cuts to commercial..] INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING PRESENTS INTERNATIONAL INCIDENT March 31st, 2002 LIVE! From Madison Square Garden [Fades out...] [Cuts to backstage..] [The door in Mike Wise’s dressing room swings violently open as Mike re enters and SLAMS the door behind him, he is not happy. Wises wife and kids are still there and now his two brother’s are too. Mike doesn’t say anything he just stands in the middle of the room staring at his wife. One of Mikes brother’s looks at Mike and then starts walking toward Bunny and the kids.]
Dewey Wise: Come on kids lets go and get some ice cream. [He picks up Mikes two year old son and takes his daughter, Jessica, by the hand and starts leading her out of the room.]
Jessica Wise:[Now starting to cry] I want to stay with Mummy. Dewey Wise: Don’t worry Jess, Mummy’ll be here when we get back and we’ll bring her some ice cream back.
[Everyone leaves the room leaving Mike and Bunny alone. Mike slowly walks towards his wife. As
Mike gets closer Bunny just sits there trembling and looking at him all wide eyed. Bunny takes a big gulp and we cut away.]
[Cut to ringside..]
