ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Card

Countdown to International Incident

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
5.... 4.... 3.... 2.... 1..... [The screeen EXPLODES! Smoke covers the screen and slowly fades away as we see the words...]

The fire begins to fade away as we find ourselves in none other than Madison Square
Garden! The camera pans around the arena as tens of thousands of wrestling fans jam pack the arena. The camera makes it's way around and to the ring as Lance Johnson stands there. The camera shifts around to the table near the ring as we see the men who call the action every week for ICW, John Williams and Shane Propone!]
[Williams looking professional as usual and Propone looking....like only he can. The camera zooms in on the duo as the mics comes to life and
John Williams begins to speak...] JOHN WILLIAMS: WELCOME TO THE COUNTDOWN TO ICW'S RETURN TO PAY-PER-VIEW, INTERNATIONAL INCIDENT! Tonight we will crown an International Champion in a one night tournament! It's nights like these where Legends are made! Tonight we will see men like
Alex Cote, Tommy Lionheart, Dave Pietka, rookie sensation Alex Hawkins, the returning Masked Superstar Jobber and many others...
SHANE PROPONE: But only one man in the whole bunch matters and that is Setzer
Van Strife! For some reason he seems to be playing to these morons in the crowd...but despite that he is still "2 Smooth" baby!
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife is by far the heavy favorite, but you can never tell what may happen! Here on Countdown we will fill the final slot of the Tournament with a 5 man Battle Royal, the winner goes on to face Alex
Hawkins in the first round. Before we go to the ring I am told that we are to hear from one of the participants in the back, let's go to it...
[The scene cuts into a shot outside of "Sadistic" Shane Alexander's dressing room. The large blue metal door swings open with a quick thrust.
Behind the door is Shane Alexander. He is standing there with no shirt on, just his black ripped jeans. The grotesque scars on Shane's stomach are quite visible. Shane looks into the camera with his bloodshot brown eyes.
His hair hanging on the sides of his face, still dripping with water.] SA: "Tonight..." [A long pause before Shane cracks his neck.] SA: "Tonight is the night to crown the International Champion. Tonight is the night that Shane Alexander returns to the wrestling ring."
[Shane pauses again.] SA: "Madison Square Garden is the lucky place to be tonight. I am in a battle royal match tonight against four other men to see who is going to get a _spot_ in the tournament. That to me is a blessing in disguise. Not only do I have to win a battle royal match, just to be entered in the tournament, but then I have to face Alex Hawkins. And then another man, and then another man."
[Shane stops and counts out with his fingers until he holds seven fingers in front of the camera.]
SA: "What other way can I prove myself to everyone that I am back and better than ever? Defeating seven men to claim the International Title belt, it seems the best way to become a champion. Showing my endurance.
Inflicting my pain. Tonight ICW..." [Shane pauses.] SA: "... I have _NO REMORSE_ for my actions." [Shane slams the door before the camera cuts to black.] [Fades back to ringside....] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [BATTLE ROYAL] [WINNER ADVANCES TO FIRST ROUND OF INTERNATIONAL TITLE TOURNAMENT] "Top Notch" Nelson Alexander V/S Sid Starr V/S "Sadistic" Shane Alexander
V/S Buddy Costello V/S The Masked Superstar Jobber
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS IS AN OVER THE TOP BATTLE ROYAL! THE ONLY WAY TO WIN IS TO THROW YOUR OPPONENT OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE FLOOR INTRODUCING FIRST... ["HE'S A LOSER" by STATIC X begins to blast on the PA systems as green, red and blue lights begin to flicker on and off spontanously thoughout the entire arena.Down the aisle comes TMSJ running towards the ring he runs and slides through the bottom rope and stands up to a massive crowd response. Many fans in the arena are holding 3 fingers up in the air. TMSJ feeding off the response goes rushing the ring ropes only to get tangled up on them, which gets some laughs from the crowd.
After a few minutes he gets free from the ropes and grabs a microphone and signals for his music to be cut with hand. ]
T M S J: IT FEELS GOOD TO BE BACK IN ICW! [ M A S S I V E P O P ] T M S J: BUT IT FEELS EVEN BETTER TO BE BACK ON PAY PER VIEW! [ M A S S I V E P O P ] T M S J: ICW is back in business and so is _DA JOBBAH!_ Well thats why I have come out here today see..... I want to be cool. Yep, thats right you heard me, I want to be _COOL!_ I am going to do something that I should have done a long time ago... I am going to do something that is going to turn the wrestling world upside down, I am going to do something that has been long coming. No... no... I am not going to get naked in front of thousands here tonight and the millions at home watching television. No I am going to do the unexpected, the impossible I am going to . . .
CHANGE..... MY............... NAME.........!!!!!!! [ The whole crowd stands in shock. ] T M S J: From here on out I will not be known as The Masked Superstar Jobber... nope. From this day forth I will make the change from this loser that I am to a winner... a cooler me... I am no more a dork that will get tangled up in ropes, nor fall tumbling all the way down the aisle upto the ring. From this day forth and on I will be recognized as, _SIR SUPER DUPER
CULO COOLER SWOOPER GOD I AM A DORK FOR CHANGING MY NAME RODRIGUEZ!_ Thats right I have joined the _TOP NOTCH_ club of name changing to making myself a bigger and better cooler wrestler.
[Starts smiling at the crowd. ] T M S J: AND IF YOU BELIEVED A DAMN WORD I SAID THEN... YOU.... MUST BE... CRAZY....!!!!!!!! [ M A S S I V E P O P ] T M S J: _DA JOBBAH_ will never change cause I will always be that crazy sumanbitch that I always am. As for _TOP NOTCH_ he can stick with his club cause for me, I rather stick with the Invisible Gorrilla! Maybe
_TOP NOTCH_ could give _SID STARR_ some pointers on a better, innovative personality. I mean _STARR_ I know that you _KNOW_ me, and I _KNOW_ that you think your the best and I also _KNOW_ that you _KNOW_ what I ate for breakfast. _STARR_ give the I _KNOW'S_ up and get a personality dude, you need it!
[ TMSJ digs into his tights and takes out a LoliPop and puts it up so the whole crowd can see. ]
T M S J: In my hands ladies and gentlemen I hold a LoliPop. Not just any LoliPop but my buddies, LoliPop. This LoliPop is for my buddy and also your buddy, _BUDDY COSTELLO!_ See I wanted to show buddy that theres no hard feelings about tonights match and I want for him and me to remain good friends. You know let me tell you guys a story about my
_BUDDY_. See one day me and _BUDDY_we were on a road trip and he starts telling me that he's never been with a woman. I was like
"Dude your for real?" And he was like "Yeah I don't know what being with a woman is!" And I was like "Dude are you for real?" And he was like 'Yeah man! I don't know what it is like!" And I was like "Dude are you for real?" You have to excuse me but I was real and I mean real "high" if you know what I mean that day. But back to the story he was
"Like yeah man!" So I was driving and we stopped in a gas station and I got off ,went inside bought a LoliPop gave it to him and I started running to the road and hitchhiked my way out of his presence cause he was giving me hints he wanted to be with a man and I just don't go that way. Let him suck on a LoliPop and not me, no way!
[ Snickers and laughs are heard throughout the crowd. ] T M S J: So just to show theres no hard feelings for that day brought _BUDDY_ a
LoliPop. Since we know he sucks, he might as well suck something!
[Lance Johnson gets the mic back...] LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Sad But True" by Metallica starts to blare over the arena.] LANCE JOHNSON: "About to make his way down to the ring weighing in at 263 pounds and standing in at 6 feet 3 inches, he hails from Boston, Massachusetts. He is...
... "SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER!!!" [As the announcer finishes the name, the curtains split and out walks Shane
Alexander wearing a pair of old black ripped jeans and a black leather jacket that is stained with blood and dirt. The jacket hangs down to about his mid-thigh. Shane has his head down, his long dark hair filled with water hangs in front of his face. As the song hits it's first acoustic break Shane snaps his head back and the fans react with a large cheer.]
# Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who takes you there # Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who cares [Shane begins to make his way to the ring staring at the ring, almost as if it is sacred. His eyes have not come off of the ring since he has shown his face. His eyes have massive black bags under them.]
# They # They betray # I'm your only true friend now # They # They'll betray # I'm forever there [Shane makes it into the ring stepping through the middle rope. Once inside
Shane opens his jacket and exposes a very gruesome scar across his stomach. Shane now takes off his jacket and waits in the corner.]
# I'm your dream, make you real # I'm your eyes when you must steal # I'm your pain when you can't feel # Sad but true [The music begins to fade out as Shane stretches in the corner.] LANCE JOHNSON: AND THERE OPPON... [Johnson quickly out of the way as Shane rushes Jobber and tackles him to the canvas and begins laying into him with right hands...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Looks like Shane Alexander doesn't want to wait for the rest of the competitors to come out, he's got the jump on Jobber...
SHANE PROPONE: Not a hard thing to do... [Shane pulls Jobber to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Jobber comes off and Shane goes for a back body drop but Jobber flips over his back and lands on his feet behind Shane. Shane turns and Jobber goes for a dropkick but Shane sidesteps it. Shane now with Jobber against the ropes trying to dump him over...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Shane trying to get Jobber out but no luck....AND HERE COME THE OTHERS! [The rest of the competitors in the match rush down the aisle. Sid Starr,
Nelson Alexander, and Buddy Costello. The three roll into the ring and
Alexander over and tries to help dump Jobber over but Jobber hanging on with all he has. Starr and Costello meanwhile bump into each other and start at it. Starr and Costello lock up and Costello backing Starr into the corner and then breaks the lock up and lays into Starr with an open hand chop to the chest...and a second one. Costello continues the assault in the corner as on the other side of the ring Shane turns his attention from trying to dump Jobber out to Alexander and nails him with a hard right hand catching him off guard and knocking him back.
Jobber uses this to get to his feet and step to the side as Shane floors Alexander with a clothesline. Shane now takes Alexander and whips him into the ropes. Alexander comes off and gets caught with a back body drop sending him over the top rope but onto the ring apron. Shane turns to go after him but Jobber off the top turnbuckle with a missle dropkick sends
Shane crashing to the canvas...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Alexander was an inch away from elimination there but he managed to hit the apron and he now rolls back into the ring...
[Jobber now working over Shane on the mat, hitting a series of leg and elbow drops as on the other side of the ring Sid Starr now with the upperhand on Costello as he tries to dump him over the top rope but Costello hangs on like a spider monkey! Costello almost out but he hangs onto the ropes and slips through the middle rope back into the ring. Starr pulls Costello up and sends him into the ropes. Costello however grabs onto the ropes and stops himself. Costello now pointing to his head..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Yeah, you're smart, we get it... [Starr comes after him but Costello moves out of the way and tries to throw
Starr over but Starr hangs on and slips back into the ring! Costello stomps on Starr as he slides in stopping him from trying anything.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr and Costello trying to get each other out, but neither men able to get the job done...Jobber and Shane still going at it and Alexander staying off everyone's radar right now..
[Jobber with Shane in the ropes. Jobber doing everything he can to try and dump him over but no luck...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Everyone is hanging on for dear life! No one wants to be the first man to go out...
[Nelson Alexander sees his opportunity and rushes over and tries to dump both
Jobber and Shane over the top rope! Both men go over and
Alexander turns in triumph but both Jobber and Shane hung onto the ropes and pull themselves back into the ring. Shane and Jobber both on there feet and look at each other then at Alexander who still has his back turned. Alexander turns and gets a double thrust kick from Jobber and
Shane sending him back into the ropes. Jobber and Shane both follow in and throw Alexander over the top rope to the floor!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: ONE DOWN! LANCE JOHNSON: "Top Notch" Nelson Alexander has been ELIMINATED! SHANE PROPONE: Once a bum always a bum... [Jobber and Shane both looking over the rope at Alexander on the floor then at the same time turn and tries to dump the other over....]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men with the same idea there! Shane and Jobber at it once again... [Sid Starr and Buddy Costello once again at odds on the other side of the ring. The two rolling across the ropes, neither one able to get an upper hand. The two hit the ropes and slip through the middle rope and out to the floor...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr and Costello on the floor but they didn't go over the top rope! [Starr and Costello now going at it on the floor. Costello with a low blow on
Starr as he was pulling Costello to his feet. Costello now takes Starr and rams his back into the ring apron! Costello now takes Starr and goes to whip him into the steel barricade but Starr able to reverse it.
Costello hits the steel and rebounds off. Starr with a back body drop. Starr now rolls back into the ring and runs right into a dropkick from
Jobber. Starr hits the ropes and Jobber tries to dump him over but Starr hangs on...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Where's Shane going? [Shane slips out of the ring and grabs a steel chair from ringside and goes around to Costello who slowly gets to his feet. Costello sees Shane coming around towards him with chair in hand and quickly enters the ring. Shane follows in, chair still in hand. Costello backing away and hits the ropes. Shane closing in and swings but Costello able to move out of the way and hits a back leg sweep sending Shane to the mat. The chair lands on Shane's face. Costello hits a legdrop on Shane then discards the chair to the side.]
SHANE PROPONE: Nice one Shane, what's your next trick? [Costello now over and tries to help dump Starr out. Starr hanging on for dear life as Costello and Jobber continue to push him over the top rope. Shane Alexander slowly getting back up, blood now pouring from his forehead. Shane picks the chair up from the mat, marked with his blood and makes his way up behind Costello slowly. Shane spins Costello around and Costello drops down to the mat and scrambles to the ropes. Costello up, Shane moving in on him and Costello jumps over the top rope to the floor...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What the? Costello just eliminated himself! SHANE PROPONE: Can you blame him? Look at the look in Shane's eyes, he's insane! LANCE JOHNSON: Buddy Costello has been ELIMINATED!! JOHN WILLIAMS: And then there were three... [Shane now over and grabs Jobber from behind and hits a German suplex! Jobber's head bounces off the canvas! Sid Starr slips back into the ring and slowly gets back to his feet. Shane now over and goes to spear Starr but Starr sidesteps and Shane through the ropes and to the floor...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Again out through the middle rope and not over the top... SHANE PROPONE: Anyone with half a brain would know that....But seeing as how
99% of the people watching this don't have half a brain I guess the explanation is needed...
[Shane on the floor, Sid Starr in the ring turns towards Jobber who is getting back up but Shane from behind grabs him by the feet and pulls him out from under the bottom rope. Shane with Starr and hooks him into a cobra clutch then drops him with a neckbreaker like move!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a move by Shane Alexander! Starr is down and out, but still in the match...
SHANE PROPONE: If he wakes up that is... [Shane standing over Starr with a sadistic grin on his face. Shane turns towards the ring just in time to see Jobber come through the ropes with a suicide dive! Jobber sends Shane to the floor and rolls off...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: All three men down on the floor...But none of them eliminated.. [Jobber slowly back to his feet. He looks at Starr and Shane on the floor. Jobber pulls Starr up and rolls him into the ring. Jobber pulls Starr up and starts to throw him over the top rope but just at the last minute Starr comes back to life and grabs onto the ropes...]
[CROWD: HEEL POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: What is he doing here? [Alex Hawkins strolling down the aisle. Jobber still hasn't noticed him as he tries to throw Starr out. Hawkins around the ring and right to where
Jobber is facing and waves at him arrogantly..] JOHN WILLIAMS: What is he doing? SHANE PROPONE: He's just getting a look at his competition...I mean he is the only man who doesn't know who his first round opponent is, that's a real disadvantage!
[Jobber dumps Starr to the canvas as he eyes Hawkins. Jobber pointing at Hawkins who just stands there, a grin on his face. Starr now from behind Jobber and dumps him over the top rope to the floor right at Hawkins feet!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: OH COME ON! LANCE JOHNSON: The Masked Superstar Jobber has been ELIMINATED! JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins already leaving...so much for looking over his competition... [Hawkins slowly backing up the aisle with a cocky smirk on his face. Starr in the ring thinking he has the match won..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr thinks it's over but Shane Alexander is still in it... [Shane Alexander rolls into the ring. Starr sees him and rushes over dropping a leg to the back of his head. Starr pulls Shane up and tries to dump him over the rope to the floor but Shane hangs on, blood dripping down his forehead into his eyes...]
SHANE PROPONE: Looks like Shane is seeing red...literally... [Shane with an eye rake stops Starr's attempt to get him out. Shane to his feet and takes Starr and whips him into the ropes. Starr off and ducks a clothesline attempt from Alexander. Alexander now goes off the ropes, Starr turns and Shane goes for a cross body block. Starr hangs on and hits the ropes. Both men go up and over...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men going over but Shane hangs onto the ropes and slips back in as Starr hits the floor!
[DING,DING,DING!!] LANCE JOHNSON: Sid Starr has been ELIMINATED! Your winner, Advancing to the first round of the International Title Tournament
"Sadistic" Shane ALEXANDER!! JOHN WILLIAMS: Alexander goes onto the first round, but who knows who may have won if it weren't for Alex Hawkins getting involved...
SHANE PROPONE: He didn't get involved! It's not his fault Jobber can't keep his attention on the match at hand.
JOHN WILLIAMS: Well it doesn't matter, Shane Alexander faces Hawkins in the first round. Coming up in a matter of seconds, the ICW's return to Pay-Per-View! Ladies and gentlemen it's time for International
Incident! [Fades out...]
