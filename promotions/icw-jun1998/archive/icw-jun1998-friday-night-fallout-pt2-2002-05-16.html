Fallout as

INTRODUCING FIRST... [The lights in the arena suddenly go out, as a faint swooshing noise is repeated over the speakers. A recording of an orgasming girl saying, 'Ooh baby' is then played, as blue spotlights shine down from the rafters, and begin doing figure-eights in the spot directly under the video-wall. The wall suddenly erupts in a plethora of color, as
'Sober' by Tool
blares throughout the arena.]
[The videowall suddenly changes to a black and blue ying-yang, with the letters
'JK' written
across it is silver cursive, that is engulfed by flames. The song pauses for a moment as the word "Quicksilver" is whispered. The song kicks back on as the word echoes in the background.
Silver pyros suddenly explode off. When they die down, we see a familiar individual standing there.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Hailing from Beverly Hills,California...Weighing in at 266 pounds here is,
'Quicksilver' Jeff KEENAN!!
[The crowd erupts!!! Blue pyros then explode, as he makes his way down the aisle and into the ring. He's got a heavy duty neckbrace on, a result of the Sid Starr/Setzer Van
Strife set-up
of last week, and looks very distrought. He calls for the mic, and as he raises it to his mouth, everyone begins chanting his name...'Kee-nan... Kee-nan, Kee-nan.' A grin falls across his face.]
JK: Helloooooooooooooo UNIONDALE! [The crowd pops again, as Jeff's smirk fades.] JK: According to the doctor, I shouldn't even be here this week. [Jeff's tone of voice changes...] JK: Last week, when that bastard Van Strife, and his cock-goblin partner Sid
Starr spiked me
into the t.v. title with that piledriver...
[Jeff sighs.] JK: They broke my damn neck... [The entire building falls silent.] JOHN WILLIAMS: I heard rumors about that, but I didn't think they were true...Oh my. What the hell is he doing out here?
SHANE PROPONE: He's gonna tell the world his carerr is over, I LOVE IT! JK: When I arrived at the emergency room, I was given two options. I could either get immediate temporary surgery, and see how I am doing in a few months...or do the safest possible thing. Permanent Surgery, rehab, and over a damn year out of the ring, if I can ever come back at all.
I chose the first option. [Keenan lowers the mic, rubbing his eyes.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well it's a shame. Looks like Kennan will be out of action for awhile because of those two disgusting human beings...and I use that term loosely.
JK: And as I lay in my recovery bed that night, the _only_ thing I could think about, was getting vengeance on Sid Starr, and beating the Smoothster into a massy pulp.
So when the Dr. released me from the hospital and said, "Call me in a few months..." [Keenan pauses and grins.] JK: I said, "Doc...I'll call ya in a week, right-after I beat the hell out of
Sid Starr and
Setzer Van Strife."
[The crowd erupts at this statement, once again chanting his name.] JK: Heh, the doctor informed me that I may be risking paralysis...but I told him it didn't matter, as long as I can cripple Starr and Van Strife as well.
[The crowd pops again!] JK: That's right. I thought everyone might like to see them crippled. So this is the deal everyone. Tonight, I'm gonna walk down that aisle, against doctor's orders, take that sneaky little coniving fool Sid Starr... and beat him so bad, that the only thing he'll be wrestling with for the rest of his life...
[Keenan grins.] JK: Is his zipper! [The fans let out a huge "He just tore Starr apart" chant.] JK: Heh, and Setz... I gotta tip my hat to you. That was some pretty crafty S[CENSORED] you pulled last week. I really do hafta hand it to ya.
But rest assured Smoothster, you'll get yours. And I'm sure the fans That you turned your back on, want to see me kick your ass _just_ as bad as I want to.
[All of a sudden Starr's voice breaks in across the PA system.] SS: That's a nice theory there, junior. [A loud *MEOW* sounds from the PA system, then a rockabilly/swing bass kicks in, followed by a guitar and some drums... The opening strains to "Stray Cat Strut" by the Stray
Cats. The
instruments are soon followed by the crooning voice of Brian Setzer.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen. Introducing, from the City of Angeles,
Los Angeles,
California and accompanied to the ring by your ICW Television Champion, Setzer
Van Strife! He
stands six feet, two inches tall and weighs in tonight at 224 lbs of veteran ringskills. He is the self-proclaimed coolest thing in the ring. He is...
...... SID STAAAAAAAARR!!! [At this point Starr and Van Strife step out onto the aisle, smirking like a couple of cats just finished with an all-you-can-eat canary buffet. Making their way down to the aisle, Sid with a microphone in his hand, they seem to almost savour the boos raining down on them from the fans. The two of them step through the ropes and move to the center of the ring.]
SS: Thing is, theories don't mean much inside the ring, do they? Heya, Jeffster. How's the neck? A little sore? [Starr smirks as the fans react negatively to his gloating at Keenan's injury.] SS: Now, far be it from _me_ to point out someone's faults and mistakes... [Again he smirks, looking about as sincere as a lawyer in a room full of people ready to file lawsuits.]
SS: But I can't in good conscience let a poor, innocent kid like yerself live with such delusions. You see, Jeffy, you may think you've got a chance at... Heh...
Revenge tonight. You may _think_ you've got Sid Starr and the ICW Television Champion, SETZER
VAN STRIFE, where
you want them! Hell, you may even think you're walking out of this arena tonight on your own two feet, but the problem here, junior, is that you're making snap judgements without all the necessary information available. See, you want the Champ, not me. I know that.
[He gestures at the audience.] SS: Even _they_ know that. Problem is that to get to the Champ and his belt you gotta get past yours truly, and that... Heh... Well, you don't stand a snowball's chance in hell of pulling _that_ off.
[Starr pauses, then smirks, looking as smug as ever before.] SS: Hey, Champ... [Sid looks over at Van Strife standing next to him.] SS: Ya feel like informing the Clueless Wonder here, and all the little couchpotatoes watching us as to why life as they know it is about to come crashing down around them?
Why Jeffy here is about to become number one in a long line of casualties? [Sid tosses the microphone to Van Strife who easily snatches it out of the air.] SVS: That's quite simple. You see folks, right now you are looking at the _elite_ performers in ICW. Forget the shortcomings of guys like Lionheart, Fresco, and even you, Keenan. When two men of talent and skill come together, it's a force that not a damn soul will be able to handle.
[Keenan starts stepping forward, as to start a fight with Van Strife. Flashing a trademark smirk, Van Strife puts a hand up.]
SVS: You need to stop and take a few steps back. You might want to take a moment and reconsider your decision tonight. The doctors have not cleared you for this match, yet you signed a medical waiver? Surely your health is just a _bit_ more important than a little revenge?
[Keenan shakes his head, motioning Van Strife to take him on.] SVS: Me? Come on, sunshine. Jeffrey my boy, you've had enough chances to take me down. You had a one-on-one title match, which you lost. I then gave you _another_ chance to beat me, without anyone's consent. I could have been fired for my actions! And yet, you _still_ couldn't win!
JOHN WILLIAMS: I think there's a reason behind that. SHANE PROPONE: Yeah, Keenan sucks! JOHN WILLIAMS: No. Guess again. SHANE PROPONE: Umm... Van Strife rules? JOHN WILLIAMS: It's hopeless with you. [The crowd shows their displeasure at last week's outcome.] SVS: However... For a reason that I don't even know, the ICW "suits" have granted you another shot at the ICW
Television Title... _if_ you can get past my associate here tonight. Though your chances to win are slimmer than Dustin Diamond making a comeback...
[SCREECH POP~!] SVS: I'll honor their request. You beat Sid Starr in the ring tonight, you will get a shot at Canadian Conquest. Van Strife... versus Keenan... for one last time. I hope you popped a lot of pain pills before coming out here, 'cause Keenan...you're going to need a _miracle_ to be victorious tonight.
Now ring the goddamn bell. [Van Strife slides out of the ring, and the bell rings.] JOHN WILLIAMS: There goes the bell and Keenan with his eyes glued to Van Strife... SHANE PROPONE: Dumbass, keep your eye on your opponent... [Starr levels Keenan from behind knocking him throat first over the top rope...] SHANE PROPONE: Told ya... [Starr now choking Keenan over the top rope as the referee counts. . . 1... 2... 3... 4... 5...The ref warning Starr but he doesn't let go. The referee pulling Starr off. Sid and the ref in each others faces as Van Strife from the outside nails Keenan with a neckbreaker over the top rope...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Oh come on! Keenan pretty much in a Handicap match here... SHANE PROPONE: What are you talking about? This is a one on one match between
Starr and Keenan.

Keenan who is now
flat on his back after the neckbreaker from Van Strife. Starr pulls Keenan back to his feet and hooks him up and nails a driving DDT! Starr hangs onto the head of Keenan compounding the impact even further..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Starr concentrating on the neck of Keenan here. Doctors told
Keenan he shouldn't
even be in the ring after what happened last time and now Starr and Van Strife are looking to finish him off for good...
[Starr now still with Keenan's neck locked up rolls into a small package for the pin.... ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHRRR....] [CROWD: POP~!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Keenan kicks out of it! Keenan's neck...and a second one....]

[Starr going for the pin.... ONNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRRREE....] [CROWD: "HOLY SHIT" POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY GOD! KEENAN KICKED OUT OF IT! HE KICKED OUT OF STARR'S TRADEMARK MOVE! SHANE PROPONE: No friggin way! Keenan didn't kick out, he's dead, that was just one of those post death twitches...

JOHN WILLIAMS: The ref is throwing Van Strife out! He's telling him to get to the back! [Van Strife not listening but the ref over to Lance Johnson...] LANCE JOHNSON: The referee has told me if Setzer Van Strife does not leave ringside he will disqualify Sid Starr..
SHANE PROPONE: HE CAN'T DO THAT! JOHN WILLIAMS: You better believe he can! [Starr and Van Strife both going nuts! Setzer slowly making his way up the aisle, utter disgust on his face. Starr in the ring screaming at the referee and from behind Keenan rolls Starr up....
