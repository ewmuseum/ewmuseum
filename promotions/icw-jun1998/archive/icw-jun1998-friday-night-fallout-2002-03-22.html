[The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. The fans, who normally boo once this song hits, surprisingly cheer. The curtains at the wrestler's entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the light display is Van Strife himself. The crowd cheers, and Van Strife raises his arms in the air, as to thank the crowd and to celebrate.
Van Strife flashes his trademark cocky smirk, then looks at his empty hands. He pauses, then puts up a finger, walking to the back. A moment later he emerges once again with his trusty
Singapore Cane. Van Strife raises the cane in the air, and the crowd responds with a huge pop!
Van Strife dresses in a New Jersey Nets jersey, with "KIDD" and "5" emblazoned on the back. In addition, he wears a baggy pair of blue jeans, along with a pair of black wrestling boots. As always, he also wears his trademark pair of black Oakley sunglesses.
With a grin upon his face he makes his way down to the ring, walking down to ringside and actually slapping hands with the fans! Van Strife then makes his way to the ringside area, where he jumps onto the ring apron. He then vaults over the top rope, standing in the middle of the ring. He then calls for a microphone, which he receives.
As the music fades, Van Strife begins to speak.] SVS: New Jersey... how are you doing tonight? [A monstrous pop from the crowd for mention of their city!] SVS: Hmm... sounds like we're all doing damn good. It's been awhile since I've been in this state. I caught a New Jersey Nets game, got to see my New Jersey Devils, and now I'm here to kick some ass!
[A hometown team mixed with "you said ass" pop!] SVS: Tonight I'm here to continue my trek to Madison Square Garden, where I am set on winning the ICW International Title. I've already taken care of Tommy Lionheart, and tonight I'll take another giant step, as I take care of "The Hammer" Alex Cote.
Mr. Cote... you are going to be quite the hurdle to jump over. All of these great know who you are, and they know what you've accomplished here in International Championship
Wrestling. To
beat you inside this ring tonight would be a big step for anyone facing you.
Tonight, we're going to have a battle worthy of an International Title Match. I'm going out there tonight to give nothing less than 110%. I can only hope you're going to give the same.
My goal is to take whatever you dish out... and step it up tenfold. [A small pop from the crowd.] SVS: You can make all the comments you want about being the man that is most associated with this promotion. You're exactly right when you said that I was _the man_ in
LVPWA. There wasn't
a man that could come close to the legacy I left in Las Vegas... not even you.
To make such a bold statement about the ICW is just ludicrous, and tonight I hope to make you eat your words.
[A "kick Cote's ass" pop!] SVS: When the smoke clears and that bell is rung, only one man will be left standing. And as the crowd cheers for a performance for the ages, that man is going to be _me_.
I'm just too hungry, and have too much determination to have any other outcome besides a winning one. You are an ICW legend... and tonight I hope to come just one step closer. My path will not be denied... tonight or any other night.
So Alex, I hope you plan on bringing out the "A" game. You might think you're an heir to the throne because of past glory... but there's someone standing in your way, someone who wants it more.
I'll see you in the ring. [With that "Stand Alone" by Godsmack plays once again, the crowd cheering as
Van Strife tosses
the microphone down. He takes a moment to climb the turnbuckles and pose for the fans, before exiting the ring.]
SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH! HE'S THE MAN! JOHN WILLIAMS: Alex Cote certainly has his hands full tonight...But right now let's get to the ring and our opening match...
