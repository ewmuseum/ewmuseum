It slowly fades away as we begin to see the Fleet Center in Boston, Mass. The camera pans around the arena as the crowd is on there feet for the return of International Championship Wrestling! The camera makes it's way to the commentary table as we see none other than John
Williams and Shane Propone. Williams takes his cue and begins the show...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING IS BACK! And with that
Friday Night Fallout is back on the air! If you don't remember me, I am John Williams and also back is the man sitting next to me Shane Propone!
SHANE PROPONE: No need for an introduction, who could forget me? JOHN WILLIAMS: Lord knows I've been trying for several years now... SHANE PROPONE: You know you love me John. JOHN WILLIAMS: Right.....Well This is the return of Friday Night Fallout and what a card we have lined up. We have returning ICW stars such as Alex Cote, Dave Pietka, Tha Dragon, Higor Rosa and Tommy Lionheart. Not to mention new faces to the ICW like rookie Alex Hawkins,
Mike Wise, Mogan Ashe and a man that is known around the world as one of the best, none other than Setzer Van Strife!
SHANE PROPONE: Not one of the best, he is THE best! Van Strife is thy god! JOHN WILLIAMS: Well that is arguable, and tonight he has a tough match as he takes on Tommy Lionheart in our Main Event.
SHANE PROPONE: Van Strife will tear him limb from limb! JOHN WILLIAMS: That remains to be seen. But right now we have our opening contest between Morgan Ashe and Craig Bolton. Let's kick things up to the ring and Lance Johnson..

He begins to walk quickly toward the ring, only looking up once every few seconds and taking no time to acknowledge the crowd. He reaches the ringside area and rolls into the ring under the bottom rope. Then, he takes off his robe and sits down in the corner.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT! [Darkness.] V/O: On the 6th day, God created man. God saw all that he had made, and it was very good. And God blessed the 7th day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done. On the 8th day god...Broke The Mold....
["Broken Mold" flashes over the screen as the "The Grudge" by Tool begins to play.] #Wear the grudge like a crown of negativity. Calculate what we will or will not tolerate. Desperate to control all and everything. Unable to forgive your scarlet lettermen.# [A pale red light shines down on the top of the ramp way where The Salience stands, his head is held down as his long black hair hangs around his head and face. #Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Justify denials and grip 'em to the lonesome end. Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Terrified of being wrong. Ultimatum prison cell.# [Morgan ducks his head forward and throws his head back, the hair and water being knocked away from his face as he looks out over the arena. Ashe slowly makes his way to the ring, the spot light following him to the ring. The fans reach out to touch him but fall short as he stares at his home--the ring!]
#Saturn ascends, choose one or ten. Hang on or be humbled again.# [Ashe stops at the ringside area and looks up at the ring. For a long moment he stands there.]
#Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Justify denials and grip 'em to the lonesome end. Saturn ascends, comes round again. Saturn ascends, the one, the ten. Ignorant to the damage done.# #Wear the grudge like a crown of negativity. Calculate what we will or will not tolerate. Desperate to control all and everything. Unable to forgive your scarlet lettermen. Wear the grudge like a crown. Desperate to control. Unable to forgive. And we're sinking deeper. Defining, confining, controlling, and we're sinking deeper.# [Morgan Ashe walks back around the ring in the other direction and reaches up grabbing ahold of the top rope and stepping up onto the apron. Morgan Ashe stands on the apron looking to the side for a moment glancing back out over the crowded arena before flipping over the top rope into the ring.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: This should be a great match. [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: And there goes the bell starting this one off.. [Ashe and Bolton move towards the center of the ring and lock up right off the bat. The two men trying to get the upper hand on the other but neither seem able to and they break away. Bolton and Ashe circle and lock up again. The two men going it again. They push each other across the ring and into the ropes. Ashe goes into the ropes first but spins it around and puts Bolton against the ropes. The ref asking for a break and
Ashe steps back and lays a hard boot to the left knee of Bolton. Bolton buckles and goes down to one knee. Ashe lifts him back up and shoots him into the ropes. Bolton comes off and Ashe with a seated dropkick onto the knee of Bolton!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe is going right for the wheels of Bolton here. Bolton is known as a bit of a high flyer, Ashe obviously has done his homework.
[Bolton down on the mat and Ashe going in and locks on a half Boston crab using the leg he has been attacking. Ashe flips Bolton and has it locked in. Craig Bolton in tremendous pain. The referee checking on Bolton, asking if he wants to give it up but Bolton says no. Ashe wrenching on the knee of Bolton..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe is just punishing that knee of Bolton. If this trend keeps up the ref may have to make a judgment call and end the match.
[The ref asks Bolton if he wants to give up but he shakes his head no. Ashe finally just drops the hold. Ashe now with the left leg of Bolton and just plants a boot right into the back of Bolton's knee. Ashe now takes
Bolton up to his feet and lifts him up. Ashe with Bolton over his shoulder but Bolton able to wriggle free and land behind Ashe. Bolton rolls
Ashe from behind into a pinfall attempt...
ONNNNNNNEEEE..... TWWWWOOOO....] JOHN WILLIAMS: Not enough! Ashe kicks out of it easily. Bolton hoping to catch him off guard enough to pick up the win but no luck there.
SHANE PROPONE: Blah blah blah. I don't know which is more boring, this match or listening to you prattle on.
[Ashe right back up and gets right back on Bolton. Ashe with a forearm to the back of Bolton's head as he was getting up. Ashe now lifts
Bolton to his feet and hooks him into a full nelson. Ashe now sweeps Bolton and drives him face first into the mat. Ashe rolls Bolton over and goes for the pin...
ONNNNNNEEEE..... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Bolton kicks out of it, but he didn't have much power behind it. Ashe's offensive strategy is working perfectly thus far.
[Ashe right back up and stomps on Bolton. Ashe now lifts Bolton up and nails a quick scoop slam. Ashe now over and goes up to the ropes and out to the apron. Ashe stands waiting. Bolton slowly begins to pull himself to his feet. Bolton turns as Ashe springboards up to the top rope and comes off. Ashe goes for a hurracarana but Bolton somehow able to gather enough strength to hold onto Ashe and just drops him with a powerbomb!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: A move of sheer desperation and it pays off, but Bolton goes down to the mat as well, the pressure of catching Ashe took it's toll on Bolton's weakened knee.
[Ashe and Bolton both down on the canvas. The ref checks on both men then begins his count...
1.... 2.... 3.... 4.... 5....Bolton slowly starting to respond. 6.... 7....Bolton makes it up and the ref stops the count. Bolton limps over and pulls Ashe to his feet. Bolton takes Ashe and hits a forearm to the back of Ashe's neck to stop him from trying anything. Bolton taking Ashe into the corner. Bolton hooks the head of Ashe and slowly ascends to the middle turnbuckle. Bolton now gives all he has and jumps and spins around drilling Ashe head first into the canvas with a Tornado
DDT! Bolton rolls Ashe over and tries for the pin.... ONNNNNNEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: HE GOT HI...NO! WoW! It doesn't get any closer than that. Ashe just popped that shoulder out.
[Bolton back up again and takes Ashe up. Bolton shoots Ashe into the corner but
Ashe controls himself and climbs to the top turnbuckle, he turns and leaps off and catches Bolton with a hurracarana!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT A MOVE! Ashe just run up the turnbuckles like a flight of steps. SHANE PROPONE: Whoopity-Doo.... [Ashe back up and takes Bolton. Ashe whips Bolton into the ropes, Bolton comes off and ducks a clothesline attempt. Bolton off the other side, Ashe turns and Bolton goes for a cross body block but Ashe holds on and uses the momentum to his advantage and hits a tilt-a-whirl into an Ace Crusher!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: MY GOD! Ashe just hit what he calls the Eternity Driver! Amazing move! Bolton is down and has to be out!
[Bolton down and Ashe but doesn't go for the pin. Ashe with the legs of Bolton and locks in a figure four ankle lock!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: The Engraved! Ashe is sending out a message here and Bolton is the unlucky one to be on the receiving end of it!
SHANE PROPONE: How do you know the names of all these guys moves? Do they send you a memo or something?
[Ashe with the Engraved locked in. The ref checks on Bolton who is clearly out of it after the Eternity Driver. The ref lifts the arm of Bolton which falls limp to the canvas. The ref turns and calls for the bell..]
[DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Bolton obviously unable to continue and the referee calls for the bell. LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner "The Salience" Morgan ASHE! JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe with an impressive win here... SHANE PROPONE: Anyone else notice that he still has that hold on Bolton? JOHN WILLIAMS: What? Ashe still with the Engraved locked in on Bolton! The ref trying to get him to let it go but Ashe holds on. Bolton is helpless!
[Ashe with the hold locked on as the ref pleads with him to let it go.] JOHN WILLIAMS: The ref now counting Ashe... [1.... 2.... 3.... 4....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Ashe finally lets go! What a night, and it's only just begun! We'll be right back...
[Cuts to a commercial...] INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING PRESENTS INTERNATIONAL INCIDENT March 31st, 2002 LIVE! From Madison Square Garden [Fades out and comes back to the ring in the Fleet Center..] JOHN WILLIAMS: The ICW returns to Pay-Per-View in just over 2 weeks! The first
International Championship of this era will be crowned that night..
SHANE PROPONE: Pillow and Riche should just save time and hand the belt right to Van Strife.
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife is certainly a favorite, but you never cantell what will happen. Up next we have a returning ICW star Tha Dragon taking on "The Dentist" Mike Wise...

INTRODUCING FIRST... [Loud Speakes blare] ".............GUESS WHO'S BACK............." ["Cocky" by Kid Rock sounds in the arena.] "......THEY SAY I'M COCKY, AND I SAY WHAT......" [The Fans jump to their feet, as the lights in the arena darken.] "....IT AINT BRAGGIN MOTHAEFFER IF YA BACK IT UP!!!" [From the back, Tha Dragon walks out, and stops. An emerald green cylinder shaped spotlight casts on him. Illuminated green, Tha Dragon raises his head, wet jet black hair dangling over his face. Looking to one side, then the other, he begins to make his way to the ring. Half way down the aisle, he raises his arms to a mixed pop, as the cylinder of light around him diminishes. Now, making his way to the ring, he hops up onto the apron, knee first, then pulling himself up. After glancing at the crowd for a moment, he makes his way into the ring between the top and middle ropes. He goes to Johnson and asks for the mic. Johnson gives it to him and Dragon goes to the center of the ring...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Tha Dragon obviously with something on his mind here... [Tha Dragon stands in the middle of the ring, holding the mic. He slides his thumb under his neck, signaling to cut his music. He raises the mic, and speaks.]
THA DRAGON - Well, here we are. International Championship Wrestling is back, and so is. . . actually, stop right there. Somethin' has been buggin' me for a very long time. My entire career, actually.
[Tha Dragon lifts his hands, palm up, then lowers them, showcasing his body, and atire.] THA DRAGON - This. That. Everything. It's not me. I didn't make it. I don't even like it, to tell ya the truth. There's a story behind it, and like it or not, you're going to hear it -- right now.
[He moves to the far corner, puts an arm on the turnbuckle, and looks out to the crowd.] THA DRAGON - When I decided to be a professional wrestler, I decided to learn from someone who had done it, and done it well. I wasn't interested in paying my hard earned cash to some worthless, fat, accountant, who never experienced what we go through. So, I went to my best friend. At the time, he was working in this craphole federation, that held it's matches in a pub. You may know him best as former ICW
International Champion, Shawn Sylkk. Shawn made me, everything about me. He gave me the idea for my name, which I've always despised, but never wanted to change for fear of losing identity. It's derived from his idol, and me being his protege, he named me after something he liked. We'll come back to this in a second. . .
[Tha Dragon moves again, back to the center of the ring, where he begins to rub his scalp.]
THA DRAGON - This long jet black hair is one of my well-known marks. It gives me that whole "dark" look. With these baby blue eyes of mine, you may wonder why i have black hair. No, it's not dyed. Worse. This atrocity was his stupid idea, too!
[Tha Dragon pulls at the front of his forehead, and slowly peels back the black hair, removing it from his head. Leaving some white globs of glue, and a few strands of black hair stuck to his head.]
THA DRAGON - That's right, blonde! Short, blonde hair! Goes much better with the blue eyes, don't you think? At least I'm not wearing that stupid half steel mask anymore, right? And oh yeah, that was his idea, too!
Everything you see, he created. He layed down for me, so I could win the DPW Heavyweight title. He brought me to ICW, and with his stroke got me my shot at the Television and North American
Championships! Hell, he even let me into the elite stable "EGO." Tha Dragon is nothing but a could-have-been, but gone-bad shell of Shawn
Sylkk! [Pulling off his bland, black tights, Tha Dragon reveals a fresher style. Still full-length, but baby blue in color, with gold trim. Down the left leg reads "TNNA."]
THA DRAGON - It's high time I make a name for myself, of myself, by myself. We'll start simple, very simple. I was born Nelson Alexander, and from this day forward, that's what I'll be known as. Hell, let's throw a spin to it. . . "Top-Notch," since that's exactly what I'll be outside of
Mr. Sylkk's shadow, and without him here.
["TN" NA drops the mic, and backs up to the ropes, looking toward the entrance ramp, awaiting his opponent.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Wow! Tha Dragon is no more, he is "Top Notch" Nelson Alexander! He has the desire in his eyes, he wants to go all the way to the top! SHANE PROPONE: Good for him, to bad he doesn't have what it takes! [Lance Johnson with the mic back goes to announce his opponent..] LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT.... ["Hundred Mile City" by Ocean Colour Scene begins to play..] LANCE JOHNSON: Hailing from Solo, London. Weighing in at 240 pounds here is
"The Dentist" Mike WISE!
["Hundred Mile City" by Ocean Colour Scene plays as Mike Wise steps through the curtains. Mike stands at the top of the ramp staring down at the ring. He then runs towards the ring, slides under the bottom rope, runs across the ring to one of the opposite corners, jumps up onto the second rope and slowly raises his hands above his shoulders as if to show off his great body to the crowd while looking out at that crowd.
Wise jumps down from the turnbuckle as he looks over at his opponent...] [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Here we go again... [Alexander and Wise walk to the center of the ring and go face to face. Both men trying to overpower the other.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men have a lot of power. Wise is outsized by an inch and about 25 pounds though.
[Alexander uses his greater size to shove Wise off and down to the canvas. Wise lands and quickly gets back to his feet. Alexander leaning against the ropes as Wise gets up. He now bounces off the ropes and catches
Wise off guard with a clothesline sending him against the ropes.

SHANE PROPONE: <cringes> Oh Mommy.... JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise with a low blow to Alexander cutting his momentum short. [Wise up to his feet as Alexander is doubled over. JOHN WILLIAMS: Wise to the floor to regroup after that little miscue there. Alexander still in the ring trying to gather himself. [Wise on the floor pacing a bit as Alexander looks out at him. Alexander follows in and goes for a pin....
ONNNNNNNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRRR.....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Wise gets his leg over the rope stopping the count. Alexander may have had it won if not for that, he took the wind out of Wise with those two moves on the floor.
[Alexander right back on Wise as he pulls him back to his feet. Alexander takes
Wise and sends him into the ropes. Wise comes off and
Alexander catches him with a spinning powerslam holding him all the way down and right into the pin....
ONNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! So close but not close enough! Wise able to lift the right shoulder off the canvas.

[Cuts to commercial...] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...]
