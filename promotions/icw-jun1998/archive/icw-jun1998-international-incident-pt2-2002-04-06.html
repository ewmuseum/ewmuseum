International Incident, 6 April 2002 (part 2 of 5)

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fades back in to Madison Square Garden...] ["Stand Alone" by Godsmack begins to play over the PA System.] [The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. The fans, who normally boo once this song hits, surprisingly cheer. The curtains at the wrestlers entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the light display is Van Strife himself. The crowd cheers, and Van Strife raises his arms in the air, getting another loud pop as he does so.
Van Strife flashes his trademark cocky smirk, then looks at his empty hands. He pauses, then puts up a finger, as if to tell the crowd to hold on. He walks to the back, emerging once again a moment later with his trusty Singapore Cane. Van Strife raises the cane in the air, and the
New York crowd responds with a huge hardcore pop! Van Strife wears an old "Lynch Mob" t-shirt, which bears the likenesses of himself, LOCO, "The
Confessor" Rey Jeran, and TLM. In addition, he wears a baggy pair of blue jeans, along with a pair of black wrestling boots. As always, he also wears his trademark pair of black Oakley sunglesses.
With a grin upon his face he makes his way down to the ring, walking down to ringside, slapping hands with the fans! Van Strife then makes his way to the ringside area, where he jumps onto the ring apron. He then vaults over the top rope, does a somersault, then lands on his feet, standing in the middle of the ring. He then calls for a microphone, which he receives.
As the music fades, Van Strife begins to speak.] SVS: Madison... Square... Garden. [The crowd pops for mention of their beloved arena.] SVS: This is the epitome of stardom. Every wrestler dreams of coming here and wrestling in front of a packed crowd. Since I was a child, I remember watching some of the greats compete in this ring. And now, once again, I return to participate in this arena... to
_win_.
[Van Strife smiles proudly as the crowd cheers.] SVS: There's an old saying, "You can talk the talk, but make sure you can walk the walk." Over these past few weeks, we've heard newcomers and ICW legends alike. They talk of winning the
ICW International Title. They talk of winning 3 matches, putting it all on the line to take what they think is rightfully theirs.
Well tonight, boys, it's time to "walk the walk." I've come into the ring twice in ICW, I've said I was going to overcome and win... and I did.
When my opponents said they had improved, I overcame. When my opponents said it was their time,
I overcame. When my opponents stooped to petty school-aged insults... I overcame. And tonight... in front of this capacity crowd... I _will_ overcome. [Another loud pop from the crowd.] SVS: The first man to overcome... Morgan Ashe. I remember back to the times when I was just like you, Ashe. The times when I needed to search my soul to find out just who I was. I remember when I just couldn't get my life straight. The only difference between us? You're making the changes look _damn_ good.
When looking at the ICW wrestlers, I pictured an ICW International Title match between you and
I. However, as fate might have it, we're squaring off in the first round. Instead of fighting with a title on the line, we're fighting just to get to the next round. It's going to be an all-out war, Ashe. The effort given by the two of us could very well overshadow even the main event. When it comes down to it, we're nothing less than two of the favorites to win the title. Whoever wins the match could very well be the man to take it.
[Van Strife taps the Singapore Cane on the mat, a bit nervous.] SVS: I'm not going to stand here and make promises, because tonight I can't guarantee anything.
No matter how much we want the title, it comes down to who can outlast the others. Tonight, I will do everything to show this crowd that even if I don't win it all... I am
_the_ contender.
I am the man who will bring the ICW back to its legendary status. I am the man who will make sure the crowd goes home happy. And tonight... I hope to be the man who will walk out of this arena the ICW
International Champion. I've got the "A" game ready to bring to the table... I hope each and every one of you in the back does so as well.
I'll see you in the ring. [With that, "Stand Alone" by Godsmack begins to play once again. The crowd cheers as a confident Van Strife ascends the turnbuckles, raising his arms into the air to allow the fans a nice little picture opportunity. He then walks to the back as we cut to the commentators.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife has his sights set on the Inernational Title, and to say he is the heavy favorite would be putting it very lightly...but it won't be an easy road, he faces a very dangerous opponent in Morgan Ashe in the first round later on, right now though we have Dave Pietka and Tommy Lionheart. Lionheart is in the back with Max
Power. Let's hear
what he has to say...
[Cut to the backstage area where, beside ICW interviewer Max Power, stands ring veteran and fan favorite, Tommy Lionheart. The Englishman looks focused and ready for action.]
MP: Tonight, as we are all aware, is the night the ICW crowns its International champion. Standing beside me now is one of the favorites to capture the title here, Tommy
Lionheart. Tommy, are you ready for what could be, a very long night? [A surprised look comes across Tommy’s face, as he answers the question.] TL: Max, I was born ready. [Slight pause.] TL: As you quite correctly pointed out, this IS the night the ICW crowns their
International
champion and what a great selection of athletes there is who could walk away the champion. You have the awesome Setzer Van Strife, the guy who is the firm favorite with the bookies. Then there’s Alex Cote; he’s always gonna be there or there about. Not to mention guys like Alex
Hawkins and Morgan Ashe. That’s a pretty healthy batch of guys all competing for the same goal, wouldn’t you agree?
[Max nods.] MP: Indeed it is. As is your first round opponent, Dave Pietka. [Tommy seems to agree.] TL: The guy is very talented; he’s proved that time and time again. Wherever he’s gone he’s performed, he’ll be a tough nut to crack. If I’m honest, I see this match as the hardest first round match to call –a match pitting two, err, household names and ring veterans against one another. It’s not gonna be easy, that’s for sure.
MP: Then… [Tommy interrupts.] TL: Saying that, though, I go into this contest coming off a very comprehensive victory over a
highly impressive up and comer, Alex Hawkins. With my morale high and my confidence even higher, I have no doubts whatsoever, that I will advance and continue in my quest to become … the champ!
[Suddenly, out of nowhere, a figure comes in and strikes Lionheart in the knee with a crowbar!
Heel pop!] JW: What the hell?! SP: Thank God! It's about time someone ended that interview... [The camera zooms backwards, revealing none other than Alex Hawkins to be wielding that crowbar.
After a brief grin, Hawkins brings the steel crowbar down onto Lionheart's left knee again and again! Heel pop!]
JW: IT'S HAWKINS! SOMEBODY STOP THAT MANIAC! For God's sakes, Lionheart has a match later tonight!
SP: Ooh...so I guess that means he won't be wrestling, huh? JW: Of course not! [Hawkins picks up the microphone left behind by Max Power.] AH: You see, Lionheart, I don't like things going unfinished. So, just now, I just finished things. No, wait a second --
[Hawkins drops the mic, grabs the crowbar with both hands and lifts it up into the air. With all of his might, Alex then brings the crowbar straight down onto Lionheart's left knee again.]
JW: Goddammit! [Hawkins once again picks up the mic.] AH: _Now_ it's finished. [With that, Hawkins throws the mic down and simply exits the interview area without a qualm.
Fade.] JOHN WILLIAMS: My god...Lionheart is about to get into the ring and Hawkins just attacked him with a crowbar! Lionheart may have just had his chances taken away from him...

Hailing from London, England ... weighing TWO hundred and THIRTY-FIVE pounds ... The current NWWA Television champion ...
TTTTTOMMMMMMYYY LLLIOOONNHHHEAARRRRTTT!!! # Show me it’s worth # # Make me understand it # # I’ve been crawling in the dark, looking for the answer # [Lionheart strides towards the ringside area, slapping as many fans’ hands as he can. The Englishman strolls up the ring steps, and into the ring.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart looks rearing to go here tonight, but he'll has his hands full with
"Heavy Mental". I'm surprised Tommy is even able to walk out here though.. SHANE PROPONE: Who cares? ["Chop Suey" by System of a Down fades in. A scream echoes through the PA, and is sounds a lot like the "Psychoholic" scream of the Harbinger of Madness, the Hardcore
Spokesperson of the 21st Century, and your Idol, Rolemodel, and H-E-R-O! The spotlight hits the end of the entryway, and there stand three people. The largest one, wearing a black
"SECURITY, PUNK!" T-shirt, jeans, and unremoveable Ray-bans is immediately Identified as "Marz" Candress. The woman dressed in see-though leopard print blouse and pants with a black sports bra and Rio-cut
Bikini Bottoms with Holographic Oakleys turns out to be "Double D" Devin DeMasters. And the one in the middle, wearing the "FINITE BRAINS, INIFINITE BALLS" T-shirt and black jeans and boots is...]

[Pietka and his entourage walk down the aisle as the quick tongue of SoaD starts screaming.
The Heavy Mental One shows himself off to their crowd, a mixture of love and hate and hateful love from his "fans". He does a little spin around... before reverting to a more serious look.
Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress follow from behind, Candress standing close to DeMasters.
Pietka and DeMasters stand in front of the ring, and Candress steps up and opens the ring ropes for them. DeMasters steps through the ropes, while Pietka climbs over the turnbuckle and
Moonsaults himself onto the canvas. Candress releases the ropes and steps over them. After getting up from his flip, Pietka points to the signs in the crowd, some reading
"HEAVY MENTAL MANIA", "Dave Pietka = Crowd Pleaser", and "BRING BACK DEVIN'S DOUBLE Ds".] @YOU WANTED TO! #Grab a brush and put a little makeup! @YOU WANTED TO! #Hide the scars to fade away the shakeup! @YOU WANTED TO! #Why'd you leave the keys upon the table? @YOU WANTED TO! [Pietka spastically jumps around the ring, spouting out God knows what, slapping himself in the head as the song becomes louder and harder... and then he calms down when he hears...]
#I don't think you trust... #In... my... Self-Righteous suicide. #I... cry... when angels deserve to... #DIE! [Pietka stands in the middle of the ring, his arms spread out and his psychotic grin on his face. DeMasters lays herself down in front of Pietka, and Marz stands behind him. Pietka's smile seems to grow bigger as he looks up and shoves his hand into the air and...NOTHING YOU
IDIOT! This is Dave Pietka... He don't need no stinkin' pyro! The three move from their pose.
Marz steps over the ring ropes again. Pietka and DeMasters exchange a quick kiss, and then she steps through the open ropes provided by Marz. Pietka just trots around the ring a bit, waiting for that opening bell... a crazed look on his face as "Chop Suey" fades out.]
SHANE PROPONE: WOOO!! "Double D"!! JOHN WILLIAMS: Control yourself. [DING!!!!! DING!!!!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: And we're under way. [Lionheart hops on his feet in the corner anxiously awaiting for Pietka with anticipation while Pietka just stand in the corner looking at him with a stern face.]
SHANE PROPONE: Lionheart looks like a bunny out there! JOHN WILLIAMS: Yeah, the Easter Bunny! SHANE PROPONE: Don't' try to make joke, John, you're just not good at it. [Pietka walks towards and Lionheart's hopping slows as his knee injury begins to effect him and slow him down. Pietka approaches him quickly and they lock up. Pietka pushes Lionheart into the corner. Pietka stops and begins to back up but then comes at
Lionheart with a quick right but Lionheart ducks under the punch and drop kicks Pietka into the back driving him into corner. Lionheart backs up as Pietka turns back and looks at Lionheart with a small grin.]
SHANE PROPONE: And Pietka doesn't event care! JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart gets first strike but Pietka shrugs it off. [Pietka rises to his feet and shakes his head. Pietka goes right at Lionheart again. Lionheart side steps and takes Pietka down with a drop toe hold. Lionheart starts to back up but Pietka kicks backwards hard knock Lionheart back, between the middle ropes and to the floor. Lionheart tried to regain himself and looks up to see Candress coming towards him.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Get out of there, Lionheart! [Lionheart scuffles back under the top rope quickly, avoiding Candress, but catches a few boots in the back from Pietka. Pietka with control, pulls Lionheart to his feet and whips him into the rope. Pietka goes for a clothesline but Lionheart ducks but before reaching the rope
Lionheart's knee gives way and his falls to the mat.] SHANE PROPONE: I don't see Lionheart going very far into this tournament. JOHN WILLIAMS: He's got heart but who can get through three matches, including a cage match, with a knee injury like that?
[Pietka turns to see Lionheart down. Looks for a second then shrugs with a smile. Pietka gives Lionheart a few more boots to the back as Lionheart tries to stand.
Pietka backs up and measures Lionheart waiting. Lionheart stumbles up and Pietka runs at him and jumps over him with a sunset flip.]
ONE..... TWO..... THRRRRRRR....... [Lionheart pushes himself up and sit on Pietka for the pin.] ONE..... TWO...... [Pietka rolls him back over for the pins again.] ONE..... TWO...... THHHH..... [Lionheart kicks Pietka in the face escaping the pin.] SHANE PROPONE: Well, that was pointless. JOHN WILLIAMS: Close match here thus far but how long can Lionheart go with that leg injury?
SHANE PROPONE: Not like he would win the belt anyways. [Lionheart stumbles to his feet first as Pietka checks his nose for blood after the boot to the face. He looks up and Lionheart drop kicks him to the face. Pietka falls back and Tommy falls holding his knee. Both men lay on the mat as the ref counts.]
One..... Two..... Three..... Pietka begins to stir. Four..... Five..... Pietka to his feet. [Pietka jumps at Lionheart and lands a knee onto Lionheart's knee. Tommy yells out in pain.
Pietka begins to viciously punch away at Lionheart's knee with both fist.] JOHN WILLIAMS: This match has to be stopped! SHANE PROPONE: What? It's just getting good! JOHN WILLIAMS: You're sick, you know that? SHANE PROPONE: Eh. [Pietka jumps up and hits Lionheart with a knee on more time. Lionheart lays on the mat writhing in pain. Pietka to his feet he backs up to the ropes and stands for a minute catching his breath after that onslaught of rights and left. Suddenly a roar from the crowd is heard through out the crowd.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What is that? SHANE PROPONE: LOOK!! [The Masked Superstar Jobber hops the guardrail behind Devin DeMasters. He grabs her from behind and rips her shirt off revealing her black lacy bra. The crowd goes crazy.]
SHANE PROPONE: YEEEEESSSSSS!!!! [TMSJ jumps back over the guardrail, narrowly missing the grasp of Candress. He turns back to the ring and smells the shirt.] JOHN WILLIAMS: That's just sick!! SHANE PROPONE: Oh come on, John! Looks at her!! JOHN WILLIAMS: I'm a married man!! SHANE PROPONE: You mean you're on a short lease! [Pietka looks back yelling towards TMSJ, Lionheart comes up behind him, underneath, and rolls him up with all he has.]
ONE..... TWO...... JOHN WILLIAMS: Pietka kicks out!! THREE!!! SHANE PROPONE: But not in time!! [DING!!!!! DING!!!!!!] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, Tommy LIONHEART! JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart pulls it off! SHANE PROPONE: Only because of that idiot Jobber! JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart advances here but I don't know what good it does him. If that match had gone on much longer I think Pietka would have come away with the win. Can
Lionheart
recouperate enough before his second round bout with Cote to have a chance? I guess only time will tell...
SHANE PROPONE: Hopefully they elimiante each other, then Van Strife can have a bye right through the finals and have the title handed over to him!
[Cuts to commercial...] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...]
