Friday Night Fallout, 22 March 2002 (part 2 of 5)

INTRODUCING FIRST... [Loud Speakes blare] ".............GUESS WHO'S BACK............." ["Cocky" by Kid Rock sounds in the arena.] "......THEY SAY I'M COCKY, AND I SAY WHAT......" [The Fans jump to their feet, as the lights in the arena darken.] "....IT AINT BRAGGIN MOTHAEFFER IF YA BACK IT UP!!!" [From the back, "Top Notch" Nelson Alexander walks out, and stops. An emerald green cylinder shaped spotlight casts on him. Illuminated green, Alexander raises his head, his now blonde and a cocky grin on his face. Looking to one side, then the other, he begins to make his way to the ring. Half way down the aisle, he raises his arms to a mixed pop, as the cylinder of light around him diminishes. Now, making his way to the ring, he hops up onto the apron, knee first, then pulling himself up. After glancing at the crowd for a moment, he makes his way into the ring between the top and middle ropes. Moving to the far corner, he drops to his but, sitting, awaiting the start of the match.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... [Darkness.] V/O: On the 6th day, God created man. God saw all that he had made, and it was very good. Thus the heavens and the earth were completed in all their vast array. By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. And God blessed the 7th day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done. On the 8th day god...Broke The Mold....
["Broken Mold" flashes over the screen as the "The Grudge" by Tool begins to play.] #Wear the grudge like a crown of negativity. Calculate what we will or will not tolerate. Desperate to control all and everything. Unable to forgive your scarlet lettermen.# [A pale red light shines down on the top of the ramp way where The Salience stands, his head is held down as his long black hair hangs around his head and face. He isn't large but his muscular form is imposing in the shadows and shilloute as his chest heaves and falls.]
#Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Justify denials and grip 'em to the lonesome end. Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Terrified of being wrong. Ultimatum prison cell.# [Morgan ducks his head forward and throws his head back, the hair and water being knocked away from his face as he looks out over the arena. Ashe slowly makes his way to the ring, the spot light following him to the ring. The fans reach out to touch him but fall short as he stares at his home--the ring!]
#Saturn ascends, choose one or ten. Hang on or be humbled again.# [Ashe stops at the ringside area and looks up at the ring. For a long moment he stands there.]
#Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Justify denials and grip 'em to the lonesome end. Saturn ascends, comes round again. Saturn ascends, the one, the ten. Ignorant to the damage done.# #Wear the grudge like a crown of negativity. Calculate what we will or will not tolerate. Desperate to control all and everything. Unable to forgive your scarlet lettermen. Wear the grudge like a crown. Desperate to control. Unable to forgive. And we're sinking deeper. Defining, confining, controlling, and we're sinking deeper.# [Morgan Ashe walks back around the ring in the other direction and reaches up grabbing ahold of the top rope and stepping up onto the apron. Morgan Ashe stands on the apron looking to the side for a moment glancing back out over the crowded arena before flipping over the top rope into the ring.]
[DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: And here we go. Ashe and Nelson both trying to make a name for themselves here in the ICW. Both picked up good wins last week, but only one can repeat that same success...
[Alexander and Ashe lock up in the center of the ring, Ashe with a side headlock on Alexander.
Alexander trying to find a way out. He backs Ashe into the ropes and goes to shoot him across but Ashe holds onto his head and bulldogs him into the mat. Ashe now pulls
Alexander back to
his feet and sends him into the ropes, Alexander under a clothesline but gets caught off the other side with a spin heel kick right on the butt of the jaw. Ashe now up to his feet and he takes Alexander back up with him once again. Ashe takes Alexander and lifts him up. Ashe hooks him up and nails an inverted atomic drop. Ashe hooks Alexander up and drives him into the mat with an inverted side Russian legsweep.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe just dominating in the early goings here...Let's see if
Alexander can turn
the tides..
[Ashe now rolls Alexander over and hooks the leg for the pin... ONNNNNNEEEEE... TWWWWWOOO... THRR... And Alexander kicks out of it! Ashe now pulls Nelson back to his feet and backs him into the corner. Ashe nails him with a few hard shots to the jaw then climbs up onto the middle turnbuckle and begins to punch away...]
[CROWD:1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8. . ] [Alexander pushes Ashe to the mat. Ashe pops right back up and Alexander charges out and levels him with a huge lariat! Alexander now on the attack, he puts the boots to Ashe.
Alexander then pulls Morgan back to his feet and shoots him into the ropes. Alexander catches him coming off with a boot to the gut. Alexander now off the ropes and nails
Ashe with a
swinging neck breaker. Alexander now back up to his feet and takes Ashe with him. Alexander hooks the head of Ashe then runs across the ring and springboards off of the middle turnbuckle and comes off nailing Ashe with a tornado DDT. Alexander hooks the leg for the pin...
ONNNNNNNEEEEE... TWWWWWWOOOO... THRRRRRR...] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Ashe kicks out of it! SHANE PROPONE: Of course he does. Alexander is anything but "Top Notch" he would have never been anything without help from his former friends.
[Alexander right back up to his feet and nails Ashe with a few kicks as he is still on the ground. Alexander pulls Ashe back to his feet and shoots him into the turnbuckle then runs in nailing him with an avalanche in the corner. Alexander now over the top and onto the apron, he now hooks Ashe and nails him with a hangman's neckbreaker over the top rope.
Alexander climbs back up onto the apron and all the way to the top rope, he measures Ashe and comes off with a
MASSIVE big splash! Alexander now hooks the leg for the pin again... ONNNNNNNEEEE... TWWWWWWOOOO... THRRRRRRE...] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Ashe got his foot on the bottom rope before the three count. What a close call there! [Alexander thought he had him there. He pulls Ashe back up to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Alexander catches him coming off with a huge spinebuster. Alexander now pulls him to the middle of the ring and climbs back up to the top rope. Alexander comes off with a big legdrop...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: ASHE ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY! Alexander landing right on his tailbone! SHANE PROPONE: HA! Loser... [Alexander down on the mat as Ashe pulls himself up by the ropes. Ashe back to his feet and goes over and takes Alexander up by his hair. Ashe with a European uppercut to the jaw of
Alexander. Ashe now sends him into the ropes. Alexander comes off and Ashe catches him coming off with a dropkick. Ashe now pulls him back to his feet, lifts him up and drops him into the mat with a tombstone piledriver. Ashe now going for the pin...
ONNNNNNNEEEE... TWWWWWWOOOO... THRRRREE...] JOHN WILLIAMS: YES...NO! Alexander somehow managed to get the right shoulder up in time, I thought he had him there for sure.
[Ashe getting a bit frustrated now, he pulls Alexander back to his feet and goes to send him into the ropes again but Alexander reverses it. Alexander ducks his head and catches Ashe with a back body drop. Alexander now pulls Ashe back to his feet once again and sends him HARD into the turnbuckles. Alexander runs in nailing him with a clothesline. Nelson now takes Ashe and shoots him across to the other side and runs at him again. . Ashe now moves out of the way and
Alexander hits the turnbuckles hard. Alexander turns around and Ashe with a boot to the gut.
Ashe hooks Alexander up and..] JOHN WILLIAMS: A MARTYR'S END! WHAT A MOVE! [Ashe now going for the pin.... ONNNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRREEE....] [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Ashe pulls off the big win here! LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, "The Salience" Morgan ASHE! JOHN WILLIAMS: We need to take a quick break, but don't go away! [Cuts to commercial...] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...] [Fades back in to the arena..] VOICE OVER: ARE YOU PREPARED TO BE HAMMERED?! [Crowd begins to cheer loudly as the lights fade.] ["What comes around" by Ill Nino begins to play over the PA system.] #I HATE YOU..... #I HATE YOU..... #I HATE YOU..... #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! ["The Hammer" Alex Cote steps out from behind the curtain as huge flames blast from under the entrance ramp and the lights flash back on. The cheers because almost mind numbing.]
[APPEARANCE: Black jeans with a pair of black steel toed army style boots. Also, a midnight blue tang top with the words "PREPARE TO BE HAMMERED" on the front. Around his neck hangs a silver chain with a silver dagger on it, a pair of black leather gloves cut at the knuckles on his hands, and a black leather trenchcoat covers him from shoulders to ankles.]
#YOU THINK THAT I'M THE ONE TO BLAME #EVERYTHING I LOSE IS JUST A PIECE OF WHAT THERE IS #TO GAIN #YOU THINK IT'S COOL AND ALL IS FINE #NOW IS THE DAY WHEN YOU PAY, THIS IS MY TIME #I FEEL NOTHING FOR BUT PAIN #I AM WHAT YOU WILL BE, YOU ARE DYING IN ME #I LOVE YOU.....I HATE YOU.....I MISS YOU..... #YOU ALWAYS THINK THAT YOU'RE SO PERFECT #THOSE THOUGHTS DROVE ME AWAY FROM HOME #BUT IF YOU PUT ME THROUGH YOUR TEST #THEN I WILL FADE..... #I HAVE NOTHING TO SAY #BUT I FEEL LIKE MY MOUTH IS WIDE OPEN #EVERYTHING THAT IS REAL #COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! [Alex stands at the top of the entrance ramp with a serious face.] JOHN WILLIAMS: "The Hammer" looking very focused tonight! SHANE PROPONE: Because he knows Van Strife is going to give him one hell of a whoop'n! JOHN WILLIAMS: Whoop'n? SHANE PROPONE: Get with it, Williams! [Alex looks into the crowd as the music fades. He raises the mic up to his mouth.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: New Jersey..... [Crowd pops. Cote looks out at the raging audience and wait for then to settle down.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Tonight is not a pay-per-view.....tonight is not the crowning of a new champion, and tonight is not even anything truly special to the world.
BUT.....but for me it's a very special night. When you look up here you don't see the usual Alex Cote. I'm not here wise crack'n with my known cocky grin. Tonight is the day where I find out whether coming out of retirement was worth it, whether I have what it takes to stand atop the wrestling world once again, tonight, my stepping stool is YOU, Setzer Van Strife.
[The crowd cheers at the mention of SVS. Alex continues to stare out.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Van Strife, a name known through out the world, a true
"bad guy". A
man, who when you step into the ring with, you don't expect to walk out on top.
But tonight.....tonight we shall see if the man lives up to the legend. He's faced the best around the world, in the EMWC, the INWI, the SCRA, and even the LVPWA. But,
Setzer, have you
ever faced a force like....."The Hammer"?
[Crowd explodes.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: You've been around the globe, you've been in some of the biggest and best matches in wrestling history but NEVER have you faced a man of my magnitude! NEVER have you faced a man with my power. And NEVER have you faced a man with such......heart. You're facing the man who MADE the ICW, and ICW's hero, mascot, hero and all these people here are the prove of that!
[Alex holds his arm up spanning the crowd as they cheer loudly.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I've trained my whole life to get into the ring with a man like you, a man at the top, and I will not blow this opportunity. This is not the EMWC, this is NOT the
INWI, this is not even the LVPWA [Alex starts to turn red and his face turns to rage] DAMMIT!!! THIS IS THE ICW!! THIS IS MY HOME!! THIS IS MY FEDERATION!! I WILL NOT LET YOU BEAT ME!! I WILL NOT LET YOU REIGN IN MY FEDERATION!! DAMMIT!!! YOU'RE IN MY YARD NOW BITCH!!! [Crowd goes crazy as Alex begins to calm himself.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE[sighs]: Time is quickly moving like a freight train,
Van Strife, and
soon enough we will stand in that ring, face to face, mano a mano, and when it's all said and done, when the smoke clears, and blood has been shed, you will lay down on the mat a BEATEN man for the 1....2....3! VAAAAANNNNN SSSSTTTTRRRRRIIIIIIFFFEEE!!!!! You better prepare to be hammered cuz I.....TAKE.....NO.....PRIIIISSSSOOOONNNNEEEERRRRSSSS!!!
[Alex slams the mic down and walks to the back as his music starts up and the crowd cheers.]
SHANE PROPONE: WHOA!! JOHN WILLIAMS: What is with Cote? SHANE PROPONE: He's lost it! JOHN WILLIAMS: Lost it? He's just passionate about the ICW. SHANE PROPONE: No....he's lost it. He's crazy! JOHN WILLIAMS: Cote is focused...and he will need to be if he wants to walk out with a win here tonight. We have another match to get to but I am hearing we have something gonig on backstage...
[Mike Wise is seen in his dressing room. He is sitting on a folding chair winding his black tape around his right wrist, his left wrist is already done. Wises wife and kids are also sitting in the room with him. Mikes wife Bunny has a very noticeable fat lip.
Mike finish’s taping his wrist and stands up. As Mike does this his daughter suddenly hugs
Bunny’s arm as if
trying to cower behind it.]
Mike Wise: What’s wrong with her? Bunny: [Now hugging her daughter back] Nothing Mike. Good luck tonight darling. [Mike ignores her and leaves the room.] [Cuts back to the ring as Lance Johnson stands in the center..]
