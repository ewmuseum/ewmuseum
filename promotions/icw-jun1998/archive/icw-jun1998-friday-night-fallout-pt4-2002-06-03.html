[CROWD: MASSIVE POP!] LANCE JOHNSON: IT IS A TAG TEAM MATCH SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... ["Head Like A Hole" by Nine Inch Nails cranks up, and the crowd does to, booing incessently.]
LANCE JOHNSON: He weighs in tonight at two hundred and thirty-two pounds, and hails from Grosse
Pointe Farms, Michigan... [The second he peeks his head out of the curtain, the boos grow in intensity, as in two weeks, this man has worked his way into the disdane and hatred of every man, woman, and child in the audience.]

Bikini Bottoms with Holographic Oakleys turns out to be "Double D" Devin DeMasters. And the one in the middle, wearing the "BOBISM! ~ www.gotbob.net " T-shirt and black jeans and boots is...] LANCE JOHNSON: Accompanied to the ring by "Double D" Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress... [Pietka and his entourage walk down the aisle as the quick tounge of SoaD starts screaming.
The Heavy Mental One shows himself off to their crowd, a mixture of love and hate and hateful love from his "fans". He does a little spin around... before reverting to a more serious look.
Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress follow from behind, Candress standing close to DeMasters.
Pietka and DeMasters stand in front of the ring, and Candress steps up and opens the ring ropes for them. DeMasters steps through the ropes, while Pietka climbs over the turnbuckle and
Moonsaults himself onto the canvas. Candress releases the ropes and steps over them. After getting up from his flip, Pietka points to the signs in the crowd, some reading
"HEAVY MENTAL MANIA", "Dave Pietka = Crowd Pleaser", and "BRING BACK DEVIN'S DOUBLE Ds".] @YOU WANTED TO! #Grab a brush and put a little makeup! @YOU WANTED TO! #Hide the scars to fade away the shakeup! @YOU WANTED TO! #Why'd you leave the keys upon the table? @YOU WANTED TO! #I don't think you trust... #In... my... Self-Righteous suicide. #I... cry... when angels deserve to... #DIE! [Pietka stands in the middle of the ring, his arms spread out and his psychotic grin on his face. DeMasters lays herself down in front of Pietka, and Marz stands behind him. Pietka's smile seems to grow bigger as he looks up and shoves his hand into the air and...NOTHING YOU
IDIOT! This is Dave Pietka... He don't need no stinkin' pyro! The three move from their pose.
Marz steps over the ring ropes again. Pietka and DeMasters exchange a quick kiss, and then she steps through the open ropes provided by Marz. Pietka just trots around the ring a bit, waiting for that opening bell... a crazed look on his face as "Chop Suey" fades out.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND THERE OPPONENTS... ["Sad But True" by Metallica starts to blare over the arena.] LANCE JOHNSON: "About to make his way down to the ring weighing in at 263 pounds and standing in at 6 feet 3 inches, he hails from Boston, Massachusetts. He is...
... "SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER!!!" [As the announcer finishes the name, the curtains split and out walks Shane
Alexander wearing
a pair of old black ripped jeans and a black leather jacket that is stained with blood and dirt. The jacket hangs down to about his mid-thigh. Shane has his head down, his long dark hair filled with water hangs in front of his face. As the song hits it's first acoustic break
Shane snaps his head back and the fans react with a large cheer.] # Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who takes you there # Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who cares [Shane begins to make his way to the ring staring at the ring, almost as if it is sacred. # They # They betray # I'm your only true friend now # They # They'll betray # I'm forever there [Shane makes it into the ring stepping through the middle rope. Once inside
Shane opens his
jacket and exposes a very gruesome scar across his stomach. Shane now takes off his jacket and waits in the corner.]
# I'm your dream, make you real # I'm your eyes when you must steal # I'm your pain when you can't feel # Sad but true [The music begins to fade out as Shane stretches in the corner.] LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS PARTNER... [Hoobastank's smash hit "Crawling in the Dark" begins to play over the PA system.] LANCE JOHNSON: Hailing from London, England ... Weighing TWO hundred and THIRTY-FIVE pounds ... [Every fan in the arena turn their heads to the entrance way, awaiting their champion, as the vocals kick in.]
# I will dedicate # # And sacrifice my everything for just a second's worth # # Of how my story's ending # # And I wish I could know if the directions that I take # # And all the choices that I make won't end up all for nothing # [The lights dim down, as a bright white spotlight flashes down just before the entrance.] # Show me it's worth # # Make me understand it # # I've been crawling in the dark, looking for the answer # LANCE JOHNSON: He is the ICW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION ... Here is ... TTTOOMMMMYYY LLLIOONNHHEAARRRTT!!!! [The International champion, right on cue, appears from behind the curtain into the arena to a huge face pop from the sold out crowd in Uniondale, NY. The champion, looking happy and confident, begins his stroll down the aisle. Like always, slapping as many fans' hands as he can along the way.
He jogs up the steel ring steps, the spotlight still shining down on him, and enters the ring via through the top and middle ropes. Without wasting any time, the champ leaps up onto the top turnbuckle and raises his title belt high above his head to another great reaction. He leaps back down, and grants the referee with the belt as the music fades out and the lights return to their normal brightness.]
