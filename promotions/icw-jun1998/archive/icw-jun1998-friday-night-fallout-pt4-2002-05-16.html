Friday Night Fallout, 16 May 2002 (part 4 of 5)

16 May 2002

=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [TV TITLE MATCH] Dave Fresco V/S "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife WRITTEN BY - [name withheld] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [The crowd is at a low hum of everyone talking amongst themselves. Awaiting another high- impact match, the crowd is in eager anticipation, when the guitar riff of
"Surfacing" by
Slipknot blares over the Public Announce System. The crowd throws a mixed reaction as the drums pick up and the lights dim down so that green and grey spotlights glow out from the entranceway.]
[Thick smoke begins to pour out from the entranceway as the crowd is in an excited mood.] LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way to the ring from Miami Florida, weighing in at
258 pounds... DAAAAVE FREEESSSSSSCOOOOOO~!! [The crowd continues to throw that mixed reaction up in the air as the song continues. Out form the smoke, steps out the tall dark Hawaiian Giant, Dave Fresco.]
[Clad in navy blue denim-looking sweatpants, a black Tee-shirt that reads "I am..." on the front in blood red lettering, and a pair of black Tims, he walks down the aisle with his head held up and a smug grin on his face. His eyes a narrowed, showing focus and energy flowing through his whole body.]
[As he makes it down to ringside, Fresco pulls himself onto the apron, via the middle rope.
The back of his shirt can now be seen and it reads "The Greatest!!", Fresco puts his gigantic size 15 EEE foot over the top rope, followed by it's twin. He puts himself onto the second rope and extends his arms out, then clenches his fists and curls his arms down, flexing for the crowd. His urban voice with a hint of a tropical accent is heard in the form of a deep scream to the crowd. His face goes back to it's normal grinning as the song fades out. He climbs down and bounces off the ropes, awaiting the opening bell.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Fresco looks ready as he is about to go after the ICW Television
Champion. SHANE PROPANE: Yes he does, but... his opponent is a man that needs no introduction, but here he comes!
[The beginning guitar riff to 'One Step Closer' by Linkin Park plays.] [The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. A thick smoke begins to fill the wrestlers entrance, as the fans in the arena that already know who is about to come out begin to boo loudly.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way down the aisle, hailing from Chicago, Illinois... standing at six feet, one inch tall and weighing in at 213 pounds... here is... "2 SMOOTH" SETZER VAAAAAAAAAN STRIIIIIIIIIIFE!!!!!!!!!! [The curtains at the wrestler's entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the smoke and light display is Van Strife himself, the crowd booing even more loudly. Van
Strife stands
there, his trademark cocky smirk shining through the flashy show as he raises his arms in the air.
Van Strife dresses in a silk anime shirt, unbuttoned with no shirt underneath. He also wears a baggy pair of black jean shorts and a pair of black wrestling boots. Accompanying the ensemble is his trademark pair of black Oakley sunglasses and his Singapore Cane. With a grin upon his face he makes his way down to the ring, walking down to ringside ignoring the fans that wave signs in his face and threaten him. Van Strife then makes his way to the ringside area, where he jumps onto the ring apron. He then vaults over the top rope.
Van Strife then moves to his corner to prepare for his match.] *[DING DING DING]* JOHN WILLIAMS: There's the bell, so let's get down to business. [Van Strife and Fresco step up to the middle of the ring where they both lock up in a collar and elbow tie-up. Strife with the upper hand as he turns it into a standing headlock. He wrenches down to cause as much pain as possible.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: He looks like he really wants to put a punishing on Fresco. SHANE PROPANE: Of course he does, Dave Fresco is trying to take the belt that
Van Strife has,
away from him. How would you react?
[Fresco able to get Van Strife to the ropes, and shoots him to the other ropes. Fresco waits... and hits a beautiful standing dropkick. Van Strife is quick to his feet though.] [Strife is met with a hard right hand, that drops him, but he is once again to his fet quickly.
Fresco hits another right, and this one sends Van Strife to the outside to think things over.]
SHANE PROPANE: A smart champion. Rolls to the outside to catch his breath and throw Fresco off his gameplan.
JOHN WILLIAMS: Or he could be shaken up by the way Fresco is coming at him tonight. [Setzer wipes his mouth, checking for blood before returning into the ring. Fresco has a full head of steam now and tries to hit Van Strife with a lariat as he gets in the ring but... Van
Strife ducks and sends Fresco hard to the outside of the ring with a back body drop.]
[The fans give a nice HEEL pop!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife with the veteran ring knowledge, he seen Fresco coming and sent him flying to the outside.
SHANE PROPANE: And look, he is going after him now... this is where it gets good. [Van Strife climbs to the outside as the ref starts his count. Van Strife picks up Fresco and slams his head into the announcer's table.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Watch out Shane, they are right out here in front of us. ..1.. ..2.. ..3.. ..4.. [As the ref reaches the four count Van Strife rolls Fresco back into the ring. He follows in and lifts Fresco to his feet only to drop him with a big-time DDT. He goes for the cover...
OOOONNNNEEEEE... TWWWWOOOOOOOOO... THR- ... and Fresco gets the shoulder up.] [BIG FACE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: This kid is not just going to give up already. He knows this is his one chance at the TV Title.
SHANE PROPANE: If he was smart he would just lay down and take the loss, I mean there are going to be other title matches in his career. Van Strife will destroy this kid, watch, you'll see.
[Van Strife lifts the groggy Fresco to his feet. He grabs Fresco's head and sets him up for another DDT, motioning to the crowd that it is over... but Fresco reverses and sends the TV champ flying with a belly to belly suplex.]
[HUGE FACE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Good reversal there by the champion. [Fresco now tries to gain some offense as he follows up on Strife with a leg drop. He now picks up Van Strife and sends him to the turnbuckle with an Irish whip. Van Strife comes off the turnbuckles staggered. Fresco follows in and scoops up Strife with a big body slam.]
SHANE PROPANE: This kid's attitude makes me wanna puke! [Fresco now looking to the crowd, he seems like he knows he is in control now. He lifts up Van Strife, lifts him in what appears to be a reverse suplex... and DROPS Van
Strife with a
stunner, from the vertical position.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: That move right there is called, "The Coconut Crusher." And it looks like Van
Strife could lose his belt right here. All he needs to do is co--
[John Williams is cut off as the camera pans to the entranceway, where Sid
Starr is standing.
He begins to walk down towards the ring. In the ring Fresco has just noticed that Starr is on his way out here.]
[HEEL POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: What the hell is he doin' out here? SHANE PROPANE: What? A fellow wrestler can't come down and get a good view at this TV Title match? Jeesh!
[As Fresco watches Starr make his way to the ring, he is about 10 feet from the ring now, Van
Strife has managed to get back to his feet, and whacks Fresco with a dropkick to the back of the head of Fresco, sending Dave over the top ropes... right in front of
Starr.] SHANE PROPANE: Uh oh! This one looks like it could get ugly. Both men on the outside of the ring have a history here in ICW. But wait, Van Strife seems to have hurt his knee and is asking the ref to take a look at it.
JOHN WILLIAMS: IT'S A SET UP! Look at this. [As Van Strife distracts the ref, Starr begins to lay some punishment on Fresco. Sid Starr begins to lay some HARD boots to the head of Fresco. Starr now lifts up Fresco, and sends him
CRASHING into the ring post. Fresco's skull was just busted open by that ring post.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: This is wrong... it shouldn't end like this. Dave Fresco is just out here to try and win the TV Title. Not to fight in a handicapped match.
[Starr now rolls Fresco back in the ring. Where as soon as this happens, Van
Strife seems have
healed his knee. He goes over and covers the busted Fresco...
OOONNNNNNEEEEEE... TWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOO... THR- ... and Fresco just gets his shoulder up before the ref counts three. Van
Strife is PISSED!]
[HUGE FACE POP!] SHANE PROPANE: WHAT?!?! This ref can't count, that should have been three. I can't believe that the ICW can't afford some refs that can count to three.
[Van Strife and Starr are equally upset at the count. But Van Strife gets back on the offensive, lifting Fresco to his feet. Van Strife now sends Fresco to the other side with an irish whip. He goes for a clothesline, but Fresco manages to duck, hits the other ropes and connects with a big flying elbow, right to the side of the the champ's face.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Fresco counters and puts Van Strife on his back, he goes for the pin... but no referee as Sid Starr is on the apron arguing with the ref.
[Fresco notices that no-one is counting and immedately sees Starr on the apron. He goes after Starr swinging with a right hand, but Starr jumps off the apron just in time. Fresco now saying something to Starr.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Keep your eyes on your opponent, not the outside. This Sid Starr is becoming a real thorn in the side of Dave Fresco.
SHANE PROPANE: C'Mon Setzer, get up, you can do it. [Fresco now turns towards Van Strife who is still laying on the mat from that flying elbow.
Fresco moves in and... SCHOOL BOY BY THE CHAMP, the ref begins the count... OOONNNNNNEEEEEE... TWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOO... THR- ... and Fresco kicks out!] [MASSIVE FACE POP!] SHANE PROPANE: AGAIN! This is ridiculous. JOHN WILLIAMS: No it's not, it's the resiliancy of a young superstar who has his heart set on that Television Championship. Only he has to go through TWO MEN to get it tonight.
[Both men return to their feet and begin to exhange right hands. They battle back and fourth, with Fresco coming out on top, dazing Van Strife long enough to hook him up for a Northern
Lights suplex. Fresco follows in and lifts Setzer again.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: He is motioning to the fans again, saying that the end is near. He hooks him up and... [Setzer Van Strife is able to once again reverse out of Fresco's hold. and nails a hangman's neckbreaker on Fresco. Van Strife points for Starr to grab him a chair, Starr does so and throws it in the ring. But before Van Strife is able to pick it up, the ref has it in his hands.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Thank god the ref got that chair out of here, because if he had used it... [John is interrupted as when the ref takes the chair, Sid Starr throws in a metal chain, and the ref notices nothing. As the ref throws the chair back outside Van Strife picks up the chain, wraps it around his fist and...]
*CRAAAAAAAAACK!!* [... Van Strife nails Fresco with a right hand that has been wrapped in chain.] [GIGANTIC HEEL POP! BOOS AND GASPS FILL THE ARENA.] JOHN WILLIAMS: What the hell was that? Sid Starr has just given this win to Van
Strife. SHANE PROPANE: What? What? Van Strife is just so talented with his punches, it's not his fault.
Wait, wait... here's the pin. OOONNNNNNEEEEEE... TWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOO... THRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!! *[DING DING DING]* JOHN WILLIAMS: This is just wrong, there is no way that Dave Fresco could have won this match, not with Sid Starr on the outside.
SHANE PROPANE: WHO THE HELL IS THAT?!?!? [Someone begins to run from the back down the ramp and towards the ring, this man goes right after Setzer Van Strife, spearing him as he turns. That man is...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: JEEEEEFFF KEEEEEENNNAAAANNN!!! He is here. And he wants Van
Strife in the worst
way.
SHANE PROPANE: He can't be out here, let's get some security out here. SECURITY!! SECURITY!! [Keenan begins to lay some hard shots to the face of Setzer Van Strife. Not getting off despite the ref asking him to.]
*[DING DING DING]* [Sid Starr now gets in the ring and grabs Keenan by the back of the head. He sets him up for an inverted DDT, but instead is kicked in the face by Dave Fresco's HUGE BOOT!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Dave Fresco is conscious again, and is coming to the aide of
Keenan. [Both men now beating their respected men. Keenan lifts Van Strife and drops him with a hard
DDT. Fresco hits Starr with a skull shattering piledriver. Both Van Strife and
Starr roll to
the outside.]
[GIGANTIC ROOF BLOWING FACE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well folks, Jeff Keenan had seen enough of the shananigans from both Starr and
Van Strife. He came out and he cleaned house.
SHANE PROPANE: I GUARANTEE this is not over between these four men. But we have to cut to a commercial so, will, be back.
[Both Keenan and Fresco are celebrating in the ring, showing off to the crowd. They both turn around and... *WHHHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!!* *CRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!!* [Setzer Van Strife just nailed Jeff Keenan in the head with his singapore cane, and Starr shatters the already bloodied head of Dave Fresco with a chair.]
[The crowd boos as the camera pans over the ring. The bodies of Jeff Keenan and
Dave Fresco
lie motionless on the ring apron, standing over them are the team of Setzer Van
Strife and Sid
Starr. Both men smirk evilly, as Van Strife is handed a microphone. He then begins to speak.]
SVS: Take this as a lesson, folks. This is what happens when people try to get in our business.
It wasn't enough for Keenan to humiliate me with my own move, but he had to come back for more.
Well Jeffrey, it looks like your career has been abruptly _terminated_. [Sid chuckles as the fans mercilessly boo Van Strife for gloating about Keenan's injury. He shakes his head, then nudges the prone form of Dave Fresco with his foot, then looks over at
Setzer with a smirk.]
SS: Aaaaw. Look, Champ. Davey's fallen, and he can't get up! [Sid laughs as the boos increase in intensity, if that's even possible.] SS: Hey, I don't know what _you_ couchpotatoes are so upset about. When little children like
Davey here put their noses where they're not wanted they get a spanking to teach them a lesson.
How else are they gonna learn? [Van Strife picks up the remains of his Singapore Cane.] SVS: You see, we are the ERT. We are the men hired by ICW management to make this a better promotion. No longer will we accept scum like these 2 to be able to walk around here. No longer will we accept champions like Dave Pietka and Tommy Lionheart. Consider this
"new management."
[The crowd boos loudly, and the 2 men take it all in, loving it.] SVS: You might be wondering, "Who hired you?" What kind of man would be so evil, so vindictive, that he would hire the 2 deadliest men in the ICW to do his work? What kind of person would send the 2 of us to come out here and rid the
ICW of midcarders who don't pull their weight?
SS: Who indeed, Setzer? [Starr smirks, obviously knowing who their benefactor is.] SVS: I have to admit, I was a bit skeptical. After all, we've been bitter enemies in the past.
Our hatred for each other burns like an inferno you backwoods hicks have never imagined. However, just like the two of us, he's looking for an improvement of the quality of the ICW.
SS: You mean... our beloved owner? SVS: That's right, Sid. [A shocked pop from the crowd, as Van Strife smirks.] JOHN WILLIAMS: James Pillow? That bastard just sold us out! SHANE PROPONE: Finally, a man worthy of owning the ICW! JOHN WILLIAMS: James Pillow has hired these two cowards to do his dirty work! SHANE PROPONE: A smart decision... he hired 2 superstars! [Sid is now walking around in the ring, soaking up the boos with a grin on his face. Walking over to where Fresco is lying he leans nonchalantly on the top rope as Van
Strife hands him the
microphone.]
SS: I told ya last time we met, Davey, that if you would just be a smart boy and look to Sid
Starr for an example of how to run your career then you might possibly have a future in this business, but nooooooo. You couldn't do that, could you? I mean, seriously.
You _could_ have just been smart and backed out of tonight's match with the Champ. After all, you _must_ have seen what happened to the Jeffmeister last week right?
[He pauses slightly, smirking at the camera.] SS: But no, you just had to go and be a "toughguy". You just had to put your nose where it wasn't needed or wanted, getting in our way when we had better things to do.
Tell me, Davey, don't you wish now that you'd just gone in to the front office and told them you didn't wanna fight Setzer Van Strife? Don't you wish now that you'd done the smart thing and just stayed home? I bet you do, but it's too late now, isn't it? Now, showing up for the match was, of course, an example of stupidity in action, but then you went and made it all worse for yourself...
[Another theatrical pause as Sid looks out at the crowd.] SS: You decided to back up Jeff, the posterboy for frontal lobotomies, when he came down here to attack the Champ for no good reason! You decided to _help_ him, junior!
You decided, in other words, to screw over any hope of a productive career you might ever have had! Well, here's a scoop for ya, Einstein! BAD [BLEEP]IN' DECISION, KIDDO! Trust me,
Davey, wrestling
ain't the business for you. You don't have the intelligence for it, so to protect you from yourself the ERT in general, and Sid Starr in particular, have decided to terminate your employment with the ICW.
[He smirks and soaks up the negative reaction from the crowd.] SS: For your own health, of course. Now, we can do this the easy way, with you just leaving, or we can do it the hard way with you being forced to live off disability insurance for the rest of your life. Your call, junior.
[The boos are now almost deafening in their intensity.] SHANE PROPONE: You tell 'em, Sid! JOHN WILLIAMS: I think I'm going to be sick. Does he really think this is the end of this situation!
SHANE PROPONE: Nobody with half a brain would go up against Van Strife and
Starr, you know.
Which explains why the two particular bums face down on the mat there did. [Van Strife is handed the microphone back.] SVS: The ICW has some new sheriffs in town. Two of the roster cuts have already been targeted... and there are _many_ more to come. I hope you boys in the back have taken notice.
Once the owner hands down word, I can assure you that we'll strike down upon you with a great force.
You all better start growing eyes in the back of your head. SS: The Executive Response Team is officially open for business. [With that, Starr throws down the microphone, and the two men climb opposite turnbuckles, celebrating their attack on the men still lying inside the ring. We then...]
[Cut to a commercial.] INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING RETURNS TO PAY-PER-VIEW! CANADIAN CONQUEST 2002 Date TBA LIVE! From The Molson Centre in Montreal, Canada [Fades out..]
