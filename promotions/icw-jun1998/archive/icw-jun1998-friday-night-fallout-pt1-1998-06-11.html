Friday Night Fallout (part 1)

11 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Black and White - Inside of a family living room in the Early 60's. They all sit around watching Television, on it is a man in a doctors outfit standing in front of a chart which shows the picture of an atomic bomb. The man then begins to speak as the people watch on. . .] MAN - In the case of a nuclear bomb blast the first thing you should do is Duck and Cover. That's right, Duck and cover. . . [All of a sudden you hear faint whistleing in the air which rapidly grows louder. . .the camara then switches to outside of the house. The whistleing continues to grow louder until we hear an EXPLOSION loudly then the sky lights up and the shockwave of the blast makes it's way across the lande until we see the house in front of us begin to be totally annihilated. . the camara begins to get fuzzy until it's totally blank. . ..] [It comes back on as we see the words "ICW PRESENTS" in fiery red lettering. The screen then explodes as a Mushroom Cloud overtakes the screen, it begins to go down and the underneath it reads in smoke . . . . FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT!!!!] [The letters begin to break apart into smoke and cover the screen, it then parts as we find ourselves looking into a jam packed Madison Square Garden. The crowd is going wild as they stand on there feet holding signs in hand which read "STEVE MILLA IS TRULY STYLISH", TREVOR WILLIAMS TALKS TO MUCH" , "HEY SAXIST GO BLOW YOUR OWN HORN" , "IT'S TIME TO GET UNCENSORED" and sooo many more. ] [The camara then moves over to the broadcast position as we see John Williams and Shane Propone sitting at the broadcast position. Williams has on a navy blue blazer, a pair of black dress slacks,and a pair of gucchi shoes. Propone has on a three piece black armani suit, with a white dress shirt , white tie, his dark hair is slicked back. The camara zooms in on John as he begins to talk. . .] JOHN WILLIAMS - Hell everyone and welcome to the very first FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT! Boy do we have a great card for you tonight, jam packed with top notch matches, and you never know who may show up. I am John Williams and with me is my broadcast partner Shane Propone. SHANE PROPONE - You talk to much Williams. Everyone knows that the world is watching this program to hear me tell it like it is. But like you said, we have an action packed night here. Seven, count em, seven great matches. Including our main event where two of my personal favs "Rock Slid" CHip Newton and "Stylish" Steve Milla will go head to head. JOHN WILLIAMS - And that's just one of seven matches you will see here tonight. But we're going to go right up to the ring and kick things off, the first match of the ICW will take place right now, and you don't wanna miss it. ************************************************* MATCH #1 - THE SAXIST V/S "EXTREME" ERIC EROSION ************************************************* JACK BRYCE - The following contest is schedualed for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first. . . [CUE: Rhapsody in Blue by Gershwin} JACK BRYCE - from Portland, PA, Weighing in at 239 pounds. Here is THE SAXIST! [Rhapsody in Blue is slowly played as the lights dim. A sax solo can be heard on the ramp. The Saxist is there, glaring towards the ring as he hits the High F. As he hits the F, highest note in the solo, gold fireowrks shoot from the ring, forming a Musical note] [The Saxist then begins to make his way down the aisle as the fans boo him and throw things at him. He has on a Black shirt with black jeans, and a black trenchcoat...and his Sax in hand. The Saxist steps into the ring and begins to play again as more fireoworks go off forming musical notes. The music then fades away as he goes over to his corner.] JACK BRYCE - AND HIS OPPONENT! [CUE: Where the Wild Things Are by Metallica] JACK BRYCE - Accompanied "Super Sexy" Jill Stephenson. Hailing from Woonsocket, RI. Weighing in at 271 pounds here is "EXTREME" Eric EROSION!!! [The curtain parts as Jill and Eric walk through to a huge pop from the crowd. Eric has on black tights, with a red skull on it which is exploding. Jill walks by his side, she has on a tight black skirt, short white T- shirt, her long black hair hanging down as the males in the crowd make cat calls towards her. .] [The two climb up the stairs onto the apron. Eric then holds the ropes open and Jill steps between them, he then follows her and goes to his corner and beginb to talk over strategy. . .] JOHN WILLIAMS - Both are now in the ring, Referee Ernie Wyclef going over the rules with each guy and now calls for the bell. [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS - And the very first match of the ICW is now underway. Eric and Saxist slwly circleing each other, they go to lock up but The Saxist backs up, playing a bit of a mind game with Erosion here. SHANE PROPONE - It's called smart wrestling dumbass. Get into your opponents head and you got the match won, plain and simple. JOHN WILLIAMS - Well you have a point there, but there is a fine line between playing mind games and just being annoying. The two now lock up. Erosion with a arm wringer on Saxist but the Saxist quickly pulls himself over to the ropes forcing the break. The Saxist now shaking his arm and staying over by the ropes, Erosion tries to come over but Saxist makes the ref stop him. Wyclef now making Erosion go over to the other corner and the Saxist jumps him from behind nailing him with a double ax handle between the shoulder blades. SHANE PROPONE - HA! I think I'm starting to like this Saxist guy. . .he's my kinda wrestler. The Saxist now putting the boots to Erosion. He pulls Eric to his feet and whips him into the ropes nailing him with a back elbow as he comes off. Saxist Saxist now going over to where Jill is standing and the camara picking up what he says. . . [SAXIST - "Hey baby, you know you want a piece of the Saxy Boy"] SHANE PROPONE - I don't know if that's true or not. . but I saw Jill looking over at me a few minutes ago. I am a chick magnet after all. JOHN WILLIAMS - Ohh get off of yourself already, can't believe I gotta go through this for 6 more matches after this. OY! Back to the match Saxist now pulling Erosion up by his hair and getting warned for it by the ref. Saxist lifts Erosion up and nails him with a big back breaker. Saxist going for an early pin but not hooking a leg. . . ONE. . . . TWO. . .Easy kickout by Erosion. Saxist actually looks surprised that he kicked out. Saxist now pulls Erosion back to his feet and nails a scoop and a slam. Saxist now bounces off the ropes and goes to drop an elbow. . . SHANE PROPONE - He calls this The Sax's Elbow. . . JOHN WILLIAMS - He can call it whatever he ants, but he just missed it and slammed down to the canvas. Erosion now getting back to his feet, he sends Saxist into the ropes and nails him with a spin wheel kick, great agility for a man his size. Erosion now climbs onto the middle turnbuckle and jumps off nailing Saxist with a fist between the eyes. Erosion hooks the far leg for the cover. . . ONE. . . TWO. . . . THRR. . Kickout by the Saxist. Erosion pulling Saxist back up and whips him into the turnbuckle then runs in nailing him with a lariet into the corner! SHANE PROPONE - COME ON SAXIST! Erosion putting Saxist up on the top turnbuckle and climbing up after him. Erosion hooking up Saxist but Saxist nails him with a punh to the kidneys, and another one. . boy that'll sure make ya have to go. Saxist now pushes Erosion off and he goes crashing to the mat. Saxist now perching himself up on the top turnbuckle he waits for Erosion to get up. Erosion to his feet and Saxist jumps off nailing him with a missle dropkick to the back of the head sending Erosion through the ropes to the floor. JOHN WILLIAMS - Saxist measuring Erosion. He bounces off the ropes and. .TOPA SUICIDA! He nailed Erosion with it but they both went crashing into the guard rail. Wyclef is starting the count on both men. . . 1. . . 2. . . 3. . . 4. . . 5. . . 6. . . SHANE PROPONE - Erosion is slowly crawling to the ring, using the apron to pull himself up but the Saxist grabs onto his ankle and is pulling himself over to the ring. . 7. . . 8. . . 9. . . 10!!! [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS - Erosion pushes Saxist off of him and rolls into the ring but it's to late, Lets go up to Jack Bryce for the official word. JACK BRYCE - The referee of this match up has counted both men out therefor this match has been declared a Double Count Out. JOHN WILLIAMS - Well Eric Erosion is in the ring with Jill trying to get Saxist back in the ring but The Saxist just looks in at him then begins to walk away. What an exciting first match and there's plenty more to come so don't go anywhere, we'll be right back. [Go to Part 2]
