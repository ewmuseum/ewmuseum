International Incident, 6 April 2002 (part 3 of 5)

6 April 2002

[Fades back in to Madison Square Garden...] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ROUND 1 INTERNATIONAL TITLE TOURNAMENT "The Salience" Morgan Ashe V/S "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL AND IS A FIRST ROUND MATCH FOR THE ICW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP....INTRODUCING FIRST... [Darkness.] V/O: On the 6th day, God created man. God saw all that he had made, and it was very good. Thus the heavens and the earth were completed in all their vast array. By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. And God blessed the 7th day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done. On the 8th day god...Broke The Mold....
["Broken Mold" flashes over the screen as the "The Grudge" by Tool begins to play.] #Wear the grudge like a crown of negativity. Calculate what we will or will not tolerate. Desperate to control all and everything. Unable to forgive your scarlet lettermen.# [A pale red light shines down on the top of the ramp way where The Salience stands, his head is held down as his long black hair hangs around his head and face. He isn't large but his muscular form is imposing in the shadows and shilloute as his chest heaves and falls.]
#Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Justify denials and grip 'em to the lonesome end. Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Terrified of being wrong. Ultimatum prison cell.# [Morgan ducks his head forward and throws his head back, the hair and water being knocked away from his face as he looks out over the arena. Ashe slowly makes his way to the ring, the spot light following him to the ring. The fans reach out to touch him but fall short as he stares at his home--the ring!]
#Saturn ascends, choose one or ten. Hang on or be humbled again.# [Ashe stops at the ringside area and looks up at the ring. For a long moment he stands there.]
#Clutch it like a cornerstone. Otherwise it all comes down. Justify denials and grip 'em to the lonesome end. Saturn ascends, comes round again. Saturn ascends, the one, the ten. Ignorant to the damage done.# #Wear the grudge like a crown of negativity. Calculate what we will or will not tolerate. Desperate to control all and everything. Unable to forgive your scarlet lettermen. Wear the grudge like a crown. Desperate to control. Unable to forgive. And we're sinking deeper. Defining, confining, controlling, and we're sinking deeper.# [Morgan Ashe walks back around the ring in the other direction and reaches up grabbing ahold of the top rope and stepping up onto the apron. Morgan Ashe stands on the apron looking to the side for a moment glancing back out over the crowded arena before flipping over the top rope into the ring.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Stand Alone" by Godsmack begins to play over the PA System.] [The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. The fans, who normally boo once this song hits, surprisingly cheer.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way down the aisle, hailing from Chicago, Illinois... he stands six feet, one inch tall and weighs in at 213 pounds... "2 SMOOTH" SETZER VAAAAAAAAAN STRIIIIIIIIIIFE!!!!!!!!!! [The curtains at the wrestler's entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the light display is Van Strife himself. The crowd cheers, and Van Strife raises his arms in the air, as to thank the crowd.
Van Strife flashes his trademark cocky smirk, then looks at his empty hands. He pauses, then puts up a finger, walking to the back. A moment later he emerges once again with his trusty
Singapore Cane. Van Strife raises the cane in the air, and the crowd responds with a huge pop! Surprised, Van Strife stands there for a moment to take in the cheers. Van Strife wears an old
"Lynch Mob"
t-shirt, a baggy pair of black jean shorts and a pair of black wrestling boots. Completing the ensemble is his trademark pair of black Oakley sunglasses. With a grin upon his face he makes his way down to the ring, walking down to ringside and actually slapping hands with the fans! Van Strife then makes his way to the ringside area, where he jumps onto the ring apron. He then vaults over the top rope.
Van Strife then moves to his corner to prepare for his match.] [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: This should be an interesting match. The high flyer Van Strife and the technician Ashe...and both men can go the other way when needed, which should make for some real interesting situations...
SHANE PROPONE: Van Strife is gonna take this guy to school! [Van Strife and Ashe move around the ring. Ashe staring a hole in Van Strife who simply moves around, not flinching...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Morgan Ashe's persona could be considered intimidating to most...but Van Strife has seen them all, mind games aren't going to get the job done against him.
[Ashe and Van Strife move in and lock up with each other. Van Strife with a quick go behind into a wrist lock on Ashe. Ashe reaches back and grabs the back of Van Strife's head but Setzer cranks up on Ashe's wrist. Van Strife now floats over and locks a headlock on
Ashe. Ashe backs
Van Strife into the ropes and goes to shoot him off but Setzer hangs on. Ashe trying to fight out of this hold. He nails a hard shot to the ribs of Van Strife then lifts him up and hits a side suplex...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men quickly back up. Trying to get a feel for each other, both men taking there time, they know if they have two more matches to get through if they win here...
[Ashe and Van Strife looking across at each other and move in and lock up again. Ashe sidesteps and plants a hard knee into the gut of Van Strife then grabs Van Strife by the hair and slams him back and down on the back of his head. Ashe now stomps on the shoulder of
Van Strife
then grabs his arm and locks on an arm bar but Van Strife quickly to the ropes forcing the break..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe going right to what he does best but Van Strife isn't going to go down that easily..
[Ashe with a boot to the ribs of Van Strife as he pulls himself up by the ropes. Ashe now takes
Van Strife and sends him into the ropes. Van Strife off and Ashe catches him in a tilt-a-whirl but Van Strife locks his legs around the head of Ashe and takes him over with a headscissor takedown. Ashe hits the mat but quickly up and Van Strife takes him over once again with a
Japanese arm drag. Van Strife hangs on and goes to lock on an arm bar of his own. Van Strife hooks it in as Ashe fights it. Ashe gets up to his feet and goes to lift Van
Strife up but
Setzer uses his leg strength to take Ashe back down...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife going to the mat here early in. Setzer obviously wants to try and save as much energy as he can for the second round if he picks up the win here..
SHANE PROPONE: IF? You mean when.. [Ashe manages to fight his way out of the hold and back to his feet. Van Strife up as well and takes Ashe and sends him into the ropes. Ashe comes off and Van Strife with a
tilt-a-whirl of his own and Ashe now with the head scissors takedown!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe now showing a little of his aerial ability with that reversal... [Again both men quickly back up. A sign of frustration beginning to show as neither men able to get any real offense going thus far. Van Strife and Ashe circle around once again then go and lock back up. Van Strife tries for a go behind but Ashe steps away before he can lock it on.
Ashe nails a boot to the gut of Van Strife then hooks him up and hits a double underhook backbreaker! Ashe holds Van Strife over his knee driving his knee into Setzer's spine. Ashe finally lets go and drops Setzer to the canvas. Ashe back up to his feet and turns Van Strife onto his stomach. Ashe hooks Setzer up and locks on a modified camel clutch.
Morgan pulling back on the hold, bending Van Strife's spine..] JOHN WILLIAMS: Ashe trying to wear Van Strife down with this hold. He has the arms of Van Strife hooked under his legs making the move even more painful...
[The referee checks on Van Strife. He asks if he wants to give up but Van
Strife says no. Ashe
pulls back on the hold harder, Van Strife let's out a sound of extreme pain as the ref asks again and gets a rather explicit answer. Ashe now standing up and jumps up and comes crashing down across the back of Van Strife. Ashe now to his feet and lifts Van Strife up. Ashe lifts
Van Strife and hits a big back breaker. Ashe follows Van Strife down to the canvas for the pinfall....
ONNNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHH...] [CROWD: POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife kicks out of it but it was close. Ashe has punished
Van Strife's back
for a good 3 or 4 minutes straight. Setzer has to be in a lot of pain.
[Ashe pulls Van Strife up and throws him out to the floor. Ashe follows out to the apron then comes off with a double ax handle to the back of Van Strife. Ashe now takes Van
Strife and hooks

Strife into the edge of the ring. Ashe now takes Van Strife and whips him hard into the guardrail. Ashe follows in and gets a backbody drop but he lands on his feet behind Van Strife and pulls Van Strife down across the railing. Ashe climbs back over and takes
Van Strife up and
rolls him into the ring. Ashe follows in and goes for a pin....
ONNNNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOO.... THHHHRRR...] [CROWD: MONSTER POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: VAN STRIFE OUT OF IT! It takes a lot to keep that man down, few have been able to accomplish it....but if Van Strife doesn't get something going here Morgan Ashe may be added to the list of those who got the job done...
[Ashe pulls Van Strife up and shoots him into the turnbuckles. Ashe follows in with a clothesline and connects. Ashe now lifts Van Strife up to the top turnbuckle. Ashe follows up after him.
Ashe lifting Van Strife up and hooks him for a super-plex. Ashe lifts Van

[CROWD: S-V-S, S-V-S, S-V-S...] [Van Strife down on the mat, he and Ashe both slowly starting to get to there feet. Ashe in a little better condition as he grabs ahold of Van Strife. Ashe goes to send Van

[CROWD: MONSTER POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: FROG SPLASH! WHAT ELEVATION! [Van Strife lands on Ashe perfectly but rolls off holding his own midsection in pain from the impact. Van Strife slowly makes his way back over and goes for the pin....
ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHRRRRREE....] [CROWD: <Silence>] SHANE PROPONE: Did he get him? [The ref up and holds up two fingers and indicates that Ashe got the shoulder up.] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! Ashe still in it! Oh my! I don't know how he is still in this one after that frog splash!

Ashe up and

The heavy favorite Setzer Van Strife is gone in the first round, not only that but so is Morgan
Ashe. The winner of Alex Hawkins and Shane Alexander will have a straight shot right into the finals!
SHANE PROPONE: AHHH! But....Oh please let Hawkins win his second round match, he has to win the title! ANYONE but Cote or Lionheart!
