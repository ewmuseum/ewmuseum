The Return of Friday Night Fallout, 15 March 2002 (part 4 of 4)

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fades back in to the Fleet Center...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Welcome back to Friday Night Fallout! It's time for our Main
Event as Tommy Lionheart takes on Setzer Van Strife!
SHANE PROPONE: Just a warm up for Van Strife before he takes his rightful place as the International Champion in two weeks!
JOHN WILLIAMS: That remains to be seen, for now let's go to the ring... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= [Main Event] Tommy Lionheart V/S "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 60 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... [Suddenly, "Crawling In The Dark" by Hoobastank blasts over the PA to a good face pop. The crowd's intensity increases as none other than
Tommy Lionheart steps out from the back, wearing a confident smile.] LANCE JOHNSON: Now introducing ... hailing from London, England... weighing
TWO hundred and THIRTY-FIVE pounds ... A former ICW
Continental and Juniorweight champion ...TTTTTOOMMMMMMYYYY LLLIOOOONNNHHEEAARRRTTT!!! [Lionheart strides towards the ringside area, giving out as may high-fives as he can to the fans. He rolls into the ring, where a spotlight appears. He raises his arms in the air as the music fades out.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Stand Alone" by Godsmack begins to play over the PA System.] [The lights in the arena slowly dim to complete darkness, as a display of colored lights dance and swirl across the entranceway. The fans, who normally boo once this song hits, surprisingly cheer.]
LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way down the aisle, hailing from Chicago, Illinois... he stands six feet, one inch tall and weighs in at 213 pounds... "2 SMOOTH" SETZER VAAAAAAAAAN STRIIIIIIIIIIFE!!!!!!!!!! [The curtains at the wrestler's entrance are thrown back, and stepping out from the light display is Van Strife himself. The crowd cheers, and
Van Strife raises his arms in the air, as to thank the crowd. Van Strife flashes his trademark cocky smirk, then looks at his empty hands. He pauses, then puts up a finger, walking to the back. A moment later he emerges once again with his trusty Singapore Cane.
Van Strife raises the cane in the air, and the crowd responds with a huge pop! Surprised, Van Strife stands there for a moment to take in the cheers. Van Strife wears an old "Lynch Mob" t-shirt, a baggy pair of black jean shorts and a pair of black wrestling boots. Completing the ensemble is his trademark pair of black Oakley sunglasses.
With a grin upon his face he makes his way down to the ring, walking down to ringside and actually slapping hands with the fans! Van Strife then makes his way to the ringside area, where he jumps onto the ring apron. He then vaults over the top rope.
Van Strife then moves to his corner to prepare for his match.] [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: And our Main Event is underway! This should be an interesting match, both men are well known for there aerial moves and a willingness to take big risks...
[Van Strife and Lionheart simply staring across at each other. The two men move to the center of the ring, neither men taking there eyes off the others...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: You can cut the tension with a knife here. These two men know each other well, and it's safe to say there is no love lost what so ever between them...
[Van Strife and Lionheart now step back and lock up with each other. Van Strife pushes Lionheart across the ring but Lionheart plants his back foot and stops the momentum and begins to push Van Strife back the other way.
Van Strife now steps down and stops Tommy's momentum. Both men now trying to get the advantage but neither able to and they break away...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Both men just feeling each other out a bit. Both men have been out of action for a while and are taking things a bit slower than they're known to do...
SHANE PROPONE: Don't worry, Van Strife will kick it into high gear and blow
Lionheart away!
[Van Strife and Lionheart circle around each other. The two step forward and lock up again. Lionheart with a quick go behind and takes Van
Strife down to the canvas with a double leg takedown from behind....] SHANE PROPONE: Setzer better watch out, Lionheart likes being in the rear... JOHN WILLIAMS: Oh will you stop! [Lionheart quickly up and hits a spinning leg drop to the back of Van Strife's head as he was attempting to get back up driving him face first into the canvas. Lionheart in control early now as he pulls Van Strife to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Van Strife comes off and
Lionheart catches him in a tilt-a-whirl and drives him down with a sidewalk slam! Lionheart going for an early pinfall attempt...
ONNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOOO....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Van Strife kicks out easily. Lionheart hoping to get a quick win but no luck.
SHANE PROPONE: Puh-lease! There is no way anyone can beat Van Strife that easily. [Van Strife quickly back to his feet before Lionheart can capitalize after the pin. Van Strife watching Lionheart as he moves around the ring. The two men try another lock up but Van Strife pulls Tommy in and plants a hard knee right into his gut. Van Strife now hooks Lionheart up and hits a double underhook backbreaker. Van Strife holding Lionheart over his knee stretching him out. Van Strife now rolls Lionheart to the canvas and quickly gets to his feet. Van Strife over and quickly up to the top turnbuckle. Van Strife sits perched on the top turnbuckle as he waits for Lionheart to get to his feet. Lionheart to his feet and turns around as Van Strife comes off and drills him with a missile dropkick right on the jaw!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife moving a bit slower than we're used to seeing, but he seems to be getting heated up now..
SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH! Kick it into overdrive baby! [Lionheart hits the mat and rolls to the floor. Van Strife poised as he watches
Lionheart pull himself up to his feet. Van Strife off the ropes and flips over the top rope and comes down onto Lionheart with a pescado! Van
Strife back up and plants a boot to the chest of Van Strife before going over and grabbing a chair from ringside. Van Strife over as Lionheart starts to pull himself up once again. Lionheart turns and Van Strife just throws the chair at Lionheart! The chair connects dead on with Lionheart's skull knocking him back down to the floor. Van Strife now takes the chair and throws it down onto Lionheart's chest then quickly hops onto the ring apron and comes off with a picture perfect somersault senton driving the chair into Lionheart!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife putting his own body at risk with that move as he connects hard with the steel as well as driving it into the chest of Lionheart...
SHANE PROPONE: Right into his heart! [Van Strife now pulls Lionheart back up to his feet and throws him back into the ring. Van Strife follows in and goes for the pin....
ONNNNNNNNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWWWOOOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRR......] SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH! JOHN WILLIAMS: Don't jump the gun Shane, Lionheart got his shoulder up. He is still in this one...
SHANE PROPONE: Bah, only delaying the inevitable... [Van Strife arguing the count but doesn't get to distracted as he pulls Lionheart back up with him. Setzer takes Lionheart and backs him into the turnbuckles. Van Strife lays in a few hard forearm shots to the jaw of
Lionheart then shoots him across to the other side. Lionheart hits the turnbuckles hard and rebounds out. Van Strife follows in after him with a clothesline but Tommy ducks under it and Van Strife goes into the corner. Setzer stops himself short so he doesn't hit but Lionheart with a dropkick to the back of his head driving him abdomen first into the turnbuckles. Lionheart now over and lifts Van Strife to the top turnbuckle.
Lionheart pulls Van Strife down by the head and hooks him by the neck. Lionheart climbs to the middle turnbuckle then leaps off taking Van
Strife with him and hits a big reverse neckbreaker!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: A great move by Lionheart! That will take a lot of the smoke out of Van Strife.
SHANE PROPONE: Meerly a small set back. He wouldn't want to just come out and dominate totally...Van Strife is an entertainer, he's giving the crowd a good show and Lionheart some false hope that he might have a chance in hell of winning...
[Lionheart now pulls Van Strife up and hooks him up and hits a snap suplex...Lionheart hangs onto Van Strife and rolls over and lifts him back up and hits a second snap suplex...again he hangs on and lifts Van Strife up.
Lionheart goes for a third suplex but Van Strife blocks it...and he blocks it again. Lionheart with a quick jab to the kidney of Van Strife then hits the snap suplex and rolls on top for the pin....
ONNNNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRRREE....] SHANE PROPONE: KICKOUT! HA! JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart with a near fall there but Van Strife is still alive. [Lionheart pulls Van Strife to his feet and hits a quick back breaker and drops
Setzer to the canvas. Lionheart now over and out to the ring apron and up to the top turnbuckle. Lionheart measuring Van Strife and he leaps...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: FROG SPLASH! Lionheart connects with TREMENDOUS force! [Lionheart rolls off of Van Strife holding his own abdomen from the impact but he slowly rolls over and ontop of Van Strife for the pin....
ONNNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHHHRRRRRREE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: YE...NO! WOW! Lionheart didn't hook the leg and it may have just cost him a big win!
SHANE PROPONE: Come on Setzer....Don't let this limp wristed pansy beat you! [Lionheart pulls Van Strife back up once again and hits a scoop and a slam. Lionheart going to the corner again and up to the top turnbuckle. Van Strife struggling to his feet and he hits the rope crotching Lionheart on the top turnbuckle! Lionheart facing out towards the crowd as Van
Strife tries to catch his breathe. Van Strife now out to the ring apron and climbs up. Van Strife moving slow but Lionheart still dazed. Van Strife springboards off the ropes and...]
SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH!!! JOHN WILLIAMS: MY GOD! HURRACARANA OFF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE ALL THE WAY TO THE ARENA FLOOR! LIONHEART HAS TO BE OUT OF THIS! SHANE PROPONE: Kiss all your dreams of victory goodbye Tommy-boy... [Lionheart on the arena floor in a heap, eyes glazed over, not moving at all..] JOHN WILLIAMS: The only movement I see out of Lionheart is his chest moving up and down from breathing...and even that seems limited.
[Van Strife down on the ring apron, very much out of it as well...] SHANE PROPONE: Come on Setzer, finish him off once and for all! [Van Strife reaching up for the bottom rope and is trying to pull himself up. Van Strife slowly getting up. Van Strife breathing heavy as he eyes Lionheart lying on the arena floor. Van Strife looks out to the crowd and turns and begins to ascend the turnbuckles...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What is Van Strife going to do now? SHANE PROPONE: Whatever it will be, it will be great! Get your cameras ready, history is about to be made...
[Van Strife makes it to the top turnbuckle and looks down at Lionheart. Van
Strife takes a deep breathe and leaps...]
[CROWD: SUPER-ULTRA-MEGA OH MY F'N GOD~! POP~!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SPACE FLYING TIGER DROP! DEAR GOD! VAN STRIFE CRASHING DOWN ONTO THE BODY OF LIONHEART! [CROWD: S-V-S, S-V-S, S-V-S....] JOHN WILLIAMS: The crowd is going nuts after that move! Van Strife showing the world what made him one of the best in the business!
[Van Strife and Lionheart both down on the arena floor as the ref begins to make the count...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Van Strife used all he had to hit that move and he and Lionheart are both down and out...this thing could end in a double countout.
[1.... 2.... 3.... 4.... 5.... 6.... 7....Van Strife slowly moving... 8.... 9....Van Strife pulling himself up by the railing...] JOHN WILLIAMS: The referee has stopped counting, that was a surefire double countout right there.
[The referee over talking to Lance Johnson..] SHANE PROPONE: What's going on here? [Lance Johnson stands up, mic in hand...] LANCE JOHNSON: The referee has just told me that he has declared this match to be No Countouts and No Disqualification's!
JOHN WILLIAMS: WHOA! WHAT AN ANNOUNCEMENT! This match will have a guaranteed winner! SHANE PROPONE: Come on Van Strife! [Van Strife on his feet, a slight grin on his face after that announcement. Van
Strife making his way around the ring to where his trusty
Singapore cane sits and takes it in hand. Meanwhile on the other side Lionheart slowly starting to get to his feet. Van Strife up to the ring apron and makes his way around to where Lionheart is and leaps off the apron swinging the cane but Van Strife sees him out of the corner of his eye and nails Van Strife hard in the gut! Van Strife slumps to one knee.
Lionheart tears the cane from Van Strife's hand and holds it up...] [CROWD: LION-HEART, LION-HEART, LION-HEART...] SHANE PROPONE: DON'T DO IT!! <<<<CRAAACCCKKKK!!!!>>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT A SHOT! Lionheart just cracked that cane right over Van
Strife's skull! He is busted wide open!
[Van Strife down on the floor, blood seeping from the opening on his forehead. Lionheart throws the cane into the ring and pulls Van Strife up. Lionheart rolls Van Strife into the ring and grabs a steel chair from ringside. Lionheart to the ring apron and leaps over with a rolling splash hitting the chair in between him and Van Strife! Lionheart over and goes for a pin....
ONNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRRRREEE....] [CROWD: MASSIVE POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOULDER UP! MY GOD! HOW MUCH MORE PUNISHMENT CAN THIS MAN TAKE? SHANE PROPONE: As much as Lionheart can dish out! [Lionheart pulling Van Strife up but Van Strife reaches and gets ahold of the
Singapore cane and lifts it up between the legs of Lionheart stopping him short! Van Strife now jams the end of the cane into the throat of
Lionheart as he is doubled over. Van Strife now to his feet and winds back...]
<<<<CRAAAACCCKKK~!>>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! Van Strife has broken the cane over the back of Lionheart's neck! Lionheart drops to his knees as Van Strife discards the broken cane. Van Strife now grabs the steel chair and makes his way to the corner. Van Strife to the outside and up to the top turnbuckle. Lionheart slowly starting to get up. Lionheart moves to the corner and grabs the leg of Van Strife who kicks him off. Lionheart tries again and manages to bring Van Strife to a sitting position on the turnbuckle.
Lionheart starts to climb up after him but Van Strife jams the steel chair into the gut of Lionheart knocking him back. Van Strife now with a side swing and plants the chair across the side of Lionheart's skull!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Lionheart knocked back and down to one knee. I don't know how much more he can take!
SHANE PROPONE: This is the beginning of the end! [Van Strife drops the chair and perches himself up top once again. Van Strife waits as Lionheart pulls himself up to his feet. Barely able to stand
Lionheart turns just in time to see Van Strife take to the air...] JOHN WILLIAMS: FUTURE.... [CROWD: MONSTER POP~!] SHANE PROPONE: SHOCK~!!!! JOHN WILLIAMS: VAN STRIFE NAILED IT! SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH! [Van Strife shakes his head as he recomposes himself after hitting the Future
Shock. Van Strife makes his way over and lays on Lionheart hooking the leg and putting everything he has into the pin...
ONNNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRRREEEEE....] SHANE PROPONE: OH YEAH! [DING,DING,DING!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT A MATCH! LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, "2 Smooth" Setzer Van STRIFE!!! JOHN WILLIAMS: What a match-up! Lionheart gave it his all and showed that he is a big time contender but it was just Van Strife's night tonight as he comes away with the win! What a night this has been! We'll see you next week! Goodnight everyone!
[The camera pans back as Van Strife stands in the middle of the ring making the belt motion with his hands as the referee looks over Lionheart...]
[Fades out...]
