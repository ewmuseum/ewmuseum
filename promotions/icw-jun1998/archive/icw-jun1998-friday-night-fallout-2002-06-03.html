Friday Night Fallout, 3 June 2002 (part 1 of 4)

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera fades backstage as we are instantly subjected to a wide shot of ICW superstar, Shawn "The Scorpion" Viper's locker room door. The fans inside the Air Canada Centre explode with a monstrous face pop at the sight of Shawn Viper's door. The camera man pops open the door as Shawn is seen sitting on a plush leather couch, lacing up his boots as he is decked in full ring attire. Viper has on his new red and black RVD style singlet, which has a picture of a scorpion and in jagged letters the initials "SSV" on the back. As the camera pans around the locker room we see a stainless steel mini- fridge, a black leather couch, a coffee table, and a few plants. The camera looks to Viper as he looks up to the camera man and nods for him to come in. Viper finishes lacing up his boots as he pops his feet on the coffee table. The camera man zooms in to Viper's face as he smiles and looks relaxed for his upcoming match.]
SSV: I requested this time to address a few more things. You know ... I have had a lot of questions fly my way about my upcoming match with Setzer Van Strife but I have had just as many questions pop up about my match last week with Kenny
Morlock. [Viper reaches down to the floor and picks up a water bottle sitting on the floor. Viper pops the top and downs a swig of water before returning his focus up to the camera.]
SSV: Let's start with Kenneth Morlock. People have been asking me about my friendship with Morlock. Well, Morlock and I were friends a long time ago. Him and I ran together back in the old days of wrestling. Back when wrestling was just beginning to take off ... him and I were part of the old school crew. I had a lot of respect for the man ... until last week came around. You see, Morlock and I would get together and talk whenever we crossed paths at an arena. When we would square off, I thought it was friendly competition. I guess after losing time after time it ate away at the poor bastard and he just couldn't take being 2nd best anymore.
[Viper leans forward and places his hands on his knees, as he looks directly into the camera]
SSV: Morlock ... maybe I can put your mind at ease. You are not the only one to be 2nd best to Shawn Viper. I've beaten guys better and worse than you before, so you might want to think twice next time before you go and piss me off like you did last week. Now I have to waste everyone's time by handing you your ass yet again, just to beat the point into you that
when it comes to you Vs me ... YOU AIN'T SH*T! [Viper leans back and composes himself yet again] SSV: Setzer Van Strife! Damn it has been a long time since you and I have gone toe-to-toe. Tonight is a big night for both of us SVS. Tonight I get to prove that I can still beat your ass any time, any place, anywhere. Tonight, you get to prove that you can last in a match without having some of your boys give you a
"helping hand." Setz, just to give you a little clue to what your in for. The last time you and I went one-on-one, I wouldn't have gone for an arial move to save my life ... now, you aren't gonna be able to figure out what's going on when I hit you with the "Split-legged Scorpsault,"
"The 4 1/2 Star Scorp Splash," "The Scorpion's Tail," "Light's Out," or my favorite ... "The Stinger Kick." Hell son,
I've got a lot more than that, and when that bell rings tonight ... there isn't a DAMN soul on this earth that's gonna save you from the beating I'm about to give you. Don't think that Pillow gave you just anyone to fight ... he gave you one pissed off Scorpion!
[Viper gets up and grabs his bottle as he looks ready to go] SSV: Tonight is bittersweet for me Setzer. Its bitter because I have to watch out for Morlock, Pillow, and Starr ... but its sweet because if they get in my way, I will plow them down JUST LIKE I AM
GOING TO DO TO YOU! Strife ... be careful out there, because I am a lot stronger, faster, smarter, and flexible than you last saw me. Shawn Viper is like a fine wine ... he gets better with age. Tonight the International Icon of professional wrestling makes Setzer Van Strife an example to the world, to the ICW, and more importantly to James Pillow that NO
ONE can beat Shawn Viper ... when there's gold on the line. Oh and Setzer ... don't forget ... ALL WHO OPPOSE ME WILL BE
...
[Viper puts his hand to his ear to listen to the fans ... and they respond with a thunderous ... ]
Crowd: [A noticeably faint] ... DESTROYED!!! [Viper gives one last smile as he heads out the door and the camera fades to black.] [Scene: Black and White - Inside of a family living room in the Early 60's. front of a chart which shows the picture of an atomic bomb. The man then begins to speak as
the people watch on. . .] MAN - In the case of a nuclear bomb blast the first thing you should do is Duck and Cover. That's right, Duck and cover. . . [All of a sudden you hear faint whistling in the air which rapidly grows louder. . .the camera
then switches to outside of the house. The whistling continues to grow louder until we hear an
EXPLOSION loudly then the sky lights up as the Shockwave of the blast makes it's way across
the land until it hits the house totally annihilating it. The camera begins to get fuzzy until

fades away as we begin to see The Air Canada Centre in Toronto, Canada. The camera pans around the arena as the crowd is on there feet for International Championship! The camera makes it's way to the commentary table as we see none other than John Williams and
Shane Propone.
Williams takes his cue and begins the show...] JOHN WILLIAMS: WELCOME TO TORONTO! WELCOME TO THE ICW'S SHOWCASE OF THE STARS! WELCOME TO FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT! I am John Williams and as always I am joined by non other than Shane Propone... SHANE PROPONE: You're welcome... JOHN WILLIAMS: What a show we have lined up for you tonight. We have a re-match between Sid Starr and Jeff Keenan and that will take place inside of a 15 foot high steel cage!
SHANE PROPONE: And Keenan will be put down once and for all! JOHN WILLIAMS: Setzer Van Strife defends his TV Title against a man he knows very well in Shawn Viper...
SHANE PROPONE: And like EVERY time they have met in the ring Van Strife will dominate and destroy!
JOHN WILLIAMS: And of course, in our main event of the evening we will see the first ever Tag Team match since the return of the ICW as International Champion Tommy Lionheart and Shane Alexander team up to take on Brian J. Blottie and the ICW North American Champion Dave Pietka...
SHANE PROPONE: What a night this will be! First we'll see Setzer Van Strife hang onto his gold, then we will see the demise of Jeff Keenan at the hands of Sid Starr then the end of Tommy Lionheart at the hands of Brian Blottie! I LOVE IT!
[The camera pans to the front row as we see a very attractive women sitting there..] JOHN WILLIAMS: Looks like we have company, that is the wife of Shawn Viper. SHANE PROPONE: She needs a real man, someone like Van Strife to show her what she's been missing..
JOHN WILLIAMS: Right...Well in our opening bout we will see a man who has been having his problems with Sid
Starr in Dave Fresco take on Kenneth Morlock who after last week has a furious
Shawn Viper looking for payback. Let's go up to the ring and see what goes down here...

[The crowd is at a low hum of everyone talking amongst themselves. Awaiting another high-impact match, the crowd is in eager anticipation, when the guitar riff of "Surfacing" by Slipknot blares over the Public Announce System. The crowd throws a mixed reaction as the drums pick up and the lights dim down so that green and grey spotlights glow out from the entranceway.]
[Thick smoke begins to pour out from the entranceway as the crowd is in an excited mood.] LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way to the ring from Miami Florida, weighing in at
258 pounds... DAAAAVE FREEESSSSSSCOOOOOO~!! [The crowd continues to throw that mixed reaction up in the air as the song continues. Out form the smoke, steps out the tall dark Hawaiian Giant, Dave Fresco.]
[Clad in navy blue denim-looking sweatpants, a black Tee-shirt that reads "I am..." on the front in blood red lettering, and a pair of black Tims, he walks down the aisle with his head held up and a smug grin on his face. His eyes a narrowed, showing focus and energy flowing through his whole body.]
[As he makes it down to ringside, Fresco pulls himself onto the apron, via the middle rope. The back of his shirt can now be seen and it reads "The Greatest!!", Fresco puts his gigantic size 15 EEE foot over the top rope, followed by it's twin.
He puts himself onto the second rope and extends his arms out, then clenches his fists and curls his arms down, flexing for the crowd. His urban voice with a hint of a tropical accent is heard in the form of a deep scream to the crowd. His face goes back to it's normal grinning as the song fades out. He climbs down and bounces off the ropes, awaiting the opening bell.]

Fresco sees him coming and drops out of the way.
Morlock runs chest first into the turnbuckles and rebounds out into a clothesline to the back of his skull. Morlock back up to his feet and Fresco nails Morlock with a standing drop kick, both men roll up to there feet as Fresco nails Morlock once again with a standing drop kick. Both men back up quickly but Morlock yanks the ref in front of him, Fresco tries to get at Morlock but the ref pushes Fresco a step back as Morlock comes around with a right fist to the jaw of Fresco flooring him....]

Morlock, I can't imagine what it feels like...

JOHN WILLIAMS: Morlock trying for the Lock Down, that would end this match in a heartbeat but Fresco able to avoid it...

Fresco back into the ring. Morlock follows in and goes for the pin.... ONNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRR...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Fresco with his foot on the bottom rope saves him from defeat. [Morlock back to his feet and takes Fresco up with him. Morlock whips Fresco into the turnbuckles.
