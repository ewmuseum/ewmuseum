The Return of Friday Night Fallout, 15 March 2002 (part 3 of 4)

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in to none other than Alex Hawkins in the lockerroom. He's dressed in his wrestling gear, along with a sleeveless ICW T-shirt, and is doing a set of push-ups. Alex is interrupted, however, by a knock on the door.
After an annoyed groan and grimace, Hawkins stands up and opens the door. It's none other than Tommy Lionheart, who greets him. Hawkins, though, doesn't look too pleased with meeting the English
Sensation.] TL: Hello Alex. Welcome to the ICW. Congratulations on making it to the big-time. [He holds out his hand.] AH: Um, hey... [Alex looks down at Lionheart's hand.] AH: No offense, man, but I don't do handshakes. [Awkward pause. Alex has this look of realization on his face all of a sudden.] AH: Ah, I know what this is all about. Look, man, I know you're a sensitive guy and stuff...but I don't swing that way, you know what I mean?
I think you're a great guy and all...a stand-up individual. But I'm just not attracted to men, if that's what you're thinking...
[Alex cocks his head a bit and glances at Lionheart.] AH: You _are_ gay...aren't you? 'Cause if you're not, it'd be quite embarassing. [Tommy looks at Alex; a look of concern, anger and confusion all rolled into one.] TL: I think we've got our wires crossed here, mate. I'm a married man. Hey, I only came here to congratulate you and wish you all the best for the future. Don't flatter yourself.
[Alex nods.] AH: Hey, thanks, [mockingly] mate. Now, I'd love to share some tea and crumpets with ya, but we'll have to save that for a later date, you know? I'm about ready to bitchbeat someone that goes by the name of Alex Cote in about a few minutes...
[With that cocky grin plastered all over his face, Alex pats Tommy on the shoulder.] AH: And I kinda need to perfect my "swing." Hey, good luck with Van Strife later tonight, [mockingly, again] mate. You'll probably need it.
[Tommy, realising the youngster's constant piss-taking grins.] TL: Thanks. [Tommy begins to turn away, but then looks back at Hawkins.] TL: Oh, by the way, if you ever accuse me of being gay again, well, lets just say you'll be enterting the Lion's Den. Enjoy your match tonight...
mate. [With that, Tommy turns around and slams the door in Hawkins' face. Alex snarls.] AH: Gee, don't be so bitchy, Elton. [Fade.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Well that was....interesting. SHANE PROPONE: I always thought Lionheart was a little..."Funny" and that just proves it. JOHN WILLIAMS: Right...Well up next we have a virtual rookie Alex Hawkins taking on ICW veteran Alex Cote.
SHANE PROPONE: You mean ICW's biggest joke. JOHN WILLIAMS: As you can tell Shane doesn't like Cote very much, so it's pretty obvious who he wants to win this one.
SHANE PROPONE: I don't care who wins as long as Cote doesn't. JOHN WILLIAMS: Right...Well let's go to the ring.. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= [Alex V/S Alex] Alex Hawkins V/S "The Hammer" Alex Cote -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-= LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL WITH A 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST... # I AM! # [The pounding riffs of "R.P.M." by Sugar Ray begins blaring over the PA to a moderate face pop as the cocky Alex Hawkins emerges from the entrance portal. Hawkins is decked out in his usual ring attire: navy blue tights with a kalaidoscope-type abstract design down the outside of each leg; white boots, and white tape around his wrists. His dark brown hair is spiked up and he's clean-shaven, as usual.]
# You're beautiful # # It's all or me # # You're beautiful # # It's me! # # I will let you # # I will let you down # [Hawkins walks towards the ring, barely giving the fans any high-fives and the like. He slides underneath the bottom rope and into the ring.
Alex doesn't showboat or anything, but rather, he just stands there with that cocky smirk plastered all over his face.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... VOICE OVER: ARE YOU PREPARED TO BE HAMMERED?! [Crowd begins to cheer loudly.] ["What comes around" by Ill Nino begins to play over the PA system.] #I HATE YOU..... #I HATE YOU..... #I HATE YOU..... #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! ["The Hammer" Alex Cote steps out from behind the curtain. The cheers because almost mind numbing.]
[APPEARANCE: Black jeans with a pair of black steel toed army style boots. Also, a midnight blue tang top with the words "PREPARE TO BE HAMMERED" on the front. Around his neck hangs a silver chain with a silver dagger on it, a pair of black leather gloves cut at the knuckles on his hands, and a black leather trenchcoat covers him from shoulders to ankles.]
#YOU THINK THAT I'M THE ONE TO BLAME #EVERYTHING I LOSE IS JUST A PIECE OF WHAT THERE IS #TO GAIN #YOU THINK IT'S COOL AND ALL IS FINE #NOW IS THE DAY WHEN YOU PAY, THIS IS MY TIME #I FEEL NOTHING FOR BUT PAIN #I AM WHAT YOU WILL BE, YOU ARE DYING IN ME #I LOVE YOU.....I HATE YOU.....I MISS YOU..... LANCE JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, coming down the aisle, hailing from "The
City of Brotherly Love", Philidelphia Pennsylvania, he is three hundred and twenty eight pounds of twisted steel and sex appeal, he is your lord and savior, and the most devastating wrestler in sports entertainment today, here is.....
!!!!!_"THE HAMMER"_!!!!!_ALEX_!!!!!_COTE_!!!!! #YOU ALWAYS THINK THAT YOU'RE SO PERFECT #THOSE THOUGHTS DROVE ME AWAY FROM HOME #BUT IF YOU PUT ME THROUGH YOUR TEST #THEN I WILL FADE..... #I HAVE NOTHING TO SAY #BUT I FEEL LIKE MY MOUTH IS WIDE OPEN #EVERYTHING THAT IS REAL #COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! [Alex stands at the top of the entrance ramp. He raises his hands high slowly and then slams then down as spark gush from both sides of him virtually enveloping him. The sparks settle down and Alex turns around and looks up at the big screen where he watches scene of him pounding on his opponents. He laughs and turns back around. He walks down the aisle slapping the hand of his fans as the music plays.....]
#YOUR STUPID FACE JUST MAKES ME SICK #I SEE YOU CHANGING EVERYDAY #TO FIT INTO THE NEWEST CLIQUE #I KNOW YOU, BUT EVERYTHING YOU DO #IS JUST A PART OF YOU, YOU'LL NEVER SEE THE TRUTH #I HATE THE WAY YOU MAKE ME FEEL #I HATE THE WAY YOU THINK YOU'RE REAL #YOU VOICE.....IT DESTROYS.....MY ONE CHOICE..... [Alex walks up the steps and climbs into the ring over the top rope. He gets in the ring and does a little spin and them stops and flexes.....]
#YOU ALWAYS THINK THAT YOU'RE SO PERFECT #THOSE THOUGHTS DROVE ME AWAY FROM HOME #BUT IF YOU PUT ME THROUGH YOUR TEST #THEN I WILL FADE..... #I HAVE NOTHING TO SAY #BUT I FEEL LIKE MY MOUTH IS WIDE OPEN #EVERYTHING THAT IS REAL #COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND.....COMES AROUND #SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! SHUT UP!!!!! [Stands in the corner and removes his sunglasses, silver chain, and his trenchcoat revealing black elbow pads and "CUZ' I TAKE NOT
PRISONERS" on the back of his tang top, and waits for the bell as the music fades....] [DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Here we go, a bit of a David Vs Goliath type of mentality here, let's see what the rookie Hawkins can do against the much larger and more experienced Cote...
[Cote and Hawkins move around the ring. Hawkins a little reluctant to lock up with Cote. The two move in to lock up and Hawkins with a quick go-behind into a waist lock on Cote. Hawkins unable to move the big man who just stands his ground. Cote now whips an elbow back knocking Hawkins back. Cote turns and shakes his head with a grin on his face.
The two move in and lock up this time. Cote uses his superior size and strength to simply HURL Hawkins into the corner...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Cote trying to unnerve the rookie here by tossing him around the ring. This is a good test for the rookie, let's see how he responds.
[Cote laughs and flexes towards Hawkins, obviously trying to intimidate him. Hawkins begins to move in, he looks like he's going to lock up with Cote again but he quickly changes he move and nails a dropkick right to the knee of Cote causing it to buckle and drives Cote down to one knee. Hawkins moves in and Cote grabs him and shoves him to the canvas and quickly gets back to his feet. Cote shrugs it off and simply pulls Hawkins back up. Cote takes Hawkins and whips him into the ropes. Hawkins comes off and Cote with a power lift but Hawkins rakes the eyes and drops down behind Cote. Hawkins immediately with a dropkick to the back of the left knee once again sending Cote reeling into the ropes. Cote holding himself up with the ropes and Hawkins runs to the corner and springboards off the middle turnbuckle and lands a dropkick to the face of Cote as he steps back from the ropes. Cote hits the mat and
Hawkins off the ropes and springboards off the ropes for a moonsault but Cote able to roll out of the way and Hawkins hits the mat...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Cote's cocky exterior seems to be going away as Hawkins has been able to hit him with a few good moves. Cote looks like he's starting to get a bit frustrated...
[Cote with a sneer on his lips as he pulls Hawkins up and just THROWS him into the corner. Cote moves in with a powerful clothesline then begins to lay into Hawkins with a series of rights and lefts to the head and abdomen. Cote now takes Hawkins and whips him across into the corner. Cote goes to follow in but Hawkins sidesteps and Cote slams chest first into the corner. Hawkins with a roll up from behind for the pin....
ONNNNNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOOO.... TTTHHHHHHHRRR.....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Cote powers out of it! A strong kickout but still a near fall as Cote's anger seemingly almost cost him the match there..
SHANE PROPONE: No, his utter stupidity and almost cost him the match. He's a big ape with a pea for a brain.
[Cote pulling himself away from Hawkins and into the corner. Cote uses the ropes to help himself up as he keeps his eyes on Hawkins. Cote trying to compose himself as he steps out of the corner. Cote watching Hawkins as the two move around the ring. Hawkins goes for a chop block on the left knee but Cote sees him coming and stops him short with a forearm to the back of the neck. Cote now in control of Hawkins as he pulls him up and lifts him into a gorilla press. Cote holding Hawkins up for a few seconds before stepping forward and dropping Hawkins sending him crashing down face first into the canvas. Cote now off the ropes and drops a leg to the back of Hawkins neck then rolls him over and goes for the pin....
ONNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRR....] JOHN WILLIAMS: And Hawkins just pops out of it. Cote uses his size advantage well in pinfalls making it hard to kick out and it almost worked there..
[Cote pulls Hawkins back up to his feet. Cote with the arm of Hawkins and pulls him into a short lariat. Cote hangs onto the arm of Hawkins and lifts him right back to his feet. Cote now takes Hawkins and sends him into the ropes. Hawkins hits the ropes and hangs on, Cote runs in after him but Hawkins drops down and pulls the top rope down sending Cote over the top to the arena floor...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins able to buy himself a little time to regroup here... [Hawkins to his feet, he watches Cote pull himself up on the floor and measures him. Hawkins goes off the ropes. Hawkins off and springboards off the middle rope to the top rope, he springboards off the top rope to the top turnbuckle and comes off onto Cote with a somersault plancha landing right on the head and chest of Cote driving him down into the floor!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a move by the rookie! He took a big risk and it paid off! Hawkins now in control of the tempo of the match, let's see what he can do with it. [Hawkins right on Cote. He kneels over his chest and lays a few hard shots to the head of Cote. Hawkins now getting back to his feet and pulls Cote up. Hawkins rams Cote's head into the guard rail. Hawkins now takes
Cote and whips him into the steel steps and Cote crashes into the steps knees first! The steps knocked loose and Cote flips over and lands back first on the top section of the steps. Hawkins now takes a step back and runs in. Hawkins uses the bottom half of the steps to springboard himself and comes off onto Cote with a senton backsplash!
Cote sandwiched between the steps and Hawkins, his back driven into the steel.] JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins starting to get into his groove here, if he can continue to control the pace this way he could pull it off...
SHANE PROPONE: Not like beating Cote is much of an accomplishment anyway...I guess it's a start.
[Hawkins now takes Cote and pulls him to his feet and rolls him into the ring. Hawkins to the ring apron and leaps over the top rope and drops a big leg drop to Cote before going for the pinfall...
ONNNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRREE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: KICKOUT! WOW! Cote just able to kick out of it, the ref's hand was less than an inch from hitting the mat there..
[Hawkins showing a bit of frustration there as he thought he had it won. He pulls Cote back to his feet. Hawkins with a couple stiff kicks to the left knee of Cote stumbling him. Hawkins now off the ropes and hits a seated dropkick to the knee dropping Cote to one knee. Hawkins now off the ropes again as Cote slowly stands up. Hawkins jumps up for a hurracarana but Cote able to hang on and nails a sitout powerbomb!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Cote able to reverse that move out of desperation but that dropkick took it's toll on his knee as he is unable to capitalize on the powerbomb...
[Cote on the mat trying to regroup. Both he and Hawkins starting to move towards the ropes. Cote and Hawkins both pulling themselves up with the ropes at opposite sides of the ring. All of a sudden "Crawling In The
Dark" by Hoobastank starts to play over the PA..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: That's Lionheart's music.... [Hawkins distracted by the music looks towards the entrance but no one is there. Cote meanwhile standing behind Hawkins whips him around...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: FIST OF IRON! What a DEVASTATING punch! Hawkins has to be out cold! [Cote goes for the cover... ONNNNNNNEEEEEEE.... TWWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHHRRRRRREEEE...] [DING,DING,DING!!] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner, "The Hammer" Alex COTE! JOHN WILLIAMS: And Cote gets the win here after Hawkins became distracted. That's all it can take, just one small mistake and it can mean the difference between victory or defeat...
SHANE PROPONE: Figures, Cote can't win the match without interference. What a loser... JOHN WILLIAMS: That was Lionheart's music that caused the momentary distraction...I guess after the little run in with Hawkins backstage
Tommy decided to give the rookie a little test. I guess he failed it, well up next Lionheart will be stepping in the ring. The Main Event is next..
[Cuts to commercial...] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...]
