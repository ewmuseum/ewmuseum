[Into the ring steps Max Power! with a microphone in hand.] MAX POWER: Ladies and gentlemen, I'd like to call out at this time a man who made a _very_ quick name for himself last week here in ICW, when he attacked ICW's
International Champion, Tommy Lionheart, from behind. Ladies and gentlemen... "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie. [The chorus of boos starts even before "Head Like A Hole" can crank up, and get even louder when the brash, egotistical Brian J. Blottie steps through the curtain, wearing a black suit with white button down shirt underneath, and a very flashy multi-colored tie. A few fans take swipes at Blottie, which are all scoffed at by the "much too sophisticated for you neanderthals" attitude of "The True Living Legend".
He climbs onto the ring apron via the steps, wipes his feet, then carefully steps into the ring between the top and middle ropes, taking his place next to Max Power, his music cutting off.]
MAX POWER: Mr. Blottie, I think you owe the fans an explanation for your actions last week...
BJB: I don't owe the fans jack, nor do I owe the fans shit. So they can just go "bugger off", as Tommy Lionheart would tell them backstage after asking for an autograph.
[Immediate heel heat, and Brian just soaks it all in.] BJB: But I will say this. The attack on Tommy Lionheart was _completely_ justifed. [Oh, that pissed the crowd off.] MAX POWER: Howso? BJB: You see, Tommy Lionheart had this dream. The dream of becoming a _heavyweight_ champion. And he fought long and hard for this dream, and finally, after it seemed like he'd never achieve it, he won the ICW International Title. You know what everyone in the back did when that happened? No, they didn't celebrate. And they certainly didn't pat Tommy on the back.
They _wretched_. They were disgusted that Tommy freakin' Lionheart, at his bitchy _worst_, won a title that all of them deserved. All of them but the man who won it.
["BOO!" goes the crowd, except for one guy three rows back, holding up a sign that says "Tommy
Lionheart: HE'S A FREAKIN' LUCHA-BRIT!" He cheers wholeheartedly.]
MAX POWER: That still doesn't explain your attack... BJB: Oh, but it does! You see, I'm not here to yank off the front brass for a title belt. I'm not here to suck [BLEEP] so I can get a name for myself. I'm here because
Tommy Lionheart
holding _any_ title is an injustice, but to hold the _top_ title? That's a damn shame, and a circumstance that _must_ be rectified. And I feel that I'm the rectifier in this situation.
["Huh?" goes the crowd, as they don't understand the big words. This _is_ an
ICW crowd.] MAX POWER: So... you're here to cost Tommy Lionheart the title? [HUGE HEEL HEAT! Wow, they understood _that_.] BJB: Damn straight. And by the time I'm done with Tommy, a _real_ champion will have that belt strapped around his waist, a man who _deserves_ that title. Tommy
Lionheart will be champion no mo... [Before the fans' start a riot, "Crawling in the Dark" by Hoobastank blasts over the PA system.
With Brian J. Blottie looking on, disgustedly, stepping out from behind the curtain comes the
ICW International champion himself, Tommy Lionheart.
With the title belt strapped around his waist, Tommy raises his arms in the air to a well- received face pop. The champ strolls down the aisleway, slapping as many fans'
hands as he can, before jogging up the ring steps and stepping through the ropes into the squared circle.
Tommy pats the belt around his waist, before being granted with a microphone.] TL: Phoenix, Arizona ... your champion has arrived! [FACE POP~!] TL: Well, look who we have in the ring ... if it isn't Brian J. Blottie. Welcome to ICW, Brian. [Tommy waves, sarcastically; Brian just snarls.] TL: So Brian, or can I call you Blottie? Either way, let me get this straight
...Your excuse
and reasoning for attacking me, in an unprovoked attack last week is...
[Scratches his head.] TL: ... What exactly? Because the boys' in the back are jealous that I am the champion? Because I worked my ass off at International Incident, defeating three men in one night to become the champion? I think not. You wanna know what I think?
[Pause.] TL: I think you're here, because you are scared. Yeah, you're afraid, Blottie. You see, you've wrestled in some pretty top notch wrestling organisations in your time, and you've battled some of the finest athletes in the business. But that was then, my friend, and this is now. We're not in 1998 anymore, Blottie, this is the year 2002. You can't digest the fact that your days at the top are over. A new wave, a breed that includes yours truly, is here and here to take wrestling forwards. Why are you here? You're here to regain past glories, to try and prove you're the same guy you were before, in your WWO days. Well,
Blottie,
frankly, you can't and you wont. So, why don't you do us all a favor, and take your five dollar suit and hasbeen ass out of the ICW and out of the wrestling world for good!
[It doesn't take a rocket scientist to find out that the crowd is popping huge for Tommy right now, and Brian J. Blottie is _seething_ with anger.]
BJB: I'm scared? Screw you, you self-righteous bastard. I see where all that gold has gone;
straight to your damn head. And that's one of the reasons I'm here to strip you of that belt.
The last thing we need is Tommy Lionheart thinking he's anything other than a cruiserweight champion for life.
[Brian walks closer to Tommy, and taps the title around his waist.] BJB: Comfy fit? Does that fifteen pounds of ego hold up like you thought it would? I hope you enjoy it, Tommy...
[And with that, Brian goes face-to-face with Tommy, his nose centimeters from
Tommy's.] BJB: ... because it won't be on you for long, "Wanker". [Tommy grins.] TL: Is that so? Well, Blottie, why don't you and I have ourselves a little match, say, next
Friday night on Fallout. Maybe that way, I'll get to shut your big mouth once and for all.
[Max Power is aghast, though we can't tell if it's because of the challenge or because he's getting no time to actually conduct an interview segment here.]
BJB: Friday night at Fallout, huh? I'll take you on. But I want it to be _NON-TITLE_. [And the crowd goes MILD!] BJB: I want to prove to all these pre-pubescent Tommy Lionheart fluffers that I can beat you clean in the ring, and I _deserve_ that piece of gold you so proudly wear on your waist. I'll see ya next Friday, then?
TL: Friday! [Tommy pushes his microphone into Blottie's chest before dropping it to the canvas. "Crawling in the Dark" by Hoobastank begins, as Tommy turns to exit the ring.]
BJB: Oh, and Tommy? [Hoobastank keeps playing, and Tommy turns around... and eats _another_ superkick square in the jaw, cutting the music off mid-start. Brian hovers over Tommy, grabs the title around his waist, and yanks it off.]
BJB: Interrupt me again, and I'll make sure this title belt follows you... over the nearest damn bridge. JACKASS!
[Brian tosses the title forcibly down, onto Tommy's skull, then drops the microphone. "Head
Like A Hole" starts up in Hoobastank's place, leading Brian J. Blottie out of the ring, and to the back.]
