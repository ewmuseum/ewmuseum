Hawkins looks ready as usual, hair all spiked with the gear all set.]
# Who could ever... # # Who the fuck could ever take the place of me? # LANCE JOHNSON: Making his way to the ring, from Sacramento, California...weighing in at 229 pounds...here is... ALLLLLEX HAWWWWWWWKINNNNNNS! [Hawkins makes his way towards the ring, paying no mind to the taunts and boos of the fans.
However, as he goes up the steps on the ring, he looks out at a single fan with an obnoxious smirk. After uttering some kind of obscenity at said fan, Hawkins enters the ring.]
LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... ["Sad But True" by Metallica starts to blare over the arena.] LANCE JOHNSON: "About to make his way down to the ring weighing in at 263 pounds and standing in at 6 feet 3 inches, he hails from Boston, Massachusetts. He is...
... "SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER!!!" [As the announcer finishes the name, the curtains split and out walks Shane
Alexander wearing
a pair of old black ripped jeans and a black leather jacket that is stained with blood and dirt. The jacket hangs down to about his mid-thigh. Shane has his head down, his long dark hair filled with water hangs in front of his face. As the song hits it's first acoustic break
Shane snaps his head back and the fans react with a large cheer.]
# Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who takes you there # Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who cares [Shane begins to make his way to the ring staring at the ring, almost as if it is sacred. His eyes have not come off of the ring since he has shown his face. His eyes have massive black bags under them.]
# They # They betray # I'm your only true friend now # They # They'll betray # I'm forever there [Shane makes it into the ring stepping through the middle rope. Once inside
Shane opens his
jacket and exposes a very gruesome scar across his stomach. Shane now takes off his jacket and waits in the corner.]
# I'm your dream, make you real # I'm your eyes when you must steal # I'm your pain when you can't feel # Sad but true [The music begins to fade out as Shane stretches in the corner.] JOHN WILLIAMS: This should be a great match-up! Alexander still with stitches in his forehead from his match with Pietka last week.
SHANE PROPONE: I hope Hawkins makes him bleed like a pig again! [DING,DING,DING!] [Hawkins and Alexander move to the center of the ring and circle around each other. The two go to lock up but Hawkins pulls away and moves to the ropes. Alex stretches out on the ropes and watches Alexander move around the ring. Hawkins moves back towards Shane and the two go to hook up and again Alex pulls away..]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Alex Hawkins playing some mind games here with Alexander. Hawkins may be a rookie but he is learning fast, you can already see the frustration on the face of Alexander... [Hawkins again stretching out on the ropes as Alexander eyes him, pure anger showing through.
Hawkins makes his trek back to the middle of the ring, his usual cocky smirk on his face. Hawkins and Alexander move to lock up and this time Alexander pulls away and laughs...] JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins getting a taste of his own medicine and does not look happy about it!
[Alexander and Hawkins move back towards each other and finally lock up. Alexander with a side headlock on Hawkins in the center of the ring. Hawkins fighting it, he tries to shoot Alexander into the ropes but he hangs on to Hawkins head stopping that short. Alexander now floats into a hammerlock on Hawkins but Hawkins with his free arm nails Alexander with an elbow to his face. Hawkins turns and jumps up and nails Alexander with an enzuguri to the back of his head sending Shane to the canvas. Hawkins over and nails a couple boots to Shane as he is down then places his foot over the throat of Alexander choking him. The referee right there and lays the count of Hawkins...
1... 2... 3... 4....Hawkins lets up on the choke then pulls Alexander to his feet. Hawkins lays Alexander against the ropes, he ties his arms in between the ropes leaving Shane fully exposed then just lays into him with a MASSIVE open hand chop!]
<<<SMACK~!>>> JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! That chop rang out throughout the arena! Hawkins' hand print is now a permanent part of the chest of Shane Alexander!
[Hawkins now shoots Alexander across into the ropes. Shane comes off and Hawkins catches him with a Japanese arm drag! Hawkins hangs on and flips himself over and locks on a Fujiwara armbar! Hawkins with the hold locked on as Shane tries to fight out of it...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins with a nice Fujiwara armbar locked on tight. He wants to sap as much energy from Alexander as he can with this hold...
SHANE PROPONE: Way to point out the obvious...I can't believe you get paid for this. [Alexander looking for a way out of the hold. He kips up to his feet but Hawkins pulls him right back down. Alexander now flips over and onto his feet and holds Hawkins down to the mat.
Hawkins shoulders down for the pin... ONNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO...Hawkins lets the hold go to escape the pin...] JOHN WILLIAMS: What a move by Alexander using Hawkins hold against him. [Alexander and Hawkins both on there feet. The two circle around each other and lock up. Alexander with a go behind and nails a quick German suplex! Hawkins driven onto the back of his head as Alexander lets the hold go and drops him to the canvas. Alexander to his feet and lifts Hawkins back up. Alexander hooks Hawkins into a full nelson. Shane lifts
Hawkins off the
canvas then just DRIVES him into the canvas with a massive full nelson slam!
Alexander going for the cover... ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO....] JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins kicks out of it! Alexander with some high impact offense thus far. If he keeps it up he could pick up the big win!
[Alexander pulls Hawkins back to his feet but Hawkins goes down low. Alexander doubled over and
Hawkins with a double arm DDT! Hawkins rolls Shane Alexander over and goes for the pinfall attempt...
ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOO.... THHHRRRR....Alexander rolls the shoulder off the canvas. Hawkins right back on him laying in shot after shot to the forehead of Alexander. Hawkins pulls Shane to his feet and tosses him through the ropes to the arena floor. Alexander hits the floor as Hawkins in the ring watching
Alexander. Alexander slowly to his feet and Hawkins off the ropes and comes over the top onto
Alexander with a somersault plancha! The back of Alexander's head driven into the floor as
Hawkins gets back to his feet. Hawkins pulls Shane up and rams him face first into the steel ring post...and a second time. Alexander slumped down, hanging onto the post to keep himself somewhat upright as we see his forehead is busted open...]
SHANE PROPONE: YEAH BABY! JOHN WILLIAMS: That wound on the forehead of Alexander reopened here by that steel ring post.
You can see the blood clearly on the blue ring post. [Hawkins rolls Alexander into the ring and follows in for the pin.... ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHHRRRRREEE....] [CROWD: EXTREME POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: ALEXANDER KICKED OUT OF IT! Shane once again showing he can take as a beating and keep on going...
SHANE PROPONE: Yeah but like last week he will eventually crumble! [Hawkins pulls Alexander up and hooks on a side headlock. Hawkins with his free hand starts to drive his knuckles into the gash on the forehead of Alexander trying to open it up even further. The blood running down the face of Alexander as Hawkins sends him into the ropes.
Alexander comes off and ducks a clothesline from Hawkins. Alexander off the other side, Hawkins turns and Shane leaps and nails a shoulder tackle sending Hawkins to the canvas but
Alex pops right back up. Alexander grabs ahold of him and nails a belly to belly overhead suplex! Hawkins sent crashing down to the canvas. Hawkins back up and with a boot to the gut of Hawkins then hooks him up and lifts him into a vertical suplex! Alexander holds Hawkins up letting the blood rush to his head then drops down nailing a brainbuster!
Alexander goes for the pin.... ONNNNEEEEE.... TWWWOOOOO.... THHHRRREEE....] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! Hawkins able to wiggle out of it! [Alexander pulls Hawkins up and hits a short arm clothesline!] JOHN WILLIAMS: That's usually the setup! Alexander giving the signal... SHANE PROPONE: But Hawkins isn't stupid! He got out to the floor! [Alexander clearly giving the signal for No Remorse but Hawkins wisely to the floor. Hawkins on his hands and knees on the floor as Alexander follows out after him.
Alexander pulls Hawkins up and...] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH! Hawkins with that infamous crowbar to the gut of Alexander! The referee didn't see it though! [Hawkins rolls Alexander into the ring and follows in. Hawkins with the pin.... ONNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOOO.... THHHHRRRREE....] [CROWD: MEGA POP!!!] JOHN WILLIAMS: OH MY! ALEXANDER GOT THE SHOULDER UP! [Hawkins to his feet, utter frustration and anger showing on his face rather than the usual cocky grin. Hawkins slips out to the floor and grabs the crowbar. Hawkins trying to conceal it as he slips into the ring. Hawkins leans back to throw a punch but the referee catches the elbow to his eye...]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins just nailed the referee! SHANE PROPONE: It was an accident..That's what that idiot gets for standing so close.. JOHN WILLIAMS: I don't know about that... [Hawkins with the crowbar out as the referee not looking as he tries to regather his thoughts.
Hawkins swings the crowbar but Alexander catches it and gets to his feet. Alexander with a boot to the gut of Hawkins then rips the crowbar from the hand of Alexander. Hawkins drops to the canvas acting hurt. The referee turns and sees Hawkins down clearly over acting how hurt he is and Shane with the crowbar in hand...]
[DING,DING,DING!] JOHN WILLIAMS: WHAT!? The referee calling for the bell here? [The referee leans out of the ring and talks to ring announcer Lance Johnson...] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner as a result of a Disqualification, Alex HAWKINS! JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! SHANE PROPONE: YES [Alexander FURIOUS! Crowbar in hand he moves toward the ref who quickly exits the ring. Shane turns towards Hawkins to see he has made a hasty retreat as well. Alexander throws the crowbar to the canvas, blood still flowing from his forehead as we cut to commercial...]
[A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...] [The scene opens to show that we are inside of a locker room. It appears to be
Shane Alexander's by the long, black, dirty leather jacket hanging inside one of the lockers. Next to the jacket is a first aid kit. Suddenly the door opens and in steps Shane
Alexander.]
SA: "WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOIN IN HERE?!?! GET OUT!!" [Shane moves aside, then puts his hand up to say wait. The camera now can focus on Shane's attire. He is wearing a pair of stone-washed jeans that are baggy and hang over his black boots. He wears a black shirt that reads "GUNS DON'T KILL PEOPLE, I KILL
PEOPLE" in yellow letters. His hair is thrown to the back in a sloppy ponytail.] SA: "I think I got something for everybody to hear... I mean my mind has been on one thing for the past few days. 7 days exactly!"
[Shane begins to walk towards his locker. Once standing in front of it he speaks.] SA: "Friday Night Fallout... last week... Dave Pietka... These were the ingredients in a recipe to piss me off something ugly. When you beat me last week for the title, I didn't really get pissed... but then it lingered in my head for a week and now..."
[Shane's voice gets louder and more vicious.] SA: "... NOW... I AM PISSED!!!" [Shane punches the locker. Than breathes very deeply before returning to normal.] SA: "But Dave... I am not the type of guy to sit out here and bitch about how that whore raked my back with those nails. Or how "Marz" Candress pulled the ropes. I don't care... it just adds fuel to the fire. And that fire Dave... that fire is going to burn you. So how about this?"
"What's say you and me get together sometime soon. Let's say at the next pay per view. You bring that two dollar whore, that lacky "Marz", and that belt of yours and I will bring the most HARDCORE match that you can think of."
[Shane nods his head as to say "YES."] SA: "But Dave, I am not going to just spit out some match that I think is proficient for our feud to be ended or maybe just intensified. No, that is not me. I am a gentleman... I will give you some choices for our match... they are..."
"How about a... BARBED WIRE ROPES MATCH?!?!" [Shane gives a sinister grin then speaks.] SA: "Or maybe a... TAPAI DEATH MATCH?!?!" [Now Shane smiles with an even more evil smile than before.] SA: "So Dave, I hope to be hearing from you, real soon. Oh yeah, at the next pay per view... I will walk out with that belt, because...
.... I HAVE _NO REMORSE_ FOR MY ACTIONS!!!"
