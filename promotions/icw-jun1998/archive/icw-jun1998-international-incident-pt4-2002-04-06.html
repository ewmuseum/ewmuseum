International Incident, 6 April 2002 (part 4 of 5)

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ROUND 1 INTERNATIONAL TITLE TOURNAMENT Alex Hawkins V/S "Sadistic" Shane Alexander =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LANCE JOHNSON: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE FALL AND IS A FIRST ROUND MATCH FOR THE ICW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP....INTRODUCING FIRST... ["Sad But True" by Metallica starts to blare over the arena.] LANCE JOHNSON: "About to make his way down to the ring weighing in at 263 pounds and standing in at 6 feet 3 inches, he hails from Boston, Massachusetts. He is...
... "SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER!!!" [As the announcer finishes the name, the curtains split and out walks Shane
Alexander wearing a
pair of old black ripped jeans and a black leather jacket that is stained with blood and dirt.
The jacket hangs down to about his mid-thigh. Shane has his head down, his long dark hair filled with water hangs in front of his face. As the song hits it's first acoustic break Shane snaps his head back and the fans react with a large cheer.]
# Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who takes you there # Hey # I'm your life # I'm the one who cares [Shane begins to make his way to the ring staring at the ring, almost as if it is sacred. His eyes have not come off of the ring since he has shown his face. His eyes have massive black bags under them.]
# They # They betray # I'm your only true friend now # They # They'll betray # I'm forever there [Shane makes it into the ring stepping through the middle rope. Once inside
Shane opens his
jacket and exposes a very gruesome scar across his stomach. Shane now takes off his jacket and waits in the corner.]
# I'm your dream, make you real # I'm your eyes when you must steal # I'm your pain when you can't feel # Sad but true [The music begins to fade out as Shane stretches in the corner.] LANCE JOHNSON: AND HIS OPPONENT... # I AM! # [The pounding riffs of "R.P.M." by Sugar Ray begins blaring over the PA to a moderate face pop as the cocky Alex Hawkins emerges from the entrance portal. Hawkins is decked out in his usual ring attire: navy blue tights with a kalaidoscope-type abstract design down the outside of each leg; white boots, and white tape around his wrists. His dark brown hair is spiked up and he's
clean-shaven, as usual.] # You're beautiful # # It's all or me # # You're beautiful # # It's me! # # I will let you # # I will let you down # [Hawkins walks towards the ring, barely giving the fans any high-fives and the like. He slides underneath the bottom rope and into the ring. Alex doesn't showboat or anything, but rather, he just stands there with that cocky smirk plastered all over his face.]
[DING,DING,DING!] [Hawkins and Shane move in towards the center of the ring. The two men go face to face. Hawkins with a cocky grin on his face. Shane just looks at him then slaps him right across the face knocking the smile right off of Hawkins lips. Hawkins back in his face, more serious now and throws a right hand but it's blocked by Shane who floors Hawkins with one of his own. Shane now pulls Hawkins to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Hawkins comes off and
Shane hits a
swinging neckbreaker! Shane right back up and plants a couple hard boots to the chest of Hawkins before pulling him back up. Shane now takes Hawkins and hooks him for a DDT but
Hawkins pushes
Shane into the corner. Hawkins drives his shoulder into the gut of Alexander further. Hawkins now takes Shane and whips him across to the other side. Shane hits and rebounds out. Hawkins follows in and springboards off the ropes then floors Shane with a springboard dropkick!]
JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins getting the upperhand here...Let's see if he can make something of it...
[Hawkins over and pulls Shane to his feet. Hawkins hooks him up and hits a quick snap suplex and floats over onto Shane for the pin....
ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWWOOOOO....] JOHN WILLIAMS: Shane kicks out of it! It's still to early in this match... [Hawkins pulls Shane back up to his feet and hits a hard European uppercut rocking him. Hawkins now hooks Shane up and nails a Russian legsweep. Hawkins right back up and takes Shane up with him once again. Hawkins stands Shane up and goes for a standing dropkick but
Shane moves out of
the way and grabs the legs of Hawkins and SLAMS him down to the canvas! Shane drops a quick elbow to the chest of Hawkins before he could move. Alexander now lifts Hawkins up, hooks him and DRILLS Alex with an Inverted DDT! Shane now going for the pinfall....
ONNNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRR....] [CROWD: HEEL POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins out of it before the three count! Shane looking to put this away fast, he knows if he wins here he goes straight to the finals and he wants to conserve as much energy as he can to make it a lock...
[Shane pulls Hawkins up to his feet and slams him into the corner driving
Alex's head into the
turnbuckles. Shane now hooks Hawkins up in the corner then steps back and lifts
Hawkins and
HURLS him across the ring with an overhead belly to belly release suplex!
Hawkins crashes down to the mat hard. Shane quickly over and drops a leg to the back of Hawkins head before he rolls him over and goes for the pin...
ONNNNNNEEEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOO.... THHHHRRR...] JOHN WILLIAMS: KICKOUT! Hawkins still alive! [Shane trying not to show any frustration as he pulls Hawkins back up. Shane takes Hawkins and sets him up for a piledriver. Shane goes to lift Hawkins but Hawkins blocks it.
Shane tries again but Hawkins drops down and nails Shane with a low blow!] SHANE PROPONE: HELLO! JOHN WILLIAMS: That will change the pace of this match.... SHANE PROPONE: Among other things... [Hawkins quickly hooks Shane into a small package for the pinfall attempt.... ONNNNEEEE.... TWWWWOOOO.... THRRRREE....] [CROWD: POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: NO! SHANE POPS OUT OF IT! Hawkins hoping that low blow would be enough to hold
Alexander down for the three but it wasn't quite enough...
[Hawkins now taking Shane up to his feet and hooks his head. Hawkins to the corner and climbs to the middle turnbuckle. Hawkins leaps for a tornado DDT but Shane hangs on and drops Hawkins back to his feet then quickly lifts him and hits a northern lights suplex! Shane bridges it into a pin....
ONNNNNEEEE.... TWWWWWOOOOO.... THHHHRRRRRE....Hawkins gets the shoulder up!] JOHN WILLIAMS: SO CLOSE! Shane was less than an inch away from picking up the win! SHANE PROPONE: An inch, a foot...he didn't win, the end. [Shane and Hawkins both getting back up to there feet. Hawkins turns and Shane with a boot to the gut. Alexander hooks Hawkins up and goes for a hangman's neckbreaker but
Hawkins able to
slip free. Shane turns around...]
[CROWD:HEEL POP!] JOHN WILLIAMS: What a Superkick! Hawkins nearly took his head off! [Shane down and Hawkins to the corner. Hawkins going up to the top rope and measures Alexander.
Hawkins comes off..] JOHN WILLIAMS: SHOOTING STAR PRESS! OH MY! [Hawkins nails it and hooks the leg for the pin.... ONNNNNEEEEE.... TWWWOOOOOO.... THHHRRRREEE....] [DING,DING,DING!] LANCE JOHNSON: Here is your winner Alex HAWKINS! JOHN WILLIAMS: Hawkins pulls off the win and he is now in the Finals! SHANE PROPONE: Hopefully Cote and Lionheart will destroy each other in there match, save us some time..
JOHN WILLIAMS: We'll be back in just a moment with our only Semi-Final Match! [Cuts to commercial...] [A voice is heard...] ICW FRIDAY NIGHT FALLOUT LIVE! Every Friday Night.... Missing it would truly be a Disaster..... [Fades out...] [Fades back in to Madison Square Garden...] [The lights in the arena suddenly go out, as a faint swooshing noise is repeated over the speakers. A recording of an orgasming girl saying,'Ooh baby' is then played, as blue spotlights shine down from the rafters, and begin doing figure-eights in the spoy directly under the video-wall. The wall suddenly erupts in a plethora of color, as 'Sober' by Tool blares throughout the building.]
John Williams: What the hell is going on here? Shane Propone: Shut up. If this means what I think it does, the competition in
ICW has just
stepped it up a notch.
[The videowall suddenly changes to a black and blue ying-yang, with the letters
'JK' written
across it is silver cursive, that is engulfed by flames. The song pauses for a moment as the word "Quicksilver" is whispered. The song kicks back on as the word echoes in the background.
Silver pyros suddenly explode off. When they die down, we see a familiar individual standing there.]
John WIlliams: Who is that? Shane Propone: Oooooooh boy. Ooooooooh boy. John Williams: What the hell is wrong with you man? You better go to the bathroom and check your pants for any discharge.
Shane Propone: Shut up Williams. If you even knew who is standing there right now... [Blue pyros then explode, as be makes his way down the aisle and into the ring. He does a standing backflip into a split, and the crowd erupts. As the music dies down, the man flashes a trademark grin.]
Man: Tonight's event has now become _THAT_ much better. [The crowd erupts once again.] Man: I'm sure all of you would like to know what I'm doin' here. [The camera shifts back to the announcers table...] John Willians: Oh wait, I think I figured out who that is... That's... That's... [Propone beams with excitement.] Shane Propone: 'QUICKSILVER' JEFF KEENAN, and he's _HERE_ in ICW. [The camera then shifts back to the ring.] JK: Well it just so happens that I was havin' a conversation with my fellow
INWI alumni about
the good ole' days of this sport, back when I had just broken into the business, when "Ess Tee
Bee" brought up the ICW.
After gettin' deeper in ICW knowledge then an eight inch wanger in Jenna Jamison's throat, James handed me a ballpoint, and I signed my name on the dotted line. That's right folks... [Keenan pauses, and adjusts his black Oakley's.] JK: Jeff Keenan is the newest member of International Championship Wrestling. John Williams: What an announcement! JK: So what does that mean for the ICW wrestlers? Heh, it means that you all better have your
'A' game when yer in the ring from now on.
Yeah, I've been away from the game for at least a year, an' I'm sure many of ya will quickly assume that I've lost something, or that sincw I'm bigger than I used ta be, that I'm gonna be slower and less agile.
[Keenan pauses for a moment, and then raises his head shaking it left and right.] JK: That's just not the case. See...the whole time I've been gone, I've been trainin' harder than I've ever trained in my entire life. For once, Jeff Keenan was _all_ business, and no show. You say
I''ll be slower...
Well, either take my word for it, and know that I'm quicker then I've ever been in my life...
[Keenan grins.] JK: Or just wait 'till I step into the ring and let my wrestling do my talking. Heh, and you can rest assured knowing the same goes for my agility. [Jeff adjusts his sunglasses.] JK: So enough BSing. Lemme tell ya why I'm _really_ here... [The arena goes silent. Jeff looks around, runs toward a turnbuckle, and backflips off, drawing a _HUGE_ reaction from the fans.]
JK: I figured it's about time Jeff Keenan shows what a dominant force he can be in the ring. I mean, I've always been told I had talent...but either I wasn't focused enough, or I lacked the experience it takes to truly be a great in this business.
Well, that may have been true. BUT...here we are over three years later. I've gotten the in-ring experience with the big boys.
I know what it's like to step into the ring with some of the best on the planet. And more importantly than that... [A huge smile forms across his face.] JK: I know what it's like to _beat_ some of the best on the planet. [Huge pop from the crowd.] JK: As for my focus, heh...when I entered this great sport I did so just like any other young wrestler that tore through the indies.
You know what I mean. I had one of those large egos that made me think I was unbeatable. A twenty-plus match undefeated streak in the INWI didn't do much for me in the ego category, until a man by the name of 'Vindicator' Vinny Vitrone handed my ass to me on a silver platter.
But even then I was too think headed to get a clue. In fact, three years in the ring couldn't even get me focused enough to be what
I had the talent
to be.
It was my time away that truly helped me out in that category. [A dramatic pauses by Jeff.] JK: But now I'm back, and the fun has only _just_ begun. I'll see you boys soon. [Keenan drops the microphone, as 'Sober' by Tool begins to blast through the arena once again.
He flips over the rope and out onto the floor, and slowly walks back to the locker room area, the whole time drawing an amazing crowd reaction.]
JOHN WILLIAMS: What a surprise! Jeff Keenan has signed with the ICW! He's been out of action for awhile, but if he can get going again he will surely be a force around here!
SHANE PROPONE: Yeah, he's alright... JOHN WILLIAMS: Well, let's now get to the ring and get this semi-final match underway!
