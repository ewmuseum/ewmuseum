"The Hammer" Alex Cote Return Flash

7 March 2002

I know Ish may not be around but I want a blunt, mea ass critique, [name withheld], rip it apart if you're out there. It's been a long time since I rp'd so be as blunt and as mean as possible heh.
[name withheld] [Fade in.....] [SCENE: Logan Airport, Boston, Massachusetts. TIME: 9:30 PM. Scene fades in to an empty airport terminal. The camera spans left to right seeing no one.
Out in the distance someone turns a corner. A very large man with a duffle bag drapes over his shoulder. He quickly marches towards the camera. The grin on his face widens as he approaches the camera. He stops directly in front of the camera, drops the duffle back, and puts his hands on his hips looking out into space with a very cheap pose, it's "The Hammer" Alex Cote.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: [makes a sounds of a trumpet with his mouth] THE TIME HAS COME!! THE ICW BEGINS ANEW!! [Alex gives a laugh and stops the pose. He looks into the camera with a large grin.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Boy, retirement has been nice. Laying around my house in Philly, doing nothing more than sitting on my ass, watching TV, sitting back and relaxing with a Coke and a smile. Of course I took my time to work out and keep my skills in check, hey, if you [flex his right arm in front of the camera] looked this good, wouldn't you want to stay that way? [Puts his are down] Aw well, not everyone can look as good as me.
[Alex reached down and grabs his bag.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Well, I knew eventually I would be back. It's been awhile for me, since the closing of the LVPWA and my winning the IWF/WOW North
American championship, I let myself fade, but I ALWAYS knew I would be back. But when James called me to let me know that the ICW was coming back, well, of course you knew "The Hammer" would have to pack up and get on the first plane he could to sign up. Hell, I've held the ICW Television title, the ICW
Hardcore title, and the ICW Continental title..... [Alex sighs and rubs his goatee.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: .....but never the International title. Well, I've had my chances, the cage match with Christian Madison, the fact was that it wasn't my night. Then my match with Shawn Sylkk. A barage of interference, but it still wasn't my night.
[Alex begins to hops on both feet.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: But I'm hyped, I'm ready, and I feel it running through my veins, my time is close and I'm ready. WOOO! The ICW International title will soon be within my grasp! Come hell or high water, I'll get what
I've been striving for since my first day in the professional wretsling world. I've held world titles before, but even then it didn't have the same taste as ICW gold. The ICW is something else, it's a new breed. A place where the best of the best come together and show off their stuff, and soon I will be the king of that mountain!
[Alex begins to walk forward, the camera man staying in front of him.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: WHEW!! I'm hyped up bad! Just getting back into the ring gets my adrenaline flowing like a tsunami! A week and two days till it comes together and I'm counting down each and every second!
[Alex turns and stands on an escalator.] "THE HAMMER: ALEX COTE: James faxed me the currect roster, letting me know what I'm up against, and I must admit I am a little upset, I was expecting more old ICW names like Tommy Grayson, Shane Diamond, and Mike Masters, but hey, can't get everything you want.
[Alex walks off the escalator and towards the luggage pick-up area.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: But I'm glad to see a few new names, like the one who I am to face at the return card, the return Fallout. [stops and stares out into space] Boy, to say who I face at ICW's Fallout fills with with such joy,
I could almost cry. [begisn to act like he is going to cry] Waaaa-! But I'm not. [Alex walks over to the conveyer belt and looks around his his luggage.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: If they lost my luggage-! Anyways, lets see, who am I facing again? [stares blankly] Crap.
[Alex reaches into his pocket and puls out a piece of paper.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Alex.....Hawkins? Who the -! [Alex puts the paper back in his pocket and continues to look for his luggage.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Alright then, Alex Hawkins. Let me start off by saying.....who the hell are you? Not meant with animosity towards you but I've never heard of you. I've been around a while and I've been to a ton of feds and I've never heard of you. [Alex shrugs] Aw wells. Well, Alex, I don't know you, I don't know what kind of person you are but at Friday Night Fallout
I'm going to take you apart, mash you into mush, take you beyond the limit, throw you around like a ragdoll, and if you want me to rap it up, lets just say that I'm going to beat you so bad that you'll wish that bitch you call a mother empted for abortion or swallowed the load instead of shooting you out of her ass. Now, it's nothing personal, but do you really think that at the return show of the ICW that I am going to let you take me down? ME?! Listen here boy, when people think of the ICW they think of a few names, James Pillow,
Shane Diamond, Tommy Grayson, and Alex Cote. I helped put the federation on the map and at it's return I will help rebuild a fallen empire.
[Alex sees his luggage and grabs it. Alex begins to walk towards the exit. He stops when he hears a couple arguing near him. Alex looks over to see the man slap the woman in the face. Alex's face turns to rage.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Excuse me for a second. [Alex drops his bag and begins to walk towards the man. The man has his face away from Cote and towards the woman. And woman looks up and stares at Cote with amazement.]
MAN: How dare you talk to me like that! What are you looking at!! WHAT-! [The man spins around to see Alex right in his face.] MAN: OOOOOHHHHH SSSSHHHHIIIIITTTTT!!! [Alex grabs the man and throws him onto the luggage conveyer belt. Alex walks back, grabs his luggage and continues to walk towards the exit.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I hate woman beaters. [sighs] What was I talking about? Oh yea, Alex Hawkins, Friday Night Fallout, return of the ICW, and all that junk. Alex, if you know anything about the world of wrestling, you've heard the name Alex Cote. Numerous titles in the ICW, DSWA North American champion, IWF/WOW Tag team and North American champion, eWa World champion,
LVPWA Las Vegas champion, a long list of other championship and an even longer list of awards. I've been through it all, up and down, I am a walking, talking, breathing, drinking, eating, smelling, running, working, and wrestling legend and it will take all you have times twelve to take me to the limit.
[Alex walks through the door to the outside where there is a limosine waiting for him.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Alex Cote, take a cab? I think not. I ordered myself a cab, call me arrogant, call me narcisistic, so what, maybe I am, but unlike all those other posers running around flexing, calling themselves the best, I have the record, the statisics, and the reputation to back me up. Alex
Hawkins, let me wrap this up. Like I said, it's nothing personal, it's business, and wrestling is MY business. Come March 15, 2001, at the Fleet
Center, right here in Boston, Massachusetts, the ICW empire will be rebuilt and the ear of "The Hammer" begins anew. So for the first time in a long time but far from the last, Alex Hawkins, you better prepare to be hammered because, like always, I TAKE NO PRISONERS!!
[Alex gets into the limosine. The follows it as it pulls away.] [Fade to black.....] [name withheld] "Tis better to remain quiet and look the fool then open your mouth and relief all doubt.....take me for example!"
