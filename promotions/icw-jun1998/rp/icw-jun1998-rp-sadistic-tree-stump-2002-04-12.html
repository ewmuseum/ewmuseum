[Roleplay] Critique Please! ("Sadistic" Shane Alexander)

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critique this please, let me know what I should work on. Thanks. - Chris. ---------- [The camera fades from black to a shot of what appears to be the middle of the woods during sunfall. The scene in front of us is that of many trees almost wrapping around one tree that has been cut down. Sitting on top of the tree is none other than "Sadistic" Shane Alexander.] [He is wearing his usual attire of black ripped jeans hanging loosely over his black boots.
His legs dangling over the stump and not even touching the floor. His black leather jacket is seen lying next to him. He has a white wife beater on, displaying his many tattoos on his arms.
His hair is slicked back but still loose. He has what appears to be three stitches on the top of the head from International Incident.] SA: "Amongst these many trees, one lies shorter. One lies unmistakably out of place. This brings to mind my opponent this week. "The Hammer"
Alex Cote..." [Shane hops down from the tree stump and stretches.] SA: "You see, Alex Cote is not like those punks that got their asses handed to them at International Incident. He is a true warrior. He talks a lot of crap, and then he backs it up. That is what I love to see in ICW. Someone like me, that can go out there and toss someone around just because he said he could."
[Shane stops and gives that sinister smile of his.] SA: "But that is why I am going to give you a beating unlike any beating you have ever received."
[Shane stops smiling and looks away.] SA: "Alex, I am not going to beat your ass because I am the school bully and you are the computer geek. That is not what type of relationship we have. I am going to beat your ass because I know you can take it."
"I know that if I hit you with a piledriver on a chair, you are going to eventually hit me with that same chair. I know that if I DDT you on the concrete, you will suplex me on the concrete. It goes hand and hand."
[Shane begins to walk away from the tree stump still not looking at the camera.] SA: "It's like I have said before and I _know_ I will say it again. I _love_ pain. All aspects of it. I love to see the six car pile-ups on the highway just for the bloody body count. I am the guy that is still rooting for the comeback of public executions. But that doesn't mean that I just love to see other people in pain. I love to see myself in pain."
"My own blood trickling down my face leaving my vision impaired... my chest ripped open from barbed wire... being put through a table... I love it all."
[Shane turns towards the camera finally, again walking towards the gigantic tree stump.] SA: "So just like this tree stump represents how Alex Cote sticks out in the ICW. Blood is going to represent the match between myself and
"The Hammer." Who's blood is going to be drawn, well even I don't know that, but either way I will have a smile on my face."
[Shane once again gives that sinister smile that send chills up spines.] SA: "Alex Cote... ... I have _NO REMORSE_ for my actions!!" [The scene fades out with Shane still smiling.]
