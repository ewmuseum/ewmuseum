ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Roleplay

Mike Wise RP 1

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, I'm going to post my last two roleplays on here. Please let me know what you think. [It look's like a studio. A spotlight shines down illuminating only a small area in the middle of the room. Beyond that everything is pitch black. In the middle of this spotlight there is a chair. It's your average, simple, office computer desk chair, you know the ones that spin around.]
[Now is maybe a good time to mention that sitting in that chair is "The Dentist" Mike Wise. Mike seems relaxed enough on this chair, leaning back with his legs crossed and out straight in front of him. Mike is wearing a nice black suit, with a pair of black shoes and a red shirt that has the top couple of buttons undone.]
[Mike's face is expressionless. The only indication we have of the way Mike Wise is feeling right now is his hands. Mike has one hand clenched into a fist and the other one cupping his clenched fist. The cupping hand , yes I said cupping, is rubbing other the knuckles of his clenched fist. Every now and then Mike will pull his fist away from the other hand to slap it back into it.]
Mike Wise: Alright there everyone. Before I get started on International Incident I have a video to show you. The reason I'm showing you the following video package is because some people are a little confused about my nickname. I KNOW, I know, this is partly my fault for not introducing myself propably BUT C'MON
PEOPLE.... [Hands apart, _SLAP_, back together.] Mike Wise:... chances are that if I am not talking about drilling holes in your teeth I am not called "The Dentist" because I actually believe I'm a dentist. That's like suggesting Alex Cote is called "The Hammer" because he actually believes he's an hammer. IT'S IDIOTIC.
Anyway, I had this video put together to sort of introduce myself to everyone a little bit better. I hope you all enjoy it.
[Fade to black.] [The screen is completely black for awhile. Then we hear a Cockney accent doing a Voice Over.] VO: On the 14th of March 1976 the third and last son of legendary London Gangster Reg Wise was born. [Black and White baby pictures fade up over a black picture.] VO: Micheal Wise. Micheal was brought up into a life of crime but it didn't always look like he was going to follow in his fathers footsteps. Mike excelled in school.
[Pictures of a young Mike in school uniforms and sitting at a desk writing on a piece of paper.]
VO: Mikes two older brother's never bothered much with school but Mike was always the top of his class. Mike also excelled at sports though. At just 8 years old Mike Wise took up boxing.
[Pictures of the little lad wearing a pair of gloves and punching big punching bags twice his size.]
VO: Make no mistake about it, Mike was a mean little bastard who loved to fight. Boxing is a little limited though. You can only use your hands and there is a lot you're not allowed to do, Mike wanted a bit more freedom. When Mike was 14 his family took a holiday to Great Yarmouth.
[Postcard snaps of English seaside town Great Yarmouth followed by holiday pictures of Mike and his family.]
VO: This is where Mike Wise found more freedom. [Suddenly images from recent ICW programming explode across our screens as "It's On" by Korn plays. We see Alex Cote driving the air out of Setzer Van Strife with a Superplex, Mike Wise hitting
Nelson Alexander with his big clothesline "Say Argh", Van Strife _nail_ Alex Cote with the Smoothkick and Tommy Lionheart leaping off of the top rope and coming down on top of Alex Hawkins with the Lions Den.]
VO: Mike Wise found professional wrestling in Great Yarmouth. At a seaside carnival Mike found an attraction that let guys step into the ring with a professional wrestler. Any man willing could pay there £'s, step into the ring and all they had to do to get triple there money back was to still be standing at the end of 10 minutes. Mike had a go and....
[CRUNCH. The music cuts out and we see Dave Pietka hitting Mike Wise with Heavy Mentality on Fallout.] VO:.... at only 14 years old he got his ass kicked by a 30+ year old pro wrestler. [We see Nelson Alexander nail Wise with a enzuguri, Dave Pietka Powerbombing Mike on the arena floor, Pietka again now, this time coming down on top of Wise with an Arabian Facebuster and Nelson hitting Wise with Tha End.]
VO: Wise was beaten pretty badly but he fell in love with the sport as he got kicked senseless. When Mike told his father about this he was soon paying that same wrestler to train Mike to wrestle in Soho.
[The picture fades back to black then more pictures of Wise as a young man in school uniform.]
VO: At 16 Mike done something his two older brothers couldn't do, he finished school. Then Mike done something that shocked all of his teachers, he decided not to go to college. Instead Wise got a job working for his father, as a debt collector, while at the same time being taught the ins and outs of the family business by
Reg Wise himself. [We see pictures of Mike wearing a suit, shirt and tie, some of them with his father, some of them with other fella's clad in suits and ties.]
VO: Mike was doing great, his name was starting to go around London in those "this is someone you _don't_ want to mess with" conversations and he had more money than he could ever spend. But when Wise was 19 he met a stripper going by the stage name Bunny, it's a shame Mike didn't know her real name.
[Pictures of a teenage Bunny fade up.] VO: This is Jennifer Coltrain, Daughter of Harry Coltrain who just happens to be a bitter enemy of Reg Wise. The Wise and
Coltrain family's have hated each other generations. Both family's want to have complete control of London but they had managed to avoid all out gang war up 'til' now. Had Mike not married Bunny though, they wouldn't have avoided it any longer.
[We see the couple's family wedding photo. There's plenty of people in this photo but only one of them is smiling, the bride.]
VO: It seems Mike decided to sleep with his new stripper friend, get her pregnant but never speak to her again, until her father found out. To make a long story short there were arguments, a lot of people were unhappy but the two fathers sorted it out when they met and decided _for_ there teenage offspring that they were to get married. It was a sort of peace treaty.
[Pictures of Mike and Bunny with there new-born Daughter fade up. Bunny has been smiling in every photo so far but anytime Mike has been seen with Bunny he has been miserable.]
VO: This didn't bother Bunny much, she fell head over heels for Mike. Mike on the otherhand was brought up hating anyone who was a Coltrain and is still bitter toward Bunny to this day.
[The picture's we see of Mike Wise now are now of him as we see him today. In these picture's he is a bit bigger and has a bit more of a rugged look with stubble on his face and the tie has now disappeared. Earlier we saw picture's of Mike Wise the boy and now we're seeing Mike Wise the man.]
VO: Despite his personal problem's Mike remained a professional in his job and by the age of just 20 Mike Wise pretty much ran
Soho, London. This is when Mike first earned his nickname "The Dentist". Though he didn't often do it himself, if someone got on the wrong side of Mike he would make the order for there teeth to be removed. Painfully removed.
[Now we see picture's of Mike in his Black wrestling trunks.] VO: There's been a few false starts along the way but now at the age of 26 Mike is trying to leave his past behind him. Mike wants to turn his hobby into his profession and not only that but someday he wants to be known as "THE GREATEST OF ALL TIME".
[We see a moving picture of Madison Square Garden. As the picture pans around the arena we see it's packed with cheering fans.]
VO: In just a few days time Mike'll have the biggest match of his career. ICW Pay-Per-View, International Incident, _MADISON_
_SQUARE_ _GARDEN_, against Alex Cote. And maybe the gangsters son from London can pull off the upset, who knows? Personally I'm not betting against it.
[Fade.] [Fade back to the studio with Mike Wise.] Mike Wise: OK then, I hope that's helped clear a few things up and now onto the Pay-Per-View. Ever since I first saw Hades wrestling in Andru Edward's FWF, I knew I'd have to come to
America to achieve my dream. At International Incident I have a chance to achieve my dream and I have a chance to prove a point.
My career in ICW so far has been a little disappointing. I've wrestled two and lost two but I'm not going to let that get me down. I'm going into Madison Square Garden with high hopes. I know no one expects me to go far but then nobody expected Hasmin
Rahman to beat Lennox Lewis last year did they? Yeah, that's right I'm comparing myself to Hasmin Rahman, I AM the Hasmin Rahman of Professional Wrestling. I am going to upset everyone by winning this thing. First Alex Cote goes down, you just can't see it coming can you? but it will come. Then I'm going to go all the way.
I'm going to do exactly the same thing Rahman did when he shocked the World by knocking out Lewis, nevermind the rematch.
I'm going to lay flat everyone before me starting with Alex Cote. Sunday night is going to be my night and I'm walking away with it. Now is that what you want to happen? BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT'LL
HAPPEN! ["The Dentist" stands up and walks off into the darkness. Fade to black.]
