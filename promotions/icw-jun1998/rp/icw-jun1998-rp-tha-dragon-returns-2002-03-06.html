ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Roleplay

Tha Dragon's Returning RP

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, Both ICW, and myself, have returned to the game. I've decided to just use my old character, Tha Dragon. It's been since ICW shut it's doors since I have
RPed. I'd like some feedback, and maybe even some invites if your fed is decent. No newly started feds, please. Hell, you may all think I suck. Well, here goes.
[Scene opens inside the ICW training facility. The ring is shown, and two men are working out inside of it. As the camera pans closer, we notice it is former ICW Television, and United States Champion Tha Dragon, working with developmental talent Joe Tough. Tha Dragon whips Joe to one side if the ring, then runs after him, nailing him with a big clothesline after Joe bounces off the ropes, and turns toward him. Quickly dropping to his knees, Tha Dragon places Joe in a headlock, and works it. Shortly thereafter, Tha Dragon releases, and both men stand up. They shake, and Joe departs the ring. Tha
Dragon notices the ICW camera, wipes his brow with a towel, then hops out of the ring.]
THA DRAGON - Ah yes, a familiar sight. It's been a while since 'ol TD has worked. The last time ICW operated, to be exact. But, it seems we're both getting another chance to shine. I've got a couple pounds to knock off, and some dust to brush away, but I don't think it'll be so bad.
[Tha Dragon stops, takes a drink from his bottle of water, then continues.] THA DRAGON - I'm not so sure James Pillow or Shaun Riche see it the same way, though. I happen to be a former ICW Television, and North American Champion.
I'm a former member of the group that turned this place upside down -- EGO. Hell, if it weren't for that piece of trash, Sensuous Samantha, killing ICW,
I'd be an International Champion. It's well known I had it locked in. It was my turn. I paid my dues, and I earned it. But hey, there's no need to dwell on that, when there are much important things to worry about now.
[Tha Dragon moves over to a chair, sits down, and thinks.] THA DRAGON - Looks like after all my accomplishments, I'm sittin' on the undercard, fightin' some nobody named "The Dentist." Yeah, that gimmick will get over real well. . . So, if that wasn't enough, I see a couple familiar faces. namely, Alex Cote and Tommy Lionheart. I beat both of them while defending my ICW North American Championship, and yet, here they are on the uppercard. That piece of trash Lionheart is in the main event! hey, it's cool. I'll play by their little rules. Put me against weaklings, and they get squashed. ICW will only allow that for so long before they're forced to put me against the big boys. I'm all for climbing the ladder the easy way.
[Tha Dragon stands up, and moves back to the ring. Rolling inside, then bouncing off the ropes a few times. He stops, and looks back to the camera.]
THA DRAGON - So, some "old faces" may show up at the card, as the program says. Maybe some new ones. I wonder who could be there. Shawn Sylkk? Nick Dinsmore? The Masked Jobber? The list of former ICW greats goes on and on. Whether it be the smooth one, The Bulldog, or the Jay Oh Bee, one thing is certain. Tha Dragon is going to make this go around count -- no matter who is here. Do ya think I've been working out with these developmental losers for nothin'? In fact, I hate to cut this short, but I have a lotta work to do before the big show. So, if ya wouldn't mind. . .
[Another developmental talent, Timmy Johnson, enters the ring, bounces off the ropes a couple times then walks over to Tha Dragon, arm extended to shake hands. Tha Dragon stops, turns, and sees the red light on the camera is still on.]
THA DRAGON - What're ya still doing here? I said have work to do. What? Oh, you're waitin' to here some fancy catchphrase, eh? Something that will make my opponents shake in their boots. Something that will make the fans, those wonderful fans, love me, and cheer my name? Okay, I guess I can do that for you, since you gave me this TV time.
[Tha Dragon clears his throat] Okay, here goes. . . [Tha Dragon turns, and grabs Timmy Johnson by the neck, who has his back turned, stretching. He punches him in the small of the back, then pulls him in. Locking Johnson in a standing Dragon Sleeper, Tha Dragon applies massive pressure. After a few moments, he lifts him up into an inverted suplex, holding him vertical for a moment, and then. . . BOOM!! He drops Johnson face first into an Ace Crusher. He then stands, and looks back to the camera.]
THA DRAGON - . . .Tha End. [Tha Dragon stands over Timmy Johnson, his jet black hair dangling over his face, as he peers at his motionless body. Scene fades to black.]
