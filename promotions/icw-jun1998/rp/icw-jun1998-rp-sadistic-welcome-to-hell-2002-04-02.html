ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Roleplay

"Sadistic" Shane Alexander (roleplay)

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, This is my first roleplay since I have been back. Just lemme know what you think of it, what I could fix, what you liked, what you didn't like. You know all that good shit. Thanks ahead of time.
- Chris. ------------------------------------------- [The screen is filled with darkness, but as the dark fills your eyes, a voice fills your ears. The deep, cold voice has a Boston accent.]
??: "Before, we had respect. I mean, we would do the things that are called naughty, but never out of disrespect."
[With these words spoken, an inside shot of an old abandoned church fills the screen. The voice continues to speak.]
??: "Look at this place.. a church. The place where no matter what happens, is supposed to stay sacred. Protected from the evil of the world. Protected by a force, by which the powers have not fully been seen."
[The camera pans around showing us that most of the stained glass windows depicting saints have been smashed and broken. The pews have been tossed around, stacked, and broken. On the walls are broken pictures and grafitti.
Some of the things written on the walls, say such things as, "666, Devil's
Playground, and Welcome to Hell." The camera then focuses on the front of the church.]
[In the front of the church, there stands a very large cross with Jesus Christ's body crucified on top of it. The cross is hanging from a large half circle of glass panes that surrounds the front of the church. Sitting beneath the cross is a man. The man is sitting with his head down and his long black hair hanging in front of his face. The man snaps his head back and lifts his arms. The man is none other than "Sadistic" Shane Alexander.]
SA: "Does this look like a place that has been protected?" [Shane suddenly seems to get angry and raise his voice to the point of almost yelling.] SA: "Well... Does it?" [Shane stands up from his sitting position. When Shane stands up you can notice that he is wearing an oversized leather jacket that hangs to about his mid-thigh. The black jacket is stained with mud, dirt, and what appears to be blood. Under the jacket is a plain white t-shirt that is tucked into his black jeans. The jeans are slightly baggy with rips above his right knee and on his left thigh. Shane's face seems to lighten up from before, but yet happiness does not replace anger. Shane still seems to be angry, but his face seems laxed. His beady brown eyes, surrounded by black bags are staring into the camera. He begins to speak.]
SA: "It doesn't look like anyone has been looking after this place for years. Back in my day you would not see this no matter what. No matter what type of bad things we did, we would never disrespect."
"I see a few similarities between this church and the ICW. A few years ago, you would not have to watch over the ICW. It was a place where some of the greatest competitors met week after week to put one hell of a show together."
[Shane stops and pauses.] SA: "But now, you have punks. People who do not care what happens to the other guys. They are just in the business for the money and the fame. Well, let me tell you something. Once, you put money in front of business... you lose. Me, I could give a shit less whether the ICW pays me or not to compete in this
International Incidient battle royal we have comming up. Just as long as I get to beat the hell out of all these punks that seem to think they are greater than life... without getting put in jail."
"'Cause see when it comes down to pain, there are two categories... giving it and getting it."
[Shane stops once again only this time he starts to walk towards a wall on the far left side of the church. He continues to speak as he walks.]
SA: "I love both dimensions of it. You hit me with a chair, I'm gonna come back with barbed wire. Not because I am upset that you hit me with a chair, just because if I am going to feel pain... so are you but much, much worse."
[Shane stops in front of a wall looks at it for a moment then turns. The wall has something written on it in red letters, but can not be made out because
Shane's body is in front of it.]
SA: "At International Incident, I guarantee I will feel pain, but not nearly as much as you glory hungry punks. As of right now... ICW..."
[Shane stares deeper into the camera and points his finger sharply at the camera's lens.] "... I have _NO REMORSE_ for my actions!!" [Shane steps out of the way and we can read "Welcome to Hell" on the wall. The camera freezes in on it before fading to black.]
