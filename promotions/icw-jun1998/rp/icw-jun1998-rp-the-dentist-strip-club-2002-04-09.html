ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Roleplay

Mike Wise RP 2

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"ERRRRRRRR" [A huge, mean looking black man in a suit hurls a man out of a door and onto the concrete pavement in front of us.]
The huge guy: SHE SAID "NO TOUCHING" ASSHOLE. [The man who was just thrown to the floor picks himself up and walks away grumbling to himself.]
The huge guy: Pervert. [We are directly in front of a black door with a bulky chrome handle bar attached to it and a small circular window near it's top. Next to the door on one side there is a chalk board that has
"LAPDANCING" chalked up on it. Next to the door on the other side stands that big black bouncer that obviously don't want to mess with. Then next to him we see the front of a queue of men that goes out of our sight as it goes back and out of the picture.]
[A neon sign above the door and street lights light up the dark night sky. The dark grey pavement in front of the door lights up with several different colours as the neon sign above switches lights from one part of the sign to the other to look like a dancing woman.]
[Three men now walk into sight. They walk straight up past the queue and towards to door. Regular viewers will recognise these three men as "The Dentist" Mike Wise and his two older, bigger brothers. They are all wearing overcoats over there usual suits.
They walk straight to the door and are about to enter when....] The huge guy:[standing in there way] And where do you lot think you're going? [No answer.] The huge guy: Go on, get to the back of the queue. ["The Dentist" just looks at one of his brothers who then steps forward and says something quietly into the bouncers ear. We'll probably never know what the Wise brother said but it was enough.
The bouncer now steps to the side and opens the door for the brothers Wise.] The huge guy:[as they walk in] I do apologise sir's. Enjoy your night. [Fade to black] [The picture fades back outside the same strip joint. The only real difference between now and earlier is the guys in the queue have changed. The black door swings open and out comes "The
Dentist" Mike Wise. Wise walks directly towards us.]
Mike Wise: That took me back, I haven't done that in a long time. Me and my brothers used to go to the strip clubs all the time back in Soho. Without me and my family there wouldn't be any strip clubs in Soho. But hey that's not what you want is it?
[He stops in front of us.] Mike Wise: No. You probably want me to talk about the Pay-Per-View. Or I could talk about this coming Fallout and my match against Sid Starr, no I think I'll get to that later. For now I want to talk to you all about Alex Cote. I don't know what
Cote's going to have to say about our match at International
Incident. He may choose to feed you all a bunch of crap about kicking my ass or he may decide to tell you the truth.
[Mike smiles and gives his stubble a rub.] Mike Wise: I sat in the back of Madison Square Garden and I watched Alex Cote say he wasn't impressed by my wrestling, he wasn't worried about my, he didn't see me as a threat. In his mind Cote had already won the match but as I entered Madison
Square Garden I had other ideas. Cote could tell you he beat me easily or he could tell you the truth. The truth being that I was just one, just _one_, move away from beat Cote.
[Mike Wise holds one finger up.] Mike Wise: Cote, you'll probably Deny it but it's a fact that I pushed you to your limit, I tested you. I lost this time Cote but
I showed at International Incident that I have what it takes to make a name for myself around here. So we may meet again someday
Cote and rest assured if we do I may just be able to hit that
_one_ move that'll win it for me. Now as for Si......
Voice: Hey Mike, I bought your drink. Hurry up. [Mike Turns around to see one of his Brothers in the doorway of the strip club.] Mike Wise: Yeah. I'll be there soon. [Wise now turns back to us.] Mike Wise: Now where was I? Oh yes Sid Starr. Now if you look at the stats for this match you could say Starr just has the advantage. Starr keeps reminding us all how experienced he is where as I'm still a rookie around here. But the way I see it is
I've just come off of the best match of my career and my adrenaline is still pumping _that_ gives me an advantage. I'm on a losing streak and I'm desperate to end that losing streak, I'll do anything to pick up my first win here in ICW and _that_ gives me an advantage. I've started to learn from my mistakes......
[Holds one finger up again.] Mike Wise:...... I now know when I should nail that one move that'll put the match away and _that_ gives me an advantage. Sid, my losing streak is coming to an end and you're just the poor muppet who's going to get a thumping for me to end that streak.
Friday Night Fallout, Friday the twelfth of April 2002, The
Providence Civic Centre, "The Dentist" Mike Wise is going to give
Sid Starr that beating of his career. Now Sid, tell me _is_ that what you want to happen? BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT'LL HAPPEN. I'll see you all on Friday.
[Mike Wise turns around and walks back towards the strip club as the picture fades to black.]
