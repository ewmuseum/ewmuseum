ICW (Rhode Island, 1998–2002) — Roleplay

Shane Alexander Roleplay

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades in from black to an old wooden door. The door has cracks and chips all over. Surrounding this eerie pathway are walls with cobwebs everywhere. The pale yellow walls also have chips and cracks scattered amongst it's area. A muffled voice can be heard from beyond the door. Some of the words can be made out.]
??: "I told 'em, I told 'em. Nobody thought that......" [The voice becomes too muffled to be understood. So the camera-man opens the door, turning the dust covered crystal door knob. Once the door swings open we are shown a fair sized rooms with absolutely no furniture. A nice-sized window is shown in the front of the view, behind it on the outside world is a tree that keeps tapping against the window, it appears to be raining. The dark rain makes the room seem a lot more creepier, as the camera focuses in on the corner of the room where a man with his back to us is shown watching a black and white television. The television sits on the old wooden floors with a VCR next to it.
The man... nor what he is watching can be made out, but the man begins to speak.] ??: "Friday Night Fallout... as it happens to be my favorite day of the week, I would have to say... I had a good day."
[The man moves aside, still not revealing his identity, but alowing the camera to get a clear shot of the television in front of him. The camera zooms in and what is being shown can be made out. While your eyes are filled with scenery your ears hear silence, there is no sound on the television, just the sound of the tapping branches, and puttering rain against the windowsill.]
[Cote with chair in hand as he waits for Alexander to get to his feet. Shane slowly up and turns as Cote swings but Shane side steps then boots Cote hard in the gut making him drop the chair to the floor! Shane now with a second boot to the gut then hooks Cote up...]
SA: "BAANG! POWERBOMB ONTO THAT STEEL CHAIR!!" [With the clip ending in blackness the camera pans back revealing that the man has turned around revealing that it is indeed "Sadistic" Shane Alexander. He is still sitting on the floor, his legs are crossed but his dark jeans can be made out briefly. His jacket lies next to him balled up. He sits wearing only a white plain t-shirt. His hair dangles in front of him loosely as he begins to speak, still looking at the camera with a turned neck.]
SA: "Alex Cote... I can not be blamed for what happened to you here. You grabbed that chair and you were going to use it on me. I was not the one that wanted to get violent. So let's just say you broke the seal... the seal of what proved to be a violent, and in your case, near career-ending night."
"But even though you did grab that chair..." [Shane's voice gets deeper and colder, still looking right into the camera.] SA: "... it felt so good dropping you on the back of your head... SMACK! Right there on that same chair... yeah it felt real good, I can't lie." [Shane's voice returns to normal.] SA: "But then from there, like I knew you would, you got even, and Cote... I applaud you on this one..." [Shane turns his head back towards the television and once again the camera-zooms into the scenery on the t.v. screen. Once again backed by only the sound of rain and tapping from outside.]
[Shane sets both tables up. Cote meanwhile slowly gets back to his feet in the ring. Shane climbs to the ring apron only to get DRILLED with a big boot from
Cote. Shane falls back but manages to grab onto the top rope. Cote though off the ropes and...]
SA: "CRAAACK!! CRASHING THROUGH ONE OF MY OWN TABLES WITH A HUGE CLOTHESLINE!!" [Once again the scene ends on the television and the camera pans back to show that Shane once again is looking at us.]
SA: "Good job, that one left me with a sore back! But like I have said, it put a smile on my face and an idea in my head. So what happens next? Oh yeah, Cote, you lost, you were hit with a move that can put men out for good. A career ender to say the least."
[The camera pans into the t.v. once again... showing Shane Alexander hitting
"No Remorse" on Alex Cote. The second rope Death Valley Driver looks insane as the massive Cote lands on the back of his neck.]
SA: "NO REMORSE!!!" [The scene still rolling fades to another shot from Friday Night Fallout.] [Shane making his way back to the ring and slides in. Cote getting up and
Alexander nails him from behind knocking Cote through the ropes to the ring apron. Shane now with Cote hunched over on the top rope then leaps over the top rope hitting a sunset flip..]
SA: "SUNSET-FLIP POWERBOMB!!! Sending your half-dead corpse CRASHING through that table!!" [The camera pans back again, only this time Shane begins to stand, his dark jeans drop over his boots loosely as Shane stretches. He walks over to the window which is still releasing that tapping noise from the tree.]
SA: "I told everyone what was going to happen with Alex Cote, and from the sight which I came to see... I DID EVERYTHING I SAID I WAS GOING
TO DO!!" "And I think the ICW realized this. They now know that they are not dealing with some shit-talking punk that calls on the world and when it comes down to it runs and hides. They know that when you hear Shane
Alexander's name, backbones begin to give off a sharp pain, just from what he will probably do. So what do they do?"
[Shane, still looking out the window lets out a sigh, almost as if he is upset.] SA: "They booked me in a MAIN EVENT match, and not any main event match, a match for the ICW North American Championship."
[Shane shakes his head.] SA: "And against a man that has some rather eye opening credentials. A man that goes by the name of "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka."
[Lightning is seen striking in the background of the window as a thunderous boom is heard, nearly shaking the room in which we stand. Shane backs away and looks directly into the camera, snapping back his head so his hair flops behind his face, showing his brown mysterious eyes, with bags lying underneath. He begins to speak with a more serious tone now.]
SA: "Now this is bad... very bad. Maybe for me, but most definately for the cencorship board of the ICW. Up 'till now the ICW has only seen a drop of what "Sadistic" is about, and now that I am in a match against another fellow hardcore... <bleep>ed up... can't really focus 'cause the head has been hit more times than a two dollar whore on a Saturday Night in Chinatown, son of a bitch..."
[Shane takes a breath.] SA: "I think it is about time we let the fans see what they really want... blood! Massive amounts of blood. They like tables too... don't forget about the tables!! And then there is..."
[Shane stops and rubs his long died-red goatee.] SA: "We'll just have to wait and see what kind of fun things go on when I take on Dave Pietka. A man that says he is mental. In my books the only thing mental
about you Dave, is the fact that you actually signed your name on the contract. Even after you read, or had that heroin addicted whore read to you that my name was the one that you were facing." "You really just messed up man... see I think it is my time to show the ICW what the "Sadistic" one is all about, and you have the unfortunate task of making me _NOT_ show these fans what a man looks like after he gets his neck nearly broken in half from one single chair shot. Or from being dropped through a table, or maybe two?" [Shane again walks toward the window, staring out into the storm.] SA: "In final... "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka... on Friday Night Fallout, I _am_ leaving the American West Arena with the ICW North American championship. Whether I walk out on my own, or by a stretcher. I am walking out with that title. Behind me, there will be nothing but blood... aaand some pretty good shots of what happens when someone is the victim of hardcore violence." "Oh yeah, and Dave... just me and you, this one might get to graphic for that whore of yours, so I might have to cover her eyes, and that other idiot... "Marz" whatever... if you get in my way..." [Shane stops and takes a deep breath.] SA: "No... you are gonna get your ass kicked too. Just 'cause you pis me off. So all three of you's have been warned... ... I have _NO REMORSE_ for the actions I take!!!" [The camera cuts to zoom into a shot on the television that depicts the enterage of "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka, including "Double D" Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress. The three seem to be posing for the camera, then the screen pauses, and the two stooges begin to fade away leaving only Dave
Pietka.] [Fade to black.] - Chris. Handles "Sadistic" Shane Alexander ICW [2-1-0]
