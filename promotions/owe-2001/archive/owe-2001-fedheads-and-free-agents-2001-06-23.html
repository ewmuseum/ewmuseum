OWE Can Help Both Fedheads and Free Agents

23 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, this is [name withheld] again, and I'm here to say somethings about the OWE... HOLD ON!! Don't leave just yet.. The OWE is a place designed to help out the fedheads and free agents. So, I'll put this into two parts. Fed Heads: Weather your fed is a small promotion or a large one, help is needed at some point and time. What is the OWE? The OWE, which is short for Organized Wrestling Entertainment, is a concept that has been tossed around the net for quiet along time. The concept that we're talking about is that a web site is sat up for one thing, and that's to help out federations and wrestlers also. The OWE is that web site, committed to helping out, but is sat up in a Inter-fed type Format. In reality, the OWE is just a mediator/media center between the feds in it but, in character, will play the role of the Owning Business (or Television Syndicate). For all that we will offer to your federation, we ask for one thing.. a link to us on it's main page. That easy.. but what is that you say.. What does the OWE offer? Great question, we thought you looked like a smart person.. The OWE web site will do almost everything under the sun, except hosting your page. Hosting your page would take a hugh chunk out of our time, thus the time that we use to help out the federations will be decreased. Now, what we do offer up is a web site that will display not only your Shows and Columns/Articles, but will have our own also. Articles: Every month there will be two or three of either "E-wrestling (in general)" or something written as a one-time-only thing. Most of the time, a wrestler in (or outside of) the OWE will write about something going on or on their mind. Columns: Weekly columnist write about the happening going on around the OWE's federations. Some are for news, and others are for rumors.. the topic will be posted with it, letting the people know what it is about, and if it is for a certain federation. Interviews: It's clear as it can be.. Interviews with staff members and wrestler's will be put in this category. Talk Radio: This is one of our best services that we give to you. At this moment, only one host has been hired to do a show. His name is Blake Stone, an award winning host, and he will be doing "Loads of BS".. The shows format will be in order from lowest ranked federation to the highest ranked.. The show is in Real Audio format for the time being. Top 100: Every year the OWE does a list of the top one hundred wrestlers in the OWE's database. This shows where the talent is at, and gives the wrestler/federation bragging rights. One last thing, there is a money system for the OWE Federations. Here is the system... 1. Federation Signing Bonus: Right now, the federations that sign-up with us between the dates of June 10th and July 1st will be given an a Signing Bonus of $15,000. (That might not seem alot, but in the OWE money is handed out, more then taken in). 2. Weekly Shows: Everyweek the Federations in the OWE have their cards rated. It's on a scale of 1.00 to 10.00. For every 1.00 your card is given, you make $200. So if you get a 7.00, your fed will receive $1,400. Remember, if your fed has two cards a week, the highest rated of the two will be posted. Also, if your card is late, the rating will not be affected, but the money will decress by $100 for everyday it is late. 3. Pay Per Views: Pay per views are incouraged in the OWE, seeing as how most federations pull in the money by having a pay per view. Your federation will be given $500 for every 1.00 that a Pay Per View is rated. This means, if your PPV makes a 7.00, your fed will recieve $3,500. Remember that only one PPV a month will be rated and the ammount of that PPV paid, but if there are two PPV's a month, the better rated of the two will be posted and paid for. 4. Jump Ship, or Pink Slip: If a person leaves your federation to join another one, or is fired by a OWE federation, the ammount of money they were worth will be halfed and paid back to the federation. Free Agents: We know that sometimes it is hard to find any good feds out their. When you come over to the OWE's website, we give you the chance to sign up as a Free Agent. As a free agent, it is almost impossible to not be picked up by one of the feds in the OWE. These are the precedures of picking up a free agent in the OWE. 1. With in one week of their Free Agent notice, the OWE federations can bid against each other, and the person with the highest bid gets the wrestler and pays the price they bidded for them... 2. After a week has pasted and the wrestler hasn't been hired, his/her application will be posted in the Free Agent Listings page. If you pay their price, you get them.. first come, first serve. 3. And last... Ever month there will be a Draft. There will be at least two rounds, meaning that the min number of wrestler's in a draft is two times the number of Federations in the draft. No money is paid, just a chance to get the free agents into the OWE, and not losing them to another promotion.. But if you are not Drafted into a fed, then you will be left on the Free Agent Listings until we are told that you want off of it. This allows you to be contacted by the feds in the e-wrestling world... That's just a tip of the iceburg... You can visit the OWE's website and check it out... We are starting now, so we don't have any feds under our belt. www.owehq.homestead.com Thanks for your attention and time.. and tell me that you think about this idea or website.
