""Hollywood" Tony Pride's History"

24 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/ 'Hollywood' Tony Pride "A Star is Born..." -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ The story of a man named "Hollywood" is a long and interesting tale. Tony Pride was born Anthony Prissintino in Cocoa Beach, Florida, on July the first 1972 to Eugene & Doretha Prissintino. Prissintino was born into a middle-upper class family. As the only child, Tony always got his way. Eugene Prissintino was a Naval Officer and went off to sea 6 months out of a year. Doretha Prissintino was a mother who would do anything and buy anything for her son... and usually did. Prissintino went through private schooling all the way through his High School years. Pride was a three sport athlete, All State Cornerback in Football, All State in Wrestling along with breaking State Records in the Long Jump in Track and Field. Prissintino was offered various Scholarships to many schools. In 1988 after graduating from High School and deliberating with his parents in peers, Prissintino decided to go to the University of Miami (Coral Gables). During his summer Football camps Prissintino blew out his left knee. His football career was in question now, yet Prissintino always had one love... Professional Wrestling. Going back in time, Prissintino was always a huge Pro Wrestling fan. A fan of the Independent Regions like Georgia Wrestling, Mid South, Florida Wrestling and the early National Wrestling Alliance. Prissintino idles were the "Nature Boy" Buddy Rogers, "Nature Boy" Ric Flair, Roddy Piper, Nick Bockwinkel, Bruno Sammartino along with a few of the modern day "Rock & Wrestling" wrestlers. Prissintino was lost without being able to use his athletic ability. The only thing that occupied was watching wrestling and being with women. Prissintino found himself being very unhappy, because of his situation. He called home and complained to his mother every night. The women in his life were only one night flings, and Prissintino was always a loner and because of that had few friends. One late Saturday Night Prissintino went to a party. Being bored and starved for attention Prissintino went into the bathroom, grabbed a razor and bladed himself across the forehead and ran out into the party screaming, "I'm a wrestler, I'm a wrestler." One of Prissintino friends ran to a phone and called his mother and told her the situation. Prissintino got on the phone and after talking with his mother for a while, it was mutually decided that he should come home. After finishing the first semester at University of Miami, Prissintino headed on home to Cocoa Beach. While home Prissintino sat down with his parents and told them what he wanted to do, "become a professional wrestler!" Prissintino parents agreed under two conditions, 1) They'd pay for his wrestling school only if, 2) He found a job to help bring in extra money. Prissintino agreed and so the next day he called up the nearest wrestling school in his area and the instructors name was Boris Malenko. Former Pro Wrestling great now running a school in the Florida area. Prissintino also had to hold up his end of the bargain, so two days after finding the school, he found a job. Working at the local Chip & Dales in downtown Cocoa Beach. Both of these moves would affect Prissintino more than he could ever imagine. Prissintino was coming along fine in the wrestling school under Malenko's training. He perfected the game of mat wrestling after about 3 months in the school. He signed for his first match which was against a local opponent named Raymond Smolka, a lumbering man from the Jersey Shore. Prissintino had his problems with Smolka yet in the end used his finisher (Then a swinging neck breaker) to get his first win. The Malenko's were impressed with Prissintino, yet he wasn't. Prissintino at 6'0 and 220lbs was a light heavyweight in many people's eyes, yet wrestled like a heavyweight. So with the blessing of his parents and the Malenkos, he went on a 4 week training vacation to Baja Mexico, to learn the Luchador style. Prissintino took a months leave from his job and headed to Mexico. While there Prissintino learned many dare devil tactics and impressed many of the Mexicans with his grit and determination to be the best. After completing the 4 week training, Prissintino was honored as an official Luchador, something he held in high esteem. Upon returning to Florida, Prissintino along with the Malenko's felt he was ready to step in the squared circle...Yet where? Prissintino continued to work in the Chip & Dales, and while there he met a man named Steven Sirjowski. Tony & Steve became very close friends, Steve himself had just graduated from wrestling school in California and had come out to Florida looking for an opportunity. Steve was a few years older than Tony and told him a lot of the pitfalls of the independent wrestling scene, yet he himself had never been in the "majors." Prissintino continued to work some independent shows in the Florida area but could never break through. Prissintino was almost on the verge of quitting and going back to school, until she walked in the door. She, being Ms. Europe, former Valet of Scorpatoa and current wife of Global Wrestling Alliance Hall of Famer "Nature Boy" Ric Rogers. The Global Wrestling Alliance was an up and coming federation, it had just signed a new 4 year television deal with America Online TV. Prissintino recognized her right away, yet was scared to approach her. Prissintino let Steve know that Ms. Europe was here... Upon hearing this, Steve dropped what he was doing and darted over to her table. Prissintino, could not make out what was being said, yet after almost an hour of talking, Steve & Ms. Europe walked out of Chip & Dales together. Three hours passed and Prissintino was getting nervous about his friends where a bouts. Suddenly Steven burst through the doors with a two papers in his hand, a smirk on his face and smelling of expensive perfume! Steven yelled, " I did it, I did it!". Prissintino looked puzzled at his friend. Steven calmed down a little and explained, " After we left here, we went back to her hotel room. After a few drinks I gave her the ride of her life. You could say it was X-rated. Anyway, after that she leads me down the hall to meet none other than her HUSBAND! "Nature Boy" Ric Rogers. I told him our story and he called up the head of the GWA, Irwin Ref. Irwin heard him out and offered us two contracts on the spot, he faxed them over and here they are! " Prissintino was excited over these new developments. Both men quit their jobs on the spot, packed up their things into Steve's 1986 Honda and headed from Florida to the GWA's offices in Oklahoma. Steve & Tony arrived there in January 1996 and were greeted with open arms and told the ropes of how the GWA worked. Prissintino was a lot less outgoing than Steve. Steve was immediately thrown into a gimmick. They changed his last name and had him grow out his brown hair and die it blonde. In February 1996, Steven Sirjowski became "Sexy" Steve Sire! He debuted in a few dark matches on the GWA's television programming and after becoming "over" with the fans was made a mainstream heel and given tons of interview time. He feuded over the AOL Title, and raised a lot of eye brows with his old school wrestling style, similar to that of Ric Flair's. All this time Prissintino sat in the shadows, the GWA suit's could not find a good way to bring him in. The rookie crop of the GWA was it's best it had ever been and they were scared Prissintino would get lost in the shuffle. Ronny Smith, Steve Sire, Ken Nemesis, Tex Steel and Sting would be just a few of the rookies Prissintino would be in against. So the GWA suits decided to hold off on Prissintino until they could find a suitable gimmick. In May 1996, after 5 months of sitting on the sidelines, Ric Rogers & Steve Sire came up with a brilliant plan. They were going to restart the Four Horsemen of the GWA. The Four Horsemen was at that time, the most storied stable in wrestling history. Steve pushed hard for the GWA suits to make Prissintino the third Horsemen. Finally after many phone calls and brainstorming meetings, Prissintino was made a Horsemen. He'd make his on camera debut in June... Yet there was one problem, his last name! Prissintino is not a marketable name, on top of that what would be a good nickname? Prissintino figured since he and Sire were close friends, their gimmicks should match a little. So since Sire was "Sexy," Prissintino would become "Pretty." "Pretty" Tony Prissintino... The suits liked it, yet still didn't like the last name. So finally after a few days of going through last names, Prissintino stormed into the offices and exclaimed... " I've got it"! "Pretty" Tony Pride! The GWA Suits loved the name and Pride & Sire were ready to go as the newest Horsemen. Upon Pride's arrival in what is to be called one of the largest upsets in wrestling history... Steve Sire won the GWA World Championship from then champion Tony Flair. This was to be Pride's night to debut and shine... Yet he was overshadowed by his best friend becoming World Champion. So Pride's debut came off very lackluster. Soon after Sire would lose the World Title and fall into a slump. While a fourth Horsemen was finally found... A man by the name of "Ravishing" Robby Rite. Pride did not like Rite, yet the suits teamed them up and called them the "Young Guns." Rite & Pride despite personal feelings were a great tag team combination. While both were. Five hundred wrestlers in the singles scene, they excelled in tag team competition. On July 26, 1996, they faced the then World Tag Team Champions, "The Midnight Riders," John Mckoy & Tex Steel. Members of the now very powerful family called the Ring Syndicate. In what was deemed a Tag Team Classic, the match went to a 45 minute time limit draw. The two teams generated so much heat that they signed to meet in two weeks inside a steel cage with an hour time limit to crown the World Tag Team Champions. The night finally came, the Young Guns were heavy underdogs in this situation. The two weeks passed and the teams met again. The cage match was all that could be expected and more... Both teams pulled out all the stops. All four men were busted open and were all exhausted. After 40 minutes of grueling bloody action, Pride hit Tex Steel with a running savat kick and got the dramatic win as the impossible had happened... The Young Guns had became World Tag Team Champions. Yet that is when the problems started for these men, as Pride & Rite's hatred for each other escalated. In their defenses they would argue and even come as far as throwing punches at each other. Rite was jealous of Pride who had just received a United States Title match at the September PPV vs. "Iron Man" Tex Steel. Rite felt he deserved the shot. On a segment of the interview show, "Peep Show w/ Steve Sire." Rite left the Four Horsemen and challenged Pride to a one on one Match to decide who would hold the tag titles. The winner would pick a new partner and keep both belts, the loser would walk away and lose his half of the tag belts. The match was signed on the same night of the September PPV. Pride was going to have to pull double duty in a sense. This was the biggest night of his career. Not only to try and hold onto the World Tag Titles, yet to win his first singles title. He would have to defeat the "Iron Man" Tex Steel, a multi-time World Champion and current USA Champion. In a match that would set Tony Pride's career on fire, Pride for the second time in his career, pinned the "Goliath" in many peoples eyes, Tex Steel and won the USA Title. Later on that night he went onto defeat Robby Rite and won both tag belts. He named Bruiser Bill as his new partner. Pride was on top of the world until, things started to turn sour. The Four Horsemen disbanded because of Ric Rogers retirement. Irwin Ref stepped down as GWA Commissioner and Blind Reff took his place. Steve Sire was caught in a hotel room with a GWA Intern and was suspended until 1997. Then Pride got the worst news of his life, his father had passed away after a sudden heart attack. Pride dropped everything he was doing and headed home... In the mean time the GWA had no choice but to strip him of the USA Title and for Bruiser Bill to find a new tag partner. Yet Pride didn't care, he was more concerned about his family than anything. In November 1996 after a month of mourning, Pride and his mother decided it'd be best for him if he went back to wrestling. It was the only thing that made him happy. Pride came back and was now labeled as more of a singles wrestler, the GWA suits decided they'd make use of his hiatus and change his nickname. He went from "Pretty" to "Hollywood." The GWA came up with an angle to explain his disappearance, he was off in "Hollywood" filming movies. Pride came back with a new attitude and a new valet, Ms. Destiny. Pride & Destiny reeked havoc on the league as Pride's mic work and ring skills sky rocketed him to unbelievable heel popularity. Pride was showcased on TV as he was booked in what would become one of the best feuds in GWA history... Pride vs. Steel. Tex Steel & Tony Pride developed a rivalry that took a back seat to none. Pride came back with a vengeance and captured the USA Title back from Tex Steel. Only to lose it to his former partner Robby Rite two weeks later. Yet the next week Pride regained it to become a 3 time USA Champion. Pride ended the year by defeating Tex Steel again in a steel cage match. January 1997 was a new beginning for Pride. The GWA was always known for it's surprises to start off the year and 1997 was no different. In shocking events, the top two faces of all time in GWA history, Ronny Smith & Ken Nemesis turned on the fans and joined the Top Heel Family the Ring Syndicate with Classy Blasy and Pride's rival, Tex Steel. With those two turning heel the GWA needed a top face... They only had one person to turn to... Tony Pride. Yet Pride pulled a power play and told the offices the only way he'd turn face is if they reinstated his best friend, Steve Sire. The GWA decided to listen to Pride and reinstated Sire and brought him back in as a face. Pride & Sire started a new family called the Upper Echelon. They joined with the 911 Kid who was fresh off his face turn. These three former monster heels went on to become one of the strongest face families in the GWA. Pride's popularity skyrocketed as his change of attitude brought a change of valets, out with the evil Destiny, in with the beautiful and shy Ms. Starr. Pride would soon become the leader of the Upper Echelon and along with Ms. Starr became a heart throb. Pride was then thrown into what would become the biggest angle of his life...The Upper Echelon was scheduled to face a mystery team at the next PPI. Little did Pride know that, that night would be the end of that Upper Echelon. On that night the mystery Team was revealed to be.... Steve Sire & The 911 Kid joined by Tony Flair. Pride was turned on by his best friend and the lowly 911 Kid. All this did was sky rocket Pride's popularity as he was now an island to himself... Yet not for long. There were also two very popular men in the GWA who stood for what Pride believed in. James Naismith and Jon Atlas... After a couple weeks of negotiating, Naismith & Atlas joined the Upper Echelon along with Ahmed Reed, and they formed what was undoubtedly the best family talent wise in history! Soon after, Pride captured his first World Title from Lance Havoc. Pride would hold onto the belt for a Month and a Half before losing it to Classy Blasy. Yet with the skills of Atlas, Reed & Naismith the Upper Echelon was making a push as the top team in all of wrestling. All 4 men enjoyed great success for the next 4 months as Pride went on to win 4 more United States Title before the summer of 97. The Upper Echelon came into the summer of 97' with great expectations as Atlas & Naismith had stepped into their own as well as Reed. On the Night of the July 97' ppv Pride's career would take yet another turn. He would lose in a grueling Texas Death Match to Mr. Sinister. Yet later in the night Pride would watch his friend James Naismith win the World Title. Pride, Atlas & Reed were teamed together that night to take on the Syndicate, Ronny Smith, Classy Blasy and a Mystery Man. The Mystery Man would take Ken Nemesis's spot who retired a month before. The Mystery Man came out with a mask on calling himself Masked Franchise. The match was a grueling match in which Pride took a lot of punishment. He went to his corner to make the tag to Atlas. Atlas then turned as Pride was getting up and spit in both Pride & Reeds faces. Atlas went over to the masked man and unmasked him to be... James Naismith! Atlas & Naismith had left the Upper Echelon for the Ring Syndicate in a shocking turn of events! Pride's career then went into a tailspin as he lost match after match. He was so hurt and betrayed, every friend he had in his life had turned their back on him. After losing 6 matches in a row Pride took some time off, two months off to sort out his head and to find his smile. In September 1997 Pride returned to the ring and stormed the GWA. He challenged the Ring Syndicate (Ronny Smith, Classy Blasy, Naismith & Jon Atlas. Tex Steel had retired) to a War Games match in which those 4 would challenge Pride and 3 other men. The match was signed and the night came, Pride's men were Ahmed Reed, Sam Leever and the man that shocked everyone, former Ring Syndicate member, Ken Nemesis! The match was a grueling match in which the Ring Syndicate dominated and once again thwarted the "good" guys efforts. Pride did not let it bother him though, he vowed to start a new Upper Echelon. Sam Leever was the first to join Pride as he started to turn his career around. Pride came back and won 11 straight matches. Then GWA Supercard Promoter [name withheld] came up with a match to headline the October 97 PPV. It'd be called ThunderDome: A massive cage structure filled with electrical wires and such. Pride's Upper Echelon once again faced off against The Ring Syndicate of Blasy, Steel, Atlas & Naismith (Ronny Smith had been kicked out of the Syndicate in favor for the returning from retirement Tex Steel). Pride recruited the Reaper & LudiCris to stand by his side against the Syndicate. Yet he needed one more person... What many did not know was that Pride was secretly in talks with his former best friend, "Sexy" Steve Sire! They had mad up many months before after Sire's daughter died in a car accident. To the crowds shock Steve Sire walked through the curtain as the Mystery man to a roaring standing ovation. He entered the cage and the match was an exciting and grueling match until the kick that was heard round the world happened! In the middle of the action... Pride turned around and kicked his partner LudiCris in the mouth with his finisher now called "Superstar Superkick." Ludicris dropped like a ton of bricks as the crowd was stunned. As Pride, Reaper & Sire left the ring and left Ludicris laying in the middle for the Syndicate to pound on. Afterwards, Pride made it clear that he was tired of being the 'fans man.' The fans got him nowhere but flat on his back. Pride then vowed everything he did he was doing for himself. The New Upper Echelon was born... Sam Leever, The Reaper, Steve Sire & Tony Pride. Pride on national TV fired Ms. Starr and rehired Ms. Destiny. Pride then went on a tear through the league capturing the SWA Title as well as two more United States Titles. Pride then set his sights on the World Title, which was held by once again the leagues top face... Ronny Smith. Smith was thought to be unbeatable as he held the World Title for 3 months. He compiled a 31-4 win/loss record. No one had beaten him since September and it was now Mid-December. Yet Pride through underhanded tactics weaseled his way into a World Title match. During Smith's entrance, Pride had Sam Leever & Steve Sire distract Smith and Pride came running down and clipped Smith in the knee with a baseball bat before the match started. All three men pounded on Smith before the match could start. Smith made a valiant effort yet couldn't over come the earlier beating and was laid out with a Superkick from Pride. Pride shocked the wrestling world and beat Smith in the center of the ring for the GWA World Title. Pride vowed to be a fighting champion, just because he wanted to be on TV all the time. Pride made four defenses in 8 days... Defeating LudiCris and Aveon Aorta. Along with Ronny Smith in a brutal scaffold match that went down in the GWA record books. Pride then defended his World Title in a 4 Way Match against Aveon Aorta, Dustin Haas, and Ludicris. Haas was quickly tossed out and Ludicris & Aveon doubled up on Pride to get him out. Pride was furious with this and vowed that he would never step foot in the GWA again since they obviously planned this match to MAKE him lose the World Title. In the passing weeks Pride was voted into the GWA Hall of Fame and came in second place in Wrestler of the Year voting along with winning Most Hated. Yet Pride did not show at that awards banquet...Many thought they'd seen the last of Tony Pride. Soon after the GWA lost it's contract with AOL TV and many of their major stars left. [name withheld] along with Ken Nemesis, James Naismith & Al Lucente started the National Wrestling Council. A new up and coming promotion with revolutionary ideas. After seeing many of his old enemies and Friends, Tex Steel, Eric Rage, Dread Saxon, Jon Atlas, Ken Nemesis, Soul Shadow, Mr.Sinister, The 911 Kid. All on TV, Pride knew that the NWC would be the place for him. He made his NWC debut in typical Tony Pride fashion, trying to steal the show. He came out and leveled Soul Shadow with the Superstar Superkick in the finals of the World Title Battle Royal's. Where Soul Shadow was facing Prides arch-rival Tex Steel. It was obvious Steel & Pride had formed a partnership. Soul Shadow overcame the odds and became the first ever NWC Champion. So Pride, not liking that, traveled up to Canada (CCW Region) and cut a few interviews for that promotion. Pride was booked to join the Scholar, Jay Turner, and masked Steve Sire to become the top heel family in Canada... Yet former wrestler and Syndicate Member James Naismith, now CCW's head, soon had personal problems and had to close down the region. Tony Pride was left without a home until the CCW relocated to Virginia, under the leadership of Pride's old promoter Rick Mayer. Mayer was a CO-promoter in the GWA which had a joint partnership with the CWF in which Mayer ran. Pride wrestled a few matches in the CWF, yet preferred the GWA back then. Mayer signed Pride and VCW would begin with a bang. Pride became the self-proclaimed "Foundation" of not only VCW but all of wrestling. So Mayer in a vengeful show of jealousy booked Pride in a match against a woman named Ice Diamond. In a match that would be called anything but one on one... Ice Diamond defeated Pride after interference from The Reaper. Pride would not tolerate this and a few days later cut a "shoot" interview breaking all FCC rules and vowing that Mayer or VCW would never be the same when he was done. Pride went onto brutally beat the Sandman and then outlast Ron Lauren to become VCW's first Television Champion. Pride all this time had a major feud brewing with former enemy & friend, Dread Saxon who was now in the MCW region. Pride and Saxon had a history in the GWA, Pride continued to brag about how he had never lost to Dread Saxon. Pride attacked Saxon from behind not once but twice. Saxon issued Pride a challenge for a 30 minute Iron Man match at the NWC's 2nd PPV "Across Enemy Lines." Pride and Saxon finally met in the much anticipated Iron Man match. The match was not a let down as both men won a fall a piece and went the full 30 minute time limit...Forcing an overtime. In which Saxon beat Pride with his own move, a Saxon "Superstar" Superkick to get the win. Even with that set back, Pride has vowed to become NWC World Champion and continue to set the wrestling world on it's ear.
