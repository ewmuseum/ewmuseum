National Wrestling Council — Wrestler History

"Nightmare's History"

24 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/ A NIGHTMARE TO REMEMBER: -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ Jeff Wells was born in October 28th, 1971 in the large city if Atlanta, Georgia. He lived a normal life with one brother and one sister. He was a spoiled kid and often got whatever he wanted. Whenever he didn't get what he wanted, he would show his bad temper and throw things around. He has been like that since the age of six. His parents were Paula and John Wells: two attorneys that spent most of their time at offices in downtown Atlanta. Jeff was the oldest, at age seven, so he had to baby-sit his younger brother, sister and cousin Sean Wells. His brother's name was Kevin and his sister's name was Dorothy. They were "rotten" little kids and always screamed and cried. Jeff would usually just ignore them and gaze outside and watch other kids playing all kinds of sports, like football and baseball. Jeff was always jealous of them, and they teased him for it, because he couldn't come outside. Jeff and Sean would always try to think of things to do around the house. Yet, they never found anything tointerest them fully. Sean was the same age as Jeff, but Jeff was a little bit older. When Jeff's Mom got home it was always too late for him to go out, so his Mom would bring him home whatever he saw on TV that he liked. Jeff wanted a four hundred dollar toy, though, and his Mom refused to get it. Jeff then started going nuts! His Mom would start screaming at him and Jeff would actually hit her. He, obviously, was an exceedingly violent child. He instantly knew that when he hit his Mom his dad would step in that's why he would only fight back when his dad wasn't home. His father was a very muscular man, an officer in the army who had fought in many great battles. He would slap his son, and Jeff would feel it for a couple of weeks. That's why he never disobeyed his father. On Jeff's 8th birthday he had a birthday party outside. He loved it out there so much, there were so many things to do instead of being cramped up in the house. Jeff and Sean would fight and pick on the little kids. His Mom was mad, but still, it was his day to do what he wanted. He desired a Swiss army knife. His Mom again said no and Jeff entered into one of his fits again. His Mom gave him a rubber knife just for a joke and Jeff went absolutely crazy! He started running around trashing everything in sight. No one stopped him. He just went on knocking this and that. He was so mad that he went to the kitchen and brought out a knife. Everyone in the house ran out side as Jeff chased everyone out. Jeff soon dropped the knife and began to cry. But his Mom didn't comfort him, they weren't like that. They never really cared what their kids thought. At age ten him and his cousin Sean started pee wee wrestling, he never knew what wrestling was but his father pushed him into it. He tried it and began to enjoy it. He watched wrestling on TV and didn't understand why they didn't have those rules at school. He would watch TV all day on weekends and study their moves. When he came back to school he would put moves on the other boys that they never taught. He would do suplexes and all sorts of other moves. He was very strong for his age. His instructor would yell at him and teach him the right way. But Jeff never listened. He would keep on doing the moves. The instructor would go to his parents and his parents would beat him. Jeff began to get a really bad temper and every one in school would stay away from him. When he was thirteen Jeff started smoking whenever his parents weren't looking, his cousin Sean smoked too only because Jeff smoked. He would hang around school late at night because the school wasn't far from his house he would smoke until his heart couldn't take it anymore. He would come home late at night and no one would be awake, no one cared what he did. His parents only wanted to make their life good for themselves. That made Jeff mad once again and he would smoke inside the house to wake up his parents. No use. Jeff would never bring in his homework for school. He would just say that he didn't care. He was at the principal's office at least twice a day. But of course his parents didn't care. They were over at the office working and earning money. Jeff would continue not bringing his homework for along time. He was trying to get his parents attention. Jeff started back talking is parents but all he would get was a punch in the face. Jeff started fighting back at his Mom but his dad would step in and Jeff would have to go to the hospital. They said it was from a bicycle accident but he knew it wasn't true. When Jeff was fourteen he tried out for modified football, he wasn't very good but his Dad urged him to give it a shot. Unlike wrestling football he was not at, he was never outside to play football. He told his Dad that but his Dad said he was making excuses. Jeff tried out for whatever position he could. They tried him at running back, couldn't dodge the defense. They tried him at line backers, couldn't block defense. They tried him in every place every defensive line. Jeff was too little to be defensive line but they didn't want him to feel bad for not making the team. Unfortunately, he was cut from the team and his father punished him and beat him every night. Jeff swore that he would get back at his dad. His dad would beat him every day also just for fun. Jeff got so tired of it that he ran away for two weeks. He lived in an alley in downtown Atlanta. When he went back home his dad told him to go back where he came from and that no one missed him. Jeff went to his room and stayed there for along time. At age 15 Jeff had his first real girlfriend. Her name was Sara Hesser. They would spend a lot of time together until one punk kid stepped in Jeff's way. His name is David Leonard. Him and Jeff were always in competition. Finally Davis punched Jeff and that set off his temper. David and Jeff got into an all out brawl. David punched and kicked but Jeff did his wrestling moves. In the end Jeff came out the winner until a bunch of David's friends ganged up on him. Jeff was soon suspended for two weeks and David wasn't suspended at all because he couldn't stay home. Jeff and Sara soon broke up after that fight because she didn't like violence. When Jeff came back to school Sara was dating David, this made Jeff mad and he brought a gun to school. He later got kicked out of school. David pranked Jeff's house all of the time and wouldn't leave Jeff alone. David got some friends to throw eggs at his door. Jeff ran out with a shot gun and started firing. They never went to his house again. Jeff only injured one student but they never sewed. David came over to his house to start another fight but Jeff saw him coming, when David knocked Jeff opened up and nailed him in the face knocking him out cold. David woke up twenty minutes later in an alley somewhere in Atlanta. He started crying and whining for his mommy. Jeff was able to be home and not worry about homework or not worry about what teachers thought of him. He was free. He was actually glad that he did what he did. But it didn't stay for long because the school sewed Jeff for attempted murder. Jeff couldn't believe what he was hearing. Attempted murder? He wasn't thinking about killing David. Just frightening him a little bit. Jeff's Mom and dad didn't support their son on this one, they wanted to keep out of it. Jeff was found not guilty for attempted murder but found guilty for endangering others lives. Jeff was sentenced two years with a possible probation in seven months. Jeff was confused, he new that it was wrong to bring in the gun but he wasn't going to kill anyone. Jeff had a rough life in juvenile hall. He was locked up with all kinds of criminals..... This one kid was in hear for 20 years for killing his parents. Another for killing his principal for not letting him go on some field trip. Now that was nuts, Jeff thought he was messed up he thought about the others. He didn't want to be like them. A criminal. He worked his but off in juvenile hall for seven full months and his Case came back up again. He was free for doing his duties in prison. Jeff promised to his self that he would never do something stupid like that again. For Jeff's free time he would lift weights, he would do reps every day and bench press every afternoon. He had nothing else to do so why sit at home and watch TV? He did that enough when he was younger. He wanted to get strong like his father. His father was a sports fanatic. All he cared about was his work, and sports. His father was a football star over at the Atlanta Falcons but had his career cut short when his vertebrate was damaged. He always felt sorry for his dad but his dad didn't want anyone to feel bad for him. At eight teen he was looking for a job and barely had enough brains to make it in the real life. He remembered wrestling and signed up for a try out. Their were a bunch of people trying out. People trying out just for a job. Like Jeff. He then joined the EWF and began to wrestle against rookies. He had one problem, he couldn't think of a name. He thought tough and went back in his mind about his child hood. His father always called him "Nightmare" because whenever he had his fits he would go out of control. Jeff Wells was no longer called Jeff Wells, he was called Nightmare. He was looking for a friend in the EWF to help him out and he didn't have to look long. He found a professional wrestler named Johnny Rotten. Him and Johnny Rotten were best friends. They would go out to town and have a great time. Johnny told him all of the basics of wrestling and Nightmare met Johnny's other friends. His friends were Doom, Grim Reaper and The Crow. Nightmare and the other three formed a stable called the Masters Of Darkness (MOD). Johnny Rotten and Nightmare tagged up together and their tag team was called DarkSide. Nightmare would have never survived if it wasn't for Johnny. Nightmare went on to the EWF with a decent record. He and Johnny Rotten had a terrific record for tag team. They won the EWF tag team belts. Nightmare and Johnny kept those tag belts until they were defeated by Blood and Meat. Nightmare and Johnny Rotten regrouped to pursue them again, and soon got them back, within two months. Nightmare's biggest match in the EWF was when he went against the Intercontinental Champion Steel. Steel hated the MOD and likewise, the MOD despised Steel. Johnny told Nightmare to fight his hardest and to bring back the belt to the MOD. But Nightmare was still too young he lost the match when Steel hit him with Steel crow bar. But it was still his biggest match ever. After that match, Steel left the EWF and the EWF was losing it's talent. The MOD stayed there for a while until it closed down for good. Johnny Rotten said good-bye to Nightmare but that wasn't the last time that he would see Johnny. Nightmare went on to the AWL (Alternative Wrestling League) were he met his cousin Hurricane. It was such a surprise that they started jumping up and down, he and the President (Bundy) were good pals and Nightmare met a new wrestler named JF. They became tag team partners and were known as the DarkSide. They never won a championship but they were still regarded as a great team. The AWL was too boring, so Nightmare decided to go to the KWF (Killer's Wrestling League). JF followed behind him and they joined a stable called (V)oney and Power. Nightmare had his second chance to get a belt and he was more than successful. He earned the KWF European Belt and brought it back to (V)oney and Power. That was one of the most proud moments in Nightmare's career. At home, Nightmare's Mom went nuts herself, and started a impressive string of robbing banks. When Nightmare heard the news he started losing his mind yet again. He was looking all over for his Mom, hoping to stop her. Paula was on "America's Most Wanted" for killing 27 innocent people in a bank heist on April 23rd, 1989. Nightmare was only 18 years old at the time. He went back home to Atlanta and therefore, had to depart from the KWF and give up his European Belt. On October 24th, just four days before Nightmare's birthday, his Mom was sentenced to the electric chair. On October 27th, Nightmare was able to see his Mom for the last time in his young life. It was the first instance in a while that he cried. His Mom was crying and saying that she didn't know why she did it... Two days later, his Mom was executed and the whole world stood still for Jeff Wells. Nightmare didn't go back to wrestling until two years. He was Twenty at the time and millions of agents were looking to sponsor him. And thousands of fed owners were looking to bring him into their fed. Nightmare signed a two-year deal with it Nike, for him to advertise their new show. He became a star just because of the incident with his Mom and that he was coming back to wrestling. Nightmare, however, didn't like the idea that he was famous because of what his Mom committed. He hated the idea entirely. He wished to be known as a great wrestler, not as some kid that had a murderer for a mom. Nightmare soon joined the FWO, and took out his cold feelings on their wrestlers. He wiped out wrestler by wrestler by wrestler on his way to the top of the FWO. The fans loved it, much to his surprise. The Dome was jammed packed every night just to see Nightmare take on another super star. Nightmare as on a winning streak, standing invincible at 45-0. In the FWO, no-one could tame the Nightmare. At twenty-one Nightmare was still in the FWO. He had a record of 89-2. Both of those two matches were lost against his cousin, now the "Hurricane." But Hurricane wasn't better record-wise than Nightmare, standing at 76-9. All of those nine losses came from Nightmare. Those two were undefeatable except against each other. Nightmare and Hurricane went on to be tag team champs when they tagged up with each other. They had a historic 34-0 record in the FWO. At age twenty-two, Nightmare had his big appearance on MTV. He was able to do some guest announcing over the span of one week. It was certainly one of the biggest accomplishments of Nightmare's career. Nightmare got to meet such singers and artist like Busta Rhymes, Celine Dion, Will Smith, Snoop Doggy Dogg, Janet Jackson and many others. He also appeared on Saturday Night Live. Nightmare got to meet Chris Farley before he passed away. Some speculate that Nightmare killed him, but he denies it. At age twenty-three, Nightmare was still hitting the publicity circuit, he was on every wrestling show you can name. He wasn't just some high school kick out. He was a somebody. After the FWO he was on to bigger wrestling companies. He went on to train with WCW. He trained with many wrestlers such as Sting, "Hollywood" Hogan, Bret Hart and many more. Nightmare was the first wrestler to tame Bill Goldberg. He stopped Bill right in his tracks with a new move. Some interested parties asked questions on what the new move was and Nightmare replied "It's the Pain Killer" Nightmare's new finisher was called the Pain Killer. The word went around really fast and Bill Goldberg was humiliated. Nightmare found a girlfriend at age twenty-four, her name was Samantha Thihman. Nightmare found her over at a swimsuit-ad shoot. Nightmare and Samantha were instantly in love ,and they were married within 2 months. They were soon divorced, after Samantha said that she couldn't live by herself all of the time while Nightmare was out wrestling. After the split, Nightmare was so depressed that he went on to a 0-15 record because he couldn't focus on wrestling. People said that Nightmare was done. Nightmare made this landmark statement: "I'm not done. No, I'm just beginning." When Nightmare was twenty-five, his life was increasing when he found a new love. Her name was Elisa Barnum. She was twenty-four and she was from Houston Texas. She and Jeff were perfect together. Nightmare was happier then he was in years. Jeff and Elisa had two children. A boy and a girl. The boys name is Joe Wells and the girls name was Durae Wells. Nightmare bought everything for them and let them do whatever they want. He made that he wasn't like his parents and ignore his children. Joe Wells is just like his father. He had a bad attitude. And Durae was more like her mother. She was sensitive and kind. She never cried but laughed all of the times. They were the perfect family. Nightmare spent more time at home instead of the ring. At age 26, Nightmare's life began to change once again, news going out that Nightmare was losing his touch, and that he wouldn't last two more years. But Nightmare quit working with WCW and went on to harder federations. Nightmare and Hurricane found the National Wrestling Council. It wasn't just a lone fed. It was the ultimate fed and that's were Nightmare belongs. Hurricane joined the VCW and Nightmare joined the MWA in search of champions and that's were he found them. He was with greats such as Mr. Sinister, "V.I.P" Eddie Zam, Big Hurt, White Tiger and many others. He started off good against Avalanche and ended up with a 2-0 start. But it slowly decreased to 2-2. Nightmare was once again looking for a tag partner when he ran across the right man for the job. Vulcan was a great wrestler in the MWA but they were soon broke apart when Vulcan left the MWA in search of other federations. Vulcan was a great tag partner and will be remembered with Nightmare for a long time. Nightmare turned into a champion right away when 911 (his boss) let him have a chance at the PPV. 911 is one of the best wrestlers of all time. Another great friend in the NWC is [name withheld], the Executive Director. The gigantic interpromotional PPV was called "Across Enemy Lines." Nightmare had a chance to show the NWC that he was a champion, in the Hurricane Cup. He came into that event all set, but ran into one problem. Dial0Kid was more ready, that night. Nightmare lost in the first round when Avalon Britton hit him with a foreign object and got the 1...2...3! He wouldn't let the loss on PPV discourage him, though. Nightmare then had a chance at the brand new belt. The Ozark Mountain Belt. Nightmare was placed into the tournament and was challenged by "V.I.P" Eddie Zam in the first round. Nightmare was up to the challenge and defeated the champion to go on to the next round. Nightmare has had many victories in his career but none as good as this one. Nightmare will have many more challenges to come. But, for now, he's looking for a tag team partner. He's has the talent but he needs the brains behind the operation. Nightmare is certainly one of the most talented wrestlers in tje MWA's history.. yett can he make it with the whole NWC? "No Problem," says the Nightmare himself.
