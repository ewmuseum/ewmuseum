National Wrestling Council — Recruitment

"Let Me Ask You Some Questions..."

22 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you really any good? I'd like to know. Read on. Visiting Detroit? Try to compete with men like the NWC World Tag Team Champions, th Long Riders, "Mr. MCW" Mike Rogers, Motor City Champion The Dread Saxon, The Royal One, Michigan-National Champ Dark Angel, or "Smooth" Stevie Blair. Visiting Missouri? I dare you to step in the ring with the FORCE that is the MWA Champion "Sinister." Experience the pain of Masked Mystiq and B.A.D. Blood together, try to hang with the man who fears no-one, Ed Extreme. How about the explosive fury of "Dynamite" Corey Dunn? Or the man, 911 himself? Care to put it on the line on the card known simply as "Genocide." Can YOU handle it? Visiting Canada? Lock up with the NWC World Champion, Soul Shadow. Try their Canadian Heavyweight Champ, Eric Rage.. lace up the boots and stare at the immortal "Iron Man" Tex Steel, the legend himself. Take on the cunning duo of "Terrible" Jay Turner and the Scholar, the True Pimps, Stampede Champion "The Man with Class" Ron Lauren, or "Hurricane" Andrew Pierce. Wanna see writing on the wall, literally? Take a look at "Psycho" Sam Leever, or the phenomenon known to the wrestling world as... Nightstalker. Visiting New York? In the BAWF, you'll find their champion, "Bullseye" Shawn Arrows to be more than a handful. Or what about Manhatan Champion, rookie sensation Jason Jinx? Or tag champs the Masters of Disaster, Creed and Stephen Douglas? Match wits with the inimitable 911 or "Legend" Lyle Lorens on the boards. Try to solve the mystery of the Schiano family's return to wrestling. Witness the bitter and gruesome feud between Spellbinder and Sephiroth. Let yourself go, Big Apple style. Visiting Dallas? Just TRY to top the tremendous talent in Lone Star Wrestling. Walking in the door you'll immediately take notice of their tag team champions, the legendary Desperados, "Real Deal" Randy Neil and "Outlaw" John McKoy. From hot newcomers like Samuel Ten Bears and aging ex-champ "Playboy" Rick Rose, to stars like "Excellence in Ebony" Avalon Britton and the LSW Champion himself, the Thunder from Down Under, Kiwi Justice. The powerful JackKnife and the enigmatic GodFather add more intrigue to the proceedings, and more talent is being signed day by day... You're welcome to see just how good you are. Take a look in the mirror. Got a really big ego, I'd guess? Most of us do. But those who have fought through the now 165 members of the infamous National Wrestling Council have EARNED that ego. Can you say you've been World Champion of a league with that many folks? Or are you afraid to really test yourself? Think you're better than something like this? If so, I call you a coward. If not, I openly invite you to put your old character, or preferably a new one on the line in one of our prospering regions. Visit the NWC Website: http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/nwc.html Take the tour, read the rules, read the latest Pay-Per-View, cruise the regions... and there will be no doubt left in your mind. The NWC is the place for you to join wrestling's elite. Period. [name withheld] NWC Staff Reply to: [detail withheld]
