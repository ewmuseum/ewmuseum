National Wrestling Council — News

"A Landmark Week"

11 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings, wrestlers, on behalf of the entire NWC staff. I apologize in advance for this rare NWC-wide mailing, yet I feel it is necessary to express to you the numerous events taking place in our great organization in the near future! MCW's Motor City Madness this week will feature a gigantic lineup of matches, including the Royal One putting his Michigan-National Title on the line against the unstoppable WAR PIG! But this Sunday, the gigantic "CoboSlam" event will be taking place in the MCW! It will all be headlined by a HUGE NWC World Title Match, pitting former teammates against each other in a cross-promotional match. MCW puts fan-favorite Dread Saxon up against reigning World Champion, from VCW, Soul Shadow! Mr. Sinister broke up the friendly contest Soul and Dread had in Ring 2 at "A New Era," dumping Saxon over the top rope. Now, these two superstars have expressed their respect for each other in statements across the country, and it is time to settle the score in what will certainly be the biggest NWC Level Title Match to date. Don't miss it... and check out the MCW website for more detailed info! Moving on.. the debut of the new NWC region run by veteran league-head [name withheld], the VCW, has been met with much praise and accord. [name withheld] has managed to retain almost 100% of the overwhelming talent pool that the CCW acquired in its existence, and has put it to good use thus far. Their first show will be a Supercard titled "IN YOUR FACE", and that name symbolizes exactly what [name withheld] and his 'Virginia Boys' will give the NWC fans who tune in to the VCW: in your face action! Moving even further on...in David Cooper's prestigious Lone Star Wrestling promotion, a HUGE Friday Warfare card is planned... and the NWC World Tag Team Titles will be on the line! The newly crowned champions, MCW's fearsome duo of Armaggedon will clash with the LSW's top tandem, the crafty Atlantic City Connection. If for no other reason, you should check out that match for the incredible clash in wrestling styles.. it promises to be a historic bout. Is that quite enough? No! The BAWF, the self-proclaimed core of the NWC, is about to break down the house with their first pay-per-view event, entitled "Terror in Times Square!" On this monumental card, the phenom of the BAWF, Jason Jinx, will defend his prized Manhattan Title against Spellbinder of the Bloodclan group! Lots of hype and rumors surround the TV Title Match, which promises some high-flying action! 911 will defend against Keith Schiano. And..you won't want to miss this one folks, as outspoken BAWF Champion Shawn 'Bullseye' Arrows defends in a brutal ladder match against his arch-enemy, the man who swallowed him from the Georgia Dome at the first NWC PPV, Sephiroth! On the NWC site itself, this week is ground-breaking as we launch our newest effort, the "KNWC Radio" RealAudio program! It will be hosted by [name withheld], with comments and clips from voices all over the NWC. Do you want to be heard on "KNWC Radio"? Send either a .wav file with your voice, or a short written statement to [detail withheld] , and it WILL appear on this Friday's debut broadcast! Everything under the NWC sun will be discussed on this program! Am I done yet? No... because, looming in the background is the next National Wrestling Council Interpromotional Pay-Per-View, called "Across Enemy Lines." Preliminary Information is already up on the NWC site, with multiple graphics for the event, and the cities that might host this event. The regions have tons of great angles and heated feuds going on, and making it to this card could prove even harder than getting a spot to go to "A New Era!" Now, I will talk about the exposure we've gotten over the internet recently, as the Fantasy Wrestling Illustrated ( http://fwi.hypermart.net ) magazine has, in its opinion columns, several articles that mention the NWC in high accord. You'll want to visit their site in due haste and scope out what the e-wrestling populace at-large thinks about the NWC! That's it for this update about the upcoming landmark week in the National Wrestling Council. Now is absolutely the best time to be involved with this organization, and I am thrilled to be associated with each and every one of you. Let's keep up the marvelous work, and keep recruiting new guys in here to take part in the NWC's true roleplaying experience! Best wishes, [name withheld] NWC Executive Director [detail withheld]
