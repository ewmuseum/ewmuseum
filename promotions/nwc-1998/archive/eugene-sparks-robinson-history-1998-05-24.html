"Eugene 'Sparks' Robinson's History"

24 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/ Eugene 'Sparks' Robinson -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ Eugene "Sparks" Robinson was born in 1970 in a small town in Minneapolis, Minnesota. He lived with your standard family with three older brothers. He developed a temper at a young age, due to the fact that his brothers picked on him a lot, because he was smaller and younger than them. This anger was maintained for Eugene’s whole life, to this day. He took up shootfighting at the young age of 9 and was considered to be a true prospect in the future. However, after three years of shootfighting he decided it wasn’t for him, and he wanted to wrestle professionally. Eugene started to wrestle in a small gym at the age of twelve and was very small for his age. The coaches and trainers said he would never make it but his will power kept him going on. He was forced into becoming a technical wrestler because of his side and developed a wide variety of moves at a very young age. He entering local contests and a state tournament, where he came in second to a young boy named Alexander Isaac. Isaac truly was the old school type wrestler, but his cockiness brought him to new heights. Eugene took a quick disliking to Alex Isaac, when the two met in the finals of the Minnesota Youth Wrestling circuit Alex bad mouthed Eugene for a week leading up to the match. Eugene was kind of a shy kid, but still won over the Minnesota fans. During the match, Eugene was about to put on his finishing move (at the time a figure four leglock), but Isaac used his free leg and landed a low blow, Eugene went down extremely hard and the referee missed the whole incident. Isaac went onto win it with a bridging waist lock suplex. Eugene and Isaac had started a feud that would build further than anyone could imagine. Eugene entered Madison High School at the age of 14 in 1974. He was immediately asked to join the wrestling team. Eugene put on a show at tryouts, he made the Varsity team for the 120 pound division. He became the fist freshmen to ever make the varsity wrestling squad. Eugene seemed a little uptight in his first pre-season match as he lost to Jeremy Birmington of Montevideo High School. Eugene took that match as a learning lesson and finished pre-season with a record of 6-1. Next up was league, Eugene had all ready been building a reputation, he rolled through the first eight league matches until he tied Peter Van Wallen of rival school Dawson High School. Eugene finished 15-2 overall and 9-1 in league. He qualified for the state championships. Eugene was overlooked by the judging committee because of his age and received a poor ranking. He pulled of a huge upset in the first round. In the second round he met a familiar nemesis, Alexander Isaac. Again, Isaac got the best of Eugene. Isaac boasted and bragged and told the reporters that Eugene was simply a pushover. Isaac lost the next round to Warren Brown of Goodridge High School, who went on to win the 120-pound weight division for the state of Minnesota. Eugene continued with his education and seemed to catch the attention of his class mates, one young beautiful girl in particular, Evelyn Jones. A gorgeous woman, she had long brown hair and a tan that looked like she hailed from the sunny state of California. Eugene fell deeply in love, and the two went out from that day on all the way through High School. Eugene finished his freshman year training for the next, he had gained about 25 pounds and grown three inches. He was now standing 5’9" instead of a runt at 5'6". Eugene had a wild summer and his growth spurts continued, he was now weighing in at 165 pounds and standing an incredible 6 feet tall. Nobody knew what to think, and when Eugene went back to High School, the coaches dropped down in astonishment. Eugene had already lettered as a freshmen, and was practically crowned up to the 165 weight class. However, the person whose spot he took didn’t seem to like him, his name is Ryan Allen. Allen was a 6'1" 170-pound God in High School, but the coaches told him he couldn't compare to Eugene, and he was booted from his spot. He was forced to gain ten pounds so he could qualify for the next weight class, and now he was bigger than ever. Allen held a silent grudge against Eugene and Robinson didn’t even have a clue! After an undefeated pre-season of 7-0, Eugene rolled right through the league. He was up against people he had never met before, and because of his dramatic growth spurts, they didn’t know what to expect. Eugene put on a great performance and turned quite a few heads. Eugene finished the season an incredible 17-0 and he was first wrestler from Madison to ever remain undefeated during the regular season. Eugene eased his way through the first three rounds of the state championships, he then was shocked as he met up with none other than Alexander Isaac in the semi-finals. Eugene thought if Isaac would ever see him again he would pass out of fear, but Eugene couldn’t believe that he was facing a new Isaac, a 6 foot 2, 177 pound Isaac, who just made the mark for this division. Eugene started out hot in their match scoring the first five points and was on his way to victory, but, Eugene’s luck ran out with ten seconds in the match as Isaac pinned him with his waistlock slam into a pin. Eugene was in udder horror and was depressed. Eugene went back to school and seemed to be a totally different person, he was skipping classes, disobeying teachers, he was always a good kid, but Isaac brought out the worst in him. His girlfriend dumped him, because she was "sick of him walking around with his head hanging low." Two weeks later, she was dating someone else, Ryan Allen. Eugene was extremely angered and took it personal. He confronted Allen and Allen pushed him into the ground. Eugene got up and attacked Allen, they rolled all over the floors of Madison High School knocking over trash cans, other students, and anything that got in their way. They kept it clean using wrestling holds and nobody dared to step between them. Finally, seven teachers broke them up and when Eugene was tripped by Isaac after they got up, a pocket knife fell out of his back pocket. He was expelled from school with no questions asked. Eugene failed to meet the requirements to get back into school his junior year, which meant no more wrestling, Eugene had grown to full size, 6 foot 3 and he weighed an incredible 225 pounds. Eugene kept to himself that year, not really getting out much, he found out one day that Allen and his old girlfriend broke up and he arrived at her door. Evelyn had not seen him in four months, and when Eugene showed up at her door, she fell in love all over again. The two were somehow linked together. Eugene creeped onto campus from time to time to be with his love, but he stopped because if he were caught, he would be at home school his senior year. Eugene mourned for the rest of the year but when it came to the school board, he was accepted back into Madison High School. Eugene had Evelyn in his arms and vengeance in his blood. He made the 175+ weight division which was as high as it went, and who did he knock off for the position? None other than Ryan Allen. The decision was reached when they had a match, Eugene was fired up and mopped the floor with Allen scoring seventeen points before Eugene decided to pin him. Scholarships were knocking at Eugene’s door left and right: St. Paul, Minnesota State, Michigan, UCLA, USC, Arizona, Oregon, and all around the United States Eugene was being offered scholarships. Eugene had another undefeated season and brought the Madison Tigers back to their winning ways after a fourth place finish last year, when Eugene wasn’t on the team. Eugene crushed his opponents in the state tournament, he had one goal, to beat Alexander Isaac. Eugene received this chance, ironically, in the state finals. Eugene didn’t even care if he won, he just wanted to hurt Isaac, and that’s exactly what he did, he outweighed Isaac for the first time in his life, and it was by thirty pounds, Eugene won the state title and was living a fantasy life. His senior year came to an end and the big senior trip was planned for the Bahamas but Eugene didn’t want to go. Instead, he persuaded his parents to let him go to Africa on a tourist's journey of life. After weeks and weeks of arguing, Eugene left the United State for a two week trip. These two weeks became the next ten years of his life. Eugene Robinson met a tour guide named Raymond Ollinger. Raymond showed Eugene the sights for the first week, and Eugene was thrilled with every new adventure. He confessed to Raymond that he wanted more, so Raymond told him about a small village in central Africa called the Wariri Tribe. Eugene was fascinated by the stories and didn't care about the return trip, he wanted some excitement. Eugene was taken on a week and half long journey of hiking, swimming, climbing mountains, and recreational running from safari animals. But, when he got there he felt like he was in heaven. It wasn’t the riches, the jewels, or fine women that he saw, but he saw freedom to do what ever he wanted, with absolutely no worries. Eugene spent a month there getting familiar with the cultures of this new life, he wrote a letter to his family and friends explaining what he was doing, and how he was going to live, he didn’t leave a return address so no-one could find him. After about a year, Eugene seemed to be growing tired of the Wariri tribe, so, he moved on out packing a pillow, a blanket, and one change of clothes. With all that, he was off to seek a new life. Eugene Robinson met many new people, and one thing everyone wants to know, is where did he get the nickname "Sparks?" A week after leaving the Wariris, Eugene found himself in a bit of trouble with a native African. Eugene had taken a bath in a river, not knowing it was property of one Itelo Marrada. Eugene was fiercely hunted down by the villagers and after running for three and a half hours in circles, he was worn out. The natives couldn’t believe his speed and endurance, but nevertheless, they had to kill him. Eugene was tied with a bull rope to a tree and had a ring of fire surrounding him. What the villagers didn’t know was that Eugene was an expert on untying knots, and he was able to wiggle one hand free. Once he got his body loose he kicked a rock into the flames and sparks went flying everywhere, Eugene took off running, he ran into the forest and ran straight off a cliff! He only fell about ten feet because his shirt got caught on a an old branch hanging over the side, just as the shirt ripped Eugene was able to grab hold of the branch and pull himself! The natives captured him again but let him go, as he was a legend, they now knew him as "Sparks". Eugene decided to stay with this new tribe, the Watanabes. Eugene learned many things in his stay there. He battled with himself, his inner demons, for over four years. He learned the strengths and weaknesses of life itself. He learned how to control his anger and hold it inside. Some of his most notable accomplishments while he was there were his battles and victories over self-confidence, will-power, mental and physical strength, and the ability to act out this emotions to a full content. If you look at Eugene it is almost impossible to tell what he’s thinking, he doesn’t show much emotion. As previously mentioned, Eugene saves all his anger and keep it to himself. But one time, after being in Africa for five years, he almost snapped. A man took from him one of his most prized possessions: a small cloth, it has two parts to it, one is from the shirt that got caught on the branch when he almost died after running from the natives, and it’s sewn onto another piece of cloth, this part was from a gift of Evelyn, it is his last memory of her and he never talks about it. He keeps it tied around his right arm, but he takes it off from time to time. One day it got wet after some heavy rain and when the sun came out he put out to dry. A native thought he found it and used it blow his nose, Eugene came out and snapped. He grabbed a spear and held it to the natives throat, it took eight other men to contain him and keep him down, they said one day they wouldn’t be around when Eugene finally snapped, and they don’t plan on being part of it because they fear their lives. Eugene was then kicked out of the Watanabe tribe and he spent the next three years traveling from city to city and meeting new people. He felt like he had finally found himself, but he still didn’t want to go home. One day a government official approached him with an important letter. He opened it and the message implied that Eugene should "become a citizen of our land or go home." With that in mind, Eugene filed to become a South African citizen. He passed and he belongs to Africa now. Two exciting years later, he caught a glimpse of the most beautiful woman he thought he had ever seen. She turned around, and it was Evelyn Jones, she had come to take him home. Evelyn tried and tried to convince Eugene to come home, but he wouldn’t budge, she finally broke him down by getting him to come home for one week. Eugene and Evelyn took the first plane back and they landed in Dallas, Texas. Evelyn now lived in Texas by herself. Eugene and her caught up on old times and Eugene told her many stories of his African journeys. On the next to last day, Evelyn told Eugene to meet her somewhere and dress casual, he went to the meeting place and when he realized where he was, he was standing at the front door of the Lone Star Wrestling Headquarters. There was a big sign on the door that read, "Now hiring new talent". Eugene was persuaded by Evelyn who showed up behind him to tryout "just for fun," actually hoping he would make it and stay. Eugene stunned the competition, nobody had ever heard of him before, he was unknown to all the recruiters: Commissioner David Cooper didn’t hesitate to hire Eugene. Eugene started off rusty but was able to outlast a man named Virus after he hit one of his many patented moves, the "Chancery Grip" (a modified Cross face chicken wing). Eugene still wasn’t into the swing of things, as he met an uprising Canadian Star named Samuel Ten Bears. After a long and grueling battle Samuel Ten Bears pulled of the win after doing something that is rarely done, making Eugene submit. Eugene finally felt he was ready to take it back to the beginning, he started utilizing his technical background and his new interself, with this he rolled on to win his next 3 matches with wins over Tiziano, "Outlaw" John McCoy, and Mr. Frankenstein. Eugene then was to have a match at his first PPV, "Dallas Destruction" against Cole Young. However, Young’s contract was terminated a few days before the PPV and Eugene decided to take a trip out to St. Louis, Missouri. It was here he met with his first rival in the National Wrestling Council. A man by the name of Big Hurt. Eugene came out through the crowd and distracted Big Hurt as he was pinned by the "Natural" Sam Hansen. Eugene was then given another week off so he decided to show up for the re-match. In a steel cage Big Hurt and Hansen were set to meet again. The match was running smooth until a few MWA stars decided to show up. Eugene left the color commentary booth and found his way into getting involved in the match. He and a few others forced the two men to wrestle to a no contest. Eugene was set to face Big Hurt at "Across Enemy Lines," the second National Wrestling Council PPV. But, before that he had a rematch with Samuel Ten Bears, this time for the LSW Regional Title. With a lot of courage, strength, and determination, Eugene "Sparks" Robinson became the new LSW Regional Champion after he beat Samuel Ten Bears. A week later, Eugene lost to the man Samuel Ten Bears beat for the title, "Excellence in Ebony" Avalon Britton. When Eugene thought things couldn’t possibly get worse, Big Hurt and his new tag-team partner Eddie Zam won the NWC World Tag-Team Championship. Therefore, they would have to defend them at the AEL PPV. Eugene’s match was dropped, but he faced White Tiger instead. In an epic battle with nothing but respect on the line, Eugene was able to come out on top. Since this match, Eugene has taken a backseat for three weeks training for his next match, the Dangerous Destiny Match. It is a tournament of all sorts of different stipulations, to decide the LSW’s first Television Champion. Why would Eugene lower his standards to a television championship after winning the Regional Championship? Basically, he wants to become the first triple crown athlete of the LSW, and the television title is on that list. So, at "Face-off at High Noon," it will be interesting to see how Eugene "Sparks" Robinson fares against the rest of the pack.
