NWC Strikes Again!

25 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSPWF.. I'm here representing the National Wrestling Council once again to bring you one of the high quality cards that we see each week in the NWC. This weeks feature card was one of the most exciting and highly anticipated matches in our long and storied history. For those of you who have not followed the NWC, we have been around the e-wrestling world for more than a year now, and with us from the very start was the federation out of Michigan, Motor City Wrestling. This super card, known from the earliest days of the federation is known as "Coboslam" hosted at the historic cobo hall in downtown Detroit. For many of the NWC'ers the arrival of this card was filled with a bitter sweet sentiment. The MCW's fine commissioner [name withheld] announced in the weeks prior to this card that this huge attraction would be the final card before the federation closed it's doors forever. [name withheld] contributed remarkable amounts to the growth and advancement of the NWC and the e-wrestling community in general. His innovative ideas and top of the line federation will be truly missed. The wrestlers worked with all that they had in order to put on such a great show for this final card. After this event went down they all prepared for the move to their new federations inside the MCW. The Coboslam tradition was one that continued for over 14 memorable cards.. But for the first time on the RSPWF boards, courtesy of the National Wrestling Council, we will present to you COBOSLAM XIV!! If you like this sample of what you see, don't sell yourself short.. check out the whole thing, with a pre-cobo card put on by the Missouri Wrestling Alliance, and all the matches in their entirety. Like what you've seen so far? Want to know more about the NWC's past present and future? Drop by our own website. Be sure to check out the comprehensive title history site, along with the many other options that we offer. And don't forget to drop by our sponsors to learn everything you'll ever need about the wrestling world, both real and fantasy. Sit back, relax, and enjoy a piece of NWC history. [name withheld] Director of Publicity National Wrestling Council
