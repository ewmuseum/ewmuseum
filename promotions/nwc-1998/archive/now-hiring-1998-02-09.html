National Wrestling Council — Recruitment

"Now Hiring! Read to Find Out Why We're the Best!"

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you tired of single-minded feds, run either by an indifferent simulator that doesn't reward role-playing or an egotistical fed head who keeps you down just because he doesn't like you? Are you sick of being in stuck in every fly-by-night "Extreme", "Ultimate", or "Hardcore" fed, and are looking for a truly unique wrestling experience? Are you an old-school wrestling fan who is just looking for an incredibly entertaining, and always exciting e-wrestling association? All your expectations are met, and more, in the National Wrestling Council! We are quickly becoming the most innovative, and most popular e-wrestling organization on the internet! Praised on sites such as Fantasy Wrestling Illustrated and the NAWA, we have more than enough proof that we are where it's at when it come to pure, e-wrestling entertainment! What can you look forward to when you come to NWC? * Diversity - We are the internet's answer to the NWA of old. Five regional e-wrestling promotions, all contained under the NWC banner, have a collection of over 160 of the best, brightest, and certainly most creative e-wrestling superstars you can find anywhere. The Big Apple Wrestling Federation, Virginia Championship Wrestling, Motor City Wrestling, the Missouri Wrestling Alliance, and Lone Star Wrestling each offer a different and unique atmosphere unlike that of any other e-wrestling promotion. Once every two months, these five federations clash in a PPV extravaganza that is already being called one of the best e-wrestling events! *Creativity - No other promotion is quite like the NWC. Each federation under the banner offers a different opportunity to make new characters, expand on old ones, and make them into some of the most interesting and innovative e-wrestlers around. We feel it's important to foster creativity, and the more unique the character, the better the chances of winning! *Fairness - Tired of illerate idiots who post roleplays like "Im GoNnA KiK uR AzZ!" and still continually win? So are we! If you're a good roleplayer, we want you to run to the NWC, because we can guarantee you will a big gun in our federations! No biased fed-heads, either! All of our comissioners are probably the fairest and most unbiased leaders in the e-wrestling world. We want to keep your patronage, so why would we make you lose? These are but a few of the things that makes the National Wrestling Council, in my opinion, the best e-wrestling fed on the planet. If you really want to find out about the NWC, you're just going to have to go to http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/nwc.html and find out for yourself!
