National Wrestling Council — Wrestler History

"Raymond Pierce's History"

24 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/ "A Glimpse of Perfection" The Tale of Raymond Pierce Raymond Pierce was born on January 9th, 1971, the first child of a rich Orlando, Florida couple. Pierce was an exceptional athlete, excelling in anything he tried. He had a bright future in football and baseball and the possibility of him getting a scholarship for basketball was there as well. As such, Pierce was pampered by his coaches and treated like a god by his friends. The special treatment instilled in him a feeling of superiority. Pierce eventually went to Florida State on a football scholarship and enjoyed two mediocre seasons. The talent was there, but the desire to use it was lacking. After his sophomore season, Pierce quit the team. He was still very influencial at the school, though, as he worked his way up to president of the entire Greek System. The feeling of being the top dog in an elitist system did nothing to shrink his already swelled head. At about this time, Ray's little brother, Andrew Pierce, was making big waves throughout Florida with his tremendous wrestling ability. Two times named Florida's Wrestler of the Year, talk soon started about little brother being the better of the two athletes. This angered Ray immensely. Later on, Andrew would go to and be kicked out of Florida State, Miami and Florida for his attitude. After college, Ray had nothing left to do. His family was wealthy enough that none of its members would ever have to work again and Pierce had no intention of doing so. Finally, he decided to take up bodybuilding. Pierce competed in bodybuilding for about a year and enjoyed minimal success. Soon, though, his interests drifted to various types of one-on-one combat. He tried different types of martial arts, but it was an article about Frank Atlas' historic Dungeon that really got his interest. Ray realized that this would give him the challenge he desired as well as the chance to prove to his family and peers that he was indeed better than Andrew by excelling in his brother's sport of choice. Pierce decided against traveling to Atlas' Dungeon and instead hired various professional wrestlers, past and present, to train him. Pierce picked it up quickly and once again had people telling him how great he was. Pierce's ego seemingly enlarged to unheard of sizes as he fired all of his trainers, claiming none of them were as good as he was and were not worthy enough to train him. Pierce used his father's business connections to get him an interview with the hierarchy of the Grandstand Wrestling Alliance. He walked through the door wearing a t-shirt that said "I am perfect. You are not." His interview was shaky with Pierce demanding superstar type money despite never wrestling. Finally, though, a contract was signed and for the first time, the name "Perfect" Raymond Pierce was used. Pierce won his first match in a come from behind manner, debuting the "Flawless Flip" finisher. Shortly after, Pierce "allowed" NukeDust to be his tag team parter. NukeDust entered the ring and dominated their opponents. Finally, Pierce tagged in, did the Flawless Flip and got the three count. This was the first time Pierce manipulated a colleague into doing the dirty work for him as he stole the spotlight. This would become a trademark of his down the road. A couple of weeks later, the calender year of the GWA ended. One of the stipulations of the contract Pierce signed stated that either side could void the last two years. The GWA put it in to protect themselves in case Pierce bombed. The thought of Pierce using it never crossed their minds since he had no other options. Pierce, however, opted out of his contract. He claimed that there was no challenge left for him in the GWA where he was, technically, undefeated at 1-0 in singles, 1-0 in tag. Soon after, the up and coming Cyber Wrestling Federation signed Pierce to a lucrative deal. Pierce was a perfect fit for the rebelious league. He walked in the first day and talked about his "flawlessness" and soon drew the ire of CWF World Champion, Ronny Smith. Pierce drew in some more attention as well. CWF executive, [name withheld], was planning to start his own stable of wrestlers and offered Pierce a spot. Pierce secretly accepted and a plan went into effect to get Pierce a World Title shot in his first CWF match at the new CWF's big event, Clubbamania. If Pierce's title shot was shady, his entrance made favoritism seem all the more obvious. For the first time, Pierce came out to "O Verona" and donned one of his very expensive sequence robes. However, instead of walking down the aisle, Pierce was lowered to the ring in a Lambourgini. Pierce went on to win the CWF World Title in his first match (with a lot of help) and was revealed shortly after to be the new member of the Schuk Foundation. It was after the win that Ray Pierce actually came to believe that he was perfect. Before, his ego was incredibly large and he felt superior to almost everyone. However, the combination of an undefeated career record and a World Title win pushed him past the point of large ego into the realm of believing his own hype. Pierce defended the title successfully once and then had a big rematch with Ronny Smith. Pierce defeated Smith in a steel cage match, but one hour and thirty minutes later, the ruling was overturned and the match was restarted. As Pierce complained to the ref, Smith came from behind and sent him into the cage, rolling him up for the three count. With his undefeated streak ended and his World Title lost, Pierce came to believe that there was a conspiracy against him. As the self-proclaimed leader of the Schuk Foundation, he decided to bring in some protection. Shortly after, wrestling's legendary big man, the Mighty Lothar, showed up as Pierce's personal bodyguard and tag partner. In his first match, Lothar beat another legend, the 911 Kid, in under a minute. Pierce watched on as Lothar was claimed the next big thing. Jealousy started to form as Pierce couldn't understand why others didn't realize his perfection. Once again, [name withheld] used his power to get on of his Foundation members a title shot and the big man took advantage of it as Lothar pinned Ronny Smith to become the new champ. Pierce, already complaining constantly about being screwed, listened to the television in shock as Smith came on publically to make the same claims. Pierce showed up the next week and did an interview, professing that Smith had received special treatment and that Pierce was the one who was screwed. The lights went out and suddenly the Phenom appeared. He grabbed the mic and denounced Smith's and Pierce's claims, saying that he truly should be the World Champ. Soon, the three started brawling and a great feud began. After the World Title loss, Pierce became so caught up in proving his own perfection, his ability in the ring suffered. He became more and more cowardly as he didn't want to be proven inferior, or even an equal, in the ring. More and more he would hide behind one of his fellow Schuk Foundation members, making them do his dirty work. More often than not, Lothar would be the one doing it. The Phenom, Smith and Pierce met up at the second big supercard of the CWF and Pierce was the first man eliminated. That, coupled with his earlier loss, pushed Pierce over the edge. From that point on, he would never meet up with anyone in a one-on-one manner. Sneak attacks and outside interference were the only means Pierce would use. Soon after, [name withheld] left the CWF and took a job with the GWA. Pierce continued to lead the Schuk Foundation and soon gave it a major overhaul, insultingly dumping half of the members like he had done to the GWA and NukeDust earlier. Pierce received a couple of intriguing calls shortly after. The first was from his only real friend in the world, Sam Leever. Leever, through the help of Pierce, was getting out of jail early after spending ten years in a cell. Leever, who had a lifetime of mental problems, killed a woman a decade earlier in a violent frenzy. Leever was getting out and Pierce vowed to get him a job. The next call came from Ray's parents. Ray's little brother, Andrew, had just been kicked out of his third University and the two were worried about him. Finally, Ray agreed to help Andrew get a job in the CWF. Upon dumping the excess baggage in the Schuk Foundation, Ray announced the two newest members. "Psycho" Sam Leever and "Li'l" Andy Pierce were brought in. In an effort to make his brother look bad, Ray insulted him all the time and didn't allow him to talk, giving him the demeaning "Li'l" monicker. At this time, a man started appearing wearing a black mask. Fittingly, he called himself the Black Mask. A different man would appear each time and occasionally, more than one would come out at once. Though the whole thing was confusing, one thing was known...The Black Mask was after Ray Pierce. Pierce and Lothar were scheduled to take on the CWF World Tag Team champs at the time, but Pierce was attacked by the Black Mask. Lothar won the match two-on-one by disqualification, setting up a rematch the next week. In that match, the two walked away as World Tag Team Champions. The Black Mask would seemingly be a bane to all that Pierce tried to accomplish. The following week, Ray Pierce was humiliated in the center of the ring by an up and coming superstar named Eric Rage and his partner, Magnum SB. This tag team, the Regulators, were quickly becoming the hottest and most popular tag team in the world and they pulled off their first major win, taking the World Tag Titles from Pierce and Lothar. Lothar, however, was mysteriously absent from the match and the Black Mask claimed to have taken him out. More mystery would be added to the Black Mask saga as another man appeared, donning a red mask. The Red Mask was very philosophical in his interviews and claimed to understand the Black Mask's real motive. The Red Mask claimed that the Black Mask's evil would be stopped. Ray Pierce, using the same cowardly actions as always, demanded that the Black Mask go through his little brother to get to him. The next night, Andy Pierce was found unconscious with a black mask laying beside him. Pierce then demanded that the Mask go through Leever. Once again, Pierce's plan backfired as the "Psycho" appeared at the next show assaulted by the Mask. Finally, Pierce sent the big man after the Black Mask, claiming he had to go through the Mighty Lothar. All of this was watched by the Red Mask as the other mystery man calculated his plans. Finally, it was announced that Ray Pierce would meet the Black Mask at the following CWF Supercard. With minutes to go before the match, Pierce was "injured" and couldn't perform. Obviously, the cowardly display was an effort by Pierce to avoid the Black Mask. The CWF hierarchy demanded a replacement be found and Lothar stepped up to the plate. During the match, many Black Masks came out of the crowd and surrounded the ring. Lothar, as he had done all season, defeated the mystery man. He pulled off the mask and to the surprise of the entire world, Ray Pierce was under it. Lothar looked on in shock and then was assaulted by the crowbar-wielding Masks. Pierce grabbed a mic and taunted his former partner, showing the jealousy that was spawned months earlier. Pierce had three of the Black Masks unmask to reveal his new team, Ray Pierce's Elite. The members were Sam Leever, Pierce's enforcer. Andrew Pierce, Ray's lackey. Finally, the fourth member was another surprise. "The Man With Class" Ron Lauren, formerly a member of the Ringmasters, revealed himself to be Pierce's protege. The beating continued and then Pierce called into the ring the other men on the outside donning the black masks. He told these local wrestlers that he had lied to them about giving them a spot in the Elite and sent them back to the lockers. One man, however, didn't go. Ray Pierce ordered him to leave but still he didn't budge. Pierce laughed and ordered his enforcer to unmask the man and take him out. Pierce turned away as Leever pulled the black mask off. Underneath, to the surprise of many, was a red mask. Not just a red mask, but THE Red Mask. The Red Mask cleaned house, all the while Pierce thought it was his Elite giving the local kid a beat down. Pierce finally turned around and fell to his knees in terror as he saw the crimson mask. Slowly, the masked man reached up and pulled the mask off. The world again was on the edge of their seats as the legendary Jon Atlas revealed himself. Pierce rolled out of the ring and took off in fear. Halfway down the aisle, while looking back in the ring, he ran smack into the bodies of Eric Rage and Magnum SB. The Regulators, missing for weeks, returned to the aid of Atlas and Lothar. Pierce's embarassing attempt at destroying Lothar only made him into more of a liar as he claimed his plan an immense success. Soon after, he started feuding with Tod Tempest in a brutal series of barbed-wire matches. Pierce, who attempted to get out of every match, ended the feud victoriously as Tempest's career was ended by the violent matches. During the feud, a match occured that was a microcosm of Ray Pierce. A series of matches teaming hated rivals paired Tempest in Pierce. Their opponents, Aztex and Apache Blaze, used good teamwork for enemies, but Tempest refused to tag out. Pierce, however, was fine with that. In the end, all three men laid unconscious in the brutal battle. Pierce reached down and tagged his almost dead partner and made the cover for the victory, not once being touched. Pierce would pull a huge upset a few weeks later as he stunned Mr. Sinister and won his second CWF World Title. However, once again he would get "screwed" out of the belt. In the rematch, Pierce had Sinister pinned. Sinister's partner, Rockshade, would make the save and cause the match to continue. Rockshade interfered again, this time in front of the ref. The ref called for the bell, awarding the match to Pierce. After, though, he asked a weary Pierce if he'd like the match to continue. Pierce, completely out of it, agreed. Sinister made the easy cover and the belt was once again his. Another major occurance happened around this time in the story of Ray Pierce. The Black Mask mysteriously returned and was once again stalking Pierce. At first, Pierce thought it was a joke. Then, the new masked man appeared in the crowd during a match, causing Pierce to lose. This happened for weeks with no explanation. With the year heading towards its end, Pierce grew tired of his brother. The younger Pierce hadn't enjoyed the same level of infamy of his older brother, but was arguably the better of the two in the ring. Finally, Ray's jealousy broke him down and he had the Elite viciously assault his brother. This set up a match between the two at the year ending Supercard. The younger Pierce debuted the "Hurricane" Andrew persona and received a loud ovation as he was perceived as the underdog. That was a bad perception as he stepped into the ring and dominated his older, yet smaller, brother. Andrew won the match very impressively and watched on as the Black Mask hit the ring and attacked Ray. Leever ran into the ring to make the save, but instead started kicking Ray. The Black Mask unmasked as Ron Lauren and it was revealed to be an Andrew Pierce plot all along. Later that card, almost in tears, Ray Pierce quit the CWF. He denounced the league for screwing him over during his entire stay and it was apparent that he would not recover from the betrayal by his only friend and his only teammates. Ray Pierce disappeared for a while and then tried to restart his career in a few other leagues, most notably the NeCW. Despite the big name drawing power and his obvious potential, Pierce was turned down numerous times. Often, he was described as a cancer in the locker room, although the NeCW had given him another reason. They claimed that he was too similar to their main event wrestler and, thus, they wouldn't offer him a contract. Pierce, bruised ego and all, found himself in San Antonio, Texas when an acquaintance mentioned to him the LSW. He scheduled an interview with David Cooper, but heard the same response as he had been told in the many other leagues. He was bad for morale. After pleading, Cooper agreed under the condition that he help shore up the depleted tag ranks of the LSW. Pierce unwittingly agreed, but couldn't think of any man from his past he hadn't betrayed or been betrayed by. Cooper told him that he was having talks with a major talent and once the ink was signed, he would be informed as to who his new teammate would be. Pierce begrudgingly accepted the deal, and then went back to his old tricks. Pierce brought in old veteran, "Terrible" Jay Turner. Turner, last seen in the CCW as that region's tag champ, would be a perfect compliment to Pierce. In their first big match at LSW's "Faceoff at High Noon", Cooper came out and said that Turner was never meant to be Pierce's partner. Then, "Back in Black" came on and one of Pierce's old nemesises, Eric Rage, walked out from the back. Pierce took off in fear as Rage assaulted Turner. Rage was not the same man as the CWF version. The young rookie had developed into one of the world's best wrestlers, dominating the competition in the CCW during his entire stay. He had disappeared after the region folded and now appeared, 15 pounds of solid muscle heavier. Rage, a no-nonsense man, was the complete opposite of Ray Pierce. Add to that the fact that neither man liked the other and an interesting tag team was born. That leads up to today. Presently, Pierce has done his best to either control Rage or take him out. Attempts at each have failed miserably. Despite his actions, Pierce has enjoyed a successful career. His three world title reigns put him in the national spotlight and he does have an argument that each ended in extreme controversy. However, maybe the most impressive victory for the flamboyant star was in the CWF. For his entire stay, Pierce was consistently voted by the other wrestlers as the Best Spokesman for the league. That meant more than anything to the "Perfect One". There will be many more stories in the career of Ray Pierce. Will the man that once insulted the super-legend, Tex Steel, to his face ever hold the big gold again? Will the liar that claimed a three minute squash win over Terminator X despite never once meeting up with him overtake the competitor that DOES exist inside of him? Will the leadership qualities that brought both the Schuk Foundation and the Elite to prominance surface again? Or will the numerous enemies Pierce has made in his past, including NWC Executives [name withheld] and Ken Nemesis, lead to his ultimate demise? Only time will tell.
