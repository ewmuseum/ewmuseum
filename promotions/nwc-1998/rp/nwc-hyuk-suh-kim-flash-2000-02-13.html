National Wrestling Council — Roleplay archive

Hyuk Suh Kim, 13 February 2000

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Excuse the HTML. I'm much too lazy to remove all of it. A bit of background for all you anxious first-time readers. I've been running
Kim for a year, first in the NeWA (National e-Wrestling Alliance) and now in the NWC (National Wrestling Council). Kim himself is a 160 pound martial artist/submission expert without a personality to speak of.
He finished #19 on the RSPWF 200 last year. The following is a roleplay used against fading NWC legend Dread Saxon.
[<I>The scene comes up with a black screen. Over the darkness, a slight clattering of metal on metal is audible. A few seconds pass before a man begins whispering hoarsely, his exact words unclear. The following fades up in bold white lettering on the black backdrop.</I>]
<CENTER><b>"Whenever you find yourself on the side of the majority, it's time to pause and reflect."</b>
-Mark Twain</CENTER> [<I>The scene comes up now, a determined Hyuk Suh Kim with it. He stands, shirtless, with his black karate pants and both his hands and feet taped. It is his wrestling gear. His chest and hair are dampened and he stands in front of a simple red brick wall. He begins to speak quietly.</I>]
<b>Hyuk:</b> Saxon, Dread. The <I>great</I> Two Time NWC World Heavyweight champion. The man, the myth, the legend.
[<I>The young man pauses, his head down, his eyes up.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> Do you expect me to be in awe of your accomplishments, chump? Do you think you are deserving of my never-ending admiration for what you have done throughout your [<I>sounding a bit forced</I>] crappy career?
[<I>Kim's tone lowers. He is dead serious . . . or at least trying to be.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> I admire no one, especially someone with as poor of taste in music and cigarettes as yourself. [<I>awkwardly</I>] Moron.
[<I>His unwavering gaze is steady and unrelenting as Kim lets his seemingly out of character words sink in. A few heartbeats later, he sees fit to continue.</I>]
<b>Hyuk:</b> Do you really think that short stuff Baxter Vanin considers me a threat to Hunter Sabuani? Do you really think that, ugh, slut Madison considers me a threat to her "Peerless" . . . sex partner?
[<I>Kim's expression darkens noticeably.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> [<I>bitter</I>] Don't be such a fu*k. Try thinking about -
[<I>Sighing, Kim abruptly stops, his eyes rolling in pure disgust. He looks to the left of the camera and shakes his head back and forth in disapproval.</I>]
<b>Voice:</b> Cut! [<I>The scene cuts to a wider shot . . . and a studio. Several spotlights beat down on Kim from nearly every angle. The red brick behind him extends for only a few feet to either side of the original camera's view. Several technical hands run about, fixing this or that. Three large video cameras are set up at different angles around the center of the room. Mounds and mounds of
mechanical equipment which is used for God knows what surround the set. All in all, the room has a plastic and artificial feel to it.</I>] [<I>And at the center of this mechanical hell stands an angered Hyuk Suh Kim.</I>] [<I>The scene cuts in closer to a short, balding, older gentleman approaching
Kim with a clipboard in hand. He, also, looks pretty damn upset.</I>]
<b>Man:</b> What's the problem now? <b>Hyuk:</b> [<I>impatient</I>] I'm wasting my time. <b>Man:</b> No, you're wasting <I>my</I> time. And everyone else's with it. Just read the blasted cue cards. We wrote out your <I>whole</I> damn promo for you. Even you can't mess this up.
[<I>From off screen, a younger man runs up to the speaker. He, too, carries a clipboard.</I>]
<b>Kid:</b> Hey, Justin? Madison just called. She wants this promo in her hands ten minutes ago.
<b>Justin:</b> [<I>scratching what little hair he has on the top of his head</I>] See, I knew it! Did you hear that, Kim?
<b>Hyuk:</b> I do not understand why I cannot just say what I want to say. Or say nothing at all.
<b>Justin:</b> Because what you want to say is boring! <I>Very</I> boring! Gosh, all you have to do is throw in some witty insults, some offhand out of character references, a few swear words, and <I>BANG</I>! You're GCW material.
Haven't you seen Dread's promos? Chalked full of all that good stuff. <b>Hyuk:</b> And you want me to be exciting. <b>Justin:</b> Hell, kid, I'm no miracle worker. I just want you to be watchable. Let me tell you the cold, hard truth here, you're a terrible mic worker. <I>Terrible.</I> And, in this day and age, no matter how great you are in the ring, you're not gonna get to the top without some ability with the stick.
<b>Hyuk:</b> The stick? <b>Justin:</b> Yeah. The stick, the microphone. <b>Hyuk:</b> [<I>flat</I>] I am not doing this. [<I>And he turns, pacing away steadily. Justin looks around desperately, then calls after him with something that stops him dead.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> [<I>with a wry smile</I>] And what will I tell Madison? [<I>Kim stands there for a moment, his back turned to the camera. The entire crew has gathered behind Justin and watches in silence.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> She'll be very disappointed if she doesn't have something to show the public this week from you.
[<I>Hyuk stands upright, quite obviously listening.</I>] <b>Justin:</b> And after she saved you from Hunter last week, don't you think this is the least you can do?
[<I>The number one contender turns to face the man slowly, a few of the crew stepping forward to cut off what seems to be an inevitable attack. But the wrestler makes no such move. After a few seconds, he speaks.</I>]
<b>Hyuk:</b> No childish insults. <b>Justin:</b> Yeah, those weren't really working out anyway. You're right. Yeah, out with the insults. Check. <b>Hyuk:</b> And no out of character anything. I am Hyuk Suh Kim, not Roderick
Brown. You'd do well to remember that.
<b>Justin:</b> How could I forget? Yep, you're Hyuk, no problem. <b>Hyuk:</b> Tell your people to ready their equipment then. I will speak my piece. [<I>The director only has to glance back at his crew to get them scurrying into position. He, too, returns to his chair, a prideful bounce in his step. Hyuk walks slowly to the brick wall, looking back at the camera as Justin points to a stage hand.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> Water him down. [<I>The same blonde kid from before comes forward, spraying a bit of water on
Kim's chest and hair.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> Okay, let's get the wind going too. [<I>From stage right, three tech guys shove a massive fan into place. Flipping it on, it silently begins an ongoing breeze from Kim's right.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> All right, then. Lights. [<I>The brilliantly bright lights return around Hyuk and it takes him a moment to adjust to them, using his right hand to soften the blow of their glare.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> Camera. [<I>The original shot of Kim standing alone in front of that red brick wall returns, Hyuk's neutral expression revealing no signs of the frustration of the past several moments.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> And . . <I>action!</I> [<I>All is silent as Hyuk stares into the camera's lens, his knowing eyes devoid of emotion.</I>]
<b>Hyuk:</b> You say you relish a challenge . . . [<I>Kim's short, jet black hair and flowing pants move gently in the "breeze".</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> Dread Saxon, what challenge can you find in me? You stand eight inches taller. You weigh ninety pounds more. Your accomplishments in your time make my short-lived success seem almost humorous.
[<I>A long sigh.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> Is it because of Baxter Vanin's confidence in my abilities? Come now. If Vanin believed I could win, he would have allowed Madison to wager on
Hunter instead of forcing her to choose another horse to ride. With Pride losing to Sloane and Vatch throwing away his opportunity, my slot was thrust upon me by default, hardly the fashion I had intended. Is it because of Hunter
Sabuani's confidence in my abilities? He's hardly raised an eyebrow in my direction. There was a time when I was Hunter's equal, yes. But now he reigns. It is his time. I am his challenger, nothing more. Tell me then,
Darian, where is this great challenge?
[<I>Kim stops, lowering his voice.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> Is it because you think I'm special? [<I>A small smile creeps onto his face as he ponders the thought.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> That is what you said, isn't it? That everyone thinks Hyuk Suh
Kim is "special". How novel. The little upstart, demolishing all in his path, is that it? Hardly. I have survived, nothing more. What then? What is it?
Where is your great challenge? [<I>Hyuk exhales slowly, taking a moment to start a new train of thought.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> But let's talk about you, Darian. You and your motivations. What is it that drives you these days? I hear you speak of profits and checks and benefits. Is that what you've become . . . a mercenary?
[<I>Kim's eyes narrow to slits.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> I don't believe it. Not for a second. [<I>A slight noise, possibly a cough, rings out from somewhere off camera, a not so subtle reminder of Kim's true surroundings.</I>]
<b>Hyuk:</b> Your current status <I>eats</I> at you, doesn't it, Darian? I subject to you and the viewing audience that you could care less about the money. And, apparently, you could care less about Madison Justice. Why then?
What is it that you are setting up? [<I>Kim's smile fades at last.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> I dare not guess. After all, does it really matter when all is said and done? When Wednesday arrives, you will face me. Bring your Coterie friends if you must. Leave them behind if you wish. Once the bell rings, nothing will matter - not even your legendary status. And certainly not Hunter
Sabuani.
[<I>The number one contender nods slowly, the long evaporated water being replaced by the hot light induced sweat on his brow.</I>]
<b>Hyuk:</b> And now, Darian, the cold, hard truth. [<I>Kim exercises a long, dramatic pause, letting the silence flow in.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> You, despite your accomplishments and ability, <I>are</I> a stepping stone. A rather large step, admittedly, but one I <I>must</I> take.
I am using you, Darian. Using you to prove myself to not only Baxter Vanin and
Madison, but Hunter, his Coterie, and the GCW with them. When my hand is raised, I will have arrived and you, my legendary friend, will be left clinging to your past.
[<I>Hyuk's head tilts to the side a few inches, his tone threatening and low.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> Face it, Darian, you have nothing left to prove. What will putting down another upstart show anyone? Who will be impressed? Or surprised? You have become an embittered, stale shadow of what you were and only a true challenge can bring out your greatness. Perhaps an opportunity at your third World Title would bring out that spark you once had. Maybe a match against your old friend Soul Shadow would have you moving you around that ring like you used to.
[<I>His head slowly returning to its normal position as he continues.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> These things, of course, will not be happening this Wednesday - or anytime soon, if ever. I question your motivation, Darian. I question your dedication. Trying to live up to your own standards of excellence must be difficult. I do not envy your position. After all, that is what you are fighting for when we really get down to it, isn't it? Your own legend?
[<I>Kim nods to himself, letting that point linger in the silence.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> You tell me I live in the past? [<I>That look of disgust returns, Kim's voice lowering one more time.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> Hypocrite. [<I>His eyes fall into those slits, his expression that of total contempt.</I>] <b>Hyuk:</b> This coming Wednesday, in Cobb County, Georgia, you will see why it is that I am like the wind . . .
[<I>Abruptly and without warning, Kim walks off camera. The scene cuts to the wide shot of the studio. Justin stands as Hyuk continues to wade his way through mechanical equipment to make it to the small studio's side door.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> Hyukstar! Hey, we need you for just a few more . . . [<I>But he's gone, the door slamming behind him. Justin shakes his head, forcing an angered exhale out as the blonde stage hand approaches him.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> [<I>quietly</I>] How did it turn out? <b>Kid:</b> Pretty damn good. <b>Justin:</b> All right, ship it over to Madi then. <b>Kid:</b> Say, Justin? Think Kim can beat Saxon? [<I>The director looks at the young man, handing him his clipboard, his hands find his hips.</I>]
[<I>He seems to ponder the question for several long seconds before at last answering.</I>]
<b>Justin:</b> Nope. [<I>Fade to black.</I>] [name withheld] -liked this one
