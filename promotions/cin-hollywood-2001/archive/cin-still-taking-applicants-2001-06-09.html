NEWS & RUMORS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -Los Angeles, California citizens were taken by storm with the announcement of life long resident and CEO of CCW Entertainment Jefferson Lyons was found dead in the trunk of his Jeep Cherokee less then two weeks ago. A memorial service was held in a small chapel in Santa Monica, California immediately after the announcement of his death. He will be greatly missed. -The mourning Lyons family was taken by surprise when it was announced that CCW Entertainment was NOT left in their hands. Instead, Jefferson Lyons had a pre-recorded video where he officially handed the keys to his brainchild to whomever the current CCW Champion was at the time of his death. It was found in the pocket of his leather jacket by police officials at the scene of the crime. The LAPD couldn't be reached for comments as to who are being considered suspects in the Lyons case but an inside source has informed us that the CCW employees have not been ruled out. Lyons ran a firm shop and in his business enemies are everywhere. -Miles Gloriosus, the new CEO of CIN Enterprises, has announced that he is entrusting CIN to local film director Dean Cicerone so that he may pursue the unclosed Lyons case in hope to avenge the death of his mentor and father like figure. -In honor of Lyons' death CIN's first official order of business will be a charity event held in the Los Angeles Theater Center. All tickets are ten dollars a person and will go towards the Lyons Memorial Recreational Fund that helps provide children in the Los Angeles area with adequate sports equipment and facilities. Rumor has it the event will feature an inaugural sixteen man tournament to crown the first ever CIN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. There isn't an official word on when the event will be taking place but we will keep you updated. -CIN is all ready receiving heat from other Southern California promotions who are unhappy with the style of this organization. It has been said that the "rival" companies fear losing fan base to young adult audience with it's upbeat and original flavor. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3.
