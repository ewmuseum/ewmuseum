NEWS & RUMORS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= - The rumors about an inaugural charity event in honor of Jefferson Lyons have proven to be true. The event will be held in the Los Angeles Theater Center on Saturday June 30th in a one night extravaganza. Tickets are still on sale and will benefit the Lyons Memorial Recreational Fund which honors the young athletes of the Los Angeles area. - The Lyons family have been said to be boycotting this event due to their court case in which they are attempting to regain financial control over what is now known as CIN. When attempted to contact concerning the matter the Lyons family had no comments to leave us with. - On a related note. Acting owner Miles Gloriosus will NOT be present at the first show. He has left the program entirely in the hands of acting president Dean Cicerone. It has been said that Miles has yet to recover from the death of Jefferson Lyons and is in fact been rumored to have left the state of California all together for the time being. - President Dean Cicerone has promised us a wide variety of talent throughout the globe. Rumor has it that CIN has signed talent from Mexico, England, Asia, and of course Italy. This is said to be only the beginning. - A few stars struggled to get releases in time for CIN’s inaugural event from their former employees. Expect a few new faces to pop up at the show itself. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3. ROSTER (see below) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Coming soon... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 4.
