Extra: Application ------------------------------------------------------------------------ 1. What is CIN? 1.1 Fantasy Explanation Where as most feds initials "stand" for something, ours is "short" for something. That something being CINema. CIN is a spin off of a make belief organization called Cin-City Wrestling or more commonly known as CCW. The organization closed on a fateful night where the owner, Jefferson Lyons, was found dead and in his will he had left the organization to his protege and last remaining champion Miles Gloriosus. Miles, unaware of the political aspects of the sport, handed CCW over to the current owner Dean Cicerone, a struggling film producer. In hopes to give CCW a new edge and bring it to new heights Mr. Cicerone has deemed CCW under the name of CIN due to his love and passion for the film industry and because of the surroundings it has been born into in the place known as Hollywood. Dean is left with the task of creating a new style of wrestling from the ground up while Miles Gloriosus has sent himself on a mission of redemption in hopes to find the killers of his father-like figure Jefferson Lyons. 1.2 Regular Explanation CIN has been created by myself, or for the those unknowing of who I am the man simply known to the rest as Guzz. I hope to bring a new and aspiring creative edge to the hobby known as e-wrestling. The idea of running a fed based on a past fed was something I had always wanted to do and where as some feds truly are spin offs of old feds, we have the liberty of being able to make up our past as we move along which gives us unlimited angle and story-line ideas. I added in the "CINEMA" twist due to my passion of writing and films in hopes that the gimmick approach would add a certain flavor to CIN that people could enjoy. At this time, the fed is run single handily by myself. I will book all matches, control all the "non-wrestler" personnel, and so forth. CIN will be a small independent organization with a roster aiming roughly at 20-25 participants, five matches a week, and the hope of giving EVERYONE at least a match every other week so that nobody is sitting around for long periods of time. If given the proper support by other parties CIN could expand but at this time I feel I can run this operation as it is from an executive stand point by myself though all help is appreciated and accepted. The beginning stages of laying the foundation may be rocky but that is where you, the handlers come in. The more you put into this place, the better it will be and the continuos goal of a fun and entertaining environment will turn into a on going reality. 2. Who runs CIN? Again, it is run by myself, Guzz from an Out of Character stand point but will be ran from an In Character stand point by an owner, president, and commissioner. More on that below. 3. What "style" does CIN use? The only thing I am a stickler on as far as "writing style" goes is the actual "shows" [weekly shows and pay per views] themselves. We will be using my favorite writing style "Action Brackets System" or better known as ABS which allows for more descriptive action packed spots and segments and easy to read/follow dialogue. For example: [Austin stalks the champion from behind after connecting with a vicious spear that sent the crowd into a frenzy. The Rock, nursing that left knee, slowly rises to his feet and as he turns towards the challenger he is met with a stiff boot to the midsection followed almost immediately with a Cold Stone Stunner!] JR: Austin with the Stunner! J"K"L: We could have a new champion! Etc. Also, all on card segments must use these [ ] brackets. I will edit them if they aren't used but please it makes my job easier and I like to have a solid format for the show. For flash shows or anything else you can use whatever you want, just try and help me out on the actual show. I won't punish you if you don't, just curse your name from behind my desk while you laugh out loud from afar. 4. Where is CIN based? As of now, CIN is an organization based out of Southern California with it's main headquarters out of a Hollywood office. We will tour small gyms, arenas, and various venues in hopes to capture the atmosphere that suits CIN at that particular stage of it's existence. 5. How are matches decided? CIN is a booked league which means everything is decided by a person rather then a simulator or a pair of dice [or even WWF No Mercy :)] Basically, there are four factors that will decide if you win or lose: 1) Flash/RP 2) On Card Segments/Participation 3) Angles/Heat 4) Strats All these will be explained in the next question... 6. How do I succeed in the CIN? As stated above, there are four factors that will make you successful. 6.1 Flashes Flashes. To most, this is the KEY to winning and in some feds is the ONLY way to win by out flashing or out Role-Playing your opponent. In CIN, it is important, but not the only means of winning. This is your chance to let the world know who your character really is. The better you portray him here, the better the match writer will be able to portray him in the ring and the easier it will be for me to put you in angles. It is a huge part in deciding the outcomes of a match and though more times then not the person with the best flash usually wins, it doesn't mean that you will ALWAYS win. Which takes us to the other key factors. 6.2 On Card Segments/Participation Participation can be the key to a big push. I don't require much from the handlers but here's where the people that will rise the fastest will excel by putting in that extra effort. The more you participate in that particular week can add to your chances off success during that week. However, keep in mind that NOBODY will go undefeated forever. This fed wasn't created for one man to become the greatest wrestler in the history of make believe wrestling. CIN was created for the enjoyment of ALL parties. The way a handler reacts to a loss can prove just how dedicated he is to CIN and just how creative his mind is. People overlook that playing off a loss correctly can add SO MUCH volume to your character physically and mentally as well as you, the handler. On Card segments consist of anything from an arrival to an in ring to a backstage segment. Remember, it's a game, it's suppose to be fun. Game = Fun. 6.3 Angles/Heat Angles are the biggest factor in the push a wrestler receives. Winning AND losing can elevate a character to a higher level if done correctly based on an angle a handler is taking part of. Though at times you may have out RPed your opponent, you may be booked to lose based on an angle that furthers the story-line of someone who participated more. I'm not saying someone can angle their way to the top and never RP cause that'd be absurd but sometimes the "heat" a character has can push them over another wrestler. 6.4 Strats The last remaining factor is a strat. For CIN, this is a tiebreaker although it is REQUIRED. For the most part, it won't affect the outcome of the match unless YOU DO NOT DO IT. The only element I require is a RING ENTRANCE and you can copy and paste the same one every single week if that's what you want. However, keep in mind that the writer has the creative freedom to do as he see fits [though I will proof read] with your wrestler and the more you help him, the more he'll help you. The extra effort you put in is greatly appreciated and I can't stress that enough. This is your chance to explain to us what exactly you want your wrestler to do during and most importantly, at the end of the match or even after it. People can add anything from OOC notes, Move List, Spots, Win/Loss Reactions, End Scenarios, post match speeches, etc to their strategy. If you want a basic outline of what I would like to see from all of you [though as I said only the ring entrance is required] is: a. Ring Entrance [please, keep them under a page and even that's a lot] b. Character Mindset [Your character's attitude during the match, how they'll wrestle, what their manager/guard will do, etc.] c. Spots [Description of move sequences or moves you would like to see your wrestler perform in the match. This doesn't mean they will ALL be used]: d. Win/Loss Scenario - [Character's reaction to winning/losing and/or how you would like to see your character win or lose]: e. Other [Other.] Do that and you will be loved by all. 7. What are the shows that CIN runs? 7.1 CIN On Location. Using the ABS system On Location will be the MAIN show of CIN that will feature fully written matches and host all the angle work and segments that are written by the handlers and the executive staff. This show will be run "hopefully" on a weekly basis [at least that's what the goal is] but I do understand that sometimes things come up in your life and you should all understand that sometimes they come up in mind. However, I will give notice if I plan on leaving for longer then a day at a time and if I don't then more then likely something I couldn't avoid happened [lets pray that these unavoidable situations are far and few between]. 7.2 CIN Sneak Preview This is a little something extra I like to add to the feds I run. It will consist OOC notes, rumors, rankings, future and current line ups, and will always come out shortly after the release of the latest On Location or PPV/Supercard show and marks the beginning of a new week. 7.3 CIN Director's Cut This is a where your weekly flashes are installed. You will have an adequate amount of time to write them and can air them from anywhere you see fit [please be realistic]. You can state what is on your wrestler's mind, they're feelings towards their opponent, or even a certain activity that your wrestler has partaken in that will further develop the character or add to a particular story you are attempting to write. It is what most people like to simply call a "Flash Packet". The name itself, Director's Cut, is up in the air for change. If anyone can think of a better name feel free to let me know. 7.4 Supercard/Pay Per View These are the biggest events CIN has and is the final showdown of the biggest feuds going on over the last "quarter" of the year. Supercards are different then PPV's, and since CIN is brand new we will not be holding a PPV till the fed grows thus we will be using Supercards, which are basically a Big On Location show. When/if CIN grows, we will more then likely be on a three month time frame as I like to make PPV's worth something by spreading them out. A PPV every month would demean their value in my mind. 8. What titles/awards does CIN recognize? 9.1 Heavyweight Title The biggest prize in the federation, this title is also one of the most coveted in the sport. It's what every wrestler has his eye on when they enter the organization, is held by the best, and is defended AT LEAST once every 3-4 weeks but may be defended more often pending on angles and booking decisions. 9.2 Golden State Heritage Title The secondary title to CIN. This is a stepping stone for some but for others this will be as far as they will ever reach on the CIN ladder. It is by no means a stab to the handler of this wrestler as it will be the most competitive title in CIN due to the mid-card division more times then not having the most people in it. It will roughly be defended once every 2-3 weeks. 9.3 Hollywood Title This is the first step to climb in CIN but as of CIN's opening this title will and may not ever exist pending on the roster size of CIN. It will, if used, be a Television Title that will be defended on EVERY TELEVISED CIN show. If a man successfully defends this title five times in a row he will be offered a chance to challenge for the Southern Heritage Title show immediately but if he accepts, win or lose, he will vacate the Hollywood Title. 10. What are the main policies of CIN? 10.1 Flash Policy a. One flash a week is REQUIRED for anyone who has a "match" angled or not. Remember the emphasis in CIN is to have fun and not on winning thus the requirement AND maximum number of flashes is the nice well rounded number of one. By doing this, it is easier to decipher a winner of this category based on a head to head scale. If you chose not to write a FLASH then you MUST have an "in-ring" on the show but keep in mind we will base a greater emphasis on a RP over an in-ring when deciding winners. If you fail to RP for three matches in a row your CIN contract will be terminated. b) Swearing is acceptable but PLEASE keep it minimal. If you feel the need to use it, bleep it with F[Bleep] or #### or in some fashion that the word itself isn't used but we can still get a basic idea of what was being said. c) Racial slurs will not be tolerated even if it is "in character" for you to do with your wrestler. If you have a question about a word/phrase feel free to ask me or another handler. Use your judgment here folks. d) Using other wrestlers in your flashes without their permission WILL NOT be tolerated. If you want to do a joint RP with someone, ask them. I have created two FIELD REPORTERS to be used in flash packets and numerous characters to be used for on card segments. However, for on card segments please let me know what you have planned and what character that you would like to use so that another handler doesn't use them at the same time in a fashion that would be unrealistic. Do not hurt/harm/beat/kick/have sex with/kill [and so forth] any of the CIN characters. If you would like to plot an attack for some reason, let me know, and we will discuss the issue. I'm a lenient guy, just use common sense. e) Deadlines will be announced on a week by week basis in the SNEAK PREVIEW mails you get. 10.2 Booking Policy As stated before, there is something everyone must understand. a)YOU WILL LOSE!! If you want to come in with a Goldberg character, who never loses, I only have one thing to say... ..Fat chance. :) If you can't handle the idea of losing a match, do not fill out an application. We _do not_ want an Excedrin headache because someone lost a match. This is the part about being a fedhead that we hate the most. Can't we all just get along? Just think of the federation as a big soap opera. The people who have the best story-line going on get the most airtime on the show. Those who don't, don't get very much time. So, the people who have the best angles, and role-plays, will get the airtime, in other words, win. So, write like (Add favorite author here), and you should be all right! 11. How do I join? First. Make sure CIN is the place for you. If you can't cope with the information above, don't join, because more then likely you aren't going to be happy. Other then that, I welcome anyone to apply for CIN. Secondly, go check out the roster. Look through it, and check out what types of gimmicks we already have incorporated. Come up with an original character, and fill out an application. But, remember, CIN likes to keep a rather small roster, typically about 20-25 singles wrestlers. This way, people don't get lost in the shuffle and everyone stays active. If at some time we do have a waiting list I apologize. My priority goes to the handlers all ready involved and it's not fair for me to give someone the shaft to bring someone else in. If this is the case I will let you know as soon as I can and then all I can do is recommend to take a number and wait. That's all I can say. People come and go like the wind, so you may have to wait a week, it may be a few months. I'm not sure. To get into CIN your application has to look good to me. From there, I'll contact you and let you know if you've been chosen, put on hold, etc or in some cases, let you know that I think you are CIN material but your choice of wrestler is either over used or unfitting to the CIN atmosphere and attempt to work something out with you [either tweak or create someone new.] 13.
