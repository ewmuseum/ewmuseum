Many of you said, on your apps, that you’d be willing to help write for CIN shows in some form or another so what I need to know is, who wants to write and how often. What I am looking for, to have on a regular basis, is an EIGHT man match writing crew. NEWS & RUMORS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= - Summer Blockbuster 2001, Premiere, is now behind us and with it came the first ever Heavyweight Champion of CIN. Congratulations to Mac Rollins who defeated The "Upstart Kid" Boy Hedges in a match that will go down in history as one of the best matches ever for this promotion, no matter what kind of performances follow here after. But these rewards did not come without injury. Due to the alliance of Juan Vasquez and Tripp Shade these two Hollywood Heroes were forced to continue fighting after the epic battle had come to an end. Payback is said to be in the near future. - One of the biggest mysteries thus far is the "Cloaked" figure who belted President Dean Cicerone over the head with one of his infamous packages. In a disgusting display, random body parts were being found throughout the night in various places, including the President’s office. What will our cloaked enigma have in store for us this week? Will he even show? And what is the meaning behind these repulsive and sickening games. - In a somewhat humorous turn of events, "Sex Pistol" Jamie Roberts has made it known to one El Satanico that he has the best executed moonsault in the game. During El Satanico’s second round match it was Jamie Roberts that cost Brian J. Blottie’s lucha warrior off guard, costing him a win and a possible chance at gold. With Roberts wrestling in the main event and El Satanico out of action, it seems it might only be a matter of time before Satanico seeks revenge. - Hollywood big rig Actor Corey Ryland was pummeled by a die-hard fan this past week and Dean Cicerone has made comments that he will be upping security in the weeks to come to protect the stars from the fans. Oddly enough, this particular fan, going by the name of Laramee, has been signed to CIN as...a wrestler. Ryland refused to sign the young fan’s autograph book and the boisterious young man took it upon himself to force a signature from Ryland. He hasn’t got his John Hancock yet, but we’ve got plenty of time ahead of us. - Vladimir Stukovski sustained injuries to his left leg at the hands of Corey Ryland in the first round of the Blockbuster tournament this weekend and doctor’s aren’t quite sure when the "Marxman" will be able to return to action. Our best wishes go out to the man who has been deemed by us, the "Mad Russian". - The Lyons family did in fact, not appear at this event which came somewhat as a shock to Dean Cicerone and the CIN company. Rumors conmtinue to spread that they will strike back, in hopes to regain their company by any means possible. Could they be waiting for Miles to return from the hospital? Or are they just plotting their course and waiting for the right opportunity to make themselves be heard. - Local fans seem to have taken a liking to CIN this weekend, calling it an event to remember due to fast paced action that comes with it. Teenagers and young children are still buzzing about the Falling Star Senton performed by the Boy Hedges off of the fifteen foot ladder and through the table and all ready word has been received by the local news about a nine year old male breaking his leg as he tried to emulate the move. Remember kids, don’t try this at home. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3. LINE-UP =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= CINEMA SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT PROUDLY PRESENTS... X-RATED!!! LIVE!
