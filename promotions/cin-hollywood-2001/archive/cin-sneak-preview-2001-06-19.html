NEWS & RUMORS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= - Wrestling enthusiasts have been buzzing as of late about the completely fresh faces that CIN has brought to the table. It’s been said that this will be a place to create new stars in the wrestling, ones that will eventually be some of the most well known athletes around the state of California, and possibly, the world. - Local bookers are having an extremely tough time placing accurate odds on the tournament brackets in the early stages of the tournament. It’s been said that the event is wide open and at this point it’s any man’s title to win. - After immediately departing the state of California after the funeral reception of Jefferson Lyons, Miles Gloriosus has finally been spotted just outside New York City. Why he’s there is anyone’s guess but we’ll keep you posted on his whereabouts and actions. - The Lyons family have confirmed that they will NOT be present at this Blockbuster event. Thus, CIN will completely in the hands of a man who is entirely new to sport. Some long time CCW followers are extremely skeptic about what Dean Cicerone might bring to the table while the fresh new group of CIN fans are highly anticipating this inaugural event. - Several new wrestlers have been signed to CIN, more new faces for people to wonder about. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3.
