CINEMA SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT PRODUCTION ..................................................... 1. Juan Vasquez 2. Carl Blair 3. Tripp Shade 4. The Boy Hedges ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT................... "El Cholo" JUAN VASQUEZ ..................................................... [Fade in...] [Scene: The City of Angels. Nuestra Senora la Reina de Los Angeles de Porciuncula. Or, if you wish...Los Angeles. And in this city of beautiful people and movie stars, we happen to come upon it's most _BEAUTIFUL_ person and it's _BIGGEST_ star. Ah yes, that's right...] "Boo!" [...it's our SAVIOR~!, the sensation sweeping the nation and object of Katie Holmes' self-stimulation, your sister's dream, her menstrual cycle's nightmare, and an all-around nice guy..."El Cholo" Juan Vasquez(~!). The shot pulls back, to reveal Vasquez's humble abode, a dilapidated home somewhere in East Los Angeles. Juan is dressed in a pretty spiffy white, dress shirt, with the sleeves rolled up to his forearms, power tie left dangling in the wind, and a pair of black slacks. He fixes his tie and smiles a rather obnoxious smile for the cameras.] JV: Oh, lord...if they was ever a time fo' you peeps to dance in the streets naked, filled with rapture and ecstacy beyond yo' wildest imaginations, blinded with pleasure and joy, this would be it. [He chuckles.] JV: 'Cause as of June 22, 2001...yo' SAVIOR~! is an employee of CIN! [He suddenly breaks off into a somewhat dramatic pose. A voice can be heard from the background.] "Oh, Juan...get over yourself." [We cut to a shot of America's sweetheart, the 145th most downloaded woman on the internet, reigning little Miss Cutie Pie '85, and all-around bitch goddess, Anna, lying back on a lawn chair. Calm down, perverts...the Queen of the Catfight is dressed in her cotton pjs with little fat cows floating on clouds adorned all over. Her hair is down, covering part of her face, and she's wearing some big fluffy Hello Kitty slippers.] JV: Woman, shut yo' mouth. Don't be ruinin' the moment. Anna: I'm just saying... JV: Shhh! Anna: All I was trying to do was... JV: Shhh! Anna: But... JV: Shush, foul temptress, 'lest I smite thee! [What the hell?] Anna: What the hell? JV: Work with me here, I'm tryin' out new material. Anna: Uh huh... JV: But enough of that...it's time to get to the impo'tant stuff. Mainly, why *me*, yo' favorite wrestler in the whole goddamn world, Juan Vasquez...ain't in the heavyweight title tourney. I mean, things like *that* just _DON'T_happen by coincidence. [Anna rolls her eyes.] Anna: Oh God, Juan...why does everything have to be a conspiracy with you!? JV: Woman, is you *doubtin'* me? Is you sayin' that they ain't plannin' to keep that title offa' 'mah waist? I mean, is they _ANY_ reason why 'mah magnificent ass shouldn't be in that tournament!? Anna: I dunno, maybe the fact that you been employed by the company for all of three hours would have something to do with it. JV: So *that's* it! Anna: What? [Juan shakes his head in disbelief.] JV: I shoulda' known....I shoulda' known...I mean, it happens to in every f[bleep]in' place I go to... Anna: What? JV: Ain't it obvious!? The front office is incompetent! Anna: Oh... [Anna sighs. Here we go again...] JV: That can be the *only* reason why L.A.'s favorite son ain't battlin' fo' the heavyweight title. [Juan holds up three fingers.] JV: Three, count'em _THREE_ motherf[bleep]in' hours. That's one hundred eighty minutes...ten thousand eight hundred seconds that they's could've put to good use, but did they lift a finger? Did they even _TRY_ to put me in the tourney!? They's had _THREE_ hours to kick someone's useless ass out and put me in they place, but did they? NO! [He loosens his tie, tearing it off from his collar and throwing it down onto the ground. Soon, he's laughing quietly to himself.] JV: Hehe...but...it's cool. It's alright. I ain't mad at'cha. You peeps can go right on ahead with yo' little tournament. You can fight yo' matches. You can wrestle yo' little hearts out. Spill yo' blood fo' that title. But in the end, you's just delayin' the inevitable. [He cracks his knuckles.] JV: 'Cause no matta' what happens, that title can only end up in one place, people. [Juan holds out his arms and smiles.] JV: Around _MY_ waist. [He sighs happily.] JV: And that 'mah friends....is all they is...to it. [Fade...to black.] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT................... "Your True Life Fantasy" CARL BLAIR ..................................................... [FADE] [Ever have a little brother? Annoying aren’t they? Always following you around, “tagging along” if you may. Well, the same goes for the story between “Your True Life Fantasy” Carl Blair and his younger brother the self proclaimed “Beautiful” Bobby Blair. Bobby’s constantly following him around, and now it appears he too will be appearing in CIN City Wrestling. Not as a wrestler though, he’s far too scrawny. When the camera opens up we’re in that big ass Ryder truck we saw at the Blair residence last week. Bobby Blair’s driving, as Carl looks unpleased in the passenger seat. He sits with his arms crossed. A look of displeasure is upon his face. Bobby however can’t help to be happier. He’s tagging alongside his big brother and appears to be coming to the promotion as well. What luck?] “B” BB: I can’t wait Carl! I simple can’t wait! [Carl about half asleep/half pissed off fires back.] “YTLF”: WHAT can’t you wait for? “B” BB: For the start of CIN, me and you, you and me, we’re going to be great! I mean look at it from my perspective; you’ve got “Your True Life Fantasy” Carl Blair and in his corner, stands “Beautiful” Bobby Blair! It can’t get any better than this! [How great?!? Carl tries to move across country to get AWAY from his younger brother, but now it appears he’ll be stuck with him even longer.] “B” BB: First round comes up, you’ll beat El Satanico. I know it. Then you just have the rest of the tournament, that’s where my help comes into play… “YTLF”: Listen, I don’t need your help. I didn’t need it in the past and I sure as hell don’t need it now. You wanna be apart of this? That’s fine, but remember one thing, I run the show, I call the calls, you do nothing but sit back and watch. Got it? “B” BB: Got it. I got a question though. What exactly do you plan on doing about the tournament? I mean, you have to beat more ‘n one guy. Satanico’s the start, then it looks like you have the Boy Hedges. If you beat him… “YTLF”: There’s no IF, about anything. You and I both know, June 30 will be my time to shine. “B” BB: Well, like I was saying after you get past The Boy Hedges, it looks like Scott Hale or Corey Ryland in the semi finals. Then whoever comes out on top of Pool B. So whatcha got planned? [Carl pauses, obviously going over his thoughts.] “YTLF”: Well, when I said I didn’t need your help. That wasn’t entirely the truth. I’ve got something planned for Summer Blockbuster ’01… [FADE.] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT................... "The Actor That Cares" TRIPP SHADE ..................................................... [What happens when a man bottoms out? When he has no where else to turn? No one else to talk to? What does he do, when he has nothing else to do?] [Well, he turns hand-slapping babyface, of course!] Tripp Shade stars in, "The Unexpected and Completely Out of Left Field Babyface Turn" [Scene opens in the luxurious Hollywood Estate(translation: cramped Northridge studio apartment) of CIN Superstar slash Hollywood Hanger-Oner, "Box Office" Tripp Shade. Shade lays about lazily on his couch, leafing through a magazine. Which manly magazine is he leafing through, you ask? Cosmo, of course.] TS: F<bleep>. F<bleep>, F<bleep>, F<bleep>. Does this F<bleep>in' thing have page numbers!??! HOW AM I SUPPOSED TO GET TO THE F<BLEEP>IN' "What's Your Perfect Color Combo" QUIZ ON PAGE 247, IF THESE PAGES AREN'T NUMBERED?!?!?! HAAAAAAAAAAAATE~!! [Shade throws a momentary tantrum, kicking his legs about, like a spaz. But then.. he stops. And, he has, what some would call... a moment of clarity.] TS: Dear lord.... what am I doing with my life? What am I doing in my apartment, reading Cosmo, when I should be getting ready for my big debut in CIN- [Shade is interrupted by a ringing phone. Yes, this, friend, would be WRESTLING ROLEPLAY CLICHE #47--The One Sided Phone Conversation~!] TS: Yellllooooooo? Just who I needed to talk to!! Yes... listen... I'm in need of a friend, right now. What? You... oh. I see. Well... yeah, I guess. What's that? Do I think you care? What--wait! That's it!! Caring!! Thanks! What's that? No, for the last time, there is no Guadalupe here! Goodbye! [Shade, with a sudden vigor, hangs up the phone, and hops off his couch!! He leaves our shot, and we fade out...] CUE TRIPP SHADE MONTAGE-VISION® [The next shots we see, will probably be disturbing to some. In fact, they're disturbing for this hear narrator, to relate. But, fact is, that phone call, wrong number, or not, changed Tripp Shade. Did it change him for the better? That remains to be seen. But the fact is, Tripp Shade was no longer the dellussional, smug, uncaring, ass that he once was! He suddenly cared about people.....] [Cut to Shade helping an old lady cross the street. He's also carrying her groceries. And just to REALLY put this whole thing over, the old lady is blind. And crippled. And, why the hell not, she's got one of those tubes in her nose, to help her breathe.] [Not only did he care about old, near-death people...he cared about hot looking ones, too!] [Cut to Shade helping a foxy blonde cross the street. He's also carrying her Fredricks of Hollywood bag. And just to REALLY put this whole thing over some more, the foxy blonde is carrying around a set of D cups. And she's wearing a halter top. And, why the hell not, she's got on one of those thongs, to help reduce panty lines.] [You get the point. It didn't matter whether it was people near their death bed, or people Shade would like TO bed, Tripp Shade really >CARED< about people! So much so, that he went to CIN Headquarters, and he asked--nay, he DEMANDED--that his nickname be changed from the smarmy, self-involved "Box Office" to the truer, closer-to-the-heart, "The Actor That Cares"! And that's where we stand now. So let's restart this scene, shall we?] [.....] [Scene opens to a park. It's a beautiful Southern California day. The kind of day that SCREAMS, "Let's help mentally retarded kids play baseball". Well, apparently, that's what this day is screaming to CIN Superstar, slash Darn nice guy, "Actor That Cares" Tripp Shade! Tripp helps the various members of this group of ragtag Re-Re's swing the lumber, when he notices the CIN camera.] [Yep, it's WRESTLING ROLEPLAY CLICHE #75: Noticing the Promotion's Camera.] [Oh, and also, CUE TRIPP SHADE PROMO-VISION®] TS: What? Oh, CIN, eh? Heh.. didn't notice you. What brings you guys out here today? Out to play some ball with me and my special friends, here? [And just because we can, it's WRESTLING ROLEPLAY CLICHE #34: The Talking Cameraman] Talking Cameraman: Actually, we're hoping we can get some comments on the big tourney comin' up. TS: Ooooh, so you want me to [does quotation fingers] "cut a promo", eh? Heh.. well, I don't want to take time away from my special guys here, but... oh, what the hay, here goes nothing. Okay, Andy, I want you to try and hit the ball, okay, buddy? [Shade rubs the little guy's head, ruffling his hair, and walks with the cameraman.] TS: You know, it's hard enough winning one match a night. It's nearly impossible to win 4. But when you add in the fact that the fourth is a ladder match--one of the most physically strenuous matches on the planet, and you might as well get on your knees, and pray. But I believe in karma, and I also believe that CIN wants that Heavyweight title around the waist of someone which can be a rolemodel to thousands. Someone who can bring together the best of Hollywood and the best of wrestling. Most of all, someone who cares. And that's me. I'm Tripp Shade. I'm an actor. And I care. I'm The Actor That Cares. I care about these kids. I care about the CIN Heavyweight Title. But most of all.. I care about all my Tripp Troopers out there. So right here, right now, I say this--guys, I'm doing this for you! [Shade stops, and gives a genuine smile. He pounds his chest, where his heart is, and gives a thumbs up to the camera.] TS: I'm not going to take anything away from anyone in this tournament, because they are ALL dedicated performers. Guys, it's my pleasure to be in the ring with you. Good luck to all... but at the end of the night, God willing, if my hand is held high.... it'll be a new era in CIN.. it'll be the "Actor That Cares" era... the Care-a, if you will. Now, I'd love to stay and chat some more, but I've got to get back to my kids. Thanks for the time. [Shade waves, and trots back to his group of specials. He motions for them to all come close to them. And as we fade out--we fade out to a group hug. Awwww.] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT................... THE BOY HEDGES ..................................................... Yet more, yet more openings of scenes. We’re in what apparently seems to be an official CIN interview area, looks like our mildly-deadly duo are going up in the world. The CIN logo adorns a nice cloth hanging in the background, with a preacher-esque lectern stands in front of it. Behind the lectern, our men stand. Christopher Troisi wears a perfectly fitted navy-blue suit and trousers, but ruins it by having a official “The Boy Hedges” merchandise tee-shirt underneath. Hedges stands to his side, wearing a tee-shirt with a picture of Ally Sheedy in The Breakfast Club adorning it. He is slowly biting the skin off of the side of his thumbs whilst Mr Troisi delivers the following speech) Seemingly, Gunner Vaughn is about as talkative about Joey Ramone at the moment, which gives me precious little to talk about here. Give me some material here guys. I’m, here to cut the promos- when there’s no decent promotion coming my way, my talents are wasted. Same with the Boy here- there’s no decent wrestlers for him to fight, so his talents are wasted. I’m seriously considering pulling the guy from CIN, to be honest. He’s going to go rusty from week after week of under one minute meltdown against Jeff Weinerth or whoever. He’s also going to go into manic depression from week after week of (makes quote signs with fingers) “humorous” Will Brody vignettes. Will, I might as well address you, I’m getting paid by the minute here. You like music, do you? Well, music makes the bourgeoisie wanna rebel, yeah. Will Brody, alternatively, makes most people want to claw the very skin off their own faces before rubbing sea-salt onto the raw hamburger-meat flesh, just to avoid him wittering on and on about how “nice” he is. William- Hedges can be “nice”. He can help old ladies across roads. He can read stories to orphaned children. As long as the story is “Catcher In The Rye”, admittedly, but the thought is there. He can clean rubbish off streets for hygeine purposes. But, he doesn’t. He does a different act of samaritanism. You know what he does? He takes jesters like you, and drags your useless backsides to five star matches. Do you understand how much compassion that takes? My man here has love for his fellow man, y’know? But, like you said, wrestling is a lot like music. That would make you the Milli Vanilli of the situation, admittedly. Tripp Shade would be The Offspring, as I swear that they both had something of some mild interest to say once upon a time, but that could be the mind playing tricks. Mac Rollins would be Pink Floyd: self-indulgent, over-long, and turgid. One only hopes that during his matches he arranges for a group of builders to construct a wall around him, sparing the general public his attempts at wrestling. Gunner Vaughn would be Vanilla Ice, insofar as neither of them actually have a character as of this Sunday. And Jaime Roberts would be D-12. Because he’s sh(BLEEP)t. So, is that it? Is that all we need? Is that all I can say until the next time you see me, when gold adorns Hedges’ waist? Look, I could vent bile and spawn and whinge and complain until the damn cows come home here. But I don’t want to. Just look at the man. Look at him. Observe. I want every one of you suckers at home to understand what a champion looks like. Because when people talk about CIN in ten, twenty years time, they’re going to talk about one thing. The Boy Hedges. He’s the opening paragraph and the epilogue of CIN. And Vaughn, and anyone else who is dumb enough to wrestle him, is going to learn that through repeated pain. We’re providing a service here. You should be thankful. (And with that, we fade) .....................................................
