"Sex Pistol" JAMIE ROBERTS ...................................................... [What would you say, if I sang out of tune…? Probably 'shut the f*ck up', at a guess. What would you say if I said, hey, yo, it's the 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts and his wonderfully gorgeous manager, Claire Maynard? The answer may be very similar… but this is MY RP, not yours, and you think I care what readers think? Nope. So, hey, yo, it's the 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts and his wonderfully gorgeous manager, Claire Maynard. They're wearing clothes, and standing in a room. Descriptions suck.] CM: Is this gonna take long, sweetie? Only I was kinda hoping to get to bed soon... JR: Don't worry, honey, so was I. [They chortle, sharing a private joke. Chortling is the easy way to get heel heat which doesn't involve shooting on dead guys, apparently.] JR: It'll take about as long as my complete domination of the roster of CIN, I'd imagine. CM: Hardly worth starting the tape for, then. JR: Firstly, I'd like to say how sorry I am that Jefferson Lyons is dead. For one thing, it means my cousin Jose has got 'Worst Promoter of the Year' wrapped up already. Secondly, I'd like to give the guy who took over from him some advice. Retire. After all, you have just signed Jaime Roberts. Believe me, Dean, it doesn't get any better than this. Go out while you're on top. CM: Damn right. But how come you're not in the title tournament, baby? JR: I held back with my offer to come and work here, for a simple reason. The tournament ends in a ladder match. You know my thoughts on ladder matches - if God had intended men to have to take down a belt suspended from a ceiling - I'd have made their legs longer. [They laugh.] JR: Although that can be arranged, if anyone is aiming to spend an extended amount of time in one of my figure fours. No, seriously, why would I want to join that title tournament? After all, looking at the line up for the first show, there are three very interesting alternatives… talking to Tina Allen, talking to Sage Ragno, and talking to Donna Darling. CM: Very funny, Jaime. One more crack like that, and it won't be bed you're coming to once you've finished this, it'll be the broom cupboard. JR: Relax, honey, just kidding! Anyway, we have a broom cupboard? CM: I was speaking figuratively - you know, like when Will Brody said he had a chance of winning the title tournament. Or, at least, would've done, if he hadn't been too busy ogling Miss Airhead. JR: Oh, I get you - lying. CM: That's right. JR: Although, looking at Brody's opponents in that tournament, he may just pull off an upset... after all, first we have 'The Marxman' Vladimir Stukovski. When asked which Marx he took after, I was told it was a mixture - he wrestled like Groucho, cut promos like Zeppo, and had about as much personality as Karl. Although still more than his opponent in the first round, Corey Ryland. I will be the first to admit, I know nothing about Ryland. And I care about him even less. CM: B*****d. JR: You're upset because I insulted Corey Ryland? CM: Yeah - I was about to say that about Jeff Weinerth. JR: Sorry. You wanna talk about Scotty Hale instead? CM: Oh, yeah, Scott Hale. He doesn't need gimmicks, he doesn't need nicknames, he's just an ordinary guy. JR: For 'ordinary' read 'completely unmarketable'. Sometimes, when a guy is coming through the ranks, but hasn't got quite the right look, he'll be given a gimmick so that he can sell lots of T-shirts when he starts getting big wins. Let's just say, I doubt anyone will need to bother doing that for Mr Hale. Four down, twelve to go. And I think wrestling them would actually have been easier than finding something remotely interesting to say about them... But here goes, anyway. El Satanico. He's a slightly overweight luchadore. The hilarity. That ranks right up there with 'Lady with a hint of a moustache' and 'Magician who occasionally messes up a trick' in 'The top 10 of things you wouldn't bother going to the circus to see.' Coincidentally, El Satanico ranks right up with Seth Rogers and Gunner Vaughn in 'The top 10 of things you wouldn't bother going to the arena to see'. Of course, they're all slightly behind Carl Blair in those stakes. Mr Blair calls himself 'Your True Life Fantasy'. Only if your fantasy is 'Dungeons and Dragons', sadly. And, finally in Group A, we have The Boy Hedges. Let's see. Christopher Troisi is a man with a brother famous throughout the world of wrestling. He's a man with more mic skills than 90% of this roster put together. He's a man who could train a chimpanzee to become a better wrestler than Jake Summers, although that's probably damning him with faint praise. And he's managing a guy who's so stupid, he has to say the answers out loud when playing quiz machines. Something isn't right there. Moving swiftly onto Pool B, which manages the quite amazing feat of being WORSE than pool A, we start at the bottom, in more ways than one, with "Sensation" Eddie Valentin. Eddie, I know from experience that it's hard being admired by women between the ages of 13 and 39 worldwide, that it's difficult to live up to their expectations by being the champion, the best looking, and the coolest on the mic. I'm telling you this, because, despite your hopes to the contrary, there is absolutely no chance in hell of you actually experiencing that hardship yourself. Who else? JD Carter and Rob Payne both seem to want to let their wrestling do the talking, so they're doomed to everlasting silence... Mac Rollins let one of his friends commit suicide, and is trying to keep his sanity by reaching the ladder match, while stalking that guy's sister. Mac, there's nothing left to keep, you can go now. Preferably far, FAR away from me. And, finally, the one guy who I've seen briefly in the past - 'Box Office' Tripp Shade. Tripp, like me, has something unusual in CIN. It's called talent. The amount which he has is nowhere near mine, but we can't all be Jaime Roberts, can we? Seriously, though, Tripp, it's good to see you here. Once you win that belt, do me a favour, and keep polishing it until I get my shot. In return, I'll send you a signed photo of me wearing it - I'm sure that's the kind of 'Hollywood' touch you'd appreciate. CM: Is this where we fade? JR: Thankfully, yes. Much as I'd love to insult the referees as well, thinking about 16 guys with a combined IQ of 240 tends to take it out of me. [You heard what she said - fade, brother.] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... SETH ROGERS ...................................................... [We open up on the outskirts of Hollywood, California. The weather outside is anything but desirable. It's the middle of a huge thunderstorm. Thunder booms and lightning crashes. The roads are pretty much deserted, except for the occasional party car cruising up to a party in the valley. The camera pans around stormy afternoon skyline, and it's full of dark, gray clouds. This storm looks to be here for a while. The camera goes back to the roads, where it seems a car has stalled. The car is an old, beat-up, black pickup. The bed of the truck is covered by tarp, protecting whatever is back there from the rain. The driver of the truck steps out into the rain. All he's wearing is a dark red and brown flannel, a pair of blue jeans and work boots. He puts his hand through his head, which isn't entirely bald. He goes to the back, and checks on the tires. He kicks the back left tire, and luckily, it's full. But the back right is not. The man sighs..] MAN: This cross-country trip was supposed to be fun. Now all this is, is very annoying.. [He scratches his chin, and then, smiles.] How can I complain? This is just pointless. Here I am, about to fulfill a dream I've had for a long time, and I let a little speed bump like a flat tire get in my way? Na', I'm not lettin' it happen like that. And I am not going to let "Box Office" Tripp Shade ruin my chances of winning the CIN Heavyweight Title. [From the way he's been talking, we can only assume he is the newcomer, Seth Rogers.] ROGERS: Tripp Shade, you may not have a clue in the world to who I am. Hell, a lot of people don't, but that is fine by me. I have to make a name for myself by showing my talent, not by running my mouth. Ya'see Shade, I've seen you wrestle before. I know you're good, and I know that you know you're good. Don't think I'm oblivious to you, Shade. I tuned in regularly to the RCW, and I saw you. You impressed me. You impressed me a great deal. Too bad what overshadows your talent is your arrogance. You do everything the wrong way. Everything you've done in the ring is overshadowed by the shananogance you pull out of it. But hey, you live your life like that. I don't really mind how you live, but I do have a problem when it carries over to the CIN. [Rogers pulls down the end of the bed, and from under the tarp he pulls out a spare tire, and a jack.] Tripp, I've always prided myself on doing things the right way. I've always believed that if you follow your heart, you'll always win. But when I see you, all I see if someone following money, all the way to the bank. Now Tripp, all of these things I knew before I saw Director's Cut..... [Rogers takes off the tire. He uses the jack to get the car back up. Before he begins to put the new tire on, who wipes the rain out of his face..] After I saw that, I wasn't angry. I wasn't even close to mad. All I was, was amused, because according to you, I'm "THE FIRST ROUND GUY". Hey, that is fine by me, like I said, no one knows who I am. But what you did was prove you know nothing. I am glad you're looking on to the finals, because I'm taking this one match at a time. I'm focused. I'm pumped. I'm ready to go. [CRACK! A bolt of lighting flies out of the sky in the distance. Rogers just tilts his head and laughs. He finally gets the new tire on, and is ready to go..] Well, Tripp, the only roadblock I had has been fixed. When the CIN: Premiere hits, I'll show that actions do more harm then words. Until then, have a wonderful day. [Rogers wipes his hands on the grass, and makes his way back into the car. He starts the engine, and begins his path to Hollywood..] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... "Your True Life Fantasy" CARL BLAIR ...................................................... [It's Saturday morning, around 11 o'clock. The setting is outside Carl Blair's immaculate house. In the drive way, a backed in Ryder truck sits, the back of it is open, and furniture is in the process of being loaded into it. Bobby Blair comes out from the garage, he's clad in a t-shirt and jeans. Carrying a box of junk, he throws it into the back of the truck. "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair follows, clad in a "YTLF" t-shirt with blue jean shorts, and a pair of Nike shoes. He starts to bitch at Bobby.] YTLF: Don't just throw my shit around. BB: That's exactly what it was...shit. Don't worry about it I didn't break anything. YTLF: It's not shit, most of that stuff holds alot of sentimental value. [Just to be an ass, Bobby peaks into the box, grabbing something out of it, he holds it up. A jock strap, looks to have fungus growing on it.] BB: So your jock strap from twelfth grade holds sentimental value? YTLF: Your an ass...just help me get the TV. [They enter the house, a few minutes pass and out comes the TV on a dolly, it's a big screen. Obviously Carl's spoiled rotten.] BB: So anyway, where ya gonna live out it California. YTLF: CIN hooked me up with a phat apartment out in the projects. Nah I'm kidding, I'll be staying at Franklin Plaza, two bedroom one bath it ain't bad, but it sure doesn't compare to this. [Camera scrolls over to his house, WOW!] YTLF: On the count o' three, lets pick this beast up and throw it in the back. Think you can handle that? One...Two...Three... [The two grunt, as they lift. Picking it up, they set it down in the back of the truck.] YTLF: That about does it. [Carl slams the door down and latches it.] BB: So who ya face first? In CIN that is. YTLF: What's this twenty questions? Sheesh. BB: I just want the 411, you know me. YTLF: El Satanico, Brian J. Blotties managing him. BB: El Who? YTLF: El Satanico, he's your typical illegal immigrant from Mexico. Can't speak a lick of English. I say if your gonna enter MY country you atleast learn the language, right? Right. Instead he's got Brian J. Blottie, kissing his ass and praising him for all it's worth. It's nothing but a sick joke. BB: Yeah? YTLF: Yeah, supposedly he's got a sad story. But don't we all? His was drugs and alcohol, been there, done that. If it's sympathy he's asking for he sure as hell isn't going to get it. Blottie said he saw something in Satanico's eyes, and that's why he took him in. Yeah, I see something too, it's this little thing called FEAR. It's a simple four letter word and it signifies the demise of El Satanico and BJB. How's about this Brian, you can take a look into my eyes, the thing is you might not like what you see, as you'll be looking into the eyes of the future CIN Heavyweight champion... [Bobby Blair is intrigued. Walking into the garage, they enter the house. Both head towards the kitchen for a nice cold cup ice water.] BB: So this Brian Blottie, I take it you know him? [A little water drips down from Carl's chin as he chugs some water down.] YTLF: Alittle, from back in All-star Wrestling of the Motor City. We never met in action, everyone knows I woulda kicked his ass. Last week he mentioned me as "The same Carl Blair who stunk AWMC up." That's funny, there's something he needs to realize. This IS NOT the same Carl Blair, it's been two years since AWMC and alot of things have changed, my attitude has changed, most importantly I've changed. It's different now, I'm not the curtain jerker anymore. I don't open the card, I _FINISH_ the card, and that's what I got planned for Summer Blockbuster 01'... [--- Pit Stop In Paradise? ---] [ Fade in... ] [ A small white road sign quickly passes by the screen as a feminine voice calls out... ] "Rodeo Drive... Why the hell everyone makes such a fuss over this place is beyond me. Hell it’s just some street with bunch of high priced stores. The funny thing about the whole situation is that the majority of those that bust a nut over the place can’t afford the prices of the items in the shops..." [ Sarcastic laughter... ] "Yeah, but that’s something we’ll never have to worry about. But hell, Faith, you’ve got to admit it’s a hell of a lot better than some damn rodeo in Ft. Frigging Worth, Texas..." [ We suddenly zoom through the blacked out windows of a stretched white Lexus limousine. The first image we see is a pair of long sultry legs crossed over one another. The split of a white dress leads up the thigh, but yet our attention is drawn away by pendulum action of a pair of red spiked heels. ] "Yes dear thank god you didn’t take that offer when MSW relocated to Texas. Who knows what kind of diseases that a body of work such as yours could have caught especially being around a bunch of rednecks and city full of cow shit." "Frankly my dear LSW and the NWC can kiss my white ass. The six zeros at the end of the monetary amount on my contract was well more than enough to persuade the "Golden Boy" to come to Hollywood, California..." [ The scene opens more revealing the rest of the body that the long sexy legs was attached to. Long blond locks hang over the shoulders of a golden silk shirt. Beside this voluptuous blond with lips as read as blond sets none other than the world famous cruiserweight wrestling, "Golden Boy" Jake Summers. Jake flicks the tips of his fingers off his chin and turns to his beautiful wife, Faith. ] "The Golden State is a whole new start for me. The troubles, the feuds, the disappointments, the let downs... All of it has been left behind in Minnesota, but the greatest thing I must say is that I now have Jeb with me and I no longer have my parents in my hair." [ Faith glances at one of the luxurious shops then turns back to her husband. ] "That’s great dear I hope for this to be a new start for us. Perhaps with your parents no where in site our marital problems can be resolved." "Well Faith hopefully now you understand that I was telling you the truth. The truth, that my parents had indeed kept Jeb a secret from me." "Jake, lets not think about things like that. The essential part is that your son is back with you and that’s the most important thing about the whole ordeal. However right now there are other things you have to worry about. CIN is a whole new endeavor for you. For the most part all your peers are unknown to you. Well except for Rob Payne..." [ The Golden Boy shakes his head, quickly blowing off the concern that his wife has... ] "My wife, always the worrywart. Faith right now I could care less who is in the place. Whether it be some former big name or local buckwheat trying to get his fifteen minutes of fame, at this point in time each of them exist as the same person. The same target... The same result... Victims! Because by hook, crook, and any other damn way I please each of the others in this rinky-dink tournament Cicerone has stuck me in shall become greasy spots on "The Road To Glory!" [ Both husband and wife smile at each other as their two sons, Jacob and Jeb set across from them steadily occupied with Game Boy Advanced... ] "I’ve never been one to be over-confident, but when one’s opponent doesn’t give a damn about anyone then I can help think fate has dealt me an easier hand than expected. Indeed Mack Daddy..." [ Faith interrupts... ] "It’s Mac Rollins..." "Names really aren’t all that important anyhow. But nevertheless Mac don’t expect anyone to feel sympathy for you the least be me. Fact is I don’t know whether to take you from granted or thank of you as some Vietnam veteran on an acid trip flash back. Speaking of which before you continue with such, please do yourself a favor and save the time. No one, but those that you call friends give a damn if you cause some kids death or not. Indeed you could continue to question yourself over and over, but fact is that's the past and right now there’s really nothing you can do to bring his punk ass back." [ Faith rolls her eyes... ] "Jake that's really not that nice.." [ Jake laughs... ] "Who ever said I was a nice guy. Feeling sympathy for someone show weakness and in order to win this tourney I must show no mercy. Continue swimming in your sorrow if you must, but I shall shed no tears when I drop you on your head. Like it or not you’re nothing more than a stepping stone. I’ve seen your kind before. You wish for your peers to feel sorry for you, hoping that they’ll let their guard down and then you’ll be able to take advantage. Sorry macaroni, but I’m up on your game." "I know you think you're special, but face it you're just another nobody that’ll never make a name for himself. Wrestling may by a pass time that's let you take your aggression out, but to me it’s a career. One that's been very damn good to me so far and I'm not about to let things change now. So Rollins, when your step into the ring you’ll realize what many before you have already learn. There’s no damn way you can’t stop me. And you certainly can’t contain me, and deep down you know there’s no chance in hell that, you or anyone else in CIN will be able to deal with me." "If your sanity indeed depends on you being one of the last two men remaining in this tourney the unfortunately you’ll soon be in the asylum. You see when one is golden they can’t help being anything less than great. Indeed I’m not perfect, however regrettably I’m not that damn far from it!" SCOTT HALE ...................................................... [We come up into the scene.] [It's the inside of a wrestling gym. There's a really elegant ring sitting right in the middle of the room. When we say really elegant, we're talking top-of-the-line everything here. There's a couple of sets of free weights scattered about the room, and mats on the floor just about everywhere. There's some punching bags about, some jump ropes scattered on the mats, and towels strewn on the benches that line the walls. 'Course, there's no people about, but that's a mere technicality.] "You know, at first I thought this all was a big joke." "But then, I thought about it a little bit. And the more I thought about it, the more I realized, it's not a joke. It's just plain pathetic." [And the source of the voice comes in from the left. Scott Hale, looking just the same as he did the last time we saw him, except for the fact that he's wearing a full outfit this time. It's a workout outfit, a pair of sweatshorts and a tank top, both a pale gray. He's got an exercise bag in one hand, and a towel drapped across his shoulder. He walks in, keeping his gaze the ring.] "Here I am, saying to myself, `Hey, it's a shot at the big times. Hollywood. You don't get much bigger than that. Not even New York has anything on Hollywood.` A really good place to put together some competition, I thought to myself." "So, why's the narcissist convention in town?" [He shakes his head, and drops both the exercise bag and the towel at his feet, right near one of the corners of the ring. And then he starts making his way around the ring to the other side.] "If these people were any more full of themselves, it'd be pouring out of every orifice. With so many of god's gifts to women in this place, you'd think that we'd be babe capitol of the world." "Like I said, pathetic." [He walks up to one of the heavy punching bags. He does a few quick arm stretches, and takes up a boxing stance in front of it.] "You see, here's what really gets me. You've got fifteen other guys who think that right off the bat, they've got what it takes to beat out a whole bunch've people they've never seen before and grab a belt... actually, that's not what gets me. That's something that I expect right off from the start." [He takes a quick glance at the camera.] "But that's because EGO seems to be the word of the week." [And back to the bag. He starts punching that bag around like it was nothing.] "Nah. What gets me is that we have to go through this whole tourney garbage. I never liked tournaments to begin with. But at least it's set up sensably. All the fights in one night. This way, we can get a real champion... or at least as close to one as we can hope for right now." [He continues to beat up the bag for a minute before stopping, looking a little bored with it.] "Or, we can end up with a guy who sits there, waving the belt in the air screaming `I'm the Champ!`, and runs away screaming at the top of his lungs like a little girl every time someone looks at him cross-eyed." "But you know, it's not the guy who's the strongest, or the fastest, or the most experienced that's going to win this whole thing. It's going to be the guy who's the smartest. The guy who can push his ego aside and plan out the best strategy that's going to go all the way." [He motions to the heavy bag with one arm.] "An argument can be made for strength. But being able to push around a bag like that all day and night isn't going to do you much good. I could be able to lift a car on my back, and it won't really help. Because there's a million and a half ways to take care of someone's strength. Am I going to go into them all? No. Mostly because we don't have a year to sit here and listen to me babble on and on and on." [He points further down the wall, where there's one of the peanut bags hanging from it's post.] "You can brag about speed all you want. You can have all the speed and coordination in the world, and be able to make that bag rattle like a machine gun until next tuesday. But what good's speed if you can't do anything else with it? This isn't a foot race or a juggling contest. Having speed's nice, but you gotta have the muscle to back it up." [He walks away from the bag, over to the ring, and slides on it. He stands up and walks to the center.] "And experience means squat. Especially in a tournament like this. I don't care how many years you've been in this business, experience and a ten dollar bill'll buy you a cheap cup of coffee in this town." "If you want to win, you've got to know what the winning key is. What I'm talking about here is strategy. Knowing what to do, and when to do it." "And as far as I can see, I'm the only guy who's still got enough blood flowing to the right head to actually think out a good strategy." [He pulls off his tank top, and tosses it outside the ring, landing in a small pile by the rest of his gear.] "If you want to win, you've gotta plan for the worst, and hope for the best. Advice that none of my future opponents are going to take to heart." "So they'll just have to take it to the locker room after they've been swept off the mat." [He takes up a boxing stance once again.] "See you at the show." [And he begins to shadowbox.] [The shot fades out.] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... "Leading Man" COREY RYLAND ...................................................... [Fade in.] [A dark studio within CIN headquarters. Just a lone stool, and a black tarp in the room... ...we hear the faint sounds of footsteps, and the squeaking of the stool as it's adjusted. Then, sitting down infront of the camera is the man with the best profile in CIN, "Leading Man" Corey Ryland and his manager "Agent to the Stars" Ted Masterson.] CR: Communism. [Camera angle changes, like a cheap looking documentary. This time, from the side of Rylands head.] CR: The belief that all men and/or women are made equally. [The camera changes again, this time, a full on frontal shot.] CR: [Scoffs.] Hardly. [Here goes that cheezy camera work again.] CR: I'm living proof that all men aren't "equals". I think that Mr. Stukovski needs to take notice, that Corey Ryland isn't his equal but rather...his better. [The camera pulls out to show Ted Masterson standing next to his #1 client, Corey Ryland. Decked out in his scarft, directors cap, slate, and cheap looking 70's director suit. He seems almost overly enjoyed at this moment; almost orgasmic.] CR: Vlad Stukovski of RCW fame. Former champion there I hear. [Mock clap.] CR: BRA_VO. You have one great performance to your name, one time you stood infront of the masses, and had your name cheered. One time you were under the spotlight, whereas I...the worlds single _GREATEST_ natural performer live under the hot beams of the spotlight every single day... [Pause.] ...Some men burn up under the spotlight, others thrive Vlad. From the looks of your apparent fall from grace, I'd say you're rather blistered right now. [As Ryland wraps up what he has to say, he looks and nods to Ted Masterson, who starts his shilling.] TM: Ryland #1 baby! Ryland all the way! Ryland is a star baby!
