CIN (Hollywood, 2001) — Roleplay

Director’s Cut 06.21.01

22 June 2001

CINEMA SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT PRODUCTION ..................................................... 1. Will Brody 2. The Boy Hedges 3. Mac Rollins 4. El Satanico Notes: Strats due Saturday June 23 - 11:59PM Pacific Time LAST [4th] RP Packet due June 25 - 11:59PM Pacific Time ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... WILL BRODY ...................................................... [Fade in: The scene opens to a music store somewhere in San Diego, California. Save for a few employees who drift in and out of the scene, the place has been cleared out for interview purposes. As we pan down the rows of CD's, cassettes, a few magazines, headphones, and other miscellaneous music paraphernalia, we come across the reason for the cleared out area... CIN's own Will Brody. The young twentysomething is digging through a section of Pop/Rock CD's, browsing the selections. He is dressed casually in a pair of brown sandals, loose fitting khaki cargo shorts, and a white and black La Jolla High School Athletics XXL T-shirt. A pair of black sunglasses rest atop his head, rather than over his eyes. Will glances at the camera briefly, then returns his attention to the CD's.] WB: You know what I love best about music? There's something for everyone. It doesn't matter who you are or what you do, there's a style of music for you to enjoy. I mean, think about it... you've got classical, oldies, jazz, country, heavy metal, pop, punk, alternative, soft rock, techno... you got your mainstream music and your obscure, indie, or garage stuff... I could go on and on, and every different category breaks down into a thousand others, and some over lap... well, you get the point. The fact is, one way or another, there's some variety of music that can entertain ANYONE who's willing to listen. All you've got to do is open your ears. To me... wrestling is a lot like that. There's something for everyone -- young, old, male, female, it doesn't matter. Wrestling, like music, transcends all barriers... if you're willing to open your eyes. I just always thought that was one of the best reasons to be involved in this sport... the fact that you can be out there, performing, and know that somebody is watching because of you. [Will turns his attention away from the CD's and starts to walk towards the camera, which in turn begins to roll back slightly.] WB: I didn't sign with CIN because I was looking for some quick way to become famous or rich... I did it because of that love for the connection you have with the people. It's the same buzz I get with music, and that's why I'm here... and maybe that's why I don't understand a lot of my peers in this tournament. See, everybody's so sure that they're gonna win the whole thing. How many of these interviews have you seen where within the first minute, the guy's already proclaimed himself "the next CIN World Heavyweight champion." [Will laughs, shaking his head.] WB: I'm not about to do that. The fact is, I don't KNOW if I'm gonna make it past the first round, let alone win the whole thing. I'm not the kinda guy to pump himself up full of false bravado. I'll do my best, and sure, I'd like to win, but I'm not gonna make any promises I can't deliver on. There's going to be fifteen men going home losers... and I got a feeling not too many of them can handle losing very well... that they can't appreciate the positive things that happened... and that bothers me, to be honest with you. People can be downright stupid sometimes. So WHAT if you don't win the tournament? We are getting PAID to live out childhood fantasies, guys... this is what we DREAMED about when we sat in front of the television set on Saturday mornings, dropping elbows on the couch cushions... and we get to go to jam packed arenas with thousands of people there to see us perform... and maybe for some of the older guys, they forget what it feels like... but I haven't. The chill that goes down your spine from the electricity of that pop... that, folks, is why I am a professional wrestler. Hell, I know what you're thinking... the new kid is too naive. Wait 'til he gets tossed around a bit. Wait 'til he gets a taste of victory. He'll forget all about just "enjoying the pop" and become dead set on money and glory like everybody else. Screw that. [Will pauses, a slight smile across his face.] WB: The fact is, I'm not your typical wrestler. I'm not here because I get off on beating up other men to make myself feel better. I'm not here because I was too big and stupid to go to college. I _chose_ this over college, knowing I'd be able to go back when I was done living the dream... but I AM living that dream, folks, and there's nothing anybody can do to spoil that. I am here to have fun, goddammit, and that's what I'm going to do... and MAYBE, just maybe, I can convince a few people that taking things lightly isn't the worst thing they could do. Poor little Will Brody... kid doesn't know what he's getting into, right? Wrong. I'm fully aware of the situation. I don't intend to lose every match -- I'm just cognizant of the fact that somewhere along the line, losses will happen. I'm also aware that expecting perfection is a sure fire way to drive yourself crazy. So go ahead... laugh, shake your head with wonder... how the HELL can this carefree newbie survive in this rough and tough world? [Will stops, flashes a big grin.] WB: Watch and see. [With a wink, Brody turns his back to the camera, going back to the CD racks. The camera slowly backs off and then fades to black.] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... THE BOY HEDGES ...................................................... (Care to open scene? We’re in… a lobby or foyer of some sort. “Control Tower” Christopher Troisi and his charge The Boy Hedges are seated on some kinda sofa/settee type situation. Leather. Troisi is dressed in a The Boy Hedges merchandise shirt, with a powder blue blazer over the top… lovely. The Boy wears a camouflage tee-shirt and chinos. Hedges appears to be muttering to himself. Troisi smiles at the camera, and delivers his spiel) Gunner Vaughn... talk to me. I’m getting lonely here. How dare you deprive the world of your, no doubt, plentiful oral skills. Come on, please Gunner, I’m begging you- get the CIN camera crew in, and cut an amazing promo. A godlike promo. A promo that will have the internet dirtsheets creaming themselves for weeks to come. Please do that Gunner. Because that’ll be the last ever chance you’ll have for stardom. At Premiere, your career takes what us in the managerial business know as a “TWA 800 Manoeuvre”. The man forcing you to go down that way- The Boy Hedges. (Camera focuses in on Hedges, who is quietly singing “I Touch Myself” by the Divinyls under his breath. Camera stays on him as Troisi speaks over “I love you, I don’t want anybody else, and when I think about you, I touch myself. Oooh. Ooooh. Oooooh. Aaaaaahhhhh” etc etc) Gunner- I know why you’re not speaking to bimbos #1 or #2 on our interviewer roster. It’s because, wherever you are at the moment, you are wondering what the hell you go yourself in for. You were thinking that maybe you’d come to CIN, have a nice easy win over Scott Hale in the first round, destroy Jaime Roberts in the second round… you thought you had a good chance of making something of your career. Hedges is going to make something of your career Gunner. A stress relief toy. He’s going to take it in his hands, and bash it about so much… you’d best have good dental records, that’s all I can say. Alternatively, we could take samples of your saliva off of the sheep you keep in your garden. Whatever’s best for you really. Just look at this man! Look at him! Some people call him the space cowboy, some call him the gangster of love. I call him the man that’s going to shutdown the talent in this federation like Napster. It’s ironic that we’re calling the event Premiere when my man here is going to be responsible for the endings of at least four careers… and, hey, he might even turn up and end a few more for the craic. Or, in Jaime Roberts case, for safety of people who can actually rather enjoy the English language, and don’t like to see it butchered by some Ricky Martin-idolising jester. We, me and Hedges, we really were hoping for a bit of a challenge here. Seriously. We chose CIN as we thought that this fed might take longer than, say, a week to lay complete waste to. Chance would be a fine thing... Gunner- I’d really panic if I was you. Seriously. I’d be on the next flight out of whatever godforsaken hellhole you come from. That’d do two things: firstly, it would increase the property value of your area no end. Secondly, it’d mean you wouldn’t have to have the Hedges Experience. Gunner, The Boy here is going to make an example of you. He’s gonna literally snap you in half with every Goddamn plancha, pescado, tope, rana, splash, and dive in the book. He’s going to destroy you. Not out of maliciousness- but to prove a point to everyone else in the back. We’re gonna beat you so hard, that every other guy on the roster is going to refuse to fight Hedges. Satanico, Blair, Hale- it doesn’t matter. None of them can withstand the Hedges Experience. It’s a goddamn maeltstorm. We beat you around, everyone else refuses to wrestle Hedges, we win the world title in about 20 minutes, and we get home in time for Ally McBeal. It’s the one where Billy dies, I believe… (Camera pans back to Troisi, a smug smile on his smug face) Are we all instructed now? Nothing can stop this man. Heaven, hell, high water, or Carl Blair promo. Nothing. Not a single thing. So, Gunner- get that scriptwriter in. You’ll need it. (And Fade) ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... "Truck" MAC ROLLINS ...................................................... [Black.] [The scene materializes effortlessly from the obligatory darkness and the sheer intensity with which the early afternoon Californian sun takes it over is almost blinding. The local is upbeat and fast moving as most of Hollywood is known to be. There are busy streets and crowded sidewalks in every direction, for as far as the eye can see. The camera however pans to the left and becomes fixated on one sign, which rests nonchalantly amidst all the others. The camera zooms in a few feet, allowing us to read the writing more clearly. With a sudden fade the scene shifts and we’re apparently now inside the small building on which the sign apparently sits.] [Welcome everyone, to the BVI (that’s short for Ben Van Iten for all you uneducated simpletons out there) sports bar and grill – downtown Hollywood location. The décor is simple, but elegant. Picture your local Applebee’s or Lonestar, and as with them, the theme here is obvious. Hanging on the wall are glossy action shots of Ben Van Iten in all his in-ring glory, BVI jerseys, t-shirts, replica NWC championship belts, and even a favorite of the Tower of Mental Power, Chester. This t-shirt reads “The Idol and I play catch.” Anyways, you get the point. Also, noticeable is a slight Hawaiian theme. The waitresses have on coconut shell bikini tops, grass hula skirts, and rings of flowers crown their heads. Oh.. did I mention the big BVI = HIW mural on the ceiling. So, it’s not exactly the Vatican, but hey, it aint bad.] [Panning to the left, the camera comes to rest on a man known to a small portion of Southern California as Truck Rollins – to everyone else he’s just some guy named Mac. Truck sits high aloft a stool, leaning across the bar. His money is in his hand as he frantically tries to flag down the bartender amongst the madness. He has little success. So I’ll take this little window to tell you what the big, bad man is wearing. A black wife beater is pulled taught against his chest to the point that the fabric is stretched nearly to its breaking point. A pair of faded blue jeans cover his lower torso, and generic ‘Doc Martin’ STYLE boots completes the inept ensemble. Suddenly, a dainty little bartender enters the picture and our scene finally springs to life.] Bartender: What can I get you? Mac: I’ll have another Killians Red and a martini on ice. [She takes his money and retreats behind the counter as fast as she came, muttering only one final..] Bartender: Sure thing, sweetie. Off-screen Voice: So anyways, this wrestling business you say you’re in, you any good? [Snap. The camera darts 45 degrees to the left revealing a gruff old man. He’s small. Maybe 5’8”, 145 lbs. It’s obvious that this is a conversation that’s been going on for a while and we’re just entering in the middle of it.] Mac: Well, it’s tough to say, man. I’ve had my share of success to this point, but that pails in comparison to some of the other guys that’ve landed themselves in CIN. [Pause] Maybe I’ll make it, maybe I’ll fall flat on my face. Who knows? [Shrugs] Old Man: CIN? Neverheardofit. Used to watch a little GSW back in the old days. I’m sure you’ll do fine though. You’re big, you’re healthy, and ya look strong as an ox. Mac: Thanks, Benny. [FINALLY, the waitress returns to Mac, placing his drinks in front of him. He smiles cordially as she walks away.] Mac: I gotta admit that so far, I’m not terribly impressed with what I’ve seen on Directors Cut. Though, there’s only been two shows and I’m sure most’ve my peers would say much the same. I won’t find out what I’m really up against until we get in the ring. Benny: Smart lad. Never judge a book by its cover. That’s what I always say. Mac: Yeah, I know. But so far everyone’s been spewing the same garbage. It seems they all want to argue about who’s the sexiest.. or just totally write everyone else off as untalented and unthreatening to “THEIR” reigns as CIN heavyweight champion. The denial of some of these guys is amazing. Mac: I mean, take me for example, I’m pretty confident of my abilities in the ring. But I’m like most of the other guys in that I’m not familiar with most of the competition in this tournament. So I acknowledge the possibility of someone in this tournament being just a little bit better than me. It’d be foolish to just rush in, assuming that I’m the be all end all of what professional wrestling is supposed to be about. Mac: Unfortunately though, my peers ALL seem to be hiding under some mystical guise -- convinced of their invincibility. I’ll tell you one thing though, at the very least, fifteen proud men are going to be proven wrong in one night. One of those men, Benny is a former member of the National Wrestling Council. A former junior-crown champion, who likes to think he’s gonna drop me on my head. Benny: Junior crown? Mac: Yeah. It’s for the lightweights. Benny: Lightweights? Is ‘at boy nuts? Mac: I dunno if he’s checked the roster sheet or not, but he’s at just a little bit of a size disadvantage. Though, I can’t wait for him to come out here, pounding his chest and screaming about how size doesn’t matter and how he’s beat men bigger and stronger than me before. It’ll give me something to laugh about. I might even smile for once. Benny: That’d be a sight for sore eyes. Mac: [chuckle] Speaking of which, that woman of Jake’s is quite the looker. Real nice lady too. Benny: Oh yeah? Mac: Yeah. It’s just too bad she’s tied to such an ignorant sonofabitch! Benny: What d’ya mean? Mac: Mr. Summers seems to be caught up in the idea that I want people to feel sorry for me. When really, it is him that’s worried about his mystique. I could care less what people think of me, but Jake.. Jake parades around in fancy limousines and speaks freely about the “The six zeros at the end of the monetary amount on his contract.” It’s quite intriguing really, that someone so materialistic and prissy makes his living in a sport like this. Hell, I found it funny that a man that lady-like works in a sport that’s gonna get that pretty little face of his all busted up. It’s ironic in the truest sense of the word. Benny: Does seem a little odd. Mac: Nah, Benny. What’s odd is why a man like Jake Summers, a man who’s been everywhere, done everything, etc. etc. would refer to me as a stepping stone only –moments- after making such a profound statement as to say that I’m somebody who’s never going to amount to anything in this business. I’m paraphrasing of course. BUT, if I’m just a nobody who’s not going anywhere, where exactly is he planning on getting by beating me? How is beating a simple peon like Truck Rollins going to further a man like Jake Summers career? Benny: No idea. Mac: Exactly. What I think Jake is forgetting here is that he’s the one with everything to lose, not me. I’ve already lost everything I care about. Now, it’s just about me and tryin’ to hold on to my last ounce of self-respect. I think it’s funny that Jake and everyone else seems to have taken my comment about my sanity being at stake in this tournament so literally. I mean, puh-lease, I’m pathetic and all that.. But it was metaphorically spoken. It’s a wrestling match, nothing more, nothing less. I’m not going to the asylum anytime soon.. win OR lose. [A few moments of silence and then.] Mac: Well, it’s been fun Benny. But I’ve got get some shut-eye tonight. I start training tomorrow. Benny: Another case, eh? Mac: Yeah, Benny, another case. [Flash!] [With that our scene goes down, but quickly makes its way back up as Mac stumbles – and I mean STUMBLES from BVI’s fine establishment. He can barely walk on this brisk summer night.] [CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!] [Mac crumples into a little ball on the ground.] [Enter ‘The Sensation’ Eddie Valentine] [CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!] [The cracking is the bending of a ‘No Parking’ sign as it collides with the skull of Mac Rollins.] [CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!] [CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!] [CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!] [Eddie turns; he no longer has his profile to the camera. He glares directly into the camera.] Eddie Valentine: The alcohol wasn’t enough to slow this truck down, but somebody had to stop that goddamn rambling!!! [Fade to black] ....................DIRECTOR'S CUT.................... EL SATANICO ...................................................... [We open the scene on two eyes, a nose, and a red and black mask staring directly into the camera.] Voice from off-camera: Satanico, back off a bit. [The voice is recognized as that of "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie, who appears in the scene as El Satanico turns, takes a few steps back, then turns back to the camera. The scene? The pool, of course.] BJB: Some people might find it amusing to stick their ugly mugs as close to the camera as possible and act tough and whatnot. But all it does is make you look foolish, as was just demonstrated a few seconds ago. Then again, isn't that wrestling in general? Pimping, preening, posing, and making yourself look as tough as possible, even though you're just as tough as the next guy to walk out of a wrestling school. Carl Blair is one of those men who enjoys to get his face shoved on camera as much as possible. And if there is any man who exemplifies the statement "ugly mug", it's Blair. [DA SMIRK~!] BJB: But I'm not here to talk about Blair's social life, or lack thereof. No, I'm here because I've noticed that a lot of internet sites have taken an interest in our fair little promotion here, and they've started to post their "odds" on who will win this thing. So I decided to get in on the act myself, and throw out there what _I_ think of the field of competitors here. And by "I", I mean "we". As in Satanico and myself. Because whatever I think, he thinks. It's just one of those things you'll have to learn to accept. [More smirking from Blottie. More... nothing from Satanico.] BJB: So, let's get down to it. We'll start with El Satanico's first round opponent, a man who's ego precedes itself, Carl Blair. Blair is coming into this thing with his ego, his wrestling "skill", and his overwhelming attitude. That's about all he has going for him. He's out in the first round. [Can you say... smirk?] BJB: Next, we can pretty much eliminate several of the competitors right off the bat. Jeff Weinerth? Jake Summers? Rob Payne? Eddie Valentin? Hrgh. Now I know why they accepted Satanico without any second thoughts. All of these men's names can be tosses away now, because they're not making it anywhere near the finals. [A tossing motion with Blottie's hands, as Satanico stares at Blottie's hand motions, intrigued.] BJB: What about those men that _could_ possibly make it? Well, we got The Boy Hedges for starters... heh. Ok, maybe I forgot a name from the last catagory. But I do see a bit of spunk behind Hedges, so he might upset. I'd give him hundred to one odds in this thing. Gunner Vaughn, on the otherhand, might just turn Hedges' hopes and dreams for a loop, as Vaughn is a mean son-of-a-gun, and definitely has what it takes... to get past Hedges. After that, he's got a long road ahead of him, and not enough gas. His odds are the same as Hedges', one hundred to one. [El Satanico suddenly takes a few steps forward and whispers something into Blottie's ear.] BJB: Huh? El Swimmo? I guess... [Satanico's demeanor perks up a bit, as he runs away from Blottie and dives into the water, a large splash erupting from the pool on impact. Blottie shakes his head.] BJB: Well, at least it's good cardiovascular training. Anyway... back to where I was. Vlad Stukovski is the Russian competitor in this thing, and you can never count out the Russian. They're always pesky, they're always big, and they're always willing to tear you to bits and pieces. Corey Ryland has his work cut out for him, but I really do see the kid pulling one out over the Russian man-beast. So Stukovski is placed at fifty to one, while Ryland is twenty to one. [Smirk, as Blottie reaches down and picks something up off the ground. It's a white piece of cardboard with four words written across it... "Scott Hale Sucks Penis".] BJB: 'Nuff said. [Blottie drops the sign, as we hear and see El Satanico swimming in the background... still fully dressed in his luchadore mask and costume.] BJB: Mac Rollins would be an odds on favorite if it weren't for his monicre. Who the hell calls themselves "Truck". Well, just like a certain "Cold Locomotive", this engine's going to run out of steam before he gets going. Plus, he has the enviable task of trying to get past Tripp Shade in the second round. Good luck, chump. Seventy-five to one odds. Will Brody rocks my socks, and if anyone can beat Shade, it's him. Five to one odds, bay-bee. Unfortunately, that leaves Drifter out in the cold. Syanara, sucker. Fifty to one odds. [Smirks are on the house tonight!] BJB: And finally, my favorite two men for this tournament... Seth Rogers and Tripp Shade. Both of these men entertain me quite a bit, and on top of it, they're both top notch athletes. Sure, Shade is ego-driven, but he deserves that ego, because he's one of the best young prospects in this sport. Seth Rogers himself is a quickly rising star who will probably end up putting this small California fed on the map. These men should be on seperate sides of the brackets, but since they face off in the first round with each other... Shade gets two to one odds, Rogers gets three to one. That's how good I think these men are. [And now the smirk changes to that of a quizzical glance.] BJB: Am I forgetting anyone? [Satanico stops swimming and starts waving his arms in the air.] BJB: Hm. I don't think I am. But I'm not sure. [More waving, and now splashing.] BJB: Keep it down, man! I'm trying to concentrate! [Satanico shrugs, and re-commences swimming.] BJB: Eh, I guess not. There are _my_ picks for this tournament, with odds and everything. Damn, screw Michigan. I should move to Vegas. While I think about that, you go run along and do something useful... like clean the gutters or something. Chow. [Blottie walks off, which leaves the camera focused on El Satanico, who appears to be doggy paddling. Fade to black.] ......................................................
