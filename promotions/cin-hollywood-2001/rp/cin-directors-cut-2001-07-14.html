WILL BRODY ..................................................... [Black.] VO: And so it begins... [Fade in: The scene opens to a skating park somewhere in Southern California. The first is to himself... to be the best that he can be, to push himself to the peak of his abilities, to strive to be number one... and number two, he has a duty to the fans... the audience... to entertain them however he sees fit. Whether we like it or not, this business isn't just about _us_, the wrestlers... it's about the people watching at home or in the arena. We're not in this just for our own greedy, selfish, self-indulgant pleasures... at least, I hope not. We're here because we love to entertain. Yeah, some wrestle for the money, or the fame, or for the physicality... but deep down, beyond their personal reasons, we all share a common bond... we are the ones who have a connection with a thousand unknown faces... we are the ones with the power to make a sea of people rise to their feet... we are the ones who can ignite the passion of a crowd... it's something special. It's something we have on our fingertips, this ability... this opportunity... to make a connection. I don't know about the rest of you... but I _live_ for that connection. Would I have liked to do better? Sure. So why am I so happy? I got to perform in front of a live audience... doing something I have dreamed about since I was a little boy. How the hell can you walk away from that and _not_ be happy? I'm living a childhood fantasy that most people dream about, think about, talk about, but never _do_... I consider myself pretty damn lucky to be able to do what I love for a living. I also got to make that connection with the crowd... does the average person know what it's like to look into a sea of thousands of people and hear them cheering for you... do they know what that feels like?
