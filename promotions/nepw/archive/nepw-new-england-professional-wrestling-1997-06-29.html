[NEPW] New England Professional Wrestling

29 June 1997

Hey everyone, [name withheld] here, once again starting a brand new e-fed. :) I am former WFSW president, as well as former WCOIW co-president. I also handle several e-wrestlers, such as Hazard, the Sanctuary (Invader/Olvan/Destroyer), "Rude Dude" Eugene Skidmore, and the now-retired Vegas Fighters. NEPW is almost ready to go! We have a decent number of handlers, but we still need a few heel wrestlers as well as one or two more tag teams. This doesn't mean you can't still enter face wrestlers, however. We are still completely open to new wrestlers. Anyway, for those of you who don't have time to read the handbook, here is a small summary of what the fed will be like. NEPW is New England Professional Wrestling, a regional, independant-type fed based in the New England area. It's completely booked, with pushes decided by heat, angles, and quality of rp's. I look at _all three_ of those things when deciding who gets pushed/who wins and who doesn't. In order to enforce "quality over quantity", there is a three rp per week limit-- this will be further explained later. This rule is in effect because a) not everyone has the time to rp every day, and b) more time can be spent on rp's, since you won't have to worry about pumping out 10 rp's a day. And in most cases, more time equals more quality. As for the fed itself, there are two shows per week-- Monday Night Assault and NEPW Explosion. Assault is on Monday (of course) and Explosion is every Friday. Assault is the NEPW's "big" weekly show, with 5-7 live matches, surprises, in-ring interviews, etc. All the stuff you'd expect from a cool card. Explosion is more of a news/interview type show, with 3-4 dark matches, late-breaking news, rumors, schedules, and updates. Also during the week, there will be standard Updates and Roleplays will be sent in packets whenever I feel that I have enough to send them out. NEPW has three titles-- the NEPW New England title (the top title), NEPW Massachusetts State title (secondary title), and NEPW Tag Team titles. We're not sure how they will be decided yet... and you'll _never_ know unless you join! :) There are four supercards per year-- not PPVs, since we don't have enough money to pay for those. These supercards will each be very prestigious, and two of the four (Mystery Date and Survival of the Fittest) have _very_ interesting, original events at them-- check out the handbook. Well, there isn't too much else to say about the fed, except that I really feel that this will be the best fed I've ever headed, and that it will be a ton of fun for all who are involved. I give you my word that I will try my hardest to make the NEPW the best it can possibly be-- and anybody who knows me knows that when I try my hardest, good things happen. You said it, not me. :) I have experience, and I have a ton of great ideas that I plan on using in the NEPW. I really hope you decide to join. Anyway, following this is the handbook/creation sheet. Creation Sheet ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I. Introduction - What is NEPW? NEPW stands for New England Professional Wrestling. It is a regional fed covering all of New England, including Massachusetts, Connecticut, Maine, New Hampshire, and Vermont. The NEPW strives to be the best fed it can possibly be. I promise you (you meaning _every single handler_) that I will work my ass off _whenever I can_ to make this fed fun for everyone. I want to have a blast with this and I want you to have a blast, too. NEPW's style of wrestling will probably remind you of old NWA stuff more than anything you see today. It breaks away from lame aspects of wrestling like stupid gimmicks, bad angles, etc. and tries to put out a product that is both entertaining and interesting. I don't want to say that NEPW is one thing, and have it be limited to that. I don't want to say it's not "hardcore" or "extreme", because at times it may get that way. I also don't want to say that it's a technical wonderland because there are times when I may want to break away from that also. What I'm trying to say is that I don't want the NEPW to be categorized, because it will be spontaneous and basically anything can and will happen. I will try to make it a good mix of every aspect of wrestling that I think is fun. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ II. NEPW's Booking System The NEPW is run on the [name withheld] Booking System... meaning, I decide who gets pushed and who doesn't, who wins and who doesn't, etc. However, I am not biased towards or against anyone. If you contribute, write good flashes with decent consistency, and try to create good angles for your characters, you're pretty much guaranteed a push. If you can't deal with this, don't join my fed. Also, don't join if you're going to cry about losing. I _CANNOT STAND_ that one bit. If it happened, there was probably a good reason why it happened. That's all I'm going to say. I will sooner remove your wrestler then argue over something stupid. Sorry if all that sounds kind of harsh... just had to get it out of the way. :) And I'm sure most of you know what I mean anyway. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ IV. Cards NEPW has two cards per week. The bigger of the two comes on ever Monday and is called NEPW Monday Night Assault. It is a live show and will probably have 5-7 matches on it per week, plus in-ring interviews, and plenty of surprises and other things to keep you interested. :) Your hosts are Timothy Hyatt (face), Sasha Lee (heelish neutral), and Steve Orbit (heel). The second card is on Friday and is called NEPW Explosion. It's more of a news/update type show. It will have 3-4 dark matches and _possibly_ 1 PBP match. It will also have an interview/flash section. It is hosted by Jessica Hughes (neutral) and Bryan Clark (faceish neutral). Roleplays will be sent in packets whenever I feel I have enough to send out. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ V. PPVs Well, due to the fact that the NEPW is a smaller independent organization, it can't really afford to do PPVs. But, no fed would be complete without supercards, so of course we have those: ==================== * FEBRUARY: NEPW Mystery Date - This is one of the NEPW's most prestigious supercards. It houses the Mystery Date tournament, which is run like this: ROUND ONE: Eight names are picked from the Heavyweight Division. Four random teams are made and there are two tag team matches. The two losing teams are eliminated from the tourney, and the four members of the two winning teams advance to the second round. ROUND TWO: The second round is a four-way elimination match... however, only two men will be eliminated. The two "survivors" will go on to the final round. FINAL ROUND: The final round is a bunker match designed keeping the Valentine's Day spirit alive. It takes place inside a roofed steel cage. Attached to the top of all four corners of the cage will be a heart shaped box with a weapon inside. Once the weapon is out of the box and in the ring, it can be used. The only way to win this match is by pinfall. There is no time limit, no dq, and no countout. ==================== * May: NEPW Mayhem - Nothing special, just a plain ol' supercard. Expect great matches at this one, all titles on the line, and of course the World Champion will defend against the winner of the Mystery Date tournament. ==================== * August: NEPW Survival of the Fittest - SotF houses one of the most dangerous matches in wrestling history. It is called the Tower of Terror match and it is set up like this: Three cages, stacked on top of each other. The first is on the bottom and is standard side (15 feet high). On top of that is one that's a bit smaller and 10 feet high. The third is even smaller and 7 feet high. And in the middle, there's a 35 foot ladder that goes all the way to the roof of the tower. It looks like this: | <--- ladder ###|## # | # #####|#### # | # <--- cage # | # #######|###### floor # | # | #|=====|====|# <--- ring \ / #|~~~~~~~~~~|# ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Now, it starts out with 20 men in the bottom cage. The match starts, and they do battle for 15 minutes. At the 15 minute mark, a buzzer sounds. The door to the second cage is then opened for 60 seconds. The wrestlers must try their best to make it up the ladder and into the next cage before the 60 seconds are up, because after that, whoever remains in the bottom cage is eliminated. The wrestlers who made it to the second cage must now brawl for 10 minutes. After 10 minutes, the door to the third cage is opened for 30 seconds. The wrestlers try their best to make it to the third cage, and when the 30 seconds is up, the door is closed and whoever's still in the second cage is eliminated. Now whoever is left in the match has 5 minutes to brawl until the door to the roof is opened... and after that, there's only one thing left to do, and that's reach the floor before anybody else, because the only way to win this match is to be the one to touch the floor before anybody else. And the winner of this match... well, he gets to be the one who can brag about winning such an awesome match. :) Oh ya, he also gets a guaranteed World Title shot, whenever he wants it, in whatever kind of stip match he wants. ==================== * November: NEPW Armegeddon - Armegeddon has no special tournaments or anything like that, but it will just be an awesome card (like Mayhem). We're talking stip matches, war games, major feud enders, title matches-- this will just be an awesome card. Things you never though could happen will happen here. I guarantee it. ==================== ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ VI. NEPW Championship Titles NEPW has several championship titles. As NEPW is a smaller regional fed, none of the titles have "World" status. * NEPW New England Championship [top singles title] * NEPW Massachusetts State Championship [secondary singles] * NEPW Tag Team Championship ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ VII. NEPW Characters The NEPW has quite a few entertaining people on the payroll. They all work as either commentators, hosts or interviewers. Here is an intruduction to all of them. * Paul Jerden - 44 year-old NEPW President. Former big time face wrestler, using some of the cash he made during his wrestling career to start up a promotion of his own. He's still over with the fans, and he's still in pretty good shape. * Timothy Hyatt - The NEPW's lead broadcaster for NEPW Monday Night Assault. He does mostly play by play with a little bit of color. He is fairly unbias, but is a big fan of sportsmanship. * Sasha Lee - Extremely attractive broadcaster on NEPW Monday Night Assault. She is very outspoken-- not necessarily a heel, but not a face either (she's a bit like Sunny, only a bit less of a flirt). Mostly just a color commentator. * Steve Orbit - Another broadcaster on NEPW Monday Night Assault. Orbit is a former wrestler who was never really that great in the ring, but always a big talker. He claims to have an injury that keeps him out of the ring but nobody really believes him. He makes a lot of wisecracks about Hyatt, unsuccessfully tries to flirt with Lee, and insults faces and any wrestlers he doesn't like. * Bryan Clark - Co-host of NEPW Explosion. He's a young guy who's new to the sport but a great host nonetheless. He's confident and a great asset to the NEPW team. Most people agree that he has quite a future. * Jessica Hughes - Jess is the other host of NEPW Explosion. She loves attention and loves to be on camera, and often wears clothes that really show off her "assets" (she's often referred to as Jessica Huge-- you figure out why). She's constantly flirting with Bryan. One would question why she was hired... the answer, she is an _excellent_ interviewer and is not afraid to pry or ask any questions she wants to. She's hated by most female wrestling fans, but loved by most of the male ones. * Ed White - The NEPW's head referee. He's a very experienced ring official and will always make the best calls he can. * Mark Hanson - The ring announcer/time keeper. A long-time wrestling fan who's always wanted to get some kind of job in the sport. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ VIII.
