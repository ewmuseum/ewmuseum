UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING FEDERATION OPENS!

12 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, My name is [name withheld]. I used to run the EGWF and the WWCWF. Well, you have probably heard of one of the two. Anyway, I am looking to start up a new fed, but it will be VERY different from others. Included under this introduction is and Outline, Please read over it and distribute this to anyone you know who is into e-wrestling. I have big plans for this fed, so please, if you are interested, respond. USCWF GENERAL INFORMATION ------------------------- *For one we will have 2 cards a week. We will also have a Pay-Per-View once every two months, this way, PPV'S become sacred again, like they used to be. I believe this will make PPV'S in the USCWF more prestigous than any other PPV'S out there. *There will flat out only be three titles in the USCWF. One World Title for the Singles and one United States Title for Singles as well as One World Title for the Tag Teams. This way, if you are a contender, or a champion, you must be working hard. To be a contender in this fed I am hoping it will be difficult, this way, only the most dedicated will be contenders. *Everyone singles wrestler or team is limited to one roleplay a day. Roleplays here, as in every other booked fed, are based on quality not quantity! We will not have a web page, so we will be able to focus more on the fed and not a web page. USCWF STAFF ----------- *For one, I, [name withheld], is NOT the president. I am the comissoner of the USCWF, and I plan to do big things. First off there is the hiring of the staff, I am looking for one great president, 6-8 great writers, and about 25-30 singles wrestlers as well as 5-15 tag teams. You can be a writer and a roleplayer, but if your a prez, thats all you can be. *The presidents job is to simply book matches along with the commisoner as well as deal with any questions, comments, or problems the wrestlers of the USCWF might have. The writers are there to write matches, they will be instructed ahead of time on who should win, and they will probably need to write 2-3 matches a week. NEEDED: 1 PRESIDENT 6-8 DEDICATED WRITERS 25-30 SINGLES WRESTLERS 15-20 TAG TEAMS I promise you all as the commissoner, I will be looking out for whats best for the fed. As soon as I see someone slacking off, whether it's a prez, a writer, or a roleplayer, they will be fired.... period. This federation is about dedicated people and I hope this is you! USCWF MATCHES ETC. ------------------ * Matches here in the USCWF will be very well orgainized and written to say the least, if you have an idea, don't be afraid to send it to the commisoner, and he will see what he can do. This federation is for the people and if you choose to have your wrestler wager his carrer in a match, it's your buisness, we will NOT stop you. *Overall in this fed, what the staff decideds goes, but again, if you ever have an idea of any sort, please mail the commisoner. If you are interested in this prosporus federation, please mail me with one of these 3 sentences as the subject to [detail withheld] President: I would like to apply Writer: I would like to apply Wrestler: I would like to apply Again, you can be a writer and a prez, but you can only be a prez by itself, I assure you, whoever is chosen to be on the elite staff of the USCWF is top notch. The hardest part now, is getting off the ground, this is where you come in, we need YOU!!!! So if you are interested, please inquire by mailing a response to the commisoner at [detail withheld] . And if you now anyone who like e-wrestling, please pass this on. Again, if you have any questions, please, don't be shy to ask. Thanks for your time.... Sincerly, [name withheld] ([detail withheld])
