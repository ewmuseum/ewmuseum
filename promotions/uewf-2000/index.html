Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Ultimate Extreme Wrestling Federation, hosted on its own website, surfaces in March 2000 already a week from its first pay-per-view, BackStab 2000. Its owner posted that he was the fed's only card writer, calling it “very tideous to write two six match cards a week and keep them good,” and that roleplays were being folded into two existing shows, Warfare and Sizzle, rather than a planned separate flash-packet show, Wired, for want of enough talent. The same post describes wrestler, crowd and staff graphics being built with Poser 4.

By 18 March the fed was in “heavy preparation” for an April follow-up pay-per-view, Armageddon, and had signed members from “the now defunct Gyeah Wrestling” to appear on it.

The federation relaunched that October, recruiting for itself and a new farm promotion, “UEWF 2,” and asking for five outside fed heads “not currently involved with another fed” to sit on a new Board of Directors writing cards and articles. It offered free e-mail on its own domain and promised a place on its next pay-per-view, Slamaphobia, to anyone who joined; roleplays still moved through cards and flash packets, with a message board planned but not yet running.

No card result, roster or title record survives from either the spring or the October run, and no document later than the relaunch notice is held.
