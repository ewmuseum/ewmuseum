Role Players Wanted

8 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah, yeah, yeah, yet another E-Wrestling federation has formed. Why should you join my federation, which is a booked league? Well, three reasons why you should give this federation a shot. 1, I have been watching wrestling on TV, PPV, and in person for more than 20 years now, ranging from the AWA, NWA, WWC to WWF, WCW, ECW (which is the best I have seen in years IMHO.) I have a pretty good idea about how to call a match, and develop story lines and angles. 2, I am very dedicated to my craft and so is my "working their asses off" crew. and lastly 3, I'm an experienced role player when it comes to role playing. From that standpoint, I can relate. Besides, aren't you frustrated w/some federation which folded after two months of operation? I sure am. But, there's more. As a handler in my federation, Concilio de Lucha Fantasia del Mundo, which is World Council of Fantasy Wrestling in Spanish, CLFM for short, you will receive a weekly newletter, "Latest Happenings in the CLFM". In that newsletter, you will receive the latest breaking news in the CLFM. Also, ratings in the single and tag-team ranks, and for an extra bonus, I would ask you (chosen in random) to do a guest editorial for that issue. The editorial is a no-holds barred segment, but it must be done in good taste. Also, I will choose another wrestler or manager, at random, for an interview given by one of my member of "The Crew". There will be a live taping every Saturdays, "Saturdays Night at the Fights", except for the week that a PPV will take place, which is every 3 months w/two months notice. The titles up for grabs are in the following: CLFM World heavyweight Title CLFM TV Title CLFM Light Heavyweight Title (Wrestler's weight can't exceed 235 lbs.) CLFM World Tag-Team Title and CLFM National Tag Team Title LIMITS: I will have 10 tag-teams and 30 single wrestlers (with at least 10 being a Light Weight Wrestler.) To Participate, email me at this address with this form, please, no attachments. Everyone is limited to two wrestlers. Whether you want to form them into a tag team is totally up to you. NOTE!!!!! Keep this as real as possible. I don't want my federation to be flooded w/a multiple of The Undertaker or Ric Flair. Also, no out-of-this-world gimmicks. All applicants who has something missing in their form of ignore the last 2 requests, I will return their application unproccessed!! NO EXCEPTION!!!! Your Name: Your Email Address: Wrestler's name: Height (No shorter than 5'6" or taller than 7'4"): Weight (No lighter than 175 lbs or heavier than 600 lbs): Wrestler's Residence: Ring Attire: Ring Entrance (What he does on his way to the ring): Entrance Song: Personality: Gimmicks: Valet/Manager (Optional) Ten Regular Moves: Finisher: Description of Finisher: Alternative Finisher: Description of Finisher: Heel/Face: Style (Brawler, Aerial, Daredevil, Technical, Power, Agile, Power, Speed): If filing two wrestlers, send a separate sheet for that wrestler. Is wrestler #2 a tag team partner? (y/n): If yes, complete the following. Tag Team Name: Entrance Music: Five Double Team Moves: Finisher: Description of Finisher: Alternative Finisher: Description of Finisher: Well, there you have it. The card will be done in English. :-) Well, I am looking forward to hearing from you. CLFM CEO [name withheld]
