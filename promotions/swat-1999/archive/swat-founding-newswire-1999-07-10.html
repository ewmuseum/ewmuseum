SWAT — News

Regional Wrestling at its Best

10 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AUSTIN, TEXAS [AP NEWSWIRE] - On July 7th, 1999, legendary wrestler
Denny "Blackjack" Langham announced to the city of Austin and the wrestling world in general the finalization of his plans to bring high- caliber wrestling back to the great state of Texas. The result -
SHOTGUN WRESTLING OF AUSTIN, TEXAS - or SWAT, for short.
"Damn straight I'm happy ta be bringin' wrasslin' back ta Austin," said the life-long West Texas native. "It's high time we show them boys up north how it's REALLY done!"
Funded by the proceeds of his wrestling career along with community- based support and donations, Langham looks to get SWAT started by signing a small number of free agent talent to build the roster.
Langham is currently in negotiations with a few talented performers. "Once this here thing gets started, it's gonna be hotter'n hell on the
Fourth of Ju-ly . . ." said Langham from his office in Austin.
Only time will tell. SWAT hopes to begin operations near the first week of August.
