[EWF] Breakdown 4/26 LIVE! From Las Vegas, Nevada..ONE WEEK BEFORE ISOLATION

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[this page is best viewed when maximized] BRIEF RESULTS BREAKDOWN ON THE TBS SUPERSTATION! LIVE from the Thomas and Mack Center in Las Vegas, NV 16,181 People Pack the Arena April 26, 2002 9:00 PM The broadcast begins with an opening video summarizing the end of last week's show. It focuses on Spent pinning Ash and then we fade into the live
Thomas and Mack Center with a great crowd of 16,000+ fans. Todd Helton and Jay Pauly welcome us to the show and tell us that we will be hearing from all 4 of the World Title contenders tonight plus there will be some more
Isolation updates. They then inform us that Hard Core will be facing Sly Vester up next. Both Hard Core and Madden of the Canadian Connection enter the arena and walk to the ring. The crowd boo's them as if they were criminals. Then, Sly Vester makes his way down to the ring quickly as he begins the match with a couple faithful dropkicks. It was a very surprisingly fast paced match with many offensive spurts coming from both men. At one point Sly had Hardcore set up for the Sly Guy Bomb, but Madden from the outside tripped him and then Hard Core took the advantage. As they tailed to the end of the match, both men became a little tired. Sly gained the advantage with a side russian legsweep. He once againt attempted to nail the Sly Guy Bomb but Hard Core got out of it. Vester then whipped him into the ropes and nailed a lariat clothesline. After the impact, Vester couldn't stop and accidentally ran into the ref, knocking him down. Vester then looked as if he had no idea what to do. As he was trying to revive the referee, Pawn ran down to the ring with a chair in hand. Pawn got onto the apron and Sly Vester tried to get him down, Pawn swung the chair nailing
Vester in the head knocking him straight down to the mat. The crowd was loving it and cheered Pawn all the way. Hard Core crawled over to Vester's body and draped one arm over the body as the ref came to and began to count,
1....2...NO! Vester kicked out at 2.5. Pawn already at this time was at the top of the aisle way and was shocked to see Vester kick out. Pawn then went to the back. Hard Core was just as surprised. As both men got up and to their feet, Hard Core attempted to nail a ddt on Vester but he countered it and locked on a wristlock. He then set Hard Core up for the Sly Guy Bomb (Al
Snow's Snowplow) and nailed it. He hooked the leg and the ref counted 1-2-3. Sly Vester has beaten Hard Core. As Vester begins to celebrate in the ring, we fade to the back where we see Fallout and the Pop Starz talking.
Fallout tells them that he in no way shape or form asked to be in the 6 man tag with them. He doesn't expect them to do much but when need be, to do what they're supposed to. The Pop Starz oblivious to Fallout's wishes tell him that they will singlehandledly win the match for them and then go to
Isolation and win the Tag Titles. Fallout then looks down at his boot's and shakes his head in disbelief. We then fade out to commercial.
------------------------ EWF ISOLATION NEXT SATURDAY MAY 4TH, 2002 LIVE! FROM THE LA SPORTS ARENA THE WORLD, NATIONAL AND TAG TEAM TITLES WILL ALL BE DECIDED HISTORY WILL BE MADE AND ONE PERSON WILL BE...... ... ISOLATED ------------------------ We come back to commercial and Jay Pauly and Todd Helton inform us that up next The Lost Soul will be in his first EWF singles match against Pawn. They then inform us that at Isolation, Pawn will go one on one against Sly Vester with the winner becoming the #1 contender for the National Title. Pawn comes out and enters the ring to almost an entire arena of mixed reactions. Most however are cheers. He enters the ring and awaits for his opponent. He stands in the ring for about 2 minutes before he grabs the mic and asks where the hell the Lost Soul is. For the next few moments he is talking to the crowd and about the Lost Soul until Sly Vester rushes down the aisle and slides into the ring. Pawn and Vester begin to brawl in the ring as the ref is trying to stop them. Each man throws a few punches and they roll around the ring for a while. They then roll to the outside where they begin their brawl there. Pawn takes Vester by the head and throws him into the steel post and steel steps. Vester gets back up and throws Pawn backfirst into the crowd barricade. At this point, a few more referees have come out and try to stop the brawl. They are now battiling up the aisle and Vester nails a snap suplex on the cold steel floor of the Thomas and Mack Center. They then begin to continue punching eachother all the way to the back.
Jay Pauly and Todd Helton have no idea what happened to the Lost Soul, but he appears to be...."Lost"...no pun intended. In any case they send the camera back to the Popstarz where they are now talking with the Mystery Guy.
The Pop Starz are in their own locker room this time and speaking with the masked mystery man. He is still dressed in all black and even with a black mask. The Pop Starz tell him that their is no doubt in their mind that they are going to walk out of Isolation as the first ever EWF Tag Team Champions.
They ask what the Mystery Guy has planned for Isolation on May 4th, and he is silent. The Popstarz stop talking and then he speaks. He tells them that he will soon be a new man and nobody, not even EWF Owner [name withheld] will be able to control him. It's just a matter of time before everyone gets what they have coming to them. After, the Pop Starz interrupt him and hardly care and continue talking about their tag title match. We now head to a different locker room, this one has LLCK, The Booty Babe and Miami Cool and Raphael in it.
LLCk (with the Babe at his side) tells them that he has much more important things to worry about then this little pesky 6 man tag later tonight. He doesn't show the least bit of any type of content on the fact that he will be teaming with Miami Cool and Raphael. Miami Cool then asks LLCK what he thinks of Fallout and the North Riders or whatever the hell they call themselves now-a-days. LLCK replies saying it doesn't matter what the hell is sided with next Saturday. LLCK then tells them that he will do whatever it takes to win the National Title. Raphael in return says that like LLCK they will do anything they can to win the Tag Titles. They say that if the
Pop Starz won the tag gold, godforbid, that the EWF would go into a state of shock. LLCK looks at the Booty Babe with a distant look in his eye and shakes his head as Raphael and Miami Cool are laughing and assuring themselves that they will win. We then go to another commercial.
---- When we come back from commercial, the newest tag team Undisputed is already in the ring. Helton tells us that also the EWF's two newest recruits, the
Dawson brothers are front row for this match and are watching the competition here in the EWF. Rude Boi and Spliff Star then come out as the entire arena showers them with boo's. They hardly care as they enter the ring and Spliff Star and Rekast begin the match. Spliff Star was taken to school by Rekast with a bunch of legsweeps and takedowns. Spliff didn't know what he could do so he tagged in Rude Boi who tried to gain some offense.
Rude Boi damn near took Rekast's head off with a clothesline. Rude Boi and Rekast fought back and forth until Rekast tagged in his partner, Guizmo. Guizmo whipped Rude Boi into the ropes and caught him with a cross body and got a 2 count from the ref. He got back up and attempted to suplex Rude Boi but he reversed it and Guizmo ended up going for the ride. Rude Boi then went on to tag Spliff Star who came in while Guizmo was still on the mat trying to get back up. Spliff Star gave him a few kicks and trash talked to him. Spliff Star attempted another kick but Guizmo was smart and tripped him. Guizmo then picks Spliff Star up and nails a brainbuster. He then tags
Rekast in and props Spliff Star up onto his own shoulders. Rekast went to the top rope and nailed a missle dropkick on Spliff Star who was proped on
Guizmo's shoulders. They just landed their finisher, 'Stage Fright'. Rekast went for the cover as Guizmo knocked Rude Boi down as he tried to make the save. Rekast got the 1,2,3 and Undisputed has won their very first match here in the EWF. As Rude Boi is recuperating in the ring, Spliff Star is trying to get up on the outside. From nowhere, DOOM comes and begins his attack on Spliff Star. These two men will meet in an LA Street Fight next
Saturday at Isolation. Doom is basically kicking Spliff Star's ass and they battle to the back. Meanwhile, Undisputed is still celebrating their very first win in the ring. Then while they are still doing so, Gang Star jets down the aisle and jumps into the ring knocking both Rekast and Guizmo to the floor. Rude Boi then gets up and helps his tag team partner, Gang Star in the attack. Rude Boi sets up Guizmo for their finisher, Lockdown Forever
(spike piledriver) as Gang Star goes to the second rope. Gang Star leaps off the rope and connects with Rude Boi on the spike piledriver on Guizmo. They then do the same thing on Rekast and now both members of Undisputed are left laying in the ring. If this is why Gang Star and Rude Boi haven't teamed once in the EWF then their is no telling what they're capable of at
Isolation when they attempt to become the very first EWF Tag Team Champs. We now fade to the backstage area where it appears Ash is.
::The scene opens backstage in Ash's locker room. The lights are off and it' s pitch black. Then a candle is lit, then another, then another. Soon the whole room is fairly well lit with the light of the candles. We see Ash, sitting on a bench and lighting these candles. He seems...sad. He smiles at the camera.::
Ash: I sit here in the dark with only the light of these fires to keep my fears at bay. I'm sitting in the dark, not because I'm trying to be morbid, or scary, or some other nonsense. No, I'm sitting in the dark because It's how I feel. My defeat last week left my confidence shaken. Over the last week I have agonized, worried, paced around my home and asked my self this question: Why not just quit? I mean hell who would give a damn? $pent would go to the final round for free and it isn't like anyone else would care.
Hell to most it isn't even a question of 'if I lose' it's more 'when I lose. ' Why even try if you're going to fail, right? People would rather cheer for the Big Booty Daddy because he is "cool" so it isn't like some little kid would cry because his favorite wrestler won't win the title.
::Ash blows the candles out and walks across the room and turns the lights on. He sits back down on the bench.::
Ash: But you know why it will not quit? You want to know why I just don't say "screw it" and go home? Because all my life people have told me I will never do it. Just look at my childhood, everyone on the playground pushed me around because I was small. Now look at me I'm 6'6. Then I went to wrestling school and trainers said, "Kid, you're too damn skinny. You can't pull the moves off and you bitch when you get hurt. Sorry kid but I don't think you'
ll make it." Guess what? I made it. So now they tell me, "Kid you will always be the top of the bottom or bottom of the top." Guess what? I'm fighting for the World Championship on the fourth. So now they say, "You won
't make it. You just can't win that title. I mean last week you had your ass handed to ya'. No way you can do it." Guess what? I'll prove you wrong again.
Ash: I have worked my whole life to achieve this. I have never been a World Champion, I've always had the secondary titles, or I was just a champ in a certain region. Now I have a shot, not only to be a World Champion but to be the granddaddy of a long line of great champions. I have the opportunity to be the "Isolated One."
Ash: I used to joke around and say, "One day I'll be the best wrestler in the whole entire world." Now I can make it happen. Now I can say to all the doubters and everyone who said that I wasn't good enough, "F**K you! I did it!" Now is my chance to make my blood, my time, my energy...Now it's time to make my dreams come true, and I'll be damned if I let one loss get in my way.
Ash: So here we are. I don't care who it is. I must say, I believe. I care. This belt means so much to me, your support means so much to me. That is why I'll go out there and I'll do what I've always one so well. Then in the end, maybe the guy you never thought could do it...will.
Ashes to Ashes...Dust to Dust. ::Ash smiles as the scene fades to commercial:: --- We come back from commercial and head straight to the ring where we are ready for the 6 man tag match between Miami Cool, Raphael and LLCK versus
The PopStarz and Fallout. :: "I stand alone" blares over the PA system as Fallout comes through the entrance curtain , to the sound of boos. Fallout shrugs them off, enters the ring, and gets psyched for the match. Then we here the sound of "DAMN! WE
LOOK GOOD" as The Pop Starz, Revo and Ray Ray dance their way to the ring to another chorus of boos.::
:: "#1" by Nelly hits as the fans go nuts For "LLCK" Mark Krauchick walks down the isle, he stops midway down the isle and he and Fallout have a stare-down. Then a drum beat hits and Miami Cool and Raphael walk down the isle. They meet with LLCK in the middle of the isle. High fives are traded and all three of them, then they suddenly bolt to the ring.::
::It a Pier Six Brawl as it is: LLCK and Fallout, Revo and Raphael, Cool and Ray Ray. Simultaneously LLCK, Cool, and Raphael's team gets the advantage and soon the Pop Stars head for higher ground as LLCK and Fallout trade blows in the middle of the ring. LLCK gets the advantage, whip, reversal.
Fallout ducks for a backdrop, sunset flip by LLCK, Fallout rolls out of it and bounces off the ropes. LLCK goes for a clothesline, duck by Fallout,
Fallout comes back off the ropes again, LLCK goes for the Superkick, Fallout grabs the foot. LLCK attempts an ensiguri but Fallout ducks. LLCK is down,
Fallout holds on to his foot and grabs his other leg, he picks him up by his legs and slams his down face-first. Stomps from Fallout, Fallout picks LLCK up, Military Press, and he drops him down! Tag Ray Ray.
:: Ray Ray comes in with some vicious stomps to LLCK's kidneys in rapid succession. Ray Ray picks LLCK up and rams his head into the post, punchery fallows, and LLCK slumps down into the corner. Now LLCK is victimized with sharp kicks to the ribs. Ray Ray gets him out of the corner, finally, on referee's orders. He whips him into the ropes, attempts to lock on a sleeper hold, but LLCK immediately sandwiches Ray Ray between himself and the turnbuckle. LLCK gets the tag to Miami Cool.::
::Cools comes in a house of fire, big punches on Ray Ray. Whip into the corner and Cool charges and monkey flips him. Ray Ray gets up fast and goes down faster as Miami Cool hiptosses him back down. Clothesline greets Ray
Ray on his way to his feet again and Miami Cool is on fire! Miami Cool grabs Ray Ray by his hair and tries to pull him to his feet, but Ray Ray with the low blow on Miami Cool. Ray Ray makes it to his corner and tags Revo, he enters the ring. Ray Ray hooks Miami Cool by the arms and Revo unloads with stiff chops to Miami Cool. Ray Ray exits the ring and Revo continues to work over Miami Cool. Revo with a bulldog on Miami Cool, which gets two. Revo picks him up and executes a fine neck breaker, two count again. Revo now goes down with tow fistdrops, but at the third he misses and Miami Cool dashes for his corner.::
::TAG to Raphael. Raphael is a house of fire. Big punch and Revo goes down, Ray Ray comes in and he gets a hip toss for his troubles. Revo tags Fallout. Fallout enters the ring and Raphael is determined to knock him down. Punch, Fallout doesn't even wink, Punch, Fallout is dazed, Punch, Fallout is wobbly, dropkick and Fallout goes down to a huge pop.::
::Just then the camera focuses on the entrance ramp. It's the Mystery Guy dressed in all black and a black mask. He walks to about mid-isle and watches the action.;:
:: Raphael is trying to take advantage of Fallout being down. Corkscrew legdrop, stomp, stomp, fistdrop. Raphael picks him up and hits a spinning neck breaker. That gets a two count and tag to LLCK.::
::LLCK wants Fallout in the worst way. He ascends the turnbuckle as Fallout makes it to his feet. MISSILE DROPKICK! LLCK starts to totally hammer
Fallout, rights and lefts, he then starts slamming his head into the mat three or four times. LLCK quickly gets to his feet, Fallout makes it to his feet, LLCK taps his boot. Fallout, dazed, walks near LLCK and SUPERKICK.
No, Fallout ducks, Revo comes in the ring and tackles LLCK before he can try the Superkick again. Revo with some punches. Miami Cool and Raphael try to get in the ring but the ref stops them. Now Ray Ray has entered the ring and all three men are stomping LLCK. Fallout claps his hands, as if he tagged
Revo, the ref turns back to the action and he has no clue that Revo isn't the legal man. Revo continuing the onslaught with a fisher man's suplex, which only gets two.
::The Mystery Man has seen enough and just walks away.:: Revo now has LLCK in position for a suoplex. He tries to lift LLCK up but he blocks it. Again and he blocks it. LLCK reverses the momentum and he hits the Suplex. The fans go nuts. Tag to Miami Cool!
Miami Cool comes in Revo quickly tags Fallout. Punch, Punch, Punch, Miami Cool is giving it all he has. He bounces off the ropes and goes for a huricanranna on the dazed Fallout, but Fallout doesn't budge and turns the attempted huricanranna into the Ground Zero running power bomb. Fallout hooks the leg and the ref counts 1........2............3! Fallout and the
Pop Starz have won the 6 man tag. As we see the Pop Starz celebrating, we fade to commercial.
-------- We come back from commercial and Todd Helton is already in the middle of the ring ready to conduct an interview with all four of the final participants in the EWF World Championship Tournament. He first welcomes Stevie Wish out and he receives a mixed reaction from the crowd. Once he gets to the ring,
Helton begins to ask him how he thinks he will fare next Saturday at Isolation. Wish responds saying that he will no doubt beat the Booty Daddy and whomever else he has to, to become the very first EWF World Champ.
Helton's next question is about Avalanche and if Stevie Wish has spoken with him in the past few weeks. Wish responds saying that he will not be associated with such a punk, next question. Helton then begins to ask what he thinks the Booty Daddy is capable of in their semi-final match. Wish responds saying that it doesn't matter what he is capable of because the fact of the matter is that he is the better wrestler and will prove it.
Helton asks Wish to step aside as he welcomes the next finalist, Spent. He gains a very hated reaction from the capacity crowd.
Once Spent steps in the ring, he asks Helton to make sure that he doesn't ask any stupid questions. Helton sarcastically agrees before asking him whether or not he thinks he'll be able to pin Ash one more time. Spent then says that last week when he pinned Ash for the 1,2,3 was no fluke. He'll do the same thing at Isolation and whenever he has to because he can. In the corner of the ring, Stevie Wish is just staring into the crowd and engaging in conversation with a few fans. Meanwhile, Helton asks Spent whether or not he'll enlist the help of his friends from the Ender's. Spent says that he doesn't need their help but if the opportunity presents itself, you never know. Helton then asks him what he thinks of the chances that both he and
Hard Core could walk out of the LA Sports Arena with gold. Spent says that if he were a betting man, he'd bet it all that the Ender's are leaving LA with all the gold, one way or another. Helton asks him to step in one of the other corners as he welcomes our next finalist, the Big Booty Daddy. He makes his way to the ring accompanied by cheer's from just about everyone in the crowd.
Once he steps in the ring, he grabs the mic and says that this must be Booty Country and the people shout wildly back at him. Spent begins to laugh in the corner and Wish is still doing his own thing. The Booty Daddy makes a dirty look at Spent before listening to Todd Helton. Helton asls him what he's been up to since last week. The Booty Daddy replies saying that he's been training and helping LLCK and the Booty Babe out with their training.
He then went on to say that he hopes Spent is a betting man because he'd love to gamble with him. He tells Helton that if he thinks the Ender's are leaving with double gold, he is sadly mistaken. Spent feels threatened by this comment and walks toward the Booty Daddy. Spent walks up into the face of the big man and begins trash talking. The Booty Daddy then tells Helton that he'd better get out of the ring before he gets caught up in the tension. Helton does as he is told and walks back to the commentators table.
Meanwhile, Spent and the Booty Daddy are still face to face trash talking eachother. All of a sudden, Stevie Wish comes from behind and attacks the
Big Booty Daddy. Spent and Wish continue to beat on the Booty Daddy with kicks and punches. As this is happening, we see a man run down the aisle and jump into the ring and it's ASH! Ash goes straight for Spent who is now trying to get away but has failed. Ash is pounding away on Spent with both fists. The Booty Daddy has just gained the offense on Stevie Wish and the
Booty Daddy knocks Wish over the top rope with a huge clothesline. Spent manages to get away from Ash and rolls out of the ring. Ash and the Booty
Daddy are in the ring and facing other directions with their backs to one another as they look at their respective Isolation opponents. Ash and the
Booty Daddy back up until they back into one another and they both turn around. Both men then begin to fight one another in the middle of the ring as referee's and officials are pouring down to the ring to try and restore order. At this point, Spent and Stevie Wish begin arguing on the outside and they too begin brawling it out, only on the outside. Referee's are trying to halt the melee but all four men have different ideas. Before we fade out of the broadcast, Todd Helton reminds us that next Saturday will be the EWF's
VERY FIRST PPV, ISOLATION. There will NOT be a Breakdown next week. Afterwards we fade out of the broadcast.
