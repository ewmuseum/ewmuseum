EWF Rumors and Happenings

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BACKSTAGE HAPPENINGS The following has happened recently backstage --The feeling backstage is that Isolation was a great show. Just about everyone thought the show was the best of the EWF so far. -Contarary to previous reports, the Booty Daddy and Spent were satisfied with their match. Both men afterwards congratulated eachother on a job well done and are looking forward to meeting again.
-Miami Cool/Raphael and The Pop Stars have formed a sort of friendship between eachother. Although on TV they may look as if they are bitter enemies, they are good friends backstage.
-Both Undisputed and the Canadian Connection were happy with their work at Isolation and think that a match between the two teams would be hot. -Stevie Wish left the arena right after his match without even notifying anyone. Contarary to previous reports he was never causing a ruckus backstage. -All talent active and inactive were notified that their work will be even more closely watched within the next three weeks. Those who are not felt as being up to par, may be released.
-After the pay-per-view ended, LLCk, the Booty Babe and the Booty Daddy all partied due to their title victories. A party was thrown for the production crew and athletes after the event.
RUMORS The following have been reported but are only considered rumors. These are not factual, but only rumors.
-Reports have said that Fallout was not particularly happy with his match at Isolation and spoke to officials about the matter. -Stevie Wish has been in deep water with officials over the past two weeks and due to his behavior at Isolation, he may be on the receiving end of a hefty suspension or even worse.
-With the first interfed event involving the EWF and the WIHQ, their will be in fact a match with four different companies world champs. As of now, the Booty Daddy should be heading into the event but the person who will be in will definately be the best the EWF has.
-The reason for the abrupt ending of Fallout's career in the Ruff Ryders was due to a major disagreement between him and Spliff Star. The two have taken a bitter disliking for one another backstage.
-The feeling backstage is that the entire "Mystery Guy" angle was blown way out of proportion and is going no where. You can either expect to see a rapid unmasking or a rapid cover-up. The angle was suppose to be the brainchild of EWF Owner [name withheld] which began in about the third week of
Breakdown.
-The Fish and the Dawson Brothers are all happy with how they debuted. Ash however was not happy with how he lost, citing that a rookie in the EWF "stole a freakin' toy". There is no apparent problem backstage. -Jack Battle and Diablo were given their releases earlier in the week due to failure to work plus inability to cooperate.
