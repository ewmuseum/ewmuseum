Jonathan is reading a newspaper, he has on black shorts, no shirt sowing his well defined chest, and a pair of silver ray ban sunglasses. Shaun is sitting across from him talking into a cell phone. Shaun just grins. . ] SHAUN RICHE - THat's what I thought, I knew it'd be only a matter of time till you came to get your first comments from your _FUTURE_ tag team champions. As they slowly move towards the WPCW logo you see the sunlight reflecting a perfect shadow. The camera slowly makes it's way down to the entrance. It skips through several hallways and down several flights until it stops in a lockerroom area. Sitting in the window area with the sun shining in is none other than "WPCW Legend" Mr.Excellent. Excellent has on black jean shorts, black wrestling boots, knee pads, and a white and black t-shirt. Mr.Excellent looks at his hand and reflects for a minute. Then turns his head towards the camera and begins to speak. ] MR.EXCELLENT: Jack Vars, I look down at these hands and I see the hard work over all the years. I also see a bright future that is still ahead of me for many more days. Then I look at you. I question whether your washed up brutha. Vars, I've seen you practically everywhere. In fact, too many places. These days, you have to watch your back. Looking over your shoulder at everyone. That leads to here. The WPCW. No allies, no stables, no nothing. Nothing for you to fall back on Vars. All you have is your pure skill. Do you think you can handle that? You see, in most of the places I've seen you you've been in some sort of alliance. You trick a lot of people Vars. Most would think you're pretty tough because you travel everywhere, The matter of the fact is, you're not that tough. You make sure you have friends watching your back. Ever since the days Rodman taught you the tricks of the trade you've been hiding behind others. You easy to find but hard to find alone. Then, there's WPCW. An great organization you've signed with. Noticably, this time I see you running alone. Now this may not be for long but the matter of fact is...you always have an ally lurking somewhere. What allies do I have? I have more enemies than allies and that's the way I choose. Something you rarely do. Are you ready for something like that? [ Mr.Excellent walks over to the locker. He opens up the locker and grabs a white towel. He wipes his face off with it and let's out of a sigh. As the sigh comes to an end he whips the towel over his shoulder. He then slams the locker door and locks it. ] MR.EXCELLENT: Two out Three falls Vars. That means if I want to beat you I have to pin you two times. One time is hard enough but I'm more than prepared to take you past your limit into my ZONE. Can you pin me twice? Who even knows if you can pin me once? _NOBODY_! We've been chosen to headline the return WPCW Main event. I'm giving the fans a show they won't forget because WPCW is returning and this time was mega effect! This has been a long waited return and what better than the Franchise player to headline the first return event. Vars, you've been wrestling in gutters, night clubs, and all kinds of independent events but none are like the WPCW. Innovation is the motto here and if you can't cut it then get out. [Mr.Excellent walks over to a corner and begins to scoop up his bags as he continues to speak.] MR.EXCELLENT: Vars, you're digging your self a hole right now. Then after you finish I'm going to make you crawl in that hole. And then you'll be stuck there and all I'll have to do is throw in the dirt and watch. [Mr.Excellent opens the door and walks up the stairs slowly with his bags. Upon exiting into a limo that's parked outside the WPCW Towers and he leaves us with some last words.] MR.EXCELLENT: Jack Vars... I've got five good words for ya... CAN.... YOU..... TRUST.... ME?!
