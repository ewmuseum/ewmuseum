GCW/Executioner History

What this is A past president’s own history of his years with the federation.

GCW/Executioner History By Ryan, the Executioner [Introduction]

Executioner here ... and seeing Masked rehashing the good old days (especially the ones to which I had little/no knowledge of) got me in the mood for things. And, I might as well steal his format just like I stole GCW from him, lol. Just so you know, this is going to be a two pronged type of history. The initial parts will be Ex-centric [as in centered on the Executioner, not as in eccentric] because I don’t want to step on Masked’s toes especially since he’ll know more about the overall state of GCW in the early stages that I did. When it gets to the point where I was in ‘charge’ I’ll be able to give a better overview of how all of GCW was doing [not to mention that Ex was just a figurehead then and less ‘involved’ then when I was roleplaying]. Things started off innocently enough. I was a wrestling junky because my friends were and they got me into it ... as most things in life go I always surpass my friends in whatever ‘new craze’ they tune me into. I was also on the net a lot with ICQ and chat rooms and stuff because of those same friends. And then, I got an ICQ message from some guy I don’t even know, from England, named Lozza. He asks me if I want to join an e-fed, I asked him what that was, and he gave me a link to his website. Simple as that, I was staring out at EICW (Extreme Internet Championship Wrestling). A small little website, nothing much to look at and a guest book posing as a roleplay board. I decided “what the hell, why not?” and went to the character creation application form. I created a guy of Undertaker-esque proportions, and looking at the wrestling shirt I was wearing, came up with my name and finisher. The shirt said “Coup de Grace: The swiftness which the executioner ends, by death, the convicted’s suffering.” So thus the Executioner, and his finisher the Coup the Grace was born. It was a Sharpshooter because, I’m Canadian ... and all Canadian’s have to do it. And there just aren’t enough submission finisher’s in the world.

[Prologue: Ex’s Past]

So Ex started a reign of terror in GCW that would never be seen again. It started out with the one liners and fun stuff like that, but because I joined up early, and the alleged ‘big name from the e-fedding world’ never bothered to show up, I was able to become the first champion of EICW. As things went on, the roleplays got bigger and better, and I decided I could handle a couple people, but the other would be a joke that would never reach World Title level. And then was born, the Masked Jobber. Executioner continued on being unstoppable as World Champion, defeating people in ‘extreme’ match ups like the Spanish Inquisition match [An I Quit Match but with various torture implements available]. Things started to get a little boring, so I decided to shake things up. A strange series of events went down and I was now handling 4 guys, plus had some allies and friends in the federation. A team of Skull and Crossbones were tearing up the ranks, but were eventually revealed to be Ex and the Masked Jobber. Ultimately, this formed the stable that was conspiring against everyone known simply as THEM. They had a pretty big reign of terror, but the end result was the same. I was bored, and wasn’t losing the title ... so I was going to do something drastic. In another ‘extreme’ title defense, Ex defended his title in an electrified Hell in the Cell. It was electrified randomly so he and his opponent were able to make it to the top. He was chokeslamed through the top of the cage, but ended up getting tangled up, the wires around his neck choking and electrocuting him until they cut the power and got him down. This let me take some time off (just doing stuff with Masked Jobber) and let someone else get a run as champion. When Ex returned, he won again easily, and eventually I dropped the title at our first annual ‘Wrestlemania/Wrestlebowl-esque’ show [I forget the name]. With the first person to beat Executioner for the title he got the credibility he needed, Ex had past the torch, and wasn’t needed there anymore. Around that time I had found out about GCW and signed up. I’ll get into that in a bit, but I’ll finish up Ex’s tenure in EICW that ran alongside Ex’s early days in GCW. Having cemented his status as a legend so early, the Executioner turned his sights to the only thing ‘bigger’ than the World title ... absolute power. In what would foreshadow a whole lot, the Executioner was able to [at least for a while] take control of EICW from its president in true heel fashion. OOC this had a lot to do with the fact that, from the early going, I was always giving the president suggestions for ‘cool angle ideas’ and he’d often ask my opinion on various matters. When his VP took off, he asked if I’d help, and I said sure. That was part of the reason for Ex’s retirement, and the take over angle was a way for use to ‘share power’ and have fun with the whole two president system. The whole thing didn’t last two long, as I was realizing that GCW needed my full attention if I intended to be successful. So eventually Ex packed up his bags and headed down to Georgia [I think they even wrote a song about that, lol] and was given a fond farewell by EICW as he was put up in the Hall of Fame and won a final match where all the former World Champions competed to see who was the ‘best of the best’. I still check on EICW, its website is still up. It hasn’t been updated in forever ... but at least it’s still there. With that in place, I’m ready to start talking about the important stuff, GCW.

[Part 1: The Gauntlet, Stables and the Masked Jobber]

It wasn’t much of a start, but it had to start somewhere. A tall large man came out, clad in black even a black leather mask. Yup, Ex started out as the Gimp-erTaker basically. Smoke, mirrors, standard goth stuff. And then the open challenge to anyone that wanted a piece, and I ‘mysteriously’ disappeared with a gauntlet left for those who would accept the challenge. I got to beat up Satan or one of the Blondes ... which is always fun, for my debut. This was quickly followed up by getting to beat up an idiot [other than Satan/Blondes]. See, some guy decided he was going to fight me and someone else ... in a HELL IN THE CELL. In his second match with the company. So Masked bitch slapped him and said he’d have 2 matches, both one on one against both of the people he challenged. He was able to pick up a win against the first guy, but I killed him when he went one on one with me. With the basics laid out, things for Ex in GCW started innocently enough. With Forsaken as the big heels on campus and Scorpio, Shamrock and company as the faces, there wasn’t much interaction between the upper and lower card, and I was happy to battle it out with the other new guys, including some recent grads of the Dojo. Scott Steiner offered me a chance to join his stable and I replied ... with a roll of quarters to the side of the head. Steiner and I feuded off and on, and I eventually got a tag title shot, but my partner ran away because his old partner was set on fire or something ... I don’t quite remember. Things for the most part weren’t going anywhere, so I decided to just do some stuff to have things to do. One thing was to bring in the Masked Jobber, from EICW, to stalk Ex in GCW. He became a hanger on and pseudo-manager ... just so I’d have someone to work with. He wasn’t a pivotal character, but he’d become one eventually. I got a shot at a TV title shot at Halloween Horror [my first PPV] in a fourway match, and it began an interesting path with Scott Steiner. I was the first eliminated from the match, because of my going to get a chair [which I never used]. Angered, I tossed the chair into the ring and distracted the ref long enough for Fox to be eliminated via an unseen chairshot. Steiner defeated the final member and earned the number one contendership. This coincided with Overlord winning the TV title. I knew that I would have a good chance to get a title shot from Steiner, since we had never resolved our issues in a clean one on one match. So, a tenuous alliance was formed, where if he won the TV title, he’d give me the first shot. Until he won the title I wouldn’t attack him, etc. Unfortunately, Johnny MacGrath would ensure that Overlord did not lose the title to Steiner. I would petition (unsuccessfully) for a title shot from Overlord, but it would not come to pass. Then came the first ‘big moment’ in my career and what would begin a HUGE turn in GCW’s history. At the moment, Scorpio was the World Champion and Micheal Hoss was the Georgia Champion, while Triple Threat aka Stephen Frost and Jack Anthony. Anthony and X-Pac were trading the Cruiserweight Title around. And of course, Overlord was TV champion. Now, for months, Forsaken had been making the lives of many of GCW’s biggest names hell. Shamrock, Scorpio, Hoss, X-Pac, and more all had problems with Forsaken, and despite their best efforts, Forsaken was still running roughshod over GCW. Steiner decided that new blood might help and challenged all of Forsaken to a tag match with anyone that wanted to help him open to. He got no reply from Hoss. He got no reply from Shamrock. He got no reply from Scorpio. It looked like Steiner had bitten off more than he could chew and may end up 3 on 1 against Forsaken. And then ... the Executioner threw his hat into the ring. Making cryptic messages, the Executioner accused the ‘heroes’ of GCW as being too soft, and too concerned about public image to get the job done. Besides a small distraction by X-Pac, the Executioner and Steiner had to fight on their own against Stephen Frost, Jack Anthony and HBK in a 3 on 2 match. Despite the odds, the Executioner was able to pin former World Champion Frost ... by playing possum and getting a surprise roll up when Frost went to pick him up! It was a huge upset and both sides were fired up from the result. Steiner and Ex demanded a title shot, since they beat the Tag Champions AND their stable mate in the middle of the ring. The champions were furious and responded in kind. However ... the following week the champions would be pinned by Scorpio and Hoss. Steiner took the snub in stride, continuing to make his name for himself. The Executioner on the other hand ... would not take it lying down.

[Part 2 : Ex Goes Nucking Futs]

Ok, this was a turning point for the Executioner as far as GCW was concerned. Up to this point, I was a cocky pseudo goth kind of Undertaker wannabe heel that had just had the win of his career beating the premiere heel stable of GCW in a 2 on 3 handicap match with someone he’d feuded with in the past. It was one of the biggest upsets for GCW. And ... we never got a title shot out of it. I got pissed and posted an RP where I blasted GCW for screwing over the new talent in favor of the old guard that were rarely seen or heard from by that point ... at least not on the rp boards. Steiner talked some sense into me and I decided two things. The first was, I wouldn’t be around for Survivor Match because of Xmas vacation and the second was ... one good week won’t get me a title shot ... I need to PROVE myself. So ... Ex went out and accused Irish Poison of conspiring against him, and stealing his title match from him. And then he accused Forsaken of going behind their backs and reneging on the title match. And then he accused Overlord. And the JOB Squad. And Steiner. Ex was having a nervous breakdown as he believed a conspiracy was out to get him. I decided that to explain my time off ... Ex would be institutionalized leading up to Survivor Match. His conspiracy theories grew wilder and wilder as he went on a short, intentional, losing spree being too unnerved to concentrate. Ultimately ... his own manager, the Masked Jobber was revealed as the one behind it all, and Ex was deemed mentally unfit and put in the rubber room. And thus, Ex was gone and Survivor Match happened without him. As a short aside, this was the Survivor Match that was temporarily postponed due to the death of Patrick Little. When I returned from vacation, I heard about what had happened, and about all the things that were going on with GCW. I never really knew Patrick Little but I saw how much the rest of GCW did. It was a major blow for GCW, and I knew it took a lot for Masked to keep going after that, but we had all decided that it would be the best to continue GCW because most believed it’s what Patrick would have wanted. To my end, I roleplayed my heart out despite not being on the card. Partly it was because I needed to explain the Executioner’s return, and I was still trying to prove myself. But the other part was that I found ... nothing takes your mind off things, OR gets more people to motivate themselves to rp ... than to do so yourself. I’ll leave Masked to go into this subject in more detail if he wishes, there isn’t a whole lot that I can say about it. With Survivor Match going by and Stephen Frost winning a chance to reclaim the World Title that Scorpio beat him for, the Executioner was getting out of his prison. An intrepid young reporter, Jay, and his cameraman, Bob [who didn’t talk much]. Had gotten a tip on the location of the GCW wrestler. They were able to sneak inside the sanitarium and after an argument with the guards that kicked them both out [but didn’t notice they dropped the camera] they witnessed the Executioner in a straight jacket being beaten by some of the orderlies. This footage being shown on are allowed the Executioner to be sent to a different facility with Jay and Bob in hot pursuit. Ex broke out and a chase was on between Jay and Bob that led to the Sky Dome in Toronto. In the ensuing chaos, Ex lost his mask, Bob was kidnapped and ransomed to Jay in exchange for Jay signing papers classifying the Executioner as sane. As crazy as that all was ... it showed GCW that I could tell a story and it also allowed me to completely revamp the Executioner character. Following this ‘transformation’ Ex tracked down Masked Jobber. When the two parted ways ... Ex knew why Jobber did what he did, and the Masked Jobber was in traction. Ex met with some people who were apparently behind the conspiracy all along ... and they revealed that they were merely testing Ex and that he had passed with flying colors. They invited him to join the conspiracy ... and he excepted with open arms. Gone was the leather mask and the gauntlet thrown to open the challenge. In its place was the Executioner, a seven foot tall, three hundred and fifty pound monster in a black suit and shades who entered the arena with his music proclaiming ... “I am the Conspiracy!”.
