Join the GCW: rules (August 2003)

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The rules an applicant agrees to by joining.

These rules apply to both the GCW and the GCW Dojo II. Applying constitutes agreement with said rules.

1. You may create a wrestler of any size or shape, but if you create him/her with insane proportions you will have difficulty winning matches and functioning physically. You may only have one (1) wrestler in the GCW at one time without consent of the president.

2. You may not post images or music files on the role-play board, nor may you link to a role-play put on an outside page.

3a. If you challenge a wrestler, the match is automatically accepted, and must be declined by the participant.

3b. If a wrestler already has a match, any challenge is automatically declined, unless the wrestler accepts.

4. A wrestler can have only one singles and one tag match per card without getting a 'penalty'. If a wrestler has two of either, all points for role-plays are halfed and split between the matches. (If, for example, there are 2 singles and 1 tag matches for the same person, the tag match would get full marks, each singles would get 50%). This does not apply if one or more of the matches are 'surprise matches' (announced during the show), since this probably involves an angle.

5. If you copy old role-plays, then you will be given a warning. A second offense results in being kicked out of the league.

6. If you blatantly vandalize the role-play board (e.g., send a message 20 times, put "this fed sucks"), you will be kicked out of GCW immediately. Yes, vandalizing the role-play board includes making derogatory comments towards the federation unless authorized by the President and making any kind of coding errors to damage the page.

7. A requirement is to download a free copy of the AOL Instant Messager. These are useful tools for the fed, it is surprising how many angles you can make with someone when you talk with them live. My screen name is listed on the administration page. This is mandatory. I might make an acceptation if you talk to me about it. HOWEVER... AOLIM is the way we do business. It is as close to real time conversation as is possible with the current internet technology. Applications without an AOLIM screen name will be considered.... however... you have TWO weeks to give me your screen name before you are fired. PERIOD.

8. This is NOT an "extreme" e-fed; all basic matches go by standard rules.

9. The e-fed may close for any holidays (over Christmas), if there is enough 'support' for it. If not, and I'm away, someone will be given temporary control.

10. You cannot mention other feds in your role-plays. The only exception is when you are brand-new and introducing the character into the league.

11. You cannot have an extreme/gimmick match unless you (A) get permission by E-mailing the president, or (B) otherwise told.

12. You cannot put your hands on a GCW official unless you are wrestling them. The first time is a fine, the second is a suspension, the third is a longer suspension, the fourth your contract is terminated. You may not attack a wrestler without permission from the wrestler. You MAY make a "sneak attack" on them, but they have the option of giving the role-play the cold shoulder, so to speak, and not following up on the attack. In that case, you end up looking bad and your role-play grade goes down the drain.

13. Other than what is noted in the rules, you are free to put whatever you would like in your role-play. However, common sense rules apply in that putting something outlandish via loophole will only have your role-play tank when it is graded.

14. If you consistently whine about losing (I don't mean one time--I mean you get on there day after day and complain) you can come under the discretion of the council over disciplinary actions.

15. You may not kill people or commit unlawful acts in your role-plays without permission.

16. The Jackson Rule: no role-play hording. You may post THREE role-plays per day maximum. If you stash three role-plays and post them all in one sitting (all three within one hour), ONLY ONE WILL COUNT.

17. Kayfabe Rule : The use of 'insider' terms are looked down upon in MOST cases. One of the exceptions is that the Bombers and Satan ARE referred to as jobbers, but in a sense that they suck, not that they 'do the job' in the actual sense. Any mentioning of scripts, or predetermined results (i.e. admitting wrestling 'is fake') is a definite NO-NO. On the other hand, a non-wrestling character (a reporter for example) COULD say wrestling is fake, but people 'in' the business shouldn't say it. Also, terms such as heel, face, pop, heat, marks and smarts, or other generally kayfabe/insider terms are not banned, but will reduce the value of your rps.

18. No ethnic slurs. No sexuality taunts. No serious profanity. Use language that is acceptable for cable television. Bleeps are acceptable... however, profanity is usually saved for the most extreme circumstances. Use WITH CAUTION.

19. Here at the GCW, we are all about having a good time. We understand that real life is more important then your "internet friends". Having said that, we expect two things of our wrestlers.

1) You will have the decency to tell us you need time off

2) That you uphold our RPing standards. The GCW has been around four years for a reason. We have a good time and love e-feding. If you're like us, you will go far here. However, we ask that you post at a minimum of twice per week. We also recommend your posts are over 50 lines long. If that doesn't sound like something you can do, then we wish you well. The GCW isn't for everyone. We love angles, stories, and generally enjoying this fantasy sport. If you need time off, tell us. If you just stop RPing... that's a problem. After two weeks of inactivity, you will be emailed and placed on the injured list. If we haven't heard from you in four weeks, your contract will be terminated.
21. Upon your first week of being accepted you will be automatically assigned to a match against one of our jobbers. See the roster page for the list. You MUST post at least TWICE to beat the jobbers and move on in your GCW career.
If you accept these rules, join now!

NOTE: These rules will be changed whenever necessary. Please make sure to look over them very carefully.

Q. Does the GCW have any examples of what it considers great role-playing? (and) Q. I'm thinking of applying, is there somewhere I can go to read some of GCW finest in action?
A. Of course! Visit GCW LiveWire X2 to read our roleplays. You can also visit the GCW archive page that contains Role Plays. Here you will find some of the more notable posts in recent history.

Q. What does the G.C.W. stand for? A. A. Global Championship Wrestling

Q. How do I get the GCW MAJOR leagues? A. Work, work, and more work. Show us your interested. Put up three or more role-plays a week. Give us "angle" pitches. Bottom line, the better the attitude, the shorter the stay in the "minors".

Q. How do I role-play? A. After being accepted, register your wrestler name on LiveWire X.

Q. How do I sign matches? A. Challenge your prospective opponent by issuing the challenge in a role-play. Rules on challenging someone are listed in the rules section. ADDENDUM: When you challenge someone on the role play board, you create a new topic. Title the topic "so and so vs. so and so". Your opponent then REPLIES to that topic. This continues through the week.... So on and so forth.

Q. How do I win? A. Role-play, Role-play, Role-play. Show us you care. Show us your improving. Do NOT put up 7 role-plays in 2 hours that are 6 lines long. QUALITY over QUANTITY. Put in the work, the rest will follow.

Q. When is the challenge deadline? A. Thursday night at 11:59 pm. (note, I may make acceptations to this rule... however... you need my permission to issue a challenge any later then Thursday night at 11:59 pm.

Q. I'm confused... I don't know where to Role Play!!! A. This link will bring you to our boards. AFTER you are accepted to the boards you may Role Play. If you are accepted into the GCW, role play in the GCW board. If you are accepted into THE DOJO II, role play on The Dojo II board. DO NOT POST out of your federation. The first time is a warning... the second is termination.

Q. Where do I see if I won? A. Look for the most recent show to be linked on the main page and/or go to the events archives page.

Q. Can you tell me what these terms mean? LiveWire - LiveWire is the loose name for our programming network. You will hear it mentioned frequently in RPs. Everyone knows what CNN or MTV is. Livewire is our programming network. Its like a cable company that is dedicated to the GCW. Feel free to use your imagination with this concept. Some of our best RPs have come from using this concept to its fullest extent (ie. creating shows on LiveWire.... the "interview show" is a popular one) When the GCW isn't having an actual live event (Sunday Night Rage) LiveWire airs wrestler promos (RPs) and anything else wrestling related.

Mecha T - He interviews, commentates, etc. Occasionally, someone who looks a lot like Mr. T but doesn't talk, act, or sound anything like Mr. T will appear in RPs. THIS is what we call MECHA T. Mr T and Mecha T hate each other..... for good reason. BEWARE the MECHA T.

Masked Marauder- Masked is the former president and founder of GCW entertainment. He is our God Father... so to speak. Masked is now retired happily in Oxford, GA. He will stop by occasionally to see what's going on.

Q. When are your shows? A. As mentioned before, the GCW is broadcast on LiveWire. The GCW has its televised flagship program (Sunday Night Blitz) on, surprisingly enough, Sunday. Blitz will be up Sunday afternoon - evening. Having said that, people also use the "house show" as a favorite place to interview or be interviewed in RPs. House Shows are not televised. However "clips" containing your character may appear on LiveWire. House Shows are basically publicity venues for cities we haven't visited on Blitz lately.
Q. What's your (Crow's) screen name? A. deceptivehero20

Q. My role-play won't send! A. If you experience any problems with LiveWire, it may be because we are doing maintenance on the board, which should be indicated ahead of time. If this is the case, then you can go to the back-up board and post there, or save the role-play in a notepad and post it when the board is working again. Also, report any times that the board goes down on the OOC board (when it's back up) so people will know to check out the back-up board for some role-plays. If for some reason this doesn't work for you, don't hesitate to email me. HAVING SAID THAT.... there is NO excuse for not RPing.

Q. WHEN can I get a shot at "this" belt? A. In due time.

Q. Who is Jolt... and why should we care about him? A. Jolt is just another reason the GCW rocks. However, "Who is Jolt" is a question that takes some explaining. Jolt is a former wrestler in the GCW who was, appropriated, by several wrestlers over the GCW's existence. He doesn't exist in "reality" anymore... he is kinda like the "Job Squad" (see the roster page) in that he is controlled by the GCW officials. Jolt, basically, is a legend in his own mind. He does flips into the rings, yells his moves before he does them, and is incredibly popular with the crowd.... for some reason. IF you are feeling... sassy... he can be used as an interviewer... but you better contact the GCW management first.
