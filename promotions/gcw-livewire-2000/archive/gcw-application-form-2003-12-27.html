Join the GCW: application form (December 2003)

27 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own application form, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 27 December 2003: its field labels with the fed’s notes to applicants (the cruiserweight limit, the Minor League, the sample roleplay, the referral question). The form’s entry boxes are not carried.
Ready to join GCW? Fill out the following form to the best of your ability, and you've taken your first step towards becoming a GCW superstar. Entries with a red asterix (*) are mandatory. Wrestler Name* Your wrestler's name. E-Mail Address* Your E-Mail address. Can this address be posted publicly? Yes No AIM You MUST have an AOLIM screen name to join. Put it here. Can this name be posted publicly? Yes No Height/Weight* The cruiserweight limit is 235 lbs. Height - Weight - Finisher & Description* Please tell us your finishing move and a description of it. Finisher - Description - Character Alignment* A babyface is a good guy, a heel is a bad guy, and a tweener is neutral. Babyface Heel Neutral Entrance* Describe how your character's entrance into the arena would normally go. At least include theme music. Characteristics* Please give us as accurate a description of your wrestler and his/her style as possible. Credentials* Please put here all previous experience you have personally in e-wrestling. Sample Roleplay* Please create a sample roleplay, or use one you made before. This is very important to us, so please make the roleplay an accurate representation of YOUR AVERAGE ROLEPLAY. GCW Minor League* In the event that a) we think you need improvement, b) we have too many people debuting this week already or c) we are 'full' in either fed at the moment, you may be referred to the Minor League, where depending on the same factors as above, you'll eventually be called up into GCW as long as you stick with it. In order to help us out, please tell us if you'd be totally against going to the Minor League if you aren't accepted straight away to GCW. If you say no, and aren't accepted, you can always apply again as often as you like. - I would be willing to join the GCW Minor League - I would NOT be willing to join the GCW Minor League Character Image If you have an image of your character on file, either list the URL here or send us the image via E-Mail. Anything Extra Have anything else left to say? Put it here. And Finally...Referrals* This last question is one of the most important. Please fill it out honestly.
