GCW: A History (December 2003)

26 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A multi-author written history of the federation, from its roots in July 1998.

 

-Multiple Authors Global Championship Wrestling: A History by Strike

Global Championship Wrestling, GCW, whatever you want to call it, has been around for four years and running. On July 1st, 1998 the Masked Marauder founded the roots of Global Championship Wrestling. From there GCW sprouted it's wings and flew with stars like Shamrock, The Hitman, and Kane. Mike Mosall said that a history needed to be made, we have the Hall of Fame, we have the Title Histories, but the whole story has yet to be told. I am honored to be writing this for Global Championship Wrestling, and I hope that I do a good job with it. The beginning, unfortunately, will be shorter than the end and although that doesn't seem right, the beginning shows of GCW, NeWA, NWF, and ICW are not available to me at this point in time, on the events that I can read and do research on there will be more information on them and the wrestlers.

Let's begin.

The early days of Global Championship Wrestling are extremely confusing, and probably will remain that way for some time. Wrestlers such as Devon Greene, Shamrock, Kane, and Hitman all began their rise to the top about this time. Global Championship Wrestling was formed out of the ashes of ICW and NWF as GCeW, which merged with NeWA. Devon Greene was the NeWA World Champion, and Shamrock was the NWF/GCeW World Champion. Devon Greene apparently left and Shamrock became the World Heavyweight Champion in the tournament on the first of August in the year 1998. After this point the history smoothes out a bit. Shamrock lost the championship to Hitman at Halloween Horror '98, and Hitman defended his championship honorably until Shamrock overcame him in late November. Also in November a group began to sprout called the Forsaken, led by Kane. Hitman was taken out in a car wreck and Kane took the opportunity and challenged Shamrock to a match at Broken Hearts in December of '98. And shock waves were sent through Global Championship Wrestling as Shamrock laid flat on his back as Kane pinned him, one, two, three. Also at Broken Hearts was a great match between Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Unknown, a feud that had been going for some time and was personal. Steve Austin did not like The Unknown. Unknown had always escaped from him in some fashion or another, a cage match was to be fought at Broken Hearts. Unfortunately Unknown fell off the map just before the match and the feud sizzled, although the match still happened. Shamrock began to throw matches and cost Forsaken victories and The Messiah hit the scene, sent from above to destroy Kane. The career match was signed between Kane and The Messiah for Wrestlebowl and Shamrock began doing things right. At the original Wrestlebowl Messiah defeated Kane and unveiled himself as Shamrock. This brought down Forsaken for good ... or so we thought. Shamrock loses the title to Steven Frost in late September, who loses it a week later to Scorpio. Scorpio gives Shamrock the championship shot at Halloween Horror '99 with The Hitman as the Special Guest Referee. Hitman nails Shamrock with the World Heavyweight Championship at the end of the match and locks in the Sharpshooter. Hitman tells Scorpio to drop Shamrock's hand, but SCORPIO WON'T DO IT! Stephen Frost and the "Man in the Mask" walk down the ramp and the "Man in the Mask" throws Shamrock and Scorpio into theguard rail.

Blitzkrieg GCeW July 1998 - October 1998 By Sidaiaon

Hello everyone, for those that don’t know me, and that makes up a vast majority of the Fed, I am Sidaiaon. I am one of the initial 10 wrestlers that made up GCeW. At MMM’s request I am writing a series of Articles on the history of GCW. Consider these the “Lost Records.”
To give everyone a good understanding it’s best to start at the beginning. GCeW was founded in July of 1998, out of the ashes of (as Strike has already explained in his history of GCW). The ICW and NWF, which as a whole merged with the NeWA, probably the biggest E-fed hub at that time. You had legends in its starting roster like Shamrock and Hitman. There was the Wolf Pac stable and the 4 Horsemen, but a third stable you will have never heard about until now. That was the DoD, or Dungeons of Darkness. This stable consisted of the “Blitzkrieg Team” that was Afterburn, Donavin, Triple S, and Striker (not to be confused with TPG Pres. Strike). Things were heading for a big change in GCeW at this point. The DoD brought a new standard to role-playing that has never been seen in anything but the best of E-feds. While everyone was writing short little 1 paragraph role plays, the DoD were pumping out 1 page RP’s and putting them out faster then the competition could ever hope. These RP’s were what started some of the biggest changes in GCW history. They gave the talent out there something to see and take notice from.
The DoD steamed a path from worst to first in a matter of 1 month, winning all but 1 belt. The GA Championship Belt (later to become the World Heavyweight Belt). Donavin immediately launched an insane RP campaign against Hitman, the current GA Champ. Hitman accepted the challenge and in the RP war he was soundly defeated. However, some information leaked out that the DoD was predetermined to lose every single fight that night, information that turned out to be false. With what they beleived was an unjustified move, the backed out of all their fights, and left the fed. This left the Fed in big trouble as the DoD had become an integral part of GCeW. So with Halloween Horror incoming, and almost no plot to go with, the DoD was banned from returning to GCeW and would remain that way for some time.

Needless to say, troubled times were ahead.....
