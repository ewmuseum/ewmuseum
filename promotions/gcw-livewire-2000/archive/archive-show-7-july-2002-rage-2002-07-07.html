The Proving Grounds - Pre-Rage Television Tapings

HHM vs. Chambers vs. Gooby

As TPG comes on the air, this 3 way begins. All both HHM and Cambers are in awe of Gooby... the match begins and Goody gets the early advantage by DOUBLE CLOTHES LINING both HHM and Chambers. Both men recover and the three way truly beings. HHHM looks good in this match up. Gooby, after an initial surge fades quick. HHM dominates much of the match with Cambers getting his lick in here and there. End result? HHM with the victory.

Tenney vs. Tone

Moe Tenney and Tone circle each other for a few moments and then lock up. Tone puts Tenney into a headlock and Tenney pushes him off into the ropes and hits a shoulder block. Tenney rebounds off the ropes and goes for a quick running elbow drop. Tone moves out of the way and goes for a quick cover on the down Tenney…1 count. Tenney gets back up and throws a few punches. He knocks Tone down and grabs his right elbow. It is sore from the missed elbow drop and Tenney shouldn't have thrown the punches. Tone recovers and rises back to his feet. Tenney tries to punch him again, but Tone blocks the blow and hits a hard clothesline on Tenney sending him to the ground. Tone locks on a sleeper hold to slow down the pace of the match. Both men are winded. Tenney is going to sleep. The ref raises his arm. 1…2… Tenney gets his arm up and is coming back to life. Elbow to Tone's stomach. Another elbow and Tone releases the sleeper hold. Tenney grabs Tone's arm and twists it around. He stomps Tone in the stomach and then sets him up for the Money Maker. Tone knows what is about to happen and struggles wildly, but it is to late. Tenney lifts Tone up and slams him down hard. He grabs his elbow as pain shoots through, but still makes the cover quickly. 1…2…3.

Chang vs. SSR

SSR wins fairly easily. Chang just doesn't seem ready for this match and SSR looks well trained after a week of hard work.

Eric Russell vs. HellRaizor

Close match, real close match. Back and forth with HellRaizor getting the advantage near the end. However, Russell with some dirty tricks takes the victory. After the match, MMM appears... and promotes both Russell and HellRaizor to the GCW....

MH : Well, sounds like we have stuff going on backstage. Crow has been spotted in the parking lot.

Backstage we see Drago, as he makes his way into the parking lot. The door slams closed behind him, and locking noises can be heard from the other side. The area is dark in the parking lot except for the weak glow coming from the lights over various vehicles. Drago wanders around, looking for Crow.

A garbage can can be heard being overturned. Drago turns as he sees where the garbage can was. He makes his way over, and there, crouching is Crow, dressed in a long black trenchcoat and full make up. Drago goes for it, and Crow quickly grabs Drago's arm. SNAP! CLICK! Drago smashes Crow in the face, before he notices what happened. Crow uses the bat to trip Drago to the ground, then Crow grabs Drago's wrist again, and slams it against a pipe against the wall. SNAP! CLICK! Drago goes for another punch, but he's been handcuffed to the pipe. Crow stands up, still holding the bat and he's standing tall over Drago. SEVEN FEET TALL?

EX : Sorry for the ruse Drago, but I needed you alone, for two reasons. One, to teach you a lesson. NEVER touch me again. And that's not just a warning Drago, I've just drawn up some contracts, looked through the rule books. From now on, any wrestler touches me, or Mosall, we can fire them. By touch, obviously I mean if you attack us. So, ANYONE touches me or Meep, they can be fired on the spot. Of course, Meep can't fire me, if you catch my drift.

EX : Shut up Drago, I can do anything I want right now, so you're in no position to tell me what to do. You see, the second thing I want, is for you to give Mosall a message. You tell him that I know what he's doing. You tell him that this whole charade has come to an end. You tell him, that I know that he's tried to distract me with a frontal assault while the weasels sneak in the back and try to take the castle. YOU TELL HIM that it's not going to work. And most importantly Drago, you tell him to fight not with monsters, lest he BECOME a monster. And Drago, I trust you'll tell him, seeing as we are both from the ORIGINAL Brotherhood. Brothers 'til the end, eh Drago? And, Drago, since your such a good messenger boy, you will get your wish. Mosall's probably looking through the paperwork right now. There will be some things for you to sign that I think you'll enjoy.

Drago tries to strugle but his hand is still handcuffed to the pipe. Ex makes his way over to the running limo, and gets into the back. It drives off as Drago continues trying to get the handcuffs off the pipe.

DR : GODDAMNIT!

"The Game" hits the speakers and the fans boo loudly for Triple H.

Taintking (c) Vs. Hunter Hearst Helmsley

Taintking and Triple H advance slowly and begin circling, each men have fought each other enough to know each other's strengths and weaknesses. Taintking makes the first move, going toward Triple H and grappling to gain an advantage. Triple H, however, stays steady and does not allow Taintking the advantage so easily. Taintking raises a knee into Triple H's gut and Triple H doubles over. Taintking takes this advantage to nail a piledriver to start this advantage with a bang. Triple H holds his neck as Taintking lays in the boots to his abdomen. Eventually, Triple H realizes his only chance is to get to his feet. Triple H uses the ropes to climb up to his feet, and he's on one knee. Taintking with a right hand! Triple H is on both knees now, but struggles to his feet. Triple H drops Taintking with a DDT, and the referee just watches in wonder, wondering how Triple H got the advantage from Taintking so quickly. Triple H's face suddenly becomes livid, and he slams Taintking in his forehead with a double-hand blow. Taintking goes down. "That's for stealing my TV Title, bitch!" Triple H mounts Taintking and begins with more rights and lefts, but Taintking seems to have recovered from the low-blow, and kicks out with authority, throwing Triple H over by the ropes. Triple H shoves Taintking, Taintking with a right hand on Triple H, Triple H comes back with a right hand of his own, NO! TAINTKING DUCKS! Triple H turns around and STUNNER! Taintking just nailed Triple H with a stunner! Triple H is on the canvas, staring up at the lights. Taintking pins him! One! Two! NO! Triple H has his foot on the ropes.
