gcw archives
Welcome to the GCW archive page. This page is, quite simply, where all the shows and some of the best RPs will be stored. The GCW has such a storied past, Executioner and I decided the best way to ensure it exists forever is to dedicate an entire web page to it. Feel free to browse the past and get caught up on GCW history (four years of it). This page is no frills for a reason. We want nothing to distract you from the sheer depth of the GCW. Quite simply, you will find the shows, archived chronologically, and the RPs in a similar fashion. We have been around for a while and have a lot to be proud of. For the new comers... check some of this stuff out. Its worth it. By no means is this every show from every week for four years... but we are doing our best with it. There are holiday breaks (New Years, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Sept. 11, etc). Its as complete as you can ask for. Enjoy.
