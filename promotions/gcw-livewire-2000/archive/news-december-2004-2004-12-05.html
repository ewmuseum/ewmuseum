     A Retro Rant

      12.02.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- The Masked Marauder has returned to write a column for Global Championship Wrestling, and the first edition can be found here. If you're interested in GCW history, this column starts from near the very beginning, November 1998. It starts just after GCW's first Halloween Horror and covers the beginnings of the run-up to GCW's first annual Survivor Match. It's expected that Marauder will continue doing these enlightening columns for all of our enjoyment.

     This Week in GCW

      11.28.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- Henry "The Hopeless" Oswald has published his first column entitled: "This Week in GCW" on this site. You can view it here. It covers everything from the best parleys of the week to the most valuable wrestlers of the week. They also detail the funniest moment of the week. Make sure you check it out.

     Ambition 5

      11.28.2004.; GCW; Honolulu, Hawaii -- The transcript for the fifth edition of GCW Ambition has been posted on the site for your viewing pleasure. It was an event in which the World Heavyweight Championship was defended against the number one contender and the in-ring return of Mike Reed (pictured right) was one of the biggest stories. The rumble for the first entrant in Survivor Match took place and all of the wrestlers took their place in history. But only one came out the victor. The show will be rebroadcast on Wednesday at eight eastern on LiveWire. A column is expected later today called "This Week in GCW".

     cW Compact

      11.21.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- Word has come in today that officials from Core Wrestling and Global Championship Wrestling have come to a short-term deal. Global Championship Wrestling and Core Wrestling will participate in several matches in upcoming weeks featuring talent from both of their prestigious rosters. Core Wrestling Chairman Matthew Lanier (pictured at the right) was said to be pleased with the deal, as were GCW President HuMus Iscariot and Vice-President Brandon Murphy. Veep Brandon Murphy had this to say: "GCW has just made a big step. We're pleased to be involved with this great organization. I'd like to thank Core Wrestling and Matthew Lanier for their continued support of GCW and I hope that our talent can give their proven roster a run for their money." The matches are tentatively scheduled to begin December 05th, 2004.

     Coming Out of a Downward Spiral

      11.21.2004.; GCW; Auckland, New Zealand -- It was a show that showcased GCW's premier talent and debuted Lexi Ciccone (pictured right). Many wrestler battled one another in a five-match event. The number one contendership for the GCW Limelight and GCW World Heavyweight Chmapionships were determined and many returns came to be. It was a night that will not soon be forgotten, and a night that will be very important to the upcoming Downward Spiral. Next week on Ambition the World Heavyweight Championship will be on the line and a rumble will occur between twenty GCW stars to determine the first entrant in Survivor Match.

     Onward

      11.14.2004.; GCW; Sydney, Australia -- In the hometown of GCW's Jimmy Thunder, the second round of the GCW Red October tournament took place. The crowd at Sydney couldn't be more enthusiastic to host the pride of GCW. Strike was in action in his second match since his return. Andrea battled with Suicide. And, in the first match, Johnny Muleson and Chris Kain did battle. The card for next week's Ambition has been posted and it looks like things are shaping up toward GCW's annual pay-per-view, Downward Spiral.

     A Change In the Wind

      11.13.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- Global Championship Wrestling, making it's way to the annual Survivor Match event, now entitled 'Downward Spiral', will be going through major changes, say many officials. Some say that a new swath of talent will be making it's way among the GCW ranks. "But never fear," one said, "your old faces aren't going anywhere." A relaunch of the tag-team division seems to be on the top of the priority list, although no one's making any promises. "We're happy to keep those belts where they are until we can launch an effective tag-team division, but we're certainly working on it." In the past the random pairing tag-team tournament has strangely brought together some unique pairs that went on to dominate the division. But for now it seems like "Ballistic" Bryan Deas has some time to rest on his laurels.

     Survivor Match 2005?

      11.10.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- After fierce negotiations over whether to move forward and abandon the successes of the past or to embrace history as a mentor have come to a close. GCW has decided that on January Second, 2005, its next pay-per-view will be held. It has been entitled "Downward Spiral" and will host an annual GCW match known as Survivor Match. Eight participants in one steel cage until only one stands, the number one contender. This is considered one of the most prestigious titles in GCW. First there is a battle royale to determine the first entrant into this fabled match. This gives the opportunity for up-and-comers, such as Brad Adamms (pictured right), to have a shot at the biggest Championship in the land. And then a random tag-team tournament to determine six other men in this match. And then a wildcard entrant will be added. The winner of this match goes on to face the GCW Champion at the next pay-per-view, which is tentatively scheduled for March Sixth, and entitled No Regrets III.

     Ratings In

      10.17.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- Ratings have come in from last night's edition of Saturday Night Ambition and the news was a surprising surge. Fans across the globe rated the commentary team as better, ratings both seemed to go up from two titles being on the line, a new administration, a new commentary crew and the return of a former Global Championship Wrestling President and Champion to the active roster. Ratings are expected to go up again next week as the tournament begins. Early rumors are that Strike will be in this tournament, even though it was previously announced that he will not be. Some believe that Humus made a deal with his arch-enemy, a spot in the tournament for the World Heavyweight Championship that Strike ran off with on Ambition. It is expected that he will return in a match against Andrea which will only bring the ratings higher. "Primetime" Joe Milone (pictured right) has been seen with his newly won championship, and so it is believed that a deal quite possibly could have been made.

     GCW's First Ambition

      10.16.2004.; GCW; Kabul, Afghanistan -- Global Championship Wrestling proudly aired its first Ambition tonight, filled with many excitements. GCW stars Andrea, "Primetime" Joe Milone, "Ballistic" Bryan Deas, Brad Adamms, Defoe Smith, A.C. James and others were all in action in this event. Two Championships were on the line, and the event proved to be a very important one for Global Championship Wrestling. And they couldn't have been prouder than to air it in Kabul, Afghanistan, where servicemen from the United States, Great Britain, Canada and others viewed the event as welcome entertainment and got to see one of the greatest main events in recent GCW memory as Jimmy Thunder (pictured right) defended his World Heavyweight Championship against "Primetime" Joe Milone. Lt. Barry Manilow described the event as "Wonderful, absolutely great stuff for military men to watch. It gets their mind off of their daily turmoils. GCW is the only federation to make Kabul part of their tour, the rest were all too scared. But we're very glad to have you. Thank you all for stopping by and we hope you come again." Many others echoed this rhetoric. The GCW tour left Afghanistan very quickly after their event to sovereign South Africa.

     Legendary Voice Retires

      10.13.2004.; GCW; Yorkshire, England -- On tour in Yorkshire, England GCW received it's second batch of bad news in two days. Due to the rehabilitation of Michael Hayes and recent statements of the new GCW President, Outlaw has decided to resign from his commentary position. He said today in a press conference: "I have had a great career, a blessed career. I've been with two great commentating partners. Bruce Spencer left years ago and Michael Hayes is in rehabilitation for his alcohol problems. I've been told by the President that its unlikely that Hayes will return to the commentary booth. No offense to the current administration, GCW, and most especially to the lovely Hillary Cortez-Rush, but I don't think that it's good for GCW if we have a duo that doesn't have a great chemistry that Spencer and I or Hayes and I shared. And so I've decided to step down as commentator. I don't need the money, I want what's best for GCW." Preceding Outlaw's commentary career were months of domination inside the ring. He is noted as one of the best up and comers in Global Championship Wrestling history. It is anticipated that Outlaw will travel with the tour when they return to the United States but in a much less vocal role, in a way that he can take time off to be with his family. GCW President Humus has already pressed Ryan Jones into the commentary role that Outlaw used to hold. The GCW President offered no comment on the situation and his most vocal critics became even more assertive.

     Rehab for GCW Great

      10.12.2004.; GCW; Yorkshire, England -- Michael "PS" Hayes, GCW commentator and former GCW Tag-Team Champion, did not get off on the right foot with the new Global Championship Wrestling administration as they caught up with the tour today in Yorkshire, England. Global Championship Wrestling President, Humus Iscariot, found Michael Hayes passed out with several bottles of alcohol lieing nearby. Michael Hayes was sent back to a detox center in the United States to sober up. Some believe Iscariot was overly hard on the beloved alcoholic, a great friend and drinking buddy of GCW's famed Strike. In any case the commentary booth had a slot that needed to be filled. It was announced today by Global Championship Wrestling officials that that void would be filled with a very familiar figure to both Global Championship Wrestling and Dynasty Wrestling Alliance, Hillary Cortez-Rush. She was appointed the position by default through time spent playing second fiddle backstage. Rumors are that Humus Iscariot is not pleased with this appointment, thinking that Cortez-Rush's tongue may very well place his reputation at stake. GCW's other commentator, Outlaw, said through his agent that he is "discouraged" at the steps taken by the administration on Michael Hayes but admits that they are necessary. "Michael Hayes and I have had our disagreements," the statement reads, "but we've always worked through them. He is a great man, a great former GCW wrestler. I'm glad that he'll have the opportunity to come back later on in his career, but detoxification is a long, hard process. I pray that he can get through it." Humus Iscariot has said that it is "doubtful" that Michael Hayes will be allowed his former position at the commentary table upon his return, but that he recognizes Hayes' unsackable agreement and will honor it and him with an on-screen job that will satisfy both the Fabulous Freebird and his fans.

     Stockholders Trade Warhorses

      10.11.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- In the past week Global Championship Wrestling has seen quite a bit of action on all fronts. Today, however, at 6:30 in the evening eastern standard time, representatives from AXIS, Global Championship Wrestling and Dynasty Wrestling Alliance went into a board room in talks. They came out two hours later with a plan. Dynasty Wrestling Alliance and Global Championship Wrestling would merge under the banner of Global Championship Wrestling, very surprising considering the momentum of both federations of late. The representatives in these meetings were The Executioner from GCW, Humus from DWA and Michael Cedeno from AXIS. Rumors are that stockholders of GCW wanted out and gave Executioner, Strike's bane, permission to buy out DWA and also to work out a new deal in which Strike would no longer be Global Championship Wrestling President. This, of course, was not run by GCW's former President, Strike. Strike was said to be irate on tour in England. In a press conference, in a fit of rage he swore revenge. "[Humus] has been after me ever since his ex-wife decided that I was better than him," Strike slurred, obviously having had liquor earlier. "I kid you all not. My contract has a no firing policy, I'm with GCW, collecting my paycheck until the end of my days. And Humus may very well be the new GCW President. But you know what? I'm going to make it hell for Humus Iscariot. I'm going to make his administration hell. You heard it right here, folks. If you want excitement in GCW, you've got it. Strike's back as a wrestler, and he ain't merciful." Strike left in a storm. Later his wife, Hillary Cortez-Rush (pictured right) would comment through her agent that Humus is "nothing more than a jealous bastard". More information is expected soon from GCW's new President, Humus, on the direction of this new GCW. This week's Legacy is still on. Early reports indicate that it is changed to a Saturday show instead entitled GCW Saturday Night Ambition.

     Key GCW Signings

      10.11.2004.; GCW; Atlanta, Georgia -- After days of dismay and worry, GCW has made three signings that their fans can be proud of. Three new Global Championship Wrestling superstars will be joining the tour in Baghdad, Iraq for the Tribute to the Armed Forces Fourth Edition of Legacy. Former GCW World Heavyweight Champion, and recent comeback king, "Sensational" Scotty Ryan is said to be very pleased with the opportunity to prove himself once more. He will be getting an opportunity to do just that as he goes against "Mad Man" Defoe Smith for the coveted Georgia Heavyweight Championship that both men have held before. The second addition to the roster is a less notable, but just as important one. Suicide, coming straight out of the indie leagues, has hit the big scene with Global Championship Wrestling. GCW officials are said to be very pleased that they were the ones to reel in this big catch. The third and perhaps most notable of all these signings was the addition of the controversial Johnathon Chernega. Chernega, a former GCW superstar, holds the record for both the most consecutive wins and the longest undefeated streak in GCW history. He surpassed that record in early 2003 under the first administration of Chris "Strike" Rush. Some believe that he lost that streak in June of 2003 to "The Guardian" Steve Jones (pictured right). The current administration does not recognize that defeat, as it is widely rumored that the match was a conspiracy to end Chernega's streak. In any event it is complete now as he lost to Brad Adamms just months ago. He hopes to avenge his loss to Brad Adamms on this week's edition of Legacy.

     Last Minute Extension

      10.10.2004.; GCW; Yorkshire, England -- AXIS officials granted GCW an extremely short extension to air LiveWire interviews and this week's episode of Sunday Night Legacy. It is interesting to note that GCW's President, Strike, was not active in these meetings for the extension. Humus (pictured right) said in a press conference after the agreement that he met with some GCW officials and that they would be working together, without Strike, to achieve a deal that would represent the best interests of both the AXIS Network and Global Championship Wrestling. One of the GCW officials, the only one present, Mr. Viktor Eustremski, said that a very important GCW official got Humus to agree to compromise. "We managed to bring in some very influential people to show Humus that we do have a good product lined up and that all he has to do is give us the means to get it out to the public and that we will all reap the benefits," Eustremski said. "It became apparent to the GCW officials that Strike was allowing some personal feelings to get in the way of these negotiations. We decided that a different touch might be needed, and it looks like we're finally making some progress." Strike is remaining with the tour, but is said not to be in the greatest of moods. In fact a British reporter said that Strike could have "spit fire". GCW Sunday Night Legacy was aired on the AXIS Network and drew "fair" ratings. GCW's current contract with AXIS will expire at 10 PM EST Sunday, October 17th, 2004, directly after the conclusion of the next episode of Sunday Night Legacy.

     GCW to Close Doors?

      10.10.2004.; AXIS; Las Vegas, NV -- GCW and AXIS reporters have both reported troubling news for Global Championship Wrestling. As many GCW fans know, GCW has not had a show since WarCry, which received a very low buyrate comparatively to GCW's other pay-per-views. GCW's contract expired with AXIS at WarCry. AXIS fans know that GCW's contract was bought up by AXIS at GCW's Coming Unglued, where GCW figuratively "died" but was resurrected months later. Now that contract is finished and negotiations between AXIS and GCW have been far less than successful. AXIS reporters have noted yelling in the conference room between two men who were once great friends, Chris "Strike" Rush (pictured right) and Humus. GCW, which has been the prime product of AXIS since it's revival, is said to be getting a cut in airtime for its LiveWire interview shows. This is believed to be because of a new and exciting AXIS fed named the Dynasty Wrestling Alliance (DWA). Yes, the same DWA that rush partnered with in early 2003. DWA fell apart in early 2004 due to mismanagement and a huge blunder that was called AWA, a byproduct of several AXIS federations including GCW. Chris Rush has refused to talk to reporters and many believe that if he cannot secure this deal with AXIS, GCW will have nowhere else to go and either fall into a non-televised scene or cease to exist. Many GCW fans are worried and hope that things can come back to the way they used to be. Some blame Humus, some blame Rush, but both are feeling the pressure to get this deal through.
