Turmoil results (8 July 2004)

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

July Eighth, Two-Thousand and Four A.D. Denver, CO

Turmoil Segment #1: "Travesty"
Biocide, GCW Staff, Walter Joseph Kovacs

(Sirens are heard all about, ambulances and people are rushing all about the streets of Denver, Colorado. The camera pans quickly to Ryan Jones, who stands in front of a car that looks like it's gotten hit by a train. Ambulances, police cars and even a fire truck is at the scene.)

Ryan Jones (RJ): "This is Ryan Jones reporting live from the streets of Denver, Colorado where an accident involving a revered former Global Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion has taken place. GCW's Biocide was hit when an unidentified vehicle swerved and collided with him head-on. The other car sped off, but Biocide's vehicle went into a telephone pole. A crowd has gathered around, it's confirmed that Biocide is unconscious. His injuries are serious and he will is being taken to the hospital as we speak. Any information about the other car should be given immediately to the Denver police force with their toll-free number. 9-1-1. I repeat, 9-1-1."

(Jones looks over his shoulder.)

RJ: "Thank you for watching this special report, I'm Ryan Jones."

(The camera pans out to show Walter Joseph Kovacs looking at the television screen with wide eyes. He turns back to the camera and a twinkle enters his eye.)

Walter Joseph Kovacs (WJK): "Yes ... yes ... what a wonderful idea, precious ... WJK will make everything alright!"

(Fade.)

 

Turmoil Segment #2: "Statement"
"Primetime" Joe Milone, "Mad Man" Defoe Smith

(The camera fades in and we see Defoe and Milone standing in a room in front of a PTE symbol)

Primetime (JM): "To all the people who thought Primetime Enterprises was dead, they are sadly mistaken! As long as Defoe and I are standing and breathing PTE lives!"

Defoe (DS): "That’s right Joe. This Sunday at Manifest destiny I will go on to defeat David Angleon and win the Georgia championship. David, I am not messing around, I will take you to your limit and when the dust settles it will be my hand raised in victory."

JM: "That’s right Defoe and well deserved too. Now onto my opponent Tony Aielli, Tony I will not let you try to make a name for your self by humiliating me. Just like the people want to see Defoe win the Georgia strap, the people want me to become the number one contender and move on to Wrestlebowl and realize my destiny of becoming the GCW world heavyweight champion. Tony ever since you worsened my concussion, I’ve been counting down the hours until we go one on one and nothing will please me more to take you for a trip down the walk of fame, a trip you may not survive."

DS: "Oh but Joe what of our good friend Shawn Blaster? Because I know if I get my hands on him I’m going to tear that Mother-"

JM: "Easy Deef, this is a PG show. Shawn maybe your just a little insecure with the ladies, or your tired off living at home with your mommy or that your jealous of us, whatever it is stay out of our way! Blaster I swear you will regret ever sticking your nose in our business."

(Fade…)

 

Turmoil Segment #3: "All Forgiven"
Strife & The Otaku II

(The camera fades in on Otaku II in his locker room stretching his legs in preparation for his match. Suddenly, he hears a knock at his door and Strife walks in. Otaku looks up from his stretching.)

Strife (S): "Hey, how it's going?"

Otaku II (O2): "I am well, Strife. My knee hasn't felt this good in a while."

S: "Nothings up really, you up for this match at the PPV?"

O2: "Yeah. I've been training hard even while I wasn't getting matches so I'd in top shape for my return."

S: "That's cool, but I just wanted to make sure there was no hard feelings between you and me. You know, since I kicked your's and Deas's ass in my debut match."

O2: "None here. Business is business. It was every man for himself. Besides, I haven't wrestled in a while, I want to win."

S: "Cool, but I don't think we have anything to worry about. Who are we going against? Lucy Flagg & Kai? We can take them easly 1,2,3."

O2: "Don't get arrogant. Graham Pendleton, or Lucy Flagg, or whatever he wants to be called now, is a very, very dangerous man and Kai is a tank."

S: "I'm not worried about it and you should't be either. They are push overs and we are going to prove it."

O2: "Listen, I speak from experience. GGP was once my greatest rival. He's no pushover."

S: "OK, but I know from experience as well. If you worry, you lose. Why do you think I lost to Deas at the last PPV?"

O2: "He cheated, if memory serves."

S: "Yeah, with a sharpie. but I also lost because I had doubt. Without doubt my friend, your enemies can't touch you. You will be undefeated."

O2: "Doubt nothing. I'm just saying that you should respect what your foes can do. This is GCW. The top of the crop. Nobody here who wrestles on a regular basis is without talent. Maybe these two aren't the next coming of Radically Awesome or Catch 22, but they're here for a reason. They can definately wrestle."

S: "You know Otaku, I have wrestled before too, in another fed. I was another man, fighting the good fight with my Bro. I beat the best of the best and I will do the same here with or without your help."

O2: "I never said I wouldn't help you. I'll do my best in that match and you can guarantee that if I see you in a pinch, I'll do my best to help."

S: "I know. I appreciate that too Otaku. It's time we show our opponents that we are ready to kick ass! What do you say?"

O2: "I say I'm as ready as I'll ever be."

S: "OK, good to hear you are ready, see you in the ring."

(Strife turn and leaves, and Otaku goes back to his stretching.)

 

Turmoil Segment #4: "Seeing Double"
Maher Reims, Hillary Cortez

(The scene fades in backstage. Maher Reims is scrambling. He's wearing his old "Man of the Moment" t-shirt and racing through doors.)

Hillary Cortez (HC): "Hey Maher, can I get a word about the title - ?"

Maher Reims (MR): "No ... no, I've got to go see Biocide. I need to go. Bye."

(Hillary narrows her eyes as he runs off.)

HC: "Since when has he cared?"

(Suddenly Maher walks by from the hallway.)

HC: "Hey ... I thought you were going to see Biocide?"

MR: " ... what?"

HC: "You just said you were going to see Biocide."

MR: "I don't have time for that Hillary. What have you been smoking?"

(Hillary looks at Maher and shakes her head, absolutely shocked. Fade.)

 

Turmoil Segment #5: "A New Leaf"
"Mad Man" Defoe Smith, "Primetime" Joe Milone

“And now we take you back to Tuesday, where we caught up with Defoe ‘the British Brawler’ Smith just as he was about to address the crowd at a GCW house show. This is what ‘The Mad Man’ had to say…”

(The scene starts with Defoe stood in the middle of the ring and at first the whole arena was chanting ‘asshole… asshole’, but as the camera zooms in on the two smartly dressed wrestlers you can see that they are not laughing at what the crowd thinks, they actually seemed concerned by the fact. Joe suddenly leans closer to Deef and whispers something to his team mate, then shakes his head. Defoe raises his hands to beckon for some order and indeed silence, they all hush up a bit but not completely, so Defoe starts to talk anyway.)

Defoe: "OK…so we more than likely deserve all of these asshole chants, but just listen up for a minute …please people!"

(The crowd simmer down to complete silence.)

Defoe: "I know that over the last year Prime Time Enterprise has done some pretty awful things…globally, but believe me folks, from now on in things will change. So first on behalf of Joe Milone and myself I would like to apologize and promise you all that a new leaf has been turned, I am not saying that we will angels or anything like that, but we will do our utmost to prove to you all that we are not asshole’s."

(Defoe steps back and Joe steps forward, he takes a quick glance around the arena, looks at Defoe who nods and then he begins to speak into the microphone.)

Joe: "As an act of good will, if you present your ticket stub to any toy master shops that sell our action figures, you will get a chance to buy one and get one free, and that’s not all! The first one hundred people to present their ticket stubs will receive both ‘Mad Man Defoe Smith’ and ‘Primetime Joe Milone’ action figures totally free."

Defoe: "Nice one Joe, now onto a more serious note, as you know next week is another huge pay per view, Manifest Destiny …and Denver is in for a real treat that night I can tell you, with all of the titles on the line it will be one hell of an evening, and who knows maybe even the third time lucky for me. But that’s enough about me, as I want to address that mean old…urm I mean I would like to address MR Blaster. You see, when you made my partner and my friend Joe Milone wrestle last week, you could have ended his career, or worst still he could of died! We believe that you are a threat to everyone’s safety and something should be done to stop you, what that something should be, well everyone has their own opinions. "

Joe: "Thank you Deef, that was a valid point you have just made, but I would like to add that I am still able and WILL compete at Manifest Destiny, I would also like to wish all competitors the very best of luck for there matches. Thanks everyone for taking the time to hear us out, I am sure it means as much to Defoe as it does to me."

(Joe drops the microphone to the floor and exit’s the ring along with Defoe, the crowds reaction as the two remaining members of PTE walk up the ramp was mixed but one thing is for sure…these two super stars are about to take their business to another level.)

“So there you have it folk’s, make sure you tune in for GCW: Manifest Destiny, until then, watch your backs going out…and watch your backs coming in.”

 

Turmoil Segment #6: "Sic Transit Gloria (Glory Fades)"
"X Factor" Tony Aielli, Hillary Cortez

(The scene opens focused on a door. A gray door. More specifically a gray door with a blue nameplate and white letters that reads "The Recluse" Tony Aielli. Outside this door stands Hillary Cortez, smoothing over a dark blue business suit that fits closely to her body showing off her curves and good figure. Her hair is tied behind her head in a business like ponytail, and she grins mischievously before reaching one hand up and knocking three times on the door.

For a moment, nothing happens.

Hillary Cortez shakes her head, the small grin on her lips widening ever so slightly before she knocks three more times. Time passes.

Again, nothing happens.

She giggles to herself, the grin on her lips widening even more, her fist rising to bang on the door again. Just as she is about to knock, the door opens and standing there before us is "The Recluse" Tony Aielli. He's not disheveled. It doesn't seem as though he's rushed anything. He just stands there, simply, in the form fitting short sleeve black tee shirt and long, tight fitting, cuffed blue jean pants pants and shoes. Long hair hangs simply and loosely about his face, loose strands over cool gray eyes. He hasn't applied his face paint, but there are no signs of lipstick or anything else. Hillary Cortez, herself, seems a bit surprised with the composed sight before her. Tony Aielli grins upon seeing her, stepping out and shutting the door rather quickly behind him.)

Tony Aielli (TA): "You rang?"

(Hillary Cortez nods, smoothing her suit once more before pulling the microphone in her hands to her lips.)
Hillary Cortez (HC): "Seeing as nobody really ever interviews you often on GCW, besides Shawn Blaster--"

(Tony Aielli cuts her off, that grin still lingering on his lips.)

TA: "Don't remind me." (Hillary Cortez smirks as she continues.)

HC: "As I was saying, I was wondering if I could ask you for your feelings tonight on your match against "Primetime" Joe Milone at Manifest Destiny next Sunday."

(Tony looks down at her, a rather complex look set on his face.)

TA: "That's all you want to ask me?"

(Hillary nods, rather surprised he would ask her that.)

TA: "Well, what more can I say then has already been said? What do you want me to say?"

(Hillary Cortez shrugs, answering.)

HC: "I...I don't know. Just maybe how you feel about the match. Your odds?"

(Tony Aielli gives her a look as though she's gone mad.)

TA: "Are you serious? This is why I avoid backstage interviews, there's really no point unless you specifically arrange the interview. No offense to you, you're a great interviewer. But, there's nothing more that can be said. I have to face Joe Milone. I have to beat him to earn a shot at Adam Masterson, title or not, at WrestleBowl. He's a good wrestler but I can beat him. I'm not scared of him. I don't let his antics get to me, because I know it's all just a front for him. He has ability in the ring, whether his maturity outside shows that or not. I have to respect it. But I won't let him get the best of me. I prepared for him just like I prepared for everyone else. I'm going to get into the ring with him and face him. And I will beat him."

(A slight pause.)

TA: "Simple as that."

(A momentary silence.)

TA: "Any other questions?"

(Hillary Cortez nods, perhaps a bit stunned.)

HC: "Shawn Blaster..."

(Tony glances down at Hillary Cortez, shrugging.)

TA: "What about him?"

(Hillary Cortez continues her question.)

HC: "What's with this new alliance we have started seeing in the recent weeks?"

(Tony Aielli shakes his head.) TA: "You're enjoying that question entirely too much." (Hillary Cortez smirks, as if to say, "Oh well.")

TA: "I have no alliance with Shawn Blaster. For all I care, he shouldn't even be involved in my match with Milone. He's a head figure in GCW, and that's fine. He has a problem with Milone, and that's fine. But I do not have an alliance with him. I don't have an alliance with anyone. Not yet anyway."

(Hillary Cortez nods and Tony Aielli uses the pause to his advantage.)

TA: "Now, if you'll excuse me..."

(That grin slips back to place on his lips as he opens the door and slides back into the locker room, shutting the door swiftly but quietly behind him as the scene fades.)

MH: "He definately does have better things to get to."

BK: "You can say that again."

MH: "He definately does have better things to get to."

 

Turmoil Segment #6: "The Show Must Go On"
GCW

(The scene of a long office table at GCW HeadQuarters can be seen. Stockholders sit around the table, arguing and bickering with each other. One speaks.)

Stockholder (SH): "Listen, we know what we need to do. The show must go on. You'd think a tragedy like this would draw our President out of hiding, but he's not here. We know that. It's our responsibility to make the decision. Biocide may very well be fine after this is said and done. They can't really sue us. We need to appoint an interim Vice President while Biocide is gone. Now, let's buckle down, and let's select him. We'll have him there for Manifest Destiny. And God be with Biocide and his family."

(The stockholders nod, take a seat and the scene fades.)
