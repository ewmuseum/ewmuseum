Rules

Global Championship Wrestling has been a successful promotion for four years, much longer than the average lifespan of a federation today.  That being said, we have rules in place, and they are there for a reason.  If you are found to be in violvation of these rules, you will be dealt with dpending on the situation.  There is not a lot of tolerence for stupidity, so be warned.  If The rules below are too much for you to follow, then we wish you the best of luck in all future endeavors.

Overview

GCW is a roleplaying based federation with a 25% focus on angles.  This means that there wil be times when predetermined outcomes will be sought after, and both parties will be notified beforehand.  Unless otherwise noted, consider your match, and thus the furthering of your character Your Responsibility.  This includes, but is not limited to segments, roleplaying, and submitting of angle ideas to the administration. 

Any questions regarding any decision should be directed to the President or Vice President PRIVATELY at all times.  Under NO circumstance is it acceptable to use Livewire as a form of venting towards the Administration.  These topics, if found, will be deleted upon sight, and punishments will be given out as needed.

Cbaracter and Conduct

First and foremost, any theft of roleplays from another federation, recycling old ones, or otherwise 'reusing' of material that has already been published once will be met by immediate release.  There are no buts about it here, folks, that's just the way it goes.

All Characters must be original (Adapted with acception during the Chris Rush era)  There are a few members who have been granted exceptiion to this rule because of their history with the company before this rule was in place.  However, as of October 16, 2004, only original wrestlers will be accepted.

All Out of Character Matters must be placed inside Livewire, and not on the Parley Board.

If you fail to appear on the Parley Board for two weeks without notice, you run the risk of being fired.  COMMUNICATON is the best way to ensure that you are accounted for and do not lose a match simply because we did not know not to book you.

You are expected to be able to roleplay on a weekly basis.  There are exceptions to this, and arrangements will be made when necessary.

Roleplaying

There is no set limit or format for roleplays.  All we ask is that your work is complete (with a beginning and end) and it is able to compete with those already on the roster.

You are graded on the quality of your work, not your ability to tell a completely irrelevant story.  Now, if you tell a story, and it is used as an outlet to better establish your character, it is considered relevant.

'Keyfabe' or using Out Of Character terms in a roleplay is frowned upon, and your grade for that week will suffer for it.

 

.Please note that these rules are subject to additions, addendums, and other changes without notice.  If you have any questions, pleaese visit the staff page for contact information.
