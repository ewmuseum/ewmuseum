Legacy Opening: "A Champion's Farewell"
Ronnie McNeil

(Ronnie walks into the arena, and the crowd ROARS! He enters his locker room, sits down, pulls out his newly won Georgia Title, and loks into the camera.)

R. McNeil (RM): "WarCry was one of the best moments of my career. I finally got somewhere here in GCW. I'm the Georgia Champion. And now, it must all come to an end. Dr. Andrews tells me that my knee is deteraiorating, and that if I don't slow down, I run the risk of losing my leg. That's something I don't want to live through. So with a heavy heart, I hearby vacate the Georgia Championship, and announce my retirement from GCW as an active wrestler. I'll still be around, but not in the ring. Thank you.

Otaku II: "Ladies, gentlemen, fellow otakus, I am here right now because I want to remind you of something very, very important. This is an election year. Some out there complain that the government does not represent them, that government does not help them. I ask them, “Do you vote,” and every single one of them say that they do not. How can government represent one when one does not tell government what to do? Do you expect to get food when you don’t give the waiter your order? Of course not. There will be no excuse for you if this year you do not register. You can ascend to power, and tell the government what YOU want it to do. Thank you."

Match #3: Singles Match
Donavan Faust Defoe Smith

Defoe and Faust circle each other as the bell rings. They both feint shooting in for a grapple. They finally lock up. Defoe applies a hammerlock. Faust reverses, and then he gets reversed. Defoe keeps the hammerlock, and hits a Steele Slam. Faust gets to his feet, shaking his arm a bit, and smiles as the Madman of GCW, as if to acknowledge that yes, Defoe Smith can put up a decent fight. Defoe decides to stop wasting time, and goes right as Donovan Faust. He opens up with a flurry of punches to wear down his opponent. Faust covers up as best he can, but when Faust blocks high, Defoe begins kicking Faust in the midsection. Faust eventually begins doubling over, so Defoe Smith drops him with a DDT! Defoe mounts Faust and rains down clubbing blows with his fists. The referee has to step in and pull Defoe Smith off to check on Faust. Meanwhile, Defoe gets out of the ring and walks along the barricade demanding a “bloody chair.” Someone finally gives him a seat, and he takes it with him into the ring. Defoe blasts the ref from behind with the chair, then pulls up Donovan Faust. Low Bass onto the chair! LOW BASS ONTO THE CHAIR! Faust is dead! The Madman drags the unconscious ref over to where Faust lies, then makes a loose pin with one arm as he makes the ref’s limp arm count three with the other. The bell is rung in hopes that Defoe will go no further.

Main Event: GCW Television Championship
Andrea GCW Television Championship "Freak" A.C. James

Both AND & James stare off from the start of this match. AND is starting the trash talking and is telling James that she will walk out of here as the brand new GCW World Television Champion but James is just shaking his head no. Now, they both slowly circle around the ring to size each other up and then, they lock up in the center of the ring. AND uses her strength to push James back against the ropes, but James grabs them tightly and stops himself from going over the top rope and as he glares at AND, he dusts himself off. AND then looks at James and nails him with a huge spear as James is besides himself as AND is just celebrating like she won something. James gets up by dusting himself off again, and then AND charges at James again, but James moves out of the way as AND hits her shoulder on the steel ringpost. Then, James grabs AND & executes an excellent belly to back suplex on her. James goes for a quick cover; 1...2....and no, AND is able to kick out. James picks up AND by her hair and irish whips her into the ropes, but James makes the mistake by lowering his head, so AND capitalizes on this by nailing James with a piledriver. Then, AND runs off the ropes and nails James with a knee drop right to the face. And then picks up James by the hair and lifts him up for a military press slam but as AND has James up in the air, she throws him over the ropes and onto the ring floor as the crowd is in shock as this. James is down on the ground, holding his back in pain. Then, AND climbs to the top rope as James is just getting to this feet, but as James gets up, AND flies into the air and nails James with a double axe handle to the back area, as James goes down again.
AND picks up James by the hair and irish whips him right into the steel steps. AND gets back into the ring as the ref begins his count but AND doesn't want to win like this, so she goes back to the outside, and grabs James by the hair, but James pokes her in the eye and then dropkicks her in the stomach area in which she goes down, and then, James gets back in the ring as the ref continues to count; 1..2..3..4...5...AND is getting back to her feet; 6..7...8...but AND gets back in the ring by the count of 8 and the ref stops the count. James runs in the opposite direction and as he comes off the ropes and tries to do a Lou Thez press onto AND, but she counters it into a huge spinebuster, but which both combants are down on the mat. AND is trying to get to her feet and she does, but so does James. AND charges at James with a clothesline but James ducks and then, he kicks her in the gut and attempts to nail AND with the EndGame Stunner, but AND accidentally pushes James right into the referee. Then, AND charges at James again, but James ducks and hits AND with the EndGame Stunner. Then, all of a sudden, "Ballistic" Bryan Deas comes from the back with a baseball bat in hand, but James doesn't see him, and then, Deas nails James from behind, in the leg, with the baseball bat. Then, Deas puts down the bat and picks up James and nails him with the Last Chance. He then quickly revives the referee and then, he exits the ring. AND doesn't know what happened, but she sees a fallen James so she crawls over and puts an arm over James as the referee makes the count; 1....2.....and no, James gets a shoulder up as both AND & Deas can not believe it. Deas runs back to the ring area, as he jumps on the ring apron, but then, James knocks Deas off the apron with a right hand. Meanwhile, AND sees and grabs the baseball bat that Deas left in the ring so AND nails James in the ribs with the bat, and then, she throws the bat out to the floor. The referee doesn't see all of this, cause he is still a little out of it. Then, AND rolls up James for a pin attempt as the referee has come to and makes the count; 1...2....and 3! The referee calls for the bell and hands the GCW World Television Title Belt to AND as she celebrates in victory as Deas comes in to join her, but James gets back in but Deas throws him out of the ring as both Deas & AND celebrate in victory.
