"Everyone has their Breaking Point."

Or so they say. But how many breaking points will be surpassed this very night? Who will snap and why? Are you, as a fan, prepared for the epic proportions of Global Championship Wrestling's second pay-per-view extravaganza? Two is not a memorable number, number two is always overlooked, but in GCW that's different. Breaking Point shall ever be remembered as a turning point in the overall large-scale war. If not memorable now, it shall be classic later. Two of the biggest superstars in recent wrestling history square off for the biggest prize of them all in the square ring where it all comes together. Will Jimmy Thunder again falter and fall before the GCW World Heavyweight Champion, or will he take what the fans will he have for his own and cast Maher down and end his reign of destruction?

Here I am, On The Road Again.

That is yet to be seen. Four titles will be on the line tonight. The legendary brass knuckles championship, the Unsanctioned King of the Deathmatch will be on the line between the new champion "Ballistic" Bryan Deas and "The Franchise" Ronnie McNeil. That match will be followed by a match that will have unbelievable technical skill with wrestling prospects and legends mixed in. Graham Pendleton, Robbie Styles, Gizensha and "The Prodigal Son" Jeff Harris will be fighting in a No Holds Barred match to hope to relaunch or launch their careers to heights potential. The Television Championship will be on the line in an elimination match-up. David Angleon, Brad Adamms and "The Freak" A.C. James will be challenging the new Television Champion, Andrea, for her belt. The match afterward has been built up for nearly a month, and that great feud will come to a head under the watchful eye of Donovan Faust. Point-Blank and Defoe Smith will finally meet mono-e-mono. Finally the feud that hasn't had much time to simmer, the champion, "Sensational" Scotty Ryan, made the bad choice of selecting "The Ruler" Theodoric Solomon, to battle him tonight for that Georgia Heavyweight Championship. Solomon is out for gold after being cost the Television Championship last week. And then after that World Contendership is on the line between "The Tyrant" Michael Reed and "The Guardian" Steve Jones. Steve Jones has incapacitated the once seemingly-stable duo of MR Squared, and Michael Reed will be out to spill Jones' blood tonight, if only for his own private satisfaction.

There I am, Up On the Stage.

And finally the main-event that was once only dreamt about. Jimmy Thunder, a man who tore through the ranks of wFw and moved on to DWA to conquer it. Within one year Jimmy Thunder achieved a great deal more than anyone would ever expect. He is a pearl amongst shiny rocks, and he gets his shot at Global Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship gold here tonight. But it won't be an easy bout. Jimmy Thunder will be facing the man who dared challenge the history of GCW, who dared to spit in the face of everything GCW once was. He will be facing the World Heavyweight Champion, the man who is "All Souled Out". He has innumerable tricks up his sleeves and he will be employing all he has to retain that World Championship here tonight. One thing can be said for sure, Jimmy Thunder will not be laying down for Reims. The wildcard in this match, of course, is Jimmy Thunder's new manager, Michael Reed. Has Reed truly turned on Maher Reims or is it all a ruse to get Thunder vulnerable.

There I Go, Bein' a Star Again.

But, as always from a GCW production, the unexpected will spawn from the expected. For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction, and tonight one might expect the inverse of Broken Hearts. The set-up may be similar, the names familiar, but in the end expect neither congruency or the inverse. Lightning doesn't strike in the same place twice. Tonight GCW will hand you an event to be remembered and may you never forget it. I present you the best talent in the best company on the best show thus far. I present you GCW Breaking Point.

Deas reaches out for the ropes with no avail, Ronnie leaning back further and further, Deas tapping furiously on the ground. The ref watches over this with an urge to call the match, but realizes that it’s a hardcore match. He finally taps McNeil on the shoulder, Ronnie letting the grip go. McNeil then decides to finalize the match, pulling Deas to his feet. He then pulls Deas’ head between his thighs. He then pulls Bryan up, dropping to his bottom for a piledriver and covering him for the pin. The ref drops to the floor, giving McNeil and easy One…Two…Three.
