Match #3: Singles Match
Kristian Alfred Fundo I GCW UKotD Championship Strife

Both competitors are inside this hell of a structure but Fundo doesn't know what to do in this match but Strife is just licking his chops, just liking his type of a match and looking to end Fundo, right here tonight. Strife and Fundo start off in a lockup, but Fundo gets Strife in a side headlock, hoping to get an advantage in this weird match but Strife elbows Fundo right in the stomach area and irish whips Fundo into the ropes but Fundo reverses the irish whip and whips Strife into the ropes as his back hits the barbwire and he goes down like a sack of potatoes. Fundo sees that Strife is down and he is holding his back, he kicks Strife right in the back with his foot. Then, he irish whips Strife into the barbwire ropes again, and he hits hard. Fundo is now smiling that Strife can't fight back. Fundo then looks the other way and sees the weapons on the poles, so he climbs one of the turnbuckles and grabs the sledgehammer hanging from the pole. Fundo then climbs down and waits for Strife to get up, and as Strife is getting up, Fundo charges with the sledgehammer in hand, and nails Strife with the sledgehammer right in the face. Strife is now busted wide open as Fundo is smiling at him. Fundo then points at the straight jacket which is hanging from the other pole, but as Fundo walks towards the turnbuckle, Strife is getting back to his feet as Fundo just looks on in disgust.

Fundo goes over to Strife and attempts to nail him with a right hand but Strife gives Fundo a low blow, right in the groin area as Fundo goes down. Strife then looks at the straight jacket but then, he turns around and looks at the other pole and sees a barbwire baseball bat. So, Strife climbs the pole and grabs the bat from the pole and climbs down the turnbuckle. He waits for Fundo to get up, but as Fundo gets up, Strife swings the bat and misses Fundo, and Fundo nails Strife with a low blow, right in his groin area. Then, Fundo gets up and grabs the barbwire baseball bat and begins scraping it on Strife's face. Fundo then picks up Strife for a military press slam, but somehow, Strife pokes Fundo in the eye and lets go of Strife as Strife lands on his feet and then, Strife dropkicks Fundo in the back as Fundo goes down like bricks. Then, Strife grabs the sledgehammer, that is laying on the ground, and waits for Fundo to get up again, and as Fundo gets up, Strife charges at Fundo and nails him with the sledgehammer, in which, Fundo goes down again.

Strife is now bleeding from the head, and he senses victory. He looks at one of the turnbuckles and climbs up it and grabs the steel chair and climbs down it. He lays the chair right in the center of the ring as he picks up Fundo, and hooks him up for the Strife Spike and nails him with it perfectly right in the center of the ring. Strife is now sensing victory as he climbs the turnbuckle, and then, he climbs the pole and grabs the straight jacket. Strife gets ready to put the straight jacket on Fundo, when all of a sudden, everyone sees someone coming through the ring, but Strife nor Fundo doesn't see it as the hole is revealed and out comes Bryan Deas. Strife doesn't see Deas behind him, but Deas pulls up Strife by the hair and irish whips him into the barbwire ropes as Strife does down. Deas now waits for Fundo to get up as Deas charges at Fundo, and nails him with a big boot to the face as Fundo goes down. Then, Deas sees Strife down, so Deas waits for him to get up, and as Strife gets up, Deas nails Strife with the Simply Ballistic. Then, Fundo tries to get up, but Deas grabs Fundo by the throat, but Deas just stops and pushes Fundo away. Fundo just looks confused and backs off of Deas and then, Deas waits for Strife to get up as he is slowly getting up, and as Strife gets up, Deas picks up Strife and nails him with the Last Chance. Then, Deas picks up the straight jacket and puts it on Strife as Strife is out like a light. Fundo is just watching on as he wants nothing of this. Deas picks up Strife and throws him on the outside platform where the casket is. Deas then opens the casket and puts Strife in there and closes the casket. Then, Deas grabs some matches and throws them in the hole, which causes on fire and then, Deas grabs the casket and throws it into the fire pit with Strife still in it. He then smiles and laughs as the referee calls for the bell. The UKotD belt is declared vacant .
