Singles Division

The singles division of GCW has been one of the most competitive in e-wrestling history. The Television Title has been a launch pad for success over the years, as the names on the list prove. The Georgia Heavyweight Title is the belt that is soiled with the blood of some of the greatest and most memorable feuds in the history of the federation. Of course, the World Title needs no description. Even with the NeWA World Title reigning over the GCW Title at one point in the vast history of the federation, there has always been an aura around it. To hold that title is to hold both the greatest prize and the heaviest burden. The Curse has claimed many, and many others simply cracked under the pressure. But no matter what happens to people once they win the title, everyone will tell you that shiny belt is the ultimate goal.

GCW Television Championship

Defended At Least 1 Time Every 2 Weeks The GCW T.V. Title has been held by men who are starting the climb. While some people argue differently, this championship title is mainly described as a stepping stone to the two "big titles". While this title is sometimes overlooked, it usually has some of the toughest competition in the world going after it.

Extreme Title

( Defended under Brass Knuckles rules (also known as the Unsanctioneded King of the Death Match) Upset with the 'restrictions' of GCW's rules and regulations, Cyan Dracos established the Unsanctioned Title, out of the remnants of the then retired Cruiserweight Championship. The title is defended in GCW sanctioned matches (i.e. a GCW ref is present) although it can also be 'defended' without GCW officials present. Sanctioned Title defenses are signed between 'champion' and 'challenger' under stipulations designated by the champion and the winner is given the title. However, outside of the 'sanctioned' matches, title changes can also occur through 'capture' of the belt. Since there is no governing body for the title, there is no way to force someone to return the belt to the rightful owner, and therefore whoever possesses the belt is the 'champion' as far as GCW is concerned. Although the first two champions have chosen to defend in 'DeathMatches', any type of match can be used, whether normal, shoot, martial arts, etc. . . it is up to the champion to decide. MMM renamed the USKODM to the Extreme Title on 8/19/02.

GCW Georgia Heavyweight Championship

Defended At Least 1 Time Per Month. One of the two "big titles" in GCW, this title is named after the home of the promotion, the state of Georgia. This title has been around since the beginning of the "new" GCW, and has been a focus of competition to get the number one contender spot for the biggest of the all, the World Title. This title has many more benifits than the GCW T.V. Title, and is second only to the GCW World Title.

Tag Team Division

Truly, the GCW Tag Team Division has had the most ups and downs of any other section of GCW history. From times when the division was red hot, when the likes of Outlaw and Ma$e fought the Horsemen, to times when the division was nearly dead, the tag division has always proven to be interesting television.
