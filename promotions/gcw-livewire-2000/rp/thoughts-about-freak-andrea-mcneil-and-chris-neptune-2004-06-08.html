Thoughts about Freak, Andrea, McNeil, and Chris Neptune
Brad Adamms
Global Championship Wrestling Parleys #199
Date: 8 June 2004
Location: Genesis

::The scene is backstage after Genesis. Brad Adamms sees the cameraman walking towards him and stops.::

Brad Adamms: This is where I am supposed to go on about how I am not going to be a stepping-stone. This is supposed to be where I say that ‘Brad Adamms is going to beat Freak’s a$$ with the thousands of Bradiacs in attendance cheering him on.’ Well, that is just boring to me. Its been done before. I’ve done and said that before.

The thing that stands in my mind the most is that Freak and I are so similar. We work on the same wavelength. But we are also equally different. We both respect talent. We both know that in order to get anywhere in this business, we need to win matches. To win matches, we need to keep our concentration on the task at hand. He does it for different reasons though. He is driven by greed, the lust for money and fame. That is all nice and fine, but I do it for a different reasons. I get satisfaction by putting on a great performance, both athletically and mentally. I honestly enjoying giving the fans pleasure with my wrestling. That is where we differ. He could care less about the fans even though they are the ones that put the cash in his pocket, the stylish suits on his back, and the four star meals in his stomach.

Freak, I am not scared of you. I have stood in front of you twice in recent weeks, nose to nose, and you have put your hands in the air, almost surrendering, and walked away from me. You see, what Evan and myself did to you backstage was something you earned. Not to use one of my favorite sayings, but you got what you deserved. What happened to Andrea was just a matter of her being in the wrong place at the wrong time. I do know that Evan enjoyed taking her out almost as much as he did you, Freak. The Franchise knew it was best for him to let it continue and just walk away.

But enough about them, I have to focus. I have a match at this week’s Turmoil against Chris Neptune; The Lords of the Depths. Once again, I have to say that Chris Neptune is not a wrestler that I have much knowledge on. I do look forward to facing him in the ring. I wish him the best of luck in our match even though I obviously look to be the victor.

::Fade.::
