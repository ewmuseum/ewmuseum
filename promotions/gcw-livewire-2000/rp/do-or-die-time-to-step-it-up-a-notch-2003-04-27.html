Arez · 27 April 2003, 01:27 AM All rp's count for GGP vs. Arez (for a spot in the New Faith) Arez · 27 April 2003, 03:56 AM Personal Journal Entry # 473 3-22-03 "The Big Day" I can't sleep. So I'm sitting here, watching my baby (read: Fiancee) Renee sleep. The "stadol" shot administered by Kay, the RN, has in fact knocked her loopy. She looked like she'd gotten into a J.D. chugging contest with Hiro and held her own, for a human. I hear the machine pulsing along that is monitoring our still yet unborn child's heartbeat. I glance over at the television to see Godzilla fighting some six-legged alien being as the Japanese stare on in horror. "What's going on?" This Japanese woman asks quizivally, and I look over to see a pained expression on my girl's face just as a contraction peaks. "What is going on?" I think quietly to myself. With all this in the Middle East, the END TIMES seemingly happening already. How far away is Judgement Day? I fully expect to hear Gabriel's trumpet pierce the air at any given moment as Armageddon falls upon us all. And in my infinite "wisdowm" bring an innocent child, not just any child, MY CHILD into this....THIS!! My flesh, my blood. A part of me that will live on after I'm gone. Personal Jounal Entry # 474 3-22-03 "The Big Day (continued) Now I'm looking down into my baby's eyes. God, they're so beautiful, SHE is so beautiful. While I may have achieved a moment of clarity and perfect happiness while holding her, I can't help but feel guilty. Guilty for bring her, an innocent, into this awful world. I know that there will be times where she is unhappy with life, and there will be nothing that I can do about it, that is except unleash the dogs on the first Sixteen year old punk that steps foot on my lawn. Every channel in this room has ongoing coverage of this war, save the cartoon network (and there's only so much Scooby Doo that a man can stand, heh, "those darn kids."). I watch my daughter sleep, and I wonder how many soldiers are over there in the desert looking at pictures of their children that are halfway across the world from them. They are fighting for, defending, and dying for their country. Whether this war is about freedom, oil, terrorism, or vengeance makes no difference. It's happening, and there are father's and mother's that will never see their babies again. My heart goes out to them, as does my support. I watch people around the world burning the American flag. I watch the protestors. I hear people, Americans, MOTHER FUCKING AMERICANS booing during the playing of our National Anthem, and it makes me sick. Where was their anthrax laced envelope? Why weren't they inside the Twin Towers in NYC on that fateful September day. They should be the ones getting tortured in some forsaken foreign county instead of the soldiers that are. Those men and women are laying their lives on the line for us so we can have a life of freedom, have the luxuries of going out and catching a movie or sporting event, and this...THIS is their thanks. So yes, I feel guilty, somewhat, for putting my child into all this, but then I look down to see her smile. It warms my heart, a joy that some may never know. I see how lucky I truly am, and I thank God almighty for this blessing. I look at her and see life, beauty, purity...and I finally know what TRUE love is. A golden dagger pierces the flesh and talons rip out his still beating heart. Vivid tales of incalculable pain with Death's hand having it's part. Martyr's rejoice this murderous hour at long last their will turns to power. Cries of a nation tears of the poor the day of reckoning has begun once more. Slaughter and mutilation control the headlines while greed and cowardice control their yellow spines Tales of a hero, the sirens do sing. The clattering of millions, as the end times ring. GCW Journal Entry #1 4-27-03 They've grown stronger over these last few weeks. I contemplate still, their true meaning. Is it this new Fatherhood thrust upon me with my subconscious running amok, causing these strange dreams? Is it me knowing that I'm at the end of my rope in wrestling, and this is my final go-round in the business causing me stress while adding THAT MUCH MORE pressure on me? Or maybe, maybe it's something from my past crying out to me, needing to be heard. A grim reminder of all those I've wronged over my career and throughout my life. Could it be though, a voice from the future, my future, sending me messages through the times. Forewarning me of the violence that lays in wait. Which is it? Is it even any of those that I have in mind? I don't know the answer to that, all I know is this. Time will tell, as it always does. I wait with baited-breath to find out myself, but I'm assured that these dreams will only get worse, far more bitter, far more worrisome, and far more realistic the further I march along on this quest in GCW. *The scene opens, moments after Blitzkrieg goes off the air. Fans are filing out of the arena, some of the wrestlers have already made their way out the door as well, only a few still remain. Arez is one of the few that are still here. He sits in a metal folding chair, his hands clasped together. He's wearing an old "Hellrazors" t-shirt from the days of the dojo. Tattered and torn, weathered and faded beyond it's years to say the least. His black gi pants are unravelled around the bottoms and slit up the sides about six inches or so revealing a pair of black boots. His black and grey H-D race team jacket is folded neatly on the back of the chair he is leaning against. Something odd for Arez, he smiles, proudly, then begins to speak.* Arez: So I've returned. The true me. Not a man behind a mask, as Aurick Ulystre. Over the years in GCW, I've come and went. I've been a nomad. Always feeling like I didn't belong. It was in my own stupidity though. Through selfishness and ignorance, I've dug myself a hole that few would care to try to get out of, and even fewer that could. Good or bad though, I'm back, and back with a vengeance. *Arez runs his left hand through his shortly cropped jet-black hair, then strokes his color-coordinated goatee.* Arez: I know I've a lot on my plate now. More so than Pendelton. I've got a lot to prove. There's been a gnawing at my soul, my very core these last months, as I've finally come to realize and understand GCW. It struck a nerve in me, some would say finally given me the motivation I've always needed to do in GCW what I've done everywhere else I've ever been. Every other ring I've ever stepped in, I've dominated everyone. Why didn't I do this in GCW, you ask? Lack of motivation, lack of respect for GCW. It's the truth. The elders know it, they can see it more clearly than any of you, the boys behind the curtain. It's never been a lack of talent, not to sound conceited, but anyone with half a brain can see that I've always had the tools to be a major player inside GCW. Time and time again, I've felt like that I've gotten the short end of the stick, when in all actuality, I've been tested, and failed. I can see that now, whereas pride blinded me before. Now pride...pride sometimes gets in the way of your objectives, your goals in life. The fate gods will sometimes put unimaginealby difficult decisions in front of you. It will make you step back and re-think, assess your priorities. Do you want that World Title bad enough? Bad enough to kiss the bosses ass? Not a question I've ever had to ask myself, but I'm sure a few in GCW have had to once upon a time. Some replacing the World title with the Georgia, or Television, or hell, maybe even their spot in GCW. Decisions, they're a bitch. *Arez stands up, grabbing his jacket off the chair and slides his arms through the sleaves. He walks over to the door, flipping the light switch off. He steps out into the hallway, stretching his arms and taking a deep breath.* Arez: Graham Pendelton, you are now faced with a decision yourself. Do you want that spot in the New Faith bad enough? Huh? Surely you've went back and watched the tapes of Stable Warz past, and seen the carnage. Watched as careers have been shortened, or even ended. Some walk away from Stable Warz a changed man. Some changed for the better, and some for the worse. Some are never the same. Do you want to have your career ended at Stable Warz? Surely you don't, but if you do, if you want it that bad, and decide to face me in the ring next week on Blitzkrieg, I will bring that burning question to the forefront, and show you what hell is really like. *Arez tugs at his collar for a moment, then continues.* Arez: If you think what Biocide did to you was bad, if you think those injuries, that pain...if you think that was a testament to your manhood, think of this. Biocide did that out of nothing...NOTHING. He had no reasons, aside from merely wanting, WANTING to hurt you. I have a reason. I have the motivation. I want that spot in the New Faith. I will lead the New Faith into Stable Warz, OVER your dead body if need be. I've a lot to prove to GCW, as I will admit, I've dropped the ball MORE than a few times when I've come here. That's all about to change though. I'm going to make a statement, to anyone and everyone in GCW...past, present, and future. I WILL be a force to be reckoned with. You, you're nothing. Like I said to you earlier after I Yakuza Kicked that chair into your face, you're not strong enough to be in the position you are in. You have no place, no business there. I'm here to right a few wrongs, both yours and mine. I will take you out of your position, and I will show ALL of the ones that say I'm past my prime that I'm not, I will show all of the ones that say I'm a non-entity that I am, I WILL SHOW EVERYONE WHY I AM EVERYTHING ....EVERYTHING ....EVERYTHING ....everything that I say I am! *Arez turns to walk away, but the camera follows him.* Arez: Pendelton, I've got so much more riding on this than you do. I'm sure that this will be a mid-card match at best, something that I've not had to deal with since I first broke into the business. Still though, still you're in over your head. You don't have the slightest clue as to what my mindset is right now. You are just a stepping stone. You are to be but a footnote in my career. You will be the first casualty in my ascendance through the ranks of GCW. Finally ...finally, I will right all the wrongs I've done to GCW. To do that though, to get the start I need to have, I have to go through you. Trust me Graham, please trust me when I say that WILL NOT be a problem. Think about it Graham, you yourself have to admit you are outclassed here. Look back over your career, and look back over mine. Whose made more of an impact? What is it that you've ever contributed to this sport? Hmm? What sacrifices have you ever had to make for the betterment of a promotion, aside from taking an ass-kicking by your 'ole lady? *Arez turns back around facing the camera while smirking, then crosses his arms.* Arez: Pendelton, you're in for the fight of YOUR life. YOU are about to be tested. I want your spot, and I am more than competent enough to take it. Nothing stands in my way, aside from your pathetic carcass laying unconscious thanks to an Arez Driver or the Sealer of Fates. Gear up, buckle up, knuckle up Pendelton, I'm gunning for you. I'm aiming to take you out. Out of the New Faith, out of Stable Warz, and if you try me, out of wrestling, PERMANENTLY! *Arez glares into the camera as the scene fades out.* Arez · 30 April 2003, 01:36 AM I sit and recount all these midnight horrors, where a demon waits, lurking 'round every corner. That look of elation gleaming in their feral eyes, means to those who witness every last one dies. Gruesome and bloody, showers of crimson rain, they beg for the end to release all their pain. The demons oblige, Whilst the "Morning Star" does rejoice. For he has garnered more souls, they no longer have freedom nor choice. Soon they all succumb, to these mortal wounds. And their carcasses since buried beneath those desert sand dunes. Vengeance is brought though, on "Azrael's" swift and mighty wings. While all the demons cower running scared of all things. ~~~--==BEEP!==--~~~ *Arez is jolted from his sleep by the sound of his alarm clock buzzing. He wipes the sweat from his brow, looking to his left. A woman lays beside him, her long auburn hair flowing across the pillow. "Sleep well Renee," he whispers, gently patting her hip. Arez eases out from underneath the covers, sliding his feet into a pair of well-worn houseshoes. He preses a button on the alarm, then steps over to a white bassinette. He looks inside it, and you can see the joy in his eyes. "Good morning Alyssa, Daddy loves you!" Arez steps away as the scene begins to fade out.* GCW Journal Entry # 2 4-30-03 I had another of those horrid dreams last night. Seems they are growing closer to each other now, as I have them more frequent. They are more potent now as well. The images are stronger, sharper, more clear. Dreadfully scary, the end of the world. Angels fighting Angels, killing. The words, the poems that I remember, simple as they are, I still can't understand them. Something with the delivery, begging to question why. I'm unsure at any rate. I've heard though, that dreams can be the gateway to the futre, a looking glass. I've also heard that everything is the opposite of it's true meaning in a dream. Everything is flipped, switched, moved around. I dunno. It's all so confusing to me, hopefully soon I'll have a grip on it. I may need to get in touch with Hiro about this...hell, maybe even Kojima. *Thick steam clings to the mirror, and you see Arez with shaving cream covering his face. He dips his razor in the sink, shaking off the water before gliding it down the side of his face. He continues this a few times, and you can see his eyes very clearly now. His face reminds you of careworn map a few years old, the vessels in his eyes seem akin to an interstate. Even after a night's sleep, Arez still looks tired, his eyes a dead giveaway. It's early yet though, granted he hasn't fully awoken, not without his morning cup of coffee that is. Arez steps into the shower, the main source of all the steam, passively lowering his head, letting the water beat down on his neck as he rolls it from side to side. His jet-black hair is soaked, it's now laying down flat. The look engraved on his face now points towards aggrevation in Arez' direction, his spirit seemingly wounded.* ~~~--==A few hours earlier==--~~~ *A heavy fog clings to the walls of this evergreen valley. The surrounding mountain tops are made to look even more ominous and majestic. A jet engines hum can be heard from miles below. The gentle noise of raindrops falling onto a foam plate commands most of the attention to sound. Cigarette smoke is blown away by an eastwardly wind, which brings along with it a slight chill. A dilapidated car, originally white, now more of a cream/rust color rumbles by, piercing the serenity. Black-gum tree limbs loom over a lush clearing, mistle-toe hanging off a few branches.* "Goddamnit Renee, I'm going back, END OF DISCUSSION!" *A baby's cry now bludgeons your thought process, directly shattering any clear train you may have had. Indoor now, you see a woman with auburn hair (same woman as before) pulled back tightly in a top-knot swaying to and from, holding an infant. Arez steps into view, instantly recognizable by his build and the way he carries himself. He's wearing a black "Slayer" sweat-cap, long-sleeved black shirt with a few intricate designs done in red, and a pair of ankle-slit levi's.* Renee: All I'm saying is WE NEED YOU! SHIT! She's barely a month old... *Renee fires back in the verbal joust with a lethal blow.* Arez: DON'T FUCKING ACT LIKE THAT! Don't act like I'm skipping out on the both of you. You FUCKING KNOW I LOVE YOU, and GOD KNOWS I LOVE HER TOO! I'm trying to provide for this family.. *Arez is fuming, his blood pressure raised a few notches as the muscles are tensed to the maximum in his neck and face.* Renee: Bullshit, BULLSHIT! You're doing this to satisfy your own ego. Damn you, damn your pride! You want to see if you can still cut it, that's all. The money isn't the issue, we both know it. *Arez is taken aback by this. Money isn't the issue, he must admit. They could survive, but he wants more for his family, a better life. He knows he can give that to them, but he knows it will be a difficult task to perform, on all fronts, especially the home. To him, it being GCW and having the opportunity their to correct everything he's done wrong is merely a bonus.* Arez: One more run...One more tear, that's all I need, all WE need. I'll be able to retire comfortably then, and take care of the both of you the way I WANT to take care of you, can't you see that? Renee: You've just justified it in your own mind, so you won't seem as selfish. *Arez slumps down into the back of the couch, placing his hands on his forehead, then pulling the cap off only to throw it into a far corner.* Arez: Why the hell can't you be more supportive of me? Huh? Why the fuck can't you just see what I'm trying to do here? IF I DIDN'T WANT TO BE HERE, I FUCKING WOULDN'T BE! If I didn't love you, I wouldn't be with you. Regardless of Alyssa. I want it to be till we're old and grey. *Arez retorts with vigorous conviction before his eyes ease into a calmer state.* Arez: It being GCW, that's just a bonus. I have messed up so much there. I've got a chance, one last chance, to prove my worth STATESIDE ya know? Yeah, maybe it is to stroke my ego some. I know I'd never be happy in life in just lying down and giving up now. I have this golden opportunity here, can't you see that? It's right there, in my hands one more time. One last time. I can't pass it up baby, you know that. *Arez drops his head, resting his elbows on his knees and folding his hands behind his neck. Arez reaches to the dash, flipping on the radio. The chatter of a local station's commercials are heard for a moment before Arez speaks up.* Arez: Pendelton, I at least pegged you for someone that had some form of backbone to them. I guess I was wrong. Three days I've waited for word from you, to see if you accept. Three days I've been disappointed. Then again, that's another common thing with you, being a disappointment. *Arez leans back in his seat, and for the first time today, grins. You can almost see his eyes light up behind the Oakley sunglasses.* Arez: I can't say I'm surprised though, something inside me said that you wouldn't want to be tested by the likes of me. Now you're showing it though. Now you're showing how weak you truly are, by running scared in the face of adversity. First sign of turmoil, first sign of a blocked path, and you stop dead in your tracks. You don't want it bad enough Graham, that's why you'll be forced throughout your career to be what you are now, a no-talent hack who jerks the curtains open for the rest of us that give a damn. *Arez places both hands firmly on the wheel, then continues.* Arez: Do you think you'll have the balls to step in the ring with me this week on Blitz Graham? Think you can grow a set by then? I hope so, at least for my sake. I'm wanting to work some of this rust off me. If you can't make it though Pendelton, I understand. You're not the first chicken shit sonuvabitch I've ever had the displeasure to ever have to babysit through a match in my life. That goes with the territory though I guess. *Arez reaches down into the passenger-side floorboard, retrieving a bottle of water. He untwists the cap and takes a generous drink.* Arez: It goes without saying that on Blitzkrieg, I will beat you, and lead the New Faith into Stable Warz, it is common knowledge.
