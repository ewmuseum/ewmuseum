Continuing To Kill The Snake, D2 first victim, Reed Next

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is One wrestler’s topic in the GCW Studios roleplay forum of the fed’s LiveWire X2 board, 2 posts from 6 April 2003 to 8 April 2003, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 31 January 2004 (topic 2 of the six on the page the fed kept at gcw.e-wrestling.org/gcwarchives/5031.html). Each post opens with the poster’s board name and its date and carries the post’s own signature; the board’s member details, address lines, links and design credit are not carried.
Biocide · 6 April 2003, 09:34 PM All Rps count towards Biocide VS Michael Reed King Of The Deathmatch Title on the line if he accepts The tail of the snake has been dismembered so lets move on up. Tyrant of GCW VS Tyran of the Undergrads Seal Your Fate To The New Faith Lets Get Down With The Sickness -------------------- "Oh people, know that you have committed great sins. If you ask me what proof I have for these words, I say it is because I am the punishment of Our God. If you had not committed great sins,Our God would not have sent a punishment like me upon you.'' "Seal Your Fate To The New Faith" Biocide · 8 April 2003, 05:10 PM [A wood splitting screams is heard as we begin the slow fade in. Three series of rapid booms are heard as we see the focus. Biocides' head is busted open with blood pouring down his face. The locker in front of him is dented from the repeated head butts he has given it. Two words appear on the bottom of the screen.] ~After Blitz~ [Biocide is dressed as he was before. His all black wrestling attire, but tinged with blood soaking in. Off camera we can hear a banging and Biocide stops. His head slowly turns the door. The blood is dripping down his cheeks and onto his shirt. His eyes are cold, and dark. A suppressed anger boils up inside him.] [Biocide closes his eyes for a second and stands straight. He walks towards the door and opens it, his eyes staring directly at the person on the other side. The man standing there is clad in a blue jacket and pants. His polo shirt is white tinged with a little dirt. His head is covered with a particularly bad comb. The man appears to be in his thirties. He looks at Biocide, with a mixture of shock and disgust.] Biocide: Where are they? [Biocide’s voice is cold. Desperation leaks through with every word.] ”Uhm… Uhm…" Biocide: ANSWER ME! [Biocide’s eyes flare in anger at the man’s stutter. Biocide’s shout is loud and piercing. The man gulps and takes a deep breath. The predatory eyes of Biocide focused on him.] ”They left the arena, and headed west. Do you want me to call the cops.” [Biocide sneers.] Biocide: No, I want you to call the funeral parlor… cause I’m going to kill those f—kers. [Biocide pushes past the man who spins away, the back of his jacket reading security in yellow letters. Biocide walks down the hallway like a man possessed. He walks down the drab hallways as nameless people step aside in a mixture of fear and disgust. Biocide finally arrives at the exit and walks out into the parking lot. His eyes shifting around.] [Biocide turns his head slowly and takes a half step back. He looks behind himself and glances at a yellow coat clad valet. Biocide licks some blood off his lips and smiles wickedly. He turns to the young man.] Biocide: Where is my car? ”It was take…” Biocide: Taken? ”Yes sir.” Biocide: By whom? ”The said they where called, Scum Of The Earth.” [Biocide shakes his head and mutters to himself.] Biocide: Dead men walking. [Biocide reaches his hand into his pocket. He draws forth a small black and silver cell phone and flips it open. He carelessly wipes the blood from his eyes as he begins to dial. He slowly draws the phone up to his ear.] Biocide: I need a taxi. [The scene fades out.] [The Next Day] Biocide: God. [The voice of Biocide is heard, cold and distant. He sits propped up against a brick wall. His left leg is straight and his right is bent. His foot pressed firmly into the concrete sidewalk. Biocide’s head is bowed.] Biocide: A subject that several people want to talk about. [Biocide is dressed in black athletic pants with a blue stripe; his sweatshirt is dark blue with The New Faith written in black. His white sneakers dirty yet in good shape. His brown hair is wild and unkempt.] Biocide: It seems that faith is like the wind, you know it’s there even though you can’t see it. The problem is this, I can feel the wind when a cool gust of air blows across my face. [Biocide slowly lifts his head up. His forehead still bandage from the dame inflicted on him, and by him.] Biocide: I have never felt faith and I have never smelt faith or saw it. Nor can it be heard. So you want to tell me about it being there, and once in my life I had enough of it to agree with you. Now I have had the love of my life ripped away from me, and you want me to talk about faith. [Biocide closes his eyes. He winces from his internal torment.] Biocide: If I have faith in God, that means I must accept the devil as real. What do I do about my dead friend? I saw his face in the casket as it was placed into the ground. Barely twenty-one and he is already dead. He left his child fatherless. [Biocide exhales deeply in despair.] Biocide: I looked upon his face, and it was pale. It was at peace. He left the world with my sister; he left the world with my two students Russ and Drake. I have had to deal with the grief since the accident; no God saw fit to dim it. It burns like a conflagration inside me, eating me up inside. [He pauses, wincing again.] Biocide: Yet God saw fit to let a demon return in his guise. Should I have faith in that too? Should I believe that my Family has risen like Christ, but with the purpose to destroy me and steal the soul I have inside? Should I allow him to torment what is left inside me, all because of a God that doesn’t care about us? [He shakes his head slightly, so slight hat it barely moves.] Biocide: Tell me the answer? [He places his right hand onto his right knee and rubs his chin with his left hand. His eyes are cold, and filled with hopelessness. He drops his left hand and grabs his ribs.] Biocide: You can’t, can you? [He lets slip a no hearted laugh and looks deeply into the camera.] Biocide: Reed have you ever seen the women that you love beaten. Have you ever heard her scream out in pain? Have you ever touched her swollen head after she was attacked because of you? Have you ever smelt her perfume mixed with her spilt blood? [His eyes narrow.] Biocide: Do you really want to play ball with me, kid? [He breathes in deeply, trying to hold his rage inside.] Biocide: I feel sorry for you Reed, cause unless I get my hands on the SSR, I will take my frustration out on you. I’ve been robbed of my happiness, and what could have been your best day will turn into the darkest hour. You are damned to feel my pain, my rage. [Biocide slowly rises to his feet, using the wall to aid him. He uses his right hand to brace himself as he cocks his head towards the camera.] Biocide: GCW and SSR want me to reawaken the deadly side of Biocide. Well sorry; I already beat you to that, a few months ago. You want me to be a monster? Be careful what you wish for. [Biocide takes a step off the wall and clenches his fist. They begin to turn white under the pressure he is placing them under. He exhales deeply expelling the darkness within.] Biocide: Though I will not become a monster, I will get what is mine back. I did not lead you down the path your in SSR, you did. You aren’t good enough, and though I have my faults, I am. I will slay the beast. [Extreme close up on Biocide’s face.] Biocide: Cause that’s what the Sickness does!. [Cut to black.] -------------------- "Oh people, know that you have committed great sins. If you ask me what proof I have for these words, I say it is because I am the punishment of Our God. If you had not committed great sins,Our God would not have sent a punishment like me upon you.'' "Seal Your Fate To The New Faith"
