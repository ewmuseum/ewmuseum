MichaelReed · 17 April 2003, 10:44 AM All this pain I’ve been going through has been because of just one single person stealing my belongings. But somehow, this is now my problem. I can only blame myself now. I had the chance to get my beloved pictures back and I didn’t. I was delirious and passed on the opportunity. I FORGOT ABOUT THEM! These memories were what I was fighting for and forgot them. How can this day become more painstaking? I haven’t felt this depressed since a day in February 1990. February 11th, the day the world was shaken and Mike Tyson was defeated by James “Buster” Douglas. That was the day my hero and idol became vincible. After that day, Mike Tyson was sucked up by a torrent of ill-famed events. He went from “The Champ” to the man who said “I eat cho’ childwen”. So now the big question is... where are my pictures? Who the hell has them? Who has the lack of heart to give me yet another low blow? Oh well, I’m gonna get them back this weekend. I guess you don’t need pictures to reminisce those memories. Why do you need still pictures when you can have moving pictures. I guess you don’t need pictures to reminisce those memories. Life sucks, the past rules. Example: (Mike Reed and Shaun Warren are sitting on Mike’s white couch in his living room. Their voices are inaudible, but they are chuckling some and it looks like they are having a good time. They are talking about past things they’ve done, good times they’ve had. Their voices become loud and hearable.) Shaun Warren (SW): ...and that’s when she said, ‘That’s not a cat, that’s a Rototiller”. (They both burst in laughter and Shaun falls off the couch. Shaun continues to lay on the floor as Mike stands up and stretches his arms out.) Mike Reed (MR): Man, grab that black television remote next to you and push play. SW: This one? (Shaun points his hand at the remote and Mike nods his head vertically. Shaun grabs the remote while still laying on the ground facing towards the television. Mike is sitting on the white couch in anticipation, waiting for the reaction of his sometimes overly-zealous compadre. Shaun points the remote towards the TV, pushes the ‘TV Power’ button and then pushes the ‘Play’ button. Mechanical noises come from the old VCR that is located an inch or so in front of the TV. The view zooms in a little closer and this kid looks like a young Mike Reed, go figure. Who could have guessed, the tall lanky teenager next to him looks a lot like the beast they call “S-Dub”. The camera zooms in all the way, so the visions on the television are only of those on their TV. The kids are laying back against their seat and chillin’, just shooting the breeze. Mike is wearing a Buffalo Bills sweatshirt, while you can’t see what he is wearing for pants. A red Micheal Jordan Chicago Bulls jersey is resting over Shaun Warren’s shoulders. Little Mikey begins to speak in a slightly higher voice than what you hear from Michael Reed now...) MR: Hey all, this is Mike Reed and we’re going to the National Sports Convention in St. Louis. This is my buddy Shaun Warren... (He points to the lanky Jordan fan next to him) SW: Yeah, we’re here to see Hulk Hogan, Dan Marino, Joe Montana, George “Iceman” Gervin, Wilt Chamberlain, Gordie Howe, Kirby Puckett, Nolan Ryan and ANDRE WARE! MR: But you know the real reason we are here... SW: ...to see the greatest boxer in the history of the world, the one and only... BOTH: MIKE TYSON! (They said Mike Tyson very much in unity, seeming like the things they just said were pretty scripted.) SW: The man is pretty much invincible. He is undefeated and is gonna whoop Buster Douglas’ ass... (The man driving the car looks back to the back seat straight at Shaun with a stern tone) SW: Sorry Mr. Reed, I won’t say that unless I’m not around you next time. MR: That is Geoffrey Reed the Third, also known as Mr. Reed or my dad. He’s gonna drive us down to St. Louis because he is awesome. While we are running around like paparazzis in the National Sports Convention, he’ll be chilling in his hotel or bowling or at strip clubs or something. He’ll do whatever he pleases. (Shaun is still cracking up in the background about the “strip club” comment, he knows it is pretty true.) MR: By the way, this little guy controlling the camera is Cheeko D’Amico, or C-Man. You’ll have to bear with us with the camera angle being very low. Geoffrey Reed (GR): Damn guys, after all this driving you’d figure you’d notice when you’re at your destination. (The camera zooms out at the huge dome. The guys open there doors and jump out immediately. The camera tries to follow them but the door is slammed in his face and he runs into the sliding van door. MR: Cheeko, what the hell are you doing on the ground? WE’RE HERE! (Mike grabs the camera and helps Cheeko up. He gives the video recorder back to Cheeko and they run towards the distant super-dome. SW: Of course, Mr. Reed... we won’t do anything we’ll regret when we are twenty-six and watchin’ this on video. GR: That’s good, I gotta run guys. See ya lata... I was like “what the f*ck?” (Mike and Shaun rip off a few swear words because everyone knows that only the cool kids swear. They’ll cool it down as the trip goes on. They begin to walk up a flight of stairs to an entrance to the show. They quickly move up the ample amount stairs swiftly, but cameraman Cheeko trips again and the camera goes rolling all the way back down the stairs. The camera’s view is now fixed on the sidewalk with people quickly moving by it. You hear Mike shout in the background.) MR: WHAT THE F*CK, CHEEKO? I’M RENTING THAT SHIT, MEANING IT IS NOT MINE! (The screen pauses) ==The camera zooms all the way out to show Mike and Shaun sitting on the couch== SW: Man, I gotta go to the bathroom. (Shaun runs off the couch and into another hallway) MR: Well, I guess that is perfect timing. I’d like to speak about my match at this point. First off, who could possibly be better than The MR Connection? I mean, we have a cooler name than Turner and Hooch, that is for sure. But who could possibly have more potential than Mike Reed and Maher Reims. I STILL haven’t been defeated since my return to GCW at 9-0. Maher is now a top contender for the Georgia Heavyweight Title, he’s been around for like two months. The sky really is the limit for this team. If all goes planned, this match is all ours. The competition really is overrated in this case. I’ll start with the team of Graham Pendleton and Guardian. GRAHAM PENDLETON IS BACK! GRAHAM PENDLETON IS BACK! Here’s the question: who cares? You were never anything special in your first run in GCW. Why should anyone fear a Graham Pendleton coming off an injury and a three-month break? Guardian wins one match and he is suddenly on top of the world. It’s odd how things work like that. I work my hardest, beating quality opponents and not losing a match. And somehow this Guardian guy was here for a shorter time than me, lost half of his matches and was handed a very prestigious title, single-handedly fucking over the rich history and credibility of Global Championship Wrestling. Another thing that Guardian loved commenting about recently was that I have not appeared on LiveWire yet. So what is your point? Do I need to address these scummy fans every time I breathe or wipe my ass? I’m not underestimating the fact that those same people are pretty damn sick of you. Who would want to see Guardian’s face every time they are looking to see Mike Reed’s? Another knock I have against you guys is that you are affiliated with Biocide. Biocide is the most cowardly, low-blowing person I’ve ever seen in GCW, or in the world of wrestling, period. Then there is Taintking and Vortex, the happy-go-lucky team that form Catch 22. I don’t have as much to say about you guys. I’ve already beaten Vortex pretty easily, and The MR Connection plans on doing the same to both of you. Again, any group of people that is affiliated with Colin Baird, a man I’ve beaten three straight times, must be pretty bad. And then there is the “Mad Machine”. These guys must be like the most feared guys in GCW. First, there is a guy in Fox that it took four years to finally win a title. It took Maher a month to win that exact same title. Kind of ironic that Maher Reims will be getting his revenge very soon. Otaku, I’ve beaten you on various occasions. You’ve always been in my shadow and you deserve to be there. There is nothing you can do to become better than me, because you can’t and never will be. You guys can sit there, play in your oatmeal and act like you guys can beat ANY of these teams, but you can’t until you disprove that theory. (Shaun comes back from the hall and sits back down onto the couch. Mike looks at him..) MR: Damn, it took you long enough. To be continued...
