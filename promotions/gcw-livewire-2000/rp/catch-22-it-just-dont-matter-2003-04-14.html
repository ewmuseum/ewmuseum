"It has come to my attention that several of my opponents in the GCW have failed to grasp the simple concept of a 'deserving champion'. Years ago, the idea was simple...work your way up through the ranks, soundly defeat every opponents that stands in your way, and ultimately reign on high as the best in the business. New talent runs into the federation automatically looking for the benefits, like the GCW Corporate Credit Card, the company limo, the media exposure, and last but certainly not least, the lucrative paychecks. These are all things that have become the new focus in a modernized GCW. I feel it is my responsibility to calmly explain what I call the '6 Easy Steps to Deserving Your World Title'." STEP ONE: The Unsanctioned King of the Deathmatch title, or for short, the UKOTDM Title. This is the platform belt for those wanting to get their foot in the door. Many people feel this belt has no meaning, that it is given to those who don't deserve the others, but on the contrary, it is a quite prestigious belt to hold. SOME people don't understand this, and wish to move on to other belts immediately, but this is not a sound task and ultimately leads to the lack of credibility of those more..."over zealous" talents. So, in other words, f**king start here. STEP TWO: The Cruiserweight Title. This step is sort of a wild card, because not all wrestlers are fit to wear it. No, I don't mean they lack the talent, it's just that the belt is so small that only men of a certain weight class and under can fit it around their scrawny waists. I jest. The Cruiserweight Title is a worthy title to hold, and recently it's honour has been brought back to it by it being posessed by The Irish Fox. *cough* So, in other words, this is your NEXT f**king step. STEP THREE: The Television Title. Here is where we begin to see the rise in the ladder. This is where the names are made. The Television Title is a very good title to hold, and if it's owner defends it well, it is proof that they are willing to go that extra mile and are deserving for a shot to one of the big two titles. For the most part, GCW talent with a little experience in their back pocket begin vying for this title. After being in the GCW for a couple months...this is definitely the title you should pursue. Being defended every two weeks, it forces it's holder to be the best he can be, proving himself for the Gorgia Title, and ultimately, the World Title. So, in other words, try f**king making a name FIRST. STEP FOUR: The Tag Team Titles. As you move up in the singles brackets, you'll begin to notice that you require an edge to most of the wrestlers that are at your level or above in order to make it in this business. The Tag Team Titles give you a perfect oppurtunity to learn an extra skill you may have lacked before: Teamwork. Some of the greatest World Champions of all time were part of major tag teams. Crow, Bloodstorm, Drago, Michael "Meep" Mosall, the Executioner, all of these men have been part of Tag Team Championship holding tag teams. The ability to learn to work as a team will allow you to even further your own ability in the ring. Also, as you move up in the GCW rankings ladder, you'll begin to notice that many more people are putting you in their sights, so it never hurts to have a teammate to back you up when you need it. So, in other words, GET SOME F**KING FRIENDS. STEP FIVE: The Georgia Heavyweight Title. Now we start moving into the last two steps that seem to be the steps everyone wants to skip to. Let's face it, most of the newer talent in the GCW want to have their cake and eat it too, and not worry about earning their titles. What's the quickest and easiest way to a title? Fine the champion of a high up title, piss him off until he gives you a title shot, and then beat him on a technicality. That's what I've seen lately. The Georgia Heavyweight Title is reserved for those with some considerable GCW experience on their belt. They need to have proved to GCW administration that they're not some fly by night waste of space. It seems this requirement has been dropped, lately. This belt should only be pursued if you have completed steps one through four. It's the best way. Ask any alcoholic, and he'll say that you can't skip eight of the twelve steps to recovery without something terrible happening. So, in other words, wait your f**king turn. STEP SIX: The WORLD TITLE. This belt is the mother of all belts, and should be treated with the utmost respect and admiration. The best of the best of the best in the past have worn this title. No one person is better than this title. This is the pinnacle, the apex, the climax, the top of the hill. Once you earn this title, it's all downhill. It doesn't get any better than this. People who have fought many battles and have had experience with most, if not all, of the previous titles, should make the step up to this level. This is where EVERYONE wants to be. Here is the unfortunate part. This belt has so much class associated with it. It has to much style, grace, and gives it's bearer a new outlook on the GCW just by having it around his waist. Knowing you have become the best of the best is something that's overwhelming at first, but then you defend it as if you were fighting for it again. This is the level where few will rise. So, in other words, NOT A F**KING CHANCE, PUNKASS. Anarchy is MY Business...and Business is GOOD!!!
