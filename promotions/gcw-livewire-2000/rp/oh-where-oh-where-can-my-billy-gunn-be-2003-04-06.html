Taintking · 10 April 2003, 02:11 PM Casa De Taintking, Downtown Atlanta Baseball season has begun. What else would any true American be doing other than watching baseball on TV? The Taintking, like every other red-blooded American man is enthralled with a sport that requires one ball, a stick, and some gloves. This year, the Taintking has gone all out in his love of baseball, as he has joined several fantasy baseball leagues, giving him yet another excuse to follow baseball. However, along with fantasy baseball, comes the obsession of following EVERY single baseball game that involves a player on the Taintking’s team. This large task is possible however, with the help of Cable’s exclusive “Baseball Lovers Pack”. This pack, for extreme baseball lovers, has 162 games, plus the post season, for all 30 Major League teams. How much closer to perfect can life possibly be for the Taintking? Since the beginning of the baseball season, and lasting up until the World Series has finished, the Taintking’s schedule is remarkably consistent. 1. Wake up early and go to the gym, two hour workout. 2. Come home to his apartment, shave and shower. 3. Eat lunch is next on the agenda, either at Lebuis’ Steakhouse or a quick bite at the apartment. 4. For the rest of the afternoon and for most of the evening the Taintking takes refuge on his extra long, L-shaped couch, flipping back and forth between different baseball game. When possible, the Taintking will watch his favorite sports team of all time, the New York Yankees. Now, the Taintking isn’t the typical, front-running Yankees fan that makes them the most hated sports team in the country. No, the Taintking is a true Yankees fan, the type that still rooted for them in the late 80s and early 90s, when they were today’s Tampa Bay Devil Rays. The Taintking can remember watching Yankee games when his favorite player of all time, Don Mattingly would wear his Yankee Captain uniform and play with more heart than anyone else in the game. 5. For dinner, the Taintking usually orders out, Vietnamese, Pizza, and Indian food containers are regulars. Each of these choices can be easily washed down with a Fuschino’s Finest. 6. Watch more baseball, however, if no games are on, the Taintking takes pleasure in playing “All-star Baseball 2003” for the remainder of the early evening. 7. Either go out and get his proverbial “drink on” or go to the gym again. This of course depends on who he’s fighting in the upcoming weekend. For pay per views, big matches, etc, the Taintking of course hits the gym until about midnight, when he goes to bed. For matches against wastes of space like Guardian, Ross Majors, and his match this weekend, Alkaline, the Taintking goes out to get shitty. All work and no play would make the Taintking a dull boy. Of course who wants to be a dull boy when your competition hasn’t cut a promo in seemingly years? Not the Taintking, so drinking heavily on weeks against these morons is a must. Tonight is one of those late nights where the Taintking must make his decision to either go out and party or go to the gym and pump some iron. Noting that Alkaline is his opponent this weekend the Taintking, he makes the obvious decision…to go out. 220 West 48th Street, Downtown Atlanta Vortex paces back and forth outside of the Hooters, in downtown. Looking back and forth at his watch, he continues to pace. A few minutes pass, as Vortex continues to pace, looking up and down the street as he walks. Finally, disgusted, Vortex reaches into his pocket, pulling out his Cingular cell phone. With a couple presses of buttons, Vortex hold his phone up to his ear. 530 West 48th Street, Downtown Atlanta The Taintking cruises down West 48th in his jet black Audi. Tonight is a warm evening and because of this, the Taintking has his windows down, treating everyone in earshot to his wonderful musical selections. Currently, Audioslave is pumping through the Taintking’s 6 Bose speakers. In his change pocket, in the front of his blue jeans, the Taintking feels a gentle vibration. Reaching into his pocket he sees “Tex Cell” flash across the front LCD. The Taintking flips the top of his phone and presses “talk”. “Sup playboy? ” ”Where the hell are you? ” “Sorry, the Yankee game went to extra innings, I couldn’t leave the house. ” ”Thanks jerkoff, it’s not like it’s my idea to come to this. ” “What? To Hooters? You got us a table already right? ” ”No and no. I meant coming to watch this stupid hockey game and I didn’t get a table because I’m waiting for your late-ass. ” “What are you scared of girls? You know I’m habitually late, just go inside and get a table, I’m almost there. By the way, we’re not talk about just any hockey game, we’re talking about Cornell Frozen Four hockey. ” ”You care about that game because? ” “Moron, first of all, college hockey is exciting as hell, secondly, you know my brother goes to Cornell. ” ”That’s right, he did get all the brains in your family didn’t he? ” “Damn that hurts coming from the guy who’s too scared to go into Hooters because there’s girls in there. Don’t be a homo, just go in there, I’m sure the girls won’t bite…at least AT work. ” ”Just get here asshole because I’m sure this hockey game is going to be as boring as sitting through a Guardian promo. ” “Sweet, just found a parking spot. Don’t wait for me to hold your hand going into Hooters, get a table and a pitcher of Fuschino’s. Be there in a couple. ” Vortex, hangs up his phone, still shaking his head. (to himself) ”Dumbass needs to learn how to read the clock. ” After parking the car, the Taintking makes his way to Hooters. Vortex, just gets seated at the table as the Taintking peaks his head in the door. Vortex motions him over to a table near the bar. Smiling at every single waitress in Hooters, the Taintking walks over to the table and sits down. “Order us a pitcher yet? ” ”Settle down, it’s on its way. Who’s Cornell playing in this game anyway? ” “University of New Hampshire. ” ”How’s Yale’s hockey team? Probably not as good without Coolguy going there yet huh? ” “Yale blows, Cornell destroyed them. ” The waitress, who is tall, well-tanned, with blonde highlights walks over to the table with a tall pitcher of Fuschino’s Finest. Two ice cold glasses accompany the pitcher as the waitress sits down at the table. She pours the two GCW superstars beers. ”Howdy yall. ” Not recognizing who they are, but certainly recognizing that the Taintking and Vortex are “hotties”, the waitress is a all smiles. We’re not just talking the typical “I have to look hot and smile because I’m a Hooters waitress and want a good tip”. “Hi hun, I’ll have 20 breaded hot wings please. ” ”Can I get a breaded chicken sandwich please, hot? ” ”You sure can boys. Let me ring that in and I’ll be back over to chat.” “One more thing, can you please change the TV channel on the TV here to ESPN 2? ” ”I sure can darling. ” The waitress gets up and walks over to the bar to get a TV remote to change the channel. The restaurant is starting to fill up, as it’s just about dinner time. As soon as the waitress walks away, the Taintking and Vortex speak, at the same time. “There’s someone coming to meet us here. ” They both stop, look at each other and laugh. ”Like I was saying I have someone coming to meet us here. He said that he’d be here in a little, or at least I think he did. ” “Well, I’m glad you got a four person table, because someone else wanted to tag along and eat with us. He said he’d be here in a few. So, don’t be all secretive, who’s coming with you? I know it’s not a hot date or anything, knowing you at least.” ”Funny asshole. I actually think his cab just pulled up. ” Vortex perks his head up, trying to look out the window. The Taintking turns around as well, to look out the window. “I knew it was a guy. ” ”Well, who the hell is coming with you? ” “I actually think HIS cab just pulled up. ” ”And I’m the homo? ” “Basically, YES. ” The two GCW superstar’s eyes remained glued on the door, waiting to see who will come in the door. A few seconds pass and the door swings open. Two men, if you can call them that, try to squeeze through the door at the same time. Both “men” wouldn’t weigh 100 pounds combined, soaking wet. Trying to squeeze through the door at the same time however, isn’t successful for these two as the bump shoulders and are seemingly stuck. The two begin to wiggle and twist their lower bodies, in attempts to get free. This is to no avail however, as it’s their shoulders that are stuck in the door frame. The Taintking and Vortex stand up, to see the commotion going on and both men’s jaw drop when they realize who the two idiots that are stuck in the front door actually are. The Vortex’s mouth opens just before Taintking’s does, in disbelief of who each other had invited to join them at Hooters. ”Cool…” “…Cad? ” They stare in awe as both Coolguy and Nicad have managed to get stuck in the front door at Hooters. Taintking · 12 April 2003, 10:38 AM Hooters, Downtown Atlanta We left off with the two most intelligent “men” to ever grace the GCW being stuck, side by side in the front door frame of Hooters. A small crowd quickly starts to form around the entrance area of the restaurant as the two continue to try to wiggle around to get out of the door frame. This is of course still unsuccessful, as all that is happening is that Coolguy keeps kicking his legs, and Nicad is only moving his head. Before long a crowd starts forming outside of Hooters as well, several people staring in awe at this calamity. For the first few minutes of this, the Taintking and Vortex are torn as to what to do. On one hand, they could save face by cowering in the corner, hiding themselves from view, avoiding the sure-fire embarrassment brought to them by Coolguy and Nicad. Of course, this wouldn’t be either the Taintking’s or Vortex’s personality. So, the two GCW superstars get up from their table and walk to the front of the restaurant. 15 minutes have passed since the start of this fiasco, and the crowds both inside and out of Hooters has become quite sizeable. The Taintking and Vortex hang out at the back of the crowd, watching the situation unfold. Nicad’s head is on a swivel as all he does is repeatedly move it back and forth, trying get free of Coolguy. Coolguy, whose feet are actually slightly off the ground, because he is just a little shorter than Nicad, just moves his feet back and forth, scuffing them on the ground. Neither will obviously ever get free at this rate, as they are doing nothing to possible get their shoulders unstuck. 10 more minutes pass and still nothing has changed as far as the two being stuck. However, the situation has gotten worse as both Coolguy and Nicad have become agitated with each other. Along with the useless movements, screaming and yelling start from the duo caught in the door jam. “Fish fried picture frames leave the lights on in the bottled water!!! ” ”I am the co-prez. ” “Apple juice on the tip of the microphone plays garbage frisbee! ” ”Listen fatso, MMM is a drunk and I will pay you 25000 to be commish. ” “Tickets to the virus have the flag on a name tag. ”I will fly to Sealte to prove that the stockholders are paying me. ” The argument escalates and becomes increasingly louder. The manager of Hooters has pushed his way to the front of the crowd and doesn’t help the situation, just makes it more of a mess by trying to yell over Coolguy and Nicad. The manager is a large man, about 6 foot 4, 260 lbs. He is built like a brick shit-house, and looks like he’d be a good bouncer and certainly a gym-rat. Fortunately, this doesn’t last long as Coolguy and Nicad quickly drown out the manager. Clearly pissed, the manager pushes his way back through the crowd to the back of the restaurant. “Paper towels in the security envelopes makes the flashlight burn.” ”I got a 2150 on my SATs. ” “When fighting the calendar, I hope you enjoy your stay at the Vanilla Ice factory. ” ”I will hire the Hardcore Thugz for 32000. I will pay you 10000 to be the GCW secretary and you won’t give you as much paper work. ” In the back of the crowd, still stand the Taintking and Vortex. The Taintking begins to laugh as he turns to Vortex. “Did he just say he was going to hire the Hardcore Thugz? ” ”I think he did, it’s obvious that he’s not in his right mind. ” “Yeah, both Alkaline and Gunn combined aren’t worth 32 cents let alone 32000. Luckily Gunn has been shown the door, I wish Alkaline was next. ” ”Scared that he’s going to beat you? ” “Scared? Alkaline’s promos are few and far between, I’m not scared of that jerkoff.” ”Who gives a crap, it’s going to be “O and 1” when I’m done with him this weekend. ” The Hooters manager emerges from the back of the restaurant, with a bullhorn in hand. He flips the bullhorn on and pushes his way back to the front of the crowd. Irate that no patrons can come into the restaurant the manager stands directly in front of Coolguy and Nicad, screaming into the bullhorn. With the bullhorn, the manager is able to talk over both GCW icons. ”You two dumb fucks better get out of my restaurant right now. You’re costing me business and I’m going to take it out of your ass. ” When the manager begins to verbally berate Nicad and Coolguy, Taintking and Vortex look at each other and nod. The manager, red-faced, continues to scream as Coolguy and Nicad just continue with their useless movements. ”I said GET THE HELL OUT OF MY DOORWAY. I swear to god you two... ” By this time, the Taintking and ‘Tex have moved to the front of the crowd. While they move up to the front, the manager has now dropped the bullhorn and appears as though he’s prepared to grab Nicad and Coolguy. Just before he places his hands on them, the Taintking reaches out and taps the manager on the shoulder. Fuming, the manager spins around, getting ready to mangle whoever has interrupted him. Coolguy and Nicad, who have not stopped their screaming since the beginning of this, don’t stop now either. Both Coolguy’s and Nicad’s face light up as they see the superstar collective known as Catch 22 approach. ”I will fly up to NYC and give you 20000 to beat him up, the manager is a drunk. ” “Alkaline charged Shop-Vacs are lazy when the butterfly is interviewing the Christmas tree truck.” The manager, who didn’t expect to be standing face to face with two men bigger than himself seems a little taken back. He takes a step backwards, nearly bumping into Coolguy and Nicad, who would with even a little push easily be knocked out of the door frame. The two GCW superstars laugh at the manager’s reaction, causing to become even more pissed off. He steps forward, cocking his fist back, and swinging at the Taintking. Tainter tries to avoid the punch, but without crushing the crowd behind him, this is impossible. The gym-rat manager connects with the Taintking’s jaw. Expecting to knock the Taintking on his ass, the manager cracks his knuckles, smiling with his work. Tainter however, has other ideas, shaking off the punch and running his hand over his face, causes the Hooters manager’s face to turn from smiles to a fearful look. Meanwhile, Vortex has strategically positioned himself between the manager and the door, which still houses Nicad and Coolguy. Not knowing this, and seeing the fire beginning to flair in the Taintking’s eye, the manager turns around, preparing to bust out of the door. Vortex however, will have none of this, as he hits a EUROPEAN UPPERCUT on the Hooters manager. This causes the manager to spin around, coming face to face with the biggest ass-kicking machine in the GCW, the Taintking. A toothy grin comes across the Taintking’s face as he stares down at the Hooters manager. The grin is followed shortly by the TAINTBREAKER, TAINTBREAKER!!! Not only does the manager hit the floor like a ton of bricks, shit hits the fan in the restaurant. Patrons start trying to push through the Taintking and Vortex, trying to exit the restaurant. The crowd outside also begins to quickly disperse. Not being able to hold the crowd back for very long and not wanting to be trampled, Vortex turns around to be face to face with Nicad and Coolguy who are still yelling at each other. Vortex, with ease pushes the shoulders of both GCW personalities, sending them flying out towards the street. Vortex and the Taintking are the first out the door from the frantic crowd. Police sirens are heard in the distance, as the two GCW superstars, run over to curbside, where Nicad and Coolguy are lying on the ground. The yelling between the two has not ceased, but not wanting to explain this mess to the police, Tainter grabs Coolguy under his arm as Vortex grabs Nicad under his arm and they take off down the street. Covering Nicad’s and Coolguy’s mouths, respectively, Vortex and the Taintking bolt for the Taintking’s car. After running about a block and a half, with Nicad and Coolguy in tow, Tainter and ‘Tex finally reach the Taintking’s Audi. The Taintking uses his keyless entry to unlock the car and he then opens the back door. They stuff Coolguy and Nicad in the back, before climbing in the front of the car. The yelling between Coolguy and Nicad continues. ”You are fired, I am the co-prez. ” “Computer paper flipped off the baseball hat on top of the VCR tape.” “Both of you shut the fuck up back there, you’re like a Guardian promo, because you’re giving me a headache. ‘Tex, you want to still go watch the game? ” This quiets the two down, at least long enough for Vortex to speak. ”With everything that we’ve gone through so far today? Sure what the hell, let’s go to Abatto’s. ” This is all that Vortex can get out of his mouth, as the yelling between Nicad and Coolguy starts up again. Vortex and the Taintking look at each other, just shaking their heads. “Dragonball bikes are stored in the fridge so I can come to the ring with a fish. ” ”I will hire Abatto for 27000, he can wrestle with…” Scene fade. -------------------- *I *L *Y TAint, it's what's for dinner The King Hath always been here, Taintking, the other white meat.
