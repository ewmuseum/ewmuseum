GCW (Atlanta, 1998–2004) — Roleplays

A new label. (J.W. Levy)

7 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A new label.
J.W. Levy
Global Championship Wrestling Parleys #200
Date: 08/07/2004
Location: Tatoo Parlor in Austin.

The scene opens just in time to see a red, white, and blue American Chopper blazing by at an excessive speed. Then the cameras catch it making a swift left hand turn into an alley where the Chopper is stalled, and eventually turned off. The camera crew and technicians make their way down to the alley where they get a glimpse of a man wearing a black leather jacket, black leather chaps over his blue jeans, a rebel flag bandana, and black cowboy boots walking through an opened steel door. They persist to follow him, and he leads them to room where another man is setting up equipment to give a tattoo. The man that was on the chopper turns around, takes off his Oakly sunglasses, and reveals that it is JW Levy. In this room is a chair, a massage table, a holster for all of the equipment, and a TV hoisted in the corner. JW Levy takes off his leather jacket, and his white undershirt. On JW’s back is a tattoo of the American flag being carried by an eagle, on his left arm is razor wire tattoo rapped around his arm, on his right arm is a tribal band that extends down to the bind of the arm, and on his stomach is where he prepared to get another one tagged to him. Before they get started, the other man talks and walks JW Levy through safety and precautions.

JW Levy: I understand all of this man, I have had many of before.

Man: I understand, but Texas state law.

JW Levy: Hey do you mind if I was to cut on the Television for a moment?

Man: Sure, watch it as long as you want.

JW walks up to the TV, and on the first channel is a man talking trash about Thane Givens. JW is beside himself as Strife willing reveals his own idiocy. Even though JW takes the time to hear Strife all the way out, but he remains indifferent.

JW Levy: I swear, some people wouldn’t know their mouth from their asshole.

Man: What do you mean?

JW Levy: If you think about it, shit comes both wholes, and most people get those wholes confused.

The man laughs, and turns on the outlining needle.

Man: That was funny.

JW Levy: Eventually people will learn their asshole from their mouth after I physically pinpoint with my boot where shit should come out.

The man begins to carve the outline, and JW doesn’t even tense a muscle.

JW Levy: If Strife only knew that Thane Givens was to come over to a GCW event to touch Strife, he would have lawsuits all over his ass.

Man, continuing his job: Why?

JW Levy: First of all it would mean a breech of contract for this other organization he works for, secondly Strife could sue the shit out of him, and thirdly so could GCW. That’s why I am here, getting paid, to ultimately do it for him.

Man: Wow, what a nice guy.

JW Levy: It won’t be nice this Thursday, or this Sunday.

Man: Why?

JW Levy: I am pitched in a match where I will fight two guys at the same time. However all three men are for themselves, so it sort of proves the name given for the match.

Man: What’s that?

JW Levy: Triple threat.

Man: Yeah, I am not that much of a wrestling fan.

JW Levy: Then you ain’t a true Texan!

Man: Nah, I just like stabbing people with needles, listen to heavy metal, smoke pot, and drink.

JW Levy: Well when you’re young, your young, and you do exciting things. Shit I remember back then too. It was crazy.

Man: You are only a year older than me.

JW Levy: That’s true, but still I had wilder times a year ago. Now I do what I love to do, and that’s to seriously make over god’s creation into the most destructive thing that anyone could witness be done to a human being.

Man: You’re insane.

JW Levy: Indefinitely.

A moment of silence creeps on them both until the man is finished with the complete outline. JW takes a look at it, and he displays it for the camera.

JW Levy: Son of Dixie!

The tattoo shows a waving rebel flag with torn edges, and a foreground cursive text that reads “Son of Dixie.” JW takes pride in it, and the man continues his job.

JW Levy: Here’s a new label…

-The scene slowly fades out in silence-
