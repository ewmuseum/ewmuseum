Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

“JOIN THE GWF” opened its recruitment pitch on 26 November 1995, billing itself as “THE HOTTEST AND NEWEST FANTASY WRESTLING LEAGUE AROUND,” and was candid about its own size: “IT IS DONE BY 2 PEOPLE AND IT IS GREAT!” It offered a dice-roll format rather than a scripted one — “we don't make up who wins and who loses, most is the luck of the roll” — open to entrants making a wrestler “any way you want him to be,” with no limit on how many a member could enter. Its planned schedule was two cards a week plus one themed “Blockbuster Event” a month, along the lines of a royal rumble, a Survivor Series or a War Games, with interviews built in for wrestlers to brag, issue challenges or talk trash. It asked nothing of anyone who joined: “It is absouletely free!”

The same pitch was reposted, near-identically, the following day.

Nothing further survives: no card, roster, title or result from any point after the ad ran is on record, and how or when this GWF closed is not known. It is one of several distinct 1990s federations that used the bare acronym GWF.
