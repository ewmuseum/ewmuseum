The things money can’t buy

15 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 15-19 Richard Thomas Whittaker Born June 22, 2002 at 5:25 PM Son of Neil Whittaker and Stephanie Randell Whittaker Weight 8lbs 10oz and 23 inches. Godfather Gideon Fontaine As you can tell time is ticking away and soon Ricky will turn 5. There have been a lot of things talked about and on that day Ricky’s life is going to change in ways he never even thought of. He is a good kid and his parents trust him a lot. They know that they have taught him the difference between right and wrong. Then you have Gideon, Tony, and Bobby, 3 men that Ricky idolizes also helping to instill a sense of values and respect in Ricky since the day he was born. They have never talked down to Ricky and have largely let him make decisions based on what he thinks is right and wrong. Even though he is less then 5 he understands things that so many adults can’t even comprehend about values and respect. The decision they have come to was not made lightly. Stephanie and Neil are worried like most parents would be. Gideon and Bobby aren’t worried because they trust Ricky a lot. Tony doesn’t think it is a good idea at all considering how screwed up Gideon is on occasion. On June 22 Gideon will tell Ricky the big surprise. On that day when Ricky turns 5 he will ask him if he wants to be trained in the martial arts. Neil and Stephanie will start training Ricky physically. Some children might get a new Transformer toy for their birthday but for Ricky this will be different because everyday for the rest of his life he will have the most important gift they can give him and that is knowledge. But before that happens there is lots of things they have to do to prepare. ########## ########## ########## ########## A martial arts supply store in L.A. June 5, 2007 ########## ########## ########## ########## Stephanie is looking around. She is trying to find some Martial Arts Gi in Ricky’s size. She has his measurements memorized but a lot of the smaller sizes aren’t what she is looking for. The sales clerk comes to help her. ???: May I help you miss? Stephanie: I am looking for Martial arts Gi for my son. ???: How old is he? Stephanie: He is going to turn 5 on the June 22. ???: You are looking at the Gi for children in the 7 to 10 year range. Stephanie: I know. I still don’t see any with sleeves long enough or legs long enough. ???: What do you mean? Stephanie: Let me try again in Spanish then. Todavía no veo ninguno con bastante mucho tiempo de mangas o bastante mucho tiempo de piernas. I don’t know if that helps too much because my Spanish is pretty shitty. ???: I think you said “I do not see yet any with enough a lot of time of sleeves or enough a lot of time of legs.” Stephanie: Hmmm my Spanish is getting better. Can you make Martial Art’s gi based on measurements? ???: We can get them custom made. Stephanie: How long would it take? ???: About a week. Stephanie: Good. Stephanie gets out a notepad and starts writing down all Ricky’s measurements though it is a tad larger then his current size because he is a growing boy. Stephanie: Do I pay for it now or when it come in? ???: There is a 100 dollar deposit. With a smile Stephanie starts getting all the details set up. She is getting 10 of them made for Ricky so that they can leave 5 at Gideon’s Mansion. With any luck Neil is able to get the things he is looking for as well. ########## ########## ########## ########## An Athletic supply store in L.A. June 5, 2007 ########## ########## ########## ########## There is very few things Neil hates in this world. Well that is a bit of a lie. Neil hates tons of things and the list gets larger on a daily basis. Still 2 of the top things he hates is shopping and dealing with customer service. He is here to get some weights and a bench so Ricky can start training. Of course getting things the proper size for a 5 year old isn’t easy. He has a few heavy bags already ordered and being shipped to his house. A Customer service rep walks over to Neil seeing him looking around with a mixture of annoyance and impatience. ???: My name is Josh. Can I help you? Neil: I’m looking for weights and a bench for my son. I don’t see a bench small enough. Josh: How old is he? Neil: Turning 5 on the June 22. Josh: And you are making him lift weights?! With those words Neil looks at the young man. Neil is not known for being patient. He tolerates attitude a lot less. Neil: I am not “making” him do anything. On his 5th birthday he is going to be asked if he wants to start learning martial arts with his Godfather and if he wants to start training. In order to be able to do that he has to be able to have weights to lift because the ones I have you wouldn’t be able to lift with a buddy helping you. Neil said this all calmly without raising his voice. Despite the calmness Josh gulps knowing that he has annoyed Neil and that probably wasn’t a good thing to do. Neil: You seem like a nice kid. If not I’ll pretend you are anyway so I don’t lose my temper. This isn’t about me or Stephanie or anyone else. This is about giving Ricky an opportunity to decide what he wants. I didn’t start hitting the weights until high school. If Ricky starts now he will have over 10 years as a head start. I’ll show you something. Neil takes off his leather jacket and holds it out to Josh. Josh looks at it and Neil confused. Neil nods and Josh picks it up or at least tries to. It hits the ground with a large thud. Neil picks it up and puts it back on. Neil: There are heavy metal plates in the jacket. I wore it when I was bounty hunting because it was able to stop a bullet if it had to. Doesn’t look like much because of the way it was designed. I’m sure as hell not going to let Ricky throw his life down the toilet. This jacket saved my life when I had a sawed off shot gun shoot me in the chest at point blank range. The plate blocked the blast and held up because it was thick. If I wasn’t in shape I’d have never been able to wear anything like this and I’d have died that day instead of having bruised ribs. You understand where I’m coming from? Josh: Completely. Wish my dad was cool like that. Neil: Can you help me get what I’m looking for? Josh: In theory. I’d have to do a bit of research though. I think most of what you are looking for you’d have to look for from a medical supplier. When someone has a stroke and loses strength in an arm they usually have light weights to build it back up and I think that would be pretty much what you need for your son. The bench though should be no biggie because it can be cut down. Neil: How long would it take? Josh: I’ll be off work in a few hours. When I get home I’ll look up a few sites on the net. Are you able to comeback tomorrow? Neil: Sure. I appreciate the extra help. Josh: No problem. I’m a wrestling fan and I saw you and Mr. Fontaine wrestle a lot. If I can help you out that would make my day. Neil: Thanks. Josh: I’d have asked for an autograph but you came here to get something so I didn’t ask. Neil: Appreciate that a lot. Tell you what. You help me get the stuff I’m looking for and I’ll see what I can do about getting you some of the harder to find DVDs of places Gideon and I have been. We keep archives of matches we have been in but I can barely play solitaire on the Fuckin computer. It can copy DVDs though so might be able to get you some of our stuff from our North American debut in UJW back in 99. Sound cool? The look on Josh’s face is a lot like a small child being told he is going to a candy store. Any wrestling fan that hears that he could be getting DVDs of matches of Gideon and Neil dating all the way back to 99 has to be drooling because you can’t get that stuff anywhere. Though they will be able to on several Torrent sites when Josh gets the DVDs. ########## ########## ########## ########## Meanwhile at Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## Neil and Gideon are drilling shoot takedowns. They aren’t really doing defense this time. They are going over their takedowns and come Killzone 42 on Wednesday they are going to be taking down Ralph and Phillip Edmonds pretty much at will. That was pretty much expected anyway but the Alliance are not the type that likes leaving anything to chance. Gideon and Neil have had their distractions and they hate losing a lot. When they lose they comeback more focused more driven and even more violent. Gideon has only recently come back to thinking clearly. When Neil and Gideon train for a match they have a gameplan and generally have a back up plan in case the first one isn’t working. They have lost a cluster fuck of a title match before when God’s Own Drunks got the Fatal Faction titles. The Alliance never won the match but they didn’t lose the match either. The next cluster fuck of a tag match they never won but they weren’t the ones that lost that match either. This week the Alliance comes off their second cluster fuck of a match where they never won but also they never lost. Ralph and Phillip Edmonds never lost the match either but they were not even in the match. Odds are they were playing the Halo 3 demo while the match was going on and never knew their match was the first one up. Gideon is getting up after a double leg takedown Gideon: So what are the plans for the match? Neil: Punish them. Make them tap but I’m not really looking to seriously hurt them. Gideon: Me neither. They are sports entertainers but they don’t anger me. Neil: We have to win this match decisively. Gideon: The Cowboys made the mistake of letting things get personal. They could have had that match if they didn’t get DQd. They made the biggest mistake they could make against the RPG. They allowed them to get inside their heads and throw them off their game and that cost them huge. Neil: They are going to try and annoy the living hell out of us. Gideon: Key word is try. Neil: Lucky for them we aren’t angry at them. Hell anytime we get an opportunity to hurt a sports entertainer we make their life hell. These guys aren’t a couple of jack offs that think the world owes them and that because they are in a wrestling company they are pro wrestlers. They aren’t the type of guys that think because they have their name on a billboard or a piece of paper that they are better then everyone on the planet because they are famous. Hell we don’t have to worry about them going into Smitty’s office and demanding things and being self important douche bags. Gideon: All they are is a couple guys having fun and trying to be wrestlers. They are going to get hurt with some of the nutcases running around FUSE. If we stretch them we can help motivate them to actually learn some wrestling. Neil: At least learn enough so they don’t get hurt. Gideon: Lets go to the judo tosses and armdrags. After saying that Gideon and Neil start practicing judo tosses and landing. The tosses are fast but Neil and Gideon are rolling to their feet very fast. This is more of speed conditioning then anything else. They hit the ground then they get up as fast as they can after absorbing the impact. When they face Ralph and Phillip Edmonds they are going to punish them and make them learn about what wrestling truly is. They aren’t going to bloody them because they don’t feel the need to make them bleed but there will still be a lesson taught. When you enter the world of wrestling and face a team like Neil and Gideon you learn things about yourself. They are two men that have spent a life time fighting and take everything they do seriously. While the Alliance aren’t unbeatable they are one of the most physical and dangerous team in wresting because they are the most well trained wrestlers you could ask for. They are spending a lot of time preparing for June 22 and celebrating Ricky turning 5. They are training hard for their match against the RPG. They will not and can not let themselves lose focus and slip out of contendership. They will teach Ralph and Phillip Edmonds what it means to enter their world. They will not accept a loss here. Come Killzone 42 the Alliance will face the RPG and they plan to win decisively. They are not overlooking the RPG because the day you overlook anyone is the day you become arrogant. They have certain values and a work ethic they have earned over decades. No matter what happens in life your pride and your work ethic should define who you are. The fame and the fortune comes and goes. Anything you get easy means little because there is no sense of earning anything. The most important things are the things money can’t buy.
