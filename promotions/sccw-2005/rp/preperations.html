Date: Today The morning was crisp. Paul Searle was waking up, hoping that the night didn’t bring more snow. The Durants had him shoveling snow as a way to pay for his rooming there. He didn’t complain, it was somewhat of a workout, better than what he had been getting in recent weeks. "Kid, get down here, we need to talk." Mike yelled as Paul walked towards the door to the apartment that he had been staying in. He threw on a parka that his mom had sent him and a pair of boots that he picked up at the local Army-Navy Store. He walked carefully down the stairs that were outside of the apartment as the rain had come down overnight and frozen to the wood, making for a very slick surface. "Yes, Mr. Durant." "First of all, how many times do I have to tell you? You’ve lived here long enough that you can call me Mike." "OK, Mr. Durant." Obviously, the naïve kid routine was starting to get on Mike, but he didn’t mind it all that much. He knew that the kid was not in a place where he could just go and be alone anymore. The kid needed help, and as the protégé of his best friend, Mike knew that he wanted to give Paul that help. "When you go to the airport today, don’t take the car. Take the truck. It will handle better on the ice with the four wheel drive." Paul had another title match coming up, and Mike was more concerned about what kind of car Paul was going to take to the airport. Maybe it was the laid back personality that Paul exhibited, but this wasn’t the Mike Durant that was one of the strongest men in Minnesota State Wrestling. "You sure, Mr. Durant?" "I’m sure, Paul. And call me Mike. Please, try it out. You might like it." Paul took the keys for the truck from Mike’s hand and heads back up the stairs to his apartment. He was missing his shows on Cartoon Network. The gym was intense this time of day. Paul looked on as older men were pushing more weight than he could imagine. Weights that would be attainable for him if you gave him a couple of years and a good amount of steroids. This was going to be his last workout before he left for Defiance. He knew that having three other opponents to worry about wasn’t going to make the match easy For Paul Searle, this really wasn’t a matter of life and death, just a matter of winning and losing. Many insiders are already calling for Searle to get rookie of the year honors in Sin City Championship Wrestling. Paul didn’t want to hear any of that talk. He wanted to be ready for a four man war. He knew that no matter what, he was going to be in for a beating that you can only say was completely justified. He was rising too quickly without paying his dues. He lucked out in the fact that he was even getting this second chance at gold, as he wasn’t able to capture it his first go around. Paul met with his personal trainer and they head towards a scale. "Paulie, hop on up." Paul got on the platform of the scale and the trainer moved around a couple of black weights. "Good job, kid, but you have a long way to go.
