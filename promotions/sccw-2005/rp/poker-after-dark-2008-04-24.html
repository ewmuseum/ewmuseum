Poker After Dark

24 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24 It’s three in the fuckin’ morning in the poker room at a casino ninety-five percent of the country don’t know exists. I’m at the final table of a tournament. No, it’s not the WSOP, or the WPT, but the winner of this bad boy gets ten thousand smackers, and that’s good enough for me, goddamnit. Heads up, too, as a matter of fact, up against this kid who’s been suckin’ out time and time again, all day and all night long. Amateurs. If luck wasn’t involved, I’d win every goddamned time against punks like this. But Lady Luck’s a cruel mistress. I’ve had her on her knees beggin’ for ol Big Stack, and she’s kicked me out of the house in the middle of the night buck-ass naked. Wonder what she’s got in store for me today. I look down at the felt in front of me, a nice stack of chips to my left, a card protector in the form of a topless woman to my right, and two cards smack dab in front of me. Ain’t matter what I got, and that’s a fact. Not when you’re this good, but it don’t hurt to check anyway... Squeezing the cards, my eye goes down, and I like what I see. Six-five, both in diamonds. This is the sort of hand I break people on, the sort of hand that only a pro knows what to do with. It’s all about readin’ the beady little eyes of the other guy. You like what they see, and you’re swimming in money. You don’t…and you’d better get the fuck outta dodge, or you'll be swimmin’ in cow shit. He starts out the betting, throwing a raise at me, but fuck, it’s the final two, and he’s in the small blind. Not raising would be inviting me to raise him right back. I’ll call for now, see what the flop brings in. Five. Eight. Nine. Rainbow. Hell, this is where it gets interestin’. I’ve got bottom pair. I’ve got a gutshot straight draw. I’ve even got a chance at a flush if I suck out real nice the next two cards. Before I can get all of that in my head, the kid across from me fires two stacks of chips to the middle of the table. He wants you gone. It’s a flash of intuition. My gut. Ain’t nothing scientific about it. He wants me out of this hand. But why? If he had a big hand, odds are he’d try to keep me in, lead me along, unless he’s smart enough to put me on something like what I’m playing. If he’s got a made straight, I’m likely boned. High pair, I’m in trouble, but I can make a comeback. Or he’s got something like Ace King. Ace Queen. King Queen. He wants me the fuck out. That settles it. "How much ya got, kid?" No poker player, not even an amateur, wants to hear those words. It means that the guy askin’ has more than you do, and he’s trying to figure out how he can take the rest of what you’ve got. "One hundred grand in chips," the kid says, voice calm. Doesn’t miss a trick. For a rook, he’s got the face down, I’ll give the lucky punk that much. "And if you want to stay in this hand," I croon, pushing every goddamned chip I have to my name into the center, "it’s going to cost you every last one of them." He freezes. Perfect. I’ve caught him napping. He’s got nothing, and he’s going to get the fuck out of the way… "I call." Or not. Shit. Did I just fuck this up? A quick flip of his cards reveals the truth: Ace/King, hearts. Anna Kournikova. Pretty broad, but she’s never letting you go anywhere with her. I’m ahead with my shitty pair of fives. He’s cursing under his breath, but it’s his own damned fault for calling my all in with AK and hoping I had shittier shit than he did. Turn card. King of diamonds, and it’s all I can do to not yell "FUCK!" at the top of my lungs. The smug bastard across from me’s all smiles now, but I know it ain’t over yet. I’ve got diamonds in the deck. They win it for me. A five gives me three of a kind and I win. A six gives me two pair and I win. A seven gives me a straight and I win. River. Seven of diamonds. Come to poppa, bitches. And that’s that. I’m ten thousand to the good, and I’ve got a pissed off rook across the table who’s settling for twenty five hundred in cash and a story with an unhappy ending to tell his friends. Once he’s out of eyesight, I reach up and take off the black cowboy hat I’m wearing, to look at the sweat soaked rim. Never let them see you sweat if you can help it at all, I always say. Especially when you’ve got a new challenge on the horizon. Poker and wrestling ain’t as different as you might think. They’ve both got a liberal coating of bullshit involved. Ain’t no one better at bullshittin’ than Phillip Kennedy, so I figure I’m gonna fit right in real nice-like. It’s three in the fuckin’ am though. I can deal with that shit tomorrow. For tonight, it’s a nice beer to go to bed with and Cops on the shitty motel TV. One day I’ll be famous, for poker or for wrestling. Until then, ain’t no Excalibur or Venetian in my future. Least not the rooms. That shit’s expensive. ‘Sides, Dolly’s Roadside Inn suits me just fine.
