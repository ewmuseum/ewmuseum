Bishop to Knight Four

17 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 17, 2008 There was no specific show that marked the 'official' end of SCCW's 'Stabbing Westward' leg of their tour, but for the members of Magnum Opus, it ended when they were no longer able to casually drive back to Phoenix and kill time between shows at Casa Mayhem. By the time Sin On Spike rolled around, they had their routine down. Oz reserved several rooms in a nice hotel, as well as a large suite located in close proximity to those rooms. This gave the members privacy and a common meeting place, as well as keeping them somewhat close together so Oz could keep an eye on them. Al in all it felt very much like Casa Mayhem, a home away from home. Oz had even started booking a room for Austin 'The Wombat' King, the young Australian who was somewhere between Magnum Opus' sixth member and mascot. It is the morning after Temptation in Minnesota, Magnum Opus plus one are sitting in the suite relaxing. Mayhem sits on the couch finishing his breakfast with his girlfriend Misery curled up against his side, half asleep. The group's red-headed imp, Mischief was working the Chicago Sun Times crossword puzzle, occasionally pausing the chew at the end of the disposable pen she was writing with. Oz, the group's manager, sat cross legged on the other couch, typing with each hand on separate laptop computers, one on his lap and one to his left, pausing to look up every so often at the large television tuned to CNN. Misfit and Wombat both lay on their stomachs on the floor, a chess set between them , but there was little chess actually being played as the two argued about their future with the federation. The blond Australian shook his head, his surfer haircut bouncing as he did. 'Roight, so what I'm sayin' is we makes a team that's the dog's bollocks, wot?' 'Okay, yeah, so we've done pretty good against the other curtain jerkers in house shows, but I don't think we're ready to barge into Smitty's office and say 'RAR WE WANT TITLE SHOT!'' Misfit shot back, moving his knight forward as he did. 'Wait a fucking minute,' Mayhem said, setting the remains of his third steak and eggs on the large glass table in front of him. 'Is being the dog's bollocks a good thing? I mean, that's the dog's balls, right?' 'Oh, too roight, big guy!' The way Austin said big guy made it sound like one word, 'bigeye.' He didn't even look at the chess board as he slid his bishop forward. 'It's ripper!' He turned back to his opponent, 'Check.' Mayhem just shook his head and watched television for perhaps fifteen seconds before he sighed. 'Hey Oz, when's this thing you're waiting for going to be on? I want to watch something that doesn't put me to sleep.' Oz smiled and closed both laptops, and then picked up the remote, turning the volume up. 'As a matter of fact, it is coming on now. Quiet everyone, this is important.' 'Six passengers and both pilots were killed last night in Dallas, Texas when a chartered passenger jet crashed on approach to Dallas-Fort Worth airport, leaving no survivors. The Lear jet was several minutes from landing, when it suddenly veered off course, crashing into a hilltop. National Transportation Safety Board officials have issued no official statement at this time, but it is believed this was no accident, as an unidentified source claims the pilots radioed that a female voice was speaking to them over the cockpit radio and apologized to them for what was about to happen, just moments before the jet deviated from its flight path. No voice was heard by the tower or any other radio, but officials hope to recover the craft's black box and investigate more thoroughly. Also no report on the possibility that this was a terrorist action, however, the jet crashed into an area heavily covered with radio towers, and the accident left most of the Dallas area unreachable by cell phone and radio for a short time last night. One communications expert said this crash could not have been better placed if you were trying to cut Dallas off from the world momentarily until companies were able to boost signals at other towers to compensate. We here at CNN will keep you updated as we find out more. Republican Presidential hopeful John McCain was in-' Oz clicked the television off with the remote, a thin grin spreading across his face. The rest of the room was silent, looking at their Maestro. It was Mayhem who finally broke the silence. 'So there was a plane crash in Dallas and you made Desade think you were responsible? That's brilliant!' Oz merely tilted his head slightly, a nod, perhaps a bow. 'I...I don't think that's all that happened, Vin,' Misery said, trying to squeeze her muscular body behind Mayhem, almost hiding from Oz. 'I don't think that's what happened at all.' 'But that would mean...' Mayhem started, scowling as he began to put two and two together. 'You crashed a plane and killed eight people just to tell Alex Pierce to mind her own fucking business? DUDE! I apologize for every bad thing I ever said about you! You are so fucking hardcore enough to be my manager!' 'Vincent, Vincent, Vincent,' Oz chuckled. 'How could I have crashed a plane in Dallas when I clearly was in Minnesota when it happened? And while you think about that, I have some phone calls to make, I will be in my room and do not wish to be disturbed.' The room was silent for some time after Oz left, the members of Magnum Opus staring at each other quietly, until Austin broke the silence. 'Checkmate, mate!'
