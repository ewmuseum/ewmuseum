Keep Your Enemies Closer

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today 'Vas ist?! Gott im Himmel!! Vas ist eet joo seenk joo are doo-ink!? Joo are pathetic! Joo are VEAK! JOO DO NOT DESERVE TO BE TRAINED BY ZEE ALMIGHTY VERNER VON KLUMP!!!' The squat fireplug of a man with the thick German accent and the elevated blood pressure is indeed Werner von Klump, and the location of his invective is the Von Klump Academy of Wrestling Excellence, located in beautiful Glendale, Arizona. (For the sanity of our readers, as well as the writer, we will translate Werner's accent into something approximating English.) 'Is that how Werner showed you how to do that move? Do you think you will win a match performing your moves that way? You will do it again! And again! And you will do it right, or Werner promises you, you will get to spar with him!' The 'him' that Werner is referring to and pointing out to his students is Mayhem, and if there is one thing that motivates Werner's trainees as much as being screamed at by Werner, it is the prospect of being forced to step into the squared circle with The Path of Destruction. Mayhem is off to one side of the warehouse converted into wrestling school, and is practicing moves on a wrestling dummy. As Werner approaches him, he is trying, unsuccessfully, to perfect a Go 2 Sleep. 'What is it you are working on, Vincent? Some new fangled move to make the girlies squeal and David Gibson to scream like he has lost his mind?' Werner scowled as he watched Mayhem drop the dummy off his shoulders and fall backwards as he attempted to strike it on the way down with a knee. Mayhem sighed as he picked himself back up, 'I really wish you would call me Mayhem, since, oh, I dunno, it's my name now....' Werner snorted in contempt, 'Werner von Klump needed no nicknames! You are Vincent Matthews, not some cartoon sideshow freak! What is next, some stupid mask? A cape? Silly clown makeup? You are better than these things!' Mayhem picked up the dummy and hefted it onto his shoulders. 'This coming from a man who for the last thirty years has been known as 'Der Tuefel' by everyone he has trained, from East German Olympic teams to wrestlers here in The Valley.' The German laughed, an ugly barking sound. 'When the students call Werner 'The Devil,' it is a show of affection! They are letting Werner know they appreciate his no nonsense training! And speaking of nonsense, what are you trying to do to that poor dummy?' Mayhem launched the dummy off his shoulders again, and managed to keep his balance this time, but managed to completely miss the dummy on the way down. After kicking the dummy in the head, Mayhem turned. 'They call it 'Go 2 Sleep' and-' Werner cut Mayhem off by suddenly stepping towards him and hammering him in the jaw with a driving uppercut, stunning the tattooed man, and then he slipped behind Mayhem and locked his arms around his waist. Grunting heavily, he lifted Mayhem up and slammed him backwards onto the padded mats on the floor with a textbook German suplex, and then sprung back to his feet. 'Fuck! What was that for, you crazy old German bastard!?' Mayhem sat holding the back of his head where it had bounced off the floor and scowled at Werner. 'Did Werner need flippy? Did Werner need cutesy hoppy moves? Or did Werner just use fundamentals, and a sixty-four year old man dumped The Almighty Mayhem on his cranium?' Mayhem took Werner's offered hand and stood. 'Yeah, well, fundamentals are all well and good, but...When I joined SCCW, FUSE, whatever, I was the biggest man there, by far. Now it seems like I can't turn the corner without bumping into another musclebound freak. So it isn't good enough to just hit people and suplex them anymore. I need something to stand out with, or I'm just another big man with power moves, stuck in the middle of the card. And now that I have to be Hunter's tag partner this week...' Werner waited for Mayhem to finish his thought, and when he didn't, Werner asked, 'So, this is still about Sabuani and your issues with everyone thinking he is better than you? And you are planning on picking him up and dropping his face on your knee, in glorious tag team partner hatred?' Both men walked back to where Werner's class was still practicing DDTs, and Mayhem shook his head. 'No, quite the opposite actually. Dru made an excellent point of that is what everyone expects me to do, to attack Hunter when the chance is right, or maybe even abandon him. I know that is what they expect me to do, and is probably the reason Smitty put me as a tag-team partner for Hunter. He thinks he can use me to punish Hunter, and through Hunter, Desade and that way he skirts the whole Monet thing. But Smitty Duluth is in for a rude awakening if he thinks I'm so easily manipulated. I have a manager, and friends like Dru to watch my back.' Werner scowled, 'You need to be careful, Vincent. You are in deep water, and you swim with sharks. Not all of them are in the other school, for that matter.' Mayhem straddled a chair, turning his attention from the wrestlers to Werner. 'What's that supposed to mean?' 'Werner met your Drusilla at the ESPN show. She smiles, and giggles, and does all the right things at the right time, but behind it all....She gives Werner the heebie jeebies!' Mayhem laughed, 'Dru? Skipping around with her hair in a braid, spitting pink cotton candy mist Dru? If I had a list of stablemates most likely to jump me and smash my face in with a chair, Drusilla would be last on that list!' Merely shaking his head, Werner took a moment to yell at his students about the proper way to deliver a DDT, then returned to sit next to Mayhem. 'So, why have you come to Werner? The only way Werner will teach you to make people go to sleep is with a nice sleeper hold.' 'I don't just want to be a good tag team partner for Hunter,' Mayhem said, rubbing his chin, 'I want to be better than him. I want everyone watching that match to know, without a doubt, that I am the best wrestler of the four in the ring. So, what's your advice, Werner?' Werner snorted, 'If you have a hot psychiatrist, and you are going to have sex with her, do it somewhere there are no cameras!' Both men shared a chuckle, and then Werner got serious again. 'Werner's advice is to focus on the fundamentals. Do what got you here, Vincent. Leave the fancy moves to the twenty year old kids whose joints will creak and hurt before they are thirty. And beyond this next match, Vincent, Werner's advice is to keep your friends close, but keep your enemies closer. Now if you will excuse him, Werner has a class to teach.' Mayhem sat a while, watching Werner's class before he hit the weights and headed home. What he wanted was to come home to an empty house. A famous philosopher once said 'You can't always get what you want.' Mayhem's house was not empty when he arrived. The Devil had come to dinner at Casa Mayhem, and her name was Desade.
