Things ain't always set in stone That being known let me know Let me I He didn't want to be here. Neither did the rest of them. Craig stood next to them, shoulder to shoulder, but in all reality he couldn't be further away from him. The churning sensation from his stomach was his conscience desperately telling him to just walk away and never come back. Sometime within the last decade though, he stopped listening to his conscience, and decided to do whatever the hell he felt. Yet, his soul had the same fear that his heart and mind had; tonight he would take his final breath. Even with those warning signs, he stood there next to them, and looked up at the building that was illuminated by the radiant moon light. It was the look on Derek's face that worried Craig the most; it was full of confidence. 'So,' Derek began, his eyes danced amongst the darkness. 'Are we sure about this?' Craig coughed. 'Yeah.' Craig heard Derek sighed and somehow found the strength to let it roll off his shoulders. His hands moved inside of his pockets, and he could feel his temper more even then it had been in quite some time. The building before him was one of artistic value, shaped and crafted in a form that Alexander had wanted years ago to show just how intellectual he was. He remembered the very saliva that left his lips on that as it landed upon Alexander's face when Craig had heard the story. Tonight... tonight, he would burn it to the ground in a celebration of both smiles and tears. Craig never stood in front of them, instead either standing behind them or beside the group. His eyes, his attitude, and his general sense of things showed that he was extremely calm and reflective, picturing and feeling the moments as they happen. Craig had to be loose or else everything would fall apart and he wouldn't be able to react in the seconds that he needed to be ready for. Some people lived their lives by the minutes or hours, but on this particular night, Craig had to see and feel each and every second, live within those seconds, or else he would be dead in those seconds. His eyes flicker from side to side as he washes away at the stress in his throat with a simple swallow. 'Derek and Samantha,' he said calmly before he looked at Samantha. Her eyes gave it away. She knew everything. Felt everything. Saw everything. The one person he could put his faith in. 'You're going through the backdoor. Quietly.' The duo wasted no time as they began to walk away with a carefulness to their steps as Samantha looked over her right shoulder again and her eyes locked with Craig for the briefest of moments. Craig couldn't respond to her, not now as instead he glanced over to Eliza. 'You,' he began and then his eyes shot behind her to the large man that stood confidently behind her. '...and James. There's a side entrance. Three guards are posted there. That's Alexander's entrance. You'll get to him the fastest that way. That's your goal. Just get to him.' Tyler watched as they began to walk away before he turned towards Craig, his hair partially covering his eyes. 'What about us?' His voice is steady and even as he watched the facial expressions that emitted from Craig; there were none. Yet, Tyler could see the gears turning ever so calmly inside of Craig's head and it frankly worried him. 'We... we're going through the front door.' The distress in Tyler’s eyes gives his stance on this away. 'What're you, crazy?' Tyler asked him incredulously. A smile appeared on Craig's face as he began to walk and stepped his way past Tyler with his eyes firmly planted upon the front door. Then, he wrapped his arm around his waist before he lifted up his loose fitting shirt and felt the cold steel hilt against his bare fingers. As he pulled the gun away from the waistline of his jeans, a smile slowly swept across his face. There was joy there now. 'Oh yeah, Tyler, I'm a buck fifty in craziness. We're gonna go through the front door and wreak havoc,' Craig said as he could feel Tyler walking behind him. 'Give the other four a distraction so they can get to Alexander... and we get to have some fun like the old days.' Even though Craig couldn't see Tyler's eyes he could feel the daggers in his back and the general sense from the bad boy of the group that he didn't like this idea. Craig could care less. He was going to have some fun. All clear. She motioned for Derek to follow behind her and he did so carefully as he shut the door behind them. The room was a rather simple one with a set of stairs to the left and right of them, meeting at the same exact place. The back entrance had been reserved more so for people leaving then entering, a way for people to either conceal their faces from anyone they might not want seeing them, or for the dead. 'So, how many people do you suspect are here?' Derek asked Samantha who could only shrug her shoulders in response. 'Personally, I'm more concerned about the number of security guards they might all have. That's what should really worry you,' she said, her voice nothing more than a whisper as her eyes scanned the top of the stairs before she began to walk up them. Derek slowly followed behind her, with his shoulder towards her back as he made sure no one came in behind them. As they reached the top of the stairs, Samantha scanned the long hallway that followed and continued to move, each step weighted carefully as Derek's eyes continued to wander around. 'I'm a little surprised you're on board for this, to be honest,' Derek said after moments had passed and the two were rounding a corner as Samantha was a little more focused on the fact that the entire building seemingly seemed quiet. The design of the building had essentially called for it to be divided into three portions; the front entrance, the back entrance, and the side entrance. No one could go in the front door and come out the back by just going straight. With someone so mired in controversy and crime, it didn't come that much of a surprise to those who had watched Alexander for years. He needed absolute control on every facet of his organization. 'Sometimes, you just gotta do what you've gotta do, Derek. It's as simple as that,' she responded, her voice still rather hushed as her hand touched the glass on a door in front of her. Her ears perked up as she held up her opposite hand to Derek as she was certain she had heard something. Her eyes darted wildly until she could feel the blood dripping from the back of her skull. The blow to the back of her head caused her to stumble slightly forward as the man responsible for said attack came out of the shadows before catching Derek under the jaw with an elbow. Samantha turned around to see Derek stumbling into the wall and narrowly dodged a kick to the face from the security guard! As the guard blew by her, Samantha slammed her wrist into his stomach and could feel a rib crack from the pressure. The man though was trained for this kind of battle as Samantha hopped onto the railing before spinning around and kicking the man in his face, and could feel the jaw soften from the blow. Derek, recovered from his earlier shot nailed the guard with an uppercut and could feel the jaw loosen even more as the guard connected with a back elbow to the face of Samantha before he flipped Derek over his back and onto the floor! As Derek struggled to get back up to his feet, the guard slammed his boot across his face and followed it up by slamming his fist into the face of Samantha who took the momentum from the blow and did a backflip. The guard continued towards her, but Samantha headbutted him before nailing a roundhouse kick that sent the guard crashing through the wooden rail and down forty feet to the ground below. The cracking noise told Samantha that the man's neck had been broken and it would only be a matter of time before he died. As her eyes met Derek's, they both knew that things were going to get really hairy. 'Well, maybe no one heard us,' Samantha said, knowing those words to be the opposite of the truth. Derek just shook his head as he looked around and found a door flush against the wall. He opened the door slightly and looked around before finding the set of his stairs he needed to find. As he started to pull out the wire, he could feel a flood of emotions slowly overcome him. Somehow, he managed to ignore it as he tied the wire across the threshold of the door. 'This will send 100,000 volts of electricity through Craig's body. Enough to kill him, probably, but at the very least, enough to render him unconscious,' Derek said to Samantha as he pulled it into a nearby wall. The wire was rather old, antique almost, but Craig would never see it coming as he rushed up to meet Alexander for their final battle. 'Derek,' Samantha began, and as Derek looked at her she pleaded with him using just her eyes. 'No, it's time to finish this, Samantha. No more of this.' Samantha sighed as she pulled her gun out and pointed it right at his head. 'Put it down, Derek. Just put it down.' 'This is some of the freakiest shit that I've ever seen.' Her words refer to the various statues and paintings plastered across the room that were borderline schizoid. As Eliza and James looked around the room, there was nothing about it that comforted them, and seeing as how it was one of Alexander's personal rooms it filled them with great confusion as to who this man truly thought he was. James moved around the room and found no visible doors that would lead them to any other room in the building. Eliza continued to walk around the room, her eyes wandering ever so slightly until she found a plaque on the wall, blended perfectly with the other paintings in the room. The words were ever so simple. Look and you shall fine. Eliza looked around until she found a statue resting in the corner, a statue of Alexander the Great. 'Well, this guy really thinks highly of himself,' she said as James glanced over her way without muttering a word. As she approached the statue though, she found something peculiar with it as the statue seemed to be holding a lever. Eliza started to pull on the lever but found great resistance to it. 'James, come over here and help me.' The large man rushed over to Eliza before he wrapped his hands around the lever. He could feel the resistance given by the lever, but slowly it gave way until he heard a clicking noise and a door beside the statue opened up. Eliza looked over at James who could only offer a shrug of his shoulders before they walked through the threshold and up a set of stairs that curved around and around, offering seemingly no end in sight. 'Do you ever feel like you've jumped into the far end of the pool?' Eliza asked James who stopped and looked over at the woman he had grown very close to over the past months. The question took him by surprise, but as it settled he began to walk up the stairs again. Eliza followed behind him and wonders to herself why he doesn't answer her right away. 'Why do you ask, Eliza?' A smile appeared on her face as they continued to climb the stairs. 'Just wondering. That's how I'm feeling, what with all of this suspicion and fear that everyone's been feeling as of late. Everyone thinking that Craig is out to get us, the risky missions, all of it. It just feels like you're five years old and just jumped into the pool for the first time, never really understanding what it is that you're doing. Then, when the water surrounds you, you panic until you figure out who to survive in there,' she replied to him, her voice seemingly distant the entire time. 'I agree with you in that it's all been crazy, but I would jump into that pool a million times over, Eliza.' Eliza paused in the middle of the stairs as she looked at James. 'Why is that?' 'Because,' he began as he stopped with one foot on one stair and the other foot on another. 'It meant that I got to meet you, become closer to you, and fall in love with you.' Her words, while not a surprise, still take her by shock. James can see it in her eyes as he turns around and continues up the flight of stairs and it takes a moment before Eliza finds the strength in her legs to follow behind him. 'We're here!' As she heard the powerful voice from James fill the chamber, Eliza ran the rest of the way until they found a locked door. James looked over at Eliza before he took a deep breath in. 'I know this isn't the best time to ask you this, Eliza...,' he began to say before his voice trailed off, his eyes darted to the ground, and his overall mood turned somber. 'What is it James?' He looked into her eyes before he smiled. 'Will you marry me?' The question effectively takes the breath out of Eliza's chest and then without warning, James turned around and slammed his foot through the door as they walked into the personal office of Alexander Lewis. Eliza, jarred by this, walked through the door and looked around. 'It's empty. Why is it empty?' In the streets... Craig ducked an elbow from one of the guards before jamming his knee squarely into the chest of another man who crumpled to the ground. As he spun away, he could feel a hand on his shoulder and flipped the guy over his back and onto the floor before breaking his arm. Craig regained his center of gravity as he took a shot across his jaw, but the pain was minimal as he followed up with a punch of his own before he threw the guy into the wall. Behind him, Tyler was having fun of his own as he slammed his elbow across the face of one man before wrapping his arm around another man's neck and snapping it with too much ease. For Tyler, this reminded him of the old days; the days when you could get into a bar fight with Craig and never have to worry about anything happening to you. He had your back through and through and almost relished it. Tyler could feel another man coming up behind him with a knife in his right hand, but before Tyler had the chance to attack Craig slammed his knee into the back of the man's head, causing the knife to fall on the floor as the guard crumpled to the ground. Craig turned around and was met by a swift kick to his jaw that caused him to stumble backwards. He wasn't fast enough to duck the punch either as he cursed under his breath before he slammed his shoulder into one of the guard's stomach and then he grabbed the wrist of the other man and snapped it in three different places before flipping him inside out with a clothesline that left the guy firmly on his chest. Meanwhile, Tyler dug the sole of his boot into the face of another man and watched him as he crashed to the ground in a world of pain. As the two looked around, they found that they had run out of people to play their games with. A smile appeared on Tyler's face as he looked around at the carnage, rather pleased with himself. Craig then knelt on the ground and picked up his gun that had been knocked out of his grasp. In one swift move, he spun around and fired three bullets into the gut of Tyler. The force from the impact knocked Tyler clean off of his feet and onto the ground as he moaned from the pain. There was no emotion on Craig's face as he walked over to Tyler; no remorse that Tyler could see. As he knelt beside him and put the gun against his throat, Tyler could see the fire in Craig's eye as Craig looked Tyler dead in the eyes. 'G-G-Got me first, huh?' Tyler asked, not really expecting an answer in return, as he could feel the blood in his throat and wanted it to just be over. 'It was either kill or be killed, right Tyler?' Tyler just shook his head. 'Don't put this blame on us, Craig! Just don't!' Someone he didn't choke on his own blood, but he could feel the coldness starting to seep in. 'You all pushed my hand this far, Tyler. I didn't have any choice lef--AHHHH!' Craig roared before he pulled back to reveal a knife sticking from his shoulder. Without even thinking about it, Craig slammed the butt of the gun across Tyler's jaw before he ripped the knife out of his shoulder and didn't think twice as he jammed the knife through the same hand that Tyler used to stab him with. As Craig looked at his wound, he could feel the pain subsiding in relative hurry as Tyler didn't pierce anything major. 'So, Tyler,' he said before he looked back at the pained face of his former friend. 'I know this plan wasn't all of your idea. So, what did Derek do to turn you?' A smile appeared on Tyler's face. 'Don't think I could make this happen, Craig? It wasn't Derek, but it was me. I had a chat with--,' and then before he could finish his thought, it had dawned upon him that he had been played... played badly. He opened his mouth again, but the strength wasn't there as Craig just stood up and walked away, leaving Tyler to his final seconds on this Earth to himself. The room had been picked clean, dust barely even settled on the ground where the furniture had been. As she looked around, she couldn't imagine why in the least bit Alexander's office had been cleaned out. Eliza glanced back over at James who just stood there, seemingly not caring about this in the least bit. 'Let's keep going,' he said, his voice reflecting the look in his eyes. Eliza just shook her head though. 'This doesn't make sense in the least bit!' she yelled back at him and it was evident to him that she didn't feel comfortable with the fact that this wasn't part of the plan. The party was supposed to be in this room, the people, the guards, everyone was supposed to be here, but yet there was nothing. 'I just don't get it...,' she said, her voice trailed away as she looked out at the window and the moon washed against her face. James remained right inside of the threshold of the door as he looked at her for what seemed like an eternity. Finally, he licked his lips and opened his mouth, his eyes showing more than he was letting on. 'It was all fake, Eliza. Everything was a setup, a ruse,' he said with such steadiness in his voice that it troubled Eliza. When she turned around, there was a gun pointed at her from James, and fear quickly settled in as she looked at the gun before looking at James. At first she tried to speak, but there was nothing there. Eliza fought through the fear as fast as she possibly could. 'What the hell are you doing James?! How the hell do you know this is a ruse?!' she shrieked at him before she fell to her knees, the strength leaving her body as James walked to her and put the gun against the temple of her head. 'Because, I'm the one who planted everything. The seeds of doubt. The plans for the dinner tonight. I was working for Alexander Lewis this entire time, Eliza, and no one knew about it. Not even Craig. Tonight, all of these games end once and for all. The group will be dead. Craig will be dead and Alexander will finally get the peace he's deserved all of this time,' he informed her and he just smiled at the tears that poured from her eyes. 'You--.' she began to say before her voice choked. She furiously cleared her throat as the tears gently danced across her face. 'You wanted to marry me! You said you loved me!' Everything she had known had betrayed her, taken her away from this world and this life. 'All a lie, Eliza. All a lie.' BANG! Her life vanished within seconds as her body slumped onto the ground and her blood stained the marble ground. James tossed the gun onto her chest and walked away from the already decaying body of the woman he had tricked into loving her. James knew that Tyler would be dead within moments and that Samantha wouldn't be far behind him. Then, it would be Derek and Craig left, and James would win that fight. Easily. 'Time to end this game.' As he looked at Samantha and wondered to himself what exactly was she doing, he shook his head at her. 'I knew you would always stand by him. He could never do wrong by you, now could he?' he asked her and she just smiled at him. 'I'm giving you a choice here, Derek. Put the wire down, or take a bullet to the head. The choice is ultimately yours,' she said to him, calm and steadily. Derek glared at her as he turned to attach the wire. She looked at him and closed her eyes before she pulled the trigger. She never heard the gun go off or could barely even remember the next few seconds as she opened her eyes to see Derek, face down in a pool of his own blood. Samantha desperately wanted to cry, to show some sort of emotion, but there was none there. She couldn't force herself to do it as she turned Derek onto his back and looked at him. 'I'm sorry,' she managed to say through gritted teeth. 'I wasn't left with any choice, Derek. You were going to kill him! You had betrayed him for Alexander and I couldn't let you do that. I just couldn't.' Certain that he was dead, she was shocked as he opened his eyes and looked at her. 'I-I wasn't...,' was all that he could manage before he closed his eyes again and took his last breath. In her ears was this loud ringing noise as she stood up and could hear a symphony seemingly playing in her ears. She took a step away from Derek's body and could feel years of sadness overcome her body. Yet, instead of wallowing in it, she continued forward as she removed the wire and slowly walked up the stairs, feeling the pounds upon her shoulders as she came to a small hallway and the door to Alexander's roof. It was now or never, and never wasn't really an option. She checked her gun once and then again before she started moving to the door. As her hand wrapped around the knob, everything went black as the blow came behind and knocked her onto the ground. Alexander asked his man on the inside. James, with his back to the door that he had walked out of twenty minutes ago, looked at his boss as the sky hung precariously above him. 'Yes, sir. Now we're just waiting for Derek and Craig to join us,' James responded in kind, his voice and stature very businesslike, much like Alexander's. Lewis had been waiting for this moment for he didn't know how long. Somehow he had been successful though in taking out everything that Craig had worked all of this time on. He had occupied Craig's entire mind for so long that he wondered if Craig had even noticed it. Everything Craig had done for nearly a year now had been centered on him, and if anything, Alexander could consider that a victory. But, watching Craig's body, dead and lifeless, before him would be the real victory in all of this. 'I've waited far too long for this, James, you know that right? You, you will be rewarded with treasures beyond your wildest dreams. You will become legend in this world. You will be known as the man who put down Cr--,' but before he could finish his sentence his entire face was gripped with fear as the blood and brain matter of his protégé splattered across his face and the body of James fell forward on top of him as they both went crashing to the ground. Alexander looked at James and saw a white nothingness emanating from his ghastly eyes. Alexander tried desperately to push James off of him, but the large frame of the dead body proved too much for him. As he closed his eyes and tried to push the body once again, he found it too much for him, and opened his eyes again only to be met with the deathly eyes of Craig staring down upon him. 'Don't move,' Craig said as he wrapped his uninjured arm around the neck of James and rolled him off of Alexander. As Lewis started to move, Craig slammed his foot into Alexander's chest to emphasize his point. Alexander looked at Craig and could see the man wince from his knife wound and the stained blood all over his shirt. Even though he didn't know much about killing people, he could tell when people had loss too much blood, and it was clear as day that Craig was on that line. 'You need medical attention,' Alexander remarked, but Craig didn't appreciate the sentiments as he jammed his foot into Alexander once again. The pain on Alexander's face was enough for Craig as he looked down at him. 'You did well for yourself,' Craig said through pained breath. Alexander just raised his eyebrows as he wasn't really in the best of positions to take credit for his master plan. 'I did my best,' Alexander began before he took a long, hard look at Craig, and felt pride overcome him. 'Fuck that. I did better than my best. I turned everyone against you. You have nothing left Craig except for their deaths on your conscious. Everything that you've worked so hard for, your one link to the past that you once had is now all gone. Because of me. The one person you thought would never defeat you. I did it, Craig, and that's that.' Craig glared at Alexander and did everything he could to keep his eyes open, but the wound that he had once thought to be nothing more than a mere wound had turned into his downfall as he stumbled to his left and fell onto his back as the world spun around him. This isn't how things were supposed to end, not on this evening, but as Alexander stood over Craig with the gun pointed at his face, it seemed destined. 'So, let me ask you something. How're you going to deal knowing with the fact that you thought Derek had turned against you? That the reason Kristen is dead is because of me planting James in there and ratting out her position? I'm the person responsible for the death of your best friend, your girlfriend, and your unborn child! I'm the person responsible for your demise and now, I will be the man responsible for your death. You will rot in hell Craig, wondering to yourself how you could've fallen so far, and I will stay here living my life in spite of me,' Alexander rambled on and on as Craig could feel the darkness threatening to take him over. His senses came to life as Alexander pulled back on the hammer with little effort. Alexander thought about what else he could say, but found nothing left. It had all come to this point.
