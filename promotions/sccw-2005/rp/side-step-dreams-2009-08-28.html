Side-Step: Dreams

28 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/28/09 Dreams are funny things, you know? When most people think about them (if they even think about them at all), they’re usually about strange images and being able to fly and not having cheese before bedtime (which is mostly true; not the cheese thing, though). On their most basic level, dreams are where your subconscious mind hashes out the things that you’ve pushed to the back of your conscious mind: sometime in a literal way, sometimes in a very metaphorical one (at least, I’ve never actually encountered anyone who’s had the unicorn and dominatrix experience for real). But they can be a whole lot more than that. They can sometimes be so vivid, so crystal clear that you feel as though the line dividing reality and fantasy is just gone. And sometimes, just sometimes…other things can come in through your dreams. Some of those folks that call themselves "psychics"? Well, they’re not lying. Dreams are how their visions come to them, how they can see things that they otherwise have no business knowing. See, you folks only use about ten percent of your brain at last count…and you’d be really, really surprised at what you’d be capable of if you were using more. Doesn’t even have to be much more. Then there are times…not often, note you, but they do happen…when even those folks who don’t have "the gift" get a little nudge from beyond. When even normal, everyday folks get a little message in their sleep. That’s where I come in. It’s the gym with him. It’s almost always the gym with him. I’ve never quite understood that part about him. Most folks, when they go into their dreams they look for somewhere comforting, relaxing, soothing…but for him, it’s all about working out the anger, the aggression, the rage. He so doesn’t want to hurt Alanna or Zach…won’t even raise his voice to either of them. He loves them dearly, would give his life to protect them. I’ve seen him do it, more than once. If he’s even remotely aware of the fact that he’s almost completely alone at what should be one of the busiest times of the day (at least if the shadows he’s conjured up are any indication), he’s not showing a bit of it. Instead, he just keeps on pumping out the reps, lifting that weight where a lesser man would be screaming in pain. I wait until he stops before I go up to him. I know I don’t have a lot of time. "Hello, Lance." He looks at me with a confused expression on his face. "Do I know you?" "No, but I know you intimately. Unfortunately, we don’t have a lot of time. Now, I need you to listen to me." The confusion turns into a scowl. "Forget it. I’m not in the mood to play games right now. I should have known you were another one of Alex’s little soldiers". He begins to stomp off. Damn, it’s this form. It’s gotten so that you can’t have red hair anymore without everyone thinking you’re some kind of psycho bitch. And I don’t have time for a temper tantrum right now. So I decide to stop playing around. "Stop." And he does. He doesn’t want to, I know that. I can feel that. I can feel him straining with every muscle in his body to try and take another step…but he can’t. Because he’s not in charge right now. I step forward until I’m facing him, our eyes locked. He’s looking at me with such anger…but I can’t let myself get distracted. "Hate me all you want, Lance. Maybe that will help some of this sink in once I leave. I just wanted to tell you this: as bad as you think things have been before now, they are going to be getting a lot worse. Secrets are going to come on, decisions are going to be made, things are going to absolutely explode around you. Trust me, you will not be immune. You will be hip deep in the shit on this one. Guard your wife and child carefully for they will be the ones to help you through what’s to come." "Who the hell are you?!" "That’s not important. Now…" She is much more like the rest. I can feel the breeze coming in off the ocean, hear the waves crashing in the distance, and smell the tang of salt in my nostrils. She’s sitting on the sand, just staring out into space, losing herself to the gentle rhythm the waves. I walk over to her quietly, taking a seat next to her. Before I can even begin to speak, she turns around and faces me. This is a surprise. "It’s going to get really, really bad, isn’t it?" I just nod. "I mean, I know what getting involved with Alex entailed. We’ve been through some serious shit because of that woman and her cronies. We’ve been threatened, we’ve been attacked…hell, we even lost our livelihood at one point because of her." I already know all of this but it’s good to let her get it all out. "Alexandra is…you are going to see a side of her soon that you could not have possibly imagined. As bad as you think she’s been, as horribly as she has acted, as low as she has sunk in the past…that is nothing compared to what is to come." She nods, sniffling. "It’s been like a house of cards…and there’s a hurricane on the way." "Indeed." "What can we do?" I smile, trying to give her hope. I have seen this play out already, thousands of times. If they are to have any chance of making if through what is to come, they need to hold onto themselves, cling tightly to the things they hold most dear. "Survive, Alanna. Endure. Let her know that, no matter what may come, you will not be bowed or beaten." She gives me a smile back. It is weak but it is the best she can do. "Business as usual, then?" I laugh. Oh, how I love her spirit. "Indeed. Now…" The park is located just a few miles down the road from the house that the little one and his parents call home. He comes here several times a week: to play with friends, to run around and act crazy in a safe environment or just to sit on the swings and try and launch himself into orbit. It is on the swings that I find him now. Just swinging slowly, singing softly to himself under his breath. #Nobody likes you Everyone’s left you They’re all out with you Having fun# I sit down on the swing next to him, slowly swinging backwards and forwards. "I haven’t heard that song before, Zach? Did you make it up?" The little one shook his head. "I heard it on one of my daddy’s CDs." He looks up at me, a polite but confused smile on his face. "I’m not supposed to talk to strangers." "But I’m not a stranger, Zach. Am I?" He just shook his head, looking down at the ground. "No." "You love your mommy and daddy, don’t you, Zach?" Zach shook his head up and down with the unique kind of energy that only the very young possess. "Yeah. ‘Course I do." "That’s good, Zach. You’re a very good boy and your mommy and daddy love you very much, too. I want you to remember that, no matter what happens. Even if it looks like things are getting really bad, I want you to remember that they love you more than everything." My "heart" starts to ache as I see the tears rolling down his face. "I’m scared." "I know, sweetie, I know. But it will get better, I promise. Now…" "…wake up." "…wake up." "…wake up." My name is Destine. Tell me yours…
