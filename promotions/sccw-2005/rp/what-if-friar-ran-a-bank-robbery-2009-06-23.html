What If Friar Ran A Bank Robbery?

23 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 June 2009 Tokyo, 1986 Four Man sits in the back seat of the fresh-off-the-lot 1985 BMW 330. The car travels down the main strip of Tokyo in the heat of this summer day. Pedestrians pass, cars pass, and times does the same. He looks up with a glint in his blue eyes. "Man One," he says in a deep voice. "Are we ready?" The car comes to a halt on the side of the road. Inside the car, all wearing the same, brown mask with a giant white F on it, four men sit and look to one another cautiously. They wait for an answer. "In position," the driver says through a thick English accent. "These tossers lost a bit of their security this mornin’, too – Man Three took out a whole truckload." "It’s true," the passenger says through a chuckle, turning around to give the ol’ thumbs up. The man behind him slaps him in the head. A few seconds of curious staring pass by before the man up front questions the one in the back with a squishing of his face, "What the fu—" A pistol draws from the hip of the man in the back. It presses to the temple of the passenger. The man pushes the safety off with his middle finger and stares intently on his potential victim. "Let’s go over this again, shall we?" he says with an annoying tone in his voice, echoing off the back. "You’re the Two Man. You take care of the guards and hostages. The One Man…" he pauses and glares in the other’s direction, "That’s you, dumbass. You take the clerk to the safe. Three Man runs the back door open with this heap. Four Man…" He stops and grins a bit. "We all know what the Four Man does. Now do whatever we need to get out of there as rich as Bill Gates." Four Man steps out into the rain as if it is a summer’s breeze with his eyes scoping out his new terrain from underneath the leather disguise. He stands upright with the door ajar, focusing in on his surroundings, and looks up at the twenty-five story Japanese National Bank in the heart of the Japanese megalopolis. He flicks up his trench coat collar to shield anymore rain from rolling down his ass crack. It is easy to forget to pack clean underwear in this line of work. Taking the first few steps by twos, Four Man approaches the top of the staircase just as a small child runs right into his leg and stares up at the mask. He winces in frustration as he scowls at the young boy who can’t help but stare. He smiles curiously at the boy and just keeps moving toward the bank entrance. With his crew behind him, Four Man flings open the glass door with reckless abandon. They arrive like a pack of wolves would enter an open chicken coop. 30 Minutes Later "No!" Two Man screams, removing his knife from its’ sheath. "I said shut! theFUCK! UP!" He kneels down, clutching a man by his shirt collar and dragging him into the open so all may see. Women somberly mourn their situation and men grumble about their distress. Two Man seems to have grown quite impatient and antsy. He places the blade unstably under the man’s chin and on his jugular artery. "You wanna save the people, Ching-Ching?" he says, demeaning the old man even more. "Go for it, Bruce Lee! Try me." He hoists the man to his feet and quickly shuffles toward him. The man staggers backward but, in all the fright and fear, he falls flat on his backside with a smack. Two Man laughs as Four Man walks out from behind the desk. "Enough," Four Man commands. "We don’t need any of that shit today. In. Out. Remember?" With the thud of each boot stomp, Four Man stands inches away from the victim, the blade, and Two Man. "Think about it, mate. You’ve got a lot more to worry about than some… Charlie-ass mother-fucker who wants to be a hero. Let’s go get rich." And just like that, Two Man crashes into the ground. His face is gone. Instead a massive, gaping hole replaces it and leaves Four Man scattering for cover. He slides back and looks up to notice that the smoking barrel happens to belong to somebody he works with. "What the fuck are you doing?" Four Man says with a shaky voice, staring at a man in the same mask and get up. "This ain’t part of the plan!" Three Man aims it at Four with a shifty stare. "The plan!? Oh yeah, great plan, Boss. I mean, sure… I have all the cash in the car. That was my job. Yours is the easiest, to keep these people quiet but you can’t even keep one old man in line? This is my gig now." Three Man grins mightily under his mask scanning the crowd. The smile fades quickly though as he looks down his torso at his chest, where blood spreads like a wild flame all over his clothes. His eyes drift upward, locking with Four Man’s before rolling back into his head like two expired parking meters. He drops to his knees and then onto his back, lifeless. "Pigs!" Four Man screams to One Man, who is working on the safe. "Hurry the fuck up, One Man!" One Man turns to Four Man who runs back to the vault and to take cover. Both have their weapons out and ready to fire. One looks to Four. "What in the Hell do we do now?" One asks, innocently. A bullet answers his curiosity from afar as the impact snaps his head backward and into a sitting position against the vault door. Four Man simply stares at the dead body of his former colleague. His lip quivers and then locks onto the other, trying to gather himself. He notices a boot next to him and looks at it. He looks up, noticing the detail put into the stitching and handiwork the mask looking back at his has, and notices the face get closer when the man kneels down. "Let’s go," the man says, grabbing Four Man by his arm. "They’re getting close." Four Man looks back at his team and notices the vault, wide open, and completely empty. He then turns to his new friend who was also his new driver. Before jumping into the car, the mystery man tosses a small rifle into the trunk on top of the duffle bags overflowing with bills. The trunk slams before Four Man’s hand can touch it. "Get the fuck in, kid," he snaps. "Now!" The squeal of sirens and tires catch both of their attention, causing more time to go by before the driver can slam the gas into the floor and take off. A few impatient taps on the wheel before he removes a pistol, props it underneath the young man’s chin, and squeezes – not pulls – the trigger and ends all waiting issues. Four Man stalls in the air on his feet then crumbles into a brainless pile of dead person in the middle of Tokyo. The driver doesn’t hesitate to whip the BMW around, slamming the side door shut, and speed off into the heart of the city. Many patrol cars give chase but some stop to assess the damage. Patrons of the bank spew into the street to watch the story unfold further. 2 Weeks Later "Enjoy your flight, Mister.. Friar," the woman at the ticket booth says shyly, tearing the stub off and handing it back to the soon-to-be passenger on Trans-World Airlines flight 7810, non-stop service to Mexico City. The Hellhound looks at her, from underneath his mask, with solemn, icy blue eyes. He softly smiles, "Thank you." "Excuse me, sir" she stops him with a quick hand on his shoulder. "You dropped these." Friar looks back just as she bends down and lifts a pair of small, black keys from off of the ground. She holds them up in front of his face. A small pit of despair begins to build inside the Hellhound. "That’s an expensive car," she jokes. "A... BMW? Wow. I sure wouldn’t want to lose that! Heh heh." He briefly glances at the keys before walking down the wing to the plane. With a small grin, Friar shifts his focus to finding his next victim - coach seat 15C. 'I hope I'm by a window,' he says to himself. 'I love the window.'
