Test #1: A giant task (RP for Young Lion's Cup Tournament)

14 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/07 Time, the one foe that cannot be beaten or contained. It eventually catches everyone. It doesn’t matter if you are a prince or a pauper. You may be able to out run the devil, but you can never outrun the hands of time. With time, comes change. The young become old and are soon replaced by the young, completing the famous circle of life. I too have had the hands of time snag me by the seat of my pants. For the longest time, I was the pinnacle of what every supernatural hunter wished to be. There was nothing I couldn’t stop. Bring on your vampires, werewolves, Frankenstein’s monsters, and all the rest. I could take them all down. However, you cannot go on forever and eventually, I found my way into heaven, even becoming one of the almighty’s top archangels. Who would have thought? Like stated before, I needed to be replaced. You just can’t leave a job like mine without someone filling your shoes to keep the cycle going. A young lion must replace the old. There must be someone to lead the pack. Therefore, I sent out my associates to find such a person and many were chosen as the next slayer. The problem was that none were able to make it past the "interview process". Then, my mistress of the mystical arts, Jade, directed me to this one young man. He was brash, volatile, and had a huge chip on his shoulder. I wasn’t sure he could the mustard, honestly. He was redneck with a capital "R" and lacked the training and knowledge of the world that the slayer would need. Sure, he probably had seen more horror movies than any other person on the planet and the amount of bars he wrecked single-handedly was impressive. Yet, that doesn’t mean he can handle the job. I believe in the fact that everyone gets a chance, so I started watching him closely in GCW and after he escaped prison, a prison overrun with werewolves by the way, my attention was definitely peaked. Soon, enough my associates made contact with him and after a little bit of convincing, also known as him getting to knock boots with a couple of my female associates and buying him a new rifle, he began actively working with my agents. However, that’s when I first saw his cunning mind at work. He used me to get the women into his bed and obtain a weapon, despite being willing to do the job. I could tell at that moment that his personality greatly differed from my own. I saw slaying, as a way for me to defend the good people from the darkness that lurks in every shadow. He sees the position as his chance to eliminate the darkness, which took his child, and trust me, zealous doesn’t even come close to describing how driven he is to do so. In my most rage-fueled moment, I was not half as determined as he is. He may seem like an immature horndog ninety percent of the time, but he sets goals and will do whatever it takes to accomplish them. Even if that means he must align with Lucifer himself, things get done. Despite our different outlooks, he was chosen as the next hopeful. "But with such a task before him, why is he wasting his time with wrestling?" is a question you may ask yourself. The answer is a very simple one. It puts money in his pocket and allows him to travel all around the world, which is convenient for what he does in his free time. Bullets and visits to the doctor’s office don’t pay for themselves. Wrestling kills two birds with one stone. It really is as simple as that. The man known to you as Gunnar Saint eventually found his way to FUSE. Though, some would say not without a helping hand from above. Nudge nudge wink wink. With his debut at the pay per view, he has put his cover into motion. He is set to prove he is the cream of the crop that is the new blood that has been transfused into FUSE and climb his way to the top, proving he is not only the best in battling the undead, but with combating human foes as well. The tournament for the Young Lion’s Cup is the perfect opportunity to do this and prove that the circle of life not only pertains to nature but to the wrestling business as well by eliminating multiple foes in one night and taking his place among the elite in FUSE. However, I have my own little gauntlet for him to run first… "Why the fuck do ya have to tell that fuckin’ story every damn time I’m ‘bout to do somethin’?" Gunnar growls, as he shifts his ’68, pitch black, Chevy Camaro SS into fifth gear and turns it sharply around a corner. "Yer stupid ass is here wit me, so why do I need a fuckin’ play by play. Give me the directions on where to go, so I can get this over with and ya can go back to suckin’ god’s dick or whatever it is ya do in heaven." "Continue down this road and you dead end into the local church and cemetery," I instruct him, as I relax in the backseat. "I am surprised you can hear anything with Lynyrd Skynyrd playing so loud and still be able to drive with the amount of empty Jack Daniel’s bottles on the floor back here." "Good lord… Shut yer yap, ol’ timer," he replies with his southern drawl, not even slowing down a bit as he hits the dirt driveway of the graveyard. "And if you find any panties, let me know. I don’t want my next female to find that particular article of clothing from my past ones in the back." "Now that you are here, I shall bid you fond farewell," I say with a smirk and vanish with a burst of white light. "Have fun, kiddo." "For a feller who kicked the daggum bucket, he sure as hell don’t give people a chance to miss his ass," he mumbles to himself, while looking around the cemetery. "What in the sam hill am I looking fo’ anyway? I swear this is dumber than tits on a boar hog." My protégé soon finds what he was looking for. Out of nowhere, a massive spiked club slams down in his car’s path and he quickly swerves around it, doing a complete one-eighty before coming to a stop. A flick of a switch and the high beams kick on, exposing a pair of massive feet, which are wrapped in cloth and tied tightly at the ankles. "Tarnation…" the young man says in disbelief at what he is seeing, as he pulls himself out the driver’s side window. Each step the giant takes shakes the ground like a T-Rex in Jurassic Park. The monster’s body is wrapped tightly in chains with severed heads dangling from them by the hair and rags to keep his privates from being exposed. That second part is definitely a plus, mind you. His massive beard drags on the ground between his legs while he marches towards Gunnar. Saint pulls a duffle bag out from the back seat and quickly draws a military grade semi-automatic assault rifle from it. "I heard ya’ll from ZZTop were larger than life, but I reckon now we’re pushin’ it," the young man informs the approaching monster, while loading ammo into rifle. "I love ya’ll’s music, but I think it is time to put the horse out to pasture." A rear mounted holster for said rifle is quickly slipped on and the front strap has various tools of the trade attached to it. The trademark red dot of a laser scope appears on the mammoth beast’s chest and just as it targets where the heart should be, the self-proclaimed "thrillbilly" unloads a round. It lands right on target, but doesn’t even phase the huge creature. A trio of rounds soon follows and nothing changes. "I’m gonna need bigger fuckin’ bullets," he growls to himself, raising a brow as the beast continues towards him. Trees and massive memorials are knocked over or cast aside by the giant on his way towards the heavily tattooed Saint. Gunnar begins to back away from the massive creature, firing a string of shots in a vein attempted to hit a weak spot. Of course nothing works, as the bullets are like gnats to the gigantic being. Not one to worry about collateral damage, the wise ass redneck quickly takes a grenade and launches it towards his pursuer. "See if that don’t rattle yer chain, fucker," he yells to the monster, as the massive explosion finally halts the beast in its tracks. The split second distraction gives my protégé enough time to dip behind a stone memorial and out of the beast’s sight. He quickly starts to reload his weapon and tries to devise some sort of strategy for this situation. "Think you got enough grenades to take him down, there redneck," a voice asks Gunnar from out of nowhere, disrupting his train of thought. The young man catches sight of a gorgeous blonde girl leaning against a nearby by tree. She’s just hanging out without a single care in the world like she is in a pub or something. She is totally ignoring the fact that a massive creature is in the area while calmly lighting up a cigarette. Gunnar isn’t too sure what to make of her outside of the fact that she has an amazing rack. A rack that is barely contained by a low-cut Metallica tanktop. "I got enough, darlin’," he replies back, not even trying to hide the fact he is talking to her breasts. "Even when your life is in danger, you still only think with one body part," she sighs before tossing him a katana. "Take this. Old methods sometimes work better than the new ones." "I reckon this would do the fuckin’ job, if I was goin’ to shave his daggum beard," he snaps back while examining the weapon. "Oh, like your bullets work so well?" she fires back and mumbles something about fuckin’ newbs giving her shit before taking a drag on the cigarette dangling out of her mouth. "Just try to aim low." The ground quakes like a fat girl dancing, as the monster steps into close proximity. Thus, the woman’s breasts jiggle and Gunnar is temporarily distracted. "Get goin’, you fuckin’ inbred hicktard," she exclaims and Gunnar steps out from behind the memorial. "What the hell is she talkin’ ‘bout, ‘Aim low’?" He asks himself aloud, marching towards the beast. "Does she want me to circumcise the bastard with this fuckin’ sword?" A pair of trees is sent flying in opposite directions, as the giant clears a path for his route to his would be victim. Despite the odds not being even slightly in his favor, Saint continues on with a lot of doubt about the sword working, but not a single iota of fear. The humungous beast gets within range and tries to crush his foe with his gigantic spiked club. Common sense tells us that smaller targets are harder to hit and Gunnar exploits that fact by diving out of the way and avoiding a trio of club strikes that follow. "Keep swingin’ there, Sammy Sosa," he mocks the enormous creature from a three-point stance. "Maybe the draft will give me a cold." Continuing at this rate, will get him nowhere and Gunnar knows this. He hopes the monster has some intelligence and he takes his second of three grenades. He fires the explosive at the giant with a throw that would have made Mickey Mantle jealous. Luckily for the young man, the beast had a semi-working brain and was able to learn what those little bastards can do. The bearded monstrosity raises its arms to defend itself. However, it was a trick. Saint never pulled the pin and used the opening to slip between the beast’s legs, safely positioning himself behind the creature. Using the weapon he was given and his surroundings, Saint slices the backside of the chain-sporting goliath’s knees after leaping off a sturdy headstone. The blade went through the skin and tendons faster than Rosie O’Donnell through a package of Little Debbie snack cakes. The weight of the monster is its demise, as the leg gives way and he tumbles to the ground with a thunderous boom. "Looks like someone fell off the beanstalk didn’t they?" Saint can’t help but comment upon climbing onto the monster’s chest. His stay on the beast’s upper torso is a short one, as the massive spiked club comes heading right for him. Gunnar luckily gets out of the way in the nick of time and is able to slice the inside of the monster’s elbow. The slice causes the creature to lose control of his arm and the club smacks the bearded giant square in the face, knocking it out cold. The tattooed "thrillbilly" grins a devilish smile, as he climbs atop the beast one more time. "Just to show there are no feelings, let me fix that mug of yers," Gunnar innocently smiles, as he shoves a live grenade down the gigantic gullet of the monstrous being. "Old school is fine and dandy, but new school kills assbags dead." Pieces of the beast’s face and blood rain down from the heavens, as the good ol’ boy makes his way back over to the blond, who is still taking it easy. She launches her finished cigarette and Gunnar hands her weapon back, which she gracefully accepts. "Not bad. A little messy for my tastes, but it worked," she lets her feelings be known while placing her sword in a sheath on her back. "Now, for why Jade sent me here. Try to keep up." "Hold on to yer horses," Saint commands, blocking her path, to which she sneers up at him. "I ain’t going to just follow ya like a puppy on a string. Answer me some questions first. Numero uno, what the fuck was that? It sure as hell ain’t Garth Brooks, I can tell ya that." "They don’t have Jack-in-Irons in America, do they hillbilly boy?" she responds, not intimidated by him in the least. "It is a supposed monster that decapitates people on lonely roads here in England." "I’ll be damned. That’s a pretty nice creature to have roaming the streets," he can’t help but being sarcastic, as he feels all the places in Europe have stupid monsters like this to fight. "Question number two is: who the hell are you besides the newest piece of ass I want to hear screaming my name?" "Okay! Does anyone have a big ball of jeebies? Jeebies to go with the heebies that I just got from you. Walk and talk ingrate. The name is Carys," she replies loudly at first and eventually hits her normal tone but a very cold version of it, trying her best to put the last part of the comment out of her brain. "I was sent here to see if you could make it past the big ogre there, so I can show you where to go for step two of your test." "Ah so yer the girl who gets all damp in the panties when the old man shows up," he says with a chuckle and a smirk. "Though many others prefer the updated model." "Despite what you have heard, that was a crush that I have long since grown out of," she growls, unhappy that such information is such well-known fact that even the new people know it. "Now, if you open your mouth one time, you’ll get a kick in the dick for your troubles. Now stop dawdling, so we can get this over with and you can go fuckin’ die like the others." "Lead the way, darlin’," he replies, trying not to chuckle at her, which he finds easy, as he focuses on her apple of an ass in a pair of tight torn jeans, as it sways from side to side before his very eyes. "I’ll follow that there caboose anywhere."
