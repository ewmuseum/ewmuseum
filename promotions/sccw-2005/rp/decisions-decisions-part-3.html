Decisions, Decisions (Part 3)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today There’s a saying that ‘Pride comes before a Fall’, the Rejects were hoping that this would apply to Steve Know at the last Temptation more so after Knox had decided to try and deride Legion in some sort of psychological game right before the match. To the majority of the internet fans the result was a given: Knox is still awesome and Legion is still a second stringer in the game title or otherwise they cried and yet they forget what had happened later on backstage… At least some were speculating on the choosing sides statement Legion made with the meeting with Savant, let’s catch up with the Devilish Duo after Temptation shall we? In the Hotel following Temptation Legion: Tonight went quite well I thought… Luci: WELL?! You lost against Knox, we’re just lucky Kensington didn’t put the belt on the line, I thought we came to Sin City to get away from the jokers at NFW yet I saw Aylas last week and now Knox shows up, if SHE turns up next week I’m going to feel a serious case of Deja-vu! (Legion chuckles a bit, it looks to him like Luci is going through withdrawal symptoms after Savant had left the arena.) Legion: Luci, calm down, I know what you’re truly waiting for, besides this weekend we take Teresa out, save the anger until then ok? (He goes over to hug Luci to calm her down) Luci: You’re right, take it all out on Teresa, if only there was some other way to relieve stress. Legion: Yes but Savant’s not here right now so let’s just punch Teresa to death. (Suddenly Legion’s phone vibrates, a text message pops up from SCCW mobile, and it reads: Early-Out, Early-Winner Battle Royal! Invited Participants: Ash Plato, Legion, Friar, Starks, Charlotte Ramone, Reed Gamble, Kelly Masters, Austin King, Brock Alyas, and Jay Phoenix!) Legion: Look at this, we still have another chance in a battle royal and look who’s on there. (He points at Ramone’s name) Luci: Guess the plan may be bumped up then… why cut a meeting with Ms Pierce when we can prove it by out wrestling her! Legion: Hmm, get the mini camera setup for ten minutes! Ten minutes later (The following segment would be shown on SCCW’s Youtube page) Legion: So Kensington has decided to put me in a battle royal with a special prize at the end along with nine other people, not a bad idea looking at it but there’s some names I’d like to address for this. Ash Plato, the man from Vegas that suddenly disappeared last week after losing to Facey so at a guess this Rick Malloy character will be taking his place so I don’t expect much from Mr Malloy except doing his impression of Joaquin a million times. Brock Aylas I finally get to wrestle and considering who you are aligned with elsewhere you are on my list to eliminate quite early and you will be crying back to Eddie Mayfield of how the so called ‘joke’ finally beat you and is now worth a damn round there! Jay Phoenix I consider it a honour to be in the ring with you, IF you beat me I actually will be fine about it for once, let the best man win there. Finally I come to you Miss ‘Ramone’… you hide behind the masks, trust Luci and I to know what that is like my dear and we all know what happened last time, once I prove myself to you my true intentions will be ever closer to fruition. Temptation will be the night when the livewire champion ascends closer to what he craves as the storm grows ever nearer come the time of the Pay-per-view and I advise all to watch VERY closely.
