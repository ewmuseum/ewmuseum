Date: Today It all started so well for me this time around, showing up at Defiance as a fan for the first time in two years watching all the events unfold but at the same time scouting potential opponents for the upcoming No Limit tournament enjoying the night with Luci, at least there was no distractions or so we thought… Parking lot after the show " So the plan tomorrow is to get the flight back to Detroit and finish our plan for the upcoming NFW shows plus ringing up Kensington to finalise the contract right?" "Correct, I can’t wait to see the look on Eddie’s face when he finds out who’s representing his federation in this, the man who wants to start an uprising against him, the irony of it all just makes me so powerful again." She smiled after that… it had been a long time I had seen that without a plan behind it, the events in NFW had taken some strain on us both physically and emotionally but it just takes one second… "I agree, the look on his face shall be priceless and at least it’s less crazy round here, no Koopa Troopas or Mystic Arcana for half of the time right? Luci? LUCI?" She had gone… there were feelings of anger rising because I had an idea of who could have done this, after all I did have Luci hang the girl with a noose of barbed wire last year so the revenge was inevitable, that’s why we agreed to let her beat Luci up in Charlotte but obviously the other plan of ours had caused her to snap… A rented apartment in Detroit Having a rented apartment sorted via Luci helps when you’re travelling to and fro like we do especially if due to your machinations you never when an adversary will be looking to get a cheap shot outside of the ring and considering the contract I was about to sign some of the new peers I would be working with have a habit of doing just that. A few hours later I received an e-mail from the SCCW office: Dusk was to be my first victim and considering the mind set I was in not even the Dead Man’s Hand probably helping me beat him would bother me. Besides maybe in the future if desperation amounts to anything I may have to deal with the devil once again, given the power she welds the ultimate revenge could be created. For Dusk, wrong place, wrong time because I’m going to be envisioning the pitiful girl who’s been trying to get her tit for tat as I take out all the anger onto you. The past week has led me to believe one particular way in my life: It just takes one second for everything to go wrong and turn into chaos but it takes an expert to control it in the storm.
