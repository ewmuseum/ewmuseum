Short and Sweet

18 April 2008

Date: 4-18-08 Some people think that tradition and expectations are what this sport is all about; that those things make it fun. But how fun can it really be for someone who has to live up to those traditions and expectations set forth by those who came before them. That is something that has plagued the young career of Jadian Bridden thus far; not to mention his manager, who had to live under the shadow of a successful father and uncle. Julissa Minorez tried her best to make a name for herself, donning a jeweled and sequined mask to conceal her identity from the world under the guise of Reyna Preciosa during her run in FUSE a few years ago. With no one knowing anything about her past, Julissa should have been free to blaze a trail behind her and lift herself to prominence without fear of comparison to her family. Well, she should have, but she could never shake the thoughts out of her own mind, always hoping to do enough to bring the fans to their feet, to make them cheer her name even if it was not her own. She did it too. The fans of FUSE loved her. Not only for the revealing outfits she wore, but for the daredevil moves she performed in the ring on her way to becoming the first FUSE LiveWire champion. A title Jadian just happens to be competing for in the upcoming tournament to crown a replacement for Elliott Rollins, who was injured by Wyatt Connors. One can only wonder how much pressure Bridden must be in, knowing that no one believes he can beat Bishop, much less the tournament. Such is the case whenever he is scheduled to face men much larger than he is. Given his track record, the doubters indeed have a case. Jay seems to have problems facing large men, Lance Marshall and Mayhem being two of the most recent examples. How can someone shake that stigma though? How do you escape from the shadow cast upon you like a scarlet letter? You win, that's how. You face the next challenge without hesitation, diving into the deep end without worry of how full it is. Of course, sometimes you wish the next challenge wasn't as large... * * * "This is bullshit!" Julissa Minorez turns her attention away from her Blackberry, coming face to face with a rather angry Jadian Bridden. "You did this, didn't you?" Jay asks accusingly, a sheet of paper dangling from his fingers. "Did what?" Julissa questions, her eyes narrowed as she takes the sheet of paper from his grasp. "Don't act stupid," he replies as her eyes scan the words printed on the paper. "I told you to let Duluth handle my booking this week." "Well he must have a sensational sense of humor," she says with a chuckle. "Because I have nothing to do with this." "I don't find this amusing." Jay snatches the copy of this week's card for Temptation from her hand as he mumbles something inaudible under his breath. "I seriously can't have luck this bad. It's like no one wants me to succeed." "Oh I want you to succeed," Juli turns her attention away from the whiny Bridden and back to the next message on her handheld device. "You just haven't been able to do it." "Like that's my fault." "I never said it was," Julissa's left eyebrow arches high as she reads over one of her messages. "I just know you can do better." Jay pulls a cup out of a cupboard, as Julissa grabs a sheet of paper and a pen, then walks over to the refrigerator as she places her phone on the table and starts to write something down. "You know," Jay says as he pours himself a glass of orange juice. "I'm really not sure how much more of this I'm going to be able to take. I understand why, but I just can't figure out what the difference is going to be." "Don't worry about it." "Easy for you to say, you're not the one in the ring." "No, I mean don't worry about it," she smiles, her icy facade crashing down around her as she steps away from the table and turns to look at him. "You go out there and don't hold anything back." "Are you sure?" he asks, a perplexed look on his face as he holds his glass a few inches away from his lips.. "Positive." "Good," he replies as he downs the last bit of his juice. "I'm tired of losing." "Well, let's see what you can do."
