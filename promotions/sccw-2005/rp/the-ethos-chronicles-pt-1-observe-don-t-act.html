The Ethos Chronicles Pt. 1 - Observe, Don't Act

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today ***Begin Transmission*** They were making a mess in the shop. Everything that they came across would find its way to being destroyed by a baseball bat or a crowbar. They didn’t care. They were having their fun, even if it did come at someone else’s expense. The alarm in the shop was going off, but they knew that during the night, the cops weren’t going to making a mad dash there. It was after midnight, not much they could do if they couldn’t catch the perps. The crime was being committed, and for all they knew, there was no one there to stop them. They were going to get away with it. For these kids, it was all about the thrill. The adrenaline rush that came from the chaos. Little did they know, they were being watched. The man that stood outside the shop normally wouldn’t cause any suspicion. He wore a hood that covered his face. The white trenchcoat that he wore was emblazoned with runic symbols. It definitely wasn’t one that came off the rack. The man saw what he needed to see. He pulls the hood back to reveal the white mask that was also emblazoned with runic symbols. The man had come to be known as Ethos. He watched, and he knew that if something wasn’t done about these misguided individuals, that they will continue a reign of chaos that would sweep the city. ***End Transmission*** "I have watched them. I tracked the one that they call Babyface to his home. I can fight them, I can fight for those that can’t fight for themselves." Ethos says into an old fashioned speakerphone setup. The darkness of where he was at didn’t give much as far as details of the room. It was just enough for anyone to make out basic shapes. "Ethos, I want you to hear what I have to say carefully. You are to take the information that you have, and you are to go to the police with it. You are not a vigilante. You are a hand of justice, one that cannot break the law just in the name of justice." "But I can…" "Enough. Your misbegotten quest for that which you cannot have will be your downfall. You were not brought here to be a white knight, but an observer." "I can do more if I am allowed to take action." The voice on the speakerphone grew angry. "You will do nothing." A look of desperation comes to Ethos’ body. "All that is necessary for evil to triumph is that good men do nothing." "Do not quote Burke to me. You don’t make the decision on what you do out there. I make the decision. You weren’t brought along because you could think. You were brought because you are muscle, that is all. You are to follow my instructions, to the letter. Do you got that?" These words didn’t make Ethos think that he was going to be effective for the fight that he was brought in for. Instead, he was now feeling like he was going to be no better than the average person. This wasn’t what he came for. He doesn’t even bother saying goodbye before he hangs up the speakerphone. ***Begin Transmission*** One of the young men that destroyed the shop only two nights before was messing with some of the local drug dealers. Apparently, destroying that store wasn’t enough of a high for him. As soon as he finishes up with his "purchase", he gets into his black early 90’s convertible. Little does the young man know is that once he pulls away, that he’s going to be followed by a man on a white and silver Sportbike. Ethos knows that he can’t follow this gentleman too closely, so he hangs back a bit. Every so often he speeds up and passes the car. This was something that Ethos has done before. He was confident that he wouldn’t be picked out by the young man as someone that was following him. After nearly a half hour of this, the convertible pulls underneath a bridge. "You bring the stuff?" another young man queried as he walks up to the car. Both of them are oblivious to the fact that a scant 100 yards from them is the one witness to what they did the other day. "You know I did. You got my money." the driver asked. Ethos looks as young driver takes a wad of cash from the other young man. He then hands over a baggy that contains what looks to be a small quantity of crack-cocaine. "Dangerous substances for deviant minds." Ethos thinks to himself. "You were told not to act, only to observe. That is what you should do. "Do not take justice into your own hands. You are better than that. You aren’t a vigilante. You are a protector. You need to protect the streets." Ethos continues to watch as the transaction completes, and then hears a ringing in his ear. He reaches up with a gloved hand to hit the button on the Bluetooth module that was attached to his helmet. "Ethos, the Master wants to know where you are." The voice was that of his Angel, the woman that had pulled Ethos from the gutter and brought him back to life. "I’m observing, just like he wants me to." Ethos answers as he continues to look at the young men completing their transaction. "I’m watching one of the kids from that crew dealing drugs to another youngster." "Well, the Master wanted me to remind you that you are to be in Montana, apparently he was able to talk someone named Legion into putting you onto their wrestling show." "Well, you can tell the Master that I can’t observe that which he wants me to observe and be a ‘wrestler’ at the same time. It doesn’t work like that." "The Master wants you to do this so you can learn more about human behavior. He figures that the more you understand your limitations, the better you will be when justice is to prevail. Eventually you will have your chance to be not only a Hand of Justice, but also her weapon. 'Do what you need to, Ethos. But, remember to come home to me safely.' ***End Transmission***
