Date: 02/16/2009 From the Hidden Journal of Rick Malloy, recovered 04/17/2045 by nomadic tribe of unknown origin: Date - 11/08/2012 ‘Curfew began tonight. The darkness outside is made even darker by the fact that no one is allowed out after a certain time unless they are going to their government job. Everyone else is stuck inside until six in the morning. This should be a fun night spent drinking apple juice and watching the news until I fall asleep on my couch. I hope this Judge nonsense ends today since it has turned this town into a virtual police state.’ The nomad who found the journal stops reading it as one of the other nomads walks into his tent. The second nomad walks over and points to the journal. "Find anything interesting yet?" the nomad asks the other nomad who is reading the journal. The first nomad who was reading the journal nods his head. "Yes," he replies. "I knew this man." "Really? Who is he?" "He was someone I knew once a long time ago, back in Salt Lake City." "What’s Salt Lake City?" "It was a city that existed before the war, before judgment day." "Before our tribe was formed?" He nods his head. "Yes. Right before the tribe was formed after the fall out from the nuclear blasts. After joining up with the scattered remnants of humanity in the area, the two other survivors of the Salt Lake City attack and I formed the tribe. However, I am the last one left. This journal is a record of a past that only I have seen. The same stories I have told are told here." The nomad reaches up to his facial covering and starts to unwrap it since he is indoors and the desert cannot harm him. He finishes taking it off and then removes his goggles to reveal that he is in fact James von Doom, one of Rick Malloy’s two best friends. Looking older now, his face is wrinkled and shows the scars of old age. He is the most weathered person in the tribe, the elder when he was a troublemaker in his youth. He was also an assassin too and his survival skills helped the tribe survive. Charisma he learned from the wrestling business he was in part-time with both Rick and Ash helped him become a leader when the tribe first formed. Turning back to the other nomad, he points to the journal. "Rick kept this hidden so no one would find it except myself or Ash. I’m surprised it still exists after the nuclear attack on the city. In fact, I’m shocked that I still survive. * * * 11/09/2012 Night. The curfew has been in effect now for a second day and I’m already losing my mind. Since the Judge killings, the imperial police have been on the highest possible alert of any terrorist activity. It’s nine o’clock now and instead of being out at night hanging at the bar with my two closest friends Ash and James I’m stuck in my apartment watching the news. Not much is going on besides the top story, which is a major world event. Our Imperial American Air Force has bombed the United Kingdom’s capital city of London and now our forces are invading the island. The horror of learning about the land invasion has made it a shocking day for me. I didn’t think our government would take it this far but they have. Attacking our closest ally in the region over some misguided sense of imperialistic power and ambition. Government sponsored hero Captain Velocity, who is retired but does commercial work for the government, stated on the television that, "The monarchist British communists were behind a vast conspiracy to overthrow our government and take back the country that once belonged to them." Everyone knew he was lying. Hero work had been banned for more than two years so there’s no way he could be doing any crime fighting and adventuring. With all of the madness going on here in Salt Lake he would be stupid to do so. That would be like signing your own death warrant and Captain Velocity is pro-government ass kissing scum and he would never go against the wishes of President Clinton. After Captain Velocity’s little speech they had some annoying commentators and political pundits talking about what had been happening. Of course almost all of them were pro-government. The ones who sounded off against it were quickly taken off the air and replaced with pro-government people on there. When they got bored of talking about our world domination, the government sponsored station began talking other world events. Some of these included the ground war pitting India against Pakistan. Heavy casualties have been inflicted on both sides. Apparently it’s been a really bloody battle and no end is in sight. On the other battle fronts, the Ukraine forces have withstood the Russian attack again. However the war is still raging. The battle between the Arabian Empire and the Balkan Alliance has become an air battle now. Neither side has an advantage in this battle either. I look to the future with a sense of pessimism. This world is going to fall apart more sooner than later. At this rate nuclear war is right around the corner. This only makes it more apparent that the world is screwed more than it ever was before. Sitting on my couch, I can only wonder how long it’s all going to last before everything spirals into oblivion. Reaching for some apple juice in a cup I have next to the couch sitting on an end table, I grab the cup and take a sip before putting it back where I grabbed it from. This sense of dread will not go away. Better get some shut eye before I stay up all night thinking. * * * 02/11/2009 "Screwed again!" Crumbling up the card match sheet from this week’s show and throw it in the garbage. I don’t even want to think about what could’ve been. A ton of people didn’t even show up to the building. And to top that off, some new guy won and didn’t pick me to go into the next round. Instead he picked Aaron Nothings. That has me steamed more than anything. "DID HE NOT EVEN WATCH THE LAST FUCKING SHOW?!" That stupid Meat! He’s signed his own death warrant. If he thinks that Nothings won’t do the same thing to him that he did to me, then me must be living in a Twilight Zone fantasy reality. Nothings doesn’t give a shit about anybody but himself. As I think this someone comes into the room. It’s an official with Just Wrestling. I don’t know his name but I’ve seen him before. He’s one of Shipley’s goon squad. Walking over to me, he hands me a piece of paper. "The card for next week’s show," he says in a somewhat annoyed tone. "You’d better keep showing up because Shipley’s pissed right now and the last thing you need is to be on his hit list." "Don’t you mean shit list?" I ask. "Nope," he replies bluntly before leaving my locker room. After he is gone, I look down at the sheet of paper he gave me and a rage I have never felt before boils up inside of me the moment I see what my match is. "Rick Malloy vs. Buck Fledger?" I read aloud before getting even more pissed than I was even before I found out who I was facing. "What the fuck is this shit?" Apparently they’ve altered the tournament because of all the people not making their show appearances and of all the people, I’m the one who gets fucked in the ass because the other talent doesn’t have a GPS system in their rental cards. "Well this is just fucking great." Before I know it, I’m ripping up the card sheet that they gave me. The anger inside me is at a boiling point now. I think it’s time to kick some people’s teeth down their throats. It’s Just going to be a nightmare for everyone from now on. I’ll make sure of it. Starting with Meat and his little bitch boy Nothings, the tour will never be the same. Pulling out my cell phone, I dial a number and make a call. "Hello? I need a favor…"
