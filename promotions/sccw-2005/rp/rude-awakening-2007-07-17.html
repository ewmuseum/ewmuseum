Rude Awakening

17 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-17-07 FedEx Forum -- Moments after FUSE 45 'It's kinda weird, being left off television like this.' Jason Cruise rests on a long bench inside of the community locker room area. It's a different feeling for a man who once held the most prestigious title in a different organization and had his own personal space to relax and do as he pleased before and after his weekly matches and more often then not, main event moments. It's an adjustment period, he knows this, because whether it's a week, a month, or even years before he climbs up the ranks once more, just as he has always done, he knows there will come a time when finding a moment like this to himself will come a bit easier. However, when your bags and belongings are stashed in a room with that of about thirty others it's not often that you get a moment to reflect to yourself in your own serenity. 'Seems like yesterday I had my name on billboards, posters, and little kids chasing me down city streets just to get a chance to meet me or have me sign their shirts. It's kinda a reality check'. Cruise rubs his fingers through his white ring shorts as he remains seated in a pair of faded denim jeans and a black designer tee that hugs his athletic physique. You can faintly hear the tapping of his flip flop against the concrete, a normal habit of his as he was never one who could sit still for more then a minute. 'Tonight I came here, marched out to the ring, and gave it my all. I left it all out there, everything I've got inside of me, it's the only way I know how to do it. But for what? A few fans that showed up early to avoid heavy concession stand lines. I think about fifteen people saw me dispose of El Diablo tonight and my only hope is that one of them was someone of significance. That even our own company took the time and tuned in for ten minutes before the real show started and saw what I had to offer, what I am capable of doing.' Jason lets out a soft sigh. 'It's not even about the win or loss in this case, it's just about getting noticed. About being able to deliver something that someone out there likes in hopes that they'll give you a chance, an opportunity, to rise up and share the spotlight with someone else and being to take it away from them. I've got it in me, it ain't even buried that deep. I know that there isn't a single soul in this place that can hold me down.' 'But nobody knows this.' He shakes his head. 'Nobody fears this.' Jason hooks his thumbs into his chest. 'And more importantly, nobody respects this.' Like a fire burning inside of him, the Prophecy rises to his feet. 'But I'll be damned if it's gonna stay this way. Tonight, I may not have wrestled under the arena lights, but tomorrow is a new day, a new beginning. And if nobody is going to give me a chance to shine under the FUSE lights then there's only one thing I can do.' His stare hardens, intensity was never something Jason lacked. 'Make my own moments, make my own spotlight, and I'm going to shine down on this company like nobody has ever been able to do before me. Because the day of men feared outside of the ring is over. From this point forward the man that will bring the opposition to their knees will not be a murderer, a street thug, or a backyard bully.' His head shakes confidently. 'It will be a wrestler.' 'It will be the BEST athlete around.' 'And I'll be damned if ANYONE here gets it done better then me.' Jason Cruise, the man, the Prophecy, the wrestler. Fade. * * * * * * The Apartment of Jason Cruise -- Early this morning. 'Wake the hell up!' Jonathan 'Johnny' Monroe's words were loud, obnoxious, and even worse... a very disturbing awakening to Jason Cruise who was sleeping peacefully in HIS bed in HIS house. Yet the house guest that doesn't know the meaning of over staying your welcome was relentless in getting his dear old friend out of bed on this sunny afternoon in Santa Monica. If you haven't figured it out by now, Johnny is THAT guy. He's smooth with the ladies when it comes time to close the deal yet a complete dick to them the other ninety-nine percent of the time. There's a lot of guys that claim to be players or asses as sort of a pick up line, they need to reassure the girl that they aren't a nice guy because they don't want to get stuck in the friend zone playing guitar hero on a Friday night or third wheeling it with their best friend and his chick. Yet, most men simply don't have the balls to go the entire way when it comes to playing the role of dipshit boyfriend slash one night stand. However, Johnny is different. He's inappropriate, he's rude, he says what he's thinking and what he insists that others are. He's the friend you're almost embarrassed to have at times but you just love the fucking bastard so much that you can't help yourself. But back to the matter at hand. Half asleep, Jason mutters, 'Dude.' He lifts his fists to his eyes, tiredly rubbing them and praying that when he reopened them that it would just be a bad dream. 'Are you serious right now, it's seven in the morning.' You know that annoying friend who is Mr. Morning person, yeah, that's Johnny. Exhibit A is the fact that he's already dressed, clean shaven, smells refreshed, yet is only wearing a pair of flowery-teal board shorts. The bastard is ready and anxious to catch the morning break at the beach. I mean who gets all dressed up to go surfing unless they've been up for hours already. 'Yeah get up bitch, the waves are closing out perfectly right now, get your shit and lets go.' All I hear is his annoying voice and as much as I want to go back to sleep I know with Johnny it's not an option. I'm getting up either on my own accord or he's going to drag me out of bed half naked and looking like shit. 'Fuck,' says Jason, pulling himself up. 'Give me five. I had a long night.' I should have just left it at 'fuck'. I know by even subtly mentioning last night that I'm about to open a can of verbal berating and forced story telling followed by juvenile sex jokes that you would expect from a 6th grader who is going through puberty. But hell, what are friends for? 'Ahhhh yeah, that's my baby boy. A little Peter North in the making. I knew you'd come through for me, how was it?' I mean it's not often we actually ask for an earthquake in Southern California, we get our fair share, but if there were ever a time I could use a slight natural disaster, you know just enough to rattle a few glasses and maybe knock over an old High school wrestling trophy that would happen to find it's way into Johnny's skull, well that would be just great. I mean seriously, is that too much to ask? Jason turns away from Johnny, 'Good.' 'Gooood like you fucked her brains out for three hours and then kicked her ass out of bed unless she promised to make breakfast in the morning or good like, uh, you were too busy being a vagina yourself on the date that you forgot about hers?' He really just does lay it out there. At least he's consistent. 'Yeah, something like that.' 'Pfff, what the hell does that mean? 'Something like that?' When you ask a girl if you can just lay it in there to see what it feels like or how about just the tip... that's something like sex. Are you telling me you gave her the two minute tip drill?' Why are friends such assholes when it comes to this stuff. 'No bro, I mean it started off well.' Jason fumbles around the room, scraping a pair of red board shorts from the ground and shoving a Dodgers have onto his head, matting down the wonderful bed head of black hair he has going on. 'The date or sex?' Johnny asks in a bit of curious but now more so concerned tone of voice. 'Nah man, the date. The date started off well.' 'Honestly Jason I don't really care about if you guys held hands or played footsies under the dinner table. I don't want to know that when your elbows touched you gut butterflies and looked each other in the eyes and both of you bashfully smiled. This isn't Boy Meets World man, save the Disney date story for another time.' 'If you're gonna be an asshole about it I'm not going to tell you anything, ' claims Jason as he stumbles around the room, looking for his second sandal. 'Oh, big tough guy,' Johnny puffs his chest and shoulders out, waddling around the room in a mocking manner. 'Boy gets a little piece of ass and is suddenly too good to tell his best friend about it.' 'Yeah, that's it.' Frustrated, he walks out of his bedroom, only to trip over an open suitcase and a mad pile of clothing, nearly falling to the floor. Instead Jason manages to grab a hold of the backside of the couch, pulling himself up and glaring at Johnny. 'Dammit Johnny, pick up your shit!' I'm awake now. 'Hey, Hey! Stop kicking my shirts! That shit is Armani!' As they enter the living room they are met with a soft, sensuous voice. 'What's with all the yelling? What does a girl have to do to get some sleep around here?' Johnny's head snaps to the sofa in the room, adjusts back towards Jason, and then back to not only the sofa but the young woman curled up into a ball with a light blue blanket and a matching pillow which she begins to bury her face into to block out the noise. Naturally, it's Marie. Johnny scratches his head, 'Now how did I miss that?' Jason smiles at him, 'Beach?' 'Yeah, umm -- Johnny's words trail off, losing his train of thought. 'I'll tell you on the way.' Black.
