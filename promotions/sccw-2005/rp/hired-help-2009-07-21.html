Date: 07-21-09 Charles Slaton sat at the bar nursing the bottle of Budweiser he had ordered over half an hour ago. It wasn't that he didn't enjoy the beer, but the fact that he was still working. Twenty two years on the job, but the economic crisis had put him back in the private dick business. He tried his best not to meet clients while he was supposed to be investigating murders, but this potential client said this would be the only time he had available, so he accepted it for what it was. Work. A man slid onto the stool next to him and ordered a shot of Jack and a Shiner to chase it down with. Chuck was quick to give the man the once over, making mental notes about the way the man was dressed. Faded blue jeans with the thighs worn to near white and a navy blue Diamond Wrestling Syndicate sleeveless shirt that hung loose over his brown leather belt. There was an aura of uneasiness about him, from the four day old scruff that covered his face to the way his eyes darted back and forth over the crowd. "Mr. Minor?" Charles inquired of the Hispanic man with eyes as green as the sea. A nod was the only response he received from the older man with salt and pepper hair, before he tossed the shot of whiskey into the back of his throat. He swallowed hard, the sour look on his face the only evidence that it had any effect on him. They sat there silent for a moment, before Slaton decided to speed things up a bit. "You said you had a job for me?" "I do," came the reply. Another moment passed, and Slaton's impatience was beginning to show. "Well, I haven't got all night." "Your partner's covering for you," the man said as he motioned for the bartender to bring him another shot. "Just like you do for him when he does his security gigs." "How did," Slaton sat there dumbfounded, unsure exactly what this man wanted and how he knew so much information about him. "I'm not in a position to run into things blindly, Mr. Slaton." For those that have no clue as to just who the man is, his name is Benito Minorez. Fans of the National Wrestling Council would remember him as Benny Minor, one half of the Pacific region tag team, Minor Details. Fans of Sin City Championship Wrestling would barely know him as the absentee father of the Mexican Minx, Julissa Minorez. "I see." Charles watches the bartender place the new shot glass in front of Benny, a rather creepy smile on his face as he eyes the two sitting alone at the far end of the bar. "I doubt you do," Benito commented. "But I wanted to be sure I wasn't picking the wrong man for the job." "So you think investigating me means I'm just going to take the job." "Far from it," Benito smiled. "You're free to take off whenever you want. You'll just miss out on all the fun if you do." "So what's the case?" "Do you love your daughter, Mr. Slaton?" It was Slaton's turn to toss the shot down into the back of his throat, the burning sensation quick to make him wince. "I'll take that as a yes." Benito said solemnly. "I know about the situation with your wife, Mr. Slaton, but what I don't understand is why you would so readily give up the connection you have with your child." "It's a long story," Charles motioned for another round, then turned toward Benito. "I don't see what your problem has to do with me and my daughter though." "It has a lot to do with it," Benito smiled. "You see, I love my daughter much like I'm sure you do yours, but we are not on speaking terms." "Yeah, well, I'm sure you two have your reasons." "She does," Benito thanked the bartender for the two new drinks, then pulled the beer up off the bar without bringing it to his lips. "I'm just respecting her wishes." "I still don't see what this has to do with me though." "I need someone that is willing to be discreet, Charles. Someone who knows that the bond between a father and his daughter is one that should never be broken." Slaton, who nursed his beer up to this point, downed what was left in the bottle like it was water. Beads of sweat began to build up on his brow, and a lump got lodged in his throat. "My daughter blames me for things that happened in her life," Benito continued with his story. "She thinks I ruined her career, and in turn she took away the one thing she knew meant the world to me." Slaton watched the man wipe a single tear that had built up in the corner of his eye. "It should have been her, I can admit that now, but it wasn't." Benito turned to face the man that had become enthralled by his story. "I cared more about finding the next big superstar than I did the one I had right in front of me. But there has to be more to this than that, and I want you to find out what it is." "Seems like this would be a lot easier if you just asked her yourself." Slaton downed his shot, another wince followed before he shook it off. "A lot cheaper too." "I can't," Benito answered. "Juli can't know I'm back." "Just who is this daughter of yours, Mr. Minor?" * * * * * * "Hey, Juli!" Julissa Minorez stands in front of a full length door mirror, her hair still damp and glistening from the shower she just took, wearing nothing more than a white hotel room towel. Seeing as how she did not bring her own, and ended up using one of the complimentary ones, you really have to hate the hotels for providing them. "Yeah," she answers, teasing her hair with her left hand to shake out some of the water. "Hurry up, I'm starving out here!" "Oh, hold your dick!" She screams in a way very unlike a lady, as she adjusts the towel before it slips down from around her very ample breasts. "I'll be out in a second." "Don't make me come in there and slap you!" "If you're hungry, you better not," she says with a sheepish grin that he cannot see. "You know what happens when you start to slap me around." "Shit, you're right." The man in the other room replies. "So how does Chinese sound?" "Sounds good," Jadian Bridden leans into the open doorway, his arms folded neatly across his chest. "I've been dying to have some Egg Foo Young." "I haven't had spring rolls in for-" Julissa is cut off by the sound of someone knocking at the door. She and Jay exchange a glance. "You expecting someone?" "No," he replies. "But it might be security the way you were screaming earlier." "Oh shut up and go answer the door." "Yes ma'am," the sarcasm practically drips from his lips as he turns on the balls of his feet to head for the door. "And I wasn't the one crying like a little girl when I came, so don't put this all on me." "Oh, you wanna go there do you?" he says with a laugh as he pulls open the door, only for the smile to fade away when he sees who the woman standing before him is. "What the hell do you want?" Kathryn Shaw smiles as she walks past the former LiveWire champion. "Nice to see you too, Jay." "Don't give me that shit, Kat," he glances out the door to make sure no one saw her come in, then closes it just as fast. "You know better than to just show up like this." She practically glides across the room, her eyes scanning the room just as Julissa walks in wearing a floral patterned white halter top sun dress. "Kat?" She appears a bit more surprised than Jadian is, but the look they exchange gives off the feeling that neither one of them is sure why the Sex Kitten had shown up unannounced. "I know," Kathryn Shaw replies with a smirk. "I had a hard time believing it was me too." "It's not..." She sees Jay shrug as Kat slinks back onto the couch in the center of the room. "What are you doing here?" "We need to talk about your decision to stand by Alex's side." Shaw folds her right leg over the left, her hands neatly placed on her thigh as she parts her lips in that incredulous smile that she normally uses as a weapon. "It's the wrong one and you and I both know it." "Is it the wrong one?" Juli answers the question with one of her own. "If you ask me, you're both putting personal feelings ahead of business. Wasn't that the first thing you taught me, Kat, to make sure that nothing gets in the way of business. That's exactly what I'm doing." "That's not fair." "Neither is putting me in the middle of this little 'war' the two of you have going on." "That isn't something we can discuss right now," Kathryn nods in the direction of Bridden. "Like she's not just going to tell me what you say later," Duncan Drake would have grounds for copyright infringement if he saw the smirk on Jadian's face. "I'll leave if it'll make you feel better, though. I've heard stories about how you get when your panties are in a bunch." "I'd like to hear what she has to say," Julissa, standing there across from the Sex Kitten, tilts her head ever so slightly as her eyes shift in the same direction. Bridden understands full well what she was trying to say without saying a word. "Fine," he throws his arms into the air. "Let me just stand outside like an idiot while you girls talk." "Jay, don't be an ass about it." "It doesn't help that I've been hungry for the last hour and a half." The bitterness in Jay's voice isn't lost on the Mexican Minx, but he still smiles nonetheless. "Make it quick." "Do you think he'd wait another hour," Kathryn throws the question out there with a mischievous grin. "I know he would," Julissa fires back. "But he's not going to. Like he said, make it quick." "Never one to beat around the bush," Kathryn crosses her legs to the opposite side as she leans forward to rest her forearms on her thighs. "That was the first thing Alex told me when she mentioned you." "If all you wanted to do was reminisce on old times you could have posted on my facebook." "Sorry," Kathryn scoffs at the statement. "Twitter is taking up all of my time right now." "What are you doing here, Kathryn?" Julissa folds her arms across her chest and purses her lips as her eyebrows rise slightly. "I'm here for you," is the reply given. "Haven't you noticed the difference in her lately?" "No," Julissa snaps her neck to the opposite side. "All I've seen is you playing Pretty Woman with Kennedy. You know what can happen...what she can do. Why risk it?" "You haven't been around long enough to see the difference," Kathryn stands up, straightening out the wrinkles in her black skirt. "Do you remember when we first met?" "Again with the past," Juli sighs, her arms up in frustration. "I know you're the one who taught me the ropes, and you're the reason I am where I am, but as much as you want me to remember...you apparently forgot that she's the reason you're here at all." "I haven't forgotten anything, Juli." Shaw moves in Juli's direction. "Try as I may, I have never been able to forget everything Lexi and I have done together over the years. But you don't have to let that happen to you." "You know what," Julissa steps aside and motions toward the door. "You need to go." "I'm sorry you feel that way." "No you're not," Julissa replies. "You know damn well that I'm not going to turn on Alex, and you knew that before you came down here. So I'll ask you one more time before you go...What do you want?" "I want you to tell me what her plans are." And there it was. "I need someone on the inside." Julissa didn't answer her, she just stood there staring at her. Jadian was sitting on the ground outside wondering when he was going to get his Chinese.
