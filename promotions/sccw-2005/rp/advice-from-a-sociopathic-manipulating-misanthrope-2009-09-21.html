Advice from a Sociopathic, Manipulating Misanthrope

21 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/21/2009 Inside the Boulevard Mall, I’m wandering around amongst the people of Sin City. They all look like criminals to me. Tattoos all over their bodies. It’s like I’m inside of a prison movie. I don’t know what to think at this point. I’ve been seeing hallucinations for weeks on end. I’ve been in about fifteen alliances at the same time. I haven’t won a match in weeks. In other words, I have no idea what to do at this point. I’m booked in a match teaming with Josh Marquez as my partner. That’s all I know… I’m out of it. I’m lost. At no time in my life have I been surrounded by such turmoil. My wrestling and rapping careers are going down the toilet both at the same time. "This sucks." My beard is itching so I start scratching it. People start staring at me so I stop and keep walking. I continue walking through the mall humming to myself. Still people looking at me. They probably think I’m a homeless ex-porn star or something. Walking past some more gawkers, I find the mall exit and leave. Once outside, it’s like a zombie invasion. All of the mindless people are heading toward the mall to go shopping for things they don’t need. "So much for the fucking Recession." Shaking my head, I walk past some women who are dressed like they’re about to walk the streets for money. They’re dressed like skanks, just like all the women now days. They all have STD’s and all they want to do is spread them around. Well except for my loves Desade and Aimz. They’re clean. They have no diseases in their vag, unlike Spacely who is spreading his alien swine flu around the country like that monkey from Outbreak. However he is not what I’m worried about all of my eighteen thousand opponents this week on Equilibrium. There’s Jay Draven. Legion. Josh Marquez. Darius Drannen. Mitchell Quinlan. And it’s in Vegas so I know that von Doom will be there somewhere too. He says he wants to help me but I think otherwise. He’s a sneaky bastard, that one. I walk around the parking lot like a traveling vagabond. My car isn’t anywhere to be seen. "Hmmmm…" Continue to look around for my car but still not seeing it. Just then I realize that I walked to the mall and didn’t come in a car. "Shit…" Head for the sidewalk with the idea of riding a bus but I decide to keep walking. See some gang bangers walking toward me. They think I’m homeless so they don’t mug me. Instead they get the guy behind me and rob him instead. Maybe the FBI was tailing me or something. I shrug my shoulders. "Probably something to do with von Doom." Keep on walking through the night surrounded by creatures of darkness and a bunch of non-hetero vampiric and lycan entities that women are fingering themselves over right now. Continue walking down the street until a car pulls up next to me. Looking over, it’s von Doom. "Get in," he says. I shrug my shoulders and walk around to the passenger’s side, getting in. I buckle up as he starts driving. "Rick, we need to talk," he says to me as he drives. "Ok, let’s talk daddi-o," I reply. "Rick, you seem to need some guidance and I think I’m the guy to give you the advice that you need," he says to me. "I can give you the leadership that you really need. Your career has hit a maelstrom. It’s in complete turmoil and you only have yourself to blame." "You’re too busy pandering to these hypocritical fans that don’t even give a shit about you. They only care about their ‘heroes’ that are really drug addicts, adulterers, and basically the scum of the Earth. The fans are nothing more than harbor seals that clap like mindless zombies for the same wrestlers." He continues to talk as he drives. Looking out the window, we’re stuck in traffic near the Strip. Casinos are everywhere. This is like a dark paradise or something. "No one is an individual anymore and that’s the thing. They only like shit just like in the music industry. Nothing but shit. You need to tell the fans to stick it and do your own thing. You don’t need anything from them. All you need to do is do what you have to in order to win." "Now this is what I want you to do. I want you to go into this pay-per-view and show how much hate inside your heart. That is your only chance." He then pulls the car over to the side of the road. "Now get out and think about what I said," he says. I get out of the car and am about to say something as I close the door but he speeds off so I don’t have a chance to say anything to him.
