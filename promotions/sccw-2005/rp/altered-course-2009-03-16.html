Altered Course.

16 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/16/2009 The stinging crush of defeat… My heart beats a hundred times a minute. I feel like I’m waiting at death’s door. A never-ending nightmare that has seemingly no end. Perpetual darkness blinds me after the show. Hands hurting, feet on fire. Sweat pours down from my forehead like a mini-faucet dripping water into a sink. My back feels like street pavement after being driven over by a semi-truck. Head pounding like I’ve been stoned to death in ancient times. The world spins around me and yet I am still. Stars and planets colliding all around me. I have no idea at this point what exists and what is an illusion or possibly a mirage. The backstage area melts into nothingness as everything fades to black. Silence. Darkness. All that keeps me company… Are the dreams in my head And the music… Oh, what beautiful music it is. Sounds like classical, but more modern. Just then… AWAKE! Awaking from my slumber, I look up to see the Spanish janitor staring at me. After waiting a few moments, all he says to me is this. "Everybody is gone, senor." He then quietly turns and walks away. Rising up from the bench I was laying on, I see that the whole arena is empty except for me. Just Wrestling is vacated now. Wrestlers have been waxing and waning more than the phases of the moon. I’ve seen all sorts of things here. Lots of strange and weird things. It’s almost like the Star Wars Cantina scene but without the aliens. In the backstage area at shows, I’ve seen some weird stuff. Voodoo rituals… Bags that look like they have bodies in them… Weird vibrating mushroom objects,,, And then the shows begin. Some bad ass wrestlers here, all of whom I couldn’t even lace their bootstraps. In fact, I might even be their bootstraps. Aaron Nothings is a legend but he still hit me in the head with a chair. I became an afterthought in Just after that incident. The Illustrious Face-Eater is still eating faces and drinking the blood of the innocent. I think he may even be rabid. The legend known as Jon Kano is here too. He was in PCW and some other places. People lick his vagina a lot, kind of like Jenna Jameson but a dude. Meat has been epicly awesome since he arrived. He beat me in his first match, no less. He even had a bromance with Nothings last tour. Now there’s a guy named Blake Grey who I’m now facing for the second week in a row. Last week, he beat me. This week, I’m going to beat him. And not beating him like you beat a penis but beat him like a father does to the punk who knocks up his little girl. On the other hand I’m also facing Erik Magnum, one of the most bad ass wrestlers in the federation. Blood will flow from my face this week. It will be like a battle from an ancient battlefield where conquerors and heroes battled until almost no one was left alive. Flesh will be ripped open. The wounds will never heal. Maim will be the word of the day. No one will survive. No One Survives In The End. Except me… And I will survive. However, the same cannot be said for Magnum and Grey. The aches and pains of my war this week weaken my body as I walk over to my bags to find my clothes. Pulling out some clothes, I start to change under the dim lighting of my locker room. My almost naked body creaks and groans as I put on my shirt and pants before tying my shoes. Creak… Groan… Pop… "ARGH!" When will this ever end? I will never stop wrestling. Pain is a part of the job. I will be broken and destroyed. My body a wretched, destroyed heap. I will no longer be able to move. Then I will finally die. That is when the universe shall show me the real truths. And when Just Wrestling will fade from my memories forever. * * * Black… Darkness… Twilight…. Stars… Endless universes… Clouded dreams… Dawn… AWAKE. Looking over toward my dresser, I see that it is 8:45 am. Past that I see that items related to my servitude to Mr. Shipley. Mountains of relics and merchandise from Just Wrestling. I am a slave to the system for which I am in. Destined to be in carnivals and high school gyms until I die. Radiohead is playing from somewhere outside my apartment window. Images run through my head of endless deserts with a moon rising above in the twilight sky. Vastness of sand is all over. The dunes spread out to the horizon line like an endless wave in a giant ocean. Shaking my head, I finally awake. Just Wrestling has taken over my brain! I feel like Dorothy in Oz. I see Just everywhere I go. Every face I see looks like Shipley. I can’t escape from it. I feel like this place is my prison and the other wrestlers are stuck in here with me. No escaping… All hope gone… DEATH. Wrestling will be my death, that is the conclusion I have come to. I know this and so do some of the other wrestlers probably. I can feel my face melting as I think about it. Feels like an egg on a frying pan. Life falling apart… Pieces Jigsaw Scattered Like glass. I am nothing now. Just a man named Rick Malloy. Nobody will care about me. Nobody ever did. * * * Journal of Rick Malloy: 3/16/09 Life falling apart. Not ready for match. Going to get ass kicked. That much is obvious. Can’t believe my bad luck. Maybe I was cursed by someone or something. Life sucks. Death is my escape. Too bad I ran out of rope last year. Facing Alex Grey and Erik Magnum. I am nothing compared to them. NOTHING! Who is Rick Malloy? A nobody. A never-was. A never-will-be. I am a loser. But, then again, there are always losers in wrestling. There are losers in almost every match. That is the optimism I need right now. Keep having visions of an endless desert that I cannot escape from. Note to self: Avoid deserts at all costs. Writer’s block. Journal entry over.
