BOOM! Goes the dynamite at the base of the bridge. (PRIME Rev200)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today In the summer of 2009 King Blueberry, formerly a contracted employee of PRIME, was asked to return for the 200th edition of Revolution. As part of this agreement, he was also asked to take part in the filming of some promotional spots to air on the FX Network. Unfortunately none of these clips ever made it to air, as network executives decided that they were too strange, too bizarre, and too outlandish to be shown to the general viewing audience. Recently these clips were sto… err… liberated (yeah, that’s a good word) from the FX vault, which is why we can bring them to you now, unedited, and in their original state. * * * FX-PRIMEKB001x01 SCENE: Dressed in a pale blue lab coat, King Blueberry stands behind an infomercial-style table display. On the table before him are bricks made of what appear to be a dark metallic material. The camera zooms in closer as a light, pleasant jingle plays softly in the background. BLUEBERRY: Hello, I’m King Blueberry, and I’m here today to talk to you about a special product from our friends at Vashaun Industries. SCENE: The music ceases, and King Blueberry slips his right hand into a welder’s glove, padded and thick. The King picks up one of these bricks, holding it as far away from his body as his arm will allow. BLUEBERRY: Vashaun Industries brings to you the Kaiser-grade Personality Magnet. You’ll notice I’m holding this pretty far away from my body. You see, the Personality Magnet is a cold, lifeless object. On its own it holds no natural interest, no personality. It also has the uncanny ability to suck the personality away from anything it touches, rendering that object as dull and lifeless as the magnet itself. SCENE: King Blueberry carefully replaces the brick on the table, where it proceeds to do absolutely nothing. BLUEBERRY: Make sure you pick yours up at Revolution 200! SCENE: The King gives the camera a hearty thumbs-up as the scene fades to the PRIME logo. A professional-sounding voiceover narrates for us. NARRATOR: PRIME presents Revolution 200 live from Madison Square Garden on Wednesday, July 8. Tune in to watch all your favorite wrestlers on this milestone show! Revolution 200, live! SCENE: The logo fades away, leaving only darkness. BLUEBERRY (OSV): Suck my berries, baldy! * * * FX-PRIMEKB002x01 SCENE: King Blueberry is about a hundred yards away from an old, run-down Hess gas station. The gas station itself appears to have been abandoned years ago, as weeds and small shrubs have overrun the pavement. BLUEBERRY: Hello, I’m King Blueberry, and I’m here today to talk to you about a fun project for you and your friends. SCENE: The King gestures at the gas station behind him. Of note is a large suspension bridge off in the distance. BLUEBERRY: Across this great nation there are hundreds, neigh thousands of run down old buildings in need of a new life. Today I will show you how we can give that new life to this particular Hess station… by blasting it straight to hell. After all, this is professional wrestling. Shit like this happens all the time. SCENE: The King steps off screen. BLUEBERRY: And three… two… one… SOUND EFFECT: BOOM! SCENE: One massive explosion (CGI?) later, and the scene fades to the PRIME logo. A professional-sounding voiceover narrates for us. NARRATOR: PRIME presents Revolution 200 live from Madison Square Garden on Wednesday, July 8. Tune in to watch all your favorite wrestlers on this milestone show! Revolution 200, live! SCENE: The logo fades away, leaving only darkness. BLUEBERRY (OSV): Who’s getting arrested for this? Nobody! * * * FX-PRIMEKB003x01 SCENE: King Blueberry stands in an empty classroom, leaning nonchalantly against a blackboard. On the board are written a number of words, all of them pluralized, all of them having their final "s" replaced by a "z". BLUEBERRY: Hi there, I’m King Blueberry, and I’m here today to tell you about a fabulous new way to communicate with your friends and family! SCENE: The King steps away from the blackboard and around to the front of the teacher’s desk. Moving a stack of paper aside, the King hops up onto the desk, making himself comfortable. BLUEBERRY: With this new proven system you’ll not only win the admiration of all your friends, but they’ll be so impressed by your edgy ways… sorry, wayz… that they’ll collectively rush as one to the internet and tell all their friendz how great you truly are. Now, I know what you’re thinking. "Surely it can’t be that easy." Oh, don’t be fooled. It zure as zhit iz! SCENE: He gestures to the blackboard behind him. There, in chalk, are the words "retardz", "foolz", and "dumbazzez". SCENE: The scene fades to the PRIME logo. A professional-sounding voiceover narrates for us. NARRATOR: PRIME presents Revolution 200 live from Madison Square Garden on Wednesday, July 8. Tune in to watch all your favorite wrestlers on this milestone show! Revolution 200, live! SCENE: The logo fades away, leaving only darkness. (Yes, this part is being recycled.) BLUEBERRY (OSV): Now try it with numberz, jackazz! * * * FX-PRIMEKB004x01 SCENE: As if you’re not completely sick of him yet, King Blueberry now sits in a stall of a local truck stop restroom, his arms pulled in tight to make sure that they don’t touch the walls. Also, he’s wearing pants, so let’s just get that out of the way right now. BLUEBERRY (whispering): Hi there, I’m King Blueberry, and I’m here today to tell you about an interesting phenomenon that is sweeping across our nation’s rest areas. SCENE: The King pauses at the sound of the restroom door opening. Footsteps echo on the grungy tiled floor, and are quickly followed by the sounds of a zipper and… yeah, you get the rest. BLUEBERRY (whispering): In restrooms just like this one a special breed of man can be found. He is a hunter of sorts, and he hunts a special prey. You see, this kind of hunter searches for only one thing: the venomous trouser snake. He will crouch low, and peer through special holes drilled just for hunting these animals. Sometimes the snake will spit its venom in the hunter’s eye, causing them to peer less. It is these "peer less" hunters who have taken control of our country’s rest areas, so if you happen to see a man in baggy orange pants, run. SCENE: The scene fades to the PRIME logo. A professional-sounding voiceover narrates for us. NARRATOR: PRIME presents Revolution 200 live from Madison Square Garden on Wednesday, July 8. Tune in to watch all your favorite wrestlers on this milestone show! Revolution 200, live! SCENE: The logo fades away, leaving only darkness. (Seriously, are you sick of this yet?) BLUEBERRY (OSV): Peer Pressure, my ass… wait, not literally! * * * FX-PRIMEKB005x01 SCENE: White blazer. Red wig. King Blueberry. Desk. GO! BLUEBERRY: Good evening. I am King Blueberry, and I am here this evening to administer a warning to anyone who wishes to attend this, the 200th episode of Revolution. SCENE: Did we mention that he’s padded his chest to give the appearance of breasts? No? Well, now we did. Also, if you haven’t noticed these scene blocks are getting shorter and shorter. BLUEBERRY: As the only cold-hearted, iron-willed redhead in professional wrestling… SCENE: His/her speech is cut short by the appearance of a smaller man running into the scene, approaching the King behind the desk and whispering into his ear. We’ll call him Tyler. It’s a common enough name. BLUEBERRY: What do you mean "another one"? TYLER: (Whisper whisper whisper whisper) BLUEBERRY: Oh, well then. Okay, as the first cold-hearted, iron-willed rehead… SCENE: Again he’s cut off. TYLER: (Whisper whisper whisper whisper) BLUEBERRY: What the hell. Fine. As the BEST… SCENE: The King pauses, waiting for Tyler to interrupt. This time, instead of whispering, Tyler simply shakes his head "no". BLUEBERRY: Oh, fuck it then. SCENE: The scene fades to the PRIME logo. A professional-sounding voiceover narrates for us. It’s the same shit you’ve seen four times already. NARRATOR: PRIME presents Revolution 200 live from Madison Square Garden on Wednesday, July 8. Tune in to watch all your favorite wrestlers on this milestone show! Revolution 200, live! SCENE: The logo fades away, leaving only darkness. BLUEBERRY (OSV): What do you mean I can’t have tattoos either?! * * * FX-PRIMEKB006x01 SCENE: Finally, the last of the lost promotional spots! King Blueberry stands in an open field, dressed rather casually save for his trademark lucha-style mask (which contrary to popular belief isn’t shaped like anything) and a rather ornate Native American headdress. BLUEBERRY: How! I’m High Chief Blueberry, and I’m coming to you today from the great wilderness with an important message. SCENE: A gentle breeze blows through the area, sending an empty bag of Doritos floating across the landscape. High Chief Blueberry notices this, and a single tear forms at his eye. Of course, you can’t really see it because of the mask, but it’s there nonetheless. BLUEBERRY: Now, you may be wondering why a giant fruit is dressed in Native American garb, but it’s actually not that uncommon here in PRIME. In fact, you’d be surprised to know that there are members of the roster who are very much into the totem pole, specifically the bottom – the power bottom - and who frequently take a big ole suck from the peace pipe. So please, don’t judge a man by the size of the tee-pee he’s pitching, judge him instead by his shitty, shitty theme music. SCENE: PRIME logo. Voiceover. Copy and paste. NARRATOR: PRIME presents Revolution 200 live from Madison Square Garden on Wednesday, July 8. Tune in to watch all your favorite wrestlers on this milestone show! Revolution 200, live! SCENE: The logo fades away, leaving only darkness. BLUEBERRY (OSV): Ha! Your name sounds like ‘Gay Penis’. * * * And that’s how you executive produce.
