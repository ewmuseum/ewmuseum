Large Opportunity, No Anchovies

18 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/08 A 7:20 departure from LAX en route to Reno on a passenger plane the size of a roach motel. This economical flight was a thrifty way to save a little dough, and Charley didn’t mind a little close quarters with his peers. Slumping into his faux-leather bucket seat straight out of 1974, the fresh-faced character fumbled through his Ipod to get to his Red-Eye playlist. It was only fifteen minutes into the journey, and the petite redhead beside him had already succumbed to sleep. Sneaking a glance down at her skirted legs and making a quick assessment of the Fire Engine Red-colored lipstick that appeared to be fresh, he contemplated forcing an accidental shoulder bump to wake her up and hopefully initiate some kind of conversation. His gift of gab and razor-sharp charm might be enough to net him a couple of drinks and a complimentary make out session when they landed, but as soon as the music hit, he favored against the attempt. That kind of shit only works on camera, anyway. Cookie let his eyes wander, eventually diverting them to the larger woman sitting in the aisle seat, spilling over a bit into the path. She was obviously a bit self-conscious in her plus-size dress, making it a point not to squish her cohorts together too much. I mean, it’s not like she appeared to be pregnant with twin ottomans…but her awareness shone like a Buddha statue from her plump stubby fingers all the way down to just below her cankles. Staring ahead with a blank expression, the woman gleamed uncomfortable as she gazed up at the emergency posters…contemplating exactly how the inflatable lifesaver would fit around her fat neck without choking her to death. Charley couldn’t help but feel sympathy for her. Although he didn’t exactly have the sixth sense, the perspiration on her brow and the can of Slim-Fast between her thighs set off enough alarms for him to decipher. He wanted to rub her shoulders, give her a big ol’ smooch on her big ol’ cheeks, and tell her that she had nothing to be ashamed of. The mere fact that she felt uncomfortable about the possibilities of making someone else feel uncomfortable made her more of a person than the rail-thin supermodels that she longed to be like. He had been blessed with an uncanny ability to turn the negative into the positive. While he didn’t suffer from a weight problem, nine out of ten shrinks would agree that his sexual mentality was quite abnormal. It’s not every day that one tries to take a lick of a toe before making their way up between a woman’s thighs. This drive of the libido usually led to a dead-end road in any sort of serious relationship that Cookie had ever ventured toward…but his lemonade-making skills only seemed to grow more prudent as these follies continuously happened. Luckily, his new career choice didn’t leave him too much time for settling down to have a couple of Chips with a ball-and-chain. While Charley wasn’t a huge fan of having his cheeks spread open to check for a wick in his ass at various airports around the United States, the travel aspect of the business certainly didn’t bother him. As long as he was going somewhere, he wasn’t going nowhere…and as long as he was getting beat up for the sake of entertainment and a paycheck, his big ol’ heart had a big ol’ smile. He missed his mom. When it came to family, she was all he had. And while he didn’t exactly believe in Heaven, he knew that she had to be beaming with pride last Friday night as she watched the flat-screen positioned strategically on the face of a cumulus cloud. Maybe his Dad was up there too, cleaning her pool just after going down on her. Charley never questioned why he and his mom shared a love for professional wrestling on television. He didn’t suspect that maybe she just enjoyed men in skimpy outfits putting each other in compromising positions…or that she only pretended to like it because her son did. It wasn’t the norm for a hoity-toity divorce-rich woman to know the schematics of a German suplex, but Cookie’s sole motivation for entering the sport was because he knew that she’d enjoy him being in a skimpy outfit putting someone else into a compromising position. Maybe. Of course, what motivates one to start doing something doesn’t necessarily facilitate what keeps them going. Crisp could add Friday’s first television experience to that furnace. It was everything he could have dreamed that it would be: a lot of attention focused solely on him. If there’s one thing that everyone likes, it’s attention. That Saturday, yet another log was shoved into the stove. Opportunity. An opportunity to have the aforementioned attention increase tenfold. To go from just another face on the roster to having his face shoved into the cameltoe of recognition. He was just on the cusp of becoming a teenager when he piped his goal half-heartedly to Ms. Crisp. "I wanna do that one day, Momma." "What," she joked, "wear tights?" Charley laughed. "No! I wanna bodyslam someone!" "Bodyslam your sisters." "Too fat," he quipped. "Now Cookie," she scolded, "you shouldn’t joke about that. That’s not nice. So you’re telling your dear old Moms that you want to wrestle those muscley old brutes on TV? My baby might get hurt!" "Shoot! They can’t hurt me when I’m bodyslamming THEM. I’ll be the champion! Of the UNIVERSE." Ms. Crisp smiled at the aspirations of her tiny little twelve-year-old, and decided to play along to his whims. "Of the universe, huh? So you’re going to wrestle on Mars?" "Nooooo! Martians will come to Earth just to wrestle me. And get their butts beat." Ms. Crisp shook her head. "It won’t be that easy. They’ll come to Earth with their anti-gravity belts, and they’ll be able to flip around better than Woobs or Lightning*. That, and they might use a Destructo-Ray. How are you going to beat their butt if they use a Destructo-Ray?" "Mom, Destructo-Rays aren’t even real." "They might be! On Mars." The Crisp-coined persona of "Mart Shan" quickly became a staple in the San Francisco-based Holy Cow Championship Wrestling, where shows were held bi-weekly on open dates at the world-famous Cow Palace. He thought it was a clever-enough schtick…donning a green jumpsuit and painting his face to match while wearing rabbit ears and an "anti-gravity" utility belt for his matches. Fans absolutely hated this character, and Charley loved every second of the reactions he would garnish…whether it be the boos that would result when Daft Punk’s "Around The World" would hit and he’s "ride" down the entranceway on a cardboard cutout of a spaceship or the "I come in peace" handshake offering to his opponent only to kick him square in the balls when their hand was extended. He oftentimes threatened the young fans a blast of his own Destructo-Ray, which was a Super Soaker that contained dyed water. Those Cow Palace evenings were the events that originally got him noticed by the bigwigs of FUSE just before the changeover. Unfortunately, when Mart Shan charged up his saucer to refill on Destructo Juice, the gimmick hit an asteroid belt and became star dust in a matter of minutes. There wasn’t a character that Charley ‘Cookie’ Crisp could play better than Charley ‘Cookie’ Crisp. As far as the green spandex costume goes, it still hangs in his closet for a certain mistress who requests he wear it during their sessions. (She’s one sick fuck.) Cookie traced the cursive outline of the Dodgers logo on his T-shirt, trying to push the nervousness of his upcoming venture out of his mind. He had been called back to Vegas on the request of SCCW management to shoot a commercial to air on the Temptation broadcast, which threw a kink into his training regiment. Fortunately, Charley and ‘kinks’ go hand in hand, and the only thing that could tear him away from a pre-match performance anxiety was to do a little acting. "Never gonna give you up…never gonna let you down…" he muttered in unison along with his headphones, unconcerned if any strange looks may fall his way. Straightening up into his seat and lowering his eyes to the stitching pattern of the fabric in front of him, Charley was overcome with a force of nature so powerful that even the tightest of spincter clinching wouldn’t be able to quell it. It is never a good idea to double up on the black beans when ordering a spinach-wrapped burrito just before getting boarding a flight…however, he was on the last week of his bulking cycle and needed the extra shot of protein to pack on a few more pounds of lean mass. However, the thought of interrupting the sweetheart’s slumber by shimmying to the head to create a few ‘snakes on a plane’ made him hesitate. Instead, Charley smiled as he opted for the alternate recourse. Such a devilish thing he was about to do…but the humor factor of his master plan was too much to resist. It was as if the Foot Clan were in the midst of breaking-and-entering and Turtle Power had risen stealthily from the sewers to lay their attack. A masterful squeeze to alleviate the pressure, going off completely undetected. Still, in order to maintain his defense, Charley acted as if nothing was happening, still nodding his head in time to Rick Astley’s voice through his headphones. The large woman at the end was the first to feel the wake of Analcane Katrina, shifting in her seat and making a concerned look as her jaws loosened. Cookie looked down to notice her leg quivering ever so slightly, prompting him to make his move. He slowly tilted his head and shoulders forward and gazed at the woman, matching her facial expression with one of his own. Shrugging his shoulders, he pointed to the sleeping beauty in the middle and wrinkled his nose, smiling a bit. She responded by shooting him a smile of her own, nodding knowingly. Mission accomplished. While people like her may have been graced with immaculate beauty, untouchable talent, a gift for gab, and completely undeniable charm…everyone fucking farts at some point or another. It was a risky opportunity. And he took it. When it knocks, not only should you answer it…but come to the door naked, so you leave a lasting impression. Wiener wafting in the breezeway, wearing nothing but a smile. And a lucha libre mask. Not that I’ve never done it to an unsuspecting pizza deliveryman or anything… ‘Hi, I’m Opportunity and I’ve come to…HOLY SHIT, DUDE.’ ‘Sorry, you caught me in the middle of my weekly All-Male Applesauce Wrestling night. So that’s two pepperoni, one supreme…how much do I owe you?’ That’s a lotta meat for a tasty chance like this one. *Old references may not particularly match up to proper age.
