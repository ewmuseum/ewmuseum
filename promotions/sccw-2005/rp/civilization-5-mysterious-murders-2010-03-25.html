Civilization #5: Mysterious Murders

25 March 2010

Date: 03/25/2010 "Hey, what are you talkin' about, 'allegedly' tells it like it is. I am the man who tells it like it is!" -Jesse Ventura to Howard Finkel in the ring, Wrestlemania III Von Doom I 03/07/2010 Afternoon After coming back to Civilization headquarters after getting back the boxes from Vegas, I was very relieved to be inside the bunker that houses our base. Seeing the helipad for the first time upon arriving made me smile like I was in love. It’s been a few days since I’ve been back here. Had to go to Vegas to retrieve some boxes that were stolen from one of our warehouses in Vegas. Killed four gang members in the process to do it but it got done. The forest surrounding the base is great for keeping it concealed from anyone who might be looking for it. It looks similar to the base from the movie Avatar. The helicopter I’m riding on swoops down close to the base to the point where I can see it. Butterflies are in my stomach as we get closer and closer to landing. Can’t wait to see what my reward will be for saving the day. The pilot lands the helicopter on the helipad near the entrance to the facility and let me off along with the boxes. Some of the Red Sparrowes Military Wing of the organization came over to get the boxes out of the helicopter. "Hey guys," I say to them as they each grab a single box out of the helicopter. "What the hell’s in these boxes anyways?" "Parts for the HAARP weapons," the first Red Sparrow soldier says to me. At this point I’m about to go on a homicide spree after hearing this. Why in the bloody hell would we be storing important equipment relating to our HAARP machines in a small, unsecured warehouse? It doesn’t make any sense to me. As the helicopter leaves I storm into through the front doors of the facility as I head straight toward the office of Natasha Smirnoff, the financial front of this organization. It’s going to be hard for me to keep my composure when talking to her about this stuff. Why the hell would she not tell me important need to know information like this? I decide to take a walk and come up with a plan in how to deal with this situation without putting a bullet through somebody‘s head. * * * Slam Boomsdexter I 02/23/2010 Just outside of a small village in the Yukon Territory of Canada, the ‘legendary’ Canadian hero Slam Boomsdexter received a phone call from Las Vegas the day before. The call was from a vigilante hero known as Judge. He needs help in facing a mysterious organization and thought that Slam and his sidekick Joey Mason would be of assistance. Judge was right. Slam is his only hope because he is the only person that has seen this kind of thing before. That was when Slam and Joey became a team. Joey was being hunted down by a evil cult in the Yukon woods and Slam was a former member of the cult but was kicked out after he became an alcoholic. Slam and Joey then went on further adventures after destroying the cult in the Yukon woods. Now they are national celebrities in Canada and known as folk heroes throughout the Yukon Territory as well as throughout the other sparsely populated northern Canadian territories. In his usual denim jacket and jeans attire, Slam is getting the Boomsdextermobile (which is code word for really old crappy blue and white Chevy pick-up) ready for the trip but Joey is a bit perplexed as to why they would be leaving for Las Vegas, Nevada of all places. "Slam, why are we going to Vegas?" Joey asks inquisitively. "Because Judge needs help taking down a criminal organization," Slam replies as he puts some oil in the engine. "But why you? Why us?" Joey asks. "Because only Slam Boomsdexter can deal with this situation," Slam responds, seemingly a bit annoyed by the questions but is really trying to remember when the alcohol store closes this evening. "Come on, Joey. The Boomsdextermobile is ready to go." Slam stands back and closes the hood of his beat up old truck. He then goes and gets in the driver’s seat as Joey goes to sit in passenger’s side seat. After they both put their seatbelts on, Slam pulls out his flask from the inside pocket of his jean jacket before taking a swig from it. He then screws the cap back on the flask before he puts it away. "Come on baby, don’t let me down," Slam says as he tries to start to the ignition again. He starts to wonder if he can drink the liquid in the car’s engine from his flask as he again tries to start up the Boomsdextermobile once again. "I won’t, Slam," Joey mutters out. "I was talking to the car," Slam replies as he wonders to himself if there’s beer on the Sun. "It’s a truck," Joey stutters out. "They both do the same thing," Slam replies. "…" Joey starts to say something but decides against it. Slam obviously is drunk and not thinking clearly but that’s normal. He’s been an alcoholic since before Joey met him two years ago when they took down the mysterious evil cult of the woods in the Yukon territory. As Slam starts up the Boomsdextermobile, it makes a sound that almost sounds like the name Boomsdexter. The beat up old truck finally starts up much to the delight of Slam and Joey. Slam smiles as he puts the truck in gear before they speed off down the dirt road that is the only way out of town. Slam is ready for whatever is in store for them. * * * Von Doom II O3/07/2010 A half hour later… I just arrived at the headquarters today only to find out that some parts of the HAARP Weapon were hidden in Las Vegas. This pissed me off to the point where I took some time to simmer down before going into Natasha Smirnoff’s office. I have a plan and I’m going to find out what’s really going on. After the guards allowed me into her office, Natasha looks up from her desk looking as sexy as she always does as naturally I start spouting off like a crazy person which is exactly the opposite of what I had planned. "Why were there parts to our HAARP weapon sitting in a warehouse in Las Vegas instead of being here at the base or at one of our secure warehouses?" I ask her. Natasha gives me a strange look like I’m the one whose nuts. I may have some mental issues, but I’m not an idiot like some of the other members of Civilization are. She must be smart enough to realize this. I’m the only one here other than Dr. Feelgood and her that has any sort of high intelligence level. For her to pull some stupid stunt like this is a serious lapse of judgment. "Why do you want to know that, James?" she asks as she looks at me like I’m the one who is crazy. This only adds to the clear tension in the room. "Because I want to know why in the hell that major parts to our biggest weapon were in simple unmarked boxes. That has disaster written all over it. If some cops look for criminals in one of our storage containers in some random city. If they find boxes full of HAARP weapon parts, then we’re screwed. Do you understand that Natasha?" "Yes, I do," she calmly replies to me. "It’s nothing to be worried about." This response nearly sends me over the edge. I can’t believe I’m hearing this from a supposedly rational thinking human being. Trying so hard to keep myself in control but it’s not going to happen. This is some bullshit and I needs to say something about it. "Nothing to be worried about? Are you insane?" I say in the most sarcastic tone possible just to piss her off. "This is going to be our downfall. You realize that, right? Please tell me you do. We are going to fall apart because of little lapses in judgment like this. If any government agency finds out about what we’re doing, then we’re screwed. It’s as simple as that." "James, calm down," she says to me again in a calming, soothing voice. "We’re going to be more careful in the future with the parts." What the hell is this idiot woman talking about? She’s lost her damn mind with this crap. I feel like I’m working at some small time retail store instead of a massive, global conglomerate with many projects going on worldwide. "More careful in the future? We should’ve been careful from the start. We’ve used the HAARP weapon in Chile, Haiti, Japan, Cuba, Taiwan, Turkey, and in California. We are already well under way of setting up a worldwide weapons system that we can use against all countries and you willingly endangered the operation by housing some of the parts to the machines in unsecured warehouses with minimal security and a spotty surveillance system. This is unacceptable." Judging by her look, she can tell that I’m about to go on a homicide spree over this issue. If she’s thinking that then she’s one hundred percent correct. "Calm down," she says again in a soothing voice. I’ve had it with the pleasantries. Now it’s time to show her that I mean business. Reaching around to the back of my waist, I grab the handgun I concealed to sneak in here and pull it out with my right hand. Cocking the trigger, I point it right at her head. She calmly looks at the gun barrel before putting her hands up. "As you can tell your little Emotion Removal Therapy is regressing. That’s why I am pointing a gun at your head right now. I’m feeling a lot of anger in me. Now what I want to know is why in the fuck would you be so stupid to let something like this happen?" I can’t wait to hear the answer to this question. What she did is the equivalent of leaving four solid gold bars in the middle of a homeless shelter. They were going to get stolen quite easily and that’s exactly what happened. "Had to know I could trust you," she responds. Obviously I’m not believing what I’m hearing from her. Why would she have any reason not to trust me? I’ve been a part of this little organization since the beginning. That alone should give her reason to believe that I’ve got her back, which I do. Something fishy is going on and I’m going to sniff it out. "Whoa, what the hell are you talking about?" I ask her as I‘m obviously out of the loop as to what she is talking about. Judging by her facial expression, it appears to be something very serious. Her eyes show emotion for once instead of the usual macabre deadness that I find so appealing about her. "Two Civilization members have been found dead in the past few weeks and we think that some of our enemies are behind the killings," Natasha tells me as I can only put the safety on the gun, uncock it, and put it back where I had it on my waistband. "I get it," I tell her. "What is there to get?" "That some of the enemies of Civilization are killing our membership and you need somebody to deal with it. What I get is that the person you want to deal with it is me, correct?" "Correct." she replies. This only makes me laugh. She seems taken aback by my genuine show of emotion. "I really get it, Natasha. I’m just your errand boy that deals with every dirty job that comes along. Yeah, that makes me feel good. I’m just your he-bitch man-slave that does all of the dirty little errands that you need done." "James, somebody is killing our members. The only person around here smart enough and qualified enough to do a full blown investigation into these murders. I also need someone I can trust to deal with this. You are that person. Find out whose doing the killings and take care of them. It’s as simple as that." "Good, simple is better than this sneaking around shit." Just as I turn around and am about to leave her office, she drops another bombshell on me. "Oh, and you’re going to have a partner." Stopping right where I’m at, I turn around and raise an eyebrow. "What partner?" She then reaches over to the phone on her desk and picks it up. She presses the button to her out office, waiting a moment for her secretary to answer the call. "Send him in," she says before putting the phone back on the charger. Motioning toward the door, I turn around to see some guy I’ve never seen before walk into the office with us. He has a five o’clock shadow, short hair, is wearing a brown suit, and looks to be of Spanish descent. "This is Juan Martinez from our project in Spain," she says as she motions to him. "He can help you out. He has a background in criminal justice…" "I don’t care," I tell her with a growl. "Save this boy his life. I have a feeling that the shit’s going to hit the fan after all is said and done." "I’ll handle myself," Juan interjects into the conversation. I give him a glance. Not impressed at all by this guy. He looks like the typical suit within in the organization but appearances can be deceiving. "Alright, kid. You handle yourself while I try to figure out what’s going on and keep you from getting killed." I turn and leave the office as my new ‘partner’ scurries behind me to catch up as I head down the hallway toward the building’s exit. This is going to be a long investigation and I can already tell is that this is going * * * Von Doom III 03/10/2010 Another Civilization member has been found dead at our base of operations. This is the third one in two days. So far the victims have been low level operatives in our main facility. Naturally I have to deal with it since apparently I’m Dirty James, the Dirty Harry of Civilization since in get stuck dealing with every dirty job that comes along. Natasha Smirnoff, the money behind the entire organization, is the one sending me on this little investigation. She thinks that one of our enemies is behind the series of murders. I, however, don’t think so. This isn’t the typical modus operandi of our enemies. They usually like to fight from the front which is the opposite of this. To me whomever is doing all of this likes it. Now it’s all a matter of investigation and finding the person or persons responsible for these heinous acts. My partner Juan Martinez is with me too. He hasn’t annoyed me to the point of killing him yet. At the latest crime scene, some of the Unholy Knights are standing guard as me and Juan are looking around the body of the worker for clues. Dr. Feelgood, the resident mad scientist who doubles as both forensic specialist and medical examiner, is there too looking at the crime scene. The body is on the ground with its back facing us. He’s in his Civilization black uniform. Taking a quick look around, the first thing that stands out is no forced entry. If someone snuck onto one of our facilities, we would know about it. Checking the body, I notice multiple stab wounds. Whomever did this is not a professional. They knew he was dead after the first two stab wounds but did the others just for fun. Right I’m leaning against the theory of it being Blue Jay and Cave Hulk that everyone seems to think it is. In fact, I’m starting to think that this is an inside job. However if I said anything about it without proof it would mean a quick death for me. I have to get some evidence. * * * Von Doom IV 03/11/2010 Early afternoon… Juan and I have arrived at the secret hideout of the assassin Blue Jay and my former friend known as Cave Hulk. Naturally it’s in the middle of nowhere in a large forested area in the Smoky Mountains of the Southwestern United States. Places like this are where movies like Wrong Turn and the Hills Have Eyes take place. This made it a pain in the ass to get a good stakeout position. Luckily there’s a US Forest Service station that we’re using for cover that’s maybe two hundred yards from the entrance to Blue Jay’s hideout. Instead of watching them like we were told to, I park the car and immediately get out without telling Juan what’s going on. He quickly gets out of the car to find out what the hell I’m up to which does not surprise me at all. In fact, I planned on him reacting this way just to see if I can trust him. "James, what the hell are you doing?" Juan asks me as I continue to walk toward the hideout. I ignore him and continue walking just to see what the kid does. "They’ll see us!" "That’s what I’m counting on." "But why would you do that?" I finally stop and turn in his direction to address him directly about what my plan is in regards to this situation. "I’m going in there and I’m going to cause a fight," I tell him as he looks at me in disbelief. "Why would you do that?" he asks, giving me the usual ‘you’re crazy, von Doom’ look I always get from people when they think I‘m out of mind. In this case, I’m not. "Because I don’t think they’re behind this." "Are you insane?" "No, I’m not insane. Because if they just fight me and don’t kill me, then I may be right about something that’s been nagging at me." "What are you talking about it?" "If we make it out of this alive, I’ll tell you." "Whatever you say, James. But I still think you’re crazy for even thinking that these two aren’t behind the murders." I lead the way as Juan follows me as we sneak toward the door of the small house. Using the trees as cover, we quickly scamper to the tree that’s closest to the house. Once we’ve got some good cover, I hunch down before sneaking up to the door. Juan’s right on my ass as I do this. Once at the door, I stand up and motion for Juan to follow me in. He nods his head. I move off the wall of the house and stand in front of the door. Right as I go to kick the door open, it opens up on his own and Cave Hulk comes barreling out like bat out of hell! He knocks me over, running straight at me with a duffel bag in one hand and a giant club in the other. Juan quickly comes over to check on me before turning right into a leaping kick from Blue Jay who comes flying out the door! Looking up as they run off, I notice that Blue Jay is armed with a ninja sword and has a big bag with him. I turn to see if Juan is okay. He seems a bit out of it to be honest. Worst case scenario he has a concussion because Blue Jay’s kick nailed him straight in the face. Shit! Almost forgot about Blue Jay and the Cave Hulk. Looking over in the direction they ran off in, both are out of sight. I hear a car start up before it clearly speeds off judging by the noise of screeching tires that echoes in the distance. Smiling, I turn to Juan who is holding his head in pain. He’s giving me a very strange look. Maybe the kick knocked him into the Looney Tunes universe or perhaps he thinks I’m crazy for smiling after what just happened. "So, guess what? We’re still alive," I tell him with a grin. "What does that mean?" "That I’m right." "Right about what?" "Right about them not being behind us. If they wanted to kill us, they’d have killed us right after Hulk bolted out of the door. We’re still alive. That tells me that they are innocent." Juan still has that perplexed look on his face. "Then what the hell does that mean?" "It means that I’m right about something that’s been nagging at me." This makes him raise an eyebrow. A slow breeze comes in from the northwest as I look back at the small house. "What’s been nagging at you James?" "Not now, maybe later. I need evidence to prove it and if I tell you, you’d think I was nuts." "What do you think will be inside of the house when we check it out?" "Nothing. It’ll be clean. They knew we were coming, Juan." "But why would someone tip them off?" "Because they wanted me dead, them dead, or hopefully both. They were tipped off. Someone is behind this and I’m getting too close to figuring out what is really going on." "What should we do?" Juan asks, still scratching his head over the whole scenario. "Go inside and look around. We’ll find nothing, I assure you. It’ll be as clean as the top of a bald man’s head." "Then what?" "We go tell Natasha that there’s a big fucking problem in Civilization." I turn and head inside the small house with Juan stumbling behind me. Just as I predicted, it’s almost barren except for some food on the counter. This is clearly the wrong direction to go in this investigation. Next thing I have to do is call it in to base, have the clean up crew come out, and then head back to base to talk with Natasha alone. She probably won’t like to hear what I have to say but oh fucking well, she’s gonna hear it anyway. Knowing her, she’ll probably have me killed on the spot.
