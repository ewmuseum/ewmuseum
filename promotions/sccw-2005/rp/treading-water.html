Date: Various Sunday, February 28, 2010 I don't even know It's been two and a half years since I've written in here. I got this book out of the vault where I kept it because I thought it would help keep me grounded. Some mistake that turned out to be. I couldn't take it. I couldn't take it, and I cracked not once but fucking twice, and I came so goddamn close this time, and now... There's no manual for this. There's no "Congratulations You Didn't Kill Yourself" card. There's just me, a big empty house, and this. I figure I should probably talk to someone about this, but I'm not sure I want anyone to know. I can't tell Amy, because she has enough going on without all of my bullshit. I'm sure as fuck not telling Darren or Gabe. I can't tell Sebastian because he'll probably want to lock me up again. I'm pretty much fucked here. What the hell do I look forward to now? Friday, April 2, 2010 Japan, AKA 'The Worst Fucking Country on Earth' Seriously, I hate it here. I fucking hate it. Every time I come to this place something bad happens. And not just bad-bad, but monumentally awful. The last time I was here was for a funeral. I stood in the back and hid so the family - a family I was almost a part of - wouldn't know I was around. The time before that? I don't even like to think about. But last night was something new, and I don't think I'm going to forget it any time soon. She made me promise not to call anyone. She asked me if we were okay. Like a thousand times she asked me. And I got sit and watch as she unraveled, and there wasn't a fucking thing I could do about it. I had to be strong, and not let on that I was just as scared as she was, because that's what she needed. And today? Today I've got nothing left. Fuck cocaine. Fuck withdrawal. Fuck Alex fucking Pierce for fucking bringing that cancer back into Amy's life. And fuck this country. Nothing good ever happens here. Ever. Supposed to meet an old friend tonight. Don't really have the energy, but it's been three years, and I'd be a real dick to blow it off now. I hope I never have to come back here again. They stood on the sidewalk, basking in the cool evening air and brilliant neon glow of downtown Tokyo. 'You sure you wanna do this?' He took Amy's hands in his as he turned to face her. 'I mean, I totally understand if you want to head back to the hotel. Izzy can be kind of rough even on the best day. He means well, he's just... Yeah. You can say no it you want.' 'There're people who'd tell you I'm a beast on my best day.' The response came with a genuine, bright smile as one hand gently moved to Jared's cheek. Her eye contact was supposed to be reassuring, but the ice blue-against-brown contrast made even her meekest expression travel a more intense edge. 'And I've had drinks with Lane Stevens - I can handle 'rough' by now.' Her hand slipped down and around to the back of his neck, giving a gentle squeeze to ease the tension there. 'The more you warn me about this guy, the more I'm thinking I stand to like him.' The sudden rise in Jared's eyebrows told her that she was likely very, very wrong about that. Still, Campbell chuckled as she pulled away. 'We'll just play it friendly so he doesn't get to have too much to say, 'kay? Seems like that'll make you feel better. I dunno what you're thinking I'm gonna walk into here, but trust me when I say that it can roll right off my back...' There was a pause and a stifled laugh. 'As long as he's wearing pants. Please tell me he's a fan of pants.' His eyebrows peaked and his mind drifted. He could recount at least a half dozen times when he'd seen Isaiah Gordon without his pants, and none of them were pleasant experiences. The worst of them all involved some photographs that Jared was later assured were destroyed. 'Honestly? Depends on his mood. He's... I guess 'different' is the nicest way I can say it.' A slight breeze sent his already overcharged nerves on edge and a chill ran up his spine. 'He, umm, he might say a few things. Like, tell a few stories. Just take anything he says with a grain of salt.' 'Except now that you've said that, I'm thinking the stories are probably true and you're trying to mindfuck me into thinking he's crazy. You're not the first spy I've bedded, Jared Sykes!' He cringed at the thought. 'Yeah,' he sighed. 'Guess not.' His eyes were left to wander. They took in a group of nearby businessmen engaged in a heated discussion. They drifted to a collection of twenty-somethings in vibrant, brightly-colored clothing across the street. They strained under the glow of the burning neon cityscape. But as loud as the city was, it could not drown out the thought of Alex fucking Pierce. 'So, yeah. Dinner.' As much as she hated Japan's overcrowded cities, Amy wasn't too distracted to pick up on that cringe. 'C'mon,' She urged, 'It was a joke. I need to learn to get better at those, I know. She's gross, and on your mind more than mine. Don't make me start listing the ways you're better in public. There's personality - as in, you have one... the whole 'sanity' thing... living in the real world... being someone I can actually spend time with...' She was interrupted by a boisterous shout from across the street. 'Hey, Sykes, you fruity-ass berry bitch!' 'Oh fuck,' Jared whispered. The man who approached was tall, a little lanky, and grinning like a fool. A mop of dirty blonde hair hung over his eyes to his chin, where the rest of his face was obscured by goatee that hadn't seen a razor in months. Jeans and an untucked, rolled-up dress shirt were the only things that indicated that he was not, in fact, a hobo. Isaiah Gordon bounded between cars as he crossed over four lanes of traffic, ignoring the honking horns and aggravated shouts from the few motorists he passed. He only paused at the last car he passed, taking a moment to offer a wave and a smile at the young woman behind the wheel. 'So how you doin', bud? Still gay?' 'Hey, Izzy.' A handshake turned into a hug, complete with a firm slap on the back, before Gordon quickly ducked back, still grinning. 'Alright, ya' big homo, don't get no ideas here.' His eyes fell on the Red Raver, and his grin widened. 'Why hello there, little lady.' 'Oh, right. This is Amy Campbell. Ames, this is...' 'I know who she is, numbnuts.' Isaiah tipped his head forward, with none of the grace of a true Southern gentleman. 'Name's Isaiah. Some people call me Izzy, or Iggy. A few call me EJ or Eej...' 'A few call him 'Fuckface',' Jared whispered under his breath. Isaiah heard it, it was clear enough when his eyes darted to Jared and back. 'But you can call me whatever you'd like, as long as I get to be on top.' When she closed her eyes just a little longer than a blink would have lasted, Jared could almost hear her thinking 'Don't hit his friends. Don't hit his friends...'. Instead of striking out, Amy extended a hand (that she'd certainly wash a few times before dinner) to Gordon. 'Let's make it Isaiah for now. It's...' Again, Sykes could have sworn he heard her screaming on the inside. 'It's, uhh... Jared's told me a lot about you.' 'Did he now.' Izzy's chuckle sounded a little too much like a hyena for Jared's comfort, and the Black Sheep squirmed when the slightly larger Gordon snaked an arm around his shoulder. 'Well then, I guess it's my turn to return the favor now, ain't it?' * * * * * Train wreck: they were the only words that Jared felt appropriate to describe the experience of doing anything with Isaiah Gordon. There was something about him, an intangible je ne said quoi that made it impossible to look away, even if he did seem to level everything and everyone he came in contact with. And at the rate things were moving, it seemed inevitable that the evening would derail eventually, crushing Jared under the weight of a few tons of steel and one angry redhead. He pushed his chair back away from the table and was about to stand when Isaiah spoke up. 'You know I'm tellin' it just the same whether yer here or not, right?' 'Fuck,' Jared sighed, flopping back down and conceding. 'So anyways,' Gordon continued. Campbell suddenly looked a lot less enthused about hearing the rest of the story. 'Tell me this doesn't involve a hooker...' 'Girl, if there was a hooker involved, the I wouldn't be tellin' this story, would I Spanky?' He leaned on the last word, drawing another sigh from the Black Sheep. 'And believe me, I'd'a rather seen a' hooker.' Isaiah poured himself another glass of sake, and killed the drink in one gulp. Instead of responding, Amy halted her giggles just long enough to say a prayer. 'Please don't say 'a dude'. Please don't say 'a dude'. Please don't--' Izzy slapped his hand down proudly on the table. 'Nobody! Not a soul 'cept himself, and the naked girls on television. Turns out that Ninj' and I walked in on a little 'alone time', if you know what I mean.' 'Aw, dude!' In between the bursts of laughter - and the tears that came when it wouldn't stop - Amy turned to Jared. 'You're a guy! Isn't that what the shower's for?!' 'I was... it...' Jared's face had turned a bright shade of red, and a bead of sweat was visible on his brow. He leaned forward in his chair, resting his elbows on the table and trying to press the palms of his hands clear through his eyes. 'Man, I hate you sometimes.' Gordon pretended not to hear, instead turning his attention back to Campbell. 'You wanna hear another? I've got, like, dozens of 'em.' 'I think I'll pass, he seems like he's gonna suicide. Oh, c'mon.' Amy snaked an arm around his back, planting a peck on Sykes' neck. It was the first display of affection she'd given in front of Isaiah - though the man likely wasn't too blind to have already caught on. 'It's a funny story, and actually the best way it could've ended if you still wanted to get lucky ever again. I'm not cool sleeping with men who bang hookers or guys.' She snickered, 'Well, I'd even overlook guys in some cases.' Isaiah simply nodded. 'Kinky.' * * * * * Mercifully for Jared, dinner had ended, and, in a rare stroke of generosity, Isaiah had been kind enough to pick up the check. 'You can get the next one in three years,' he'd joked. Jared wasn't sure that another three years would be quite long enough. The trio stood in the lobby, decorated with elements from the Tokugawa period. Nervous and eager to run, Jared shuffled on his feet, shifting weight from one to the other and then back again. Isaiah's grin had brightened in contrast to the redness of his cheeks, an unfortunate side-effect of too much rice wine. 'Blast as always, dude.' Jared feigned a smile. The last bits of his pride had been shattered during the first round of after-dinner cocktails, when Gordon regaled the table with a story of the time he and the Ninja Lawyer had cut the crotch out of all Jared's wrestling gear. 'Yer a terrible liar, you know that?' Gordon chuckled. 'Nah, ya' know I'm only fuckin' with ya'.' This time the handshake stayed just that. 'Hey, you mind if I get a word with yer ladyfriend for a minute?' he continued. Jared cast a hesitant glance towards Amy. In truth she was more than a match for Iggy should things get out of hand, he was sure of that much. Still, the thought of leaving her alone with him turned his stomach. Can you get me a bottle of water from that shop next door or something to spare me the embarrassment of having to try sorting yen again?' 'Yeah. Yeah, sure.' He wasn't even out the door before the instinct to turn back took over. He cast a quick glance back over his shoulder, but a reassuring nod sent him on his way. The last thought Jared enjoyed before stepping through the door was of Gordon bloody on the floor, flailing, and pinned under an angry redhead. The door to the restaurant swung shut, and that's when Isaiah opened his mouth again. 'So, Amy Campbell, looks like the rumors I been hearin' are true after all.' 'You could've just asked him on the phone or something. I mean, I don't think he was hiding that we were together from you or anything... so I wouldn't worry about it too much if I were you.' Isaiah grinned, but when he spoke again the playful tone, the sense of mischief, it was as if it had all evaporated. 'Ain't me I'm worried about. Ain't really you either. Tuesday, May 18, 2010 Home, in bed, and wired I keep having this recurring dream, and it’s starting to freak me out a little. So in this dream I’m wearing a white jumpsuit, like what you’d expect to see in prison, and there are shackles at my hands and feet. I’m being led down a corridor by two men, one on either side, and they’re both in solid black uniforms. No names. No numbers. Nothing to really identify anyone. The hall itself isn’t very spectacular. Kind of reminds me of high school. The walls are just cinder blocks painted white, someone installed the worlds longest drop ceiling, there’s a few fluorescent lights (I’m guessing, I don’t really know), and then the floor itself is speckled tile. It’s pretty cold on my feet, but apparently whoever it is that has me decided that I don’t need shoes. The guards or whatever, they have shoes - shoes with hard leather soles that echo off the tile. I love that sound. I don’t really know why, but listening to the sound of my own footsteps kinda makes me feel important - like I matter. Anyway, the hall stretches on for what feels like forever. 'Where are we going?' I always ask them, and the answer is always the same. 'Today you get your freedom,' is what they tell me. I’m always happy when they say that. Always. It’s like the world is suddenly lifted off my shoulders. I don’t know how long the corridor is, and there’s no clear ending in sight, but the destination is worth the effort. I smile – I always smile, and keep walking. Sometimes I mutter to myself, wondering out loud what it’ll be like to be free, but the guards never say anything else. At least, not until we get to the end. The rest of the walk drags on endlessly, it seems. I don’t know if that’s actually the case, and that it takes hours of dream-time, because I remember once hearing that entire dreams are only seconds long, and dream-time and real-time aren’t the same thing. At first I’m hesitant, so I look to each one of the guards for some comfort. No faces on either of them. No eyes, no mouths, not even skin. They’re just empty. The dream always ends the same way. I step through the door, and tears start to fall from my eyes. That’s when it happens. Cold steel meets the back of my neck, and the last thing I hear is the sound of a gunshot.
