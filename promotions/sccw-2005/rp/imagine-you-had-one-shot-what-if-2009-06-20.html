Imagine You Had One Shot... (What IF?)

20 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/6/09 If There Was A Single Day I Could Have, I Trade All The Others Away- Randy Blythe When we last left our intrepid ‘Rejects’ deals had been made through Luci and Savant for a meeting sometime after the Tempe show, we now catch up with the two rejects in a car, Legion at the wheel… Legion: Which way did Lauren tell you to go again? Left at the next junction right? Luci: I said RIGHT! This is why I wanted to do this on my own, you were the one that insisted to come with me to this ‘meeting’. Legion: Luce, what Alex interprets her ‘meetings’ as *I* interpret them as well an actual meeting so I feel it’s only fair I get involved here, if it wasn’t for me… Luci: If it wasn’t for me what? (Legion realises he was about to give his game away…) Legion: Nothing, I’ll explain later… (under breath) much later! Where the hell are we? Luci: A place where it seems there’s hardly any civilisation… just great Gabe, just GODDAMN great. You’ve made us possibly miss a very important part of our fight against Alex… I’m going to give her a call, you stay there! (Yes folks, when it comes to matters of Savant Legion, at least as Luci’s aware, has let Luci pretty much take control…. But it raises a question.) Mysterious figure: That’s where I come in… I am the Wat- We then hear a voice (You can’t use that… do we want ANOTHER lawsuit on our hands or not… go with something else and NOT grandmaster!) Mysterious Voice: Fine! I am the Chronicler (‘Better!) and I traverse space and time to answer the questions What If, in the case of our two ‘rejects’ what would have happened if Legion had joined the DMH at Homecoming? Some things still remain constant in the world so don’t worry the Amy/Desade ‘affair’ will continue (we hear cheers from the small voice) in this world… Let us now look into the mists of time… (We go through a mix of colours and shapes as we arrive at Salt Lake City.) Legion: So to the Dead Man’s Hand I say… DEAL! Gibson: Damn it, they snagged another one! How many more will Desade ensare into her web? Ware: I think it was down to Savant and Luci this time Gibbo and that means more possible HLA! (Ware tries to get up and applaud mixed with a dance but fails by falling over onto the floor) After the show in a van in the parking lot Legion: So this is the ‘initiation’ huh? I get the Hawk rather than the organ grinder or is she in a celebratory mood with the champ? Mr. Hawke: Small steps, Gabriel. Small steps. Just because you signed the contract doesn't mean you get to see the president. Legion: I joined you guys for a reason ok? For Luci’s sake with her infatuation with Lauren mostly. Sure, there’s a part of me that wants some power growth that possibly only Desade can provide but things need to be done, Savant finding Luci a good mental and physical trainer in the ring will strengthen the resolve and besides Black Ops could always use a new female in the ranks right? (We then hear Roderick Ashe’s voice from the backseat of the van.) Mr. Ashe: Lemme at 'er, 'cuz I gotta make sure she's a real woman undah that. Pretty sure, but there's only one way to test. Legion scowls at the lecherous Ashe but then shows a slight grin as he turns round… Legion: It makes a change to have a real woman in the back with you than the action figure if she did! Mr. Ashe: 'Ey, at least I'm getting somethin' from someone, Gabey. You can't fuck a history book. Legion: Well on that note, welcome the Knight to the game. Let the storm ride along with it. (We then see time flash to a few weeks later, Desade has disbanded the Dead Man’s Hand, we see Luci and Legion in a hallway) Luci: Now what are we going to do? You spoke to Hawke a few weeks ago right and now Savant wants me to stand up against Alex… Legion: We’ll stick with the plan, it’s time to let the connections I’m making slowly gain some speed. Luci; You mean? Legion: Yes. I need you to ask Savant about her life outside the ring, in fact immerse yourself into it… if push comes to shove ( we see time flash again) (We cut to sometime in the future of this world… Legion being bludgeoned with a chair, the camera angle not showing the attacker until the last minute) ‘What I said about the attack down the line has to be done’ (The attacker turns out to be Luci.) (We then see even quicker glimpses of time involving members of Desade’s group including Legion Vs the Resistance led by Kennedy. It all culminates with a red laser sight landing on a woman’s body in shadow…) Chronicler: The mists get hazy after that… I’m sure by now in the real world everything’s sorted (we glimpse back into where we started, Luci’s mouthing that she’ll meet us at the Mexico show… they then drive off to the airport…)
