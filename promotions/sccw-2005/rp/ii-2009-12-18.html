Date: 12/18/2009 "And you judges who would be just, What judgment pronounce you upon him who though honest in the flesh yet is a thief in spirit? What penalty lay you upon him who slays in the flesh yet is himself slain in the spirit?" - Kahlil Gibran, On Crime and Punishment II 12/13/2008. Las Vegas is degenerating into a state of anarchy. The city is wild just like in the days of old, before the humans colonized this area. Back then the desert that covered this area was wild and the animals roamed free. Now the people here are like the wild animals of old. They fight for little small patches of territory their gangs own or little scraps of food or paper money. This has all gotten worse since the economic meltdown which is now a months long recession. People are losing more and more and all the while getting more desperate. This isn’t helping matters when it comes to the nightfall when I make my rounds. The more I think I’m getting saner the more people are getting insane. These are dark times for the city that I love. No matter who you vote for, it’ll either be Soylent Green or Blade Runner in twenty years. Things are at a breaking point. It’s about time somebody decided to take out the trash of this city. And I’m just to guy for the job. This city is filled with scum. They need to be judged for their crimes. Crimes are either punishable by capture or death. I will sentence the criminals in proportion for their crime. Their captures or executions will be a reminder to the rest of the scum infesting this city that if they commit crimes, they will judged… JUDGE * * * Early evening, 12/14/2008... Night, when the city is at it’s darkest. And these have been dark days for Las Vegas. Vigilantes, criminals, roving gangs, and corrupt politicians and business people have taken over this once quiet city. Now it is complete chaos. There was another shooting in a casino this evening as well as three more drive by shootings as well as a string of robberies. There’s also been a series of serial killings tied to a big Native American man dressed in a Pilgrim outfit. Authorities say that he "harvests" the bodies of his victims. As the sun fades into the horizon and the darkness starts to take over the sky, three warmly dressed men in sweats are walking toward their car from a liquor store where they have just purchased a large amount of alcohol. They are walking toward their car with their purchase continuing their talk about something one of them read about in the newspaper. "You hear about that guy in the Pilgrim suit who kills people?" the first man asks. "No," the second man says. "Never heard of him. "The news says he is a Indian," the first man continues. "They say he harvests his victims." "What do you mean?" the third man asks. The men turn toward their vehicle and see a large man hidden by the shadows standing next to it. The three men stop walking and stare at the man. The large man is wearing some sort of strange costume. It looks like something from the seventeen hundreds. The men look on as the man smiles slightly but then a scowl comes across his face. He pulls out two hatchets he was hiding behind himself and throws one of them. It nails the first man in the head! He falls backward and hits the ground. The glass bottle he is holding smashes against the ground. After seeing this, the two other men are frozen with shock as the large man comes out of the shadows to make his move. He is wearing a brown colored Pilgrim outfit. The two men look at each and gulp, realizing who the large man is as he throws the second hatchet at them. He watches on as it strikes both men and smiles when he hears the thud of their bodies hit the ground. Harvesting time is here and now he has three more bodies to harvest for the winter. * * * Approximately 10:30 p.m. PST Outside of the PT’s Pub off of Sahara, two men are running toward their getaway vehicle after robbing the pub and everyone there of their money. The two men are African Americans and both are wearing black shirts with blue jeans and both are wearing . The one on the left is slightly taller than the man on the right. They run toward the vehicle and the first man opens the passenger side door to the car, a Red Toyota Corolla. After jumping in, the second robber opens the back passenger side door. Just as he is about to get in, someone grabs his arm! "Wha--" the robber says as he turns around and is shocked to see that the black-clad masked vigilante Judge is the person who has grabbed him. His face turns from a look of bewilderment to a look of disbelief in a matter of seconds. Judge’s all black outfit with a big gray J on the front, black gloved hands, and modified ski mask face covering aren’t helping matters. "YOU!" he exclaims in a shocked manner. Judge smiles at him under his mask before punching the robber directly in the face! The robber hits the back of his head on the car and falls to the ground. Blood pours from his nose, which is likely broken, as the other robber pulls out his gun. He fires a round through the window but it misses the mark. The robber then rolls down the window to try and see where Judge went to. This allows Judge to pull him out the window and throw him hard onto the ground! The robber drops his weapon which Judge kicks away before punching the robber in the face repeatedly. After he feels the robber is out of it enough, he goes to get the driver of the get-away car but the car starts to speed off! Judge looks over and sees the hand gun lying on the ground. The car starts to pull into the street right when Judge starts firing his newly acquired at a hand gun at the speeding car! Five shots are fired until one shot hits the driver in the head. Judge watches as the Toyota Corolla turns at a high rate of speed before hitting the sidewalk and flying into a streetlight. Upon hitting the streetlight, the car bursts into flames. As the flames flicker in the bitter cold of the Vegas night air, Judge watches with a sense of glee that one might expect from one of the psycho killer criminals that he catches. He stares at the car for a few moments but stops when he hears coughing and rustling on the ground nearby. He turns around to see the two robbers who ran out of the pub are starting to come to. He quickly goes over and ties their hands together with PlastiCuffs before they get awake. A few moments later, when they come to, they are greeted by the imposing figure of the Judge pacing back and forth. "I think it’s time for you two to be judged," he says with demented glee as he reloads the gun with a new clip that he took from the pocket of the robber that tried to shoot him earlier. "You," Judge says as he points the gun at the first robber’s head. He cocks the hand gun and readies to fire the weapon. "You are sentenced to jail time," he says before pointing the gun in the air as the robber breathes a sight of relief. "The cops will be here within three minutes to pick you up." Judge turns and starts to walk away much to the dismay of the two robbers. The second robber is pissed over being left tied up on the ground as a matter of fact and isn’t afraid to voice his opinion on the matter. He’s also a bit bewildered over not being judged like his accomplice. "HEY!" the second robber yells out in an angry fashion. "What about me?" Judge turns around and fires a bullet from his hand gun right at the second robber’s head! The headshot kills the robber instantly. His body flops over beside the first robber and lies limply on the ground. "You are sentenced to death," Judge replies with a scowl as smoke comes out of the barrel. "For my attempted murder." Judge then turns around and walks toward a parked car waiting nearby. The car is a black Toyota Supra that has been modified for street racing purposes. One thing that is noticeable about the car is that it has no license plates. Judge walks over and climbs into the driver’s side door. Once inside he closes the door and turns on the ignition. The car motor revs up loudly before quickly speeding toward exit to the street, which is Sahara. He then turns onto Sahara before speeding past the burning wreckage of the get away car that crashed into the stoplight. The black Supra then turns at the next stop light which is Decatur and speeds as fast as it can down the street before going out of view just as the police arrive at the crime scene to find the first robber weeping over the deaths of his two accomplices. * * * 12/15/2008 The following morning the city is shadowed by rain clouds hovering overhead. Soon the rain starts pouring down like teardrops from Heaven saddened over the state of affairs in Vegas. Some areas got snow, mostly in the far western portion of the city known as the Elite Empire where an elitist attitude and the people from the highest socio-economic status live. The main portion of the city is currently getting drenched with rain. Amazing the chaos a little bit of water can cause. This is what’s happening currently in the city that Ash loves. He’s lounging around his apartment, sitting on the couch watching the news as his best friend and agent Todd arrives wearing a great looking two piece suit. Todd closes the door behind him before walking over to sit down on the couch next to Ash. He looks at the newscast that Ash is watching and sees the news report about Judge’s antics the night before. "Wow, two deaths caused by that maniac last night," Todd says, shaking his head. "Not surprising. We allow vigilantes to take over this town and all of a sudden it’s anarchy." Ash looks at him, a bit intrigued by his thoughts on what has happened. "Well they just showed that the serial killer Pilgrim killed three more people last night," he replies. "And besides, Judge is just a citizen who’s had enough of the crime and corruption that has taken over this city. Violent gangs and home invaders are all over the place. The corporations are just as bad, thinking they control everything. They’re just as bad as the gangs. This Pilgrim guy maybe the worst of the lot." "True," Todd replies. "But there’s also Rick Roll running around robbing people as well." Ash nods his head in agreement before noticeably yawning loudly. "You tired or something, buddy?" Todd asks, a bit concerned. "Have a long night?" Ash nods his head and points to his newest sculpture in the back of the room. "I was working on that till three a.m. before I fell asleep on the couch," he responds before stretching as Todd goes back to their original conversation at this point. "You really think this town has reached its breaking point?" he asks as Ash nods his head to answer said question. "Yes," Ash replies." No matter who you vote for, it’ll either be Soylent Green or Blade Runner in twenty years. This town is a cesspool and is in a state of anarchy. Sometimes in order to combat anarchy, only more chaos is the only way to regain order." Todd looks at him in disbelief as Ash turns back to the news, watching intently as it continues to be broadcast.
