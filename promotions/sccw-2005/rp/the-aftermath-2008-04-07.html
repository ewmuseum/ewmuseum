The aftermath.

7 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/04/08 "Your buddy, when we catch him is going straight off to Guantanamo, you are not far behind if you don’t start co-operating you little jackass" Those pissed off sounds are coming from a very angry investigator for the American military. Today he hasn’t had the chance to shout at anyone yet, his wife was not at home when he woke up, his son had already left and his dog had gone. Then not to mention that last night two terrorists got into Miramar air force base, and by sheer luck didn’t manage to blow it and all of the stationed marines. Yes, this fella was nasty work and he is going to be hard to break. He had only just walked through the door to see a man grinning vacantly as he looks at the mirror window in front of the room, he looks like he would wave if his arms weren’t strapped. Yes, this man was going to be tough to break. "Jackass? Me? All I did was try and blow up an air force base, you invaded a country for oil!" Tricky indeed, he got him with that one, nevertheless, no one tries to blow up America, not on the watch of Major Dwight T. Theribald. He picks up the file in front of him, on the solitary table in the room. Andre Jensen, professional wrestler, rich beyond compare, Sin champion as of this weekend. Clever bastard, manages to get a good profile while managing to be successful, money probably comes from drugs. "What the united States of America do is bring democracy to the world you little shit. Now tell me what I want to know!" AJ blankly looks at him in a mild sort of comprehension, then shrugs. "Are you refusing to tell me what I want to know? I have been authorised to do anything, and I mean anything to get to know what you know." The Major gets up close to AJ’s face, anger and hatred in his eyes. AJ smiles back at his captor, in more a confusing way than anything. "You haven’t asked me anything" "What?" comes the roar from the major, "did you not hear anything Sergeant Bryant was asking you in the past 4 hours?" "Oh, he was asking me stuff? I was too busy thinking about what happens when I go back to work and everyone sees this gold around my waist." "So you didn’t hear a thing?" "Nope" The Major sighs. Tricky, tricky indeed. Play his game, keep him sweet, then he will talk. "Ok Andre, can I call you Andre?" "If you want, people call me AJ usually." "Ok, AJ then." "Suits me," "Tell me everything, from the beginning." "Everything?" "Everything." "You sure?" "I’m sure" AJ shrugs, "Ok, you comfortable?" "Comfortable?" "Yeah, it might take a while." "A while?" "Yeah." "ok, well, begin." He sits on a chair opposite AJ, looking at him intensely as AJ takes a deep breath. "Ok, well it began for me at birth, like it does everyone else. I was born to my parents in England and………" "Whoa, whoa what are you talking about?" "You asked me to go from the start." "I didn’t mean like that! Are you some sort of retard or something?" AJ shrugs again and smiles at the major. His eyes twinkle with mischief. "What I meant to say was, tell me everything about the Taliban, the bombing attempt and the accomplice of yours called Sameer Al-Jezuri." "Ah, that." "Yeah, that." "What do you want to know?" "Everything you know." "What do I get for it?" The major now laughs, the audacity of this kid, he is in deep deep shit and he is asking what he gets for it? If it were up to him, this kid would be strapped to a rocket and used as part of a missile finding Bin Laden. "What do you get? You are lucky the military didn’t kill you when they found you in the desert, the only reason your sorry ass is alive is because we need information you have. You get to live, and if you are very helpful you might even get to live in minimum security." "Minimum security eh? You do realise I have more money than God right?" "And?" "The way I see it is this, I can give you all the information you need right here right now, I can give you a million dollars right here right now, if I do both of them, I am thinking that maybe, just maybe I can avoid prison. If not you are putting a SIN champion out of action, and I really don’t think my boss would be much pleased." "Your boss? You tried to blow up an army base, you actively engage in terrorist activities, and you think I should be worried about your boss? He should be worried about me. I cant believe he allows you to run around doing what you want to do on that show!" "What can I say? He likes me." "Well, I don’t. are you going to tell me everything now or do you want to see me angry?" AJ still hasn’t stopped smiling, all throughout the exchange he has his usual vacant smile fixed on the major, a permanent charm offensive. "2 million, and the whereabouts of bin laden, I will do some community service or something though." "Why are you still negotiating?" "3 million" silence. "Ok, 10 million, Bin Laden is in Missouri." "Say what now?" "I just said, Bin Laden is in Missouri, oh and I can write you a cheque now if you want. I need to let my arms free up though." The major now is a bit more hesitant. This guy is offering him 10m plus he has already told him where bin Laden is. If this works out he can be rich, rich and famous. Loads of money and the guy who captured Bin Laden! He can already see the lights, the green, the fortune. In some sort of trance he manages to untie AJ and sign his release papers, signing him up for a week of community service. In his trance he also receives a cheque for a cool 10 million. With thanks from AJ. AJ smiles and pats the Major on the shoulder. "Good man, good man. I am going to have to split now, I got a party to go to, we are champs you know!" With a nod the Major looks at his check, a 1 and 7 zeroes. He doesn’t care where AJ goes at this point. AJ leaves with a smile on his face and without a care in the world. All he has to do now is find his damn partner, and hope he doesn’t want to kill him when he finally finds him.
