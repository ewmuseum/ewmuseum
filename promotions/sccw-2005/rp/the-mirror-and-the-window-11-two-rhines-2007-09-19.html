The Mirror and The Window 11: Two Rhines

19 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/07 11.1 the mirror: two Rhines There are two Jonathan Rhines at the moment; you have made it quite clear in your head before that this must be the only explanation for all of the conflict you have felt ever since you signed your name on a FUSE contract. There is Jonathan Rhine that everyone knows as the hottest man in FUSE, the man leading the Highwaymen straight at The Affliction, the man who has yet to have his shoulders pinned to any canvas for longer than two seconds. And there is the Jonathan Rhine who spends his time away from wrestling hiding in the shadows of a double life, living with one woman and thinking of another. You aren’t really sure about the mechanics of the situation, but you do know that the two lives aren’t completely linked to each other. For instance, the fans don’t see you as that man who is afraid to tell his girlfriend that he cheated on her. They see you as the man who doesn’t back down, the man who is going to bring wrestling back to its former glory. Comparatively, you aren’t that shining star in the small suburbs of Indianapolis. No one in the community sees you as a major player in one of wrestling’s hottest stages. They see you as the neighbor who doesn’t talk much, who takes the trash out on time and always makes sure his lawn is spotless. You’ve talked about the division to Sarah before, but you have downplayed it, as if it was only a slight distraction in your life. After War In Washington, though, there is no mistaking that there are, in fact, two Jonathan Rhines. One is ready to take on Clinton Sage and show the world that the evils of the wrestling world will not survive… And the other is looking at your girlfriend with horror as she holds a small piece of paper in her hand and starts yelling. 11.2 the window: embarrassed "Now, the question I’m about to ask you is one of those questions you should think long and hard about before answering," Katie Malick says, eyeing her boyfriend with contemplative anger. Jonathan Rhine freezes, looking at her and waiting for the bomb to drop. And it does, as the paper is thrust in his face. "WHO THE FUCK IS SARAH?" she asks, and Jonathan grabs the paper and looks at it for a second, then sighs. Katie takes this as an admission of guilt. "Well?" Jonathan looks up at Katie, his eyes filled with sincerity. "I’m sorry I hid this from you. She…" "YOU’RE SLEEPING WITH HER!" Katie shouts with the anger and fury only a scorned and paranoid woman can produce. She tries to slap the wrestler, but he instinctively blocks her. Dropping her hands, he calmly answers. "She’s...she’s my therapist." This stops the ball of womanly fury, who simply looks at Jonathan, confused. "Therapist?" Jonathan nods and walks away from her, sitting on the couch. After a second, Katie walks over and puts her arms on his shoulders, sighing as she leans forward. "Why didn’t you tell me you were seeing a therapist?" Jonathan’s shame shows on his face ironically. "I just…I guess I was sort of embarrassed." "You don’t have to be embarrassed," she says, and she moves onto the couch with him, then hugs him. "Everyone has problems. Just because you’re a big macho man doesn’t mean you don’t have everyday issues you need to talk about. There’s nothing wrong with seeing this woman, Jon." Jonathan turns the other way to hide his face from her, because he can’t control the red seeping into it. "I guess not." Katie sighs again. "I’m sorry for going off. I just got really worried when I saw a woman’s name, you know? I’m just really relieved that she’s your therapist and not someone you’re sleeping with." Jonathan remains silent. 11.3 the mirror: lie machine It was just one lie, you told yourself. When you told Katie that you were going to the all-night gym instead of telling her the truth about Sarah, it was supposed to be just one lie that didn’t escalate, that wouldn’t harm anyone. And now you’re a regular lie machine. You aren’t sure why you didn’t just tell her you were seeing a therapist anyway. It wasn’t something you had to lie about, but the moment you saw Sarah Riley and felt an attraction to her, you felt it was best to not mention it. As if bringing attention to it would make the situation more real. Well it’s as real as it can get now. It all goes back to this double life you’ve created for yourself. As much as you want to avoid it, it keeps coming back. One Jonathan Rhine fights the good fight and has given thousands of wrestling fans hope for their sport. The other cheats on and lies to the most important person in his life. 11.4 the window: great life "I’m really glad we can talk about this kind of stuff," Katie is saying to a somewhat distracted Jonathan. "What? Oh, yeah. I agree." Not noticing that he probably missed most of that, Katie continues. "I know you have a lot of problems with spending one day a week being a wrestler and the rest of it being just normal, everyday Jonathan Rhine, but I was thinking…" Jonathan looks up suddenly at her, afraid she is about to ask him a question he won’t be able to answer. "…maybe I could come to the shows? I mean, they wanted me to be your ‘valet’ or whatever, right? Maybe I could give that a shot." Jonathan’s shock is readily apparent as he slightly tilts his head to the side. "Really? You want to come to the shows? Be a part of them?" Katie shrugs. "Why not? I don’t have a job and school’s almost over. I don’t think anyone’s going to be banging down the doors for a History major. I figure this way I can be with you more often, and I can get paid for it, too." Jonathan doesn’t say anything for a few moments. Katie takes this as a bad sign. "You don’t want me to do it anymore, do you?" "It’s not that," says Jonathan, grabbing her hands. "It’s just that the stakes are raised, now. Before when I wanted you to be my manager, I didn’t have an army of ruthless people breathing down my neck. I just…I just don’t want you to get hurt." Once again he is met with a shrug. "I’ll be fine. I can defend myself pretty well, and if it comes down to it I’m sure my knight in shining armor will protect me." She giggles, but Jonathan doesn’t share the amusement. "It’ll be okay, Jon. This will be good for us. I mean, we’ve had such problems over communicating because I don’t understand what you love about your job. Give me a chance to understand, okay?" Jonathan sighs, then nods. "Okay. I’ll get my agent to call Mr. Duluth. We’ll probably be able to get you onto the next show." She smiles, clapping her hands. "Great! This is what our relationship is about: sharing each other’s loves. You quiz me on history tests, I watch as you beat up buffoons. It’s a great life." She smiles widely, hoping her sarcasm lightens Jonathan’s seemingly foul mood. It doesn’t. "I’ve got to go," he says, standing up and moving her arm. She looks up at him, confused and a little hurt. "What?" "I’ve got to go," he repeats. He looks at her for a moment, his eyes trying to express some of the remorse he feels for leaving. It doesn’t work. "I’ll be back later. I just need some time to think about stuff." He closes his eyes as he leaves the room, and Katie hugs the pillow tightly, pushing back tears. 11.5 the mirror: two girls You feel terrible about leaving her there. You feel terrible for lying to her. You feel terrible for continuing to drive a distance between you and her. But you don’t think you can stop doing it. She was right. You had wanted her to come with you to ringside ever since you joined FUSE, and for awhile you were actually insulted that she wouldn’t. You felt that she wasn’t showing respect to the business that you loved, and consequently she wasn’t respecting you. But now you don’t know how you feel about it. It’s not just because of the Highwaymen either. You are worried that Sage or Stevens might pull something, but you’re pretty confident in your abilities to stop them. What really worries you is that you are beginning to get more and more uncomfortable spending time with your girlfriend. And now that she’ll be following you everywhere you go, essentially, there’s more time to be uncomfortable. More time to sit right next to her, knowing you’re a liar and a cheater. More time to see her excited to be with you, imagining how hurt she would be if she found it. More time to be reminded that you are more than FUSE’s favorite person, and that the person you are isn’t someone you can be proud of. More time to look directly into her eyes…and see Sarah’s. That’s why you’ve been working out so much since before War in Washington. That’s why you’ve gained weight. That’s why your body is more sore than usual. Because you can’t go home without looking at your girlfriend and seeing the face of the girl you cheated on her with. There are two Jonathan Rhines, and two girls. The matchups are pretty elementary, you think. 11.6 the window: conflicted "Hey," comes Sarah Riley’s weak greeting as she lets Jonathan into her home. He walks to their usual meeting spot in her living room and stops before sitting, looking around at the misplaced glasses, dishes, and general clutter everywhere. "Sorry," she says, coming up from behind him and making sure to give the New Life a lot of space. "I haven’t really done much lately." Jonathan sits down, moving aside a large pile of clothes. "Are you feeling okay?" She sits down on the couch opposite of him, making no attempts to hide her sad smile. "That’s not why you’re here. We don’t talk about my problems, Jon. The roles don’t reverse." "But they can," he says. "It’s not like you’re inhuman or anything. You’re a person just as much as I am and just as much privileged to talking about how you feel." She nods, and a tear drops down her face. "Okay. You’re right." She sighs. "You want to know how I feel?" "Yes." She looks down at her feet, putting her hands on her lap. "I’ve been doing this for so many years, Jonathan. Ever since I got my degree and found out that therapists who aren’t doctors don’t do much business. I’ve been finding football players, baseball players, coaches and trainers, so many different people to understand and come close to. I offered them both services, but no one was interested. They all wanted my body, not my advice." She sighs again. "And with every one, no matter how good looking, how wealthy, how popularly desirable, I kept at least some manner of professionalism. I made sure I had at least one real session with all of them. Some of them came back for more sessions, but most, including Foster, only did it once and left. They didn’t want to explore their mind at all. They just wanted something they knew they could get." Jonathan frowns. "I’m sorry about Fos…" "No," she cuts him off. "It was okay. It is okay. Like I said, I never got attached. This is my job. I’ve been called a whore, and I most certainly have the qualifications for that position. And if we go by that, the ultimate rule is that we never ever fall for someone we’re working with. Never mix that professionalism with personal feelings." "Don’t like the wrestler," Jonathan concludes. "Exactly. But I couldn’t help it. Everyone else I’ve ever dealt with had this air of arrogance, of superiority, of general bad attitude that subjected everyone and everything around it. Everyone, including your mentor, treated me like some piece of ass who they could laugh at when she tried to take herself seriously. And for a long time I was content with it, because I convinced myself they were only there for providing me with money anyway." She looks up, and the tears are now out in force. "But then you came. "You were interested in me professionally, because you had never come to grips with anything in your life. I knew right away that you had lived your entire life not questioning anything that happened to you, and it was all building up once you started wrestling professionally. I knew that because of that reason, you valued my assistance. "And then you rejected my advances," she says, smiling at the thought. "That just made me want you more." "I didn’t want to do anything to hurt my girlfriend. I was really attracted to you, but I really wanted to avoid that and just come to you for advice because I felt that it was helping both of us," is all Jonathan can reply. She smiles wider. "You’re sweet. And you’re right. It did help me. The fact that you wanted me more for my position than for on a sexual level made me try much harder to use what I learned in school, to understand you so I could help you ever better. And then the lines between wanting to help you and wanting to be with you sort of blurred together, and then we…" she shrugs, looking contemplative. "…you know the rest." Now Jonathan is the one looking down at his feet. "Yeah, I do." Sarah reaches across the table and touches Jonathan’s knee. "I’m so sorry that it happened. I never wanted to do anything to you or your girlfriend, and I can’t imagine all the trouble I caused. I didn’t want to do this anymore because I didn’t want to make things more complicated for you. "But I was excited when you called. And even now, I can’t look at you without wanting to do it all over again. I guess…I guess I’m just conflicted." She sighs once more. "I’m conflicted too," says Jonathan. Despite trying not to, Sarah Riley can’t stop the smile from her face. "I could tell." Jonathan leans forward a little bit. "I don’t want to ever hurt my girlfriend, and I would have never thought I’d be a cheater, but…I can’t stop thinking about you. And I can’t stop seeing you in any capacity." She nods, grabbing his hand. He quickly squeezes it. "So," she says, "what do we do now?" Jonathan closes his eyes and squeezes her hand once again, but gives no answer. "What do we do now?" she repeats. 11.7 the mirror: counter-transference You have no fucking clue. You know the complications. You know what is at stake. You know what you want. But you don’t really. Because no matter how many times you tell yourself on the drive over that this is the last time you’re going to see her, somehow Sarah Riley ends the night by scheduling another appointment. You didn’t do anything with her tonight, but you all but opened the calendar for future happenings. You feel the remorse. You yearn for this all to be washed away. But you can’t help feeling that there is something real with Sarah, no matter how the rest of the world would view it. Adulterous? Definitely. Morally corrupt? Without a doubt. But fake? Not at all. Sarah Riley showed emotion to you tonight, and it made you feel for her even more. There has been more than a simple patient/therapist transference; there has been counter-transference, and back again. She is your therapist, she is forbidden, she is off-limits… But she is now in your head more than your girlfriend is. There are two Jonathan Rhines, and you hate the one that’s taking over right now.
