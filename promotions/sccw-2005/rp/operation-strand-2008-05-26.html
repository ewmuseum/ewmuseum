Operation Strand

26 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/08 It is a typical Monday morning in Indianapolis: Don Stuck is conducting his daily radio show, the traffic is just starting to slack off, and Tavon Bishop and Sarah Riley are arguing. "But there are risks involved," Sarah Riley is saying reservedly as Bishop swings a wooden baseball bat lazily. "Every task that Ms. Pierce assigns has a lot of dangers that could lead to…" "Sarah," the big man says, throwing down the towel he was using to wipe off sweat after a long workout. "I ain’t a rookie. I injured a wrestler on my first night in FUSE. Now I know that we’re talking about the big, bad, former champ, but we’re also talking about the soon to be Livewire Champ. I am perfectly capable at ending this punk’s career." Sarah shakes her head. "That’s what Clinton Sage thought, and now no one knows where he is. Everyone underestimates Jon, it’s probably the biggest reason he is as successful as he is. Nobody expects him to be tough, but believe me, this isn’t as easy as sending Itachi out of the federation." Bishop shrugs. "Why you so concerned anyway? It’s not like Jon gives a shit about you anymore. You should be the one all behind me, playing the part of the crazy ex girlfriend and all." Sarah doesn’t say anything, and Tavon sighs, moving closer to her. "Look. Whatever reservations you have about this little favor for Desade, don’t worry about it. I have a plan, and I’ve got some of Desade’s goons helping me out. Rhine’s career will be done by nightfall, and then you don’t have to get all mopey every time you pass his locker room door at shows, ‘kay?" Sarah looks up, rage emanating from her face. "I don’t get mopey." Bishop smiles. "Sure you don’t, sweetheart. Listen, I got to go, that Hawke guy wants me to meet the chick who’s helping me out. You might know her, name is Shweta?" Sarah, still a little taken aback from the argument, half-heartedly shrugs. "Never heard of her." "I’ll talk to you later." Seeing that she still isn’t responsive, Bishop grabs her chin and lifts it so that she is facing him. "It’s gonna be fine. Trust me, okay?" Sarah sighs, then nods. "Sure. Be careful." ***** Six hours ago Shweta Kallemulah is idling in a large body of water, lying on the reclined back seat of a small boat, two hands lotioning her legs slowly, when her telephone chirps obnoxiously. "Yes?" she answers, not bothering to mask the annoyance in her voice so that the phrase "Why are you interrupting my fucking vacation time?" is not stated but strongly implied. "Enjoying your vacation, Miss?" the crackled voice of Mr. Hawke enters her ear and brings a frown to her face. Sighing, Shweta rolls over closes her eyes. "You know what? I am, Mr. Hawke. I’m enjoying it immensely. I’m enjoying it so much that the thought of hearing your grating voice undoubtedly trying to get me to end it makes me want to cut someone else open," she says; then, after a pause: "Which is probably what you what me to do, isn’t it?" Hawke chuckles without amusement. "Not today, no, sorry to disappoint. I do know you must enjoy doing it very much, however, since it seems apparent to Desade and I that you left for the sunny beaches as soon as your job was done without checking in." Shweta groans. "What do you want, Old Creepy?" "I need you in Indianapolis by the end of the day, and somewhere near a phone for a 4:30 conference call where you’ll get your instructions. Is Foster with you?" Shweta looks over to her massager and nods. "Yeah, Foster’s here. You think I’m stupid enough to go on vacation by myself?" "Seeing as you can’t even kill a man properly, I don’t have a proper answer to that question. Really, Shweta. I appreciate your... zealousness... in closing the hole with Karl Mullins, but the least you could do would be to clean up after yourself." After a pause, Hawke clears his throat and continues. "Get rid of your boy toy and find a way to Indiana. More to come," and he hangs up. Shweta hangs up her phone and growls, then opens her eyes to see a dejected, dark-skinned man staring back at her. "The Pierce camp needs you to work again?" She nods, sighing again. "How ever did you know, Sherlock?" "The way you pretended that you were with Foster instead of me." Shweta laughs, then sees that her companion isn’t laughing along. Sitting up and grunting, she grabs his hand. "Look, Kal, you know how it is. I love the money I make for doing what I do, but it’s a dangerous job. Not just for me, but for anyone I love. So as far as they know, I don’t really love anyone, okay? I promise it’s safest this way." After a moment, Kal nods. "So you gotta go then?" "Yeah. I’ll call you when it’s over, and we can pick up where we left off, okay?" Kal stares at her, then shakes his head. "I just…you got into Emory’s Med School, Shweta! Why are you even doing this?" "I can’t explain it, Kal. Look, after I’m done we can either go back to this vacation, the water and the lotion and the two of us happy…or we can go back to this argument. But either way, it has to wait." As the boat goes back to shore, Shweta looks out into the ocean in silence, waiting for the trip to end so she can finish her vacation and be in the place where – dangerous or not – she is most comfortable. ***** "Okay, so wrestlers get beaten down all the time. Chairs, kicks, and punches do a lot in the way of bruises, but nothing so much in permanent damage. It’s why you dumb motherfuckers keep coming back for more every time instead of just limping to the hospital like a smart dog. Got it so far?" Bishop snorts at Shweta Kallemulah’s lecture. "Yeah, I got that you don’t like wrestling and you’re a bit of a cum guzzler. Continue." Shweta’s face brightens up. "Thanks! That means a lot coming from you. Anyway, the reason I said that was to open you up to something that might take a while to sink in: you’re not just putting a normal beatdown on a guy where you pull punches because Smitty doesn’t want to hurt one of his talents too badly. You’re really trying to make sure Jonathan Rhine doesn’t walk ever again. So here’s what we’re going to do." Shweta reaches in her purse and begins fiddling around. "At a happy hour bar about three blocks away there is a man named Gerard Rinkman. He’s a divorced, poverty-line-straddling factory worker who needed a couple of cold ones after a hard day’s work, and it’s his lucky day, because there’s a beautiful woman there who seems very interested in him; he knows this because she keeps buying him drinks. It’s a good thing she’s buying his drinks, because if he were to try to purchase them on his own, he’d be sure to notice that his wallet was gone." Finally finding what she is looking for, Shweta tosses the wallet to Bishop, who immediately looks inside. While he fishes through it for money, Shweta continues. "There’s a good chance that Gerard is going to be busy all night, so our time window is pretty big. However, we want to make sure that Jonathan Rhine gets unfortunately mugged while he’s on his way home from the gym, which places the timeframe somewhere around 7:30 to 8:00." "So I’m mugging him?" Bishop asks, still looking at the wallet. Shweta eyes Bishop incredulously. "No, you fucking troglodyte, you’re beating him within an inch of his life. But what the police report will say is that while Rhine was walking home, Gerard Rinkman tried to hold him up because he recognized him and figured Rhine would have enough money on him to help Gerard ease the pain in his pocketbook. What Gerard forgot is the reason Rhine is rich made him a formidable victim. Unfortunately for the former SCCW Champion, Gerard brought…" and Shweta tosses another object at Bishop, this one longer and a bit harder to catch. "…an equalizer." Bishop scoffs again. "A lead pipe? I thought we were injuring Rhine, not killing Mr. Boddy." "Gerard Rinkman must have taken some classes and learned that the worst place for a wrestler to be hit with a lead pipe is on his patella and tiba/fibula, because the bones on both legs were broken. Rhine survived and will probably never wrestle again, but at least Gerard was dumb enough to drop his wallet at the scene of the crime so he could be brought to justice." "All right, sounds solid. If this is all I gotta do to pay Desade back for helping out my pops," Bishop says, putting the wallet in his pocket, "then I can be Gerard Rinkman for the night." ***** It’s been said often lately, but Alexandra Pierce does indeed have an umbrella. That entire umbrella seems to be connected tonight, as members of her employ stand in a room that has two large screens and a speaker system in the corner. There is a platform with a podium for no other reason than to give attention to the person standing behind it. This particular person is Shweta, her hair tied back in an authoritative bun. Sarah Riley and Mr. Hawke also are in attendance as crackled sound comes from the speaker system. "The door of the gym is opening…no, wait, not him. Everybody hold…" A few seconds pass of nothing, and then the static comes back. "It’s him! Rhine is leaving the building now, all Strands in position." Sarah looks up suddenly. "Strands?" Shweta smiles. "Strands. Like of silk." Sarah’s confusion does not evaporate. "Like part of a web." Sarah shakes her head. "She’s the SPIDER. Jesus!" Bringing the microphone to her lips, Shweta says, "Roger. Strand 1, give the signal when he reaches the next corner, and then we’ll send Gerard out." "Roger." Hawke leans against his cane in the corner, watching the screen with an odd smile. On the screen, Jonathan Rhine walks slowly, his head down, visibly breathing heavily. After a second, he reaches an intersection and stops, checking, before crossing it. And the speakers come alive. "Now, Strand 2. Send him out." ***** Bishop is shoved roughly in the back, and he spares one look at the guy who called himself "Strand 2" before leaving the alleyway and following Jonathan Rhine. Bishop rotates the lead pipe between his fingers, trying to even his paces so he doesn’t walk too fast. Despite what he had told Sarah, Bishop feels a twinge of anxiety as he follows one of Sin City’s biggest stars. As much as he enjoys beating up people who hadn’t earned their place in wrestling, he almost feels guilty for what he is about to do. Then, he thinks of his father. His pants ripped, his mouth bloody, suffering because of something he couldn’t control. And he thinks of how Alexandra Pierce was able to ensure that it would never happen again. Swallowing hard, he nods and gains resolve. If Jonathan Rhine needs to go down, then he’ll do it. Because if you can’t deal with the Hell it brings, you shouldn’t be dating the Devil’s secretary in the first place. ***** "Station, we are go." "Do you have to say it that way, Strand 4? I know you want to sound secretive, but…" "Strands, shut the fuck up," Shweta shouts, then eyes the screen as Bishop closes the distance between himself and Rhine. "Tell Gerard to hurry up, Rhine’s car is only a block away." Sarah Riley watches the screen, her fingers unconsciously tapping against the table. She looks down at her feet, then back at the screen again. As her face contorts in fear, she looks up to Shweta. "Wait, call it off." "Why? It’s going to happen in, like, ten seconds." "Call it off." Shweta gapes at Sarah. "I’m not calling off anything, this took hours to set everything in…" "FUCKING CALL IT OFF!" "No." The answer comes, not from Shweta, but from the woman in the back of the room who no one noticed but everyone somehow knew was there. Sarah Riley turns around in a while and stares at Desade for three seconds, her resolve nearly crushed. Then, she shakes her head. "Fuck it, I’ll call it off." And before anyone can react, she pulls out her phone and presses a button. ***** Bishop is just about to raise the pipe when his phone blares a disjointed rap tune. He nearly drops the pipe in surprise, and Rhine turns around quickly. "What the…" Rhine says, but his body moves faster than his words as he instinctively swings and knocks Bishop back with a forearm. Not wanting to press the advantage, Rhine sees his car and sprints, looking around in the darkness for other attackers. He finds none as he jumps into his car and drives away. Nursing his cheek, Bishop looks down at his phone, which is still blinking and emitting loud music. He grabs it, sighs, and answers: "What the FUCK, Sarah." ***** Sarah doesn’t answer. Instead, she merely looks at Alexandra Pierce, who is now standing. The Spider in the Web stares at Sarah Riley, unblinkingly, for nearly a minute. Then, she nods. "It is no matter," the Director says quietly. "Another day for Jonathan Rhine. I repay all of the transgressions against me." At this, she stoops, gathering the evening newspaper, still folded as crisply as if it were new. "In the order those debts were obtained." She leaves the room, and Shweta and Hawke both turn to Sarah Riley, whose face is quickly turning red. "Do you know how long it took me to come up with the codenames Strand? Jesus, Riley, you ruin everything." As they both left the room, Sarah heard Bishop’s voice on the other end: "Sarah? Sarah, what happened? Did Desade call it off? Are you…" Sighing, she hangs up.
