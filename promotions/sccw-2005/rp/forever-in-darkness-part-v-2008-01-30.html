Forever In Darkness: Part V

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.30.08 ~ The sounds of waves crashing against the shore resonate through Eryc’s ears as his eyes slowly flit open and shut. He can feel the grains of sand between his fingers and toes as he stretches out his muscles. The last thing he remembers is Corvus’ flunky smashing him over the head with a glass and the impact was so hard that it knocked him out cold. He sits up and opens his eyes once again to find himself on a beach only he doesn’t recognize it. Eryc tries to search his mind to recall this place but nothing commits itself to memory. It isn’t like Corvus to give up and dump him someplace. The son of a bitch would do whatever it took until the game was over and as far as he knew it wasn’t even close to that. Out of the corner of his eye he sees a huge rock formation. He decides to take a closer look at it to see if it will give him an idea of where he is; it takes all the strength he has to rise to his feet. Eryc might not have much fight left in him but he’s going to keep going until there is no longer a breath in his body. Much to his surprise he is wearing clothes: a pair of gray cargo shorts and a white dress shirt that is done up most of the way except the last three buttons. In Corvus’ dungeon of fun all he had on was the torn pair of pants he was wearing the night he was abducted and it feels strange to him. It’s amazing how much a person forgets when they aren’t used to it. Eryc trudges through the sand as soon as his legs get their bearings. It seems like the huge rock formation is miles away no matter how close he gets to it. His lungs expand and contract as he hasn’t exerted this much energy in what is now likely months. He becomes dizzy and falls to his knees. Eryc attempts to push himself up again but the attempt fails and he is now on his hands and knees. The only thing he can think to do is try crawling the rest of the way no matter how long it takes. He struggles to go a little farther but his body simply can’t handle the burden. Eryc falls face first into the sand and lies motionless until he can regain enough momentum to go the rest of the way. His arm extends out and grasps a handful of sand before tiredness overcomes him and he closes his eyes. The last thing he hears before he succumbs to sleep is a whisper by a familiar female voice. "You’re almost home." ~ The next thing Eryc wakes up to is the sound of a crackling fire. Someone found him and brought him to the rock formation that he was trying to get to earlier when it was sometime around mid-afternoon he would have guessed. The sunlight is almost completely gone from the sky. Beautiful hues of orange, yellow, and red align the sky as if it were on fire…such an irony for a man once called "The Dark Flame". He is nowhere near that same man now and may never be again if he doesn’t find his way back to the remains of his life, which has probably been completely destroyed by Corvus. The chill in the air finally grabs a hold of Eryc as he shoots up into a seated position and moves closer to the fire. He rubs his arms as fast as he can to not lose any heat. His head is full of cobwebs and cannot account for how he even got to the rock formation. He thinks to himself that there has to be someone else around….just has to be. His questions are answered as he hears movement behind him. In his mind he has a difficult decision to make: does he wait for this person to come closer or should he get on the defensive in case he is in hostile territory? His actions take over for him as he quickly turns his body ready for anything except what his eyes see in front of him. Eryc’s complexion lightens even more where he’s just as white as a sheet of printer paper if not more. Standing before him in a white flowing gown is his wife, Krissy, who he lost several months ago when a crazed psychopath shot her right in front of him in a mall parking lot. There’s a good chance that maybe now he’s dead himself or he’s dreaming. It doesn’t matter which to him…he is just happy to see her beautiful face again. "How did I get here?" Krissy doesn’t answer at first as if she didn’t hear him; she puts more sticks and twigs into the fire and tends it to keep it going. Eryc moves closer to her to find out what is going on. His hand extends out to touch her and she turns to him with a smile and takes his hand into hers. They are just as soft as he remembers them to be. Krissy moves closer to him and sits next to him. He asks again about how he has reached this point in time. "Your mind is dealing with everything that has been happening to you. All of the mental and physical anguish that you’ve endured sent your mind into shock. As much as I wish this were real my love…this is your dream." Of course it is too good to be true, he admonishes himself inaudibly. Maybe if he searches deep within he can find the solution to free himself from Corvus and be rid of him for good. Maybe by talking to Krissy he will discover these things. "I’m not really sure what I’m supposed to do…I’ve been Corvus’ personal plaything for I don’t know how long. I don’t think anyone is going to come for me or even knows I’m gone. There’s little hope left that I’m going to survive much longer." Corvus has used every bit of psychological warfare he could think of yet it hasn’t totally broken Eryc down. It is only a matter of time before he gets desperate and starts with full-on physical torture. When they were one whole person, Corvus cut a man into pieces and put him in a duffle bag all because he was following him. Eryc can only think about what he’d do to him because Corvus despises him. "Don’t lose focus Eryc. Whether you remember it or not, you have friends who care about you and love you; and there is another man who is out there doing everything he can to make sure you are found." Eryc cannot be blamed for forgetting the bond he shares with a man he’s fought crazy wars with. Joshua Kosidlo is a man who has risked his life in the past to try to keep the balance of good and evil at an even keel. His first born son died due to events which transpired from his association with Eryc yet he never once blamed him for it. Eryc learned from O’Malley that they were distant cousins; despite the hundreds of years of difference between them they’ve been close as a family could be. Josh has never faced someone like Corvus before and the thought scares Eryc more then anything. He’s lost his wife and his brother and he couldn’t stand to lose someone else. The only thing that Eryc can’t put his finger on is who this other man is that Krissy mentioned. At first thought he would say it is O’Malley but he has rules against getting involved and he wouldn’t be able to interfere. He does have associates that he might send out but that don’t seem much like his style. Last but not least is Brennan, his estranged son. Some progress was made between them but that was moments before he was kidnapped by Corvus’ thugs. Could he have set him up and is still working with the Ravenloft Agency or Corvus for that matter? There is a good chance of it but Brennan showed signs of wanting to repent for his actions as a member of the agency. No matter…all there is to do now is persevere. Krissy moves closer and rests her head on Eryc’s shoulder and he leans his against hers. The smell of her perfume sends chills down his spine. Their hands are laced together and he squeezes gently wanting to be able to keep reminding himself of her so he can do as she advised and not lose focus on what matters to him: finishing his mission. "You have to go back soon…" She says sullenly. Tears start to well up in her eyes leaving the possibility that this was something more then just a dream. "What if I don’t want to?" Eryc tries to fight back his own emotions. "There is no such choice my love." Krissy pulls her husband’s face closer to hers, their lips separated by centimeters. He whispers to her that he will be back to her soon. "I promise you." They lay each other down and press soft lips against one another in a passionate kiss. All things surrounding him are lost as he’s in his lover’s embrace. Time and space seem to be frozen as he closes his eyes. Before he returns back to reality he hears the words, "This may hurt a little." ~ The sound of flesh tapping on flesh stirs Eryc out of his dream like state. He feels a hand lightly smacking his cheek repeatedly. His eyes open to see the voluptuous fiery haired Lindsey standing before him. Last he recalls she was being used as a drugstore for Corvus’ unnatural feeding. She looks a little pale still but it’s likely because she’s still being used as sustenance for the crazy son of a bitch. Lindsey is no longer being held captive in the abandoned building that Corvus is using as his house of horrors. She’s a willing participant in his sick schemes which makes things that much worse. Her reasoning skills have deteriorated under Corvus’ powerful mind. "Wake up, it’s time we had a talk." She says as Eryc’s eyes struggle to focus on her. Eryc forms a small smirk on his face. He knows that Lindsey is not herself so he’s not going to treat her as he would outside of Corvus’ mind control. "You’re going to be doing most of the talking…I’m kind of tied up at the moment." Lindsey sneers and grabs his chin, squeezing it in a rough manner. "Don’t be a smartass." It is evident that she has been in Corvus’ grasp because her actions exhibit his tendencies. She lets go of him after a moment and begins a personal tirade that he has no choice but to listen to it. "Several months ago I had everything I could possibly want: a high paying career and a man who adored me and because he was so broken inside I was all he could turn to…" Lindsey gets closer so that her eyes are drilling holes into Eryc’s, "then you came along." She is referring to Scott Taylor, the unofficial member of the True Saints. Eryc took him under his wing and helped him with the stressful issues in his life. Taylor the last he knew had been turning his life around. "Scott was nothing but a drunk, a loser, but I was able to mold him as I wanted him and he didn’t know any different. I had him wrapped around my finger and he followed me around like a puppy dog until he started hanging around with you." Eryc coughs a little and does his best to try not to laugh. Obviously she is mad because he helped someone who needed it to become a better human being and not just some mindless slave which is ironic in a way seeing that is what Lindsey is becoming for Corvus. "You’re pissed off because Scott is sober and he realized you were a manipulative bitch and wouldn’t sleep with you anymore? That’s a little petty if you ask me." Lindsey’s temper starts to flare. Even for a man who is chained to the wall and rendered helpless, Eryc can still use his words to cut her. Her voice no longer hides the anger she has for Eryc. "You filled his head with fairy tales. Promising him a better life if he gave up alcohol and handing him bullshit about a world full of vampires and other stupid things. Right now he’s probably off looking for unicorns because of you." Lindsey reaches into her hand bag to retrieve a bottle. Upon closer inspection it is a bottle of beer. "Even though I know now that vampires do exist it doesn’t change the fact that you took Scott away from me. You tried to ruin my life and now it’s my turn to do the same to you." She pops the cap off of the bottle and grabs a hold of his chin again despite his protest. When Eryc became a mortal again he vowed that he wouldn’t degrade his body with impurities such as drugs and alcohol like he had done when he was a vampire. Lindsey pries his mouth open and begins to pour the liquid into his mouth and he does his best to resist until she can’t fight him and pours the rest over the top of his head. "I’m going to come in here every fucking day with one of these and force it in you until you become dependent on it." She presses her body against him forcefully; Lindsey brushes her lips against his before she drives a knee straight between his legs. "You’re going to take Scott’s place whether you like it or not." Lindsey’s attention turns sharply towards the door to see her new man standing there with a bag slung over his shoulder. He steps in and makes sure the door is shut securely behind him. She backs up from Eryc and moves to him and he grabs her by the hair. "I like your style…but for right now I have other plans for Mr. Morgan here. I want you to leave us…I have work to do." Lindsey nods subserviently and slips out of the room. Corvus continues to make his way over to the table that is roughly ten feet away from Eryc and sets the bag down on it and a clinking sound is heard. "She’s a real firecracker isn’t she?" A low chuckle comes from the sadistic, cold-hearted individual. "Probably makes you think dealing with me is a day at the park." Eryc doesn’t give Corvus the satisfaction of an answer. It’s not exactly fun dealing with him and now his psycho girlfriend is added to the mix. All he needs to do is remain positive and he’ll be able to outlast anything their twisted minds come up with. Corvus opens the bag up and reaches inside. The bag clinks and clatters as he pulls out a railroad spike and approaches Eryc waving it in front of him in a demented manner. "I figured you and I are going to have a special moment here" Corvus taps the spike against Eryc’s exposed skin. "You make yourself out to be some kind of hero…a savior of the down and out. To me you’re just a martyr and like one man before you are going to be crucified for your beliefs." This is going to hurt a little. Corvus takes hold of Eryc’s right arm and drives the railroad spike right through his wrist. Eryc grits his teeth and grunts from the unbelievable pain he’s experiencing with tears starting to flow down his cheeks. "How does it feel, champ?" Corvus says mockingly, "Ready for more?" The monster goes back to the bag and pulls out another spike returning to Eryc and taking hold of his other arm. Without any remorse he pushes it through the skin and hears a sickening sound of his wrist breaking with the sheer force of how the metal is being pushed through the skin. Eryc fights through the pain trying not to pass out. He will not let Corvus believe he’s won…not now and not ever. Don't lose focus, Eryc. "Well since I don’t feel like trying to push one of these metal railroad spikes through your feet…I’m going to do something a little different. I hope you understand…this is for the best dear Eryc. There can be only one of us in the world and I’d like to think I’m the stronger of the two of us." Corvus walks over to the bag for what should be the final time and takes one more spike out of the bag. He walks back to Eryc again and lightly runs his hand through his hair like a father would a son except this father is the most despicable bastard to ever walk the earth. It is before he finishes his work that he recites a line from Stephen Crane. In the desert I saw a creature, naked, bestial. Who, squatting upon the ground, held his heart in his hands, and he ate of it. I said, 'Is it good, friend?' 'It is bitter…bitter,' he answered. 'But I like it because it is bitter, and because it is my heart.' Corvus lightly kisses Eryc’s forehead before he plunges the spike straight into Morgan’s heart. He begins to laugh hysterically at the sight of Eryc spitting up blood and slowly dying before him. Corvus places his hands on his hips and slyly retorts. "Let’s see god fix this one." The wretched Corvus slides a finger over a flow of blood coming from Eryc’s chest. He takes a taste for his triumphant victory but it doesn’t last long. He stumbles towards the door but he doesn’t make it. Instead he falls to the floor and writhes for a few moments as images of rebirth zip through his mind. He is also introduced to David O’Malley, the man who has been guiding Eryc through his new life. It becomes crystal clear what it will take to end Eryc Morgan once and for all. Corvus picks himself up from off of the ground, dusting himself off. He looks over at the remnants of his former host. Is he dead? Is he not dead? It doesn’t matter because he still has him and if he’s alive when he returns he’ll have all the answers on how to put him to rest for good. "Looks like I’m going to have to get in touch with my faith." He chuckles and exits through the door. His next plan is to find out where this O’Malley is and have a nice little talk with him.
