Free Destiny

16 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Homecoming Free Destiny “Control your own destiny or someone else will.” - Jack Welch *** “Do you know why you’re here?” “Yes.” “That’s a good start. Now, do you know your name?' “My name is Craig.” Beat. A large bead of sweat slips down the interrogator's furrowed brow while a look of worry washes over his face. “No, try again. What is your real name? A man sits in a chair and looks down at the concrete ground below him. His eyes are red, but glazed over, having no control over them. The man glances up with a confused look on his face as torches burn both behind him and in front of him. The man asking him questions sits across from him, shrouded in darkness that could swallow a moth. “My name is…” The man’s voice fades out and he sighs out of frustration. A second man enters the picture and looks at the man with red eyes. “Why is it so difficult for you to tell us your real name?” the lanky man asks sincerely. The crimson eyes move towards the new man, but no answer comes right away. A cave in the middle of nowhere houses the three men on this night. “Because,” the man begins with an honest, but controlled tone. “I have no name. I no longer know my true name, thus I’ve taken the name of the man who I give guidance and fortune. For it is he who I’ve become, and it is I that he yearns to be.” The only man standing in the room looks at the blue-eyed man and shakes his head violently. “He lies!” he voices his outrage only for the man to hold his hand up in protest. “But, sir—,” This time it’s the look from the man that silences him. “Why am I here?” the ruby-eyed man asks which causes both men to turn their attention towards him. Then, they look back at one another with a tense look in their eyes before the seated man stands up and begins to walk away from Craig. The other man takes one of the torches and follows behind the man. As they move to the corner, the fire lights up several different drawings, paintings, and carvings that adorn the wall. “Adeodatus,” the blue-eyed man starts before he looks over at Craig who is still sitting in the chair. “You have to be careful of what you say around him. We’ve gone through great pain to bring him here, to find out what has happened, and what has changed. Things are different this time, you understand this, right?” Adeodatus sighs before he nods his head in agreement. “What do you think has happened? Why do you think he is… this way?” Adeodatus questions the other man, but there is no answer there. At least, not at first. The man begins to walk away from Adeodatus, but stops before he reaches Craig once again. Turning around, he looks at Adeodatus for a brief moment. “I have my theories, but with those theories comes fear. Fear that can’t be shaken,” the man mutters as Adeodatus looks on with fright etched upon his face. The man returns to his chair and looks at Craig with heaviness in his eyes. “What do you know and what do you remember?” the man questions Craig who just sits there for a moment and takes it all in. A large smile appears on Craig’s face as he sits up in the wooden chair that they insist on making him sit in. “What I know would fill legions of books. I know of life and death. I know of sorrow and happiness. I know of destiny and free will,” he rattles off the top of his head with a sickening cockiness. He pauses as his red eyes burn brightly against the fire that surrounds him. “Most importantly, I know how this ends, Vitus.” “Y-you know my name?” the man asks Craig. It’s clear that he’s startled by this revelation. Craig laughs. A subtle laugh, but one that still sent a chill down the spines of the two men. “How does this end?” Vitus asks Craig and the man just laughs at the suggestion before he shakes his head. Vitus looks over at Adeodatus, and he can see the fear in his hands. A dagger is on the edge of his palm and Vitus shakes his head at his pupil. “You two, you’re so lost and clueless. Summoning me here and offering me nothing. Giving me nothing. I don’t like being played with,” Craig threatens the two men, but it’s Vitus who takes charge as he inches closer towards Craig and the fire dances dangerously close to his face. “Don’t think you’re the only one here who knows things, Craig. I know that you bonded with Craig’s spirit the night that Alexandra turned on him. I also know that you’re not in complete control of Craig otherwise you would execute your endgame much quicker. Most importantly, I know that you’ve destroyed the essence, the history behind Craig, and with that, you’ve changed everything,” Vitus explains to Craig and the man just sits there in stony silence. Craig’s lips didn’t move for a moment. The thinness in them was more evident on this particular night more so than they would be any other day. His raw hands represent the pain and loss he has experienced over the course of his life. “So many people, they like to talk about destiny and free will. You want to know what I think? I think it’s all bullshit. There’s neither.” His voice roars much like a lion’s would while his eyes wavers back and forth between the two men. “How could there be neither?” This time, it’s Adeodatus who speaks. “One or the other must exist, not both, but neither of them existing is impossible as well.” Craig snorts. “Are you so sure about that?” “Yes.” Craig rises from his chair and looks at Adeodatus. “Then, how do you explain me?” *** I’ve been having dreams lately. Dreams that I can’t explain. Dreams that I don’t think I want to be capable of explaining. The sun is slowly setting into a fading fireball as Craig stands on his porch. His red eyes are soft and cold tonight while he feels the breeze against his hardened skin. Craig moves towards the edge of the wooden deck and takes a seat while he takes in the quiet air around him. More and more each and every day, I feel like part of me is slipping away. That at certain times I’m not in complete control of myself. There are times that I’m wondering if I’m even in control of things anymore or if I’m just moving along the steady beat that is the drum of destiny. Possibly towards a fate that I can’t stop. A life that I have tried to avoid for so long. More and more, Alexandra is on my mind. Her tears are still fresh against my callous hands, and I wonder if she can change then why I can’t? Am I that dead inside? Is it possible for me to be that detached from the world that the woman whose heart is nearly black can change to be a better person while I'm incapable of doing so? The thing that tears me apart more and more is the fact that I’m jealous. Jealous of her; jealous of Amy. They have the happiness that I can’t have, and it sits heavily on my soul each and every day. A bird rests in the grass just sheer inches away from Craig. The man looks at the bird and notices the content happiness it has just by being able to relax and enjoy the disappearing sun as the moon begun to peek out from its hiding place. Am I involved a war not worth fighting any longer? Sometimes, it feels that way. I look at Alexandra and Amy, they seem on the verge of moving on from their lives and creating a new one for themselves. Outside of the ring, I’ve held onto grudges for far too long, and what has it gotten me? A life where I’m alone, where I’m sad, and looking down the barrel of death. In the ring, it’s even worse. I’ve waged war against Alexandra before, but never on this turf. If it wasn’t for me, she wouldn’t even be on this playing field. Somehow, I spent years able to avoid her, but something drew me back. It always seems like something is pulling me back. This time though, I can't control it. A rage is burning deep inside of me to the point that I no longer have a choice. I no longer had a choice… A moment passes as that thought lingers in his mind and a gloomy look overcomes his face. Why is it that I came down this path? Why is it that I find myself in an eternal dance with Alexandra? For some reason, I must be tormented by her and feel the burden and pain of the day she broke my heart. Is that normal? Will I ever let go? *** “Well, well, well… you brought me back for a second night. Not satisfied with last night’s results?” Craig asks the two men with a sly smile on his face. He sits back in his chair and folds his arms behind his head as he looks on. “Last night,” Adeodatus begins. “Last night, we didn’t get a chance to scratch the surface with our questions, with trying to figure out your place in all of this and why you’ve changed the order of everything.” Craig’s lips curl in an odd shape as he lets the sentiment settle in his mind. “You know, it’s interesting to watch you two try to discover what it is that I’m doing here with Craig. I know why it irks you. You like your perfect circle. The life of Alexandra and Craig, they represent so much, and to see it broken troubles you. They’ve been doing this song and dance for thousands of years and now, but now something is different. The question I have for you is how do you know this isn’t how it was always meant to play out?” Craig questions the two men as his crimson hued eyes looks into the fire. “Because,” Vitus raises his voice this time around. “It’s like you say. They’ve been doing this for what seems like forever now. Alexandra has always represented destiny while Craig embodied what it meant to have free will. They would love and then lose each other, only to find each other again. Alexandra was always meant to be the one that hovered towards the darkness while Craig was meant to be the white light. It’s how the world works and without them playing their roles, the world ends.” The explanation though didn’t even faze Craig who shrugs his shoulders at the suggestion. “You talk about how Craig is the very nature of free will, yet, he continues to follow the same exact pattern time and time again. How is that free will?” the being inside of Craig poses the question that initially stumps the two men. Vitus looks over at his pupil who offers nothing. “Because, Craig is the one with the destiny. He is the one that the stories foretell in their prophecies, yet he doesn’t fulfill them.” Craig chortles before he lets out a boisterous laugh. “Really? Is that what you think? My goodness, you two are more clueless then I could have ever imagined. Craig is a weapon. A means to an end. So,” Craig begins, before he stops himself and stands from his chair. “Since you two don’t understand how this game is supposed to work, I guess I should explain it to you. Ready?” There is no answer from the two men as Vitus sits back in his chair and gives Craig the floor. “You think that Alexandra is meant to be destiny while Craig is free will. Alexandra, in this incarnation is darker then she’s ever been. Haven’t you wondered why? Sure, she’s toed that line before, but here’s the kicker. She only did it to protect Craig. This time though, things have changed. Alexandra is the sheer embodiment of free will right now. She has veered so far off the plan that it’s not even funny. Alexandra took Craig’s place in destiny time and time again because of love. It's that inherent flaw that is absent from Alexandra this time around. They’ve succeeded at changing everything,” Craig explains to the two men. Adeodatus steps forward and looks at Craig with skepticism. “Who do you speak of?” he asks Craig. “The Order of St. Julian. You can only thwart them for so long before they decide to see what they can change. You see, for years and years, they wanted Alexandra in their fold. Yet, it was always Craig who would rescue her from their grasp. Anger takes over them until they decide to change the game. Push Alexandra into the darkness and convince her that Craig needs to be taken out. Play to her insecurities and test her love in a way that she couldn’t think of. Sacrifice. Of course, that wouldn’t fix everything,” Craig continues as he can see the expression on the men’s faces. “Destiny,” Vitus whispers and Craig smiles at the suggestion. “Exactly. At the end of the day, they could not shake Craig’s destiny. His place in the world. So, they had to… change him, but how do you change the purest person around? You can’t take him and make him. No. He wasn’t the impressionable one that would be Alexandra. Instead, you put a force into him that would change him forever,” Craig informs them before he points at himself. “That would be me.” “My goodness,” Vitus gasps. “When they poisoned him, it wasn’t meant to kill him. It was for you.” The smile on Craig’s face grows wider. “Exactly. They tricked Alexandra into thinking she had killed Craig. Never in her mind would she realize it, but underneath it all was that which turned her heart black; the lost of her eternal partner. The Order had changed everything and the consequences were very clear to them. The only way that they could break the power the fates had over her was to kill the connection between the two.” “And they severed it.” Craig returns to his seat as he looks at the two men. “Exactly. In the process, they placed them back in their original roles. Alexandra with free will, and placed Craig with destiny. They switched their roles in life, back to how they were supposed to be, and in the process, created two of the best warriors this world has ever seen,” Craig finishes before he rests his head on the back of the chair. “This is why the prophecies have turned out the way that they have. The Order, they’ve messed with fate. Is that why the world is going to end?” Adeodatus blurts out which only causes Vitus to look at him with contempt. “Sometimes,” Craig begins. “Sometimes, sacrifice is necessary. Without it, things remain the same. With it, it allows changes to happen. But, do you really think we would let the world end?” Silence. “We have a plan for everything.” *** When did my life become more and more about killing, about the work I do outside of the ring, instead of what I accomplish inside of it? My life was once all about competing, but now I find myself dealing more and more with everything but. It’s part of the reason that I find myself back with Alexandra, I think. Even though I’ve tried, I can’t stay away from her. The moon sits on top of him, without any sign of letting up. Crickets are in the backyard and they’re almost like a symphony compared to the thoughts that litter his mind. I’m going to face Lane Stevens in a few days, but I can’t focus. Not on him. My thoughts are dragged elsewhere. Yet, I know what I’m going up against, and I can’t bring anything less than that. I know what I’m capable of in the ring, my skills and abilities, but I also know he can beat me. In a way, I respect him. However, I still want to beat his brains in. Lane represents a dying breed. The River Rat as they call him, is slowly losing his place in the world. He knows he’s found one of the last people that will warrant his presence in Alexandra. I think Alexandra feels it too, the pull between us, but she doesn’t want it there any longer. She brought in Lane to stall for a little more time. To find her place in the world, to maybe find a moment of happiness. We’ll step into that ring, two men who don’t know the meaning of the word quit, and somehow only one of us will walk out victorious. Can I do it any longer? I know I need to if I hope to stop the Hand. But, what if I can’t? What if it’s not my destiny? Why am I always thinking about destiny? My dreams, they’ve been so focused on it lately. Why? What are my dreams trying to tell me? The past months, everything is changing around me, including myself. I feel different. Every morning, I wake up, and I can just feel something is changing within me. Just like its changing Alexandra. Lane Stevens is my last chance, my last hope. If I can get by him, then maybe, just maybe, I can stop Alexandra. Sometimes, I think that’s what she wants. I think Alexandra looks at me and prays that I can pull this apart. It’s her chance to escape from the pain and reality, to slip away, and to just live her life the way that she wants. A life of happiness. Doesn’t she deserve that? She’s found love. Quinn deserves that too. And what do I deserve? A date with destiny. A night with the Rat River. Drown him in water or it’s the end of me. No pressure though, they’re not looking any longer. Except they’re always watching wherever I go. Will it ever end? *** “What are you thinking?” The voice of Adeodatus cuts through the silence in the room. Vitus glances up from his seemingly endless moment alone. “I think they’re going to win this war. I think there’s nothing that we can do anymore. It’s obvious the kind of person that Craig has become. For so long, he managed to stay away from that which Alexandra did. For centuries, they lived two very separate, distinct, but lovely lives. They cared for one another, wanted better for one another, but lived within their boundaries. Then, they changed everything,” Vitus speaks in a very somber tone. “That thing that’s living in him--,” Adeodatus begins, but Vitus interjects. “It’s made him perfect. It’s completed the other half of Craig. He’s the perfect killer. Untouchable because of destiny, and graceful because of skill. It's what he was designed for. As he said before, you can only thwart the Order of St. Julian for so long. They want what they want and they could care less about fate or destiny, even if it means destroying the natural order of things,” Vitus explains in the same exact tone as before. Adeodatus looks at the carvings in the wall as sadness filled his heart. “There’s something though,” he says out loud and Vitus looks over at him with confusion on his face. “What is it?” “Craig, he’s not pure evil or pure light. If that creature had his way, and we pulled him out to look at him, there would be no good left in Craig, right?” Adeodatus asks and Vitus thinks for a moment about this. “I think you’re right. The vibe I got from him is that he’s incapable of truly controlling Craig, unless it’s a rare circumstance. He can suggest ideas, and that goes a long way, but to actually control him… because if he did--,” Vitus said before he found himself looking at the carvings on the wall as well. “We wouldn’t be living in this world,” the two men said at the same exact time. Adeodatus points at the various items on the wall. “The world, it’s supposed to end, correct?” “Yes,” Vitus whispers as he reads the story before him. “What if we have this all backwards? What if it’s not Alexandra who brings the end of the world? If she’s truly exhibiting free will, embracing it unlike she’s been able to do previously, then how would she cause this to happen?” Adeodatus asks and Vitus doesn’t have an answer for him. “There’s much left to learn, my son. But, I think I know one thing for certain,” Vitus teases Adeodatus who looks at his mentor. “I think the Order, they’ve changed everything for the worse. These two, they can’t exist any longer, and they know that. They planned it this way,” Vitus continues. Adeodatus shakes his head at this thought. “What do you mean?” he asks Vitus who feels the tension in his neck as the reality hits him in the face. “I think, in order for the world to be saved, Craig is going to have to sacrifice himself...” *** Man is a being with free will; therefore, each man is potentially good or evil, and it's up to him and only him through his reasoning mind to decide which he wants to be. - Ayn Rand
