Chapter one: Training Days

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-4-07 Cyrus had returned to Lou's the previous evening with the items he was told to get, the roll of dimes and electrical tape. The big man with the grotesque nose had told him to keep them and he would show him what they were for in the morning. Cyrus returned the following morning with the items in tow and had been greeted by the big man at the door before he entered. 'Come on Cyrus' the man said and when Cyrus asked where they were going again the man pointed out that rule number one was as to do as your told and not ask questions. They got in the mans car and began traveling towards the river. Cyrus sat studying the man who would be a 'mentor'. He was tall, barrel chested, with dark hair that was kept cut close to his head. He had a pocked face and a nose that had been broken so many times it nearly hung limply from his scared face witch seemed to be set in a grimace continually. The man turned and looked at Cyrus who quickly stopped with his examination of the man. 'The names Butch, Butch Mayes' the man said. Cyrus turned towards the man and introduced himself. Butch smiled at Cyrus 'Your alright kid. I think you'll do just fine.' Butch said as he drove ever closer to the river. Butch pulled the car to a stop in front of a squat building on the river. 'Here we are.' Butch said as he began to get out of the car. Butch and Cyrus walked to the the building and Butch let Cyrus enter first. It took a few moments for Cyrus's eyes to adjust before he could see clearly the inside of the building. At first the room appeared empty save for a few I-beams holding the upper floors and some dirt on the floor. Then Cyrus noticed the lone figure in the center of the room, tied to a chair, a gag tightly in his mouth. Cyrus quickly turned back to Butch who began introducing the man in the chair. 'Meet Milton Presgrove, Cyrus.' Butch said as he began to take off his leather jacket. 'Milton here has agreed to help in your training in turn for Lou being gracious enough to forget the debt our dear boy Milton here owes.' He said as he hung his coat on a nail that was protruding from the side of a beam. Butch now began to roll up the sleeves of his white button up dress shirt. 'You see Cyrus, Milton here bet on the Bears.' As Butch reached the man he extended his hand and grabbed the man by the chin, staring down into his face.'In fact, we bet quite a bit of money, didn't we Milton?' Butch turned around and winked at Cyrus. 'This is where the fun begins.' And quicker then Cyrus would have thought possible Butch wheeled around and struck the captive man full in the face. The chair tipped over from the force of the blow dumping Milton on the back of his head, a barely audible moan escaping his gaged mouth. Butch turned to Cyrus and showed him the palm of his hand in which there was something wrapped up in electrical tape.'Remember the roll of dimes and the tape? Thats what its used for. Make your own when you get the chance.' Butch turned and grabbed the man by the arms and set him back up right. The man known as Milton tried to raise chin off of his chest but his head lolled back to his chest every attempt he made. 'You see Cyrus, Milton here owes Lou, our boss, ten thousand dollars, and has refused to pay. So now our job is to collect the money for Lou, either by cash, or by blood, but either way the debt has to be payed.' By this time Milton was able to raise head and look around the room. Butch turned around to see that Milton had regained some sense of his bearings. 'Hey glad to have you back with us Milton.' This time Butch hit Milton with a thundering uppercut that brought the chair off the floor. Again Butch picked the fallen man and chair back up and set them right. Cyrus watched as his teacher reached into the back pocket of the blue jeans and pulled out a pair of pliers. 'A handy mans best friend.' he said shaking the pliers at Cyrus. 'Great for breaking fingers, noses, toes, or just general carnage. But we don't really have time for all of that right now, we are busy men after all.' Butch said as he turned back to the limp figure of Milton slumped in the chair. Butch reached into the front pocket of his pants and pulled out a knife and began cutting the ropes at Milton's feet. Over his shoulder he called to Cyrus to go to the car, open the trunk and pull out the little present he had for Milton. Cyrus left the building and had to brace himself against the building as a wave of nausea washed over him. He made it to the car and opened the trunk, inside there was only a spare tire, the jack stand for it, a sledge hammer and a jug of some undisclosed liquid. Cyrus grabbed the Sledge hammer and the can, not sure what Butch had really wanted. As he walked back into the building the sledge hammer in his right and and the jug in the other he was greeted by the site of Milton's blood covered body still tied to the chair with a block of wood between his feet and another between his knees. Butch looked at Cyrus and the sledge hammer then at the bottle and a look almost of disgust crossed his face, then split into a broad smile. 'Your a sick, sick man Cyrus. But I like the way you think!' Cyrus stood mutely with his feet nearly rooted to the floor as Butch motioned for Cyrus to put the bottle down and bring the sledge hammer with him. Cyrus reluctantly moved to stand next to the big man. Now from Cyrus view point he was able to see Milton's features clearly. He was a clean shaven man, with his hair cut in the popular style as of late. he wore a white dress shirt which was now stained crimson with his own blood, he had on black slacks of the finest cut and his high dollar Italian leather shoes were thrown a few feet away. They mans nose was clearly broken and his eyes were red of puffy from where he had been crying, Cyrus had compassion on the man and wished this lesson was all over, but he knew it wouldn't be, something in his gut told him it would not end here. 'Break his legs.' Butch suggested. Cyrus turned to the big man a with a mix of shock and horror on his face. Cyrus could only stand there and shake his head no at the big man. Butch looked down at Cyrus and gave him a slight smile, 'Beginners jitters, I understand.' Butch took the sledge hammer from Cyrus and put his hand on his shoulder, pursed his lips and gave him a wink. He then turned to the man tied helpless in the chair and with two fell swoops broke both of Milton's legs at the shins. Milton through his gage howled in pain then after a moment his head slumped and he began to weep again. Butch handed Cyrus the sledge hammer and told him to put it back in the car. Cyrus did so and as he returned to the building he saw butch pouring the contents of the bottle over the helpless Milton. The smell drifted to Cyrus's nose, gasoline! Butch was walking towards Cyrus pouring the liquid on the floor towards him, the bottle ran out just as he reached the Cyrus and the door. A wicked grin played across his lips as the lit the match. That day would life as a day of infamy in Cyrus's mind for the rest of his life, the sight, the smell, and most horridly the screams that were heard as they drove away. It had taken most of the remainder of the day for Cyrus to stop the vomiting and the dry heaves. Later that night Butch came by the check on Cyrus and see how he was faring, When Cyrus saw him he jumped back wards and began back peddling away from the monstrous man. With a slight questioning look Butch said 'Why you acting like that? It was your idea after all.' Cyrus stared at the man with his eyes wide open in sheer amazement the man would make such a claim. Butch walked over to Cyrus and pulled him to his feet, 'But this is neither the time nor the place to discuss all of that, besides we've got another person to visit. Cyrus shook the mans hands off of him and shook his head no Cyrus told Butch he wasn't going on another trip with him. Butch assured him that this one was different, this one was only a warning visit and nothing bad was going to happen. Reluctantly after some pushing and coaxing from Butch Cyrus relented and agreed to go. They stood outside a night club in the shadier side of Detroit, Cyrus had been given a picture of the man they were looking for. The plan was to have Cyrus go in, find the man and then convince him to leave the building where Butch would we outside waiting for him. Cyrus went in and looked around for the better part of an hour but to no avail, and counting it a lost cause left the building. He rounded the corner and was greeted by the site of Butch with his arm around the neck of another man. Cyrus with an up turned eye brow asked if they wished to be alone, to which Butch replied by giving Cyrus the bird. 'I would like to introduce you to Jerome Williams' Jerome was a skinny and gaunt man, his afro was twice as wide as his body, he was wearing a multicolored outfit consisting of orange and yellow swirls, the gold chains he had around around his neck glistened in the neon lights of the local establishments. His front left tooth was also capped in gold, if Cyrus was the stereotyping sort of person he would have guessed that Jerome wished he was a pimp.'New guy?' Jerome asked Butch who merely nodded. 'Yo, what it is my man?' Jerome said as he raised his hand towards Cyrus. Cyrus extended his hand in the same fashion looked around unsure what to do.'No you got to put your hand down here, not up there.' Jerome tried to explain. 'Oh never mind, yall white folks don't get it no how.' Jerome said as he gave Cyrus a reassuring smile. Butch stepped up and put his hand on Jerome's neck with his right hand and held out wide his left hand. 'You see Cyrus Jerome here is an old Friend of Lou's. And in fact, if I'm not mistaken.....' Butch swung his left hand around in a vicious clothesline that knocked Jerome flat on the pavement, 'He owes Lou some money.' The big man walked menacingly towards the dazed man laying on the ground, he yanked the chains from around his neck and tossed them to Cyrus. 'Well just call that a down payment, Jerome.' Butch said. Jerome coming to his senses screamed in outrage at Butch. A wicked smile played across Butches lips as he stared down at the man. Jerome began scrambling back wards, his back hitting the cold bricks of a building, a look of horror in his eyes as he stared at Butch who was reaching into his back pocket pulling out his pliers. He plunged the pliers straight at Jerome's face. A few minutes Jerome was kneeling in a pool of his own blood, as Cyrus and Butch turned the corner and left the alley Butch flicked something up in the air to Cyrus. 'Call it your payment for todays work.' But said as Cyrus caught the object. The item in Cyrus's hand was a half bloody golden tooth. 'You know it was fortunate we saw Jerome tonight, it brings me to another lesson. If you don't get what you want, take what you can.' That was another lesson Cyrus would not soon forget.
