Cloud 9 Chronicles: Introduction

Date: Temptation vs. Ethan Frost The greatest and worst five words in the English language, in the same meaning, are 'All You Can Eat Buffet.' I love them because I'm a guy who loves to eat and damned if it doesn't feel like robbing people to stuff my plate 5 or 6 times, eating about $90 worth of food for a $12.95 lunch fee. Everything's there -- the soups, the meat, the pasta, and even the ice cream and cake. Mentioning the fact that there is salad is just like a buzzkill. That 'Coulda had a V8' bop on the head will get you a fork in the eye. Straight gangsta. Silvio: It still irritates me that they continue to bring up the Reverend Wright soundbyte. Silvio Fiore, he used to be a wrestler in the indies. Well, still is, I guess, when he's not training. He was a young started, I think something like 16 at the time he began in 2005. Got his GED out of high school, graduated early. Could've gotten the diploma but he didn't want to wait. Now he's at Cloud 9 training other people to wrestle while he's taking classes at Kaplan or Everest or DeVry or whatever one of those bitch-ass 'continuing education' accelerated adult nontraditional crackpot universities he attends. Sorry, but they're crackpots. They act like they're so great, but employers frown on online degrees. Something about not treating them like real degrees. Sky: I agree, the clip is taken completely out of context. And then Skylar Green, aka Ciel. I think his last major title was when... 2004 or something like that? Some people can balance having a family and working the road as a wrestler, but Ciel puts it too much on his daughters and wants to stay with them too much. He probably won't even be in SCCW for more than a couple of weeks. Silvio: As a devout Christian I cannot personally disagree with the man's words, even if I am white. The United States carries itself with the belief of infallibility as though it were God, and according to the Bible that will bring God's wrath down upon us. I hate eating lunch with these two because they always go into current events or politics or some deep stuff like that. Why deep? And around me? I'm Vangellus Oxios, the Sexiest Man Ever and former PRIME tag team champion du jour. If you wanna talk around the table, you can bring it up about American Idol or Playstation 3 or how Disney's Gargoyles is the greatest cartoon ever. That's how I roll. These two are on some whole other level. Sky: While as a nondenominational Taoist I disagree with the sentiment, I do have to say that it is odd that the Obama camp is not mentioning that Reverend Wright isn't the first person to say this. Heck, they've got recordings of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. saying the same thing, in more detail and far more inflammatory tone. Should Obama be called upon to denounce and reject a the deceased Nobel Peace Prize winning civil rights activist whose dream Barack continually invokes? Silvio: It'd sure be interesting. So while they yak on about nothing important, I'm tearing it up on some sweet and sour chicken. We've got a window booth at New City Buffet, perhaps the only Chinese restaurant with the name is entirely in English and without the words fortune, lucky, silver, jade, or dragon in the title. There's a few of those little fried noodle crisps or whatever they're called. I dunno, I don't care, but they're soooo good. But yeah, I'm crunching on those things while Sky and Silvio act like it's Hardball with Chris Matthews. 'So who're you?' I ask since the other guy is also being quiet and eating like there's no tomorrow. He's loaded up with cabbage and fish. To pass by you'd think he was trying to eat healthy, but you would be missing the huge plate of hush puppies and fried chicken he had just a few minutes ago. Dude's only about 5'7 and possibly, and that's a huge maybe, 145 pounds. And he eats like Goku before a big fight. Yes, a DBZ reference. Yes, I get to do those. Brack: Brack Conway. That was it. He said his name and went back to eating as if he was a bear preparing for the long winter hibernation. Now, I'm Vangellus Oxios. When I say that name, it means something. People remember me for being RPW Eclipse Champion. Or for being PRIME tag champs with Scott Matthews. Or for being a top class kickboxer. Or because my production company, the Van World Order, gives equipment and airtime to all those struggling indy trash-talkers who still think it's cool to put out a promo at 1 am in the morning talking about how much your opponent sucks. Doesn't get them to the bigtime but hey, I get to rent them the tech to make an ass of themselves so it's all good. 'You might... giving me a little more to work with? What do you do? How do you know Green?' It took damn near 10 seconds for him to answer me. Gulping down a huge glass of Lipton BRISK Ice Tea. Brack: I'm a wrestling manager. Mostly I've worked with a single client throughout the years, La Sombra. Yeah, I heard of him. That guy who did okay in the HSW J-cup thing a few years back. Not to diminish it but come on. It was Headstrong Wrestling. Think of the worst wrestler you know and he'd have gotten a push there. Plus all their dudes were so big and slow that a cruiserweight would easily run circles around them. Brack: I met Sky two years back in GCW when he and Sombra were both in a stable called Ascension. Back then he came off as a know-it-all but he said some things about not killing yourself for the sport that Sombra took to heart, which was good because the way that kid was rolling, no matter how much he stayed on his toes he was gonna have a tag through them. But now he's slowing down, healing, trying to start a family and doing his career the right way. And all the time this young Al Pacino looking dude is never meeting my eyes. The attention is squarely on his plate and the quickly disappearing food. Meanwhile Ciel and Silvio are still talking and going on to other things. Silvio: It's despicable what they were doing to those poor women and children in the FLDS compound. I'm against polygamy in general for the sanctity of marriage between two consenting adults-- The kid (yeah, he's only like 19 or something) at least deviates from other hardcore Christians because he has no problem with gays. Which is cool because I'm all about the man on man action and if he was a bigot we'd be throwing fists out back every day and night. I don't care if Fiore doesn't feel pain or something; he can still get knocked out. Silvio: -- but I'm definitely against the exploitation of defenseless persons. Sky: Not to approve of what happened there, but to play Devil's Advocate for a moment, if it were not for the fact that you have been taught to disapprove of polygamy and underage arranged marriage with older men, you wouldn't see it as a problem. Ciel's an okay enough guy, but you can't ever just say stuff with him. He has to go on all the philosophical ramifications of it and just gets annoying as hell. And it isn't like he doesn't try to put off any of those feelings about him, walking around in that gray suit and tie like he's Ben Stein. Silvio: No, I still wouldn't like it. Especially when it involves teens being married to older men. I think it's shady enough just seeing the guys from Coconut Creek High School lining up in their cars out by Margate Middle trying to attract the girls as they get out from class. And that's a 4 year age difference. I'm 19 and already I have a hard time talking to a 16 year old about anything because the things I deal with in my daily life are so far removed from theirs. Add ten years to that. Twenty... thirty.... what are those two people going to have in common besides a sexual relationship that's imposed on the female? They can't grow old together... the man is growing old while she's just growing up. Sky: Very good point. During all of this, Silvio's arm waving and animation about his points has caused his wonton soup to spill drops all over the place. Doesn't show up on his dark blue button-down Nautica shirt. God, I hate Nautica. Nothing good has ever happened to me in one of their shirts. I decide to chime in on this because I did see a little of it. Nancy Grace is my home-girl for realz. 'Well, I know it's wrong and everything what was happening to the girls, but I see their mothers. And those genes pass on down the lines. I look at all the pictures of the women of various ages who were involved in it and they make Vanessa Redgrave look like Kate Hudson. Arranged marriages might have been the only hope for those girls to get a man.' Apparently, despite the fact that I was only talking to the people at my own table, something like 6 other tables around me all stop what they're doing and look at me like I just suggested that we go out and assassinate Oprah. I'm used to having the attention all on me, and not even in a totally good way all the time because I love the alcohol but I'm a lightweight with it. Don't even get me started on that one time Joe Rogan kicked my ass. I'll... skip ahead in the story for a bit because there was some name-calling involved and Brack actually cried for a bit and I don't wanna make him sound like a punk. Long story short, we ended the lunch on this joke that I did, which is faithfully recreated as best I can remember: 'Yes, the Kung-Pao chicken was a quagmire. You thought that it would greet you as a liberator from the dingy kitchen you ordered it from. But it was still the right thing to do. Your bout with food poisoning will be a long, hard slog, but the pages of history will judge you favorably. Sure, there were no chestnuts in the stir-fry... this revelation even after you told everybody that there would be, and had Colin Powell stand in front of the U.N. to get everybody to chip in on a bigass order of the Chicken & Chinese Vegetables platter. But the absence of discovered chestnuts doesn't mean that there never were chestnuts at some point in time. And knowing now that you do now about the food that you ordered, if you were to be able to go back to the same point in time to make that order, you would do the same thing again, because you are The Decider. Oh, and your lucky numbers for this election are 9 and 11.' And that, my friends, is George Bush's fortune cookie.
