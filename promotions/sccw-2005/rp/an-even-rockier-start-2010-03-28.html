An Even Rockier Start

28 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/2010 Tokyo International Airport March 25th, 2010 Marching right into the hustle-and-bustle that was the Tokyo International Airport, I glared around in wonder at the madness surrounding me, allowing myself an obligatory, uncomfortable shiver. The very rare March snow outside was shrouding everything in sight, scaring anybody unlucky enough to be caught in it. And because there’s a deity up there who looks down on assholes, Ryan, Mack and I were all victims of circumstance. Mack kept stealing occasional glances at himself through the reflections of the window, choosing not to focus at the dour conditions outside, but rather on his own appearance. He didn’t think anybody was onto him, but me and Ryan both were. It was an amusing way to pass the time, seeing how many times he could sate his narcissism in one hour. Usually, Ryan nailed the over/under on that bet. And speaking of God’s Reason for Ritalin, Ryan was doing his meet-and-greets with some of the locals, some recognizing him from his previous stint here in Japan. Not two years ago, the guy was blazing it up on this side of the world; he was one of those gaijin ace people they loved to have bouncing off the walls like… okay, already used a kid-on-Ritalin joke. Sorry. As the tired old adage goes, he was kind of a big deal here. As for me? Pfft. Another day, another dollar. "Uh… excuse me?" Naturally, I turned around when I’d heard the voice. Instead of it being Scarlett Johannson begging me to ravage her as I’d hoped, I looked right down at a pudgy, balding Japanese mass of sweat wrapped up in a gray three-piece suit. Panting heavily, clutching a notepad in one hand and his heart with another, he attempted to catch his breath, looking up in between agonizing wheezes. "Oh… uh, sorry…" he spoke with an almost perfect American accent. "Frank Silver, yes?" I gave him a small nod. I SHOULD’VE given him a total 180 and a "whatever, bitch." Then I remembered. Crap, we were supposed to meet with this guy from one of the local presses covering the Temptation: Tokyo event. It wasn’t often that SCCW found itself in a place where our sport was treated like a sacred practice. "YAMAMOTO-SAN!" Ryan greeted the chubby reporter with a hearty handshake. He seemed happy to see a familiar face. "Gallway-san!" The rolly-polly got his second (fifth?) wind back. "It’s gonna be great to have you," Ryan’s eyes perked as they turned to face us. "Here, meet my homeslices." Yeah. He said "homeslices." He led Yamamoto up towards Mack and offered up a damp palm, still looking pretty sweaty from what had to have been a good run down the fairway. Mack plodded towards him and extended a meaty paw of his own, engulfing his entire hand in the process. Then came my turn. "This big guy is Mack Brody. And this guy here is my good friend, Frank Silver. Guys, this is Junichiro Yamamoto. He did a few stories for Tokyo PRO from time to time. Thought he could use an inside scoop about our show, so I told him he could hang out with us for a while." There was a silent hiccup in the conversation before I fumbled to extend a handshake to him. Yamamoto shook my hand. As he turned to Ryan, I wiped his gross sweat on my good pants. Better on those than the $500 silk shirt I bought just so I could look my Sunday best. "You’re with The Tokyo Shimbun, yes?" I finally replied. "That’s right." "Good, good!" Ryan spoke up, happy that the painful first impressions were complete. After another long pause hung in the air, Ryan nodded to us, obviously trying to gesture us along so things might be a little less forced. We walked towards baggage claim, but it wasn’t long before Yamamoto showed off what many years of journalism classes were all about. "My, you’re large," he gestured to Mack walking next to him. "And you’re small," Mack fired back with a granite face. "Apologies, sir," Yamamoto nodded. "I meant it as a compliment. You’re quite the physical specimen. Most of the wrestlers I’ve interviewed in the past have been well… not you. Or Mr. Silver. It’s a great opportunity." Ryan nudged my side with his elbow. "Hear that, Frank? You guys are gonna be BFFs before you know it!" I let the thought resonate for all of negative two seconds before shuddering to myself. I hated the press. Reporters, TV shows, even embittered YouTube shoot colums, anything paparazzi, really. Bunch of self-serving, nosy fucks. Yamamoto seemed more like the idealistic, gung-ho, I-haven’t-woken-up-and-smelled-the-shit type, but press were press. Questions were questions no matter who asked them and I hated being prodded like a suspect during an interrogation, on trial for a crime I didn’t commit. Mercifully, the small talk came to an end when we reached baggage claim. The belt started whirring to life, but as the next few painful moments passed waiting for our belongings, I could see Yamamoto breathing another uncomfortable air into the silence. He looked like he had to do something to break the tension, so like clockwork, the "So, I know Ryan’s visited here with us many times," he began. "Mr. Brody, how about you?" "Once or twice," Mack muttered, focusing more on a pair of insanely hot-looking schoolgirls walking by, straight out of a Bud Light commercial. He probably wanted to teach them a thing or two. With his dong. Yamamoto then turned my direction, so I gave my full attention back to the dreary weather outside. Maybe he’d get the hint and go away. "How about you, Mr. Silver? Ever been to our side of the world before?" ****** Conrad Tokyo Hotel, Tokyo, Japan January 19th, 2008 This wasn’t the first time that she’d come back to me. It wasn’t the first time I’d come back to her. Sasha was with somebody now, but if you read the Good Book, it’s gonna tell you that temptation, greater-than sign, fidelity. We were our own Devil’s playthings. There were giggles between kisses and caresses we’d shared, but even those couldn’t really obscure the gravity of the situation from me. This amateurish affair we’d kept on for the last few months was comparable to ignoring the pending devastation heading our direction. The beauty of it, though, was I found myself perfectly content in the eye of the storm. Especially when I could enjoy cupping the back of that beautiful dirty blond hair and gazing into a pair of the bluest, most playful eyes I’d ever seen. She was a diamond to me, so brilliant it was impossible to see anything else. We had met when we were both ten. We were each others’ first, both kiss and… well, you know. But as good as this was, we were star-crossed at best. Our timing seemed to be God-awful; try as we might, this relationship had gone through every possible permutation except the one we both wanted. I was ready, she wasn’t. She was ready, I wasn’t. The one constant among all the variables was when we had times like this, I treasured everything. Sasha’s coy smile got me again, just like it always had. I pulled her in for another deep, moist kiss. Everything about this was nothing short of taboo. For God’s sake, she was here with me in a parking garage halfway across the world from where she told him she’d be, back in Utah visiting her mother. The boyfriend was back home filling out useless paperwork, crunching numbers, replacing ink toner… whatever the fuck he did. But I was lost in everything. It would take a search party to get me out. "Frank…" Or that. The hesitation. My old friend. Those seductive baby blues I spent forever and a day touting were now drenched in guilt, seeing nothing but her transgressions. She’d expressed doubt just doing this, but I thought I’d talked her out of it. Boy howdy, was I wrong. "…What the hell am I doing?" It was a meaningless question. I couldn’t give her the justification she craved. I sat back and watched her unravel as she pointed a shaky finger up at nothing. "My boyfriend is out there, thinking I’m somewhere else…" Tears welled up, slowly descending the edges of her cheeks. Her significant other, to put this eloquently, was a condescending douchenozzle. Allen Riley, a guy that went to school with us. Born and bred into riches, rubbing it in people’s faces because his future meant everything… so, like me, but with money to back it up. He was the type of guy that plotted out everything to the last microscopic detail, not even taking a dump without weighing the pros and cons of somebody having a communicable disease that used it before him. Sasha always had a soft spot for the guy and they’d been pretty steady the last two years, so no doubt she was also in his quote-unquote plans. But she wasn’t right for him. As I said before, Sasha was everything to me. To the envy of others, I’ve gotten to see her inner workings. To the envy of me, it was never enough. "Just… stay with me here," I blurted out, not even considering the ramifications of such a decision. I had scraped together just enough for my living arrangements and with Tokyo PRO, I was making bottom of the barrel. For her, though, I would’ve knocked off liquor stores in a heartbeat if I could buy her way out of this Hell she’d placed herself in. "I know it’s stupid, you don’t even like wrestling, but it’s a beautiful place. You would love it." "Frank," she blushed, "That’s sweet, it really is…" She tried to smile a little bit and shoved me backwards in the seat. The blue in her eyes sat back, trying to remain calm, but still seemed overwhelmed as soon as we shared another stare. The smile she sent back at me was weaker, still attempting to appease me. "It’s a great place... the exact kind of place that you’ve always wanted to visit." I tried to appeal to that sense of impulsiveness that I loved about her. "This doesn’t have to be just a visit. Hell… pick a spot. Anywhere on the globe. I don’t care, I’ll find a way to keep us together." "France." Right after that word fell out of her mouth, I was ecstatic. She would dump that chump and… then I saw the crushed look on her face. This wasn’t some place she wanted to be whisked off to. This was something she… no, he planned. "Huh?" "France… me and Allen are leaving in two weeks. He sprung this on me before I left, but it’s a fantastic opportunity for him." Granted, we were quite possibly in different tax brackets, but I’d made a living on both one-liners and shit-kickings with people like him. The former would just make her mad and the latter would make her mad AND get me in jail when I needed this whole wrestling gig here. I remained obediently silent. "…Good." I turned away from her, seeing her chest heave with discontent. With myself. With her. With her boyfriend. We’d had these emotional outbursts with one another before, but this one was especially twisted. This wasn’t the kind where we would end this on a great note, complete it with goodbye sex, then move on with our lives until the next go-round. This was vastly different from any of those. "You know… you know that I love you, Frank." Out of every bad breakup I’ve had, I’d used this situation to verbally rip women up into dog meat. These were women whose company I’d enjoyed. A couple I’d have stronger feelings for her, but Sasha was far and above all of those. I knew she was waiting for me to say something, anything that’d make her feel better; something to alleviate what was already a gut-wrenching scene. "…Whatever. I’m not the cheating whore here." No. Not even she was exempt from my tongue-lashing. On that happy note, I got out of the card and slammed door shut behind me. I didn’t turn to see her face, I didn’t even turn to see her uncontrollable sobbing. I couldn’t. Right now, I was bound to this dream I had little chance of fulfilling in a place I still knew little-to-nothing about. She could leave on her flight in a couple of hours. Soon, she’d realize I was an inconsiderate prick, that I was totally wrong for her and she’d forget all about me to go live that life with him. Honestly, she’d be better off for it. ****** Tokyo PRO Headquarters Tokyo, Japan April 8th, 2008 If one stepped through the same doors I did, it’d be easy to forget that one was in Japan. The office was flawless, practically undefiled by unnecessary trinkets and other garbage. Everything had a reason for being there. I was a little surprised that the man sitting across from was unmistakably NOT Japanese, but an American man that looked every bit the part of an elderly fellow with glasses and a business suit that looked a few levels higher than my own pay grade. This was Del Marx, an emissary and one of the head bookers for Tokyo PRO. On a scale of Mom-And-Pop federation to the Wuh-We, they were about a WCW, less the geriatrics and almost all cruiserweight division. By this point, I’d wrestled here a few months, mostly in preliminary tag matches where I get teamed up with other foreigners, but this was the first time outside of my initial orientation where I’d met the guy one-on-one. "Hey, Frank, welcome!" He offered me a handshake which I took immediately. "We’re very happy to have you in here for contract negotiations and know that you’re going to translate well to our audiences." "Hey, thanks for having me." "Okay, first off, before we begin, I have to say that Mr. Silver was right about you. Your in-ring potential is astounding. I’ve never seen anybody take to the ring like you have in quite some time." Aw, shucks, I couldn’t take another compli… go on. "However… before we’re able to continue, we do have a few concerns about the kind of person that you’ve been portraying out there in the ring." Oh, joy. We were gonna talk about that one thing… Kay…fah-bee? Sonny told me I’d need to brush up on my ‘wrassler jargon.’ "My apologies, sir, but how do you mean?" I had to try and indulge him with my best cooperative drone impression, but I knew every bit what this was going to be about. Some of the same tendencies I’d picked up in American rings were not going to play here in the Land of the Rising Sun or some bullshit. "Well, Frank, honestly, I and some of the staff are a little bit… concerned." He was searching for the right words to draw attention from the fact that he was probably asking me to completely change my schtick. "See, wrestling here is a little bit different than that of the North American contingent. Here, the audience treat the sport with a little more tact and grace. They don’t come to see wrestlers dole out thirty curse words in the ring at one time and they most certainly don’t come to see people disrespect their fellow wrestlers on-air." Guilty, and guilty. "Well, sir," I replied, calmly. "I love wrestling here, don’t get me wrong. I really want to make this work. Sonny has told me wonderful things about this place from his stint here years ago. But… out there when I compete in the ring, that isn’t some cockamamie on-air anything shining through. That’s me, through and through." My voice remained flat and somewhat uncaring now, but I desperately had to make this work. If I didn’t, I’d be forced to go back to the States and start honing my craft in shopping mall parking lots. "Frank, let me come at you from another angle: we have big plans already in the works for you here. Look at you. You’re only twenty-one years old and in the best shape of your life; practically built like a tank that could, quite frankly, shoot down a lot of the flyers that have made Tokyo PRO the success that it is. You could easily be a reliable face in this company that we’d love to have aboard, but you cannot be defecating on a sport that so many people hold dear." I didn’t have any reply and he knew this, so he pressed on with his play to get me to join Tokyo PRO full-time. "In addition, the in-ring characteristics that you display… nothing short of backhanded. This in conjuction with the trash-talking are not traits that are valued highly," he told me pointedly. "Now, they’re certainly not bleating sheep who don’t know what run-ins and weapons shots are, but overall, it has the legitimacy of a real sport. You just need to change this and work on your wrestling style a little bit and you’ll be an ace of this company before you know it." I wasn’t about to sit through any more of this. "With all due respect, Mr. Marx, this is horse shit. These people out there have been giving me great reactions ever since I started. Now, I’m a little bit big to be running around out there doing fllippity-doo-dahs and 860-degree splashes where I can kill myself landing on my head, but I’m as good as the Surgeons, your tag team champions, that big Russian dude, Azimov, and that sawed-off midget asshole, Ryan Gallway, you guys are fellating all over your publications." I’m almost positive name-dropping some of the guys on his roster wasn’t going to help my cause any but hearing the words come out convinced me that I was good --- nay, better than any of the Tokyo PRO darlings. Marx just sidestepped my biggest salvo. "Mr. Pierce, quite frankly, if you are trying to help your case, you are failing abysmally," he spat. "Sure, you’ve gotten some word of mouth going, but the fans have only had a chance to see a little bit of you. While the flashes of brilliance are there, you’re in tag team matches only so that way you can work on your conditioning and skills. If we were to stick you in singles matches against any of our bigger talent, you’d flounder since you don’t have all those skills yet. You’d be exposed as a lumbering rookie with a style being passed off as brawling. If we wanted to deal with any of that, we could’ve plucked some gorilla out of a zoo." He could make all the Frank-is-an-ape comparisons until he turned blue in the face. This ape was about to go primal on his old ass. "So, I was dreaming when I saw a bunch of bigger guys throwing chops, elbows, and headbutts until they bleed? That’s not something a monkey would do?" I was going to corner him. If I could make my point, maybe he’d listen to reason that I was fine the way I was. "No, Mr. Pierce, I’m fully aware of the goings-on of the Puroresu style. Yes, things get physical." Check, bitch. "But what you do is an Americanized style. It’s crude and unpolished. Those people have been doing this for years now and know the inner workings of a match. But to you, Frank, here is the bottom line." He tied his hands together in front of him, shooting me a look as serious as terminal cancer. "We need your total cooperation with this or else this can’t happen." Goddamn it, Marx. Check and mate. I thought this would’ve been some kind of rib or some mess to really test a rookie’s mettle before they shower him with opportunities. The man was dead-set on giving me an Extreme Makeover: Puro Edition, making me some big slug that threw chops and insanely hard shots to the head for twenty minutes. Ew, no. That would just ruin my downright amazing physique…. and possibly render me a paraplegic. Sonny wanted this to happen for me, too. He’d arranged for me to fly to Japan and perfect my craft. But this wasn’t just some minor in-ring change. They were asking me to be some milquetoast foreigner instead of a proud, outspoken American. Even here in this hopeless situation, I had to make a choice and I’d have to answer it carefully. "Mr. Marx… FUCK. YOU." In retrospect, yup, big mistake. See, after that little tête-à-tête, Del Marx kindly introduced me to creations of Dr. Frankenstein dressed as security. They kindly escorted me out of the building right into the back alley. You know, where there were no witnesses. One of them threw a punch, which I was considerably lucky enough to block with my face. The kicks continued to come in endless waves right into my arms and chest, but fear not. The ogres were also mindful not to ignore my head. After about five minutes that would make Grand Theft Auto look like a day at the spa, they walked back into the building as I spat blood at them, silently cursing the big fucks. Two days later, a courier had delivered me my last paycheck which essentially went to my outrageous hospital bills. Without hesitance, I hopped my ass on the first plane right back to the good old U.S. of A. Hell, the shopping mall parking lots were looking better and better the closer I got. I wanted nothing more to do with a place that had robbed me of four months of my life. My dignity. My pride. And her. ****** Present Day "Uh… Mr. Silver?" I turned my head to see Ryan tugging my sleeve like an anxious child. "Wake the fuck up, dude," he chided. He jerked a thumb back at Yamamoto, still looking back at me. "Sorry, Mr. Silver," Yamamoto spoke up, crossing his arms while Ryan went back to waiting for our baggage. "I was just asking if you’d ever visited Japan or is this your first trip as well?" I just kept looking out into the cold. It was a lot easier that way. "…Yeah, first time."
