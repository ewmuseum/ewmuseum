"I'll be over by the rail." the Hispanic Harlot flashes a devilish grin. * * * * * * Benito Minorez stepped into the dusty old gym, his home away from home at one time. Julissa knew exactly what she was doing when she took this away from him, because it damn near broke his heart to stand there now and see what had become of it. "It's a shame ain't it?" Benito turned to find his brother, Miguel, in the open doorway that lead out onto the street. The man, that was once his tag team partner, stood there in a pair of black slacks and an untucked, short sleeved, two button polo shirt. "You're actually going to stand there and act like you didn't have anything to do with this?" "No," the younger Minorez brother shook his head as he approached the elder sibling. "She told me why she did this," Benito turned to his right and spit on the floor. "But what was in it for you?" "What was in it for me?" Miguel laughed. "After everything we had been through, you just up and bailed on me when I needed you the most. How do you think it made me feel that I couldn't even count on my own brother?" "You know why I had to quit." "No, I know the excuse you used." "It wasn't an excuse." "Don't give me that shit!" Benito shook his head. "It was always about what you wanted. Wasn't it, bro?" "Oh, don't pull this shit on me," Miguel sighed out loud. "We were a team, carnal." "A team..." it was Benito's turn to laugh. "Don't you realize it was your fault that no one ever respected us?" "We never earned that respect," Miguel snapped. "Because you were a hot head," Benito barked back at his brother. "How could we ever gain the respect of the fans when you walked around with your chest puffed out like a fucking peacock!" "Fuck the fans," spittle landed on his chin as he screamed. "You always wanted to please those bastards." "And all you ever wanted to do was win," Benito shook his head. "Are you happy now?" Miguel just stared at him, his eyes red with rage as his nostrils flared with each breath taken. "You took my daughter from me," Benito began. "You took my gym, and you took my life." "You did all of that to yourself," his brother replied. "You were the one who was never home, who spent most of his nights here in this gym instead of at home with his daughter." "Why, because I don't have any kids?" "That's exactly why." "This is the same argument you gave me back in two thousand two, Benny." "And it hasn't changed." "It was probably a rat or something." "That was no rat." "You're just trying to change the subject," Miguel attempted to reel him back in, but another whimper coming from what used to be Benito's office catches their attention once more. "Am I now?" Benito is the first to head in the direction of the small office, where the noise originated as far as they could tell. "Come on." The two approach the slightly opened doorway, but Benny is the first one to peek in. "Oh my God." "What is it," Mikey asked, hoping it was some sort of rodent.
