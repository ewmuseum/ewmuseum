An Unwanted Truth

24 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/24/2007 At long last El Diablo had some idea of what happened to him at Happy Hills Mental Institution, it wasn’t a happy truth but it was true nonetheless. Unfortunately for The Mexican Devil though, he found out the injections he had there made him sterile, and now he had the task of trying to explain that to his loving wife Angela. El Diablo was well aware that he only knew a few pieces of the puzzle and would have to go to the International Pharmaceutical Corporation Headquarters to discover more and find the people who were behind the treatment that made him sterile. SATURDAY 21ST JULY Lying down on a hotel bed was the greasy long black haired Mexican, El Diablo, with his red haired wife Angela next to him. They were on top of a cheap red and white duvet, with their heads resting against the dirty white coloured pillows. "You did it," exclaimed Angela. Turning on the bed to look at her husband, Angela was excited that he had won the FUSE Livewire Title in his first attempt at a FUSE Championship Belt. "El Diablo, FUSE Livewire Champion. Has a certain ring to it, don’t you think?" asked Angela. Looking up at the cracked ceiling, Diablo wasn’t really listening to what his wife was saying. As happy as we was to finally be a champion, all his thoughts were about what Dr Murray revealed to him earlier in the week. "Don’t you think so?" Angela repeated. Diablo was awoken from his daze. "Yes, it’s great," he responded. Sitting up and resting his head against the headboard, The Mexican Devil adjusted his grey t-shirt and yanked at his dark blue sweatpants which had rolled up to his thighs. "I thought I’d feel better about it," he stated. Angela in turn sat up and inched closer to her husband, "Why? What’s wrong? I thought this is what you wanted," she uttered. Scratching at his stubble, Diablo was pondering what to do next in FUSE and what to do next regarding the IPC revelation. "It is what I want, I just have a lot to think about," he replied. Standing up from the bed, Angela removed her fluffy white bathrobe, revealing herself in just a black bra with matching panties. She walked by the brown wooden cabinet where the television was hosted to her rather large black suitcase, in search of some clothing. "The Affliction?" she queried. He was thinking about The Affliction’s offer, but he was thinking more about what to do with the International Pharmaceutical Corporation situation he was emotionally involved in. "Yes, The Affliction," he remarked. Pulling out a blue silk nightgown, Angela dressed herself in it. "What are you going to do about that?" she asked him. El Diablo stood up and walked across the ghastly blue coloured carpet to switch on the television, and then sat down at the foot of the bed. "I don’t know," he commented, "But this hotel room is disgusting," he changed the topic. Nodding in agreement, Angela again sat down next to him, with the noise of the television in the background. "Well we do have to save money, now that you’re only getting fifty percent of your pay check," Angela remarked. Once again standing up, Diablo walked to the television and turned the sound off as he prepared to talk to Angela about a serious topic, before sitting back down. "That’ll be over soon enough. And when that’s over, the sooner we can stay in better quality hotels. But I need to talk to you about something," he tried to stay calm. Trying to pluck up the courage about the shame a man must feel in not being able to provide his wife with a family, El Diablo was going to tell Angela that he couldn’t have children with her. "OH! That reminds me, how did things go with your friend?" Angela interrupted. Clueless, The Master of Manipulation had no idea what Angela was talking about. "What friend?" Diablo mumbled. Although Angela thought he was lying to her last Sunday, she now knew for certain that he was a liar. "The old friend you had to meet up with on Tuesday," she said. Remembering his lie, Diablo chose to play along. "Oh, Diego. Yeah that went well," he commented. Placing her left arm around her husband, Angela wondered what he really did with his time. "That’s great," Angela exclaimed. By now, The Mexican Devil had forgotten all about telling Angela concerning his sterility, and had just decided in his own mind to go to the IPC Headquarters to get some closure on his mental health past. "Yeah. Angela, I need to go see him again actually, on Tuesday," he lied to her once more. Knowing he was lying, Angela was curious regarding what El Diablo was really doing, but knew she had to let him to do it, after all she was positive it wasn’t an affair he was having. "Okay, honey," she sweetly said. Relieved, The Creator of Controversy only had one more thing to say to her. "I need to go alone," he stated, "Then you can come with me, the next time," Angela really was intrigued now, but decided not to reveal her true emotions to him. "Now we have that sorted, we can celebrate your Title win for a few hours," Angela said seductively. Standing up, she switched the television off and walked towards the light switch, passing the torn cream wallpaper along the way, and pressed the switch. TUESDAY 24TH JULY After a long journey, El Diablo had reached his destination. Walking cautiously through the opening corridor, he was inside the International Pharmaceutical Corporation Headquarters, heading towards the reception area. The IPC was obviously a multi million dollar company, as it was the nicest building The Mexican Devil had ever been in. "At last," he said to himself. Finally reaching the reception area, El Diablo walked across the white coloured tiles towards the big black counter which read "RECEPTION" "I’d like a meeting with the CEO, please," he requested. The young, blonde haired woman behind the counter, was only 24 years old, 5’5 and 117lbs, she was quite pretty, but looked perplexed at El Diablo’s request. "No one sees the CEO without a prior appointment," she said. Aware that he didn’t look the part to see the CEO, in his black jacket, blue denim jeans and black sneakers, El Diablo was insistent that he see the CEO. "I have to see the CEO," Diablo stated as he slammed his fist down on the counter. Scared, the blonde woman "Kathy" as her name tag read, was determined he wouldn’t see the CEO today. "I’m sorry, you can make an appointment, but you won’t be seeing him today," she retorted, confident that security would intervene if he made a scene. El Diablo was angered that his trip would be wasted, but fortunately for him a man walking by had recognised him. "Hey, come here," the man rudely called out to El Diablo. Intrigued, The Mexican Devil walked away from reception towards the 6’2 258lb man, who had dyed black hair and was quite chubby. "Do I know you?" remarked El Diablo. Dressed in a black pinstripe suit with a red tie and neat black shoes, the 63 year old man started to walk past reception and further inside the building, indicating for El Diablo to go with him. "No, but I know you Mr Diaz," said the man. El Diablo was very curious and followed the man down a long corridor, passing many doors until the man stopped at a door that read "MR HALFPENNY" "How do you know me?" Diablo asked him. Opening the door, Mr Halfpenny let Diablo into his office and shut the door behind them. The office was quite large, with a nice wooden floor and painted white walls. The décor was stunning, with a sturdy wooden desk at the end of the room and comfortable black swivel chairs to sit on. The office was full of metal filing cabinets and had a great view of an open field when looking out of the window. "Take a seat," Mr Halfpenny suggested. The Mexican Devil complied and sat in the seat directly in front of the desk, as Mr Halfpenny walked around and sat behind the desk. "I had a feeling that we would meet again," stated Halfpenny. Now knowing that Mr Halfpenny played some role in Happy Hills, El Diablo wanted answers. "Look Mr Halfpenny," said Diablo. "Call me Vincent," he interrupted. Diablo nodded. "Look Vincent, I don’t care for the pleasantries, I’d just like some answers," The Mexican Devil said sternly. Smiling, Halfpenny leaned back in his chair, placing his hands behind his head and his legs up on the desk. Vincent Halfpenny was a very relaxed man. "I know what your company did to me. I’m aware of the injections, I’m aware of the failed medicine and I’m aware it made me sterile," Diablo made his point. Vincent nodded along with every word The Master of Manipulation said. "I know, Dr Murray called me after your visit to him. That was a mistake Raul," Vincent calmly stated. Angered, Diablo stood up and pushed the contents of Mr Halfpenny’s desk onto the floor, prompting Vincent to remove his legs from the desk and sit in a more serious upright position. "YOU RUINED MY LIFE!" shrieked El Diablo. Chuckling, Halfpenny got to his feet. "Ruined your life? You’re of a much sounder mind now, than you were back then. What we did helped clear your mind, which made you more accomplished in every aspect of your life. You’re a better wrestler because of the injections. You’re much more equipped at dealing with people now, before you were a hermit and barely interacted with anyone. These injections did more than just cure your mental problems, they’ve made you a better person. We fixed you," Halfpenny informed him. Not able to believe what he was hearing, El Diablo was a mixture of furious and upset. He began to pace the room, as he usually did when in this frame of mind. "You think you fixed me? I have no friends, I still hate interacting with people and I’m still very much happier when I’m alone. You call that fixed?" Diablo responded. Looking confused, Halfpenny pondered what to say next as The Mexican Devil stopped pacing and stood in front of the desk again. "If that’s the case, then maybe you need more injections. Just like the old days," Vincent happily exclaimed. Horrified at that prospect, Diablo eyed all the escape routes of the office- the door he entered from and the glass window. Running through his mind was how Halfpenny must be a madman and that he should probably escape the room, only his feet were firmly planted to the floor and Diablo couldn’t make himself move. "NO!" yelled Diablo. Shrugging his shoulders, Halfpenny calmly sat down. "Fair enough," he said. Hearing that he wouldn’t get injected, Diablo could suddenly move again. It wasn’t usual for The Mexican Devil to be scared of someone, but this was an unusual case involving his bleak past. "I want to see the proof of what happened to me," requested Diablo. Using the wheels of his chair, Halfpenny slid across to a nearby filing cabinet and pulled out a file. Sliding back, he threw the file onto the desk for The Mexican Devil to pick up. "That’s it? All I am is some writing on a page?" queried El Diablo. Picking up the file, Diablo flicked through the pages of it as he sat down. "You wanted the proof and there it is," stated Halfpenny. Smirking, Diablo read about what was done to him. Finally he had some evidence about the Happy Hills Mental Institution. "Don’t get too comfortable, our lawyers assure us that it is a case that will never get to court," said Halfpenny. This news was very disheartening to The Master of Manipulation, as he wanted to reveal everything that had happened to him and to expose the IPC and Happy Hills for what they really are. "Then why are you showing this to me?" questioned Diablo. Adjusting his tie, Vincent smiled and moved his chair closer towards the desk. "You asked to see it," he responded. Bewildered by the reply, The Creator of Controversy was wondering if he’d ever get closure on this chapter of his past. "Besides, it doesn’t change anything Mr Diaz, you were still our guinea pig and thanks to guinea pigs like you the medicine works perfectly when used every three months," Halfpenny educated him. The Mexican Devil wasn’t a genius, but he knew that he was injected every day for ten months until his release and he hadn’t been injected once for the last eight years. "Then how am I fixed? That’s the way you worded it. How am I fixed? I haven’t been injected with that medicine for eight years," enquired Diablo. Standing up and turning his back on The Mexican Devil, Vincent Halfpenny decided to look out of his window at the luscious green fields and trees outside on a hot summers day without a cloud in sight. "If you used version 15.1, you cannot use 16.1. You can have all the 15.1 shots you want, but 16.1 could seriously damage you and we’re just not into that kind of practice here," Halfpenny retorted. Still flicking through his file, El Diablo could now see that the human testing period began with 15.1 and 128 subjects, but only he was in there for acute paranoia and that fact gravely disturbed him. "The medicine is really quite perfect, it cuts off the certain receptors in the brain that cause the problems and cures people, albeit temporarily but every three months they can have another injection," Halfpenny continued. Turning around from looking outside, Vincent stayed on his feet, anticipating that his talk with The Mexican Devil would soon be coming to an end. "Thank you for helping us with 15.1, but we couldn’t have a mainstream medicine that left people unconscious. We’d be investigated for that," stated Vincent. Getting to his feet, Diablo placed his file back down on the desk and deliberated what he thought would be his last question. "Why me?" Diablo asked him. Staying silent, Halfpenny wasn’t going to answer him. "WHY ME?" he demanded. Running his hands through his dyed black hair, Halfpenny was trying to think of how he could tell him the truth. "Your wife," he mumbled. That revelation left El Diablo reeling. "Your wife led you to us," muttered Halfpenny. The thought of his wife betraying him was running vividly through his mind, until he snapped and launched himself over the desk to attack Vincent. "YOU’RE LYING!" Diablo wailed. Managing to connect with one punch squarely on the jaw, security rushed into the room and dragged The Creator of Controversy off of their boss. "LET ME GO" yelled Diablo. Kicking at the air, The Mexican Devil was getting dragged out of the room. Halfpenny stood up and rubbed the side of his face before addressing El Diablo. "Why would I lie?" he said honestly. Still furious, The Mexican Devil was thrown out the room and eventually out of the building. Had Angela really been behind his stay in that repulsive mental institution? Was his married life a lie? Is he sterile because of Angela? El Diablo needed to know these answers even more so than he needed answers from Dr Murray and the International Pharmaceutical Corporation.
