A better reason to retain

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-09-07 Two weeks after becoming the Livewire champion Cyrus Raynes was still in awe of the whole turn of events, in a matter of moments he had gone from a man on a four loss loosing streak to the Livewire champion. It had been incredable, mind blowing even. The last couple of weeks has rushed by like a blur and it still hadnt sunk in completly to Cyrus yet. He sat on the bed in his hotel room, holding the title, staring down at it still in disbeliefe. Something clicked in Cyrsus mind, and he remembered that he hadnt even called Sam yet and told her the news. Cyrus threw the belt over his shoulder and walked to to phone, picked it up and dialed the numbers. Ring. Ring. Ring. Ring. 'Hello' Sams beautiful voice drifted over the receiver. 'Hey, Hey!' Cyrus exclaimed. 'Hey Cyrus! Long time no see. How have you been?' Sam asked. 'Great! Just........great. Youll NEVER guess what happened to me a couple of weeks ago.' Cyrus said. 'What?' Sam asked. 'Bah, you know im not going to make it that easy for you. You have to guess!' Cyrus said excitedly. Sam sighed. 'Ok, lets see........ You found out your pregnant!' Sam joked. 'Sam.' Cyrus said with a flat tone. 'Okay, Okay. You big kill joy. Lets see now, ummmm, you decided to.........Cyrus, I really dont know, can you just tell me, please?' Sam sweetly asked. Cyrus never could resist Sam asking please. All Sam would have to do is say the word 'Please' and Cyrus would do anything she asked. And Sam knew it, but she was too much of a lady to use it agasint him. So of course Cyrus relented. 'Well I got a title shot a couple of weeks back and I won! Can you belive it? I won. Im the LiveWire Championship!' Cyrus bellowed into the phone. 'Oh my God! Cyrus, are you kidding? Your a champion now? That is so wonderful! I am so happy for you!' Sam told Cyrus. 'No, Sam, im not kidding. Im holding the LiveWire belt as we speak, in fact I havent put ti down for two weeks, I mean I take it with me where ever I go. It has not left my hands since I got it' Cyrus told Sam 'Oh, Cyrus I am so proud of you, congratulation Cyrus. You deserve it. So tell me what happened, how did the match go?' Sam wanted to know. 'Well, funny thing about that match Sam, I really dont remember what happened. I know that sounds bad, but I was so pumped up for that match that I remember the bell ringing and then my hand being raised. Thats really all I remember.' Cyrus told her. 'So what now?' Sam asked. 'Well, I have my first defence coming up here in a couple of days against Fred Fusion.' Cyrus said. 'Is he that guy with like amnesia right? He always has to introduce himself to people over and over again? Am I thinking of the right guy?' Sam asked. Cyrus Chuckled 'Yeah, thats him. And he doesnt have amnesia, he just has, well, to be quite honest I dont know whats wrong wiit him.' Cyrus said. 'Well what ever it is, it will be much, much worse if he ends up on the receiving end of the Down Pour I would venture to say.' Sam giggled. 'Yeah, I hope thats the way the match ends, but I dont know, hes a pretty bad dude, its going to be a tough match. Defently a big win for me should I retain. I am not going to take him lightly or think he is some kind of push over, he could very easily win should I make a mistake.' Cyrus explained 'Wow, you really think youll have to worry about him?' Sam asked. 'Oh yeah, hes good, I cant deny that. Of course dont tell him I said that. His head would get so big you couldnt kick him through a set of double doors.' Cyrus chuckled. 'Well then 'champ' you need your rest if your going to be at your best, so Im going to get off of here so you can get some sleep.' Sam explained 'Aww, do you have to?' Cyrus groaned 'Yes, I do. Now you, mister champ need to go to bed, and Im serious about that. Go to bed. And no beers either, I mean it.' Sam threatented Cyrus Cyrus had to laugh. Good ol' Sam, always watching out for his well being, Cyrus really was glad to have her as his fried. 'Alright, alright, ill go to bed.' Cyrus said. 'With no beers, Cyrus, no, beers.' Sam scolded Cyrus. 'Ok, ok, no beers.' Cyrus relented 'Alright then, good night...........My Champion' Sam said with a giggle. 'Good night...........uhhh..........Sam.' Cyrus said. Cyrus hung up the phone, and sat back on the bed, this time with a new resolve, not just to stay the LiveWire Champion, but to remain Sams Champion. Because in Cyrus' eyes, that was truely the highest prize of all.
