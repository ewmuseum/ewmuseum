Palatinus: Part Thirteen - 'Before It's Too Late'

2 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2nd, 2007 Palatinus: Part Thirteen 'Before It's Too Late' 'I wonder through fiction to look for the truth Buried beneath all the lies And I stood at a distance To feel who you are Hiding myself in your eyes And hold on before it's too late Until we leave this behind Don't fall just be who you are It's all that we need in our lives And the risk that might break you Is the one that would save A life you dont live is still lost So stand on the edge with me Hold back your fear and see Nothing is real til it's gone' 'Before It's Too Late' by Goo Goo Dolls (Transformer's Soundtrack) ******************** January 14th 2007 Despite the cold, Joshua stood stone still in front of the headstone. It took a long time to get here, over a month. A month that he never brought himself to this site, this spot. Everything had changed, and yet now he knew things were still the same. He missed his first match back to Global out of grief, and it looked like he may never return. That's not what Andy would have wanted, Andy would have wanted his father to continue doing what he did. A single tear rolls down Joshua's face, the last tear he would shed over this. He needed to be strong, because his son was strong. Andy was stronger, and braver then most men Joshua knew, and to live life in shambles, permanently stricken by grief would be an insult to Andy's memory. Joshua knelt into the thin layer of snow, despite the cold and placed a single white rose above the final resting place. 'I love you son, and I miss you.' He whispers, because Andy can hear him no matter how loud he speaks. Andy is in heaven now, right where he belongs. Joshua rises from the plot, plunging his hands into the deep pockets of his leather coat. Things were going to be different, sure. But that didn't change anything. ******************** July 14th, 2007 5:35 AM Joshua's footsteps echo down the shadowed hallway, the confines of this tunnel have been traveled by many a men, tonight they are traveled by only one. At the end of the hallway Joshua comes upon a brass knob of a solid oak door. Through the door is a room more accommodating, filled with the luxuries of modern life. On the walls are classic paintings by Van Gogh, Picasso, Rembrandt and even a Da'Vinci. The ample space provides a contrast to the cramped corridor that lead here. Kosidlo doesn't notice the expense in the apartment as he's been here plenty of times before. A virgin eye however would notice the parquet flooring with oak finishing to match the furniture's oak trim; a hunter green wall paper compliments the shade of green used in the fabrics of the drapes and the upholstery. 'I am glad you made it.' A voice speaks from the side of him with a hint of an Irish accent. Joshua turns to meet the gaze of David O'Malley. O'Malley is a small man, with shoulder length pale strawberry blonde hair. He appears no older then perhaps thirty, and his face hardly show signs of age. There are only three lines in the crease of his eye to indicate any age to him, other than the intensity within his pale green eyes. 'So then I supposed you were expecting me?' Joshua asks, taking a seat in a comfortable armchair. O'Malley nods taking his place in the chair across from him. 'I was expecting you soon yes.' O'Malley replies. 'So I take it you're in need of something?' Joshua nods his head slowly, in that nod speaks all the trials that lay ahead, and behind. 'I'm in need of understanding.' Joshua replies, shifting his gaze to lock eyes with his guide. 'What happened, how did it happen, I don't even know when it happened.' O'Malley turns his gaze from Kosidlo toward the coffee table between them. After a moment he looks back up, and with a solemn face replies. 'It's always been there Josh, since the day you watched you shot your father to save your mother, it has always been there.' Joshua's mouth nearly falls to the floor, he never thought anyone knew what really happened that night. 'What are you saying?' Joshua manages to spit out, 'my whole life's been a lie?' O'Malley shakes his head no, and after another moment's hesitation he continues to explain. 'It's always been there, it's been a sort of coping mechanism, but after Andy died you began to believe all sorts of things, all out of grief.' 'How do I know what was real?' Joshua asks, 'I mean I know where some inconsistencies are, but where is the line drawn?' O'Malley raises from his chair, 'Joshua you came to me five months ago on this very day, and everything that has happened since then has not been the way you thought it was.' 'Like my wife leaving me?' O'Malley nods, 'That is one example, the two of you got into an argument, and you told her to leave. You started driving everyone away from you in fear of losing them too.' Joshua clasps his hands in front of him, placing his head upon his thumbs. 'I see...' and the truth was he actually did see, he was seeing everything now as clear as it was the first time. He sighs deeply, then buries his face in his hands. 'Sanctum will always be a part of you Joshua, you just can't let him overtake you.' O'Malley states, 'What you need to do is find a way to destroy the second identity, and become a whole man again.' Joshua nods, and rises from the chair, his hands at his side. 'Thank you, but I've got to be going now, I have a lot to do.' O'Malley nods, 'I know...' Without exchanging another word, nor even a glance Joshua walks out of the apartment for what will be the last time. O'Malley can't do anything else for him... not anymore. ******************** The warm July night has left Joshua feeling sticky. The muggy weather makes it difficult to breath, especially at the moment. Joshua clears his throat, and nearly coughs up a lung in the process. The air is too dry. 'I guess it all started here...' Joshua states through a clogged voice. As he speaks what now lay upon his mind, his gaze lays focused on the soft curve of the headstone. The name was chiseled with care, as were the mere seventeen years of life. 'It's time to move on, I know now there was nothing I could have done for you, but there is something I can still do for Jason, and for Bonita.' Joshua pauses for a moment, holding back a tear. When he starts again his voice cracks at first, then steadies out. 'And for your step mom.' 'I wish things had been so much different, and that I had known you so much earlier in life... but things just weren't meant to be... and I'm sorry.' Joshua's gaze falls onto the fresh white rose, laid out by him no more than half an hour ago. A single tear falls from his eyes as he turns to walk away. Once his back is turned, he begins to cry. The humidity breaks, and soft cool drops of rain fall to the earth, washing away the faded memories. ******************** July 14th, 2007 12:01 PM Joshua exits the shower and heads into his bedroom. On his bed lay out a long sleeve white shirt, and beside it lay one of Joshua's gray Armani suits. He dresses quickly, though his attention remains on the dresser across the room. Along the black mahogany finish of the top are various family photos of Bonita, Angel and Jason. One picture, an infant, newborn in a little blue knit hat and a pampers diaper. Various tubes extend from his body, and one from his mouth. It is a picture of Jason on the day he was born. Another picture showed Joshua and Angel on their wedding day, Bonita standing in front of them holding a little bouquet of baby blue roses and white carnations. Joshua was dressed in a blue pinstripe tuxedo with a baby blue rose pinned to his lapel, and a blue striped bow tie. Angel was in her long white wedding dress, with baby blue roses sewn throughout the material. Her hair was pulled back, and she too carried a bouquet of baby blue roses and white carnations. The final picture was of the whole family. Joshua was dressed in a gray suit and tie, his hair was slicked back and his face sported a warm smile. Angel was dressed in a white peasant top with light brown trim, and a light brown dress with ruffles throughout. In front of them was Bonita in a little pink and white dress with a pink bow across the white top. In Joshua's arms just above Bonita was Jason, in a little pair of dark blue pants and white dress shirt with a blue and brown tie, and a blue and brown sweater vest over it. This, was the biggest picture of all. His gaze falters once the tie has been tightened. A sigh escapes his lungs, and he heads into the dining room. In there he does his best to avert his eyes from the family photos on the walls. With his back turned he faces the dining room table. A second deep sigh escapes, and he approaches the table. He turns back, and glances once more at what lay around the house, the memories both real and fake, and then climbs to the table top. Sanctum Nocturna is a part of him, and Sanctum needs to die... Joshua steps from the table as the noose tightens around his throat. A snap, then a crack echoes through out the empty house. But no one comes, because Joshua is alone. ---- Next Series 'Reborn Again' coming soon.
