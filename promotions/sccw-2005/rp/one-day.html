One Day

Date: Today Freedom from the doldrums of life was always one of the many things that man has fought for over the years. Freedom to do what you want, when you want. Freedom to be the person that you were meant to be, not the person that society told you that you had to be. Freedom is the knowledge that there is nothing that can stand in your way. Xander Searle now has this freedom. He wasn’t letting it go to waste. He doesn’t care if the things that he says to people offend them. He doesn’t care if the things that he does will anger people. As long as he stays on this side of the law, as long as what he does doesn’t impede on his new found sense of freedom, at least to him, it doesn’t matter what other people think. That is the freedom that he has been looking for his entire life. Now he has found it. He’s not going to give it up without a fight. Xander Searle is only a few days removed from what many of the morons in the internet wrestling community would call the "biggest win of his career", winning a guaranteed title shot against either the Gateway Champion or the Livewire Champion. Those titles now reside with Phillip Kennedy and Rick Malloy respectively. He’s not thinking about that right now. Instead, he’s looking at how he’s going to spend his "winner’s purse" from the SCCW Homecoming Pay-Per-View. The one thing that he learned from his former teacher is that with every victory, you need something to remember it by. Jason Ryan collected programs and posters. With the money that he got from the win, Xander didn’t think that a poster or a magazine would suffice for this win. Instead, he found himself driving through Los Angeles. Past the strip joints, the churches, the tattoo parlors, the porn theaters and the clubs. He could see it in the faces of all the people that he passed on the street. He saw the jealousy. They looked at him like he could be a major star at one of the movie studios. His confidence went past the border into cockiness, and that exuded from his pores like the old 90’s wrestler Razor Ramon oozed "machismo". He knew that his future was going to include a shot at one of the lower tier titles in SCCW. He wasn’t going to be satisfied with just that. For Xander Searle, he knows that his future in this business isn’t to be a curtain jerker, but to be a major star. Some of the dirt sheets have already started to talk about him. That was a start. Only, they weren’t talking about his star potential, but rather that he was an undersized big man. That for him to succeed in Professional Wrestling, that he needed to add close to 60 pounds to his frame. Maybe he’ll eventually get there. But for now, he knew that he was starting to become a hot name. Going into Homecoming, everyone looked at Xander Searle and thought of nothing but the man that pulled him from the crowd. Now, with a new attitude and newfound aggression, Xander was starting to get out from the lofty shadow that Jason Ryan once cast. He continues to drive and finds himself at the World Famous Sunset Strip, the birthplace of many of the rock legends that are now trying to do what most wrestlers were also trying to do, stay relevant beyond their years. That wasn’t a problem for Xander. He was becoming relevant, faster than anyone anticipated. Winning at Homecoming helped to solidify that. As the days pass until he takes his first title, he knows that he isn’t out to build a niche, a career. He’s out to build his legacy, a legacy that when everything is said and done won’t start with the words "Alexander Searle was pulled out of the line for the Mezzanine Level Nacho Cart" but rather "Xander Searle is considered one of the greatest to ever lace up the boots." His future isn’t with the riff raff that crowd the streets, hoping to become a roadie or a groupie for the next Mötley Crüe or Poison. His future is for the women that are so hopped up on crank that they would allow Verne Troyer to fist them to want to be with him, and he laugh as he walks away with the higher class women. His future is for the same mullet wearing peon that wants nothing more to carry Metallica’s road gear to wear his T-Shirts while he tells them how much of a loser they are. For Xander, the future is going to be good. For these people that are looking in awe at him, the future still sucks. Xander found his way to the West Hollywood location of High Voltage Tattoos, the shop made famous by the TV Show LA Ink. Xander had called earlier to make sure that TLC wasn’t filming. He didn’t know if the show was in production for an upcoming season, but he wanted to make sure that if he came, that he wouldn’t have been seen on TV. After careful deliberation with his artist, Xander chose to go with a tribal dragon on his calf. He sits in the chair as the needle drags across his skin, each moment, more and more ink entering into his once unmarked flesh. Sure, there was a little discomfort, but Xander knew that this was nothing compared to some of the pain he’s been in as a wrestler. As the time passes, Xander feels the endorphins being released into his bloodstream. Now he understands how Amy Campbell came to be covered in ink. It was a high that no drug could match. A high that when he comes down from it, won’t result in a crash that will cause him to overdose to reach it again. As the minutes turn to hours, Xander finds himself almost at peace. As his artist finishes, he looks down and is amazed at how detailed the tattoo is, how much the colors just pop. It doesn’t matter that there’s a little trickle of blood flowing down his leg. The rush was worth it. Xander finds himself back at his new condo an hour after getting the tattoo done. He stands out on his balcony looking over the neighborhood that he moved into. A knock can be heard at his door. Xander heads towards the door and opens it. It was a man from UPS. Xander signed for the package and noticed that it was from the SCCW marketing department. He opens the package to find four t-shirts, all of them dealing with his new "persona". He tosses them aside, not wanting to be bothered by the trivialness of "merchandising". He turns on the shiny new plasma that was just delivered in the morning. He flips through the channels and gives up on trying to find something on TV. Deciding that there was nothing better to do, he takes his keys and leaves.
