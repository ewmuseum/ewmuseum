1.1 - Not A Magic Carpet Ride

16 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 16 Their bodies beaten and sore, the two men that make up MEDS are packed up and ready to leave Tokyo behind for the next show. Seeing as SCCW is on an overseas tour, they have decided that they aren’t even going to bother heading back to the states until after the next show. Matt is icing down the knee that still hasn’t completely healed from Defiance, and Devlin is icing down….well, he's in a bath tub full of ice. "Dude, I thought you said that I would get used to this." Devlin blurts out as he pulls his body out from the ice and the water that fills the tub. He grabs a towel to cover his bluing skin, and tries to get warm. He closes the door so that he can get changed. Matt, on the other hand, just looks at him and shakes his head. "Quit the complaining, take some Tylenol, and….well, uh….do whatever you can right now without blazing a joint." "You serious? No buzz? Nothing?" "Yes, I’m serious." Matt responds with an exacerbated look on his face. "In about an hour, we’re getting on a plane to Okinawa, and they aren’t going to let you on if you’re even a little high." "The fuck? Okinawa? What are we going to do there?" Devlin asks as he strolls out of the bathroom, having changed out of his board shorts into a pair of carpenter’s pants and a Buzzcocks T-Shirt. "That’s where we get on our flight to the next show." Matt answers. "I told you that this wasn’t going to be a one shot out of the states." Devlin, looking at his open bag, responds, "I wanna go home. This is bullshit." Matt places his hands onto his forehead, and just starts shaking his head. "Dev, right now, you don’t want to piss me off. It seems that every time that you’ve wrestled solo, I somehow ended up staring at the lights." "HEY! I can’t do shit about that. You saw that I hit that last motherfucker with a steel chair. Right to the squash. Dented that fucker too." This just causes Matt to give off a little sigh and laugh. Kinda like the type of sigh and laugh that you would give if you were about to throw someone out of the hotel window and to the concrete below. Unfortunately, the sprung for a cheap motel, and their room is on the ground level, so that it wouldn’t do much to Devlin. "Remember that stuff you did at Defiance? Remember what you were doing when we were in that big ass tag team match?" Devlin looks at Matt, trying to come up to the right answer to that question. "I….don’t remember much from that night. There was some great Panama Red at the party I went after the show." "You’ve got to be kidding me. No, no. You didn’t say that. Even if you did, I’m going to pretend that you didn’t. I’m going to try and imagine a world where I’m not teamed up with the one guy that can outlast Cheech and Chong." "You know, that’s the nicest thing that you’ve ever said to me, man." "Sometimes, I really hate you." Before Matt can slap the shit out of the moron he calls a friend and tag team partner, a knock can be heard at the door. "Are you two lads decent in there?" A sweet, yet almost aristocratic sounding voice can be heard through the door. "Come on in, Kirsten." "Kirsten? Who’s Kirsten?" Devlin asks. Matt responds to this question with the epic, almost unblockable and feared Picard Facepalm (it’s not just for use on wrestling transcripts folks.) and shakes his head in disbelief. "Well, I am, silly." Kirsten answers as she pulls her keycard from the door, and closes it behind her. "From the looks of things, you two lads are just about ready to go too. There should be a coach coming to get us shortly." "Us? You’re with us? TITTIES!" The filter in Devlin’s brain is completely fried after years of weed, Robot Chicken and masturbation….and many times, all three at once. "Charming. Well, you lads better hurry up, because the coach will be here shortly." Devlin gets close to Matt, and whispers to his tag team partner, "Dude, why is she worried about us getting packed if a coach is coming?" This draws a glare from the more professional member of MEDS. "What did I say?" Matt then spins, grabs Devlin by the collar and screams, "SHE MEANS A FUCKING CAB!" Matt the releases his grip and walks into the bathroom and shuts the door. After about a minute, you can hear the sound of what quite possibly is Matt kicking the fixtures in the room. "God, why have you done this to me? What did I do to deserve this? I know that Kirsten is a blessing…" Devlin looks at Kirsten and then laughs, "Dude’s got the hots for you. You hurt him, I kill you." "…but what did I do to deserve the rest of this shit." The next sound to come from the bathroom is the telltale sound of porcelain breaking. "MOTHER FUCKER! MOTHER FUCKER!" Devlin looks at the door, and then back at Kirsten, "You think he’s angry about something?" Kirsten just rolls her eyes, shakes her head, and gives a giggle that almost says ‘I’m thinking the same thing as the guy that just took out the toilet.’ Matt exits the bathroom, and shuts the door behind him. Half of his right leg is wet, he pulls the ice pack off his left knee and throws it down. "If it wasn’t for the fact that I promised your mother….FUCK! Let’s just check out." -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- MEDS and Kirsten are in the cab heading towards the airport. They know that in about three hours, they are going to be leaving Tokyo for Okinawa. "Well, after we land, we are going to head towards the Air Base, and catch up with the rest of the SCCW crew that we are going there with." Matt says as he reads the travel itinerary for this tour. "I’ve been on these trips before. They aren’t all that fun. Especially travelling with Alexandra. She could be a little bossy at times." Devlin turns from looking out the window. "Who’s Alexandra? Or are you talking about that Widow chick that is like, well, kick ass and stuff?" "Yes, that would be the same Alexandra Pearce." "You travelled with her? Lucky you. I’m pretty sure the two of you got into some pretty banging parties. I personally think that she’s the hottest wrestler in SCCW." "Devlin, can you do me a favor, and shut it. I’m trying to get an idea of our touring plans when we get to our final destination. From what I’ve heard, these shows tend to be logistical nightmares." "Where are we going? Somewhere cool, like Thailand? Hong Kong? TOKYO!?" Matt looks up from the itinerary, pulls down his glasses and gives a glare that if he had lasers coming out of his eyes, would burn Devlin Shannon to ash. "We are LEAVING Tokyo." "We are? And I didn’t get to go to any of those shops that sell Hentai? Dude, I know you like, well, have the hots for Krissy….." "Kirsten." Kirsten responds quickly to correct Devlin. "…..but you’re supposed to look out for me as well. You know, bros before hos…..no offence, I really don’t think that you’re a ho." Devlin finishes. "Well, Devlin, we’re going to Camp Hammond." "Camp? Sweet. I’ll tell those little kids some crazy ass ghost stories, and get them to make me some s’mores. Kick ass." Kirsten starts to laugh at Devlin. "No, you silly lad. It’s military base that you have." Kirsten responds to Devlin’s thoughts that they were going to wrestle in front of kids at a summer camp, even though it’s still the middle of April. Devlin looks to the left, and then to the right. "Military Base. Well, at least we’ll be safe there. Where’s it at?" Without missing a beat, Matt replies "Baghdad. Iraq." Devlin’s eyes open wide. "What? Iraq? No, no, I can’t go there." Matt cringes when he hears this. Then again, he cringes enough when Devlin talks that you might think that the face that Matt makes when he cringes is what his face actually looks like. "We’re going to be protected by the greatest fighting force ever assembled. We’re performing for them to give them a slice of home, even though they are in that hell hole." Devlin starts to try and beg off, but seeing as they’re in a cab, he can’t do too much. "You don’t understand, dude. I can’t go there. It’s not safe for me there. " At this point, Matt has started to tire of some of Devlin’s antics. "Dev, you’ll be safe." "Matt, you don’t understand. When I was living in Detroit, and well…..kinda gave a VD to a girl from Dearborn. Her uncle put some kind of fat wad down on my ass, and her family in Iraq….well, they’re gonna fucking murder me until I die from it." "Devlin, lemme see if I can handle this when we get to Okinawa. I’m sure you’re going to be fine." Kirsten says to try and console one of her clients. "You’re damn right…..because I’m not going. I….I can’t." "Devlin, you know what…..I don’t want to hear your excuses." Matt says as the cab pulls into the airport.
