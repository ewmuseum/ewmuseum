Five Weeks (Part 1)

21 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/07 * * * * * 5 Weeks Ago The Main Event of Killzone 45 was just moments away, although by looking at Clinton Sage, you’d never know. Thoughts of dismantling Damien Cruz inside the ring were more than likely present in his mind, although right now there was something else garnering his attention. Between his fingers, he held a small golden locket. It was heart-shaped, and when Clinton opened it, he saw the most beautiful woman he had ever seen. Her brown hair spiraled down and seemed to float over her shoulders, framing slightly high cheekbones and a cute, slightly upturned tip of the nose. Her eyes were light brown – the color of sand – and to Clinton they seemed to look out through the boundaries of the photograph within the locket, and latch onto his own. He stared down at the picture for minutes at a time, enchanted. Standing in perfect silence by the door to the locker room was Saul, Lane Stevens’ manager. His hands were clasped together at his waist, and a scar ran across his cheek. "Do you love anyone, Saul?" Clinton asked. There was an uneasy calm in Saul’s voice, "No." Both men sat in the closest thing to silence that a wrestling locker room could provide. The roar of the crowd could be heard as someone did something that pleased them. Soon, once Clinton stepped out through the curtains he would undoubtedly be booed unlike any other. Tracing the small contour of the locket, Clinton Sage grinned. Those eyes, reaching out through the photograph, warmed him. "How did you meet Ms. Thornton?" Saul asked, suddenly hovering right over Clinton’s right shoulder. He hadn’t heard the old man shuffle across the room at all, perhaps lost in the loving pull from Michele Thornton, or perhaps Saul was always careful in everything he does, even navigating across a locker room. ‘The Seventh Sin’ turned and looked up at Saul, who stood there once again motionless. There was no curiosity painted across his scarred face, but Clinton sighed audibly at the admission he was ready to reveal, "We’ve never met. I’m pretty sure she isn’t even aware I exist." Somewhere, deep down, Clinton Sage was sure Saul had already known that. Once again behind them, out in the arena, the crowd roared to life. In only a matter of moments, they would boo. Clinton would make sure of that. Bending over and whispering into Clinton’s ear, Saul’s voice was cold and flat, yet it was exactly what Clinton needed to hear, "Perhaps I can introduce you to someone that could change that." Turning and looking up at the mysterious man, Clinton Sage closed the locket and placed it around his neck. Rising from the chair, he stood and towered over Saul, grabbing the Universal Championship from the table it had been resting atop. "Set it up." Saul nodded. "Don’t tell Lane. Not yet." "Of course, Mr. Sage." "Who won between Covel and Tanaka?" "Mr. Tanaka." Clinton Sage sighed, annoyed. He pulled the locket up, kissed it, completely uncaring if Saul found this uncomfortable, and made his way out through the locker room door. Saul followed a moment later, closing the door behind him as they left. Damien Cruz awaited Clinton Sage in the middle of the ring. In a moment, the crowd would be booing as Clinton Sage entered the ring, and thunderous when he left it victorious, the Universal Championship still in his possession. * * * * * "Oh Yoshi, Yoshi, Yoshi, where have you gone?" Clinton Sage stood before the camera, staring into it with a smile. Behind him hung a black dropcloth with a red Affliction Biohazard logo embroidered on it. The FUSE Universal Championship rest on Sage’s left shoulder. Standing off to the left, halfway in the frame and blending in with the black backdrop was The Scourge. He stood in perfect silence, as still as a statue. His eyes remained unblinking. "Here we are on the verge of our big, (finger quotes) heated match-up, and the only thing that anyone has said about you is that you’re nowhere to be found. Two weeks ago, you barely make an appearance and send your little multiple-personality disorder buddy Timo Bolamba to confront me. He ended up getting a few lucky shots in, but ultimately I spoiled Harry Potter for not only him, but the whole world." Lifting his free hand, Clinton Sage traces the small cut that goes from the inner corner of his nose down slightly to his cheek. The stitches have been removed, but the scab is still in place and the anger is still boiling in the black eyes slightly above it. "And yet, last week you were silent. I figured there would be some sort of attack, be it physical or verbal on your part, you know…to try and swing momentum in your favor. But, you proved your worth yet again, and let the door hit you on the pussy as you left the arena. You fucking coward. Did you honestly expect me to come hunting after you? Was that your plan? To be the guy who refused to attack Clinton Sage, instead driving him batty by…vanishing? The Scourge remained sentient, arms folded across his chest. The black mask revealed no details of the face hidden beneath. The ‘Seventh Sin’, however, had a face painted with disbelief and amusement. He removed the Universal Championship, holding it between the camera and himself, filling the shot completely with the highest prize in all the game. "When you find your balls, you’ll know where to find me. In the center of the god damned ring in Washington, waiting to end any waning hope you once had of becoming Universal Champion. Because, after all, that IS your thing, isn’t it? To build up such glorious momentum, and then when the time comes to use that momentum to advance your career, you do something that everyone has come to find predictable." He pauses, and smiles. "You fail. You run in fear. You hide. Like a prisoner at a German POW camp, when the spotlight comes searching for you, you end up hiding and praying it never finds you. Except you’re a fucking chink, not some American prisoner of war. But I promise you this, when that bell sounds in Washington, there WILL be a war. And much like your grandparents in Nagasaki and Hiroshima, it’s going to be a war you won’t walk away from victorious." Clinton Sage pulls the Universal Championship back away from the camera, once again slinging it across his shoulder. He turns momentarily back towards The Scourge, and we can almost see the eyes hidden behind the mask dart over towards Sage. Clinton turns back, amused at himself. Egotistical, yet confident. "History has a way of repeating itself, Yoshi. The circle of life. You keep running away from the sweet, seductive voice of success like a coward. I keep winning, and proving that I’m the best to ever step foot into a FUSE wrestling ring. Comparing us is like comparing apples and oranges." Clinton pauses a minute, thinking. "Well, more like comparing someone who belongs, and someone who doesn’t." * * * * * This Week The Sage property was vast, quite a few acres from side to side and corner-to-corner. The mansion sat in the far right corner of the property, allowing plenty of rolling grasslands to occupy the rest of the land. For a reason unknown to Smitty T. Duluth, Clinton Sage had a love for thoroughbred racehorses, and kept three of them on his property during the warm months of the year. Two black colts grazed in the grass a good distance away, with a perfectly white mare walking up the hill towards the small stable. They paid no attention to Smitty, who was nestled behind a rather large Oak Tree and didn’t move a muscle. He watched through the large telescopic lens, and listened through the small buds in his ears. They were attached to the long-range microphone that was attached to the top of the lens. Smitty T. Duluth had been approached by Saul, Lane Stevens’ manager, only two days prior with an offer to side with The Affliction. After shooting down the offer over a stack of pancakes, Smitty decided that he needed to figure out just exactly how his only daughter, Michele, had fallen in love with someone so coldhearted. Five weeks ago, Michele wanted nothing to do with wrestling. She barely even cared about Smitty himself, a thought that made his heart ache in his chest. She had always looked for Smitty’s approval, even though she never would admit to it, but she absolutely loathed wrestling. Falling in love with Clinton Sage made absolutely no sense. But here, as Smitty watched from hundreds of yards away, Michele and Clinton lay in the grass of his front yard, watching the thoroughbreds graze. Clinton Sage propped himself up with his elbows, his feet crossed. Michele sat Indian-style, occasionally picking at the grass and tossing it into the air, letting the gentle breeze grab hold of it. Through the long-range microphone, Smitty eavesdropped. He felt vile for spying on his daughter, but god damn it, this made no sense. Something was wrong. How could his daughter completely change her stance on wrestling, fall in love with a man so wrong about life as Clinton Sage? All in five weeks? "The horses are beautiful," Michele started, pausing a moment to turn and look back at Clinton, "What are their names?" Clinton Sage smiled at Michele Thornton, and even from hundreds of yards away, Smitty could see exactly what Saul had told him only two days prior. There was love in his eyes. True love. Even with the warning that Clinton had given Smitty on Killzone49, he didn’t truly believe it. Until now. The air left Smitty’s lungs, and he whimpered slightly. A father defeated. "You can name them, if you like. Anything you want. I’d given them names before, but they don’t really fit their personalities. So why don’t you think about it, and pick three new ones? The white one is a female, so don’t go all butch with that name." Michele burst out laughing, a laugh that Smitty loved. Even though they had a strained relationship ever since Michele was born, he loved her laugh. It appeared he wasn’t the only one. A father is supposed to approve of the men his daughter dates, even through a rough patch at the start of most courtships, where the intentions of the guy are always unknown, a father eventually has to learn to love the man his daughter cares for with all her heart. Not this time. Football fields away, Michele wrapped her arms around Clinton and pressed him down into the grass. She giggled as they tussled on the small hill, the horses grazing only a few dozen yards away. The day was perfect, sunny with a cobalt blue sky overhead. Smitty found no comfort in the day. He had resorted to spying on his daughter, hoping to find some malevolent plan being constructed and executed by Clinton Sage. Instead, he found a concrete foundation in the truth he wished were lies. "Tell me you love me," Clinton started, as Smitty watched. Michele said nothing for a moment, which raised Smitty’s speculation. He adjusted the zoom on the telescopic lens and tried to zoom in as far as he could, focusing in on the couple as they lay on the grassy hill. Michele straddled Clinton, who continued to lay on his back, looking up through his charcoal-black eyes. Eyes that always seemed hungry for light, two little blackholes that opened up into the Devil’s soul. Instead, on this day, they were filled with joy. A cold, unnerving joy. When Michele hadn’t answered the question immediately, Clinton propped himself up on his elbows and cocked his head to the side in curiosity. He waited a few more moments, staring into Michele’s eyes. His lips pursed together in slight disappointment for only a split second. "I am the author." Clinton said. "And I am the book," Michele replied immediately. Smitty whimpered. He knew Michele loved books, probably more than she actually loved Smitty himself. Clinton smiled once again, "Tell me you love me." "I love you." "Kiss me." Smitty watched as Michele gently leaned in, and passionately kissed the man that Smitty T. Duluth hated on this planet more than anyone else in the history of disapproving-fathers-hating-their-daughters-new-boyfriend. How had this happened in five weeks? Five weeks! "Take off your shirt," Clinton ordered, placing a gentle kiss on Michele’s neck and slowly working his way up to her ear, where he whispered, "and let’s make love." From hundreds of yards away, Smitty T. Duluth dropped the telescopic lens just as his daughter pulled her shirt up and over her head, throwing it down onto the grass beside the newly-in-love couple. It grated at Smitty to know that his daughter was about to make love to Clinton Sage, and he couldn’t tolerate this anymore. He couldn’t bear to watch it. An act of passion, for the man Smitty had once thought lacked any semblance of passion. Smitty had forgotten about the buds in his ears, and before he could yank them out, he heard his daughter moan in delight. Fumbling quickly, he managed to yank them out of his ears, but he heard the final request of Clinton Sage, and it drove him even madder. "And I want it loud. Scream your fucking head off." Smitty’s body quaked with anger. This was so unlike his daughter. Something was wrong. Very, very wrong. * * * * * Still standing behind Clinton Sage, off to the left, is The Scourge. Still atop Clinton Sage’s shoulder is the FUSE Universal Championship. And the look across Clinton Sage’s face is still the same: arrogance peppered with confidence. "You see, our careers in FUSE couldn’t be better examples of how-to, and how-not-to. You walk in through these doors, and immediately go after the smallest title this federation has to offer. And even then, you couldn’t beat a real wrestler to get it. Me? I come in and take revenge on the one person who slighted me, and it leads me directly to this fucking championship." Clinton slams an enraged fist against the Universal Championship, still irked at the notion of losing to Jacob McKail. "But that’s not all I’ve done. While you’ve dabbled with Timo Bolamba and Dave Gibson or whoever the fuck else the washed-up wrestle store tossed out into the garbage, I’ve done something in FUSE that nobody else has ever dreamed about. That nobody else has ever managed to pull off with success." Clinton Sage steps aside, revealing the crimson logo of The Affliction. "So go ahead, pretend you’re some great wrestler. Pretend that wrestling Jasmine Drogan makes you some sort of threat to my championship. I’ve built a fucking empire. The Affliction has become the most dominant force in wrestling, collecting championships like they were baseball cards. In fact if memory serves me correctly, ever since our inception a few months ago there hasn’t been a day that gold has been absent from our possession. Be it the PTC Global Championship, or this Universal Championship, or when Lane held the LiveWire belt, or now with The Mexican Devil holding it. If you think you’re even in the same league as me, think again. You’re not even in the same fucking sport." From beneath the black mask, it appears The Scourge is smiling at the mention of The Affliction’s success. Clinton continues, his obsidian eyes wide with rage. But there is an underlying pleasure in this humiliation of Tsuyoshi Tanaka. "When you were cheating your way to victory over a woman, I was busy making the greats in this business tap out. Angelo Deville, Rich Rollins, Doctor Curiosity, Kimbusa. When you were fucking around and blowing chances in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, I was busy seeking vengeance on John Covel and Jacob McKail, managing to beat both of them soundly enough to send them packing from FUSE. Congratulations, Yoshi, you earned your shot at me by beating someone I had already crushed quite easily, both emotionally and professionally." The shark-like grin appears on Sage’s face. "So remind me again why you’re here? You’re a hotshot you just can’t stay hot. I’m a God inside the squared circle. You’re someone who dabbles around with old-timers, hoping to leech onto their past glory because you just can’t create any glory of your own. So, come on down to Washington with those slanty-eyes and a pocket full of tricks to rip this championship away from my hands. I’ll send you back home feeling just like you’ve always felt, an over-achieving failure." ‘The Seventh Sin’ shrugs his shoulders, smiles in a "I told you so" fashion, and storms off to the left of the camera. The Scourge remains standing there, staring forward at the camera through his mask. He nods slowly, and the shot goes dark. * * * * *
