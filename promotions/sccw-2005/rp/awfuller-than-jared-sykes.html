Date: Awful day 'There are generally two reasons people put on masks. The first reason is because they don't want anyone to know who they are. The second reason is because they want everyone to know who they are. These reasons are not mutually exclusive.' -- Sultan Strawberry, in one of his rare serious moments, when asked why he'd put on a turban mask and gone under the moniker of the Smoothie Criminal. ----------------- Our scene opens up today in an inocuous enough setting, a mall. There are malls all over the country, and this one's location really doesn't matter. What does matter is the two people sitting at a table on the outskirts of the mall food court. The first one is your stereotypical hot chick. She's yammering away on her phone, about what lord only knows. Clutched in her free hand is a strawberry smoothie. As you might imagine, this may become important later. Otherwise why the Hell would I, your narrator, bother to mention it? The other person at the table is, presumably, the girl's boyfriend. While she yammers away about lord knows what, he's tucking into a meal of Chik-Fil-A chicken strips, waffle fries, and a peach milkshake. Driving around the girl everywhere she wants to go shopping and carrying her bags is hungry and thirsty work, after all. What matters is that they're both distracted. Both going about their usual routine, while mere feet away, coming out of the bathroom is a man who's going to send their day downhill. Strawberry: One forty niner, one forty niner! This is Rhubarb Pie, coming in loud and clear, I repeat! RHUBARB PIE HEARS YOU LOUD AND CLEAR! Dressed in full spandex ring attire and strawberry turban, one might wonder why the Hell he doesn't attract more attention than he does, especially while he's screaming into a walkie talkie (that may or may not have anyone on the other end). The answer is this mall's in New Jersey (I totally lied about the location not mattering.) That naturally means everyone's baked. Mallrats taught me that. After a pause, presumably to listen to a voice on the other end, the Sultan pulls out a pair of binoculars, using them to zoom in on our soon to be broken up couple at the table, and the woman's bre--err, the succulent, sumptuous strawberry smoothie in her hand. Strawberry: Target acquired. I repeat, target acquired. With that, there's only one thing left to do. Strawberry: IT'S BERRY TIME! Tossing the walkie-talkie over his shoulder (it hits a Cinnabon employee on the forehead), the Strawberry sprints into action. He's upon the poor defenseless bastards before they know what's happening, snatching the smoothie from the girl's neatly manicured hand. She squawks into her phone, then at her boyfriend, but he's too busy with his inferior frozen fruit beverage. Turning on a convieniently placed boombox, which begins spouting one of the late, great Michael Jackson's best known hits...well, let's just let the man himself say it. Strawberry: YOU'VE BEEN HIT BY -- YOU'VE BEEN STRUCK BY -- A SMOOTHIE CRIMINAL! Taking a satisfied slurp from the straw of the sensational strawberry smoothie, Sultan Strawberry spots a servant of order in the distance, approaching on his segway. Slowly, but still. Strawberry: Time to make like a banana and SPLIT! And yes, that is the moonwalk he's doing as he makes his getaway. SCCW, you hired him. Your fault.
