Returning to the A-List

19 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/07 Synopsis - Returning to the A-List A few short weeks ago, "One Take" Carson Riley was denied entry into CineSpace, an upscale club in Hollywood. Carson had grown accustomed to "A-List" treatment, but saw all of that fade away once he joined FUSE. Could a win over an established FUSE veteran help Carson re-establish his name around town? Perhaps. Or maybe, it will only buy him more time before his long-term fate is made apparent. A New Chapter - A Familiar Place: Time: 11:32 p.m. Carson approaches the entrance to CineSpace Nightclub. He is decked out in a black sports coat, a black dress shirt, and a pair of classic fitting jeans. He is alone and walks with confidence and a smile on his clean cut face. He stands at the back of the line before the bouncer motions for him to cut to the front. He meets Carson at the entrance with a hand shake and a smile. It is the same bouncer who denied Carson entry a few short weeks ago. "Hey Carson. Nice to see you." "Thanks, packed crowd tonight I see," comments Carson. "Yeah, we’re expecting some big guests. Word has it Tom Cruise and Kate Holmes might stop by," says the bouncer in a whisper as he leans in towards Carson. "Nice. Haven’t seen Tom in quite some time." "Hey, I wanted to apologize for last time. I feel like such a prick." "It’s cool. You were just doing your job," says Carson, conveying that he truly doesn’t harbor any hard feelings. "You’re the talk of the town these days. That win last week was pretty impressive!" "Thanks." "Well, enjoy yourself." "Will do." The bouncer opens the door and Carson walks in. As he enters, he is approached by Greg, the owner of the club. Greg’s face lights up as seemingly he as also forgotten giving Carson the cold shoulder. "Hey! If it isn’t the underdog kid himself! Mighty impressive! How are you doing Mr. Riley," asks Greg overzealously. "I’m great, man. Just here to have a good time. Maybe have a drink or two." "Anything you want is on me. Anything." "Appreciate it. Hey, the bouncer tells me Kate and Tom might come by." "Yeah, cross my fingers. That’s always good for business. You’re good for business. Not as good as Kate and Tom but good nonetheless," says Greg with a laugh. "Ah, I’m just an average joe." "Not anymore, there’s a buzz. Where’s your sweetheart at?" "She’s in Toronto visiting her family." "Well, you have a good time. If you need a lady friend, let me know. I got some nice girls around here." "Sounds like a plan." "Hey, Jennifer, give Mr. Riley whatever he wants. It’s on my tab," screams Greg at the bartender. Carson walks up to the bar and immediately receives the attention of the lovely bartender. "Heeeeey, I know you. You’re that wrestler." "Yeah, Carson Riley. Nice to meet you." "What can I get you?" "Red Bull and Grey Goose, please." She fills up a large glass cup with his drink of choice and slides it across the bar to him. He lays a tip on the bar. As he turns away from the bar, a somewhat large man bumps into Carson and spills the drink on both of them. The stain from the Red Bull is apparent on the man’s white shirt. Carson grabs a napkin and dabs his shirt dry. The man proceeds to step into Carson’s face and pushes his chest into Carson’s. "Watch where the hell you’re walking, motherfucker," says the man with anger. "You bumped into me, bro," says Carson calmly. "No, you turned away from the bar and dumped your drink on me." "Well, if that’s the way you see it, I’m sorry." "You should be, this is a good shirt, asshole. You owe me a shirt." "I don’t owe you anything. Just let it go, you’re the one running through the club," says Carson, starting to get a bit irritated. "For the record, I know who you are. I’m not scarred of that fake shit you do. I’ll kick your little ass." "Whatever. I’m sorry. Just let it go." "I’ll take care of you. Just wait," says the man as he turns and walks away. Carson shakes his head and begins to walk around the club sipping what’s left of his drink. He stands as the corner of the dance floor and watches the girls doing their thing. He is approached by two smokin’ hot blondes. "Hey, Sexy. My friend thinks you’re cute. Come dance," says one of the girls. "I’m good. I just got my drink. How are you ladies?" "Good. Do you come here often," asks the second girl. "Well, sort of." "What do you mean, sort of?" "It’s a long story. Are you from Hollywood," asks Carson as he drinks a little faster so he can hit the dance floor. "No, we’re in town from Tampa. We’re auditioning for a movie." "Oh yeah? I used to do films." "Really? What kind?" "Adult films," says Carson, ribbing the young beauties. "Yeah?" "Nah, just messing with you. I was a stuntman. But, now I’m in a career change of sorts," says Carson, trying to avoid the topic of wrestling. "Maybe you could hook us up with some of your connections." "Yeah, definitely. So, how about that dance," asks Carson as he places his empty class on a nearby table. Carson seems to be in great spirits stemming from his great treatment at the club. As he grabs both of the girls by hand and leads them to the dance floor, Greg grabs Carson by the shoulder and pulls him aside. "Sorry to do this, Carson, but I’m going to have to ask you to leave." "What? Why?" "Someone said you spilled a drink of him and were being a bit of a jerk." "What? No. He bumped into me and I apologized anyway." "Sorry. I believe you and all but there’s a problem. See, the guy you had the encounter with is Tom’s cousin. He said if I don’t kick you out Tom won’t show." "This sucks, man. I’m having a good time. Look at them!" "Yeah, I have to do it. Business is business." Greg leads Carson out of the club much to the dismay and confusion of the two young ladies accompanying Carson. Just as things seemed to finally be looking up after so many bad breaks, Carson is back out on the street. Was it coincidence or is Carson destined to be snubbed for the rest of his career? The "A-list" has it privilege; however, it seems safe to say Carson is not quite the "A-List" material he once was.
