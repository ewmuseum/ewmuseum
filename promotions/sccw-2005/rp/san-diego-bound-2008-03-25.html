San Diego bound

25 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/2008 The scene opens up just outside the city limits of Las Vegas. Sitting alone on the edge of the desert, behind large wrought iron gates stands the extravagant estate of one Wyld Child Zakk Wylde. Activity around the estate seems to be pretty normal…well at least normal when you have enough money to hire a staff to take care of your every whim. The landscape is meticulously taken care of by a full staff of gardeners, the fleet of exotic cars and Harley Davidson motorcycles taken care of by a team of mechanics that would make a NASCAR team envious. Inside the housekeepers scurry around to ensure that the standards of the house are maintained, the kitchen staff begins the weekly tasks of shutting down the kitchen until Wylde returns from the Pay Per View. Upstairs more staff members are cleaning, others are preparing Wylde’s luggage for the trip. The only place there isn’t any activity happening is at the end of the hallway inside the home office of the Wyld Child. Inside we find Wylde sitting in the darkened room behind a mahogany desk. Lost in thought he stares out the window overlooking the desert. Wylde: Another week has gone by and its time once again to hit the road. It’s nice to just come in here and get away from it all for a moment before the craziness resumes. Wylde slowly rises from his black leather chair and goes over to the set of French doors that leads out to a balcony. He steps out into the early evening air and takes a deep breath. Wylde: But then again I live and breathe this craziness don’t I? After a couple more moments of privacy he takes one last deep breath and heads back inside. A few moments later he emerges from the estate along with his director of household affairs Mark handing him several different packets of paperwork that require his attention. Mark: Please don’t forget that while you are in San Diego you have to take part in that conference call about the expansion plans for overseas. You will find all the info you need about the call in the blue folder. Wylde: Efficient as always Mark…Thank you. Mark: You’re welcome Sir. The airport has been alerted that you are on your way. The flight plan has been filed and you will be cleared for take off as soon as you arrive. Wylde: Excellent. Mark: We all know how much you hate to have to just sit on the jet waiting to take off. We wish to make sure you trip is as pleasurable as possible. With that Wylde disappears into long black limo. While on the ride to the airport he multitasks by returning some personal phone calls and sending e-mails taking care of Wyld Child Enterprises business. As the limo pulls up to the private jet we can see a small gathering of reporters that have been camped out eagerly pull out there tape recorders and cameras as Wylde emerges. They rush toward the plane trying to get Wylde’s attention. Reporter #1: Mr. Wylde……Mr. Wylde has your time back on the road brought up any old injuries or regrets on returning to the ring after such a long layoff? Reporter#2: Mr. Wylde…care to comment on your fellow Black List member Gideon’s lawsuit against FUSE/SCCW? Reporter#3: Zakk….Zakk Wylde!! How do you feel about having to face the same person two weeks in a row…this time with you Strength in numbers Championship at stake? Wylde stops in his tracks and takes a deep breath. Pulls off his Oakley M-frames and singles out the reporter in question. Wylde: How do I feel about it? How do I feel? You don’t know me very well do you? I have the greatest job in the world. When I go to work…people get hurt. It doesn’t bother me if I face the same guy every week or someone different every week. My objective when I set foot into an arena is to put someone down for the 1….2…..3! It’s as simple as that. I don’t have any feeling about it what so ever. If Duluth sees fit that I face the same person the so be it. Besides who says I am defending the SIN Titles at Snake Eyes. Reporter #3: You are refusing to defend the titles? Wylde: How long have you been following SCCW? Like two minutes? I don’t have to be one of the participants in order to defend the SIN Titles. Realistically I defeated one half of the team that we are facing this weekend so you would think that I would be a logical choice to participate but when you have a group like the Black List anyone of us could step in and be just as effective. It could be Gideon and Caleb. It could be Caleb and Mr. W….could be me and Gideon like it was supposed to be last week…or it could even be me and Mr. W…wouldn’t that be a sight? So my friend it doesn’t matter who we send out there we are going to do our patriotic duty and take down these so called home grown terrorists for the second week in a row and continue to make our mark here in SCCW. The Black List is on one hell of a roll and its not going to stop in San Diego. We’ve got huge plans in the works and it doesn’t stop at the SIN titles. Wylde slips his Oakley’s back on and begins to board the private jet. Wylde: You tell Jensen and that overgrown bastard Sameer that the Black List and the Wyld Child are coming to San Diego and Hell is coming with us!!!! Wylde waves to the group of reporters as they continue to shout out questions. As the door the plane begins to close Wylde pulls out his cell phone and reads a text message that just came in. Wylde: Not again!!! Fade to black…..
