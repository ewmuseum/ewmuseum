The jigsaw puzzles

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today "Miss Monday, I appreciate you’re joining us." Special Agent Finn Roberts circled her in the interview room. She couldn’t help but feel nervous. She was unsure as to why the FBI and a detective from Albany would call her downtown to the local precinct in Portland, but the way they spoke on the phone she knew there was a sense of urgency required for her visit. The waiver in his voice when she spoke to him. The way he chose his words. He was being political in every way he approached her. Even the way he spoke to her now, never once looking her in the eye when he spoke but doing so every time he awaited her answer. "It sounded important on the phone." Roberts nodded. Finally, he turned to face her to speak. "It is important, Miss Monday. Very." The older detective, the one from Albany, smoked a cigarette as he sat watching her. One arm folded across his chest and the other leaning in the nook of his wrist. He just drew back and puffed out, like a chubby little dragon without any flame. His gaze wasn’t worrisome, but there was a curiosity about his gaze. She waited for them to begin the good cop / bad cop routine. Then it struck her it might just be something they used in television dramas. "You know why you’re here?" Roberts pressed his palms on the table. It was more a rhetorical question than anything, but it made April feel obliged to answer, the way he left the dead air awkwardly between them. She sighed. "I have my thoughts, Special Agent Roberts. I have a fair idea why you called me in." "Miss Monday-" "Please. Call me ‘April’." "Okay…" he hesitated "…April. April, the reason I-" the little dragon interrupted him with a clearing of his throat, which engaged Roberts in a roll of his eyes "…that WE called you in here is we have some questions." "About Brock," chimed the older detective. "Do you remember me, April?" She squinted, like it helped her unlock her memory banks, before nodding slowly. "Renoir? Renault?" He glared at her. "Renner. DETECTIVE… Seth Renner. Miss, I’ve been asked to assist the FBI with their investigation. This’s very serious business, Miss." Renner held her gaze, hoping to intimidate her only into considering the truth, the whole truth, and nothing BUT the truth. April had no intentions to give them anything less. "I’m sure it is, Mr. Ren-" "DETECTIVE… Renner." Glaring still. "Right. But what does this have to do with me? Why are you calling me in at eleven at night?" Stepping forward, in his tailored New York City suit, Roberts’ face was one glazed with focus. The low lighting cast a shadow on his brow that illuminated the cogs turning inside his head. For a moment, April felt a flutter of butterflies in her belly due to the handsome looks of the New York City Federal agent. "Recently, in Memphis, Tennessee, we recovered two bodies. Females. Mid-twenties. Highway workers. They were found in an abandoned apartment which was recently fled by a local crack dealer, yeah? They’d both been murdered." Roberts bit his bottom lip, like he was looking to see if she gave a response. He let some dead air build between them for dramatic pause. "One of them had a kid, April. Their kid was just two years old. Little boy. Now… now he got nobody. Right shame. Gonna go into the system. The system is flawed in Memphis, April. That kid’s gonna be a problem for someone just like Detective Renner someday all coz his mama had to what she had to do to make ends meet. You know what that’s like, don’cha, April?" Slowly, and unassured, she nodded her head. "That poor kid’s mama got taken away HARD, April. Real hard. I’d show you pictures but I don’t think that’ necessary. Or fair. I don’t wanna give you no nightmares." He smiled, like he was trying to make her feel comfortable again. Roberts was attempting to take her emotions on a roller coaster ride. It was a classic tactic he’d learnt through his years of interrogation. April Monday was no suspect, but she could have some information that could split this investigation wide open. Pulling out a chair at the steel table, Roberts sat. He reached across and plucked Renner’s cigarettes out his shirt pocket, surprising Renner as he did. Flipped the lid open and offered April a cigarette. She held her hand up and waved them away. "I don’t smoke neither. Bad fer ya libido." He shot her a wink. First sorrow. Then comfort. Now, he was messing with desire. "Now, April, I don’t wanna go jumpin’ to no conclusions or nothin’ like that but we think that someone you used to be involved with has something to do with this murder." April’s hand clutched to her chest, like it might protect her heart from bursting out of it. A great heaviness began to weigh inside her chest. Had she been standing she feared her knees might buckle beneath her. "And another in Phoenix. One in Portland. Another in Albany. Do you remember the one in Albany, April?" She nodded. Slowly. Her face grew gaunt and the blood began to rush out of her face. Sweat began to form on her brow and her throat began to close over. She could feel it. Feel her breath begin to struggle through her oesophagus. It began to become erratic. Roberts waved Renner to the door. "Get water!" he barked. Renner sat there, watching April as her eyes threatened to roll back into her head. She began to sway. Roberts thumped the table, startling Renner back to life. "WATER! NOW!" Up rose Renner and he exited interview room D, bursting down the hall for water. Roberts caught her, before she fell and hit her head against the linoleum floor. Awkwardly, he shoved her upright again and into her seat. He stroked the hair gently out of her eyes. She gasped for air. Roberts snatched up the manila folder off of the table and began to fan her with it, trying to generate cool air to relieve her of the overwhelming warmth which was trying to steal her consciousness. As he fanned, two photos shot out of the manila folder. One hit April in the chest and the other hit her in the forehead. The one that hit her chest gently twirled theatrically onto her lap. A Polaroid. One of the Memphis vic's. Right hand removed with one clean cut. Eyes wide and empty, yet the pleading for mercy still resonated in the dilated pupils. The mascara ran from her eyes from the tears which had escaped, leaving long lines down her cheek. Her face was white, like almost all the blood which formerly pumped through her body, had dispersed through the gaping wound that was once where her wrist and hand met. April’s eye locked onto the victim’s eyes and her blood chilled. Her mind spiralled out of control as her dream began to stream to the front of her mind. Everything was placing together. It was all like one big giant jigsaw. What she tried to force out of her mind only seemed to come back and haunt her. Slap her in the face. She kicked out of her chair and began to scream at the top of her lungs. A blood curdling scream that frightened the shit of Special Agent Roberts. He grabbed her by the arms and held her tight to his chest, forcing her into her seat. "WHAT’S WRONG? TELL ME!" Roberts grabbed April’s face between both his hands, forcing her to make eye contact with him. Her eyes wandered. He gave her head a gentle little jolt to bring her gaze to meet his. Struggling to keep it, Finn moved his head to keep his eyes locked on her. Gently, he whispered to her. "What’s wrong?" April’s eyes closed, tears welling in them, rolling down her face and down the nape of her neck, into her blouse. When she opened them, her brilliant green eyes plead with him as she spoke. "Don’t you understand?" Finn shook his head. "He’s coming, Roberts." Roberts shrugged, struggling to comprehend what she was talking about. Understand what she meant. The riddle hadn’t clicked but as she spoke the hammer fell. He could see it fall into place. "He’s coming for me."
