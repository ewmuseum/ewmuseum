Alone With Rome

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-30-08 Since making her shocking debut a few weeks ago on UpRoar, Julissa Minorez has spent most of her time coming up with futile attempts to convince her father that it would be in his best interest to sign over complete control of his gym to her. All attempts have failed, though the attack on his prize pupil came very close to succeeding. As hard headed as her father, Julissa is not one to just give up and walk away with empty hands. She has to convince her employers that their decision to allow her to handle this on her own was a wise one, and this is where we so happen to jump right in. "I understand how all of this may seem, but rest assured that the only thing on my mind right now is fulfilling my end of the bargain." With her tawny brown hair pulled up in a tight bun, her features are plainly visible as they change from various forms of emotion. With her left eyebrow cocked up slightly into a peak, her lip curls up at the corner to form a slight grimace on her once pursed lips. "I know that it is taking a bit longer than expected," Julissa interrupts whoever happens to be on the other end of the line. "I miscalculated a bit on the time frame, but it doesn't really matter because I still have everything perfectly under control." Her eyes widen a bit as a sigh flares her nostrils slightly. "I was sure that attacking Bridden would get him to cave in," she says, her voice a noticeable octave higher than usual. "It may be a little harder than expected, but I can guarantee that I will have complete control of the property very soon." The words on the other end must be confident ones, because the expression on Julissa's face changes once more. Her lips curl up toward her ears at the corners, the blush of her cheeks naturally enhanced by the fullness of her smile. "You have enough things to worry about," she remarks, her hand reaching toward her ear where the Bluetooth unit seems to be permanently attached. "I'm planning on having another heart to heart with my father real soon, so I'll keep in touch." A slight nod of her head accompanies a 'uh huh' response before she ends the call, "Very well, talk to you soon." And with a press of the lighted button glowing beneath her finger, she ends the call and begins to flip through the most recent issue of Women's Health; though her eyes seem to be focused on something in the distance. "Now what the hell can be taking so long." "Now from Los Angeles, it's alone with Rome." A slick beat plays as the camera pans away from footage of Jay Bridden in action against Ryujin, VON DOOM, and Perfect Raymond Pierce. Sitting comfortably in a black suit with a white button up shirt is Jim Rome, his hands folded neatly across his lap as he smiles. "Welcome back, my guest is a newcomer to FUSE wrestling, but is far from being lost in the crowd. He debuted a month ago against a man many are familiar with in the 'Hollywood Hellion' Duncan Drake, and has continued to show flashes of brilliance though coming up short in what little time he has had in the ring; I'm joined by none other than Jaiden Bridden." The image shifts to Jadian Bridden standing in front of a FUSE on ESPN backdrop. The smile on his face makes him look five years younger as he stands there in a teal button up polo shirt with the buttons unfastened. "Jaiden, nice to have you; how are you?" Rome asks from off screen. "I'm good," Jadian replies, clearly ignoring the mispronunciation of his name. "Thanks for having me." "Alright," Jim begins. "I would imagine you would be really hyped up for your first match on ESPN. Now, you weren't around for the last one, but did you watch?" "I did," Bridden answers with a slight nod. "I mean I had been following FUSE off and on for a while, but being able to watch it on basic cable was a good opportunity for those unfortunate enough to not have access to Showtime." The screen divides in two, with each man on either side with their names and locations beneath them. "That's true. Though, one has to wonder, with the Super Bowl looming – a game many believe to be the biggest game of the year, and that's not even including the historical significance of the Hoodie leading his team to a perfect season – how can you convince the general public to tune in if they're not even a casual fan?" "Wow Jim," Jay says, biting down on his lower lip. "You know, that's a good question. I mean, most people would probably be tuning to ESPN to catch up on the latest news on injuries, or what Tom Brady had for lunch, but end up finding us on the screen. It may be strange, but I really think that the everyday guy reading the sports ticker scrolling across the bottom would glance up and watch a really good match going on and just get hooked." "What Tom Brady had for lunch, classic." Jim says with a chuckle, flashing a huge smile as he is genuinely amused by the comment. "Now, you mentioned the matches. You, yourself, are in a pretty big match on the show... Why don't you tell us a little bit about that." "Well," Bridden pauses a bit, just enough to catch his breath and wipe his brow of a trickle of sweat that began to tickle his forehead as it traveled toward his eye. "There really isn't much of a history to my match. Last week I faced the same guy, goes by the name of Mayhem, but his manager tripped me up and cost me the match. I was a little upset, since I felt I had the match under control and could have actually won against the guy." "Now when you say Mayhem," Jim interrupts. "Are you talking about the same guy that powerbombed someone off of a cage and ended his career?" "Uhm," Jadian shrugs his shoulders a bit and shakes his head slightly. "I'm not really sure. I know the guy is tough, and he's pretty big too." "Speaking of big," Jim laughs. "How is it that you decided to get into this in the first place. I mean, you're not really the prototypical wrestler if you don't mind me saying." "No, not at all," Jadian responds with a laugh of his own. "I heard it quite a bit when I first started to train. Only a handful of small guys ever make it big in this business, and while I'm not half as good as some of the big names that did it, I've been pretty pleased with what I have been able to do. Eventually I'd like to hear my name being mentioned among guys like Jonathan Rhine and Clinton Sage, but right now I'm really enjoying the fact that I'm here and I have the chance... you know." "Right," Jim nods his head. "It's funny you would mention Sage. Now I'm not a huge wrestling fan, but I do keep up with things when I know I'm going to be talking about them, and Clinton Sage seemed to be a real jerk. Why would you want to classify yourself with such a man?" "You're right, he was a jerk. No one backstage trusted him, and even less than that liked him." Jay pauses a bit, exhaling deeply before continuing. "Thing is, he's freaking awesome in the ring. The man was talented, and I'd like to be half as good as he is before I'm done." "I can see that. I mean, who wouldn't want to be mentioned amongst someone that held FUSE's top title for so long, not to mention holding PTC's Unified title at the same time." "Exactly," Jay replies with a simple nod. "Well, we're just about out of time, but I wanted to know how you feel going into this match on Saturday." "You know, Jim," Jay says with a good natured smile. "I feel real confident. I know Mayhem is bigger and stronger than me, but without going into a huge David and Goliath comparison, I really think I have a good chance at beating him. The fact that his manager won't be at ringside makes me feel a little bit better about chances too. Then you factor in the whole Jungle Karma thing and I'd say I have a great shot at turning some heads." "Jungle Karma..." Jim pauses slightly. "I take it you're a fan of the show?" "Oh yeah," Jadian says enthusiastically. "I'm an avid fan of the smack off too." "Jaiden Bridden," Jim nods his approval. "Thanks for coming on the show. I'd like to remind everyone to tune in to catch FUSE on ESPN this Saturday at Eight Pee Emm Eastern time, Five Pee Emm Pacific." "I'm surprised you didn't correct him for saying your name wrong." The ESPN logo flashes across the screen as it slowly becomes evident we've been watching it on a television set. The camera pans around to find Benny Minor sitting behind his desk, a huge smile on his face as he shakes his head. Jadian Bridden is sitting across from him, a dejected look on his face as he shakes his head and stares at the ground. "It's Jim freaking Rome," Jadian says defeated. "How am I going to correct him on his own show." "I'm just messing with you, kid." Benny laughs softly to himself, though it appears he is trying to catch his bearings. "Look, I'm gona finish up some stuff here then head to the house-" "Forget that," Jadian interupts. "Why don't you let me treat you to dinner. We'll hit up Texas Land and Cattle for some rib eyes and baked potatoes, what do you say?" "What do I say," Benny answers him, his eyes squinting and his lips curling into a smirk. "Since when have you ever seen me pass up free food, let alone a steak." "Good," Jadian stands from his seat. "Leave all that crap for later, it'll still be there tomorrow." "Alright, just let me grab my coat." Benny stands up as well, moving his seat back with the back of his legs as he does. "Supposed to be getting chilly later." "I know, damn Texas weather; it was hot all afternoon." The two make their way toward the door, Jadian taking the time to reach back and flip off the light as they exit the office. "So, we taking my car or yours?" Benny looks over at Jadian and shakes his head. "No way I'm getting in your car," he says with a chuckle. "I was sick for two days from all that bouncing. I don't know how I ever rode around in a car like that back in the day." Minor pulls his keys out from his pocket and locks the doors to the gym, before the two walk through the parking lot toward Benny's car. "Hey, it's not my fault you're old," Jadian makes sure to elbow Benny in the ribs as he says it. "Besides, there's nothing like hitting the switches on some punk with his radio blasting." "Yeah, what I wouldn't give to be-" The sound of tires squealing against asphalt interrupts his words as he looks up just in time to see a gray sedan barreling toward the two of them. Benny reaches over and shoves Jadian aside just before he flips onto the hood of the car, then up and over the roof before landing awkwardly on the ground with a dull thud. "BENNY!" Jadian screams as he lifts himself up off the ground. He looks around but the car is speeding away and there is no one in the parking lot to help him. He rushes over to Benny as he pulls his cell phone from his pocket. "Benny, BENNY!" Jadian checks on his mentor, careful not to move him for fear of injuring him more as he begins to push the three buttons to call emergency. "HELLO!" Jadian yells into the phone. "My friend... He just got hit by a car. No, the car kept going. Where am I at... I'm at the corner of San Pedro and Oblate. Please, HURRY!" Jadian flips the phone closed and kneels down next to his friend. "I'm so sorry," he whispers. "It's all my fault."
