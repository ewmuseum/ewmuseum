Footsteps Down the Path of Self-Destruction - The Vincent Matthews Story

Will you bite the hand that feeds? Will you chew until it bleeds? Can you get up off your knees? Are you brave enough to see? Do you want to change it? --nine inch nails 'The Hand That Feeds' INTERVIEW - Sven Aldridge, www.heelheat.com Aldridge: Hello everybody, this is Sven Aldridge of heelheat.com, where our motto is 'Being good is okay, but being bad rules!' Don't shoot me, I didn't make that one up. If you've been following wrestling lately, you'll know that it's been a cornucopia for heel fans out there. Just recently, Xavier Kannon put out Jon Rhine's eye with a high heeled shoe, and that was about as low as anyone could go, at least until SCCW's Temptation in Dallas, where today's guest, Mayhem, assaulted a twelve year old boy in the ring, a boy who is dying of terminal brain cancer! Mayhem: Two things. One: he was ten years old, not twelve. Two: When I agreed to do this, that topic was expressly forbidden. Aldridge: Right, right, and that's the last time it will be brought up, just wanted to frame things a little... So, do I call you Mayhem? Isn't your name Vincent Matthews? Mayhem: That was the name I was born with, but I had it legally changed to Mayhem. I had seen some fighters get screwed out of their ring names by scumbag promoters and federation owners and I figured it was easier to change my name instead of break their fingers one at a time until they agreed to sign the rights to my name back to me. Aldridge: Okay, so Mayhem it is! I want to thank you for inviting me into your home here in Phoenix, Arizona. I notice there's a very nice looking sign over the door that reads 'Casa Mayhem.' Can you tell us a little bit about that? Mayhem: I wanted to put 'Abandon All Hope Ye Who Enter Here,' but that's mostly just to keep the Mormons from knocking on my door. I'd been living here...alone for some time, but right around the time Magnum Opus was formed, I figured I'd just let anyone who was part of it move in. I mean, I got the space, right? And I'm not sure, but I think it might have been Dru- Aldridge: Mischief, another member of Magnum Opus, correct? Mayhem: Yeah. Mischief was the one who dubbed it 'Casa Mayhem' and it just kind of stuck. Aldridge: It's a very nice house. Mayhem: Thank you. Aldridge: Speaking of other members of Magnum Opus, we're being filmed right now by...Misfit? Misfit: That's me. Mayhem: Yeah. He's filming a documentary. Or something. But definitely NOT a reality show. INTERLUDE - Footsteps in the Path of Destruction The camera and erstwhile cameraman is wandering down another tiled hallway in Casa Mayhem, Misfit still providing stream-of-consciousness narration. Misfit: So, earlier this morning, Mayhem didn't seem very enthused about The Footsteps Project, and I can't seem to find any other members of Magnum Opus. Of course, as big as this place is, you could hide a marching band in here and I'd never find them. So... let's take a tour of Casa Mayhem! The shot spins, as if we were trying to determine where we were in the maze of hallways. Misfit: Ah, this is where Dru... Mischief is staying now. She moved down here after... Let's just say that Magnum Opus is a symphony out of harmony most of the time and leave it at that. Ooooh, here's her bathroom, shhhh, let's snoop and see if there's anything juicy in here! Again the camera lurches as our first person view tilts to open the door. What is inside is not so much juicy as disturbing. Rosa Dawes, Jimmy's older sister Misery, sits on the floor by the toilet, sobbing. In one hand she has a small mirror, in the other, a shiny straight razor. Long stands of black hair are literally everywhere; on the floor, the lid of the toilet, the bathtub, stuck to the blood covering the woman's hands and running down the side of her face from the top of her ear. The camera jerks again, blurring out of focus as Jimmy sets it down on the vanity top, until the automatic lens compensates and we are left with a view of floral wallpaper. Misfit: Whoa! Sis, what's... Are you okay? Misery: Jimmy, get out!! Misfit: But- We hear the sound of something breaking, and it doesn't take much to discern that Misery has turned the mirror she was crying into into a missile. Misery: I SAID GET THE FUCK OUT!!! More jerking of the video as Misfit grabs the camera and retreats with it. As it so happens, we are treated to a tilted angle shot of the bathroom door as Misery slams it shut, blood and tears streaking her face under a very choppy new haircut. The video rolls, obviously having been paused in its recording, and rolls when the taping resumes, Misfit speaking into the camera. Misfit: Yeah. So. My sis and Mayhem have always had a fucked up relationship. This is the second time around for them. The first time... was pretty much the same as this, Rosie couldn't deal with the anger issues. Despite the fact my sister was in the military, and is combat trained, I'm not really sure she's cut out for this business. She's so sensitive. And they call me emo. INTERLUDE - Lunchtime Confessions Mayhem: You know, this would have been a lot fucking easier a month or so ago. Would have kept me out of jail, would have probably saved that dude you were with a bundle on dental surgery. The woman, whose name still was not Tina Grace, sipped from her mocha latte before placing it back on the black wire grate table and scooted forward to place herself further under the umbrella over the table. 'Tina:' Yeah, I know, and I'm sorry. I'm sorry about that, I'm sorry about last year, I'm sorry about lying to you so much. I don't even know where to start making things up to you... Mayhem eyed a group of young boys inside The Coffee Plantation, busily pointing at him, and started to regret agreeing to meet with the woman who had been his girlfriend at one time. Something about the woman and how she had adamantly insisted she wasn't Tina Grace and didn't know Mayhem in Memphis set his nerves on edge, so meeting in public had been his idea. In the waters he waded in, one couldn't be too careful around anyone with a vagina. Mayhem: You could start with your name. Your REAL name. The woman shifted again in her seat, uncomfortable under Mayhem's scrutiny. Stephanie: Stephanie Sims. And I'll just start back at the beginning of all this. A few years back, I was doing theater in LA, and that's where I met Dru. She and I became friends, but fell out of touch a short while afterwards. So last year, she said she knew someone who was very avant garde, way out, had some experimental 'life theater' thing he was working on and she thought I'd be interested. My role was to be your girlfriend. Mayhem scowled, his forehead wrinkling. Mayhem: So... You don't normally go up to strange scary guys in bars and tell them 'You're going to buy me a tequila?' She laughed, a rich sound that threatened to pull The Path of Destruction back in time eight months. Stephanie: No. He told me where you normally hung out, gave me a pretty quick crash course on your career and wrestling in general, told me you were a tequila drinker. It was totally a set up. Running his hands through his hair, Mayhem thought a moment. Mayhem: And I fell for it. You reminded me so much of... A girl from my past, you even looked like her, but not so much now. Buuuuut...that was part of it too, wasn't it? Cut your hair like this, wear this color lipstick, this brand perfume. She nodded, sipping at her coffee, flinching slightly as he twisted his head to one side, cracking his neck. She remembered it was something he only did when he was trying to avoid getting upset. When he spoke again, it was slowly, and in measured tones. Mayhem: So. Who hired you? Stephanie: He told me his name was Arthur, but it was your manager, Smythe. This caused the large man to pause, obviously having to process this information. Mayhem: Oz? Oz was behind this? Stephanie: What are the odds that he would call with an offer to work for FUSE, on the exact same night I finally convinced you to watch the show with me? That was him I was texting while you were in the bathroom. I was supposed to stay with you and keep an eye on you. More than once Smythe referred to me as your leash. I realized early on that this wasn't an experimental theater thing, it was a con. And I couldn't do it any more. That's why I left. He drummed his fingers on the table, placing events together with dates. Mayhem: So why couldn't you see the con out? Why'd you drop out? Despite the oppressive heat, the woman across from him shivered, and suddenly refused to look at him. Stephanie: I... I really started to care for you. I was faking it at first, but... I knew eventually things would come out, so I left before either of us would be hurt any worse than we were. You probably hate me and think I'm horrible. Mayhem: No. No, I don't hate you at all. You are as much a tool in this as I have been. He reached across the table and took her delicate hand in his, and she finally looked up at him. Mayhem: Hey. This is all so much to take in right now, and I'd like to talk to you some more about it, but I am fucking starving. What say we get some lunch and get out from under this scorching ball of 'Oh Fuck It's Hot!' and get some dinner? You weren't lying about loving steak, right? You're not secretly a vegetarian or something? Again, that laugh, every note pulling on his heartstrings. Stephanie: No, no, I still love meat, the bloodier the better! CODA The woman we no longer know as Tina Grace but as Stephanie Sims shifted in the front seat of the black 1991 Honda Accord, trying to get comfortable as the car bounced along the unpaved road in the Arizona desert. Stephanie: We've been driving for hours, Dru, where are we going that is so important at this hour of the night? The woman driving the car made a 'tsk' sound at her road trip partner. Mischief: It's a surprise, silly girl. If I told you, it would ruin it! Besides, do you have anyplace special you have to be? The blonde in the passenger seat flopped again, reclining the seat back. Stephanie: No, but I was out late last night, and didn't get a lot of sleep today. Mischief: Ooooh, were you out having fun? Stephanie turned to face the redhead, her face illuminated faintly by the instrument lights of the dashboard. Stephanie: I...okay, you have to PROMISE me this remains a secret, right? Drusilla bounced in her seat, more so than the bumps in the road would cause, smiling happily. Mischief: Cross my heart and hope I die! I'm a PRO at keeping secrets, dearie. Stephanie grinned, warming up to the girl talk. Stephanie: Vincent and I were out drinking, he took me to a place called Stoney's. Dru put on her best 'I'm shocked!' face looking over at her passenger. Mischief: So you're the reason Vincent didn't come home that night! Naughty naughty! So...I assume this means you might plan on staying around this time, maybe becoming part of our little family? Stephanie's response wasn't nearly as confident as Drusilla's question. Stephanie: So, it's true Rose and him are having a hard time? I've really missed him, to be totally honest with you. Mischief: You do want to be a part of the family! In that case, tonight is going to be extra special. They drove on in silence for a while, turning on to a road that was actually nothing more than hard packed dirt winding past small sand dunes. Stephanie: Where did you even get an Accord anyway? I don't remember you having one, and a 91 Accord really doesn't seem your style... The Lil' Troublemaker stopped the car close to a clump of scrub brushes and killed the lights. Mischief: I stole it. We're here! Stephanie: Wait... what?! Dru! Dru didn't answer, she had already hopped out of the car and closed the door, making her way to the trunk. Stephanie followed her in the warm desert air. Stephanie: Did you just say this is a stolen car?! Mischief: Don't worry, the 91 Accord is the second most stolen car in America, nobody will miss it. Now come here and give me a hand. By now Dru had the trunk open, and as Stephanie's eyes adjusted to the half-moon lighting the desert she saw Dru pull a pair of shovels out and stick them in the ground. Mischief: Now, the most stolen is the Civic, but that's a hatchback, and as you're about to discover, I really needed a trunk. Now come on, give me a hand. This is important if you want to fit in. Stephanie reached into the dark recesses of the trunk, feeling what she thought were garbage bags, or possible a thin tarp wrapped around something large, lumpy and heavy. As her hands grasped her end and she lifted, she felt something cold and sticky seeping through the tarp, and once she got it into the moonlight... *THUD* Stephanie: AHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! Ohmygod ohmygod ohmygod!! Mischief stood, waiting until Stephanie stopped screaming, hands on hips impatiently. Mischief: Boy, if he was still alive, cracking his head into the bumper like that would leave SUCH a mark. Stephanie's breath came in panicked, ragged gasps. Stephanie: So...this is....body.... Mischief: It's all part of the induction into the family. Most of the times that involved killing someone, I'm just asking you to help bury a body, you're getting off pretty easy, you know? Stephanie: Most of? You've done this.... Who is this? Mischief sighed, kicked the feet of the wrapped body. Mischief: You know that annoying Russian lawyer prick, Yuri? I don't like competition, and he was well on his way to replacing me as Oswald's right hand. And I cannot abide that, not when things are so close... So yeah, I got him drunk on cheap vodka, straddled him for fucking, and bashed his skull in with a lamp. I was originally planning on getting Charley Crisp to help me out as his induction, but he's stopped answering my calls. But then you came along, and opportunity only knocks once... Stephanie stared at the woman she thought was her friend, the woman who no more than thirty minutes ago was giggling and sharing girl talk with her. Stephanie: You're...you're fucking insane! You are a psycho fucking cunt!! Dru visibly wilted as the younger woman yelled at her. Mischief: I...I'm only doing what I'm told! You don't know what she's like, the things she makes me do! Dru began softly sobbing in the pale light of the moon. Mischief: You...you think I like the things I have to do to survive? You think I like setting up these elaborate schemes to ruin people, to destroy their lives??! Dru continued to sob, quiet spasms wracking her body, and in spite of everything, Stephanie moved closer to the distraught woman. Stephanie: Hey, hey, look- Dru's head lifted, a mouth full of teeth grinning as her arm raised at the same time. Mischief: You're right. I fucking love it. *BANG* The .38 caliber snub-nose was a dull black, and Stephanie Sims never had time to realize what it was the redhead was pointing at her. The bullet entered through Stephanie's right eye, popping it and spraying thick vitreous humor outwards. It then ricocheted off the bone at the back of the eye socket, spinning though the brain, liquefying grey matter in it's path. It exited at the base of the skull, carving a bloody chunk out of the spinal column and brain stem on its way. The woman who used to be Stephanie Sims collapsed in the sand next to the already dead body of Yuri Stechanovich. With a resigned sigh, she lifted the woman into the trunk, and with some effort, placed Yuri on top of her. Next came the shovels and Dru paused, pulling out a pay as you go phone and dialed a phone number. Mischief: I'm ready for the extraction. No, just me, she failed. To be honest, I didn't think she was up to it. She let things get too personal. You still remember how to get here? See you in five. The redhead tossed the phone into the trunk and slammed it shut. She walked around to the driver's side of the still running car and reached in, placing it into drive. It ambled forwards, crashing through the brush and dropping out of site down the open mineshaft, one of hundreds that dotted the barren Arizona landscape. Shortly thereafter, a black Humvee with the DSTRUXN license pulled up, and Dru climbed in. Mischief: Thanks for this, but I thought I told you not to smoke those things in here. The man next to her laughed, a thick guffaw as he waved the stump of an unlit cigar about. Driver: Naw, I 'membered. Ain't lit it all night. Wanted ta, lemme tell ya! Fuggheddaboutit, Ms Devonshire. I wanted ta drive Vinnie's Humma since I first seen it! What if this whole crusade's A charade And behind it all there's a price to be paid For the blood On which we dine Justified in the name of the holy and the divine Just how deep do you believe?
