I'll Show You Mine If You Tell Me Yours

undated

Date: ten minutes pre-deadline Only two days after Amy was choked unconscious by Clinton Sage, she had turned on her new friend fiercly - going so far as to spit on his house, which had really angered the nuns going about their work inside. By the time she was at home, the redhead had elected not to speak to her pal - that 'God' fella - until he showed her some sign that he'd been listening in the first place. After being attacked moments before her losing battle on KillZone, her newfound faith had run awfully dry. ''You're asking way too much of me. I threw myself at you, and you didn't budge,'' she said, scratching lightly at scrapes on her collarbone from a Universal Champion's roughly soled boots, ''I can't begin to tell you how much that irritates me.'' The redhead bowed her head. Though she spoke to the skies, her eyes averted themselves to the floor when she continued, ''Everything I said, everything I did... it all fell short. You're the all-knowing one, you already knew I wouldn't be strong enough to tell him. Not then. The time just wasn't right. But you knew that, too, didn't y--'' Because it all occured behind her line of sight, Amy hadn't bothered listening for the sounds of eavesdroppers. Darcy's best friend was usually ambling about, raiding their fridge and avoiding a spat with his wife. This time, though, the intruder was slightly welcome and, although she didn't hear the door creak open behind her, Amy sighed softly when she heard the familiar footsteps and voice interrupt her private argument with a diety unanswering. ''Am I interrupting?'' Still, she couldn't help but be jolted from her ramblings by the instrusion. However, since there was no proof of her holy dealings, she easily recovered from the surprise and the intruder chalked her uneasy tone up to something typical of his new wife, her habit of simply talking to herself to organize thoughts. Amy smiled sweetly as she turned to face him. ''Maybe,'' she said, ''But I wouldn't want to have to kill you, so we'll let it slide for now.'' ''I guess mercy sex is out of the question, then?'' 'Sperm versus egg in an ovarian cage match?'' Amy shook her head, trying to rid herself of the mental image and the memories that the very concept of sex brought up at that point, ''Sorry, really bad timing...'' Darcy's eyebrows arched, ''Weird, usually that's closer to the end of the month.'' ''Not that!'' replied the redhead, punching him firmly in the bicep. During that action, she'd almost slipped up and told Darcy what was really on her mind -- that overdose so many years ago, and what would have been their child, ''I, uhh... 'A Baby Story' was just on, I accidentally flipped to it while I was looking for hockey on the TV. So you're not gonna be able to touch me until those delivery room images are wiped clean.'' She offered him a sad smile, hoping that the diversion to television talk was enough to keep him from wondering why his newlywed partner would be thinking of children otherwise. Then, her mind wandered to the diety she'd just been speaking aloud to and his well-known distaste toward bending the truth, especially on issues he'd already seemingly punished her for avoiding. 'Ah, what the hell do you care?' she thought, hoping for something to be listening to her internal complaints, 'If you're even up there, you already clearly won't let my better deeds affect my outcomes. Another little lie won't hurt.' But there was one flaw in her lie, aside from the fact that God would probably get pretty pissed about it... ''You hate hockey, though.'' Drat. ''I, uhh...'' Amy stammered, suddenly finding herself fighting the urge to toss up some twisted Baptist Hail Mary. Lie number two was ready to dock... ''I meant Dr. Phil. Sorry,'' she explained, cracking her neck to relieve a little bit of the stress pressure, ''My head's in the clouds.'' ... And Lie Deux has anchored in Port Agnostica! ''Either way,'' Darcy grinned, ''I hope you're ready to spar. Time to defend what's ours... again!'' ---------------------- Later... Amy Campbell's lithe figure darted easily in and out of Darcy's striking range. Agility was one of her better points in a fight, as the normally clumsy redhead traded up fumbling and twisted ankles outside the ring for feline grace within it. They normally didn't train in anything resembling a wrestling ring, but sparred in their home's basement on blue gymnastic mats to hone their abilities on the ground -- something that came to be a strong point for Crisis, but one of the weaknesses of his much smaller wife. As per usual, the competetive streak in both forced the couple to match eatch and every one of the other's moves. When it got more heated, they were still using loose grapples and soft landings, but after a purposeful fall onto his back that landed a little too hard, Darcy found himself wincing from the sudden ache in the back he'd long been hiding injury in. ''Everything alright?'' Amy brushed her hair back over her shoulders to properly see Darcy's face below her, ''That looked like it hurt a little too much.'' Darcy got to tell his own little lie in response, ''Just a kink.'' ''C'mere, turn around...'' ordered the redhead, cracking her knuckles when Darcy sat back and obeyed, ''I'm good at this.'' She kneeled behind him and pushed her palms firmly into his shoulders, feeling out the tense energy seizing up his back. But the moment Amy's surprisingly strong, but tiny hands got nearer to his spine, Darcy reeled around and put an immediate stop to the massage in progress. His instincts said to hide the injury no matter what, but the wince in an otherwise strong face gave it away. ''Are you sure you're okay?'' she cocked her head to one side, ''You've been acting really sensitive about your back lately, and it just gets crazier as time goes on.'' It seemed like the jig was up. With the weight of 'holding on' no longer an issue and the status of their relationship no longer in any question, marriage had wisened Amy to some of her husband's secrets. Now, he had no choice but to tell her about the injury he'd been keeping to himself -- the reason he didn't ventue near singles combat, and why he wouldn't allow himself to become a major player in FUSE. ''Look, I've got somethi---'' Little did he know, Amy was caving into her own secrets at the very same time. ''Me first,'' she insisted. Something seemed terribly wrong, because it was rare for the redhead to display such anxiety at nothing more than the idea of speaking to her betrothed, ''You know how you've been talking about retiring from wrestling, poking around at the idea of having kids and all?'' Darcy's eyes widened, ''Actually, I was gonna add a little more to that retiring th--'' ''WHO NEEDS A BEER?!'' From the top of the stairway came a voice that, at the time, was rather unwelcome. Darcy shook his head angrily. ''Johnny.'' When her husband's best friend came barrelling down the stairs with a six pack in hand, Amy seemed to be a split second from beheading him... and Johnny noticed. ''Am I interrupting?'' It was the second time she'd heard it that day. First, from Darcy when she was talking to God and now from Johnny, while she was talking to the only man more important. ''Yes,'' growled Darcy, ''And if you don't mind...'' Johnny smiled, ''Actually, I do. I know Ames lost that big match, and you two have a title defense this week... plus, now that I'm here, you look tense as fuck. Sooo... I brought some cocktails to lighten my favorite boy and ball 'n chain up!'' At the very least, the dolt had managed to breathe a sigh of relief for both Amy and Darcy. Their secrets were safe and their sorrows would be drowned... for now. ------------------------ Five years before... Amy put the bottle down for a moment, stipling what little sense she had left out of respect for what MIGHT be. Long before she considered the existance of God, she was thinking about the possibility of another being who'd be feeling everything she did. With her wits about her, she continued humming along to an older Machine Head song, trying to keep her mind off of little white plastic sticks and the colors that could stain them. Tons of people had kids. It couldn't be that hard. After all, her own mother had eight. ''This can't happen,'' she stammered, ''C'mon, don't be blue...'' She wasn't describing her emotions, unfortunately. Darcy had left almost two months before she sat on the edge of that bathtub, recalling the curious absence of her normal PMS-induced outbursts over the past weeks. She was holding the one test she never wanted to pass, waiting for any sort of result. She hadn't been able to find him since the swift and not-so-painless breakup, and dug deep into drugs the moment he'd been out the door. Darcy had found his way to the rainforests, unbeknownst to Amy, which is why he was virtually untraceable. In the process of finding himself, the crippled former champion had left his home, his girlfriend and, growing among the things he'd abandoned, a complete stranger. That much was confirmed when the little + sign on the stick turned blue. Life, as Amy Campbell knew it, would never be the same. If the 99% positivity ratings of this little pharmacy-brand pregnancy test were for real, she was a few months away from a completely new career. Just as she'd been hired to a new federation, fighting was one of many things she now couldn't do. No booze. No drugs. No unhealthy food. No daredevil job. No father. The mirror smashed under her fist, and the bottle she'd put down only a few moments before the concept of motherhood at 19 became real wasn't too far behind it - shattering against the wall when the redhead flung it. Within a minute, nearly everything in the bathroom was destroyed. Amy found herself clinging her knees to her chest, sitting on the floor against the closed bathroom door. Nothing about anything was right or felt fair in that instant. It was sensory overload. Nothing could have prepared her for the news that she was, in fact, pregnant, even though the signs had been all over the place. Her back hurt, she was eating twice as much as usual -- which amounted to about a full cow and a half a day -- and what little she had for breasts seemed to be swelling up without the aid of surgery. Why did she have to be so easily tricked into bed before she met Darcy? If it weren't for that god damned birth control needle, she would've noticed the missed periods. She found herself wishing that Darcy's mother had used something a little stronger before she concieved, and Amy wouldn't have been anywhere near a mess like this in the first place. Nobody short of a few thousand irresponsible teenagers could imagine how she felt. Moreover, nobody could know. But it was too late for that. Jepharee was knocking on the door she was pressed against. He must have let himself in, as per usual, and heard the commotion of her destroying everything in sight. ''AMES!'' he shouted, obviously assuming from the screaming that it wasn't Amy's normal chemical-induced temper tantrum. In his mind, there could easily have been an intruder behind that door with her, ''Are you okay? Should I call the cops?'' He kept pushing the door, which was starting to ache pressed against her spine, and it caused the redhead's first verbal reaction since she'd looked down to see the blue sign on the pregnancy test. ''I'm fine.'' With the recognition of a caring presence, tears began to drop from behind her lower lashes as she stood up shakily, steadying one arm against the wall to keep her weak knees from buckling while she opened the door for her friend. The room was destroyed. Towel bars were pulled from the walls, the faucets were thrown into the tiling and the floor was washed with droplets of blood from her hand, which still had shards of the mirror attached just under the skin. Jeph could only wonder what drug or drink could've driven Amy to that level of pointless madness. But that's when he saw the plastic device that had been thrown to the floor, in amongst the broken glass. Perhaps it wasn't so pointless after all. He fell over his words as he helped her up. Amy noticed that he was staring at the test while he spoke, ''You... uhhh... you're...'' Jeph mumbled, ''Are you sure?'' They went into the livingroom, leaving the dreaded stick behind, and Amy picked up a paper towel along the way to wrap her bloodied hand in. While they walked, Jeph threw even more questions, the more obvious of which was ''Whose is it?''. That made Amy snarl, ''I would've told you if I'd fucked anyone since he left. I've been too busy sitting around here feeling sorry for myself to bring anyone to bed.'' ''So it -- he or she... it's his?'' ''It's his. My belly belongs to the magical disappearing man.'' At that point, she started to sob. ''... I'm carrying the god damned baby of Darcy 'Houdini' Markson.'' ''He has to know,'' Jeph said. Amy nodded, ''Someday. For now... what the fuck do I do?'' She had no clue. Nor did she have any idea that it would be a half decade before Darcy would be told about the child. More importantly, the girl who loathed the concept of abortion was already assuming the wrong thing -- that the baby would ever see the light of day. Some things just aren't meant to be. Others, like title reigns, drug habits and pregnancies, can be ended any way you want... it just depends on how far you're willing to go. ps. sorry if it seems like i'm phoning it in. bad week! apologies to any readers who might have wanted more of the story revealed. more next time!
