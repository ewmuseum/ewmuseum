Heartaches, Headaches, and Violence

14 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14, 2007 As I lay there that night, with the cool breeze floating in to my bedroom window, listening to the traffic below, I contemplated. My life. My family. My career. Now, I’m still breathing. I eat, breathe, sleep, and shit. Those are positives. I’m not homeless and I don’t piss myself in my sleep. More pluses. I’ve damn near given up on pursuing any kind of culinary goal, and instead have taken up residence in FUSE, where every week brings another loss. Every week brings more Sterling. Sigh. When the one thing you have to look forward to is also your cause of dread, it’s time to rethink your life. I mean, he’s pompous, arrogant, and his mouth throws out more garbage than our President. Yet, week after week I wait. For what, I still don’t know. Revenge? Soon. The feeling of importance? One day. A title shot? Blew that. Hell, a win? That would be helpful. Life’s a joke. I mean, what happens? You’re ripped from your own comfortable darkness, and thrust into the arms of strangers, screaming in protest. Slowly, you grow, learning along the way. You live your life according to its pattern. Sleep, eat, drink, work, sleep, and the cycle continues until death releases you. They say that it’s what you see and learn along the way that makes you become you. Bullshit. Life has nothing to teach other than heartaches, headaches, and violence. No refunds or exchanges. Just deal with it. There will always be doubts. There will always be what-ifs. And then, there will always be these days… A single knock at the door snaps me away from my thoughts, like a rubber band back to the present. I yawn and head for the door, reaching it just as another knock strikes. I open the door, and find Andrew and Melissa standing on the other side, staring up at me. Andrew’s eye is forced half closed with a purple bruise and Melissa’s face is streaked with tears. Not a word was said as the kids came inside. I grabbed their bags and set them in a corner. As I tucked Melissa in to bed her small finger traced the roadmap of scars across my chest. I grimace and turn away, flipping off the light and silently closing the door behind me. Andrew flinched as I grabbed him by the chin, and for that I am sorry. Turning his head, for a better view in the light he pulls away. "She do that?" His hand unconsciously reached for his eye and his head dropped towards the floor. Rage pulsed through my veins. "No. A man did." "What?!" "A man. He brought her home tonight when we were packing. He tried to grab Lissa so I kicked him." He says it as though it’s no big deal. To him, it wasn’t. Little bastard reminds me of me. That scares the hell out of me. It was then that I realized he was staring up at me, eyes eagerly seeking my approval. I fake a smile and ruffle his hair. "You did good kid" Not another word was said as I pulled an old blanket from a closet and he made his bed on the couch. Shortly after flicking off the light switch his breathing became steady and I allowed myself to drift back into the confines of my mind. And just like that, life throws me a curveball. A single man raising two children. Scratch that. A single wrestler raising two children. Even worse. A man involved with quite possibly the most violence crazed lunatic that FUSE has seen. This is where life’s road splits in two. Either path could change their lives, and possibly mine, forever. The one road sends them off to an abusive, unstable home. Essentially repeating my own life. The other, sends them in to the unknown. A life on the road, surrounded by a different kind of violence. How it’s different, I’m not exactly sure. Hell, it may not even be much different, but it makes me feel better. It’s like they say, whatever helps you sleep at night. If I turn them away now… No. That’s out of the question. Oh, I’m sure Dr. Rouge is going to have a field day with this one. Shit. I almost forgot about her. Talk about something that can either make or break a career. Anyway, I’ll have plenty of time to deal with her later. Quietly I scribbled a note on a battered piece of paper and laid it by Andrew’s head. "Went out. Everything’s fine. Don’t worry. I’ll see you in the morning. Keep the door locked. We need to talk when I get home. –Mayson" I slipped out of the house silently, locking the door behind me and slipping the keys in to my pocket. When I walked in to the night I had no plan. No destination. I had only my thoughts to keep me company. Being alone with your thoughts is a scary thing. I’ve finally come to realize that you can only help someone who wants to be helped. Although you can offer, you can not force your help upon anyone. The concept of this tears at my heart. Seeing someone you love hurt themselves, potentially killing themselves is a heavy burden to bear. However, life sometimes gives second chances. A new beginning, if you will. A chance to make things right, although not by the person you wronged. Through someone else. A chance to make a difference in things that you had given up on. This is my chance. This is my time. This is the night my life changed forever. I don’t remember where I went that night, or what I did for that matter. But the day is drilled in my brain forever. The day of second chances. Forgiveness to ones self. The time of change. Forever.
