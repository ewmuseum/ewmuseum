These Are The Times That Try Men's Souls

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The sound of a guitar riff can be heard faintly in the background as we see Luci staring in a mirror in the bathroom of the Orange Lantern club, the look of her face is one of weariness from both the travel and the sleepless nights since the death of Lauren months ago. At least tonight she thought she could relax for one night before she had to help Legion prepare for the Loser leaves match at Sin On Spike. Following a crick of the neck she decides to step outside and rock the night away however she didn’t expect to see during the DJ playing Type O negative’s Black No 1… A girl in purple hair wearing a Between The Buried And me shirt. ‘Lauren? Couldn’t be!’ Luci thought as she headed to the bar to talk to the girl in question, nevertheless the reminder was strong in her mind, maybe it was best to get to know her. ‘Hi! I’m Luci’. ‘Hey, my name’s Laura.’ "Close enough" Luci thought. For now we will leave the women to get better acquainted, meanwhile in a room somewhere in a rented apartment we see Stigmata. The room is in near darkness bar the glow of a television sitting in the corner and a pile of tapes on the floor, the tapes themselves are Best Of Amy Campbell throughout her career. Levi watches intently studying the Dead Aim and the move that put him down just a few weeks ago in the Freetekno, the rage at remembering what happened in Manchester suddenly builds up and a fist suddenly hits straight through the television screen. As Stigmata removes his hand we can see the glass shards causing blood to flow, however rather than doing the more logical thing of wrapping the wounds up he first removes the shards slowly one by one each time getting a sick grin of pleasure from the pain he has just self-inflicted. He also mutters under his breath: ‘The truce was broken, now the pain must flow’ as he removes each glass shard like a mantra programmed into him by both Legion and Luci. The location once again fades into a room where a fan is seated by his computer checking on youtube when e see his mouse pointer hover over Sin On Spike Promo on the account Rev 22:20. What follows is a transcription of the promo itself: (The scene is outside the Prudential Center, we can hear a voice begin to speak.) Legion: It’s amazing how déjà vu can suddenly creep on you so quickly and easily without you at first realising it is actually going on. Last time there was a Sin On Spike event I was up against you James in a falls count anywhere match in which we bled, we fought and I was the victor and look what’s happened. Once again it is you facing me however this time it is a loser leaves SCCW match. Looking back on the past year I realise that no matter what either of us do we always end up back doing this dance whether it be in the land of the New frontier, facing a lackey in the Just Wrestling promotion or in the city of Sin. Throughout this time however the fans have wondered why out of all people did I target James von Doom? At a NFW show last year I gave the answer to be that I was told to take him out by Eddie Mayfield over the risk of being able to compete there. That was exactly what I wanted them to believe at the time, the truth is more deeper than that. I always thought that the mindset of von Doom was one that made this business a joke, a man that was never satisfied with what he already had so he decided to baffle everyone by changing his gimmick more times than a booker can scream rewrite. When he didn’t the respect he thought he deserved he took it out in his own self-loathing world, created characters for friends to be a part of and claimed he was the puppetmaster lulling us all into a false sense of security. I wanted to prove to him that the joke wasn’t funny anymore so when the show they titled Legion and von Doom must die I took my chance and I left you bleeding on the floor begging for mercy and then I read of the retirement. A few months later you came back, now I know retirements are virtually non-existent and I did give you credit for wanting to be punished again in an endless loop but now it is time for the game to end. Déjà vu will hit because once again you will be laying n a pool of blood and once again you will not have pinned me in a SCCW ring. Of course you’re wondering what about Luci and Stigmata well James they’ve been told to stay back. At Sin On Spike it will just be you and I doing the final dance as these next few days to paraphrase Paine will be the times to try men’s souls. Jason Ryan knows I can end careers, you shall know it for the second time. Let the bells begin to toll for the end of James von Doom in a wrestling ring. (Legion walks off camera as the screen pans over to the arena and fades out.)
