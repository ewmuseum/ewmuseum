Business. Pleasure. Stir. Repeat.

3 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/09 "You can go a whole lifetime without feeling anything but skin" – Chuck Palahniuk * * * Vail, Colorado. It was the kind of place that, especially during the peak ski season, catored to people from several walks of life. Yes, everyone from the rich to the absurdly rich had a winter home here, or came on vacation. The Stamm House was no exception. With several menu items over fourty dollars a piece, and aruably the most impressive wine list in the state, it was safe to say that very rarely did a station wagon pull into this parking lot. And if one did, its occupants found out rather quickly that they did not belong. This was, by no means, a casual environement. The lighting was romantic, the waiters were all very handsome, and there was actually a dress code. "I’m going to have to get suit jackets for you and your guests if you are going to join us this evening. We have several colors and styles, surely we could find something that agrees with your tastes." The hostess was impossibly tan, and impossibly thin. Her blonde hair was wavy. To the man she was addressing, she seemed like she was beamed directly out of a glamour shots advertisement. He imagined cumming inside of her. It was okay. "I’ll take blue, my boys here will have pink if you got it," Lane Stevens announced, looking proud of himself. The River Rat was wearing a "GET FUCKED" t-shirt which did not fly with the previously mentioned dress code. Behind him Jadian Bridden and Miles Cavanaugh were dressed just as casually, however not nearly as offensively. Miles rolled his eyes at the color suggestion, while Jadian was too busy talking on his cell phone to notice. The hostess smiled and nodded at Lane, showing her impossibly white teeth. A moment later she left to retrieve the jackets. "How the hell did Jeb get out of this?" Miles asked. "He’s at the flower shop actually…taking care of something a bit more personal for me," was the Warlord’s reply. Miles’ brow raised slightly, but he didn’t inquire further. "This sure ain’t your scene, Lane." "You got that right." "So what’s the deal?" "Alex has business, so that means I got business, which means you got business…ya dig?" Lane replied, spitting his gum out on the floor. "I dig," Miles uttered flatly. The hostess was in sight now. "Whose wearing the gay pink jackets?" Jadian finally asked, and chuckled. * * * If you've ever wondered why women travel in pairs to the ladies' room, this... is probably not it. Hazel eyes followed a swinging door as the tawny-haired woman spun away from the granite sink. 'Clear,' she singsonged as she threw a bronze bolt to lock the restroom. 'I thought that cow was never going to leave. It's like, you're never going to be pretty, no matter how much lip gloss you put on.' The woman who slipped out of the far stall was hardly recognizable as the one who'd gone in. A flowing, wine-colored velvet dress brushed across the floor, a small slit up the side to reveal an expanse of naked thigh. The soft, feminine wardrobe clashed with the hard look in Alexandra Pierce's eyes – even the baby blue contacts that hid her stony eyes couldn't hide the ice in the gaze. 'I don't understand why you're going through all this.' Julissa Minorez crossed back to the sink, tossing her mascara back into her small clutch. When Desade spoke, there was more of a lilt to her voice, shattering the frigid whisper to reveal the smoothness behind the words. 'It's necessary because I say it is.' 'That's not really an answer.' Juli leaned her hip against the granite, her own strapless gown fighting to keep her cleavage contained. 'I mean, I know you have an arsenal, and you're basically a ninja. So why go through all this effort to negotiate with a bunch of guys who'd listen better to fists?' 'Not every problem can be solved with fists. Sometimes, the best way to win a fight is not to have a fight.' Juli sighed. 'I just think there needs to be more door-kicking and less flirting with icky guys.' Alexandra set a small shopping bag down at her feet, tipping a smile to Minorez that seemed almost friendly. 'Don't tell me you're getting cold feet, sugar.' She drew a small box out of the bag, setting it onto the sink. 'Gah, it's just creepy when you do that,' Juli grumbled. 'It's just... Jay's going to be out there and I don't want him to think...' Pierce gathered her bloody red ringlets under a light brown wig cap. 'I'm not askin' you to fuck him, Jules.' Alex's whisper completed its metamorphosis into a soft Southern accent. 'Just keep him busy. Ain't different from what you do with the refs.' She opened the box, easing a shoulder-length blond wig out. 'Guys like this one react better to bein' in control, hon.' Pierce completed her disguise with a pair of rectangular eyeglasses. 'Take my clothes out to the car. You know the signal.' Her heels click-clacked on the tile floor as she unbolted the door, offering a shy smile to the woman on the other side. The man waited impatiently across the restaurant. Alex watched with practiced eyes as he turned his teacup on its saucer, his eyes flicking nervously around the restaurant. She put a little bit of haste in her stride as she stepped closer. A large man in an ill-fitting suit moved to block her, and Pierce drew to a stunned halt. 'Konbanwa, Takeshi-sama.' The Japanese sounded stilted with an accent, and she pronounced it carefully, like she'd memorized it. She made sure to catch the man's eyes as she bowed respectfully. She did not rise as she continued. 'Ms. McCall apologizes for her inability to attend this meeting, but I assure you, I have been given full authority to act on her behalf.' 'I do not deal with underlings.' Takeshi sniffed, imperiously turning away from her as he lifted his teacup. 'I had thought Penelope was interested in the business I can offer her. Apparently, I was mistaken.' Pierce lifted her head, but she did not rise from her position. 'Ms. McCall is very interested in making this transaction work, but she is unavoidably delayed. Please...' The smile she affixed to her lips was a humble one. 'She is offering another percentage point by way of apology for this affront.' Takeshi returned his gaze to her; it was a look intended to make its target uncomfortable. 'Three,' he said mildly into the cup. 'Two,' Desade countered. 'That's as high as I can go. But—' And she straightened. 'I'm supposed to offer y'all another gift, too.' Julissa had that hip-swaying walk down pat, striding up to the table with gum snapping in her teeth. 'This place is gorgeous.' She'd injected some modicum of a Brooklyn accent into her words. She shrugged off a short, bolero-style jacket, tossing it towards the security guard with a smile. 'The scenery's not so bad either.' The man didn't manage to catch it, but bent to gather it as Julissa leaned over the table, one hand extended, nails pained Congo-Line Coral. It was Desade that provided the introduction. 'Takeshi-sama, allow me to present Luisa Olivia Liana Alvarez.' 'Lola,' Julissa corrected as the man took her hand, brushing his lips to her knuckles. 'It's an anagram.' 'Acronym,' Pierce corrected. 'Whatever.' Juli blew a bubble through her wide grin. 'May we sit, Takeshi-sama?' 'Really, Miss...' 'Darwin. Heather, please.' Takeshi's smile wasn't nearly as tight as it had been before Julissa's arrival. 'You ladies are more than welcome. All I ask is that you stop being so formal. It is Taro, I beg of you.' 'Sweet, you're named after those weird fortune-telling cards?' Juli curled into the booth next to the man. 'Can you predict my future?' Takeshi's grin lengthened. 'I am seeing great pleasure in your future.' Desade levered herself into the bench opposite him; her posture was perfect, straight, businesslike. She allowed just the faintest note of irritation to creep into her voice. 'Can we get down to business, sir?' * * * "May I recommend our Terlano Blanc?" the watier asked. The small nametag pin on his white button up shirt said "Michael". Michael was a broad man in his late twenties, seemed to be the athletic type. His hair was dark and spikey. The faint goatee on his chin had a way of making him look younger. "Recommend the shit out of it, my man," The River Rat replied, putting down the menu for a second. Sitting next to Lane, Miles Cavanaugh still studied his menu intently. And on the other side of the booth Jadian Bridden seemed distracted. Michael smiled nervously for a moment. "Its one of our finest italian whites, a refreshing citrus flavor, spectacular minerality. A great pairing with some of our seafood items, especially smoked fish." "Hmm, do you have any Cantina Valle? Looking for something a bit crisper, maybe a bit of spice" Miles inquired, the words sounding natural coming out of his mouth, as if he had worked at a place like this in a past life. "The fuck?" Lane replied almost instantly, staring at Miles like he had seen a ghost. "Absolutely," the waiter said. "Would you like me to bring a bottle?" "You’ll like it, it’s good," Miles justified to his boss. Lane shrugged. "Fuck it, we’ll take two," Lane barked at the waiter. "Splendid. I will be back in a moment," Steven said, and was gone as quickly as possible. There was something so dead in Miles’ eyes, and so alive in Lane’s. Were these men capable of absolutely anything, or was he just paranoid? Whatever the answer was, they put him on edge. "You’re one classy motherfucker Miles, had no idea," Lane prodded. "Eat a penis," Miles replied casually, and buried his head in the menu again. The Warlord turned his attention to the other part of his dinner party, the Ace in the Hole, Jadian Bridden. He followed Jay’s eyes across the room to where his girlfriend was being rather flirtatious with a japanese businessman. She was laughing uproariously. "It’s just the game, my man. Just the game," Stevens offered. "I know. I mean, I trust her…its just weird to look at. You just start to think, if she’s that good at acting, maybe sometimes she’s just acting with me, ya know?" Lane nodded. He understood Jadian’s concern, but couldn’t really relate to it. In his mind, all people were actors. "Just look at ‘em, though. They’re too fucking good at this," Jadian mused. "No shit," Lane commented, and the two shared a laugh. Michael approached the table again, with two bottles of wine on ice, and three glasses. "Would you like me to pour the first glass gentlemen?" the waiter asked. "Does that mean I have to tip you more?" Lane asked. "Oh, no sir I just…" Michael stammered, having never been asked that question before. "Eh, Mommy is giving me extra allowance for this anywho. Pour away chief!" Tenatively Michael reached for one of the bottles, and filled each man’s glass about halfway. "No problem sir, are you ready to order? Perhaps an appetizer?" "Whoa, hold your ponies buddy. I’m just getting acquainted with the menu here. There’s no hurry right?" "Of course not, I’ll be back in a few" Michael said, and was instantly gone again. After he left Jadian finally took his eyes off his girlfriend and looked at The River Rat. "Why do you do that?" "What?" "Fuck with everyone," Jadian said. Miles chuckled. "People don’t make good decisions when they are uncomfortable. And if they don’t make good decisions, I am likely to have the upperhand," The Director’s WMD explained. "But there’s no upperhand here…we’re ordering food at a restaurant, there are no winners," Jadian explained, a slight bit of exasperation in his voice. Stevens shrugged. "Maybe if I call the steak a cold dog turd later we’ll get money off the bill. Maybe he’ll make sure we get bigger portions cause he doesn’t want to deal with a complainer like me. You can always gain an upperhand, my friend. Always. And at the end of the day it comes down to decisions. It’s one thing me and Miss Pierce agree on. If we make all the correct decisions tonight, and things still go poorly…she will not be angry with me. But if I make a poor decision, and things miracously work out anyway…shit, I might be out of a job. I can admire that." Jadian took all that in with a nod, and took a sip of the wine. "This is good, this is really good." "Fuckin’ A right," Miles mumbled, feeling vindicated. "But we certainly don’t agree on everything…" Lane continued, locking eyes briefly with The Director from beyond the room. It was such a brief look, but said so much: things were not going great, be ready. * * * "…I don’t like to act weaker than I am. When I’m strong, I want someone to know it. Which in turn lets them know they are weaker. It’s simpler that way, if everyone is on the same page." Twenty minutes later, behind the Stamm house, Takeshi-sama’s night had taken a turn for the worse. He was up against a dumpster, being pummeled in the rib cage by Jadian Bridden’s fists. Miles Cavanaugh held up the Japanese businessman as the Ace in the Hole went to work. To the left Takeshi-sama’s bodyguard laid face down on the pavement, stirring slightly. "I didn’t…I didn’t know!" Takeshi managed to cry out just before taking a knee to the groin that sent him to the ground. The River Rat squatted down, his face humorless for a change. "The fact that you didn’t know it was his girl doesn’t change the fact that it’s his girl. Pretty sure I saw you put your stinky sushi eating lips all over her hand bro. I mean, goddamn," Steven said. "Goddamn," Miles re-iterated just as Jadian stomped Takeshi’s midsection, causing him to cry out again. "But that’s not even the real issue here," Lane started again, "the real issue is your blatant disrespect of my boss, and her offer. I mean, shit, I’m not a worldly guy…but it seems like a pretty good offer to me." "That offer rules!" Jadian chimed in, suddenly feeling a little self conscious. "I did not mean to disrespect Miss Darwin!" Takeshi exclaimed. "Miss who?" Lane asked, and a second later he understood, "so that’s what it is today, alright." Stevens studied the businessman’s face as blood from his nose was steadily entering his mouth. Maybe we were getting somewhere, he thought. "So does that mean you wish to apologize to Miss Darwin, and make things right?" Takeshi nodded eagerly. "And you want to sign that piece of paper, don’t you?" He nodded again. "Eh, I’m not sure if this deal is really going to work. What do ya think Ace?" The Warlord asked, not turning around to face Bridden. "Yeah, I mean…if he doesn’t really want to do it, I don’t think we should make him," Jadian remarked. Lane smiled fiercely. He liked Jadian’s improv there. "No! Please!" The River Rat nodded thoughtfully, as if he was actively considering all of this. The look on Takeshi’s face, it was the reason he did this. Here was a man that lived his entire adult life thinking he was in absolute control, and it had been so completely robbed of him. At this point Stevens was going through Takeshi’s wallet, looking at pictures as they talked. "This your wife?" Lane asked, showing him the picture he was referring to. "Sister," was the frightened reply. The River Rat leaned in closer, and whispered. "Something like this happens again, and I’m gonna have to fuck her to death, ya hear me?" Takeshi’s eyes got wider then, and Stevens broke into laughter. "Oh, dude, should have seen your face. Just kidding. Not my type anyway," The River Rat mused, and returned the wallet. "Let’s get you nice and presentable like, alright?" A moment later Miles Cavanaugh was pulling Takeshi back up to his feet. And then, for seemingly no reason at all, Lane took a running head start and just punted the bodyguard directly in the head as he was getting up. "My bitchkiller owns!" Lane exclaimed, and high fived Jadian Bridden. * * * It was awkward, to say the least. Most conversations in the restaurant abruptly ceased as Takeshi-sama walked through the restaurant, bloodied and battered. His hair was disheveled and his face was swollen. Behind him walked the three men that had done a number on him just a few minutes earlier. Lane waved at random people. As they arrived at the table, Alexandra and Julissa did not break character. "You poor thing!" Julissa offered. "Oh, my gosh!" the Director said. "Looks like ya took a heckuva spill out there! We can have the waiter bring ice?' "That won’t be necessary, I will be leaving shortly," Takeshi replied, and sat down. Behind him Lane Stevens took a croissant of the plate of an older woman at the next table, and took a large bite. The woman was so shocked by this, that she did not utter a single word. The crunch made Desade look up at her lackey, eyebrows beetling. He replied by winking. Takeshi was no longer ruthless, or charming. He was frantic, and jittery. With his head down the entire time, he put his signature on several documents before abruptly rising from the table and storming out. "Didn’t even finish his steak," Jadian observed.. "Nice jacket," Julissa giggled. The Ace looked down at his attire, as if he had forgotten about it. "Brings out the color in my eyes, don't you think." The restaurant had started to return to normal, conversations were resuming. Stevens stepped forward, and grabbed a green bean off of Takeshi’s plate and dipped it in steak sauce before eating it. This almost made Julissa gag. "Anyway, we’re hittin’ the road…unless you need something else my liege." Stevens bowed in mock humility. "That will be quite all right," Alexandra said. Bits of her coldness slipped into the aw-shucks persona as it became unnecessary. She only looked back for a moment. "Jadian, your increased involvement is something I am quite pleased with." Julissa beamed proudly at this, staring directly at her man. "Glad to help," Bridden replied, having a hard time making eye contact with Desade. She still kind of gave him the creeps. "Show him a good time, Lane," Alexandra said stiffly as she signed her credit card receipt. The humble, sycophantic Heather Dawson was almost entirely gone by the time she rose. "But not too good of a time," Julissa chimed in. * * * Before they had taken the businessman outside, before anything had gone down, they were just three customers sitting in a restaurant sipping wine. "You really believe what the good guys are always saying, about how Alex is going to throw us away in the garbage heap one of these days?" Jadian asked. Stevens shrugged. "We are all in the garbage, my friend…in the shit, rolling around in it. Some of us are just smart enough to know it." The River Rat punctuated that statement by slamming the rest of his glass of wine. The Ace in the Hole wasn’t quite sure what that meant, but he figured the longer he rode with Lane Stevens, there was a pretty good chance he was going to find out. * * * And now, an interlude. It was mentioned earlier that Jeb Stewart had an errand. This was its end result. Alanna Marshall woke with a start at the sound of the doorbell ringing. She’d only intended to close her eyes for a few minutes, grab a quick few moments of rest before she and Lance headed out to the latest in a seemingly endless round of doctor’s appointments. More poking, more prodding, more stand here and turn this way and hold your breath please…Alanna knew, logically, that all of it was needed but damned if she wasn’t getting well and thoroughly sick of it. A quick glance at the clock showed that what had felt like two minutes asleep had actually been closer to forty-five…and that it was getting perilously close to when she and Lance would have to leave for the doctor’s if they wanted to beat the traffic. As she started to stifle the yawn building in her throat, the doorbell rang again. "I’m coming," she tried to yell, her voice breaking around the yawn. She’d been having these moments more and more lately. She figured it was probably just an after-effect of the treatment but she’d make sure to ask the doctor at the appointment. Which she would definitely need to get ready for her. She hated being late and, at the rate she was going, Lance would definitely have to wait for her to get ready…which she also hated. Her mind racing, she began to speak as she opened the door. "I’m sorry I’m running late, baby, but I…" She stopped as two things dawned on her. One was that Lance would have no need to ring the doorbell as he would simply use his key. The other was that the man on the other side of the door was most definitely not her husband. Standing there was a tall, gangly, bespectacled kid who looked all of sixteen. He was carrying a bouquet of flowers which easily looked half his height. "I’m looking for Alanna Marshall," he asked, his voice cracking slightly. Alanna gave a brief wave of her hand. "That’s me," she answered as she took the flowers from the delivery man. After signing for the flowers, she dug into her pocket and handed the delivery man the five dollar bill that she’d managed to find there. With a brief exchange of pleasantries and a "Have a nice day, ma’am," the delivery man had been on his way and Alanna had been back inside the house. There had been a semi-regular stream of bouquets and gift baskets sent to the house ever since news of Alanna’s…condition…had become public. She appreciated the sentiment, certainly, but couldn’t help thinking that it was a bit of a waste. Nonetheless, she placed the flowers on the kitchen counter while pondering whether or not she had a vase they could fit into. She smiled as she looked the flowers over, searching for the card. They were certainly beautiful, she thought. As she picked up the card, her smile quickly froze as she began to read what was written on it… …and quickly turned into a snarl once she had finished reading it. "Oh, hell no," she spat, flinging the card onto the kitchen counter. Thoughts of vases now abandoned, she walked into the pantry and returned quickly with a large garbage bag. Taking care not to spill anything, she placed the bouquet inside the bag before sealing it up and putting it aside in the corner. She’d take it out to the curb later, she thought. For right now, she had other things to worry about. She picked up the kitchen phone and quickly dialed the familiar number. There was only one ring before the other side answered. "Hey, baby. Are you gonna be home soon?" "Okay, I’ll be ready when you get here. I just want to make sure we get to the appointment on time." Alanna glanced quickly at the card still lying on the kitchen counter and sighed. She knew this next bit would not go over well…but she couldn’t keep this from him. She had to tell him. "Oh, and baby…something happened just a few minutes ago. No, no, don’t worry, I’m fine…but we need to talk." The particular reason the Marshalls need to talk is still sitting on the kitchen counter. Written on the rather generic looking square of stiff paper are a few very simple yet rather meaningful words: Get well soon, Lane Stevens
