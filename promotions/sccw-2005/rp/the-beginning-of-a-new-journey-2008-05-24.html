The beginning of a new journey

24 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-24-2008 It was a fair weather day in Toronto ,Ontario ,Canada. The temperature was around 65-70 degrees Fahrenheit with a light breeze that was just cold enough to be pleasant and yet firm enough to let you know it was there. Some of the fair citizens were wearing short sleeves and shorts while some still opted for the long sleeve shirts and blue jeans. It was one of those weather days to make you happy to be alive as some would put it. At a home that was about 20 minutes from the Skydome a young lady of 25 was getting ready for a trip to somewhere ,but where no one knew. The woman had on a white tank top with white bra on underneath ,jean shorts and a pair of Nike Air sneakers and as she tied her hair into a ponytail she thought back to her one of her first matches in Tokyo ,Japan... March 25th ,2001 Tonight was a night that was going to be a great night for the All-Japan promotion! They were going to have 5 different title matches on the card at Budokan Hall and they had acquired the services of a young lady whom had just graduated from a wrestling school in America and passed with flying colors named Tina Davis! The young Canadian was sitting in her dressing room when one of the few female American wrestlers that made the trip came into the locker room and began to speak to the rookie "How you feeling about tonight Tina?" The young woman asked and Tina just smiled as she finished putting on her ring gear for the night "About as good as one can be doing when you are asked to compete in the opening match of one of the biggest cards for a Japanese promotion Rachel. How do you handle first match jitters?" Tina asked and all Rachel could do was shrug which scared the hell out of Tina! "Its not the easiest thing to get out of your system that I'll admit" Rachel began "when I first started wrestling in the States my mentor came with me to calm my nerves and he told me to just 'take it easy and have fun with it' and that's what I did when I competed in the match." Rachel explained and hearing that actually calmed Tina considerably as she finished lacing her boots and turned to face her friend "I appreciate that advice Rachel" Tina began "I know that to these people wrestling is sport and I have to do my best to give them all I have every time I walk out to the ring while I'm here and I think that maybe why I'm so nervous ya know?" Tina asked and Rachel nodded before answering "I hear ya Tina. Yes there are some that dress up in costumes and what not around here but they all take their craft seriously no matter what! You better be ready to keep up with the pace of the wrestler that you are facing tonight or you could be in some trouble." Rachel explained and as Tina finished zippering up her jacket she turned and kissed Rachel on the cheek as her way of saying "thanks" leaving a stunned woman standing in the doorway as she made her way down to the entranceway where her music ,which at the time was "Anything She Does" by GENESIS began to play as she was greeted warmly by the Tokyo faithful as she entered the ring The match was a good one and the crowd never got bored with it as they watched the 18 year old from Toronto and although she would eventually lose to what eventually would become her finisher in the Northern Light Bomb she put up quite a fight against the established vet and held her head high as she saluted the fans before heading back up the entranceway... -------------------------------------------------------- Back to the present day... "Get that headlock clenched in Tina!" Yelled the voice of a young man in his early 20s and as if being controlled Tina did as asked and during the rest of the exercise was able to control her opponent. After Tina finished with her workout in the ring the man that was in charge of the gym smiled at the job his student had done! "How was that Michael?" Tina asked and here friend smiled as she came out of the ring with her workout partner "Good but just a tad sloppy. You have to get used to being able to control the person you're facing a bit better than what I saw today!" Michael exclaimed as he held up the sheet for the new promotion that Tina was working for and showed her the highlighted match on the sheet "See this? This is the first match for you in a place called 'Sin City Championship Wrestling' and there's one new guy in the match and a guy that's fairly established in the company and I'm sure that neither of them will shy away from going after someone whom lets up on them ,so you have to correct those problems and quick Tina!" Michael exclaimed and Tina nodded before going to get a shower and thought about changing back into her "casual clothes" but decided on another option "Hey Michael" Tina shouted from the locker room "Wanna see the new ring attire that I had someone make for me?" Tina asked and Michael gave a half-hearted sure because he knew Tina ,or so he thought and knew that Tina was a pretty simple girl and didn't do that many flashy things with her ring attire right? Not in this case! When emerged from the locker room she had on a sparkling red and white jacket with a maple leaf on the back of it and her bodysuit that she used to wear was replaced by a matching outfit that showed "just enough" to keep the guys wanting more and even some of the women drooling! Michael was pleasantly surprised to say the least! "WOW!" Was about all that Michael could say as he glanced up and down enough to give himself a sore neck and Tina walked over to the table with the match on it and looked it over before speaking again "So" Tina began "I got myself a three way match against two men do I? I sincerely hope that these two men don't think that I'm a pushover because I don't flinch at anyone whom is bigger or allegedly 'stronger' than me. I've wrestled guys 350-400 pounds and shocked the world when I went in and kicked their asses up and down the ring!" Tina exclaimed and Michael liked seeing the fire in his friends eyes "Yeah one of them is called the 'Big Stack' and NO I don't think its cos of what's in his pants but more about some gimmick of being a poker player and another dude named Simon King. Apparently BS has been in the company for a little while now and King is another new person that was signed to SCCW." Michael explained and Tina was still half laughing from the comment about "Big Stack". "That is an interesting nickname to call yourself isn't it? When you call yourself that and you associate to his gimmick it would naturally mean he's a winner right? The funny thing about poker is that you can be on a roll one minute and the next you are walking out in a 'Wife Beater' and your jeans...if you're lucky and in Kennedy's case I think he just might go bust!" Tina exclaimed as she turned to go back to the locker room and change into her street clothes and as she had a strange feeling about something ,so she stopped and spoke again "One more thing Michael" Tina said and Mike was all ears waiting to hear what Tina had to say "Stop staring at my ass! Its not a gentlemanly thing to do." Tina explained calmly as she went into the locker room and got changed... Class IS in session!!!
