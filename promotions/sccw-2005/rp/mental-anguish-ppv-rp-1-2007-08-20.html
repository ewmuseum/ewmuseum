Mental Anguish (ppv rp#1)

20 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/20/2007 After his dream, over a week ago, El Diablo had struggled to sleep. He had become somewhat of an insomniac, as every time his eyes closed he saw his dead brother Jorge’s body on that cold hospital slab in Juarez, Mexico. For a reason unknown to him, the memories of Jorge’s death were beginning to stir within him, and it was happening at perhaps the worst time possible, with War In Washington fast approaching. With a LiveWire Title defence against his nemesis Jason Cruise, and the Tower match, El Diablo needed all the rest and relaxation he could get in the lead up to this event, but that wasn’t going to happen. So, with Angela by his side, El Diablo went to his hometown of Puebla, Mexico to try and gain clarity of the situation. MONDAY 20TH AUGUST 2007 7:30PM "Are you sure this will help?" asked Angela. Wearing her light blue, figure hugging dress with matching high heel shoes on, Angela was making sure she’d be able to cope in the Mexican heat. "Of course it will. I need to work things out in my own mind, so that I’ll be able to sleep and be ready for the War In Washington," Diablo responded. Looking across at his beautiful American wife, with her red hair flowing behind her and sweat glistening her face, El Diablo was beginning to regret bringing her with him. "So where are we going?" she asked. Walking across a dusty road, El Diablo really had no idea where they were going. He just needed to be in familiar surroundings to help sort his head out. "I haven’t decided yet. I’ll let you know when we get there," he replied. Sighing, Angela couldn’t take walking much longer, not in the high heels she was wearing. "Well I hope we get there quick," said Angela. With big black bags under his eyes, to go with his blood shot green eyes, The Mexican Devil was clearly exhausted. He hadn’t shaved for a while and it showed with the black bristly stubble covering his face. "I’m sure we will," he muttered to her. The Mexican Devil was scarcely in the right frame of mind, but with lacking sleep and remembering past traumas, he really was mentally exhausted with things. This mentality meant he looked a mess, not only with his eyes and facial hair giving the appearance that he was a homeless man, but his choice of clothes wasn’t much to be desired. Wearing a torn, faded white t-shirt and blue denim jean cut-offs with brown sandals, El Diablo didn’t appear to be himself. "Will here do? My feet are so tired, I think they‘re going to drop off," Angela exclaimed. Luckily for her, El Diablo was just as tired and they sat down on a brown bench. The weather was fine as they sat down for rest, with the sun still out, sending out blistering temperatures, but beginning to go down as night time fast approached. "I need to think for a little while," said The Creator of Controversy. Nodding, Angela shuffled closer to her husband and placed her head on his shoulder, indicating she may drop off to sleep. "Thanks. It shouldn’t take long, and then we can go to a hotel or something," he remarked. Thinking back through the years, El Diablo had to focus back to when he was just fourteen years old, which was nearly twenty years ago. SUNDAY 2ND OCTOBER 1988 Inside the Diaz family home in Puebla, Jose and Ofelia Diaz were absolutely distraught at losing their eldest son. Sitting in the dining room, at the wooden table, they were both in floods of tears, not knowing what to do with themselves. "I can’t believe it," blubbed Jose. At 48 years old, a father shouldn’t lose his son, and Jose was inconsolable about it. For Raul, it was strange to see his father in this way, as he was 5’10 and 243lbs, bald but with a bushy black beard, so to see the big man breaking down like this, it was hard to take. "Why us?" Ofelia, tearfully said. Ofelia (47) was the physical opposite of her husband, standing at 5’5 and weighing 135lbs, but their physical differences didn’t equate to anything when grieving a lost son. She had long black hair and was very slender, she was so heartbroken it looked like she could physically break. "It’s not right," said Jose. Standing up from the table and heading into the living room over the uncarpeted wooden floorboards, Jose could see his other son Raul, with his short black hair and skinny build. He was sat on the floor, knees up around his head, in the corner of the room crying. "What are you doing?" Jose asked sternly. Looking up from his spot on the floor, Raul saw his father standing by the old black and white television they had owned for years. It’s not like Raul had to search for his father though, as other than the television in the room, all they had were several cushions in which to sit on, no furniture. "WELL WHAT?" shouted Jose. The shouting only made Raul scared and unable to answer coherently. "I’m cry--, just ups--," Raul mumbled. Shaking his head in disgust, Jose marched over to where Raul was sat, dragged him to his feet and slapped him harshly across his face. Falling onto the wooden floor, Raul was clearly shaken as he clutched his face and cried. "You have no right to mourn, Raul. If you’re going to cry, then you cry about that," Jose expressed himself. Surprisingly, Ofelia remained in the dining room, despite hearing what had happened. TUESDAY 4TH OCTOBER 1988 Having recovered from the incident a few days earlier, Raul was feeling a little better about things. Jose and Raul were sitting at the dining room table, as Ofelia was in the adjoining kitchen, cooking something up for dinner. "HURRY UP," shouted Jose, rudely. After the slapping incident, Raul and Jose had barely spoken. Raul was angry about being slapped, while Jose was livid and saddened about the death of Jorge. "IT WON’T BE A MINUTE," Ofelia yelled her response. Pondering what they would be having by the smell, Raul was looking forward to eating. They weren’t destitute, the reason he hadn’t eaten was because he simply couldn’t eat after what had happened to Jorge. He had been too upset to eat, but he was ready now, because he was famished. "COME ON, I’M STARVING," shouted Jose, once more. Remaining silent, as not to anger his Dad further, Raul’s stomach rumbled. This rumble caused Jose to turn and look at his son, with a stern look on his face. "What was that?" asked Jose. Raul dreaded to answer his father. "I’m hungry," Raul replied. Laughing, Jose’s large frame was shaking the wooden dining table, much to Raul’s curiosity, as he couldn’t understand why he was laughing so loudly. "What’s funny?" he queried his Dad. Stopping laughing, Jose again had a serious look on his face. "You being hungry," stated Jose. Bewildered, Raul didn’t understand why that was funny. But as his mother walked into the room, with just two dishes of chilli con carne, he soon understood. "You’re not eating," Jose said calmly, as his dish was placed before him. Ofelia soon sat down opposite her husband, and began to wolf down her food, as Raul sat watching them, licking his lips as his mouth watered vigorously and his stomach churned, digesting the imaginary food. "Why don’t I get to eat?" he asked his parents. Silence. His parents carried on eating. "WHERE’S MINE?" Raul shouted. Again, there was no response. It was evident that they were ignoring him, as the aroma from the chilli just made Raul hungrier. "FINE," he shrieked. Getting up from the chair, Raul headed towards the kitchen, only to be halted. "GET AWAY FROM THERE AND GO TO BED," Jose roared at him. Fuming and distressed, Raul stormed out of the room and headed to bed. WEDNESDAY 5TH OCTOBER 1988 With his stomach rumbling to a loud beat, Raul was starving. The family had no more food in the house after the last of the chilli con carne was eaten the night before, and Raul hadn’t any money to go out and buy any food and he certainly wasn’t going to go out and beg for food. He was better than that. "I’m so hungry," he slurred under his breath. Alone in his bedroom, which he had shared with Jorge, Raul was too afraid to leave the room in search of food. His mother and father had been very harsh to him in recent days, by not allowing him to eat, shouting at him and then ultimately ignoring him. "Why are they doing this to me, Jorge?" Raul asked, perhaps in search of a response from beyond the grave. Of course, there was no response as Raul clutched his stomach. He was so hungry that he was in fact having stomach cramps, leaving him in absolute agony. "I need food," he mumbled. Despite being very quiet, there was soon a loud banging noise on the closed red wooden door, followed by a voice. "KEEP IT DOWN IN THERE," shouted Jose. Shocked at his treatment, Raul broke down in tears. Lying down on his bed, Raul wept into his faded white pillow, trying not to sob so loud that his parents would hear. "What’s wrong with me?" his muffled voice said. Sitting up carefully, to avoid banging his head on the top bunk, he let the tears roll down his youthful face. Raul scanned the room for a moment. "It’s all Jorge’s," Raul whispered. Indeed it was, as the white painted walls and black carpet were Jorge’s choices, even the bunk bed was Jorge’s decision. Not to mention all the posters covering the walls, the white wooden chest of drawers and a rusted metal radio. The only decision Raul had with the room was sleeping in it, and even then it wasn’t much of a choice as there was only the floor to sleep on as an alternative. "I don’t feel well," he mouthed, without actually saying the words. In a sudden motion, Raul heaved onto the chocolate black carpet, but having not eaten for several days all he was throwing up was the vile stomach bile. After a few moments of this, he had nothing left in him to throw up and was dry heaving. Unfortunately for Raul, his father had heard him and launched himself into the room by almost knocking the door off its hinges. "WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING? IF YOU’RE GOING TO BE SICK, DO IT IN THE TOILET," screeched Jose. Marching up to his 14 year old son, Jose yanked him by the arm off of his bed and dragged him out of the room, before throwing him into bathroom. The bathroom was very small and only consisted of a filthy looking toilet and a bucket. There was no carpeting or tiling, just the wooden floorboards, which Raul, who was now crying his eyes out, was lying down on and feeling very unwell. "Can’t this torture be over?" he whispered. THURSDAY 6TH OCTOBER 1988 Having survived yesterday’s ordeal after finding some chocolate bars hidden in Jorge’s chest of drawers, it was time for Jorge’s funeral, in the local funeral parlour. It was certainly a morbid day, especially as it was a family only funeral, and Raul really didn’t feel a part of the Diaz family lately. "Is this everyone?" asked the funeral parlour director. The director was a small man, only 5’3 and weighing 145lbs, with short grey hair and long sideburns. He was 57 years old and looked very distinguished as he dressed in formal black for the funeral. "Okay then," the parlour director stated. The funeral director was standing at the front of the small, enclosed room. The atmosphere was very melancholic, with all the windows behind black curtains and the lighting in the room dimmed, presumably purely to set the mood. At the front of the room was a podium where the director was currently standing, with Jorge’s cheap wooden coffin just behind him, ready to go into the incinerator. The rest of the room was full of seats on either side of the aisle in the middle of the parlour. "Does anyone wish to speak?" the director queried. Sitting down in the front row of seats were Jose, Ofelia and Raul Diaz, all looking very solemn as they prepared to say their goodbyes to Jorge. Jose was dressed in a nice black, rental suit, while Ofelia was in a long black dress. Raul, however, was not given a rental suit and was instead in a torn white t-shirt and black sweatpants. "I’d like to," said Raul. Standing up, Raul was prepared to go to the front of the room and for once speak his true feelings about his older brother. Only, he was stopped by a tug of the arm. "You can’t go up there dressed like that," commented Ofelia. This remark had both angered and upset Raul. "Well you knew I didn’t have a suit and didn’t buy me one," he remarked. Jose stood up, and peered down at his son. "What use is a suit for a 14 year old?" questioned Jose. As Ofelia let go of Raul’s arm, he began to walk towards the podium. "DON’T GO UP THERE," Jose shouted. Raul stopped dead in his tracks and turned around to look at his father, who was red in the face with anger. "IT WAS YOUR FAULT JORGE DIED," he yelled at his son. Taken aback by that comment, Raul could now realise why his parents had treated him so poorly over the last few days. "IF YOU HADN’T HAVE WANTED TO GO THAT WRESTLING EVENT, HE NEVER WOULD HAVE DIED," Jose continued to shout loudly. Not in the mood to confront his parents, Raul charged up the aisle and ran out of the funeral parlour, not knowing what to do or where he would go. MONDAY 20TH AUGUST 2007 8:15PM Angela was asleep on El Diablo’s shoulder as he jutted forward, rudely waking her. "Hey, what was that for?" she moaned as she gained awareness of where she was. Scratching at his thick stubble, El Diablo was still tortured by the days following Jorge’s death. "Sorry, I just remembered my childhood and how terrible it was after Jorge died," he stated. Angela sat up and wrapped her left arm around her husband. "Isn’t that how it is when people die?" she tried to reassure him. But Angela wasn’t aware of El Diablo’s starvation and mental degradation after Jorge’s death, culminating in his parents blaming him for his death. "No it’s not Angela. They treated me like crap, like it was my fault. I WAS ONLY 14 YEARS OLD. I needed my parents, but they practically abandoned me," said The Mexican Devil. Hugging her husband tight, Angela had no idea any of this had happened to him. "I’m so sorry. Why didn’t you ever tell me about this?" Angela attempted to comfort him. Grateful for her caring nature, The Master of Manipulation just wasn’t ready to explain everything to her. "I don’t know. But why is it coming back to me now? I have some of the biggest matches in my life coming up and I need to focus on them," he said. Not knowing what to say, Angela just kept on hugging him tight. "I have to defeat that cretin, Jason Cruise if I want to keep my LiveWire Title and then be fresh to win in that Tower match. I deserve a double victory in Washington and I’m not going to let my past stop me. I have to confront it and defeat my demons," The Mexican Devil remarked. Angela let go of him and looked into his green eyes. "What do you mean by that?" she asked. Smirking a devilish smile, The Creator of Controversy knew what he had to do. "I’m going to Jorge’s grave tomorrow," he answered her. Would going to his dead brother’s grave really give The Mexican Devil the clarity he is looking for? Or would he remain sleep deprived and in a state of mental anguish? Either way, El Diablo had to know and finally put the past behind him, for good.
