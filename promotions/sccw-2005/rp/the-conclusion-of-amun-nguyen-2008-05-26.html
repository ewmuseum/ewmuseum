The Conclusion of Amun Nguyen

26 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/08 The moonlight caressed her skin as she slowly walked back to the Nguyen estate; her needs filled and ready to enter the final phase of their plan. Cleo slowly entered the house without making a sound. She climbed the stairs deliberately making the heels of her boots click against the marble. She wanted to them to know that their fate was coming. She enjoyed instilling fear in their hearts. When she reached the top of the stairs Vassago sat patiently outside of the prison. She knew by the look in his eyes that she couldn’t have chosen a more suitable gatekeeper. Cleo nodded to Vassago, "It’s time." She said this without emotion and slowly opened the door to face her tormentors. "Now that I have all of my ‘favorite’ people in the same room," adding a sarcastic tone to the word favorite, "I can finally end all my pain and misery." Cleo said shooting daggers at her restrained father. A hysterical laugh caught Cleo off guard and she spun to meet her sister’s burning eyes, "End all your pain and misery," Delylah hissed using the word your as an instrument of torture, "I guess the whole world has just treated you so badly hasn’t it?" Cleo narrowed her eyes knowing that she no longer had the fear card to trump Delylah. She stood there in complete shock as her sister ranted and raved at her. "You’re the daughter of a once very respectable doctor. The best in the Gynecological field of medicine, and being the best won him a daughter that he couldn’t produce because of his inadequacies." Every word dripped with venom as Delylah closed the distance between her and Cleo. "I know what you have done to him," Delylah hissed as her gaze locked on the body of Elias Nagatti, "If you hated him so much why did you make him that way?" "Shut up, you have no clue," Cleopatra clamped her mouth shut in an effort not to show her sister she found her weakness, "My childhood wasn’t what it should have been." Cleo looked down at her adoptive father and mumbled low enough that she thought no one would hear, "No clue what it was like to not exist." An abrupt end came to the battle for control when a shriek pierced all of their eardrums. Nebt-Het had woke up, her eyes filled with horror. She looked at Cleopatra and she began to tremble she searched the bed beside her with her eyes making sure her husband was still alive. Her frightened eyes shot to Delylah as she fumbled on her words, "I… I know what she has planned Del." Nebt-Het finally spat out the words and then went into a trance trying to suppress the vivid memory of her dream. "You only think you know what I have planned mother. I only let you see the half of it, I wanted you to see your precious daughter die." Cleo spoke each word deliberately filled with hate and envy. "I know even if you would have kept me she would still have been your favorite." These words made Nebt-Het flinch with pain. "I… I would never…." "Shut up!" Cleo shouted making her mother retreat back into her shell of timidity. "I don’t need to hear your words to know the difference between truth and fiction." Amun let out a slight moan that caused Cleopatra and Delylah to snap their heads in his direction. Cleo was the first one to regain sight of her mission and took Delylah out with a chop to the throat. Delylah dropped to the floor grasping her throat. Cleopatra cackled and met Paul with a boot to the groin as he charged her. As soon as he dropped to the ground she assaulted his temple with a vicious kick that sent a shut off notice to the light that lit his eyes. She dropped to her knees beside her gasping sister. "You never stood a chance in outwitting me sister. Now it is time for you to sit and watch as my plan unfolds." Cleo whispered softly in her ear, "It is time for our parent’s to repent for their sins." Cleo moved toward the body of Elias Nagatti and slowly undid his bonds. He crawled to his knees, and slowly climbed to his feet. His eyes rested on Delylah’s face, showing that her life force was a drink that he would savor. Cleo pointed toward their father and Nagatti was on the move. Delylah tried to get to her feet, she had to try and stop him. Cleopatra kicked her in her ribcage, a loud crack resonated through the room and Delylah was back on the ground. Cleo’s face filled with satisfaction and she stomped the stiletto heel of her boot into her sister’s hand. She had to ensure that Delylah was not a factor in their father’s demise. Cleo yanked Delylah up by her hair, locking her arm around her throat so she could see Elias standing over Amun’s body. He watched his prey with intensity, his eyes only flickering away to see the warm body of the now unconscious Nebt-Het. "Do what I have brought you here to do father. You show your forgiveness for the sins you committed against me, your maker." Cleo emphasized the word me making it clear that she was the victim. Delylah tried to yank her head away, not wanting to see Amun’s canines pierce the tender flesh of her father’s neck. Cleo held Del completely still, "You will watch this," Her tone filled with a regal tone, "I command you." With those three words Amun Nguyen’s fate was sealed. Elias’ fangs pierced through the skin and into the carotid artery. The blood sprayed into his mouth as he sucked every last ounce of the vital sustenance that kept him roaming this world. A self satisfied smile spread across Cleo’s face as she saw the blood covered face of Nagatti emerge from her father’s neck. He stood next to the bed looking at Nebt-Het with hunger in his eyes. There was a loud thud that turned both Nagatti’s attention to the far corner of the room. In a split seconds time Delylah freed herself from Cleo’s clutches with an elbow to the bridge of the nose. Paul flew through the air hitting Elias with a shoulder in the side. Elias landed on the cherry night table shattering it. Paul acting on instinct picked up a shard of the wood and plunged it into his black heart. Fear, sadness and shock flooded Cleopatra’s eyes as she bum rushed the door quickly leaping into Vassago’s arms. Vassago held her frame close to his body as he reached behind her locking the door to the prison once again. "I need to sleep Vassago, we have to leave this house now." Cleo told him rashly, "I need to get away from them… now." The word now almost sounded full of fear. "They killed him… they killed my father." A single tear zigzagged down her cheek. "I will have Rubis clean up the mess. You get yourself as far from here as possible and I will catch up." Vassago told her coolly, his voice always exhibiting complete control over any situation. Vassago watched her voluptuous body quickly descend the stairs and exit the front door of the house.
