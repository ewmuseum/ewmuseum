A New Mission

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/4/07 Jacque’s mind whirled with questions as he drove towards his mother’s home. After the episode when he and Dianna were there he insisted that she stayed home with the kids. This was something he had to do by himself. In all honesty he was afraid of what he might find in his father’s files. He knew his dad kept paperwork on every detail of their family. "Maybe just maybe there might be some clues as to what my father wants me to set right in there." Jacque thought out loud. The same dream plagued him every night. The distraught look etched on his father’s face haunted him. His father had always been the calm, cool, collected type and never really showed any signs of distress. That is what bothered him the most. When he pulled up in front of the house he just sat there staring at the grand house and large yard. Memories of his childhood filled his heart. All the barbeques and family reunions that had been held here would be no longer. It was only a matter of time before his mother passed to the great beyond and joined his father. Jacque quietly opened the front door to his family’s plantation house. He knew that his mother was at a doctor’s appointment this morning so he had time to investigate his father’s office. The things that spewed out of his mother’s mouth left him wondering what the hell she was ranting about. There had to be something in his mother’s words that his father wanted him to know. He slowly walked through the living room to the door that was his father’s office. He slowly opened the door and flipped on the light switch. The mahogany desk was covered in dust, as were the metal filing cabinets. He felt wrong going through his father’s personal records but he knew that the answers had to be inside. When he pulled open the first drawer it let out an ear-piercing moan of protest. Jacque thumbed through the files to see if anything stuck out. Nothing looked out of the ordinary so he decided to pull the file labeled with his name. He took the file to the desk and sat down in his father’s high backed leather chair. He blew some of the dust off before placing the file on the shiny mahogany top. He flipped through old report cards, shot records and the typical stuff that most parents kept. He perused through his medical records recalling all the broken bones and how they were accumulated. When he found nothing in his file that looked abnormal he tried to decide whether to look in his mom or dad’s file first. Something drew him to his father’s file when he went back to the cabinet to replace his. Jacque looked up at the ceiling, "I’m sorry dad but I have to know." He slowly pulled out his father’s file and began to thumb through it. There were papers inside that dated all the way back to when his father had joined the military. "This is going to take a while," Jacque thought out loud and sat back at the desk. Finally when he reached the end of the folder he came upon a set of family court documents. Jacque thought that this was weird because he never knew of any reason for this type of paperwork. The files were dated December 17, 1979. He began to look through the court documents out of curiosity. When he did he felt the sick taste of stomach acid rise in the back of his throat. The papers were the same as the ones he had brought to Keegan to sign. His father had signed off all parental rights to a little girl named Ellisa Willett. Jacque’s jaw dropped and he felt faint. His father had done the same thing Keegan had done. This meant that he had a little sister somewhere that never knew his father, their father. This must be what his father wanted him to find. With the papers in hand he exited the office and ran head on into his mother and brother. "What the hell is this all about mom?" Jacque exclaimed. "She is no condition to answer questions Jacque. She hasn’t had a lucid moment since you left here yesterday." His brother Gerard told him. "What the hell is that you got any way?" "Take a look at what our father did." Jacque said handing him the papers with a disgusted look on his face. Gerard slowly read the papers and just looked at him blankly. "Where the hell did you find these?" "In dad’s filing cabinet." He said flatly. "Can you believe that we have a sister out there that never even knew we existed or dad for that matter?" They watched as their mother slowly ascended the stairs with her head hung low. As if she knew what they had found. "I can’t believe dad would do a thing like this." Jacque shook his head disgusted. "Ya know bro you really can’t throw stones when you live in a glass house." Gerard told him matter of factly. "I would NEVER EVER give up custody of my child!" He screamed, "That is just unnatural." "Yeah but you asked Keegan to do that. YOU were going to forcibly make him do that if need be." Gerard said pointedly. "There is a big difference in the situations dear brother. Keegan is a killer, a monster. Our father was not." "Maybe dad had his reasons the same as Keegan did." Gerard told him softly. "I will never be able to forgive dad for this Ger, there was just no excuse for it." Jacque spun on his heels and ran out of the house. He slammed his fists on the steering wheel in frustration. Trying to figure out why his father would do such a thing. Jacque did what he had to do with Keegan. He was an addict and a convicted felon someone that didn’t deserve a child. He just couldn’t understand why his father wanted him to find this of all things. This made him change the way he felt about his father in a way. He lost some of the respect he had for the man. He had always instilled the value of being a good father in him since he was a child. "It takes a man to be a dad Jacque. Anyone can be a father it takes someone special to be a dad." His father’s voice echoed in his head. "Yeah right dad. If that is the case why did you do what you did?" With all the questions whirring in his head he started to get a killer migraine headache. As soon as he got home he climbed into bed and pulled the covers over his head. He slowly drifted off to sleep only to be visited by his father. "I drew you to those papers for a reason Jacque. Even the greatest of men do things that they regret in the end. I thought you of all people would understand why I would do what I did." His father told him. "I need you to understand. It will be pertinent in the future that you come to an understanding of why I had to give up your sister." Jacque tried to scream back at him but he couldn’t speak. He wanted to understand his father but he just couldn’t right now. Flashes of his father’s past flew through his mind as his father gripped his arm. "Please Jacque I only did what I did for you, your brother but most of all to protect your mother." Jacque saw all the torrid events of his father telling his mother of the affair, the child and his decisions. His mother’s face was filled with grief. He wanted to comforter her but he knew this was just a dream. "Please Jacque you need to make things right. There are things about Keegan that you don’t know. Things that you couldn’t imagine." Jacque jumped and his eyes snapped open at the wailing of the babies in the next room. His body was covered in sweat and he was filled with a new need to know. He had to find out what his father was talking about he also had to find his sister. How he would do this he didn’t know but he would find a way. First thing next week he would go and visit Keegan and then he would find his sister. He needed to find her; needed to let her know she had two brothers and a father that was sorry he had given her up.
