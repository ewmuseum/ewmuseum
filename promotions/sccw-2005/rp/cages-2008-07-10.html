Date: July 10, 2008 You wired me awake And hit me with a hand of broken nails You tied my lead and pulled my chain To watch my blood begin to boil But I'm gonna break I'm gonna break my I'm gonna break my rusty cage and run - Soundgarden 'Rusty Cage' June 26, 2008 - Memphis, TN - Memphis Police Department Jail The distance from Pyramid Arena and the Memphis City Police Jail was roughly three-quarters of a mile. This was a fact that surprised Mayhem when he was brought in on assault charges the night of the June 26 Temptation show and quickly shoved in a cell. If he listened hard, he could almost imagine he heard the cheering coming from Pyramid Arena. 'RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!' Jon Rhine just came out and was all 'Aw shucks' and adorable. 'BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!' Desade just did something sneaky and underhanded. '...' Gibbo was left speechless by something Eugene Ware said. Mayhem looked around the tiny room as he lay on a steel table that served as a bed, at the featureless grey concrete of the closet sized call. Swinging his feet off the edge to sit upright, he voiced the first of two questions: 'Who the fuck was the first person who thought of making a shitter out of concrete?' After he watched the water jet out the hole in the concrete toilet on its way to freedom post usage, he sighed and ask himself the more important question: 'What the fuck is taking them so long? If Oz was here, I'd be out of here already.' As the man born Vincent Matthews dropped his considerable bulk back onto the steel slab, the door opened almost on cue, and a guard stuck his head into the room. 'You must be some kinda important fella, Mister Mayhem. Someone bumped you to the head of the line, got a judge to set a pretty reasonable bail and paid it for you. You're a free man for the time being.' Mayhem had stopped correcting them when they referred to him as 'Mister Mayhem,' it was as if their minds refused to process that Mayhem was his first, last and only name. He went through the familiar process of collecting his personal belongings and signing the necessary paperwork that returned him his freedom. This was not the first time Mayhem had been placed in a cage against his will. With a grin, he pushed his way through the dingy green door into the waiting area, smiling as he saw Rose and the rest of Magnum Opus waiting for him. He made his way towards them, ready for a quick reunion... ...only to be intercepted by a thin balding man in a rumpled suit. 'So, thees is the Mey-him which causes Oswald to call Yuri Stechanovich and wake heem up, sayink 'Geet my Mey-him out of jail cell. Money is no obsticle!'' Mayhem's head tilted like a dog listening to a high pitched sound. Something about this man's thick Russian accent and the way his hook-shaped nose wrinkled as his spoke seemed familiar... 'Stech... anovich? Yuri... Yuri the Stick?' Mayhem's eyes widened in recognition. This time it was the stranger's turn to tilt his head, subjecting Mayhem to closer scrutiny. 'Vinnie? Vinnie Violence! Comrade! How long has it been!?' As the two men shared a hug, Misfit turned to his sister Rose and mouthed 'Vinnie Violence?' All Rose could do is shrug. May 5, 1986 - South Central Los Angeles If you were a typical American male and it was your sixteenth birthday in 1986, odds are your parents threw you a party, renting a local rollerskating rink where you awkwardly held hands with the object of your teenage lust after opening your new Nintendo Entertainment System. 'Yo, Vinnie! Man, this motherfucker gotta be twice your fucking age. I hear he killed a man in an Underground fight in Compton by stomping on his throat!' Vincent Albert Matthews did not grow up like a typical American male. He gave his fellow gang banger a glare before he looked back across the dirty, fenced in basketball half-court at the scarred African American known as 'Mr. Brownstone.' His real name was Malcolm Stone, but he was an enforcer for a gang that handled more heroin traffic than nearly all the other gangs combined. On the side, he fought in The Underground, a brutal and often deadly bloodsport that the larger criminal organizations often used to settle differences when they didn't want to engage in open war on the streets. 'You there! Vinnie Violence! I would have a word with you.' Vincent made his way over to the fence where the beak-nosed man was speaking to him. He was every bad stereotype of 1980's California, complete with a slick ponytail on his head and wearing a loud floral shirt open halfway to his navel in order to show off his gold chains better. 'Are you knowing who I am, Vinnie?' The young man nodded, flexing and stretching a body already huge and musclebound for one celebrating his sixteenth birthday. 'Yeah. You're Yuri the Stick, Vlad the Impaler's hired shark. What the fuck does the Russian mafia want with me?' The Stick smirked. 'Mister Vladimir Androsk understands your mother owes a sizable debt to some less than savory individuals. Mister Androsk offers to settle your momma's obligations... providing Malcolm Stone never fights again.' 'So what? You want me to kill Brownstone?' Yuri chuckled, an oily sound in the warm night air. 'If you wish to take things to such extremes, be my guest... durak.' The fight was short, and brutal. When Stone had heard he was fighting a sixteen year old white kid, he hadn't taken it seriously. Less than two minutes after the door to the small basketball court had been chained shut, Malcolm Stone twitched on the asphalt, his spine shattered from being rammed repeatedly into the metal pole that help the hoop aloft. Vincent didn't take Yuri's offered hand of congratulations as he walked away from the court, instead grabbing the Russian by his gold chains, holding them in a bloody hand and pulling him close. 'You ever call me stupid again, Stick, and I will snap you like your namesake. Da?' June 29, 2008 - Memphis, TN - Memphis Zoo 'Thank you, Charley! You didn't have to stand in line in this horrible heat just to get me food!' Charley Crisp shrugged at Mischief, handing her the plate. 'Aw, shucks, ma'am, it weren't nothing! One chicken breast and asparagus plate, as requested. Man, food at the zoo has come a long way from when I was a kid.' Dru looked at her plate, cocking her head slightly, not responding. She pushed the piece of poultry with her fork, staring at it until Charley spoke again. 'Earth to Drusie, come in Mizz Chiff! Penny for your thoughts?' She suddenly snapped her head up, regarding her date seriously. 'Did you ever wonder if the food they serve at the zoo might not be an exhibit that failed to perform, or was untrainable? Who is to say that marmoset doesn't make an excellent stew, or maybe this was a parrot who wouldn't perform.' Charley settled, shoveling nachos into his mouth, 'That was SO not what I was expecting you to be thinking.' Dru held the breast aloft with a fork and began to squawk. 'Polly doesn't want crackers! Polly is sick of crackers! Polly is a Shakespearian trained thespian!' Charley had to cover his mouth in order not to spray nachos all over the table. Suddenly, Dru slammed the breast on the plate hard enough to snap the handle of the fork off. 'YOU DO WHAT YOU'RE FUCKING TOLD! IF WE SAY YOU WANT CRACKERS, YOU EAT THE FUCKING CRACKERS! AND DO YOU KNOW WHY?' Charley flinched back in his chair, blinking. Other zoo patrons, mostly parents with children, reacted in shock, most of them picking up their food and hurrying away. Dru grabbed the breast with her hand, squeezing it so hard it oozed grease and seasonings between her fingers as she brought it close to her face, snarling, 'Because you are replaceable, and you taste yummy as well.' With that, she bit what would have been the head off, had this been an actual bird, and tossed the remains on the plate. After she swallowed, she took a handful of napkins and methodically cleaned her hands. 'Do you know why I like you so much, Charley?' Her voice was low, and Crisp hesitated as he leaned closer to hear. 'I feel like I can be myself around you. Like you're never judging me... all the time, judging me... They. All. Judge. Me.' Suddenly, she was all smiles again, 'I don't know about you, but I've had about enough of the zoo. All these cages are bringing me down!' Charley silently put their plates in the trash barrel as Dru waited for him. She practically skipped as she took his hand and they left. 'Plus, it smells like rhinoceros poop here! This has been the most fun I've had in DAYS!' Charley kept an eye out for zoo security as he steered Dru towards a gate. He filled the uncomfortable silence with smalltalk. 'So, uh, yeah, what were you doing the other day that was this fun?' 'Oh, you know, Girl's Night Out stuff, dress up, talking about who has the bigger dick. I can't tell you more, we had a pinky swear on it!' July 09, 2008 - Memphis, TN - French Quarter Suites Hotel Mayhem slammed the door behind him, startling the rest of Magnum Opus as he entered the room. 'Ok, it's official. I'm sick and fucking tired of Yuri. He's EVERYWHERE. I go to breakfast, he's there 'Are you wanting the cheese of cream, comrade?' I go to the pool 'In Soviet Russia, sun screens you!' I take a shit, and he's popping under the stall 'Are you having the toilet paper, babushka?' I expect him to be in the nightstand when Rose and I go to bed 'Are you protected?' I'm glad Oz got him so quick and got me outta jail, but it's starting to feel like...' Mayhem stopped, a look of realization spreading across his face. But it was Dru who finished his thought. 'Like Oswald doesn't trust us, and Yuri is here to babysit us?' As Mayhem nodded, Rose added, 'Ok, yeah, Jimmy and I were taking bets on whether or not he was ex-KGB, because it TOTALLY feels like he's spying on us.' Dru grinned, 'You just let lil' ol' Mischief handle this. I'm sure I can distract the zoo keeper long enough for the animals to get out of their cage. And when Oswald comes back, we'll have to see if Yuri really was here to keep an eye on us, and if so, we'll need to decide what to do about it.'
