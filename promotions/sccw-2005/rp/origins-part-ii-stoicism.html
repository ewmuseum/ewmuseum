Origins Part II: Stoicism

Date: Today Sometime before Equilibrium The location given was somewhere that both parties knew they would be safe, away from prying eyes, away from the gossipmongers who would try to start a similar scandal akin to the events concerning Amy Campbell and Alexandra Pierce on the last Temptation. Granted these two had made it a bit more clear of their ‘friendship’ but given the history of one the location for this meeting had to be done away… The two people in question: one goes by the name of Luci4, the other has gone by many through her life… The Orchid being one but most fans of SCCW willl know her as Savant or by her name of Lauren Fox. They have just returned from their first ‘date’. But this story is not about the date in question, more about clandestine meetings, yes they agreed for the ‘date’ to be just the two of them but one had to get some answers about the other from the closest ally they both had. 3 hours before *Knock Knock* A male voice can be heard from the other side of the door: "Who is It?" "It’s Lauren, Luci’s not there is she with you?" The door opens to reveal Legion in the room. Legion: No, she decided to leave for a few hours, for reasons I will never understand she wanted to get a new outfit and get hair done, the usual things, probably because her last beau always wanted the opposite, then again he turned out to be and believe me when I say in the most Twilight Zone situation possible, a android. Lauren smiled a bit, in that slightly genuine, slightly WTF way whenever you hear a story’s that seems outlandish. Legion: that part always leaves people with that look… that’s this business I suppose. Now I’m gathering you are not here for small talk. Given what you are about to do is something I know Luci’s been looking forward to you came here to find out more. Lauren: What’s puzzled me for weeks is how you mentioned that she could be more crazier than Cozen Legion: Right, I guess it’s time you learnt how I did about the ‘episodes’ that she can have… [u] The morning after the night at Club Nephilim [/u] She woke up in what could be only described as a basement masquerading as an apartment, her mind didn’t feel fuzzy but it was nice of the guy to offer a place to stay. The weirdest thing to her was the fact he didn’t try to be the usual chat up guy you find at nightclubs, he just walked into another room, said goodnight and let her sleep. Footsteps could be heard and for some reason she found herself upright ready to attack, she had a feeling in her mind that it was the man from the club but her impulse suddenly led her to lunge at him as soon as the door opened knocking him to the ground, fist raised about to punch. Legion: Hold on a second, what the hell are you doing? You remember me? Gabriel from last night , come on. Let go now. Laura: I’m sorry, don’t know what came over me. I just get these impulses sometimes that just happen. Legion: hmmm, you know I had a feeling, you kept looking around like someone was after you throughout the night. Something happen to you? Laura (lying) Nope, Legion: Right. You know the reason I was at the club last night had a motive to it. There’s something, more importantly someone I’ve been needing to help me fight against a few things… Laura: Like what? Legion: the problem is I can’t quite tell you unless you fit in the plan that’s the thing, you seem to work of a different mind set than I do, tell me Laura have you ever heard of Stoicism? Laura (looking exasperated): Great, I end up meeting a fucking Scientologist. Legion: This is far from that bullshit. It’s about suppressing emotion, making sure they don’t get the better of you, however I think you may be the opposite which could give us the element of surprise. Laura: You’re crazy! Legion: Says the woman who has in her bag some books… something about a balance of the world- Legion suddenly gets attacked again by Laura. Laura: YOU WENT THROUGH MY THINGS?! Legion: Yes ‘Laura’ I did… It’s about time that you worked with me here… I think we need to talk about this ‘balance’. Back to The Present Legion: That’s also how I knew some info about Winters… but there’s one thing I still don’t get, why did Alex freak out? Anyway soon as the date is done let me know… phase one will be complete. (And then the ‘date’ happened… but that’s for another day.)
