Date: Not Future or Present ------------- People often make the mistake of judging a man by where he ends up. Man is not built in one day; therefore, judge him accordingly. Aren't the path he travels, the obstacles he overcomes, and how he carries himself the true test of a man? ------------- Greensboro, North Carolina Kelly Masters glanced up from his bowl of Total, sprinkled with raspberries, at Nikita, her figure scantly clad in black lace Victoria Secret underwear and a black tank top. She was a sight to behold, but that's to be expected from an ex-model. Life had certainly been arduous as of late, and having to look over his shoulder whenever he went to the grocery store or gym grew vexatious. Nikita's presence made it all bearable; perhaps, they weren't soul mates, or even compatible, but she was a great woman (not a bad lay either). Kelly pitched in around their duplex occasionally, but Nikita, who temporarily veered away from modeling, generally handled all the household chores. He'd often thought of love, and whether or not he'd had those feelings toward her. The decision was ultimately that he loved her, but more so in a sense of convenience. 'Kit,' Kelly chirped, his index finger beckoned her to join him. He pushed his breakfast to the side and pivoted his chair toward the advancing Nikita, a provocative smirk adorned her strawberry lips. She seemingly glided across the room, her long blonde hair swayed like a pendulum. She straddled his legs, then eased her hips down upon him. 'That's better,' Kelly said with a satisfied grin. Nikita wrapped her arms around his exposed upper body, leaned in slowly, and gently kissed Kelly's neck. He ran his arms down her figure, as he'd done so many times before. Moments such as this, passion and raw sexuality igniting a lustful flame, kept Kelly and Nikita together. Bzzz ... Bzzz Lips locked passionately, both stole a glance toward the distraction. The once still cellphone, resting on the oak table, had erupted in vibrations; while the task at hand continued, Nikita reached out, and groped around to find her phone. She finally halted the festivities, did a double take at the caller's number, and then popped up to her feet. 'I've got to answer dis,' Nikita informed him, planting another kiss on Kelly's lips. She walked into the living room, phone answered and to her ear. Meanwhile, Kelly furrowed his brow. 'Bullshit,' he grumbled to himself. He stood up and watched as Nikita, still occupied, headed down the hall and out of sight. 'So much for that,' Kelly murmured. He grabbed his cereal bowl and poured its contents down the garbage disposal. His morning plans had changed much to his dismay. No worries, he'd simply head over to 'Arrows Family Gym' a bit early, maybe catch a quick lift before he started his late morning classes. After putting his dishes in the sink, he spun around and made way for his bedroom. As he rounded the corner, he nearly plowed right over Nikita, no longer scantly clad, instead attired in a white blouse and knee length skirt. 'Whoa,' Kelly managed while sidestepping his girlfriend, 'where you off to, little lady?' He hooked her arm with his as she passed by, and spun her toward him before she even realized they'd almost collided. 'I 'ave to go out for a bit, Kelly,' she replied, glancing up at him with a smile. 'Alright, well I'm headed off to AFG and probably won't be back until seven or so. Thinkin' maybe we could head down to Jack Asters' and catch a bite, how's that sound?' Kelly asked, lifting his eyebrows at the end. 'Good, let's plan on dat,' Nikita answered. They embraced in a kiss, one that Kelly expected to be brief, but instead was a long, drawn out, yet steamy kiss. She broke away and left Kelly in the living room, who despite the exchange carried a furrowed brow. He noticed details, always had. The look and kiss she gave seemed strange somehow, and left Kelly slightly perplexed as he walked into their bedroom. Per usual, his gym clothes were laid out on the bed; he smoothly transferred them into his mesh gym bag. The groan of the wood floor, along with footsteps, meant that Nikita was back and much too quickly. 'What'd you forget?' Kelly asked, sticking his head out from behind the doorframe, only to be surprised at what he saw. Two things. First, the gleaming, silver barrel of a gun. Second, the large man holding said gun. Although brief, the glimpse he caught was memorable. The man had slicked back, light blonde hair with a red streak running down the middle. His face was expressionless, dead like those who crossed paths with him. A lit cigarette dangled from his mouth, the end glowed an ashy red. He, also, wore aviators and all black; everything from his shirt to shoes were black, the absence of color. How poetic. His life, like the color black, was absent of color, or meaning. He killed, lied, and lived without companionship. Amongst all the darkness and discontent were two angelic eyes, shining brightly through a wasteland of death. Those eyes were mesmerizing, enough to make a person stop in their tracks. Except he was wielding a gun, enough reason for Kelly to pull his head back as a bullet ripped through the bedroom window. Kelly somersaulted over the bed, yanked open the night stand's drawer, and produced his deceased father's gun, a Sig Sauer 9mm, commonly used by Navy SEALS. Expeditiously, he leaped into the master bedroom's bathroom, slammed the door shut, and locked it behind him. His mind raced as slow, but steady footsteps progressed down the hall. He drew in a deep breath, his best attempt to calm his nerves. Good thing Nikita had gotten out beforehand. Wait a minute, instantly the gears in Kelly's head began turning. 'She got a phone call, had to leave, and now I'm getting shot at', all the pieces of the puzzle were falling into order. She betrayed him. She left him for dead. The people that had been looking for him, had certainly found him. 'Nikita, you miserable cunt,' Kelly growled. ------------- ------------- A Few Months Earlier; Toyko, Japan 'For the love of God, please stop!' No such luck for Kelly, the hotel room phone continued ringing, apparently uninterested in God's love. His deep and well-deserved rest had been interrupted prematurely, with no end (to the ringing) in sight. Ripping the sheets aside, he stood with a groan. Navigating across the caliginous hotel room proved unwise, his outstretched arm managed to knock over, and, judging from the sound, shatter the porcelain lamp that sat near the phone. Doing his best Helen Keller impersonation, Kelly fumbled around in the darkness, his hands finally grasped the ever elusive receiver. 'Hello,' Kelly greeted, half-dead and hoarse voiced. 'Jesus, you sound horrible!' was the caller's reply. Kelly instantly recognized the voice belonging to Paige Allen, his good friend from Greensboro. Paige was twenty-five, worked at Smith, Stratton, and Feingold, the biggest law firm in Greensboro. She'd met Kelly through mutual friends; the two meshed well, and quickly became best friends. It was idiosyncratic duo; her bubbly personality and his steady, serious disposition should adverse, but that was never the case. After Kelly split, however, the pair struggled to speak bi-weekly. 'Well,' Kelly started, only to be interrupted by a yawn, 'it's two in the morning Paige.' 'Oops,' Paige giggled, not quite apologetic, 'I forgot about those silly time zone differences!' Kelly, much too exhausted for banter, shook his head in his palm. 'Sooo, Brady overhead something interesting today,' Paige continued, her words slightly stammered. Her tone suggested Kelly already knew what she'd been told by her brother, Brady. Kelly, however, wasn't as engaged by what she'd heard; instead he'd keyed in on her speech. 'Wait a minute, what's wrong with you? Are you drunk?' Kelly asked, his interest peaked. 'No, why would you think - okay, maybe,' Paige flip-flopped. Kelly frowned, not quite what he expected to hear. 'You never drink, Paige. Well, not never, but not very often. What's the scoop?' Kelly pushed, Paige acting out of her norm had him meddling. 'There's no scoop, and I do to drink! Especially when I'm celebrating!' Paige screamed, her voice getting higher and higher. Jubilation blended with an excited, high-pitched yelling spree was vintage Paige, but yet, something seemed amiss. 'And what, exactly, are you celebrating?' Kelly continued prying, determined to crack the case (slow down Hardy Boy). It was not in his nature to play twenty questions, but his relationship with Paige allowed for such behavior. Kelly chose his friends very carefully, blocking out many that tried to get to know him. Her persistence and honesty had won him over in the end, creating a bond, which often sent Kelly into 'concerned brother mode' despite being years younger. 'I'm celebrating Kelly, duh,' Paige scoffed. 'You're celebrating me, huh?' Kelly replied, chuckling with a shake of his head. Grabbing the base of the phone, Kelly headed toward the comfort of his bed, halfway anticipating the conversation to persist until dawn. In all likelihood, Paige would zonk out long before sun up, but foresight obliged his body the delight in his flocculent duvet. 'Your big two-two birthday is this week! And the day after you have a fight, which,' Paige paused, letting out a hiccup, 'is why I called. Brady told me, that Jess told him, that you'd be home in a weekish. That made me happy, so I celebrated with a night out.' She hiccupped again, what a classic (lightweight) drunk. Kelly closed his eyes, even this spastic conversation couldn't keep him awake. 'I'll be stateside in a week, so how about we talk then?' he responded, pressing to finish up and get some shuteye. 'Okay,' Paige sighed, 'I better go to bed anyway, I'm sleepy.' 'You and me both,' Kelly assured. 'Kelly?' was Paige's drawn out response. 'Yeah?' 'I missed you!' Paige pouted. Kelly sighed, 'Get some sleep Paige. I'll see you in a few,' with that said, Kelly hung the receiver back on the base. His digital clock read 2:45, the flashing red digits taunted his weary eyes. Despite the lack of sleep, Kelly couldn't help but speculate at what'd gotten into his best friend. Since the word 'go' there'd been chemistry beyond friendship, but it'd never materialized. He envisaged there would be changes, eighteen months away would insure that. He, however, had anticipated the inverse, not that Paige would become enamored with him. Kelly looked down at his bare chest, more specifically, the presence of a soft hand. 'Who was that, 'oney?' The accent was Russian, her (yes, a woman) accent was thick, and had a constant twinge of seduction even in the wee morning hours. 'Just a friend,' his words trailed off. The Russian beauty nuzzled her head against Kelly's shoulder. As he closed his eyes for the final time that morning, or so he hoped, Kelly's mind raced with the mess currently intertwining itself with him. He could only wonder what sort of complications lied ahead. Christ, if he only knew. ------------- ------------- American Forces Network; Radio Tokyo 'Well, boys and girls, I'm pleased to announce that it's that time again. Yes, our 'American Wrecking Crew', Kelly 'Machine' Masters will take on Ken'ichi Tomokazu live in the Nippon Budokan in only four days. The FISTS of FURY Heavyweight Title will be on the line, and the reported rumors are indeed true that this will be Masters' final fight with FISTS of FURY. A quick tidbit, which I know I mentioned previously, but Kelly Masters is the son of retired Navy Captain Scott Masters. So, let’s rally behind our boy here folks, and hope 'Machine' ends his career undefeated. Best of luck, Masters. Win one for us!' ------------- ------------- 'Throw the fight?! Are you fucking serious?!' Kelly roared. The KISSA GINZA nightclub was louder than loud, yet Kelly's boisterous voice had no difficulties making his response crystal clear. He leaned forward, arms resting on the private booth's table. The club was packed, women danced and men chased tail, however, Kelly did not currently share their frolicsome mindsets. His face had turned to stone the moment he heard 'throw' and 'fight', and he was damn curious to see if this was a farce or not. 'I can assure you, Mr. Masters, that I am more 'dan serious,' the reply was laced with thick Russian accent. The voice belonged to Maksim Grigorovich, a man often associated with the Russian mafia and highly ranked if you spoke to the KGB. Maksim was tall and slender; his face clean shaven and his hair razor short. He seemed to follow a particular clothing trend with black slacks, black undershirt, and a black leather jacket. His charcoal eyes contained no cheerfulness or joy, simply an empty, dark stare. 'Well Maksim, I can assure you that I'm 100% serious when I say,' Kelly paused, wiping his mouth, 'not a snowballs chance in Hell I ever throw a fight.' He sat back into the booth with arms crossed defiantly. Maksim grinned, showing his pearly white fangs. 'You do realize Mr. Masters dat you stand to make,' he chuckled, tilting his head, 'a substantial amount of money, yes?' The confident Russian snapped his fingers, prompting the burly, dark haired Russian behind him to produce a small manila envelope, which he handed to Grigorovich. 'Twenty-five grand, it's yours. You will receive the other 'alf upon completion of your task.' Cold, hard cash was kryptonite, even to the strongest of men. 'Money ain't everything, Maxy,' Masters replied matter-of-factly. 'Dat is a fair assumption,' Maksim responded, nodding in agreement. His focus drifted behind Kelly. 'Money is only one of da important dings in life, beauty is another,' he stated with a sly grin, pointing his head toward the approaching blonde, 'wouldn't you agree, Mr. Masters?' Kelly glanced over his shoulder, he too locked onto the blonde and the black dress clung tightly to her curves. He, of course, was quite familiar with her. Nikita Romanov was her name; a semi-known model throughout Europe and Asia that Kelly had been seeing for nearly six months. She slid into the private booth beside Kelly, lightly pecked him on the cheek, and flashed a cordial smile at Maksim. 'I 'ope dat you boys were playing nice,' joked Nikita. Both men shared a knowing glance between one another; the conversation had been less than friendly. Kelly remained deadpanned, while Maksim exerted a brief chuckle. 'Da, da,' Maksim replied, 'we were discussing a bit of business. You know, what your papa wanted me to speak with 'im about.' Maksim worked for her father, Alexander Romanov IV, who happened to be a powerful man. Kelly had met him once, albeit briefly, after a fight. He accepted what Nikita's father did, yet had no respect for such a man. 'That we were, but we're done now. Grab your stuff,' Kelly stated coldly, his tone aimed toward avoiding objections, or so he thought. 'But Kelly, I wanted to dance!' Nikita's bottom lipped instinctively jutted out. Kelly seemed unaffected, motioning toward her coat. She complied; however, her actions were in protest, exhibited clearly by a not-so subtle huff. Kelly stood, and extended a hand to help Nikita from the booth. She shared a hug with Maksim, before rejoining Kelly. 'Actually, I'd like to speak with Nikita for a moment,' Maksim requested, his eyes wandered from Nikita to Kelly, 'privately, if you don't mind Mr. Masters. I'll have Vassily walk her out when we're finished.' Nikita put her hand on Kelly's arm, giving him a nod of approval. 'Alright, make it quick.' Without so much as a word, he departed, leaving the two old friends to 'talk'. He weaved his way through the busy dance floor, growing angrier with each step. Kelly Masters throw a fight? He'd gone undefeated for thirteen months, spent endless amounts of time training, and they thought he'd throw it away for fifty grand? The harsh reality, danger in standing against the Russians, quickly followed in thought; Nikita and himself could both become targets, although odds were the boss' daughter would be safe. As he pushed the club doors open, the cool breeze did little to dissuade his angst. He handed the valet his parking ticket, and watched him scamper off to find the correct vehicle. Shortly after, Nikita, accompanied by Vassily, joined him outside. Vassily was a large man, but not the same burly brute with the envelope. He was probably in his mid-forties, at least his leathery skin suggested such. His slicked back hairdo was light blonde. He leaned, arms folded, against the club's brick exterior. Kelly, even while focused on Nikita, could feel Vassily's cold stare through his dark lenses. 'Kelly,' she began, only to be interrupted. 'I know what Maksim wants you to do, Kit,' Kelly informed. 'Well, will you do business with 'im? He says it's important to Papa, which makes it important to me.' 'I just don't know.' Nikita wrapped her arms around his waist. 'He says it's a once in a lifetime opportunity. 'onestly Kelly, I dink you should take the offer, whatever it is. It will be good for us, no?' As she finished, their rented Mercedes Benz pulled up to the curb. The valet exited, left the door open, and scurried around the car to open the passenger side for Nikita. 'Good for us, sure,' he answered as Nikita slipped into her seat, which prompted Kelly to close the door behind her, 'but not for me.' As he circled around the front of the Benz, he realized that a most difficult decision stood before him. ------------- ------------- The fight was over; it had seemed like mere seconds compared to the days leading up to it. Kelly put Tomokazu down with an astounding head kick. His arm was raised high in triumph, yet he felt no emotion toward his final night in Fists of Fury. His mind was preoccupied with the current dilemma at hand: the crossed Russian mob. Surely, major money had been lost, and they'd be motivated to retaliate swiftly. There would be no post-fight celebration, or mingling with spectators at the host arena, Nippon Bukodan. Kelly, instead, immediately set out for his personal locker room backstage. Nikita, who had never watched fight, would be waiting for him. After a myriad of backslaps and congratulations, he reached his room and pushed the door open. As expected Nikita was there, propped on the training table in the back. She dressed casually in light blue jeans and a black halter-top. Her concerned expression quickly transformed into a sparkling smile, her man was in one piece and had obviously won. 'You won!' she exclaimed. 'Yep,' Kelly mustered, closed the door behind him then immediately began to disrobe. 'What's the rush, baby?' Nikita asked, puzzled by Kelly's hasty behavior. She hopped down from the table, concerned enough to desire a closer look at the situation. Kelly took a deep breath, 'We need to leave for the States tonight,' he shot a glance toward Nikita, who was incoming, 'at least I need to. You're not in any danger.' Kelly dressed urgently, tossing on blue jeans and a black Harley-Davidson long sleeve. 'Danger? What are you talking about?' Nikita asked quizzically. Her question went unanswered, or unheard. Kelly packed his fighting attire into a duffel bag, but was soon attentive as Nikita's hand grabbed his face, bringing them eye to eye. 'Kelly, talk to me,' she demanded with a slight whimper. Kelly reached up, removed her grip, and gently caressed her hand. 'The meeting with Maksim, the business arrangement with your father. They wanted me to throw my fight, Kit, and I'm thinking that your father and his associates lost a lot of cash,' Kelly explained, while he packed the remaining equipment. 'We're booked on a flight leaving in two hours, and our luggage is already at the airport.' Confusion and stress overwhelmed Nikita, her eyes welled up with tears. Kelly, duffel bag slung over his shoulder, took notice. 'You can stay, or come with me. I won't lie, Nikita, shit is going to get complicated and real messy. If you decide to stay, I wouldn't hold it against you. But there's not a whole lot of time to spend mulling it over.' There was recognizable urgency in his tone, yet a soothing calm even during the distress. His father, Scott Masters, had told him as a young boy that you never really knew a person until you saw how they operated under duress. Well, if he didn't know Nikita before, these next ten seconds would surely show her true colors. 'I 'ave decided,' Nikita stated, her tearful eyes fixated on the floor. ------------- ------------- Inside a black Mercedes, a man remained still, lifeless. Aside from the lit Sobranie Black hanging from his lips, the interior was pitch-dark, ideal settings for a tail vehicle. His striking eyes peered through the windshield; Across the street, Kelly Masters, his target, stepped out from a taxi and onto the curb. The man reached into his jacket, and produced a cellphone. He pressed a single key, and brought the device to his ear. 'Yes sir, Masters just arrived at Tokyo International. Yes, of course, I'll take care of 'im, sir.' He paused; his eyes surveyed the area surrounding Masters. 'No sir, I don't see Nikita.' Activity around the taxi regained his attention. 'Wait, yes, she's with 'im.' Nikita, assisted with a helping hand from Masters, emerged from the cab. She stuck closely to his side, both arms wrapped tightly around his waist. The man listened intently. Meanwhile, Masters leaned over to the driver, handed him cash (presumably), and rejoined Nikita. Side by side, the pair entered the airport with two carry ons in tow. 'The situation will be 'andled,' he reassured. Acting quickly, he flipped his phone shut, and shoved it back into the confines of his coat. Our 'observant bystander' exited his Mercedes, dashed across the busy street and into the airport. With what he had in mind, there was no need for luggage.
