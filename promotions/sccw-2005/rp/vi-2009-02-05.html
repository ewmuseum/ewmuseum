Date: 02/05/2009 "My old friend, do you know the Klingon proverb that tells us revenge is a dish that is best served cold? " - Khan, Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan VI 12/22/2008 11:50 p.m. The late evening World News broadcast on Channel 13, Action News is interrupted by breaking news. After the breaking news screen is shown, the newscast switches back to the anchor’s chair where anchor Ron Smugell is sitting down looking grimly at the camera. "Sorry to interrupt be we have breaking news," he says. "We have just received a video sent to this station by the criminal known as Rick Roll. Apparently he has captured the hero known as Judge and is holding him hostage somewhere in the city. We warn you, the images you are about to see may be disturbing." The newscast then switches to video shot on a camcorder. Judge is tied to a metal chair in the middle of the room. Dim lighting creates a somewhat dark scene of the crime. Boxes are scattered in the background with dust on the floor. Just then the voice of Rick Roll is heard from behind the camera. "Hello people of Vegas," he says in a droll manner before coming out from behind the camera and holding it in front of himself so that he is now filming himself. His Rick Astley mask is prominent as he begins to speak. "I’m here to show all of you just how feeble you really are." He motions toward the Judge, who is unconscious. "Look at your hero now." Rick Roll points the camera at him and zooms in to get a closer look. A moment later he then zooms out again and then focuses the camera back on himself. "With Judge out of the way, this city belongs to criminals again. You all now play by my rules. And my rules are simple…there are no rules. The Urban Decay and the Gerson Park Kingsmen are already fighting in the streets. The Rat Pack is most likely sitting at home, cowering with fear, or running around like a chicken with it’s head cut off. They cannot stop me. They cannot stop Death. Tonight you all are going to get a show. And this show will surely end with a bang." Rick Roll cackles maniacally before shutting off the camera. Loud noises are heard in the back of the studio after the video is shown. Smugell looks grimly at the camera once more. "Police urge you not to panic. They are currently searching for Rick Roll. Any information can be sent to Metro’s Tips Hotline. There may be a cash reward. We now send you back to your regularly scheduled broadcast." The news story blips out and the World News comes back on. * * * 12/23/2008 12:17 a.m. The moment he left the warehouse where Judge was being held, the demented Rick Roll has started putting his master plan into motion. First he’s gotten into a taxi and killed the driver. Now he’s headed for the Strip and is talking on his cell phone to Hamburglar as he is driving. Hamburglar is in charge of the rest of Death, the team of super criminals Rick Roll has put together. "Are you and the team in place?" he asks. "Yes," Hamburglar replies on the other line. "We’re here." Rick Roll smiles underneath his mask. "Good, good. Are there any potential victims there? I need a distraction so I can pull of my end of the plan downtown." "We’re circling the mall now, sir." A few moments later, Hamburglar responds to Rick Roll with an answer. "Yes," he replies. "There is a van of teenagers parked in the parking lot. They may be college students, but they look pretty young." "Good," Rick Roll replies with demented glee. "Tell our new friends to have some fun with them. The police and the Rat Pack will be there to try and stop them but they won’t succeed. That’s when my part of the plan will be put into motion. Media and police will be there. However it’ll only be a diversion. Once I escape, the true plan will be revealed. We’re basically going to Rick Roll them into thinking one thing when something else entirely is going on." Rick Roll smiles deviously under his mask. "Tell Pilgrim and Exorcist to kill everyone in the van. Once the police arrive, tell them to kill as many as they can. I want MAJOR media coverage for our activities. The age of heroes is over. The Age of Death has arrived and no one will survive it." He then hangs up his cell phone and continues driving. Only he seems to know the full plan and that’s how he likes it. * * * 12:45 a.m. After Rick Roll had left, the pirates are still patrolling the warehouse as I am trying to get out of the chair I am tied and handcuffed to. This has been a nightmare day. First my date with Amanda gets ruined, then I get captured, and worst of all I find out that my best friend is really the super criminal who captured me. The moment he took off his mask, I felt sick inside. It felt like my stomach was being stabbed repeatedly with a butcher knife. Stabbed not just by anyone, but my best friend in the world. This is the nadir for me. I hang my head low amidst this depression that has taken me over. My heart feels like it is being crushed by an iron fist of hate and contempt. Suddenly there’s a crash in the back of the warehouse! The pirates who are supposed to be guarding me run off in the direction of the noise. I look backward to see if I can see anything but there are boxes blocking the way. However I do see my mask lying on the floor. Just then I see a pirate skidding across the ground in my direction. Another one goes flying in the other directions. SHIT! The boxes are still obscuring my view of what is going on. I know somebody’s here to save my ass, I just don’t know who it is yet. A few moments later the warehouse is silent and all I hear are footsteps heading in my direction. A shadow appears on the ground heading in my direction. I watch it as the person stops at my mask. They then bend over and pick it up. Looking up I see a sight for sore eyes. "AMANDA!" I exclaim in a very happy and jubilant manner. She smiles as she strolls over to untie me, laying my mask on my lap as she does. "Sorry I took so long, baby," she purrs out before she finishes untying me. She then kisses me on the cheek as my hands are freed. "My feet are cuffed too." "I noticed." She holds up a pick before kneeling down to work on the lock. Reaching down to my lap I grab my mask and put it back on. "How did you find me?" "Woman’s intuition." "Seriously?" "No, seriously I remembered the name when you said it earlier and wrote it down. I figured it was the best place to start looking." Adjusting as Cat Lady undoes my leg shackles, I almost forget the horrible news I have to tell her. "Amanda, you’re not going to believe what I found out." "What is it, Ash?" I sigh out loud, not really wanting to tell her but I know I have to. "You remember how Todd was kidnapped by Rick Roll and the Hamburglar guy at the gala we went to earlier?" She nods her head. "Yeah, so?" "So…he wasn’t really kidnapped." She raises an eyebrow. "What? What do you mean?" "Well, you’re not going to believe this but Todd IS Rick Roll." Her jaw almost drops to the floor. "You mean your best friend set you up?" I nod my head. There’s no other way to acknowledge it. My best friend is a bastard criminal who wants me dead. What ironies we experience in life. "Yes he did." She places her hand on my shoulder to console me. What a loving woman. "I’m sorry, Ash." At this point, I stand up. Anger fills me up with a rage I have never felt before at the mere thought of Todd. However he is no longer Todd to me. He is Rick Roll and his ass is dead. "I’m not. Todd is no longer a friend of mine. He is Rick Roll and he is going down. I will kill him personally for all the crimes against humanity he has committed." "Ash, you’re going to kill your best friend?" I shake my head and turn to her. "No, I’m going to judge him. He’ll be found guilty. And all guilty charges for the crimes he committed come with a death sentence." At this point, I can do nothing but smile. Smiling over the impending death of Rick Roll. Motioning to Cat Lady, I lead the way as we head out of the warehouse. That fucker is going down. One way or another, Rick Roll is going to get his. * * * 12:56 a.m. Outside of the Galleria Mall in the parking lot, police are in the midst of an attempt to apprehend the criminals known as Pilgrim, Hamburglar, and the Exorcist. The three men attacked a van filled with college students who were smoking pot and killed all six of them. However, things are not going according to plan. When the police went in to apprehend the suspects, three officers were wounded and another three were killed by Pilgrim and the Exorcist. The Hamburglar didn’t do much so the police have changed his status to a non-lethal threat. SWAT arrived right after the failed apprehension and is about to go in when the Rat Pack arrives. Astronomer’s white Chevrolet Trailblazer SUV leads the way with Abrams black Hummer right behind them. The two vehicles park and the heroes exit them in short order. The aqua blue costumed Astronomer gets out of the driver’s side of the SUV with the yellow and red clad caped superhero Captain Velocity gets out of the passenger’s side. Exiting from the back doors of the SUV is the homeless hero Vagrant, the Halloween costumed Lady Bug, and the black clad Bailiff. Exiting from the Hummer’s driver’s side door is the gigantic Abrams and the passenger who leaves the vehicle is the black clad yet snobbish egomaniac hero known as Mustang whose car was totaled the day before during a car chase. After exiting the vehicles, the Rat Pack walks over to the police commander in charge of the scene who just happens to be Police Chief Mike Twain who handled the McDonald’s explosion crime scene a few days before. An officer walks over and taps the chief on the shoulder. "Chief," the officer says. "The Rat Pack is here." The Chief turns around and is greeted by costumed adventurers much to his surprise. "Ah, the Rat Pack," he says to them coyly. "Good to see you again." "Good to see you again too, Chief," the Astronomer says with a smile. "What’s the situation?" The Chief motions to the parking lot, pointing with his finger as he does so. The Rat Pack looks where he is pointing. "See for yourself." They look over toward the parking lot of the Galleria Mall and see Pilgrim throwing a hatchet at an officer! The police officer is hit in shoulder by the hatchet! Another officer is being hit by a giant metal cross wielded by the Exorcist! "That’s our second attempt to apprehend these guys. SWAT is about to go in." Abrams growls in the background. "We can take these bastards down," he says with a snarl. "You sure about that?" the Chief asks with a raised eyebrow. Abrams nods his head without any hesitation at all. "Yeah I’m sure. These bastards are going down." Abrams starts storming down toward the parking lot, pushing aside police tape as he does. His eyes are set squarely on Pilgrim. Clinching his fists, the confrontation is soon to happen. The rest of the Rat Pack is right behind him. The police officers who are still alive in the parking lot crawl to safety. Many of them are seriously wounded. Pilgrim walks over and pulls his hatchet our of the shoulder of the officer he just killed with it. He peers to the south of himself to see that a huge man in a camouflage fatigues is heading his way. This brings a sick smile to his face. Pilgrim stands up as Abrams begins running toward him. He then tosses his hatchet at him but Abrams dodges it and it clangs on the ground behind him. The two monstrous men then collide in the parking lot like two Mack trucks. The rest of the Rat Pack then goes after the Exorcist and Hamburglar. The Exorcist fights off Lady Bug, Bailiff, and Captain Velocity with his giant metal cross while Hamburglar is easily dispatched by Mustang, Vagrant, and the Astronomer without putting up a fight. The Rat Pack then turns all of their attention to the Exorcist and Pilgrim who are far more deadlier threats than the feeble Hamburglar. However Pilgrim has taken down Abrams and is now going after the rest of the team. Astronomer struggles to get the giant metal cross away from Exorcist but does so with the assistance of Lady Bug and Mustang. The three of them finally disable Exorcist and knock him unconscious. That leaves Pilgrim against the rest of the Rat Pack. The Pilgrim uses his fists to punch down Mustang and Captain Velocity. Bailiff jumps on his back only to be thrown off just as quickly! Pilgrim then holds off the rest of the team with punches and kicks. Once he sees that all of the Rat Pack is down, Pilgrim picks up Hamburglar and they make a mad dash for his car that is hidden on the other side of the mall. Pilgrim can’t drive so he knows that he’ll need the otherwise worthless Hamburglar just to get around. After their escape, the SWAT team arrives at the scene and puts the handcuffs on the Exorcist. Once the Rat pack regroups, they look around and notice Pilgrim has escaped. "DAMN!" Abrams exclaimed. "If only the Judge was here to shoot that bastard in his big fat fucking head!" Captain Velocity, still holding his head from the blows he sustained in the battle, shoots him an angry look. "Let’s not go into that debate again." Astronomer then starts thinking about what Abrams said. "Speaking of which, where is the Judge anyway?" he asks. * * * 1:13 a.m. My car is eerily quiet as I drive toward the city. Sitting in the seat next to me in her Cat Lady costume, Amanda is as quiet as I am. We didn’t have a marital dispute or anything, Instead of talking we’re listening to the various police calls on my police scanner as we head down the street. Listening closely, there are some intriguing calls. "We have a multiple 187 off of Sahara and Nellis. Two bodies. Gunshots were heard in the area. Suspect still on the lose." That’s the third murder call tonight since we turned on the scanner upon entering my Toyota Supra. Luckily it’s a modified street racing car so we’re making good time. My mind starts racing, wondering about the murders. Wondering if Rick Roll has anything to do with them or not. However the descriptions of the first two crime scenes don’t match with his usual tactics. This is something new. Someone is going on a one night shooting spree in town. This makes me think that the person is a hired hit man here on a job. Just then there’s another call on the police scanner. "All points bulletin. The criminal Rick Roll is in front of the Wynn Resort off of Sands Boulevard with a hostage held at gunpoint. 187 already on the scene. Use extreme caution. He is considered to be armed and extremely dangerous." I turn to Amanda. "We‘ve got him," I say to her with a grin. And that’s all I have to say to her, too. She smiles and nods her head. "Let’s get him, baby," she replies. Never argue with a woman. So I gun it and speed down the street in the direction of the Strip. Time is of the essence. We have to get there and stop him before it’s too late. It’s the city’s only hope. * * * 1:25 a.m. Back at the Galleria Mall, the rest of the Rat Pack is being attended to by medical personnel after their brutal and ferocious battle with the super criminal group Death. Pilgrim and Hamburglar escaped, but they were able to detain the Exorcist. Abrams is still smarting from the fact he wasn’t able to take out Pilgrim during the fight. Bailiff and Lady Bug suffered some bruises. Captain Velocity, Astronomer, and Mustang were a bit shaken up but are otherwise fine. Vagrant is too busy looking through the garbage to care. As the group is recovering, an officer walks over with a small radio. He hands it to the Astronomer. "I think you should hear this," he says to them. The Rat Pack crowds around as they listen in to what is being broadcast on the police scanner. "All points bulletin. The criminal Rick Roll is in front of the Wynn Resort off of Sands Boulevard with a hostage held at gunpoint. 187 already on the scene. Use extreme caution. He is considered to be armed and extremely dangerous." Astronomer turns to the rest of the team. "I think we know where we need to be," he says to them. Without hesitation, they get up and run toward their two vehicles. In a flash, they speed off as quickly as they reach the vehicles. Downtown is fairly close to where they are. They can only hope that they get there before anything else happens on this very bloody evening. * * * 1:32 a.m. Once arriving at the scene on the Strip, after parking my car on the side of the street I exit my black Supra and Cat Lady does the same. We then see a mob of people with some police cars in front of them blocking something in front of the Wynn Hotel and Casino. "That’s where we’ll find that bastard," I say with a growl as we push through the mob to get to where the action is. The thoughts of what may or may not be happening race through my head like a brain freeze after chugging down a Slurpee. Rick Roll is a maniac so anything is possible at this point. After pushing through the crowd, many of whom are gawking at Amanda and I in our costumes, we come upon the scene of the crime. Police are everywhere. There are five squad cars by my count. The lead officer doesn’t have to say a word but he starts flapping his jaws like a common idiot. I ignore everything he says as I look toward what is going on. Rick Roll has a hostage and is pointing a gun at her head. There is a victim shot dead on the ground beside them. Police have the entrance to the hotel blocked but then I notice him looking in my direction. He then motions to police. "I WANT THAT BASTARD!" he exclaims. The head officer then is handed a megaphone. "WHAT DO YOU WANT?" he asks. "LET THAT BASTARD JUDGE APPREHEND ME!" The officer, whose name I don’t even bother to read, turns to me with a deeply concerned look. "You up to it Judge?" he replies. "I don’t want this bastard getting lose on the city streets." I nod my head. "This guy dies tonight." He stares at me blank faced. Just as he is about to reply, Rick Roll then pulls his screaming hostage toward the entrance of the casino. I look over to see him using her as a human shield in order to enter the casino. I jump over the police caution tape and run after him. However, as he enters the casino, he shoots the woman in the head! The echo of the gunshot rings throughout the casino floor! People then start screaming and running for the exits. This allows Rick Roll to run through the casino and have a lot of people blocking the police from getting to him and arresting him. It’s a mad dash for the door. The people who are all trying to escape seem to me to be like horror movie zombies trying to capture the last remain humans. Basically they are a giant mob with a single purpose. Luckily for him only this part of the hotel is effected by what happened. He runs full speed through the rest of the casino and out one of the back exits. I follow him, running as fast as I can! Nothing will stop me from ‘judging’ this bastard tonight. Rick Roll is going to die and I will be the one that kills him. Rage and hatred fuel me as we run across the street, which I notice is Sands Avenue, through a mass of tourists until we reach the Venetian mega resort. Rick Roll runs inside but I will not relent. I follow him in and we run through a Chinese tour through the casino. After that he runs up some stairs and then takes off his mask before going to the entrance to the nightclub Tao. He greets the doorman and is let in. Then it hits me. "FUCK!" I forgot that Todd has connections and nobody knows he’s Rick Roll but me! SHIT! Now what the hell am I going to do? Looking around the crowded nightclub, the mass of people there makes it hard to see where Todd, I mean Rick Roll ran off to. I need to stop thinking that he’s Todd. He is Rick Roll and he is the most dangerous threat to the city. He must be stopped at all costs. The mass of people in front of me is making it hard for me to pick out where he went to. Everyone is dancing so picking out one particular person in this mess is about like looking for a needle in a haystack. However, as I make my way through the throng of dancing people, near the back of the dance floor I see someone that intrigues me a lot. The name of the person is Frederick Montague and he is a big corporate player on the Strip. He was one of the people on my list of suspects behind the ninja robbery attempt at the Full Moon Art Gallery. This is a very intriguing development. Maybe Rick Roll and him are working together. It would all make perfect sense. Rick Roll creating chaos in the other casinos and Montague raking in all the cash. However, that scenario would be too easy. With Rick Roll, there’s always something more going on than it seems. Looking in the direction of Montague, I notice something else. No Rick Roll. Looking around the club some more, I finally pick out Rick Roll. He’s back in his mask again and sneaking around the direction opposite of Montague. What the hell is going on? If they’re not working together, then for all I know he could be a victim of this madman. That’s when I hear gunshots to my left! I turn around to see a man in a suit shooting in the direction of the big boss. Looking closely at the man as the who is doing the shooting I notice that he looks vaguely familiar. Running through the throng of people who are running out of the nightclub, I reach the man and grab his arm. He turns around and we are face to face. That’s when it finally hits me who the guy is. "von Doom," I mutter out. He looks over and we lock eyes. I know it’s him. I’ve seen him on the television. He’s a pro wrestler from Las Vegas who suddenly retired a few weeks back. The guy is known to be eccentric and many people feel he is idiotic. All of that turns out to be bunch of hogwash with this revelation. It was an act and looking into his eyes, I can tell he’s killed before. He turns to look at Rick Roll and he nods his head back at von Doom. HOLY SHIT! Rick Roll hired him to kill Montague! What the fuck is going on? Maybe this is what he meant by "the Ultimate Rick Roll." von Doom then turns back toward me. He points his gun in my direction and starts firing the weapon! I dodge out of the way as people start screaming. Panic fills the nightclub and everyone starts running out. I get up and turn around to see two suited men dead on the floor behind me. Looking back toward where von Doom was at, I see him struggling to fight off two more suited security officers. I look around some more and see everyone running out of the . Turning back to where I was looking, von Doom kills the two security officers with a knife he got somehow when I was looking in the other direction. Grabbing his gun off the floor, he fires at two more of the security officers who were sent at him by Montague. Von Doom then fires three shots at Montague, killing him with a headshot. Von Doom then turns and points his gun at me. He hesitates and doesn’t shoot. Instead he walks out looking like nothing happened. After he leaves, I take action by getting the rest of the people out of the club before I go myself to capture Rick Roll. Running down the stairs and through the mob of people, I go outside the casino and look for Rick Roll. Moments later, I come to the realization that he’s gone. I missed my chance. There may not be another one and I blew it. I got distracted which threw me off my game. It won’t happen again. The next time Rick Roll does anything and I’m there, he will be judged and the sentence will be death. * * * "I don't know; every man has his breaking point." - Red, The Shawshank Redemption
