The infinite desert of white powder that led me the lair of the evil alien Spacely and his secret invasion force plotting to take over the planet Earth.

Date: 07/09/2009 Been wandering around the American Southwest for a few weeks now. Somewhere in New Mexico now. Or maybe I'm in the country of Mexico. Haven't seen any gringos in awhile. In yet another sleazy motel on the road. On the balcony, I peer out into the distance to see my surroundings. Look around and there's nothing but desert in all directions. Now I know how Lawrence of Arabia must've felt. All sorts of dunes and small little bushes dotted across the landscape. A slight breeze makes it feel really nice. Late afternoon is always slow in small towns like this one. Nothing to do here except whatever woman I pick up from the local bar, pub, tavern, or cantina. Every town I go to follows the same script. Nothing ever seems to change. Every day seems exactly the same as the one before it. All of them are just a giant blur to me, one day unchanged from the one before it. Only a few vague memories of meaningful, non-sexual events stay in my memories. The rest is just lumped up garbage and a whole bunch of things that I want to forget for the rest of my life. The bulk of the things I want to forget involve drug use and sex with ugly women. Well mostly the sex with ugly women... Who have no teeth... Or vaginas. ... Brain starts to fry like eggs on a skillet. Sizzling, fizzling, burning. Can't think anymore. No thoughts mean that I'm not going to be on my game. Facing Spacely for my title. A title belt I don't even have. Milton Berle stole it from me. That punk ass loser. He is nothing but another ogre from a Dreamworks movie. Or is it Disney? Or PIXAR? Shrugging my shoulders, I could care less at this point. Look back out into the burning desert of New Mexico that surrounds me. Creatures of the night roam around in the darkness. See swirls all around me. Everything is getting sucked into some sort of a giant vortex. Is this real or am I imagining all of it? At this point I don't know. Before coming out onto the balcony I had just snorted some cocaine off of the breast of the hooker I just fucked. The white powder is still sticking to my nostrils like glue. I sniffed glue once. It wasn't as tasty as this is. Sweet smelling powder ringing around my nose like salt around a martini glass. Sore from the fucking. I need to get in shape. Weight rooms in hotels are crap. I got a staph infection once from using the barbells in one of them. Had to take two weeks off from work to deal with that shit. Never going through that again. Luckily the continental breakfast at said hotel made up for it. Best bacon and cheese omelets in three states. Well, three states that I actually remember being in. All three are right next to each other, ironically. Alabama, Nevada, and Alaska are their names. The American Northeast is where there are located. I must go to Alaska to see some Red Sox games. Boston is a suburb of Juneau I think. Might be one of the Aleutian Islands near Alcatraz although my geography has always been bad. Turn around and go inside the motel. Shutting the sliding glass door behind me, the air conditioning feels great as I go inside. Outside the sun is melting the skin off of my bones faster than sex with a gymnast. I probably look like one of the aliens from They Live at this point. Inside the naked hooker is lying on my bed. Her curves look like an old school bottle of Coca Cola. Her ass has a crack on it. Then again, so does the state of California with the San Andreas Fault. Thinking more about it, I realize that I have a crack on my ass too. Looking down at the naked woman on the bed gets me to thinking about SCCW. There are two women there that get my blood boiling like water in a frying pan. Their names are Aimz and Desade. My nether regions immediately rise up the moment I think about them. Lesbian lovers with belts like mine. However I don't have my belt but they do. They probably had Searle steal it to get more bling. Wheels in my brain start turning the more I think about it. It all makes perfect sense. Mr. Ashe probably arranged the whole thing to involve me in their plot to dominate SCCW. Somehow I became a pawn in this scenario. I am nothing more than a goon for Lance Marshall in this whole thing. One of his goons to take on the evil empire led by DNA and all their little buddies. It's like Pokemon but with chicks I want to fuck in the ass numerous times over the summer. Eyes start to burn. Most likely the cause of this is the smell of either my aftershave or something that came out of the hooker's ass while I was outside. Hopefully she didn't die and I end up with cops shooting at me like Marv from Sin City. My life flashes before my eyes for all of five seconds. All I see are a few wrestling matches, some drugs, and a talking dragon telling me to take a crap in the woods. That is the most I know about my life. I probably have some form of selective amnesia. Start to wander around the motel room looking for my clothes. Look on the chair and don't see them there. Turn and look on the floor. Maybe they gor thrown off before I had sex. Examining the floor area surrounding the bed, I see nothing. Look on the bed next to the snoring hooker and see no clothes there. Only place left is the bathroom. Top toe in there and again the clothes are not where I am looking. Wander back out of the bathroom and look around some more. When I put my arm out in front of me, I notice something. There is a suit sleeve covering my entire forearm. I look down and see that I am wearing my clothes. 'Hmmmm...didn't expect that.' Alarm clock goes off. Hooker gets scared to death and jumps out of the bed screaming her head off. She didn't even do that when she achieved orgasm. The faker! She runs out of my motel room naked and out into the hallway. The screams seemingly scare everyone in the motel out of their rooms. They all stare at her as she runs screaming for the endless desert that surrounds this little shithole of a town. The commotion lasts all of a minute. By this point everyone else in the motel is now staring in my direction like I'm some sort of eyesore that's dotted their landscape. 'Uhhh...check please.' That's about all I can mutter out at this point. My beard starts to itch. Maybe I caught a case of the crabs from the hooker. Or even worse, I have Crab People trying to kill me! Fucking Crab People. They were behind the Tunguska Blast of 1909 but the Russians covered it up. That's what started all of that Bolshevik Revolution stuff there in 1917. The aliens were behind it all. And by aliens I mean the Crab People that are infesting themselves in my beard. There's probably going to be mutant crab babies crawling all over my skin within a week. Gather my belongings into the duffel bag I brought with me and quickly check out. Hopefully they don't sue me for all the cum stains all over the walls in the motel room. Besides that, I'm late for the show. Well, not really. I'm just using that as an excuse to get my ass moving out of there before someone lynches me. Get into my rental car and start it up. Takes three tries. Piece of shit. Quickly get on the highway to go to the show. For some reason I'm destined to only go thirty miles an hour. DNA probably sabotaged my rental car. Better that than my dick. For some reason lesbians hate my guts. I blame it on Rosie O'Donnell. Either that or some long lost Nostradamus quatrain that has to deal with me. Continue driving past an endless desert that surrounds me on all sides. All of the cars look the same. These modern cars look like something out of the Star Wars universe. The drive is a long and lonely one for me. No one to keep me company. Well, if you don't count the Crab People living in my beard. Landscape never changes for miles. Somehow I end up in the mountains. I'm probably near Aspen or something. See a sign that says Taos on it. Where the hell am I? I must've driven to Guatemala, possibly Paraguay. Continue to drive through the mountains. Begin seeing horrifying visions as I drive along. I see visions of catastrophic world events. I see a nuclear bomb exploding in a major city followed by images of wars, famine, death, and citizens riots. Then comes the most shocking vision I have ever seen in my life. Satan appears to me and laughs before showing me an image of Spacely having sex with Yoda while wearing my championship belt. I pull over to the side of the road and park my car before getting out to vomit. My stomach is bubbling as I start to feel even more sick. After throwing up again, I get my composure back and wipe my mouth. Look down and see pieces of the hamburger I ate yesterday on the ground. Smell climbs up my nose. Quickly I get back into the car to get away from the smell. It stinks like a dead body. Forget how I know how a dead body smells. Probably had sex with one once. Turn my car ignition back on and start driving down the highway again. The mountains start to blur together until they look like a horde of Decepticon Transformers trying to kill me. This makes me drive and swerve around like a drunk driver. Weird creatures are roaming on the sides of the road. What to be attacked Final Fantasy style by some of them. Where's the rest of my party? I need a red mage, a black mage, a healer, and a knight pronto. Wait for a few minutes but no one shows up to join my party. This makes me drive faster. After what seems like fifteen hours, I'm finally out of the mountains. See a sign that says Albuquerque is only eighty miles away. Continue to drive along the endless highway. Now that I'm out of the mountains, all I see in all directions is an endless desert just like before. The heat blurs everything together. In front of me are giant sandworms like from the movie Dune. As they jump in and out of the sand, I see blue armored Tron warriors fighting them with metal spears. Drive past a Jawa Sandcrawler and some Womp Rats jumping through a canyon to my right. Drive down the highway some more until I reach a grand sea of white cocaine. The cocaine is all over the place. It's like a Sahara Desert of tasty white goodness. I can snort it from here. The tender dots of white goodness goes into my nose. The effects are immediate. A dazzling array of colors surrounds me like a giant rainbow at the end of a rain storm. My mouth waters as it is filled with the taste of a bag of Skittles. Upon going about a mile longer, I pull over to the side of the road and park my car. Quickly getting out, I scamper over to the white powder all over the ground and sniff it. Snorting as much of the awesome white powder as I can, my nostrils are now filled to the brim with the white powder. However, after waiting for a few seconds, nothing happens. That is until I sneeze. I then continue to sneeze for about twenty seconds. I then realize that I have not been snorting cocaine and that I have in fact been snorting white sand. Then I come to the realization that I am in the White Sands National Monument. Almost forgot about that being in this country. Or is this a territory? I'll probably be killed by a whole horde of Navajos before I even get to the show. Run back to the car and get back into the driver's seat. Start it up and drive down the highway until I reach Albuquerque. The entire time driving there seems like a blur. The landscape all around me looks like something out of A Nightmare Before Christmas. The sky suddenly becomes dark and gloomy. Outside the landscape appears more grotesque and distorted. Strange shadows appear on the ground like wild, savage beasts. I'm waiting for Tim Burton to yell cut at this point but nothing happens. Drive through the somewhat desolate desert city until I reach the Fairgrounds where Temptation is taking place. Park my car and exit the car soon after. Go around the car and open the passenger's side door. Reach in, grab my bag, and close the door before locking up the car. Head for the backstage entrance. Realize I'm missing something. Where's my belt? Look down and see I'm wearing one but that is not the belt I'm missing. Forget why it is missing. Probably something to do with von Doom. Go inside the backstage area and look around. Find the fragments of the Roswell crash being moved to some undisclosed location. Realize that they are taking it to Spacely's compound/lair for the gradual takeover of humankind that is located somewhere on the Fairgrounds. I must find it and destroy it before the show begins otherwise he'll melt my brain with his laser shooting penis! It'll be like Mars Attacks except it won't be fucking stupid. Maybe it'll be more along the lines of Aliens in the Attic. Nah. A Star Wars armada battle would be more like what would happen. Yeah...and with Wookie creatures and Storm Troopers battling to the death. What a fine day to die this will be indeed. Quickly run to my locker room like a teenaged boy running to the top of the hill to have sex with his girlfriend before the cops arrive and make everyone leave. Find my locker room and quickly go inside. Lock the door behind me and change into my battle gear. Once I'm finished, I unlock the door and go into the hallway with my gear. I'm dressed like a Ghostbuster 'Be Kind Rewind' style. My proton-pack is ready and so am I. Start to spout out my own lyrics to the Ghostbusters II theme song. 'I ain't afraid of no aliens! Yo, I ain't afraid of no aliens!' Ready to go to battle with the alien scum infesting SCCW. Spacely Sprockets, Spacely's front for his secret invasion of the planet Earth, is going to be destroyed by days end and I will have my title back from Milton Berle. Today will be a great day for Rapmeister Rick van Malloystein. Either that or I'm killed by the lasers shot from their giant tripods. In any event, it will be interesting indeed. I just hope I can remember it this time tomorrow during my mid-day cocaine and rapping binge...
