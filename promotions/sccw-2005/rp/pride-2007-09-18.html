Pride

18 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 18, 2007 The word pride is defined as ‘a becoming or dignified sense of what is due to oneself or one's position or character’. Or, in simple terms, self esteem, or self respect. Everyone goes through an arrogant phase. A cocky phase. A period of time where they’re over confident. I have. Life is about achieving balance. Controlling what you can control and letting go of the things you can’t. Easier said than done. With each crossroad in life comes new opportunities. New opportunities bring new responsibilities. The new responsibilities bring more choices, more opportunities. It’s easy to see how drastically one decision can affect a life. For better, or for worse. Making a decision is inevitable. The problem lies with when. Fuck it. * * * * "I’m sorry." Her eyes narrowed as she glared at me. She folded her arms across her breasts, and shifted her weight back to one foot. To make matters worse, the other foot was tapping against the tile floor. Never a good sign. I let out an audible sigh and started towards her, preparing my jaw for the incoming slap. I seemed to be getting quite good at that lately. "Cassie…" Of course, the slap came as no surprise. I was beginning to think the woman used it as foreplay, until another sent my head spinning the other way. I cracked my neck and rubbed my jaw as I began forward again. With ears ringing, one could only hope for better results. "Mayson, I don’t want to hear it! I don’t know if I can get you out of this mess this time around. The judge wants to see you in court. There’s nothing I can do about that. You, Melissa, and Andrew. She’s fighting for them, Mayson. She wants her kids back." Her words hurt more than any blows could. My heart felt stuck in my throat. My legs felt like jelly. The room spun. Why this? Why now? She wouldn’t. She did. "We’ll fight her. We have to. I have to." I rubbed my eyes, but the room still seemed so fuzzy. Out of focus. Andrew and Melissa. Family. Nothing else mattered. There was nothing else. "Mayson, we’ll fight her. I promise." It was all I could do to not lose my breakfast on the kitchen floor. * * * * Once again I found myself ripped from my sleep by indescribable dreams. Nightmares. I had been running, not only for my life, but for ours. Running, from nothing more than darkness. Darkness, and laughter. I was terrified. That much I knew. If only I could remember more. If only I could stop caring. If only I were like him. * * * * "I’m proud of you." Her words came as a shock. Her sense of pride was evident in the way she looked at me. It’s not that I didn’t believe her. It’s not that I wasn’t happy. Plain and simple, no one had ever said those words to me before. As happy as those words made me, it left a pit of dread in my stomach. "I love you." Her eyes sparkled with the light only a lover could understand. Her embrace was warm, and comforting. I could feel her heart beating as her chest pressed against my own. "I love you too." I had promised to forgive, but found it impossible to forget. As I found out, forgiveness is only the beginning. A man can only forgive so much. When faced with an impossible trial there is bound to be a breaking point. Sooner, rather than later. Enter, love. They say that love can conquer all. Now, I’m not a religious man. However, if I were I’d be on my hands and knees praying that this was true. That life was that easy. That love was that strong. That people were that good. Disappointment. Shame. Disgrace. I can’t bear to bring that upon them. Upon her. If only this was so simple. If things were so cut and dry. * * * * "Let it go. He can’t be all bad." She didn’t understand. She had no right to try to justify the man, or his actions. The single individual that ruined my life, yet at the same time molded me in to the man standing before her today. He cursed enough to make a sailor blush. He could out drink a Russian. He had taken away everything I loved and held dear, and now he’s back. No, not Sean Sterling. My father. Curtis Allen Colby. "I just don’t understand why you can’t forgive him. It’s been so long. I think we’re on the right track, with you forgiving Sean so easily. Maybe you’re ready for the next step." Forgiving Sean so easily? The single decision that left me feeling ripped apart. The single hardest decision I’d had to make, until now. The decision I made for her. The decision I may grow to regret. I can feel the pit of dread growing bigger, ready to swallow me whole. * * * * "I’m coming with you." His face was determination set in stone. Mouth scowling, hands on the hips. I’ve said it before, he reminds me of myself. And it scares me. Before I can even think of a response, he starts in again. "It won’t happen again. I promise I won’t be in the way. I need to go." With those little eyes pleading up at me, I had no choice. My head dipped a slight nod, but I pressed a finger to my lips. I can already feel the aftermath from this one. I may not have a home to come back to. "Keep it between us, kid." * * * * Good versus evil. The battle that’s been plaguing mankind for centuries. What happens when the battle is internal, rather than external. In oneself. Will good conquer? Will evil prevail? Or, perhaps, will life as one knows it be ripped away? Destroyed in the aftermath. Four more weeks. To wait. To plan. To hope. To stew. There will be bloodshed. There will be pain. Either one walks away, or neither walks away. This I promise. This I guarantee. Sean Sterling. Mayson Colby. One man will prevail.
