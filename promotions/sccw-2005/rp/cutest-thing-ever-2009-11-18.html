Cutest Thing Ever

18 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18/09 Dear SCCW, I've not had quite the enormous amount of free time I would have liked this week. I find myself behind on projects, behind on my studies. I spend up all my free time studying for the bar, going to school, traveling, and preparing my career in professional wrestling. So, in lieu of some melodramatic stage play that resembles an episode of Gossip Girls, I present... PUPPIES! Enjoy the preasdfjiosp ** 'ALRIGHT STOP!' Flyer shouted, slamming his palms forcefully into Mary-Lynn's keyboard. The preceding was written by Mary-Lynn, who had her hands covered by Flyer's She turned over her shoulder to meet her mentor, confused. 'You can't just phone in your first SCCW story! It's how you're going to be remembered! Do you want to be remembered alongside puppies for the rest of your car-actually that's not so bad of an idea. People love puppies. You could get away with murder!' Flyer frowned. 'You could already get away with murder, being a lawyer and all. Think you're ever gonna murder someone?' Looking around her pristine cleaned kitchen, hot plate and toaster oven on the late 90's marble counter tops, you could notice the dychotomy between living room and eatery. Old Entertainment Weekly's and Time magazines scattered among the wreckage of copies of 'Criminal Law, 4th edition,' 'Trial of John H. Surratt in the Criminal Court fo the District of Columbia, Hon. George P. Fisher Presiding' & 'Women in Public and Private Law Enforcement,' in the living room. Stacks and stacks of books almost looked like foundations to the apartment, stacked as high as the ceiling. Her makeshift kitchen was the embodiment of Mr. Clean. Her living room and bed area? More like a disheveled library after a Typhoon and a Tornado has struck. MLM calls that a Tynado. 'I figure the faster I get this done, the sooner I can get you off that DUI charge.' Mayweather quipped, biting on the tip of a pen as she spun in her computer chair to a large stack of books. She reached in the middle and played Jenga, pulling out 'The DUI Book,' Written by William C Head. 'I mean, you were only riding a bike through Kansas plains. Not driving a commuter train.' 'You ever drive a commuter train?' Flyer asked. 'No. Why? Have you?' Mayweather said, turning to look Flyer in the eye with inquisition. 'When would I have driven a commuter train in my life?' Flyer outstretched his arms. 'Same question right back at you.' 'Hmmm,' Flyer said, winking. 'Touche.' Mayweather turned away, sighing. She dove her head nose first into the open print of the DUI book, reading through Kansas legislature. 'Why exactly where you in Kansas again?' Flyer rubbed the back of his head. 'Chasing tornadoes.' 'Tornadoes?' Mayweather frowned. 'Plural?!' 'Listen, you gotta make a good first impression.' Flyer began. 'When I joined PRIME, remember? I wrote that speech for you? Then I made you frame it and put it above your fireplac-Hey, why isn't the speech framed?' Flyer searched the room. 'Because I don't have a fireplace. Look, okay. I know you really enjoyed this silly goofy whatever you call this spiele with the banter and the flim flam,' Flyer frowned at the use of the word flim flam. 'But I'm a busy girl. I work 15 hours a week at your gym training students. I'm studying 30 plus hours a week for my bar exam next February. Right now I can only practice in California. I'd like to practice elsewhere. Just in case you get in trouble in Arkansas Or, say, maybe Kansas?!' Mayweather stood quickly, tossing her book off to the side. 'Not to mention all the time I personally put into my career and my body, the time i need to sleep, to eat, to travel from gig to gig, place to place. Not to mention you treat me like an assistant!' 'I don't.' Flyer winced. 'But that reminds me about this dry cleaning...' 'You see, this is why I joined SCCW. I wanna make it on my own Jack. I wanna see if I have it in me. And most of all, I'm sick of picking up your dry cleaning.' Mayweather stomped her feet. 'Really? I've always loved the dry cleaners. The smell of the fresh clothes.' Flyer inhaled deeply, 'The small talk with the asian who barely knows Engrish. Those little quarter machines where you can get bouncy pool balls. What's NOT to love?!' 'Then YOU Go!' 'Eh. I've got a lot to do.' Flyer said, rubbing the back of his neck. 'Y'know, Modern Family's on tonight. Plus, dinosaurs.' 'Dinosaurs?' 'Yeah. I have to go and commentate some dinosaurs.' 'Dinosaurs don't exist.' Mayweather blinked. 'Yeah. You think that.' Flyer smiled, making his way to the exit. 'It's because we're so good with our first rule. Tonight's T-Rex vs. Teradactyl. Guess who I'm going with!' Flyer reached up to some hooks hanging coats, and grabbed his. He bundled himself up and made one last look to Mayweather. 'Well. Have fun.' Mayweather called out. 'I'm gonna go get black out drunk. After the dinosaur fight club, I'm gonna need a ride. I dunno where I'll be at, so you have the program set to track my location by my iPhone, right?' 'Yes Jack. Everything's fine. Go have some fun. I've got to get some studying done.' 'PEACE!' Flyer shouted, slamming the door behind him. For a moment, there was nothing but peace in the room. Mayweather lowered her guard, shoulders hunched. She calmly returned to her computer chair, and spun it to her desk, where the open 'Trial of H Surratt' laid. As she read, she spent quite a long time, head propped up by her fist. After a few minutes of complete silence, Mary-Lynn Mayweather passed out, exhausted. She would have to continue her studies another day.
