Date: Today A long time ago, in a California far, far away... ''He won't be back.'' When she wanted to, Amy had a smile that could light up a room. ''At least not for a while,'' she finished. Well, he thought she finished. She tucked her head under his arm and seemed to be drifting to sleep, but that unusual silence and heavy sigh told Darcy to keep his eyes opened just a little longer. Sure enough, there was more to say. ''Y'think it should feel so good to have hurt somebody that badly?'' With her curiosity came the energy to raise up on her elbow, idly scratching his half-grown beard with a stray finger. He knew not to answer yet, because she had a follow-up question. She always had a follow-up question. ''Doesn't that make me as bad as him?'' When it was finally his turn, Darcy couldn't help but smile softly. He took a moment to make sure their eyes met - it always seemed to soothe her anxious thoughts if she could see absolutely everything in his expression while he spoke. ''In this case, you should throw a party for hurting someone - him, especially - that badly.'' He ran his hand through her hair and, thanks to the size difference between Markson and his tiny wife, ended up cupping half of her head when he said ''And trust me, you will never - and I mean ever - be as bad as Lane Stevens.'' ''On any front,'' he added, ''Because you're ten thousand times the person he'll ever be. You might not always make the best decisions, but you could eat a kitten and it still wouldn't put you in the same league with that asshole.'' Amy smirked at him for trying so hard to be so sweet, ''You don't think anybody would ever compare me to someone like that? I mean, I am kinda violen--'' She was interrupted only because Darcy put his hand half-jokingly over her mouth. ''Trust me, anybody would have to be crazy to compare you two.'' May 2008 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The diner wasn't gorgeous, but it was hardly shabby - especially for what the club sandwich cost him. Darcy Markson's wife was a safe distance away, so he welcomed the first beautiful woman he saw to sit with him. Thankfully, it's the same one he invited to be with him that morning. The woman saw him across the room and, after a brief pause to scan the restaurant for redheads, she sat down across from Mister Kathleen Amelia Campbell. ''If this is some kind of trap...'' You've got to admit, Katie Malick was smart to be asking that - but Darcy was smarter than to deal with that. He rolled his eyes, ''I live with her, Katie. Trust me, I barely have the stamina to keep up with one angry woman.'' ''Well, she's angry enough for the both of us.'' ''On the right day, she's angry enough to conquer most small nations.'' There was a pause between them, because Katie wasn't sure exactly how to react. Seeing her best friend's husband behind said best friend's back was a little questionable. Then again, that's only for people operating on the assumption that Katie and Amy were still friends. It was okay for him to rib the absent party, but she'd never interacted enough with Darcy to know if it was okay for her to do the same. So, the silence remained for a few moments and, after years of knowing one another, it seemed that Markson's wife had really rubbed off on him. More specifically, she'd rubbed off his sense of tact, and it didn't take long to become apparent. ''And just so you know,'' he added, ''This isn't anything creepy or sneaky.'' ''No, I know. You're not exactly the master of sneaky. I've heard the stories.'' ''She's just...'' Darcy sighed and shook his head. There was no polite way to finish that sentence. Katie knew it, and she offered him a kind smile, ''Actually, where is she?'' ''She's asleep. I stayed up most of the night hearing how stupid it was for you to kiss Jonathan, so I'm sure she'll be in bed past her usual noon o'clock wakeup.'' Darcy couldn't help chuckling when he commented on his wife's oft-nappy ways, but he couldn't laugh long. ''Speaking of which, where's Rhine?'' ''Also still asleep.'' Katie smiled as her cheeks lit up with the peculiar shade of red that could simply be labeled 'Let's not go there' rose. In kind, Darcy decided to move right along to the real reason they were there. ''You know more than me right now,'' he said simply. ''You've seen the... I dunno, the 'real' side to some of the people she's dealing with.'' ''I have,'' nodded Malick, ''Look... she knows I can forgive her for doing the wrong things with good intentions, but something is really wrong when you can sit around and have a friendly conversation with Alexandra Pier--'' There are a lot of worst case scenarios. In this case, the worst outcome would probably play out if Jonathan Rhine and Amy Campbell both happened upon the diner in their search for greasy breakfast foods, a fire ensued and small children were trampled. ''Why aren't we just eating at the hote---'' Actually, it was probably close enough to 'worst case' to count when Amy walked in. ''Oh, this is cute.'' No fires. No dead babies. Just a confused redhead sliding into the booth; Not beside her husband, but by Katie Malick, who was officially pinned between her former friend and the wall. ''So you two hang out now?'' Amy sneered, ''All chummy, huh?'' ''Not exactly,'' said Darcy. ''Before you're pissed at me, thinking I arranged some kind of meeting between Katie and I... I'm gonna go.'' He grinned before explaining ''Because you and Katie here need to talk, and in a situation where nobody has to watch their back.'' Katie shifted nervously beside Aimz, but she was more concerned with the redhead's husband. ''You called her here?!'' For the first time in a long time, Darcy Markson was the ringmaster. Instead of following Amy into an undesirable situation, he smiled - fully knowing that he'd thrown a curveball, and it seemed to have worked. Because she'd been so kind to come, Darcy offered Malick his own version of an apology. ''And yes, this was a trap - just not the kind that Amy would've put together. You've got nothing to be afraid of.'' With both of the women looking like deer caught in the headlights, he stood and kissed one of them on the forehead. No sense in trying to guess which one it was, or exactly how close she came to one-shotting him in the testicles that he so casually kept shielded by his hand in passing. After a moment of stunned silence, the two sighed and looked at one another. For a split second, it seemed like they'd been transported to the weeks and months before that moment, where they could sit next to each other without that elephant (or Rhine-o, if you will) in the room. ''Look, Ames - that whole 'you're no better than anybody ever said you were' thing, that was...'' Of course, like both Amy's friendship with Jonathan Rhine and Katie's trust of Darcy Markson, the moment was cut incredibly short in one dramatic motion. Amy wordlessly slid out of the booth and stood, extending her hand to her former friend to help her up. Katie cocked her head to one side but, after a few moments of silent contemplation, took the gesture. Neither moved at first. Finally, the woman otherwise known as The Red Raver put her hands in her pockets and stepped aside. ''You didn't order yet, so I'm going to give you a good ten seconds to leave before I do something stupid.'' ''You don't have to act so tough, I know you're not gonna-'' ''If I leave first, you're gonna stay here and I'm gonna come back and probably say or do something I don't mean.'' ''Amy, that's ridiculous.'' She was biting her lip when her friend spoke, if not only to avoid showing Malick the weakness she wanted to. ''Be smart about this. Walk away.'' ''That's j--'' ''Please fucking GO.'' That evening... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Admittedly, Amy wasn't the biggest fan of her husband's surprises. That was particularly true in instances where he threw her into an uncomfortable situation, which happened far more than she'd ever admit. To stick with the theme of things she would or wouldn't admit, the redhead would not relent in her silence -- the entire time they packed, the ride to the airport, the plane ride, the ride to the house... When she stepped in the door and tossed her bags next to where she was taking her sneakers off, the other scruffy, dark-haired man in Amy's life promptly pranced over to greet her. In the meantime, Darcy had picked up the bags she'd dumped and dragged them out of the entryway - pausing only when he heard her voice. Maybe the obedience had amused her. ''Aww, hey Reaper!'' Well, somebody's obedience had amused her. Once more, Darcy Markson had lost a war to the overweight feline he shared his wife with. At first, Darcy nodded. He could accept that she was angry. After a few seconds of thought, though - he couldn't let it go. ''So you're talking to the cat and still ignoring me?'' Once her hoodie was off, Amy had curled up on the leather sofa on the edge of the open livingroom. It wasn't the most comfortable, but it was the closest object to land on. ''The cat doesn't text me to meet him so he can trap me into some ovarian pow-wow.'' ''Okay...'' he sighed, ''I'm not even going to get into the logistics of that aside from pointing out HE DOESN'T EVEN HAVE THUMBS.'' Amy simply rolled her eyes and flicked the TV on with the remote she'd fished from between cushions. Her other husband quickly joined her, while Darcy stood preparing a rant on the other side of the room. Before he could speak, though, his redhead's lips curled into a grin. She ran a finger down the spine of her feline couch companion. ''Reaper, isn't it silly for Daddy to stick his nose where it doesn't belong?'' Wait for it... Wait... Well, no response from the cat. Darcy, however, had plenty to say... to Reaper. ''How about you tell Mommy that Daddy might not stick his nose where it doesn't belong if she could keep hers out of everybody else's business?'' Blink. ''Daddy seems to think Mommy can't handle herself.'' Stretch! ''When Mommy proves that she can handle her relationships like an adult, Daddy might not be forced to do things like trick her into talking things out with Katie, or... argue with her through the cat. What do you think, Reaper?'' Yawn. ''He probably thinks that you should shut up and pet him,'' Amy finally took her eyes off the black ball of fur and shot her husband a rather nasty stare, ''Which would officially make Reaper the only one in the room getting rubbed any time soon.'' ''You know the whole 'cutting me off from sex' thing doesn't exactly work when I'm the one who can go more than two days without crumbling.'' ''Hey!'' she growled, ''Don't knock my hobbies - you're their prime beneficiary.'' Finally, and after quite some delay, Reaper submitted his opinion. ''Nice. Are you happy? You made the cat leave.'' ''I'm the one who feeds him, Darcy - I'm pretty sure you're the one who pissed him off again with the whole 'no petting' thing.'' ''Are we going to bring the marriage counsellor back in, or should we leave him alone with his ball of yarn?'' After asking that, Markson forced himself to pause. He kicked off his shoes and sat next to Amy's outstretched legs. ''And do you think we could, y'know, talk about what happened after I left you and Katie alone?'' Amy's brow furrowed - she simply kept staring at the TV Guide channel. ''We kissed and made up. The end.'' ''... Oh my God, you killed her!'' ''I didn't kill her. I didn't talk to her. I made her leave before she had a chance to argue about what a fucking moron I think she is.'' Darcy sighed again, ''Seriously, do you plan on keeping that shit up?'' ''What sh--'' ''Don't even think about playing innocent,'' he commanded, ''She deserves to be happy, even if you think the guy's an asshole.'' ''Let's not forget you also think he's an asshole.'' ''Yeah, but I thought he was an asshole back when you started hanging out with him.'' said Darcy, ''I'm just capable of not letting my personal opinions force me to make someone else miserable by trying to control them.'' The TV was shut off within a split second, and the ensuing silence was nearly deafening. The only thing worse than that kind of silence is what follows it -- a very evil female stare. ''What do you call telling me what to do, or forcing me to sit in a room with someone who you know I feel like choking?'' Amy's eyebrows looked like they were about to join her hairline. ''Or doing everything but dragging me away from some of my peers?'' ''Desade isn't your peer.'' The argument was about to be over. If you'd ask Darcy, it was because Amy ended it. If you'd ask Amy, it was because Darcy decided to name names. ''Desade also isn't any of your business.'' Even as his little redhead stormed out of the room and announced that she was going to bed early, Markson couldn't help himself. Amy Campbell wasn't the biggest fan of facing reality at times, and it had always been Darcy's job to force her back down into it. ''You've always been easily influenced by bad people.'' He spoke up to make sure she could hear, even all the way down the hall. ''Everyone likes power and climbing the ladder, but you need to stop flirting with disaster before it really bites you in the ass, Ames.'' One day, a long time ago, somebody somewhere said something about 'Truer words'...
