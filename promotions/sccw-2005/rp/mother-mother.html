Date: Today Mother, mother There's too many of you crying Brother, brother, brother There's far too many of you dying You know we've got to find a way To bring some lovin' here today -- What's Going On - Marvin Gaye Momma, Hi, how are you? I hope this letter doesn't give you a heart attack! Sorry Jimmy and I haven't had a chance to call, it always seems like we are so busy and by the time we get some place where we could call, it is way too late. It is just after 11 here in California, which means it is after two there, and you've been in bed a long time. Speaking of Jimmy, I told him I was going to write to you and he said to tell you 'Hello!' and he loves you. That's his way of weaseling out of writing you a letter, I'm sure. I know you don't approve of what me and Jimmy do for a living, but they pay us so good! And it is nice being able to keep an eye on my baby brother and hopefully keep him out of trouble. We don't really have to wrestle that much, at least not on TV. Jimmy does some wrestling at the shows they don't put on TV. Most of the time girls in wrestling are only there to look pretty, but sometimes I think the reason they don't let me wrestle more is because they are afraid I will make the men wrestlers look bad! Right now all our shows are out west, but as soon as we head for Florida, I will let you know. You will come to a show, won't you? That would make me and Jimmy so happy! I'm part of a group still, just like last time, but this time I don't have to 'dress like the spook show' as you called it. We call ourselves 'Magnum Opus,' which despite what Jimmy will try to tell you, has nothing to do with penguins with guns. Our manager, and the man who got me this job is Oswald Smythe, only we call him Oz. He may be the smartest person I've ever met, even if he is a little strange sometimes. He has this amazing 67 Dodge Charger he calls Amanda and he won't tell any of us why! Weird, huh? Remember when Jimmy was a little boy and he loved to watch wrestling and he would run around the house with that ridiculous accent pretending to be The Rapscallion? Oz was The Rapscallion! Kind of funny how things come full circle like that. Besides Oz, Jimmy and me, Magnum Opus has Dru in it. You remember Dru, she came with me and Jimmy to the BBQ party for Great Aunt Emma's 100th birthday. She was the girl you said had 'such a pretty smile, and such sad eyes.' Even though Dru has been my best friend since Jenny Carmody in junior high and we've been from one end of the country to the other over the last five years, I sometimes feel there is so much about her I don't know. I just found out recently her parents are dead, and it started me thinking of all the things she and I never talk about like friends usually do. Hopefully, I will get a chance to get to know her better soon. Ok, Momma, I know you don't watch the shows since we're on cable and you don't believe in that, but I know you have to have heard that Vincent is part of Magnum Opus as well. And yes, Vincent and I are dating again. It hasn't been easy, but you always told me nothing good ever is. He reminds me so much of Dad sometimes it is scary, but isn't that what every little girl wants when she grows up, a man just like her father? He is very intense, and sometimes it almost seems like there are two people inside him, one of them this strong, caring man that I am absolutely head over heels in love with. But then sometimes there is the other Vincent, the one who is stubborn, and sometimes kind of childish, and maybe a little violent. And every bit as much as I love the strong, caring Vincent, I just want to hold this Vincent and tell him everything is going to be ok. Vincent is really the star of Magnum Opus, he was working with Oz before there was a Magnum Opus. He got into this again for the same stupid reasons I told you about before, the last time when he and I broke up. But Oz has been working very hard to show him there is more to wrestling than proving yourself against one person, and gradually he is getting there. He is more successful and bigger here than he ever has been anywhere before. He isn't here right now, he left to visit his mother's grave since we're here in southern California where she is buried. We had a fight before he left. It seems like we're fighting more and more lately, and not just over stupid things either. At our last show, one of our announcers (Dave Gibson, you should meet him sometime, I think you'd like him a lot) mentioned three people who he considered to be the meanest nastiest people in wrestling today, and he included Vincent in with the two others. One of them is Alexandra Pierce, and to say she is mean and nasty is like saying a gator has a few teeth. She is evil to the point I feel like crossing myself whenever I am in the same building with her. The other person I don't know much about, his name is Wyatt Connors, and since he used to work with Alexandra Pierce, I'm willing to bet he's a real puppy kicker too. Any way, it upset me that Vincent was included in that group, but he thought it was a good thing, a sign of just how successful he is. When he left we weren't speaking to each other, which means he will probably come back with flowers. Or chocolates. I'm hoping for flowers because while I may have got my height from Dad, I got my hips from you. I suppose I should wrap this up and find a stamp and envelope and mail it. Give Aunt Ernestine a kiss for me when you see her. I Love You! Your Little Rosie PS HI MOM, I LOVE YOU! - JIMMY Mother Tell your children not to walk my way Tell your children not to hear my words What they mean What they say Mother -- Mother - Danzig 'Hey Vi, you're looking good today.' Mayhem set the bundle of flowers he was carrying down in front of a gravestone in a small, private cemetery in the hills just outside of Los Angeles. The dark granite slab bore the words 'Violet Ann Matthews - Loving Mother - 1953 1999.' Sitting to one side of the grave, Mayhem took a drink from his bottle of water, scratched his head, and continued. 'I was, uh, just in the area, we're wrestling out here in California and since I had some time off between shows, I thought I'd stop by and.....I guess say hi, and catch up a little, I hope you don't mind.' The heavily muscled and tattooed man sat, quietly for a moment, almost like he was expecting an answer. 'Things are good right now, probably better than they ever have been. I'm part of a good team, I'm working for a decent company, they pay me to do what I do best, lay a hurting on people, but without any danger of the cops arresting me for it. I love it, absolutely love it. I think you would have too, it's a shame you OD'ed or I'd take you to the show and introduce you to some of the people. I think one of our announcers, Eugene, would have really liked you a lot.' With that, Mayhem chuckled to himself, taking another drink of water and watching a family paying their respects to one of their dead in the distance. 'I feel like I am finally getting somewhere. I've been winning a lot lately, and even when I don't win, I still look good. I have wrestled for a title, even if it wasn't one of the big ones. I didn't win that one, as a matter of fact, I kinda got fucked out of that, but I didn't really want that stupid title anyway. I'm the main event this next show, I get to beat up some old man with lung cancer. Ok, so he's only older than I am by three months, but still. If I can't beat Eddie Lambert I have no business in this sport.' Mayhem leaned over and pulled a few weeds from around the base of the marker, tossing them to one side and finished his bottle of water. 'This last show, I got included in a list with a couple of other wrestlers, Alexandra Pierce and Wyatt Connors! How cool is that? Fucking means I'm somebody now, you know? It would be like, I don't know, having my face put up on the Mount Rushmore of Wrestling's Bad Asses. Alex Pierce, Wyatt Fucking Connors and Mayhem, like the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse! Only without the horses, and there's only three of us...' He stood, brushing the grass off the legs of his jeans. 'Ok, so that was kind of stupid, but you get what I mean. I've got to get out of here, have a bunch of people to stop by and say hello to since I'm in the neighborhood. Don't get any funny ideas and go moving anywhere, I paid good money so I would know where to find you any time I wanted to.' He turned to leave, but stopped after a few steps, and turned. 'It's funny.....Mom, but you were always right. Always telling me I needed new friends, the bums I hung around with were holding me back. And it took Desa...Alexandra Pierce to make that clear to me. At the last show, she said something to me that sounded so much like you. Yeah, it was a lot fancier than how you used to say it, but.....' He cleared his throat and recited the words Desade had said to him during the Jon Rhine-Aimz cage match at Snake Eyes. 'There comes a time in everyone's career, Vincent, where you have to make the choice between those you hold dear, and success. I do hope that when you have to make that choice, you choose wisely.' 'But yur lil girl will fix that, won't she? Yur lil Dru will finally do somethin' ta make her mother proud, ta be worthy of her mother's love.' If you were in the same room as Drusilla Crawford, you would see her drain the rest of the fifth of whiskey and fall over on the bed, the empty bottle and the photograph both falling to the floor. If you were in the same room as Drusilla Crawford, you would be able to see the photograph where it sits on the floor, a grainy image of a pretty red headed woman, standing arm in arm with a smiling and much younger Oswald Remington Smythe. If you were in the same room as Drusilla Crawford, and you were to lean just a little closer to that picture, you would be able to make out the thin, spidery writing across the bottom of the picture.
