I forgot to fill in a title

8 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 8, 2009. Columnists, interns and reporters were running wild in Vegas each and every one trying to get themselves ready and situated for the press conference taking place in-regards to some of the new talent SCCW has raked in over the past couple weeks. The press conference also was going to cover other topics and discussion from in and around SCCW. One of SPIKE TV’s columnists, the geeky slender intern Steven was over-worked and in serious need of some rest. He’s split the past 6 days between writing a vigorous amount of columns about SCCW and spending time at Caeser’s Palace. An odds guru and world-class mathematician he was an excellent gambler but he got little to no respect for his day-job which was to highlight specific events in and around SCCW and ensure nothing got to the media before it was on Spike! Today’s task for Steven was to keep tabs on SCCW’s newest superstar, Brock Alyas. Steven was used to having to take care of the SCCW’s newest talents and ensuring they were well taken care of as well as specifically directed to the podium to answer a few questions from the media. Although Steven wasn’t a huge fan of telling professional wrestlers where to go and what to do, it was shitty job – but somebody had to do it. The podium was on the stage, the chairs were accounted for and it appeared as if SCCW finally were getting a hang of things and these press conferences were becoming strikingly easier as time passed. However, the SCCW didn’t exactly realize when they were signing a "superstar" performer, they were selling their souls to the devil. Instead of veering away from the acclaimed "drug induced" championship contenders around here – they’d signed a man who not only encouraged the use of hard drugs, made it clear he was on them on a regular basis. Long story short, Steven would have his hands full with trying to persuade Brock to show up to this conference sober. HAHA! Sober! Poor, poor Steven. The extra-medium sized button-up shirt stained with coffee and thick coke bottle glasses wrote a 500-page novel about Steven. Steven was running around like a wild man, clip board in hand, in search for the man he’d have to keep tabs on today, Brock Alyas. "Do you know where Brock Alyas is?" "Have you seen Brock Alyas?" "OMG where in the world is Brock Alyas?" Steven was becoming frantic as his job was definitely on the line with this one. Although, assigning Steven to keep a watchful eye on Brock and ensure he was following ‘company procedure’ was a mission impossible. One of the TV crew guys, with eyes as red as the devil’s dick pointed Steven outside the doors and towards a black Ford Explorer parked in the valet parking area. Steven wasn’t exactly thrilled about having to go and check-up on Brock but he was assigned a job and he’d have to complete that job. So Steven went marching out towards the car with good intentions. Steven was obviously very, very unaware of stoner etiquette because he knocked on Brock’s driver-side front window like he was the pooooleease. Inside the car, hiding behind jet-black tinted mirrors were four serious pot-heads in the midst of a very serious stoner talk about how buildings stand and were rudely interrupted in doing so. The SCCW obviously wasn’t truly ready for Brock Alyas because he wasted no time in rolling down his window and shoving a Glock45 pistol in the face of our intern, Steven. Who at this point was literally shitting his pants. Literally. Like help the poor guy find a new set of tighty whiteys. Seriously it was fuckin’ gross. "Yo what the FUCK do you think you’re doin’!?" Brock now opened the door and let about as much smoke as a small forest fire would’ve produced out of his car. "I-I! I’m ex-extremely sorry Mr. Alyas! I’m Ste-Steven and I’ve been asked to make sure you make it to the podium today!" Brock didn’t look very convinced. He was still upset his point about how the foundation of a building is like the skeleton of a human being, or some shit was cut short. At this point Brock’s fellow session-mates, most of which bearded, greasey television equipment men had to intervene on the situation. All of which were too stoned to properly put a sentence together, although Brock understood stoner ettiquite. "Yo, he’s coooool maaaan. He’s alrite. It’s Steven. He’s here to make sure you’re not too high to find your way around brooo," Brock lowered the hand-gun and snapped back into reality. The mortified look on his face had faded and he put his firearm away and extended a hand to help Steven up from off his back. Steven, reluctant to offer his hand and would’ve much rather pulled himself to his feet was skittish in offering Brock his hand. He stood to his feet, eyes never leaving Brock’s massive figure in comparison to his own. Still skittish as hell, Steven was still trying to collect his thoughts and reasons for his life flashing before his eyes. Brock was far too high to realize the terror he’d stricken in the eyes of Steven who at this point said fuck his job and was seen running off the scene still with clipboard in hand. "Aye yo, is that shit still lit?" The second Brock entered the arena backstage he stunk the entire place up. SCCW big-wigs we’re running around screaming and wondering… "WHO THE FUCK LET A SKUNK IN!?" The fresh scent of killa krypto chronic kush filled the backstage as camera men and janitors were quick to pick it up and ask Brock if he had anymore. For the most part, Brock said meet him in the parking lot in 20 easily making conversation with any common folk who liked their AAA dojah. In fact, Brock seemed more interested to talk to these guys about how compact the nuggets in his ounce bag were… much more interested than answering questions on a podium. He wasn’t even aware at this point that he’d even be on stage. "Mr. Alyas you really ought to consider using marijuana on your own time," And that was the first SCCW official to get straight back-handed… surely not to be the last. And Brock backhanded this man without second thought and reacted as if he were pouring a pot of coffee. Non-chalant as FUCK. Everybody was at a loss of what to say, they kind of just looked around and at each other. So after a few minutes of the reporters and interns waiting patiently… the press conference began with a big introduction for Brock Alyas who was assumingly a super-star performer from Detroit with a Ford F150 running motor and with a will to win that was second to none. Brock wasn’t too impressed with all the lights and flashes so he decided to cover-up how high he was with his pair of signature Gucci stunna-shades. SCCW officials considered this "inappropriate" and Brock refused to even use that word in a sentence. About mid-way through the conference where officials of SCCW quickly learned they’d need to speak as much as possible and keep Brock away from the mic at all costs, a question came directed at Brock. ‘Mr. Alyas… you’re up-coming debut is going to be against former SCCW Universal Champion, Lance Marshall who they call the Lion. What’s your opinion of Mr. Marshall and some of the talent you’ll be facing here?’ "If anybody has seen me compete before… there’s a reason not many are quick to sign up for a rematch. I’m not one to talk trash but for all you gambling addicts – I’d put your money on the rook. Former champ, current champ… who ever it is won’t enjoy the out-come of a match with me. I’m the type that makes guys consider early retirement." ‘Mr. Alyas… you’ve been known to be a bit of a trouble maker behind the scenes. SCCW is a very strict company who keeps a stern eye on their performers. How do you feel about those days coming to an end?’ "To be brutally honest with you, I’m high as fuck right now. I’ve got a gun in my waist line and I’m pretty drunk. I also plan on shot gunning some beers with the janitors after this and smoking some more dope then getting into my car and driving very, very fast with my eyes closed. I don’t think those days are done what so ever." The press conference turned into an up-roar of disapproval from quality SCCW sponsors and investors. Brock’s ignorance was the reason for him not quite understanding why everybody seemed so upset. "You guys seriously need to consider smokin’ a fatty. Look at you. Calm the fuck down there, Agent 007. Seriously though, how much was that suit?" This is the point in the interview when Dr. Nigel Kensington looked at one of his closest advisors and just shook his head in shame.
