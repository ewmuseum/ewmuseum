New Line O' Work

1 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-1 ((Apologies for how short this is, got caught up with schoolwork this week. It's finals time for me.)) 7:30 in the AM. That means it’s time for me to get my wake-up brew and get my ass out the door to whatever Vegas poker room I’m gonna be spendin’ my next eight hours in. Least that’s how it was for months and months for me, fresh outta the University of Maryland, with three years towards a degree completed and an obsession with online poker that was fucked up by the feds. Had no choice but to move out here. Atlantic City ain’t got the variety or quantity of poker that Vegas does, and that’s a fact. Past six months, it’s been the same beer and the same 7:30 in the AM, but it’s been me dragging my ass out the door to Sam’s trainin’ school before I spend eight hours in a poker room. Learnin’ me a new craft. Learnin’ to be a wrestler. You watch it on TV and you laugh, but then when you go to do it you realize that it’s no fuckin’ joke. My ass hurt every damn day I went to a poker table after trainin’, and sittin’ down for a long ass time ain’t good for it, but it’s who I am and it’s what I do. I’m clutchin’ a letter in my hand, from Sin City Championship Wrestling. Sent ‘em in a tape lookin’ for employment, and they’ve obliged me with a match this week on a premium cable channel and a train ticket to Boise, Idaho. I ain’t see nothin’ sinful about Boise, but what the Hell, I can’t be a picky bastard. Life on the road’s gonna be odd. Been livin’ outta this damn roadside inn in Vegas for one, maybe two years now. They tell me I’m gonna get upgraded to real hotels. I’ll be damned. Least I don’t own much in this world, can pack it all in a couple o’ duffels and drag the things with me. This is my chance. My shot to be someone in this world, other than in poker. Poker’s great. Ain’t never gonna say a bad word about sittin’ at a felt table for hours doing what I love. But it ain’t enough sometimes. Sometimes, you gotta have money comin’ in. It’s a shitty thing to wake up and not know where you’re gonna get your next meal, or to know that you’ve got to make $300 in a day to keep a roof over your head. An’ that’ why I’m getting on a train to Boise, Idaho, and leavin’ behind the bright lights and the big city. Came out West to try to make somethin’ of myself, so what’s a couple hundred more miles? Sides, I’m a gamblin’ man. You don’t get anywhere in this world without risking somethin’. Puttin’ my body on the line and some time I could be spendin’ playin’ poker, gainst another man’s skills. Heads up. Just the way I like it.
