Back In The Saddle

20 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20th 2009 It wasn’t exactly like riding a bike. It didn’t all come back to him like a shot. There wasn’t a great epiphany when he set foot on the canvas. Not at all. In fact, it was hard as hell. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘Jesus Christ that hurts! Have they changed the way they make these things?!’ The other man in the gym laughed loudly, a deep, hearty laugh that came from somewhere near his solar plexus. ‘You’ve just been out of the game for a while man, it’s your body that’s changed, not the ring.’ He was right. Three years is a long time in any profession and wrestling was no exception. To go from pushing your body to its limits every day to a ‘normal’ existence is hard. To go back to the wrestling lifestyle, however, is infinitely harder. For Josh Marquez, once of AWC, everything was just a little harder now. The ropes felt tighter, the canvas harder, the other guys bigger, stronger, faster. His training partner wasn’t helping. ‘Whatever happened to easing me back in Harry? I feel like I’m a boot-camp again! I just wanted a little refresher, get back into the swing of it slowly, you know?’ ‘Screw that, we need to go hard and get you back to your old self as soon as possible. It’s going to be hard enough to get another contract; if you aren’t 100% we might as well just leave it now.’ *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Ah yes, a contract. The grail at the end of this current quest. When Josh had left the wrestling scene, way back in 2006, he had done so like a thief in the night, quietly easing himself into pseudo-retirement without fanfare. He had shut himself off completely from the industry, focusing instead on developing his various business interests on the West Coast. Until recently, he had been happy with this. Then something changed. It wasn’t a considered adjustment, more a chance occurrence, a snippet on a website concerning the return of the GTT tournament. An innocuous matter you’d think... But like that falling apple, it had huge repercussions. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘Harry, I’m coming back.’ That was the minute that Josh Marquez moved from ‘Retired’ to ‘Active’. It was also the moment that reality hit him. AWC was gone, it’s roster dispersed far and wide. The easy way back, calling in old favours, was no longer an option. He was going to have to do this the hard way. So he hit the gym harder, watched his diet more closely, cut back on the late nights. He did his research, found out who was who in the business now, reading up and watching endless video on both the new faces and the old stagers. He stopped thinking like Josh Marquez entrepreneur and high-roller and started thinking like Josh Marquez, professional wrestler. Which brings us back to the gym. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘That felt…good’ ‘You’re getting it back Josh, I can see it. Everything is getting sharper, back to the way it was.’ ‘The way it was isn’t enough. I need to be better. I’m going to be up against some of the best in the business.’ ‘Well, okay then. If that’s what you want, I have a few things up my sleeve to get you to the next level.’ Marquez looks up at his friend and smiles, not a smile of humour but one of appreciation. ‘Give me all you’ve got.’
