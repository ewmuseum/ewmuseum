Living In Tequila Time

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 30, 2008 Prologue - Before the Bar Although the Path of Destruction is billed as 'living in Phoenix, Arizona,' his actual ZIP code is 85253, which places him in the affluent city of Paradise Valley. It is in Mayhem's home that Oz currently stands, examining himself in front of a full length mirror in the den. 'I just wanted to say thank you once again for letting me stay here while FUSE does this whole 'Stabbing Westward' thing. It is a refreshing change of pace from the usual endless string of hotel rooms I am used to. Are you sure you don't mind?' Mayhem didn't turn from his seat on the leather couch where he was scowling at the plasma screen on the wall, flipping channels in rapid succession with the remote. 'Dude, this house has so many rooms, I forget the number. I once got lost in the middle of the night trying to take a piss. It is way too much house for just me. You can stay here as long as you want.' Oz laughed, before coming over to sit in a recliner next to the couch. 'What happened to...oh, what was her name, the one you were watching UPROAR with the night I called you?' 'Tina. Yeah,' Mayhem replied, frowning. 'She split on me the moment she found out how much I was going to be on the road.' Oz shook his head. 'A shame, that. But it is not an easy life being a wrestler's significant other if you are not in the business.' 'So, what about you? Did you ever have anyone? Are there any little Ozlings out there?' Mayhem snorted, and turned the TV off, tossing the remote onto the marble-topped coffee table. Oz's eyes grew large. 'Good heavens no! I never put down roots permanent enough for a long term relationship.' Mayhem thought a moment, his face a mask of conflicting emotion. 'Did you ever regret it? The miles, the hotels, the greasy food in every dive along the road?' Oz replied without a pause. 'I strive to live my life without regrets, my good fellow. Makes things much easier, I find.' Mayhem frowned, and was about to point out that striving to live one's life without regrets and not actually having any regrets were not the same thing when Oz's Blackberry chirped. Oz read the message, and stood. 'And that is my cue to take my leave. I shall return late tonight, no need to wait up.' 'Where you going? Got a hot date?' Mayhem raised one eyebrow and fixed Oz with a look. 'Ah. Well. Ummmm,' Oz stammered. 'Actually, Drusilla lives here in Phoenix, you know. And there were some of us getting together at a bar in Scottsdale...' Mayhem's expression looked like the kid who knows he's about to be picked last in gym class. 'Oh,' was all he said. Oz sighed. 'We talked about inviting you along, but...I will be honest with you, you know if Dru is there, then Rosa will be there as well, and we just were not certain you would want to be a part of that.' 'It will be at a bar, with lots of other people around,' Mayhem said with a shrug. 'I think it would be nice to see everyone again. Where you guys going?' 'Some new bar in Scottsdale called Stoney's, I guess it is all the rage. Tonight is something called 'Kamikaze Karaoke' or some such. Go change, I am sure everyone will be happy to see you.' After Mayhem left the room to change, Oz added, 'Or at least almost everyone.' Chapter 1 - Before the Tequila Even though it was just barely past seven, and a weeknight at that, there was a line snaking around the side of the building as Oz pulled his newly purchased 1967 Dodge Charger to a halt at the valet stand. The large faux-granite sign over the main entrance proclaimed this was 'STONEY'S.' Mayhem stepped out of the passenger side of the car and looked warily at the line and the large, bald African American with the clipboard who kept the undesirables out of the bar. On the other side of the car, Oz smiled at the valet as he handed him the keys, along with a folded one-hundred dollar bill. He leaned in close to the man to give him instructions on the proper care of a classic car. 'Make sure she is not parked too close to anything that may ding her. If you are still working when I get out...' He paused to look at the young man's name tag, '...Bradley, and she is as pristine as she currently is, there are two more of those for you.' 'Sir, I'm gonna park this one dead ass in the middle of four spaces. Hell, if I had velvet ropes, I'd put her on display for you.' Both men watched the car pull away and started towards the entrance. 'So, they're already here, yeah?' Mayhem questioned. 'Cause I'm not sure I wanna wait in line for fucking karaoke.' 'Vincent, Vincent, Vincent. Oh ye of little faith. Observe, and learn.' Oz approached the Bearer of the Clipboard, Bastion of the Beautiful People and Bouncer at Stoney's, and smiled widely. 'Hello, my good man, I think you will find our name on your list. I am-' 'First, I ain't your man.' Even though he interrupts Oz and is technically answering him, it is Mayhem he fixes with his stare. 'Second, yeah, I know who you are. You're Mayhem. You probably don't remember me, though.' Mayhem shrugs slightly. 'Didn't think so. I was working security at Wells Fargo Arena night of the first televised Heatstroke card, and was one of the guys you kicked the shit out of during your tantrum. So if you so much as sneeze funny in my bar tonight....' Mayhem rolled his eyes. 'Look man, that was, what? Over two years ago? You gotta let that shit go, man. It'll eat you up inside.' Next to Mayhem, Oz gave him a sidelong glance with one eyebrow cocked, but Mayhem never saw it. Our Bald Bouncer of Bad Attitude doesn't look at the clipboard as he steps to one side, addressing Oz this time. 'Yeah, you're on the list. Can't miss 'em. Enjoy your night.' Stoney's is a bar like the vast majority of bars of its kind. A large open dance area on one side, pool tables and dart boards at the end of that. At the other end of the room are tables for people looking to sit and socialize. In the middle, along the wall farthest from the entry, is the bar, with its selection running the gamut from cheap pitchers of domestic beer all the way up to shots of premium liquor that will set you back nearly as much as Oz tipped Bradley to park his car. Next to the bar is the towering DJ booth, where on a normal night someone with a creatively spelled name like 'DJ K-AHHHH-TIX' or 'mistah lemonjello' hammers the patrons with a wall of audio assault. And people think wrestlers have funny names. But this is no normal night, this is Wednesday, and at Stoney's, that means one thing. 'All right! Let's give it up for our last singer, Steve!' The vaguely Asian woman with the headset mike and far too much enthusiasm takes a wireless microphone from Steve as the crowd cheers and woots for him, most of it from the table of Steve's drunken frat brothers. 'I've never heard Nirvana sound like that before!' The woman climbs onto their table, using an empty chair as stairs. Most of the college boys don't even try to be subtle as they stare up her dress. 'It looks like we've got a lot of new faces in here, so I'm going to do my spiel one more time! My name is Kamikaze Kallie, and tonight is the Super Bowl edition of...' She holds the mike down to the table and like Pavlov's dog hearing a bell, they respond. 'KAMIKAZE KARAOKE!!! WOOOOOOO!!!!' 'That's right boys, it's Wednesday night at Stoney's, and that means Kamikaze Karaoke!!' She dismounts the table, gracefully sliding down the largest guy, who grins like an idiot and shares high fives with his buddies as she moves out to the floor. 'The 'rules' are simple. You grab one of the numerous binders making their way around the room and find a song you think you want to hear someone sing! You will notice there are dollar amounts next to the songs, and there is a reason for that! There are certain songs that just should cost more, either because they are embarassing, or hey, we just get tired of hearing them! 'Friends In Low Places,' I'm looking at you! Next time your waitress comes around, you hand her a slip with the song name, and your money, and point out your victim! They'll bring them to me or one of my lovely assistants, and we'll challenge them to sing your song! And the best thing is, they'll never know who challenged them unless you tell them it was you!' By this time, Kallie has moved over to where the pool tables are, and the group of muscleheads in their Patriots jerseys (all six of them are wearing a Tom Brady jersey) stop playing pool to admire her. 'Now, I know what you're thinking, because besides being hot, I am also psychic! You're thinking 'But Kallie, what if I don't want to sing the dumb old song someone picked out?' Well that's fine, because here at Stoney's, we don't FORCE anyone to sing! Of course, you know what that makes you?' She holds the microphone out to the table of New England Bradys fans, and one of them grabs it and yells 'IT MAKES YOU A PUSSY, JUST LIKE THE GIANTS! YEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHH!!!! GO PATS!!!!!' The room both boos and cheers, being split pretty much down the middle on who they think will win the upcoming Super Bowl. Kallie just laughs as she moves away from them, but she does toss off a parting comment. 'By the way, you boys DO know there are at least a few dozen other guys on the team with him, right?' With that, she moves up to the bar and hands the wireless mike to the bartender. 'Right now, we're going to take a short break and maybe spin some music by the artists who actually recorded it, because Kallie has to go powder her nose.' The room is filled nearly to capacity with an eclectic demographic: blue collar workers dressed up and out to rub shoulders with the rich; bored college students out blowing off the research paper that is due next week; tech geeks who make up for lack of pedigree with money gained from their startups; prowling cougars, their age camouflaged beneath layers of makeup applied like bondo over their growing wrinkles. Add to this mix everyone in town this week for Super Bowl XLII, and Stoney's is one step from qualifying as chaos. In the corner of the room is a table that stands out here, and would stand out nearly anywhere. In the corner is an enormously fat man with greased jet black hair and sideburns, working on a mound of chicken wings nearly as big as the petite oriental woman leaning against him. Next to her are the most ordinary pair at the table, a balding middle aged man and the woman who you just know by looking at is his wife. On the other side of the table is Supporting Cast Nobody #3 from the backstage buffet in Albuquerque, all pale, sulky and emo in his Dashboard Confessional t-shirt. Next to him is a woman in jeans and a black t-shirt with the Tapout logo on the front of it, but she makes the simple outfit seem like haute couture. She wears only the barest amount of makeup needed, which only makes her natural beauty all the more striking. Her red hair is pulled back loosely, all curls and ringlets, a few escaping to stray over her face, but they look like that's what they're meant to do. This is the table Oz and Mayhem are headed towards, until Oz sees the redhead. He stops, blinking at her a moment, until Mayhem turns and asks, 'What's wrong, dude?' Oz shakes his head and says 'It is nothing. For a moment, Drusilla reminded me of two very different women at the same time.' Back at table, standing at its head, gesturing animatedly, Austin 'Wombat' King holds court. Thanks to Andrew Pierce's Nose Bleed last UPROAR, both his eyes are blackened and he sounds slightly congested. Combined with his thick accent and Australian sayings, it's a wonder he even understands himself. 'So's I does what any red-blooded bloke would do, and I told the cobber 'Unhand the Sheila, mongrel, or face the Wrath of the Wombat!' So I sees he's 'bout to piss like a roo on a rock, and we circle, preparing t'hava blue, right? Next thing I know, some drongo with the cam's up in me gob, and I'm all blind. Damn freckle decides that's his ticket, and WHAM! He dent's me trunk. So I'm down, but not long, I'm mad as a cut snake, and about to give him what for, then the fuzz shows with the boys from the ambo, and I-' Austin stops his reimagining of last week's events when he sees Mayhem. 'Whathafuck you doing here, mate?' Drusilla smiles and stands. 'Now, Austin, no need to be rude. I invited Oswald, and since he's managing Mayhem now, it was only natural he come along.' She moves over and hugs them both, Oz first, and then Mayhem, asking him 'Seems like it has been forever, how have you been?' Mayhem stepped back from the hug, looking her over. 'I'm good. Man, Dru, you got hot. Last time I saw you, it was the short black hair and goth vampire queen gimmick.' 'Ugh! Don't remind me of that! What were we thinking? The Succubus Club?' The redhead rolled her eyes before turning to the table. 'Everyone, this is Mayhem. Mayhem, this is Toluta'oa Tatafua, he retired from wrestling and is now Tonga's number one Elvis impersonator, and the lovely lady at his side is his girlf...excuse me, his fiancee, Kimmie Harada. Next to them is Bink and Brooke Stevens, Bink decided being a wrestling referee wasn't challenging enough and is now a kindergarten teacher. Of course you already know Jimmy and Austin.' Mayhem looked around a moment. 'Hey, nice to meet you. Where's Rose?' Jimmy had stood to make room at the table for the new arrivals, and now he stepped up to Mayhem, drawing himself up to seem as tall as he could. Given that he was exactly a foot shorter than the Path of Destruction, he still seemed far too short. 'Rosie's in the bathroom, asshole. I can't believe you had the balls to come here.' Jimmy's speech was slightly slurred, and his breath stank of alcohol. Mayhem just looked down, his face emotionless. You could almost read the branching logic behind his eyes, 'Ignore him' and 'Kill him' were the two options being debated. Luckily, before either conclusion was reached, Drusilla intervened. Her voice was honey sweet and she put her hand softly on them smaller man's arm. 'Jimmy, sweetie, would you go get me another Shirley Temple, I seem to be out.' Jimmy immediately stepped away from Mayhem and nodded, perhaps a little too eagerly. 'Sure thing Dru, I'll make sure they put two cherries in it like you like.' With that, he practically jogged to the bar. After Jimmy was gone, Oz nudged Mayhem in the back, then nodded his head in Austin's direction when Mayhem turned. Oz spoke no words, but his expression made his meaning clear. Mayhem rolled his eyes and sighed, then turned to Austin. 'Hey, look, sorry about the clothesline, and busting up your match, why don't I buy you a beer as a way of saying sorry.' Austin thought about this for about half a second before his face split with a smile. 'Make it a pitcher, mate, and we'll be too right!' Once they had left the table, Drusilla immediately began changing the seating. 'Okay, when they get back, let's put Austin down there, at that end. He'll want to be the center of attention anyway, and that's the best place for it. We'll put Rosa next to him across from Brooke, and then Jimmy, and I'll sit next to him, and you,' she pointed at Oz, 'will sit next to me, and we'll put Mayhem at the end, as far away from Rosa as we can. If he steps out of line, Tolu, sit on him. You will have the best chance of shutting him down, but be careful, if he's been drinking, and let's face it, I expect him completely intoxicated before this night ends, then he is even more dangerous.' The massive Tongan grunted around a chicken wing, 'I got your back, you know that.' Tapping her chin, Drusilla said, 'I don't think there will be any trouble, but then again, I didn't expect him to show up, either.' Drusilla was so intent on the seating, she failed to notice the missing member of their table come back from the bathroom. 'You didn't expect who to show up? Hey Oz, how are you?' Rosa Dawes made her momentary splash in the world of professional wrestling as Black Rose, Drusilla Crawford's bodyguard and secondary enforcer of The Succubus Club, a goth themed stable. When she wrestled, she was billed as 6' 3'' and 201 lbs, which was only a slight exaggeration. She was rock hard, with pale white skin, long jet black hair, lips, fingernails, and even black contacts. The woman who stepped in close to hug Oz was barely recognizable as the same woman. Where some women her size were hard and almost masculine, with distorted features, Rose displayed none of this. In her own way, she was as beautiful as Drusilla was, if you liked them that big. She was still tall, of course, nearly freakishly so for a woman, standing over six feet in sneakers. Her weight was anyone's guess, but her features and body were much more feminine now, softer and curvier while still being fit, filling out her low hanging faded jeans and black Rhinestones t-shirt admirably. Her hair was a soft blonde, cut short, pixie style, and had a few pink and purple strands in the back. Oz stepped back, his jaw hanging open. 'Will you look at you? Both of you ladies have come so far once you ditched that horrid goth look. Now if we could just get your little brother to come around.' Mayhem and Austin returned to the table, Mayhem carrying a serving tray with three pitchers of beer in three different shades and a stack of plastic glasses. Austin was in the middle of telling Mayhem another story about his backstage exploits. 'So 'ere I'm thinking she's bout to drop t'her knees an clean me pipes right 'ere in the hall, fronta god 'n' everyone, then the Pierce girl comes down the hall with her Uncle, Randy Andy Pierce hot on her heels, and 'at was 'at.' 'Yeah, wow. That's really something,' Mayhem said, obviously not paying attention to the Aussie. He sat the tray on the table, and turned to Rose. 'Hey.' 'Hey yourself.' Her response was as stiff and forced as the hug that followed it, minimal contact, with as much feeling as hugging a side of beef. It was almost painful to watch for those at the table who had known these two as a couple in happier times. When he pulled back, Mayhem looked at her shirt and made a face. 'You never struck me for a Rhine fan. Hey, you should ask Oz about getting backstage to meet him, I understand he can do that now.' 'I...that is....There was a waitress in the Denny's in Flagstaff, where Mayhem and I stopped for breakfast the morning after the Albuquerque show...' Mayhem took the seat at the end when Drusilla motioned him towards it, a sarcastic smirk on his face. The Tongan next to him belched, tossing the last bones of his chicken wings onto the pile. 'Hey bra, I'm about to order some more wings, you want in on that?' Chapter 2 - Three Shots SHAKE IT. SHAKE IT. SHAKE IT LIKE A POLAROID PICTURE! HEY. HEY, YEAH. His name was Alfred, and he was a grandfather celebrating his eighty-second birthday at Stoney's. He was also an amazingly good sport, singing a nonsensical song by a band who didn't even know how to spell the word 'outcast' properly. Back at the table of wrestlers, those who had made the switch to hard liquor had just slammed down the third round of tequila shots. Drusilla was still drinking Shirley Temples, obviously playing the role of designated driver for her BFF Rose and Jimmy, Rose's overprotective little brother. Oz had opted out on the grounds that he never drank anything stronger than wine, and doing shots of wine would just seem wrong. Kimmie, the quiet fiancee of the enormous Tongan used this opportunity to announce to everyone at the table that she was pregnant, and Bink had to work the next morning, and as he had put it 'Hangovers in a room with thirty-two five-year olds hurt worse than chair shots.' Of the other six, it was Bink's wife Brooke that had surprised everyone when she said she wanted in on shots if they were tequila. She had explained her love of tequila as part of a long conversation with Austin about the more colorful parts of Tijuana. Brooke and Wombat had hit it off well, so well in fact that Bink was left squirming in his seat uncomfortably. Austin resumed his telling of his first experience at one of Tijuana's notorious 'donkey shows' once the shot glasses slammed on the table, living up to Drusilla's prediction that he desired to be the center of attention. At the far end of the table, Mayhem only half listened to Austin's outlandish tale, carrying on a conversation with Tolu. 'So, I love the shirt, but aren't you a little big to be a luchadore?' Tolu nodded towards Mayhem, making reference to the white t-shirt he wore that indeed, was emblazoned with the word 'Luchadore' across the chest. Mayhem laughed. 'This was a gift from 'Perfect' Raymond Pierce. He gave it to me and kept calling me 'Mikey Mayhem' and reminiscing on the feud I had in Puerto Rico with 'El Tigre Phantasma' or something. I've never even been to Puerto Rico!' 'Oh, wow, Raymond Pierce gave you a gift? Isn't he, like, an eighteen time world champion or something?' Both Mayhem and Tolu looked at Kimmie, and Oz started to set her straight before she started giggling. 'I even hear he won a Nobel Peace Prize, but lost it on a DQ!' 'I'm giving Pierce your number in case he ever needs to replace Joe Bob,' Oz said and then drained his glass of wine. 'No, no, really, Succubus Club was NOT a bad gimmick,' Jimmy was proclaiming drunkenly. 'We should so totally get it back together! If this Oz guy down here is as hot shit as you say he is, Dru, he could get us into FUSE no problem!' Drusilla laid her hand on Jimmy's arm, calming him. 'I have no doubt that Oswald could get us into FUSE, but as far as The Succubus Club is concerned, I think I can speak for your sister when I say if either of us never, ever see another bottle of Black Number One hair dye again, it will be too soon.' 'A-fucking-men!' Rose added, eliciting a round of laughter. 'Wait, wait. Hold on.' Mayhem turned to where Oz sat at his left hand. 'Didn't you tell me you had five open contracts? And you also told me we were going to need a stable. Jimmy's high flying insane spot-fest style is a perfect balance to my high power brawling. Hell, his name's Misfit, I'm Mayhem, we just need to come up for M names for Rose and Dru's new looks and we have a theme going!' Oz looked skeptical. 'I don't know...' 'I'll be Misery,' Rose chimed in almost immediately. Drusilla looked around the table. 'I think this could work. I'll be Mischief, and Oswald here can be our Maestro.' 'Crikey! Count me in! I'll be Wombat!' Jimmy looked at Austin, puzzled. 'Dude, weren't you listening? Wombat doesn't start with M!' 'I'm from Australia, mate! It's upside down!' Chapter 3 - Eight Shots YOUNG MAN, I WAS ONCE IN YOUR SHOES. I SAID, I WAS DOWN AND OUT WITH THE BLUES. I FELT NO MAN CARED IF I WERE ALIVE. I FELT THE WHOLE WORLD WAS SO TIGHT. The six Patriots fans in their matching Tom Brady jerseys are lined up on the dance floor, and most of the capacity crowd at Stoney's is on their feet and doing the Y-M-C-A with them. The song ends, and while the cheering dies down, Kallie collects the microphones from the group. 'Is everybody having a good time?!' Kallie shouts into her headset mike. When the response isn't quite what she hoped, she asks again. 'I SAID IS EVERYBODY HAVING A GOOD TIME!!!!?' This time the bar is loud enough that Kallie seems satisfied. 'It has come to Kallie's attention that we have some celebrities in the house, some sports stars!' The crowd roars its approval before Kallie continues. 'No, we don't have anyone from either of the teams in Sunday's Super Bowl, but we do have some wrestlers from FUSE which will be having a show the day before at the Jobing.com Arena!' As Kallie is making her way towards our table, the crowd is obviously disappointed at not getting to see players from the Giants or Patriots. Still, there are enough FUSE fans in attendance to keep the cheering from being too weak. When she reaches the table, she stands between Austin and Rose. Austin, ever the attention whore, smiles and starts to stand, until Kallie turns her back to him and faces Rose, handing her a microphone. 'Hi there, what's your name?' 'Um, Rose.' It is obvious Rose was never given very much time on a microphone, she's too far away from it and speaks softly enough that it is hard to hear her. She leans into the mike instead of moving it closer, and repeats herself. 'Rose.' Kallie continues to smile, eternally enthusiastic. 'And are you a wrestler, Rose?' 'Yeah. Well, I used to be. I might be...Yes. Yes, I am a wrestler.' Rose looked around the table, and Kimmie gave her a thumbs up with a nod. Kallie placed a slip of paper in front of Rose on the table. 'Well, Rose, someone here wants to see how good you can sing!' Rose picks the paper up and reads it, frowning. 'I don't.....' Kallie waves her hands, encouraging the crowd, and they oblige her by starting to chant 'ROSE! ROSE! ROSE!' Rose sighed and pushed her chair back, standing. Kallie seemed surprised at Rose's size, blinking a few times before remembering her job. 'Everybody give it up for Rose!' The crowd whooped and cheered as Rose stepped back a bit more to see the monitor for the words of her upcoming performance. Kallie turned and whispered something into Austin's ear and gave him a wink as she walked off, leaving the Aussie grinning from ear to ear. The guitars began their jangling, to be joined by the thumping of the drums as 'Who Knew' by Pink began to play. YOU TOOK MY HAND YOU SHOWED ME HOW YOU PROMISED ME YOU'D BE AROUND UH HUH THAT'S RIGHT I TOOK YOUR WORDS AND I BELIEVED IN EVERYTHING YOU SAID TO ME YEAH HUH THAT'S RIGHT IF SOMEONE SAID THREE YEARS FROM NOW YOU'D BE LONG GONE I'D STAND UP AND PUNCH THEM OUT 'CAUSE THEY'RE ALL WRONG I KNOW BETTER 'CAUSE YOU SAID FOREVER AND EVER WHO KNEW The crowd cheers during the short pause between lyrics. Rose's singing voice is good, particularly to drunk, rowdy ears. At the table, there is some consternation among everyone except maybe Austin, who is complete in his cluelessness. This song parallels Rose's and Mayhem's relationship nearly exactly, and nobody is certain how Mayhem will react. Tolu casts hard stares around the table, letting everyone know if this is their idea of a joke, he doesn't appreciate it. Everyone shrugs, silently proclaiming their innocence. Mayhem's reaction is to sit, his face a blank mask, staring at the table in front of him. IF SOMEONE SAID THREE YEARS FROM NOW YOU'D BE LONG GONE I'D STAND UP AND PUNCH THEM OUT 'CAUSE THEY'RE ALL WRONG AND THAT LAST KISS I'LL CHERISH UNTIL WE MEET AGAIN Collectively, the crowd begins to quiet. It is obvious that this is not just karaoke to the young woman singing, this is a purging, a cleansing of her soul. Rose continues, tears escaping from her closed eyes to leave twin trails down her cheeks. AND TIME MAKES IT HARDER I WISH I COULD REMEMBER BUT I KEEP YOUR MEMORY YOU VISIT ME IN MY SLEEP MY DARLING WHO KNEW MY DARLING MY DARLING WHO KNEW MY DARLING I MISS YOU MY DARLING WHO KNEW WHO KNEW The song ends, and Rose tosses the mike onto the table with a loud *THUD* and practically runs to the bar. Her brother is up and unsteadily weaves his way behind her. 'I....Wow. I think we need a break after that one, folks. DJ, play Kallie something peppy!' Kallie gathered the mike and left the table, with Austin following her moments later. Mayhem finally looked up from the table and started to stand, only to be pulled back into his seat by Tolu. 'Let her be, bra. You ain't gonna fix this one right now.' Tolu followed this up by waving for their waitress, who gave him an index finger up as she was taking orders from the table next to theirs, and Tolu nodded, signaling that would be fine. At the bar, Rose was approached by several men, each of them wanting to console her. Most of these she waved off, but there was one who was as persistent as he was drunk. Rose sighed, 'Look, it's not that I don't appreciate the offer, but....' 'He is here! Point him out to me! It ain't that goofy little fucker with the black eyes, is it?' Rose's would be hero sat his beer on the bar and began rolling up his sleeves. 'He'll have to shit through a straw when I'm done with him!' Rose sighed again, just shaking her head. She pointed to where Mayhem sat with a scowl on his face. 'Oh. Yeah. Him? I wish you guys all the luck in the world, have you tried counseling?' Tolu had just finished ordering an entire bottle of tequila from the waitress, and he leaned in close to his wife-to-be. 'I know you said you didn't want me drinking anymore, K, but my man over here is hurting, and I don't want him to have to drink alone. Is that okay?' Kimmie nodded, smiling at her man. 'That's fine, my Tongan teddy bear. I'm going to go make sure Rose is ok.' Chapter 4 - Tequila O'Clock FREEDOM'S JUST ANOTHER WORD FOR NOTHING LEFT TO LOSE NOTHING, THAT'S ALL THAT BOBBY LEFT ME BUT FEELING GOOD WAS EASY LORD, WHEN SHE SANG THE BLUES HEY, FEELING GOOD WAS GOOD ENOUGH FOR ME GOOD ENOUGH FOR ME AND MY BOBBY MCGEE It is quite obvious that listening to Jimmy that his sister Rose not only got all the size of the family, she also got all the singing talent. Still, what he lacks in talent, he makes up for with drunken gusto. At the table, Tolu has stopped drinking tequila, but only because Mayhem holds the bottle and is drinking directly from it now. The table is mostly silent, with the exception of Brooke and Austin, who are singing the 'la la la' parts of Janis' song along with most of the bar, which is to say loud and off key.
