How did I get here?

24 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/09/07 How did I get here? Why did it come to this? What chance did I ever have as a kid to do anything normal? Kids my age growing up were playing action man, watching turtles on TV, pretending to be ninjas, having a childhood. I was in riots, actually helping to create weapons, explosives, learning how to actually be like a ninja, instead of playing one because reptiles on TV do. What chance did I have when my mam decided to cop off with that militant Marxist of my father? What are the odds on that happening? Billions to one? Billions against that I would have to pop out and be born to those two, I must be the most unlucky man alive. Then again, am I? I have just escaped a life sentence for killing someone, which I suppose is a bonus; then again, I have had to move to a different country to escape backlash. I am a marked man around the streets of my home, why? Because I grew a conscience, because I actually figured my life was a waste, fat lot of good that helped me. I have no money, one set of clothes and an address for a Jimmy, corner of 59th street. I have no idea who he is or what he can do but apparently he can help me. It has been 3 days now, living on the streets. If the fella has a nice shower and a warm bed for the night he has helped me more than anyone else in this place. Ever since getting off the plane I have not been welcome here in New York. Apparently I am only here on a tourist visa – and my details have been scanned into the machine. Great, 3 months and I will be deported unless I find my feet and get a job or disappear. Mind you, disappearing isn’t too hard, a country with countless people, all in the system, crowds everywhere. I can get away easily. I just need to find some work, no questions asked kind of work. Underground I would say, you know, like they do in the movies. Pit fighting? Gym work? Hell, even a bartender would be good around now. Anything to get some cash, so I can go to some random Slovakian apartment landlord and live with some cockroaches. 25 and living with cockroaches, hah – there is a thought, gotta love this world. All the injustice and suffering makes you just want to smile, at least it isn’t just me though eh – looking around this dark alleyway I can see at least 3 bums, all with stories to tell. One of them told me last night that he used to be a dentist, successful, rich, and powerful. Then he found his wife was cheating on him with his brother – but she had filed for divorce already without telling him on the grounds of him being an abusive husband, she managed to get to court, swollen, tearful and upset, after her new lover; his brother of all people; agreed to pound the crap out of her! Naturally the jury fell for it, hook, line and sinker. She took everything and he ended up here, another bum – just like this lawyer and the actor, one was disbarred because he fiddled taxes for a living and was found out, the other just couldn’t get a job. Life is tragic for some, probably tragic for all at one point in their lives, but grief, well true grief at least and tragedy come hand in hand with joy and success. All these people around me have experienced joy and success, now life sees fit to give them a new purpose, a new challenge, whether they are up to the challenge only they can answer. The whole life thing, it’s a game to us all, a game where you are forced to play, the only time you stop playing you die. Success can be defined how? Well it is all down to the player at the end of the day. I suppose only the player can decide, at the end of the game whether it was a win or a loss. Motivated by money? Fine, you win if you have had a lot of it by the end of your life. Motivated by love, I suppose if you have loved and loved until you die then you win also. Myself, I haven’t got a clue what motivates me any more, to survive, compete, that is all I can do right now. I guess I will find out what motivates me eventually once I have found my feet but I really need to sort out my crap first. Let’s leave these bums behind and get to Jimmy, see what he has to say. The sun is up now and its dawn, let’s hope he is an early riser. It seems 59th street is actually a church, Jimmy is a bishop, it surprises me, sitting here in the church now listening to him preach about this and that, talking about getting a fairer deal for all people and that God and Jesus are watching over us. I never thought they were, I figure if they are then they are laughing and we must be in some sort of weird reality show because how can God let us all suffer like we do? Maybe I will ask Jimmy when the service is over. I like this church, it has an air of peace over it, it is warm, comfortable and when I came in no one seemed to judge me with my unkempt homeless appearance and my one bag of clothes. "Good morning Mr Jensen, please come with me" A whisper in my ear, after a tap on my shoulder, in a church! This isn’t good. Temporarily I freeze, thinking that the people back home are spreading here too. I whisper back the only thing I can think of, which, understandably is a bit of nonsense: "Who, what?" "All your questions will be answered in due course, come with me – I am a friend" Naturally, I am hesitant, but what can I do? You are in a church service and some guy comes and tells you to follow him, if you protest then you draw attention to yourself and your enemies just might go for the jugular there and then. So I follow this guy in silence down the back of the church downstairs into the basement. Now I am ready for anything, I mean, I can handle myself, but going down to a dirty old church basement still makes you on edge, even at over 6 feet tall and athletic! Everything echoes in here, the fella ahead is chatting away to me but I aint listening, I am just concentrating on my footing and making sure I don’t fall into the river I can hear in this dark church basement, well at least I think it’s a river, it definitely has a watery sound even though I cant see it. My head is pounding with that watery noise; it’s like a waterfall, getting louder and louder. Where are we heading? Toward the sea? It seems like we have been walking for an hour yet we still go on. The air smells salty, definitely going toward the sea, still in darkness. Yet I see a small light ahead, just a pin prick of hope – I think the tunnel is coming to an end. Upwards we are going now, the slope is steep. The claustrophobia I feel because I am in a tunnel is beginning to subside, fresh air is starting to fill my lungs – the passage is opening to a huge cave now and through the cave entrance I see people, not just people, but people fighting against each other. Fair enough, if this is the sort of help I am getting then this is the help I am getting, then this is the help I get. I can measure up against all these I would bet. Now we are out of the cave, I can relax a little bit; we are on a soft beach somewhere in New York, or by the ocean. I don’t know where this place is but it seems pretty untouched. A circle of around 50 men and women surround a huge hexagonal cage on the beach, in the cage people are fighting with shirts off, one of them is using a capoera style and the other mui Thai kickboxing. Weapons are strewn all around the sand inside the cage; blood and sweat muddy the golden sand and change it into a more dark hew. The odd thing is though, the cage is two tiered. In the lower tier there are 2 people fighting with weapons and trying to scramble up a ladder in the middle of the cage to get up into the top tier through a hole in the middle of the top tier. In the top tier there are 2 men also fighting with weapons with each other, when a guy climbs the ladder to get into the top tier though they get beat down by the people up in the top tier. "What’s going on over there" I ask the guide. Without looking over once and in a voice which has explained this a thousand times he responds. "That is the cage of forgotten hope. Well at least that is what it has been called. Basically the rules are simple – you can only win the match in the top tier, it is every man for himself. All 4 fighters start at the bottom and have to work their way up the ladder, or up one of the side pods on the cage to the top tier, the way they win at the top is to either stay up there alone for a full minute, or making someone submit or be knocked out up there. Everything else goes." "So what you are saying is that giant cage is a huge game of king of the castle?" "In a manner of speaking, yes. But in king of the castle do you see the winner win by throwing his opponent through the cage onto the floor below?" "Well, no. That must be 15 feet" "15 feet, yes. The hole to allow the people in the middle is a square of 6 foot by 6 foot, to the side it is enough for just one man to get up there, the whole cage is 30 foot long and 30 foot wide, it is a good event, until you prove yourself here you wont be able to fight in there yourself." "I understand." It looks as thought he caught me checking out the action and I must have had a yearning for actually getting in the cage and proving my worth but obviously I cant do that in the cage. Following the guide away from the action (I still don’t know his name) he leads me into a different cave. Jimmy the Bishop was in there, now dressed in normal street clothes and he beckons me over. He’s sitting on a nice comfortable sofa, you know the type where you go and sit on it and need a visa to get off it. I sit down opposite him on a less comfortable leather armchair, but it was still plush compared to the street I have lived on the past few weeks. The whole cave seemed like it was financed by the collection box of the church, gold dripped everywhere and for some reason Jimmy had a lion in a cage nearby. Jimmy is probably 60, he seems to be someone who can handle himself, his grey hair cropped in a military style, the old man was a very well built man in his day it seems. It has sagged a little now but you can see by his shoulders and his bicep lines that he used to be ripped. At around six six I would guess he used to be 280lbs of pure muscle. His penetrating gaze looked me straight in the eye; his bright blue eyes I could see showed no fear for anyone. I fixed him a stare straight back, I am not backing down from him. "I see you are the limey who I was told about then, some of my limey associates in Liverpool told me to look after you when you came over. Friends of theirs are friends of mine." His thick New York accent, possibly Brooklyn, (who knows as I don’t come from New York), shone through while he spoke with a crooked smile. This guy knows how to lie for sure. I have no doubt that he can set me up, but at what price? I sit in stony silence, the less said the better. "And I see you don’t talk much, I guess you just want to talk business. Just like so many of you people before, well, ok, here it is. I can set you up with a nice job, but you have to prove yourself first. I want you to take out Danny here" I glance over at Danny, a giant of a man, over 7 feet tall and built like a machine. He doesn’t even give me chance to stand before he rushes at me in my armchair. I flip up with my hands and somersault backwards over the chair kicking out at the chair to fly toward Danny. Landing on my back I can’t hear the thud of the chair hitting Danny, and I don’t want to wait for a giant to stomp my chest in. I get up as quickly as possible and see Danny still charging at me. Holding my ground I get into a wrestling stance, waiting for him to get close enough to perform a routine leg take down. With lightning speed I take him down using my legs and his momentum and with a crack I smash the back of his bear with my elbow as we fall. He groans while on the ground so I quickly find something, a gold plated mirror, pretty heavy, just the job. CRACK onto the back of his head, knocking him unconscious. I quickly turn around to face Jimmy, just in case there are any more surprises from him. He is clapping. "Well done, I heard you were reasonable, but never knew you were this strong, I must have underestimated you. The usual guys I look after are gone by now. Well anyway, I think there might just be something you can get your teeth into. I know a guy in a position at FUSE wrestling, have you heard of them?" Non committal I shrug, the name rang a bell somewhere in my head but professional wrestling, where everything is scripted and it is more a pantomime than fighting? Suppose it pays the bills. "Well, they are recruiting at the moment, they are one of the biggest organisations around and my friend owes me a favour, I can get in you in there for say, 15% of your earnings for the next year. Plus you have to win a title." "Win a title? Which one?" "The only one that matters! The Universal title of course!" I tilt my head incredulously to the side, looking at this man and thinking what it is he actually wants from me, he is offering me a spot in a wrestling organisation for a small cut of my earnings, and all he asks for is for me to whip everyone in the organisation and get the title in a year, there must be a catch somewhere because that sounds too easy. Face up then, I will just have to ask. "What’s the catch?" Gleefully he smiles at me, showing pearly white teeth which have been hand crafted more than likely from the finest dentists his obviously corrupt money can buy. It is a menacing sort of smile; this is where the bombshell hits. "The catch, my friend, is this. If you don’t become a champion in a year, then you lose everything again – I will take your home, your job, your money, and you will be back on the street like a bum. If you win the title, then you are mine." "Yours?" "Have you ever heard of a henchman? Basically I can call whenever I want for you to do a job for me, and you will have to do it." "How long for?" "Until I see fit." Have a job for a year, win the title and I am some fellas henchman bitch, lose the title and I am all on my own again? Can I do that? Can I be in the debt of some criminal? What other choices do I have? The cogs in my mind work overtime, my senses say no, it will be a big mistake, a complete catastrophe. Who would do that? But then again If I don’t then when am I gonna be able to get a job, to pay my way? In a year a lot can happen, he could be dead, I could sort him out? Who knows? When you are this desperate you have no leverage, that’s what I need right now. He is obviously waiting for his answer, he doesn’t seem the kind who actually lets you go away to think about it. It’s now or never. "Ok, I accept your terms, but on one proviso" What am I saying? Trying to negotiate? He looks pissed. "Oh, Mr. Jensen, I never knew you were in a position to negotiate, what is this proviso you are asking for? I wasn’t aware I opened the deal for negotiation, I would strike you down right now, but I want to hear what you are offering, I am a fair man after all" Ok, so I haven’t just been shot, there’s a bonus, what can I think of that would be interesting to him? He likes strength, sees it as a virtue, and likes money for sure. Vanity, yes, I can appeal to his vanity. "I reserve the right to challenge you in the cage of forgotten hope" Laughter, I was expecting that, a cold heartless laughter. I will just stare back at him without fear, it is the only way. "For what Mr. Jensen, you have nothing to offer me!" "Currently I haven’t, what about when I win the title, when I have all the money and adulation that comes with it? I know that you want the FUSE Universal championship for yourself, and this is the only way you can see about getting it because FUSE won’t let you join because of your background, is that about right?" I think I touched a nerve, or at least hit something, he seems interested, push it home "Think about it Jimmy, the FUSE Universal championship around your waist, forever, me as your bodyguard or henchman whatever you ask, if you beat me when I am champion this is what you get, in return if I beat you, that’s it, you leave me alone." "Interesting proposition, I like you Mr. Jensen, you are on, if you win the title, then beat me I will leave you alone, if you win the title and lose to me, then you will lose everything AND be my henchman, fair?" "Fair" Then it begins, with the shake of a hand with a crook I am a part of FUSE, whether or not I have just sold my life away who knows? Time will tell, time will definitely tell.
