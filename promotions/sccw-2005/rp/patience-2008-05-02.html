Patience

2 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-02-08 I Somewhere in the distant future… She could feel the presence of her sister Delylah sitting just on the other side of her resting place. Cleopatra slid the lid off from her tomb stepping out onto the cold dirt. Vassago had helped her build a special room designed to fit her needs under the back half of the warehouse. She took a deep breath knowing she would finally face her sister Delylah. She climbed up the makeshift staircase and into the back storeroom, sitting there in a folding chair in front of a folding table was Vassago, his bald head reflecting the soft torch light. "I’ve been waiting for you." He stated slow and calm. "Devlin is itching for something to do, you know how impatient he is." Cleo slowly walked toward Vassago with a menacing smile, "I take it my sister and Paul have arrived?" She asked trying to sound surprised. "Did Rubis take care of Mr. Nagatti?" "I did it already." He answered, rising from his chair. "As for your sister, I saw their car outside." "We need to be patient then, she doesn’t realize you two are here." She explained joyously, "We will wait until they actually think they have a chance to escape," Cackling at the thought, "Then we drug Paul and knock my sweet, darling sister out." She hissed and spat the last three words as if they scorched her tongue. "I’ll handle him." He replied, meeting her gaze. "You should go and prepare the house." She beamed an enlightened smile, "Yes…" The mere thought of all the torturous things she was about to inflict on her sister. "They will all pay for my suffering." Vassago watched as she left the back room, he to turned and went out the opposite door and across a dark hallway into a different chamber. As he entered the room Rubis glanced up and met his gaze. A smile crossed Devlin’s face; without saying a word Vassago turned and left the room as quickly as he had entered, Devlin was in tow. II "NO!" a voice screamed down the corridor. "GET THE HELL AWAY FROM ME!" Vassago stopped and turned to Devlin, the torturous screams of a condemned man pleading into the darkness brought smiles to their faces. They continued along the dark hallway drawing closer to their destination by the second. "You WILL do as your told! Take your mother and leave, this is my fault and I will not take you three down with me." "It appears the cavalry has arrived," Vassago comments, glancing at Rubis. "As expected." Four torches that were anchored to walls of stone dimly lit the large room as Vassago stopped at the edge of the halls darkness. Within the chamber were the biological parents of Cleopatra Nagatti as they had left them, however beside them was a tall man comparable in size to Devlin and a shorter, more feminine companion. His eyes fixated on the woman, her long raven hair, her strong back and slender waist, the heart shape to her hips and ass. In every essence she was her sister’s twin, only this one was mortal. Cleopatra’s innocent half. Innocence was always the most intoxicating of all aphrodisiacs. Devlin’s hot breath at the back of his neck ended the fantasy, slowly Vassago turned to face him focused again on the task. "Now we wait until the time is right." Devlin’s eyes narrowed on his’, "You are hesitant." "I am patient." Vassago replied, turning away from Devlin. The man and woman were tearing furiously at the knots in the ropes. Vassago turned to face Rubis and nodded, the two slowly entered the chamber behind Delylah and her boyfriend. Nebt-Het was the first to notice them, then Amun, finally the man and woman turned around. Swiftly Rubis swung his cane at Delylah as Vassago chopped Paul in the neck, causing them both to collapse to the ground. Vassago kneels beside Delylah, gently pushing the hair away from her face. Devlin knelt beside Paul and stuck him in the neck with a needle while he injected the tranquilizer into the massive man. Vassago tucks his arm underneath Delylah’s neck, tucking his other under her legs; slowly he rises to his feet carrying her away from the parents. Devlin watches curiously, noting the care in which he is taking. Devlin continues watching as Vassago places Delylah at the brink of the torchlight, and watches as he turns back around his expression ice once again. Rubis grabs Paul by the arm, tossing it over his shoulder as he rises to his feet. Effortlessly Devlin carries Paul to the side of Delylah dropping him on the ground beside her. "Careful with him." Comments Vassago with a hint of agitation in his tone, "My mistress wants him intact." Without facing him Devlin replies, "She’s a child, she doesn’t know what she wants." Turning back around, Devlin fixates his gaze on Amun. "Just like all little girls, her father is the center of her life." Devlin pauses before finally turning his gaze back to Vassago. "Just like all the little boys of the world." Vassago holds his gaze without quavering. Devlin is trying to take a stab at him; Devlin thinks he’s weak because he only recently disposed of his own father. Devlin doesn’t understand the importance of patience, nor does he comprehend the importance of perfect timing. Devlin is the first to turn away; he does so quickly and focuses on freeing Amun. Vassago watches Devlin as he approaches Nebt-Het, slowly he unties her from the chair, making sure to keep her bound at the wrist and ankles. When Vassago finally finishes, Devlin has Amun on his shoulders; the little Egyptian man remains calm and peaceful, a total contrast the hulking frame of his captor. Vassago hoists Nebt-Het onto his shoulders, she too seems resigned to her fate, as the man and the beast escort them to their destiny. III Vassago stands just inside the bedroom door as Devlin and Cleo tether the couple to the bed. Just beside him the lukewarm pale body of Dr. Elias Nagatti, precisely where he had left him. "I’ve changed my mind." Cleopatra commented to Devlin. "Move him downstairs, we’ll bring him back up here after she’s here." She continued, as Vassago could hear her drawing nearer. His gaze shifts, Vassago meets her eyes and for the first time he notices something. Sorrow. The glimmer of emotion evaporates from her eyes as quickly as it appeared. She reaches towards him, handing him a black garbage bag saturated with blood. "This will need to be disposed of." Vassago accepts the bag, "What’s this?" "A rabbit." She replies coolly, "It will be enough, until dinner anyway." His gaze drifts to the lifeless body of the man who had raised her. "I’ll take care of it." Vassago responds, turning to head into the hallway. "Vassago." Cleopatra begins; he glances back to meet her eyes again. "When they show up lock her in here." She pauses a thin smile crossing her lips, as she closes the distance between them. "As for lover boy, you can tie him up in the basement." He nods slightly, turning away and heading down the stairs to dispose of the dead rabbit. Cleopatra veered off at the stairs and continued down the hallway and into her sister’s room. Devlin grabbed a chair from the vanity and exits the bedroom, locking the door behind him. He places the chair against the wall and takes a seat. Vassago steps into the foyer, enjoying the tranquil darkness. As he strolls leisurely toward the front door while he contemplates the events of the evening. So far everything was going as they had planned, except for one small chink in the chain. He opens the front door, and walks out to the road dropping the post-mortem-pet in the patch of grass by the mailbox. As he turns from the roadside he notices to small eyes shining at him in the night. Curious, he approaches the set of eyes to find a Badger half in a hole in the yard and half out, staring at him. After a few moments the badger returns to its hole, Vassago however remains for a few seconds longer staring at where it had been before heading back to the house. He still had to prepare. With preparation and patience all things would take care of themselves. IV The following morning… A soft creek, followed by a man shifting his weight on the staircase sparked the opportunity. Vassago watched with his eyes parted very thinly as the hulking frame of Paul Samson made it’s way up the stairs toward the bedroom door, behind him was Delylah, her face contorted into an expression of panic. Vassago had to suppress a smile; the impatient always thought things were this easy. Hastily Paul reached for the doorknob just as Vassago had figured. Swiftly he grabs Paul’s wrist in his hands, rising to his feet. Vassago expressed no emotion on his face, and when he spoke it was calmly. "I don’t think so." He began, fixating his gaze on the man before him. "My mistress has plans for you two." Out of the corner of his eye Vassago caught a glimpse of a left hand, throwing his own right arm up he blocked the swing and slammed his head into Paul’s instantly ending the struggle. Vassago released his grip on Paul’s wrist and shoved it away causing the hulking frame to go crashing down the stairs. "PAUL!" Delylah screamed turning to head down the stairs, but Vassago wasn’t letting that happen. With a handful of the soft ebony hair in his grip he pulled her back toward him, secretly stealing a whiff off her hair. With a quick gesture he has his arm around her midsection only an inch below her breasts. With his other hand he twists the doorknob and tosses her into the room, she slides a few inches and collides with the footboard of the bed. Vassago took a step into the room, her face was etched in pain yet still held it’s innocent quality. A slow smile crosses his face, "You just sit tight and Cleo will be with you as soon as she wakes up." Pausing for a moment he glances up at the tethered parents. "I’m sure you will just love what she has planned for tonight." Denying her a chance to speak, he slammed the door behind him locking it. She would have plenty of chances to talk, she only needed to be patient.
