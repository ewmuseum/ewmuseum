Someday You Will Be Loved

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/30/08 17.1 the jukebox: you will be loved I once knew a girl In the years of my youth With eyes like the summer All beauty and truth In the morning I fled Left a note and it read Someday you will be loved. I cannot pretend that I felt any regret Cause each broken heart will eventually mend As the blood runs red down the needle and thread Someday you will be loved You'll be loved you'll be loved Like you never have known The memories of me Will seem more like bad dreams Just a series of blurs Like I never occurred Someday you will be loved 17.2 the mirror: soon This is essentially what you told Sarah Riley when you broke it off. You just didn’t expect "someday" to be so fucking soon. It’s a bit of an overstatement, you know. You don’t know if Sarah has fallen in love with Cyrus Raynes, nor do you know if there is anything beyond their newly-formed professional relationship. But you do know that it bothers you. You also know that Sarah didn’t choose your stablemate by accident. You also know that you have to keep it to yourself. Because Katie can’t know that it bothers you – or, more importantly, can’t know why. This has been a problem ever since you and Katie started dating – not being able to tell her how you feel. But it’s supposed to be different now. You’re supposed to be learning more about her and showing her more about yourself. You just can’t let her see that particular part of you. 17.3 the window: help out "Jon?" Katie asks with worry. Jonathan has been sitting on the couch for the past fifteen minutes, not moving a muscle, and barely blinking. He looks up at her suddenly and shakes his head. "Huh?" "What’s bothering you?" Once again, Jonathan shakes his head, trying to focus his eyes anywhere else. "Nothing. I’m just tired." Katie frowns. "You slept for ten hours today. What are you thinking about?" Jonathan looks away once again, trying his best to think of a viable answer. "Nothing." He fails. "Is it Malachi?" Katie sits down next to Jonathan and grabs his hands. "Jon, listen to me. I’m in the business now. I know that this world gives you a lot of pressure, but now that I’m your valet you can vent to me. You can let me know what’s bothering you at work, and I’ll understand." Jonathan doesn’t answer. "Jonathan." Still nothing. She grabs his hand and he merely shrugs. "Jonathan? You can talk to me." He doesn’t. Katie’s hands fly to her hips as she stands up and looks down on him, her anger flaring. "You wanted openness. You wanted to talk more. You said that you missed out on our relationship and you wanted to find that place where we could just talk to each other. Why don’t you talk to me now?" "Because…" he starts. He looks at her and tries to say it, tries to let everything he’s thinking out of his head. "Because there’s nothing to talk about." Again he fails. "If you won’t talk to me, you should talk to someone. Why not your psychologist? You haven’t gone to see her in awhile, maybe she can help out." 17.4 the mirror: her favor This is the sort of moment you’d find in some comedy, a situation that would make the crowd laugh anxiously and wonder how the character could possibly escape it intact. Too bad you don’t find it fucking funny. You haven’t seen Sarah in awhile because you wanted it done; you didn’t want to continue your life of adultery and immorality. You wanted to be normal again: to have a normal life with a normal job and a normal girlfriend. You know that’s not possible now. But still, you don’t want to see Sarah. You don’t want to come to her in time of need because it turns the relationship back in her favor. You envision your relationship with her as a see-saw, a children’s game where the momentum shifted from one person to the other consistently. It started in hers as her eccentric lifestyle and unique perception kept you on your toes. Then, as you showed your individuality as well in avoiding her advances, the advantage sliding into your hands as she began to lose her professional exterior. If you go to her now, and you bring up Cyrus, you lose. She has the power again. And you don’t know if you can get it back after that. But something in you disagrees. You want to see her again. Just to resolve this issue. To find out what is bothering you, and to end it. You want to feel good again, and then never have to worry about Sarah or your problems again. At least, that’s what you think you want. 17.5 the window: wrestling storyline It’s only been five minutes and Jonathan is already saying, "Stop." "What’s wrong?" Sarah asks, her frown almost mocking. "Is it suddenly wrong now?" Jonathan shakes his head. "It was always wrong. I just never had the brains to end it. I’ve already done that, and I’m here for a different reason." "Very well," Sarah Riley says, her cold professional demeanor returning effortlessly. "What is troubling you now?" "You and Cyrus. I want a reason." Sarah smiles. "What do you mean? I told you, Smitty told me that…" "You’ve never spoken to Smitty in your life. You wanted a way to distract me, to make me lose my cool." "Did it work?" Jonathan only takes a second to think of this before angrily shaking his head. "No, it didn’t work. I don’t care if you are with him or not, I just want to know why you chose him specifically. If your agenda is that one-dimensional, I was going to suggest just quitting and saving yourself the trouble. I’ve made my decision." "I know you have." Sarah nods and leans back in her couch, crossing her legs. "It was fun while it lasted, right? Jonathan Rhine got to see what it was like to be the heel for a second. To feel what it would be like to be booed." Jonathan’s face is stone. "Don’t use wrestling terms." "But isn’t that all it is? You’re not angry because I’m with another man…you’re upset because he’s another wrestler. If I started fucking an accountant, it wouldn’t bother you nearly as much. You’re married to the game more than you could ever be married to Katie." "What about you?" Jonathan snarls. "What about me? I’m merely trying to find someone who needs help and give it. Just like I gave you help. You were shy, introverted, afraid when I first counseled you. And now you’re more. Now you have Rhinestones." Jonathan blushes. "I don’t care about any of that." "Of course you do," she says. "Your mind only thinks in those terms. It’s why you chose Katie over me, and it’s why you are now upset that I came to the ring with Cyrus. Your life is nothing but one long wrestling storyline, and it’s going to eat you alive." Jonathan shakes his head and stands up. "That’s not why I came here." "Then why did you come? For some magical advice? Here, I’ve got some for you. When you’re with Katie, think of her as a human, not a valet. When you’re with Amy or Jason, they’re friends, not stablemates. Train your mind to think like an actual human. Then these crises might not bother you as much." Jonathan grabs the door and opens it, turning around and staring at the woman who, for one moment, he swore he loved. Then, he slams the door loud. Sarah smiles. 17.6 the mirror: stupid It would be stupid to listen to her. Everything she says rings with bitterness, with objectivity. She hates that you chose Katie, so she is going to make you miserable every chance she gets. She’ll taunt you, yell at you, do anything to make you think you’re making a wrong choice. But it would also be stupid to ignore her. Would you have been upset if she moved on and found someone new outside of your business? Most likely; in fact, you’d have been relieved. She’d be out of your hair. But she’s still here. Still around you. Still a part of your life. And of course that’s the main problem. You want the wrestling storyline, like she says. You want the drama and the excitement of being blasted by a chair from Malachi, from facing long odds and overcoming them every time. You want that to be part of your on-camera life. But you also want the life where your name doesn’t mean anything other than just a boyfriend, or a son. You long for that unknown, face-in-the-crowd effect you always had earlier in your life. For the first time since signing your contract in FUSE, you wanted to be anyone in the world but Jonathan Rhine. You wanted to make the letdown easy on Sarah Riley, but she’s now making it tough on you. She’s making you unsure of what you are. Unsure of what kind of life you’re living. She’s making it war. And you wanted to let her know that she would be loved someday, you wanted to give her the good treatment. But now? Now she’s taking it to the ring. Now she’s making it personal. And now you don’t care if anyone ever loves her again.
