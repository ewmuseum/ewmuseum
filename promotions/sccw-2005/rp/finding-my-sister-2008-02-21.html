Finding my Sister

21 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/08 Jacque pulled up in front of his parent’s plantation house. The old lop-eared hound sat on the top step and lazily swished his tail back and forth. He sighed heavily and turned the key to kill the engine. This was a day he had hoped would not come, the day that his mother would be institutionalized. The movers were already there taking boxes of his mothers things to the storage unit his brother had purchased yesterday. He had informed everyone to stay out of his father’s office. Jacque wanted to take care of that himself. Jacque slowly slipped out of the car and walked up the front steps stopping only to scratch the loveable hound behind his ears. He pulled open the screen door that protested with a loud moan and headed straight for his father’s office. Once he was inside he flipped on the lamp that sat on the desk and began sorting through all of his father’s papers. He hoped that there would be a clue to the whereabouts of his estranged sister Ellisa Willet. Jacque thumbed through his father’s file and found the court documents. He needed to find the lawyer who oversaw this and there the name was, John LaFayette. It looked like these papers were drawn up and enforced while his parents were living in Canada. "Canada... " Jacque thought. "Nothing like finding a needle in a hay field.". He knew it would be something like this. Fate was like a newborn baby, things always had to be harder then needed to be. Jacque folded the documents and placed them in the back pocket of his faded blue jeans. When he got home he would research this John LaFayette and see if he was still practicing and hopefully be able to get some information from him. Now he had to transfer his thoughts to his mother. Her being institutionalized for dementia was something he didn’t want to deal with. His mother had always been the glue that held their family together and now with her gone there wasn’t anything left. Jacque jumped at the sound of his brother opening the office door. Gerard had been taking care of their mother since the beginning of her ailment. His once young and vibrant face looked like that of a fifty year old man. Worry lines etched the corner of his eyes that were accented by huge dark circles underneath. Jacque stood up from the desk with a simple nod knowing that it was time to take their mother to her new home. He put a hand on his brother’s shoulder and followed him into the living room where his mother was sitting for the last time in her favorite rocking chair. The slight squeaking from the rocker cast an eerie tone to all that was about to happen. They had explained to her where she was going but it was like talking to a two year old that was preoccupied with their favorite television show. "Ok mom it’s time to go now." Jacque told her as he crossed the room. "Go? Where are we going Gerald?" She asked with a confused look. "Mom, It’s Jacque. I am not dad. Don’t you remember Ger and I telling you that you needed to go to a new home? A home where there were special people to take care of you." Jacque explained trying to choke back any tone of sorrow in his voice. "Yes I remember Gerald." Jacque just shook his head. He knew there was no reasoning with her that he was not his father. She saw what she wanted to see. "We have everything in the car so let’s get going." Jacque waited for his mother to start toward the door and stopped Gerard. "Ger, I... I just can’t do this man. I am so sorry for being such a shitty brother to you but I have some things that I need to look into." Gerard just shook his head and headed out the door behind his mother. Jacque knew that he was disappointed with him but he just couldn’t do this. Not now with so many other things weighing on his mind. Not only did he have to find his sister but also he had to find out what his father meant that there were things about Keegan that he couldn’t imagine. "Where should I start first?" Jacque thought. "Should I take a flight to go see Keegan and find out God only knows what or should I find my sister." The overwhelming need to make his family complete won in the end. Jacque raced down the front steps of the plantation house and climbed into his car. He headed for the library to do some research. He pulled up in front of the small brick building and killed the engine. Once he was inside the library he slowly walked toward the row of computers that were situated near the back. The smell of old books strangely calmed his nerves as he settled in front of the computer. He did a search on his sister’s name but found nothing. He searched and searched and finally located John LaFayette whom was still practicing in Alberta, Canada. "Well at least I narrowed it down possibly to one providence." Jacque told himself with a smirk as he took down LaFayette’s address and phone number. He continued to search the net for information on relinquishing parental rights. From all of the articles that he perused through he knew he had to find his father’s lover, Ellisa’s mother, Alisha Willet. The only way to find her would be to persuade Mr. LaFayette to tell him. Jacque decided to book a flight for Monday morning to his hometown of Calgary. He would find his sister if it killed him. Jacque slipped into his car and pulled out the court documents. He had intended to just stick them in the glove compartment and leave it at that. Something, someone told him to look through the papers and when he did a piece of stationary folded in fourths fell into his lap. Slowly he unfolded the paper that was stained with age to reveal a letter written in feminine handwriting. December 16, 1982 Dear Gerald, I don’t have enough words to express how much we thank you for signing the papers. I am tormented with grief at the solution we had all come up with but what were we all to do. You were a happily married man with two children and I was a soon to be bride who had made a huge mistake. Any way enough about all the un-pleasantries of the past. I am writing you now not only to thank you but I wanted to send you at least this picture of Ellisa. You deserve that much. You sacrificed knowing your child to protect everyone involved. Aaron and I made it through that rough patch and we have now been married 3 years I hope that everything worked out for you and again thank you very much. Alisha He slowly pulled into the driveway of his parent’s house. He knew that if she had sent him a picture it would be in his father’s office. Jacque sprang up the front steps and dashed into the office. He rifled through all the desk drawers like a madman. In the very bottom of the last drawer he found what he was looking for. There was a small silver heart shaped photo frame with a picture of a girl about two years old. The little girl Jacque could tell was his sister. She had the same chestnut brown hair and sky blue eyes that he had. Her hair was a mound of curly tresses with a lavender bow to tie them back, dressed in a matching shade of lavender dress. Her cheeks were chubby with baby fat. Her eyes twinkled like diamonds. Jacque slowly slipped the photograph out of the frame. On the back the words Daddy’s little girl were written in his father’s handwriting. Jacque stared at the picture and then the letter with his mouth agape. Everything his father was trying to tell him hit him like a ton of bricks. He needed to talk to Keegan for Jordan’s sake. Jacque knew Keegan must have a reason why he would just sign off on his son with out a fight. That just wasn’t his style. "I get it now dad." Jacque mumbled as he flopped down in the leather desk chair. Jacque had to find everything out about Keegan’s past. He knew that would be like pulling teeth. How would he explain his change of heart to Dianna? He loved Jordan like he was really his own son. He had to do the right thing but what was that? Did he talk to Keegan or did he just let things go the way they were? In order for him to be a true "dad" to Jordan he needed to find the reason his father gave him up. After everything that he had found out about his own father’s past he just wouldn’t feel right otherwise.
