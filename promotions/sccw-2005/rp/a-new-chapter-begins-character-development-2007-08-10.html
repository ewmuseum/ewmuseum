A New Chapter Begins (Character Development)

10 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/07 We open to the sight of a blond-haired, blue-eyed man walking down an upscale street in Hollywood, California. Dressed in trendy jeans, a graphic t-shirt, and a sport coat, he draws many looks as he passes by the masses of people on the streets. The man has a pair of aviators resting on top of his head and wears a rather fancy watch around his wrist. As he continues to wander around seemingly aimless, he is stopped by a young married woman, her husband, and their young daughter. "Hey, hey! We’re such fans of yours! My daughter loves you," the mother exclaims. "Yeah, we’re actually going to see your new movie tonight," says the father. An all-too-familiar look sets on the mans face. He grins and lets out a small laugh. He looks down at the little girl as she holds her teddy bear under her right arm with a death grip. She smiles up at him. He smiles back. He looks the mother and father in the face and begins to shake his head from left to right. "Nah , nah, nah. I’m sorry, but im not who you think I am." The mother and father look at him as if he’s trying to play it off to remain low key; however, the his tone suggests otherwise. "Who do you THINK we THINK you are," asks the mother. "Him." He point to his right where a huge sign for "Bourne Ultimatum" hangs on the side of large building. The mother and father look at the sign and then back at the man. They begin sizing him up and comparing him to the Godzilla-sized poster of Matt Damon. "Are you sure? I mean, DAMN, you look JUST like him." "Haha. Yeah, I’m pretty sure." He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a stack of business card. He hands one to each of them. They both mull over the card for a second before he begins to explain. "The name’s Carson Riley. I actually worked with Matt on the movie, and several others." The father takes another look at the business card and looks back at Carson with a nod. "Stuntman? Oh, so you’re like his stunt double," The father asks in intrigue. "Yeah, whenever Matt or anyone else gets cold feet...they call me in. Jumps, flips, chases, crashes...you name it." The father begins to chuckle under his breath as he slips the card into the pocket of his shirt. He looks back up at Carson and speaks in an enlightened tone. "I always knew that Matt Damon looked like a sissy," says the father. The wife immediately looks at him and backhands him gently across the chest. Her eye brows curl upward as she gives her husband an evil stare. "Fred! Don’t talk like that in front of your daughter! She loves Matt Damon!" Fred looks down at his daughter. You can tell Fred doesn’t care but know he needs to do something appease his wife. Fred pats her on the head and shows a brief, cheesy smile. The little girl remains goo-goo-eyed, staring up at Carson as if she could care less who he really is. Carson shoots back at her with his million-dollar smile. "Sorry, hunny," says the father. Fred and his wife refocus their attention on Carson. "Uh, so what’s the next movie," the mother asks politely. "Bourne Ultimatum is actually going to be my last film. I’m changing professions." "So you’re stepping out of Damon’s shadow and hitting the big screen, aren’t you," asks Fred. "Nah, I’m actually going to be a professional wrestler. FUSE wrestling, maybe you’ve heard of it?" "My wife doesn’t let me, ahem, I mean....I’m not into that stuff really. But good luck." Fred receives one last stare of death from his wife. Fred looks the other way and rubs his hand on his head as if he knows he crossed the "Wicked Witch of the West" one too many times. They all sense it’s time to wrap up the conversation. "Thanks. I have a meeting with my agent to get to. Before you go..." Carson reaches into his pocket and pulls out a Sharpe pen. He reaches out and takes the business card from the little girl. He marks the card with his ‘John Hancock’ and passes it back to the bubbling little girl. She smiles ear to ear, jumping in excitement as she shows it to her mother. "Take care," says Carson. Carson continues to walk forward, passing a few shops and restaurants in the process. He pulls his i-phone out to check the time before stopping at a restaurant called "Lucky Devils." Carson opens the door and holds it open for a couple exiting the facility. He enters and approaches the hostess. "Hey, I’m Carson Riley. I’m meeting my agent for lunch, a Mr. Andrew Green." She checks her book and makes a mark on her seating list. She grabs a menu and motions Carson to the right side of the restaurant. "Right this way, Mr. Riley. He’s already waiting for you." They approach the table where a well dressed man sits alone talking away on his cell phone. Carson pulls out the chair directly across from him and takes a seat. The hostess hands Carson a menu and walks back towards the front of the restaurant. Carson picks up the menu and begins to browse patiently. "There is no way in hell I’m letting this get away! You tell Tarantino this is Paul’s movie and no one else’s! Not Shia, not Leo, not Tim Roth...Paul fuckin’ Walker. And Carson Riley will be on set to back up Paul, and anything else would be a major fucking mistake." Andrew, red in the face with veins protruding from his neck, closes his phone and slams it on the table. He takes a deep breath and sits back in his chair in disbelief. He looks at Carson while shaking his head. "Who the hell is Tim Roth? Dark Water was a piece of trash. I’m telling you, Carson, this is a great gig. Quintin doesn’t make shit movies. Bourne is going to be the biggest hit of the summer and you’re going to follow up by adding Tarantino to your resume. Beautiful," says Andrew. "Well..." "Let’s get some food. What did you need to meet with me about anyway?" "The movie...I can’t do it." A confused look sets over Andrew’s face. The waitress approaches the table and opens up her notepad. She pulls a pen from her uniform pocket and clicks it open. "Can I start you off with something to drink?" "Can you give us a minute please," asks Carson. "Sure can, just let me know when you’re ready to order." The waitress walks away from the table as the color once again returns to Andrew’s face and the veins once again rear their ugly heads. "What? You’re too good for Tarantino now? What the hell do you want? You want the new Michael Bay flick? I’ll see what I can do." "I’m done with film. I found a new gig, and I found it on my own." "Yeah, and what’s that," Andrew asks in a smug tone. Carson leans in towards the table and speaks confidently. He has a very serious look on his face and speaks like someone who has made up their mind. "I’m getting in the ring. I’m going to be a professional wrestler. I just got the call from FUSE yesterday confirming it." A look of disbelief sets over Andrew’s face before he begins to laugh hysterically. He takes a drink from his water glass and tries to compose himself. Carson sits back in his chair and looks the other way. "You’re fucking with my right? That fake shit? You’ll be making peanuts," says Andrew in a mocking manner. "Nah, nah. I’m actually serious. Plus, I won’t have to give you 10% my money. And by the way, I already talked to Quintin. Tim Roth got the lead in ‘Inglorious Bastards’ so it looks like Paul will be stuck doing another B-rated movie." "This is career suicide, Carson. You can’t go from the biggest film of the summer to wrestling in a bingo hall in [fake southern accent ] MOB-ILE, ALA-BAMA." "Funny. I’ll be fighting at the Verizon Center if you’re interested in investing your time into seeing how big this could pay off." "Fuck you. I don’t want shit to do with this. If you’re looking to screw things up, it seems like you’re doing just fine on your own. What a moron, can you believe this guy?" Andrew begins to laugh again as he looks around the restaurant for approval. He draws a few irritated glares. Carson gets out of his chair and pushes it back under the table. He places his aviators over his eyes and begins to walk away from the table. The agent begins screaming at him from across the restaurant. "You’ll fucking regret this, Carson. You’ll regret this, damnit." Before exiting, Carson stops and whispers to the hostess. He takes a twenty-dollar bill out of his right pocket and inconspicuously slips it into hers. "Excuse me, that gentleman over there in the pink tie is causing quite a scene. We have children in here, how about you see to it that his meal isn’t a pleasant one? Have a talk with the cook, if you would." The hostess smiles back a Carson flirtatiously and nods in approval. Carson turns and exits the restaurant, opening the door to a world of potential.
