Live or Die, Part II 'Nightmare's Return'

Date: December4 Live or Die, Part II Nightmare's Return Part I "I'm Watching You" There has always been something special about the hunt. It is something that always made the monster feel strong, powerful, to stalk his unsuspecting prey like a lioness in the tall grass of the Serengeti. It is probably his favorite part of the kill. But the beast is no lioness, nor is he any sort of animal at all. No, rather the beast is a human being. A man who, like most of his profession, is completely unremarkable, and very much easy to forget. This man is, however, evil incarnate. That may sound cliché, but it is painfully true. And now, he is on the hunt. He is watching, his slate gray eyes peering intently through the scope of a Savage .30-30 rifle, centering the crosshairs directly on the heart of the creature he wishes to kill. His finger tightens on the trigger, only an inch of so more of pressure will unleash the fury of the bullet. But something stops him. Why? Why is he holding back? "I'm watching you." His voice is low, but is has a hissing sound to it, like a snake. It breaks the monotonous sounds of his own breathing in the darkness and the steady ticking of the clock on the wall above his head. "And you don't even know it, do you?" This place has become a refuge of sorts to him. He has had to flee across an ocean, and remains far from his home. He has only one goal, one purpose, left. To hunt. Before he was practicing, refining his methods of cruelty and torment. Becoming better at his art. But now, the hunt begins in earnest. He can feel the electricity course through his body, a tidal wave of excitement and of lust for blood. It raises the mud colored brown hair on the back of his neck like an electric current, causing it to stand straight on end. The muscles in his jaw clench, the current forcing them into rigidity. "You are suffering." That very idea is like an aphrodisiac to him. A beautiful woman, to him, is something to be tormented and eventually cast aside into the clutches of death, but pain, beautiful pain, is another story entirely. Suffering... he can feel the rising motion of his genitals, the blood flowing into certain spots and creating a noticeable bulge in the front of his plain black sweat pants. "Easy..." His hissing voice is panting now. "Easy..." The hand not steadying the gun drifts downward, stroking the bulge with the tips of his fingers, but the beast does not desire release just yet. He does not desire the climax that only came at the conclusion of the hunt. No, he wants to play with his meal before he eats. He wants to see a little more torment, a little more pain. Dr. Sarah La Vega paces before the big picture window, her eyes misting over with tears. Even the beast, with all of his perverse desires, has to admit that she is astonishingly beautiful, the sorrow etched into her classical features only enhancing her beauty. His eyes widen behind the scope, traveling up the long, athletic contours of her legs, spending a few moments eying the beautifully rounded contours of her buttocks. She turns back towards him, and he allows his eyes to drift to her beautifully rounded chest, not massive, but full enough to attract the attention of any man. Her face, wet with tears, next grays the gray eyes. They roam over her mouth, full and welcoming, and linger on her nose, upturned in the way that the beast had always liked. His own wife had once had a nose like that, before he had smashed it in with a hammer. But her eyes, her eyes. They are the most striking feature on the entire beautiful package. Round, and as brilliantly blue as the sky on a beautiful day. Through the scope's powerful lens, he can see another tears drip from beneath their long lashes, and the pressure of his crotch meeting the tips of his fingers increases. He longs to pull the trigger. To squeeze off a round that will fly true and end this woman. But after all, shooting somebody from a distance can barely be considered a kill. The person has to want to die, has to desire it with everything they have. They have to suffer more of that beautiful suffering, feel more of that erection inducing pain and agony. They have to become his plaything first. He can see Sarah screaming something at the closed door, though he cannot hear what it is. He can see her beautiful eyes spitting sparks as her lovely mouth spits pure venom at whoever or whatever is on the other side of the door. She obviously, for all of her counseling and her talking to her patients, has a whole closet full of her own issues to deal with. She screams something further, and the monster can read her lips. "I HATE YOU!" She screams, over and over and over again. The little hypocrite. The little liar. "I can see everything, Doctor." A sick, twisted smile creeps across his face, a small shaft of light glinting dimly on his twisted, yellow teeth before darkness once again swallows all but the glint of moonlight on gunmetal. "I can see your words becoming hollow. I can see your life falling apart..." And this is the best part. "I can see my means to an end." After all, he is here to kill a man, not a woman. But the good Doctor will be an enjoyable plaything to poke and prod at, and provide him with a route to his true business along with his pleasure. A route to what he was put on this planet to do. When Sarah La Vega took the man he wishes to kill as a patient, she signed her own death warrant. For a brief moment, the clouds of insanity close in on him, and he cannot remember the name of the man he wishes to slay. What is the name... oh god, he cannot even remember his own name. He can see the man's face, a sneering, aristocratic looking piece of shit with black hair and cobalt blue eyes. He can picture him walking down a metal ramp, wearing black tights, and with black tape on his hands and feet. He knows that he has seen him recently, and he knows that Sarah in there knows him.... And all at once, the pieces of the puzzle fall into place and things become clear to him. He is Harrison O'Dell. And he was sent back to the world of the living to ultimately kill one man. Joaquin Pierre. Part II "I Don't Need This" Just a few feet away, on the other side of the thin door, she can hear him raging at her. His voice is not the low, sexy tone that she once was awed and mesmerized by, no, those days are long gone. These days, his voice when he addresses her is harsh and strained, and even when they aren't fighting, which isn't often, is underlined with a current of tension and suppressed anger. Sarah doesn't even know where their problems started, which for somebody who spends a large portion of her working time doing marriage counseling, is strange. But when the problems in somebody's life are closest to home, that person tends to overlook them, or to ignore them in the name of trying to be happy. Sarah knows that she has tried with him, and in his own way, Antoine has tried with her to fix the problems that have developed. But things appear to be broken beyond repair. This latest war is one that should never have happened. One that could very easily have been avoided by both of them not being so damn stubborn. Sarah came home late, something that is not overly uncommon anymore, in fact it has become a regular occurrence. And, another not uncommon sight, she found Antoine sleeping, the children not fed or looked after and a half empty bottle of booze sitting beside the couch. Outside of the door, she can hear a mighty crash, accompanied by the shattering of a glass bottle. A small river of what looks to be whiskey trickles under the door, soaking into the taupe colored carpet. A sigh bubbles out of Sarah's pursed lips. She doesn't need this shit anymore. "FUCK YOU!" The roar from the other side of the door doesn't surprise or frighten her in the least. "This is..." Ridiculous? Stupid? Silly? A waste of perfectly good time? "THIS IS ALL YOUR FAULT!" All her fault? How is his alcohol problem her fault? How is his infidelity her fault? How is the fact that he stubbornly refuses to see what he is doing to their family her fault? 'DO YOU HEAR ME, SARAH?' Loud and clear, Antoine. How has she ended up in this state? How has somebody who spent her entire life helping others ended up completely and totally unable to help herself? It was simple. She has stopped caring. 'Antoine, just go away.' Another smash on the door, and she wonders if he is perhaps trying to break in, or just taking out his rage on the flimsy portal. It shudders, but doesn't break. 'Just go. Please. I don't need this.' Everything goes silent, deathly quiet, except for the heavy, rushed breathing she can hear on the other side of the door. When he speaks, his voice is quiet. 'What about what I need, Sarah? Have you ever thought of that?' What does he mean? 'I need...' His voice is cut off by a loud cracking noise, followed by a crash of breaking glass and a cry of pain. The sound of his body crumpling to the floor startles Sarah, and his breathing turns into a harsh gurgle. 'Antoine?' Her bare feet hit the soft carpet. 'Are you all right?' The alcohol soaking into the plush carpeting is being flowed over by something else, something dark red. It runs in a spreading pool over the floor, puddling around her toes as she creeps slowly towards the door. Her eyes widen, their depths showing fear now. He is dead. Hi eyes are glazing over as the soft breeze through the shattered window ruffles his black hair. Blood dries to a thin crust in his hair and on his clothes, and right around the hole where the bullet entered his skull. 'Antoine...' For a time, she simply holds him, her mind a torrent of mixed emotions, hurricane force winds of love, hate, sorrow, and gratitude spilling around inside of her head. When next she can feel consciously, she is aware of the sound of her own doorbell. It makes a sharp chime, annoying on the ears, and Sarah gently lays her husband's head down on the blood and booze soaked carpet. The note is simple. It says only a few words, with a funny, scribbled signature on the bottom. Sarah, you are most welcome. See you and the frog soon. Part III 'An Unexpected Friend, and Unwanted Help' He kneels in the soft earth, his eyes looking at the marble of the tombstones through a thick veil of tears. How is it possible that this is all he has of them now? That this, these stones and this earth, is all he has left of his beloved? It is not fair, really. A family torn apart. A love destroyed. And a man shattered. Joaquin's mind is reeling, both with the effects of the emotional storm, and with the effects of his medicine, coupled with the contents of the large silver flask in his hand. His clothing, black pants and one of the special edition FUSE tee shirts that will be found in stores soon, is dishevelled. Several days worth of beard covers his face, which is wet with tears. 'Why do you...' He cannot finish the sentence. His eyes roll up in his head, and he shakes it to try and clear the cobwebs. Is it possible that he is dying? 'I...' Slowly, surely, the Frenchman collapses onto his side, his breathing becoming shallow and his eyes closing. His right hand reaches out, its shaking fingers touching the black surface of his wife's tombstone before falling limply into the dust. 'Oh, shit!' The sound of pounding footsteps and the feeling of a hand on his arm is the last thing he is conscious of, and the blackness claims him.
