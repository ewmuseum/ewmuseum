Thug gone pro.

20 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/20/2008 Mar 11, 2007. Yokkaichi Dome. Yokkaichi, Japan. "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the Dangerzone I am one half of your elite commentary crew… Donovan Knight." Announces the smarmy color commentator of Pure Japan International. "Allow me to introduce my slightly less important but better paid counterpart… Rich McCool." "Thank you I think" coos the balding McCool sporting cheap shades and wrinkles. McCool didn’t exactly live up to his name but the business hadn’t passed him by. He was an experienced English speaking commentator who had married a Japanese woman. He had experience and a green card, that made him very desirable to a low budget, high talent company like Pure Japan International. Donovan Knight didn’t have a green card, only a visa. But he knew the right people. As personal attorney of PJI star "American Youth" Josh, Donovan simply formatted Josh’s contract to fit the needs of both individuals, allowing Knight to work as Josh’s manager. Through brutal tactics and downright sleaze Josh and Knight had managed to secure financial gains in the company. This allowed Knight to appoint himself to the commentary team, and allowed Josh to twist the arm of management in order to gain "more desirable booking." With significant financial stake in the company Knight and Josh found themselves wielding a lot more power. Everyone answers to money. With Knight on the mic, Josh had a medium through which he could broadcast his agenda. And with Josh constantly booked at the top of the card, American Youth found himself in a lot more championship matches. "Tonight will be an epic night for Pure Japan International, as American Youth goes one on one with the Korean Phenom Shin-Il Sun', decried McCool. "That’s right, its the final match in the 32 man PJI World Championship tournament!" "Something tells me that things are going to get ugly tonight, don’t you think Dapper Don? "You’re damn right McCool and if you call me Don again… things are going to get ugly in this booth." McCool was right, things got ugly. For almost 30 minutes two men dropped one another on their respective heads, drew the others blood and did everything in their power to defeat their opponent. Approximately 28 minutes and 12 seconds into the match Shin-Il Sun hit his finisher, aptly named the Final Sunset. The referee began his count, one… two… th – … and Donovan Knight leaves the commentary booth and pulls the referee out of the ring. Knight calmly explained to the referee that he had pulled him out of the ring for his own safety, pointing to the referee’s untied shoe. The referee grudgingly tied his shoe before turning around to face the ring. He had only missed about 30 seconds of the match. What can happen in 30 seconds? What happened in that Japanese arena had taken less than 30 seconds. Shin-Il Sun had rolled off of American Youth and raised his arms in victory. When he saw the ref was distracted he decided to give Josh another Final Sunset, just to ensure that he would still be down for the count when the referee returned to the ring. As Shin-Il Sun began to pull Josh to his knees "the great gaijin hope" hit a home run with the Korean Phenom’s balls. As the master of the Final Sunset sank to his knees Josh reached into his tights and pulled out… a black object. The black object was a very neatly concealed taser, which Josh pointed straight at his opponent’s face and fired. Shin-Il Sun took about 50,000 volts directly to the forehead as he collapsed into a twitching, jerking mass of flesh. "Josh just killed Shin-Il Sun!", screamed an overdramatic McCool. "And that son of a bitch Donovan Knight helped him do it! This is a sham!" The referee returned to the ring at about the same time Donovan Knight returned to the commentary booth, the ref dropping to his knees as Donovan dropped back into his seat. "One… two… three!", counts a grinning Donovan Knight as the referee pounds out the rhythm of his words. "American Youth is the new PJI World Champion!" "That should come as no surprise to you, you helped him win it!", McCool bellowed, showing that his name hardly reflected his mannerisms. "What? I was only trying to make sure that my employee didn’t hurt himself, if that referee had tripped and injured his counting hand we would have had to pay him workman’s comp and hire a temp. I own a fifth of this company, I have to think of my business interests too!" That was the first time that Josh and Knight robbed the PJI fans, but hardly the last. Josh went on to hold all of the major titles in PJI and usually retained them or "passed them on" to the rare allies, whom he employed as merciless guerillas. With Josh and Knight in the management structure there were numerous "strange happenings" that took place in PJI. There was the backstage reporter who Josh abrasively approached, asking her to "score him some blow" and then blow him. She lodged a complaint with management and was fired shortly thereafter. A timekeeper refused to leave his position during a match in order to get a "thirsty" Donovan Knight a glass of water. He too was fired. But these weren’t the "strange happenings" that took place. PJI fans began to cry "foul" when American Youth would be down for the count only for the lights to shut off in the arena. When they came back on his opponent would be down, so would the referee. As Josh mounted a comeback a fresh referee would be supplied. It was always the same referee… the referee who reportedly made double the annual salary of his co-workers. Josh never lost a match in JPI. Debuting in December of 2006, following his firing from Global Wrestling due to a heavy drug addiction Josh took JPI by storm. He took out his rage (and withdrawals) on everyone who stepped in his path, but winning wasn’t enough. Knight and Josh met privately with PJI investors and spoke with them about buying their shares in the company. Of the five major investors only two agreed to negotiate. When their prices were well above the price Josh and Knight offered, tragedy beset them. Mike Amen, a former masked star on the Japanese Wrestling circuit had been one of the original founders of JPI. Wrestling under a hood and the horrible name of "Dyno-Mike" he rose to stardom, eventually retiring to lay the foundation for JPI. Amen was a man of principles and had hired his son Robbie Amen, to wear a hood and compete as Dyno-Mike Jr. When Dyno-Mike Jr. rose to the top of the talent pool and was considered one of the best in Japan, the senior Amen suggested he take his act to the states. Before leaving, Dyno-Mike Jr. found himself booked in a match with American Youth. Josh, also one of the top stars in Japan systematically destroyed the other man, ending the match by intentionally breaking both of his opponents arms. Bone by bone. PJI cameras later caught part of a heated cell-phone conversation that American Youth was having with an unseen party that Josh referred to as Mike. This left little speculation as to why Mike Amen agreed to sell his share in PJI the next day at a price that matched Josh’s initial offer much closer than before. The other investor, Hideo Nokishi, a wealthy businessman from Tokyo had also met with Knight and Josh to discuss selling his share in the company. He too had declined Josh’s initial offer but immediately reconsidered after his son was killed in a storm of Yakuza bullets as he returned home from college. This started the rumor that Josh had strong ties to the Yakuza. Knight, acting as Josh’s attorney, denied all allegations while Josh remained silent. His ties to the Yakuza became a constant cover page for the Japanese tabloids. But the tabloids hardly knew the whole story. Josh’s ties to the Yakuza ran deep, bound through blood and millions of dollars of cocaine that Josh had arranged for transport to and from Japan. Japan was the life. Coke, money, Asian girls, vast power… what more could Knight and Josh strive for? But as is the case with real life, when you’re on top of the mountain you don’t see the rocks at the bottom that began to break away and tumble down the hill to the valley. You never see your power structure crumbling until it’s all around your ankles. Jan 7, 2008. PJI Headquarters, floor 3. Tokyo, Japan. "There isn’t an easy way to say this…" PJI shareholder Taka Boji began, "… but we want you out." "Fat fucking chance", sneered a taken aback Donovan Knight. American Youth chimed in. "What the fuck man?" "Since the two of you joined the chair of PJI, we’ve seen a monopolization of power, with the two of you running the place like you own it." Injected shareholder Andrew Yoosin. "I think you’re forgetting… we do own the place." American Youth spat in retort. "No. You collectively own two fifths of Pure Japan and you’re using your power to control your own fate, while spurning the wishes of the PJI fans", Boji countered. "Fuck the fans. Hire some real talent around here and give me some competition", growled an angry American Youth. Yoosin’s anger matched Josh’s. "It’s hard to hire talent when they know that a small but powerful group is automatically working against them. We’ve lost contracts because of the heavy hand that your position in management is playing!" "I don’t think we’ve abused our powers in the slightest", said Donovan Knight through his trademarked wicked lawyer grin. Yoosin went after Knight. "How can you say that? The two of you have tarnished the legitimacy of our titles and shit on the reputation of this company." "Fuck you Andrew", Josh spat. "Fuck your fairness too. I go out there and win, that’s what I do… and if you or these stupid fans have a problem with the way I win…' "Enough!" Boji tried to restore order. Josh shot a dirty glare at the two board members and bit his tongue, Donovan Knight didn't. He was the lawyer, it was his turn to handle things. "Bitching back and forth isn’t going to get results gentlemen, lets get down to brass tax. We understand that you don’t like us a hell of a lot and I get that. Frankly, we don’t care much for you either. Now that we’ve got that formality out of the way, how do you propose we settle this?" Before Knight could get his answer the door to the conference room opened and the fifth shareholder stepped through it. Arthur Dirk, a retired millionaire from New Zealand stood ashen faced in the doorway. "Arthur, you’re late. Is everything okay?" Boji asked. Even through his spectacles Arthur’s eyes were bloodshot and scared, he avoided looking at Knight and Josh as he approached the table. "Everything is fine Taka." Dirk replied, lying through his teeth. "Unfortunately I have pressing family matters that I must attend to." "But you’re here for the meeting?" Asked Yoosin. "No." Arthur shook his head. "I just came to bring you this." Arthur Dirk dropped a thick brown envelope onto the table of the conference room and turned away. "I’m sorry Taka. I’m sorry Andrew." Without another word Dirk turned on his heel and walked out the door he had entered less than a minute ago. "Now what the hell is this?" Grumbled an exasperated Andrew Yoosin as he tore into the envelope, extracting the papers. "Maybe you should read it and see." Knight suggested while Josh faked a yawn. "Gee wilikers Andy, what could it be?" Josh taunted across the table knowing damn well what the envelope contained. Yoosin’s jaw dropped as he slid the paper over to Taka Boji. Boji fumbled over his sentence. "Dirk resigned and… has turned his shares over to… Josh…" Knight smirked. "Well, how about that?" Josh feigned surprise. "I never would have expected that…" Yoosin snapped. "Fuck you. I know what happened, I know exactly what happened. You assholes sent the Yakuza after Dirk. Knight smiled. "My client has no proven ties to the Yakuza." The word "proven" was the best fit. American Youth had deep connections to the Yakuza and Yoosin had nailed it. Josh had made an unsolicited offer to Dirk two weeks ago. Dirk had declined the offer… and had subsequently suffered two hellacious weeks. The first week his 16 year old son had been robbed and beaten by a gang of "masked men" before being thrown on the doorsteps of the Dirk family home. The second week Dirk had his tires slashed outside of his office. Later in that same week Dirk had been caught on video with his mistress, a 14 year old Japanese girl. A copy of the video had arrived in his mailbox along with a note. The note detailed a meeting he was to have in a sushi restaurant. When he had arrived he was met by American Youth and Donovan Knight as well as four men who remained silent, their forearms covered in tattoos, their pinkies missing. No further details of the conversation emerged, nor did another copy of the videotape. "I’ve always felt that Dirk viewed me with a sense of awe, he must be getting into his mid-sixties now, he’s probably ready to take a step back from the fast lane and hey… who better to give a piece of the pie to, than yours truly?" Josh shot a smile at Boji and Yoosin that would scare the devil himself. Knight didn’t miss a beat. "Well… with Dirk’s share in Josh’s hands… I guess that means we hold the majority." 'Or maybe he viewed me more like a son...' Mused a sly American Youth. "This is an outrage…" Boji snarled, beginning to lose his cool. "Stop." Knight warned him. "Lets keep this civil. You want us out and we want you out. Lets make a deal… "We’re not selling out." Yoosin stated with defiance. "Wait… at least hear our offer." Coaxed Knight. "Ginji, bring in the money." Ginji the security guard posted at the end of the table nodded and spat something in Japanese into his walkie talkie. A moment later the door opened and two men stepped through it, each carrying a briefcase, each missing a pinkie and each with a large bulge under their arm. "I think you’ll be quite pleased with our offer." American Youth said with a smile. The two men with missing pinkies opened the briefcases. "What… what the hell is this?" Yoosin objected. "That my friend, is one hundred thousand American dollars." Knight announced. Yoosin was outraged. " One hundred thousand..? My shares are worth almost a million!" American Youth decided to press the issue. "Oh, but its a package deal. Ginji… have them turn off the cameras." Ginji raised the walkie talkie to his mouth, once again speaking Japanese. He waited until chatter returned to him through the walkie talkie before giving Knight and Josh the nod. It was then that the two men without pinkies reached into their attache cases. Josh’s voice was cold as he spoke. "We’re offering you each one hundred thousand dollars cash… plus your lives. I suggest you take it and walk away." And that… is how a slick lawyer and a thug gone pro managed to wrangle away complete control of a Wrestling organization worth approximately 12.5 million dollars. How they managed to lose it, well… that’s another story.
