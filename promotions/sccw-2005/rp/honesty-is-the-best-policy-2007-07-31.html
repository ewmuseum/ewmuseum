Honesty is the Best Policy

31 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/2007 (text in italics are El Diablo’s thoughts) Taken aback by Vincent Halfpenny’s revelation that his wife Angela led him into the grasp of the International Pharmaceutical Corporation’s testing facility at Happy Hills Mental Institution, El Diablo had to get answers. He had already gathered answers about his time at Happy Hills from Dr Murray and Halfpenny, but this time getting answers would be a lot more difficult as it was the woman he loved that he had to extract the truth from. If what Halfpenny revealed was genuine, then The Mexican Devil didn’t know how he would react. TUESDAY 31ST JULY The sun was blazing throughout this Chicago neighbourhood, there wasn’t a cloud in sight, it truly was a beautiful day. The beauty was amiss though, for the greasy black haired Mexican, who was sat in his, cheap maroon coloured, 1992 Proton 1.5 SE, for he felt like he had the weight of the world on his shoulders. "How did it come to this?" he said to himself. Dressed in his new black Affliction t-shirt, black denim jeans and black sneakers, El Diablo thought about the past two weeks. A great two weeks in his FUSE career, winning the FUSE LiveWire Title, qualifying for the War In Washington Tower Match and joining The Affliction. It was two weeks that any wrestler would be proud of, but all that was going through The Mexican Devil’s mind was whether Angela really was behind his incarceration at a Mental Institution. "How could she? Halfpenny had no reason to lie, not eight years after it had happened," he continued speaking aloud to no one but himself. The weather was in direct contrast of how El Diablo was feeling, he felt hurt, angry and betrayed. If the weather matched his mood it would be rain, thunder, lightning and the four horsemen of the apocalypse riding through the streets. "It makes sense, but then it doesn’t make sense," Diablo struggled to perceive the situation. Sitting and waiting, Diablo was parked outside of his home in a suburb of Chicago, with the windows closed and the 90 degree heat only getting hotter within the car. He sat with his hands on the wheel, contemplating whether or not to go inside his home and see his wife. If he did go inside, he didn’t know what he would say to her, or do to her. "I just don’t understand how she could do it. I trusted her and she betrayed me," he said bitterly. Maybe it was the heat, or maybe it was his anger but The Creator of Controversy was going bright red in the face and had to get out of the car. "Why, Angela, why?" Diablo remarked. Opening the door of the car, Diablo stepped out and slammed the door shut. He stood staring at the home he had shared with Angela for six years, wondering if all of their history was a fallacy. "I’m vulnerable Angela, and I hate being vulnerable," he said to himself. Walking up the paved driveway, Diablo carefully studied his home, looking at the long green grass and the flowerbed of the front garden with honey bees buzzing in the background, looking at the evergreen bushes that separated the house from his neighbours and the mailbox that read "THE DIAZ’S" which was in an unorthodox place on the porch, a stark reminder to him of what was at stake if Halfpenny had told him the truth about his wife. "Here it goes," stated Diablo as he placed his right hand on the door handle. Slowly pulling the handle down, The Mexican Devil finally entered his home, as he readied for the confrontation with his wife which he was about to engineer. "Honey I’m home," he said sarcastically. Angela walked out from the living room, across the brown coloured carpet and wrapped her arms around her husband, unaware that anything was wrong. "You’re all sweaty," commented Angela. Smirking, El Diablo wiped the sweat from his brow with his hand and ran it down his denim jeans. "That’s disgusting. Why don’t you just go and get a shower?" Angela suggested. Remaining silent, The Mexican Devil didn’t seem ready to talk to her. Sliding his shoes off, Diablo walked through the baron hallway, past the cream painted wall and into the living room, where he lunged himself onto the black leather sofa. "This is relaxing," muttered Diablo. Soon following him into the living room was Angela, wearing a red button up blouse and a black mini skirt. She sat down on the black leather chair, nearby to the sofa and stared intently at her husband of eight years. "So, you’re through to that Tower match," stated Angela. Turning his head to look at her, Diablo simply nodded and then turned away. Intense thoughts of hatred towards his wife running vividly through his head. "You’ve had a great two weeks in FUSE. Things finally seem to be going your way," BITCH! The Mexican Devil didn’t look at her this time, instead he just stared at the silver coloured television, which was opposite him on the brown wooden tabletop. "Yeah, it’s all going great," Diablo gritted his teeth. Standing up, Angela walked across the smooth floral patterned carpet, passing the red tinted walls and glass coffee table and took a seat next to her husband by shoving his legs out of the way and forcing him to sit up properly. "I’ve missed you," she whispered to him. LIAR! Grinning, Diablo ran his hands through his greasy hair and sighed as his thoughts were running wild, conjuring up all kinds of circumstances where Angela had enjoyed betraying him. These fabricated images were only making The Master of Manipulation angrier. "I’ve missed you too," he lied. As much as he tried to ignore her he couldn’t, as she looked as beautiful as ever, with her long red hair flowing down past her shoulders and her beautiful green eyes locked precisely on his. Diablo was angry with her, but she still took his breath away. "It’s your first title defence this week," she commented. LIKE YOU CARE! Scratching his stubble, The Master of Manipulation was contemplating how to bring up the IPC situation, without getting furious with her. He thought that she would comply easier if he remained calm, but to someone full of resentment, like he was, it would be a tough task. "Yeah, it should be a straightforward defence," he said confidently. Leaning in to him, Angela kissed her husband on the cheek, not realising he was bursting with pent up aggression. "It’s great that everything is going so well for you in FUSE now," exclaimed Angela. DON’T SOUND SO SURPRISED! Sliding over to the far side of the sofa, El Diablo was getting incensed, despite trying to remain calm, and Angela started to notice that something wasn’t right. "Raul, what’s wrong?" she asked. Breathing heavily, it was like a switch went off inside of The Mexican Devil as he finally exploded in an outburst of sheer anger. "EVER HEARD OF THE IPC?" he shouted. Furious, El Diablo leapt up off of the sofa and almost crashed into the glass coffee table, which quivered vibrantly. Meanwhile Angela’s heart was racing a mile a minute as she knew something was seriously wrong, but tried to disguise her anxiety. "What’s the IPC?" queried Angela. El Diablo was outraged at that response, as he knew very well that she was lying to him. "DON’T LIE TO ME!" screamed Diablo. Having never seen her husband this livid before, Angela was scared for her well being and began to breathe very heavily as she attempted to speak. "W-w-w-where h-h-have y-you b-b-b-been?" she stuttered. This question threw The Mexican Devil off guard as he couldn’t believe the audacity of her questioning him. In his fury, Diablo began to pace the room, trying to walk away his rage as Angela watched in horror, not knowing what may happen. "DOES IT MATTER?" he yelled. Somehow finding the strength to get to her feet, Angela was prepared to stand up to her husband, whatever the consequences may be. "O-o-o-of course it matters," she muttered. Despite trying to calm down, there was an uncontrollable rage within El Diablo that he just could not get rid of. "I REMEMBERED ANGELA! I REMEMBERED!" Diablo shouted at her. Aware that she could not contain her lie any longer, tears began to stream down Angela’s beautiful face. Diablo looked at her, and was absolutely disgusted with what he was seeing. "DON’T START THE WATERWORKS. I’M THE ONE THAT WAS BETRAYED!" screeched El Diablo. Wiping away the tears, Angela was beside herself with worry, not only for herself but for her husband’s well being also. "I WENT TO THE IPC HEADQUARTERS AND I FOUND OUT EVERYTHING," he bellowed once more. Feeling betrayed by the one person he loved, The Creator of Controversy headed towards the door to leave his home, perhaps for good. "NO, DON’T GO!" Angela screamed to him. Chasing after him, she grabbed his arm to try and stop him from leaving, but Diablo shrugged her off of him causing her to slam down hard to the floor. "You don’t know everything, just calm down and I’ll tell you everything," she wailed. The Mexican Devil wasn’t a woman beater and felt guilty about how he had knocked her to the floor, and despite his ongoing frenzy, he still loved her and helped her to her feet. "I’ll tell you everything," she said again. The two of them walked back into the living room and sat back down on the sofa. Beginning to calm down, El Diablo was more hurt than angry as he awaited to hear Angela’s side of the story. "When we met, my mother had been very unwell for a long time and we were barely paying the bills, so we couldn’t really afford her medicine and mom was getting worse and worse. We didn’t know what to do," Angela informed him. More tears were welling up in Angela’s eyes as she told her husband her story. The Mexican Devil could see that Angela was very upset, but all he could do was nervously scratch at his stubble as he tried to suppress his rage. "Then the company we got the medicine from contacted us, to try and set up a pay scheme which we just couldn’t meet and my father and I were at a loss for what to do," she continued on. Beginning to feel sympathy for her, El Diablo had never known about Angela’s mother being so mentally ill and was wondering why she had never told him until now. "You could have come to me," Diablo interrupted. Wiping her tears away, Angela placed her right hand on his left hand and looked straight into Diablo’s soul, knowing that he was a good man and that she had wronged him. "We’d only just met, I didn’t want to put all my baggage out there for you to see and then leave me, because of it," Angela stated. Hearing that statement being made, The Mexican Devil knew that he would have left her if he had known and that Angela must have really loved him by not telling him about her mother and marrying him. "The company must have done some research, because they found me and talked to me about your troubled past," she carried on telling the story. It was clear where the story was going, but the Creator of Controversy had to hear the words being said to him so he could have some closure on this chapter of his life. "They said they had some new medicine but needed to test it on suitable subjects and that my mother could use it for free once the trials were finished," said Angela, visibly upset. Running through Diablo’s mind were the first few months of their romance all those years ago. He could now piece together why she had seemed so distant and miserable, it began to make sense to him now. "They said they wanted to use you and I said no. We had only just got married and I didn’t want to lose you for however long the trials would last. But they kept pushing me and my father, and he told them to do it," Angela told of the incident. Relieved to know the truth, Diablo threw his arms around his wife and held her close to him as she cried softly onto his shoulder. "So it wasn’t all your fault," expressed Diablo. Sniffing, Angela lifted her head up from his shoulder and kissed him on the cheek. "When my father died, I got you out of there," she whispered. The Mexican Devil still felt betrayed, but could understand the reasoning behind it. He still loved Angela and had to tell her something important, something he had planned to tell her over a week ago, but couldn’t muster up the courage for it. "Angela, the injections they gave me, they, they made me sterile," he remarked. Angela let out a large cry as she latched onto her husband. She felt guilty as she was partly responsible for him being in there and they could no longer have any children together. "It’s okay," he whispered into her ear. Holding her close to him, El Diablo was upset but pleased that this chapter of his life had at long last ended and that they could put it behind them and come out stronger from it. "We still have the future to look forward to, and my future in FUSE is looking very bright indeed," commented Diablo. The newest Affliction member had many new memories to make and was optimistic about his life for a change. Now he could leave his mark on FUSE by dominating with The Affliction and putting all his past tragedies behind him. For once, the future looked bright for The Mexican Devil. THE END!
