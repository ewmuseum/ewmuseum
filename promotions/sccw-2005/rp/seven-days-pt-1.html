Date: Today WAR Webster’s Dictionary defines war as a state of usually open and declared armed hostile conflict between states or nations. That’s what the scholars want you to think. But for those of us that make the squared circle a battle ground, it doesn’t matter if you’re armed or unarmed. We are at war every day of our existence. The scholars don’t know shit. In preparation of war, the combatants that are going to be locked in the immortal struggle between good and evil do many different things. From pumping their bodies full of chemicals. For others, it’s the fierce training that is involved. Yet, for the few that don’t take this war seriously, they spend their time in leisure. But there is one thing for sure, that no matter what you do to prepare for this war, you are going to be in for the fight of your life. ---==One week until War In Washington ===--- The Cowboys from Hell are leaving the Dean Smith Center in Chapel Hill , North Carolina , having just defeated three other tag teams to unify FUSE’s Fatal Faction Championship with their titles. A pair of arena attendants is carrying a rather large case out to the SUV that they rented for the evening. It is assumed that in this case are the eight tag team title belts that the Cowboys from Hell accumulated over the course of the show. They are looking towards War In Washington as legitimizing the Fatal Faction Championships as true World Tag Team titles by defending them against a team that they had never seen before. The Rising Sons. The Cowboys know not to take anyone too lightly. After all, the Alliance underestimated them, and that cost them the titles. The Cowboys from Hell weren’t going to make the same mistake. Even though they’ve seen the picture of two scrawny Japanese Wrestlers, they knew that looks could be deceiving. They already had it in their heads to prepare for anything. These two little kids could know some kung fu. They might be able to fly around the ring like some luchador. All they know is that whoever these two men turn out to be, that the Cowboys from Hell are going to be ready for them. As the Cowboys are being driven back to their hotel, Jake and Araceli are embracing each other in the backseat, while the driver and Wyatt are fighting over the radio. The driver wants to listen to smooth jazz. Wyatt wants to listen to anything else. But there was no compromise on this car ride. "Penny, for your thoughts." Araceli said as she laid back into Jake’s arms. "After War in Washington , after Wyatt and I defeat these two little bastards that Riley has brought in to face us, what’s left for us? We’ve already defeated the Affliction, and they still have their hands full with Aimz and Crisis." This level of wonder was something that Araceli wasn’t accustomed to, at least coming from Jake. It was almost like he was contemplating leaving FUSE after War in Washington . Maybe even the wrestling profession. The years of in ring battles were starting to wear on him. Araceli knew this. She saw it in his eyes. The fire and passion that attracted her to him was starting to fizzle out. ---==Six Days until War In Washington ===--- The day of rest. Wyatt was back in Calgary , while Araceli was in a rather unfamiliar surrounding for her. She was sitting on a fence, watching Jake moving bales of hay from the back of an old, beat up pick up, down to where his horses would be able to feed on them. An old time rancher, still using old time balers to make up the feed for his animals. This was what Jake enjoyed in life. This wasn’t the life that Araceli ever thought that she would come into, but the ranch hand that just happens to be a professional wrestler embedded himself into her heart. With the sun beating down on Jake, he knew that it was almost time to take a break. Sweat was pouring from his brow. Finally, his arms were getting exhausted from lugging around the seventy pound bricks made up from Alfalfa. Araceli came down from her fence and brought over a bottle of cold water, which Jake proceeded to douse himself with. It cooled him off, but Araceli saw something right then and there that she hadn’t seen in a while from her cowboy. The passion that he once had in the ring was back. But this wasn’t where she expected to see it. She thought that maybe it was because of the lack of opposition in the Fatal Faction division. But now, she wasn’t so sure. Maybe it was the fact that this was the life that he was made for. Not the glitz and glamour of appearing on a weekly television series. Not the lights and nightlife that she was accustomed to in Toronto . But the hard work ethic that he has taken upon himself in Wyoming . This was where Jake belonged. No matter how much she tried to get him to relocate to Toronto , she never understood that. Until now, that is. She saw that this was the environment that he thrived in. He was at peace with himself here. Overlooking the solar panels and wind turbines that an energy company installed on his land. Watching over the livestock that his family has made a living raising and selling. For Jake Ennis, this, no where else, was home. His employees, his friends lived here, and she tried to take him away from this. She felt bad that it took her this long to realize this. Decided to spend the week with him here, instead of meeting up with him later on, when he’s getting ready to head to the next city gave Araceli a new understanding on who the "Wyoming Cowboy" really was. He wasn’t Connor Chesney. He wasn’t Conner Ennis. He was Jake Ennis. "Jake, what would you say if I decided to come live here?" At first, she didn’t get an answer from him. She looked down, like she didn’t say anything, maybe he didn’t hear it. Or even if he did, she could try and pass it off like she had asked something else, and that he just heard it wrong. But that was the thing about Jake. He had great hearing. He heard her, but contemplated his answer. Carefully. "Because I think that it’s beautiful here." Araceli was taking a big step. She didn’t know if she was ready for this, but she knew that she didn’t want to lose Jake by forcing him into coming to Toronto . She didn’t want to make him choose between her and his family’s tradition. She knew that if she tried it, that she would lose. But Jake still didn’t comment. Instead, he thought. This was why he enjoyed the range, because it was a place in which he could think and not have to worry about anything. But he thought. About what Araceli had asked, about his future, about their future. "Yes." Simple, effective, the answer that was the best that he could come up with. After thinking long and hard about it, Jake knew that this was a good idea. It would get rid of the last worry, the final stress in his life. For Jake Ennis, it was time to go to the next level. That meant that he had to go the next step with the woman that he had fallen in love with. ---==Five days until War In Washington ===--- "Was there anything that I could help you with?" a sales lady dressed in a low cut top and red blazer asked. She was leaning over the counter at a jewelry store. Towering above her, looking into the case was her customer, Wyatt Colton. "Yes, I would like to see that ring." He sets his finger on the glass, pointing at a ring encrusted with three cut topazes. "That’s it. It’s perfect." The sales lady went to pull it out to show it to Wyatt, but he stopped her. "I don’t want to see it. Box it up and ring me up." The sales lady smiled because of the assertiveness that came from the 6’5" Fatal Faction Champion. "It’s a beautiful setting, who’s the lucky girl?" The sales lady was now starting to ask too many questions. "My mother. I haven’t seen her in a couple of years, and I was coming up for the fifth anniversary of my father’s death." OK, he lied. Really, the ring was for Camilla Renault, known to many that have followed Wyatt’s recent life as Cammy. Wyatt left her up in Whitehorse to meet his parents, and during that time, he had the chance to meet hers. She didn’t know about this meeting, but they told him a bit about her. As he was sure that his parents probably shown her the bear rug picture. But he knew that this was something that he wanted to do. She was special to him, and he wanted her to know that. He didn’t want to leave anything to chance. Yellow topaz, her birthstone. It was the little things in life that most people miss. She didn’t tell him that she liked the stone, which was something that her parents did. He wasn’t expected back to the building until tomorrow. Wyatt was going to surprise her. He exited the jewelry store with the ring in hand and got into his car. Cammy was going to be surprised. That was the only thing going through his head as he drove home. He didn’t prepare himself for what he was going to find there. As soon as he put the key in the lock, and the tumblers adjusted to unlock the door, he noticed that it opened a little too easily, as if the door was already unlocked. He opened the door, and saw that his living room was in a state of disrepair, with two men moving his couch back. "Who in the hell are you?" The men were surprised and dropped the couch, it landing squarely on one of the men’s foot. Before Wyatt could do anything about the two men that he thought were in the process of breaking and entering, Cammy entered the living room in paint splattered overalls. "Wyatt, they’re helping me out. Why are you home already?" Wyatt has a rather inquisitive look come to his face as he realized that the two men had been moving the couch back to where it had been. He then looked around and seen that his living room had gone from white to a more earthy brown color. To say that he was shocked was an understatement. She wanted to surprise him, and he came home too early for it. "Wow." That’s all that he could say. "I wanted you to wait until it was done before you saw it." Wyatt didn’t know what to say. He opened his mouth to say the first thing that could come to his mind. "I looks good." A smile came to Cammy’s face, as her hair was covered in paint and she scrunched her nose in that cutesy fashion you see many times in bad romantic comedies. She walked over and went to give him a hug, but he stopped her with the box that he got from the store. "For me?" She gently took the box from his hand and opened it to see the ring that he just purchased. The light that was shining was nothing compared to the bright smile that came to her face. "How did you know?" "Your parents came to see me in North Carolina , wanted to get to know me a little better. The told me that you liked yellow topaz." "It’s……" A tear started to stream down the side of her eye. "It’s beautiful." She took the ring out of the box and put it on. It was a tad loose, but she knew that any jeweler could fix that. The happiness that came to her face was well worth it. Inside the arena, he was rough and tumble, befitting being called a Cowboy from Hell. But now, he was just a big teddy bear. Happy that his girlfriend knew how much he really cared for her. "But…" Famous last words. Usually, when a woman hears them, something bad was going to happen. "What?" Cammy almost didn’t want to hear what Wyatt was going to say. "I wanted you to know that I have to cancel the plans that we had for tomorrow night. I’m going to be flying out to Wyoming . Jake and I are going to be getting ready for War In Washington." A bit of sadness came over Cammy’s face. She missed Wyatt. He had been gone for the last two weeks, and she didn’t want to spend even less time with him. "I don’t want you to go." "I know, but it’s what I do. But I talked to Jake, and you’re more than welcome to stay on his ranch with us. Araceli will be there also." Wyatt saying this brought the smile back to Cammy’s face. "I can handle that."
