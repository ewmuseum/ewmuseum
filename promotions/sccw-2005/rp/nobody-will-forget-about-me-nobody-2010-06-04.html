Nobody will forget about me. NOBODY.

4 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/04/2010 This is the end time. I’m a member of an industry that is slowly falling apart at the seams. Being ripped and torn apart by it’s own means is what’s really going on. The egos are running rampant and the hotter temperatures outside aren’t helping matters much. Everyone seems to think that they are the biggest thing in this industry and at the top of that list is Legion. Attacking a poor, helpless announcer shows just how much of a coward this man really is. You want to beat up announcers? That’s fine. Fight a real man like myself and let’s see how you do in the ring. However I’m stuck being your partner against some dumbass named "The Violence" Vince Jones who has no business being in the ring with me. This guy is about as entertaining as staring at an acorn for three hours. What a joke he is. And then he has his little bitch Princess Jasmine talking before he starts referring to himself as ‘V.’ What the hell is he, a miniseries? PLEASE. This match is a total waste of my time. Jones is a wanna-be version of Brick from the old GLOBAL hybrid wrestling organization. First he panders to the fans and then he heels out. Reality check, Jones. I did that over a year ago and it’s been done many times in the past so you’re nothing special, just a rehash of people who are more talented and literate in comparison to yourself. Wrestlers like Vince Jones are why the wrestling business is going straight down the toilet. You want a prime example of why this industry sucks more than most female porn stars do? Then all you have to do is watch this past week’s SCCW Temptation when Vince Jones was on and see what I’m talking about. If I wanted to see this shit, then I’d just go hang out with my buddy Painmaster Tyrone and turn on the BET network or watch some shitty MTV documentary show about a crappy rapper that’s on par with Rappin’ Rick Malloy. Vince Jones, your street cred isn’t worth jack fucking shit until you back it up in the ring. The fact that you talk like a thug and then claim to be God’s Gift to SCCW’ like just another common egotistical heel which is EXACTLY what you are. You’re just another dime a dozen heel that nobody will give two shits about in six months. I did this same exact spiel over in the WWA a month ago and the difference between me and you is that I backed it up. You won’t. Next on my list is Jadian Bridden, the former GateWire Champion and another egomaniac wrestler roaming the halls of SCCW. You know what? The egomaniac wrestler archetype is getting so fucking stale it’s not even funny. I’d take ten Spacelys over one of these guys. I don’t care how good you are, you still aren’t as good as you think you are ninety percent of the time. That’s why my strategy has been this: just sit back and let the egomaniacs kill each other off while I just watch from the background. It’s worked for years yet I’m considered to be fucking crazy by the wrestling world. Probably a lot of that stigma has to do with my many associates in this industry and how eccentric they are yet I’m the one who gets blamed for it and rightly so. I trained the lot of them to be honest about it. There’s more psychology in what I do than people think. I do things all the time to see how people react and it’s usually negative. That shows me whose skin I can get under the quickest. That information works really well in the ring. The more I piss someone off on the shows leading into the match, the more mistakes they make in the ring against me because they’re too busy trying to kick my ass. That gets their mind off of match strategy and they’re going on emotions instead which plays into my gameplan. It’s a plan that’s worked for years too. I’ve got Legion already wound up and we haven’t even hit the ring yet. People have wanted me dead for years and I’m still walking around here kicking people’s asses. My own friends tried to eliminate me and it didn’t even work and they KNEW all of my weaknesses. I’m still creating chaos and nobody will ever stop me from doing that. NOBODY. I’m the most underrated talent in this industry, period. Look at it from my perspective. I’ve had people trying to end my career constantly for over seven years now and guess what? I’m still walking and I’m still talking. I’m not talking jive like Vince Jones, rambling incoherently like Legion, or pandering to the fans like your heroes such as Jared Sykes. This match is not about who the fans like because they hate all four of us. This match is about kicking each other’s asses and that’s all there is to it. I may be broken and beat up but I can still take on all three of you. Vince Jones is on here talking about the WCCWFEECW or some garbage like that. Nobody gives a rat’s ass about some rinky dink indy fed that none of us ever heard of before. I don’t give two shits about it. You’re career highlights are from pre-9/11 America, that’s how old they are. This match is going to be your nightmare. You’ll be a flash in the pan, however your kids and grandkids are going to be talking about me. There ain’t nobody like me in this business. When history looks back on this time period, people will see me and think that I’m a bizarre, eccentric individual. That’s why I will be remembered and you will be forgotten. Nobody’s going to forget about me. NOBODY.
