All That and a Bag of Chips

21 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-21-09 Decisions. Have you ever been in a part of your life when you realize that the decision you make on a certain subject could end up changing what happens in your future? The answer to that question should be a resounding yes, because every decision you make alters what lies ahead. Some of them may be insignificant, but others may very well leave a lasting impression. Sometimes though...sometimes those insignificant ones can end up leaving more of an impression than you first thought. Xander Searle made a decision to be an arrogant prick, without first earning the right to be one. He chose to stand alone in a war that he was outnumbered in on more than one front. He wanted to face Desade's Hand, a task that has been tried and failed by members of the roster with far more experience. He chose to alienate himself from not only the fans, but those that could aid him in his uphill battle. A war where he has yet to even gain a foothold at the bottom of the hill. His arrogance did not end there either. No, he actually believed that holding the Livewire title gave him some sort of importance over others. Apparently, a guaranteed shot at the title did not mean the same thing in his pea-sized brain. He felt he could add a stipulation, he decided that there had to be more on the line in the match at Equillibrium. He wanted Julissa to be his trophy. His ultimatum was laughed at, tossed aside like the ranting of a fool. Not in fear that the match might be lost, but because NO ONE told a member of the Hand what they were or were not going to do. Nigel Kensington tried to call the Hand's bluff, only to realize the hard way – the difficult way – that it was not a bluff and even he was not in a position to be handing out threats. But Xander isn't the champion anymore, so all of his boasting and braggart only ended up making him out to look like the fool. Hilarious when you take into consideration that he lost to a man that dresses up like a damn fruit. Baron von Blackberry pulled a victory out of thin air, effectively throwing a monkey wrench into not only Xander's plans, but mine as well. This match went from being a chance to choke the life and fight out of Searle with relative ease, to being a triple threat where one of those idiots can pin each other and end up as champion without me being a part of the outcome. It sucks. Blackberry acts and dresses like a fool, but the man can hold his own inside that ring. There is something about him that I cannot put my finger on, but he may not be the fool he makes himself out to be. That could turn out to be more of a problem than I first thought, but it is not going to keep me from going out there and proving that I deserve to have that title around my waist. Something Searle said a few weeks back has bothered me since that day, the fact that I was supposed to gain a higher standing in Sin City after losing the Livewire title. I had to take some time off due to an out of ring injury, an injury I have yet to forget or forgive my attacker for. What he said bothered me for quite some time, but then I realized something that he did not. It wasn't his fault, because it is clear the man does not see everything without rose colored glasses. What he did not realize is that I have moved on past the Livewire title. So much so, that people can see this title as something legitimate for the first time in a long time. It may sound like I am being a bit arrogant, and while I have pretty much earned the right to be full of myself...this isn't me claiming the world revolves around me and light shines out of my ass. But I do bring a bit of legitimacy to this match. I know, I can't help but sound arrogant. It's as much a part of me as my arm. When I first won this shot, I said that I really did not want it. I was not lying. I did not enter that match wanting to win so I could have anotehr shot at the Livewire title, but I did what I wanted to do and won the match because I wanted to prove that what people saw in me when I won the title was still there. I did not come back to give someone another mark in the win column, unless that someone happens to be me. I came back to win, and if that meant I had to go through Saint, Mitchell Quinlan, and Xander Searle to prove it that is what I was going to do. If I happen to win the Livewire title then so be it, but do you really think that if I don't win I'm going to be broken hearted. If you do, then you don't know me very well. Searle has high hopes, aspirations of becoming a name in Sin City. I have the talent to actually get there, and this time I don't plan on stopping until I get there. So, to those I step over on my way there...I apologize for what I'm about to do. * * * * * * Jadian Bridden is standing in front of a section of apples, one of the golden delicious beauties is in his hand as he gives it a quick search for imperfections. He finds one too. It was under his middle finger, a slight discoloration that felt a bit mushy to the touch. He places it back with the rest of them, running his hand over the top of the apples as he searches for another piece to add to the five he already has in a bag. He carefully plucks one from beneath another two, trying his best to keep from sending them toppling to the floor. "It isn't rocket science, pal," someone says in a raspy voice from behind. "Just grab one and go. I ain't got all day." Jadian turns slowly, a wide eyed look of surprise on his face when he finds the person that sounds like they've been gargling razor blades, is an old woman with a plastic repirator tube wrapped around her face. Her face is shriveled up like a prune, twisting her lips into a slight scowl as she waves Jadian away with the back of her left hand. "You like what you see?" she asks angrily. "Take a damn picture." "I'm sorry ma'am," Jadian says as he steps back, grazing her basket as he moves away. "I didn't mean to upset you." "You're gonna make me miss my damn soaps, you little bastard." Wow, this lady is full of spunk, he thinks to himself as he places the apple in his hand into the bag in his basket. "Well, you have a nice day." "Moves slower than old people fuck," sh emumbles under her breath as she walks back to her basket, closing the flap on her purse before walking away. "Well, you were pretty civil." Jadian finds Julissa Minorez behind him when he turns this time, her crooked grin judging him from a distance. "She's probably seconds away from sucking her life away with another cigarette." he says with a slight grin of his own. "I don't think I need to waste my time speeding things up." "Still, I really thought you were going to trip her when she walked away." "It did cross my mind," he glances back as the woman places the few things in her basket on the conveyer belt of the quick checkout register. "But then I realized that she reminded me a lot of you." "Oh, now that's sweet." Julissa places a package of batteries in the basket, scanning its contents for a moment. "Where's my iPod?" "Probably in the electronics department," he answers, pushing the basket away from the produce section with Juli not too far behind. "But I already had one in the basket," she tells him as she tries to catch up. "Where's that one?" The alarms start to sound in the background, followed by the voice of a man asking to check the contents of someone's bags. It is the raspy voice of a woman telling someone to fuck off that causes Julissa to turn back. "Oh, I'm sure it will turn up somewhere." "You're such an ass," Julissa says with a shake of her head. "Maybe next time she'll think before she runs her mouth." Jadian turns the basket in the direction of the electronics department. "Now what color iPod did you want?" That raspy voice screams out one more time, "Don't taze me – OOOOOHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!" * * * * * * Carmen De La Fuentes stepped out of the stuffy board room and gently closed the door behind her. The negotiations were going perfectly, and pretty soon another wealthy client would have more than enough money to pay for the services that had been requested. Though it wasn't the money her client was after. He had said the money made no matter, when compared to the valuable information that Volante Pharmeceuticals had stashed away in its files. Unfortunately, the client was not the only one interested in the various formulas and information the company kept a tight lid on. Ms. Pierotti had been very adamant when she told Carmen that she needed to find the president of the company's office and break into the safe. This seemed like as good a time as any to do that. A security guard stepped up from behind an aluminum desk as she made her way down the hall, the click of her heels in a steady rhythm as she approached the very dark latino man. "I'm jus - Necesito utilizar el baño." she remembered the man had spoken little to no english when he asked for her credentials earlier. She nodded as he motioned down the hall. "Gracias." She remembered passing Dr. Enrique Volante's office when they stepped off of the elevator. Carmen hoped the security guard wasn't paying attention when she passed the door to the bathroom and continued on to Volante's office. She stood in front of the office door and turned the knob before she pushed it open. It wasn't locked. The furniture was mostly modern décor. But it was the print of Massacre of the Innocents by Peter Paul Rubens that held her attention. She rushed over and carefully removed the print from the wall, the safe behind it brought a smile to her face. Her ear was pressed against the wall as she tapped the door a few times. It was a decent size, and held the most important projects the company worked on. The aerosol spray can of dust she pulled from her purse was small, but she didn't need much to complete the task. She sprayed the keypad lightly and allowed the dust to settle on the most pressed buttons. It was either a three digit combination, or one of the butons was pressed twice. She figured she would chance it and try a few variations of the three digit combination. It was the sixth variation that caused an audible click, and a loss of breath. It was the voice that caused her to take a step back. "Que tas..." the thick spanish accent called out from behind her. "No te muevas!" She lifted her arms, the door to the safe still slightly cracked open inches away from her. He extended his pistol, a nine millimeter semi-automatic to be precise. His hands shook as he approached her, the fact her arms were raised in the air did nothing to calm his nerves. He could feel every beat of his heart in the back of his throat, as he reached toward her shoulder with his free hand. She moved with such grace and speed that he was not sure if he froze up, but her hand gripped around his wrist and twisted it in a fashion that caused him to release his weapon when she applied a slight bit of pressure. It did not take long for him to feel her elbow smash against the back of his skull, that soft spot where neck and head connect to be exact. The manuever managed to get him to drop down to a knee, dazed but not unconscious. That did not last long, as he could see her spin around from his left side with what appeared to be a knee. His vision was so blurred he could not be sure, but something definitely slammed against his nose and made everything turn black. He would not find out that he dropped back with his legs tucked beneath his body until a few hours later when he was awoken by a nurse. Carmen, on the other hand, had something she had to do before she returned to the conference room. With the ease of a professional thief, she glided across the linoleum floor to where the safe was. A few files were pulled out and snapshots were taken. Then, with everything she came here to do was accomplished, she smashed a large vase over the head of the much larger security guard. The move was unnecessary, but if everything went as she figured during those few minutes she had to plan, no one would suspect that the lithe woman with straight black hair and an innocent smile could ever have taken the larger man down. Now she just had to hope that the security system had been taken care of as planned. Well, there was also the problem of looking like a victim. She approached the door, took a few deep breaths as she walked slightly to the left of the entrance and closed her eyes. She filled her lungs once more, eyes squeezed shut as she placed her palms on the wall and leaned her head back. 'No hesitation,' she thought right before she slammed the right side of her face against the wall. The pain rose up through her body, her knees buckled, and she collapsed to the floor. Every breath that followed was short and quick, her heart rate needed to beat quicker than it was. She not only had to look like someone who was attacked, but she needed to look genuinely afraid that someone was about to hurt her. No more time could be taken, she had to head back to the room. She removed one shoe at a time, and made sure to toss them haphazardly on the ground. Then she screamed at the top of her lungs and took off in a dead sprint toward the conference room. * * * * * * Jadian and Julissa walk through the sliding doors with a few bags in their hands, as security and a police officer struggle to stuff an old woman screaming obscenities into the back of a squad car. Julissa nudges Jay on the arm, motioning toward the commotion with a twist of her neck. "You're not going to do anything about that?" she asks him, a genuine look of concern for the woman on her face. "Nah," Jay answers with a shake of his head, not even bothering to look in that direction. "They don't look like they need my help." She sighs out loud, rolling her eyes as she shakes her head. "You know that's not what I meant." "Fine," he says, his shoulders dropping in defeat. "You go start the car and I'll figure out how to break her free." "Jadian," she slaps him on the arm. "Tell them you accidentally dropped it into her purse or something." "Yeah right," he laughs. "Serves the bitch right for talking to me like I was some punk kid with snot running out of my nose. She probably would have got a slap on the wrist, but like a few people I know, she doesn't know when to keep her mouth shut." "Are you talking about Searle again?" "No," he chuckles heartily. "I'm talking about you. Do you realize you haven't stopped talking since we stepped through the doors. I'm not helping that old hag out. Besides, we need to get back to the house so you can pack for your trip." "I would've finished earlier if you could learn to keep your hands to yourself." "You shouldn't have been bent over in your thong. Looked like a bullseye when I walked into the room, so I hit it." "Asshole," she says with a grin. "It was there." he replies with a shrug of his shoulders. "Jerk," this time she punches his arm, the neverending foreplay between the two. "That isn't how I meant it." "I know, but I like teasing you." The door of the squad car slams shut, and four men in uniform - trying to catch their breath - stand in front of it. The old lady starts to kick at the glass, her skirt up around her waist with her wrists apparently handcuffed behind her back. "See," he motions with his head for her to take a look. "I told you she reminded me a lot of you." * * * * * * "Miss De La Fuentes!" Everyone at the table jumped up at once when the woman burst through the door. She crumpled in a heap in the open doorway as a few of the men in the room rushed to her aide. It was the man limping toward her with the hawk head cane that spoke first. "Give her room to breathe," he said loudly as he slowly made his way toward her. "What happened?" one of the other men asked her. She took notice of the dark gray pinstriped suit and knew without looking up that it was Volante himself. "Someone attacked me from behind," her words were slow and deliberate. "He dragged me into a room and threw me on the floor. I'm not sure what he wanted, but he was saying something in Spanish and kept repeating the name Gloria." She heard a woman in the small crowd gasp, as Volante screamed down the hall for someone named Gustavo. Probably the guard. "There was a noise outside the door, so he told me to turn around and face the wall. I heard someone walk in, then glass shattering. When I turned around whoever the man was hit me. I barely made it here after he left." The man with the cane stooped down beside her, the strain in his knee rather unbearable in a position like this. "Are you sure everything is okay?' he asked her. She knew right away he was more concerned with what she was supposed to do, than her well being. "I'm fine," she replied with a nod, the answer tucked safely in her eyes where no one but those looking for them would find, and the man Alexandra Pierce called Mr. Hawke was on one knee next to her staring into them. "Where's the closest hospital?" he asked. "I'll drive her there while you find out who these people were." "Yes," Dr. Volante said. He stood up and started talking to one of the women in the crowd, then turned to one of the men and started barking orders. "Time to go," he said under his breath as he helped Carmen, a woman he normally called Julissa, to her feet. He let her lean against him as they made their way down the hall, Dr. Volante by his side as they passed the office on their way to the elevators. "We will find who did this," Volante promised. "And they will pay dearly for what they have done." They were right there in front of him and he was doing nothing. She wanted to laugh, to scream, but she held her tongue. There would be plenty of time for that later. Too bad Carmen would not be there.
