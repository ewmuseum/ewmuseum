Sins of the Father

25 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.25.08 "So this is what you consider more important than college?" The former FUSE LiveWire champion lifted her head, the light shimmering off the jewel encrested mask that conceals her identity as her eyes widened and began to tear up. Still dressed in her provocative ring gear, which would be labeled as lingerie at most retail outlets, Reyna Preciosa reached behind her head to unlace her mask. "H-how did you fi-find out," she asked, an evident lump in her throat as she stumbled on her words. "Do you think I'm stupid?" The man who spoke before stood across the room from her, his arms folded across his chest and his eyes narrowed in anger. "No," she replied as she pulled the mask off of her head and let her curly locks of hair fall loosely over her shoulders. "I just wanted to prove to myself I could do this." "Are you happy now," he asked, a tinge of anger in his tone as he appeared to scold the young woman. "Is that what you had in mind when you signed your name on that contract?" "That wasn't supposed to happen." The FUSE crowd knew her as Reyna Preciosa, the quick and agile masked girl that came out of nowhere to become the first ever LiveWire champion. The wrestling world knew him as Benny Minor, one half of the now defunct NWC Pacific Region tag team The Minors. She knew him as 'Dad'. "And what was supposed to happen, Juli?" It was very apparent that he was angry, but only Julissa understood why. "John was supposed to get disqualified after Xantippe climbed into the ring and chokeslammed me," she said as she pulled her powder blue cotton track pants up and over the bottom piece of her ring gear. "I don't know what happened out there, but someone had to have been given the wrong information." "Honestly, I'm surprised that's the worst they did," he replied. "I figured they'd make it more believable." "What do you mean," she asked, her brows furrowed slightly as she pulled a white t-shirt, with the Longhorns logo on the front, up and over her head. "That was your last match, mija." "I know you're upset, dad," she began, pulling her hair out from beneath her sweater before letting it fall around her shoulders. "But I have a contract to ful-" "Had," her father said bluntly. "You had a contract." "What are you talking about?" she asked, her eyes wide and slowly tearing up. "I'm sorry, but I won't let you ruin your life with this business." It wasn't the words she wanted to hear, but it was exactly how he felt. "I told you before I started training you, that this business was ruthless... That it would chew you up and spit you out like a piece of gum, and you chose to ignore me. I won't let you make the mistakes I made, so I bought out your contract." "You what," she asked, nearly out of breath as her face turned a pale shade of pink. "How could you... Why would you do that to me?" "This business changed your uncle," he told her. "I won't let it do the same to you." "You won't let it," the stunned look on her face was accompanied by a few stray tears that ran down her cheeks. "I'm not ten years old anymore. I don't need you standing at the bottom of the slide waiting to catch me. I can make my own decisions, choose what I feel is right for me... Why can't you see that?" "You're too young to understand, Juli," he answered her. "I know you think you know everything, that I'm just being mean for the sake of keeping you from doing what you want to, but one day you'll understand that I'm just doing what I feel is right. What I know is right." "What you know," she repeated, her head shaking from left to right slowly as she speaks. "Since you know everything... Did you know I was going to do this?" Julissa stood up unexpectedly, threw the mask she wore during her short tenure in FUSE and walked out the door. Benny picked the mask up off the floor, his left hand open wide to see the complete face of the mask... "Hey, you got a minute?" Benito Minorez lifted his gaze from the jewel encrested mask his daughter once wore to the ring - a pile of bills were scattered messily across his desk, the least of his concerns these last few weeks - and cracked a small smile. Standing in the half open doorway was the young man he had all the faith in the world in a few months ago. "Jay," Benny said with a wave of his hand. "You know you never have to ask. Take a load off, kid." Jay pushed his way through the door, but it did not close behind him. Following close behind happened to be the woman that has reluctantly become a permanent fixture in his career, Benny's daughter Julissa Minorez. The smile on Benny's face quickly faded when he saw his little girl skipping along behind Bridden with a wide smile on her face, and a brown file folder in her hand. "Nice to see you too, Benny." "What's the meaning of this," the elder Minorez asked, his attention focused on Jadian. "Ask her," he said with a jab of his thumb in Julissa's direction. "I'm just tagging along with my mana-" "Oh come on, dad," Julissa interrupted. "You're going to give me a complex acting like you don't want to see your own daughter on the holidays. I know I'm too old for an Easter basket, but the least you could do is treat me with a little bit of decency." Benny chuckled loudly, struggling to get his words out through the laughter. "Decency?" He shook his head from side to side. "You come into my gym and demand me to sign it over to you, attack Jay here and make him miss show dates in FUSE, then squirm your way into a contract you have no business even thinking about after the mess your career turned out to be. But I'm supposed to be decent... Right." "I guess you could say I take after my dad," Julissa said with a smirk. "Then again, my memory is a bit foggy after all these years... Why don't you remind me about how my career in the ring ended." "Why don't you just get to the point." Julissa reached across the desk, took her mask in her hand, and lifted it up to get a better look at it. "I only want to return the favor... To do for you what you did for me." The slight smirk on her face grew into a deceitful grin. "I just want to save you before this business ruins your life. Oh wait... It's already too late." She threw the mask back down onto the desk in front of her father. "You're still angry about this?" he asked as he lifted the mask and held it a few inches above the desk. "That's what this is all about?" "Oh, believe me, I stopped being angry a long time ago." She shrugged her shoulders slightly, her lower lip jutted out a bit as she batted her eyelashes over her puppy dog eyes. "Whether you know it or not, you took my life from me that night... You took away everything I had ever dreamed about the moment you dragged me here. I was a champion, but you still had more pride in this dump and him." Jay lifted his head to find Julissa jab her thumb in his direction. "How do you like him now? Has he met all of your expectations, been as successful as you hoped?" She shook her head slowly, allowing a single chuckle to escape through her slightly flared nostrils. "I hope you've enjoyed watching your protoge thrown around like a rag doll by men two and three times his size, because this is just the beginning." "Don't listen to her, Ben," Jay finally said something, though he did not stand from his seat across the desk from Benny. "I can take care of myself in that ring. I did learn from the best, you know." "Whatever you think you're doing," Benny sat straight up in his chair, his cheeks flushed with color as he stared holes through his only child. "It's not going to work." "It already has," she replied with a wide grin. "You just haven't realized it yet." Benny's face is devoid of any emotion as he watches his daughter straighten up and walk toward the door. "Make it quick," she told Jay as she pushed the door open. "We need to be at the airport by three." The door clicked close, and the father came out of Benny, but not for his actual child. "All the macho crap aside, you really doing okay?" "I'm alright," Jay replied. "I've had the wind knocked out of me far too many times than I want to remember, and I've had the lights go out during a match once or twice, but I'm not going to give her the pleasure of seeing me in pain." "I'm really sorry about this, kid," Benny shook his head, the mask once again being held in his massive hand. "Things shouldn't be like this, and it's my fault that they are." "If nothing else," Jay said with a smile. "It's getting me a lot of attention." "Just don't get yourself killed out there," Benny clenched his fist, the mask still firmly in his grasp. "Julissa doesn't give a damn about you. All she cares about is proving that I made a mistake." "You did what you felt was right." "No," he shook his head, regret in his eyes. "I did what I thought was best for me. I didn't want her to prove me wrong, so I talked to Steven Caldera, the guy who had just bought FUSE and I got her released from her contract." "Look, Benny," Jay stood up and approached the desk, then placed his hand on Benny's shoulder. "Don't give her what she wants. I don't care if she makes me run a gauntlet against Mayhem, Marshall, Asher, Bishop, and Vassago. I'll deal with it in my own way." "She's just so stubborn." "I wonder where she gets that from." "I just miss her so much." "I know you do," Jay replied, his hand still on Benny's shoulder, comforting him as much as he can. "I know you do."
