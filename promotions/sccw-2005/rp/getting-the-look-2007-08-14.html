Getting the look

14 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-14-07 The morning dawned bright and early over the town of San Francisco, Cyrus Raynes walked down the sidewalk, the early morning sun reflecting its red and orange hues off of the glistening road helping to burn away the fog that had settled during the night in the city by the bay. Cyrus is wearing a pair of black combat boots, dark blue silver tab jeans, and a black t-shirt tucked in to his pants, the logo on the front of the shirt reads 'That which does not kill you, obviously didn't try hard enough'. Cyrus is caring a manila envelope which he stops and opens. Then looks around at his surroundings, judging by the numbers his destination was still a few blocks away. Another half an hour later Cyrus reached his destination. Aaron's costume shop, Cyrus stopped and looked at the sign, the A's looked funny, like Greek letters or something, but other then that nothing out of the ordinary. Cyrus walked across the street, and entered the shop. The bell over the door rang as the door was opened. 'Entré vous' came a yell from the back in a Tallie voice. Cyrus thought he caught a hint of...........naaaa. Standing in the shop surrounded by so many costumes made Cyrus wonder if coming here was such a good idea. It was a small shop, with costumes of every shape and size imaginable crammed together on the the racks. Cyrus looked around the shop, a glass display case on which the cash register was, walls adorned with various posters and pictures, and a door way leading to the back covered by bead curtains. Cyrus began to notice that there was an unusual number of posters of.... 'Oh, my, god! Look at you! Tall, bald and handsome! How could I get so lucky?' Cyrus turned to see the beads swinging back together from where they had parted, and in front of them stood a man of about five and a half feet tall, gaingly arms and legs hanging from a rail thin body. The man had a black handle bar mustache and jet black hair tied back in a pony tail. The man was wearing sandals, a pair of black leather pants he obviously had to give up breathing to get into, and a purple lacy silk shirt unbuttoned to his navel. The man was playing with the lace on the front of his shirt as he eyed Cyrus like a ravaged wolf would a tender piece of steak. The man strutted toward Cyrus swinging his hips from side to side like a pendulum. The man hung out his hand, palm down, limp at the wrist. 'Hi! I'm Aaron, but you can call me............when ever you like!' The man said with arching eyebrows. Cyrus took the hand, squeezing quite a bit harder then was necessary. Aaron recoiled his hand quickly, then cocking his arm back and slapping Cyrus's chest with his hand like a wet noodle. 'Don't be so rough, you big meanie!' Aaron said. Cyrus just stared down at the little man. 'Strong and silent, I like that!' Aaron said as he winked at Cyrus. 'I noticed you looking over my posters, see anything you like? I know I do! I noticed you staring at that one particularly hard' Aaron said pointing at one particular poster. 'I met him once you know. Wow! Now that is an example of what a real man is, his black hair, that tan muscled up body, and those pearly white teeth.' Aaron said as he sucked in air through his teeth. 'I could just eat him up he looks so good! And that accent, have you ever heard a more sexy accent? I don't know where exactly he is from, but if the rest of the men on the island look anything like him, I'll be in heaven.' Aaron said with a longing look in his eyes. 'Oh, I'm sorry, enough about me.' Aaron chuckled. 'What was your name?' Aaron asked. Cyrus considered lieing and telling Aaron a false name, but decided it didn't really matter anyways. 'My name is Cyrus, Cyrus Raynes.' Cyrus said with a sigh. Smitty would die for this one. Aaron gasped. 'Your the one who is going to..' 'Yeah, yeah, that would be me.' Cyrus said. From the change in the mans eyes at the realization of who was standing before him, Cyrus knew that this visit has just taken a turn for the worse. 'Hey look, I don't make the decisions, I just have to carry them out.' Cyrus tried to explain. *Humph* was the only response Cyrus got. 'Hey, man, look, I'm just trying to....' 'Fringe bin!' Aaron yelled out as he turned and strode to the back corner of the shop. Cyrus hung his head, this was not going as he had planned. Smitty would in fact die for this one. Slowly....... Cyrus looked back at the door, if he ran now..... 'Well come on, I can't very well measure you from all the way over there.' Aaron yelled to Cyrus. Cyrus sighed and drudged toward the back of the store. 'Stand here' Aaron pointed a a spot on the floor. Cyrus stood on the spot and Aaron produced a step ladder and a tape measure and began to take Cyrus's measurements. A few minutes later, after measuring Cyrus's inseam for the fourth time Cyrus cleared his throat and glared down at the much smaller man, Aaron realizing his scheme was up decided it was time to finally look for some cloth in Cyrus's size. After a few attempts to get Cyrus to try on a pair of crotchless chaps Aaron finally gave up on that idea. Finally after about three hours Cyrus stood in front of and array of angled mirrors staring at his new cloth. His black boots, Smitty wouldn't be taking those under any circumstance. white leather pants with, yes, fringes on the sides of the legs, and a white spandex t-shirt with gold lettering that read........ 'You know Aaron, once you get right down to it, you don't do half bad work, you know? This really don't look half bad.' Cyrus said, examining himself in the mirrors with his arms outstretched. 'Your right about that, it doesn't look half bad.' Aaron said with his head cocked to the side and his hand on his chin looking Cyrus over, but not his cloth. Cyrus snapped his head toward Aaron who had already began to examine the ceiling and the walls, before Cyrus caught him looking at him. 'So, ummm........when am I going to be able to see you in my work?' Aaron asked. The question sounded wrong, but Cyrus still knew what Aaron meant. 'Couple of weeks, something like that, maybe sooner, maybe later, I'm not quire sure, all depends on when things come together.' Cyrus said with a genuine unknowing shrug. Cyrus turned to face the the costume shop owner. 'So, are you satisfied?' Aaron asked. 'Yeah.' Was Cyrus's simple answer. 'Ok, then, ummm, how do you plan on paying?' Aaron implied. Cyrus produced a credit card from his wallet and handed it to Aaron. 'Ok, Ill be right back.' Aaron said as he walked to the front of the store. Cyrus turned back around and admired his reflection some more. Aaron came back a few minutes later. 'Umm, Cyrus?' Aaron sounded somewhat concerned. 'Yeah?' Cyrus said turning around. 'It appears that your card has been declined, so it looks like you'll have to work for it' Aaron said as he ran a finger down the front of his lip, staring at Cyrus with puppy dog eyes. Cyrus sneered at the the little man, and clenched his fist. 'I'm kidding, I'm kidding. Sheesh, you are so feisty!' Aaron said as he handed Cyrus his card back. Cyrus took it and put it back into his wallet, then stepped in to the dressing room to change back into his street cloth. A few minutes Cyrus emerged and walked out the front entrance of the store, ready to unleash a new kind of hell on FUSE. Per Sey.
