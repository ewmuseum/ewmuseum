A Day In The Life.

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/6 The hotel suite that the Dead Man's Hand currently occupied was, at least to the suite-experienced mind of Reginald V. Lovecraft, quite boring, when it came right down to it. Luxurious? Of course. Staying in such suites meant not dealing with a lot of questions. People who threw money around as if it was about to be abolished were treated with utter deference. Deference was good, especially when one demanded privacy in his or her every day work. That was Lovecraft to a tee. His laptop was set up, the once-politician typing away on it and the Blackberry that sat plugged into the wall right next to it. Having hands in many cookie jars meant not only that one savored many sorts of cookie, but that one also had to refill the jars quite often. The Elder Statesman of the Dead Man's Hand thoughtfully looked over a piece of correspondance, hovering the cursor over the 'send' button when he heard something that sounded an awful lot like a giggle, followed by a soft sigh from the direction of the bathroom. 'Ah,' he said, to himself, 'They're probably enjoying the rather spacious hot tub...' Lovecraft didn't waste too much time thinking about it. Phillip Kennedy was a young man, after all. He had needs, and moreover, sex was an easy, bloodless way to control him. Kathryn, for her part, loved sex as well, so it seemed to him that they were a match made in...well, Heaven probably didn't let Dead Man's Hand members in. The fact that he could pick out Savant as the sigher didn't bother him much either. He didn't know much about her personal life, save that she seemed stressed all to fuck most of the time. Maybe this would do her good? Reginald clicked the 'send' button, even as he pondered exactly what roleplaying a Tyler Rayne-Lindsay Troy-Angelica Brooks threesome consisted of. Probably Rayne threatening to cut himself, the big man mused, without mirth. Though, it certainly *sounded* like a lot more fun than that in there. Another sigh. Followed by a moan, and this time, Lovecraft stood. The fact that he could feel his cock twitch slightly had nothing to do with the fact that he now decided it was time to leave for a while. Nothing at all. It did, at least, remind him that while he was indeed old, he was most certainly not dead. 'I will be heading out for a while to take care of some work that must be done,' Lovecraft announced, in the direction of the lockerroom door. 'Have fun, you three.' 'Oh, we will,' the teasing voice of Kathryn Shaw intoned, followed by a 'good luck, Uncle Reg' from Lauren Fox. Kennedy didn't say anything, but there were many possible reasons for that, that Lovecraft was determined not to think about. Plucking the Blackberry from its plug and slamming it down into his pocket, the former politician let himself out of the luxury suite, and into the hallway of the hotel. 'There.' Reginald took a slow, deep breath, confident that he was calm now. It would be some time before he would be able to interact with one of his usual escorts, and the last thing he needed was to make a snap judgment with some whore who hadn't been bribed and sworn to absolute secrecy. Lovecraft felt a vibration at his hip, and he groaned momentarily before realizing that it was simply the Blackberry. With the grace of a man who did the following dozens of times a day, Reginald pulled the device from his pocket and looked at the screen, finding a simple message there. 'I am downstairs at breakfast. We need to talk. DWS.' DWS, Lovecraft knew, meant David Walter Smith, Phillip Kennedy's agent, and a man of many curiosities that were not easy to satisfy. 'Well', Lovecraft mused, rubbing at day old stubble, 'perhaps a skirmish would do me good.' Affecting the hard stare that had broken down hundreds over the course of a twenty year poltiical career, the one time aide to the Governor of Georgia sauntered towards the elevator, ready for a meeting of the minds. --------------------- Two omelettes sat at the breakfast table, coffee and orange juice beside them. One man's had hash browns, the other breakfast potatoes. One's omelette was ham, bacon, and cheddar cheese, the other was egg whites with broccoli, peppers, onions, mushrooms, and mozzerella. Rarely in any line of work save professional athletics did two three-hundred pound men sit at the same table. Reginald Victor Lovecraft and David Walter Smith were both professional wrestlers, one old but functional, one young but temporarily in a wheelchair. Both also had minds like steel traps. 'I am worried,' the younger man stated, forking a mouthful of omelette. 'From what Katsidy tells me,' Lovecraft shot back, 'you are always worried.' The once and future Logic nodded. 'If you were in my position, Mister Lovecraft, I would hope that you too would be worried. I represent a member of one of the most reviled groups in this sport today. As you might imagine, it is somewhat difficult to get him sponsorship deals. I have my own investment in him, as you are well aware. And with this business with PRIME going on, I feel that my worry is well founded.' Lovecraft took a bite of breakfast, winding up to return the volley. 'And as *you* are well aware, Mr. Smith, Phillip is one of four active in ring members of the Dead Man's Hand; alongside Jadian Bridden, Mayhem, and myself. Desade does not compete on a regular basis. Hunter Sabuani is AWOL. Kennedy is of vital importance to all of us.' Logic followed a batch of breakfast potatoes down with a swig of juice. 'I am well aware of this, yes. Keep in mind, however, that I know nothing of the 'Dead Man's Hand', nor anything of Desade. My suspicions, however, are not especially positive.' 'As well they shouldn't be,' Lovecraft offered back, with a grin. 'We don't wear white hats in the Dead Man's Hand.' 'Certainly not. But my real question is if my client is involved in things with real liability. Though I *do* think that much of your conduct on shows is criminal, the odds of prosecution are slim and none. Mr. Kennedy is an impressionable individual, and I do *NOT* want him rotting in prison.' That garners a chuckle from Lovecraft in response. 'Do you honestly think he'd ever go to prison? You DO understand why Desade employs me, do you not?' Lovecraft digs into his hash browns (sadly not smothered, as they are at the Waffle Houses of his youth), and smiles. 'In case you don't, I'm on the payroll to make sure whoever needs to be bribed is bribed, and whoever needs to be gotten off is gotten off. Desade has many connections. I have connections that she does not...or simply can not, for that matter.' Smith scratches at his chin, sighing softly. 'She's a right proper dictator, isn't she?' 'I wouldn't go that far,' the Elder Statesman replied. 'She is what she is. I don't ask more questions than necessary, and her checks continue to roll in. I cash them, they never bounce, life goes on.' David sighed as he drank his coffee. An impasse, this was. 'I don't suppose I'll get any more out of you, eh? At any rate, may I see Phillip? It has been some time since we spoke in person.' 'The answers to those questions,' Lovecraft began, 'are no and no. The second is because Master Kennedy is currently engaging in...I believe the children nowadays call it a 'threesome.' 'With Katsidy and who,' came David's immediate response. 'I don't see how it's any of your business, Mr. Smith,' Lovecraft offered, with a faint grin on his face. 'Ask him if you're so interested in his love life. For now, though, I've got to prepare for my first wrestling match in a few years. The bill is on me, David.' With that, Lovecraft stood, throwing down several twenties on the table, leaving David alone to finish his omelette. The former Logic took another bite, and sighed to himself. Every member of the Hand he met seemed more guarded and difficult to deal with than the last. ------------- Reginald V. Lovecraft didn't train anymore. Not really. He was kind of old for it. Generally his goal was to stay on his feet and fight as best he could with brawling tactics. Sure, that didn't always work, but working out made him sore, and going into a match sore would be a rather large problem for Lovecraft. He could always go back to his suite, but he was quite afraid that the triumverate there had probably moved from the bathroom to some other section of the suite, and likely weren't wearing very many clothes. So that left the hotel gym, even if he was just sitting on an exercise bike, not using it, and watching other people work out as if he could get in shape by mere osmosis. The Elder Statesman sighed, watching the news coverage of the presidential election. While his bread would be buttered with a McCain presidency...he had to admit that Sarah Palin was not the most inspiring choice of vice-president, in his opinion. 'If McCain dies we're boned,' the Democrat-turned-Republican sighed, under his breath. 'Though we are likely boned as is. I have never seen a political climate quite this bad.' In the end, whether or not it truly mattered was arguable; even in an Obama presidency, corrupt officials on all levels of government would continue to exist. They might or might not be harder to find, of course, but Reginald Lovecraft was nothing if not someone who could ferret out those who'd take some extra cash on the side. Punching in the name 'Hiro Saotome' on his Blackberry, pages of information instantly came to light on the NWC star's wrestling career, style, and match history. Lovecraft allowed himself a tight grin. 'This'll be most interesting,' the once career politician told himself. Saotome was the veteran, even compared to him...but Lovecraft had logged considerable time in the ring himself. Another vibration of his Blackberry alerted him to another incoming text, which he quickly read. 'Decided to play Krissy/Charlotte Ramone/Phillip Kennedy. We might be here a while. - Stack.' With another sigh, Lovecraft decided that it would probably be best to eat lunch and dinner out this evening.
