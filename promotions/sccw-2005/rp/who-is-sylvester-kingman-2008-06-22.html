Who is Sylvester Kingman?

22 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/22/2008 'Right! So 'ere I was, back ta back wi' Jonny Rhine, surrounded on ev'ry side by the flesh eatin', mindless ZOMBIES of the Dead Man's Hand!' Austin King is a professional wrestler, wrestling under the moniker 'The Wombat' for Smitty T. Duluth's SCCW promotion. Truth be told, Austin's wrestling ability was not the main reason he was hired. What got Austin hired was his infectious enthusiasm and boyish grin, which gave him the ability to make even the most outlandish exaggeration seem plausible. 'So I sez ta him 'Rhino,' cuz yannow, that's what his friends get to call him. 'Rhino, things look mighty grim, mate!' And he sez ta me 'Well hey there Austin, not a problem. With you at my back, these varmints will be easy work.' Apparently the Jon Rhine of Austin's world sounds a LOT like John Wayne. But even with Austin's charm and gift of stretching the truth, surely THIS tale of his exploits with newly recrowned SCCW Universal Champion Jon Rhine had to be more than any audience he could possibly be telling it to would believe... Not if the average age of the audience was 6, and the location was Chuck E. Cheese. 'So we wades in togetha' Jonny throwing punches, left, right left, down a zombie would go! Me, I've got.... THE WOMBLASTER!' Austin shook his fist at the table of mostly young girls, before rearing back and throwing a mighty haymaker in the air. 'But just cuz it takes The Wombat only one punch ta knock down a zombie for every three Jonny Rhine throws, don't think I'm better than he is, nosiree! He's FAST, kids, and for every two zombies that fall in front of me, three have dropped in front of him! So we's almost through the zombies, and we can see The Evil Mister Hawke with his Hawk Headed Zombiemaker Staff... The hawk's an Eygyptian symbol of dead things like zombies, right?' 'I thought Egyptians used mummies...' said Miranda Landon, the young girl that this party was for. Austin looked puzzled for the briefest of moments, before continuing. 'Well, right, afta they been out in the desert and they git all dry, then they's mummies! But these was still undried mummies, and they calls them zombies! Ooops, hang on kiddos, The Wombat's got a phone call to take.' As the table of children went back to pizza and talking about how gross zombies were, Miranda turned to her aunt Janet. 'He's pretty awesome, Aunt Janet. How did you get a real pro wrestler to come to my party anyway?' Janet Landon chuckled at her niece. 'Well, Austin came to the restaurant where I work to get some food, and when I got off... It's all kinda complicated, Miranda.' Miranda nodded her head matter of factly. 'He stayed over last night, huh?' Janet blinked a couple of times, 'Why would you say that?' 'Well, Daddy is always saying, 'My kid sister is such a slut, has a different man over every night and fucks him, I bet.'' Miranda made her voice as deep as a six year old could, frowning afterwards in her best 'I am a grown up' way. This time Janet was not chuckling. She forced a smile back onto her face before she continued. 'Well, Miranda honey, first, just because Daddy uses grown up words doesn't mean you get to. And second...' Janet didn't get a chance to continue as suddenly Austin stepped up, kissing her on the cheek. 'Sorry 'bout this, I gotta go!' As the girls at the table all let out a collective 'Awwww,' Janet ask 'Is everything ok?' 'No, by a dog's bollocks, no,' The Wombat replied. 'Me fucking mum's in town.'     There are people who think that simply because of its location, Memphis, Tennessee is a rural town, despite its size. They expect every restaurant to be 'Bubba's Rib Shack,' and every menu to have grits on it. These people have never been to Erling Jensen's. If you were to ask the Dane nicely, he might actually make you 'grits,' but likely it would be served as 'Polenta with Shaved White Truffles and Foie Gras Butter.' Austin stared at his appetizer, Escargot en croute with Mushrooms in Blue Cheese Mornay Sauce, pushing it around with his fork since it actually didn't seem very appetizing at all. 'What's the matter, Sylvester? Why, it's no wonder you're so thin, you aren't eating!' Melina Kingman was one of Australia's top 10 female business owners, and was known not so affectionately by those who had dealings with her as 'The Sydney Shark.' But to Sylvester Austin Kingman, she was still known by the same name he had called her his entire twenty-six years. 'Mummy, they're snails! I don't want ta eat snails.' Melina put on a pained expression. 'Of course not, your mother comes all the way here, gets reservations at one of Memphis' finest restaurants and has them fix you a delicacy, and you don't want it. You don't want to stay around and help your mother with her business after your father dies, you have to run off, to America of all places, and become some sort of stunt clown in the circus. What is a mother to do?' Sylvester ('Call me tha again, an I'll dent yer snout, mate!') popped a snail into his mouth, chewed several times and swallowed. 'Huh. Tha' ain't so bad now. And it's not a circus, Mummy, it's wrestling. Professional wrestling!' 'Circus, wrestling, what does it matter? It's not a job a respectable young man would have! And what's with with 'Austin The Wombat King' nonsense? You're from New Zealand, not Australia! I've never even heard of Humpty Doo!' Austin grinned. 'Right, neither had I, but this Smitty bloke, he's my boss, it was his idea. Said nobody cared about no Kiwis, but 'The Wombat King' and Humpty Doo would put their asses in the seats! I mean, all I know about Humpty Doo is they got a giant paper mache alligator with boxing gloves on outside town!' The Sydney Shark smiled, when he got excited about something, her Sylvester reminded her so much of his father, the late Noah Kingman. 'You really enjoy this don't you? Well, if it makes my Sylves- my Austin happy, it can't be too bad.' Austin smiled back. 'Would you like to come to the show? I can get tickets, maybe take you back stage, introduce you around?' Melina shook her head. 'No, sweetie, the only reason I'm even in Memphis and not already in Los Angeles is I found out Elisabeth Pierotti is here as well, and I've been trying to organize a meeting with her for months now. But that's business and I didn't come here to tell you about business. So Uncle Hyram, you remember him, the one with the wooden leg? He decided he was going to become a platypus breeder, and...' The rest of the evening isn't really important though, Austin got the approval of his mother, discovered haute cuisine really isn't that bad, and still had enough time to make it to Janet's to 'stay over.'
