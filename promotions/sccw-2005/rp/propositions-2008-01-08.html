Propositions

8 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 08 Type into google "Forbes rich list" and see what you find. This is exactly what AJ did on one Tuesday while he was having a week off from competition. His plan, simple. Get the combined wealth of 10 richest men in the world, apart from him of course – and get them all to agree to help him in his crusade. Easy you would think? Well that is what he is thinking at the moment as he is standing at the head of the table looking at all of them, all of which are intently looking at him waiting to see why they have been hounded for the past week by a crazed AJ asking for a meeting, seeing them all in person, considering they come from 8 different countries spanning from India to the USA it is quite an achievement, air miles were clocked, first class was used. It was messy. Anyway, AJ managed to track down the following 10 men and hound them with the lure of more money, cookies, Asian women and in one case a batch of poptarts (don’t ask) all of which eventually gave in and came with AJ, with an extra billion in their pockets which which helped: Bill Gates: Net worth $56b. Microsoft Warren Buffet: $52b. Berkshire Hathaway Carlos Slim Helu: $49b. Telecom Ingvar Kamprad: $33b. Ikea Lakshmi Mittal: $32b. Steel Sheldon Adelson: $26.5b. Casinos Bernard Arnault: $26b. LVMH Amancio Ortega: $24b. Zara Li Ka-shing: $23b. Various David Thomson: $22b. Inheritance All of their fortunes are still dwarved by AJ’s now estimated $500b fortune he luckily managed to get by a combination of winning the lottery and a stock market crash meaning he ended up owning 100% every major company on the stock market, legally, and managed to sell the shares back at 4 times the value. This, somewhat unsurprisingly causes resentment from at least 8 of the people in the room and the real reason they wanted an audience with AJ is to, well, get their money back – but they haven’t told him this yet as they just arrived, and may as well listen to what he has to say – all he kept telling them is, "I have a unique investment opportunity – you wont want to miss it" AJ stood at the door around 5 minutes previous, while his newly appointed assistant showed the men into the conference room. He shook each of their hands with a beaming smile and said "Pleased to meet you, Andre Jensen, richest man on the planet" to which, with gritted teeth replies varied from "charmed" "hello" and in one case "you owe me, big time" So now, grinning like a giant Cheshire cat at his waiting audience he begins his speech, which he had been rehearsing for an entire 5 minutes, so he pretty much has everything worked out which he wants to say. "Hi" He looks expectantly at the men in front of him, all of which are just staring straight back at him, showing nothing. "I am pleased you all came, maybe the fact I gave you all a portion of my wealth persuaded you, maybe you already want to join the cause, I don’t know, I brought you all together in order to show you my grand plan." Stoney silence still follows – 10 billionaires look at AJ with blank expressions. "And, erm, well. That’s about it. Any questions before I unveil the plan?" Bill Gates puts his hands to his temples. "When are we getting our money back?" "Your what sorry?" "Our money?" there are murmurs of agreement "that you have" "Your money?" "Yes, our money, the money we all had to pay you to get our companies back. I still haven’t got 50% of my shares, I couldn’t afford it." Mittal chips in "Yes, me too – my steel companies are now only 30% owned by myself. I demand to know when I get back my companies which you managed to steal from me. AJ smiles again – another beaming smile in the face of questioning. "Relax guys, everything I did is legal, it isn’t my fault that I was the last person who bought a stock before it went bust and for some reason that ended up meaning I owned all companies on it. You all made your money back, so don’t worry." "But the money was never yours." Chirps in Ingvar Kamprad. "Ingy, baby, what is it you do again? Ikea is it?" "Yeah, but……….." "I tell you what, I don’t like flat pack furniture anyway, I will do a deal with you one day possibly, just lets do this meeting first eh?" "But…." "But you came here to Andre Jensen – richest guy on the planet, to invest. Not talk about things like companies and who owns what and where." "Hmmmm." "Hmmm away, but I need to get on with my presentation for sure. I like goooooooooooooold" "What?" "Oh, nothing." AJ sniggers a little bit while Kamprad looks on confused. "Yesh, shexy timesh!" "AJ" "Kampy" "You do realise I am not Dutch, I am Swedish, so the Goldmember thing doesn’t work" "Swedish?" "Yeah" "Oh, not dutch?" "No" "Not even a little dutch?" "No" "Do you like Orange?" "A bit" "Dutch enough then. Anyway gentlemen, enough squabble about who owns who and where all the money is coming from already. Lets talk business." He clicks his fingers and the lights suddenly dim, a plasma screen TV drops in from the wall, emblazoned with the logo of AJ’s new company TLEBN. A video begins with AJ’s smiling face, his jacket slung over his shoulder in the same vein as a senator or politician. The American national anthem plays in a soft subtle tone in the background. He walks over to a park bench while looking to the camera. "America sucks! Hi I’m Andre Jensen, richest guy on the planet wanting to give you all here a very special offer to be a part of the new world order." AJ walks along the path after picking up the lead of a little poodle dog and begins to walk it with him. "Meet little fluffy here, fluffy is fed up of the capitalist dirge too, only yesterday fluffy actually crapped on a picture of George Bush in order to show his protest against the evil regime of America. Fluffy is doing his bit to help save the world. You can too." AJ stops and stares directly into the camera as fluffy runs off to the background, presumably to mount something he saw in the background. "My offer to everyone in this room is simple. If we all combine our funds, we can actually go and buy America for our brothers in the middle east and once and for all be in a position where real change can happen!" AJ beams his customary smile back into the camera and points – reflecting the colonel poster from the American WW2 recruitment drive. He signs off with the tag "Invest, for your children’s sake!" The video ends and the lights go back up, AJ proudly beams to everyone in the room, all of who look at him in complete incredulity. It takes a number of minutes before any of them break the silence. "What….. what was that?" asked Thompson. "Why it is the offer I am presenting you with, you invest in TLEBN, we all buy America, hand it over to the middle east so they can do what they want with it and they pay us a handsome salary for doing so. "So you dragged me all the way from India to ask us all to buy a country with you, one which so happens to be all of our major clients?" claimed Mittal "Well, yeah, except for Thompson over there, he doesn’t work – he got his money from his dad." Was AJ’s response. "This has to be the most stupid idea I have ever witnessed in my life." Stammered Bill Gates. "I mean, the whole idea of buying the States is just, well, unworkable!" "Says the guy who created vista! I mean come on you guys, it’s simple, we go to Bush we say – Hey Georgie man, I know you are gone next year, how you wanna sell up and we can keep you on as honourary chief executive of the new country – if the Arabs want to keep him of course. It’s a sure thing." A couple of people have already stood up and are making their way to the exit, AJ nods quickly and the doors lock shut. "No one leaves until we get full co-operation, you are all here for your skills as well as your money. Bill can create the IT infrastructure, fair enough its windows so it will be shit, but the mac guy wasn’t available. Carlos can create the phone network, Goldmember over there can sell his furniture in the houses that Mittal builds! Then Carlos provides the entertainment, bingo bango – all sorted." The room suddenly develops an air of, oh my god something is going to kick off in a second. All 10 men suddenly look at AJ, some roll up their sleeves, Bill gates straightens his glasses and AJ suddenly feels like getting out of there wouldn’t be such a bad idea. Bill Gates pulls out some sort of contraption from his belt, which looks pretty life threatening while security guys from 10 different billionaires begin to bash the door down. It seems like a distress beacon located in Mittal’s cufflinks alerted them to the fact the meeting is over and they are not being allowed to leave. The 10 men suddenly start to converge on AJ. Slowly backing him into a corner, he looks all around and the panic in his face resembles the panic anyone sees when they know they are just about to get into a fight which they have no chance of winning whatsoever. He takes a deep breath and then lets out a small chuckle as they all lunge at him. Smoke billows up from the smoke bomb AJ had just decided to let off, which he keeps in his pocket for occasions such as these. This confuses the men who are just about to give a billion dollar kicking to AJ enough for him to be able to sneak through the trapdoor he had cunningly put in the wall of the room straight to the outside – looking through the window from the inside all you could see is AJ running straight to the waiting car and diving in the window while the car screeches away. He manages to get himself straight in the car, sitting in the passenger seat while his assistant drives like a maniac around the roads. He dusts himself off. "That went better than I expected" He says with a small laugh.
