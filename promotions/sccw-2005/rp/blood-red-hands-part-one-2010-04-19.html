Blood Red Hands (Part One)

19 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th April 2010 The past few months had been a mixed bag for Suzanne Larsson. She had recorded her first ever PPV victory at her first attempt, seeing off the nefarious James von Doom at Defiance III; she had suddenly lost contact with the man who got her the SCCW gig in the first place, and now she was being plagued by dreams. The same one, over and over again. The first thing she would notice, each and every time, was that everything started to become blurred, as if she was developing myopia. Then, as soon as she was getting confused, stumbling around unable to make eye contact with anyone, a strange shade of red would engulf everything. It was at this time that terror started to grip her, as she knew with the kind of certainty you only get in dreams that there was someone out to get her, and there was nothing she could do about it. No matter where she had previously been in the dream, be it an old house or a surreal landscape, she was now in a small room. She could sense that the walls were near but she couldn’t see them, and the person, the threat to her that she could not raise her head to face, it was nearby. Then it was above her. And then, all she could see was a pair of hands. A pair of hands coming for her throat, covered in blood, filling her vision and starting to choke the life out of her. It was always with a gasp that she awoke, her lungs on fire as if someone had been holding a pillow over her head, smothering her. It was at times like these when she would have liked to speak to the man who knew so much about her, the man who understood her Gift and her life, but he had disappeared, and was not returning her calls. The other option, the one that filled her mouth with bile was contacting the Icelandic Council, but that was something she would not yet do. Though there was nothing concrete to tie them to her husband’s death, she still harboured deep suspicions that they, too, were trying to manipulate her. So it was with relief that on the eve of her trip to Iraq, she woke in the middle of the night, sitting bolt upright not with her throat dry and gasping for air, but to the sound of her cellphone taking a call from an unknown number. She reached for it quickly, and as she held it to her ear, the voice she had now come to recognise spoke to her, and as usual he was one step ahead of The Valkyrie. "So, you’ve been having dreams, haven’t you?" *** From the window of the private single propeller plane that Magnus Larsson had ‘borrowed’, the view of the volcano was magnificent. Eyjafjallajökull glacier had broken and was spewing forth thousands of tons of carbon dioxide on a daily basis. The majority of flights across Europe had been grounded due to the risk of engines failing due to the intake of ash, but if you knew the right people and had the ability, or perhaps the Gift of coercion, it was possible. Despite the environmental factors, Magnus still had a clear reception for his phone, and knew that if he wanted to use it, he’d have to do so before he was clear over the ocean and headed towards the United Kingdom. By the time he landed and had tied up a few loose ends, his daughter would be boarding her own plane headed for Iraq. He needed to set some things in motion, ensuring that she knew what was ahead of her. It was late in the States, but Suzanne hadn’t been sleeping well recently, and the sound of her phone going off would be like a clarion call to her. It was picked up on the third ring. "So, you’ve been having dreams, haven’t you?" said Magnus. "How did you know?" replied Suzanne. "It’s my business to know, Ms Larsson. I have invested time and money into you, and I wouldn’t want to see that all go to waste." "Sure, whatever. Why are you calling, and what do you know about my dreams?" "Always to the point, my little Valkyrie. I called you to give you a warning." "After, what, six weeks of silence, now you call? I needed you." "You have done well for yourself without my help." There was a pause on the line, and Magnus had observed his daughter for long enough to know that she was shrugging her broad, muscular shoulders. "So, the warning?" she asked. "Yes. You know the Icelandic Council, of course, and the interest they take in you?" "Of course." "Well, It has come to my attention that both Gunnar Arnisson and Lars Halldorsson have been granted clearance to be in a particular country right at this very moment. Can you guess which one?" "Iraq." "Correct." "What are they planning?" "I do not know," said Magnus. "All I know is that have been looking for the right moment to strike. They cannot be sure how strong you have become. They will have worked out that you are being trained, being tutored by..." "By someone who I know almost nothing about and someone I haven’t seen for a month and a half! Nice work tutoring me!" interrupted the Valkyrie. "By someone familiar with their work," said her father, carrying on as if she hadn’t said a word. "So all I can say is be careful, and watch out for yourself. See if your Gift can help you, if it can give you any visions of danger." "I’ve tried, but all I see are hands. Blood red hands trying to choke me." "Then you’re scared of something, or of someone." "Who? Wyatt Connors? I’m not scared by him or anyone in SCCW... and before you jump in about Iraq, we’ll be in a military base the whole time. There’s practically no danger at all." "Oh no, Suzanne. Your fear is not about your career, or your location, it’s about finding out the truth, and coming face to face with your enemy." "The Council?" asked Suzanne. "It may be, but that remains to be seen. Have a good trip, but be careful. They may appear weak, but the Council have hands that could right now be closing in on you..." With that image left in Suzanne’s mind, he hung up the phone and returned to admiring the volcano. Setting his daughter up to be in a state of watchfulness bordering on paranoia had taken some time, some hidden cameras and a sizeable bribe or two, but as he looked over again at the smoke billowing into the air, he smiled. Sometimes you just had to play the long game. And compared to exacerbating the volcanic eruption to cause this level of disruption, it had been a walk in the park. *** The military camp was surprisingly like the television shows had made out. Given how many journalists were embedded within the army these days, though, it should perhaps have not come as so much of a shock. There was, perhaps, less tension in the air than depicted in Hollywood, but the dustiness, the general hubbub and the barracks all rang true to both news reports and war movies. The first day of acclimatisation passed without great fanfare or event. She was shown what to do in case of a mortar attack, armed assault or even a chemical attack. In all three instances the idea seemed to be, ‘Get The Shit Out Of The Way’, with perhaps a gas mask added in for fun in some instances. With the jet lag overcome and a thousand shirts signed (Suzanne proving unexpectedly popular amongst the military), Suzanne took the walk back to where she was bunking with several of the other female members of SCCW. It was not something she enjoyed, but as long as they paid her, she was willing to accept it. The night was surprisingly cool, but it was a welcome break from the day, which had been painfully hot. For all her time spent in Texas, her genetics continually reminded her that her roots belonged to Iceland. And then she saw him. There he was, walking towards her, as boldly as if he was the commanding officer. Even though Suzanne knew that the Icelandic Council were trying to meet with her, she had never thought that it would be so boldly. Even at night the perimeter was lit up like a football field, and anyone could see the barrel-chested, bearded man calmly striding up to her. "You have no right to be here," said The Valkyrie. "Be that as it may," replied Gunnar Arnisson. "I am here. May we talk?" "I have nothing to say to you." "We had expected that you would say that. You have been avoiding us, Suzanne Magnusdottir." "Stop calling me that. I don’t have the time for your stupid naming tricks. I was raised as Suzanne Larsson. That’s my name, no matter what you want to pretend." "I meant no harm by it," said Gunnar, raising his hands defensively. "It is what we always knew you as." "So you tell me." Gunnar brought his thick, weather-worn hands up to his lips, pausing for a moment before he spoke. "We think you are being fed misinformation." "We?" said Larsson. "This is the first time I’ve seen you without your little buddy. Where is Lars? Was he scared of coming to Iraq?" "Not at all," said Gunnar, his nostrils flaring as Larsson dared to cast doubt on his best friend. "He was unable to be here because of the volcano. There’s been a blanket ban on all flights for days now." "Strange he didn’t foresee that. He must be getting old." "Oh, he knew that something was happening, just as he knows that someone is speaking to you about us, feeding you poison and lies. Don’t trust them." "Strange," said Suzanne, with a wry smile. "He said much the same about you." "So, there is someone. A man..." "Look," said the Valkyrie, her demeanour instantly stiffening into an aggressive posture. "You can get the fuck out of here if all you want to do is try to trick me. I don’t trust you and I never will. I can’t prove it, but I know you were responsible for my husband’s death." "I assure you we were not. You must trust me." "It doesn’t work on me, Gunnar! You can’t Coerce me! I know you’re used to sweet talking everyone into walking over broken glass for you, but it doesn’t work on the Gifted. You know that." "Old habits die hard," said the bear of a man. Suzanne was getting more and more frustrated, and walked right up to stand toe to toe with Arnisson. In some ways it was almost as if there was a strange kind of mirror held between the two. He had a few inches, a large beard and a few pounds on her, but the stoic set to their bodies, the steadfast determination in their eyes, the muscles bulging; they were more like an anachronistic set of twins than Suzanne and Charlie. "You didn’t come here to try and Coerce me. You know better than that." "That much is true. You won’t like what I have to say, though." "Say it." "You need to be careful. Do not be overconfident that you have the better of people that you don’t know enough about. You stand to lose a lot more than you know." "And what’s that supposed to mean? Are you talking about my career or making a threat?" "This is no threat, Suzanne. Be careful, or you will get yourself killed." Gunnar Arnisson barely had time to blink before the Valkyrie uncoiled herself like a spring, all the tension in her body turned into a headlong assault, furious and relentless. Her fists moved as if powered by pneumatics, pumping down and down into Gunnar’s face as she tackled him forcefully to the ground. "You will never" ... THUMP... "ever" ... THUMP ... "threaten me again!" Arnisson was helpless against the sheer overwhelming power of Suzanne’s attack. In under ten seconds he was barely conscious, lying on his back, pinned to the floor by the muscular frame of the woman he had risked so much to come and see. Standing up, Suzanne’s mood started to abate. She could not believe that these people, the ones who murdered her husband would follow her and threaten her in the middle of an American military installation. "Guards! Help!" she yelled, unsure if that was really the right kind of terminology. Either way, it worked, and within seconds there were uniforms surrounding her. "This man," she said. "I don’t know how he got in here. He said he was smuggled in for something. I had to attack him before he did anything else." "Shit. Someone get the CO. Let’s make sure he doesn’t get up." The Valkyrie backed off, dropping down to sit on the dusty ground, watching as the soldiers scurried around, and Arnisson was led off for some fate that she didn’t want to think about. And as Suzanne Larsson looked down at her hands, it was almost like she was back in the dream. The colours weren’t quite right, and she could still see, but only now did she realise that the hands in the dream, the hands trying to choke her to death... ... they were her own.
