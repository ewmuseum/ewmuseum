The Demon Vassago: The Prologue

28 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/28/08 The Introduction Present Day – 2 Weeks Ago One stone step led to another as young Spencer Truman made his way down the spiraling stairs. Spencer was a first year journalism student out of Brown University with aspirations to one day be the most renown reporter in the entire world. Spencer was tall, long armed and long legged with a lean body. He had short brown hair parted on the left side of his forehead, leaving a small feathery portion to frame the side of his face, and hide his bland brown eyes. Ten years ago Spencer’s idol, investigative reporter Jasper James made his career on a series of articles related to a man named ‘Vassago’. It was only recently that Spencer had gotten the opportunity to interview the very man that made his idol’s career. Following the wall down the stairs, the scent of decay and mildew assaulted his nostrils forcing Spencer to repress his vomit. At the end of the stairwell a soft amber glow led him toward a stone chamber. One fat candle sat on a plate in the center of a dusty old card table; two chairs surrounded the table directly across from one another. The chamber held one hole in the stone for a window; about eight inches from the ceiling, and outside Spencer could see the moon bright and full. "I see you found the place okay." Spencer nearly leapt out of his skin. Slowly he turned and found himself facing the six foot nine inch beast. Beads of sweat formed across his forehead, never in any of Jasper’s writings had he truly understood what an intimidating being this man truly was. "Mist-Mist-Mister Vassago?" Spencer asked, stuttering and stumbling over his words. The man smiled, displaying his porcelain white teeth. "Just Vassago will do fine." His deep blue eyes felt piercing, and Spencer was becoming ever more uncomfortable. Vassago took a seat in the chair closest to the door, indicating that Spencer should take the other. Spencer nodded taking his seat. From his wool coat he withdrew a small silver tape recorder. Spencer hit the record button, and placed it in the center of the table, the single red light added to the illumination of the very dimly lit room. "This is Spencer Truman, interviewing the soon to be professional wrestler Vassago, the date is Monday January 28, 2008." Truman came into this with a whole sheet of questions outlined in his mind, but after laying eyes on this mammoth man all of the questions had faded from his mind. "Do you have any questions, or are you just going to sit here?" Vassago asked, his thickly accented voice sending chills down Spencer’s spine. "Uh, ye- yes sir." Spencer replied, his cage rattled. If he was ever going to have a successful career in journalism he was going to need to get over these factors of intimidation. Sucking it up, he began his interview. "In your sessions with Jasper, you never got into your early life, so my first question to you is, where were you born and how old are you?" "That doesn’t matter Mr. Truman." Vassago replied plainly. "Next question please." "O-okay. Um, what brings you into the wrestling business at this point in your life?" Spencer asked shakily. A thin smile crossed Vassago’s lips, "now that is a question worth answering," he replied. "All my life I’ve strived on the pain of others, to me causing others pain is better then bedding any woman. I’ve come into the wrestling business to cause pain, to hurt people Spencer. All I want to do is hurt people, and there is no better business to do that in then the wrestling business." Spencer nodded, "What would you like people to know about you?" "Nothing." His simple one word response sent chills down Spencer’s spine for the third time during the course of this meeting. Spencer was at a loss. He never imagined that an interview could be this difficult and began to question why he had taken up this man’s offer. "Why exactly did you ask me for this session, it seems you don’t have a lot to say." Unlike previously Vassago did not smile thinly this time, his mouth widened and a look of near ecstasy came to him. "I was hoping you’d ask that question." "Oh?" "Yes, and I’m glad you did." Vassago replied. "You see I’ve never found another man I’ve ever held any respect for, until I met Jasper over ten years ago. The odd part is, when I first met him my plan was to kill him." "Kill him?" Spencer asked, nervously. "Yes." Vassago responded. "But, during our first session there was something about Jasper that struck me differently. He was a man looking for death, and I was man looking to deliver it, yet death never came to him." Vassago paused for a moment before adding, "at least not at my hands." Spencer wasn’t sure if he liked where this was going, but found it impossible to turn his attention away. Vassago continued, "you see Jasper had a family at one time, but when his wife and daughter were killed during a break in all the happiness he once found in the world was drained away, he was left a shell of a man. There would have been no satisfaction in me killing him, so I let him live." "So, you tracked me down to tell me why you let Jasper live?" Spencer asked, nerves evident in his voice. "That is one of the reasons, yes." Vassago replied. "What are the other reasons?" Vassago rose from his chair. "To claim what was denied me ten years ago." As he said this he pulled a serrated blade from out of the small of his back. Slowly he approached the young would be reporter. Spencer didn’t know if Vassago was kidding or not, but he would soon find out. Soon Vassago would ‘claim’ what was denied him, and soon Spencer Truman would be nothing but a memory. Very soon.
