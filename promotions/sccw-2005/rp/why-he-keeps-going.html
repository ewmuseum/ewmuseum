Why He Keeps Going

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/19 - 7/4 June 19, 2008 "For the last time, we’re not doing this." "Jon, stop fighting it, we’re already in the parking lot." The day after Jonathan Rhine was viciously attacked by Xavier Kannon on PRIME’s ReVolution 163, Jonathan sits in Katie’s Kia, looking out at the large white hospital building in order to avoid his girlfriend’s gaze. His elbow is firmly on the lock button, and he’s frowning. "Jon—" she tries again before getting cut off. "No," the newly crowned SCCW Universal Champion Jonathan Rhine growls, still refusing to look at her. "I was at the hospital all night. We’re finally back in Indianapolis. I just want to rest and think of a plan to get back at Kannon." "You can rest here," Katie sighs, placing a hand on his arm. "I know you hate hospitals, and I know you’re too proud to admit you need help. But something terrible happened to you last night. We can’t just ignore that because you’re a wrestler and that makes you too macho to accept what happened." "I know what happened, Katie. I’m a fucking pirate." Katie’s expression softens as she runs her hand through his hair. "I know, baby. And I feel so terrible about it." This finally gets him to turn around and face her, his anger gone. "You know it wasn’t your fault." "It was my—" "Your heel, I know. But you didn’t use it. Listen, Katie," he says, grabbing her hand. "I know you want what’s best for me, and you feel responsible, and you can’t imagine why I’m not showing more signs of weakness. But it’s because I’ve become too trained by this game. I can’t let the bad guys know that they hurt me, because then they win. It’s all about the poker face." She nods, looking down for a moment before returning his gaze with a hopeful smile. "I know. But will you at least bring that poker face into the hospital? I trust your doctor here more than I would someone from a random emergency room in Cincinnati. I just want to make sure everything’s all right." After a long pause, Rhine finally nods, then opens his door. "Yeah, all right. Let’s go." After everything that happened to you in PRIME, it’s easy to wonder why you even showed up in the first place. Yes, it was your hometown. Yes, you had a show with SCCW two days later. But PRIME and SCCW have had similar schedules before. Why even show your face in a place where most people don’t even know you exist? It felt good, though, that first show. When you interrupted the spot of a PCW legend and got the crowd behind you in a new venue, it made you feel special, important. It made you feel wanted. And that makes you remember why you showed up. Why you started down the path that led to your injury. You went to ReVolution because you weren’t happy with what was going on in SCCW. Not just the fact that you had been cheated out of your title, or that STD was ignoring your appeals for an overturn. It was because, simply, you were tired of being fucked with. You didn’t want to be the guy who attracted the big attention just because he didn’t like to hit people with chairs or concoct long and laborious plans just to hurt someone. You just wanted to go under the radar for a little while. It didn’t exactly work. "Well, it’s not infected. I guess that’s the good news." Jonathan’s doctor, Dr. Armstrong, removes his light from Jonathan’s socket and gestures to the nurse, who begins applying new gauze to his wound. Jonathan makes this slightly difficult as he tilts his head, looking at the doctor. "And what’s the bad news?" Dr. Armstrong chuckles at the New Life. "The bad news, son, is that you lost an eyeball. I sort of thought that was obvious." Jonathan does not share in the humor of the situation. "So what should I do?" The doctor writes something in the file. "Well, I want to keep you for a few hours, just to make sure that the antibiotics I applied to the wound are working effectively. There’s a chance that some dirt or something else I missed could have been left behind in there, and the infection could still spring up in a few hours. So maybe just stay in the hospital for, say, three or four more hours." Jonathan scowls at Katie, who blushes and looks down. "All right, doc. I guess I’ll just lay like a board for a few hours then, pretending to sleep. Good night." "You don’t have to be bed-ridden for these few hours, Jon. Other than that empty hole, you’re completely healthy, so sitting in bed isn’t going to help you any. You’re free to walk around the hospital or go to the library to read a book…or, if you were interested, there’s another wrestler in the hospital who works for your company. He was sent here last week after he sustained a lot of heavy throat and face injuries." Jonathan already knows who it is, as he closes his eye and shakes his head, but Katie does not. "Do you remember who it was, Dr. Armstrong?" "Yes, his name is Tavon Bishop. Perhaps you want to visit him, make sure he’s holding up all right." Katie grimaces. "Ugh, no way. That guy isn’t exactly one of our friends. He even tried—" Jonathan looks up suddenly. "I’ll pay him a little visit. What room is he in?" You guess that it’s your fault, really. You were the one who baited Kannon, made him focus all of his ire on you. And you thought that since you’ve brushed with Desade and came out mildly unscathed that some Scientologist blowhard wouldn’t be much of a problem. Again, wrong. And now, as you head to the room of someone else who was badly hurt by evil forces in the wrestling world, you wonder if you’re really up to continuing to subject yourself to this torture. Every time you reach up to scratch an eye that isn’t there, the same two questions keep coming to the surface. Why does this always happen to you? And how much more can you take? You’re not really sure of the answer to the first question yet, but the answer to the second one is becoming more and more clear, and it frightens you. If you have to get out of this hospital and fight Xavier Kannon, which is your ultimate plan, and then take on Amy Campbell when she comes after your belt again, which is your other plan, the target on your head certainly won’t diminish. And that’s why it always happens to you, you realize. Because you don’t give in. You’ve always been praised and rewarded for your vitality and determination. The fact that you fight for what’s right and don’t give up in the face of danger is one of the things that you feel defines you as both a wrestler and a person. But now, it’s cost you. And you think that as long as you keep fighting, it may happen again. So that brings you once again back to the second question. Can you take much more of this? "Why are you even going in there, Jon? This man tried to end your career." Jonathan and Katie had found out about Desade’s plan later because Shweta had (on Desade’s orders) told Foster about the plan. Jonathan shakes his head as they stop in front of the room Bishop is supposed to be in. "Yeah, he did. But then he got attacked by the same people I’m fighting. He didn’t deserve that, no matter how bad he is. So I’m just going to see how he’s doing." "Fine, but I’m not going in there. He gives me the creeps." Shuddering, Katie kisses her boyfriend on the cheek and walks down the hallway. Sighing, Jonathan opens the door and slowly walks in. Connected to a few tubes, the sleeping body of Rashaun Tavon Bishop lies awkwardly, his feet sticking out over the end of the bed. Jonathan closes the door softly and steps into the room. The sound of his steps causes Bishop to open his eyes. A bit of a scowl reaches his lips as he identifies his visitor. "What are you doing here, punk?" His voice is deeper than usual, strained. Jonathan points to his bandaged eye and shrugs. "I’m a patient, not a visitor." "Ha," says Bishop, chuckling and trying to sit up. "So the Queen Bitch got to you too, huh?" "No, no, this was the result of a side project I’m running. So," he says, looking at the machines connected to the SCCW monster, "how you holding up?" "I’ll be fine. Doctors say my throat will eventually get better, but I won’t be singin’ any time soon." Jonathan nods. "And then it’s back in the ring?" Bishop scoffs. "Fuck no! After this it’s back to Mississippi. Shit, I’ll probably coach high school football, or snatch purses, or something else to get cash. I don’t need to make a fool of myself again." "Really?" Jonathan shakes his head, then sits down in the chair near the window. "You don’t love the game enough to come back and stick it out? To show the people that Desade won’t end your career?" "Listen, Rhine," the big man says, chuckling humorlessly. "I like fighting. More than that, I like punking out little bitches. And I had a few good times in Sin City, for sure – I ain’t gotta tell you that Sarah Riley fucks like it’s her job." Jon frowns. "It used to be." "No shit." Bishop laughs, then shrugs and winces at the motion. "But anyway, none of that matters after you get your throat impaled on steel. I was just doin’ something that I sort of enjoy. And if that gets me injured because I wasn’t able to do something for some redhead who thinks she’s Corleone with tits? Fuck that, I don’t need this. I don’t care enough to keep putting my hand on that bitch’s stovetop." Jonathan nods slowly, then stands up and walks towards the man who had been ordered to take him out. Holding up a fist, he waits for Bishop to pound it. He doesn’t. "What you tryin’ to be buddy-buddy with me for? I tried to take you out, remember?" Jonathan smiles. "Yeah, but you didn’t. Get better, Tavon. And when you start to miss the game, give me a call and I’ll see about getting Smitty to give you another chance." "You are one delusional white boy, Rhine. But sure, I’ll hit you up if it happens." And now Bishop raises his fist and pounds Jonathan lightly. The New Life nods, then walks out of the door, the smile still on his face. 7/4/08 After your conversation with Tavon, and after how the next few weeks played out, you changed your answer. Yes, you can keep taking what they give out. And it’s because of the big difference between you and the man hospitalized for failing to hurt you. You care. It’s what makes you oppose the Alexandra Pierces, the Amy Campbells, the Xavier Kannons of the world. Because you care too much for the sport, for the fans, and for yourself to let something you don’t believe in sully it. While Bishop is going to crawl away after getting knocked back down, you spring yourself back up every time because you just can’t see yourself ever giving up. And losing an eye won’t change that. You’re still the SCCW Champion. You can still fight, as your match with Kannon showed. And you still love the game. You’re still enjoying what you do, even though it caused you so much pain. You still care. Smitty Duluth has been injured, and it seems that Desade and company are more serious than before about getting revenge. Your streak of escaping her clutches might come to an end soon. In PRIME, Xavier Kannon is sure to be furious after ReV 164, and if he took out an eye for ruining his celebration party, who knows what he’ll do to in reaction to a loss. Not to mention Lance Marshall is your next title defense, and you know from your trainer’s stories that Marshall is one of the best wrestlers around. You care enough to fight back, and that means the hits are going to keep coming. But you also care enough to take them and pop up, waiting for more. And you know that if you keep doing this, eventually the hits will stop, so you can’t stop. And you won’t, no matter what happens next.
