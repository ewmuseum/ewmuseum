Learning from your mistakes

17 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 17 There comes a point when you have to look in the mirror. When you stare at the person reflected you have to take a long hard look at your life and ask yourself several questions. 1: Do I accept who I am. 2: Have I done everything I can. 3: What can I do to make things better. Some people are perfectly happy to be who they are. It doesn’t bother them that they take the easy way out. People can do horrific things in the name of greed or pride or even a higher calling quoting various scriptures and gods. Anyone can have a motivation. Anyone can have goals. Do you let that motivation and goal drive you or do you use it consume your soul. The Alliance accept who they are because they work hard for everything they get. At the end of the day they can say they have earned everything they have gotten. They may be the most ruthless and driven men on the FUSE roster but they have a work ethic. They will not take a short cut simply to win. They know that if you don’t earn something it means nothing. Some people think because they are handsome they should be treated better and the world at their feet. There are people that merely having breasts that people will think you are special and can get whatever you want by batting your eyes and showing cleavage. These "pretty" people are offensive to men like Gideon and Neil. The Billion dollar babies with no sense of respect or responsibility annoy them. "Wah I can’t go to jail it is unfair they don’t have Perrier water and cell phones there. My hairdresser isn’t allowed to make visits. That is like so wrong and I can’t live like that because it’s cruel." Tell Gideon a story about life not being fair when he had internal injuries by taking a full on shin kick to the stomach at age 6. The Alliance work hard on their conditioning. They train constantly to keep in top shape and no matter who they face they train to be better then them in the ring. Some people take short cuts but the Alliance work hard because that is their work ethic and they accept nothing less. While people bitch and complain about not getting what they want the Alliance plot and plan. They have patience. They ask what can I do to make things better with no idea of why they aren’t happy with life. The Alliance looks at the problem and slowly breaks it down. They lost the tag titles. They didn’t cry they worked to get them back in the 3 team cluster fuck match. When that failed they waited until the next opportunity and took the titles back. That is "Learning from your mistakes" ########## ########## ########## ########## At Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## Neil and Stephanie are watching TV on the couch while Ricky is at Gideon's training. There is nothing on TV at this time so Neil turns off the TV. Neil: Did you ever think that we'd be watching TV while Ricky is training with Gideon? Stephanie: No, but this kinda nice. She chuckled as she nuzzled up close to Neil. Stephanie: Wanna talk about anything? Neil: Yeah. Didn't feel like training today. No idea what the hell is up this week. We faced the Cowboys from Hell 3 times already and they still haven't gotten serious yet. I nearly killed Wyatt with the Fatal Fracas. Then we beat them twice after that. Stephanie raised a brow. Steph: Well you don’t have to tell me why you don’t feel like training. That’s crap that you’ve already had to face them three times. What happened to variety? Neil: You know what the tag divisions are like. We beat Aimz and Crisis so they are going to have a match against the Affliction. The thing that pisses me off is the Cowboys are lazy. They have enough ability that if they ever decided to actually work they might eventually get the titles. Instead they coast by and look for the easy path. Hell they jumped Amy because they thought that jumping her would make them top contenders for the tag titles. Amy kicked their asses next week then it was Aimz and Crisis VS Alliance for the tag titles again. Steph: I’m afraid a lot of ‘em are that way, babe. Everyone wants the easy way out. No one wants to put in actual effort for anything anymore. She shook her head. Steph: I’ll tell ya, the world’s going to hell in a hand basket, and it began with those lazy assholes. Neil: It isn't like it is hard to understand. You work hard and put the time in and you get ahead. Steph: Yeah, but no one really cares anymore. It's sad, really... Neil: They think because they are hardcore they are over. Hell anyone can swing a chair or take a chair shot. How many idiots have we seen in the bars that think they were something special because they were 'Bad Asses'? Steph: Oh, I can't tell ya. And we laid out practically every one of 'em too, missing a few here and there 'cause the chicken shits ran away before we could even reach 'em. Neil: It takes heart. When we fought Aimz and Crisis they fought tooth and nail for the titles. There was no doubt they wanted them and they fought like hell to keep them from us and then fought even harder to get them back. Then once they got the titles Gideon and I worked our asses off to get them again. That is the way it should be. Steph: I know, but you can't tell the Cowboys any different. Once a dumb ass, always a dumb ass. Neil: Yup. Do you think they will ever get it? Steph: Doubt it. They're rednecks. Neil: We should probably go get Ricky. Steph: It can't be that late. What time is it? Neil: 4 o'clock. Until Ricky starts getting the basics down Gideon doesn't want to train him too long. Steph: Do you think they're really training? I bet ya they went twenty minutes before Ricky talked Gideon into letting him play video games or something. Neil: I don't think so. Ricky idolizes Gideon and respects him. I think the hard part is Gideon figuring out how to train him. Gideon's early years weren't exactly pleasant. Steph: Yeah, and Ricky's only five. She sighed. Steph: I dunno, Neil. I’m still a little freaked about this. I mean, I’m all for training him, but at so young? I guess it’s just the mom in me worrying about him. Neil: Me too. But Gideon knows how dangerous the martial arts are. Neil and Stephanie head to the garage. It is a 20 minute drive to pick up Ricky and things just don't seem the same without him around the house. Steph: You know, 'dangerous' wouldn't be the word I'd use while trying to reassure someone, Neil. Stephanie got into the car and fastened her seatbelt. Neil: If we didn't trust Gideon he wouldn't be training Ricky. Steph: Yeah, I know. But when I thinking about how much Ricky has grown, I still can't believe that he's old enough to even consider martial arts training. I still remember the day he was born and how tiny he was then. Just this wiggly little thing in my arms... Neil: Yeah......... Scares me a lot. Stephanie looked over at her husband and smiled gently. Steph: Me too, babe. And I miss it. I mean, the time when he was little. Sometimes I think about......Neil, do you ever wish that we could have another one? Neil: I barely survived Ricky being born. I don't think I'm lucky enough to live through a second kid. Steph: Eh, I've mellowed out a lot since then. Hell, I made into my thirties. That's gotta say something. Neil: So have I or I'd have killed Caleb by now. Steph: There you go. And I know it’s silly to think about this. It was a fluke that we got Ricky. Still though… She trailed off as she looked out the window. Steph: It’d be kinda cool to have a little girl. Neil: Could always call Kathy Lee. I think she sells them on E-Bay. Stephanie gave him a look. Steph: I’m serious, Neil. Call me crazy but I would like to have a daughter. She shook her head and sighed; frustration was clearly etched in her features. And the funny thing was, she didn’t even know why she was frustrated. Steph: But it’s stupid to even wonder about it. It likely won’t happen, so I should be grateful for what I have. Neil: Who knows. We had Ricky so maybe someday we will have a daughter. Steph: Yeah, I dunno... They continue on their drive to Gideon's and think of what the future could hold………… ########## ########## ########## ########## At Gideon’s Mansion in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## As Gideon is training Ricky he is watching him kick a heavy bag but on the 17th kick Ricky plants his foot wrong and twists his ankle and falls down crying. Gideon goes over to Ricky with a towel and wipes the tears from his eyes as he looks at his tiny ankle. Gideon knows he just twisted it and has an arm around him as he sits next to him as Ricky is calming down. Gideon: You are going to be all right. I’ll get some tape and some ice for your ankle. After running to the kitchen Gideon comes back with the tape and the ice and Ricky is sniffling. Gideon: You feeling better Ricky? Ricky nodded and wiped his face with his arm. Ricky: I think so… Gideon: Want to hear a story? Looking up at Gideon with is brown eyes, the boy nodded again. Gideon: When I was your age I was hurt real bad when I was training. Ricky: You were? What happened, Uncle Gideon? Gideon: There were older students and they were bullies so they took advantage of me and hurt me. Ricky: Did you have to go to the hospital? Gideon: Yup and I had a hard time breathing because they knew how to hurt me. Ricky: How did they know? Gideon: They were training for 3 years and they learned how to kick and where to kick. They didn't like me and they wanted to hurt me because I was new. I was in a different country in a hospital and hurting really bad. I was only 6 pretty much the same age you are. Ricky: What did you do, Uncle Gideon? Gideon: I trained hard. I was focused on getting even with them for picking on me because I wanted to hurt them back. Ricky: Oh...Mom says I shouldn't fight because of that. I should only fight to protect myself. Gideon: That is why I'm teaching you. I was driven to hurt people that hurt me. We never want you to be like we were. There will be times you will have to fight but we want to make sure you are better able to protect yourself then hurt the other guy. The little boy nodded quietly. He looked as though he was thinking it over. Ricky: Uncle Gideon? Can I watch TV? Gideon: Yup. Gotta tell you something kiddo. The more you train the less chance there is of you hurting yourself. Come on I'll carry you in the living room. Gideon picks up Ricky in his arms and carries him to the living room and puts the ice back on his ankle as they watch TV. A little while later, Stephanie and Neil showed up at the door. Steph smiled as she walked into the house (she was yet to see Ricky's ankle, so her greeting was cheerful). Steph: Hey fellas. Done with training? Ricky smiled happily. Ricky: Hi Mom! Uncle Gideon showed me some neato stuff. Gideon: Hello. Steph: Hi Gideon. He did, huh? Like what? As Stephaine came around the couch, she saw Ricky's ankle. Steph: Oh, honey... Instantly, her gaze turned on Gideon. Gideon: He twisted his ankle. I wrapped it up and put some ice on it. Ricky: I'm all right, Mom. It doesn't hurt anymore. Ricky insisted this as his mom sat down on the couch and began fussing over his ankle. Steph: But how did this happen? Gideon, what did you show him? Gideon: Some routine shin kicks. They were basically to teach form and learning how to keep his balance on one leg. He was going too fast and planted his foot wrong. Neil: Are you sure you know what you are doing? Steph: Yeah, are you sure you're not teaching him this stuff too soon? Maybe five is a little too young yet... Gideon: If I wasn't sure he was ready or that I was able to teach him I wouldn't risk him getting hurt. When I was taping Ricky's ankle I told him about when I was young and the difference between being able to fight and being able to defend yourself. Ricky: Uncle Gideon told me the same thing you and dad told me. Fighting to hurt a person isn't good. Steph: That's right, Ricky. Neil: So how do you like your training? Ricky: It's fun. Can I do it some more? Steph: I don't know, Ricky.... Ricky: Please, Mom? Uncle Gideon says if I do it more, I won't get hurt as much. Steph: I know, but honey...I'm not sure if you're old enough for this yet. Gideon was six when he started training. Maybe we should wait another year. She looked at her husband. Steph: What do you think, Neil? Neil: I think he is old enough to learn but I think he better learn more on conditioning so he isn't as likely to hurt himself. Ricky still has a long way to go before he can start trying strikes. Gideon: Yeah better strengthen up the ole ankles so they don't hurt as much. Steph: What do you have to do for conditioning? Gideon: Pretty much Yoga. Steph: Yoga isn't bad. Ricky: So can I do it, Mom? Please? Steph: Yeah I guess. But just Yoga for awhile Gideon and nothing else. Got that? Gideon: It will be a learning experience for all of us. We take for granted all that we have learned. Now we get to share our knowledge and mistakes with Ricky. Stephanie sighed. Steph: Yeah, the fun of raising a kid. Neil: At least we keep him away from Caleb. Steph: And we're gonna continue to do that until he graduates college. Ricky: But what's wrong with Caleb, Mom? Steph: Plenty, but we won't get into that. Ricky: Get into what? Steph: Ricky...you're too young understand a lot of this stuff, but your Uncle Caleb is...well... Neil: Unable to understand things. You know how he keeps calling you young Caleb? Ricky: Yeah. I don't know why he doesn't call me Ricky. Neil: Because he thinks the world is based on him. Steph: Caleb thinks he's God. As Stephanie shakes her head it was clear that she was concealing a snicker. Gideon: That is another thing we want to teach you and will teach you for many years. Never be arrogant. There is no such thing as being better. You can be faster, smarter, and more powerful but on that day it may not even matter if your opponent is better trained. Ricky: It won't? Neil: All 3 of us have been beaten by someone at sometime. The best thing you can do is prevent them from hurting you because everyone can be hurt. That is the reason why Gideon and I train so much so we don't get hurt. This is the first of many lessons Ricky will learn in his young life. He will grow up to be a man. His parents and his godfather Gideon will support him. He will always have Tony and Bobby looking over him. He has many more things to learn. He only recently turned 5. His whole life is ahead of him. He has found out something important as he had his ankle wrapped and covered with ice. You can learn many things over your life. The most important thing will always be……… Learning from your mistakes.
