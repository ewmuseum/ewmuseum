Mr. Ashe's Old School Promos #1

20 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/08 We're gonna go old school with this one. [The face that greeted the webcam wasn't one that would be advertised as 'LIVE WEBCAM' bait on the Internet. It was like a bucket strapped to a barrel, a face full of lines and divots, and two thick, chapped lips gripping a nub of a cigar.] Mr. Ashe: 'Ey. [His words were somehow less appealing than his looks on the grainy screen.] Mr. Ashe: Name's Rod. Roderick Ashe. Yeh, I'm the real guy. The 'dumb one' in the Dee-Em-Aitch, though I'd argue that nunya too goddamn bright these days. I'm comin' to ya live an' in livin' Technicolor on tape from friggin' DC. I hate this place, but you'll find I hate a lotta places. An' things. An' people. An' little aminals, though I figger they're prob'ly things, too, so I'm good an' covered. [Ashe leaned towards the camera, lips curving (further) into a sneer.] Mr. Ashe: Way I getcha, I been the guy what gets thrown in front'a dumbfucks like Ed Lambert the last eight months, so's the pretty and/or crippled folk don' get their face kicked inta the t'ird row. An' I been okay wit' it, y'know? Ain' complainin', 'cept when I gotta get knocked onta somethin' fuckin' hard. Tables an' concrete floors an' chairs, they ain't great, an' any'a you fucks what think wrasslin' is bogus should let me drop you on a chair. See if yer bright-eyed an' shiny afterwards. [A sausage-fingered hand reached out, thumb briefly obscuring the shot as he adjusted the angle of the webcam, sending the shot to waggling until it focused just over his shoulder.] Mr. Ashe: I ain' s'posed t'tell shit like where we is or whatev, but those'a you what saw the Great American Nightmare pay-per-view saw me pound some sand outta dat Twilight sisterfucker's face. It felt good, y'know, real good, to show up an' kick ass, even if the result was me getting' pounded. I hear they're sendin' Craiggy-kins a trophy fer bein' the eight-billionth guy what thought that he was a bigshot 'cuz he smacked around a cripple an' a fat guy. You think that's great, order the replay all week long on In Demand or DirecTV. [When Mr. Ashe grinned, it was not an appealing thing, teeth all crooked from being punched and yellow from the amount of tobacco he imbibed. With his squished-flat nose, the whole of it was kind of horrible.] Mr. Ashe: Figger it won' be long before this vid gets blasted by people you don't wanna nunna, dolly, so I'ma get right to the point. I'm sick'a bein' second-fiddle. I won one, two, t'ree, four World Tag Team Titles 'fore Allie closed the Enn-Dub. Know who else did that? Fuckin' Loki an' that's it. Ain' nobody in the storied hist'ry of the Council what did what I did, an' now I gotta be the meatshield for Kathi fuckin' Shaw? I don' think so. Bitch owes me twenny bucks, bee tee dub, so Kate, if yer watchin' this, pay up. [He tapped out the cigar into an ugly, bright orange ashtray at his side, brushing his fingers together in the international 'YOU OWE ME MONEY, BEYOTCH' symbol.] Mr. Ashe: When folk look at what Doc Kens churns up for a lineup every week, I figger their eyes stop at the main event. 'Look!' their inner fuckin' child says. 'Lance Marshall's gonna pound himself a bitch! He's the strongest motherfucker ever!' 'Cept they ain' doin' that this week. They're lookin', goin' 'Who the fuck is Misery?' or 'Austin King? Fer serious?' Maybe, 'Is dat really Reggie Love's hair?' The answers to those quessions are, 'Some nobody bitch who got played like a fiddle by Dru-girl,' 'Yeh, fer serious -- I don' get it either' an' 'What? Like fifty-year-old guys can't have hair?' [He snorted his chuckle, pulling the cigar up to his lips to take a long inhale, the end of the charcoal gray thing briefly flickering orange.] Mr. Ashe: Den they look all'a way down the lineup. They go 'Fuck you, Wy-Wy.' They go, 'Shit, them Indecency Motherfuckers sure must'a pissed off the yokels,' an' they get down to me. Fuckin' curtain-jerkin' with a has-been an' a couple never-weres. Now, I get that we're all friggin' 'enlightened' in the PTC an' we don' spend our time hatin' on fuckwads, but I ain' sleepin' till the buzz wears off an' Shway told me that I ain' allowed to find me no redhead whores to placate my time because -- get this -- that's 'just creepy'. [The Man-Mountain shook his head as he leaned forward, cupping his lips as his voice dropped to a faux-conspiratorial whisper.] Mr. Ashe: 'Cuz we fatties ain' supposed to pay for it, neither, see. [When he leaned back, the jagged smile on his ugly mug was somewhat contented. Unlike the other members of the Dead Man's Hand, who had spent the week dealing with their leader's sudden infatuation with Amy Campbell, Mr. Ashe was blissful. Of course, part of that was because he was ignorant of the Campbell deal, so... perhaps he wasn't the best off of the Handlers.] Mr. Ashe: So I figgered I'd take the time to uplink a vid usin' Lor's comp an' tell the faceless droves on the Internets just why it is I plan on making a ruin out of Josh an' Clay Pigeon. Here's my issue with these guys. It's a case'a relevance. Josh -- who, I wus disappointed t'fin' out, ain't the Eighth Dwarf -- used to be a bigshot, right? He used to be somebody in Global, which, hey, they let Ed Lambert be World Champ twice an' Chrissin Dork was big there too, so... yeah. [Look at that bushy brow hold itself up! It was like he was being sarcastic or something. Which didn't wear well on his craggy features.] Mr. Ashe: Den he comes in to be a Sinner an' 'e's all, 'I AM AWESOME' an' we're all, 'Fuck off, toadlicker.' An' he does! Figure nothin' was perfect fer this bastard after he got dropped on his noggin by... wait, who was it... some masked girl. She was kinda great, I'm told. Den that's it. Ain' showin' up to work, ain' bein' booked to wrassle, an' so I wonder why? Is losin' to a chick that goddamn bad? Is it 'cuz Allie is that great? Or is it 'cuz he's a pussy? An' you know what we do with pussies? We stick our fists in 'em, exactly. [A nod, a grin. Roderick jabbed the cigar into the pile of ashes, putting the thing out.] Mr. Ashe: On the other side is this Tex-Mex lookin', cowboy motherfucker named Clayton Byrd. He's from Texas -- know how I know, 'cause he talks like he's got fuckin' marbles in his mouth, chewin' on his words. An' if I am sayin' that, you know it's fuckin' bad. He's hired by the Doc to clean up this place, because, y'know, folks like Cycle an' Crispy ain' cuttin' it. An' I get it, I get wantin' t'be the sheriff. [With a sudden lunge, the Mushmouthed Man-Mountain's entire face filled the frame. Gone was the blank, cookie-cutter hotel room behind him. It was all Ashe, all the time.] Mr. Ashe: 'Cept I knew Damon Hayes, boyo, I kicked ass with Damon Hayes, an' you ain' no Sheriff. [Without a cigar to entertain them, his lips curled deeper into the frown as his voice picked up a mocking, high-pitched lilt.] Mr, Ashe: 'Oh, no! Ashe jus' made a reference to the NWC! He's old and I'm young an' I don' got no idea who Damon Hayes is! I am better than him because I'm current an' shit!' [Roderick spat on the floor; hey, it's a hotel room, the maid's going to vacuum] Mr. Ashe: Yeh, 'cept how you ain't. Know why you never heard'a Hayesie? 'Cuz he didn't succeed. He didn't win against the motherfuckers of his day an' age. In three years, when I'm sittin' down an' we're talkin' about how great we were in our first year in Sin City, I'm gonna remind folk that I slammed Clay Byrd's skull into the steel steps so hard he didn't see straight, an' people are gonna be like, 'Who the fuck was Clay Byrd?' [There is nothing as amused as Roderick Ashe's face was right then, a mix of lines drawing a roadmap of pleasure across his flesh.] Mr. Ashe: Way I see it, way I figger, I could beat both'a yer asses by myself, reestablish some cred that getting ass-kicked by the likes'a fuckin' Dusk mighta' cost me. 'Cept I don't gotta do it by myself. I got this other guy with me, his name's Malik. At first, I wondered if he was that ref guy, but, no, turns out this guy's some brickhouse motherfucker. Six-four, two-ninety. Didn' amount to a hill'a beans when he an' a guy named -- no shit -- 'GOD' both got their ass kicked by Tex Byrd, but I figger we make a more dominating team than most folks'll admit, so long as he brings his A to Worcester. [He pronounced it 'Worse-ter,' of course. Because he's uncivilized like that.] Mr. Ashe: An' if he don't, I'ma drop him on his face, so... one more for the meat grinder. [A small, leather-bound cigar case slid onto frame, snapped open by his chubby fingers for another barrel of lung cancer to be yanked out.] Mr. Ashe: Now, 'fore I go on an' try to catch some shut-eye or maybe some snatch, I jus' got one more thing t'say. Clay Byrd punched Hunter Sabuani in the kisser on 'is first show, like dat was some kinda big fuckin' deal. Now, while I usu'lly enjoy watchin' Hunner get his face kicked in, sucker-punchin' a guy 'cuz you ain't got a better comeback is low. I won the tag belts with Hunner back in the day, an' if anyone's gonna punch Subby in the face, it's gonna be me. [Biting off the nub of the cigar, he at least had the decency (if you can call it that) to lift the trash can and spit the end into its plastic, where it landed with a hollow thump. His smirk was sideways as he spoke.] Mr. Ashe: Not that I had anythin' t'do with what happened durin' his show filming, of course. [Lighting the cigar up, Ashe took a few long puffs from it, blowing his smoke straight up before he tureds his gaze to the camera again with a light shrug.] Mr. Ashe: Fer now, I think the point here is that Tex an' Sleepy should go suck a goat tit, an' that Mal Roland had best show up. I... don' got no cool catchphrase, so... bye. [Thick hands fat-fingered the keyboard, but eventually, they got the program filming this to go off, with Ashe's mutter the last thing audible.] 'Now. How the fuck do I load this on the Internet? [End.]
