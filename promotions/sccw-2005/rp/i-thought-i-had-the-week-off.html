I Thought I had the week off

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today So we're doing monologues this week are we? One lined remarks about why we're the best and why we're going to do this, that, or the other. I could do that, list my credentials and fit right in with this dick measuring contest that is currently going on, but what's the point when there's no bigger dick in SCCW than von Doom. James you wanna talk about how you've done this and done that, but when this and that mean changing your mind about whether you want to be someone the fans hate or someone the fans hate on a weekly basis...Well, what does it matter? Do you really think anyone cares whether the fans like you or not? Do you think Lane and I sat in the back asking Kat if she had any clue about whether or not the fans were going to be cheering or booing our opponents? James, take your head out of your ass and face reality for once in your damn life. You sit back and let everyone else tear each other apart because when push comes to shove, you don't belong in this industry. You're nothing more than a bearded lady in a one ring circus, begging for attention because mommy took too long to pull you off her tit and now you think you actually deserve it. You've done it all, von Doom, and still haven't done a damn thing worth mentioning. And no, it wasn't because you were picking your spots, it's because you just aren't very good. And then there's the second longest running joke in Sin City...Legion. If there was one person that could rival James von Doom for being the world's greatest curtain jerker, it would be you. So when I got the message that I was going to be in a match against a team of the two of you I knew it wouldn't matter who my teammate would be, because Austin King sees your name on the lineup and scratches his head in wonder of why he hasn't had a match in months. It gets to a point where people see your name across from theirs and wonder why they weren't just given the week off. I tell you what, why don't you and the other two Devil's Rejects wannabes just cut a little deeper next time and save us the time and money that could be better spent on getting a popcorn machine for my locker room... I love popcorn.
