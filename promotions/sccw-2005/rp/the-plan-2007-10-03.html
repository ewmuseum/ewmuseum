The Plan

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/07 June 16, 2007 8:00 PM Grady Memorial Hospital Atlanta, GA The soft yellow lights shone across the crib where Jordan laid fast asleep. His face was that of a small cherub freed of all tubes and breathing apparatuses. Jacque sat there deeply in thought about how he was going to make sure that Jordan would be in his life forever. He felt that he was the one that was there for him through all the complications that arose with his birth. Not Keegan. Jacque deserved to have Jordan for his own. Jacque slid out of the aqua leather glider and headed toward the door. He glanced over his shoulder to peek at Jordan before going home for the night. Dianna’s mother had finally gone home and now was a better time than any to ask her what she was so desperate to tell him the day she delivered Bella. This could be the thing he needed to assure Keegan would never take Jordan from him. Keegan had already taken too much from him. He wasn’t going to stand for it anymore. June 16, 2007 9:30 PM Jacque’s home Atlanta, GA Jacque entered the front door and headed for the sounds of Bella cooing. He slowly slid open the bedroom door and a sob caught in his throat. Dianna was laying on her right side with Bella curled up to her. She was singing a soft lullaby preparing Bella for sleep. Lullaby, and good night, You're your mother's delight, Shining angels beside My darling abide. Soft and warm is your bed, Close your eyes and rest your head. Soft and warm is your bed, Close your eyes and rest your head Jacque knew the sound of Brahms Lullaby. It was the same one his mother used to sing to him and his brother when they were small. Slowly Jacque entered the room and as he did Bella’s eyes fluttered closed. “Hello Jacque. How is Jordan?” Dianna asked without even glancing at him. “He is doing fine. The doctor said he’d be ready to start bottle feeding in the next couple of days.” Jacque told her softly. Dianna didn’t respond to his words. She methodically put Bella in her crib and floated past Jacque. He knew she resented him. He had basically made her a prisoner in his home. She didn’t want to be here and she made it known. The only reason she was here was for her children’s safety and her sanity. Jacque slowly followed her out of the room, keeping his distance. He needed to talk to her, ask her about Keegan. “What do you want Jacque?” She asked haughtily. “I need to know what you were so insistent about telling me the day Bella was born.” Jacque blurted. “Why does that matter now? Keegan is no where to be found and I don’t think he wants to be found.” “I need you to tell me this Dianna. Tell me now.” Jacque demanded. “I am sick of your demands Jacque Noreault. I feel like a prisoner here with you watching my every move.” She raised her voice enough for emphasis but not enough to wake Bella. “If it is something as bad as you made it seem then it is in Jordan’s best interest that you tell me. I don’t want some lunatic around my kids.” Dianna laughed hysterically, “You don’t want some lunatic around your kids huh. Take a long look in the mirror Jacque. Besides Jordan is not your child. He belongs to Keegan whether you like it or not.” “I know I have a bad reputation for my amateur boxing days but I am not that same person. You know it and I know it.” “You sure fooled me the day I came to you for help. You treated me like common street trash. Like someone that wasn’t worthy to even look at you.” Dianna told him with a tear streaking her cheek. “I know Dianna and I am sorry for that. I was a jerk and I had no reason to be. It’s just... you just hurt me really bad and I lashed out at you. How would you feel if you were in my shoes at that point in time?” Jacque asked flipping the blame to her. “I know Jacque but like you I can’t change the past. Now that we got that out of our systems I guess I really should tell you about Keegan’s past.” She said with a sigh. They both sat down in the living room. Jacque sat in his favorite chair and Dianna curled herself up on the sofa. She sighed heavily and then began to tell Jacque what she had found out about Keegan. She had found out that he had been in prison for murder. She wasn’t quite sure of all the details but she knew that much. Jacque’s jaw dropped in shock. “There is no way in hell I am letting him anywhere near Jordan.” “That is not your decision Jacque it’s mine and mine alone. I still allow you to be around Bella and you’re past is basically the same.” Dianna told him sternly. “Well this is my home and I don’t want him here. We are not alike in any way. The man I killed was an accident and from what you have told me Keegan killed that man on purpose.” Jacque told her poignantly. Dianna hung her head in defeat. She knew there was no use in arguing with him. He was too pigheaded. “So what do you propose that we do then?” “Don’t you worry I’ll take care of Keegan.” Jacque said as a twisted smile crossed his lips. He has always wanted to exact his revenge on Keegan and this would be the perfect way to do it. Keegan had taken everything that Jacque had loved from him. Now was Jacque’s turn to twist the dagger into Keegan’s gut. To make him feel the epitome of emptiness that Jacque had felt until Bella was born. Jacque knew that it was spiteful but at this point in his life all he cared about was his children. Jordan would be his and no one would stop him.
