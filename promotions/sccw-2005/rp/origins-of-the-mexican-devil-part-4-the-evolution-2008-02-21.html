Origins of The Mexican Devil- Part 4: The Evolution

21 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/2008 SATURDAY 24th FEBRUARY 1996 11:45PM Placing the bundle of money into an envelope, Raul had been victorious at his first high stakes poker game, thanks to the money that his boss Chester Portland had given to him. With six players all starting with seven thousand five hundred dollars, Raul had won thirty seven thousand five hundred dollars and with keeping his share had forty five thousand dollars in the envelope to walk away with. Luckily for the twenty-one year old, he was not alone, for Chester Portland was there with him, to make sure if anyone tried to steal the money from the youngster that he’d be there to stop them. "You played a good game kid," Portland remarked. Proud of his day’s exploits, Raul smiled as he wiped the sweat from his brow. "I thought they’d be better than that," Raul commented. The usually mild mannered and calm Portland actually chuckled at his employee’s confidence. "Don’t start getting cocky. This was just the first game of many, and each game will be for more money and certainly be more difficult," Nodding, Raul watched as Chester pulled a black comb out from his jacket pocket and began to comb his dark brown hair back, a frequent action by the forty-four year old man. "I understand, but the hardest part today was the length of the game. I wasn’t prepared for it to last that long," Raul explained. Putting the comb away into his cream jacket pocket, Portland adjusted his red tie and white shirt and held out his right hand to Diaz. "I’ll be needing the money now, Raul," he bluntly stated. Looking at his winnings, Raul reluctantly handed the envelope of money over to his boss. "Don’t worry about the length of the games. You have to learn to pace yourself. If your opponents see you being tired and anxious to leave the table, they will see it as your weakness and try to take advantage of you. Personally, I do not care what you do at the table, just make sure you win. Once you lose, you’re no longer a valuable asset, so make sure you do not lose Raul," Portland warned. Gulping rather loudly, Raul Diaz was only just beginning to learn of the harsh realities of life while doing this job, but knew that he could not get out of it. The best he could do was to keep winning. "I can do it," Raul cockily remarked, hiding his insecurities. Taking some money out of the envelope, Portland handed Raul a few dollars, more than a few dollars, a lot of dollars. Counting it, Raul had been given five thousand dollars for his day’s work, which was more than enough to cool his prior insecurities. "Lose the cockiness, kid. It really doesn’t suit you. About the money, the more that is won at the table the more you get to keep. I’ll keep you updated on where and when the next game is," Placing the money inside his cream coloured pants, Portland went to leave the basement where the game had been held. "Come on kid, I have to take you back to the hotel," Putting his black hooded top back on, after removing it during the game, Raul was ready to leave with the most money he had ever seen in his life, even though he’d only be keeping five thousand dollars of it. He still saw it as an achievement though in his evolution as a cash game poker player. "Okay Chester. But I’m curious, where does the rest of the money go?" Stopping in his tracks, this was the question, that the black sunglasses wearing, Portland had not wanted to be asked. Removing his sunglasses, revealing his piercing bright blue eyes, Chester turned to face Raul, and did not look happy. "When you need to know. I will let you know. You’re a rookie Raul, you need to earn your stripes first before you move up the food chain," he told him. Nodding his head, Raul knew there was more to it than Portland was telling him, but had to stay on Chester’s good side. "As long as you understand. Now, lets go," he demanded. Chester Portland put his sunglasses back on and left the basement with the young Raul right behind him. THURSDAY 21ST FEBRUARY 2008 7:00pm Flicking through the dollars in his wallet, The Mexican Devil sighed loudly. There was just two ten dollar bills and a one dollar bill, not a lot of money at all for the first ever SCCW Gateway Champion. "How did it come to this Angela?" he asked his wife. With her long red hair flowing eloquently, Angela sat next to her husband on the hotel’s bed. "Debts. We need to cut back on things," she replied. Looking at his wife, El Diablo noticed she was dressed up for their evening out. She had a low cut dark blue dress on, which showed ample cleavage, but looked very elegant as the dress went down to her ankles where she had on matching blue high heels. She had the silver necklace around her neck, that he had given her for their fifth wedding anniversary, as well as both her engagement and wedding rings on her left hand. She was beautiful. The problem was though, they were haemorrhaging money and were going on yet another night out, to bleed away even more money. El Diablo could see the problem, whereas Angela could not, or she chose not to. "It shouldn’t be this way. I am a champion and should not be short on money, ever," he stated. Angela looked carefully at her necklace and smiled. "We’ve had better times, yes, but we’ll see better times again. You’re at the pinnacle in your career and that can only mean that money will start rolling in," Angela remarked, optimistically. Her optimism was very refreshing to hear, as El Diablo needed her to believe in him. "I hope so, Angela, I hope so," Going against all logic, The Master of Manipulation was the one not ready for their evening out, when in most relationships it’s usually the women that run late while getting ready. Wearing just a plain white t-shirt and black sweatpants, El Diablo knew he would have to get ready soon, but really did not feel like it. "Do you want me at the arena for your next match?" she asked. Remembering the problems he had when Angela was there with him in PCW, and the limited time she spent with him in RPW and FUSE, El Diablo really did not want her there. She was an unwelcome distraction for him, as he always had to worry about her well being, but with her away from the wrestling business he no longer had those worries, and was enjoying great success in his wrestling career. "As much as the extra pay check would help, I’ll get our cash flow back up to where it should be, all by myself. I do appreciate your offer, though," Diablo responded as kindly as he could. Smiling, Angela got off the bed, in the cheap hotel that they were situated in. "Come on. Hurry up and get ready," Rolling his eyes, The Creator of Controversy not only had money on his mind, but also his upcoming match on the first event of the newly christened SCCW, Sin City Championship Wrestling. "I should be Main Eventing this show, Angela, but they demoted me," His tone turned to anger. "They billed it for more than a week, El Diablo vs. Quacking Pierre in a Gateway Title match as the Main Event. Then I find out that they’re taking that away from me. How dare they take that away from me? I am The Apex of Excellence and they drop me," he bitterly stated. Concerned for her husband, and for herself, Angela just had to ask. "Not Main Eventing means less money, right?" Diablo slowly nodded. "It’s that Smitty T. Duluth, he’s had it in for me ever since I joined his company," Looking into his wife’s blue eyes, he could see her concern for him and the situation they were in. "Don’t worry about the money. I’ll just keep winning and show STD that I should be Main Eventing whenever I have a match. That French guy will wish he was never awarded this match against me. I’ve beaten Timo Bolamba, Aimz and Jacob McKail in recent weeks, all champions in their own right, so Pierre doesn’t have a prayer, and believe me, he needs one," The Pinnacle of Perfection confidently said. Flashing a fake smile at her husband, Angela walked over to her blue handbag and picked it up, placing the straps over her left shoulder. "I have faith in you, Raul, but can you just get ready? We deserve a night out together," Getting up off the bed, The Mexican Devil walked towards his wife and kissed her softly on the back of her neck. "You wait here. I just need to make a phone call and then I’ll get ready for you," Walking out of the main room, Diablo stepped into the bathroom, pulling his cell phone out from his pocket and quickly dialling a number he hoped he’d never have to use again. "I need your help. Get here as soon as you can," Diablo told the person at the other end of the phone. Ending the call, The Master of Manipulation left the bathroom and returned to the room, where his wife was waiting for him. "Gonna get ready now?" she asked impatiently. Diablo nodded. SUNDAY 25th FEBRUARY 1996 1:15am With Raul being taken back to the hotel, Chester Portland could now deliver the money to his boss, the boss that the young Mexican did not know Portland had. In fact, this man was also Raul’s boss, only he did not know of this man in that way. Raul only knew of him as the man who is out to kill him, a story told to him by Portland, who told him that story under strict orders from his boss, Aces McGhee. The name was no doubt an alias, but being under Portland’s so called protection, he felt no need to be fearful of this mystery man any longer. "He won," remarked Portland. Remaining in the shadows, McGhee was only slightly visible thanks to the lit cigarette dangling from his lips. "Good," his hoarse voice muttered. Portland was stood quite a distance away from McGhee, as those were always his orders. The pair were in an abandoned warehouse to exchange the money that Raul had won and talk further more about business. "It would appear that you were right about him, Chester," stated the mysterious McGhee. Smirking, Portland began to comb his hair back as he watched McGhee take another long drag of his cancer stick. "He’s got natural instincts. He will win you a lot of money," Breathing smoke out in an "O" shape, McGhee’s body language indicated that he wasn’t there for small talk. "So where is the money, Chester?" Putting the comb away, Portland removed the envelope from his jacket pocket and threw it to the ground just in front of Aces McGhee. "I assume it’s all there," McGhee asked as he kneeled down to pick up the envelope. Shaking his head, Portland soon realised that McGhee could not see this action. "It’s five dimes short. That’s the fee I gave the kid," Flicking through the multiple dollar bills, McGhee quickly counted that it was just five thousand dollars short. "That’s a fair fee. Here’s your fee, Chester," Throwing two bundles of five thousand dollars towards Portland, McGhee dropped his cigarette and quickly scurried to pick it up and put it between his lips where it belonged. Portland picked up the ten thousand dollars and placed it inside his jacket pocket. "When’s the next game?" Portland queried. Coughing loudly as all smokers do eventually, McGhee had to clear his lungs before being able to answer his employee. "Not for a few weeks. I want to challenge the kid, the next game is for hundreds of thousands of dollars. I want to see how he copes with the pressure. If he copes with it, he might just have a future here after all," Grinning, Portland was glad that Aces was finally beginning to realise the potential in young Raul, as he had done almost instantly. "He will win it," Chester stated confidently. Finally pocketing the remainder of the money, McGhee turned to leave. "As usual, Chester, if he doesn’t, you’re in the line of fire too. So you best make sure your protégé wins," McGhee warned. Throwing his lit cigarette to the ground, Aces was vanished from sight, leaving Portland standing there, contemplating his own future as well as Raul’s. THURSDAY 21ST FEBRUARY 2008 7:30pm Adjusting the collar of his black shirt, El Diablo was finally ready to leave for dinner with his beautiful wife Angela. For a change, he had scrubbed up well, as his black shirt was very neat and matched the black pants that he was wearing, as well as the nicely polished black shoes. Angela seemed very impressed with her husband, despite being frustrated at how long it had taken him to get ready for their evening out. "Ready to leave now?" Angela inquired. Shaking his head as he looked in the mirror, The Creator of Controversy was trying to delay their leaving for some unspecified reason. "I have all the plans, Angela. Be patient," Angela scoffed, as she knew she wasn’t being unreasonable. "Fine. Take all the time you need, but don’t ever complain when I leave you waiting," she warned him. Rolling his eyes, Diablo was desperate to get things done. "Hurry up," he muttered under his breath. To further delay things, he even began combing his long black hair back, an act he very rarely does, drawing scorn from his fiery wife. "OH COME ON RAUL! I don’t think I’ve ever seen you comb your hair the whole time I’ve known you," The Apex of Excellence smirked. "I guess I’m just trying to better myself for you," he lied. Storming around the room in sheer frustration, Angela chose to run into the bathroom and locked the door behind her. "WHEN YOU’RE READY LET ME KNOW!" she shouted from the bathroom. Grinning like a Cheshire cat, El Diablo knew his wife too well and fully expected this behaviour from her. "I will," he called back to her. Waiting was driving The Mexican Devil crazy, until finally there was a gentle knock at the door. "At last," the Mexican muttered to himself. The Pinnacle of Perfection walked towards the door and slowly opened it, revealing a shadow of a man in the doorway with a lit cigarette dangling from between his lips. "Aren’t you going to invite me in?" his hoarse voice croaked. Stepping back, Diablo motioned for the mysterious man to enter his hotel room. "Funds must be low," he cracked, as he blew smoke into El Diablo’s face. Refusing to be intimidated, Diablo stood face to face with the man he had phoned, although his face was hidden by a low worn hat and a thick beard. "I’m glad you could finally make it, Aces," Diablo remarked.
