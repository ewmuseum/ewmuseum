IX (In Judge We Trust, Part 2)

3 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/03/2009 "Who the hell do you think you are? You live off people while insulting them, nobody complains because they think you're a goddamned lunatic... Do you know how hard it is being your friend?" - Nite Owl II, Watchmen IX 01/05/2009 The sky is the color of the ocean. It makes the lunch date I’m on with my girlfriend Amanda even more amazing than it should be. Her brown hair sways in the breeze as we sit outside and eat our lunch at the Baja Fresh off of Sahara and Decatur. It’s been such a wonderful day and she seems happy but there are underlying thoughts in her head. I can tell because of the little crinkle she gets on her nose when she’s thinking too hard about something. After taking a sip from my drink, I finally decide to ask her about it. "What’s wrong, sweetie?" I ask as she looks over at me. I can tell she doesn’t want to talk about but much to my surprise, she does so anyway. "I think that the team is going to fall apart," she responds in a rather melancholy fashion. "I mean, everyone is on edge. Captain Velocity has got some of them thinking that we shouldn’t use lethal force and are sinners for allowing you to kill criminals. They think that the people will want heroes who let criminals live, not kill them like you do." "Pfft. Fuck them. They’re just spreading propaganda to try and turn the team on me so that they can control everyone in the group. It’s all a big cop out for their big scheme," She raises an eyebrow. I can tell she’s thinking something again and doesn’t want to say it. Although it might just be the sun in her face. We are outside, after all. "Ash, you need to be a bit more optimistic." "I am being optimistic and I’m being realistic too. I know that there’s no way this team is going to stick together unless those two ceasefire with me." Now she looks concerned. I can’t understand her! Besides, I stopped understanding women a long time ago so this should come as no surprise. "I think you may be right about that but the three of you need to be more cordial with each other if this team is going to work." "I know that. However the two of them need to be less domineering to the rest of the team, especially me. They think that the team is just supposed to do whatever they say. See they think that they have dual halos over their heads." I then make a halo motion over my head with my hands to further emphasize my point that both Captain Velocity and Mustang both think they are angels. After doing this, I bring my hands back down and shake my head. "It makes me sick. I know the team won’t split up, but those two are going to sew the seeds of dissention. They’ll be the end of us. Not soon, but in the future. It’s like the Untied States Senate. It works but there’s always partisanship within the ranks. That’s how it relates to our team. We either work together or it will implode. I just hope we don’t get to see it happen." After these words leave my mouth, there is an uneasy silence for a moment. This scares me for a little bit. However after a few moments she smiles at me which calms my nerves very quickly. We continue to eat in peace. It’s nice going out without all of the cameras and news crews following us around or the sounds of police sirens everywhere we go. A day of peace, quiet, and tranquility is what we need. It won’t last forever. Nothing ever does… * * * 01/06/2009 The Rat Pack has been called in to help in another chaotic police situation. Multiple homicides and a guy on the loose with a gun. In other words, just another night in Las Vegas. Luckily this one is taking place in Northtown instead of downtown. I hate going downtown. It’s sleazy and there’s always gawking tourists down there. They’re almost as bad as the locals. Once we’ve arrived at the apartment complex where the incident is taking place in our separate vehicles, everyone in the team gets out and we head over to where the police barricades are. They let us all in and we then walk over to Police Chief Twain who is waiting for us by his police car. He gives us a distressed look as we walk over to find out aboutthe situation. "So what’s the assessment on this situation?" Mustang asks once we reach the Chief. "Not good," he replies grimly. "There’s a man with a history of mental instability in there with a gun. He’s killed four people already and two of my officers have been shot." He stops and then looks at all of us, "You guys ready for it?" I nod my head in agreement. "We’re more than ready to take this guy down," I reply with a smile. "In a non-lethal manner, of course," Captain Velocity chimes in, giving me a knowing look. This pisses me off, so naturally I flip him off and head toward the apartment complex where the criminal is holding himself up in. Cat Lady quickly catches up to me and talks me into going back for a team meeting to decide how to approach this situation. We decide to use the tactic of splitting up and surrounding the building before going in. Of course, I’m stuck going in with Captain Velocity. He doesn’t seem too thrilled about it either. The other pairings as we go in are Mustang and Lady Bug, Vagrant and Cat Lady, and Abrams is paired up with the Astronomer. The four pairs split up as soon as we get inside, each taking a floor. Me and Captain Velocity get the fourth floor, which is top floor of the building. The moment the two of us arrive on the top floor, the shit hits the fan because, of course, I’m what the police call a ‘shit magnet’ and this kind of stuff happens to me all the time. Gunfire in our direction coming from the third door down the hallway. Captain Velocity ducks and covers, cowering in fear like the wussy he is. Instead of following his stupid example of cowardice, I dodge and keep running instead of impersonating the Cowardly Lion from the Wizard of Oz. Charging at the perp, he quickly sees that he’s shit out of luck and turns to run away. I go after him, continuing my pursuit down the hall until he picks up a pot and tosses at me. He misses but it trips me up and I fall face first to the floor. Looking up, I see him kick open the last door in the hallway. Running inside the apartment right behind him is Captain Velocity. As I rise up to my feet like a zombie being resurrected to go eat human flesh, I hear noises inside the apartment. Running over and going in to investigate I go inside to see the criminal on the ground with my teammate pulling him back up. I use this time to catch my breath and watch what unfolds. After catching the criminal who just tried to kill me only moments before, Captain Velocity starts talking to him like he’s some kid who just took a candy bar from another kid on the playground. What a fucktard this guy is. That shit isn’t going to work. "You made a bad mistake," he says to him. He then notices me walking over toward them. He gives me a look, one of those looks that is both incredulous and scared at the same time. "For my attempted murder…" I say with an angry look on my face. "Don’t do it Judge," Velocity tells me but I disregard his hogwash. "Fuck you," I say to him before turning back to the criminal. "You are judged to die." Pulling out my handgun, I point it at the criminal’s head and pull the trigger. The sound of the bullet being fired from the barrel of my gun echoes throughout the area. Brains splatter all over Captain Velocity’s ugly costume as he looks down at the corpse in shock. Starting to giggle, I put the gun up as he looks at me in complete shock before the look of shock quickly turns to anger. He lunges at me, grabbing my throat. "MURDERER!" he exclaims as his grip tightens. I push him back and then punch him in the face. I punch him in the head which makes him stagger backward like the weakling that he is. "I JUST SAVED YOUR LIFE BEFORE THAT! He would’ve killed you moments ago and then you capture him. I just made it possible so he wouldn’t do it again." "That doesn’t change the fact that you are a murderer!" The rest of the group runs over and pulls us apart. And right before I was about to knock his ass out too which pisses me off even. "Judge just murdered this guy," Velocity says to them with his whiny, annoying voice like he’s supposed to be the good guy in a bad situation. "WHAT?!" Lady Bug yells out. I give Captain Velocity a look. He always tries to play up like he’s always right. What a joke. All he’s doing is trying to make himself look and create dissention within the team. "I judged him for attempted murder. He tried to kill both of us right before that!" "That doesn’t change what you did. AND HIS BRAINS ARE ALL OVER MY COSTUME!" His response both annoys me and makes me want to laugh at the same time. Of course my nonchalant response doesn’t help matters much. "Pfft. It’s an ugly costume anyway." I guess I pissed him off judging by his response. "You son of a bitch." "Takes one to know one." Looking at velocity’s face, he’s had it. His face turns as red as his little prissy costume and he lunges at me, choking my neck as the veins in his neck pop out. "I’LL KILL YOU JUDGE! I’LL KILL YOU!" As he tightens his grip on my throat, I push him off and then elbow him in the chest when he tries it again. The group then swarms us to keep the two of us from killing each other. "HYPOCRITE! ALL YOUR PANDERING IS JUST BULLSHIT! THAT PROVES IT!" I yell across the room at him as Abrams and the Astronomer are trying to keep me from killing him. Getting loose from their grips, I pull out my gun and walk over to Velocity as everyone gets scared. I then point the gun at his head and them clinch my other fist. I then show him both as I then yell in his face. "WHAT ARE YOU MORE AFRAID OF? THIS OR THIS?!" He quickly stammers out an answer. "THE GUN! THE GUN!" "SEE?! I’M RIGHT AND YOU’RE FULL OF SHIT!" I then unload the gun and put it away as he gets his composure back. The rest of the Rat Pack seems shell-shocked by what just happened. Captain Velocity’s shocked look soon turns to anger and he looks like he’s about to have a temper tantrum. "I’M DONE!" he whines out. Velocity then turns and starts walking away from the rest of the Rat Pack much to the surprise of everyone. "WAIT! COME BACK!" the Astronomer yells across the room, motioning for Captain Velocity to return but he is ignored. "I ain’t never coming back." Velocity leaves the area and Mustang, his closest confidant in the group, is right on his heels. "Nice going Judge," Lady Bug growls out before following them. This only makes me shake my head. That stupid red and yellow idiot was the one instigating all of this and I’m the bad guy? What a joke. This team deserves to fall apart. I know it won’t, but it deserves to. "What the hell did I do?" I ask, shrugging my shoulders before motioning in the direction of Captain Velocity. "THAT ASSHOLE IS THE ONE WHO STARTED THIS SHIT!" "Judge, don’t worry about it," the Astronomer says with a smile. "We’ll get over this tomorrow." "Yeah, right." Great. Half of the team has a bug up their ass apparently because of me. What a joke that is. The team is imploding because of one man’s self righteous attitude about hero work. * * * 01/07/2009 3:00 pm Looking down at a piece of paper, the Astronomer has a grim look on his face. He doesn’t want to read what it says to the rest of the Rat Pack. He hesitates for a few moments before finally reading what is on the paper. "Six to two," he mutters out in a rather melancholy fashion. "That is the vote." Everyone looks around silent in the headquarters of the Rat Pack. The vote has been tallied and the group has just voted to split. This is a sad day for us. I’m more in shock than anything else. Looking around the room at the rest of the costumed heroes in the group, they are not as in shock as I am. I really was gullible enough to think that the group would stick together despite all of the disagreements. I was wrong, dead wrong, and now we’re stuck here standing or sitting in a townhouse looking at each other without a clue as to what to do next. "So now what do we do?" I ask looking around. "Everyone except for me and somebody else voted for the group to break apart. Do you realize what that means?" "Yes," Captain Velocity says with a sly little smile on his face. What a cocky son of a bitch this guy is. "It means we now decide who goes with whom?" "You and your pro-death cronies can have your own little team," he continues. "Whereas me and the rest of the life saving heroes in the group, the God fearing men who love life and protecting life, will form our own team." I can only shake my head at his thought process. This idiot is going to ruin everything. "Ok hotshot," I reply, rolling my eyes as I do so. "So we’re going to split into two groups, how are we going to make this work genius? This is one big city and we can’t share. What if our team members meet up on a case? What happens then? And how are we going to decide who goes on what team?" Of course, as I already knew, the idiot didn’t fully think this through. So he’s left stammering for an answer. Of course he skips the first part of my questioning and only answers the very last part. "We’re going to have a vote," he replies in a snobbish, pretentious manner that only makes me want to punch him in the face even more than I want to already. "The heroes who want to join with my team whose mission is a peaceful one will come stand by me when we vote. The other heartless killers can go with you." This asshole is getting on my nerves with his commentary. "QUIT BEING CONDESCENDING YOU PRICK!" "YEAH!" Bailiff hollers out. "Stop being a prick! You’re only making things worse!" "KILLER!" "You’re the one who started this," I respond in an angry fashion, motioning toward Captain Velocity. "Boys, boys," the Astronomer says, trying to keep the peace. "Can we please just vote and be on our way before we end up killing each other before we get a chance to? There has to be a peaceful manner of going about this." "You’re right," Captain Velocity says as he regains his composure. "We can’t let these barbarians drag us down to their level." I shoot him a glare. The moment he said the word ‘barbarians,’ he motioned toward myself, Bailiff, and Abrams, which doesn’t sit too well with me. The two of them don’t seem to be too pleased about it either. He then turns to the Astronomer. "Those who want to join my team," he says as he looks around at all of us. "Come over to me now." As I look toward the rest of team, a few of them, about half, slowly trickle over toward Captain Velocity before stopping next to him. The heroes in our group who have decided to go along with his pro-life stance are the cocky rich boy Mustang, Halloween costumed Lady Bug, and peace maker the Astronomer. Turning toward everyone else whose left, they then stroll to my direction without hesitation. This group includes the homeless hero Vagrant, the giant camouflaged monster of a hero named Abrams, and my girlfriend, the lovely Cat Lady. Both groups look at each other as Astronomer motions around. "So it’s settled," he says in a very unhappy fashion. "We’re now two different superhero teams." Before he can continue, Captain Velocity interrupts him to blurt out some more of his right wing propaganda. "Two teams," he says with a big grin on his face. "One who fights for peace and the preservation of life. The other for death and chaos." "That sounds about right," Mustang mutters out with a chuckle. Both of their comments only further annoy me. "It’s not like you two have dual halos over your heads," I say before smirking at the looks on their faces after hearing my words. "Well, you don’t. You don’t have halos over your heads, you’re not angels, you’re just two misguided souls who divided our team like Republicans and Democrats. We’ll see who was right and who was full of shit by the time all is said and done. The halo angels over there are the ones to blame for this. Not me, just Captain Velcro and his horse Pokey." I then turn to my group and motion toward the door. "Now let’s get the hell out of here before I smash their faces in." Abrams, Vagrant, and Cat Lady follow me out the front door of the Astronomer’s house. The uneasy tension quickly fades as we all soon realize that the arguing and debating would be over for the time being. Today is a new day for us. The Rat Pack is no more. I can’t wait to see the news media headlines for this in the coming days.
