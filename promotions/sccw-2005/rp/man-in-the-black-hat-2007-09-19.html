Man in the Black Hat

19 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/07 Detroit, Michigan. Inside a small CBS studio, the mother company to the Showtime Network, walking distance from the Joe-Louis Arena. A bright UPROAR banner makes up the entire backdrop of the scene. There are no flower pots or sofas, no end tables with coffee mugs, this isn't a Late Night talk show setting or lunch time with Montel Williams. No, this is a wrestling promo and it's slick and clean, just like the man standing in front of the backdrop... Jason Cruise. His sleeveless arms are against his chest, strategically folded over one another just underneath the Highywaymen logo on his black shirt. Around his waist is the symbol of his hard work and dedication over the past few months, the FUSE Livewire Championship and it looks spectacular. The hard brown eyes of the Highywaymen member stare forward at us, they are focused, they are ready. 'Sometimes I feel like I'm surrounded by walking hallmark cards. Good against evil, man against time...' 'Woman on woman. Yeah, that shit is hot.' 'Dammit, stop. Johnny you're so retarded sometimes.' And if expected, the red light on the FUSE camera dissolves instantly. Jason's stare readjusts on someone off-screen, someone who for some reason he insists on letting tag along with him through his young journey here in FUSE. Johnny. 'Seriously though, you can't deny that you're into that,' Johnny says. Looking over at his friend, Jason responds, 'I'm trying to cut a promo here. Can't you be quiet for like two minutes?' 'I bet that's not the first time you've used that line on someone.' Jason shakes his head. 'Seriously, Johnny.' 'You invited me, what did you expect me to do?' 'I gave you a rubik's cube, I thought that would keep you busy for hours.' Johnny holds it up in the air, all the colors perfectly matching on each side. 'I don't get what's so tough about these... how hard is it to peel the stickers off and put them on the right side?' Sighing, 'That's not the -- nevermind. 'Just,' Jason pauses. 'Just give me a minute here... please.' 'Yeah, whatever.' 'Sometimes I feel like a walking hallmark card...' Coughing, 'Weak.' Jason looks over at Johnny, frustration mounting on his face. 'Oh, and you could do better?' Johnny shoots out of his seat and shoves Jason out of the way, 'You bet your ass I can!' 'Ah fuck,' Jason mutters. * * * * * Downtown Detroit. The Roma Cafe. Wood paneling lines the interior of this more than one hundred year old house turned Italian restaurant. Tuxedo clad waiters and waitresses filter between the tables, hot plates on their arms and forced smiles on their faces. In the back of the room underneath a large curtain is a private booth with a bottle of champagne chilling over ice in the center of the table. The waiter's hand grabs the bottle just underneath the neck and begins pouring the bubbly into a thin stemmed flute in front of a familiar looking young woman. Marie. Kansas Marie. For once her light brown hair is lifted from her face save a few errant strands that drip over her right cheek. A charming smile adorns her delicate face as she nods to the waiter who places the bottle back in the stainless steel cooler. She gently grabs the glass by the stem and lifts it to her thin lips, taking a small sip before returning the glass down to the table, red lipstick now staining the lip of the glass. Just as she looks up her eyes widen as a man in a black hat is seated across from her. 'Marie,' he says in a deep drawl. 'You're late,' she says. 'Surprised?' He calmly questions her. 'Not really. I just don't have a lot of time.' 'So you went with Italian?' 'You don't like it?' 'It will do. But if you were in such a hurry we could have met over coffee,' he says very matter of factly. Her smile fades. * * * * * 'Oh man, you must look pretty ridiculous right now, El Diablo. Chasing Jason Cruise down week after week.. first losing your pocket watch, oh my! Then losing your title.. the travesty! I don't know how you talk to yourself in the mirror each morning.' Off-screen Jason just puts his hand over his face as Johnny continues to 'cut a promo' on his behalf. 'But fear not my firey little Chicano.' 'Mexican,' mutters Jason off-screen. 'Mexicano. Losing to Jason Cruise in a wrestling match is nothing to be ashamed of. Sure, you punch like a girl and have the wrestling skills of a tackle box but miracles do happen. Unfortunately for you, they only happen in Kurt Russell movies and Saturday morning specials, not on pay-per views televised around the world. Sometimes you just have to sit back and appreciate the happy little moments that life throws your way. You got to be a champion for nearly two months and quite frankly, nobody in their right mind could have predicted that! It must feel damn good deep inside your little corona filled tummy.' Johnny rubs his belly as he says this. 'Sure, maybe it wasn't fair of the FUSE hierarchy to put you against Jason again but I'm sure they'll let you pummel a few more opponents and ride the coat tails of Lane Stevens and Clinton Sage a little bit longer to get your hopes up before they let Jason squash you for the third time in an over-hyped melodramatic re-rematch. Just like they are doing with Mayson Colby. Hey there Mason,' Johnny waves to the camera. 'Your ex called, she's ovulating right now and the restraining order is on the way. She invited me over for milk and cookies and I just might take her up on the offer!' You can hear Jason letting out a deep sigh off-screen. 'I can't help but feel sorry for you though, Mayson. You worked so hard to beat a guy once nicknamed 'The Lethal Cocktail' and the son of a legend that nobody in FUSE even knows or cares about. And if that wasn't enough, you then defeated your arch rival who if he didn't drop the dreaded 'C' word on live television one hundred times nobody would even know who he was. Wow. You really earned this shot against the toughest of tough competition, didn't you?' Maybe next time around you can step it up a notch and win a grueling game of tug of war or connect four against a monkey. Wouldn't that be special?' Johnny nods. 'However, regardless the reason or way you earned this shot at the Prophecy...' Johnny pauses, and with a whisper to Jason off-screen, 'Seriously bro.. you need a new nickname, that's kind of corny.' He returns his attention to the screen. 'Ahem. Where was I, ah yes. Regardless the reason that got you here, you are in fact... here. You're sweeping over the company, tearing down the walls, wreaking havoc, scorching the Earth, and bringing anarchy onto the world of FUSE and giving forgiveness to the man you hate most in attempt to perform some Clinton Sage rape fantasy to climb the ranks and bring home the gold. But standing in your way is THIS MAN!' Johnny gestures over to Jason who is quietly sitting in his chair. 'Yo, help me out here.' Jason perks up a bit. 'THIS MAN!' Jason does the spooky fingers and Johnny smiles. 'Awesome. And unfortunately for you, I conducted a survey in this room about who was going to win this match and you lost two to nothing, Mayson. It was a one sided ass kicking. I tried my doodly damndest to find a reason that would support you becoming a champion this week and quite frankly... I couldn't find a single one.' 'Sucks for you, Mayson.' Johnny gives a thumbs down. 'Sucks for you.' Jason gets out of his chair and walks out of the room. Johnny looks over at him and shouts after him, 'How was it?!' * * * * * 'I thought it would be nice to meet for dinner and catch up.' The man in the black hat says nothing as Marie takes a short breath, turning away momentarily to hide the frustration on her face. 'I guess we'll get on with it then,' she says disappointedly. 'Very well.' He waits, not giving her much help to carry the conversation on. 'I'm in.' she says in almost a regretting tone. 'And?' 'I signed a contract last week. Ten shows.' 'Ten. That is not what we discussed.' 'I know, but that's all Mr. Duluth would give me with all the changes in the company going on.' 'I guess things will have to progress quicker than I expected.' 'I'm sorry.' 'It gives less room for error on your end. It will be fine. What about him?' Her head slightly tilts left to right and back. 'Jason doesn't expect a thing.' 'Good, keep it that way.' She nods. The waiter approaches the table, a black book in one hand and a pen in the other. 'Were you two ready to order?' The man in the black hat stands up. 'She is.' He reaches into his wallet and pulls out a few hundred dollar bills and flings them onto the table. 'Keep me posted.' Her eyes follow him as he walks away from the table. The waiter, a bit confused, looks back at her. 'For you miss?' 'More champagne, please.' Black.
