Date: Today "I can always tell when we are in for a particularly cold winter." The speaker, Edward Lambert, stared through the large window in his office at the yard outside. He wore a long flannel and a simple pair of jeans. "I can feel it in my entire fucking body. My hands ache, my back, my knees, even my fucking jaw aches. I hate this time of year." Slowly he turned his back on the window and walked to the large oak desk that dominates the rear of the room. His guest, the videographer Nick Black nodded at him from the red leather chair in front of the desk. "I used to like the winter. The cold, the crisp air, the promise of new life come spring. Now… now I look at it, and all I see is death." Edward seats himself behind the desk, reaches out and pulls a Lucky Strike cigarette from at pack that sits on the desk. With the flick of a thumb, fire and tobacco meet and after a moment, he looks across at the seated Nick. "You probably love the winter don’tcha? I mean, what with you being from Chicago and all." "Not really. I was raised in Southern California; I only went to school in Chicago because they offered me a partial scholarship. It wasn’t my first choice, but when your options are joining the army to get money for college, or eating ramen, I chose to eat Ramen." A flicker of a smile crosses Edward’s battered face. "There were times in my life I would have been happy to eat Ramen. Hell, if I had taken a different path in my career, I’d probably be eating Ramen right now. Hell, maybe…" A strange look crosses Edwards face for a moment and is gone. "But, it’s neither here, nor there. Lets’ get this show on the road shall we Nick?" "You’re the boss." * * * * * "Fucking Desade, that goddamned bitch just… I don’t even know how to fucking say this, but goddamn what I would give for five minutes alone in a dark room with her." [Edward Lambert’s face is almost purple.] "That bitch fucked me over, she used me to fuck over the entire NWC by saving a jackass that I didn’t really like from doing what, in retrospect, was utterly right! She fucked me, and in doing so, she fucked a goddamned lot of people." [The cigarette in the ashtray smokes while he looks forward, his teeth clenched.] "And so, six years later, the bitch shows back up. I don’t give a fuck about Subby. He’s just a piece of icing on the cake of Desade." [He grins.] "And he’s gonna get fucked up the next time I get a chance." [Edward pulls the burning cigarette from the ashtray and takes a deep draw on the cancer stick.] "You know. I probably fucked myself up back then. I took the high road. I tried to be a [air quotes]"nice guy". I tried, in my own way, of leading a war against a guy who I took to be the ultimate evil. I stood in his way, I tried to cover for people who needed help, and at the time, Desade seemed like the one who needed the help." [He smirks.] "Wasn’t I fucking wrong? I took a stand against my own best friend, I took a stand against a guy I respected, and against a guy I fucking hated. I took my arrogant roadblock attitude and I stood there. I did my best to fuck up the plots and plans of a guy named Chris Schukar in his attempt to close down the NWC." "My own best friend, Hiro Saotome sided with him, so I beat Hiro into a body cast. A guy I hardly knew came to me with an olive branch trying to get the differences between me and Schukar worked out. I sent him to the ER with glass ground into his face." [A wry smile crosses his face.] "The funniest part of that whole situation is that I was fighting to protect the same fucking thing I was fighting against. Chris wanted to kill the NWC and close it. Desade wanted to do the same fucking thing, but rather than come out and just tell us that she wanted to do it, she manipulated those of us who tried to do the right thing into fucking ourselves." "And who was right out front wearing a great big "FUCK ME" sign?" "Me." [He grinds the cigarette out in the ashtray before him.] "And now, six years later, Desade crawls out of the woodwork looking to do the same fucking thing again. But this time, it’s different. I’m not the same gullible fucktard I was then. I’m not going to try playing the noble jackass I once would have. Call me harder, call me bitter, call me what you will, I’m not going to let her play the same game this time." [Edward pauses a moment and stares out at the window across from him for a moment.] "Just so that you know, everyone in the world is a fucking liar. Everyone you meet, your best friend, your boss, your co-workers, your parents, your lovers, all of them. Each and every one of them is a great big fucking liar. Big lies, little lies, mean lies, nice lies, they are all lies." [He reaches down and pulls another cancer stick from the pack on his desk.] "Me for example, I’m lying. Well, not right now, not this very second, the part about everyone being liar is utter truth. But I’m sure that any minute now I’m going to utter a fucking lie and not even think about it. The kicker is, most of these things that we all say, they aren’t what you instantly think of when you think about a lie. They aren’t all malicious, they aren’t meant to hurt you or cheat you, or screw you over." "Well, not always, some times they are, but not always. Sure there are those evil fucks out there who like to lie. Some of them lie to make themselves to be better than they are; they are trying to sell themselves to you. It’s like a used car dealer trying to sell you his wares, or a college frat boy offering you a drink." [He puts the cigarette between his lips and reaches for his monogrammed Zippo.] "Most of the lies, they are about the person wanting you to think better of them, as if it were a huge fucking thing that you, a statistical nobody, gives a fuck about them. For the most part, none of us give a goddamn about anyone else. We worry about when we will feel better, when we will get a chance to have something to eat, to drink, to fuck all of it. We worry about ourselves before others, and doing otherwise is inhuman." [With a flick, the cigarette is lit and the lighter returned to its resting place atop the oak desk.] "I remember talking to my friends in college about my hopes and dreams. I remember having discussions about how I wanted to change the world, and make it a better place…" [Edward snorts.] "Ok, no. That’s a lie. I never gave a shit about the world. I gave a shit about when I was going to get laid next. See, that’s the truth. That’s not a fucking lie. I did have conversations about wanting to be more than I was, more than I am. When you are young, it’s ok to want more. You yearn to be bigger, better, stronger, faster, smarter, harder, richer or what the fuck ever word that ends in ER. Now?" [He takes a draw from the cigarette.] "Now it’s all "How are you doing Bill? How are the kids?" "Oh everything’s great Paul! MY wife is great! The Job is great! My kids are great!" [Edwards looks straight at the camera.] "And yes, that is a great big fucking lie." "When you tell Paul that everything is great, you are a liar. You want something more, somewhere inside you; you are thinking about that chick you almost married, or that car you almost bought, or that job you almost took. You feel conflicted each and every day. You desire, no matter how secretly, to be better than you are. You live vicariously through the people on the television; that are better than you. You live through movies, you live through books." "You live through watching me slam some son of a bitch through a plate glass window. But you know what? Those rock stars, or movie stars, or me, we are all liars also. Each and every one of us; we want more. We want to be different. We want more, more, more, and more." "Me, I want to lead a normal fucking life." [Inhale, exhale, and the smoke wreathes about his head in a gray cloud.] "Everyone spends their time trying to convince everyone else that they are excited about life, or that they are happy. And they are fucking liars. Every day you turn on life, you are lied to." [He stares at the cigarette in his hands and shrugs.] "And what do you do when they stop lying to you? What do you do when the guy walks up to you on the street and tells you he hates his job, he hates the shrew he married, and he wishes he had aborted the fucking kids? You look at him in abject horror that he actually said what you are thinking. You think he’s crazy, that he’s drunk, or that he’s some kind of social psychopath." "But the truth is, he’s just you without the thin veneer of civility." [And now a slight smirk crossed Edward’s face.] "Because civility, and civilization, that’s the greatest lie of all. It’s when people stop being civilized that they are truly free of the lies, and can both see, and act on the truth." "And that, my friends, is exactly what I am going to do."
