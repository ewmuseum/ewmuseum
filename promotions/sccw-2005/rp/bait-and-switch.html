Bait and switch.

Date: once upon a time... The knock on the door echoed through the Monday abode with gruff veracity. As if fate itself were alerting the small family to the impending doom of what news the visitor would bring. Etching slowly to the door, August paused to look back at April, who clutched Adam to her hip out of comfort for herself more so than the toddler’s. At the door stood Detective Seth Renner, who’d be helping the Fib’s investigation into what the media were now calling the Right Hand Man Murders. He was, after-all, the first person to come in contact with the monster that was Brock Shepherd. And despite what the old cops would call to be their greatest instinct, his gut, the evidence never pointed at foul play and begrudgingly, he’d let the beast out of his cage and back into the world. A world which waited with baited breath for the monster to cull again. And again he did. And again. Again. And again. Renner stood peering over the shoulder of Special Agent Finn Roberts, the man heading up the investigation. Always dressed immaculately in the latest tailored suit from the Big Apple, Finn always seemed appropriately dressed for the task at hand. Vaguely, August recalled their meeting where he wore a black suit with a red pinstripe, designed as somewhat of a power suit, hoping to gain the upper hand in their conversation from the get go with a facade of power created solely by the suit’s aura. He had underestimated August’s inability to desire material possessions and it hadn’t given him an edge in either way. Today, he wore a gray suit, as if it would lighten the mood but the storm cloud which followed over his head, echoing in his eyes, told far more than any suit could on this day. Roberts' eyes pushed past the monster in the doorway, glancing down feebly at April like a child who'd promised not to touch the cookies and had broken the jar trying to get them. There were far worse things than a broken cookie jar on the horizon. 'What?' Monday grunted. 'Can I come inside?' 'You can say plen'y from here, Roberts.' 'I think it’s better if you sat down.' Finn echoed his words with a gesture for Monday to take them to the lounge. 'He here? He in Portland?' Roberts' eyes dropped momentarily and he waited no longer for invitation. He passed August heading directly to April, their eyes locked in a silent conversation where he was pouring his emotions out on the floor and trying to explain it all to her but there was nothing to explain. With Crime Scene Investigators working around the clock on any clue they could possibly muster they felt like they’d been sealed in a glass box looking out at their killer, without any way in the world of touching him. They knew who it was. They knew who was committing these atrocities. But they couldn’t do a thing about it. In this day and age, there was no grabbing their prime suspect and shoving him into an interrogation room, beating an admission out of him. Those days lived firmly in the past, despite Seth Renner praying for them to come back. Though, all that would be somewhat easier to worry about if they were able to find the shadow that continued to elude them. The only communications they’d had with Shepherd were the brutal crime scenes he’d left in his wake. Puddles of blood and handless women, left like a taunt to those who haunted his trails. They were chasing a riddle wrapped up in an enigma and every time they felt they were closer to their target, they were pushed farther away. 'April-' 'Is he here?' 'It’s hard to say.' 'What’s so fucking hard about yes or no, Finn?' Sighing into the palm of his hand, Roberts let his body fall into August’s armchair again. 'It’s not that easy. We’ve discovered a crime scene where the MO replicates other scenes we’ve attended which we believe Brock to be involved in.' 'English,' August demanded, exclaiming his words by folding his arms across his chest, presenting an impenetrable wall. 'Same scenes. Right hand removed. Evidence of penetration. No motive. Well... that might not be true.' April’s head tilted inquisitively. Ague’s snarl contorted with more ferocity. He did not like being left in the dark. Detective Renner paced the kitchen; like he had ideas brimming inside his head and if he were to stagnate himself they might blow the top right off. Finally, he paused to rest, palms flat on the kitchen’s bench and drawing weary breath, as if exhausted by his own thoughts. 'Doctor Veronica May was murdered in her in broad daylight on eighteenth of October. Middle of the afternoon.' 'Middle of the fucken day?' August repeated. Finn nodded. 'Her last known patient was an Adam Brock. We’ve since done our research to find that patient supplied the receptionist with a fake name. It’s not unusual for out of town folk to go to a private practice and pay with cash. Medical insurance isn’t necessary if they can pay over the counter-' 'Quit fucken us around, Roberts. Get to the point.' Finn sighed. 'We’ve checked up on security footage, August. There is CCTV footage of the parking lot leading into the building. We’ve had experts analysing the footage and we’ve come to believe that Adam Brock was Brock Shepherd.' A point in the horizon captured April’s attention. It was just a place she could focus on while the words struck her like venomous snake bites to her consciousness. Subconsciously, her hand pressed to her chest like she was trying to avoid her heart bursting out and flipping around on the floor like a fresh caught fish floundering for oxygen. 'April,' Special Agent Roberts called gently. Her special place disappeared and she replaced her view with Finn’s eyes. 'Yes.' 'Brock murdered that doctor right after she saw you. Right after your appointment with her.' Biting his lower lip, Finn pre-empted the repercussions. For some reason, he figured Augusts temper would get the better of him and any rights of Don’t shoot the messenger were lost on the behemoth. But the strike never came. Instead, August sat down. His leathery, weather-beaten face gaunt as the facts resonated. 'Right after?' he asked, in almost a whisper. Roberts nodded. 'The very next appointment. He would have seen April leave the waiting room.' 'She’d have noticed him,' interjected August. April’s head shook as the day’s events replayed in her mind. 'I wouldn’t have. I left. I was upset. I don’t like being upset in a public place so I kept my eyes to the floor. I never even paid on my way out. I just left.' Placing Adam on his feet, April’s arms wrapped around herself as she imagined being in the same room as the man who haunted her dreams and reality. A man she’d shared so much with. The father of her son. Adam’s daddy. The toddler rushed at his Pop, arms outspread and he was scooped up easily, turned and sat on the Portland Powerhouse’s knee. A veritable sight for any who knew the temper and power Monday possessed. A weakness and strength all at once. Right now, August tried with all his heart to be strong for the sake of his little family. A large, rugged hand smoothed the toddler’s hair as he thought, perhaps trying to comfort the toddler. But for the first time, in a very long time, August felt like he needed comfort himself. 'You know where he is?' Monday asked aloud. Renner looked up, a fire burning in his eyes. 'No. We don’t. We’ve been tryin' to chase the sonofabitch but we wind up just chasin' our tails lookin' like idiots.' 'Renner, that’s enough,' Roberts barked. Renner ignored him. 'This monster’s on the loose and I got reason to believe he’s comin' right for you, Miss Monday.' 'ENOUGH, RENNER!' Finn Roberts stared the stare that would evoke a thousand deaths onto the detective from Albany, trying to quench Renner’s opinion in its wake. 'NO, FINN! It’s NOT enough. These folks have got a right to know. We owe it to 'em.' 'I don’t want them being scared, Renner. Use your fucking brain.' Removing the plump toddler from his knee and sitting him on the sofa, Monday rose. An index finger as prodded the clavicle of Roberts with all the force of a man ready to snap. Roberts bit his lip to quench the idea of yelping. 'Listen here, you suited cunt. Who’re you to be the one who says what we deserve to know and what we don’t. We got a RIGHT to know this bastard's comin' after Ape. Don’t you think we deserve to know that?' 'I don’t think its right for you to let this monster frighten you.' 'I'LL BE THE ONE WHO SAYS WHAT THIS FAMILY CAN AND CAN'T BE SCARED OF, JERKOFF!' Huffing and puffing, ready to blow the house down, Monday stood with fists clenched for war. He turned to Detective Seth Renner. 'How do you think we should do this?' 'I’d go Man on Fire style. Eye for an eye. Tooth for a fucken too-' 'RENNER! You’re an officer of the law!' 'We ain't lookin' for no bag snatcher, Roberts. I’m done with your way. We’re huntin' a fucken monster, Finn. A monster! You’re computers and law books and badges don’t mean shit no more. We need to stop him before he strikes again.' Renner pointed across the room, directly at April Monday. 'Before he strikes her.' 'There is protocol. We will find him. We will find him and we WILL arrest him.' 'You ain’t done nothin' yet 'cept mop his fucken mess up.' August Monday’s massive frame fronted up to the Special Agent, who seemed to shrink as the general population began to turn into a mob ready to string him up. 'What’re you doin' to make sure he don’t get at my daughter?' A warm, blanketing feeling of security seemed to envelope April Monday as her father came to her rescue. She knew, that with all of Finn’s rules and regulations that come Hell or high water, if August Monday managed to get his hands on Brock before any of these coppers did there would be a problem no more. There was a silent promise from her father the day they’d met, she could see it in his eyes, that no matter what happened in their lives nothing would take them away from each other ever again. August would move mountains to ensure that happened. 'These things take time. We need to set-up so protection. I’d like to move April to a safe house.' 'No.' April stepped forward to have a say in her own future. 'That’s not happening. I won’t be away from my father or my son.' 'Then they both have to come.' Again, the Chip Off the Ol' Block shook her head. 'We’re professional wrestlers, Finn. We perform in front of thousands of people every time we go out to the ring. The chances of this happening are slim. There’s no chance Brock would be able to get to me in broad daylight.' Her own words echoed in her head. Roberts frowned, his face the picture of despair and lost hope. He didn’t want to lose someone under his watch. Not to the media frenzy which was beginning to pick up the pace. 'I got an idea.' Renner’s words broke the silence. All eyes fell on the detective as he came around from behind the bench, taking the stage to deliver his words to the awaiting ears before him. 'We could bait him.' His eyes flickered as they danced from face-to-face. 'Bait him?' repeated August. Roberts shook his head with certainty. 'Nuh-uh. No way, Renner. That’s ASKING for trouble.' 'What do you mean by bait him?' April asked softly. August never said a word. He simply listened and calculated in his own mind. 'He’s watchin' you. He’s got eyes on you. You’re the prize. I know it. You know it. He wants to get his hands on you. But ya see, April, there’s only one thing that’s stoppin' him from doin' it right now. Just one... thing.' Renner turned his head slowly, looking at the Raging Fear, who listened intently. In response, Augie ran his hand down his face, tugging at his beard thoughtfully. He could only muster a laugh of disbelief. 'Yer sayin' if I get outta the picture he might come for Ape?' Renner nodded. 'That’s what I’m sayin', August.' 'And who’s gunna be there to catch him when he comes?' 'It can’t be me.' Renner shook his head and pointed at his own face. 'He knows my mug.' Collectively, the trio all turned their eyes to the man suited in tailored fashion. The man who wanted no part in putting April on a platter. Special Agent Finn Roberts. 'No way. I’m not doing it. I’ll never agree to it.' April made her way across to the FBI Special Agent, her hand extended to take his. Without consciousness, his own hand extended and he accepted her. Gently, she stroked the outside of it and looked him in his eye, silently pleading. 'Finn, I need this to all be over with. I can’t bare the thought that any moment he could snatch me away. Take me from my son. From my father. I’m not strong enough to even think about that. I want this to be over. I NEED... this to be over.' Roberts shook his head. 'I’m not losing you. Not on my watch.' 'Nobody’s losing anybody.' She leant in and kissed his cheek. 'I trust you, Special Agent Roberts.' August stepped forward, the ferocity had departed his body language and he was the picture of pure calmness and composure. His meaty hand clasped on Finn’s shoulder. 'I trust you, too. For what it’s worth.' Roberts' eyes made the journey across to Detective Renner’s, who slowly nodded his head. Despite all their arguments there was a quiet respect between the pair. The understanding of law enforcement officers. The silent code where nobody wanted to lose somebody under their guard. Renner understood how Finn felt and he knew this needed to come to an end. Too many bodies were hitting the floor and it was only time before another innocent victim was lost to the Right Hand Man’s carnage. The media were having a field day with their latest serial killer. Roberts knew himself it was time for the headlines to come to an end. Meeting the questioning gaze of the three consenting adults in the room, Roberts nodded his head grimly, drawing breath as if to hold his heart in place around the lungs capturing it. His nod grew more confident the longer he held that breath in. 'Okay.' He expelled all at once. 'Let’s finish this.'
