Date: When I sleep When I was just a little girl I asked my mother, what will I be I had that dream again last night. The one which leaves be gasping for breath in the darkness. Leaves my chest feeling like an elephant's been sitting on it. Leaves me with the sting of unwanted tears in my eyes. Vinegar tears they almost feel like. They feel unnatural. Perhaps because I sob too hard when the dream happens. I hate the dream because it feels so real. It feels like it's not a dream but a premonition of the future. I wonder if it were a premonition and that I would get de ja vu when the day arises. Or if I'd just go through in slow motion, like everything I can see happening to me I've seen before. But there's nothing I can do about it. That all I can do is watch it all happen to me. Watch it all unwind again. To watch my own death. Will I be pretty, will I be rich Here's what she said to me. I think the creepiest thing about the dream is the Doris Day song I can hear. The one about when she's a little girl and asking questions about her future. The one about her mother telling her that her fate is sealed and she should live each day as it comes. I can never remember the name of that song. I sing it to people to see if they can help me remember it's name. Can never get the words right. I prove that I'm a mother in the way that every time I sing it someone corrects me for singing the wrong words. It's a mother's prerogative to get song lyrics wrong and make awful pop culture references 10 years behind their time. Que Sera is the name of the song. The song is just the lyrics being sang. Acapello? Occapella? I don't know how you say it but it is God awful frightening without any music behind it. It echoes through my brain into the darkest of it's cavities, living off the fear it's creating to feed itself. I used to like the song but now when I hear it... it sends shivers down my spine and I can feel my bladder flutter. Feel my blood run cold. The birds are chirping and the sun shines down in rays of magnifiscence. Picturesque and beautiful. I'm wearing a lovely blue sundress with little pink flowers on them. I'm not a flower kind of girl so I couldn't tell you what type they were. Although they are pretty flowers. For a strange and unapparent reason, I'm skipping through the green fields as the wind gently laps over the blades of grass, they dance in it's slight power. I stop, in my dream, and I seem to hover out of my body, floating behind me as if watching what I were seeing in a third person or something. Like I was watching over my own shoulder. Over the horizon there was not clear blues skies. There was dark grey clouds which threw lightning bolts toward the earth. Crashing into the ground with mushroom clouds of dirt and destruction. Amidst the destruction was the silhouette of a man. A man who came toward me. Slowly. As if he were stalking me. Like I were his prey. Yet all the same time not afraid to let me know of his intents and purposes. That there was danger ahead and he was the bringer. I would receive. When I was young, I fell in love I asked my sweetheart what lies ahead I try to move but I cannot. My feet feel firmly planted to the ground but I do not feel panicked. Just patient for what is to come. Like the me in the dream has had this dream, too, and know's what to expect. Knows the feeling that comes with her own death and is at peace with that feeling. I cannot help but wonder if I will be at peace when my time comes. If I will be this patient. Will we have rainbows, day after day Here's what my sweetheart said. He nears and I can hear her, or my, heart beating. It's erratic but it does not sound like it is panicked. It feels like it is beating faster due to the man coming. Our breathing heightens in expectancy... to almost a pant. Dreams have a tendency of melodramatically heightening the behaviours and feelings and our breathing almost reminds me of the lusty wolf from the cartoons. All that's missing is our jaw on the ground. It's a confusing feeling. Que Sera, Sera, Whatever will be, will be The future's not ours, to see Que Sera, Sera What will be, will be. He arrives to me in the dream and his face I recognise but every time I wake up I cannot remember for the life of me who he is. Who the man is standing before me. He carries a big plastic bag which is filled with the hands of other women. Some of them so dainty. Some of them so pretty. All of them cold and white and dead. In his other hand he carries a meat cleaver. It glimmers with the lightning which has now surrounded us. The explosions of it's contact on to the once dancing grass. The darkness of the clouds overhead. I am so calm I cannot breathe for the fear it generates in the real me. My dream me seems happy to see him. The real me is frightened to the state I fear I might relieve myself. Now I have children of my own They ask their mother, what will I be With his free hand he hits me across the back of the head and I fall. As I fall my clothes fall free of me and I fall to my back. He ravages me. The dream me seems to enjoy it yet the real me is frightened. He constantly brushes my face with the cleaver. I cannot believe the two different reactions. They almost suffocate me. I feel like screaming. I feel like waking up. But I can't. Part of me wants to stay in the moment. Part of my is curious to see how it ends. Part of me is enjoying it. It's the last part which frightens me so. Will I be handsome, will I be rich I tell them tenderly. When the man has finished the dream me has also. He rises above me, extending my right arm out and the cleaver comes down upon my wrist. Severing it cleanly. The blood does not trickle like it would in real life. It dances around the ground, swirling into a floral patterns and the dream me smiles as it does. The patterns are hypnotic as they form while I watch, floating above them. The man smiles and stands. Simply picking my hand up from the ground and placing it in the bag with the rest of his souvenirs. The moments he leaves it the moment when I rush to meet my dream body again. And that's when I feel the pain. That's when I feel the panic. I begin to scream and squirm. Writhing in agony as the man turns and walks away from me, still as naked as he was moments ago. I try to drag myself to my feet yet the blood loss is too dramatic. No longer does it swirl in floral patterns on the ground. It gushes and puddles, making it harder to attempt to gain a vertical base. Each time I try to stand I slip in my own blood. Coated in mud which had been created by my own vital fluids I try to drag myself after him, pleading him for help yet he never turns. He carries on his way with no regard for my being. The grass disappears and I am on a concrete floor. I look up and the man is confronted by my father. My father and another man in a cheap suit, smoking a cigarette with a gun pointed to the man's head. My father clutches Adam, who is wailing and peering into my eyes. Our eyes are locked and mine fills with tears. The tears soon swallow my vision and I am in darkness. The darkness feels like it lasts for an eternity and I struggle to wake myself up. I once heard that if you die in your dream you could die in real life. I never believed it but was always wary of an old wives tale as such. I always fight to wake myself. And I always wake. I wake right after I have died. Que Sera, Sera, Whatever will be, will be The future's not ours, to see Que Sera, Sera What will be, will be. Que Sera... Sera...
