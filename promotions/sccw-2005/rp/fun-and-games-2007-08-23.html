Fun And Games

23 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/08/07 "For I have sworn thee fair, and thought thee bright, who art as black as hell, as dark as night." William Shakespeare You will struggle to find a more beautiful and charming woman than Maria Lopez. She had everything a man, and even some women, could ever need. Each aspect of her had the potential to lure and attract. When she spoke, people listened, when she entered a room, people stared. It was irrelevant what she wore, high class cocktail dress or everyday clothing; she could still push the little boys out of their mind and send the curious girls into overdrive. Sometimes she didn’t have to try, it just happened. Bothersome at times but generally entertaining nonetheless. Sometimes they would get a little bit too involved and forget their place; she would always deal with that though and remind them who the boss was. All these feelings, such a waste of time and effort when all she wanted was to have a little fun. They soon got the message when they did misbehave and they would fall in line, they all did, it was just a question of when. She was such a thing of beauty. Everyone wanted her on their team, even those who already had a team. Her mind was powerful, sharp and intelligent, a mind that she knew how to use. She was no mannequin; she was a woman with an opinion and a strength that many men could only dream of having. Maria Lopez could put others in the shade as quickly as a click of the fingers; it had happened before and would happen again. Over and over again, the cycle would continue until it bored her enough. It was like a drug, an addiction that she craved and didn’t want to let go of. When one fell, another would be waiting around the corner then that one would drop and another take their place. She had no preferences; they were all as small and insignificant as each other. There was no harm in it, they all wanted her so it meant they got what they wanted, no matter how brief the experience might be. What’s wrong with having a hobby? She was an angel sent down from heaven above. The engine of the plane could be heard gently humming as it made its way towards its destination. Passengers stared vacantly at the screens in front of them, in flight entertainment dulling the brains. She had found her own form of amusement though as she sat with her legs slightly up, feet resting against the seat in front of her. To her left, a young couple in their early twenties no doubt heading for a romantic break or some other tedious activity. He had clearly been the college jock, muscular, good looking but undoubtedly nothing between the ears. As for her, she was semi-attractive but nothing to write home about. The ugly stick had left her be but she hadn’t been in the front of the queue for sex appeal. She had left for the bathroom and Maria prayed that she had gone to fix some kind of make up because by God she needed it. This wouldn’t even be a challenge. She’d already caught his eye on numerous occasions and this time was no different. Casually chewing her gum, she trailed her forefinger along her lip and then slipped it slowly inside her mouth. He was sat one row behind her and on the other side of the plane in the seat next to the aisle and his eyes had been wandering. She’d felt them running up her legs which were covered by black tights and a short black skirt leaving not a great deal to the fertile male imagination. In fact, she doubted that he had even got as far as her breasts, her cleavage struggling to keep within the confines of her blouse. With a smile, she sucked on her finger and then slowly withdrew it as she toyed with the gum wrapped around. He swallowed hard at the vision before and shuffled uncomfortably, she was getting to him so easily and she knew it. It was just a little harmless fun. The door to the toilet closed and footsteps could be heard coming from behind. Maria turned to face the seat in front of her, subtly taking the gum back into her mouth and setting her legs down. The boyfriend sat like a naughty schoolboy feeling as if he had been caught with his hand in the cookie jar, or in this case, Maria’s pants. A moment passed and to her surprise, Maria found the ‘Plain Jane’ girlfriend standing next to her seat. Looking up with an innocent expression and a polite smile, Maria pleasantly addressed the woman. "Hi there, is everything okay?" Of course it’s not okay, Maria had been spending as much time as she could get away with, entertaining Plain Jane’s boyfriend, there wasn’t much ‘okay’ about that. However Maria was smarter than that and had hidden it away brilliantly, better than the boyfriend who had desperately crossed his legs to hide the blatantly obvious. There was nothing more amusing than watching a man squirm, especially in front of his significant other. She was smiling on the inside as she looked up at the unsuspecting woman. "Yeah everything’s fine; I just wondered if you had a hairbrush, only I’m having a fashion disaster at the moment!" Reaching into the handbag by her side, Maria pondered whether this fashion disaster had only lasted for just the current moment, as the woman was about as inspiring as a piece of blank A4 paper. One dimensional, uninteresting and unoriginal, it would take more than a hairbrush to make her boyfriend stop looking at Maria. Still, doing work for charity is always in fashion and one good turn deserves another. Actually that was bullshit, Maria just enjoyed having fun and the couple had kept her entertained over the last couple of hours. She idly passed the hairbrush to the woman and smiled courteously. "I know what you mean, air travel plays havoc with your image doesn’t it?! She was being polite of course because she never had any issues with her image. Her hair was beautifully long and slightly curled at the moment, make up perfectly applied and her figure, well that spoke for itself. Maria Lopez was not outwardly familiar with the issues of a ‘normal’ woman, she had no time for it. She smiled to Plain Jane both out of fake friendliness and genuine satisfaction. Yes it had been easy but it had been fun too. As the woman handed out her heartfelt gratitude, Maria simply accepted and went back to her self-satisfied contemplation. This was just a training game, the main event was yet to come. Washington was looming. Watch out Paul. The Bitch is coming. How can something so beautiful be so dark? Paul held the ring between his fingers that had been left as a calling card in his locker room. He had spent the last few minutes just staring at it, hypnotized almost. Then with a frustrated grunt, he hurled it across the room and it struck the wall and dropped to the floor. He held his head in his hands as he paced up and down. Two consecutive defeats were weighing on his mind, not because he believed he was incapable of playing the game anymore but because he knew SHE was responsible. He could feel her working her magic on him, a power that only she had. The scent of her perfume had seeped into his system and poisoned his mind with anxiety and paranoia. It had made him feel sick to his stomach and pushed him to his mental limit. The sight of the ring was like facing a ghost and he knew she had been there. Only she could have had that gift he had bought her and only she could have returned it. Maria Lopez was too close to him, too close to his life and it was driving him insane. He twirled around and slammed his fist down on the desk by his side. A moment’s silence after was then broken by a deep voice. "She’s winning, Paul." Damien Kahn entered the room and leaned against the study wall. Their story was another shady element of Paul Cain and his past. Despite his criminal past, Damien had always been a loyal friend and he hadn’t forgotten the vengeance Paul had served on those who had taken Damien’s brother from him. While Kahn was more into the physical pleasure of violence, Cain was the brains of the operation and had managed to snare the little group that had tormented Damien without ever once having blood on his hands. Now it was Damien who was desperate to ensure that Paul didn’t lose his composure and didn’t revert back to the ways that had twisted him and made him something he had left far behind. "You’re letting her get to you, she’s trying to get under your skin man, you can’t let her, not now, not after all this time." Paul was leaning down hard on the desk, his knuckles turning white and his breathing heavy. "You don’t know her like I do." Damien turns and picks up the ring from the floor, tossing it up in the air and catching it in his hand, closing it into a fist. "I know enough." Scoffing heavily at the ignorance shown by Damien, Paul looks over his shoulder towards his friend. "You don’t have a fucking clue." Damien’s tongue rubs against the inside of his cheek as he attempts to control his temper at the irritated Cain. Nodding slowly, Damien approaches Paul and shrugs his shoulders. "So your ex girlfriend is a bitch? Big fucking deal, join the rest of the world. You already got one bitch waiting for you in Washington, Aimz and she ain’t going easy on you. Pride’s waiting on you, Sterling who owes you, the likes El Diablo, Rhine, all of these people have you in their targets and they WILL take you if you ain’t on your game, man. You are LETTING her screw with you, your career, your life, ALL of it. Get a fucking grip!" Paul turns his back to Damien and approaches the window, his head a mess and mind in turmoil. The feelings were all too familiar; this was the effect she could have. She’d having you eating out of the palm of her hand like a little bird, then she’d grab you around the throat and crush you, leaving you broken. It was all a game of puppet on strings, nobody could truly understand unless the game had been played with them. He could feel it happening again, she wanted her revenge and she was going to do whatever it would take to get it. For now, it was just him but soon it would be FUSE, the fans, his wife, family, they would all become puppets. Everything was out of his hands, he had no control over any of it and she held all the cards. "It’s not as easy as that." Enough was enough. Damien stormed towards the window and shoved Cain to one side, forcing it open so the night air whisked into the room. "It’s as easy as this!" With that, Damien threw the ring with fury out of the window and watched it plummet down to the ground three floors below. He cast a frustrated glance in Paul’s direction, who just stood and stared at the window. "So now what, Paul? Huh? Now what?!" Everybody has a choice in their life, the fight or flight concept. Do you go into battle or do you run like a coward? Washington was calling Paul to go to War and war it would be. Fight for your pride, fight for your very career and then fight your inner demons. Confront the person you fear the most, the one that holds the power that controls your very being. Stand up to them, don’t let them push you around and manipulate you. They’re only as powerful as you allow them to be. She will destroy your world if you give her the tools to do so. This is your time Paul, this is what you got back into the business for, don’t let it be taken from you. If she comes be ready, if she doesn’t, be ready. What now? It’s an easy question now. "What now? I enter the Tower." A beaming smile appeared on Damien’s face and he nodded his approval. "Damn right son, damn right!" Paul then turned to face Damien with an expression that Kahn had not seen for a long time. A time that hadn’t been long enough ago. "Then when the Tower’s been left behind, I’m going to do something I should have done a long time ago." With his hands jammed into his pockets, Paul walks away with a step indicating a purpose. Damien’s face suddenly goes ashen as he recalls the expression so very well. "Paul. Paul. Paul!" Meanwhile, outside the family home, Paul’s wife stands admiring the ring that had dropped near to the trashcan that she had gone to empty a few moments after it had landed. She doesn’t recognize it, it’s not hers and she suddenly gets a queasy feeling inside her stomach. Something isn’t right, something really isn’t right. And so… The War in Washington begins.
