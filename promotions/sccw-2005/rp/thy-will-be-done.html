Thy Will Be Done

Date: Today For those of you who believe Phillip Kennedy a simpleton...consider the following correspondence, having taken place over the course of six or seven weeks.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dear Lindsay Troy, My name is Phillip Kennedy. You may remember me (not so fondly) from the Cataclysm pay-per-view, in which I was one of Alexandra Pierce's goons. Things change. Last episode of Temptation, I smacked her in the back of the head with a steel chair and assaulted Amy Campbell's husband. I'm probably in the shit right now, but I'll cross that bridge when I come to it. I'm coming to you for advice on a subject that no one in SCCW can really help me with. It concerns my agent, David Walter Smith. Once upon a time, I know that he competed with you in both the fWo and in PRIME. He has told me that he was injured severely in PRIME, but best I can tell, his rehab is over, and he's walking about fit as a fiddle. I would be an idiot to not look at every possibility in a potential war with Alexandra. David is a monolith of a man, and if I could talk him into doing battle with Desade, I would have a staunch ally in the ring as well as out. My question to you is simple: What do I need to know about him? He is very quiet when it comes to his career, and doesn't talk much. I would imagine that he would have returned to the ring without prompting somewhere once healed, but he doesn't seem to have done so. He is a logical man. I doubt I can convince him to return without a reason, and for a reason, I need to know the history that he won't tell me. The man doesn't even have a Wikipedia page. That is most confusing to me. Any help you could give me would be greatly appreciated, Your Highness. - Phillip Kennedy ~~~~~~~~~~~ Mr. Kennedy, Kennedy. (Yes, I had to.) I was looking forward to addressing my reply to you as something less savory ('Scum of the Earth' came to mind immediately). However, upon receiving confirmation that you, in fact, did appear at PRIME HQ to deliver this letter in person (and won't your employer just look favorably on that, might I add?), and upon taking the time to do a bit of research into the events you described previously, the only harm I see in my telling you about David is if David finds out. If he does, I'm sure he'll be more angry with me than he will be with you. Why I am even taking the time to tell you all of this, I don't know. Maybe it's because I can appreciate a good chair shot. As I'm sure you're already aware, David entered fWo under the name of Logic some years ago. He joined up with Craig Miles and the Craig Miles Professional Heel Academy (word of warning: I wouldn't go contacting Miles looking for info on David, mainly because he's a dickbag and won't help you), where he would eventually earn a degree in Heel Trigonometry. Personally, I think this 'degree' is bullshit. You don't need to join a stable to learn how to talk down to others and hit people hard. Oh wait... I digress. I first ran into David, quite literally, backstage one night after he suffered a loss. I remember him being upset to the point of tears, which were out in full force. Myself, I was concerned with other matters; most importantly, the matter of why fWo's parent company Disney thought it wise to swindle $250,000 out of a deal they struck with my and my sister's company prior to my signing with them. I was in no great mood to play comforter, but the big lug didn't look as if he was ready to turn off the waterworks, so I gave him some advice and went on my merry. Unfortunately, I seemed to have made an impression on the then-young and highly-impressionable David, as he would develop a crush on me over the next few months. At the time, I was part of the tag team named Silver and GOLD with Sonny Sil-- (This is where our announcer Richard would interrupt and say 'MISTER SILVER, FORMER SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT LIAISON OF PRIME!') --ver, born out of mutual hatred for Disney and what the higher-ups were doing to the both of us. For Sonny, they were screwing him out of a shot at the World Title. For me, they were screwing with my family, which seems to be a never-ending constant in my life. Since Sonny and I swindled the team of Fatal Attraction (Disney Darlings, they were) out of the tag belts, he and I decided to just piss people off as much as we could, however we could, until someone upstairs decided to stop acting like gigantic fuckbags against us. For my part, I used David's affections towards me against him, softening him up emotionally and mentally so that when the time came to defend the belts against him and Doctor Curiosity, he wouldn't be in a fundamentally sound state to compete at one hundred percent. This is something I regret doing, but this wasn't the first time I went down this road in my career. It was a power-play maneuver your girlfriend Katsidy would have been proud of, only she would have fucked him too, I'm sure. I never took it any farther than flirtation. David did extract some measure of revenge for my trick and has, understandably, hated me since. To your question of his injury, it occurred during a match with a former fWo wrestler, Crucifix, who joined PRIME for a short period of time. Crucifix (aka David Cross)'s face had been horribly disfigured not once, but twice, throughout the course of his life. The first time, I have no knowledge of. The second time was after he took a shotgun blast to the face while trying to save his, and my, pupil Alex Creed from his father 'Kodiak' Vic Creed. No relation to Sabretooth. Crucifix had been wearing a mask to cover his injuries, and finally had enough money to hire a doctor to repair his face from the trauma he suffered from Vic. But David wanted an answer to a challenge he set forth and Crucifix didn't want to accept. He only went to the PRIME show that night to give an in-person resignation to CP Cantrell, because the chance for a normal life after this surgery was an opportunity that he didn't want to pass up. It was taken from him by a Proof Positive and a grinding boot to the face. Crucifix would take twelve months from David's wrestling career as a result of this, at Colossus last year, thanks to a heel hook. I have not heard from him since. My attempts to locate him have failed, and Alex has no idea where he is either. The two of us have tried hospitals, wellness clinics and mental facilities both in the States and in Japan, to no avail. How all of this information will help you convince David to take part in your vendetta against the Marquis, I don't really know. I'm sure something in here will strike a chord for you to use to your advantage. I wish you luck with that. And since your letter has piqued my interest, be advised that you may have two keen hazel eyes trained on your progress as you venture forward in this endeavor. Nemo me impune lacessit. -The Queen PS: David doesn't have a Wikipedia page because Sonny probably wrote nasty, anonymous things about him on there. ---------------------------------- David Walter Smith was generally used to being the 'idea man' in his endeavors. Whether wrestling or working in his capacity as an agent, he came up with the ideas, the plans, the documents. He then presented them to others for approval. He was used to this. Pretty secure in that. Hell, if you were a card-carrying member of Mensa, you probably would be too. It wasn't, therefore, particularly strange that Mr. Smith was slightly worried when he'd gotten a note from his star (okay, technically his only) client, Phillip Kennedy, that 'things had changed.' Carrying a briefcase with him, David arrived at the location he had been given, to find that it was, of all things, some sort of gym. Still, Smith wasn't too confused. Perhaps Kennedy had been working out, after all. Stepping inside the small building, David quickly spotted Mr. Kennedy standing in the middle of a...wrestling ring. Phillip bore an odd, unreadable smile on his face. 'Welcome, big guy,' Kennedy grinned. 'Come on, hop in. We need to talk.' Phil patted the turnbuckle, as if the squared circle had somehow become a hot sportscar. David's expression remained neutral, even if he *was* starting to wonder what this was all about. Placing his briefcase down on the ring apron, Logic crawled up to the ring apron, using the ropes to steady himself. It was, he suddenly realized, the first time he'd done that in about a year. His muscle memory continued as he stepped between the ring ropes, and was suddenly looking down at his client in the center of the ring. 'Alright, David,' Phillip began, 'we're going to have a nice exchange here. You're going to agree to do something for me, and in exchange, I'm going to agree to do something for you.' 'I must admit,' Smith replied, 'that I do not like conversations that begin this way. Nonetheless, I will hear you out. What is it that you wish of me?' Phillip reached up, placing one hand on his agent's mammoth shoulder. Before David realized what was happening, his own hand shot to Phil's opposite shoulder, and each man placed a hand on the other's elbow. In the wrestling ring, the action was nigh automatic; most matches started with it: the collar and elbow tieup. Struggling a bit with David's size, Kennedy smiled up at the bigger man. 'This is what I want. You, in the ring.' The tieup was abruptly broken. 'No,' was David's only word, face remaining impassive, all the emotion that Phillip imagined to be behind the refusal simply not present. 'You didn't even listen to hear what I'm going to offer you in return,' Kennedy stated, matching the serious look on his agent's face. 'And don't even tell me you're injured. I've seen you walking around the past few months.' The easy excuse was gone for David. 'I have no desire to step into a ring again,' the agent said, though his usual conviction was ever-so-slightly lacking. Months ago, Phillip Kennedy would have left it at that. But months of hanging around Kathryn Shaw and Lauren Fox, coupled with his own poker-honed ability to read people, allowed Phillip to read his agent that much better. 'So, you want the last image of your career to be a masked guy heel hooking you out of the sport?' David Walter Smith's left hand clenched into a fist. 'I...deserved what happened to me,' was his only reply. 'I probably deserve worse for some of the things I've done,' Phillip shrugged. 'But that's no reason for you to not wrestle again. If it truly bothers you, get in the ring and do something for the side of the angels.' 'Wrestling has brought me little but sadness and pain, of the physical and emotional varieties. I would be a fool to continue. Once, I did consider stepping back into the ring once my wounds healed,' he admitted, 'but now, I assure you, this will be the only ring that I stand in.' It was difficult for Phillip to believe that the man before him had finished third in the fWo Survivor IV contest. Difficult to believe that he had utilized a move dangerous enough that it had once been banned to protect the promotion's star from potentially being crippled. 'So, you're content to have your legacy being a hopeless infatuation with the woman voted Most Likely to Go Into Fetish Porn, David? I highly doubt that.' Anger flashed across David Walter Smith's eyes, and his face contorted into a grimace. 'Why are you doing this, Phillip,' he asked, a hurt tone in his voice. 'Have I not done a good enough job as your agent? Why would you demand more of me?' Phillip's eyes met those of his agent, the Gateway Champion's gaze not yielding a single inch. 'You have been an excellent agent,' he stated, 'but right now, I don't need an agent for what I have planned. I need a warrior. I need the man that I saw in fan-made videos on Youtube damn near shatter necks. I need six feet nine inches of calculated power. I need three hundred pounds of reasoned intensity. I need Logic, David.' If David closed his eyes, he could still hear the roars of crowds in his ears. Could still see himself standing on the top rope at PRIME UltraViolence 2008, about to leap off onto a prone Killean Sirrajin, the latter man stretched out on the announce table... '...the answer is still likely no, Phillip. But I will at the very least humor you. Why do you need me to step into the ring so badly?' Of course, Phillip couldn't explain that without revealing more than he'd like. Still... 'I,' the Las Vegas Lariat proclaimed, 'am going to tell you what I would have told you had you agreed to help. I am going to do this for two reasons. One, you are my agent, and you should probably know. Two, once I tell you, I think you're going to be more willing to help me out.' David took a step backwards, and then another, until he was resting in the corner. 'This must be an interesting story, if you believe it that important.' 'Oh, it is,' Phillip replied. 'You know how you were wondering exactly what Alexandra Pierce and her merry band of misfits were? I know.' Even practice can't keep the look of surprise of David's face. 'You do? How did you come across such information?' 'Kathryn told me,' Phillip replied, immediately. 'You saw the show, I'd imagine. What you saw at the beginning are more or less the battle lines in SCCW. It's me, Kathryn, and Lauren against Alex, Amy, and Lane.' 'Tell me, Phillip,' Smith said, voice tinged with curiosity. The neutral expression on the agent's face was gone, and the big man leant into the conversation. 'What is she? What are they?' 'Apparently,' Kennedy began, the story sounding slightly ridiculous no matter how much conviction he put into it, 'they're part of an organization that strikes a balance between good and evil in the world. Yeah, I know, it sounds like a load of shit, but I can tell when Kathryn's bullshitting me. She wasn't.' Phillip expected David to laugh. The big man did nothing of the sort. 'Probably some sort of secret society,' David muttered, seriousness in his voice. 'I was afraid of something like that.' 'You were?' Smith's laugh was without mirth. 'I was, yes. Alexandra Pierce is far too coordinated, far too intelligent to be merely a wrestler. I knew she was *something* else, the question was what. And now, you seem to have told me. Another question, then. How dangerous are they?' Suddenly, Phillip got the sinking feeling that, before this, he really only had in the presence of members of the once-Dead Man's Hand. The feeling that the person he was talking with knew something of import that he didn't. 'From what Kat told me...they're all capable killers. Lauren, Kat, Alex...that's why she came clean to me, actually,' Philip said. 'Said she was afraid Alexandra might try to exact vengeance on me outside the ring.' David's face reverted to its usual stony nature. 'If she wanted to, I would imagine that she could succeed easily. No offense, Phillip, but if she is a trained killer...' 'No, I know, David,' Kennedy replied. 'So far, she seems content to take me on within the confines of wrestling. She seems to enjoy it. How did Kat put it...it's a nonlethal way for her to pit herself against rivals, some shit like that.' David looked at his charge. 'You would be a chess grand master, then,' he asked, and suddenly, he understood. 'Yeah,' Kennedy drawled, 'and I'm gonna need some pieces to put on the board. A 6'9' one would be nice. And no, you're not going to be needed to wrestle often. I just need you on call. Because right now, I've got some plans to put into motion this week on Temptation. And you're the man to be planned for.' Heaving a deep sigh, David nodded. He knew the thrill that came from matching oneself against a capable opponent. Usually, though, he controlled the pieces. Phillip was asking him to become one. 'Alright, David. I will go along with your plot. Within reason, of course.' With a clap of his hands, the Las Vegas Lariat walked over to the Thinking Man's Grappler. 'Excellent. So, what are your thoughts about Craig Maloof?' This, Logic decided, had the potential to be a long day.
