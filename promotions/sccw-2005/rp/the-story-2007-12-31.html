The Story

31 December 2007

Date: 12/31/2007 'I don't know if you guys know this but I'm sort of a big deal.' - Phil Brooks Part I - The Story VON DOOM has been driving around Las Vegas for a few days trying to figure out what the hell he was going to do with his wrestling career. Today he’s driving his blue Volkswagon Beetle to run some errands but still can’t get what happened out of his head. His best friend Art is there with him in his car riding in the passenger seat as VON DOOM drives to the Wal-Mart down the street. Art’s been really bored here lately and is sick of his crazy girlfriend acting psychotic. So now he’s hanging out with VON DOOM to get away from the bitch. "Ok, tell me again why you’re so pissed," Art says a little confused. VON DOOM sighs and starts to retell the story he had told earlier. "Here it goes," he replies to Art. "I went to wrestle on an indy show as a favor to the promoters of my main promotion to hype up our big show in the next big town. So I get there and the booker wants me to be part of some story for the match." "And how is that a problem?" Art asks, a little confused. "I mean, I don’t get how it could be a problem." "Why in the hell do matches have to have a story?" he asks in a sarcastic tone. "I mean, seriously, come on. I’m not an official member of their esteemed federation and have no role in the storyline so it would be stupid for me to jump into it now just for one fucking show. And what’s the point of having a story anyway? Matches should have no story. It should just be two guys beating the living hell out of each other and trying to steal the show, not this overly dramatic bullshit that goes on now days in sports entertainment." Art starts laughing his ass off over this as VON DOOM stops for a red light. "This is fucking great dude," he says as VON DOOM gets a devious little smirk on his face. "What else did you say?" "Stories are for pussies," he says with a devious grin as Art starts laughing. "I think matches should just be that. Matches, not stories. I want to just go out there and wrestle, not be a part of some lame ass story that the fans aren’t into just to put the rub on some rookie who gave the booker a blowjob just to get a spot." Art’s laughing hysterically now. "What did the guy say?" VON DOOM grins and replies, "He basically threw a temper tantrum and told me I would never work in that territory again. He then said I was the most disrespectful wrestler he’s ever met." "And what did you do?" Art asks with a grin knowing full well VON DOOM probably went busto on the guy. "Well here’s what happened," VON DOOM replies. "I went out to do the match but instead of doing the match, I took the microphone and said ‘Fuck this shit, I’m gone.’" "What happened next?" Art asks. "I left," VON DOOM replies. "You left?" Art asks, a bit confused. "Meaning you just left the ring?" VON DOOM nods his head. "Yes, I just left the ring and headed out of the building," he replies as he starts driving as the light turns green. "Did anyone try to stop you?" Art asks. VON DOOM grins deviously before replying. "As I headed down to the exit," he continues. "My opponent came out and tried to attack me but I beat the shit out of him! He swung at me, I ducked, and then came around and nailed him in the back of the neck with a forearm shot! BAM! His ass goes down!" Art’s laughing hysterically as VON DOOM continues with the story. "So then some other jobber indy noobs come out," he says with a smile. "And they try to attack me for beating the shit out of their friend. So naturally I kick their asses too. So now there’s four of their top guys down on the ground and the officials come out to try and get me out of the arena and to the back. Once I got there, it wasn’t pretty." Art starts grinning from ear to ear knowing that it’s going to get even better. "So I go to the backstage area," he continues. "And there’s the booker looking at me all pissed with his hands on his hips. So he comes over to me and starts getting in my face, yelling and cussing. So then I punched him in his big fat fucking head." Art looks at him like he’s crazy but is still laughing over this. "So you pulled a Montreal?" he asks with a chuckle. VON DOOM nods his head. "God damn right I did!" he replies. "That bastard had it coming too. Nobody talks down to me like that fucktard did. So I punched him in his big, fat, ugly jaw and sent his ass down to the floor." "Why did you do that?" "Because his stupid ass deserved it for being so naive," VON DOOM replies. "The stupid moron thought that I would just do whatever he wanted. Well fuck that. Shit! First off his story idea was stupid as hell and lame. There should be no story for a match. Psychology is different than story. What he wanted was for the guy to come down and get involved in a sneak attack when the ref is distracted by me." "How were you going to distract the ref?" "By acting like something poked my eye." "Like what? A penis or something?" "No you moron, a finger from an eye poke." "So how was he going to get attacked?" "By a Tonya Harding pipe to the leg." "That’s lame." "Same thing I said. I told him it was a really fucking stupid idea and that no one would buy it because it was a stupid, lame, southern wrestling-eque nineties crap storyline idea that was used back in ninety-four." Art’s really laughing after that one. "I remember that! What did the guy say?" "He was like ‘You have no idea what you’re talking about. You’re just a stupid wrestler who’s lost one too many brain cells." "And what happened after that?" "I yelled out what he said about wrestlers not having any brain cells to get everyone pissed at him and not me. Then I said that this retard wants something people will be talking about to happen so that’s what I gave them." Art’s laughing again. "You did something bad, didn’t you?" VON DOOM nods his head. "God damn right I did," he replies with a grin. * * * Part II - What Happened... 'Tonight, we pull something out that the wrestling fans haven't seen in...two weeks.' - Kevin Nash Reading, Pennsylvania Independent Pro Wrestling of Pennsylvania and the Other Old Territories VON DOOM is backstage at an independent wrestling show that he agreed to appear on as a tune-up match for the Encore debut show. Besides, he needs a workout by beating up some small time local jobber before he beats up on the big time wrestlers in a few days. He’s looking around and seeing a whole bunch of wrestlers he’s never heard of before. Some of them know him but he doesn’t have a clue who the hell they are. He can tell this by the pointing they’re doing in his direction. "Fucking noobs," he groans as he heads toward the area where all the officials are just standing around. The officials look over and see him coming. A few of them roll their eyes where others giggle. "So who am I working with?" VON DOOM asks with a smile as they all start looking away as if trying not answer or pay attention to him. The head booker smiles and replies, "We’re planning on having you work with this green rookie named Miles Charger. He’s pretty green but can work a match. We’re doing this storyline with him and this other guy on our roster named Martian Mike." VON DOOM raises an eyebrow and puts his hands on his hips. His mind is starting to think about what stupid ideas they will try to use during the match. "Ok, what the hell is the plan for the match?" he asks in a growling tone. The head booker smiles, almost enjoying the thought about VON DOOM’s reaction to what he’s going to tell him. "The plan is this," the booker says with a grin. "We’re going to have you come out and do your thing. Then Charger is going to come out and then he’s going to act all pissed off. During the match, you’re going to tell the story of..." VON DOOM puts his hand up and stops him right there. "Why in the hell do matches have to have a story?" he asks in a sarcastic tone. "I mean, seriously, come on. I’m not an official member of your esteemed federation and have no role in the storyline so it would be stupid for me to jump into it now. And what’s the point of having a story anyway? Matches should have no story. It should just be two guys beating the living hell out of each other and trying to steal the show, not this overly dramatic bullshit that goes on now days in sports entertainment." Seeing that the booker is getting pissed, he continues on with his rant. "Stories are for pussies," he says with a devious grin. "I think matches should just be that. Matches, not stories. I want to just go out there and wrestle, not be a part of some lame ass story that the fans aren’t into just to put the rub on some rookie who gave the booker a blowjob just to get a spot." The booker looks at him and gets really pissed. "You listen to me you little asshole," he spouts out with a growl. "Nobody gives a shit about you and your opinion, understand? Nobody cares. I’m the big, bad motherfucking wolf around here and you’re nothing more than one of the scared little pigs. So get your shit together because you’re on in two minutes!" VON DOOM gets pissed and walks away to go think of a plan. He patiently waits for the two minutes and then his crappy indy intro theme starts playing as he walks into the building. He can tell that it’s by the Used but doesn’t know the song title because he doesn’t listen to emo or whatever it is these teenagers in tight pants listen too now days. He heads down to the ring and climbs inside. In the ring, VON DOOM has the mic and this is not looking good for anyone. The music cuts out and he’s ready to open his mouth, which more often than not creates chaos and madness. "Fuck this shit, I’m gone," VON DOOM says as he throws down the mic and exits the ring. The crowd doesn’t know what to think as VON DOOM heads to the back as Mike Charger comes out. Charger, of course, is pissed and tries to shoot on VON DOOM. VON DOOM ducks as Charger tries to nail him with a punch to the head. He then turns around and punches Charger in the back of the neck! The crowd loves it as Charger falls down and VON DOOM is laying the boots to him. Some other green wrestlers come out to break it up and VON DOOM punches both of them down when they come over. The crowd is even more into it now and is actually cheering for VON DOOM to beat the shit out of the newcomers because they suck and the crowd hates them. Mike Martian then comes out for his part in the angle as VON DOOM grabs a chair from ringside. VON DOOM then swings the chair at the unprepared Martian and nails him right in the head with a sickening that echoes throughout the arena! The officials finally get VON DOOM to the back and the booker comes over. "What the FUCK was that?!" he screams at VON DOOM. "You stupid son of a bitch! You’ll never work in this town again! You and your stupid antics have cost me a lot of money! You are without a doubt the worst wrestler that I’ve ever seen!" At this point, VON DOOM punches him in his big fat head like he’s been thinking about for about the past ten minutes or so. He then heads to his locker room and goes inside. VON DOOM quickly snatches up his bag and heads out of the locker room. One of the guys tries to confront him on the way out. "Hey dumbass," he mutters as VON DOOM punches him in the head before he can try anything. The local wrestler goes down to the ground with a thud as VON DOOM simply walks over him like nothing happened. He then walks past the fallen booker and heads right out the door to exit the building. VON DOOM then goes right to his rental car and opens the door. He throws his bag in and shuts the door. He turns the ignition, starting the car, and then drives out of the parking lot as fast as he can. He could care less about getting paid because to him, bullshit like this isn’t worth getting paid for. But it’ll be worth it when the video shows up on Youtube by the end of the day so he can watch it over and over again. Until then, however, its just him and the open road until he gets to the airport to go to the big show. Hopefully it turns out better than this...
