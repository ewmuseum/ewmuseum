The redhead she'd woken up with (she vaguely remembered being curled protectively around her) didn't move, though her brows quirked with discomfort, hand dropping to her side. ''Shit,'' Pierce muttered, her already soft voice almost a hiss. She swung her legs off the hard bed, easing slowly to her feet. Stumbling into the motel room's tiny bathroom, Alex closed the door firmly behind her, turning the hot water faucet all the way up. She bent over the sink, leaning hard on the porcelain. Beads of sweat formed on her brow. When Alexandra looked up into the narrow mirror on the wall, her reflection sneered at her, straightening her immaculate blazer with a sharp tug. ''Just what,'' Desade asked Alex archly. ''Do you think you are doing?'' A whimper caught in her throat. ''I... I don't...'' ''Contraction.'' Her reflection arched an irritated brow under the curtain that shielded her gaze. ''I do not know.'' Alex's whisper was weak, almost fully swallowed. ''That is an unacceptable answer. Amelia Campbell is a weapon -- no more than a tool. She is but a gun in your hand and only a fool fucks her firearm.'' ''But...she could be more.'' Her mirror image waved off the thought with a flick of her fingers. ''Ridiculous. Have you even thought this through? Of course you have not. Do you recall why you forbade Kathryn from seeking vengeance against her or Markson? I know you do.'' ''She...she is...'' ''She is loyal to him. All you have served to do today is to make the inevitable rift that much wider. Pawns like Kennedy may be able to be moved across the board with emotion, but your hold over Campbell is too tenuous. Make her choose between you and she will choose badly.'' ''I...'' She dipped her head, bangs falling, unable to meet the gaze of the woman in the mirror. ''Must everything be about choices and manipulation?'' When she dragged her eyes back to the mirror, it wasn't even Desade she was looking at, though she knew who it was. With perfectly coiffed gray hair and gold-framed, cat's-eye spectacles (not to mention the perpetual moue of disappointment on her lips), Pierce could not hope to miss her mentor, Margaret Winters. ''Look at my scared little Rabbit, cold and alone,'' the old lady whispered. ''I knew you were weak.'' ''No, I'm not -- '' Pierce felt a warm tingle on her cheek, the ghostly memory of too many open-handed slaps against her flesh. ''You will speak as I have taught you, Rabbit.'' ''Fuck you, Maggie. You cannot order me around any longer. I killed you.'' ''You did, because I was weak. Because I had a care for your feelings about your daughter.'' Alex shook her head feverishly, unkempt mane waving. ''No. Because you would not give me the choice to raise her.'' ''What would you have been? A homemaker? A fucking housewife? You were meant for more.'' The surface of those unyielding stones she watched the world through dampened. ''I would have been happy.'' The desperation was palpable in her voice. ''Happiness is a chemical reaction designed to perpetuate behavior that is likely beneficial. You cannot tell me you were not happy when you ended Jonathan Rhine.'' ''Yes, but...'' She looked over her shoulder to the form sprawled across the queen-sized bed. ''It isn't the same feeling.'' The third contraction drove the specter of Margaret Winters away, but it didn't restore Alex's image. Instead, the tight, neon purple dreadlocks and impish grin of her protégée, Lauren Fox, stared back at her. ''You're going to ruin everything,'' Savant-in-the-mirror promised. ''Everything we've worked for will be torn apart. You know, a great woman once told me that love is a distraction and lust a virus. You told me that. Are you telling me you lied -- or that you're somehow different?'' ''But I want...'' ''It doesn't matter what you 'want', it only matters what you can get. Want is a weakness; it's a hole that someone can dig into to bring you down. Need is a handicap. It's much better to deal with the cost and the benefit. Is she worth Malice and Mayhem? Is she worth you being considered weak? She could cost you everything.'' ''Stop quoting my own lessons at me -- that is just rude.'' ''These are your own words you're ignoring, your lessons going unheeded. I believe in you, but more than that, I believe in Desade. Don't disappoint me.'' ''Don't disappoint me.'' The mirror's face morphed to Drusilla Devonshire's, starting with her triangular, Cheshire grin and spiraling outward. ''I will burn this world down around you. I'll turn Mayhem loose on her and she'll breathe through a fucking tube. You know it's true; you know I will. And her blood will be on your hands. I know you think me cruel, maybe a little mad. But it will be kinder than what...'' The change of the reflection from Drusilla back to Desade to complete the circle was easier than the others, but Alex wouldn't acknowledge her similarities to the Little Troublemaker. Still, the frigid smile painted on her mirror-opposite's face chilled even the Director's blood. ''It will be kinder than what you do,'' her duplicate warned. ''If you open yourself up to her, if she breaks your heart, you will leave her in shambles. You will be ruthless. You will be cruel.'' ''It might not come to that.'' Alexandra was no fool; she knew far too well what she was capable of for her to deny it. ''It will. She is too willful, too independent. You cannot make her happy.'' ''Maybe I don't care!'' All thoughts of proper English fled as Pierce lashed out. ''Not everything can be plotted out according to diagrams. We fit together, we work.'' Her reflection didn't flinch at the outburst. ''Will you still 'work' after she learns you conspired to get her back on her greatest vice? Or will she lash out; fight you with every fiber of her being? You're not a dedicated lover, Alex -- you are a monster, a snake, and a traitor. Every kiss between you poisons her further; you corrupt her with your presence. There is no future for this but ruination. She will destroy everything you are, you will destroy everything she is, and then you two will destroy each other. Perhaps the world will be better -- '' ''NO!'' Alex punched the mirror and the world shattered into broken glass. She found herself in bed again, her arms wrapped around a soft form that she knew instinctively was Amy. ''Chéri?'' Pierce whispered. ''Chéri, wake up.'' She ran her hand down Amy's side, smiling soft. The hand came back red and it came back wet. Frantically, Pierce tossed back the sheet. The bed was soaked in blood -- more than there should have been and more than the human body held. Weakly, Campbell strained to look over her shoulder. ''A-Alex?'' she croaked. ''How could you do this to me?'' Alex fruitlessly pressed her hand to the wound, closing her eyes. When they opened, she was wrapped around Amy again, the sun streaming through a crack in the curtains. Amy groaned, rolling away from the light. Desade just held the Red Raver as she drifted back off to sleep, not daring to move a muscle. Were she not fundamentally broken, Alexandra may have wept from her dream. As it was, a few stray strands of wetness caught in the Spider's bangs. It was nearly ten minutes that Alexandra Pierce slid out of bed, disturbing the Universal Champion just enough to generate a low, pitiful, ''Nnngh... pas encore, trop... trop endolori.'' Pierce dressed in silence, slipping out the door as quietly as she could manage -- which was very quiet indeed. Alex did not use the restroom a single time before she left. She was afraid of what the mirror would tell her. ------------------------------------------------------------------- The first time's hard enough But you made it through The second time, I see the changes in you She's got you somehow...
