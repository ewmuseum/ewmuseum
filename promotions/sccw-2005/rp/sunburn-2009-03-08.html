Sunburn

8 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/08/2009 After the last show on the Lethal Lottery Tour… The tour is over and all I can think about is what could’ve been… I, Rick Malloy, could’ve been a contender but I blew it. Blew it because I was a gullible idealist in a pessimistic world. This Lethal Lottery tournament left a bad taste in my mouth. My partner turned on. I could not make it back into the tournament either. Therefore I was stuck in limbo after that. The rest of my tour sucked after that except for a win over Buck Fledger. Now my tour is over and I can relax. However, without my wrestling? What do I have to do with my life for two or three weeks? That’s when it hits me. I have nothing but my work. I have nothing outside of wrestling. My life is in shambles because of my commitment to the sport I love. My agent and the industry people are about the only friends and family I have anymore. Just Wrestling is the life and death of me. I sweat and bleed for this company. This is my home and nobody is going to take it from me. However I get a text message from my agent. Pulling out my cellphone, I flip it open and see what it says. Hey Rick. Tour’s over. You’re being brought back for the next one. Now go have some fun. Go to a race or something. Talk to you soon. Closing my phone, I can only shake my head. "Have fun? How am I going to have fun?" I never should’ve told him about my friend being in Vegas to see the NASCAR race. Now I’m almost stuck going. Fun is just another curse word to me here lately. The only fun I’ve had here lately was smashing Aaron Nothings in the head with a chair. That is my fun. How sad is that? I sigh and go to get ready to head back to the hotel. Such is my lonely life as a professional wrestler. * * * The following weekend… After my debacle in Just Wrestling, I have decided to take the advice of my agent and go to Las Vegas to meet one of my friends there to go see the NASCAR Shelby 427. We left his house at about ten minutes after nine in the morning. We decided to take Las Vegas Boulevard all the way up which would be quicker than the bogged down freeway that is being repaired. The street was fairly busy but nothing too bad except downtown. But then we reached the northern part of town and it was all downhill from there. The traffic was the usual nightmare. Cars as far as the eye can see in all directions. Everyone was trying to get into two lanes at one point when there were four open traffic lanes. That was fun to get through. All these cars trickling like ants from four lanes into the two lanes that led to the parking lot. Good thing we had shit to talk about because it would have been boring as hell otherwise. Finally we were able to part the rental car. Once out of the car I was the only one of us to put on suntan lotion because my friend doesn’t want any. Of course we both have on jackets and it’s hot as hell outside. This city is like a giant microwave. I can’t wait to get out of here and go back to the east coast for the Just Wrestling tour that’s upcoming. It’s going to be a lot like this race. Hot, wild, and out of control. Everyone will be jockeying for position just like everyone here is jockeying for the best parking sports. It’s a dog eat dog world and in Just Wrestling, I’m just another dog that’s trying to claw his way to the top. After leaving the car we head toward the racetrack. Stopping to see what’s on sale at the little tent at the end of the parking lot, we then cross the street with a mass of people and head toward the gate where we are sitting. That’s when we get stuck in the real mass of people once reaching the track store. Once reaching it, there are people as far as the eye can see. It’s like a zombie invasion or something! It reminds me of a horror movie like Night of the Living Dead where the zombies converge on the small farmhouse where the humans are hiding. The tent sales area for all the teams and the speedway track store are basically like that. It’s a madhouse inside. The lines to the bathrooms look like soup kitchen lines from the Great Depression, especially the women’s bathroom line. It snakes across the ticket sales area like an anaconda. After surviving the bathrooms, we decide to go take a look around the store. The only things of interest are the M&M’s Kyle Busch t-shirt which amuses us both and the hat that goes with it. I think about buying it but decide we should go look at the other sales areas before making a decision. Then we trudge through the large crowd of sleazy looking people and out of towners, we decide to go back the zombie-like crowd and head back to get the Busch shirt. Once I get my hat and shirt we head for the food area that we saw in the massive festival. Once reaching the food area, I get some chicken fingers and fries with a Coke while my buddy gets nacho cheese fries and a Coke. Eating as we walked through the mass of humanity to find our seats, the sleazy slime of Sin City makes me almost gag up my food. Here I was expecting to see a bunch of hot chicks when instead we get to see a bunch of forty something year olds and a few dudes without shirts on who look like they are going to club seals in the desert after the race. After walking almost all the way around the stadium, we finally get to our gate and go inside before finding our seats amidst the mass of people there to watch the race. The sun nearly melts our faces as we sit down. And it continues to do so as we stare out at the vast track during the opening ceremonies. The race then started and the sun blazed in our faces as the race went along. I could feel my skin melting off of my body. Yeah, this was a good idea. I’ll be burnt to a crisp when I go back for the next tour. Just fucking great. Of course the race goes on and on and on and on some more. It’s like the movie Cars but without the animation. I wish those cars would’ve talked. Would’ve made it more interesting and made the heat more bearable. The best part of the race was my driver Kyle Busch winning it all. Made me glad I bought his stuff instead of Tony Stewart’s stuff. After winning, his car did a spin and then he drove to the winner’s circle. Then we left and that’s when the adventure began… The mass of people leaving the track was a lot more than the horde that infested the concession festival area before the race. It’s really like a zombie invasion now! We trudge through the chaos and finally reaching our parking lot area. Only one problem. We can’t find the rental car! We look around the first street area and wander aimlessly through the gigantic, unlit parking lot. They really need to put some fucking lights in this parking lot. What a joke. They have this big ass racetrack and can’t even put lights in the parking lot. We see vans, trucks, and cars but not my car. Almost tripping over rocks and pebbles, we both almost fall over. Finding my car now is at a point where it’s turned into Where’s Waldo? We look everywhere and then decide to walk across the street but then I look over and remember we parked before the helipad. So we walked south and sure enough, we found my car. After getting in, I put the car in drive and we slowly get out of the parking lot. Then we finally get out but have to slowly go down Las Vegas Boulevard. A full hour and a half after the race, we finally are out of the traffic and head for the hotel. Once there, after driving down endless roads, all I can think about is going back to Just and showing everyone just what I’m made of.
