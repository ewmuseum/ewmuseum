Decisions, Decisions Interlude: God And The Devil Are Raging Inside Me

22 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/4/09 Title: Sorry It’s been a while… a quick recap! It’s weird, normally all you see of me is the happy-go-lucky, makes cultural jokes, sarcastic side, the main reason for this is mostly there’s less stress here than my other place of employment. My first year at the other frontier consisted of doing a ‘Kathryn Shaw’ with a man by faking a resurrection of the man’s dead girlfriend, it opened some places in me that I never thought possible, at least I didn’t think it was. The thrill of him being the dominant one was great initially but I had to regain the control in order to fulfil Legion’s plan and all was well until [B] SHE[/B] took it all away. People like Teresa Quaranta don’t just manipulate they fuck everything up, she cost me the things I loved at the time so I just snapped… It was then I realised my control over my rage was slowly losing itself. Every single time I tried to face her she always found a way and then the person I thought I loved turned out to be a fake, not just any fake but (and this may sound strange) an android of all things! It’s weird but I suppose working in a place like NFW… Sin City though I see a bright future: you see you readers of this blog may not get it but I feel I found a kindred spirit, there’s no (as that chick on PRIME put it) jealous bitchwhores of the arcane for me to encounter yet and at the same time I’ve met someone that was different. The strangest thing is it’s the sister of the woman they call Katsidy that’s got my attention, I feel a craving I can’t quite replace so easily. Maybe it’s what Trent Reznor called the Perfect Drug back in the day. Legion I can’t quite feel is trying to warn me not to give in just yet… probably because the Dead Man’s Hand have ‘recruited’ people in this way before…. he’s heard rumours of that ‘Malice’ chick (who seems to remind me of me sometimes…) and Desade being Sapphic buddies so he didn’t want people to think the same of me. That’s Legion for you though… always thinking and looking out for me (well he thinks so!) That’s it for now, cheers guys and gals. Have to plan things against ‘Dusk’ now. Mwah Luci (Taken from a Livejournal posting 2 hours after the last Temptation) (We cut to Luci finishing typing the entry on a hotel room computer, Legion on the bed opposite…) Legion: You finished typing that up? Luci: Yep, so I guess you didn’t hear anything off Rick then about research? Legion: nothing, which is good because it keeps him out of our hair for a while I don’t need another Vegas crazy man hounding me for months on end again… in fact remind me to tell Nige my ‘Vegas’ clause… Luci: You mean the security ? Legion: Exactly, thank god for the Southwest… (Suddenly a you’ve got mail voice hits on the computer) Luci: looks like we have a problem… (The screen reads the Malloy and ??? Vs Legion and ??? match) Legion: Oh no! Well looks like I have to prove my dominance over Vegas again… I mean I took one guy from that town out of this sport… plus saves me a job at Homecoming, least on the wrestling side… (Suddenly Legion’s phone vibrates… the tone being that of Crystal Skull by Mastodon) A text message shows: Hope this is the right number. At Temptation we need to talk about tonight’s events… also how do you want to initiate what we discussed a few weeks ago? LF Legion begins to type but the camera cuts to Luci… Luci: Who was that? Legion: Oh just some NFW business along with people laughing at the south park reference that’s all. (We fade to black…)
