Forever In Darkness: Part VI

26 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.26.08 ~ In the desert I saw a creature, naked, bestial. Who, squatting upon the ground, held his heart in his hands, and he ate of it. I said, 'Is it good, friend?' 'It is bitter…bitter,' he answered. 'But I like it because it is bitter, and because it is my heart.' Brennan shoots up from his bed clutching his chest. He feels pain coursing through his body even though his heart has not functioned for hundreds of years. The images are so vivid in his mind that it feels like he is right there when Corvus drives the railroad spike through his father’s chest. A sense of defeat comes over Brennan as he lies back down and looks over at the clock. Almost four in the afternoon…he’ll be getting out of bed soon to start a new evening but first there’s a phone call he should make. He doesn’t want to but it’s necessary. He reaches over and picks up the phone and dials a number waiting for someone to pick up. A young woman answers and he hesitates but then asks if he can speak to Joshua. She tells him to hold on a moment and after his keen hearing picks up the sound of children in the background, Joshua gets on the line. "Joshua, this is Brennan Hawthorne…we really need to talk. Are you available this evening?" Joshua puts the phone against his chest to talk to someone who Brennan imagines is the man’s wife, Angel. The sound of Joshua’s rapid heartbeat echoes into his ears until Joshua can resume the conversation. He tells Brennan that he could speak with him if he wants to come by the house at some point. Brennan doesn’t have anything planned so it isn’t a problem for him to go there. He does need to feed before he does otherwise it won’t be a fun evening for anyone not that it is going to be anyway with what he is going there to talk about. "I’ll be there after dinner." Brennan says to Joshua not really thinking about whose dinner exactly. Joshua chuckles and inquires as to which one it is and as much of a mood as Brennan is in he slightly laughs too. "Mine of course, I’ll show up after you’ve put the children to sleep. I’d rather them not be around for our conversation." Joshua agrees strongly as he has kept his children safeguarded from some of his darker secrets. His wife on the other hand knows about these things and it has been her support that Joshua is leaning on to do what must be done for his friend who also happens to be Brennan’s father, Eryc. What Brennan doesn’t know is that Joshua is also in tune with what’s been going on with Eryc and Corvus. He’s experienced many of Corvus’ tortures upon his friend through visions much like Brennan just had. He’ll tell Brennan of them tonight when they speak; he sees no reason to discuss it now over the phone. Joshua is always careful about what he says in his home because his phone may be wire tapped by the secret society he has come to know as the Ravenloft Agency. His and Eryc’s mentor, David O’Malley shed light upon them several times in conversations he’s had with him. Joshua knows that Brennan worked for them and is hesitant to help him but it seems that Brennan is out for his father’s best interests. "I will see you this evening then Joshua; thanks for having me on such short notice." Brennan says his farewell and turns the phone off dropping it onto the bed where he puts his hands under his head. He stares at the ceiling and hopes that his father is not dead as he saw in his dream or whatever it actually was. Brennan also wonders if this Joshua is a straight arrow only having spoken to him on the phone and going on the word of a man he doesn’t know; one that is a man of god. It’s been a long time since he last had any faith because of what he is but it doesn’t hurt to try to believe in something even if it is not exactly the kind of faith you get out of praising a god. ~ Corvus steps out of the crappy rental he received from the airport; he straightens himself out before he hands the keys over to the valet with a scowl on his face. He gives a brief glance at the outside of the building which once served as a theater in its earlier days; a shell of its former self but suits the owners very well. The Bathory Club serves as a den of iniquity in the vampire world. Many deals are made and broken within the walls of this particular establishment; very rarely do humans gain entry unless they are to become one of the proprietors’ latest meals. It also happens to be the biggest front for the Ravenloft Agency. While there are many of them for example: the law firm headed by J.P. Chevalier and his cronies, the club is where the heaviest activity takes place. Many of the world’s leaders and potential threats to the vampire race have had the end of their lives planned right at one of the gambling tables. The security is thick at the door when Corvus reaches it and they are not about to let him in. He flashes his canines for a brief second and drops Chevalier’s name and that he’s expecting him tonight. The two beefy guards glance at one another to which Corvus automatically knows they are talking to one another through mindspeak. Corvus grows even more impatient waiting for these two lesser vampires to decide if they should let him in or not. Another moment or two and he’ll be ripping out their insides and making a jump rope with their entrails. Just when he’s about to make his move they step aside allowing him entry into the club. Just past the doors he takes a sharp left and begins to descend down the concrete steps into the music lounge where the sounds of a sultry singer echo through the air. Corvus scans the different tables but does not see who he is looking for just yet. It isn’t until he turns his attentions towards the booths near the stage that he finds Chevalier, sitting with a bevy of gorgeous women. A slight chuckle escapes from Corvus’ lips as he always pegged the man to be a fop but it could be just for show in public. He blends in and manages to sneak past the security watching over the very important man. Chevalier sets down his drink as he spots Corvus standing in front of him and his guests. His facial expression is not one of welcome as he is not pleased to see one of the biggest threats to him and his organization standing before him. In fact Corvus was not being expected by him as was told to the guards but Chevalier allowed him entry for fear of the commotion he might cause if he wasn’t granted entry. "Ladies, if you wouldn’t mind stepping away for a moment this gentleman and I have a little business to discuss." One by one, the bevy of beauties exit from the booth and Corvus slides in after them. Chevalier offers him a glass of bloodwine which Corvus declines. The one time hired assassin for the Agency props his elbows onto the table and presses his hands together at the palms. The space surrounding the two men is quite tense because of their extreme dislike of one another. "What is the nature of your visit this evening, Corvus?" Chevalier speaks with a hint of malice in his tone and as far as he’s concerned the quicker their conversation is over the better. Corvus feels the exact same way and if he would have had any other choice he would not have come to the club in the first place. He doesn’t know where to start looking for information about the monk he saw in his visions when he tasted Morgan’s blood. With Ravenloft’s vast resources he has no doubt they at least know something about this man and what his connection is to Morgan. Corvus narrows his eyes as he speaks, "I need a little information about someone." He reaches into his pocket then places a piece of paper onto the table. Corvus slides it over to Chevalier for him to look at a sketch of O’Malley. "I need to know who this man is." Corvus leans back into the cushy leather backing of the booth and reaches into his pocket for one of his trademark clove cigarettes. He is unsure if the scowl from Chevalier is from recognizing the person in the drawing or the clove he just lit up. The drawing is put back in front of Corvus as the Chevalier takes a heavy drink from his glass. He hasn’t been reminded of O’Malley in some time but he’s been expecting something for a while now. The two have opposed one another many times over the centuries and possibly even longer then that, O’Malley being on the side of light and Chevalier of course on the side of darkness. The first time they met, Jean-Paul and a group of other vampires had been sacking villages in what is now Ireland. O’Malley resonated with goodness which in its purest form is like poison to a vampire. Most in this day and age do not have the same kind of faith that existed back then so these night dwellers roam wherever they feel like without consequence. O’Malley disposed of nearly half of Chevalier’s companions and nearly ended him as well but he managed to escape. Years of research brought to his attention that one man is chosen by God to maintain a proper balance between good and evil. It’s cliché but true. O’Malley is the most recent man chosen and it is his duty to take upon his wing a warrior with a strong will as well as constitution and prepare him to aid in making sure the balance is kept. Both sides have tried to thwart one another many times and one of the reasons why the Ravenloft Agency was formed to build as many resources as possible to try and upend the balance so that one day the world will become completely darkened and vampires shall roam without restriction. "He has gone by many names much like you, but today he is known as David O’Malley." Chevalier shows a glint of his sharpened canine as he hisses his enemy’s name. "If there is one man I hate more then Eryc Morgan it would be him." Corvus seems to find enjoyment in anything that bothers Chevalier; watching him get gutted about some religious nut is right up his alley. Of course it means egging him on and taking jabs as well which is something Corvus does better then anyone. "So what did this guy do to get your panties all in a twist?" Corvus smirks as he takes another hit off the clove between his fingers. "Must be bad if you hate him more than Morgan because normally he’s all you talk about. Morgan must suffer…Morgan must die…wah wah wah." Chevalier clenches his fist so hard droplets of blood run down his hand; his eyes turn a crimson color as he glares directly at Corvus. "How dare you insult me and expect me to give you information you reprehensible, unworthy, wretch! One day you will learn your place and if you don’t then you’ll be put to death." Rolling his eyes, Corvus takes the piece of paper in his hand and studies it a little more. "Well, the way I see it is if you don’t tell me who he is I’m going to find out anyway and when I do I’m going to go to him and finish him off." He presses the end of the clove against the paper right on the forehead of the depiction of O’Malley. "You’re going to have your work cut out for you boy if you think you can take him so easily." Chevalier knows first hand exactly what Corvus will be dealing with if he does find him. Many of the strongest of their kind have sought to do the same thing yet none of them return to tell the tale. "He’s not the average fleshbag you try pushing around…he’s bound by divinity, he’s immortal." Corvus stifles a laugh. He’s never been afraid of anything before not even those of his own kind and some crazy guy who thinks he’s indestructible is no different. If there’s a way to kill the priest then he’ll find it. So many weapons to choose from at his disposal one of them has to do the trick. Chevalier is prompted to ask him the one question that has been circling his thoughts since he saw the drawing, "How did you find out about him anyway? He’s nowhere near your league." Divulging information when it isn’t warranted is not Corvus’ style. He doesn’t feel like Chevalier needs to know where he came into contact with this knowledge but if there is ever going to be any ground covered he has to throw him a small bone…a really small bone. He also doesn’t want to tell him that he’s killed Morgan because he wouldn’t have any leverage getting what he wants. "Morgan, I managed to force it out of him that this priest has been helping him which is why he is still alive after I left him for dead on top of that building that you use as your little day hideout." Corvus doesn’t believe in telling the truth and he’s not going to start now. He makes sure he keeps his thoughts blocked from Chevalier just in case he tries to pry into his mind and read them. It was Morgan’s blood that was toxic enough to give him the visions of his interactions with O’Malley. News of O’Malley aiding Morgan does not settle well with Chevalier. It is the last thing he wants to see. Morgan is enough of a threat to the Agency without being taught by O’Malley. If Corvus can get the job done it may just prove his worth to the Agency despite his being the loose cannon that he is. "Where is Morgan now?" "He is still in my custody and believe me he isn’t going anywhere...not anytime soon anyway." Corvus speaks with a self-assured tone as he puts the butt of the clove out on his hand. "Now, what do I need to know about this guy?" Chevalier lifts up a solitary finger and gets onto his cell. He calls what seems to be someone from the law firm and tells them to bring a cd-rom copy of the file on David O’Malley. It is expected to arrive expediently so Corvus will not have to wait long. "The file I’m having brought over should tell you everything that you need to know about O’Malley history and all. It also has the location of a contact who can tell you exactly where he’s hiding." Jean-Paul figures if Corvus does find O’Malley he wins no matter what happens because one of them will die and that is something he can live with. "I don’t expect you to accomplish this but if you do…you may earn that position we discussed in my office the last time we met." Corvus rolls his eyes in reaction to the statement and has a quick rebuttal for the stuck up elder of the vampire world. "If I accomplish my task you won’t need to worry about giving me that position because by that time I’ll have eclipsed you and can take your position as a VIP for the agency." He smirks and twists the knife a tad deeper, "How do those apples taste?" As irate as he is, Chevalier takes it in stride knowing there is no possible way that this imbecile will be able to vanquish David O’Malley. He’s almost glad that soon he won’t have to see him ever again. Never before did a project by the agency fail as badly as the one involving Corvus. "We shall certainly see won’t we? Your conceitedness will be your downfall one day if not soon." Before Corvus can respond the gentleman that Chevalier summoned to bring him the information on O’Malley approaches the booth and slides it over to him. Corvus slips it into the inside pocket of his leather jacket and stands up. Just as he is about to leave he turns to Chevalier and winks at him. "You’re right we will see but if I were you, I’d start cleaning out those belongings in that desk of mine. I’ll be coming for it very shortly." Chevalier curses to himself as Corvus leaves the club with the swagger of a Class A cocksucker. As much as he’d like to have O’Malley out of the picture he’s leaning more towards favoring the death of Corvus slightly more. He finishes up his glass of wine and motions for the ladies who were his company before the intrusion to rejoin him now that business has been taken care of. ~ Brennan checks the address on the house with the one written on the piece of paper he wrote it down on after speaking with Joshua to make sure that they match up. He approaches the door of the white gothic designed house with columns on both sides. It was definitely in good taste as far as he’s concerned. Not many mortals are as fashioned minded as the people he associates with. His pale finger presses the door bell and he waits for someone to answer the door. This isn’t particularly easy for him to be seeking out someone for help but this man is the closest thing his father has to a friend. Footsteps approach the door from the other side. They are heavy, and Brennan’s exceptional hearing can denote the shifting of weight from one leg to another. The occupant of the house stops at the door for a few brief moments before pulling the door wide open; dressed in a simple white t-shirt and pair of plaid pajama pants stands Joshua Kosidlo. He looks Brennan up and down a couple of times before finally greeting him. "I’ve never known your kind to ring the doorbell before." Mr. Hawthorne chuckles and makes a witty retort, "I guess I just wasn’t in the mood to dispel the rumor of not being able to come in unless invited." Of course he doesn’t need to be invited to go inside the house but he was being polite and has exceptional manners for his race. Joshua steps aside so that Brennan can come in. After closing the door he brings him to the kitchen where they both sit down at the table. His wife Angel is putting on a pot of coffee as that is one of Joshua’s preferred beverages. She turns to their guest and offers to pour him a cup when it is ready but he declines having just fed. Brennan is slightly skeptical about Angel being in the room with them while they discuss things but he senses that she knows plenty about what is going on so he relaxes more. He fixes his gaze on Joshua and the concern he displays immediately is caught by his host. "After all this time what’s made you decide to be so concerned about Eryc all of a sudden? From what I know you were playing for the other team which has set out to make sure he’s dead at all costs…" pausing he glances at his wife, then adds "and those closest to him." Brennan dislikes being called on his decision to join his father’s side after leaving the Ravenloft Agency but Joshua does have a point. Leopards don’t change their spots but he’s been through enough to make sure that he won’t be going back to the old lifestyle he was once a part of. "I’ve been lied to one too many times, Joshua. I understand your concern for the well-being of my father and it should be known that we have the same interests. I’m not sure what we can do now but we have to act soon." The visions Brennan had this morning flit through his head. "Otherwise the outcome is not going to be what we want as I already fear the worst." Joshua too had the same visions though more in depth then his best friend’s son. He had more faith in Eryc still being alive because he knows more about the type of power that men like David O’Malley and his associates have. It would take more then spikes to stop them and he’s seen Eryc handle situations tougher in nature then this. "You fear that Corvus has killed Eryc don’t you?" Joshua speaks calmly. Brennan nods and his eyes cannot even look at Joshua anymore because of the anguish going through him. He wants to make sure that his father comes out of this alive and for Corvus to be put to rest forever. "Your father is tougher then most people give him credit for. I’ve fought against him and fought with him side by side and if anyone wanted to know what pure of heart is then they need look no further. Eryc is the real deal and he’ll get through this no matter what but he does need help…" Joshua pauses for a moment looking over the creature before him. Then, reluctantly, "our help." Brennan agrees with Joshua but is unsure of how to help. "What should we do? We don’t know where he is or where Corvus is holding him and following him around is not a good idea because he’s an expert at reconnaissance and will thwart our best efforts." Joshua places a leather bound book on top of the table; it looks very old and slightly worn from use. Brennan eyes the book but doesn’t see how it could help them in finding Eryc. "This is O’Malley’s journal. I swiped it the last time I was over there and have been flipping through it to see if I could find a clue to where Eryc is being held and I have a slight idea from the visions I’ve had combined with notes in this book." Stealing from someone as powerful as O’Malley is not something that happens easily. Brennan has heard stories about him from former colleagues in the agency and it has been noted that he is very secretive and hard to find much less leaving an opportunity open to have items from his personal belongings to be taken. "What good is a journal going to do?" Brennan let his thoughts out of his lips. "It’s only going to make this O’Malley guy mad at you and then what? And don’t say that he doesn’t know you took it because I’m sure he does." Joshua is impressed with Brennan’s research on O’Malley. It has always been said that O’Malley knows everything and such a monumental slip up isn’t in his nature. He didn’t simply leave it laying around just because…he left it there for a reason. Joshua starts to get a sickening feeling in his stomach. "Brennan, I think we need to leave here quickly." Joshua stands up and turns to his wife kissing her on the cheek. "I’ll be back as soon as I can." He gets up and walks to the coat rack by the door and slips on his coat with Brennan moving quickly behind him. Brennan doesn’t even know what Joshua’s reasoning is to rush out of the house so quickly. "What’s going on? Why are we in such a hurry to leave?" Joshua doesn’t waste any time in telling his guest where they are heading off to in such a rush. "We’re going to O’Malley’s place…I just had a really powerful feeling that he’s in danger."
