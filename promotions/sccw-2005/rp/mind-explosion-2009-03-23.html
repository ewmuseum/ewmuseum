Mind Explosion.

23 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/23/2009 The lonely, endless road is in front of me. Lines on the road become blurred. All of the lanes appear the same to me. All of the cities I go to seem the same. The same with the towns. Wilderness and technology get blurred together. Nothing seems to be different than anything else. Everything seems to be the same. I feel like Jonas in The Giver. Everything is the same to me. No color, no emotion, no nothing. Just deadness in space. Driving from town to town, I am everywhere and yet I am nowhere at the same time. The only thing that keeps me sane are rendezvous with various women in hotel and motel rooms. Sex is the only thing that keeps me from losing my humanity. Women come and go on the road, each one looking different than the last one. It’s almost like a giant buffet of orgasmic pleasure every time I go to a city or town. Each town is just the same as the last except for it has a different view. Whether it be forests, industrial plants, plains, deserts, swamps, corn rows, skyscrapers, trailer parks, endless plains, farms, beaches, or whatever in the background, it really doesn’t change the fact that society is headed for oblivion much the same way Just Wrestling is. The champion here is named Aaron Nothings. He is nothing more than a sick, perverted cheap shot artist that no one can cheer for. However he’s facing Erik Magnum this week, another lowlife. He calls himself the Lone Gunman. For some reason the nerdy guys who lived in their parents’ basement from the show X-Files pop in my head every time I think about him. What garbage. Speaking of garbage, I’ve seen a lot of it here lately. Trash on the street, both the discarded kind and the kind that walks around on two legs. This country is falling into a hole that leads only to oblivion. Death is coming for us all. The empire is falling. The country cries out in anguish and the only things that can hear it are the pigeons that roost on the rooftops. Just Wrestling is falling apart the same way. No shows, degenerate wrestlers, and unmarketable champions are causing this company to fall by the wayside. There’s also a child murdering cannibal on the roster, too. I’m facing this man this week. His name is Maynard Crane. He’s just like all of these wrestlers and fans, according to some guy named David Jericho who called me this week to try and recruit me for some sort of team put together by Biff Busey. What he basically said was "Crane is a hypocritical liar, a meandering parasite just like each and everyone of the losers who come to the shows." When I declined to join his group, he vowed I would pay. Fuck him. He’s all talk just like a lot of wrestlers in Just. All they do is pander to the people with their diatribes like shamans to their villages. I can hear the voices of the ghosts of wrestlers long dead cry out in terror when they see these bastards in the ring. Makes them sicker than it does me. There’s people like… Clark Fox Meat Jon Kano The list goes on and on. All I can do is close my eyes and let it all go away. All the garbage in and out of the ring. The blur that is my life fades into nothingness. Blackness all around me. Visions of terror when I close my eyes. Explosions… Death… Chaos. The future but I sleep… Sleep… S L E E P … . . . AWAKE! The sound of fucking above me and construction outside awakens me from my heavenly slumber. I can feel the weight of the world on my shoulders as I sit up out of bed to stretch before I being my day. Looking down at the bed, there is a naked woman next to me. Don’t remember having sex with her but I probably did. She’s probably a Just ring rat. Knowing my luck she probably caught VD from Kano or Shipley. Fuckers. Go to take shower. Water feels warm. Then it gets colder. And colder… And colder… FREEZE! Turn off shower and dry off. Put my clothes on and when I come back to the bed the woman is dressed. She doesn’t say a word, only leaves which doesn’t annoy me too much. I haven’t been talking as much here lately. Maybe I’m really a machine and don’t realize it. Maybe I’m a Terminator… Nah, that would be too silly and stupid, kind of like the Stimulus Package or the AIG fiasco. Financial markets going on a tailspin again today. Slimy pessimistic investors are going to be the death of capitalism. Greedy, slimy rats who only care about money. That’s what they are. Kind of like politicians except politicians also care about sex, affairs, and power as well as money. I hate politicians. They all lie. Although I am a bit jealous that they get to make the laws the same way I’m a bit jealous about Nothings being the Just Wrestling Champion. I know I’ll never be a champion. The gold will never be mine… NEVER… But I still go on craving it. It is my reason for being. It is the reason I am in the sport of wrestling. However, I am not blinded by the light of the gold. It does not permeate the very essence of my being. I keep going on… Driving down the endless blue highways to the middle of nowhere… Everything blurred together again. The highway seems endless. I wonder where it all leads to? I wonder if after all this driving, I am going to end up driving off a cliff at the end of the road. Maybe it all leads to an infinite space filled only with stars and planets. Maybe it only leads to perpetual darkness. Maybe it leads to salvation… Or my death. Everything blurs together again in a oneness you only read about in holy books. Colors mix together. People become one. Cars are only colored blurs. Light mixes with darkness. Nothing is singular. Everything is one. The ride to the show is even more lonely than usual. Why do Just shows have to be all over the place? I feel like a lost soul trying to reach Heaven from Purgatory every time I drive to a show. Rental car stinks. I make a note to myself to complain about that. Have to get to show fast. Roll down windows to get stink out. Maybe Crane hid a body in the trunk. Wouldn’t surprise me. Sick fuck molests himself before eating human flesh. Speaking of psychos, Biff Busey called me again and tried to get me to join his crew. Again I declined. He was not pleased. Said something about sending the Gingerdead Man after me, whatever that means. Few minutes later SCCW wrestler Ash Plato calls and invites me to SCCW’s next show. I accept that offer. I ask him about Busey and says they tried to recruit him too. He also declined the offer. At least I have one friend in the world. He seems tired and gets off phone quickly. Go to hotel in town. Hooker outside. In my room we have sex after I get my key and bring all my luggage in. No body in trunk. Naked woman’s body in bed, however. Humanity restored briefly. After she is paid and leaves, the loneliness sets in. I feel like an island floating in the middle a vast ocean. Nothing else around… I am nowhere. I am nothing. Put on soothing music and watch porn. What a sad life this is to live. Hate it and love it at same time. I am practically a homeless vagabond at this point. Most homeless people are supposed to be insane. I know I am. Mind going everywhere… EVERYWHERE… SCATTERED… Like glass from a broken window. SMASH! Mind explosion… Only scattered remnants remain inside the fried pink shell. I don’t know what is happening to me. I smell something burning. Must be my brain. Maybe Hannibal Lecter is coming to eat it. Get things together and leave for show. After arriving I change clothes and get into my gear. Mindset ready to go. I have everything I need. My tights, boots, kneepads, elbow pads, tape, and my mitts. Clenching my fists, I look down and see a Just Wrestling flyer on the floor. I see all sorts of names on it. Erik Magnum Illustrious Face-Eater Aaron Nothings Meat However, one name that is missing is mine. What a crock. I’m nothing to Shipley. Just another warm body. Speaking of bodies, I smell what appears to be a rotting corpse’s horrible smell coming from somewhere. Following the smell out of my locker room, I head down the hallway to find the source of the horridness that is infecting my nose with putrid sensations. That is when I come to a door with Maynard Crane written on it. Makes sense now. He must be eating at the moment. Sick bastard. However, he will not be thinking about eating human flesh after I face him in the ring. How could he? He’ll have no teeth to chew with… Kind of like an eighty year old man with dentures. Just Wrestling will soon know who their true hero is and his name is Rick Malloy. Hopefully my brain won’t explode like a male orgasm again before that happens… HOPEFULLY…
