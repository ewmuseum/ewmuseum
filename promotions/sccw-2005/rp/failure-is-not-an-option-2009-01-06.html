Failure Is Not An Option...

Date: Jan 06, 2009 ...It's his way of life. Jadian Bridden stands in the center of the ring inside of the Minorez gym, the neon lights from the bar across the street the only thing illuminating the otherwise dark and empty facility. His white tee shirt is tucked into a pair of dark denim jeans, his tawny brown hair is unkempt and his green eyes are practically glowing. *flash* ## What do I do to ignore what's behind me? Do I follow my fate and escape blindly? Do I hide my pride away from these bad dreams And give into sad thoughts that are maddening? ## A young boy stands near the entrance to the gym, a pair of black basketball shorts dripping with perspiration trickling down his bare chest. His tawny brown hair is damp and unkempt, while his green eyes are practically glowing. *flash* ## Do I let it go and try to stand it? Or do I try to catch them red-handed? Do I trust some and get fooled by phoniness? Or do I trust none and live life in loneliness? ## Jadian launches himself from the top rope, performing a picture perfect moonsault onto an empty canvas as a group of students await their turn to attempt the same maneuver. Benito Minorez leans against the open door to his office in the distance, his arms folded across his chest as he watches the teenage boy lift himself off the mat. *flash* ## The sunlight burns the skin of sleeping men I make the right turns but I'm lost within I put on my daily façade but then I just end up gettin' hurt again ## The young boy is a little closer to the ring, his arms hanging loosely at his side as he takes slow deliberate breaths. He doesn’t look to be more than about ten years old, a little boy lost with no one to guide him. He slowly lifts his head, as his mouth slowly opens and he lets out an unheard scream. *flash* ## By myself (Myself) I ask why (but in my mind I find) I can't rely on myself (Myself) I ask why (but in my mind I find) I can't rely on myself ## Jadian and Benito are walking out of Benny’s office, smiling as the former member of Minor Details playfully ruffles Jay’s tawny brown hair. Julissa Minorez watches from a few feet away, her full lips curved into a scowl as she watches her father forming a bond with the ‘son he never had’. *flash* ## I can't hold on To what I want when I'm stretched so thin It's all too much to take in I can't hold on To anything watchin' everything spin With thoughts of failure sinking in ## The young boy slowly approaches the ring. Each step seems to flow into the next as tears begin to trickle from the corners of jaded eyes. Everything he has ever wanted is right here in front of him, within arm’s reach of his grasp. The tears staining his cheeks, are of joy. *flash* ## If I turn my back I'm defenseless And to give in to fate seems senseless If I hide my pride and let it all go on Then they'll take from me 'til everything is gone If I let them go I'll be outdone But if I try to catch them I'll be outrun If I'm killed by the questions like a cancer Then I'll be buried in the silence of the answer... ## Jadian watches as Julissa and Benito argue in his office, but apart from his name being screamed a few times here and there, he isn’t sure what the dispute is about. A forearm to the chest quickly draws his attention back to the training session inside of the ring, but not quick enough to avoid the scoop slam that leaves him out of breath - flat on his back. *flash* ## By myself (Myself) I ask why (but in my mind I find) I can't rely on myself (Myself) I ask why (but in my mind I find) I can't rely on myself ## The young boy now stands in the center of the ring, his legs weak as he struggles to keep them from wobbling. His entire body sways slightly as the euphoria of living his dream - of being where he belongs - slowly settles in. With his chin pointed to the heavens and tears streaking down his face, the boy crumples to his knees. His fingers curl into fists and his chest heaves with every breath, but his arms just seem to dangle at his sides - fingers brushing across the canvas when he extends them. *flash* ## I can't hold on To what I want when I'm stretched so thin It's all too much to take in I can't hold on To anything watchin' everything spin With thoughts of failure sinking in ## Bridden’s eyes roll back behind his eyelids, his mouth wide as a low moan of pleasure escapes slightly moistened lips. Julissa’s fingernails leave a trail of red as she drags them across his sweat-dampened, bare chest. She straddles her father’s prodigy, rocking to and fro to a beat only she can hear as his hands explore her breasts. The satisfaction of becoming a professional wrestler, of living out your dreams, is nothing compared to the euphoria that rocked his body near convulsion. In more ways than one, Julissa had her claws in deep. *flash* ## How do you think That I've lost so much I'm always afraid That I'm out of touch How do you expect That I will know what to do When all I know Is what you tell me to ## The boy slowly pushes himself to his feet, composing himself with each passing second. His once glowing green eyes narrow and darken. The corner of the boy’s lip lifts into a sneer, baring his pearly whites. His nostrils begin to flare, no longer the naïve young boy he appeared to be. The boy who slumped to the ground in a fit of tears was gone, as muscles began to tone and bones began to harden. The young man practically growls as fingers wrap themselves into fists, muscles stretching themselves to the brink of snapping. *flash* ## Don't you (know) I can't tell you how to make it (go) No matter what I do, how hard I (try) I can't seem to convince myself (why) I’m stuck on the outside… I'm stuck on the outside ## Jadian Bridden stands in the center of the ring inside of the Minorez gym, the neon lights from the bar across the street the only thing illuminating the otherwise dark and empty facility. It is hard to believe this is where it all began, and Jay was that naïve little boy just happy to be here. It has been a long time since he was happy, and a long time since he was a little boy. Deep down he felt he had never changed, but the pathetic bastard that stares back at him when he stares into a mirror proves otherwise. ## I can't hold on To what I want when I'm stretched so thin It's all too much to take in I can't hold on To anything watchin' everything spin With thoughts of failure from with...(in) ## Behold, Sin City, the failure in your ring. December 18, 2008 Somewhere on the East Coast To say that Jadian Bridden was not living up to the potential many expected of the young man would be an understatement, as opportunity after opportunity passed by the wayside. He was a joke, the weakest link in the armor of the Dead Man’s Hand. Everyone saw it. Everyone was quick to exploit it. Not even the Hand’s ‘Ace in the Hole‘, Phillip Kennedy, could manage to escape the gravitational pull of Bridden’s case of bad luck. Sitting there, his knuckles white from the tight grip he holds on the steering wheel of the silver Chrysler 300 C class, Jadian Bridden replays the moment over and over again in his head. Not only the sound of the referee’s hand slapping against the taut canvas mat, but the disgusted look on Kennedy’s face before he turned his back on him and walked away. Julissa told him that he had nothing to worry about, that he was going to get through the drought when it mattered the most. Didn’t that match matter? The Strength in Numbers titles were the last championship belts the Hand was in possession of, and he was responsible for that loss. He wondered what the rest of the Hand really thought of him, whether or not they just kept him around because of Julissa. Considering what happened to Duncan Drake when his services were no longer necessary, he wasn’t absolutely sure if the moniker actually bothered him. He doubted he’d ever be tempted to actually ask the question, and hoped that no one else would even think about it. *tap* *tap* *tap* The sound of a finger tapping against the passenger side glass spooked him enough to make his hands slip from the wheel and slam against the horn. It doesn’t take him more than a brief moment to realize that it is Julissa leaning forward to peek through the slightly tinted window. A quick tap of the door switch flicks the lock open, but he still wasn’t ready for the sudden wave of light that fills the interior of the car when the Mexican Minx pulls the door open to slide into the rental. "What the hell are you all jumpy about?" she asks, the door slamming shut as she sinks into the plush leather interior. "Are you kidding me?" he asks in turn, his eyes a bit wide with surprise. "After the shit we just went through?" "It was just a couple of questions," she practically dismisses the rising situation involving Desade. She reaches over and ruffles his hair, a toothy grin mischievously playing across her face. "Besides, do you think anyone would try to hurt her son?" "Quit the crap, Juli," he growls, his head jerking away from her reach. "Don’t get your panties in a bunch, Jay, I’m just teasing you." "Well, to tell you the truth, I’m not really in the mood for it. I had enough things on my mind already, and now I feel like Eunice is going to be breathing down my neck ‘til we get through all of this." His hands slid back onto the steering wheel, his grip just as tight as it was earlier. "Eunice doesn’t care enough about you to do that," she giggled a bit, just loud enough to make sure he heard it. "All of her attention is on Alex right now, so don’t worry about what just happened." "If you say so." He fumbles to get a grip on the keys already in the ignition, his eyes narrow and his brow furrows as his lips slowly curl into a frown. "What’s really bothering you, Jay?" The sound of the Hemi engine roaring to life, as Jadian turns the key, nearly drowns her out. Knowing this, Juli sort of repeats herself. "Are you going to tell me what’s going on or what?" "You know what’s wrong," he snaps at her. "Things were supposed to be different, but nothing’s changed… And I doubt anyone still believes I’m playing my part." "So you dug yourself into a hole," she replies flippantly. "It’s not like this can last forever. Hell, you’re bound to catch a break sooner or later. You’re due." "That really makes me feel better." "You know what you need to do." It was more of a statement than a question, but she still managed a slight pause before she continued. "You need to take this time off to go visit your parents. Get away from this for a little while - be yourself - and everything will fall into place on its own." "Be myself?" he lets out a hearty chuckle. "I doubt I’ve ever been able to be myself around my parents." "It’s the closest thing you have to a normal life, Jay…" she trails off, the fact that she drove away the only thing resembling a normal life like a bus driver on the express lane. "Take advantage of it when you can." "You wanna come with me?" "Please," she scoffs at the suggestion. "Your mother hates me." "Come on, it’s not like you have anything better to do." "I’ll think about it." "No you won’t." He was right, she wouldn’t think about it again.
