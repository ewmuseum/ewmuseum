In Sickness And In Health (Young Lions Tournament RP)

16 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/16/2007 As much as he didn’t want to admit it, he saw something in little Amy Walker. He sat in his bed, watching her check her MySpace profile from his old computer. On the night stand next to his bed was a box of DayQuil, some Kleenex and a half eaten bowl of chicken soup. "You know, you don’t have to stay." Matthew was just a little uneasy about things. She had just turned 18, but she was still a High Schooler. He didn’t know what she saw in him that she didn’t see in High School boys. Maybe it was the fact that he wasn’t in High School. Maybe it was the fact that he was now somewhat of a local celebrity. He’s now considered one of the most famous wrestlers to come out of Western Michigan. Maybe, maybe it was because of the crush that she had on him years ago. He didn’t know, but since she turned 18, he’s seen something in her that he hadn’t seen before. A glow almost. "Yeah, I do. Your mom wanted me to make sure that you felt better. That’s all. After all, who else is going to make you more soup when you need it?" She was right about that, Matthew was the kind of cook that would burn water. "Matt, how come you don’t have a MySpace page. I mean, there are a ton of wrestlers that do. It’s a way to reach out to your fans." "Because, I have better things to do than mess with some website." "Like World of Warcraft?" Shit, his secret was out. He had just installed World of Warcraft on that computer. He hadn’t started up his account yet though. "Uh, well……ummmm………" "Don’t worry about it, if you want, I can start up a MySpace account for you and run it for you. That way you have a chance to communicate with your fans." Matthew sunk down into his bed and pulled the covers over him and closed his eyes. "Yeah, you can do that." After a couple of hours, Matthew opened his eyes, and felt an arm wrapped around him. Amy had fallen asleep next to him, he turned over and looked at her face. She opened her eyes. "Hi." She sat up, she couldn’t believe that she fell asleep. "You were having a nightmare, and I wanted to let you know that you weren’t alone. I can’t believe that I fell asleep. I’m so sorry. I think I should probably go." "It’s OK. You don’t have to leave if you don’t want to." "You….you mean that?" Matthew thinks for a moment, and then looks right at the back of neck. He slides himself closer to her. "Yeah, I do." "Why?" That was the toughest question that she could ever ask him. An answer that he didn’t know the best answer to. No one did. "I don’t know. But over the last few weeks, I didn’t see you as Lance’s sister. I stopped seeing you as the little girl that used to see you as. I don’t know, you just, grew on me." "I didn’t want to ask you this, but I have to. Why weren’t you at his funeral?" She never knew that he was there. Even his parents thought that he didn’t go. Only one other person knew that he was there that day. He didn’t even know that they knew until only a couple of days ago. ‘I miss him every day. He was my shining light. I knew that every day, he was protecting me from the evils of the world. No matter how old I got, he was still my overprotective older brother. When I first heard about what happened, I asked God, "Why did you do this?" ‘For the first time in my life, I didn’t have that blanket of protection. For the first time in my life, I didn’t have my older brother there, protecting me from the evils in this world. But then I realized that he was still there, still looking over me, still protecting me. Only, now he’s doing it from God’s side.’ Amy turned in the bed and looked at Matthew, a tear rolling down her cheek. "I said that at is funeral. How did…?" "You asked your mom who the man in long hair was. She told you that she didn’t know and when you went to the guest book, you looked down the list of names. You came across the name Walker Chandler. That was what I signed the book as. I didn’t feel right being there, but I had to be there." More tears came down Amy’s face. For months, she didn’t forgive Matthew for not showing up at the funeral. Eventually, she finally forgave him. But for her, it wasn’t easy. She closed her hand around the dog tags that her brother wore when he was killed in action. "I didn’t let them bury him with these. He told me that when he shipped out, that if anything ever happened to him, that he wanted me to have them, that they would protect me." Amy then took the dog tags off and handed them to Matthew, closing his hand around them. "He would want you to have them. You were always like a brother to him. Even when the two of you had that argument, he didn’t think anything badly about you. He knew that you were hurt, but he also knew that you were scared. Scared that you were never going to see your best friend again." Matthew clenched his hand tight, and then opened it back up. "I can’t accept these." "If you care for me, then you will take them. Wear them to the ring with you." "I can’t." "Yes, you can. I want you to, and I’m sure that Lance would want you to also." "OK, but these aren’t mine. When the time comes, I’m going to return these to you. They belong to your family." "Matthew Elias, don’t you realize? You are family. Always have been, always will be. Just because Walker blood doesn’t run through your veins doesn’t change that fact." "Thank you." Matthew puts the dog tags on and lays back in the bed. Amy then pulls herself back and lays in his arm. Putting her hand on the dog tags and sliding them over his heart. "Do you ever wonder, what it would have been like if Lance was here and saw us like this?" "I’m pretty sure he would want to kick my ass right about now." Amy gets a chuckle out of this and lays her head on his chest. "I feel safe, right here and right now. Lance looking down on me from Heaven, and being here with you. I don’t feel like anything bad can happen to me right now." Matthew puts his arm around Amy’s body, giving her a little hug. "Matt, have I ever told you that I loved you." "Not in a long time." "Well, I don’t think I just love you. I think I’ve fallen in love with you." Some peace came to Matthew. He drifted back to sleep, but not before kissing Amy on the top of her head.
