Date: 2/18/09 'If you want - truly want - to stand on the opposite side from me, I wish you the best of luck. I will be in College Park, as I have been at every broadcast of this program. One day, you will look back and say that I gave birth to Sin City.' - Alexandra Eloise Pierce _________________________________________________________ Ten minutes after calling out arguably the most dangerous person in wrestling, and Phillip Kennedy was already on his way out of the arena. He hadn't even taken a shower yet, and showed no inclination of doing so. He carried his duffel bag in his right hand, power-walking to the back door even as he reached into his pocket with his left hand and fished out his cell phone. Pushing several buttons to bring up the screen needed to change his voicemail, Kennedy spoke into the phone, even as he stepped outside into the cold Philadelphia night. 'My phone's off for the next week,' Phillip said, 'while I train for my match next week. I'll return any calls after Temptation: Maryland. Kat, I love you, but this is something I've got to do myself. I'll see you next week, once this is all said and done.' Click. With the finality of an executioner's axe, Kennedy stabbed at the 'off' button again, holding it down until the phone turned itself off. Tucking it safely back into his pocket, Kennedy arrived at his rental car. He unlocked the car, throwing his duffel bag into the passenger's seat. Phillip followed it in, closing the door with a resounding 'slam' and gunning the ignition. As Kennedy backed out of the space and burned rubber away from the arena, he turned on the radio, twisting the dial until he found a rock station. Music determined, he wound down the driver's side window, letting the cold wind sting him as he sped down the road. His eyes darted from side to side, as if searching for something. The car was moving fast enough that if a cop had been in the vicinity, Kennedy would likely have seen some flashing lights. Fortunately, though, the fuzz seemed to be busy elsewhere. As if on a whim, he performed a last second, ninety degree turn into the parking lot of a diner. Kennedy threw the car into park in the first parking space he found, and exited the vehicle. Phillip walked briskly towards the front door. A few moments later, and he was seated at the counter. In front of him was a large glass of Coke, and an order for a double bacon cheeseburger with onion rings had been sent to the kitchen. With everything else he needed at the moment taken care of, and his mind momentarily not busy, the enormity of the evening began to settle in. 'Well, Hell,' the Big Stack murmured, under his breath. 'Now what the heck do I do?' Kennedy took a sip of his soda, and nodded to himself. Well. When Lauren or Kathryn had problems, it seemed to Phil that they always came up with plans. Usually elaborate ones, probably because they'd been trained by the queen of the elaborate plan. Paper wasn't a problem; after all, Phillip had a rather nice paper placemat in front of him that he could use. He reached back into his pockets, searching for a pen of some type, but came up empty. 'Shit.' No pen. How was he supposed to come up with a plan to earn Alexandra Pierce's respect if he didn't have a pen? He took another sip of soda. The Dead Man's Hand thought outside the box. He could work around this difficulty. It was about then that he caught a family of four walking into the diner. As they were led to their seats, Phillip saw the waitress reach behind her podium, grab a small packet, and hand the packet to the youngest child. Of course. With a silly smile on his face, Phillip caught the waitress' attention on her way back to the podium. 'Excuse me, Miss,' he drawled, putting on his country accent, 'but could I trouble you for something?' * * * * * The Master Plan of Phillip Benjamin Kennedy (as written down at the Colonial Diner on a placemat in red crayon) 1. Turn off cell phone so I have no distractions (already done) 2. Go train harder than I have ever trained in my life. 3. Figure out some way to get the fans behind me. 4. Beat Alexandra Pierce with a billion lariats. 5. Point and laugh at Amy Campbell for doing something she could never do. P.S. If she tries to claim she's a higher ranking champion than I am, explain to her that being champion of sucking off co-workers isn't especially prestigious. _________________________________________________ 'But with that said, you owe me an explanation of a few things, Director. One thing I said before you arrived is absolutely true. I tried to contact you for two months. Two months to talk to you. At the time, I was your primary soldier on the ground. Waving the banner of the Hand. Mayhem was AWOL. Bridden was AWOL. You had me. And you had me turned away at the door multiple times in favor of...her.' -- Phillip Benjamin Kennedy __________________________________________ Phillip Kennedy's eyes narrowed as he glared daggers at the red heavy bag swaying before him. He was already exhausted, t-shirt matted to his chest with sweat, a fine sheen of perspiration coating his skin. That didn't matter though, now. Nothing mattered except for the heavy bag before him. He looked down at his left arm, bruises formed on it from throwing lariat after lariat. He'd have time to heal later. For now, he needed to know how hard he could throw one. *THUNK* Sweat flew from the impact of arm on heavy bag, sending the bag swinging back and forth on its chain. Kennedy groaned, running the fingers of his right hand over the bruises on his dominant arm. Still, he was satisfied with the sight of the heavy bag continuing to swing, back and forth, back and forth. *THUNK* Another lariat, and another yell of pain. The bag continued to sway back and forth, but by now, Kennedy didn't see a heavy bag. He didn't even see Alexandra Pierce. What he saw was Amy Campbell, Universal Championship draped over her shoulder. *THUNK!* This time, the yell of pain muffled the sound of impact. Even so, the bag swung with more velocity than it had yet. Kennedy growled, cradling his arm, admiring his handiwork. The lariat was his dominant weapon. It was, to use an old wrestling cliche, what brought him to the dance. As an actual wrestler, he wasn't especially skilled. Brawling was his forte, power moves mixed with punches, kicks, and his devastating lariats were what had allowed him to ascend to the post of SCCW Gateway Champion. And it was going to be what would have to put down Alexandra Pierce at Temptation. Kennedy scratched at two day old stubble on his face as he walked to a bench and took a seat, draping a white towel over his head. He'd told Desade that it didn't matter who won or who lost the match, and to a great extent, that was absolutely true. Kennedy winning wasn't, in and of itself, going to make him do something stupid like break off from the Hand, nor would Desade winning guarantee that he would remain a loyal member. The purpose of this match was to feel Pierce out. See what she could and would bring to bear against him. She had not, he knew, been pinned in a SCCW ring. Some of that was undoubtedly due to the Hand, but the rest? She had won the match at All In without aid from the group. Fuck, she'd pinned the two members of the Hand that had been in the match en route to victory. Desade had talent in the ring that Kennedy simply didn't, a graceful way of moving and executing complex moves. Coupled with her potent mind, she was a difficult opponent for a veteran, let alone a man with Kennedy's experience. 'Then,' he asked himself, perhaps reasonably, 'why are you even doing this?' That, at least, he knew the answer to. He wanted to know just how lethal Desade could be, true, but this was about far more than that. He claimed to want Desade to prove herself to him, but the opposite was also true. Desade's proverbial love affair with Amy Campbell seemed...irrational, at least, to Kennedy's eyes. Amy was a veteran of the sport, yes...but she had proven no capability to win on her own without a Desade plan behind her or copious amounts of interference. Lance Marshall had bounced her around like a pinball at Defiance II, for Christ's sake! There, of course, was always the possibility of an ulterior motive. Maybe Alex simply wanted to keep a potential enemy in sight. That was reasonable, at the very least. Reasonable, but it didn't solve his more immediate problem. The bottom line, in Kennedy's mind, was that Alexandra Pierce underestimated Phillip Kennedy. She underestimated his ability in the ring; the man Charlotte Ramone pinned was most certainly not the man who was Gateway Champion now. More importantly to Phillip, she underestimated his mental capacity. Underestimated his ability to think for himself. And why? Because he had a certain affinity for a beautiful blonde -- who happened to be in Desade's employ? The entire Desade/Kathryn situation made about as much sense as the Desade/Campbell one. Which all brought him back full circle. Were Alexandra Pierce a poker player, she would be a passive-aggressive one. Kennedy knew the type well; they were tough to play against. They would keep their cards close to their vests, rarely raise, call call call, check check check. Until, that is, you checked, and then they'd fire a couple thousand worth of chips to the center of the table without batting an eyelash. Such opponents were tough to read, period. And it was the way that Desade lived her whole LIFE. On Temptation, Phillip Kennedy was well aware that he'd pushed all his chips to the middle of the pile. He was equally well aware that Desade had called. All that was left was to turn the cards over and see what the turn and river brought. __________________________________________ 'I want to be standing in this ring and hear the music hit and know that Alexa--Desade is coming for me. I want to know what Jonathan Rhine felt. What Jared Sykes felt. Just once. Once to confirm that you are everything that you say you are.' -- Phillip Benjamin Kennedy _____________________________________________ The third part of his plan seemed easier said than done. After all, he wagered, Phillip Kennedy's fanbase was somewhat...miniscule. The Dead Man's Hand did have its fans and supporters, but they were limited. Probably because they were a bunch of assholes, Phillip had reasoned. But they were out there. They had to be. There were also, in all likelihood, those that would be willing to jump onto his bandwagon for one night. Alexandra Pierce was the most hated person in wrestling. Kennedy, by comparison, was just some asshole who liked to brag about banging Katsidy. 'How the Hell am I gonna be Jonathan Rhine if no one likes me?' For Kennedy, step three was vitally important. He had asked her to be Desade, but that wouldn't mean as much if he wasn't the hero of the day. Kennedy wanted a crowded house. He wanted to put Pierce on the defensive, make her have to compete against him with the crowd firmly on his side. Only then would he understand just how Lance Marshall got back up, time and time again. It would be his edge. The x-factor. The ace in the hole. Alexandra Pierce could prepare for Kennedy in ring. But she couldn't prepare for Phillip Kennedy: Megaface For The Evening. Of course she couldn't; it had never HAPPENED before. Kennedy had asked for the logical, robotic, Terminator like Pierce. If she showed up, fan support could be the one thing that put him over the top. Of course, it was a moot point if he couldn't GET that support. Kennedy shook his head. Everything was always so damned complex. Frustratedly, he turned back to the virtual poker table in front of him on his laptop, and folded another bad hand. Today seemed to be a day full of bad hands, both in life and in poker. Phillip closed out of his software, and pulled up his web browser. Poker wasn't going so well, so why not try something else? Cocking his head to the side, the Big Stack typed the words 'Phillip Kennedy' into the Google search engine. He hadn't Googled himself since after his first poker tournament victory. That was...a long time ago. If nothing else, he was pleased to see the number of websites that mentioned him had increased exponentially. Most intriguing, though, was the first hit -- something called 'TheBigStack.com.' 'The Hell,' Kennedy asked, clicking on the link, and seeing a well-made page with his image on it, and buttons linking to what seemed to be every aspect of his career. 'I've got a fansite?' It certainly appeared that he did. A meticulously researched one, to boot. One click on the 'biography' page confirmed it. 'Shit,' Phil observed, 'they even have where I went to high school. College too.' ...College. He'd almost forgotten. 'I'm such a moron,' Kennedy groused, realizing something that he knew but hadn't quite understood the significance of. Temptation Maryland was taking place in College Park of the University of Maryland. His alma mater. The arena would likely be full with drunken U of M undergrads looking for someone to scream their heads off for. He was the hometown boy. So, what did he know? He had a fan site, and an arena potentially full of Big Stack partisans. Potentially wasn't good enough, though. So how could be turn 'potential' into 'guaranteed'? The flashing 'Contact the Webmistress' button on the bottom of the page seemed to offer a strong possibility. _______________________________________________________ TheBigStack.com -- Your Internet Home For All Things Kennedy Hey, guys! It's me, your ever faithful Webmistress Jennifer. We've been running this site for six months, and it gives me great pleasure to announce that we've been discovered. By the very subject of this site, no less. Phillip Kennedy emailed me over the weekend, and asked if I could publish something on this site. He told me that he wanted to reach all of those who even would consider supporting him in the toughest match of his career this week against Alexandra Pierce. It goes without saying that I accepted. His words are below. Hi, everyone. This is the first time I've ever done something like this. I've known that I have fans, of course. There's always a smattering of people in Dead Man's Hand, or even Phillip Kennedy shirts in the arenas I travel to. Quite frankly, I don't know what the future holds for me. I firmly believe that I am the best the Dead Man's Hand has to offer. I firmly believe that Amy Campbell can't cut it. And I firmly believe that Alexandra Pierce, to borrow a phrase from our former president, is misunderestimating me. Last week, I stood in the middle of the ring and asked the bitch who retired Jonathan Rhine to come to Maryland this week to face me in the middle of the ring. I told her that I wanted to see what it felt like to be Rhine or Jared Sykes. I'm confident Alexandra Pierce will oblige me, in spite of her lack of presence over the past few weeks. I called her out in front of millions of people. Desade will come for me. She can be Desade with little prompting. Now, me becoming Jonathan Rhine's going to be a little bit more difficult. I'll be blunt. I'm not a good guy. I wear a white hat because Katsidy's dubbed me the savior of Sin City. But Hell, you go to this site. You know who and what I am, and you accept me regardless. So here's what I need. For any of you Sinners in the Maryland area, get yourselves a ticket to Temptation. If you've got one already, great. If not, see if you can get your hands on one, and come on down to College Park. It's my alma mater. I'm hoping to have the place jumping. I want to see thousands of people there to cheer me on, to chant my name. If I'm going to play the hero for once, I don't want to stop at half measures. I want Alexandra Pierce to have to deal with MY crowd. She calls herself the Spider in the Web. She calls herself the Director, and you know what? Maybe she is. But I've got to prove a bunch of things to her. I've got to prove that she's not infallible. That she's flesh and blood, just like everyone else. She's never been pinned in SCCW, and she put my shoulders to the mat once...as Charlotte Ramone. I'm going to show her that she's not invincible. I've got to prove that I am what I say I am. That I'm the King of the Dead Man's Hand. Jadian Bridden can continue to pretend that he's the Ace, but at the end of the day, I'm the best wrestler in the Hand. I throw lariats from any and all angles. I'm tough as the leather on the cowboy boots I wear to the ring. And Alexandra Pierce is gonna damn near have to kill me if she wants to put me on my back for a count of three. Some of you love me. Others of you probably love to hate me. I imagine that many of you are fans of the Hand as well. Let me assure you that I'm not trying to blow up the group. The Dead Man's Hand has given me all the success I have in this sport. I just want a fair shake, and I hope that you, as unbiased observers, recognize that I have not yet been given one. For those of you who are able to make it, I have worked something out with this site's faithful webmistress. I will have 1,000 t-shirts made available at the door to Temptation, to be passed out to those willing to back me, whether henceforth or for one night only. She and I will be there to distribute them. Thank you all for reading. With any luck, this week will go a long way towards proving my case. Whether Alex knows it or not, I'm holding the nuts. - Phillip Kennedy _____________________________________________________ 'Something to drink, sir?' Kennedy looked up from his laptop to find a pretty flight attendant smiling at him, standing behind her cart. He thought about the question for a brief moment before replying. 'Orange juice, please,' Kennedy said, minimizing the fansite on his computer, and punching keys to open his email. In spite of himself, he smiled as he looked at the addresses. Jennifer was there, of course. So were Kathryn and Savant, offering words of encouragement. Even David Walter Smith, Kennedy's agent, so quiet and behind the scenes lately, had sent him an email. The attendant placed the plastic cup of orange juice on Kennedy's tray before moving along to the next passenger. Phil took a long sip of his beverage, and leaned back in his seat. Soon, he would land at BWI airport, a stone's throw from the University of Maryland at College Park. His current plan was to spend some time on campus, catching up with old professors and friends who remained in the area. He'd done all the training he could. He'd set up his plan to the best of his ability. Even with a couple thousand people chanting and cheering for him, the matter at the end of the day was extraordinarily simple. It was Phillip Kennedy vs. Alexandra Pierce, a match that most in Sin City probably thought never would happen. Setting his orange juice back down, Phil closed his eyes. He could easily imagine the match in his head, Desade using her combination of aerial artistry and hard strikes to keep him off balance. Kennedy answering right back with his power and arsenal of lariats. He could imagine the conflicted tone in Eugene Ware's voice, the fat announcer stuck watching two of his favorites do battle. He could hear Dave Gibson, taking, for the first time ever, his side, because the alternative would be too horrible to contemplate. Phillip could see himself poised, launching forward to strike the Spider in the Web with his devastating lariat to the back of the head. Just as easily, though, he could see Desade leaping into the air, knees pressed to his face, to deliver the move that had felled all of her opponents to date. What would happen at Temptation? Lord knew the actual result. But win, lose, or draw, Phillip Kennedy would leave his alma mater with a clear conscience. -Fin- ___________________________________________ 'Those who make peaceful revolution impossible will make violent revolution inevitable.' -- John F.
