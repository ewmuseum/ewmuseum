Self worth and agendas

21 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/21/2008 An orange sun is slowly setting behind Simon King in a sky that is tainted by violets and reds. Behind him is the Vegas strip. On the mahogany desk in front of him are stacks of papers. King is wearing a black dress shirt that is open; the sleeves have been cuffed and rolled to the elbows. He rolls his head in a circle. Pushing away from the desk he stands up turns and looks out onto the strip. His reflection showing up in the glass. "Simon how are things going? Celeste’s voice is soft and causes King to turn around. He slides his hands into his pockets and sits back down. Celeste takes a seat facing King. She’s wearing black pin striped pants and a black v cut shirt with quarter cut sleeves. Her hair is loose and hangs freely just barely touching her shoulders. "They’re going as well as could be expected. On one hand things aren’t going exactly how I wanted them to go in one of the business endeavors. On the other hand everything is going better than expect on the other front." "Simon, speaking of the other endeavor. Do you think it’s wise to keep on that current path? I mean after all... "Stop right there. I know what you’re going to say and you’re wrong. We never know what we’ll be good at in life if we don’t make an honest effort into it. Where we sit right now is because either me giving a total effort to the matter at hand or me not giving a damn. So, the casino situation is all set because of the effort. The sports entertainment aspect of why we are out here I haven’t focused so much on. I only have myself to blame for that." "Simon, do you really want to put all of your focus into it though. I mean think about in all reality. Right now you’re sitting on a bunch of money. What exactly do you have to prove by flying across this country and stepping into a ring with somebody who is chasing a dream and chasing money?" With those words said King stands up and turns his back on Celeste and runs his fingers through his hair. "Simon, it’s a dream isn’t it." "There has always been something nagging at me. I will never go as far as the others that you hear say there is something missing. Not even a void, because I always competed. Whether it was grades or whoever had the biggest and best of everything. The desire has been there and it’s not like I didn’t compete in sports because I did. Soccer, the swim team and I did well in those areas too. But there is something very primal in this. Something that I can’t put my finger on and that is what will drive me." Celeste stands up and King turns around. Her head is down is would almost seem that she’s disappointed in the fact that He is going to continue on. When she does raise her head though there is a faint smile on her lips. A bit of color is in her cheeks. She brushes her hair from her face nods approvingly. "I hope you can understand Celeste." "I think I do Simon." "Well, I’ll be up soon. I have to sign off on some of these papers and there is some union issue that I have to run past the lawyers first thing in the morning and I need to get that all organized." "After that there’s a GCW video from about seven years I want to watch and then I should be up to the penthouse." "I’ll wait up. After you’re done we can go out and get something to eat." "Sounds good to me Celeste." --- On the outside of King’s office Celeste gets out her cell phone and works over the keypad feverishly. It rings and Celeste looks over her shoulder as she walks down the hallway away from the office. "Lordes it’s me." Celeste looks over her shoulder again just to be sure. She holds her hand to her mouth to conceal what is being said. "He’s going to go forward and keep on with the wrestling. I didn’t even get a chance to get a word in otherwise. I’m doing everything just as you said. It seems that he is intent on chasing down some dream. I don’t know what happened once you two were separated." The door to King’s office opens and he watches Celeste for a moment. She hears him and cancels the call. "Celeste wait up. I’ll just get up early and do the stuff with the legal team then." Celeste lets out a low curt sigh. She turns to face King and is all smiles as if she were just united with her best friend in the world.
