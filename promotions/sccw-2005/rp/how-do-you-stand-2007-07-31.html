How do you stand?

31 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31, 2007 The bright lights of the city shine through a car’s windshield, causing shadows to dance across Mayson Colby’s face as his car crawls down the street. He has the windows rolled down to feel the brisk night air that is San Francisco. As the breeze picks up Colby takes a deep breath of the salty air. COUGH Well, the smell of the sewers isn’t all that appealing. The light changes from red to green and the car crawls forward again, only to stop another block away. This time a voice drifts in to the car, and a woman’s face appears in the window. Teeth stuck out every which way, hair snarled and dirty the woman looks as though she hasn’t showered in a month. The smell of rotting flesh floats in the air around her. "The Lord is coming! How do you stand? Oh, the Lord is coming! Your judgment is coming. Repent! Save yourself while you can. He’s…" The light changes and Colby stomps on the gas. Quickly turning the wheel the car flies around the corner, narrowly missing the world’s next prophet as she jumps back, raising her fist, and surely shouting some kind of advice or words straight out of the Lord’s mouth. The next street is nearly pitch black, and feels as though all the happiness has been sucked out of the place. Dark shapes huddle around small fires, and voices call out as he slowly passes. Just as the smell of a hundred different toilets hits the car, Mayson remembers to roll up the window. Colby cranes his neck and turns on the brights as he searches for his destination. The dark shapes cower from the light, and Colby raises his hand in apology. "Ah, there it is!" Colby pulls the car to a stop in front of a metal door with peeling paint. Dark figures scurry away as the engine shuts off. Leaning over the seat, Colby grabs as many paper bags as he can carry. Getting out of the car he bumps the door closed with his hip, and shifts the bags to one arm to set the car alarm. It sounds with a beep, echoing off of the dark buildings. Pulling open the heavy door reveals more bodies cluttering up the hallway. Many are limp and lifeless, yet there are a few who glance up as he walks past, their wide eyes following his every move. Carefully stepping over limps and bodies, Colby makes his way up a narrow staircase and down a poorly lit hallway. Rounding a corner the hall ends in front of a door that once used to be a shade of blue, but now is chipped and a dull gray. Once again shifting the bags, Colby gives the door a hard knock and it pushes open. Suspicious, he pushes the door open further with his foot and sticks his head inside. "Hello?" No answer. "Hello? It’s Mayson. I’m coming in." With that, Colby enters the gloomy apartment and close the door behind him. Immediately to the left sits a small table, where the bags are carefully place. Spinning around, he gets his first full view of the apartment. A trash can sits in one corner, its contents overflowing and spilling out on to the floor. Dirty dishes clutter the sink, their contents crusted on long ago. Flies have free range of everything. The furniture is faded, and in many cases cut or torn, its stuffing poking out. Colby sighs, and sticks his head down the hallway. A small walkway is cut through mounds of clothes. "Hello?!" Colby takes a few steps down the hall and the sounds of a television float out of a closed room. Raising a fist as though to knock, he shakes his head and pushes open the door, stepping inside. Two sets of eyes look towards him as the door opens, and seconds later a young boy and girl dash towards the door. Mayson’s mouth drops with surprise and he takes a step back, bracing himself against the doorway. "Uncle Mayson!" The children wrap their arms around Colby’s legs and look up at him, their eyes sparkle as their smile widens. He looks down at them and puts an arm on each of their shoulders. The corners of his lips rise, resembling a smile. "How’ve ya been kids?" The boy steps back and looks up at his uncle, beaming a smile. "Mom says we’re doing alright. Been awfully hungry though." Colby bends down and scoops up the little girl, throwing her over one shoulder as she giggles. He motions towards the door and the boy follows. When they reach the kitchen, Colby pulls a small chair out from under the table and shoves a pile of junk to the floor. Then he gently places the little girl on the seat as the boy cleans off another seat and sits down. "What do you want to eat?" The boy sits forward and rummages around in the paper bags in front of him. Reaching his hand down he pulls out 2 jars. He holds them towards Mayson and nods. "Peanut butter and jelly!" Colby winks and grabs a loaf of bread from another bag. Giving another look at the sink of dishes, Colby goes back in to the bag and pulls out plastic utensils and some paper plates. Before long, a pile of sandwiches sit on a plate in the middle of the table and both children have their mouths full, and fingers sticky with jelly. "Shamk ‘ou!" Colby laughs and pats the boy on the back. He leans in close to him, and glances at the girl only to find her full attention on the food in front of her. He whispers in the boy’s ear. "She still not talking?" The boy shakes his head and reaches for another sandwich. He grins as he holds one in each hand, alternating bites between the two. Colby stands up and looks down the hallway. "Is she back there?" Both children nod, but Colby is already heading that direction. He walks cautiously down the narrow hallway, as though not sure what awaits him. It seems the further he walks, the darker it gets. At the end of the hallway is a door, slightly cracked open and emitting a thin line of bright light. Colby presses a hand against the door and tries to push it open, but it won’t budge. Leaning down and putting his shoulder against the door, Colby pushes and goes stumbling in to the room. He turns around and sees a small dresser shoved to the side. A weak barricade. As he turns around, Colby’s grin fades. Standing an inch away from his face is a pale young woman with long dark hair. Her arms and legs look like sticks, and her collar bone protrudes through her shirt. She points a long finger out towards Colby’s chest. "You! Get out of my house! We don’t need any charity from you. I don’t want nothing from you. Not "Mr. I is famous so I’m better than you"! Out!" She drives her finger in to Colby’s chest with each word. He remains calm as her fingernails try to pierce his flesh. She raises her hand to him as though to slap him, but Colby grabs her wrist easily. He holds her arm up to his face before narrowing his eyes and throwing her on to the bed. "Track marks?! I thought you said you quit that shit? You’re going to fucking die and leave Melissa and Andrew without a mother. I won’t let you fuck up their lives too!" The woman looks up at Colby and grins, or at least tries to with the few teeth she has left. "And your life is so fucking great? What have you done to make this wonderful world so fucking great? I don’t see you buying me a house! I don’t see you sending me money." Colby looks around the room, and spots a syringe, lighter, and soda can on the dresser. He walks over and picks them up, waving them towards his sister before walking over to the window and tossing them outside. She jumps off the bed and leans out of the window before spinning around and slapping Colby’s cheek. He makes no effort to move, simply stares at her. "That is why I don’t give you money. You don’t deserve a cent. For those kids out there, I would do anything. For you, rot in hell." Colby turns, ignoring her screams of profanity and slams the bedroom door behind him. He finds Melissa and Andrew standing near their bedroom door, staring at him. Colby tries to fake a smile and pulling out his wallet, he hands Andrew a wad of bills. Squatting down, Colby hugs each of them. "You may need that one day. Put it in a place your mother won’t find it, you hear me?" Colby stands and waits for the boy’s nod, before heading back towards the kitchen. The children disappear in to their room and Colby puts away the rest of the groceries. He fills the sink with hot soapy water for the dishes to soak, just as the kids come around the corner. "It’s safe." Colby nods and starts towards the door. "You two go get some sleep. Drew, come lock the door behind me. Don’t forget what I told you." With that said, Colby heads out in to the hallway and disappears in to the darkness.
