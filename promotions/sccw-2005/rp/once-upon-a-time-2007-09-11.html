Once Upon A Time

11 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/09/07 "We are given one life, and the decision is ours whether to wait for circumstances to make up our mind, or whether to act, and in acting, to live" Omar Nelson Bradley Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Maria. She was ten years old and wanted for absolutely nothing. She lived in a huge house with a bedroom that was like a children’s toy store. Unlike her school friends she grew up in a family where both parents were still together, she had a younger sister who was her best friend and decent money was coming in so financial hardship was alien to them. For her, she lived in a Mexican success story while those around her were not so fortunate. If you were to drive by her home or spend a little time with the Lopez family, you would believe that they had the perfect life. On the surface they did but in reality things were not as rosy as this fairytale beginning may seem. Ramiro Lopez fought against the pressures of life. Every day he strived to be the best father, the best husband and the biggest success that he could be. Yet the more he pushed himself to be all those things, the harder it became to do it alone. Eventually when those pressures of day to day living became too much, he found a crutch with which to lean on and it came in the form of a bottle. It started off with a small flask of whiskey tucked away in a desk drawer and quickly whisked out for a pick me up in the afternoon. The addiction did not take long to take hold however, particularly when his wife Juanita announced she was expecting their second child and Maria’s future sister, Claudia. With her birth things did not get any better and in fact, they just kept getting worse. This particular afternoon was no different to any other. Maria had been dropped home from school by a friend of the family and she walked into the yard with a smile on her face as she saw their German shepherd dog bounding towards her. Flinging her arms around him, she giggled as he licked her face playfully. "Jugaremos pronto" she laughed to him as she ruffled his fur and picked up her school bag that she had dropped on the way. She was forever being told off for ignoring her homework and spending more time with her pet than the studies even at this tender age. Cheerily she dashed up the front steps and through the porch door into the house. Dropping her satchel down, she glanced around the lounge to find nobody around but then the loud voices upstairs gave her parents presence away. Fear gripped her but she couldn’t resist the urge to make sure that everything was okay. She had heard these fights more and more as of late and even though she was only a little girl, she knew just how serious they were. Her legs felt heavy with trepidation as she slowly carried herself up the stairs. "Usted ramera desagradecida!" was the yell from her father which was met with an incomprehensible scream of anguish. Maria tiptoed upstairs and onto the hallway as she crept towards her parents bedroom. Their arguing was more intense than ever before and she could feel her stomach tightening through fear. At night when the arguments were at their most severe, she would press the pillow against her ears so she couldn’t hear them raging against each other. It had been peaceful for a few weeks before today and she had truly believed that Mummy and Daddy were happy again. Now this latest battle was a shock to her system and as she made it to the bedroom door, she could see the shapes of her parents moving around the room. Her father had his back turned while her mother was screaming at him in Spanish, her hands gesticulating wildly. Maria’s little fingers slipped between the slight gap where the door was not quite shut and she gently opened it a little further. She did so just in time to see Juanita take hold of Ramiro’s arm and try to force him to face her. With that, Ramiro swept around and slammed his fist hard into his wife’s left cheek. The force of the blow sent her crashing hard into the chest of drawers next to her and she instantly grabbed for her right arm as she felt pain rushing through it. Her husband had the wildest of eyes as he grabbed her by the hair and pulled her up to her full height. The verbal abuse flew at her as he tossed her onto the bed like a rag doll. Within a rage fuelled instant, he began to choke her with both hands around her neck extracting every breath he could from the helpless victim. Juanita’s struggle was frantic and desperate yet she knew she could not compete with the physical superiority of her much larger husband. He refused to release her as he just kept swearing and abusing her in their native tongue, he would not be satisfied until she learned respect. "Mama!" Ramiro froze on the spot and looked down at the panic stricken Juanita whose hands were still attempting to prise his own away from her throat. He had lost that animalistic rage and instead his eyes were wide with shock as he gradually turned to the left to see his ten year old daughter, Maria, standing in the doorway with tears cascading down her face. She had seen everything and he knew it too. Slowly his fingers released their grip and the shock caused him to almost collapse off the bed onto the floor. Juanita scrambled up to a sitting position and drew her knees up to her chest protectively, quickly beckoning Maria towards the bed. Even though she was a child, she was desperate to do something to protect her mother so she ran over and jumped up to Juanita’s side and cuddled up close to her. Ramiro was gripped by guilt and shame as he pressed himself against the far wall and slowly slid towards the door. Neither female would dare to look at him as Juanita turned her face away and Maria reached up to stroke her mothers hair. He tearfully murmured some kind of weak apology as he slipped away into the hallway, leaving the physical and emotional trauma behind him. Once upon a time, does not always mean happily ever after. While Maria Lopez hid her motives and intentions behind a wall of make up and a smile, Paul Cain was being transparent in his feelings. Of course it was hard not to be when mosquito like reporters were busily flying around, playing guessing games at just what Maria Lopez big statement could be based on. One such annoyance had decided to park her car directly in front of his meaning that without a rather large insurance bill later, Paul was going nowhere. She had blonde hair, blue eyes, a tight top, short skirt and an annoyingly girly giggle. You could have written the word "Bimbo" on the head and nobody would have complained because it appeared so accurate a description. Even more so when this alleged ‘reporter’ opened her mouth and came up with the well researched question of. "Mr Cain, are you a racist?" Paul looked at her, then looked behind at the confused expression on the face of his rather dark skinned friend, Damien Kahn. "Um. Are you a natural blonde?" was his smart mouthed reply. It turned out to be a little too smart for the young woman who stood with a blank expression on her pretty little face. "I think the evidence behind me kind of speaks for itself don’t you? Now what is it exactly that you want from me, other than to delay me further and make my already flustered ‘partner’ think that I’m going to bail out on him?" Paul Cain had never been a big fan of the media and he was even less of a fan of the bimbo species of female. Since Maria Lopez had stirred up her little hornets nest with FUSE, he had gone from being anxious of her presence to slightly ticked off, to put it mildly. Had she stuck to her mind games she might have had more of an impact on his life but now she was beginning to get under his skin in an itching flea biting kind of way. Now she had created a stir and journalists in the wrestling industry and those of more local interest to FUSE were buzzing around and looking for answers. Paul was determined to give them nothing, the only person he owed an answer to at the moment would be himself as he questioned whether he still had the lust for glory and gold that he had all those years ago. Could he still do whatever it required to serve his own desires, could he side with someone he distrusted and then be prepared to stick the knife in them immediately after as FUSE officials seemed to hope? Paul Cain needed the same answer that everyone was looking for. What kind of man is Paul Cain? "We just want to know Paul, what is the truth behind this situation with Miss Lopez?" A smile crept across Paul’s face as he thought carefully of his response. "Other than the fact that she’s run out of batteries for her vibrator and she’s run out of men who are stupid enough to fall for her games so now she can’t get any real action? That’s not what the answer you wanted is it?" The deadpan reaction from his opposite number said it all. "Maria Lopez has petitioned for you to not only be removed from employment at FUSE Wrestling but also that you should be investigated by police concerning a previous complaint. Everyone wants to know the truth, who is Paul Cain and what is going on here?" Paul could feel the eyes of Damien on him and from the corner of his vision, he could see the massive individual go to move forward. Cain raised his hand and warned him off before addressing the woman softly and calmly. "That seems to be the million dollar question, who is Paul Cain? You know what, even *I* don’t know the answer to that at the moment. Am I the kind of man who can sacrifice my own glory and walk away from a fight to potentially sabotage that of a partner? Am I the kind of man who can fight the good fight for the team and then spill the blood of the man who stood by my side only ten minutes beforehand? Am I the kind of man who will see the win for my team then go on to fight the fair fight and shake the hand of my former partner, win or lose? Right now, you don’t know who Paul Cain is and neither do I, so if WE don’t know, you can be damn sure that a wronged ex lover from four years ago doesn’t know who I am, today." The cogs in the brain of the young woman go around and around as she tries to figure out whether there is a clue somewhere in that statement. She quickly scribbles down some notes and then goes to raise another question which is quickly halted by Paul’s raised hand. "We will all find out really soon who Paul Cain is and you know what, I can’t wait to find out and when I do, you’ll be the first to know sweetheart." With that, he dismissively brushes past her and sighs under his breath. He had grown tired of these pointless questions and he had already decided that confronting it head on was better than hiding away. The parasites would eventually get their wish but only when he was ready to reveal it all. In the meantime, he needed to find out the truth about his hunger, his desire and his will to win. Was The Lethal Cocktail really alive, or was this just a ghost of a past existence? "WAIT PAUL, WAIT!"" He groaned and turned his head back in the direction of the reporter. "So does this mean we can rule out you being a racist then?" A grin creeps across Paul Cain’s face as he slowly and sarcastically claps her. "If this is the best that the female species has to offer, I think things are going to be alright." The scene slowly fades to black as Cain turns away from her and heads towards his car. So, who wants to tell Paul that things are NEVER alright in this business?
