Johnnin'

5 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/08 There had been somewhat of a sickening smell that decided to take up temporary residence since Charley had left on his spiritual pilgrimage. Dropping his luggage on the kitchen floor of his Las Vegas condo, he sighed a heavy one and put his nose to the air in order to trace the odor’s whereabouts. It was nice to hang his hat in a dwelling that finally offered a little familiarity. With all the moving around in these past six months; driving for miles in rental cars, the Honduras trip, and the countless airport terminals that had offered about as much comfort as a bed of razors, this upscale little joint was his space away from space. It was the little things that made it his - the Nerf basketball hoop hanging off the front of the bedroom door. The Raiders throw blanket draped over the middle of the couch. His extensive collection of 60’s and 70’s country vinyl. The roll of toilet paper sitting on top of the commode: not on the holder due to sheer laziness, but as a matter of convenience. A photograph of he and his mom (age 8) posted on the fridge with a California Raisin magnet. The big silver trashcan that Charley would often pretend was an R2-D2 when he disposed of his waste… "Oh shit…the garbage." The scent was not of familiarity, nor was it of patterned behavior. It was a mixture of banana peel, leftover primavera, and the remnants of a half-eaten turkey sub. Charley lifted the lid off the receptacle, making sure to beep loudly in order to properly mimic the Star Wars character it was fashioned from. He pulled the bag up and proceeded to set it on the tiled floor, but a carefully folded envelope caught his attention before he could pull the Gladlock straps tight. Charley reached inside and snatched the letter, shaking his head at the fancy curly-Q style font that the Troublemaker was fond of using. The still-sealed envelope was originally waiting for him inside his locker after All In months ago. Curiosity was indeed a killer, and although Charley had assumed that a much nicer explanation of things that were revealed on camera rested inside, he folded it up and had promptly disposed of it once he had returned home. Back to now, it had been a refreshing trip that had cleansed his mind, and Cookie no longer held any ill will to the play. The curiosity was still there, even though it had been some time since their last encounter. Charley reached his pinky finger in the crease and split the envelope at the top, pulling hastily scrawled words out of its cell. Charley, When you read this, I’m sure you will have nothing to say to me. However, I feel as though you’re at least entitled to some form of closure. I first set my sights on you because you seemed like the perfect target: a pawn on the board that I planned to use as a side decoy to win the checkmate. You were so simple to deceive…and although I was thoroughly repulsed by you upon first impression, I did grow to like you very much. I’m sure you won’t believe that, but take it as you will: the conversations shared…the cute little facial expressions you made when we kissed…your smile…all of that made me feel like a lovestruck little girl for the first time in ages. But, you and I are two very different people: while you may make light of it on-camera, your personal life and business life are two completely different things. With me, both are one in the same. I used you, Charley, not because I wanted to…but because I had to. I wish I could say that this wasn’t the end. Please don’t take it personally if I don’t acknowledge you if we were to cross paths backstage. - Dru. Charley stood complacent, shaking his head. Never before had he imagined that the characters in this place would mirror their fucking personas at the local supermarket. "Fuck me," Charley blurted aloud. "If only I had decided to keep the Martian bit when I came to this goddamn nuthouse." * * * * * * * Creak! The garbage chute had seen better days, but apparently the renovations made to the complex two years ago didn’t include WD-40 for the hinges. With no effort, Charley dropped the bag…along with the crumpled note…directly down the hatch to its new home in the dumpster below. Closure? The pleasure was all his, Ms. Crawford. Smiling to himself, the Cookie Monster turned and paced the fifty feet back to his bachelor pad, free of the smell of deceit. Later that night, his canary yellow bedspread never felt so warm… "His dreams of her were red and violent, like the storm in Jupiter’s eye." Sitting atop a throne, talons replaced her fingers as she clawed at her kneeling subjects. Blood sprayed from open wounds like automatic sprinklers in Hell, covering the palace walls as if though someone were firing shotgun shells from a water pistol. She smiled and licked her lips at the boy, rising in her royalty as see-through robes flowed along the contours of her body. The boy cowered in the middle of an Egyptian rug, shutting his eyes tightly to melt away the horrible sight. A razor-sharp caress of his cheek slit the skin like a red fountain pen pressing hard on notebook paper. He could feel her forked tongue tickle his left lobe, and the hiss was plenty audible. "I feel like I can be myself around you. Like you're never judging me…" Her sunkissed hair splashed him in the face as she spun back, vanishing into thin air. * * * * * * * There was mild perspiration stained on the pillowcase as Charley’s eyes squinted, adjusting to the light that fought hard to break through the Venetian blinds. Sighing, he examined the clock – he’d hoped to have a comfortable cushion before having to catch his flight…but this would not be the case, as the LCD displayed 10:42. The nightmare was a fleeing though. Although Charley rarely recalled his dreams anymore, he felt no need to reflect, as there was a more important issue at hand. "Hey, rise and shine." His hand gently brushed the strands of red hair out of her eyes. With her mouth partially open, she did not even stir. A man has weaknesses. Charley’s included a) a "healthy" addiction to the finest Las Vegas had to offer, and b) a lack of stick-to-itiveness when it came to resisting that urge. When your pockets are deep and when great sex is merely a phone call away though…why not? After another failed attempt to wake Jenny…Gheri…Janice…whatever her name was…Cookie sat up and stretched his arms skyward and rose out of bed. He still needed to take a morning shit and wash his face – maybe after that she’d wake up, so he wouldn’t have to resort to dirtier tactics to get the call girl the fuck out of Dodge. Why she even stayed was a mystery to him: in the typical male fashion, Crisp had KO’ed only a minute after climax. Maybe she didn’t want to leave without payment. Maybe she just wanted to experience a good night’s sleep on an authentic Serta mattress. Hell, she might have just wanted to spend some time next to a John that didn’t weigh 300 pounds and reek of shame and desperation. Either way, Cookie was fine with it. Now he didn’t feel so bad about having to stiff her on the gratuity. The duke was that of magnificence – as there was nothing like dropping your kids off on your very own throne. Throne. Throne. Throne. Wisps of the Drusilla dream came sweeping back, rendering him unable to focus on the August issue of Esquire. It set fire to a small fuse inside – the contents of the letter had finally started to get to him. He wasn’t used to taking any sort of "break-up" personally…most relationships he had were doomed to failure due to the fact that most women were on a completely higher wavelength than him. This was different, though, as the girl who called herself Malice seemed more internally skewed than he was. He held nothing against the bombshell that was dropped at All In. He took her panicky episodes in stride. Shit, he would’ve went along with her if it didn’t mean having to kiss the ass of the man who tried to break his neck from the top of a cage. That motherfucka. It got personal that night, for sure. Charley wanted nothing more than to dip his balls into a vat of HGH and teabag him. Subject of thought was a dinghy headed for Cuba. Staring in the mirror as he dollped some Crest on his toothbrush, Charley pondered his other "failed relationship" that had developed with the enemy. His newfound desire to wax heroic might not have been such a great idea after all: Charlotte "Alexandra" Ramone knew plenty about the nappy-headed ho looking back at him in the glass. Certainly enough to ruin any sort of life Cookie may have dreamed of having after he hung the boots up for good. Backstage camaraderie and a lack of substantial friends always ends up with one sharing their heart to someone who seemed to care. Habits, situational decisions, family matters, the restaurants he frequented: Desade didn’t need to do any digging – all she needed was a Talkboy. Trust was never an issue for Charley Crisp. Cookie smiled at the makeup-smeared face of Jeannie as she stood across from him at the doorway, previous night’s skirt and halter stuffed carelessly in her pocketbook. Wrapped up inside was a wad of hundreds he’d given her for the…maid service… Cookie also had the decency to give her a pair of sweatpants and his old high school football state championship T-shirt so that she remained inconspicuous in her walk out of the condo lobby. Who the fuck was he kidding: with a look like hers, she couldn’t have been more conspicuous if the shirt read "I just received cash for sex from Charley Crisp and all I got was this lousy T-shirt." She left her business card (I’m sure the folks at Kinko’s had a good chuckle over that one) and a very satisfied customer in Charley. After a quick sweep check of his humble abode, Charley grabbed his suitcase and headed out the door minutes behind her, taking his last glimpse of the apartment for the next few weeks. "Hey, Chuck!" There was only one person he tolerated using that handle. "Jerry!" Charley turned around after closing the door, stuffing his keys into his pocket to free the hand he used to greet the friendly maintenance man. "Off on another trip?" he asked. Charley nodded his head. "Vermont." Jerry smiled. "Pretty country up there. Got a young one in that nick of the woods. Says crime isn’t a problem…everyone up that way just minds their own. Must be nice." Charley patted Jerry on the shoulder. "Maybe you oughta just quit this job and come along with me, then. Little time outta here would do you some good, don’t you think?" The fix-it clad in blue coveralls gave a tremendous belly laugh. "Hell, you think a place like that’s gonna get me a blowjob for fifty? Snooty ass Yankee whores don’t even offer a handshake for that much." Cookie cackled as he drug his suitcase behind him, heading eastward to the promised land of the million-dollar dirty sanchez.
