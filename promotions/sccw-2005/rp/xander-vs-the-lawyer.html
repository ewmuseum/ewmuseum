Xander vs THE LAWYER!!!!

undated

Date: Today "How soon can it be done?" Xander asks over the phone as he pulls into his parking spot. "You can assure me that it will be done by then. Good, that’s real good. That will work then. Just remember that it has to be done by then." Xander hangs up the phone and pulls a couple of dry cleaning bags from his car. He looks around and makes his way towards the elevator that goes from the garage to the condos. He notices that there are other people that are there already, so he tries to blend in. Well...blend in as much at a 19 year old that stands 6’8 and can afford one of these units on his own can. He enters with the rest of the people that were waiting. One of the people looks up at him and instantly recognizes him. Normally, out in what would be called "the real world" would come the awe of literally standing in the shadow of a celebrity, even a minor one at that. But this man didn’t feel awestruck in being in the presence of the SCCW LiveWire Champion. In fact, the moment he realized who he was standing next to, he started to get nauseous. The man didn’t just not like wrestling. He flat out hated it. This man had single handedly tried to prevent wrestling in the State of California for the better part of the decade. He was a family law attorney who specialized in suing the Video Game industry because of the perceived increase in violence because of them. He also unsuccessfully sued SCCW after a young kid gave his next door neighbor a Rhine Rewind from the top of a table to the patio below. "Well, I see they’ll let anyone move in here." the attorney said loud enough so that everyone in the elevator could hear him. "Excuse me." Xander replied. "I take it that you aren’t a fan of what I do." "Wow, the ‘entertainer’ actually has a brain. That’s surprising for the people in your line of work." the attorney continued. This wasn’t going to be fun for Xander, he already knew this. "Let me ask you something, young man, do you consider what you do to be ‘entertainment’?" Xander looks down at the lawyer, lowers his sunglasses and responds, "I don’t know. Do you consider what you do practicing law? After all, every time I see your sorry ass on TV, all you’re talking about is how parents need to keep their kids from playing Grand Theft Auto and trying to get my line of work banned in this state." The older gentleman was taken back by this as everyone else in the elevator turned around. "Let me ask you something. Where do you get the right to tell parents what they should allow their kids to play or watch? Last I checked, this was the United States of America, not the United States of Overblown Windbags that pad their coffers by filing frivolous lawsuits that end up becoming the demise of companies like XPW and Midway." Xander knew that the last part wasn’t true, that XPW was doomed because it was owned by a porn company and well, Midway was making garbage games like Mortal Kombat vs DC Universe and TNA Impact. But it was enough to stop the salty haired litigator in his tracks. "Also, if you ever watch me on SCCW telecasts, I tell people to turn the crap off, not to order the pay per views, not to buy tickets and not to waste their money on merchandise that was going to go out of style quicker than plaid bell bottoms." He was rolling now, and still doesn’t allow a rebuttal from the lawyer that had just insulted what Xander did for a living. "No, I’m not an ‘entertainer’(complete with finger quotes). I do not portray a ‘character’ (more finger quote action here people) like Spacely or King Blueberry or Rick Malloy. I am a…" Xander notices that the litigious leach is shaking his head at this point. "Don’t you shake your head at me. You got me started, sir. Let me finish, and then if we haven’t gotten to your floor, you can finish whatever moronic thought that you had going through your head. Now, as I was saying, I am a wrestler. Not only am I a wrestler, but I’m also a champion. "With that, I think I’m a little more qualified than a pencil pusher that makes ten large an hour trying to sue because Mass Effect is ‘pornographic’ when you haven’t even played the fucking game. Let me make a suggestion for you. Stop and think before you file a lawsuit or insult a guy that is more inclined to break your neck than to have a friendly conversation in an elevator that is no bigger than his bedroom closet. "Do you get me?" Everyone in the elevator at this point is staring at Xander as the doors open. "Well, this is where I get off. I hope you…oh fuck that. Get bent. Asshole." The doors close and Xander heads towards his unit. http://www.youtube.com/SearleNation Xander is writing at the desk in his hotel room. Lying on the bed behind him is the prize that he had won earlier in the night. The SCCW LiveWire Championship. He continues to write as his cell phone starts to ring. Xander picks up the phone, takes a look at who’s calling and answers. "Yes, mom. I’m fine, just a little sore. Thank you. I know you’re proud. I know that Aunt Connie is proud too. Well, I couldn’t tell you, because if I did, then it could have gotten out and been posted all over the internet that I was going to be in the match, and I couldn’t have that happen." Xander listens as his mom says a few things over the phone and Xander just shakes his head. "Because Paul is not a wrestling name. If you didn’t want me to wrestle as Xander, you wouldn’t have named me Alexander. I understand that I’ve always been your Lil’ Paulie, but I’m grown up…..mom…..Mom…..MOM! Let me finish, please. I’m a grown man. Yes, I know I’m not old enough to drink. If this was Canada, I would be old enough to drink. I don’t drink, mother. Are you kidding me? I don’t do drugs either. I’m hanging up. I’m hanging up the phone now." Xander stops for a moment and rolls his eyes. "I love you too." Xander hangs up.
