But What Am I Gonna Do?

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/2008 She was a pretty girl, tall and athletic, with blond ringlets pulled back loosely behind her head. Her name tag revealed two facts about her: Her name was Maggie, and she worked at IHOP. 'Right, Mags, lemme ask ya sumfin, if I might.' The one speaking to Maggie is a handsome man, also tall, also athletic. And if he were wearing a name tag, it would read: WOMBAT, SCCW. The Wombat, also known as Austin King, didn't wait for Maggie to give her consent to be questioned before he continued. 'Right, so, why do they call it the International House a Pancakes? I've been all over the world, an there ain't none a these little IHOPs anywhere else. Ya know how many Kakenhausen there are in Germany? Nein. An I don't mean one more than eight, eitha. Nevva ate at no Pancake Pagoda in Japan. An when I held the Oro Gigante south a the bordah as Los Wombatos, I didn't see any Casa Pancacca. So what's the deal with International?' Maggie finally turned her attention away from Austin's breakfast companion to scowl at The Wombat, eyebrows knitted in a frown. 'Uh, I could get you my manager...' Maggie answered. Austin waved his fork dismissively. 'Nah, s'aright. Bring me some hot syrup and more creamah, we're straight.' He let out a long sigh as he watched Maggie's hips sway out of sight. 'Seems like I nevva getta have no fun when you're around. Wonda if the sheila eva thought bout bein a wressla?' When he didn't receive an answer, Austin sat, picking at his hashbrowns until he found the silence so uncomfortable, he was forced to speak and fill it. All told, it took about four seconds. 'Right. I guess I should thank ya for comin. Wasn too sure ya'd show, all things considered. It's just...' Austin paused as a young man named Maurice dropped a pitcher oozing brown syrup and a saucer full of flavored creamers on the table. 'Maggie's on break, my name is Maurice, and I would LOVE to take care of you! Anything else I can get for you? Either of you?' Austin shooed Maurice away without a word, and when he was gone, filled a cup halfway with creamer and sugar, pouring coffee on top and stirring. 'It's... well, things ain't the same. It's worse even in the time you've been gone. Yannow, even when Vinnie was at his worse, things was still kinda fun hanging around the Opus. Course, since there is no Magnum Opus, and the The Deadies seem to be EVERYWHERE... Nah, it's more than that. Rhino's gone, an I nevva even got a chance ta tell him what a joy it was to see him compete.' With this, the young man paused, shoving a sausage into his mouth and following it with an obscenely sized fork of omelet. 'Then there's Connors. I mean, he's always been King of the Douchebags, but... this new Connors. He's kidnapped all a me friends! Hell, I'd even play cards with that Truck fella if he was around! Verde, Fuego, I'll bet he's why I can't get ahold of Jimmy too. Now I'll havta talk try and make friends with , I dunno, Charlie Crisp? That Blueberry Jared guy? If ya take away Lance Marshall, who's left to look up to? Sean 'Pottymouth King' Sterling? Things needta change. Things HAFTA change! But what am I gonna do?' Austin watched as the woman sitting across from him reached across the table to spear his last sausage with a long fingernail, lacquered a shiny black. She was, at one time, a pretty girl. She was beyond tall, beyond athletic, planted firmly in the camp of Amazonian. Her once long hair was cut severely short, spiked so you could see pale scalp beneath the tarry black. She popped the sausage in her mouth, and grinned, a rictus smile between lips painted black. The woman, once known at The Black Rose, had taken the name 'Misery' as a joke, needing an ominous sounding name that started with M. These days, it was the only name she would answer to. 'What we are going to do, Austin, is take back this federation. Piece by piece, inch by inch, by whatever means are necessary. Unless, of course, you're afraid.' She waited, eyes rimmed black with mascara sparkling with a gleam just a few degrees below sanity, as Austin grinned boyishly. 'Ain't you heard? Wombats is too stupid to know fear!'
