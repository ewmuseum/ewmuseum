Date: 07.30.07 It’s been months since I looked at the contract that I was offered. I turned down one hundred forty-six million dollars and the chance to live out my dream of playing for the Chicago Cubs because I loved wrestling. I felt more at home having forearms driven into my face than I did throwing baseballs at home plate. Was I wrong? It’s been a month since my last in-ring victory against Lane Stevens. Prior to that, I lost my previous two contests as well. I’ve lost to the likes of Tanaka, Timo Bolamba and of course Clinton Sage. That bastard has now stolen two wins from me. But my losses don’t stop in FUSE. In the second round of the Chris Williams Memorial Cup I lost to the Illustrious Face Eater. Should I look for a third career? It wouldn’t be so bad if I still had the fans. But I don’t. They might cheer me when I walk out to the ring. They might pop when I fly from the ring and float to the outside. They might applaud me and "ooh" and "ahh" at my many piledrivers, but that all ends when I leave the ring and push my way through the curtain. Out of sight, out of mind as they say. To me those people are using me. They use me as somebody to entertain them until Jonathan Rhine and Fred Fusion are up. I used to be a main event superstar, but now I’m nothing but a mid-card filler. It’s not just the fans either. Clinton Sage used me as his bitch week in and week out. Tanaka used me as his stepping stone to the Universal Title. Even Smitty used me as the pawn to help him protect his family. I’m just somebody that’s here, making money and putting on stellar performances each time I go to the ring. But nobody cares. Nobody noticed that I was holding my neck as I walked through the curtain at KillZone 45. Nobody asked if I was alright. Except for Jonathan Rhine; the one person that seems to care about John Covel anymore. Nobody gave me a pat on the back for a great performance. That ended long ago. It ended when the same man that was concerned for my well being entered FUSE. It wasn’t easy. Was that how Mikael Lundgard felt when I stepped into FUSE? Did he feel as though I was stealing his spotlight? He couldn’t have felt that way. I CREATED my own spotlight. A spotlight that was leaps and bounds above and beyond the one already existent. I was it. Now I’m far from 'it'. Night in and night out my body is put on the line for people that don’t even care. At the end of the night I’m far from everyone’s minds. Sometimes I can be in the ring and be forgotten about. Do I love wrestling? I do. I mean I did. I mean, I think I do. Should I tough it out and endure loss after loss for the rest of my career? Because that’s what the outlook is. Or should I pick up my phone and call into Chicago? It’s not too late to help the Cubs make a run at the World Series. Or have those fans forgotten me as well?
