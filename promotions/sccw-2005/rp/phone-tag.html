Date: 2010/07/17 Shining high in the midday sky, the sun brightened the beautiful cerulean and occasional delicate white cloud floating on by. With another warm summer afternoon greeting the citizens of Richmond, it wasn’t hard to see why the people were out in full force. Maymont Park was bustling with activity. Toddlers being chased by careless parents. People walking their dogs. Elderly folks wrapped up in chess. Gaggles of idiotic and possibly drunken fraternity brothers playing tackle football. And of course, can’t forget about any of the joggers. One jogger in particular stands out form the rest, gaining quite a bit of attention from people he’d passed. James MacArthur Brody, aka Mack, maintains a steady speed. Sweat glistened over his massive bare torso as he continued his pace, offering up a smile and approving nod to a red-headed spitfire. He’d caught her out of the corner of his eye, looking up at him once or twice. Mack had stolen a few glances of his own, but his eyes remained hidden behind a pair of Oakley O ROKR Pros. Most likely one of the more expensive pieces of… well, pretty much anything that he’d ever worn on his person. "Frank, look… I told you that I’m sorry. Look, this is me telling it to you again for the thousandth time: I dropped the ball, dude. I should’ve been looking out for the SIN Championships instead of letting Brokeback Mountain get the drop on us." An awkward silence hung in the air. Mack thought about screaming back into the Bluetooth built into the O ROKR Pros, but the displeased voice of Frank let him know he was there. "It’s done and over with, Mack," Frank told him on the other side of the phone. The bronze giant was at least thankful that he didn’t have to fumble with his cell phone while jogging and checking out the hot women frolicking in the sunshine. He exchanged smiles at a couple more buxom blondes before turning his attention back to the redhead he’d been eyeing. "And besides," Frank added. "Today is a new day; we’re all about twenty pounds heavier than before. Things are looking up and we’ll deal with the Cowboys from Low-Card Purgatory soon enough." Mack barely caught any of what Frank was saying as beautiful young brunette bounced on by. A heavy sigh on the other end of the line brought him back to reality. "Do you think you could possibly think about anything other than tits at a time like this? We’ve got to worry about Connors’ boys and we can’t afford any distractions." But he’s all but lost at this point. Mack turned his head to check out another woman who’d taken to jogging at his side. He flashed his pearly whites, which incited a sultry wink in return that brought a tear to his eye… the one in his pants. He gazed at her tracing the tip of her tongue around a pair of full ruby red lips before speeding up. Instead of trying to match pace this time, he preferred to lag behind her so he could partake of the newfound scenery. "Mack," an exasperated Frank cried out. "I know. You have a healthy appetite, okay? And as entertaining as the whole look back on the fuck-ups of Mack Brody has been, we do need worry more about next week. While some of us are out and about, trying to get more box than FedEx, some of us are actually somewhere else, strategizing and keeping ourselves on the winning team." "Yeah, yeah, yeah," Mack casually brushed it off. "Don’t get your nuts in a wringer, Frank." The redhead with the ass more out of this world than Spacely himself turned to face the muscle of the Heirs. Jogging backwards, she put a thumb to hear ear, pinky stretched down to her mouth. Getting the message loud and clear, he flashed her the digits with his free hands. Impeccably, she signaled her own right back. "Do you think you could hold on for a second, Mack? I got a call on the other line." "Yeah, yeah, sure…" The other line went dark for a moment as Mack let out a sigh of relief. Reassured that this exchange was most likely going somewhere, Mack made a ring with his thumb and index finger, driving the finger through the hole. Many times. The woman seemed to be more than pleased with Mack as she gave him another wink and turned away from him. Now having turned her back (oh, and can’t forget about her ass) to him, she sped up once more, trailing away to the left at the next fork in the path. Mack beamed proudly, turning his head to watch her leave before starting to turn the opposite direction. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Frank Silver wiped the sweat off his forehead with the sanguine towel at hand. Blackberry via Bluetooth was at its finest as he continued his daily sunning regimen on the balcony of his hotel suite. A gorgeous day greeted him; the city of Richmond practically lifting her dress for him, showing a cloudless blue sky. While business was the play of the day, he couldn’t help but smile. Sure, he’d given crap to Mack for doing his own exercise regimen, he was also talking shop and evening out his bronzing of the chest. It was hard to keep up that kind of multitasking. He tapped the button on his Blackberry, switching to the other line. The smile quickly disappeared. "Frank!" The frenzied voice one Tim Teegan jumped across the line. A recent addition the camp of the Heirs of Wrestling, their personal assistant was quickly finding out how excruciating the road truly was. Just two weeks in and he’d been whipped to the proverbial bone. Seriously. His bones were even starting to ache now. "Timmy!" Frank declared, still admiring the glorious setting. A tall glass of lemon-tinged water was his savior, trying to keep him collected for whatever Tim was going to throw his way. "What’s the happenstance, my man?" "I’m sorry I missed your call, I know, I know… stupid, stupid, stupid!" Tim cried out on the other line, the sound of either a skull or a fist taking Frank aback for a moment. "Look, Frank… it won’t happen again. I have your guys’ photo shoot for the new SCCW website booked this afternoon for 4:30." "Fantastic. And generally, Timothy, it’s very polite in agent-representative relationships to let one’s client know when one is planning to disappear from sight and give no clue as to what he might be doing." "I told you, I’m sorry," Tim sputtered, but he was cut off before he could get much else out. "You know, I’m not going to patronize you. You, sir, are a grown man, just like we are." He stopped. There was clearly another part coming, one to underscore just HOW patronizing he was about to be."But I can’t help feel a little bit slighted, Tim. I don’t do well with being slighted. And if you decide you want to go gamboling around someplace else, we get to have the chat." "It won’t happen again, Frank. Look, I know you guys are the boss, but…" he let out a sigh. "Don’t you think you could cut me a little slack? I kind of helped you guys win the SIN…" "Ah, ah, ah," A condescending tone cut him off again. "See… there’s that hot streak Sonny always liked about you, Tim. The same one that got you blacklisted from every wrestling promotion in North America. Now, do you want to make some money and help a couple of brothers out or do you want to be a putz washing cars for minimum wage… AGAIN?" No sound on the other end was enough to give Frank his answer. "Sweet, sweet. Now, when we’re back at headquarters, we can all grab coffee or do something. You can relax a little bit, and we’ll all be copacetic." "..Sounds like a plan." "Oh, and Tim? Next time, pick a mask that’s a little less satanic. That other one made you look stupid." "Thanks." With Frank’s mind still fully engaged, he switched back to the other line. "Hey, Mack, I’m gonna call you back. I gotta let Ryan know what’s going down. Call you later." ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ He took the first bite of his breakfast potatoes and eggs with salsa and looked across the table at her. Will O'Gallway relished in the ability to take off to Miami after SOS V and getting to see Hunter for a few days. After all the chaos that happened in Tokyo and with April Monday, He was happy to find this point of stability. This was normal to him. This was right. Will tried so hard and wanted someone so badly. Someone like Hunter. The new couple enjoyed their first summer brunch. The sun was low on the horizon but the oppressive Florida heat already wrenched everyone at the bistro. Stationery fans created their vortex, attempting to cool off the patrons but to no avail. The misters were a better idea in theory than application. All they really did was contribute to the hanging blanket of humidity. Will's mauve linen shirt and beach pants were not just a fashion statement, but a necessity. Hunter's kelly green dress stuck to her glistening olive skin. A half empty carafe of mimosas and sweating glasses of water sat between them. Will smiled at her as he swallowed his satisfying bite. 'I forgot how good this place was. I never really get to eat brunch or anything like that on the road. This is your first time here?' Hunter looked up at Will from pushing around her quiche. She peered at him from behind her sunglasses and smirked at the naivete displayed by her boy. 'Babe. I grew up in Miami. You act like I wouldn't have the pulse of this town. Will, you gotta smarten up your act. That wide-eyed shit doesn't fly when you are around me,' Will winced and it made Hunter's smile turn tender, 'It's cute though. But you gotta act the part, I'm surprised folks aren't out here taking our photos for the mags. This is me we are talking about.' She already had a hold on his heart. They both knew it. Will was falling for Hunter and tripping for her hard. The socialite and her now champion wrestler boyfriend. She brushed a lock of her raven hair behind her ear and giggled silently. He couldn't see the wink she offered, but was assured as the heiress of the club empire placed her hand on top of his. 'Relax Will. I'm playing. You are so uptight. I can get you--' 'No,' he interrupted, 'I can't get messed up before the flight.' Will's iPhone vibrated on the table. He peered over and sighed. This nice break was now over as 'Frank Pierce/Silver - Mobile' flashed on its screen. 'Sadly, I'm going to have to take this,' O'Gallway said with a moan. He grabbed the phone and stood up. 'It shouldn't take too long.' 'It's ok, answer it.' Will held the phone to his ear, Frank came in loud and clear from Virginia. 'Ryno! Are you at the airport yet?' bellowed Will's partner, 'Tim booked us in a photo shoot.' 'No, I'm at brunch with Hunter. When's the shoot? My flight gets at like 6 tonight. Is it tomorrow before the show or later tonight?' 'Ah yeah. Tell her hey from me and that I'm sorry that I'm going to have to cut into your couple time. It's at 4:30 today.' '*Sigh* Well, has Teegan rebooked my flight yet. We gotta do something about that dude. Really weirded me out on our ride to the Arena last week. It messed up my Chi and you how I value my moo.' Frank went on a tangent about how Sonny always vouched for him but yeah Timmy Teagan was fucked up and yeah yeah yeah. Will zoned out and glanced at his girlfriend. Hunter was peering down at her french tip fingernails in boredom. She sensed his gaze and smiled back at him. That turned Will into an acid-tripping lucharesu Texan puddle on the South Florida cement. 'Yo Ryan. RYAN!' 'Yeah man. Sorry. Something caught me eye.' Frank paused. He knew this meant that his tag team partner was choosing his next words carefully. Will's heart sunk. 'Hey Will. You okay? I know you are in this honeymoon stage with Hunter, but since you've been with her you've been off your game. Both you and Mack have ass on the brain and I'm not going to let it mess up what we have. Think about it. We've worked through all those other companies and we have it. Finally. We're here.' 'I'm good Frank. I'll see you at the shoot,' Will said and ended the call. He placed the phone into his pocket and walked back to his seat. She was beaming. Will trembled before he sat back down. He clinched his fist in order to physically restrain his shakes, she didn't notice how afraid he was about him leaving early. Hunter would give him hell for leaving early. She'd make him accompany her to a function or some charity event. Will wouldn't mind. For once in his life, his two personalities were satisfied. William O'Gallway and Ryan Gallway. He had both the belt and the girl. His life would not get any better.
