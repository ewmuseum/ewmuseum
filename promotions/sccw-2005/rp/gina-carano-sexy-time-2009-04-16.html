Catch a snazzy tune in my head. Brain like a radio transistor playing the beats. I start rapping as a crowd of crazed, psychotic wrestling fans who surround me like a horde of ravenous zombies. The beats blast through my head as I unleash my lyrical skills upon the crowd. Road sucks me in like a black hole in the deepest, darkest regions of space and the street lights are like stars shining to far away planets. Stomach growls with hunger.
