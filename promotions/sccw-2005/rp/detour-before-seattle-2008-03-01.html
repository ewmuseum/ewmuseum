Detour before Seattle

1 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2008/3/1 There is an old saying that goes you don’t know what you’ve got until it’s gone. To some that may just be cliché but for some it is reality at its most basic form…. It is early in the pre-dawn hours that we find a long black limo quietly winding through some back roads leaving the city of Las Vegas behind. After a few more twists and turns the car comes to a stop in front of a large wrought iron gate. The car stops briefly so the driver can get out, ignoring the sign that says open from sun up to sun down, he opens the gate and pulls the car through. In the glow of the tail lights we can make out the words Greenlawn Cemetery. Why does the cemetery close at sundown anyway? Is it so the living doesn’t bother the dead at night? How much of an inconvenience could it be…the occupants aren’t going anywhere are they? Or do they all come back to life and have some sort of huge after life rave…with fog machines, disco balls and techno music thumping the night away….and you thought the fog was there to give the place a scary look…how silly of you. The limo finally comes to a stop the driver quickly gets to the rear of the vehicle and holds the door open. A black boot with silver skull boot straps sinks into the gravel and out steps none other than the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde. Driver: Sir we only have a few moments. Your plane is awaiting your arrival and ready to take you to Seattle. Wylde: Thank you for the reminder Mark….but you know this is a little more important than leaving on time. Seattle will still be there when I’m done. Driver: Understood Sir. With that Wylde slowly makes his way down the gravel road. The sound of his boots crunching against the gravel seems deafening at this early hour. He stops next to a marble bench that is sitting along side a matching headstone. He takes a deep breath before taking a seat on the bench. Wylde: I guess this should have been my first stop a few months ago. Before I let my ego talk me into returning to the ring. Yeah you heard me right…I returned to the ring. Gideon and I made a great business deal and we started talking about old times. One thing lead to another and a few bottles of Southern Comfort later I was making another deal to join him in this organization. I’ve actually been back on the road for a couple months now. Between finalizing the deal, popping up at a few independent shows to shake off the ring rust, and the first few matches for them its been a bit crazy. I’m sorry I haven’t been visiting as much lately. Its not that I didn’t want to…I just didn’t know how I was going to tell you. I didn’t think it was possible to want this again. To ignite the fire to compete again…especially without you by my side. Wylde lets out a deep sigh as he runs his fingers through his jet black hair. Wylde: It’s never been the same since you’ve gone. Truth be known that was the biggest reason that I went into seclusion and a self imposed retirement. Yeah the lawsuits and headaches associated with trying to revive NEW didn’t help any…but that was just business that I could have dealt with. Not having you there I couldn’t deal with. I could really use you now. Just then the silence in the cemetery is shattered by the sound of Drowning Pool’s Step Up blaring from Wylde’s cell phone. He fumbles through his pockets looking for the phone, like he is getting an icy stare from some uptight librarian that is pointing to a sign showing no cell phones allowed in the library. He finally pulls the phone from his pocket and in one motion flips the thing open as smooth as Captain Kirk in the original Star Trek TV show. A text message from Gideon begins to scroll across the screen. …wound on leg not healing as well as expected. I’ll make the trip to Seattle but it looks like I’ll be on the bench for this one. Sorry….Gid Wylde: Son of a bitch!! That means I’m going to have to tag with Caleb. Not that Caleb isn’t a good enough wrestler…its just sometimes he can be a loose cannon. That boy has been banned from more federations than Pamela Anderson has been rode hard and put away wet. Wylde slams the phone shut and stuffs it back in his pocket. He begins to rub his eyes and hold the bridge of his nose. Wylde: I feel another migraine coming on. What was I just saying…oh yeah…now more than ever I could use your services. You were the one that pushed me to my limit. You made me work not only harder but smarter as well. You never steered me wrong. You always knew what was best. I need that shot in the arm right now. You see my big return hasn’t been so triumphant. Lost my debut match, then was off for a week or so, then came back to a triple threat and didn’t even factor into the decision. See this is the point where you lighten up the mood by saying something like…well technically you didn’t lose the match. The sun is just beginning to creep up over the horizon. Wylde reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out his Oakley M-frames and slips them on. He pauses for a moment to feel the warmth of the sun on his skin. Wylde: This isn’t exactly what I had in mind when I thought we would be waking up together for the rest of our lives, or the place for that matter. We should be enjoying this sunrise on the balcony of our home. Well before Mark gets his panties in a bunch I’ve got to get going. The plane is waiting to take me to the next event. Gideon and I actually had a match for the tag team championships but he is too injured to compete. You know how badly I want to score my first win in this company? And for it to be for a championship would be that much sweeter. This was supposed to be our night…the Black List’s coming out party…our chance to show the rest of the roster what they had to deal with. Wylde reaches to his side and picks up a bouquet of pink roses and lays it gently at the base of the headstone. Wylde: Actually maybe it’s a good thing that Gideon can’t make it…I think its high time that the SCCW find out what its really like to take a walk on the Wyld Side. I just couldn’t bring myself to leave without being by your side on this of all days. Wylde stands up and places his hand on the top of the headstone, wanting to be close to her and also to partially steady himself so he doesn’t fall down. Wylde: Wish me luck! As Wylde returns to his impatient driver the camera zooms in on the writing on the headstone. In Memory of Miss Domino October 21, 1980-March 2, 2006 Fade to Black.
