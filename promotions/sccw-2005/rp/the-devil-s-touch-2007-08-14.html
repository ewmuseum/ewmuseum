Date: 14/08/07 "When a woman behaves like a man, why doesn’t she behave like a nice man?" Dame Edith Evans He had to keep control of his emotions, his temper and his fear. Times had changed and now he had responsibilities, things that he could not turn away from. He could not allow himself to be corrupted by the Devil that he had once shared a bed with, night after night. It had been one of the most tumultuous times in his life, a roller coaster ride of extreme highs and madness inducing lows. He had been a different man back then, seduced by greed and lust, those were his two weaknesses. The yearning for glory and the never ending appetite for pleasure had led him to a path of self destruction. While he was revelling in gold, the Devil had been whispering in his ear and scratching her nails down his back. Like a little boy he kept on succumbing, mesmerized by the power wielded over him. Only when the glory faded, did the grip held on him become released. Yet still the memories remained and he would never forget the effect that the Devil had on him. Now the Devil had returned and she wanted her pound of flesh. Paul Cain had stood toe to toe with Clinton Sage and talked the Good Fight. He had promised to stand up for those who were living in fear of The Affliction and who believed that there was no alternative. The choice had been made, he was willing to stand with the crosshairs locked on him, he showed no fear and he would take whatever consequences came his way. He was the walking Paragon of Virtue, charged with helping to clean up the company soiled by the dirt of The Affliction. Then there was the side project of leading Prince Pride from the darkness of self destruction into the light of glory. How times had changed, greed and lust had given way to selflessness and morality, Paul Cain was finally a ‘Good Man’ freed from the shackles of past corruption. Her games had begun anew and she craved for him to succumb. The fact that he was even holding the glass of scotch in his shaking hand could have been a sign of weakness. He had not touched a cigarette since leaving PRIME, yet now the fingers from his free hand tiptoed over to the emergency packet he had left behind. It had been like a visit from Death, the coldness he had felt when he recognised the writing on the envelope. His prayer of her appearance only being fleeting had not been answered. Instead, she had found him and she had placed her mark upon him. He was determined to fight what she was doing to him, slamming his glass down in defiance on the pine desk and ripping at the envelope. No matter what poison had been put before him, she wouldn’t get into his head. He was determined not to let it happen, he had to remain pure and strong, the slightest corruption could jeopardise all he was working towards. With a slight pause, he pulled the letter quickly out of its envelope and his eyes widened at the bloody signature which caught his attention. He could almost feel those nails down his back once again, her touch felt chillingly close. Hey baby, It’s been so long hasn’t it? Just because you haven’t looked into my eyes, doesn’t mean I’ve not thought about you. I still remember how good you felt on those late nights where we’d fuck until the sun came up, you’d beg for more and I’d happily give it to you. I used to love it so much and you know what baby? Don’t lie to yourself; you’re thinking about it right now, how it would be to do that again. I’m the one thing you can’t escape from, the one thing you would risk it all for. Admit it to yourself, that is the first step my darling. You should do because one day soon, I will be back for you. I will not be kept away from you my love; I will not be kept away from my destiny. You have so much to make up for and I have so much to give you. It’s time that we came together again my darling, because we have been apart for too long. I am watching you from afar, thinking of you, you won’t ever be lonely again. Let me inside you. Let me back in. You will let me back in. You have to. There’s no choice. Remember me in your dreams my darling, remember me in your fantasies, and remember our past because soon it won’t be the past any longer. I am coming to rescue you my angel, soon. It’ll be just like old times baby. Pretend to HER that you don’t want it but you don’t have to lie to me, I know you want me and I know that I am all you can think of now. Why? Because I’m thinking of you my love and soon we will be back together again. Making up for lost time. All my love, undyingly. M L His eyes were simply staring at the letter and perhaps he had not even taken in half of its contents. In fact his mind was on autopilot as he glided his forefinger over the dried blood which formed the letters M L as the signature. Her perfume was sickeningly sweet and it made his throat tighten as he felt close to retching. This was no love letter, this was a warning, a threat and a promise all rolled into one. He knew now he wasn’t safe, his life and career were at risk from a woman who wasn’t in control of herself, a part of his past that he wished had died with the rest of his legacy. Yet she was very much alive and she was watching his every movement, biding her time. He must not succumb, he must not be afraid and he must not sacrifice himself because of her. He had lost his mind because of her once before, he could not allow it to happen again. Down the throat went the scotch. Across the room went the glass, shattered into so many pieces. The Devil was coming for him and it had a name. Maria Lopez. "That was so fucking good, you are so gorgeous babe!" She didn’t even look at him as she lit her cigarette; taking a drag and watching the smoke rise up into the bedroom. The post sex treat that she always allowed herself, such a manly thing to do but then she didn’t believe in feminine behaviour, it was old fashioned. She could feel his eyes all over her as he pulled his underwear up, like a horny little teenager. She’d put the black satin gown on to quickly close the door on any hopes he may have had of a repeat performance. When would these boys learn that just because she had sucked his dick, it didn’t mean she gave a damn. "I want to see you again babe." "Don’t doubt that." Her flat nonchalant reply as she amused herself by attempting to blow smoke rings from her cigarette. Kudos to her, she had given a great performance tonight. She had been writhing around on the bed like she was possessed and ridden him like a bucking bronco. She had made all the right noises at the right times and made the right touches in the right places. For him, it was like every Christmas and birthday present had come at once, she’d even made him think that he was a half decent lover in the process. To the casual observer, yes voyeurism isn’t a bad thing, she’d loved every moment of it and it had been the best sex she had ever had. Or not, as the case actually was. "I thought maybe I’d give you a call in a couple of days?" He stood by the side of her bed, shaved head and smooth chest with denim jeans covering his lower body. She casually ran a hand down his stomach, trailing her fingertips down to his groin and then patronisingly patting it like a puppy dog. "I’ll be washing my hair." She had been ‘entertaining’ herself with these ‘toys’ for the last few nights but without much satisfaction. Men were so tedious and frustrating; all she wanted was to let off some steam but they suddenly decided that she was the best thing since sliced bread and that they couldn’t live without what she could give them. Whatever happened to the real men, the love ‘em and leave ‘em types that would be rough, fun and best of all, temporary? The modern male was so feminine and clingy that it had driven her crazy on more than one occasion. Even now, this guy whose name she vaguely remembered began with a ‘D’ was standing far too close to her personal space. Ugh, get out. "Shouldn’t you be somewhere?" Anywhere, I don’t care where, just somewhere that isn’t here she thought. They weren’t all bad in truth but they all suffered from that puppy dog syndrome which ruined anything that might have been slightly enjoyable. He cast a sad look in her direction as he picked up his T-shirt and slipped it over what was, in her opinion, a no better than average body. Hurry up, you won’t get anything more by hovering around like a bee to a honey pot. Even her thoughts were irritated and impatient. He picked up his sneakers and moved towards the bedroom door yet she didn’t even look at him. "You can see yourself out." Pausing, he held the door ajar and looked over to her with a puzzled expression. "We were having fun; we were getting along so well. I don’t understand?" She stretched out the full length of her body, with a slightly satisfied sigh of contentment. "You will, when you’re all grown up." The final insult as this late twenties male stormed out of the room, his footsteps heavily stomping down the staircase. That should do the trick, he won’t be back. She smiled in satisfaction as she simply lay there, looking up at the ceiling. It was irritating to have to go to these lengths to get rid of the toys but these things had to be done. After a slight pause, she heard the door to the apartment crash shut as another jilted lover stormed his way home with his tail between his legs. Mental age of a pre-schooler she mused to herself as she slowly swung her legs over the side of the bed and rose to her feet. She felt a bit wobbly but that wasn’t through pleasure, it was simply through great over-acting and hard work. She moved her hand down the bedside cabinet and opened up the top drawer. Her fingers glided over some papers and then found what they were looking for, the only other picture she had kept. Pulling it from it’s home, she held the photograph in her hands, of Paul Cain standing in the NWC Atlantic ring with his Heavyweight Championship held aloft. "Now if that’s how I treat people I don’t know, what could I do to someone I loved?" She ran her hand over his face and down the length of his body. Then with a scowl, she simply tossed the picture down onto the floor in disgust. "I warned you not to hurt me, Paul. I told you what would happen." Her fingers pushed their way through her long hair as she walked over to the window and stared through it for a moment. Then the scowl faded and was replaced by a deranged smile and a twinkle in the eyes. "I can’t wait to see you again baby. We’re going to have so much fun." She turned to walk away and as she did so, her black nail varnished feet trampled on the photograph of Paul Cain, in happier times. Symbolism at it’s very best. "Ethan Frost has had a bad time of it." "Ethan Frost has had the worst few months of his life." "Ethan Frost nearly drowned in his own turmoil." "Ethan Frost was a menace to society." "I can save you Ethan." "I can save you." "If you allow me." "Will you Ethan?" "I understand you." "I know how it feels." "Together we could make it." "Together we could save the world." Paul Cain stands on the edge of the rooftop to his apartment building. He’s wearing a long black leather coat, black T-shirt and pants. He looks down to the traffic below, pausing for a second. Stretching his arms out wide, he takes a deep breath and looks up to the sky. "Together we could save ourselves." "The Devil won’t win." "We won’t allow it Ethan." "Together, Ethan. Together."
