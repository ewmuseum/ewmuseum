Date: Today "Most of us spend our entire lives defining ourselves by who we are not, rather than who we are." Edward Lambert stares out the large bay window in his office. He stares across the freshly sprouting lawn, and at the birds sitting on the branches of the oak tree that is just beginning to bud in his front yard. "I used to define myself the same way Nick. I used to consider everything about me by what I wasn’t rather than what I was. I did it professionally, socially, and personally. I wasn’t a drug addict, I wasn’t a friendly person, and I wasn’t the bastard that some people made me out to be.." He turns from the window and with a noticeable limp, walks to the desk and the pack of cigarettes resting thereon. Nick, his young videographer stares at the video monitor of the camera he has directed towards Edward. "I figured that once I had defined everything that I wasn’t, then I could define exactly who and what I was." Edward reaches down and pulls a cigarette from the pack. "The problem with defining yourself by who and what you aren’t, is that you never define exactly who you are. You never look in the mirror or draw a line in the sand. You never really take a stand on your own life." Nick nods as Edward lights the cigarette and turns to look directly at him. His permanently bloodshot left eye has lost most of the swelling around it, and the stitches that are forming another scar across his forehead disappear into his widows peak hairline. "If it sounds rather defeatist, it’s the truth. It’s the way that a lot of people look at their lives tho. They say, Oh, I don’t have this, or I don’t do that, yet they never talk about what they do have, or what they actually fucking do." Edward takes a long draw from the cigarette and returns to the bay window. "And that is the problem with the people today. They define themselves by what they aren’t, not by what they are. That’s the problem with Desade, and with Hunter, and with most other people in this industry. They look around and when they see someone with something that they don’t have, they use that lack to direct their lives and focus themselves." He turns from the window and stares at the camera. "That’s why people like Desade hate those that actually stand for something. They build themselves up based on not who they are and what they don’t have, and those of us who understand who we are, and what we actually have are their diametric opposites. It’s the same reason that other countries fear, hate, and envy the United States. We have what we want, and we keep getting more of it, and they see what we have, and they covet it, and they fear us because of that." Edward smirks as the smoke wreathes his battered face. "That’s the reason that Desade pulled her stunts and that is why eventually, she will lose. She may believe that she’s smarter than anyone else in the world, she may have her schemes, and her plots, but in the end, that utter lack of definition will be her end." With a faint grunt he pushes the window open and with a flick, discards the cigarette out into the yard. "I put the first nail in her coffin a few days ago Nick. I kicked the living shit out of her hand picked champion. I took the man that she knew was one of the best wrestlers in this business and I beat the shit out of him. He knew that he was in for hell when he got in that ring with me, but he didn’t understand what he was getting into. He knew how good he was, he knew that I’m a piss poor wrestler. He knew that I couldn’t possibly match him in a wrestling match. He defined what he knew I couldn’t do, and he set out to prove it, and along the way, he forgot that I don’t play by his fucking rules." Nick walks around the desk and stands beside Edward, his camera pointed directly at the profile of Edward’s face. "I define myself by what I can do, and who I have done it to. My devil danced with his demon, and that dance hasn’t ended yet. Oh, I beat Hunter, and Desade probably thinks that she and her cronies got the last laugh, but this game is far from over. The fiddler has not yet finished his tune." Edward turns to Nick and the camera and grins his feral grin. "But what Desade hasn’t figure out yet, is that she isn’t calling the shots in this game anymore. I played by the rules, and she turned those rules around and used them against me, and now, now I’m throwing the rulebook out the fucking window. She thinks she knows how dangerous I can be, she thinks she knows exactly what I am capable of, but she’s wrong." The grin grows teeth. "Dead fucking wrong." * * * * * With a flick, Nick turns the camera off and puts it down on the desk. The moment the camera hits the desktop, Edward pulls a piece of white cloth from his back pocket and commences coughing. "You ok Mr. E?" Edward waves off the help and after a moment the coughing subsides. "No I’m not fucking ok Nick. I’m fucking dying." Edward folds the now red flecked cloth and returns it to his pocket. "That last fight took a lot out of me Nick. Back when I was younger I could have fights like that on back to back nights, and wake up ready to go again the next day." He shakes his head as he reaches out his hand and takes a cigarette from the pack on the desk. "Now… Now I can hardly fucking breath without pain, and I don’t know how much longer I can fucking do this. Hunter took me further than anyone has taken me in years Nick, he hurt me worse than I’ve been hurt in a long time, and I don’t know how much longer I can do it." With a flick he lights the cigarette and stares out the window at the patchy lawn. "I’ve got to finish this soon, or they’ll win simply because they fucking outlived me, and won’t that look great on my fucking tombstone. Here lies Edward Lambert, he who died first and lost." He coughs again. "Is there anything I can do Mr. E?" Edward turns and walks around behind the desk. "No Nick. There isn’t anything anyone can do. I’ve walked a long and dark road for a long fucking time, and I’m getting close to the end of that road. With each step I take it seems to get narrower and narrower. I’m pretty much walking on a razors edge right now, and one wrong step will send me over the side." Nick nods his head and after lifting the camera from the desk, he walks out of the office leaving Edward alone. "And I don’t want to know what will happen if I stray off the path again. To many people are counting on me to do this my way, and that’s the fucking problem. I don’t think I can do it my way anymore, I’m gonna have to start doing it their way."
