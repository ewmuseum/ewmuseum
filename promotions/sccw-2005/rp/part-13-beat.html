Part 13: Beat

Date: Today Wyatt Connors walked through the house on tiptoes. Sometimes, even soldiers in God's Army are afraid of something. In Wyatt's case, that something was God's nearest representative in the vicinity. Every night, there was a debriefing session, where Connors would speak with the true master of the Decency Compound, providing information on both the community and on the presence of the Decency Crusade in SCCW. Mostly it had been good news, which allowed the bad news to be shrugged off. Lately, there had been some disappointments in Wyatt's reports, which had been met with force. And while a ring veteran such as Wyatt Connors was used to pain, there was something about the physical rebukes he received in this house that stung his very soul. And in the words of the wise and noble prophets, Great White: 'Once Bitten, Twice Shy.' He had slowly been heading toward the guest bedroom where the meetings took place--the master rarely left the room, and never left the house; still weak from his imprisonment and escape. Wyatt paused in the kitchen, not wanting to move any farther. The bad tidings weighed heavy on him. Soon, he fell to his knees, looking to the Lord for assistance with the burden. He whispered, 'Bless me, Father, and give me the strength to bear this unpleasant news.' 'Very well. Strength is granted.' Wyatt gasped, and spun around so fast that he fell over. When he finally righted himself, he got a good look at the speaker. 'Forgive me, Master,' he said timidly. 'I did not hear your approach.' Reverend Ibrahim Seck smiled. It was a fierce smile, impossibly wide with teeth gleaming more than they should. 'Am I not in all places, like my big brother? Do I not exist in all men's hearts?' 'Of course you do, Master.' 'Now then. Tell me of this unpleasant news.' Seck's calm smile did nothing to disquiet Connors' nerves. The 'False Prophet' stammered for a few moments before beginning. 'Once more, I am to fight the unbelievers on your behalf. This time, it is against someone...you have met before.' A bead of sweat formed on Seck's brow. 'Who?' 'One known as Lance Marshall.' 'Ah,' Seck answered. His lips parted in a smile, showing off his immaculate teeth. 'I remember Lance. It's too bad his penis led him astray; think of all the good he could have done by following me instead of that wife of his.' 'Indeed.' Wyatt silently hoped that the conversation would continue along this line--perhaps he could get out of it without ever having to mention-- 'How is that unpleasant, my son? Certainly, the prospect of facing Brother Lance cannot have you that worried. What's the real problem?' 'There's a...umm...'special guest enforcer,' they call it. Apparently, this Kensington person feels that the Crusade presents an unfair advantage. I ask, how can it be unfair? They are the blessed children of--' Once again, Seck could tell that Connors was stalling. 'Who is this special guest enforcer?' 'Honestly, I plan on protesting as soon as I arrive. There is no reason for this persecution of--' 'Who. Is. It.' Wyatt averted his eyes and muttered something. 'Out with it, Connors. Timidity does not become you.' Now, Connors was staring straight down at the floor. After great internal deliberation, he finally spoke the word, 'BloodAngel.' Snap your fingers. That was the length of time it took for Reverend Seck to go from calm and controlled to stark raving mad. His face turned red, his eyes bulged out, and spittle flew from his mouth. 'DO NOT SPEAK THE CURSED NAME!' he screamed, and his hand flashed like lightning toward Wyatt's head, knocking the False Prophet down to the floor again. Instinctively, Connors rose back up, and he was immediately met by another strike. 'Did you think you could hide this from me?! Me, the second son of God, with all the powers and omnipotence implied by such a relationship! You have run afoul of the most evil demon in hell's domain, and you tried to keep it a secret--he could very well be banging on the gates right now, and we might have been none the wiser!' 'I'm sorry, Lord, but I--ahh!' Another strike cut off the smaller man. 'Silence, you imbecile! How long has he been active? What does he want with you?' Seck jerked his wheelchair forward, coming to a stop only when Connors' left hand was pinned underneath the wheel. 'AAAHH!' 'TELL MEEEEEEEEEE!!' 'He wants...you...' Seck's face fell, and his features became ashen. 'He knows...oh Lord, he knows...the Scarred Man,' he whispered. The shock only lasted for a moment, and soon his mind began racing, oblivious to the world around him...including the anguish of his disciple, whose hand was still bearing the weight of his messiah. There was something. Something from the past that he knew...or rather, used to know, about Edward Lambert. Something Lambert didn't even know himself. But the hard years made recollection tricky. Remembering those hard years made him even more frantic. I cannot let him take me again. There is a secret, something I can use...what was it? Something about that demented spawn of the worst of hell's demons, that, played correctly-- And there it was. 'Of course.' Once more, the serpentine smile crept back onto Ibrahim's face. His eyes remained manic, but they now projected fervor rather than terror. He even rolled his wheelchair back, finally freeing Connors' hand. Wyatt immediately clutched the injured extremity to his chest and whimpered. 'Fret not, my child,' Ibrahim said in a soft voice. 'Soon, we will have this particular agent of Satan where we want him. And who knows? Perhaps I can finally accomplish my ultimate goal of saving the Scarred Man...' The end of the sentence was not spoken, but still hung heavily in the air. 'From Himself.' Reverend Ibrahim Seck spent the rest of the night quietly planning. And somewhere, in the deep recesses of his mind, in a place not even he was aware of... ...so did Wyatt Connors.
