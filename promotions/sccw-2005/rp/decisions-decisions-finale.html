Decisions, Decisions (Finale)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Temptation: Rushmore, following the Luci/Savant kiss (What we saw at Rushmore was only one side of the story, as we have gathered Legion has been getting friendly with Savant albeit not to the same extent to the Luci kiss but as we now see… more of a cerebral nature) "I take it she finally showed her feelings to you then? " Maybe you should recreate that sometime!" (He chuckles) "Kidding of course, I don’t want the dead Man’s Hand to be the new ‘L Word’ now do I?" (Legion suddenly hears the skipping of feet down the hall and starts to speak quieter…) "We’ll talk later, I can hear Luci coming in no doubt ecstatic over what just happened, you just do what you need to do now…" (Legion hangs up the phone just in time for Luci to open the door, the grin on Luci’s face is scarily happy with a Cheshire cat grin crossed with someone that’s in a midst of a manic episode) Luci: You got the meeting now, told you I find a way soon enough (she winks) I feel good though, she felt the fire burn, they always do… (Luci’s expression suddenly turns sour) But what if she turns out like Rook? A fake or what if she’s been part of a game by Desade in order to mess with me to get to you like Teresa did? (She suddenly shows a look in her eyes… the look that could probably even scare the Universal champion if she ever got caught in it’s crossfire) And if that’s the case that person better hope they have a grave made and a coffin built because I will do things that not even Wyatt Connors has ever predicted as punishment! (Legion notices this change in Luci’s expression and smiles… she’s starting to fall into his game just unbeknownst to her) Legion: The scary thing is I know you will do it but don’t do it in great haste at least not until I’ve sorted everything out my dear, maybe you should take some aggression out in the gym, I’ve asked Lauren to help in finding a good trainer for you! Luci: A trainer? I thought you’ve done a good job so far! Legion: Only on what I knew already, she knows someone that can help with conditioning of a physical and mental nature and make you stronger not only for Sin City but elsewhere as well (For those that don’t know Luci was suspended for a month from New Frontier wrestling after losing a match!) (Luci suddenly gets up with the smile returning to her face, she suddenly hugs Legion who’s slightly surprised by the reaction) Luci: That is AWESOME! When’s it for? Legion: Following the PPV, now what we need to do now is wait for next week, I have an idea to bring the Revelation to Sin City… (Time passes with surprises occurring: Savant is removed by Desade for questioning loyalty and Legion decides to take a trip to Salt Lake City which in his eyes would be the perfect place for a Sinner’s Choice promo, however when he arrives…) Outside the EnergySolutions arena (Legion is walking outside the arena telling orders to the camera crew he had flown over) Legion: What I want is a wide shot of myself underneath that sign there *points to the LED screen advertising Homecoming* followed by a close up on my face! Now if you excuse me I have to make a call *takes phone out of pocket* (The display screen says Savant- the cell number blanked out) (All the viewer can hear is a ringing tone followed by Legion punching a four digit code) Savant: ‘Halllooo?’ Legion: Only when you have lost everything do you realise what you have my dear. There's a pause, and a sharp sigh. Savant: You of all people know better than to be sarcastic at a time like this. Legion: The weirdest thing is you think I’m being sarcastic… when Alex has just done what I thought she’d do. Alex is more getting snared into the web of the Red Raver, trying to be human and gaining emotions when she’s denying her true self so she’s getting rid of those that slowly disagree with her new ‘religious’ experience. I expect Katsidy to be next but even then she’s standing behind Phillip and considering what he did last week… I expect her to side with Alex, the concept of loyalty overriding all . Savant: You are the master of the self-absorbed analysis, and we've had just about enough of that this week. But nevermind that. I'm sure Kathi will make the right choice. Legion: Lauren, have you ever heard of the idea of self- realization by Abraham Maslow? It’s a theory that a person has to earn certain things in life and only then will they fully become content, Alex seems to feel that with Amy she’ll be able to achieve this. I however subscribe to a different theory: in order to fully realise the self you must let go of the emotions that plague you, it’s only then can you remain focused, now you’ll probably ask if that’s the case why do I have Luci as a confidant or for a better wording… protégé? Go on say it! Savant: You're not going to make me – (another sigh) why do you keep her around? Legion: Because she provides balance and lulls people into a false sense however you’ve managed to flip it around, the problem is she’s scared after what happened, I saw a look on her face that I’ve only seen one other time against Teresa… If she ever found out of the plan you and I would probably be in the crosshairs and not even Alex at her most vicious could stop her… Savant: I very much doubt that. Legion: It’s why I tried to drum into her mind the ‘Love is a virus’ mentality… you don’t learn underground teachings though without hearing of the woman who is one of London’s urban myths. The woman that trained an army… least that was the interpretation I was told. Savant: I've been down that path, walked a mile. You don't want to try. Legion: We both know that it hasn’t worked… I want Luci to be able to focus but remain with her mind on you and I, I can help her with the physical and you can help with the mental and the ‘physical’ side. Savant: I told you I'd get you your meeting – isn't this stuff you should save for her? Legion: Because you are easier to grasp and the one that is the most close to MY ideology along with Alex’s seemingly former ideology combined proving the most useful. Now listen closely what I’m going to reveal to you my decision but you feign innocence even at Homecoming… (Legion finally realises the camera that has been watching the entire time and pushes it off the stand) (We once again hear the last line of Legion's sinner's choice: Homecoming, guess I'm dammed either way. Now onto the possible most achieving yet scariest day of his life, though he wouldn't admit it!) (Credit to Joe for the Savant cameo)
