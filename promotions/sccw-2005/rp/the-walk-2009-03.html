The Walk

March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 2009 (Pre-No Limit Tournament) DISCLAIMER: A series I wrote to introduce Friar in a fed that never got off the ground. I've reworked the things like tense, dialogue, and other minuscule things. I liked it a lot and hope you will too. Enjoy! The Walk Chapter 1 The city of Tel Aviv sleeps deep in the night. Young men and women at the clubs scatter the streets along with vendors and night-time strollers. The city doesn’t really sleep, enough sound coming from all around to create a constant notion of activity. But enough dies down around 3a.m. to notice that people are dreaming, resting, and relaxing. One man believes otherwise. That man would be Friar. He strolls down the beaten path in the shadows and dim street lamps. He is wearing a black t-shirt, a pair of black slacks, and, of course, his mask. Stepping over a gap in the concrete, he notices somebody approaching rather steadily. "Help me!" A girl screams, falling into his arms. Her face, drenched with tears, stares into his eyes with fear. "He-he-he-help me! Puh-please, I’ll do anything!" Friar looks up, noticing the footsteps and checking his surroundings. It’s never been beyond dirtbags to use women as bait, he thinks to himself. Through the eyeholes of his mask, Friar spots her pursuers and moves her into a nearby alley. "Calm child," he says, cupping his hand over her mouth and stretching an index finger over his. "We’ll be heard." Her frightened eyes stare at his. The stomping of boots and shoes nears closer. At their closest moment, Friar clutches his arm in a striking position. He relaxes as three men wearing suits and ties jog by, not even glancing once in their direction. The Masked Miracle looks to the pretty, young blonde who has mascara streams down each cheek. "Don’t scream. Don’t yell. Take a deep breath (Friar pauses while she takes in all the air she can through her petite nostrils). Now..." Friar removes his hand, slowly. Their eyes lock and he waits with his hand ready to return the muzzle at the sign of any possible outburst. "Who were those men?" She looks around and winces, gripping her right side. "My stomach- oh my," the girl keels over and falls to her knees. Friar crouches lower and looks at her face. She begins to tear up. "Please..." Her eyes begin to drift into her head. "...help me..." Her head falls into Friar’s hands and he stares at her for a few long seconds... What now? Who were those men What did they do to this girl? This is NOT OKAY! Friar reaches under her head and legs with both arms and picks her up. Looking to the street, he inches toward the corner of the shadows and the light to see if he can spot anybody of question. "GOTCHA!" A giant hand lunges toward Friar and the girl. Spinning, Friar kicks the knee inward, tearing the ACL, PCL, and MCL all in one swift swipe. The man’s head registers the pain before his mouth can. "Gahhh! Dear God, ohh god no!!!" Friar shuffles over him and places a solid heel into this misfit’s face, removing a few teeth and memories for that matter. He looks down the street, spotting the other two running toward him. Thinking quick, the Hell Hound grips the woman tight and takes off for Sulleimon Park, the local spot for dog walking and late night runs. Friar knows the place well but is certain these ruffians will, too. "He’s going to the Park!" One of the suited men shouts to the other. Thinking like true hunters, they separate; one running down an alley to head Friar off. The mask doesn’t allow for peripherals but Friar hears the footsteps. They near closer and then, as he looks left, he notices a black and white blur coming toward him. "OOOOFF!!" The sound of Friar’s lungs emptying into the chilly night’s air sounds like a fog horn. The black and white blur turns out to be one of the two remaining thugs. He kicks Friar in the ribs. One cracks. Another might have broken. Friar’s gaze locks onto the young ruffian’s as he slumps into a seated position. The girl is picked up. "The fuck?" The man smirks, scowling at Friar’s appearance. "Check this out, Kip. We’ve got ourselves another super hero.". Damnit, Friar curses under his breath. As Kip walks forward, carrying what Friar notices to be what caused this whole thing...the girl. "Looks like he’s not that good at it, huh? Hahaha." The two idiots laugh and Friar sits in agony, holding his side and feeling around for any puncturing or bony shards. Kip flicks his head to the other one, grinning. "Why don’t we show him what happens when you cross Domino Jones?" They laugh and Kip nods to his partner. "Go ahead, Qo. Give him his glimpse into the future." Qo, the bigger of the two, grins and then steps to Friar, reaching out his big paw. He lifts Friar by his long, blond ponytail and then rattles his head with a solid left haymaker. Then a right lands on Friar’s face. Then Qo swings for the third and final blow – but finds himself in a seriously bad place when Friar catches his wrist and pulls forward, wrapping it around his neck and then letting Qo hear a bad, bad sound. SNAP Qo drops to his knees holding his wrist in awe. His hand dangles at the joint while Friar uses this chance to slam his right foot onto his face, jumping off of Qo’s head – subsequently ending his night - and then goes after Kip. In the air, Kip drops the girl and rocks Friar with a solid left straight-arm to the chest. The battle begins. "Silly playing Batman if you ask me. No reward," the arrogant thug states, matter-of-factly. Friar jumps up, kicks toward Kip’s legs, but misses. To no avail, it is blocked and then his left jab is blocked as well. Kip jumps, lands a quick straight kick to the stomach, and sends Friar down to one knee. He bends over, laughing, "Why don’t you give it up?" Damn. Who is this guy? Friar charges forward and Kip rolls his eyes. This time, Friar lands a flipping heel kick to Kip’s body and the two go to the ground. The Hell Hound scrambles to mount the suit. He grabs his tie and pulls Kip’s head off the ground. The mask doesn’t allow him to notice the pistol Kip was holding. One for me. One for your buddy. One for your other buddy. One for her. One for Jesus. One for God. One for the almighty Spirit. Ahmen. Friar looks down at the bloody man underneath him. Motionless and lightly breathing, the Hell Hound finds himself in a place he did not want to be – on the side of Sin. The bubbling blood from Kip’s lips trickle down his face as his eyes stare lifelessly at the mask. The soul is a terrible thing to watch leave a man’s body. Friar watched Kip’s leave him that moment and felt a piece of his go with him. Lord, forgive me. I did not mean it. I was protecting the girl- the girl! Friar turns abruptly noticing the motionless heap of bones and beauty on the city street is gone. He crawls around, turning to find that the other man, Qo, is gone as well... No. With disdain, Friar looks at the night’s sky and then stands to his feet. Looking at the ground, he bends down and scoops up a locket. His eyes seem somber and humble. Walking down the street, the sunlight begins to shine on this dark, dark night. The sun lights up Friar’s eyes. His shirt is torn and skin cut. Blood smears over his blond facial hair. His hands are bloodied after bludgeoning the three men. Yet his mask remains intact, pure, and solid. What does all this mean? The girl... The suits... Friar comes to a halt in front of a vast structure. He kneels down before it. A man wearing black approaches him and Friar places the locket in his hand. "Forgive me for my sins, Father." Friar and the old man lock friendly eyes. He then glances up to the radiating cross atop the Church of Tel Aviv. His eyes narrow as the sun seemingly highlights everything in a way to show him what lies ahead. He turns around, noticing the streets filling with people who have begun to investigate the strange scene in their city’s streets. Some begin to look at Friar, who bows his head in response. He reluctantly looks at his hands and then the priest. God will not favor the weak. He will reward the just and righteous. With eternal life. With strength, courage, and honor. Friar begins to walk toward his place of residence, two blocks away. Surprisingly, people begin applauding as the man heads his own way. He begins jogging before he hears the applause. Soon he hears it loud and clear a few seconds after. Stopping, he looks back to the priest and the people. Their faces said it all. I must find Domino Jones... Chapter 2 Tuc La Roc Night Club March 13th, 2009 5:18pm The large Oaken door swoops inward and lets the dark room fill with beams of light. A group of men – eight to be exact – storm into the vast chamber filled with what most might believe to be "priceless artifacts." If only those people knew how costly they truly are… "Je- JESUS!" A big man sits in a makeshift hospital bed under a dim light. Bandages cover his head and are stained with loose blood. His face grimaces each second he is awake. The face cannot be totally recognized but as the group approaches, his eyes open. "Boss? BOSS! They got Ki- KIP! We got-" "Be still, Qo." Of the crowd emerges a shorter, slender figure. A slender finger presses to his lips. The woman stares into his good eye for a few long seconds. Her eyes, soft and kind, move up and down his torso to assess the damage. She purses her lips slightly. "Who did this?" Qo’s eye grows wide. He pulls his mouth back but can’t speak. She shakes her head. "Forget that. How are you feeling? Did you find Mika?" "Found… Mika…" Qo takes up his cup of water. "Feeling… weak... but fine. (looking up) I’m sorry. Kip is dead." The woman’s face goes from soft to cold to callused. Her eyes narrow as she tilts her head to inquire. "Please, Qo… be calm. Take your time, but please, tell me; what happened to Kip? Was it the Triad? The Grogs? The policia?" Shaking in humor, Qo smiles for the first time through his dismantled face. "One guy! One man did (using his only good limb to display himself) THIS! One guy got Kip. We were after Mika… running even… and next thing we know she’s got this guy in a black mask on her side." Qo pauses. "Maybe a hired hand. Probably professional. Either way, he's no joke." The woman waits for him to regain his breath. "And…?" "He took Flash down first, don’t know where he went after-" Qo continues. Motioning around her. "We haven’t seen him or heard anything, either." Qo sits up, slightly. "Wha- Flash hasn’t come back?" "We were hoping you could tell us more about that. He was the one who found Mika. She told him what she knows and then when he didn’t come back, we thought maybe they had mutinied against us. Where is she?" Qo scratches his face and drinks more water. "Upstairs, in the Coop." "Murray." The woman holds her finger up, looking over her shoulder to the largest of man in her troupe. He stands next to her and scowls at his broken comrade. The woman turns to him, evil burning in her eyes, and whispers softly: "Mika knows too much. No one can find out. No one. I’ve got a lot riding on this deal. Are we clear?" Murray nods, "Crystal." "Play dumb and act innocent. Ask a lot of questions. See to it that she tells us who is helping her… and where I can find this Mask fella." Murray runs into the hall and up the stairs. "Why don’t you ask me?" A raspy voice comes from the darkness just before the lamps lighting the room from afar smash to the floor with a startling crash. Everyone inside turns back in time to watch the looming Oaken doors shut them into darkness. Qo’s eye locks with the woman’s. She turns around, removing a silver and black Magnum pistol. Then rest of her party follows suit. "What the fuck is happening, Qo!?" A thud comes behind them and then another. Two bodies drop, turning the eight-man brigade into a six-man show. And then another. Down to five. The woman spins on her heels, firing two shots into the dark. "FUCK!" Another shot and another rant. "Who the FUCK is that!? (Turning to her four flanks) Quit standing there! SHOOT!" She points into the dark and then fires another shot. She notices that she is speaking to no one. No Qo. No doctor. No more anyone. Just darkness, her Magnum, and whoever she was looking for. "Whose there!?" Frantically turning around in circles, the dark hair of her head seems to cover up her face. A snap and crack from the distant darkness causes a few more rounds to go into the air. Then silence. "WH-" The woman stares at her gun. The sound coming from it clicks an empty sound. And clicks thrice more out of hope. Her eyes fill with terror, looking into the darkness, "Sick Nicky? Look… it wasn’t personal… I never meant to-" Before the last word sounds, a stern hand grips her throat like a ziptie. She cannot see who it is. Her hands wrap around a strong arm, trying to pry herself free. Her struggles are all for not as the hand wrenches tighter. Suddenly, it releases. "Guhuh uh huh- WHO the" Dropping to her knees, this woman is not given any break. A strong boot connects to her back and slams her body into the concrete floor. A light turns on above her. Squirming and fighting to witness the attacker, she can only see the bright beam coming from above and the shadow looming under it. "Who THE FUCK are you!?" she demands, noticing the muscular man is wearing all black, holding a black rope, and reeling it in over his shoulder. His shadow erases the light each time the lamp swings from left to right. Her eyes fight to their corners but still can’t make a face. "Do you have any fucking idea who I am!? I’m-" "Domino Jones…" The voice answers, cutting her off with force. A strong boot flips her over to her side in agony. Clutching and crying, Domino looks to her assaulter, face to face. He kneels to her level. "I know exactly what you are, Domino." A look of fear grows over her face but quickly fades out as she regains her street tough mentality. She reaches up, touching the black leather of his mask. She notices the rosary tattoo on his arm. His eyes follow her fingers as she trembles in fear on his arm. "Are you… the Devil?" A cold glare comes from beneath the mask. Eyes bluer than the sunniest of skies but hold such darkness behind them. He begins to tie her legs and feet like a wild capture. "Quite the contrary, Ms. Jones. Although, I don’t doubt you’ll be seeing him soon." The mask-wearing enigma stands to full height. He tugs on the rope slightly. Domino realizes her legs are rising toward the ceiling. She squirms but is bound too tight to herself. He finally wrenches with full strength and lifts Domino to eye level, upside down. "My name is Friar," he begins in a more than terrifying tone of voice. "You may call me nothing. Mostly because I will be asking the questions and you will simply be giving me answers – or just buying time until I kill you. I’m leaving the choice with you." "KILL ME!?" Domino squirms around more, twisting herself around and dangles in effortlessness. Friar shrugs. "That is if you don’t start talking." "Get lo-" Friar slams his fist into her stomach and fires back with a standing sidekick to the same area. "UUUHHH!!!" Friar stands still, folding his arms over his chest. "Where is the girl?" "What girl?" Friar kicks again and then punches her in the face before grabbing a fist full of hair. He gets inches from her, snarling, "You disrespect me once, never again. The next time you speak out of line I will perform my last trick… the one where I make you disappear." "Mika? Oh, Mika!" Domino’s eyes brighten. "My sister. She’s upstairs in her room. What’s the-" The sound of two gunshots echo from up the stairs. Friar stands attention to the interruption and Domino looks in the general direction with fright. "Murray- FUCK!" Friar’s eyes go blank and he sends a sharp boot into her face, letting go of the rope. SNAP The thud and snap behind Friar comes from Domino’s neck breaking as all 131 pounds of her delicate frame crashes onto the wooden floor. Friar’s moves are not seen through the dark room but his figure makes it out of the doorway and into the lit up hallway. He stares up the staircase, all thoughts are on the girl, Mika. Dead or alive. I have come this far to fail. What was Domino talking about? Where is Murray? What does Mika know? Friar takes a deep breath and then a long, cautious stride up the stairs. Don’t run. Don’t hide. I must be strong. I must be right. Righteousness holds no judgment. But tonight I will. Chapter 3 "I don’t think one man could do all of this…" The sound of flesh and blood underneath the soles of Detective Monroe’s shoes squishes like a wet sponge. His face clinches, grimacing of the thought of death and lost lives. "It’s impossible." "Impossible?" says a man, stepping into the doorway. A cloud of smoke creates a foggy halo around his dark image. He strolls to the edge of the dim light, exhaling another cloud of smoke. "Think again. I’ve been to ten of these calls. Every time... it’s damn near perfect." Detective Monroe’s eyes wince. "Perfect? Who the Hell-" "Just relax, kid. Sergeant Denning, Organized Crime Unit. H-Q told me to come down and…" He took another pull on the cigar, glancing to the scene before him. "And yeah…perfect." Monroe’s eye blew up like stars. "Oh shi- Sorry, Sarge. I didn’t recognize ya. I’m-" "Monroe," The smoke cloud flushes the young gumshoe’s vision. "Can it. We’re here to do police work, not make friends. Now what do you think happened here?" The green-eyed Brit grins. He nods and looks to his notes and throws a palm to the sky. "Well, let’s see here...five severely injured, two officially dead; this one was tied up, hung by her feet and dropped. Broke her neck. An’ then the other wanker fell ill due to a punctured lung from a broken rib. Bloody mess..." The smoke rolls into the light, bringing Sgt. Denning into the room and light. His brown fedora hat and trench coat cover a less than normal outfit for a Sergeant from Interpol. His shirt is black, his pants are black, and his hands are covered in black gloves. His face is covered in… black leather. "Wrong again, rook." Friar exhales smoke in the general direction of Detective Monroe, who turns around, looking for anybody around. "We’re alone." He is right. Monroe realizes that everyone in his unit is gone. They are alone. His hand moves to his sidearm, eyes never moving from the masked man. "Who are you supposed to be; the Hitman?" "Hardly," he says softly. "I’m the reason you’re here. I give you..." Friar pulls the white blanket over the limp corpse of Domino Jones. "What you asked for." Monroe holsters his piece, clicking the strap back over it. Exhaling, he points around the room. "Where is Mika? And Matt Murray? This is-" Friar pushes himself out of the light, dragging a man by the collar of his jacket. The Interpol officer looks down to see the man, bound and gagged, and much to the Brit’s surprise... "He’s alive." Friar laughs, "Very much so, Detective." "And the girl, Mika? Where is she?" Monroe looks around Friar to the darkness, expecting another wonderful surprise. "We’ll need her to-" "Dead," Friar looks at Monroe’s blank face. "This is all you need." Squirming and kicking, Friar removes a manila envelope from his captive's back pocket and hands it to Monroe. The detective looks inside, briefly. "Holy shit. Is this... Igor Romanov? The Russian shipping mogul?" "That’s for you to figure out," Friar adjusts his hat, shrugging. "All I know is...he’s no saint and not just into shipping. Ms. Jones was his middle-man in Tel Aviv from Moscow. They chose this place as a safe haven; a hideout." "I’ve worked at Interpol for almost five years now. We know all about Romanov’s dealings. We also know he is somehow affiliated with the wrestling industry. How, we're not sure. We think he has a few men working in the US. And I..." Monroe's train of thought derails, watching Friar head for the door. Monroe raises his hand, "Hey where ya goin’?" Friar stops, puffing on the short stogy. He turns and looks over his shoulder, dropping the burning ashes to the ground before stomping them out. "To find me a way into wrestling...and to get some payback. The old fashion way." Chapter 4 Tel Aviv International Airport March 20th, 2009 1:50pm Among pedestrians and the public, Friar sits patiently in the airport with a brown burlap sack slung over his shoulder. His eyes are closed, trying to catch up on some lost hours of sleep due to the past week's events. 'Good afternoon ladies and gentlemen,' starts the woman behind the counter. 'Now boarding - Trans-Continental flight five ninety-three, non-stop service to Las Vegas.' Friar's eyes spring open like unlocked curtains. He glances to his watch, noticing that the time had come... In this world or the next A time will come. A time when our past will be dismissed. Our sins, forgiven. When eternal glory is within reach. A time when choice is the chance. A time when we must all cross into the Darkness Friar stands, gathering his things, and makes his way to the line before the walkway to his ride to redemption. He looks at the woman tearing the tickets, the people in nice suits, and then to his own reflection in the window. He grins. "My time is now."
