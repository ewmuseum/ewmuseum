Putting on a Brave Face

21 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/07 Synopsis - Putting on a Brave Face The career of Hollywood stunt double Carson Riley is hotter than ever. That was, of course, until he changed course. Now Carson is facing a new challenge, that of a professional wrestler in the world’s hottest organization. Carson has grown accustomed to a laid back lifestyle and awaits to see how his new venture will effect all the other things in his life. Chapter 1 - An Unexpected Visitor: Time: 7:30 a.m. The endlessly annoying sound of an alarm clock fills a dark and compact bedroom. Daylight has begun to creep through the cracks in the blinds which cover a sliding glass door along the western wall of the room. The male figure in the bed slowly creeps up and reaches over a half-naked, smoking hot blonde next to him. As the light hits his face, we reveal the identity of Carson Riley. Carson smacks the alarm clock off and falls back into his previous position. He lays flat on his back as the woman begins to show signs of life, wiping the sleep from her eyes. Although many in Carson’s previous profession are likely to wake up next to a random groupie, the girl of choice on this occasion is his girlfriend, Kate. "Oooooh, what time is it," asks Kate in a bitchy tone. "Seven-thirty." "Jesus, why did you set it to go off so early, babe?" "I set it before we went out last night. I didn’t anticipate on it being such a long evening." Carson begins rubbing with forehead with both hands and squints his eyes. "My head hurts. I told you to stop ordering so many shots of patron. I hate tequila," mutters Carson. "It’s good for you," says Kate with a straight face. "How is tequila good for you," questions Carson. "It has less calories than beer. It would’ve taken a dozen bottles of beer to make up for all the tequila we drank." Carson shakes his head and rolls his eyes left to right. Kate rolls over towards Carson and lays her arm across his bare chest. "So would the tequila have been good for me had I wrecked a forty-thousand dollar car on the way home?" "Hey, I told you we should’ve called a ride." "Really? I seem to remember you being the one begging me not to make you ride home in a ‘filthy’ taxi," says Carson while turning at smiling at her. "Mmm, drunken memory. I choose to recall it my way," says Kate while giggling back at Carson. As the two move closer and begin to kiss, a loud noise in the next room breaks their concentration. Carson sits up and once again glances at the clock. He pushes the covers off and begins to stand up out of the bed. He reaches towards the night stand for his phone and begins to flip through it. "Who would show up unannounced at seven-thirty in the morning," asks Kate with an attitude. "Probably Andrew, I have six missed calls on my phone." "I thought you fired Andrew?" "Well, he kind of broke things off with me. He just doesn’t want anything to do with the wrestling," answers Carson as the banging continues in the background. Carson slips into a paid of athletic shorts and exits the bedroom. As he approaches the front door of his apartment, the doorbell begins to ring over the sound of the banging. Carson turns the padlock and opens up the door. His former Hollywood agent, Andrew Green, stands at the door. Andrew is wearing a torn, white undershirt and a pair of beaten up jeans. He is covered from head to toe in sweat and holds out a newspaper while stepping into the apartment. Carson shuts the door behind him. "Carson, Carson! Look at this!" "Andrew...what are you doing here? I thought you didn’t want anything to do with me?" "After you see this you’ll want to hear what I have to say! Bourne Ultimatum did another 32-million over the weekend, it’s up to 133-million!" Carson grabs the newspaper and looks over the box office results. "Yeah, and it also dropped to number two. I like how you left that part out." "Carson, this movie is smoking hot just like I told you. You haven’t wrestled a single match yet, I want to give you one last chance to get out before it’s too late. Stop now before the damage is done, kid." "We’ve been over this. Get out of my apartment, Andrew." "Not until I get you to change your mind. I’m going to handcuff myself to your apartment until you come to your senses." "Well, If you think you can find something to handcuff yourself to go right ahead." Andrew reaches into the pocket of his jeans and pulls out a pair of police issue handcuffs. He adjusts one of the cuffs around his left wrist and frantically begins to search the room for something to latch himself onto. He walks into the living room area and locks the other cuff around a circular base of a table lamp. Carson’s eyes follow Andrew around the room in disbelief. He puts his hands on his hips and begins to shake his head. "You’re going to have to do better than that," says Carson in an almost mocking tone. "I’m going to sit my ass on this couch until you realize how ridiculous this whole thing is." "What’s ridiculous?" "You’re career, or lack of a career now." "No, ridiculous would be you handcuffing yourself to a table lamp like this is some kind of a sit-in." "I know what I’m doing. You just let me be," says Andrew poutingly. Carson walks over towards Andrew. He leans over the arm of the couch and unplugs the lamp from the wall. Carson picks the lamp up off the table and hands it to Andrew. He grabs him by the arm and forces him off the couch. "Come on, You’re done," says Carson while leading Andrew to the door. "What do you mean I’m done? "You’re out of here. Now." "With the lamp and everything," questions Andrew, not knowing how else to react. "Yeah, consider it a gift. I never got you anything for your birthday anyway." Carson opens the door and leads Andrew out of it. As he begins to shut the door, he pauses. "Don’t show up here again, Andrew. I’ll see you tonight at the Bourne party. Other than that, just leave me alone unless you have something to say besides how big of a screw up you think I am. Let me be the judge of that," says Carson calmly. "You’re an asshole, Carson. Fuck you," shouts Andrew with the lamp tucked firmly under his arm. Carson shuts the door in Andrews face and walks back towards the bedroom. Chapter 2 - Looking the Part: Time: 9:58 a.m. Carson is spotted walking alone down a rural street. He stops at a small tailor shop and attempts to enter. The door is locked prompting him to look at his watch. Carson reads the sign on the door of the facility and discovers the shop opens at 10 a.m. Carson proceeds to wait patiently outside of the shop. As he does so, he is approached by a eager young man. "Hey! What’s going on, man? I know who you are," says the young man enthusiastically. "Nah, I’m actually not who you think..." "You’re Carson Riley! I saw you’re promo on Killzone. That was badass," interrupts the young man. Carson isn’t used to being recognized on the street. More times that not, he is mistaken for other people. He takes a deep breath and lets out a sigh of relief. "Ha, yeah that’s me. Nice to meet you," says Andrew with a smile. "So you must be pretty excited about War in Washington!" "Yeah, should be a good card." "And you think, you’re making your debut in the tower match against the entire promotion!" A look of shock sets over Carson’s relaxed face. His eyes squint upward slightly as he tilts his head and looks back at the young man. "Say what? Nah, I think you must have me confused with another superstar." "Nah man, they just added you to the website. Every man on the roster and even a mystery opponent." Carson’s phone vibrates and he retrieves it from his pocket. He opens a text message from an unknown number that reads "See you in Washinton. You’re debuting in the tower." Carson shakes his head and takes a long, stressful breath. As he slips his phone back into his pocket, an employee unlocks and opens the door of the tailor shop. Carson extends his muscular arm to the young man and shakes his hand. "Well, nice to know I was the last one to find out. Thanks, take it easy bro," says Carson humbly. "No problem, good luck. You’re going to need it. Jonathan Rhine all the way, baby," says the young man as he turns and walks away. Carson enters the shop and approaches the clerk at the counter. "Hey, I’m Carson Riley. I’m here to pick up some wrestling gear I ordered." "Nolan just finished it up last night. It looks great. Let me go in the back and grab it for you." The clerk walks in the back and comes back with a pair of sky blue pants with white designs. She reaches under the counter and pulls out a pair of black wrestling boots. She sits them on the counter as Carson picks up the pants. "These are all wrong. I asked for royal blue with black designs. These look a little fruity," says Carson in disgust of the disastrous pants. "Well, Nolan is the only one here who makes tights and he won’t be back until Monday." "I need them before Sunday." Carson holds out the pants and turns them around to the back. The back of the pants read "Riley Carson." "My name is backwards. Carson is my first name." "Like I said, Nolan will be back Monday. You’ll have to take it up with him. Do you want them or not?" "I can’t exactly wrestle in my underwear." "Why not? Most of those guys do anyway." "Those are tights. I’m not going to walk out in red BVD’s. I’ll take these, it’s better than nothing." Carson pays for the gear and exits. Chapter 3 - (Another) Night Out: Time: 7:45 p.m. Carson stands in front of a mirror in his bedroom, slowly pushing the windsor knot of a black and silver tie towards the collar of his shirt. Kate walks out of the bathroom, decked out in a stunning black dress. They are both putting the finishing touches on their appearances before attending the party celebrating the success of Bourne Ultimatum. "Look...at...you! Stunning," says Carson. "I have to look my best. Should be a lot of important people there tonight." "I feel a little ridiculous going to this thing." "Why? This should be a big moment for you. They couldn’t have made the movie without your sacrifices." "It’s in the rearview. I’m a little apprehensive about attending another event where I’m destined to run into people who have questions that I’m not in the mood to answer." "Is this about Andrew, again?" "No, I just want to be taken seriously. I’ve wanted to be a wrestler since I was a child and as soon as the opportunity presents itself I feel like I’m making a bad decision." "Don’t let it get you down. We’re going to that party and we’re going to have a hell of a time." "Yeah, I suppose. Just promise me one thing, never give up on me." "I’m support you no matter what," says Kate with sincerity. Chapter Four - The Road Less Traveled: Time: 9:51 p.m. Carson and Kate arrive arm-in-arm at an upscale nightclub called CineSpace. They walk to the front of the line and approach the podium where the bouncers await with the guest list. "Hey, welcome. Name please," says the bouncer with the guest list. "Carson Riley and guest. We’re here for the Bourne Ultimatum celebration." The bouncer points at the list with a pencil and looks the list over. "No, I’m sorry sir but I don’t see you on the list." "That’s not right. Check again, I should be on there." "I looked and I don’t see you. I’m sorry, but this party is by invite only. If you’re not on the list you won’t be able to join that party," says the bouncer with conviction. "Can I speak to the owner? I was in the movie and I was invited. There’s no reason I shouldn’t be on that list." "Sure, just wait one moment." Carson puts his arm around Kate and shrugs his shoulders as she looks at him puzzled. The bouncer calls for the owner. A middle-aged man in an Armani suit walks to the door and shakes Carson’s hand. "Mr. Riley. Good to see you. What can I do for you," says the nightclub owner. "Yeah, I’m supposed to be on the list but they won’t let me in," explains Carson. "Yeah, someone called this afternoon and cancelled your reservations. We have a tight guest list and your spot was filled by someone else." "Wait, I didn’t cancel any plans." "Someone from Andrew Green’s office called. They said you were busy and would be unable to attend." "He no longer represents me. How can he cancel my plans?" "We called and verified it all. He still legally represents you so we had no reason to think anything was out of the ordinary," explains the owner. "And there’s absolutely nothing we can do?" "I’m sorry. I can’t bend the rules. You have a good evening." With Carson left outside in the cold, he owner heads back inside. Carson, looking as dejected as ever, glares down at an angry Kate. They slowly walk away from the club and down the strip towards their car. Kate grabs Carson’s hand in comfort and looks up at him with compassion. "I can’t remember even not getting into a club," says Kate. "Babe, I think it’s safe to say that things have changed." "We can go to Mood instead if you want." "No, I think it’s best that we head home. I’ve had a really bad day." "It’ll get better. Come on, let’s go have a drink and forget about it." "I’d rather just call it a night. I leave for Washington tomorrow morning," says Carson in a monotone voice. "Washington," asks Kate in a snapping fashion. "Yeah, I have my first match on Sunday." "When in the hell were you going to tell me?" "I just found out today. I was waiting for the right time." "And I guess I’m stuck here all alone," asks Kate dejectedly. "Yeah, I’m so sorry. I don’t want you to be there for this. It’s going to take some getting used to and I need to do this one alone." "You’re right, things have changed, Carson." Carson and Kate reach their car, enter, and drive off into the night. They have come so far together but only time will tell if their relationship relies on Carson’s perks that may have temporarily fallen to the waste side. For Carson, this is a life he isn’t used to. In a matter of days his life has changed drastically. At War in Washington perhaps all of Hollywood will learn whether or not his gamble was as strategic as he would prefer it to be.
