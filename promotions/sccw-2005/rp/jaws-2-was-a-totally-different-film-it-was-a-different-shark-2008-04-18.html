Jaws 2 was a totally different film! IT WAS A DIFFERENT SHARK!

18 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/04/08 It’s amazing how fascinating a piece of dry wall can be, just the patterns which can be merged through the intricacies, the paint that had been absorbed into the wall causing formations and patterns…. Ok so that’s all bullshit, its just a piece of wall, but sometimes you find yourself staring at a wall for no particular reason, it’s not so much that it’s the wall itself, but a way into your own mind. This wall had been the subject of Frosts attention for the past hour or so. Ethan are you alright? You have barely said anything all evening" The words were soft and calming but with more than a hint of compassion laced around them. The owner of the voice was a woman called Jen, she and Ethan had been together for a few months now, in fact it was down to Jen that he had returned to the wrestling industry, but at this present moment in time, she couldn’t help but think if he was really enjoying being back in the spotlight. "Yeah, I’m fine babe, just thinking." This wasn’t a lie as such but it wasn’t the whole truth, it was more a civil answer for them both to be able to carry on without actually delving into what was truly going through his mind. If the truth be told Frost wasn’t enjoying his return as much as he’d hoped, everything was just a little too different for his liking. It wasn’t just the name change, that was something purely incidental, that couldn’t have been averted, but the whole feel of the place was just wrong. It was too organised. Take Smitty for example, when the hell did he turn into that guy, what happened to the lovable rogue that had been Clinton Sage’s bitch for months on end, had enough finally been enough and had he snapped. Everything just seemed so regimented There was no Prince Pride no Fred Fusion, no madness, just straight edged down the middle rules of how the company should go, no sense of anarchy not even in the controlled sense. Well unless you include the worlds richest man on a quest to destroy the world, but that happens everyday doesn’t it.? There was one thing that was getting to Frost more than anything though. He was tired. For the first time in his career after his match he had felt tired. Not just tired but exhausted, physically and mentally. "Jen, do you think I am getting too old for this?" Frost asked with a heavy echoing distain in the tone of his voice, his eyes hadn’t darted from the position on the wall that had occupied his attention for the past hour, staring endlessly into space. From behind him Jen sighed and shook her head, her auburn hair appearing to float like silk as the movement flowed through each stand of hair. Wrapping her arms around him she squeezed him tightly. "Ethan, the day you are too old for this you will know, because when you wake up in the morning, you won’t want to get out of bed. You will just lie there and forget about everything. Forget about your training, forget about your job and just move onto something else, probably sell sort of cereal." She kissed him lightly on the cheek and returned back to the dishes. In her heart of hearts, she knew that Frost wasn’t at the end of his tether yet, he was just getting back into the swing of things, like a new kid on the first day of school. "I know, believe me sometimes though I don’t want to get out of bed, I want to stay in their with you." Frost finally pulled his attention from the wall and turned his eyes to Jen and allowed himself to smile. Her back was turned to Frost but he was still captivated by her, he’d often found himself staring at her for no apparent reason, other than to look at her. He seemed to find a certain comfort in just being with her. Sensing that she was being watched Jen turned around and smiled at him. "Yes that’s all very sweet Ethan but then I kick you out and tell you to get to the gym so that doesn’t last for long. Now will you snap out of it, you are tired because you haven’t slept in four days, not properly. You think I don’t notice these things? Jen moved herself away from the dishes and approached Frost who had suddenly become very quiet and edgy once again. Almost guilt ridden it could be said. "I thought you were asleep." Frost’s voice was barely audible and his head had dropped. This caused Jen to sidle over to Frost and slide her arms around him again, however as Frost felt Jen’s arms touch his own he got to his feet and moved away, walking to the window and staring out over the river. Oh sure there were reasons why Frost was asleep, there was reasons why every night when Jen fell asleep he snuck out of bed and spent hours in this very position staring out across the river, the moon often glistening off the surface. "Sorry Jen….just had a lot on my mind…." ~~~ "Jonesy, Jonesy, Jonesy…oh dear, oh dear, oh dear, you have got yourself into a situation here haven’t you." My name is Ethan Jones…..call me Ethan, the thought rang through my head as the rage grew, but rage wouldn’t help me here, not in this situation, I needed to keep a calm head, I needed to think my way out of this. The shank was held tightly against my throat, I could feel a trickle of blood begin to ebb its way down my throat and down my bare chest. As I allowed my eyes to open I looked into the eyes of my tormentor. Funny to think that he was one of the men who was supposed to be responsible for my safety and general well being in here. "What’s the matter boy, not got anything to say?" What the fuck was I expected to say, Yes I am in a tricky situation aren’t I. Would you mind awfully if you removed that slightly rusty blade from my throat. Dick. "You’re scared aren’t you, you know that with a slight twitch of my wrist I could end your life, it would be slow and it would be painful. Bullshit that’s all it was, he’d never be able to get away with actual murder, sure he could make certain things look like an accident, but not slicing someone’s throat that was ridiculous. Just keep quiet, let him get his power trip out of the way and I can get back to my fucking cell and inedible gruel, Christ where the fuck did this all go wrong. "Look at you you’re shaking like a leaf, you’re a fucking coward Jonesy." No far from it you prick, I am not a coward, I happen to be stood in the freezing cold stark bullock naked, that’s why I’m shivering don’t go getting ideas above your station. One of these days you’ll fucking pay for this…you aren’t a man you’re an animal who managed to play the system to your own sick and twisted needs. Sure I fucked up, that’s why I’m in here, but you are here through your own choice, that’s the difference between the two of us, it’s why I’ll always be better. "Answer me Jonesy or I swear I’ll slice your throat!" A dull thud is heard as a body falls to the ground. Yeah that’s right, hit me…it’s all you can do, isn’t it, fucking pussy, sure I can taste my own blood now but one day I’ll have my revenge…then on that day… My thoughts end there as a boot connects to my head, I physically feel my eyes roll back in my head as unconsciousness grips me once again. It’s sweet respite. However when I woke up, well that was the problem I did wake up, I often waited for death to come, I wanted it to free me so I could just walk away from everything, well not so much walk but you get what I mean. There was no sign of my tormentor, but that would not be the end of all this, it never would be. ~~~ "So it’s the nightmares again." Jen wasn’t overly surprised by this, Frost had mentioned that he always had a number of disturbing dreams and they had been recurring on and off for as long as she had been with him. "Pretty much." "Well I tell you what, why don’t we go to bed now, to get some sleep." Of course there was no intention of sleep on Jen’s mind, it was intended more as a distraction than anything else. Hopefully wear him out a little, just take his mind off it, he didn’t need this going into a match, he needed to be rested up as best as possible. "Ok, I will be there in a few mins. Frost remained at the window and stared out for a while, taking in the view once more allowing his mind to wonder. That was the thing with the past, how were you supposed to walk away from it, when it was there as a part of your life… ~~~ "Dad, I’d like you to meet Ethan, my boyfriend." My eyes met his…his met mine. Nothing was said, there was a nod and the briefest of handshakes. I swear though that I heard some words echo through my head that I hadn’t heard in a long time, something that I had put behind me, a history long ago. "Hello Jonesy."
