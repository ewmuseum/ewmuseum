A Bedtime Tale

Date: Today Paul Searle, its story time my friend. Tuck yourself in, and pay attention. - - - - - - - - - Two weeks ago, I decided to catch the New York Knicks playing host to the Cleveland Cavaliers, you know, NBA and all that jazz. Good game indeed, I enjoy watching NBA from time to time. If you’re curious, the Knicks were only down by one at halftime, which is a small miracle itself. Lebron James was his usual out of control self, making Knick players look silly (er than usual). However, that’s neither here nor there. Amazingly, guess who is announced as being the half-court shot winner? If you said Kelly Masters, then BINGO! I stroll down onto the court, and let me tell you, the feeling was great. MSG has played host to some of the NBA’s Greats. I mean, how many times did MJ drop more than fifty on the Knicks? I feel like the real deal now, and I practically burst when that scrawny tool, employed by the Knicks, hands me the basketball. This is pretty neat, all these fans cheering around me. Probably cheering for me to miss, fucking New York pricks, but cheering none the less. So I hoist up my shot, she’s a real dandy too … CLANG The ball rolls harmlessly toward the benches, the Knicks get to keep their money, and my opportunity has gone awry. Here comes tool bag, his insincere smile pisses me off, but I’m not about to KO the tiny bastard in front of all these witnesses. He remarks, "Nice try!" offering me a handshake. I accept, no sense in being rude, but I do have an ulterior motive. I suggest, "Maybe I could hang around and shoot with Lebron." Bewilderment etches itself in his face. "That’s not going to happen, you’re not a professional," he explains. "I’m down here though, you brought me onto the court." I hold my hands out, confused myself. "It was a one shot deal. Bringing you out from the crowd doesn’t make you a pro." I nod my head, makes sense. Back to my seat I go, to watch the professionals play the rest of the game. - - - - - - - - - I know it’s not the "Little Boy Who Cried Wolf", but do you understand? Can you pinpoint the lesson I’m teaching you? Huh, figured that’d take. I’ll have to go phase two, which I must say I’m pleased about. Phase two starts and ends in West Virginia with me knocking your head off your shoulders. Story time is over pal, time for Dream Street.
