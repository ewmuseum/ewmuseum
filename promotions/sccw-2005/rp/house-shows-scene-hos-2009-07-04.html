House Shows & Scene Hos

4 July 2009

Date: 7/4/09 'Hello, old friend.' Thad Denver was not one to question the logic of anyone or anything in the hierarchy. This was a primary reason why the inexperienced greenhorn was fairly well-liked by the majority of the locker room, as he adhered to the old Major League rookie credo of keeping his eyes open and his mouth shut. As kiss-ass as it might have been, he knew Spacely and Quasar's place on the food chain, and he preferred to still collect a check for the weeks to come...however meager it might be. This is why no brows were raised as to why they could afford to ship him this from the home office to the Old Rock Inn just outside Sioux Falls. This was where he was currently shacking up with Steve Darlesi, a first-year camera operator, and the only guy Thad had the nerve to ask to be his travel partner. Not necessarily because Steve was a fellow newbie to the staff, but because he seemed like the only one ballsy enough to run the risk of being kicked out of hotels with strict 'no pets' policies. Quasar, quite glad that it wasn't a period where he had to wear the VISOR on his face, walked over to the box Thad held on his lap and began to sniff it. He looked up at his owner and began to pant, which he always mistook for a smile. Thad assumed the contents inside reminded Quasar of his home, as the guitar had spent a lot of time in that household. Thad put the lightning-bolt shoulder strap over his head and rested the body of the Alvarez acoustic on his thigh. Instinctively, he toggled the knob for the E-string and plucked it repeatedly with his pointer finger. Fidgeting briefly, once the top string was in key, he worked his way down to complete the standard tuning. Thad slid his left hand between the 1976 floral print comforter and the ass of his frayed khaki cargo shorts to fish out his wallet: a pink vinyl Powerpuff Girls Velcro-seal. Fishing out a guitar pick from the pouch, he sighed as the only other contents were his Georgia state driver's license, a useless debit card from a bank account he had no money in, and twenty-six dollars in cash. 'Free-BIRD!' Steve, while being a fairly stereotypical large Italian from the New York area, had the southern jackass impression down to a T. He chuckled something that sounded like a mix between a gargling infant and a trumpeting elephant. Thad smiled as he called his bluff, strumming the 'And this bird you cannot change' bridge much to Steve's amusement. However, like most music snobs his age, it wasn't very long until he could no longer listen to the riff he was playing. Fiddling around with some basic arpeggio sweeps that sounded like a third-rate acoustic power metal solo, he sighed as the feeling of reminiscence hovered on top of him. It had been almost eight months since he'd even touched a six-string, but the ability to play had merely laid dormant in his memory. Just like riding a bike, his blessing of talent that for years he'd considered his curse had not faded into the horizon like his silhouette did when he'd left home to start his journey into professional wrestling. Thankfully, he had it now when he actually needed it. 'So you gonna do it for real, huh?' Steve pondered. Always one to spark any sort of conversation he could, Steve was not the type who could sit in silence for extended periods of time. 'And you aren't even promised any dough?' Thad nodded in response. 'Paul says there's usually a good turnout at their shows. At least there was with their band. Besides,' he continued, 'I'm pretty fortunate they're willing to put me on the lineup, even though there's no other touring bands on the show.' 'Shit, man,' Steve mused, a linger of doubt revealed in his body language as he reached into his duffel bag to retrieve his sack of mids. 'I just find it hard to believe playin' at some basement is gonna be worth your time. I mean,' he praised, 'you're pretty good, you singin' and all. I knew you was just horsin' around backstage that one time, trying to get Mary to like you and all...' 'I totally wasn't doing that to get Mary to like me,' Thad interrupted, looking up from his guitar and throwing daggers with his eyes at the camera operator. Steve howled in laughter in response. 'Look man, nobody sings 'Kiss From A Rose' when a chick's around if they aren't trying to impress the broad.' 'Why? It's a good anytime song.' 'That so?' Steve questioned, shooting a curious gaze at his Seal-loving companion. 'I'll take you one day and introduce you to my brothers at the next Freemasons meeting, tell 'em all about yer pipes of gold, and you can sing 'em that one. See if it's really a 'good anytime song,' ya fairy!' Thad shrugged his shoulders, then pointing an accusing finger at Steve. 'Fairy, huh? Says the guy who's complimented my singing of a supposedly 'fairy' song, no less. Looks like you're the Tinkerbell, you sprite you. Not to mention the shirt you're wearing of your favorite woman wrestler,' Thad sneered, mocking the Desade T-shirt often worn by his new enemy in Lane Stevens. 'Can I see your Oprah Book Club membership card again?' 'Fuck you,' Steve fired back. 'I already told ya, I stole this from the merch cart 'cause I didn't have a clean tee for the next day.' The kid who portrayed Spacely laughed out loud. 'Don't they give away free merch to staff? Steal my ass,' he joked, setting his guitar down onto the bed beside his dog, who was now stretched out in preparation for a quick nap. 'Well, it was the first one I could grab, considerin' there weren't any 'Spacely' shirts to be seen,' he mumbled as he carefully stuffed his piece with the weed he'd been traveling with. 'Sold out already?' Thad remarked, a smirk on his face. 'Naw,' Steve smiled back, exhaling and softly coughing from his lungs. 'No reason to make a shirt nobody would buy.' 'Touche,' Thad replied, accepting the lighter and the bowl from his co-worker and finely-tuned bullshit machine in Steve. Igniting the green and sucking the fumes into his throat, all thoughts on planning his set list for tomorrow night's show would have to wait a couple of hours. Nothing like wrestling, where he was meticulous down to a pinpoint on impressing an audience...music was as natural as breathing and making love to him. Well, maybe not so much the latter. He probably sucked at that. ----------------------------------------------------------------- Joey Taylors, Daniel Smith, and Becca Lymon had all rented and lived in the same three bedroom/two bathroom for all five years of their tenure at the University of South Dakota. Without a neighbor for at least a quarter mile, their basement became famous for punk and hardcore shows as well as some of the better parties held amongst the hipsters, crusties, emo kids, indie rockers, and 'normals' around the campus. The Fourth of July party had gained a tradition as being one of the rowdiest soirees that the house threw during the year. Everybody who stuck around Vermillion during the summer ended up coagulating at the said celebration, and Daniel was more than thrilled to receive the random e-mail from the out-of-towner who was very well acquainted with the members of one of his favorite bands. TO: [detail withheld] FROM: [detail withheld] SUBJECT: 4th of July Show? BODY: Hey Daniel! This is going to be totally out of the blue, but I hope either Paul or Stephen (iwouldetc.) has contacted you already about it. I'm doing some traveling for work and am looking to play here and there on the side, mainly for fun. The guys said they played last 4th and had a blast, and was wondering if you had room for me on the show? Just acoustic, I'm not heavy...but I've got an arsenal of covers and I think I might go over well. Totally understand if you can't, but give me a call anyway at [detail withheld] if it's a possibility! Thanks again man, Thad D. At about seven o'clock is when Thad finally got to meet the extra-friendly voice he'd chatted with a couple of times over the phone. With his guitar strapped behind his back and a faded Atlanta Braves cap that looked like it'd been run over repeatedly by a lawn mower, Thad shook the hand of the skinny red-haired Daniel who seemed just as warm in person. Standing next to the black Corolla, he opened the right rear door to release the golden retriever from the prison, who immediately leapt out and sniffed around the edge of the grass looking for a place to relieve himself. 'Whoa...yeah, you might want to leave that dog in your car, man,' Daniel remarked with surprise. Thad shrugged his shoulders. 'Not a problem at all.' Daniel smiled widely and slapped him firmly on the shoulder. 'I'm just kidding. We've got two of our own here. Just hope he gets along with other monsters.' After a minute of obligatory barking at one another, Quasar, Bandit, and Buster were hanging out like a trio of long lost best friends. The cocker spaniel and black lab respectively gave the bandanna-wearing goldie several whirlwind tours of the property, stopping only for the occasional attention from the party goers who populated the back yard. Four poles about sixty feet apart from one another marked off the designated square -- each corner with an iced-down keg pit containing Miller High Life to keep the celebration going for as long as possible. Being the host, Daniel had left Thad with a red Solo cup full of the nectar of the gods in his hand before wandering off to greet some of the other arrivals. As he gulped it down, he smiled as he counted about seventy or so already hanging out in small groups...and according to Daniel, they'd just tapped the beer. In a matter of minutes, it was time for a refill. As he walked toward the near left corner just next to the old wooden garage door leading into the basement, the sound of footsteps approaching quickly coupled with a female voice stopped him just before he could pump. 'Hey, I like your shirt!' Thad turned, giving the girl another look at his brown shirt which has a picture of a boomerang with the term 'BRB' above it...always one for the 'smart humor' when it came to slacker fashion. He grinned at the petite and albeit incredibly cute complimentor in jeans cuffed up to the knees and a cheesy light blue Venice Beach t-shirt with a dolphin jumping out of a crudely drawn ocean scene. Her sorghum-colored hair was styled like she'd just walked out of a sock hop at Rydell High, but it was a look that certainly suited her cute and bubbly demeanor. 'Thank you,' he replied, hoping there was a twinkle in his eye to hold her attention...not necessarily for the sake of later activities, but just so he wouldn't have to stand alone like a schmuck until it was time for him to perform. 'Need a refill?' he offered, grabbing the nozzle and holding it out. 'Please?' The girl extended her cup out and Thad obliged, making sure to tilt the spout to minimize the foam content. Thanking him, she turned and walked back over to her group of friends in the middle of the square, leaving the stranger with nothing but the booze to keep him company. Shit, he thought. I really ought to learn how to talk to girls. ----------------------------------------------------------------- He might not have been great at conversation using his own words, but after several beers, everyone wanted to hang out with that guy who 'fucking killed that CCR song.' It doesn't matter how old you are, what kind of clothes you wear, what kind of cigarettes you smoke, or how wealthy your parents are...pretty much everybody finds common ground being drunk together and singing along to oldies and 80's covers. A basement crammed full of college kids with arms around each other belting out the chorus to 'Purple Rain' was coined by Daniel as 'the finest moment in House of Houses history' as they stood amongst a group that included several offering their congratulations on a great set, including Stacie -- the girl who had complimented his shirt earlier that night. 'Man, thanks again for playing...seriously, you're welcome back here anytime you want. I know it's not much, but we took up all the donations I think we're gonna get tonight,' said Joey, the other male resident of the home. 'No man,' a now tipsy Thad responded, 'thank you, from the bottom of my heart, man. This was seriously the most fun I've had in months, and I really appreciate you guys' hospitality.' Daniel had handed him about two-hundred bucks in ones and fives collected in a giant jar on the kitchen table. It was a true sign of the amazing energy and positivity of the guests -- no asshole had tried to dip in and steal it when no one was looking. Not bad at all, especially if your night's work consisted of playing a forty-minute set and getting drunk for free. Eyes wandering around the circle, Thad caught Stacie giving him a drunken fuck-me stare, tracing her finger around the rim of her cup. At least that's what it seemed like to him. He chose not to return it, however, and the group was broken up by the yell of someone across the lawn. 'Let's shoot these motherfucking FIREWORKS!' Stacie announced to everyone her intentions to go and take a quick piss, asking that they didn't start the light show without her. Lightly bumping into Thad to get past him, she let her hand brush across his arm flirtatiously as the rest all started toward the mortars and bottle rockets that were sure to drive the dogs bat-shit insane. Thad turned and walked with the rest of the crowd, but was met with two palms on his left shoulder pulling him towards a pair of whispering lips. 'She wants to fuck you. Don't do it, she's kinda the local scene slut around here.' The lips belonged to Becca, who hadn't said much to him the entire night until just now. Thad's hazy gaze moved up from her worn out Chuck Taylors all the way up her denim-covered legs and ending up at her clavicle as he nodded his head. 'Thanks for the warning.' ------------------------------------------------------------------ She wasn't a standout by any means, and certainly not as striking as the rather disappointed 'scene slut' who'd left the party without the cute boy who looked directly at her when he sang The Cars' 'Drive.' Hell, Thad probably wouldn't have even given her a thought if he'd been sober. However, Becca was sanguine, a fantastic kisser, her hair smelled like lemons, and he swore her bed was made of 100% cumulus cloud. Which made it hard for Thad to get up from under the spread...luckily, she felt their awkward conversation was interesting enough not to kick his ass out. 'Just so you know,' Becca stated just after yawning morning breath directly in his face as they were still nuzzled close to one another, 'I don't have sex with every musician that stays at our house.' 'Just the shitty ones, then?' Thad responded playfully. 'Nah, you don't seem like the type.' 'Neither do you,' she smiled, lifting her hand to run her fingers through the platinum mess on his head. Thad shot her a sly look. 'How do you figure?' 'You're still here cuddling with me, aren't you? What fuck-happy rock star does that?' He nodded in agreement. 'You got me pegged.' Thad bit his lower lip in thought, looking almost comical as he decided to confess to the stranger with smeared makeup next to him. 'That was actually the first time since...well, since my relationship.' 'How long?' 'About a year and a half, I think. I can't really remember,' he replied. 'Awww,' Becca cooed. 'I've had one of those kinds of break-ups before. You're just constantly trying to find someone who measures up to that someone you thought was so perfect...' Thad couldn't help but to interrupt. 'Until you find someone who does?' Becca blinked slowly, smile forming back on her lips. 'No. Until you snap the ruler in half and throw it in the garbage.' Thad 'Spacely' Denver did not smile, instead grew more solemn as a result of what was probably some of the best yet most simple advice he'd ever received on the subject. 'They don't make rulers that tall.'
