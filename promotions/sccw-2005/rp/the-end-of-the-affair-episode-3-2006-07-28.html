Date: July 28th 2006 July 28th 2006 It had taken a while for Matt to decide exactly what he wanted to do with the cuckold. The most extreme route he considered was also the most expensive. A hit in this town could be arranged from as little as ten grand but if you wanted a proper job done, you were talking more like fifty. After some brief consideration though, he discarded it. As much as he loved Anna, ordering a murder was too much, even for a man with his background. He’d killed enough people in his life, in the name of liberty and justice, without sanctioning a hit in the name of love. The other extreme was to do nothing, let it ride as they say in this town, see what happens. This route he discounted almost as quickly. For if he was to let Anna decide in her own time, there was always the chance that things wouldn’t work out in his favour, and that wasn’t an appealing idea. After all, this city had enough gamblers without adding himself to the number. No, the extreme methods weren’t going to work this time, there hadto be a middle ground option that would work. So as he relaxed beside his pool, having finally managed to take a day off, he had a hard time focusing on what Anna, lounging at the bed beside him, was saying. Truth be told, he had a hard time even appreciating her figure in the new white bikini she had on, the lightness of the fabric accentuating the ever-darkening tone of her skin, the sweat droplets glistening on her skin adding to the allure that would normally have had him struggling to…control his excitement. "-Torrie wanted to go to LA for a few days…Are you listening to me?" (Was she talking to him?) "Matt?" (Looks like it.) "What? Oh, sorry, I was miles away." The laugh that comes from Anna’s lips is light and playful, as is the touch of her hand as she touches Matt’s arm, a movement that reminds us of how she ran her hand up the chest of another man just a few days earlier. "What’s wrong baby, you’ve been out of it all day. Is it work?" (I wish.) As D’Angelo responds, he forces himself to come to the here and now, to put aside any other thoughts or plans, lest Anna somehow divines his true worries and intentions. To this end, he feigns tiredness, a yawn emanating from deep within him, as if on cue. "Yeah, you know what it’s like, just catches up on you after a while." "Aww, poor baby!" Anna says this with a giggle, one which Matt reciprocates falsely, keeping up the pretence while other alternatives of how to deal with this Marquez problem ran through his head. And then it him, the thought just popping into his brain - money. This Josh guy was a gambler after all, and there was nothing his kind valued more than money, especially money obtained without any risk. Yes, he’d go the old ‘offer he couldn’t refuse’ route and the guy would willingly leave Anna’s life forever. Well, that was the reality in D’Angelo’s mind anyway… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* The plan went into effect just a few days later. Matt had arranged for Anna to have a short break, going down to LA with Torrie for a couple of days, with a brand new AmEx card and a chauffeur for the entire trip. Just a little gift to ‘show her how much he loved her’, was how he explained it. After all, the truth wasn’t exactly for her to know about. Saying goodbye to her on the terracotta steps of his house, Matt was as easygoing and ‘normal’ as ever, right up until the TownCar taking her to the airport disappeared round the bend. At the very second she left his view, his demeanour changed to all-business, pulling his cellphone out and dialling a number, then speaking three simple words. "Where is he?" Then it was over to the black Navigator, a steely look on his face and a bag of twenties in his left hand as he went to eliminate this pest from Anna’s life. As he put the SUV into ‘Drive’, he reassured himself that the wrench lying on top of the money was strictly for insurance… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* July 31st 2006 Today was Josh’s day off - if, that is, you would classify gambling in a multitude of casinos in Sin City as a real job. He was, as usual, using it wisely though, getting away from the constant rattle of slot machines and queries of waitresses and relaxing poolside at the Luxor, the black pyramid that was the centrepiece of the resort shining like a precious stone in the afternoon sun. Normally he would spend this time examining the wide array of female forms filling up every available sun lounger round the shimmering blue lagoon. Today though, he didn’t have the slightest interest. He felt, rather oddly, like a gay man in a brothel, seeing phenomenal bodies all around him yet not feeling even the slightest stirring in his loins. Not that this lack of attraction to the opposite sex worried him, he didn’t start questioning his sexuality, but he was shocked at just how powerful his feelings for Anna were. In the past having a girlfriend wouldn’t have stopped him from chatting up another woman, maybe even giving away his phone number. He was, after all, a red blooded male. This time though, even thinking about doing that was taboo and doing it, well, that would be a betrayal that even his sometimes questionable conscience wouldn’t be able to handle. Actually, now he thought of it, even being here was bad. Already three girls had tried to start a conversation with him and he’d had to explain that he was taken. It felt rude and, if he was honest, a little awkward - he wasn’t used to turning down 32DDs. Not back to the slots or the tables though, today he felt like unwinding in the sun so he’d move somewhere more private. Somehwere like the secluded pool on the roof of his apartment complex. As he gathered his belongings, throwing his iPod and this month’s edition of GQ magazine into a Jack Spade satchel, slipping his feet into a pair of Havaianas, he had a strange feeling, a sense of being watched, one that he dismissed offhandedly, probably one of the girls near him. However, as it was to transpire, he shouldn’t have been so indifferent… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* As Marquez pulled into the lot of Sunset Tower, Matt D’Angelo readied himself for the upcoming confrontation, both in his head and physically. Mentally, he was rehearsing how he was going to approach the situation, figuring out the best way to leave physicality as a last option, albeit an option that he had every belief would come to pass. As far as physically went, well, he was already in great condition but there were other measures, that needed to be taken, namely a pair of disposable latex gloves. The reason for these should be obvious to all of us who have ever seen an episode of CSI. The flat number was already committed to memory, the information delivered in, fittingly, a military style report to Matt’s email address, the one he used strictly for correspondence that fell into the ‘grey’ area of the law, safe from prying eyes. He’d give it a minute or so, then he’d go in and sort this problem out once and for all. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Josh brought the blue S2000 to a stop in his usual spot near the entrance to the building. The spaces here weren’t reserved but residents quickly learned where it was acceptable to park their vehicles without disrupting the hierarchy. The rules were simple - the bigger your apartment, the closer your space was to the entrance. Marquez was the owner of the second largest flat in the complex so he got the second space. Just another one of the laws of the urban jungle. As he opened the door to exit the roadster, however, something caught Josh’s eye. A Lincoln Navigator, a car abundant throughout Las Vegas and indeed America, was sitting on the other side of the lot. Nothing too strange about that, at least not at first glance, but something twigged in Josh’s brain. Hadn’t a similar car pulled out of the Luxor lot just after him? And didn’t it look a lot like a car that had slowed down shortly after Josh turned into the little known shortcut to his apartment? Probably it was nothing, but past experience with collectors of various debts had taught him that the old motto - ‘better safe than sorry’ - was one to be heeded. So when he entered his building, he did so with his senses alert, eyes and ears open and adrenaline coursing through his body, readying it for the possibility of confrontation. Waiting for the lift to the 8th floor, the rudiments of a plan formed in his head. If he was in fact being followed, his hunter would want to take him by surprise, storming into his apartment and putting him on the back foot. He wouldn’t be expecting the tables to be turned. The linen closet across the hall would do nicely, giving Josh a view of the stairs, the lift and his apartment door… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* D’Angelo reached the top of the stairs and opened the door cautiously, wary of meeting any of the other residents. Luckily the corridor was empty though, allowing Matt to get to work on the apartment door lock immediately. His hands moved with the skill of a professional, the picks working the mechanism smoothly and without scratching the surrounding area. Clearly this entrance was meant to remain a secret to the outside world, regardless of the outcome of the imminent confrontation. As the final tumbler clicked into place, Matt rose to his feet and twisted the handle slowl- -BAM- Whatever hit him from behind hit him hard. His first thought was that it was an elbow that had caught him at the base of his skull, a thought that was immediately replaced by thoughts of fighting back as he felt an arm slip round his neck, looking for what mixed martial artists would call a rear naked choke, a sure winner in any fight, whether in or out of a ring. It was at that second his Marine training kicked in, flipping the guy over his back, causing his assailant, undoubtedly Marquez, to land hard on the wooden floor of the apartment. Before he could pin him down though, he was back on his feet, looking at Matt while standing in a boxing stance. This was interesting… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Whoever this guy was, he knew how to handle himself, thought Josh. First impressions were that he was solidly built, probably knew some judo and jiu-jitsu and was far too well dressed to be a debt collector, even for this town, where fortunes could do a 180 on the roll of a dice. "Who are you and what do you want?" A holdall lay at the intruder’s side, its colour matching the black gloves that covered his hands and its contents a mystery to all except the trespasser. Still, the black gloves confirmed the idea of this man as a pro. "Oh you don’t know me Josh, but we have something in common." Okay, the guy new his name and where he lived, so obviously he had resources at his disposal, but the ‘something in common’ confused him. Josh’s brain went into gear, thinking of people he’d crossed, toes he’d stepped on or feathers he’d ruffled recently. He came up blank - who was this guy? "What are you talking about?" "There’s a hundred thousand dollars in this bag. It’s yours if you break up with Anna, have no contact with her and keep this to yourself. No strings." As his SUV pulled into the driveway of the house where she spent most of her time now, she calmed her nerves. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Indoors, sitting down in the spacious lounge she broke the news, didn’t skirt the issue.
