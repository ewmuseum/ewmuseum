Comebacks and such

17 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/07 'Yes sir, Mr. Pierce.' 'Certainly, there won't be a repeat of Atlanta.' 'You can count on me, sir.' Everyone at some point or another suddenly realizes that their life is vastly different than what they imagined it would be growing up. That desk in an office working for an asshole boss is as far away from whatever the dream was -- a basketball court, flying a jet, famous actor -- could possibly be. A long time ago, Joe Bob Smith accepted his lot in life. He embraced his mediocrity. Somehow, realizing this same fact for the man on the phone depresses him more than his own life ever could. 'Okay, sir, I will get it done immediately.' As Joe Bob watches his friend and boss, Raymond Pierce, VP of Pierce Industries answer to his younger brother, Andrew Pierce, he can't help but remember a time when they traveled the world as superstars -- well, certainly Joe Bob was never a superstar, but he got to live life like one. Even though Joe Bob never had what it takes to be the wrestler he dreamed he'd become, he was an important helper to a man that did make it. And now, that man has settled into a second career, a second life. A life he hates. This entire office is a shrine to the past with pictures commemorating the career of the man known as 'Perfect' Raymond Pierce. Joe Bob noticed long ago that every time his boss talked business on the phone, he'd look longingly at pictures of past glory and accept his mediocrity even more. There was a time that Raymond would never have allowed himself to become a powerless figure-head working under his hated younger brother. That time is long gone. Just as that Raymond Pierce is long gone. As Pierce hangs up the phone, Joe Bob can't help but be sad. 'Hey, Joe, what's that?' Joe Bob lifts a brown delivery box up and looks at it, 'It's a 40th birthday present from Damon, Ray.' Pierce leans back in his chair and locks his hands behind his greying hair. 'He probably just made Unmentionable action figures out of dried up dog poop.' 'Umm, that's exactly what he did, sir.' Joe Bob casually drops all of Ray's mail on his desk, strangely yearning for a time when he'd get yelled at for referring to Pierce as 'Ray' or for having the audacity to even place something on his desk without permission. Instead, Raymond shuffles through the assortment of junk mail, invoices and... 'Hey, what's this?' 'Ummm, Ray, don't be mad...' Joe Bob knows his request is fairly pointless. He has no doubt Ray will be mad when he sees the brochure that he slipped into the mix. ''The Young Lion's Cup'?' For just a moment, the old Pierce anger bubbles to the surface as he crumples up the sheet and throws it across the room where it bounces off a dart board (technically, it bounces off a picture of Ray's sister which is pinned to the center of the board and full of dart holes). 'Are you mocking me, Joe?' 'No, Ray! No, I swear! Look, you hate it here. I know that. You know it's true. This is your opportunity to get back to where you were! You could be the best again. It hasn't even been announced yet. You'd have a head start preparing for it!' Raymond Pierce wasn't known as a man that lacked confidence, but that person is long gone. '...Joe. I'm a 40-year old man. I haven't wrestled in years. Just...let it go. I did.' 'No. No. No! NO!' Joe Bob never frequently yelled at his boss, as in, never ever, but for the first time, he found the strength to let loose. 'You're Raymond Pierce, one of the greatest wrestlers of all time. You were an eight time...!' '...nine, actually...' '...World Champion! You are NOT some office worker that answers to your younger brother. I don't care that you're 40-years old today...(happy birthday, by the way)...you're going to enter this Young Lions Cup and figuratively start your career anew!' Pierce expected that outburst less than Joe Bob expected to make it. For minutes, the silence and tension consumed the room. Finally, Pierce pushes the speaker option on his phone. 'Andrew...?' 'Yes, Ray?' 'I quit.' '...' 'I'm coming out of retirement.' '...' 'I am going back to professional wrestling.' '...' '...' '...' 'HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAclick' Pierce swipes his hand across the desk, knocking the phone off the table. 'Okay, Joe Bob, we do this...we do this my way.' ***** [After years of inactivity, it can be an intimidating experience walking into a gym for the first time. That's especially the case when you're a 40-year old retiree.] 'Perfect' Raymond Pierce (PRP): This is crazy. [Despite constant reassurance from the one man that believes in him, Pierce clearly hasn't fully embraced the return.] Joe Bob Smith (JoBS): Ray, you need to remember who you are. You still have it in you. We'll get you ready and you will once again be the... [Pierce waves a hand to cut him off.] PRP: I don't even have anyone to spar with, anyone to lead the training. This is ridiculous. [Joe Bob reaches for the door the gym as a smile crosses his face.] JoBS: Oh, I took care of that! ['I'm Too Sexy' by Right Said Fred begins to play, seemingly out of thin air, and in through the door walks, nah, struts...] PRP: Jesus Christ. SSS: Not quite, but lots of people have made that same mistake over the years! JoBS: 'Sexy' Steve Sire! PRP: Joe Bob, you're fired. SSS: That's the spirit! JoBS: But... I was just trying to help, sir! After all, who else out there knows how to play it as dirty as you used to?! You want result, don't you? You want to win, right? SSS: Through round, quivering jowels, your blubbery buddy speaks the truth, Pierce, and you know it. PRP: Fine, whatever. Joe Bob, you're un-fired. For now. ***** [One week later.] JoBS (whispering): Is he getting worse? SSS: That is definitely not possible. [In the ring, Raymond Pierce whips his sparring partner into the corner before awkwardly charging in, only to find a well-placed elbow hit his face.] SSS: Charge low, lead with the shoulder and when in doubt *drop* *down* *and* *low* *blow*! ***** [Another week.] JoBS (whispering): They're announcing the tournament next week. Is there any chance he doesn't get hurt? SSS (yelling): AGAIN! (whispering) I think death is more likely. [In the ring, Pierce's sparring partner puts him in a headlock. The ref yells 'begin' and Pierce grabs at his wrist and attempts to spin it around into a hammerlock, but his younger, stronger opponent somersaults through, flipping Pierce over.] SSS (yelling): AGAIN! [Pierce glares over angrily at Sire before allowing himself to be put back into a headlock. This time, he reaches down for his opponent's ankle and tries to shift his leverage. The young wrestler quickly lifts his leg and stops on Pierce's hand, causing him to yelp in pain before being yanked back up into the original position.] SSS (yelling): AGAIN! [Pierce exhaustedly lifts his opponent up in an attempted atomic drop but the youngster shifts his weight forward and hiptosses Pierce over to the mat. This time, the opponent chuckles a little before talking to him.] OTP: Ray-Ray, thanks for this opportunity to prove how ready I am for the big time. The name's Otis T. Preston. I'm assuming you'll hear that name a lot over the next few years. Steve, what now? SSS (yelling): A-GAIN! [Pierce stares down at his former rival with a mix of animosity, exhaustion and failure.] PRP (through deep breaths): ...i...can't...do.. SSS (yelling): DO IT AGAIN UNTIL YOU DO IT RIGHT! [Preston slaps the headlock on Pierce again. Pierce once again tries to reverse it into a hammerlock. This time, Preston spins free, wraps around and grabs...] [...nothing...] OTP: Oooooomphf!!! JoBS: YES! [The quick youngster spun free of Pierce's reversal, ready to grab the old man in any number of holds, only to see no one standing in front of him. Instead, Pierce had anticipated the move, dropped to his knees and raised a fist straight to Preston's groin.] [Pierce stands up and hovers over his fallen opponent.] PRP: Nice... [Stomp to the head.] PRP: ...to meet... [Stomp.] PRP: ...you. [Stomp.] PRP: *I* am the IMPECCABLE WARRIOR. [Stomp.] PRP: *I* am the FLAWLESS ONE. [Stomp.] PRP: *I* am HE WHO NEVER ERRS. [Stomp.] PRP: UNDEFEATED. [Stomp.] PRP: UNDENIABLE. [Stomp.] PRP: UNSTOPPABLE. [Stomp.] PRP: The only ELEVEN TIME WORLD CHAMPION. [Stomp.] PRP: The original member of the FUSE Hall of Fame. [Stomp.] PRP: Your Young Lion's Cup tournament winner. [Stomp.] PRP: I AM 'PERFECT' RAYMOND PIERCE! [Stomp.] PRP: And this has been your unending pleasure to make *MY* acquaintance. [On the outside, Joe Bob and Steve Sire look into the ring as Pierce stares down at his victim with contempt and disgust in his eyes; drops of blood splatter out with every stomp.] SSS: Now we're talking. [Sire smiles at the violence.] JoBS: He's going to do it. I knew he could do it! He's actually going to do it. PRP (yelling): YOU HEAR ME? I AM 'PERFECT'! [Stomp.] PRP: RAYMOND! [Stomp.] PRP: PIERCE! [The Flawless One kicks the non-moving body of his opponent out of the ring.] PRP: Now Go Away!
