Decisions, Decisions (Part 2)

undated

Date: Today (We fade into a locker room, Legion and Luci have just returned from his confrontation with Dusk, the Devil's rejects look visibly annoyed after Dusk's nonchalant attitude towards them...) Luci: That's all he could say... boo-hoo I have a bounty on my head and can't be bothered to convince you today, if YOW hadn't made that Watchmen crack he would have probably been more willing to talk, even if I do look good in a Silk Sapphire costume! (she winks at the camera) (Legion turns his head with a slight smile but his body language tells otherwise, he looks tense probably due to the No Limit tournament or it could be that the stress of picking sides is finally getting to him...) Legion: It's weird though, we move here to gain some steam and in three weeks he's the only one to cost me a match, at least I have the Livewire title in my possesion... which reminds me have you heard of Lauren since our encounter? (Once again Luci starts turning red with embarassment and a slight giggle creeps in, some internet people compared the first instance at the previous temptation to how CoZen reacted to a Tyler Rayne mention months ago...) Luci: Not yet but I got some friends to fly over a gift for me to give her... the Crack The Skye limited edition plus a t-shirt from my excursion into the business. (Legion mutters 'subtle' under his breath) Legion: Well there's two more people left... Amy and Desade then we decide... and before you say hun i'm not going to go all 'Type O Negative' on them! (Luci sulks a bit) Luci: Fine, I'll get back to watching this then... (as luck may have it Savant shows up onto the screen as we cut away...) (We now cut to a couple of days after SOS to a darkened room... Legion is sitting on a chair holding the Livewire title in his hands looking down at it still in bewilderment even though it's been a couple of weeks since he won it.) Legion: So I come from the nice vacation I had last week to a beginning of a quest, the objective being to get a shot for Amy Campbell's Uni- (Luci: SUPERVERSAL!) title. To my surprise Mr Kensington decided to put me against a man that has talked about me a lot in my other home of NFW but we've never fought and yet Steve Knox you were willing to put me on a shameless homage to Richard Nixon's enemies list... When you came in no one had heard of you yet you had a belt for some promotion that had 'died' and was reborn in TSC and then you won the Elite and National belts for PTC and NFW, yet now things have changed, the fall has took your pride and I'm the one with the belt! In Knoxville all the jokes that I've heard in NFW will end as you will send a message to BOTH SCCW and NFW that I am one to be feared with! Oh and Amy dear, regardless of whether I side with the devils or the angels, IF it happens give me ALL you have because I will stop at nothing to cement my place here... (We fade to black)
