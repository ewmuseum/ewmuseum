Step One: Rebirth

11 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 11 [Belleview, NY] He can smell it all around him. It is on every man, woman and child that walks past him. What is that smell, you ask? It is the smell of conformity. The smell of a belief that one cannot think or act for himself. It is the antithesis to the reason he sits in this prison, this asylum for the free thinkers of the world. They call him crazy, he calls himself ready to start a revolution. The padded rooms are only for the really special people, the truly criminally insane. It is in these rooms that he learned to fight. To wrestle. (Are you surprised?) He bounced around the room, taking bump after bump and learning the skills of his trade. Two nights a week they would allow them television time where he could watch superstars such as Tristan Belmont and Mike Meyhu wreak havoc on unsuspecting victims in the middle of the circle that was squared. The names, however, faded and new superstars have been born since then. Men such as Sean Sterling and Jonathan Rhine have taken the spotlight and he watched these men. He studied them, knowing that one day he would be in the spotlight and fighting them, playing a game of human chess and manipulating them into his game. Well, finally, that day has come. That day... Is today. *** 'It is with great pleasure', the doctor says in a delightful tone, 'that young mister Aleister has been cured of his sickness and can resume his life in the normal world.' Chris sits in the corner of the room, no longer shackled in a straight jacket or handcuffs. This is his phoenix process as from the ashes of his past life, he begins anew. With a large smile on his face, he listens to what the good doctor says, talking of his cure and glad to hear such nice comments said about him, compared to the days when the nicest comment he had ever heard was that one day he would eventually die or be killed. 'After his 21st birthday', continues the doctor, 'we started a rigorous process to rid Christopher of his demons and, after working long and hard, finally worked through his aggression enough to come to terms with his problem and work past it.' He has been cured from the original thoughts that had brought him in here. Once upon a time, he had a dream inside of his head. It wasn't a bad dream but it was one that involved bad things. Or so they told him. His dream was to be an individual, to not believe in what people tell him he should believe but to believe in what he himself thought he should. He called it non-conformity, they called it a sickness. So the doctors fought long and hard to rid him of this sickness until the day that the two parties finally agreed that the sickness was gone. 'So today marks a new day', the doctor is slowly finishing up. 'It is a day where young Chris Aleister will be able to walk among us. He will walk among society hand and hand and not be a threat to anyone. January 11th, 2008, I am proud to say, is the day that Chris Aleister is reborn, a beacon of our society. Are there any questions?' Yes, it may be hard to believe, but not very many people actually leave Belleview. Some escape, some leave in a body bag but few and far between is there EVER a case where a man is actually released from Belleview's institution to be free in the world again. So this is a big event and, in order to fit its magnitude, the press are there to show the importance of such an ordeal. And, proving they are press, as soon as there is an opening for questions, their hands shoot straight into the air. The doctor points out a hand from the crowd. A nerdy young man stands up, it wouldn't be very surprising if he was reporting for some high school newspaper, your normal pencil-pushing nerd, glasses and the full garb including some small press pass hooked to his shirt pocket. As soon as he stands up, he pushes his glasses back to the top of his nose with the hand holding his pen before starting a small diatribe of questions for the doctor. 'Doctor', he starts. 'Is it true that this is the same young man who tried to assassinate a governor, deface a public structure and destroy a post office all in the name of so-called non-conformity? Do you think that there is really a cure for the supposed sickness that he has?' The story of young Chris' life, always being doubted for what he did. His intentions were never to hurt anybody, all of the doctor's know that. The point of all of it was to show the world what could happen. To prove to the world that, these people weren't needed to have a thriving society. It is to be re-iterated, however, that he is free of all of the sickness though. So, he sits on his hands, his mouth shut and just listens as the great doctor defends him. 'There is a cure to every sickness', the doctor retorts. 'Young Chris had a sickness. He believed things that were not true and, luckily, we were able to reverse said sickness before it truly became a problem that would stay with him for the rest of his life. This young man, he has ambitions, he wants the live a life that doesn't involve confinement and uniforms. We here at Belleview believe that we just helped him out with the rest of his life.' The doctor's smile, the confidence oozing out of it. He is one hundred percent sure that he will never see Chris Aleister again inside the confines of Belleview Asylum. Chris shares that same smile along with the same confidence. It is at this point the the doctor ushers Chris to stand and walk to the podium. The second he rises to his feet, the flashbulbs hit him. The spotlight always felt weird to him but as soon as this attention turned on him, he loved it, he felt like a superstar. This, he thinks, is what it feels like to be a professional wrestler. He is silent, he cannot think of the words to say while tons of reporters shout out questions at him, some derogatory, some trying to get personal information from them. One certain one strikes a chord with him and makes him think. The question 'What do you plan to do?' is the one thing he grabs from a million voices and, it doesn't take him long to think about it when he blurts a response. 'I'm going to be a professional wrestler.' There is a small amount of shock from the room but, as the statement sinks in on everybody, it isn't hard for them to believe that a crazy person would enter such a world. Hell, from what they have seen, crazy men live in that type of lifestyle every single day. And just as quick as everybody stops talking to listen to his statement, a whole barrage of other questions begin being tossed at him. He stays cool and confident and decides he isn't going to let them decide what to answer or say, he is going to go into business for himself (Sound familiar?). 'My name is Chris Aleister and it will be a name you will soon relate to when you hear the phrase professional wrestling. FUSE Wrestling has matches at the beginning of their cards where they place quote-unquote Young Lions into the ring with each other to fight for supremacy. Well they may consider me a Young Lion but the world already knows me as a cold and calculating animal. When I step into the ring with them, I show no mercy. Good day.' The world is shocked to hear that, the doctor knows this is publicity though. Chris Aleister has been cured and would never harm a soul, he just wants fifteen minutes in the spotlight while he takes a chance with professional wrestling. As Aleister walks off the stage, they share a happy glance with each other and shake hands. In the background, everybody continues screaming questions out as the doctor turns back, happy to know that his young student is finally cured. Chris Aleister turns back with the same smile on his face, knowing that he isn't.
