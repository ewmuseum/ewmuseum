Date: Today Sin*Con had come and gone with no sign of Legion at least on camera, what viewers ended up missing was Legion being distracted by von Doom costing him a chance at a future reward by winning the Future considerations match, later on in the show steps were laid down for a future match between the man that Legion had been trying to ‘help’ in Jay Draven and the Mastodon in Roderick Ashe in a Last Man Standing match at the Sin On Spike 4 with Legion as the referee. The reason that Legion had not been seen however was according to the SCCW front office technical difficulties between the Cinemax signal throughout the night due to ‘extreme weather.’ We now go into the inside of the hotel room that Legion is staying in before moving on to Texas, he is holding a Nokia N97 in his hand punching in a phone number, the display on the screen when he presses call comes up N.Kensington… However instead he gets the answering machine message: " This is Nigel Kensington, I’m sorry I can not get back to you right now, in this case please leave a message and I’ll get back to you." Legion muttered to himself "Ever since the Lane incident he’s never answered his mobile but this needs to be rectified… The beep in order to leave a message hit. "Nigel, this is Gabriel, we need to talk about last week’s debacle, you forget our little deal in making sure my plan for these few weeks go ok or I let the whole roster know why we ended up in Mexico or why the Elite title matches weren’t screened half the time… " Legion then hung up the call and moved onto the SCCW mobile web site to see if the line up had been revealed, he suddenly notices who he’s facing and puts the phone down slowly. What the other patrons in the hotel can hear for the next hour is laughter that could be described as inhuman. For those that don’t know James von Doom and Legion have had a very storied rivalry in the New Frontier Wrestling promotion which culminated in March with Legion believing he had retired von Doom from wrestling altogether until he showed up with Rick Malloy last week… Thoughts during the laughter hour raced through Legion’s mind of how to combat the man calling himself the ‘Puppetmaster’ as for once it was going to be a normal match, something he believed was something von Doom wasn’t used to when wrestling him but he realised that James may have some experience… Following the hour he decided to relax and mentally prepare when suddenly the Nokia N97 started to flash with the sound of ‘My girlfriend’s Girlfriend’ by Type O Negative blaring through the speaker… Luci was calling. "Luci, I take it you noticed the match also?" he said. "You heard off Lauren yet today?" Legion was surprised he suddenly thought that Luci figured out the plan that him and Savant had been doing for the past few weeks but he suddenly realised he hadn’t heard off her for a few days now, last time they saw each other was a few days before Sin*Con… "Strangely no, I’m sure she’ll be all right, she’s probably trying to get in touch with you, maybe she’s decided to get an early flight?" Luci suddenly began to sob… " I’m just scared that she’s been noticed by an enemy and something’s happened…" What Luci said next surprised Legion to a great degree, the three words he was hoping weren’t said so soon. "I love her and not in the way I did during the business with Rook and Teresa, I just know something clicks with her that I have never felt…" Luci suddenly starts crying more hysterically. Legion’s eyes suddenly grew wide… "I’m sure she’s OK Luci, listen I’m just going to have to call you back. If I hear anything I’ll ring you OK?" Legion ends the call and in his mind he suddenly worries that everything he’s planned could be finished in one fell swoop and with the added twist of what Luci just said to him he wonders if he’s gone too far. There’s a song by The Cure that suddenly described the moment that Legion was experiencing… the stoicism he showed suddenly beginning to crack however unlike his actions at the PPV he couldn’t control it. "And there is nothing I can do When I realise with fright That the spiderman is having me for dinner tonight!" (Lullaby by The Cure)
