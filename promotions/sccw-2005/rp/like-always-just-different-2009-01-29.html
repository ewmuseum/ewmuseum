Like Always, Just Different

29 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 29th, 2009 Opportunity is missed by most people because it is dressed in overalls and looks like work.' - Thomas Edison - - - - - - - - - - THUD. Kelly Masters' left forearm blocked the incoming right from Levond Daniels. For fifteen minutes they'd been lions, both yearning to be the alpha male, neither being triumphant. Sweat dripped down their faces, blood from Masters' nose, and Levond's eye was swollen partially shut. Toe to toe in the old ring, in the old gym, identical to before. Hours and hours had been spent in the Greensboro gym, the Arrows Family Gym to be exact. Enough reminiscence and memories though, both these warriors simply desired for his counterpart to be on Dream Street. SWOOSH. 'Fuck, he kicks hard,' he thought to himself as Levond Daniels' powerful left roundhouse kick went sailing over his ducked head. The time for thought was concluded, action was demanded. Masters shot in, sweeping Levond's right leg with his right arm, just as he had done thousands and thousands of times. Amateur wrestling had always been Kelly's claim to fame, and in Massachusetts or hell, maybe the country, Kelly was the best. Levond should have known better, leaving his legs vulnerable. Kelly's submissions had broken more folks than waterboarding, and Levond knew that. Too late. His limb was soon his enemy, a heel hook applied, which expeditiously put him down onto the ring floor. Decision time, to fight or live another day? How about option one? Using his left leg, he aimed a moderately strong kick at Kelly's nose, which he saw coming. The 'thud' was much sicker than the blow, as Kelly simply lowered his head absorbing the hit. Kelly had been referred to many as a 'Bull', unwilling to relent and dangerously aggressive. He twisted the ankle further, to its breaking point. 'Alright, alright,' Levond laid back opting for option two, prompting Kelly to release the hook. Kelly grinned, taking a deep breath, 'You're getting tougher, D.' 'Fuck you bitch,' he groaned, pointing a finger at Kelly, 'I used to be on the right end of these fights,' He shook his head, a small smile escaping. 'We oughta do this more often, with Shawn's knee all banged up there's nobody to spar with.' 'Hey, atleast you'd have a shot against Mr. Simply Perfect, bum knee and all!' Kelly joked, shooting a glance over to the witnesses of his 'sparring' session. The aforementioned Shawn Arrows, FWO Survivor contestant Jessica Jenkins, Kelly's on and off again girlfriend Paige Allen, as well as the drug dealing duo of Petty and Lee, or maybe it was Lee and Petty (they'd love to argue for hours as to the order). Collectively, this group was known as the 'Greensboro Gang'. Or atleast to Kelly, the Virginia Beach, Virginia native they were. He had never meant to meet this faction, no sir. Life pushed Kelly towards collegiate wrestling, sure he was a troublemaker, but his talent was truly breathtaking. Iowa, Cornell, and Oklahoma State licked at their chops to recruit this kid. In the end, Oklahoma State had won out, the kid would be starting fall semester there. Funny thing about life, it can push you in one direction and rip you back just as fast. During the summer, post graduation, Kelly had been in an 'altercation' with his stepfather. No, no, not an abusive, alcoholic stepfather. Simply a man with an ego, a big dock workin' sonuvabitch. Problem was, Kelly had an ego of his own, which seems to be the trend among wrestlers. That portentous evening he had beaten his stepfather to within an inch of the promised land, only a tiny thread holding his world together. There were lawyers, judges, and potentially prison time. Charges were dropped for some reason or another. Perhaps because Kelly would be released, angry, looking for vengeance. Simply afraid was his stepfather, not uncommon, especially when somebody forces their will upon you in such a manner. But charges, or no charges this blackmark would not be undone. It meant no more Division I schools, no more scholarships, no more dream. It was Division I or bust for Kelly, no alternatives. So he packed up his old military, canvas shoulder bag, prepared to leave his aspirations and Virginia behind. With about three thousand dollars and his bike (Harley, not Schwinn) the young Kelly Masters traveled to Greensboro, where a wrestling trainer was looking for pupils. He trained, learned, and became quite talented. Steady girlfriend, promising career, but Kelly drifted away. He'd always been a free spirit, and apparently would be forever. So he was gone, his dreams behind him. He'd been through a lot on the road, away from Greensboro, but that for was a different time, a different place. He was back in Greensboro. Back to messin' around with the gang. Back to those gorgeous, sky blue eyes locked solely on him. Hmm, good to be back? - - - - - - - - - - CRACK. The explosion of billiards balls instantaneously filled Coliseum Billiards and disappeared just as punctually. Levond's eye was finally looking healthy as he chatted away with Jessica and Paige. Somewhere Lee and Petty were making fools of themselves with the opposite sex. Kelly had just opened up his billiards game with Shawn. He loved shootin' pool almost as much as ridin' his Harley, which he nearly liked as much as fighting. If ever there was a free spirit it was Kelly, it was both his best and worst attribute. 'Good ta have ya back in town, kid.' Kelly nodded, his gaze meandering towards Paige. A smirk crept across Shawn's face, 'Of course, that isn't muh sentiment alone.' He flipped his head in Kelly's ex-girlfriend's direction. Instantly, the free bird's face hardened, leaving the Arrows Family Gym trainer to only shake his head. 'Yer stubborn like a fuckin' mule Kelly. Ya spend all yer time fightin' ta be free, no strings attached, an' ya might end up that way. Women an' wrasslin', Kel.' 'Aren't we just a Dr. Phil now? Coulda fooled me with that mess'a hair.' 'Simply Perfect' could only smile, Kelly had always been on him about his perfectly spiked hair, and the witty asshole had really zinged him on this one. 'Yeah, yeah.' sputtered the elder, taking his jab. 'Speakin' a wrasslin', Ah hear ya ah headed ta Delaware. Back on tha ol' horse, ah we now?' Kelly remained silent, cue in hand. It was true, he was headed to Delaware in a few days. He'd signed on to wrestle a tour for Just Wrestling. For a smallish 'promotion', Just Wrestling was jam packed with stars, both current and past. An excellent opportunity for him, a chance for him to finally begin that wrestling career. It seemed everytime he arrived in Greensboro, the next week he was headed out by some force of nature or himself. God, he'd like to stay. Just look at her man, look how beautiful that girl is. That girl was Paige Allen, and yes she was gorgeous. Long, curly, fiery red hair. Mesmerizing sky blue eyes, the skin and smile of an angel. Things felt different with her this time, perhaps it'd work this go'round. Yeah, they certainly could, but (groan), there always was a but. But you need to go to Delaware, it's not even a choice. Handle your business there, come back, and figure out your affairs with Paige. Decisions, decisions. However, his decision making process would be interrupted. 'Dude, look at Kelly.' 'He's gone all zombie on us, Charles.' Charles, as in Charles Lee. 'Probably smoked so much, his brain like ... died.' 'Yeah, died.' 'Ahh dude, let's get out of here before he starts to smell.' 'Oh, gross dude.' The duo scampered off after their next unsuspecting prey, giving Kelly the ample chance to shake his head in disbelief. Shawn was speechless, too engrossed in the laughter from his bonehead friends. Moving past the random stupidity from Lee and Petty, Kelly's attention moved back to the pool game at hand. 'It's your shot old timer.' he chirped, winking toward his former trainer. From his leather jacket, hanging from a nearby chair, the ringtone of Breaking Benjamin's 'Blow Me Away' caught his attention. He pulled his Blackberry from his jacket, investigating the disturbance. Ahh, a text message. We need to talk. - Paige 'Yeah, I know we do,' Kelly muttered to himself, looking over to Paige. He tilted his head, motioning towards the door. She nodded in response, grabbing her coat, and exchanging a hug with Jess. 'Sorry Shawn, more pressin' matters it appears. Next time ... it's still your shot,' he turned away, slipping on his leather jacket. Paige leaned against the door, giving Shawn a smile and fingers only goodbye wave. Shawn tossed his cue onto the table, disturbing the remaining scattered balls. Scooping up his beer, he headed towards Jess and Levond, 'Nah kid, this time it's your shot.' Those were the last words Kelly caught as he accompanied Paige out of Coliseum Billiards that night. - - - - - - - - - - 'Well,' Paige smiled, her face flush, 'not exactly what I meant when I said we had to talk.' She let out a deep breath, running her fingers through her curly hair. In all honesty, she had planned on coming back to her apartment and having that much overdue conversation with Kelly, but after not seeing, touching, or kissing him in six months, things just, well, went in another direction. 'I know, I'm sorry Paige.' 'No, don't be, this was on me.' Kelly's head and shoulder leaned up against the bedroom door, his blue eyes distant. Certainly she viewed this as a discernible warning flag, staring her directly in the face and saying, 'Look at me! Look at me!'. Her hands grasped her moonlight blue covers, holding them tightly to her body as she slid upright in bed. 'What's the matter?' she probed, expressing noticeable concern. Still no answer, only a heavy sigh escaped his lips. The look of concern vanished, replaced by a trembling lip. 'So it's true, Jess mentioned that you might be headed for Delaware, and it's true, isn't it?' Her query was answered, simply with a nod. A singular tear rain down her cheek, her breath short. 'I can't keep doing this ya know, this back and forth relationship. You leaving, coming back, leaving again. It's too damn hard Kelly.' Kelly stood vertically, his arms folded across his chest, 'It's only a month long tour Paige, 'til the end of February, then I'm back.' 'You were just gone six months ... back less than a week, and you're already leaving.' 'Things are different this time, I need to do this.' She shook her head, 'Same story, different day. Like always.' 'Not like always babe, I'd even ask you to go with, but the timing is wrong,' he explained. 'Always the timing Kelly, there's always an excuse.' Kelly scoffed, tossing a hand in her direction, his frustration level far beyond its limits. He tossed on his clothes, grabbing his leather jacket off the door knob. He spun to leave, hesitated, and turned back. 'I do love you Paige Allen, always have, always will.' 'Sure Kelly, just run away. It's what you do.' He put on his jacket, heading down the hall leading away from the bedroom. He stopped, short of being engulfed by darkness. 'I'm not running from anything Paige, no more running. I'm headed straight for something, full head a' steam, and lets see if they can stop me.' - - - - - - - - - -
