Date: Temptation: West Virginia Disc One, Track Six: Pinocchio’s Story Its so crazy I got everything figured out But for some reason I can never find what real love is about No doubt Everything in the world figured out but I can never seem to find what love was about I The sun glistened as it rose slowly over the horizon and as a result the lake glowed from the newfound light. Craig sat on the edge of the lagoon and looked across it as his red eyes appeared glassy from a distance. Deep down, he abhorred his red eyes. People would glance at him and Craig could see the fear and horror in their eyes, but most importantly he could feel the pity coming from them. Most of civilization didn't look like him or have this condition, and it set them on edge. It was quite interesting for him to be focused on his eyes at this particular moment though when there were so many other thoughts on his mind. However, it was those eyes that might have the most impact upon his thoughts because of what they symbolize. Alexandra Pierce. The grisly details had never escaped him as he remembered every moment of the painful experience. A sickened burning sensation filled his eyes, the curling screams that would put the hair on your arms on end, and the sick laughter behind him as she watched and cackled with delight. It was the last day that he would ever care for her; the last day he would ever love that woman. To him though, on that particular day, the woman he had once fallen in love with had vanished. Deep down, Craig had wished for the longest time that he would never have to come face to face with Alexandra again, but he knew even then there would be no way for that to happen. It was written in the stars, destiny foretold it, and the two souls would forever be entangled together as they were now. Hours ticked away as he lost himself within his thoughts, the sadness of days gone by living on in his memory never to die. Craig knew the next step in his mind, but to actually execute everything would trouble him for the rest of his life. To move on to the next chapter in his life, to rid himself of the distractions so he could finally come to terms with his upcoming fate, his long foretold destiny, it would come with great pain and sorrow. Craig remembered a particular nightfall, Alexandra and him stared at the stars as they talked until the early hours of the following morning. The two of them had prophecies and destinies assigned to them far before they were even born. Somehow, fate had led them to this point and he just remembered feeling as if they could defeat these prophecies and live normal lives. There was one prophecy though that they couldn't buck; it was the one that would be their downfall. How had life come to this? When had he grown up and been burdened with this life, this world? Why couldn't it have fallen to someone else? The pain and the agony, the scars and the wounds, the blood and the death, they all rested on his hands. He just wanted a normal life, a real life, but it continued to evade him while laughing in his face at the same time; just like Alexandra did when he suggested that very notion. The notion of her loving him back had never crossed his mind in those days. However, Craig had hoped and prayed for at least the chance to live life outside of these ridiculous constraints. Craig had been so close to having a regular life; Alexandra ruined it all. Now she would have to pay that price. 'You've been out here for a while. Everything alright?' His eyes didn't even have to move from the beautiful lake for him to know who it was that approached him. The woman known as Mary-Lynn Mayweather simply sat down next to him and looked out at the breathtaking view. She had never been able to truly appreciate the new house that they lived in, but for the first time she could take it all in and in this very moment it was simply astounding. Craig cleared his throat before he could even respond to Mary-Lynn. 'Before the end of tonight, Mary-Lynn, there's going to be more blood on my hands. I will be responsible for their lives and their souls will live with me for the rest of my life. So, I would have to say that no, not everything is okay,' he told her solemnly and sarcastically. Mary-Lynn didn't have a response for that right away, instead she licked her lips, and continued to look out at the lake. 'Life sucks that way, I guess. I don’t know, it's not my world,' Mary-Lynn begins to say, as she tried her best to find the words that would comfort one of her closest friends. 'But, I know I wouldn't wish what you have upon anyone else. At the end of the day though, Craig, the only thing I can offer you is my support and my love, and the honest truth that you have to do what you think is best from this point forward.' Craig listened to what she had to say and slowly he nodded his head. He hated having to make the difficult decisions, but in the end, he managed to make them. Somehow he had been able to take the lives of people and live with their deaths on his conscience. Craig would have to find the strength to do it once again. As he looked out at the lake and felt the abundance of emotions rise in his chest, he had one request for Mary-Lynn. 'Could you do one other thing for me?' he asked her softly and she looked over at him. 'Could you sit here with me for a while, as my friend, until I'm ready to go back in?' She offered Craig a warm smile. 'Of course.' Those words comforted him more than she would ever know as he felt his heavy heart, broken and shattered, beat away inside of his chest for the first time in a long time.
