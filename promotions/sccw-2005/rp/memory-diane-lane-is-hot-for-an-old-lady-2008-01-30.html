Memory (Diane) Lane (is hot for an old lady.)

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 30th CH 1. Then. I couldn't stand the sight of him. Of course, I knew him as 'Darcy Crisis' -- he was my age then, and had all the pomp and arrogance that came with it. I was an even shittier person, using my tattoos and bright hair to hide the fact that I was nothing more than a scared little girl who needed a man to tell her what to do. I was being supported by my boyfriend at the time, who also happened to be my boss, and I reluctantly had to tolerate Darcy's presence in the crappy cable-TV federation said boyfriend ran. Both on-screen and off, Crisis and I were enemies. I guess it's more accurate to say that in a company that seedy, almost everybody was his enemy. Nobody could imagine the kind of things that went down backstage -- imagine your local indy fed, right down to the beers and drugs in the dressing rooms of the skating rinks they compete in, then add a little bit of money to pump up the equation. It was heaven for a teenaged version of me. I'd been picked up in New York while skipping out on an art school scholarship. What? It was my parents' fault for ever letting me go stateside, though they probably wouldn't have stopped me anyway. That's where I discovered that my love for punching things could be used to get me a little money in underground fighting circles, and this was especially good considering I'd blown through my college money on drugs. So much for Amy Campbell, almostkindasorta productive member of artistic society. My roomies hated me, and I eventually got whisked away by a fella promising to train me to make money doing what I really loved... no, not coke -- fighting. A few months later, I was youthful eyecandy for a few wrestling promoters who helped support my habits and drew me into their world with promises that I knew were lies. It didn't matter that they were full of shit, though -- they got me what I needed. My parents chose not to believe the things they heard when I moved back to Canada to wrestle for my latest suitor's promotion, instead holding onto the believe that their little girl was just going through an attention-seeking phase by doing that silly wrasslin' stuff. With Eddie, the owner of the federation where I first met Darcy 'Crisis', I had a warm bed, a good dealer and a lot of publicity thanks to the whole dynamic of 'bangin' the boss'. I didn't say it was fantastic, and I know what it made me. It hardly even bothered me that my bed was only warm because he'd had multiple women in it by the time I got home from the bars I'd fake my way into. Okay, okay... that's a lie. It pissed me off to no end. I had no confidence at that stage of life because I had nothing. I might be an idiot in my actions, but I'm not stupid -- I knew exactly what I was, and it didn't make me the least bit proud. I'd been walked all over my men in my teen years and, while I liked to think that I was a big, tough grrrrrrrrrl, I simply looked the other way while it happened to me again and again. I was allowing for this behavior, and it really hit me when I caught my boss/boyfriend at the time in the act. After mistakedly opening my hotel room door to see him doing things I'd been ignoring throughout the past couple of months, I decided to spend the rest of the night in the bar. I closed the door quietly and did the walk of shame all the way back to the elevator, wondering the entire time whether or not I should've gone back to that room with a knife and taken out Eddie right alongside whatever random ring rat I'd just seen him dry-humping. The bar that night was as shitty as my mood at the time. I was in a foreign town somewhere in Alberta for a show that night. It was freezing, and apparently their local dives didn't appreciate heating. Needless to say, I didn't appreciate the company I found... the place was full of seedy strangers that I can honestly admit now I was a little afraid of going near because I knew all about date rape and roofiecoladas at that point. In my mood, a familliar face was welcomed, even though it was his. Darcy hated me -- I wasn't the only one who knew exactly what I was. He thought I was a disgrace, and probably a festering pile of diseases to boot, but it didn't stop me from seeing the tiny dark spot of pity in his eyes when he saw me stumble in. I was already half-tanked, and he was working a glass of scotch that he'd later claim was one of many. That night in 1999, I thought my pathetic excuse for a life was over. I was prepared to slink back to Nova Scotia and admit that I was a fool from the age of 12. Like always, though, I managed to find a warm bed... but it was the last one I thought I'd find myself laying in. -------------------------------------------------------------------- January 27th, 2008 Today's the anniversary of the day that Darcy came back to me. I don't know why I'm sitting here, trying to write my feelings... and I really don't know why I'm forcing myself to look back on the thousands of bad times I had with him. It might be because I know all of my sad stories actually end happily. After I'd caught yet another boyfriend cheating on me, Darcy ended up being the biggest and eventually the best 'mistake' I'd ever made. -------------------------------------------------------------------- CH 2. Then + A Few Weeks. After that night, we both tried to forget about it. He dismissed it as a fling, I claimed it was a huge mistake and went crawling back to the boyfriend I thought I was done with. You have no idea how many times I checked into a clinic to make sure my blood stayed clean while I was with Eddie O -- I just couldn't afford to give up the free ride. Rehab was expensive, my reputation was at stake and a wannabe wrestling whore sure didn't make much money. Not to mention the fact that the federation was run like the mob, and I just so happened to be Tony Soprano's lady at the time. If I shamed him, I could almost guarantee that I wouldn't walk, much less wrestle ever again. He had too many roid-raging goonies that he was promising WWF contracts to. But like I said, I'm an idiot with my actions. Despite knowing the consequences, I turned on him and I tried to do it in style. In an unscripted and unfortunate turn of events, I actually bashed the faces of him and our 'friends' in with a chair in the middle of a wrestling ring, at the end of a pay-per-view. It was a moment for the books, where even the dumbest fans knew that something unusual had just happened... because that sure didn't look as smooth or calculated as everything else on the show. To top it off, Darcy Crisis was in the match -- it was him I'd accidentally been protecting when I finally snapped. I didn't realize it at the time, but he was a true professional. In an attempt to maintain the drama and to inject his name into the story that'd no doubt be tearing up internet forums everywhere on Monday morning, he grabbed and kissed me right there, right then, as a sort of 'thank you' for inadvertantly helping him out. I don't think he realized or wanted to believe exactly how unexpected my actions at that show were. Needless to say, I hoofed it after that. Once the medics checked on Eddie and his crew, I was screwed -- they'd have my hide. I think Darcy almost immediately knew that letting himself so much as touch me was also a grave mistake, and that's why he was headed for the parking complex at the same time I was. You don't kiss the boss' girl and get away with it, especially when she just royally fucked him over on live broadcast. Once again, I found myself as a single, scared and lost puppy in front of Darcy Crisis. I knowingly tailed him in the parking lot, nervously pulling my hood over my head to the point that it was starting to block my vision for the sake of disguise. That's when he turned around and said the first words I'd heard from him since I woke up in a hotel suite with him a month and some change before that; ''What the fuck was that? Are you crazy?'' ''Am I crazy? Yes, we established that.'' I'd said, ''Can we just get out of here?'' I'd assumed way too much about my safety, and he promptly put me in my place. ''Oh, you think you're coming with me? Do you know the size of the target you just put on BOTH our backs?'' ''You're the one who took it further.'' ''I gave you a peck so the fans would go home with a cute picture. Just because I took your easy ass back to my room a couple weeks ago doesn't make that any more meaningful than every other stilted, scripted part of this shit.'' he said, ''I know that Eddie didn't plan what you just did for ratings, but get the hell away from me if you think you're gonna find a soft place to land.'' I told him to go fuck himself, and I gave up on my life again in that moment. I knew if I took the car I'd rented with Eddie, he'd report it stolen -- so I started walking. I was too dizzy to run, but I was out of the complex and leaning against a brick wall next to the liquor store across the street by the time Darcy pulled out. I like to remember this part without the fact that I was sobbing, but it's hard to because's that's exactly what ended up getting the realization to hit him. He rolled down his window to see me at my weakest. I put on a good show every day of my life, but this was one of those rare moments of me not manipulating my way into a beneficial situation. I'd just screwed myself, and I knew it. He knew it, too. He also knew that he was seeing something much more than he ever had -- the flicker of what had drawn him to me that drunken night a few weeks before. ''Amy, are you the person I think you are?'' I think he knew I wasn't. ''I wish it was that simple.'' Both of us declined sharing a warm bed that night. We stayed up afraid, awake and talking the entire time. He found out everything he wanted to know about me, I found out his last name and I'm pretty sure Darcy Markson saved me by getting me out of town that night. -------------------------------------------------------------------- January 27th, 2008 The way he'd treated me up until the night that I finally broke free of Eddie and that entire company had made me lump Darcy in with the rest of the assholes I'd come across in life. It didn't take long for me to break through to what he really was, even though that quickly got fogged back up by another string of bad decisions. -------------------------------------------------------------------- CH 3. Then, A Few Weeks And A Few Months. He saved me, and a few months later he left me. I'd later find out that he thought he was helping me by disappearing like that -- he'd been injured in a cage match, his back nearly crippled and I don't think either of us wanted to deal with that fact. The way Darcy saw it, he was sparing us both a lot of pain. It's what he didn't know that hurt the most, though. Shortly after he walked out on me, I found out... yep, it's that old cliche -- I was 'expecting' in the worst way that a newly single girl could be. My solution to pregnancy was hardly civil, but I was gutless -- too scared to imagine myself having to take care of anything but myself, and too scared to 'take care of the problem' like some people's mothers would have suggested. So, I went my own way with a little too much cocaine, hoping it'd at least rid me of my swiftly growing issue, or kill me. Not my proudest or smartest moment, but Lane Stevens recently brought it to the surface... so I think I'll go back to trying to repress the memory. The most amazing part of it all is that I managed to survive for five years after Darcy took off. I'd even kicked drugs for the most part, and started establishing my career in a way that didn't involve having to give a good blowjob to an impartial boyfriend of the moment. I was in AWC when I saw him again. The staff had been discussing a 'new recruit', and I'd thought nothing of it. I thought I was behind Darcy Markson at that point -- he never left the back of my mind, but I made sure to keep him there. ... Well, until I realized who they'd just recruited. For two months, I avoided and resisted him. I hated him more than I could ever imagine hating a person for what he did to me. At least the other men who'd scorned me had been blatantly cheating and didn't give a damn -- Darcy had clearly cared about me, and he just left. He didn't do anything terrible to stab me in the back or make it easier for me to spend five years hating him. He just... left. And after the longest years of my life, he was back in front of me - just like that. He knew what he'd done to me, at least the parts that he'd been made aware of, and he knew to keep his distance if he didn't want to lose a testicle. But just like we'd been drawn together twice before that, I found myself speaking to him without that pang of anger in my voice. It didn't take liquor or a horrible turn of events to draw me to him this time. He wasn't my safety net and he wasn't my drunken fling -- he was the man I'd been trying so hard to loathe, but could never hate fully, no matter how hard I'd tried. The next thing I knew, this asshole had managed to get me into a wedding dress. -------------------------------------------------------------------- January 27th, 2008 We waited a long time, at least by our standards, before we took the next step. He'd come back knowing that he needed to right the wrongs, but I wasn't having any of it for quite a while. To this day, I can't stay mad at him even for the tiniest things. Through appearances from ex-girlfriends, to people like Lane Stevens doing their best to tear us apart, we've lasted. Even though being with Darcy has been more chaotic and painful over the years than any other relationship I could ever imagine being in, it's paid off with a hand of happiness tenfold. I can't imagine what it'd feel like if he was actually anything like 'other guys'. -------------------------------------------------------------------- CH 4. Now. Amy snapped out of her prance down memory lane after the gleeful tones of her cell phone sounded a few times. She was at her desk, hands on her laptop and head in the clouds when she picked up the phone. ''Hello?'' Soft sounds float into her ear, but they're not the least bit pleasant. She checked her caller ID. ''Katie, are you crying?'' As much as she couldn't imagine what it would feel like if the love of her life was anything like most men, she knew exactly what it might feel like if he ever cheated on her. Her world would fall apart. Kind of like Katie Malick's, if Jon Rhine was up to what Amy had suspected. -------------------------------------------------------------------- January 27th, 2008 Enough of my happy love life. I've been through enough, certainly enough to know that I don't want the friends I cherish to ever experience the same things. That's why I'll never forget saying what I did to Jonathan Rhine a couple of weeks ago: ''If you fuck things up with her, I'll make sure that your own mother can't recognize your face.'' The worst part is that I wasn't exaggerating. Not even a little bit. There are few things that rank lower than cheating on the scale with which morality is measured. I've experienced those few things - they're not nice by any means, of course - but being cheated on will hurt no matter how good, bad or ugly the spouse. If this ends up being the phone call I think it is, the Gateway Title will take a back seat when I see my friends on ESPN. I'm listening to Katie Malick as I write this, and I'm starting to hope Rhine's mother has a good, clear picture of his face for the sake of her own happy memories.
