Enter The Violence

4 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/04/2010 The screen is pitch black. All of a sudden the instrumental to 'Thug Luv' by Bone Thugs N' Harmony and 2Pac begins to blare out,shattering the lull of nothingness. Next, the low gruff, battle-hardened voice of a man carrying a slight northern accent can be heard following shortly behind the opening of the song. 'For years, people have tried to figga out 'The Violence'...' A clip of the wrestler, 'The Violence' Vince Jones, at different stages of his career flashes onto the screen parting the darkness. First he is seen slowly sauntering down the aisleway to the ring in his AWC days with a cocky smirk plastered on his goateed-face. Following along behind him to the ring is his lovely valet, Jasmine, The Crown Jewel of Wrestling. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) Ya know something', man? When you look at a thug like V mahfuckin' Jones you lookin' at one of the only bright spots in this fuckin' game! 'Nuff said! The fans in the audience shower him with their hatred as he just mocks them all and points a black, Louisville Slugger bat in their direction. ' ...They've claimed that they know what makes a man like V. Jones tick... '         Next, Vince Jones is seen standing in the middle of a soldout Bloodbath Wrestling Alliance ring, pacing back and forth while ranting and raving with a mic in his hand and his lovely valet, Jasmine 'The Crown Jewel of Wrestling' standing behind him looking as gorgeous as ever. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) Its obvious why all these bitch mades in the audience are here tonight!They here to see the man that keeps this whole damn wrestlin' league floatin'! Now when one of you punk asses in the back got a boat big enough to take on the seven mahfuckin' seas called 'The Violence' Vince Jones then holla back, holla back at cha boy! Vince Jones suddenly whirls around and points at the crowd as they errupt with a loud chorus of boos.   '...They've tried to figga out what goes through the head of V. Jones...'   A clip of Vince Jones sitting in the locker room with his head down after a loss is shown. A PRIME reporter approaches him and he immediately flares up in rage and runs the reporter out the locker room. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) You think this a game! This is not a fuckin' game! Vince Jones can can be seen storming down the hallway of an arena after a loss in Lights Out Wrestling. The scene switches to street at night where Vince Jones suddenly whirls around with a baseball bat and bashes it in shattering the lens and knocking it to the ground as the video feed goes to static. '...They've tried to understand the man known as 'The Violence' Vince Jones. The more they try the more they totally off key...' A reporter is seen standing in front of Vince Jones' apartment door. He opens it and immediately slams the door in the reporter's face. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) You think you know, V! Well, you know jack sh*t 'bout V and all that he been through! '...People see The Violence, the thug, the man that knows no bounds, the man that's willin' to to do whateva, whenever, to whoeva to get what's his in this damn game. They see the path of destruction he's left behind him throughout his career so far and they start thinking' that they got him all figga'd out...' A clip of Vince Jones in his 360WE days along with fellow Dynasty of Destruction members can be seen beating the hell out of a former member within an inch of his life in the middle of the ring. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) V. Jones and the Dynasty of Destruction are the only damn things in this game that matta! 'Nuff said! '..People see V. Jones, hear him speak, watch him operate and think they know him inside and out. Hell nah! V ain't like no damn picture book or some sh*t! You don't just flip him open, look, and everything you wanna know 'bout him is right there or nothin'. Ya know? There's more to V. Jones than that...' Vince Jones can now be seen walking down the halls of the backstage area of Core Wrestling show alone and in silence. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) This game ain't big enough for more than one supastar! The scene switches to a AWC wrestling ring where Vince Jones can be seen standing on the top rope gazing out at the crowd as if in a trance while the cameras flicker and flash and he is showered with the crowds hatred of him. 'There's a hell of a lot more to V than that. Cats see V and he be reachin' out for the money, the power, and the respect in this damn game and cats think that's all the man's about, gettin' his. ' A clip of Vince Jones taping a deodorant commercial is shown. Next, Vince Jones can be seen signing autographs with a smug expression on his face. Then, a clip of Vince Jones chilling in a Jacuzzi with a variety of beautiful women can be seen. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) The name of the game is M-P-R, money, power, and respect! You either got it all or you ain't got sh*t! ' ...Wrong! Let the people think what they wanna think 'bout V. Jones! When its all said and done and V's career is finally ova maybe, just maybe these cats'll realize what it is that makes V. Jones who he is inside and outside the ring...' A clip of Vince Jones can be seen as he turns his back on his agent, Reno Banks after a heated argument in cW. ' Maybe one day they'll realize what it is that keeps V's fire burnin' in this game...' A clip of Vince Jones can be seen as he is in the gym working out and completing successive bench press reps. The concentration, intensity, and determination in his eyes can be seen almost as clearly as the sweat that trickles down his face. ' Who knows? For the time bein' the money V gets, the power V receives, and the respect V claims is like that side dish to the main course and all. You enjoy that sh*t while you can, but soona or lata you gotta move on to the real deal, the real sh*t. ' Vince Jones can be seen sitting in a limo ride with an expression on his face that appears as though he is disinterested with all that he currently has. ' Has anyone that has eva stepped in the ring eva come out explainin' what makes V tick? No! Why? Cuz the mindset of The Violence can't be explained through simple words and expressions out the mouth.' A clip of Vince Jones in wHw is shown as he is standing in the hallway glaring into a camera mouthing the words... 'Prepare to enter my hell, my realm, and my game' don't tell nothin'. A clip of Vince Jones standing across the ring from Black Widow of cW is shown as as he mouths the words... Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) PREPARE TO ENTER MY HELL, MY REALM, AND MY GAME!!! 'Prepare to Enter 'The Violence', don't explain jack sh*t!' Even if the words... A clip of Vince Jones in wWw mouthing the following words to long-time enemy, Ravage, right before the beginning of their match against one another. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) You 'bout to get yo punk ass SILENCED!! '...you 'bout to get yo punk ass silenced' come out The Violence's mouth, that still don't mean sh*t. Nah. The mindset of The Violence can't be explained through words. Its explained through all the punk asses that have stepped up to the plate and have actually been silenced in and out that damn ring!'   Clip after clip after short clip are shown of countless opponents of Vince Jones from federations from his past are being dropped on the back of their heads by his inverted brainbuster DDT, The Silencer. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) V. Jones will put each and every mark ass, trick ass, b*tch in this mahf*ckin' game out of commission if he has to until these cats start realizin' what in the f*ck they dealin' with! ' Its explained through all the b*tches that have walked that aisle with they full health and left the ring, half crippled on a stretcha...' A clip is shown of Vince Jones standing in the ring of WWCW during his reign of dominance as a self-proclaimed career killer watching an opponent of his being carried out on a stretcher. A cold, hard stare is etched across his face as the EMTs carry the man out the ring. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) Let the body count continues to rise, one punk ass at a time! ' Its explained through all the cats that have overlooked the real threat V. Jones posed thinkin' he was nothin' but a street thug lookin' to get paid only to end up gettin' stunned by V! A clip of a cocky Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright is laughing at Vince's presence before him right before their epic battle in Vince's NYUFC days. Vince Jones Soundbyte: (echoing) If the b*tch gotta go, then the b*tch gotta roll the f*ck on! That's the way V. Jones sees it! And that's the way its goin' down for each and every last one of these small-time, pretendas!! Another clip is shown at the end of their battle as Vince Jones can be seen tossing Jack Wright's body from off the top of a catwalk that hangs over the dungeon-like arena in slow motion.. 'Its explained through all the b*tch mades that have had to claw their way to survival in a match with The One Man Dynasty in this game!' A clip of Vince Jones in his one-time battle Chryste of DWO is shown. She is on the mat writihing in pain as Vince Jones has her locked in an anklelock submission hold and refuses to let go. She can be seen clawing with all her might to reach the ring ropes. 'Its explained through all the cats that have eva had to look directly into the eyes and soul of a madman and realize that they no longa safe...' A clip of Vince Jones and his former friend and DoD stablemate , Jay Cash from 360WE is shown as the two stand in the ring and glare into one another's eyes with pure hatred for one another. 'And its explained through the blood of his victims that have been left lyin' on they backs starin' up at the ceilin' lights and bleedin' from ear to ear...'   A clip of Vince Jones staring down at his own brother, Keshawn Jones, who turned his back on him at one point in time is shown.He is laid out on the floor bleeding after Vince Jones attacked him in his home federation, WMW. Vince looks down at him in disgust and spits on him in rage.   'Add all that shit up and maybe you onto somethin'. Until then, the closest thing that the people'll eva be able to use to explain the mindset of The Violence is what happens when the day comes that V. Jones is standin' toe-to-toe with the last man or woman that sits in the way of The Violence and the championship title strap. When the belt sittin' around V's waist the only thing that the fans, each and every wannabe up down Sin City, and the whole world will be able to say is...' Nuff said! The screen fades to black. All of a sudden a message begins to fade onto the screen, word by word. PREPARE TO ENTER MY HELL, MY REALM, AND MY GAME!! The message fades out as another slowly materializes into view. ENTER THE VIOLENCE!! The words become embossed by flames and then slowly fade away to black.
