Date: 9-19-07 * * * * * Two Weeks Ago Michele Thornton was two days removed from her professional wrestling debut as a valet, providing escort service to her boyfriend of three weeks, Clinton Sage. She sat at her living room table, Indian style, dressed from head to toe in sweats. Across from her was Donna Mintz, best friend since the age of 6 when they met in girl scouts. Both women were flipping through gossip magazines and eating bon-bon’s, perhaps intending to enjoy the sweet candies before working out immediately afterwards. Donna looked up and watched Michele as she flipped through a magazine, contemplating for a minute. She popped another bon bon into her mouth before she just figured it didn’t matter, and blurted it out, "So…how is he?" Smiling before she pulled her gaze away from the magazine, Michele’s eyes were almost as bright as her smile. She stared across the small rectangular table for a moment, "Not the best I’ve had, but far, far from the worst." Donna snickered to herself, "I don’t think anyone is ever going to beat Benjamin, in your mind." "Give him time, Donna. We’re still working out the bumps. But I think in time, he’ll be number one with a bullet." "You really feel that strongly about this one? I mean, what, it’s only been four weeks since you actually met the guy?" Donna asked, mindlessly turning the page of the gossip magazine. Michele was bursting with excitement, repositioning herself a few times before leaning across the table towards Donna, trying to get closer for whatever reason women always need to get close to each other to reveal secrets, "I know! That’s what’s so crazy. Donna, I know I love Clinton. He’s just the most amazing person. And it’s nice that I’ve got a boyfriend that I can feel safe around…which is something Ben couldn’t provide." The best friends shared a silence glance, with Michele’s smile never relenting. Donna studied her lifelong friend for a few moments, nodding her head at the thought. She remembered how small Ben was physically, and now compared to Clinton…seemed almost childlike in stature. "He seems like an asshole," Donna started, trying to choose her words carefully, "from what I’ve seen on television, I mean." "He’s not like that when we’re at home." "…oh wow." Even Michele realized it as soon as she said it, "I can’t believe it. When we are at home?!? Donna, what’s happening to me?" Finally matching Michele’s smile with one of her own, Donna leaned in, "Because you’re in love, hun. It’s pinned across your face in a toothy smile and a gaze that seems only focused when he’s around. I’m actually starting to get jealous." Tipping herself backwards as she popped another bon bon into her mouth, sprawled out on the floor looking up as the ceiling fan spun endlessly, Michele Thornton smiled and repeated it to herself, just so she could hear it, "I’m in love." They were both closing in on being 30 years old, but you could have misplaced them for teenagers excited about their first boyfriends. Donna crawled around the table and sat beside Michele, propping her head up with her hand, elbow on the ground, "So, let me ask you something. I mean, I knew when I first fell in love with Chris, so when did it happen?" Looking down at Michele, who was still chewing the bon bon, Donna waited a moment and got no response. In fact, she noticed that Michele’s chewing had slowed slightly. "Come on, spill it. How and when did you fall in love with Mr. Clinton Sage, professional asshole but ‘at home’ charmer?" Donna pressed again. The fan continued to spin above them, and Michele’s gaze seemed to fixate on it. She didn’t blink, and continued to slowly chew the bon bon. Finally, after what seemed like an eternity of silence between them, Michele blinked her eyes three times and then looked up at Donna. "Well?" The smile was gone from Michele’s face, "I don’t know." Smirking and smiling, she playfully nudged Michele, "Come on, stop screwing around. Tell me. What’s so special about him? Was it the roses…because dear God, Chris won me with the roses and the song he wrote…" Michele’s eyes danced back and forth, watching the previous four weeks of her life in a split second. The glow seemed to leave her cheeks, and there wasn’t even the slightest hint of a grin. Sitting up and staring at her best friend, Michele Thornton appeared a bit worried. "…you really don’t know, do you?" She had no idea why she loved her boyfriend, or even when she first loved him. It was the most puzzling feeling Michele had ever encountered. * * * * * Last Week, Killzone 50 "I have done sick things still. I have helped Clinton Sage ruin Mr. Duluth’s sanity…" * * * * * Two Weeks Ago, War in Washington Smitty T. Duluth watched the monitors closely. In fact, so closely that he was actually holding one of the seven-by-seven televisions only a few inches in front of his face. FUSE staffers passed by, some casually saying hello while others seemed uninterested in Smitty at all. Wrestlers walked by, most of them preparing themselves for the FUSE Tower Match that was to follow the current match being wrestled in the arena. Clinton Sage versus Tsuyoshi Tanaka. Gripping the monitor tightly enough to turn coal into diamonds, Smitty watched with pinched breath every time the action managed to find it’s way near his daughter, Michele, who was Clinton Sage’s new valet. That bastard. The biggest thorn in Smitty’s side since signing a FUSE contract, not only dating his only daughter, but having her ACCOMPANY him to ringside?!? On the monitor, Tanaka was thrown sliding beneath the bottom rope, with Clinton Sage stalking close behind. Tanaka skirted across the canvas and plopped onto the ringside mat only two feet from Michele. "Get away from him!" Smitty said aloud, not caring what any passer by might wonder to whom he was barking orders at. Tanaka was scrawny and manipulative, and he would no doubt use any method to strike at Clinton Sage. Bursting with a sigh of relief, Michele Thornton casually walked around the corner of the ring, away from the man she apparently loved. Smitty closed his eyes and dropped his head backwards, looking towards the heavens. Please, God…if you can hear me, just let her get through this match without any problems. I’m not sure I’m ready to accept the fact that she is in love with that asshole, but just let her get through this match unhurt. Tanaka will likely be the only person on our roster to try and use her for… "DON’T YOU GO NEAR HER!" Smitty screamed, almost womanlike, into the monitor. Tanaka had turned his attention towards Michele from inside the ring, but only for a moment because Clinton Sage had apparently seen his advances and planted a boot right into the side of the bastard’s skull. A Clinton Sage boot had never looked so good. Ever. Someone brushed past Smitty, but he didn’t care. For only the second time in his entire FUSE career, Clinton Sage had pleased Smitty Duluth to no end. The first was winning the PTC Global Championship, and now the second, securing his daughters safety. The match continued on for minutes, until Sage managed to sink in the "Garrote" and send Tanaka packing. On the monitor, Smitty watched his little Shelly jump into the ring and wrap her arms around ‘The Seventh Sin’. There was love in her eyes. That’s when Smitty dropped the monitor in disgust, still confused as to what her little girl sees in such an evil soul. Smitty stood there for a moment, relieved and angered at the same time. It was a tug of war of emotion, pulling in one direction for a moment, but then forcefully being yanked in the other. Looking around, he saw the wrestlers preparing for the Tower Match that was only moments away. He needed to be in his office. The stripper poles would relax him. The leather chair. The Dancin’ to the Oldies CD’s. He needed to have something familiar, because the last few weeks had become something of a nightmare. Unfamiliarity was drowning his world. His little Shelley, all grown up. Michele. Not his Michele. When Smitty opened the door to his office, he immediately froze. Propped up against the back of his chair was another piece of cardboard, and he recognized the writing of The Scourge almost immediately: "I’M SORRY FOR YOUR LOSS." * * * * * Seven Weeks Ago Clinton Sage stepped out of the Marx home, feeling dirty from head to toe. Unnerved. He had only felt this way once before in his entire life, and it was in the presence of Harlan Raines. Raines had this ironclad stare that just haunted Clinton, and still does, managing to rattle his cage only slightly. Artur Marx had the same quality. Their meeting had started with very few words, but soon exploded into a conversation where Clinton wondered exactly how Artur had some information. Marx smiled, tipped his head a little, and always replied with a flat voice, "Methods of my own, Mr. Sage." Regardless, Clinton Sage had survived the visit with little more than jittery nerves. And now, clutched in his hand, was the address of someone that would cure his current problem. Dr. Lionel Ernst 667 Wright Street Bangor, Maine Looking back at the Marx house, which was no different on the outside from any that surrounded it, Clinton Sage mentally noted to himself that the next time he saw Lane, he would thank him for this suggestion. Amid the unnerving feeling that Marx radiated, he seemed willing to help Clinton. Who can deny a man in love, anyhow? * * * * * One Week Ago Michele Thornton stood before her vast bookshelf, a collection that she cherished almost as much as Clinton Sage himself, her eyes dancing over various titles. She couldn’t quite pick out what book she wanted to reread – a favorite hobby of hers, particularly because she always found something new in a book the second time through. Clinton sat on the couch, studying wrestling tapes. He knew he was better than 99.99% of the wrestles out there, especially this fucking Jonathan Rhine, but this was one part of the sport that was always underplayed. Preparation. Not with The Affliction. As Michele’s eyes wandered about her book collection, Clinton’s obsidian eyes soaked up everything he could about Jonathan Rhine. The tapes were of his previous matches, the Tower Match from War in Washington included, and Clinton was taking notes on a small rectangular notepad. Perfectly scripted, meticulously, were 10 word sentences per line. Any more words and Clinton thought the notes looked jumbled and thrown together, any less and they looked incomplete. 10 was the perfect number. 10 was also the number of wins that Jonathan Rhine had. Clinton noted this as well, although putting an asterisk next to the note and signifying it with a "WHO FUCKING CARES?" at the foot of the page. "Babe?" came from behind him, as he still watched the tapes. "Could you help me pick out a book? I’ve felt so indecisive lately, I just can’t put my finger on it." Pausing the tape and turning with a smile, Clinton Sage watched for a moment as Michele continued to search the shelves for something to read. He loved the way her waist tapered in and then how her thighs and ass bulged out slightly. She was athletic, and that was a turn on. She turned, and caught him looking at her ass. "The books, jackass." "Which one did you read last time?" asked the man who goes by ‘The Seventh Sin’. "King’s latest. Nothing too special, but I think I might dive into ‘The Manchurian Candidate’ again, just for old times sake. What do you think?" The tapes and note taking could wait. The way Michele looked in her jeans and sweater was just too much for Clinton Sage. He could feel himself bulging in his pants, and instead of suggesting a book title, he suggested something else entirely. "How about a cheesy romance novel in the bedroom?" he said with a smile, the predatory wolf-like characteristics completely absent when talking to Michele. She thought about it for a minute, never taking her gaze away from the spines of her collection. Perhaps searching for ‘The Manchurian Candidate’, "Not now, babe. Later tonight, I promise." "Don’t make me beg…" She chuckled to herself, "The mighty Clinton Sage, begging to get laid." "Would you sleep with me if I was an author?" "I’d jump you right now." Michele responded, laughing even harder. She enjoyed when they played these little games. Turning around and flashing a flirty smile, she continued, "I might even rip that expensive shirt of yours clean off of you…" "Oh really?" "Ah hah!" Michele exclaimed, finally finding the books he was looking for and pulling it off of the shelf. She turned towards Clinton, excited, and began walking over towards the couch. The flirty game had come to a clean and decisive end, and it angered Clinton. His fists balled, slightly crushing the notepad. "I am the author." Clinton said, as Michele plopped down next to him on the couch. He watched her register the comment, and when her head turned towards him, she seemed less interested in the book than she had only a moment prior. Her reply was soft and emotionless, "and I am the book." The tension left his fists when Clinton Sage saw it was working once again. He could feel his pants tighten in one specific area. He leaned in close to Michele and placed a gentle and loving kiss on her shoulder. "Do you like it when I kiss you here?" Her voice seemed slightly distant, "Do I?" He smiled up at her, "Beg me to kiss you there." "Please kiss me there." "Beg me like you mean it, I want to hear the yearning and desire in your voice." And she begged him, this time her voice filled with passion and desire. Clinton thought she was the most beautiful thing when she begged, and it aroused him more than anything had in recent memory. "Plead with me. Beg me to fuck you." "Fuck me, and fuck me hard." Oh, sweet, sweet release. * * * * * Six Weeks Ago Dr. Lionel Ernst was short and fat, with a head of thinning hair that allowed his scalp to shine through in places. Clinton noticed that his office was lit with softer tones, perhaps to dull this effect. Either way, he annoyed Clinton to the point of near murder. Lionel Ernst had a habit of running his tongue over his obviously capped teeth after nearly everything he said. Quick and darting, the pink tongue would appear and then coat the teeth with saliva, then disappear almost as quickly. A psychiatrist, but a very fucking annoying one. "How long has she been coming to visit you?" Clinton asked. This session was unlike any other Clinton Sage had ever heard of, as he was asking the questions and Dr. Ernst was providing all the answers. "Since Mr. Marx recommended her for my services three weeks ago." Ernst replied in his mousy voice, quiet and sharp. And then the tongue kept the metaphor alive, appearing and quickly disappearing once more, from left to right. "Marx? But I only went to see him last week…" "That’s something I wouldn’t put too much thought into, Mr. Sage." Clinton’s fists tightened once again in anticipation of the chubby bastards tongue appearing, but it didn’t, so he continued to ask questions, "And the program has been installed?" There it was, this time before he answered. Right to left, like a cresting wave crashing on the shore, "Absolutely positive. I’ve tested it for myself." "You what?" Patting the air with his chubby hands full of chubby fingers, Lionel Ernst could detect the animosity in Clinton Sage’s voice. Perhaps enough to keep that tongue of his from once again coating his grin, which didn’t make an appearance, "Don’t worry, Mr. Sage. It was a simple memory check. She passed with flying colors." Still troubled by the fact that he wasn’t going to be the only one to use this program, Clinton Sage relaxed only slightly. He couldn’t stand being in the room with this man, not for one more minute. "So if you’ll just tell me the key phrase, I’ll be on my way. And once I verify that it works, you’ll be sent your check." "I have no doubt it will work, so you can just give me the check right now—" Clinton Sage took a step forward and leaned down to Ernst’s level, "I have my doubts, asshole. Michele Thornton hates my guts, in fact, she hates wrestling. She’s not too fond of her father, so you’ll get the check when I can prove that none of that exists after tomorrow. Is that understood?" Clinton flicked his own tongue out, coating his teeth in a mocking fashion. He witnessed Ernst’s ego crawl back into it’s hole, as the psychiatrist once again smoothed his thinning hair back down to his scalp. "You simply need to say ‘I am the author’, and she’ll be unlocked. Immediately after that, she’ll reply with ‘and I am the book’ to signify that she’s ready to be programmed." "You make it sound so easy." "It is that easy, Mr. Sage." Ernst replied, pink tongued for only a split second as he realized what he was doing. His eyes shifted from side to side, nervously, "Any command you give her, she’ll obey. If you ask her a question, she’ll simply reply with a question. A little kink I haven’t been able to work out." He went over a list of things he could say to Michele Thornton the moment they met, and she was open for his programming. Her love was the first thing that came to mind. Her body was the second. He felt a swelling just thinking about her body. "What if things go…wrong? What if the program breaks?" No tongue, just narrow eyes above a slimy smile, "Well, there has been a few patients to experience ticks, here and there. But I’m certain that your little Michele won’t have any of those." "What makes you so certain?" "I’m not, really. But if things start to get a little weird, you can always just ask her to jump off of a bridge somewhere after composing a suicide note. It’ll all be legit, and it works." Lionel Ernst giggled to himself. The horrors this man must have done. It was no wonder he had an ego, despite his small stature and build. Ernst was once again filled with that powerful ego, only matched by the power he saw dancing across Clinton Sage’s black eyes. The doctor seemed to have figured out the power of God – an ability to control the human mind – and at a recommendation of Artur Marx, Lionel Ernst was now sharing some of that power with Clinton himself. * * * * *
