A Draven Story: Pt 1 'At The Blowhard'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today "Master of Puppets" had died down in the Blowhard Bar. Charlotte Ramone and Krissy Jamison are being mercilessly hissed off the stage by the drunks that were there. Jay Draven didn’t look at his tag team "partner" as he continued to drink the bottle of beer that the Bartender claimed was Corona. "You never did answer Krissy’s question." Edie said as she drank down from her bottle of ‘Corona’. "Don’t think that this is the place where I want to discuss it." "Mr. Draven…." "Ryan, the name is Jason Ryan." "Sorry, Mr. Ryan, but this is a bar. Therapy at the cost of a beer." "It would be great if the moron behind the counter would make it the right kind." Draven takes another drink from the bitter liquid that was in the bottle. "Anyways, I don’t want to bury you with my problems." "Mr. Ryan, what if I told you that dealing with my sister after all these years have made me an expert of different kinds of problems." "I’d say you haven’t come across a pro wrestler in his late thirties that has a history of drug addiction, three failed marriages, a tag team partner that he can’t really stand, and has made more enemies in this industry than friends." "It can’t be that bad." "I was in a stable when I was suspended for illegally obtaining pain killers. He hurt my wife, two of my best friends, and I responded by shattering his cousin’s ankle." "Ouch." "Yeah." Draven says as he finishes off the bottle and calls for another one. "I am loved in this business. I proved to be great at what I was intended to be, another meaningless drone. But when I got too big for my britches, some asshole was there to knock me back down." Edie takes a long look as Draven, noticing that there was pain in both his demeanor and his voice. "Why don’t you just quit?" "It’s not that easy. For as much pain, and as much suffering as this industry has given me, it’s all I’ve known for the better part of two decades." "Seems like it causes you more pain than you want to admit. I’m sure there are other things that you are good at." "Lemme think………nope, nothing." "You have to be kidding me? Personal trainer?" "Nope." "Teach at a wrestling school?" "I’ve only had one ‘student’ succeed in the business, not a great track record." "Real Estate? I hear a bunch of wrestlers become real estate agents." "Don’t have the patience for it." "Motivational Speaker!" Draven just takes a look at Edie with a look that, well, because of his sunglasses, we couldn’t tell what it was like. "You’ve got to be kidding me." "Don’t knock it until you try it." "Listen, you little piss ant. You will use the new cover sheets on the TPS reports or I’ll hit you with a Spinebuster." Draven chuckles a little bit as the bartender brings another brown bottle. Draven takes a swig from it. "See, I think you would make a very good motivational speaker." "Jason Ryan, Office Enforcer." Edie just shakes her head and laughs. "You know something, Jay. You’re OK when you get out of the arena. Definitely not like how my sister described you." "I’m surprised misogynistic wasn’t part of how she described me." "Actually, I think she’s glad you treat her like one of they guys, instead of shying away from getting physical with her because she’s a woman." The sound of a phone ringing is heard. Pulls a phone out of his jeans pocket. "Shit, I have to take this." Draven gets up and walks away as Edie looks over at him. Right about now, her sister would probably see the look in her eye and go "Ewwww, gross." Or something like that. It doesn’t take long for Draven to return. "I have to go. Apparently, my soon-to-be ex’s lawyer just made some rather ludicrous demands." Edie pulls out one of her business cards and hands it to Draven. "Well, Jay, if you ever need to talk, give me a call." "OK. Hey, barkeep, can I borrow a pen." The bartender hands Draven a pen and he jots something down on a napkin. "You too."
