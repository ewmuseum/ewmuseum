Absence Makes The Heart Grow Fonder

13 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th August They say that absence makes the heart grow fonder, but that isn’t always the way things are. For one thing, absence mostly just ends up in someone being forgotten, becoming a whisper on the wind of a forgotten memory. It’s ok when you leave of your own free will but occasionally it doesn’t work like that, something can be torn away from you, when you have absolutely no control over the events as they spiral out of control and your hopes and dreams are taken away from you. "Your Winner and New Livewire Champion…." The rest of the sentence hadn’t mattered to him, Frost had lost his title, just one week after claiming it. He had been on a one man war against ‘The Affliction as it stood at the time." He had taken apart everyone in front of him all that stood before him was Clinton Sage his ultimate goal was so nearly at full fruition. It wasn’t even the loss of the title that had affected him so much, it was the fact that he had shown a sign of weakness that he had been horrifically off of his game. The week before he had destroyed Lane Stevens, he had been a true warrior, now though he was nothing. He wasn’t going to instil fear into the likes of Clinton Sage with a loss, by being a paper champion. It would set back his assault weeks. Or at least that is what he thought, if that one loss would have proved to be the least of his problems he would have been thankful, but fate had a different card to play, there was something far more annoying. Sat alone in the locker room Ethan Frost tendered to his wounds, which for the most part were superficial, the majority of the pain was in his head, the self hatred and deprecation that had begun to encapsulate his soul once again. He had been down the road before, he commonly referred to it as ‘the dark place’ the part of his mind which led him to not even consider his actions, when in the dark place is Frost had a goal, he would follow it until the very end, regardless of the consequences. The only thing that had kept him grounded was Fred Fusion, but things with Fred had seemed different lately, well that technically wasn’t the case. Frost had just been quite standoffish, he had been trying to go it alone, the lone solider, one man army type of thing. The type of thing which always failed and backfired and sooner or later they learnt the lesson of how friends and colleagues saved everyone. For now though he simply couldn’t find Fred, when in all truthfulness, he really needed him. He needed to be told that everything was going to be ok, that he was going to bounce back and everything would all be fine in the end. Fred had his own problems though, more and more Fred had been being pushed into the main event, battles which although they weren’t above him, he just didn’t care about them enough. To Fred FUSE was just a joke, Fusion Wrestling was what he had lived for. Matches against the likes of Clinton Sage, simply weren’t a burning issue for him. Frost in his defence had tried to get across the importance of it all, maybe that was the problem, maybe he had actually got through to Fred, that’s why he wasn’t around as much? All Frost knew was that he was lonely and there is only one way to solve loneliness, the true mans way… Sat silently at the bar, the empty bottles began to stack up in front of him, from a management point of few it was poor bar service but to Frost it was a testament to how much he had drunk, a mini trophy in its own right. He had been here for a number of hours now, the bar was beginning to empty as were any thoughts of rational which had dared to try and enter Frost mind. As he glared across the room his mind was fully focused on one thing, with every man in the bar, he weighed up the potential targets, the threat level that they posed him, maybe it was the alcohol or just the male hormone kicking in, but he was in the mood for a fight, especially after his loss earlier, he needed to avenge himself, probe his self worth. "No-one, there’s no-one that would even be a challenge, not even you, ya bastard, what the hell do you think you’re looking at you son of a bitch." Frost slurred whilst swaying on his bar stool. That kind of attack would normally instigate a reaction of sorts, however when you are that drunk you are attacking yourself verbally, receiving any sort of reply is wishful thinking at best. Someone that had noticed though was the barman, who had been watching Frost drink his own body weight in alcohol. "Sorry there chief, you’ve had enough. Finish up and get out." The barman wasn’t an overly intimidating person, but there was a certain aggression in his tone, that had drawn Frost’s attention, who now was staring intently at the barman, who had adopted the cliché barman position, cleaning an empty glass with a towel while perusing his bar. "Who the hell do you think you’re talking to? Do you know who I am?" Frost tried to pull himself to his feet, but slipped off the barstool and slammed his chin into bar causing the rest f the bar to wince in pain, as they had been drawn to the heightening confrontation, you know the sort, the room goes quiet for all intensive purposes a spotlight may as well have been shined on them. Slowly Frost pulled himself up and using all of his strength managed to keep himself propped up using his arms, leaning across the bar. "No and mores important I don’t care." Calmly and slowly the barman had put down glass that he had been cleaning and threw the towel over his shoulder, so that it casually draped over and remained in place, it was a move that had been passed down the ages from barkeep to barkeep. He stepped forwards towards his side of the bar and shook his head at Frost. "To me you’re just another drunken no-one." "Why you sarcastic sun of a bitch." Frost’s fighting instincts were beginning to kick in, as they normally do when your stomach is filled with the devils drink and he swung wildly at the barman. The punch was slow, wild and ineffective and was around a meter off the mark, causing the barman to reach across the bar and push him back onto his stool. The lack of stability caused Frost to fall backward over the stool, ending up sprawled on the floor. Like a dog on a tiled floor, Frost struggled to get back to his feet and in the mean time the barman had casually reached under the bar and placed a baseball bat, calmly but with a large degree of intent "Sit down and we won’t have a problem." However the fire in Frost’s eyes were burning now, there was no stopping him "Problem, I’ll show you a fucking problem!" Another drunken lunge forwards that was prevented by the large bar in the way, by now though the barman’s patience had been put past breaking point as picked up the baseball bat and glared at Frost who was readying another attempted assault. "Don’t say I didn’t warn you." The first swing connected solidly with Frost’s ribs causing him to fold in two momentarily before the butt of the baseball bat connected with his chin sending him sprawling backwards into the embrace of darkness, forget a hangover in the morning, Frost would be searching for his teeth. Normality in the bar was restored; it became apparent by the totally normal reaction of rest of the bar. "Give me a hand to take this bum out back." Mumbled the barman as he stepped out from behind the bar and had grabbed Frost by the scruff of the neck and dragged him out the fire exit, two burly looking men flanked him as Frost’s body began to grind against the cobbles on the floor. The two burly men then took over and hoisted Frost up to his feet before throwing him into the alley behind the bar. There was not the slightest reaction from Frost his was dead to the world, slumped against the wall. "Let’s leave him with a little souvenir shall we?" The barman suggested, the burly men didn’t even bother responding and simply nodded as the barman reached inside his pocket and pulled out what appeared to be a needle filled with a clear liquid, grabbing Frost’s arm he swiftly injects the fluid into his throbbing veins, veins bursting from years of working out. A light murmur escaped from Frost’s lips before a swift blow to the side of the head sent him on his way again. "Call the police." … … It was around an hour later that the police arrived on the scene, following an anonymous tip off that a man had been beaten up behind the bar and was unconscious, he wasn’t seriously injured according to the report but the smell of alcohol that was flowing freely on him was a cause for concern. The tip off had suggested that a night in the cells just to clear his head might be appropriate. The two policeman had approached the body cautiously, in cases like this the drunks would often react violently through confusion, there was no major reaction from the crumpled figure of Frost had not moved much in the few hours other than slumping over to his side. It was now around three in the morning and the rain had just started to pour freely. "Looks like we’ve got a live one here, give me a hand we’ll slap him in the cells overnight he’s in no fit state to go home." It wasn’t anything that was said out of spite, it was simply the truth anyone slumped over in the gutter at three in the morning is not in any fit state to do anything as the chief moved towards the guy with the blue hair who was passed out the younger officer stared at the man who lay unconscious. A memory stirred this wasn’t just another drunken bum, something here wasn’t clicking, something was out of place. "Chief I know this guy." The officer had now dropped to one knee to get a clearer look at his face, there weren’t that many people with blue hair about but he had to make sure, as he was doing so the chief responded in the patronising way that he had done on numerous times before. "That’s a lie Jack, you don’t know anyone." "No seriously, I’ve seen him on the TV. He’s a wrestler. "Jack you’re a grown man why do you watch wrestling?" "The kids watch it" "You don’t have any kids." "None my wife knows about anyway, let’s just get him up." The two shared a slight chuckle before the chief leant down to try and hoist up Frost, the two men struggled to lift him up however as the did so, an object with a metal glint fell out of Frost’s jacket packet, landing squarely in the chiefs arm, causing him to drop Frost, who’s head ricocheted off the wall, causing the slightest inkling of blood to trickle out into a small puddle that was forming. The chief was staring with abject fear at his arm as the officer approached his commanding officer, a look of worry on his face as he did so, he had seen what had happened. He also knew the possible consequences. "Ouch son of a bitch." "What’s wrong chief?" "It’s a fucking needle…." … … The next few months were the longest of Frost’s life, he couldn’t remember what had happened not fully. All he had were the stories that he had heard, stories that had been developed overtime, exasperated and so defused from the truth that he was unable to dissect what had truly happened. He had attacked a police officer? He had injected himself with Heroin? He had then proceeded to attack the officer with the needle, endangering him with HIV or AIDS? He had been enforced to have numerous drugs tests, to check he was clean just to ensure the officers safety, despite his protests that he was a professional athlete where blood was a very likely eventuality, he was clean there would be no need to worry, that had just resulted in more ridicule and beatings by fellow inmates and police alike. For two months he had protested his innocence to no avail, his words fell on deaf ears, no-one cared, whoever said that stars in America didn’t have to do their time was lying, Paris Hilton must have just got lucky. The worst thing was though that in the full two months he was inside, the whole two months of losing his edge and his physique trying to fight the temptation to fall into the readily available drugs in the penal system. The thing that got to him the most was the solitude, he had never counted on people as being majorly important before, choosing to be a one man army, but now, being locked alone every night he realised that he couldn’t operate that way. He needed people around him, he needed his friends and he needed Fred Fusion. The most important thing though was that he needed FUSE. He had forgotten just how much it had meant to him, sure he had to fight off the forced of evil week in week out, but to him FUSE was a home and all it would take was a few weeks to get back into his mindset, to get the fitness back, to get his edge back and him and Fred would be there once again. The dynamic duo was set to return to FUSE wrestling. Or so he thought…
