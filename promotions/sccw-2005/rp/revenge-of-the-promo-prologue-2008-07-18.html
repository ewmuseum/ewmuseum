Revenge of the Promo - Prologue

18 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/18/08 Some people have better things to do in the heart of the summer than drive golf balls into the Charles River from the roof of their apartment. Some people are not King Blueberry. In fact, almost everybody is not King Blueberry, and thank God for that. Joining him on this sunny afternoon are the two poor bastards stuck in the life of indentured servitude that involves being Blueberry Dragon and Blueberry Mask 14, Blueberry Dragon and Blueberry Mask 14. In addition to the tee that has been setup on the roof, the cadre of fools have also brought with them a pair of Adirondack chairs, an inflatable wading pool, and a hibachi grill. King Blueberry placed a bright pink golf ball, most likely stolen from the nearest mini-golf course, and placed it gingerly on the tee. 'Alright kids, I'm aiming for the one in the rowboat. How many points?' 'How many people in the boat?' In a rare moment of tomfoolery, Blueberry Dragon was encouraging the king's sophomoric behavior. He stood from his seat in the wading pool, shaking the water off his Commander Riker swim trunks, 'If it's just one person then you only get one point.' 'Ass. What's the score again?' 'You've got 7, I'm at 0, and Lloyd has a score of minus-two golf clubs.' 'I thought we were a bit light in that department.' Blueberry Mask 14, known to his friends as Lloyd, had in fact been responsible for the untimely demise of two golf clubs. Specifically, one driver and one 5-iron had met their end due to his powerful swing and lack of any desire to actually grip the club. Lloyd had instead taken to reenacting scenes from Jaws 4: The Revenge in the wading pool. As King Blueberry drew back on his swing, a Battle-Action Mario Van Peebles action figure flew by his head. 'The hell...?' 'Bernice says Mario Van Peebles is not as tasty as Michael Caine.' Lloyd was now feverishly slamming his plastic shark into his Limited Edition Talking Michael Caine action figure. 'Jamaican food makes Bernice gassy.' Even under a mask, it's easy enough to tell when King Blueberry is confused. 'I thought the shark's name was Bruce. You know, for Spielberg's lawyer or something.' Lloyd interjected before King Blueberry could answer his thought. 'BERNICE IS A PRETTY PRINCESS!' 'Whoa! Easy there, big man,' King Blueberry backed-off, 'Trans-gender man-eating sharks: I'm sure it'll be the wave of the future.' King Blueberry refocused his attention on the immediate task at hand: hitting unsuspecting sailors with golf balls. Noticing the ball had slipped from the tee, he quickly replaced it and tightened his grip. He was about to swing when he caught a new target in his line of sight. 'DUDE! We've got a rowing team at 2 clicks! How many do I get if I can capsize the ship?' 'What's a click?' 'It's Army-speak for ‘Answer the damn question because they're about out of range SO HURRY THE HELL UP WITH THE POINTS ALREADY!'' 'Gah! Okay! Knock them over, and I'll let you name your prize. Not like you can do it anyway.' 'DEAL!' Thwuck! That's the sound a golf ball makes when it's hit from 5 stories up from street level in Cambridge, Massachusetts. As King Blueberry and Blueberry Dragon watched, the ball sailed through the air, looking as though it had a chance of hitting the intended target: the Harvard ladies' rowing team, out for a practice session. King Blueberry had dropped the golf club and was now doing his best Kirk Gibson impersonation, almost willing the flight path through jumping and frantic arm waving. 'Yes, yes...!' Dragon, having already agreed to let King Blueberry name his prize for connecting with the target - and naturally was dreading the possible outcomes, was not as excited. 'No, no...!' While the ball didn't actually hit any of the girls in the boat, it did land in the boat. This led to confusion, which led to one girl almost jumping out of her skin. This in turn led to an imbalance in the stability of the ship on the water, and before anyone could get a handle on the situation there were 8 girls cursing and trying to swim back to their boat. King Blueberry was laughing so hard that he'd fallen to the ground in hysterics, tears running from the corners of his mask. In the time it took for the king to recover from his hysterics Blueberry Dragon had run through a list of things he might now be signed on for. He was almost reluctant to ask the question that now filled his nerdy little heart with dread. 'So, um, what am I in for now?' The king made it back to his feet and dusted himself off. 'Well, this one is actually pretty simple. I want you to get in touch with Spidey, and get him to book us some studio space. We're going to go old school here, pal. We're going to do something that I haven't seen since we've been back.' 'And that is...?' 'We're going to film ourselves a promo.' Dragon breathed a sigh of relief. This is something that was most definitely in his realm of ability. Not only that, but it seemed on the surface to be easy enough to pull off. 'That's it? Really?' 'Well,' the king paused, 'no, not really.' 'What else then?' asked Dragon, the fear starting to creep back in. 'You're going to help me bring back the square bracket.'
