Jumping to your misconceptions

2 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec 2, 2007 There are always reactions to everything a person does and no matter what you do or why you do it some person will automatically think you are wrong. It doesn’t matter how much you plan or how well you prepare. The truth is that people by nature will jump to whatever conclusions they want. As Gideon is reading this E-Mail he has to shake his head. It seems that a woman has taken a rather enthusiastic dislike for Gideon’s recent segment on Uproar. It boggles the mind really. Has this woman seen TV or movies recently? Other then Halle Berry in latex was there ANY redeeming qualities in the abomination to all comic fans they called Catwoman. The lady has got valid points about the objectification of women as objects and sex toys for men. Sure you can say Pamela Anderson got her roles in Barb Wire and Baywatch for her verbal skills and mind. Unfortunately you would be overlooking the most obvious reason and that reason is 36DDD. Still it is a fact of life that everyday you see women in roles where they are not recognized for their minds. Why is it that women are objectified? Simply put large breasts are appealing to most males and even some like minded women. Gideon: Hmmm I should address these issues. Heaven knows she has a valid point but not too much an understanding of the situation. With a smile Gideon hits reply on the E-Mail and starts typing. [ Miss Montegra I have received your E-Mail and would like to discuss the points you brought up as well as some things you have overlooked. In the segment you have mentioned you mention about the ladies being dressed in as you put it "Bang me slut wear". You failed to take into consideration that I was wearing less being on the table in my boxers covered by a small towel. ] Gideon: It’s amazing that people can blindly complain yet overlook the big picture. Complaining about the objectification of women yet not saying anything about pro wrestling and it’s promotion of juiced up 7 ft tall body builders VS the average man that can wrestle yet is not what people accept as being a professional wrestler. [ The ladies in question wanted to be in the segment and I felt they could use the TV time to get a bit of mainstream recognition. Amber the woman I refered to as Ashley has done movies in the Adult Entertainment field. Like many women in the Adult Entertainment field is working her way towards mainstream TV and movie roles. She is a nice lady I met her 4 months back through a mutual acquaintance and we had dinner. As we talked I realized we had a lot in common. I likely never would have met her if she were employed at a Wal Mart so truthfully doors were opened. When I had the idea for the seg I called her up and asked her if she would like to be in the segment and explained what I was planning to do.] Gideon: I should thank Nina (Nina Hartley) for the introduction the next time we have lunch. [ I met Estelle at a meet and greet in the LA area. Her name actually is Estelle. When she asked for an autograph I was going through the motions. She asked about a wrestling move I did in a Pacific Pro Wrestling house show. She knew enough about wrestling to recognize that I converted it from what looked like a DVD to a bulldog. I admitted that Bird of Prey threw me off balance so I converted it to a Bulldog at the last minute. I was impressed that she caught that because I was certain that I managed to cover up the slip. She mentioned that she went to School with Laura and when they were in College she would talk wrestling all the time. Hmmm I’m a bit off track here. Anyway when I heard about her audition tape at Fontaine studios I asked her if she wanted to be in the segment. ] Gideon: Kind of surprised she never mentioned sending tapes around. I have to respect her not wanting to ask for favors. [ Obviously as you can tell I have actually met and talked to both women rather then just go to a web page and simply hire 2 women with monstrous racks. Yes the style of dress they were wearing was a tad suggestive and the dresses so short that they did very little to hide their undergarments. When I appear on TV I am "The Osaka Assassin" Gideon Fontaine billionaire, womanizer, and professional wrestler. I’d like to say that isn’t the real me but truthfully a lot of what you see is taken from many of my real life traits and tendencies. ] Gideon: Hmmmm I have to think on the differences a bit more. Hell the only real difference I think is dealing with egomaniacal prima donna nut jobs with delusions of grandeur and in wrestling there is a referee. [ The purpose of the skit was to show that Gideon Fontaine while polite can be a womanizer. The implied sexual activities in that segment were just that implied. After the segment I got dressed and we went out to dinner and did some dancing. They enjoyed the segment and found it amusing though I think Amber enjoyed the massage part a bit too much. Still I know she respects me as a person. ] Gideon: Boy am I glad there was no camera in the room after that segment. Man the things Amber wanted to do to me they wouldn’t even air on Showtime after midnight. It’s pretty bad when what she had in mind was more…… risque then some of the movies they put on. For the life of me I will never understand why in hell that they put on a porn movie dubbed in French with the sex scenes completely removed. I have to wonder if the concept of completely removing the plot of the porn movie and showing it on TV was the inspiration for the Catwoman movie. [ I am sorry you were offended by the segment. I hope this E-Mail helps you better understand what went into the segment instead of just what appeared to be the objectification of Estelle and Amber. Sincerely Gideon Fontaine. ] After Gideon hits send he gets up from his chair and leaves the office. His secretary Amanda turns towards him taking a minute from the report she is typing out. Amanda: Going out for a walk? Gideon: Yeah wrote a reply to an E-Mail about how I was how did she put it…… "A piece of shit womanizing bastard dressing up strippers in bang me slut wear." And other things I can’t say in front of a lady or most of my drinking buddies. She laughs as Gideon smirks. Amanda: Well I saw the segment Gideon. I wouldn’t have dressed like that in the privacy of my own home let alone appear like that in public. Gideon: Well I must admit the dresses were a tad short. Amanda: A tad short? She raises an eyebrow and looks at Gideon smiling. Gideon tries looking innocent then starts snickering. Gideon: I tried to keep a straight face but I can’t do it. When I asked for them to come in a nurse costume I never imagined that Estelle would bring those. Not that I’m complaining mind you it was a very nice view but I can’t take the blame there. I thought she was a nice girl but she definitely has a bit of a wild side. Amanda: Any messages for people calling? Gideon: Not really. Anything important page me. If not then I’ll deal with it when I get back. Amanda: Sure. The door is shut behind Gideon as he exits his office. Where he is going is something he doesn’t want Amanda or anyone else to know about. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** The streets near Fontaine Enterprises ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** As Gideon leaves the office he gets out his cell phone and starts dialing a number. After it rings several times he gets voice mail. Gideon is cursing a bit. He REALLY hates answering machines in any form. Gideon: Timo this is Gideon. We are in the Live Wire battle royal and I want to get together to do a bit of training and talk over strategy. There is mild cursing as Gideon hangs up. He is shaking his head. Gideon: Call forwarding on a goddamn cell phone. The purpose of a cell phone is to carry it around with you. If he was female at least he could justify having it for the vibrate feature. Hell I swear the last cell phone I had didn’t have a vibrate setting it had "Who needs a man"…… I should take up drinking when I let things like this annoy me. Surprisingly not many people pay much attention to Gideon walking down the street. There are stares as people recognize him from TV, sports, news, and movies but no one bothers him. There is a pizzeria down the block that has very good pizza. He orders a pizza and waits. He is a bit early for a meeting. He never mentioned it to Amanda. An older man comes into the restaurant with a briefcase. ???: I’m glad to see you are here Mr. Fontaine. Gideon: I took the liberty of ordering ahead of time. They have a very good Hawaiian pizza. ???: I’m afraid I won’t have time for any. I brought with me some documents on behalf of my client. The man opens the briefcase and passes Gideon a large envelope. Gideon smiles as he puts it inside his jacket. Gideon: I assume he found the information to his liking then. ???: Yes. I am assuming you will pass those documents on to Mr. Duluth. Gideon: Definitely. When he contacted me to catch up on old times I got to admit I was a bit surprised he would do this. Of course when I brought up the name of Tsuyoshi Tanaka he was a bit intrigued about the federation. Any federation that could get Neil out of retirement likely had him very interested. I’m assuming the details in this envelope are accurate. ???: As you well know there were certain legal issues between my client and Rage TV as well as legislation by some of the wrestlers of the now defunct New Era Wrestling promotion. In layman’s terms he pretty much told them how did he put it…… "to go fuck themselves". The laughter of Gideon can be heard in the pizzeria. Gideon: I would have loved to see the look on their faces when they realized that their juvenile stunts in NEW cost them the high 6 digit contracts they were so happy about when they were employed by the late Bryan Chase. These……… children caused the company paying them large sums of cash to be unable to book them on a show because of their "creative control" or because "they don’t want to work with someone". Their antics were why I stopped wrestling for a long time. ???: It’s a tragedy when something you worked so hard on dies. Gideon: And infuriating beyond belief. I would have loved to have a few of them in an alley with no witnesses. I’ve been in the wrestling business for years and I understand the bullshit and the egos involved. Even Neil and I were assholes. How they could have the gall to claim to love New Era Wrestling and want it to succeed yet did absolutely nothing to make it succeed. That offended me greatly. "I didn’t feel motivated to cut a promo" and "I didn’t want to do a segment". It is a good thing that Bryan Chase was dead and didn’t have to see first hand the "respect" and "dedication" these men showed his name and his company. He cleaned up their messes be it drugs, prostitutes, or property damage and they couldn’t even manage to take an hour or two from their "busy" schedules to pay the man respect he deserved for dealing with things that would have gotten them fired permanently from any other promotion. ???: Some wounds never heal. My client agrees and that is why he is making sure that none of those men involved in killing NEW will get a single penny. There are a few people he has tied up in the legal rights to their ring gimmicks and names. There isn’t much basis for the claims but anything that makes their day worse makes my client very happy. By the time the courts settle the issues they will be forgotten. A large man with an apron comes over to the table with a large pizza box and some paper plates and napkins. Gideon: Are you sure you don’t want any pizza? ???: Yes. Before I forget my client says good luck in the battle royal. Gideon: Timo and I are going to do our best to make sure luck isn’t the deciding factor. ???: Speaking of Mr. Bolamba. Do you foresee any complications with the plans? Gideon: None. The two men shake hands. Gideon goes over to the counter and pays for the pizza. Gideon: I hope Amanda is hungry it looks like we have extra. It is a short walk back to the office with the pizza. Gideon is smiling. The documents he has in the envelope should be what Smitty is looking for. The man he has been in contact is a professional all the way. The secrecy is necessary because Gideon is way too visible a figure to deal with directly without it appearing on half a dozen web sites. "Gideon Fontaine seen in pizzeria accepting a large envelope from Daniel Stienmetz of the Stienmetz and Forsburg law firm." The media had a story to report and they wasted no time in reporting the news. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Later that evening after Gideon has gone to sleep ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Even without any knowledge of who the man represented or what is in the envelope there are already 12 news stories out there. Despite having no real knowledge the Internet is buzzing. Nothing stirs up the Internet more then a good story. "Gideon Fontine dealing with lawyer representing Alice Cooper. Possibility of FUSE wrestling bringing in the rock legend in a possible wrestling role?" "Papers received by Gideon Fontaine at 1:57 PM this afternoon rumored to contain a possible contract from Dana White. Sources close to the UFC suggest that Gideon is one of the men who may be the surprise opponent to fight for the now vacant UFC heavy weight title against Tim Silvia." They fail to realize that a story should have facts. After all anytime a reporter writes they should strive for the truth. Without truth the story is just that a story. Anyone can write a story. If you don’t look for the facts all you are doing is……… Jumping to your misconceptions.
