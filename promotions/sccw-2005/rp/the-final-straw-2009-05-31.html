The Final Straw

31 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-2009 [Davenport, Iowa 1994 11:30pm] The beer can click-pops open. Randy takes a long pull on the Pabst Blue ribbon beverage. He lets out a satisfied sigh of pleasure. Glancing to Leon, he grins. "Now that’s a man’s beer, Leon. Let me tell you." Randy’s younger roommate looks at him with a look of humor in his eye, "Are you serious?" Leon takes a sip on his bottle of Labatt’s and throws his feet up on the couch. The two sit in silence watching the hockey game on the television. It’s the type of silence that is filled by television announcers and fans cheering. A knock comes to the door. "Who the fuck is that?" Randy asks looking at the noise. "It’s a little late for cold calls." Leon shrugs, setting his bottle down and standing up. His long blond hair falls down his shoulders as he walks to the peephole. Another knock comes with an impatient tone in its’ rhythm. Leon opens the door and finds himself face to face with the last person he expects to see making the racket. "Elise?" Leon says looking to Randy. She steps inside. "Come on in. We were just watching the-" Elise marches into the living room. Leon cocks an eyebrow as she sits next to Randy. She grabs his hand hurriedly and touches his face. The two roommates share another curious exchange of glances. "Randy…" she stops half way and turns, fighting back tears. "Elise." He takes a drink of his PBR. She looks back at him with a teary stare. "I love another man." Leon darts behind the corner trying not to eavesdrop. Randy processes the statement through swishing the beer around in his mouth. "Yeah?" he asks. "Who?" She keeps her gaze on him. Her blue eyes overflow, tears running down her face and off of her cheeks. She grips his hand tightly. "Leon," she says with poise. "Leon Christmas." "Is that right?" Randy’s eyes narrow. "Leon? You got anything to add?" Under the long blond locks Leon’s eyes slam in frustration and then he does the same with his head into the wall behind him. He takes a swig on his beer before turning the corner to see the judging eyes upon him. "Look Randy," he starts with a passive stance. "It’s been a few weeks since she told me. I know it sounds bad but… we never acted on it. I couldn’t do that to a brother. Not you, man." Randy takes another long drink off his beer. He rattles the beer around and crushes it with his hand. Elise locks eyes with her boyfriend with sorrow under every intention. "Is that right, Elise? You two never get down to business?" "Randy!" she snaps like a lady. "Please. I would never." "Why not, Leon?" Randy says before click-popping another beer open. "She ain’t pretty enough for ya?" Leon shakes his head with a sarcastic smile. "Back off. I’m being honest with you. Don’t get yourself into a tiff… or hurt… it’s bridge under the water." "Do you love her?" Randy says, finally. Leon looks at Elise. She locks her gaze to his feet, looking at him from the side view. Slowly, her eyes drift up to his. Randy watches on in disgust. "Yeah, Randy, I do." […2 hours later…] "Five years, man!" Randy shouts as another thud comes from Leon’s head hitting the wall. "My girlfriend for five years and you pull this!? I was gonna ask her to marry me you fucking son of a bitch!" Randy releases with his right hand and drills Leon in the stomach, dropping the big blond man to the ground. A hard right shoe hits him in the face which knocks him to his back like a wounded turtle. Leon locks eyes with his attacker. "Randy!" he cries out trying to catch the leg. "Stop it, man! It just… HAPPENED! She’s always here, you’re not, and she is really nice. She came onto me!" "So she’s a whore!?" the angry brute says, dropping his knee into Leon’s ribs. Randy punches Leon in the face, twice, and picks him up by the shirt. He gets inches within his face and wraps his hand around Leon’s throat. "You can have that WHORE!" Leon’s eyes grow in rage. He shoots a free hand into Randy’s nose and then launches the man across the room. Leon jumps on his roommate instinctively with a top mount and drives tow fists into his face. Pulling off, Leon looks down at his bloody friend. "I told you before," he steps over Randy. Leon sits on the couch and looks around the demolished room. "I wouldn’t do that to you. I haven’t. I won’t." Randy stands up drunkenly and staggers into the wall. He touches his face, looking at the blood, and smiling. He looks up at Leon and through a smile, points a menacing finger in his direction. "You’re a fuckin’ dead man, Leon. You never shoulda fucked around with her! OR ME!" The big man turns and storms through the entryway. He yanks the door open, stepping on an already broken picture frame of Elise and himself, and then storms off into the night. Leon sits in the couch and runs the clean hand through his hair. […2 hours later…] Leon pulls the keys from his pocket. He stares at them, not really sure if he should leave or not. He slides the key in and locks the door behind him. He hops into his white Jeep Wrangler and fires it up. Friar stops and holds the steering wheel. Two hours ago Randy left in a fit of rage. His temper was flaring and things were getting hostile. Leon still felt sorry for punching his best friend in the face but when it came down to it – he had no choice. Backing out of the driveway, Leon speeds out into the night in search of his best friend. […2 blocks from home…] Leon spots a figure staggering in the night. He pulls the Jeep up to the curb, turns it off, and hops out to talk, face to face, with Randy. He approaches wearily, watching him drift from side to side in a stop-go rhythm. "Randy?" Randy locks eyes with Leon and slowly comes to a halt. Leon draws a smile over his big mug and extends a sigh of relief. "Holy shit, pal," the blond says gladly. "I’ve been lookin’ for you for hours. Look, brother, I am sorry about…" A soft groan comes from his buddy which cuts Leon off. He looks on in confusion to see in the moonlight that the white t-shirt Randy is wearing is not the bright color it should be. The color is red – blood red. Leon clutches his friend just as he drops to one knee. Randy grins through his half-dead gaze. "Hey buddy." The two fall to the ground under the bluish spotlight that the Moon casts from above. Leon holds his friend’s head, looking around to notice that they’re in the middle of nowhere. "What the Hell happened?" Leon asks his bloody friend. Randy winces in pain but manages to move his head enough to see his friend and the look of concern on his face. "Some mugger…stole my fuckin’ twelve-pack, man…" Randy coughs a bit. "He wanted all my cash, too. Told him to fuck himself…" He coughs amidst some chuckling. "Then the fuckin’ prick stabbed me…" Leon presses down on the wound but knows nothing else to do. If he leaves he may bleed to death. If he stays, there will be no telling how long until help arrives. Randy laughs a bit more. "Look, bro," Randy begins after clearing the blood out of his throat. "It’s bad, right?" Leon can’t respond. The look on his face is enough for Randy. "Then I’m sorry." Leon looks at Randy who has the look of a man on his death bed in his eye. "Don’t worry, Randy," Leon says dismissively. "I never meant anything by it. Honest." "I’m sorry, Leo," he starts after coughing up more blood. "For losing it back there, for cursin’, for generally bein’ an asshole. I was a jerk to you, Elise, everybody. I’m sorry, bro. I really am. I wish you and Elise the best in every… everyth…" "Randy," Leon barks. "Hey! Stick with me, man." The mid-length brown hair falls in Randy’s face as his head and jaw drop into his chest. Leon looks around. "Please! Somebody help me. My friend has been stabbed!" The plea for help echoes off the insulated houses of the neighborhood. Leon jumps to his feet and runs to his car. He runs past it, into the road and right into the front door of the house across from the clearing with his friend dying in it. He storms through the house and is met by a big man and his two large sons behind him. They all stand to their feet as Leon stops in his tracks to spot a phone – right behind them. "I don’t have time to explain… I need to use your phone." "Don’t worry fella," the older, bigger man points to the phone. "I heard ya from the street. We phoned it into the cops. They’re well on their way." Leon runs back out the front door, not thanking the man just nodding. He storms out the front door and into the clearing again, to find his friend, lifeless and cold. Leon just bows his head into his dead friend’s chest. […LAS VEGAS - PRESENT DAY…] Leon steps out of the Airport Shuttle and the very first thing he sees is a man wearing a red vest. "Good day, Mr. Friar. Big match this weekend." "Thank you," says Friar glancing to the name tag, "Franco." He sees his hand and slips the young man a five-dollar bill. "Now get me some ice-cold Pabst Blue Ribbon."
