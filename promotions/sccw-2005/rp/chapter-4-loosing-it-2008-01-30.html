Chapter 4: Loosing it

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-30-08 The dark thunder clouds flashed with electricity. Outside the relative warmth and dryness of the car in which Cyrus sat, the city was covered in water. It had rained off and on for the past three days, sheets of driving rain that easily penetrated the skin soaking straight to the bone in no time. Cyrus blue eyes stared out of the window as the city rolled by in a bleak, gray haze. The water running down the window giving the city a distorted sort of view as the buildings seem to roll by like broken segments of there former grandeur. Cyrus sighed fogging up the window. 'god, I hate the rain.' Cyrus commented. Butch just shrugged, 'Weather is weather, weather you like it or not.' Butch said with a concealed grin. Cyrus rolled his eyes, 'Are we trying to be philosophical today?' Cyrus asked. 'No, just making conversation, I guess.' Butch said as he continued to drive. It had been a slow few weeks for Butch and Cyrus. Not much to do, having to stay low for a little while due to Cyrus's beating of the bus driver Earl Stanely. As of late it had seemed to Butch that Cyrus's'' out bursts of violence had become increasingly more common. Over the past few months Cyrus had quickly evolved from a person who could barely stand the sight of blood, to a monster who seemed to find no greater pleasure then preying on the innocent and helpless. Cyrus was heartless at best, an unfavorable tone, a rude word, or even a lingering side long glance would send Cyrus into a violent tirade inhumanely brutalizing the person who caused the offense. Fingers seemed to be Cyrus's' point of most interest, the gleam that came into his eyes as he heard the snap or plop of a finger being bent to its breaking point then finally giving way to so great a force, the absolute elation in Cyrus's' manner when he severed a finger from the hand. It was all very quickly becoming a vulgar display of power in which Butch would have to put an end to before Cyrus lost his mind. The car slowly rambled on down the road to there destination. 'Where exactly are we headed, anyways?' Cyrus asked. 'Were just taking care of another customer for Lou. Just leaving a warning with this guy that he needs to pay up pretty soon, else well have to come back.' Butch explained. 'Oh.' Was the only response Cyrus gave. The past few months for Cyrus had been an awakening for him,a euphoric experience, to the level of which he could never explain. He had felt a change in him self as of late, and truth be told, he wasn't exactly sure how he felt about it. He was constantly angry, always ready to fight at the drop of a hat. Before, Cyrus had been a scared boy afraid of the things he could do to a person, now however, just the thought that he might get a chance to hurt someone made him start to tingle. He now realized what it was like to know power, to hold someones fate in the palm of your hand.Cyrus could never describe the rush he received hearing someone scream out to him for mercy. Just the thought of the suffering he had the ability to inflict upon another human being sent a shiver down his spine. Even through all the thrills and pleasures these acts gave him, he still remembered how it felt to him to have his brother taken from him so effortlessly. Cyrus had to wonder if he was becoming so much like the man he despised, the man who took his brother from him. Cyrus's' mind faltered a step, maybe they weren't so different after all, maybe they were becoming increasingly alike with every scream Cyrus heard, or with each drop of blood that Cyrus's' split. Ultimately becoming the same type of person, a heartless predator who prayed on the innocent and helpless. Could it be.......? The car came to stop in front of a two story house in the nicer part of town. Butch looked over at Cyrus who was staring out the window, lost in some deep thought. Butch trying to get Cyrus's' attention snapped his fingers in front of Cyrus's' face a few times. Cyrus came to with a start, shaking his head clear of what ever thoughts had possessed his mind. Cyrus looked at Butch who merely motioned his head toward the house. Both men exited the car and quickly mounted the steps to the front of the house. Standing on the porch out of the rain they both looked at the screen door for a moment before Butch reached his hand out and knocked. They waited a few minutes before the door was opened by a little girl. She could not have been more then seven years old, her raven black hair hung in curls around her rosey cheeks. He eyes were blue as sapphires, and she was dressed in a a pair of yellow pajamas. She stood looking at the two men with a look of uncertainty. Cyrus stepped forward and knelt down in front of the little girl. 'Hi there, is your daddy home?' Cyrus asked. 'No, popa isn't here right now.' the little girl said. 'Oh, well thats okay, can you give you daddy a message for me?' Cyrus asked as he reached into his back pocket and began pulling out his pliers. Butch stepped in between Cyrus and the little girl, 'Here just give your dad this note.' Butch said as he handed the girl a folded up piece of paper. 'Now you run along inside now, its to cold for you to be out here. We don't want you catching a cold now do we?' Butch said as he ushered the girl back inside her house. When the door was closed Butch wheeled around and faced Cyrus, grabbing him by his jacket and hauling him to his feet. 'A girl? A little girl? What's wrong with you!?' Butch screamed at Cyrus. Butch threw Cyrus off the porch toward the car. Cyrus cleared the steps, hit the ground hard and rolled, coming up on his feet, left hand balled and the right hand still clutching his pliers. The lightning flashed, illuminating Cyrus face and showing the unchecked rage burning behind his eyes. Butch looked at Cyrus and cocked his head to the side. 'Oh, whats this boy? You wanting a fight?' Butch asked as he unbuttoned the cuffs of his shirt. Butch removed his shirt and started the decent down the steps. The rain running down his bare, scared chest, the muscles of his arms and shoulders bulging, no longer hidden by the long sleeve shirts he was accustomed to wearing. Reaching the ground he faced Cyrus ready for the attack that was soon to come. Cyrus wasted no time, he took a quick step as he slashed out with his right hand, Butch dodged, Cyrus slashed backhand, Butch dodged again, again Cyrus lashed out trying to connect with the bigger mans face. But again Cyrus hit nothing but air, pain exploded in his stomach, the wind rushing from his chest, his lungs on fire. Cyrus doubled over from the well timed blow. Butch reached out with his right hand and grabbed Cyrus by the wrist applying more and more pressure until Cyrus dropped the pliers. Taking his left hand and putting it into Cyrus's arm pit he then spun once and slung Cyrus crashing into the porch steps. Butch reached down and grabbed the pliers from off the ground and put them in to his back pocket next to his own pair of pliers. Cyrus was just getting his bearings back when the powerful hands of Butch seized him by the front of the jacket and threw him on to the hood of the car. Cyrus quickly rolled off the hood then charged at Butch, his shoulder dropped, intending to run through the bigger man. Butch was to quick for that though. As Cyrus reached the bigger man Butch dropped to the ground on his back, and put his feet in Cyrus's' stomach and propelled the younger man ten feet crashing into the mud. Cyrus was back up as Butch began to advance again. Cyrus waited until Butch was in range then swung his right fist, nailing Butch full on in the side of the face. Butch's' head snapped sideways. Slowly Butch turned back toward Cyrus, an evil grin playing across his lips. 'That all you got boy?' Butch asked menacingly. He reached out, grabbing Cyrus by the front of his jacket again, yanking him forward as he slammed his forehead into Cyrus's' with a thundering head but. Cyrus' vision began to become dark around the edges, then it quickly enveloped his whole world. Then all went black. As Cyrus lay unconscious the past cam flooding back to him, as he remembered the events of the night that his brother died, every detail so crisp so clear. He could see the events unfolding around him, but he was still helpless to stop them this time just as he was the night it happened. When the replay of the events got to the part where the gun went off, just as the shot was fired Cyrus woke up with a start. He was covered in sweat and was laying on his bed back at the cat house. He looked around and noticed Butch sitting a chair in the middle of the room. 'Looks like I hit you harder then I thought.' Butch said. Cyrus sat up and stared at Butch. 'You know Cyrus, you have been wondering why I listen to the opera, and why I read the books I do. Well, I'll tell you. You know anything about butchers?' Butch asked. Cyrus shook his head no as his only response. 'Well, when they kill a cow, they have a pen that the cow is herded into and there is a person standing above the pen with a sledge hammer, and as the cow is in position he swings the hammer down between his legs hitting the cow in the head and killing it. You see Cyrus, people in that job are in rotations, they only kill the cows a few weeks at a time, then they get moved to the next area for a few weeks then another and another unlit a month or so has passed, then there back on the cow killing slot again. You know why they do that?' Butch again questioned Cyrus. Cyrus only shook his head no trying to figure out where this was all going. 'Well you see, if a man does things of that nature too much, for too long, it starts to effect him, messes with his mind, makes him go crazy. Thats why they have the rotations. And in a round about way Cyrus, that is why I do the things I do to relax and take my mind off of my 'job' because brutality effects the human mind Cyrus. Its easy to get blood lust in our business, and today my friend you almost stepped off the deep end. A child Cyrus, think about it man.' Butch pleaded with Cyrus. Cyrus sat thinking about the things Butch was telling him. 'Cyrus, your becoming no better then the man you hate so much, in fact if you keep on down this road, you'll end up worse then the man that killed your brother.' Butch barely got the words out before Cyrus was up on his feet boring a hole straight through him. Butch jumped to his feet faster then a man his size should have been able to. 'You sure you want to go for round two boy?' Butch asked as he raised his right hand two fingers extended. Cyrus stood for a long moment staring straight through Butch, then with a huff he turned his head and gazed down at the floor. 'Good, I didn't think so.' Came Butches response. 'Now sit back down.' Butch pointed to the bed. Cyrus reluctantly sat down on the edge of the bed. Butch reached below the chair he had been sitting on and pulled a book off the floor then tossed it on the night stand next the Cyrus's' bed. 'Read, take up painting, poetry, something, anything to get your mind away from the things we have to do. If for nothing more then your own sanity.' Butch said as he left the room, closing the door behind him. Cyrus laid down on the bed and looked over at the white hard back book that now laid on his night stand, with a sigh Cyrus picked it up and looked at the title. 'A Tale of Two Cities' written by Charles Dickens. With a sigh Cyrus opened the book and began to read. 'It was the best of times it was the wost of times, it was the age of wisdom, it was the age of foolishness............'
