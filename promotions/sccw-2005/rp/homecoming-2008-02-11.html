Homecoming

11 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/11/2008 STRESS By definition life IS stress. How we handle that stress is as diverse as the problems that we all face. Some people throw themselves into their work, ironic usually that is the real stress they are running from. Others go to the gym and workout or take up a sport that they will only be average at best at. Some even take up hobbies like building model cars or ships…or the ever intense stamp collecting or bird watching. Still others need something more. To really get to the core of their issues they need to take a step back and examine things. This is more than just looking at yourself in mirror and realizing what needs to be done. Sometimes you have to go back to the place where it all began. To get in touch with your past, remember where you came from, to really find the answers that you seek. ***The scene opens up high above the planet, cruising above the clouds at about 35,000 feet. After a few moments the camera begins a slow progression back toward the ground. Gliding quietly we emerge from the clouds and we see a landscape with a smattering of lights. As we continue our downward spiral the smattering of lights become clusters, and even closer we can begin to make out structures. Even closer and those structures become more defined and we realize that the city that is coming into focus is none other than sin city….Las Vegas. Soon we are on ground level moving along the world famous strip. The lights that only moments before were one are revealed to be millions and millions of flashing bulbs, and neon. Weaving in and out of taxicabs and rental cars the camera seems to zero in on a mysterious figure riding down the strip on a motorcycle. The man is shrouded in black, from the locks of his jet-black hair to the leather boots upon his feet. The bright lights of the strip reflected in the sunglasses that hide his eyes. The Harley Davidson motorcycle roars through a red light and makes a sharp left turn. Only a few miles after that turn we find ourselves in a much darker part of Las Vegas…a side that you won’t find in the travel brochures. Back on the strip everything is fun, a happy go lucky a place where people can forget about everything else and maybe if they are fortunate enough to maybe realize a dream or two. To the people that are frequenting this darker part of Vegas, this place is anything but fun. Crime, Drugs, and prostitution have taken over….not that the strip doesn’t have these things it’s just that on the strip everything is about high class. There is nothing high class about the pushers hanging out on the corner, or the strung out street walkers looking for their next John so they can get their next fix. Along the way we see several run down homes, apartments and motels. The man on the motorcycle brings the iron horse to a stop in the parking lot of one of these no name motels. He takes a deep breath and dismounts the bike, he heads right past the minimum wage earning night clerk behind the bulletproof glass, who is paying more attention to the porno on his 13"TV than his surroundings. The man quickly makes his way up a set of rusted iron stairs to the second floor. A man on a mission he marches to the end of the aisle of rooms and pauses outside the weathered door marked with a 13. Inside he hears voices. Switch to the interior of the room. Inside we see one of those strung out street walkers half naked and on her knees while a middle-aged man is buckling back up his pressed dress slacks.*** Hooker: I did my job…now pay me so I can get back to work. Man: Shut up whore… ***The man growls as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a wad of crumpled bills and scatters them across the dirty green carpet. The hooker quickly snatches up the money and even though she doesn’t have more than a junior high education she realizes that the amount of money that she was just given was not the agreed upon price for her services*** Hooker: Look you son of a bitch…this ain’t right! I said $35 for the job. Where in the fuck is the rest of my damn money! Man: Look slut be lucky you got that…you weren’t even all that good. Besides what are you going to do…call the cops? ***Pissed off that she doesn’t have enough to feed her habit she lunges at the man. He turns around his shirt still open and catches the feeble attempt to attack him.*** Hooker: Stop it! Stop…..you’re hurting me you fucking asshole! ***Outside the man in black has heard enough. A well-placed size 12 by the doorknob and the door flies open. The clouds break up allowing the full moon to bathe the room in its light. The sound of the door being kicked in causes the man to loosen his grip on the hooker and she pulls away from him and falls onto the floor with a thud. The businessman rattled somehow finds his voice and shouts at the shadowy figure.*** Man: Uh….Look pal…I paid for a full hour….so just back off I’ll be done with her when I say I’m done. ***The man in black responds with a quiet deliberate tone.*** Get out. Man: Fuck you pal! If you don’t get the hell out of here right now I’m going to have to remove you….this is your last chance. ***The man in black just shakes his head and sighs. They always want to do things the hard way, he thinks to himself. Then like a cat he takes two steps across the room and wraps a gloved hand around his throat.*** I’m not going to say it again. I’m going to give you one more opportunity to leave under your own power. ***Choking and a bit nervous but not scared enough to realize what his mouth is saying before his brain can process the information he manages to get out the following phrase.*** Businessman: Fuck you!!! ***With one motion the man in black tightens his grip on the man’s neck and flings him across the room where he crashes into the open door. Before he can compose himself the man in black is right behind him picking him up behind the neck and by his belt and tosses him out the open door. His shoulder landing awkwardly against the concrete barricade.*** Hooker: That’s right you sorry sack of shit! That will teach you to screw with me. Make you think twice before trying to short change me!! Businessman: You…you…broke my arm…son of a bitch. You fucking broke my arm. Hooker: Thanks Mister…I had him right where I wanted him though…but you certainly helped with that distraction. Just get out….NOW! ***The man in black replied. After seeing what happened to her John even she realizes when its time to get out. She grabs her top and makes sure she has her cash and out the door she dashes, kicking the middle-aged man while he was down on the way. Despite his pain he spots a broken beer bottle laying next to his feet, he reaches with his good arm and picks it up. As the man in black approaches the door the businessman struggles with what strength he has left and he makes a desperate attempt to cut him with the bottle. His clumsy lunge misses wildly and the man in black chuckles to himself as he brings his leather clad boot crashing down on the businessman’s wrist. There is a sickening snap, kind of like when you snap a carrot in half, and the businessman drops the bottle and screams in pain. The man in black casually walks over near the bed and picks up the John’s suit jacket. After going through the pockets he seems to find what he is looking for and then he tosses the jacket out the open door and over the railing where it sails silently down to the parking lot.*** Businessman: W…W…Who in the hell…are you…anyway? Your worst nightmare, the man in black replies. Businessman: I…I…know what you look like…this is an assault… Don’t even think about going to the cops… ***The Man in black kneels down to the businessman’s level. The full moon finally reveals to us who our mysterious man in black is…none other than the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde. In his hand is the businessman’s wallet. Wylde opens it up and takes out a driver’s license.*** Wylde:…Mr. Kenneth Mann of 598 Edgewood Drive of Columbus Ohio. What you fail to realize here is that you are in MY backyard. We aren’t back in the safety of your suburban neighborhood. You could go to the cops but I’m somewhat of a celebrity around here. Who do you think the police are going to believe? You or me? I was just out for a ride to clear my head when I saw you chasing one of our upstanding citizens half-naked around this fine four roach establishment. I was just doing my part as a good smaritian and stopped this deplorable behavior from continuing. I know that people think that the police are stupid and all…but they will put two and two together and you will be spending the rest of your trip here courtesy of the taxpayers of this fine city and I’ll come out smelling like a rose. ***The businessman has propped himself up against the open door the mask of pain that he was wearing begins to fade as he realizes that he is in a no win situation.*** Wylde: That is only one of the many options that I have. ***Wylde pulls out a business card.*** Wylde: How do you think your bosses would react when I make a phone call posing as the desk sergeant of the Las Vegas police department wanting to verify your employment and address? Of course they are going to be curious and I’ll be more than happy to inform them that you were booked for solicitation of prostitution. And that I suspect that you were using your per diem money that they provided you to represent your company at their big convention. Then I would make a suggestion that they find a new field representative. What do you think they will do Kenneth? ***Next out of the wallet Wylde pulls out a picture of a woman and two children.*** Wylde: Just how would you explain that one to your wife? How would you explain that sudden dismissal of your duties? Hope you have plenty of savings to keep things afloat while you search for a new job. I guess you could tell your wife the truth. How does that old saying go about the truth? Businessman: uh…uh..it will set you free? Wylde: Right! I knew it was some sort of pompous crap like that. You could tell her the truth…and I’m pretty damn sure that not only will the truth set you free but your wife would pack up those kids and set you free as well. Not before she takes half of everything you own, not to mention the child support she would get. Once that court gets done with you...you'd be lucky to afford a place like this to live and to eat your dinner out of a can from a hot plate. ***An evil grin creeps across Wylde’s face.*** Wylde: Or better yet. I could hop aboard my private plane and land in good old Columbus Ohio before you can cash in your return ticket for a different flight and get through airport security. Why would I go to Columbus you might ask? ***You could almost see all of the blood rush out of the businessman’s face as his mind continues to think about all the bad things that could come of this damn night.*** Wylde: I could find out just where in suburbia Edgewood Drive is and pay this little wife of yours a visit personally. A late night visit…maybe treat her like you were treating that whore I caught you with. Or I could just simply tell her what you have been doing on this business trip. Something tells me that this isn’t the first time that you’ve done this. Am I right Kenneth? ***The businessman drops his head in shame.*** Wylde: I thought so. I’m not here to shame you though. I’m not going to take the moral high ground and preach to you about how you should change. No, that’s not my thing. What you do on your time is your business. ***Up until now Wylde’s tone had been a bit lighthearted but now he becomes a bit more harsh.*** Wylde: All I asked of you was that you get out of this room! GET OUT!! It seems like a pretty simple request. I know I speak pretty good english so you couldn’t have misunderstood me. So after all that we have been through…I’m going to ask you one more time to get the hell out, you don’t have to go home but you can’t stay here. I’m on a bit of a time constraint and you have taken up way too much of my time already, so please don’t force my hand any further. ***Realizing he is getting a chance to dodge a potentially huge bullet in his life the businessman collects himself and using the door to help steady him, he pulls himself off the ground and limps slowly down the hallway. Before Wylde slams the door behind him he tosses the businessman’s wallet over the railing and down to the street below.*** Wylde: Better get down those stairs fast Kenneth or all you’ll have left in that wallet is that cute family picture! ***Wylde then disappears back into the room slamming the door behind him hard enough to make the windows rattle. After taking a deep breath he strolls over to the bed a disgusted look crosses his face after he actually thought for a minute about sitting down on the paper thin sheets.*** Wylde: This room doesn’t look like it’s changed after all these years…no telling who or what is crawling all over that mess. ***Near the door is a small table and chair Wylde grabs the chair and places it backwards in the center of the room. He removes his sunglasses and begins to speak.*** Wylde: For those of you that have been living under a rock. Please allow me to introduce myself. My name is Wylde. Zakk Wylde, professional wrestler, businessman, night club owner, millionaire and all around son of a bitch! While a great many people viewing this message know of me and my reputation, unbelievable as it may seem there are more of you that don’t know of me. ***Wylde pauses and looks around the room.*** Wylde: This is why I brought you all here today. You see this place this room in specific, holds a special place in my history. You want some insight on who I am? Take a look around. This place wasn’t just a place where I had some sort of revelation…this was the place of my re-birth! This very room is where the Wyld Child was born. I’m not saying that my journey into this business was any harder than the next but the journey defined who I am. The journey made me stronger. I always had the passion…the drive to succeed, but it wasn’t until I came here that everything made sense. My path from that point on was crystal clear. If there is one thing that you all will soon learn about me is that I am someone who lives on the edge and that I’m willing to do anything and everything to get what I want or that I really don’t give a damn about the consequences of my actions. When it comes to business I’m going to do any and all of those things in order to reach the desired outcome. We could sit here for hours and talk about all of the things I’ve done in order to get ahead. The people that I’ve stepped on, the women that I’ve used, the rules that have been broken, the men who have had their careers ended by my hand. Am I proud of that? No, but I won’t apologize for it either. If they had been half the man they thought they were they wouldn’t have had it happen to them…but that is just the way the ball bounces my friend. On any given day it could be your turn to face the cold hard fact that your time is up. Nothing and I mean nothing will stand in my way when I set my mind to it. ***Wylde gets up out of the chair and paces back and forth.*** Wylde: I’m not here in FUSE to make a name for myself. My name is made. It doesn’t matter if these or any fans of this sport remember my name. My name is made. I’m not going to stand here today and brag about being a World Champion in each and every federation that I’ve set foot in. I could tell you about being the first ever World Champion of an upstart federation UW. But it would be all for nothing. It’s nothing but posturing and it doesn’t impress you, it doesn’t intimidate you. Plus all of that is in the past. I have a theory that if you live too much in your past glory that should be your clue to you to hang it up. You’ve peaked…you will never achieve more than what you already have. The only thing about the past that I do find valuable is in this room. I’ve visited this room several times over the years since that fateful night usually before a big match. That feeling of desperation still hangs heavy here. It is almost like when the world is going crazy around you, that here, time has stood still and it gives you a quiet place to pull your thoughts together and listen to that voice inside your head. When I’m focused that voice inside my head has never…ever done me wrong. ***Wylde stops pacing and walks over to a small mirror that is barely hanging on to the wall above a dirty sink. He glares at his reflection as he continues.*** Wylde: I do have a more selfish reason for coming out of my self imposed retirement. I have something to prove. I didn’t leave my last company the way a champion should. I had become soft and let someone get inside my head. It forced me from the ring and into solitary confinement inside the sanitary environment of a mental institution. Much to my dismay when I finally got out I found out that my federation had closed its doors due to the owner’s untimely death. Content to enjoy my freedom I came back to Las Vegas to pick up where I left off, running my night club located in the MGM Grand Hotel, playing some golf, and enjoying the unique night life that only Vegas can provide. Within a couple months though there was this feeling that was building deep inside me. The desire to return to the ring, some people have even called this "the sickness", was becoming too great. I had spent so much time in my last federation that nothing else seemed like it would compare. So I did the only thing that I could do. I bought the rights to run my own federation under the banner of my former employers. I fought an uphill battle with the lawyers to purchase the name, then with a ruthless cable channel who wanted to force me to live up to a contract that the former owners had signed. So again I did the only thing that I could do took my federation to the world wide web and gave my show away for free. Boy…you should have seen those son of a bitches when they dragged me into court and the court said they couldn’t force me to stop giving my show away for free…talk about a Kodak moment. But then I was blind-sided by a partner that disappeared on me sighting personal reasons. A group of crybaby wrestlers that wanted things handed to them on a silver platter. Fed up with all the bitching and moaning I once again did the only thing that I could do. I walked away…shut the damn place down once again leaving the whole bitching crew to fend for themselves. That is the one thing I want to once again re-enforce to you that I will do ANYTHING in order to get my way. As much as I cared about that federation, as much money that I sunk into that money pit, I killed it!! If I would do that to something I cared about….what do you think I will do to you? Please don’t take that as a threat…that is not what it is…it’s a cold hard fact! I spoke earlier about this was a spot of my re-birth…actually it was the genesis of the man that you see standing here today. Considering what I really came up to this room to do that night so many years ago it becomes so ironic that visiting here every so often centers me and helps me clear my head. I started in this business just like everyone else. At the bottom, setting up chairs, selling tickets, eventually helping set up the ring just to be around the business. I had the sickness from the moment that I stepped into my first show. My parents laughed me out of the house and right into the sport that I fell in love with. Their lack of confidence in me was the thing that motivated me to work hard and learn my craft…again how ironic that same motivation caused me to break down after several years. You see I had a good look, I knew what the hell to do and when to do it. I had crowds eating out of the palm of my hand. Yet every damn time I got close to my goal of winning a championship…some sleazy promoter would pull the rug right out from under me. The first time that happens to you it stings. Then each time after that it takes a little piece of you with it. After numerous times that this happened to me I packed up my car, pulled together what little bit of cash I had left and ended up here. ***Wylde walks across the room and looks out onto the street through the broken mini-blinds.*** Wylde: My plan was simple enough. Find a place someplace cheap to stay, take what was left over and go have one hell of a party cause once that money ran out, my time on this planet would also run out. ***Wylde returns to the center of the room and places his black boot on the chair.*** Wylde: Yep, if you can read between the lines I think you all have had enough time to realize that I came here to end my life. My parents and the sleazy promoters had won. I was throwing in the towel. While out enjoying the bright lights and sin that Vegas is known for somewhere in my drunken haze had an interesting encounter with a strange man at a blackjack table. There was something about this man, something that was drawing me to him, he made me feel comfortable…so comfortable that I began to spill out my whole miserable life story to this complete stranger. I thought he was joking when he said he had connections in the business and could get me what I wanted. I figured at this point I had nothing to lose…this guy was never going to come through with anything that I hadn’t seen before so I told him sure…hook me up..but what was it going to cost me? That is where I almost fell off the stool…he told me only thing it would cost me was my soul. When I say almost fell off my stool…it was because I was drunk and laughing so hard as I signed my name on a cocktail napkin giving him total ownership of my soul. One miserable worthless soul, I wrote across in my drunken scribble. I don’t remember much after that. The next thing that I remember I was back in this room, passed out on that thing they call a bed and the phone ringing off the hook making my head pound. I sat up and saw the sunlight attempting to penetrate the blinds. My next thought was damn, I come all this way to kill myself and I can’t even get that right!! And that damn phone won’t stop ringing. I didn’t want to pick it up…who the hell knew I was here…NOBODY. But it wouldn’t stop….so I picked it up. It was a promoter that was given my name by a friend. He wanted to sign me sight unseen upon recommendation of this friend. As he went on and on about all of these grand plans he had for me, I started to zone out, I had heard all of this stuff before. Today it seemed different. I found myself going against everything that my head was telling me and answered the man from the heart and the rest they say is history. Everything changed from that day forward. Within six months, the time usually when the rug was pulled out from under me, I was a world champion for the first time. After that offers began to pour in from all over the world. Everyone wanted a piece of the Wyld Child. Everyone wanted to hitch their wagon to me. Even metal guitarist Zakk Wylde wanted in on the action. Much like another band that tried to cash in on selling their name to be used in professional wrestling gimmick, Mr. Wylde wanted to see his name live on in the world of wrestling and asked me to change my ring name to mirror his. The Wyld Child Zakk Wylde was born. It was almost too good to believe…I was getting paid to use someone else’s name, and his music while I worked in a sport that I loved. Even though the contract ran out years ago…I kept the name and have remained a close friend of Mr. Wylde to this day. ***Wylde walks over to the 20" TV bolted to the nightstand. The TV doesn’t respond right away a jumbled mess of static and thick black bars fill the screen. Frustrated Wylde gives the set a well placed fist to the side and the screen instantly comes into focus. On the screen we can see a video montage of Wylde’s opponents for this week Vassago and Itachi playing. Wylde stares intently as he continues.*** Wylde: So after my debut match in FUSE I once again find myself here in my fortress of solitude. Oh sure…I put on a hell of a show but we came up short. It’s not Gideon’s fault, I’m sure that damn veal I ate didn’t help me any but I’m not going to sit here and make excuses. It is what it is. All I can do now is prepare for this week. I guess by now my opponents know a hell of a lot about me yet I still know little about them. One is another newcomer, "The Demon" Vassago. Forgive me if I’m not intimidated by your status as a Demon. I’ve been face to face with several demons in my times, most of them my own, and I’ve never backed down to one yet. The other seems to be someone that has some history here in FUSE, Itachi. I respect our peers from the Orient. They are fierce competitors with the discipline to match. I look forward to this clash of styles it should lead to an interesting match to say the least. Its okay I don’t need a whole lot of motivation to get up for this match. Boys we are in MY backyard. The arena where we will be competing is in the same hotel where my nightclub is located. Maybe you’ve heard of it…The Devil’s Own? No matter…to show you what a sport I am, I’ll put you both on the guest list for the huge celebration that I’m having afterward. I guess it won’t be much of a celebration for you two but maybe the distraction will take your mind off of the beating that you are going to receive at my hands. I know I’m not going to be embarrassed in my backyard boys so please bring your "A" game. Unfortunately for one of you two by the end of the night you WILL know what its like to be one of the Devil’s Own!! ***Wylde walks to the door opens it, once again bathing the dingy room in the full moon light. He pauses and turns back toward the camera.*** Wylde: I do want to offer up one last piece of advice for you gentlemen. Don’t give me an opening. You saw what I can do when I get even a little window of opportunity when I was dealing with Mr. Mann earlier. He went from tough guy to broken man in about 2.5 seconds. Don’t worry yourself about his sweet little wife. She couldn’t handle the Wyld Child, and if I did give her that late night booty call she’d divorce that loser in a heartbeat. ***With that Wylde steps back into the night slamming the door behind him***
