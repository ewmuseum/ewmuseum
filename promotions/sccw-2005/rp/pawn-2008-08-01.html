Date: 8/1/2008 Crystalline blue skies for miles and miles. Just below it you have the tree tops and that’s all you can see from the vantage point provided. The sound of rushing water can be heard and for a moment you think that you’re in the middle of the wilderness, just then an airliner interrupts the serenity. Standing at the edge of a pool looking down in the shimmering water is Simon King. He rocks slowly from the balls of his feet to his heels. Bare chested with arms crossed in front of him he stands there. His faded blue jeans are cut low. The hours in the gym are finally beginning to show off but there are some deep bruises that would have never been on his body a year ago. The bumps and the bruises of the business are taking their toll on his body. King’s lips start to move but the words being said are not audible. The rocking slows. He runs his left hand through his hair and it’s followed by his right and then he pulls on the back of his slowly and begins to rub the back of his neck. The mental part is beginning to play with his mind. The fragile psyche of a professional is tested day in and day out. You never know who you can trust or can’t. One day you’re surrounded by loved one and friends and the next moment those same people are trying to take a chunk out of you to further their own careers. ** Celeste Montgomery is sitting with her blackberry sitting on a desk. Clad in a black three quarter sleeve dress shirt and charcoal skirt. The look on her face clearly indicates that she’d rather be somewhere else. "What exactly has Simon been up to as of late?" Lordes St. Claire’s voice is clear as day. Montgomery begins twirling her blonde locks around her index finger. Miming Lordes in a fashion that she could only pull off from a distance. "If I could say for certain I would tell you but I honestly can’t Lordes." Montgomery’s voice is soft. "You’re not being paid the salary that you are for you not to be certain about exactly what Simon is up. You were given precise instructions. I gave you clear detailed agenda to follow." There is an edge to St. Claire’s voice that is aggressive in nature. "I don’t understand…" "Of course you don’t understand because you were never there before take it from someone who has been there. The path that I set before him was clear and concise for a reason. All I’ve seen are mistakes after mistake starting with him being face to face with Colby the night of his debut. Can you get anything right!?!" The agitation is prevalent now. "This tirade will not change what’s already been Lordes, you know it and I know it. If you want Simon to follow your precious regimented rules than you can come and wield the riding crop and beat him into the wrestler you want him to be. I was against this from the very beginning once you told me what it was that you wanted me to do and wanted him to do." *** Simon King is sitting on a black leather couch his feet propped up on the coffee table. Same blue jeans he’s only added a grey t-shirt with that appears to be two sizes too big. His attention and focus is on the action on the television across the room. King has his hands cradling his head. Celeste walks into the room and watches him for a moment "So, Simon what exactly to you have planned for this extravaganza they have planned for you? Or are you going to wing it like you have the past view weeks?" She crosses her right leg over her left leg. Simon looks at her from the corner of his eye. His focus clearly taken away from the television. "I plan on competing." "That’s it.?" King glares at her. "Competing isn’t enough, you know it and I know it. On that car ride out here you talked pretty big and so far you’ve come up pretty small." King stands for a moment and then sits back down. His eyes narrow further and his lips curl into a snarl. "It’s easier said then done." "Change that to turning the industry on its ear. Change that to dominating." "Dominate huh…" "Did I stutter? Half ass attempts have gotten you what?" King rubs his bruised shoulder. "Exactly so how about you stop watching the relics and become the future that you claimed you were going to become all those weeks ago." King stands up and walks across the room and looks out of the window. His reflection in the mirror is one of a conflicted individual. There is a glint in his eye that was never there before. A knowing that to be that very thing he claimed he was going to become he is going to have to push himself further than he has ever done in the past. He knows now that there may not be a change to ever go back once he goes full tilt and puts the effort necessary into this. "Stop it King. Stop thinking and just do it. Everything this far has been tentative. React, just react." "Oh, they’ll get a reaction alright. Mark my words they’ll get a reaction."
