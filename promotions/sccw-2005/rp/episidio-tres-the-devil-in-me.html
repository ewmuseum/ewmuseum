Nick Cave & the Bad Seeds – Red Right Hand (Let Love In, 1994) 21st of May, 2009. Special Agent Finn Roberts stood in the centre of the ladies bathroom of local nightclub From the Ashes. A clever play on words for the city which it resided in. Phoenix, Arizona. But unlike the mythical bird, this victim who sat in a stall would not be as graceful. Riga Mortis had already kicked in and the body had barely been dead for six hours. He stood out like a sore thumb, in his tailored New York City suit, sweating in the middle of an Arizona summer, yet his fashion sense was too great for him to remove his jacket. He’d thoughtfully undone the top button of his shirt and loosened his tie to give his body a little vent, but not enough to deal with the climatic surroundings. And the cheap conditioning unit which was obviously on the fritz was not making the female facilities anymore comfortable. "They all musta thought this broad was still takin’ a shit. It ain’t strange for a broad to fall asleep on one’a them shitters, officer." Tony Bonaducci pled his case with Special Agent Roberts. Finn frowned at him, yet did not give him the satisfaction of the reaction by avoiding his gaze. His attention was on the forensics team doing their thang with the stiff in the cubicle. They hadn’t properly ID’ed her yet. Her belongings had been taken from the scene. Whether it is a college girl prank to run off with her clothes, or some bottom feeder pinching her purse, those minor facts Roberts didn’t care for. All he worried about was the stiff in his cubicle and this now being called a federal case under his jurisdiction. Bonaducci scratched at his head and tested the water. Curiosity getting the better of him and clearly forgetting what happened to the cat. "Why you feds involved? She some sort of government or poli?" Roberts frowned again. This time staring at him. "Poli?" "Y’know? Poli-tician?" Finn shrugged, returning his attention to the forensics team. Flash bulbs momentarily made the female lavatory facilities of From the Ashes illuminate with an eerie white on occasion. The flash bulbs gave quick glimpses of what Finn thought could have been seven or eight different health code violations. This place wasn’t half as bad as some of the scenes he’d attended. Some places he’d been he was certain there was no such thing as sanitation. Still, it made Finn’s gizzards do a tumble-turn on the insides, thinking about what mildewy monsters crept across this floor. This was why he was grateful to not be in forensics. Having fought the urge, Finn had finally grown tired of the nightclub owner’s protruding thoughts and he bit. "Mr. Bonaducci. I can assure you…" Finn paused, giving off the air that he might be making some great profound speech, yet his facial expressions twisted and the spite laced his voice "…that none of this is ANY of your God-damn-business, yeah?" He transformed back into nice Finn. "So if you could find it in your heart to answer MY questions… we’re gunna get along oh-so-much nicer, yeah?" Tony nodded, taking the moment to understand that Special Agent Roberts would prefer him to speak only when spoken to. This was difficult for Tony. Some say he needs to talk to breathe. And this silence was near strangling him. "Have any of your patrons identified the victim, sir?" Roberts licked the end of his lead pencil at the ready to notate the answers of the owner. "Anybody come forward askin’ if your security had seen anybody? Maybe they were askin’ if they seen somebody with somebody else?" "You think this broad was gettin’ her fuck on or somethin’?" Bonaducci’s eye widened perversely, which put Finn off guard, raising the heat under his collar. Roberts dropped the notepad and grabbed Bonaducci by the collar, shoving him hard against the tiled wall. Just hard enough for the back of his head to thunk and knock a groan out of him. Made Tony fight the feeling to let his eyes roll into the back of his head and consciousness escape him. Finn rolled his fists tighter into Tony’s collar, tight enough to press both sets of knuckles into his wind pipe and put a dampener on his airways. "Listen here, you sick, little, money grubbin’ fuck… You need to learn how to respect the dead or I promise you that you’ll join her soon enough and THEN you can ask her any of your perverted little fucken questions yaself… yeah?" "Yeah." It was meek and all Tony could muster given the choking predicament and the throbbing at the back of his head, which was almost deafening inside it now. "I hear ya." "Loud and clear?" Bonaducci nodded. "Loud and clear." Finn dropped the hold, letting Tony get flat on his feet. Condescendingly, Finn brushed Tony down and straightened his collar. Picked a couple of mothballs off of his shirt and smiled at him. "Now… I want you to go find ya security team and ask them all to meet me in your office in five minutes. I want the log book these guys sign in on. I want them all standing away from the doorway against the far wall. Facing the door." "You think they might’ve done it?" Bonaducci’s perverted little glaze flickered over his eyes again. The idea of one of his staff being the predator in his nightclub must have stirred some sociopathic nerves in his nether regions. Roberts gritted his teeth. "You’re pushin’ ya luck again, pal. And while you’re at it you’ll get five God damn coffees. Wait…" Roberts called to the cubicle. "Sal!" The big thick necked body of Salvatore Vatorre, the most unfortunately named man in the existence of the human race, stepped out from the cubicle neighbouring their Jane Doe. "Yeah?" Finn made a drinking motion. "Coffee?" "Tea." He shoved his glasses up his nose. "Chai tea." "You got chai tea?" Roberts gave Bonaducci one of them looks so stern your blood turns cold. "No." Bonaducci gulped. Not sure where the volatile special agent’s temper would take him next. "Get some." Bonaducci’s eyes widened, but not in his perverted way this time, and he scurried out through the front entrance of the women’s toilet facilities and into the nightclub. Surely barking orders at his underlings to get some coffees and for someone, for the love of god, to go and get some Chai tea. Roberts smirked to his own pleasure while Sal shook his head. "He ain’t a suspect." Sal shovelled his glasses up his nose again. Roberts noticed the shovel. "Don’t they make glasses for heads as fat as yours?" Sal’s right hand clenched into a fist. If Finn didn’t know that Sal was a forensics expert, he’d have sworn he was a pro-wrestler or one of those live for the moment S.W.A.T. guys. He had a wicked glimmer in his eye. Others in the agency thought him to be a sociopath, for his placid nature, enigmatic lifestyle and that fire which danced behind his eyes. Maybe he was. But he was a darn nice guy and the best in the biz. His attention to detail was superfluous. He was a savant in the way he could almost profile a killer by the evidence, or lack thereof, on the body. Had an eerie understanding on the killer’s mind and why they did things like this. He knew where to look for evidence and where not to. The science behind moulding their unsub into an actual subject was hand-crafted for Salvatore Vatorre to master. Maybe this was why they looked at him like he was some sort of psycho. "You’re a jerk sometimes." Sal shook his head and shovelled his glasses up his nose again. Sal was also a right pussycat. And Finn wondered if his constant ribbing was making that sociopath, which slept dormant deep inside of Salvatore Vatorre, write his name down on his list of Hits. Finn stepped across the tiled floor and patted Sal on the shoulder. "Hey… I’m jokin’ right. You know that." "Yeah." Sal was bashful with his response, trying to mask the pain Finn’s words left by moving on to the task at hand. "We’ve got evidence of penetration." "Use a condom?" "Looks like it. There’s half a torn wrapper in the next cubicle… half in this one. We’ll check it for partials. I don’t think our unsub uses gloves. Too hard to tell at this stage but we’ll take it away for analysis and my team will have some prints for you. I’m pretty sure on that." Sal pointed at the two halves of condom wrapper on the tiled floor. Next, he motioned toward the Jane Doe. "There’s residue of an oil unnatural to the human body on her inner thigh and I bet when we do an internal we find more of that oil. So I’m thinking that this is the wrapper for the unsub. I’m guessing it’s a water based lubricant associated with our unsub’s Trojan." Roberts nodded and began patting his breasts and side pockets like he were looking for something. He looked around his feet and then noticed it on the tiled floor. "Ah shit." Roberts turned and looked down at his notepad on the floor. "I don’t think I wanna use this no more." "Scared of a couple of germs?" Sal tried not to laugh as Roberts toed the notepad with his shoe. "Look at this shit hole. I’m surprised I haven’t caught hepatitis A through Z for breathin’ in this crapper." Sal let the laugh out this time. "Never mind, Finn. I’ll give you a copy of my notes." "Thanks." "The thing that strikes me as odd-" "No murder weapon?" Roberts felt like he was reading Sal’s mind. Maybe he was ahead a few pages. Sal shook his head. "That nobody heard her scream." "Yeah." Finn looked around to see if there were ventilation ports to allow fresh air into the facilities, but his mind wandered to the constant barrage of bass thumps and dog- pestering treble tones that would have been rocking the dance floor inside. But even in spite of the noise inside, Finn knew that female toilets were constantly populated in the duration of a night. Especially a club as supposedly popular as the owner would have them understand. Surely they would have heard something. "That is fucken odd." He looked around the tiled floor. "Have you found it?" "The murder weapon? No trace of it at this stage. I’m thinking that it was something a little heavier than a regular knife. There’s no hacking. It’s a straight through cut. Our unsub is remarkably strong. Most butchers would struggle to get through that in one go. Our unsub is definitely someone who utilises health facilities for muscle mass. There’s no sign of struggle to remove the limb. I believe it was done in one motion by the cleanliness of the cut." "Body builder?" Roberts asked, with a secret vendetta against every muscle-head in high school who’d put him on the inside of a locker. "No. I doubt it. Accuracy would be an issue there. Large fingers and motor skills are lost when somebody does that much work on their muscles. We’re possibly looking at an elite athlete who requires enormous strength to perform their duty," Vatorre told Roberts with another shove of his spectacles up the bridge of his nose as his mind jumped to conclusions. "Line backer. Maybe. Professional wood cutter." "Professional wood cutter?" Roberts stifled a laugh. "Are you clutching at straws or you’re for real?" "The strength it took to get through this wrist with as little effort as possible comes down to someone who has enormous strength in their left hand. The-" "Whoa! Left hand? Really? How can you tell?" Vatorre dropped to one knee, narrowly missing some of the pooling blood in the bottom of the cubicle. He used a HB lead pencil’s rear-end to point to the wrist that was clearly missing a hand. Cutting through the air with the pencil, Sal mimicked the action he believed removed the limb. "If you look closely at the angle here, and then take into consideration the confines of this cubicle… there is no way a right handed person could lop somebody’s hand off in this amount of space. He would not be able to position himself properly to make the impact required to remove the limb. Especially in a fluid movement like our unsub has done. No room to pull the arm back. No room to position. There are no footprints which suggest that someone did that either, particularly in the way of a cut like this. We would have bloodied footprints all through the nightclub. So with the use of summation… our unsub is left-handed." "Same as Portland?" Vatorre nodded. "Portland AND… Albany." Roberts sighed, pinching at the bridge of his nose in thought and the hope of relieving pressure. He ran the same hand over the top his forehead and through his hair, letting out a sigh. "It’s official." Again, Vatorre nodded in testimony to Finn’s own thoughts. "We’ve got a serial offender on our hands, Finn. And with the length of time between these last two attacks… we could have more victims on our hands very soon." Finn Roberts pulled the cell phone out of his inner jacket pocket and immediately began to scroll through his contacts list. The cogs of war had begun to turn in unison within his mind, piecing together a puzzle and planning his next move. His next three moves. Finding what he was after he connected the call and placed the phone to his ear, turning his back to Vatorre, who snatched up a camera to take some shots of the condom wrappers before placing them into evidence bags. "Roberts here… what’s the name of that small-time from Albany? … Renner. Get me, Renner. I want him here in Phoenix by tomorrow morning." Roberts pressed a hand against the wall as he listened to the other end of the phone. Then he realised where he was and pulled his hand away from the wall, making a face and wiping it on his trouser leg, miming the words "God dammit!" Behind him he could hear Vatorre chuckle, he must’ve caught the candid moment whilst putting the wrapper in the evidence lock up. "Database says he’s on leave? Where’s he on leave? … Oregon? Portland, Oregon? No Fuck. Okay… put out an APB on Detective Renner. I want him transported to Phoenix by midnight tonight. Anybody gets a visual on him he is to be presumed armed and dangerous. I repeat… armed and dangerous. He’s to be considered the main suspect in this case until further notice. I want pictures up from here to Albany. Anybody spots him he’s to be reprimanded until we’re able to speak with him. Nobody talks to him but me. Got it?" Vatorre stood up, hearing Roberts swinging into action on his cell phone, watching him begin to pace back and forth across the lavatory floor. "You think the detective did this?" Vatorre asked. Roberts covered the mouthpiece of his phone. "I don’t like coincidence, Sal. He could be working in cahoots with whoever has done this Phoenix girl. And if he’s not… it’ll make him hungrier to help us. He already fucked up in Albany." Vatorre nodded, seeing the method to Finn’s madness. They were on the trail of a killer’s whose movements were entirely sporadic and unconventional, yet at the same time there was a pattern. They just hadn’t figured it out yet. He’d struck twice before doing so here in Phoenix, which they knew of, and would more than likely strike again. It was only a matter of time. Sal Vatorre knew that it was only a matter of time before they got another call. Before there was another crime scene. More tape and more evidence. Before there was another Jane Doe. Before America’s newest serial killer struck again. Episodio Tres The Devil in Me: dreamer Present Day. "April, I been meanin’ to tell ya somethin’," August told her, coyly, from across the room. His boot toeing the shag- pile. His daughter was paler than usual, wearing a thick woollen jumper like she was chilled to the bone. She looked up from the armchair where she’d been fighting the urge to doze. She’d been nodding off for the last two hours but wasn’t able to stomach the idea of sleep. Sleeping wasn’t a nice place for her anymore. There was no rejuvenation from the experience. She simply awoke more exhausted then she’d gone to sleep. These dreams were taking over her days. "What? What’s wrong, Augie?" Her voice was tired and coarse. With a heavy sigh, August began stroking his beard in thought, like he was choosing his words carefully. Like he were trying not to rattle or upset her. He grunted out of frustration, knowing fully well he had insufficient verbal skills to find words or sentences to piece together. The ones which would protect her from what he wanted to tell her. Drawing on his courage, August took a long breath to assist him and when he exhaled he let the words out with the breath. "I got some weird call the other night." Her brow furrowed with curiosity. "Whaddaya mean weird?" "Just weird. Some guy called me. On my cell." "Cell? Who has your number?" she asked, even more curious that her father had remembered it and given it out to somebody. "You. Pop. That’s it. I ain’t given it to nobody else. I don’t even remember the fucken number." August threw his hands up in the air like he was completely baffled. "What was the call about?" Concern laced her words as she waited with baited breath for her father’s response. Augie walked into the lounge room, sitting in the other armchair directly across from her. His eyes awash with the worry of a man who first the first time in a long, long time was unable to comprehend. Unable to subtract anything from the equation which plagued him recently in the wake of a strange phone call. A phone call which had left him restless at night, on the other side of the wall to his restless daughter. "I didn’t know who it was from. Just someone ramblin’. Askin’ me for help. Sayin’ weird stuff." "Weird how?" Her heartbeat was quickening. She felt like she was tied into what August was saying but she didn’t realize how deep she was involved. Involuntarily all the same. "He kept sayin’ he’d gone an’ done it again. Didn’t say what it was but just kept sayin’ he didn’t wanna do it. That somebody else was makin’ him do it. Know what I mean?" "Making him do what?" April’s desperation was beginning to quiver her vocal chords and her last question exploded in an almost blur. She could feel a cold sweat lining her brow as her intent to know what her father was talking about was beginning to consume her. "I dunno. He never said nothin’. Just said it was the red hand that made him do it. Sayin’ it wants more. Red, right hand. That’s who it was doin’ it to ‘em." The blood flowing through April’s veins chilled immediately and she could have sworn her heart stopped mid-beat. A shiver rose up her spine. She couldn’t fight it. Nor could she fight the tears building up in her eyes. She didn’t know why she was crying but she could only assume it was because of the connection her mind had made. The connection between the red, right hand and her dreams. The dreams of seeing her own death. The silhouette. The man with the bag of hands. And the cleaver. Ravaging her then collecting her right hand as well. She tucked her knees to her chest tightly. Stared into the wall, through it even, and into her dream, trying to identify the man who’d taken her life in her subconscious. "I had someone check out who called me. Y’know Chuck at the bar?" April never responded. She was barely listening anymore. "He got some cop buddies to check it out. See who it was that called me phone. Then… when he come back to me… I didn’t know what else to do. I ain’t said nothin’ to nobody. Just been lettin’ it eat at me Ape simply coz I dunno if I’m scared or angry. Guess a little of both." April looked up at her dad, with tears streaming down her cheeks. He kept speaking aside from her tears because he felt like she knew what he was talking about already. Maybe she’d got the call, too. Maybe she had some sort of woman senses that men missed out on and she was reading his mind ahead of him being able to think stuff. The fact was the tears and her mood never gave him the feeling to stop so he just kept going. "Ape… he found out who called me." Her eyes locked onto her fathers. The dream was flashing through her mind. The man’s face. It was beginning to clear. The shroud of mystery lifting and she could begin to identify certain features. Portions of his face. An eye. Cheek bone. A partial of his chin. The desperation in her gaze was enough to let August know she was asking who made the call. Asking who the man on the phone was. The man in her dream. She knew they were one and the same. "Ape… it was Brock." The Devil in Me: nightmare 26th of July, 2009. Blood ran down his left arm, dripping off the end of his elbow. It glistened in the moonlight which danced through the window onto his naked body. He’d fed the Red, Right Hand again. He was growing with his hunger and every time he was fed he seemed to only grow hungrier again. Brock crouched in the centre of a dingy dive of a bedroom, in a tatty apartment that nobody would pay more than thirty bucks a month for. Anything else was murder. His body was saturated in the blood of two cheap highway workers, ready and willing at the idea of an easy one hundred each just for doing a three-way. The man behind the wheel had looked so handsome and they felt sorry for him missing a hand. What harm could a one armed man do anyways? A lot apparently. In his hand was a cell and it rang in his ear with its tinny, metallic sound. He was not calm. The feast he’d given the red, right hand was not enough. Nothing seemed to be enough anymore. There were five he’d given to it yet it still craved. It still wanted more. The phone rang and at the other end of the line he’d heard the voice. Perhaps the voice of his salvation. Oh, how he begged for it to be his salvation. "Augie here. Who’s this?" The sound at the other end of the phone was barely audible. The receiver was shouting, struggling to hear what was being said. "August. It’s Brock. August?" Shepherd could barely hear his response, and figured that he went unheard. "Hang on. I’m goin’ outside." Brock’s heart began to beat faster. He could feel the red, right hand coming back. Coming to ask him for more. Brock sat between them. The bodies of the two women. Bleeding profusely and incredibly into the cheap school grade carpet of the pungent apartment. Brock sat between them and waited for August Monday to get outside. "Hello?" "Augie? That you?" "Yep. Who’s this?" A sigh of relief escaped him, only to be foreshadowed by the guilt which rose again like a tide coming into shore. He glanced to his right, at the blond who shuddered her final death twitches. She’d passed out long ago from the pain. Darkness would come for her soon. "I done it again." He spoke in an almost whisper. Left hand shaking like a leaf as the blood ran down his forearm and dripped off the point of his elbow, down to the tattered carpet below. "Done what again?" Shepherd was barely listening. He was trying to get the words out. Vent what he’d been doing. What was happening to him. How he wanted to stop but he couldn’t. He would not dare to disobey the Red, Right Hand. He knew he must listen but if he couldn’t stop it… maybe August could. Maybe August could save him from this monster that lurked beneath the surface. This monster that forced his hand to do his bidding. Brock tried to speak but words tangled and thoughts collided. It all came out at once and it barely made any sense. "I can’t help it. It… it just happens. I just… I did it again." "Done what again? The fuck is this anyways?" Emotion overcame Brock as he met the cold gaze of the brunette to his left. Her cold and lifeless stare still pleaded with him to stop. To give them a chance at life and leave them be to their petty existence. Petty only to the rest of the world. It was her life and she was still living it. Those eyes begged for Brock to let her get back to it. The voice barked at the other end of the phone whilst Shepherd began to sob whole-heartedly for his sins. "Done what again? Tell me who the fuck this is!" "I can’t…" He paused to draw breath and focus between the sobs. "I can’t stop it, Augie. I can’t stop it. I can’t. I can’t. I can’t." Brock burst to his feet, pacing back and forth across the room. His body was glistening red within the moonlight yet he was not worried enough to draw the curtains. He could almost hear the footsteps of the Red, Right Hand drawing closer. He could almost sense it’s presence. Come to look over what Brock had brought for it. He should be pleased with two. Maybe that could stop him for a while. "Can’t stop what, son? You gotta tell me." Hysterical crying had consumed Brock and he tried to speak through the snot and the tears. The footsteps began to echo through the room and he knew it was only a moment before the Red, Right Hand was here. Too soon. Too soon to be saved by Augie. The only man he’d ever grown to look up to and respect since the passing of his father. The only man who could save him. Brock wailed at the top his lungs, trying to speak between bursts of sorrow. He knew he was on the other side of the door. It would only be a moment before he was here again. Before the Red, Right Hand would be with him. "The hand. The red, right hand. It wants more. I can’t give it, Augie. I CAN’T GIVE IT! WHY DON’T IT LEAVE ME THE FUCK ALONE? I DON’T WANT THIS! I… DON’T… WANT… THIS!" With all of his might, Brock wound up like a championship pitched and tossed the phone at the wall across the room. It smashed into a thousand pieces and sparked once the plastic broke. His legs gave way beneath him and fell to his knees, over onto his only hand and he sobbed hysterically. His body convulsing with each new sob. He gagged on his tears and was lost for breath. Almost upright, Brock ran his forearm across his face and stared ahead at a filthy mirror. It did not contain his reflection. Standing in the mirror, looking down on the kneeling Brock Shepherd was the Red, Right Hand. An almost replica of Shepherd despite the fact his right hand was there. It was not missing. And it was a luminous red which shone like the very fires of Hell itself. He too stood naked before Brock, yet there was no blood saturating his body. He stood proud and tall and strong. A force not to be reckoned with. "Who have you told? WHO HAVE YOU TOLD?" he roared viciously. Brock’s lip quivered as he knelt before him. He shook his head, fighting the sob. He did not want to cry before the Red, Right Hand. He spoke in near a whisper. "Nobody." "You want this to end? To be finished?" Slowly, Brock’s head began to nod, yet he could not raise his head to look him in the eye. "Look at me." Brock did not obey. "LOOK… AT… ME!" Their eyes locked. Brock’s tears eroded into nothingness once their gazes met. He felt frightened and consumed by the presence of the Red, Right Hand, yet now their eyes had locked he could not break their gaze. "If you want this to end… you bring… her… to me." "Her?" Shepherd knew who he spoke of, but dared not think of the actuality of his request. He didn’t not want to bring anyone to the Red, Right Hand. Least of all her. He loved her too much to do that. He could never do that. He could never kill April Monday. Could he?
