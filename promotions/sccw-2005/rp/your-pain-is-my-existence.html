Your Pain Is My Existence

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today We fade in to the locker room of Legion and Stigmata after their loss in the fatal four way at Defiance, Stigmata’s face is one of rage over the loss while Legion can be seen as slightly annoyed at how they were outsmarted by the Heirs Of Wrestling. Stigmata: Those damn heirs deciding to make us all collide like a car crash, they’re lucky I didn’t get chance to destroy them at least this time around. (Luci suddenly enters the frame.) Luci: I take it the match didn’t go well? Legion: They know now how we are going to act in the future but I think despite the loss we sent a valid message, if only it had been us vs the Heirs… Luci: You think so? Stigmata: I know so although seeing that Devlin guy take out his own partner shows he still needs to learn a bit about this business… (Cut to a few days later) We now fade in inside the usual setting we have seen for the youtube videos in the past however Legion is not in front of the camera, we see Stigmata in a Children of Bodom t-shirt (Blooddrunk cover) and long denim jeans lighting a cigarette to camera. He then puts it out with the combat boot on his left leg. "You’re all wondering why I am in front of the camera for this promo, the reason is this: I am going to explain to each and every one of you what I am about…" Stigmata begins to crack his knuckles. "When I first came into this wrestling business I was just a face in the crowd, a minion to a woman who believed she was priestess of a religion that may have been wackier than the Scientologists in Discordia but I was abandoned by her after she suddenly decided to get wasted at a party one night." "When next we met the rage consumed me as I nearly broke her nearly right in front of her mother not once but twice thanks to a certain lady in Luci giving me the information on where to find her, following those incidents I decided to live a life of solitude and said to the Master that if the knight ever needed the enforcer to return he just needed to say the word." "The master then wanted me to help protect him from the chance that he could be attacked by Alex Pierce following what he believed would be actions by Luci following the death of Lauren Fox and he knew that she trusted me during the debacle elsewhere. I then decided that I wanted to help Legion in combating his enemies in the ring and with the help of Miss St Germain I have acquired a contract and in order for help in the future she wanted me to take out a thorn in her side." (He gives a fixed glare to the camera.) "That thorn is you Devlin… you and Matt saw what I can do at Defiance and consider that you decided to annoy a very good friend of ours, I shall relish in shedding your blood onto the canvas as I unleash my own brand of strong style. You will feel the biggest amount of pain ever as I prove why I exist. Your pain will become my existence for that night, the pain will require Meds to be taken as you become broken." (He lights another cigarette as we fade out.)
