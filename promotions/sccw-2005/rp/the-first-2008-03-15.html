The First.

15 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/08 'Sin City Championship Wrestling.' 'Last week, I told you that the wages of sin is death.' 'Now, I wish for you to look into the face of your destroyer.' [Fade up.] We find ourselves in a dark room with very few discernable features. The only light is from a single bulb overhead, which is not strong enough to fill the entire room--the walls and corners are shrouded in darkness. It is, however, enough light to cover the most important thing in the room. That thing is a beast of a man, six feet ten inches of solid muscle. He stands before the camera, bare-chested so as to fully display his lightly tanned skin, covering his bulging pectorals and rock-hard abdominals. He has a flowing mane of shoulder-length light brown hair, which frames a face that is rugged and mean, but not wholly unhandsome. The look in his eyes could cut diamonds. This monster-disguised-as-man stands proud and upright. Unflinching. Unmoving. 'Frightening, no?' The words come as something of a surprise, as the man who fills up so much of the shot had not moved a muscle, even in his face. It would seem that he is not alone in the room. There is movement in the shadows behind him. It would seem that there is a smaller man who had been standing behind the larger one. He emerges from obscurity and takes up a position one step behind and slightly to the left of his larger compatriot. He is covered in a heavy black robe, and a hood obscures his face. He speaks again, and the voice is familiar--it was he who had been speaking just now, and it was also his voice featured on the tape at Temptation. 'You now gaze upon the True Prophet. He who was promised to arrive in SCCW and annihilate all that lay before him. He has come to show you the way to salvation. Unfortunately, that way is fraught with obstacles. The willful and the weak; the deviant and the depraved; the fearsome and the foolish. Those poor lost souls will choose to stand up to the True Prophet, and they will be brought down.' 'Poor lost souls like RYUJIN.' At the mention of the opponent's name, the large man's face shows a hint of a sneer. But only a hint. 'RYUJIN has committed a great many sins, no doubt. But his greatest sin is that of envy. He has looked around SCCW and, though he may not want to admit it, has discovered that he is surrounded by his betters. He can look at almost anyone on the roster, and burn with jealousy for their success. He covets so much of what they have. Elliott Rollins' talent. Amy Campbell's skill. Hunter Sabuani's charisma. Jonathan Rhine's title. Even the attention surrounding the pending debut of the True Prophet...RYUJIN wishes his appearances would garner that kind of attention.' 'Alas, it is not to be. For all his yearning to be exemplary, RYUJIN must accept that he is no more than average...if even that.' 'Worry not, RYUJIN. This week on Temptation, the True Prophet is prepared to give you a distinction that no other man can claim, and one that no one can ever take away from you.' 'You will be the first.' The smaller man cross his voluminous sleeves in front of his chest, while the larger turns his neck, emitting a cracking sound. 'The True Prophet will cut a wide swath of destruction through Sin City Championship Wrestling, so that the sheep may follow that path to their eternal reward. It begins with you, RYUJIN.' 'You will not be the greatest obstacle in the True Prophet's path. But you will be the first…and even that distinction carries some honor. For, before he can deliver holy judgment unto the Mayhems and Marshalls, first he must do battle with the lesser beasts. Each victory, no matter how inconsequential the opponent, brings glory to the Lord, which in turns brings more power to the True Prophet.' 'Your crushing defeat will serve the greater good, RYUJIN. How many others can say that?' There is something odd in the voice of the smaller man. It’s not arrogance, because everyone in this business has that. Rather, he speaks with a sense of finality. As if this book has already been written. 'You cannot hope to fathom the eternal tapestry weaved by the Almighty. However, you must understand your place in it. You are the sacrificial lamb. You are the life that must be ended so that the True Prophet can deliver his message to the masses.' 'There are those who have heeded the message already, and have taken flight from Sin City Championship Wrestling. The other who called himself 'The Prophet' has disappeared, knowing that things would not go well for a pretender such as him. Even the famed El Diablo, former champion and leader of a powerful stable, has taken his leave rather than face the True Prophet. Indeed, although the devil may play his games and amass power, it is all for naught in the face of a superior force. So, let them run. Judgment will visit them soon enough, even though it might not be at the hands of the True Prophet.' 'For the rest of the wrestlers in SCCW, there will be no quarter, and no mercy. There will only be judgment, swift and harsh.' As if to show just how harsh it will be, the larger man growls. It isn’t loud, but it is definitely audible…and unpleasant. 'The True Prophet is the wrath of God, made flesh. He can reach into your very soul and crush it. Perhaps he does so to punish you for your evil deeds. Perhaps he does it to prevent you from committing more. It is not for me to say. The Lord, as it is said, works in mysterious ways.' 'We know not where it may end, but it begins on St. Patrick’s Day, and it begins with RYUJIN.' 'You may take solace in the knowledge that we will remember your sacrifice, even if nobody else will.' With that, the smaller man fades back into the shadows made by his larger associate. Now that there is no other voice to distract you, your attention is once more fully focused on the hulking monstrosity. He stares back into the camera, as if he is trying to see you own sins, so that he may punish you for them. [End.] # # # 'I think it went well.' 'I think it was the stupidest goddamn thing I've ever been a part of.' 'I'm sorry you feel that way, friend. I was hoping that you would embrace your role.' 'You say that as if I had a choice in the matter.' 'That's true. You don't."
