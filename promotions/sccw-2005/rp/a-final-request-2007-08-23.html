A Final Request

23 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-23-07 Sitting alone on the footsteps of The Lincoln Monument as night descended on Washington D.C. "The Dastardly Bastard" Timo Bolamba wistfully pondered his uncertain future. The large Samoan cast a shadow as the moon began to plow upwards, full of itself and radiant within the sky. The very eyes of Lincolns’ statue looked down, observing all, including the immigrant, who was still nursing wounds to both his body and ego. Wind blew in from the west, tousling strands of the wild Samoan’s hair as it whipped all around him. Clearly the world’s toils and rigors had finally caught up with The Bastard. Slowly, a trapping of feet caught the attention of the melancholy wrestler, and he turned his head to the left, spying his quarry. "The Samoan Silencer" stepped lightly to the side of his nemesis and sat down next to him, looking across the cityscape with his paint clad face. "Beautiful night, isn’t it Bastard?" The first utterance had been broken by the two halves of the Samoan. The Bastard looked around and nodded slowly, belying his ambitions, and for once just smiled. He was clearly enjoying the fact that he was able to stop and think about life instead of impulsively acting without reason. "You finally found me, did you little mouse?" The reply came from a cold voice. "I trust you did not have too difficult a time." The Silencer cracked a smile at the corner of his lip and whisked a tuft of hair from his face. "You’ve made it easy these last few weeks." His smile fades silently. "So why do you want me here? It’s obvious that you need something, or you never would have allowed the window to open again." The Dastardly One stands up slowly and walks down a few steps. He holds a hand up to the surrounding skyline and waves it across slowly. "This world is not for me Silencer." He sounds broken, like his heart is no longer in it. "I do not know how you have made it this long in the degenerated society." He turns around and looks his enemy in the eyes. "All of this that has transpired…None of it was as I wished." The voice trailed a little. "How do you do it? How did you go all this time and not want for the world to be the best? How did you learn to never hear the adulation of the masses chanting your name?" "Well…" The Silencer began. "I really don’t have an answer for that question. Sometimes I miss it. There are times that I looked back and thought that I would give anything to be like you." He looks down and begins to fidget with a rock on the ground. He eventually settles on picking it up and showing it to his foe. "Over time, I realized that I am just one stone in a world full of mountains." The rock rolled in his hand. "Yet without enough stones, even this monument would have fallen long ago." The Bastard paced a while. Perhaps he was pondering the eventual outcome of tonight’s events. Perhaps he already knew. Deep inside, things had changed, and there was only one man to blame for it. "So you know then." The Bastard noted sullenly. "You know already why I let you find me. I should have figured it out…you were always the smarter of us." "Aye, and you were always more crafty, yet impulsive." So, with smile reserved, The Dastardly One sat down again. He propped his head in his hands, supporting what felt like a tremendous weight. All this he did while sitting next to the man he hated more than any other. Long tense moments passed before the silence was broken. The stars shone through the skylight tonight, a million pinpricks in a vast expanse. "You know that you cannot save me, don’t you?" The awkward tension was broken. "Yes." "You are going to try though aren’t you?" "Yes." "I thought you would say that." He pauses before turning to his arch enemy. "Don’t." Suddenly, The Bastard stands up and rushes forward, oblivious to the world around him or underfoot. He can hear The Silencer running behind him. The fool, he didn’t know when to listen. He makes haste past another monument, this time not stopping to bask in the shadows. Hot on his heels is The Silencer. The pair of Samoan personalities ran for what seemed like hours until they reach a bridge. Below, traffic passes, and the sound of passing motorists fills their senses. The Bastard had put a few steps on his foe, and in the confusion had managed to make his way to the ledge of said bridge. A cautious approach from the good half of the two was enough for the evil party to dangle a foot off the edge. "We aren’t so far alike, you and I." The Dastardly Bastard smiled and looked back at the chaser. "It’s cliché, but so true. Tell me Timo, why do you even follow me? I am giving you what you want!" The face painted warrior shakes his head in disapproval. "No, you are doing that which you have always done. You run from your problems instead of facing them head on like a man." A concerned laugh is the only response he receives. "So you want me to continue living in a world that is unfair and full of people who will only work to destroy what I build?" He guffawed. "No thanks. I’d rather let you have the burden." The Silencer stepped closer, hoping to reason with his mad other half. "You would be leaving me at the ultimate disadvantage!" His response raised a brow from the counterpart. "Think about it Bastard…Without you, there is no reason to be me!" A sudden deep realization crept across the face of The Dastardly One. His other half was right, utterly and completely right. He watched as The Silencer extended a hand and to his own disbelief, he found himself reaching for it. "Now more than ever Bastard, I need you." The Silencer looked without pity, but more remorse for having forced his other half to the side for so long. "Together, we can be stronger than our halves ever would be!" Heh. What a dilemma. On one hand, you got the life you’ve always wanted. Women. Cars. Booze. Fun every night. On the other, you have a crazy bastard with a great point. Aww hell. You already know what you are going to do…don’t you? The two clasped hands, and in an instant, the world changed for Timo Bolamba. He no longer felt the pull of both good and bad in opposite directions. He no longer felt the desire to be something he did not want to be. He simply existed as one and at peace with himself for the first time in a long time. "So, you did find a way to save me, eh little mouse?" A brash voice called out into the night. "I should have known you would." "If you hadn’t left the opportunity for it to happen, it wouldn’t have." A long pause marks a quiet night as Timo looked out into the long dark. He did not know if he would be able to ever be what he once was, but for once, he didn’t care. "Always gotta have a backup plan little mouse." A slight chuckle rekindled The Bastards humor. "You’ve got a lot to learn if we’re gonna be working together now."
