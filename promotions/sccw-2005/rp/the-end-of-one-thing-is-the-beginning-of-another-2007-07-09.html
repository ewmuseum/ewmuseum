'The end of one thing is the beginning of another'

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-9-07 * * * * * 2 Weeks Ago The sound was like that of knuckles cracking, just louder and…juicier. Sinewy popping and tearing, followed by the most eerie and surreal silence that Clinton Sage had ever experienced. It wasn’t long before he fell to his knees beside Isis Hollinger, watching the last of her breath form a small blood-bubble between her lips. The air started to smell metallic, a bit coppery. The blood was everywhere, pouring out of Isis’ head wounds and her icepick-pierced stomach, as well as the corpse of Harlan Raines only a few feet off to the side, his head staining the wooden floor a deep, dark red. It would brown in time, but there would never be any way to get the wooden floor completely clean. Nathan Hollinger breathed slowly, unconscious from the kick Harlan Raines had given him. Clinton knelt there in disbelief, eyes wide with the realization that he had just unexpectedly taken a life – the very life he was trying to save – but he could have sworn he saw Nathan Hollinger’s mouth curl into a momentary smile. The husband remained unconscious, but his lips were moving. Murmuring slightly in his slumber, Nathan Hollinger appeared to be having a conversation with someone. Unable to find the strength to lift himself, Clinton Sage felt comatose. This wasn’t happening. He was supposed to save her. He wasn’t supposed to be the one that killed her. Clinton wasn’t supposed to kill. Ever. "…don’t go..." Nathan whispered, among other unintelligible things. Clinton Sage felt nothing. And not the kind of nothing that is immune to empathy or sympathy, but the kind of nothing that is simply a black void. He didn’t feel empty. He didn’t feel hollow. Not guilty. Not shamed. Not fearful. Not anything. He listened to a husband whisper in his sleep, perhaps dreaming of the wife he would never see alive again. Clinton Sage felt nothing at all. Numb from head to toe, the knees of his pants beginning to soak themselves with Isis’ blood. He tasted nothing in his mouth, and even the coppery scent of the cabin had vanished, along with the chilly air that had been blowing in through the open doorway. No, no, no! It wasn’t supposed to happen that way!, he thought. Clinton’s eyes fell down to Isis’ corpse. Her head was caved completely in on the right, parts of her white skull stabbing outwards through flaps of loose skin. Her right eyeball, once green and beautiful, had been dislocated and hung slightly out of its shattered socket. Blood covered the iris, dripping down past her flattened nose and shattered teeth. The normal side of her face, somehow, remained untarnished by blood. The lifeless gaze of her left eye fell upon her husband, who slept. "…no…" Nathan said, whimpering. Sweat was starting to form across his forehead. His body was twitching. Underneath his eyelids, Nathan Hollinger’s eyes fluttered back and forth. He was dreaming, obviously a nightmare. And then the nothingness vanished, and Clinton Sage stood up suddenly. The machinery started up again. The thought process of how things should be done in order to get himself out of this without any cloud hanging over his head. He would deal with the fact that had taken a life sometime in the near future, but right now he needed to simply handle the situation before him. Self-preservation. Stepping carefully between the pools of blood on the floor, Clinton picked up the baseball bat. He studied the thick splotches of blood on the barrel for a moment, before walking over to Harlan Raines’ corpse and placing the bat in his lifeless hand. Using the sleeve of his shirt, he slowly removed the ice pick from Harlan Raines skull. It made a sickening, nauseating sslllip as it fully exited the wound. A moment later, he placed the murder weapon in Isis’ slowly cooling hand. Stepping backwards, the wave of emotion started to wash over him. The sudden realization that he had gone against everything he had ever promised himself – to never kill someone, to never play god and take a life – was now gone. His life would be changed forever. Pushing those emotions down into the dark recesses of his mind, Clinton Sage ignored the thoughts. Self-preservation. He needed to act quickly. The scene was set up perfectly for when the police arrived. Raines fingerprints all over the icepick, along with the wound in her stomach. The baseball bat in his hands, and her skull distorted because of a crushing blow. The upwards stroke of her last ditch effort to save her own life ended that of her killers. It wasn’t perfect. It wouldn’t even matter, because once the authorities tried to piece together what happened, they would ultimately realize exactly who the man with the hole in his skull was, and realize that he had been executed years prior. It would be silenced, and silenced quickly. From authorities much higher than the Colorado State Police. Picking the unconscious body of Nathan Hollinger up was no easy task, considering his size. Nearly a mirror image of Clinton, it took every ounce of his own strength to maneuver the slumbering widower. Leaving Nathan Hollinger to wake up beside his dead wife was not an option. He was also believed to be dead, and that would only complicate things. He would need to be taken care of. Carefully. Clinton left the cabin through the front door, tossed Nathan Hollinger into the passenger’s seat of the Corvette and carefully drove down the mountain, never once looking back at the bloodbath. The sky was bruised and battered, the clouds heavy and stuffed with snow. They would soon be unleashing a fresh blanket across the mountaintops, and Clinton would wait until a few inches had fallen before calling the police. His tire-tracks needed to be concealed and carelessly run-over by paramedics and detectives. Beside him, Nathan Hollinger was still unconscious. He murmured no more, instead, slept in perfect silence. There was no flickering underneath his eyelids. Clinton’s longtime nemesis wasn’t dreaming. Leaning forward, Clinton Sage programmed the trip-planner in the dashboard for a drive across the country to Pennsylvania. After a few minutes, the course was plotted, and his long journey home was mapped out. Finally, his body relaxed. He took deep breath after deep breath. He could feel the guilt and the sadness continuing to bubble under the surface, but he continued to suppress these emotions. He needed to remain calm, fixated. Once he got home, everything would work itself out. And the nothingness, the all-encompassing numbness, returned and swallowed Clinton whole. * * * * * Today The promo feed jumps in to Clinton Sage sitting in his living room, feet kicked back atop his desk. "So, they keep feeding me the so-called legends of the business. First it was Timo ‘I can’t figure out who I really am’ Bolamba, and now they’re sending me Damien Cruz?" ‘The Seventh Sin’ yawns, and stretches. His back cracks a few times, before he finally rolls his eyes and sits forward in the chair. He doesn’t laugh. He doesn’t yawn again. He just sighs, and continues. "You know what, Damien? Let me cut right to the proverbial chase. You’re yesterday’s news. You might be another two-time FUSE Universal Champion, just like myself, but there is one thing for certain – you won’t be a three time FUSE champion. The only three in store for you will be the referees count, be it slapping the mat after I pin you, or your arm dropping when I choke your spic-ass out." Clinton bobs his eyebrows, and the devilish smile appears. His eyes widen with delight as he continues to rant. Smacktalking into a camera isn’t one of his favorite things to do, but hey, it seems to be the thing to do these days. "You’re nothing but a has-been that continues to think he’s a should-be. You might be named after the son of Satan, but one thing is for sure, when you step into the ring with me, you’re going to be squaring off against the Devil Himself." Waving his hand in the air, Clinton seems annoyed at the thought. "And since you’re such a ‘legend’ I’m sure there is no doubt you’ve heard all of this before. I’m just another asshole who comes out and throws racial slurs in your direction – you beaner – or says that you’re washed up and will never ever feel the warmth of the wrestling spotlight once again. I’m sure people have told you time and time again that you’re going to once again take a stand, and halt the downward momentum that has claimed the last few months of your career. You’re the original John Covel, for crying out loud." He smirks. "Well, those people are stupid. I’m telling you, man-to-Mexican, that you’re going to lose. You might call yourself a FUSE Legend, and I’m happy for you if that’s the case. The fans might call you a FUSE legend, and good for them…it just proves their stupidity matches your own. But there are a few of us who refuse to acknowledge that claim, and let me name them for you…" "EVERYONE ELSE." The FUSE Universal Champion stands up and begins to pace around his study, his hands clasped behind his back. The camera follows, obviously manned by a cameraman this week, rather than Clinton himself, like last week. "Do you think that the FUSE higher-ups, the CEO’s (whoever they are this week), or even the announcers want to acknowledge that you’re some kind of legend? I mean…look at the cold-hard facts, THE COMPANY CANCELLED YOUR ‘LEGENDS MATCH’ BECAUSE NOBODY GAVE A FUCK! And I’d be ashamed at being in a match where my opponent was The Great Bobino, and legendary status was at stake. Thank God they cancelled that snorefest. That was a few months back, but that lack-of-commitment from your bosses has got to really dampen your spirits, no?" Clinton Sage almost snarls, feeding off of his anger. "And when I see weakness, I exploit it. Then I bludgeon it, batter it, and choke it out inside of the squared-circle as your fans realize their legend is nothing of the sort. You’ve got no choice in the matter." Clinton Sage turns, winks, and the camera feed cuts to black. * * * * * Two Days Ago "You OK, man? You look a little weirded out." Lane Stevens asked, either pretending to be concerned or actually showing the emotion for a change. Clinton didn’t respond immediately, instead, he studied the photograph clutched in his hand, ignoring Lane’s attempt at starting a conversation. Usually, times weren’t like this. Lane Stevens continued, "I first noticed it last week. A few days before your bout with The Dumbfuck Samoan. You seemed a little…vacant." An uneasy moment passed in silence. They were both in the limousine, Jeb Stewart nowhere to be found, nor Saul. It was just the two heavies of The Affliction. Lane Stevens adjusted himself in the seat, unsure of how to continue. "Who is this girl? She’s gorgeous." Turning the photograph around, Clinton Sage held it out for Lane Stevens to look at. The River Rat’s jaw dropped for a second, before twisting itself into a look of curiosity. "Where’d you get that?" "I found it in your bag, I was looking for a knee-brace last week. She’s gorgeous. So…who is it?" Lane Stevens could see the difference in Clinton Sage, but he couldn’t put his finger on it. Something had changed recently, and even though it hadn’t affected his performance in the ring, it had certainly added a different dimension to Clinton Sage’s personality. Or lack-there-of, unless you love self-adoration. Which, Lane did. The eagerness to find out the identity of the woman in the photograph was heavy in Clinton Sage’s eyes. Lane could tell that Clinton either had a crush on the woman in the photo, or was simply infatuated with her for another reason. Considering the nature of their work, and her identity, it could be the latter. It probably was the latter. Lane smiled, carelessly flicking his hand towards the picture, "That’s Smitty’s little bitch of a daughter." Clinton’s brow immediately furrowed, and he snapped back quickly, but not stern in tone, "Hey! Don’t call her that…she’s, wow…Smitty’s daughter, huh? Absolutely beautiful." Maybe it wasn’t the latter. Lane felt a bit uneasy, and made himself a cocktail at the small bar in the back of the limo. Clinton went back to staring at the picture, licking his lips ever so slightly in the process. Son of a bitch. He better be thinking of a way to use this against Duluth. * * * * *
