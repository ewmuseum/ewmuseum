Five Stages Of Grief (Prologue)

Date: Today The Day after Temptation: Gateway To SIN It had been a weird night for the Devil’s advocate Legion, once again it was decided that a match featuring could not be broadcast in which Legion decided to turn on Jason Ryan by delivering the Gateway To Hell on the steel chair, he had also burnt Rick Malloy’s face. Mind you in the mind of Gabriel it was due to to Malloy decided to take a unscripted joint out to light which led to the attack. Now Legion had been writing in the hotel room ideas for Sin On Spike, how would he counter von Doom’s attacks, how he would counter the inevitable attack by Jason Ryan and making sure that he kept an eye on the latest war between Craig Maloof’s team and Amy Campbell’s. Legion felt that in the case of war Games he would learn two things: Firstly how to fight in a War Games match as he had one forthcoming in the New Frontier promotion and secondly to keep an eye on Cozen. He had expressed concerns to Lauren Fox a while ago on the inevitable meet between Cozen and Luci, stating that Luci if the rage ever got too much would be even more manic then Cozen herself. As the writing of plans was coming to the end the Blackberry on the desk suddenly vibrated with the caller name saying Charles. For those that don’t know who that is Charles is the Kent Fusiliers representative that introduced Legion back at the Devil’s Advocate show, since then they had kept in touch regarding various events. One event in particular being the piece of paper Legion had found regarding Sin City’s money troubles. Legion then pressed the answer button and began to speak. "Charles, good to hear from you again, I’m guessing you can probably guess what this is about?" Charles nervously replied: " Y-Yes. Legion started to raise his voice: "Why didn’t my match air tonight? Hell why didn’t the MAIN EVENT air? ‘Technical problems’ again?" Charles then whimpered "That’s the official line yes." Legion growled: "Of COURSE that’s the line, hell that’s been the line since the Elite title match had the problem all those months back." Charles then suddenly stopped and then began to say something that Legion probably didn’t want to hear at that particular moment. " Mr Knight, I’d hate to tell you this but your match has also been changed per Mr Kensington’s orders for Sin On Spike. Jason has had to take time off again for family reasons, this now means you will be facing von Doom in a match with a stipulationo falls count anywhere." Legion begins to laugh uproariously "AGAIN?!, Him deciding to not show up until the last minute at the last PPV was one thing but this is going too far, yet I saw Malloy do some freebasing on screen. Plus von Doom’s crazy, he seems to have forgotten the last time I faced him in that environment." "This, Charles is why I want to see Nigel at the Temptation following Sin On Spike, ever since the incident with Lane he seems to have lost his grip, Malloy taking drugs on camera, men being near crippled, it’s pure chaos." Charles then sounded inquisitive in his response: "But Mr Knight didn’t you say you revelled in chaos?" Legion then smiled and replied: "Charles, I only believe in chaos when I’m the one pulling the strings. Make sure the meeting goes ahead." Legion then hung up the phone and smiled, everything was about to fall into place. Meanwhile… The dark night had started to envelop the landscape outside the club Nephilim, the place had many significant memories for her, she had met the person she come to count as a mentor here. The woman known to Sin City fans as Luci4 had decided to walk in after receiving a text message from a friend from her past, she agreed to meet them. When she had entered the club she recognised the face, the last time she had seen the man in question she had him attack an enemy back in New Frontier yet he was never seen on camera. Luci herself was wearing a hoodie containing the old Rev 22:20 logo of the snake wrapping an upside down cross "Stigmata, why did you seek me out now? I set you free after the battle with Teresa, what do you want?" The man drank his whiskey and then got up out of his seat, he then turned round and then exclaimed a thought Luci would find puzzling… "The Master wants you to return to him, do you feel ready?" Luci then removed the hoodie to show a mark of red tears on her left side. "Tell Legion that Sin On Spike will be the day of reckoning.. time to help him set the world ablaze." And Stigmata you shall be at our side, for we are many. (With that we fade out....)
