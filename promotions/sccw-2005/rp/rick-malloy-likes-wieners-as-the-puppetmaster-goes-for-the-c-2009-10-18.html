Rick Malloy likes Wieners as the Puppetmaster goes for the Checkmate

18 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/2009 Thursday 10/16/09 Von Doom’s house, afternoon… Inside my house, Rappin’ Rick Malloy is sitting on my couch ranting and raving about how’s he’s worried about his future in SCCW. He must not have too much faith in me and my schemes. He’s bugging out, probably on drugs, and I’m trying to calm him down so he can stop worrying about his career. "We’ve got nothing to worry about, Rick," I say to Malloy. "None of these people are going to see it coming. Me, you, William, and our new buddy are going to totally take over SCCW. I’ve got all of these fools wrapped around my finger." "What are you talkin’ about, homie?" he replies. "Think about it. None of them view either of us as threats. I mean, you’re a former SCCW Livewire Champion and nobody is scared of you. Isn’t it brilliant? If none of them view either of us as a threat, then they are more apt to not go full out that week and expose themselves for us to sneak in a win." "How the hell do you come up with this stuff?" "I’m the Puppetmaster. It’s my job to make everyone think and do what I want them to do. Right now, my plan is for everyone to think that we’re weak and that we’re not a threat to any of them. That plan is clearly working. I want all of these people to not even think about losing against us. In fact, this will give us in the advantage in long run since no one will expect us to win, they’ll be expecting an easy win. Suckers." "How long have you planned this out?" "Ever since Kensington brought me back into SCCW." "Ok, now that we have a plan, what are we going to do next?" I stroke my chin, plotting and scheming before smiling deviously. Just came up with a brilliant scheme. "I say we lay back. I say we do our own thing and then ease in." Malloy looks amused by this. "Alright." "So let’s just hang back for now and then make some alliances with some of our more like-minded brethren here in SCCW. Some of them will be real, others will be fake to help us complete our agenda." "Yeah, I think that’ll work out nicely." "Then, when we bring in our new buddy, then we can really get the wheels in motion. One or two wins and we can fuck this shit up fast!" Malloy starts laughing out loud. "I love this plan already." "Kind of figured that. But now, in the meantime, we need to find something to do otherwise we’re just going to be sitting here for a long time talking about wrestling and strategy." "But that’s fun." "True, but we haven’t fucked with anyone yet. We haven’t suckered anyone into our trap yet." "I don’t know why people are still dumb enough to fall for it. Of course I’m going to stab them in the back the first chance I get." "All you ever do is lie and everyone falls for it." "You’re right. It’s HILARIOUS! I cause all of this trouble and nobody’s even mad at me. Legion knows me from NFW and even he doesn’t know what I’m capable of doing. I won my first round match in GTT and he probably still thinks it’s the same von Doom who couldn’t win in NFW. It’s mind control and all of the puppets are doing what me, the Puppetmaster, wants them to do. If I want them to think I’m weak, I’ll make them think that. If not, then I’ll sucker them in and show them just how dangerous I really am. It‘s really quite amusing." "You’re awesome like that." "Of course, but I think Mizanin will sue me for gimmick infringement if you keep referring to me as awesome. However, if I had a hot Mexican girlfriend, I’d love to reform the little group they had when they first came into the big organization up north in Stamford." "von Doom, what the hell are you talking about?" "Hmmm…good question." Even I don’t know the answer to that question. I’ve been rocked all week long. It’s been up and down and filled with all sorts of chaos. I’m in GCW and AWE now. The Church of the Unholy is back, the Bermuda Triangle has been formed, and I’ve made it to round two in GTT7. I don’t know what to think. "Well, fuck it. Let’s go play some X-Box games." Malloy’s face lights up upon hearing this. "HELL YEAH! HALO BABY!" He then runs over to the entertainment center to turn the game system on as I smile at the whole scenario. Now I have time to be happy for the first time in ages. * * * The next day… 10/17/2009 After week of shockers and madness, to celebrate my win in GTT7, I get tickets and go to the UNLV/Utah college football game with Rapmaster Rick Malloy and my girlfriend Laurie. The game got boringly one sided so I decided to start talking about the tournament to Laurie while Rick is off getting food. She finally gets sick of it and tells me to stop. "Alright," I tell Laurie. "I’ll stop talking about the tournament." Turning back to the game, I look at them make "What the hell is up with this? UNLV’s sucking ass right now. It was 28-6 at halftime!" "James, that’s not a nice thing to say!" "Well look at the score!" "Look at what’s gone on. Our team sucks worse than the St. Louis Rams!" "You do have a point there." "Our team can’t play offense or defense. We can only play special teams, but not very good. We’re more wide left most of the time. However field goals are all we’re gonna score at this rate." "Be optimistic." "How the hell can I when we’re at the recreation of Little Big Horn. UNLV is Custer and his men. Utah is going to continue this massacre until the game ends." "It’s really not that bad." "Not that bad?" "This is worse than Squideon and Skylar Montgomery combined! It’s over! It’s over!" "James, hush your mouth." As UNLV kicks another field goal, I motion toward the field in a sarcastic fashion and begin speaking in a similar sarcastic fashion. "LOOK! LOOK AT THAT! Another field goal! It’s over, it’s over. Stick a fork in them. They’re done. If we pull this off, it’ll be like a community college beating the University of Florida. It’ll take a miracle to win and I don’t see it happening. It’ll be like me winning the GTT7." "There you go talking about your wrestling again." I shrug my shoulders, looking at her funny while giving her a strange look. I don’t normally get this riled up but damn, I’m not in the mood. "WHAT?!" "You’re making this about your little wrestling gig instead of watching the game?" I turn and motion toward the football field where Utah is again moving the ball down the field against our crappy defense. "What game? All I see is Utah making plays and UNLV just standing around like a bunch of mindless zombies just letting them run all over the field. Then all of these hypocritical, fickle slaves to the system in their little red shirts continue to cheer for this garbage. They’re just as pathetic as the wrestling fans. They both cheer for crap and it makes me very, very sad." I turn to Laurie and she is giving me an incredulous look like she usually does when I say something crazy. "What?" "Are you done ranting?" "I’m not ranting." "You’re not?" I shake my head. "No." "Then what do you call this?" "Monologuing." She shakes her head upon hearing this. "You’re not a super villain, James." "Not yet." Laurie seems rather annoyed with me at this point. "Oh dear lord. You’re ranting and raving! How the hell did I fall in love with you?" I shrug my shoulders, looking on as Utah is about to score another touchdown aginst UNLV‘s shitty defense. "Don’t ask me. I don’t believe it either. Must be something to do with hormones." "Probably so." At this point, Malloy comes back from the food vendor and is carrying a large drink and four hot dogs. He sits down next to us and looks right at me for some reason. I give him a look as he smiles, motioning at the hot dogs. "Hey, either of you two want to munch on some wieners?" Both me and Laurie give him a strange look before turning back to the game. Malloy shrugs his shoulders and sits back down in his seat before chowing down on some hotdogs like a hungry animal. I will give him credit for one thing. He's hungrier than our defense, that's for damn sure.
