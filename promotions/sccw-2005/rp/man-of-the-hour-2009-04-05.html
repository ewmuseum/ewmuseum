Man of the Hour

5 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/09 'This is ridiculous, Phil. Don't worry, I'll get Desade to talk to that old British jerk. We'll take care of this.' Three people sat perched on a king sized bed in Phillip Kennedy's hotel suite. One of them was, naturally, Phillip Kennedy himself. He sat against the headboard in his pajamas, quite understandable since it was something like seven in the morning, a good two or three hours before the Big Stack generally liked to be awake. The second person on the bed was considerably better dressed, having already done important things like showering and brushing her teeth. Lauren Fox barely had a hair out of place, a serious expression on her face as she regarded her two companions. The protestor, naturally, was Kathryn Shaw. She was far less dressed than even her boyfriend, but for the sake of decency, had draped herself in a bedsheet. Sure, she was with her lover and her stepsister, but even the Sex Kitten had occassional notions of decency. Particularly when the topic of conversation was of professional importance. 'Doubtful anything can be done,' Lauren observed. 'Kensington's got the right to make a title match. The fact that it's in the middle of the tournament is a bit suspicious, but not out of the realm of possibility. Besides, I already checked Phillip's contract. He's actually better off than Amy Campbell is. Phil has most of the protections we do, but nowhere in there does it say that Kensington can't put him into matches that aren't designed to punish him.' 'In case you two haven't noticed,' Phillip Kennedy finally pointed out, looking pointedly at his two companions, 'I have my *own* plan for SCCW. And whether Dr. Nigel Kensington knows it or not, he is playing right into my hands.' Both Lauren and Kathryn look at the Gateway Champion, skeptical looks on their faces. 'Forgive me for asking,' Katsidy asked, 'but what's this plan of yours?' It was a rare occassion that Phillip looked even a touch annoyed with his girlfriend. This was one of those occassions; the Stack barely able to hide the glower on his face from her. 'It's wrestling oriented, Kat,' Lauren observed, nodding at Phillip. When one hung around Desade a lot, it was easy to think of plans in terms of blackmail, murder, and other such fun tasks. Phillip Kennedy was a wrestler. And he was quite proud of that fact. 'Kensington's plan is quite good, in theory. Triple threat match, followed by a singles bout. Two chances for me to lose the Gateway Championship. Two good opponents.' The Big Stack smirked at his female companions, and offered a shrug. 'The flaw with his plan is that it's created on the assumption that I'll lose.' Confidence wasn't a quality that Phillip Kennedy could ever really be accused of lacking. 'When I win, I am halfway to the four title defenses that I need to earn a shot at the Universal Championship...which, to be honest, won't matter if I get my title shot in this tournament. Even in the off chance that I'd actually LOSE, I defend the belt two more times and bank another shot. It's a win-win situation for me, because our beloved owner puts far, FAR too much trust into Dusk.' Really, it was sort of ridiculous to Phillip. Take an also-ran from PRIME, put him in SCCW, and Kensington creamed over him as some kind of savior. Well. If that was Nigel's best hope, Kennedy would be more than happy to shatter it. 'I still don't get what your plan is, Philly-bear,' Katsidy responded, her tone slightly harsh in spite of the affectionate term. 'My plan is win. My plan is show Kensington that he's fucked because he can't use the sort of shit he'd like to against us. He has to deal with us in the ring...and the point I have made against Edward Lambert and Sean Sterling is that when it comes to me, he can't. There's no one in this company that can beat me, one on one, one on one on one, whatever. I am the future of SCCW. I can cheat for myself, or with the aid of you two. I don't need the 82nd Airborne. Amy Campbell does, but I don't.' Both Savant and Katsidy simply looked at him. To some extent, he was right. Even in the beginning, Phillip Kennedy had been a very resourceful man. 'So your plan is...beat Dusk and Face Eater? That sounds...boring.' Kathryn ran a hand through her hair, bouncing a little bit on the bed, which in turn made her breasts bounce underneath the sheet. 'He's with us to win,' Lauren pointed out. 'That's what he does well. He's the King of the Hand for a reason.' Even if, Savant noted, that nickname had been self-appointed. Bridden called himself the Ace..though that nickname had lost some of its luster considering Jadian's Livewire Title loss to Legion. Mayhem had, once upon a time, been the Wild Card, an entirely accurate name. Phillip's name was somewhere in between. He might well have been the King of the Hand in the squared circle...but if so, he was uncrowned, because the crown was in the possession of one Aimz. 'I know that,' Kat replied, 'but I really do like the plans where we all run in and beat on people, or ruin their lives. Those are the most fun!' Another reminder for Kennedy that, no matter how pretty and good in bed Kathryn Shaw was...she was a sociopath. So was Lauren. So was Desade. Hell, by most definitions right now, so was he. 'Phillip is being prudent,' Lauren interjected. 'Kensington is showing no qualms about evening the odds where he can. Staying within the rules -- well, as much as he will, anyway --preempts that. Nigel won't respond to a chain pulled out of Phil's pants -- he might if Phil wins due to a seven on one beatdown.' Kennedy shot Lauren a grateful smile. He was thankful that, at the very least, *she* seemed to be approving of his direction. As much as he loved Kathryn, he had always seen Lauren as his strategic advisor in the group, the woman that helped him think on the level needed to be a member of the Dee Em Aich. 'I prove myself with every victory. Soon, there will be none who can deny me,' Phillip Kennedy proclaimed. None save Desade, of course. The threesome continued chatting for several more minutes, until Lauren Fox and Kathryn Shaw locked eyes, sharing a look that they had shared many, many times before. The look that said 'we need to talk.' It didn't take much to get Kennedy out of the room, just a whispered, 'I'm sorry, lover, but my sister and I need to talk. Could you give us a little bit?' Phillip Kennedy took only a scant five minutes to get dressed and head out of the room. Where he was going was, for most purposes, utterly unimportant. It was only once the door had shut and locked that Kathryn began to voice her concerns. Was he really ready, for example? Could he handle two admittedly strong talents by himself, or with minimal help? He wasn't as good at planning as she was, or Savant was. Didn't Lauren agree? 'No,' Savant said, voice firm. 'I don't agree with you.' Lauren Fox looked at her stepsister. This wouldn't be the first time that Lauren and Kathryn disagreed, and it almost certainly wouldn't be the last. 'I understand your concerns,' the Orchid said, 'but he's ready. If this was something outside of the ring, I'd agree with you, but this is wrestling. When it comes to anything that's inside the four ropes, Phillip is the best we have.' Kathryn went to retort, but Lauren continued on. 'I wouldn't have believed this a year ago, when you lured him to us...but he's good, Kathryn. Very good. And he's only been in the sport less than a year. He's the Gateway Champion. He's beaten people like Edward Lambert and Sean Sterling with minimal help. He took Desade to her absolute limit. I think that if she stays her hand, he can beat Amy Campbell.' Both of them knew which 'she' the purple-haired woman was talking about, but it had fallen out of vogue to mention her affectionately. 'So do I,' Kathryn admitted. 'I guess when you put it that way...' Fox stood from the bed, her face serious. 'It's more than that, though. He's better than Amy, now. He might be better than Hunter was as we speak, and if not, he'll be there soon. He might even, with more experience, be better than Pierce is. From an in-ring standpoint, he is the best weapon that we have. He's perfectly willing to be deployed...and we're not deploying him.' 'Probably because he's got man bits. Pity, that's one of my favorite things about him.' Quip aside, Kathryn knew Alexandra Pierce as well as anyone. Looking at Alexandra Pierce's inner circle, two members of which were in the room right now, one found a distinct lack of Y chromosomes. 'Partially, yes,' Lauren admitted. 'It's not just that, though, it's Desade's...special relationship with Amy that makes things more problematic. Deploying Kennedy right now would send him roughshod over SCCW...including Amy. I know you've probably noticed, but Amy and Phillip don't like each other.' Understatement of the year, that one. 'I'm pretty sure she knows how good Phillip is,' Kat replied. 'Probably third in her mind, to herself and Amy. But she'd much rather have Amy be champion than Phillip. She trusts Amy. Cares about her. Far too much, if you ask me.' 'That's what Phil's worried about most. You saw the show last week. He's confident he can beat anyone, with the exception of Amy Campbell backed by Desade. Without Desade involved, he thinks he can steamroll Amy.' Shaw nodded her agreement. 'He does. That's one of the reasons I like him so much. Good solider, confident, and cute to boot. Long as he doesn't find out about my other activities, everything's going to be fine.' Lauren Fox looked at her sister, and nodded. 'There are two things that would drive him away from us. That's one of them.' 'And the other,' Kathryn asked, as if on cue. It didn't take Lauren Fox long to think of the answer. It was, ironically enough, something Phillip himself had suggested solely to piss off the Red Raver. Something that he couldn't possibly have known the truth of. 'If he finds out that Desade actually IS fucking Amy,' the Orchid stated, 'I think we'd have to take him out. My concern would be the amount of damage he could cause before that happens.' Phillip Kennedy, when it came to the Dead Man's Hand, wasn't playing with a full deck. He only knew certain cards in play. Behind the scenes were a wide variety of sordid secrets and information that he likely only had the vaguest idea of. 'What do you mean, damage,' Kathryn asked. 'First of all, I am positive I can control him. The boy's quite addicted, if you catch my drift. Not that I can blame him...' The look Lauren shot her step-sister inspired the Sex Kitten to get on to the substantive portion of her statement. 'And second, he's a wrestler! If worse comes to worse, we take him out off-camera, away from the prying lights...' 'You're assuming he finds out in a way that's possible,' Fox responded. 'If he finds out on camera...he's going after Desade, or Amy, or possibly both. If he gets to Desade with a weapon in his hands, you don't think an angry 6'4' man could cripple her given half a chance, especially if she doesn't see it coming? Not to mention the fact that means we'd lose him. We've just been talkng about what he can be, what he could mean to us.' 'So, what do we do then,' Shaw asked. 'I can always try and wrap him a bit tighter around my finger...' That idea seemed quite nice to her, really, but sadly, Savant shook her head. 'The opposite actually might be prudent,' she replied. 'He loves you, but he does resent that you treat him like a kid sometimes. He's not a rookie anymore. Phillip is capable of being Universal Champion. Treat him like it a bit more, and he'll only want you even closer.' Kathryn Shaw nodded. It was a bit out of the box, but likely to be effective. Katsidy was little if not out of the box when it came to solutions to problems. 'Ultimately, we're in charge of him, really. Desade is...busy, for the most part. With Amy, and with a lot of other things. As long as we manage this properly, he'll follow us into the depths of Hell.' Lauren Fox was pretty sure that she and her stepsister could pull this off...but there was always the risk otherwise. Always the risk Kennedy would find out, and they would have a powerful opponent lined up against them. Life, though, was much better with risk. Facing potential problems and overcoming them was much more fulfilling than a difficulty-less romp. Besides, if things were easy, Lauren Fox and Kathryn Shaw would be entirely unneeded. ------------------------------- 'Are you serious?' Phillip Kennedy asked, looking across a desk dubiously at the man put in charge of his career. For his part, David Walter Smith remained stoic, merely nodding his head once. 'Your success in Sin City has attracted no shortage of suitors. Particularly considering the terms of your contract. You have a one year deal with possible exit clauses that I insisted be put in the contract, primarily in case of issues with the Dead Man's Hand. If you wanted, I could make a very good case for you to get out of your contract here and now. That is not, however, what I am suggesting. What I *am* suggesting, Mr. Kennedy, is that you think about this for the future.' The names of the federations on the various pieces of paper in front of Kennedy were unimportant, though if nothing else, Phillip was surprised by the amount and name value of the companies. 'Why all the attention,' Phillip asked, letting a smirk curve his mouth. 'I'm an asshole working for the biggest group of assholes in the sport. For all I know, Alex could authorize me to go over to one of them and build my own Hand cell and take the place over.' David nodded. 'Yes. Such is a possibility. In my opinion, the reasons you are being pursued are myriad. You are a rookie talent in the sport with as much potential as anyone. You have survived almost a year in one of the toughest environments in the sport, allegiance with Alexandra Pierce or not. Quite honestly and frankly, the sky is the limit for you. I am not suggesting that you break your contract, or ask me to find you a way out. What I AM asking is that you at least think about the possibility of leaving when your contract expires in several months.' Kennedy's facial expression went stony, as he sighed at the man in command of most of his out of the ring affairs. 'And you decided to bring this up to me now, on the eve of the most important match of my career?' Smith replied evenly, 'Yes. That is my job. Just as it is well within your job description to tell me to shove these offers up my ass until after No Limit is over. I am your agent, I relay all offers to you.' Phillip chuckled a bit. 'I think I'll be invoking that right, to be quite honest. I really can't see myself anywhere but Sin City, though I'm sure you'll tell me to keep an open mind.' 'You know me all too well,' Smith stated, with a wave of his hand. 'Though if your opinion remains the same, Dr. Kensington is fortunate to have you around, even if your aim is dominance of his company. Most young talents would flee the place in a heartbeat for a supposedly better promotion, or more money.' Kennedy knew that. Many talents scheduled to debut for SCCW had either no-showed or refused to sign, often showing up in PRIME or GCW, or other notable promotions around the world. 'Most people can't handle SCCW,' Kennedy said. 'Can't handle the Dead Man's Hand, or can't handle being a *member* of the Dead Man's Hand.' That last bit was important. Being in the Hand was not sunshine and roses, as Phillip had found out. It meant dealing with secretive people, and a leader whose reasoning was all her own. It also meant dealing with Amy Campbell, the Universal Champion, and queen bitch who thought she was hot shit. 'Other places pay more, and have better TV coverage, but Sin City's home. Been working for a year to get respect and show that I'm the best in this company. I'm going to become Universal Champion and run this place on behalf of myself and the Hand.' '...noble aspirations, those,' the once-Logic responded. 'Nonetheless, I can appreciate the desire to finish what one has started.' A glance at Kennedy was enough for Logic to know that Phillip's interest in leaving was nil. Money was unimportant to the Big Stack now. So was fame, and the prestige of being in a so-called 'big promotion.' Sin City Championship Wrestling was Phillip Kennedy's home. It was the environment that he was struggling to master. And if Phillip Kennedy had anything to say about it, Dusk and the Illustrious Face Eater were just two more speed bumps on his path to mastery of Sin City. -------------- Carrying two large pink bags, one in each of her slender hands, Kathryn Shaw was starting to wonder if she'd ever quite figure out what Victoria's secret happened to be. She figured that she'd nearly set her credit card on fire enough times in the lingerie establishment that she was entitled to know. Kathryn counted herself lucky, for the most part. Certainly, affairs with Alexandra Pierce were often more complicated than she would have liked, but Shaw loved her work. It helped that often, said work involved her sleeping with a variety of attractive men. Granted, sometimes, her targets weren't quite so attractive, but into every life a little rain and all that. Phillip was her current project, and no matter what other business she had, in many ways her most important. Things with Desade were...tenuous to say the least, and if she needed to go to battle, it would help having a man of Kennedy's stature and ability in her corner. She was tempted to call him, but decided against it. Knowing how serious he'd been lately, he was probably in the gym, getting in a final workout before his match. The thought of him half-naked and sweaty brought a wry smile to her face. He was in love with her, that much was certain. She couldn't *quite* say the same, but even so, she did quite enjoy his company. Phillip was good to talk to, looked quite nice on her arm, and was a more than capable companion in bed. If she closed her eyes, he was reminding her more and more of the man she'd seconded to greatness, Hunter Sabuani. Phillip wasn't quite there yet, but his confidence and ability in the ring were growing by leaps and bounds. Initially, he'd been arrogant, but it was mostly a shield, an act, words projected to convey a point. Now, Phillip Kennedy believed what came out of his mouth. Believed that he could back up every word. She hated Amy Campbell as much as Desade loved her. If this was the only way Phillip could get a crack at the Red Raver...she would support him as much as she could to make sure the two met for the Universal Title. Another image replaced that of the man known as 'Peerless'. This time, it was her wrapped around the tired yet victorious body of her most frequent lover, SCCW Universal Championship belt raised triumphantly in his left hand. At that thought, she couldn't resist the urge to call him, pushing the single button that would speed-dial the Big Stack. Two rings, and he picked up, unable to see the positively wicked smirk on Kathryn's face. 'Hi Philly steak,' she drawled. 'Sorry for interrupting you, but I just went shopping. I'm curious...what would you like me to wear to bed tonight for our victory celebration? Here are the options...' The rest of the conversation was the sort of thing most people pay $2.50 a minute for.
