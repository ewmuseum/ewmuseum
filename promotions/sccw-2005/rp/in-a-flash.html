In a Flash

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/2008 "The future is the past returning through another gate." –Arnold Glasgow "This, this is going to be uncomfortable isn’t it?" "We’re both professionals, we both have a job to do" "My job is to deliver your message to the masses, tell people who you are and why you’re here, what exactly is yours?" Nervous chuckle "My job? My job is to be ‘The Foundation’, to be Tony Pride, the past, the present, and maybe just maybe David Gibson, the Future." It’s weird when two old friends, whom are enemies, but not really, get together. Tony Pride and Dave Gibson on the air in FUSE have not been friends, but there was a time, when they were. When they traveled the highways and byways of this country together chasing a dream. Both of them realized their dream, in two different circles, but dreams realized nevertheless. Now they sit across from each other, Tony Pride in an open collared white button down shirt, black slacks and black dress shoe sandals. Dave Gibson across from him in a FUSE polo shirt and blue dress pants. They sit in two plush black love seats, with the FUSE logo backdrop behind them. Both men adjust their microphones and stare at each other uncomfortably. The lights flash… FLASH "You don’t know how much this means to me Prince..." Tony Pride nervously scribbles down an address on the hotel room stationery. "1636, West Fox St., you say a tan building, what’s the apartment number?" His face flushed, but with excitement, his leg nervously twitches as he scribbles quickly, his head pressing the phone against his shoulder "781, got it Prince. Son, I don’t want to sound presumptuous, but what’s brought this on, I mean, this is what I want, but…" Pride listens intently, gone is the arrogant façade, gone is the always self assured smile, this is a father, whose son has finally reached out his hand, this isn’t the "Foundation", this is just Dad. "…Thanks Prince, I’ll be there after the interview with Dave, and Prince…" Pride takes a deep breathe "…I love you Son." Tony Pride, Dad, hopes his son heard those words FLASH Gibson: Welcome FUSE fans to an exclusive FUSE.com interview with the one and only "Foundation" Tony Pride! Pride nods at Gibson Gibson: Tony, great to have you here, we’ve both agreed to put our differences aside for the sake of this interview. Nervous chuckle Pride: Dave, we don’t have any differences, you’re a washed up wrestler whose only claim to fame is to call the action of those of us still capable enough to do this, and me? Well, nobody needs to be reminded of who I am, because I’m the Foundation, I’m Tony Pride. Pride smirks. Gibson, barely restrains himself, he clutches the love seat and stares at Pride for a moment, before recoiling back into the chair. Gibson: So we’ll cut to the chase then, you’re not the prototypical FUSE wrestler. FUSE is built on youth and ability, you are a man whom has spent nearly 3 decades in this business, the question on everyone’s mind is, why are you back, and why FUSE? Pride leans back and stares up at the lights. Pride: Quite honestly David, I’m 49 years old, I’ll be 50 in July. I wrestled my first match in Norman, Okalahoma on Easter Sunday, 1978. I wrestled a 15 minute Broadway against a man by the name of Jerry Pectorol… Gibson smiles and waxes nostalgic. Gibson: The Peckster! Pride chuckles and then stops. Pride: Yes, but don’t cut me off again David. Awkward silence Pride: As I was saying, I wrestled a 15 minute draw against Peckster, the owner of the promotion whom decided he wanted to make me the "Future" of his company, but only after he used my superior talent to make his inferior talent, look otherwise. Pride pauses Pride: And I did that, I did that then, I did that in 1988, I did that in 1998, and guess what David, and I’m still capable of doing it in 2008. You and everyone else ask why am I back? For one reason and one reason only…Redemption! Gibson: Redemption? Redemption for what? Pride nervously chuckles and stops, and states the following matter of fact tone. Pride: My life! FLASH Stuttering "So, Prince, listen, I’m sorry about what happened between me and your mother, and, well, I’m definitely as sorry for the way you found out about your sister, but, but, I’m the sorriest for You and I, I mean, you’re my son, you’re my Prince, you’re…" Pacing "No, no, damnit, that won’t work, too corny." Multi-time world champion, lived his entire adult life in front of the cameras, but in the hallway of this run down apartment building, he’s the "Foundation", he’s Tony Pride, and he’s, rehearsing. Rehearsing what he’s going to say to his estranged son, now that the opportunity has finally come, now that Prince has finally opened the door of forgiveness. He knows he won’t get another chance at this, hell; he’s surprised he’s getting this one. "Get yourself together Tony!" Self slap to the face "He called you, remember? He’s the one whom invited you over for grilled cheese and tang, he’s your son, and he’s finally ready to accept you, for you. You’ve finally arrived Tony, you’re family" Pride chokes up a little, his face and voice in disbelief "You’re family, reunited, no strings attached, no more drama, no more hate, come on, you can do this, you’re the "Foundation", you’re…" A ear to ear smile, genuine emotion, genuine Pride "…Daddy!"
