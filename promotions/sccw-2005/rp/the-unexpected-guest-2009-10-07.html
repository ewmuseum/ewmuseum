Date: 10-7-09 Jadian sat in the living room of the hotel room, a tall glass of soda in his hand as he stared at the television. An old NCIS episode was on, but he was only half listening to what was going on. Julissa was on the couch adjacent to him, both legs curled beneath her as she leaned against the arm of the couch. These are the moments that he usually longs for. The two of them alone, stripped free of the pressures of working for Desade, and just being able to lounge around and be comfortable for a change. He was wearing a pair of black basketball shorts that extended to just above his knees, the burnt orange stripe running down the side matched the Longhorns tee shirt he had on. Juli had on a pink Tony Parker jersey, and a matching pink thong. He loved the fact that she could look hot in something as simple as a basketball jersey. Not to mention the sex was awesome. He brought the glass to his lips and took a sip, still surprised at what she just told him. "It's really hard to imagine her as human." "Yeah, that's nice," she replied, still kicking her self over letting the subject of her conversation with Alex slip. As awkward as the whole thing was, she was rather proud that someone considered her relationship with Jadian as a success. "I'm sure we'd both die in our sleep if it ever came out though, so I suggest you keep this between the two of us." "I don't think anyone would believe me anyway," he laughed softly, his head shaking as he leaned forward in the recliner and put his glass on the coffee table in front of him. "I may as well be telling people that I met the Easter Bunny on my way to tell Santa Claus what I want for Christmas." Julissa tilted her head and gave him a twisted little grin. "People do crazy things when they are in love, and Alex is smitten." "Smitten?" Jadian's brow wrinkled as he turned to face her. "Did someone learn a new word today?" "Oh, go to hell," Julissa tossed the small pillow that was behind her at him, but he managed to swat it out of the air before it hit him. "I'm just saying," he watched the pillow fly past his drink, missing it by mere inches. "Who the hell uses the word smitten." "I just did, you jerk." she said with a firm jaw. "And you sounded stupid saying it," he threw his arms up in defense as her legs stretched out and she stood from the chair to charge at him. "Okay! OKAY!" She made her way to him rather quickly, wrestling with his arms as she straddled him on the seat. The playful jostling soon turned to a passionat ekiss that had his hands exploring every inch of her body. He pulled his mouth away from hers for a moment, just long enough to say something that made her heart race... "I think I'm smitten with you." * * * * * * Charles Slaton sat alone in his car, a hot cup of coffee keeping his hands warm as he watches the air escape his lungs and turn to frost right before his eyes with each exhale. He had been out here for quite a few hours, and was about ready to give up when Jadian and Julissa finally returned. He wasn't sure how he had lost them, but Jadian drove like he knew how to shake a tail. They both did. This job had become a nightmare from the first day he had tried to tail her, it was as if she was always trying to make sure no one was foillowing her. Whether it was something she did naturally, out of fear of some childhood memory or because of who she associated herself with, he was unsure. He did know that she travelled a lot. Not just because of the little wrestling promotion she was a part of either. It was as if she led a double life. One time he thought he had seen a woman walk out of a restroom that vaguely reminded him of her, but her nose was bigger and she had straight black hair. Staring at that woman must have distracted him long enough for Julissa to sneak out, because he never saw her walk out and there were no windows for her to sneak out of. This woman was very sneaky. At times he felt he was following Jason Bourne, the way she semed to disappear at will. Still, when he told her father, Benito Minorez, that they were in New Mexico, Benny told him that he would make the trip over. He wasn't sure why, but the man seemed adamant about meeting him here. A loud rapping on the passenger side window caused him to spill a bit of his coffee in his lap. He startled, the heat searing his testicles as he cursed under his breath. Fuck! He screamed internally, his eyes shut tightly as he exhaled. Another tap against the glass caught his attention, and he turned to find Benny Minor standing right outside the door. He was pointing at the lock, motioning for Slaton to unlock it. He did. Benito took hold of the handle, lifting it slightly to disengage and pull it open. He slid onto the seat, pulling the door closed behind him before turning to face the private detective with a smile. "Sorry about that," he motioned toward the wet stain on Slaton's pants. "Don't be," Slaton replied. "I like to make sure I still have feeling down there every once in a while." "I really hope you're kidding," Benny said with a slight chuckle. "There's better ways to test it." "Yeah, I think I'll look into that." Slaton reached between the seats and toward the back, retrieving a manilla folder that he placed on Minorez's lap. "You might want to take a look at that. Your daughter is a tough nut to crack, Mr. Minorez." "Tell me something I don't know," he answered as he flipped the folder open. "Like why." "I don't know," he really had no clue how he would be able to answer that question when he was barely able to follow the woman. "It's not like I've had a chance to walk up and ask her, you know." "Yeah," Benny replied as he flipped through a few pages, glancing over notes and speed reading highlighted portions of text. "If it was that easy to figure out I wouldn't have hired you." "Well, everything is in there," he pointed to the folder in Benny's lap. "Well, as much as I could figure out, at least." "You've done a great job from the looks of it." "I just wish I knew what you are going to do with this information," Slaton glanced over and saw Benny's eyes scanning the page at a rapid pace, absorbing the information like a sponge. "It's not like it explains what is going on in her life." "Oh, it says plenty," Benny answered as he flipped the folder closed and tapped it against his lap. "Hell, I wrote the damn notes and I don't understand half of them." "That's because you don't know Alexandra Pierce like I do." Charles could see the look of a worried parent in Benny's eyes. "That woman could convince Satan to sell her half of hell if she wanted." "She can't be that bad," Charles said with a soft laugh. Benny did not even crack a smile when he replied. "No, she's worse." "So what do we do now?" "We don't do anything," Benny turned to face him. "All I need to know is what room they are in." * * * * * * Jay and Juli were cuddling up on the couch when a knock on the door drew their attention away from the television. "You expecting someone," Jadian asks. "Oh yeah, I told a few of my girlfriends to come by so we could take turns fucking you raw." She peels herself away from him, wiping away a bit of sweat from her side where the two of them had been touching. She reaches down and grabs the jersey Jadian had tossed to the floor when he pulled it up and over her head. She slips it on, tucking her panties under the couch with her foot before standing up. "Is it my birthday already?" "Just put on your shorts and go answer the door." She grabs her hair and pulls it back into a ponytail, using her teeth to stretch a rubber band that was dangling like a bracelet around her wrist so she can tie her hair back. "Yeah, yeah," Jay pulls on his shorts, leaving his shirt on the ground as he walks over to the door. Another knock is heard before he calls out to whoever it is. "I'm coming...Actually, you're lucky I already came." He grabs hold of the knob, twisting the lock to disengage the bolt and pull the door open. "Am I now?" The cocky grin, that cocky grin, slowly fades from his face as he stares at someone he was not expecting to be standing on the other side of the door. "You going to take that chain off the door and let me see my daughter, or are you two not done playing house?" "Uhm," Jadian is beside himself. He turns back toward Juli, his hand on the chain that keeps the door cracked slightly. "Juli..." "What?" she asks the question, surprised by the paleness of Jadian's skin as he stands there staring at her. "Well, who is it?" "It's your father," Benny bellows out from the other side of the door. Now she understands why Jadian had a look of fear on his face. As much of an asshole as he was, he had a hell of a ton of respect for the man on the other side of the door. The man had not only trained him, taught him everything he knew, but he treated him like he was part of the family. Now here he was, doing everything but knocking up his only daughter. This was going to be awkward. Even moreso if she couldn't find her panties. "Open the door," she looks at Jadian as she walks toward the bedroom. "Give me a few minutes to put something a bit more decent on." "You really want him sitting on that couch?" "Just let him in." "If he kills me," Jadian does not have a smile on his face. "You're telling Alex." "Yeah, fine, whatever." She closes the door behind her, sepearating herself from the family reunion she wasn't expecting for just a little bit longer. Fuck.
