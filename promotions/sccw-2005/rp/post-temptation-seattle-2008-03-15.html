Post Temptation/Seattle

15 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/2008 What separates the veterans of the sport of professional wrestling from the rookies? The vets know when its time to call it a night. They know after a big win that it’s okay to enjoy the moment but their attention is soon on to the next match, the next town, the next opponent. They return to the solitude of their hotel rooms, make sure they get up extra early so they can hit the gym, watch every piece of video of their opponent in order to gain some insight and hatch their next game plan. The rookies would win a championship and paint the town four shades of red…staying up all night long drinking and socializing with the fans, especially the females ones until the next thing they know they are getting the rude awakening of the front desk reminding them that check out is in thirty minutes. The vets have lived this lifestyle and realize that with their advancing years combined with the younger and stronger talent that is breathing down their necks that this sort of rock and roll lifestyle will take its toll on you sooner than later. They have to keep their edge or they will soon be replaced by that younger, hungrier talent. This describes most of the older guys and most of the younger ones as well. But you can throw this rule out the window when you are talking about The Black List…and the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde in particular. Shortly after The Black List secured the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championship it was reported that the group hit the Emerald City to sample the nightlife. After checking out several of the "hot spots" in the city buying rounds of drinks and drawing crowds wherever they went, the group started to go their separate ways, all but the Wyld Child that is. Not to be outdone by any rookie…Wylde continued to pound southern comfort and cokes, alternating that with shots of Jagermeister, with several young ladies all clamoring to get close to this mysterious man. Before you can realize it the bartenders and waitresses are informing the partying group that it is last call. When you are the Wyld Child you can’t let a little thing like local liquor laws get in the way of your party. So after saying some good byes Wylde picks out two of the hottest women in the club and heads out to his waiting limo. Then it’s off to his luxury suite at the Hyatt Regency Hotel. It is here in the lobby of the Hyatt we find SCCW interviewer Kendall Heath. Heath strides through the lobby with a purpose, pausing only for a few moments to check his hair and brush off his suit before resuming his stride to the front desk. Desk Clerk: Welcome to the Hyatt Regency Sir. How may we assist you? Heath flashes that cocky grin of his as he takes his hand and slides something across the desk toward the clerk. Heath: I need to know what room a friend of mine is staying in. We were out on the town and we got separated he told me I could crash with him tonight instead of going home to the wife and getting myself in more trouble if you know what I mean. Desk Clerk: I’m sorry Sir. I have had no instruction by any of our guests to release any such information. Heath moves his hand away and leaves a crisp new $100.00 bill on the marble counter top. Heath: I’m sure this might help you point me in the right direction to my friend? Desk Clerk: Sir, our guests have an expectation of privacy and that does not have a price I assure you. Let alone the possibility of losing my employment. If you don’t have any other business that I can help you with… Heath becomes annoyed that his bribe was refused swipes the money off of the counter and stuffs it back into his perfectly pressed pants and walks away in a huff. He plops himself down onto a white leather sofa facing the front entrance to gather his thoughts. Heath: Wylde is here and I’m not leaving until I talk to him. Meanwhile upstairs on the top floor the party has continued. More alcohol has been consumed and now the next part of the evenings activities are about to begin. Wylde leans over whispers something to one of the ladies, she giggles and nods in agreement, gets up and walks into the other room. Before disappearing she slips the straps of her black mini dress from her shoulders and allows it to drop to the floor, showing off her black lace thong in the process. Wylde then leans over to the second lady placing his hand on the small of her back as he whispers into her ear. She seems surprised by his request…almost repulsed …yet after a few moments longer of whispering in her ear she can not deny Wylde’s request. She two gets up and heads for the same door the first lady disappeared behind. A little more shy than her friend she keeps her clothes on as she enters the other room. Wylde leans back into the black leather chair he was sitting in and smiles to himself. After a few moments he gets up pours himself another shot of Jagermeister and downs it and then heads to the other room as well. Back downstairs Heath is still trying to figure out what room Wylde is staying in. He sinks down into the fine leather couch and pulls out his cell phone and dials the number to the hotel. Desk Clerk: Hyatt Regency front desk, how may we assist you? Heath: (trying to disguise his voice) Uh yeah…this is CEO Smitty T. Duluth of Sin City Championship Wrestling. I’m trying to reach one of my wrestlers. I believe he is staying in your fine establishment. His name is Zakk Wylde. I need to send over some important documents, I need his signature on them its very important. What room is he staying in again? Desk Clerk: I’m quite sorry Sir. Our guests have an expectation of privacy and Mr. Wylde left us specific instructions not to be disturbed this evening. His plan thwarted again he slams his phone shut hanging up on the desk clerk. Upstairs in Wylde’s suite the glass door to the shower opens and steam comes rolling out into the room filling it up. Out steps Zakk Wylde, he grabs a towel and wraps it tightly around his waist. Wylde: Ladies I’ll be back in a moment. Please don’t stop on my account. Keep each other company until I return okay? Back downstairs in the lobby Kendall Heath is still trying to figure out how he is going to get his interview with Zakk Wylde. Suddenly the answer to his question comes walking through the front door. Two buxom blondes in short skirts, silk tops and ridiculously high heel pumps walk into the lobby, pause at the front desk and then head for the elevators. Heath leaps to his feet and tries to be sly and arrive at the elevator just as the two blondes do. He holds the door open and motions ladies first. Heath follows the ladies in and settles into the back of the elevator. The two ladies are whispering and giggling with each other but its nothing that Heath and make out. The one pushes the button for the top floor. BINGO thinks Heath, now I just have to figure out how to follow them off of the elevator without seeming like a stalker. After a few moments the bell dings and the doors slide open on the top floor. Blonde #1: Are you here to see Zakk Wylde also? Heath: Uh…oh yeah. He is an old friend. I know it’s late and all but he said to stop by anytime. Blonde #2: Yeah…he seems really cool that way. The trio exits the elevator Heath lags behind the women as not to arouse Wylde’s suspicion. The blondes knock on the door and wait patiently. Inside the room Wylde comes out from the bathroom still wrapped in his towel, water dripping off of his sculpted upper body. He hears the knock at the door and takes the detour from his original plan on getting another shot and answers the door. Wylde: Ladies. I’m so glad you decided to come over. The more the merrier I always say. Wylde holds the door open and the ladies look at each other and giggle as they cross the threshold into the suite. Wylde: Make yourself at home, the bar is still open get whatever you want I’ll be right with you. Blondes: (in unison) Thank you….we will… Wylde stands there in the door way admiring the view as the ladies make their way to the bar. Before he shuts the door he catches something out of the corner of his eye. He turns around and comes face to face with Kendall Heath. Wylde: Damn!!! Does Duluth make you all work 24/7? How did you know I was up here? Heath: Just follow the hot and hotter blondes and you will find what you are looking for. Wylde chuckles to himself and shakes his head. Wylde: I guess you’ve got me there. I’m sure this hotel doesn’t see too many ladies that dress the way they do huh? Heath: I’m sure they don’t and if they do they are pretty high class escorts like the ones that the former Governor of New York was banging. I hope you don’t mind me crashing your party. With that Heath tries to walk into the room and Wylde places his wet hand onto Heath’s Armani suit. Wylde: Whoa cowboy. Where do you think you are going? This is a very….very…private party. There are only two types of people in this party. One is to be one of those hotties over there, which you aren’t. The other is to be the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde…and let me tell you brother there is only one Zakk Wylde and that is me! So if you will excuse me I’m just a little busy here. Heath: I guess I can appreciate that. Could you at least give me a quick interview so that my whole night isn’t a total waste? Wylde: I guess…but make it quick. I hate to keep the women waiting. They tend to start without me and I hate to miss that kind of show. Heath: So earlier tonight you and Caleb won the SCCW Strength in Number championship…I can see you are celebrating the victory…what’s it like to score your first championship in SCCW? Wylde: Well I guess to some this would look like a celebration worthy of a championship victory but I think what several of you all in SCCW still don’t understand about me is this is just another day in the life of Zakk Wylde. I would have had the same ladies here looking to take a walk on the Wyld Side if we would have lost the championship. We all knew that wasn’t going to happen but if by some miracle we hadn’t life would still continue. As far as what it feels like to have my first taste of gold here…it feels damn good! I’m not going to lie. I didn’t come out of the gates like I wanted to and this was the match that I and the rest of the Black List was looking for. Heath: So after winning this championship most people would want to take a week off to survey the landscape and plan there next move but I understand you have asked for a singles match next week? Wylde: Damn right. I think the way Smitty put it was no rest for the wicked. I don’t rest on my accomplishments I step right back into the line of fire and keep this ball rolling in the right direction. Heath: In the line of fire is an interested choice of words since you will be going up against a man that considers himself a terrorist Andre Jensen. Wylde: Ah yes…the English Terrorist. How intimidating. I don’t know what to do. He might hurt me with some bobby trapped fish and chips….or spiked earl grey tea. Heath: You sound like you are overlooking Jensen? Wylde: I respect his ability in the ring, I kid because I can. I just want to let Mr. Jensen know that I’m not intimidated and WON’T be terrorized in San Jose. The only person that is going to be terrorized is going to be Jensen himself. You see I’m going to use this victory tonight as a spring board to bigger and better things. You think the Black List is happy with just the Strength in Numbers titles? Oh no. We want more….we want much more. We have our first taste of gold and we are looking for more. As Wylde finished his thought, the bathroom door opened and the original two ladies come out of the steam wrapped in only a towel. Wylde looks back at them, then over at the ladies sipping on their drinks by the bar. Wylde pushes Heath back into the hallway. Wylde: Where that path will take us we will have to wait and see. I say it starts in San Jose. That is where I’m going to leave it and you. You see I’ve made these ladies wait for way too long now. I need to make sure they also get to take a walk on the Wyld Side! Good day Sir. Fade out on the door slamming shut in Heath’s face.
