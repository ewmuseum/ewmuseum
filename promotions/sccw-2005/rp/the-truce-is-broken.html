The Truce Is Broken

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today In a night of the full moon we can see a location that looks similar to the locations people may have seen in the Rev 22:20 videos that were used by Legion and the New Flesh last year. We can hear a faint voice coming from one of the rooms which as we get closer suddenly turns into a scream of rage then a smash can be heard, the door opens and we see Luci standing in a doorway. The look in her eyes is one of annoyance towards the person that we can only seethe back of at this moment in time. Luci: Levi, the whole reason I wanted it to be here was NOT to attract attention, screaming and destroying the window isn’t going to help us now is it? The man suddenly gets up and turns around, it becomes clear that the man referred to as Levi is the man called ‘Stigmata’ on SCCW programming. Stigmata: I’m sorry but after last week and the actions that occurred by that so called woman I’m feeling a little bit pent up at the moment, I mean it’s not like I can find a lookalike and torture them after all. Luci: True but the fact that Amy decided to attack you makes her more of a fool than she realised, she could have tried to take me down but no she felt it was right to attack you to send a message, what she doesn’t realise is she will have unleashed a fury greater than anything that her ex could have cooked up. But that’s not why we’re here right now, there’s something important we need to discuss… (At this point the door opens again and we see Legion walking into the room looking slightly flustered about the events that occurred a few days before at Temptation.) Legion: Luci, Stigmata I have something interesting that you may want to hear right at this moment, it seems that Adrienne still hasn’t got the point clear of what I meant back in Venice and despite her actions forcing the break of a truce she feels that I need to exorcise some demons. Luci: Let me guess she’s making you team up with someone we hate and forcing us to co exist with them? Legion: Yes. Damn it stop ruining the suspense (laughs) She obviously doesn’t watch any tapes from 2009… Luci; You mean? Legion: Yes. It’s time Adrienne learnt that I’m not good with teaming especially when it comes to the man now calling himself the evil doctor. Since von Doom's all alone he's more vulnerable to attack and we'll make him believe all is well... then the sword of Damocles will finally strike and third time WILL be the charm.
