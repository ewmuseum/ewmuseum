Date: 7-20-07 It has become clear to Jason Cruise that his FUSE career is taking a turn for the better. Only a few weeks ago he was opening up the show with an untelevised match and after securing a second straight victory he found himself with an opportunity to become the first entrant in the WIW Tower match. After disposing of a former Livewire champion last week he has once again been rewarded, this time with an even greater task... a title match with Jonathan Rhine. The Santa Monica apartment. Nothing says bachelor bad like a flat screen TV, a deck of video consoles ranging from XBOX 360 to Sega Genesis, and the cluttering of vintage wrestling tapes and mobster DVDs. 'So doesn't getting a title shot kind of ruin the whole 'world is out to get me' shtick?' said Johnny. 'Yeah, sorta, ' mutters Jason. 'So now what?' replies Johnny. 'Well after I win I'll find something else to bitch about only I'll do it with a title around my waist and people will start to listen.' Normally Jason would be joking here, but his facial expression suggests otherwise. 'That's so heel of you,' Johnny says with a smile. Jason nods, 'Sometimes you rub off on me.' Johnny stares at him blankly. Jason shakes his head, 'Don't even go there.' A grin overtakes Johnny's face, 'I didn't say anything -- There's a knock at the door. Johnny reaches for the knob and Jason tries to step in-between the door and his friend, preventing what is about to happen. Keen on his attentions, Johnny brushes Jason away with his shoulder and rips the door open. 'Oh wow, hey there.' Beneath the loose strands of light brown hair is the lovely face of Marie Hauser. Her soft green eyes widen at the speed of which the door is opened, taken back by the mere force of the event. Jason tries once again to maneuver his way into the picture but of course Johnny is quick to erase this fatal attempt. 'Nice capri pants and punk rock shirt, that's so Avril Lavigne of you.' says Johnny with a smile. 'It could be worse,' she says confidently. I could be a poor man's version of Fabio meets Spicolli. That long hair look is so out of date.' She brushes past Johnny who just stands in the doorway a bit shocked at the quickness of her reply. Marie's eyes meet Jason's and soon they interlock in awkward hug that happens when two people see each other for the first time after a big date. 'You ready?' she asks. Jason nods, 'Lets get out of here.' Just like that the two of them head out the door and as it begins to shout Johnny can't help but to try to think of something witty or embarrassing to say but as the door gingerly shuts behind them he is left alone and without anything playful to say. 'Fuck that,' he says to himself. * * * * * Choosing a location for a date is never easy. First you need to take into consideration the status of your relationship partner. Is the comfort level there yet, are you still getting to know the person, do you need to be in a crowded place with limited talking because you don't have much to say to her and really just want to get in her pants? Those thoughts are currently running through the mind of Jason Cruise as Marie and himself settle into a quaint cafe alongside the sunny shores of Santa Monica, California and all he can really tell himself is that he's still undecided on Marie. 'The food is great here, you'll love it,' he says as they sit beside one another at a small table top on the outside patio of a hole in the wall French bistro. 'It better, I'm starving,' she says with a smile. 'I hope you brought your wallet, I'm not one of those girls who is afraid to eat in front of guys, I came to eat.' He grins, 'That'll be attractive.' 'You act as if I'm trying to impress you?' she says playfully. Normally a man's confident meter would drop a bit here but not Jason, it's not arrogance mind you, he just knows she's a sarcastic person and it's one of the things that makes him attracted to her. 'Impress you?' he laughs. 'I just hate eating alone so I figured I'd let you tag along.' 'Wow, a real smooth talker aren't you? I bet that arrogance gets girls back to your apartment all the time.' 'It got you there, didn't it?' he says. 'It got me to your house the other night, too bad for you that you couldn't seal the deal. I wouldn't go bragging about that.' She smiles as she takes a sip of her water, he nods, acknowledging the truth behind her statement. 'It's tough when your date passes out on you, probably would have been the best night of your life.' Her eyes widen. 'Oh really. The way you were sucking down those whiskey-sours I imagine it would have been a night of disappointment for the both of us.' He's a bit taken back by her forwardness, but he's not about to back down from this game of verbal cat and mouse foreplay. 'I'm surprised you remembered what I was drinking after the way you took down those cosmopolitans.' 'Even a girl has to unwind after a long week of work.' They waitress approaches the table and sees their menus still open. A bit disgruntled, she knowsfrom experience they aren't ready to order or even really looked over anything yet. She stomps away back into the designated waiter station outside. 'Besides, I'm not the type of girl who is just going to give it up on the first date.' she says. 'Good thing for me that this is our second date then.' For the first time she blushes, letting the strength of her pro-feminine character down momentarily. 'Do you really think I'm going to put out for a fifteen dollar meal?' Quickly the redness of her cheeks fades, she's back in game mode. 'Oh, you're one of THOSE girls.' He says this knowing it'll set her off, no girl with half a brain wants to be stereotyped as materialistic. Only, she doesn't bite the bait the way she expected. 'Excuse me, waitress?' The young girl scrapes her feet across the concrete, hating her draw of tables right now. 'Yes?' says the waitress. 'Your finest bottle of pinot noir,' says Marie. The waitresses frown quickly stretches in the opposite direction. 'Yes, your finest bottle under thirty bucks.' says Jason. The waitresses look of excitement quickly diminishes, still, it's not a bad sell. She muddles off away from the table, leaving the duo to humor one another with some more sexual innuendo. 'Big spender,' Marie jokes. 'Baby steps. If fifteen bucks doesn't get you, I'll try thirty next and work my way up.' 'It could be months then before you ever get me into bed.' 'Well at least now I know I'm going to get you there.' 'Don't get ahead of yourself, you're not that smooth.' 'I'm not that rough either, I can sense that I'm wearing you down, tonight could be the night.' he says confidently. 'I guess it depends how good your thirty bottle of vino is.' Just as those words escape her mouth the waitress returns, bottle in hand.. sort of. 'What is that, a bottle of wine for midgets?' The waitress rolls her eyes, 'No sir, it's a split bottle, it's the only one under your spending limit.' Marie smiles as the direct jab at Jason from the waitress. 'I guess it's not looking good for you tonight.' Marie's hand covers her face, she's trying to hide her laughter. 'Please, you can only fight it for so long.' The waitress opens the bottle, pouring a small sip for Jason who just gestures for her to pour a full glass for Marie, not caring to taste it. 'A true connoisseur, aren't you?' Marie leans back in her chair, she's getting comfortable, definitely favoring Jason's odds. 'No, I'm just going to keep feeding you different sorts of alcohol until I find one that lowers your morale a bit and gets you back into my bedroom.' She smiles, 'Wine might be a good choice then.' Jason's curiosity peaks. 'Cheers to that.' Their glasses cling, they sip, we fade. * * * * * The room we are in is unfamiliar to us. Pink is the color of choice and it litters the walls in all shapes and forms. Faint pink paint spits wildly across the walls in a style that only a real art enthusiast would truly appreciate while darker representations of the color outline several gray paintings that are scattered in an odd pattern throughout the bedroom. What is more intriguing is the articles of clothing that lie on the otherwise empty white carpet. Recognizable are the capri pants and soon thereafter, a small, female punk style t-shirt. Beside them are a pair of gray and white camouflage shorts, a pair of black socks, and what appears to be a Thundercats designer tee. Some guys just can't let the good times go. The bed itself is no different. A fuchsia dove comforter is pulled back against the wooden railing while a light blush colored linen maneuvers like a rocky mountain landscape over a pair of bodies on top of one another. Light brown hair spills over the face of Marie as she pushes herself upright over Jason, straddling her legs over his waist as he runs his fingers over her shoulders and down to the middle of her back, unfastening her bra strap, and gently pulling it down. His hands trace her slender features, from around her thin waistline and back up, eventually cupping her breasts and pushing back in a manner that causes her entire torso to bend back. Her head snaps back, flinging hair from her face as her chin rises upwards. She moans softly as her fingers tighten on his midsection. Almost habitually, his chest tightens as his fingers disappear under the sheets. The linen ruffles downwards as his hands work in that direction and as they do, her eyes open and they are directed towards his. Softly, 'I want you.' The words every man lusts for. 'I want you too.' Without hesitation, his left hand appears out from underneath the sheets, his boxers and her panties in hand. Casually, he flings them to the floor. Instantly, his hand vanishes under the sheets once more, he uses both hands this time to lower her body further down on top of his. This is it he thinks to himself as he positions himself comfortably underneath her petite frame that fits perfectly onto his. Thirty dollar wine jokes cloud his head, it'll make a great story later, he smiles. Then it happens... 'Wait,' she says. His smile clears. 'You have protection, right?' 'Yeah, it's in my purse,' he jokes. 'Seriously. You brought a condom, didn't you?' His eyes dart around the room, looking for a drawer, a dresser, anything that might resemble a condom lock box. 'Umm, no, we're at your place. Don't you have any?' He can feel the wine fading from his system but more importantly... from hers. 'No, I'm.. I'm out.' 'What do you mean out? How many guys are you sleeping with?' He laughs, she doesn't. 'Are you really asking that now of all times?' His laughter diminishes. 'Just get a pill tomorrow. It's not that big of a deal, I'll go with you.' She shakes her head. 'Wow, that's awfully nice of you.' 'Seriously Marie, I'm clean, I'm a good guy, lets just -- She leans into him, her lips meet his. She pulls away for a second only to embrace his lips with her own once more. Their eyes lock, Jason gazes deeply into her and delivers the most genuine stare that a man can possibly give. 'So?' he asks.
