One Year

16 May 2009

Date: 5/16/09 'I am not a pawn on a chessboard. If I was, I'd be an extremely bored one, because you have shown little indication of moving me over the course of the game, and so I moved myself. You know what happens when a pawn makes its way across the board? It becomes a better piece. A stronger piece.' Phillip Kennedy, Temptation, 2/10/09 ---------------------------- Temptation had gone off the air, but the fans in attendance hadn't moved an inch from their seats. They were, after all, expecting something. Retaliation of some kind. Amy Campbell to run out from the back after checking on her husband and kick some Kennedy ass. Neither of those things happened. What did happen was relatively more mundane. Phillip Kennedy removed his foot from the back of Alexandra Pierce, and placed the SCCW Universal Champion back on the timekeeper's table where it belonged. Confident as he was that it would be his soon, he figured Amy deserved her last few days with the golden prize that she'd admittedly brought to prominence. It was one of the more pleasant things he felt Amy deserved, considering the others were things like 'being raped in every orifice by well-hung elephants.' He took a moment to soak in the...it wasn't adulation, really. More shock. Shock that he'd pulled the trigger on a rather audacious move. It was an audacious enough move that he'd been debating whether or not it was a good idea up until the point he slid into the ring behind Alexandra and cocked the chair back. Phillip was close enough to the commentary table that he could see the opposite expressions on the faces of Eugene Ware and Dave Gibson. Eugene, the man who so often supported Phillip on color commentary, looked absolutely crestfallen, eyes darting back and forth from the Big Stack to the Director, mouth contorted as if pained. Dave Gibson, on the other hand, wore a small smile, and nodded in the direction of Phillip. Months ago, Dave had taken up for the Stack during his initial questioning of Hand authority. As Kennedy had told Lance Marshall earlier, it was unlikely that he'd ever really wear a white hat. Still, the King of Old School appreciated a good asshole. He'd been one quite often in an illustrious career, and respected the art of douchebaggery as it existed before people like Alexandra Pierce and Lane Stevens entered the public consciousness. 'That's mine at Homecoming,' Phillip stated, gesturing back to the title, and there were a mixture of cheers and boos from the nearby fans. Redirecting his gaze back to the fallen Director, Phillip Kennedy couldn't help but smile. She wasn't an immortal after all, some sort of mythical creature that ran SCCW with an iron fist. But...no. Alexandra Pierce was human. Real and warm. A chairshot to the back of her head knocked her unconscious. For how long was another question, but the basic fact still held true: she was a person. Even so, Phillip had no desire to be in the immediate vicinity when Alexandra woke up. Then, too, was the problem of Amy Campbell backstage tending to her husband. There was always the possibility of her making a grand return. Possibly with weaponry. It was, in all likelihood, time to beat a retreat before he was beaten *into* retreat. Kennedy hopped the guardrail, wading through fans who were now starting to make the walk from their seats back to their vehicles and get home. After all, it had been quite the momentous night in Sin City Championship Wrestling. The main event of its next PPV had been determined. Perhaps more importantly, the company's battle lines may well have been forever altered. Even as fans whispered excitedly to one another, or stopped on the way out for some merch, Phillip Kennedy remained silent as he walked towards the doors. There was no way in Hell he was going back to the locker room. Not after that stunt. Good thing, then, that he had his bag loaded up in his rental car, ready to take him wherever he needed to go. Only now was it hitting him -- really hitting him. There was no going back now. Alexandra Pierce did not strike him as a forgiving soul, and really, that was the bigger concern. Assaulting Darcy Crisis was one thing -- directly tied to his match with Amy. Alex could understand, Hell, maybe even accept that. But he'd gone and hauled off and smacked Desade in the back of the head with a steel chair. Phillip didn't NEED to do that to make a statement to Amy Campbell. The statement was already made. Then why'd he do it? Simple, in Kennedy's mind. He wasn't fighting Amy Campbell. He was fighting DNA: both redheads. He had heard Eugene Ware shriek on commentary about how he'd picked a fight with Amy and Alex within five minutes of one another, and in his mind, he'd said the words 'damn straight.' So long as Alexandra Pierce remained, beating Amy Campbell only meant so much. It was certainly his main goal, of course, but there would be much work to be done after Homecoming even if he hadn't chaired Pierce. Those who had stepped up to take on the Spider in the Web had been consumed. Jared Sykes was gone as a result. Lance Marshall always knew what could happen to him when he stepped into the ring. Phillip Kennedy's name was now inevitably placed across that of the Director. Because realistically, as far as Kennedy was concerned, he could only be a success, only truly be the top name of SCCW two ways. One was Pierce giving him her blessing. The second was getting Alex out of the picture. Neither of them was going to be especially easily achieved. ------------------------- Residence Inn 2.67 miles from Mount Rushmore When one dealt with the Dead Man's Hand, one learned to take certain...precautions. Call it a hunch, but Phillip Kennedy hadn't thought it the best idea in the world to meet Savant in the hotel they'd been staying at before the show. Kennedy's propensity for travelling light meant that he could take his duffel bag from the show and go pretty much anywhere. Anywhere, in this case, happened to be a Residence Inn. Non-ostentacious, the sort of place a family would go when on a trip to Mount Rushmore. In the morning, he would fly out to...somewhere, and begin the preparations for the fight of his life. Before that, though, he had to talk to the one person in the entire company who seemed to have anything resembling confidence in him. The woman who had been fired from the Dead Man's Hand for that confidence. Originally, that would likely have been the sole focal point of the conversation, but then Phillip had gone and decided to do something, for lack of a poker analogy, best considered doubling down. It had felt good. Far moreso than he was willing to admit. Really, he was tired of the pissing matches. Tired of Amy Campbell running her mouth like a spoiled rich kid with all the toys. Tired, for that matter, of Alexandra Pierce playing the role of that spoiled rich kid's mommy. Three moves were all he'd needed. Chain lariat. Curbstomp. Chairshot. Bang-bang-bang. All within the space of mere minutes as seen on TV, though the Crisis assault had been just early enough for him to get back into the arena to set up Desade. The adrenaline of it still coursed through his veins, better than the high from any drug he could imagine. That was perhaps why he was a bit jumpy, pacing back and forth in the Residence Inn lobby, waiting for Lauren Fox. 'You really are an idiot, aren't you, Phil?' Kennedy reached into his pocket, pulling out his cell phone. 12:30, on the dot. 'Punctual as always, Lauren. Though I could say the same thing for you, really.' The Orchid looked up several inches at the Big Stack. Kennedy looked right back down at Savant. And they both said roughly the same thing at the same time. 'What happened?' 'You start. You're the one that officially got fired, though I'm probably not too far behind.' That, Phillip figured, was probably one of those understatement things. Lauren's eyes met his momentarily, and it was as if she was trying to figure out the best way to put what she wanted to say. There was so much that she couldn't tell him -- even if it was likely inevitable that she would have to. Finally, though, she decided to frame the issue in the way he would understand: SCCW. 'Alex...Phil, from the standpoint of the Dead Man's Hand, the best possible outcome was you and Amy winning. Either way, at Homecoming, the Hand retains the title, either with you or her. That was the plan going into the tournament, the whole concept. That's how we fuck over Doctor Kensington's glorious plan. And that's where we are today. We got what we wanted...even if it probably doesn't matter anymore.' Phillip nodded along, though really, she wasn't answering the question he wanted answered. 'I figured that much, Lauren. I just want to know...why'd you help me? You've known Alex forever, you've known me a year. Wouldn't have blamed you if you'd just let Marshall Code Red me.' Kennedy remembered being up in the move before he'd quite known what was happening. Before he'd had the chance to try a counter, Savant had intervened. Quickly. Just enough to give Phillip the opening he'd needed to put Lance Marshall down for three. Again, Phil could practically see Lauren's mind working. Thinking. Calculating. Savant had been through a lot more than he had on the night -- the reprecussions of her actions had already begun, while those of his had not. Watching backstage on the monitor after his match, the fact that the shit was hitting the fan had been quite obvious. 'Because,' Lauren Fox finally replied, 'I am loyal to Alexandra, yes. But I am also loyal to the Dead Man's Hand, and those who have been loyal to us. As I said on TV, Phil, you've been with us the entire time. You have done whatever has been asked of you: taken beatings, defeated opponents, provided distractions. One year ago -- to be absolutely honest -- you were brought to us by my stepsister to be a soldier in the fold. We had no expectations. You were a rookie, a young kid in the terms of this sport.' Once, that admission would have insulted him. Nowadays, however, with the benefit of perspective...he understood. He HAD been a raw wrestler back then, a man who lost three of his first four matches. 'And you got thrown into the fire because you were with us. Make no mistake -- you would not have been placed in matches with Sean Sterling and Wyatt Connors within weeks of your debut otherwise. But you would not be driven away. You have proven yourself to be a persistant, loyal, and capable ally, the epitome of what a member of the Hand should be. And for all of your services...it is not right that you have been marginalized.' She fell quiet -- perhaps thinking she had said too much, or perhaps simply wanting Kennedy to say something by way of response. 'Thank you, Lauren,' was all he said at first, followed up quickly by an 'I appreciate it.' But the dam holding back Phillip Kennedy's feelings about the group that he'd championed for a little under a year had already burst earlier that night, and everything that had been on his mind was rushing out. 'If there was still a Dead Man's Hand, I'd be glad to fly the flag, Lauren. Fact is, though, that the Hand's dead. There's Alex and Amy, and then there's everyone else. I mean, I knew she didn't give a shit about me -- but Alex has been fighting with Kat for the past month plus, and now she *hit* you. Bridden's gone. Mayhem's gone. The Hand consists of three active competitors, one of whom is the very definition of a lackey. I feel sorry for Lane Stevens. The only person Alex seems to give a shit about is Amy. I'm sick of it. I'm tired of it.' Savant's body language was treading the ever-careful line between sympathetic agreement and 'are you out of your fucking MIND?!' Lauren Fox wasn't an intimidating woman by most definitions. Well, not unless you knew what she did for a living. Nonetheless, when she raised herself up to tippy-toes, facial expression grave, Phillip Kennedy was well and truly intimidated. 'I understand that, Phillip,' the Orchid began, the use of his full first name indicating to Kennedy that he was probably in trouble. 'What I don't think you understand, however, is the enormity of what you have done.' 'I smacked Alexandra Pierce in the back of the head with a steel chair,' Kennedy retorted. 'I'm not exactly expecting sunflowers and daffodils from here on out, you know.' She tried hard not to shoot him the 'you are SUCH a dunderhead, Phillip' look, but she was kind of failing at it. 'Look, Phillip, I know she hasn't exactly been the picture of terror during most of your time here, but DO NOT underestimate Alexandra Pierce. Ever. Or it will be the last mistake that you ever make.' They might both have been in similar situations right now, but Lauren Fox had the distinct advantage of knowing exactly what Alexandra Pierce could bring to bear against her if ever so inclined. Phillip had no idea. And when he found out, probably sometime soon, Savant didn't imagine that the response would be pretty. 'I'm not happy about this,' Phillip responded. 'Sure, it felt damned good to brain her after all of this shit, but it's not an ideal solution. If there was an ideal solution, I would have taken it. But now...now I've just got to go into Salt Lake City, beat Amy Campbell, and become SCCW Universal Champion. After that, I'll deal with Alex. Believe me, Lauren. I know what I'm in for. I know what she can do...she's had Sin City cowed ever since before I got here.' The look on Kennedy's face was solemn, as he nodded, once. He shouldered his duffel bag, looking over his shoulder at the check-in counter. 'I'm going to crash here for the night. Would you like to stay with me?' At her arched eyebrow, Kennedy couldn't help but smile. 'Two beds, Lauren. One for each of us. You look exhausted, and all of this drama can't be helping.' Lauren shook her head. The problem was that, no, Phillip had absolutely no clue what Alexandra Pierce could do. At the same time, he was right about one thing. 'Thanks, Phil,' she finally agreed, clutching the handle of her own suitcase. 'I appreciate it. I'd go back to the hotel, but I don't think either of us are welcome there right now.' With a weary glance at one another, Phillip Kennedy and Lauren Fox began the short trek to check-in. Kennedy had no idea what was in store for him in the future. He had betrayed his leader, and further enraged the woman who would be defending her title against him. Nothing would be easy from here on in. Nothing would be easy, but in the back of his mind, the Big Stack couldn't help but think this was how it was meant to be, all along. -------------------------- ESPN Studios Bristol, CT After Sinner's Choice went off the air... 'Someone get people out here, NOW! This is ridiculous! Those pricks told us nothing like this was gonna happen tonight!' Alexandra Pierce's appearance at the ESPN studios had sent technicians and employees into a veritable dizzy, running back and forth to try and take care of the necessary issues. One, get Desade the fuck off ESPN property. Two, deal with the result of her fury. That happened to be Phillip Kennedy, in an agonized seated position, with two broken rattan fighting sticks lying around him. One medic was kneeling by Phil, asking him a variety of questions, the other medic patting around the floor with rubber gloves, in search of a tooth that had been forcibly driven from Kennedy's mouth. Hey, no one ever said the Spider's Kiss was pleasant. 'You okay,' the first man asked, surveying Kennedy's injuries. Sure, his eyes were still a bit unfocused, his ribs hurt like Hell, and he was minus a tooth, but really now, this was a tad excessive. 'I'll be fine,' Kennedy snapped. 'I don't need a damned medical examination. I can tell you exactly what's wrong with me: I got jumped from behind by a redhead with more issues than a newsstand.' Never mind the fact that Kennedy had been the initial backfighter on Temptation; he was one pissed off former poker player right about now. 'We're just making sure, Mr. Kennedy,' the medic explained, attempting to be patient. 'You've got a big match coming up and everything...' As if Phil really needed to be reminded of that fact. 'I took a few sticks to the ribs and a Spider's Kiss,' Phillip retorted. 'I think I'll be okay in the long run.' Experimentally, he poked his tongue through the hole in his mouth left by the missing tooth. Wincing, he felt his tongue run over a jagged...something. It wasn't a clean break. He'd probably have to go get that taken care of sometime. Sometime, though, was not now. Nor would it likely be until after Homecoming. Kennedy winced as he felt a gloved hand feel over his ribcage. 'No breaks,' the medic announced, cheerfully. 'They'll probably just be sore for a few days.' No shit, Phillip thought. That tends to be what happens when someone whacks you in them with rattan sticks. 'Found it!' Kennedy heard a voice chime in, and he looked up to see the other medic standing, what remained of one of his teeth laying in a rubber-coated palm. The medic looked around to try and find one person in the studio not in a panic over Sin City's introductory ESPN effort, screaming out a hearty 'Someone get me some milk for his tooth, please!' Really, that worked? Phil had always thought it was an old wives' tale. All of these people around him was not helping his mood. He would be fine in the long run, bumps, scrapes, bruises, and lost tooth aside. Right now, Phillip wanted to be alone. Away from people, so he could sulk in private and realize that he really should have seen this coming. 'You're lucky,' the medic finally said, standing up from his crouch at Phillip's side. 'No major injuries, you'll be good to go for Homecoming.' That had been his first thought once he managed to drag himself to a sitting position. That he was fortunate Alexandra had been confronted with security when she had. But that thought, he'd since realized, was erroneous. Pierce had been behind him with weapons. If she'd really wanted to take him out...she could have gone for his knee. Tried to take out his lariat arm. Done something more...productive. But she hadn't. She had left him more or less whole going into Homecoming. What had she said to him again? That she wanted Amy to beat him in the cage. That Amy didn't want any help. He was positive of the first, and to some extent, positive of the last. Campbell was a proud human being, and he didn't doubt that it bothered her that she needed help to retain her belt. At the end of the day, though, if Pierce wanted to send in the troops, the troops were being sent in. He harbored no illusions about that fact. The Dead Man's Hand was seemingly in tatters, so her ability to call in troops was limited, but-- The pain in his ribs and throat was suddenly dulled by a pain that seemed to come from the center of his brain, a pain caused by one horrific thought. What if it was all an act? What if Katsidy's disagreements with Alex were all staged? What if Lauren being fired was, too? What if this was all some sort of twisted test of loyalty that he'd miserably failed? Sick as he was, one thought rose to his mind in response: who the fuck cares? Pierce and Campbell had pushed him to this. And even if he couldn't count on Lauren or Kathryn...what did it really matter? He was in the cage with Amy Campbell. Amy was all he could focus on. He could worry about other things, but Aimz and the Universal Title were his objectives. When he stepped out of that cage, he could deal with any fallout that would occur. Reaching down with both hands to help push off the ground, Phillip Kennedy rose to a standing position. His SCCW Gateway Championship belt was on the floor a few feet away, courtesy of his missed swing at the Director. Bending over, and ignoring the pain in his ribs, Kennedy picked up his title, slinging it back over his shoulder where it belonged. Kennedy scowled, the pain coursing through his body acting as a reminder of why he was doing this. Why he'd attacked Darcy Markson and Alexandra Pierce to begin with. Before this, he had been ignored by the Director of the Hand, written off as a second-rate champion, just one of many human shields. Now, he had Alex's attention. And Amy's too. The Universal Champion could prattle on all she wanted about being one of the best in the business, and be a sarcastic prick, but it didn't matter. Her mouth would have to cash those checks at Homecoming, and Kennedy was a firm believer that her bank account was running dry. 'Got it,' Phillip heard someone say, and moments later, the second medic on the scene was all smiles, presenting Phillip with a glass of milk. 'If we get you to the dentist fast enough,' she said, 'we might be able to get the tooth back in your mouth.' Honestly, the damned tooth was the furthest thing from Kennedy's mind at the moment. He accepted the glass of milk nonetheless, and held the medic's eyes. He almost felt bad for her; clearly, she thought she was doing him a kind service. Saving him from a lifetime of having a ruined smile. 'Thanks, doc,' Phillip replied, holding the glass up, as if for a toast, 'but no thanks. Cheers.' With that, Kennedy tipped the glass of milk over, pounding down the moo juice until nothing was left but residue. Wiping his mouth off with a satisfied 'ah', Kennedy regarded the now slightly terrified medic with a lopsided, gapped smile. 'I don't want my tooth back,' he explained. 'I want to be able to look in a mirror for the rest of my life and know exactly why it's gone. More to the point, I want to look in that mirror every day until I step into a cage with Amy Campbell and add that tooth to the list of things that she and Alexandra Pierce have cost me. Though, next to my dignity and pride, what's one tooth really, anyhow?' Looking down at the remains of the weapons Desade used to assault him, the Big Stack manages to hock up a sizeable wad of bloody phlegm, and spit it down on the rattan sticks. The ensuing laughter is mirthless, as he clutches at sore ribs with his right hand and exits, stage left. Where exactly he's headed to is unknown, but with him gone, we're left with a bunch of ESPN employees left to clean up the carnage. Suffice it to say there's probably not going to be a second Sinner's Choice at this rate. ------------------- EnergySolutions Center Salt Lake City, Utah The stands might have been empty, but the arena itself was alive with the sounds of construction. What, you just thought pay-per-view sets and rings magically built themselves? No, hours of hard work by lots of guys who don't get pyro and ring music is what it takes to ready the arena for hours of SCCW action. For the most part, the wrestlers are backstage during this time, or still arriving at the show, having a meal, shooting the shit, or getting ready for battle. Phillip Kennedy, on the other hand, was watching them put the set up. More importantly, he was watching them put the cage up. It was the most impressive thing he'd ever seen in his professional life. Steel mesh supported by steel beams, four sides and a top. He'd never competed in a cage before, but he'd heard the boys in the back talk about it. The cage could change careers. Alter destinies. The steel cage was the perfect battleground for the finals of the No Limit Tournament. It would, as much as was possible, ensure that things were between Phillip and Amy. That didn't mean, of course, that Kennedy wasn't nervous as Hell. Even so, he couldn't resist the urge to walk up to the structure. Cocking his head to the side questioningly, he got tacit permission from the ring crew nearby, and walked up the steel steps to the cage's door. 'Good Lord.' People weren't meant to be caged. One only was caged...imprisoned for doing bad, bad things. He supposed that in that sense, it was also appropriate. He wasn't a good person, not by a longshot. Nor was Amy. They were two pricks doing battle for the most important prize in their chosen profession. With that thought firmly in mind, Kennedy opened the door, and stepped through the ropes into the cage. Closing the door behind him, the Big Stack got his first real sense of what it would be like to be in the structure. It was smaller than he'd thought it was. The ring itself was the same size, but there was no room to regroup. No room for anything, and the cage had a top. Cautious fingertips pulled at the steel mesh, only to find precious little give. No, being thrown into the steel would produce that much talked about 'cheese-grater' effect. Would likely split him open. It wasn't a fun thing to bleed, but in a match like this, it was probably an inevitability. 'Whatcha doing in there? You're not on for a good long while.' Kennedy's attention was diverted from the cage to one of the men walking around outside of it. Dave Gibson was called the King of Old School for many reasons. If anyone knew about the old-school environment that was the steel cage, it would be Gibson. 'Just getting the lay of the land, Dave,' Phillip replied, tugging a bit harder at the mesh. 'Never been in one of these matches before.' Gibson scratched at his chin thoughtfully, circling the cage with a small smile on his face. Lord, how many cage matches had he been in over the course of his illustrious career? They were among the reason he'd been driven to the commentary table. 'You want some advice, Stack,' Gibson asked, the veteran ascending the ring steps. Kennedy nodded, and moments later, the Big Stack was joined by the King of Old School, surrounded on five sides by mesh. 'First thing to know is that there's no way to come out of this match not hurt, so don't bother trying.' Dave's grin was toothy, and he spoke from experience. Kennedy nodded. 'Second...get over here. Need to show you something.' The Big Stack complied...only to be met with a lightning-fast kneelift from Gibson that drove the air from Phillip's lungs long enough for Gibson to grab a handful of hair and enough momentum to drive Kennedy two steps forward and into the steel, face-first. Phillip realized pretty quickly just how little give there was to the mesh. His face and upper chest hit hard, and for a moment, it felt as if the world had shut down around him. He could barely move, but he remained on his feet, leaning against the ropes. Dave Gibson's voice seemed distant in Kennedy's mind. 'Second is that. What it feels like to be thrown into that cage. There's no way to be prepared for it unless it happens.' 'I appreciate the demonstration,' Kennedy retorted, when he found his voice again. Phillip ran his fingertips over his forehead in search of blood, but found none. 'Least I'm not split.' 'Oh, you will be,' Gibson laughed. 'Fact of life in a cage. But you've reacted pretty well to tasting your own blood, so I'm not worried about that.' As soon as the words left his lips, Dave's face took on a more serious tone. The King of Old School's eyes met those of the Big Stack, gazes holding firm. 'Tonight's the biggest fight of your life, Phil,' he said, as if Phil truly needed a reminder of that fact. 'I say a lot of things to the world every night, and I'm not necessarily a fan -- but it's time for change around here. I watched Amy Campbell when she was a Highwayman, one of the greatest competitors this sport's ever seen...and then she sold it all out for this. For the Hand.' Kennedy remained silent. Really, he didn't want to talk about the goddamned Dead Man's Hand. Not now. Not today. 'Dave,' he finally said, 'as I've been telling everyone, I'm not going to be a good face of this company. I'm an asshole, and I'm quite happy with that.. Right now, I just want to focus on the goal of the evening: beating Amy Campbell and winning the Universal Title.' Gibson nodded, and for another moment, the two men's eyes remained locked. 'I wasn't exactly a Boy Scout either. I'll leave it at that, though. Best of luck to you. ' As Dave Gibson left the cage, Phillip's eyes darted around a bit more. Everything was...more or less ready. The steel chain that he'd asked one of the ring crew to tuck under one of the turnbuckle pads was there, if he accepted the man's assurances at face value. What was there left to do? He didn't want to go to the back. Not yet, anyway. And then, it hit him. An idea. The sort of idea that he should have rejected out of hand, but just couldn't resist. With a practiced eye, Phillip Kennedy looked around ringside for the people he'd need. There was that referee...Hamill, was it? There was also a female ring tech with long red hair. Not Aimz, not even close, but for the sake of argument, she'd do. What Phillip Kennedy wanted to do was petty. It was silly. Arguably, it was far too dorky for a Universal Title challenger to do with his match so damned close. But then, Phillip Kennedy never danced to the beat of the most popular band. * * * 'Hey, Jason, get over here, I've got somethin' for ya.' The voice of Sin City looked back at Phillip Kennedy with a 'who, me?' expression on his face, but dutifully walked over to cageside, where the Big Stack smiled at him. 'Mind doing me a favor, Jason,' Kennedy asked, waving a crisp Hamilton and Washington in the air. For his part, Jason Myers simply shook his head, muttering under his breath. 'That was a goddamned Council rib,' he said, but stepped inside the cage and took the money from Phillip nonetheless. 'What do you want me to do?' 'Just announce me as the new champion, Jason,' Kennedy replied, a sort of giddy anticipation in his voice. 'Like you will when I win tonight. You know. Just for a bit of added motivation.' Myers shot Kennedy a look much like those Hamill and the rather confused ring tech had given the Big Stack, but he ultimately shrugged. 'Never had that sort of request before,' Jason said, 'but why the Hell not?' With all of the personnel in place, it was showtime. The redheaded ring technician advanced on Phillip, hitting him about half force with a forearm to the chest that caused Phil to 'stagger' backwards. Her next move was slightly better, a leaping front dropkick to the chest that she'd seen Charlotte Ramone do a bunch of times. This time, Phillip staggered back into the ropes, and she rose to her feet, pleased. Philliip's body fell between the ropes, unable to get the usual momentum that he would due to the presence of the steel cage. Filing that fact away in his mind for future reference, he used the ropes as a pendulum as best he could, flying back at a now terrified ring technician, who covered up just as Kennedy tapped her lightly across the chest with his left arm in a mockery of his Acecracker Lariat. On cue, she fell to the mat in a crumpled heap, as Kennedy fell forward, completing the follow-through of the move. If he closed his eyes, he could hear the capacity crowd at full throat, wondering if this was it, wondering if, at long last, Sin City would have a new champion. Kennedy crawled over to the downed tech -- by now she wasn't that, she was Aimz --, and threw himself across the redhead's body, hooking the leg deep as David Hamill dropped to count. Each slap of the mat rang out throughout the mostly empty arena. One. Two. Three. There was no ring bell, but Phillip could imagine it ringing easily enough. Feigning exhaustion, Kennedy fell off of the beleaguered ring technician, who seemed to be doing a pretty good job of pretending to be unconscious. That, Kennedy figured, or she was just bored. David Hamill took a replica title from one of the ring crew cageside, and walked back over to Kennedy. As in character as he could be, the referee kneeled and patted the Big Stack on the shoulder. 'You did it!' Hamill called, as Kennedy slowly made his way up to a seated position, and held up three fingers, which the referee met with an enthusiastic nod. With that scene playing itself out, Jason Myers raised the microphone to his lips, and gave it his all. 'Your winner of the No Limit Tournament,' Myers screamed into the mic, 'and NEW SCCW Universal Champion...the BIG STACK, PHILLLIIIPP KENNNEDDDDDDDYYY!' The referee raised Kennedy's left arm -- his lariat arm -- skyward in trumph, handing over the replica title. Phillip clutched the belt to his chest as he imagined he would later that night, though this simulation could in no way compare to what it would feel like when the fantasy became real and warm. Phillip was as ready as he could be. For one year of his life, he'd been preparing for this moment. Taking his lumps from veterans like Wyatt Connors. Dealing with people like Kathryn Shaw and Lauren Fox and Alexandra Pierce, all on the road to getting this chance, this one moment in time that he would headline the biggest event in SCCW's history. It would not be easy, but he knew that. To achieve his goal, he would have to go through an enraged Amy Campbell. Through Alexandra Pierce, a woman whose sole goal in the company for months and months had been to keep the Universal Championship around Amy's slender waist. The question was not whether he could beat Amy Campbell. The question was whether he would, whether for one night, he could summon together all of the courage and skill that he possessed to do what many thought unthinkable. Clutching a replica of the prize he wanted so very badly, Phillip Kennedy smiled as he looked out at rows and rows of empty seats that in mere hours would be jam-packed to capacity. Wrestling, he'd decided, was so much better than poker. Once you put all your chips in the middle in the card game, lady luck was all you could rely on. You had to watch helplessly as the cards fell. In wrestling, though...you controlled your fate after such a decision. Even if you quite possibly made your life a living Hell by doing so. Even if you sacrificed your cushy existence as Gateway Champion for a chance to be the man. It was, in the mind of Phillip Benjamin Kennedy, well worth it. After all, the Big Stack was a gambler. And when you're a gambler, with the mindset of risk woven into the fabric of who you are, life's just...better when every single chip you have rides on the flip of a card.
