Chapter 472: One for every victory over El Diablo.

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.04.07 'I don't know how it all came to this.' The voice of 'The Prophecy' Jason Cruise was rasp, a near whisper. The LiveWire Champion stands unmoving on the sidewalk outside of his apartment in Santa Monica, CA. He looks serious, just as he always does, but this time, it's dire. He looks upset as has become the norm as of late, but this time, the intensity of the emotion could be called operatic. He's always been thin and muscular, but standing in the dimly lit room underneath a single soft bulb overhead, he looks like something a bit more daunting. His black hair is matted down underneath the hood of a gray sweatshirt that cover his ears, his eyes, and most of his angular face. If you strain your eyes enough, you can somewhat capture the images of the scars and burns on the left side of his face, though the lighting of the room makes it near impossible to really gain more than a glimpse. His left hands is tucked in the front pocket of the unbuttoned sweatshirt revealing his muscular midsection that bares more resemblance to a gymnast or a swimmer than a body builder stretches down towards the black warm up pants and his signature red kicks. His right hand however, clutches the LiveWire Title over his shoulder. 'How it went from showing up at the arena and just wanting to wrestle for the people of FUSE to wanting nothing more than you gone from the very sport I love. You never seemed like the type of guy that took losing easily, Raul. Not at cards, not with family, and most certainly not in the ring.' The chill in the night is evident, his breaths convalescing in soft gray clouds. 'From the very first time I beat you in the center of the ring, clean as a whistle, you've had it out for me. Since the first time the Highwaymen came out on top of the Affliction you made my life a target of all the hate that burns through your body.' 'All because of your inadequacy to get it done in the ring.' 'All because the very thing in this sport that you treasure the most, my LiveWire Title...' 'You can't have.' Finally, he begins to pace. His brain is turning now. 'So instead of training harder, instead of working your ass off to get better, you did the only thing you knew how to do. Instead of trying to beat me, something that has proven time and time again impossible for you...' Jason shakes his head. 'You went after her.' * * * * * The black digits on dashboard of his 1971 Volvo P-1800 read 11:55pm. Aleksi Kravchenko remained seated in the driver's seat of his vehicle that was parked outside of the Ford Center in Oklahoma City. Two and a half hours had passed since he had arrived on the 24th of November, and there were so sign of The Man in the Black Hat. He was certain that at some point the mysterious figure would arrive on UPROAR. If correct, it almost meant something else would be inevitable. That at some point in the evening, he would have to leave. But as he sat there, the minutes creeping towards the end of the show, there had yet to be any sign that his plan would unfold accordingly. It was time to improvise. Aleksi glanced at the clock on his dashboard and then started to pull out of his parking place. He reached for his cell phone and made the call. 'Kristoff,' Aleski said, waiting for a response on the other end. Instead he was met with up tempo beats from something out of a dance club. 'Aleksi! Just the man I've been waiting to here from. Hold on a second, it's too fucking loud in here!' Aleksi continued to drive, the phone pressed to his ear. 'There we go,' Kristoff paused as he gathered himself on the other line Has everything been taken care of?' 'No, not yet.' Aleksi remained calm as he waited for the lashing from his boss. 'Aleksi! This isn't a god damn game of risk. It's like fucking checkers. Jump the bitch already and don't call me back until you do.' Click. * * * * * 'That was the biggest mistake you ever made.' The Prophecy continues to walk down the empty streets outside of his apartment. 'The moment you made it personal. The very second it became more than who was a better wrestler, a better player in the grand scheme of it all. There's no doubt to anyone out there that you don't belong in the same ring as me. Your cowardly acts that you try to pass off as 'controversial' are nothing but over zealous acts for attention.' 'Did you think stapling your demonic little sixes on my back was going to slow me down?' He shakes his head. 'It made me madder.' 'Did you think burning my face was going to put an end to me and help you get your title back?' He reaches a hand to his cheek. 'It pissed me off.' 'And did you think that threatening the woman I love would prevent me from getting my hands on you in fear of losing this.' Jason pulls the title off of his shoulder, holding it out in front of him. 'Come fucking get it, Raul. The only thing you've accomplished over the past four months is making me realize why I fight the good fight, so pieces of shit like you never get to call yourself a champion again.' 'I know you sold your soul a long, long time ago in hope that it would make the road to the top shorter. And in a sense, it did. It could have been ten more years before you ever got another chance at me, watching me climb the ranks of this sport from far below. But your actions, your cowardly attacks, it got you a third and final chance in record time to cement that little fact that you have been blocking out of your brain from day one.' 'You'll never beat me, Raul.' 'Not in this lifetime or the next.' * * * * * The glowing red ember of a lit cigarette hangs from the lips of Kristoff Melnikov. The ring leader of the Grey Wolf Gaming Club takes one final drag from his Camel Turkish Gold before flicking the remaining small bud from his mouth. Melnikov reaches for a bottle of Staraya Vodka that's odd shape resembles more of a miniature Taj Mahal than a bottle of Russian liquor. Melnikov grimaces after a quick swig of the ice cold vodka, slamming the bottle down near his weathered black combat boots that rest over the top of the desk in front of him. 'Yeah, so I stuck it to her, you know? I had to... the little slut was asking for it all night. Running 'round in that little tight skirt of hers, shaking her thing like it was the last night on earth for both of us,' Melnikov says, reaching for the bottle again. Standing in front of Melnikov is Aleksi Kravchenko. The Grey Wolf Gaming Club's top level 'collector' had grown accustom to listening to his boss talk, he did a lot of it, and Aleksi wasn't much of a speaker but he'd force himself at times to please his boss. 'Sounds like great fun,' Aleksi muttered. 'That it was.' Aleksi's eyes wandered around the room. For someone of his nature you would think he would be able to stand strong in front of Melnikov but ever since he was young he was never able to look Kristoff straight in the eyes. 'Now then.' Kristoff stated, using his free hand to shuffle through some papers. 'What did you find out about this...' 'Man?' Aleksi questioned. 'Person.' Kristoff said, his eyes glaring back at his employee. 'Nothing.' Kristoff's playful demeanor dissipitates within an instant as his grip on the bottle of vodka tightens. He stares back at Kravchenko, waiting for an explanation. 'The man...' Aleksi catches himself. 'The person is very evasive. I tracked 'em down twice but on both occasions he, or it, was able to elude me. Instead of wasting anymore time trying to find this.. needle in a haystack as they call it, I took a different direction.' 'For your sake, I hope it was one with better results.' 'It was,' Kravchenko said calmly, his eyes slowly becoming more focused on Kristoff as he explains himself. 'Instead, I decided to take your advice.' 'I'm listening.' Kristoff said, leaning forward. 'I decided to follow the girl.' * * * * * 'So I want you to listen closely, oh mighty Creator of Controversy. I want you to cherish this match, I want you really to embrace our time together at DEFIANCE. Because quite frankly, I'm getting damn tired of beating you, Raul. Cruise glances at one or two people as he continues walking along. 'And I'm getting even more tired of you trying to fuck with my life.' 'This is your chance at redemption. This is your opportunity to drive the stake into my heart because if you don't... I promise you that you'll end up with one in yours. That's what all of this has come down too. I can't express anymore what I want to do to you.' 'I can't come up with anymore clever ways to get it through your thick skull that you don't belong out there with me.' 'Fact is, I'm tired of having to preach to the world that I'm better then you and I'm sure they're getting tired of listening.' 'I've already proven my dominance over you a hundred times over but for everyone out there that wants to see me do it again.' At this point, Cruise pauses, embracing the cooling wind of the night. 'For the Highwaymen.' 'For Marie.' 'And for myself...' 'I'm going to do it one last time.' * * * * * 'Marie?' Kristoff questioned. Aleksi nods. 'That wasn't part of the deal with Jason.' 'There wasn't any other way to get the results he wanted.' Kristoff ran his fingers across the stubble on his chin, taking this in. 'Did you harm her?' he asked, his chest now hunching over the desk top. 'Of course not. Even I have my limits.' 'What exactly are we talking about here then?' 'I went inside her home.' 'In Santa Monica?' 'Minnesota.' Kravchenko said casually. 'I'm not following.' Kristoff muttered as he took another swig of vodka. 'She's not exactly who Jason thinks he she is. Her father -- well.' Kristoff's eyes widen. 'What? What did you find out?' Aleksi looks up, his eyes locked on those of Kristoff. 'Everything.'
