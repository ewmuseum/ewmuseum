A Tough Visit(ppv rp#2)

23 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/23/2007 Years after his brother Jorge’s death, El Diablo was constantly remembering that fateful day and the miserable days that followed. He was unable to sleep because of the memories and with the biggest two matches of his FUSE career in one night, he had to find a solution. Losing his older brother was hard enough, but the treatment from his parents after it practically destroyed him. They blamed him, they blamed their fourteen year old son for the death of their eighteen year old son. Most people would crack under that kind of emotional stress, but somehow he survived it and became the man he is today. Of course he still struggles with his emotions when he remembers how his parents treated him, and has decided to exorcise those inner demons by visiting Jorge’s grave. Perhaps then he will gain some clarity on the situation and be able to get closure. Perhaps he will beat his recent insomniac ways. TUESDAY 21ST AUGUST 2007 3:45pm The sun blazed over the horizon on the hot summers day, the sky was blue and there wasn’t even a cloud in sight. The humidity was overwhelming, but that wasn’t going to stop El Diablo on his march to his brother’s grave. "Not long to go now," he said to himself. Walking up a slight hill, along a dusty path, The Mexican Devil was preparing himself to see Jorge’s grave for the first time in over five years. He passed several large trees as he slowly headed towards the graveyard. As much as he had wanted to go to clear his mind, he was still somewhat reluctant. "The long trek home was always going to be difficult," he stated aloud. Dressed in a white vest, long black shorts and brown sandals, El Diablo looked a mess. His long black hair looked thin and unhealthy, he hadn’t shaved for a while so had a silly looking beard partly grown and his green eyes were severely bloodshot, with deep black bags hanging beneath them. He looked in such a mess that people could easily confuse him for a homeless man. "Come on Raul, you can do this," he tried to gain some confidence. The Mexican Devil was normally a very confident person, especially when it came to his wrestling ability and his stature in the wrestling business. However, this confidence never spilled over into his personal life. He certainly wasn’t confident around his family growing up, or with members of the opposite sex, in fact his attitude away from wrestling is that of an insecure man who lacks confidence in all the key areas. But somehow he manages. He manages by pretending that he is normal and that everything is okay in his life. "Almost there," he whispered. The sun beamed down on him as he reached the top of the hill, being able to see the entrance to the graveyard. As usual there was a stone wall surrounding the cemetery, and big black gates which were open, as if it was an invitation to enter the morbid world of dead people. "Stop wasting time, just go," said The Creator of Controversy. Wiping the sweat from his brow, El Diablo began to walk down the hill towards the cemetery entrance. He gulped in nervous anticipation of his destination. "Okay, here," he proclaimed. Reaching the gates, The Master of Manipulation stepped onto the long faded green grass, which looked like it hadn’t been mowed for several months. The weather had been so hot lately that the ground was perfectly solid as he walked over it, in search of Jorge’s grave. It wasn’t really a search, as despite scarcely visiting it in recent years, El Diablo knew perfectly well where it was located. "Hello Jorge," he remarked. Standing a few feet away from the grave, El Diablo noticed how badly kept the grave had been. Tall grass surrounded the grave, with thick weeds wrapped all around it. Jorge’s name wasn’t even visible because of the grass and weeds. "They couldn’t come and tidy you up?" he stated. Looking around the cemetery, The Mexican Devil noticed that most tombstones had been well looked after, Jorge’s seemed to be one of the few exceptions. "Does Dad not come to see you?" Diablo asked. Stepping forward onto the grave, El Diablo kneeled down and started to tear the grass away, so that he could read Jorge’s tombstone for the first time in five years. "I’m sorry I can’t get rid of the weeds, they’re too thick and wrapped around tightly," he quietly said. El Diablo stood up and took a step back, to see if the writing on the stone was visible. JORGE DIAZ 5/12/1970 - 9/29/1988 The words were mostly visible, which was a minor victory for The Mexican Devil. "I’m sorry that I haven’t been here the last five years," he mentioned to his brother as if he still existed. The Creator of Controversy was unconcerned that he was standing on his brother’s grave, as Jorge had been cremated like most of the other occupants of this graveyard. "Angela and I are doing well. We had a bit of a rough patch recently, but worked things out. She’s actually here with me, not right here, but here in Puebla. We’re still going strong, despite her being a reason I ended up in that mental institute. That reminds me, I won’t be able to keep my promise to you and name my first child Jorge, as unfortunately I can’t ever have children. I’m sterile. I know, it’s not great news, but it’s something I can tell you," he told his brother some private information. As El Diablo told his recent life to his brother, an elderly man was walking by and heard what he had said. "Don’t worry about that son, there’s always adoption," he called out to El Diablo. Interrupted from talking to his dead brother, Diablo turned to the old man, who was only 5’5 and stick thin, with white hair and wearing spectacles, and stared a vicious hole right through him. "I wasn’t talking to you," Diablo snapped back. Noticing it was his cue to leave, the old man began to slowly walk away, much to The Master of Manipulation’s delight. "Sorry about that, Jorge," he apologised, "But yeah, there will be no more Jorge Diaz’s," he finished. Sighing, El Diablo rubbed at his exhausted eyes. "Why are you haunting me? I can’t sleep because I’m having non-stop dreams about the crash and seeing you in the hospital. Why are you doing this to me?" he stated loudly. Slowly, the hot day began to cool down as large white clouds started to fill the blue sky overhead. "You know Mom and Dad blamed me for you dying. How terrible is that? I was fourteen and somehow I was responsible for you? You were my older brother, you should have been responsible for me. But no, I got treated like shit by them, I endured weeks of mental torture by them. NO WONDER I AM HOW I AM!" El Diablo shouted to him. Angered at his recollection, Diablo stomped at the ground like a little child would. "You die and somehow that is my fault? When grandma died was that my fault too? Hell, whenever anyone dies is that my fault? I’m not the fucking grim reaper, but apparently Mom and Dad thought so little of me that they actually believed I was," he ranted. Soon enough the sky was full of clouds, which were beginning to turn grey, as The Mexican Devil yelled at Jorge. "I shouldn’t have been surprised at how they treated me though. They always loved you more. The only things I ever got were your hand-me downs. Mom and Dad never bought me anything new, it was always you that got the new things. You got the new clothes, the new footballs, you even got the choice of what meal you wanted. I was only an afterthought to them," Diablo stated. While El Diablo complained about his childhood, the clouds in the sky had turned a dark grey colour and sure enough, rain started to pour down as the heavens opened. Wearing summer clothing, The Creator of Controversy was getting soaked with rain, but didn’t seem to notice or more accurately didn’t really care. "Do you have any idea what it’s like being second best? Not just once, but your entire life. I was always second to you. I was born after you and I was treated like I was lesser than you. I don’t blame you Jorge, but fuck, you could have helped me out, you were my big brother, it was your job to look after me. Instead you just went along with your life and didn’t think twice about how they were towards me, YOU COULD HAVE STOPPED THEM! THEY RUINED ME, I AM DAMAGED GOODS BECAUSE OF WHAT HAPPENED, AND I’M NEVER GOING TO FORGIVE THEM OR YOU FOR THAT!" Diablo screamed his grievances to his late brother. Loud rumblings of thunder began to echo through the skies above as the rain ran down El Diablo’s body. His white vest was now completely see-through and stuck tight to his chest, while his feet were red raw as the rain poured down onto them. "Since I was last here, I’ve retired and then returned to wrestling. I’m in FUSE now and I’m the current LiveWire Champion," he told him, beaming with pride. Having calmed down a little, The Mexican Devil was proud to be informing his deceased sibling about his wrestling success. Shortly before the car accident which claimed Jorge’s life, Jorge had mentioned he was going to be a wrestler, so El Diablo became a wrestler as a dedication to his brother, although in recent years he had lost his way. "Everything is going perfectly there. I’m winning matches, I’m a champion and I’ve aligned myself with the most dominant group in wrestling history, The Affliction. Since joining them my career has really taken off, you’d be proud of me. I’m at the top of my game and as long as I’m part of The Affliction, I always will be," he proudly proclaimed. As he continued talking to Jorge, the thunder had ceased and the rain began to slow down. Soaking wet, Diablo’s clothes were sticking to his body, as his wet hair dripped down onto his tired face. "The big pay-per view War In Washington is just around the corner and I’m that indispensable to the company that I’m in two matches. Not just any old matches, but a match for my title and a new concept Tower match. Two of the most important matches on the card and I’m in them both. I told you that I’d become a top wrestler and here I am, before you, a vital part of the show, a vital part of the company and a vital part of The Affliction," El Diablo stated. The rain had stopped, but the mud beneath the grass which had been rock hard, was now very wet and Diablo’s feet were buried in mud. "I open the big event. I open it by going one on one with Jason Cruise. I know, I know, you’d be thinking that he’s related to Tom Cruise, but he’s not, although I’m sure he is just as big a freak. He’s probably a scientologist too, but does wrestling really need another scientologist? Hell it didn’t even need the first one. I promise you Jorge, that I will defeat Jason Cruise and not only will I retain my LiveWire Championship Belt, but I’ll get back your gold pocket watch which he stole from me. He will not go unpunished for stealing that. It will be the first and last thing he ever takes from me," El Diablo warned. Just thinking about Jason Cruise with that pocket watch was enough to make The Master of Manipulation’s blood boil. Running through his mind were all the scenarios of what The Prophecy could be doing to the watch, after all he already knew how far Cruise could go. "I hate to think about what’s happened to it, but I’ll get my revenge in the ring and then take it back, so that I leave that ring with my LiveWire Title and your pocket watch, our pocket watch, the Diaz pocket watch. He’ll regret all that he’s done to me," remarked The Mexican Devil. Looking up to the sky, El Diablo noticed that the clouds had dispersed, yet the sky remained dark in colour. The Creator of Controversy then looked back down, with his eyes fixated on Jorge’s tombstone. "Ironically I’ll be facing him again in the Tower match, and eight others. But I have an ally, The River Rat Lane Stevens, so the tactic will be to eliminate everyone, leaving it as El Diablo vs. Lane Stevens. But we’ll work something out so that we don’t have to square off. But everyone else is fair game, and I intend to inflict pain upon all of them unlucky enough to reach the second phase of the match," he said in some sinister delight. Suddenly the sky began to turn blue again, but the sun was nowhere in sight, as tiny crickets surfaced, chirping away to an annoying tune. "Damn crickets," he remarked. Pulling his feet out from the mud, El Diablo started stamping around to try and kill the crickets. But to no avail, as they continued to chirp and The Mexican Devil’s feet remained covered in mud. "Perhaps I should be grateful for my childhood. Perhaps I should be glad with what I had to put up with, as it is all that pain and anguish that spurs me on to be a success. I have been successful and I’ll continue to be, and for that I owe some element of thanks to Mom and Dad, not much but some," he cruelly stated. Scratching at his now damp beard, The Mexican Devil noticed the sun coming out as his body temperature began to rise after being out in the wet for so long. "I’ve always fed off of pain, it makes me a better person. However, I loathe the people that inflict it, and thankfully you only hurt me inadvertently, Jorge. I will always remember you, but I hope that now I have faced my demons that I’ll be sane once more. I need sleep Jorge, and as much as I love you, I need to let this go," Diablo said remorsefully. The sun was shining vigorously as evidenced by the rainbow he could see in the distance. Suddenly though, El Diablo was taken aback by a shocking turn of events. "You better remember me," said the voice. Recognising the voice, El Diablo slowly turned around, and as clear as day he saw his brother Jorge standing right in front of him. "Jorge?" he queried. Nodding, Jorge was still eighteen years old, 5’7, 186lbs with long black hair and a thin moustache. "This can’t be real," The Mexican Devil tried to make sense of the situation. Smiling the very same smile that El Diablo remembered from a few moments before the car crash that claimed Jorge’s life, he seemed very real. "Anything is possible Raul," Jorge responded. The Creator of Controversy was unable to fathom it, as he saw Jorge before him, wearing the same clothes he wore on the night of the accident. "NO! This is just my lack of sleep. It’s causing me to hallucinate," Diablo tried to reassure himself. Closing his eyes for thirty seconds, El Diablo tried to prove this wasn’t possible. When he opened his eyes, sure enough, Jorge was gone. "I really need to get some sleep," he said aloud. Beginning to walk away, The Master of Manipulation felt he had finally exorcised his demons. "You can’t get rid of me that easily," Jorge’s voice said. Shocked, El Diablo turned around, but no one was there.
