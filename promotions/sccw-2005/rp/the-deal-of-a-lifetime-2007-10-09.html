The deal of a lifetime!

9 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/07 “My head is so sore today, I feel like I have been backed over by an elephant driving a train” AJ is lamenting to himself as he gingerly walks around his new apartment doing general morning stuff. He stretches an early morning stiff yawn as he flips the kettle switch and puts a tea bag into the cup. “Why do I do this? I can’t find a decent cup of tea in this city. Where are the PG tips when you need it?” AJ cracks his neck as he wanders over his open plan kitchen into the lounge area as he drops himself wearily onto his sofa. His black dressing gown embroidered with the FUSE logo on his left breast in blue is tied at the waist, his chest shows at the v shape from neck to the tied section, AJ closes his eyes and presses down his right hand onto his chest, letting out a deep, uncomfortable grunt. “This is what it feels like to be beaten then? Fuck it hurts” AJ’s kettle clicks off and AJ manages to drag himself up from the sofa to the kitchen again and pours himself a cup of tea. In doing this, he flicks to ESPN on the TV. They are running down all the sports stories from the world of sport, taking a sip, AJ sees that England have beaten Australia in the rugby world cup, AJ smiles to himself, remembering the days in England while he was a young boy, there was not much to smile about while growing up, but he remembers fondly trying to sneak off to watch the rugby games at the local park. At another time, if he was someone else – he dreamed of being a rugby player. Those days are long gone, now he is here, in FUSE, smarting from his debut and first hard fought loss. AJ flicks over and settles on the wrestling channel, which is showing a documentary about influential families in the world of wrestling. Half interested, half distracted by the searing pain in his neck, AJ finishes his tea and heads toward the bedroom, before he takes two paces he suddenly stops after hearing this on the TV. “…..And after the break we take a look at the Tian dynasty out of Pittsburgh. They have helped generations of superstars begin their careers, we have an in depth interview with the new owner of the Tian academy, Ms Mian-Yi Yeo.” Suddenly interested in the TV, AJ forgets about his pain and practically jumps back onto his sofa; a documentary about the woman in the gym he was in the other night has piqued his interest. He turns the TV up slightly and watches with intent. Sure enough after the commercial breaks the interview begins and there she was, Mian-Yi, looking as delightful as she did that day, wearing a peach coloured blouse with a black suit top. Her hair tied back again into a Japanese crop and her smile radiant. The interview begins with the slightly pug faced interviewer. “So, Ms Yeo, welcome to the show,” “Thank you” “We have been looking at the influential families in the world of wrestling, how does it feel to be ranked up there with the Mcmahons and Harts of this world? Mian- Yi smiles a luxurious and charming smile, slightly nodding as she takes the compliment. ”Well we just do what we can you know, since my great grandfather started it all up the philosophy has been simple. Train raw talent when they need us in the ways of wrestling, it is an art form which not any old fighter can take up and be successful in.” ”I understand you actually train the wrestlers for free? All of your money is gained through merchandising once they have made it?” “That’s right; the only payment we get from our talent is the merchandising rights they sign over to us when they begin training. We guarantee them success and a career if they follow the 3 simple rules of Tian’s academy.” “Which are?” “Rule number 1. You have to be serious about the sport, and have a desire to win.” “Obviously” “Rule number 2. When in training, our talent has to, I mean MUST, do everything they are asked, we ask them to do things for a reason and that is to develop into a wrestler which the world can be proud of.” “It makes sense, what is the third rule?” “We are a very selective academy, basically we will only take on one person at a time and exclusively manage, train, and advise them. So the third rule is simple. You must be the kind of person who fits in with our image. We will select the individual, they won’t come to us.” “Wow, that sounds very exclusive, so let me get this straight, you pick up a guy who you like to train and manage for free? Then ask him to hand over all his marketing rights to you for your business? What’s the catch?” “No catch. Its how we have done it since the beginning. We will only choose someone else when the person we are managing retires.” The interviewer switches his seat slightly before continuing with the interview. “Are you managing anyone at the moment?” Mian-Yi thinks for a moment, and shakes her head “No, not at the moment, the past 10 years have been difficult, the talent dried up and we couldn’t find anyone with the appropriate talent, image or mindset who would be able to take the training. We will only recruit if we know they will go to the top. I just haven’t found anyone I can connect with yet.” “You do the training yourself?” Mian-Yi laughs a small, I can’t believe it sort of laugh “You don’t have to sound too surprised. I have had generations of wrestling in my family, I know everything there is to know about wrestling and how to wrestle, just because I am female it doesn’t mean that I can’t grapple and fight with the best of them.” The interviewer seems embarrassed now he has managed to annoy his guest by assuming. “I’m sorry” he flushes “lets move on” Mian-Yi smiles in semi forgiveness. Nodding to the interviewer as he continues “Do you actually have anyone lined up for the future? Anyone in the world of wrestling catch your eye?” “Well there is one. He isn’t the usual type we go for, a bit brash and cocky, as well as being in the business before, but we can work with him. We already had an initial contact with him and he is very passionate about improving, something we find high on our priorities. We may be in a position to make an announcement in the coming weeks, we just have to contact him again and see how interested he will be before we negotiate. If he agrees then it will be a very exciting time for him and the federation he is involved in.” “Rumour has it that you spent a long time with Andre Jensen, is he the man you are talking about?” On hearing his name, AJ suddenly sits up straight. She DID speak to him last week; they DID talk to him for hours. Is he the man they are discussing then? AJ carries on watching intently. He sees Mian-Yi smile, and in the diplomatic way that only a businesswoman or politician can come up with answer. “Well, we will just have to see won’t we? Next week I am sure there can be some sort of announcement if everything goes to plan with who we want.” “Is it AJ or not?” AJ sits up straight, this opportunity for him seems like it could be interesting, Mian-Yi as a manager? The lady he has had thought about for the past week constantly – the woman who seems to know him better than he knows himself at the moment. His manager? Surely it isn’t him she is talking about? He looks back at the screen again in rapt attention; Mian-Yi flushes and nods slightly. “Fine, ok, the cat’s out of the bag. The man I am looking at is actually AJ. We haven’t spoken to him about the details yet, I only spent a couple of hours with him last week, but to be honest that was enough.” Mian-Yi is positively beaming with this revelation, the joy and excitement evident on her face. Watching the TV, AJ is sitting there in silence, stunned at this revelation. Mian-Yi, wants to manage him? She wants to train him, all for free? A thousand questions go through his head. How will it work? What will I need to do? When does it start? Will it be Mian-Yi who does it? What does it mean? He jumps suddenly as his phone rings. Scurrying across to answer it he nearly falls over his sofa. The person on the other end wasn’t Mian-Yi, but someone who he least wanted to speak to. “What the fuck is this AJ, you have the Tian’s managing you now? You don’t cross Jimmy the Bishop! I sent Smythe to look after you because of your past, if you know what is best for you then you will tell that woman to get off my turf! You are my star, I aint having you signing over your marketing rights to no two bit academy of fucking Asian sluts!” With that Jimmy slams the phone down furiously. AJ holds the receiver in his hand for a long while, thinking about 4 words. Because of your past……… Your past? That’s a few times now people have mentioned his past. He also thinks about what Mian-Yi said. “Been in the business before.” When, where? Why can’t he remember any of this? AJ wonders for a moment, even Barry Kitana mentioned his past. What is so special about a guy who left England from a prison a month ago? AJ still stands with the receiver in hand for a number of minutes, mulling these questions in his head. He is snapped back into attention by the door knocking, and a lot of commotion outside. He walks to the window first and sees a scrum of media outside all jostling for position, trying to get the best angles possible. He narrows his eyes and closes the curtains muttering to himself. The door bangs again, louder this time. “AJ, it’s me, can we talk?” It was the unmistakeable voice, (for him anyway) of Mian-Yi. Yeo at the door. Obviously the interview wasn’t live. AJ answers the door cautiously and Mian-Yi walks into the room, alone, with the paparazzi attempting to take good pictures. She looks at AJ, then the paparazzi and takes a deep breath before putting her hand up to shoo the paparazzi away. Amazingly, they do go with no quibbles or murmurs. She shuts the door when they have gone then looks at AJ, the businesswoman in her is there, looking at AJ professionally, no smiles, no charm just a negotiation. “So, I take it you saw the interview? We were trying to get a hold of you before the interview aired so at least you would know about it, sorry about letting you find out like this.” AJ, somewhat flummoxed can only shrug and blurt out some words “Phone, no connection, move in.” Mian-Yi smiles, the first time she had smiled since she walked into the room. AJ manages to muster up enough wits to offer Mian-Yi a seat and a drink. Mian-Yi sits on the leather sofa and asks for a green tea. While she does this she opens up the briefcase she carries and starts taking out folders and documents. “So Mr Jensen” “Call me AJ” “Sorry, but today is business, I would prefer Mr Jensen if that is ok with you?” AJ shrugs and hands the tea to Mian-Yi while he sits on the table opposite her. She looks at AJ sternly, after all he is sitting on a table wearing nothing but a dressing gown while she wants to negotiate, realising this AJ thinks for a moment. Pausing while he sips his tea “As it is business, I like to be comfortable Ms Yeo, I hope you don’t mind.” He lets off a cheeky grin as Mian-Y slightly blushes and carries on producing the reams of files from her briefcase. AJ thinks to himself that the briefcase must be some sort of magic, as there is no way that they can hold all this. He thinks nothing more of it though. “So, Mr Jensen, as you have no doubt seen, you will be offered the training scholarship of Tian’s academy…..” “In return for my marketing blah blah blah, I get trained, managed and looked after through my career and you guarantee success.” “In a nutshell, yes.” “I have a few questions” Mian-Yi looks at AJ imploringly, she had either not done the negotiations before on her own, or she hadn’t done them for a while. She was obviously not used to this stage of proceedings people asking her things anyway. “Go ahead” “You said last week, you don’t own the company alone, at least not the gym. On the interview you said you do, I am confused.” Mian-Yi nods appreciatively. “You are on the ball Mr Jensen. I did say those things. Things are a little…..Complicated in my family. I am not the sole owner of the company. That much is true, but I do control most aspects of it. My brothers, well, they haven’t really got the wrestling nous or brains to carry on the dynasty, they would rather see it dismantled and turned into a casino.” Mian-Yi looks disgusted at this and takes a moment to calm herself down before she continues “But enough of that, you will meet them in due course I expect.” AJ nods slightly as he takes a sip of tea and shifts on his table, making himself more comfortable. “How exactly would all this arrangement work if I did agree?” “Good question, all I can tell you at this stage is that I would be your manager and in charge of overall training. I would not be onscreen, I would be a behind the scenes manager making sure you get the right deals off screen, every decision you make in your wrestling world at FUSE would be yours and yours alone. You can ask me for advice, I will tell you whatever I can and help you at any time, but you would be in overall charge. All I insist is that you would protect your image at all times so to maximise your marketing potential. As far as training goes, do everything we ask of you and it will work.” “Do I have to be face or heel?” Mian-Yi looks at AJ inquisitively, here is a man last week who only knew how to fight, and had a blasé idea of the importance of wrestling, now he knows the term face and heel, not even good guy and bad guy. “The crowd decides that.” “What do you mean?” “In a way, Frost was right this weekend, wrestling fans are a fickle bunch, if you do good things they cheer, if you do bad things they boo. However, what Frost doesn’t realise, or maybe doesn’t care about is that if they respect you, and you put your body through the mill and entertain them, it doesn’t matter. Win the crowd over with talent and you can pretty much do what you like and still have them on your side.” “You are saying even if they boo then they might still like you?” “Yes, a true measure on a guy’s popularity is how many signs in the crowd, how many t shirts are sold, fans vote with their wallets AJ. It’s the first rule of business.” Taking this in, AJ shrugs in agreement; it makes sense when you put it that way. “Anyway, all I want is an answer from you AJ. I appreciate I am probably boring you and you need to go to recover from last night……” “I’m in” “You didn’t take long to think” AJ again shrugs; he fixes a stare straight into the eyes of Mian-Yi, a stare of conviction and some warmth, which Mian-Yi gives back to him. “Ever since I met you in the gym last week Ms Yeo, I have thought of nothing else but meeting you again. You see, I need a manager. You know the business as well as anyone it would seem. I am going to be honest with you and say you interest me as a person as well, some of the things you were saying were fascinating and……” “I know where this is going Mr Jensen, it is strictly business you and I when this contract is signed” “I wasn’t…” “No explanations needed Mr Jensen. Please just sign” Mian-Yi seems a little flustered now, as if she can’t wait for AJ to sign the documents and she can get away from the scene. AJ takes the hint and signs the paper where Mian-Yi points. Hurriedly she packs away her things and searches for her jacket and glasses. Making mumbled apologies she storms out of the building in a flash, the paparazzi following her every movement again like a bunch of wasps around a cream cake. It seems that the whole press thing is more for her than the contract, maybe one day AJ will find out about that. AJ still holds the pen in his hand, bemused by what had just happened. He mumbles to himself the end of the question he was about to ask before she got the wrong idea “I was wondering why everyone is saying I have been in the business before.” AJ sighs as he heavily drops himself onto the sofa again, grimacing in pain as he feels a shooting stab up his back. “And if this management comes with a damn masseuse”
