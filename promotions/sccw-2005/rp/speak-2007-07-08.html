Speak

8 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.08.07 July 8th, 2007 2:17 P.M. CST John paced back and forth, his hand rubbing his chin. Behind him was a black cloth backdrop, holding the FUSE Wrestling logo upon it. He glanced from the floor to the logo then back to the floor and to the technicians as he paced. "Are you ready, John?" One of them asked. He quietly shook his head and continued to pace, now running his hand through his hair. As part of the big number one contenders match at KillZone forty-five, John had been requested for a small segment prior to the match. "Just give me a minute or two more." John replied. The cameraman and producer sighed deeply as they watched John exhale sharply as he swung his arms at his side. He knew he needed something to rival what Tsuyoshi Tanaka was going to record, but John just couldn’t think of anything. "What’s the problem?" The producer barked. John held up his hand as he paused in front of the curtain and stared at the FUSE logo. The company that made him a star was relying on his creative abilities to help hype their up-coming show and nothing was clicking. "Just, let me think." He said. He sighed heavily as he dropped his glare back to the floor and began pacing again. Tension was building between John and the producer. It was evident that she either had other places to be and better things to do. But coming up with something worthy of broadcast on National television wasn’t easy. Even if you had been given a week to work on it. "I got nothing." John said. He threw up his arms and let them fall, smacking his legs. The producer buried her face in her hands and began speaking under here breathe. John was a little taken back by her reaction. "Okay, fine. Turn on your camera. I’ll give you what I’ve got and you can determine whether it’s worthy of broadcast." He said sternly. The producer slowly looked up as John stepped in front of the backdrop and cleared his throat. A deep breathe followed as the producer counted down from five and pointed towards the Paragon. John’s arms were folded, producing a somewhat intimidating look. "Ten weeks ago Clinton Sage stole the FUSE Universal Title from me for the first time. But it wasn’t the last. The bastard did it again just five weeks ago. And now I have that chance to take one more step towards avenging his tasteless actions. I take on Tsuyoshi Tanaka to determine a number one contender for the FUSE Universal Title. As part of this huge match, FUSE has asked me to drive the hour and a half from my hometown to Chicago and proved something entertaining for you fans to watch. They want something to match-up to what Tanaka is going to produce. And I’d love to give it to you. There’s just one small problem. I entertain in the ring. I don’t need to stand in front of a camera to prove how tough I am by pledging to beat my opponent for the week or say how much I hate them. I prove how tough I am by climbing into that ring and fighting. If you guys want to put my speaking up against Tanaka’s, then do it. No one is going to walk away saying "Oh wow, John Covel talked way better than Tanaka." And I don’t give a shit. I learned the wrestling business as an in-ring competition. So what if I can’t stand up to Tanaka or Lane Stevens on the microphone. My entertainment happens in that ring. That’s how I make a living in this business. My work ethic is second to nobody in FUSE and very few in the wrestling business today. I go out and work my ass off for the fans of FUSE. Whether they give me the respect I deserve, I don’t fucking care. I get self-satisfaction out of the physical aspects in wrestling. So go ahead and air this. Put it up with Tanaka’s masterpiece of a promo. I guarantee you when we step into that ring, I will prove just why I should be the number one contender. I will prove why I belong in FUSE. And I will prove just why I am the Paragon of Piledrivers. I will show you just what John Covel stands for." John pushed past the camera and grabbed the door handle. As he pulled the door open. He looked over his shoulder. "Take it or leave it. That’s what you’re getting." He walked through the doorway and released the handle, allowing it to close.
