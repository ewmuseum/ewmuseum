Part Thirteen: So Endeth The Lesson

7 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/07/09 Why are we here? What is our purpose upon this planet? Philosophers have devoted whole libraries worth of book to debate on the subject. People with plastic smiles and empty words offer up meaningless platitudes and empowerment slogans. We spend an obscene amount of money desperately searching for that one thing that will make us thin, rich, happy…that will enable the universe to make some form of sense. And all the while, G-d/Allah/Jesus/Yahweh/Buddah/the Universal Consciousness/whatever the hip and trendy word is this week…that force which keeps the universe from skidding out of control just observes us. Observes our words and our actions and marvels at how we ever managed to crawl out of the primordial ooze in the first place. But it still hopes. It has faith, it believes in us against all evidence. And it tries…it tries to show us, in its way, what the universe is fundamentally all about. Once you strip all the bullshit away, life is a series of lessons. Things happen, events occur and the universe does its damndest to let us know just how much we still need to learn… Some of us get it. Some of us take those lessons to heart, learn what is to be learned and continue on with their lives. Some of these are the great and good, the folks who try to leave the world a better place than they found it. Just as many are the average and every day, those folks who just try and lives their lives as best they can. They are not the ones to worry about. Then there are the others. The ones who are fine so long as the universe supports their particular viewpoint…but stumble when it does not. The ones who do not question or probe but choose instead to rail against the inherent wrongness of the universe…simply for its failure to conform to their wants and desires. The ones who change the facts to fit their views. As a wise man once said, that can be particularly dangerous if you’re one of the facts that need changing. Still, the universe persists in simply trying to get us to understand. For along with its infinite knowledge, it has infinite patience. Which is a good thing. For there are times when it needs every bit of both. Lesson: Nothing is ever as it appears to be…whether for good or for ill. Alanna Marshall was resolutely failing to understand Amy Campbell. Which was, come to think of it, not an uncommon state of affairs. It wasn’t the trite, unimaginative insults that Campbell had struggled to come up with that were the cause of her confusion. Indeed, she was considering levying a "boredom tax" against the next person to call her mannish, Lance stupid or homicidal (or both) or their marriage some sort of sham just so that folks who prided themselves on their "smarts" showed at least the slightest bit of creativity every once in a while. No, "mouthiness" was something Alanna Marshall understood. She’s know plenty of folks who could be described as "mouthy", having the apparently compulsive urge to say the exact wrong thing at the exact wrong time. Most of them, however, had at least enough intelligence to realize that sometimes the best available option was to shut the fuck up. That didn’t seem to be the case with Ms. Campbell. Even when the basic concept of self-preservation would seem to dictate keeping one’s lips zipped, Amy Campbell just kept on yakking away. That meant, thought Alanna, that Amy would end up fitting into one of two categories. Truthfully, she wasn’t sure which possibility disturbed her more. The first category was the stupid. No, not stupid, exactly…more like "blissfully oblivious". The folks who believed that, because they were so beautiful/handsome/talented/gifted/"smart" that they were untouchable. That consequences happened to other people, not them. Alanna had known folks like that. Without exception, she had watched them crash and burn. The second category was the suicidal. These were the folks who didn’t shut up not because they didn’t believe in consequences but because they were looking for them. These were the folks who made a point of saying the wrong thing to the wrong person in anticipation of the explosion that would follow. They weren’t afraid of getting hurt…hell, getting hurt is what they wanted. Alanna sighed heavily, annoyed at the train of thought her mind was pursuing. Amy Campbell had ended up becoming far more of a thorn in her side…in both of their sides…than she would like to have admitted. Part of what Lance had promised her on their return to the business was that they would try and have more fun this time around, to try and enjoy themselves more…something which was most certainly not happening at the moment. Aimz was part of the problem, certainly. But the inane jabbering and constant verbal diarrhea masking a pathetic need for validation and approval…if it was just that, things would have progressed no further than just "g-d, is this bitch annoying!" What tended to complicate matters was Aimz’s involvement with the Dead Man’s Hand. Alanna had been convinced that, upon first seeing Aimz unite with the Hand, that things would turn out very, very badly…for the Hand. Aimz, she had thought, was a brand of crazy that Alex had never dealt with before. She still felt things would end badly…but now it was Aimz who was in for a world of hurt. Aimz certainly seemed to feel comfortable having the Hand as her own personal goon squad (a bit rich, Alanna thought, for someone who claimed to never need backup). There were two things Alanna knew for sure, however. One was that she knew the Hand (Desade especially) a lot longer and a hell of a lot better than Aimz did. There were things that Desade had done…if Aimz had any sense at all, she would run for the hills. The second thing? The second thing was that Aimz obviously had no idea what Alex was all about. And when she found out… …well, that wasn’t going to be pretty. Lance Marshall exhaled deeply, his brain coming to what he believed to be an inescapable conclusion. Alex Pierce was nowhere near as complicated as she pretended to be. Conniving? Sure. Duplicitous? Definitely. Evil? Well, that wasn’t a word Lance liked to toss around casually…but if any human being came close, it would certainly be Alex Pierce. But complicated? Heck no. What Alex Pierce boiled down to, Lance had decided, was a textbook control freak. Someone who needed to be in a position of power at all times, to have others subjugated to her will as a matter of course. If Alex Pierce was to say jump, the only acceptable answer was "Off which cliff, Madam Director?" Lance was barely aware of the clank made as he placed the weights back into their slot in the weight back. The burn he could feel in his muscles certainly meant the day’s workout had had a physical effect on him, without a doubt. But if you had asked him what he remembered about the workout, what he had specifically done during his time in the gym…he wouldn’t have been able to tell you. He wasn’t sure whether or not to be worried about that. As the hot water burrowed its way into his muscles, Lance leaned back against the shower wall, letting his mind drift slightly. He had already come to the conclusion that there was only one possible…screw that, only one acceptable outcome for his match at Defiance. The belt needed to be taken from Aimz. In as definite and decisive a manner as possible. Not because of Aimz herself (although Lance did find her unutterably annoying) but to serve as a message to the Hand. Lance had long ago accepted that he and the Hand would always hold each other in utter contempt. That they didn’t like each other didn’t bother him. But most of the Hand did hold a grudging measure of respect for him…if only for his sheer bulldog tenacity. But Aimz…Aimz outright dismissed him. Regarded him with contempt and a sneer, secure in the knowledge that the Hand would make sure that she always came out the winner. That could not be allowed to continue. Lance started slightly as the shampoo ran down his back, a stray thought suddenly barreling its way into his mind. It had been the match a few weeks back between Mayhem and Aimz. Lance remembered Mayhem kicking seven shades of hell out of Aimz (probably the only time he’d ever approve of anything Mayhem would do) when, all of a sudden… The image stood in his mind, as clear and as vivid as if it was happening in front of him at this very moment. Aimz lying dazed and defeated. Mayhem ready to strike once again. And Desade… Desade pleading with Mayhem. Begging him to stop. Desade. Begging. What the hell? Alex Pierce did not plead. Desade did not beg. Order Mayhem to stop, sure. Demanded that Mayhem stop, definitely. But begged him to? There was only person in the world that Desade would be willing to do that for. And Desade would only go to those lengths for Quinn because… Oh. Oh lord. Ha. HAH! Lance began to laugh, a slow chuckle slowly erupting into a full throated belly laugh. There was only one conclusion that he could draw. Oh, was this going to be good. There are those who seek to control those around them by making sure that everyone is under their thumb, ruling by intimidation and fear. Making sure that they have control have everything around them and bringing down an iron fist on anything that threatens that control. This is fine. There are those who call this particular style of rule "Machiavellian". This is not fine and is typically an indication that the person in question has never actually read Machiavelli. What Machiavelli does advocate is cultivating the admiration and respect of those beneath you. To those who choose to rule by fear and intimidation, Machiavelli argues that such a style of rule is a recipe for disaster and complete collapse. It isn’t a question of if. It’s a question of when. We now return you to your regularly scheduled lives, already in progress.
