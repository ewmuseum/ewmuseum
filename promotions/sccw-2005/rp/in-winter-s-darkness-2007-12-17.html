In Winter's Darkness

17 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/2007 'Why dont you go hang out in the back with the other curtain jerkers, while the main event guys stay here and handle their business.' - Jean Paul Levesque Route 15, Maine, on the way to Rockwood The cool winter breeze chills as the day to turns to dusk on the countryside. Trees on both sides of the road are covered with thick sheets of ice and have icicles hanging down from the branches. Snow covers the ground from the first flurry of the winter storm overhead. The fog coming in from the coast isn’t helping matters at all. The drive to the next town is an exhausting one. But so is the life of a professional wrestler. It’s just one long drive after another, going from town to town like a gypsy or a bard of old. Wear and tear breaks down your body until it suddenly hits you with hammer force after enough elbow drops, suplexes, and DDT’s have been given to you over the years. This is the life of VON DOOM. After going to Japan and then coming back to film a B-movie, he’s back on the road again struggling to make ends meet. He has been doing this for five years and has been having the time of his life until recently. It seems to him as though he is working harder in small venues where he’ll make only five bucks for the show than for the big shows at larger venues. The most fun he’s had here lately was in a deathmatch tournament at a high school gymnasium that had only ten fans there. He had gotten the edge back because of that night. It made him feel like he was when he was a child and first saw wrestling on the television. Ever since he was five he had wanted to be a professional wrestler and now he was living his dream. But it has been nothing short of a wild ride. Here recently, however, it has gotten to become a nightmare of epic proportions. The world of wrestling has made him depressed because of the state of things. Huge scandals, lots of deaths, and even more politics has been driving him bonkers. Most people outside the industry don’t know the hardships that wrestlers like him go through. It’s a hard way to make a living and that’s if you can survive the politics backstage. He never was well liked or beloved until here recently but hard work just doesn’t seem to pay off for him. He’s also known to be a loose cannon and a troublemaker, so that isn’t helping matters with his career either. He’s been trying to find peace for months but only can seem to grow more insance by the day. These long drives through the country to get from show to show have quickly become a peaceful experience for him. Soothing music plays as he drives along without a care in the world. Piano keys can be heard playing in the background as he heads to the hotel that he is staying at this evening. A soothing sense of calm surrounds him as he drives down the highway. The only time VON DOOM seems to feel at peace anymore is when he has been alone. Too long has he been surrounded by miscreants, deviants, psychopaths, and strange weirdos. He is most known for associated with these types but has done well at distancing himself from them as of late. Being a loner for once has done nicely for his reputation and gotten himself good marks in Japan from both the fans and wrestlers alike. For too long he was a joke and was made fun of by fans and peers alike. But since he returned from Japan, he has gone in a new direction. Destroyed are most of his former allies and all at his hands. Everyone hated almost all of his associates. His return to America led to their downfall. At small shows and big shows alike he attacked them. The most notable casualty is Captain Jack Morgan whom he left in a bloody heap during an Elimination Chamber match. He attacked his former friend three times during the matches, each one more brutal than the last. Morgan is in a hospital somewhere but that is not the subject or person that VON DOOM is about to address tonight. The dim street lights barely light the way through the woods as the sky turns to darkness. His rental car continues down the almost empty highway toward the next town. Animal eyes can be seen in the trees peering out into the darkness. The last sign said it was only eight miles away and that was awhile back, so he must be getting close to town. The trees sway as the wind picks up. The rental car he is driving starts to skid on the road from the black ice that covers this area of the highway. VON DOOM is starting to realize that this is not a good situation for him and maybe it’s a best idea to park on the side of the highway until morning where he can see. Just as he is about to do just that, he sees lights in the distance. This brings a smile to his face. "Well final-fucking-ly," he says upon seeing how close the town is. The car drives to the turn off before exiting the highway and heading into town. The town is dark and dreary at the moment, covered in fog as thick as rain clouds during a summer storm. Street lights can barely been seen and are creating an eerie glow on the fog as the car drives slowly down the road. Buildings on the sides of the streets can barely be seen. VON DOOM stops at a stop sign and looks at the paper on the seat beside him that has his hotel information on it. He picks it up and reads that he is looking for the Moosehead Motel. He has to reach Bangkor by three the next day for the show but has to stop there to meet an old friend. He drives down the lonely streets. Everything seems empty. Not a thing is stirring on the streets. The town seems rather empty to him. Lights are on in the windows but nothing is moving around. The whole place almost seems dead to him, like something out of a Stephen King novel. He drives through the fog until he comes upon the Moosehead Motel. There’s a big moose head on the top of the motel so he knows he’s at the right spot. The rental car drives toward the parking lot and slides into the nearest empty space. VON DOOM puts the car into park and shuts the lights off before turning off the ignition. He then sits in the driver’s seat for a moment staring out into the snow. He has many thoughts running through his head as he stares out into the snow covered bushes on the side of the building facing the parking lot. Then he gets back his train of thought and looks down at his attire. He’s wearing a dark blue sweat jacket with a black ‘OOC - Out Of Control’ T-shirt on, jeans, and black Vans tennis shoes. His short mid-length hair falls onto his face and he brushes it back. His cold blue eyes continue to stare down at the ground for moment. He then looks out the window again and sighs. "Yeah, I really dressed for this weather," he mutters before finally getting out of the car. The door is slammed shut and he walks to the back of the car. He then goes opens the trunk and pulls out his bag which weighs a ton. He has two weeks worth of baggage from all the shows he’s been appearing on. Most of it is clothes but he also has wrestling attire, wrestling boots, and other paraphernalia related to his job. VON DOOM then closes the trunk and then drags the bag to the front entrance of the motel. He looks up and sees the big moose head covered in snow looking down at him almost as if it is watching his every move. This gives him an uncomfortable feeling of dread, but he feels as if it is nothing more than simple paranoia. So upon realizing this, he walks inside of the motel and into the lobby. The lobby area is very big. There is a fireplace with an open flame that has a large moose head above it. Next to that are two couches with a coffee table and to the left of that are some tables for the breakfast area as well as a small television set that is currently playing the Weather Channel. He shakes off the snow and wipes his shoes on the doormat before going over to the reception desk. He goes over and smiles at the man there. The man is an older man who is rather obese. He is rather sluggish looking, has parted yet at the same time balding black hair, glasses, and hasn’t shaved in awhile by the looks of things. The man doesn’t even look up at him. Instead, he continues to read whatever he’s got on his lap so VON DOOM politely rings the bell. Upon hearing the bell, the man’s head slowly turns upward and looks at him. "Hello," he says in a rather droll tone. "How may I help you?" "I have a reservation for this evening," VON DOOM replies, handing the man the paper with all his information. "Check out at noon tomorrow?" The man slowly reaches into the drawer on the side of his desk and opens it up. He pulls out a key and hands it to VON DOOM. "Room twenty six Mister…," he pauses and then looks at the name on the paper. "von Doom." The man then hesitates for a moment as if to say something but then waits again before finally saying it. "Are you James von Doom the wrestler from the TV?" he asks in a slow tone. VON DOOM sighs and rolls his eyes as if he’s been asked this question a million times by a million fans. "Yes, I am VON DOOM that guy from the TV," he replies in a monotone just to show how uninterested he is in his celebrity. He has more important matters on his mind. As he turns to head down the dark, gloomy hallway to his room, the man just has to ask him the most annoying question any wrestler wants to hear because they get it all the time from fans. "Can I get your autograph?" the man asks as VON DOOM growls with displeasure. He then walks back, signs the autograph quickly, and then heads to his room as the man looks down at the autograph to see that the name on it says John Ellis. "HEY!" the man exclaims but by then VON DOOM is gone and halfway down the hallway. As he heads down the hallway to find his room, he notices room twenty and eyes it closely as he walks by. The person in that room is his real reason for being here, not just having to make a stop before a wrestling show. VON DOOM quickly heads down to his room and slides the key in to open the door. He sneaks inside and closes the door behind him before dragging his bag over by the bed before turning the light on. He looks around and sees how smallish the room is but he doesn’t care. This is not about a room or a show. It’s about saving his legacy. For all these many months he has been perfecting his plan in secret. In his solitude, he has been marking wrestlers off his secret list. A list that all of the people on it only share one thing in common. They were brought into the wrestling industry by VON DOOM. In his opinion, all of them are a disgrace to the sport and his reputation has been damaged due to his association with each and every one of them. Some of them are a disease upon this sport and he is the cure in his mind. VON DOOM goes over and unzips his bag. Once its unzipped, he reaches in and pulls out a black baseball bat. He looks at it closely and smiles. "Next one off the list," he says with a devious grin as he looks at the list and pulls out a pen. He goes down the list and every name is checked off. Some of them include Captain Jack Morgan, "Army Ranger" John Collingwood, Chris and Mike Savage, Dick N. Uranus, Schism, and Mantra. He looks down and marks off the next name on the list before he folds it back up and puts it in his pocket. Like a statue he does nothing for hours during the storm but sit and wait in his room until the right time. The wind slams into the motel for hours. The power went out about three times in a span of two hours but still he waited until everything was quiet enough to go about his plan. Now, it has reached that time. VON DOOM gets up off his bed and goes over to turn off the light bat in hand. Once he is done doing that he quietly tip toes to the door so as to not draw any attention to himself. He slowly opens the door up and closes it behind him. The door slowly creaks before the door finally closes all the way. He then peers up and down the hallway. Nothing is stirring so he sneaks over to room twenty. Quietly he puts his ear to the door. He hears nothing. This brings a smile to his face. This is exactly what he wanted to hear. He slowly brings his hand to the door and knocks three times. Quickly he puts his ear to the door. Inside, he hears stirring and the groggy moan of a man just getting up. "I’m coming," the voice says from the inside. Soon, he hears the rustling of sheets followed by the footsteps of a man walking toward the door. He smiles in anticipation as the footsteps stop at the door. VON DOOM grips the bat tightly, ready to end this guy’s career. The door to the room opens and there stands a man with long dark hair. He’s got an average build and looks groggy. He’s wearing boxers and a tank top white undershirt. "Hello?" the man asks as he starts rubbing his eyes. VON DOOM smiles deviously as he raises the bat in preparation of swinging. "Hello Evil Dead," he says with a demonic grin. "Remember me?" He then swings the bat wildly. A thud is heard. He walks in and shuts the door. The sounds of a bat striking a man repeatedly is heard from the inside of the room. Soon, the door opens again and VON DOOM comes out into the hallway. He quickly closes the door and strolls back to his room like nothing happened. He opens up the door and goes back inside. Not a sound is heard from room twenty six the rest of the night. The next morning around ten VON DOOM calmly exited his room and turned in his key before leaving still acting as if nothing had happened. They didn’t find Evil Dead until later on in the afternoon. He has multiple injuries, including a collapsed lung, and may never wrestle again. The police say there was no motive and think that it might’ve been a revenge attack by the boyfriend of a woman who he has been seeing. The man was arrested later that day. No word yet on when he will face the judge.
