High Heels n' Heel Hooks

5 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 5th, 2009 > 'Fire is the test of gold; adversity, of strong men.' Roman Philosopher, Seneca - - - - - - - - - - Just Wrestling's Lethal Lottery Tour made an ample setting for Kelly Master's professional wrestling debut. The former amateur wrestling prodigy and well known MMA fighter picked up a victory over The Illustrious Face-Eater and Captain Pollution in a tag match. It was Masters' extensive submission training that proved to be the deciding factor, as he tapped out Captain Pollution with his patented Achilles' Heel. It's only one small challenge met along a difficult road that now runs through PCW Hall of Famer, Jon Kano and young upstart, Erik Magnum. - - - - - - - - - - Hello victory, old sweetheart. Kelly leaned back in his chair, legs propped on the bed's edge, hands interlocked behind his head. He had been lounging around, no shirt and sweatpants, all day in the Delaware hotel room. Given his mood, it was deemed a fitting celebration despite an auspicious showing in Just Wrestling's Lethal Lottery Tour. Hell, he'd even traded verbal barbs with Max Danger, a wrestling legend. Sure he was pleased, but outlying factors weighed on his fatigued mind. He hadn't heard from Paige since departing Greensboro, and even his old crush, victory, was only semi-gratifying. Maybe she'd call, check to see how the match went, or perhaps not. Based on their last conversation, Paige could determine to never speak with him again. The excuse, timing, was the same, but indeed different. Kelly had major laundry, and it required airing out, but not last week in Greensboro. Not when his attention must focus solely on wrestling. God, he just wanted to explain to her why he'd been acting so reclusive. Problem solved, right? 'Jesus Kelly, give it a rest,' he attempted to convince himself. Be active, go do something. Seriously, in Harrington, Delaware? Harrington, Kelly ... Harrington. Kelly sighed, his brain made a good point, 'Fine, I'll go for a run.' He snatched a black T-shirt from his bag, throwing it on as searched for his room's keycard and the hooded sweatshirt he'd seen earlier. The keycard was located, sitting in front of the mirror on the small dresser. Picking it up, his eyes locked on just what shirt he'd grabbed. 'CALIFORNIA CAGE FIGHTING' Heh, funny how that happens. The problems in his relationship with Paige could be linked directly back to CCF, where Kelly had spent his six months away. His stare simply fixated on those three words, his brain processing inordinate amounts of information. What if he never spent that six months in California? - - - - - - - - - - The First Impression Kelly rolled into California on his prize possession, a 1990 Harley-Davidson FLSTF Fat Boy, and not much else - little coin in his pocket and a couple sets of clothes. Back in Greensboro, he'd left behind a wrestling career and a fairly serious relationship on a whim. Television ad pops up one day looking for MMA fighters, reasonably interesting. Very next day, he's packing his bag and getting ready to leave, just like that. Now here he was, preparing to meet the CCF Head Honcho. 'Excuse me son, but are you the one looking for me?' a middle aged man inquired, his moosed back hair as white as snow. He was J.P. Lassiter, the creator and owner of California Cage Fighting. Prior to that, he was a Hollywood big shot, writing and directing many of his own movies. None that Kelly had seen, but Lassiter certainly had that look, the suit he wore probably cost a small fortune itself. Kelly extended his chiseled arm, laying a firm handshake on Mr. Lassiter, 'Sure am, name's Kelly Masters, and I'm here to fight.' 'You think so, do you now? Fight anywhere before son?' Lassiter eyed the young man, good face and good body, this boy could make it. 'Nowhere, I've never fought MMA before.' The CCF Owner scoffed, 'Nowhere? And you expect to compete here in CCF? I think you ought to get a little training first, at least give yourself a fighting chance.' Kelly shook his head, 'No, no, I don't need any MMA training. I've got an amateur wrestling background that puts others to shame, and I've worked extensively in submissions, just not for MMA. You see, I was training to become a professional wrestler.' 'Son, you've got moxy, and you've got some marketable traits, but I don't feel right putting you inside a cage with professional fighters. You'll get mauled.' The young man's face hardened, 'Damnit, put me in the cage, and I'll be the judge of that.' He took a deep breath, trying to calm his temper. One of his greatest pet peeves was being talked to as if he didn't have a chance, when he knew that he'd damn well hold up and then some. J.P. Lassiter was a bit taken back, no one had spoken in such a tone to him for years, nobody. 'Easy son, easy. You want a shot?' Kelly nodded slowly, bringing a sly smirk to Lassiter. 'You've got it. Not only do you get to fight, but you get a shot at the Heavyweight Title. One month, you face Edgar 'The Sandman' Gonzalez, for better or worse.' Lassiter reached into his jacket pocket, pulling out a white card, 'Here, take this. You can train at the CCF Gym in Thousand Oaks, just ask for the Martinez Brothers.' Kelly received the card, spinning toward the door he'd entered. 'Son,' J.P. Lassiter's voice halted the youngster, who looked back over his shoulder, 'for your sake, I hope you're as good as you think.' The Cambridge, Massachusetts native sneered slightly, jerking his head. In one month, he'd be ready, hell, with his present conditioning he'd probably go fifteen minutes. Rotating his head back around, he caught the glimpse of a tall, beautiful blonde on the second story. No time for women. No time for celebration. Winning was his business, and business needed tending to. - - - - - - - - - - The Fight One month of training, non-stop, that's all Kelly did. He was a workhorse, always had been, constantly in the gym or watching tape. The Martinez Brothers, Manny and Mike, were a driving force behind this attitude, or they surely didn't hinder the effort. Both had their areas of knowledge, Manny was the well versed striker with a penchant for saying 'fuck' while Mike opted for submission, ground attacks, and never stopped talking about his 'Martinez Special'. That month flew by, no more training, no more sparring, simply two men attempting to impose their will on one another. Edgar Gonzalez was known to have 'heavy hands', good to great ground skills, and an 'iron jaw'. Kelly remembered very little from the fight, he recalled the furious pace and its short duration. Of course, that surprised no one, the outcome on the otherhand was unconscionable. Manny would recall, 'Kelly nailed him with that big right hook, fuckin' southpaws,' he shook his head, letting out a chuckle, 'stunned Edgar, I mean really rocked him. Then, he fuckin' quits punching. I'm pissed, screamin' my ass off. Mike's over there, yellin' for the Martinez Special. That little bastard talked about that move for a whole month, ya think that was in Kelly's mind?' He rolls his eyes as he continues, 'Sure enough, Kel sweeps his leg, puts that fuckin' heel hook on in midair. Game over, fuckin' Edgar is tappin' in a heartbeat. New champ, how 'bout that shit huh?' - - - - - - - - - - The Fox 'How's this? Kelly 'Machine' Masters. You like it?' J.P. Lassiter rushed the new champion, the fight had been over for less than five minutes, and already marketing ideas were being tossed around. Heck, a fighter with Kelly's ability and looks could be a major cash cow. CCF could compete with UFC, as Lassiter had initially envisioned. Kelly grinned, his shoulder gleaming golden rays, 'Yeah, sounds about right.' Manny and Mike each had a hand on his shoulder, their faces beaming with pride. 'How about a celebratory dinner, son? My treat,' offered Lassiter, his arms stretched out nearly as wide as his smile. 'Sure, why not. Of course, as long as Manny and Mike can come along,' he smirked, shooting a glance back at his training partners. 'The more the merrier Kelly,' he exclaimed, dollar signs spinning in his eyes. 'Brooke, my daughter, will also be joining us. Brooke!' Brooke Lassiter, wow-za, the perfect word to describe her. Long, blonde hair past her shoulders, nearly six-foot of slender frame, fantastic breasts, and legs clear up to her ass. She was the kind of woman in magazines. You know, the gorgeous women, who are then altered photographically. Right, she was a post production model, a ten in anybody's book. To say that Kelly was smitten, would likely have been the understatement of the century. All thoughts of Paige had instantaneously vanished, leaving only this beautiful blonde radiating before him. 'Brooke, meet Kelly. Kelly, Brooke.' 'Hello Kelly,' her eyes wandered away from his, to his sweaty, but glistening physique. Sparks flew between the two, an immediate mutual attraction on this evening. Her exquisite green eyes finally settling back on his, an eyebrow of curiosity perking. 'Hey,' mustered Kelly, not much, but it'd work. What ensued was predictable. An inseparable, passionate love affair between Kelly Masters and Brooke Lassiter. Life was fantastic, high end restaurants and gold championships. Only money, success, and purely sexual pleasure couldn't restrain one small bump in the road ... Paige Allen. Months in, she had slowly but surely crept to the forefront of his mind. He realized these material things didn't matter to him, and that being in Greensboro with her truly made him a happy man. Leave it to Kelly to undo what he'd done, by doing it again. Same story, different city. Without hesitation, he handed over the shiny, gold championship, turned down a promising, wealthy career, and dumped the most gorgeous woman he'd ever laid eyes upon. All to head back where he could have been all along. Back to Greensboro, back to Paige Allen, the girl he loved so very much. - - - - - - - - - - Kelly's California Trip was halted, his Blackberry playing the catchy tune of The Beatles' 'Hello, Goodbye'. He gazed at the mirror for a brief moment still, finally turning his attention to his phone. Frowning at first sight of the number, Kelly answered, his curiosity peaked past its limit. 'Hello?' he questioned, more so than a greeting. 'Hey baby, it's me,' a female responded, her voice was soothing. Kelly replied, slightly apprehensive, 'Oh, hey, what's up?' 'Not a whole lot, was just thinking of you,' she responded. 'To be honest, I wasn't expecting to hear from ya.' The female's voice perked up, a hint of excitement, 'Well Kel, I've got news.' Kelly ran his hand over his face, muffling his mouth as he spoke, 'News, huh? Good or bad?' 'It's wonderful news.' 'Really? Well -' 'I'm pregnant, Kelly, with our child.' His legs became unsteady, mere wet noodles holding his large frame up. Sights set on the bed, backpedaling his knees met the mattress first.. Not even attempting to sit, he simply crashed down onto his back. Flat on his back, something he rarely, hell never, had experienced in his career, MMA or wrestling. His personal life though, damned if it hadn't dropped him twice this week. 'So ... Kel, we have to talk.' Talking, yes, there's always talking to do. He simply stared at the ceiling, in a trance almost, 'Yeah, I know.' She pushed further, 'Where are you?' 'Delaware still ... headin' to Fairfield, Connecticut.' The white ceiling closed in around him. 'I'll call when I get there, bye Kelly.' Click. Just in time, the white light was too strong now, it had overtaken him. - - - - - - - - - -
