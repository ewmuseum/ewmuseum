Date: Today 'It's like a bad episode of batman, what will El Diablo do next?!' 'I don't see the resemblance, but whatever helps your joke come to circle, Johnny.' Washington D.C. is known for both its cultural and scholastic traditions. Some of the country's finest institutions and divine attractions are presented to both tourists and locals throughout the area as well as historical monuments that fill in the lines between buildings and structures that are theistic in their own right. From the Lincoln Memorial to the Smithsonian, or 'The Wall' to the White House, the land is littered with prestige, honor, and tradition. However what it is not known for is the Tom Tom Bar and Grill on 18th Street down in the Adams Morgan district. This is where FUSE wrestler Jason Cruise and his beloved behind the scenes co-star Johnny find themselves right now on a Thursday afternoon, surrounded by the visible debauchery of customers filling their mouths with half off bud lights and cheap Kamikaze shots instead of going to work or college. 'Well batman without the cape, the cool sound effects, and the sweet ride,' says Johnny. Cruise questions, 'so he's nothing like batman?' Johnny shrugs, 'Well, no, I guess not. But if he really wanted he could have someone hold up action signs when he punches you in the face. Maybe he could ask his dead brother the next time he talks to him in his dreams.' Holding back a smile, 'that's just wrong,' says Jason. 'It's not my fault the guy is going to eventually be wrestling's version of Van Gogh. I give it a week after you take his title before he cuts his left ear off.' 'Somehow I don't think it'd hurt his career.' 'Yeah, fans eat that kind of shit up.' * * * * * A few days ago Jason Cruise was faced with a question he wasn't comfortable responding too, little lone giving a direct answer. Marie, the new love interest of his life, had asked him if she could accompany him to a FUSE wrestling event. Not as a trophy on his sleeve or someone in his corner, just as a fan, a friend.. a girlfriend. She wasn't asking for anything out of the ordinary, just to watch the man she was falling in love with at work. His response was a simple one... No. However, the car ride out to Washington suggested otherwise. Sitting in the front seat of a Mini Cooper is Jason Cruise, his back hunched up a bit in the driver's seat while Marie sits beside him, her small frame a bit more relaxed in the passenger seat. Behind them, folded up like an accordion and out like a rock is Johnny, his ass crack hanging out of his designer jeans and bare upper body curled up into a tight ball. 'I can't believe you picked this car,' a tired Jason said. 'The other ones smelled like cigarettes and cheap booze. They really need to hire someone to clean them once in awhile.' Jason cranks his neck from side to side, trying his best to stretch out in the small vehicle. 'I can't believe we made it through three days together in the car already,' he says. 'Are you serious? I can't believe we even made it through the first night. I thought I was going to strangle him.' Marie motions back to Johnny. 'Yeah, he can be a bit of a handful.' There's a brief pause here, a moment where they lock eyes like two parents talking about their disobedient child. Jason breaks the stare by putting his head down and taking a deep breathe. Marie reaches over the center divider, placing her left hand on his knee. 'You're still worried about this, aren't you?' 'I thought we were done talking about it. I said it'll be alright.' 'Alright? I don't want it to be alright, I want you to be as excited as I am.' Jason sighs. 'Look, I want to be excited, I mean -- I AM excited, I'm just nervous. For you, for us.' 'If I can survive a night of Johnny singing the Oompa Loompa song and dancing to the Macarena in the back seat I think I handle just about anything.' 'This isn't an annoying song or dance we're talking about here. This guy, he's -- Cutting Jason off, 'A big mean dirtbag? I get it. I can handle some third grade name calling and bad jokes, Jason. I'm not a little kid.' 'Do you really think I'm scared of him calling you names? I know you're tough, Marie, that's never been in question. I'm worried about him hurting you.' She laughs. 'Hurting me, why would he do that?' 'He doesn't just hate me, Marie. He wants me out of this business, he wants to beat me so bad that I never wrestle again and if hurting you meant getting to me well...' 'Well what?!' Her voice level raises. 'Well I wouldn't put hurting you past him.' 'Are you on crack? It's all staged. Wrestling is fake.' Jason's grip on the wheel becomes uneasy, you can sense a slight tremble in his fingers as he fastens his lock on the steering wheel. As often as he can, he looks over to Marie who sits their quietly for a moment. 'To a degree, Marie. Yes.' 'What does that mean?' 'That means that point A leads to point B and point B eventually leads us to point C but it's the stuff in-between. It's where we are allowed to be creative, to well, improvise.' 'So improvising means hurting each other?' 'No, it means sometimes there are certain liberties taken. Sometimes what you see on Television while fixed is well... real. Every once in awhile a punch really is a punch, or when I get dropped on my head it gets done a bit harder than usual. It's why there's such a strong bond between guys like Johnny back there and me. We trust one another, we have too. Our lives depend on one another.' 'So what's the problem?' 'I don't trust him.' Confused, 'I don't get it. What does that have to do with my safety?' Jason ignores the selfish response for the moment, realizing it's all a bit much to take in at once. 'I don't expect you to understand. Maybe one day you will, but not yet, not now. What goes on in that ring this weekend is going to be two people putting on a show for the fans. That's what you'll see, that's what everyone will see.' 'But?' 'But it's never that simple.' * * * * * A waitress in her late teens swings by the table, dropping of a pair of red baskets filled with food in front of Jason and Johnny. Jason stares down at his grilled chicken breast, steamed broccoli, and cup of brown rice and then just pokes at it with his fork. He looks up at Johnny who has already began shoving a cheeseburger dripping with grease into his mouth, washing it down almost immediately with french fries and pink lemonade. 'That's just sick,' says Jason. With his mouth full, 'Whatever -- how's the El Pollo Puta?' Jason shakes his head in reference to his food being called 'The Bitch Chicken'. 'All you gotta do now is throw it up afterwards and snort a line of coke and you've mastered the Paris Hilton diet.' 'I can feel you getting fatter just sitting here.' 'Don't hate on my greasy goodiness, this is amazing.' Jason takes a bite of his food and then just lays his fork down on the side of the plate as if he were done. Johnny takes a moment from divulging himself with his meal. 'Hey big guy, don't worry, you'll get to cram your face with this kind of shit in a few days.' 'It's not that,' Jason says softly. 'Oh man, don't tell me.' 'Yeah, I know, her.' 'We've been through this, just tell the slut to stay in the hotel.' Jason peers up at Johnny. 'Yeah, yeah.. Marie. Tell Marie to stay in the hotel. We'll come get her for the after party at the reflecting pool. Open bar baby, we're gonna bungee off the Washington Monument into that swimming pool, baby!' 'Dude.' 'What?' 'You do realize that's not an actual pool. It's only like a foot deep.' 'Wait. What?' Johnny looks on, a bit perplexed. 'Didn't you ever see Forrest Gump? He runs across it to meet up with that chick it took him like fifteen years to sleep with.' 'I can see why you would remember that, you two share a lot in common.' 'Shut up.' 'But seriously... I thought there was a deep end.' Johnny says. 'You could always bungee in and find out.' 'Yeah, and you could always let Marie come down to ringside and see what happens.' 'Dick.' 'I'm telling you man, leave her behind. Wait till things cool over.' 'She's come all this way, she'd kill me.' 'Who'd kill you?' says a soft, feminie voice. Marie stands over the two of them at the side of the table, her hands full of shopping bags and her shirt filled with buttons and red, white, and blue graffiti. 'You two kids miss me?' Johnny interrupts, 'We were just talking about you.' 'Oh?' * * * * * Jason Cruise stands in front of a simplistic backdrop. Black wall, fancy FUSE logo, a WIW banner streaming over his head. It's nothing fancy or fitting for such an occasion but it's to the point and gets the job done, just the way Jason like its. Dressed down in a pair of red warm up pants and a stretched out white 'Prophecy' shirt he stares directly into the camera. 'I imagine you think this entire week is devoted to you, Raul. That would be pretty flattering of me. Fortunately for you, it's not. Otherwise your PPV would get under way with a false start and your eyes fixated on the arena lights over your head, my silouhuette and new title blanketing the lights from turning you blind. But for your sake, there's a much bigger issue to address.' 'The WIW Tower.' Jason shows us his best spooky fingers. 'A melodramatic mind and body fuck that FUSE has annointed on us in hope to save face since Clinton Sage and his backyard bullies doesn't seem to be enough anymore. I guess the villains splashing water in the fat kids eyes gets old after awhile, who knew. Yet here I am, mocking the deathtrap that will soon surround myself and half the roster, but in the back of my mind I know that the matter at hand is no joking matter. Because the Tower is in fact a beast that could make or break a career or even worse, the man himself.' 'It seems this is the same spot I've been in every week since I've arrived here. Battling and defying the odds, wondering what to do, wondering how to keep my head high enough to stay afloat around here in a place filled with so much talent and so many stars. I've been thinking about it now more than ever thanks to everything that has gone down recently and the only conclusion I can come up with is that I just gotta keep doing what I've been doing...' 'Snappin' necks, breaking arms, and taking names.' Jason cracks a smile. 'I haven't accomplished a lot here yet but what I have done I have done it all on my own. I carried me. Whatever I did, I did it my way, I just gotta keep it going. I just have to ride this wave until it ends on its own. Guys like El Diablo are more than just a stepping stone. He is a chance to prove that the Livewire Title is worth a lick and when I win it, I'll wear it proudly into the tower and I'll make people respect a title that is often forgotten. And when that bell rings and hell breaks loose in that monstrous cage, it'll be all business. I've come too far just to cough up the ball now... I didn't make it through these past few weeks just to lay down and die.' 'People can keep talking about Lane Stevens, Jonathan Rhine, and Timo Bolamba... about guys like them have this Tower all but locked up. But I didn't get here to just give up because my name has suddenly dissappeared from the radar.' 'I'm still here, FUSE.' 'I'm three sweet seconds from being the LiveWire champion and forever etched into the history books of this place I now call a second home.' 'And until El Diablo proves me otherwise, I'm not going to stop claiming that fact to be true. Because unlike most of the people around here, I'm going to be fighting to represent myself and this entire organization because with victory comes a chance to bring prestige and honor to a title that seems to have lost it's well-mannered luster. Week after week I've watched the title be tarnished and disgraced without a care or concern in the world for this company, for the fans, for the people that love and respect what FUSE really means. So if putting my body on the line means I have just a small chance to carry FUSE on my name, on my morales, and on my back. Then so be it. Because it isn't even about the Universal Title to me, I've got enough gold to worry about for one night. It's about restoring order in a place that has lost all of it. It's about turning a new page and writing a chapter that the fans of FUSE can be proud of. It's about a chance, one shot, one defining moment that will change the face of this organization forever.' He punches one first into the palm of the other. 'The tower will the mark the beginning of the end for Clinton Sage. Whether it be at my hands or someone elses I guess we'll just have to wait and find out. But I promise you this...' He leans in. 'If it's me, and god willing it will be, death will never have looked so good to Clinton Sage.' A faint smile. 'And that's saying something.' * * * * * 'So what's the deal, Jason?' Marie's usually soft voice has a bit harder of a tone too it. 'I - I can't do this right now.' Jason rises up from his stool, laying his napkin over his food. 'I didn't come all this way to sit in the hotel room and wait for you.' 'You could order little decorative chocolates and sink yourself into a nice bubble bath,' says Johnny. 'Shut up, Johnny.' 'Guys, please. I need to think.' Jason begins to walk off. 'What's there to think about?!' she yells back at him. 'You can just leave my ticket at the box office window.' Marie looks over at Johnny. 'What?' he says with a smile as the camera fades out. Black.
