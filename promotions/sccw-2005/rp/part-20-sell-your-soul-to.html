Part 20: Sell Your Soul To

Date: Today (Editor's Note: The RP that is supposed to be here isn't finished, but contains important storyline things. Here's a synopsis, with the hopes that I'll post the actual RP someday.) The present. Wyatt Connors--or, as it turns out, Franklin Watkins--is still tied to the bed in Reverend Seck's 'conversion room.' Also present are Brothers Seth and Macarthur, Reverend Ibrahim Seck, and Harley Roebuck (identified as James 'Buck' Watkins.) Buck tells Frank that the woman in the picture--Cheryl--died three months ago, after spending nearly twenty years in a coma. Buck then asks Frank to tell the story of what happened all those years ago--the sequence of events that would eventually turn him into Wyatt Connors. # # # Flashback 1: The Picture. The photo was taken the day Buck returned home from the US Marine Corps. Buck, Frank, and Cheryl then went to dinner, where they discussed their lives and the future. At the time, Frank is still in college, and is also in the early stages of his professional wrestling career. Frank revealed his plans to propose to Cheryl someday, stating, 'I always come back to her...and she's always waiting.' # # # The present (but later). Sometime after the interrogation, Brother Seth feverishly packs a few things and plots an escape. But he knows that he can't leave without Abigail Proctor, who is the only other member of the community who is not under the influence of Reverend Seck's brainwashing Kool-Aid. He goes to her, telling her that they have to make their escape that night. But before they could leave, he had to retrieve an important item--a metal case belonging to Wyatt Connors, which Seth had procured after Connors' car accident that stranded him in this location over a year ago. # # # Flashback 2: The Blood. A few years into his wrestling career now, Frank Watkins (under the name of Kid Fantastic) wins his first regional heavyweight title, defeating the champion under somewhat questionable circumstances. The now-former champion issues some veiled threats, but Frank shrugs them off and heads home...not knowing that his opponent is following him. Frank arrives at home, greeted by his two-months-pregnant wife, Cheryl. Frank goes out on a brief errand, and when he returns, he finds Cheryl bloody and unconscious...and the man standing over her is the same one he just beat for his title. In a rage, Frank grabs a lamp and bludgeons the other man to death. Afterwards he panics, and flees the scene before the police arrive. # # # The present (shortly after the interrogation.) Just outside the room, Buck and Reverend Seck are discussing matters pertaining to the upcoming match at Homecoming against BloodAngel. Buck thinks that BloodAngel is ready to take the fall, being driven into a frenzy over his son, as well as the attempt to set him on fire at Sinner's Choice. Seck is pleased, and states his desire to be on hand for BloodAngel's conversion. Buck isn't sure that it's such a good idea, but Seck is adamant. # # # Flashback 3: The Fear. Several years later. Frank is still on the run from the law. He takes odd jobs and still does a lot of wrestling, sending the money back home to pay for his wife's medical expenses. Unfortunately, the police keep tracing the source of the checks, and they get closer to catching Frank. Eventually, he calls Buck and begs for help. Frank had heard about a man named Dr. Lionel Ernst, who was an expert on personality-alteration techniques, especially hypnosis. Frank has a plan to give himself a completely new identity, to go with a new face (from cosmetic surgery.) Ernst explained that to build a new personality in someone, it must at least have some grounds in the old personality, so Frank and Buck crafted the persona of Wyatt Connors around certain aspects of Frank's nature--chief among them, the talent for lying. The goal of Wyatt Connors was to acquire as much money as possible, which he would then give to Buck for the purposes of paying Cheryl's medical expenses. Only Buck would know the key phrase that would trigger the change...so that, if Cheryl ever got better, and the cops stopped looking for him, Frank would be able to come home. The procedure itself took some time, but it ended up being more successful than any of them had imagined. Three days after they had finished, Connors hired some local thugs to break into Ernst's office and destroy seemingly random files...one of which was his own. Eventually, Frank played the part of Wyatt Connors for so long that he started to forget that Frank Watkins ever existed. For the last ten years or so, he couldn't remember anything from his previous life, and came to accept Connors as the only person he had ever been. # # # The present (time unknown.) A text message is sent from the Decency Compound. It reads: 'He's about ready to crack. Should have what's yours by this time tomorrow.' # # # The present (after the flashbacks.) Buck asks Frank just how much money he had acquired--several million dollars, as it turned out. Buck then pressures Frank into telling him where the money was. After some resistance, Frank caves in and says that it was in a metal case that had gone missing over a year ago...causing Seth to become very nervous, and he asks to be excused. Afterwards, Buck whispers something into Frank's ear--the phrase that triggers the re-emergence of Wyatt Connors...who is none too happy about the situation. Seck then makes mention of the fact that he needed his loyal Crusader once more...meaning that Connors would have to go back under the influence of the Kool-Aid. Wyatt Connors refuses, even after Buck explains that there was no reason for Wyatt to hang on to his old life--his money would be gone, and his secrets would be exposed. Wyatt continues to protest, so they would have to force the drug-laced drink upon him. Buck requests that he prepare and administer the Kool-Aid himself, since he was responsible for so much of this. Seck agrees to this, and once more the will of Connors is subjugated. # # # The present (sometime after midnight.) Seth and Abby are out in the middle of a field. Abby is holding two bags, while Seth is digging in the ground with a shovel. Eventually, he finds what he is looking for--the same metal case that he stole from Wyatt Connors' wrecked car, back in January of last year. They immediately take off for the nearest road...but as soon as they reach it, they are confronted by Harley Roebuck...who is armed. Harley says he'd prefer not to kill them, but it wouldn't bother him much either way. Seth tries to plea for Abby's freedom; Harley refused. He uses the butt of the gun to knock out Abby, then Seth, and takes them both hostage.
