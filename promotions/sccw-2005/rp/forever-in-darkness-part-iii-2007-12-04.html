Forever In Darkness: Part III

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.4.07 ~ x ~ The sound of light footsteps creek against the old wooden flooring in a house that is just as silent as death. With each step the pale looking visitor looks with careful eyes to try and find even the hint to where the owner of the house might be. Something was not right and Brennan knew it. From the moment Corvus resurfaced in the public it raised a lot of suspicion within him. There was a slim possibility his father could have reverted back into the vampire he once was, but Brennan knew for certain that the essence of the monster had been removed with the help of dark magic and harvested into a new vessel. The creature took on the same physical characteristics as its former host but it did not share the same passion and reverence for life that the man Brennan knew to be his father had. Corvus cares nothing about the world around him and if he has his way would rape and pillage it until there is nothing left. That is what separates him from other vampires. They prefer a healthy balance. Without mortals in the world they would cease to exist in time. Brennan realizes his efforts are for nothing and that he should move on elsewhere for clues. He starts to make his way to the back door of the house when he hears the faintest sound of movement behind him and sharply turns around. "I wouldn’t suggest sneaking up on someone...it could be bad for your health." Brennan speaks as he scans the room for any sign of life. Even with a far superior set of eyes compared to a normal man, he cannot see a thing. To his surprise he is given a response; the voice has a thick Irish accent accompanying it. "Fear not, I am not here to cause any harm to you, I am only here to offer you assistance in your quest." Brennan is not comfortable communicating with someone he cannot see. How can he prove that this person will not try to hurt him despite their claims? "Show yourself" the vampire calls out, "it is the only way I can take you for your word." Stepping out from the shadows is a middle aged man of average height. He is wearing a religious white robe with a golden design woven intricately into it. His long strawberry blonde hair shimmers in what little light there is in the room. Brennan looks at him closely to see that he has emerald green eyes which would explain his Gaelic dialect. "I am familiar with many people associated with my father but I’ve never seen you before." Brennan crosses his arms hoping that this man has some kind of answers as to where his father is. The suspicion that this man has something to do with it crosses his mind. "Who are you exactly?" He doesn’t expect the robed gentleman to tell him but there’s a small piece of hope in the back of his mind. If he would have wanted to do something to him he would have probably done it already. Based on the way he is dressed Brennan would likely say that the man is a priest or something very similar depending on his religion. "Who I am is not necessary for you to know at this time" the man in the robe replies, "but I do know who you are Brennan and I know what it is that you seek." He takes one or two steps towards Brennan. "You and your father have had your differences in the past which is the reason why it surprises me that you are interested in his whereabouts." This man certainly knows more then he is sharing and it is making Brennan slightly uneasy. How much does this man know about the relationship between him and Eryc? Does he know about how he unknowingly killed his own mother? Sure he didn’t know that she was his mother but it doesn’t excuse him from what he has done. If he knows about those things then he may know more about what happened to his Uncle as well as maybe a clue or two to where his father is. It is a high risk to get involved with the man of spirituality but it is one that he is willing to take if it will help him get his father back. "Contrary to what you may or may not know sir, I have taken a different stance when it comes to my father, the man who now goes by the name Eryc Morgan." Brennan explains to this mysterious character. "For a long time I was lied to about the past as well as he has. There is documentation of this. However, I have reason to believe it may be in the hands of someone who poses a threat to my family." The robed man knows about the file folder that Brennan gave to Eryc prior to his disappearance; he also knows where its location is. He retrieved it from Morgan’s abandoned car a short time after he had been thrown into the van by Corvus’ associates. Corvus knew nothing about the folder so he didn’t make it a priority for his men to take it. As Brennan watches the mysterious gentleman he does not know that the man is reading his aura to see if he is telling the truth about trying to mend the rift in his family. The Irishman can see that he is telling the truth and it allows him to speak a little more freely. "I know of the information you speak of and I can assure you that it is in a safe place until Eryc is able to make use of it again." The man pauses for a moment deciding what he should do about the situation. It is his job not to interfere in any Brennan’s affairs or Eryc’s for that matter, but he can give him bits and pieces of where to go to get help. There is one person who is highly involved in the life of Eryc Morgan that will care about what happens to him. "I cannot tell you where to find your father even if I do know where he is. I’m not allowed to get involved...only guide you along the way. I know of someone that you may want to get in touch with." Aggravation sets in as Brennan stares the Irishman down. What does he mean he can’t tell him where his father is? If he knows then it would make it so much easier to get him back. He resigns himself not to grab hold of his man and tear him to shreds to get the information he needs. There are better ways then that and like his father before him...there is some good left in him. "Who is it that you want me to reach;" Brennan asks, "will he be hard to find?" The Irishman reaches into his robe and returns with a small card in his hand. He holds it out for Brennan to take which he does quickly and looks at the information written on it. It is the phone number for a man named Joshua McClain. The name sounds familiar but Brennan cannot place where he has heard it before. "Contact Joshua," the Irishman says with advisement "and when you do you might find that some of the pieces of the puzzle will connect to give you a bigger picture." Riddles are not a favorite with Brennan but he’ll do as the man suggests if it will help him find his father and bring him back from whatever hell he may be enduring. As easy as it should be to find Corvus himself, the man is older and more powerful then he is which could be why this man is having him seek out this Joshua to help him. "I thank you...what for I do not know but if you are trustworthy then I shall give you even more gratitude when all of this is over." Brennan turns to the door to leave but the robed Irishman addresses him one more time. "I need no thanks but I shall utter a prayer for you and your family, even if you are not one who follows the path of righteousness." The man clasps his hands together. "Be safe my son on your journey." Brennan’s pale form just stares at the man for a minute. He blinks and the Irishman is no longer there. Where did he go? Brennan asks himself over and over if he really did see the man in the white robe. It seems too good to be true until he turns his attention back to his hand and the card with the phone number written on it. "I must find this Joshua McClain and hope that he can help me in some way." Once again Brennan slips out of the house the same way he entered without tripping the house’s security system. He left his car several blocks away to ensure that the neighbors would not see him in the driveway and call the authorities. As his shoes clap against the pavement, Brennan slips the card into his inner coat pocket so that it is safe on his person until he can return to his hotel room to make the call to Joshua and start to get some answers ~ x x ~ Eryc’s eyes slowly flutter open as the pungent smell of basement along with the sound of a large metal door opening. His thoughts are jumbled from the lack of sustenance in his body. It’s been at least a week or two since he has had a meal. Very slowly his body is failing from not eating but even if he dies from starvation he will not die a complete death; there is only one that he can be killed but only he knows the way. It is the only hope he has of getting out of this prison and doing whatever it takes to end the miserable stain on his existence that is Corvus. Eryc has very little strength to keep himself up and his body is sagging towards the floor with his arms being held up by the chains that keep him in place. During his time in captivity his hair has become a few inches longer and his face is showing a lot of growth as well. He looks a lot like one of those mountain men that live in Wyoming and put larges fences around their houses and shoot trespassers. His eyes meet with the source that disturbed his slumber. Corvus approaches with a cocky swagger and demeanor as if he’s the alpha male kingshit of the world. He brings a chair over to Eryc and sits down, leaning his back against the chair and folds his arms. "I’m saddened to see that my effort to break you down with the water torture didn’t take," the vampire scoffs, "but that doesn’t mean that I’m going to stop trying." "Go ahead and try as much as you like...but you won’t be the one to send me to my maker." As haggard as Eryc is he still manages to point out to his adversary that he is still going and nothing he does will change that fact. "If one of us is going to come out of this with their life I’m going to put everything I own on me because nobody bets against Eryc Morgan." Corvus suppresses a laugh by covering his mouth with his hand. Morgan’s words are the most ridiculous thing he’s heard in some time. "Perhaps maybe I should conduct a few experiments to see what makes you tick."The idea of torture excites Corvus. "One way or another I will BREAK you." The strength to keep his head up has diminished but Eryc still manages to speak. "History has proven before that I am stronger then you and no matter what you think it will take to down me you better think again." Eryc coughs from the damp conditions of the basement. "I will not fade away simply because you want me to." "I don’t know how you’ve come out of some of the situations that I have placed in front of you but it just gives me more opportunities." Eryc is forced to gaze into the eyes of his captor; his evil twin of sorts. "You will have to tell me sometime what it is exactly that you are fighting for; from my standpoint...you don’t have a damn thing." Morgan’s eyes begin to glaze with the pains of hunger coursing through him. He shakes his head in every direction to fend off going unconscious as he battles to speak. "I have the hope that one day you’ll be sloppy enough that I get free and when I do," Morgan laughs somewhat hysterically, "I won’t bother to play with you. I will straight out kill you." To Corvus the words are but idle threats falling on deaf ears. It is highly unlikely that he will make any mistakes that will allow his sworn enemy to escape. He has this game between them in the bag and he knows it but he just has to get Eryc to realize it as well. Corvus walks over to an interrogation table and takes a walkie-talkie from it; Eryc is unable to hear what he says as he fades in and out. After setting it down the vampire approaches him again cupping Morgan’s chin within his hand. "The vengeance card is all that you have left?" Corvus finds amusement in the fact that revenge is the only thing that Eryc is fighting for. "Wait...I forgot, it is all you have left. I mean your pretty little wife expired right in front of you and you couldn’t do shit about it. Add the fact that I now have thrown your personal life and career down the toilet it really leaves you with the shit end of the stick." The steel door creaks open as Corvus turns to see his new accomplice, Gregory, whom he refers to as just "G". The man is every bit as big as Corvus save for fact that he is two inches shorter and is about twenty or thirty pounds heavier. G has dyed orange hair in a spike and is wearing hunting coveralls. Accompanying him is a blindfolded Lindsay, who is not trying to struggle with him to get free. She has started to resign herself to the fact that she is a prisoner until Corvus sees fit to let her go or take her life…whichever one happens first. Since Lindsay’s arrival Eryc’s head has dropped down so he didn’t see her come in which gives Corvus all the more reason to taunt his enemy and goad him all the more with his new breakfast choice. "G, keep her steady for a moment. I want my other guest to meet my newest conquest." Gregory simply nods at his boss’ order and holds Lindsay in place just in case she decides to get frisky and try to get away. She stays put even though she cannot hear a darn thing only that Corvus is in the room with someone else besides his goon. The vampire encroaches Eryc and grabs his long black mane jerking his head back up to force him to focus on the beautiful woman who is standing a few feet away from him in the clutches of G with a blindfold and wearing nothing but a silk kimono. The idea of what Corvus may do to this poor creature nauseates Morgan as he tries to hold it in the best he possibly can. His body begins to shake more from anger then the lack of having anything nutritional in his body. "You can’t do what I think you’re going to do…she doesn’t deserve that type of fate Corvus." Morgan closes his eyes for a minute trying to stop the shaking. "We both know it." Corvus gets right into Eryc’s face and instantly he can tell that there is no sort of care in the monster’s gray irises; they are as cold as the stone they were chiseled from. The antagonist releases his grip on Morgan’s hair and watches his head flounder. "I can do whatever I damn well please and the beauty of it is you get to watch the whole show for nothing…free admission!" The razor sharp sarcasm cuts right through Eryc but there is little he can do. He is depleted of his strength and even if he would be at full capacity he would have a hard time breaking free from the chains in which he is bound. Corvus walks back over to the interrogation table and opens up a small pouch that is resting on top of the smooth oak surface. Inside are several small vials and syringes to match. Right next to those items is a rubber tourniquet that he picks up first. He looks over to G and tells him to leave the room for a few moments and that he will let him know when he needs to return. G responds by nodding and hands Lindsay over to Corvus and exits the room and closes the door behind him. "What is going on?" Lindsay inquires as she did not know the reason why Corvus would tell his assistant to leave. Corvus remains silent as he takes hold of her arm and ties the tourniquet around her upper arm. Lindsay starts to freak out when she feels the tight rubber being tied to her skin. She starts to squirm fiercely but Corvus is too strong for her. He whispers into her ear, "The less you fight me the better off you are…believe me the both of us are going to enjoy this." The words are still not enough to prevent her from trying to push him away. Corvus does his best to prep the syringe while he’s hanging onto her. He could use G’s help but he doesn’t have enough of a hold on him yet to have him be directly involved in his dastardly deeds which is the reason he ushered him out of the room. The evil bastard turns his attention back over to Eryc who still has his head down and appears to be unconscious. Corvus is pissed at this because he wants him to see everything that happens in the next few minutes. "Hey, wake up over there jackass!" Corvus reaches into his pocket with his free hand and pulls out a coin, whipping it over at Eryc and listens to it smack off the exposed flesh of his chest. "The show is about to start." Eryc slowly raises his head to see Corvus filling up the needle. His voice is nonexistent even though in his head he’s screaming for the evil bastard to stop what he’s doing. Morgan knows that enough lives have been ruined by both him and Corvus and another isn’t necessary. Corvus can see in Eryc’s eyes that he knows exactly what is going on and what he is set to do. The syringe is now half filled and he taps it gently and squirts the excess from the needle. "You know exactly what I’m doing, don’t you Eryc?" Corvus’ toothy grin full of arrogance, "This is the same thing the two of us did back in the day. I thought you might appreciate it…like a blast from the past." Eryc forces the chains to move slightly but is stopped short of his goal of breaking them from the wall. The nauseating sound of his shoulder popping out of the socket invades Lindsay’s ears making her even more confused then she was previously. "Who are you talking to?" Corvus shrugs off her inquiry; he takes her arm and lightly taps the flesh at the bend of her elbow. "You’ll find out soon enough…but first it’s party time." He injects the needle containing the diacetylmorphine into her bloodstream the effects of the drug do not take very long as Lindsay is calmed easily and the feelings are quite intoxicating. Corvus removes her blindfold allowing her to look straight at his other guest and she is too mellow to even care that the man who is holding her looks exactly like the man chained up against the wall. "This is a little weird…" Lindsay speaks in what is barely a whisper. Corvus brushes her fiery red hair away from her neck and revels in the look of protest coming from Eryc. It’s all a part of the game in which he is seeing how much it will take to break Morgan mentally and physically. Without a moment more of hesitation Corvus bares his sharpened canines and sinks them into the supple flesh of his new plaything and begins to imbibe on her life essence and in connection with the drug he gave her is giving him the ultimate state of euphoria. The ecstasy was not his alone as the feelings she was experiencing were beyond anything she’s ever had before. It was better then alcohol, better then cheesecake and surprisingly better then sex itself. It is so much for her that she slips into a blissful sleep as Corvus finished his feeding. He licks his lips and then her neck to seal the two wounds closed so that G or anyone else wouldn’t suspect a thing. Corvus turns his attention back to Eryc who was once again unconscious and he chuckles very lowly as he grabs the walkie-talkie and calls for G to come get Lindsay and take her back to her holding room. Everything is cleaned up by the time G walks back in and notices the limp body of the young woman in Corvus’ arms. He furrows a bro and wonders what happened during the time he was outside. "Is she alright?" He asks his new mentor as he steps closer to take her from him. Corvus allows G to scoop Lindsay up into his arms; he’s already prepared a response to G’s question. "Everything is fine; she fainted at the sight of our other guest that’s all. Please take her back to her room and then you can go home for the day." G doesn’t say another word and follows orders once again. He figures if he doesn’t ask any questions he’ll be better off. Corvus has promised him a shot at the big time in the world of professional wrestling and he isn’t going to screw it up. Once G is gone with Lindsay Corvus simply stares at Eryc as he is seated upon the top of the interrogation desk. He studies him closely and is disappointed the he hasn’t found a way to be rid of him permanently. "I’ve tried starving you to death and I have tried to drive you insane but neither of them has been successful." He grumbles to himself as he rehashes the accounts of his torture sessions. "It seems I will have to take another approach but it will have to wait." Corvus slides off of the table and then walks to the door, shutting off the lights and looks back with a slight smirk on his face. "I have some old friends to visit."
