Two Wednesdays: Parlor Tricks and Pain

undated

Date: 2/20//2/27 2/20 This is a story about two Wednesdays. The first Wednesday is about parlor tricks. Jonathan Rhine and Katie sit across from each other, eating a small meal and smiling. Well, Katie is smiling. Jonathan is going for the more "stoic detachment" look. "So I was talking to my advisor, and she said that Carnegie Mellon might be a little out of my reach, but then she recognized my face and told me that the dean of graduate admissions there is a big fan. So I was thinking…" She looks up, hoping he will get the hint, but she sees that he is looking at the table. "Jon?" "What?" Katie sighs and puts down her fork and knife, setting her elbows on the table. "I’ve been talking about this for the past twenty minutes, and I can’t even get more than a grunt out of you. What’s wrong?" Then, before he can answer: "This is happening a lot more recently." Jonathan shrugs. He knows, but he doesn’t intend to change it. Because telling her what’s wrong would lead to more problems, and he’s just not ready for that right now. Instead, he stands up. "I’ve got to take a drive. I’ll be back later and we’ll talk about helping you out at Carnegie, okay?" She smiles, and he knows that everything is okay. On that end, at least. Walking out of his house, Jonathan takes out his cell phone and dials a number, then waits. After a moment he hears a gruff voice on the other line. "Hello?" "Hey, Mr. Feeny, I’m having troubles in life again and I need your advice." Foster Nackedy chuckles on the line, causing Jonathan to smile. "Hey, Jon," says Foster. "What’s up? Haven’t talked to you in a while. Been watching, though." "So you saw what happened with me and Sarah on UPROAR this past week." Another chuckle. "Yeah, I saw it. How do you let yourself get into these messes, man? Sarah Riley was supposed to be a one-time thing. You felt locked up in the wrestling business, she was your key. You don’t have to keep using the key when the lock was opened 6 months ago." Jumping into the car, Jonathan starts it and begins driving. "You don’t understand, it’s not that eas—" "How do I not understand, Jon? I fucked her too, remember? And from what I remember there was no superglue in her you-know keeping me stuck to her. Went in, went out, felt better. That’s all you’re supposed to do. Instead, you created a pile of shit bigger than Mayhem to get out of." Jonathan sighs. "I know. I need to tell Katie soon. I mean, all she would have to do is watch last week’s UPROAR and she’d know." "Katie’s not even the issue right now, Jonny." "Really?" Jonathan asks, then swerves to miss a car stopping suddenly ahead of him. After blowing his horn, he continues. "I had sex with another woman on live television. Don’t you think that dealing with the person I cheated on is at the top of my priorities right now?" Foster’s voice is cold and just a little ominous. "Not when Alexandra Pierce is in your federation, no." After another near-miss, Jon decides that it’s easier to have this conversation in a stationary position, and he pulls into a mall parking lot. "Desade? Why should I be worried about Desade?" "Because she’s evil, man. Like, not your garden variety evil either. She’s intense. You saw what she did at FUSE on ESPN." "Yeah, but what would she want with me? I’ve never talked to her, I haven’t tried to stop her from doing anything yet, there’s no reason for her to…" The interruption is jarring because Foster’s voice sounds so serious. "Listen, champ. You are the most famous member in a federation that this woman has made clear that she wants to destroy. You just made a huge mistake that begs to be taken advantage of. Don’t you think she’ll go for the throat? Don’t you think she’s going to hit the federation where it fucking hurts: the pretty boy champion?" Foster’s voice is frantic as he concludes, "Don’t you think AT ALL?" Jonathan’s eyes go wide, and he hangs up the phone, pulling out of the parking lot and making a quick U-Turn. In less than five minutes, Jonathan Rhine arrives outside of Sarah Riley’s home and office, twisting his ankle against the sidewalk as he jumps up towards her home. As he gets to the steps, however, he stops. Because someone else is coming down. He’s too late. He looks at her warily, his breath stopping in his chest. Her dress is professional, and her curtain of red hair somewhat covers her eyes at this angle, but the sight of Desade here is enough to make Jonathan cringe. As she gets to the bottom step, her eyes raise to his and her smile is confident. "Good evening, Jonathan." His anger takes over and he steps towards her. "If you hurt her at all…" Desade’s eyes flicker to the sky for just a moment, her hair falling back on her shoulders before facing Rhine again. "Looks like it might rain, though." Jonathan watches her walk past him and down the sidewalk, then bounds up the steps and into the house without knocking. "Sarah?" he shouts, dreading that he’ll find something terrible, a beaten body, a ransacked room… But all he finds is Sarah Riley, sitting on her couch, a playful smile on her face. "What…are you…did they…?" he tries, but finds he can’t even find the right question to ask. But she still has an answer. "No, Jonathan. I’m fine. Better than I have been in a long time, actually." So Jonathan stands there, not for the first time, without any idea of what to do. You had no idea what was going on then, only that it couldn’t be good. You asked Sarah for an explanation, but she wouldn’t give you one. You tried your best to find Desade to ask her, but that was a hopeless venture. In the end, you went home, and you didn’t sleep. Not that night, or the next night either. You stayed up for three days before flying out to Utah, not sure what terrible things went on in that same house that you lost your morals in. You just kept playing over Foster’s conversation in your head. "Not your garden variety evil." "You made a big mistake." "Don’t you think AT ALL?" Apparently you don’t. And you weren’t sure what the punishment for that was going to be. But now you do. And it’s fucking terrible. 2/27 The second Wednesday is about pain. Jonathan Rhine is woken by the ringing of his phone. He looks at the number and sighs. Beverly Gould. Beverly is his manager, but not in the same way Katie is (or was; he hasn’t talked to her since Temptation). She’s an agent of sorts, setting him up for appearances and making sure that the face of SCCW is a good one. She doesn’t call very often, because for the most part Jon makes her job easy by not causing trouble. Today’s different, however. "Hello?" "Jonny, what are you doing? Are you just waking up? Jesus, it’s almost one o’clock!" Jon rolls over and checks the clock to confirm this statement. "You had a physical at 11 in the morning, and there’s an autograph signing at 2!" Jonathan sighs loudly, trying to fully wake up as he sits up in bed, looking at his surroundings. The small hotel room feels even more cramped in the morning than it did the night before, and Jonathan’s frown gets more defined. "Sorry, I overslept." "I rescheduled the physical to 3:30, so we’ll have to cut the signing short but it should still be okay, we’ll give away some free shirts to make up for it. And then…" "I don’t know, Beverly," Jonathan says groggily. "I don’t want to go." "To what? The signing or the physical?" "Either. I feel fine and the kids will understand; we’ll schedule another one soon." Jonathan rolls to the edge of the bed and opens his suitcase, grabbing a black shirt and sniffing it. Then, he pulls it on and lays back down. Beverly’s anger is piercing, and it causes Jon to close his eyes. "The kids will not understand, they’ll cry to their parents all night and hate you for it. And the physical is extremely important, Jonny, because Aimz made you into an accordion last week! They’re showing a picture of your face in UNLV med school classes so the students can identify lacerations and bruised tissue, for Christ’s sake! Come on, Jonny! Get out of bed." Rolling back onto the mattress, Jonathan sighs again. "I don’t feel like it. I’m not feeling well. If I need to take a physical, I’ll take one in Seattle." "Bad idea, Jonny, that city is cursed! Everything that you do in that city will end in tragedy, which is why I told you not to make the match for this show. And…" "Handle it, Bev. I know I can count on you." Jonathan hangs up the phone and sets it on the dresser, then once again lets out a huge breath and closes his eyes. He’s been at this hotel for the past few days. He worked out a deal with Foster to live with him temporarily after the next show, but for now he wants to stay in the city, for no other reason than he isn’t ready to let go yet. Rolling over to the remote, he flips on the TV and finds the PTC channel; one of the reasons he chose this hotel over the other fifty-five in the area. Conveniently and coincidentally, the SCCW half-hour is on, and they were just finishing talking about him. "…and of course the question that now remains is will Jon Rhine, battered and broken both physically and emotionally, be able to overcome that in his SCCW Universal Title defense next week against former GLOBAL champion Christian Darke. Back to you, Ethan." Jonathan grimaces as he watches the feed cut to Ethan Peters, looking uncomfortable in front of the camera. "Thanks, Pete. Continuing on the subject of Jonathan Rhine’s emotional troubles, new backstage reporter Kendall Heath was outside after the cameras stopped rolling on Temptation, and he caught Katie Malick leaving after the embarrassment of having Rhine admit his affair on live TV. We bring you this exclusive footage now…" On the screen, Katie Malick’s pace is quicker than normal. She looks down to the ground, her hair hiding cheeks that, when the hair moves suddenly and exposes them, are red and wet. Kendall Heath sees her and zooms in for the kill. "This is Kendall Heath with Katie Malick, Jonathan Rhine’s maligned girlfriend after what just happened at the end of the show. Let me ask you, Katie…did you know that Rhine was a ratfuck?" Katie finally looks up, and the sight would make most men step back a step (Heath, of course, doesn’t). She wipes tears from her eyes and says, "What?" "Well, Miss Malick, you have been wonderful and faithful to a man who obviously did not return the favor. Can you give your thoughts on this? Feelings? Your home phone number?" Heath raises his eyebrows in true perverted fashion. Katie looks at him and faintly shakes her head before a yellow taxicab pulls up in the parking lot. She sighs and walks to the cab, entering it. Heath watches her go, then faces the camera and shrugs. "Something I said?" Jonathan turns the TV off and plops back down on the bed. That was the first time he’s seen Katie since the incident. He came home to find the door locked and two suitcases of his clothes and essentials sitting in the garage with a note. Dear Jon, I won’t get mad and tear into you. I won’t tell you how horrible you are. I won’t do anything to you to punish you for hurting me. Because I’m way too hurt to do any of that. I don’t want to see you. Not for a long time. Maybe one day we can talk about it…but not today. And not tomorrow. I just don’t think I can look at you without feeling sick anymore. Sorry, I wasn’t enough, Katie Seeing her again made everything worse. Putting his pillow over his head, he wanted to forget about everything and just lie there all day. No signings. No physicals. He’s not even sure if he wants to go to Seattle next week. Everything just suddenly seems so trivial. The phone rings. "Hello?" "Mr. Rhine, this is Jeffrey from the front desk. You have an unheard message on your phone from last night – would you like me to play it for you?" Jonathan sighs and shrugs. "Sure." "Have a great day." Jonathan grimaces as he hears a click and the message transfers over. "Jonathan. Hey, it’s Cyrus. I…man, you don’t know how hard it is to get a hold of you. I had to call everyone in the world just to find out what hotel you were staying in. You know you can crash with me if you need to, buddy." There’s a long pause, and Cyrus’s voice gets a bit lower and more serious. "Listen, Jon. I know things are really hard right now. Amy, Katie, and everything seems to be blowing up in your face. But you’re the strongest, most determined guy I know. I know you won’t let this get to you, and I can’t wait to fight by your side and watch you take down the Undertow guy and then we can face Amy together. I got your back, man. Later." Jonathan hangs up the phone and nods, then stands up and puts on a pair of pants. Grabbing his cell phone and dialing, he opens the door before saying, "Hey, Bev? You didn’t cancel the signing yet, right? Good. I’ll be there in ten." You can’t just feel sorry for yourself all day. You made a mistake. You fucked up and now you have to pay for it. What you don’t have to do is sit around and letting the world pass you by because you’re too weak to face your problems head-on. You’ve got a title match in 7 days, and it’s not something that you can just not worry about. You are the champion of this federation because you worked your ass off to get there, and you didn’t take anyone lightly. You’re not about to start now. You’re going to do what got you this far, because hiccups in family life, as much as you used to think so, will not affect what you do inside the ring. Because at home you might be a cheater, a jerk, someone who is in a state of depression and disarray. But in Seattle you’ll be a fighter. A force to be reckoned with. A champion. You’re going to do it not just because it’s what Cyrus expects. You’re going to do it because it’s true, and that’s why Cyrus expects it. You’ll deal with Christian Darke. You’ll deal with Amy Campbell. And then, maybe later, you’ll deal with your personal life. But none of that matters next week. Next week, it’s on.
