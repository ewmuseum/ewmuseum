Someday, and this day may never come...

Date: 4/26-5/11 4/25/08 Alexandra Pierce has an umbrella, and it stretches as far as the eye can see. Part of the reason she inspires such fear is that without being any sort of omnipotent being (as far as we know) is that she is everywhere -- there are moles, spies, so many workers for her that you can’t accept an apple pie from a nun without being somewhat suspicious (you should be anyway; can nuns even bake?). Desade is the sort of woman where every move is calculated, every detail combed over incessantly until the desired result is attained. That would imply that Tavon Bishop is always operating under some sort of agenda, seeing as he is dating a woman who is slowly creeping up the ranks and gaining Desade’s respect. However, Bishop has still never seen Desade’s face, and not just because her red hair always hangs over it. Sarah Riley has done an excellent job of keeping her ebony beau away from the business side of the business. Until now. Alexandra Pierce walks through the door of Sarah’s bedroom, her eyes down as she finishes what appears to be a long order: "…and I want transcripts. Anyone who thinks there was not a camera in every room of the ARCO obviously does not understand that privacy is a dream. They most likely said words between the sickening sweetness. Discover them." "Certainly, of course," Sarah says quickly, then looks over at the couch, where Bishop is lounging, bare-chested and eyes closed. "Tavon…what are you doing here?" The Beast from Biloxi opens his eyes and smiles. "Just waiting to surprise you, milady." He looks from her to the Spider in the Web and his smile increases. "Ah, Ms. Pierce. We haven’t met. I assure you it’s a pleasure." He gets up to introduce himself properly and ignores Sarah’s anxious countenance. "I actually wanted to talk to you about something, since I know you have, like, the entire city of Las Vegas on payroll and shit." Desade smiles simply. "If you believe my detractors, you would include seventy-five percent of all major port cities as well." Bishop doesn’t know whether to laugh or accept this as truth, so he does both. "Well, I have sort of a favor." "Tavon, this isn’t the time," Sarah sighs, anxiously looking at her employer and frowning. "I’m sorry, Ms. Pierce, I didn’t know…" "I am willing to listen to anyone who wants something, Sarah. I have time enough for Mr. Bishop’s request." She then flips her eyes back to Bishop, a move that stuns him in silence for a few seconds before recovering. "Oh, uh, yeah. So I know you have people who do favors, and I guess you’re kinda like the Godfather and shit, but I can pay you back whenever. There are these guys who have my Pops in a bad position, and I really don’t know what to do about it…" "My methods, Mr. Bishop, are slightly different from the film. I do not grant favors for someone in hopes that they may someday do the same. I appreciate the fact that your loyal girlfriend has helped me in my endeavors, so I will do whatever I can to rid you of this nuisance, but there will be no ‘someday, and this day may never come.’ This day will come, and it will come soon. Sarah will update me on the details of this, but I will also have her update you on what I need you to do to help me. Is that suitable for you?" Bishop looks at her for a long three seconds, trying to figure out exactly what she said, once he realized that it was, in fact, in English. When he finally does, he nods. "Sure, ma’am. More than fine. I’ll let Sarah know all the stuff, and you can let me know what I need to do." Nodding, Desade turns, her hair slow on the uptake to follow. Sarah Riley walks her to the door, murmuring apologies, before shutting it and turning around to glare at her boyfriend. "What the hell did you do that for?" "Do what?" "Harass my boss," Sarah says, quickly closing the difference between them. "You ask for a favor from her, just because of your connection to me? For some little problem with your dad that you could probably take care of yourself? Do you realize what helping Alexandra Pierce means?" "From what I hear, it means sleeping with Jonny Rhine, right?" Bishop offers, smiling at the discomfort Sarah shows. "Yeah. I may be a big guy, Sar, but I could just find out who these guys are, beat the crap outta them, and then what? They recover, get better, and try to get revenge. No, Sarah, I think I’d rather go about things the Alex Pierce way. And I know what that entails, so don’t look at me like I’m some innocent kid. I can do the seedy stuff, believe me." Sarah stares at him for a few seconds before sighing, then bending her head and sobbing. "I know, Tavon, I know. I’m sorry, I just…" she stops as Bishop grabs her and holds her, putting her to his chest. "I just have so much pressure to do what she wants, and I had no idea what I was getting myself into. I am not a bad person, Tavon. I’m a good person. But I’m doing all these things…I’m ruining people’s lives, and I don’t know how it all started." "It’s okay, babe," says Bishop, putting his head over hers. "We gonna be okay. This’ll be my first and only dealing with the Hand, and then we’ll see about getting you out, okay?" She nods, then says, "I’m going to go take a nap, okay?" Without waiting for an answer, she rushes to the bedroom, hoping that she can hold in the laughter until she gets there. ***************************** 5/11/08 James Bishop’s life has become a dreadful routine. In the morning, he wakes up and checks out of whatever hotel he is staying in, then heads to whatever bar had a neon "Open 24 Hours" sign – today’s bar is called The Anaconda, and it specializes in a drink called the Constrictor – a secret mix of liquor that is supposed to "squeeze the life out of you," as the sign says. James downs several of these drinks in the course of the morning, then stumbles out in the street for the next few hours. These hours are spent earning money in any way he can. Two years ago James tried to get a job at a cardboard box manufacturer in Meridian, Mississippi, but a coworker found out he was gay and got him fired, saying "dis ain’t no fag factory." Turns out there weren’t any fag factories in the area, so James remained unemployed. To get money now, James usually steals. He needs to have fifty dollars by the end of the day each night, but he usually shoots for 100 so he can get a meal and a crappy motel to sleep in after he pays Ricky off. Today he gets sixty-two, which is good enough for McDonalds. Looks like it’s the dumpster again for shelter. James Bishop has a cell phone in the back of his pocket that only has one number in the contacts, listed under "Home." Every day at five o’clock sharp, this phone rings, and James needs to answer, follow the directions, and pay the money he earned. There have been a few times that James has not had the money when the phone rings, breaking the routine. The result has not been pretty. He notices that it is 4:58 and walks to a park bench. He sits and waits, knowing that hearing the voice on the other end is much easier to take when he is sitting down. 5:00 passes, and there is no phone call. James stares at the phone, confused, but continues to wait. After the phone has not rang for fifteen minutes, James’ confusion turns to worry. He is too concerned with his current routine to be happy that the call hasn’t come yet. In fact, as bad as it has become, he is praying for the phone to ring just because he doesn’t know how to deal with the unexpected. At 5:26, the phone finally rings. However, the number is not under "Home." It is restricted. "Hello?" James asks, his voice cracking. "James Bishop?" "Yes." "You are no longer the servant of Richard Eugene. You can spend that sixty-two dollars however you see fit, and if you go the dumpster behind the Tavern you were in earlier this morning, you will find more money to go with it." James shakes his head, confused. "What are you talking about? What happened to Ricky?" "Nothing dreadful. You can discard this phone at any time, or use it if you want to keep contact with your family. The bill will be taken care of." "I…thank you…who are…" James asks, but there is a click, and James is left by himself on the bench. For a few moments he sits there, looking down at his feet in silence. But after a moment he walks two blocks to the tavern and looks in the dumpster. Pulling out a brown bag and finding assorted bills there, he begins to smile, then laugh wholeheartedly. Skipping into the street, he repeats, "Thank you, thank you, thank you…" ***************************** "Thank you, Ms. Pierce," says Bishop, his smile wide and sincere. Desade nods, then turns to Sarah Riley. "Please inform Mr. Bishop of the agreement, and contact me when it is completed." She turns back to Bishop and nods once more, splaying her red hair over her shoulders and once again causing the big man to take a step back. "Good bye, Mr. Bishop," she says, and she leaves Sarah’s home quickly. Bishop’s grin is quickly erased as he turns to Sarah. "So, what’s the damage?" Sarah sighs, then looks down. "She wants you to end Jonathan Rhine’s career." "Oh. Cool. Tell her to give me a few weeks and I’ll get it done." Bishop leaves the room, whistling. He doesn’t give Sarah a second look, so he doesn’t see that her face is crestfallen, and this time it is completely genuine.
