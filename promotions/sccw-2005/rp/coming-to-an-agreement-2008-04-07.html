Coming To An Agreement

7 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/07/08 Jacque slowly pulled into the driveway of his ranch style house. He knew that he and Dianna would have to talk. They needed to figure out this whole Keegan mess. Jacque sat for hours in his family’s house and had waited in hopes of a sign of what he needed to do. It was just now as he pulled in his own driveway did he know what it was he needed to do. He slowly walked up the walkway and opened the front door hours after he was supposed to be home. He heard the giggling and cooing of two small children and his heart warmed. He headed down the hall and into the nursery to give his children a good night kiss. Bella’s eyes light up like a three year old on Christmas morning. Jacque beamed as he headed to her crib first. Jacque picked his little girl up curling her closely to his chest. She had that sweet new baby smell that only a parent could understand. He placed a kiss on her forehead before tucking her in. He turned to Dianna who was dressing Jordan in a pair of light blue pajamas that were adorned with airplanes and locomotives. "We need to talk about some things after we get the kids to sleep." Jacque told her trying to hide the cynicism in his voice. She cocked an eyebrow and nodded as she laid Jordan in his crib and turned on the baby monitor. Jacque leaned down and placed a kiss on the cheek of the already nodding off baby. They both walked hand in hand toward the living room as Jacque ushered Dianna to the couch. "What’s wrong Jacque?" She asked curiously. "Don’t tell me nothing either I know you better than that." She said immediately after curbing Jacque’s chance to deny anything. "I don’t exactly know where to start," Jacque sighed, "I found out some things about why my mom freaked out on us when we brought the kids there." Jacque started. Dianna leaned forward on the couch denoting that he had her full attention. So he continued, "My father had an affair with an engaged woman shortly after I was born," Jacque gulped hard to actually hear himself admit his father’s imperfections, "My mother and the woman’s soon to be husband found out about the whole thing." Dianna looked at him and knew that it was hard for him to continue. She reached out to him but he just couldn’t sit at that moment. She reclined her hand and let him continue. "There was a child Dianna, a little girl named Ellisa. The woman, Alisha, asked my father to give up his parental rights just like we did to Keegan. My father regretted it every damn day of his life. That is why I have been having those awful dreams." Jacque blurted it all with out hardly taking a breath. "Yeah Jacque, your father wasn’t a killer though. Keegan was." She said as a rebuttal but Jacque cut her off before she could complain any further. "It still wasn’t right Dianna. We both know Keegan and how he is. Why would he just give up his flesh and blood?" Dianna shot to her feet with fire in his eyes. "Damn you Jacque. You know why he gave Jordan up. He wanted to hide the fact that he is a killer. There is a total difference between what we did to Keegan and what happened with your father Jacque." Jacque just shook his head at her, "Ya know maybe it was exactly like what my father did. Maybe, just maybe Keegan was trying to be a good father by letting him go. Sometimes you just have to let the things you love go to make them happy." Jacque flopped down on the couch leaving Dianna standing there with her jaw dropped. She sat down on the couch next to him and placed a hand on his left leg. "Maybe your right Jacque. I never thought of it that way." "I would like to send him a picture of his son just so he has it." Jacque said as he pulled out the picture of his sister and gently rubbed his fingers across it. Dianna nodded silently and knew they had finally put that chapter of their lives to rest. Jacque sat in silence after Dianna had excused herself to take a shower. Now that he had put the whole Keegan situation to rest he could focus his attention on finding his sister or figuring out if that is something he should really do. There was a lot that Jacque needed to think about over the next few days. Right now he needed sleep, for him sleep brought answers. Sleep brought dreams of when he was younger playing ball with his father. Dreams of all the fun family things that they used to do. In his heart Jacque knew he needed to find his sister even if she ended up not wanting to be a part of his life. He needed this for his own piece of mind, for his father’s peace in the afterlife. When Jacque awoke he knew that he needed to make arrangements to fly into Canada and have a meeting with Mr. LaFayette. He would have to leave right after his match this week on Temptation. He was facing a handful of new comers that he had no clue about. All he did know is that one of them was a woman. Inter-gender matches were something Jacque despised in professional wrestling. Women fighting women is one thing but he was always brought up not to hit a woman no matter what. In this sport that can be a sever disadvantage. Jacque told Dianna of his plans and asked if she wanted to go with him. They really haven’t gotten the time to spend together since the children were born. "I… I don’t know Jacque. The kids are so young I wouldn’t feel right leaving them." She said skirting around the subject. "We could have your mom watch them. Come on babe we need this time together and I would like you to be there if I actually get to meet my sister." Jacque pleaded with his eyes knowing that she could never resist. She cracked a small smile; "She has been wanting to spend more time with the kids. Let me call her." She said with the nervous laugh of a schoolgirl. After having all their arrangements made Jacque felt a huge weight lifted from his shoulders. He just hoped that once they were in Canada that everything would go smoothly. He knew that Ellisa was more than likely not living at home and he would have to go through Alisha… a task he wasn’t relishing. He didn’t want to meet the woman his father cheated on his mother with. He didn’t know how well he would be able to handle that situation. Lately Jacque wasn’t capable of handling much of anything. He has been on a losing streak for sometime now and his life just kept getting worse. As soon as things start to look up he gets dropped right back to the bottom of the pit. Maybe this trip to Canada was what his life needed. It could be a chance to straighten out his relationship with Dianna and spark a relationship with his sister. The relationship with his sister would put all the demons of many lives at rest.
