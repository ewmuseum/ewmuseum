A Dose Of Reality - Episode 2

3 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 3rd 2009 September 3rd 2009 ‘Do you have a forwarding address?’ Anna had known that this was a long shot but having already tried to contact Josh by phone (number disconnected) and email (address does not exist), she was left with few other options. So now she was here in the lobby of the Sunset Tower apartment building, baby Luca in his stroller beside her, waiting while the building’s security guard, an overweight woman named Susan, searched through her enormous file which held the particulars for all those that had lived there since the building’s opening some 12 years earlier. ‘Let me see…Macy, Malkin, Marley- ah, Marquez. Josh, wasn’t it?’ ‘Yes.’ ‘Says here he only moved out a few weeks ago, no forwarding address though, just a post office box.’ The look of disappointment on Anna’s face was clear and Susan picked up on this immediately. ‘Sorry to let you down like that ma’am. I can give you the box number if you want, maybe you’ll be able to pick him up there?’ The tear that was threatening to trickle down Anna’s cheek was stopped in its tracks, her mind replacing her sadness with a determination and purpose. ‘Thank you, that would be a great help.’ The box number was quickly copied onto a piece of paper and as Anna set off to find her lost lover, Susan couldn’t help but smile and secretly wish her all the best. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ September 5th 2009 Today’s workout had been hard. Really hard. He’d pushed himself the whole session to lift just a little more, to squeeze out another rep, to go for another 100 metres. Now he was paying for it. ‘Jesus I need a drink! Harry, toss me a Corona!’ Harry Reed, Josh’s training partner and general best friend laughed as he walked over from the refrigerator. In his hands he held two bottles, neither of which had the word Corona on them. ‘No Corona’s this week buddy, it’s strictly protein for you!’ As he took the bottle of protein shake from his friend, Josh let out an exaggerated groan, before laughing. ‘This is the part I haven’t missed. Although I have to say…’ Josh takes a swig. ‘…These things have gotten a hell of a lot better! What’s the plan for the rest of the day?’ Harry was opening his mouth to respond when the conversation was interrupted by the ringing of Josh’s cell phone. Josh looked at the caller ID and stood up. ‘Sorry, I’ve got to take this one.’ Harry waved his hand in acknowledgement and flicked on the television as Josh made his way out onto the balcony. ‘What’s up?’ Marquez’ tone is even and everyday. Little does he know that the news he is about to receive will change his life forever. As the person on the other end of the phone speaks to him, his face drains of colour and a look of bewilderment is written across it. ‘Okay, I’ll be right there.’ *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘I never thought I’d see you again.’ ‘Did you want to?’ ‘Like I told you back then, I was with someone an-‘ ‘Cut the crap Josh, Matt told me everything.’ ‘What do yo-‘ ‘About the money. About how he paid you to leave me.’ ‘Look I never mea-‘ ‘Josh forget it. It’s done, it’s in the past. I’m here to talk about the future.’ ‘The future?’ ‘I’m not with Matt. We were married and we split up. I could never be happy with him, not after you, and believe me, I tried. God did I try!’ ‘So you’re here because you want to make me feel bad? Or what?’ ‘No Josh, I’m here because stupid as it may make me, I love you. I’ve never stopped loving you and I’ll be damned if I’m going to just leave it without trying. You’re the father of my child for Christ’s sake!’ You could have knocked him down with a feather. ‘What? Child?’ ‘Did he not tell you on the phone?’ ‘Does it look like he told me?! We have a child?!’ The full gamut of emotions are running across Josh’s face. Happiness to confusion to anger to sadness. Finally he calms down and the look on his face is unmistakeable. Joy. ‘Yes Josh, we have a son. His name is Luca.’ ‘Where is he?’ ‘Inside, your assistant is looking after him.’ ‘Well can I see him!?’ ‘Of course. You’re his father.’ Anna leads the way into the office block where Josh’s business empire is run from, with a shellshocked Marquez trailing behind her. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ He’d only laid eyes on him for the first time 10 minutes ago but already he felt a bond, an unbreakable link between him and the child. No, not the child, he corrected himself; his son, Luca. For the boy was undoubtedly his, from the shape of his face, to his shock of dark hair to his skin tone, everything about him marked him out as Josh’s progeny. Anna was looking on with a film of tears in her eyes. The enormity of what she was attempting to do had only hit her when she made her way through the glass doors towards the reception desk of the building. She thought she knew Josh intimately, felt that he would react well to the news she had for him but did she really know him? They’d had a whirlwind romance and he’d left her the second somebody flashed some money in front of him. Would he be happy to see her? Would he be happy about Luca? Or would he toss them both out and ignore them, cutting all ties as he had done all that time ago? Seeing this, though, vindicated her. She had been right about Josh and about the future. They could make a life together as a family and the last few years written off. ‘Josh…’ She approached him and took his hand in hers. As he rose up to his feet, she looked him directly in the eye. ‘…I want us to work. For us to be together with Luca. I know this is a shock and I have no idea if you’re with someone else now but I just know that this is how it was all meant to turn out.’ Marquez was silent for a few seconds, composing himself before he responded. ‘You’re right, it is a shock. It’s huge. Now if you’d come to me just a couple of months ago, I would have told you that I didn’t want you, that I’d look after Luca as much as I could financially but nothing else-‘ Oh no! She’d got it wrong, he was just going to ignore them, ignore the chance they now had! ‘Don’t do thi-‘ ‘That was a couple of months ago. I’ve been making some changes in my life recently and I’ve reached a stage where I will take some risks in my personal affairs, risks I used to shy away from.’ He places his hands on her face, pulling her close to him as he kisses her forehead. ‘So let’s do it. Let’s try and see if we can’t give this little guy the parents he deserves. Truth is, I’ve not stopped thinking about you, every day you’re in my head. I guess I must love you.’ With that, it was done. Josh Marquez, the suave, debonair bachelor, suddenly had a girlfriend and a son. The wild child had responsibility and what was even stranger was…it suited him.
