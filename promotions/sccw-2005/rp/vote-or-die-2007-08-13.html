Date: 8/13/07 'What we call the beginning is often the end. And to make an end is to make a beginning. The end is where we start from.' -TS (Thomas Stearns) Eliot, 'Four Quartets' "Why you sir, why not?" [We’re inside something we rarely see anymore] "I mean, I know a person of your fine and moral upstanding nature wouldn’t want something like this to continue to effect America’s, nay the World’s children any longer?" [Several taxi cabs whirl by as we see the wild lion manned hair of your Savior and mine…] "How do I know? I know first hand, I’m a product of the environment, my father was…" [His voice trails off as a large semi-truck blares past our screen] "Oh, you’ve heard of him? Well I can tell you first hand of what it’s done to my family and my life, and I want to help take it down from the inside out!" [We pan wider, we’re on a street corner] "I have your support! Excellent sir, and what about some of your other peers, the more people we have backing this, the better." [He’s wearing his usual attire, basketball shorts, basketball shoes, a loose fitting T-Shirt and a bandana, he’s locked inside of the four walls of glass and steel] "Wonderful! I’ll look forward to meeting with you, and I appreciate you donating those two buses for this week, they will come in handy, I’ve already gathered enough support to make a statement this week, but come August 18th sir, my name as well as yours will go down as the saviors of many peoples lives…" [A pedestrian saunters by and glances into the conversation] "What am I going to do sir? [Slight chuckle] Well I’m going to lead the charge, match by match, win by win, starting this week against some violent scourge who was just arrested for unlawful conduct a couple of weeks ago…" [Silence] "Yes, Mayson Colby is his name sir… [We pan further out as a large red piece of linen obstructs our view of our savior] "…No, he shouldn’t but much of a problem, as long as I keep his illegal weapon usage to a minimum, I mean he harmed a fan in his melee…" [A blue piece of linen now obstructs our view] "He’d be the perfect person to make an example out of sir, I like your style. [Smile] So I have your word you’ll be there on August 18th sir? [Now white covers our screen] "Good God sir, that is awesome! I’ll see you then, yes sir thank you for all your support, and God bless you." [Click] [We pan out to see your Savior standing inside of a lone phone booth, smiling] "They want a war, [nervous chuckle], it’s only just begun!" [He smiles as he steps out and stares over the marvel that is the United States Senate, he nods his head in approval and mockingly salutes the flag and begins to hum in a sing song voice] "Oh say does that Star Spangled Banner yet wave, or the land of the freeeeee…." [Sinister smile] "…And the home, of the….Saved!" [He strolls away as the annoying sound of the phone dangling from the booth and the operator repeating herself takes over our screen and we fade…]
