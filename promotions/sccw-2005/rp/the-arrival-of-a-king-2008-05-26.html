The arrival of a King.

26 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 26 A black ’69 Shelby streaks across the desert heading towards Las Vegas. "So, this is where we are today?" The words were just spoken from the honey blonde haired female sitting in the passenger seat. Strands of the honey blonde hair come down and frame her delicate porcelain doll like features. Her nose is perfectly placed below lucid deep set eyes. A blue cotton baby doll dress hangs loosely around her. "Yes. Celeste." His tone is pleasant; almost as if he were attempting to appease a child. Simon King’s black sunglasses shield his eyes; his hair is cut in a short layered style. The layers are perfectly cut and styled so that they lay neatly and flat against his head. The black fitted dress shirt that he’s wearing clings to his torso. "Why are we here again Simon?" "There’s always been something about the glitz and the glamour of the strip." "Right, glitz and glamour is why you dragged me here!?! I know you better than that Simon." King’s thin lips curl into a coy smirk. "What other possible reasons could there be for us to be here?" Celeste asks as she crosses her legs. "It sure the hell isn’t glitz and glamour. As I recall you stated a long time ago, glitz and glamour are liabilities." "Fame.." "Fame and fortune are not found in Vegas either. Unless you’re prize fighter or own a casino." Celeste’s eyes open wide as if she were having a moment of clarity. "Off of it now King. Right this minute, no song and dance. Save the smoke and mirrors for has been gay magicians with hungry zoo talent working with them." "I have no idea what you’re talking about. There is nothing for me to get off of. I wanted to go on vacation." King’s comments are a soft backhanded attempt to keep this conversation from happening. "Vegas is NOT a vacation spot! Especially not this time of year, actually anytime of the year! They don’t even have their own water supply for Christ sake." "In the past five years have I ever steered you wrong Celeste?!?" King’s tone is losing the appeasing nature it had earlier. A moment passes. Celeste breaks the silence. "In the last five years you’ve never been secretive Simon. Everything has been laid out on the table; no unnecessary risks were ever taken. That was the deal. Now you have me riding into Vegas with no idea of what is going on and you think that by telling me that we’re going on vacation that all is going to be well." King begins to speak but is cut off. "A vacation was a month on the Mediterranean. Not Vegas." "Tantrum done?" King asks as his thumbs dance against the steering wheel. Celeste crosses her arms and slouches in the passenger seat. "Now, there is no secret that I’ve always wanted to visit Vegas." "Yes, visit is two to three days. Vacation, clearly not." Her tone is far from passive at this point. "Well, you touched on it before since you won’t let it go." "Did I?" "Yes." "Boxing isn’t your foray and I checked the financial statements after we got back for Europe and there isn’t I own a casino money in any of those accounts." "Who said boxing was the only prize fighting these days? Which financial statements did you check Celeste?" Celeste ponders this for a moment and then her eyes narrow. Her nostrils flare as she leans forward. "King! You have been keeping secrets. Which casino!?" "There’s something I want to check out first hand and then I’ll tell you everything that you need to know on both things." "No, you’re going to tell me now!" King turns towards her and lowers his sunglasses to reveal piercing blue eyes. They flicker with a hint of annoyance, his lips tighten and he slowly pushes the sunglasses back to cover his eyes. "I will tell you when I feel like it!" These words are delivered with a voice that resonates through the cabin of the car. King’s grip on the steering wheel tightens and his knuckles begin to turn white. ---- The exterior of the MGM Grand. Inside of a Penthouse suite. Simon King sits at a burgundy felt covered table. He’s wearing a chocolate colored Egyptian cotton suit with tan pinstripes with hints of thinner blue striping. Underneath a blue silk shirt is buttoned to the top and he is wearing a tie that matches the suit to perfection which matches his olive complexion. His sandy brown hair has an out of the shower look to it. "Mr. King do you have the items needed to play the game?" These words come from the man that we will presume is in charge of the activities for the evening. King turns his head slightly towards the mass of the man with him. The bulk’s grip on the steel case in his hand loosens and tightens. Hired help in the form of a man who stands about five foot ten inches tall and built like a spark plug. King turns his attention back to man sitting at the head of the table. "Does Mr. Lexington have the papers necessary to play the game?" Sitting across from King is real estate mogul Christian Lexington. He owns or has his hands on quite a few pieces of property that line the strip. Lexington is wearing a cream linen suit, white shirt and a yellow and white striped tie. His shaven skull shines under the lights. He works a toothpick in the corner of his mouth impatiently. "I have the papers necessary to play the game but that really doesn’t matter because you’ll be leaving that case here." "Are you sure about that Mr. Lexington?" "Positive." "Shall we get on with it then?" "Yes, we shall." A black brief case has been placed on the table. King reaches into his breast pocket and removes an envelope. Lexington does the same as King; they open the envelopes at the same time and remove keys. The keys are placed and turned at the same time and the brief case opens revealing a deck of cards. A dealer opens the sealed deck of cards and shuffles them by hand. Before the cards are dealt Lexington removes three documents from his breast pocket. King’s mass of a man places the steel brief case on the table. Simon rolls his thumb over the combination and the case opens and two velvet trapezoids. "Five card stud is the game per the arrangement." The dealer’s voice is dry. The cards are dealt and Lexington looks at his hand casually. King picks up his cards one by one and is holding the ace of spades and the ace of clubs along with the eight of spades the three of diamonds and the two of clubs. King’s head tilts to the left and his eyes make their way over to case containing the bars. "Regrets already Mr. King? You can always walk away now and keep one of those or face the embarrassment of me taking both of them." King places the three of diamonds and the two of clubs on the table face down and pushes them towards the center of the table. Lexington looks back at his hand. He’s holding the ace of hearts, the ace of diamonds the five of spades and the two of hearts. "No regrets at all Lexington. None at all." Lexington passes the five and spades, two of hearts along with the four of diamonds to the center of the table face down. "Two for Mr. King and three for Mr. Lexington?" The dealer asks. The dealer turns to King and he nods and the dealer turns his attention to Lexington and he nods in agreement. The cards are dealt to Lexington first and he taps the two cards in front of him and then picks them up. His eyes glaze as he looks at the eight of hearts, the eight of diamonds and the queen of spades. There’s a glint in his eye as he tucks the cards away. King picks up his two cards and his lips purse tightly. The eight of clubs and the king of spades. His eyes dart around the room wildly. The hand of legendary myth is his. The fate of the owner to whom the hand was named never opened his eyes again after looking at the hand. A bullet rudely found it’s way into the back of his skull and he died on the spot. "Mr. Lexington, Mr. King shall we see the hands one card at a time please." Lexington places the ace of the ace of hearts, King places down the ace of spades. Lexington places down the ace of diamonds; King places the ace of clubs. Lexington’s eyes narrow. Lexington then places down the eight of hearts; King follows by placing down his eight of clubs. Lexington now tilts his head as he begins to disbelieve what he’s seeing. He then places down the eight of diamonds and King places down his eight of spades. "Note that Mr. Lexington his holding two pair and that Mr. King is also holding two pair. Both containing aces and eights. The next card shown will determine the winner." Lexington flips his final card over revealing the queen of spades. He slides his chair away from the table slowly and begins to reach for the steel case. His hands grasp the velvet and he slides out two shimmering bars of gold. Lexington’s eyes dance as sheer greed consumes him. King clears his throat and begins tapping his final card on the table. "Mr. Lexington if you will please return my bars to the Mezzi and hand over those very important documents I would be extremely happy." Lexington’s eyes focus on the card as King continues to tap it on the table. "You’re going to tell me you beat two pair aces and eights and I have the queen of spades." King flips the card over and lays it down for everyone to see. "Isn’t that a bitch; Mr. Lexington? Oh wait, you have the bitch and I have the King." Lexington lets out a muffled growl. "I’d say today is a very good day to be the King, wouldn’t you Mr. Lexington." "You cheated King! I will have my lawyers…" King throws his hand up in feigned defiance. "You will do no such thing Lexington." Lexington’s face contorts as King stands up and adjusts his suit jacket. "Lexington as far as this is concerned tonight never happened. Simply because you will continue your duties, yes duties and you will report directly to me. You will be my puppet on a string and you will do my bidding without question." "Why on God’s green earth would I do something that ridiculous!?!" King reaches over the table and smoothes over Lexington’s lapels and he leans in close. "That’s rather simple Lexington, I own you. I own those documents that you bought with you and if you will please place those in the Mezzi with the gold. I want you to forget how you lost the game because after all that was a game of chance and luck and she wasn’t with you tonight. This is what I want you to do though, I want you to remember who you lost to and that you are no longer the most powerful entity in the city of Las Vegas." The statement is delivered in a whisper that is barely audible. King eases away from Lexington and there is a faint smirk on his face. One of the most powerful men in Vegas just lost a lot of land on the strip due to luck. A man just inherited a lot of land on the strip due to destiny. The future of one is looking bleak and the future for the other is beginning to glow. Hayato watches Lexington reluctantly place the gold bars back into their velvet sleeves. Lexington then places the folded documents into the Mezzi. King exudes a bit of arrogance, "Lexington if you will do the honors of closing that. My business is done here." Lexington thinks for a moment. In that moment Hayato takes half a step towards him and Lexington does as he is told. -- King and Hayato are walking down the hallway towards the elevator. Hayato’s grip on the Mezzi is firm, his stride matches King’s step for step. "Mr. King." "Yes, Hayato." "That hand you had, was that the infamous Aces and eights?" The question is almost rhetorical in nature. "Dead man’s hand Hayato?" King nods solemnly. Hayato’s face tightens into a grim death mask. "Next stop Mr. King." Less rhetorical. "Sin City Championship Wrestling offices." "Mr. King are your abilities up to those that compete there?" "Let’s put it this way Hayato; if I had their hand, I’d cut off my arm." King and Hayato enter the elevator and the stainless steel doors close before they can turn around.
