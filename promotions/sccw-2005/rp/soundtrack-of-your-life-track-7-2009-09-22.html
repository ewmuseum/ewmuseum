Soundtrack of your life: Track 7

22 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 22, 2009 Driving on the wrong side of the road, the oncoming traffic dodging each way to avoid this maniac, the sound of horns buzzing through the air. I look on with horror and dismay, able to do nothing but push that pedal closer to the floor. This trip is looking to come to an abrupt and violent stop, and all I can offer is to toss my head to the side, not wanting to see anymore of this. And then the force asserted by the airbag enveloping my torso and face. Fuck I hate this dream. It all seems so real as it happens. Why do I do nothing but speed this voyage of doom up? It has been quite amazing to note that when I decided to follow my dream – which in fact is a simple misnomer; I am chasing goals – then that my sleeping thoughts attack with such ferocity. I’d like some Jungian head doctor to try and figure this one out for me. Was it of things to still happen? Argh…. I don’t need this. I could really stand to be in a ‘happier’ place altogether. I rub the sand from my eyes and peer over to the alarm clock posted bedside. Its dull red light configured at a time of 1:25 am. The low wattage red did little to fight against the black the coated the walls and consumed the entire room. Quiet enough that I could hear the covers being ripped off my body. So tired, yet I could not sleep, stuck for the sake of decency to the two mere metres down the hall still fast asleep to remain in this room that I used to call mine. But that was at a time before the countdown was set. That was a time before strangers raided the whole of my home with sights of killing my memories, building their own on top of it all. While I was growing more comfortable with idea of my parents doing something that they wanted, that would see them escape jobs they hated, I would still not shake the feeling of betrayal. Though I travelled the course of North America with Sin City, there was one place that I longed to come back to. Superman’s fortress, and I do not even consider myself as arrogant to think myself a hero, much less a super one, was still needed by the most powerful imagination. I had no where worth longing for. I yearned for nothing but to find myself that feeling of home again. And so when I was beginning to find a niche in wrestling, where I could be successful, and financially secured, if not often injured, I was less than happy to face this place falling away from me as well. I could not figure any of my problems as being my fault by any reason other than not walking away from them sooner. All this was the sort of heavy emo thoughts of sleeping in a house no longer a home, and yet another career change, which were enough of a monkey to keep me awake in the wee hours. Managing to make my way across the room, over a floor scattered with clothes and proof of my lazy attitude toward cleanliness, I flicked over the switch and brought light to the darkness. And over to the desk, where I sought to communicate through paper some thought to act as my light in the presence of this looming fog. Feverously I took up a pencil and tried to draw something that might make this fade away, at least long enough of to let me sleep the night away. I started with the only quick thing I thought I could do half decently, but the eyes I drew just stared back at me in a judging way. Crumpled and now flying toward the trash basket, that paper was quickly replaced by another. This time I drew something of a goal in itself. The one thing that might make at least one of my problems dissipate – the LiveWire Title. The picture itself looked childish and displayed the disrespect that I projected onto that strap in the past. The two past failed chances to see it shine on my shoulder had done something to damage any reputation it might hold, but it was not gold it represented now. No longer was it some bragging right, it meant saving a career still in its infancy. It was not a step up, but steady ground to build upon. But to study the image closer is to see it in its small scale, in correlation to the artist’s probably of attaining it. And it became so clear then, that nothing I could think of in this frame of mind would bring about joy, happiness or relief. This was a man determined to not wallow in sadness until the tears put me to sleep. Happiness would be found by eternal sources. Not television. No I have surfed the late night airwaves and found nothing but the selling of sex and other random things, none of which I sought. My iPod was filled with either alt rock, or the heavy stuff I used to make me angry enough to run a pair of knees into the mouth of a guy I had barely known besides passing in the hallways. So music presented songs to grow the depression I swam in, or to pump up the adrenaline coursing in my veins, assuring sleep would not come any time soon. Something boring enough so that I could sleep after, but uplifting to push away the dreams of misery, and then like a Frisbee getting larger in a park, it hit me. When I first took to task the efforts to become a professional wrestler, I spent more than a few nights sitting at this desk, not drowning in despair, but brimming with excitement. When I was a man, a young man, leaping into his dream, I did this same process. I was never the type to keep a journal, or even a blog that my generation had taken with such an embrace, but I do remember a letter. Oh were might I be keeping such scribbles in this heap of mess and unorganized thought? Brief digging yielded this letter and unfolded the time capsule. Without haste I started reading. ---- To whoever is reading this, Although I am most certain it will be myself who finds these words, or my mother who in the process of looking to understand my motivations has stumbled upon this letter, I write now openly and honestly, so that this might stand as a shining example of the hope that I once carried in my heart. I am writing this as of the 5th of May, the year 2007. And now I have completed little over a month’s worth of training to the profession that I had grown idolizing as a child – professional wrestling. I am twenty one years old, and those twenty one years on this planet have distanced me so much from what it was that made life great as a child. This city, the world I live in, has made it so hard to live with the dreams of a child, such to the fact that this is the last I have not given up hope completely, and here now I give myself to it completely. This will not be a topic of conversation to share amongst friends in my twilight years, to yearn for the fantasies of youth let slip by and no longer capable. The short time I have practiced, I have seen great improvements. This is, and the positive words from the coaches, enough to tell me I have a real chance of doing something special. The pain I feel now is tempered and controlled; something I know will get me above the other men who are forced to turn to drugs to make this go away. This lifestyle will see me focus on my goal. And here is where I will make this clear, and I promise not to compromise. I will be respected. I don’t care if they love me or fear me, they will respect me. I want to become the best in the world at this craft. I want to be in the minds of the fans. I want to be as hated as was Meanstreak. I want to be as loved as was Tempest. As talked about as is Aimz. I want the fans to chant my name as they have had. And here I take pause to offer words of encouragement to myself, as I am certain that this ride will not be easy and would never be with a simple choice. So here it is: Please don’t give up on this. You are worth this. We are more than worthy to stand up and take something that we want. Please never be content to fade away into that sleepwalk existence. I know that is a waste of this gift of size and strength, of life in general. But I know also that this choice is not entirely ours. Do what you must to grab a hold of this, but do not sell out wholly. In the end, this may leave your body more broken as our metal makeshift left is at this moment, but do not give them your dignity. That is the one thing no one may take from you. The only way to lose this is to give it away, and when you do, that is when they will leave you with nothing. I have the confidence in us to have this become reality. Do not lose that. I want this to become a man worth respecting. I realize that my motivation to produce a child of my own is slim, but I will do everything I can to be seen as someone that Emily could look up to. For that little girl I would give the world. As for my own family, I wish to no longer be the young one, that burden. I want you to be proud of me, or if not that, than at least as an equal to the man they reasonably expected me to be. Tim, I will finally have that upper hand in our tussles. If anyone else has found this, I hope you have gained some insight as to why I have done what I did. Why I am on this path I am. And if you still don’t really get this, than fuck you. You are not meant to be privy to my thoughts. And here’s to brighter and better, Mitchell James Quinlan ---- And there it was. That hopefulness of me just two short years ago, that I counted on being contagious. A man sure about at least one thing, and that was myself at this moment. I was sure that I would not betray my own wishes as outlined. In Las Vegas I would fight to become respected. But now, now I sleep. ----- The silver sliver of moonlight played behind dark drifting clouds that drenched the ground. The rusted cast iron gates creak open and invite you to walk amongst the stones that make the way of sadness and souls past. The ones that do have flowers left beside them have withered and are barely there. Did anyone care? You carry your walk to the sound screeching through these hallowed grounds. The bagpipes hurt your ears, but I love them. A few paces more before it finally comes into full sight. Two men stand before an open grave. The first is a burly Scottish man who presses the noise out the tubes of the instrument. The second was the priest, in black vestments and reading from the Good Book. I think you can gather that this is the send off to some, who looks rather ill-liked in his living days. Feeling some sense of sorrow or pity you move closer to stand witness to the laying of a casket beneath a cactus plant in bloom. Take a look around you, and through this scenery of death and gloom look around to see the neon lights emanating from the oasis in this desert just few miles away. But back to the grave, where you scoop a handful of dirt, say a small prayer and cast the first dirt on it. It is several moments after the piper and the priest had made their way away before you gaze to the headstone and squint past the rain to read: "Here lies the Career of Mitchell Quinlan. It was short. It was forgettable." And in a rage I wake from the latest attack of sleeping thoughts. Curse these dreams that curse me. I cross my hand over my forehead, to my chest, and then to each shoulder, making the sign of the cross. "Devil of self-doubt I cast thee out! In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost be gone from me." Vegas was in my dreams. But I was not following my dreams. I was on the hunt for my goals. And at Equilibrium, let the hunt continue.
