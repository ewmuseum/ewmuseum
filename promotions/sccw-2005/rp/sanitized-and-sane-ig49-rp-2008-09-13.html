Sanitized and Sane (IG49 RP)

13 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.13.2008 September 13, 2008 6:03pm EST Boston, MA A pot of water had been heating on the stove for the last twenty minutes. He’d been wrestling for months as King Blueberry, though that was not his real name. In the last few months he had become entangled in conflict with the Dead Man’s Hand, a group of individuals that he felt could conceivably rival any super-villain establishment. In doing so he’d become somewhat friendly with Charlotte Ramone, former LiveWire Champion and former Infinite Gauntlet holder. On September 8, King Blueberry had won the LiveWire Championship in a tag team match. Later that night he’d watched as Charlotte Ramone revealed herself as the Hand’s ruthless leader, Desade, and participated in perpetrating a conspiracy against Jonathan Rhine, ultimately ending his career. A few days removed from those events, King Blueberry now sat at the small circular table in his kitchen, drumming his fingers on the tabletop as he eyed the stove, waiting for the water to reach a rolling boil. Inches away, and placed atop a folded towel, rested the LiveWire title belt. After that night, before heading back to Boston, he’d made what Blueberry Dragon had interpreted as an off-handed comment about how he intended to boil the belt as a means to "cook the Desade out it." This was exactly how the masked ‘Duke of Deliciousness’ had planned on spending his Saturday. "I still think you’re insane," came the voice from the hallway, seconds before the oddly named Blueberry Dragon poked his head into the kitchen, "it’s not like she spreads some highly contagious, flesh-eating bacteria, you know." The look on King Blueberry’s face made it quite obvious that was a sentiment he disagreed with. Dragon stepped into the kitchen, taking a seat at the table opposite his friend. "Okay, so maybe it’s a little like that," he continued, shifting his gaze to the pot of water, "but don’t you think this is a little extreme?" "No, I honestly don’t." The sincerity in his voice was startling, catching Blueberry Dragon slightly off guard. King Blueberry stood from his seat, picking up both belt and towel before stepping over to the stove where the sound of agitated bubbling had alerted him that his water was ready. He reached into a nearby drawer, pulling from it a pair of stainless steel kitchen tongs. "And I’ve got to tell you, Li’l D.," he said, clamping the tongs down on the championship’s faceplate, "There’s still a few more things that ‘Charlie’ had that I need to sanitize." "What else is…" Dragon started to speak, but cut himself off when he realized what King Blueberry was referring to. "You’re not. Please tell me that you’re not." The belt hit the water with an audible plop, its size causing some of the water to splash out on the stovetop. King Blueberry placed the tongs down on the counter, and turned to face his friend. His smile lived somewhere between playful and psychotic. "Oh, I most certainly am."
