Date: 20/09/07 "So how long has it been going on Ethan?" To admit you have a problem is one thing, but to actually go out of you way and seek help is another, Ethan Frost had made that decision, a decision to better his life, he knew that he was in trouble and needed to rectify his life and the torrent of pain that was flooding his way, he needed to put a stop on the continual downward slide that he had found himself on and as a result he had gone and seen a psychiatrist. It had all been done very quietly, he had been quite impressed in fact, that despite his stature, that everything had been kept so quiet, it was as though they genuinely cared. Lets be honest it wasn’t like he was an A list celeb who the paparazzi would love to catch checking in and out of rehab to sell a few thousand more copies of their magazine. Ethan Frost was a wrestler, not even a major league one, the only people who would be interested in the development and the twists and turns of Frost’s life would be the sort of person who would be sitting awake at midnight pretending to be a wrestler in some internet based e-fed. Whatever that was. So Frost had gone to the doctors and explained the situation, he had explained everything, the heroin, the self harming, the loss of Fred, everything and to be honest the doctor had looked at him as though Pandora’s box had just been opened in his face and there was no closing it. That is where Dr Jenkins came in, a woman in her mid forties but seemed to have aged a hell of a lot more than her years, her skin was cracked and wrinkled, as though that every stem of doubt and despair that had ever been thrown at her, had been absorbed by her skin and taken into her soul, her career had aged her far more than it ever should have. However she insisted on hearing everything for herself, she gauged reactions, tone of voice, body language anything that would give her an insight into the psyche of her patient, anything that would assist her in making a diagnosis so that she could help her patients, it was all she wanted to do, help people, however some didn’t see it that way. "I’ve already explained all of this to the other guy, you have the notes there, you have everything you need, why do you need me to say it all again. I don’t need to tell you everything that has happened in my life for you to know I’m crazy, I just don’t want to wake up in the morning feeling like this anymore, I’m simply asking for you to help me. Ethan Frost, a man who had lost it all and was simply looking for help now, he had little to no motivation in life that was propelling him forwards, nothing was driving him anymore except one little thought in the back of his mind, something that he dare not comprehend, something that he dare not allow to think of as a possibility. It wasn’t even a pipe dream, a pipe dream was something that you aspired to down the line somewhere, this was something a lot darker, something rooted deep away an evil side waiting to be released, but you know that should you release it you will never turn back. "Please Ethan, I understand it’s hard but I need you to tell me in your own words how long it’s been going on. There is no shame in feeling weak, or upset you are only human and as a result are prone to moments of weakness, you have already accomplished the hardest thing and come for help, please Ethan, let me help you." So Ethan spoke to her, he told her everything that she wanted to hear, ultimately the truth as she sat there writing down every single word, her eyes never seeming to leave his, she put him at peace, she made him feel comfortable. Frost was no psychologist, but he knew a few basics, he had studied it briefly at college before his amateur wrestling background began to dominate his time, he knew that as he spoke he was almost entirely closed towards her, his head hung low, arms closed across his body, doing anything and everything to avoid eye contact, this soon faded though and found slowly but surely he was beginning to open up to her about everything. Well almost everything. Some things are too dark to even comprehend. It was only a t-shirt after all. An invitation, an invitation to the dark side. Dr Jenkins continued to ask the questions and Frost continued to answer, she even started to dwell upon his childhood, trying to find another reason for the self harm in his life, his career, trying to find if somewhere along the line he had become addicted to the pain and having been out of action for so long had sought the same release elsewhere, in the form of a blade, slicing into skin, even as far as tearing sinews of muscle in the worst case scenarios, but nothing was found out, not really, everything seemed to stem from his heroin addiction, well everything that he was willing to share anyway. He only knew a bit about psychology, but he knew how to lie with the best of them, he was a wrestler, it was his job to lie on a day by day basis. He had fallen in love with pain, but it was long before he had discovered the guilty pleasure of a warm blade. It happened months ago when Frost was forced out of FUSE for the first time. It happened when The Affliction had broken his arm. It was a second aspect to the pain itself that he was truly drawn to though, something secondary to the pain. It was the ability to deliver it. To see the pain and suffering in another mans eyes. It was all about the power. "I understand how hard this must be for you Ethan." She didn’t know, not really she hadn’t live through it. She didn’t crave the opportunity to hurt people, far from it. Her life revolved around helping people and cleansing their wounds, but with every single heartbeat, every single second the passed Frost was drawn more and more into the inner recesses of his mind. A mind where a simple t-shirt was hanging up on the wall, all he needed to do was take it off the wall and slip it on, give in to the hunger and allow himself to in a real essence be free. Free from self doubt and worry! Free from responsibility! Free from always having to do the right thing! Free from everyone else fucking relying on him! "Yes, it’s very hard" A reply was uttered, but simply because he felt he should, the silence had dragged on for far too long for his liking. "I do appreciate you telling me everything though Ethan, however I get the feeling there is something you aren’t telling me, something you want to say but are either holding back or for whatever reason, fighting against, there is no-one to fight here Ethan, it’s just me and you and I want to help you, but you need to be one hundred percent honest with me. Is there anything else that you want to tell me?" Well that’s really two questions in one there isn’t it. There is something else to tell, but whether it will ever be told is another matter all together, because as far as Frost saw it, this other thing running around his mind was not a problem. His drug addiction was a problem His self harming was a problem The thought of accepting an offer from ‘The Affliction’ to further his career to have back up and security for the foreseeable future? That did not sound like a problem at all, it sounded like a sound business proposition. Frost had spent his life on the lower rungs of the ladder, his days in UWF he was cannon fodder for Foster Nackedy, week in week out matches in which a rookie should not have been placed, he was made to look like a joke, someone that was thrown to the wolves repeatedly and no matter how valiantly he fought, no matter how many times he put his body on the line to impress, it was never enough. It was then that something clicked in his mind, if the room had been just the slightest iota silent it would have been audible. A small smile formed on the face of Frost, subtle and almost indistinct. "No Doctor, there is nothing else that I want to tell you at all, you have been most helpful, most helpful indeed." … Case Notes. The Patient EF seems to demonstrate feelings of remorse for his actions, showing that cognitive faculties are still working, he is still able to distinguish between right and wrong, which shows huge signs of promise and with the correct cognitive therapy should lead to a full recovery, however his chemical dependency has led to several other issues, most notably his continual self harming and withdrawal from reality. EF in his own words has noted that on numerous occasions that he now often seeks solace in solitude, in which he goes to a place in his mind where he retreats, this coupled with his harming has resulted in a different type of dependency, a new type of euphoria. It should be noted that EF’s chemical dependency has dropped significantly based on his answers and whilst I still have doubts about the true extent of addiction I am inclined to believe him as he is showing all the regular indications of a man who is overcoming his battle. What strikes me most about EF is that he had sought help, something which is extremely positive in my opinion, a man who is willing to put himself on the line and admit that he has a problem has taken the first step in the right direction. EF claims that a huge weight has now been lifted from him and whilst I am currently waiting on the results of some blood work I am confident that from the chemical dependency he will come through with minimal damage, there should be no long term effects. What does perturb me though is EF’s current mental status, as mentioned previously I feel that EF is trying to take on far more than he is currently capable of, his situation at work has placed him under a considerable amount of stress and has once again forced him to a crossroads, whilst it is my professional opinion that EF should simply be at home recuperating around loved ones and family, something he claims to not have. The only reference he ever makes to a friend is someone by the name of Fred Fusion, however no-one has seen nor heard of Fred Fusion in months, leading me to believe that Fred Fusion is a character in EF’s mind a companion that he has created himself as some form of last ditch grasp on reality. Having heard some of the stories that EF and FF have shared, it would be ridiculous to believe that FF could ever exist, a man like that would have trouble getting up in the morning. Returning back to the problem of EF’s mental status, in the next few weeks EF is going to have to make a choice which I do not feel he is ready to do at the moment without taking everything into full consideration, if he is forced into making a decision now I feel that it would be an honest opinion that was true to who he really is. I have scheduled another meeting with EF in a few weeks in which time the blood cultures will have come back and I will be able to look at a more pro-active way of assisting with his recovery. Tentatively I believe that EF will be able to overcome the chemical dependency as he has shown signs of wanting to quit himself, however his methods of combating this have proved to be most detrimental and as a result feel that he may need, should his dependency on self harm continue or increase on any level a time signed off from work, in a situation where he could be monitored on a regular basis and receive regular treatment. It also worries me greatly that EF has not been fully honest with myself, there is clearly something which he is withholding from myself, something he clearly feels that he is not yet ready to share, I just hope that this does not have a detrimental effect on his personal and private life. Dr Jenkins. … The journey home had almost been…calm. It was simply because in his mind he had come to a conclusion, his way had been so crowded he did not know where he was going, he couldn’t see the path before him, as it had already been laid at his feet. Someone however had now shown him the way, the path to enlightenment had been shown to him all he needed to do was clasp it with both hands and claim what had been offered to him. So why wasn’t it that easy? It was literally in front of him now. A t-shirt, that’s all it was, just a simple t-shirt that had been given to him, thousands of people in America owned the same t-shirt but this one was extremely different, this one was an invitation to something special, another world. The t-shirt was now off of the hanger from which it had previously been rested on as he allowed it to run through his hands, he allowed his hands to run across the logo on the front and closed his eyes, allowing his mind to wonder what could be, the opportunities that it would present him, no longer would he be looking over his shoulder, wondering whether or not he was going to be attacked anymore, instead he would be the one doing the attacking, he would be making his mark on the world. The world would need to stand up and take note, no longer would he be the man who was tossed to the wolves. He would be a member of the pack. A decision needed to be made and it needed to be made now. … … … So as he stood he stood there in front of the mirror staring at himself a wry smile on his face. ‘The Affliction A’ biohazard symbol staring back, the very same shirt that Lane Stevens had offered him last week at the very last Killzone at him he admit. He didn’t think being evil, could ever feel this good.
