The Family You Choose

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/07 Family is a strong word. Families are usually comprised of people you never get to choose, yet you feel you owe everything to. Some people are lucky enough to be allowed to choose their family, and some are chosen by their new families. As Gai sat at the table enjoying some freshly cooked ramen, he looked up and smiled at one of the two men that had taken him in and literally saved his life. Kisame Morishawa looked back and chuckled, as he woke his business partner from bed, already running late. “Why doesn’t Mr. Kobayoshi ever wake up early?” Gai inquired through a mouthful of noodles. “The same reason I’m always left to drive, because that’s just how he works, and you don’t try and change a system that works. Also, how many times has he told you to stop calling us that? Kisame and Itachi, no more of this Mister crap, ok?” Kisame reminded their guest. They had been trying to break Gai of that habit since he started staying with them. Family don’t address each other in that matter, they use the most comfortable name possible. After their discussion, Itachi lurched into the room, fully dressed, and half asleep. He walked to the opposite side of the kitchen and began to brew himself a fresh cup of green tea. The duo went through the usual morning rituals, gathering their belongings and heading out the door, off to work. Lately one of them had been staying home, to tend to their injured guest, but as his cuts and bruises seemed to be clearing up, Gai had convinced them both to leave, feeling like a burden on their lives. The last words shared amongst the three before the Rising Sons left were “Please stop worrying Mr. Kobayoshi, I promise I’ll be fine.” ~*~ There’s no worse feeling in the world than to open the door to your own home, and find complete and utter chaos. Furniture overturned, glass broken, plants killed, personal items discarded. The second worst feeling in the world is to then realize that somewhere in this mess somebody caused, you have a member of your family missing, wounded, or worse. Itachi and Kisame searched franticly, but to no avail. They met back in front of their door, both looking towards the other, hoping to find an answer. As they stared on, disappointed in themselves, an answer presented itself to the duo in the form of a harsh, demanding knock. As the Heroes of Japan opened their door, they saw a man standing with an envelope in his hands, the rain pouring down, compromising the integrity of the message in his hand. “I was told that you two might be able to use this.” The only words the man spoke, before handing the envelope off to Kisame, and walking away. If they gave awards for being ominous, this guy would’ve taken at least third. Kisame franticly opened the note, to find a disappointing lack of text on it. Only one line, one simple line. “5178 Orchid Lane”. The two looked at each other, grabbing their jackets, obviously knowing where their next destination was. ~*~ On nights like this, the rain was more deafening than any traffic, animal, or person could drown out. The rain was harsh and brutal, and in this case, it was also the only ally the Rising Sons had at the moment. They walked through the darkness of the side roads and alleys, being shrouded by the lack of lighting on Orchid Lane. This was not the place you wanted to be alone on a dark rainy night in Osaka. Luckily, for Itachi and Kisame, they weren’t alone. Itachi had been aware of their follower for a good two blocks, walking just slow enough that he could stay with them, but just fast enough so he didn’t suspect a thing. Itachi looked over to Kisame, who nodded back, letting him know they were both aware of the situation, and decided that here, in this dark secluded alley was as good a place as any to confront him. They both spun on their heels, looking the hooded man in the face, catch him so off guard that he stumbled backwards. “So, were you sent to take us out… or to simply alert everyone else when we arrived?” Itachi knew the people that were usually given these tailing jobs were easy to push around, and wasted no time cutting to the chase. “It’s…It’s not what you think… I just need help.” The man looked up at the pair he had been following, towering over him, ready to pounce at the drop of a hat. “Really now… why can’t you ask us like most people, during the day… and to our faces?” Kisame didn’t seem to buy into the man’s pleads for even a moment. “It’s not that simple… I can’t just walk wherever I want and talk to anybody I want during the day. I can’t do anything without his permission.” The man fought tears as he realized that if they didn’t help him… he had lost all hope. “Calm down. Take a breath.” Kisame was better at calming situations and people, which is ironic, since he’s the one with a short temper. “You said you needed his permission… who is he?” Itachi needed answers, as they were burning precious time here, when Gai needed them. “Hyamoto. My boss. My captor… the man you’re looking for. The man that sent me to watch you.” The man’s honesty was either an actual plea for help, or an overly-intelligent trap to lure them in. “Hyamoto is watching us? Why? What have we done to spark his interests?” Kisame was as concerned as his partner about having a big name on their trail. Hyuuga Hyamoto is Japan’s Al Capone. He is above the law, his crimes are known across the country, yet the police cannot touch him. He is untouchable. “I honestly don’t know, he had me start watching your house two weeks ago, to let him know when and where you were going. Today I relayed the message that you had both left for the day, and less than ten minutes later, they came in and destroyed everything, and took that American boy.” The man had regained his composure, fighting off his emotions, realizing now that he had help. Itachi paced in the alleyway, not knowing what to think. He looked back at Kisame who was trying to tend to the man pleading for their help. “We’ll help you get free; we’ll help all of you. All you have to do is your job. Report to Hyamoto, tell him we’re on our way. Tell him we’re coming for the Gaijin. Tell him he’s going to answer to us.” With those words, Kisame helped the man to his feet, and let him begin to walk off, towards their destination. They waited back, giving him ample time to get the message where it belonged. “Itachi, what are we going to do when we get there? He has more power than we can measure how can we even begin to imagine what we’ll have to deal with?” Kisame’s words of sensibility rang in Itachi’s ears. He was right. “Kisame, we’re going to go in and do what we always do, help those that need it, and teach Hyamoto that he may be invisible to the authorities, but he cannot escape destiny.” Itachi’s words filled his partner with confidence, as they turned and began their trek towards their Orchid Lane, towards their own destiny, towards their family.
