Terrorising the world!

10 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/06/08 Tap tap tap tap tap, this is the sound of a keyboard in the land of computers, it could be a tap dancing Ukrainian, but in this case, it is just the keyboard. We are looking at AJ, sitting there behind a computer screen, furiously tapping away and using his mouse as quickly as humanly possible, concentration etched into his weary face as he taps and clicks in this dimly lit room, take away food and soda bottles are strewn around like homeless people in an alleyway. Sameer flies into the room, slamming doors on the way through; he looks at AJ, sighing, and shakes his head. "How long have you been on that thing now?" He asks AJ in a kind of parental tone "Today?" comes the reply, without AJ looking up "Well, today, in general, you have been on it all week." "This is important" "Important?" "Yeah, it’s my latest plan to terrorise, I figure, there are so many people online to do terrorism stuff to, it helps us get exposure and I get to work from home, double whammy." "Terrorise people online? This is a first, how many people are you planning to do it to then?" "Couple of million, it’s all good." "Dare I ask, how?" "Well, I figured, if I go on World of warcraft, right, I can terrorise people that way." "World of Warcraft?" "Yeah" "Is that not the game which a bunch of kids make elves and dwarfs and then go round killing each other?" "That’s the one matey, I have managed to make me a night elf, and I have set up the Taliban guild, look." AJ spins his screen around to show Sameer the character onscreen, AJ types something and the character waves onscreen. "I called him AJ, he is like a level 120 warlock and he has a little demon thing, which I have called Sameer. We go round the whole of Azeroth ganking nubs on the pvp, I am that leet, they all go wtf omgz when I nuke ‘em and they end up having to spawn back at the GY after I cast my curse of weakness." Sameer is lost. "You do what now?" he says, puzzled. "Never mind, anyway, I have ultimate terror here, the level cap is actually 70, but, with me buying all of the shareholders of Blizzard a yacht, I have Games master rights, which means I can pretty much do whatever I want. I got all the imba skills and my druid pwns all." "Right. And this is what you have been doing since we left the arena at SoS?" AJ looks back at Sameer incredulously. "Of course, what else would I be doing?" Sameer sighs, walking over to the couch in the corner, picking up some documents from the coffee table on the way. "Well, you know what we talked about the other day, I only came back because you now serve a purpose again for the Taliban, and we need to get the plan together so we can announce it officially on the next temptation. If it was up to me, we wouldn’t use you, but you have exposure still, people tend to watch you – so you are still the best candidate, Allah help us all." "Oh right yeah, that thing. Well, I have worked on it a bit….kinda – I mentioned it to one of my guild buddies haxxor l33t uber spoogestain, he thought the idea pwned all." "Can you please stop talking like that?" "Like what?" "Pwns, what is that?" "It’s Leet speak man, all the cool kids do it wtf roflmao" "Roflmao?" "Roflmao" AJ looks at Sameer with a big grin, as if Sameer perfectly understood everything and it is all normal. Sameer just blinks a couple of times and tries to ignore it. "Ok, well I just hope you can plan this properly with me later, and switch off that infernal game." "Ok skip, will do. Want to see me do an elvish moonwalk." "No" "How about a tree dance? Bet you aint seen that?" "No" "Hmmm, ok, never mind then. I’ll come off now." AJ types into his chat window /afk and then switches off his monitor. He spins round quickly in his chair, finally facing Sameer. "Ok, so what is the plan?" "We announce it on Temptation, as agreed. You will have some support, you know, the usual, I expect a bit of backlash from the fans, but if you follow the cue cards – then everything will work out fine." "Announce, backlash, cue cards, fine. That makes sense, I am sure we will be fine anyway" "Maybe, you have to convince everyone, but if there is one thing you can do, Allah knows how, is actually charm people. The new world order is upon us AJ; we will be able to make a real difference to the world, starting this week on……" Sameer notices that AJ has turned back over to the computer, shaking his head." "What are you doing?" asks Sameer, slightly annoyed. "Oh, sorry – I just got attacked by a raid party of horde while I was sitting, they have been fighting me since you started talking, and they have me down to 80% health and have managed to leech 5% of my mana" "Does it matter?" "Do you want me to cyber terrorise the world of Warcraft or not?" "I am talking about how we are going to begin phase 1 of operation Khazballa, and you are just interested in what you are doing on a computer game?" "No, not at all, as I said – there are millions of people on here, if I can terrorise all of them, then it is well worth it. WoW terrorism FTW!" Sameer reaches around the back of the computer, and rips the power lead from the plug socket. The computer dies with a sudden spurt. This prompts AJ to look around with a scowl on his face. "HEY!" "Now pay attention AJ, you idiot. As I said, the Taliban still needs you, and you have a lot of making up to do. After the announcement on Temptation, you will need to be on your game at all times. Your image is everything, you need to be snappy, smart, witty and most of all charming. Do that, at all times, we will be fine." "Aint I always like that?" Says AJ sulkily. There is no response, other than a deep fixed stare from Sameer to AJ – which even AJ picks up as "If you say one more word out of turn I will rip out your throat." "What would help immensely is this. You go on and win this Heaven and Hell match thing then win the title. Do that and you will have a much better chance, a bigger profile and a greater influence?" "A greater influence eh? I don’t know if you know me, but I am Andre Jensen, richest guy on the planet" "All the money in the world, yet you still are the lowest paid on the roster and your colleagues treat you as if you are some sort of joke AJ. You have to get more focussed and you have to win that title." "We have two belts Sammy; we are champions of the world." AJ pats his belt, which funnily enough he is wearing, with his normal street clothes. It looks like he has taken to wearing it at all times now, oh well. "It doesn’t matter; universal champion is what you need. You do not need to be blasé about it." "Want me to buy it?" "No, and you can’t" "I can try" "People won’t accept it" "Why?" "You have to do it on your own merit" AJ exasperates, slouching down in his chair. "Ok, ok," AJ sighs these words "I suppose I will try it your way, I would prefer just to go to Smitty’s office tomorrow and pay him like a billion for me getting the title, but let’s do it your way. We can get started tomorrow, I have a headache and I want to go and solo Kara tonight." "You want to do what?" "Solo Kara, no one has ever done it before" "Not that I care, but sure, knock yourself out. Just don’t mess up again, because the Taliban are not ready to give you another chance if this gets messed up. We have high hopes to end this war started by the evil pigs within a year and the peace will be restored through Allah’s will" "Allah’s will! Sounds like a quest item! Where can I pick that up?" Sameer, trying to hide his complete contempt for AJ at the moment grits his teeth, picking up a lamp and getting ready to bash AJ over the back of the head with it. It takes all of the willpower he possesses for him to not do it, and he puts the lamp back before taking a deep deep breath. "It is worth it, I am at one with Allah" Sameer mutters below his breath. He recites a quick Arabic prayer and leaves the room before he actually does kill AJ. AJ already has his computer booted up again and he cracks open a can of soda, chugging it down, waiting for "something to happen in Stormwind"
