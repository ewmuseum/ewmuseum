Intermission.

14 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-14-07 What if you could live forever? What if you could exist beyond being? What if you had no beginning and no end? What if there was no such thing as ‘injury’ to you? What if you existed above everyone’s perception of reality? What if you were your own god? What if you never had to eat again? What if you never had to sleep again? What if you never had to fear death again? What if all you had to do was go back in time and kill one of your parents? Would you do it? --- "Please, pick up." My voice came out broken, sounding sore and raspy against the receiver. My fingers had fumbled with the quarters, dropping them to the dirty ground at least twice before they managed to push them into the payphone’s slot. As the dial tone rang incessantly, I tried to remember her number in the back of my head. My dead cell phone laid in the back of my vandalized car, which had been keyed to death by the very same girl I was now trying so desperately to get in touch with. It had been nearly a week now, and I had seen neither hide nor hair of Judith. "Pick up, pick up…" I managed to dial the number and now the phone rang as my body rocked back and forth, anxious and nervous. The air was thick with the smell of cow shit from the field next to this decrepit old gas station, somewhere between Greeneville and Chapel Hill. This payphone was ancient and pushed to the side of the station, out of view from any prying eyes. Graffiti littered the wall it was pushed against; everything from swastikas to phone numbers advertising a wide range of decadent sexual activities, for your pleasure. Above me, a flickering light hung lonely, attracting moths and mosquitoes. The interstate remained alive nearby, but everything here seemed frozen in time. Stuck in a time loop, forgotten and left to gather dust. "Pick up, pick up, c’mon, pick up…" Anticipation turned to annoyance, and annoyance turned to terrible realization as her voice came through the receiver by way of her voicemail, perky and inviting me to leave a message after the beep. "Goddammit!" Beep. I used the payphone like a Catholic uses a confessional. I said my apologies and asked for forgiveness, or at least some sort of closure. My words were buried beneath an avalanche of depression, tears running rivers down my cheeks, my whole body shaking from withdrawal. Withdrawal from her, withdrawal from drugs, withdrawal from the life I was coming to accept. I told her everything she could possibly want to hear. I said I’d change my ways. I said I’d be there for her, completely. I said I loved her. I said I loved our baby. I said it all, and received no reply. Not even an automated voice piping in to tell me the message was too long. Nothing. Just deafening silence. My words fell to hushed little whispers until they faded out with one last dying ‘please’… and then I hung up. Defeated and embarrassed. So it goes. I slid my hands into my pockets, and turned to retreat back to my car, to rejoin the traffic on the interstate, to keep driving with no one to talk to. The radio and my own bullshit loneliness would be only companions as the road stretched on and on and on, endless. I looked up from the ground, my face red and wet from the tears that that wreaked havoc across my cheeks… and there he was again. Standing silent and clad in black, buried in a sea of shadows, nothing visible besides that wide and familiar grin. The omen. The ghost. That had stalked me for days now. Nothing but silence stood between us and that grin only grew wider until it exploded into laughter, loud and stretching to the heavens above. "What the fuck do you want?!" I yelled, as he laughed and laughed. "You want to kill me? You here to murder me? Go right the fuck ahead and give your best shot!" Nothing but laughter. "Cmon! What are you waiting for?! Who the fuck are you, anyway?!" He stifled his amusement, his laughter dwindling down to occasional giggles underneath his breath, until his voice came out, old and just as familiar as the grin stretching across his wrinkled face. He said, simply, "Do you believe in time travel, Mr. Sterling?" --- The philosophy of time travel is a complicated subject that most people cannot even begin to comprehend. There are several different theories on the matter, all with their own array of unfounded points. Most people think of time travel as an impossibility due to the so-called ‘Grandfather Paradox’. Simply put, if you go back in time and murder your own grandfather, you will cease to exist. Not only this, but due to another theory known as the ‘Butterfly Effect’, this one tear in the past will bring about more significant changes in the present, as well as the future. In other words, one mistake in the past will bring about a definite end to the present. One tiny fuck-up will fuck up everything. Therefore, if time travel was indeed a possibility, it would be far too dangerous to attempt because you could destroy the future as we know it. But this begs the question, if one person went back and reworked the past, how would the rest of us know? Would anyone even care? You could change everything, but what would it matter to us? Who’s to say that time doesn’t get reshuffled all the time as it is? The present we know is the present we know. How would we know that it changed at all? The reality you know today could be entirely different than the reality you knew yesterday, but you would never know this, now would you? However, simply put, this way of thinking is wrong. First, it’s best to think of time as a book as opposed to a running river. Instead of flowing on and on forever, think of time as having a definite beginning, a definite middle, and a definite end, already written out and completed. Time travel would be just like dropping that book, losing your place, and picking up from a different page than where you left off. Do you follow? Second, realize that changing something in the past will affect the future, but only to the people involved in this change. If you were to marry a girl in America in the past, it won’t affect the whole world. The idea that a single butterfly can change the course of history is idiotic at best. Am I going too fast? Third, don’t worry about the idea of different timelines and planes of existence and etc. All that matters is your own timeline, your own dimension, your own reality. To talk about the idea of all the different possibilities and different existences would be mind boggling. The amount of possibilities and combinations are endless. Don’t worry about anything other than your own existence. Nothing matters except you. However, in case you were curious, one theory states that the second you change something in the past, that change splinters the single flow of reality into two parallel branches. For example, if you killed your ancestor, reality would fork into two paths: one reality in which you continued to be born and your ancestor did not die, and one branch in which your ancestor died and you would never be conceived. But none of this matters. Honest. You’re not confused, are you? Fourth, let’s pretend you murder your mother or father in the past, before you’re born. You would be without beginning and without end. Without ever being born, you would never have to worry about dying, would you? You wouldn’t have to sleep. You wouldn’t have to eat. You could do whatever you want. In simple terms, you would be a god. You would live outside the confines of reality. You would be suspended outside of the linear movement of time that human beings experience. Simply put, you would not be subject to the natural processes of hunger, exhaustion, and decay. You would live by your own laws of existence, and no one else’s. It’s the opposite of what we see throughout mythology. Gods have created themselves as mortals in every religion by bearing children with mortal women. A simple seduction brings about the result of the mortal offspring. The divine made flesh. The infinite made finite. It’s when you cross mythology that the Grandfather Paradox becomes reversed. Instead of the divine put to flesh, flesh may be put to the divine. You severe your origin and you make yourself a god. Did you ever wonder how Zeus became such a force to be reckoned with after overthrowing his own father? Are you getting this? All I’m saying is, what if time isn’t the fragile butterfly wing that everyone has perceived it to be for so long? What if time is a chain link fence that you can hardly fuck up? And even if you did fuck it, ten thousand times, what would it matter? Who would know? The present is the present. No one will ask any questions. We get what we get. You can do whatever you want. --- The smell of his cologne mixed with the aroma of the cow-shit fields nearby and the burnt rubber of the interstate to create a terrible assault on the senses. It was enough to make my eyes water as my nose felt desperate to have it’s nostrils plugged up. It was enough to make me cough before my words came out, growing angrier, growing to a scream that rose above and beyond the honking horns and speeding vehicles. I yelled, "What the fuck are you talking about?!", as though I would get a reply that would make sense. His voice came out calm, almost soothing, doused in a sort of undeniable ‘wisdom’. He said, simply, "Answer me. Do you believe in time travel?" "Fuck no, I don’t believe in time travel!" I sneered, turning away and beginning to walk for my car, trying hard not to look or sound as shaken up by this as I actually was. Act tough. Be a man. Show no fear. All that nonsense. But I can honestly say, I’ve never been more afraid of a man in my entire life, and I can’t even explain why. I yelled back to him, loud enough that the clerk in the gas station could probably hear me and wonder what was going on. "Now, I suggest you leave me the hell alone and stay far, far away from me! Otherwise, I’ll be calling the cops to get your ass thrown in jail!" Grin. He kept grinning wide behind me as his footsteps fell on the asphalt, mimicking my own as he followed me toward my car. He said, "Do you think you scare me, Sean? Am I supposed to be intimidated by you? You’re nothing more than a weak man who hides behind tough mask, am I right?" Grin. Wider and wider. "Of course I’m right. I know you better than anybody, after all." "Listen man, I don’t know who the fuck you are and I don’t care who the fuck you are, but this is the last warning I’m giving you. I’ve got a gun in the glove compartment and I’m not scared to use it for self defense. In other words, if you don’t stay the fuck away from me, I’m going to shove a bullet down your throat." I hid behind a mask of threats. He didn’t bother to wear a mask, other than the shadows that hid his face even in the pale light at the front of the gas station. I walked to my door and opened it wide, climbing in quick, and locking the door. He came to stand beside the car, looking into the window as I turned the key, trying to turn on the ignition. Even now, he spoke calm, just loud enough for me to hear through the glass. "I’ll have you know, Sean, that time travel is very real." I turned the key hard. "I wouldn’t be here otherwise." The engine tried it’s best to start up. "Don’t you know who I am?" I could feel the sweat form on my forehead. "I’m you." The car backed up as quick as I could put it in reverse, leaving skid marks on the dirty asphalt in my wake. I can't explain why I was so horrified by the words of some psycho. I can't explain why I panicked. I can't explain a goddamn thing. What I can explain is that I sped as fast as I could for the interstate, leaving him standing in front of the gas station, grinning that goddamn grin. --- Now, imagine some of the things you could do if time travel were a possibility. By going back in time, you would be living alongside history, knowing the news would be since you’ve already lived it once before. Memorize enough lottery numbers or which horse won what races and you can be a millionaire, no sweat. You could invent the world’s most revolutionary products, if you knew how. You could have enough money to make Bill fucking Gates blush. All I’m saying is, what if all of our leaders, all of our pop culture icons, all the people that have made significant changes in history, were just people with the means of traveling back in time? What if Charles Manson killed those people to avert some tragic disaster he knew would happen otherwise? What if The Beatles knew rock’n’roll would be the wave of the future and simply traveled back to capitalize on it’s future popularity? What if Jesus was just some dumb kid who managed to go back and use his knowledge of the future to make himself one of the most significant figures in all of history? If this is making no sense to you, go ahead and give up now, because it never will. Pretend you’ve never heard this. Go back to your daily life and forget you ever heard anything about time travel. Move along, nothing to see here, etc. Time travel doesn’t exist anyway, right? In a world where billions of people believe their deity conceived a mortal child with a virgin human, it‘s amazing how little imagination most people display. --- His words had sent a cold chill down my spine as strange thoughts began to pulse and weave in the back of my head, as curious as they were confused. That smell of cow shit, burnt rubber, and that goddamn cologne still piled itself up in my nostrils as I pulled into Chapel Hill, the radio turned up as loud as it could possibly go in a thinly veiled attempt to keep myself from thinking into things too much. That guy was just some stalker, right? Fame brings the crazies out in droves, yeah? Just call the police and keep a cautious eye out for that weirdo when I’m in public, uh-huh? Nothing to worry about. Stay cool. Stop panicking. Everything is fine. The sun was rising over the Carolina hills, bright and as powerful as a chorus of angels. My hands felt sore from gripping the steering wheel so tight. With heavy eyelids and dried sweat covering my skin, I couldn’t wait to get to the hotel, to get to my room, and just rest. Fuck my worries and fuck my problems straight to hell. All I wanted to do was collapse in a soft bed and sleep forever. As soon as I hit the pillow, my eyes shut tight, and I drifted into a deep, deep sleep… --- I dreamt of skies on fire, clouds burning with twisting spires made of stone reaching up from the ground. The streets filled with debris and a black hole hovering in the sky where the sun once made it’s place. Buildings fell like dominoes as the cadavers of crushed cars laid dormant in the streets. A dead American flag unfurled in the wind, gyrating to the will of the breeze, with tears all along the edges. Ashes and dust everywhere. The sewers and streets collide as one, muddied with ancient rain and the blood from a thousand lonely suicides. Planes crashing like missles. Decay dripping off of every surface. An endless stretch of city turned to waste, rubble. Reeking of death and havoc and heartbreak. Strange times. Strange days. Billboards leered, splintering up like antennas held to heaven. Forests of trees turned to black burnt skeletons. Every house a tombstone for a dead family. No life. Nothing. Solitude. Silence, except the wind breathing hard against my skin. A skyline on fire, the sky washed orange and tattered. Thunder, tornadoes, terror, and me. And, of course, him. Standing amidst the rubble in the cracked streets. Dark as a shadow, a terrible omen put to flesh. Apparently, my future self. Apparently. It went like this: We rushed toward eachother, his laughter still towering over me even in this dream. Fists collide with skin, anger unfolds in the streets, thunder cracks and lightning flickers, scattering those dark clouds in the skies. Roaches and rats fled beneath our stomping feet, blood dripping from both our faces until all his shadows dispersed and I saw myself staring back at me, bleeding and grinning and laugh-laugh-laughing. The skies gave way and opened wide as the pavement cracked and quaked, crumbling. The last thing I saw was him ascending like an angel to the heavens above, bapitized in bright light like Jesus fucking Christ, as I fell screaming into the abyss, into oblivion, into eternity, only to wake up in a cold sweat, tangled in my bedhseets, eyes wide, the afternoon's sunlight shining in through the open window, my phone ringing loud on my bedside table. --- Breathing heavy, I spoke into the phone, a quiet ‘hello?’ only to find the front desk clerk on the other line bantering on and on about how some girl is downstairs to see me. He said, she’s crying. He said, her mascara is running trails down her cheeks. He said, this looks serious. He said, she’s desperate. He said, she wants to speak to me. The next voice I heard was Judith’s, soft and fragile. Begging, pleading, apologizing. Hormones spinning her dizzy until it finally explodes into a flood of stuttering "I’m sorry"s and broken-voiced "I love you"s. So I told her I love her too. I told her it would be okay. I told her my room number. I told her to just knock and I'd come to the door. I told her I was sorry too. Blah, blah, blah. What did you fucking expect me to do?
