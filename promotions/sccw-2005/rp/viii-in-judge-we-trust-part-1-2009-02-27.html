VIII (In Judge We Trust, Part 1)

27 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/27/2009 'I don't trust him. We're friends.' - Bertolt Brecht VIII Ash Journal Entry, 01/03/2009: A few quiet days have passed since the incidents on New Years. This city has changed completely since the criminal super group Death was eliminated from being a threat to the city. The police scanners have been quiet, which is surprising. After the death of Rick Roll, the city has been quiet. Without his goons running around, it’s been an easy job cleaning up the streets for me and the rest of the Rat Pack. However the skies have remained gloomy and cloud covered so my optimism is weak. I’m not Obama. I don’t see a happy ending at the end of this rainbow. After I killed Rick Roll, someone took his mask. Right now I’m expecting someone to blackmail me or something with it. Probably some scumbag wanting money. Fucking criminal scum still is the running this city. That much hasn’t changed. What has changed is that the top criminals are running scared or not doing anything because of the Rat Pack, the team of heroes I am a part of. We all get along so well. It’s been a tad boring, actually. However the fact that I’m dating Amanda is making up for it. Nobody knows that we’re a couple except for the Rat Pack. She’s the Cat Lady but only me and her know our secret identities. She knows that I’m artist Ash Plato, which no one else knows except for my brother. I love her, I really do. My personal life is so much better now. The only bad news is that the criminals Hamburglar and Pilgrim are still on the loose but we’ll get them soon enough. Things couldn’t be going any better. I can’t wait to see what the future has in store. * * * 01/04/2009 6:00 pm The Rat Pack is meeting in the apartment of the Astronomer, which doubles as our base. It started peacefully until things quickly got out of control. The team is starting to turn into squads almost like the movie platoon. Half of the team, my ‘sidekick’ Bailiff, my girlfriend the black clad Cat Lady, the giant camouflaged Abrams, the homeless hero Vagrant, and myself are on one side. The other half of the team, the yellow and red caped Captain Velocity, the black leather clad Mustang, Halloween costumed Lady Bug, and the aquamarine costumed and blue caped Astronomer, who is on their side since he’s the peacemaker in the group. They are the pro-life element of the team. The debate has been raging for about an hour now and it isn’t going to get any better. Right now the main debaters are myself and Captain Velocity. we’re practically nose to nose, about to come to blows. "You just don’t want to make the crucial choice because you have no balls," I say to him after hearing another one of his ‘sermons.’ This clearly annoys him. I can tell by the furled wrinkles on his forehead. "Nobody should die at someone else’s hands," Captain Velocity says in a snobby tone. "True but if you’ve got a wild eyed maniac pointing a gun at you, you do what you can to survive. Otherwise you’re dead." "But life should be PRESERVED, not taken away. No matter what the circumstances, God said that thou shall not kill. I agree with that sentiment. Life is the most precious commodity there is." This makes me raise an eyebrow after hearing it. "I thought water was the most precious commodity." "Don’t be a smart ass." "WELL THIS ISN’T SUNDAY SCHOOL! God, Jesus, and none of that garbage has any place in crime fighting. If you want to convert criminals to your cult of righteousness, then that’s your deal. Mine is making them pay for their crimes. That’s how it should be regardless of how it is done." "Death is NOT an option, Judge." The way he says this pisses me off to no end. "In the most heinous of cases IT IS a viable option." "You need to ask yourself this question. What would Jesus do?" "Do be philandering me with your religious ideologies, Captain. Since you seem to not be getting what the hell I’m saying, let me give you this scenario. Your girlfriend, wife, or significant other is raped, mutilated, and left for dead. The criminal rat bastard who did it is in your sights. You catch him. He did horrible things to the person you love the most in this world and you know he’s going to do it to somebody else if he is not dealt with. Now do you let this person live or do you give him the justice he deserves?" "Well, I…I would let him live." This guy is so full of shit and I can’t myself back from telling him so. "You are such a liar!" "I am not! I am a man of God." "What a cop out that is," Abrams growls out. "You’re one to talk," Mustang snarls. "You’re just siding with Judge because you’re a big fan of his and it’s deluded your mind." "No, I agree with him because he makes sense to me unlike you and the Bible Brigade over there." "I’m not a Christian," Lady Bug responds. "But lethal methods are not the answer to this issue." I then reply to all of this in my own special way. "Yes they are. See you think this is just some Sunday school bible lesson. The decisions we make lead to consequences. If the criminals know we are lax with our methods and have no balls, then they’ll just continue doing what they’re doing. That’s why I prefer lethal methods. We need to scare the shit out of these assholes so they don’t commit crimes! Just giving them warnings and letting the police deal with them isn’t going to solve matters. It’ll just lead to more of the same. The last town this city needs is more of the same. We need to get law and order here and the only way to do that is to make the criminals fear us. Lethal methods are our best option for achieving that. That‘s the best option." "Judge, killing people will only make things worse," Mustang replies. "Captain Velocity is right. We need to preserve life instead of taking it away." "See?" Captain Velocity says in a self-righteous tone. "I’m not the only one who thinks that. Life is the most precious thing and if we take it away, does it make us any different than the criminals we encounter? I think not. Killing is the worst crime any person can commit. You do it on a regular basis and it makes us look bad." "You weren’t saying that when the Rat Pack first formed when you guys were originally my fan club." "What are you saying, Judge?" he asks/ "I’m saying that I’m the best. You modeled yourself after ME, not anybody else." "So? What the hell does that really mean?" "It means in order to be the best, you have to model yourself after the best? And who’s the best?" This really pisses off Velocity, which makes me happy. "You asshole." I quickly respond to this statement in a very Shakespearean way. "Look whose talking, jackass." Velocity and I charge at each other, throwing some punches as the rest of the team breaks it up. The Astronomer tries to play peacemaker again. "Guys, calm down," the Astronomer says, motioning to both Captain Velocity and myself. "We need to stay united as a team." "RELAX! Now let’s have cooler heads prevail. Let us go out on our patrols tonight and we can discuss this later." "Cooler heads won’t prevail," I reply before turning toward the door to get the hell out of here. "This will just keep happening until the team splits in two." "Are you nuts, Judge?" the Astronomer asks before I stop to turn around and respond to his question. "No, I’m not. Two differing philosophies with half of the team following one and the other half following the other. It’s just not going to last." "The government works the same way and it’s still around." "But look at what partisan bickering did to this country. It’s in shambles. I don’t want this city to go down the same route." The group finally calms down. We were about to come to blows. Well, at least me and Captain Velocity were. That asshole doesn’t know what he’s doing. Mustang instigating things didn’t help matters either. He’s another asshole. More and more I’m starting to think this team is falling apart. I’m the best and even I can’t save this sinking ship. * * * 10:17 pm The call came in a half hour ago on the police scanner. Downtown there’s a robber whose killed the store clerk of the convenience store he had robbed. Now the robber is on the loose and the Rat Pack has been called in to deal with the situation and catch the criminal. We quickly sped over to the scene of the crime off of Hacienda and Eastern in our vehicles. Cat Lady rode with me as Abrams drove himself in his giant Hummer, and the rest of the team rode in the Astronomer’s white Chevy Tahoe. After arriving in the area where the events transpired, we soon came across two police cars. Gunshots fired a few yards in front of us inside a sleazy looking apartment complex. One whizzes by my head as I get out of my car after parking it. I check for blood as Cat Lady gets out of the passenger side. She looks over to see if I’m hit or bleeding. "ARE YOU OKAY?!" she exclaims as she runs over and starts looking at my head, checking for any damage. I nod my head. "Yeah, I’m okay," I reply, a bit rattled but more pissed off than anything else. Whomever shot me just signed their own death warrant. The rest of the Rat Pack parks and then runs over to where Cat Lady and I are standing, which is right next to my car. "What’s going on?" the Astronomer asks, a bit concerned. "Some fucker just tried to shoot us," I say with a growl. "He’d better hope I don’t find him…" Captain Velocity and his buddy Mustang both shoot me looks. Honestly, I could give a fuck less. "So you’re just going to channel Rick Roll and go kill him?" Captain Velocity says in a condescending tone. "That’s just not ethical." I walk over and get in his face. He seems intimidated which doesn’t surprise me at all. "WHO ARE YOU TO SAY WHAT’S ETHICAL AND WHAT’S NOT? I JUST GOT SHOT AT! What ethics are there in getting shot at?" The rest of the team quickly breaks us up before there is a fight between the three of us. Pushing some of them away, I head in the directions of the gunfire as they start discussing things back on the street behind me. I could care less about having a moral debate. Some maniac is on the loose with a gun and all they seem to give a shit about is life or death. All I know about life and death is that someone is going to die rather than live if we don’t do something right now. Running toward the apartments, the first thing I see is an officer down. More gunshots. Screaming in the distance. Blood curdling screaming, not that fake shit from the horror movies. This gets my blood pumping. Shit’s hitting the fan, which makes this my kind of night. Going over to the officer who is down, I lean down upon reaching him. He is wounded in the leg by a single gunshot wound. Nobody shoots a cop in my town and lives to tell about it. I motion in the direction of the gunshots. "Where’d the perp go?" I ask as the cop groans out in pain. "He went in the alleyway behind the apartments," he tells me with a wheeze. Must’ve hit a nerve in his leg or something the way he’s acting. "My partner and two other officers went after him. Get this guy, Judge." I nod my head and rise to my feet before going into a full run. Bailiff and Cat Lady are soon behind me running as well. "What happened back there?" I ask as we run through the apartment buildings toward the robber who is somewhere in the distance. "Did Captain Anal and his horse Pokey get a bug up their ass again or something?" Cat Lady lets out a giggle after hearing that. "By Pokey you mean Mustang?" she asks as I nod my head. "Damn right. Mustang is that clay horse from Gumby." "Well, in that case, yes." "They think you’re dangerous to society and to the group," Bailiff butts in. I may have had my doubts about the kid when he first wanted be my sidekick, but he’s alright. He also seems to be one of the ones who thinks like I do, which is what we need more of in the team. "Oh really?" I ask with a grin. "So what happened?" "They bitched and moaned before we all decided to split up and find the criminal," Cat Lady replies. This makes me groan out a little. Those two idiots are going to be the death of the Rat Pack. More gunshots in the distance, only this time they are close by. Looking forward the first thing I notice is some people laying on the ground ducking for cover. However, upon closer inspection, the people on the ground aren’t moving. They’re also wearing police uniforms. Must be the other officers who went after the robber. This pisses me off to no end. Cat Lady and Bailiff soon are up where I’m at. They notice the bodies quicker than I did. "They dead?" Bailiff asks as I nod my head. "Yeah, they’re dead," I reply with a growl. "And this fucker is going down." "JUDGE, WAIT!" Cat Lady exclaims but I am hearing none of it. I’m on a mission now. This robber is a cop killer. He won’t stop until somebody takes him down and sends him to Hell with all the rest of the murdering scum that infests the streets of my city. I get up and run full speed down the alleyway behind the apartment complex. More gunshots are fired in the distance in my direction. Ducking and dodging out of the way, I see a shadow in the distance reloading a gun. Now is my chance to take him down. As he readies to fire again, I get close enough to him to grab his arm and twist his arm in a fashion that takes him down to the ground. After doing this I stomp repeatedly on his hand and wrist as he screams out in pain. He drops the gun which I quickly kick away before pulling out some PlexiCuffs and handcuffing him with them. Now he’s sobbing and crying like a little child who didn’t get their way. At this point, I pull out my gun which makes him whimper even more. It’s judging time and he knows it. "Time to be sentenced…" I mutter with a certain sense of joy knowing full well what the decision is before I even pulled my gun out. "And for your crimes, which include robbery with a deadly weapon, assault with a deadly weapon, multiple police homicides, murder, theft, and my attempted murder, you are hereby sentenced to DEATH." He begins to scream out in terror as I point the gun at his head. "NO! NO! NO!" he exclaims before I pull the trigger and deal him the killing blow. The gun molds to my hand almost like an extension of it. My bones rattle after firing the weapon. Echoes of the gunfire can be heard throughout the area. Looking down at his body, I hear footsteps coming up behind me. Turning around, I see Captain Velocity shaking his head. "You maniac," he mutters out, his ugly yellow and red costume stinking up the night. He starts doing his usual religious pro-life bullshit as he motions toward the dead body laying in front of me. I don’t trust him one bit, either. He’s a snake in a yellow suit. "WHY DID YOU HAVE TO SHOOT HIM JUDGE?! You know we don’t participate in street violence like the criminals we go up against. We are here to preserve life, not take it away." This Captain Velocity is becoming a bit too self righteous for my taste. His whole pro-life stance for some reason makes him think he is better than any of us who are pro-death in the group. Finally I decide to speak up due to the fact that this guy is annoying me beyond belief. He sounds like one of those radical activists or something. "Ok, that’s it," I say as I interject myself into the situation. "I’m sick of you belittling everyone who doesn’t share your opinion. You’re like one of these leftist political analysts on the television." "And what the hell does that mean?" he asks in a pissy tone. "It means stop being an asshole." That comment really pisses him off. He looks constipated, which is how you can tell he’s really pissed. "Why the hell am I being an asshole?" Just then the rest of the Rat Pack arrives just in time for the show. At this point I ignore their arrival and go off on Velocity. All of the anger built up inside of me explodes and I unleash my wrath on him. "Your self righteous, pious attitude is going to get us all killed. Let me tell you something. These criminals don’t give a shit if you’re in a costume or not. They don’t care that you save cats from trees or walk old ladies across the street. They don’t care. They will fucking kill you the first chance they get. You understand me, Captain? Criminals aren’t like they are in cartoons and comic books, They will fucking shoot your ass if you try any shit with them. Get that through your big fat fucking head." "Hey Judge," Astronomer says in a calming tone as if it’s supposed to calm me down. "Calm down." "ARE YOU SAYING I’M DELUSIONAL?" Captain Velocity yells at me with his little squeaky and annoying voice. He sounds like a fucking teenager. "Yes I am," I growl back. "And it’s because you are a delusional idiot. Your pro-life stance isn’t going to get you anywhere. It’s just going to get you and probably some of us killed." "Life preserves life, killing does not preserve fight. But oh yeah, that’s right. You don’t give a shit do you?" "You’re the one who doesn’t seem to give a shit Mr. High and Mighty. Do you actually think a criminal is going to be scared of you if he knows that you’re just going to give him a slap on the wrist like some little fucking kid? Hell no! He’s going to be like ‘Fuck this mother fucker!’ and bust a cap in your ass because he knows you’re unarmed." Captain Velocity gives me an incredulous look like he has no idea what the hell I’m talking about. "You’re crazy. You know that? You’re crazy! A slap on the wrist is better than killing the criminal. We’re saving people to preserve lives, not take them away." "THESE CRIMINALS NEED A SWIFT KICK IN THE ASS IN ORDER FOR THEM TO STOP!" "YOU’RE A CRIMINAL JUST LIKE THEM JUDGE! A CRIMINAL!" "Guys! KNOCK IT OFF!" Cat Lady exclaims trying to regain order as the police arrive on the scene. "We need to stop fighting each other and think about how we’re going to take on the criminals. Instead of fighting each other, we need to be fighting for the people." After she says this, cooler heads prevail. I decide that it’s a better idea to fight as a team than against each other. Looking over at that asshole Captain Velocity, he has the same look on his face that I do right now. He may be a self righteous and pretentious asshole, but at least he has some common sense left in that brain of his. "Good," the Astronomer says with a smile. "Now let’s put all of this behind us and start fresh tomorrow. Saving the city is what we’re here to do." As the police cordon off the scene of the crime, the group disbands and start talking to various officers at the scene probably to avoid any more arguments. However, I know for a fact the Rat Pack is falling apart and I only have myself to blame. I am the best, but even I can’t save the team.
