The perfect belt

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9th 2007 'Ooohhhh, glitter glue !' I said with a big grin on my face, as I reached out and grabbed the tubes off the shelf. Gold, silver, red, it was all there, which only made me smile harder as I dropped them into my shopping basket. After a few moments of thinking forwards to what I was going to do with all this stuff, I turned my head and continued down the aisle of the little party store near my current hotel room. This was going to be great, I was in just the right place for what I needed. Even more so now that I had the glitter glue. The FUSION Title belt was going to look better than ever, a necessity in my quest to have it recognised. All I needed now was some… "Tinsel! Tinsel!" I screamed. I raised my free arm into the air as if celebrating a victory, then did a little imitation of a crowd cheering with the back of my throat, before reaching out and grabbing all the Christmas tree tinsel I could with one hand. I dropped it all into the basket, and after performing another victory act, I turned back towards the check out, and strolled down the aisle towards it. "Wow, that’s a lot of decorations you’ve got there! Getting ready for Christmas early I see?" I looked up towards the source of the voice, to see a small grey haired man with thick glasses looking up at me from behind the check out with a fake grin on his face. "Nope, I replied, this isn’t for Christmas silly…" He looked at me, confused, in my basket I had everything you can possibly imagine putting on a Christmas tree, from tinsel to balls going through the flashing lights. "For some kind of surprise then?" he asked, a little intrigued. "Indeedo speedo my friend, it’s for a surprise." I replied politely. "What kind, a birthday?" "Well if I told you what kind of a surprise would that be?" This rhetorical question was asked in a colder tone that just before. He had clearly crossed the line when he tried to find out what I had planned. "Now sir, I continued, if you could please just tell me how much I owe you, I will be on my way…" I accompanied this sentence with an evil stare, which however didn’t seem to phase him much. He just shrugged as he started to pull the articles out of my basket and scan them. As he did I continued to think about what I was going to do with all this stuff… No one would be able to ignore the FUSION World Heavyweight Championship once I got through with the belt. "87 dollars and 30 cents please…" The little man asked. Although clearly a ridiculous amount of money to spend on Christmas decorations for a title belt, I was so happy and anticipating the final look so much that I didn’t hesitate in handing over my credit card. Only a few moments later he handed it back to me with almost a glimmer in his eye. "Thank you sir, he said, and good luck with the surprise…" Appreciating his encouragements, I discreetly gave him a wink as if to say "don’t worry about it, it’ll be awesome thanks to you bud", well in fact I said that very same sentence to him after the wink, which for some reason he seemed to take as a joke, as he started to giggle. Choosing to ignore this, I nodded as a thank you, before picking up my back of props, and walking out through the small door at the front into the street. Luckily my hotel was just opposite, so I rushed across the street all happy with myself, and in a hurry to get to my room and start my art. A few minutes later I was in the elevator going up to the fourth floor towards my room. Of course once I got there I tried my key, I remembered that my room was in fact on the second floor, not the fourth, so I re-entered the lift. Finally on the second floor, my key worked this time, and I entered my hotel room in a rush, throwing my back of props onto the bed and running over to the cupboard where I left the FUSION Title belt. In my rush however, I opened the cupboard a little to quickly and the door collided violently with my forehead, causing me to fall to the carpeted floor like a sac of potatoes. A couple of minutes later, my vision began to un-blur and I got slowly back up to my feet. As I stood up, there in front of me, next to the little blood stain on the cupboard door, was my FUSION Title belt in all its glory… only a taste of what it would be like in half an hour. I pulled the belt off the shelf, and after a little cuddle with it, I threw it onto the bed with the decorations. I sat on the edge of the bed, grabbing the belt in one hand, and the big tube of red glitter glue in the other. I bit off the top, and although I swallowed what was probably a dangerous amount of the glue in my rush, I started to apply it to the leather belt. However, before I could really get started on the FUSION logo, I was distracted by a faint beeping sound. I looked around the room in panic, only to realise the sound was coming from my laptop on the small table next to me. The top of the glitter glue tube dropped from my mouth, as I turned toward the computer and turned on the screen, revealing a blinking text saying "You have 1 new message". "Awesome!" I cried as I raised my arms into the air, letting go of the red glitter glue tube, causing it to fly all over the hotel room wall. It didn’t really matter though, because Fred Fusion had a message on his laptop. I immediately clicked on the icon, and up popped a text box with email in it. "FUSE Booking" was the title of the mail, and as I read down, I noticed something familiar: Fred Fusion. After a few seconds of concentration, I realised why it was familiar. It was because it was in fact my name. I read what was around it. "Livewire title match against Cyrus Raynes…" I read out loud. Although I didn’t understand the part about rain, I was intrigued by the little picture just above the text. A screenshot of the Livewire title. "Wow…" I muttered as I ran my fingers across the screen. It was pretty, very pretty. I raised my hand in which I was holding the FUSION Title. I held it up in front of the screen next to the screen shot, and in turn looked at both belts. As unbelievable as it was, there was something about the Livewire title that was more than the FUSION Title. It couldn’t be all that gold, since the FUSION belt has its own chunk of gold… but it was just so pretty. I looked over towards the bed where the bag of Christmas decorations. I stared at the tinsel and glitter glue for a few moments, before looking back at the screen with the Livewire Title picture. I then reached out and grabbed a little bit of green tinsel, and like I did with the FUSION Title, I held it up against the screen next to the shot of the Livewire title. The green seemed to marry itself so well with the shiny belt. The colours just linked together in harmony, making the belt look even more beautiful. I then grabbed the purple glitter pen from the bag, popped off the lid, and brought the tip towards the screen. I squeezed the tube ever so slightly, and started to draw on my laptop screen. After about thirty seconds, I threw the purple glitter pen to one side, again spraying glitter all over the hotel room wall, and moved back a little for a better look. F U S I O N The purple glimmer of the words shining from the light of the computer screen mixed in with the shine of the Livewire Title belt in what was a perfect combination of colour and style. No matter how much love I had for my original FUSION Title, I was just overwhelmed with the beauty of this one. This title belt was… "Perfect."
