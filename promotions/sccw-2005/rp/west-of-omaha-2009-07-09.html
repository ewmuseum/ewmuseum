Phillip Kennedy raised the chained arm to his face, and took a deep breath. The unpleasant metallic tang, similar to that of the scent of his own blood, assaulted his nostrils. He could practically taste the sea and steel on his tongue if he closed his eyes. No, Phillip Kennedy would never challenge Alexandra Pierce at her own game. The police would never be involved. He would never ask Kathryn or Lauren how to engage in her with guns or knives or poisons. He didn't need to, for he had realized the truth that the Spider in the Web had long ago. One could achieve a great deal of vengeance in the wrestling ring, where bones could be broken, flesh cut and torn, and livelihoods ended all within the questionable rules of the sport of kings. In a few hours, he would do battle with her for the second time. The first had been dubbed the Collision in College Park by fans. That had been a battle of respect. At least, he'd respected her back then. Looking back, he doubted she'd had the same respect for him. This second clash, this Fight at the Fair, was occurring under entirely different circumstances. The creature that was Desade had been laid bare to him. A woman with no morals, no scruples. A human being that made him look like the offspring of a choir boy and Mother Theresa. A knock came at the door, and he remembered where he was: backstage at Temptation at the Fairgrounds, in a locker room. The knock meant only one thing, the same thing it meant in New Mexico or in New Dehli: bell time in five minutes. The knock jarred him back to the immediacy of his task. Desade was a monolithic figure in Sin City, unpinned and unsubmitted since her arrival. If he was successful tonight -- and like Hell was he going to be anything else -- he'd be the first man to truly blemish the sparkling record that the hideous creature beneath wore as a badge of honor. It was time for him to go. He'd arrived at the fairgrounds in his battle attire, black tights with his name on the back in neon orange and pink, matching boots, and the same t-shirt that he'd worn the first time he did battle with Alexandra Pierce: one depicting a boot hovering above a fat, bloated spider, perched cluelessly in its web. The imagery had motivated him then. It would motivate him now. Opening the door, the Las Vegas Lariat stepped outside, and found a microphone thrust in his face. 'Jared Davis, from Primetime Central,' the microphone's owner said, mouth moving a mile a minute. 'Any thoughts before you go one on one with Desade for a spot in the Elite Championship match at Colossus VI?' 'I'd like,' Phillip found himself saying, the words coming from his mouth before he even really knew what he wanted to say, 'to dedicate tonight's match to Lance Marshall. To the BloodAngel, Edward Lambert. To Los Luchadores. To Jonathan Rhine. To Lindsay Troy. To each and every wrestler who Alexandra Pierce has ever fucked over, in ways big and small.' In his mind, the words 'To all of Alexandra Pierce's victims' were said, but went unspoken. 'To the fans of SCCW, PRIME, and PTC in general,' he continued. 'To every single person who has ever bought a ticket to a wrestling event or ordered a pay per view in the hopes of seeing Desade's shoulders pinned to the canvas, or forced to submit, and not gotten that wish. I dedicate tonight's #1 contendership match to all of you.' Kathryn Shaw. David Walter Smith. Lauren Fox. Reginald Victor Lovecraft. The people who had helped him get this far, helped make him the wrestler he was today on this stage with the chance to do what so many craved yet none had achieved. 'Because tonight,' Phillip Benjamin Kennedy growled, glaring into the camera, into the computer screens of fans watching on PrimetimeCentral.net, 'Alexandra Pierce pays for her crimes.' ~~~~~~~~~ Here I am, on the road again. There I am, up on the stage. Ah here I go, playing star again. There I go, there I go.
