The End Of the Affair - Episode 2

19 July 2006

Date: July 19th 2006 July 19th 2006 Almost two months had passed since Anna had entered his life and it had been undoubtedly one of the best periods in Josh Marquez’s life. The days had been a blur, blending into each other in one phenomenally enjoyable and emotional rush. The physical side of it was unbelievable, the best he’d ever had, and he’d lost count of the number of nights they’d spent together but as it progressed and they got to know each other, Josh felt something he’d never felt before. He thought he was falling in love…and it scared the shit out of him. He’d never been a trusting guy, probably a side-effect of his gambling lifestyle, especially where women were concerned, but with Anna he felt totally at ease putting his life and thoughts in her hands. In the past when his girlfriend had told him she was going out with her friends, he’d automatically thought she was cheating on him. With Anna, he accepted it, even if her phone was switched off and her apartment empty until late the next day. As it was to turn out though, Josh should have kept his guard up… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* July 25th 2006 The day had started out simply enough for Matt D’Angelo, well, as simply as a day could ever start for the General Manager of a multi-million dollar casino. He’d taken over the reins of Treasure Island just 5 months earlier, in the process becoming the youngest of his kind in all of Las Vegas at just 41 years of age. He didn’t have the intelligence community background that most of his peers possessed but there was a general consensus that his network of contacts was just as, if not more effective at getting information, than any of the famous acronyms. This morning he’d already had to take care of a pair of card counters and a seventy-six year old man who thought he’d try to beat the ultra-sophisticated security measures in the casino by grabbing chips off the craps table then attempting to outrun security on his electric wheelchair. You got all sorts in this town. It was two-thirty in the PM now and he was wishing that he had taken this day off as had been the plan last night. The time had just dragged on and by the time he realised it, four AM was showing. So, he’d decided to stay on, take tomorrow off instead, maybe get a round of golf in. "I’m just going down to grab some lunch. If there’s a problem page me." It was something as simple as this, his decision to grab a bite to eat at that particular time of that particular day, that was to change the path of three lives forever. The 30 floor descent in the elevator usually only took a few short seconds but today it seemed like everyone in the resort wanted to use it and so Matt had time to think about his life and in particular his relationship with Anna. Things had been alright until a few months ago, she hadn’t been too upset by his extended working hours and hadn’t voiced any other concerns over their relationship. Then one night he had come home and something had changed. He couldn’t put his finger on exactly what it was but the dynamic was just slightly different. He’d put it down to tiredness and paranoia but even now the feeling persisted, not always apparent but inevitably bubbling just below the surface. The elevator came to a halt on the lowest public access floor of the casino and Matt stepped out amid a crowd of Japanese tourists, cameras, as always, hanging from their necks. Usually Matt would just have waited for them to disperse before continuing on his way but he hadn’t eaten in 9 hours and hunger got the better of him. As fate would have it, the extra few seconds that he would have spent behind the crowds would have been enough for two figures on the casino floor to disappear from view, leaving D’Angelo none the wiser. However, as he pushed through the tourists, a sight he would never forget greeted him… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* (Why the hell did he have to be here?) Anna made her way through the main entrance of Treasure Island with a degree of apprehension. Although Matt wasn’t working today, there was always the slim chance that some of his colleagues might recognise her and spill the beans. It wasn’t that she was afraid of being found out, in fact, she’d love to be able to tell both Matt and Josh the truth but she couldn’t. She couldn’t for one very simple reason - she was scared. Scared that she would lose them both and scared that she would make the wrong choice. This way, the sneaking about, the constant fear of being caught, was far from the exciting lifestyle she had seen in so many movies and she continued with it not out of enjoyment, but out of necessity. (There he is.) ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Josh spotted her a few seconds later, looking as gorgeous as ever, even in a simple black top, jeans and stiletto boots. He’d left the tables a half hour earlier and had been waiting at the bar for Anna to arrive so he could take her to lunch. She’d seemed reluctant to come and meet him, saying something about how he was always in some casino or the other but Josh had laughed it off, promising her that it would be worth it when she saw what restaurant he had booked for them. Then she was there, her hand resting on his shoulder as he pulled her towards him and kissed her. One of those ‘Nice to see you.’ kisses, the other arm round her waist letting everyone know that she was here with him and him only. "You ready for lunch then?" "Of course. Come on though, I hate being indoors at this time." "Well do you think you can survive for an hour or so?" (What’s he talking about? I thought we were going for lunch.) "Why?" "Well, you’re fabulous boyfriend - me - has managed to get us a table in Francesco’s for lunch." "But it doesn’t do lunch." "Today it does. The manager owed me a favour. So, you ready? " (Nonononono! This couldn’t be happening. No way was she going to stay here and risk getting caught.) "Josh, I’m not really in the mood for a big fancy lunch. In fact, I fancy something else right now…" Anna’s fingers walked up Josh’s chest as a coy smile appeared on her face, leaving her boyfriend in no doubt as to what was on her mind. "Well…I guess Jess wouldn’t mind that much. And we can always do it some other time, right?" "Right." (That was a close one.) "Now come on boy, I have plans for you." Taking his hand in hers, she led him out of the building, walking just a little bit faster than normal. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* (What the hell was that?) Matt was beyond shocked. He’d never in his worst nightmares imagined that Anna would ever, could ever, cheat on him but he’d seen it with his own eyes, unequivocal proof that the woman he loved was messing him about. Still, it always took two to tango as they say, and that guy certainly didn’t look to be fighting her off. Maybe a little background check on him would be useful. Pulling out his cellphone, D’Angelo dialled the number for the surveillance room. "Dan, I need you to do a check on someone…camera 34-B about a minute ago, at the bar…soon as possible….thanks." ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* The search went into motion and within hours Matt had all the details on Mr Josh Marquez. Address, criminal convictions, associates, the whole nine yards. D’Angelo decided then and there that he wouldn’t confront Anna, no, he’d handle this situation in his own way. After all, if the competition disappeared then she would be sure to stay with him…
