The End Of the Affair - Episode 1

23 May 2006

Date: May 23rd 2006 He’d never really liked roulette. It lacked the camaraderie that came as part and parcel of other games like blackjack and poker and to him, was just a very sterile vice. Consequently, when Josh played the magic wheel of fortune, he tended to go hard, to hell with the odds. None of the ‘safe’ bets for him, no, for Mr Marquez it was pick one number and let ride. Sure, he didn’t win a whole hell of a lot but when he did, well, people took notice. The AWC newcomer’s match against Pierce Lavelle a few weeks ago was the wrestling equivalent of roulette. He’d known that recording a victory over the icon was a long shot but he’d gone for it, all out, holding nothing back. Things hadn’t gone his way but, fuck it, sometimes they don’t. The only problem he had was the fact that the win column in his AWC record was looking decidedly bare, something he hoped to rectify this week against Horazon. Right now though, the upcoming Fresh!Special was the last thing on Josh Marquez’s mind… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* May 23rd 2006 The multitude of bars and nightclubs in Sin City often makes choosing a party location a difficult task. No matter what kind of night out you want, it’s possible in this wonderful desert city, sitting like a huge neon-lit jewel in the middle of endless plains of sand that comprise the majority of the state of Nevada. Most of the places that people flock to are well known establishments dotted along The Strip or downtown Vegas but once in a while, the big casinos hold very exclusive, invitation only parties. Tonight one of these private parties was being held at Paris, the casino based on the capital city of love. Far above the constant stream of traffic and people that forms the background for every event in the metropolis, Anna Kingsley was enjoying the atmosphere on the roof terrace of Paris. Normally it wouldn’t be advisable to drink with a 26 storey drop just feet away, but given that this was billed as a cocktail party, there wasn’t any way round it. So, it was without too much persuasion that she took another vodka over ice from the barman and made her way over to one of the deluxe sun loungers that had been moved up here especially for tonight. Normally she would be up on the pseudo dance floor formed by the chequered rugs in the centre of the terrace, keeping her two girlfriends company and out of trouble, but she hadn’t had the best week and was quite content to just chill out and enjoy the expensive liquor that was being dished out free of charge, at least to her. Having connections in Vegas society did have some advantages, despite the numerous annoyances it brought. Sitting here with a glass of Grey Goose in her hand, jazz playing through the large speakers nearby and with the sounds of the city largely diminished, she was finally able to think clearly about the events of the last few days. At least, she was able to until a figure moved into her field of view and sat down facing her. He was dressed smartly, though his style was more effortless than the rest of the men here, who looked like they’d walked straight out of the store after buying an outfit they thought was a sure way to get some action. His face was unlined and spoke of healthy living and youth. The cut of his suit subtly accentuated a muscular figure and the lack of ring on his left hand only added to the attraction. (Shit! Had she been staring at him?) Josh had noticed the woman now sitting across from him the second he stepped onto the roof. She had a natural beauty that had struck him from 20 metres away, a beauty that was visible in the way she moved, gracefully yet confidently, across the terrace to the loungers in the corner, overlooking the city. She had the slightest hint of Asia about her round the eyes, and coupled with blonde hair, high cheekbones and lithe body of an international gymnast, she was simply stunning. Right then and there he’d decided that he had to have her. Tonight. (She’d been looking at him, time to start the conversation.) ‘Hi’, he said in a totally everyday way, ‘I’m Josh.’ (Well, I guess I may as well talk to him.) ‘Anna’, she responded, changing her position on the lounger so that she could look him in the eye. ‘So who dragged you along to this?’ The usual end to the question - ‘your boyfriend?’, was left an unsaid and Anna realised that this guy wasn’t like the rest. And those eyes. ‘Just a few of my friends,’ replied Anna, gesturing vaguely in the direction of the dance floor as she absently flicked her hair. ‘How about you?’ (The old hair flick, this just got interesting.) ‘Well, I’m meant to be meeting a few buddies here but I doubt they will manage to tear themselves away from the tables for another few hours.’ He gave her the smile, the full one that included the eyes and she rewarded him with a slight giggle. (Why are we beating about the bush, we know what’s going to happen.) ‘I don’t mean to sound forward but would you like to go somewhere quieter and get a drink?’ Josh inquired. ‘You do mean to be forward’, came the reaction, ‘But I think a quiet drink sounds good.’ ‘After you then’, said Marquez, standing and extending his hand to help Anna up. ‘Thank you.’ (Should I tell them I’m leaving?) ‘Do you want to tell your friends that you’re going?’ (Sensitive too. This one was full of surprises.) ‘No, they can look after themselves and they can always call my mobile if they need to.’ And so it began. The quiet drink quickly led to a long drive and eventually back to Anna’s 16th floor apartment overlooking The Strip. Josh quickly became infatuated with her, more so than he’d ever been before. She was simply phenomenal in every way - smart, beautiful, funny, the usual stuff that guys love about women. He wanted to be with her every second of every day…but for her things weren’t so simple. You see, when she met Josh she was still involved with someone else, a fact that she conveniently forgot to mention over the last few months to her new lover. And as is always the case in situations like this, it was only a matter of time until the truth came out…
