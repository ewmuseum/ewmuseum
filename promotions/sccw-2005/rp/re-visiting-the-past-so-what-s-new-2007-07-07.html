Re-visiting The Past. So What's New?

7 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/7/2007 PRESENT DAY! To look towards the future, one has to reflect on the past. October 18th 1999 1:00pm DINNER TIME! "DINNER!" A plastic dish with bright red tomato soup in it was shoved through a slight opening in the door. "No spoon again?" remarked Raul Diaz. "Not after last time. We can’t trust you with a spoon, so you’ll just have to drink it," the voice replied. The opening in the door soon closed, leaving Raul in his own company. "Another day, yet more soup," he said aloud to himself. Raul Diaz, aka El Diablo, was in a small room, with nothing in it except a mattress and a duvet. This lonely, baron room was inside a mental institute, a place he was put in by his wife Angela shortly after their marriage. Angela became concerned when he was overcome with paranoia and turned into an emotional wreck, for reasons she did not understand. Eight long months later and Raul was still inside this institute. The monotonous routine would be enough to drive any man crazy: 8:00am Awaken 9:00am Breakfast 11:00am Group Therapy 1:00pm Dinner 3:00pm Individual Therapy 6:00pm Medication 9:00pm Bed Time Eight months, two-hundred and forty days, five-thousand seven-hundred and sixty hours of this very same routine, if you weren’t insane when you entered this mental institute you sure would be if you were lucky enough to leave it. Itching to exit this institute, El Diablo had already attempted to break out of it. Using a plastic spoon, snapped in half, he cut himself open, waited to be noticed and taken into medical care and then attempted to run away, only he was caught by the staff and sent back, never to be able to use any of the cutlery ever again. Being inside a mental institute is a lonely life for a withdrawn human being as El Diablo was. He had no friends and felt like everyone was out to get him. The only place he would feel safe was in his own room, outside of those four walls he felt vulnerable. October 18th 1999 3:00pm INDIVIDUAL THERAPY! Walking through the corridors was an eerie experience, hearing the whaling of the mentally ill inside their rooms was nerve shattering. Being hurried along the corridor by a staff member was Raul, in his white gown which scraped along the ground as he was led to Dr Murray’s office for his individual therapy session. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! "Enter," said the voice within the room. The door handle rattled as El Diablo nervously made contact with it, slowly turning it, trying to delay his entry into the office for as long as possible. He eventually opened the door and shuffled his way into the room, closing the door behind him. "Take a seat," Dr Murray suggested. Reluctantly, Diablo found his way to the couch and laid down on it. "You look terrible," stated Dr Murray. His long hair thinning, a thick beard and large black bags under his eyes, El Diablo did look terrible. "Lets pick up where we left from last time," commented Dr Murray. Doctor Murray was a small man, only 5’6 and weighed 168lbs. He was 63 years old, balding with patches of grey hair and a grey moustache. If El Diablo wanted to physically intimidate him, he could, but The Mexican Devil was in no mental shape to do that. "I’d be walking to work, walking around at work or walking home from work and I noticed people were following me," Diablo stated, almost emotionless. Coming across as a broken man, El Diablo was prepared to talk to this man. "I could hear their footsteps, but when I turned around there was nobody there," he continued on. The doctor scribbled away in his pad as El Diablo spoke. "Perhaps, there wasn’t anyone following you?" suggested Dr Murray. Panicking, Diablo began to tremble. "I could hear the footsteps. I could hear them breathing. There was someone there," Nodding his head, Dr Murray continued to write things down on the pad, which prompted Diablo to wonder just what the doctor was writing. Throughout his life, he would grow suspicious of even the slightest of things if it involved him, like the time his mother arranged a surprise party for him, as he knew something was going on because of her secrecy, and this was the start of his paranoia. "Perhaps you’re lonely, perhaps you were hoping someone was there," retorted Murray. Sitting up from the couch, Diablo looked annoyed. "Why would I want someone to be there? I was scared for my life," he snarled. "The mind can often create fictitious people when lonely," the doctor replied. The Mexican Devil stood up and began to pace around the room. "If I was creating fictitious people, wouldn’t I see them?" queried Diablo. Stroking his moustache, the doctor pondered how to respond. "Usually that is the case, Raul. But you’re an unusual case. You’re paranoid, a loner, very disassociated with society, a bad temper and you’re emotionally scarred," Hearing the doctor describe him was a strange sensation for El Diablo, as for his whole life he never understood what was wrong with himself and now for the very first time he had heard it out loud. "I can promise you I didn’t imagine someone following me," Diablo told Dr Murray as he stopped pacing the room and took a seat on the couch again. Once more Dr Murray took some notes down in his pad. "What about your emotional issues? Can you tell me about them?" asked Dr Murray. A look of despair overcame El Diablo, as he recalled the death of his brother Jorge in a traffic accident. "I miss my brother," he said softly, "I wish he was still around, but he isn’t, so I just have to go on," he finished. "How did it feel when he passed on?" questioned Murray. It was like a black cloud had gone over Diablo, as he looked shell-shocked at this question. It brought back memories of the night Jorge had died, as Raul was in the car that Jorge was driving when they got ran into by another car. He could remember the horrific sound when the cars impacted and the horrible screams by Jorge as the car flipped over. "I wish I had died that day. Jorge was a better man than I am," stated Diablo solemnly. The doctor nodded to indicate he was listening thoroughly. "Why do you say that?" Wiping away a tear, Diablo tried to regain some sort of composure. "He was an academic and an athlete. He had it all and would have been a great success in whatever he did," Diablo commented. The doctor proceeded to take further notes, enhancing the paranoia going through Diablo’s head. "You don’t think you’re a success?" the doctor asked him. Shaking his head, The Mexican Devil clearly did not think so. "I’m just a wrestler and have yet to achieve any sustained success," As he answered the doctor’s question, paranoid thoughts were running through his head. Just why was the doctor making notes about him? Why couldn’t he see what Dr Murray was writing about him? Could he not go home because of a conspiracy within this institution? "And if you achieve success?" asked Dr Murray. Agitated and shaking because of his paranoia, El Diablo tried his best to answer him. "Then I’ll accept it, but I’ll forever wish that my brother could have been by my side," Again Dr Murray took some notes, pushing Diablo’s paranoia to it’s boundaries. "WHAT ARE YOU WRITING ABOUT ME?" Diablo screeched. Taken aback by this sudden outburst, Dr Murray placed the pad and pen down on his desk. "What do you mean?" the doctor asked, still trying to examine Raul’s psyche. Slumping to the floor from the couch, Diablo began to rock back and forth as he breathed very heavily in panic. "JUST TELL ME WHAT YOU’RE WRITING," he managed to scream at the doctor. Tearing out strands of his hair as he awaited an answer, Diablo had snapped in front of Dr Murray for the very first time. It was this episode that pro-longed his stay at the institute for another two months. "I think we can finish this later," stated Dr Murray. Dr Murray pressed a button from behind his desk, and immediately two white coated staff members arrived in the room and pulled El Diablo up from the floor. "NO! I NEED ANSWERS" yelled The Mexican Devil. Raul tried desperately to fight the staff members off, but didn’t have the strength and was eventually dragged kicking and screaming out of the room, watching as Dr Murray took down more notes on him. October 18th 1999 6:00pm MEDICATION TIME! Having calmed down from earlier in the day, El Diablo was back in his room awaiting his medication. "I have to know what he was writing," he whispered to himself. The day’s incident was being replayed over and over inside El Diablo’s mind, and the only conclusion he could come to was that Dr Murray was another person that was out to get him. It was this self perceived revelation that now made El Diablo feel unsafe everywhere within this mental institute, no longer safe within these four walls that he called his home. Very suddenly, the door cranked open and a white coat was standing there holding varying medications. This man was young, 6’0, 188lbs and clearly looked like he didn’t want to work at this mental institute. "Ready for your medication?" mumbled the youngster. Knowing the young man had broken protocol by opening the door, El Diablo stood up and nodded to indicate he wanted his medication. The young man approached and placed a pill inside Diablo’s mouth and handed him a plastic cup of water. "Go ahead," the young man said. He raised the cup of water up towards his mouth but in a quick motion threw the cup at the young man and at the same time threw a punch with his other hand which connected perfectly, knocking out this young man. Diablo stood over him and spat out the pill as he searched him for some keys. "Got them," he whispered. Looking cautiously Diablo exited the room. He moved quickly down the corridor, looking out for other staff members as he attempted another escape. "Eight months in here and I don’t even know where I’m going," said the emotionally drained Mexican Devil. Trying to move around this mental institute unnoticed was a difficult task, as he’d turn corners and find dead ends and open doors and discover they led nowhere. It felt like he was in a gulag which he was never going to escape. "WHERE IS THE DAMNED EXIT?" he screamed. With that scream, he had forgotten about a stealthy escape and was now desperate to get out of this institute. "WHAT THE HELL IS THIS PLACE?" shrieked Diablo. Sprinting around the corridors and finding no exit, El Diablo could not understand what was happening to him. He knew something wasn’t right about this place, yet continued opening doors and finding dead ends. Tired and out of breath he collapsed to the ground in a heap and began crying uncontrollably. "Why can’t I leave?" he sobbed. Eventually he fell fast asleep on the floor and was unconscious for what seemed like days. October 18th 1999 9:00pm GRIM REALITY! Inside a bright room, strapped down onto a bed was an unconscious El Diablo. He was sweating profusely and looked very unwell. "The rapid eye movement has stopped," stated a voice from a man approaching the bed. The man was Vincent Halfpenny, 55. He was 6’2 258lbs with dyed black hair. He was not a doctor. "He’ll need another injection then," bellowed Dr Murray. Grabbing hold of a sharp needle out of a drawer, Dr Murray walked to the bed and placed the needle in his left arm. "OH SHIT!" shouted Dr Murray. The thing that spooked Dr Murray was that El Diablo’s eyes for a few seconds opened and saw vividly what was happening. Quickly though, Dr Murray injected him with a green liquid which soon saw Diablo’s eyes flicker and close again. "He won’t remember this will he?" Halfpenny queried. Doctor Murray removed the needle from Diablo’s vein and shrugged his shoulders. "I don’t know. His eyes were open, but I highly doubt he was conscious," responded Murray. As he was checking Diablo’s vitals, Halfpenny turned to Dr Murray. "He must never know what really happens here. No one must know," Vincent warned Dr Murray. Dr Murray nodded. "Don’t worry, I know the company must be protected," Dr Murray reassured Halfpenny. PRESENT DAY! Of course El Diablo was not aware of the grim reality of the mental institute he was a part of. It was only now as he thought about it that he was having flashbacks of these injections. Many questions regarding his stay there were running through his head, like did he really try to escape twice or was he always strapped to the bed? He knew he had to get answers soon to discover what really happened there.
