2033...the Black and White Invasion v.2 hits Me like a Mack Truck

27 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/27/2009 After the show, I am getting ready to leave when I hear a noise inside my locker room. Look around, I see nothing. No one is there and I am alone. Shrug my shoulders and continue to get my stuff ready to be packed. I am going to start heading to the next show when I hear another noise. Turn around and BAM! Get nailed in the face. Out cold. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . AWAKE! I awaken to find myself tied to a chair in a dimly lit room somewhere. Look around and there’s no one in sight. I hear some voices in the background. None of them sound familiar. Must be the same guys from before. The bastards. Look ahead now and see the whole gang of goons headed my way. The leader is in the front. He has a big grin on his face. "Why hello Rick," he says with a smile. "You feeling better?" I look at him closely and he looks vaguely familiar. "Yes I do," I reply before asking a question. "Who are you anyway?" The man smiles. "I figured you might ask that," he replies. "I’ll tell you this much about me before I reveal to you my name. In the year 2031, I became the youngest heavyweight champion of all time. I won it at age 21 in PRIME." I look at him funnily. "2031?" I ask. "Yes," he responds. "I was the biggest face in the industry. The people loved me. I was considered to be the most popular baby face at the time. Women loved me, kids loved me, and I got PWI wrestler of the year. Technically in the ring, I was the best but I could brawl too. Wrestling had become a cross between what the people of this time call Puro, MMA, and American style pro wrestling." "I was widely regarded as the best in the world and then I suffered an injury causing me to forfeit the title due to being unable to compete. I held the title for fourteen months. Nobody could beat me and to lose the title like that was a hard blow to take for me. When I came back, the people started giving me a lukewarm reaction every time I came out to the ring. The people had forgotten me while I was gone. The receptions I got were horrible. So then I brought in some wrestlers from other organizations to take over PRIME." "PRIME is still around in 2031?" I ask as he quickly nods his head. "Yeah," he replies. "But when I came back, I realized that the product had become stale. The people clapped like harbor seals for the lame comedy and pretty much boring, stale matches. The writing for the shows weren’t much better because it had gotten gradually worse after I had left. So I looked around the archives and found something that caught my eye." "And what was that?" I ask. "Old footage of a group formed more than ten years ago," he says with a smile. "Called the new World order. They did things that just astonished me. At the same time, they were also the virus that killed the company that created them. So I decided to bring the virus back and kill the entire sport of wrestling. It had become so pointless that it reached the point where it needed to be killed. From that point on, nWo 2033 was born. I came back and joined two invading wrestlers to make history repeat itself at Colossus 2033. So I got some more guys together and we started invading the shows. I went from the most beloved hero ever and turned into the most hated." "But why would you do that?" I ask the man. "Because I can," he quickly replies with a sly smile. "But I wasn’t alone. I have twelve guys with me and they’re all bad ass. Arturo Maximo from Spain and two guys whose last names you may recognize. Their last names are known to you as they relatives of the men from your time. We even have a Hart in our group. But most shocking of all, the group is led by me." At this point I don’t know if he’s telling the truth or what so I have to ask him one question. "What’s your name, anyway?" I ask with a quixotic look. He looks at me for a moment before smiling. "My name is von Doom Jr.," he says with a grin. "How…how can there be a von Doom Jr.?" I ask before suddenly coming to a realization. "You aren’t even born yet!" "You said it exactly," he responds. "And now that you know too much, you’re either with us or you’re against us. And if you’re against us, then you’re dead. Got it, chump? Because the nWo is the only way to go and whatca gonna do?!" I gulp and start to sweat. This von Doom Jr. means business. He’s not a fuck up like his dad. He’s in shape, he’s smart, and he’s evil. I have no other choice but to give him the answer he wants. "I’m in," I reply with much reluctance. He cackles with glee. "Good, very good," he says with a grin before pulling out a nWo T-shirt from behind him and tosses it at me. It hits me in the chest before falling into my lap. Some of his goons come up behind me and untie me. Quickly getting up, I put on the T-shirt and start walking with von Doom Jr. as I try to get some more info out of him. "When you guys started this whole group up again, did anyone try to stop you?" I ask. He turns and looks really annoyed for some reason. "Yeah, a few people," he responds. "Most notably some washed up midcard veteran who had a late career resurgence. His name is Biff Busey." "BIFF BUSEY?!" I exclaim loudly. "That loser couldn’t even hack it in NFW!" Von Doom Jr. nods his head. "He bounced around a lot in his career," von Doom Jr. replies. "But eventually he ended up in PRIME to help train the rookies and had a career rebirth. He wasn’t too bad, actually. Only he was left to face us. Well, him and my loser relative Darth von Doom. That bastard used some sort of time travel device to send us back to this time. MY ONLY FAMILY TURNED ON ME! Now we’re going to make him pay in this time. Him and anybody else who gets in our way." "So what about SCCW," I say with an intrigued tone. "Is it still around in your time?" He shakes his head. "No," he replies. "It closes in 2015. However we have more important matters to discuss. We have an invasion to plan, Rick. And you are the key." As von Doom Jr. and I walk through their little base of operations, I hear one of his goons in the background whispering. He says, "Why is this guy so important? He isn’t even a midcarder." "Because he can get us into SCCW," the other man whispers back. "And that’ll be our in for the GTT tournament. That’s von Doom Jr.’s plan. To win the tournament and make us famous in this time." "Brilliant," the other goon replies before the two see me look back and quickly scamper apart. There’s more going on than they’re telling me. But, for now, I’m now a goon of the new World order 2033."
