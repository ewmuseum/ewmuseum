A Dream within a Dream.

4 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/04/2009 . . . . . . . . . W A K E U P!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Alarm going off. Awaken to find myself inside of a dungeon. Maybe I’m in one of those sadomasochistic type places where the women sexually torture the men. … Nah. Freddy Kreuger is a more plausible explanation than that. Realize I am in a bed. Look over and see a naked woman. She sits up and starts to lean over to kiss my lips. Reaching over to brush her hair, her hair suddenly falls off leaving hair all over the bed. Horrified, I run over to put on my clothes. Slime is still on my head as I do so… Slimy… Dripping… Nauseating. Want to believe the slime is my own cum but I highly doubt that. Look over to the door and see a dark passageway leading to some stairs. Run out of the room and head down the stairs. Darkness surrounds me… Hear noises. THWACK! BAM! SCREECH! BOOM! Explosion in the room I was just in! Pieces of building flying everywhere! Run for my life down the stairs. Jump out of the way of an explosion that is shaped like the head of Clark Fox. Safe at last, I look around and see all sorts of weird creatures roaming about in the depths of the cave I am in. At least it looks like a cave… Damp… Dark… Endless. It might also be Tim Shipley’s office. Realize it’s not a cave when I see stars shining in the sky above me. Planets, asteroids, stars, and nebulas floating all around me. Ships fly through space all around my head. Some are shooting lasers at one another. A laser almost hits me in the head! ZAP! SLAM! BAM! ZIP! BOOM! The universe soon dissipates and there standing in front of me is a giant dragon! It has the scales of a reptile, the wings of a bat, legs of a gigantic herbivorous dinosaur, and the head of Aaron Nothings! As the beast flies toward me, I look around for a weapon. For some reason the Just Wrestling Championship is lying next to me. I pick it up and swing it at the mighty beast. It staggers back only to charge once more. BAM! WHACK! SLAM! I go flying through the air and end up falling through a giant sinkhole but it is no ordinary sinkhole. It’s the giant head of Tim Shipley and the sinkhole is really his open mouth! AAAAAAAHH Hhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh Hhhhhhhhhhhhhh!!!!! SPLASH! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Darkness… Look around and see only infinite blackness. Ground below me is squishy. Look down to see that I am standing on a giant tongue. EWWWWWW…. Gross. And to make matters worse… It’s the tongue of my boss. Sickening smells all around. Reminds me of the locker room. Maybe Crane killed a baby or something. Look around some more to try and find something useful. BUMP! Slide… STOP! Go over and pick up the object. It’s a flashlight. Turn it on. LIGHT! See that I’m stuck. Turn around and keep walking. Hopefully I reach the show before Shipley eats me. Wait, did I just think that my boss is going to eat me? Sick. Get that thought out of head and keep walking. Have a MVP Battle Royal and a match with Clark Fox. I can’t be stuck inside of a giant mouth walking on a squishy pink tongue that belongs to the man who pays me with food stamps and peanuts. Walk south and head down the esophagus. Once there I am somehow out of Shipley’s body and now in a giant ballroom. People are dancing. Look around and see that they aren’t people. Instead the women are evil looking troll creatures and the men all look like Meat. The ballroom quickly spirals out of control before turning into a Just Wrestling tour show. All of the other wrestlers on Just’s roster are in the ring staring at me with flaming red eyes. Wrett Warren Clark Fox Maynard Crane Skillet Erik Magnum Fallen in Love Meat Aaron Nothings Aaron Cross Malcolm Dred-King Blake Gray Hate Jon Kano Veljumin Rippa Scraps Wone Lance Spencer All of them are there and they all have one thing in common. They all have weapons in hand. All of these weapons have blood dripping from them. Taking a closer look, all of the wrestlers have clawed hands with long, gnarled fingernails at the ends of their deformed fingers. Peer around at the crowd and all of them are humanoid monstrosities with big, grayish colored bodies and wearing normal human style clothing. Some even are holding up signs. One says "Rick Malloy, Run for Your Life." I think this fan might be onto something so I listen to the advice. Heading for the exit, the giant head of Tim Shipley appears in front of me. The giant head floats toward me and starts shooting green jelly out of it’s mouth! Sticky Icky Ooey Gooey I can barely move… Stuck in the green goo… Utterly disgusting. I try to move but I can’t! I’m stuck! Time stops. Can’t move anywhere. Tim Shipley’s head starts sucking me in like a black hole. The whole arena that the Just Wrestling show is taking place in is starts spiraling out of control faster the US economy. Pieces of the building starts spiraling toward Shipley’s mouth. The humanoids in the audience and the wrestlers in the ring do the same! E V E R Y O N E I S A V I C T I M. N O O N E I S S A F E . As everything around me continues to turn into chaos, I continue to be stuck to the ground by the greenish colored goo. Look down and see the foundation of the arena below me falling into nothingness. Look around and I am floating in space. Nothing is left but me, the goo stuck to my feet, and the giant head of Tim Shipley. The head looks at me and smiles, licking it’s life. This is it for me. I’ve lived a good life. No one can take that away from me. Not even the giant head of the man who hired me. The head then floats over and tries to eat me but something zaps it! ZAP! A cosmic battle unfolds in front of me as the giant head of Tim Shipley battles my savior…the Silver Magnum! Silver Magnum shoots lasers from his hands as he flies at the giant had of Shipley on his cosmic surf board. ZAP! ZAM! SLAM! BOOM! ZOOM! BAM! The battle wages on for hours. And hours. And hours. Neverending, perpetual chaos. Finally the head is weakened and the Silver Magnum swoops in, zapping it in the head with a laser blast. B O O M !!!!!! When the head explodes, the goo on my feet disappears. I look down and then start to fall through space! "OH SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIITTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!!!" Falling for what seems like ages, I end up crashing through some clouds and entering the atmosphere of some planet. Falling even further, I crash through the top of a skyscraper and land on the top floor. Standing up, I brush myself off and look around. Seems like the office of a business of some kind, probably one dealing with Wall Street investments. Upon further review, all of the people there, male or female, have the face of Clark Fox. I shrug my shoulders and try to leave only to see the Grim Reaper appear in front of me. The only thing is that this Grim Reaper has the face of Aaron Nothings and is holding the Just Wrestling Championship belt. He charges at me with his scythe. He swings and misses, killing about half of the Clark Fox people in the office. I then combat his attack by blocking a scythe attack with a laptop I pick up off a table. Blocking his wild shots, he eventually slices me with the scythe! Whiteness… Darkness… Fade away. End up in a world of only white. White is everywhere. It’s just me. I knew this wrestling job would be a bad idea. I should retire and be a rapper. Yes, yes I should. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . WAKE UP! Rise up out of bed in a cold sweat and look around to see a dingy motel room. No monsters, no creatures, no evil beings trying to kill me. Look next to me and there is no woman. However there is a used condom laying there instead. Yes, things are indeed back to normal.
