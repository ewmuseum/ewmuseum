'Hide any weakness. Even from yourself'.

28 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 28, 2008 What is the worst thing you have ever done? What have you thought of doing when you were really angry? What hits all those buttons and makes you lash out? Gideon Fontaine by nature is generally calm and methodical but this isn’t quite the case anymore. The more he is wrestling the more things annoy him and eat away at his patience and leaves him angry and looking to make people suffer. The last Uproar was an example of this. There were two people that became targets of Gideon’s anger and being the man Gideon is he certainly never pulled any punches. The first person Gideon attacked verbally was Timo. Timotoga Bolamba certainly never expected the words that came out of Gideon’s mouth. There has been a long history between Timo and both members of the Alliance Gideon Fontaine and Neil Whittaker, known to the FUSE fans by his ring name of Neil Fracas. It wasn’t based on any favors like so many others in the wrestling business. They met a long time ago and respected each other’s work ethics and drive. It seems like only a short time ago he sent Eric Evans and Jason Bass to train with him when the Alliance were in Prime. "He did what was best for him and what was best for him was teaming with another main eventer like Amy." You can’t imagine the anger Gideon felt as he said those words, even by looking at the anger in Gideon’s eyes. Generally, Gideon takes insults with a grain of salt and plans revenge accordingly. This one hurt him a lot, because he never would have expected it from Timo. The match wasn’t all that important but to Gideon it was a clear cut sign. Despite Timo saying that he respected Gideon and appreciated someone having his back it wasn’t true. Timo didn’t trust Gideon to have his back against McKail and his mystery partner and he didn’t think that Gideon belonged on his level. Timo would never say that but he never had to his choice said all that needed to be said. "He did what was best for him and what was best for him was teaming with another main eventer like Amy." Despite their past and Gideon having Timo’s back, Timotoga Bolamba is just like nearly everyone else in the business concerned more about what is best for him and his career. Whether or not Gideon is right or wrong the damage is done. Timo and Amy have a long history as well. Gideon knows of their past and how much they mean to each other. Gideon also has watched as Timo has gotten beaten and bloodied and very rarely did Amy come to his aid. When her future husband Darcy Markson leaped off the top rope and drove a metal chair into the head of Timo, she was not there. Gideon Fontaine was. When Timo was in the ring knocked unconscious from hitting the back of his head on the metal bar of a steel folding chair, she was not there. Gideon was not there when Timo and Dave were taken away in an ambulance after they nearly killed themselves in a match. He was arranging a 250,000 dollar payoff for Dave Gibson and Timo Bolamba selling the "Hatred That Can Only Be Hardcore" T-Shirt with the DVD of the match where they hospitalized each other. Gideon never made a penny off that T-Shirt. The reason he made the shirt was to get his friend a pay off for what could have ended his career because heaven knows FUSE would never have looked after him. The other man that Gideon lashed out at is Smitty T. Duluth. There has been a good relationship between Gideon Fontaine and Smitty T. Duluth for a very long time. There isn’t any brown nosing by Gideon to get title shots. In this day and age there is so much politics and backstabbing it is rare to find any sort of relationship not based on the "What’s in it for me" mindset of so many people these days. "Gideon Fontaine, who I used to think was an okay guy with good taste in strippers." Gideon never even heard those words spoken by Smitty. The only people that heard those words were Raymond Pierce and a stripper by the name of "Amberlynn". After her shift ended at 4 am she would forget the name as she checked on her daughter before going to bed. If you were just to base everything you know on the Smitty you see on TV you would think he is a breast obsessed jackass. All you see is the man on TV. Sure there isn’t much difference between the man on TV and the lovable womanizer Smitty T. Duluth. There is one thing that is golden however. He loves his daughter and would kill anyone that attempted to hurt her. "If you were less concerned about strippers and more concerned about being a real father you wouldn’t have had to be worried about her safety. I can’t even imagine what type of self-absorbed asshole it takes to leave his daughter vulnerable while he is playing a ladies man with hired prostitutes. A smarter man would realize that maybe the arena isn’t the safest spot for a young girl unless you want her to get a head start on being the next ring rat in the back sucking off the boys for the attention that she isn’t getting from you." That wasn’t a promo. That was a slap in his face. That was to hurt him as much as humanly possible. That was aimed at the one thing Smitty truly cares about, His daughter. What inspired Gideon to take a pot shot so hurtful at Smitty? When Gideon said those words he knew that he knew that he went too far. Gideon is frustrated and angry. He is looking at his career and getting more resentful by the day. If any other man said those words that Gideon did they would be looking for a new place to work. Hell Smitty considered firing Gideon. He could tell by looking at those eyes that Gideon wasn’t "Cutting a heel promo" trying to get a cheap pop. Why is Gideon Fontaine still employed by FUSE? Smitty knows that there is something wrong with Gideon. You can insult the man for the way he acts but when it comes down to it Smitty loves the business. You can’t even begin to imagine what it is like to deal with all the wrestlers on the roster. The whining and the belly aching would drive any sane person to snap and start abusing very strong drugs. Gideon was one of the few guys that understood what Smitty went through on a daily basis. When Gideon arranged for the odd perk like a massage from the woman with the gigantacular breasts, it wasn’t because Gideon wanted anything. Gideon did that because he wanted to. Neil and Stephanie called Gideon after seeing that promo. They didn’t have to be told that Gideon was shooting. For a man like Gideon to go after Smitty and his daughter like that something is wrong. For Gideon there has always been a line. There has always been a certain unspoken standard. There was always a pride in what he was doing and integrity. Smitty is going to be calling Gideon later on for an explanation. ########## ########## ########## ########## ########## The master bedroom of the Fontaine Mansion. ########## ########## ########## ########## ########## Only the mirror knows the Gideon Fontaine that is standing in front of it. Even the mirror is amazed at the change in Gideon over the past few months. The man that loved the business and held himself to that higher standard isn’t there any more. The man that is standing in front of the mirror is a different man. The Osaka Assassin Gideon Fontaine adjusts his tie and looks at his jet black Armani suit. The look in his eyes is empty like a piece of his soul is gone. The smile on his face is that of a man that has long since learned to hide any weakness, even from himself. The Osaka Assassin has an agenda and it is unfolding very slowly. Zakk Wylde is joining him in FUSE. There will be others joining the banner of the federation formerly known as NEW. The people that have been on the Black List of wrestling society are going to be rallying under a common cause. When the name Black List was discussed before it was under different circumstances. How long ago those few months seem. Gideon wasn’t even wrestling when the idea was being discussed about a stable of men that have been lost in the shuffle in FUSE. It wasn’t until after it was discussed that Gideon had finally decided to comeback to wrestling. The easy path would have been just to manage Caleb Alan and the stable. Gideon was inspired to comeback because he saw this stable as something that was going to be fun and enjoyable. It was going to be one of those rare times when you felt you were part of something that was going to matter. Ethan Frost was one of those guys that busted his ass and enjoyed wrestling. After Fred Fusion left a lot of what he enjoyed died. The Legacy was gone so there was no enemies to defeat anymore. This could have been a great time to excel and show he was ready to take the next step in his wrestling career. Ethan left FUSE. Tsuyoshi Tanaka is one of the top guys in the business with a striking skill that maybe 7 guys wrestling today could hope to be able to deal with. No one could imagine that Gideon and Tsuyoshi would ever join forces. They even set things up so fans were expecting the inevitable explosion when both men would meet in the ring. Tanaka left FUSE. Timo Bolamba is the final man of these 4 men. The heart and the desire of this man combined with a resilience and work ethic made him a dangerous man to deal with. The skills Timo brings to the table are impressive as well because of the drive to be the best he can be technically as well. Timo Bolamba is still in FUSE. The Black List was over before a theme music was even chosen. ########## ########## ########## ########## ########## The Gymnasium of the Fontaine Mansion. ########## ########## ########## ########## ########## As Gideon is walking into the Gym there is a wide variety of equipment. Over to the left however is a variety of stands with wooden blocks held in several spots by mechanical stands of various heights and angles. On the wall a mere 12 feet away is a sword on a stand. It is a Katana. The lovingly polished blade is gleaming from the lights above, waiting for it’s master to call upon it’s use. The light from the Rolex on Gideon’s wrist and the gold cufflinks shine as his hand clasps the hilt of the blade. A smile forms as he removes the blade from his resting place and walks back to the stands. Gideon: It has been far too long since you have called to me. Too long since I have let you feel the air caress you as you cleave through my targets. I think I shall name these blocks of wood. What Gideon has named these blocks of wood id unspoken as he hits a button that starts the mechanical stands moving in erratic patterns. Gideon: Far too long…… Drink the wooden sinews of the blocks and remember this day. The smile on Gideon’s face is disturbing because of the mirth and glee involved in what he is doing. As the blade of his Katana whistles through the air a block is cleaved like butter. It falls to the ground and you can’t help but feel uncomfortable. Gideon’s skill with the sword has been honed since that day at the age of 5 when he was dropped on the doorstep of Master Hiroshiro Tanimoto. January 12th he turned 35 and he has honed the Zangi-Shinginkin-Ryo (Swordmanship favored by the Samurai) and Muei Thai under Tanimoto’s guidance into the deadly killing machine he is today. His skills have been honed for nearly 30 years and his body just as long. The few men that can match his skills with the Katana and Wakizashi and none of those men have the speed, reflexes, and strength of the Osaka Assassin. Gideon: Rest. You have earned it. The blocks are lying scattered across the floor cleaved by the blade of Gideon Fontaine. This light workout is just another day at the office for this man. Gideon puts the sword back on the stand. The blade glints as Gideon walks out of the Gymnasium. Now it is time to go to the office and think about other matters. ################################################ Fontaine Enterprises office January 28, 2008 ################################################ As Gideon walks into the office Amanda is sitting at the desk outside his office doing some paperwork. She is always there at 7 AM sharp to get the paperwork started for when Gideon arrives at 8 AM. Gideon has a smile as he walks to the desk. Gideon: Good morning Amanda. How are you today? Amanda: You wouldn’t believe the shit storm of calls you have been getting Gideon. Gideon: I am a pro wrestler. I can even believe that Jonathan Rhine has charisma. Seriously though anything important? Amanda: Depends on what you consider important Smitty Duluth called but I told him that you weren’t in until 8. Pro Wrestling Insider called and Derrick Stone wanted to do an interview. Since he is the only guy you’d talk to I scheduled him for lunch. A few of the dirt sheets called but I told them that you were unavailable for comment due to personal issues. 15 bucks says they will say it’s steroid related. The laughter from Gideon echoes through the room. Gideon: That’s beautiful! I imagine that Smitty has some questions about the things I said. Amanda: He isn’t the only one. Neil and Stephanie were concerned as well. That isn’t like you at all. Gideon: I know. I’m chalking that up to frustration with the way things are going in FUSE. I’ll be going over to Neil’s later today to train for the tag match. I think he might have a few pointers from the triple threat match he had with Cyrus Raynes and Kid Cool. Amanda: How are things between you and Timo? You shot on him pretty hard. Gideon: Who knows. I haven’t talked to him in a while and I’m getting the feeling that all I am to him is a supporting cast member in the Timotoga Bolamba story. Amanda: Ouch…… Gideon: I don’t even know if he even bothered to watch the segment to be pissed off about it. That pretty much tells me where I stand in importance. Amanda: Should I schedule a few interviews for the dirt sheets to cheer you up? Gideon: You read my mind Amanda. Think I’ll do some paper work until my lunch with Derrick. They both laugh. Gideon takes the pile of folders and goes into his office. There is work to be done before his lunch with Derrick and he’s going to drown himself in paper work to silence all the dark thoughts in his mind. The demons of the wrestling business even can get to a man like Gideon. ################################################ Antonio’s Pizzaria, January 28, 2008 ################################################ Gideon Fontaine is sitting at a table with an old friend. Derrick Stone is a man that Gideon has known many years since becoming a wrestler. There has been several wrestling federations. UJW, LWA, Titan Wrestling, UPWF, PPW, NEW, MLWC, Core, Prime Wrestling, and FUSE Wrestling. Derrick Stone was even invited to the wedding of Neil and Stephanie. He has had many rough years however and you can tell from the disheveled look and the 5 o’clock shadow. For those that say marriage is a wonderful thing they obviously do not have 3 ex wives. The horror stories Derrick could tell you. The motel that Derrick lives in would be condemned even in Mexico. He lives there because the lawyers of his Ex-Wives are bleeding him dry of every penny they can. There is a hot plate collecting dust and the stove hasn’t worked in 79 years. The reason the hot plate is collecting dust is he doesn’t like heating up the food for the cockroaches. Despite living in this 3rd world disaster area he hasn’t committed suicide. He has too much pride and keeps his chin up. Though there is a bit of a cynicism from nearly a decade of poverty. You can tell that Derrick Stone doesn’t like people paying his way. Even though he has 17 bucks and 15 cents to his name he still has his pride. Derrick: Thanks for lunch Gideon. Gideon: No problem Derrick. I like you and you are one of the few guys that I consider a friend. Derrick: Can you tell me anything about your plans in FUSE with Zakk Wylde. Gideon: I’d love to. You are going to have to keep this under wraps though. Derrick: Sure. Gideon: Lean forward. When Derrick leans forward Gideon whispers the details into his ear and enjoys the look of surprise. You can see Antonio getting a couple bottles of water as he is getting the food ready to take over to the table. Derrick: You are serious?! Gideon: Yup. Derrick: Wow! I can’t believe you are letting that whack job do your PR. The food is put on the table. There is pineapple garlic fingers, a small spaghetti, and a Caesar chicken salad. The two bottles of water set in front of Gideon and Derrick. Antonio: I’ll have the other order ready for when you leave Gideon. Gideon: Excellent. As they are eating Derrick can’t help but ask the real question he has been wanting to ask. What is the story behind your pre recorded rant on FUSE Uproar. This is going to be both off the record and on the record. On the record the shoot about Timo was a pretty stiff shot. With those words Gideon takes a drink of water. Gideon: I’m not going to beat around the bush Timo and I may be friends but I took offense at him blowing me off and choosing Amy. Sure they have history but then again we have history as well. When you come right down to it the big difference is tag experience. If you just go by our tag experience in FUSE Amy and I are even. If you add in the total experience I have there is a vast difference and most of those tag titles I was never defeated for. Timo knows full well what I bring to the table. Want some more garlic fingers? Derrick: Sure. Grabbing a napkin Gideon takes a few of the pineapple garlic fingers and sets them down then passes the tray to Derrick who takes a few. Gideon eats one before continuing. Gideon: I’ve been curtain jerking since I came back and I’m not even on the radar after several matches. I won my match against Rollins. In that cluster fuck 8 man battle royal I lost ground and that match made me look bad. I faced Gunnar Saint and the only reason I was in that match was to make sure he never came back to FUSE ever again. I don’t mind being the go to guy but facing a guy like Gunnar Saint does nothing. His only contribution was pissing in the middle of a ring on a PPV for F$%k sakes. The only positive was it was a great spot for Zakk to debut. Derrick: Is there anything you can tell me about that? Gideon: Then I faced Randall that night and I was pissed. He has no heart, no drive, no work ethic, nothing that resembles a guy that shouldn’t have been fired 4 times over. He didn’t want to be in FUSE and just like with Gunnar Saint I was put against him to lay in a very stiff beatdown. Matt Randall makes me owe so many useless lazy assholes an apology because he has them topped by far. Reaching across the table Gideon starts spooning the Caesar chicken salad and spaghetti on his plate. After eating a fork full Gideon is ready to continue. Gideon: I was there ready to go after the match Randall walked away from like a coward. That match could have reminded everyone what I can do. It wasn’t like I was winded or hurt from the match. I never even broke a sweat. I’ll go one better Matt Randall never even broke a sweat. He will never admit it even to himself but he went with Amy because that was better for his career. She was a main eventer while I was curtain jerking. Derrick: Have you talked to him about it? Gideon: No. He is doing his own thing and Zakk and I will do our thing. Derrick: Off the record now to talk about Smitty? You can see the look on Gideon’s face that he has a bit of regret but also anger. Gideon sticks his fork in the salad and is eating trying to clear his mind a bit. Gideon: Smitty and I have had a pretty decent relationship. I like the guy and he puts up with a lot of shit. I’ve been there and I know the type of shit you deal with baby sitting some of these egomaniacal prima donnas. I’m angry. I’m 4 and O in FUSE and it doesn’t matter in the slightest. I might as well be invisible. When I said those words the thing that crossed my mind was this "Am I invisible now you cocksucker!". He didn’t deserve that…… I’ll spin it as me acting and trying to draw more heat. The calls I’ve been getting about that shoot have had my phone ringing off the hook so it definitely drew heat. Even if I lie to Smitty about it he knows that I intended to hurt him. He will give me the benefit of the doubt because he knows that isn’t my style and that I like him. I’ll have to have a sit down with him sometime. There is a somber tone as they sit there eating the rest of the food. There is a bit more small talk and Derrick hears about Ricky learning martial arts from Gideon as well as things going on in the Whittaker home. The details about what he is doing this week are a tad surprising. ################################################ The bedroom of Gideon is silent as he is resting for the night. It has been a busy day. He has done a movie deal that will have him making 14.5 million. He has seeded 17 false rumors on the Internet. He has talked to Derrick and trained with Neil for his upcoming tag match. Most importantly he has talked to Smitty. It was a short meaningless call but it was important for what wasn’t said. There is more things to do of course. Gideon and Zakk have a huge shock planned when they announce the Black List and the man that is the poster child for Black Listing joins them as Gideon’s manager and PR man. The wall mirror stands vigil watching over Gideon as he sleeps and remembers these words. "Hide any weakness…… Even from yourself".
