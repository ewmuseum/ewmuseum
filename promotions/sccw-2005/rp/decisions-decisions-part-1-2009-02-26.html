Decisions, Decisions (Part 1)

26 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/2/09 (In the locker room following the match at Maryland… for SCCW’s online extra video) (We fade this promo in with the devil’s rejects Legion and Luci both showing feelings of exhilaration, the sacrifices throughout the past year, all the manipulation of wrestlers in NFW, the constant put downs and being held down by ‘legends’ claiming their glory with Mayfield in that same company had all come to this: THIS was his first shot at glory for him since returning from the wilderness of ‘tribute acts’) Legion: Can you believe it? For someone that the internet had said showed signs of improvement I proved them completely wrong by not only out wrestling him but dominating him in the process, those small bursts of comeback he showed I LET him do and tonight the whole world has seen the true Legion: One that I thought had been lost. Luci: May I add unlike certain women that seem fit to get involved when the moment strikes them to go all ‘Look at me, Look at me’ round here and doing other things to get attention (she winks) I let the wrestling do the talking in that ring and I will send a warning to Minorez for when the match comes: The only way Bridden will win will be if he suddenly prays to whatever deity he chooses and asks for a miracle because if you try even ONCE to cost Legion the victory I will unleash a fury not seen yet in SCCW and that is a promise! (After the cameras are off) Legion: I wonder if Alex or Dusk were watching tonight, you caught the mission statement from our superversal champion? Luci: Yeah, I’ve always had a thing for how she at least takes no shit, something I know all too well, speaking of which did you send the info to Fiona Love about the Indy NFW show? Legion: Yes, I’ve told her not to tell certain people about the plan… Teresa took it too far this time and the striking back will be nothing short of chaos. (Suddenly there is a knock at the door, the door opens and an intern shows up) Intern: Mr Legion- Legion: Please, no mister kid, Legion will do just fine Intern: I’ve been told to tell you that Dr Kensington has changed plans, instead of Sin On Spike your match against Bridden is next week. (Legion and Luci suddenly smile) Luci: Why thank you, what’s your name? Chris: Chris Legion: Chris, for providing with that information (he pulls out a ten dollar bill) get yourself a drink on me! Chris: Thank you! (Chris then leaves the room) Luci: Explain to me why we had to do that just now? Legion: Because I want the place to think I’m playing the right game just a little longer, the world of Sin City needs heroes does it not? When the time comes to make the decision then they’ll know… Luci: Is this because of the crowd a few weeks ago? Normally you just brush it off when the cameras aren’t on, besides there’s two people you haven’t spoken to… Legion: Yes and with one of them I dread to think how she feels given the fact I’m proving her Ace isn’t exactly so next week and she’ll probably hate it if I win (he winks again) Luci: Are you saying…? Legion: Give it one more week to let the others try, either way it’s their own fault for underestimating me like everyone else. The true nature of chaos shall be unleashed soon and when it does one side shall be weakened severely. (A couple of days after the show an e-mail was sent to Legion’s account… when he opened it contained a picture of a deck of cards… he thought at the time it was a fan making some promo art for the next show… but if you looked closely there was a mark of ‘K’…)
