Cherish Your Life

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today *Flashback to Equilibrium* Legion: Phone you? Believe me... knowing you Jason you probably would have hung up as soon as either myself, Luci or Savant called the number! Isolation doesn't work JASON, you need to realize that before somebody close to you gets KILLED. *flash edit* Draven: You had better watch your tongue little man. I will kill you. I WILL NOT FUCKING SAVE YOUR ASS AGAIN. YOU TALK TO ME LIKE THAT ONE MORE TIME. Draven holds Legion there with the cast that is on his left arm, and gets closer. Draven: I will destroy you. Do…You…Got…That! At this point, Legion pushes Draven away and takes him down with a double leg through a table. Legion: THAT's who I wanted to see tonight... the hate flowing through you... you have some potential left Jason but next time you try and take me down like that, expect worse. Draven, not wanting to let words do his talking, pops his hips, is able to turn over and bring Legion to his back. The former Modern Day Warrior jams the cast beneath Legion's chin. Legion reaches up and grabs at Draven's t-shirt, tearing it as he’s hit under the eye by a hammerfist. At this point, Mary Jackson, Krissy Jamison and Charlotte Ramone rush in to try to pry Draven off of him. Mary: Jay...Jay...JAY! Get off. GET OFF! Draven releases his grip and finally gets up as Luci and Savant come in to check of Legion, who is now bleeding under his eye. Krissy and Charlotte pull Draven away as Mary looks around and surveys the damage. Mary: Damn it, Jay. Legion shows a smile similar then to the one a few weeks ago, Luci mutters under her breath of next time he tries that on you, as she says that Legion shouts across the room: "THE CHAOS IS RISING!" and laughs maniacally as we cut away… Of course due to other events going on throughout the night (the end of THE STREAK being one of them) we never got to see the reaction by the Devil’s Advocate, the Orchid and Luci4 at least until now… We see Luci and Savant picking Legion up after Draven’s attack, the manic laughter starting to slowly subside as he is carried back to the trio’s locker room and when they enter he is placed down onto the bench situated in the center of the room. Luci and Savant’s faces are one of concern, they had never seen Legion show the manic style of laughter that Luci has exhibited in the past, was it an act done to intimidate Draven or did the fragile ends of sanity suddenly decide to start giving up destroying the stoicism that he so far had portrayed in Sin City quite well after losing the chance at another livewire title shot? The doubts in the duo’s mind would only be answered when he began to speak… Legion: That went exactly as I wanted it to go, phase one of Chaos is complete down to the smallest detail. Luci shoots Legion a quizzical look. Luci: So Jason basically attacking both of us you’ve wanted to happen? I’ve known you for about nearly 3 years and I’ve NEVER seen you act like that… do you want us to be portrayed as lunatics? That’s supposed to be von Doom’s job! As soon as the word von Doom is mentioned, well everyone’s seen how our new champion Wyatt Connors gets that little twitch in his eye when ever Desade is mentioned, this applies to Legion when von Doom is said. Savant: Never mind about the so called puppetmaster for one minute, you should be proud though; the other person I’ve only ever seen him this angry with was Alex. Besides this though it needs to be said that whatever the ‘emergency’ is can’t be what you Gabriel thought it was. Legion: So you don’t think he was faking it in order to teach me some sort of lesson towards my actions from a few weeks ago while he’s drinking in a bar somewhere? Luci: So what do you think we should do then? Stake-out Mary until she spills the truth? Legion suddenly raises his head with a smile on his face… Legion: You’ve just given me an idea! Lauren shall go and talk to Mary in order to gain the information, I don’t care which alias you decide to create, just get the job done. However there’s something else I need to talk to you about Lauren first… Did you know about Kathryn going back into the Spider’s clutches? Now as many people know Lauren Fox has learnt via her teacher in how to lie VERY convincingly and given there had been clandestine talks with Legion over recent weeks as part of his game she knew what to say here in order to avoid risking everything. Savant: No I didn’t, Kathryn’s been very different with her actions of late but what I saw tonight proved the old adage when it comes to her… Luci chimes in with a quick quip. Luci: Once a whore, always a whore? Savant (chuckles): If you want to say that one then fine, I was going for ‘a leopard never changes its spots.’ Legion: Glad to know you’re still on our side then… anyway I plan to watch the main event so if you excuse me. Legion decides to get up, at first he struggles due to the attack but under the combination of what is possibly willpower and adrenaline he stands upright and walks out of the room… A few days after the event… 24 hours before Sin*Con There are some nights where Legion decides to be alone, this night though he decided to go to a local rock bar in Tuscon to calm himself, even though he subscribes to stoicism he is still slightly nervous on whether Lauren managed to get any information. His train of thought is then interrupted by Mastodon’s Quintessence blasting off his Nokia N97, the caller ID saying Orchid. Legion: Hold on a second, I just need to get outside, you know how these clubs are. Legion then walks to a quiet place around the corner. Legion: You manage to get any information on Jason’s situation? Lauren: Mary was very tricky to gain any information from. She seems to be the only person in Jason’s life that knows about what truly happened, now call it intuition or the fact I’ve had shall we say a past with Jason but I’m thinking the emergency turned out to be something more personal, maybe somewhat tragic. The look on Legion’s face is one of shock but only for a brief moment as he suddenly laughs to himself… Legion: I see, maybe as a feeling of goodwill towards Mr Draven it’s time to initiate phase two . Lauren: Phase two? Legion: Well you know all about how plans work and by the by that was a very good lie to Luci the other day, having you as a double agent playing off Alex and Luci shall work wonders and makes it possible to do the next phase. Somewhere Lauren is surprised that Legion figured the lie out… given what he mentioned to her having an unleashed Luci would be the last thing on her mnd. Legion: You didn’t think I had thoughts about you possibly passing on the information to one of Alex’s minions, like you said a leopard never changes its spots, or to make my own phrase: the orchid always shows it’s full bloom when the time is right. Lauren: What is this phase two then? Legion: You remember I promised Jason the chance to get revenge on Roderick Ashe all those weeks ago? Legion’s eyes begin to narrow. I want you to work with Ashe to make sure it goes exactly as I want it, this shall benefit a lot of people. I’ll see you at Sin*Con, all the players shall know their places on the board soon enough. And with that we decide to leave the Chaos for another time…
