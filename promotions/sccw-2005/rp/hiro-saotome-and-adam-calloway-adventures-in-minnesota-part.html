Hiro Saotome and Adam Calloway adventures in Minnesota - Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today There comes a time in every man’s life when the decision is made to leave the house. Not a total separation from the family, while in some cases this may be true, but many times this is an exciting time for both the parents and the child. The parents are relieved their child is ready to head out into the world, hoping and praying their body of work becomes a productive member of society; carry on the family name and to achieve more than they could. It’s a lofty goal but a sincere one none the less. For the child this experience is a cocktail with parts of excitement, nerve wracking and fear that generates an aftertaste of freedom. There is a potential hangover, though, and that’s of fear. Adventure is around the corner, it’s always there, but it is up to the individual to take the steps to feel the excitement. Hiro’s move to Minnesota, on a whim, was a risky decision but one that was embraced and encouraged by his father. He earned a college degree – BA in Education – but was not ready to jump into the "real" world and start his career. Hiro Saotome is only twenty three years old…he’s got plenty of time to get his feet wet and his nose to the grindstone. Genma: Imagine the stories you’ll have if you actually make it. You’ve got a degree to fall back on and remember that the classroom is going to be a lot easier on you then wrestling. Genma Saotome, Hiro’s father, sits in a brown leather overstuffed chair. His light brown beard peppered with gray and white hairs only accentuates his olive skin and draws out his blue eyes. The years have not been kind to Genma. His hair is cut short to prevent the "Friar Tuck" look and his stomach is bloated from years of diabetes. His stomach is only accentuated by his "Balboa Park" tee shirt that is tucked into his grey sweat pants. Hiro: Yeah, who knows. If I don’t make it then I’ll get a job for a little bit and move back closer to you guys or something. I want to be here for you guys… Hiro sits in another brown leather overstuffed chair, next to his father, sipping from a can of Diet Coke. When living in the home of a diabetic its diet or it’s nothing. He’s wearing a pair of red mesh shorts and a matching "Balboa Park" tee shirt. As his voice trails he grimaces, glancing at his father and smiling. Hiro’s eyes soften as he looks at Genma – his mentor, his hero. Genma: You don’t have to move back home. God knows we don’t want to support you anymore! Hiro: Ha, ha, ha. I just want to stick around in case you guys need me for anything. Genma: Yeah, we appreciate that. You’re a good son. The two go silent, eyes focused on the old episode of Mission Impossible on the big screen. Genma pulls a blanket over his barrel chest and a homemade, black, nit skull cap from the end table. Sensing the growing tension from his son, Genma pulls the hat over his eyes and tilts his head back then looks at Hiro. Genma: Sup vato?! Why you look so sad? Emilio take your bitch? Hiro: Jesus, dad… Hiro and Genma share a laugh then go back to watching the famous fifties television show. Hiro glances over, watching as his father adjusts his skull cap and sink farther into the chair. His eyes soften when he looks at his father in this state because he cannot get out of his head how his father was when he was young. Genma was an athlete and an avid outdoorsman. He was a wrestler and football player himself but took more pride in his academic prowess then any athletic achievement. Hiro: You know dad I just want to be here for you and mom. Tatewaki doesn’t come down enough to help. You guys are getting old. Genma: Getting old? Hell, we are old! Seriously son, your mother and I are very proud of you and appreciate how much you want to help us. You’ve got to get out and live your own life. We’ll be here for you. Have you found a place to live? Hiro: I guess I’ll be rooming with another wrestler for a little bit while I’m trying out. If everything goes well then we’re off on the road. Genma shifts in his chair, smiling brightly, he looks over at Hiro. Genma: It’s going to be an adventure, Hiro. You’re going to have a lot of fun! Hiro: Hey, I’m Hiro. Adam: Adam Calloway, nice to meet you. Yep, that’s how it started. Nothing too flashy, just a casual conversation between two people who shared something in common, specifically their work. Despite his gruff exterior, amplified by his stocky features, goatee and constantly narrowed eyes and scowl, his voice paints another picture about his personality. In life you wear many hats, on stage Adam plays the role of a jerk who wears a Cowboy hat and hits people with golf clubs. How did this come about? The promoter saw that Adam was from the great state of Wyoming and played Varsity Golf in high school…so it was a logical fit. Same goes for Hiro. Being born in Japan and having a martial arts background automatically made him someone who was either going to be really-really good or really-really bad. Since he had a college degree and was easy on the eyes, not to mention being so green in the business, Hiro was given the easiest role of them all. Be the nice guy, the overly rich nice guy despite the fact Saotome has a little over three hundred dollars in his bank account and about two hundred in his wallet. Hiro: So…what’s the plan? Adam: Well, I guess we’re going to do your try out tomorrow. After that if you’re in then we’ll be hitting the road. Hiro: Sounds like fun. So, uh, what is there to do around here? Adam: Drink. Watch some football. Mainly drink. Hiro: Interesting. Owatonna, Minnesota, is not the largest city in Minnesota but it’s not like the two are miles away from civilization. Saotome is use to the larger cities…Portland, San Diego and Tokyo while Adam is accustomed to places called Worland, Ten Sleep and the booming metro of Casper. Culturally speaking the two come from different worlds. Hiro’s idea of conservative thought is viewed by Adam as liberal whining while Adam’s perspective of the world is interpreted as narrow minded and backwards by Hiro. Amazing the two get a long, let alone lived with each other. Calloway’s cell phone rings, he looks at the screen, smiles, presses the talk button and brings the phone to his ear. Adam: What? I’ll talk to you however I want. Not much, just chillin. Sure, where at? Cool if I bring a friend? No, that’s bitch pop and I don’t drink bitch pop. Fine, bye. Adam ends the call and puts his phone back in his pocket. Adam: Remember how I told you that we drink a lot out here? Well here’s your first chance to experience it. Hiro: Oh, cool. I’m not that big of a drinker though. Believe it or not I’m allergic to beer. Adam: No shit? Hiro: Seriously. If I have it my throat closes up real quick. I drink the hard stuff but if I have too much too soon…well you know how it is. Adam: Yeah, you’re a fag who can’t handle his beer. Hiro: Fuck you, hick. Saotome’s comment draws a raised eyebrow from the Cowboy golfer. Adam: There’s a big difference between a hick and a redneck. I’m a redneck, not a hick. I don’t fuck my cousin or do shit like that. You know where they get the term redneck? Hiro: Nope. Adam: From working in the fields all day. Your neck gets red from all the sun. Salt of the Earth, man. Hiro: Interesting. So how do I know you don’t fuck your cousin? Adam: How can you drive those slanty eyes? I bet I look like I’m three inches tall. Ah, yes, the house party. The dynamics and rules at a house party are universal and strictly enforced. Don’t believe me? Try breaking some of the rules and see what happens. When at a house party you respect that it’s the person’s home. If beer is in the fridge you go ask for a beer UNLESS it is specifically expressed by the owner that it’s cool if you have free reign. You don’t use the bathroom in the owner’s bedroom, you don’t ask for food and if you make a mess you clean it up. If the keg is in the garage then hang out by the keg if you’re drinking. Nobody wants to clean up your messes if you’re spilling shit. Most importantly, though, at a house party there are always going to be a few girls and they get preferential treatment. If one happens to be the owner’s girl then you treat her with the most respect. A majority of the time a house party is going to be a sausage fest so if you run off one of the girls not only does it look bad on you but it normally pisses the owner off. Once the girls are gone why is he going to keep any of you around that he doesn’t know? Hiro is lucky that one of Adam’s buddies owns the house…and that Hiro is big enough to hold his own incase Adam’s buddy starts shit. Since he wasn’t going to be drinking any beer it was real easy to keep out of trouble and not to break any of the rules. Six foot three, well built Asian people always blend in at a house party in the Midwest. Adam and Hiro arrive at the party and immediate split ways, mainly because Adam ditched him to talk with his friends. Hiro takes a seat on the couch and watches some TV while snacking on a bag of chips that happened to be on the coffee table. Female Voice: Hey! Where’s your drink? Hiro: I can’t drink beer and I didn’t see anything else. Female Voice: Oh, do you want a Coke or something? Hiro: Sure, thanks. Hiro stands and smiles at the young lady standing before him. As if an alarm went off Adam walks over and playfully punches the girl in the arm. Female: Ouch! Asshole. Adam: Psh, I know you can take it. You don’t want to talk to him. He’s a child molester. Hiro: Fuck you, Calloway. Female: Oh, this is the guy? Adam: Yep. Hiro: Excuse me? Female: Oh, Adam here has told me all about you. Hiro: Only good things I’m sure. Adam: I told her that you like sushi, samurai swords and computers. Hiro: You forgot I’m great at math, too. Female: I’m Jeni. Hiro: Hiro. Jeni smiles, punches Adam in the chest then walks into the kitchen and starts to go through the fridge. Remember the rules? They don’t apply to girls. Like Adam, Jeni appears to be a little rough around the edges. She’s tall, around five foot ten, and athletic. She is wearing a gray turtle neck sweater that accentuates her features and a pair of fitted blue jeans. Her strawberry blonde hair is styled in one of those medium length messy-yet-professional styles. Hiro: So how do you know her? Adam: She’s a friend of mine from my home town. We went to high school together. Hiro: No shit? She wrestle? Adam: No, she’s going to school up here. Hiro: That’s cool. Thanks. Jeni hands Hiro the drink and puts her arm around Adam, it kind of looks funny since she is taller then Calloway. Jeni: So who can kick who’s ass? Adam: That’s easy, I can kick his ass. Hiro: I doubt it. Adam: Bring it. Hiro: Soon enough little man, soon enough. Adam: I eat Tempura for lunch. Hiro: I bet you do. Jeni: I don’t know Adam, I don’t know what’s getting bigger…your belly or your bald spot! Jeni runs off after the playful comment, laughing all the while. Adam smiles as he watches her run off. Adam: She cracks me up. Full of piss and fire. Hiro: She’s pretty cute too. Adam: …yeah…
