A woman in your head.

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/07 The scene opens to an old gym in the heart of inner City Pittsburgh. The building is dimly lit apart from a solitary bulb hanging over a wrestling mat in the middle of the floor. The run down lockers to one side are open, and empty, old crusty peelings of ex fighters adorn the walls, sometimes back from as early as the 30s. All mean looking, all severe. Old and busted gym equipment line the other walls, equipment which was probably used way back then by the men in the 30s. The gym was silent, apart from the rhythmic pounding of one man, in the ring, hitting each rope in turn and running back, while hitting the other. There is a loud bang as the door opens. A man dressed in an elegant pin striped suit, holding a leather attaché briefcase saunters into the room. His slimy smile, indicative of the wealth he has accrued over the years is on full beam as his gold watch, bracelet and neck chain gleam brilliantly in the early morning sunshine. His black hair, scraped back with more wax than Tussauds actually looks like a bowling ball. The only blemish on this man as far as anyone is concerned is that he is slightly plump, fat on the wealth he has cultivated throughout his career. He introduces himself to Andre, who is still pounding the ropes. “Mr. Jensen, hi, Cyril Smythe here, I am representing Mr Bishop in certain transactions, it’s time to set you up with your new home! I am guessing you are bored of hotel rooms?” Andre stops pounding the ropes at this point and nods to Smythe, not confirming either with a non committal grunt as he begins to perform stretching exercises. “Well anyway Mr Jensen, Mr Bishop wants you to be perfectly comfortable in this endeavour as you need to be the happiest person you can be in order to confirm his deal. Now we have properties we manage all over the country, where do you want to be based? Miami is nice this time of the year, we have beautiful beach properties up there, we also have properties in the delights of Beverly Hills, you can either live next door to Top Cruise or Gwyneth Paltrow, and they are very luxurious resorts. As part of your agreement with Bishop, he has taken the liberty of letting you live in those places for free for the year.” Andre takes a quick glance to Smythe as he performs a very difficult and painful looking split style stretch. “I aint a charity case, I can find my own place” “But Mr Jensen, Mr bishop has expressly instructed us to look after your affairs, consider me your personal manager, I will look after all aspects of your life which means you only have to look after the business of winning matches in FUSE.” “Plus, you get your own commission for doing this as well?? GO away please, I am NOT interested” Andre is keeping an air of calm, continuing his training and stretching in the ring while Smythe stands on the ring apron, pen and paper in his hand waiting for Andre to turn round and sign the paper he had taken out of his briefcase moments before, he obviously hadn’t heard Andre tell him to leave, or he had and was too arrogant to even consider it. After a while he breaks the silence, as Andre is just ignoring him to this point. “But Mr Bishop insists; it is part of your obligation to him.” Andre, now furious, loses his temper completely. ”WHAT DAMN OBLIGATION YOU SLIMEY LITTLE MAN? HE GOT ME IN FUSE, TO WRESTLE WITH PEOPLE WHO WEAR FAKE TAN AND HAVE STUPIDLY LONG HAIR. WOULD YOU JUST GO AND GET OUT OF HERE RIGHT NOW!” Taken aback, Smythe purses his lips and puts the papers back in his briefcase. ”So be it Mr Jensen, Mr Bishop shall hear about your gratitude. I will see you in a few days, and then you will be asking for my services.” Andre stares after Smythe until he hears the door bang behind him. He closes his eyes and takes a long deep breath before continuing his workout. 5 minutes later There is another bang. Andre stops his workout in mid flow to shout obscenities at the new entrant “DAMN IT SMYTHE, I TOLD YOU TO JUST GO AWAY! I DON’T WANT YOUR HELP; I DON’T NEED YOUR HELP!” A female voice answers, light and airy, yet with a businesslike tone which is used to authority and obviously getting their way. “I don’t think you do need my help, but you ARE trespassing on my family property, so either get out or explain yourself” Andre is quite shocked to hear this latest voice echo from the hallway, he hears footsteps walking toward him, high heeled shoes, businesslike stride, and this woman knows confidence just by her walk. He peers out into the shadows to see where the woman is. She has stopped just in the shadow still, he can see a faint outline of her, she seems slim and toned by her shadow. Her hands are on her hips, in a stance which can only be described as the sort of stance only a woman can make when a man is in deep trouble. Warily, Andre calls out to the shadows “And you are? I was told this gym had closed down; I needed some place to stay while I trained. I hope you don’t mind” It seems the softness and apparently sincere apologetic tone works with the lady, she softens her stance a little bit, moving forward into the light so Andre can see her. When he catches glimpse of her in the light his jaw drops. In front of him was a sharply dressed woman, whose dark brown hair is tied into a business like bun. Her features hold a distinctly Oriental look about her but in a western way. Her dark eyes light up with the sight of Andre wearing nothing but sweat pants and a light cotton vest. Momentarily distracted, she adjusts her grey mid length skirt and pulls her grey matching jacket a little bit straighter while she flushes and composes herself. Andre, at the same time sniffs around his armpits and then figures there is nothing he can do for himself, so continues to check out the woman in front of him, taking note of her slim figure and long legs. “Wow” he mutters to himself, and then takes a sip of water from his cooler in the ring corner to try and cool himself off slightly. The newcomer walks up to the steps by the side of the ring and Andre helps her into the ring from the bottom rope. When she is in the ring she faces Andre and puts out her hand ”Mian-yi Yeo, I own this place, well, my family does at least, my father was the sole owner until he passed away a few years ago and now it is split between my family. I wasn’t aware this place was being used” She looks around and spies a makeshift bedroom which Andre had set up in the corner; obviously he had been living here as well as training. “Especially by illegal immigrants.” Andre lets out the sort of noise you make when you have been busted and really don’t know what to say and you wonder just how the person knew already without even asking any questions, is it that obvious? “Don’t worry, I have seen more than enough immigrants in my time to see the signs, I have enough family who do it so you are safe.” Andre finally realises that he actually should be conversing with this lady, not that he knows how to mind – his life of riots, fighting and Marxist socialist movement by his family kind of means that he doesn’t really know how to speak to a lady, but what the hell, they are the same as men are they not? Just talk naturally. “What hulla, eh? Er, yeah. Andre Jensen, me I am.” He flushes as he realises he sounds like a 4 year old with a speech impediment, composing himself he speaks again “Sorry, I meant, Andre Jensen, hi, how are you” Mian-yi smiles warmly and lets out a soft laugh, “It’s ok Mr Jensen, that sort of reaction happens sometimes. Do you mind telling me where you are from and why you are in my Gym?” “Ah, well, that’s a good story, I should tell you properly sometime, but the short of it is, I am from England, I am in America with nothing and I start wrestling next week” “Oh, so simple then? Tell me, who was the guy I saw leave before I came in? Is he your manager? He seems a bit of a creep to be a wrestling manager.” Andre thinks for a second, the whole Smythe episode had actually gone out of his head momentarily and for a second he stares blankly at her, before suddenly remembering “Oh, him! I don’t know, he was some guy sent by some bloke in New York who got me a job, according to him I have his services but I really don’t need them, it is only wrestling after all, it’s all fake.” Mian-Yi weighs up Andre in her mind, judging him with the quick judge of around 20 seconds where you know all about a person and decide whether to carry on the debate or to stop there. She decides to carry on. ”Well, Mr Jensen, you would be surprised to know that what you have just said…. Well, it’s frankly a big bunch of crap.” Shocked by the outburst from Mian-Yi Andre takes a second to recover and retort. “Oh, how so?” Andre silently curses himself for the sheer rubbish ness of his retort, as he remembers he is in a gym, owned by this ladies’ family and it was full of wrestlers at one time or another, of course she will know about wrestling, probably more so than he does. “Wrestling and fighting has been in my family for generations Mr Jensen, from my great ancestors to modern day, my great grandfather came here in the 20s from China and set up this academy Tian’s wrestling academy, as you see on the wall. My grandfather took over the academy, my father took over from him, do you get the picture?” Conceding defeat, all Andre could do was nod in silence at this ticking off. “I presume you are here for FUSE’s show on Friday, your debut I guess?” “Yeah, apparently I am in something called a fatal four way? Tell you the truth, I don’t know much about wrestling, I just know how to beat people down.” “Aha, then you are going to need some help, let’s go through the rules of most of the matches you might actually encounter in FUSE.” Andre and Mian-Yi talk for hours about wrestling, she taking him through all the matches, the history of them, current champions and how much it means to many people, she also explains all about how the general conception is wrestling is fake because there are so many federations out that that actually do script things, but ones like FUSE are pure wrestling, and the grudges, feuds, fights and shows are real. Andre, for the better part is attentive, asking occasional questions about stars, shows and rules. Before they know it, it’s almost dark. “So in essence, I have to pin one of the other three guys to win, his shoulders have to be on the mat for 3 seconds?” AJ is responding to Mian-Yi explaining the fatal 4 way match, telling him what he needs to know about the match... “In this case, yes, and the three you are facing are no easy people. I saw you on Uproar the other day with that Bishop man, and you are way too dismissive of the whole thing, you seem to think that everything is scripted and these people won’t fight and you can take them out. Well it’s all good you having some sort of fighting doctorate and growing up surviving the riots or whatnot, but you really have to be aware that these men and women have been surviving in wrestling – that is much more dangerous than any outside thing. They are as tough as anything, and you will lose if your head isn’t right.” Andre scoffs at the suggestion that surviving in wrestling is harder than surviving in a riot fuelled city, but again he is curtailed by a stern gaze from Mian-Yi. “You are serious?” “Completely serious Mr Jensen, you had better concentrate, because if you slip up in FUSE once, if you dismiss summarily the competition then you are never going to make it.” Andre reflects on this warning by Mian-Yi as she stands up to leave. Snapping back into focus he stands up with her. “Oh, you are leaving?” ”Yes Mr Jensen, I will see you around, pleased to meet you. You can stay in the gym while you are here for the show, but I do suggest you get somewhere to live that isn’t my gym. One more thing you need to know is that you can’t be a fully functional wrestler and win matches if you are living out of a bag. Get stable, be patient and everything will come. Oh and have a bit of fun, you were too tense last week.” Andre nods and they shake hands again, he helps her out of the ring once more. “What are you doing Friday night Ms….Mrs…...” “It’s Ms; I am on a date on Friday night Mr Jensen, why do you ask?” “Oh, no reason, thanks for all your help Ms Yeo” “It’s Mian-Yi.” She flashes a smile at Andre as she walks away from the ring and out of the gym. Andre smiles to himself, in the knowledge that after 3 weeks of being in America, he finally has met someone who isn’t out to screw him over; well at least he doesn’t think so. He stares after her long after the door had closed, thinking over what he had gone through today with her. He was so lost in this thought that he didn’t notice Smythe come back in with a couple of Jimmy the bishop’s no necked henchmen. “Mr Jensen, you are going to reconsider our offer now. We need you to co-operate immediately, or there are repercussions. It is simple, you sign here for us now, we can set up a nice home, I get commission, you get a place to stay, and Jimmy gets his undivided support from his new FUSE star. What do you say?” Andre thinks for a second, then smiles at Smythe, talking in careful considered tones. “You know what? You tell Mr Bishop, that I will take his offer of a house, I don’t need your counsel though, I have someone already who will help me out in my affairs. I am sure they would do a much better job than you. Tell Mr Bishop I am grateful for his support and sponsorship, but to remind him of our agreement that all the decisions I take in my career are mine and mine alone. If he doesn’t like that, tell him I am contracted to FUSE and not Mr Bishop. Rest assured he will get his star in FUSE, but he is going to do it in his own way.” “I will pass on the message, but he won’t be pleased. It’s why he sent George, and Bungle here with me to………… well, convince you, so to speak.” He snaps his fingers to summon the attack, quick as a flash, Andre flies at George with a Mui Thai flying knee to the chest, knocking him senseless, while spinning round to Bungle with a quick forearm, breaking his jaw immediately. Smythe’s eyes become wide; he stands, paralysed in fear looking at Andre who smiles back at him. “I think I will take a house here in Pittsburgh Mr Smythe, please arrange it and tell Jimmy that I will be grateful if he keeps away from me and lets me do the job he wants me to do” Picking up his kit bag, Andre walks out of the gym, whistling the tune “Just A Day” by Feeder, he will start his career in FUSE with the optimism and the bounce of a man who has just found a woman who he connects with, if only for a few hours. Now all he has to do is find her and convince her that she wants to stay with him………
