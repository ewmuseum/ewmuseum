UNiversity Life.

3 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/12/07 "Ok class, be seated. Today we have a special presentation from one of the main stars right now in Fuse wrestling. Here he is, Mr Andre Jensen!" This is the sound of the not so true but flattering introduction anyone gives to anyone if they are presenting them to an audience when they are due to give a speech. I suppose it sounds better than "Here is that fella from Fuse who no-one quite knows but ah well, he has to speak to you anyway" Andre is at a university today, Phoenix University to be exact. Quite why, it depends on who you ask really. Andre would class it as the first step on his tour to Educate America, the class would class it as something to do, and the teacher would class it as Clinton Sage being unavailable. Either way, however it goes down, AJ is all set to give his speech. He is standing at his pulpit, beaming smile on his face. (You know the type of smile only a gameshow host or an idiot could pull off, let’s just say AJ doesn’t know what "the price is right is".) "Good morning students" says AJ in a loud, confident voice. "I am here to talk to you about America" There is a collective groan as the students kind of realise what is going to happen to them in the next half an hour or so, bored senseless over patriotic drivel or something about forefathers and rights. "You see, America is one of those countries with great power and wealth." AJ pumps his chest out as he surveys the crowd. "However, it doesn’t have as much wealth as me, being the richest guy ON THE PLANET and all, and what do people do with wealth? Yes American boys and girls check under your seats, go on, you will find a treat." The collective shifting, grinding of chairs and rustling of things makes a hotchpotch of various sounds, a lot like Bjork on a good day. "As you see, I have gone and distributed my wealth, you will all have a thousand dinaris attached to the underside of your chair. You can thank me later." There is a small murmur of approval followed by a larger exclamation of surprise and a hundred raised hands in front of AJ. AJ points to one student in the front row who has a big confused expression on his face. "Mr Jensen, what is this?" He holds up a piece of paper from which the dinaris were attached to. AJ smiles and points to the paper he holds in his hand. "Thank you for asking Mr?" "Smith" "Mr Smith, thank you. That is a contract for you all, if you fulfil the terms on that contract I am offering to pay you all your scholarship for university and whatnot, it’s the opportunity of a lifetime!" "And what is in the contract" "AH! Good question!" AJ presses play on a remote control in his hand and calls for the lights to be dimmed. A video begins to play from the projector onto the screen behind AJ’s podium. The video is an old black and white stock manual camera effect video of soldiers marching and having a laugh, messing about and generally doing what soldiers do on campaign videos. On the video there is commentary from some up beat chirpy voice over guy. "Are you tired of being called names at school? Are you fed up of moralistic crusades against our homeland? Are you sick of terrorist action being taken against innocent countries? Well if so, this is the place for you!!!" The video continues in the same grainy black and white vein of soldiers shooting and things of other soldiery nature. "We don’t discriminate against any race colour or creed, unlike the imperialistic American scourge." The atmosphere out of the room suddenly took a dive as a hundred breaths suddenly all were taken in shock. "We are taking the fight to our freedom and the freedom of the world by routinely targeting their awful campaign of terror come and join us if you want a free earth!" The video ends with a picture of Osama bin Laden with winking eyes and his thumbs up in some sort of fonz like pose in a rosette – underneath this is a logo saying "Join the Taliban, fight the evil" The lights go up and AJ turns around to the students with a big smile on his face "Any questions?" One furious looking linebacker type shouts from the back of the room "What is your problem you ass!?! AJ looks back to him and smiles "I know, the video is a bit corny but there you go" A girl pipes up "Its not that you cretin, are you trying to recruit for the Taliban?" "Of course, that’s why I am here, consider the money an advance on your salary, there is plenty more to come." "Why would we want to fight in a war against America, for the Taliban?" Asks one particularly spotty student "For cash? You are American, you would do anything for a fortune, 1,000 Dinaris is a good start, and if you sign up today then you get your scholarship paid. What other employer can offer that good a service?!!" "It isn’t even that much though! 1,000 dinaris is 82 cents." "Where did you get your information from?" "I just googled it on my laptop…But" AJ cuts him off with a hand up, while he picks up his cell phone and puts it to his ear. While he was waiting for the answer he looks at the student at the back and asks for his name. to which he get a response of Edgar "Yeah Mike….. I’m ok…………. Look do me a fav……….ah yeah that’s right……..Google…….a billion? Ok deal……….and do me a favour as well, I am going to sms you something, can you do it for me? Ta" AJ hangs up, and then sends his SMS while students look on in a mixture of confusion and anger. Within 30 seconds AJ gets a reply "Right smartass, check google now." There is a tapping of keys as the kid with the laptop goes to google. "Hah, very funny, now whenever you go to Google all it says is Edgar, the guy at Phoenix university who asks stupid questions is actually his own dad." "Yes, I bought Google just to do that." "What, because you are the richest guy in the world?" "THE PLANET!" "No it isn’t, the world is like small compared to the planet – idiot. Check it on Google if you don’t believe me" There is a groan at the back as someone who obviously finds this too much to bear lets it out. AJ picks up on this and stares the bloke down "What are you groaning at lardo? Is a planet not bigger than the world?" "I am staying out of this one dude, you go do what you want" "Oh I will, I have the cash to prove it." "What? That didn’t even make sense" "So’s your face" "Dude, what is your problem?" "Your face" "What?" "Never mind" Seeing this start to get out of hand, the teacher begins to get involved. "Ok, I think we have taken enough of Mr Jensen’s time, thank you for coming Andre" "Wait I haven’t finished yet, I want to talk about the recruitment drive!" The teacher looks at a security guard who is standing guard outside the room, the kind of look which says, help, or people are going to kill. Dutifully, the guard walks into the room and slowly but surely starts to edge AJ out of the room as AJ begins to rant. "America is evil, we need good hard working people in our cause, we give great pensions and benefits as well as shares!" His voice is getting quieter and quieter as he is taken away from the class room. The last thing anyone hears him say is something to do with winning a title at fuse and helping the cause that way.
