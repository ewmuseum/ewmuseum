Drama Queen

21 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/07/07 "Advice is seldom welcome; and those who want it the most always like it the least.." Lord Chesterfield "You aren’t even paying attention to me, are you?" Paul Cain is hanging upside down which might in fact be the dead giveaway that his attention is elsewhere. Both feet are wrapped around a beam connected by two upright posts and his hands are clasped together behind his head with his hair flopping down. Beads of sweat glisten on his smooth body with his lower half covered by black sweat pants. With a deep breath, he restarts his sit-ups as he allows the sentence to linger in his head before coming up with a suitable response. After a number of reps and a healthy dose of suspense, Cain finally decides it’s appropriate to reply. "I was…[the groan of effort interrupts]…But then you lost me at the…[another groan of effort]…ridiculous notion that…[heave ho]…I’m suppose to grow a tail…[one last rep]…Stick it between my legs…" Paul then unwraps his feet and jumps down to the floor, landing in front of his 6 foot 8 African American companion. "Then run like hell because of one name…C’mon now…You know me better than that D." Damien Kahn folds his tree trunk arms and shakes his head. "I’m not wrong…It IS him." A look of complete confusion crosses Paul’s face as he picks up his towel from the wooden floor of his very own gymnasium, a tribute to his Championship days with pictures of glory around the walls. They can take your belts but they can’t take your memories was Paul’s theory in a brief moment of brooding self-pity. Now though Paul was invigorated and full of self confidence which left him completely mystified by this solemn concern etched on the face of his old friend. "So let me get this straight D…Here you are…A man who I bailed out of jail after he near beat three guys to death all in one night…Someone who had no fear about using his fists and then his brain later…A guy who held no fear…Now you are standing on that very spot of wood telling me, Paul Cain, that I should be shitting bricks because of the slim possibility that this Prince Pride might HAPPEN to be the son of a washed up champion who is probably wrapped up in a tattered blanket on a sidewalk somewhere…Bent over and trying to remove that annoying little parasite Leonard Aarons from his ass before going to the doctors and asking for an STD test…I always had my suspicions on how Aarons got his push… Paul rubs his chin thoughtfully then quickly shakes his head. "You’re getting too flustered amigo, I mean c’mon what IF you’re right? Nobody in FUSE is going to give a damn about Prince Pride’s family tree and as for "The Foundation" himself, nowadays he’s broken down, as useful as a chocolate teapot!" He stretches out his left hand and lays it on Damien’s shoulder. "You need a vacation." With frustration, Damien swats his friends hand away with a look of complete exasperation. "I’m telling you RIGHT now, there’s something wrong here! Don’t you find it just a TINY bit strange that you turn up in FUSE and five minutes later so does the son of a man who you had just a SMALL amount of trouble with three years ago?! There’s a coincidence and then there’s THIS!" Paul Cain leans against the centerpiece of the gymnasium, an old wrestling ring used for sparring and positional work. He looks up to the ceiling and then straight into the eyes of Damien Kahn with focus and forcefulness. "Only a short few months ago, my family was being stalked and pursued by a sexually depraved maniac known as Michael Sloan. The things he threatened to do to them were worse than anything you would have heard in prison, Damien. He was relentless and the things I wanted to do to him, I couldn’t because of the confines of the law and the company I was with. Do you know what that can do to a man? Do you know how crazy that can make him when his own family is under attack? Only now are they safe and I can get back to doing what I was put on this earth to do. So forgive me if I’m not running around like a headless chicken worrying about chasing a random shadow from my past that just MIGHT have a connection to my present. It’s just another name and he’s just another ‘thing’ I have to deal with." Looking at the floor, Damien resigns himself to being the second fiddle once again with no voice of his own or freedom to his own opinion. "Just watch yourself, something doesn’t feel right." Paul smiles to himself with pride as he sees the doors to the gym open up and his wife steps in with their child in her arms. She is looking stunning as usual with her long brown hair tumbling down past her shoulders and her dress hugging a curvaceous body that made every single man look twice. They kissed deeply before Paul took a moment to stroke the head of his baby girl. He winked at Damien as he picked up his towel with a grin. "I’m the man that’s got it all, gorgeous wife, beautiful little girl, the house, the money, the power, I’m not scared of a washed up family name…Spread the word, the real Paul Cain’s back and if another Pride wants to get in my business…Well, he can join Daddy on the sidewalk when I’m all done…Now if you don’t mind amigo…My people await…" He smiles and slaps Damien on the back as he heads towards the doors. Kahn simply watches him leave before uttering around his breath: "Some things never change."
