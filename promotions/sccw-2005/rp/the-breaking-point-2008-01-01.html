The breaking point

1 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 1, 2008 There comes a time in every man’s life where they reach a point where they are broken. It doesn’t have to be physical like an injury or even the wear and tear life has inflicted on them. It can simply be mental as well. Gideon has reached this point. Some might say his love of wrestling died when NEW closed. Or when NEW reopened and the owner blacklisted him. This was despite all the contributions he has made and the fact that he has earned his spot. Mabye his love of wrestling died when he helped Craig Clayborn and Zakk Wylde reopen NEW for what would be the last time. Maybe it was the time Neil and Gideon were in Prime. Then again maybe it was the meltdown that caused Neil to retire from wrestling during their run in FUSE. You can guess all you like but only Gideon know the answer and he isn’t saying anything. While normally a very talkative man Gideon does not like talking about his feelings and that causes problems. When a man like Gideon is not grounded and doesn’t care he is a dangerous man. The skills that Gideon has in unarmed combat are well documented and known across the globe. The name the Osaka Assassin isn’t just a colorful name created by a handful of media consultants to give him a colorful gimmick. Gideon Fontaine is the Osaka Assassin outside the ring in real life. A few weeks ago he was in a Battle Royal and he was hit by an inspiration. An awakening of something he long tried to ignore. Wrestling is dead and has been dead for years. The men that paved the way and died over the years mean nothing now to the average fan. Their broken bones and the shattered lives of their friends and family are forgotten within day and even hours. You can give up all hope. You can give up everything you hold dear. You can do whatever you need to regardless if you lose your soul doing it You can complain and think that will matter. Or you can plan revenge. They say revenge is a dish best served cold. Whether or not this revenge will be hot or cold is debatable. The one thing that is guaranteed is that Payback will most definitely be a bitch. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Fontaine Studios 10 miles outside L.A. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Gideon and Caleb Alan can be seen sitting down on chairs and enjoying the weather. Gideon is smiling and Caleb is a tad distracted ogling some of the strippers Gideon has here for the wrestling segment he will be shooting. This has been planned and the budget for this is excessive to say the least but it will be worth it if this goes as Gideon wants it to. Caleb: This seems like an awful lot of effort for the match you have. I don’t think the match itself would even take a fraction of the time you invested just in the special effects alone. Gideon: I know I miss the old days when you could simply look at the mike and say things like. This match I have with insert name here is going to be the longest day of his life when I hit him with the insert colorful finisher name here and go onto be the number one contender for insert title here. I don’t even respect Gunnar Saint enough to bother going through the routine. Caleb: So what is the plan? Gideon: Well given that wrestling is dead I think I will simply do a segment on whatever I feel is important at this time or random things not pertaining to wrestling in any way. This is going to be a bit of a shoot on all the ridiculous gimmicks in wrestling. When Gideon gets up from the chair he walks over to the other side of a door and waits for his cue. You can see several men in costumes and cameras getting in position. Things are set up to look like the hallway of an apartment building. The narrator is preparing his lines and the director starts giving the countdown. 5… 4… 3… 2… 1……… Antoine: ACTION!!! "This is the start of a very bad day for Gideon Fontaine. He has just recently returned home after fighting off an alien invasion in Wyoming and was tired. It was a battle that lasted 3 days and 17 hours and ended when Gideon decapitated the head of the last one with a long since discarded Paul Anka CD. He looked in the fridge of his brownstone apartment and realized that the worst thing that could ever happen has just happened. This will make Wyoming look tame. The milk in the fridge was spoiled and he had to go to the store to get more." The door opens into the hallway and Gideon walks through. On the outside are 5 ninjas. They have a variety of weapons. Gideon is smiling as he holds up a finger for them to wait. He goes back into the apartment and all of a sudden "Kung Fu Fighting" by Carl Douglas starts playing and Gideon is back in the hallway. Gideon: Everybody is Kung Fu fighting Bitches!! The sounds echo through the hallway and Gideon is jumping through the air at the first Ninja with a jumping sidekick which is evaded then the 5 of them surround Gideon. With a forearm block Gideon deflects a roundhouse kick of one and tosses him at the next guy. As Gideon jumps over the heads of the 2 rushing him he kicks off their backs and land on his feet with a somersault flip. "Everybody was Kung Fu Fighting." Gideon elbows one in the jaw koing him and then backfists the man beside him koing him. "Those cats were fast as lightning." After a sidestep Gideon jumping knees the 3rd man in the side of the head koing him him. "In fact it was a little bit frightening." As those words echo through the hallway Gideon. Gideon hip throws one upside down into a wall and superkicks the last man under the jaw koing him. A man in a red Star Trek uniform comes out of a door. Gideon goes back into his apartment and comes out with a CD he snaps in half. Gideon: I hate that [BLEEP]ing song. Sorry about the noise Kenny… LOOK OUT!! Before Gideon could push Kenny out of the way there is a ninja star buried in the center of his chest and you can see blood seeping between his fingers. Gideon: Oh my god!! You killed Kenny!! You BASTARDS!! Gideon grabs the ninja by the neck and drives him head first through a wall. He sees Kenny motioning for Gideon to come closer. Kenny: I’m not going to die a virgin am I…… Gideon: I’m not going to lie to you Kenny. Your wounds are bad and I’ve never heard of a Trekkie getting laid in 27 years. You can see Kenny shudder as he breathes his last breath. Gideon turns you see a tear rolling down his cheek and he stands there with a look of rage in his eyes. Gideon: He was my neighbor and he died not knowing what a breast looked like in real life. I make this vow on all that I hold dear. When I get my milk it will be 2% so his death isn’t in vain!! ……… CUT!!! With a snicker Gideon tosses the Visine bottle on the floor and walks over to where Caleb is waiting off camera. Gideon: So next part in 35 minutes Antoine? Antoine: The special effects crew should be set up by then for your battle with the vampires Mr. Fontaine. Gideon: That Visine works wonders. You could almost feel the emotion as the tear rolled down my cheek. Caleb time to go to catering and get a beverage. Gideon and Caleb walk up and are laughing as Gideon is explaining the next 2 parts that are going to be shot. To say that Gideon is annoyed and shooting from the hip is an understatement. Normally this would annoy him to be doing something like this. Now he simply does not care and is only doing things that amuse him. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Fontaine Studios 35 minutes later. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Gideon is in an alley backdrop. There are several men in what looks to be cheesy 70s style robot suits. There are also some people dressed up in Vampire garb as well. There are about 10 of them and Gideon is smiling as the director is explaining what is going on to the stuntmen. The Narrator is looking over his lines. As they are getting ready the director grabs his seat and starts counting down 5… 4… 3… 2… 1…… Antoine: ACTION!!! "Gideon Fontaine has seen his neighbor Kenny killed before his eyes. The resolve can be seen in his eyes and you can see that he is not going to be stopped. The Vampire minions of Lord Darke Darkstone have found Gideon and they have him surrounded." Gideon: Are you Goth or Emo? I really can’t tell the difference. Minion #4: We are the elite guards of Lord Darkstone. We will sink our teeth into your throat and drink your blood. Gideon: Blood sucking parasites defiling all things living wallowing in human misery and death. So you are Republicans. There is a hiss of anger and the 6 of them are attacking Gideon. "Bodies" by Drowning Pool starts playing and Gideon is springing into action and jumping into the middle of them. Gideon is taking the first out with a Roundhouse kick with his right leg and jumping side kicks a second dropping him. "Let the bodies hit the floor……" "Let the bodies hit the floor……" Gideon: For the love of god what is up with that bloody song being played every fight scene?! With a burst of speed Gideon drops the third with a backfist and the fourth with a back elbow to the throat. "Let the bodies hit the……" "FLOOR!!……" Gideon: I didn’t mind it in Jet Li’s the One. It fit and it was actually semi fresh. I started getting annoyed when it started being played in Triple X or Triple X State of the union. Then it started being used by at least one wrestler in every single federation. "Return what for…" "Can’t take much more…" Gideon grabs the head of the fifth one and snaps his neck with a twist. As the last goes to sink his fangs into Gideon he is headbutted and dropped. Gideon: Let the bodies hit the floor… Yay… By the way FUSE fans in case you were wondering Jorge Samuelsson’s theme was "Bodies" by Drowning Pool. Yes even FUSE wrestling is not immune to having someone come to the ring by "Bodies" by Drowning Pool. "As Gideon Fontaine stands there celebrating his narrow victory against the forces of darkness……" Gideon: Narrow victory my ass! They stood as much chance as the Cowboys do of winning without T.O. "Now the smart assed Gideon Fontaine stands there silently contemplating his next move and how rude it is to interrupt people while they are talking. A clank of metal rings in the air as 2 elite humanoid killing machines come into view. They are the products of Aggrotech Robotics and have been targeting Gideon Fontaine." Gideon: Why are these robots attacking me again? "No idea. It’s a very crappy script and I’m getting a drink." With a shrug Gideon advances on the 2 robots. With a blur of motion Gideon attacks the first with a right, left, right, left, and a right uppercut dropping the first one. With 3 right shin kicks to the chest of the second robot and a left shin kick to the head of the second robot it drops too. Gideon: I am Gideon Fontaine, I am the Osaka Assassin, I am the most dangerous man you will ever face, and I won’t be denied. Before the sun rises I will have my milk and it will be 2%. SO… I… SWEAR!! ……… CUT!!! Gideon: You know the narrator had a point. This script doesn’t make much sense. Antoine: Have you seen wrestling segments these days? Half the writers are high and/or coked up. Most of them are insane on a good day and that is after taking their Prozac and Ritalin. Gideon: Good point. With a snicker Gideon walks off. Antoine: You have 20 while the next scene is set up. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Fontaine Studios 20 minutes later. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Gideon is standing outside a convenience store as the crew is setting up. As they are getting ready the director grabs his seat and starts counting down 5… 4… 3… 2… 1…… Antoine: ACTION!!! "It is near over. The long ordeal is just 15 feet away from being completed. Kenny paid the ultimate price for Gideon to get this far. Now he stands before these doors and a movie poster of an Adam Sandler movie is on the door a perfect backdrop for the pain and suffering he has gone through." Gideon walks into the convenience store and goes to the cooler and grabs a gallon of milk. A siren goes off and lights start flashing. He looks around in confusion. 2 large chested women come from behind the counter. One takes the milk from Gideon and the other giggles. Darlene: You are our millionth customer!! The women start kissing and stoking each other’s bodies. With a giggle they remove each other’s shirts and bras and reveal their breasts for the cameras. They are pixelated to the camera audience of course as they start pouring the milk all over their bodies. Caleb Alan stands next to Gideon smiling and looking at the 2 ladies. Caleb: They are pretty remarkable breasts. Gideon: Of course you realize that was the last of the milk don’t you? Caleb: It was? Gideon: Oh well. Ladies what do you say we go out and get something to eat. I think you might want a foot long to hold you over until we get to the restaurant. Caleb: You do realize that they won’t air that even on Showtime. Gideon: True but I’m pretty sure Rob Black would. Lets face it it is only going to be a year or 2 before wrestling officially becomes porn anyway. As the cameras fade to black the laughter of the 2 men can be heard. ……… CUT!!! Gideon: You know I really should have mentioned Gunnar Saint’s name. Caleb: Why? Gideon: Well I think it could have been the greatest wrestling segment in the history of the universe. It has Ninjas, evil robots, Vampires, naked breasts for no other reason then to amuse me, "Bodies" by Drowning Pool, A Star Trek reference, and just for my personal amusement an opportunity to say "Oh my god you killed Kenny". Mentioning Gunnar Saint’s name would have made this wrestling gold. Caleb: Just have the narrator change Lord Darke Darkstone to Gunnar Saint. Gideon: Hmmmmm you know that would work. Antoine change Lord Darke Darkstone to Gunnar Saint. Caleb: Do you think this will air? Gideon: It has two hot women with large breasts topless. Smitty couldn’t care less about anything else. The two women join Gideon and Caleb for the evening. Of course you can imagine what goes on during this time. Since this is not a letter to Penthouse forums we can only guess what transpires after they leave and the cameras have long since been put away. Well the ones run by a camera crew at least. Anything else is purely speculation on our behalf. If Gideon or Caleb are asked they would not comment of course after all they value their privacy. A great American president summed up things very well. "I did not have sex with that woman." Of course whether or not he got a blowjob is a totally different story. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Gideon is not in the best of moods these days. He sees his career going nowhere and wrestling all but forgotten except by the most diehard fans. There is frustration and anger as he sees what he cares about ruined and defiled. There comes a time when you can’t take anymore There comes a time when a line in the sand must be drawn. There comes a time when you must make a stand. There comes a time when you must fight back. There comes a time for war. This is……… The breaking point.
