One week ago!

24 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24//03/08 1 Week ago. AJ is sitting at the bar in his favourite restaurant in L.A. chatting away to a dusky brunette stunner, who seemed to have an Adams apple the size of a melon yet AJ had not even noticed. He was busy explaining how, because he was the richest guy on the planet he wanted to help good causes and things, he was just about to get onto the part where he was planning on overthrowing the US government when he got a tap on the shoulder. He turns round to see a 7 foot Arabian guy staring him down with a stern face, unshaven, with very dark eyes which have seen much pain. This guy is Sameer al-Jezuri. "Mr Jensen, come with me." Says Sameer "And you are?" is the reply "It doesn’t matter at this stage; we need to talk in private" "Look if it is something about Sheila here, then I can explain" "Sheila?" AJ gives the sort of face which teenage girls are so adept at giving when an adult says something plainly obvious, you know the DURH face. He then nods toward Sheila sitting next to them. "Oh, I see, no, I do not know this man." Sheila lets off a cry of indignation before picking up her/his purse and scampering away "Now see what you have done! I’LL CALL YOU!" AJ shouts back at Sheila as he/she runs out of the pub. Sameer, nonplussed looks at AJ again. "Now can we talk?" "Sure. Barkeep, 2 flaming sambucas here, we will be in my private room k." "Sure thing AJ!" Sameer tuts as AJ gets up out of his seat and beckons him to follow. "I thought you were Muslim, why do you drink alcohol?" "What? Oh it’s for medicinal purposes." Sameer raises an eyebrow at this, but decides to let it slide because he needs AJ. The make their way to the back of the restaurant and AJ opens a door to a room market private, opening the door for Sameer, politely holding it open for him. AJ follows him inside and sits on a luxury black sofa on the far wall, Sameer takes a seat on an equally luxury armchair adjacent to him. Shortly afterwards the bartender arrives with AJ’s drinks, putting them down on the coffee table as he quickly scurries away. AJ lifts one sambuca, cheers Sameer and drains it. "So what would you like Mr….?" AJ leads on the question to try and get Sameer’s name. "My name is Sameer, I have been watching your career over the past few months with interest, and I believe we can help each other Mr Jensen." "You are going to help me get a better paid deal at SCCW?" "Not exactly." "You are going to help me to get with that Amy Campbell? She is a bit of a goer I hear." "No." "So you must want to help me get my own back on…." "No, no, no, not at all Mr Jensen." "Then what is it?" Sameer sighs for a second, leaning in toward AJ with a serious stare etched onto his face. "I am talking about helping you in your crusade against the American filth. I have connections, you have the funds, and together we can bring this country to its knees, all on prime time television. We will destroy the country and the citizens will think it is just Showtime." "I see what you are saying! You are going to bring some of those women over here!" "What? No! I am talking definitive action, I have a plan for the next Pay per view event in the Air force base which will shock the foundations of the world and show the Americans we are still bringing the fight to them." "Hmm, I see what you are saying, and I can help you there, but you need to help me first. I need protection you see. The owner of SCCW has a real grudge against me; he has already tried to censor me, twice. Now I am told I have to go up against two of the members of the blacklist at once." "I will offer protection, no problems. Then come Snake eyes, we must deliver a shocking blow to the world." AJ rubs his chin for a moment and nods. He puts his hand out to Sameer to shake it and drains his other sambuca afterwards. The day after Temptation. "Sammy, Sammy, look at this!" AJ bounds up to Sameer like a little child who has just got an awesome Christmas present. "It’s Sameer." Comes the less than warm response. "We have a title shot! For the SIN titles, Gold baby, gold! Think about how much weight we have to throw when we have championship gold, we can do real change!" "We already have plans to really change the world AJ. Championship gold won’t even come into it." "Think about it man! If we have championship gold we will be untouchable afterwards! I will also get a pay rise!" "After the plan, there will be no point. We are meeting Ahmed in an hour. Get prepared." "We still have to fight the match though, people will be suspicious." "I know AJ, we will fight and we will play the game until the plan comes. After which we get out of there, we will be wanted men." AJ shakes his head and shrugs. "Nothing that all the money in the world won’t be able to solve." He then smiles. Sameer looks at AJ sternly again. "Come, we had better get to the meeting." Sameer then purposely strides out of the San Diego apartment complex, leaving AJ to try and scurry behind him, trying to keep up, and chatting about Championship gold in his ear all the way to the car. After an hour of AJ talking about gold, titles, winning, and all the women he can get from this pay per view, they finally get to the destination. Getting out of the car, Sameer wastes no time in striding toward the men who are standing there waiting for him. A 4th man stops AJ as he gets out of the car as Sameer greets the man who seems to be in charge. "Sameer, welcome. Who is this you brought with you?" "Ahmed, meet Andre Jensen, he is one of us and he has connections" Ahmed looks at AJ distrustfully and gives a non committal shrug. Then he looks back at Sameer. "Sameer, you called this meeting, what is he doing here?" The 3 men are standing in a disused warehouse in a nondescript part of town, Ahmed is flanked by 2 men, armed with sub machine guns, both looking as mean as anyone can. AJ fidgets nervously as he is under the close scrutiny of Ahmed, as well as being uncomfortable with the very rough rubbing down he got from one of them to check for wires and guns. "Relax Ahmed, as I said, he has connections, he is committed to the cause, he also has big pockets. I can get to do so many things for our cause just this week; we need a package though." "A package? How big?" "Stadium size" "That will be easy, but expensive." "Will Visa do?" Chirps in AJ, hopefully. Ahmed stares right back at AJ with a look which could kill a thousand people over again. "Seriously Sameer, where did you get him?" Insists Ahmed. "To cut a long story short Ahmed, I have been watching him since he turned to our cause and I got in touch with him last week through a trusted source. AJ offered everything he could at the time and he is committed as any I have seen, he knows the book, he knows the way. Trust me on this brother." Ahmed sighs and takes a long look at AJ, who has the look of a simpleton etched upon his face, AJ shrugs as if to say "what can I say." "Ok Sameer, you keep an eye on him though." "Thank you Ahmed, now our request." "Stadium size it is, it will be delivered Thursday, make sure he brings the money." Ahmed points to AJ on he. He then looks at Sameer "You are like a brother to me, make me proud" Sameer and Ahmed bow slightly to each other and kiss cheeks in goodbye, Ahmed then gets into his car and drives away. Sameer turns to AJ. "Visa?" "Yeah, I have enough on my card to by China; I am the richest guy on the planet you know." "AJ, the idea is to be untraceable; you can’t go and buy a package like ours on a visa." "Well how am I supposed to know? Jesus, you think I know everything? Why do you think you are involved, you know this stuff, I don’t?" Sameer sighs. "Get in the car AJ, we have work to do." AJ follows Sameer with a beaming smile on his face. "Championship gold baby!" he mutters under his breath, clenching his fist in celebration.
