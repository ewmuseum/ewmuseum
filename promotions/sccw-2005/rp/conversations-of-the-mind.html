Date: Part One Conversations of the Mind: Part One December 15th 'What happened?' He licks his lips. The woman across from him, with her left leg crossed over her right one, looks down upon him. The only thought racing through his mind is that he's a God and yet she looks down upon him. 'You bore me.' He speaks for the first time in over an hour. She writes something down on a pad of paper. He looks at it and wants to snatch it out of her hands. You don't write notes about God. 'What happened?' His eyes glance towards the window behind the woman. 'Fuck you.' The words sting, but not as much as he would hope. She just continues to write on the pad of paper, observing every one of his mannerisms. Her breasts nearly fall out of her shirt, a simple white button down one. A blazer is folded across one of the arms of the wooden chair. Every time she writes on her pad, he gets a perfect view of them, and can't help but have thoughts about her. 'My name is Jasmine Wright. I'm a psychologist here. Do you know how long you've been here?' A split second pasts and he's already on his feet. She glances to the corner where a security officer stands, ready to act at a moment's notice. He walks around the room, admiring all of the different trinkets in the woman's office. 'You want me to speak to you? Fine. Say my name.' She glances up from her notepad at him and looks at him oddly. She removes the glasses from the bridge of her nose and sits back in the chair. 'This pretend name that you've come up with? What's your real name?' His head snaps over to where she's sitting. 'Say it, bitch.' Now it's her turn to lick her lips. 'The drugs, they make you funny, don't they? You fancy yourself a God, correct? The profanity coming from your lips, those aren't your true actions. The thoughts you're having every time you glance at my breasts, it's not natural for you. The drugs have lowered your inhibitions but still haven't truly changed you. I can change that.' Her eyes follow his eyes, not once breaking her line of sight. 'If I say your name, will you tell me what happened?' He thinks about it. His eyes seemingly tell her yes. 'Then, tell me your story, Malachi.' A moment passes and he looks at the security guard. The look on the guard's face gives him not a single ounce of comfort. He goes back to his chair and sits down. His face looks scarred, but it's just from the lack of sleep and the drugs, taking away his color. He has stubble all over his face. His eyes are dead. It's a sad sight. 'He found me. I wanted to die.' Jessica begins writing on her pad. She pauses, realizing that Malachi has stopped talking. She looks up and puts the pad on the desk behind her. 'Sorry. Who found you?' His eyes close for a second, almost reliving it all. ~~~~~~~ After Defiance 'What the fuck are you doing?' He looks at the well dressed man standing there in the middle of his locker room. 'What?' Malachi speaks, his voice dry and lifeless sounding. 'Look at this! You've got bottles all over the place, pills scattered across the room. What the hell are you doing to yourself?' Malachi rolls his eyes. He doesn't have time for this. You don't berate God. 'Fuck you, Smitty. You don't own me.' Malachi stands up and walks over to the bathroom area. He grabs the bottle of pills sitting on the counter and grabs the random alcoholic beverage standing next to it. In mere seconds, he's in front of Smitty's mouth, his breath reeking of alcohol. He throws the pills into his mouth and takes a long swig from the bottle. 'Malachi, stop. You're killing yourself.' Malachi just laughs. 'Maybe that's what I want, have you ever thought about that?' Smitty sighs. 'Fine, Malachi. I didn't want to have to do this.' Smitty then turns around and pulls open the door. In walk two police officers. Malachi just looks at them and then at Smitty. 'What the fuck?! What the fuck?! Fuck you, Smitty! Fuck you!' The officers move towards Malachi and begin to grab him by the arms. Smitty just looks on. 'Arrest him.' They push Malachi into the wall, but Malachi fights back. 'Get the fuck off of me!' He pushes them away, refusing to be taken to jail, but he's too high to do anything about it. They push him back into the wall and put the handcuffs on his wrists. He fights as much as possible, but the exertion begins to wear him out and he can barely think. They begin to escort him from the locker room. 'Stop.' They do exactly that as Malachi stands in front of Smitty. 'I don't feel so well.' Out of nowhere, he throws up on Smitty's shoes. Smitty just looks at Malachi and shakes his head. 'I'm going to get you help Malachi or you're going to jail. I promise you that. On my soul, I promise you that Malachi.' Malachi just stands there nearly void of life. 'Get him out of here.' The officers drag Malachi out of the locker room and begin to escort him out of the arena. 'You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law...' ~~~~~~~ December 15th 'Your boss arrested you?' Malachi just sighs and rolls his eyes. 'That's what I told you, isn't it?' Jasmine nods her head. 'Yes, you did. Then what happened?' Malachi shakes his head. She looks at him with great interest, wondering why he would refuse to talk to her all of a sudden. 'Come on, Malachi, tell me what happened next.' He spits at her feet. She knows that it's the drugs acting, not the actual person. Yet, Malachi was just a persona for the real person there. The drugs hadn't helped him shed this personality, just calmed him down and made him more willing to talk. 'I'm tired. I want out of here. You want me to tell you what happened next? Get me the fuck out of here.' She nods her head before standing up. 'Okay, how about this? You told me something, now I'll tell you something. You've been here for ten days. I observed you each one of those days and have pages worth of notes on you. I have my thoughts about you, but I need to hear you talk, Malachi.' He chuckles. 'Why? Is that how you get off? Will that get you wet for me?' She looks at him with a curious look. She walks up to him and kneels right in front of him. 'You're God. So, how would you react when I do this?' Jessica grabs Malachi's weakened right and forces it down her shirt. She rubs his hand on her bare breast and observes his reaction. At first, Malachi is disgusted by all of this, but is too weak to fight her off. Yet, eventually his primal instincts and he begins to enjoy. She's in awe of this as she removes his hand and walks back to her chair. 'Did you like that, Malachi?' He glares at her. 'Fuck you.' She beams. 'I imagine you would. We'll touch more upon your psyche though later. First, I want to ask you about your brief stay in jail.' Yet, he refuses. 'I'm done talking to you.' She shakes her head, a move that Malachi isn't a fan of. 'No you're not. Talk to me about the guy who died.' He looks at her, a rage in his eyes. 'Did you kill him?' Silence. He just sits there, stewing. 'Did you kill him?' To Be Continued
