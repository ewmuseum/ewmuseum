Why is this happening now?

25 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/25/2008 "Sin City…can a man truly change?" A man signs in a darkened room. A small sliver of light from a cracked door offers little illumination. The creak of a chair is heard as if someone is getting up out of one. "I haven’t been here in so long…not since before…" A knock on the door changes the sliver to a crack and more of the room is illuminated. A desk sits in what appears to be the middle of the room. The tension is palpable, as if the knocker knew at once the mistake they made. "What is it?" the man says calmly diffusing the tension as best he can. A young woman steps into the room impeccably dressed in a well-tailored suit with hair, black as coal, pulled back into a tight ponytail. "What are you doing here sir?" she inquires flipping on the lights. Suddenly the room is awash in a sea of light. A man stands looking out the large floor to ceiling windows across the room from the woman, his back to her. It’s an office, a plain office at that. The desk is a dark cherry wood, two chairs made of the same wood sit on the floor in front of the desk. A high back leather chair is pushed into the desk. The woman crosses the room and stands behind the man. She looks down at the desk; it looks as though it hasn’t been used in months. A small frame picture houses the only picture in the entire office. "That’s me Sera," the man says without turning around, sensing the rising curiosity in her, "My wife Jaslene Myst and our daughters Inara and Kayliana. All three of them were murdered about a year ago." Sera looks back to the man with what can only be described as horror on her face, "I’m so sorry sir. I had no idea I was intruding." "It’s ok Sera," he says assuredly, "What can I do for you?" "Everything is ready to go Mister Stryker," she says moving back across the room to the door, "We are just waiting for you." "Thank you Sera, you have been a tremendous help these past few months," Adam says to her, "I’ll be down shortly." With that Sera walks out of the office, closing the door behind her. Adam takes one more look over the city. "Sin City…I guess we’ll find out if I truly am a changed man though somehow I doubt it." A small contingent of reporters is sitting in a small boardroom. There is a low buzz in the room as they talk amongst themselves. That all ceases however as Sera enters the room. All attention turns to her as she takes her place at the head of the table. "I’d like to thank you all for coming today," she begins to say. "What is this all about?" someone from the crowd cuts off. "I was told that some major announcement was about to be made." Another person adds. "Ladies and gentlemen," Sera says calmly, "My employer asked for each of you to be here today. I only set up the meetings I don’t actually know what this is about." "Well then where is your employer?" an angry voice calls out, "We’ve been here about an hour!" "I assure you he will be on his way shortly." Sera answers trying her best to placate the group. "Fuck it." A man says standing up; "I was called away from my own son’s birthday party by my editor for this." The man turns and heads for the door. As he is reaching for the handle, it swings open. Adam Stryker is standing in the doorway. His shoulder length blonde hair emphasizes the darkness of his suit. The man who was about to leave takes a step back, a look of shock comes to his face. "What in the hell is this!?" one of the reporters demand jumping to his feet, "What in the hell is going on!?" Adam crosses the room as everyone springs out of their seats. Adam takes his place at the head of the table. Sera steps back to stand behind him. "Questions?" Adam smiles as he says this. Immediately everyone begins to shout questions in his direction. Adam merely smirks as he waits for the question that he wants to answer. Frankly, he could care less about what they wanted to ask. This was his moment and his return to the limelight; he would control it. "Adam could you tell us how you managed to disappear for almost a year?" "I want to reiterate for those of you who don’t remember, the FBI never considered me a viable suspect. I was never a person of interest in the accident that killed my family. So me disappearing for year is far less condemning an act when that is taken into consideration." Adam answers calmly, " Besides it’s not like it is very difficult with the resources I have, and by resources I don’t just mean money. While that’s important it’s also about the people you surround yourself with. I could never have disappeared so thoroughly without the help of Sera here. Attention shifts momentarily to the black haired woman standing at Stryker’s back. "You see she has been invaluable to me in the past six months," Adam says, "I honestly thought of coming forward before this but after I hired her all of my doubts were erased, she helped me to see what I needed to do." "And what was that?" comes a voice from the crowd. "That’s another story for another day." Adam responds, "But I suppose you may want to know what I have planned and why I am in Sin City of all places, seeing as how I absolutely detest the desert." "Well what of it? Why are you here?" "I was offered a more than fair contract from a third party to join a local wrestling organization known as Sin City Championship Wrestling," Adam states clearly, "Luckily for me my reputation precedes me and this was no problem at all." Adam chuckles and pauses. "What third party?" someone calls out almost immediately. "Oh that you’ll find out soon enough," comes his response, "I suggestion you tune into Spike for the upcoming SIN, all…well most of your questions will be answered then. As for my plans outside of the wrestling world…" Behind him Sera clears her throat. Adam turns his head to her what she has to say without giving anyone else a chance to overhear. A couple of nods later, Adam faces the room of reporters again. "I’m being told that our time here is coming to an end so I’ll be quick," Adam says, "Stryker Enterprises is in the process of moving it’s global headquarters to this very building. Think of it what you will but I must be going, I have another appointment that I must keep. I want to thank you for your time and to apologize again for keeping you for so long." Almost every reporter in the room is speechless. Adam walks through the crowd and heads out the door, with Sera following at his side. They say nothing to each other until they are on an elevator heading for the basement. "Is this a wise move sir?" she asks him. "It’s the only move Sera." He answers her, "You said it yourself in the meeting just now." "I only did that because I could feel your doubt coming back." Sera says in response "If there is one thing you can’t afford, it is to show doubt. You must be extremely confident in everything you do from now on." "Don’t worry about that Sera, confidence is the one thing I have plenty of." Adam says as the elevator comes to a stop. The door rings as it opens. They are in the basement of the building. Only one car sits down here now, a black Mercedes S600. Adam pulls the key from his pocket and unlocks it as the two make their way toward the car. "By the way Sera, who is this appointment with?" Adam asks opening the door for her. "A Doctor Rifka Sirsin sir," Sera answers him sliding into the car. Adam’s eyes open wide with shock as Sera pulls the door close. Adam walks behind the car and pauses for a moment. "Sirsin? Shit…I hadn’t counted on this…how am I going to handle this" Adam opens the driver’s side door and gets into the car. He slides the key into the ignition and turns the car on. It roars to life as the headlights turn on. Adam shifts into gear and takes off. "Just out of my own curiosity Sera but how did Sirsin know where to find me?" Adam asks as the car emerges from beneath the building, "And why did you set up this appointment without running it by me first?" "I’m sorry sir, she contacted me and told me about how she was your therapist after…" Sera trails off unable to bring herself to mention the accident, "She told me that she had something vital she needed to show you and that it wouldn’t wait." "Wouldn’t?" Adam says, his brow raising at the use of this word, "She said it wouldn’t wait?" "Yes sir," Sera nods, "I can call her back and tell her that you’re not feeling well or that you have been detained elsewhere if you like." Adam takes a moment to think. "What are you getting at Rifka? Wouldn’t wait? What does that mean? Why do I find that to be a particularly significant choice of words?" "Sir?" Sera says breaking him from his semi-trance. "No Sera, that won’t be necessary." Adam’s grip on the wheel tightens slightly, "I want to thank you for setting up this appointment without my knowledge in face. If I had known about it then I most definitely would not have agreed to see the good doctor but now I must." The two drive in silence for a little while longer. The desert scenery gives way to that of the Vegas Strip with all of it’s lights and flash. "There!" Sera points to the Bellagio, "She said that she was staying there. You’re supposed to ask for her at the front desk." Adam pulls the car to the side of the road and gets out, leaving the car running. Sera gets out of the car as well. She stops him, grabbing his arm as he passes her. "What am I supposed to do?" she asks. "Take the car and go have some fun Sera, you’ve earned it." Adam answers her. She loosens her grip on him and watches as he walks toward the massive hotel. Sera waits until he has disappeared into the lobby before getting into the car and taking off. In the hotel, Adam walks up to the desk. A serious looking woman is standing on the other side of the counter. "Can I help you sir?" she asks, her tone matching her look. "I’m here to see a Doctor Rifka Sirsin, my name is Adam Stryker." He says before adding, "I believe she’s expecting me." "You’re right Mister Stryker, she is expecting you." The woman says as she types into the computer in front of her, "She is one of the penthouse suites on the very top floor, I’ll have one of the bellhops take you up." Moments later, Adam is riding in an elevator with a bellhop. The young man is staring at Adam with a strange amount of curiosity. "You know it’s rude to stare." Adam says while keeping his gaze forward. "I’m sorry," the bellhop responds, "It’s just you look so familiar to me but I can’t place it. I know I’ve seen you before." "It’s likely but if you keep staring at me I’ll be the last thing you ever see." Adam snaps back, his emotions starting to get the best of him. With a glup and great effort the bellhop pulls his eyes from Adam. The elevator finally reaches the top floor and opens. Adam steps out into the hallway. The bellhop however simply leans out the door and points down the hall. "She’s in that one down there." And with that he ducks back into the elevator just as it starts to close. Adam heads down the extravagant hallway heading toward the door the bellhop pointed out. As he approaches the door, Adam can hear the television on the other side of the door. He stops and listens for a moment but quickly decides to simply knock. The television turns off as soon as he stops knocking. The sound of shuffling feet can be heard and a moment later a woman with wire- rim glasses and a streaks of gray in her hair answers the door. "Doctor Sirsin," Adam says through a smile, "How nice it is to see you." "Don’t bullshit with me Stryker," Sirsin says standing back from the door, "I know you better than you think and I know that if you had it your way you never would have seen me again. Come in." Adam steps into the room. It’s extravagant beyond belief. Adam walks further into the suite and comes into a living room area with a couple of couches. "Sit down." Sirsin orders. Adam taken aback with shock does as he’s told. Rifka walks over to a small bar and pours out two glasses of Vodka. She walks back over to the sofa and places one of the drinks in front of Adam before sitting in a chair across from him. "Well doesn’t this feel familiar," Adam says with a laugh, "Only our surroundings have improved immensely." "Shut up and drink the Vodka," Sirsin again orders. "Sorry Doc but I don’t drink anymore." Adam answers her sternly. "You will after you hear what I have to say," Sirsin responds curtly. "You are going to put me up in this hotel for the next two weeks and provide for me a stipend with which I can do as I see fit. Further more when this little vacation is over for me, you will come back to New York at least once a month for regular appointments." "And what makes you think this bout of generosity is going to come from me," Adam says eyeing the drink. "Because of this," Sirsin pulls a piece of what appears to be a newspaper out of her pocket and hands it across to Adam. He takes it and unfolds it. All he reads is the title before dropping the paper to the floor, his eyes widen as his mind races a mile a minute. "How could she possibly know? Why is this happening now? I was so close to being free of it all." Shaking with what could be described as a mix of rage and fear, he takes the drink and downs it in one shot. "You understand then?" Sirsin asks nonchalantly, "The information I know about you could be detrimental." "Fine." Is the only word to escape from Adam’s lips. He takes one more look at the paper, it reads: Body Pulled from East River IDed as Missing Leiko Rane
