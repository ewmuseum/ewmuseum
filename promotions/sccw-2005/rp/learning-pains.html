Date: Today When you’re a professional wrestler, there are things that you take for granted. One of them is the fact that your wrestling gear tends to get rather, well, funky. Now, when you’re not a seasoned veteran, you ask yourself one thing. When you have a pair of white trunks with green accents, do you throw them in with your weights or with your colors. Because you really didn’t want the colors to start to fade. That would be bad. Unfortunately, Paul Searle wasn’t a seasoned professional. So he finds himself standing in front of a washing machine with a bag of rather rank smelling trunks. He thinks to himself, ‘At least when I was wrestling with Mr. Ryan, I wore basketball shorts, those were easy to figure out.’ "Mrs. Durant!" Paul yells out as he runs out of the Laundry Room. - - - - - Paul Searle was now sitting, looking at the trees as the leaves are turning. The little bit of rain that was falling wasn’t keeping his spirits down, nor was the fact that he was in a tag team match partnered with a man that, only weeks ago, his mentor attempted to break the wrist of. Paul wasn’t worried though, because there was just too much at stake for Jared Sykes to hang him out to dry. That is, unless Jared wins the first fall. Then Jared wouldn’t need Paul anymore. It was just in that moment of reflection that Paul Searle came to one realization. He was screwed. Paul tries to convince himself that what he fears will happen, won’t happen. But he doesn’t know that. What does he know about Jared Sykes? Other than the fact that he was once King Blueberry, pretty much not a damn thing. He was more than screwed, he was fucked. And not in the "Oh Paul, do me and I’ll suck your cock" fashion, but more like the "Open Wide, here comes your Cockmeat sandwich" variety. Paul doesn’t want to eat a Cockmeat Sandwich. Literally, or figuratively. His three pairs of wrestling trunkes were hanging. Mrs Durant game him directions, but he still had to do the work. Right now, to him, the laundry that he had to do was just a simple metaphor for his career. He was completing the work that Mr. Ryan had set out in front of him. Having to realize as often as he does without proper training is badness. No one ever accused Jason Ryan of being competent. But heknew what he was doing with this kid. Then again, no one else did either, including the kid himself. - - - - - "Come on, kid, you can hit harder than that." the boxing coach at Gold’s Gym yelled as he works out with Paul Searle. This wasn’t easy for Paul. Until he first set foot in a wrestling ring, he had never been in a fight. Mike Durant thought that it would be a good idea for him to start to learn to use his extended reach to an advantage, and brought him to a gym that was attempting to bring about some of the young and competitive boxers as it was. Paul is just there for the ride. He knows that. He knows that he would be best served to be sent to a wrestling school. Only, he doesn’t have the time to do that. And SCCW is going to make as much money off of him as they can. He continues to throw punches that couldn’t hurt a fly that was stuck to flypaper. His reach was there, it was the power behind the punch that he was struggling with. "Listen kid, before you came here that you wanted to learn to throw a punch. Well, instead of trying to knock me back, just do it. With that, Paul Searle launched a punch.
