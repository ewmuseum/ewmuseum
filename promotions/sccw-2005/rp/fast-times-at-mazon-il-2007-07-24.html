Fast Times at Mazon IL

24 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/24/07 'There is nothing, and I mean nothing on god's green earth as boring as a John Covel promo.' Timo Bolamba, also known as 'The Dastardly Bastard' flipped off his television and shook his head. He had just finished a marathon of tapes to try and prepare himself for the in ring exploits he would have come KILLZone. He felt certain at this point that there were two things he now knew for sure about John Covel. 1) He loves to refer to himself as the Paragon of Piledrivers. Timo knew this of course because it was the intimidating nickname that John had no doubt concocted for himself after he had run out of other ideas. Throwing together a couple same consanants in the name was easy. Hell look at Timo's other nickname 'The Count of Crotch Shots'....it has a nice ring to it. The again, Timo had on several occasions thought perhaps Covel needed a new nickname to call himself by. What could it be...The Baron of Bathrooms? Well, the world may never know. 2) Watching John Covel wrestle and/or flap his gums was akin to watching grass grow. If this was what Clinton Sage had to put up with every week, it's no large wonder he killed someone. The Samoan turned away from the drudgery of trying to discern a method to Covel's style and decided that sometimes against people such as this, it was best just to wing it. Big city words and ways were far beyond the grasp of the uninitiated mind. John Covel was convinced in his head that he was both a small town fireman and a wrestling superstar...and the doctors called Timo crazy. Walking slowly to the window of his very small and underfurnished hotel room, he looked unto a world that was both bleak and lifeless, at least by his standards. How on earth could there be a town so beneath The Bastards standards that was so close to his own home of Motor City? It made no sense. The Samoan felt as though he had taken a trip with the wayback machine, and the only sight he had in his vision as he looked out his window could be summed up by this weblink: Mazon Illinois Finally summoning the courage to walk outside in the small town community, he took time to observe his surroundings. Before him a small cottage with the sign 'Mazon State Bank' spread. He noted the very small billboard just to the left of the entrance that displayed the weather, local mortgage and savings rates, and the score of the local high school sports teams. The Dastardly One struggled his way into the ramshackle financial institution and pushed open the door. Just inside he watched as the old white ladies that manned the teller windows looked at him as though he were an alien, and The Bastard flipped out his shades and slipped them on slowly. He felt as awkward as a pair of cousins sitting on the local Mazon 4-H hayride together. Too many feelings rushed at him that he could not explain, and The Samoan held up a hand slowly. 'Okay, this won't take long...' The people inside the bank suddenly drop to the ground and The Bastard takes stock of the situation slowly. He holds a hand up and beads his eyes back and forth behind the Maui Jims. 'Okay, I realize I probably look not unlike a 'city slicker' or whatever it is you people call civilized human beings...but I don't need your money, I just want to know where your bathroom is.' A fair question, to be sure. One of the ladies stands up slowly and in a feeble voice responds. 'Okay sir, there is a bathroom right down the hallway there...You will have to excuse us, it isn't every day we get someone like you in here,' she mused. The Samoan shrugged. 'I guess it is rather special for you guys to see a person living well above their means, so I can forgive you this once. Now, on to the chase...' He walks down the hallway with everyone watching him as he does. His silk shirt ruffles and his patent jeans swish as the muscular Samoan passes the chalk white old ladies. He props open the door to the bathroom and flicks on the light. 'John, are you in here John?' The Samoan looked around, and shrugged before nonchalantly walking out the door the way he came. He looked down the cracked and well in need of repair road (notice the word 'road' is singular...it's because we are in Mazon people.) and without much ado, he made his way down it towards his next destination. Walking up to a joint that looked like a little slice of heaven, The Samoan's stomach began to rumble. It was then that he noticed the sign for 'The Feed Bag'. In the back of his mind, Timo didn't really want to believe that this was the name, in fact he was almost sure the people just called it 'The Restaurant'. Sighing slightly as his stomach rumbled again, he looked left...nope, no restaurants...and to the right...again nothing. He collared up and pushed the creaky door open, and smelled the bounty of deep fried goodness barrage him all at once. Sitting down in a dusty corner, a waitress sauntered up to The Bastard and put down a pot of coffee and a cup. He pushed the shades down his nose slightly and looked up at her. Without bothering to look at the menu, he waves his hand in the air and gesture to the lady as he speaks. 'I'd like a T-Bone steak and a baked potato on a bed of rice please. Bring me a fine imported beer too while you are at it...uhhh...Gladys.' The old white lady walked away slowly and soon returned with his beer. The Samoan looked down at the bottle and then back to the lady with disdain in his eyes. 'I said imported you wench, this is Budweiser. It isn't even technically beer, let alone imported!' The lady shrugs a little and smiles. 'Listen buddy, around these parts, there's only two kinds of beer: The stuff you get imported from the big city, and the stuff Farmer Hank makes in his barn. Take your pick.' The Samoan raises a brow and shakes his head. 'Imported Budweiser it is then I guess.' He silently sips on the swill and eventually his meal arrives. Gladys coughs a few times as she puts the plate in front of the Samoan, then pulls out a cancer stick. He watches as she begins to inhale the vile smoke and then looks down in disgust at his meal. 'Christ woman, how did they cook this thing?' She looks at The Samoan and coughs, not removing the Marlboro from her lips before answering. 'It's Shake and Bake...and I helped.' Timo looked dumbfounded. 'Wait, so you and what I assume is an alcoholic cook put some sort of breading on a t-bone steak and threw it into a fry daddy? And what the hell, I asked for it on a bed of rice with a baked potato. You have here a bunch of tri-taters and top ramen! Holy hell lady, whats the dessert, Hamburger Helper?' She smiles. 'Beef Stroganoff flavor.' The Samoan stands up suddenly and shakes his head before throwing down his napkin on the table. 'Where is the bathroom?' She points to the corner and The Bastard makes his way over to the doorway. he props it open slowly and peers inside, expecting perhaps to find something. 'John...John Covel are you in there? No?' Hearing no response, The Samoan turns and walks out of the restaurant, his appetetite not satiated in the least. He held his rumbling stomach as he slowly made his way down the street of Mazon. As he walked past the Mazon Farmers Elevator, two crusty old white men pulled their daughters inside at the sight of the well to do man walking in front of them. He slowly passed a trainyard and noticed the several burly men looking at him as though he did not belong. Pushing by the area quickly, Timo made his way to the Mazon Market, and went inside the small grocery store. The Samoan looked around nervously and even though he had his Maui Jim shades on, he still felt the pressure of the small town eyes upon him, closing in on him everywhere he looked. He backed up slowly until he was near the bathroom and turned to go in, looking around frantically for the man he had come to find, but John Covel was not in this bathroom either. The Samoan ran as fast as his feet would carry him until he came across a volunteer fire department. A young blonde man walked out to greet him and extended his hand. 'Hi! I'm Robbie!' The Samoan leaned down on his knees, sucking in the dirty small town air that putrified his lungs as though it were made of acid. He held up a hand and Robbie pulled his own back, then waited for Timo to catch his breath and explain himself. 'Listen...Robbie...Let me tell you how my day is going. I've been everywhere in this half horse town. I woke up in a hotel room barely fit for Kid Cool after watching eleven hours of John Covel promo's about doctor notes and doing drugs and shit. I then make my way to some bank where they look at me as though I'm trying to rob the place. After that, it was the local eatery where I am fairly sure the health department has forsaken any bonds of service. The trainyard and granary were no different, and finally the grocery market...' Robbie nods. 'Well fella, you do kinda stand out with with being...well...you know...' Timo stands up and looks confused. 'What? Well endowed? Spit it out boy wonder!' Robbie shakes his head and whispers something into Timo's ear. The Samoan stands at full height and screams into the air. 'I'm a god damned Samoan, you corn fed ingrate! Don't you people know what the Samoan islands are? Fiji? Hawaii? This makes so much sense now...' He nods and looks completely content with his discovery. 'I can see now that I've been going about this all wrong. I have searched every bathroom in Mazon, and I have not found John Covel. There has to be a connection between the racial domination in this community mixed with the fact that John Covel isn't in any of the bathrooms I searched.' He looks at Robbie. 'Is there a secret white person bathroom that us 'colored folk' aren't allowed to use?' Robbie shakes his head. 'Look, nobody is calling you anything sir. Nobody here cares if you are from this Samoa or whatever those other made up places you spoke about are. I mean look at the facts sir...Hawaii? Doesn't that sound just a little bit fake to you?' The Samoan turns away, but Robbie grabs his arm and begins to shake it slowly. 'You can't leave. You are a part of Mazon now Timo...' The Samoan begins to scream and fight back, but suddenly Gladys grabs his other arm and smiles a four toothed smile. The white bank ladies join in, and Timo begins to kick out at the approaching farmers. 'Your's ours now Timo...Timo....Timo...' Suddenly The Samoan opens his eyes slowly, and standing over him is his new friend Tsuyoshi Tanaka shaking him. 'Timo...Timo...dude wake up! You are going to miss the nudie bar if you don't get off your ass man! Tonight is two for one lapdances!' The Samoan looks around in his locker room and groggily yawns. 'Dude, Yoshi...I just had the worst dream. There was hamburger helper, and Covel wasn't in any of the bathrooms, and pasty white people everywhere.' Tanaka nods. 'Yeah, I hate pasty white people too. Let's get you some fine imported beers and a nice t-bone steak, and it will all go away.' The Samoan laughs at the irony and they pair walk out the room and down the hallway. As they pass by the bathroom, Timo grasps for the handle, and then stops before opening the door. He shakes his head slowly and walks away with Tanaka. He takes one final look back and ponders. 'Nah, can't be.' They round the corner and out of the bathroom steps a tall muscular fellow from Mazon, Illinois. Under one hand he has a newspaper, and in the other he has a coffe mug marked 'The Feed Bag'. He walks the other direction down the hallway singing a jaunty tune. 'Aint no woman like the one I got...'
