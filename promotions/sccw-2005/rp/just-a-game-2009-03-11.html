Just A Game

11 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11, 2009 In an abandoned warehouse down by the docks, a man in a blue-grey double breasted shirt paced back and forth, gloved hand rubbing his chin as he considered carefully what he was about to say. Finally he sighed, tipping back the white cowboy hat for which he was named. 'No, what I'm saying is, I feel like a bunch of chickens holed up in a coop waiting for the varmints to come get us! And that's a feeling I don't like one bit' A young teen girl floating nearby, her legs folded under her in a lotus position, her blond pigtails waving, supported by a yellow force field. 'Hat, you're always so serious. The Twins and I are relaxing, just sit back and be mellow.' Next to them were what must be the Twins, both long haired and clad in black and red spandex, leather straps and shiny metal buckles. The only difference between them were the letters on their chests, one featuring a 'C,' the other a 'P,' and the activities they seemed to find relaxing. C was relaxing by laying on his back atop a pile of pallets he had organized into an impromptu bed, looking at his companions upside down, and P was doing a series of increasingly more difficult handstands. 'Daisy's right, it's not like the Oracle Stone-' P started talking, but C seamlessly picked up the sentence. '-has ever lead us astray before. We're supposed to meet someone important here, and the fate of the city-' '-nay, the whole world-' '-hangs in the balance!' If you weren't looking directly at them, it was impossible to tell which of the Gemini Twins were actually speaking, although the leader of the UltraProtectors, White Hat, claimed he could tell them apart with his eyes closed. Suddenly, there was a roiling ball of red in the middle of the room, accompanied by the rustle of fabric as the ball rapidly expanded, spreading out to form an impossibly large cape, billowing as if it had a life all its own. There was a sudden... sound, as if the very universe groaned and ripped asunder. And then there was a man attached to the cape, the space around him warbling as he appeared from nothing. In addition to the red cape, he wore a simple grey spandex body suit, and black leather boots. His hair was a dirty blond, slicked back to his head. He stood in the center of his cape as it waved around him as if it too were alive, one red gloved fist clenched and held in the air in front of him. But none of these were the most noticeable of his features. That distinction belonged to the eight inch long hunk of green crystal, as big around as a golf ball, that stuck out from the socket of his right eye, the flesh around it angry and inflamed, a trickle of blood running down the crystal to drip from its tip. In a voice that sounded as if it were two voices in one, one coming from his mouth and the other coming seemingly from the crystal itself, he began to speak. 'I am Shard, and if you are one-tenth the heroes I have been told, I need no other introduction. And I am here-' *ZAAAAAAAAP* 'What the fuck are you doing?!' yelled Daisy, in a very in a very un-mellow tone.   ---- 'What the fuck are you doing?!' Rosa Dawes said, looking at Austin King, who sat next to her at the table. Across the table from them sat Benji Dill and Jordan Cross, both members of SCCW security, Benji shoveling Cheetos in his mouth as Jordan was sketching the word POLUX under an image of a spandex clad man with a P on his chest. 'Dude, it's got two Ls. P-O-L-L-U-X,' Benji advised around a mouth of crunchy orange snacks. Austin waved a piece of paper around excitedly, 'I''m blastin' tha git, that's whathefuck I'm doin'! Now, lemme see, White Hat has an offense of 9, but has 4 extra levels in Blast.... E, do I gets a bonus cuz ol' one-eye's nevah gonna expect this?' At the head of the table sat E Allen Peters, Austin's best friend, manager, and tonight's game master. 'Um, are you SURE you want White Hat to just take a shot at Shard? I mean, before you hear what he has to say?' 'Nah, I'm sure Senoir Sombrero Blanco already took his shot. I said it, he did it, em's tha rules, roight? Sides, I got twenny five points fer 'ABIDE NOT EVIL' an it'd be cheatin not ta play it.' Austin was busy pointing out where ABIDE NOT EVIL was written on his sheet. 'Yes, you said it, but I'm offering you a chance to take it back and rethink it.' Peters was busy looking dubiously at Shard's character sheet, all three pages of it. Austin snorted derisively, and grabbed a handful of dice and shook them in his hand before tossing them onto the table. 'Nah.' Benji put his head in his hands, groaning 'We are SO boned.' The other gamers leaned forward, watching the dice spin to a stop on the wooden table top. 'BOOYASHAKA!!! That's what I'm talking about, bitches!' Jordan jumped up, knocking over his chair and dancing back and forth, imitating a train as he did. 'Well well well,' E said, 'a natural 3. That's a critical, I'll let you roll an extra half on your damage, make it look good.' Austin grabbed a double fistful of dice, borrowing some from the others, grinning wildly as he shook them up in his hands, and dropped them on the table with a thunderous clatter. Then it was Peters' turn to stand, pointing at Austin and declaring dramatically, 'INSOLENT WHELP! I SHALL GRIND YOU TO PASTE!' With that, E sat and began writing on a piece of paper. 'Okay, the surprise round is over, I'll need everybody's speeds.' Austin gathered up all the dice and passed them back to their owners. 'Wait, that dinnit do nothin but piss tha bloke off?' 'If it did anything else, he's not showing it,' E said before cracking open a Diet Mt. Dew. 'We are-' Benji started. 'SO boned!' Jordan finished. ---- 'Okay, so that's another... 56 Stun and 19 Body BEFORE the knock back through the concrete wall...' Peters said, grabbing more dice to roll for knock back damage. 'Don't even bother, man. That drops Castor like a stone, and I'm all we had left,' Benji frowned. An awkward silence followed, E biting his lip in thought. 'Bollocks! Bloody fucking bollocks!' It was Austin who broke the silence. 'I'm sure ya run a good game, E, but it ain't nothin like a comic book!' Rose put her hand on Austin's arm trying to calm him. 'Austin, relax. What did you expect when you attacked Shard? He told us this was one of the biggest of the big bads.' 'Yeah, Wombutt-head.' Jordan said, putting his dice away in a purple Crown Royal bag. 'That was like, I dunno...' 'Like kissing Amy Campbell in front of her husband AND girlfriend at the same time,' Benji offered. Austin flopped down on the bed in the hotel room, still scowling. 'Again, bollocks. We's playin a game a soopa heroes. S'possed to be like a comic book. If'n it was a comic, it'd be called 'The Mega-awesome UltraProtectors.' An there's no way some bad guy, no matta HOW bad ass, would kill the whole team in their own bloody book.' E folded his arms, staring down the Aussie. 'In a real comic book, much like real life, occasionally the good guys have to work with the bad guys in order to take on something even more bad.' Austin jumped off the bed, 'I don't think I like the way this conversation is heading, particularly from someone who is my new manager.' He moved up to level a finger in Peters' face. 'The Wombat doesn't compromise, The Wombat doesn't work with bad guys.' Peters blinked. 'Whoa, whoa, whoa! Overreact and read too much into things....much? It was just a game, dude.' Austin thought about this a moment. 'Oh. In that case....who wants to go annoy hotel security? Elevator Tag, and I'll even start as it this time!'
