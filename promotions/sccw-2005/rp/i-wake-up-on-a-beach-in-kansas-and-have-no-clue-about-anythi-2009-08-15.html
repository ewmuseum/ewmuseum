I wake up on a beach in Kansas and have no clue about anything except for I need to go Up and not Down

15 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/15/2009 Blackness surrounds me. Hear waves crashing against the beach. Wake up and look around. On a beach somewhere. Don’t know what country or state. Have no clue what the hell is going on. Got attacked by some guys in black shirts at the SCCW show. The Jungle Cats are in the Amazon hunting alien scourge. Nothing is around me except for the sea birds flying out toward the endless ocean in front of me. Look down and see my entire black suit is covered with sand. Rub my eyes to look around again. Surfers are the only people around and they’re in the water. Hopefully the Mega Shark, Giant Octopus, Jaws, or Orca don’t attack them. Further proof I’ve seen too many cheesy horror movies about animals attacking humans. Now all I have to do is figure where I’m at and how the hell to get to the show. Get up and brush the sand off myself. How the hell did I end up here? I have no clue what happened. Must’ve been drinking. Look around… The rum’s gone. "Why is the rum always gone?" Start to wander the beach. Sand beneath my shoes makes it hard to walk. Feet slipping in the sand. Crunching of sea shells and sea weed underneath me. Sand castles everywhere. Look behind me and see some sort of a small beach town. Probably on a coast somewhere. Most likely I am in Iowa. That is a big time beach state. Then again, so is Kansas. Head off the beach and reach the town. Don’t know what the hell the name of it is but it’s something in Spanish. Probably Los Angeles, Kansas. Biggest city in the west. Smell the ocean air with my nostrils. Then the smell of exhaust from the trucks bringing kayaks to the beach hits me. Damn kayak tours. Surfers walk past hitting me with their boards. I say nothing because they are most likely Bodi and his gang from Point Break. The air is cold and the fog is still covering the sky above me. Stores are just now opening up. Don’t know where my rental car is. I probably knocked up a hooker last night too with my coked up Rick Rollin’ sperm. Luckily I kept my SCCW Invisible Championship belt around my waist. A crab scuttles down the street toward the beach. Must’ve just jumped off of a sailor’s vagina. Damn that Bruno for spreading such filth to the United States. He almost ruined this country by spreading the AIDS and the Ebola to the mainland. Hope I don’t catch it from any of these skanks I bang on the road. Or worse, give it to my girls Desade and Aimz. "EEK!" That would be a nightmare. I need a GPS. Still lost so I keep walking. Don’t bother to look at street signs. I won’t know where the hell to go anyway. Keep walking until I reach a light. Some yuppies are trying to cross on the other side. Look around but don’t see anything that I recognize. Maybe aliens abducted me and took me here for experimentation. Hopefully I didn’t get anal probed by a homosexual alien or anything along those lines. Light turns green I walk across the street Wondering if sharks can kill on land However I realize only humans and lions can do that So I keep on walking. Now I’m walking up a hill. This only makes me more sore. Finally see something I recognize. My rental car! Go over and see that it is covered in seagull shit. "Lovely." Reach into my right pocket and pull out a key. Go over and open up the driver’s side door. Get in and close the door. Put the key into the ignition. Turn it. Car starts up. Smells like a dead hooker in the car. Turn and look to the backseat to make sure there isn’t a dead hooker in the back that I don’t know is there. Luckily there is no dead hooker so I turn and put on my seatbelt. Start driving down the streets. Driving aimlessly, pointlessly around until I find the highway. Turns out I’m in California. "What the fuck?" California isn’t an ocean state! It’s in the Great Plains for crying out loud! This really messes me up as I drive north on the highway. Need food. Stop at a gas station and get some gas and food. People swarm me like a horde of zombies. As a matter of fact, they are zombies! I grab a stick to fight them off before jumping quickly back into the rental car and speeding off. Drive down the highway. Going everywhere… Going nowhere. Losing my mind. Zombies almost killed me. Beat up by strange men in black T-shirts. 2012 may be the year the world ends but 2009 is the year SCCW drives me insane. Wait to drive off of a cliff… Nothing happens so I keep driving down the highway to the show. I’ll probably show up late or something. Have a Wii that I bought in the trunk. Got a Wii Fit and Wii Sports Resort to train with. I bet I’m the only wrestler who does that… Wait, von Doom did that like two years ago. Nevermind. That guy was fucking nuts but he’s ahead of his time when it comes to certain things. Heard he threw matches on purpose too to make his opponents think he was weak and not a threat. He also made himself hated and controversial on purpose. Met that psychopath once. Don’t want to meet him again. Continue to drive through the day and into the night, only stopping for food, bathroom breaks, and sex with hookers. Bought some more cocaine. I have more cocaine in my system than most rap groups do. Continue to drive through the night while being chased by demons, satyrs, and minotaurs before finally reaching Colorado Springs. Get a motel room. Do my usual routine of picking up a chick at the bar and having sex with her before going to sleep. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . WAKE UP! Look around. Chick is gone. I probably knocked her up. Get my stuff together after taking a shower and check out before going to the show. Arrive and am attacked by the same guys in black shirts! They beat me up. . . . . . . . . . . . . . AWAKE! Hold my head and look around but the goons are gone. Then see something shining in the distance. I know it can only be one thing… "ALIENS!" Pick up my bag and run for the arena. UFOS chase me inside but I fight them off using Slim Jims and fly swatters. Make it to my locker room, closing the door behind me. Take a moment to catch my breath. I still have a match to compete in. I’m tired and exhausted. Got little sleep, fucked a woman, and am coked up. It’s official… I’m totally screwed. Stick a fork in me, I’m done. This match is already a lost cause. I then start preparing to lose by going back to sleep. Yeah, this is going to go well.
