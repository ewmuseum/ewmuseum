Like Being Batman

22 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22, 2008 Tuesday, July 22, 2008. Cambridge, Massachusetts. Forecast for the day is cloudy, with a 65% chance of rain. Not every day in the life of a king is full of sunshine and roses. When he’d first moved into his apartment almost 8 years ago, one of the first things that the man behind the mask of King Blueberry had done was to get permission from the landlord, a short polish man named Stanislaus Krawczyk, to modify the roof a little. 'I’m kind of a public figure, or at least I will be if everything works out the way I hope. Basically I just need a place to hide out for a while, kind of away from everyone else. I can give you another few hundred a month, if it helps, that way if anyone asks why I get to go up there you can just tell ‘em that I paid for it.' The only permanent change he’d made, the occasional lounge chair, putting green, or inflatable pool notwithstanding, was to install two metal brackets, one on either side of the door, which could hold a sturdy wooden beam between them. In effect, he’d created a barricade to lock out the rest of the world. While this was one of his favorite places to be, often spending weekends up here with the rest of his mischievous crew, it was rare that he’d come up by himself, shutting everyone else out. They’d learned early on though that if he wanted to go, it was best to let him go. ‘Dude, roof,’ was normally enough to get the message across. Unfortunately for everyone involved, this happened to be one of those days. 'But if it’s something that we can help you with, that’s what we’re here for. God knows you’ve bailed us out more times than I can even remember.' Unfortunately for the man behind the mask of Blueberry Dragon, he was deliberately ignoring that unspoken rule. 'Is it about us, me and Lloyd? We’re fine, you know. We’re both aware of what can happen out there. Don’t forget that we signed on for this,' he paused, ruminating for a moment over what he’d just said, 'Well, I signed on for this, he just kind of... Look, I’m sure he’d have left by now if he didn’t want to deal with it.' King Blueberry had been trying hard to block him out. Whatever was eating at him, it marked the first time in recent memory that he’d broken character even within his own home. The mask was off today, though his face was still mostly covered. In place of the blue spandex and Lycra was an oversized black 'Minnesota Mafia' sweatshirt, the hood pulled up over his brow. Silver-rimmed sunglasses hid his eyes. 'Nope, not even close.' The king’s response was curt, but there was no ill will behind it. 'I mean, don’t get me wrong,' he continued, still moving towards the door which would ultimately take him to the roof, 'I don’t dig on you two getting hurt like that, but if I’m being honest with you...' He stopped in his tracks, looked over his left shoulder, and shot Dragon a half-smile. 'Watching Lloyd - big, 6 foot 4 Lloyd with his beard made of anger and Jesus get taken down by a 90 pound girl, well, that’ll never get old.' The king fished around in the pocket of his sweatshirt for a moment, removing a pair of headphones whose wires he started to untangle as he resumed his path to the door. Dragon, becoming increasingly brazen, would not relent. 'Okay, so what is it then? Am I going to have to keep guessing?' The king stopped again, but this time he didn’t turn. Instead he inhaled deep, exhaled slowly, and rolled his shoulders. His responses were becoming increasingly forceful. 'Merv’, let it go. I’m heading up.' The door shut and the board slid in place, he wasted little time in falling to the all-weather carpet that covered the roof. Lying on his back, hands behind his head, legs crossed at the ankles, he stared listlessly into space, the music from his iPod serving as an audible barrier. Mnemic, 'Liquid.' Liar. Liar, liar, liar! Tell your best friend that you think it’s funny a girl threw ‘m to the floor. Pretend that you’re not concerned. You’ve made an enemy in the Dead Man’s Hand, and you’re going to try and convince yourself that he - either of them, won’t feel some sort of repercussions? Imagine it’s a great big joke and maybe it’ll go away. There’s no way either of them could ever hope to legitimately defend themselves. You do know that, don’t you? Okay, so maybe the big one can take a punch. Maybe he can even throw a punch. I’m sure he’d be a right fucking threat if he were lucid enough to understand anything that was going on, but he isn’t, and anything that happens is your fault. Blind Guardian, 'Nightfall.' You’re putting your friends in danger. 8 years. They’ve been safe for 8 years, right? Of course they’ll be safe forever. Roll the dice, take your chance, bet their safety. You’re a terrible person. He sat up abruptly, not realizing that he was engulfed in a cold sweat. Hands shaking, he reached to his brow and wiped his forehead. The back of his hand grazed the bandage residing there. Behind it were two scars. The first had come six years prior at the hands of Lance Marshall, the same man who’d run to his aid and the aid of a defenseless child. 'Seriously, Shaq has nothing on ole Lancy-pants. The dude palmed my head, gave himself a running start, jumped over a kid on a chair, did a 360, a windmill, and then tomahawked my face off a cage. He got a 9 from Jordan. Dr. J only gave him an 8. I don’t know how I’d score it, but everything tasted like bananas for 5 days. I seriously had no idea gorillas could be so agile. Like, imagine throwing a tennis ball against a wall, but with more screaming.' The second scar was only in its early stages, as stitches still held the wound closed, but it came at the hands of Mayhem and a crutch. This would not be the only scar left by this incident, though it would be the only one visible. Don’t dwell on it. Don’t dwell. It won’t do any good. You didn’t know. You weren’t prepared. Spiderman was never contacted. Management never told you. You didn’t know. You didn’t know. You tried to stop it, but you failed. You were powerless. You were weak. You are weak. 'Shut up,' he whispered to no-one in particular. Coheed and Cambria, 'In Keeping Secrets of Silent Earth.' The family won’t likely forget, you know. Even though you tried to play the hero, even though it was out of your hands, it was you he wanted to see. It’s you that they’ll blame. 'Shut up,' he said again, this time louder. He allowed his hand to graze the bridge of his nose, but pulled it quickly away. The area was still a little tender from where it had been introduce to Mr. Bridden’s knee. For all he knew it was broken. He didn’t let the doctor get that far. You should have had it checked out. You know you should. And why not? What is it then? Afraid that someone else, someone new, will tell you that you’re crazy? Maybe that you should be locked up, committed, bound in a straightjacket? 'Shut up!' He was on his feet now, pacing. Hid hands clenched behind his head, forearms pressed against the headphones that covered his ears. Royal Bliss, 'Save Me.' The girl, Ramone, are you really going to fight with her? You know you can’t do that. You know you won’t do that. It’s against everything that you believe, everything that you’ve been brought up to believe. Your opportunities have never been plentiful, but we know that when it comes right down to it you’ll be respectful, won’t you? You’ll play the plucky hero, and it will earn you nothing. Another woman will walk all over you, and you won’t do a damned thing about it. Don’t tell us to shut up. You can’t drown us out, can’t make us go away. Casting Crowns, 'Set Me Free.' The gambler and the anti-hero, both are irrelevant. The 'holy man', Connors, he is quite relevant. Can you bring yourself to fight him as well? Two of your closest friends, traveling partners, teammates, they trusted him. They trusted you. What would their reaction be if you were to hurt him? He’s smaller than you, you know. 3 inches and a few dozen pounds. He’s wiry, he’s intelligent, cunning, and vile. Whatever you are, he is the inverse. By right you should destroy each other. Tell us what you’ll do, your highness. Justify it to Your Conscience. He wandered the roof for a few moments, finally coming to a stop near the river-facing wall. The king crouched low, resting his chin on his folded arms, which in turn were resting atop the wall. The rain had started falling, it’s timing coaxing a smile from the otherwise melancholy king. 'This must be what it’s like being Batman.'
