Date: Whenever ONE. Fear and Loathing at The Bottom of the World. Random musings on a night like tonight. Bad vibes and worse weather, rain clouds hovering overhead and thunder fucking with the stars. You can’t see the moon for the skyscrapers, and even if you could, they would all look as dirty as the streets beneath your feet. I hate it here, and so does everyone else. We drink and dine on our depression and self loathing, breathing smoke and drinking warm beer turned sour. Someone pats me on the shoulder to tell me they’re my biggest fan. If they really loved me, they’d kill themselves and leave me alone. Nashville is a hub town where all the filth of the South come to mingle. You have your failed musicians in one corner, your white supremacists in the other, a famous face staggering down the sidewalk after a coke party at some west side loft, and your leftover rubbish making due with whatever scraps everyone else has left behind. My hometown is an hour away, with tumbleweeds and fields of dust. While Nashville tries to look alive, the rest of Tennessee sleeps on an acre of bones, bleeding dust and waiting for something interesting to happen. Nothing interesting ever happens here. With the heat driving us all rabid and dreams of Alaska still beating the back of my brain like some impossible utopia too far for me to reach, I stand and stumble on to the next bar, the next shithole, the next pitcher of beer. Somewhere, John Dent is crying and his lovely wife is thinking of me. At least some things are right with the world, even if they’re few and far between. Baptized in neon light, finding your savior at the bottom of a beer glass, reading scriptures off of bathroom stalls. Here, at the bottom of the world, with the right kind of eyes, you can almost see God. And he’s laughing at you. --- TWO. John Dent’s Saturday Night Special. I stumbled into my hotel room just before the dawn, when the night was darker than a bottomless pit. I flipped on the lights and made my way for the bathroom, my stomach turning flips and my throat already clogging itself up with vomit. I knelt, with the toilet as my altar, and coughed up my mortal remains. It tasted like beer, cigarettes, and a piss poor hamburger from down the street. That’s when I heard the click of a gun and felt the cold stare of a broken man. I looked over my shoulder, wiping my lips off and raising an eyebrow. "Hey John," I muttered out, looking down the barrel of a stub-nose revolver. "How’s the wife?" He hit me with the butt of the gun, sending a quick pain through my entire head. I fell to the tiled floor, my head busted open and blood dripping down in a little stream. I grinned a crooked smile up toward John, laughing out loud as he shoved the nose of the revolver into my mouth. "Sterling, you’ve gone too far this time," He muttered out, between clenched teeth. "I could forgive all that bullshit from our childhood. Hell, I don’t even mind that time when you told the newspaper I was a closet faggot. But this time? This time you’ve gone too far." With a revolver between your lips, all you can do is speak in vowels. It wasn’t that I didn’t want to reply with some witty remark, it was more that I couldn’t. "Do you know how many years Brenda and I have been married, Sean?" He sneered down at me, his eyes already beginning to tear up, the snarl on his lips quivering with every word. "Five years, Sean. Five perfect, irreplaceable years. Then, in one fell swoop, you come riding in on your high horse and destroy everything. Do you realize how that feels, Sean? Do you have any fucking idea?!" His finger quaked at the trigger, quivering and scared, as he knelt down and looked me dead in the eyes, a voice of anger and absolute resentment escaping from between his lips, "Do you know how it feels to watch while your world begins to crumble and you can’t do a damn thing about it?" The tears began to stream, his eyes watering like rivers down his cheeks. "This is what you get, Sterling. This is what happens, you insignificant little fuck." Silence, save the sound of the ceiling fan spinning in the next room. I closed my eyes and waited for the bullet, holding my breath as John Dent’s finger pressed at the trigger, either building up suspense or too nervous to get the job done. I cracked an eye open and looked up at John, his eyes glued closed and his teeth grinding against themselves. His breath went heavy and the veins in his forehead pulsed. I rolled my eyes, slowly pulling my lips away from the gun. John did nothing to stop me. Carefully, like walking through a minefield, I stood to my feet, putting a hand on Dent’s shoulder and shaking my head back and forth, as if disappointed. "Johnny, you never did have the balls to get the job done." He wiped his eyes, sputtering like a kid who just found out Santa Claus wasn’t real. I reached into my pocket and took out two cigarettes, one for myself and one for John. I put one between his lips and lit it for him before beginning to walk away, out towards the living room. Just as I crossed the thresh hold, I heard his voice one more time, "Fuck you, Sean." And then, I felt the bullet rip through my shoulder. --- Random musings on a night like tonight. Gunshots and mad dogs on every street corner, crack heads and cannibals eating themselves alive. Laugh, and the world laughs with you, even if there was nothing to laugh at in the first place. I’ve killed men and men have tried to kill me, for one reason or another. Mostly, I’ve done it out of self defense, but there’s always that occasional just-for-the-fuck-of-it. We’re a nation founded on death, so why not keep the tradition alive? I used to fear death, but now I just accept it. It’s unavoidable. Eventually, no matter who you are or how successful you’ve been, you’re going to float off into that infinite nothing to meet whatever god or devil you’ve made your peace with. How I’ve managed to stay alive this long is anybody’s guess, whether it be by the splendor of luck or the grace of God. I never asked to be a part of a world this fucked up. I never asked to be brought to life by some immaculate eye in the sky that can’t be stopped, but lo and behold. Here I am and here I’ll be, until someone or something takes me back to where I belong. While we all collectively piss our lives away, all we can do is look on the bright side. Someday, somewhere over the rainbow, this will all be over and done with. Or at least I certainly hope so. --- THREE. Visions of Heaven. I woke up in a daze, gasping out loud and laying on the carpet, dyed red by my own misfortunes. John Dent wasn’t anywhere in sight, it was just me, the roar of the television, and all the hells of the world come to whisper in my ears. Delusional and delirious, I crawled across the carpet and pulled myself up on the couch, an indescribable ache coursing a path through every bone in my body. I closed my eyes and gritted my teeth, letting my head fall back against the cushions, taking deep breaths and trying to decide what to do now. One look at the clock on the wall would tell me how long I had been out for, but I was almost afraid to look. The sun was up with no traces of night. All the demons of this town had made their way home and were now replaced by corporate zombies staggering through the deadpan streets. One more deep breath and I sat up, craning my neck to look back at my shoulder and the red wound bleeding through my shirt. It hurt, but I’d been through worse. While I was out, I had seen visions of heaven, the pearly gates opening wide like a gaping mouth, and I stood awestruck by it all. Angel trumpets rung out from nowhere and everywhere, the immaculate beauty burning my eyes. Pure. White. Perfect. And in all this mind numbing grandeur, I saw myself in flames, burning alive with eyes wide and an unmistakable horror on my face. On a throne behind me, John Dent sat with the aura of God draped over his body, looking down with a knowing grin as I fell to my knees and dissolved to ashes. And then I woke up. --- FOUR. Amerika. I stumbled down the street, away from the hospital, the burning sensation still pulsing through my back, but the bullet removed. It would leave a nasty scar, but not much else. The city sighed, and so did it’s people, dragging their bones down the sidewalk, bitter and bemused by the workings of the world. Ruined lives in a town where gossip is as good as gospel and every little mistake catches up with you eventually. Television sets glistened in storefronts, preaching politics, while radios puked up bubblegum pop, to take the strain off these tired times. We’re all slaves in one way or another, stumbling down the road to perdition. I found my way around corners and across street walks, looking for something, but I wasn’t quite sure what. My eyes glowed red with anger, more thoughts of revenge running rabid through my head. I walked around all my good intentions and found that there were none, just horrific plans and terrible ideas. I found myself standing in front of a gun shop, looking in with a slow grin forming on my face. I pulled the handle and stepped inside, licking my lips with cold steel all around. The clerk tipped his hat with a knowing smile, as if he was reading my intentions just from the look on my face. This was where madmen made their last stands and broken souls found their way out. With a new gun tucked in my coat pocket, I staggered back out, sneering with some blind rage as I hailed down a taxi. I fingered the cold trigger in my pocket, everything suddenly seeming so obvious and revenge suddenly seeming even sweeter. Amerika the beautiful, where death can be bought for a fraction of the cost. --- Random musings on a night like tonight. Cold air and crimson skies, a summer long drained of it’s majesty, only to replaced by malice and spite. Malevolent forces flow tonight, dreaming schemes up for non-dreamers and shoving them into their heads as if they had been there all along. The human race began with the burden of free thought and some half-assed excuse for intelligence. Though I’m sure it will end just as it began, I’m also sure we’ll all be worse off than ever before. Take the gun and take the shot, turn the tides of time. The things we do to each other, as well as ourselves, is insane at best. While the world weeps for our asinine decisions, we carry on as if we’re really living. But that’s the thing. That’s the whole grand joke of it all. None of us are ever really living, we’re all just going through the motions and pretending we’re happy. Hang the heathens, hate the peculiar, own nothing except your body and it’s worthlessness. Die alone and hope for Kingdom Come. Mankind is a plight, a disease, a misfortune for all parties involved, and that’s how it will always be. Point your fingers and sing your songs, save yourself with religion’s uselessness or hope’s hopelessness. But don’t forget how trivial it all really is. Feed the animals and fuck the world. --- FIVE. A picture of her husband and herself in happier times sat on the dresser, all smiles. I was smiling too. "Sean," She muttered out in a voice as meek as a mouse. "You don’t have to do this, Sean." "I’m afraid I do, Brenda…" I sighed lazily, propping myself up on my shoulders. "You see, unlike some people, I have some pride, some archaic morals that transcend what’s right and wrong. And trust me, love, this is definitely right to me." Tears began to stream, realizing that her life was about to come to a terrible halt. "I’ll make sure to send John along soon after you go, so I promise you won’t be alone for long," I smiled, kissing her cheek. "Shh, shh, shh. Don’t cry, Brenda. It’s just death." "T-That’s not why I’m crying, Sean…" She said, voice barely rising above a whisper. "I… I found out today… that I’m pregnant. My first child. I haven’t told John yet." "Don’t worry, Brenda," I purred seductively, tangling my fingers in her hair. "I’ll be sure to tell him for you." Bang. The gunshot rang throughout the house as the blood splattered on the pillow, her eyes gone wide and her mouth fully open. I yawned and rolled off the bed, pulling up my pants and sliding the gun into my pocket. I left a note at the end of the bed reading, "Here’s to you, John." and left the house with a cigarette between my lips. Fools will flee and fires will flicker as failures and friends douse themselves with liquor. All is well and nothing is wrong, so until the end, we’ll carry on. Good night.
