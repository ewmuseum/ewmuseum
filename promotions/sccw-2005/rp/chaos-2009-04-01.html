Chaos.

1 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/01/2009 This world is turning into chaos… Because when there is too much order, chaos is the ultimate result. People have been too complacent with the order of their existence. Their happy little worlds are falling apart before they even realize that there is no hope left for them. All of this happened because the order on both ends of the spectrum slowly gave way to chaos and anarchy. Civilization crumbles the moment everybody realizes the truth… We didn’t have a choice. It’s in our nature to destroy ourselves. Sad part is, a machine from the worst Terminator movie made thus far said this and what makes it even sadder is he was correct. Nothing left of the order we once had. The greedy, highly educated scum of the world got too greedy and have destroyed the economy. On the opposite end of the spectrum, the free thinkers have gotten too out of control and bred common sense out of humanity. Only people left are crazies, psychos, and idiots. The only thing left is… H O C A S Everywhere Nowhere To escape to. Nope hope for us all. I have come to the conclusion that no Luke Skywalker or Jesus will come to save us. We’re F U C K E D Screwed And the worst part is We deserve it. Wake up laying on a scroll of some sort that I am using as a mattress. Next to me are two empty bottles of Miller Light and a doobie. What the hell happened last night? I shrug my shoulders and get to my feet. Looking around, I’m downtown somewhere. Fucking Vegas. All the casinos look the same. Look down at myself. Feel homeless… Look homeless… Maybe I am homeless. At this point I wait for Garbage Bag Johnny to come in and shot me in the head for gimmick infringement but soon realize that I am not looking at myself but the guy next to me. Wiping my brow and relief, I look down at the scroll and realize that it is a poster from the arena of the last Just Wrestling card of the tour. Luckily it wasn’t a Dead Sea Scroll or something. For some reason I wait for a power up. It never comes. Looking down, I see my opponent is Clark Fox. Great, I’m facing a Fox. Just then a spaceship comes in from the clouds piloted by Star Fox! Running from laser blasts, I hide behind a building covered in graffiti from one of the thousand or so gangs here in Vegas. Living and dying in Vegas is what I’m doing Clark Fox is the last thing on my mind. Well, I also have to worry about losing in the MVP Battle Royal again. It’ll probably be something like this with either Erik Magnum, Meat, or Aaron Nothings playing Star Fox and trying to roast me like a marshmallow over a fire. The end result will be my death. FAINT . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . A W A K E Look around and see Tupac, Elvis, and John Lennon staring at me. Closing my eyes and opening them again, the three men are gone. In their place are three Mexican guys talking about me in Spanglish. I hope they aren’t cholos. Upon further review, they are just regular illegal immigrants. I get up and start walking down the dark, deserted street. Shade barely gives me any relief from the burning hot desert sun. Walk past a tree and it S T R E E E T C C C H E S It’s fingers out to try and grab me like the giant hand of God. Shadows follow me. Shadows in the forms of monsters. Dark, evil beings from the dark side. Coming from Hell to take me down with them. At this point I begin to think that this is all a rib put together by Clark Fox just to fuck with my head. Then I realize that Fox is an idiot and couldn’t possibly plan this. After all, he is a fox. Foxes are little red dogs that belong in a zoo, not in a Just Wrestling ring. Sun blinds me Cover eyes Run for shade But there is none. Dark omen for the future. Going to lose twice this week. Once to one of the super predators of Just in the MVP Battle Royal and once to Clark Fox, a man who drinks beer and eats vaginas. Male vaginas That are dirty. And small. See a hot Mexican chick walking across the street. Fuck the shade! I run over there. It’s really a man. He looks like Shipley in drag. Image perturbs me Mind melting S U N Melting B R A I N. I am now brain dead. I can finally get why Disaster Movie is supposed to be funny. Pull out gun and shoot myself in the head. , . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . But then there would be no match. I am alive. Look over and see my gun was really my index finger. Must’ve channeled Travis Bickle there. Or maybe Maynard Crane. I wait for a piece of Meat to hit me in the head with a Skillet. It never happens. Bored. I keep on walking And walking And walking. Streets have no end. Sidewalks become attached to my feet. Now I know what Godzilla feels like. Have a match this week. Instead stuck in Vegas wandering aimlessly. Find a motel. Get a room. Find a slut. Fuck her. Go to sleep. ZzZzZzZzzzzzz ZzzzzzzzZZZZZZzzzzz ZzzzzZZZzZZZZzzZzZzZz… . . . . . . . . . . . Mind filled with images of giant horned lizards attacking Vegas. I run for my life only to realize they are giant hamsters. Look around and see I am in South Park. Maybe von Doom has finally taken over the world. Highly unlikely. Door opens and I go inside. There I find a giant ostrich waiting for me. It talks to me about Buddhist philosophy and why I should join the religion. He quickly sees that isn’t going to work and tries to convert me to Mormonism instead. Doesn’t work either. Blake Gray then attacks me with a pierce of barbed wire! Aaron Nothings then hits me with a chair! Bum rushed by Erik Magnum with a baseball bat! Rub eyes Look more closely And they are really the Three Stooges! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . AWAKE! Look around, no Stooges. However next to me is a naked woman. I fucked her. Hangover from all the alcohol kicks in. Think I’m starting to turn into a nocturnal feeder. Maybe I’m a vampire except for I crave human pussy instead of blood. I wait for the sunlight to burn my flesh. Look out window. Realize it is night. Feel like an idiot. At this point I kill myself with my index finger again. Can’t die. Have a match And Shipley will kill me. He has had people killed before. Ever wonder where all of those old superstars from rW and AWC went? Yes. Shipley killed them. With a candlestick in the hallway no less. Col. Mustard and Professor Plum saw the whole thing. They told me last night before they tried to kill me for having sex with their daughters. I escaped after they ran out of beers and forgot about me. Everything turning to chaos all around me. A giant wormhole appears in front of me, trying to suck me in like a vacuum cleaner. See images of strange and powerful forces floating in front of me like bubbles… Images of Meat… Erik Magnum… The Illustrious Face Eater… Aaron Nothings… Aaron Cross… Jon Kano… Wone… Hate… Maynard Crane… Blake Gray… TIM SHIPLEY… Dr. Giggle… Get back to this universe. Stare at wall. Get my stuff together and get ready for show. Realize I have no car and will probably have to walk for a year through the desert of Nevada and the other wilderness areas of the United States. Wish I had a car Realize I have one It doubles as my house Realize that my life is in shambles. Everything is in chaos. It’s just like the state of the world. The smart people with the high GPAs and degrees caused the economic mess, the crazy zealots from the Muslim world are attacking and causing conflicts, elected officials are in scandals, heads of state are bickering like children, and common sense has been bred out of the normal people so now most everyone is crazy. All of the order that we built for ourselves… Has crumbled into chaos. Chaos is inevitable. We’ve had order for too long. It’s about time everything turned into anarchy. Just Wrestling Is The Same Way. It will eventually become chaos. I know this. The fact that I know this scares me. Maybe I’m more tuned into the universe than the rest of the roster. Aaron Nothings is the champion and will never lose. Nobody will defeat him. He’s like an Evil Jesus or something. Convert or die. He will kill us all. But I must destroy him before Just simply just fades away into the darkness with all of the other lost souls that will die when the chaos becomes too much and the end comes. I must save everyone… I MUST! And I must save myself first before it’s too late. I’m the only hope for humanity. That very fact should scare the shit out of everyone. Too bad they don’t give a shit about the fate of the world.
