Trouble with The Black List??

6 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-6-2008 The scene opens up inside the production facility of SCCW located in Las Vegas. We can see SCCW’s sexiest interviewer Mary Jackson leaning up against one of the boards being fitted for a microphone by one of the techs. He scurries off camera and she adjusts her blouse which makes the microphone come to rest in-between her ample cleavage. Jackson: Good afternoon, Mary Jackson here, coming to you from the headquarters of SCCW. As Mary talks a graphic appears over her right shoulder with the words The Black List in bold letters with the ghostbusters type slash through the words. Jackson: The fallout from SCCW’s first Pay Per View continues to unfold. Rumors have been swirling all week about the demise of one of the newest factions in SCCW The Black List. Rumors began as early as right after the Pay Per View itself. Even before the ring had begun to be torn down there was tension backstage. That was where I got an earful from a bloody and aggravated Wyld Child Zakk Wylde. The graphic behind changes into a shot of Zakk Wylde backstage in the Miramar Marine Corps Air Station in San Diego the location of Snake Eyes. The camera zooms in as the graphic springs to life. We see Wylde still dressed in wrestling attire. He is exiting the trainer’s office with several of the staff trailing after him urging him to return and let them continue their work. Wylde: If you all don’t get your hands off of me….I swear I will sue your asses for everything your great grandchildren will own!!! Mary Jackson comes into the picture microphone in hand. Mary: Mr. Wylde…Mr. Wylde could I get a word with you about what just happened out there a few moments ago? Wylde looks overjoyed to see a microphone and camera in his face. Wylde: What happened out there? You seriously want to ask me what happened out there! Do they not let you guys watch the show from where ever you lurk? Waiting to pounce on us at any given turn? I guess I should be thankful that its you that found me and not that idiot Ethan Peters, you are a lot easier on the eyes than that guy. If you really want to go for the obvious question first, I got my ass handed to me and I didn’t have all the back up I was supposed to….that is what happened out there!! I’m standing here blood running down my face and I’m about 15 pounds lighter without my strength in numbers title belt around my waist. Mary: You really seem to need medical attention. Why would you refuse to be treated…to be totally honest with you, you don’t look so good right now. The camera zooms in on the face of the Wyld Child. His face is the proverbial crimson mask, as Wylde continues to speak and his emotions continue to boil over you can actually see blood continuing to pump from the gash in his forehead. Wylde: I wouldn’t let those medical school drop outs touch me no matter how hurt or how much in pain I was in. I’ll have my personal physician flown in to treat me. Besides the way…how did you put it…not looking so good serves a purpose right now. Mary: Serves a purpose? What could that be? Wylde: It fuels my rage. It serves to remind me how pissed off I am right now! Mary: Over losing a tag team title? Things happen… Wylde shakes his head and a combination of sweat and blood fly from his forehead, his voice changes to almost a growl as he cuts Mary off. Wylde: Things like this do NOT happen to Zakk Wylde!!! It doesn’t matter if it’s a tag team title or a Chinese checkers championship! Zakk Wylde is NOT a loser…Zakk Wylde does NOT accept a defeat, especially one like that out there tonight! Jensen you and your freak of a partner have something that belongs to me. Please…please I am begging you to take really good care of those title belts cause if I have anything to do with it…they will be coming back to me where they belong really…REALLY soon! Mary: Well I guess I can see your point but is it really worth risking your health over it, I’m sure you have a rematch clause in your contract. Wylde: I’m not only pissed off about the loss of those titles. After staggering back to the Black List dressing room looking to rip Caleb a new ass for that half ass performance out there what do I find? Nobody!! No Caleb, No Gideon, No Mr. W. The place was totally empty. So I’m sorry if I’ve been a bit tense at this moment. That I’m not representing the SCCW in the most positive light right now. Well tough. When I surround myself with someone…when I allow someone in my inner circle I’m supposed to trust them completely. I don’t allow just anyone to get that close to me. So when I look around and they aren’t there….yeah…I tend to get a little pissed off. I put this whole deal together and things have just not worked out quite the way I had planned for them to. So maybe I need to go in a different direction. Maybe I need to take care of business myself. The image of Wylde once again freezes. This time it’s on a close up of his face. His jet black mane matted with sweat and the blood still pouring out from his wound. His piercing ice blue eyes the only other color on his face. The corner of his lips curled up in a snarl. Mary: So there you have it. Straight from the man himself…trouble within the Black List. One of the interns comes up and whispers into Mary’s ear. Mary: Are you serious? The intern nods his head that is he quite serious. Mary: Well it seems we have an unexpected treat. Live on the phone with us right now is the former SIN champion the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde! Mr. Wylde to what do we owe this rather pleasant surprise? Wylde: (over the phone)You are quite right about one thing Ms. Jackson, this indeed is a pleasure for you. It could be in other ways if you played your cards right….but I am straying from my reason for calling. It was supposed to be a business call not one for pleasure. I’m calling in today because someone needs to set you straight about a few things. Mary: Oh really. What would that be? Wylde: Well you are spreading some pretty tall tales there to the fine fans of SCCW. Mary: If you are here to tell me that you didn’t say all of that, that I just played for the fine fans we can play it again. Wylde: Oh I’m not going to deny that I was quite upset the other night. There is no denying the legendary Wyld Child temper. But you are quite wrong about the demise of the Black List. Mary: Shall I play that last line over again..the one where you said you should take care of things yourself? Wylde: Ramblings of a man that has lost a few too many pints of blood my dear. There are NO issues with the Black List. I have sat down with the parties involved and we are in the process of making amends for the way things went down. So if you are done spreading lies I really need to get back to work on preparing for my match this week. Mary: Well since we have you on the phone…could we get your comments on your opponent. Upton Osgood. Wylde: Well I do have a few moments before my massage. Okay you twisted my arm…but just because you are so darn cute Mary. I’ve done my research on my opponent. Looks like I’m going up against someone straight from a Stephen King novel not a professional wrestler. A little too convenient that he is from the Bangor Maine area don’t you think? Again I am getting off the path here. I’ve been all over this world. Wrestled in many different places, high schools, arenas, steel cages and boiler rooms but I don’t think I’ve ever faced someone with such an unusual…mmmm what is the most politically correct thing I could say here…there is someone on the button to censor me if necessary right Mary? Mary: Let’s just forget the whole birth defect for a moment could we? Wylde: Yeah sure as you wish. I’m not going to dwell on it either. I do have a message for Mr. Osgood. Upton, you want to endear yourself to the fans by saying you are a regular guy…living in a double wide trailer. Well I’m not going to apologize for my huge house, I’m not going to say I’m sorry because I’m filthy rich. I flaunt my money not because I want to rub it in peoples faces….I do it because I am going to enjoy that money before I cash in all my chips for good. I don’t have any kids to leave the money to, that I know of. I don’t have any family that I want to take care of…they didn’t take care of me back in the day so screw them. That is also why I about puked when I heard about him trying to play the sympathy card with the stories of how he was teased and made fun of all his life. We’ve all had at some point or another a bully that we all stood up to. Your story isn’t all that special…just because you have some extra birth defect doesn’t get you extra points. Mary: I don’t think he is trying to pull anything, or try to get any sympathy from anyone. Aren’t you worried about him using that birth defect during the match? Wylde: Well he can sure as hell try Mary. He can sure as hell try. First off he has to get me in a position to use that. I’m not going to let that happen. I’m going into that ring and take care of Black List business and set the record straight. So Upton…don’t take this ass beating personally. You were just in the wrong place at the wrong time. You will be famous though…not for your defect…but for having your debut and final match in SCCW in the same night! See you on Campus kids… With that the phone line goes dead. Mary has a shocked look on her face as she had other follow up questions for Wylde. Mary: Well…I guess it was time for that massage. I’m sure it’s not on of a therapeutic variety. Well that is all we have for now….don’t forget to tune in for the next exciting edition of Temptation coming to you live from the campus of the University of California. For all of us in the SCCW control center I’m Mary Jackson…see you in the arenas! Fade out.
