Good little bar if ya like peerin’ at pussy and drinkin’ good, cold beer. You’d be surprised how little effort is spent these days in maintainin’ a good beer fridge in a bar. Not my pops. Not Ernie Monday. His staff don’t fill them fridges up at the end of shift then they don’t got no job come tomorrow. This beer in my hand… it’s damn cold. Run it over my forehead coz bein’ round so many people tends to get me wired. Can almost here the steam comin’ off the beer on the touch’f my skin. I jus’ guess I don’t like crowds. Feelin’ all trapped in and herded by ‘em. Maybe I AM a wild animal, like some folk think I am, I jus’ like that open space and bein’ able to run free. Like a fucken gazelle or somethin’. Shit. Probably a cigarette by the deep exhalation I hear next. "I done it again." "Done what again?" "I can’t help it. It… it just happens. I just… I did it again." Can’t make heads or tails outta what this idiot’s yammerin’ about. "Done what again? The fuck is this anyways?" Can hear a sob at the other end of the line. WHY DON’T IT LEAVE ME THE FUCK ALONE? I DON’T WANT THIS! I… DON’T… WANT… THIS!" Here some sorta whizzin’ noise then a big ol’ bang that makes me pull the phone away from my ear. Still wonderin’ ‘bout who the Hell was on the other end of the line. Someone that was reachin’ out to me. Maybe they had a demon so big they needed the baddest man they ever knew to help ‘em fight it. I’ve fought my demons. I’m at peace with them now. My demons got buried the minute I met my daughter. The minute I met my grandson. Whoever it was… they was in some serious trouble. And they needed me. Nobody calls me for chit-chat. They called me coz I was their last chance. Least… it sounded that way. I realise I gone an’ broke into a cold sweat by the panic I felt in my gut.
