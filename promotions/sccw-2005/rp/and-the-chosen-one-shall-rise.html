And The Chosen One Shall Rise

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The following conversation takes place following Stigmata’s match that took place in Tokyo, we first begin in the locker room of the Devil’s reject who along with Luci have just watched Stigmata decimate both members of MEDS with multiple chokeslams. Legion: And here he is now the decimator himself! Luci: That was just brilliant, Devlin’s such a glutton for punishment, I thought you’d get tired of just slamming him down after a while but then I remembered you just don’t stop when you get going right. (She winks) (Legion glares at Luci after that remark.) Legion: No need for that type of comment while we’re here, what did I tell you after the Savant incident? Luci sighs. Luci: Business before pleasure comes first due to the mistakes created in the past blah blah blah, I know you’re trying to make sure nothing’s going to go crazy again but you’ve got to allow for some fun. Stigmata: Indeed. For me killing that little annoyance was fun and all the fun that’ll I need tonight, fucker hitting me with a chair thinks he can get away with that, he was very mistaken. Luci: Has Adrienne said anything yet? Legion: Let’s see. (Legion’s phone vibrates) Ah here we are. The phone screen reads: Thanks for the help tonight, you shall be rewarded soon! Mwah ASG Luci: (sing-song) Business before pleasure! Legion: Very funny. (Legion’s phone suddenly rings.) Hello? Ah Charles good to hear from you again, it’s been too long how are things in the office? (Yes the other person on the line is Charles Faulkner, the intern for Kent Fusiliers that Legion kept a good friendship with since his Devil’s Advocate stint last year.) Charles: It’s not bad Mr Knight but I may have some news that may bring your mood down tonight. Legion: What might that be? Charles: Next week you’re going to Iraq and according to Nigel people are required to be there for morale purposes. Legion: Excuse me? I have no choice in the matter? That’s just great I’m going to be heading into a place where I could be killed, that’s just wonderful (!) Stigmata: Do you need me to sort this out? Legion: Stigmata, not yet. Let me go to ASG’s office later on. (Sometime after the show) Legion: Adrienne, I need your help here, Nigel has apparently put us into a venue I feel is a risk to all employees concerned! ASG: think about it Gabe, it’ll do well for us business wise at least, you and the others showing the alt culture side, Stigmata destroying people, it’ll go well. Legion: I’m sorry honey I just feel that it’s too risky for us to do unless you have something in mind. ASG: In fact I do, the idea is the chosen one battle royal, you get the chance to beat people like Xavier, Jared, von Doom… Legion: And what would be the reward? ASG: Now you know I can’t reveal that just yet! But you’ll love it! Legion: If you can make sure we’re secure then I’ll do it. ASG: Don’t worry I’ll make sure of it, you helped me like you promised. I'll think of something. (Legion nods in a thank you motion and leaves the room.)
