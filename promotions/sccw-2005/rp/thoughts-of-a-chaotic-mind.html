Thoughts Of A Chaotic Mind

Date: Today They always say a week is a long time in politics, they could say the same about professional wrestling, then again this business always reminded me of a quote that Hunter S Thompson once said about the music industry: ‘The music business is a cruel and shallow money trench, a long plastic hallway where thieves and pimps run free, and good men die like dogs. There's also a negative side.’ It’s weird, most people that watch Temptation every week know me as the guy that’s trying to send the man now known as Jason Ryan over the edge in order to join the Chaos stable. Of course the past two weeks seemed to have turned that plan around as Jason now decided to join me without me having to mess with his life too much. As I’m writing these thoughts down I also remember that since the last Stand show Luci hasn’t called to discuss plans, last time she rang me she was near tears… She was crying how that she never told a close friend how she loved her and considering that the friend hadn’t kept in touch she was worried that very bad things had come to her. Of course when that close friend is Lauren Fox the paranoia is quite heightened given Lauren’s associations in the past with Alex Pierce. I guess she’s dealing with her own grief at the moment, I guess the training she got from Lazarus a few months ago paid off, she’s learnt how to meditate her anger to come out at the right point. This week however I found out I would be facing my new associate along with the H.I.T in a street fight, given the fact that he had began his career in Sin City on a winning streak it was time to plan. People may think I’m crazy for trying to cover all the angles such as what if von Doom decides to get involved, whether if Jason decides to attack me and getting his aggression out which means my 3 strikes threat towards him has to be shelved at least for that night. If Wyatt Connors can plan everything down to the smallest detail like he’s a time Master with his little Booster Gold and Skeets surely the same could apply for my plans. Everything’s no longer a game but then again was it ever one to begin with?
