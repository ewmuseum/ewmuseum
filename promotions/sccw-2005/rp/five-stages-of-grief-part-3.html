Five Stages Of Grief Part 3

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The car pulled up outside Legion’s apartment, inside of the car were two occupants: Luci and the man driving the wheel being Roderick Ashe. The look on Luci’s face was that of a child sulking because it hadn’t got it’s own way, in this case the way it wanted was to have caused great harm to Alex Pierce after accusing her of murdering who she considered her lover in Lauren Fox. Ashe turned off the ignition and carried Luci to the door and knocked. Legion opened the door to the sight of Luci kicking Ashe’s back and gave a look of disapproval, the kind a parent would do after a child had disobeyed them by drawing on a wall when told not to. He then opened his mouth trying to not let the anger get the best of him "She showed up at the wake didn’t she? Just put her down Roderick for me and I’ll talk to her." Ashe then put her down making sure she sat down on the ground, digging a charcoal gray cigar from his pocket. He flicked a match against the wall, scoring the white paint. 'Yer chickadee is either fuggin' insane or rilly loved her. Tried to pop Allie inna jaw 'fore she left. At a fuggin' *wake*. Ain't cool t'disrepect the dead like 'at.' Legion then gulped when he heard about the attempted attack, the feeling of dread started to overcome him as he always feared a reprisal from Alex Pierce ever since he had turned her offer to join her back at Homecoming, granted he thought times were different, she had Amy to help humanise her in a way. Maybe, just maybe he would be safe. 'I figger you oughta expect a phone call inna next day or so, 'cuz 'Lex is bent up about this death fing.' The big man unleashed a puff of foul-smelling smoke towards Legion's face. 'Be nice, be respectful. Fings're changin' but yer girl's pushin' buttons y'don' wann'er to, catch?' Ashe’s last words made Legion realise he could be wrong the words ‘Oh Shit’ suddenly ran through his mind over and over. Legion then with a nod of his head beckoned Luci to follow him in. The anger finally could be shown as Legion’s voice bellowed: "WHAT THE HELL HAVE YOU DONE? Do you realise that we are now going to probably FUCKING die now you tried to attack Alex at Lauren’s wake, I told you not to go there! The retort from Luci was just as sharp: "You know fuck all about love Gabe, you and your closed emotions to the world in order to make so called plans work which to be fair haven’t had much success have they? Legion then said something that maybe in the future he may just regret: "You’ve felt LUST and only lust, in fact that’s why I made sure it was you I picked from the start before the night at the Club. The room suddenly became silent until a vibration of a phone could be heard. Present Day Once again the Rev 22:20 logo filled the video screen, this time in order to access the video the password was the name off the third track off the second album by the band Protest The Hero. We see Legion sitting on a chair wearing a Crack The Skye t-shirt leaning back staring at the camera, behind him is Stigmata and Luci (for those wondering the camera is placed on a tripod for this promo) "So it seems that my prophecy of destruction is coming two weeks ago unwittingly came true, we now have Lane Stevens as our main representative as he finally believes he can grasp himself to be more than the sidekick of a man who claimed to be The Seventh Sin." Legion then begins to laugh quite heartily at the mention of Clinton Sage. "I mean the man said so himself, he represented Kathryn Shaw’s Dead Man’s Hand, not Lane Stevens’ Dead man’s Hand, the reason I mention this is that we have been invited to a summit that Mr Stevens is holding in a cursed town to try and defeat Desade, consider this my RSVP to your little summit because after all things can happen that may prove fruitful indeed." "But onto my main focus for this week as I am in a triple threat match against Jacob Mckali, a man before he made a triumphant return was the Scourge of FUSE and then got embroiled in match fixing…, Suzanne Larsson who made a mistake by trying send a message by attacking me following our match at Canadian Chaos and in the middle of it all…" Legion then gets up and walks closer to the camera "James Fucking von Doom being the referee, when are you going to learn James, I beat you fair and square at Sin on Spike and I thought that was a message for you to get the hint, obviously not and the fact we have a man that could potentially fix the match so he wins and your little game of alliance then stab in the back I feel that I may have a bit of a battle here." Luci then chimes in a quick response: "Legion, doesn’t Suzanne want to make von Doom and I quote ‘wish he wasn’t fucking born’ this week? "This is true Luci and you forget I have one friend in high places that can make sure von Doom doesn’t try and pull any tricks, so james I’d watch your back along with Jacob and Suzanne because any tricks and the ninth gate will be coming to you." "One last thing Nigel don’t think that I’ve forgotten about you either, to quote Protest the hero ‘Those who ride against us will be murdered where they stand... the royalty must die.'' The screen faded out with the line All Hail the New Flesh.
