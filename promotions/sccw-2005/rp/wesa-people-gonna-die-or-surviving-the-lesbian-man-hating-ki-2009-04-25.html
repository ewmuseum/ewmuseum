Wesa People Gonna Die?! or Surviving the Lesbian Man-Hating Kingdom of Queens Aimz and Desade as they try to kill me in my Dreams.

25 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/25/09 WAKE UP! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Alarm clock goes off again… Eyes try to open… Can’t open them. Feels like I’ve been run over by a Mack truck. Look over and see two naked women next to me. That might explain why. Head throbbing… Wish it was my other head. LOOKS DOWN Fucking chicks made me limp. Well, for now they did. Probably broke my dick. These psychotic, wild women I keep sleeping with are insane. Well not as insane as the normal human being that you run into the street. Except these bitches be crazy in bed. I get a full body workout from sex whereas most guys don’t get this much muscle tone from roids. Get up, get my shit together, and head out. Turn my key to the man at the lobby but then look over and see a picture of a legion of soldiers on the world. That makes me think of my match in SCCW… Or is it Just? Or is it Empire Pro? Can’t remember…can’t think anymore. Facing a legion this week… A legion of monsters! HOLY SHIT! WESA PEOPLE GONNA DIE?! This week in a tag match and I am teaming with a question mark. So is the Legion of Doom. Wait… That’s gimmick infringement. Jesus is gonna sue somebody. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Suddenly, the musical beats run through my head. I can feel the words coming out of my mouth. Before I know it, I’m rapping out my newest lyrical jam. [lyrics]Legion of super villains Rollin’ across the plains Like lambs through a lion filled savannah And not the one in Georgia Lost like Will Ferrell This summer in movie theaters Or a cock Stuck in some chick’s ass Going around talkin’ about the end of the world Like some sort of apocalyptic cult Or going on a hunt like Vincent in Collateral But I mean it literal I’m a wolf on the hunt And no, I’m not smoking any blunts Even though four twenty was this week Your future is bleak If Legion and his question mark win the match There will be no more pussy to snatch Because the world will end And no one will survive, not even you my friend[/lyrics] The guy who works at the check out area in the lobby is looking at me like I’m insane. I have no idea why. Generally I figure most humans break out into raps every third or fourth day of the week. I go out to my rental car and climb inside. All alone again… Everyday feels the same… All of it is just a blur. A blur that all leads to nothing and nowhere. I am headed toward oblivion and Legion is ready to send me there. Driving down an endless highway, I see nothing but cars. No nature or wildlife anywhere. Just a giant concrete jungle that never ends. The people are the wildlife. They have all sorts of tattoos and piercing which make them look like some sort of African tribe on the Serengeti. Wrestling is the same way. It’s a traveling tribe of psychotics and maniacs that go raping and pillaging from town to town while beating the crap out of each End up at the arena for some indy show. Of course I’m only going to get paid in food stamps. Fucking Tim Shipley does that. Have my match soon after changing. Lost, of course. Shower. Put clothes on. Clean up my stuff and leave. Drive to hotel. Check in. Pick up woman at bar. Fuck her. Go to… S S S S S LL L L L L L LLLLLLL EEE EEE EEE EEEE EE E E E E E P . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . End up in a dream world of some kind where Desade and Aimz are the queens. Lesbian queens who want all men to die. Sounds like my ex-wife. Wait, I never had an ex-wife. Nevermind. Walk through a haunted forest. Visited by the ghosts of dead wrestlers. They ask my why I’m just another rapping wrestler. I tell them because I do it better. They laugh. I walk away. At least I’m not on roids. Speaking of which, Roid Monsters start chasing me through the woods! Trees sway as they bang into them as they run after me. Juicers. Hate juicers. Except Juice Williams. He’s cool. The Kool Aid Man stops me and tries to convert me to Scientology. Tom Cruise then flies in and kills all the roid monsters with his witchcraft. Conversion begins. They talk to me about Thetans and Xenu. I talk to them about Tupac Shakur and Andre 3000. They have no clue what I’m talking about and say I’m an agent of Xenu. I then PULL OUT MY GUN R.Kelly South Park style and kill them both. Feel someone tapping on my shoulder. Turn around and the Predator in the form of Wyatt Conners is about to kill me. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . AWAKE! Look around and check for bullet holes. I’m alive. Thank god. The song Wrong by Depeche Mode plays in my head as I get up and go about another blur of a day still alive to fight another day.
