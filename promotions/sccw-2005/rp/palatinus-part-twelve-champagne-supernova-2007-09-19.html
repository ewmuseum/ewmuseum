Palatinus: Part Twelve - 'Champagne Supernova'

19 September 2007

Date: September 19th 2007 Palatinus: Part Twelve "Champagne Supernova" "Cos people believe that they're Gonna get away for the summer But you and I, we live and die The world's still spinning round We don't know why Why, why, why, why." "Champagne Supernova" by Oasis (What’s the Story Morning Glory) ******************** June 18th, 2007 3:30 A.M. Joshua leaps from his bed covered in a thick sticky cold sweat. Standing in the dimly lit bedroom, he takes a moment to catch his breath. "What the fuck was that about?" He asks himself aloud. Now it’s been no real mystery that Joshua has been troubled with sleep for many months now, but these more recent dreams have been even more then he can take. "I told you none of it was real." The voice in the back of his mind tells him. "All of it was in your head, all of it and so much more has just been inside of your head, none of it has been real." "None of it’s been real?" Joshua asks himself not feeling the least bit ridiculous. "What do you mean none of it’s been real?" This time the voice doesn’t answer him; instead the phone beside his bed begins to ring softly. A feeling of dread fills his chest, but rather then let the phone wake everyone up he decides to answer it. "Hello..." Joshua says hesitantly. At first there is no response, then finally O’Malley’s voice replies over the phone. "Hello Joshua. I trust you’re beginning to figure things out?" "What do you mean?" Joshua asks his all knowing guide. "I told you that you would need to figure things out on your own, I trust you’ve begun to put the pieces together?" O’Malley asks, a hint of skepticism in his tone. "None of it’s real..." Joshua replies without thinking about what he is saying. "It’s all in my head..." "That is correct Joshua, now you must figure out why it’s there." "Someone is putting it there..." Joshua replies, again without realizing what he is saying as though he knew all along. "That is also correct, but who is putting it there?" O’Malley presses. "Sanctum... Sanctum’s doing it to me, I know he is." Joshua replies allowing the words to flow from him with ease. "Ah... but who is Sanctum?" O’Malley asks. This time Joshua draws a blank. In truth he never really knew who Sanctum was. "I... I don’t know." "When you discover that, you’ll finally understand why this is happening to you, I trust you’ll figure it all out pretty soon." "Can’t you just tell me? That’s not really interfering, you’d be giving me the information I need to find things out on my own." Joshua urges. "I’m sorry Joshua, but this is something you need to figure out on your own. I promise you when it all comes together you’ll understand why." O’Malley explains, then adds "Good night Joshua." And with that the phone is hung up. Joshua stares at the silent receiver before placing it back into its base. O’Malley says that it all lies in the identity of Sanctum Nocturna, and if Joshua ever wants to sleep again he’s going to need to find that out. Joshua looks over to his bed, where Angel lay curled up in the sheet, her ivory skin reflecting off of the little illumination coming in from the window. He sighs, and then returns to bed curling up against her bareback. Quietly he whispers, "I love you." He closes his eyes, and returns to sleep. A sound rattles him. Then another. Slowly, Joshua sits back up in bed, "I’m probably just hearing things." He lets out a long lazy yawn, and then begins to lay down again. As head hits pillow, something hits something in the house. Joshua jolts back up right and flicks the lamp on quickly hopping out of bed. After checking the bedroom Joshua knows nothing was knocked over, or fell from anything in the bedroom. Finally Joshua decides it was something of a dream and returns to bed. He glances over toward Angel and realizes she’s not there, in her place is only a message. "I’m in the house." -S ******************** 4:30 A.M. Sweat pours down his face as he looks under the bed, in the closet and then finally down the hall. His cellphone remains in his right hand, as he leaves Sanctum another message on his voice mail. "I don’t know what you think you’re doing, but let’s finish this!" Joshua hollers into the cellphone, as his feet slap against the hardwood of the stairs. Upon reaching the downstairs he darts to the kitchen to see if Angel is making a snack, or if Sanctum is lying there in wait. What’s he want from me? Joshua asks himself, tearing through bare cupboards and glancing into an empty fridge. Something just doesn’t feel right. He takes his cellphone again and checks his messages, no response from Sanctum. As he storms out of the kitchen and through the living room it dawns on him he can’t find any sign of Angel or the kids anywhere. Using the quick dial button for his wife he waits for her to answer, afraid there won’t be one. When Josh is about to give up a soft voice answers, "hello?" Immediately Joshua feels better, "I’m so glad you picked up, I didn’t see you in bed and thought the absolute worst." A long pause leaves Joshua uncomfortable. "Babe, you still there?" After a second longer, her voice returns however it does not sound soft any longer. "What are you talking about?" Angel asks, her voice becoming accusatory. "What do you want?" "I don’t know…" Joshua says, beginning to get an awkward feeling in his stomach. "But I think I’m starting to understand…" "Are you drunk again? Or are you flying high as a kite, which is it now Josh?" Angel demands. "I’m neither…" He responded softly. "I haven’t been for a long time." "So then, what do you want? Why now, after all this time did you decide to call me?" Angel begins to shout, "It’s been two months and I haven’t heard a word from you, and now you call me and ask why I’m not in bed? Never once did you call to see how Bonnie was, or how Jason was, and now you’re calling me and asking me why I’m not in YOUR BED?" He’s speechless, and when he remains so Angel hangs up the phone, there is nothing left she has to say to him. Joshua places the phone down on the dining room table, kiddie corner from where the living room mirror is. He takes a moment to let it all sink in, but there’s still one piece he hasn’t quite gotten yet. The beep from his cellphone goes off and he notices he has a new voice mail. Snapping out of his pity party he places the cellphone on speaker and plays the message. "I don’t know what you think you’re doing, but let’s finish this!" His own voice hollers back at him from his phone. After a second Joshua releases it’s the same voice message he left for Sanctum. With his back to the living room he drops the phone onto the dining room table, it’s all beginning to make sense now… "So you’ve finally figured it out, eh Joshy?" Sanctum’s voice mocks. Joshua turns, and faces the eyes of his predator. "You didn’t exactly try to hide it." A booming chuckle erupts from Sanctum, "Still I didn’t think it would take you this long, perhaps your not as sharp as I gave you credit for." A sneer crosses Joshua’s usually complacent face, darkening his olive complexion. "Your time is up Sanctum, you’re done for." A second chuckle turns into a maniacal laugh, taunting Joshua mocking his convictions. "That’s where you’re wrong Joshy, my time has just begun, I’m in control now." "Not if I can help it." Retorts Joshua with a snide tone. "I will see you burn in hell for everything that you have done." "This is one rubber tree plant you can’t move, you lost this before it even began." Replies Sanctum’s voice, coolly. "We’ll see about that." Joshua concludes, as he turned to walk away leaving his own reflection diminishing in the mirror as he leaves, and he repeats, "We’ll see about that." Palatinus: Part Thirteen - "???" coming soon.
