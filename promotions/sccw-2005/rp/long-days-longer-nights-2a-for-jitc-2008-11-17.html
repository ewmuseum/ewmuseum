Long Days, Longer Nights (2a for JITC)

17 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/2008 August 14th 1988 The stars twinkled in the dark Texas sky leaving a low pale light across the quiet pasture. Clayton Byrd and Sharron sat silently entwined in each others arms. The only noise heard was the sound of crickets chirping as the two teenagers lay there clutching each other tightly. Clay moved a bit and looked directly at Sharron. "Sharron, what's gonna happen?" Clay asked as he stared down at his beautiful companion. "What do you mean Clay?" She inquired back, cracking a small smile. Clay stopped and stared at her for a moment, he loved that smile. It filled him with purpose and happiness, that sly grin meant the world to him. "You know wha' I'm talkin' bout hon," Clay stated as he looked at her, he could see the reflection of the stars off of her brilliant blue eyes. "You mean our future?" She said as she looked at Clay, he nodded a bit smiling. "Whatever you want to have happen will happen Clay, you just have to want to make it happen." She stated as the grin grew a bit more on Clay's face. That last statement was the last bit of assurance that Clay needed, he reached into his pocket pulling out a small box. "Sharron, will you be my wife?" He asked, a tear rolling down his cheek as he opened the small black box revealing the ring with the small diamond on it. He had worked all summer on the farm to buy it, and earning every penny from his father really mattered to Clay. It proved he was a bit more than an overgrown child. Sharron's eyes filled with tears as she reached out and picked the small ring up from the box looking at it. She slid the ring onto her finger and locked lips with Clay, the two shared the moment for as long as possible. For that one moment the world seemed to revolve around just them, hell with football practice and school. Right now was a moment for two people to share, the most important and beautiful moment of their lives. November 17th 2008 5:00 pm. Slowly Clay stepped out of the taxi cab and began his trek to the door. He was dressed a bit better than usual, trading in his black t-shirt for a black button down shirt that he left untucked with a new pair of jeans he had bought earlier that week. He stopped at the front door, readjusting his collar and straightening his shirt. Clay Byrd was nervous. He stepped into the ring with the worlds best athletes and tried to fight them, it didn't scare him. This though, a social situation with his ex-wife and her husband was going to be a nightmare. He inhaled, thinking back to the night on the pasture in Texas. That night he had been so frightened, he broke down crying when he was talking too his older brother about what he was going to do. Now? He had no reason to be nervous, but still the butterflies were there as he rang the doorbell. November 17th 2008 3:00 pm. Chad Hollisting had just finished up an early day at his accounting office and he was quickly on the train heading home from downtown New York. By some fluke he'd agreed to go to dinner with Clayton Byrd, his wife's ex husband. It was a nightmare for a five-foot eleven accountant from New York City. Clay was a monster of a man, built athletically and strong, with a temper to match the steel that his body was seemingly made out of. Truthfully Chad liked Clay. Clay had always been nice to him when he came to pick up Jake, but Sharron was never around on days like that. She never saw Chad shaking as he opened the door, even though Clay was a nice guy. He knew one day the two would have a confrontation, and Chad was terrified of when that moment would come. When he and Sharron first began seeing each other while Clay was in Japan he began having nightmares of the big man from Texas knocking down his door, juggernautesque and popping his skull like a pimple. While the horrible fantasies had faded, Chad was still uneasy when Clay was around. Which is what the small clear cellophane bag in his pocket was for. A friend at the office had recommended the home remedy to Chad, and as he drove home from the Subway station he almost regretted having purchased the white powder. Even an accountant from New York had his vices though, and snorting a line while hanging out with the guys seemed to calm him down a bit. Why not with Clay? Chad stopped the car in the garage and walked into the house, quickly heading to his home office. He started up his computer and loaded up his hobby while laying his vice out in front of the keyboard. He typed his user name and password and logged onto the internet poker site and then took a deep breath as he rolled the dollar bill. He paused for a second before taking a huge drag from the table. The white powder flew up his nasal cavity. Each small granule of the substance collided with his brain in a matter of seconds. He smiled as he slumped back in his chair and loaded online poker up in multiple windows. "This is the life..." he mumbled as the game began to deal. November 17th 2008 5:05 pm. Clayton could hear Sharron and Chad yelling back and forth at each other through the door, part of him wanted to go running into the house like a maniac. But he knew Sharron was a bit stubborn and hard to deal with. It was probably just a squabble about what Chad was wearing, but still Clay strained to listen closer. He heard the sound of someone running down the stairs, and then he made out the sound that made him snap. His son yelling and Chad yelling back. It could have been a family squabble, but Clay had a bad feeling in his gut. Whether it was instinct, or just a fatherly intuition, with out thinking he reached out and opened the door. He marched into the kitchen, now hearing exactly what Chad and Jake were screaming about. "I FUCKING HATE YOU!" Jake roared as Clay turned the corner and saw Jake taking a swing at Chad Hollisting who ducked a bit out of the way. Chad instantly froze as he saw the big Texan step through the entryway, Jake immediately stopped and turned, running to his father and hugging him. Clay felt the warm dampness of tears press through his thin shirt. Jake was hyperventilating, his sobs becoming labored as Clay stared a hole through Chad. He sat Jake down in the chair before he approached Chad, he could feel the anger rising from the pit of his stomach. His fist clenched, his knuckles slowly began to whiten as he looked in the corner and saw Sharron with a red welt across her cheek. Chad backed up to the counter and reached in the drawer for a weapon, anything to fend off the massive Texan. Finally grabbing the wooden handle of a knife, he swung it at Clay who immediately caught Chad's arm. Clay grinned as he began twisting it until it made a popping sound and Chad screamed in pain. Clay pushed the smaller man up against the sink, bringing his glare down, nose to forehead with Chad. "If you evah touch her like that again..." Clay paused as Chad tried to jerk his other arm up to punch at Clay. The big man smiled as he took the shot on the side of the head, giving it no regard other than grabbing Chad's attacking arm by the bicep and slamming it into Chad's side. "Nah you stop fightin' and start listenin'," Clay said, as Chad slumped down a bit knowing he was defeated. "If you evah touch her like that again... or so much as try to do somethin' like that to my son," Clay paused to make the point. "I'll fucking kill you." He heard Chad whimper and finally let go of the arm he dislocated. "Nah you call yourself an ambulance, and I'll be taking my family somewhere else," Clay said as Chad nodded. "Jacob, take your ma out to the van, I'll be right out." Clay said as he stood looking at Chad fumble with the phone in his left hand. Snatching a piece of paper from the refrigerator, Clay scribbled his number down hastily. "Gimme a call when you think you can manage a proper family Chad. 'til then," Clay reached out and grabbed Chad's index finger as he fumbled nervously with the phone. His hand was tight around Chad's slender digit. Chad screamed again as Clay snapped the finger. "And that should make sure you give it enough time," said Clay, as he reached for the fallen telephone and dialed 911. Chad Hollisting could only sob as Clayton shoved the receiver into his sweaty palm. Clay spun on his heels and walked to the garage. He slid into the driver's seat of the mini-van. The key ignited the engine and he put the car in reverse. As the car lazily rolled backwards, he paused looking both ways. Sharon smiled, 'Clay, thank you.' "Notta problem darlin, been waitin' to do that for awhile now," Clay said as he backed the car into the street and began the journey out of the New York Suburb. "Where are we going Dad?" Jake asked, as Clay shrugged his shoulders. "Not a clue, Jake. Not a clue." For once in the last twelve years Clayton Byrd felt complete... and he was just content with that.
