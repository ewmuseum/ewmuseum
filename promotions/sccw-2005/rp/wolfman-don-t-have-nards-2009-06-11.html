Wolfman don't have nards~!

11 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.11.09 Temptation West Virginia. Jadian Bridden stares at the small puddle that has formed between the navy blue Chuck Taylor low-tops he slipped on after his shower. The puddle formed from the water – that dripped from his hair onto the floor – was hardly anything to admire, but after a disappointing loss to Legion for the LiveWire title he needed something to distract him. "You almost ready to go?" Jadian lifts his head slowly. There was no mistaking the voice of the Mexican Minx, who stands in the doorway with her head peaking through the slight opening. "I'll be there in a sec," he reaches for the royal blue, button-up, polo shirt she bought him for Christmas, it was crumpled up next to him on the bench where he had thrown it earlier. "Just gotta throw on my shirt and we're good to go." "I know you're tired, but Alex wants us to stick around," Julissa Minorez allows her fingers to trace a trail along the edge of the doorway before she lets it go, allowing it to snap shut. "You know, just in case. You up for it?" He allows a smirk to form in the corner of his mouth. "Did she ask, or did she ask?" Anyone familiar with the Spider understands that there is a certain way the Director asks you for something where there is only one way to answer – yes. The slight arch in Julissa's eyebrow is answer enough. "I wouldn't be surprised if I end up on a permanent vacation after losing my match," he tells her without a bit of sarcasm. "Just don't let them put my picture on a milk carton, alright?" "You're an idiot sometimes, you know that?" She walked over, placing a perfectly manicured finger against his cheek before tracing a line to his chin. Her thumb joins her forefinger in taking a grip of his chin as she leans down and presses her lips against his. "Take your time. I'll tell her you were still sulking in the shower or something." "I'll be there in a bit." He takes a hold of her hand, but only briefly. "Just let me pack up my stuff and I'll be right behind you." "Alright," her hips sway like a pendulum as she makes her way to the door. Not to tease the man, but because that is just the way she walks. "You think she'll feel better if I use my rematch clause to get the belt back in a couple of weeks?" "I doubt it would hurt." She pulls the door open, turning back long enough to give him a reassuring smile. "But it would sound better coming from you." "Should I play it up like I got screwed by the ref or something?" "Nah, just tell her you fucked up." She smiles playfully as she steps out and lets the door close behind her. "She'll respect that more." "Respect..." he mumbles under his breath, because the walls apparently have ears. Jay stuffs his ring gear into a blue duffel bag, then tosses that into the corner as he grabs the shirt he tossed over his shoulder when Julissa walked in. He shakes it out a bit before pulling it over his head. Taking a glance at the mirror against the wall as he passes it, Jay runs his fingers through his hair in order to straighten it out a bit. *tap* *tap* The sound brought a smile to his face. "Aw, how cute...You waited for me." Jay grabs a hold of the door and pulls it open, but there is no one there. At least in front of the door, because a lead pipe swinging toward him means someone is there. *thwack* Unfortunately, the sudden wave of pain filled his sight with a bright light before everything went black. And the stars... If it was night, they'd be big and bright. * * * * * March 15, 2009 "So how long are we talking about here?" Jadian Bridden stares at the doctor that is standing in front of him with a clipboard in his hand. Julissa Minorez is standing to his left, her arms folded across her chest as the doctor pulls his attention away from the clipboard. "A minimum of eight weeks," he answered. Dr. Janis Strausberg had been doing this for years. The looks that people give him have ranged from anger to shock, so the smile that creeps across Jadian's face when he receives the usually bad news takes him by surprise. "Two months," Jay chuckles softly. "That's great. I've been meaning to head to the coast for some fishing anyway." "Any way we can move things along, Doc?" Juli steps toward the 'Ace', not amused by his flippant attitude. The doctor cannot help but watch her as she walks, her extremely short skirt only draws more attention. "Some extra physical therapy, perhaps?" "I'm sorry," his head shakes from side to side as he slaps the clipboard against his thigh nervously. "Any extra strain could make the injury worse." "Guess I'm gonna need to buy some batteries." Bridden's grin, as he tries to stifle a laugh, nearly becomes a full smile when the doctor's cheeks flush. Clearing his throat, Dr. Strausberg attempts to maintain his professionalism. "The injury is listed as work related; dare I ask your profession." "We're in the adult film industry," Julissa answers without missing a beat. "He was doing an anal scene with Krystal Storm when her knee slipped off the edge of the table. She bled for a week." Janis nervously adjusted the glasses on the bridge of his nose. "I'm a professional wrestler," Jadian tells him between bouts of laughter. "Somebody hit me with a lead pipe when I came out of my locker room." "Hopefully this wasn't intentional." Dr. Strausberg's eyes narrowed as a replay of the incident played through his mind. "Of course not, Doc." the first LiveWire champion rests her hand on Jadian's shoulder. "It's just a really big target." "Right..." Janis attempts to ignore the comment. "Well, nevertheless, you will need to refrain from doing any type of strenuous exercise." "So if we take it slow, we're good?" "C'mon, Jules," Jay slaps her on the ass. Hard. "You're embarrassing the man." "Party pooper." She rubs her cheek. Yes, that one. "She's just being a pain in the ass, Dr. Strausberg." Jay stands up, placing his hand in the small of Juli's back "I'll take it easy, I promise." "Schedule an appointment with the receptionist on your way out," Strausberg tries his best to keep from staring at Julissa, as she bends at the waist to pick her small purse up off the floor. "I – I'd like to see you again in six weeks." "Me?" Jadian asks with a grin, sneaking a peek at the view that has the good doctor's attention. "Yeah, right." * * * * * Present Day "She can barely look at me." Julissa turns to her left, where Jadian leans against the wall of the elevator. "She has other things on her mind right now, Jay. Trust me, this has nothing to do with you." "Easy for you to say," he snaps back at her, his eyes narrowing as his lips curl into a scowl. "You're pretty much taking Kat's spot without missing a beat." "She taught me well." Julissa's smile is full and slightly toothy. "Kat would've done the same if she was in my position." "I'm sure she would," Jay starts. "I just never thought I'd see you in so deep. Especially when you consider the circumstances." "Let's not get into that right now, okay." She turns away from him, that boastful smile replaced by a slight frown. "I'm just saying." "I get it, but you don't have to throw it in my face everyday." Jay walks over to her, placing his hands on her hips as he pulls up close behind her. "I'm sorry. It's just … Well, this isn't what we signed on for, you know. Its like the two women you admired most for their ability to control any given situation, lost control of everything for the sake of the one thing no one believed they had." "Alex knows what she's doing." "And Kat doesn't?" Jay raises the question that Juli has been pondering since this all began. "I – I don't know." "Like I told you from the beginning," Jay turns her by the waist, looking up at her a bit with a smile on his face. "I'm going to be right here. Regardless of how this all turns out, you can always count on me." "That's probably the only thing I know for sure right now." Julissa leans forward and kisses Jay just as the doors to the elevator slide open. "Please, don't stop on my account." Julissa pulls away from Jay slightly, his hand still cupped under her skirt as they find the River Rat, Lane Stevens, standing in the entrance. "This is our floor anyway," Julissa says as the couple step past Stevens as he enters. "She's in another meeting with Amy," Jay says with a slight smirk curling in the corners of his mouth. "So don't hold your breath." "I'll try to remember that, chief." "Did I sound bitter?" Jadian asks as soon as the doors close. "Just a smidge," Julissa replies, tucking a loose lock of hair behind her ear. "Oh well," Jay says flippantly. "They act like we don't know what the hell they're doing in there." "Like I said earlier," Julissa gives him a peck on the lips, sans tongue. "She knows what she's doing." "If you say so."
