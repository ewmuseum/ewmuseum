Date: Today He pulled the knit cap further down his eyes. He would not be spotted here, even if he wore a bright pink t-shirt and white jeans. He looked across the glossy bar to the reflective back. Will found it remarkable to what lengths they were willing to take to appear like an authentic dive. He did not mind. He paid his 4000-yen at the door and another 500 as a "first drink fee." Only in Shibuya, would he pay 50 bucks to get a watered down Suntory and try to disappear. "For a relaxing time, make it a Suntory time?" Hardly. This was his fourth and he was not nearly as loose as he would be if he had his eye dropper. Now he remembered why he got out of this country. Then he got tapped on the shoulder. He swiveled in his chair, disoriented as suddenly the drinks caught up with him. He stared down two younger women. Maybe 17. They dressed what they thought their age should be, trying to embrace the wanting salarymen and foreigners who traveled the world to desire them. Will O’Gallway cocked his eyebrow up into the gray brim and snarled. They got idea. Now he really remembered why he moved back to the states. The last time he was here, these were the kind of girls he normally dated and enjoyed. They would fawn over him and eagerly try to impress him. They would occupy his time. He was younger, stupider, and enjoyed the ability and power he now controlled. They used Will to anger their parents. Youth Rebellion in Tokyo. He never cared. For the past week while he has been in the country, Will was shuttled to and from dinners with former sponsors and super fans. From about 6 AM to well close to 1 AM he was on the clock. He was Ryan Gallway. Former gaijin ace of TOKYO PRO. It never was one of the "Big Three" Japanese promotions but it held this ward of Tokyo in its grasp. They took the Heirs around town. Ryan has been gone for a while so they knew they could have a huge gate at Ryōgoku Kokugikan. Moreover, if they could get the complete trio for a tour? It could make their year. They knew Ryan, they knew how to wear him down. The office knew Ryan before SCCW. Will as a nice kid, unassuming and always willing to do his best for the company. Although he did not realize it, they turned him into this. Instilling that he would enjoy the reaction he got as a Rudo, he was molded into the pompous delusional character they needed their native wrestlers to defeat. They were boring, he was lively. He fit the new persona like a glove. He led them to new heights. Again, they would not admit it, but TOKYO PRO needed Ryan Gallway. Business was down across the board, but it hit them worse in the two years since his departure. Get him loaded, remind him of what his life was here, and get him to sign the contract while he was tanked. How it felt to be on top of a promotion and all the opportunities Tokyo could give him. He could become an idol star. Maybe a big star. He heard what happened for Bob Sapp or Randy Bass. One career move made them a lifetime of money. He would be happy. He would be famous. Those dreams and temptations of Japan and the nightlife. They would be his real life. He would love being the huge star. They were wrong. All they did was push more anxiety onto an individual whom was filled to brim with it. He was without his usual outlet or escape back to Miami. It has been a week since the last time he dropped. He was able to score a cheap half-eighth in Hawaii, but that only lasted him for his time there. For the next three days, he was stuck in Japan, and had no way out. None of them did. Frank hated the country. All Will ever heard from him was how much of a shit hole it was and how he could not wait to get out. Frank actually said this about every place he visited, with the exception of the estate’s he inherited. So he was more than eager to book it when O’Gallway "played sick." He was probably in his hotel room sleeping or cursing at the toilet that dared acted as a bidet. Mack was so enamored with the clubs that he would not even notice his little buddy’s absence. He would just keep up the bottle service with the superfans, talk to the girlies and make sure all of them understood his routine of egg whites, tanning THEN gym. Gotta let them settle in, ya know? Sadly even his hotel room was getting to Will. Small, Cramped, he ran out of books a day ago, and he was facing withdrawal. So lets kill that with Japanese Grade Whisky. Ugh. Tasted much worse than he remembered. Will killed that one and signaled to the keep for another. He looked at the reflective mirror, back at himself dreary in the bar. The bartender wore a poofy white shirt, vest and bowtie. Their interpretation of how he would look in Prohibition America. Dim lights, smoky interior. Hub bub and the Charleston playing. Welcome to SPEAKEASY USA! And now noticeably drunk, he didn’t care how this was kitsch and how it annoyed him so. Unauthentic, gauche, tacky. Everything he tried to avoid. He would have laughed about it; him eagerly using this place as a refuge when he’d normally hate it. A woman walked in the bar and grasped the corner of his eye. She had red hair, he believed. Looked familiar. She saddled up next to him and ordered a Gin and Tonic. She made a comment about how ridiculous it was having to pay 50 dollars just to get a watered down splash of well gin. He laughed. She said he had a funny laugh, very likeable. Will smiled. Women like her normally would not approach him. This beautiful one was engaged in a conversation with him, and seemed to like the gawkish Texan in a knit cap and pink t-shirt. He offered to buy her a better drink. She went ‘aww’ and accepted, then remarked that he obviously was not from around here. He snorted and immediately blushed. Will said that he was in town on business. That is true if you think about it. She said she heard that a lot, she was a translator for company doing manuals. She would not let him tell her his name. That would make this out to more than what it was. He was intrigued. She was beaming. The hours melted away. They continued to down their drinks. She placed her hand on his thigh. He could not do anything but smile. It was the happiest he had been in months. He put his hand over hers. He felt the warmth and certainness under his trembling hands. She got up and led him out the door. /// "Hey" "Um hi." "We didn’t get to introduce ourselves last time. I’m Will." "I know who you are. What’s up?" "Not too much. Had my meeting at work. Cleaned up. Came here." "That’s an odd way to put it." "My job?" "Yeah. Never heard anyone call wrestling a match ‘a meeting’ or it ‘work.’" "Umm. Of course I did. Come on Gallway." "Heh. You are just full of surprises, you know my last name and I don’t know yours. This kinda takes me aback. Anyways what would you like to drink? My liver is dead thirsty." "I don’t know what are you doing or what are you up to Ryan, or Will, or whatever. But I’m not interested." "I’m confused. You weren’t like this before. You left a note." "Did I? What note? I have no clue what you are talking about! In fact, this is the first time we’ve formally met, Ryan. Just go back to your two other sketchballs and leave me alone. I’ll see you soon enough." "Hey! Wait! Wait!" /// Will woke up. He let out a huge yawn and stretched out his weary limbs. Everything about that was perfect. As someone whom was so alone and so confused, it was great for him to have someone for once. He just could never find anyone. When he was on the road, he never tried. Diversions only it seemed. And the unrequited crush he held on Alexandra Malone. She found out about his habit and that was it. Shot down even before he had an opportunity. Will took a deep breath in, for once this hotel room felt welcoming. Her perfume still wafted in the air. The blinds were opened, and the slow sunlight crept into the room. He would wrestle this day against Sapientes Gladio. A lot of folks still held the sex before shows traditions. He never really acknowledged it or believed in it. At least she gave him another spring in his step. Will O’Gallway smiled. Even with a hangover that he felt creeping over him, this was one of the first moments of true bliss he ever remembered. He saw a small prismatic rainbow on the edge of his arm. He rolled to his side and saw an unopened bottle of water, two aspirin. Under it was a note written on hotel stationery. The ink was long dry on the cheap parchment. He flailed his arm towards the other side of the bed. Hoping she would be there. Nope. All he felt was the duvet and feather top pillow. Oh well, Will thought. He sat up and quickly downed the water and aspirin. After a satisfying gulp, he read the note aloud. Maybe this would revive or teleport her back to him. No luck again. He still had a smile plastered over his tanned face. She said she wanted to see him again. She would be at the same bar tonight, at eleven. He collapsed onto his back, awash in emotion. Rather, Will was awash in happiness. For once, he had someone. He would not need to lie to Frank and Mack about where he was last night, whom he was bangin’. She was real. He had proof. More importantly, he had someone, and that made this early morning headache and awkwardness worth it. This time he would not need his eyedropper or blotter paper. These emotions were all his own.
