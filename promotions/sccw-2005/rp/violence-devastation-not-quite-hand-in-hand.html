Violence & Devastation Not Quite Hand In Hand

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/16/10 There was a point in time when I was...well...evil. No, let me try that again. There was a point in time when I was a despicable individual. No, I don't think that's it either. Well it is, but it really isn't. Perhaps the best place to start, is the end. Because it's at the end, that you usually find yourself looking back to the beginning so to speak. May 11th, 2008 General Motors Palace Vancouver, British Columbia 15 minutes removed from his first ever 'I Quit' match, Leonard Aarons sits with his barb wire Kendo Stick in hand and dried blood covering his face. For the first time in his career, he beat a man to the point where he heard the words 'I Quit' end it. The first time in his career he beat a man to the point where rather than tap out or having his shoulders pinned for a three count, two words and five little letters stopped it. But that's not what has Leonard still in his gear of a pair of bloodied blue jeans and a black Illtown Rules throwover, in a state of near euphoria. What has him still in his gear after all of this, after what could be best described as premeditated manslaughter, is that somewhere deep down inside of himself... He loved every second of it. For one night only, he broke out his old 'Illtown' gear to settle a score with Heatwave. Heatwave, his former friend and member of his East Coast Connection stable during the latter days of NWC: Atlantic. Or NWC: Aarons, if you were to ask Len himself. Heatwave turned his back on Aarons and the group shortly after the demise of the National Wrestling Council, something which Aarons didn't take too kindly to. Aarons while recovering from injuries inflicted after his last match under the umbrella of the NWC, sought out to make an example of Heatwave for his unmitigated gall in turning his back on the ECC. To send the message that without the ECC, you're finished and without Aarons specifically having your back, you're doubly finished. He helped Heatwave become Axiom Heavyweight Champion, so that his Devastation when the time came would have more meaning. That when Aarons finally took the title off of him and left his former charge lying, people would take notice that nobody gets over on Aarons without paying a heavy price. Aarons warned Heatwave shortly before his title match against then champion and fellow Aarons hater Paul Cain, that when all was said and done, all bets were off. Heatwave's family would not be excluded from the people feeling Len's wrath and sure enough, in May of 2004 after Aarons counted the fall which made Heatwave champion, they weren't. With Heatwave's son forced to watch along with a beaten and restrained Heatwave, Leonard Aarons broke the neck of Heatwave's wife Nicole with a top rope Injection of Greatness onto a steel chair with L's patented Lendo Stick wedged in between. One week later, Aarons challenged Heatwave for the Axiom Heavyweight Title and won, but something changed. The man who for so long operated as if he sold his soul to the Devil Himself, finally got it back at the worst possible time. The one thing he hadn't felt for close to two years or so, came back and with it a whole lot of something he never expected to feel. That one thing, was remorse. Remorse for the lives he ruined and left in ruins. Regret for the extreme measures he went to in order to procure what could've easily been his on just sheer ability and nothing more. Back then, beating people in the ring wasn't enough for Aarons. He wanted to send the message that not only could he beat you in the ring, but he could flat out devastate you outside of it simply for pissing him off. Still, he reached a point when he decided enough was enough. That there comes a point when sometimes, you have to let go. So around August of 2004, he took all of his old Illtown gear and buried it. He knew that as long as he could have access to the gear, the temptation to put it on again and wreak all kinds of devastation on people was too great. The only thing he could do to ensure his career would continue on, without fear of wondering if someone would wreak unto him what he had brought unto others...was to turn over a new leaf. To be what he originally set out to become when he first started wrestling, a role model for kids to look up to without actually admitting it. In essence, he set out to remake himself. ---------------------- Which brings us to the here and now... Present Day East Orange, New Jersey Watsessing Park The camera opens catching the trees as the wind sends the branches swaying, but overall, the heat is off the page. What reads as 90 degrees feels like 100 thanks to the heat index. But in spite of all that, there stands a man with his back to the camera. Sweat glistens off of his back as he pulls himself up above the bar, camera locked in on the massive tattoo on his back. The very thing which he has endeared to himself as his own personal logo of sorts. A Lion's head, with a twist. The top half of his face is covered in blood, yet the menacing snarl remains. 'Normally, most men would turn and face the camera as they prepare to speak. But to be quite honest with ya...I'm doing things differently this time around.' He continues doing pull ups, muscles retracting as the sweat drops from his elbows. Beyond his arms, his ankles crossed. 'So I hear tell that my first match in Sin City Championship Wrestling is against Vinnie Jones. Not just anyone, but 'The Violence' Vinnie Jones. Oooh, scary. Anyway Vinnie, I felt it was only just that I explain to you who I am and give you a SMALL primer as to what to expect when we arrive to Richmond for our Rumble.' He takes a short breather, but his arms continue working. His body remains in constant motion, heat be damned. 'I come here off of a two year sabbatical. I had to take a look at myself and simply put, reassess exactly what I was and what it is that I look to become. You see Vince, Vinnie, I'm nothing like you. I don't need to put things like 'Violence' in front of my name to strike a chord with my opponents. I've come to find that simply letting people know where I'm from, does that just fine.' It's here that he drops to the ground, landing on his feet as he yanks down a white towel, draping it around his neck. 'My town isn't overly huge and quite honestly, I don't think you could spot the thing on a map without staring at one of those really good ones that account for small towns. The only famous folks my town's produced would be Whitney Houston, Naughty By Nature and Queen Latifah. I'd throw myself in that mix, but well, I'm going for humbility this first time out.' A small chuckle from Illtown, as he walks away from the bar he was working out on. 'Fact is Vince, I'm a man who has been through a lot. If I seem unimpressed with you, it's because quite honestly, what is there to be impressed with? You haven't been where I've been and even if you were, I'm willing to bet dollars to dimes you couldn't raise so much as a hair on my wavy head.' He runs his head over his wavy dome for effect and for all who wonder, he takes pride in his waves being all natural. No caps, no funky grease. Again, he hasn't even cast a glimpse at the camera. 'I'm here to do what Jared Sykes accomplished in Newark. Become a Grand Slam Champion. In my career, I've done many things, but that remains the last pinnacle a man of my caliber has yet to reach. Every great journey has to have a beginning and simply put, you get to be the sacrificial lamb that gets me going on mine. All I ask is that you make it worth my while. Because to this point, what I've seen and what I've heard...is absolutely NOTHING.' His calm tenor tone picks up just a little bass after that last sentence. 'Understand, I'm not some fresh off the wagon rookie. I haven't competed in any tourneys or the like, but please believe this isn't my first barbecue. Understand that just because you have yet to hear about me or my exploits, that I'm simply going to walk into Richmond, roll over and play dead for ya. That's not happening, Vinny.' Beat. 'What will be happening is yours truly coming down the aisle, sporting my new look under an old name with a fairly familiar result for myself personally. Namely, VICTORY. At the end of the day Vince, while the sheet wonders whether or not I can stop The Violence...here's one for you to ponder.' It's here that he turns over his right shoulder, the camera catching the right half of his face as he slowly flashes a smirk. 'Can you withstand the Devastation? If ya asked me and of course, that's just if you asked, I'd say you were an Injection of Greatness away from realizing the answer to that is an emphatic...' His right arm shoots straight out, vertical-like as his right hand turns south with his thumb pointing down. 'NO. See ya in Richmond where I'm sure as violent as you're bound to get...it won't compare to how Devastating I will become.' He winks and walks off, as the camera fades to black. ----------------- May 18th, 2008 Leonard sits on a chair, clutching his signature Lendo Stick, his thoughts slowing to a crawl. It's at a point where the outside world is slowly tuning itself out, as he can hear his heart beating and at the same time, he can hear the screams of Nicole Burbank. Begging for Leonard to stop, begging for him to leave her husband alone. But there was no mercy to be had on this evening from Aarons, not after what Heatwave had tried to do to him in setting him on fire July 3rd, 2007. Nobody had ever done to Leonard what Heatwave had done and as such, Aarons felt that it wasn't a matter of just Devastation. It was about ensuring that Heatwave would never again, even contemplate coming after L for any reason. But as he sits, the very weapon he used to help accomplish his twisted ends, he finds himself asking a question he had posed to himself just four years ago. When is too far, too far? (TBC)
