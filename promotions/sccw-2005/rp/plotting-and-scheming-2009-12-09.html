Plotting and Scheming

9 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/09/2009 12/02/2009 Backstage after the show, I am sitting on a bench in my locker room next to my agent and sitting across from Calix who is out of it. I'm going over the tag match at the supercard with them since I just found out what the match is right before coming back to the locker room to chat with them. "So it’s me, Malloy, and you in our corner," I tell my new buddy Kaden Calix and my agent William Smith with a grin. "I think it’ll work," Smith replies, agreeing with me for once. "Of course it’s going to work!" I tell him excitedly. "I came up with the plan so it’s going to work regardless." Just then my brethren Rapmaster Rick Malloy comes walking into the locker room with a solemn look on his face. He walks over to us before stopping abruptly. "James," Malloy says in a melancholy voice. "I got caught on camera freebasing off of Ryan’s motorcycle." At this point I rise up out of my seat and try to keep calm for a split second but it doesn’t work. I start yelling at him for his stupidity. This idiot is probably going to get me fired and I’m going to let him know just what I feel about that. "YOU FUCKING IDIOT!" I yell at him. "You’re going to get yourself kicked out of SCCW and you’re going to make me look like a FUCKTARD because I’m the reason you came here to begin with! CHRIST!" As I try to calm down, suddenly a light bulb goes off in my head. Things aren’t as bad as I’m thinking they are so I sit down and analyze the situation out loud. "Wait," I tell them as I‘m calming down. "We’re still in good shape. If they suspend you, then Calix can always take your place. It’s going to be a piece of cake with any combination of us three." Calix looks at me with a funny look on his face. "Who the fuck is Calix?" he asks me. 'Is that...' In response, I shake my head and tell him a lie to keep him from gaining any info on his past. "No, your name is Felix," I say to him before going back to what I was previously talking about. "Anyway, you may be wrestling on the next show so we need to get you into wrestling shape again." "Again?' He ponders. 'I'm just here for a fucking refill, how are we going to get me in 'wrestling shape'?" Calix asks. "Wii Fit and the Gold’s Gym Cardio Workout on the Wii," I tell him as Malloy starts laughing and Smith shakes his head in disbelief. "No worries, buddy. I’m the most dominant wrestler in the sport and I can take on any team here by myself with one hand tied behind my back and blindfolded and STILL win the match. We have nothing to be worried about. So relax. Malloy is a former champion here and you…well let’s just say you’re more of a threat than anyone around here realizes, including yourself." "Are you fucking kidding me?" Calix asks, seemingly still suffering from the Amnesia I know he has. 'I can't even imagine the stuff you are telling me was ever real.' I nod my head in response. "Yes," I tell him before turning to the others. "Now let’s get a plan together and steal the show at Sin on Spike." Malloy sits down and we huddle together to come up with a plan for Sin on Spike 4. * * * 12/03/2009 As I’m about to go on my regular morning jog, my homeboy Rappin’ Rick Malloy comes running into the living room past my agent William Smith with a paper in his hand. It appears to be a fax or a printout from the computer. He runs over to me before stopping to hand the paper to me and catch his breath. "JAMES! JAMES!" he says frantically. "READ THIS QUICK!" Upon handing me the paper, I look down to read it out loud. "Jason Ryan suspended by SCCW," I read aloud, a little stunned by what I’m reading to be honest about it. "Tag match with von Doom and Malloy against Legion and Ryan is now a singles match pitting von Doom against Legion where Falls Count anywhere within ten miles from the arena." After finishing, I turn and give him a look because this will have no effect on the match at all since no matter what I’m going to be the winner regardless of who I’m facing in the ring. "SO?! This doesn’t change anything at all. I was going to win this match anyway with or without your help. So I’m still going to win only it’s going to be a single’s match. Legion is nothing but a toilet bug and Ryan is a nobody. Nothing changes. I’ve got this in the bag. It’s going to be a piece of cake." "James, come on," Smith says as he sits down on the couch. "Be realistic. Legion almost ended your career in NFW in a match like this and you were gone for awhile from wrestling because of said match. Be careful with this guy." "Yeah James," Malloy says in agreement with Smith. "Legion is a bad ass and he beat you to a bloody pulp. It could happen again!" I shrug off their concerns without even thinking about them. "Now here’s what’s going to happen," I tell them while trying to keep a straight face. "I’m going to drag Legion all over the city and then he’s going to get on his hands and knees before surrendering himself over to me." "PLEASE VON DOOM!" Smith exclaims. "Be serious." I can’t believe that he doesn’t believe me. I’m being honest for the first time in ages and nobody believes me. Oh well, I guess it’s probably like the boy who cried wolf or something along those lines. "I am being serious!" I yell at him. "I’m already working an angle for this match that nobody’s even thought of yet. See, I look at the whole situation with this perspective. Nobody, even Legion, would think that I would try to form an alliance with him." I look over and see their jaws drop to the floor. "Are you serious?" Smith asks. "Of course I am," I reply with a smile. "I mean, why should I even be fighting Legion? We’re not enemies…well, I’m not enemies with him. He is my competition though. So I will be wrestling to the best of my ability in a brawl. If it turns into a brawl, it’ll be his style of match. However, if I turn this into an actual wrestling match, I’ll win hands down. But I won’t need to do that anyway. This match is in the bag." "You’re out of your mind," Smith responds in disbelief. "Legion’s not going to join up with you. You’ve already got Calix, Malloy, and your mystery client that you’re managing. There’s no way Legion is going to join your alliance. No way." "But Luci is gone and he’s on his own," I tell him. "Legion seems to think that I am second guessing him. Instead I’m trying to help him out but the idiot doesn’t see it! He’s just another idiotic wrestler whose felt one too many chairshots to the head. But there’s another thing. There’s the big War Games match and everybody in SCCW knows that I am the fourth person on Team SCCW. OH YEAH! You know it baby! And after I destroy Legion and make him surrender his allegiance over to me, then I will help SCCW defeat Team Aenima and go on to my rightful place as the number one contender to the SCCW Universal Championship held by Wyatt Connors." "There’s no way that’s going to happen," Smith replies, rolling his eyes as he does. "We’ll just see about that," I say with a smile before going back to getting ready for my jog. That belt is going to be mine. Oh yes, it will be mine… * * * 12/08/2009 Von Doom’s house… As the last strategy session before Sin on Spike 4 is well underway, Smith left to go watch the newest Legion promo to get some info out of it. Inside my living room, Calix is sitting in a drugged up state on a lounge chair, Malloy is higher than a kite, and I’m explaining my plan to get Legion to join me since Luci4 is gone. Smith, seemingly done watching Legion’s promo, walks in and sits down next to me. "Uhhhh…James," Smith says to me. I turn and look in his direction. "What is it?" I ask. "Bad news," he says. "Luci is back with Legion!" "Pfft…like I give a shit," I respond with a smile. "I’m going to lead Team SCCW to ultimate victory before revealing what I really want. After the match, I’m going to drag Wyatt Connors to the center of the ring and make him surrender the SCCW Universal Championship over to me so that all the kids watching at home have a champion that they can be proud of." Smith still can’t believe what he’s hearing. "Are you out of your mind?" he asks. "James, let’s be serious here. You’re still on the undercard. Hell, you’re a curtain jerker for crying out loud! There’s no way that this is going to go down. There’s a lot of guys on the roster ranked ahead of you." "Like I care. I’ll steamroll over them. And I don’t mean one of them, I mean ALL of them. That Universal Championship is going to be MINE. I am the most underrated wrestler of the decade. All of these fucking idiots fell for my lies and my manipulations and my throwing of matches to appear weak. They all fell for it and now all of a sudden people are seeing the real picture. That von Doom is the greatest talent in the history of this sport, not Aimz, not Wyatt Connors, not Desade, or any of these other people in SCCW. I may not be in GTT anymore but I’m still better than them." "But they’re still in the big tournament…" I throw my hands up in disbelief. "SO?! Nobody cares about GTT. It’s all amateur punk stuff just like the NCAA tournament or the BCS. Nobody cares. It’s all about SCCW and who’s running the show around here? James von Doom. That’s right! So if GTT is all about SCCW and SCCW is all about me, then GTT is really all about von Doom." "I don’t even want to know how your devious mind works," he tells me. "Of course it’s devious," I reply with a smirk. "How else do you think I survived this long?" "Good point." "While Legion is whining and doing some sort of lame voodoo plot I’m over here training to win the match. It’s going to be a piece of cake. And then when Team Aenima faces MY Team SCCW in the main event, I’ll take them all on by myself." Smith raises an eyebrow. "Are you serious?" he asks. "You think I’m trying to be funny? Of course I’m serious! I’m the best wrestler in the world and Legion is going to find that out first hand. I’m the best in the world at what I do, PERIOD. It’s OVER for Legion and anyone else in SCCW dumb enough to face me in the ring here. And if they put their hands on me, they deal with Malloy, Calix, and my first managerial client. I’m taking over this place and after Sin on Spike, SCCW will be totally under MY control." "Oh dear lord," he responds. "Indeed," I tell him. "Now let’s go have a good time before I destroy everyone in SCCW." We all get up and head out for drinks before the big confrontation with Legion in the coming days. * * * 12/09/2009 The waiting is over, the time is at hand. My match with Legion is forthcoming. He has Luci4 back in his corner and I have various miscreants, drug addicts, and psychopaths in my corner. At SCCW Sin on Spike, I have a chance to make an impact and I will do it. First I will make Legion my He-bitch man-slave and then I will lead Team SCCW to victory over Team Aenima before making Wyatt Connors surrender his Universal Title over to me so that all of the children watching at home can have a champion that they can be proud of. As I tape up my wrists in preparation for the big match, all of these thoughts cross my mind. However, all it won’t matter soon because in a matter of moments, I’ll be thrust into the fires of Hell against Legion in a Falls Count Anywhere within ten miles of the arena match and then will be the fourth member of Team SCCW in the War Games main event. Tonight is going to be the biggest night of my career. I must be ready…
