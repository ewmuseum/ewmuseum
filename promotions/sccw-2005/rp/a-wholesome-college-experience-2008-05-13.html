A Wholesome College Experience

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/13/08 Camera: ON. 'You got it focused?' 'No. You guys aren't even on the screen.' 'Dammit, Todd, let me see it.' *Random shuffling/clanking* 'I tried that already, smart ass.' Success! Our layout is that of your typical dorm room. Lofts are set up on both sides of the 45' x 80' luxury roach motel, and the usual amenities are scattered in strategic positions amongst the area. A gray Sony television rests atop a Kenmore mini-fridge. Cerulean TV tray holds up a fifteen-dollar microwave oven complete with three settings: slightly thawed, room temperature, and German incineration. Covering the whitewashed brick walls of the average college dwelling are pennants featuring the Twins, Vikings, and Golden Gophers. Posters of airbrushed goddesses, an upskirt of Anna Kournikova, and 'that ol' gag' of the lickable ass crack of the blond in the schoolgirl outfit reminding you to 'study hard.' This dorm is so goddamn stereotypical, it makes you wonder if it's actually a set for a viral video instead of an actual suite. Our first bit of attention is devoted to everyone's favorite purveyor of the taint, poised for the camera and 'colleged out' with a pair of seersucker shorts, a white Coed Naked Badminton T-shirt, and brown leather Rainbow sandals. 'The Fro That You Know' is currently tightened down in corn rows, however, and a small part of us misses the warm and comforting glow of the giant hairdo. 'It's ya boy Cookie, SCCW...and we're here at the headquarters of Local 524 Tail Hunters Union - University of Minnesota chapter - bringing you into a little world we like to call Finals Week. Now, seeing as though I never went to college, I can't say that I've experienced firsthand the stress that goes along with the end of a semester. But let me tell you what I do know about scholastic achievement...in the REAL world, grades ain't shit compared to experience!' Charley extends his arms and places them around two cohorts who stand alongside him - a rather chubby fellow wearing a maroon U. Minn. sweatshirt who slightly resembles Donkey Lips from Salute Your Shorts, and a well-built athletic type with curly blond hair and a tribal band on his arm. 'So, in lieu of that, the Crisp-us Attackus has assembled a crackerjack squad to place a check mark next to a collegiate experience that has long been forgotten since the days of Louis Skolnick, Pee Wee Morris, and the Deltas of Faber College...' All four team members shoot a smile toward the camcorder and declare the mission in unison: 'PANTY RAID~!' 'Let's go down the wire and meet everyone. First thing's first...to the far left here: the brains behind the operation. He's carrying a 4.0 in every class, and once talked a co-ed into having sex with him using the schematics discussed in Hawking's 'A Brief History of Time.' Mort is his name, and science is his game. The science of underwear snitchology.' 'I'm on it,' Mort states, adjusting his horn-rimmed sunglass attachment over his regular prescription lenses. 'Next in line is the hoss who's going to be capturing these magic moments on tape so we can bring it back here and brag about it to all our friends later. He's currently studying to become a business guru, and wants to eventually market his own line of erotica...a man truly after my own heart...Todd!' 'I loves the pussy,' Todd grunts, forming his fingers into a V-shape and swiveling his tongue in between them. If there's one thing this film needs more of, it's junior high humor. 'And last...but certainly not least...standing at six-foot-four and weighing in at a musclebound two-hundred fifty pounds! The captain of Golden Gophers lacrosse and the vice-president of the Freshman Girls Welcoming Committee...he's just looking to add to his already-extensive collection of thongs and boyshorts...Aaron, ladies and gentlemen!' Aaron is obviously the quiet, mysterious type who lets his actions speak for themselves. Which is why his pelvic gyrations seem suitable enough for a one-liner. Go Gophers. 'Gentlemen...we reconvene at the rendezvous point in oh-oh-forty five. Get your ninja suits on, and we'll meet there for a final debriefing.' 'There she is, boys. The coup de gracie.' A light positioned just above the camera gives us a full view of the two-story red brick compound where Heaven awaits. The bright white Greek letters of Delta Zeta (easy DZ) shone through the darkness like a neon strip joint placard. The view immediately lowers behind the shrubbery of the starting point and onto a carefully constructed map of the area. The voice quietly giving the game plan is that of Mort's, who tilts the light toward the center of the paper, using his gloved hand to pinpoint the areas of concern. 'Okay, let's go over this one more time. It is absolutely crucial that you follow these orders to a 'T,' lest you be killed in the line of battle.' 'Yes sir, General Dickhead,' Todd responded, erupting a chuckle amongst Charley and Aaron. 'Fuck you, tubby. Now listen...around the back left here is the point of entry, which is the window of Kara Schaefer's suite. We lucked out 'cause she always has it open during the daytime, and she's been at the Student Center for about thirteen hours now studying for Calc. If it's closed, try pushing it, because chances are it's not locked.' 'What if it is?' Charley quipped, securing his bases. 'The back basement door here, on the left edge. There's no lock on it, but once you're inside, you'll have to be careful sneaking into the ground floor, because that's where the main hallway to the foyer is. Keep your ears to the door for footsteps, and don't go in unless you're absolutely sure it's safe.' 'Make sure it's safe...gotcha,' Charley repeated. 'Then what?' 'Well,' Mort continued, 'Kara's room is about 85 percent certain to be the point of entry. From there, you've got access to that whole floor...and if you're really brave, you can make your way up the stairwell and scout that territory. But, if you're discovered up there, you're royally fucking yourself, because escape isn't going to be easy. I'd stay downstairs if I were you guys...though...we all know that Cindy Sherlock's room is on that second floor.' 'Cindy Sherlock,' the three students mused, picturing her slip on a pair of satin sniffables over her creme-colored nether regions. 'I take it she's the cat's pajamas,' Charley guessed, looking for a little more to feed the motif. 'Dude,' Aaron piped, breaking his typical aura of silence, ' the ass of J-Lo with the face of Elisha Cuthbert.' 'I'm getting that fucking underwear,' was Cookie's immediate response...a semi on the verge of forming in his black sweatpants. 'That's the spirit!' Mort chimed in. 'But seriously, be careful. Make use of the headsets, and grab as much as you can as quickly as possible. We're under a deadline if we don't want to be caught.' 'Let's do this,' Todd demanded, tired of the talk and ready for the action. 'Todd, you follow Charley and take the footage. Aaron, split up and cover the rest of the ground. I'll be here to warn you guys in case campus security decides to stick their noses where it doesn't belong. And gentlemen,' Mort commanded sternly, 'if someone is captured, don't be the hero. Save yourselves.' 'Keep the masks on, fellas. If you're caught, you know NOTHING about who else was involved...and for God's sakes, Todd...whatever you do...do NOT drop that camera. That's evidence against all of us, and I can't afford to go to jail,' Charley warned. 'Don't worry,' Todd assured him. 'The camera stays with me at all times.' The mission was a go. Charley 'Cookie' Crisp, Aaron, and Todd crept around the side of the sorority house as if they were fresh out of military training. A safe arrival at the window of Kara Schaefer's Promised Land led to even more positive thoughts, as they discovered that Mort's hunch was absolutely right: the window was wide open with its tongue sticking through the frame, just begging for three mischievous boys to climb inside of it. 'Good work, Mort...it looks like the coast is clear in here. We're preparing to go in,' Charley reported. 'Roger that. Make as little noise as you possibly can.' 'Alright guys...let's get Todd up there first, then you can boost me through, Aaron.' The professional wrestler and the stalwart sports star easily lifted the husky Todd up and through the passageway, although it omitted quite the thud on the red carpeted floor of the suite. CAMERA: On. Todd focused in on the fully cloaked Charley Crisp, with nothing but his eyes visible through the holes of the mask. Cookie somersaulted on his dismount through the window, sitting up and shooting the camera an 'A-OK' gesture with his left hand. Aaron's hands could be seen grasping the ledge, as he prepared to hoist himself on top of the fixture. Offering an arm out, Charley assisted Aaron and thus pulled him inside. The three had successfully completed the first phase...it was like they had entered the Konami code at the start of Contra the way they made it look so easy. 'Hallelujah!' Charley piped, immediately walking toward the dresser on the left hand side of the room and opening it ever-so gently, pulling out a lacy little number and giving it a smell for added perverted measure. 'Oh man! We really made it!' Todd yelped. Their celebration seemed short-lived, as a small creak could be heard at the door. 'I'll be there in a minute! Just let me put my books down!' Fantastic. The view rapidly shifted directly to its side, as Todd clamored underneath the bed, moving faster than he ever had before. He jerked down the edge of the blanket next to him, shading any sort of presence and filling our eyes with a close-up of the stitching pattern of a four-leaf clover. The impact of texts hitting the bed startled the cameraman just a bit, but not as much as the ever-so slight edge of the mattress coming down onto his back as Kara sat down, humming the bars to John Mayer's 'Your Body Is A Wonderland.' Todd held his breath, careful not to make any noise. 'It stinks in here,' Kara said to no one in particular, rising off of the bed and shuffling around her dresser drawer for something. 'Where did I put it?' she asked. 'Maybe in the closet.' R. Kelly couldn't have scripted it any better. 'KARA!' a voice called out. 'COMING!' 'No, you aren't!' the unrecognizable voice responded. 'What are you looking for?' 'The Febreeze. Does it not smell funky in here to you?' A sniffing sound. 'No. Don't worry about it, I need to show you this before I go to bed!' 'Fine, I'm coming.' Creak. The sound of the door shutting was immediately followed by three simultaneous exhales, and the opening of the closet door. Todd grabbed the camera and rolled out from under his hiding place, putting the view onto Charley. 'That was fucking close!' Cookie mouthed, motioning to the door. 'Let's get out of here.' Miraculously, the coast was clear. Aaron had started his creep down the hall, unbeknownst to the residents of the Delta Zeta house, while Todd had an ass shot of Charley as they started up the stairs. 'I don't know about this,' Todd questioned. 'Relax...they're probably all asleep. We'll just get to Cindy's room, bust in there, and make a run for it.' They had made it to the top of the stairs. The first door of the hall was wide open, but the lights were turned off and there seemed to be no signs of life inside. 'A quick stop first,' Charley whispered, pointing toward the opened room. The two made their way inside and slowly closed the door behind them, flipping the light switch and examining their surroundings. 'First door on the right upstairs, Mort...who's room is this?' 'Dunno,' Mort stated, 'but Cindy's should be about two doors down on the opposite side.' Charley smiled at Todd, beckoning him closer so that he could get a full shot of the contents of the underwear drawer. Grabbing the handles, he pulled the compartment out... 'WHOAAAAA!' Todd and Charley both gasped in unison. It was not underwear...but something much more intriguing. Crisp pulled out the instrument and wondered whether or not the resident used it for rolling dough as well as for it's intended purpose. He wanted to stand it upright on the nightstand and watch a daredevil attempt to climb it. Cookie laughed aloud. 'Fantasy island if she's looking for a real-life version of this. Is there a scale model of the Eiffel Tower in there, too?' 'EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!' Both Todd and Charley looked at one another as the piercing scream shook the walls of the Zeta house. This could only spell one thing... 'Mayday! Mayday! I'm bailing!' ...Aaron had been discovered. Without warning, Todd made a run for the door and opened it. 'What the fuck, dude? Chill out, or they're going to catch us!' 'Forget THAT! We've gotta get out of here! We've got to...OH SHIT!' Feet, legs, and hysteria. MINUTES LATER... 'Look...the fat one forgot something!' CAMERA: Off. Quite honestly, the fate of his cohorts was the last thing on his mind at the time. Todd had created the perfect diversion as Charley held his ground inside the room. The pitter-patter of angry girls rushing past and down the stairs was enough of an all-clear for the ninja to peek out. They were gone for the time being. And two doors down, adjacent to his side...it was as if God was beckoning him with a giant finger from inside of it. 'Come, my son. Bask in the almighty warmth of moist undergarments here, in the land of milk and honey.' If you'll recall the old tale, Moses led his people to the Land of Israel. After enduring hardship after hardship, crossing seas and scaling mountains, convincing his peers that a burning bush had spoken forth to deliver some guidelines to live by, and putting up with all sorts of conniving bullshit, he had made it. Only to be denied his boating rights by God himself. The world went black just after a feminine yelp of surprise and a blow to the head from the hottest Pharisee to walk on the Minnesota campus. CAMERA: On. Charley was bound tightly to a computer chair...double reinforced with rope and duct tape. Like sorority sisters had this stuff handy in case of an intruder, or something. His mask off and his eyes still groggy, he gazed up to the camera as a chorus of giggles amassed. This was not Cindy's room. This was the room where the Tower of Babylon had been found stored openly in the top drawer of a cedar dresser. Said blond strutted gingerly in front of the camera, revealing a Crest Whitestrips commercial smile. With her hair pulled up in a ponytail, Twins jersey doing nothing to accentuate her figure, and a pair of yellow cotton pajamas, she still looked as dashing as the description. 'Welcome to...um...'Girls Gone Wild.' I'm your host, Cindy!' More giggling. 'Today, we have some guy...who decided it would be a wild idea to break into our house and steal our underwear! How original!' Cindy seductively made her way over to Charley, perching hard onto his lap and causing him to grunt with pain. 'What, did you think this was Revenge Of The Nerds or something? Well...answer me!' Charley cracked a half-hearted smile. 'I was just doing market research for Victoria's Secret.' The girls did not seem impressed with this attempt at humor. 'You talk too much already. Here,' Cindy said, peeling her sock off of her foot and balling it up, 'that oughta do the trick!' Shoving it into his mouth, they all laughed at Crisp's moaning and groaning as he tried tirelessly to spit out the disgusting gag. 'Now, normally, we'd just have the police take care of a matter like this. But, since you've had to interrupt us during FINALS of all times...and since she thinks you're cute...' Charley's eyes lit up. He loved it when a plan came together. 'Our very own house mom is going to teach you a little lesson in respect. OH MOM!' Time out for a second. In the world of Charley Crisp, deviant debonair, virtually anything isn't taboo when it comes to sexual encounters. Virtually. The best thing about writing, producing, directing, and starring in your own little films is that you decide what's off limits. There were a few things that Cookie declared off limits. The eyes of Crisp told the story as the House Mom walked into the room. The sound of laughter as the girls, including Cindy, dissipated as they all walked out of there and shut the door behind them. 'I see in your adventures, you found my little boyfriend,' a much older, much deeper-sounding voice could be heard from behind the frame. 'Maybe you two should get more acquainted, hm?'
