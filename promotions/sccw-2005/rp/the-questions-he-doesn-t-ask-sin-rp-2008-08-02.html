The Questions He Doesn't Ask (SIN RP)

2 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/2/08 Life must seem pretty peachy for Phillip Kennedy, eh? Two huge matches at his first ever PPV event. Katsidy wrapped around him like a second skin. A potential spot in the most dominant stable in wrestling seemingly being dangled before him every day like a steak before a hungry bear. Well, you'd be right. Life owns for him, much more than it owns for most other people. But he still has things he wonders about. These things are The Questions He Doesn't Ask. * * * I. 'Am I a member of the Dead Man's Hand?' The way Phillip Kennedy sees it, he is sort of like the myriad 'connected men' to the Italian Mafia that are commonly seen in mob movies. You know the type. They fence stolen goods, participate in minor crimes, and basically do whatever the Hell they're told to do in hopes of one day being a made man with the full protection of the mob. The difference, of course, is that the Mafia has an induction ceremony. You sit down in a darkened room, swear your allegiance, and shed some blood. This usually comes after you've 'made your bones', ie, offed someone the mob doesn't like. Best Kennedy can tell, the Dead Man's Hand has no such pomp and circumstance. Being a member of the Hand seems to be something that's gained from direct association with the Spider in the Web herself. That's how Hunter got in. How Katsidy and Savant are in. How Ashe and Hawke are in. He doesn't quite know the full story behind Bridden, but as much as he wants to ask, he won't. At All-In, he figures, he'll find out one way or another. He's teaming with a member of the Dead Man's Hand. Not a wrestler, per se, but really, other than Jadian Bridden, Kennedy is the only wrestler under the sway of the Hand, depending on how Hunter Sabuani happens to feel in the coming days and weeks. He is, at the very least, connected to the group. Connected at the hip, some would argue. Even Kennedy himself admits that the Hand is the reason he's in the Heaven & Hell match. Sure, he beat Bridden on his own, but Charley Crisp and Andre Jensen were eliminated by himself courtesy of some good old fashioned DMH shenanigans. The Big Stack is one of the only soldiers on the ground. The other soldiers work best behind the scenes, plotting and planning and putting into motion the ideas that need muscle to be carried out. Kennedy is that muscle. So is he a member of the Hand? For all intents and purposes, probably. II. 'Do I want to be a member of the Dead Man's Hand?' So he's in. Probably. But does he want to be? The Dead Man's Hand assures many things. It ensures that Kennedy will always have back up whenever he needs it. It assures the spotlight. Money. Power. Respect. But it also assures that he'll be...a target. Kennedy knows this. It hasn't happened yet, but there are a whole bunch of people opposed to the Dead Man's Hand called the Highwaymen. Phil's even been in the ring with one of them, Sean Sterling. Sterling won the match, which was to be expected really. He beat a rookie in said rookie's third match. As time goes on, though, Kennedy wants to reach the top. That's why he's in this business. It isn't all money and Katsidy for the Big Stack. He's a competitive son-of-a-gun who wants to be the best. Arguably, being in the Hand is the best way to ensure that, since you have backup out the fucking wazoo. Elliott Rollins, Jonathan Rhine, and Sean Sterling haven't really come calling yet. Nor has BloodAngel, a man locked in a death struggle with the Hand's fearless leader. Nor, for that matter, has Lance Marshall, the uber-honorable Universal Champion who spoke to Kennedy last week and didn't come to blows with him. 'Course, if Kennedy wins Big Slick, he'll probably wait till Marshall's hurt and then kick him in the dick a lot to become Universal Champion. World sucks like that. There are a shitton of people who could fuck Kennedy up very, very badly if he stands by the Hand. Of course, there are a lot in the Hand who could do the same if he doesn't. Sp, does he want to be a member of the Hand? Again, let's lean towards the positive end of the spectrum. III. 'So, Katsidy, what exactly are we?' A more personal question, this one. He knows that they are, collectively, tag team partners, set to compete in a few days for the Strength in Numbers Championship He knows that she does absolutely wonderful things with her mouth. He knows that she says he does absolutely wonderful things with his mouth. But really, what are they? She refers to him as 'lover', a perfectly nondescript terms. They are lovers, but likely not in love. In the deepest pit of his soul, he doubts Kathryn Shaw is capable of love. Why he doubts this is uncertain, though he figures it's because she's Desade's right hand...a very touchy-feely hand. Savant worked behind the scenes. Katsidy did the dirty work on the front lines...the very dirty work, really. Was she even attracted to him? Probably, that. At least a little bit. Using sex to keep someone in line was one thing...the sheer quantity and quality that Katsidy seemed to employ meant that it was more than just work. Phillip Kennedy has never met Alexandra Pierce. Not once. And yet, he knows one thing. One very basic thing about the relationship that they both have and yet do not have at the same time. She believes the way to keep him happy is to keep his arms full of an often-times rather unclothed Katsidy. All things considered, even Kennedy himself has to admit it's a pretty good read. IV. 'What does Desade want?' Of the questions he doesn't ask, this is the one he not only doesn't ask, but will never ask, since it seems like a good way to get his ass handed to him by everyone else Desade associates with. At the same time, it infuriates him, because everyone else seems to know. Lance Marshall, by his own admission, believes that it's all a game to her. A game to prove the base nature of humanity, that there are people who will jump when she commands. Moreover, to prove that if she wants to ruin someone...she can. Easy as breathing. It's a hobby for her, if he believes Marshall. Just like Kennedy when he plays poker. Phil ruins wallets, Alex ruins lives. For the sane among us, that's a slightly unnerving thought. People don't like to admit that they use people as chess pieces...nor do they like to be pieces themselves. If he thinks about it rationally, Kennedy knows that he's a pawn in Alexandra Pierce's giant, all-encompassing game. How often he's been used already he doesn't know. He suspects the Sterling and Connors matches weren't created completely out of the blue by Sin City management, but of course, he can prove nothing. Desade is beyond crafty. A mastermind, really. So why is she in the rasslin' business? To Kennedy, she seems like one who could set her sights so much higher. She strikes at her enemies both in and out of the ring, with precise or overwhelming forces depending on the situation. She has obvious people skills, or hires people who do. She seems like a movie mastermind, a James Bond villain come to life. Why are her sights set on Sin City? What are her goals? Kennedy doesn't know, but he does know she probably wouldn't tell him. V. 'Just how in the Hell did all of this happen to me?' This is the big one, really. The one that lurks just beneath his conscious mind at almost all times. It's always there because it's part of his life now. Whether he's sitting in a hotel room bought and paid for with his Sin City Championship Wrestling salary, or in the ring against one of the best the sport has to offer, it all seems impossible to the Big Stack. He's been in Sin City a couple months, and he's already a well-known name with a beautiful woman wrapped around him whenever they meet. He's also got an agent calling the shots for him behind the scenes. He is, for all intents and purposes, a star. All In could make him a superstar, if all of the cards fall properly. But even knowing that. Even with that in mind, the nagging thoughts are there. Why has the Dead Man's Hand pursued him so thoroughly? They seem, Kennedy knows, quite intent on keeping him happy. He was a total rookie with two matches under his belt when Katsidy first dragged him someplace. These are the questions he doesn't ask. Why doesn't he ask them? That is a lot easier to answer than the others. * * * 'Phil? Lover? Yoohoo...earth to Kennedy...' The Big Stack snapped back to reality, pretty quickly remembering where he was: a hotel room, one night before the biggest night of his life on Pay-Per-View. The other thing he realized was that he wasn't alone in said hotel room. 'Oh! Hi, Kat,' he manages, somewhat lamely, looking up at his partner-lover, a white terrycloth bathrobe wrapped around her ample curves. 'You've been thinking again,' Katsidy accuses, a faint smile turning up the corners of her lips. 'What have I always told you about that?' Her fingers toy with the loosely-tied knot holding the robe together, and suddenly Kennedy finds it far more difficult to concentrate on his darker thoughts. 'Leave the thinking to you,' he quotes, a smile returning to his own face. 'I know, I know. But it's a big night tomorrow. We've got the SIN match, and then I'm going to be in the main event.' Main Event. The words SOUND rarified to a rookie who's been at it three months, yet is main eventing his first show, let alone his first PPV. 'We've been over the SIN match a billion times today,' the Sex Kitten purrs, pushing him gently to a lying position on the room's king-size bed. 'We have a plan, It's a pretty good plan, if I do say so myself.' The Kennedy/Katsidy team is pretty simple, really. Phillip Kennedy is the muscle, the wrestler of the pairing. Sure, Kat CAN wrestle, but it's much more fun for her to watch him beat people up. Kathryn Shaw, by contrast, is the mind. And the tits, those are probably going to be pretty important if they want to win. The plan is simple: maximize each team member's strengths, minimize each team member's weaknesses. 'It is a good plan,' the Big Stack admits, eyes dilating to the size of saucers as he watches Katsidy shrug the terrycloth robe from her shoulders, to reveal...well, 'outfit' might not be the proper word. The pink lingerie that she wears appears to be just one size too small on top, allowing her breasts to appear as if they are popping out. Well,' she queries, brows lifting and lips quirking in the same 'Gotcha!' expression a big game hunter might have when they bag a rhino. 'You've said that you like pink lingerie...' Her hands are already moving, one of them to take his right hand and place it at her hip. The other moves to turn out the lamp on the nighttable next to Kennedy. 'I do,' Kennedy replies. Slowly, he's getting used to this; used to the fact that Kathryn Shaw just might be insatiable, and used to seeing her in various, equally lovely stages of undress or near undress. 'Though it occurs to me that there's one last thing that we need to do before tomorrow.' 'Really,' she asks, her question a whispered purr in his ear. He doesn't reply by words, doing something instead in the darkness that prompts a moan from her that Eugene Ware hears in his wettest fantasies. 'Yes,' the Stack admits, fighting back a grin. 'I may only be a rookie, but there's a chance you'll be in the ring tomorrow. I should probably show you some of my more...potent manuevers.' 'Will they require practice,' the Sex Kitten asks, her attempt at an innocent voice not quite convincing. 'I do hope so, because the ones I have planned to teach you most certainly do.' 'Lots of practice,' Kennedy replies, fighting back an anticipatory shiver. 'Long, hard practice. Over...' 'And over?' she asks, either hopefully or amusedly. 'And over again,' he agrees, before a muffled series of sounds begins to echo throughout the darkened hotel room. There is wrestling going on, probably, though I doubt Kennedy's trying to teach her, say, the rear naked choke. Rear and naked, though, are likely involved. * * * So, yeah. SHE'S probably the primary reason that they remain The Questions He Doesn't Ask. But there are others. If he asks them, for example, he'll find any of the dozens of reasons to stop the path he's going on. He'll realize that his new friends are sociopaths and worse. He'll realize that he, for all of the hemming and hawing he would do if ever confronted, is little better than they are. He'll realize that he should get the Hell out of wrestling while he has the chance.
