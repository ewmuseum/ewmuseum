A New Beginning

Date: 16th July New Fedhead Mini-Synopsis of Past Events! Suzanne Larsson. Former bodybuilder and indie wrestler whose twin brother, Charlie Larsson, bagged her a spot in GTT. They didn't know their father, Magnus Larsson, who disappeared when they were young, shortly after they moved from Iceland to America. Magnus left America due to falling out with the Icelandic Council dring the Fischer vs Spassky World Chess Championship match. The Council were a group of men possessed of 'Gifts', such as Coercion and Foresight. He was thought to be a 'Blank', with no Gifts, but that turned out to be extremely incorrect. After fleeing to America with his children, the Council searched for him, first to reconcile him with them, and then eventually as a danger to their plans. Utilising the help of the CIA, the Council tracked him down, but he managed to use his Gifts to fake his own death sufficiently well to avoid further tracking. Back to the present (or at least the recent past), and Suzanne's appearance in GTT alerted the Icelandic Council to the whereabouts of the missing twins. The Council, though, had been eroded over time, and there were just two remaining members. Lars Halldorsson (father of Magnus Larsson) and his cousin, Gunnar Arnisson. Lars had the gift of Foresight, but it had been fading as he aged, and Iceland's financial prosperity had floundered worse than the rest of the world in the financial crisis. Magnus fought a cloak and dagger war with the Council for the attention of Suzanne. The first shots were fired when Magnus Coerced Suzanne's husband into suicide and framed the Council for it. This escalated until a showdown in the week before SIN on Spike, where Suzanne Larsson chose to believe her father, and shot dead both Lars Halldorsson and Gunnar Arnisson. Upon carrying out her father's dirty work, he revealed the truth of the matter and she turned her wrath on him. Whilst strangling her father, Suzanne found out something she hadn't expected; that her father had planned this all along. As she choked the final gasps out of his body, Magnus Larsson utilized a Gift thought long lost to the members of the Council, and transferred his mind into her body, with the effect of sending her mind into his. As her father took control of her person, she realized the truth too late, and her story ended. And thus begins the story of Magnus Larsson. *** The first thing I noticed was the smell. Everything was different. I knew it would be, really. You don't go from inhabiting one body to another without things changing, but the stench was unexpected. I've been with plenty of girls in my time and expected it to be something kinda nice, but this was like being assaulted by a dozen sweaty lesbians, and not in a good way. It offended my senses somewhat more than the fact that I had just caused the death of both my father and my daughter. The aftermath was just strange. I hadn't expected to feel nothing, but it was just like taking care of business. I called the men I had in place, and they carted away the bodies, as I grabbed Charlie and got the hell out of there. Charlie. I guess my big question no is what to do with him. He still looks at me and sees his twin sister. My new muscles remember how they have always walked, how they moved and how they can hit and throttle people. I've got the perfect disguise, but there's always the chance that I could fuck things up. It could be one of a million little ways, and for the moment at least I don't seem to have access to my Gifts. I know that Suzanne had Foresight on occasion, but I think it'll be a matter of time before I can work out the, well, the science of it all. So I will keep Charlie around for the time being. He can act as the barrier between me and the world, fielding my calls, protecting me from any questions I might not know the answer to. And who knows? Maybe one day I'll actually need his body, either to go directly into him, or perhaps him children. I know it can only be a family member that I can transport myself to, but it's not the kind of thing that you just try on a whim. I was desperate when I played my game with Suzanne. She didn't know that I had terminal cancer, and I didn't know if it would even work. I had studied it all, and I had done everything I could, but it was a gamble. All in all, it went pretty well. Despite the constant smell that I can't get rid of no matter how many showers I take, this has been a pretty unqualified success. I've even ended up with a job I can really get behind while I try to work out how to use my Gifts again. What more could I want than to be able to use my new body to hurt people, smash them in their faces, bang their heads off the mat and even hit them in the face with whatever I find nearby. I find the whole combination of ferocity and mind-games attractive. So now I'm in this federation of wrestlers, with new champions for every single belt within it. It's a new era of SCCW and I've found myself in the vanguard of talent shaping where it is to go. My first victim as champion is a young fool called Legion. I have ten minutes in the ring to do with him as I wish. I could defeat him easily, I'm certain of it. I can feel the strength in my muscles, much greater than I've ever had before. The challenge isn't whether or not I can defeat him; it's how much fun I can have doing it. All I can think of is which way I can screw him over the most. There are just so many options. You know what? I think I'm going to enjoy my time in SCCW.
