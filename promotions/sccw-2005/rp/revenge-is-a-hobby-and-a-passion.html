Revenge is a hobby and a passion

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Varies When someone has done you wrong what do you do. When your blood burns for payback and you want to not only get revenge but leave a very personal mark on that person what do you do? When something in the back of your mind snaps and you are blinded by hatred what do you do? Some people go the highroad and try and be the better man. Some people forgive those that wronged them. Some people lash out with rage. Some people take a much more long term approach to revenge. Gideon is the type of man that will pick his spot patiently. When you wrong him you make a very dangerous enemy. Gideon Fontaine is known across the world as a billionaire and a sharp businessman. The Osaka Assassin is a world class fighter and one of the sharpest submission wrestlers on the planet. Those that truly know him learn that his mind works on a scale not often seen. Perhaps an example is in order. Back in his days in UJW Stephanie was teamed up with a friend of hers called Liz. Her and Stephanie were a team called Witchcraft and two women that held their own against Neil and Gideon in the ring. Of course they earned each other’s respect on many occasions. Liz left wrestling and Elizabeth Morrison got married to a man I’ll refer to simply as Larry. They settled down and should have had a happy life. As you can the tale takes a turn for the worse. Larry was not a man. He was a dog in human form led by his crotch and nothing else. When married to Liz he cheated on her and screwed around. Money was "Misplaced" as he paid for hotel rooms and strippers. As you can expect Liz eventually found out and then began a very messy divorce. By the time Gideon’s lawyers got around to helping her it was too late. Her credit was ruined beyond repair. Her mother and father disowned her after Larry played up being a victim. This man ruined her life and she even lost her sense of self-respect. She lost her life as she knew it and was cut off from her family. There was depression and Liz spiraled to a point where her friends had to drag her out of the bottle. Gideon made a vow that Larry would never have a happy day again. This is something that most do not know. Neil of course has figured some things out but they have kept the details secret from Liz. They have told Stephanie at various points some of the things that have gone on but the true depths Gideon has taken this to no one is aware of. Gideon considers this his hobby and is making it his goal to ruin Larry’s life in a personal way. There are trips that Gideon has taken on "business" but unknown to Stephanie and Neil it was personal. It is one thing to see on a report from a private investigator saying what has happened. It is quite another to view with your own eyes the man’s suffering. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building on September 19, 2006. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon is overlooking some papers sent to him by a private investigator when he sees that Larry has applied for a business permit. This intrigues Gideon and he looks at the information provided to him by the investigator. It seems that Larry has found someone to invest in a restaurant and he is going to be managing it. Mr. Rothman is the primary owner on the lease for the restaurant. Larry’s credit has been somewhat ruined over the years. Of course ruining a person’s credit isn’t that hard when you have them helping you do it. "Amanda can you please get a credit check and a work up done on a Mr. Rothman, Earl of 12573 Oak Terrace New York, New York." "Sure thing Gideon." It takes little time to get the information that is out there. Gideon has a lot of the small personal details that people don’t even realize that are open. He sees something that makes him chuckle. It seems that Earl Rothman has a wife Rebecca. If Larry is true to form he is either boning her or trying to. He dials up his private investigator in New York. "Wally need you to post a few men on Rebecca Rothman. I’ll have Amanda fax your office the details. I figure Larry will be trying to bone her or is boning her." "Idiot never learns does he." "It would appear not. Fax me anything you find out. And be sure you have pictures as well." "No problem Gideon. I’ll report back in a week. Larry hasn’t shown any will power yet I don’t figure he will start now. Gideon did some work and looked over a leaflet that Timo sent him about a federation called FUSE wrestling. Gideon crumpled it up and tosses it in the garbage. "I have had enough of politics and bullshit for more then one life time." Little did Gideon know that several months later he would actually join FUSE. Even Gideon can’t predict the future. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building on September 26, 2006. ########## ########## ########## ########## On this day Gideon opens a package. It is from his Private Investigator in New York. When he sees the pictures of Larry having sex with a blonde haired woman he doesn’t have to check to see who she is. He already knows who she is but of course he makes sure it is her. Gideon has taken this time waiting for Larry to eventually "step in it" to do a bit of research into Earl Rothman’s background and knows the classes he went to while at Berkeley College. He has gotten the information he needs and has a throw away cell phone. He makes a phone call. "Earl this is Richard O’Dell. We were in Doctor Thomas’s seminar at Berkley." "I can’t say I remember you it has been a long while." "Not all that surprised it has been years and I was in the back of the class. I saw your wife Becky go to the motel on 53rd and Amsterdam. She was with a guy with long brown hair and a goatee." "WHAT!!" "I hate seeing one of my frat brothers done dirty like that. You two have been together since Berkley……" The phone is slammed on Gideon’s ear and he makes a second call to Wally. "Send a DVD of the video surveillance to his office in a day or two." "You got it Gideon." There are some things that you have to wait for. He is going to let it sink in and more then likely Earl will be having some words with his wife and likely be extremely pissed off at Larry for betraying him like that. The thing is when you find out you have been betrayed like that by your wife and someone you thought was a friend it’s a very tough thing to accept. However seeing it with your own eyes is hard to imagine. In a day or two Earl Rothman will see his wife being screwed by a person he thought was a friend. A man he trusted enough to open a business and put a lot of his money on the line. He may be able to forgive his wife but Larry on the other hand has to pay. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building on September 29, 2006. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon is opening up a parcel the courier has delivered and there is a DVD. He knows what it is and pops it into the player. He is smiling ear to ear. "I wish I had some popcorn." The footage starts playing and Larry can be seen getting out of a car. The camera turns and you see Earl Rothman come at him with a bat. Larry barely has time to move when the bat comes swinging at his head and shatters the window of his car. "YOU GOD DAMN SON OF A BITCH!!! I’LL FUCKING KILL YOU!!!" The look of fear in Larry’s eyes is something he is enjoying. There is something deeply satisfying when you see karma catch up with an asshole like Larry. Of course it never hurts to help it along. Larry is hiding behind the car. He sees the look of rage in Earl’s eyes as he swings across the hood of Larry’s car denting the hood. "What the hell is wrong with you?!" "While you were talking to me about starting up the fucking restaurant you were screwing my wife you fuckin piece of shit!" Larry keeps running as Earl circles the car. Earl decides to break the windows with the bat. Larry sees his car being destroyed. When Larry tries to stop Earl by grabbing the bat he gets punched in the face. Earl has him down on the ground and is beating the living hell out of him. At that point 3 policeman pull up and get out of an unmarked car. One pulls a gun and they are trying to talk down Earl while they pull him off Larry. Larry gets up as Earl is being handcuffed. "This bastard attacked me with a bat and wrecked my car!!" "We witnessed the attack. We are going to take him to the station and book him for assault. Lieutenant Chalmers will take you to the hospital while we wait for a patrol car." Larry leaves with the officer in the unmarked car. "He screwed my wife!" "We know Mr. Rothman. We aren’t really policemen. Right now Larry is being taken to the hospital and Chalmers will ditch him there while claiming to repark the car. Our client thought that we should be here in case things got out of hand." There is a look of confusion as Earl has the handcuffs removed by the "officers". He is confused looking at the 2 men. "What client? What do you mean? Who are you guys?" "I can’t say. I never actually met the man. I work for a PI. All I can tell you is Larry has a very powerful enemy and he must have a load of money. This might make you feel better. There will be a report that Larry got into a fight at a local gay bar. If he tries to press charges for you attacking him get in touch with this lawyer." One of the officers passes Earl Rothman a card with the name and number of a lawyer. "Of course Larry hasn’t got a clue what is going on. If he cause problems feel free to use the brawl at the gay bar as a reason for ending any business arrangements and sue him." Earl looks at these men in confusion as they walk away. A few minutes later the footage ends and Gideon removes the DVD and puts it back in the CD case. Gideon is looking at the expense report as well as a copy of the report of the bar fight that Larry caused in the gay bar. He puts all of that away in the folder and walks over to the filing cabinet and adds it to the rest of the folders. "Paybacks are hell Larry." Gideon returns to his desk and goes back to his paperwork. ########## ########## ########## ########## At Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## As you can imagine Neil is pissed off. Nothing angers Neil anything more then politics and being on the receiving end of a screw job. Neil is in the gym and he is working the heavy bag with knees and body shots. There is an edge to Neil’s mood and it isn’t good. Time to go back to the basics and what made Neil Whittaker the meanest bounty hunter in New York City. "Fucking goddamn idiots!" This is a match that Neil and Gideon have to take VERY seriously. They have a handicap match against Tsuyoshi Tanaka and they know that this is a match that will bury them if they lose. "I’d like to have the fuckin idiot that booked the match in front of me right now." Neil’s voice is calm but you can see that he is very angry as he starts working on the heavy bag. The sheer force and speed he is driving those knees and body shots into the heavy bag is frightening to watch. You are watching a 7 ft 2 and 359 pounds of muscle practicing working a man’s stomach and ribs. Add in the fact that Neil is angry and the Alliance have been backed into a corner and it is even worse. "We lose this match and one man has beaten the former tag champs. Just when I though the Fatal Faction division was getting some fuckin respect some stupid goddamn idiot books a fucking handicap match shitting on every fuckin team on the roster." To understand why Neil is pissed think it through. A few weeks ago Amy took on Clinton Sage in what many thought was a very close match. Now this week against Tsuyoshi Tanaka it is a handicap match. What the hell happened in 2 weeks when a Fatal Faction wrestler is a fair fight for the Universal title holder and now it takes two members of the Fatal Faction to be considered a fair fight for someone going after the Universal title? "This week we gotta beat Tanaka. If we lose this match we lose everything." The Alliance know Tsuyoshi Tanaka from NEW and have a lot of respect for him. They even faced Tanaka in a tag match when he teamed up with Gibson and Cool VS the Alliance and Timo. There was a receipt due now they have to beat him in a handicap match or their career in FUSE will be pretty much ruined. Tanaka can beat anyone on the roster and this is playing Russian roulette with their careers. "FUCKIN IDIOTS!!" With a snarl Neil is hammering and hammering on the heavy bag. The Alliance are backed into a corner. This is their careers on the line. They lose this match and one man beats one of the top teams in the Fatal Faction division. That shows that the Fatal Faction division is at best mid card jokes that don’t stand a chance against a main eventer. After an hour passes Neil is still working out in the gym and cursing up a storm. For them this is as real as it gets. Tanaka is hungry and the Alliance has everything to lose. After this week the Alliance may be finished in FUSE. ########## ########## ########## ########## At Gideon’s Mansion in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon has finished working out and is walking around his mansion after a shower wearing a black silk robe. You see a calm look on his face but you know as he enters the living room that he is just as driven as Neil. He sits on the couch and contemplates what his next move is going to be. He has made a few phone calls but one of the people he has called called back yet. Caleb Alan has never been one for being reliable. As he is thinking that the phone rings and he picks it up. Gideon: Hello. Caleb: I wish you had of been here earlier Gideon. Miss 36 was truly exceptional and a natural redhead. Gideon: I had to train. Caleb: Ah yes that wrestling thing. I honestly don’t understand why you bother. I’ve been retired for a few years and I have so much more time for my fans now. Gideon: There isn’t time for womanizing. This week we are planning payback. Caleb: I would hope so. I mean taken out by security guards and cowboys. Is there anything more humiliating then being beaten by men whose greatest accomplishments in life include memorizing the phrase "Do you want fries with that" or managing to see a female breast in real life without money changing hands? From the pause you can tell Gideon is thinking the same thing but he is trying to keep his cool. Gideon: There is a significant payback being planned. Caleb: Ah tell me more. Gideon: Well we are going to start off…………… Gideon goes into the plan and Caleb and Gideon share a good laugh. When the Alliance is angry they are fun to watch. Caleb Alan was part of a stable with Neil, Gideon, and Stephanie and the things they did were based on numbers and pure physical violence. They were called Violence Incorporated and they worked extremely well together. It didn’t matter who teamed up together. There were even tag team finishers based on whoever was there. Neil and Caleb were the deadliest of the 4 combining incredibly high impact moves. A move that Caleb and Neil used was called "25 to life" because of how dangerous it was. Imagine Neil power bombing someone into a Rude Awakening neck breaker. That can snap a neck in a heartbeat. These are 4 people that are driven and dangerous. The things that are intended for Killzone 47 will be a day of violence not seen in several years. ########## ########## ########## ########## At Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## Neil is in the living room watching the news while talking on the phone. It is times like these that you find out who your friends are. There have been a lot of people that have called him. There have been a lot of WTF style questions as well as wondering about the Media blackout. Of course they are all in agreement about the media and their crusade about vilifying the wrestling business. This pisses off Neil. If it wasn’t for the wrestling business he would likely be dead by now shot in a bar or back alley. If it wasn’t for wrestling Neil wouldn’t have a wife he loves more then he ever imagined possible. If it wasn’t for wrestling he would not be a father and have a son he loves so much. Neil: Yeah it pisses me off. This week is bullshit Duncan. Devane: What the hell are they thinking? Neil: Not a fuckin clue. Next time one of those assholes from the FUSE front office asks me to piss in a cup I’m dragging one of them with me and they are taking the test too. They have to be on some shit. Devane: So what have you got planned? Neil: Payback pure and simple. Let me tell you what Gideon told me earlier…………… Neil is filling in Duncan about what Gideon has in mind as well as the things that they are planning. Back when Duncan was wrestling as Stone Devane he knew the golden rule of wrestling. "Someone wrongs you. You make them pay." Simple and to the point. The Alliance have a certain MO that they have had for over a decade. It doesn’t matter the federation. You cross the Alliance and it doesn’t matter who you are a receipt is coming. This was true in Core Wrestling when Matthew Lanier felt their wrath. This was true in New Era Wrestling when Bryan Chase the 3rd felt their wrath. Phil Payne was in charge of New Era Wrestling and fell victim to the Alliance. The Beast despite being a friend of the Alliance for over a year got the same treatment when he crossed the Alliance in the LWA. Then there was when Ted Billings running Pacific Pro Wrestling. He thought that The Alliance fighting Stephanie was a good idea. Ted Billings had his testicles driven up into his pelvis and learned that the Alliance may play the role of the hero but they are not heroes. When you live the life they have led and have the values they do there are 2 people in the world. There are the winners and there are victims. Last week the Alliance were the victims and this week they are going to do something about it. They face Tsuyoshi Tanaka in a handicap match. On any other week they would do the right thing and make it a singles match. It wouldn’t matter whether Tanaka faced Neil or Gideon it would be a good fight and more importantly a fair fight. Now they don’t care about fair fights. The most important thing is winning. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon has on his desk a variety of reports and folders. There are credit card bills for Larry. There are his cell phone logs and many upon many pictures of Larry and his life being ruined. There are time stamped pictures of him screwing around on Liz with a stripper named Celeste. There were 3 flings with prostitutes. Seeing Larry’s life ruined makes him happy because of what he did to Liz. You might be asking you why would Gideon go to such lengths to torture this man and continually ruin his life. You might ask when has he paid for what he did. To a man like Gideon his friends are important. When you hurt one of them he takes it personal. The depression he saw Liz sink into was heartbreaking and the man that did that has to suffer. An eye for an eye is the easiest way to explain it in a more biblical standard. Last week there were several people that wronged Neil and Gideon. The names are carefully recorded. FUSE Black Shirt Security, Hunter Jones, Araceli Negra, Wyatt Colton and Jake Ennis, and the puppet master himself Riley Black. There will be a Reckoning Day. You can hear the theme music. # Don't want no revenge # # Ain't no pay back time # # It ain't called getting even # # Here comes the reckoning day # This week they have to beat Tsuyoshi Tanaka. They have a plan. They will do whatever it takes to win. They will deal with the rest. To men like the Alliance revenge is a hobby and a passion.
