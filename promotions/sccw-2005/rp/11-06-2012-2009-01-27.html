Date: 01/27/2009 Word Count: 1997 minus beginning quote "Throughout human history, as our species has faced the frightening, terrorizing fact that we do not know who we are, or where we are going in this ocean of chaos, it has been the authorities, the political, the religious, the educational authorities who attempted to comfort us by giving us order, rules, regulations, informing, forming in our minds their view of reality. To think for yourself you must question authority and learn how to put yourself in a state of vulnerable, open-mindedness; chaotic, confused, vulnerability to inform yourself." - Timothy Leary * * * From the Hidden Journal of Rick Malloy, recovered 04/17/2054 by nomadic tribe of unknown origin: Date - 11/06/2012 Thinking is a concept of free will. If that is true, free will died a long time ago along with the rest of society. We are not allowed to think for ourselves. The government, religious, economic, and educational systems control everything. We can’t even control our thoughts anymore. I feel like a slave to the system that controls me and everyone else. Actually a slave zombie to be more precise. Everyone walks around like sheep brainwashed by the propaganda that the government puts out. Anyone who speaks out is never heard from again. I’ve heard the stories of people being taken away in black hoods like prisoners of war. When the economies collapsed and the societies waged their wars, that was when civilization as people used to know it crumbled into anarchy. Nations fell like dominoes until only a few remain. Some here in the United Empire of America think that this is all a lie to keep the current government in power. However I am not inclined to comment on this at this time since I have not seen any evidence to prove this theory to be correct. I do think that a lot of what they say is propaganda to keep the country from destabilizing and becoming a chaotic mess of anarchy and rioting. According to today’s news, the feeble Monarchy of the United Kingdom was attacked by our superior flying squadrons which bombarded their western perimeters in preparation for our land invasion. Our forces are already underway with the invasion of the Republic of Mexico. Assimilation is well underway according to the state sponsored news channel. Pakistan is once again battling with India. The states of Russia and Ukraine are still having their energy wars. Georgia is helping Ukraine fight off the Russian horde with their military. The Arabian Empire is fighting with the Balkan Alliance over territory and oil. Everything is in chaos. All of the governments around the world are on the verge of declaring World War III. The heroes of old have disappeared. Nothing has been the same since the Judge put himself on a self-imposed exile back in 2010 after executing a criminal on live national television. Heroes were banned after that and so was wrestling which made me jobless. These are dark times for everyone. However almost all of them don’t realize it. The only ones that do are a part of the rebellion against the United Empire of America. Of course the regime doesn’t even acknowledge the rebels and instead blames it on the "British colonials" who want to get back at us for the American Revolution. I saw a firebombing attack by them a few weeks back. All of the attackers were shot dead by the Imperial Police. Ever since then, I’ve been more cautious. Even something as insignificant as keeping this journal is enough to get me killed in this day and age. * * * 11/07/2012 Night. Walking down the deserted street, nothing stirs except for the slight howling sound of the breeze that’s coming in from the mountains. Salt Lake City has been this way ever since the Mormons were taken to Americanization Facilities by the government after they resisted joining the government sponsored religion and stuck with their religion. Americanization Facilities also used to be called Concentration Camps. The Nazis used to kill people in these same sorts of camps except for these new ones have built in brainwashing or, as they call them, reeducation facilities. I keep quiet and don’t look around as there are Imperial Police milling about on the streets for some reason. Don’t really see build up like this too often. Something important must be happening. The propaganda posters and flat screen televisions that show constant government news and propaganda twenty-four seven on the walls of the buildings I pass don’t even catch my attention. With all the police around, I just want to get the hell out of here as quickly as possible. Across the street I see a man walking. His footsteps are the only sound I hear besides my own. However I notice two seedy looking men following him. From their dress and attire I would say that they are government agents. The cool night breeze blows leaves and newspapers down the street. The man across the street starts to walk faster as he notices the two men following him. Suddenly a white van speeds up and then slides to a stop beside the man. The two men who were following him quickly grab him and handcuff him. I keep walking as to not draw any attention to myself. Any resistance to the regime could be fatal. They throw the man into the van and jump in themselves before speeding off. I keep walking like nothing happened. On every building I pass the propaganda posters begin to catch my eyes now. It’s an over stimulation of government oppression. As much as I want to go home, I think being in a public place is my safest option right now after being a witness to the apprehending of the man only moments earlier. After a few moments, an idea finally pops into my head that may just keep me from being ‘re-educated’ this evening. I have to get to the bar I frequent. It may be my only hope. * * * An hour later I’m at Murphy’s Bar & Grill off of South Main Street sitting at the dimly-lit bar drinking some non-alcoholic beverage. Alcohol has been illegal for a few years now. Caffeine too. You can’t even procreate anymore without a permit. Land of the free my ass. The mere word freedom brings a smile to my face as I bring my mug to my mouth to take another sip of my drink. God I miss the days when you could drink alcohol. After setting the mug down, I look over to see the other losers like me who sit around this bar all the time so much we all know each other by name. Next to me is Ash. He’s a black haired loner who usually comes in wearing a trench coat. Has a wife at home. Says her name is Amanda. Says he comes from Vegas. I went there a few times back before this country became an Empire. Nice town after the Judge and the Rat Pack cleaned it up before heroes were banned because they were a ‘menace to the government’ or some nonsense like that. Next to him at the bar is James. He’s from Vegas too. Told me he’s a teacher but used to be a writer. He left there after it became the main Southwestern Region Imperial Base. There are a few others but those are the main two I talk to in the bar besides Tom the bartender. We have to watch what we say because of the government types running around town. They have cameras and listening devices everywhere. Ash turns to me and smiles. He seems to be a bit out of it tonight. "You see what the President had to say on the news?" he asks me, seeming a bit despondent about whatever it was that was said. In the past he has expressed his displeasure with the Judge Act of 2010 that banned heroes from working the streets. He feels that the law was passed to kill the will of the people by taking away the heroes that protected them. I can tell he’s very bitter about that for some reason. Don’t know why and probably never will. "Nah," I reply. "I was out of the house." Just as those words leave my mouth, the flat screen in the bar starts to show a replay of what she said. The President is a middle aged woman. Her husband was famous as the leader of our nation a long time ago, in the good times, before the Empire. She was the only major person in the cabinet who wasn’t killed in the terrorist attack a few years back. Ever since then she has had any resistance and any dissidents crushed. She stares out with a stone cold glare at the camera. Her image is being broadcast throughout the United Empire of America and she wants to show her subjects that she means business. I’ve seen her do this thousands of times before. Finally, after a few moments of glaring, President Hillary Clinton begins to speak to the masses. People all over this nation are watching this just like me. http://media.newsbusters.org/media/2007-03-20Hillary.jpg "My fellow Americans," she starts off saying. "Today our forces have taken over the feeble nation of Mexico. Assimilation into our culture will be completed by nightfall tomorrow." Loud cheers can be heard from the street outside. Probably revelers basking in the glory of our victory in Mexico. "In Europe our forces are still bombing the puny English before we send in our ground forces. Hopefully the cowards surrender before then, for their sake. Also today in Salt Lake City the criminal vigilante terrorist known as Judge broke the law named after him by killing three innocent civilians in a brutal fashion. He shot all three of them in the head." "We will not waver, we will not falter. We will bring this criminal terrorist to justice and make him pay for his crimes against humanity. We are a benevolent nation but will not tolerate any acts of terror. If any person, group, or nation performs terrorist acts on our soil, they will feel the iron fist of America crushing them in short order. "So, to the people of America. I say to you that we will rise up stronger than we ever have before. We will bring this terrorist to justice and destroy the evil monarchist regime in the United Kingdom until they have no choice but to cower at our feet. America, the government is power. The government will keep you safe. The government is your God. And God blesses America." The feed shuts off and everyone goes back to doing what they were doing beforehand. A cold chill runs up my spine as I think about what the President had to say. The terrorist struck right here in Salt Lake City. Inside the bar it has become eerily quiet. Everyone is probably thinking the same thing I am. Who is Judge and what is he doing here in town? Looking around I see the same frightened look on everyone’s faces. I don’t blame them. It scares me as well. Back when I was a pro wrestler, things like this didn’t frighten me at all. Now they do. This society built on fear will do that to you. As I look around the bar, only one person seems to be cool, calm, and collected. That person is Ash. He continues to drink like nothing is going on. As he stares at a poster on the wall behind the bar of President Clinton that says ‘Your government will protect you’ on it, God only knows what he must be thinking. I know what I’m thinking. Too bad saying it out loud would get me killed on the spot by the Imperial Police. Fucking bastards. Better finish my drink and get home. The night is getting darker by the minute. The sad part is the darkness won’t go away when the Sun comes out in the morning. It’ll only get darker. * * * 02/02/2009 One night away from my first match in Just Wrestling. The butterflies are already tingling in my stomach. I’m in the main event. My fingertips tingle with excitement. Hairs are standing up on my arms. I just have to survive tomorrow and earn my contract. I have to show the people that I’m actually worth a damn. Hard sell in this industry. I just hope my future is a happy one.
