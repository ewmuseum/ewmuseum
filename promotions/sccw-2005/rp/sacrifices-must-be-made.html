Sacrifices Must Be Made

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The events in Venice involving Legion have just occurred and viewers of Temptation are seeing Legion being dragged out by security to the backstage area. We now show previously unseen footage that showed up in the forums of sccw.com for 24 hours before the link was taken down. Legion: Surely you guys can let go of me now right? The message has been sent, all I want to do now is go back to my locker room and watch the main event. Branch: Don’t think so, Miss St Germain and Mr Kensington want to see you first. Legion: Fine. At least let me walk there without feeling like my arm’s going to pop out of it’s socket! (The footage then cuts to inside the office of Kensington and St Germain.) Nigel: Gabriel what is it exactly that you’ve been trying to prove by attacking Eugene tonight? Legion: Nigel… you remember what Miss Germain signed a while back don’t you? The non wrestler’s waiver in case they get injured while in the line of duty. I could have made Eugene into another Mary Jackson so easy, he wouldn’t be able to criticise anybody with a broken jaw. Nigel: That’s how Eugene is. He may cross the line at times but I know that there’s no intentional malice behind it otherwise I wouldn’t have him here. Besides this I’m tempted to have you subject to a psychological profile. Legion: If you’re going to subject me to one how about you subject Kannon to one for his delusions of grandeur, von Doom for the fact he’s hanging around with psychopaths or even Kingsley for his sociopathic tendencies and admitting murders of animals LIVE on television! You’re singling me out because I’m not like Lane and decide to attack you directly. (Suddenly Adrienne enters the room.) ASG: How dare you accuse me of fixing the Chosen One battle royal! Legion: Here we go… you didn’t like the fact I’ve called you out do you? Newsflash: Kingsley had the right point, YOU just want use this company as your own personal plaything because daddy dearest gave you the money! (The room suddenly goes into stunned silence.) ASG: You want to think I’m using this place like a plaything? Next week at your little homecoming I’m picking your opponent and if you want to prove your little send a message attitude worthy of a title shot then you WILL fight who I pick. If you don’t well I’ll make sure you’re not booked for as long as I feel is needed. How does that sound to you? Legion: I’ll be there and we shall see… I hope you make the right choice. Because if you don't I just hope that the waiver doesn't apply to you darling, would just hate for that surgery to get all messed up. (Legion grins, thereis a flicker of madness beginning to show, was it the jet lag from flying from country to country for New Frontier and Sin City or something more sinister?) Legion: Now if you excuse me I'll go and watch the main event, I suppose the squad outside the door will be following me. Nigel: Yes but for your safety. Legion: I'm sure (!) Legion then leaves the room.
