Wylde Breaks his Silence

10 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/10/2008 The scene opens up in a part of Las Vegas that most people don’t even realize exists. Think about it when someone says Vegas what does the mind usually conjure up. Neon lights, gambling, all you can eat buffets, all night parties, showgirls you get the picture. The part they tend to forget is that Vegas is an actual city, with a downtown or area that houses all the government offices and other businesses. It is in front of one of these official looking buildings we find SCCW journalist Mary Jackson. IN the background we can see several other people, along with TV production trucks from CNN, CNBC, Fox News as well as the local broadcast affiliates. Mary Jackson: Good afternoon from Sunny Las Vegas! Mary Jackson here, wishing I was here for pleasure and not official SCCW business. I have it under good authority that we will finally get an opportunity to hear from the AWOL SCCW star and Black List member the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde! As most of you know Wylde has been absent for well over a month from the rings of SCCW. Wylde was booked to appear at a recent event and was a no show. Since that night I have personally tried to contact Wylde through his handlers and have gotten absolutely no where. A source that would like to remain anonymous within SCCW stated that the front office has also received the cold shoulder from the Wyld Child. That got this reporter a little curious. So that led me to the desert of Las Vegas. I visited the headquarters of Wyld Child enterprises and wasn’t even allowed to get past the security guard in the lobby. Knowing that I was in Vegas and Wylde’s reputation as a person who likes to live life to the fullest I started to check out several of the local hotspots including his own nightclub The Devil’s Own located in the MGM Grand hotel and kept getting the same story everywhere I went. No one has seen or heard a thing from Wylde in well over a month and a half. I even reached out to other so called journalists like those from TMZ.com who like to follow celebrities and they had nothing on the radar either. In a last ditch effort before leaving Vegas I even drove out to the outskirts of the city to Zakk Wylde’s home and couldn’t even get in the security gate that encloses the estate. Just as I was preparing to head to the airport I received an e-mail about a press conference that would happen here shortly on the steps of the US Circuit Court. As Mary wraps up the background information we can see the reporters from the networks starting to mobilize and head up the steps of the courthouse to meet three middle aged men dressed in sharp business suits and carrying briefcases. Mary Jackson: Looks as if things are about to start let’s listen in. Mary joins the rest of the crowd and attempts to push her way to the front between the cameras and outstretched arms holding microphones or tape recorders. Man: Good afternoon Ladies and Gentlemen. Thank you for coming today on such short notice. My name is Mark Gentile, I am the lead counsel for Mr. Zakk Wylde. With me as well are my colleagues Mr. Jason Blough and Mr. Richard Cunningham both are also on retainer to Mr. Wylde for various legal services. Our client would like to clear up several rumors that have been out there since his self imposed exile from both his vocation of professional wrestling and his duties as head of Wyld Child Enterprises. He knows that both his employers, as well as his employees, have suffered as a result of his disappearance. He regrets not being able to explain his absence until now but he was under instructions by us to do so. He wants to set the record straight so that he can get back to business as usual as soon as possible. Mr. Wylde will come down here in a moment and make a short statement if there is time he will take questions but be advised there are several issues that we have been dealing with that can not be disclosed at this time nor may they ever be disclosed. With that being said at this time I would like to introduce to you Mr. Zakk Wylde. Almost as if they were on cue the doors the courthouse swing open and out steps Zakk Wylde. Dressed in a custom made black suit with a black dress shirt and purple silk tie, Wylde pauses after crossing the threshold and looks around at the group of reporters eagerly awaiting him as well as the crowd of fans and other just curious folks who wanted to see just what the hell is going on that have gathered in the street. Wylde takes a deep breath and bounds down the steps with a purpose he shakes hands with Mr. Gentile and the lawyers take a step back and gives their client center stage. Wylde flashes a million dollar smile as several people on the street start a Wyld Child chant. Wylde: I’m glad they still remember me! The group of reporters and Wylde both chuckle at the joke. Wylde: Good afternoon. I wanted to take a few moments of your time to explain my whereabouts over the past month or so. Most of the people that know me and how I conduct business know that I don’t like keeping a low profile. I speak my mind whenever and wherever I want. Recent situations though prevented me from doing what is natural to me. Wylde pulls a piece of paper from the inside breast pocket of his jacket and unfolds it. He raises his sunglasses and looks over the document then he shields his eyes with his Oakley’s once again and stuffs the paper back in his pocket. Wylde: I’ve also never been one to have other people put words in my mouth. All due respect to my legal team but I think I’m going to do this my way. The team of lawyers begin to look a little nervous as Wylde continues. Wylde: First and foremost to my businesses, things will be back to normal first thing in the morning. I will be stopping by the offices in the morning so to those of you watching this right now...things better be in order by the time I get there or heads will roll. We will be having a celebration at The Devil’s Own tomorrow night open to the public first come first served. There will be an open bar and a Vegas style buffet free as well. Call your friends cause I don’t just let anyone into my club for a spread like this once in a lifetime party. Wylde removes his Oakley’s and takes a deep breath. The million dollar smile fades and his tone becomes a bit more serious. Wylde: My next piece of business has to do with my professional wrestling career and SCCW. I am proud to announce that I will be returning to the rings of SCCW in the Conseco Fieldhouse in Indianapolis Indiana on Friday the 13th! What a perfect day for a return... for my opponent however it will be a nightmare. I guess my time in SCCW has been taken kind of lightly. I displayed my more playful side and that has hurt my chances of doing anything meaningful with my time. I don’t do this because for the fun of it, I came out of retirement to pick up where I left off. Kicking people’s asses and taking their championship gold. My time working with some of the people from my past has not helped me reach that goal. Then this little distraction hasn’t helped my credibility either. So it appears that I have been booked to face a woman wrestler at our event in Indy. The press release for the booking actually says she is looking to bounce back and find that elusive first win in her SCCW career. I’ve got some news for Ms. Davis. Zakk Wylde will NOT be a footnote on the record books for you, I will NOT be a stepping stone for you to boost your career. I’m sure you are a capable enough competitor or you wouldn’t even be on the SCCW roster. But if the office thinks for one moment that I’m going to take your gender into consideration when I execute my plan on Friday night, they’ve got another thing coming! I am going to return to the ring with all the vengeance and furious anger that has been building inside me during my latest dealing with the legal system. Wylde has been looking around constantly while speaking, as if he was talking to each and every person individually. He pauses almost as if he recognizes one of the faces. He shakes it off and regains his composure. Wylde: In closing I want the management of SCCW and all the wrestlers to know that the Zakk Wylde you knew before is dead and gone. The Zakk Wylde that returns Friday will be coming back like he should have in the first place alone. The Black List is no more! I have had to take my former friend Gideon Fontaine to court and removed everything associated with him from my life. I was placed under a gag order from my team of lawyers to prevent anything from going wrong during the course of the proceedings. This was a very delicate piece of litigation and the smallest detail could be exploited and used against me. Fontaine enterprises have been separated from Wyld Child Enterprises and will go back to doing their thing and my company will do theirs. The merger that quietly happened just months ago has been dissolved and we will get back to doing what we do best. My company has come out of this venture with out a scratch, I can’t say the same about my former friends company. I have raided some of his best talent and left him with the bottom performers. I have taken some of his high profile clients and made them my own, leaving his head spinning. It will take years to recover from the short partnership that we had. So be advised Tina Davis, if I would do this to someone that was my friend. What do you think that I’m going to do to you at Temptation? Wylde pulls his Oakley’s from his jacket and puts them back on. Wylde: I’m sorry we ran out of time for questions. I’m sure you have several. Please forward all questions to my legal team they will sort through them and forward the appropriate ones that I can answer to me. Thank you and good day. As Wylde makes his way toward a waiting limo the reports shout out questions anyway. Not that he was paying any attention to them. He was too busy scanning the crowd looking for someone. He pauses for a moment to take one last look before entering the limo. Then adds. Wylde: Tell Indy I’m coming... And Hell is coming with me! With that he disappears into the privacy of his limo.
