It's a thin line between love and hate...

8 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/8/07 'Love can sometimes be magic. But magic can sometimes...just be an illusion.' –Javan "Do you remember, the first time you fell in love?" […And indeed, so it begins. We’re in an unfamiliar place for us, not so unfamiliar for our savior and his merry band of people whom want to destroy the wrestling industry. From the exterior shot we can see it’s late, as the small eight passenger school buses have their head lights on while driving through the toilet bowl of America, the mid-west. There is a caravan of buses, yet our camera focuses in on the one with only two occupants, the driver, we’ll call him Oswald, mainly because that’s his name, and the young man laying down in the handicapped section, sprawling across aisle, your savior and mine, Prince Pride.] [Prince waves] […Prince stares at the ceiling longingly, pondering such questions as the meaning of life, did man really land on the moon, and when will the DC public schools realize that eight of their short public school buses are missing? His thoughts digress, as Oswald has just asked the question many men don’t talk about…] "...Did you hear me boss, when was the first time you fell in love?" [Prince Pride, our savior, ponders that thought, his lion mane like hair pressed flatly against the handicap door exit] [Sighing] "I’ve only been in love once Oswald…" [Pride drifts off as he sits up and stares out of the window] Oswald: "Really sir? Sounds like she broke your heart…" [Pride stares into the twilight] "She did Ozzie, and I loved her, unconditionally, that love that has no limits, that love that would drive a man to do crazy things…" Oswald: "Crazy love sir?" " You could call it that, I’ve learned in my young life that there is all types of love, I guess being around here the last few weeks I’ve seen all of them." [Pride pauses] "But none of them compared to her…" [Pride sighs] "It started out like all classic love affairs, I chased after her, and she played hard to get, I gave her my number, she never called, I tried to get her number, she said she wasn’t ready, she’d seen guys like me, and knew my type. Little did she realize, there is no one like me…" Oswald: "But she eventually caved?" " Slowly but surely, but our love affair started more like a friendship, I could tell she really wasn’t into me, I had to prove to her what I was about, so after some time, spending night after night, and day after day together, she learned about me, and I learned about her, it was a match made in heaven, [Pride pauses] until…" Oswald: [Curious] "Until what?" [Bitterly] "Until she cheated on me! She said she had met someone new, she said she didn’t have time for me, she said I’d never amount to what she needed me to be, and what hurt the most, she said she never loved me…[Pride pauses] she just took from me, and took from me, but never gave me back anything but hurt and pain in return.." [Pride stands up and walks down the aisle as the bus is moving, I know it’s very dangerous, but Pride is a rebel, he breaks rules because he can, and because that’s what savior’s do. Pride awkwardly stares into the black box camera that is placed on the bus] "What’s good, Sterling & Colby?" [Pride moves his head side to side, like he’s studying an exhibit] "Love’s a bitch, isn’t it? I mean there’s all types of love, friendship love, family love, romantic passionate type of love, and there’s the love you two have for each other…" [Pause] "…The kind of love that is only recognized legally in Canada and sometimes in Massachusetts. [Sneer] Listen here Will and Grace, I could really care less what you two do to each other this week, I don’t care if one of you rolls out the others grandparents and defecates on their dentures, or if one tries to top the other by digging up old Nazi family members and making them celebrate Hanukkah, none of that matters to me. You two could kill each other with a pair of tweezers for all I really care, the only thing I really need you two to do is stay out of my way, because the first opportunity I can get I’m going to stab my partner in the back quicker than you can say, Peterpiperpickedapepper" Oswald: [Gushing] "That was my favorite limerick when…" [Pride glares at Oswald as if to say, you’re a background character, keep it quiet] "Paul Cain, you’ve been a living and breathing hemorrhoid in my hind parts since I entered this wretched place. Constantly reminding me of what my father was, constantly meddling in my business, which is why I took great pleasure in eliminating your sorry self from the Tower Match at War in Washington, that moment has been freeze framed in my mind, it’s orgasmic to me, if it was a woman I’d marry it, if it was a small child I’d name it junior, [Pride digresses] and I wouldn’t be so irresponsible as to let my small child be abused on national pay per view television while I look like a sniveling idiot…" [Pride stares at the camera and coughs] [Cough] "Maysoncolby" [Cough] "…[Sighing], Paul Cain, I loved that moment so much if it was porn I’d masturbate to it." [Awkward pause] "Because the reality is Paul Cain, is that I’m not my father, and I’m damn proud of the fact that I’m not some delusional, babbling, hypocritical, psychopath, hell bent on personal vindication and the humiliation of others. [Pride blinks] And here’s the saddest reality, you’re not my father either. And that eats at you, it festers in your gut that you had the greatest amount of respect for my father, but my guess is he gave two Michael Vick pit bulls about you." [Pride sneers] 'And if there is a number lower than zero that could tell how much I cared about you, I'd use it. You see, you’re a means to an end Paul Cain,a speed bump on the highway of life, a herpes sore on Derk Jeter transmitted to Jessica Alba, something that saddens me more than Sean Sterlings cheap heat tactics at the PPV... [Pride Pauses] '...I bitch slap kids for fun, I once gave my finisher to an autistic kid, now that's HEAT! What you did, [Pride scoffs], like taking lunch money.' [Pride stares and digresses] 'Listen Cain the end of our little issue will come this week, when Colby and Sterling dry hump each other out of the way, and afterwards I show you what it means to be a real athelete, what it means to be a savior, what it means to be Prince Pride, the man…That hates you!" [Pride smiles] Oswald: "So what happened to her boss?" [Pride stares at Oswald, the awkward look now gone, it’s as if he’s snapped into another daze, he slowly sits down onto the front row seat, gazing out the large front window] [Sighing] "She’s still around, I see her almost everyday" Oswald: [Quizically] "Really, do you communicate?" "Yes, she still tortutes me, just to see her, to smell her, to be around her, still eats at my core, still takes a small piece of me, still, makes me hate her…" Oswald: [Compassionately] "So what do you do, to, you know, stop the pain?" [Pride contemplates his answer for a moment, and then stares off into the night and answers matter of factly] "I plot every day to kill her" [Pregnant pause] "To end her, to make her feel the pain she made me feel, to leave her broken, to leave her bitter, to leave her destitute, to leave her…Dead!" [Pride sneers] "You see Oswald, wrestling, wrestling was my first love, and she in different shapes and forms has broken my heart, and it is my mission to crush her like she’s crushed me, and If it takes every piece of my being, every piece of my existence, I’ll do it…" [Oswald for the first time stares at Pride, with a look of concern. Pride stares off manically into the night] "…You could say Oswald, that wrestling left met at the alter, and it’ll be my pleasure to meet it again…" [Sinister smile] "…At the GRAVE!" [An awkward silence overtakes the bus, as Pride begins to carve a small heart into the back of the bus seat, and writes "FUSE" in the middle of it, and just as quickly as it’s written, he begins to scratch it out feverishly, until the seat is just torn shreds, satisfied he smiles, and we know that this, this love affair is doomed to not have a happy ending, and that this young man, our self professed savior, may just need some saving himself, but until then we fade… …To black]
