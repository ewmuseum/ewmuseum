Civilization #9: Final Confrontation

10 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/10/2010 "People don't want a hero, they want to eat cheeseburgers, play the lotto and watch television." -William Somerset, Se7en 03/11/2010 Late afternoon… After leaving the Civilization Main Facility to spill the beans to my investigative partner Juan Martinez about who is behind the murders on base, we came back to the building where Natasha Smirnoff called me to her office again for about the fiftieth time this week to send me on another dirty job for the organization. She gives me a ten minute tongue lashing over my recent attitude problems with certain people within the organization before sending me on a kill mission back in the United States. Upon leaving her office I head to the parking garage grumbling to myself about the whole situation. Once there I head over to my car which naturally is the only one there that is not black. There’s probably some irony in that but it honestly escapes me. Upon reaching my car and pulling out my keys to unlock the door, someone says something behind me. I have a sneaking suspicion as to whom it is. "Do you realize how hard it’s going to be for you to prove that we’re behind the murders?" Turning around, I find that I am now being confronted by the four members of the Roadkill Warriors. Slash, Blade, Savage, and Beast all glare at me in unison from their futuristic wasteland vehicles. The vehicles remind me of something out of a Mad Max movie. This only makes the tension in the air even more prominent. "You psychopaths have killed twelve people already," I say to them as they continue to look at me like they want to kill me. "What’s the next part of your plan?" "Kill more people," Savage snarls. "But why?" They all look at each other, collectively seeming to all having the same answer to this question. Finally they cackle like hyenas in unison as one of them finally answers me. "Because they’re not worthy to survive," Slash replies with an evil grin. "Only the strongest and fittest should be allowed to survive the coming end of days. Civilization should only have those most suited to survive." "You guys don’t see the big picture about this whole thing," I tell them as they continue to give me the look of death. "This organization is not only about having the fittest but also about having people with enough brains to keep things running when we take back over the surface of the planet. That is why there’s not just a bunch of douche bag jocks running around here. We need brains as well as brawn." None of the four psychopathic warriors seem to be deterred from their current plan of action by anything that I’m saying which only annoys me more. "You of all people should see that what we’re doing is right and just," Slash responds with an evil grin. "You have killed more people than anyone else within Civilization. We were hoping that you played it smart and joined us to kill the rest of the weaklings within the organization so that it is as strong as it can possibly be." I peer at all of them individually before finally responding to Slash’s inquiry with some questions of my own. "Who are you going to kill next? Someone like Dr. Feelgood who can help you out in the long run just because he’s in a wheelchair and, in your eyes, is weak?" This again draws no reaction except for a smile from Savage, who just happens to be the ugliest one of them all. His blue hair isn’t helping matters either. "Not a bad idea there, Genius Boy," Savage replies mockingly with a smile. "So, are you in?" Slash asks. Once again I peer at all of them in the eye just to see if I can read anything from their faces. None of them have any expression on their face that tells me that they are filled with something other than hate. "I think not," I reply to his question. They look at each other with disappointed looks before starting the ignition on their various vehicles. "We’ll see you around, von Doom," Slash says. The four of them drive out of the parking garage as I watch on. Slash is first on his motorcycle followed by Savage in a dune buggy, Blade in a modified Sedan, and finally Beast who is also on a motorcycle. "Not for long…" I turn and head back to my car. I have a plan for these guys. As soon as I get back from my little mission for Natasha these Roadkill Warriors are going six feet under if it’s the last thing I do. Only two days I have until then. Two days and then I finish this bullshit once and for all. * * * 04/13/2010 4 p.m. It’s been a wild month but I’m finally back at the Civilization Main Facility after a day and a half of doing some rather unsavory things followed by three days in Tokyo. I’m not back half a hour before I’m already implementing my plan. A day earlier I was at the SCCW Temptation Show when I had some of my Civilization goons capture Ash Plato, Rappin’ Rick Malloy, and a third man who turned out to be Slam Boomsdexter’s sidekick Joey Mason. However, unbeknownst to all but two people within Civilization, I had them kidnapped for a reason. In my head I have created an elaborate scheme to rid Civilization of the Roadkill Warriors. Now to tell the captives about what plans I have for them. Reaching the room where they are being held, I motion for the guards to stand aside which they do. I then reach over and open the door up before walking inside which draws an immediate reaction from the captives Ash, Rick, and Joey. "What the hell are you going to do with us, von Doom?" Ash snarls out from the chair he is sitting in. The other two are sitting on the bench on the far wall. I motion for them to knock it off the moment the door closes behind me. I know that they are frightened and have every right to be. However, for once, I’m not looking to kill, hurt, maim, or blow up any of these guys with explosives. They have nothing to worry about. "Boys, boys, calm down," I tell them with a smile. "There is a good reason for you being here." Ash looks at me with an angry yet frightened look. "And what reason is that?" he asks. I look into their eyes one at a time to peer inside of their empty souls. Once I get a grasp as to where I think each one of them is at psychologically I finally respond to his inquiry. "I need your help. As some of you may or may not know, there has been a series of murders within Civilization. Now some of the members of the organization think that some of you guys or your friends are the ones behind it. I think not. Now I had a run in with the killers who are behind the murders awhile back. They know that I know who they are and I know that they know I‘m onto their little scheme. That’s where you and your friends come in." They all look at me with incredulous looks like they have no idea what I’m talking about. "What do you mean our friends?" Malloy asks with an eyebrow raised. I knock on the door and open it up to tell the guards to bring the others from the cell next door into this cell before closing the door back. A few moments later the werewolf Wolfen, the female experiment Nebula, and the leader of the Exiles the mysterious Bishop are all brought into the room. They were captured by the Killer Pirates almost two months ago. As the door closes behind them, Ash looks to be in shock at the sight of his friends. "THE EXILES!" he exclaims at them. "Are you guys alright?!" Bishop nods his head. "Yeah, we’re okay," he replies to Ash. "Did they do anything to you?" Malloy asks him. "No they treated us fine," Bishop responds to them as the door closes behind them. I look around at all of them with a smile on my face. "Now that we’ve all been acquainted again, here’s what I had in mind," I tell them as they all look in my direction. "I’m going to allow you all to escape from here." "Wait," Malloy says with a puzzled look on his face. "You’re going to allow us to escape?" The rest of the group has the same reaction he has. Even my former minions that are in the room seem confused by this turn of events. Unbeknownst to them, I’ve been planning this since the beginning. "Yes," I reply to him. "The killers I am hunting down all work in this area of the facility in the security field. When you all escape, they’ll be vulnerable by your escape distraction and I’ll take them down. You should all be able to escape since there will be minimal resistance after that. If you don’t, it’ll be your own fault so don‘t name me for it. I’ve made it so that your escape will be a piece of cake. In fact, there’s a small plane outside the exit door of this sector. I’ve arranged for it to fly you all back to the states without any harassment on my end." Bishop and Ash seem to be the most disturbed by what I’ve just said. Everyone else seems to be excited that they are essentially being let go. "How can we trust you?" Bishop asks. "It’s either this or you all die," I tell them. "Which would you prefer?" They all look at each other with concerned looks before one of them finally speaks up. "I think we trust him," Wolfen snarls out. "Trust von Doom?!" Ash yells out in his direction. "Are you insane?" "Yes, trust me because if I wanted you dead you’d be dead already. Are any of you dead? No, I think not." Just then I hear loud knocking and yelling from outside the door in the hallway. "VON DOOM?!" the person yells. It’s probably a guard or a member of the Civilization security forces. "YEAH! WHAT IS IT?" I yell back, turning toward the door. Waiting for an answer, the door opens up and a security officer appears. He calls me over as I motion to the captives that I’ll be back in a moment. "Sir, Martinez was just attacked," he whispers to me. My eyes light up and I turn to the captives with a calming smile as to not give away that I’m about to go on a homicidal rampage if I see the Roadkill Warriors. "I have pressing matters to attend to," I say to them. "Expect what I told you to happen soon." Turning and quickly exiting the cell room, I follow the security officer down the hallway in the direction of the attack. My mind is racing wildly at this point. I won’t know how to react until I see how Juan is. If he’s dead, I’m going to drop on A-bomb on his attackers. * * * Moments earlier… Von Doom’s Investigative partner Juan Martinez walking toward the holding cells in his usual gray suit humming the tune to his favorite song. The hallway is empty due to a security shift change that is happening and this has him unnerved. However the fact that he hears a noise doesn’t make things any better. "Hello?" Juan asks only to get no answer. He turns around only to get stabbed in the stomach three times in rapid fire succession by Beast of the Roadkill Warriors. Beast smiles at him as he does it before pulling the knife out and shoving him to the ground before sneaking off as he hears the footsteps of a nearby patrol closing in on the scene. * * * Ten minutes later… After arriving at the scene of the crime which is fairly close to where the holding facilities are at, I see Juan’s body laying on the ground in a pool of blood. My mind is racing all over the place. I quickly walk over to him and try to see if he’s okay even though I’m frantic about the whole thing. Bending over, I try to see if he is able to respond to me. "JUAN! JUAN! WHAT HAPPENED?!" One of the guards strolls over to me to keep me from going off the deep end which I am very close to doing right now as a rage is slowly building up inside of me. "He hasn’t said anything about what happened," the guard tells me. I look at him for a moment before standing up above Juan’s body so I can talk to the guard. Looking down at Juan, I can only shake my head at this disturbing turn of events. "So what do we know?" I ask. "We know that he was attacked here only moments before we got here," the guard tells me. "Luckily the Roadkill Warriors were nearby when the attack took place." "Nearby, eh?" I peer around to act as inconspicuous as I can whereas in reality I know what’s really going on and who’s behind it. The Warriors went too far this time. Better play it cool so that I can go through with the plan the next chance I get. "Yeah, they’re right over there if you want to talk to them," he says, pointing out the four Warriors nearby as they are talking with some more guards. Looking in their direction, I see how they are interacting before two of them look in my general direction. I can tell that they’re trying to psyche me out but it’s clearly not working. I ignore them and turn back to the guard I was talking to. I also have an idea in my head about them using the shift changes on base to their advantage as part of their murders. "I’ll think about that," I tell him. Just then some guys in lab coats and masks come walking over to where we are at. "We’re here to take Juan to Dr. Feelgood’s laboratory to get medical treatment," one of them tells me as they quickly put a oxygen mask before loading him onto a stretcher that they brought with them. I continue to watch them as the Roadkill Warriors peer over with some of them smirking over Juan being strapped into the stretcher before they go back to what they were doing before. This is a telling sign about their involvement in this incident. I glare at them for a moment before turning to Juan. "I’ll be down to see you soon, Juan." Watching as they roll Juan’s stretcher toward Dr. Feelgood’s laboratory area, I can only wonder what the good doctor will do to save my friend. I know he’s putting the von Doom Boys through the Reaper program so maybe he’ll do the same thing for Juan. The whole situation has me at a point of near rage. Cracking my knuckles, I peer over my shoulder to the Roadkill Warriors for a moment before turning back toward the direction where Juan is being carted off. "Time to end this." Heading toward my room on base, I have to get a few things ready before I take these bastards down. They went too far and it’s time for them to pay for their crimes. * * * Twenty minutes later… After getting my weapons from my room on base, I am now ready to exact my revenge on the four Roadkill Warriors for what they did to my friend Juan. I head back to the holding cell and motion for the lone guard to come inside of the cell with me after he opens the door. He complies and once I’m in there, I look around at all of the captives in the room. None of them seem too surprised to see me unlike the first time when I stopped by an hour earlier. "I think it’s time you guys escaped," I say to them as all the captives who were sitting down stand up. The Exiles, Malloy, and Joey Mason all walk over toward me so they can hear exactly what I have to say. I don’t blame them. If I were in their position I’d want to hear what I’d have to say too. "We’ve got all of the information we need from you anyway. When you escape, I’ll get the Warriors attention and lure them into my trap." Ash can only shake his head once I say this. "But isn’t Desade the actual spider, James?" he asks me. This brings a smile to my face. A sarcastic comment like that only brings levity to the situation and takes away from the impending fear of death we’ll all be feeling very soon after the plan is put in motion. "I’m no spider, I’m just a coldblooded son of a bitch and these guys I’m after almost killed three of my friends including one just a few minutes ago. Now I’m going to kill them. Nobody fucks with my friends and gets away with it." I look around at all of their faces once again so that they know that I’m not fucking around about this issue. Judging by their expressions, they know that I’m telling the truth about everything. "I think we can all agree on that." That is one thing that I honestly believe that we can all agree on. I don’t like them and they don’t like me, but we have the same philosophy when it comes to our friends. It’s weird considering that I’m on the opposite side of the spectrum as far as morals go in comparison to them. "When the alarms go off, just head for that exit I told you about that‘s at the direction end of this hallway. Just keep running straight and eventually you‘ll run into it. There’ll be minimal resistance along the way. My guard here is going to help so that the escape looks realistic. He’s going to act like he’s knocked out while I ‘act’ like I’m trying to catch you guys. If the Roadkill Warriors come from their usual place on base, then we should have no problems. If they don’t, they’ll try to kill us all and the plan maybe fucked." "What happens if they kill us all?" Malloy asks. "Then they win and I don’t like to lose so that ain’t happening." I motion to the guard and he lies down by the door acting like he just got beat up as I motion for them to follow me. After I peer out the door to make sure that the coast is clear, the plan is now officially in motion. Time to kill me some Roadkill Warriors. As soon as the door opens completely I motion for them to run out ahead of me which they quickly do. I look over to the guard and whisper for him to pull the alarm in twenty seconds. He nods his head and I head out the door running after the captives that I just let escape. Soon after the alarms are going off. The plan is going perfectly at this point. As the group ahead of me reaches past the hallway where the Warriors are going to be coming from, I continue to lag behind while acting like I’m trying to catch them, which obviously I’m not. The Warriors appear behind us with guns blazing and start firing on them as well as me which doesn‘t surprise me at all since I‘m their actual intended target. Naturally, the moment I pull my gun out from the holster I have it in, one of the Warriors shoots my gun out of my hand. Without thinking I leap to the ground and grab the gun as they continue to fire on us. Grabbing the gun with my right hand while still on my back, I turn to the side to avoid their gunfire before turning back to them. I start firing on the Warriors as they try to doge out of the way. One of my shots grazes Savage in the arm, another one grazes Blade’s right leg, and one of them hits Beast in the face. Luckily in all the commotion two of the Warriors drop their guns on the ground to avoid my gunfire. Seeing my chance, I double tap his chest with more shots and shoot him once more in the face. I watch as he hits the ground with a thud. The Warriors suddenly stop running and surround Beast for a moment before turning their anger toward me instead of the escapees. In the background, I hear Malloy call to get Bishop’s attention. I turn around to see them continuing to run away from the Warriors. "I think he was telling the truth," Malloy mutters out as they briefly look back to see Beast’s body lying on the ground. "I reckon so," Bishop replies as they continue to run down the hallway. After they are out of sight, I turn back to the Warriors who are glaring at me. They suddenly charge in my direction as I turn and run toward the next hallway intersection. Making a right down the next hallway I haul ass toward the boiler rooms. Suddenly I hear the footsteps stop thumping on the ground behind me. It’s almost as if they are contemplating who to go after, either myself or the escapees. I keep running and it’s a good thing I did. A few moments later I hear Savage yell at the others to get me. "LET’S GO!" he screams out. The footsteps then start thumping on the ground again as they are running after me. This is perfect. The suckers fell for the trap. Now it’s time to end this once and for all. * * * Moments later… I lure them into the dark boiler room area of the facility with all four right on my heels. I don’t even look back to see where they are at. Honestly, that doesn’t matter to me. The stupid idiots are falling right into my trap. I cased this area out before I left for Tokyo so I know every nook and cranny. Using the dimly lit area and the shadows as my natural cover, I sneak off into the shadows before they can get a fix on my position. There is a lot of cover in this little area which makes it the perfect place on base to spring a trap. Hearing multiple footsteps behind me, they clearly are splitting up to hunt me down. At this point I pull my gun back out. Realizing that I’m low on ammunition I think that using their own tactics against them is the way to go so I brought a knife with me too. Pulling out the knife, I put the gun back in the holster where I had it before. "Come out, come out wherever you are," one of them says. By the sound of his voice, it’s probably Blade. I can hear him walking right to where I am hiding. As soon as he walks past me, I grab him from behind and cover his mouth with my hand before stabbing him five times in the kidneys just to make sure that he’s dead. The last thing I need is this bastard giving away my position. After I know he’s dead, I lay him down to the ground as quietly as possible before sneaking off in the other direction. Soon after doing this I see that I am directly behind Slash. He has his knife out and is peering around looking for me. I sneak up on him using the shadows and pipes in the room for cover. "Where are you?" he whispers as he looks around the room. I sneak up behind him with my knife in position to slice his throat. "Here." Just as he’s about to turn around I cover his mouth before slitting his throat with my knife. I then lay him on the ground quietly just like I did with Blade. Three down, one to go. These bastards may be violent and they may have a plan but they don’t have a brain amongst them. That concerns me since this plan was well thought out. "SLASH?! SLASH?!" Savage calls out but he gets no answer. I sneak around behind him as he grabs his rifle that he has strapped around his back. Looking around frantically, he starts yelling at me to try and lure me out of hiding. "YOU’RE FUCKING DEAD!" Savage screams out. Coming around him, I walk out into the open with my gun ready to fire and pointed right at his chest. "No, you are." He turns around after hearing my voice and fires a shot that misses badly as I shoot him with a double tap to the chest followed by a third shot to the head just for good measure. One his body falls to the ground with a thud, I finally breath a sigh of relief. After all this time the murder spree is finally over. The killers have been eliminated and beaten at their own game. Just as I smile and get my wits back together, I feel something cold that feels somewhat like steel pressed against my forehead. "DROP IT!" a man yells at me. After dropping my gun to the ground, I turn around and see that the person pointing the gun at me is none other than the blonde haired baby face known as Cedric Black. He’s the cousin of Hades Corporation CEO Raphael Black. I’ve only seen him a few times around base at meetings but never up close. He is wearing a black suit similar to the one Raphael always wears. At least now I know who the brains were behind this whole thing. Never would’ve suspected him. He did a good job at being inconspicuous and hiding behind his brother’s credentials. If it wasn’t for this moment I wouldn’t have had a clue that he’d even remotely had anything to do with all of this. "You’re behind this? Are you fucking kidding me?" "No, I am not kidding you," he says with a snarl. The fact that a little known Civilization member who I’d only seen briefly a few times around base is behind this greatly troubles me. "But why you?" I ask him. "You’re the last person I’d expect to have anything to do with this nonsense." He looks at me with an angry look. It’s obvious he thinks that I’m disrespecting him. "Maybe it’s because I’m sick of you and the people like you within the organization who think that you know it all," he growls at me. His whole answer to my question makes less sense than a quantum mechanics college textbook written in Braille. "People like me that know it all?" He nods his head as he cocks the trigger to his handgun. "Yeah, and now I’m going to use your own little rebellious attitude against you," he says with a devious grin on his face. "Who’ll believe your word against mine when you get into public arguments with Natasha Smirnoff on a regular basis. Your pathetic little investigation did nothing to save you from this fate. When you are eliminated, I will take your place and bring about the new philosophy of survival of the fittest to Civilization. The meek like yourself and your three pathetic friends that the Warriors almost killed will no longer survive within the organization. The weak shall be terminated. Your time is finally up. You’re done in Civilization, von Doom. DONE!" Just before he pulls the trigger I fall to the ground on purpose landing on my back and before he can react I pull out my concealed handgun that I had taped to my shin and quickly fire three shots. Two double tap his chest and one squarely hits the target in the middle of his forehead. Instantly he falls to the ground in a dead heap. I then stand up and brush myself off before putting the safety on my gun, uncocking it, and tucking it away into the waistband of my pants before looking down at Cedric‘s dead body. "You’re right," I tell Cedric as I walk over his corpse. "I am smarter than you." I walk over to retrieve my other handgun off the ground and put it away before finding the nearest intercom to get the clean up and security crews to this area. Just another dirty job for Civilization’s Dirty James. At least now the murder case is closed. * * * Two hours later… After dealing with the clean up from the shootout and the escape, Natasha Smirnoff calls me into her office once again. I’m expecting her to rip me a new asshole after that stunt I just pulled. She’s also probably going to ask me about how Cedric Black died. Once I reach her office the guards immediately let me in. This is definitely a high priority meeting so she probably let them know I was coming ahead of time. Once I walk inside the office, I see that she’s in a blue dress tonight and sitting at her desk as usual as I close the door behind me. I don’t even have time to turn around before she’s probing for information. "Is it taken care of?" she asks me. I finally turn around after closing the door. Natasha has a bit of a different look tonight for some reason. She doesn’t seem pissed at me like she usually is. I think she’s like that because I’m the only person in the organization that she cannot control. The office looks nice. It’s organized and all of the papers that usually clutter up her desk are gone. That’s probably why the HAARP Earthquake Machine Weapons System we’re setting up worldwide are working so well. However we didn’t count on a volcano erupting in Iceland being a side effect of this. The chaos the ash from the volcano is causing is worth it just for the disruptions to travel it is causing alone. I also notice two new plants in the back by the wall. "Yes, the killers are now all dead." She smiles after hearing this before standing up from her chair at the desk. "Good," she says. "And are the tracking devices in place on the Exiles and the others you let escape?" She pushes the chair in and walks to the side of the desk as I answer her. "Yes. That was all done a long time ago." This makes her smile once again. "That’s what I like about you, James," she says as she walks over to me. "You’re all business." For some reason this seems very odd to me. She’s in a skimpy dress and now has her hand on my chest with a seductive look on her face. She’s probably a reproductive alien like in Species or something to that effect. "Are you propositioning me?" She looks me up and down before answering. "Maybe," she replies. "What if I am?" I thought this bitch hated me. Never has anyone outside of the wrestling business, a business that is filled with limp dicked douche bags and ringrat whores by the way, hated me as much as she does. "No issue, there. Just wondering. I thought you hated me." Now she puts her arms around my next and starts looking into my eyes. "I do hate you, James," she says with a grin "No one has ever made me hate them as much as you make me hate you. It’s a turn on…" For once, I am speechless. I have no response to this. Never had a woman come onto me this blatantly before without having consumed a large amount of alcohol. Right now my theory is that she hates me so much she’s in love with me. I feel the same way about her. Maybe I’m secretly in love with her too. I try to respond to her but mumble out something incoherently. "Well, uh…" She then starts kissing me. I kiss her back. Our lips lock and there’s an explosion of passion in our embrace. It rushes through my body like electricity through a wire. When our lips finally part ways, it almost feels as if I am having an out of body experience. Finally I decide to let her know that I’m listening to her advice. "I’m shutting up now." That’s all I need to say as we start making out in a passionate embrace once again. Tonight is going much better than the rest of my day. Hopefully there will be many more nights of this to come. I deserve it after all of the things that went down during my murder investigation. At least I know that I am Natasha’s ‘chosen one.’ Hopefully I will get to be Adrienne St. Germain’s ‘Chosen One’ as well.
