Conflicted No More

5 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/5/07 "You know that when I hate you, it is because I love you to a point of passion that unhinges my soul." [The blue hue of a television being on in a dark room is what our eyes glimpse into. We’re inside the very small studio apartment of the enigmatic Prince Pride. Pride sits in a beaten up recliner chair, in his usual garb, athletic t-shirt, basketball shorts, and bare feet. His lion mane like hair held back by a Nike headband. Yet something is different… …This is not the usual smug and condescending second generation wrestler we normally see, instead he seems otherwise out of sorts. His eyes bloodshot, his body sulked and bent over as he stares at a picture, a picture we’ve seen before, a picture of his mother. Her ebony skin sparkles in the picture, despite the cracked glass. Her black hair flowing down to her shoulders, her smile illuminating through the darkness, most likely in her late 20’s, she sparkles as she stares down at a child she holds in her arms, a child one can assume is Prince. A small piece of water drips from the cracked glass, a lone tear that has escaped the face of the man holding the picture, Prince Pride himself…] "Help me momma, I just don’t know what do to" [His voice cracks, the tone of his voice tells the story of his heartbreaking] "Is this the right path? Is this the way for me to do this?" [He sniffles] "Those people, he, they, this business, they did it to you, they took you away from me…" [He clinches his fist] "He took you away from me! [Pause] And they cheered! They paid for it, to see it live, to watch it on TV, to see it all in person, then they went home to their families, but he didn’t, and you, you had to suffer for their joy, we had to suffer, and because of them, because of him, you’re gone, too soon, too soon…" [His voice trails for a moment as he sits erect and stares into the television that lights the room] "You took her from me, all in the name of protecting the business you loved." [Disdained] [Mimicking a voice] "Son, it’s for the business, the business has been so good to us, to me, I have to give back, when you’re older you’ll understand, maybe one day, you’ll protect the business, maybe one day son they’ll cheer you, it’s the greatest business in the world, I love it almost as much as I love you…" [Pause] "LIIIIIIIAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRR!!" [Prince screams as he lunges towards the television, pushing it over, it hits the ground with a thud but continues blaring that blue hue] "You loved it more, you loved THEM more, it was all about the fans, it was all about you! [Sneering] "Well I’m going to take care of the business all right, I’m going to make the business proud, I’m going to do something no one has ever done before, and I’m going to do it in the name of the Pride wrestling family. I’m going to do it for me, I’m going to do it for you [pause] Momma…" [Pride calms as he sulks back in his chair, staring at her picture and then staring at the television which is a warped tape of his father’s face, one of his fathers old promo’s from the long since defunct National Wrestling Council, TSW Region, in which his father had recently made a comeback but was doubted because of his age, and he slowly became to slip into a state of paranoia…] '[I'm paranoid] only because everyone's out to get me.' - Fox Mulder in Ruins by Kevin J. Anderson [Fade in to a cold Ohio morning, a light mist overcomes the shot as we open into an empty park. Nothing to special, benches, leaves, and a man-made pond. This pond is a special one, well not really but work with me, it’s the type of pond that people toss pennies in and make dreams, and hope upon hope that they come true. And today will be no different, as sitting on the edge of the pond, wearing a leather jacket, black slacks, black leather gloves and the ever present ‘aura’ of greatness, is none other than your TSW-Tri State Champion, you know this because he holds the TSW-Tri State Championship in his lap, he is the hardest working man in TSW, "The Foundation" Tony Pride. Pride stares into the pond, as it stares back at him, as he speaks in a trance like tone] I hope… [Pride’s voice drifts off, as the last words from his latest promo pop up again] I hope, that Jake Summers and Azaul have a lot more ammunition than Genie in a Bottle stories and Holiday Shopping, because if not, then all their whining and complaining about not being man enough to be the TSW-Tri State Champion will be for not. [Pride pauses] How pathetic are the both of you? Have taken a deep hard look at your selves and asked that question? You’re both so delusional, so ‘by the numbers’ it’s ridiculous. Nothing you say, or nothing that you do has any hint or originality or charisma, you’re just carbon copies of carbon copies, and the best you can do is bring up old and outdated stuff. This business isn’t about old and outdated stuff, this business is about the here and the now, this business is about whose on top of their game, now . Jake Summers, whine and cry all you want about what I supposedly did to the J-Crown, how I could kill the prestige of a previously worthless title, that was then won by a worthless woman and then won again by a worthless specimen like yourself, is beyond me. Shit, being placed upon female shit, being won by bullshit, does not a legacy of a championship make. [Pride pauses] And Azaul, have you been taking the same neurotic medications your brother has been on for the better part of his life? Your cryptic messages about Genie and about alliances with fellow inferior talent don’t worry me in the least. [Pride nervously chuckles] I’m the ‘Foundation’, I’m a former NWC World Champion, former two time NWC World Tag Team Champion, the end all and be all of this storied promotion, you name it, I’ve done it. And unlike you, I don’t sink to lows like listing off every single accomplishment I’ve done, to validate myself, I don’t need to do that, because I’m a former NWC World Champion, and I don’t need validation, no, no I don’t. [Pride pauses for a moment, not sure if he just contradicted himself or made a good point, but he smirks nevertheless] You see I’m the TSW Tri-State Champion, which plain and simply means you’re beneath me whether you like it or not. And why shouldn’t you like it? I’m a champion you can be proud of, a champion the people can be proud of, a hard working champion, I earned this title fair and square, and you can’t say I didn’t win it in the middle of the ring, because damnit I did. Sigurd handed me the title right in the middle of the ring, isn’t that what a referee normally does after you’ve won a title, well, isn’t it? [Pride’s tone is very erratic, he states the point and almost seems to be doing so to convince himself. Pride’s eyes dart back and forth, nervously, unsure] You’re all jealous, you’re all against me. I hear the whispers, I hear you plotting, to take away the belt I’ve worked weeks, days and damnit even hours for. Azaul and Summers, plotting to take my belt, and then I get thrown in with a partner who says I can trust him, but he's plotting too, I know he is, you all are, you all want what I have, legendary status, money, fame, prestige and most importantly the TSW-Tri State Title, that I shed blood, sweat and at least two tears for. [Prides breathing is unevenly paced, his words nervous, his tone, not the normal cocky tone, but one of paranoia, and some uncertainty] I’m not old, I’m not past my prime, you ask me to wrestle a sixty minute Iron Man Match, and I’d do it, I wouldn’t break a sweat, I’m the Foundation, I’m Tony Pride. But I’m no fool, I have to be prepared, I have to be ready. This NWC isn’t the NWC I helped build, this NWC is filled with un-honest, back-stabbing people, people who won’t work for anything, people who want everything handed to them, people who get by off name value alone. [Pride pauses, and looks at himself in the reflection of the pond and sneers at the reflection] You people make me sick! Azaul and Summers, you’re the epitome of what’s wrong with this place, and starting this week, I’m going to one by one start eliminating those problems. But I can’t do it alone, not because I don’t have the skill, no, no, that’s not it. It’s because, you’re all against me, you all want to take the TSW Tri- State Title away from me, and I can’t allow that to happen. I don’t need the title to validate me, I’d be a superstar, a legend without it, but I need it so you won’t forget me, so you won’t forget that I’m a legend that I’m a superstar, my legacy needs to live, you’re all trying to take my legacy, and I can’t allow that, I won’t allow that. The TSW-Tri State Title is my new child, and none of you will pry it out of my hands, none of you. [Uncomfortable silence] So I need an entourage, all the great ones have entourages, so I too shall have one. I don’t need to hide behind a mask this time, because everything is out in the open. This time, the only animosity is that in which you’ll all feel, when I have my revenge. Because like I said before, I shall finish this game. Because I’m the Foundation, I’m Tony Pride…And soon, very soon you’ll all learn how this game is to be played. [Pride tosses a penny goes into the pond, as the shallow water ripples and distorts Pride’s face in the water. Spreading it and dispersing it throughout the pool. As the water slowly returns back to it’s natural state of serenity, another face appears in it behind Pride’s shoulder. A lovely yet devilish face, ravenous black hair, fiery red highlights all wrapped in a scarf and a long black leather jacket. The face of business manager and the mastermind behind Animosity and The Dynasty, the woman known simply, as Sass. Sass places her gloved hand on Pride’s shoulder, and rubs his face with her other hand…] Sass: No more worries Tony, your insurance policy is here, and as of now, TSW will never, ever, be the same again. [Pride’s face for the first time is reassured, as he smirks at Sass and stares back into the pond] Sass: Lets get out of here, we have a small army to assemble, if we’re going to keep the TSW Title around your waist. [Pride glares at Sass as he stares into the pond] Tony Pride: [Nodding his head] Time to finish the game. GOOOOOODDDDDDDDDDDD GAAAAAAAWWWWWWDDDDDDDD!! [The water ripples from the sound waves of Pride’s voice, and his face again becomes distorted from the ripples. But this time, the reflection doesn’t come back, his face just remains distorted as he stares into the water waiting for it to return, but it never does. Pride stands as he and Sass slowly walk away, Pride again clutching the TSW Tri-State Championship belt. Pride once again glares into the title as the camera slowly fades but not before catching a bit of conversation between Sass and Pride] Sass: What’d you wish for Tony? [Pride pauses and just glares back into the belt] Tony Pride: Nothing… [Pride looks at Sass and then back at the title and nervously chuckles] Tony Pride: Nothing at all…I’m The Foundation,[nervous chuckle] I’m Tony Pride. [The question is, was that a statement, or a question? The saga continues…] "One cannot be betrayed if one has no people. " –Kobayashi from the Usual Suspects [Prince Pride glares trancelike at the television and then his eyes widen as he stands and walks towards the television] "I know now what I have to do…" [He stares at the static filled television] "I can’t save the wrestling industry, I can’t save FUSE, I probably can’t even save myself…" [Pride gives a deranged smile, one filled with contentment and worry] "…Which means I have to…" [Pride reaches down and turns off the television the room is filled with the same darkness that now fills this young man’s heart]
