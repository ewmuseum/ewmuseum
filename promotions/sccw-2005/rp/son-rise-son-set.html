Son Rise, Son Set!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Death, the most dreaded of all evils, is therefore of no concern to us; for while we exist death is not present, and when death is present we no longer exist." -Epicurus "…So, where was I at?" "Sister" "Oh yeah, her." We are back, as in back to where we were the last time we were here. When we last left our hero and your savior, he was entering the dark apartment on the unknown man whom he was approaching to kill his estranged and now newly reunited, slash, newly minted tag team partner. The man has a deep baritone voice, his face has not been seen, he seems to hide in the shadows, which makes sense if you’re a hired assassin. Yet we can see our hero, setting on a barely lit side of a kitchen nook table, the only light coming from the closed plantation shudders. A 8 X 11 glossy photo of Kansas Marie-Pride slams onto the barely lit table "I really don’t want her hurt, even though she can be a little annoying, she’s a sweet girl, I guess I’ve always kind of liked her, even if I didn’t know who she was." SUMMER 1997 "Prince, Prince, where are you baby?!" "I’m up in the attic Mom, I need some baby pictures of me and Dad, for my paper!" "Alright, hurry up, you’re Dad’s match is coming on at any second!" Sarcastically "Oh, joy!" The young Prince Pride, rummages through the attic, going through boxes and chest after chest, he accidentally knocks over a box marked, Princess . Prince rummages slowly through the box, and stumbles upon picture after picture… Picture of Tony, Elizabeth and a baby on a merry-go round. Picture of Tony, Elizabeth and a baby at McDonalds. Picture of Tony, Elizabeth and a small girl in a dress as she rides a bike. Prince rumbles down the steps and stands in front of the television and in front of his mother, Elizabeth, and throws the pictures on the floor. "Mom, who’s this with you and Dad?!" An awkward silence fills the room as Elizabeth’s eyes fill with tears, as she stares at her son, and he stares back, her face filled with remorse, his filling with anger and confusion "Mom, who is this?!" "Princey, it’s, it’s complicated, we were going to tell you, we, we, just couldn’t find the right time…" "Tell me what?!" The silence fills the room again, but the background of the television blares "Ladies and Gentlemen I bring to you, "The Foundation" Tony Pride, Tony Pride, welcome to the National Wrestling Council!" Silence broken "Prince, that’s your Dad, that’s me, and that’s Marie, she’s…" These moments in life happen in seconds, but seem to last an eternity "…Your older sister!" Prince stares at his Mom, and back at the pictures, she stares at him tears rolling down her face, and all that can be heard is the famous phrase "WHHHHHHAAAAATTTTT’SSS CAAAAAUUUUSIIIINNG ALLLLLL THIIIIIISSSSSSS!" NOW "They didn’t even think enough of me to tell me I had a fucking sister!" "Bitter?" "No, no I wasn’t bitter. Just little information like, hey, you’ve got a sibling, could have been useful before I was in my teens, but hey, who am I?!" Awkward silence A glossy of Jason Cruise is slapped over the one of Kansas Marie-Pride. "Him!?" Nervous chuckle "The guy my father loved like a son, I wonder what it’s like to be loved, like a son by Tony Pride, I sure as hell wouldn’t know…" MIAMI, FLORIDA 2002 "No! No! No! That’s not how it’s done, how many damn times do I have to tell you that?!" Panting breathe "It’s not like it’s a side headlock Dad, it’s a running hurricanrana, not many people…" "First of all, here, it’s not Dad, it’s Coach Pride, and I don’t care if it’s move number 1,004, ARM-BAR, you’re going to be in this damn ring until you do it right!" "You’re too hard on the kid, Tony." "Stay out of this Eugene, this is my student, and more importantly this is my son, and he’s not going to embarrass the Pride family name." Underneath breathe "You’re doing a fine job of that yourself…" "Did you say something?" "Nothing, , Coach Pride." "Good, Prince, cause I want to see it done right, Jason, hey, Cruise, get over here son, and show your training partner here how it’s done." NOW Prince’s face seethes in the dim light, almost as if he’s back in the moment. "I never was good enough, I never could do right, I could never please him, no matter what I did, no matter how many times I did it, I wasn’t good enough as a wrestler, and I wasn’t good enough as a son." Nervous tearful chuckle "But on National Television, in front of the whole world, I’ll prove to them all that I’m good enough, I’ll prove that I’m not a failure, I’ll prove to the world I’m not a loser, I’m not a joke, and you Mr. Osiris are going to help me do that." Prince slowly slides a briefcase underneath the table "This is everything, my life’s worth, all my earnings, it’s in your hands. I just need you to do your part. I’ll get them into position, and you take them out, live and on national television in front of the world, one bullet, two deaths, and I’ll be remembered forever." Prince nervously chuckles "The world will know that my Revelation is bigger and better than the biblical one, and more importantly after FUSE on ESPN, nobody will forget the name of their Savior…" A glossy of "The Foundation" Tony Pride with an "X" over his face floats onto the table "…Nobody will forget the name of Prince…" Another glossy floats next to the Tony Pride one, and it’s a glossy of the FUSE logo "…Prince Pride!" The camera slowly fades in on Prince’s hand as it grabs a pen and places a large "X" over the FUSE logo "Sunrise…Sunset!" And he leans back and all that can be seen is the shadows of the pictures, and we slowly fade
