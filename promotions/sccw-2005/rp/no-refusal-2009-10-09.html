No Refusal

9 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/09/09 OCTOBER 8TH, 2009 'I tell you what I'll do, Geoff. I'm gonna hook you up with a free shirt AND a picture, but you've got to do something for me first.' As much as it resembles some shitty Hallmark cliche, there are certain moments in life that some will never forget. For Geoff Miller, several of these moments managed to be crammed into a span of four hours here in the Tuscon Convention Center. If there was one thing he loved in this world: it was wrestling. It was one thing to be into the prime-time players, but Geoff could not get enough of it, even if he had to watch it behind the closed door of his room due to the risque language. This is how he'd stumbled on Sin City Championship Wrestling six months ago on Cinemax, a network that his naive parents thought of simply as a movie channel. Since that six months had passed, Geoff never missed a week of his favorites. The evidence of this could be seen on the faded out SCCW logo emblazoned on the shirt he wore at least once a week. Geoff smiled at Jared Sykes, the new Gateway champion and one of the aforementioned heroes of the boy. His mother stood next to him, a beaming older woman with blasts of gray streaked in her hair and heavy-set build concealed in white capris and an over-sized Arizona State Sun Devils T-shirt. Although she obviously wasn't versed in the cast of characters that surrounded the rotunda, the insurmountable joy she felt almost mirrored that of her son's. It had been months since she had seen him this enthusiastic, and due to his condition, high spirits were absolutely crucial to his survival. Jared crumpled up a piece of paper and stood up from his folding chair to make his way around the table. Kneeling next to Geoff, he patted him on the shoulder and handed the ball to the youngster. Pointing at the table about thirty feet away to the left, Jared acknowledged the man sitting behind it. His makeup just so happened to match the big green star painted over the kid's eye almost perfectly. Thad did not notice Sykes and Geoff looking at him, as Spacely currently wielded the 'magical marking tool' to sign a 'picturegraph' for a member of the Alien Nation -- a freckled redhead in jean shorts and Lance Marshall tee. 'You been over to see them yet?' Jared asked, still pointing at Thad and Quasar, the dog's paw being guided by a company PR into an inkwell and onto 8x10s of the duo for a custom autograph. Geoff shook his head in response. His mother answered vocally to the Black Sheep, however. 'Not yet,' she quipped. 'That was our next stop.' Jared smiled up and winked at the woman. 'I don't think they'll mind if we cut in line. After all, I know people, if you know what I mean.' OCTOBER 9TH, 2009 If there was one thing drilled into his head that he could have cared less about ever using again -- it was fashioning a Windsor knot. Thad hated ties, as his neck was a bit too thick for any breathing room in most Oxford shirts his size. It was a struggle just to fasten the top button, and once complete, he felt like he were locked in a horizontal pillory. Constriction was a pothead's worst nightmare. PLOP. The dripping barbecue sauce falling off the rib blended perfectly with the chartreuse accessory hanging off his neck. 'Dern,' Thad mumbled, careful about his language in front of the dinner companion sitting across from him. He was a bit more casually dressed for the evening than the blond kid, in a green Izod polo and a pair of straight-leg jeans. Granted, the wrestler known as Spacely stuck out like a hammer-smashed face in his business-like attire on this Friday night in Chili's. Though that were the case, it was something his mother had drilled into his head ever since he'd entered the work force: always dress to impress when you're with the boss. Nigel Kensington chuckled lightly as he observed the whole scene unfold before him, eagerly pulling napkins from the holder and offering them to his employee. Thad accepted them, but ended up surrendering the cleanup efforts and opted to remove the fashion noose from the loops of his collar. 'I suppose etiquette and Continental cuisine are two concepts not easily mixed,' Dr. Kensington quipped. Thad half-smiled, nodded goofily as he jerked the tie over his head. 'Ribs were probably a bad choice anyway.' 'Hm,' Nigel replied, putting a fist underneath his chin in contemplation. 'They certainly look appetizing. Would you mind terribly if I...' Thad did not wait for him to finish his request, instead moving his fork out of the way and edging the platter toward Nigel's direction. 'By all means.' As he watched his boss instinctively go for a fork and knife to cut off part of a slab, Thad could not help but grin at the staggering cultural differences. If this were Jot 'Em Down BBQ out on Macon Highway back home, and this polo-clad gentleman were seen using anything but his fingers to pull off the riblet bone, no doubt many a dirty look would be cast in his direction. Thad opted to keep his mouth shut in this particular arena, which was a strategy that had already taken him far in life. 'Magnificent,' Nigel beamed, dabbing the corners of his mouth with a napkin folded into a triangle. 'The sauce is what really sets the flavor off proper, don't you think?' 'Yes, sir,' Thad agreed. 'Coming from where I did, though, you get sorta picky about it. This place has it pretty right, I guess.' His superior nodded, clasping his hands together and putting them on the edge of the table in front of him. 'Ah, yes. Atlanta, if I remember correctly?' Thad made a so-so hand gesture, and his face crinkled with the 'eh' reaction. 'Close enough.' 'How are things 'close enough' to Atlanta, might I ask?' Thad, not necessarily amused by the accent itself, enjoyed the way that Dr. Kensington had phrased nearly every sentence into a question. This was a technique he'd used to bed a couple of women in his day. He didn't suspect that his boss was treating him to dinner and being excessively nice because he secretly wanted to fuck him, but the idea was kind of funny. Then again, with inter-office love affairs being all the rage in Sin City lately, one couldn't be too sure of anything. 'I couldn't tell you, honestly,' Thad replied. 'Haven't made it much of a priority in finding out, either.' Nigel tapped his hands twice on the wooden surface of the table. 'I assume then no one has attempted to keep you up-to-date, either. I must say that when I was your age, my mother would ramble on and on about every trivial matter in the neighborhood. I'm sure the solitude you have is quite pleasant.' 'Well,' Thad joked, 'I'm holding out hope for a birthday card with a twenty in it. My mom and pop don't have much use for a son who c'est la vie's to go work for a company who refers to their employees as 'sinners,' so I'm not exactly waiting patiently on a phone call anytime soon.' Nigel returned a glance, rather stoically, as he was not much for offering words of encouragement when it came to family issues. Thankfully, the conversation was interrupted by the curvaceous Venezuelan girl who had been taking care of the two that night. Her flare spoke volumes of her charm, or at least the charm she was faking over misery-laden breaths. With a pitcher of water in her left hand and a pitcher of cola-esque substance in her right, Maria leaned over her customers' table and smiled. 'Would you like a refill?' OCTOBER 8TH, 2009 His hands worked quickly, as the signature coupled with the little picture of a flying saucer had become second nature to him. As a matter of fact, he'd signed as Spacely so many times that he had accidentally endorsed a check as his character name on one occasion. It was just something that came with the territory. He soaked up the public adoration, being the attention-seeking only child he was. Thad looked up to smile at Marc, the twenty-something who'd requested an autograph, and offered his hand up to shake it. Before he could utter a you're welcome in response, however, the Ant-Sized Astronaut felt a thump on the side of his head like a tiny piece of hail had fallen from the ceiling of the convention center. 'YES! I knew you couldn't miss that big melon!' Thad, Marc, and everyone else in line turned to their left to see none other than Jared Sykes pumping his fist in the air in celebration. Next to him was a completely bald boy, appearing to be not much older than nine or ten, who had a tint of rose in his cheeks and a bashful smile plastered across his face. Thad looked down at the ground and saw what had hit him: nothing more than a crumpled up ball of paper. Immediately getting into game mode, Thad sprung from his chair and swung his silver cape over his shoulder, pacing towards the smug Sykes and the awe-stricken little Geoff. 'Quasar! Come! For an evil-doer awaits!' Snapping his fingers, the golden retriever immediately trotted over next to the now in-character Thad Denver. Posing with hands on his hips, he exaggerated body language as he eyed the two up and down, buying himself some time to improv. 'A-ha! It is my friendly who has misused the magical invibility underwears! And, who is this young man with fantastic SPAAAAAAAAAAAACE taste in face decoratorings?' Jared leaned down to Geoff's level while still maintaining eye contact with Spacely. 'This,' he said confidently, 'is Geoff. He came here to see you, but I convinced him it'd be a better idea to hit you in the head with something. Maybe jar a couple more screws loose!' Thad kneeled down and smiled at the boy. 'Hello, Geoff! Let us join fists to pummel this lying liary, as he has blamed you for the transgression!' Geoff laughed as he exchanged looks with both Jared and Thad. 'He's not lying,' the boy said meekly. 'I hit you with that paper.' Here was where the acting really came into play, as Thad wanted to make sure that he didn't overdo it and actually make the kid upset. 'AHHHHH! Betrayed by a fellow astronaut! Well, Geoff,' he hammed, 'you leave me no choice. For now, we must ARM WRESTLE for the ARM WRESTLING MEGATRON SUPERNOVA MILKY WAY CHAMPIONSHIPS!' It took the kid about two seconds to pin down the arm of his favorite professional wrestler. As a reward, the Black Sheep, the Outer Space Nutcase, the Canis Andromedus, and Geoff all gathered around for a very special photograph: one that no other fan alive would ever have. Thad embraced the kid tightly and then reached into his travel bag to slip him two tickets to the Temptation event on Saturday, promising him that he'd high-five him before his big match. Five minutes after this, he had excused himself to the restroom, carrying the travel bag along with him that had also contained his face paint. He would have to reapply it due to the tear streaks that had run down his cheeks. OCTOBER 9TH, 2009 'And what if I refuse?' Maria had left the two very quickly after dropping off the black folding case containing the bill for the meal, obviously sensing the intensity of the discussion. Nigel Kensington's tone had changed once the matter of business had arose. Now, instead of asking questions, he was demanding an answer. 'It's quite simple, Mr. Denver,' he said solemnly. 'If you refuse, then you are invited to pack your belongings now. I don't feel as though I am asking a huge favor of you.' Thad shook his head. 'I'm not that big of a fool, sir. Basically, you're offering me the potential to stick my nose into business that it doesn't belong in, or...' He stared down at the remainder of the contents of his glass, prodding at the lemon slice with his straw. 'Or,' he continued, 'you're firing me?' Nigel nodded. 'I, along with many, enjoy your presence within the company. You have become quite popular quite quickly, but let us not forget that you are replaceable. Many are just as willing to don a silly costume and subject themselves to ridicule for the opportunities that you have been given. That,' he said, a sternness in his voice, 'and most of these candidates will think before they engage in public intoxication.' Shit. It was fairly naive of Thad to think that word wouldn't get around somehow about the incident, but he never dreamed that it would be used as a bargaining chip for a move that Kensington had wanted to make. The favor did not seem like a big deal on the surface, but the implications that it carried weighed heavily on Thad's brain. However, unbeknownst to Dr. Kensington, he would have never actually said no to his request. He had just wanted to test the hypothetical situation. 'I guess my decision is obvious, sir,' Thad stammered, a smile slowly forming across his face. 'I'm in.' How the hell could he leave? He had made a promise that he very much intended to keep.
