Date: 9/6/08 Jan 15, 2008. 7:03 a.m. Caracas, Venezuela. My eyelids fluttered open, introducing me to a sensory overload of pain. Everything hurt. The already hot sun glared through my window and taunted my blood shot eyes. I cringed and closed my eyes again. I wanted to fall back to sleep, but I was relieved to be awake. Opening my eyes I groaned audibly. "Good morning sunshine." The voice was female and rang of a strong Spanish accent. I sat up slowly, my eyes catching sight of a beautiful woman in front of me. She was naked, kneeling on the floor, snorting lines off the hotel nightstand. Her name was… Maria, or Lisa, or maybe Angelina. Kicking my sheets off I realized that I was naked too. Turning my head away from the sun I snatched my silver aviators off of the night stand and slipped them on, blocking out the sun. Pushing the shades onto my face I saw my ring and index fingers and my thumb covered in blood. "What the fuck did I do?" "Do?", she asked, slipping a rolled Bolivar into her nose. "I have blood on my…", looking down I saw drops of blood that had dried onto my chest. Turning my head I saw my pillow soaked in shades of crimson. Maria, or Lisa, or maybe Angelina snorted a line. "What did…", I tried, but the words simply wouldn’t make it past my lips as I took notice of my hands shaking erratically. "Don’t worry, it was just a nosebleed.", she said, extending the rolled Bolivar to me. It took me a couple of attempts to accept the Bolivar with my shaking right hand, the hand not covered in my own blood. I rolled over onto my elbows and edged closer to the night stand. "What happened?", I asked, inserting the Bolivar into my right nostril. "You don’t remember?", she asked, a short laugh following the question. Placing the Bolivar against a crumbling kilo of cocaine I snorted with a sense of purpose, sending a rush straight to my brain. I snorted again, just as hard, closing my eyes too enjoy the burn of cocaine entering the nasal passage. "You did a lot of cocaine.", she said. I turned to hand her the Bolivar but she raised a hand to decline the offer and I dropped the Bolivar on the floor instead. She smiled at me, her face glowing, her dark brown eyes stained cocaine-binge red. "Are you okay?", she asked. I wanted to ask her for her name, because I honestly didn’t remember it. I wanted to know who this girl was and why she was being nice to me. Given the clues it was obvious that we had fucked, that we had done a lot of cocaine and that I had a nosebleed, but the rest of the night wasn’t coming together in my head. I nodded. "I think so, I just don’t remember much." "I think you overdosed.", she said without so much as batting an eye. "Overdosed?", I asked very quietly, my mouth suddenly dry. "A few hours ago you just closed your eyes, stopped talking, stopped breathing." I dropped from my elbows to my face, my cheek resting against the soft sheets on the bed, my mind racing. My body felt weak and I noticed a wave of extreme heat, my body temperature spiking on it’s own accord. "How am I even alive..?", I asked. "I saved your fucking life." I rolled over onto my back and sat up, exerting a great deal of effort in doing so. "Tell me everything.", I requested. "Start from the beginning." "You don’t remember anything from last night?", she asked. I shook my head. "Nothing at all." She bit her bottom lip seductively. "Do you remember fucking?" "No." "Do you remember my name?" I winced. "No." She pursed her lips in disappointment. "It’s Nina." "Nina?", I asked to confirm. She nodded. "Well… thank you, Nina." She stood up and promptly sat on the bed next to me. "You’re welcome.", she said, squeezing my hand. "But it isn’t a big deal, anyone would’ve done it." "Yeah? I don’t know about that. A lot of people would be happy to see me kill myself on some stupid bullshit like this…" "No.", she insisted. "Maybe in America, or maybe in Japan, but not here. Here you have no enemies." I shrugged. "Think what you will Nina, but the truth is that people are downright evil at their core. It doesn’t matter if they’re from the United States, or Japan, or even here in Venezuela." "That isn’t true.", she insisted. "People here care very much about their friends, their families, and even people they don’t even know. Love is still very much alive in the world." "Love?", I sneered. "Don’t even try to give me a spiel about love because love is a crock of shit. It’s all one big lie that started in the stupid ages in order for men to trick women into thinking that they cared about anything other than what was in their pants." "Is that all you care about?", she asked heatedly. I shrugged. "Yeah, it really is." Her brown eyes met my shaded bluish grays as she spoke. "I don’t believe that, not even for a second." I arched an eyebrow over my aviators. "All I care about is myself, no one else. All that anyone should ever cared about is themselves, just look out for number one." "How can you say that?", she asked. "Don’t you have a family that you love? Or friends? Or a wife?" "No.", I answered sliding off of the sheets and onto the floor, picking up the rolled Bolivar. "So…", she cooed, slipping down to the floor beside me. "…you aren’t married then?" I shook my head, jamming the Bolivar into my nose and leaning over the unwrapped kilo of cocaine. I snorted again, my brain flooding with cocaine. "I’m going to take a shower.", I announced as I dropped the Bolivar and stood up. "You’re wrong.", she said as I walked away. I didn’t turn to face her, continuing toward the bathroom. "I care…", she said softly as I closed the bathroom door behind me. As the door closed I turned to the mirror and pulled off my shades, sitting them on the side of the sink. My own blood had dried on my chest and face. Cocaine stuck to the dried blood on my nose and upper lip. Stepping into the shower I turned it on and stepped inside where I was immediately met by a stream of cold water. I grimaced and checked to see if I had turned on the hot water. I had, it just wasn’t coming out. I shivered underneath the icy blast of the cold water as I rubbed a palm sized bar of soap over my naked body, washing away the dried blood and cocaine. I watched the blood drip down my chest and stomach, pooling in the floor of the shower before spiraling down the drain in the center of the stall. I scrubbed myself physically clean, though my mind remained blurred with cocaine. I closed my eyes and swore that I could hear my heart beating violently against my chest, my temples throbbing painfully. I remained in the shower briefly, just long enough to wash my hair before I stepped out of the stall, naked and dripping. I wrapped a towel around my waist and consulted the mirror in order to confirm that my face was free of blood and cocaine. It was then that I noticed how red my eyes were and I put my shades back on before I left the bathroom. Stepping back into the main area of my hotel room I saw Nina sitting on the bed, her legs spread as she pleasured herself. Goddamn, this bitch was crazy… and I fucking love that. "I’ve been waiting for you…", she cooed without even looking up. The next 30 minutes were a blur. They started with a kiss and a few more lines and ended as I came inside of Nina, her legs wrapped around my waist, her hot kisses on my ears. "I love you.", she whispered as I came. Yeah… bitch is crazy. Jan 16, 2008 8:32 a.m. Caracas, Venezuela. The name of the restaurant was Amigas. It looked more modern than most of the restaurants that we had passed on our walk here. I said that I wasn’t hungry but Nina demanded that I eat. She refused to take me to her supplier until I had "replenished my health" as she put it. The food was good and the preparation was slow. I drank two cups of the best coffee I’ve ever had while I waited for my very ‘Americanized’ grand slam breakfast. Eggs, bacon and pancakes. Pancakes drowned in syrup. "How is your breakfast?", Nina asked, smiling at me over her fruit plate. "Good.", I answered in a half-word/half-grunt. "I thought you weren’t hungry?" "Huh?", I asked, not paying her my full attention as I focused on my plate. "You’re almost done.", she said, motioning to my plate. Looking down at my plate I saw a few brave strips of bacon and half a portion of scrambled eggs liberally doused in ketchup. The pancakes were gone, their only memory left in the puddle of syrup that oozed slowly across my plate, soaking my bacon. I shrugged. "I guess I was wrong." "Hey…", her voice was low. "Yeah?" "Did you hear me earlier?", she asked. I crammed a strip of syrup soaked bacon into my mouth, ignoring the question. "When I said…", she paused, sliding her right hand across the table to squeeze mine. "… that I love you?" I chewed my bacon slowly and then swallowed. "I think you’re confused.", I said softly. "Confused?", she asked. "Of course I’m confused, I’m in love!" "Love?", I sneered. "I told you that love is all a bunch of shit, and hell… you don’t even know me." Her reaction was surprising. A smile. "I think you’re the one confused.", she said. "I don’t need to know you, haven’t you ever heard of love at first sight? Plus… we have our entire lives to get to know one another." I shoved another strip of bacon into my mouth, chewed and swallowed. It was too early in the morning for this chick to be laying her love trip on me, and I was too coked out to follow anyway. "All our lives? Do you realize that I could’ve died last night?" "But you didn’t!", she spat out excitedly. "And I’m the reason that you didn’t die. I fucking kept you alive, so maybe you could show me a little gratitude." "Yeah, thanks.", I said as I snatched the last strip of bacon off of my plate and shoved it into my mouth. I chewed and then swallowed. Leaning over the table she lifted my free hand and kissed it softly, sitting it back down on the table as she shot a star struck smile in my direction. Nina was beautiful, and crazy, and crazy beautiful, but more crazy than anything. I guess this is what happens when you mix "business with pleasure", but hopefully I would be done with Nina soon enough. As soon as I had all of the cocaine that I had come for. Ignoring Nina’s doe-eyed gaze I turned the conversation to business, rather than pleasure. "Where are we going today?" "Barrio de Enero.", she answered. "Where?" She smiled. "It’s a famous neighborhood here in Caracas." I nodded, picking up a fork to finish off my eggs. "Actually, it’s quite the story.", Nina gushed excitedly. "For many years Barrio de Enero was ruled by the police, which can be brutal here. The police sold drugs, weapons and ammunition, they tortured and killed indiscriminately, most of the crime in the neighborhood was committed by the police." "The police are corrupt here too? Big surprise.", I sneered. "But now there are no police in Barrio de Enero.", she said with a smile. "No pigs?", I asked over raised eyebrows. "Six years ago the people raided the police stations of the barrio, there were three." "Three cop shops? How big is this place?" "The last census showed that over 630,000 people live in the neighborhood, which is about twenty square miles in all. When the people raided the stations they refused to give them up, they petitioned the mayor to remove the police force from the district." "And they did?", I asked. "The people converted the police stations. One is a community center, the other a radio station and the largest station was converted into homes for several families. The police are no longer allowed there. They only come now to arrest people and when they do come they’re supervised by a civil boss." "That’s the coolest story I’ve heard… probably ever.", I said, an excited smile creeping across my face. "So what now, does the barrio live in a state of anarchy? Anything goes? No laws?" She shook her head. "No, the neighborhood is ran by community councils, we have virtually no crime." "We?", I asked. She nodded. "I live in barrio de enero, so does my supplier." "Good.", I said, finishing off my eggs. "Then lets go." We left the restaurant, Nina slipping her hand into mine. We hailed a taxi and climbed inside, heading toward Barrio de enero and Nina’s supplier. Over my shoulder was a backpack stuffed full of banded bills, soon it would be empty, the money gone, my mission almost complete. Getting the cocaine wouldn’t be the problem. Getting the cocaine back to Japan would be. Jan 15, 2008. 9:19 a.m. Barrio de enero – Caracas, Venezuela We did a couple of lines in the taxi. The cabbie didn’t care as long as we shared. He told us that he drives better high. I didn’t believe him but he weaved his way across Caracas with ease, no small feat in a city of six million. We arrived in Barrio de enero, pulling up in front of an ominous twelve story apartment building. This was us. We tipped the cabbie with coke and stepped out onto a small and crowded street. Nina entered the apartment building where her supplier lived. She walked toward the stairs rather than the elevator. I followed in stride. "We’re on the third.", she said with a smile. "I hope you don’t mind the walk." "It’s fine. How much white do you think I can get from this guy?" She laughed slightly. "It depends on how much money you have. Definitely more than the little sample that I brought you last night." "Do you trust him?", I asked, my gut suddenly in a knot, the reality of my current mission finally beginning to sink in, my mind saturated in cocaine. The early morning heat wasn’t the reason that my hands were sweating. That was nerves. "Yes.", she answered without hesitation. "It’s the same shit that we did last night, isn’t it?" She stopped walking as we reached the second floor. She turned slowly, her gaze focused on mine. "About last night…", she started only to leave her sentence unfinished. I shot her a look of impatience. "What about it?" "Let’s keep what happened between you and I a secret, at least… at least from my supplier." "Why is it any of his business?" I sneered. "I don’t even remember fucking you last night, all I remember is this morning." "Look…" She bit her lip, looking down at the floor. "He’s not just my supplier, he’s… my husband." I arched an eyebrow high over my aviators. "Whaaat?", I asked as a devilish grin emerged on my face. "After all of that love shit? I guess I was right about love being one big Hallmark lie, eh?" "No!", she protested, grabbing my hands and leaning into me, her head on my shoulder. "I really do love you." I laughed. "How about your husband? Do you love him too?" "No, not anymore." She sighed, wrapping her arms around me. "I haven’t loved him since our Honeymoon… and now, I’ve found someone else that I do love." "Look, I don’t love you." I said bluntly. "Hell, I don’t even believe in love, I told you that." "I know, but you will. I can change your mind, I promise.", she answered, squeezing me tightly. In that moment I hoped a Venezuelan drug dealer wouldn’t step into his hallway to see a gringo hippie in the arms of his beautiful and unbalanced drug addict wife. Even the thought made me keenly aware of just how dangerous my current situation was and I pulled away from Nina. "Can you just get me my fucking drugs?" I snapped. She nodded. "Yes." "Good." I said, turning toward the third flight of stairs, Nina in tow. The sweat dripping down my brow was from the early morning heat, not nerves. I wiped the sweat from my palms on my wrinkled diesel jeans and began my journey to the third floor with a new found confidence. Nina was batshit crazy, head over heels in love with me and she was trusted by her husband / supplier. Nina could get me my drugs and help me get out of the country with my drugs. My mind raced. Use Nina. Play the love card.
