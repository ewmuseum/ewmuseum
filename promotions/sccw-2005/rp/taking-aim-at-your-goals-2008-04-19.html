Taking Aim at your Goals

19 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/04/2008 Those that had seen Dawn McFarlane in the ring could describe her as a graceful woman. She had taken to the air on many occasions, wowing the crowd with displays of agility and precision. Quick and flowing kicks added to that sense of grace under pressure IN the ring, something she had never quite lived up to out of it. Unfortunately for her, Florida heat is outside of the ring. The Canadian native was flaked out on the couch of the pool house she rented from tag team partner Ciel, and shared with his employee Saadra Mehta. The blinds had been drawn, casting most of the living room into a dimly lit gloom, and a pair of standing fans swept back and forth across the room in alternating patterns. A tank top and cotton shorts comprised the entirety of her attire for the day, selected more for comfort in what she has termed the "brain baking" heat of the afternoon, and less with style or grace in mind. Her milk white complexion had darkened somewhat to a creamier colour, but she had thus far been unwilling to risk the extreme sunburns her family was infamous for by venturing outside more then strictly necessary. And when you have access to a dozen channels of old, poorly dubbed, Wuxia (Woo-shia) and Kung-Fu movies, "strictly necessary" becomes a very forgiving set of circumstances. Add to that a fairly hefty training regime for her return to the ring, and we find the Steel Rose quite firmly rooted to her tag partners couch. She stirs at the sound of the front door opening, tilting her head back over the arm of the couch to try in vain for a glimpse of the new arrival before calling out, "Sandra?" "SAAdra." Comes the amused reply, bringing a smile to Dawn's face. Her relationship with one half of the Strong Style Angels had turned out far better then she'd expected it to, with both women settling into a comfortable routine only several days after finally meeting each other at the Cloud Nine facility. Saadra's indefatigable personality blended quite well with Dawn's sometimes caustic exterior, creating a relaxed familiarity instead of contrasting tension. "Aren't you working?" Dawn calls back, her head slumping back down to a more comfortable position. "Done for the rest of the day. My classes were early today, and there weren't as many as there normally are through the week. Something about the weekend makes people..." All six foot four inches of Saadra Mehta stepped into the living room, one eyebrow raised high as both corners of her mouth rose in tandem. "...lazy." "Hey, I'm not being lazy." Dawn fires back, waving one hand dismissively. "This is very...important...ah...research. Yeah, research." "Studying up on lines for an interview?" Came the amused reply. "YOU MUST DIE! I ALONE AM BEST!" "Well it'd be good for laughs if nothing else really." The conversation paused briefly as a particularly high pitched bout of screaming accompanied the furious dismantling of an old Chinese tea house, attached stables and the better part of about forty mooks of varying styles and states of hygiene. "We should go out." Rolling her head back around again, Dawn stares at Saadra with a suspicious narrow to her eyes. "Out...like, the big room with the blue ceiling? And what's this "we" foolery?" "Oh come on, it'll be fun, and it would be good for you." Striding across the room Saadra yanked the blinds open, drawing a protesting hiss from the figure on the couch...that utterly failed to stop her from yanking open every set of blinds until the room was bathed in mid-afternoon sunlight. "Now I see, that cheery demeanor is really just a cunning disguise for an inner, sadistic core." Dawn grumbles, shielding her eyes with her forearm. "That's me!" Grinning widely, the larger woman strikes a pseudo-anime pose for a moment before turning on her heel and heading to the kitchen. "You need to get dressed though, I don't think that's really outside attire with -your- skin. I'm gonna grab a banana and change myself, a little po-tass-ium to get me going." "The last thing -you- need is more fuel for that nuclear reactor you've got hidden away inside you." Comes the grumbled reply as Dawn eases off the couch into a tip-toe to ceiling stretch. "Grrrrrrragh...good stretch. Alright I'll be back out in a second." Black was normally Dawn's chosen "flavour", but she'd come to appreciate the colour less and less the longer she'd been living in Florida. The tank top was shucked in favor of a cotton bra and a white t-shirt. Cotton shorts gave way to olive green cargo pants (shorts not being in style for someone who sunburns at the drop of a hat) and sandals. Tucking her increasingly long blonde hair into a foxtail, she threads it through the back of a navy blue ball cap and settles a pair of sunglasses onto her nose. "Hey! What happened to "back out in a second?" Comes the call from the living room. "Bite me! You don't have to slather yourself in SPF six thousand every time you want to step out into the sun!" "Wonk wonk wonk, less talking more slathering." Grumbling nasty things about Florida natives in general and Saadra Mehta in particular, Dawn grabs the omnipresent bottle of sunscreen and proceeds to "slather" it over every centimeter (that's right, she's Canadian after all) of exposed skin. Still grumbling she pads out into the hallway, rounds the corner to the living room...and stops like she's been pole axed, one eyebrow raised in disbelief. Retro tie-dye had apparently been Saadra's shirt of choice for the outing, "complemented" by black sweat pants. Saadra's purposefully poor fashion choices were practically legendary around Cloud Nine, something Dawn had only recently begun to pick up on. "What, in the name of all that's holy, are you wearing?" Looking down at herself, Saadra tugs out the corners of her shirt to eye it before looking back up at her flat mate. "What? I like this shirt. It's comfortable, and colourful, and...comfortable." "Oi...with that in mind, maybe I should take a look at your footwear of choice?" The Steel Rose wasn't exactly a fashion guru herself, but in the face of such blatantly painful choices, something had to be done. Saadra gestured to the shoe rack and Dawn raised a hand to cover her face. "Orange Converses?" "Nothing better!" Comes the proud reply. "You don't even try to match do you? Hell, you purposefully try -not- to don't you?" Dawn pointed a semi-accusatory finger at Saadra, drawing out an elaborate shrug. "What does it matter? Only other chicks care what a girl's wearing and I'm not trying to attract them." "Mm..." Sometimes it was hard to remember not everyone sees the world the way you do. "You make an excellent point. Alright then, Converse up and lets get the hell out of here." ******************************************* "So, where are we going anyway?" Despite her earlier caution regarding sun burn, it was too nice a day for Dawn not to dangle one arm out the open window of Saadra's 08 Chevy Malibu, foxtail dancing behind her in the wind. Caution is one thing, not living life is something entirely different after all. "Sawgrass, it's about thirty minutes out from Ciel's place." "Sawgrass? Remember I'm not a native. To me that sounds like a combination wood-working and weed cultivating colony or something." Neither woman was looking at the other as they spoke. Saadra, as a conscientious driver, had her eyes on the road and vehicles ahead while Dawn lolled her head to one side to take in the passing countryside. Being less then sociable tended to keep the Steel Rose inside or at least contained within Ciel's backyard or Cloud 9 facility, so she hadn't had a chance to see most of Florida outside those few places. It wasn't that she wasn't paying attention to what Saadra was saying so much as she was an expert at diving her attention. Maybe it's a skill you learn when you're in a long-term, open relationship. (Yeah, I went there.) "It's the sixth largest mall in the country or something. It's about two million, four hundred thousand square feet or so. It's got tons of stuff, shopping, restaurants, arcades, movie theaters, you name it. Even Valet parking." She cast a smile over her shoulder at the incredulous look Dawn was throwing her way. "Two million, four hundred thousand? Valet parking? Good lord. And I thought having a mall with submarines was bad." It was Saadra's turn to look incredulous. "You have a mall with submarines?" "Yup. More submarines then the Canadian army in fact...man that's a sad little factoid." "Well at least your country knows where it's priorities are." Sticking her tongue out a little, Saadra flashed another charming grin. Rolling her eyes, Dawn shakes her head, affecting a vapid tone of voice. "Lyke, oh. my. God. Did you see what the Americans are wearing these days? Who wears digital camo with assault weapons? Who do they think they ARE?!" Both women lapse into chuckling for a moment (That's right, not every girl giggles) while Saadra concentrated on merging into the turnpike. "So...how did you and Ciel end up tagging together anyways? I thought there was supposed to be bad blood between you, and now you're living in his pool house." Casting her eyes sideways, Saadra watched her blonde companion for reactions to the question. For her part, Dawn sat mostly still, pursing her lips and staring moodily out the window at passing scenery. " Ciel's got this way of...making you admit stuff you need. Not pressuring or coaxing or forcing or anything like that, just like...argh, it's hard to explain." Grinning as Dawn raked one hand agitatedly through her hair, Saadra nodded. "I think I know what you mean. He's good at getting you to talk about things." "It's more then that." Dawn sighs, slumping down into her passenger chair. "He's good at getting me to say things that I would have never, in my right mind, said before. I just came down here to apologize, to try and clear the air between us after GCW...and ended up living with you on his property and doing my workouts at his facility." "So how did that turn into tag work? Did he just offer?" "No, that's the maddening part...I asked. We talked a little every day after I moved in with you, sometimes just passing pleasantries, sometimes more. He watched me train, talked to me about what I was trying to achieve, what I'd been working for, all of that. And eventually...he asked me if I would ever wrestle again." "Obviously you said yes...right?" "Actually I said no." Frowning, Dawn turns to look at Saadra. "You gotta understand, the whole time I was in the ring I only wanted one thing: to be like my Dad. I wanted to be the kind of wrestler he was, the kind of great he was...but I couldn't hack it. I had no confidence, no real drive to push myself." "So...why then? You've been wrestling for years, why do it if you had no drive?" Still frowning Dawn shrugs, resting her chin on one fist. "I don't know. If I knew that, then maybe I'd be closer to getting whatever it is I'm looking for down here. I just know I kept at it and kept at it...wasn't until GCW that I hit the end of my rope." "And he asked you the same thing I did right? Why you'd been wrestling for so long if you didn't have any real drive?" A light had gone on over Saadra's head over the course of the conversation. "Yeah. And I told him the same thing...I don't know. So then he asks me if I felt I left wrestling on my own terms...and I didn't. I felt like I was running away, still do. And then...I dunno, it was like a little floodgate opened. I asked him if he would tag with me if I went back, if he'd watch my back." "And he said yes." "Yeah...had to look into it to make sure it was feasible and all, but he said yes. Said if it'd help me find whatever it was I was looking for, then he'd help me. And...I feel pretty good about it so far, like some of the pressure is off." "How'd you end up in singles?" Sighing explosively, Dawn folded her arms under her breasts and scowls. "I don't know, it just kind of happened. Don't get me wrong, I don't have anything against Aimz...but I didn't think I was ready for solo play yet...never mind the main event." Looking sideways at her companion once more, Saadra raised one eyebrow. "You're afraid of the main event?" "I'm not afraid!" Came the snapped reply, followed by a pregnant pause. "Ah...sorry...I'm not afraid. The only way I'm gonna work through things is to get out and, well, work through it. If they think I'm ready for the main event in Sin City then I'm ready for the main event...I'll make sure of it." "It's not a grudge match or anything? No bad blood?" "Not that I know of. Aimz and I ran in pretty different circles for the most part. I know who she is and what she's done, I've watched a bunch of her matches and tried to make myself current on what she's done...but we've never really met before now. Aside from maybe a word in passing while backstage at a joint event or something." "Sounds to me like the perfect time to push your envelope. The stakes are big enough to make you want it, but it's not like your whole career rests on this one, pivotal, match either. You can go all out without worrying about your back...even if someone -was- gonna jump you, Ciel would be there anyway." Tilting her head, Dawn rolls her eyes to look at Saadra, a small smile on her face. "When'd you get so wise anyway?" "Comes of having Tohya Miho for a tag partner, she's full of this kind of thing. You can't turn a corner without a little snippet of Zen-like wisdom popping up. It's sort of cute." Another flashed smile. Smiling encouragingly at her flat mate, Saadra turns back to the road as Dawn turns back to the window. Wisps of blonde hair float past Dawn's face as she gazes out past the other cars, green eyes distant. "I -do- want this." She murmurs. "I -am- ready."
