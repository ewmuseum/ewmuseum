Uncomfortable Conversations

21 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-21-08 He didn't make a sound, though the steady rhythmic beat of his heart echoed in his ears and reminded him that he needed to take another one of his painkillers. 'Hrmph,' he grunted as he struggled to use the little bit of strength he had available to pop off the top of the medicine bottle. His body ached, and while he knew the bruises and scrapes would heal themselves over time, he also knew that the damage he had sustained from the fall off of the FUSEWire wasn't something easily ignored. As he tossed a few more capsules into his mouth and follows it up with a large gulp of water from the bottle in his other hand, images of the last UpRoar flashed in front of his eyes like an old picture show from the Wild West. Each passing moment captured in a mental image that he knew he would never be rid of. The slide show continued to play over and over again in his mind, but the click clack of Julissa's heels could not be ignored. She was approaching him, and as much as he wished he didn't have to listen to the sweet siren call of her voice, there was no way he could ignore it. 'Come on, I've seen five year olds get up and dust themselves off after falls like that,' she teased, a playful jab that could have brought a million men to their knees due to the mere fact that it escaped through Satin Sheet Red tinted lips. 'You know, you're not going to be in any shape for your match if you keep moping around the house like this. What you need to do is get off your butt, throw on some sweat pants and go run a mile or two.' He said nothing, and her words seemed to remind him of the old teacher from the Peanuts cartoons, 'Wah wah wah, wah waaaah, wa wah.' 'Are you listening to me?' she asked, her hand on her hip and he eyebrow cocked up on one side; most people recognize that look almost immediately. 'No,' he replied without lifting his head to stare up at her. Every movement seemed to come with a burst of pain at no extra charge, but he wasn't in the mood for anything other than rest. 'You're not still mad at me are you?' She jutted out her lower lip, pouting slightly with little puppy dog eyes that seemed to glow under the dim lighting. 'You're using me,' was his answer to her. 'I would hardly say that,' she said with a laugh. 'All I did was tell you what you needed to do in order make yourself known. Mayhem clearly considers your win a fluke, and if he could care less about you... No one else will consider you more than a fly they can easily brush off their shoulder.' 'So getting my ass kicked is somehow going to make me more threatening to a roster full of guys with arms the size of my waist?' His question was accompanied by the lift of his head as he rolled his eyes and he wanted her to see him. 'Yeah, why didn't I think of that before.' 'Quit your complaining,' she snapped. 'Do you want to be jerking the curtain against guys like Andre Jensen every week? Trust me, that's not going to do anything but hurt you more than you'd ever like me to.' 'He nearly killed me, Juli...' His voice trailed off a bit, as he swallowed back his pride. But that's probably what you really want isn't it. If I'm not here there's nothing or no one that can keep you from your father.' 'Trust me, Jay,' her tone is bubbly when she speaks. 'I don't need to do anything to you to get to my father. My reasons for managing you are strictly business related, I can assure you of that.' 'Yeah, somehow I find that hard to believe.' 'I only want the best for you.' 'Excuse me while I go and check if Ed McMahon is at my door with a million dollars.' The sarcasm wasn't lost on the femme fatale, who cracked a thin smile before she responded. 'While you're up, see if you can find the shadow of your former self.' 'This isn't going to work, Juli,' Bridden said as he struggled to stand. 'Especially not with you thinking you're going to stay here.' 'Oh come on, Jay,' she said coyly as she stepped forward and ran the back of her hand against his slightly burpled cheek. 'You always asked me to spend the night when we were kids, and now you want to get shy. Trust me, you won't be disappointed. I just went shopping at Victoria's Secret, you wouldn't believe the sales they had going on.' 'I need to get out of here.' Jadian shuffled to the door to a different room and pushed his way through, then let it slam behind him as Julissa just stood there and smiled mischiviously. Benito Minorez stood there and sifted through the days mail, the sounds of weights clanking, bodies smacking canvas, and flesh hitting leather filled the air of the gym. Except for the incident between Victor Tynes and Marquis Geffer, things had been pretty quiet... Things tend to change though, and the ringing of the bell above the door caught Benny's attention. 'Been a long time, Benny.' Stood there with one of the biggest chit eating grins he could muster was Miguel Minorez, Mike Minor to those that would remember. Benny's brother and longtime tag team partner was dressed to the tee in a white pinstriped suit, the red collared shirt buttoned up to let the white tie stand out amongst the cardinal silk. 'Not long enough,' Benny replied, as his lips slowly curled into a sneer. 'Get the hell out of my gym.' 'That's no way to talk to family, Benito,' Mikey said, the smile on his face never faded. 'You're no family of mine,' Benny snapped. 'You're nothing but an embarressment that we can't forget.' 'I guess I have you to thank for that then, que no?' 'Something wrong, Benny?' Fernando Hernandez grew up around the corner from the Minorez family, but at first glance he did not recognize Mikey. 'Still a little punk, eh Nando?' 'Mikey?' realization set in, and Fernando was as puzzled as Benny is to see the other Minor brother. 'You never were very quick,' Mikey said. 'Must be why you're still mopping the floor while your buddy Jadian gets his ass kicked on a weekly basis... Still trying to figure out which one of you is better off.' Fernando began to move forward, but Benito halted him in his tracks with a wave of his hand. 'He's not worth it.' 'Always the cool headed one, that's what everyone use to say,' Mikey took a few steps forward, his head slowly moving from side to side as he took in the place. 'Of course, you pretty much chose to avoid all forms of conflict.' 'I've been civil up to now,' the elder Minorez brother interrupted. 'But if you don't tell me what you want, or turn around and get out, civil might be the one to go out that door.' 'I'm here for you, hermano,' Mikey responded. 'You see, unlike you, I realize the importance of familia... All my familia.' Unsure of what his brother was talking about, Benny furrowed his brow. 'Like my niece, for instance.' 'Julissa,' Benny thought immediately, while his face gave away his surprise. 'You ready to talk now?' the younger brother asked with a grin, as Benny motioned toward the office in the back. Seems they had something to talk about after all.
