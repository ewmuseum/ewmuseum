Round One

Date: March 13 'OOOOOOH!' Several members of the front row gasped as Seymour Almasy's knee slammed into the side of Phillip Kennedy' skull, sending the man known to Sin City as the Big Stack tumbling through the ropes to the lightly padded concrete floor. His body made a loud 'smack!' as it hit, and once again, Phillip was made acutely aware of the occupational hazards of his job. He hadn't intended on even being on camera. For all intents and purposes, he was still recovering from the hellacious beating Desade had laid upon him. All things considered, Phillip thought, Almasy had gotten off pretty easy, considering he'd been two on one for half the contest. As Phillip watched the Protagonist slink back up the aisle, it wasn't hard to understand why. He hadn't made any statements against the Hand. Sure, Kathryn had told him, Seymour had a history as a goody-goody...but he was also showing no inclination to have a dog in this particular fight. For all of Dusk's flowery prose and vitrolic rhetoric, he was a man on an island...as was being proven in the ring at this very moment. Kennedy watched the action in the ring, blinking his eyes a bit to try and clear the cobwebs. Reaching up, he felt his forehead. His fingers came down clean; the pristine white bandage covering his stitches was still in place. Perfect. In a couple of days, he could get them taken out. At that point, he'd be mostly ready for action, just sore for quite a while more. Phillip's attention was captured by the voice of his leader. Desade's voice was a frightening thing, he thought, considering its mix of calmness and inquisition. The Hell was she saying, though? Maybe the RPG bastard had kneed him in the head harder than Phil had thought. One hundred thousand dollars to the man who eliminated Dusk from the tournament (via fair means or foul, undoubtedly, knowing the Director). Kennedy's head was spinning a bit, but not so much that he didn't remember that beating the silly Wombat would give Kennedy a second round date with Dusk...provided, of course, that Dusk beat some guy Phillip had never heard of. There was also either the Illustrious Face Eater or Ash Plato to deal with, but minor details. 100K smackers was a solid motivation for sure, but somehow, it was what the duo known as DNA said next that got Kennedy's attention a bit more successfully. 'And to anyone else who thinks they will unseat the Queen of Sin?' Desade had said, the tone of her voice clearly indicating that she believed such a thing to be impossible. 'Let them try,' Amy Campbell had said, knowing full well that she had the backing of the Dead Man's Hand, and that such a pronouncement was quite...grandiose. It was particularly grandiose to Kennedy, looking on, especially because in his mind, the bitch wouldn't have lasted a month with the title without Desade and the Hand. Kennedy's mouth moved, though Pierce and Campbell were busy glorying in the fact that Dusk was down on the canvas. 'You can bet that one hundred grand I will,' Kennedy said, silently, 'and that I will succeed.' -------------------------------- Malls weren't exactly Phillip Kennedy's most common hangout. He was, after all, not an eighteen year old with no better place to go. In spite of that fact, food courts were always among Kennedy's favorite places to eat. They usually contained good quantities of items from the Big Stack's favorite food group: fried. On this day, though, he was eating relatively better: a Philly cheesesteak (oh the irony) and a large root beer. Looking around the crowded mall, it didn't take long for him to find his companion. When you happened to be six foot nine, even in a giant crowd, it wasn't hard to be spotted. Nonetheless, Phillip Kennedy's agent raised a mammoth arm in the air upon seeing the Big Stack. Kennedy placed his tray upon the table at which the former PRIME star sat, and the two men shook hands. 'Long time no see, big man,' Phillip said, smiling at the man responsible for maintaining what was rapidly becoming a modest empire for the DMH member. 'Indeed,' David replied. 'It is good to see you well. Though I must admit to having a question for you.' 'You almost always do,' Kennedy grinned. 'That's why I pay you, after all.' 'I was curious, Mr. Kennedy,' the voice of David Walter Smith began, in its usual slightly amused monotone, 'if you were aiming for Dusk with your lariat, or if you were aiming for the Universal Champion?' Phillip sighed by way of initial response. It had been some time since he had met face to face with his agent. For the most part, the man once known as Logic had been behind the scenes, according to Kennedy's wishes. After all, any attempts to stand on his own two feet otherwise would have been pooh-poohed by Desade as the massive agent's influence. 'Who do you think I was aiming for,' Kennedy finally responded. Smith pondered the question for a few moments. Phillip noted that, in the months that had passed since their last face to face meeting, the big man had ditched the wheelchair. There were no signs of crutches, either. Best Phil could tell, David Walter Smith was 100%...or as close to 100% as a pro wrestler could be. 'I believe that you were aiming for Mr. Maloof,' the once Logic said, and Phillip could have sworn he *saw* the man's mind working. 'After all, he said several inflammatory things about you earlier in the evening, and he did send your girlfriend careening into the ringside barrier.' Both of those were good reasons, really. 'Besides which,' David continued, 'you and Alexandra seem to be on somewhat better terms after your contest.' Phillip couldn't quite tell if the look on David's face was reproachful or not. 'Well, you've got me on all charges,' Kennedy stated, holding his hands up in mocking surrender.'But while we're on the subject of that, how about some credit, Mr. 'You're Just A Puppet For Desade'?' The Thinking Man's Grappler allowed himself a small smile. 'I will admit, Phillip,' he replied, 'that I did not expect you to call out Ms. Pierce in the middle of the ring. Nor did I expect you to take her to a time limit draw. But having seen those two things happen...I must express puzzlement as to why you remain with Desade's gang.' 'You would.' The Big Stack chuckled, taking a sizeable chomp of his cheesesteak, and following it down with a healthy swing of root beer. He finished swallowing before speaking. Politeness wasn't one of his big strong suits, but when dealing with David Walter Smith, clarity was of the utmost importance. 'Shit, Dave, my goal's not to be Alexandra Pierce, nor is it to be the guy that takes Pierce down. My goal is to be SCCW Universal Champion, and more specifically, it's to be the guy that Alex acknowledges as the best wrestler in the Hand. The go-to guy. Number one. The wrestler who, if that genius Kensington ever figures out a way to ban her and the rest of the gang from ringside, is the one she trusts to wave the banner and get the job done.' 'That is unlikely so long as Amy Campbell exists. Surely you are not so...naive as to believe that Campbell losing the title would promote you to such a lofty position?' 'You're one Hell of a killjoy, you know that?' Of course, the agent was right, to an extent. It was obvious even to neutral observers that Amy Campbell and Alexandra Pierce were close in a way that Kennedy could never hope to be with the Director. 'I'll never be HER number one,' Kennedy explained, 'but there may come a time where I'm the Hand's number one, if only because I prove that I have to be. She and I may not ever be best of friends. We may only ever be partners, but there will come a day where as the leader of the Dead Man's Hand, she knows who the best competitor is. Considering her propensity for decision-making, once it's proven I'm better than Campbell is, if the chips are down...' 'She may still select Campbell,' David replied. 'Pierce is a woman that operates on many logical principles, but loyalty and trust seem to be two of her most stringent criteria. She obviously trusts Campbell far more than you. What her reasoning is, I am unsure. You may, in fact, be a better choice, but her loyalty would lead her to Campbell nonetheless, her justification being that she is more confident that Amy would not betray her.' Kennedy sighed, and took another sip of his beverage. 'Which is what I don't get to begin with. I've been on board from day one. I've done everything asked of me, and I'm still a second-class citizen.' David sat stone-faced for a moment, pondering the entire situation. At 6'9', 299 lbs, he was a life-sized version of the Thinker, but his muscles were carved out of flesh and bone rather than marble. 'Figuring out Alexandra Pierce is a task that even I am not suited for. Especially as of late. Her actions are less and less predictable. While it would be easy to assume that this is simply due to her changing her tactics to confuse her enemies...I do not believe that this is the case.' By now, Kennedy's cheesesteak had been nibbled down to practically nothing. He popped the final bite into his mouth, chewing and swallowing contentedly, though he was anything but. 'You're telling me something by not telling me something,' Kennedy groused. 'Lauren, Alex, and Kat do that all the time, and it drives me up a tree.' 'What I am saying, Mister Kennedy,' Logic stated, pausing a bit to let the suspense hang in the air, 'is that Pierce's decision is not her usual calm, well-reasoned one. Most of her risks are extremely calculated. Amy Campbell is a risk. I know things about Amy that you do not, simply because you have not followed her career. Regardless of this, Pierce's decisions regarding Aimz are...emotional. And before you ask, I do not know why.' 'Wonderful.' Kennedy finished his root beer, and sat silently in his seat. Alexandra Pierce was proving to be many things. His leader, certainly. A source of annoyance. A source of confusion. If Phillip only knew the half of what Pierce was, one might question his loyalty... ------- Phillip Kennedy sat cross-legged on his hotel room's king sized bed. In front of him was his laptop, his most faithful companion. It was his window to his past world, the only way he generally had to play poker when he was on the road. Tonight, though, that computer wasn't even turned on. It hadn't been turned on for the purposes of poker since a week before he stepped into the ring with Alexandra Pierce. Sure, it had been used. It had been used for the usual purposes, checking email and CNN.com. It had also been used for scouting purposes, looking up Youtube videos of Ms. Pierce in the ring, and now Austin King in preparation for the first round of the No Limit tournament. There were MS Word documents containing possible strategies, and Kennedy's personal analyses of those whom he was to step into the ring with. To his own shock, Phillip was finding professional wrestling to be similar to, but infinitely more exciting, than poker. In the card game, you risked money, but in professional wrestling, your body, reputation, and livelihood were all on the line. Like in poker, you had to predict the actions of your opponent. The mental games that poker thrived on of prediction and bluffing were still there, but wrestling provided an additional physical component. The only time that he'd ever woken up in physical pain after a poker game was the time he and his buddies had spent a night on the town before returning to the tables at 3 AM. That hangover, however, couldn't compare to spending a night in the hospital and needing a blood transfusion as a result of the fight of his life. Thirty minutes with Alexandra Pierce hadn't broken the Big Stack's spirit. If anything, it had only stoked his competitive fires. As hurt as he'd been afterwards, it had been the thing he needed most going into No Limit, another piece of proof that he was, at a year into the business, one of the best Sin City had to offer. He was the SCCW Gateway Champion. The four seed in No Limit. One of the favorites. As far as the Stack was concerned, he would live up to that billing. Phillip heard the door to the hotel room open, and he couldn't help but smile. There was, of course, only one other person who had the key. 'Hello, Philly-bear!' As expected, Kennedy turned his head to see none other than the Sex Kitten herself, Kathryn Shaw. Particularly promising for him on this day was the fact that, amongst the various bags she carried was one labeled quite clearly 'Victoria's Secret.' 'How was your day,' Phillip queried, though he judged by the sheer quantity of bags that she was holding onto that it had been, if nothing else, productive. 'Shopirific,' she replied, before placing all but the Victoria's Secret bag down in the closet space of the hotel room. 'I'll be out in a second,' she continued, stepping into the room's bathroom. 'I've got a surprise for you. Consider it a good-luck present.' Kennedy heard the door close behind her, and he couldn't help but smile. Their relationship was an...interesting one, that was for sure. She was nearly always busy, as was he, for that matter. Still, they always made time for one another outside of the shows. He liked watching Temptation with her, but for some reason, she always seemed more intent on taking the show's name literally than actually watching it. Which was a problem for Kennedy if he actually wanted to watch the show, because when it came to Temptation, there were few better than the Sex Kitten. Phillip cared for her deeply, though he did have to admit that her various 'charms' were a chunk of the reason why. Practically, travelling with a beautiful, unihibited woman had its benefits; Phillip didn't have to go the ring rat route, or spend excessive time in clubs trying to find a piece of ass based off his celebrity. Kathryn was his girlfriend. They were dating -- at least as much as one could date someone as busy as she was, he figured. She was also valuable to him professionally -- she was one of his two managers, the sugar to Savant's spice. Katsidy had a body that could stop the crisis in the Middle East, which gave him an advantage over almost any competitor. Any referee too, for that matter; it certainly didn't SEEM like Hamill got laid considering how much he gawked over Katsidy on occassion. As beautiful as she was, and important as she was, Kennedy didn't want her to be a crutch. That was why he'd gone to war with Sean Sterling in a warehouse in the South Bronx. That was why he'd gone to war with Alexandra Pierce for thirty minutes in a knock-down, drag out brawl. To prove to himself that he could handle anything Sin City could throw at him, without his allies, in case they were ever taken from him. You mean, the quiet voice in the back of the Stack's head reminded him, if Alex takes them from you. Kennedy feared Alexandra Pierce. It was a rational fear, really, considering the Director's track record. He wasn't as close with her as any of the other members of the Hand were...save maybe Bridden and Minorez, but they didn't do much anyway. This in spite of the fact that he was the Gateway Champion and arguably, the top talent in the company. But if he pushed too hard, or too fast, or in the wrong place, his barriers of protection and advantage could collapse around him like a house of cards. Phillip had become hyper-aware of that fact in the recent weeks, as Pierce and Campbell seemed to draw closer and closer. No Limit was his chance to prove his ultimate point. He had to beat Austin King first, then survive a triple threat. If he could do that, though, and the Red Raver held up her end of the bargain... It would be Kennedy vs. Campbell in the semi-finals, for all of the marbles. A cataclysmic confrontation that would likely once and for all tell him where he stood, both in the Hand and as professional wrestler. In his heart, he knew that he could be SCCW Universal Champion. It was, in his mind, simply a matter of whether Alexandra Pierce would bail Amy Campbell out. Just as she'd done against Jonathan Rhine. Just as she'd done against Lance Marshall. Hell, just as he'd done against Mayhem, who'd been a member of the group. Just like Phillip. 'Aww, sweetie, why are you frowning?' Before he could quite determine that he was, in fact, frowning, Phillip Kennedy's head turned, and his eyes fell on Kathryn Shaw. If he had been frowning, it was doubtful that he could continue to do so. The Sex Kitten's hair tumbled over her mostly bare shoulders. It didn't take Kennedy long to see that she was wearing her good-luck present, which appeared to consist of a lacey pink and white bra that was about a cup size too small, matching panties, black high heels, and not a whole Hell of a lot of anything else. 'Well,' she asked, voice dripping with promise as she posed, sticking a hand on her hip, 'how do I look?' That, Kennedy figured, was a dangerous question. 'Like a woman who is about to be fucked to within an inch of her life,' came the response, as Phillip rose from the bed. He took the laptop and placed it on an end table, so as to keep it out of the line of fire. 'Promises, promises,' the blonde chided, impatiently. It had been far too long since she'd had him. Though realistically, Kathryn didn't go especially long between romps (with a wide variety of people, at that), Phillip was one of her favorites. In part, that was probably because she seemed to bring out something primal in him, especially lately. In the beginning, she'd had to seduce him. Not that it was an especially difficult task...but she needed him wrapped around her finger. Otherwise, it was doubtful that he would have grown from a slightly arrogant poker player into arguably the crown jewel of the Dead Man's Hand. She took a good deal of pride in the fact that it had been *her* that had done that, not Alex. And if the price for that was being fucked as often as she could take by a good-looking wannabe cowboy, so much the better. He picked her up as if she weighed nothing, carrying her in his arms. She attempted to distract him, leaning up to plant kisses on his mouth and jaw, but he was focused enough to throw her down on the bed, watching her breasts bounce intently. God, it had been a few days already. Far too long for his tastes. ----- To say that the smile on Phil's face was pleased would be an understatement. For the first time in recorded history, he had managed to tire out Kathryn Shaw. She lay clinging to him, asleep, body touching his in subtle, wonderful places beneath the sheets and blanket, her head nestled in the crook of his neck. If Kennedy were a smoker, he'd be having a cigarette right now. As it was, it was enough for him to exult. Katsidy was nestled against him, fast asleep. He was the four seed in the No Limit Tournament, the event which would cement his name among the greates in the industry. He was Phillip Benjamin Kennedy: Professional Wrestler. For perhaps the first time, that thought didn't surprise him in the least.
