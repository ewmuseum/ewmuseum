Never let your opponent dictate your reactions ( Part 2)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Varies In the game of chess there are many opening moves and many counters to that opening move. The strike and counter strike is what makes the game something that can be played by novices and masters alike. The complex layers of strategy of feints, fakes, and baiting your opponent to make that critical mistake is appealing to most of the masters. Is the piece that is vulnerable a mistake or a carefully calculated ploy by your opponent? Is it part of a grander scheme and a necessary sacrifice to draw your attention from the true target? A man like Gideon thinks on many levels and has spent his adult life exploiting weaknesses. Gideon is not ranked on any National or even a Regional level when it comes to chess but he has yet to lose a game. This does not mean he is necessarily smarter then who he is playing against. He however is a person that studies his opponents and learns how they think and react. How do they react when you give them that opening? Do they jump on it thinking you made a mistake? Do they avoid it thinking that it is a carefully planned trap? Do they take that opening to lull you into a false sense of security thinking your plans are working? The Internet was buzzing from Gideon’s interview with Derrick Stone and the bleeding of his leg in a rather routine workout at a local boxing gym. Gideon exploited what is known as the "The Barbra Streisand Effect". The Barbra Streisand Effect is named after the hapless discoverer of this phenomena. Barbra attempted to sue a company which had published a free photographic survey of the California coastline that just happened to include images of Streisand’s palatial holiday home. Streisand’s case was thrown out, and ultimately she achieved the opposite of what she had wanted; rather than protect her privacy by forcing the surveyors to take-down the photo, it brought the image to the whole world’s attention. He wanted the news out there that he was badly injured. He seeded the initial rumors with Derrick Stone. Derrick and Gideon go way back and Derrick’s integrity has never been in question in his 17 years of being a journalist. He wouldn’t go to Derrick and expect him to lie and he wouldn’t lie to Derrick either. Instead he gave him information that was very misleading and had the special effects crew at Fontaine Studios work their magic to make the small scar on Gideon’s leg look inflamed, infected, and nearly 11 inches long. He also made sure that his leg was "bleeding" during a workout at a public boxing gym with several people watching. In the privacy of his office Gideon is smirking. This was part of his plan and it worked exactly as he planned. The breaking story about Gideon’s injury posted by Derrick Stone was the foundation for this deception. Gideon didn’t give details and it came off as Gideon was hiding something. Then when there was a "story" about Gideon’s leg bleeding during a workout and him not able to put his full weight on that right leg that was all the media needed to start reporting the news. As Gideon has learned "Reporting the news" means taking a "fact" and speculating on it. Sure they could have verified sources before posting the rumors. Sure they could have talked to Gideon himself before posting about his injuries. Gideon expected them to take the easy way out. In fact he counted on it. When Gideon filed lawsuits against 17 different wrestling "news" sites to get them to take down these reports he couldn’t stop snickering. He almost felt guilty over how easy it was. Tell them not to say something and they will shout it from the rooftops for anyone to hear. Little did they know that they were being played and fell for Gideon’s plan. ########## ########## ########## ########## The Gymnasium of the Fontaine Mansion, 5:32 AM ########## ########## ########## ########## It is quiet in the Gym as Gideon is headed towards the stands with the boards. On each board is the picture of Amy Campbell know better to the SCCW fans as Aimz. With a click of the button these stands start moving and Gideon is ready. He looks at each board with a calculated gaze. SNAP!! Suddenly his right hand backfists one of the boards breaking it and ripping the picture. Gideon: This has been a long time coming Amy. I can’t blame you for thinking that I would go easy on you because of our past "friendship" you are so used to taking advantage of people. Timo made that mistake and he got hurt badly each time. Timo was hurt by Darcy after a chair was brutally driven into his skull by a top rope elbow drop. SNAP!!! With a smirk Gideon uses a reverse roundhouse kick with his right leg snapping the board and ripping another picture. Gideon: Never mind that you helped ruin Timo’s marriage or when it came to having Timo’s back against the Affliction he was left laying because as always Amy came first. It wasn’t your fault. I have yet to see anything actually be your fault. No matter what happens you always have an excuse to play the victim. Now Timo’s career is nearly over and you are going after the Universal Title. SNAP!!! Gideon uses a reverse elbow his left arm snapping another board and ripping another picture. Gideon: It wasn’t my fault. It was the drugs. It was my childhood. My cat died. My dog died. My goldfish died. My aunt died. My best friend died. My neighbor died. She will be probably using Gary Gygax’s death as an excuse next. Personally I’ve heard the stories way too long to even remotely care. SNAP!!! Gideon uses a jumping shin kick with his left leg snapping another board and ripping another picture. Gideon: You don’t know who I am and you never did. It took several thousand dollars but I found out a few things about you that you do not wish to be made public. Something as simple as offering you my handkerchief to wipe the blood off your forehead after you were attacked by the Cowboys. I’m sure you never thought anything of it. The skeletons will be swinging from the closet doors SNAP!!! Gideon uses a shin kick with his right leg snapping another board and ripping another picture. Gideon: You saw fit to ruin the night of Stephanie and Neil’s 5th anniversary. Your big run and I’ll be making sure Smitty knows exactly what he is dealing with. I know that sooner or later you will turn to the Dead Man’s Hand because quite frankly it is the easy road and what you know best. There is a coldness to Gideon’s voice as he says these words. The man known as the Osaka Assassin is plotting and Amy Campbell doesn’t exist. She is only Aimz, a target for his rage to be dealt with in the most ruthless ways he can think of. Gideon: There are secrets that you have kept from Darcy. Sooner or later those secrets will be revealed and on that day I will take great happiness in seeing the shadows come to light. There is a light hearted chuckle as Gideon turns off the stands and they stop. He walks to the door and the lights are turned off. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building ########## ########## ########## ########## As Gideon is at his desk his telephone is lit up as there are several incoming calls. He is dealing with several major wrestling news sites and the stories they posted. The "Internet effect", No matter what happens if you are famous the Internet will find out and report it. After that initial report others will find out and then things continue to snowball. "Gideon Fontaine’s leg starts bleeding badly during a workout." This is one of the first one he deals with. He is talking to Jack Shuler the owner of Wrestling News dot com and he is being very polite but there is an edge to Gideon’s voice. Gideon: Mr. Shuler I have been told about a report posted on your Wrestling News website. I would like it taken down immediately. Jack: I verified the story with several sources that witnessed it first hand. Gideon: There are people that have witnessed Jimmy Hoffa and Elvis Presley eating at a Burger King in Salt Lake City, Utah. I can tell you my leg is fine and I have several doctors that can testify to that. If I am forced to bring this to legislation I can assure you that a simple "I’m sorry" will not suffice. Have a nice day. As Gideon hangs up he hits a button and the next person is Edward Thompson the publisher of the Wrestlewire news site. "Injury to Peroneus Longus muscle may cause Fontaine to retire." Gideon: Sorry to keep you waiting Mr. Thompson. I have been dealing with a lot of news sites posting things about me rather recklessly. Am I to understand that any news stories you post are to be verified by at least 3 sources? Edward: Yes. Gideon: Then what are sources 2 and 3 in the story posted about an injury to the Peroneus Longus in my leg? Edward: I can’t reveal my sources. Gideon: Ah journalistic integrity. It is a refreshing change these days. I’ll make this simple there is no source 2 or source 3. Retract the story before it becomes a very costly error on your part. If this goes to legislation you will lose not only your livelihood but your reputation. Have a pleasant day. As Gideon hangs up he hits a button to talk to Amanda. Gideon: Can you please redirect the rest of my calls to legal. Amanda: Sure Gideon. I take it you put a few of them in their places. "Gideon Fontaine unable to wrestle at Temptation and Zakk Wylde must team with Caleb Alan." That story was a good bit of work from both Zakk and Gideon. Both men are no strangers to playing out a well thought out swerve. You see the initial plan was to milk the injury for both the match VS the Prides and Gideon’s upcoming match against Aimz. No doubt this story was leaked to the press after he called Smitty to let him know he wouldn’t be able to wrestle. That was the plan and it went exactly as Gideon thought it would. The next part Zakk came up with and Gideon had to admit it was a pretty good idea. Rather then pull the swerve at Temptation and have Gideon and Zakk VS the Prides like they planned. They went with a new idea. Zakk knew that despite Caleb and Zakk never having teamed before they had a huge size advantage and all they had to do was wait their time until they got an opening. By having Gideon sitting out it made the injury more real for next week. The best part of the whole thing had to be Caleb Alan. He was pure gold. Gideon never told Caleb about the work because when it comes to keeping a secret he would have told someone in 15 minutes. This made the sell a lot easier and it made Gideon feel good about how insistent he was about wrestling in his place. Gideon gets up from the desk and grabs his cane. As he walks to the office door he puts the cane to the floor and starts walking with it and smiles. Like any good actor the props make all the difference when telling the tale. Amanda knows the injury is a work but you never know who is around. He leaves the office to make a trip to the SCCW office. He has some info for Smitty that he will not want to know about. There is a few unwritten rules in wrestling. "I don’t care what you do. Don’t bring it to work and don’t get caught." ########## ########## ########## ########## The SCCW office in Vegas ########## ########## ########## ########## There is a sound of Gideon’s cane against the tiled floor as he is walking towards Smitty’s office. He is walking with a limp and he says hello to the people he passes by. He even mentions a few names because it makes people feel more at ease when you know who they are. The walk to Smitty’s office isn’t very long but there is several security guards for obvious reason. Gideon looks at the briefcase and at Alan Branch. Branch: Smitty expecting you? Gideon: If he was he wouldn’t be here. Branch: Good point. I’ll check if he’s busy. Gideon: Tell him I got info on Aimz substance abuse that he will want to know about. Unless he thinks cocaine is an acceptable recreational drug. Shortly after Alan Branch enters the office you can hear the word "WHAT!!" all the way out in the hallway. There is a snicker from Gideon and Alan Branch opens the door. Branch: Took it pretty well. You can see the pained look on Smitty’s face as he rubs his temples. Gideon takes a seat and opens the briefcase. He pulls out a few folders. He passes the first one to Smitty. Alan Branch stands guard at the door. Smitty: Why do you insist on giving me an ulcer Gideon? Gideon: How do you think I feel? I got stabbed by a crack whore one week and now I’m facing Aimz who carries a lot bigger knives. Smitty: How did you get this? Gideon: You remember the Cowboys from Hell? Smitty: Yeah. Gideon: They busted open Amy and I offered her a handkerchief to wipe the blood from her forehead. Being around the business all these years I noticed some erratic behavior when she was having problems with Darcy so I had the blood tested. Smitty: So this is just between us. Gideon: That and the people that might have noticed the name Amy Campbell on the blood sample. I used her maiden name to be a tad discrete. Smitty: Ok how is this getting out now then? Gideon: I had it double checked at another clinic. Apparently they weren’t as discrete as the first one and it leaked out. Smitty: ……… Wonderful… Gideon: It gets worse. Smitty: You drop this on me and now you say worse. Gideon: As you know I’ve been approached by Kathryn Shaw to drop the lawsuit against Sarah Riley, FUSE now known as SCCW, and yourself. I’ve went through the numbers in my mind and contemplated several possible motives. They want to make your life hell and are passing up the opportunity to get extra leverage. As you can imagine if they wanted to twist you they would try and get me to use the lawsuit as leverage. They aren’t stupid people. Well maybe Mr. Ashe but since he’s not in charge it doesn’t matter. Smitty: So what have you come up with? Gideon: They have moles in SCCW. We obviously caught one here with Sarah Riley. They know I’m not an idiot by trying to quash the lawsuit they pretty much highlighted her involvement. They know I caught it as soon as Miss Shaw started her bang me routine. Why would they show their hands when they love the deception and paranoia. Think about what Sarah Riley did. She set up Rhine for a fall but I’m looking at the bigger picture here. Why set up Rhine? What’s the pay off? Smitty: I’m not seeing it. Gideon: You aren’t seeing the larger picture. They thought by distracting me by the obvious that I’d overlook their hidden agenda. That is their biggest flaw. They assume that no one is as smart as they are. Who benefits from Rhine being thrown to the wolves. Amy has an upcoming Universal title match and distracting Rhine benefits her. Desade tends to like psycho bitches with anger management issues. Smitty: So what do you want me to do? Gideon: I’m sure you know about all the stories about how injured my leg is. Smitty: It’s impossible not to the way the media has been smearing that all over the place. There is a snicker from Gideon and Smitty’s eyebrow raises. Gideon: Particularly after I started suing people telling them to retract the stories. The wide eyed look on Smitty’s face is priceless as he just realized what Gideon did. There is that smile across Gideon’s face that has nearly always meant he has gotten over on someone. Smitty starts laughing his head off. Smitty: You are a manipulative bastard Gideon! That is great! Gideon: Thank you. It is so hard to sell the seriousness of an injury these days and you really have to go that extra mile to fool the fans and the other wrestlers. Any problems with Caleb Alan wrestling from the legal department? Smitty: This is pro wrestling. If there weren’t legal problems I’d be wondering if something is up. Gideon: True enough. Let me tell you about some of my plans………… They are shooting the breeze for hours. As much as Gideon may be a pain in the ass there is an understanding between the two men. Most guys go into Smitty’s office and either try and blow smoke up his ass or they try and manipulate him. Gideon Fontaine doesn’t have to do that because he doesn’t have to. When it comes right down to it which would you rather deal with? An asshole like Gideon or power crazed egomaniacs with anger management issues? ########## ########## ########## ########## At Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. ########## ########## ########## ########## Neil is sitting on a bench in the gymnasium and Gideon and Stephanie are sparring. They are all laughing as they are having a good time. You might find this a tad unusual if you didn’t know the Whittaker family. Stephanie was wrestling alongside Neil and Gideon all the way back when they were in UJW way back in 1999. She is as driven as Neil and Gideon. Stephanie: You are pretty spry for an injured guy Gideon. They are laughing as Stephanie shoots for a double leg take down which Gideon stops. Neil: Gotta work on the selling more. Gideon: Definitely. Still never got used to selling. As Stephanie goes for the right leg Gideon is favoring it. He moves out of the way using his left leg. Watching Gideon now you can see he is limping and isn’t putting his full weight on it. Despite this Stephanie isn’t taking Gideon down. It isn’t surprising given how many years Gideon has been training. Stephanie has been moving Gideon all over the ring trying though. Neil: Much better Gideon. Stephanie: Still can’t cut a girl a break can you. Gideon: You know me Steph. Neil: Chivalry for Gideon is asking the lady if she wants to be glazed or swallow. Gideon: Ouch! You are mistaking me for Caleb. They are laughing their heads off as Gideon looks hurt. Of course it is good natured kidding because they know each other for nearly 10 years. Gideon may be a brother to Neil and Stephanie but they know him well as well as his faults. He is a definite womanizer and a bit of an asshole. They also know his darkside. They don’t know his plans. Neil and Stephanie would not be surprised by the plans. They know how angry he gets and how patient he is when it comes to getting payback. They would not be surprised that he has been digging into her personal life. Finding out that he is responsible for the Internet getting wind of Amy’s cocaine use isn’t a shock. Finding out that he told Smitty about it and used it for leverage isn’t unusual either. It is all part of a personal philosophy he follows. "Never let your opponent dictate your reactions."
