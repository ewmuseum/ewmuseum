The goddess." - Marv, Sin City VII 12/25/2008 8:45 a.m. Christmas day After the events of the past few days, Police Chief Mike Twain told myself and the rest of the Rat Pack to take a break from our duties as crime fighters over the holiday. I know for sure I need one to unwind, so I went along with it and I took three days off. My brain is fried like an egg in a pan being cooked on a stove. Thinking clearly has been hard for me ever since the revelation that my best friend is the very criminal who has been raising hell all over town. Rick Roll, the nightmare that haunts me every moment of everyday, is the one person I trusted the most. However this has been a great few days for me despite the cold and cloudy weather we‘ve had here. A flock of blackbirds has migrated to my neighborhood. They probably are flying south late because of global warming. However someone has been keeping me warm at night. In the days since I as officially given a break by the police chief, I’ve been spending most of my time with Amanda. She is amazing. I don’t what I’d do without her. I’d probably go all emo and cut my arms or something along those lines. I think I’m really in love with her. This is a wonderful feeling to have. One moment I was nearing my nadir and now I’m madly in love. Go figure. As I sit on the bed with Amanda laying next to me, thoughts on the many incidents that have happened as a fall out from my last foray into hero work rush through my mind. It’s been a chaotic few weeks for me. After the Tao nightclub shoot out, the police went to James von Doom’s house and discovered that there was nothing there. He was smart enough to have everything moved out. He’s gone. Von Doom can’t wrestle anymore so the assumption is that he was working as a hit man part time but then went full time due to the economy. My thinking on him is that all of the public stupidity was an act to hide what he was really up to. The guy always had money and nobody knew why. Now everybody knows. On the other side of the incident, Rick Roll hasn’t been heard from since. I have no idea what my former best friend might be up to. However the first thing I did was change the locks. Since Todd has keys to my place it’s a logical move. Even more logical since he is completely been taken over by Rick Roll. Just then, Amanda wakes up next to me on my bed. Sitting here just watching her has been so peaceful. Moments like this are so rare. She shines like an angel should shine when coming down from the heavens. Her smile makes her all the more perfect. "Hi honey," she mutters. "Just thinking." "About what?" "The past few days. I’ve been through a lot, you know." "I know. We all have." "My best friend tries to kill me, join a superhero team, I fall head over heels for you, I almost get shot by a hit man, and I’m involved in more than one high speed car chases as well as being kidnapped. Now that’s a crazy few weeks right there." She puts her hand on my shoulder. What a caring woman I’m dating. Her touch calms me almost instantly. "Ash, it’s over. Take a break. Calm down. All you need is love like the Beatles say and all the love you need is from me." As she embraces me, our lips touch creating an instant feeling of ecstasy within us. In a moment all of the worries I have fade into the darkness, destroyed by the light my angel shines upon me. I wish this feeling would last forever. However I know that will never so I enjoy it while I can because I know the shit will hit the fan again very soon. * * * Ash Journal Entry, 12/26/2008: The past few days have a been a quiet blur. I love her with every inch of my being. * * * 12/27/2008 9:57 p.m. The night is freezing cold as police arrive at a residence after receiving a call of suspicious activity from one of the neighbors. Apparently, according to the call, a man has been seen in the house acting in a suspicious fashion. The fact that the home is foreclosed and has no one living in it makes it only more suspicious. POLICE OFFICERS SHOT! SEND BACK UP! SUSPECT VON DOOM! PROCEED WITH EXTREME CAUTION!’ "VON DOOM!" The bastard was still in town. Turning to the rest of the Rat Pack, we quickly head for the door and to our vehicles. All of us know that this guy is probably the deadliest person alive in town not named Rick Roll. He killed more than ten people only a few nights ago. I was almost one of them but for some reason he let me go. I speed as fast as I can in my supped up Toyota Supra. This guy is an animal and we have to stop him. I have to stop him. I’m probably the only one who can. * * * 10:05 p.m. After the Rat Pack arrives at the crime scene, another call comes in before I get there. According to the call on the scanner, von Doom is on foot heading toward a parking garage a few blocks away. I decide to speed over there and head him off before he gets to a vehicle. I know that one he does get a car, he’s gone. This man is not the idiot he made himself to be with his public persona. He is a deadly cold blooded killer. He hears me and the foot chase is on. Running as fast as he can, I follow him until we go inside a parking garage. The police chopper overhead veers off and leaves us. Now it’s just me and him. We begin a cat and mouse game with me playing the cat hunting the mouse, which is him. However, as I look between and under cars, I forget that he is not a mouse. This guy is a coyote. He’s a desert coyote that’s been hunting his prey for years without anybody even suspecting or noticing. I must be cautious. Just then he comes out of nowhere from behind a car and confronts me, pointing a handgun at my head. He cocks the trigger readying to fire the weapon. Looking into his eyes, I see nothing but animal instinct. The will to survive in the wild is all I see. "Let me go and I will not kill you," he says in a stern tone. I can tell he means every word that he says. He’s not the bluffing type. "You actually think I should believe that?" I reply, raising my hands. "I’ve seen you kill people in cold blood." "Sure, but only people I was hired to kill. I don’t kill unless I have too unless I get paid." "And that’s supposed to make it ok to kill people?" "You kill people too you hypocrite! Now let me go. I’m not the one you have to worry about. Rick Roll set us both up and the cartels hire me to do their dirty work. In other words, fuck with them and I am sent in. Keep out of their business and you will be fine unless you threaten me or put me in a corner. It your choice. Let me go and live to right another day or try to capture me and die. Your choice." The man for some reason makes a lot of sense to me. For one he is not the threat, Rick Roll is. I also am not his target, so there’s another reason to let him go. However he did kill in cold blood. I’ve killed too so that doesn’t make him any better or worse than me. So I go with the logical decision and let him go. Moving out of the way, I motion to the car parked behind us. "Go, but don’t come back." He smiles a devious grin in my direction before taking the sights of his gun off of me. He then goes to the car behind us and breaks in. Before going inside of it, he turns and says one last thing to me. "I ain’t never coming back," he says. "They know my face here. I can’t work in this town anymore. You made a smart move. It’ll pay off in the future because if I ever come back, I won’t kill you." Von Doom then climbs inside the car and I watch as he hotwires it before speeding off as I safely get out of the way. Deep down, I know that this seems like a stupid move now but it’ll pay off in the long run. This guy knows the cartels and could possibly help me down the line. Just a matter of where and when, that’s all. The fact that Rick Roll screwed him over too could also help me out. As the saying goes, an enemy of my enemy is a friend of mine. That’s how I view this decision. Too bad I had to let him go. Better get back to the police and let them know what happened. So I turn and walk back the way I came knowing that what just happened may screw me over sometime down the line but it doesn’t matter. Now the only thing I have to worry about is capturing that bastard Rick Roll. That’s the only thing that should matter anyway. * * * Judge Journal Entry, 12/30/2008 With the absence of Rick Roll, this city has been quiet. Flashes of light appear all around me when I glance around during our embrace. Her lips taste like sweet raspberries. She smells like tropical fruits. She is the most lovely angel on this Earth. Once we let go of our kiss, I feel like I’m stuck in a permanent state of ecstasy. For the first time, I look to the future of this city in an optimistic fashion.
