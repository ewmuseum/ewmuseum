Interviewing techniques

19 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/02/08 Buzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz. No, that isn’t the sound of a fly trap or anything like that catching some sort of malaria ridden bug in Mississippi, it is actually an intercom sound on the desk of Larry King of all people. His secretary actually presses a button on her desk, and it buzzes his so he can tell she wants to speak to him and then she speaks over it, ingenious! Anyway I digress, the point is not the buzzing intercom, it is what she says afterwards. "Mr King Sir" (As the Sir is important) "It’s that man again. What should I tell him? At this point, I should explain that man is Andre Jensen, richest guy on the planet (yes, somewhat richer than Bill Gates) and he is there at Larry King’s office for the 4th time this week, the first 2 he was evicted from his office without even seeing him, the 3rd he was asked to make an appointment. This one is the scheduled appointment, well it would be if AJ’s penchant for time keeping wasn’t "turn up when you feel like and try to buy a slot." Larry King, deep in his papers, researching for material for his show and generally figuring out what to blabber on about tonight takes a big sip of the whisky he has in his giant tumbler, then with a bitter face adds more ice from his silver ice cooler, kept conveniently next to his desk behind some books about American politics and copies of Razzle. "Which man?" For there are many people who go to Larry King’s offices to try and sell him something or get airtime for something else, like the guy who tried to get a hold of Larry so he could get an interview about the Great Azbathanian Warlord of Graaaf, more specifically that he was said Warlord and he wanted the world to bow to him in front of Larry King. Naturally he didn’t get on the show. "Erm Mr Jensen Sir, he has been here the past few days, apparently he wants to ask you for advice." "Advice eh? Larry King doesn’t give out advice, has he made an appointment?" "Well yes, for next week." "Next week! Well why is he here now?" "He says its urgent sir, he has something to do on Friday and he needs your advice right now." "Tough! Larry King is too busy." "He is offering a million dollars." "Show him through." Larry then sits behind his desk, after re-jacketing himself, straightening up his tie and generally smarting himself up ready for his guest. He picks up a copy of the financial times and begins to scan the paper, believing this is helping him look a bit more of a business man; maybe he can cut a deal for more than a million. Who knows? Either way, the man who is just about to come in through the door is quite possibly a hard nosed powerful businessman, someone important, someone who has the sort of money to offer a million just to see him in his office. "Mr King, here is Mr Andre Jensen." Says the pretty 21 year old secretary, knocking on the door and coming into the office with a headset on her head. Larry waves her away and she ushers AJ into the office. Larry King’s face kind of drops. You know the sort of cartoon face drop you see when Tom is just about to get ran over by a train/rocket/car/gigantic wedge of cheese, it is similar to that, what he was not expecting is AJ. To fully appreciate why Larry King had this reaction, then let’s just confirm what he is looking at. AJ, a 6’6 250lbs lump of muscle, wearing a matching gold and purple robe set with a turban on his head. This isn’t the unusual thing though, you see people wearing these things all the time. Neither is the big black eye he has from the fight this weekend, or the welt he has on his chin. No, the strange thing about it, which got Larry King’s cartoon like reaction was the fact he is followed by 3 people, one of which graciously bows every few seconds to AJ, another which carries an easel with a golden framed painting of Osama Bin Laden and the third was carrying the flag of the Taliban with him. The flag bearer and the picture bearer stand to the side of AJ, slightly behind, and the bower stands in front of AJ walking backwards. AJ dismisses him with a wave and the man leaves to the side, after asking for forgiveness. AJ looks at Larry and smiles, a broad, beaming, kind of stupid smile and extends his hand. "Pleased to meet you Larry, can I call you that? I am Andre Jensen, richest guy on the planet." AJ helps himself to the contents of the mini bar Larry has in his office, and sits down before being asked, making sure the man who was bowing him polishes off and checks for dirt before hand. "I am here today Larry because I need a hand, you see, I am currently in the wrestling business, and my employers, who I have a sneaky suspicion are short changing me in the salary stakes have agreed to let me have an expose slot on their show, where I interview guests. Pretty basic stuff I know, but I need training in interviewing technique, and training in how to get the exact responses I need when I ask the ultimate question." "I see, what is the ultimate question" "If they are paid more than me, oh and if they want to join me in the fight for the Taliban." A pregnant silence punctuates the air for a few moments as Larry King takes on board what AJ just said. Did he really say "fight for the Taliban?" A thousand thoughts of throwing AJ out of the office came flooding to him, but then he realised AJ was offering a million dollars just to see him, and within 10 seconds he had made his mind up. He was going for the money. "Strange beliefs, but I can help you with the interview technique, for a small bonus, say what you agreed to pay me already, plus an extra half mill for taking up the day to train you" AJ nods reverently and snaps his fingers, the bowing man quickly appears with a briefcase chocked full of cash. "Here you are, this is yours when I am fully confident in the methods of interviewing, let us begin." "What? Now? It’s lunch." "Yes, now, if you fast then you can have lunch any time, I would like to be confident of my interviewing technique, much like I am confident of my negotiating skills thanks to Donald Trump." "Ok, where do we begin?" "You are the teacher. You decide." Larry king ponders this for a moment, and walks around his office, while AJ sits and looks at him in expectation sipping the whiskey his little bowing worshipping man poured him previously. Larry then looks at AJ and sits down in front of him. "Your first rule of interviewing is to make your interviewee relaxed and calm. When you can do that the interview falls into place. Try to make me relaxed and calm." "Relaxed and calm?" "Relaxed and calm." AJ shrugs and clicks his fingers; suddenly his man is behind Larry King like a flash, massaging his shoulders and out of nowhere the other two produce violins and begin to play some soothing music. Larry King sits there and starts to enjoy it, before snapping back into action. "No, no, no. That’s not what I meant!" "It works on the ladies" "Yeah, possibly, but this is an interview, your idea is to get a good story from them or good answers, not seduce them." "Oh, I thought that if you seduced someone well enough they would give you good stories." "But I am not gay." "Neither am I, you don’t have to be to seduce a man do you?" Again, one of those pregnant painful silences kind of occurs as Larry is trying to see the logic behind what AJ is saying. He figures logic has gone for a round the world cruise and is not going to be around to say if it had any part of this and just lets it go. "Fine, but in an interview, do not do that, introduce yourself, just sit them down, be nice to them and offer them a drink or something." "Right, gotcha." "Now try that again." Larry once again stands as if he had just come into the room and gets ready for AJ to put him at ease. AJ smiles and puts his hand out again. "Andre Jensen, richest guy on the planet, thanks for coming" He shakes his hand. "Please, take a seat." Larry thinks he is getting somewhere and attempts to sit down. "NO NOT THAT ONE! DAMMIT! IT IS A SACRED CHAIR, SIT ON THE ONE IN THE CORNER!" Larry jumps with a frightened yelp and scurries over to the corner chair. AJ from the other end of the room yells back. "WOULD YOU LIKE A DRINK LARRY?!" "Er, yes, yes please" Suddenly a bottle of water appears from the man who seems to worship AJ right in front of Larry’s eyes. Larry accepts it. "How was that?" asks AJ enquiringly. "Well, it was good until you forced me to go sit on a chair the other side of the room and shouted at me. The chair I was going to sit in was perfectly placed for the interview; you need to be close to the person you are speaking to. They also need to feel comfortable and that you are pleased to have them there. You did neither. Let me show you how I do it. You be the guest." AJ shrugs, and nods in agreement, then walks to the door. He walks up to Larry and Larry smiles, shakes his hand and offers him to sit down next to him, offering a drink at the same time, all very professional. "See, that’s how you do it." Larry declares this with a beaming smile. "I see. Why do I have to be close to them? I mean we are talking wrestlers here, they don’t like you being too close unless you are going to get power bombed or something." "I see, well I am sure that what we suggested here will be suitable. Now lets move on to the interview itself. You need to keep the comfort feeling going, so always start out with a joke, or say something funny to make them laugh and put them at ease." "Hmmm ok, I can do that. What afterwards?" "Well, during the interview, you have to know where you want it to go, and know the questions; don’t be afraid to change them if you want things to develop, if they are falling flat, or not working. So you ask them a personal question, they clam up, then stay away from personal questions until they are more relaxed. Keep them all open ended, allowing your guest to talk. The best interviews are when your guest talks and you only give them small prompts in your questions, especially if you have a good rapport with them." "Rapport?" "Yeah, you know, banter, chemistry, like you are friends." "I see, so I treat him like a friend, but try to get information from him?" "Or her" "Well, yes, but I don’t think I will be interviewing women, they can’t even talk!" "What?" "You know, they are not allowed to talk to a man, so why would I be interviewing one of them?" "Hmmmm. Well, lets move on to you practicing what I told you eh?" Larry King tries to move this on from this odd topic of women not being able to talk to men in order to continue the training. He stands up and goes to the end of the room, the starting point of the interview as you will. AJ stands up and looks at him, nodding to confirm he is ready. AJ receives Larry with a smile and a handshake, saying something mildly amusing about braces and chocolate to which Larry gives the appropriate response, and AJ sees him to his chair, offering him a drink as he does. Larry at this point is thinking GREAT! "So, Larry" AJ begins. "I am glad you agreed to join me here on my show. What do you think?" "Yeah, I like it, nice décor" "I went for the Larry King look, a bit shabby, old, but still got all his marbles." Larry chuckles politely. "Anyway, how are things with you? I haven’t seen you on TV much recently. Is that because of the strike?" "Well, yeah, we came back in December though; you haven’t seen any of it?" "To be fair, no. I have been performing you see, and, well, you are just not as fun as John Stewart or something." Again Larry politely laughs. "Because we haven’t got too much time, I am going to keep this one short and sweet. I got a couple of questions." "Ok" "Do you like America?" "I love America, why?" "Interesting, do you like George Bush?" "I don’t want to answer that" "Why?" "My show is an American institution, I have to be unbiased." "Pah." Larry King shrugs as AJ makes this noise, AJ then racks his brain for more questions, having the sort of look you have when you have just been given a quadratic equation to solve in your head which has every letter of the alphabet in it. "Ok, Larry, I am going to lay it on the line here for you. I am going to make you an offer, you can say yes, you can say no, but here it is. I want you to be the official spokesman of the Taliban and help us defeat the evil scourge of American government from the world?" The pregnant silence is back. This time it is prolonged to the point of uncomfortable, Larry stares at AJ with the utmost contempt, while AJ looks back at him nonplussed, waiting for his answer. The only sound you can hear for a while is the clock in the room ticking. Larry is the first to break the silence after a couple of minutes. "What did you say?" "I asked, if you want to be a part of the Taliban, as our spokesman. US branch." "Mr Jensen, I know you came in here with a Taliban flag, and a painting of bin Laden, I thought there was something strange about you from the start. But never in a million years would I have thought that someone would have been so ignorant or stupid as to actually ask if I, an American citizen and King of the talk show if I want to create treason by joining an enemy we are at war with. Are you some sort of moron?" "Well I thought…………" "YOU THOUGHT NOTHING – GET OUT OF MY OFFICE, GET GONE AND NEVER COME BACK! I NEVER WANT TO SEE YOUR FACE AGAIN YOU HORRIBLE MAN, LEAVE THE MONEY HERE – YOU GO!" AJ shrugs his shoulders and turns on his heels, clicking to get the full parade back in motion again, walking away from a furious red faced Larry King. He gets to the secretary who is looking on in wild curiosity as she had heard the shouting from her own desk. "Wow, he really laid into you" She exclaims, rather pointlessly AJ looks at her and smiles, catching her eyes with his. A flutter of emotion swept through him – he seemed like he wants this woman. He puts his hand out to shake hers, and smiles a beaming smile. "How would you like to earn a million dollars and get away from Mr King?" He says, winking at her.
