Character Assassination (IG53)

8 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 08 All characters and events in this piece – even those based on real people – are entirely fictional. All PTC personalities are impersonated… poorly. The following piece contains coarse language and due to its content it should not be read by anybody. ---=====--- As an agent Lawrence Spiderman had been confronted by some odd situations, but he had noticed a trend in the last six months that the situations he found himself in seemed to be increasingly strange and all the more frequent. There was no doubt in his mind that this was a direct result of his picking up Jared Sykes as a client over the summer. It started with a single blueberry costume, then three people in costumes, and things seemed to just spiral from there. At one point Jared had pitched him an idea for a movie featuring a giant-sized Dean Malenko invading New York and putting the Statue of Liberty in a leglock. He'd have called it 'The Texas Cloverfield'. Earlier in the week, after the schedule for the 54th installment of the Infinite Gauntlet was announced, Spiderman had received an email correspondence from his most troubleshome of clients. Attached to it was a document that the email described as a script for an episode of South Park. He had hoped to ignore it, thinking he might be able to simply wish it away, but a string of 'did you read it?' messages convinced him that he would, inevitably, need to cave. It was Thanksgiving by the time he finally convinced himself to sit through what he expected to be a textual catastrophe. After a pleasant meal with his family, Spiderman had retired to his home and the stack of paper that awaited him. Resigned to his fate, Spiderman took up his Blackberry, and scrolled through his contacts. He stopped at an entry labeled only as 'Career Suicide', and pressed the 'send' button to dial. He sighed as the rings continued, ultimately kicking him to voicemail. 'Hey, Jared,' he began, 'it's Larry. Wanted to say happy Thanksgiving, and also to let you know that I got your email and have printed out the attachment.' Fortunately, Jared was not there to see the rolling eyes of Larry Spiderman. 'I'm, uhh, I'm going to read through it this weekend and will get back to you once I've finished. I'll let you know if I have any questions.' Seated comfortably on his sofa, Spiderman put up his feet, tossed his phone aside, and glanced at the first page. ---=====--- The Greatest Episode Ever Script by Jared A. Sykes Color by Technicolor [Let's do the usual opening. Disclaimer, 'should not be viewed by anyone', yadda yadda. They know the drill.] [Cue up some kickin' Les Claypool music. I guess the intro sequence will depend on which season they run this on, since it changes season-to-season, but, again, we'll let them decide.] [We start with a tranquil scene of the Colorado Rocky Mountains. The sky is clear, the sun is shining, and a deer is gently grazing on some grass. Aww, look at him. Ain't he cute? Then there's a bright flash, and the deer explodes.] Sound Effects: FWOOSH! [Enter our hero, a very confused and disoriented Jared Sykes. Though he may not know where he is, he remains nonetheless handsome and dignified, a true bastion of masculinity and sheer sexual prowess. In short, the pinnacle of awesome.] [Pinnacle of awesome. I like that.] ---=====--- Already frustrated, Spiderman took his left hand off the stack of papers to rub the bridge of his nose. He wasn't even half a page into the script and already he was ready to quit. It suddenly occurred to him why he kept a bottle of whiskey in the bottom drawer of his desk. Fortunately the liquor cabinet at home was stocked better than his desk. ---=====--- [Jared (pinnacle of awesome) slowly rises to his feet, surveying his surroundings. It's not long before he dusts himself off and starts walking towards the edge of the meadow/pasture/patch-of-nature and closer to town.] [Dressed in shorts and a tee shirt, he is otherwise oblivious to the biting cold of the snow-covered landscape. After a moment he happens upon a local bus stop, where three clearly identifiable children stand in a line side by side. The audience knows them as Stan, Cartman and Kenny.] [Kyle runs into the scene all excited, waving some stuff around in his hand.] Kyle: You guys! You guys! Guess what? My mom got me tickets to wrestling on Saturday. Stan: Sweet, dude. Cartman: Psh. Gay. Kyle: What are you talking about, Cartman? Stan: Yeah, wrestling's not gay. Cartman: It's totally gay. It's a bunch of greasy sweaty men rolling around and hugging each other in their underwear. Kenny: Mmmah, mmrnd mrrmtimms mrey mmrph mmothr mmn mmeh mmsss. [Cartman giggles, because every time Kenny says something it's dirty.] Cartman: Heh, you said it Kenny. [Our hero, who was close enough to overhear part of this conversation, now steps into the scene. The fact that he's wearing shorts and a tee-shirt in the middle of winter on a snow-covered landscape in the mountains is completely ignored by everyone, because I'd kind of prefer it that way.] Jared: Hey, wrestling's not gay! Well, not entirely gay. I mean, where I come from there are some lesbians. Stan: Who the hell are you? Kyle: Yeah, and what's a lesbian? Jared: The hottest thing ever. [They really are.] Jared: I'm Jared Sykes. I'm a wrestler. I'm kind of a big deal. [I really am.] Stan: Never heard of you. Kyle: Where'd you come from, anyway? Jared: I was just sitting in my locker room after the show getting ready to go home, and then the Church of the Unholy kicked the door in, all four hundred and twelve of them, and some B-movie monster in a rubber suit pointed a Zippo at me and, poof! Here I am. [The kids just stare wide-eyed. Stan, the generally accepted mouthpiece of the group, is the first one to say anything.] Stan: Dude, that's pretty BLEEP'ed up. [I'm not sure how this works? Do I type the bleeps, or do I just write the naughty words and let the censors deal with it?] [Hell, I'll just leave the bleeps in. They'll know what it means.] Kyle: Yeah, and what's a Church of the Unholy? Cartman: C'mon, Kyle. Everyone knows that's where Jews have their meetings. Kyle: BLEEP you, fatass! Cartman: Hey, BLEEP you, Jew-boy. Stan: Seriously, what’s a Church of the Unholy? Jared: It’s about 400 characters from monster movies, and another ten guys who all think they’re Heath Ledger, or the Joker, or both. Stan: That sounds pretty BLEEP’ed up. Jared: At least you don't have to see the GIF files. ---=====--- Back in reality, Larry Spiderman's annoyance was growing. It was bad enough that his client was forcing him to read what he had already concluded was the worst script in the history of television, but the recent string of anti-Semitic jokes were only making the experience worse. Spiderman, not being an expert on popular culture, was oblivious to the notion that this is how the character normally acted. For a moment he looked towards his fireplace, where a pile of logs were slowly burning away. His gaze shifted to the stack of paper on his lap, back to the fireplace, and then back to the stack of paper, entertaining the notion of torching the script and lying to his client about his opinion of it. Unfortunately for Spiderman his conscience wouldn't allow him to burn it. He did however skip ahead a few pages. ---=====--- Jesus: Your time is up, blasphemer! Violent Jesus: Eugene would like an autograph. Jesus: You and this Eugene are both going down. [Jesus leaps out of his chair on the set of Jesus And Pals, jumping up onto his chair. He cracks his knuckles, and gets ready to fight.] [Violent Jesus (who is clearly the cooler of the Jesuses...? Jesi...? Jesoxen? What the hell is the plural of 'Jesus'?) looks kind of confused, which is par for the course for Lloyd, though he does seem to understand that it's time to throw down. He removes the stuffed yellow rabbit named Eugene from around his neck, pats it on the head and sets it on the ground.] Violent Jesus: Earth! Fire! Wind! Water! Heart! By your powers combined, I am CAPTAIN JESUS! [Violent Jesus doesn't turn green or anything. Instead Eugene grows to a size of ten feet, and transforms into a living battle action rabbit, complete with armor, a helmet, and chainguns attached to his shoulders. It's pretty awesome.] [Jesus, the original one - the 'O.J.' (not to be confused with that black guy who murders people), jumps forward off of his chair and his eyes go all squinty.] Jesus: You have left me no choice, my son. Violent Jesus: No, you're my son. Jesus: What? You don’t get to say "my son". Violent Jesus: Luke, I am your father! Eugene: *bunny noises* Jesus: This is preposterous! Stop playing games with me! Violent Jesus: Eugene likes games. We play Street Fighter 2. Jesus: I'm not familiar with... [CRACK!] [Violent Jesus catches Original Recipe Jesus in the jaw with a pretty sweet uppercut.] Violent Jesus: SHORYUKEN! ---=====--- 'He has got to be out of his damned mind.' Larry Spiderman's left hand flew from his side, snatching up the Blackberry from his couch in one quick motion. He immediately rose to his feet, and began pacing a short path in his living room. When 'Career Suicide' again didn't answer his phone, Spiderman was again relegated to leaving a voicemail message. 'Hey, Jared, it's Larry again. I've been reading through this script you sent me, and I have a few,' he paused, temporarily brushing all professionalism aside, 'Look, is this for real, or are you screwing with me here? The jokes, the way this is written, I just don't get it. Give me a call.' Spiderman pressed a button on his phone, ending the call, and lightly tossed the device back onto the couch. After a quick trip into the kitchen for some refreshments to take his mind off what he had deemed a 'stack of nonsense', he once again picked up the document, flipped ahead a few more pages, and continued reading. He also resolved to murder his client at the soonest possible opportunity for forcing him to endure this. ---=====--- Jared: There's no way in hell this is going to work. Kyle: Quiet, here she comes. [In an attempt to help get our hero, who is still the pinnacle of awesome, back to his own reality the kids have brought him to school to hide out until they take him to see the crazy 'I like animals with 4 asses whose name I can't remember' guy.] [They currently have him (me) sitting quietly in the back of class and dressed as a 4th grader. The excuse is that he has a gland problem.] Ms. Garrison: Good morning class. Class: Good morning Ms. Garrison. Ms. Garrison: Does everyone have their book reports? [Ms. Garrison turns her/his/whatever (honestly, I can't keep up with the changes; pick a gender, people) attention to the new student in the back of the class.] Ms. Garrison: Uh, can I help you? Stan: He's a new student. Kyle: Yeah, transferred here today. Ms. Garrison: Oh, well, make sure you get a copy of someone's notes. Cartman: (Under his breath) Psh, so gay. Ms. Garrison: What did you say, Eric?! Cartman: I said 'he can come over and copy them today'. [There's a knock on the classroom door. Ms. Garrison opens the door, and standing on the other side is a redheaded Canadian woman in a white pant suit. You can tell she's Canadian because of her floppy head and beady little eyes.] [Her name is Amy Pierce.] Pierce: Oh hey buddy, I was postulating that you might desire to accompany me oot in the corridor for a quick exchange of pleasantries that will be both enlightening and elucidating. Dootless will your existence remain unaltered by the discourse in which we are about to engage. [Hey, so I wrote 'out' and 'doubtless' that way on purpose, to really hammer home the Canadian.] Ms. Garrison: Uhh, excuse me? [Amy Pierce sighs.] Pierce: Can we talk in the hall? It's important. Ms. Garrison: Oh, sure. Class, turn to chapter 4 of your official Jon & Kate Plus 8 biographies and read silently until I come back. [Once they're out in the hall, the kids start talking.] Jared: Who the hell was that? Stan: Teacher's new girlfriend. She's kind of strange. Jared: Does she always talk like that? Kyle: Yeah, she thinks big words make her appear smart and mysterious. Stan: But really it's just annoying and pretentious. [A moment later the door to the classroom opens again, and both Ms. Garrison and Amy pierce enter the room. As Ms. Garrison opens a drawer on her desk to remove something, Amy's eyes scan the room and settle on our hero, who looks way out of place.] Pierce: You! Jared: Huh? Stan: Jesus, dude! [Amy reaches into her coat and removes an AR-15. She opens fire on the classroom as students dive for cover under their desks. Stan, Kyle, Cartman, Jared, and Kenny make a dive for the window, but only the first four make it. Kenny is riddled with bullet holes.] [Amy Pierce does not care that she has just murdered a child in cold blood, because she is a cold, unfeeling demon, and will mallet-pound that point into your ass until you fart the dictionary.] [Oh hell, I'm probably getting sued for that last bit with the shooting and the classroom.] Stan: Oh my god, she killed Kenny! Kyle: You bastard! [Lawsuits aside, the kids and their intrepid hero are hauling ass down the sidewalk.] Cartman: Eh… ugh… you guys… eh… slow… ugh… down! Kyle: Hurry up, Cartman! Stan: Yeah, we don’t want to get shot. [Finally they stop and hide behind some bushes. Bushes are like blankets: if you’re behind them or under one, the monsters can’t get you.] Stan: (Out of breath) Hey tall-guy, why’d that lady take a shot at you? Kyle: Yeah, she seemed pretty pissed. Jared: No idea, though she did look familiar. I wonder if it has something to do with VOLGA! [Both kids (because Cartman is still running, you see) shout in unison, probably because they’re startled by the name VOLGA!] Jared: What? Stan: What’s with the shouting? Jared: You mean VOLGA!? Kyle: Dammit. Jared: You have to shout it. It’s one of those "if X is all-caps’ed, then X must be shouted" rules. It’s the same thing with PRIME! and FUSE! and I’m PRETTY SURE I’VE USED THIS JOKE BEFORE! [I totally have.] [Oh, and Cartman just caught up to the group, and is suckin’ wind like… something that sucks wind. A vacuum, maybe.] Cartman: Alright… eh… eh… who the BLEEP is Volga, and WHY ARE WE GETTING BLEEPING SHOT AT?! Jared: Volga’s a guy I’m supposed to wrestle. His first name is Tim, which is kind of screwed up. That’s like being Alexei Smith or Nikolai Jones. Or worse, it’d be like naming Sean Connery’s Highlander character Steve. Nobody would take Steve Sanchez Villa-Lobos Ramirez seriously. Cartman: Gee, that’s nice, but it still doesn’t answer WHY THE BLEEP WE’RE GETTING SHOT AT! Jared: That might be because I’m not supposed to be here. See, when the Church of the Unholy blinked me into this reality, it probably threw things out of whack. If I could get back to my own reality then the weird stuff would stop. Stan: …you’d be surprised. Kyle: Maybe Dr. Mephesto could help. Stan: Yeah, I bet he’s got something to fix this. Cartman: WE ARE NOT BLEEPING GOING TO DOCTOR BLEEPING MEPHESTO! I AM NOT HELPING SOME BLEEP-SUCKING, BLEEP-LICKING BLEEP-BLEEPER GAY WRESTLER DO ANYTHING! [Oh, you know it’s coming.] Cartman: Screw you guys, I’m going home. ---=====--- Larry Spiderman gently set the script down on the coffee table, his eyes heavy with sleep and his mind full of angry thoughts. He debated trying to call Jared once again, but given the late hour opted against it. Standing up from the couch and cradling his phone he slowly began the walk to his bedroom, knowing full well that he would have to pick up where he left off the next morning. ---=====--- Kyle: You know, I learned something today... ---=====--- 'No,' Larry Spiderman cried, as he threw his client’s script aside, "no way in hell. There's nothing to learn. This is complete nonsense; it's drivel. I've wasted all this time reading the rants of an unbridled madman, and have taken nothing positive away from it!' 'Larry, is everything alright?' came the voice from the bedroom. It belonged to Melissa Spiderman, Larry's wife. From the tone of her voice it was evident she'd just woken up. 'Yeah,' Larry replied, his voice much calmer, 'I was just finishing up something from a client of mine. Sorry if I woke you.' Dressed in a lavender bathrobe and brushing the sleep out of her eyes Mrs. Spiderman stepped into the living room, clutching her husband's Blackberry. 'No, it wasn't you. Your phone was ringing off the hook, so I answered it. It's someone from Sin Wrestling, or something. They want to talk to you about a guy named Jared.' Larry smiled as his wife handed him the phone. 'Perfect timing. First thing I'm going to tell them is that no-one should ever be subjected to reading anything with Jared Sykes' name on it,' he paused, 'Ever.'
