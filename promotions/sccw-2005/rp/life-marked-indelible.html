Life Marked Indelible

Date: 9/8/08-9/9/08 So raise your chin and raise a glass Sing 'Here’s to winning this' again and again Sing 'I don’t care what it costs' Is this the look that sells the skin Are these the names we lose again and again Sing 'I don’t care what it costs' Monday, September 8, 2008 9:57pm EST It hadn’t quite hit him yet. King Blueberry sat quietly smiling in his locker room, still soaked in sweat and staring at the newest of his possessions: the Sin City LiveWire Championship. The leather of the strap was damp from where he’d pressed it against his body on his way back to the locker room, and the plates which adorned it were anything but shiny, dulled by the patches of dried sweat that covered them. If you were to ask him prior to the match whether he expected it to win, the answer would have been an honest no. The reason for this was due to a promise he’d made to Charlotte Ramone before the Heaven and Hell match at the last pay-per-view, All In: he wouldn’t try to hurt her, wouldn’t even try to fight her. He’d hold to a sense of nobility and honor, and whatever the outcome he would accept. He’d lost at All In because of this, and he had expected to lose again tonight for the same reason. When he didn’t, when Vashaun had counted the pinfall, he was more than a little surprised. 'Check it out, dude,' he said, holding out the belt to his friend and associate Blueberry Dragon, 'we’ve gone legitimate!' Dragon had just entered the locker room himself, having gone in search of celebratory sodas. In his arms he cradled as many fifty-cent cans of Coca Cola that his fragile frame could carry (this would be '4'). 'Hooray for legitimacy!' Dragon set the cans down on a bench along the front of the lockers, cracking one open and handing it to King Blueberry. 'Been a while, huh?' 'Six years, boss. This is kind of surreal. I mean, you know I wasn’t planning on doing anything major this time around, hence the silly outfits and equally silly names,' he took a drink from his soda and continued, 'Still, it does feel kind of nice, but...' His voice trailed off. Dragon took a seat on the bench next to the soda. Violent Jesus was preoccupied, quietly dancing his stuffed rabbit Eugene along the floor. 'But what?' 'I don’t know, maybe it’s silly. I kind of feel bad in a way, like I took something that wasn’t supposed to be mine. Charlie seems like a good kid, and I know she’s had some issues lately, so...' Dragon, the sensible one of the group, shook his head. 'Hey, she was prepared to fight you, and if memory serves you did catch a couple punches in the face. Don’t think anything of it. I’m sure she doesn’t hold anything against you.' 'Yeah, I guess.' 'Cool,' Dragon nodded once, punctuating the end of this part of the conversation. 'So, now that this is yours...' 'Yeah?' 'You’re not going to do the ‘Lance Storm’ thing are you? I mean, putting the big Blueberrytown stickers all over the faceplates, please tell me we’re not going to do that.' King Blueberry started to laugh. Blueberry Dragon immediately regretted speaking those last few words. 'That is absolute genius, man. I didn’t even think of that! When Jon-o pulls it out tonight and gets his belt back, maybe we can get him in on it.' Six minutes had elapsed since first the conversation started, and it would be at least another ten or fifteen before the start of the Iron Man Match. It had been a tumultuous evening thus far for King Blueberry, punctuated by a meeting with Desade, an impromptu hallway encounter with Wyatt Connors and his ‘Crusade’, and winning the LiveWire Championship, but for right now King Blueberry was content. Content, and naïve. Tuesday, September 9, 2008 12:37am EST He took no solace in it having it, not in the wake of everything that had transpired, but nevertheless it was his. He’d tried to hide it, tried to stuff it down into the bottom of the silver Under Armor backpack, but somehow it kept creeping up to the top, peeking out through the clothes though it was a child enraptured in a game of hide-and-seek, and when his friends had ventured out into the cool Canadian evening he’d even contemplated throwing it from his hotel balcony. From the balcony he could see the lights of the city, hear the sounds of its people. None of it could shut the voices out of his head. 'And if I offer you the LiveWire Championship later, you do not also want that?' It had been hours since the show had ended, since the arena emptied and the ring dismantled. It had taken him a while to collect his bearings, because in the immediate aftermath of what he witnessed he’d felt as though his mind had shattered. His eyes had blurred, his mouth went dry, and his face burned red in a flush of anger. He had tried to run, but had been restrained, the strength and resolve that lived in the 130-pound frame of his best friend being something he was not prepared for. Thrown into the back of a car, he was sped away from the building before he was given the chance to do something he might regret, and halfway to his hotel, when his nerves had settled and his senses returned, he threw up on the floor of the back seat, the second time tonight that his system had responded in such a violent manner. 'C'mon you fucking fruitcake!' He’d showered twice already since arriving at the hotel, hoping that somehow he could wash away the shame and indignity, and when that failed he went for the only thing that would, at least temporarily, erase his memory. The glass felt cold against this skin of his back as he sat on the balcony of the Delta Ottawa Hotel and Suites overlooking the landscape below. From his balcony he could see the Ottawa River, and Quebec on the other side. He was dressed in only mesh shorts and sandals, and on the floor next to him rested his cell phone. His right hand cradled the mask of King Blueberry, and in his left he held a rather large tumbler of gin and tonic, much more than he probably needed to kill the cells in his brain that he hoped would die. A thought danced in his mind of the last time he’d had gin, when he vacationed in Daytona in 2003. He’d been so intoxicated that he’d called his sister at 3:00am, and left a voice message where he spoke like Muhammad Ali. That hangover lasted two days. He was already on pace to set a new personal 'best'. Tuesday, September 9, 2008 10:31am EST He’d slept on the floor, or at least he thought he had, since that was where he was when he found himself unceremoniously shaken awake by a large bearded man in robes. 'Dude, not so hard. You’re already so,' he paused, the synapses in his brain firing at half-speed, 'spinny.' Lloyd 'Violent Jesus' Sullivan was a strong man, and because of his psychological impairment he was largely unaware of that strength. What he most likely meant to be a gentle prod had come over as more of a hearty throttle. Using the mattress as a source of leverage, the man known as King Blueberry started to pull himself up off the floor. 'What the hell happened in here after we left last night?' Blueberry Dragon’s eyes took in the room with wild bewilderment. 'I took a rape shower or six. Then there was gin.' King Blueberry had made it to his feet, but quickly stumbled, practically falling into the much larger Mr. Sullivan. 'Mostly gin.' 'Oh man, you didn’t call your sister again, did you?' 'I don’t think so. Not quite sure, though. Phone’s got to be around here somewhere. Don’t think I left it outside.' He flopped back to the floor, digging through the tangled mass of bedding that had been his resting spot only moments ago, but he found nothing. Dragon had better luck, finding the phone tucked partially underneath a wet towel. 'Found it.' 'Alright. You do the honors.' Dragon flipped the cover open and immediately started to cycle through the call log. 'Well, you can breath easy, you definitely didn’t call her this time. But,' his voice trailed off. Underneath the spandex facemask his brow had furrowed. 'Who do you know in North Carolina, and why did you call them at 3am?' The remaining color from King Blueberry’s face had drained. He had vague memories of a call he had placed, and in his inebriated state hadn’t thought to remove the entry from the call log. It was evidence of something he did not want his friend to see. 'I... how do you know I called North Carolina?' 'It’s a 919 number. That’s Raleigh. I know because an old friend of mine named Bill lives there. I think you might have met him once or twice.' It was starting to come back to him. He didn’t recall someone answering, but he did have a hazy memory of leaving a voice message. 'Huh.' 'Yeah, well, all that aside you might want to get dressed and grab your stuff. We’re supposed to check out of here in a little less than a half hour, and we should probably get some food in you to soak up whatever’s left of the alcohol in your system. Lloyd and I already packed. Our stuff is in the next room ready to go.' Violent Jesus had wandered to the glass doors that opened onto the balcony. The shades had been drawn, keeping the sun from shining in. Without thinking of the consequences he drew them open, letting the sunlight pour into the room. King Blueberry recoiled like a vampire. 'Yo! Too bright, big man, still too bright!' Dragon, as always, was quick to remedy the situation. 'Lloyd, I think we should probably close the curtains. He had a rough night.' 'But Eugene starts everyday with sunshine and a smile,' Jesus’ pleading was childlike, innocent. 'I know he does, Lloyd, but right now we have to make an exception, okay?' Reluctantly Lloyd closed the curtains before collapsing in an empty chair to sulk. His melancholy quickly lifted however, as he soon occupied himself with trying to teach his toy rabbit how to sing 'O Canada.' Dragon turned his attention back to King Blueberry, who was still shielding himself from the light as though it would burn his flesh. 'Yeah,' King Blueberry squinted, trying to force his eyes to focus, 'but why do I see two of you?' 'Probably because you’re still drunk,' Dragon responded with more than a hint of derision. 'You better be careful, you might throw up again.' 'Well, third time’d be the charm, right?' Blueberry fell to the bed with the grace of a stumbling foal. Seated on the edge of the mattress, he ran his hands through his hair, shaking out whatever dust had settled there on the carpet. He inhaled deep, pressed his fingertips into his eyes to rub out the remaining sleep, and exhaled. 'Alright, give me a few minutes to get everything together and we’ll get out of here.' Tuesday, September 9, 2008 2:12am EST The alcohol had gone to his head, and the world had become a blur of color and sound. He’d been slowly sliding to his left for the last twenty minutes, his shoulder now resting against the glass door’s adjoining wall. The muscles in his legs had given up and his limbs now sprawled out before him. His hands were a different story. He still tightly held his tumbler, and more so his mask, the latter of which he now held inches from his face, staring into the empty eye slots. He’d only recently been on the other sides of the tinted plastic, staring out into the penetrating, calculating eyes of Alexandra Pierce. You knew what you were doing when you asked him to schedule the meeting for you. The voice he heard was unspoken yet familiar as it rang true in his head. In moments of vulnerability King Blueberry’s conscience often manifested itself as its own personality, argumentative and confrontational. 'I know.' King Blueberry would often answer it as though it were a real person. You knew that she’d see right through your little façade. You knew that a few millimeters of spandex and plastic wouldn’t be enough to shield you from her. 'I know.' And you tried to play ‘hero’, flaunting your bullshit sense of honor and nobility. She saw right through you. This time he didn’t answer, instead bringing the tumbler to his lips to drink. His motor skills sufficiently impaired, he poured more liquid on his chin and chest than he did in his mouth. And when Jonathan Rhine was minutes away from the end of his career, where were you? Chasing PRIME to the back, leaving the last vestige of true honor and virtue to fend for himself while the vultures picked him clean. 'He... he knew I was going.' A slap on the ankle? A friendly nod? Do you think this excuses you? You are no better than the rest of them. 'That’s not true.' Liar. Still clutching the mask, he raised his right arm to wipe the liquid away from his mouth, then violently throwing the fabric to the other side of the balcony into the corner. His words were quiet, angry yet controlled, a true feat given the amount of alcohol he’d already consumed. 'That’s not true.' Tuesday, September 9, 2008 11:07am EST 'I’ll have the pancakes, crepes, whatever, with strawberries and whipped cream.' King Blueberry looked like a train wreck as he sat hunched over the table, ordering breakfast to a waitress in her early twenties. Given his condition, and the aftermath of consuming so much alcohol, a meal this size was probably not in his best interests, but as his health seemed secondary at the moment he continued with his order. 'Uh, a side of bacon, as crispy as you can make it, scrambled eggs, toast, and just wheel me over a bucket of coffee.' Greasy bacon was most likely the last thing his stomach needed, but he ordered it regardless. Someone had once told him that ‘chewy bacon is for fags’, so he’d made sure to emphasize that it be crispy. The waitress had giggled at his request for a wheeled bucket of coffee, perhaps in an attempt to be polite. She scribbled down the rest of the order and with a quick nod disappeared off to the kitchen. Blueberry Dragon was beaming. The boss, King Blueberry, had decided to wave the 'masks in public' rule, so he was finally able to enjoy a meal without having to navigate a fork around the too-small spandex mouth hole. 'I’m a little surprised at you.' King Blueberry wasn’t expecting his friend to say that, but he was too tired and far too hung over to offer much in the form of resistance. 'Whysat?' 'Your pancakes. You didn’t order blueberry,' Dragon was grinning ear to ear. Now that he was temporarily free of his mask (and his friend was an inebriated shlump) he felt more empowered in mocking the idea. 'Dude, if I eat another blueberry I’m going to kill someone with a shovel. I am so sick of blueberries right now that I can’t even begin to describe it. Oh, and in case you didn’t figure it out yet you’re the one who’ll be driving us home.' 'Yeah, I guessed as much. No vomiting this time though, huh?' 'It’s an Element. You can hose down the floors.' 'I’ve already done that once in the last 24 hours, and I’d really rather not do it again.' Dragon took a sip of his orange juice and sighed. 'Yeah, well, I guess I should just be happy that you weren’t naked when Lloyd and I came in to wake you up.' 'Oh, fuck,' the King muttered under his breath King Blueberry pulled the hood on his sweatshirt up over his head before burying his face in forearms on the table. Dragon was quick to jump on this. 'What? What am I missing? Did you do something last night?' King Blueberry picked his head up, his eyes barely visible over his arm. 'No, worse than that.' Dragon just raised an eyebrow. 'I... okay, fine,' Blueberry fell back into his chair, folding his arms across his chest, 'Back when the whole world thought that Charlie... Desade... Desarlie...' 'I’ve been going with ‘Char-Ade’, myself. It seems to make the most sense.' 'Whatever. Back then I bought her a drink at the concourse before Heaven and Hell. I confessed that I was a ‘naked drunk’.' Dragon was silent at first. When he burst into raucous laughter it attracted the attention of the entire restaurant. King Blueberry could only bury his face in hands. 'She’s probably got that in a file on me somewhere. I can just imagine it, all encrypted and written in big words that nobody uses in casual conversation. As soon as we get home we’re putting on a pot of water, and we’re boiling the title belt.' 'I’m sorry, we’re doing what?' 'Boiling it. It’s evil, tainted. We’re going to cook the Desade right out of it.' 'Would you like me to heat up some wire with a torch and poke it to see if it jumps?' Dragon continued to get in his jabs where he could. King Blueberry was less than amused. 'Oh, this is the worst day ever.' Tuesday, September 9, 2008 3:08am EST The tumbler was empty and lying on its side, the last remnants of liquid pooling from the occasional drip seeping from the lip of the cup. Next to it laid King Blueberry, fumbling awkwardly for his cell phone. He flipped open the lid of the sleek, black Samsung and started scrolling through the names in the address book. Charlie PS. The PS was an abbreviation for 'Potato Salad', used as the destination on a rented tour bus years before comedian Ron White would popularize the expression for other reasons. Cinder Dave. King Blueberry figured that a call to Dave at this hour would at least earn him a verbal beating, and maybe a punch in the face the next time they met face to face. Gabe. Gabriel Simon and his wife Jamie had an 8-month old baby boy. Odds are good that one of them was probably awake, but even a drunk King Blueberry wouldn’t drunk dial in this situation. He started flipping through names in the opposite direction, and paused on one that looked for a moment to be promising. The entry read one word: Asshole. He’d all but forgotten he had Wyatt Connors cell phone number, back from the days when Wyatt was serving as an agent for Charlie and Dave when they wrestled as 'Crash and Burn'. King Blueberry quickly settled on not calling this number, as it would probably lead to another less-than-pleasant encounter. Besides, it was most likely that Wyatt had either blocked the number or changed his own, given he was not a particularly well-liked individual. 'Fuckin’... fuck,' the words were just a general expression that meant for anything in particular. Dissatisfied with the names in his address book, he began punching in numbers from the keypad. Tuesday, September 9, 2008 3:14am EST He wasn’t aware of the phone until the second ring, having thought the first to be part of his dream. When the third ring sounded he’d regretted turning the volume up so high, as it was preventing him from getting back to sleep, though he knew it would only ring once more before the voicemail caught it. Beep. 'Hi, you’ve reached the home of doctor Sebastian Parker. I’m not home right now, but if you’d like to leave a message...'
