X (In Judge We Trust, Part 3)

13 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/13/2009 01/18/2009 The newspapers have been running the stories for days about the break up of the crime fighting team known as the Rat Pack. Each day I read the newspaper it makes me even more annoyed by the day. Today’s headlines, however, take the cake. Captain Velocity has decided to pander to the media with his doctrine of "peace" and it’s making me look like a douche for going against what he preaches. ‘JUDGE A CRIMINAL ACCORDING TO CAPTAIN VELOCITY’ ‘PRO-LIFE GROUPS ENDORSE VELOCITY’ ‘CAPTAIN VELOCITY FORMS PEACE SQUAD’ ‘WILL THE RAT PACK EVER COME TOGETHER AGAIN?’ The more and more I read, the more I get sick to my stomach. Everything that the Rat Pack stood for is being distorted by one individual’s self righteousness. As I look at one more headline, my hand starts to shake. The urge to get violent comes over me. But just as I’m about to go smash up some stuff in my apartment, my girlfriend Amanda comes over to me and sits down next to me on the couch. She puts her arm around me and kisses me on the cheek. The mere touch of her skin immediately calms me from my inner rage. I keep forgetting that she’s going through the same thing. She also was in the Rat Pack as Cat Lady. However she was part of the core group that stuck with the team along with me, the gigantic Abrams, and the homeless crime fighter Vagrant. Us four decided to keep together as a unit. But it seems that some people didn’t want to do that. Instead they wanted to do whatever they pleased and make excuses about it later. She went through this same experience with me and it’s brought us closer. I just wish we didn’t have this burden to carry. Tonight we have a meeting set up with Abrams and Vagrant, the other two ‘sane’ heroes in the old Rat Pack. Hopefully everything goes according to plan. Otherwise, Captain Velocity is going to ruin this city once and for all. That is an option I cannot let happen at any cost. * * * 8:45 pm Inside the secret hide out of my crime fighting group… We’re all a bit annoyed by the events of the past week as it relates to the Peace Squad and all of their media attention and interviews. It’s become a farce in our eyes. The costumed members of my group seem a bit unnerved. The members are the giant camouflaged hero Abrams, the homeless crime fighter Vagrant, my girlfriend Amanda who is also known as Cat Lady, and of course myself. The topic of discussion has degenerated into a Peace Squad bash fest. "I can’t believe that stupid Peace Squad and their bullshit," I mutter out with a snarl. "They act like they did all the crime fighting and that we’re just mere criminal pawns who were their sidekicks. What a fucking joke." "Judge, calm down," Cat Lady says in a peaceful voice. Of course I keep on ranting. "No, I will not calm down. That Captain Velocity is driving me insane. All he does is speak like a self righteous prophet. What an annoying man he is. His costume is so lame. He looks like Captain Marvel but even more stupid." "I agree," Abrams growls. "He’s making me sick, too. His pandering to the public is only making things worse." "He likes to run his mouth about not killing as the crime rate is sky rocketing ever since we split the team up after the stupid vote. This town has gone to hell ever since that happened. You’ve seen it, we’ve all seen it." "So what’s your plan?" Cat Lady asks ion an inquisitive tone. "We form our own team and while they’re too busy doing public relations, we go out and actually clean up this town like crime fighting heroes are supposed to." "Yeah, but we need a name," she responds. "Sure we do but that can wait," Abrams replies. I shake my head. "No, it can’t." "Why not?" she asks. "Because the more time we spend here debating, the more time the Peace Brigade is out there acting like a church congregation in costumes. This city doesn’t need that. We need to get sorted out now before it gets any worse out there." "Is it really getting worse, Judge?" I go over and turn on the television. As the screen turns on, the first thing that we all see on the news is Captain Velocity running his mouth yet again. "This city is safer with crime fighters who let criminals live," he says with a smile, winking at the camera. "The days of the vigilantes and the criminals are over. Life is the most precious commodity in this world and the Peace Brigade promotes life and the saving of lives in all its forms." I can only shake my head as he continues his rambling. "We have been endorsed by the Christian Coalition and many major pro-life groups in this country and around the world! We have been embraced…" At this point I walk over and shut off the television before turning back to the rest of the group. "We need to form our group NOW before it gets even worse." Abrams nods his head. "Agreed," he replies. "Now we need a name." "Hmmm…" I look around for anything that could be used. "How about Justice League of America?" Cat Lady asks with a grin. I can only shake my head at this. "No," I reply. "There’s already a Justice League in the comic books." "Oh…" Just then, a light bulb goes off in my head. BINGO! I have a winner. Looking at my group mates I smile and blurt out the name that is in my head. "Justice Brigade." Everyone stops and looks at me. They think for a moment before anyone responds. "Yes," Abrams replies. "I like it. Sounds bad ass." "Me too," Cat Lady replies. I then look over at Vagrant who is going through the garbage. He turns to me and nods his head. "Justice Brigade it is, then," I say with a smile. "I think it’s time we show the Peace Squad just how to bring just to a city full of sinners." Everyone is all smiles. However Velocity won’t be smiling for too much longer. This city is ours and his little group of pansies had better not get in our way. Justice will be served and so will a few deaths along the way.
