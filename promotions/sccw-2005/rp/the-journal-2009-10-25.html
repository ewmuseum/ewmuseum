Date: Temptation: Houston A single strand of dark hair hung across her pale face. For Quinn Gregory, she had seen so much in her life that nothing would surprise her at this point. Having a mother like Alexandra Pierce surely saw to that. Dust settled around her as she tossed another raggedy box against the wall and wiped the beads of sweat that formed on her forehead. In true Quinn tradition, once Halloween had finished, she saw to cleaning her house, preparing it for Christmas and the impending New Year. She tore the frayed top off a cardboard box and her head snapped back from the packets of dust that went flying her way because of it. “Jesus,” she remarked under her breath as it must have been a long time since she had opened this box. Most of the boxes in her attic had been shuffled from house to house without her even perusing their contents. To do so would open up wounds she didn’t want to deal with, not yet at least. To say her life had been fucked up beyond all belief would be the understatement of the year. Books filled this box and she debated even going through them, but figured that she might find a hidden treasure in here that hadn’t been read in quite some time. Quinn was an avid lover of all books and was always game for settling down with them and a glass of wine. She started to tear through the box, finding nothing that really caught her eye until she reached the bottom of the box and felt a leathery book on the other end of her hand. Her brow furrowed before pulling the book out and looked over it for a moment. The book in her hand was unmarked and clearly unread. A red ribbon was tied neatly across the front of it and Quinn slowly undid the knot, lost as to what this book could be. As she opened the front cover, a wave of tension hit her stomach, and she found herself a little nervous. This was a feeling that the daughter of Alexandra normally did not encounter. Her eyes scanned the inside cover and found nothing at all. The first page was blank as well. She turned the page and found an inscription on it, written in black ink that looked faded. Dearest Quinn, I know it will be quite some time before you read this book. I’ve lived with that reality. By the time that you’ve read this, many of the questions that you once asked me will be long forgotten, but still unanswered. I hope reading this book will explain everything, and if it doesn’t, I’m sorry. But, I know I owe you at least something for the live you have had to endure. I could offer apologies, but they would be for nothing. Forgive me for the pain that I brought and for the love that I could no longer hide. Yours forever, Craig A tear welled up in her eye as she read the inscription and found her fingers grazing across the lettering. Craig wrote those words. The wave of emotions that hit her was unlike any other that she had experienced. Her mind started floating back to days she long thought forgotten and found them as fresh as ever. She had done her best to bury the past, but there it sat in her hands once again. She missed him so much. This was Craig’s book and it was for her. She moved to the nearby wall and brought her knees up to her chest. She wanted to read it, to remember, and to know what was in Craig’s mind during the most horrible time in her life. Her eyes fluttered towards the top of the page and saw that the first entry was dated in 2009. Thirteen years had passed since Craig started writing in this book. In that moment, Quinn couldn’t believe so much time had passed, and wondered where the last thirty years of her life had gone. That thought faded away though, as she found herself lost in Craig’s words, and those memories turned into feelings. How she missed him so much. *** I made so many mistakes in my life that it’s truly scary that I made it as long as I have. I’ve stared down the barrel of a gun more times than I care to remember. Somewhere along the way, I actually started to care though. I don’t really remember when or why though. Well, that’s actually a lie. It was sometime after you were born. You were honestly one of a kind, Quinn, and all I wanted to do was protect you. As I write this, I don’t think I’ve done such a good job of that. Your mother changed me for better or worse. She’s like me in a lot of ways, which I don’t think really surprises you. We like to see reactions, see the changes in things. We want to know how things work and if we mess with them a certain way, what will happen. That knowledge sticks with us for a lifetime. The experiences that we have had are far greater than anyone else in this world, and when you really look at it, that’s sad. The year is starting to wind down, and a difficult decision is staring me in the face. I’m going to be honest with you; my time is not for long. I’ve known that for a while, but the dreams I keep having, they’re getting worse and worse. By the time you read this, everything will have happened, and all will be good in the world. It’s the sacrifices I’ve had to make to get it to this point that hurts me the most. I’ve made sacrifices ever since you were born. They’re all because, and for, you. I’m sure you remember Nigel Kensington quite well. Annoying prick, but resolute nonetheless. For a week, I couldn’t move from the bed. Getting shot and stabbed a few times will do that to you. I knew he was getting angrier with each passing day. I had made him a promise and up to that point, I had failed to take care of that problem. I knew that the darkness in me was itching to take over, it always was. You’ve seen that darkness, and I’m sorry that you did. The phone rang non-stop that entire week. I refused to answer it, but that didn’t stop Nigel from coming to see me. He wanted to talk and judging from the voicemails he left, he was angry. Nigel wanted that darkness out. Unfortunately, I obliged. *** Knock. Knock. Knock. Craig’s red eyes flashed open without a warning. The incessant beating of knuckles against the wooden door irked him for reasons best known only to him. His body ached with pain and screamed in torture. Yet, Craig still found the strength to roll out of bed. His arms hung lifelessly next to his torso and judging from the pulsating marks on his body, Craig had just been through hell. The bedroom door flung open as more knocks were heard on his front door. His feet pounded against the wood steps and found himself in front of the door moments later. Craig opened the door and looked at the man who stood before him. Nigel Kensington. “Doc, I’m judging by the fact that you’re standing here that you didn’t take me ignoring your phone calls the way I intended for you to do so,” Craig spoke at first before moving to the side. The sweltering heat had invaded his home and started to sap his energy out of him. Nigel gingerly stepped inside of Craig’s house and without warning, the door closed behind him. Nigel looked at Craig up and down for a moment. “Glad to see you’re in top shape, Craig.” The formalities were gone with Nigel as tone was biting to say the least. “I’ve always said that you should be a comedian, Nigel. Just seems to fit you better.” There was no answer from Kensington and Craig walked over to his kitchen where he opened the pristine white refrigerator door and pulled out a bottle of water. “Thirsty?” The question lingered for a moment before Nigel shook his head. Craig just nodded in response and tore the top off the bottle and felt the cold liquid splash against his tongue. “Alright, let’s get to work, Nigel.” “Doctor Kensington, Craig.” “Yeah, yeah, whatever. I’ve got a busy day in front of me, so if you could speed this up, that would be great.” Nigel folded one of his legs over the other one and took a long look at Craig. “I signed you to a contract because you promised me that you could take care of my problem with Miss Pierce. I’m starting to get a bad feeling that this promise that you made to me is going to go unfilled. In a way, I’m here to receive payment on a debt that I feel is long overdue.” The water nearly flew out of Craig’s nose as he laughed and chortled. “Man, Doc, you almost had me going there.” Yet, as Craig looked into the eyes of his boss, he realized that this wasn’t the case in the least bit. There wasn’t an ounce of humor left in Nigel’s body. Alexandra and the Hand had pushed him to this point. Now, he was sitting in his living room. “What do you want me to do, Doc?” Craig’s question was a loaded one and judging from the tone in his voice, it was one that irked him greatly. He didn’t like being disturbed this way and when it came to Alexandra Pierce, he preferred being left alone. Somehow though, he knew that wasn’t ever going to happen. Someone always wanted to tell him how to run his affairs. “Frankly, I’m at the point where I don’t care any longer. I don’t want them around anymore. Take care of it or I will find someone who can,” Nigel bit back and frankly shocked Craig. Craig took a long moment to let those words settle for a minute, wanting Nigel to take them back. “Nige, that’s not a game you want to play with any longer, okay? Don’t make demands like that to me. I take care of things on my timetable.” Nigel instantly stood up, his back rigid, and his suit still looking rather dapper on him. “You’ve mistaken my kindness, Mister Maloof, for playing a game. I was brutally attacked by Stevens. I’ve been mocked and openly made fun of by Amy Campbell and Alexandra Pierce. I’m no longer interested in watching them walk around my backstage area. You led me to believe you were the one to take care of this. Are you or are you not?” “I’m taking care of it.” “Like hell you are!” Craig’s red eyes shot towards Kensington. “Doc, I’m doing my best.” “Your best, frankly, is a load of crock then. I see that I will have to find someone else to take care of this problem for me then. Don’t bother returning to work, Craig.” The pleasantries had been thrown out of the window by this point and Craig could feel the anger boiling under the surface as his hand wrapped around the plastic bottle. “I don’t know what kind of nerve you have, Nigel, to come to my house and make demands of me in my own living room.” “The kind of nerve that is apparently going to get something done for once. I’ve let too many people convince me that they could handle these people, and I obviously see that’s not the case. My turn then and I will get the job done once and for all. I thought you had the gall to actually do something about it. I see I was wrong.” Craig wanted to throw the water bottle at Nigel’s head, but somehow found the strength to fight that urge. His red eyes looked down at his marble counters and he took a few breaths in. “Fine.” There was a darkness to his tone and Nigel took a long look at Craig. The man he saw stared at the counters with this possessed look in his eyes. It was shocking to see as his chest rose at an alarming rate. It was in that moment he knew he had the real Craig, nothing less. “You want things taken care of Nige? You got it.” Craig finished off his water bottle and walked out of the room, leaving Nigel all alone. This wasn’t what he had anticipated when he made the decision to come over here. He wasn’t sure quite yet if it had the desired effect either. *** In my life, I have so much to be sorry for. It’s one of those things that when you feel the end coming, you start reflecting. I don’t know why, so don’t bother asking me. For some reason, I wanted to save your mother. Maybe if I saved her, she could love me again. That’s what it always boils down to; love. Love is that big great mystery in life that you just want to solve, you want to be in on the secret of it all, but sometimes, love isn’t meant for you. Sometimes, love just is meant to be a mystery and you’re supposed to struggle at that aspect of it. I didn’t want to fail in that department. Not when so much mattered. The weight of the world pressed against your back is only manageable if you have someone there to help you carry it every now and then. Alex was supposed to be that person, she even told me that herself. Somewhere along the way, she decided that she wanted to either be that weight or that she could be that person. When she realized that neither of those roles was hers for the taking, she snapped mentally. When prophecies linger in your mind, things can turn ugly. They can make you do things in this life that you would regret otherwise. Yet, when you bring in prophecies and fate into the mix, you think you’re justified. Alexandra will always think she’s in the right for what she did that it was what she was supposed to do. I don’t think that’s something she’ll ever be able to truly grasp. When Nigel knocked on that door, I didn’t know what to do anymore. I think I had stalled for long enough and now I knew I had to finally do something about her. The truth was that I didn’t think I could stand by and watch her getting hurt by someone else, to see the pain in her eyes, and be so disconnected from it all. No matter what, my heart would side with your mother. If I was the one doing the damage though, I could force myself to hate her even though, deep down, that was far from the case. Nigel wanted that darkness out of me, something I had hidden for so long, and for some reason I let it out. Fear ruled that day, and because of that, a lot of people were hurt in the process. Most importantly, you were hurt in that process and I can never forgive myself for that. At the end of the day, I thought I could save her. I thought that I could fix everything. I was so wrong. *** As Craig stood out on the balcony, the moon had come out and was shining brightly upon his face. Millions of thoughts had raced through his mind by this point in the evening and with the glass of brandy in his right hand, only more would come. To make the decisions he had to make in life, sometimes he needed to take time and really process it all. Growing older, becoming colder to life around him, none of it had made everything easier and sometimes, he really hated that. To disconnect, meant not being in control, and he had fought for so long to have that control. On this evening, he knew he was giving it up. A phone sat on a nearby table and he dreaded the phone call he was about to make. Yet, he knew there were no other options. He had been pushed into a corner and either had to act, or be left behind for good. He couldn’t afford that second option and had to live with the choices of the first one. He knew that whatever happened, it would ruin any chance for forgiveness. Some would say though that the chance for forgiveness had long since passed. Craig lifted the phone off the table, dialed in a phone number, and placed it against his ear. It rang a few times before the person o n the other end answered. “Hello?” It was a rough, raspy voice. The two barely even knew each other, but there was enough there. That’s all that really mattered. “Hey, it’s Craig.” The words just sat there for a moment before the guy responded on the other line. “Well, fuck, not who I expected to be calling me.” “Yeah, I know. Look, I need some help with a problem of mine, and there’s only one person around that I know that can make sure the job actually gets done.” There was silence on the other line at first. “Sounds like you’ve got some serious shit going down.” “Yeah, I do.” “That’s the kind of shit I like to take care of. Count me in.” *** That’s how it all began. That first phone call, that was to Alias. During the war, he was the one that kept pushing me harder and harder. I became ruthless because of him. Wait, that’s not fair. I was always ruthless. He just kept pushing me down that road when it seemed like I was about to be anything close to being soft. He would always rattle off about how this is what we need to do, that this is what we were put on this world to do. Nothing would have gotten this ugly if I just walked away from it all. Yet, I had to be part of it. I guess it was just pride by that time. Quinn, I don’t know how it could have been you during all of this. I know you were loyal to your mother and Amy. Yet, at the same time, you wanted to be around me as well. I don’t know if it’s because you suspected something or if you just wanted to feel safe. I don’t know if I will ever know to be honest with you. I just know that if it wasn’t for you, I would’ve been worse off. There were times that I had to look at the love side of things and it really made me take a step back. This is something that you might think is kind of a loser thing, but when it all began, I started taping my wrists. By this point, I was training you and the two of us were able to spend more time together. Each evening, as I got ready, I wrote the very same thing on my wrists to remind myself of why I was doing this. Q.G. You were so much of the reason why I kept my head on straight. To make this world better for you. Each day, I’ve had to live and ask myself if the things I did were pushing you away from me and making you even colder to this world. I have to think though that you turned just fine. Nigel awoke a darkness that evening, a darkness I never wanted to bring back out. I’m not sorry for my actions, please don’t get me wrong. Things had to be done and eventually, your mother just made things far too difficult to deal with normally. In a way, your mother wanted to just fight. She was hurt too, you know? I don’t know what you know, but that’s why I hope you will finish this book one day. I know it won’t be easy for you, but this is everything you need to know. Your mother, she stopped listening to reason at this point. With everything going on, it’s hard to imagine her going down a different path. I did feel for her. Still, at the end of the day, Nigel got exactly what he wanted. A war. *** I truly love my job. Really, what isn’t there to love? The darkness surrounds me and I just wait, linger in the shadows? Why do I do it? Because he asked me to, that’s why. Craig has big plans for me, I know that much, and this is just the first step in all of it. I know that the only way anything is going to get done in the timeframe that he wants me to do it in is to go this route. It’s why he picked me over anyone else to do it. I just stand there and wait, eventually minutes turn to hours, and the hours start to feel like forever. I don’t care. I just wait. During my wait, I begin to replay the memories of Craig finding me. I really just wanted to fuck him as hard as I possibly could. I was in Scotland or somewhere along those lines, drinking as much as I could possibly get my pretty little hands on. I felt so lifeless during this time period. The cutting wasn’t helping at all and it just felt like I was going to wonder around helpless through it all. Then, he found me. It was sometime between me unhooking my bra clasp and pulling off my pants that he saw the scars. So many people before him had been scared of them. Not Craig though. On that very night, I felt like my life was given back to me, and for that, I’m forever in his debt. Almost without warning, I hear the clicking of the front door shutting upon itself. The owner of the house was here and that made me a little excited. Tonight was just the warning shot. He trusted me for this, and I was honored to make sure it was accomplished like he wanted it to. I hear the footsteps up to the bedroom and can almost sense the flickering of the light switch before the thought even crosses her mind. “Don’t turn on the lights,” I whispered, knowing they could hear me. No weird movements were made and as they moved towards the bed, I knew that this was not completely unexpected. “Alexandra, I come with a message. Just hear me out.” Fin.
