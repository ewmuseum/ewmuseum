However, the roleplays themselves when viewed via the board and/or the main page latest 5 links should look magnificent. --------------------------------------- * * * * * Today Leaning forward, Clinton Sage turns on the FUSE Promo Camera that had been sent to him. It seems the higher-ups at the company have been wanting him to trash-talk his opponent, and considering he wasn’t allotted any on-card air-time aside from his match against Timo Bolamba, he took them up on the offer. For a minute, even with the camera rolling, Clinton Sage said nothing. He stared down at his hands, lost in thought. Perfect, serene silence. If he had been wearing a wedding ring, he would be turning it right now, because you can tell his mind isn’t where it should be. The eyes had seen something they shouldn’t have, and it managed to change the man known as ‘The Seventh Sin’. When Clinton Sage looked up, his eyes were vacant. They were perfectly clear, black from the edges of his irises to the centers of his pupils. Empty. Lost. His voice was quiet, broken, "There are lines drawn in the sand, lines that we’ve always told ourselves that we would never cross. Everyone’s lines are special to them, a moral boundary where, if crossed, you could never turn back from." Once again, another minute passed in silence. Clinton’s eyes hovered around his home, never actually looking directly into the camera lens. If you could put a word to it, it’d be shame. Whispering, "Crossing that line, even through indirection or by mistake, still puts you at an impasse. How do you change your life back to what it was? Do you go back to what it was? Can you go back?" Staring up at the ceiling, possibly prying through the wooden beams and the tiled roof, out past the clouds that hung overhead, through the night sky, towards Heaven, Clinton Sage continued to wonder aloud. The camera caught every movement, every twitch in his eyes and his hands. "Is it worth going back?" he said aloud, voice now more stern and filled with internal conflict, "No…nothing can ever change. Crossing a line is only a sign of advancement. You take what you’re given, because you can’t change the past. You accept who you were, and what you’ve become since then." Nodding along with his thoughts, possibly gaining either acceptance from the heavens above, or disapproval, Clinton Sage’s demeanor started to clear. It started to find footing, find strength, and accept whatever it is that had been troubling him. His obsidian eyes found the camera, squinting as he smiled. Self-realization, acceptance, or whatever you want to call it. It had suddenly overtaken Clinton Sage. The FUSE Universal Champion looked down at his hands once again, opening and closing them. The emotionless stare that had befallen his hands only minutes earlier was now completely gone, instead filled with a different emotion: Adoration. Worship. Infatuation. Passion. "For months and months, I’ve stormed through the wrestling ranks in FUSE. These hands have choked out nearly the entire roster, forcing them to either realize they can’t beat me, or forcing them to succumb to unconsciousness just to simply escape my wrath." Clinton Sage continued to stare through the camera. Coal eyes, stoked to a flame of hate. "Smitty thought he could ruin my second Universal Title reign by throwing me to the wolves, yet the only thing he’s been sending my way are the sheep. Fred Fusion? Dean Jokerlin? A woman?!?! And now, I have no doubt when I say this, he’s sent me the bottom of the barrel in Timo Bolamba. Someone who clings onto his past greatness as if it should be the current foundation for what everyone judges him upon." He pauses, licks his lips, teases an affectionate glance down at his hands, then returns his stare back towards the promo camera, "Yet, he can’t even beat Jonathan Rhine. Timo can’t even beat Tanaka, or anyone else mired in the mid-card of FUSE’s horrific attempt at a roster. So Smitty thinks he can ruin my title reign by sending me Timo Bolamba? Fuck you, Smitty. Fuck you, Bolamba…I feel retarded even trying to pronounce your God damned name. You both don’t realize what you’ve gotten yourselves into, and I find it humorous." Sighing aloud, Clinton Sage thrusts himself backwards in the chair, but not before once again flicking his gaze down towards his hands. Whatever it was that had troubled him at the start was now gone. Whatever horrible thing that his eyes had seen, or his hands had done, was now completely eroded away and gone forever. The metamorphosis was complete. "Do you know why nobody can beat me? It’s because they’re all afraid of me. And do you know why nobody cares what Timo Bolamba does? Because you haven’t made any sort of impact, other than losing more times in a month than I have in my entire FUSE career. Hell, probably my entire wrestling career. I’m the god-damned gold standard around here, has been, not you!. I don’t see Jonathan Rhine cowering in fear, hiding somewhere in his rookie locker room because he beat you, and do you know why?" Clinton waits a beat, then leans forward and inches closer to the camera. "Because you’re Timo Bolamba. You’re someone, when defeated, doesn’t do a God damned thing about it. I haven’t even faced Jonathan Rhine, and he already knows what it feels like to be on the wrong end of a Clinton-gets-pissed beatdown. Everyone wants to beat me but the fun fact is nobody has the balls to do it. You pin my shoulders to the mat, and I make your life a living hell. It’s now common knowledge that Clinton Sage losing is just about the worst-fucking-thing that can happen to your career." He holds up one finger, "Jacob McKail. Gone." Another finger, "Ethan Frost…hey, he can’t even hold onto the LiveWire Championship. And he has blue hair, for crying out loud." The third finger, "John Covel. Does everyone see the jealousy in his eyes whenever the crowd cheers for anyone BUT him? I peeled back the solitary layer of personality he had, and exposed him to the world as the bland-wannabe-superstar that he dreams of escaping." Standing, Clinton Sage steps towards the camera, close enough so that the only thing filling the frame are his two hands. He turns them over like a magician, and continues to verbally flay Timo Bolamba and any chance he might have at victory. "These hands choke the reality out of everyone. You’re no different, Timo. Neither are you, Smitty. Sometimes life throws you a curveball, and you’re changed forever. Sometimes you’ve got it going so great, you don’t even realize it until you’ve crossed that line you said you would never cross. Suddenly, you have to adapt and evolve. I change lives, Timo. When you scrape yourself off of the mat after I throttle you inside the ring and choke you out, you’re going to have a question to ask yourself… Why would anyone ever want to see Timo Bolamba in a wrestling ring again, let alone as FUSE Universal Champion?" The hands clench into fists, before stepping off-screen a second before the feed cuts out. "Last week, something changed me forever. This week, it’ll happen to you." And then silence. * * * * * 74 Hours Ago "I’m no killer. I’ll never be a killer, and nothing you say or do will turn me into one." * * * * * 72 Hours Ago Isis screamed as the ice pick sank into her abdomen, more out of shock than actual pain. The pain was sharp, momentary, but it faded away as the steel shaft sank deeper and deeper into her, and undoubtedly, the unborn child growing inside of her. A warm, numb sensation overtook her as the stranger slammed her head backwards, knocking her dizzy as he smashed it against the wall. She could feel the warmth running down her lower stomach, pooling at the top of her jeans and then dripping down her leg. All the breath had left her. The tears formed in her eyes not from the pain, but from the realization that her baby was more than likely dead. With the world fuzzy around the edges, disoriented and stricken with instant grief, Isis Hollinger didn’t want to stare down at the wound. She was afraid of what she’d see, perhaps a little unformed arm dangling out through the small wound, or perhaps blood squirting out like a kitchen faucet. When Clinton Sage distracted the bastard with the ice pick, swinging the baseball bat at his head, the madman who stabbed her turned and drove a swift, accurate jab into Sage’s throat. The baseball bat clattered to the ground next to Sage, who dropped to his knees, before receiving a rising knee to the face, and then a closed fist to the back of the head. "What the fuck do you think you’re doing?" the baby killer growled, stalking Sage for a moment, circling him like a bird of prey might circle the next meal. Sage’s voice was humbled, sounding disoriented. Perhaps it was Isis’ own dizzy mind, swirling around the loss of her unborn child or the head smashing this old man with salt-and-pepper hair had given her, "…no killing. No killing. Don’t kill them, Raines, please." And then Isis looked down, right at the spot where the Obstetrician had placed her hand earlier in the day. The ice pick was still dug deep into her abdomen, the wooden handle covered in bloody fingerprints. Blood was everywhere. Her shirt was soaked with it, her jeans were almost blackened with it, and the wooden planks of the cabin porch were now splattered with it. As the world began to close in from its edges, Isis noticed that the blood seemed darker than normal. Trying to keep her balance by leaning against the wall, she dared not remove the ice pick. The blood was darker, she thought, because it came from two bodies. Her own, and her unborn child’s. She could feel it’s life leaving her through a small hole in her abdomen, the blood of her baby was now pouring down her front, soaking her jeans, and forever staining the wood of their porch with a life that never was to be. There was no physical pain. Just a numbness to everything, as the blackness started to cloud more and more of her vision. Blurred by tears and impending death, Isis watched as the unknown murderer named Raines delivered a knockout blow to Clinton Sage, before turning his attention towards her again. Shaking violently, she screamed at the top of her lungs. She knew Nathan was nearby, sleeping. She had to save him, wake him, so he could get the guns and protect himself. She was a lost cause, so was their miracle baby, but if she used every ounce of her strength to save Nathan, it would be worth it. The man Clinton Sage had called Raines slammed her head against the wooden wall of the cabin once more, before wrapping his coarse, calloused fingers around her throat. She felt light, almost as if she were walking on air. It was serene, peaceful, although momentary. Raines yanked the ice pick out of her abdomen, staring at the metal spike with gray eyes that resembled two knives of iron forged in the fires of Hell. He stared at Isis until she could see him somewhat clearly, waiting for her dizziness to pass before he stuck out his pointed, pink tongue and licked the ice pick clean. Raines smiled, the blood of her baby staining his teeth. Suddenly, she felt as if she were floating. She could no longer feel her feet, and coldness began to wash over her. The world continued to shrink, even though her eyes were wide open, Isis Hollinger was having a hard time comprehending what was going on. All of a sudden, she was thrown against the fireplace, slamming against the slate hearth with enough force to shatter a few ribs. She slumped to the ground next to her groggy husband, cold from head to toe, the world refusing to darken completely until she saw her husband’s eyes focus on her, widen, and then get crushed under one of Raines’ boots. Floating again, Raines pulled her off of the ground and leaned in close, his breath bearing both the heat and the stench of hell, "Looks like he won’t be saving you, bitch." Anger cleared some of the darkness, peeling it away from the edges as it surrounded her vision. She knew she was going to die, she had lost too much blood and the hospital was too far away. Her Nathan was once again unconscious next to her, this time as the result of a boot to the face. Anger sharpened her instincts, like that of the ice-pick that had started her path towards heaven, hand-in-hand with the baby that would never know what a sunrise looked like, or the crunch of a freshly fallen snow. The darkness peeled away, layer by layer, and she started to see clearly once again. From the corner of her eye, she saw Clinton Sage propping himself up atop the baseball bat, staring in at her with his clear, black eyes. They were filled with sorrow. Anguish. They weren’t the eyes that had raped her months earlier. Sage’s emotions were humbled for a moment, before they were ripped away by anger as well. He jumped to his feet, and charged in through the front door of the cabin, the baseball bat cocked high over his head, ready to smash the back of Raines’ skull. The darkness started to claw its way from the edges of her vision, stalking everything she saw. She could no longer feel the sharp pain in her ribs, or the hole in her abdomen, or the dead child in her belly. Next to her, Nathan lay unconscious. Her beautiful, loving husband. The world dimming, she needed to save him. Somehow mustering enough strength, Isis Hollinger watched as Clinton Sage stormed into the cabin, momentarily snagging the attention of this man Raines, and she grabbed the ice pick out of his hand and thrust upwards with every ounce of strength that her body would allow. Ripping through his shirt on the way upwards, dragging some pectoral skin with it, the ice-pick sank into the neck of Raines, right at the base of the jaw, and the point broke out through the back of his skull. His eyes rolled backwards in his skull, he teetered for a moment, and then fell to the side. Groggy and welcoming the darkness, Isis hadn’t realized how close Clinton Sage had gotten in that moment. He had already started the downward arc of the baseball bat, his black eyes wide with resolve to stop her murder. Sage’s eyes widened in disbelief as Raines fell lifeless to the cabin floor. They immediately closed tightly as Clinton realized he couldn’t stop the baseball bat’s downward momentum, a life altering swing fueled by rage, anger, and a resolve to save a life. Clinton Sage whimpered. I love you, Nathan * * * * *
