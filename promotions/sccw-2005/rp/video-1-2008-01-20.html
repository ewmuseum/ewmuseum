Date: 01/20/2008 The letter that arrived in the mail was addressed simply. It was addressed simply to Nick Black, The School of the Art Institute of Chicago. Nick was a 28 year old art student working on his Doctoral thesis in Digital Cinematography. He was also struggling for money, and for a Doctoral thesis. The letter’s contents would take Nick on a one and a half year long ride into some dark places inside himself, and inside a man he had once considered a hero. Nick Black. One of your professors happens to be an old friend of mine. Dr. Starns and I try to get together once a month or so just to swap stories and catch up on life. We attended high school and college together, and when I made a mention to him of something that has come up in my life recently, he told me that you and I should talk. He mentioned that while I could potentially help you on your thesis, that you could help me with my problem. My name is Edward Carter Lambert. Some people call me BloodAngel because it’s the professional name that I’ve gone by for years. I don’t know if you watch professional wrestling or not, so I figure I’ll give you a bit of background. I’m a big bastard. No, scratch that, I’m a big violent bastard with a drinking problem, a wife and son, and some very bad times in sight. Enclosed is a voucher for a flight to Lexington, two thousand dollars, and my phone number. If you are want more information than what I have put in this letter you have two choices. You can either come to Lexington, or you can stay in Chicago and ignore this. It’s your choice. Yours, Edward Lambert. The letter was intriguing, the money moreso. Two week later, Nick stepped outside of the Lexington International Airport and stared at the cold and dark sky above him. He stood at the curb outside of the airport for somewhere between five and ten minutes before a dark red Ford F-250 crew cab pulled up. The driver, a dark haired man in his late thirties looked out at him through the rolled down window. "You Nick?" he scratched out in his deep gravelly voice. "Yessir." "Good. I’m Edward, throw your shit in the back seat and hop in." Moments later the truck pulled away from the curb, leaving no trace of the young man whose life would be changed drastically by his meeting with a wrestling legend. * * * "Set the fucking camera up." [The voice was deep and harsh. The man it came from stood behind a large oak desk. His black hair was pulled back in a ponytail that emphasized his high widows peak and scarred forhead.] "It’s set up sir." [The second speaker could not be seen as it came from out of view of the camera.] "Good. Now, I assume you want to do a preamble or some such?" [The man behind the desk iss a large and imposing figure. He stood about six foot six and cut a muscular figure in his black button up shirt and khaki slacks.] "Um, sure." [Another figure shuffles into view, this one much smaller. The younger man has spiky blond hair, a nose ring, lip ring, eyebrow ring, and other fucking retarded rings in various places that look fucking stupid.] "This is Nick Black, I’m here interviewing Edward Lambert. He’s a guy…" [The larger man snickered as the kid waved at the camera.] "He’s the guy standing behind you obviously." "Um, right. Anyhow, I’m Nick, he’d Edward, and this is the first tape in what will probably be uh…" [The other man cut in again.] "Several." "Yeah, several videos." [Having said his piece the younger guy dodges out of camera view, and Edward sits down. He reaches his right hand out and lifts a pack of cigarettes up from his desk. As he withdraws one the kid speaks again.] "Would you please not smoke sir?" [Edward stars at the camera, the cigarette now clenched between his lips twitching up and down as he speaks.] "Um. No. My house, my office, my rules. If you don’t want me to smoke you can go back to Chicago. I’m who I am and what I am. These videos won’t be lies. I smoke, I know I smoke to much, I’ve got ample proof of that, but you can fucking deal with it." [Having said his piece he flicks open a Zippo lighter and puts tobacco to flame.] "Right, so you want me to introduce myself or some shit?" "Uh, yeah please." [Edward stares at the camera, smoke wreathing up from the cigarette clenched between his lips and cracks a slight smile.] "Hi there! I’m Edward Fucking Lambert! Whoopedy do! I bleed on command!" [Silence dominates the next few moments before the kid speaks again.] "Urm, is that all sir?" [Edward shakes his head.] "No it’s not even close to all. But I figured I’d act like a jackass to start with and then be serious once I got it out of my system." "Oh, right." [Edward shakes his head and looks at the camera again.] "In all seriousness, Hi. I’m Edward Lambert. You may know me as BloodAngel, BA, Ed, bastard, jackass, or some other shortening or bastardizing of my name. I could give a fuck less what name you call me by. The point of this isn’t up to you. The point of this is up to me. I’m the guy who sets the fucking rules here." [He pauses.] "Oh yeah, I’m also fucking profane, so fuck you if you don’t like it." [The cough that comes from off camera pauses Edward as he talks.] "I’m going to crack a window." [Edward shakes his head as we hear the sound of a window sliding up.] "Fucking non smokers." [He sighs.] "Right, so while Nick breaths his nice clean cold fucking air, I’ll continue. I’m two people in one body. No, not some fucking retarded multiple personality shit, but two people regardless. One of them is a loving family guy. I’m an ok father, a decent husband, and a somewhat likeably guy. Sure I drink a lot, and smoke to much. Sure I’m profane and sarcastic, but overall I’m a pretty likeable guy." [He pauses as he grinds the cigarette held in his right hand out.] "The other guy is also me. As a wrestler I am a violent, sarcastic, unpleasant and mean bastard. I’m a guy who while at times funny and friendly, is more often unpleasant, rude, and willing to do whatever it takes to fuck you up. I’m also pretty fucking good at doing just that." [Edward sighs and glances away.] "I have never claimed to be anything but what I am. I will never be held up to the stars as a shining example of manhood, fatherhood, or of anything else. I have fought my personal demons most of my life, some of them I have defeated, some of them I have lost to." "Just like everyone else." [He glances down at the ashtray before him with a smirk.] "I’ve wrestled for almost twenty years now. I’ve wrestled in bingo halls, I’ve wrestled in high school auditoriums, and in the Mall of America. I’ve wrestled in Canada, Mexico, the USA, and Greece of all places." [If you could call what he does wrestling, most people would call it being a violent bastard.] "I tried to be a nice person once. I tried to be a friendly guy. I made friends and I did my best to keep them." [Edward sighs.] "The problem with having friends in this business is that eventually, all or most of them either retire or betray you. They get hurt, they get sick, they get fucked in the head and go off on some long tangent that leads to them shaving their heads and living with Hari Krishnas." [He grins at that.] "I’ve outlasted all, or nearly all of my contemporaries "When I started in this business I was a kid. Young and full of fire, ready to fuck people up on a moments whim. I was also a raging idiot who had no fucking clue what he was doing. Somewhere in the past twenty years that changed." [A shrug.] "I somewhere I also figured out the meaning of life, and that is one of the few things that I’m going to tell you." [He pauses a moment as he lights another cigarette.] "The truth of life is that nobody, no matter who you are, no matter how important you are, nobody lives forever. We all die. Winston Churchill died. Billy the Kid died. Socrates, Plato, even Jesus Christ died if you believe in him. There is no immortality of the body, no true way to march through the ages unbowed and awake." [Edward hunches forward, the smoke from his cigarette flowing toward the ceiling.] "No matter how you look at it, we are all fucked. This religion says that the only way to eternal life is through cleaving to their doctrine for salvation. That religion says do this differently and live forever. There are so many religions, each claiming their own individual path to life everlasting with but one catch." "First you have to die." [He takes a long and deep draw from the cigarette held in his hand before he continues.] "So, the only way that you can achieve eternal life is through death. And the only way that you can get eternal life is to follow each different religion’s tenets perfectly." [He grins.] "Does anyone other than me see how fucking farcical that is?" "There is immortality. Not physical immortality, but there is immortality regardless. If you don’t believe me, crack open a history book some time. Read it, from cover to cover and tell me the names that stand out. Odysseus, Genghis Khan, George Washington, King George, Adolf Hitler, and Robert E. Lee all achieved immortality. People who moved their times, people who built monuments, brilliant leaders, deceitful schemers, murderers, or kings all of them. Their names will live as long as the memory of men, and considering that we still remember the ancient Pharaohs, that means that it’s a damned long time." "Is there a point to this sir?" [Edward looks over at the kid who just interrupted him and nods.] "Yes, I understand that this seems to be leading nowhere. Indulge me." [Nodding, Edward looks back at the camera.] "People who built great monuments, such as the Golden Gate Bridge have left their marks on the world. Great inventors and architects have constructed wonders that will be remembered long after their creators are dust. So, if you discount the religious nonsense that most people have driven into their heads then the only way to achieve anything close to immortality is by being a genius in your own field." "I am not a general. I am not a statesman. I will build no great works. I will not write the greatest novel. My generation is going to have an incredibly hard time making more than a footnote in history. In one hundred years, nobody will remember me as a father; nobody will remember me as a friend. The only way that I can make an eternal impression is to be the best at what I am." "And what I am, is a violent self serving son of a bitch." "Let’s make no bones about it. My life has been the height of self promotion. I do not want others to miss the truth about me. I want them to understand that I am not a man to emulate. I am not a man to admire. I have worked hard to be where I am, and to be who I am, but I have burned myself at the altar to me. If I am to be remembered, it will be as one of the modern gladiators, a man who risked life and limb just to entertain." "I only hope that this will be enough." [He slams his left hand down on the desk top. Papers shake and the glass ashtray jumps a little.] "People have remarked to me, and about me at times, that I seem to lead little of a life outside of the ring. Yet these same people are those who watch me as I work, they watch me march out to beat the ever loving shit out of people, and they are entertained. They marvel that I focus so much of my life into my work, that in some ways it seems I have little in the way of a personal life." "What they do not understand is that my work is my life. I wake in pain from damage done to my body. I hear screams and cheers in my sleep. I do not work a job, I do not have a career or vocation. I have a life, and my life has a single bloody minded focus. I will be remembered. I will not be one of the faceless forgotten masses that history grinds into dust as a number." "Ten years from now, a hundred years from now, people will be able to watch my work. They may drive a flying car to their store to purchase a memory chip that allows them to watch me beat the shit out of someone like Eryc Morgan, John Adams, Lance Marshall, or Jake Hix. They will remember me. I will not be forgotten!" [He sighs.] "I shall live forever in the memory of those who follow this sport. In a hundred years they will still speak in hushed and reverent tones about my career and my willingness to fuck myself up so that other people can forget that they are nothing. They remember the murderers, they remember the spectacles, and they remember the accidents. They will remember the name Edward Carter Lambert. They will remember the BloodAngel. Like me, Hate me, I do not fucking care how people see me. I am not an icon of fidelity. I have lied, I have cheated, I have done my best to hurt good people. My legacy and my name will live on, not as a good man, but they Will live on." "Because now, that’s all that I have left." [He stares at the cigarette butt clenched between his fingers.] "The problem is these. These fucking addictive, deadly, and foul tasting things." [He flicks the cigarette butt into the ashtray beside him and with a flourish, produces another.] "You see. I’m 38 years old. In this sport, I’m very close to the end of my years. If I hadn’t been as harsh on myself as I have been, I may have another ten years left in me. As it is, with luck, I may have another three or four." "Sadly, because of these..." [He raises the cigarette an takes a long and deep draw of the smoke.] "Because of these, I have, with luck, a year and a half." "I’m dying of inoperable Lung Cancer."
