Why Ibrahim Seck was in the basement

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The truck motor rumbles as it drives down the interstate nearly half the way between Lexington Kentucky, and Ponca City Oklahoma. The two occupants of the black Ford F-150 sit in silence as the miles stretch by slowly. Edward Lambert sits in the drivers seat, one of many cigarettes dangling from his lips. Unlike many of his other trips over the years, on this trip his wife Kia has finally decided to stand firm on her principals. The silence between them could fill volumes, unspoken worries, unchecked heartbreak, and sorrow. For his part, Edward is sorry that he brought his wife along because she always makes him drive the speed limit. For a man who has lived his life as hard and as fast as he can, obeying a speed limit is anathema to him. For Kia, the fact that this for the first time in well over a decade, she is against seeing Edwards darker side come to the forefront. Edward’s dual nature revolves around a mental separation of his personal life, and his professional one… for the most part. When removed from the spotlight of television, Edward stops being the hardcase asshole that he portrays more often than not. However recently, he has been just as much the hardcased bastard personally as he has been professionally. Kia loves her husband, but at times like this it reminds her that life has not always been one of joy and light. Driving to some nowhere city in Oklahoma to see try to track down the man who escaped from their basement makes the situation odd. Knowing that your husband kept a man trapped in a cell in your basement that he never told you about until after the prisoner escaped makes the situation shocking. Knowing that your husband intended to take the fact that there was a man imprisoned in your basement to his grave is terrifying. The fact that the man who was trapped in the basement was once a friend of your husband makes it seem like a bad horror movie. Kia turns to her husband, the father of her son, and once again asks the question that he has flatly ignored each time she has asked it. "Why was Ibrahim Seck in our basement?" This time however, Edward shocks her when he sighs and begins talking. "You remember back when I was chasing the NWC Tag Titles in my idiotic attempt at soloing them?" "Vaguely." "Well," Edward sighs, "What I didn’t tell you is that Seck had finally flipped all the way off his rocker. He had gone psychotic to the extreme. Threats were made, accusations uttered, and… well…" "Well?" "It’s a long fucking story Kia." * * * 10:27pm. January 14th. 2001 Edward Lambert to stands at a bank of pay phones in the middle of an airport; which airport is of no consequence yet the state that Edward is in is physically obvious. Disheveled. He stands slumped and leaning against his chosen phone. The expression of his face is one a far cry from his typical boisterous self. Instead he seems slightly broken down, worn out almost. His massive body is still just as massive, his black hair and goatee are still there, but it seems that there is something missing. Not his red button up shirt, nor the black slacks and black boots. Not the Gargoyle sunglasses, nor even the cigarette which dangles from his lip. One man knows exactly what is missing, and he is far from able to speak at the moment. Men and women pass him by with a wide berth, the elderly couple, the young punk kids, and the couple pushing a baby stroller. Each one of them gives him a wide berth as he stands there. A man who is built like him leaning against a phone booth and looking around furtively is not someone you wish to walk up to and ask to borrow a quarter from. RING! Edward stares at the phone for a moment and begins to reach for it when... RING! This time he grabs the phone from the cradle where it sits and raises it to his ear. "Alan"? The voice on the other end of the phone is that of Edwards mentor, friend, and one of three people he trusts utterly. "Why the hell… no… why the FUCK am I call you at ten thirty at night there?" "Listen Alan, I can't talk long, I needed to tell you that when a large package arrives in the mail tomorrow, have the delivery guys set it inside your basement. Then, put a five-gallon jug of Kool-aid down there and a loaf of bread, then pull the phone out." "What is in this package, and why am I providing it food?!" Alan’s voice is resigned, when you associate yourself with people like Edward Lambert, you get used to very odd requests. Edward leans over the phone and cups his hands around the receiver before whispering almost too quietly to be heard. "Ibrahim Seck." "WHAT?!" "IBRAHIM SECK!" That time, Edward didn’t whisper, instead he bellowed. Several passers by stop and stare at him for a few moments before scurrying on about their ways. A large man shouting an odd name down a telephone in the center of a busy airport is not something that you see every day. Edward stares suspiciously at the people as they walk past him and again turns his attention to the phone where his friend is speaking. "Say that again? We must have a bad connection; I didn't hear you to well." Alan sighs before asking again. "Why is Ibrahim Seck coming into my house and why is he traveling inside a package rather than… say… an airplane, car, or bus?" Edward smiles a bit. "That’s easy, it’s because the burlap sack allowed him too much freedom of movement when he started to resist." "WHAT?" "Let me explain. You see…" * * * 1:49pm. January 14th, 2001. Edward is seated inside what can honestly be called a "Rent-a-Wreck". Rust brown paint covers the sides of this 1993 Buick. Buick what, nobody can tell; all anyone can see is the spots of less faded paint which under the right light, reads "Buic". The K fell off long before the rest of the logo. The rent a wreck pulls along the road, occasionally sputtering and coughing as Edward drives through beautiful down town Ponca City, Oklahoma. Actually the city looks like shit. Just like his car. Edward drives down a small road, headed toward a section of the less than reputable town, which looks more runs down than the rest of the town does. Slowly he passes inner city youth who stare in amusement at the clunker that drives down the streets. Finally he pulls to a stop at the side of the street and gestures toward two of the future inmates of one prison or another. "Hey guys, would either of you happen to know where a guy named Ibrahim Seck lives?" The rock that crashes into the passenger side window of the car puts the short lived conversation to an abrupt end. Edward makes a hasty retreat and guns the clunker down the street. Twice more he pulls up to a corner and asks the same question. Twice more he is met with rudeness that causes him to decide that leaving the scene would be a more health conscious decision. Finally, after about twenty minutes of having various things thrown at him, said towards him, and shown to him, Edward pulls up to where a young man in a sweat shirt is standing beside two younger children. About a block away is a school. Not a very nice looking school, but a school none the less. The chain fencing that surrounds the school has barbed wire atop it. That is never a good sign. Once again, Edward leans his head out of the window and shouts toward the closest pedestrian. "Hey, could you tell me where Ibrahim Seck lives?" The figure turns around just in time to give Edward the finger and turn back to whatever it was doing before with the two smaller children. Apparently it revolves around them giving him money and him handing out two small plastic bags. Edward chooses that moment to once again interject. "Hey, Fuckwad! Do you know where Ibrahim Seck lives?" Edward is a man with a violent temper, one skinny pasty faced youth giving him the finger while he sells crack to school kids does not scare or intimidate him. The two smaller children turn around and start walking away without a glance back at either the figure in the sweatshirt or the large man in the car. Apparently this kind of thing happens every day in Oklahoma. "FUCKWIT! YOU! JACKASS! WHERE THE FUCK DOES IBRAHIM SECK LIVE?" The figure turns around to face him and gives his a baleful stare. From underneath the sweatshirt the youthful face of a kid known as "Jesus Seck Jr." stares out at Edward. He raises his right hand, extends the middle finger, and after showing it to him for a moment, points down the street with it. 'FUCK OFF MAN! Go the Fuck Away!" "Listen shitheel, if you don’t tell me where your old man lives, I’m going to come over there, and show you why you don’t want to tell me to fuck off!' Jesus Seck Junior is not stupid. Why would he want to get fucked up over that crazy old bastard anyhow? "Down the street, turn left. About eleven miles after that turn when you see the trailer park. The basket case lives in number twenty-seven." Edward raises his own middle finger in thanks and roars the Buick back to life… eventually. It sputters and dies twice before it fires again, and takes off down the road headed outside of town. * * * 2:51pm. January 14th, 2001. Edward pulls the Buick into a midsize trailer park. You know the type. Cars are up on blocks, yet the trailers have wheels. Trash litters the entire lot, small children play amidst the garbage that they call a life, and old senile men sit around before their trailers, drinking beer, and spitting on the dogs. Regardless of how they view their lives, just about everyone else views it as pathetic. It is this trailer park that Edward stops the Buick in. After a few moments of waiting to make certain that the car will not explode he steps out of the sagging door, and walks towards the weather-beaten off white trailer that marks number 27. It isn’t much to look at to be certain. A standard singlewide trailer parked on a concrete slab. No car parked out front, no pets to be seen, and all the blinds are drawn shut. It is this trailer that Edward walks up to and knocks on the door of. After a few moments a creaking and rattling come from inside the trailer. "It’s palpably open Scott!" Edward opens the door and steps in. "Oi Rev?" Edward does a horrible imitation of a soccer hooligan’s accent with good reason. Outside on the mailbox there read two names. One name is Scott, the other is Ibrahim. The Scott is probably a man once known as Souser Scott, a Scottish soccer hooligan who is devoted his live to being Ibrahim Seck’s lackey. The other, well is Ibrahim Seck. "Put the Milk in the cold contraption, and come here, this confounded wheel chair is causing my hemorrhoids to act up again." "Roight." "You aren't getting sick are you Scott? You know that in my greatly weakened state I get sick easily, you had better stay outside and sleep on the roof again." Edward steps through the walk in kitchen and makes his way through to the living room section where a man with a wilted pompadour is seated in an older style wicker wheelchair. "Hello Seck." The man in the chair hops up slightly, in fact his feet touch the ground and he physically turns the chair around and reseats himself facing Edward. His face flashes with several emotions, fear, disgust, curiosity, and others. "Eddie Lambert. The Great and Mighty Edward. Come to gloat at a poor cripple? Come to stare and laugh at what your cohorts did to me? Come to stare at Jesus’s fallen brother? The man who was cast down after he was tied to the cross? Oooooh the Shame! The Palpable Palpable Shame! Me a cripple, and you, the man who inflicts pain upon himself in the name of martyrdom able to stand! The Horror! The Palpable Palpable Horror!" "Shut the fuck up." Ibrahim stops mid rant and stares at Edward. "What do you want Eddie?" Edward walks to a beaten, stained brown couch and sits down. He fishes inside his pockets for a moment before drawing out a pack of Lucky Strike Filters, pulls one out, lights it, and commences smoking. Immediately after the first gout of smoke is exhaled Ibrahim coughs ostentatiously and waves his left hand before his face. "Would you mind not doing that? In my condition I…" "In your condition you should get your ass up, and bring me a beer. You are a fraud Seck. A PALPABLE fraud." Edward snorts after speaking and takes another long draw from the cigarette. "I don't allow fermentation of grain nor fruit in this house of the lord." "I’m gonna call bullshit. Mister high and mighty Ibrahim Seck, Mister Gods errand boy, mister look at my pompadour. Scott probably has a twelve pack sitting under the couch so stop with the bullshit Seck." "I left a message on your machine Seck. Someone, and I don’t for the life of me know who that person was, told me that once again you were making threats against my wife. They told me you were calling her the whore of Babylon again. They said that when my son is born, you would…" Edward pauses a moment and shrugs his large shoulders. "Well, it doesn’t matter what they said really Seck. I told you a long fucking time ago to leave my wife alone. I told you before that to leave me the fuck alone. I told you that repeatedly in fact; in terms both pleasant and… violent. So, let me again remind you of your place in our relationship." Edward stands up, places both hands on Seck and physically hauls him out of the wheelchair into a standing position. 'You still feeling like a cripple?' There stands Seck in his full glory. Sweat pants, sweatshirt, and pompadour. This is the public image that Ibrahim Seck wishes to project to those around him. The image of a broken and sickly man who could not hurt a fly; the image is a lie. "I…I…" then with a thud Ibrahim Seck falls to the ground and clasps his hands together before him. "It’s a PALPABLE MIRACLE! YOU HEALED ME!" "Stand up Seck," Edward growls. "You aren’t a fucking cripple. You are a liar, a manipulator, and a fucking threat that I intend to put down once and for all." Seck stares up at Edward all trace of dignity replaced with wrath and fury. "And what are you going to do about it Eddie?" He stands, "Are you now going to preach to me about the glories of wrestling? The greatness of being in the squared circle while thousands of fans chanting your name? How all differences between two men are best settled there? How I should not dare to speak the name of the whore you call a wife? Are you going to tell me you came here to force me to fight you once more just to settle the matter?" A creepy smile crosses the preachers face. "Are you still naïve enough to think that it would settle anything between the two of us for you to pound on me until I bleed? I think not Eddie. I think you understand the true nature of our relationship finally. Your physical threats may intimidate lesser men, but me? A man who is brother to Jesus Christ?" he scoffs, "You still hide behind this blustering and semi coherent façade Eddie. The outside is gruff and mean and nasty, but inside… inside you are still the same scared orphan you were as a child. Except, now that child isn’t stopped by just his fears; instead that child is stopped by his own moral code that he refuses to break." Edward shakes his head in disgust. "No Ibrahim, I’m not going to do any of those. I'm just going to tell you to leave us alone. I’m not going to hit you until you bleed and things break. I’m simply going to tell you to stop or else this situation will go in ways that neither of us expect we are capable of." Seck stares at Edward. He was expecting those things he said. He was expecting bluster, perhaps even a bit of violence. He was not expecting this. "What would that be?" "Don't get me started. Are you going to do as I said?" Ibrahim shakes his head violently. "Not at all, now get out of my house." "I see, very well then." Edward sighs "I guess that this entire trip was a waste of time then." "Yes it was." Seck nods, "Now, leave and don’t ever come back." Edward nods and starts to turn away. He pauses for a moment and turns back to stare at Seck. "One final thing Seck." Ibrahim turns around once more and stares at Edward again. "What now you cretin?" The punch that Edward threw connected square with the face just below the pompadour. Ibrahim Seck crashed back into and through the wicker wheelchair as Edward stepped forward and began laying blow after blow into the face of his future prisoner. "Don’t fucking call me Eddie." * * * Now. 2008. Edward finally trails off his rambling version of the events that lead up to his sticking Ibrahim Seck in his basement. Silence once again reigns in the cab of the truck that drives towards Ponca City. Kia stares at her husband and shakes her head. "What?" "He’s a psycho Kia. That’s all there is to it. Things were said, things were done, and the sum total of events ended with Seck in Alan’s basement. I figured that maybe I could get him help, maybe get him committed or something, something so that he wouldn’t be a threat anymore." Edward reaches into his breast pocket and pulls out another cigarette from the pack there. "I figured wrong. Alan and I kept him in his basement for about two months or so before Alan told me to get him the fuck out of there. He’s been living in our basement ever since." Edward touches flame to cigarette and breaths in the rich smoke. "And if I have anything to say about it, he’s going right back into the same fucking place."
