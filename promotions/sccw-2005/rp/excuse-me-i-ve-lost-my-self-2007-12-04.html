Excuse Me, I've Lost My 'Self'

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/12/07 I've been gaining weight latey. It's not overly bad - I've always been a skinny little shit - but it's also not very good, especially with a career that depends so much on fitness and looks. I swallowed my pride and got checked out, and found out that the root of the weight gain is caused by one of the medications I use to help me sleep. As a result, I've got to cut that particular medication out of my daily rituals... and it's hardly been pleasant. Case in point, the other day: I was at a hotel, doing shitty champion radio spots and what not, and I had my new 'agent' - the Highwaymen's agent, that is - following me around like a puppy most of the day... at least, until he scheduled me for something I didn't want to do. After being attached to my leg all morning, he mysteriously disappeared - leaving me with a photographer, makeup artists and a bikini. 1.0. (This Life) ''Where is that little fuckstick? I'm gonna slap the shit outta him...'' ''I think killing your PR guy might be bad PR.'' ''My mom's gonna see this!'' ''Sorry honey, but you don't strike me as the type who's been worried about what mommy-dearest might see or think.'' This had been going on for a few minutes. She'd entered a photoshoot and gone through the makeup process, which she loathed but always worked through, and then been told to wear... well... not very much. Amy frowned at the photographer, who was pushing at least a hundred cliches by wearing a beret and munching on a croissant, ''Well, Darcy's asleep upstairs. You want me to call him down to check out what his wife's gonna show the world?'' ''I'm just doing my job,'' he answered, ''I don't know if anyone told you, but 'Underpaid Photographer' trumps 'Angry Husband' every time. Now... wardrobe sent that, and that means you're supposed to wear it.'' ''I wouldn't be caught dead in that.'' The pompous photographer chuckled and interrupted her, ''Then you can make me some extra commission and go nude. It's your choice. A year ago, I 'wouldn't be caught dead' taking pictures of a bunch of redneck wrasslers, but money makes us do strange things.'' Amy was hell-bent on arguing her way out of it. Her lip started to curl in anger as she slowly began to realize that this wasn't a good week to cut back on medications. She balled a fist, ''I'm not doing it.'' ''I don't think there's much choice in it for you,'' he grinned, happy that someone was as miserable as he was, ''And it's not like the bikini would show anything that your last big beef didn't already. What was his name again? Lane, was it?'' He sat back, thinking he'd just had the privilege of playing the trump card in the game -- but this guy hadn't been around Amy Campbell ever before, and had no idea what he'd just gotten himself into. ''You know what, you pitiful, artsy-wannabe piece of 'wish I was as French as you' garbage? You can't spell 'croissant' without ass backwards.'' ''And you can't spell 'bitch' without... well, without 'bitch'.'' he replied. As she began her exit, Amy's temper rose with the pressure in her hand - gripping a fist white-knuckle tight by the time she got to the door. The photographer, for his part, began a verbal barrage of 'loser' remarks aimed with a sniper for the redhead. --------------------------------------------------- I hit a photographer a few days ago. It wasn't the brightest thing I've ever done, but it felt damn good. Lately, I've been able to control my temper in rough situations, but something's starting to bend in my head. Maybe it's the sleeplessness, maybe it's the chemical imbalance in my brain now that I'm cutting out Seroquel. Maybe I should just start killing kittens, eating puppies and align myself with Clinton Sage... I'm not entirely sure. I do know that this past week was absolute hell on wheels. I was with Darcy, then he was home, then I was gone, then I had this new jerkoff at my side - employed, of course, through my FUSE contract. I'm a champion again, and a singles champion now. I'm a role model, a blahblahblah AND a yadda yadda... all rolled into one. In case you didn't catch that, dear journal - I get to play the part of a puppet until someone takes this belt from me and, as much as I hate my part, I'm not giving this thing up any time soon. --------------------------------------------------- 1.0.1. (Seroquel, Seroquelled.) A day later, with her new agent still MIA, Amy had her superstar sunglasses on for a long morning of media. She wasn't trying to look like Paris Hilton, though - she'd actually decided tha she wanted to look like some sort of bug. Hopefully, someone could snap a photo with a caption that didn't involve anything Lane Stevens had ever said about her. But more than anything, she was wearing the massive shades to avoid people seeing the shiner she was sporting. With her hands in her hoodie pockets, she ducked out of her rental Lancer and into yet another hotel lobby, where she was due to check in on a reservation. Sadly - as with all things - she had the worst luck. That missing agent? ''Am I being punk'd? Seriously.'' Right there, waiting like a vulture next to the front desk, he immediately wrangles the redhead into his tangled web of jargon that would make Ari Gold blush. ''Why are you wearing sunglasses inside?'' he asked, patting down the folds in the front of his custom-fitted suit, ''And look at my hands.'' Amy rolled her eyes and answered 'Okay'. ''You see anything in them?'' ''No.'' ''EXACTLY!'' he snarled, ''These hands are supposed to be holding a set of sexy eight-by-tens of every sports fan's favorite tattooed redhead, ready to unveil her very surprisingly well-kempt bikini line to the world!'' From behind the massive sunglasses, a red eyebrow peaked. Of course, that didn't stop the hyperactive hype man. He continued, gingerly taking her by the elbow toward the hotel's breakfast buffet. ''I'm...'' she began, flustered and confused by the early-morning developments, ''I'm parked in a handicap spot. And where have you been?'' ''That's not important. What is important is what I just read when I checked my e-mail. Do you know what I'm talking about?'' Amy shook her head, and the agent laughed. ''My secretary sent me an article from TMZ. It informed me all about your little stunt yesterday.'' he said, ''I guess I missed the 'I'm sorry, Dan' e-mail from you. You know, the one where you apologize for making MY job harder by breaking a photographer's nose just because he was a snooty little bastard. The e-mail you didn't write or send.'' While he stared daggers through her and motioned for a table near the buffet, Campbell shrugged. Needless to say, that only prompted further ranting. ''Now, I know what you're thinking of telling me, but they're supposed to be prissy little pricks, Amy. That's why they're good photographers! I thought you were all about that art house bullshit... was I wrong? Go ahead, tell me I was wrong. Because, and interrupt me if I'm off-base here, you excused some lovely photos that Lane Stevens distributed by saying they were 'intended to be artistic'. So please, I really want to hear what you've got to say about this whole fiasco, and you know why? Not because I care, but because I have to issue a load of crap statement about how sorry you are about that camera-jockeying asshat only being able to breathe through his mouth.'' They sat down at a table, and Amy sat her duffel bag of luggage on the floor near her seat. She was still parked in a handicap space, but that didn't matter in the moment -- not with this Jewish, Red Bull addict chewing her ears off. ''I'm not sorry about it,'' she said bluntly, ''He was beyond rude, and I only go so far.'' ''Would this outburst have anything to do with the medication change that you also didn't feel inclined to discuss with me?'' Dan Mulholland sneered back at her. He was one of the best sports agents in the business, and would be spending the week closely following the actions of his newest client... which hadn't turned out to be nearly as easy as he was hoping, ''If A-Rod puts an extra sprinkle of ground pepper on his lean turkey sandwich, he calls to tell me. You're throwing yourself onto the tracks of an emotional rollercoaster just because, what? Because I said you look fat? Even Jessica Alba has a few extra pounds... it keeps things perky.'' ''How did you know I changed medications?'' ''Your doctor might as well be on my staff now,'' he replied, ''I told you I'm thorough, and I pay a fucking fortune to be this good. You need to remember that.'' He folded his hands on the table as the waitress filled his water glass, ''Plus, since I've met you, you've either been half-asleep or a raging bitch. Not too hard to figure out when I'm dealing with someone who I know has a history of... oh, what was it... obsessive compulsive personality disorder, drug addictions, depression, violent outburts, seizur--'' Amy sighed, ''Fine, fine. I get it.'' she groaned and leaned back in her chair, ''Can I pay you to go away? I mean, it's nice having someone handling my shit and all... but my boat's sailing just fine, in case you hadn't noticed.'' ''Fine? You call being labeled a psycho bitch for knocking the shit out of a helpless photo-journalist 'just fine'?'' He appeared to be having some sort of stroke, with a face beet-red and that weird vein in his throat poking out to say hello, ''I'm here for one reason and one reason only: DAMAGE CONTROL. I was hired to do two things: Keep you rich, and keep you from running your career back into the gutter now that it's fiiiiiiiiinally got some forward momentum.'' With that vein still throbbing, Mulholland leans across the table, banging a finger on his still-empty plate, ''And just so you know, I don't give up. I'm part of your contract now, lady. I'm a publicity doctor -- I haven't lost a career yet, and I'm damn sure not gonna start with you.'' He continued, calming himself down a little and once more obsessively pawing at the folds in his coat. In a complete change, he suddenly appeared level-headed, turning on the version of himself that needed to charm his new client into seeing the light in the future. ''Now, this is your last day of PR before Defiance, and I don't think I need to explain again how important it is that you portray a good image. This starts with a good breakfast, once that I - by the way - am going to buy you to celebrate getting the rocks out of the start of this wonderful client-agent relationship. I know the road was a little bumpy yesterday, but I'm sure you'll see things my way when you're back home with your wonderful husband tomorrow. Am I right?'' he smiled at her, putting his hands over hers and shaking, ''C'mon - am I right?'' Amy wasn't stupid, nor was she much for games - but there was no sense trying to fight anybody this early in the morning, and this caffeine machine of a man was on the payroll now - a part of her every day life for god knows how long. ''We'll see about that,'' she smiled wryly, ''I'll try not to maim anybody today, but you'd better not have anymore bikini bullshit scheduled. I'll do my radio and commercial bits, and I'd better be on a plane by seven o'clock.'' ''And I want you to know,'' continued the redhead, ''I might only be in my mid-twenties, but I'm not into playing games. I hope you know what I mean.'' Then, their pre-breakfast whirlwind ended as quickly as it began, with a dead-on, hit-the-nail-on-the-head statement from Aimz' new 'handler'. ''Sorry babe, but this is the game we play in the year 2007. If you want food on the table, you'd better start learning the moves.'' --------------------------------------------------- My new agent, manager or whatever you'd call him is a riot. He's witty, charming, talkative and very good at his job. The only problem? I can't stand him. If I ever have a surplus of bullets, I'd probably choose him to be my target for practice. But that's what makes him good at his job, just like all of the bad things about me - my temper, my overtalkative nature, my irrtating persistance and complete stupidity, for example - make me good at mine. And if I want my career to pick up instead of veering back off into the breakdown lane for the umpteenth time, I have to be a part of this twisted little parade of crap. Isn't it shitty how the worst things to do are always the best for you in the long run? These hollow press statements and ugly photos that I break my back and mind over are actually benefitting me. They're putting money in my wallet. They're supporting my future family. I just wish there was a way for someone like me to provide in a way that she wouldn't be so ashamed to tell her future grandkids about. Oh well, I go home to Darcy in the morning. --------------------------------------------------- 1.0.2. (My New Moves) ''God damn this medication bullshit!'' An angry woman roared her way back into her house after a near week-long absence. With bags in tow, she immediately went into her bedroom - where Darcy was just making the bed in anticipation of her sleepy arrival. ''I haven't slept in three days,'' she groaned, ''And I missed you.'' She sighed, ''And... and God, I hate that new agent.'' ''I didn't expect you to love a walking penis who never shuts up,'' Darcy smiled as he wrapped his arms around her, ''Welcome home.'' ''This 'career cleanup' stuff is insane,'' Again, she sighed, ''I'm already sick of it. Your back is screwed up again, my entire body hurts, I've gotta face two guys who... just... ugh. I can't tell what side they're on. Everything's a mess.'' As expected, Darcy Markson provided her with a soft place to land. He rested his chin on her head, ''You're just not used to not having this one, huge enemy. I'm sure Frost or McKail will come up with something to piss you off enough, your motivation can't be too far away. Wade your way through the bullshit for now - 'cause I see you with that title, and I couldn't be more proud of you. I can tell that you're proud of yourself, too.'' ''You always come home after being away and say that everything's a mess,'' he said, ''You need to take a couple of days to unwind after everything you've been through in the past couple of months. Face it, Ames... you feed on hating people and, right now, you don't have enough reasons to hate Ethan Frost or Jacob McKail. But I guarantee when Defiance rolls around next week, you'll have come up with a few.'' ''You might be right.'' Darcy smiled, ''I know I'm right. And I know that you need to get some sleep, 'cause - no offense - you look like death warmed over. Is getting off the Seroquel that bad?'' ''I don't know... it's been such a wild week, I haven't had a chance to sit down and think about how I'm feeling or acting. That new guy - whatshisface - has been doing the thinking for me, I guess.'' ''Maybe that'll be a good thing once you're used to it,'' he replied, ''For once, you came home and didn't have to start doing a bunch of paperwork. You came in the door and now, not five minutes later, you can go to bed.'' She hadn't thought of it that way. Does playing the game REALLY have perks, or is she just getting blinded by the job security of holding a championship belt? --------------------------------------------------- The other day, I told Darcy that I was feeling guilty about what happened to Lane Stevens - about how I hurt him, and now nobody can find the guy. I thought of the family he might have and the options I may have taken away from him. What if he turned around at some point? If he put half the energy he uses on being a prick into improving himself, he could be a halfway decent man some day. I might have taken that option completely out of him. I mean, what if he has kids? Being a dick doesn't mean your swimmers don't work - he could easily get some hooker pregnant. I just stole their hope of having a real man for a father. I don't think I'm going soft, but Darcy said some things that really hit home. He told me that I didn't beat the man he 'could' be, I beat the piss-poor excuse for a human being that he is and that if I'm always going to take responsibility for every possible outcome in the lives of the people I fight, I'm going to go mental. Today, he told me that I seem to thrive on the hate, drama and righteousness that comes from having a true enemy. Maybe he's right. But the fact remains that, regardless of who I'm feeling guilt about... after all of the press, all of the chemically-induced craziness and once I've paid too much for a photographer's nose, I get to go and get the shit stomped out of me at a pay-per-view. At the very least, I have no doubt that I'll walk out of this new fight with my title still in hand, but this is a tricky one. There aren't really sides in this battle, because both men fighting to take the title away from me act as if they're good at heart. While McKail would have me believe that he's just in The Affliction to boost his career, the fact that he's cheating and manipulating for himself alone is way too selfish to ignore. He might think he's good at the end of the day, but there's nothing about that man that doesn't stink of bullshit. Ethan Frost, on the other hand, I can buy into the holiness of. He's always been a good man to me -- honest, if nothing else. The enemy of my enemy is my opponent. I fight Frost, and I fight McKail, but I'm not fighting for an unselfish reason. I'm fighting to keep my career in the headlines, and that's it. And that, journal, brings me to my final question of the week - after all of the Lane Stevens drama has come to a close, and things are finally looking up for me, I'm more miserable than I was before. I play the game, I get the career I've always wanted, I get the title and the money and the future. What I lack right now is identity. Two months ago, I was the poor girl who Lane Stevens was tormenting. Then I got to be the triumphant girl, the champion. Now, I'm what - the girl whose label is stamped entirely by her trials? I'm defined by the people who either love me, or are out to get me. So... who the hell am I without someone to hate me?
