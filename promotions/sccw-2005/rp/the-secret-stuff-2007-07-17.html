The secret stuff

17 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 17th 2007 « Who is it ? » I said at the top of my voice, responding to the loud rining of the door bell. But there was no answer, just a long silence. After a few moments I shrugged and went back to what I was doing, when against the buzzer made me jump. « Who is it ? » This time however I did get a response, as a faint female voice spoke. « Fred it’s Jessica, I’ve got the stuff you asked for ! » Hearing this I clinched my fist in victory, Jessica was a good friend of mine, and she had perfectly accomplished her mission. I immediately started to cross the sitting room towards the front door. Only then, a terrible thing happened. I could see the door knob slowly turning as I was still on the other side of the room. « Don’t worry Fred, it’s open I’ll just let myself in... » « Nooooo !!! » I screamed as the door slowly pushed open. I rushed across the room towards the door as fast as I could, as I could now see one of Jessica’s maroon eyes looking through. At this emergency I had to take extreme action, so I launched my whole body fowards with all I had, reaching out and shoving the door closed with the tips of my fingers. A loud thud echoed as I hit the floor without any kind of effort to actually break my fall. The thud however sounded different than my every day thud. I was landing on a carpetted floor, yet the sound sounded like two hard objects colliding. It was then, as I lay out on my floor in front of the door, that I realised what the sound was... « Jessica ! » I jumped to my feet as quickly as I could, and pulled the door open ever so slighty, say about five to ten inches, just enough so I could have a clear view outside. Indeed, as I had anguished, there was Jessica, holding her forehead. As I grimaced at the situation, those brown eyes suddenly stared right up at me from underneath the dark slightly curly hair. I could sense the anger. « Fred ? What the hell ? » she asked in a temper. « Uh... Sorry Jess, I just couldn’t let you come in I’m afraid... » I answered, raising my eyebrows for forgivesness. « Why not ? And why on earth are you hiding behind the door ? » « Because... » I answered, aboslutely lost for any kind of excuse. « Because of what Fred ? » « I’m doing secret stuff... » As I said this there was suddenly an awkward silence. I didn’t really know what to make of it, but after only a few moments, Jessica rolled her eyes. « Oh my god, you’re watching porn aren’t you ? » This confused me. « What’s a porn ? » I asked in all innocence. « Oh common Fred, don’t take me for a fool. You know exactly what I’m talking ab... Hey ! What’s that on your cheek ? » As she asked this I was suddenly caught completely by surprise and off guard. I turned my head to one side so as to hide my left cheek behind the door. That was a close one. She sighed, shaking her head slightly. « Fred it’s all over your face, she said, even on the other cheek. What is it ? It’s all shiny... » She then started to reach out through the small opening. Her hand got closer and closer to my face, as I for a moment considered slamming the door into a her forearm to prevent her from discovering anything. But I had already hit her in the head with a door, so that was quite enough violence for today. So against my will, she brushed a finger across my cheek. « Be careful, I said, I’m trying to save as much of it as possible... » She frowned in confusion as she gently pull her hand away and looked closely as the substance on her finger. She then looked up at me through the door in surprise. « Fred... It’s... » I nodded slowly to her observation. « Purple glitter glue, yep. » I confirmed. « But what are you doing with purple glitter glue that I can’t come in and see ? » she asked. « Well... it’s a surprise... » I answered. « A surprise, she exclaimed suddenly all excited, for me ? » « Um... Uh... Yep ! » I said, clearly under the pressure. « Wow ! That’s great ! I won’t bother you then... » Even though I had bashed her head in with a wooden door, she was suddenly so very excited and happy again. This soothed my conscience a little, because although there was no surprise for her, it did make her feel better. My blatant lie was therefore perfectly valid in this situation. « But Fred... » I sensed a change in her tone, from one of excitement to one of confusion. « These props I bought, she continued, are they for my surprise to ? » « Of course... » I answered, continuing my blatant lying. She looked into the bag she was holding and frowned. « So these black marker pens, this ducktape, these sprinkler stick, plus your purple glitter glue... are all for a surprise for me ? » I looked down at the bag, before looking back up at her. « Indeedo speedo ! » I replied, attempting to wink. « In fact, could you pass them over ? » She glared at me for a few seconds, before slowly passing me the bag of props through the slightly opened door. I grabbed the bag away from her, and grinned to pretend everything was normal. « Okay, so when do I get to see my surprise ? » she asked in anticipation. « Christmas... » I answered without thinking, although I probably would have said the same thing after a long moment of thought. « Christmas ? » I nodded. « Okie dokie then, well I must get back to it if I want it to be ready for then. » I explained, trying to get her to go away. « Um... okay I guess ? » she said, just staring at me through the opening. « Oh, and you have a bit of blood on your forehead. » I told her, as her eyes widened in shock and she brought her hand to her forehead to see. She then looked at her hand, revealing that she was bleeding quite badly. She then glared up at me in anger, but I had already slammed the door shut to avoid any aftermath. I turned around, back to the door, and ignoring the furious yelling coming from outside, I reached into the bag. I pulled out a roll of ducktape and grinned furiously. I then pull on the end, making a long stip of tape. I then looked fowards towards my work room and muttered... « Perfection approaches... »
