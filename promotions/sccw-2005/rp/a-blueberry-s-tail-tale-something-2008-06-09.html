A Blueberry's Tail... Tale... Something

9 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9, 2008 Lawrence Spiderman had the distinction of what may be the world’s most interesting surname. When he was younger, it would serve as an icebreaker at parties, a way to introduce himself to desirable young ladies whom he wished to meet. In business it was often the subject of corny jokes used at meetings and power lunches, a way to establish himself in his chosen profession. To play up the joke, he had even named his first company as 'Your Friendly Neighborhood Talent Agency.' Most of the people that he worked with knew the proper pronunciation, 'Speeder-min', and even when dealing with folks who were a bit surprised to meet what they assumed to be a comic book hero he would allow them a slip or two before correcting them. There was one person however who, for whatever reason, seemingly refused to accept the fact that Mr. Spiderman was not, in fact, a professional web-slinger. 'There are nooooooo cats in A-me-ri-ca, and the streets are paved with che-ee-ese!' A person who at this particular moment had polarized the waiting room, and had broken into the chorus of 'There Are No Cats in America.' Whoever he was, Fievel Mousekewitz he was not. So loud was the singing that Mr. Spiderman was able to hear him through the stained oak door that separated his office from the remainder of the office space, and even then it would be another 20 feet of hallway before coming close to the reception area. A young associated decided that this would be the opportune time to quietly slip a pair of headphones over her ears, hoping to drown out what was best described as 'dying bird falsetto.' Spiderman stood from his seat and loosened his tie. Four steps later he was tossing a few ice cubes into an 8-ounce glass. He’d learned early on that this particular client warranted a pre-meeting drink, sometimes two. In a single gulp he killed his whiskey. The inevitable could be postponed no longer. 'Alright, Stace’. Send him in.' Stacey, the unfortunate receptionist, couldn’t help but smile, especially seeing as 'No Cats in America' had ended and given rise to 'Somewhere Out There'. 'Soooomewhere... out... there; beneath the pale moonlight...' 'Excuse me, Mr., uh, Mr. Blueberry?' she practically shouted over the singing, 'Mr. Spiderman will see you now.' There are no adjectives in the common English dialect which do justice to just how eccentric the man who had taken to calling himself 'King Blueberry' actually is. Since having returned to the United States from an extended tour in Japan, he had taken to wearing a lucha style mask anytime he appeared in public, and even occasionally in his own home. On this particular afternoon he was decked in casual attire consisting of khaki shorts, a pair of sneakers, and a rather worn and dated 'Gigli' tee-shirt. Of course, all of this paled in comparison to his mask. Colored in royal blue, and accepted at the eyes with white, it was finished off by a small plush crown that sat lopsided and tilted over his right brow. 'C’mon, Mer... umm... Blueberry Dragon,' he blurted out, turning to one of the two men that had accompanied him, 'Time to talk business.' ********** Dear Diary, Today I was pompous and my sister was crazy. Today we were kidnapped by hillfolk, never to be seen again. It was the best day ever. 'No, wait, that’s how Firefly started...' I have a great idea for a movie. Giant-sized Dean Malenko comes out of the ocean in New York, putting the Statue of Liberty and other monuments in giant-sized leglocks. We’ll call it The Texas Cloverfield. ********** 'So, what exactly are you calling yourself again?' Lawrence Spiderman had taken his tie off, and was slowly balling it in his hands. It could be argued that this was for his own safety, as given his company he may well have used it to hang himself. 'King Blueberry. Pretty badass, huh?' 'And what’s the schtick? What’s the hook? How do we market this?' 'Don’t think I follow, Spidey.' 'So, you’re a full-grown man dressed as a blueberry. I guess I’m just trying to figure out why you’re dressing as a blueberry, and more importantly why the hell you’re wearing a mask in public. And, see, it’s not just you, but why are THEY wearing masks?' Spiderman gestured to the two figures that sat quietly on the couch behind King Blueberry. The first looked to stand well over 6 feet tall and was covered in what was best described as a robe. From under a blue lucha mask poked a deep brown beard and shoulder length hair. On his lap was cradled a stuffed yellow rabbit. For reasons unexplained, it too wore a mask. Next to him was a much smaller, thinner man. Clad in khakis and plaid, he sat with his hands folded, occasionally fidgeting with an iPhone. He also had a mask, though in his case a pair of thick-rimmed glasses protruded through the eye holes. 'Oh this is no simple gimmick, Webby. I’m here to have you promote me as the king of blueberries. These guys here,' he said, gesturing to the couch behind him, 'this is my court. Behold the illustrious Blueberry Dragon and Blueberry Mask 14! Together we are... well... we don’t have a clever name yet. Was thinking maybe you could give us one.' Spiderman looked at his empty glass, the cubes clinking together. 'I think I’m going to get another drink first.' ********** Talked to the boss today. Turns out that Vegas is the place to be, again. Apparently there’s Sin City redux, or something. They tell me it’s all new, no ties to the other one. I suppose this is a good thing, because I remember saying that I’d rather smash my nuts with a claw hammer than work there again. I don’t suppose it’ll be all that bad. The whole place looks strangely familiar. It’s as though I’ve seen these people before. Oh well, at least I won’t have Billy Page to deal with. Hugs and kisses, K.B.
