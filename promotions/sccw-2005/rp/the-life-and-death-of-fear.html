Date: Various August 30, 2008 11:46am EST It’d been over a week since King Blueberry’s back was introduced to Jadian Bridden’s vengeance, and not all of the knots had yet to straighten themselves out. The King had taken to taping a hot pack under his shirt, but with a sudden turn in New England weather towards the warm all it really served to do was increase the amount he seemed to sweat. 'You know, Little D., I think I’m pretty much over the whole ‘pain’ thing, and I’d have been willing to forgive Jadian for his mistake, but seriously, this is epic level swass here.' 'Dude.' 'I know.' The two men were seated at the dining room table in Blueberry’s apartment, a room seldom visited and rarely used for its intended purpose. Today it was serving as a medium on which to play Magic: The Gathering. King Blueberry tapped (which is to say, 'turned sideways') a Forest and an Island, putting into play a Tarmogoyf from his hand. 'And not for nothin’, but I was totally digging this title fight a lot more when the good guys were on the same side. Not sure what to think about Josh,' the King paused, examining the cards in his hand, thinking of his next play, 'but Bridden needs to die, and it’s a lot harder to shoot lasers at his face when he’s got Charlie to use as a shield. Wouldn’t be surprised if he tried to pull it off, too, that daffy bitch.' 'Yeah, so are you about done with your turn, or what?' 'Oh yeah, sorry. Go.' Dragon shuffled around the cards in his hand: an Island, a Tundra, a Force of Will, a Brainstorm, and an Accumulated Knowledge. 'Okay then, at the end of your turn,' he said, tapping two of his own lands. 'Here it comes.' 'Accumulated Knowledge to draw.' 'How many in the yard?' 'Just the one, so I’ll draw two. Does it resolve?' 'Yeah, sure. The first one’s free.' Dragon took two cards from the top of his deck, putting them into his hand. He then began his own turn, starting by untapping all of his cards in play. 'Technically it’s the second one. So you’re still keeping your word? Last time you did this it may well have cost you a match, and a shot at the big shiny.' The King placed his hand of cards face down on the table, shaking his head. He took the glass closest to him, wiping some of the condensation away, before taking a drink. There is one known cure for extreme swass: Cherry Coke on ice. 'Okay, first of all, with this whole Sin City versus Prime nonsense going around I’ve heard more than my fair share of the word ‘shiny’. At this point I’d rather hit myself in the crotch with a clawhammer until I fall down than hear it again. Unfortunately it means I’ve had to swear of Firefly until this is all over.' He replaced the glass on the table, this time setting it down atop a folded paper towel to prevent any further condensation for encroaching on the field of battle. 'Second, yes. I made a promise at All In that I wasn’t going to fight her, and it’s still standing. It’s certainly going to make this a pain in the balls to win, since now there’s only one person on that team that I can punch in the face, but I hate him enough it’s like he’s two assholes rolled into one.' 'And third?' 'New house rule. From now on, anytime anyone says Prime you have to shout it, with like 8 exclamation points.' 'That makes no sense.' 'It makes perfect sense. They’re the ones who decided to pull a Fresus and ‘ALLCAPSNOSPACES’ their damn company name.' Dragon once again shuffled his cards around in his hand, shaking his head. For a moment he sat silent, and it could be debated whether he was thinking what his next play would be or pretending to ignore what he considered inane babble coming from his friend. 'All right, no play,' he said, extending his right hand momentarily. 'Go.' I was twenty, I’d just completed my training, and the guys that I knew had pulled some strings to get me into what I thought was a pretty hot company. In all honesty, I was kind of a dick back then. I ran my mouth a lot, and wasn’t afraid to just rip into whoever I thought deserved it. (Patient takes a sip of water.) PATIENT: And in some cases I’d go after folks just because I thought it would be funny, or because they’d be easy targets. That’s how this all started. I went after a member of The Dominion who called himself Mercutio. He hung around with a pretty smokin’ hot redhead that kind of looked like the Willow chick on Buffy the Vampire Slayer. PARKER: Is this what started the tension between you? PATIENT: Yeah. It all kind of came to a head after I was caught after having broken into their locker room and stolen some of her unmentionables. When they found me, I was wearing them. PARKER: Oh? (Patient’s behavior may indicate gender confusion.) PATIENT: Like a ski mask. PARKER: Oh. (Strike previous note.) PATIENT: Yeah, but that was only part of it. See, there was another guy in the employ of The Dominion, a big dude about 7 feet tall. If Gabe was the brain, this guy was definitely the muscle. I think the pressure was really starting to show. But, yeah, about the big guy? So his schtick was that he’d wrestle as himself, but he’d also put on a black mask and wrestle as ‘Behemoth’. (The patient starts laughing. I think he just rolled his eyes.) PATIENT: It was completely fucking retarded, and what I did about then I would probably do about it again in heartbeat. PARKER: What was this? PATIENT: I got a mask of my own, and every now and again I would come out as ‘WannabeHemoth’. I even recorded my own theme music, though I doubt you could really call it music. It was basically me just chanting the words ‘scary music’ over and over again. They didn’t like it very much. (Patient seems obviously pleased with himself. I gather from this that tormenting his colleagues is something that not only does he enjoy, but will apparently go to great lengths in order to accomplish.) PARKER: Did they retaliate, or is this the king of thing that they would take in stride? PATIENT: So one night I’m in the ring with Mercutio. It’s more or less going my way. I’ve got him stuck in a knee-lock in the middle of the ring, and he’s got no choice but to give up. So, hooray me. Before the ref could even ring the bell there were about 8 guys on me. The last thing I remember was the big guy picking me up. I saw the footage after the fact. They’d exposed a turnbuckle and decided to drop my head on it. Still got the scar on the back of my head. August 30, 2008 12:03pm EST The last game had gone in favor of King Blueberry, as he was able to resolve an Umezawa’s Jitte, equip it to his Tarmogoyf, and attack Blueberry Dragon down to zero life. Thus far the second game was going more in Dragon’s favor, as the King had stalled on only 3 lands and had not yet been able to resolve a single threat card. When King Blueberry drew a card on his next turn, seeing another uncastable card, his mind began to wander. So are you going to man up and actually do it this week, or are you once again going to hide from her? 'So,' The King said, discarding his hand size down to seven cards, 'I’m thinking about trying to get a meeting at the next show, with... someone.' 'Oh yeah, who? Going to go slap Jay Draven around?' 'Hey, that was an incredibly hard sell for such a shitty move.' It seriously was. The king was laughing to himself a bit. 'Besides, I couldn’t really do anything about it. He’s trying to train up a newb, and it would bury the kids confidence if his teacher got beat. But no, nothing like that. I’m thinking I need to talk to...' Say it. '...You know, that woman...' Say. It. '...The red-headed one...' SAY IT ALREADY! Dragon, being quite the astute individual, could easily identify this as avoidance. 'Who, Desade?' King Blueberry’s eyes went wide. Damn. 'What?' Dragon’s look could best be described as ‘confused’. 'Huh? Nothing.' 'Oh.' Damn. 'What?' 'Nothing.' 'Then why do you keep saying ‘damn’?' King Blueberry’s jaw almost hit the floor, and he stared wide-eyed across the table at Blueberry Dragon. For his part, Dragon looked equally confused, not quite sure how to interpret the body language of his friend. 'I said that? Out lout?' 'Yeah, twice just now.' King Blueberry’s look of surprise was quickly replaced by an expression of relief. 'Oh, thank God. For a second there I thought you could read the italics.' Original notes appear in parentheses. (Begin video.) PARKER: So (REMOVED), I want to start with something you mentioned the last time we talked. PATIENT: Which part? PARKER: You mentioned some problems with a group called The Dominion, a group that was run by someone you’d grown up with. PATIENT: Right. Gabe. PARKER: I wanted to talk a bit about the turnbuckle incident that you’d started to describe. What the fall-out from that was. (He sighs. This is clearly not something that he enjoys discussing.) PATIENT: Dude, The Dominion were supposed to be the good guys, but their methods were a little fucked-up. They had this thing they used to do. If they had a problem with someone there was a, well, I guess you could call it 'special' way to deal with them. PARKER: What did that entail? PATIENT: They’d knock the person out, lock them in a friggin’ wooden coffin, and if the arena was close enough to a body of water they’d throw the coffin in it. (His hands are shaking.) PATIENT: I remember one night, a week or two after the buckle incident, they got all of us. PARKER: I don’t follow. PATIENT: All of us. Everyone who wasn’t in their little group; everyone except the staff like the referees, the announcers, and the production folks. They picked us off one by one, and they locked us all up in wooden boxes, brought us to the coast, and tossed us into the ocean. I remember... (The patient is visibly panicked. I have no doubts that this is a painful memory to recall.) PARKER: (REMOVED), if you don’t want to do continue... PATIENT: I remember that when they did it I could hear one of them singing 'Good Morning Starshine.' I remember that I thought it was kind of funny, given the circumstances. The next thing I remember is falling. For a minute everything felt weightless, and then I hit the water. PARKER: Can you describe the sensation? PATIENT: It was sudden, abrupt, but the box didn’t break. For a minute it just floated, and I wasn’t afraid, wasn’t nervous at all. I just expected that we’d get picked up, or the tide would take us all to shore, but it didn’t quite happen that way. (He’s trying to conceal the fact that this makes him uneasy, and to an untrained eye he’s doing quite the decent job of it. His body language is difficult to read. I can’t yet tell if the intermittent laughter is sincere or a cover.) PATIENT: I didn’t see it until after I felt it. The box wasn’t watertight, and seawater slowly started to seep in between the boards. I didn’t have the room to move, couldn’t really get enough leverage to kick out the boards and swim, and even if I could I wasn’t yet fully conscious, didn’t have all my strength back. (He’s silent for a moment.) PATIENT: I’ve never felt that kind of fear before; I’d never been that afraid of anything. To be honest I haven’t been that afraid of anything since. It does things to you, an experience like that, kind of desensitizes you to the scary shit that goes on around you. I don’t want to be that afraid ever again. PATIENT: I was convinced it was over. I knew I was going to die. (End video.) Tuesday, September 2, 2008 3:02pm EST Lawrence Spiderman had become accustomed to the once-per-month visit of his most unusual client, King Blueberry, preparing for each encounter with the same mindset he used to prepare for visits to the dentist. The purpose of this particular meeting was primarily focused on marketability and merchandising. 'So the last thing I wanted to mention is that we’ve been getting requests for any Violent Jesus merchandise we can come up with, since nothing new is on the market. Everything out there is currently on the secondary market, and the secondary prices for Minnesota State Wrestling tee-shirts are through the roof.' Across from Spiderman sat both King Blueberry and Blueberry Dragon. Violent Jesus had occupied himself by playing with some crayons in the corner of the room. 'Right now we’re looking at commissioning two designs. The first - and I should have prototypes for both of these by next week, is a throwback ‘Minnesota Messiah’ shirt.' 'Sounds hot,' said Blueberry, nodding an affirmative. 'And the other one?' 'The plan for the second one is a ‘Show Me the Bunny’ design, used as a play on that stuffed, well, thing that he keeps with him.' A crayon bonked Spiderman in the side of the head, startling him. Both King Blueberry and Blueberry Dragon both started to giggle. 'Better watch what you say about Eugene, I’d wager the first crayon was just a warning shot.' Spiderman’s gaze shifted rapidly between King Blueberry and Jesus. 'Noted. And like I said, I should have prototypes for both of these by early next week.' 'Sweet. There is one more thing that I wanted to talk to you about.' 'No problem. What would that be?' The King turned his head towards Dragon. 'Hey, Li’l D., do me a favor and take Lloyd out to the car. Crank the A.C. I should be there in a minute or two.' Dragon’s expression was one of confusion, but he did as suggested, first standing from his chair and then collection Lloyd and his array of crayons. He calmly ushered the giant would-be-Jesus to the door, closing it once both had passed through it. No sooner did they pass did King Blueberry reach for the back of his mask, first untying the knot that held it in place, and then beginning to loosen the threads. 'I need a favor, and I while they might be able to guess what it’s about, I’d rather they not have first hand knowledge of it. I don’t want them getting into anything I can’t safely get them out of.' Spiderman nodded. 'Plausible deniability?' 'Exactly. Just like the X-Files.' His mask untied and loosened, King Blueberry slowly removed the mask from his face. He ran his left hand through the short, light brown hair atop his head, freeing it from the matting the mask had forced. Bright hazel eyes met Spiderman’s gaze. 'I need you to make a call to some less-than-favorable people. I’d like to setup a meeting with Alexandra Pierce,' he paused, smirking, 'well, I’d like to meet with Pierce as much as I’d like to hit myself in the crotch with a claw-hammer until I fall down, but I figure I probably should meet with her.' 'Okay. I think I can arrange that,' Spiderman gestured both to the mask and the face both before him, 'Only question I have is who I should tell her is coming.' The King’s right hand slipped into the mask, holding it as he would a puppet. He smiled wide as he mimicked his next words with the mouth of the mask. 'King Blueberry, naturally.'
