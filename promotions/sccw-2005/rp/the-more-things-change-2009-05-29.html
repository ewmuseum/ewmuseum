She's one of the best at what she does...even better at dress-up than I am.' There was a pout on her pretty face as she admitted that fact. 'Alex has lots of...what's wrong, Phil?' 'Why the fuck would Alex lock Lauren up like this,' Kennedy asked, voice pained. 'Because she stood up for me? I'm the reason she's here?!' 'You didn't listen earlier,' Kat chided. 'I told you, Laur did something worse than you did.' 'The Hell did she do to deserve this?' Phillip thundered back. 'She *might*,' Kathryn began, a small smirk curving her mouth, 'have tried to kill Amy Campbell.' Fox gave a weak nod in her chair, and Phillip's mouth dropped to somewhere in the region of his right testicle. 'Kill....Amy? But why? That...what the fuck? I wanted to kill her, well, not kill her-- you know what I mean, goddamnit!' 'Phillip,' Kathryn cut him off, with a notable lack of subtlety. 'Lauren's been here for days. She probably needs medical attention. I'm going to call an ambulance, you stay here and make sure our friend from earlier doesn't have any ideas of coming back.' Before Kennedy could get out a 'you're dodging the question,' Shaw was out of the container and placing her call. 'Fucking figures,' Kennedy groaned, looking at the barely conscious woman in the chair. It was almost too much for him to comprehend. She'd tried to kill Campbell? Why? And what the Hell would have happened if she'd succeeded? Only now, with the danger seemingly mostly having passed, could Kennedy really reflect on everything. It was ludicrous. If Shaw hadn't been so firm and straight-faced with everything she'd said, he wouldn't have believed it. Satisified that Lauren seemed to be breathing and holding up reasonably okay, Phillip looked at the length of chain that had once been attached to Lauren's shackle. It was an easy matter to follow that chain to the wall, where it was connected to the container itself. The idea of it being him trapped in the container, with a finite amount of food and water, spurred him to pick up the hacksaw. Once more, the sound of metal on metal was enough to rouse Lauren from her exhausted lethargy. 'What are you doing,' she asked, voice small and ever-so-slightly distressed. 'Don't you worry your pretty little head about it,' Phillip smirked. 'Kat's calling an ambulance. We'll get you out of here soon enough.' 'I heard,' Fox stated, 'but what are you doing?' The laugh that came forth from Phillip was brief and entirely mirthless. 'Just getting a souvenir of my trip, that's all.'
