Five Stages Of Grief Part 2: Anger

Date: Today The events in this part during the Luci flashbacks take place during the first part of Skeletons In The Closet. The Journey had been long, Luci had been interrogating people left and right since she had the seed planted in her head that Lauren had been compromised following Sin-Con asking questions on where she could find Alex Pierce. The poor bastard that had decided to chat her up at a local bar following some downtime after Temptation found out first hand never to anger a woman who’s supposedly grieving. One judo throw through a table later and the man is left broken with Luci being escorted out of the club before the authorities are called. As she is being escorted out she suddenly screams ‘You’re all in league with her aren’t you?! Tell Pierce I’ll be waiting for her… The next day she would receive a phone call but time to do the leap… The Present Day Once again the setting is an abandoned warehouse for another Rev 22:20 online video however the password is the other half of the preface quote from Fear And Loathing in Las Vegas (He who makes a beast out of himself gets rid of the pain of being a man.) (We see Stigmata behind Legion as he wears a Machine Head ‘The Blackening T-shirt and ripped jeans) "It seems that I managed to succeed in getting under Nigel’s skin last week as he’s booked me against the SIN champions in Daddy Daughter Day without the assistance of my companions as if they interfere I’m at risk of being suspended. I pity you Nigel as I guess the mental scars from Lane’s attack didn’t really heal if you could snap so easily when you and I both know no male owner of Final Curtain actually exists, guess you wanted to prove your superiority over Adrienne. The thing is that just because Luci and Stigmata are banned from ringside doesn’t mean I’m on my own for this one, far from it. The war shall continue until you realise what I’m actually seeking from you… It’s not as obvious as you may think." Back to the Past " Luci, what the hell was last night about? I’m being informed that you shouted for people to warn Alex Pierce to be ready for when you come to avenge Lauren?!, are you seriously trying to get us both killed? Luci: Have you ever felt ripped in the heart like I have? HAVE YOU?! I’m going to find Alex, once vengeance has been done then we can relax! Legion: Ok, fine! Listen there’s a wake being held for her, if you can find it go but don’t do any avenging… please. Luci: Just don’t come looking for me for a while ok? I may have to lay low… because I’m already there. Luci hangs up the phone as she enters the hall, she can see the members of Desade’s cabal that have been associated with Lauren over her time in SCCW including some faces she didn’t recognise (such as her brother Devin Fox) and right in her line of vision Alex has just finished giving a speech/ Luci: Great job Alex, Oscar worthy performance you might say, you’ve been wanting to remove Lauren ever since she turned against you, now you got your wish… MURDERER. She runs up about to strike Desade with a punch when suddenly Roderick Ashe grabs her. As she screams kicking at Ashe we can see tears streaming down her face as the horror kicks in, granted Legion would feel the same once he found out.
