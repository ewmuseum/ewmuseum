Date: Today When Alexandra Pierce says she's got two sides, she's a little off. At the very least, one is like the other. They have the same properties, just on opposite ends of the spectrum. When I say I've got two sides, I mean it. I don't have a contrasting personality or a particularly evil face, but gears. I've got speed settings, fast and slow. One of them makes me happy, and the other makes me happy in that moment. Unfortunately, if you're switching gears like a madwoman, your engine's gonna break down sooner than later. ''Well we can't exactly stay in California and celebrate Christmas with Darcy's parents. You remember why that is?'' Quinn may have rolled her eyes, but she loves ribbing Darcy just as much as I do. ''Because you married a Jew.'' ''And the Jew married a Canadian.'' A much fuller voice chimed in from behind. ''I should've listened to my mother and found a nice American girl.'' ''Unfair statement, your mom loves me.'' She even wants grandchildren, the crazy old yenta. As much as I bitch and moan about the weather, the location and any number of things about Nova Scotia, it's home. There's nowhere else in the world that's ever been so comfortable for me, and I doubt there ever will be. ''Holy crap, it's FREEZING!'' I can't exactly say the same for anybody else. While Darcy was accustomed enough to the cold visits home to have brought a decent coat, Quinn isn't quite so... aware, but it only took her a step and a half out the revolving doors of the airport in Halifax to learn. ''I know,'' Darcy'd caught up when she stopped in the blast of cold. ''You never really get used to it. That is, unless you're a crazy eskimo like the locals.'' ''We're maritimers, not eskimos. They're farther North.'' Quinn still looked near death. ''Seriously, Ames. I paid attention in geography and all, but I don't think it gets much colder or farther North.'' I was molested by Canada jokes during the full hour of driving it took to reach home, though Quinn had gracefully opted out of any beaver references in favor of critiquing the weather, scenery and how 'Everything's still so American'. Thankfully, the conversation didn't veer too far away from Quinn's curiosity -- mostly because she knows Darcy's skin crawls whenever a story has the word 'Mom' in it. She's a thrill, but she brings out the worst side of me -- dishonest to the only man who's ever been honest to me, a cheater, liar, thief, underling -- and seems to see it as the good in me. She loves me for my faults, for the fact that I'm so human and flawed, but he loves me despite them. No matter what my thoughts, at the end of the day, Darcy is still my other half, while Desade is a pleasurable tack-on. But I'm such an obsessive person that the tack-on drives me to do crazy shit, even moreso than Darcy ever would simply because of the situations I get into due to my affair. I can't truly say that I don't want to take back every moment with her, but I can't say I'd live a full life having never met her, either. In this plane, over the province I've always known as home, I can't imagine what the hell I was thinking when I offered to sacrifice my life and marriage just to re-up my Desade fix. As soon as I'm addicted to something, sanity's out the window. I've always been like this whether it's partners, drugs or championship belts. I don't stop. I'd nearly kill myself to hit the endgame, and it will.not.stop. I can't shut that part of my brain off. The only time I can really concentrate is beside my husband, and it always has been; He slows me down, cools me off and calms me. With him, titles or random cravings don't matter, living without having to be so impulsive and hyperactive is all that he desires of me -- there're no matches or inquiry panels. No referees. No pressure. ... That is, except the pressure to be honest. He doesn't ask me to tell him everything, but I'm fairly sure he'd expect me to tell him that I'm sleeping with someone else for cheap thrills on the road, and I don't understand my feelings for that person at all, except to say that I feel more about Darcy than even myself, which is a feat when you've got the ego of a woman who's crawled up from being a shitty pit fighter for drug money, to being a shitty Universal Champion for the power trip... and the drug money. My higher gears go at a speed that blurs the events within, leaving me to struggle making sense of it when I put the brakes on and get back down to where I really belong. My third face, Aimz, sits as a strange mixture - a speed in between. That's the champion, the one who doesn't want anything but what suits her. She's a big part of what's sneaking into Desade's hotel rooms at midnight, but she's also what's helping keep a roof over my head. Heading into the coming week, even though I'm laying with my husband as his Amy now, it's only a matter of time before I become her Amy and then, when I exist for Lance Marshall, Aimz gets a moment to come out and defend the career at the core of my many lives. But no matter how many faces I acquire, there's one fundamental problem: I can't drive stick. I'm not the one who can make the choice to switch gears. I'm stuck in automatic - a random revving of so much that doesn't make enough sense to call a real life. And I know how to stop it, but when the engine gets hot again, I can't say as I really want to.
