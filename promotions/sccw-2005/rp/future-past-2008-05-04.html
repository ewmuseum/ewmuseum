Future Past

4 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/08 To move forwards, sometimes you have to revisit your past, things that had been left behind. A cold desolate courtyard stands before me as the moon screams down clearly from the freezing winter’s night. I step forwards through the crisp snow which crunches and creaks underfoot as I stand before a door which would lead me to the only part of the jail I feared, the only part I still fear. I had already passed through what the other convicts so graciously dubbed the ‘red room’. Just a box really, only a select few even knew about it. The idea was conceived be a few of the elite within the complex, prisoners and wardens alike. In every jail you have those with power and those without. The red room was supposed to sort that out, a place for the heads to bend the rules and take what they viewed as rightfully theirs. Power. The red within the red-room was of course blood, from other wardens and prisoners a like. The heads took out their aggression and asserted their dominance on the weak members of the facility, low ranking privates new to the job not used to how the prison was run and the inmates who they knew wouldn’t fight back, the ones who had already been beaten by the prison and lost their fighting spirit, the ones who has been reduced to just a number. I remember clearly the stench from the room, the first few times it hit you it was as though someone forced your contents out of your stomach it was that nauseating, the smell of decay and death hanging in the air, stagnant but always there. Rats, mice and every other conceivable type of vermin scurried along the floor searching in vein for a piece of food, a piece of raw flesh, maybe a fresh drop of blood or the greatest delicacy a tied down defenceless human. In all fairness I was shocked at how well it had held up after the fire, the red was now slightly blackened and the vermin had gone but the smell was there, I’d heard it said once that death never leaves. ~Flashback 7 years ago~ Remember Ethan, no-one ever leaves the red room. Those were the last words that my cell mate had said to me before I was dragged out and I mean dragged, my face being ground along the floor, bits of rubble and glass digging into my face as blood poured silently onto the ground. You became used to the blood in here, there were always scuffles between inmates, countless broken noses countless broken limbs. It’s how it worked here. I had no idea where they were dragging me, the blindfold saw to that, people had thought that the red room was simply a myth something which had been created by the wardens to keep them in line, fear does great things for discipline. I pretty much knew that my time was up as I was suddenly lifted up and flung onto a chair, the cold hard wood being unforgiving as I landed solidly on it. I was aware of something scurrying around my feet, biting occasionally causing me to wince in pain, like being stabbed by tiny pins deep within your flesh. I suddenly felt my blindfold being removed and tentatively opened my eyes not knowing what I was going to see. A small room, with a moving floor, full of rodents the walls dripping with blood, fresh blood I began to look around for a body, or some indication that there had been someone here recently but there was none, even if there was a body it would more than likely have been devoured by the rats below me I knew instantly that I was in trouble. "So Ethan Justin Jones…, we hear that you have been making a bit of a nuisance of yourself here, getting into fights and all that other stupid stuff" spoke a deep voice with a hint of sarcasm, he was enjoying patronising me, everyone got into fights but I’d made the mistake of fighting back to one of the bosses. I wasn’t going to take any shit why should I, it’s survival of the fittest in here. "The thing is Jonesy I can call you Jonesy cant I? Is that you are new around here and you don’t know how things work. We own this establishment and you don’t, we make the rules not you" The man’s voice sounded a lot angrier now, his tone was becoming rapid and disjointed as though he were a man on the edge. "So what are you gonna do then? Kill me?" I rather foolishly asked, I had to test the waters see what their intentions were. If I had one chance to get out of the room alive it was going to be through will and strength. The man walked around in front of me, I knew him to look at but his name escaped me, a large and heavily built man grinning maniacally at me. "No Jonesy I’m not going to kill you, we just have to teach you a lesson, nothing personal you understand it’s just the way things work here." Joy, excitement all flowing through the mans voice like a child at Christmas. "Oh I understand" I mumbled with more than a subtle hint of sarcasm in my voice as a sharp pain began rushing through my feet, I looked down to find the warden had cut two large slits in the top of them with a large knife which he was holding gleefully looking up at me his eyes wide. "Does it hurt Jonesy? No I didn’t think it did, just wait a second though" he spoke giggling like a small child. I didn’t know what he meant by that, of course it hurt he had just sliced my feet open, then a moment before it struck I realised…the rats were still there I don’t know how I’d forgotten them I could still feel the rummaging around me but something was different, they were now in a frenzy, the scent of blood in their noses, their target the large gushing wounds on the top of my feet. They began to tear away at flesh and sinew, fighting amongst themselves to get to their first meal in days, the pain tore through me like a hurricane through a paper fence, my feet burning as I frantically tried to kick them away but their were simply too many for each one I kicked another five dove towards me until I simply sat back and allowed them to feast, not fighting it, not screaming. In retrospect that was probably a bad idea it turned out the warden got some sort of perverse kick out the pain of other men, the screams of excruciating agony. He moved forwards towards me clasping the knife tightly in his hands, his knuckles where white he was holding the knife that tightly, I looked up at him with little to no reaction in my eyes as he stared down at me. I opened my mouth slightly and said "Is that all you’ve got bitch?" It sent him into a rage he grabbed my face and hit me four times on the nose shattering it on the third blow, it had been broken before so I’m shocked it didn’t go before then, I still didn’t react this was a totally different type of strength than he was used to and it infuriated him to his inner most core, he then grabbed the knife and held it against my cheek for a moment before raking it up my face up to my ear. The temptation to scream was too much as my face felt as though it was on fire, a small cry of agony emanated from my mouth which too hurt as I stretched the wound even further apart the warden however seemed very pleased with himself as he stood back and admired his handiwork. "I’m very sorry I had to do that, but what can I say it’s my job in this place you have to be unscrupulous and moral less other wise little things like this could get to you." The warden had a gleam in his eye as he came towards me and untied me took a few steps back and simply stood there. "Go-on then Jonesy, it’s your turn." ~Cutback to modern day~ "I wanted to hit him there and then, but he would have been ready for me, my face had been sliced open and I was being eaten alive from the feet up I just left the room and returned to my cell, neither me or my cell mate mentioning the blood that was dripping across the room as I lay on my bunk". "Cut" boomed a voice from behind the cameraman as Frost stared forwards looking at the door ahead of him, he is holding his face lightly running his fingers over the scar, his story telling clearly evoking memories from his past. "Great work Ethan we’ll continue filming tomorrow night it’s too late to get anything else done now, are you sure that you will be ok to continue I mean that was pretty nasty stuff you were talking about there" Frost stands there silent for a moment before nodding towards the cameraman giving them a cue to start packing away from the long nights filming. This was where it had all began and the story was not about to end yet, people had to now where he came from. ~~~ There had been many events in his past that had caused him to run away numerous times in live and in his career. The beginning was ahead of him and Corvus was the first step, it would take a while to get back into his stride, hell he didn’t know if he’d ever get back to where he was, but he as not going to hide from his past anymore, he wasn’t going to let it control him. Every aspect of his life had led him to where he was now. A man who had been given another chance, he was not about to throw it away easily.
