Three Part Disharmony

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/18/2008 - 02/20/2008 02/18/2008 A large black Humvee swung up to the curb where Mayhem waited. No dainty H2 or H3, this was an old school military surplus conversion, and Mayhem thought it suited his personality perfectly. The only problem was he never got to actually drive it himself. The day he brought it home, Rose's eyes lit up, and she had taken to driving him everywhere in it. Today's trip to the doctor for a checkup to clear him to wrestle had been no exception. Rose didn't even wait for Mayhem to finish opening the door before she started in on him. 'So, what's the word? Are you cleared to try and kill yourself this week? Instead of a moonsault off the FUSEWire or whatever the fuck Smitty is calling it, maybe you could try a plancha out of the nosebleed seats.' With a deep sigh, Mayhem pulled himself up into the Hummer. 'Uh, hey babe.....Have I mentioned how hot you look in camouflage?' 'Oh no. No no no,' she shot back, pulling her mirrored sunglasses down to fix him with her stare. 'Don't you even think you are going to sweet talk your way out of this, Vincent Matthews, at least not without flowers and chocolate. Did the doc clear you to wrestle or not?' 'Yeah, I'm clear. He told me he'd excuse me out if I wanted, but if I really wanted to, I could wrestle.' Her expression softened, 'Jesus, Vin, you're bad enough off he'd write you a note? And you're STILL planning on facing Elliott Fuckhead Rollins for the LiveWire belt?' Mayhem shrugged. 'There wasn't any internal damage, all that blood was from me biting a hole in my cheek when my head hit that speaker when I landed on Bridden. He said I was probably concussed too.' He lowered his voice and added, 'I did manage to crack three ribs though, could you swing by Walgreen's so I can get these vicodin filled?' 'I swear, you need to buy stock in painkiller companies,' she said, shaking her head. 'Promise me you won't ever do anything that stupid again, 'K?' 'I can promise you I won't do anything like that against Elliott,' he said, before switching his voice to a croak. 'ELLL-EEEEE-OOOTTTTT....ELLL-EEEEEE-OOOTTTTT! PHONE HOOOOME!' Rose just looked at him, one eyebrow raised. 'I don't get it.' 'You...It's E.T. for fuck's sake! How can you NOT get it?' he asked. She turned the vehicle into the parking lot of the Walgreen's Mayhem used for prescriptions. 'Never saw it.' He stared at her like she had just spontaneously grown a third nostril. 'You...Oh, I am SO buying that and making you watch it.' He jumped out of the seat and told her, 'Be right back.' She watched him head into the store, pulling out a cellphone and dialing. 'Hey Dru, it's me,' she said after the voice mail picked up. 'We're on our way back. Tell Oz our resident psychopath is cleared to wrestle. A concussion and three cracked ribs, but he's cleared. See you later.' She sat, playing with the Hummer's sound system until she found a station that was playing something she liked, and didn't see Mayhem sneak out of the store and she jumped when he pulled the door open, one arm behind his back. 'Flowers....and chocolate....AND E.T. on DVD!' he proudly proclaimed, handing her a bouquet of fake roses and a Whitman's Sampler box. 'Let's go home, watch E.T. and have makeup sex?' 02/19/2008 'FUCKING OW!' Jimmy yelled, picking himself up off the mat of the ring in the basement of what they had taken to calling 'Casa Mayhem.' He launched himself at Mayhem, grappling with the much larger man. 'This is supposed to be half-speed so we don't kill each other.' 'This is half-speed,' Mayhem said as he bulled Jimmy into a corner. 'Not my fault you're a wuss!' Mayhem punctuated this with a stiff knife-edge chop across Jimmy's bare chest, leaving a red welt. 'WOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!' Mayhem yelled, backing out of the corner and doing his best Ric Flair strut. Outside the ring, sitting on folding metal chairs, Oz, Dru and Rose watched the men sparring. For the most part, Rose was doing most of the watching, Oz and Dru were in the middle of a discussion regarding Mayhem's upcoming match against Elliott Rollins for the LiveWire title. 'No, that is absolutely not what I am saying,' said Oz. 'I am saying he fought plenty of fights under mixed martial arts rules, many of which were more restrictive than the LiveWire rules, and I fail to see why he cannot simply adapt his style and treat this like it was another fight.' 'Because that is the perfect way for him to lose his first legitimate title shot in his entire career? Because Elliott Rollins is a perfect opponent for Mayhem, since he isn't a little twerp like Jay Bridden and may very well attempt to go toe to toe with Mayhem?' Drusilla paused, taking a swig of water. Oz leaned in, lowering his voice, 'LiveWire bouts are called incredibly tight to the rules, and if you think Duluth will not instruct his referee to make Mayhem toe the line or disqualify him, you are sadly mistaken. And in his current state of mind, it will not take much to provoke him to another senseless act of violence likely to result in just such an ending.' In the ring, Mayhem whipped Jimmy into the opposite corner and leaned on the ropes looking down on the discussion. 'You know, I can still hear you, Oz.' 'So, the LiveWire title can change hands on a disqualification, right?' Jimmy joined Mayhem, leaning on the ropes as well. 'Why don't we just take a page from Hunter's book and get Rollins DQed? Badda boom, badda bing, your winner and new LiveWire champion, Mayyyyyyy-heeeeeeeem!' Oz made a face, 'The day I stoop to using Alexandra Pierce's tactics will be a cold day in Hell indeed.' Rose and Dru shared a look when he said this, but if he noticed, he didn't slow down. 'Besides, Elliott Rollins is not Cyrus Raynes, and McDormant is a much more seasoned veteran than Sarah Riley could ever hope to be.' Jimmy had moved behind Mayhem by now, and he suddenly grabbed him from behind and attempted a schoolboy rollup and pin, which Mayhem easily powered out of. Jimmy ran at the ropes, rebounding off them to charge Mayhem as he stood, and Jimmy lowered his shoulder and went for a spear, planting himself solidly in Mayhem's ribs. Suddenly, Mayhem roared in pain as Jimmy slammed into the cracked bones, grabbing the young man's head and driving several knees into it swiftly, three rapid shots. He grabbed Jimmy around the waist and wrenched him overhead into the beginnings of a powerbomb. In a flash, Rose was on the apron, yelling at Mayhem. 'Vin! NO!' Mayhem turned, carrying Jimmy's limp form over to the ropes, and threw him over them. Luckily, the area to that side of the ring was covered in thick padding, designed to practice moves just like this one. Clutching his ribs, Mayhem rolled out of the ring and started to stomp off before turning. 'This is fucking stupid! I don't want the fucking LameWire title! All it does it keep me distracted from Hunter another week. I've already got Jay Bridden thinking I'm his ladder out of the mid-card, and now I have to worry about Elliott Rollins and Wet Willy? Fuck that. You're my manager, Oz, get me my fucking match with Hunter, or find me someone who can!' The room was quiet, as they watched Mayhem storm out of the room and up the stairs. Rose watched him go, turning to look at her brother still laying motionless on the padding outside the ring, trying to decide what to do. It was Drusilla who made that choice for her. 'Sweetie, Oswald and I will make sure James is not hurt, you go talk to him, see if you can talk some sense into him. If the 'you will have something Hunter does not' approach gets you nowhere, try the Jon Rhine thing we spoke of. If not, get him in the hot tub and use your skills of feminine persuasion on him....' Rose could only grin at that as she took off up the stairs after her man, leaving Drusilla to check on Jimmy and Oz to sit, stroking his beard thoughtfully. 02/20/2008 Oswald Remington Smythe sighed as the voice mail on the other end of his phone call connected, then he chuckled after listening to the message. 'William! Oswald Smythe. I was hoping to have a word with you about our respective wrestlers and their match, but it would seem you are otherwise occupied, so I shall leave a message.' 'I do not want Mayhem to beat Rollins. There, I said it. You can probably guess some of my reasons why, the others are not important.' 'What is important is how your man plans to handle my Path of Destruction. The way I see it, he has three choices: He can rely on his talent and this can be a straightforward match, the very epitome of what the LiveWire championship was designed for, in which case I fear we may have a new champion on our hands. This is not some punk kid like Duncan Drake or some washed up old schooler like Tony Pride, this is Mayhem.' 'Or, he can provoke Mayhem into disqualifying himself. Should be a rather easy endeavor, Vincent is a man of quick temper. Of course, I trust you were witness to Mayhem's rampage last show. Provocation may not be the smartest idea.' 'Which brings us to choice three. Three ribs. Right side. Cracked from the suicide leap onto Bridden. His breathing is shallow at best, his stamina will be suspect. I suggest using that to your best advantage. If you are thinking of Elliott working those ribs, however, you will find that the esteemed Mr. Hawke is not the only individual in this federation who knows how to use a cane. Do have a good evening, William.'
