Broken Angels

30 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/07/07 "Evil enters like a needle and spreads like an oak tree.." Ethiopian Proverb Everyone has a story to tell and anyone who claims otherwise is lying. It can range from the most simplistic wife and two kids fairy tale, to the grim and harsh realities of life retold. You can pass anyone on the street and their face might just tell you some of the story, or some like Paul Cain, hide it all very well inside. Then there comes a trigger which runs the risk of bringing everything into the open, spilling your deepest and darkest secrets. All that you kept safe within you could begin to creep out for the whole world to witness. Act fast, do what you can to stop that from happening, find anyway to do it. Paul Cain is that man, fighting to keep the past from meeting the present and chasing his future. The public will believe whatever they see and for now they accept him and even congratulated him on passing his FUSE initiation. It would all be okay so long as he could keep the past out of the way, stop those ‘things’ from haunting him and getting under his skin. There had to be a way to do that, there just had to be. He questioned himself from the moment he stood at the church door. Paul had never been religious; in fact he’d always cursed God for dealing him the hand of "Abusive Mother" and "Absent Father". So why he found himself now sitting in the front aisle, looking up at the stained glass window of Jesus on the Cross was beyond anything he could understand. On the outside, Paul Cain was the kind of man who oozed confidence but displayed it in the right way. He respected the people, he admired his colleagues, or at least those that deserved admiration, and he believed in the fair fight. He understood family values and that morality was not a dying concept, that it could be kept alive in the modern day. You could entrust your children to him for an autograph signing and a conversation to put a smile on their faces. Despite the swagger and the self belief, he was the closest to a baby-kissing politician that you could get. On the inside though, things were a little more complicated than all that. Do not be fooled, Paul Cain is all of those things. He loves his wife and daughter even if the absence that comes with being on the road, might tell them otherwise. He would do anything for his family including die for them, something he had considered might happen not so long ago. That is a whole different story for another time and a less pure environment. The friends that he did have knew they could rely on him and that behind the brash exterior was someone who would stand back to back with them and fight until the battle was won or they had expended their last breath. He was a man of good intention but like all men in today’s world, there was always the potential for corruption and derailment. Paul Cain was indeed all these things to all these people but there were some situations that they just wouldn’t be able to understand. Those were the things that needed to be kept hidden under lock and key, never to be released. Yet there was a risk, always a risk that the past would be brought back to life. "You are afraid my son. Fear is the Devil’s work that must be ignored." Paul Cain slowly looked up from the stone floor to the priest that stood before him. The expression on the religious man’s face was one of kindness but his grey eyes showed concern too for a man who was clearly lost enough to walk into God’s home. Reverend O’Neill had seen it all before, ranging from the terrified pregnant schoolgirl to the elderly mother that had outlived her only son. The world outside of God’s loving touch was a cruel one, designed to test even the strongest of spirits. He could see the anxiety in Paul’s eyes, a man built to be a fighter but as with any fighter, there was always an Achilles Heel. Even the strongest have their frailties and Reverend O’Neill could see this clearly as he touched a hand to Paul’s left shoulder. "Do you know how long it took to get my life back on track? I nearly lost everything, my wife and my daughter, all of it. This was supposed to be a fresh start for us, after all the road humps and everything…I thought we could finally make a go of things, get our life back on track…Now, just as I get us settled again…Just as my wife can put her arms around me again, kiss me again, make love to me again…Be proud of me again…THIS happens…" Reverend O’Neill sighed lightly and sat down next to Paul on the bench. He looked up at Jesus and considered to himself the strength that he showed during his own trials and tribulations. "My son, the Devil will always use his agents to corrupt us whenever he can…This situation is no different…By showing fear, you are going to give them the strength to break you down even more…Yes, you have a new beginning and a chance to redeem yourself…restore yourself…grow stronger once more…Be the hero you once were…And you can still do that, you can still be an inspiration and a guiding light…Your mission doesn’t have to be affected by those possessed by the Devil…" Paul shook his head repeatedly in disagreement. "You don’t understand…The past’s already chasing me about…I’ve already faced the second generation of a family that despises me…" Reverend O’Neill smiles gently to the obviously anxious Cain. "And defeated him…" The response to that was obvious as Paul scoffed at the priest. "Our paths will cross again; somehow…It won’t end there..." Standing to his feet, O’Neill beckons Cain towards him as he walks to the stained glass window. He gestures with his open hand to the portrait of Jesus on the Cross experiencing his final act of sacrifice. "The Son Of God did not fear his future and he has shown me not to fear mine either. Do not be afraid of what is to come to you Paul…" Bowing his head slightly, Paul’s voice becomes hushed. "It’s not where I’m going that I fear…It’s where I’ve been…Who I’ve met…What I’ve done…" Reverend O’Neill slips his hand into the pocket of his robe and pulls out a golden Cross on a chain which he holds out to Paul. "The Devil will always return for something that he wants…When he does…You must do one thing…" Yet Paul is not listening as he stares at the face of Jesus Christ, questioning why he is here and why a stained glass window can give him the answer when he has never asked for one before. His mind asks when his true personality will be tarnished by the lies and deceit of others and when he must once again pick up the pieces of his reputation. It had almost ruined him once before and now it could happen all over again. No matter how many babies he kisses, how many men he saves, how many villains he stands up to and how many autographs he signs, the poison will be strong enough to obliterate it all. The new beginning will be stained and then the people will see him as no better than the figures of hate that they have created. No. I have to focus. Sean Sterling. The name flashes across his thoughts of chaos and turmoil as he remembers the journey he had set out on one week ago. He had defeated a Prince in his quest to become King, yet one victory a champion it does not make. He had a job to do, Sean Sterling was waiting for him and he was breathing down Paul’s neck while the vultures were watching from afar. Take out the nearest enemy and then prepare yourself for the others for they will come. They will come from far and wide for their trophy and the defense must be ready. Paul Cain had come here to become King and if he failed to do that then the Devil will have truly won. Paul Cain must do one thing. "When the Devil comes Paul…You must do one thing…" Reverend O’Neill’s repeated words caused Paul to snap out of his reverie as he turned to see the chain being held out by O’Neill’s outstretched hand. Instinctively Paul reached out his own hand and took the Cross chain around his fingers. Sean Sterling. The enemies. All of them. Reverend O’Neill’s final word hit Paul’s mind like a gunshot. "Fight." Sudden fade out to black. A female hand with long dark purple fingernails grips the pen that spills her thoughts onto the paper. The room is barely lit save for the lamp that sits on her desk, bathing the page she is angrily scrawling on, in light. The moon in the sky tries to force its way in through the curtains but they quickly snuff it out before it reaches her dark little world. She likes it that way these days, the darkness makes her feel comfortable and safe. It never used to be like this, she was always so controlled and calm, she was never this angry.. She reaches across the desk and picks up a remote control which she holds out behind her. As she does so, the music in the room suddenly roars upwards just as it gets to the chorus. The pen goes back onto the paper and she begins writing again as she spits out the words to the song under her breath. "I’ll get inside you…I’ll get inside you…" This was not a woman singing the words of a song; this was a statement of intent. She had spent all this time suffering because of his treatment and now she wanted justice. His little idyllic life of a wife, child and a resurgent career, he didn’t deserve any of it, not after what he had done. If she could bring it all down around him, if she could break his spirit, his soul, his world, then she would be appeased. How dare he think he could hurt her with no consequences as if it was the perfect crime. Her breathing was heavy with frustration and as she put the final words from her mind into print, she said to herself with disgust: "Fucking men, pathetic little creatures." She dropped down the pen as she completed her masterpiece and then leant forward to a collection of envelopes at the back of the desk. Moving the letter to one side, she picked up one of the envelopes and put it down in the middle of the table. The legs of her chair scraped across the wooden floor as she rose to her feet. There was the authoress, long dark brown hair with matching eye colour and a beautiful figure encased in a tight black dress with black stockings and knee length leather boots. She was the Goddess that men craved the opportunity to worship, it had always been that way. They would flock around her like bees to a honey pot as sex crazed little boys will to a beautiful woman. However those boys weren’t about to be in the position that Paul Cain was. To him, she was in the past, a world away from his current peaceful life. To her, it was nowhere near over with and he needed reminding of what he had done. She picked up a bottle of her favourite perfume from the bedside table and idly sprayed some around the room. "You used to be able to smell my scent from the hotel corridor, couldn’t you? I’d make sure you could; because I loved it when you wanted to tear down the walls to get to me. I remember how your eyes used to look when you saw me again, animalistic and you proved it when you would throw me up against the door…" She moved to her desk again and held the nozzle of the perfume bottle over the envelope and letter with a soft sigh. "You couldn’t keep your hands off me from the moment you walked in…Satin negligee and matching thongs drove you mad…Your lips on my neck, teeth biting at me and your hard cock bulging through your pants against my soaking wet cunt…You made it so obvious…Pinning my arms to the wall by the wrists…Making me feel helpless…You knew I loved it…My legs would end up wrapped around your waist so I could feel you pushing against me, making me wetter and wetter by the second…Then you’d tear at my clothes, rip them to pieces…Throwing me onto the bed…It was what we both wanted…" The perfume was then sprayed onto the papers, enough to last the journey. "We drove each other crazy…fucking…" Suddenly she smashed the bottle down onto the table away from her masterpiece, glass flying in all directions and the perfume flooding out. Her hands quickly scrabbled over the debris as she snatched up a piece of glass with her fingers, her heart racing and the adrenalin revving through her body. Without any hesitation she sliced the glass across her forefinger, instantly causing blood to begin to run from the cut. Her breathing changed from anxiety to almost exhilaration as she traced the letters "M L" in blood at the bottom of the letter she had written. "Now…I’m just going to drive you…fucking crazy." She slowly slipped the letter into the envelope, and sealed it with her tongue. "I’m going to destroy you…Little boy…" Scene fades out to black.
