My name is Edward Lambert...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Sitting in a rather rundown hotel room does not lend itself to casual romance between many couples other than those on secret trysts or young and newly married couples. Edward and Kia Lambert have been together for the better part of two decades now; albeit some of those early years were rather on and off. When Edward Lambert checked into the motel on the outskirts of Ponca City Oklahoma his mind was far from focused on romance despite his wife and he having traveled together on one of the rare occasions that did not involve him stepping into a ring and bleeding for the adoration of the masses. Kia however, had her mind of making the most of the time that they had together away from both their home town of Lexington Kentucky, and away from their 6 year old son Drake. Which is why, seven hours after they had arrived, Edward was still not one step closer to finding out where Ibrahim Seck had scurried off to when he escaped from captivity in Edwards’ basement. While Kia stood in the shower that had last seen a refurbish in the nineteen fifties Edward sat on the lone chair inside the room and stared down at the small voice recorder in his hands. A burning cigarette sat in the ashtray before him on the small table and a tape recorder sat beside it. "My chest hurts more as each day passes and it’s getting harder to take a deep breath." He paused for a moment as he reached across the table and lifted the cigarette from the ashtray and took a deep drag before continuing his harsh monologue. "The cigarettes aren’t really relaxing my lungs as much as they were before, so I’m smoking even more heavily than I was before. I know this; I’ve gone from two packs a day of the Lucky Strike Filters to three packs of non filtered Luckies." "I’m making this recording for anyone who needs to know why I’m doing this. Why I have to do what I must do, especially for Kia and Drake if something goes wrong while I do what I gotta do. Kia had no hand in this, so if anything blows back at us because of it, none of it will hit her, it’s all on my head, she’s here because I drug her along, not because she took an active part in any of this." Edward stubbed out the remnants of the cigarette in the ashtray and immediately lit another cigarette from the pack before him. "My problems with Ibrahim can all be encapsulated into a crisis of Faith. Not my faith, no chance of that existing anymore after my childhood. No, this all stems from a difference of opinion on how we should act and live our lives. Seck is in effect a one man cult of personality who believes in himself for some twisted form of salvation." Edward snorts audibly and blows a cloud of smoke towards the ceiling. "Personally I believe he’s full of shit. I think that the truth of life is that nobody, no matter who you are, no matter how important you are, nobody lives forever. Seck demands that people drink his kool-aid and support him as he leads them to the Promised Land and everlasting immortality. Fuck that right in the goddamned ear. The truth is that nobody gets out of life alive. We all die. Winston Churchill died. Albert Einstein died. Socrates, Plato, and even Jesus Christ died if you believe in him. There is no immortality of the body, no true way to march through the ages unbowed and awake." "Seck wants to people to let him save them, he wants them to cling to him in mind, body, and soul. He takes the weak and the idiotic and makes them agree to follow him, in essence, he makes them give up everything that makes them unique and become clones of his belief and his will. He wanted that for me, he wanted that for Kia, and he wanted that for my Nathaniel." Edward pauses a moment as he mentions the name that rarely crosses his lips, the name of his son now dead almost nine years; that of his firstborn son. "Part of our difference of faith is he believes that you MUST do all these great works, and live the way he says you have to just so that when you die you can achieve eternal life. It’s some twisted parody of Christianity save for one flaw." Edward takes another draw from the cigarette. "There is immortality. Not physical immortality, but there is immortality regardless. Try cracking open a history book some time. Read it, from cover to cover and tell me the names that stand out. Genghis Khan, George Washington, King George, Adolf Hitler, and Robert E. Lee all achieved immortality. People who moved their times, people who built monuments, brilliant leaders, deceitful schemers, murderers, or kings all of them. Their names will live as long as the memory of men, and considering that we still remember the ancient Pharaohs, which means that the immortality of human memory is a damned long time." Edward thumps himself in the chest repeatedly before continuing. "People who built great monuments, such as the Golden Gate Bridge have left their marks on the world. Great inventors and architects have constructed wonders that will be remembered long after their creators are dust. So, if you discount the religious nonsense that Seck drives into peoples heads the only way to achieve anything close to immortality is by being a genius in your own field no matter what it is. And Seck takes that away from people by imprinting himself upon them. He isn’t so much trying to save their souls, as he is trying to forge himself immortality in memory." "He’s doing the same thing that I have spent my career doing, except he’s doing it by reprogramming other people into believing in him as their savior." "I am not a general. I am not a statesman. I will build no great works. I will not write the greatest novel. My generation is going to have an incredibly hard time making more than a footnote in history. In one hundred years, nobody will remember Edward Lambert as a father, nobody will remember him as a friend. The only way that I can make an eternal impression is to be the best at what I am; by being a violent self serving son of a bitch." Edward stands up from the chair and grinds the cigarette out into the ashtray with finality. He steps around to where he threw down his duffle bag when they came into the hotel. All the while he maintains his running commentary and explanation. "I’m not going to pretend that I have anything beyond my own self interest at heart. I don’t give two twisted fucks about the lives of the people Seck has manipulated into believing in him. Fuck them each and every single one for being of such a weak character that they allow him to do this to them. What I care about is how he plans to do it, what he will do when he realizes how much power that their faith in his grants him in both their life, mine, and others. Seck will achieve his own brand of immortality, and be able to do whatever he wants to my family after I’m gone. I can’t allow a danger like that to exist." "Let’s make no bones about it. I kept the man captive in my basement for long enough to drive a mad man even further into his psychosis. I’m not going to put him away to defend the "little people" from his antics. I do not want anyone who listens to this to miss the truth. I want them to understand that I am doing this to protect the two most important people in my life from one of the worst mistakes that I ever made in my life." Edward reaches into the duffle bag and pulls out two rolls of duct tape, a set of handcuffs, a tazer, and a small truncheon. These items he throws into a smaller bag that he pulls from the duffle along with a moderately sized rectangular hard plastic case. "Understand that I am not a man to emulate. I am not a man to admire. I may have worked hard to be where I am, and to be who I am, but I have burned myself at the altar to me. If I am to be remembered, it will be as one of the modern gladiators, a man who risked life and limb just to entertain… or as a kidnapper who stuck the modern messiah into his basement for years on end. One or the other and fuck if I’m going to care soon enough." "Ibrahim is plotting something, he always does, it’s who he is. What, I don’t know or care. I’m not sure how the fuck he got out of the basement where I had him locked away. I don’t care how he got out anymore; all I know is that I have to find a way to put that son of a bitch back before he finds a way to get his claws into Kia or Drake." Edward turns to the table and the small recorder sitting there. His long stride takes him to the table in three steps where he throws a folded piece of paper down onto the table beside the recorder. "I shall live forever in the memory of those who follow wrestling. In a hundred years they will still speak in hushed and reverent tones about my career and my willingness to fuck myself up so that other people can forget that they are nothing. They remember the murderers, they remember the spectacles, and they remember the accidents. They will remember the name Edward Carter Lambert. They will remember the BloodAngel. Like me, Hate me, I do not fucking care how people see me. I am not an icon of fidelity. I have lied, I have cheated, I have done my best to hurt good people. My legacy and my name will live on, not as a good man, but they Will live on." He pauses a moment with his finger hovering over the "stop" button. His harsh and gravelly voice gets harder as he speaks the final words. "And so will my family, no matter what I have to fucking do." Then he stabs his finger down stopping the recorder. * * * Five minutes later, when Kia exits the shower, her body covered in a light blue towel and her hair wrapped inside another towel in a beehive on top of her head, Edward and the small bag he brought with him is nowhere to be seen. Sitting on the table beside the partially full ashtray is the tape recorder and the note addressed to Kia. Curious she sits down in the chair and opens the note. Her complexion pales as she reads the note her husband left for her, and then she presses the play button on the recorder. From the recorder came that harsh and gravelly voice she knew so well. "My name is Edward Lambert, and I’m going to kill Ibrahim Seck."
