Breaking into Pieces

2 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 2nd, 2008 A lifetime of agony and pain. You couldn't imagine the excruciating pain that I've suffered because of you; because of all of you. He lays there, the hallucinations and illusions getting worse by the minute. For weeks, he swore he had killed a man and had been housed in a prison. Fake. It wasn't until he realized that the walls were indeed talking to him that he knew he was free of the bonds that he swore he was shackled to. Except, in all reality, he is shackled to them, losing each moment around him while the next one hits him squarely in the face. You bitch and you moan about how your God doesn't love you, but it is indeed and truly the other way around. You just continually shovel shit upon my shoulders and expect me to deal with it. An eternity of suffrage is what I've damned to and it's because of you ungrateful peons. Sickening. Just sickening. It's truly sickening, watching a man curled up into a ball wondering when his death will come. He waits for the reaper with bated breath, but he never comes as each second passes by feeling like an eternity to him. He used to live in a world where millennia’s were like seconds to him. The throne of which he had sat upon had given him a perspective on things that he no longer had. Oh, how he longed for it. The throne that which made him King. The name is simple in itself. Malachi. The prophet of God. I'm the prophet of myself, and yet you infidels don't even believe it when you see it before your very eyes. You've asked for a sign of God and instead of giving you a sign, I've given myself. Yet, it's not enough for you all. It's never enough. When will you learn? When will you listen? He coughs. The sound is so sickening it's almost enough to make a grown man throw up on the ground before him. He fights to his knees, knowing that he has to continue to fight for no one else. Part of him wonders though if he should fight for them, if they're worth saving. He's almost certain of it that they're not. You look upon me Aimz with disdain and disgrace. Who're you to think that you can truly judge a God when you're nothing but a cuntwhore? I created you in my image and you've done nothing to live up to it. You've destroyed every stone and step that I've put in your way to lead you to success and you chose this! You don't deserve the jizz that drowns your face in ecstasy and pleasure from your paying customers. I step in the ring with you and I have to worry about catching AIDS from one of your festering sores on your bottom lips. Tell me how you've lived a just life! Tell me how you've lived a life worthy of your God?! The raised voice nearly causes him to spew his lungs and voice box. Somehow, he's able to keep it all down as he pounds his head into wooden floor. So much power is stored inside of him and it just itches to get out, but he's no longer in the eternal body he once held. No, now he lives in a body meant for mortal men. He is no mortal man. Every shred of fabric that is reality is ripping before his very eyes and he's powerless to stop it. His world is crumbling at his very feet and he can only watch on in horror. I'll step into that ring, Aimz, and try to teach you the error of your ways. Yet, you won't listen. They never do. They think they're just and infinitely smarter than that which taught them, and all it does is anger me. I was never an angry God, Aimz, but you all have turned me that way. That anger though, it burns away inside of me, and it's not use to that feeling. It's a poison that I wish I could expel from my body, but every time that I do, it increases tenfold. How could you handle it? You couldn't. The alcohol and the drugs haven't stemmed the illusions. He's desperate to find the answer, but it's not coming. He vividly remembers sitting inside of a psychologist office and her trying to get him to bear his sole. He painfully recalls the woman coming on to him. Only one problem. It never happened. The shards of glass are slowly falling to the ground, turning into pieces of sand. Will you let me show you the errors of your way, Aimz? Or will you continue to wonder... aimlessly? He rolls onto his back and clutches his chest as he feels his heart racing much faster than he had ever felt before. For a brief moment, he's sure that this is it; that this is the end. Unfortunately, it's only the beginning. Black.
