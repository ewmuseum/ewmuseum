Just Call Me The Bus Driver

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today "A mistake which is commonly made about neurotics is to suppose that they are interesting. It is not interesting to be always unhappy, engrossing with oneself, malignant and ungrateful, and never quite in touch with reality." –Cyril Connolly Who: Prince Pride When: Exactly 47 Minutes Ago Where: Students First Bus Depot Why: World Domination What: Read below "So this’ll fit a million people?" [That’s Prince Pride, resident second generation superstar and your Savior] "Yes it will" [That’s Ben, we know its Ben because his name tag says so. We would describe Ben, but why? He’s the operator of the Washington DC metropolitan area school bus chain, you can figure out what he looks like, plus, his name is Ben.] "Well, not all a million people on one bus, that’ll take quite a few buses. [Pause] Why do you need to transport a million people?" [Matter of factly and dismissively] "To start the destruction of professional wrestling and sports entertainment" [Awkward pause] "Oh, okay. Will that be cash or credit card?" "Do you accept checks?" "No" […And we fade for a moment, to the right now. We’re inside one of the before mentioned yellow school buses, Prince Pride is sitting in the drivers seat staring happily into the camera, his wild lion mane hair not restricted today, it flows freely, he wears a "Free Michael Vick" T-Shirt and a pair of basketball shorts, and Nike sandals. He smiles…] "This is a first time for me my adoring public?" [Prince pauses] "I’ve never been involved in something so large, so epic, so life defining, something that could change the face of our country as we know it, and Prince Pride, your savior, will be in the drivers seat, if you will." [Prince smiles at his clever pun] "I am not referencing my extra-curricular activities, no, no, those will be fully revealed in just 72 hours; what I am referencing is the opportunity to step inside of the squared circle for what will inevitably be one of the last times if things go my way, and have the opportunity to inside the ring and outside of the ring make the biggest impact anyone has ever seen." [Prince Smiles] "You’re probably saying to yourselves, but Prince, even though you’re our Savior, you realistically don’t have much of a chance at winning this match up. [Pride looks intense] And you know what I’d say to you…" [Prince scowls] "You’re right!" [Pride pauses for a moment] "You weren’t ready for that, were you? You’re damn right, I have no business winning this match, I’m not established, I’m not a name player, hell, I’m not even a member of a self serving faction like that Lane character, I’m not some altruistic self sacrificing nice guy like John Rhine or Jason Cruise, and I’m not legally retarded and crazy like Mayson Colby, and I’m not nearly as melodramatic as Paul Cain" [Pride shrugs] "But what I am is dangerous. [Pride sneers] And why? Because nobody expects me to win, nobody cares if I win, and that’s my greatest asset, because your indifference to me, will cause you to look past me, and right when you’re looking past me, I’ll be standing on top of you, and who knows at the end of the night, maybe just maybe, this Prince will be ready to be crowned King…" [Prince glares off] "And then what? I’ll get to face Clinton Sage or Tanaka at some other hyped up event, and I can take destiny into my hands, I can Carpe Diem, I can seize the moment. And in a stroke of athletic dominance and clear luck, maybe just maybe, just like his father, Prince Pride would be on top of the wrestling world…" [Pride stands, imagining the moment, he opens the emergency exit door and crawls onto the roof of the bus, standing triumphantly, still gazing into his day dream] "And there I’d stand with all of my little saved ones chanting, "Pride, Pride, Pride, Pride", I can hear it now, and then I’d slowly walk to the back holding the belt high, for all of FUSE to see, and in my honor have the worlds biggest bon-fire, inviting all of the fans to come outside, as I stand triumphant with the symbol of professional wrestling held high in my hands, the masses cheering, the fires burning, and I’d raise the belt higher and higher and higher, and then in the most glorious moment in this business, I, Prince Pride, the savior, the son of "The Foundation", Tony Pride [Prince sneers at the mere mention of the name]… [And slowly lowers his hands] "Would toss that disgusting belt into the fiery blaze, and watch it melt away, and spit and piss on all it symbolizes! [Pride gleefully symbolizes] "So be afraid FUSE, be very afraid, because some of you want to win because of glory, some of you want to win because of fame, some of you want to win just because it’s a Sunday night and you have nothing else to do. And some of you, some of you want to win because of a love, a love for this business, this profession, and the only thing that can destroy love…" [Pride pauses] "…Is hate! And I hate everything that the Universal Championship represents, and if and when my hands grasp it, I will destroy all it is and all it has been." [Pride laughs and moves his hands in an "awe shucks" movement] "Don’t worry, you all can thank me later, and just remember when wrestling is replaced on cable by stationary bike racing, you can thank me…" [Pride stomps triumphantly and points at the camera] "…Prince Pride, YOUR SAVIOR!" [Prince Smiles, as we begin to fade Ben walks into the picture stuffing a piece of SPAM into his mouth] "…That’ll be fifty bucks extra for standing on top of the bus…" [And surreally, we fade and get ready for the WAR IN WASHINGTON!]
