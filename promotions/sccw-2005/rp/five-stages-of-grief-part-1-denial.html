Five Stages of Grief: Part 1: Denial

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Some time after Sin-Con The tears had stained the pillow at night following the phone call she had done. Legion had reassured her everything would be alright but in the back of her mind she just knew something had happened , given the line of work that her love had done in the past she was always worried the time would come. Normally after the shows Luci would give Lauren a phone call just to make sure everything was safe but since the PPV things had changed… The thought crossed her mind again that something had happened to Lauren, the tears began to flow again but another emotion started to show on Luci’s face… that of rage. 24 hours after Sin On Spike: location undisclosed The three people had entered the abandoned place cloaked by the shadows as the door shut behind them with a sound akin to those moments in a horror movie where the main character says the immortal line of nothing can go wrong. " The video camera’s in place?" "Yes Master." " Good. Time for Luci and I to broadcast the revelation once again." Regular users of youtube would be surprised on what popped up the following morning: a video called Death and Rebirth. The only way it could be accessed was by cracking a code about who is contained in the ninth circle of hell on a website titled Chaos Storm.net A transcript was created by a user calling themselves Chaossphere87: (A logo shows up on the screen featuring a snake wrapped around an upside down cross, regular watchers of ESEN TV and a promotion called New frontier Wrestling recognise the logo to be that of the Rev 22:20 broadcasts used by Legion during his feud with Teresa Quaranta.) We hear a computerised voice… Pictures in the background are that of Jason Ryan at Halloween Hysteria cutting his promo about joining Legion’s side. ‘So be it were the words spoken by a man who had given up hope, a man pushed to the breaking point by personal problems and a demon he just couldn’t shake.’ ‘He believed that by joining the Devil’s Advocate’s idea of chaos he could try to control and quash the plans…’ (Fan cam footage is shown of the ending of the Come As You Are match in which Legion put a bloody Ryan through a chair with added glass with a Gateway to Hell) ‘He was wrong.’ ‘Following this a man who deemed himself ‘The Puppetmaster’ realised there are some people you just can’t manipulate…’ A song plays in the background while footage is played of the SOS match in the style of a Saw montage: The song being the M Shawn Crahan remix of Sleep With One Eye Open by Bring Me The Horizon. As the final chorus of Fuck You hits we see Legion putting von Doom through the windshield repeatedly. The footage then becomes that of a night vision camera (which we can only assume is held by Luci) as we see Legion in a Machine Head ‘The Blackening’ t-shirt and jeans stained in blood. We can only guess if the blood in question is von Doom’s, Jason’s or a combination of both… ‘To all that can see this congratulations on cracking the code and for figuring out that the ninth circle contains those that betrayed their friends… I expect Jason Ryan to be watching this from his home probably drinking himself into a stupor… The next question you’re all wondering is why am I here… from now on those who are lucky enough will become one of the new Flesh. Jason I figured out that you are just one of those people that doesn’t follow the rules very well, I expected after your little speech a few weeks ago you would learn that if you join someone you’re supposed to help them but the problem is you wanted to bring the spotlight onto you and your little bubble of isolation. I tried to help yet you turned me down so I let you believe everything would be fine in the triple threat and I decided the world could do without you… You being a man that doesn’t know when to keep the rage in check you were too much of a timebomb and more risk to the plans were not what I needed so I decided to replace you. However you along with everyone else will learn of him this week on Temptation in fact the name of this week is fitting in Canadian Chaos. A chance to get close to the crown of this sport in the Universal title of Sin City where I may have to face the elite in Lane Stevens, Wyatt Connors himself in the old plan of I’ll pick my own contender, the newest member of our roster in Xavier Kannon and then there’s people like von Doom. James… I will make sure that our final act will be this week if fate allows it, I retired you before, I shall do so again. But a new year brings a new start and I shall leave you all with three words: Chaos Is Dead. Turn the camera off.’ Back to after Sin-con The rage had quickly subsided, the room had become a clutter of glass and wood mixed with blood. Luci looked at the reflection of herself in the mirror and realised she had to face it… the anger needed to be controlled but not before finding out more about why Lauren had not rang her. Even if it meant dealing with Alex Pierce.
