Prelude To Despair

18 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/09/07 From Despair to Where? It grabs you like a hunger. A syringe filled with liquid is slowly inserted into his arm, a belt tied tightly around his arm causing his already large veins to protrude further, the finger tentatively hovers over the plunger for a moment before pressing down, the liquid screaming through your veins. A hunger that you can never quite quell. For a moment there is the doubt, that the hit won’t have an effect that you will be left alone in your pit of self despair then though it hits you. The heroin takes effect and then you enter the state of euphoria, even though it is a simple chemical reaction between brain and the heroin toxins but to a junkie it is a state of euphoria, you are taken to another world where absolutely nothing can touch you, the world simply doesn’t exist. Then it begins to go all wrong. The euphoria goes and you start to come down, you begin to feel dizzy and nauseous, your body begins to feel as though it has tripled in weight you simply cannot move this is the secondary stage where you just slump wherever you have shot up, fall on the floor and waste away. The addiction then begins to take full control of you, you come down from the high and the desire to obtain more and more grows, the hunger and the need to feel the same as you just did just those few blissful seconds ago and yet now for the junkie it seems like an eternity away. Even worse… You become immune to it, the more you take, the more you need to get the same hit, it’s an ever increasing circle more money is needed, more drugs are needed for the same hit. Now comes the truly fun stuff as you start to come down fully, the convulsions, the vomiting the diarrhoea and the uncontrollable body movements that take effect, they grab hold of you and can last for days so you try and fight it the only way you truly know how in the end, by hitting up again. The never ending cycle. What happens when you try and fight it though, when you know that you are in the wrong, that every single sane pore of your being is fighting it? That’s when you either fight or flee, you either do the sensible thing and try and get help, you swallow your fucking pride and admit that you have a problem, there are substitutes that you can use that give the same effects without the negative aspects and slowly but surely you can find salvation or alternatively, you can fight against yourself a one on one battle with your own humanity in a role that almost always will end in failure. Frost had taken his own battle into his own hands, he had decided to keep his problem a secret, from his friends, family and employers. The way he saw it was that he had performed adequately at War In Washington, he could account his sloppiness to ring rust which he was still legitimately working on, it had nothing to do with the fact that part way through the tower match he had received the biggest craving for the drug he had ever experienced, nothing at all. So now he had taken a new solace a new way of dealing with the hunger and the craving, that niggling little feeling that was beginning to bite away from him. He had found pleasure in pan and not the way that rich business men pay extortionate amounts of money for. Frost had begun to self harm. He was fully aware of what he was doing though, every time the urge took him, he would just spend a small amount of time on his own, lock himself away and cut himself, not deep enough to leave any significant scarring, not that it would be noticed on his ring torn body anyway, but he was very conscious of not leaving a mark, to leave a mark leaves you open to other people, they would ask questions which would lead back to his original problem. He had taken a number of precautions so that he wouldn’t be caught for a start he made sure to only cut his legs, his thighs mostly, somewhere where no-one would see, is ring attire made sure that the company would not get to see the situation, whenever he managed to get himself motivated to train at the gym he just wore tracksuit bottoms, simple as that. However it was now beginning to take its own grip, in its own strange way the pain was addictive. The pain in itself was incredibly addictive, an amazing release of pain suffering and anguish in one fell swoop, it was something that Frost felt he had control over, he was the one dealing out the pain to himself, he felt he had control. A hell of a lot more than the drugs which were taking a strangle hold on his life. As it stood now though he was not fine, far from it. His life was spiralling out of control. Forget the crossroads he was previously at, he was now on a one way decent, down to hell.
