Back to the land of the Misfit toys

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today #Talk about me, Laugh about me Cry about me... nail me to the cross I'll be a martyr for the hated The weak, the ugly, the lost#-Resurrection (Misfits) Location: ‘Exile’ Timeline: unknown The girl was running, heart pumping, pulse racing, her life of serenity cruelly destroyed by the cold sting of betrayal that had gripped her and thrown her to the floor in an act of violence. What she had done was deemed unforgivable in the eyes of her employers at the time, stealing the information contained within three disks, valuable information that started clues to a very intricate puzzle. The puzzle in question would if it was revealed be very valuable to some people in Sin City but her thoughts weren’t on anything remotely to do with wrestling, at the time it was more focused on the life she now had to leave behind. To her it could have been worse… she hadn’t married the man she loved so there was no repercussions TOO great. After what seemed like forever she saw a building that she felt she could hide in… its name was Club of the Nephilim. She would meet the man that would change her life forever that night… but we’re not ready to reveal that part just yet. Location:Colorado Timeline: on way to Temptation We now see Legion and Luci in a car driven by Luci herself (rental car of course) on the way to the next hotspot for Temptation. Legion: So much for the ‘great trainer’ I thought he was joining us on this little ride to where I feel trapped in a bad South Park episode. But no, he had to say something about (adopts a bad impression) destiny favours the bold, hell it would have not involved but YOU… Luci: You’re still going on because I dropped you in it about Malloy, you should be happy, he doesn’t have the belt of pure Livewire anymore. Xander/Paulie Searle has it! Legion: The guy that thought he could pull an Edge? The guy who pimpslapped me and thinks he’s God of all wrestling despite being Michael Jackson more than Michael Jordan with his soft- oh wait Jordan’s softball career didn’t go well now did it? (Luci laughs at the joke… dark sense of humour always wins out) Legion; Let’s all hope he doesn’t have Edge’s curse after winning MIT- or whatever Nigel called it. Suppose I should address Misfit now… Jimmy last time anyone saw you Lance rescued you from a world of religious torment from Wyatt Connors when he was going all Waco on us but now you’re all deprogrammed and ready to unleash hell by trying to defeat me… Little warning Jimmy I’ve been out of action being helped by a man who has made me stronger on a mental and physical level and you will be the first to feel the sacrifice we both had to go through to achieve that goal. In fact Temptation will be a night where the sacrifice will all be worth it… They used to say among members of a club I went to that we all belonged in the land of the misfit toys because we were broken, Misfit you’re going to end up broken and then like us you’ll be forced to be in a ‘box’ with no one wanting you again. (We then cut back in time once again…) Location: Club Of The Nephilm Timeline: unknown She looked around, the whole landscape was new to her, the music, the darkness, the look it seemed to call to her like a siren… join us it was screaming. As is custom in these places she thought to get a drink at the bar, suddenly she heard a voice. ‘You look like you feel lost in here, running away from something you feel didn’t fit right?’ She turned around and saw a man dressed in a top hat, cloak and cane wearing a Killing joke Pandemonium shirt underneath. ‘Name’s Gabriel. You look like you need a drink’ The woman replied: ‘JD and coke’ ‘1 JD and Coke double and one Jagermeister straight’ ‘Didn’t catch your name’ The girl hesitated, she wanted a new life, a new start, not having to worry about info she knew anymore… so she thought of the first name she could. ‘It’s Laura.’ ‘As in the Nephilm track? That’s sweet.’ (And that’s how the Devil’s Rejects first met… the secrets Laura was carrying however would not come into play, at least not yet.) #She's on the line to cut it all She's on the line to drop or fall She's on the line, Line to fall# -Laura (Fields Of The Nephilm)
