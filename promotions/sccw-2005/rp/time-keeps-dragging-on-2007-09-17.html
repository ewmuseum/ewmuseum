Time keeps dragging on

17 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-17-07 'So you stole his watch?' 'It has sentimental value to him.' 'It's a cell phone without the ability to order take out food and make booty calls.' Jonathan 'Johnny' Monroe and Jason Cruise never viewed life from the same perspective. The developing argument over the fact that Jason Cruise stole El Diablo's family heirloom last week at Killzone is a perfect example. The two of them sit side by side in the living room of Jason's Santa Monica condo, staring out at the glassy ocean waves that crash down walking distance from the doorstep. 'That's not the point,' says Jason. 'It might be meaningless to you and I but to him it matters. To El Diablo, it's everything. It's his booty call.' Johnny's eyes widen. 'That's the stupidest analogy I have ever heard,' proclaims Johnny. 'But the sad thing is you know what I'm talking about.' 'Well yeah,' mutters Johnny. 'But of all the things to take... a watch? What, the title wouldn't fit in your pocket?' 'I don't need to steal it to get it.' 'I suppose this is where you make your big speech about how you beat him before and you can do it again blah, blah, blah.' 'In not so many words,' says Jason. * * * * * The still, serene, submerged features of a beautiful young woman. Dark floating hair gently frames her face. Heavy liquid eyes stare up through the water. We hear, though faintly, someone calling. 'Marie?' With a rush Marie surfaces. As she gulps air, we realize that she is in fact, in a swimming pool. We hear the calling again, this time a bit louder. 'Marie?!' She listens. For a moment she is very still, then she closes her eyes with a smile, slipping beneath the surface of the water. From inside the house comes Jason, stepping through the open French doorway and slowly approaching the edge of the swimming pool in the backyard. Through the crystal water he sees the silhouette of a barely clothed Marie. Though you can't see it, you can feel him smiling as he kneels down to the poolside. He delicately runs his fingertips across the water, hoping to gain her attention. And he does. Marie's body presses up through the water once more, this time she reaches her hands up and around Jason's neck. They lock themselves into a passionate gaze before pressing their lips together. Eventually she pulls her lips back, but not her grip, as she allows her thin frame to fall back into the pool, pulling Jason with her and bringing the fully clothed Cruise splashing into the water. * * * * * Johnny lies in the passenger seat of Jason's convertible as it rockets along the freeway. His eyes begin to wonder towards the unfamiliar sides of the road that he is unaccustom to seeing. The car sways through the traffic at the hands of Jason at the wheel as an eerie feeling causes Johnny to break the silence. 'Where are you taking me,' asks Johnny. 'We're doing research,' says Jason. 'Like to plan your bachelor party you sackless bastard?' Jason rolls his eyes. 'Funny,' he remarks. 'Seriously though, what the hell is wrong with you. You've known this chick for like a month and she's got your dick on a string. I never even see you anymore.' 'That's so homoerotic of you.' 'Well shit, someone has to make me breakfast in the morning. Cut the strings Pinocchio, come back to me.' 'You mean someone has to keep letting you freeload off them until you find a job, Johnny.' 'Ha, find a job. That's a good one,' Johnny says with a hint of laughter in his voice. 'I'm serious man, when are you going to get back in the ring?' 'I'm retired.' Staring at him, Jason questions him. 'Retired? You're twenty fucking three years old. You're just lazy.' 'Hey I made my mark on the business, won my titles, used it to lure gullible and willing women into bed and now I'm bowing out gracefully. I don't care about saving the business or all the stupid crap you buy into. I fought the rules and I won.' Jason sighs, shaking his head at his lifelong friend. Johnny stares at him blankly, 'What?' 'So much talent, so little brains. We could be unstoppable.' 'Or you could be unstoppable and I could reap the benefits.' Jason brings the car to a sudden stop and gestures towards Johnny's door. 'Get out,' he says demandingly. 'What, are you serious? I'll only reap the benefits 6 days a week, Sundays can be all you!' 'No, we're here you idiot. Get out.' 'Oh.' The two get out of the car and Johnny looks up at a haunting building, eying the large sign over the top of it. East Los Angeles Chapel of Satan. * * * * * With a gasp, Jason rises. It takes a moment but his breathing calms. Then, smoothing water into his hair, he gazes into the eyes of Marie who stands across from him, her body gently bobbing up and down to keep her afloat by the tips of her feet. Jason leans against her, his arms pulling her body into his. 'I could get used to this,' he says with a whisper. 'Oh really?' Her question comes with a smile. 'Yeah -- it's nice.' 'Nice? Nice is a day out with your sister. This better be more than nice.' Jason cracks a smile. 'Good point. This is amazing.' It brings a matching smile to the face of Marie. 'I wish we had more moments like this.' 'We will.' 'I know, I just meant -- She pauses. 'More often,' she says softly. 'You're on the road all the time, I feel like I barely see you.' Her head drops down, almost afraid to see the expression on Jason's face. 'You know you get all my free time and attention when I'm home.' 'But when you're gone?' She asks this with a tilted and confused head. 'When I'm gone, I'm, I don't know how to put this. I'm a different person. The Jason you know here and the Jason on the road are as much the same as I'm allowed but..' Cutting in, 'But what? What's so different?' 'I can't explain it,' he says. 'You wouldn't understand.' 'Then show me,' she says, squeezing his body even tighter. 'What do you mean show you?' 'Take me with you.' There's an awkward silence, Jason isn't sure how to react. 'I could get some time off work, the girls will cover me, come on, it'd be fun.' Jason looks deep into her eyes, passionately, convincingly, but something isn't there. Something is missing. 'Honey,' he says. 'Yes?' 'I can't.' And her embrace weakens. He pulls her back in but she gently fights it, keeping some space between them. 'It's just -- not a good time.' 'Not a good time,' her voice rises. 'I see.' 'No, baby, it's not that I don't want you to come see me. It's...' 'What?' She's baiting a reason, an answer. Jason's eyes shut, his head drops. 'It's not safe.' She huffs. 'Not safe?! I know it's a dangerous sport, are you forgetting how we met?' Jason takes a deep breath. 'No, you don't get it. I know IT'S not safe but that's not what I'm worried about.' 'Then what is it?' Softly, 'It's him.' * * * * * A gothic, unfriendly environment heavy with religious iconography. The entrance hall is crowded with bodies hard at work fixing up the broken down building that is suffering from a recent civilian riot. The East Los Angeles Chapel of Satan is publicly criticized and hated in the community and often on the wrong end of attacks and rioting. Still, the building remains intact, embracing the warfare that litters it on almost a weekly basis. Jason stands in front of the split rows of pews at the dead center of the chapel, his eyes frozen on the diabolical artwork over his head. Surrounding him are unlit candles and an empty tub of water, often used for baptizings and offerings. 'Is this your idea of a good time?' Johnny questions him. 'Not mine -- someone else.' 'Whatever, I think one of those workers just gave me a fuck me smile. I'm gonna go check it out.' Johnny walks off into the distance, approaching an attractive, pale skinned young woman. Jason ignores him, staring towards the stage and the shrine to Satan. 'It fits you, Raul.' Jason begins to pace, it's a habit of his not out of nerves but rather when he puts himself into a deep thinking state of mind. 'I imagine at some point in your life you stood in a place much like this, offering your body and your blood, your soul and your very existence. Maybe you came here once out of curiosity, a young boy lost in the world looking for an answer and someone here lent a misleading hand and you took it. Or maybe you're just a sick fuck, I don't really know to tell you the truth.' His eyes fixate on a picture. It's an odd sight, an oddity of a figure, half man and half serpent. Over the head is a crown of thorns piercing the forehead of the man leaving a trail of blood down his cheek. The serpent is dry, void of blood but lit with a half grin. 'But this... this is just kinda sick. Not 'hey look at me I'm making myself bleed to prove a point kind of sick,' that's just retarded, this is more...' His voice hesitates. 'Surreal. This is a way of life, your life. You've told me you would go to any lengths to stop me, to avenge your loss. I don't doubt it, Raul. Any man that would carve himself up just to get into my head is someone I have to take as a serious threat... well, a threat no less. You have made it perfectly clear that nothing will prevent you from ending me and I welcome it, Raul.' 'Beat me. Beat me bad. Break every bone in my body. Make me bleed until I can't walk, stand, or breathe anymore. Do that. Do that if you want your revenge because if you don't...' He grins. 'I fucking will.' 'I swear it on every single part of my body and my life. I swear that I will make an example out of you and beat you down for the second time.' 'You seek redemption, Raul. But what you've found is that for once, time is not on your side.' 'For every droplet of blood I lose, you must lose two. For each wail of pain I release, yours must be twice as loud, twice as chilling. For each staple you put into my back, my chest, my body... whatever it may be... I must simply fall back upon the hand of my beliefs, my existence. For I realize the reality of this match, of this blood war, but do you? I'm not a big name in FUSE, I'm from a much different cloth, one that ultimately will lead to your demise. The revenge I seek isn't out of envy, greed, or jealousy. Not for professional rank or titles. No, I seek it because of you, Raul. You brought it out of me. You created a monster within me and begged me to taste your wrath, your fury.' 'But you're forgetting one thing, Raul.' 'There's one thing I have that you don't.' And Jason does it, he reaches into his pocket and removes El Diablo's family heirloom... the watch. 'This is you, Raul. This is the devil inside of you, it's what makes your heart tick.' Jason drops it to the cold, concrete floor. 'So I guess that leaves me no choice. I'll just have to stomp a hole in the devil's fucking heart.' The weight of his foot comes crashing down on top of the watch, breaking it into several large pieces. 'Time's up, Raul.' Jason bends down, gathering the broken pieces in his hand. 'Time for the devil to get his due.' Black.
