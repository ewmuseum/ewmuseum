Blackbird

17 April 2008

Date: 4/17 Today’s a little bit different, kids. Today is in need of something special, something different. Today we need Alterbridge. "Blackbird, to be exact." ********************** THE WILLOW IT WEEPS TODAY A BREEZE FROM A DISTANCE IS CALLING YOUR NAME UNFURL YOUR BLACK WINGS AND WAIT ACROSS THE HORIZONS COMING TO SWEEP YOU AWAY ITS COMING TO SWEEP YOU AWAY Bishop sighs heavily as he watches Sarah Riley sit across from him, her right leg shaking idly over her left as she nods intermittently. She is on the phone, and her fellow conversationalist? "Yes, Ms. Pierce. That sounds perfect. Hm? Oh, I’m sure he’ll be fine. All right. I’ll let him know." Sarah does not make eye contact with her client/boyfriend/whatever as she talks with Desade. If Bishop were able to see her eyes, shielded by their downward direction, he wouldn’t be completely thrilled with what he sees. There are two types of people who work for Alexandra Pierce. The first is the type who, by situations unfavorable to them, find themselves under the employ of the Spider in the Web. They are usually reluctant in their decisions and appear remorseful in their actions. On good days, MacKenzie Malone fits comfortably in this category. Then there are the other type, the ones that enjoy working for a woman defined as evil and, in fact, almost get off on the idea. Ones who would not have been extraordinarily bad people by themselves but, with the aid of Alex Pierce’s connections and ruthlessness, go off the deep end in an amalgam of passion and ecstatic pleasure. If Bishop would have seen Sarah’s eyes as she sycophantically nodded to a woman who couldn’t (hopefully) see her do it, he would have seen that, without a doubt, Sarah Riley is the latter. But he doesn’t see that, as when she finishes the conversation and looks up, her eyes are merely wide and bright in excitement. "That was Ms. Pierce. She wants me doing some case work for her during the show, so it’ll be just you and Bridden in the ring by yourselves. You can handle him, right?" Bishop scowls at Sarah’s biting tone. "Don’t act like I’m some no-talent hack because I lost to Lance Marshall. I can get my work done. ‘Sides, this is Jay Bridden we’re talking about. The kid got a serious allergic reaction to guys over 250 pounds. He sees em, and he can’t help but be on his back for three seconds." Sarah shrugs, unimpressed. "You better hope so. I’m doing everything I can to get you up in this company, and I’d be interested in seeing that the work is paying off. For all the shit you talk about earning your keep, I’d like to see you show some motivation and do the same." Grumbling, Bishop shakes his head. "Whatever, woman. I know what I can do, and I’ma make sure that little punk knows what I can do too. Bridden Buster my ass, why don’t you…" his rant is interrupted by his phone ringing. Grunting, he waves Sarah off and answers it. "Yeah." "H…hey, son." Bishop’s eyes widen slightly, and he stands up. "Pops. What’s wrong?" There’s a long pause on the line. When he finally does answer, James Bishop’s voice is thin and wheezing. "Remember how I was talking about some trouble I got into?" "Yeah, you said it was just a little bit and you could handle it yourself." Again there is a long pause before he answers. "I was…I was wrong. Can you…come to the park? I need…" Bishop doesn’t answer; he merely hangs up and runs out of the room, giving no farewell to Sarah Riley on his way out. ********************** LET THE WIND CARRY YOU HOME BLACKBIRD FLY AWAY MAY YOU NEVER BE BROKEN AGAIN Bishop looks for nearly twenty minutes in the park before he sees him, a huddled mound of clothes and dirt leaning against the foot of a park bench. "Dad!" he says urgently, wrapping his father up in his arms and lifting him up onto the bench. He leans him down and pulls back the hood of his sweatshirt, then involuntarily gasps. James Bishop gurgles from the influx of light, and blood spills from his lips and gushes out onto the grass, except for a lone glob that sticks on his bottom lip. Bishop sighs and rubs the blood off with his finger. "What happened?" "Nobody pays anymore…" is all his father says, and Bishop notices that his father’s pants have several tears in them, especially near the zipper area. There is a red splotch near the back of his jeans as well. Upon seeing this, Bishop does something he hasn’t done in several years. He begins to cry. Pulling his father to his chest, he bawls, punctuating his gasps by saying, "You dumb motherfucker, dad. What did you do?" He shakes his head. "What the fuck did you do?" ********************** THE FRAGILE CAN NOT ENDURE THE WRECKED AND THE AND JADED A PLACE SO IMPURE THE STATIC OF THIS CRUEL WORLD CAUSE SOME BIRDS TO FLY LONG BEFORE THEY'VE SEEN THEIR DAY Three hours later, Bishop enters Sarah Riley’s home, his shirt covered in blood. She looks up at him, her eyebrow perfectly arched. It’s like she’s been taking classes in that sort of thing. "What did you do? Another bar fight?" Bishop grunts in answer and takes off his shirt, throwing it in the trash can. He grabs a hand towel and begins wiping off the blood – an act that makes Sarah nearly squeal. "Those are fifty dollar towels!" Bishop scowls, but doesn’t answer. He throws the bloody towel on the ground, then walks towards the bathroom. Sarah, not sure of how to react to the silent treatment, follows him. "What’s wrong with you? You come into my house after leaving without saying goodbye, and then you come in here and don’t tell me anything…listen, Tavon. I know we have a business relationship, but we also have a personal relationship, and I’ve got a lot invested in you, both professionally and emotionally. So it hurts when you abuse my hospitality and don’t tell me what’s going on." In their short time together, these sort of logical pleas have always had a calming effect on Bishop’s temper. He’d usually sigh, nod, and listen to her. Today, though, he shakes his head. "Yeah, you right. I’m abusing your hospitality. I’ll just be on my way then." Brushing past her and getting blood on her blouse, Bishop grabs his bag and heads out the door. Sarah watches him go, affecting a saddened countenance until he leaves. She then shrugs her shoulders and returns to her notes. ********************** BEYOND THE SUFFERING YOU’VE KNOWN I HOPE YOU FIND YOUR WAY MAY YOU NEVER BE BROKEN AGAIN James Bishop is grabbed by the collar of his shirt and dragged down the sidewalk. "Where we goin, son?" Bishop doesn’t look down as he continues walking. "We gonna find those dudes who made you a bitch, and we gonna make them bitches. That shrink tells me I need some motivation? Well, I got it now. Now show me where I can find ‘em." James Bishop meekly gives an address, and the Biloxi Beast marches, his eyes straight ahead, his appearance calm. But inside he feels a fire, one that he hasn’t felt since tearing apart Itachi or Matthew Randall. This is what Earning Your Keep is all about.
