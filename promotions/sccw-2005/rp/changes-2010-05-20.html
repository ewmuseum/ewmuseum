Date: 05/20/2010 05/18/2010 Pain is something that should be cultivated, not avoided by using over the counter pain medication. In order to survive in the wrestling business you have to ignore pain in order to make an impact. Lately, though, . However, here recently, I have come to a conclusion and that conclusion is that it’s not about making an impact, getting the most titles and wins, or about the money… It’s about sending a message. I think that everyone in the sports entertainment…no, the wrestling business, has forgotten what it means to send a message. Now days most feuds are started by something petty such as accidentally bumping into another wrestler backstage. Well that’s not where my story begins. My story is a little more complex than a simple bump backstage at a wrestling show. What happened at the beginning now seems more like a dream now than something that happened in actual reality. It started back in Vegas when a man came to me when I first graduated high school. I had won the senior wrestling title in the middleweight wrestling division. The man said he would pay for my ride through pro wrestling school and that I could make a lot of money but only if I helped him out. That’s when things turned to the dark side. He had my friend "Army Ranger" Jon Collingsworth, who had also been hired by the man, teach me how to shoot. That’s how I got into the murder for hire business. That was were the real money was. It was a brilliant plan from the start. Who would suspect me, the most eccentric wrestler of the modern era, to be a hitman on the side? At each show I’d get booked at, I had an ulterior motive. All of the time that motive had to do with a kill I’d gotten hired to do. This went on for years and years. The more I killed, the more crazy my mind went. For those who knew the wrestling business, especially in the PTC universe, knew that I was slightly insane. If they knew what was really going on for years, it would all made sense. However they decided to ridicule me and basically turn me into an pariah which only made things worse. The more guilt I had, the more crazy I got on the shows. That probably explains why I went off the deep end at times. In SCCW, things have changed… I’ve changed… I’m different. For the first time in my career, I’m on my own. No goons, no minions, just me. It’s a strange feeling. It’s a reverse Lord of the Flies so to speak. I’m in the wilds of the wrestling world, but with no help. That’s the only difference…well that and no cannibalism. It’s going to be a whole new ball game from now on as far as I’m concerned. Just because I’m an honest man but that doesn’t mean I have to follow the rules when I’m in the ring. That would just make me a sell out like every other anti-hero wrestler out there. Hypocritical is much more like it. Why change from being bad when I’m so good at being bad? I’m not going to be a sell out because it honestly wouldn’t be fair to my fans. Also it would give my many critics even more hate to spew out about me. Everything changes, just like the seasons. This is just another change but the fact I didn’t see it coming made it more shocking. Maybe I’ve lost a bit of my edge with my career winding down to a close. Been a crazy few weeks. Wrestled in two battle royals, won them both. Got beat down in my last match and by my own friends. Now I am no one and have nothing. Don’t even have a game plan for my match this week which is rare. Can’t remember another time with that happening. Maybe I should do like I’ve done in the past and come on SCCW television to tell everyone that I hate them all and create a huge target on my back in the process. However this may not be the best option given the circumstances, Got to clear my head and think. Decide that the best option is to go on a walkabout like Crocodile Dundee. Maybe that will help me get on track. Spending two or three months in the wilderness doesn’t sound like a bad idea. I hate not having a clear life goal. Now is a time of great uncertainty on my end. Hopefully no one uses it to their advantage against me. * * * 05/19/2010 She gazes at me from across the room. Her eyes are like two blue orbs circling a distant star somewhere in the universe. Her lips beckon me with a silent call that only I can here. The party continues all around us as I walk over to her. Feels as if I’m being pulled in by a tractor beam. And this whole time Tears for Fears has been playing in the background. Boy how times have changed… * * * 05/20/2010 These are strange days. I don’t even feel like I’m the same person I was a week ago. Instead of worrying about wrestling, I’m more worried about what will happen in Australia’s National Rugby League when the South Sydney Rabbitohs play the New Zealand Warriors on Sunday. I think the time for my end is near. Maybe these are the last days for me. All of my friends want me dead, my career is going down the toilet after a brief revival, and I have no desire to be in the business that I loved months ago. These are not good times for me. Things are not looking good for me. Atlas Adams is one lucky bastard this week. However, he has to remember. When cornered, an animal is at its most dangerous. Maybe the Evil Dr. James will return sometime soon.
