In and Out of Time

2 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/02/08 'That man can destroy life is just as miraculous a feat as that he can create it, for life is the miracle, the inexplicable. In the act of destruction, man sets himself above life; he transcends himself as a creature. Thus, the ultimate choice for a man, inasmuch as he is driven to transcend himself, is to create or to destroy, to love or to hate.'- Erich Fromm Prince Pride stands in the doorway of his seedy apartment building, but not in his apartment. He stands at the door of apartment 6H, the H is crooked and the 6 dangles ever so slightly. Pride knocks on the door loudly and obnoxiously. "Hey, open up, I’m not a Jehovah’s Witness, it’s safe!" The door slowly creeks open, but no light, no shadow or no face or figure can be seen. Just the slightest bit of darkness, and so it begins. "So, I was sent here because I need your services and protection, and I was told you could help me." That’s the voice of Prince Pride. "From?" That’s the voice of the person he’s talking too, it’s loud and booming, but he can’t be seen. "My father, my sister and her gay boyfriend." "Why?" "Well, it’s kind of hard to explain, but I’ll give it a try…" CUE FLASHBACK SEQUENCE W/ NARRATIVE _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ DEFIANCE Is my heart supposed to be pounding like this? I’m Prince Pride, I’m the person here to save and yet destroy this business, why am I so nervous? So what I just lost a match to some kid named Matthew on my pay per view debut! So what that I ran from an announcer so I didn’t get a spanking on national television, so what! Now is my time, and damnit, nobody can slow me down now, hit my music monkey! MitBH: I expected more out of you! One of my own. Gibson: One of his own, what does that mean? Ware: Jason is his son! Jason is fucking his sister! Gibson: Shut up. Ware: Do you have any other explanation? Gibson: Just listen dammit. MitBH: You look -- pale. How easily you forget where you came from. How quick you are to forget about those that stood by you along the way. Jason snatches the mic away from him. Cruise: Listen here you -- _ _ _ _ _ _ _ "Just so you know, my entrance is the coolest, when that Bonnie Tyler mid-80’s hair rock pops on, people just lose their minds! It’s like a Karaoke party, but better!" "Continue" "Will do!" _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ # Doo doo doo doooo # # Doo doo doo doooo # # Doo doo doo doooo # # Aaahhh # # Aaahhh # Gibson: It better not be who I think it is. Ware: Your best friend... in the house! The spotlights begin to beam down the aisle, as a fast rising smoke fills the arena as Bonnie Tyler’ "Holding out for a Hero" begins to blare. # Where have all the good men gone # # And where are all the gods? # # Where’s the street-wise Hercules # # To fight the rising odds? # And emerging through the smoke, is the savior, the second generation phenom, the prodigal son, the pharaoh of paranoia, Prince Pride. Wearing a tank top that has "WRESTLING" written on it inside of a circle, and crossed out, basketball shorts, basketball shoes, and of course his trademark lion mane and wild hair, his caramel complexion glistens in the light. # Isn’t there a white knight upon a fiery steed? # # Late at night I toss and I turn and I dream of what I need # That music fades as Prince Pride waltzes towards the ring, mic in hand, and a bundle of something or rather slung over his shoulder. Gibson: I swear to God, if that little horse shit so much as looks at me cross eyed I'm going to knock his head off. Ware: Where is your FUSE spirit?! Things just got interesting! My entrance music is quite simply the best, I wonder what ever happened to that lady, maybe she’ll sing it for me live when I win the Universal Title and destroy wrestling, all at the same time. Look at Dave Gibson sitting there all smug, I’d spit in your face if I could old man. Look at Eugene Ware, looking all fat and cuddly, if I was gay I’d hump you Eugene, but I’m not, so I won’t. Oh, I should say something witty out loud. Pride: Hey, guys, yeah, all this fighting over me, this isn't the place. Prince Pride maneuvers his way towards the ring, jumping up to the ring apron, and climbing in the ropes. Pride: You know what I think the problem is?! All of them; Jason Cruise, Kansas Marie, and The Man in the Black Hat all stare at Prince who unzips the bag on his shoulder and pulls out... Pride: T-shirts! Yes, that's right, your very own, one of a kind, 'PRINCE PRIDE DRINKS HIS OVALTINE' T-shirt! Now, I know what all three of you are thinking... how do I get my hands on one of those sweet shirts, right?! Jason holds his hands out, like, 'What?' Pride: But unfortunately for some people here... He motions his head towards Jason and Kansas. Pride: Not just anyone can sport one of these hip happenin' shirts. See, these aren't for the cookie cutter All-American, Zach Morris with dark hair wannabe types Oh no people, only the very best, the coolest, guys with funny black hats can wear these one of a kind shirts! Naturally he is holding out two shirts for The Man in the Black Hat who just stares down at him. God, I’m so cool. I wake up some mornings wondering what it’s like not to be me, no wonder why the suicide rate is so high in this country, if only I could bottle me and sell me, maybe, just maybe I could, I’ll call STD about that. Pride (whispering): Don't worry, I can get you more. MitBH: Look [pause] Prince. This is not the place, I am here -- Cruise: And THAT is the real question, isn't it? Why you're here? Why you have the audacity, EITHER OF YOU, to come out here after I killed myself in this ring tonight for THESE PEOPLE... 'RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!' Cruise: And try to steal OUR moment. This win was for them, not for you and your stupid T-shirts, Prince. Not for you stupid Abraham Lincoln wannabe. Go reenact the Civil War up the street, I heard Gettysburg was hiring special needs help. Now if you'll excuse us, Kansas-Marie and I have some celebrating to do... Jason starts to walk off, but the black gloved hand of The Man in the Black Hat grabs his shoulder, freezing him. _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ "So, at this point, nothing was too weird. Everything was going to plan, except for Jason interrupting me, but he has no personaltity, cause he’s a good guy, they’re like vanilla cookies without the vanilla. I’m on cloud 9, and then…Well, here’s the rest" _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ MitBH: Wait. Jason turns his attention back to him. Cruise: Make it quick. Uh, hello, this is my moment to steal your moment, not your moment to steal my moment in which I’ve stolen from you, selfish ass! And since when did this guy in the Black Hat start giving lectures, and why is his voice so familiar, and why am I having so much internal dialogue, are they done talking yet? MitBH: Very well, I came out here to get something that belongs to me. No! He turns towards Kansas-Marie, and Jason jumps in front of her. MitBH: My shirt, please. Finally, I can get this guy his shirt, you know, in a weird, I "blank kids" type of way, I’m starting to like this guy, maybe I can get a matching black hat and we can form a force so strong that no one can stop us. He turns towards Prince who eyes beam with delight, handing him the wad of shirts. MitBH: Real nice, son. Jason, here... And he flings a shirt to Jason who looks up at the shirt being thrown to him, but before he can catch it... Or maybe not, he just gave Jason my shirt, Indian giver, so much for… ** THWAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!! ** HEEEEEY! Maybe this guy isn’t half bad, and that was a pretty sweet superkick! Cruise is absolutely drilled with a stiff kick to the face, one that knocks the mask clean off of his face and sends him crashing down to the mat. Ware: HOLY SUPERKICK BATMAN! Gibson: MY GOD WHAT A KICK! HE JUST KNOCKED THE MASK STRAIGHT OFF OF JASON'S HEAD! Kansas-Marie screams, yelling at the Man in the Black Hat who stands over Jason Cruise, his title, and his mask... and reaches down. _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ "BAM! The guy gets nailed with a superkick, and he deserved it, for being so not straight." "Solved!" "No! Not solved! The problem got worse!" _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ Hahahaha! He just got super kicked, did El Diablo just fall off the face of the earth, did he die? Oh well, anyway, I like this guy, and I like that ass on Kansas, she can come home and save me anyday… Peeling the bronze face plate off of the canvas. MitBH: I think this belongs to me. Who the hell wears a bronze mask? Where does one even purchase enough bronze to make a mask? I’ve seen that somewhere before, I just can’t put my finger on it. Oh well, this would be a great time for me to say something witty, but… And removes his hat, and brushes the long strands of salt and pepper hair out from in front of his face. Ware: NO! Gibson: Is that? Ware: NO! Gibson: I know that face! Ware: GOD NO! I TAKE BACK WHAT I SAID EARLIER, ANYONE BUT HIM!!! Gibson: THAT'S -- Ware: TONY FUCKING PRIDE! NOOOOOOOOO! DAD!?!!?!?! _ _ _ _ _ _ _ "Father?!" "Yes, my dad, right there in the flesh, I couldn’t believe it, I hadn’t seen him since after Mom’s…" Prince Pride’s voice trails off softly as his eyes just gleam, reliving Defiance in his head. _ _ _ _ _ _ _ And even more horrified then Eugene, and for good reason, is the young man standing on the far side of the ring... a wad of shirts hanging from his fingertips... his jaw nearly touching the floor. What’s he doing here? Am I talking out loud? Am I moving? Am I breathing, what in the world is happening to me?! Gibson: PRINCE PRIDE HAS SEEN A GHOST! HIS *FATHER* IS STANDING IN THE RING WITH HIM! Ware: Which means... Gibson: HIS SISTER IS STANDING ON THE OTHER SIDE! Marie is my sister? Oh my god, hold yourself together Prince, don’t let them see you sweat, you’re the savior, you’re the prodigal son, you’re…Speechless! Ware: I want my old job back. Gibson: Tony Pride just -- he just leveled Jason Cruise with a superkick to the face and the arena is dead silent. I for one am -- Ware: I'm stunned. I need a drink. Cocktail! Gibson: I don't know what to make of all of this but the show must go on folks. We still have another Main Event to air. The camera cuts away, but not before leaving us with one final image of Tony Pride walking back down the aisle in a black coat, Jason's bronze mask in his hand. And of course, a barely conscious Cruise... his head being cradled by the hands of Kansas-Marie...Pride. _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ "So you see, I need you, I’ve sold all my worldly belongings, to pay the price I’m told you charge, but I won’t pay until we’re done." "Done?" "Yes sir, I’ll take care of Jason and Marie, I can handle them, but my father…" Prince pauses "…Tony, I won’t deal with him, I can’t deal with him, and so I want you to deal with him." "How?" "I want you to do what I’m told you do best…" There’s an awkward silence as Pride’s eyes fill with anger and remorse, and then a surreal and sick smile takes over his face. "…I want you to kill him." Prince smiles. "I want you to kill my father…I want you to kill Tony Pride!" A lone tear rolls into Prince’s open mouth, as he smiles manically, and the door slowly opens and Pride walks into the darkness, the door slowly closes and we fade.
