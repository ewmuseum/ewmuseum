Looking the Part

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/07 Synopsis - Looking the Part As he seems to come closer and closer to a trademark victory with each passing week, “One Take” Carson Riley has decided to take a different approach. After losing an uber-close contest to FUSE veteran El Diablo, Carson once again looks for an edge to help himself rise above the competition. Although Carson is usually very laid back and chill, the roller coaster ride he has been on as of late seems to finally be taking its toll. As he prepares to take on yet another tough competitor in Mayson Colby, Carson has decided that if he can’t live out his aspiration of becoming a FUSE superstar, he might as well at least look the part. Chapter 1 - Not Your Average Window Shopper: Time: 1:32 p.m. Carson is walking alone down an upscale Los Angeles street. He is dressed casually in a red polo shirt, jeans, and a red Los Angeles Angels cap. As Carson passes by an Armani clothing store, he pauses long enough to study the mannequin in the window. He checks the watch on his left wrist and confirms that he has time to stop in for a quick shopping trip. As he enters, a grey haired, male associate in glasses approaches him immediately. “Good afternoon, Mr. Riley,” says the associate, greeting Carson appropriately. “Good afternoon to you too. Nice day out isn’t it?” “Beautiful. How about those Angels? Can they take down the Sox?” “You better believe it. Vlad will impale the Red Sox pitching. Piece of cake,” says Carson as he adjusts his Angels cap. “Well, how can I assist you today,” asks the associate politely. “I was just walking by and that suit caught my eye,” says Carson with admiration for the article of clothing on the mannequin in the window. “Yeah. The Collezioni is one of our finest pieces. It’s hand made. It comes in black or blue.” “Nice. I like it. I like it a lot. I could really use something like that in my closet.” “Who couldn’t,” asks the associate with a slight laugh under his breath. “Absolutely. I think I like the blue. The black is slick but the blue is more my speed.” “Well, that piece is brand new so there are no markdowns on it yet. We do have some older pieces over here that may be more in your price range.” “What do you mean more in my price range,” asks Carson with disdain for the comments of the associate. “Well, I just assumed...” “You assumed that because I’m a professional wrestler I don’t make the kind of money it costs to buy something from your store?” “I’m sorry, I was just trying to give you some options. The Collezioni is a $3,400 suit. Most people don’t window shop for $3,400 suits.” “Well, luckily this isn’t my first job and I have plenty of money in the bank. I’ll try on the blue Collezioni.” “Very well.” The associate proceeds to measure Carson. After doing so, he pulls out Carson’s size from the back room. Carson takes the suit into the fitting room and emerges looking like a million bucks. “That looks incredible. Great fit,” comments the associate with sincerity. “Yeah, this thing fits like a glove. I feel like I could walk on water in this thing.” Carson stands in front of the mirror and checks out his new duds. His face lights up as he spins around to check out the fitting of the fancy, designer outfit. The associate hands him two neck ties. Carson holds both of them up against the suit and hands one back to the associate. “On second though, I don’t know about my decision,” says Carson while unbuttoning the jacket. “Would you like me to show you those other suits I mentioned?” “No this one is perfect.” “Yeah,” asks the puzzled associate. “Indeed it is. I’ll take the black and the blue.” Carson walks into the dressing room and quickly exits holding an American Express card. “Charge it to that,” says Carson without hesitation. “I’ll gather the black one while you get changed.” “Actually, I think I’ll wear this out of here. I like the way it feels.” “Very well.” As Carson grabs his clothes from the dressing room, the associate gathers the other suit. Carson pays for his new gear and exits wearing the blue Armani Collezioni suit. Chapter 2 - Phantom Money: Time: 2:47 p.m. After picking up a few new suits, Carson decided to shop for a new car. Modesty aside, Carson walks into a Rolls-Royce showroom and quickly approaches the 2007 Phantom. Still decked out in his new Armani suit, Carson quickly gathers the attention of a nearby salesman. “Excuse me, sir. I wanted some information about this vehicle,” says Carson with eagerness. “Sure. This is the 07' Phantom. Very few of these were manufactured but it’s a great car.” “I don’t see a price tag, what do these start at?” “This one is $333,000. I have a black one around the back that is in the 310 range.” “How much!?” “310.” “Wow. These doors better grow wings and fly for that kind of cash.” “Everything on this car is top of the line. This is the definition of luxury. Nothing can compare to the feeling of security this automobile provides,” says the salesman with conviction. “No seriously, where are the wings on this thing? It’s gotta fly,” Says Carson, still trying to understand the absurd price tag. “No wings, sir.” “Okay, okay. I see what’s going on here.” “Is this something you could afford?” “Yeah, if I sold my apartment and rented my girlfriend out for cash. Maybe, just maybe, I could then make a down payment.” “Okay. You have a good day,” says the salesman as he walks away from Carson. Carson shakes his head and exits the dealership in disbelief. Chapter 3 - Driving Success: Time: 5:11 p.m. Carson is driving home from his lavish shopping trip. He picks up his phone and calls Kate, his girlfriend, as he continues to drive through a rural neighborhood in Hollywood. “Hello,” says Kate as she answers Carson’s call. “Hey, baby. I’ll be home in a second. I have a surprise for you.” “What kind of surprise, Carson?” “Just meet me outside. I’ll show you.” “I’m cooking dinner and I don’t have my shoes on. Just bring it inside and show me.” “I can’t. It’s too big.” “Too big? You didn’t get a new car did you?” “Maaaaybe.” answers Carson playfully. “Carson! If you spent too much money I’m going to kick your ass.” They both hang up the phone. Carson pulls up in front of his residence as Kate waits barefoot on the curb. He crosses her arms and shakes her head as he rolls up, parks, and exits the ride. “A Chrysler 300,” questions Kate with obvious disapproval. “Yeah. Do you like it?” “It’s bulky. I thought you didn’t like bulky cars?” “I like this one,” says Carson as he puts his arm around Kate. “Then why did you get white? It shows dirt too well. Look, it’s already in need of a good washing.” “I looked at the Phantom, but I like this better. It looks just like the Phantom for a fraction of the cost,” rationalizes Carson. “It looks like a Chrysler 300.” “You got that line from Katt Williams. It looks like the Phantom to me.” “Well, this isn’t the damn pimp chronicles. And another thing, what’s with this fucking suit? It’s hot out here.” “I like the way it feels. I need to get my swagger back if I plan on defeating Mayson Colby this week.” “To hell with you and your swagger. You need to get a real job,” says Kate as she storms back into the apartment. Carson reaches into the pocket of his suit and retrieves his aviator sunglasses. He put them on, enters his new car, and drives off into the sunset. Will Carson’s new, carefree persona help ease his mind and regain a portion of the pride he feels he has lost? After suffering back to back losses for the first time in his young career one thing seems certain...it can’t hurt.
