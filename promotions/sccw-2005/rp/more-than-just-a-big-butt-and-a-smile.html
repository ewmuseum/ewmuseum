Date: 09-08-09 ...That Girl Is Poison! "You don't really think I enjoyed that, do you?" Julissa Minorez stands there in front of Jadian Bridden with nothing more than a long white cotton towel wrapped around her torso. Her hair is still damp, water dripping from the loose strands that hang from her head as she stares at the young man with a new opponent for Equilibrium. "To be honest with you," Jadian begins, his hands resting on his thighs as he looks up at the vixen. "I'm not even sure anymore. You're getting better and better at this every week." "It's what I do, Jay," her hands find their way to her hips, where they remain as her eyes narrow and her full lips lose the curl of a smile they had. "The guys out there want tits and ass, and if that is what I have to use to get their attention; you better believe I'm going to use it." "I know all that," he says with a sigh, turning away from her condescending glare. "I'm just saying it's hard to tell when you're doing it because you have to and when you actually like it." He stands up, moving away just as she reaches out to him. He straightens out the jet black tee shirt that had bunched up around his waist, then sticks the hand she was closest to into the pocket of his black denim jeans. "Look, just forget I said anything." "Don't be like that. You know I have a job to do now that Kat is gone." He turns to face her, finding that same look she always gives him when she wants him to believe her...that same beautiful face she uses to convince others to do what she wants. "Whatever," he dismisses the thoughts floating around in his mind – the ones that whisper in his ear that he cannot trust her. "Look, you gonna need me to come with you to Kansas after all?" "No," she shakes her head, wet strands of hair clinging to her cheek and neck as she does. "I forgot to tell you earlier, but I have a plane waiting to fly me down there tonight. Alex says we need to make sure this guy doesn't forget about our little business arrangement." "So he isn't expecting you?" "It was the same way with Kat," she tells him, grabbing another towel out of the locker she's standing next to and puts it on top of her head. "There's never anything set up ahead of time. Makes it easier to deny if something goes wrong." "What do you mean if something goes wrong?" the look of concern that washes over his face when he asks her is not the reaction of someone who doesn't care. "I thought you were just supposed to give him some cash and that's it." "That's the plan, Alex found out he has reservations at McCormick & Schmick's Grille, so I'm just going to show up once he's comfortable." She wraps a few strands of her hair inside a folded piece of towel and pats it dry. "Look, I know you want to go with me, but I have this under control. Besides, you have your parents thing to go to." "It's their twenty fifth anniversary," he responds as she pulls the towel off of her head and tosses it into the bottom of the locker. She pulls a lacy white thong out of a bag and slips her left leg through. "I thought you were going with me?" "I am," she replies as she slips her other leg through and pulls them up and under her towel. "I'm just going to be a little late." "Right," Jay watches the towel that was wrapped around her torso fall to the ground. "Late to the renewal or late to the reception?" "Oh you wanted to go to the renewal together?" Julissa slips a skimpy piece of clothing she calls a dress over head,letting it slide down the curves of her body. Slowly. The slight wiggle of her hips as it slides along her flesh would go unnoticed on someone with thinner hips, but Juli has an onion booty and is not afraid to flaunt it. "That's what I thought, but apparently you have different plans." "No," she replies, slipping a bare foot into a blood red stiletto heel that matches the color of her dress perfectly. "I just wasn't sure you'd want me there for that." "Why wouldn't I?" "I'm not exactly the type of girl you want to take home to meet the parents, especially not when they are renewing their vows after twenty five years of marriage." "You're not that bad," he answers her, that smirk on his face as he tries his best to convince himself there is nothing wrong with their relationship, no matter how awkward it is. "I mean, compared to Kat, at least." "Jerk." she says as she pulls her hair back to tie it into a ponytail with a rubber band she has around her wrist. "I guess that's why I love you, though." "You mean it's not because of the sex?" "Well, you're not all that," it is her turn to smirk, though hers is not as memorable as his. "But it is the best you can do, so I guess I will just have to settle with it for now." "You're such a bitch." "And that's why you love me." She walks over, standing a few inches taller than him with her shoes on, and wraps her arms around his neck. Holding him tight against her chest as his hands find their way to her hips, the two exchange a passionate kiss that includes a bit of fondling. It is when Jadian takes a hold of her dress, gently tugging on the red material to lift it up over her waist before his thumbs tuck themselves between her skin and her thong that the two are left alone to discuss the evening's itinerary. * * * * * * She stood there in the bathroom of the McCormick & Schmick's Grille, her straight blonde hair framing her face as she stared into the mirror at her own reflection. She had been doing this for the last few minutes, getting her head where it needed to be to portray this character. She had yet to come up with a name to use, but for the most part she planned to be gone before she had to tell him one. There was only one reason for being here, give the man his envelope of cash and be gone before anyone realizes what is happening. The last thing she needed was for anyone to recognize her, especially sitting down with him of all people. If there was one thing the man from PRIME had, it was a face that everyone would recognize and no one would soon forget. She had seen him walk in through the front door of the steak and seafood restaurant from her seat at the bar. The curly blonde wig of the bubbly girl from Wichita was now stashed away in the bag she stuffed into the air duct - along with the red and white polka dot dress she was wearing when she walked into the women's bathroom – LuAnn McDonald would return to her virgin Shirley Temple in a few minutes. Well, that was what she told the cute accountant that offered to buy her the drink, when she abruptly stood up and excused herself in order to come 'powder her nose'. "How're you..." She practiced the accent she had worked on during the flight over. "How youse..." She shook her head, it wasn't coming together the way she had planned. "How ya doin', Tony?" Perfect. She thought to herself as a smile creased the features of her face. There was enough make-up on it to finance the Mary Kay corporation for a year, but apart from having a full Hollywood make-up department to change her features she had to use what she had available. Certain that her appearance was altered enough that no one would recognize her as the Mexican Minx or LuAnn McDonald, she made her way to the door. She walked through the restaurant with her head held high and a confident smile that drew most of the attention in the dining area toward her. Women slapped their husbands in the shoulder as their mouths practically dropped to the floor with a thud, and the cute accountant with the cocky smile must have forgotten all about LuAnn when he followed her slow gait toward the patio seating. There was a waiter standing in front of him, blocking his view of her as she approached. The chair opposite him was empty, just as they had figured. She slithered onto the seat as he ordered his steak and lobster, making sure to inform the waiter that he liked his meat medium well. It was then that the waiter turned to her and asked what she wanted to eat. It was also the moment he lifted his head away from the words on the menu. The scar on his face was a lot more sickening in person, but the last time she had seen him everything had happened so fast that she had paid it no attention. Showtime.
