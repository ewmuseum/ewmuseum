Forever In Darkness: Part IV

2 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.02.08 ~ x ~ Congregating in secret behind the closed doors of a conference room at the esteemed law firm of Chevalier, Neuville, Price and Associates sit four men in expensive suits. They are all well over their forties but could easily pass for mid thirties give or take a year or so. Each man has a glass containing a red colored liquid in front of them and a manila file folder. Inside the file folder is sensitive information on Brennan Hawthorne, who is the son of Charles Martel. He is known today as Eryc Morgan. "What are we going to do about Hawthorne?" One of the men says after setting down his fine glass. At the head of the table is the man the law firm is named after, J.P. Chevalier. He has short closely cropped blonde hair with a bear and goatee that seem to camouflage with his pale skin. His ring finger is adorned with a silver signet ring that bears the small image of a fierce raven. "He’s abandoned us and that will be a severe penalty which shall result in his very final death but for now we need to locate the whereabouts of Morgan." Chevalier taps his finger against his glass lightly. "It appears as if he’s dropped off the map and it doesn’t sit well with me." "You don’t suspect he’s missing because he’s working on a plan to come at us?" Chevalier shakes his head. "Morgan knows he wouldn’t stand a chance penetrating our defenses. We employ some of the world’s best assassins and he’d be foolish to try and make a play for us." One of the men at the table, a bald African-American speaks up on the topic. "Morgan knows about us and knows exactly where to find us. It wouldn’t be surprising with the training he’s had to be able to seek us out separately and finish us one by one. With all that aside do we have any more information on the re-emergence of Corvus?" Upon hearing the name, Chevalier grimaces much like you would see when a band-aid is pulled off very slowly. He was not much of a fan of the Corvus project or the man himself regardless of whether he had a conscience of Charles/Eryc or he didn’t; he was against the idea which is the main reason why he scoffs at it. "Corvus is none of my concern right now, Price. He is a fool and an abomination and to even think his name is not a good thing." A blinking light on the intercom catches Chevalier’s eyes and he pushes the button to find out why his secretary is bothering him when he has told her that he is not to be bothered for the rest of the evening. "Natalia, I thought I expressed that I wanted privacy for the rest of the night. What is so important that you need to reach me for?" "There is a young woman here to see you sir. She says it is very important." Mr. Chevalier is not expecting anyone nor does he know of any women that would wish to see him so urgently. With his luck it was Paris Hilton looking for new blood to keep her out of prison for a latest act of stupidity. "Tell her that I am busy and get her contact number and I’ll call her when I am free." His secretary doesn’t respond for a moment and then she comes back on seeming to be frustrated. "Miss, he is in a very important meeting right now and if you give me your number he will get back to…hey you can’t go in there!" Moments later the double doors of the conference room swing open to reveal a drop dead gorgeous red haired woman in a sharp black suit and white blouse that had a few of the top buttons undone to show just the right amount of cleavage. All of the men in the room could hear the thundering sound of her heartbeat from the moment she came into the room; the scent of lilac fills the air as it is her favorite type of perfume. They kept their eyes focused between both the woman and Chevalier because if he gave the silent order she’d be dinner in no time flat. Chevalier fumes, "You have a lot of nerve barging in here like this." "I hope you have a good reason for this." The young woman remains poised as she walks closer to the table, more specifically to the end where Chevalier himself sits. She presses her palms flat onto the million dollar table giving him just the right view of her ample bosom. "Pardon my rude interruption, Mr. Chevalier. My name is Lindsey Bongiovi and I’m here on official business for a client of mine." The senior partner keeps his eyes focused on her and not her endowment; it isn’t that he’s not interested but he wants to keep business as business and pleasure as pleasure. He picks the glass up and finishes the remaining bit of liquid and sets it down now ready to deal with Ms. Bongiovi and her "client". "Who is this client of yours? Does he have a name or are we just going to refer to him like he doesn’t matter?" "If I told you right off the bat I doubt I would be in this room very much longer. You or one of your colleagues might dispose of me in a way only you know how." Chevalier understands her tone when she speaks of how they may rip her to shreds or something equally animalistic. She must know a lot more then she is saying and it makes her a danger but he’s willing to see how far the conversation goes. "With all due respect Ms. Bongiovi, if you know what my associates and I are capable of then why did you insist on endangering yourself by coming into our conference room uninvited?" Lindsey pulls back from the table and folds her arms in a very serious manner. She gives no tell tale sign of freight because she has become used to these creatures now that she’s open to their world and besides if any harm would come to her then her client would spare no expense to see that these men found the sharp end of a blade. "While I am fully aware of the danger of being here without an invitation, I am not afraid of the consequences. Besides I am sure you have a lot of enemies that would love to know a lot about your operation." Chevalier grumbles slightly and is not sitting well with the idle threats this small time lawyer (at least to him she is) is making. His eyes show his disdain as he feigns the appearance of standing. He is quite imposing as he is a man of larger stature and maybe in a previous life may have been a soldier. "Enough! I have had it with this useless banter, Ms. Bongiovi!" Chevalier declared with a hint of anger in his voice. "State your business or I shall forcefully remove you from this room in whatever manner I have to." Lindsey grants his request simply because she doesn’t wish to go around in circles with the man all night. "Fair enough, the reason I am here is that my client wishes to gain a position within your Agency. He is looking for a rather high position as he feels he has a little leverage with information that he has that you might be interested in." Laughter fills the room as the men burst into hysterics over her words. Who does she think she is to make such a demand? To them she is nothing more then a part of the food supply and her client must be just as daft to believe he could just easily gain entry into their very intricate operation. The whole point of being a secret organization is for them to be secret and if they let just anyone in then they would be asking for trouble…trouble they do not want or need. Lindsey feels awkward with these men having a good chuckle at her expense. She half expected this to be their reaction but she was hoping that it would go better then it has thus far. All she can do is shrug her shoulders and bring out the heavy ammunition. "I guess I will just have to introduce you to my client and let you deal with him." A small smirk forms on her lips as she adds to her statement. "You would have had it easier working with me but ok…" As if right on cue the doors burst open again; this time it isn’t a highly voluptuous women but instead it is Corvus, and it seems that his six foot six frame is pissed beyond all comprehension. It was easy for him to get by security because they’ve heard stories about him and didn’t want any part of him. Even though they will likely be fired the moment he leaves their lives are more important to them then a job that pays jack shit. "What the hell are you doing here," Chevalier yells out, "and how did you get past my security team?" Corvus simply rolls his eyes as he takes his place next to Lindsey, who is a mere few inches from Chevalier. He takes note of the other firm partners who were really the upper tier of the Ravenloft Agency. Most of his kind would be trembling if they were in such company but Corvus if he had his way would barbecue all of the son-of-a-bitches. "I imagine they didn’t want to get their asses kicked," Corvus smiled wryly, "that would be my guess." He turns his focus to Lindsey for a second before looking back at Chevalier, "I think the reason I’m here is obvious. My lawyer has made it very clear what I want and you would be smart to give it to me." Chevalier slams his fist down onto the table with almost enough force to smash it had it not been reinforced before purchase. "I will not give a damn thing to you, you pile of refuse! You had your chance before and you ruined it so now you have to live with your mistake." Corvus does not take well to being insulted and his temper is just at the right level where he’d level the entire building if the opportunity presented itself. There are two choices that Chevalier has to decide from: either give him what he wants or end up with his head on a mantel. "I didn’t have much choice seeing as how the real refuse you are talking about happens to be my mortal conscience…you’d know him better as Eryc Morgan…or maybe you might even know him as Charles Martel." Corvus knows he struck a chord within Chevalier because his demeanor changes a little bit. They had spoken previously before when Brennan was still under his employ but at the time a business arrangement could not be made. Both men have a very strong hatred for Eryc. Corvus’ reasons are obvious but there is some hidden reason why Chevalier has it out for him but he has never spoken about it at least not to Corvus. "If I have to, I’ll go up and over you and talk to someone that has more rank then you." Chevalier does not seem all that impressed with Corvus’ threat to find and speak to one of the Elders. "I doubt you’d know where to find any of them." "What if I said that I know exactly where each and every one of them is and go to them saying that you told me where to find them?" "You wouldn’t dare!" "How much do you want to wager on that? I can be very persuasive when I need to be." Chevalier’s fist clenches tight with anger and his fingernails dig into his skin causing blood to flow from his hand. How dare Corvus attempt to blackmail him this way? No one has ever been brave enough to try crossing a member of the Ravenloft Agency. The rest of the council stand up sensing their boss is to his boiling point. Corvus keeps his eyes between both sides of the room just in case they choose to attack him. Luckily he came up with a plan before he waltzed in there. Corvus quickly reaches behind him and retrieves the 9mm that was pressed between his back and his pants and puts it right against Chevalier’s chest. He turns towards the group of men who are itching to get a piece of him. "You see this you fuckers? This gun is loaded with phosphorous rounds so if I pull the trigger you better expect that it will put a whole in this piece of shit’s chest big enough for you to fit your big fucking heads through it." Corvus turns his attention back to Chevalier just for a moment. "Tell them to hit the road" He presses the gun more firmly against the man’s chest. "You and I are going to talk privately, man to man…with the exception of my decidedly hot accomplice here." Corvus smirks and looks over to Lindsey quickly winking in her direction causing her to melt slightly. Chevalier has no other choice and motions for them to take their leave. Not one of them is thrilled with the decision for they know how Corvus deals with people. Their boss is not in a good way right now. Each man from the council walks out of the board room one after the other and Corvus makes sure that they are gone. He sends Lindsey over to the door and has her check to see if they have left and no one is there. Corvus relaxes the gun as he sits on top of the ridiculously expensive table. "So where were we?" He pauses for a moment, "Ahh yes now I remember. I recall asking to be appointed a position within the Agency." Chevalier remains silent. He does not have any intention in giving in to what Corvus wants at any cost, even if it means final death. "Oh come on Jean-Phillipe, you’re not interested in helping out your fellow man?" Corvus leans forward and fluffs Chevalier’s tie. "Think about all the wonderful things we have in common." Chevalier sneers as his tie is played with. As far as he is concerned they have nothing in common other then the fact they are both vampires. If he can manage it, the moment Corvus leaves the building there will be assassin’s sent to take him out completely. Of course this is something Corvus is expecting and knows most of the men that work for the Agency and will undoubtedly make Chevalier regret the move. "You and I are nothing alike… I despise you." Corvus chuckles and puts up a solitary finger. "To the contrary, you hate that I look like the man you’ve had it out for…for how long has it been? I think I’ve made it more then crystal clear that I’m not a big fan of Charlie…Eryc, douchebag, whatever you want to call him." Chevalier relaxes some with the idea that they both have a common enemy. He is not completely comfortable with the idea of any kind of alliance with Corvus because he is like a snake, one who will strike at a time when you least expect it. With the recent loss of Mathias, his protégé, he does have the room to bring in someone. Corvus has proved to be valuable in the past even though he is quite mad in the head. "If I were to even consider your absurd request for me to take you on as a member of the Agency, what do you have to offer me?" Corvus grins slyly as he holds his cards for a moment longer before revealing them. Knowing that in his possession is the one thing that Chevalier would love to get his hands on is the icing on the cake, not to mention the smoking hot stripper inside of it. "What if I said that I am personally holding one Eryc Morgan captive in an unknown location somewhere here in the United States? I would be willing to turn him over to you if we can make an arrangement." Chevalier’s eyes narrow in a most calculating manner. He could give Corvus a little bit of freedom within the Agency and in return he would have access to Morgan and finish him once and for all. He loosens his tie and unbuttons the top button on his shirt. His eyes drift over to Lindsey for a second before he turns back to Corvus. "Is she a part of the deal?" "Morgan is all I am offering so take it or leave it." Corvus pulls Lindsey over to him and places his hand on her waist. "She is my personal project and is off limits." Miss Bongiovi purrs lightly under her new man’s touch. Chevalier has his eyes on her because she reminds him of someone from his past, a fact that has not escaped Corvus. He takes pleasure in playing on the fact. Corvus brings his face to her neck and nuzzles it ever so lightly as he flashes his sharpened canines in front of Chevalier. He pulls back from Lindsey but the effect he has on her lingers for several more minutes. It is quite intoxicating and new for her; she no longer tries to resist him. Chevalier reaches for a few glasses and a bottle of the blood wine that he and his cohorts were partaking in before the intrusion by Corvus and his lawyer before him. He holds out a glass for Corvus to take but the deviant waves him off. "I thought we could drink to our new partnership. What do you say?" Chevalier pours some of the crimson liquid into his glass. "Oh we will drink to our new partnership but I like mine a little fresher…which is why I brought my own." Corvus tilts Lindsey’s head to the side and buries his face into her neck to drink the nectar of her lifeblood. She moans and drapes her arm around Corvus’ neck while Chevalier watches on and brings his own glass to his mouth in amusement. ~ x x ~ 'Hey wake up Morgan…I brought you some food." The man known simply as G says with a tray in his hand that contains just a glass with some strange looking concoction. Eryc hopes maybe he can convince Corvus’ flunky that he’s dead so he’ll screw up and unshackle him and then he’d make an attempt to escape this hellish prison that his evil doppelganger has condemned him to. "I’m not going to say it again after this…wake the fuck up!" G is a little temperamental and grabs a bucket of water that he filled up a while go to bathe Eryc. As time moves on he is ripe with the smells of being unclean and as much as it wouldn’t bother Corvus it does bother G because he will do what Corvus asks him and having his senses constantly bastardized is not his idea of a good time. The bucket of water is hurled in Eryc’s direction and as much as he tries to feign the affects of the cold water it is useless and he shakes some of the water from his hair. The hairs on his arms stand up on end like a porcupine’s. G notices Eryc’s movements and marches over to him and grabs him by his hair and lifts his head up so that he can see the man who is speaking to him. Corvus instructed him to give him something to eat and nothing more but if Morgan would get unruly he could smack him around. "I brought you something to eat you should be thankful. I was kind enough to talk Corvus into letting me fix you something because I’m sick of you looking like two hundred pounds of bird shit." Eryc groans as G lets go of his hair and he struggles to hold his head up as his new caretaker brings a large glass to him with a straw in it. G holds the glass close enough to Eryc’s mouth so that he can latch onto the straw. "What is it?" Morgan looks at the liquid inside the glass. He doesn’t turn his nose up at it since it is the first thing he’s been offered since becoming a captive. He’s died of starvation several times but not even Corvus noticed which helps. "It’s a protein drink. It’ll sustain you but still keep you weak enough that you don’t try to escape." G scowls at the prisoner for being so inquisitive. "Quit asking questions and drink it asshole." If it is poisoned it doesn’t matter because Eryc knows that it won’t kill him…only give him the heaves. He manages to get the straw between his lips and begins to drink quickly. It only takes a little for him to be full as his appetite has diminished since he’s been held captive. G pulls the glass away thinking that Eryc is trying to drink it too fast. He studies Morgan as close as possible making note of the similarities between him and Corvus and also the differences. It is easy to get the two confused in certain situations which is why his boss’ plan has worked so well thus far. Morgan’s weary eyes try to lock onto G as best as they can. He feels he has a chance of talking the man into letting him go if he plays his hand right. "Gregory, I have an offer for you if you’ll hear me out?" Eryc’s voice is raspy as he speaks. G walks over to the table near him and sets the glass down and swaps it for a chair that he brings within close parameter from Eryc. He turns the chair backwards and then sits down, adjusting his kilt to make himself more comfortable. "I’m listening." Eryc knows he has to tread lightly because this might be his only chance to get out of this hellhole and if he can’t convince G to free him then there is no hope in ever getting out and Corvus will not only win the battle but the war as well. "I don’t know what Corvus has promised you but whatever it is, I can do the same and you’ll still have your soul intact." G doesn’t seem too impressed by the offer and continues to stare at his boss’ "guest". His soul doesn’t seem to be in jeopardy at this point in time but he’s only been helping out Corvus for only a few weeks. "He’s taken me under his wing; he’s a good man so don’t try and undercut him. When he says he is going to take me to the top of the mountain I believe him because nobody else has ever done shit for me!" G’s expression becomes menacing as he appears to be about to get out of his seat. Eryc knows now that this poor man has already been brainwashed into the evil machinations of Corvus. There is little he can do to try and convince him otherwise if he considers Corvus to be a good man. He’s a cold hearted manipulator and uses people until there is nothing left then tosses them aside or ends their life if it suits him. "You may be right…he might take you up to the top of that mountain but after he’s used you and there’s nothing left for him to get out of you then he’ll push you straight off and laugh his sick ass off as your body bleeds out at the bottom." Eryc pauses a moment as he remembers that Corvus is also taking hold of Lindsey Bongiovi’s life as well. "Just watch, he’ll do the same to his new plaything as well. Lindsey’s life…your life…anyone’s life other then his doesn’t mean shit to him." Gregory cannot stand to hear the lies that Morgan is trying to put into his mind or at least that is what he thinks they are. He stands up and approaches Eryc pulling his hand back and delivering a hard right hook to Eryc’s jaw. Morgan spits out some blood but it isn’t enough to deter him from trying to save G before it is too late. "I can save your life…if you let me. You just have to trust me and let me out of here so I can make things right." Rage takes over G and he lets out a primal scream and stalks over to the table picking up the glass that he had just given to Eryc to drink from. He brings it back and yells for Morgan to shut up and slams the glass against his head knocking him out and sending shards of glass spraying every which way. When his temper calms down he realizes what he’s done and gets a feeling in the pit of his stomach that he’s going to be in deep shit with Corvus. He grabs the chair and puts it back and quickly leaves the room and locks it back up. The next course of action for him is to decide what to tell Corvus when he goes in there and finds the glass and a bloody Morgan. Back inside the room Morgan is teetering on the edge of consciousness with blood flowing down his face. His eyes flutter and he sees an image of a woman in white but cannot make out anything clearly from the saturation flowing from his wounds. "Krissy…is that you? I need you…I need you to give me strength to fight this. I need…" Eryc loses consciousness; his mind carries him back in time to a happier place when the horrors of being held captive by a vicious monster are the farthest thing from his thoughts.
