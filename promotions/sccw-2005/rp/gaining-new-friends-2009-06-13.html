Gaining New Friends

13 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/13/2009 Saturday night, for many teenagers, this is when you go all out for a party.  The hotel in Tempe, Arizona that was booked for Xander Searle wasn’t swanky like what someone like Aimz or Desade would get, but that’s also because he didn’t feel the need to pay top dollar for a place that he was only going to stay a couple of days at. He walks through the hall, pulling his single suitcase with him.  The kids that have also inhabited this floor are already starting to get loud.  Xander doesn’t mind too much, he doesn’t plan on staying in the room all that long.  He heard of a few good hot spots around Tempe to check out. Xander finally finds his room and enters.  He notices that he was expected, as there is a parcel on his bed.  Xander picks up the parcel and rips off the brown paper that it was wrapped in.  He sees that there is an envelope underneath the paper wrapping.  "Who sent you?" Xander thinks to himself as he removes a piece of paper from the envelope. To the young man that is still trying to find himself.  Hopefully this will help you along your way.  Once you do, hopefully you will understand who your friends really are. WES Xander placed the letter on the bed and opened the box that it was attached to.  Inside the parcel, he finds a Halliburton Briefcase.  He thumbs the latches open and then swings open the lid of the briefcase. "I could learn to love friends like this."  Xander closes the briefcase and slides it into the closet. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - The night found Xander at the Myst Nightclub in Scottsdale.  He was still stinging from the $30.00 cover charge.  The music that’s playing wasn’t what he enjoyed, but he wasn’t there for the music.  He’s there wearing an overly expensive shirt and even more obscenely priced pair of jeans. Some of the younger ladies that were in the club kept looking over to him.  They are in awe of his over 6 and a half foot frame. "Who’s the babyface?" one of the women asked the bartender as Xander walked past.  She catches his eye. "The name is Xander." he said as he walked up and sat next to her. "Well, what brings you to Phoenix?" "Business."  Xander was hoping that his confidently saying this would keep her from asking what kind of business he was in. "What kind of business?"  Xander thought only one word upon being asked this.  Before he could answer though, he had a sort of epiphany. "I’m in the entertainment business.  Company that I work for is here in town this week, and I’ve been asked to scope out some local, talent." "So, you’re a talent scout?"  Xander knew that he just shot himself in the foot, especially as the young woman walked away laughing. "Nice try, kid.  We get enough people here trying to pull that line off." the bartender said as Xander looked at the line of bottles. "I don’t want to hear it, booze slinger." "You damn kids don’t know nothing.  You think that you are high and mighty because you’re on that side of the bar." Searle looks at the bartender, who had to be no more than 23.  "Oh, I’m sorry if what I get paid in a week is more than what your trailer is worth." The bartender didn’t take too lightly to the comment.  "Listen, I don’t care who you are, you do not disrespect me here.  This bar, this bar is putting me through law school…" "Great, another ambulance chaser, just what the world needs." "You don’t like it, you can get the fuck out." "Who’s going to kick me out?  The ‘roided up bouncers that think that they’re hot shit. I think they'd rather just go home and jerk off to Road House?" The bartender motions with his head and then points at Searle.  Two rather large men walk up and flank Xander.  "It’s time for you to go." one of the men said as he put his hand on Xander’s shoulder. "You know, that dirty hand of yours is on a $500 shirt.  Would you please remove it?"  The attitude that dripped from Xander’s voice didn’t appreciate itself onto the bouncer. "Sir, we’re going to have to ask you to leave?" the other bouncer said. "You can ask, but I’m not going to."  The audacity that was coming from Xander was breathtaking to the bouncers. "It’s time for you to go, I’m not asking you, I’m telling you." the first bouncer said as he grips Xander’s shoulder even tighter. "Since you put it that way, I don’t need this place."  Xander walks back from where he was standing at the bar and heads to the door.  "You know, I almost pity you people.  Having to work at a place like this.  God, I think I would rather shoot myself than do that." - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Xander’s night ends with him back in his hotel room.  Only a couple of days before he can get out of the hell hole that he found himself in.  Once again, he’s drawn to be briefcase that is in his closet.  He walks over and slides it out from where it was hidden and places it back on the bed.  Opening the latches, he takes a look at what was packed in it. "I wonder what kind of friend you want me to be, WES?"  With those words, Xander pulls out from the briefcase a bundle of $100 bills.  "Because if you’re going to give me this without me doing anything, I wonder what I can get from you to do something."
