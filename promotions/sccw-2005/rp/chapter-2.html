Somewhere not so far away, fire trucks are rushing to put out an inferno started by some drunken, homeless bastard who just wanted a small flame to keep his hands warm. Somewhere not so far away, a child is being rescued from abuse by social services. It seems like every time I turn on the news, someone is getting saved. The cops, the fire department, the hospitals, it seems like all they do is work miracles and save lives. So it’s hard for me to imagine that the sound of sirens means anything but miracles being completed. So, alone and trapped to this hard, uncomfortable wooden chair, I can only wonder one thing. Where the fuck is my salvation?! I’ve been trapped, beaten, abused, and tortured for so long now. It’s been so long since I’ve felt just about anything. Hell, I must have an emotional IOU from God about twenty pages long. I know when I die, I’m going to hell. But, before I get there I’m heading to the pearly gates. You better believe I’m cashing that IOU in. I’ve always wondered what it would be like to have an orgy with some of the sexiest women and biggest sex symbols of all time would be like. I think that after a lifetime of pain and suffering I’ll be due. But, that is a thought for another time and place. Right now, I have to figure out how to get the fuck out of here. I have no idea how long I’ve been unconscious, or how I got here. Fuck, I don’t even have an idea where here is. But, what I do know, is that whoever the bastards are that got me here, they are some sick, slimy sons of bitches, and I would bet my tormented soul that they’ll be back to finish me off. And I’m willing to bet that it will be sooner rather than later. I get torn away from my thoughts of death and sex sandwiches by the pinching at the back of my leg. I curse out loud and shake my leg and look down. I see the source of the pain. I watch as a black rat scurries away, dragging its slimy pink tail behind it as it rushes away with a piece of my flesh as a trophy. Fucking rats. If I ever manage to get the hell out of here, remind me to get a rabies shot. I don’t have too much time to cry about the pain in the back of my leg, or the small pool of blood that is beginning to form at the base of the chair. Even though the pain is awfully distracting and even though the pooling blood is likely to attract even more of those disease-ridden vermin, I have to get back to what’s important. Time appears to be even more pressing. I can hear the sounds of tires squealing outside the warehouse. I’m almost certain they’re back, I’m almost certain that the minutes remaining in my life have been greatly reduced. Panic sets in. Frantically, I struggle to break free. I pull at my arms, trying desperately to free myself. It’s amazing how stupid someone can get in times of stress and fear. I’ve been trying for almost an hour to break these binds by force and all I’ve got to show for it is raw and bloodied wrists. I can’t just sit here and do nothing, I have to try and get away. I press my feet down into the ground. The pain shooting through my body is crippling and if I could, I would probably fall over, fortunately, or unfortunately depending on how you look at it, the chains strapping me to the chair and the screws fastening the chair to the ground prevent me from doing so. I look down at the ground, the bastards left shards of broken glass everywhere, I’m sure now that these pricks want me to suffer before I die. I can’t even imagine how gruesome the bottoms of my feet must look now, but the red stains all over the broken glass tell me that it isn’t pretty. So basically, in the last few minutes what I’ve realized is that I’m totally fucked. There is no way I’m getting out of here, at least not without my captors knowing, not without them taking me out of here. I guess it’s about time I face up to it, these guys are going to shoot me in the fucking head and throw me into the trunk of their car. Then, they are going to drive me out into the middle of nowhere and toss my lifeless body into a shallow grave somewhere and leave the maggots and worms to get rid of the evidence that I ever existed. Outside I can hear the hum of an engine. The sound of tires scratching across unpaved road echoes through the walls. I can hear the car rolling to a stop outside the warehouse. The engine is killed and all that remains is silence. Eerie silence, it seemingly hangs in the air. That is until it is interrupted by the sounds of doors opening and closing. Here they come. I feel like a kid. I feel like I am back in the small town, back in the darkness. Trapped in the darkness, trapped in my room. I can hear the monster. I can almost feel his presence. This isn’t the monster under the bed, this isn’t the Boogeyman we’re talking about, this is a real monster. This is what a true monster looks like. Bound to the chair, I am helpless just like I was back then. Helpless, scared and unable to do much of anything about it. I want to close my eyes, I want to disappear. But, I can’t. My eyes stare straight forward. Staring at the door, I wait for the handle to turn, I wait for the door to swing open. I wait for my fate to step back through that door. I see the steel handle of the warehouse door start to turn and I brace myself for whatever monster is about to step through the door. I keep my eyes fixed on the door, I keep my eyes staring straight ahead. The door of the warehouse swings open and I see the first of the shadows step through the door. I see the face of my captor, I see the face of my monster. I try my best to face him, I try my best to stare. The sickness in the pit of my stomach returns as his icy stare meets mine. I hear a noise behind me. Before I can even turn to see what’s going on, I feel a prick in the side of my neck. Before I can even tell what’s happened, everything goes black. Darkness embraces me. It was a time not so long ago. Picture a house out in the country. Picture the white panelling of the sides, shimmering in the silver moonlight. Imagine the windows, the clean glass, a looking glass into a happy family. Or so you would think... Now picture the cherry oak door. The golden doorknob and knocker glistening in the soft light of the front porch light. The porch light also illuminates the entire front yard and the soft wooden porch. Try to ignore the beer bottles for now, this is a happy home. Or so you would think... Through the door, and up the stairs, the entire interior of the house is painted in warm colors. Maple wooden walls, covered with pictures of smiling faces, all of them in cherry frames. But all of the smiles are masks, everybody in this house wears a mask. At the top of the stairs you can see the uncle. The provider for this happy family. The man of the house. But, when you see his smiling his face, remember, it is only his mask you see. I can hear him down the hallway, yelling and screaming. He’s slurring, it’s not really a surprise, its Wednesday, or Thursday, not like it really makes a difference. He’s drunk just about every night nowadays, ever since I’ve been here, it seems only like his mood has gotten worse and worse with every day I spend here. Even on the days I don’t do anything, I still end up being the target of his drunken rage. Even on the days when I do my best to avoid him, he still manages to hunt me down and blame me for something I didn’t do. When I hear the door to my room slam against my wall, when I see the hallway light starting to flood into my bedroom through the cracks in the sheets, I know I’m in trouble. I know what’s coming, but it doesn’t make it any easier to swallow, it doesn’t make it any less heart-breaking. The only way I can seem to save myself from pissing my pants in fear, is to play a game with myself. Perhaps that will be my fault. I know that the power went out downstairs when a fuse blew. It shut off his fridge, and he’s probably been drinking warm beer all night. All around him, the light shines in from the hallway. I hear him crack the leather belt one final time, warming up for the beating he’s about to give me. The light should be shining onto me, it should be warming my skin. But his massive frame blocks out the light. I close my eyes and wait for the beating. The darkness embraces me.
