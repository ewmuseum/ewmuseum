Morning (H&H)

Date: Sometime Slowly, Phillip Kennedy's eyes drifted open. Naturally waking up was rare in a world of alarm clocks and flights that had to be made, so for once he was able to savor the warmth of the sun peeking in from the curtains. Inhaling slowly, the scents of perfume and sex assailed his senses, heady and intoxicating as always. He knew that Katsidy would be gone; she usually was in the morning, but the woman's presence was comforting to him. Peeking his eyes open a crack, he spotted a bottle of champagne in the room's ice bucket. There was also a note next to it, but he didn't quite feel like getting out of bed to read it. The purpose, he figured, was pretty clear. Champagne is imbibed when there's something to celebrate, after all. In truth, he was pretty confident about the SIN title match. He and Katsidy had planned well for it, and trained hard. Very hard, in Kennedy's case. Really, they were up against a Taliban supporting American and his giant bumpkin sidekick. Not the most difficult duo in the world to outthink. Heaven & Hell was different. He and Katsidy hadn't even really spoken all that much about it, instead focusing on the tag team match. Why was understandable. After all, he was a rookie. The Hand had no expectations, seemingly, for the Big Stack to take home Big Slick. Phillip Kennedy, however, did. Stretching his arms out over his head, he took a good look at himself, looking down at muscular arms and a flat stomach that he'd never had before. Sure, he'd never been in BAD shape, but wrestling had cut him into a pretty damn good-looking man. Being a wrestler had made him the person he wanted to be. Before, he would look at women like Kathryn Shaw. Fantasize about them, all the while knowing that he didn't have the looks or the money to bring one home. And now? The money was still being worked on, but Kennedy was a damn handsome man. The problem, of course, was that he now knew it. The other problem was that he wanted the success to go with what he now had. Three months in the business be damned...he was starting to believe in himself and his meager abilities. More experience would help, but he had Katsidy and the support of the Dead Man's Hand. He could win this match. No. Really. He could. * * * The poker player looked down at his cards for the second time. Still the same. Hooks. Pair of Jacks in the hole. Whatever you wanted to call them. Pretty good hand. But with five other people at the table, someone could have him beat. The guy in the blueberry mask looked pretty clueless, but one never knew. He was the only one at the table more short-stacked than Kennedy, but all it took was one good hand. The other four were loaded. The preacher man sat behind a mammoth stack, shooting sidelong glances at every other member of the table as if afraid someone would attempt to physically steal chips from him. Next to him, the woman with the multi-colored hair was chattering good-naturedly about something or other. Kennedy had long since given up trying to get a read on her. And then there was the mammoth, with chips to spare and a poker face that radiated evil. The three of them, though, didn't matter. Not when the final of the four had just raised the Big Stack 500,000 chips, and was sneering at him. 'Make your fuckin' move,' the other man said, a cocky smirk written all over his face. Kennedy thought about folding. But he was short-stacked, and the fucker next to him was giving him a chance to double up and get right back in business. Who knew if he had another chance like this coming? Another glance around the table. Fuck it. No more playing it safe. 'I'm all fuckin' in, bitches,' the Big Stack proclaimed, pushing every last chip he owned into the middle of the pile. With a flourish, he flipped over his jacks, turning to face his tablemate, looking the other man dead in the eyes. The unspoken message was clear. 'Whatcha got, bitch?' The table remained silent as the other man reached for his cards, to flip them over. In truth, they were all there for the same thing, and this hand would say a lot. Either Kennedy would be gone, or he'd be a player once again. Either way, the battle for the bracelet would rage on. A battle that Kennedy did not want to lose. A bracelet that he fully intended to put around his wrist.
