9th Grade English Assignment - My Summer Pt. 1

19 November 2007

Date: Nomber 19th 2007 9th Grade English Mrs. Sinnote My Summer (Creative Writing Assignment) September 9 1989 My father always said, "A man is the king of his castle." This summer I learned that even a king, needs his queen. Though my father and I have always had a stressed relationship, he has been instrumental in me being able to appreciate the little things in life. The biggest thing I learned to appreciate this summer was my queen. Her name was Heather. Heather had the most amazing set of legs my fourteen-year-old eyes ever saw, and I entranced her. Heather was three years my senior, going into her senior year of high school. Her brother and I had been best friends for years, and up until this summer she never thought of me as more then Justin’s friend. A lot happened to me this summer, but what I remember most was riding shotgun to the mall beside this seventeen-year-old vixen. Her long blonde hair flowed freely with the top down in her daddy’s Corvette. There was something about the throttle of the engine, the way she handled that stick and that amazing body. God, she was hot. I remember how nice the breeze felt through my hair, and against my face. I longed for those slender fingers of hers all over my body. I was fourteen years old, and very much already a man. I remember how she smiled, and how coy she played it with me. Always hinting, dropping suggestive remarks on the ride. Of course, I was too much of a chicken to act, but that didn’t stop me from thinking. She was sweet, and easy to talk to. She wasn’t like the girls my age, and she liked the same music I did. Most of the girls my own age were very petty, and often immature. Not Heather though, Heather was something special. Heather was a goddess… August 1st 1989 Joshua arose from bed at five-thirty in the morning, he was out pretty late with Heather last night and he was still very tired. Today was Thursday, and his father was taking him fishing. He hated fishing with his father, as the only thing he was ever good at was drinking and fighting. Josh’s dad could easily be the missing link between ape and Neanderthal man, as the only things he was interested in involved killing something. Personally, Josh wasn’t much of a killer, nor a fighter. Sure, he’d been in a scuffle or two, but from a very young age he hated confrontations and only took part if he absolutely had to. As he showered and dressed that morning, his father sat in the kitchen downing one Budweiser can after another. The same thing he did all day, every day, and when he wasn’t doing that it was because he was eating. He even shit with a can of beer in his hand. That is what Joshua would have called an alcoholic. Once Joshua had dressed, in a pair of loose fit jeans and a plain t-shirt he came down the stairs and into the foyer. His father had just finished his can of beer, and rose a little wobbly from his kitchen chair. "You finally ready you little piece of shit?" His father asked, the smell of alcohol all over his breath. Joshua nodded, but said nothing. Anytime he bothered to stick up for himself, his father would backhand him anyway, and the last thing he wanted was to show up at Heather’s house this afternoon with a welt across his face. Besides, arguing with his father when he was drunk was useless anyway; the man never understood anything that didn’t involve booze or blood. His father stumbled through the archway of the kitchen, and steadied himself on Josh’s shoulder. Joshua stood there, and waited for his father to compose himself. When he finally did, the old man stumbled to the front door, and fumbled with the handle for a moment or two before he could finally get it to turn the right way. Josh followed him out of the house, climbing into the passenger seat of the car, buckling up right away. His father fumbled with the car door for a few minutes, until finally Joshua reached over the console and opened the door for him. When the old man got into the car, Josh could already tell he had made a huge mistake. "What the fuck you do that for kid?" His father growled at him. "You think I’m too stupid to open my own door?" The pungent smell of stale beer wafted over Josh’s face, nearly causing him to throw up. Desperately he shook his head no afraid that even opening his mouth would allow the contents of his stomach to empty all over the interior. Angrily his father slammed the door shut not saying another word. It took a moment for his father to get the car started as his beer vision kept displaying the ignition an inch in the opposite direction. After his father got the car started, they pulled out of the driveway and began making short time. All the while his father drove well over the speed limit, weaving in and out of traffic like a game of Frogger, Joshua found himself thinking back to last night. He shortly discovered having Heather on the mind distracted from the old man’s dangerous driving, and any concern of his own safety. His mind wandered as he thought about Heather’s smooth legs, the delicate curve where they met her hips. He thought about the roundness of her breasts, her sleek and elegant neck and most of all her eyes. Her eyes were a delicate shade of blue, with speckles of green and gold throughout the iris. Never before in his life had he seen a pair of eyes that marvelous. He felt lucky, that is until his father stopped the car. "Get the fuck out." His father bellowed at him. Meekly, he opened the car door and exited the vehicle. They grabbed the fishing gear and loaded into the boat his father had rented. A few of his father’s drinking buddies were already on the boat, sitting at the bow with opened bottles of beer lying around the deck. His father made him load the tackle alone, as he went to join his friends. After everything was ready, his father started the boat, and they were off into the lake. By noon, no one had caught anything of game and began to get restless. Most of the men, his father included were at least ten or eleven beers into the trip. It was at this point, that one of the men had what the rest considered a bright idea. Instead of fishing, one of his father’s friends whom he believed was named Leroy had decided to bring a shotgun along. Leroy, deciding that the fishing trip was becoming boring began shooting at small animals on the shore, and the occasional fish that would jump out of the water. Of course this group of destructive alcoholics couldn’t pass up the fun and before long the shotgun was being handed around as each man took a couple of shots at anything that moved. Watching them with the gun made Joshua very nervous. Once, when he was twelve his father brought him hunting with him and in a drunken rage his father nearly shot him. Since then the very sight of a gun brought on a greater fear of impending death. He didn’t want to die so young and without experiencing so much of his life. "Hey boy." His father beckoned to him. He had the shotgun in his hand, as he steps came closer. "There’s a fawn I want you to shoot, show em’ what yer’ ol’ man taught ya." His father had taught him nothing. The other men had been showing off, and now his father wanted him to join in the game. He knew he hated the idea of killing anything and that bastard was determined to make him do it. Thrusting the gun into his hands, his father dragged him to the bow. Joshua was surrounded by drunken men, stumbling and laughing around the bow of the boat, his father stood behind him with his hands squeezing his shoulders waiting for him to fire the gun. He knew he wasn’t going to go anywhere unless he fired the gun, so he brought it up against his shoulder. With the stock of the gun resting against his shoulder, his left hand trembled jiggling the end of the weapon. His eyes locked on the fawn standing about thirty yards away from the boat. The baby deer was drinking from the lake completely oblivious to the fourteen-year-old boy holding a shotgun. Josh’s stomach quivered at the thought of the blood, and the thought of firing a gun to take the life of anything. Josh closed his eyes, fired the gun and tipped the barrel up at the last second. The bullet hit a tree, and sent the fawn running off into the woods unscathed. "What the fuck do you call that?" His father hollered. Josh handed the gun to Leroy whom eagerly began looking for something to shoot at. Josh walked back to the stern of the boat. He felt sick, and nearly threw up in his mouth twice before reaching the rail. He spit out some phlegm and stomach acid, while leaning against the railing. "What are you trying to do?" His father asked from behind him. Joshua turned to face him knowing full well he was about to get it. "Are you trying to embarrass me kid?" "N-No..." Joshua muttered. "I didn’t mean to miss." "You going soft on me?" His father growled. "You turning into one of those hippies, who think eating meat is a crime?" "No sir." Joshua replied. He waited for the backhand to come, but it never did. "Did you just puke?" His father asked. "Shooting a gun make you feel sick or something?" Josh knew he had to be careful here, so he lied and told him the first thing he could think of. "I had some phlegm, I’ve been a little sick." His father didn’t reply, and that worried him. From the bow of the boat Leroy fired the gun, the other men began hooting and hollering, Leroy must’ve killed something. Finally, Joshua decided there was only one thing left he could do to please his father. "Dad?" His father stared at him, waiting to hear some additional excuses. What came next surprised him. "Do you think I could have a beer?" A smirk crosses his father’s lips. "You really want one?" Joshua nodded in response. "Alright, sure." The old man hollered to the front of the boat. "My son wants a fucking beer, someone bring the kid a beer. This one’s a chip off the ol’ block he is." Joshua sipped his beer until they returned to land. They drove home in silence; his father drove better this time, though he still drove way too fast. When they got home his father drove the car into the garage and left Joshua to unload the tackle. His mother wasn’t home, and his father was quite drunk by now so when he was done unloading the tackle he left a note explaining he was going to hang out with some of his friends. He went to Heather’s house, and caught her just in time to go to the mall. They greeted one another with a kiss, and then she gave him a present. The tape was in a plastic bag, and when Josh opened it he found the album he’d been wanting from Metallica "…And Justice for All". He smiled hugging and thanking her to no end. She took the tape, placed it in the deck in the car and they took off for the mall. To be a king was great, when you had a great queen.
