Part One: Snapshots

13 March 2007

Date: 3/13/07 BBBBBBBBBBRRRRRRRRRRIIIINNNNNNNNNG BBBBBBBBBBRRRRRRRRRR… "Hello?" "Oh, hey Ma. No, nothing much going on. Was just about to head out to the gym. How’s things by you?" "Well, that’s good to hear. Yeah, Adam emailed me pictures of Michael’s baby girl the other day. Can’t believe I’m a grand uncle already…" "Heh. Yeah, I know, Ma. And don’t worry, Zach knows better. Only Nana, no grandma or granny or anything like that. And how’s Dad?" "What? Ma, you know the doctor didn’t like him doing stuff like that! He…." "No, no, I know, Ma. Since when could any of us ever stop him from doing anything. And I know you two are careful. I just…I just worry about you, you know?" "I love you too, Ma. Look, I have to go. Give everyone my love, okay? We’ll talk again, soon." "Bye, Ma. Love you." CLICK "Come on, you pussy! Big tough guy like you, show me what you got!" "SHUT…THE…FUCK…UP…MIKEY!" With one final burst of movement, Lance Marshall powers back into a standing position, the weights laying across his back now level with where they had started. Four large plates rest on either side of the bar, the number fifty just visible on the corner of one of them. With a grunt, Lance takes several steps backward, sliding the weight bar back from whence it came. Lance’s partner for this day’s workout stands off to the side, grinning brightly. While Lance could easily be described as "scary huge", this man merely falls into the category of "big and buff". His blond hair has stayed expertly styled even with the sweat of a hard workout mixing with copious amounts of product. His face is almost cartoonishly handsome, matinee idol in every sense of the word. His teeth gleam brightly as he smiles, his eyes shining as he tosses Lance a towel. "You’re looking good, man," he says, "looking damn scary if we’re being honest. I can’t imagine anyone giving you too much trouble." "I can’t ever get comfortable like that, Mike," Lance responds. "Once you start acting like you’re the top dog, all you get is people wanting to rip your throat out." "Thanks for that visual, Mr. Gross-Out." "You know what I mean," Lance smirks. "Being good just makes you a target for everyone that wants to prove that they’re better. So you just have to keep proving that you’ve got it, that you’re not going to make it easy for anyone. Hell, you should know that better than anyone what with your day job and all. Most of the folks coming into your show would love to knock you on your ass." "Hasn’t happened yet, though," Mike smiles. "But yeah, I know what you mean. That’s why we keep doing what we do, though, busting our asses in places like these so we can go kick some ass at work." "Speak for yourself, man. I’ve had to take it easy on you today. You used to go harder than this, you know?" "Oh, fuck you. Besides, the producers don’t want me looking too big. They say I’m just on the right side of that line." "Listen to Mister Hollywood here. It’s not like folks online aren’t saying you’re juicing, anyway." "Then I guess we got that in common, huh?" Mike smirks. Lance’s response is a smile and a friendly "asshole". Any further conversation is interrupted by what looks to be someone straight out of an Abercrombie and Fitch catalog hesitantly making his way towards the duo. The kid stands tall, almost matching Lance and Mike in height. His build is athletic, with legs not quite matching the development of his upper body. The kid’s clothing seems to have been chosen as much for label recognition as for actual practical use. "Um, Mister Goliath, sir?" Mike turns towards the kid, flashing him a trademark grin. "It’s just Mike when I’m not at work. What can I do for you?" "Well, my friends and I," says the kid, pointing to a group of four who look, with only the slightest of differences, to have been cast from the same mold, "we’ve been watching you guys work out. And we’ve been wondering if maybe you could give us some pointers. You know, so we could look like you." Mike turns back to Lance. "Looks like our adoring public awaits, man. You want to go on over, give them the benefits of our hard earned experience?" "Nah, I have to head on out. You have fun, though. See ya, man." "Take care, brother." As he turns, Mike begins to walk towards the kid, going over all the things they’re going to have to address if the kid was really serious about looking like either of them someday. Lance just smiles as he watches the two of them walk away. He remembers being that eager. Hell, some days he thinks he still is. No, not thinks. Knows. Ever since Temptation, ever since going at it inside the ring again with Ashe and Sabuani…and that new kid, Mayhem…Lance knows that he’s been feeling it again. That old burning feeling deep in his heart. The knowledge that the most important thing in the world was going out there and pushing himself as hard as he could, kicking ass and taking names… He pauses, now halfway to the showers, as the thought suddenly hits him. This time, around…this time, we’re going to do something we didn’t allow ourselves to do the last time around. This time, we’re going to have fun. CLICK "The Belle of Saint Mark is a frail but passionate creature/ebony hair and eyes of deep blue green…" Most folks tend to have unconscious habits, things they do without thinking. For the Amazonian beauty more commonly known as Alanna Marshall, that tends to be singing. And as she expertly weaves her way through the Los Angeles traffic, the song of choice just happens to be a serious blast from the past. Let’s be honest, when’s the last time anyone ever gave much thought to Sheila E.? "I’m in love, I’m in love, I’m in love with the Belle of St. Mark/it tears me apart whenever I see him cry…" Winding her way through the parking lot, Alanna comes to a smooth stop in front of the double glass doors where her husband stands waiting. Lance smiles at her, throwing a wave which Alanna returns. She pops the BMW’s trunk open, watching from the rearview as Lance tosses his gym bag inside and closes the trunk with a solid THUMP. "You can tell from expressions he makes public/that he suffers from a badly broken heart…' Lance smiles as he opens the door and the song wafts out into the warm afternoon air. "Hey babe," he says as he slides comfortably into the passenger side seat and closes the door, "still on the Prince kick, huh?" "I have moved on," replies Alanna with a smile. "We’re listening to Sheila E." "Who was in Prince’s band. And, if I remember this song right, is currently singing a song written by Prince. So this is moving on how, exactly?" Alanna’s only response is to playfully stick her tongue out at her husband. Lance can’t help but laugh. "We good on time, babe?" "We’re fine," Alanna responds. "We’ve got at least half an hour, probably more." "Cool." "Got our first official notice from Smitty today. You’ve been booked for next show." "Huh. Against who?" "Vassago." Lance’s only response is a contemptuous snort. "What?" asks Alanna in response "Nothing." "Bullshit, hon." "Okay, okay," answers Lance in defeat, "but trust me, none of what I have to say about Vassago is going to be anything you’d want to repeat in public." "Well," smiles Alanna, "now you’re piquing my interest." "First," says Lance, "I don’t think I’ve ever seen someone so completely and utterly miss the point. Dude is six foot ten, 315 pounds…" "315? He’s giving up weight to you?" "Yeah. That important?" "Nah, just curious. Anyway, go on." "Anyway," Lance continues, "six foot ten, 315…and apparently the text book definition of big and stupid. When you’re that big…hell, even if you’re the nicest guy in the room, you should freak folks out simply because you’re that much bigger than anyone else in the room. But Vassago…all that crap he spouts about being The Anti-Christ, The Demon, The Devil, blah de friggin blah…he’s about two steps away from quoting lines like "Please allow me to introduce myself, I’m a man of wealth and taste."" Alanna allows herself a giggle at this. "Why," continues Lance, "is he working so damn hard at this? The intimations of having committed murder, the faux-satanic trappings that would have even Marilyn Manson going "you might want to think about dialing it down a bit", the utter cliché of using a song like "Am I Evil" as his theme tune…" "I see where you’re going," says Alanna, "it’s a case of all sizzle, no steak. He uses all the scary-scary, oooga booga trappings…because if anyone looks any closer, they’ll realize that there’s no there there." "He’s certainly not much of a wrestler," replies Lance. "I mean, what are we supposed to take from that last match? He can throw together some power moves, get thrown around a lot and squeak out a count out win. Well, whoop de doo, color me impressed." "He’s a big guy. Whoopee. He can take a lot of punishment. Ditto. He can dish out a lot of pain. Well, duh, that’s an occupational requirement in this business. Oh, and he’s strong? Sorry, but that’s what I do. That is, in fact, what I am exceedingly good at." "Smart ass," quips Alanna, giving Lance a playful thump on the arm with her right hand while holding the steering wheel with her left. "So, workout week this week?" "Oh, hell no. If nothing else, the fans deserve more than that. Vassago just hasn’t shown me anything all that impressive." "And hold that thought, baby," replies Alanna as she turns smoothly into a gravel driveway leading to a neatly manicured expanse of lawn, "we’re here." CLICK "I hope Zach hasn’t been too much trouble, Mrs. Konover." "Actually, Mrs. Marshall," replies the graying, slightly portly woman as she leads the Marshalls down the hallway, "Zachary has been a perfect angel. I believe he’s currently regaling the rest of the children with tales of your exploits." Lance rolls his eyes in mock exasperation. "Oh, Lord." The trio stops and opens one particular door leading off from the hallway. The room is painted white with crude pictures of dinosaurs and cowboys and princesses and such placed regularly around the walls. Toys, both educational and otherwise, dot the floor of the room. The gaggle of kids that would normally be spread throughout the room are, however, crowded into one corner. They’re giving their full attention to one particular kid. Even with the rest of the kids seated, he’s obviously taller than the other children here by about a head. His black hair is cut short, his eyes shine with a gleam that immediately signals that this kid is probably a serious bundle of energy…a point emphasized by his broad, emphatic gestures as he regales the rest of the children with his story. With one particularly childlike recreation of a bodyslam, he pumps his fist and gives a smile, highlighting a face that lets us know the kid will be a serious heartbreaker when he grows up. His head turns as the adults enter the room, his eyes locking on Lance and Alanna. The expression in his eyes is unmistakable…these are the two most important people in the world. Scratch that. To this kid, Lance and Alanna Marshall are the world. "Mommy! Daddy!" CLICK
