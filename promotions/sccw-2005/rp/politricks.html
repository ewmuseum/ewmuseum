Date: Today Channel 11 News at 9:00PM, live from New York City News Anchor Connie Lang: Earlier this afternoon, a riot occurred during a political rally in the Soho district of New York City after SCCW superstar, Sean Sterling, showed up with a group of friends. He took to the stage with a megaphone in hand, much to the joy of those in the crowd who recognized him. He delivered a speech supporting presidential candidate, Barack Obama, but things quickly turned ugly when Sterling brought his trademark offensiveness to the table. The following is a video from the events… --- The video opens in grainy quality, barely suitable for YouTube. A sea of picket signs wave in the air, and of course, Sean Sterling stands on a makeshift stage, a megaphone in hand and a grin on his face. The crowd chants "MAKE LOVE, NOT WAR" while policemen with riot shields and gasmasks stand by, hoping things remain as peaceful as promised. However, what Sterling was about to say and do would send a shockwave through the whole crowd… Sean Sterling: You know, I’ll be completely honest with you folks for a minute here. Even though I’m voting for Obama, I honestly doubt anything can save this country at this point. And don’t even fucking think about putting the full blame on Bush, because as much as he had to do with it, you all helped out. The crowd’s chanting almost immediately stops, instead changing to muffled muttering amongst themselves… Sean Sterling: You heard me, you’re to blame for what this country has become. A country full of dumbfounded dip shits, where idiocy reigns supreme and stupidity flows out of mouths as easily as clouds float in the sky. You’re all too busy leaning on the crutch of religion or worrying about who’s going to win American Idol to do yourselves a favor and stop. Just. Fucking. Stop. Go kill yourselves, get educated, do something. Anything. Honestly. Boos begin to resonate through the crowd. Even the policemen look over their shoulders at Sterling, wishing they had a good reason to storm the stage and tase him a little, or at least mace him a couple times. The event organizer attempts to pull Sterling off the stage, but Sterling simply shrugs him off, sneering and saying something to the effect of, "Get the fuck off me, faggot." The tension in the air could be cut with a knife. Next thing you know, Sterling is pulling a folded American flag out of his back pocket, as well as a lighter… Sean Sterling: Until then? Fuck you, and fuck America. The flag ignites, and the crowd goes wild. Punches are thrown and the levees break, the barriers falling to the pavement and the people rushing toward the stage like bulls on parade. Sterling grins as the ashes fall to the ground and a left hook catches him the jaw. Teargas and plastic bullets follow. More on this news story at 11:00pm. --- Upon Deaf Ears. From here on out, the fate of man will always live and always hang in the gallows of time. Black hearts couldn’t say it better, and neither could I. A poet prospers off his words, but when those words speak true, rid of fantasy and farfetched sanguinity, then who’ll bother to listen? Steadfast and stubborn, drag your bones through the dusty streets, and pray to whatever god gave you life. The world burns and the earth mourns, can you hear it? And when it all finally implodes in on itself, be that your damnation or your salvation, perhaps even both, then don’t look up, for I’ll be watching while you beg or bow, and I will say ‘no’. From tabula rasa to sweet fuck all. I am the quarry, so dig to your heart’s content, though the heart is deceitful, above all things. Still, perhaps your mind is salvageable, nonetheless. So, pray, beg, and plead, because only one thing can be known for sure: Sean Sterling grinned, and hell followed. --- Consequences. Call it a protest against human nature. Call it a publicity stunt. Call it political propaganda. Fact of the matter is, I just wanted to see what would happen when the shit finally hit the fan. What would really happen if I finally spoke my mind and stopped beating around the bush? Well, now I know. "Have a seat, Sean." I walked into a room full of suits, all with deadpan faces and heads hung low. I smiled as I sat down at the end of the table, opposite of the man in charge. This was one of the few times I’d actually been called to SCCW’s Corporate Offices. I couldn’t say I was enjoying my visit thus far. "Now, Sean, let’s cut the bullshit here. You do realize the effects your effects could have on the company, don’t you? We already have every single goddamn organization from here to Timbucktwo threatening to boycott us unless we let you go." "So, you’re going to fire me over speaking my mind, eh?" I concealed a grin behind a thick cloud of cigarette smoke. "Well, I’ll tell you, do what you have to and all, but let me remind you I’m the current Gateway champion and one of the biggest stars the company has. If you let me go, I’m sure one of your competitors would love to pick me up." All eyes were on me, smug faces imagining bullets ripping through my forehead. They knew I had them by the balls. "Well, Sean, while that may be true, we simply can’t condone the behavior you displayed. But, I’ll be the first to admit, we don’t want to have to let you go. So, we were hoping you would cooperate with us…" "Listen, I’ll be honest with you. I said what I said because that’s what I believe. Unless I’m mistaken, the constitution still gives me the right to free speech. This post-9/11 country can kiss the biggest part of my ass, for all I care." "Sean, just cooperate. That’s all we ask of you. Go on a few talk shows, make a few apologies, and then we can forget this whole mess ever happened. Blame it on drinking, do a stint in rehab for a week or two, and come back healed of all your devils. Do you understand?" "Oh, I understand, but that’s really beside the point… but, you know what, go ahead. Get me on some talk shows. Let me talk to Bill O’Reilly, Larry King, whoever. I don’t care. But I’m not taking back what I said, and I’m sure as fuck not going into rehab. I’ll explain myself, but I’m not going to beg for forgiveness. Do you understand?" Hushed whispers back and forth. I crossed my legs and cocked an eyebrow, waiting for a reply. The man in charge cleared his throat and stood up, leaning slightly across the table as he looked me dead in the eyes, trying to keep his cool and regain his composure. "You better be damn good at explaining yourself, Sterling, because if you’re not, we’ll make the remainder of your contract with SCCW absolute hell. Just because you’re famous doesn’t mean you can get away with anything you want." "Trust me. You have no idea what I can get away with." --- New Violence The ashtray was overflowing with cigarettes. True trembles shook him to the bones, and Sean Sterling sighed as he stared out over the city, as immaculate as God himself, lights all shiny shiny and twinkle twinkle on the streets below. A cold wind marked the wind, billboards leering, causing crude shadows to come out of the alleyways in the shapes of monsters and all the things we fear. Sterling knew what was and wasn’t, he had drifted into those alleyways time after time. He knew what went on behind those cold walls. He knew who was there and what they were doing, and he only wished he could join in. A woman laid behind Sterling, stark naked and fast asleep. In Sterling’s presence, she went all star bright and tongue-tied, and thus befell to his will and his ways. He looked over his shoulder at her, barely even remembering her name, and so he stood and walked out onto the balcony, clutching tight at the handrail as he looked down on the world from above. "This is what God must feel like," He said to himself. Fireworks shot off in the distance, some local festival for patriotism and ‘bringing the boys back home’. Sterling heard the cheers and, if only for a second, he wished he could honestly give a shit about what they were cheering about. But he didn’t. And, admittedly, that made him smile. A beautiful black hearted bastard, a king amongst peons, a philosopher in a land of fools. He wondered, if the fans knew what he really thought, who he really was, would they still cheer him on? --- TONIGHT ON THE O’REILLY FACTOR Bill O’Reilly will be sitting down with Sin City Championship Wrestling superstar, Sean Sterling, who earlier this week made comments that sent the nation into a fury. Sterling will talk about his future with the company, as well as the country. What will the man who calls himself The Third Revelation have to say for himself? Find out tonight, live at 8p.m., only on FOX NEWS! --- Television Rules the Nation The music plays and the live feed hisses to life as The O’Reilly Factor begins. Bill O’Reilly sits at his desk, shuffling through papers and trying to look as stern as possible as the cameras spiral around him. When the cameras finally settle, he looks up from the papers, a smug look all about him. Bill O’Reilly: Hello and welcome to the O’Reilly Factor. Tonight, we’ll be talking to Sin City Championship Wrestling ‘superstar’ -- I think that’s what they call them -- Sean Sterling. Now, there’s a lot of controversy surrounding Sterling these days after he crashed a New York City Obama rally where he burned an American flag and denounced the United States as a whole… I know I’m not the first to think this, but if this is the kind of people the Obama camp has in it’s trenches, is that really the man we want running this country? O’Reilly sneers, shaking his head back and forth as he sets his papers aside and continues with his usual rants, going on about things that don’t back and rumors that are almost never true. Palin said this. McCain did that. Clinton said something. The usual routine. Two commercial breaks pass by, the firing squad lines up, Sterling lights a cigarette, and he gets ready to say his famous last words before being filled up with holes. The Bible says to stone naysayers, witches, and any other ne’er-do-well. Sterling was expecting something more like a boulder. Bill O’Reilly: Folks, we’re back with Sean Sterling, live via satellite from Burlington, Vermont. How are you tonight, Sean? The camera pans out to a split-screen format, O’Reilly on one side and Sterling on the other. Sterling wears a big, shit-eating grin, cocking a brow and licking his lips, ready for the vultures. Sean Sterling: Peachy keen, Bill. How’s the wife and kids? Bill O’Reilly: Sean, let’s not bother with formalities. Let’s go ahead and cut the chit-chat and go straight to the dirt and grit. A lot of things have been said about you over the past week, including that you hate America, that steroids caused your outburst, and some have even gone as far as to say that what you did was an act of terrorism. Now, I’ll be honest, I saw the video of what happened just like everyone else, and I have to say, this could be the worst thing that wrestling has seen since the Chris Benoit incident. So, tell the truth, Sterling. What in the world were you thinking? Sean Sterling: Well, personally, I was thinking, what would happen if I caused a little chaos? I don’t hate America, I’m not going into mad fits due to steroids, and I’m almost definitely not a terrorist. It was an attempt to tip the scale, nothing more, nothing less. It’s almost comical what you can do with a flag and a butane lighter. Bill O’Reilly: So, you really don’t see anything wrong with what you did? No regrets, nothing? Not even an apology? Sean Sterling: Bill, I don’t see a damn thing wrong with what I did. I said what I said and did what I did because people need to wake up and smell the coffee. I mean, apparently, now people are saying I’m ruining America, but that’s just ludicrous. More people vote on reality shows than people who vote in elections. People are actually genuinely happy when we get Christian fanatics running the country. America, in all it’s glory, is falling to pieces before our very eyes, and it isn’t just my fault. I can promise you that. As bad as I am, there’ll always be someone worse a few steps away. Bill O’Reilly: But you burned… an American… flag. Do you realize the weight in doing something like that? Sean Sterling: I burnt a flag, sure, but I don’t see much weight in it. It’s only a symbol, it’s not like it’s a living, breathing, human being. And before you even bring it up, I did say "fuck America". And, y’know, maybe it was a bit too much, I’ll admit that… but at least I’m not another idiot American who has no idea what he’s talking about or what he’s doing half the time. If I even made one person in this whole nation take a step back and think for a change, then I did exactly what I set out to do. Truth through chaos, chaos through controversy. Bill O’Reilly: Well, Sean, I’m not sure if I agree with what you’re saying, and I definitely don’t agree with what you did, but just answer me this last question: What do you have to say to the people who didn’t like what you did? The thousands of people who were offended and bemused by your actions? Sean Sterling: Well, what’s done is done. If I offended anyone in any way, then I apologize, but I stand by what I said, completely. Bill O’Reilly: I won’t say I agree with you, Sean, but I will say that you make an interesting argument, at least. That’s all the time we have for tonight, though, so I’m afraid we’re going to have to cut the interview short. Thanks for giving us the time. Sean Sterling: Anytime, Bill. Bill O’Reilly: We’ll be back with the final word, after these messages… --- Loose Lips Sink Ships Simple minds in a simple times. Truth can always be misconstrued as lies when simple minds can’t tell otherwise. As laughing currents lay our boats aground and the nation begins to collapse in on itself, Sean Sterling will say he told you so, and so be it. Tired eyes settle on their TV sets, and Sterling speaks with a voice of thunder, a voice of reason, and though all ears hear him, some still disagree. Thus is the will of man. Sean Sterling smiled, and hell followed with him, but it was more like a flicker of light than a wall of flame. America carried on, as America always does, and we’re all left to wonder how much more weight this country can truly care. Hail to the thief, for the king is dead. --- Politics and Parlor Tricks. Ten more interviews. A thousand more blogs written about me. All apologies made. A cold wind came in from the north and New England froze over. I walked down the streets of Burlington, my hands wringing in my pockets and a low hum in the back of my throat. SCCW called and said thanks for cooperating. They told me that they would give me a second chance, but no more fuckups. I still get crosseyed stares from strangers when I walk down the sidewalk, but gossip is as good as gospel in this country, so fuck it. Something still didn’t feel right. Something deep in my heart of hearts. So it goes. On every television set in every storefront, there’s a politician waxing poetic about absolutely nothing that really matters. Abortion. Gay marriage. The war in Iraq. Things of no real importance, things that should be over and done with. And I couldn’t help but smile to myself. America kept on revolving around itself, and I carried on with not caring. Let’s all just salute the ashes of American flags, because if things don’t get better, that’s all we’re going to have left. Home of the free, land of the brave. Just watch while it burns.
