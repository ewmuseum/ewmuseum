'The end of it all'

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-04-07 * * * * * The drive from Pennsylvania to Colorado took three days, with stops. During the first few hours of the drive, Nathan Hollinger had taken advantage of the brainwashing "program" that had been subconsciously installed into the mind of Clinton Sage. Nathan had altered Sage’s mind so that every ounce of hatred Sage held towards their rivalry, past, present, anything…was now gone. Instead the black heart of Clinton Sage suddenly beat with a kinship that was forged over many years of friendship. When Clinton Sage looked towards Nathan, he saw his best friend. No longer his enemy. Somewhere in Ohio, Nathan brought Clinton out of the trance. When Clinton first looked over towards Nathan, his eyes widened not with anger, but with shock and excitement of seeing his best friend. Sage even remarked at how Nathan’s beard was a good look for him, rather than the clean-shaven look he had previously seen him with. The trip needed to go off without a hitch. The reason for removing the hatred was simple: If Sage were to accidentally come out of the brainwashing while Nathan slept, it could be possible for Clinton to pick up the pistol in the backseat, place the muzzle of the weapon against Nathan’s temple, and blow his head apart for the second time. Not this time. Occasionally on the trip, there would be miles after miles of silence between the two men. While you can remove hatred, replace it with friendship and adoration, and even implement memories (which Nathan tried: Clinton Sage remembers them playing baseball two years ago, after GTT5’s qualifying round where they faced each other), you can’t quite build a friendship past a few simple fake memories. Clinton occasionally had talked about his Corvette, which he drove with a surprising finesse. He often mentioned his girlfriend, Michele, and how they had met through his current employer, Smitty T. Duluth. All the while, Nathan just sat and listened to Clinton Sage lie through his teeth. He sat next to the man who had somehow killed him from across the country, the man who had tried to save Nathan’s wife, Isis, but instead had killed her. He sat next to Clinton Sage, listening to the lies that poured out of his mouth. So this is what its like to be best friends with the devil? Nathan knew that Clinton had manipulated Michele Thornton’s memories through the same mind-manipulation program he was now a victim of. He was aware that Sage had imbedded romantic thoughts into her head, removed any hatred she had for him (which was abundant) and even managed to erase the memory of Michele’s beautiful Persian cat, whom Clinton had choked to death only minutes before Nathan showed up on his doorstep. Worst of all, Clinton Sage had damaged Michele Thornton’s mind, and damaged it badly. Every single time the brainwashing program was activated, and memories were injected or erased, it was damaging the mind. The Psychiatrist, Dr. Lionel Ernst, had described it as writing on a piece of paper, then erasing it, then writing again, then erasing it, then writing again, endlessly. Eventually the paper wears thin, frays, and tears itself to pieces. Michele Thornton’s mind was in tatters. Sage wrote and rewrote possibly hundreds of times, against Ernst’s strict warning. The poor girl, if Clinton ever let her go, would come away with damage so severe she would probably jump off of a rooftop, or swallow too many pills one night. Poor girl. She’s in the greatest love of her life, and it shouldn’t be that way. Throughout the cross-country drive, Nathan had thought of about all these things. But mostly, he thought about his wife, dead since the summer, and how they would be re-united soon. He thought about her smile. Her green eyes, and the way they would shimmer from behind her carefully placed eyeliner. The twirl of her hair, the smile-lines, and the way her whole body would heave when she laughed. Nathan missed Isis more than anything. When they first met, his life was as complicated as anyone’s ever would be. A man with amnesia, imprisoned for crimes he hadn’t committed for nearly six years. He came to Colorado to get away from the stigma that Pennsylvania had, because once you’re accused of a crime, the common person won’t ever look at you the same. Get accused of rape, and some women will always sneer. Found guilty of 157 murders aboard an airliner and imprisoned for that very crime? Everyone stares, mouths agape. Isis never did. She had her own problems to worry about, and she loved Nathan for his intellect and his simplicity. She was the first person to fully trust him in his first life, and then the one to defend his honor after he had died. His second life came with a sudden, ripping fury, but it had shown promise. Their love had grown, and their family was going to grow as well. Before Sage. Before the other bastard who took Nathan’s unborn child, and meant to take Isis’ own life as well. That love kept calling Nathan. Over and over from the boundaries of this life, where Nathan waited, to the shouting mouth of Isis Hollinger, alive somewhere in the next. Sitting in Clinton Sage’s Corvette, with his wife weighing heavily on his mind, Nathan Hollinger looked out through the windshield towards her grave. Through the smears and globs of raindrops, he could see Clinton Sage obediently digging a hole down towards Isis’ casket. In Nathan’s hands, wrapped in a cloth to prevent any misuse, was the final Vekoma meteorite. He would have one shot at this – one wish – and then everything would be perfect. The minds of Sage and his girlfriend could be fixed, edited, and manipulated so the future would go on as if unaltered. Nathan would be with his wife again, his beloved Isis. Smiling from heaven, through storm clouds and the Colorado winter. Tucked into his belt at the small of his back was the 9mm pistol. Smiling from heaven. Forgive me. * * * * * Sneering into the camera, Clinton Sage paced back and forth in his own living room. For a moment, it seemed as if he didn’t know what to say, but then that wolfish smile crept across his face, and he came to life. "So this is it, we’re down to the final days before our little match, Jonathan. The match that will ultimately define your career in one-way, or another. You either walk into the Superdome as the challenger, and leave a beaten and broken man…the rookie that FAILED to win the Universal Championship, or you walk out the Universal Champion. And then you’re the man who stopped Clinton Sage when NOBODY else could." Sage took a step closer to the camera. "So how does it feel to have your whole career hinging on one match? Or better yet…how does it feel to have your whole career, every defining moment, centered around ME? Because Jonathan, I don’t think you get it. You’re not famous because of being Jonathan Rhine, the rookie who had that great win streak. You’re not famous because you’re the leader of the Highwaymen. You’re fucking famous because right now, YOU are the man who is trying to stop Clinton Sage." "It was John Covel before you, and Jacob McKail before him." "You are just a career tagalong, riding my name value to the main event. You are a piece of shit who is just the next challenger in a long line of people who want to elevate themselves by beating ME, Jon. ME." Sage takes a breath, letting the words sink in. "So what happens if you lose? Well? Do you understand that nobody will care about you? Do you understand that your entire hype is centered around stopping Clinton Sage? NOBODY KNOWS WHO THE REAL JOHNATHAN RHINE IS! NOBODY CARES, EITHER!" * * * * * Picking up Clinton’s cell phone, Nathan flipped through the address book until he saw Michele Thornton’s name. Sitting there in the Corvette, still watching Clinton dig into the grave of his late wife, the shadows of the rain trickling down the windshield looked like little serpents diving towards Hell. Nathan dialed the number, and waited for her to pick up. When she answered, her voice was full of an eager love, something that seemed oddly fake and vacant, as if always unsatisfied, "Hey sweetie, I haven’t heard from you in days!" Nathan could feel her smile on the other end of the conversation, and it saddened him. He remembered when he could sense Isis’ in the same manner. Using the phrase to access her mind-manipulating program, Nathan spoke sternly, "I am the author," "And I am the book," Michele replied. The underlying emotion in her voice was now missing, as was that sixth-sense smile detection. "In about twenty minutes, Clinton Sage is going to call you and want to break up. When he does so, you will agree and have a mutual break up. In the future, if anyone asks you about your relationship with him, tell them that he treated you well, and let your eyes well up with tears. Pressing the issue will only cause more tears, and expressing a desire to change the subject would be beneficial to you and whomever is asking you about him. After he breaks up with you, I want you to go through his mansion and remove any and every piece of clothing that is yours, every piece of artwork, leaving no traces that you had ever even had a relationship with him. When that particular phone call ends, so will any love that you held for him. There shouldn’t have ever been a place in your heart for that bastard, and I’m making sure of that. So when your breakup officially takes place, nearly every loving memory of Clinton Sage will vanish with the relationship status. Tell me you understand what I’ve told you." "I understand." Nathan sighed, he hated feeling like the power of God was in his own hands, but this was necessary, "Michele, you will go back to your life exactly how it was before Clinton Sage ever entered it. It will be as if you two never even had a relationship, and the only time you should ever talk about it is if someone else brings it up. It would be wholly unfair for you to yearn about someone as manipulative as Clinton Sage. When I terminate this phone call, you will have no lasting memory of it. You will go about your business until he calls you on this phone, and what I just explained takes place. Tell me you understand." "I understand." Michele replied. The rain drummed the roof of the Corvette, the pace slowing over the last few minutes. The storm was fading as quickly as the daylight. Not knowing what to say, Nathan Hollinger terminated the phone call. She was programmed and ready for the breakup, and the subsequent memory loss and getting her own life back. Step One was complete. Now onto Step Two. Opening the door of the Corvette, Nathan Hollinger stepped out as the last drops of rain fell from the bruised sky. He checked the pistol, making sure there was a bullet loaded in the chamber. Disengaging the clip full of bullets, he removed it and tucked it into the pocket of his jeans. Leaning back in, he grabbed the Vekoma meteorite, still sheathed in its protective wrapping. Walking over to where Clinton Sage was finishing with the grave digging, Nathan noticed the surrounding trees – which lacked their usual leafy clothing – were still dripping with rain, even though the storm has just stopped. The water was making its way down the bark until it reached the tips of the biggest limbs, or the points of the smallest branches. Tears fell everywhere, from a thousand different places. Nathan prayed the trees were weeping for the reunion that was about to take place. * * * * * "But if you do beat me, then your career takes off. But you know what? I’d still be more feared than you, and I’d still be the most feared wrestler in this entire fucking sport. But that’s not going to happen, because quite simply, you’re going to quit." Sage nods along with the thought. "Go ahead, say it. I Quit. Not only will you quit before all your stablemates and the thousands of fans watching worldwide, but you’re also going to quit in your own hometown, in front of your own family. How Shakespearian, right? You’ll be just another little name in the long list of people I’ve throttled in the past year and a half. Another challenger who rode the hype of stopping Clinton Sage to its apex, and then couldn’t manage to actually stop him." "Truthfully, you shouldn’t even be here. You shouldn’t even be wrestling me at DEFIANCE, for the Universal Championship. Do you really think you can carry an entire federation on those puny little shoulders of yours? DO YOU REALLY THINK YOU CAN MAKE A NAME FOR YOURSELF WITHOUT TRYING TO BEAT THE WORLD’S GREATEST WRESTLER?!?!" Clinton paces back and forth, clenching and unclenching his fists. "No, you can’t. Because you’re Jonathan Rhine, and you’re not famous for anything else other than being Clinton Sage’s current opponent. Go ahead, ask around. The fans can tell you loads of facts about me, like all the championships I’ve won, where I live, and what kind of licorice I loathe. But they can’t tell you anything about you that they didn’t see on television, as a result of me." He winks at the camera. "They do know loads about your whore-girlfriend, though. Like, for example, the fact that my name was tattooed on her thighs. But that’s just romantic, isn’t it? Your relationship, made famous by Clinton Sage and nothing else. * * * * * Nathan Hollinger approached the grave of his wife, which lay next to his own grave. Clinton Sage, covered in mud from head to toe, was pulling himself out of the grave after wiping the top of Isis’ casket clean. He hadn’t opened it, as per Nathan’s request. There was no way he wanted to see the caved-in skull of his wife, or the decaying flesh that still clung to her shattered skull. He feared his eyes would travel down to her abdomen, and even through the dress she was buried in, spot the gaping would where an ice pick ended the life of their unborn child. Clinton Sage was still under his control, his brain once again opened up with the program. He would do whatever Nathan Hollinger had asked him to. Carefully, Nathan Hollinger unwrapped the Vekoma meteorite from the wrapping, which was nothing more than a regular dishtowel. Keeping his mind free of any wish at this moment, he placed the small "moon rock" onto the wet grass, taking a step back from it. Clinton Sage watched with disinterest, awaiting Nathan’s order. Nathan took the towel and tore it in half, then tied the two pieces together. Once that was completed, he tied the pieces of towel around his head, blocking his own vision. "Clinton, please open up the casket. Tell me when you’ve done so." Nathan ordered. His heart was pounding as hard as the thunderstorm had done that very morning. Thundering. Clinton Sage used the shovel to smash open the small locks on each side of the casket, and then used the shovel itself to pry open the two doors that halved the top of the casket itself. Nathan could hear Sage grunting, then taking short, choking gasps of breath. The stench of death and decay, Nathan knew. "It’s open." "Very well. I want you to come over beside me, and guide me towards the edge of the grave. After you’ve done that, I want you to pull the pistol that’s tucked between the small of my back and my pants, and pull it free. Finding my heart with your hand, I want you to place the muzzle of the pistol exactly where you felt my heart, and then pull the trigger. Tell me you understand what I’ve told you." "I understand." Before he could get out the next word, the blindfolded Nathan could hear Sage’s footsteps approaching, and the firm grip of his enemy guiding him across the wet grass. "I want you to make sure my body falls into the casket with Isis, and then I want you to throw the pistol in with us as well. After you’ve done that, close the casket so I can be with my wife forever." Sage was behind him now, pulling the pistol free. "After you’ve shot me, I want you to call your girlfriend and break up with her. You don’t need to explain yourself. Once you’ve done that, I want you to fill the grave back in, and replace the sod that you expertly removed earlier this morning. Tell me you understand." With Sage’s hand roaming gently on Nathan’s chest, he replied, "I understand." "After you’ve filled in the grave, I want you to pick up the Vekoma meteorite and make one specific wish. I want you to wish that all your machinations, and your brainwashing never damaged Michele’s mind. She can’t suffer for your own personal gain. And then I want you to throw the meteorite somewhere in the graveyard, a useless rock. Once this is completed, all your memories of Michele will vanish. All your guilt about accidentally killing Isis will vanish, as well. Even you, my enemy, shouldn’t be burdened by that loss as we’ll be together again very soon. As far as you’re concerned, those meteorites never revived me. You will go back to being your typical asshole self, and Nathan and Isis Hollinger will never again enter your mind. Tell me you understand." "I understand" Clinton Sage replied, pressing the muzzle of the weapon against Nathan’s chest. With the bandana tied around his eyes, Nathan Hollinger saw nothing but blackness. But in the blackness, small figures of red started to form from nothingness. Swirling dots became blobs, and the blobs took shape and soon looked like someone. The shades of red started to vary, and even though there was nothing there to see, Nathan Hollinger saw Isis standing there with open arms. Isis smiled, and nodded her head. Nathan’s heart wasn’t hammering anymore, it had calmed. Clinton Sage pulled the trigger, and Nathan Hollinger fell into the waiting arms of his wife, reunited once again. * * * * * "So please, walk into the Superdome with the mindset that you can actually stop me. Because it WON’T happen, Jon. Do you really think I’m going to give this title to you?" Sage laughs, but snaps his stare back onto the camera. "I’ve raised this belt to heights it’s never been with my talent. With my desire to keep the thing FUSE cherishes most around my waist or slung over my shoulder. And you’ve got NOTHING on that, kid, NOTHING. So you won some tower match by simply not falling off of it, where as I’ve won this belt and defended it by choking the living piss out of anyone and everyone that’s stood in my path." ‘The Seventh Sin’ smirks to himself. "I’d list everyone I’ve beaten, but they only gave me five minutes of promo-time." "So like I said, come into the Superdome with high hopes. I’ll crush them. Because even if I can’t get you to scream "I Quit" via submission, I’ll find a way to win. I’ll take that battered arm of yours, and snap it in half. I’ll do whatever it takes to win, Jonathan, and that’s something I know you won’t EVER do. I’ll break your arm so badly, the bone will poke through the skin. I’ll make you scream out in pain, just so you understand that YOU AREN’T GOING TO WIN!" Clinton walks closer to the camera, kneeling down slightly to keep his face framed perfectly. "And if you don’t quit, even after I’ve broken that teenage arm of yours, I’ll find another way. You’re so fucking proud that your family can be in attendance, I’ll make the most of it. I’ll take your mothers arm, and snap it. I’ll show your father what being choked out by the world’s best wrestler is like. If your fucking grandmother is there, I’ll beat her with her walker just like I beat you with my wheelchair last week. So go ahead, bring your family. I’ll use them against you." "Because I’m the best there has ever been, kid. And you’re just another wrestler trying to leech off of my fame. After I send you packing from DEFIANCE without the Universal Belt, take time away from FUSE. Take the time to carve out a career for yourself, instead of making one based off of my success. Then come calling, and I’ll make you fucking tap out again, all the same. Clinton winks, and the screen goes black. * * * * * Days later, Clinton Sage lay in bed completely unaware of the events that the previous week held. It was late, and Sage was definitely a night owl, but he needed his sleep during these last few weeks of training. In Colorado, you would never know that the grave of Isis Hollinger had been unearthed, and that her husband had fallen into the casket with her. Clinton Sage, as meticulous as he is childish, made sure it was almost impossible to tell unless there was a close inspection. When he returned home to Pennsylvania, he didn’t even wonder where Michele Thornton had gone. All her stuff was gone, just as she. She didn’t even enter his mind, and wouldn’t enter his mind until someone asked about her. The love that wasn’t supposed to be, wasn’t. The love that shouldn’t have tested by the boundaries of life and death was once again together. Clinton Sage groaned aloud when his phone rang, and he shot a glance over towards the alarm clock, which read: 4:13am. He let the fucker who was calling at this ungodly hour wait a few rings, before picking up. "What the fuck do you want at 4am?" he roared. On the other end, the voice was weak. "Speak up, I can’t fucking hear you." On the other end of the line, the voice coughed, and spoke louder, "…help me." It was Lane Stevens. "Lane? Where the fuck have you been? You should—" The line went dead. * * * * *
