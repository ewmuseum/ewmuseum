Prologue: Keeping the Ring

18 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18 October 10th, 2009 "Tell me that isn’t what I think it is." "No, it’s actually just an empty ring box, I just carry it around to make people think I’m going to propose to them, it gets quite the response." Foster Nackedy laughs, then shakes his head at his protégé Jonathan Rhine. "Nice bite, little pup. You’re certainly in a feisty mood today." "I guess I’m still a little bitter over this whole fighting thing." The New Life sighs, leaning back on Foster’s couch. Foster opens the box and looks at the ring inside. "Which is precisely why you shouldn’t have an engagement ring, dude. I know you love her and all, but when have you ever gotten the indication that marriage was the right idea? I hope it wasn’t from looking at me." Jon smirks. "No, thinking of you and Magen was one of the three near misses, actually." "Near misses?" The door to Foster’s house suddenly flies open as Ian Nackedy and Gildenstern, former multi-fed tag champs Sound and Fury, enter dramatically. Ian looks from Foster to Jon to the ring box, then shrugs and says, "They say wrestling leads to gaysex." "Fuck off," Foster says, throwing the TV remote at his brother. He notices that Ian is frowning, and he matches it. "What’s wrong?" "I just realized something today," says Ian as his frown becomes a pout. Jon sits up, smiling. "You realized that you never were a good wrestler?" "What? I realized that years ago. No. Guys…I’m left handed." "You just realized that you were left handed," Foster says, deadpan. "No, but I realized that being left handed means I never get to go mailbox batting." Jon’s eyebrows raise. "What do you mean?" "You know where you grab a baseball bat and stick out of the window of the car and knock over mailboxes on the side of the road? I realized you have to be right handed to do that." Jon nods slowly. "Um…okay." He turns to Foster. "So, as we were saying…" Foster, however, isn’t as eager to change the subject. "Wait. Why can’t you just drive on the wrong side of the street? You’re already breaking one law, why not both?" Ian stares at his older brother for a long moment before sighing. "There’s just some things you don’t understand about baseball, Foster. Come on, Gil, let’s work on my switch hitting." As they leave, Jon Rhine shakes his head. "Thank God you got the serious gene." "Eh, being serious is overrated sometimes, it gets you too worked up over things. So tell me about these near misses. Did you really not propose because you thought of me and Magen?" Jon shrugs. "It was one of the things that made me stop one time. I’ve had this ring since before I even met Sarah Riley. And I was really close to giving it to her three times. Because there are times where I’ve never been more sure." Foster nods. "But?" "But the other times something wasn’t right. Three in particular in the past few months. They were actually based on events, things that would have probably ended in our engagement had everything gone perfect." "But it never does," Foster says, frowning. "Ain’t that the truth." June 1st, 2009 "This is so unusual, Jon. You haven’t taken me out to eat in ages." Katie smiles, a smidge of red on her teeth: makeup is also a rarity. "Well, I gotta show you how I appreciate you sometimes," Jon says, slipping his hand into his jacket pocket. "Only sometimes, hm?" she says, but she’s smiling. Jon matches it, then grabs her hand. "Katie, I really want to thank you," he says lowly. She looks at him oddly. "For what?" "For everything. For choosing to stay in my life even with everything going on. I know that it wasn’t easy, with what Hessian did to you and everything…" "What you did to me," Katie corrects lowly. "Excuse me?" Katie retracts her hand. "What you did to me. Sure, it was Hessian who grabbed me, but you put me in that position in the first place." Jon shakes his head, stammering. "B-but, I…I thought we talked about this. You said you were…" "I am okay with being at the shows, Jon, because I feel I have to. And I wasn’t going to get into a big fight with you about it, but don’t act like Hessian was some sort of big evil monster by snatching me out of my home in the middle of the night. He was just acting with the tools he was given. The tools you had given him." "I…" Jonathan looks around, then sighs. "I can’t believe we’re doing this here." "What?" she asks, looking around to match him. This is when people begin looking back. "We’re not doing anything," she continues, "I just don’t want you to forget that you were instrumental in the matter." "I happen to disagree." "So you don’t think it’s your fault at all that I was in harm’s danger to begin with, even though you know I don’t really like wrestling?" "No!" he shouts, causing more people to watch. "I think you’re just trying to find another person to place the blame on since you can’t take your aggressions out on Hessian. I think what happened was unfortunate but…" "But what, Jon? Unfortunate? Jon, I was abducted by a monster who threatened to rape and torture me, all so he could hit you with a forklift. You got a glancing blow. I was humiliated and frightened, and made to look like a helpless pawn on television. I think the term unfortunate doesn’t begin to cover it." Jon snorts. "Well if you were humiliated and frightened just because of that, maybe there was a reason you looked like that on television." As soon as he says this, his disgusted look drops, and he looks up at his girlfriend, horrified. "I’m sorry, I shouldn’t have…" "You’re right. You shouldn’t. Can we go now?" "But we haven’t even ordered…fine," he says, seeing her nostrils flare. "Fine. I’ll just get the check for the drinks." Sighing, he puts his hand in his pocket again. "What a bitch." "I can’t really blame her, though," Jon says, shrugging his shoulders. "No, you can’t," says Foster, sitting down on the couch. "She was totally right, it is your fault completely, but she doesn’t have to shove it in your face like that." "You really think she’s right?" "Of course she’s right!" Foster laughs. "You bring her to a place full of dangers for a woman, and then you act surprised when someone with an agenda against you takes it out on her? Come on, Jon, I know you dropped out of Rice but even you can’t be that dense." The New Life shrugs again. "Maybe you’re right. I don’t know, I just hate being blamed for that sort of thing. Like I said, I can’t blame her because I said the wrong thing, but I just can’t stand being called the bad guy in that sort of situation." "You weren’t the bad guy, you just made a mistake. Did that fight last long?" "Only for another hour," says Jon, shaking his head. "We talked it out and I apologized, and we ordered pizza instead of eating at a nice restaurant." "But the mood was already ruined," says Foster, frowning. "Sucks, bro. But like you said, you can’t really blame her on that one." "No, I can’t. I can definitely blame her for the next one, though." July 8th, 2009 "It’s amazing that we’ve been here for over a year and we still haven’t come to see the Circle yet," Katie says softly, looking up at the Soldiers and Sailors Monument in the middle of downtown Indianapolis. "Yeah," Jon says, then shaking his head and pulling his coat tighter. "I didn’t expect it to be this cold in July." "Me either…mind giving me your coat?" Jon looks at her nervously for a moment before saying, "Sure," and quickly moving a hand from the coat to his jeans pocket before taking it off and draping it over her shoulders. "Isn’t it nice?" he asks, looking up at it. "It looks like the Chalmette Monument from back home," she says, wrinkling her nose. "Yeah, sure, but it’s almost three times taller. This thing is like twenty feet shorter than the Statue of Liberty. Really cool stuff," he says, looking at her nervously. "Yeah, cool," she says non-committally. "Funny that you mention home," he says, grabbing Katie and forcing him to face her. "Because I wanted to talk to you about something." Katie’s eyes go wide, and she looks down. "I have something to tell you too, actually." "Oh," Jon says, tilting his head. "Can I tell mine first?" "Uh, okay," she says slowly. "Good. Now, Katie, back when we were at home, we didn’t know each other very well. But since we’ve moved out here, I’ve gotten to know you better and better, and I love what I know. Katie, I just want you to know that I love you so much…" "Jon, I cheated," she suddenly says. "…what?" "I cheated on you. Last month. I didn’t mean for it to happen, but I just…you were gone, doing something for PRIME, and I was lonely. And I was still mad about the fight we had, and…it was some nice guy from school, he sat next to me in one of my classes. And he could tell that I was upset, and we talked, had a few beers, and…I’m really sorry, Jon. I feel really awful." Jon stares at her for a few seconds before nodding. "You should." "I know. I…" "You cheated on me because I was gone? How sad are you?" Katie finally looks up. "Excuse me?" "I mean, am I going to have you come with me at all times now? Am I going to have to buy a security system and aim it at your vagina just so no one comes and enters it?" Jon’s eyes go wide as, once again, he realizes the gravity of his words. Her eyes go wide as well. "You asshole. I do feel bad about what I did, and I know you have a right to be mad, but don’t attack me as if you’ve done nothing wrong. I thought you’d understand since you did the same…" "I didn’t cheat on you because you weren’t around," Jon spits. "Then why did you cheat on me?" He stares at her for a few moments, then looks away. "Jon, I’m sorry for what I did. But I can’t sit here and be crucified by someone who did the same thing, so if we can be adults about it and talk it out…" "Talk it out?" He looks around, hoping no one is around to see them in the middle of Monument Circle. Thankfully, they’re alone, but this doesn’t stop Jon from lowering his voice to a whisper. "We didn’t talk it out when I cheated on you. You just ran off and then showed up a few months later inexplicably." "So what you’re saying is you wanted me to yell at you when you cheated on me?" Katie asks, shaking her head as if dismissing the idea. "I…" the anger drains from Jon’s face as he looks at her hopelessly. "No, you don’t know what you want. You just want to be angry, and if you want to be angry I won’t stop you. But I’m not going to let you paint me as the world’s worst person for showing the same sort of weakness you did." She looks up at the monument and sighs. "There, we saw it and it was big and dumb. Can we go home now?" Grumbling, Jon looks up at the monument as well. He then looks to his girlfriend and shrugs. "Sure." They walk towards the car, Jon drifting a few feet away from her as they do so. "So I couldn’t really do it then. Wasn’t exactly the right moment," Jon says. Foster laughs, and the New Life looks at him. "What?" "Let me ask you a question." Foster stands up and goes to the corner where he threw the remote. "Do you still want to marry your girlfriend?" "Yes." "And does it matter to you that she cheated on you?" Jon thinks before responding. "Right now? No." "Then you could have easily turned that into your right moment. You tell her you forgive her, you love her, and you want to be with her always. One knee, cheers, kiss, the works. It would’ve been magical, I’m sure." Jon stares at his trainer. "Really? You think that was the big moment? She just admitted she cheated on me!" "Yet you still love her and want to be with her." Foster grabs the remote, flips it in his hand, then walks back towards the couch. "It takes a big man to admit that he’s willing to go through the pains of relationships for one woman, and you’re man enough to admit it to me. Why not to Katie? Better yet, why not to yourself?" Jon shrugs, looking down at his feet. "It just didn’t feel right. Every moment that I’m ready to do it, it just blows up in my face." "What about the third one?" "What?" Foster holds up three fingers. "Earlier you said there were three near misses. You’ve laid out two, and I’m starting to get an idea of what the problem is. But to correctly diagnose the issue and fix your relationship," he says, ignoring Jon rolling his eyes, "I need all of the evidence. So tell me, what caused the third near miss?" Jon’s face goes red, and he mouths, "Oh." Then, standing up mainly to have something to do, he walks around for a few seconds before turning back to Foster. "To be honest…you did." August 16, 2009 "Babe?" Jon asks, entering the room timidly. Katie Malick looks up from her economics textbook, but only for a second. "Studying." She puts her finger on the page and starts slowly moving it along, mouthing the words as she traces them. "I understand that, but I have something I want to talk about." Sighing, Katie closes the book and stands up, facing her boyfriend. "What’s up?" "I’ve done a lot of thinking of our relationship, and I wanted to let you know that even though we’ve had our little troubles lately, I love you." He walks forward, putting his hand in his back pocket. "I love you too, Jon," she says, smiling. "I want to be with you for the long haul, even if you were with someone else, that does—" She rolls her eyes. "So you’re bringing up this again." "No, no, no," he says, widening his eyes. "I was just mentioning it, the bigger picture—" "Then why mention it? Why even bring it up if it doesn’t matter? It obviously does, and I’m sorry to say that’s too bad, because we’re not talking about it." Jon drops his defensive stance and takes a few steps towards her. "It wasn’t what I meant, but I am actually getting a little annoyed that you can just decide you don’t want to talk about something. I think we should get it all out there before I say what I wanted to say." Katie lets loose a small scream. "For the last time, Jonathan, I don’t want to talk about –" Katie starts before being cut off. "Well I do!" Jonathan shakes his head, letting loose spittle as he walks over to her, his rage visible on every body part. "It bothers me, and what bothers me more is you just shunting it off to the side and declaring a moratorium on speaking about it!" Katie turns away, her own rage bubbling up. "Where was this when you made the mistake, Jon? We never had a chance to talk about it because you hid it from me. But that’s because you a shrink to talk to about it." Katie mockingly puts her finger to her head in recollection, then shouts, "Oh, that’s right, you weren’t talking to her, you were fucking her!" "Don’t bring that up now, that was years ago and I—" "Oh, so we have to talk about this issue but even when I bring up your issue now it’s still a taboo subject. I am so glad you keep your morals with you even when you aren’t in a wrestling ring." "But what you did was wr—" Their argument is cut off by a knock at the door. Jon walks over to open it, finding Foster Nackedy with a big grin on his face. "Hey, Jonny, you ready to rock and roll?" "You were going to propose to her that day? Dude, when we came in she looked like she wanted to cut your head off." Jon shrugs. "Yeah, the moment wasn’t right again, and it was—" "No, Jon," Foster says, grabbing his protégé’s shoulders and staring him in the eye. "I want you to say it with me: fuck the moment." "What?" "Fuck the moment! The only thing in the way of proposing to your girlfriend is you! You’re the one who brought up the issues instead of just pouncing and telling her you love her. And if you do love her, and you do want to be with her, you wouldn’t keep trapping yourself with these things that you keep claiming doesn’t matter. Love isn’t about finding the right moment, or making excuses not to propose. It’s about making whatever moment you have together special." Foster grabs the ring box and tosses it to Jon. "So if you feel the way you tell me you feel, don’t wait for a moment. Don’t wait for anything. Just do it." Jon looks down at his feet for a few seconds before finally nodding. "No, you’re right. I’ve been so focused on other things getting in the way that I haven’t really focused on why I’m proposing in the first place. Tonight…tonight I’m going to do it." Foster raises his eyebrows. "You are?" "Yeah. No more right moment, just the right girl. I know that sounds cheesy, but this feels right. I—" He stops as he feels for his phone. "Speaking of," he says, then answers. "Hello?" "Hey, Jon," Katie says lowly. "Are you coming home soon?" "Yeah, I am. I actually have something to give you." "Oh really? Me too! I can’t wait to see you, this present is the kind that will change us for the better." Jon looks at Foster, rubs the ring box, and smiles. "I think mine is too."
