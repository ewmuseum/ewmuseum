Learning The Hard Way

10 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10/07 Synopsis - Learning the Hard Way Carson Riley, as humble as he may be, has never been shy about speaking of his success as a Hollywood stuntman. His body has been through quite a bit over the years, but nothing like what he experienced at War in Washington. A series of elbows, slams, punches, and the taste of defeat have left Carson worse for wear. His body battered and his ego bruised, Carson looks to move on and find his place in FUSE Wrestling. Chapter 1 - Dealing with the Consequences: Time: 9:45 a.m. Carson is laying in bed atop the sheets and comforter. He has a bag of ice on his left knee and holds the remote control to the DVR in his right hand. He is dressed casually in a pair of black Nike basketball shorts and a white FUSE Wrestling T-Shirt. He pushes the buttons on his remote as he rewinds and replays highlights from War in Washington. Kate, Carson’s lovely girlfriend, enters the room and shakes her head at him in disgust. "This is exactly what I was afraid of. How many times are you going to watch that tape," asks Kate. "This is the last time," says Carson, failing to take his eyes off of his 52' high definition television. "That’s what you said the last 2 times. It’s not going to change the outcome. You’re still battered and beaten." "No, but it might keep it from happening next time." "I wish there wouldn’t be a next time. I knew this was a bad idea." "I can’t give up. I put my neck out there, I have to finish what I started. Plus, people are counting on me now. I can’t back out of a commitment. I gave my word," says Carson with sincerity. Kate walks over to the bed and lifts up the bag of ice from Carson’s knee. The knee is slightly swollen and has a bit of discoloration. Carson slowly moves the leg as his face scrunches up in pain. She begins to press on his knee as he pulls himself up to a sitting position. "You need to get this looked at," says Kate with concern. "I’m fine. It’s just a little bruised. They checked me out already, I’ll be fine by Wednesday." "Wednesday?" "Yeah, Killzone in Wisconsin." "Wisconsin? You’re not planning on going to Wisconsin on Wednesday, are you? You’re not ready for that yet," says Kate in a demanding tone. Carson turns off the television and moves to the edge of the bed. He reaches into the drawer of the night stand and pulls out one round-trip ticket to Wisconsin. He shows it to Kate and explains. "This is my job now. Do you want me to be remembered as the guy who came in, got his ass kicked, and walked off with his tail tucked between his legs," asks Carson defensively. "No, but look at you. You look like shit after one scuffle," says Kate as she points at Carson’s war wounds. "I don’t know what you expected. Wrestling is a contact sport, that’s actually kind of the point, is it not?" "I expected you to be reasonable. Jumping back in with these injuries is not reasonable." "I have a meeting today with my new agent. I’ll learn my next assignment then. Is that reasonable enough for you," says Carson as he holds out his hands in frustration. "Just know the consequences now and don’t count on me to push you around in a wheel chair if you come home with the other knee busted up." "I know the repercussions, Kate." "I have to go to work, I’ll see you for dinner. In the mean time, get your shit together, Carson." Kate exits the room as Carson takes a deep sigh and places his face in his hands. Chapter 3 - A New Agent Time: 11:50 p.m. Carson has finally crawled out bed and is now wearing a pair of khaki dress pants and an olive green polo. He limps through a double set of doors into an office building and approaches the secretary. "Can I help you," asks the secretary in a high voice. "Yeah, my name is Carson Riley. I have an appointment with Jason Boone at noon." "Yeah. Give me one second, Mr. Riley." The secretary pushes a few buttons on her phone and informs Jason that Carson has arrived. She stands up from her seat and motions Carson down the hall. "He’ll see you now, Mr. Riley." "Thanks, this is a nice office," says Carson as he follows her through the building. Jason meets them at the door. The secretary turns back around towards the lobby as Jason closes the door behind Carson. Jason is a middle-aged, heavyset man with glasses. He takes a seat behind the desk as Carson makes himself comfortable. "Well, Carson, it’s nice to finally sit down face to face with you. How’s your day going?" "I’ve been better," replies Carson in a calm voice. "Things will look up. So listen, I’ve heard good things about you and your talents." "Yeah, same for you. You were referred to me on good authority." "Good. Well, the first matter of business is Killzone 50. I have spoken with FUSE and have your opponent." "Who is it?" "You’re going to be facing Connor Ennis of the team Cowboys From Hell. He’s a pretty nasty cat." "It’s Jake." "Huh?" "His name. His name is Jake Ennis. He doesn’t like being called by the wrong name. Hell, I wouldn’t either," says Carson with a snicker. "Well regardless, that’s the deal. You’re facing Ennis. Now, the next bit of business I wanted to address was those horrible pair of tights you had made." "Oh, you mean the ones with my name backwards? What on earth was wrong with those. They don’t need replaced," says Carson with sarcasm. "I took the liberty of having some new gear made. I hope you like it." Jason walks over to a closet in the corner of the room. He opens it up and pulls out a hanger with a full costume on it. He separates the black and gold gear as he lays it out for Carson. He has tights that say "One Take" on the back, black boots, a super-hero style cape, and a goofy mask. He smiles as Carson with pride as Carson shakes his head in disbelief. "Well, what do you think," asks Jason. "You want me to wear a mask?" "You wore a mask in Spiderman. That worked out just fine didn’t it?" "Yeah, well, that’s different." "How is that different," asks Jason as tries to understand Carson’s disapproval of the obviously ridiculous mask. "Because Spiderman wears a mask, otherwise he’d just be Peter Parker. I don’t want to be Super Carson. I just want to be Carson Riley." "Okay, well what about the cape?" "Nah. The cape goes with the mask. The boots and the tights are an upgrade though, I’ll actually take those." "Sweet. I designed them myself," says Jason with joy. Jason hands Carson the tights and the boots. Carson places them in his lap as he motions for Jason to take a seat. Jason returns to the chair behind his desk. "Can I be completely honest with you," asks Carson "Sure, shoot." "I really just need an agent that handles my bookings and deals with management. I’m used to having someone to do those things." "I can handle that. I already have," says Jason with a knod. "Right. And you did a good job. What I don’t need is someone who tries to make me do things that I don’t want to do," explains Carson. "Like what?" "Well, like trying to make we wear masks and capes." "I see." "I mean, the tights actually look pretty darn cool. I’m pretty stoked to wear them." "Alright. Well, I’ll keep you posted if I hear any other news," says Jason as he stands and extends his hand to Carson. "Yeah, nice meeting with you. We’ll be in touch, I think this could be a good fit. Carson shakes Jason’s hand and exits the room with the gear in hand. Chapter 3 - Carson Vows Redemption Time: 2:33 p.m. The sound of a ringing land line telephone is heard coming from the kitchen. Carson gingerly walks over and look at the caller ID. He allows the phone to ring once more before answering with a puzzled look on his face. "Hello?" "Can I speak to Carson Riley, please," asks the man on the other end of the line. "Speaking. Who is this?" "My name is Jim Bryant. I’m a reporter just trying to get a few answers out of you. Is that okay?" "Uh, yeah sure. I guess so. I’m not really sure how this works," says Carson as he stumbles around the room. "I’ll just ask you a few questions, on the record, and you shoot. Okay?" "Go ahead." Carson takes a seat on the couch in the living room. He grabs the remote and mutes the sound on the television which is once again replaying the Tower Match. "Well, how does it feel to have your first match under your belt?" "It hurts like hell. But, all in all, I’m happy to be a part of the team. I just want to prove my worth." "You made a promise to the fans to be a hero to them. Do you think you’re worthy of their trust?" "I spent my entire professional life portraying heroes on screen. This is no different, except this time I’m the one writing the script. I feel like they need someone to look up to, and I am not afraid to be that someone. The fact that I’m new to FUSE has nothing to do with my ability to reach that goal," explains Carson. "That’s a lot confidence for someone with so little experience, isn’t it?" "Someone once said, ‘the greatest barrier to success is the fear of failure.’ I don’t fear failure. I fear not trying." "How do you feel about your performance at War in Washington leading into Killzone 50?" "My performance makes me sick. In fact, it makes me so sick that I made another promise to myself. Not only will I defeat Jake Ennis this week at Killzone, I plan to go back and defeat everyone who surpassed me in the Tower Match. One by one. I owe it to the fans." "Wow. Alright, thanks Mr. Riley." "Call me Carson." "Okay, have a good day Carson." Carson hangs up the phone and tosses it onto a recliner a few feet away. Chapter 4 - In Over Your Head Time: 6:35 p.m. Carson is sitting alone at a table in a fancy Los Angeles restaurant. He waits patiently as a young boy and his mother approach the table. "Hi. Sorry to bother you but my son is a big fan," says the mother. "Hey, how are you doing, sport," asks Carson as he smiles down at the boy. "Good," says the shy little boy. "He was hoping you’d sign an autograph for him. You know, just on a napkin or something." "Yeah, sure," says Carson as he grabs a napkin off the table and reaches out towards the boy with him empty hand. "Thanks. This really means a lot." "It’s cool. Do you have a pen," asks Carson. "No," says the boy at a barely audible level. "Well, how can I sign an autograph if you don’t have a pen," says Carson in a joking manner. "I don’t know." Carson laughs as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pen. He signs the napkin and hands it to the smiling boy. "There you go." "Thanks, Carson! I believe in you. Kick some butt," says the boy. "Thanks. You got it." The mother leads the boy away from Carson and back to their table. As they exit, Kate meets Carson at the table and takes a seat. "Hey, Kate." "What was that all about," asks Kate. "Just a fan. He wanted an autograph," says Carson as if it’s no big deal. "Well, you’re just a superstar already aren’t you?" "I’m trying to do the best I can, yes." "Well, I was checking things out at work and decided to stop by the FUSE website. I saw you made a promise to get redemption against everyone you lost to last week. Is that true," asks Kate with a scowl. "Yeah. I was trying to sound confident in my abilities." "Do you know an Ethan Frost?" "Yeah, he’s one of the wrestlers I work with," says Carson as he eases back in his chair. "It seems Mr. Frost has already accepted your challenge and it could happen as soon as next week." A look of shock sets over Carson’s face. He leans up towards the table and answers back in a whisper. "Ethan Frost?" "Yeah, Carson. Ethan Frost. Now I don’t know much about wrestling but some of my co-workers seem to think he’s pretty darn good," says Kate in a bitchy tone. "He’s very good. Damn, I didn’t expect that to happen so quick," says a slightly dejected Carson. "What makes you think you’re ready to face someone with his experience already? Jesus, you just started." "I’ll be fine. Let’s just eat. Let me worry about Ethan Frost." "It’s too much, too fast. I think you’re in over your head. In fact, I don’t even want to discuss this right now," says Kate as she stands up out of her seat. "We don’t have to. Let’s just have a good meal." "You can, I’ll grab something on the way home. Besides, you could probably use the time to think about how you’re going to handle this anyway." Kate storms out of the restaurant. Carson once again leans back in his seat as he begins to ponder the weeks ahead. Has he really taken on too much so soon in his career? Can his body stand the test of time, or will he be too weak minded to battle through the toughest warriors in FUSE? Only time will tell.
