Date: 4/16/08 Agent Number: 82407 Password: ****** ***Access Granted*** Maddox St. James Journal Entry: 6585 Date: 04/15/08 Location: Somewhere in the Akaishi Mountains. It’s just a few days until we appear on Sin City Championship Wrestling once more. However, instead of being in the location where we are scheduled to wrestle, we are stuck in this bunker deep within some mountain range in Japan. Despite being from the Sunshine State, I don’t mind being out here where only the abominable snowman lurks. However, the lack of a snowboard and time on the slopes kind of kills it. I know they hide their operations up here, so no one can discover what they are doing, but would it kill them to give me some time to practice my frontside 720s? I guess that would eliminate the whole top secret shit they are trying to pull off. I mean we could be underground in some fuckin’ desert, so I shouldn’t complain. I notice and see things I shouldn’t, but for how much they pay me, I will never tell a soul. Though, I am sure the FBI, CIA, or Interpol would love to know what I do. That shit aside, Nex, Alice, and I were brought here for our latest inspection. We sat in the state of the art training area on what is called black D of the fifth floor. I try not to let these things bother me. If they wanted to get rid of me and replace me with someone else in this trio, they would have long ago. I’ve been the leader of this militia for 3 years now and I have had six teammates in that time. These two are my two newest partners and I hope they hang around a little bit longer than the others. They will, if they play by the rules. My last set was pretty impressive and would have made this trio a true force to be reckoned with. However, they couldn’t do what was asked of them. The Omega Corporation is like the old Soviet military. If you don’t do what is asked of you, you end up gone. When I say gone, I don’t mean fired. I mean you end up with a hole in your head, face down in the snow somewhere in the northern part of Russia. As for my teammates, these two are definitely different then the ones I have had in the past. Alice is only the second female that I’ve had to deal with. She’s definitely a looker. The last girl looked like Charles Manson in a dress and if that didn’t make your pecker curl up inside your body so far that it was peeking out your asshole, she flatter than a chalkboard. Alice, on the other hand, is the walking, talking personification of the lyrics from the song, "Brickhouse". She has bounce and jiggle in all the right areas with a smile that would make you tear a hole in your pants. It was like a Playboy Playmate jumped off the pages and landed in a wrestling ring. Alice’s in-ring work matched her physical appearance. I have never seen someone with balance and agility like her. Before she joined the team, I watched footage of her competing in some Japanese show called Kunoichi. This girl dominated four obstacle courses and made it look like child’s play while other top athletes couldn’t even make it past the first one. Yet, seeing it in person is a completely different creature. This girl could have been an Olympic gymnast. She was twisting and flipping off of everything in sight like a goddamn rubber ball. The bosses here found the perfect warrior to take t the skies and make the crowd ooh and ah with amazement. Then, something happened that completely changed my outlook on her. She opened her mouth. Since then, I thank god every day for being deaf. I can read lips rather well and this girl just won’t shut the hell up. She has an opinion on anything and everything. I can look around and see how her diarrhea of the mouth annoys the high holy hell out of others. I thought I had it made, but would you believe it? The girl knew how to fuckin’ sign. Just my damn luck. While that one may never shut up, the other one speaks as much as I do. Nex is a flat out freak. The man is like one of those killers in bad slasher films. They said they found him in a correctional facility, as doing time for assault and battery. Apparently, he took out fifteen guys in a bar and blew up a car. The explosion damaged his face and if a tattooed, seven-foot, three-hundred fifty pound monster wasn’t frightening enough, he now wears a mask with the blood of his opponents splattered on it. I’m not even sure the guy is goddamn human. He is a juggernaut of pain and destruction. Luckily for me, he follows commands and when given a task, he does it no questions asked. The big ogre isn’t perfect though. He has the IQ of a rock. He can’t think for himself and needs someone around at all times instructing him in what to do next. It was like god made this massive beast and gave him the brain of one of Michael Vick’s pitbulls. He is just a wild dog and when you set him loose, it is impossible to pull him off someone when he is attacking them. He gets the taste of blood and he will not stop until they are dead. Well, enough about those two. We sat in the facility for about an hour before the people in power sent in their cute little secretary to bring us into the main room. Alice and the secretary, who I found out was named Kari, led the way. I couldn’t read their lips, but I’m sure they were talking about some stupid girl shit like what tampons they use and if they liked Orlando Bloom better than Johnny Depp in "Pirates of the Caribbean". All I know is that their sweet asses were swaying from left to right in perfect time. It was like poetry in motion, though my associate Nex probably didn’t give a shit about it… the big homo. The journey finally ended with a pair of double doors that opened with a hiss like something out of Star Trek. Before us, sat the entire high council of the Omega Corporation. Though, I saw them all, it just felt like something wasn’t right, though I couldn’t put my finger on it. Anyhow, Kari stepped to the end of the table and began to sign in time with the following words of the council. On a personal note: I boned Kari later that night. How and could sign? How could I resist? "Maddox, it is good to see you again," a dark-haired man with a goatee said with a grin and a nod to which I nodded back. "I see the new recruits have been working out nicely for you." "That was some nice work with your first mission in SCCW," a blond man with an English accent complimented off to my right. "We expect it to continue," a man in sunglasses announced from the head of the table. "The Omega Corporation demands perfection from its agents. You three have been chosen to spread our influence into the profitable world of professional wrestling. We will not settle for second best. Should any of you falter, you will be replaced." This talk didn’t seem to bother Nex, but Alice wasn’t very pleased with it. You could see it in her eyes, but for once, she used her brain and kept her trap shut. "Your next assignment is to capture the SIN titles in SCCW," the man in shades continued. "This council has come to the conclusion that the acquisition of those titles will be a perfect launching point for our objective. You will be given a two month timeframe to accomplish this goal. We do expect you to have them by then. Is that in any way unclear?" "No, sir," Alice replied, as Nex and I shook our heads. "That is the correct answer," the head honcho announced and leans back in his chair. "This meeting is over. Report back here in two months with the titles or not at all." With that said, the entire room is instantly empty besides the secretary and us. I really do hate when they don’t even care enough to show up in person for these meetings. All the money in the damn world and they use 3-D holograms to conduct meetings instead of just hopping on the damn personal jet. Holograms for when you care to send the very best. "Fucking wonderful," Alice couldn’t hold her tongue for even a second after they left. "Hey, you have been in the promotion for two weeks. Why don’t you have the titles yet? Like we book the fucking place and can just line up a damn title shot. If they were actually here in person, I would have put them in their damn place." God, I wish she would just shut the fuck up. I turned around and left, giving a nod to Nex. He knew exactly what to do. The big ogre scooped up Alice and carried her out of the office. "Let me go, King Kong," she started to scream while trying to get loose. "Do I look like Fay-fucking-Ray to you? God damn it! Put me down." I really should just give him the command to kill her and save us all the headache. Her annoyance aside, the corporation really wants its filthy mitts in everything. I know they have stakes in everything ranging from professional sports teams to some of the largest conglomerates in the world. I guess getting billions upon billions each day isn’t enough for some people. You have to tap every resource you possible can. Not that I am complaining. They pay me a pretty penny on top of my contract for whatever wrestling promotion I am in. I just never saw professional wrestling as a fantastic money-making venture. But with millions across the globe watching every week, a little product placement wouldn’t hurt a damn thing. Weapons of mass destruction and pharmaceuticals just don’t give you that kind of exposure, if you catch my drift. ***Entry completed***
