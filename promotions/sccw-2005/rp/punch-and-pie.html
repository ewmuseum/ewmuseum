Punch and Pie

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The machine sits silently as its patient forcefully pulls various plugs and electrodes from himself. "Get me the fuck out of here." Edwards’s voice is harsh and gravelly from the years of smoking, long nights, and heavy drinking. Right now his voice sounds even worse. "Edward..." With a final yank, Edward pulls a final electrode from his chest and looks around at the hospital room he lies in. The walls and ceiling are a uniform puke green, and the rest of it isn’t much better. "Goddamnit Kia, I’m going to fucking die in a place like this soon enough, I’ve spent to fucking much time of my life in one hospital or another to want to spend any more here; so get me the fuck out of here!" Normally when Edward raises his voice to someone, it is full of malice, anger, or wrath. Right now the tone is petulant. Kia Kupalo, Edwards’s wife of nearly 7 years sighs as her husband points across the room at a pair of jeans folded neatly and sitting on a chair. "Edward Carter Lambert stop acting like Drake! Even your son doesn’t whine as much as you do when he goes to the doctor!" Her tone can easily be described as angry. Even so, she does cross the room to where the chair is and pick up the jeans. As she turns back to him he swings his legs from the bed and takes the jeans as she hands them to him. "Yeah, but Drake doesn’t know to hate these places yet." He mutters under his breath as he slips into the jeans and winces when he begins to put on the gray button up shirt she hands to him next. "Let me guess Kia, the doctor told you he wants me to stay a little bit longer for observation and possible cancer remission treatment, right?" Before she can answer a head pokes around the slightly open wooden door and shakes. "Urm no." Edwards foster brother, Dashielle Fairborne Jr., formerly known to the wrestling world as Azaul grins in at his dumbfounded brother. "They said your cancer is gone, your body is in perfect shape, and you should live to be a hundred! Oh, and they have punch and pie!" Edward stares at his brother silently as he steps into the room. Dressed in a pair of dark jeans and a t-shirt that reads "Ask me about my brother’s cancer!" Dash pushes the door open all the way and with a grand gesture points at the corridor. "Punch, and pie." "I fucking hate you so much right now Dash." * * * * * The large oak door slams open and rebounds off the wall plate strategically placed to stop the door handle from knocking a hole in the wall. From the numerous indentations it has seen a lot of use. "Fuck this!" Through the open door stalks a visibly seething Edward Lambert. His long stride takes him to the base of the stairs to the second floor in three paces, as he places his right hand on the wooden banister he turns and points his left hand back at the people coming in through the door. "I don’t have to fucking explain every goddamned thing I do, just so you can fucking second guess it and tell me how fucking wrong I am! I know what I’m fucking doing, I mean, Jesus fucking Christ, I’m the same fucking person I was last week, last month, and last year! I’m still dying by inches, I’m still holding on by the skin of my fucking teeth, and I’m still the same fucking person I always have been! If you think that cancer is going to make me stop fucking being me, then you have another fucking think coming!" Now his voice is one of wrath. "I knew what I was fucking doing, I knew it then, and I know it now! If what I’m doing isn’t fucking good enough for you, or is too much like how I acted when I was drinking, then fuck you Dash! For fucks fucking sake let me fucking die on my own fucking terms!" Then with a turn he stomps his way up the stairs before the two people behind him can even enter the house, moments later a door on the second floor slams closed. Only then does Dash steps into the room. After a quick glance upstairs he turns to the lady behind him and nods. "Was it the shirt?" The sigh from Kia is audible as she finally sets foot in her own home. * * * * * Kia stares across the kitchen table at Dash and shakes her head. "He hasn’t changed Dash. He’s still the same stubborn asshole he’s been for years, and if anything, he’s getting worse." Dash’s large hands wrap themselves around the mug of coffee that sits before him. With a sigh he takes a drink and sets it down again. "I know Kia. You know how he is, heck, how he always was. Ed has always had some kind of an immortality complex and that won’t change. He could be piloting a burning airplane right into a minefield filled with nuclear bombs, and he would believe, not think, not hope, but believe with every fiber of his being, that he would walk away with maybe a scratch or a cracked rib." He runs his hands through his dirty blond hair with a sigh. "He does know he’s dying right?" A faint frown crosses Kia’s face for a moment. "Oh yes Dash. He uses that as some kind of noble shield when we argue. No, I can’t fucking do that, I’ve got lung cancer and am fucking dying! I love you honey, but I can’t help you with that, I’m fucking dying! Sorry I forgot to take care of that, I was thinking about the legacy I’ll leave behind when I die of fucking cancer! It is so damned annoying!" Her Edward impression done, she reaches for the small glass before her and takes a drink as Dash nods. "Well, he is dying Kia." She slams the glass on the table hard enough to slosh some of the liquid out onto the white tablecloth. "No shit! I’m sorry Dash; it’s just that he’s been…" As Kia struggles for words Dash nods again. "He’s been more BloodAngel, and less Edward." "Exactly. He’s pouring so much of his energy into these videos he’s got that kid living in our spare room making for him and into the SCCW that it feels like there isn’t enough left over for Drake or me." She knocks back the rest of the drink, stands up, and walks to the black marble counter to pour herself another drink. "Kia, you know he’s never been quite all there." With a nod she pours a measure of vodka over the ice in the glass and turns to face the table, glass in hand. "Oh I know he’s never been all there Dash. He took the professional name BloodAngel for gods’ sake! He wears the scars of his life on his body like his own red badge of courage, his medical file reads like a text book! He’s not all there? Hell Dash, I don’t know if he was ever there!" She knocks back the drink and turns to make another. "He used to be able to turn it off and on at will. He would come home from Minnesota or wherever the hell the NWC had him at and he was as normal as he ever was. He’d sleep for a day or so, and then everything was fine. Sure some times he would come home with stitches or a broken whatsit or a cracked whatever, but he was always the same. Edward at home, BloodAngel on television." She sighs. "Now it just feels like the Edward I fell in love with, the Edward I married, is gone." She carries the glass back to the table and sits down in the chair. Dash smiles and stares at the mug before him. "The funny thing is; I think I was starting to understand him. We spent years together as kids, always trying to one up the other. When I played soccer, he played football. When I dated Ellen Waterstone, he dated Natasha Evans." Kia stares at him. "Who?" Ever the gentleman; when Dash is embarrassed he blushes. He does so now. "Uh, Ellen was kind of the home coming queen and a cheerleader with a reputation. Natasha was the prom queen and the head cheerleader, also with a certain reputation." "Oh." "Yeah." Dash coughs into his hand and takes a sip of coffee before hurriedly continuing. "He was always this way. Always trying to one up everyone around him, always pushing himself to be just thaaaaaat much better than anyone else. It didn’t matter if he was drinking or doing crazy stuff inside the ring. It’s like I said, he’s never been all there." Kia stares down at the glass before her and pushes it away with a trembling hand. "I just, I just don’t know what to do with him anymore Dash." Dash scoots his chair back, stands up, and walks around to Kia. He wraps his long arms around her and gives her a gentle hug. "We do what we have to Kia. Whatever it takes, we have to do it." She sniffs and smiles faintly. "Then you need to change that shirt." * * * * * The spare bedroom in Edward Lambert’s home in Lexington isn’t well lit. The ceiling fan/light combination doesn’t work, and the window that normally allows light in isn’t letting much in right now with the blinds down. The slices of light that do come through the Venetian blinds bathe the two occupants of disheveled double bed in a soft glow. The one on the left is a man named Nick Black. While normally, his face is full of various piercings, right now the various metal studs, hoops, and rings are sitting in a porcelain dish atop the small bedside table. Normally he also wears clothes, which right now he isn’t. The figure on the right, and bathed in most of the light, is a short redheaded woman. She lies on her bare stomach and gently traces the dragon tattooed across Nick’s stomach with the fingernails of her right hand. "What are you thinking?" Nick looks down at the woman beside him, with whom he has spent so much time this past two weeks, and with a smile props his hands behind his head. "Right now Tabby, I’m not sure what I’m thinking." "Really?" Her voice is soft and melodious. Not throaty or hoarse, just soft. "Well, I mean, I don’t know what I’m thinking." She smiles and starts drawing that same hand further down his stomach and towards what lies below. "They say that men think about sex every seven seconds." He smiles as her hand strays lower and shakes his head. "You can’t get enough can you?" Still smiling she reaches her intended target and starts doing… things as he stretches his arms out and shakes his head. "The funny thing is, right now, I think I want a cigarette."
