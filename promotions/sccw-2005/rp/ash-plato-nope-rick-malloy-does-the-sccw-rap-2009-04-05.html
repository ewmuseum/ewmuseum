Ash Plato? Nope. Rick Malloy does the SCCW Rap.

5 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/05/2009 As the video begins rolling, there is no Ash Plato. Instead there is Rick Malloy with a SCCW backdrop behind him. He has a microphone in hand. Malloy: Sorry to inform you fans but Ash Plato will be unable to perform in SCCW any more so I am taking his place. So, to keep him from no showing, I will be debuting my first rap song…LIVE! *ahem* A beat starts playing in the background and it’s really groovy. Rick then starts rapping. Rick: I can’t run I can’t hide Without Amy Campbell By my side Fine ass titties And big ass booties I eat flakes sponsored by Doug Flutie And I think that Desade is a cutie Legion is out of his mind Anyone crazier than von Doom is harder to find I spit out rhymes better than K-Kwik The ladies all love my big pixie stick Cream filling Psycho is chilling More than Hunter Sabuani And Wyatt Conners in a match that is thrilling World falling apart Faster than the SCCW midcard faces I wish I was like Eddie Murphy in Trading Places And could do a switch Going to win the LiveWire Title I got more moves than Tupac Shakur He might be related to Devin in PRIME He’s like a lemon compared to my lime My rhymes flow better than Lil Wayne He looks like Busta Rhymes if he wen’t insane West Coast is where it’s at You’re all so bad even Joaquin Phoenix could pin your shoulders to the mat The song ends and applause is dubbed in. Malloy takes a bow. Malloy: Thank you, thank you. I’ll be here all week. He then walks off the set and the scene fades to black.
