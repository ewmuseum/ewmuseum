This is awful. Don't read it.

26 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/26/09 How do people normally start these things? Seriously, do I have to put a date up here in the corner? Is that the rule? I mean, don’t get me wrong, I seriously doubt that if I fuck this up that some international conglomerate (there’s a word I’ve never used before – go me!) of brainy types is going to kick down my door and arrest me. First, nerdy types don’t kick that hard. Second, I don’t think I live in their jurisdiction. Third… Okay, I don’t really have a third. I don’t suppose "profit" counts, either. Christ Jesus this isn’t going well at all. It’s July 26, in case it matters, and it’s ass-early. Couldn’t fall asleep and decided to just say fuck it. I’m not taking my chances with the nerds. I don’t normally do this. I’m generally bad at the whole "getting my thoughts out there" thing. It’s why I have about 7 blogs and don’t update any of them. A few years ago, when I checked myself into what we’re going to call "A Happy Place" for a few weeks a good friend of mine, who also happens to have an advanced medical degree (go him!), told me that it would be a good idea if I wrote stuff down every now and again. I’m all for freedom of expression, clearly. It’s one thing to think it, but it’s hard for me to actually write stuff down. I had a wrestling promotion pay a psychiatrist to follow me around the country once, trying to prove that I was totally fucked-up nuts. Getting all journally (that a word? fuck it) is like giving those same people ammo. "Here, have some bullets. Aim for the torso, it can absorb more hits." Who does that? What the fuck am I talking about? Yeah, so Wyatt made me look stupid. Obvious joke alert! "Not like that’s a hard thing to do. Wokka wokka." Not just bad, stupid. It was my own fault, really. The guy’s a prick, no question there, but he’s also damn smart. I knew this. I knew this from years of working with two dudes that he used to represent, and even still my deluded little brain tried to come up with a plan to put one over on the guy. "We’ll have no rules," it shouted, "and that’ll throw him off." Seemed like a good idea at the time. Now? Not so much. Okay, who am I trying to kid. That idea should have come with a helmet, some dirty sweatpants, and a GI Joe lunchbox. I was thinking about it last night for a little bit, and trying to figure out just what the fuck it is I do around here. Obviously my record is total ass. Night one back and Lance plays "Throw The Jared At The Sun" for a half hour. Good times those were, hoo boy. And then, because of Lance-y (should there be a hyphen, I don’t know) we get to tool on Wyatt and Lane for a while. Hooray us! Was nice while it lasted, but as soon as we removed Muscles McKenzie from the equation, and I have to fend for myself again, things don’t go so great. Beat on Wyatt with the big guy, eat my own balls without him. Awesome. Oh, and the fine people in the front office have already been kind enough to tell me who’s next on the dance card. Is this going to suck? Damn right it is. Look at me all emo. Maybe I should just crawl into the basement with some Evanescence records and paint my fingernails black. Fun Fact: I want to be a Green Lantern. Seems like a fun job. I don’t normally do jewelry, but I think for them I’d make an exception. In brightest day, in blackest night, no evil shall klaatu barada something something. I realize I could just Google the rest, but I’m lazy. I’m also overtired, sore, and my mind is fucking scattered. Also, I have no idea who I’m even trying to justify this to, or why I’m justifying it at all. I’m talking to myself by way of a goddamn computer. Yeah, my life R-U-L-Z. That was sarcasm, bee tee dubs. Haven’t talked to Calvin since she left my house on Friday, which isn’t really that big a deal, seeing as it was only two days ago. Not going to lie, I’m a pretty amazing actor, since I don’t think she caught on that I was WEIRDED THE FUCK OUT by the random "Hey, I’m wasted and will show up at your house" thing. How the hell do you even deal with that? Like, first I’m getting punched in the head, then I’m cleaning puke off the floor. WTF?! For the record, and not like this matters, but it didn’t get weird because of me. I wasn’t the one who snuck out of the room ten years ago. Nope, I was the one who woke up alone and wondering what the FUCK had just happened. I wasn’t the one who got all distant afterwards. It wasn’t supposed to go down that way. That night wasn’t supposed to happen. Not like that. We were kids, and we didn’t know any better, and it fucked everything up. And then I went away, which makes this all my fault, or at least that’s the lie we’re all sticking to. Funny thing? I’m okay with it. I shouldn’t be, I don’t think, or maybe I should. Who knows. Just doesn’t seem right to get caught up in it. It happened. I can’t make it unhappen. I was in love, though. First time. Only time. Motherfucker.
