Horrible Memories

14 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/14/2007 TUESDAY 14TH AUGUST 2007 3:00am It was 3am and in their Chicago home, El Diablo (Raul) and Angela were sleeping like any normal couple would at this hour. Both were tucked under the blue duvet of their king sized bed in their moderately sized bedroom. They had, had some tough times recently in their married life, but for now they were doing just fine. Angela’s long red hair was covering her face as she slept, while her husband tossed and turned as he looked uncomfortable about something with his eyes beginning to flutter as the rapid eye movement set in. GRUNT The Mexican Devil was asleep, but seemed troubled by something he was dreaming about. Something he had often thought about over the years, but something he hadn’t thought about in a very long time. Something he was about to relive again. EL DIABLO’S DREAM THURSDAY 29TH SEPTEMBER 1988 "That was amazing," said Raul, beaming with delight. His elder brother Jorge smiled, as he opened the door to his rusted out car which after seventeen years of use was surprisingly still running. "Come on, better get home little bro," Jorge stated. The 14 year old Raul, yanked the passenger door hard in order to get it to open and almost fell backwards as it did, which prompted a laugh from Jorge. "Don’t worry, I’m sure you’ll grow as strong as me someday," said Jorge, reassuringly. Smirking, Raul got into the car and sat down, soon followed by his 18 year old brother. "Thanks for taking me all the way to Juarez to see this," Raul said gratefully. Jorge grinned, as he placed the key in the ignition to get the car moving and on their way back home to Puebla. "It’s fine, you know I love wrestling and tonight was pretty damned special. I mean, what an event, three hours of spectacular matches," exclaimed Jorge. The car was now moving swiftly down an open road at 11:30pm towards their desired destination as Jorge and Raul conversed about the night of wrestling action they had just seen. "I couldn’t believe it when El Super Libre leaped off of that cage with a moonsault," commented Raul. Full of joy about the night they had just shared, Raul couldn’t stay still in his seat and was constantly wriggling about, mimicking the wrestling moves with his hands, despite being strapped in. "Or when The Masked Magician of Mexico perfectly executed that 640 degree top rope splash. It was absolutely incredible," Raul stated excitedly. Concentrating on driving home, Jorge wasn’t watching Raul’s overwhelming enthusiasm by recreating the moves with his hands but Jorge had come to realise something that night, something that he thought would change his life forever. "Yes, it was a great night Raul. But Raul, I have something to tell you, and you cannot tell Mom or Dad. Promise me," he said sternly. Raul simply nodded as he awaited to hear his brothers secret. "I’ve decided that I want to be a wrestler," remarked Jorge. Raul’s face lit up with delight. "That’s fantastic news. What ring name are you going to use?" he asked. Licking his lips, and making sure not to lick his thin moustache, Jorge was pondering what to call himself. Seeing that he was struggling to come up with a name, Raul ran his hands through his short black hair and began to think up a wrestling name for his brother. "How about, Jorge El Magnifico?" enquired Raul. Bellowing with laughter, Jorge’s long black hair began to shake up and down. "It makes me sound like a magician," Jorge replied. Realising his stupid statement, Raul began to think up another name that Jorge could use. "What about, The Flying Mexican?" he asked his brother. Shaking his head, Jorge was surprised at the terrible wrestling names his brother was coming up with. Feeling let down that Jorge hated his choice of names, Raul suddenly had the perfect idea of a name that he could use. "Maybe you should use your first pet’s name and Mom’s maiden name," suggested Raul. Grinning, Jorge turned to his brother for a second. "That’s how you get your porn star name, stupid," Beginning to look angry, Raul was sulking. "Just try it anyway. I don’t see you coming out with any ideas," he snapped. Concentrating on the road, Jorge was still thinking of a name to call himself, but decided to submit to his sibling’s idea. "Okay, using your logic I’d be called Chivas Garcia," stated Jorge. This time, it was Raul’s turn to laugh loudly. "You named a pet after a football team?" he questioned his brother. Jorge simply shook his head in disbelief. "Well yes I did, but the whole pet name and maiden name thing was your idea," Realising he was right, Raul felt a little embarrassed, but as he was just in the company of his brother, it soon subsided. "Do you think I could ever be wrestler?" queried Raul. Thinking about it for a few seconds, Jorge nodded. "Well you’re only 5’2 now, but if you grow and put on a bit of weight, I think you could make a really good wrestler," Jorge reassured him. Hearing his brother’s support was a big compliment for Raul, as he looked up to Jorge as a role model and being a big fan of wrestling, had always wanted to be a wrestler since he was a young child. "I don’t think I’d use a name, I’d rather just use my name. Imagine it, Raul Diaz, World Champion," Raul exclaimed. Jorge bellowed with laughter once more as he adjusted his plain black t-shirt, which had a chilli stain on it from earlier in the night. "I don’t think you’d get very far with that name. Just like I won’t with Jorge Diaz. We both need to use wrestling names, stage names," responded Jorge. The weather outside of the vehicle was now getting bad, with rain beginning to crash down onto the roof, bonnet and most importantly the road. However, Jorge was a good driver and was very capable of driving and conversing at the same time. "Damn this chilli stain," he remarked. Snickering, Raul had thought of the name his brother could use. "Jorge "Chilli Stain" Diaz. Now that could work," Raul stated confidently. Bemused, Jorge couldn’t believe the stupid names his younger brother was spouting. "That is just ridiculous. I bet if you ever become a wrestler, you’ll choose some stupid name like El Diablo or something," Jorge expressed his disdain. Raul simply chuckled away at that suggestion. "Come on bro, who would ever call themselves El Diablo? I’d never be taken seriously by calling myself El Diablo," he responded. Keeping his eyes on the road as they approached an intersection, Jorge smirked. "And you think I’d be taken seriously by being called Chilli Stain? They’d end up calling me Crotch Stain," Jorge joked. Suddenly, as they went through the intersection, the drivers side of the car was squarely hit with a huge impact. CRASH! TUESDAY 14TH AUGUST 2007 5:00am In an instant, The Mexican Devil was awake and sitting up in the king size bed. He was breathing very heavily and sweating profusely as his heart raced a mile a minute. Wiping the sweat away from his forehead with his right hand, El Diablo knew he wouldn’t be able to get back to sleep and decided to get up slowly out of the bed, as so not to disturb his wife, who was still sleeping very peacefully. "Why now?" Diablo muttered as he crawled out of the bed. Looking at the alarm clock, he could see that it was only 5am and that he had meant to sleep for four hours longer. Diablo crept carefully along the darkened carpet to gather some clothes together, as he had just decided to go for a walk to clear his head. TUESDAY 14TH AUGUST 2007 5:30am The city was bright for this hour of the day, with the sun risen, no clouds in the sky and the scent in the air was very pleasant, like that of wet grass, although it hadn’t rained. The Creator of Controversy had already left his home and was walking along the street he lived on at a very slow pace, like that of an old man attempting to walk home by using his walking stick. He was walking slow because he couldn’t get his vivid dream out of his mind. He often thought about his brother Jorge, but this was the first time in years that he had relived that night and all he could do was ponder why he was thinking about it now, as he really had no reason to be thinking about it at all. "What’s wrong with you Raul? You’re being successful, you don’t need this right now," he muttered to himself. Wearing his black hooded top, black denim jeans and black sneakers, El Diablo was exhausted as he slowly paced on the pavement of his block. His long black hair was a mess and as greasy as ever, and he hadn’t taken the time to shave so had a lot of dark bristly stubble covering his face. "It’s been nearly twenty years since it happened, why do I need to think about it now?" he questioned himself. Passing several parked cars, The Master of Manipulation turned back around to walk back slowly from the way he had just come from. "I hated that day and I always will," Diablo whispered to himself. Picking up from where his dream left off, El Diablo decided to finish the recollection of that fateful night. THURSDAY 29TH SEPTEMBER 1988 After the crash, it was just silence for several hours, until Raul’s eyes began to flutter open. "Where am I?" asked the youngster. However, there was no one around him to answer that question. Slowly gaining awareness of his surroundings, the 14 year old Raul could see he was under a blanket and in a bed that was not his own. It didn’t take him long to realise that he was in a hospital, although he wasn’t hooked up to any machines. Feeling a little woozy from being knocked unconscious, Raul pulled the blanket off of himself and soon saw that he several little cuts over his arms and legs and some bruising, but otherwise he seemed relatively uninjured. Lifting up the hospital robe that he had been put in, he noticed some stitches across his stomach where some debris must have hit him, but again he seemed perfectly fine and unhurt. "Where’s Jorge?" he asked aloud. Still light headed, Raul hadn’t realised that there wasn’t anyone around to answer him. He began to blink ferociously in an attempt to get himself wide awake, and after a while he began to stand up, albeit shakily, from the bed onto the cold tiled floor of the hospital cubicle he was in. Checking the back of his robe to make certain his ass wasn’t showing, Raul made tentative steps, like a young child would make when attempting to walk for the very first time, and headed towards the cubicle curtain. "I’ve got to find Jorge," he muttered. Yanking the blue curtain open, Raul looked out into the depths of the hospital to see where he could possibly find his brother, but had no idea where he could be as he had never been inside a hospital before. "Where is Jorge Diaz?" he asked in a raised voice. There weren’t many people in the corridor. Just an elderly male patient sitting in a wheel chair, looking ready for death to bestow him, an American woman with a broken nose, probably from too much partying and drug use, and finally a Doctor, who unbeknownst to Raul at the time was his doctor. "You’re looking for Jorge Diaz?" the Doctor responded. Nodding, to indicate he was looking for Jorge, Raul was growing more concerned by the minute. The Doctor was wearing a traditional long white coat, black pants and black shoes and had a very serious look on his face. "Raul, you may wish to sit down," he stated. Adamant that he wanted to stay standing up, Raul studied the Doctor intently, from the "Doctor Vela" nametag on his white coat to his piercing olive eyes and short slick black hair. Standing at only 5’2, Raul was easily dwarfed by the 5’9 163lbs Vela and could only hope that this self perceived giant would be able to give him some good news. "You’ve been unconscious for three hours and in that time we battled to save your brother, but he had too many internal injuries. I’m sorry," Vela said solemnly. Tears were welling up in Raul’s eyes as he received the news. After a few seconds of trying to remain calm and composed, he began to wail uncontrollably at the news that he was struggling to believe was real. "I can take you to him, if you’d like," remarked Doctor Vela. Raul simply nodded his head slowly as tears dripped down his young cheeks. "You can spend as much time as you like in there. Meanwhile we’re still trying to get hold of your parents," said Vela. This was the longest and toughest walk that Raul had ever had to take during his short life. It seemed to take an eternity to reach the small room where Jorge’s body was being kept, and as he was lead to the room all he could wonder was if it was his fault. If he hadn’t asked Jorge to take him to the event then this wouldn’t have happened. If he hadn’t been distracting Jorge by talking to him while he was driving then this wouldn’t have happened. Raul couldn’t help but blame himself. "Here we are," stated Doctor Vela as they reached the door to the room. Gulping and breathing heavily, Raul was nervous about going in. "Do you need anyone in there with you?" asked Doctor Vela. Shaking his head, Raul nervously pulled on the door handle and took a step in, carefully closing the door behind him. For a few moments Raul couldn’t bear to look up and had his eyes fixated on the brightly lit white tiles, thanks to the good lighting in the room. "I’ve got to do this," he whispered. Slowly and steadily Raul raised his head until he could see his brother Jorge’s lifeless body lying there on what looked like a cold slab. The injuries certainly looked significant, but Raul couldn’t bear to look any longer as this was not how he wanted to remember his brother. "This isn’t you Jorge," he told his brother. Sniffing and wiping away the tears that were still running down his cheeks, he turned away from him. "You’re no longer here, Jorge. The spark that made you is gone. I miss you, I love you and I will become a wrestler like we talked about," he remarked. TUESDAY 14TH AUGUST 2007 6:15am The Mexican Devil was still walking slowly up and down his block when he finished thinking about that day. A single tear drop was dribbling down his face as he pondered that horrible day. "It’s all for you, Jorge, it’s all for you," he silently mouthed. Sighing, El Diablo decided it was time to go back home to look at pictures of Jorge and cherish the memories they had, had together and to make sure the one remaining possession he had of Jorge’s was still safe.
