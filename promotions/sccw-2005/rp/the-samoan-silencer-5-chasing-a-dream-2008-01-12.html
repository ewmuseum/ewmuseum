'The Samoan Silencer' #5 - Chasing a Dream

12 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/12/08 Sometimes in life we forget what makes us whole. Sometimes we walk past what we have in an attempt to try and get what we want. Humans, by very nature are flawed in this way. A hero is no different than the rest of us. What drives a man to throw away everything in their lives? Why do the flawed and incomplete beings that we have become risk everything over what becomes nothing? Does that pot of gold really even exist at all or is it simply a dream that we chase from day to day, with some combatants in the daily battle fighting harder than others? Scars and the situations that bring those scars to us cut on our psyche like a knife on skin. We eventually miss the things we want and when opportunities to achieve them diminish…we diminish. Such a shame that it must eventually happen to the best of us. "The Samoan Silencer" #5 - Chasing a Dream In silence, Timo Bolamba awoke. It had been several days since the incident with The Phoenix, and he still was left trying to sort the details that brought him to this spot. As he sat up in bed, a longing overtook him, and he began to wonder why he was really doing all of this. Why would a man that was so richly decorated in what seemed like a past life now want to become a hired mercenary? Often he wondered these things. A certain obtuse mode of thought often accompanied the buzzing in his ears as he pondered over the information he had relegated to the back of his mind so many times. "So many years…gone" 3 years ago --------------------------- "You are done then? Finished?" The words flowed elegantly from the mouth of a beautiful woman. Mrs. Gwyneth Cross, the wife of Timo Bolamba, sat across from him in a satin bathrobe looking deeply for an answer in The Samoans eyes. He did his best to lie, never being able to admit he was through with anything in his life, much less his greatest dream. "I cannot go back, even if I wanted to Gwyn." He looks over to a mantle with a burned FSW World Championship Belt sitting upon it. "Dave Gibson saw to that." She sighs. "That isn’t really an answer and you know it." What could he say? She was right. In fact she was always right. A cold sweat flashed on Timo and he threw off the covers to his bed. He stood up to revel in the night air and hopefully make some sense of why his life had turned out this way. A few steps to a smallish nightstand and a bottle of scotch in hand later, and Timo was pouring himself a small glass of the premium liquor. One of the perks of his position with Dalton Grey was a nice selection of some of the finer things in life. He sipped at the glass a little, the ice cubes inside clanking upon the crystal as they tumbled over one another. With a bit of a flourish he turned and walked over to the window, leaning with one arm over the frame and another cupping the glass. A Month or Two After the Previous Flashback ------------------------------------------------------------- "You don’t understand me Gwyneth. You never have understood me or why I love what I do." Timo’s voice carried though the hallway. "This is a chance for me, something real! I can be a star again!" Gwyneth holds her head in her hands, tears occasionally escaping through the cracks. "That’s all you want? To be a star? What about a husband…a father!" She looks up, her mascara ruined and her golden hair mussed. "When will you understand that there are other things in life than wrestling?" A sad look crept across the face of The Samoan. "Gwyn…it’s all I’ve got now…it’s my only chance. Can’t you see that?" The pleading voice of the once powerful man now sounded grounded and without resonance. She stands up and looks down upon her husband. It had all finally come to this. No more fighting. No more making up. No more waiting on him after matches. No more hearing about the latest blood feud. No more injuries. No more frivolous spending on the account of eccentricity. No more. "Do you know what it’s like living with you?" She asks. "Every day it’s like "Oh here’s Timo, the guy everybody knows and loves, on another ZAAAANY adventure!" Why can’t you do normal things with us as a family?" She looks at him as he hangs his head. "Why does life always have to be about the next title shot? Why does it have to be about your cars and parties and suits and everything else in excess? When I met you Timo, you were something. You were a local club wrestler who worked shows in run down gymnasiums for high school kids. You didn’t have a dime to your name and a ratty old Trans Am…but at least you had heart…guts…determination. Now look at you." She stalks around him, much like a cat to a mouse. "For all your money and fame and star power you have naught but a hole in your pocket and enemies who have sabotaged your entire career." She reaches down, grabbing his chin and forcing the much larger man to look her in the eyes. "Is that truly who you are now? Have you fallen so far into this hole of decadence that even common sense makes no sense to you? Well?" The Samoan pulled his head away. "I told you that you never understood me Gwyneth." She turned away. "I’m afraid that I’ve finally realized I never will." The Samoan Silencer watched as the night wore on through his small window. Not long ago the life he led was different. It’s true he had everything at his fingertips and a way to constantly get more. For all the posturing and placating he had tried in order to come to grips with his life and the people therewith, he had become more like the enemies he had sworn to fight than he realized. For every dollar he spent, he sunk more into the world of Tyler Nelson. For every time he allowed his sense to be overcome by wanting and desire he flocked to the side of Kremmen. When his prideful nature overwhelmed him and eventually subsided into ego…he became Jason Snow. Worst of all…the revenge. He knew deep down why he hated Dave Gibson so much. It did not deal with the injury, or the belt, or even the acts of indescribable cruelty that the man suffered onto the Samoan. It was the mindset of revenge that he had bestowed onto him. It was growing cold now, and the air was definitely thick with the frosty cold snap that was sweeping through Motor City recently. It would be winter soon, and that meant that he had to be doubly cautious in his new line of work. Pausing briefly to think about what he had been doing in the last few weeks, he again allowed his mind to drift to thoughts of The Phoenix and what he had been told. How was one girl going to throw everything into upheaval? How did it pertain to Dalton? He walked to his closet and opened the door, revealing his crime fighting garb. He had made a few modifications to the old costume since his last ordeal, adding in a couple gadgets to help him in the fight. Thanks to Dalton, not only did he have access to intelligence, but also to the Police supply room. Kevlar armor, lexan shields, and non-lethal armament were abounding. So he took it upon himself to fabricate a few items for later use. The world had changed again, and though it seemed ages ago, it really didn’t seem right to put on the clothes and be the man he once was. Then again, simply being that man wasn’t easy, though for anyone who watched him fight, they might never know why. The Driveway, The Night of The Final Argument --------------------------------------------------------------- Gwyneth looked up, tears staining her cheeks still. Blackish grey ichor finding paths to descend as the small drops fell. She wished for all the world that this day never would come, yet somehow knew it would long before it arrived. "I love you Gwyneth. I always will." Timo’s broken note hit her like a force of nature, but did not dissuade her from the path she had chosen. Maybe some day things could be different and she could learn to see his ways, but now was not that time. "If you have to leave me…I understand…but at least tell me you love me before you go." His voice cracked slightly, wavering and waiting on her words. She wipes a tear away from her eyes and starts the Jeep that he had bought for her when they first met. In the back, nestled into car seats were two small twin babies. A long drawn out sigh is met with the only response she can now give. "I’m sorry Timo…but I told you on the day we met that I would never lie to you…and I can‘t break that promise." So he watched as his wife drove away. He watched until the tail lights of the SUV turned just down the road and vanished from his life. He watched long after the rain came and began to fall on his head, covering him in the tears from heaven itself. Timo Bolamba watched the only woman he had ever truly loved leave him. Finally, in his driveway on a rain soaked night miles away from any wrestling ring, Gwyneth Cross finished the job that Dave Gibson had started. The Samoan Silencer was dead, and only Timo Bolamba remained.
