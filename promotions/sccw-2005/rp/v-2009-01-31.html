Date: 01/31/2009 "Who am I to judge and strike you down?" -Maynard James Keenan, ‘Pushit’ by Tool V 12/22/2008 Inside my studio apartment sitting on the couch watching some news story about political corruption involving Governor Blagojevich of Illinois, things are not going through my head as clearly as they normally do. My thoughts are clouded by what is to come, blinded by the thoughts of Shakespearean romance and countless nights of brutal violence that have become a normality for me. However, this is different. This is something I’m not used to. I’m really nervous about tonight. I can’t stop thinking about tonight. Going to pick up Cat Lady and go out on a date with her. It’s going to be weird seeing her without her mask. Hopefully her ass still looks as good. Oh great. Still thinking about her ass when I should be getting ready. In fact I don’t even know her address. FUCK! Didn’t think of that until just now. Better call her before it gets too late. The gala is in four hours and I’m sitting here thinking about my date’s ass. Even though she is fine, I’d better start getting ready for the date starting by calling her to get the address. My hands start to shake as I reach into my pocket to pull out my cellphone. Criminals armed with deadly weapons don’t even do this to me. Little old me afraid to call a girl. Opening up my phone, I start dialing her number. After doing so, I wait for her to pick up while some crappy pop song is playing while I wait on the line. This song makes me want to blow my brains out. Luckily before I decide to give myself the killing blow, she picks up the phone. "Hello," I say into the phone. "It’s Judge." "Hi," she says back. "Cat Lady at your service." Every time she talks my heart skips a beat. "Ready for tonight?" "Yeah, I’m getting ready right now. Just fixing my dress up now." "That’s good. I’m getting my tux ready right now too." Bold-faced lie I just told her, but I don’t care. Anything to keep her talking to hear her sweet voice over the phone. "Nice, sweetie." "Yeah…anyway, can I get your address so I know where to pick you up?" I almost blew it there. Forgetting what to say like a common imbecile. What an idiot I am. "Sure." Reaching over to the coffee table I pick up a piece of paper and grab a pen to write with. "Ok, give me the address sweetie." She giggles. Must’ve been the sweetie remark. "My address is 2785 Hilcrest Drive, North Las Vegas. It’s just off of Tropical and Decatur." "Ah, ok. You’re in my area. I live off of Rancho and Craig." "That’s good to hear." "I know. I’ll make a map so I get to the right place just to be safe." "What time are you picking me up?" I have to think about that for a moment. Have to take into account the drive time, the start time of the gala, and how long it’ll take to get myself ready. "Probably around six fifteen." "Alright. Look forward to seeing you, Judge. My name is Amanda." "Amanda…I like that name. My name is Ash." "Ash? Like from Evil Dead?" Her hotness level goes up ten points just with that one sentence. "Yeah, like from Evil Dead. Ash is also the name of the murderous robot from the movie Alien. I have a lot to live up to." "I think I know who you are now. I was looking up the artists for the gallery and saw one named Ash Plato on the gallery’s website. Is that you?" This makes me smile. "Yes, that’s me." "Good because the other artists besides you either looked too strange or two fruity. You were the only halfway normal looking one on the site." This makes me really happy to hear. At least she thinks I’m attractive. That’s a good start for me. "That’s good to hear. At lest you don’t think I’m some artist emo-freak." She giggles through the speaker. "A lot of emo guys hit on me. I don’t like them. They’re almost too sensitive." Emo guys. I fucking hate emo guys who wear girls’ pants and act like little posers just to have a sense that they are cool when in reality they are merely selling themselves out. "And they’re pussies with that stupid crap music they listen to." "Yeah, that’s true but some of it is pretty good." "True, just not the crap they play fifty times on the radio." "Yeah." I can see that I’m losing her at this point so I’d better wrap this up before she changes her mind. "Ok, I’m going to let you go so we can get ready for tonight." "Alright sweetie. See you at seven." "See you then." I hang up the phone and put it back in my pocket before staring at wall again. I’m scared to death of a date. I can’t figure it out. However I’d better get my act together. The date is in only a few hours and I’m still in shambles. If I fuck up tonight, it’ll be my ass. Everything has to be right, starting with myself. I look like shit. * * * Ash Journal, 12/22/2008: Tonight is either going to go great or be a colossal fuck-up on the level of the Titanic hitting an iceberg. I’m leaving in about twenty minutes to go pick up Amanda. I just hope to God I don’t fuck this up like I did every relationship I had before I met Jessica. Boy was I a screw up. I could find ways to screw up even the most perfect, harmonious relationship by just being me. This one time I was dating a woman named Cheryl. I didn’t want to sleep with her because I thought she was a virgin. Turns out she was a raging nymphomaniac and was fucking every guy in town. Good thing I didn’t do it to her. I might’ve caught an STD or worse. That might’ve been the best non-fuck of my life. My relationships before that were even stormier. And then I met two angels. One named Jessica, who was taken from me before we could wed. The second one named Amanda who is a costumed heroine dressed to kill and just as foxy as she is deadly. Two angels, one of love and one of grace. Never has a man been so lucky as I this past year. In other words, if I fuck up tonight, I lose an angel. I’d better not screw up. If I do, I will hate myself forever. This is it, no going back now. Time to go pick her up and hope luck is on my side. * * * 7:18 pm The night is as cold as a dead corpse as I pull up to Amanda’s house in my gold colored 1998 Saturn SL I and park it in front on the street. In the driveway is her VW Beetle. So I put the car in park and turn off the ignition. My suit is feeling all sweaty now. The nervousness has taken me over. I haven’t felt this nervous since the first time I judged a man. Now I’m getting out of a car and about to go on a date that I am unprepared for. At least I’m dressed up. However, for some reason I feel like a mob lawyer. I got her a corsage to wear too. Such sentimentality is not my style. But this is different. This is love, this is lust, this is passion. This is good old fashioned romance at its finest. Walking up to the front door I almost have a panic attack. Once I’m there I reach over and press the door bell. A few moments later Amanda opens the door and looks absolutely stunning. Her hair is down and is gorgeously style. She smells like passion fruits. Her eyes glisten like diamonds on a ring. Her black silk dress shimmers in the light. Her lips are as red as freshly bloomed roses. The perfect woman. "Hi Ash," she says with a smile. "Wow," is all I can reply. "You look beautiful." This comment makes her blush. "Aw, you’re so sweet." Ironically that comment makes me blush. "Thank you. I got you a corsage." I hand her the corsage I bought for her. "IT’S BEAUTIFUL!" She then reaches over and hugs me, before kissing me on the cheek. She then lets go and starts to put the corsage on. My heart is really racing at this point. Hopefully I don’t have a heart attack before we get there. "You like it?" She looks down at it smiling and then looks back up at me still smiling. "I love it." "You ready?" She nods her head. "Yes, just let me get my coat." She then walks over to the coat closet and opens it up. Reaching inside she grabs out a black coat to go along with her black dress. Putting it on, she closes the coat closet before coming out of the house. Locking the door behind her, I walk her to the car and open up the door for her. She gets in the passenger’s seat and closes the door. I then go around and get back in the driver’s seat. Closing the door behind me, I put the key in the ignition and start the car up wondering if this was all just a dream or if this was really happening. * * * 8:00 pm We’ve just arrived at the gala and walk inside to see everyone either casually talking or dancing in the middle of the gallery. Other random people are looking at the artwork like some Andy Warhol viewing party from the sixties. As I think this, Amanda goes and hangs her coat up before rejoining me to avoid talking to some sleazy looking guy that’s lurking in near the entrance smoking a Cuban cigar. All of the people here are well dressed but to me seem like pretentious snobs. I hate these people who are all snobbish about everything. They overanalyze every piece of art they look at. I can’t stand these people. However, the fact that I have the most beautiful woman in the world by my side makes it all the worthwhile. She smells like tropical fruits. Her hair shimmers in the night. He skin feels as soft as anything I’ve ever touched in my life. Her smile is like a thousand suns shining down on me from the heavens. Her eyes shine like diamonds. She is an angel sent from heaven. Ironically she fell into the arms of a lost soul such as myself. However, for once, I am happy. I’d better enjoy it now because just like all things, it’ll never last. I take her hand and we head for the dance floor hoping to never wake up from this lovely dream I’ve found myself in. * * * An hour later… After having the greatest time of my life, Amanda and I decide to take a break from dancing to go get something to drink. Hopefully all of the drinks are alcoholic instead of that non-alcoholic bullshit they usually serve at these functions. My best friend and agent Todd is around somewhere but I haven’t seen him for about thirty minutes. I stop thinking about him and go back to thinking about what I’m going to drink. I need something with a kick instead of some low alcohol filled garbage drink they sell at a sit down restaurant. Those types of drinks are for pussies and kids. Luckily I don’t fit into either category. As we walk over to the table with all the drinks on it in the back of the room, suddenly a loud crashing noise comes from the entrance. I look over and see Rick Roll coming into the gallery with his pirate goons as well as some idiot in a Hamburglar costume. He looks around at the frightened crowd. I can almost hear him laughing from the back of the room. I want to do something but I know if I do, my secret identity might be revealed. I must refrain from doing anything unless provoked. "HELLO!" Rick Roll exclaims as he walks into the gala with the Hamburglar and some goons who are wearing pirate costumes. Everyone at the gala parts like Red Sea as he walks through the crowd to the center of the room. He looks around at them for a moment, probably smiling under that mask as his. "And good evening to you all." Rick Roll looks around the room before focusing on me and for a split second. He then moves around and is clearly looking for someone. Rick Roll then goes into the next room where no one can see. A few moments later the pirates come in carrying my agent and best friend Todd into the room! He’s all tied up and has tape over his mouth. A card is dropped on the floor by one of the pirates. Looks like he did that on purpose. Rick Roll is nowhere to be seen. "Now we have a hostage," the Hamburglar says. "Let’s go, men." Hamburglar and the pirate goons he came with quickly head out the exit as I turn to Amanda. "You ok?" I ask her as we both try to get over the shock of what just happened. "Yeah, I’m fine," she replies, nodding her head. Looking around at the chaos all around us, we then look at each and are clearly thinking the same thing. "We need back-up." "Right," she replies. We sneak over to where the pirate dropped the card on the ground and I reach down to pick it up, faking that I’m tying my shoe to do so. After finishing my acting joke, I get up and quietly sneak out the back with Amanda after she‘s grabbed her coat. We head to my car as fast as we can. We know what to do. We have to get the rest of the Rat Pack together and save the day. I open up the doors with my clicker and we get inside the car. Before we drive off, I look down at the card to see what it says. Western Industrial Enterprises It even has an address. Now I know where to find Rick Roll and my best friend he’s holding hostage. This bastard dies tonight and it will be by my hand. * * * An hour later… Inside the Astronomer’s pad which doubles as the base of operations for myself and the rest of the Rat Pack, I’ve just finished changing into my Judge costume and have now emerged from the bathroom with my bag to find a hot debate about what to going on between the rest of the team. "We don’t kill people!" the Astronomer yells at Abrams. "We should after this shit!" Abrams exclaims. "Cat Lady and Judge could’ve been killed by that maniac and his goons and you just want to slap him on the wrist like some Kindergartener?" "No I don’t but killing is not an option." As they argue, I pull a handgun out of my bag and load a new magazine clip into it. Everyone stops arguing and looks over. "Killing is always an option," I reply in a gloomy tone to get my point across. "The only way to get people to take notice anymore is threats of death and actual killing. They aren’t even afraid of guns anymore." "Why do you have such a morbid view on society?" The Astronomer asks. "Did somebody not love you when you were a kid or something?" At this point Cat Lady emerges from the back bedroom in her costume. Amanda looks stunning. She really lights my fire whenever I lay eyes on her. "No. I just see the funny truth of it all." "And what truth is that?" "I see that this world is going to shit and there’s nothing any of us can do, costumes or not. This is the end times, people. I’ve seen it first hand. Death and life are one and the same. Every day is exactly the same, just like Nine Inch Nails said. The hard times are back, the old days back when people lynched and beheaded sinners and criminals in the streets. They’re back and there’s nothing anyone can do to change society back to the civility it once had. Civility is dead. The only way to restore order is to kill the criminals who commit the most violent of crimes one by one until there are none left. Then there will be a civil society left for everyone else." "You’re a maniac," Captain Velocity replies, a bit disgusted by my comments.. I turn and give him a look. "And you’re dressed like one of the Bananas in Pajamas with that outfit." "What the hell does that mean?" It seems like I’ve offended him. If I have, I honestly could care less. I have a point to make and none of them seem to get it. "DON’T YOU PEOPLE GET IT?! This isn’t some comic book or a television show. This is real life with people pointing guns at your head and trying to kill you! I’ve almost been killed about ten times already. Life and death are one and the same. You either need to learn to kill or you’re going to be fucked in the long run. Now you can all make up your minds while I go and rescue my best friend." I pick up my bag again and head out the door as none of them say a word. I know where the hell I’m going. If they want to continue to argue and pander like school children, then they can be my guest. However I have more important things to deal with like rescuing my best friend. * * * Approximately twenty four minutes later… Upon arriving at the Western Industrial Industries warehouse, I park my black Toyota Supra in front of the warehouse and get out of the car closing the door behind me. The night is pitch black. It’s almost as if the veil of death has covered the city like a black sackcloth. My footsteps are the only things I hear under the moonlight. In the distance a gun shot is fired. Normally I would go investigate but not tonight. Tonight is different. My best friend is in trouble, captured by a madman in a mask and his possibly mentally challenged sidekick. Tonight the old days of Vegas will be brought back. The killing days back when the mob would make their enemies sleep with the fishes. Those days are back and I’m going to enjoy this judgment. Outside the warehouse are two pirate patrols strolling about. I have to make it quiet before I go inside and make a bloody kill. The streets will be flowing with crimson tonight. That much is certain. Pulling out my knife I sneak over to the first two pirates who are strolling about. Sneaking up on them, I make the kills fast and quiet. The first one I stab in the neck, slashing his vocal cords. He dies in an instant. The second one I run over to and chop him in the side of the neck, which sends him to the ground in a heap. After doing this, I walk over to the entrance of the fading, decaying façade of Western Industrial Enterprises and go inside to find my best friend. Once inside I find that the warehouse is empty and filled with boxes. The dim lighting gives it an eerie feeling to it. Nothing but boxes shrouded in the darkness. But somewhere in here is my best friend Todd Wilkinson and he’s being held by the madman known as Rick Roll. The mere fact of this only drives me to succeed. I have to find Todd before the maniac kills him! Overhead the dim lights swing from the air conditioning. Dust kicks up off the floor as I wander deeper into the giant warehouse until I’m in the direct middle of it. There I find what I’m looking for. Todd tied up sitting on a steel chair. His mouth is gagged with a cloth that’s tied around his head. Running over, I quickly untie him and pull down the cloth from his mouth. "You ok, Todd?" I ask as I look around. "Yeah, yeah," he replies. "I’m ok." "Where’s he at?" "Who?" "Rick Roll, the man who captured you?" I look around again but see nothing. Just then something hits me on the head and I go dark. * * * At the same time… The Rat Pack is still inside the Astronomer’s pad arguing over their official "No Kill Policy" that the Judge has declared his distaste for before leaving to go rescue his best friend from the psychopathic maniac known as Rick Roll. Half of the team agrees with the policy while the other half agrees with Judge’s pro-killing stance to fight crime. The two sides are standing across from one another now. Sitting on the couch are the pro-life heroes Captain Velocity, Lady Bug, and Mustang. Standing up by the television are the pro-kill heroes Abrams, Bailiff, and Cat Lady, who just happens to be Judge’s new girlfriend. The Astronomer is remaining neutral and trying to keep the peace since he agrees with parts of each side’s arguments and reasoning. The eighth member of the group, Vagrant, is rummaging through the garbage in the kitchen looking for food so he doesn’t really give a shit about the debate. "HE IS A PSYCHOTIC LUNATIC!" Captain Velocity exclaims. "He wants us to kill people and you guys are actually siding with him? It makes us no worse than the criminals we are trying to capture." The gigantic Abrams nods his head. "Damn right," he replies. "Judge has a point about this world going to shit. Maybe he’s the one ho knows what the hell is going on and we’ve got it wrong." "Don’t tell me you’re seriously considering killing criminals now?" "Yes I am. Judge has the criminals in this town shitting their pants like high school kids before they take the SAT’s. That takes a lot to do. In a city as bad as this one, that takes a lot to do. The criminals are hesitating now more than in the past thanks to him. He inspired us to do what we do. Maybe we should follow his example more thoroughly if you catch my drift." "You’re mad. You really think more bloodshed and violence will make things better? It’ll only make the city worse than it was before he started going on his little adventures." The debate stops for a moment as the police scanner in the living room starts to broadcast a call. ‘We have a 242 at 2358 North Hollywood.’ They ignore the call since it’s only battery and nothing more severe before going back to their arguing. "He’s got a point," Bailiff says to Captain Velocity. "As much as you hate to admit it, Judge is right about the killing. He wants to end all of this "You guys are nuts," Captain Velocity responds. "I swear, this city is becoming Dante’s sixth level of Hell and no one seems to see that this is the reality. If we become vigilantes, then we will only be a step up from the criminals. Can’t you people see this?" Abrams gives him a look after hearing this comment. "What do you mean by you people?" he asks with a raised eyebrow. "This isn’t time for jokes, Abrams," Cat Lady replies. "What? I’m not joking. Not joking around at all." "It’s from Tropic Thunder, doofus. You can drop the act." He looks sad for a moment before the debate rages yet again between the two sections of the team. "Killing is wrong!" Lady Bug yells out. "The Bible says…" "The Bible says?" Bailiff replies in a mocking fashion. "This isn’t Sunday school, sister. This is the difference between living and dying. If some gang banger has a gun to a baby’s head and I have a shot to kill him, I’m going to kill the bastard to save the baby. That was the scenario Judge told me yesterday that led to his first kill. In my opinion, he made the right choice." "DON’T MOCK THE BIBLE YOU ATHEIST FIEND!" This pisses him off more than anything he’s ever heard in his life. "I’M NOT AN ATHEIST YOU STUPID ROACH! I’M A BUDDHIST!" Just then the police scanner starts sending another call out to the officers on duty. "Attention all units. We have a multiple 187 in progress in Henderson at the Galleria Mall parking lot. The suspects are the criminals known as The Exorcist, Pilgrim, and Hamburglar. Orders are to exceed with extreme caution. Excessive force has been approved." At that moment the debate stops. Everyone in the Rat Pack quickly heads out the door for their cars. The debate can wait. All that matters is that they are needed by the citizens of Las Vegas and nothing will stop them from performing their civic duty. * * * Approximately one hour later… Waking up I see Rick Roll standing in front of me. I’m tied up to a chair, too. My hands are tied behind my back and my ankles are cuffed. "Why hello, Judge," he says with a chuckle under his breath. "YOU BASTARD!" I exclaim in the highest pitch of my voice. "WHERE’S TODD? IF YOU HURT HIM YOU’RE GONNA FUCKING PAY!" Rick Roll starts laughing. "Nope, you’re wrong. I didn’t hurt Todd." He then pulls off his mask and my jaw almost drops to the floor in shock over what he reveals to me. "I am Todd." There standing before me is my best friend Todd Wilkinson, a man I’ve known for years. My best friend, in fact. The closest person to me in the entire world. And the truth is more shocking than anything I could ever imagine. Todd wasn’t captured by Rick Roll. Todd IS Rick Roll. And all of this, this elaborate scheme, was concocted so he could capture me. It was a trap all along and I fell for it hook, line, and sinker. He’s probably been planning this for weeks. The uneasy silence is ended by his laughter as he removes my mask. "I knew it was you all along, Ash," he continues before starting to pace around like an animal caged in a zoo. "I found your little costume a few months ago. I was in your apartment putting my jacket up when I came upon a box with a little black costume in it. Judge’s costume, in fact. I KNEW YOU WERE FULL OF SHIT ASH! I KNEW IT! All those nights you were making art were spent going around fighting criminals like some little comic book superhero pansy. That’s when I decided to pull the Ultimate Rick Roll on you and the rest of this city. That‘s when I decided to take action. I had to show you the error of your ways. See you‘re on the wrong side. The criminals are still in charge and you‘re going to be judged not by my gun in a little game of Russian Roulette but instead by how well you trained your friends." "You think this is all a game, Todd?" "No you idiot!" He then smashes me in the face with the butt of his handgun. Blood starts to come down my lip like a stream formed by heavy rainfall. This of course makes a big smile come across his face. This man is no longer Todd Wilkinson. He is now Rick Roll and has been for some time. The man I knew died long ago. What a sad thought this is to me. However he continues talking and making me more depressed. "This is about MORE than just meager little children’s games. This is about showing society just how fucked up it really is! My plan is simple. Capture you and lure all the rest of your little Rat Pack here including your little girlfriend. Then we’ll kill all of them to show this city and the rest of the world that society has no true heroes left. Disheartened, everything will fall apart. The very fabric of civilization will unravel because of something as simple as a Rick Roll. I‘m going to show the people that all their heroes are dead. Only then will they realize the true power the criminals have over them." I can’t believe what I’m hearing. "You’re crazy." "Am I? Or am I simply the sanest man alive?" I shake my head. "No, you’re crazy." "We’ll see about that. When I’m raping and killing your girlfriend in front of your eyes right before I kill you, then you’ll see the beauty of it." Suddenly a wave of rage builds up inside of me. The dragon deep inside my soul is unleashed and I let out all my fury like an erupting volcano as I think of the very thought of him touching Amanda as I yell out in anger. "YOU HURT HER AND I’LL FUCKING RIP YOUR HEART OUT AND SHOVE IT UP YOUR ASS!" I stop to catch my breath only to notice that he isn’t even bothered in the slightest bit by what I just said. In fact, he’s rather nonchalant about it. "Lovely. However, you don’t grasp my point." "And what point is that?" He cackles a little bit before he says his piece which annoys me a little bit to be honest about it. "That the beauty of it is that no matter what happens, the end will still be the same as it is in every life. It ends in death." He smiles that same sick smile again before putting his mask back on. Just then Pilgrim and the Exorcist walk in with the Hamburglar coming in right behind them. Now I’m the bait on the rat trap and my friends are the intended prey of these fiendish predators. My only hope is that they survive the hunt. * * * "The night is darkest just before the dawn. And I promise you…the dawn is coming." Harvey Dent, The Dark Knight
