Pieces of the Puzzle

12 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/12/08 I Present Day: Desert Springs Randall Williams was a respected man in the state of Nevada, a decorated officer and a dedicated husband. He had also been a patient of resuscitation medicine. Two months ago, he and his wife had been in a car crash, when a drunken driver of a big rig had driven them off the road, causing he and his wife Debra to drive off a cliff and crash into a river below. After twenty minutes paramedics and fellow officers arrived on scene, it was reported then that Williams was unresponsive to first aid and CPR, his wife was suffering from near hypothermia. The two were transferred to the Desert Springs Hospital Medical Center, where Debra was treated for mild hypothermia and shock. Randall was transferred to a special unit in the facility where on call physician Dr. Andrew Morgan and his team began their work. Since the near death of Morgan’s son four years previous, the Doctor had been unable to successfully resuscitate any of his patients. Randall Williams however, got lucky. It was because of Dr. Morgan’s unrelenting will that Randall was able to return from the other side, it is also because of this that Randall personally took up the investigation into Dr. Morgan’s homicide. As Williams entered the Desert Springs police department, the local enforcers eyed him carefully. Since 2001 the Highway Patrol (or State Police) had very little interaction with individual law enforcements, so when Randall Williams entered the station many of the officers became suspicious. Williams understood their circumspection, but that didn’t change his convictions. Dr. Morgan had saved his life, and as far as he was concerned he owed the man some justice. "What are you doing here Williams?" A voice questions from behind. Randall stops where he stands and slowly turns to face his interrogator. The man across from him is just pushing six foot, which is two inches shorter then he. With a round belly and handlebar mustache the man appears more of a joke then an officer of the law. "Chief Harrison." Randall replies respectfully. "I came by to see how the investigation was coming, I’d like to offer my support…" "My men have it covered." Harrison states cutting him off, as the officers positioned around the station stop what they are doing to watch the exchange. "Well I just thought we could pool our resources…" Randall begins to protest. "Nybern and Ramirez have things more than covered." Harrison replies, adding more emphasis this time around. "So then do you have any suspects?" Randall questions. "We have a few leads." Harrison comments, but Randall could tell he was lying. By now additional officers have begun to take notice. "Nybern and Ramirez are combing through all the details, and I’m pretty confident we’ll have this case solved in no time." "Well if any help is needed…" "As I’ve already told you, my department has this case under control." Harrison retorts shortly. "Now I know you may have been the first officer on the scene, but my department is better suited for this case then a crossing guard wouldn’t you say?" The room fills with snickers, but Randall pays them no mind. "Very well then." Quietly Randall pushes passed Harrison and leaves the station, he wasn’t going to give up just yet but he knew this was a dead end. Somewhere out there the great doctor’s murderer remains, and he alone would be the man to find him. It was his destiny. II Near Future: White Plains Paul Samson sits in a wooden chair in the Nguyen basement with his arms tethered together. The ever so seductive Cleopatra rises from her slumber, and her eyes fixate on the bound body of Mr. Muscle. Slowly she crosses the basement, rubbing her slender body against his rippled form. Her breasts lay heavily across his bicep. "Paul… darling," She smiles, her words filled with an insincere longing. "You do know what today is don’t you?" "Don’t touch me," he replies his response thickened with disparagement. "I do know what today is," he replies smiling before spitting directly into her face. "It’s the day you die." Rearing back she slaps him across his face leaving the beginnings of a welt on his left cheek. "You insolent prick," she hisses, "You do not want to play games with me Paul." After a moments hesitation her seductive tone returns. "Why don’t you feel the right side of your neck?" She smiles, taunting him with her gaze. She watches as he struggles to feel his neck, when he does so his eyes fill with hatred. "What the hell did you do to me?" Hysterically he begins to struggle against the bonds, but even they are too tough for the strength of Samson. "I just drained you a little; I was hungry." She replies pretending to pout, "Don’t worry you will be fine, I couldn’t kill you…" slowly the pout turns into a smile, "not yet anyway." Her cackle fills the confines of the basement as she turns on her heels. With her back to her captive she adds, "I want you to see what I am going to do to my darling sister." With that being said the demoness of destruction follows the stairs up into the main floor of the house, leaving Paul to contemplate all the things she will do to her better half. Slowly she makes her way through the main floor to the main stairwell, ascending to the second floor where her demon counterpart Vassago continues his vigilant guard of the bedroom. "Vassago, you have not slept." She comments, causing the blue-eyed beast to meet her gaze. "I don’t need it." He replies calmly. The left corner of her mouth curls up into a grin as her eyes narrow. She steps into him, pressing her bosom into his chest, she tilts her head back so that her gaze can meet his and softly she kisses his lips. The kiss lasts but only a moment, before she pulls back from him, the warmth of his breath showering her neck. "Is Devlin around?" "No." The beast of a man replies. "And soon he won’t matter anyway." Coyly she smiles, "You mean…" "Yes." He answers for her. "The dark lord requires it." III Present Day: Desert Springs Sitting in his Honda Civic, Officer Randall Williams prepares himself for what lay ahead. "There’s no point in turning back now." He mumbles to himself, as he steps out of the driver’s side, shutting the door quietly behind him. Slowly he makes his way up the cobblestone walkway toward the front door, pushing the yellow tape up he ducks under and enters the residence. The murderer took great care in destroying everything around the house, carefully Williams stepped over broken picture frames and strewn pieces of what were once finely made furniture. When first arriving on the scene he had not expected to find such disarray, but tonight he already knew what to expect. As a result, this search would be much easier. Removing a flashlight from his pocket he twists the head shining light into his surroundings. Though the electricity still worked, he didn’t want to risk a passerby taking notice of his intrusion, the last thing he needed was Harrison on his case anymore then he already was. When he first searched the house his interest was in finding Dr. Morgan, tonight however there would be no body to find, instead he was searching for some clue that had been overlooked. Crouching close to the floor his leather gloved had sifted through the debris; he found a photograph of Andrew’s family; his wife and daughter smiling widely which was a polar opposite to young Simon’s expressionless face. Something in the child’s eyes didn’t sit well with him but the young man was a teenager at the time and even Randall knew that teenagers often held contempt for the world. Along with the photograph he found something that had not been noticed the first time in the house. A small leather bound journal, curious he flipped through the pages positioning his light to better read what was written. Mostly the journal contained thoughts on surgeries, or memories of his lost family. Randall was about to put the journal down when something told him to read a little further, whether it be instinct or just dumb curiosity he chose to look for the last entry. When he found the final entry, he something clicked. In almost all of the entries not a single one referenced Andrew’s son Simon, until the one dated on the day of Dr. Morgan’s death. After four years I have finally come to the awful conclusion that Simon was in fact the one who murdered Allison and Andrea. I tried for so long to ignore all the signs, but they were always there. Much like my father before me, I believe the madness finally got to him and I, the inattentive father that I was never realized it. It is because of my own inadequacies as a husband, a father and a man that I was unable to accept this. My only comfort is knowing that when I die and go to hell that I will again someday meet up with Simon, and there in the confines of eternal pain and suffering will I be able to atone for my mistakes. Alas however, it will be too late, and Simon will have won… Upon finishing the entry Randall returns the journal to the floor, and once again picks up the photograph. Young Simon’s eyes seem to penetrate his soul. Unable to look at the photo any further he drops that too the floor and rises to his feet. It wasn’t much of a lead to go on but deep down he knew that Simon Morgan held the key to everything. As he entered the house the same way he came, Randall had already made a decision. Harrison and his men could find their own leads, this one he intended to follow up with on his own.
