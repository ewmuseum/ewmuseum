Blueberries On Demand

August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August, 2008 'And then that’s when Jonathan Rhine asked me to join The Highwaymen.' It had been a while since Lawrence Spiderman had endured King Blueberry and his companions face-to-face. Unfortunately for Mr. Spiderman, it’s never long enough, especially when forced to sit through what would best be described as an obvious fabrication coming from the king, and what at worst would be called a complete dog-shit lie. 'Unfortunately, I had to decline the offer. I told him ‘A king serves but one country, and mine is that of all Sin City.’ He looked really heartbroken.' Blueberry Dragon, silent until now, could hold back no longer. 'Dude,' he said, sounding rather indignant, 'that’s not at all what happened.' 'Uh, yes it is.' 'No. No, it most certainly was not.' Spiderman, more out of an overwhelming desire to end the conversation than it was an interest in the story at hand, decided that now would be a good time to help move things along. 'Okay,' the frustration was poorly masked, 'so what did happen?' Dragon chuckled. 'The king here,' his voiced thinly laced with sarcasm, 'he ran away. Made a literature joke, to the surprise of everyone in the universe, and then ran down the hall.' Spiderman snorted. He didn’t want to laugh, he really didn’t, but the thought of his fool client running like a frightened child gave him a bit of satisfaction. King Blueberry, obviously defeated, would not relent. 'Well,' he huffed, 'that’s not how we’ll be remembering it!' Days after a big event always seemed boring, uneventful, like late afternoon on Christmas. The presents have already been opened, the shine has already started to wear, and all the anticipation that is built over days takes only a moment to dissipate. For King Blueberry, this was a time to rest and recover. Two matches in one night, one of them considerably more violent than the other, had taken a toll. The first night sleep came easy, a crash made possible by sheer physical exhaustion. The second night was always different, harder. This was in no small part due to the aching that started to set in around midday. More often than not, a few aspirin handled the pain during the afternoon and a few Tylenol PMs would do the trick at night, but more often than not King Blueberry wasn’t using his body as a wrecking ball. He was fairly sure that he’d tweaked his shoulder when he hit the end of his rope, before gravity took over and forced a swing. He was also fairly sure that his sinuses were a little off from when Wyatt Connors had made it a point to introduce them to the floor not once, but twice. None of that was keeping him awake. As sore as he might be, his problems stemmed from not being able to throw a switch and turn off his mind. Though it was 4:18am, and he’d been lying in bed for the better part of 4 hours, King Blueberry would describe himself as being 'functionally awake.' 'If we’re done with the pointing and the laughing, do you think maybe we could talk about that thing I came in here for?' Spiderman’s laughter ended in sigh. Still grinning, he nodded his head before reaching into the second drawer on the right side of his desk, retrieving from it a manila folder. 'So I do have some news about getting you some of that television time you wanted for, what did you call it? A ‘promo’?' 'You got it, chief.' 'And, just so we’re on the same page with this one, what exactly are you looking to accomplish with this?' 'Well, I guess it’s more of a comfort-zone thing than anything else. It’s what I used to do, so it’s what I’m used to.' 'We used to be part of an organization that had a whole channel dedicated to this,' added Dragon, a faint tinge of nostalgia lingering in his voice, 'All day long it would be folks finding new and usual ways to scream at each other. Personally, I never found much use for it, though it probably helped attract fans.' Spiderman was thumbed through the papers in the folder, removing two of them and setting them aside. 'My only concern is that it might come across as an imitation. I know that at least one of your associates is in the process of acquiring a distribution deal for a reality show, though I don’t believe any of the documents I read hinted at a title.' 'Oh, well, I don’t need anything like that. Hell, I don’t know what I’d fill 30 minutes with.' This was, of course, a complete fabrication. No sooner did the words escape his lips did King Blueberry begin to imagine a grand battle of epic proportions between a legion of flaming zombies and the robo-Vikings of southern Iceland. For the record, robo-Vikings trump zombies, even if they are on fire. Cybernetic Abraham Lincoln with Nunchucks, however, trumps both. Even Chuck Norris fears Cyber Lincoln. 'Which is fine. Please don’t take this the wrong way, but while I’m sure you’re a, how can I say this, ‘niche market’ somewhere, the fact is that you’d be hard-pressed to compete with her demographic.' By 4:45am King Blueberry had given up on sleeping and had scored himself a bottle of Arizona Green Tea from the refrigerator. He made his way to the computer and made himself comfortable in a chair that had once belonged to Madison Sinclair, only recently liberated. The first site he visited (in FireFox, naturally) was a wrestling forum that he would occasionally troll. The popular topic of the day seemed to be fantasy-bidding war across a few federations. 'Only 2.5 million?' he muttered in between sips from his tea, 'That’s ass.' A few clicks later and the king was browsing YouTube. A few minutes of James Earl Jones Star Wars dub was enough to elicit a few laughs, but not enough to turn calm his restless mind. Pretending you can't hear me doesn't mean I'll go away. I have all the time in the world, or at least as much time as you do. King Blueberry could only sigh. 'Now, I’ve also heard whispers - and none of this is concrete, about another of your colleagues doing something along these lines. Vincent Matthews, I think his name is.' 'Mayhem,' King Blueberry scoffed, almost spat the name. 'He calls himself ‘Mayhem’. Even had his name legally changed. I figure it’s only a matter of time before he’s in face-paint and tassels spouting off about ‘Destructionicity’, or some shit.' For a moment the room was filled with an awkward silence. 'Seriously, fuck that guy.' This did nothing to make the mood in the room any more comfortable, though the king was quick to pick up on it. 'Um, sorry about that. Kind of, you know, got carried away there.' 'Not a problem.' Spiderman was eager to get past this, moreover to get his client out of his office. 'Anyway, here’s where we’re at. Sin City has a deal with Cinemax, which is how they air Temptation. You know this already. Unfortunately neither Cinemax or Sin City are prepared to give you a large block of time either on SCCW programming or Cinemax itself to talk about whatever it is you want to talk about.' 'So, there’s not going to be any promotional bits?' asked the King, sounding more than a little melancholy. 'Let’s not get ahead of ourselves here. A deal is currently in the works to get you a series of shorts that will run in the On Demand content that Cinemax provides to Comcast, which currently has the largest user base of any subscription service in the United States. They’ve greenlit 5 pieces to start, with an option to order an additional 10 depending on the amount of views the first 5 receive. Anyone who currently has Cinemax as part of their Comcast service will have this feature at no extra cost.' Spiderman paused to take a sip from the bottle of water on his desk. 'It’s not the perfect, but for right now it’s the best we can get.' 'Dude, that’s awesome.' Spiderman was not expecting this kind of reaction from King Blueberry. 'When do we start with the filming and the lighting and all that?' 'Right now we’re looking at early-to-mid September, if all goes well. Now these won’t be long, mind you. We’re talking about 3-5 minutes each, maybe 7 minutes tops.' 'That won’t be a problem. We’re going to kick Angry Alien and their ’30 Second Bunnies’ clips square in the junk.' 'Excellent. So then if there’s nothing else we have to discuss, I’d say we’re done for the day.' Spiderman began to stand from his desk, preparing to usher his guests from the room. King Blueberry was having none of it. 'Not so fast, Webby.' Reluctantly, Spiderman sat back down. 'Something else on your mind?' 'Yeah. Need to know if everything is set for the dear Mr. Bridden’s outfit. Did your guy come through?' 'I’ve been told that everything will be ready for you when you get to Ohio. He didn’t say exactly what he’d made, only that you wouldn’t be disappointed.' 'Cool. By any chance, did he make it assless like I asked?'
