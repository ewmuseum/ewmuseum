Just so you know...

15 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th August 2008 You don't know what you've got yourself into... Let me tell you a little story... It's a story about a man called Lori Equinox. That's me by the way, Equinox. I know you're probably wondering why the hell my name is that but in time you will find out. It's really simple but what fun would it be to let you know straight away? I mean, why end the story now when it has just begun? You see, before I even joined SCCW I had been going through, well, quite alot of stuff... My mind was melting, what was I to do? I had smashed my face into mirrors, covered myself in petrol before setting myself alight, and even tried to put myself six feet under, literally. I suppose in a good way there was one person from stopping me killing myself. Her name was Elifor. In many people's eyes she was a bitch. She took control of situations that no-one else would, and they were all fearful of that fact. She cared for me like no-one else did and I was the same to her. For many years I had been a cold hearted bastard to many people I had met, women included. I had no sense of what love was, no idea of friendship... But it has seemed to come upon me that this woman was the solution to these problems. She thought like me, acted like me and most of all, loved me for who I was. Unfortunately, times change. The woman who I loved deciced I wasn't good enough anymore and cheated on me... ME!? Lori Equinox... How dare you.... But... she was beautiful... Shut up! I can't believe you still love her I don't love her... get out of my head! You're in denial! No! No!!! Don't lie, you know you're only lying to yourself! You aren't me... Sorry to inform you but I am... Letting go of the anger... Date: 13/08/08 Location: Private Psychiatrist session, Cleveland, Ohio 'So... Lori' A deep voice bellows out in a compassionate tone, almost comforting. This is Doctor Weaver, a professional Psychiatrist working out of his own office in Cleveland, Ohio. His first patient of the day is one of his toughest yet and a man who has more issues than most. The doctor's office has a very minimal feel to it. Wall painted white with a blue flowered pattern coursing throughout. There isn't much furniture either, only two chairs and a table occupy the room. Doctor Weaver sits opposite Lori on his large dark red leather sofa, his right leg crossed over his left. His posture would give the sense to anyone that he is a man of power, but in this situation he is feeling far from powerful. The man sitting opposite him is Lori Equinox, one of the newest recruits in the SCCW. Slouched back in a black recliner chair he stairs at the white washed ceiling cursing the heavens inside his mind. This man will never understand you! Even if he is the best money can buy! Why am I even here? This is a waste of my god damn time! 'Lori?' Lori's train of thought is snapped back to reality as he looks down for the first time to Doctor Weaver, who is stroking the thick grey beard which covers his chin. 'What would you like to talk about?' The Doctor inquires, 'You seem very distant, if you want me to help you, you'll have to let me in on the loop. The loop? Pah Equinox thought, There are many loops, but I suppose I may start off at a more recent problem. 'Well Doctor' Lori says, now sitting up with an intense stare in his eyes, 'I was once in love you see. There was this girl, her name was Elifor. We were together for about four years, four happy years. But then, just last month she just packed up and left.' The doctor leans forward and locks eyes with Lori, 'I can see the hatred in your eyes Lori, did you really love her that much?' Lori could feel the rage build up within himself. He had come to seek advice not be ridiculed. 'I'll have you know Doctor' Lori begins, 'I have my anger under control thank you very much.' Of course however, Lori is a man who is known for losing his temper. Especially with himself. 'I'm not questioning that Lori, what I want to know is why you are so angry. I can't help you if I know nothing.' Lori takes a deep breath before turning his right hand upwards to show his palm to the doctor who recoils back in his chair. 'Wh...wha...what have you done to yourself?' Lori flashed the doctor a menacing grin. 'It's the only way I can control my anger Doctor, pain is the only release for this torment we call a world.' 'Bu..But...' the Doctor stutters as he wipes his eyes in disbelief, 'You've cut right through your hand, how can you even take that kind of pain?' Lori gives a stifled laugh, 'I've become accustomed to it, after all these years of suffering mentally, I need to take out my rage on something other than my mind.' The doctor reaches across himself and lifts a glass of water from his water, he drinks the entire glass in almost two mouthfuls. All the while, Lori is looking down through his hand onto his leg. The doctor sets his glass down and tries to regain his composure. He waves his hand out in front of him to get Lori's attention. 'Lori, we have a serious problem here that needs fixing, you're going to drive yourself into the ground. Did you really love this girl so much that her leaving would make you do this?' Lori sits back and once again looks towards the ceiling before outlining his problem to the doctor. 'Have you ever been alone doctor?' Lori's tone was now soft yet pain ridden. 'For all my life until four years ago I had been alone, maybe something to do with who I am. My father left when I was young, but what was worse was when my Uncle told me he was my real father. My mother soon got ejected from my life. For a long time I had no-one to turn to ya'know? Sure I went to school and should have made friends but of course I was the outcast, the little guy who everyone picked on. That's when I turned to fighting as a release.' 'Fighting?' The doctor sits up, now looking the most interested he has since the beginning of the conversation. 'Well' Lori continued, 'I felt if I was going to suffer then everyone who got in my way should suffer too. I hurt people in school if they taunted me, I even hurt them if they looked at me the wrong way. It may sound weird but I enjoyed doing it. All the years that they had tortured me had just built up. Unfortunately this got me noticed by the heads of my school and I was soon ejected. Totally alone once more. I had thought about taking fighting to the streets but it was too dangerous. All the homeless were much more caniving than I was, they had been doing it longer. That's when I discovered professional wrestling.' 'But isn't that fa...' Before the words could even come out of the doctor's mouth, Lori puts his hand up to stop him. 'I can tell you' Lori says quite contently, 'That it is very much real my friend. I went and found the sickest place I could, it was called the DWO. One of the most crazy wrestling federations of its time. People weren't afraid to hurt each other in there, they almost invited pain. That's where I got my greatest rush and also where I met the girl, Elifor.' 'She was a wrestler too?' 'Nah' Lori expresses, 'Far from it, she was one of the sound techs in the back, making sure the entrance music hits and all that jazz. I can remember the first time I seen her, her face shone like a thousand stars all combined, and that body... damn.' An awkward silence fills the room for a couple of seconds before Lori continues. 'Sorry, got a little carried away there. Anyway, we started hanging out after shows and even traveling together. After about half a year of knowing each other we decided to become an item. Everyone in the back said we were like a Hollywood darling couple, always smiling and laughing together. It wasn't all laughing and smiling though, she had a dark side too her. A side that could tap into my emotions at any one time and make me take an evil streak that even I didn't think was possible. We would play games with peoples heads, and even once we tried to ruin someone's marriage just so I could get an edge in an upcoming match.' The Doctor sighs as he tries to contemplate what he has heard. 'This is definitely strange behaviour Lori, but first, tell me why she left you.' Lori closes his eyes and his mind wanders off. He can see Elifor's face inside his mind, a smile comes across his face for a second but then turns to disgust as she starts shouting at him... You're a loser Lori! You used to be so much better! When did you become such a nice guy? Look what you've done to yourself! I never want to see you again! You're pathetic! Lori breaths out and opens his eyes to look at the Doctor. 'She thinks I'm pathetic.' 'Why?' 'I had lost control apparently, I didn't know what I was doing at any one point in time. At that point I had no job, no money, no home. But I was trying ya'know? Then she kicked me out of her house and that was it. No phone calls, no messages. She vanished there and then. That's why I decided to go back to wrestling after a year.' 'Why would you go back?' 'To prove that I'm worth something to her, to myself maybe. If I can go and step into that ring and give it my all, then maybe it will get her attention. I suppose it would help with the whole hurting myself routine too.' The Doctor nods in acknowledgment, 'I can see why it would stop you hurting yourself, others would be doing it for you. I just don't believe that you going back to being a wrestler will make this girl suddenly come back to you. You need to take control of yourself well before you even think about that. You need to set your life back on track, become happy with who you are and not what you once were. I say this because that is what led you to this point in your life. If you just go back to the person you once were then she will just see you as that pathetic guy who just gets a hit off taking utter punishment. Become more than the wrestler, become a man who is willing to sacrifice more than his flesh for his life. You need to be willing to sacrifice your mind, body and soul. Only then will you win back this woman that you love.' Lori sits forwards and looks straight into the Doctor's eyes before smiling widely. 'I understand Doctor... I will take your advice and try to put it into action. Let's just hope I can do that.' Lori stands and extends his hand to the Doctor. The Doctor accepts the hand shake and the two nod at each other. 'Thank you Doctor.' 'No problem Lori, good luck.' Lori leaves the room, ready to get his life back on track. -Fade- ------------------------------------------------------------ First SCCW Interview Date: 14/08/08 This is Josh Tolland here reporting for the SCCW website. Just yesterday I got to sit down with one of SCCW's newest and hottest prospects, Lori Equinox. He is almost an enigma throughout the wrestling world only a few die hard fans tracking his progress through the sport. After over a year's hiatus, what has brought Lori back to the ring, and what are his goals for SCCW? Josh: Equinox, it's good to see you here in Cleveland, Ohio. Equinox: Hey Josh, it's good to be here. First time in Cleveland, nice place. Josh: Indeed it is. Now Lori, first thing I have to ask is, why come back now? Equinox: Well Josh, I feel as though I have to prove something to the world. I have to prove that I have the ability to be one of the best performers in this sport. For too long I've sat on the sidelines, suffering underacheivement after underacheivement. I just thought it was time to come back and give it my all this time. Josh: Well I'm sure the SCCW fans will be happy to hear that. We all know they like a performer. Equinox: And a performer they will get Josh. Back a year ago when I was wrestling in the WWA I had no passion for the sport anymore, I had a girlfriend who I loved and life seemed to be leading my in a different direction. Times changed though and now my heart is set and coming back to the ring. I'm ready for anything. Josh: It's seems as if you will need to be ready for anything as this Monday coming you will be going against Jay Draven. He is one tough customer. Do you know much about the man? Equinox: I know very little to be honest Josh, I just know what I have heard. They say he is a great wrestler and he has been challenging for titles recently so I'll be trying to stamp my authority down in the coming match to show that I too can be challenging for those titles. Josh: Eyes set on gold already. I can see you are confident. Equinox: I have to be, I'm not going into that ring without believing that I will come out the victor, it's that simple. Josh: Well, that about raps it up... one final question though. What will be the difference between you and Jay Draven this Monday? Equinox: The difference will be Josh that I am willing to do things that no-one else will, and Jay Draven is about the experience that in full force. Josh: Thanks Equinox. Equinox: Thanks. So that was Lori Equinox. Is he over confident or will he really take Jay Draven over the limit this Monday. Tune into Temptation this Monday to find out. I'm Josh Tolland and I'm out of here!
