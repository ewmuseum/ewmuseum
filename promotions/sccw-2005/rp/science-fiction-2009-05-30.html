Date: 5/30/09 When I glance up feigning thought, it looks as though I'm pondering snowlights still hanging months after Christmas. Instead, I'm examining figures not dressed to impress, eagerly buried face first into a laptop screen with paper cups always to the right, always within reach. Ella Fitzgerald is light from speakers perched high at the corners, artwork featuring coffee and old B-films line the walls, one in particular is my little perverse fantasy in a slinky dress holding a car in her hand and reaching for the ants below. I wonder why the dress grew too, most of the time science fiction doesn't feel as though it's important to explain such things. Heh. A little hypocritical for someone like me to say that. The appeal is certainly not normal, but I wished I were in the picture underneath her, looking straight up the cloth at the heaven I was forty feet away from. It is the only thing sexy in the entire room. The reality part is stifling and is what really makes me out of place in my psyche. Girls not dolled up, not thinking about fucking or shoes or cheesecakes. I pick one out, pomp and circumstance in a North Face fleece, typecasting seventy-five percent of college towns. I picture us laughing on a couch, her knees tucked close to her chest and toes buried into the edge of the cushion. She yawns and finds herself tumbling into my arms, wanting to feel my breath on the back of her neck. Her hair smells of permafrost and a flower I can't remember the name of. I imagine our arguments and her refusal to give in -- they are innocent and short-lived and make us stronger than before. We are on the altar, making faces at one another to make the withe walls and stained glass of the church a little less traditional. Everyone at the reception tells us that we were made for one another. Not once in this trail of fantasy have I imagined her naked and sprawled, or tried to recreate the taste of peppermint saliva while making out at a traffic light somewhere in Utah. She catches me in mid-gaze and blinks once, my hairstyle either repulsing her or enticing a bit of interest. I am already nude. A smirk purses over my mouth. She blinks twice, and we are twisted in sheets, frustrations with all our faults and insecurities melt away. Like the two most beautiful creatures in the universe, note hops to note on the staff in the most amazing composition of inhales and exhales ever heard. One constantly trying to outplease the other, trading positions and cultivating every part of the landscape. I see her begin to rise out of her chair, a cherry-lipped grin with blond hair pulled back in a ponytail. I am completely ignored as she kneels to scratch behind the ears of my best friend...which isn't surprising, as he pulls quite a bit more tail than I do. 'You are such a sweetie!' she coos, responding to the soft-nosed nudge. 'What's his name?' 'Quasar,' I reply, strategically keeping my head in the same position, sunglasses shading my eyes from its gaze. 'And you?' she asks without looking up. 'Thad.' Oddly enough, she smelled exactly like my fantasy. The fragrance tickled my nose as she rose to her feet, grabbing my left hand which rest on my knee with both of her hands. 'Nice to meet you and your adorable dog.' 'Thanks,' I replied. 'Please, sit down.' The only unfortunate perk with bringing your canine friend inside a coffee shop to meet women is that you've gotta stick to your story. I don't think she'd believe that just by fucking her, I'd somehow miraculously get my sight back.
