The Line: Distraction and Release

12 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/12/09 When your entire world is dedicated to wrestling, there is a long line dividing every action you make. On one side of the line is distraction. On the other side is release. Any act that occurs between shows could land on either side of this line; there is little difference between them. For instance, dealing with a nagging fight with your spouse could get your head out of the proper mindset, but at the same time it could offer perspective and clear up any murkiness that exists there. The same can be said with any dispute or conflict between friends, family, or outside forces, and believe me: every wrestler encounters these conflicts. Looking at Jonathan Rhine, the Lost Soldiers of the Highway’s biggest chance at redemption, I cannot help but feel a little worried as he rushes into what will be a bigger battle than he anticipates. He has convinced himself that this is only the first battle in what will be a long war, but he does not know what I discovered very early on: Alexandra Pierce brings her biggest weapons to the first battle. And as Jonathan Rhine marches into what he hopes will be the Equilibrium in the power structure of Sin City, I see that he has no grasp of the line between release and distraction. He lives his life in a way that polite people would describe as ‘never a dull moment.’ But to be blunt, SCCW’s New Life is a drama factory, a machine that churns out incredible situations at a rapid rate, and it’s a wonder he hasn’t been completely buried by it already. He nearly ended his career over infidelity, and now as I watch him busy himself with insignificant relationship squabbles, I wonder if he’ll ever realize that in order to truly defeat Alexandra Pierce you must be like a perfectly made doll: no loose strings, no drooping features, no sign of weakness anywhere. Because those big weapons that Alex has? She’s only fighting with the weapons her opponents give her. And as much as I hope this is not the case, Jon is handing her the entire artillery. September 12, 2009 Oakland, CA It doesn’t matter who you are: college frat boy looking for women, a businesswoman waiting for a meeting, or a professional wrestler waiting for his friend, no one likes being the alone guy at the bar. Jonathan Rhine sighs as he checks his phone, then looks up at the television to watch some ESPN recap of a baseball game. When it fails to pique his interest he checks his phone again and says to himself (but loudly enough that people nearby can hear him) "How long does it take to get from the arena?" The lack of Jared Sykes’ immediate arrival plus Jon’s generally nervous nature each play a part in his ordering of a whiskey and coke, something he promised himself he wasn’t going to do. He doesn’t want to be the latest wrestler in the headlines for a DUI, but he also doesn’t want to have his hands empty as he waits for Jared. Of course, as luck would have it The Black Sheep enters the bar as soon as Jon finishes paying for the drink. "Sorry," he says softly, accidentally bumping into one of the patrons as he scans the area for his associates. It's only a moment before his sights are set on the bar itself, and a familiar face standing near it. The walk to the bar is akin to a slalom; moving in between obstacles, careful to avoid making excessive contact with anything or anyone for fear of the repercussions it might bring. Sweaty palms slide into well-worn denim pockets as Jared maneuvers into a gap between people by The New Life, leaning his body against the wooden surface of the bar itself. "I can't even tell you how weird this is," he says, glancing first at Jon and the over the gathered crowd. 'What the hell am I in for here?" "I wish I could tell you," Jon mutters, sipping gingerly at his drink. His face puckers up as he remembers how much he doesn’t like alcohol. Then he remembers why he ordered the alcohol in the first place, and he scowls. "I also wish you would tell me how it takes you thirty minutes longer than it took me to get here." "I have to psyche myself up for this soft of thing, if you want the God’s-honest truth. Nobody ever believes me when I say this, but television is easier than real life, regardless of whatever dumb shit I may be doing." "You’re telling me," Jon answers with a particularly heavy sigh. There’s something in it that Jared no doubt notices, but Jon doesn’t give him a chance to comment on it. "Well, the girls are in the back room, I left so I could wait for you while they talked about…more feminine things. I don’t think men will ever be able to get into a woman’s head, man. I couldn’t understand what they were talking about." Jon stands up and the two begin to walk towards the back room of the bar. A curtain separates the front and back rooms, but as they two walk through it, it becomes obvious that the curtain was more than a simple boundary. The atmosphere is kicked up as slow, blinking beer lights give way to pulses and strobes. Jon blinks rapidly as he makes the transition, and obviously it isn’t any easier for Jared, either. "Congratulations," Jared says, not quite sure whether his voice is head over the reverberations of the bass pouring from every corner of the room. "I hope you’re okay with having a seizure on your conscience. If I bite my tongue off I’ll never forgive you." The Black Sheep winces as his eyes fall into the path of a rotating light, temporarily turning his whole world a screaming canary yellow. "Seriously," he continues, "remind me why we’re here again." Jon smiles as they slowly wade through the dancing people. "We’re here because we’re buds who look out for each other. And because Katie needed someone for her friend Eve. And because Eve is super hot and you could use some distractions before the big show." "Yeah, about that ‘look out for each other’ thing," starts the man who arguably is a distraction before his voice trails off. Rhine looks at his friend and laughs, slapping him on the back – an act which, due to the music and the tense nature of the situation, causes the Black Sheep to jump, which makes Jon laugh again. "Don’t worry about it, man. Different spheres. We have our whole cause of tension professionally, and I’m definitely not turning my back on it. But like I said, we’re buds. And more than anything, that is why you’re here. To show you that no matter what we get into at work, at the end of the day we can still kick back a drink…" and Jon nods out to the moving floor of writhing people around them. "…and watch the Oakland mating ritual." "Yeah, sure, mating. Almost forgot what that was like." Jared’s chuckle is immediately followed by a melancholy sigh. "Seriously. It's been a while. You do not want to know how long." Jon’s smile is immediately erased. "You’re right, I don’t." Then, after a moment, Jon’s eyes light up and he points towards the back of the dance hall. "But hopefully that will change tonight. Look, there they are." To Oakland’s credit, Jared and Jon’s mild celebrity status does not inhibit their slow walk from one side of the hall to the other. It seems that either the contingent of this club’s patrons are not wrestling fans or they are, but are bigger fans of grinding their genitals together. In any case, it doesn’t take long for SCCW’s knights to get to their princesses (or rather princess and maiden friend). "Hey babe," Jon says, pecking Katie on the cheek before awkwardly saying, "And this, is, um, Eve." The girl, dressed in a short red dress with matching pumps, unleashes a smile that is thirty percent makeup and seventy percent teeth. She leaps forward and gives Jared a hug that both surprises him and knocks him back into the people behind him. As he stammers an apology, Eve grabs his shoulders and forces him to face her. "So, Katie tells me a lot about you, you work with Jon?" Jared watches Jon and Katie retreat into their own cocoon of conversation a few feet away, then says, "Yeah, I –" "Oh, that’s cool, it seems like a great life, of course I don’t know anything about wrestling, I am too busy with grad school, you know, got to be a CEO someday!" Jared nods. "So you –" Eve laughs and puts a hand to her face. "I know, I know, you think I look like Anne Hathaway." "Bu—" "Yeah, I know, I get it all the time, when Princess Diaries came out I suddenly got really popular in school. But I was able to fend those wolves off, you know? I am really focused on my career and can’t just get involved with whatever guy waves his dick at me, you know what I’m saying?" While Jared stammers for a suitable response, SCCW’s first couple stare on from a distance, happy with the result."Look at those two, getting along so well," says Katie, a crooked smile on her face as she sips at her pink concoction. Jon follows her gaze and shrugs his shoulders. One of them is, at least. "Are we talking about the same couple?" Jon asks as Eve playfully – albeit with force – slaps Jared on the arm. "Jared looks a little weirded out, actually." "He shouldn’t be. Eve is a really nice girl, she’s cool and funny, and she just has a refreshing perspective on men and relationships." Eve leans forward to whisper in Jared’s ear, and the look on his face indicates her teeth and tongue may be involved. Jon laughs humorlessly. "And what perspective is that, that it’s okay to hit on anything that moves?" Katie puts down her drink and stares at Jon for a long moment; her nostril flares, and only her slight sway and she points at him contradicts her serious nature. "Are you going to bring this up again? In front of all these people?" "No one’s listening, and I didn’t bring anything up. I just think Jared is a little freaked out by your liberally affectionate friend." "Don’t call my friend a slut, Jon." He sighs and shakes his head. "I didn’t call her a slut, I said…" "Liberally affectionate, which in the ‘Jon Rhine’s Spineless Thesaurus’ means she’s a fucking slut." Katie finishes her drink then grabs another one that was sitting at the bar. "I’m tired of you not saying what you mean, and I’m tired of you confronting me with these moral guilt trips just because I made one mistake and because I have a friend who doesn’t play the conventional courting game." "Could you stop calling it one mistake, please? You didn’t forget to water the plants, or get into a fender bender on the way to work, you…" "Yes, Jon! I FUCKED ANOTHER GUY! Are you happy? Do you want me to shout it out to the entire club?" She already has, as both of them notice the eyes on them growing. Katie sighs and drops her volume although the damage has already been done. "I cheated on you. I am sorry. If you want me to beg and plead and cry like you did with me, you’re looking at the wrong girl. I made penance for what I did, and I know it wasn’t the right move to make. But I won’t let you keep obsessing over it every time we have a little argument. Because I got over the Sarah Riley thing. I made my peace. Now it’s time you made yours or leave me the fuck alone." Katie finishes her second glass in as many minutes, puts her hand to her head, then says, "I’m going to the bathroom." Jon watches her go for a moment, his sigh lost in the thumping music that punctuated every angry word. Tending to his drink, Jon looks back over at the source of their argument and notices that something is a little off. First of all, there is a new member to the party. Second of all, the new member is yelling at Jared. Third of all…where did Jared get that green hoodie? Back to the primary scene a few minutes prior, where Eve just asked Jared, effectively, if he knew anything about guys shaking dicks his way. He doesn’t, and to most people the expression he wears should be evidence enough of that, but for the sake of being polite the Black Sheep lies when he nods his head, smiling. "Yeah, I can totally see how that would be a giant pain in the ass." Jared slides his hands into his pockets, hopeful that their inner lining will help to wick away some of the sweat which has pooled in his palms. It’s at that moment where he formulates a plan: keep her talking, let her do the heavy lifting. "So, business school," he continues, "What’s that like? You learning how to plan your first hostile takeover?" "I’ll answer your question with a question," says Eve, her smile growing by the second. "Do you know what it feels like to have four large black men penetrate you in varying orifices?" "What...," he starts, and then immediately cuts himself off. In most situations, or rather situations where he felt more comfortable and less out of place, he might follow this with something akin to, "Well, there was this one time..." This is not one of those situations, so when he answers it is instead with a series of strange sounds and half-words. "I... uh... what the hell." To her credit, Katie’s friend doesn’t miss a beat. "Yeah, it’s a lot like that. Sort of painful, but maybe a little bit gratifying if you’re honest with yourself. Speaking of being honest, Jarey, I want you to tell me the truth: am I being too forward?" "Well…" the Black Sheep mutters before Eve’s eyes suddenly widen and she grabs him by the wrist. "Nevermind, come over here, right now." As Jared looks at the girl in utter confusion, Eve tosses her purse on a nearby stool and begins digging through it, muttering under her breath. While still occupied in her scavenger hunt she asks Jared, "So I’d like to think we’ve hit it off pretty well so far, wouldn’t you agree?" "Sure, I guess," he says, not really confident in his answer, but not entirely sure she cares either way. She doesn’t. "Good, because I’m going to ask you a huge favor that would make you pretty much A GOD to me, okay?" Rather than let him answer this, Eve springs up suddenly, bringing a green jacket out of the purse with flair usually reserved for ancient artifacts in Indiana Jones movies. Jared gives the hoodie a casual sniff before grimacing. His face darkens further when Eve says, "You have to wear this for me." His mind reels. There is no plan for this. 'Green jacket' is not a contingency that he could have ever hoped to plan for, and so ultimately his internal programming resets to its default. Make the people happy. "Suppose it couldn’t hurt," he says, slipping his right arm through the sleeve. "Though I have to be honest with you, green's not really my color. I'm more of a fuchsia guy, myself." "Really? Fuchsia would probably make you look fat. Anyway, put it on." His brows arch at the authority in her voice, and as he’s not eager to upset her does just as she instructs. Jared slides his left arm through the corresponding sleeve and secretly questions whether he actually is getting fat. "It’s a little, umm, snug," he says, unable to bring the parts of the zipper together. "Why am I wearing this again?" Eve mumbles an answer, but Jared is distracted as he catches a glimpse of himself in the mirror on the wall. He smiles, happy with what he sees. "WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS SHIT?" Then he turns around and is not very happy with what he sees. Facing Eve and Jared is a man who, if he was in SCCW, would comfortably fit into the cruiserweight category, although a more cursory glance would reveal that he would be much more comfortable in the stands or as fodder to give the illusion that Jay Draven can win wrestling matches. His face is covered with a scruff that is either unplanned or merely unnoticed, and his eyes are wild from stress and a bit too much whiskey. "Who the fuck are you, and why are you wearing my hoodie?" the man asks. "Fuck" is what Jared thinks. With a heavy sigh it is also what he says. He slouches his shoulders, shakes his head, and begins to remove the jacket. "I'm the guy who’s at the center of what I think is a huge misunderstanding," he says, trying to slide his arms free of the constrictive material. "Oh, don’t be timid now Jared! Tell Pete what you told me! How you wouldn’t let a poor, unequivocal beauty like me be stalked by some freak, how you were going to wear his hoodie as a sign of rebellion, how if Pete so much as walked in the club you would show him your awesome fighting skills…" Eve pauses to stick her tongue out at Pete. "He’s a wrestler." Pete is obviously unimpressed. "This bitch has been posting pictures of my hoodie all over the country. She made a website about it. And now I track her down and I find it on some white trash wrestler? Well I am done with your bullshit, and I’m going to show this asshole why you shouldn’t mess with the Hoodie Hoodlum." "‘Hoodie Hoodlum’," Jared repeats, screwing up his face. "Whatever, wrestlers have..." Pete's rebuttal is cut short as the Black Sheep raises a single finger, silencing the newest addition to this scene. "What the hell are you doing?" "I'm waiting for the laugh track." "Shut up, fag, I’m gonna show you what happens when you…" Pete never gets to show Jared what happens, though, because as his hand rears back to punch the Black Sheep in the face, another hand strikes, lightning fast, across his. The Hoodlum slumps immediately as Jonathan Rhine looks down at him, shaking his head. "Wow, and I thought Oakland clubs couldn’t get any stupider." Jon feels a hand on his back and turns around to see the annoyed bouncer. "Well once we kick out you assholes, it’ll probably smarten up a bit." Jon looks at Jared, shrugs, and smiles. It’s just that sort of night. "Well, that was... ‘eventful’ is I think what we're gonna go with here," the Black Sheep says, exhaling deep in the late summer air. The two SCCW wrestlers toil outside the bar, looking at it as children would a piece of candy that their parents forbid them to have. "Katie coming out to join us, or is she playing damage control with Sybil inside?" Jon sighs. "She, um…doesn’t want to come home with me tonight. Something about ‘letting my cavemen wrestling instincts take over,’ or something. It’s sort of an issue right now." "Seems a little extreme. I mean, if there was a caveman in that building is sure as hell wasn’t any of us. Plus, and please tell me if this is out of line, it was kind of her friend who showed up with the big bag o’ crazy that started this cluster." Jon chuckles and shrugs. "Yeah, she was out there. And why were you wearing a green hoodie? I always thought fuchsia was your color." "I know, right?! That's what I said, too, but she was all ‘fuchsia would make you look fat’. It’s like, ‘way to give me a complex, girl.’ I haven’t been fat since I was twelve." "Yeah." Jon sighs again, then stops walking, forcing Jared to stop as well. "I don’t know, it’s just this whole thing. I’m getting ready to face off against Desade, and I know that when you face her you have to be on top of your game. But I can’t stop thinking about all these little fights with Katie, and…I don’t know. I know people say it’s good to keep your mind distracted when you’ve got something important coming up, but is there such a thing as being too distracted?" "You might be asking the wrong guy, my friend." Jared’s grin is wide for the first time tonight. ‘Distracted’ is familiar ground, his home turf. "Kind of the normal waking state for me. If I don’t keep myself distracted then my mind wanders to things it shouldn’t, and you’d be stunned at just how many of those things there are. I like distracted. Distracted is safe." He pauses, scratching behind his neck. "Yeah, I’m definitely the wrong guy to ask." "Yeah, maybe I should ask someone else." Jon nods, then notices they are at Jared’s car. "Well, sorry for the, um, excitement. And sorry on Katie’s behalf for thinking that you and Eve were…similar." "Eh, it’s not a big deal. I get it, people think I’m strange. Can’t really blame ‘em, I guess. Most of ‘em only know what they see on television and assume it’s always like that. ‘Sides, a little excitement doesn’t hurt every now and again." Jared reaches into his pockets, fumbling for a set of keys that live on a green MSW lanyard. "That guy though, Pete or whatever, glad I dodged that bullet." "I’m pretty sure you could take him. Hell, I’m sure Misery could’ve taken him. I just wanted to help out because, hey, that’s what friends do." Again there is a lot of undertone in this statement, and Jared notices. "Really? We’re gonna do this again? There hasn't been enough drama already?" The Black Sheep leans forward against the door, resting his arms and drumming his thumbs atop the roof of the vehicle. "Alright, make your move." Jon hangs his head, too tired to fight this battle. "No, we’re not doing this again. I’ve tried to open too many scabs tonight, might as well just let ‘em heal. I’m gonna go…I don’t know, call someone or something. See you at the show, dude." "Oh, come on now," Jared jabs back, watching Jon move further down the sidewalk. "You can’t be all passive-aggressive and then walk away. It’s against the rules." No response comes. "Jesus. And people say I need to get laid." Jon turns around and shrugs as he walks backwards. "Maybe I do, Jared. Maybe I do." Then, without waiting for an answer, Jon walks towards his car. There is an audible thud as Jared introduces his forehead to the roof of his car. He remains this way for a moment before disengaging the locks and stepping into his vehicle. It's only after the engine has started and he's pulled away from the curb does he look into the rearview mirror at his friend headed in the opposite direction. He notices The New Life has pulled out his cell phone, and hopes that he's calling Katie to try and wrap up whatever meaningless issue they had been in earlier. He isn’t. It isn’t easy to tell the difference between release and distraction initially, but it’s painfully obvious afterwards. If the outcome was good, it was probably a release. If not? Well, hopefully Jonathan’s neverending manufacturing company has taken a break on the eve of his most dangerous challenge. Hopefully whatever avenues he takes outside of the wrestling ring fill him with inspiration and energy rather than nagging feelings and doubt. Hopefully he can treat his life events as lessons rather than punishments. But what do I think will happen? Well, I know Jon Rhine is the best wrestler in SCCW. I know he is more than capable of defeating the Spider. I know that I would never root against my Soldier. But I also know him too well. So I know that right now, somewhere, somehow, Jonathan Rhine is doing something dangerously stupid. Sighing heavily, Jon Rhine reaches his car and waits for the receiving party to answer. It takes a few moments, months to Jon, but finally the click is followed by a slow, tired, muffled "Hello." "Hey, Sarah. I know you probably don’t want to talk to me, but I need some…I need to talk to you. Can I visit sometime this week?" Rhine nods as he listens, then smiles. It isn’t a happy smile, but a nervous and timid one. "Good, good. Okay, see you this week." Then he sighs, hangs his head, and gets in his car to make the long drive home.
