Visions of Darkness.

28 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/28/2009 Finally win a match. Get paid in food stamps. Not surprising, given the economy and whatnot. Everything is becoming the same here in Just Wrestling. Wrestlers all look alike and talk alike and do the same vignettes. It’s a bland sport now days. Bland tights Bland matches Bland personalities Bland shows It’s all bland. The boringness… The sameness… It’s killed the sport. I then start wondering how many wrestlers in Just will wear generic tights to the next show. Such bad marketing. No wonder I got paid last week in Chicken Ranch coupons. Some guy comes in and hands me the line up for the next show. I’m facing a guy named Skillet. Huh? What? I’m fighting a kitchen utensil? Just as I am about to ask him if this is a joke, he leaves. Nobody cares about me, not even the crew member with no personality. Fucking Just. I pick up my bags and head for the car. No need to change. I’ll probably smell like shit anyway even with the shower with all the dirty water in this area of the world. Get in car and start it up. Drive off into the streets. I soon end up on the freeway where the stream of cars is endless. World soon blurs together. Cars become one entity racing beside me NASCAR style on the long and winding highway. Road lines form one giant street instead of being four lanes. Honking of cars sounds like moaning of women during sex. Air from air conditioner feels like a blow dryer melting my face. I am here, but yet I am not. Mind wanders. I feel like a vagabond inside my head. Floating on clouds like a Norse god worshipped by death worshipping barbarian Viking tribes. Skillet. The name fries my mind like bacon on a skillet. Or steak on a skillet. Or… Or… Or? Can’t think Mind explodes Fragments scatter I am nothing now But the living dead Road not helping. Night now. Yellow line is seemingly leading me to my doom. At the end of it will probably be a cliff that leads to nothingness. Infinite darkness awaits me followed either by my death at the hands of a gigantic black hole or as the food of the Langoliers. Try to think but can’t. Road looks like wilderness now. Losing mind…hate long road trips like this. This is the nadir for me. Driving toward oblivion. Facing a skillet on wrestling show. I am a joke to Shipley and the rest of the Just roster. Mind explodes again… E V E R Y T H I N G S P I R A L I N G O U T O F C O N T R O L F O R M E. I am a brain dead corpse. Look for Yellow Brick Road to drive down to reach Emerald City and get new brain from the Wizard. Can’t find turn-off. Damn. Other cars now appear to me as karts being driven by characters from the game Mario Kart Wii. At this point I realize my wrestling career is headed toward epic fail. I’ll never be Aaron Nothings or Erik Magnum. I’ll never achieve the recognition that the Illustrious Face-Eater or Meat receive from the fans. I’ll never get as over as Clark Fox, Aaron Cross, Wone, or Fallen in Love. I’ll never eat as many humans as Maynard Crane. However I have eaten human female pussy before and man, did it taste good. However, nothing changes. I’m still just a loser. All those L’s on my roster page attest to that. There are even to L’s in my last name. It’s all destiny, fate, karma…whatever you want to call it. It is my destiny to be lose and be lost like I am on this highway. I have no fucking clue where I am driving. Find a motel. Sleep there that night. Not alone. Picked up a cougar at a bar down the street. Fucked her. Slept on pins and needles after that. S L E E P … Dreams fill my fragile mind now. Dreams of winning championships in heaven after I die. In one of my dreams I fight a dragon with a sword while riding on the back of a giant rooster. Upon defeating the dragon, the rooster kicks me off and pecks my face off. At this point I wake up in a cold sweat. Look over and see the cougar naked next to me. Quickly lay back down, putting my head on the pillow. Close my eyes. Light fades away. See nothing now but darkness. Fall asleep . . . . . . . . . . . AWAKE! Get up and look over to see the cougar is gone. Whore only wanted my cum. Reminds me of a wrestling promoter only wanting me for jobbing. Speaking of jobbing, Shipley wants me back for the next tour. I guess he wants me to break the Brawler’s all time record of futility. He was even worse than the Lions were last season. How sad. A tear rolls down my cheek like the Native American from that old anti-pollution commercial. Maybe my destiny is to be a martyr for all losers in the wrestling industry. I’m wrestling a skillet for crying out loud! A SKILLET! What humiliation. This is like teaming me up with a piece of cheese to go for a tag team championship somewhere. What a joke. Maybe the joke’s been on me this whole time… Or perhaps I am the joke. HA HA Ha aH ha h a Laughter dies faster than my career. I’m a joke now. The worst joke in the history of humans. And this week on Just Wrestling, the humiliation reaches a fever pitch. Speaking of fevers, I wonder if they’ll make me take my temperature with a rectal thermometer. Get my stuff together in the dark to get ready for the show. Just Wrestling’s tour is almost over already. That sucks. In the darkness I can hear creatures moving about. Probably rats or maybe even some trolls. Maybe death is coming for me this evening. The end is drawing near. I can feel it. Little tricks of light start playing with my mind. I start seeing things…things that probably aren’t there. Creatures with claws… And wings… Fangs too. Maybe I’ve been sucked into another dimension or something. Visions begin appearing in front of me. Visions of things that are yet to come. The end of the world. Humanity’s ultimate demise. Visions of chaos and disorder Nuclear weapons exploding and destroying cities. Bodies vaporizing into thin air. Volcanic eruptions Asteroids striking the Earth. All life ending… The end is coming soon… I can feel it. The antichrist is coming or is here already and nobody knows it. The Four Horsemen are drawing neigh, riding in on their horses. Angels breath into giant horns. Plagues engulf the world. Water drying up. People starving… Dying. Only the chosen will survive. I can’t take it any more and close my eyes. Upon opening them again, the visions are gone. Finishing packing, the noises still continue. Looking around, I see no mystic portals or demonic creatures roaming about. Only an empty motel room. The pounding sounds of rough sex in the room above me make my leaving of the hotel room only go by faster. Getting back in my car and heading down the road toward the show, the world suddenly becomes a lonely and empty place. Dead Boring Silent Endless The road goes on and on. Black death below me as I speed toward the arena. Yellow line making my eyes crossed. Waiting for the ghosts of wrestling’s past to come out of the wilderness on both sides of the highway and take me away. They will take me to a better place, a place called Heavenly Championship Wrestling where me and the other Gods of wrestling will battle it out and show all of these bland new wrestlers how it’s done. Maybe Aaron Nothings will give me a title shot and I will die just like everyone else. He will shank me like a prisoner. My soul will float up and be welcomed. But, alas, I am here driving down the road inside of a giant Pepsi can on wheels. Wind blowing outside. The Gods must be angry. World coming to an end soon Along with my career Doomed, all of us. Especially me. Maybe I should retire and become a world wandering vagabond like the Highlander. That would make more sense than going to a Just Wrestling show to wrestle a Skillet in front of a few thousand people. Shipley is trying to make me into a joke. I was a joke already. Nevermind. Arrive at show. Take stuff inside and walk toward locker room. Immediately smell something upon entering the backstage area. Sniff… Sniff… SNNNNNIIIIFFFFFFFF… Smells like some sort of beef product. A thought then crosses my mind and it scares me. Maybe it’s going to be the ultimate humiliation. Maybe I’m going to be in a triple threat match against Skillet and Meat. Smell fills my nostrils. Stomach growls. See a crane fly into the arena from outside. Someone shoots at it with a BB Gun before it flies back outside. Karma. Too bad it wasn’t Maynard Crane. He smells like dead children with a hint of oregano. Speaking of smells, there’s a lot of smelly wrestlers in Just. Body odor and cleanliness is not taken into account by Tim Shipley. Shipley, a man who has a PTC Elite Championship match down the line, doesn’t even care about his wrestlers. They’re all smelly, ugly homeless vagabonds. I think I’m the only one who doesn’t live inside of a box. In fact… I live outside of the box. Taking my bags to the locker room, I pass a man in the hallway. He smells. Must be my opponent. Never seen him before. Look over and see the crucifixion scene but with some guy wearing ugly tights on the cross instead of Jesus. Must be Aaron Cross. Or a rib at his expense. Shake head and go inside locker room, closing the door behind me as I do. Inside I find the Grim Reaper. He tells me the next tour will be the death of me. I ask him I’m already dead and he says no, but Alex Grey already killed my career. Damn. POOF! He’s gone and I’m alone. Life sucks.
