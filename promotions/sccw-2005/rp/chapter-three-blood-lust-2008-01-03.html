Chapter Three: Blood Lust

3 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-3--08 You see what power is - holding someone else's fear in your hand and showing it to them! The sirens of the police vehicles could be heard out side of the run down building Cyrus had made his final stand on. The chase was over, Cyrus had lost. He would be taken to jail, tried on counts too numerous to list, eventually found guilty, locked in prison and left there to rot. For Cyrus the good times were far behind him. The fans, the action, the friends. Everything Cyrus had loved the most was just a memory of a former life that was now long since past. Cyrus had been champion many times over, the crowd had loved him, and he loved them. Cyrus had been at the top of the mountian thinking himself better then he was. One day when his boss had come to him with a gimmick change that Cyrus didn't feel would be good for his image, he had stormed out of the building, quiting his job, and subsequently starting his downward spiral, all because of one very small but powerful word; pride. Something that Butch had quoted to Cyrus once popped into his head 'Through pride we are ever deceiving ourselves. But deep down below the surface of the average conscience a still, small voice says to us, something is out of tune.' And how true that was in Cyrus's case. A life wasted was all he had left now because of his pride. Cyrus got a glimpse out side as he was escorted by a window, there were police cars, news vans, a throng of people being held back by barricades, all because of the things he had done in his life.As they rounded the stairs he passed by a door which was open and in the center of the room sat a small child playing with a toy bus as his mother looked on from the perceived safety of her small apartment. Time slowed but an instant as Cyrus stared at the small boy playing with his bus. Images of Cyrus's younger days danced across the screen of his mind. Things he had done before he found wrestling, things that had tainted his mind and taught him to suppress his emotions, things which had forever altered his life and made him the monster he was today. Such as when the Earl Stanley had to pay.......... It had been a cold day in Detroit and Cyrus and Butch had been out running errands for Lou and the girls at the house.Cyrus had begun to become immune to the things Lou had told him and Butch to do. Cyrus had done some things in the past few weeks he would never forget. On the outside he was becoming a stone, nothing could faze him, he was becoming like Butch. But on the inside, the battle raged between what was right and what was wrong. With Lou and Butch and the rest, Cyrus had found a family of sorts. It was hard for Cyrus to imagine that he depended on these people for a home, for food. for friendship. And all it took to keep that family was a little bloodshed every now and then. Was that really so wrong? 'Yes!' The word screamed in his mind shaking him from his quite thoughts. Cyrus blinked and looked around from the passenger seat of the old car. He was riding in the car with Butch heading back to the cat house. Butch was wearing a pair of books, blue jeans, a red plaid button down shirt and a black wool stocking cap pulled over his ears. Butch was an oddity to Cyrus, he was by far the most cruel man Cyrus had ever met, but there was much more to this man the the blood thirsty machine Cyrus had known. He always seemed to carry himself with a quiet dignity not befitting a man of his occupation. There was a light in his eyes that told Cyrus he was far more intelligent then he led others to believe. And some of the words and phrases he used wee not something you would expect to hear coming from the mouth of a normal thug. No, there was something about Butch that just didn't line up right, but Cyrus would have time enough to ponder what exactly that something was later but for now his musings would have to wait, the house was coming into view. Cyrus walked into the house carrying the various bags from the different grocery stores and out let malls he and Butch had visited that day. He handed out the bags to each of the girls as they came to retrieve there stuff from him. The was thanked with kisses to the cheek and promises of repaying him later which Cyrus blushed at. After all the bags were brought in Cyrus retired up to his room for a little nap. About three hours later Cyrus awoke and laid there in bed for a few minutes before someone began knocking on his door. Cyrus got up and quickly dressed then opened the door to see who it was, it ended up being one of the girls telling him Lou wanted to see him. Cyrus knocked on the door to Lou's office, and after a few seconds turned the handle and walked in. Lou was sitting at his desk looking over various papers of who owed who money. Lou looked at Cyrus over the top of his glasses and gestured him to come in and take a seat. After Cyrus had sat down Lou began to tell him about his next assignment, who, where, and how much he owed. One thing that Cyrus had learned in his short time with Lou and Butch and the rest is that Lou didn't really care how business was taken care of so long as the punishment was equal to the amount owed. This particular time the man in question was a local florist by the name of Heath O'Connell who owed Lou only about five hundred dollars but was past due on his payment. So Cyrus and Butch were being dispatched to persuade Mr. O'Connell to pay on time should he ever decided to gamble on basketball again. Cyrus asked where Butch was and Lou told him he didn't know but more then likely in his room. And sent Cyrus to get him and inform him of there next job. Cyrus walked down the red carpeted hall to the room in the back that was occupied by his mentor Butch. Cyrus didn't even think to knock as he walked into the room. Cyrus was greeted by the site of Butch in a compromising position. Not one that Cyrus had expected to see, but one that for a long time to come would bring a smile to Cyrus's lips when he thought of it. There sitting in a dark brown leather chair, a book in one had and a glass of red wine in the other sat Butch, there was a recored playing in the back ground the song Cyrus would later find out was 'The Barber of Seville' by Rossini. Cyrus stared in mute silence as his eyes and ears took in the unexpected picture. Butch snapped his head up staring a hole through the intruding Cyrus. Cyrus shrank back as the big man started to rise from his chair. He walked to Cyrus and stood towering over him his gaze boring into him like an awl. Between clenched teeth Butch said 'You say a word of this to anyone Cyrus and I will cut your tongue from your throat and laugh as you choke on your own blood!' Cyrus tried to scramble away but was held fast by the big mans stare, 'You wanted something Cyrus or you wouldn't have barged in here. Now what was so important you could not knock at my door for?' Butch asked in a menacing tone. Cyrus's mind locked up with fear as all he could do was stammer the word 'job'. Butch growled down at him 'Don't just stand there then. put his up while I get dressed.' Butch said as he shoved the book he was previously reading into Cyrus's chest. Butch turned around and headed for his bed room. After the door was shut and Cyrus was sure Butch would not come back out for a few minutes, Cyrus walked over to the book shelf and looked for a spot to put the book, there was a spot open on the second shelf from the top between a white book and a blue book. Cyrus looked at the names on both the books, the white one was titled 'The Complete works of Edgar Allen Poe' and the blue book was 'The Complete works of William Shakespeare' Cyrus looked at the book he held in his hands, it was a gray book with the title of 'Leaves of Grass by Walt Whitman' Cyrus put the book gently between the other two. He would have to ask Butch about all of this Cyrus thought as a smile played across his lips. The bedroom door opened and Butch stormed out looking ready to cause some carnage. As he reached the door Cyrus said 'Butch, I never knew you.......' Cyrus stated. 'Not one word Cyrus, unless you don't want to see the sunrise tomorrow' Butch growled. Cyrus smiled 'All I'm saying is.....' Cyrus again started. 'I mean it Cyrus, I'll kill you dead and not even think twice about it.' Butch warned. Cyrus smiled as they walked out of the room. It was dark by the time Butch and Cyrus reached the shop where Heath O'Connell worked. The lights were still on and there was a man in the back of the shop but the sign on the front of the door said closed. Which worked well for Cyrus and Butch, because they didn't need anyone interrupting there meeting with Heath. The door was locked but Butch reached in his pocket and pulled out a lock pick set and easily opened the door. The bell over the door rang as they walked in. 'Sorry were closed..........and I thought I locked that door.' came the voice from the back. Cyrus and Butch looked at each other and Butch motioned for Cyrus to go to the left of the store as he went to the right. Heath came out of the back 'I thought I said we.......' The words caught in his mouth as he turned white as a ghost. He stood shaking so hard his knees beat against one another as he stared at the two men advancing toward him. He was trapped and he knew it, because the only way out of his shop was through the front door. Cyrus and Butch reached him at the same time. Cyrus reached out a hand and put it on Heath's shoulder to try and calm the man down. Cyrus looked the man over thoroughly. He was an older man, late fifties early sixties. with gray hair, green eyes, a frail thin body. He was wearing a yellow striped shirt with brown pants and brown loafers, he still had his apron on around his waist, on his left wrist he wore his watch on on his right hand he had a gold ring. Cyrus stared down at the man with pity in his eyes, because he knew what was in sore for this poor man. Cyrus explained to him who they were and what they wanted. Mr. O'Connell assured them that he would pay Lou the money that he owed, he just needed a little more time. Cyrus shook his head in understanding. 'But were still going to need some collateral Heath. To make sure you don't skip out on us, You understand right?' Cyrus asked him. 'What do you mean Collateral?' the old man asked. Cyrus walked around the frightened man and reached across the counter and pick up a pair of pruning sheers. 'Well, I think that ring will be good enough.' As Cyrus and both exited the shop in which Heath O'Connell was holding his right hand, crying, sitting in a pool of his own blood, Cyrus began cleaning the blood off of the ring.. 'So whats next, boss?' Cyrus asked Butch Butch meerly shrugged. 'Your call Cyrus, we can either go back home or we can look for more trouble to get into.' He said with a sly grin. Cyrus smiled back 'Trouble it is then.' They both laughed as they walked down the side walk. They had decided to take the bus to the floral shop just to keep people from identifying Butches' car. Because of that they were forced to take the bus where ever it was they wished to go that night. They had waited for the bus for a while and finally it showed up, nearly half an hour late. The bus driver had not been very happy to see them as his shift was just about to end, and having customers on the bus ensured he would not be getting off on time. The man was very belligerent and rude from the second they stepped foot on the bus. And Cyrus took a disliking to the guy almost immediately. So he and butch decided to toy around with the guy for a while. They would wait for the bus to stop and act like they were going to get off then sit back down and ride on to the nest stop. The man finally had had enough, he stood up at on stop and shouted at them did they know who he was? He was Earl Stanley and he was the bus driver, he was the best bus driver that Detroit had, and he was sick and tired of them being on his bus. The mans pride sickened Cyrus. He thought he was somebody, when he was really a no body, not even a blip on the radar of greatness. The man stood at the front of the bus and demanded they exit immediately he was through driving them around, and what he did was a service not an obligation. Cyrus had to chuckle as the man known as Earl declared that they should kiss his feet for allowing them to ride around with him for as long as they had. The pride he displayed oozed forth with nearly a visible slime. Cyrus had had enough. He stood and walked to the front of the bus, followed by Butch. As he got to the walk way he stooped and looked at the pride filled irate man standing before him. Cyrus reached into his back pocket. Unlike times before he didn't have any compassion for his man. This man deserved what he was about to get. In a blur of motion Cyrus swung his arm around pliers in hand and smashed Earl Stanley full in the face. Blood and bits of teeth went exploding from his open mouth. The man slumped backward as Cyrus began to kick the man repeatedly in his chest and head after he hit the ground. The blood lust that over came Cyrus at this point is indescribable. He continued to kick the man unmercifully. breaking ribs and tearing internal organs. Cyrus irate by the the mans actions continued to pummel him long after the man was unconscious. Cyrus still out for blood, grabbed the mans hands and one by one broke his fingers with the pliers. Reveling in the snap of each bone as it was twisted and popped out of joint. Cyrus standing to admire his handy work spit on the unmoving unconscious from of Earl Stanley, his anger finally fadeing. As they walked off the bus Butch looked at Cyrus 'I didn't like his attitude either, but was all of that really necessary?' Butch asked. ' I hate pride.' Cyrus said. 'And besides the good book says that 'pride cometh before a fall' and I sure would hate for it to be wrong.' Cyrus said with a smile. Butch wasn't smiling though, and he had to wonder what type of monster had they allowed into there happy little home?
