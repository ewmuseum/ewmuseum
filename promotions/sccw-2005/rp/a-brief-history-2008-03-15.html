A Brief History

15 March 2008

Date: March 15th ONE NIGHT IN OAKLAND, YEAR 2000 (When we last saw our hero...) ''What the fuck was that? Are you crazy?'' ''Am I crazy? Yes, we established that.'' Answered a much more frantic voice, ''Can we just get out of here?'' He laughed at her. ''Oh, you think you're coming with me? Do you know the size of the target you just put on BOTH our backs?'' ''You're the one who took it further.'' ''I gave you a peck so the fans would go home with a cute picture. Just because I took your easy ass back to my room a couple weeks ago doesn't make that any more meaningful than every other stilted, scripted part of this shit.'' he said, ''I know that Eddie didn't plan what you just did for ratings, but get the hell away from me if you think you're gonna find a soft place to land.'' ''Go fuck yourself, Darcy.'' Her life was breaking down around her, because she'd just broken out of her thickest chain -- an abusive relationship whose financial safety had held her near a toxic situation for too long. The feeling of relief that she'd hoped for wasn't there, because the man she'd left was her boss, and he had enough seedy, underpaid cronies working for him to have her snuffed out in the blink of an eye. To make matters worse, right after she broke an ancient wrestling code not to do something without the consent of the fedhead, the company's champion kissed her. Of course, now he was saying it was just for ratings. All of that coupled together with an incredibly uncertain future led her across the street, sitting against a brick wall and sobbing into her Bleeding Through hoodie. She was a mess, plain and simple -- she didn't even have the strength to walk inside the liquor store she was leaning against, no guts or motivation to drown her sorrows in alcohol. Worse yet, the car pulling out from the parking complex she'd just left was pulling up beside her. It was occupied by Darcy Crisis, likely rolling down his window to remind her how stupid she is by repeating what he'd already said. The truth was that he'd seen her leaving that garage as he got into his own car, and something about watching the complete emotional meltdown that ensued had Darcy considering the idea that Amy Campbell might actually be a human being. He rolled down the passenger's side window and spoke hesitantly to the woman he loathed. Somehow, in that moment, he'd been looking at her differently. It lead to him asking ''Amy, are you the person I think you are?'' And to her responding, ''I wish it was that simple.'' Darcy sighed and banged the back of his head against his seat, clearly struggling within himself. Almost every one of his sensibilities told him not to open the door, but one look at her proposed the idea that Aimz might not be what he'd taken her for. If she was the greedy, attention-seeking whore he'd been seeing, she wouldn't have just given up the comfortable lifestyle their boss would have provided. He saw a girl who no longer wanted to be a puppet, who didn't want to continue sacrificing her self-respect for the sake of financial and career stability. At least, he hoped that's what he saw. More than anything, he was looking at someone who was scared shitless -- and she was right, he had already invited himself into the mess. On the off chance that he'd spent a year being wrong about her, Darcy took a leap. He unlocked the passenger side door. ''I live in the bay area, it's only about an hour out from here. Get in,'' he ordered, ''But no talking, and you'd better cough up the cash for a hotel when we get near.'' When Amy tried ignoring him, Darcy put the car in park and continued, ''I don't like you any more than you like me right now, but do me a favor and get the hell in the car so I don't have to have some 'Promiscuous Canadian Wrestler Found Dead' headline on my conscience.'' She shook her head, ''You watched me toss away my life, and you probably have no idea why I did it... but you injected yourself into the situation. I didn't invite you to. So don't sit there saying it was for ra--'' ''I'm not playing the power struggle, Aimz. You needed a ride a few minutes ago, don't suddenly take to acting all strong - we both know what Eddie's gonna do the second he finds you. Now, get in,'' he ordered, ''I'm not gonna ask, beg or say it again. If you don't wanna be left to the wolves, get your ass in the car.'' She could get in the car and risk being alone with a man who probably hated her, or she could take her chances by staying on a rainy street hoping to avoid a man she KNEW hated her in that moment. Amy took her chance, and it was with Darcy. ONE NIGHT... CONTINUED. ''I know I'm not supposed to speak, but I can't help it.'' Amy shifted nervously in the passenger's side seat when she made the observation,''We've passed at least three hotels that I've seen, and you haven't asked me which one I plan on staying in.'' ''You're staying with me,'' Darcy sighed, ''God knows I'll regret allowing it, but you and I both know how quick Eddie'd find you in a hotel.'' ''You're awfully considerate for a selfish prick. Has anyone ever told you that?'' ''Don't be too appreciative, now.'' ''I'd rather stay in a hotel, at least I'd know the only hands near me were mine.'' ''Get over yourself, I've got no intentions of getting in your pants,'' he rolled his eyes, ''And you're not fooling anyone, we both know that I wouldn't have to do this much to get there. Again.'' ''That's an incredible talent you have.'' Amy chuckled, ''Not the 'getting into my pants' thing -- your ability to seamlessly go from almost being a nice guy to once more becoming a self-absorbed fuckbag. That's gotta be a hard bit to practice.'' Darcy rolled his eyes again before he set them on the road once more, beginning to slow the car as he turned onto a side street in a surprisingly clean neighborhood, ''Well, unlike you, my oral talents actually involve speaking.'' Amy was clearly desperate, or she wouldn't have been in that car in the first place. With the verbal lashings came the realization that she didn't have enough dignity to fight over at that point. ''Wow. Just... wow.'' She winced when Darcy pulled into the driveway of a split level duplex, seeing that her trip had come to its end and knowing she had to face him for more than just witty barbs. For his part, Darcy was surprisingly cordial when the car stopped. He'd gone quiet, turned the vehicle off and stepped out - going so far as to round the car and open Amy's door, possibly only out of habit. She'd taken the gesture uncomfortably, silently questioning what all of it was meant to lead to, but she honestly couldn't find a part of Darcy that liked her enough to try anything questionable. The redhead quietly followed him inside, clutching onto the backpack that held the only possessions she could salvage before dashing away from the arena that night. The place was decent enough, and a lot cleaner than she expected a bachelor's home to be. If you ignored the Fight Club frame-up, you'd have no idea that a man in his twenties lived there. Amy set her bag down, but kept her sneakers on in case she needed a speedy exit. Even more surprising than his home's cleanliness was Darcy's next gesture of good will. He turned around to face her with hands nervously toying in his jean pockets, ''That thing about 'oral talents'... that was harsh.'' ''It's fine.'' ''No,'' he corrected, ''It's not. I didn't mean that sh--'' ''I said it's fine,'' Amy said flatly, ''Don't worry about it. I don't need an apology from you.'' Darcy shook his head and cast his eyes to the floor, ''I just... you don't deserve that shit. You sort of showed that tonight, standing up to Eddie and leaving.'' When he started fidgeting with his facial scruff, he seemed to understand that it was time to move on, ''Anyway, this is it. Bathroom's two doors that way if you need to shower, and... well... I don't exactly have a guest bedroom, but I've got a room with a futon. That should do you until morning, right?'' ''Right.'' He was showing a completely different side than the one she normally saw, especially when he quickly brushed by the answering machine on his coffee table, took his own shoes off and began to shuffle out of the walk-in livingroom. ''I know this is a fucked up situation, so I'm just gonna get out of your hair for both our sakes. Just... y'know... let me know if you're gonna take off. I sleep at the end of the hall, but I'll probably be up all night - not that I think you're gonna murder me in my sleep or anything, but--'' ''I don't blame you for not wanting to go to bed with me in the house,'' she said, ''If I were you, I wouldn't trust me.'' ''Are you planning on giving me a reason not to?'' Amy raised her eyebrows, ''I haven't given you enough already?'' She attempted to look at ease, sitting on his couch without so much as glancing casually at the man who'd effectively just saved her, but it didn't take him any time to see through it. He leaned over the back of the couch to stay within earshot, putting the tiny redhead slightly on edge despite his words, ''Amy, you wouldn't be here if I didn't have a feeling that I might not hate the person you are without him. And I wouldn't have you here if I didn't think that you needed help to stay away from him.'' Darcy had decided to sit on the opposite end of the room, planting himself on a lounger next to the TV that Amy had already turned on in a further attempt to seem unphased by the whole scenario. Once again, he saw through her... but this time, even she knew it. She stood up and limped her way towards the hall, sneering at him. ''See?'' she growled, ''Back to the nice guy routine. You're unbelievable, and I need to be alone. I'm not your fucking charity case to rack up Brownie points in your own conscience. Giving me a place to sleep tonight doesn't reverse any of the shit you've ever assumed about me, so get fucked if you're gonna keep this act up. And don't say my name like you know me.'' Even though she claimed he was the actor, it was obvious that Amy glazed over her moments of intense vulnerability with equally outrageous anger. When the door to his spare room slammed, he knew that the redhead was, at the very least, safe. He leaned back in his chair and glanced to the television, wondering what further chaos the morning could bring while questioning his own sanity for being so drawn to get involved in the first place. HOURS LATER... (Year 2000) ''I said NO. Get the fuck away!'' Amy woke up from the nightmare in a cold sweat, gripping the thin sheets that covered her dirty laundry. Had it all been a dream? The hand on her shoulder answered, and brought her crashing back into the reality of her actions. God, it was an awful feeling to remember that she'd just ruined her life by escaping something that seemed to have been ruining it anyway. Worse yet, she recognized the voice that came from beyond the hand that shook her. He didn't say her name like he knew her. ''Aimz?'' It took her a moment to get over the shock that she'd been asleep within fifty yards of someone like the Darcy Crisis she knew. She'd passed out from the exhaustion and fallout of adrenaline when she stormed out of his livingroom hours before. As soon as he saw her mismatched eyes open, he shot his hand away like it had been on a hot burner and attempted to explain himself, ''I thought you were having a breakdown, I could hear you yelling over the TV.'' The nightmare and the fog of sleep left her too stunned to fully interpret the situation, but Amy was still determined not to get caught in a vulnerable moment. She shot up off of the futon and blew past her hesitant hero... ... But she didn't quite make it to the door. Seconds after, she awoke again -- still in an unfamilliar place, still with company that she wouldn't admit to wanting, and still with too much reality coarsing through her. And that voice... still concerned. ''Jesus, are you okay?'' Darcy was kneeling over her. He no longer looked anything like the man she'd been at odds with for a year -- he was panicked, upset and, as much as he might not have wanted to be, caring. ''Don't sit up, your head just went halfway through my drywall.'' Well, that explained the migraine. ''It's... ugh. It's an anxiety thing.'' She sat up and leaned against the wall, ''That happens sometimes. I get whacky blood sugar, mix it with stress and bam.'' ''Hold on,'' Darcy ordered, ''Just sit there for a second.'' Amy couldn't figure out why he'd extended himself to her like he was. He jogged out of the room and returned in under a minute with a bag of frozen vegetables and damp paper towels. This was definitely not the Darcy Crisis she knew. He looked genuinely concerned, for one - kneeling to offer his help, examining her forehead closely. ''It's just a scratch, but I don't want you... uhh...'' In that second, he'd seemed to realize how uncomfortable the situation really was for the redhead, whose life had just been tossed into a wood chipper. ''... Can't have you bleeding all over the place.'' ''And you owe me for that patch of drywall,'' he added. Amy's eyelids were getting heavy again and instead of snatching the supplies to mend her own wounds, she found herself with a lowered guard - wincing when Darcy pressed the warm, damp paper towel against her face. She was dazed, but hardly because of the fall, and sat in the midst of a comfort she hadn't felt for a long, long time. Apologetic words slipped from her tongue in that moment of calm and caring, ''You weren't supposed to be involved.'' Darcy couldn't help but grin at her -- he had no idea that he was just getting to know the Amy Campbell he'd one day marry. ''I might be a selfish prick like you said, and I know I've been an asshole... but someone had to catch you. I guess I picked up the job that night a few weeks back.'' He was talking about the few hours that they'd... err... become aquainted with one another. She'd caught Eddie cheating and decided to leave, and he'd been at the bar she walked into that night. There hadn't been much to say, but Darcy couldn't help feeling responsible for taking the drunken redhead back to his hotel that night. ''I'm sorry about... y'know, all that. I know you think I was taking advantage because I didn't have - oh, what was it you said - a 'ring rat' to go to bed with...'' ''Darcy...'' she sighed, grimacing again when he removed the towel and put the cold bag on her head, ''As pathetic as I am, I still have the power to say no. I just chose not to, just like I chose to come here.'' She was talking as if she had other options, but the truth was that Amy's only other place to lay her head would have been a sidewalk at that point. ''I'm a big girl.'' He chuckled sweetly, ''Everybody needs help sometimes. I've gotta say, though... you're so different when you're alone.'' ''Everyone is,'' Amy said, ''When I was with Eddie, it was nothing but survival instinct. Like you said, he treated me like shit - but he sustained me, no matter how unhealthy it was, so I stayed like a good little soldier. I'm done with that.'' She sighed, ''I'd rather sleep in a box than lay awake next to another cocksucker like that.'' There was still an unspoken tension, but even despite it Darcy saw her in such a different light. She had a gentle way about her, even through the tough-as-nails front that she insisted on keeping up at all times. He didn't think it was possible to regret a drunken one night stand any less than he was while he watched her just then - bloodied, emotionally broken but somehow still hinting that she was so much more. He'd almost dare to say that, when she wasn't spouting one-liners at the urgings of her now ex-boyfriend, Darcy Crisis actually liked Aimz. They sat silent while he attended to the fresh wound on her forehead, giving Amy the seconds she needed to reflect on the events of the past weeks. It seemed to her that she was getting a new shot at life - at maybe doing things the right way - and that lead her to ask for another new beginning... ''Stop for a second,'' she ordered Darcy, ''Look at me.'' He didn't know exactly what to expect, but kept kneeling as he looked her in the eye. ''I'm Amy Campbell. The last year of my life never happened,'' she said, groggily extending her hand to shake his, ''And you don't know me yet, but it's nice to meet you.'' That was their second fresh start. Neither realized how much practice it would take to get it right. Darcy, for one, didn't realize that the real reason she'd just passed out had more to do with the baggie of white powder in her pocket than anything anxiety had done to her. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- FOUR WEEKS AGO ''So... your boyfriend was your boss, you hated Darcy, your boyfriend cheated and you had revenge sex with Darcy for some reason. Then you went back to hating Darcy, left your boyfriend-slash-boss and his company, Darcy gave you a place to stay and somehow you ended up married eight years later.'' Malick laughed, ''And I thought my life was complicated.'' Amy set her drink down on the TV table while her friend beamed at her, half-mockingly. What Katie didn't know is that Amy was deliberately leaving out the five years of separation that she and Darcy had endured without the slightest hint of grace. Katie's problems with her boyfriend weren't over by a long shot, she didn't need to hear the more depressing parts of the story. Well, four weeks ago she didn't... Now that Rhine had admitted to cheating? ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- PRESENT DAY... Katie walked into the cafe without any intentions of listening to the woman who called her there. Unfortunately, it's kind of hard not to listen when that person is screaming at the poor clerk the moment you step inside. ''Seriously, Pepsi. How fucking hard is it? I don't care about your latte specials or whatever dumbass with an acoustic guitar you've got spewing about ex-boyfriends here tonight. Do scarf-wearing emo fuckhats not drink soda, or is this just the single most out-of-touch coffee shop I've ever been to?'' Well, at least it wouldn't be hard to find her. Safe in the knowledge that the firecracker was already there, Katie ducked into the washroom and pretended to fix her hair for a solid five minutes - praying that the redhead had either already left, or at least finished tearing apart the terrified teenager who was too used to serving iced caps to find a can of pop for a fighting star. Though she hadn't left, Katie found a much quieter Amy Campbell sitting at a table in the corner when she approached it, placing one hand firmly on her hip to assert herself when she ordered the redhead to ''Tell me why I shouldn't walk out the door right now.'' Amy looked up and then rolled her eyes, sighing. ''I see you're still pissed of about Captain Asshat getting his jewels put in a cup.'' ''It wasn't your place to do that, and you know it.'' Katie scorned her and then sat down, tightly pursing her lips in disapproval, ''Don't you have a match or somewhere else to be tonight?'' ''It's Gideon... trust me, I know what to do.'' Regardless, Malick shook her head. ''You still haven't told me why I'm even sitting here.'' The redhead bowed her head, ''I know you think what I did to Rhine wasn't helpful, but it's the least I could've done. What really didn't help at all was what I did a few weeks ago, telling you the story of how Darcy and I got together.'' ''And why might that be?'' Katie was giving her the cold, unforgiving stare of a woman scorned. It was a good presentation, admittedly - one that Amy couldn't help but see herself in. ''I gave you a false sense of hope that your boyfriend wouldn't be a complete fucktard. I told you the cute, cuddly version of the story and now that I'm trying to cut the bullshit, well...'' Amy sighed before she continued. ''... I might've left a few things out.'' (To Be Continued!)
