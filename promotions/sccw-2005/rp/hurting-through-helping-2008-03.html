Hurting Through Helping

March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 2008 "Jon?" Foster Nackedy is not usually a very worrisome man. He has faced many things in both his personal life and his professional career that he has convinced himself that nothing can truly worry him. However, there is a degree of uneasiness in his voice as he tries again. "Jon, I know you’re there, pick up." After he meets the passing seconds without an answer, he sighs "Goddammit" and hangs up the phone quickly, then opens the front door and begins to walk towards his car. Following him is a woman whose brown skin glimmers in the sun as sweat drips off of her body. She appears to be so comfortable in her skin, she nearly steps outside naked, only realizing at the last second to snag a towel to wrap around herself. She gives Foster a small frown. "Baby, what’s wrong?" Not looking back, Foster mutters, "He always answers." "What?" He looks up suddenly, then shakes his head. "Oh, nothing. Got to go help a friend. I think he’s in trouble – or he’s going to be soon." He slips into the convertible and stops, looking at the woman standing in the doorway, his curiosity finally melting into exasperation. "What?" "Let me come with you." Foster’s eyebrows arch in confusion. "Come with me? Why?" Foster’s new interest smiles, walking to the convertible’s driver side and running her hands through Foster’s spiky hair. "Because it sounds like fun." "Listen, Shway, this isn’t going to be fun. I know you think the glamour of the wrestling world is all great and wonderful, and you think it’s drama all the time. And it is. But this isn’t the kind of drama you want to see. Jonathan’s been in a rough patch for awhile, and…" The girl known as "Shway" lights up, her eyes growing wider as her lips curve to an almost malicious grin. "Jonathan like Jonathan Rhine? Now I’m definitely coming." Foster stares at her for a few moments before rolling his eyes. "All right, fine. But go inside and put on a goddamn shirt first." Giggling, she runs inside of the house as Foster Nackedy sighs, checking his hair in the mirror. "Dammit, Jon, you better be on the brink of suicide with the trouble you’re putting me through." He isn’t. Jonathan Rhine is alone in a hotel room, and the last time he was in such a position Foster’s statement may not have been too far from the truth. However, although he is holed up, there is no depression that can be found on his face. Jonathan is determined. He drops from a standing position and begins to push himself up; the only sound in the room is his steady breathing. He thinks about his title matches, he thinks about putting his title on the line against a heated rival and former friend, he thinks about Amy Campbell and nothing else. Or at least he tries to. His mind floats to Katie in mid-push, and the motions become slower. He’s been so focused on his job recently that he hasn’t had time to think about his ex-girlfriend, and that is definitely helpful to his recovery. However, he can’t help but think that not thinking about her is what caused these problems in the first place. "Jon, what’s wrong?" "What’s going on? Why are you so distant?" "Just talk to me, Jon." He never would, though. Jonathan Rhine has been raised to believe that you work to get where you want to be, and you don’t take anyone’s help to get there. He is self-sufficient to a fault, and that fault is now staring him in the face. But still he keeps working. He keeps working because he remembers every word he says in every interview, and he doesn’t want to be a liar. The following is a transcript of an audio segment taken from Don Stuck’s 3/7/08 interview with Jonathan Rhine, as heard on X 103 in the morning. STUCK: "Thanks for joining in, folks, you’re tuned in to X103, this is Stuck in the Morning, and we’re talking with one of our favorite morning guests here: Jonathan Rhine, SCCW Universal Champion. SOUND EFFECT: Michael Buffer screaming "CHAMPION OF THE WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORLD…" STUCK: "Although to be honest I don’t know why we’re interviewing you now that you work in VEGAS." [Several boos can be heard as Rhine chuckles.] RHINE: "Come on guys, you know my heart is still in Indy. I can’t control the fed changing directions." STUCK: "Sure you can, you’re the champ. Tell ‘em, ‘We’re not moving. Actually, you know what? We are moving. We’re moving to Indianapolis full time!’ They can’t tell the champ NO. PRODUCTION ASSISTANT: "Yeah, do that!" RHINE: [laughing] "I’ll be sure to mention it to Smitty next time I see him." STUCK: "All righty, good deal. Our final question here, Jon, is kind of a deep one, so I want to apologize in advance. I didn’t write this question." RHINE: "All right, who did, so I know who to get mad at?" STUCK: [laughing] "You can yell at Shannon, she’s our production assistant. Go on, yell at her." SOUND EFFECT: the man who found out Paris Hilton was getting out of jail early going "NO, NO, NO, NO, AWWW NO!" PRODUCTION ASSISTANT: "Don’t yell at me, Jon! I’m sorry, I just think it’s a good question!" RHINE: "No, it’s fine, Shannon. All right, Don, ask me her question." STUCK: "Now, ever since you put on the tights and started wrestling for FUSE and then after when it became SCCW, bad luck and personal catastrophe has seemed to follow you around like stink on you-know-what. You’ve won the title, but you’ve also had to deal with betrayal, personal attacks, and facing your own demons and mistakes. How are you able to continue performing at such a high level when you’re dealing with so much on your plate?" [Five full seconds of dead air.] STUCK: "Told you, Jon. It’s deep." RHINE: "Yeah, real deep. Thanks for the question, Shannon." PRODUCTION ASSISTANT: "I’m sorry!" RHINE: "No, no, it’s a great question. I guess the answer is…you have to set aside time for each part. And since my life is so hectic now, I have to leave the personal problems behind for as long as possible. Because let’s look at it this way. If I end up facing Aimz and defending my title, she isn’t going to care that my life is in shambles. She’ll relish in it. Even if it wasn’t someone who wanted me dead; say Hunter Sabuani or BloodAngel. They aren’t going to concern themselves that I don’t know what’s going on outside of the ring. They’re going to put on the fight of their life, because it’s a job. SOUND EFFECT: "Gonna Fly Now", the theme from Rocky, plays in the background. RHINE: "Funny, Don. But seriously, the way I stay on top of everything – and by no means am I saying I do a great job of it all the time – is I treat this like any job I’ve had. You have to be able to separate the spheres. Let’s say you get into a fight with Veronica before you come into work." STUCK: "You mean you heard us this morning?" RHINE: [laughing] "You’re not going to let it affect your performance here, right? You’re not going to just sit here and mope about the fight you had all morning." PRODUCTION ASSISTANT: "Are you kidding? He’s a radio jockey, his entire life is based on moping." [Raucous laughter is heard.] RHINE: "But you know what I mean. When you’re working for someone, you do your job and try not to let the rest of your life get in the way. And honestly, with Katie and everything else that’s going on, that’s the only way I can survive right now." STUCK: "And you’re doing well now, fresh off a great defense against Christian Darke. Ladies and gentlemen, this has been another great talk with Indianapolis’s third favorite son behind Peyton and Tony, Jonathan Rhine. Jon, thanks for stopping by, and don’t forget to stop in even though you’re a West Coaster now." RHINE: "I’ll be back often, Don. I promise." STUCK: "Jonathan Rhine, everyone! We’ll be right back." Until now, you’ve been able to balance everything with a smile, and it’s worked for you. You had a title, a stable, and a loving girlfriend. Now one is gone, one is fading, and the other is being threatened at an upcoming show. You wonder if the reason you had been so stable in dealing with all of the problems thrown at you was that you were being supported by Katie. Without her, it seems hard to think about Aimz, about Lindsay Troy, about anything that is in your way come Snake Eyes. But you have to, because like you told Stuck, it’s expected of you. And even if it wasn’t, it’s the only thing to keep your head from exploding. The only thing that keeps you strong. So you think about Katie. You make your plan to redeem yourself. And you see her back with you every time you close your eyes. But you also push yourself up. You push yourself up until your arms go numb, until the sweat blinds your eyes, until you hear a pounding at the door, until your former trainer is yelling at you to get up and stop ignoring him. Because you just can’t give up. Not now, and not ever. Thirty Minutes Earlier "So why are we driving halfway across the city to help the supposedly strongest and toughest guy in SCCW?" Shway asks rather loudly, trying to hear herself over the wind that’s whipping her dark hair in her face. Foster laughs. "He’s not the toughest guy, and that’s the problem. He tries to be, but everything gets to him, probably more than anybody else I know. He just takes things…so hard." "Like how?" "Well, it’s like he’s that kid who you tell not to touch the stove." Shway laughs, shaking her head. "Aren’t you supposed to be some great public speaker or something? You need to avoid clichés like the plague, baby." "Shut up," Foster says through a grin. "Anyway, he’s got problems, but he isn’t going to ask for help or take the easy way out. He has this one mode of thinking, and he takes what he is given and works with it all inside that mode." "How is that like the kid and the stove?" "Because he doesn’t want to take anyone’s word for it. He’s going to hear you, nod, pretend he knows what you’re saying. But inside, Jonathan Rhine is dying to touch the stove for himself. He wants to know that it’s hot, he wants to feel the heat that everyone is talking about. Because he works only in one way: the straight path. And sometimes it’s the hardest one to get through." Shway nods. "So that’s why he’s scheduled to fight twice in two of the biggest fights in his life?" "Pretty much," Foster says, shrugging. "He was put in a situation with Aimz, and he only saw one way out; fight for some whore’s honor. And that involves fighting Aimz if that’s on the same night as his Unified Title match, it’s fine with Jon, because to him, it’s what’s right." "Oh." There’s a short pause as Shway checks her phone quickly before looking at Foster again. "No offense, but your little protégé seems to be a little…stupid." Foster takes his eyes off of the road to shoot a somewhat confused look at his new girlfriend. "What do you mean?" "He’s fighting two matches on one show, how much stupider can you get?" she says off-handedly, looking down to her phone as it buzzes. "Ooh, e-mail!" She smiles lazily as she furiously texts on the phone. Foster shakes his head, chuckling. "That damned phone, it’s like it’s glued to your fucking hand or something." Giggling, Shway puts her hand on Foster’s thigh and squeezes lightly. "That’s not true, Mr. Nackedy, I have other things in my hand sometimes too." Foster laughs, his teeth showing in a wolfish grin. "You’re certainly right about that. That’s something we’ll have to talk about after we get to Jon’s hotel – speaking of…" Foster turns into the parking lot as both he and Shway share a smile of true happiness. Will keep posted. -S -------- A few minutes later, both the incoming and the outgoing email were ruthlessly deleted in the restroom right outside Jonathan Rhine’s hotel room. When Shway enters the room, she smiles her biggest, brightest smile, almost too wide to be natural; her white teeth shine against her brown skin and thin, burgundy lips. "So this is Jonathan Rhine?" she asks Foster, stifling a giggle. "I bet you already stole my thunder by telling him I’m his biggest fan, didn’t you?"
