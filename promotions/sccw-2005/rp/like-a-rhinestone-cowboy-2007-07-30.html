Like a Rhinestone Cowboy

30 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30/07 "I shall know by the gleam and glitter Of the golden chain you wear, By your heart's calm strength in loving, Of the fire you have had to bear. Beat on, true heart, forever; Shine bright, strong golden chain; And bless the cleansing fire And the furnace of living pain"-Adelaide Proctor "I hate moving…" [That’s a voice the audience of FUSE should get used to, the voice of Prince Pride. It’s only recognizable because of the laid back and yet constantly "irritated" tone it carries. He’s recognizable because of his wild lion mane like black hair with blonde tips, his bronzed complexion noticeable because of the mixed heritage that he comes from, his naturally athletic frame filled with both mass and definition, looking more like an NFL tight end than he does, wrestler. His boyish looks hardened by his unpleasant disposition. We pan into an apartment that honestly isn’t much, a tattered couch to our left, a folding chair to our right, a small television with a VCR on top of it underneath a dusty window with a view of a brick wall. To the left is a small kitchen, the refrigerator so close to the stove that only one can open at a time, and a faucet that just won’t stop dripping, the lap of luxury it isn’t. Cluttering the rest of the apartment are boxes of tapes, tapes labeled "CSWA", "CWF", "NWC 1997", "NWC 2001", "FUSE 2007", some tapes scattered near the VCR, others collecting dust. Amidst all of this is Prince, kicking a box aside to flop down on the aforementioned couch.] "…Done it way too much". [Pride sighs a bit, as he shuffles through his mail, tossing a few aside he gets to the pieces clearly labeled "FUSE", one envelope he rips open with his teeth, it appears to be a check, Pride sneers at it…] [Scoffing] "I put my body on the line for this? I could have sold blood…" […Pride then opens the second piece, and it appears to be a "booking sheet" for the next week, Pride looks at it, and then looks into the camera, looks at it and then back into the camera, he crumples the paper up and then leans forward once again looking into the camera] "So, I guess this is why you guys are here?" [Pride smirks] "I’ve seen it done, a million times before, times when I would see my father on television, times like my graduation, times like my prom, times like my first…" [Pride reflects for a moment] "…You’re not here for that are you? According to my [miming quotes] "booking sheet", you’re here because I’m supposed to stare into the camera and talk about my opponent this week, the purist, the mans man, the wrestlers wrestler, Jonathon Rhine…" [Pause] "I’m supposed to spout out some random insults, he’ll respond and we’ll both go on pretending like we don’t already know the outcome…" [Pride gives a knowing glance into the camera] "…Of our verbal war, not the match." [Pride rolls his eyes] "Listen Rhine, I ain’t got no beef with you bro, you don’t know who I am and honestly, if it wasn’t for us [miming quotes] facing each other this week, I wouldn’t care who you were. You’re supposedly an accomplished wrestler, a [miming quotes] "champion", a secondary one at that, maybe even a little hardcore.…" [Pride scoffs and then golf claps] "Good for you, I’m sure when this business sucks the life out of you like it does everyone, I’m sure when your broke and destitute, and your family is left behind to pay the price for your selfishness, then and only then will that title mean something…" [Sic] "…when you have to sell it on E-Bay so you can afford the Number 3 at your local taco bell." [Pride shrugs] "I’m the best pure and real athlete this business has and will ever see. Your motivation is clear, you want to prove to the business and the fans that you’re the best, and whomever wins this week between us will move onto some big deal match in Washington for the opportunity to face the top champ." [Pride waves his hands at the camera, dismissively] "Whatever! I’ve got bigger plans for that night in DC, history making plans, plans bigger than you, plans bigger than me, plans dare I say bigger than this business. So come this week in [mocking southern drawl] South Carolina, you’ll hopefully bring that same fire and vigor you had last week, and this week you won’t have some group of sycophants to assist you in your win. Because this week, "champ", your reality, your REALITY, something this business knows little about, your REALITY is quite simply this…" [Pride looks into the camera] "…You’re Jonathon Rhine, you’re just a professional wrestler, and until this moment all was lost for you, all was lost until your name was lined up next to mine." [Pride smiles as he stands, walking towards the door and slowly opening it, and turning back towards the camera, his wild hair nearly taking up half of the shot] "…And who am I?" [Slight chuckle] "…I’m just the guy Jonathon Rhine that you and the rest of FUSE, and dare I say the wrestling business will know about soon enough." [Pause] "My name is Prince Pride, and I’m… [Pause] "….Here to Save You!" [Pride sneers as he grabs the camera by the lens and leads it out of his apartment, the shot shakes as the sound of the door slamming and the sight of the camera and most likely the man holding onto it falls on the ground and stares sideways at the dingy exterior of the apartment building as we fade…]
