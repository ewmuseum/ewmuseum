To The Future

Date: Today It wasn’t a place that he was used to. The pounding drum beats rattled inside his head as the pulsating lights were nearly blinding. For a down home country boy, a rave was not the place to be. But that’s where Jake Ennis found himself. He knew that he was looking for someone, and when he found that person, he could leave and go back to his hotel room, where he had some aspirin with his name written all over it. Girls sucking on pacifiers, like they were little babies. Grown men acting like they were the retarded stepchildren that he hoped to never have. Jake brushed against a few of them, the vinyl of their shirts giving him a feeling that he didn’t like. He wanted out of there, and in the worst way. He made it up to the bar, and decided that he was going to attempt to order a drink. But his voice couldn’t carry to the bartender over the loudness of the noise that the DJ was playing. Well, that’s what he thought of it anyway. He finally was able to get the bartender’s attention, which brought him over. The noise was still too loud, but the bartender was able to figure out what Jake wanted to drink. The bartender reached into a cooler behind him and pulled out a cold bottle of Molson. Not quite Jake’s choice in beers, but it was cold and wet. The bartender popped the cap off the bottle and handed it to Jake. Jake then turned his back to the bartender and looked over the club. The vantage point that the bar had allowed him to look over the entire floor. He then saw who he was looking for and made his way to catch up with them. He struggled through the sea of people as he was apparently making his way to the VIP room at the club. His first time at a place like this, and now he was going to have to convince an over glorified rent-a-cop to allow him in. This was definitely not a night that he found to be usual. The people in the club bumped into them, as they were dancing to the beat of their own drummer. A cowboy in the middle of a rave was as natural as a neo-nazi at the Apollo. To say that he stuck out was an understatement. As he reached the VIP room, he got up to the bouncer, "I’m here to see someone inside." --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- Seemingly on the other side of the world, Wyatt Colton was relaxing in the front seat of a Ford Taurus, making the night drive up to Whitehorse. He wasn’t driving; he had driven for the previous ten hours. Instead, Cammy was taking her turn at the wheel. She was going to meet Wyatt’s parents, and stay there while Wyatt traveled back to the States for the next KillZone. "Whose idea was it to drive up here, again?" Cammy asked with a slight chuckle. She had only been awake for two hours before she got behind the wheel. Her hair was pulled back in a pony tail and was wearing a FUSE Baseball Cap, a souvenir from the first show that she had been to. "Because I think that they owe someone a very nice gift." Wyatt turned his head and had a slight smile on his face, knowing that it was her idea that they take the drive. ‘I think that it would be romantic to drive out there.’ Cammy had said, not realizing how long of a drive it was going to be. "Well, we’ll be there soon enough. This is nothing compared to some of the all-nighters that I’ve had to pull in the past. At least you had a chance to get some sleep without having another person try to tell you to go west, when you really wanted to go east." It was a long drive. They had just filled up in Dawson Creek, British Columbia. They also had a chance to eat. Something that they knew that they weren’t going to be able to do for another few hours. But it was a relaxing car ride. "Are we going to get there in time for you to catch your first flight?" When they decided to go, Wyatt had booked a flight to Vancouver to get to Kentucky the morning of the show. This was starting to become normal, as Wyatt spent less time on the road than he used to. "It doesn’t leave until tomorrow night. I’ll make it in time." With that, Wyatt shut his eyes and went to sleep. --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- Once in the VIP Room at the club, Jake was finally able to get his bearings. The noise was somewhat muffled from outside of the room. Sitting at one of the long couches that made up the seating in the room was a man in a black suit drinking a glass of wine. Sitting next to him was a strikingly beautiful woman. Jake recognized the man as Anthony Collins, the man that ran the company that was putting solar panels on his land. The woman next to him, he had no clue who she was. "Jake, come, come. I’m glad that you could make it. I take it that the flight was a good one?" Mr. Collins had sent a private jet to bring Jake to this place. Jake wasn’t sure what city he was in, he didn’t have a chance to settle down. He was told simply that he was being brought in, and would spend the night at a local hotel and then flown out to Kentucky in time for the next FUSE KillZone broadcast. "I wouldn’t reckon you for a person that enjoyed this type of thing." Jake commented, his he grinned at the young woman sitting with Mr. Collins. "Little young, isn’t she?" Mr. Collins didn’t take too much into stock into Jake’s comments. For all the young woman could tell, Jake was nothing more than a dumb cowboy from the way that he was dressed. She continued to drink from her glass, and didn’t pay Jake any more attention than she had to. "Candy, please be a dear, and get me a cigar." The young woman had a frown on her face as she left the two men to talk. "There are some exciting things going on at your ranch. I heard that the panels have arrived and they’ve started to install them. Soon enough, we will hook them up to the generators to create the power of the world. You like that. My company is thinking of using that as our slogan. ‘The Power of the World.’ Has a nice ring to it, doesn’t it." "I don’t know about that, but I’m glad to see that you’ve been true to your word so far." "Well, I can’t be in this business if I’m not. After all, who would trust a man that reneged on his contracts? Pretty soon, you should see the fruit of your minor inconveniences, as power is going to pulse through your ranch like never before." "Well, only time will tell, Mr. Collins. Only time will tell." "But, I did want to discuss something with you about the land that we were going to be using. I was concerned that people are going to try and tamper with the panels." "Don’t worry about that, Mr. Collins. I’ve already got my people erecting a fence around the area, that won’t allow unauthorized people to enter the power farm." "Very good. Now, you understand that by your undertaking this, that you are going to save us some time to get everything started." "The quicker that you have your people off my land, the quicker I can get everything back to normal." --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- Daybreak over Western Canada is said to be one of the most beautiful sights that you would ever see. Wyatt had seen it thousands of times in his life. Living in the Yukon couldn’t compare to the life that he was now in Calgary. This was the first time that Cammy had seen such a sight though, and she was enamored by it. "Wow, I didn’t realize how beautiful this is." She was in awe of the sight. Living in the city her entire life, she didn’t know what she was missing until now. Wyatt knew all about it, and it didn’t phase him anymore. "This used to be how my mornings in the summer used to be. I would wake up and watch this with my dad. He never approved of my becoming a wrestler. I don’t think that he’s ever going to come to grips with it. I feel bad, because there is nothing that I would rather have than to see him after I win a big match, or hoist a singles title above my head. But I don’t think that’s ever going to happen." "Don’t beat yourself up over it. You’re doing relatively well for yourself. You have a hot girl. You should be happy with your life as it is, not worry about what he thinks of it." Cammy really wasn’t one for modesty. In fact, the second time that Wyatt ever saw her, she wasn’t wearing a top, and had called to ask him to help her out with something. But he knew that she was right about this. That he shouldn’t live his life how his parents wanted him to live it. But how he wanted to live it. If he lived the way that they wanted him to live, he would never have gotten out of Whitehorse. He would be stuck working at the television station, or in his dad’s shop. He didn’t like that idea. He enjoys traveling the world, not having to worry about being in one place at one time. He was just settling into the life of a tag team wrestler as well. He knew that his partner would be there when he needed him to be there, and that he didn’t have to be in the same place as he at the same time. That was the great thing about his life, while he had to rely on Jake Ennis, at the same time, he didn’t need to rely on him either. --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- --- Jake had once again arrived at the airport where the plane that brought him here was taking off from. He was still in the same outfit from the night before, he didn’t have much sleep thanks to the headache that he got the night before. He knew that the plane was going to be stocked with beer, and that’s all that he cared about. He had a day before he had to wrestle, so he was going to drink until the edge of the headache was gone. He took care of the business that he had to attend to with Mr. Collins, now he had business to attend to with the Alliance. It was a little over a day before showtime. He was going to try and get some sleep before meeting up with Araceli and Wyatt in the gym.
