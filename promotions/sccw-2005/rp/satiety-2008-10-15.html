Date: October 15th I would tell you about the things they put me through, The pain I've been subjected to, But the Lord himself would blush. The countless feasts laid at my feet, Forbidden fruits for me to eat, But I think your pulse would start to rush. Now, I'm not looking for absolution, Or forgiveness for the things I do. But before we come to any conclusions, Try walking in my shoes. ------------------------------------------------------------------- 1:03 AM Patrick Gymnasium Burlington, Vermont The college kids had all gone home after the show, but the courtyard was still littered with their refuse. The small screen SCCW set up for the fans outside in the freezing cold to watch was broken down to a desolate scaffolding. She didn't notice any of that, though. The heel of her boot dug into the small of her victim's back, causing a strangled moan, but Drusilla Devonshire was beyond caring, beyond thought. Mule-heeled boots crashed down into the prone victim's back, pushing down as she pushed off, briefly airborne on her hands before both knees collided with the other woman's ribcage. Devonshire wasn't the violent kind of crazy, not usually. She pulled strings and twisted lies, she preferred the whispered word and the quiet sigh to the closed fist, but digging her fingers into her victim's mane of hair was somehow satisfying after the night she'd been through, however. ''You bitch! You fucking HARLOT!'' Drusilla's voice bounced off the brownstones nearby, but the Vermontians were beyond sleep. Usually, she sounded sweet, caring even. The desperate longing in her words would have been heartbreaking if she wasn't jabbing both knees into a ribcage still sore from the schedule she put it through. ''You have everything and I have nothing! Nothing but her!'' ''I'm ma...'' Devonshire's victim yelped when the knees connected again. ''Married. To a man!'' It's not clear what got her into this situation -- considering she didn't actually remember anything between walking to her car and being on the ground, a head injury was the most likely cause -- but Amy Campbell was almost completely helpless. Even the best of fighters can't do much if they're overrun and, though Drusilla may not have been quite the wrestler that the Universal Champion was, there's been a little saying around for a while now. Something along the lines of 'Hell hath no fury like a woman scorned'. ''Stop! Jesus Christ, Dru!'' She gasped desperately and through bloodied lips for any air that came between the shots to her already injured torso. ''I didn't do anything! I only allowed it...'' Clearly, Malice wasn't in much of a listening mood - she took a moment to punch the side of Campbell's face, causing a gash where her other cheekbone collided with the ground. ''Fuck,'' Amy continued, practically screaming. ''I only let it go on to get back at Darcy!'' But she wasn't done. As weak and blindsided as she may have been by the angrier woman, Aimz is still very much a fighter. She was tired from fighting one match already, but there was just enough left in the tank to roll over and send a fist flying into Devonshire's jaw. ''I said stop, you fucking moron!'' Drusilla rocked back to her feet -- the moment of surprise was lost, and she would have been doomed if she allowed it to degenerate into a fistfight with the notorious brawler. Eyes wide and wild, blood in her mouth, Malice didn't stop, and her pointed-toe boot collides soundly with Campbell's ribcage. ''You didn't allow it, Amy.'' Briefly gathering herself, her voice goes from desperate to whispery to desperate again. ''You caused it! You pushed her! And I'm not going to stand for it.'' It's not her usual straight-razor (that's only for special occasions) but the switchblade a twist of her wrist snaps open does have a sharp point. Drusilla takes a step forward, triangular grin sliding back onto her lips. ''I got this special for you, Amy-grrl!'' She waves the blade through the air. ''Won't be traced to me, and you'll be just another junkie whore in the gutter.'' Amy nearly laughed at first, but then she remembered who she was dealing with. All of a sudden, things were quite real -- Drusilla Devonshire was just crazy enough to use that weapon. She didn't have the voice of reason that had caused Amy to back down in a similar situation. ''If you're gonna use that, you'd better fucking hope you kill me.'' There was a problem that just made itself apparent -- while Drusilla lacks a voice of reason to keep her from such psychotic acts, Campbell lacks a similar voice in her head, and it happens to be the one that warns her when to shut her mouth. For some reason, her ego doesn't consider the situation as grave as it is... but that soon fades when the other woman straddles her, gliding the tip of the blade across her collar area. It's not enough to gravely injure Amy, but it draws enough blood and causes enough pain to send a clear message. Drusilla is, for lack of a wordier way to put it, fucking serious. ''Just st... that stings, please stop.'' Amy hissed, ''Just stop and think about it. Even if it can't be traced, people aren't stupid. I didn't push you. She did what she did and I'm sure she did it just to scare me, but she won't be able to protect you if you d--DAMNIT, that hurts!'' And, also for a lack of a better way to put it, Amy Campbell is in a lot of trouble. Even dumber than egging Malice on with backhanded comments from her ego was expressing the pain -- it fed the other woman, it gave her what she wanted. ''I had this whole emo speech prepared about how much it hurt me, but... pfft.'' She rolled her eyes theatrically. ''I wish we had more time, you know? We could have some fun.'' The parking lot was an empty place, random bits of white flicking through the lot, bathed in orange. It was a desolate, lonely scene to be sure. Until the presence of the man and two women in their direction, unaware of the situation changed everything. Six legs and a cane led Mr. Hawke, Kathryn Shaw, and Alexandra Pierce from Patrick Gym. They'd stayed to go over footage of the Spider's unexpected bout with Reginald Lovecraft (and because Shaw had ''like a million questions'' about a particular... meeting... between Desade and Aimz). ''Hamill's going to be a problem, and soon,'' Hawke said quietly. ''We need to feed him back into the pit, further down.'' ''I could...'' Shaw offered, but the man with the hawk-headed cane was quick to interject. ''Your illicit affairs began this problem, Kathryn.'' This was how it went, usually -- the Hand debated the situation and Pierce made the decision; rarely did she give her own opinion, for fear it would sway the decision being made. That being said, she was awfully quiet, and you can bet that Kathryn Shaw would have railed about that later on this evening. Except for a little altercation in the lot. Men prattle on about the pain of being hit between the legs, and that's fine, but Amy was gaining a new appreciation for that when Drusilla pressed her knee down there, still holding the knife - which was getting much closer to the redhead's throat. It was lucky that Kathryn Shaw loved heels, because the snaps of her foot on the pavement alerted Campbell to her opportunity, one she hadn't had to openly take in years. ''GET. OFF. OF. ME.'' She roared behind gritted teeth and shut her eyes, just in case the real cuts started coming. That was Amy Campbell screaming for help in her own way - loud enough for the owner of the approaching heels to hear, and not quite pleading enough to pain her dignity. ... But when the tip of the knife reached a point at Amy's throat, the barely-there cuts on her collarbone became clearly just a warning shot. Dignity went out the window, and her follow-up was a near death howl, ''Somebody GET HERE! This is NOT a part of the fucking sho--'' Thankfully, what followed was simply a slap to her face and not a knife through her throat, though the recoil of the strike caused the edge of the blade to draw blood again. ''My affairs aren't the problem, you know. Am I to be taken to task for the fact that I do my job well? You know, unlike -- '' It might have devolved into a full-on tiff; Lord knows Shaw and Hawke had a mile of old wounds between them. Then there's the scream, and it was Hawke that noticed first, since it's his job. The redhead-on-redhead violence drew a narrowed gaze, every detail taken in an instant. He produced a snapshot of the scene, but it wasn't hard to figure it out, as, even with a jacket to protect her arms from the cold, there are very few women who have that amount of ink on them. ''Shit,'' Hawke said, picking up his hobbling pace. ''That's Campbell.'' ''What?'' Katsidy glanced in that direction, eyes narrowing. She half-turned towards the position she expected Alexandra Pierce to be, a half-step behind them. ''Wait, isn't that -- '' Shaw interrupted herself, since the space behind them was empty, and there was no sign of the Director in front of her. ''She's gone ahead to stop this herself, it would seem,'' Hawke said. Even without that head start, Alex Pierce would have reached the scene first, as Hawke's leg hampered him, much as Shaw's heels. ''Shut up!'' Drusilla pressed herself up into a handstand again, knife loosely held against the asphalt. She dropped down with all her weight into a double-knee strike to the ribs. ''Do you really think someone is going to -- '' Alexandra Pierce's approach went unseen by Aimz' assailant; even Campbell saw only a white blur in the ill-lit lot. Desade solved a lot of problems with a carefully chosen word, but a flying tackle both got Malice's attention and separated her from utilizing Aimz as a shield or a bargaining chip. Plus it felt a whole hell of a lot better. Neither Desade nor Malice said anything. Twin dark gazes met across the SCCW Universal Champion; the younger girl rose to her feet and skulked off, snatching the knife up. ''Jesus fuck,'' Katsidy said as she caught up to Desade and looked down to Campbell, ''What the hell did you do?'' When she was satisfied that Malice was well in the distance, Amy tried to lift her head, but the sudden onset of one hell of a headache set her neck limp again. Her face was a mask of more pain than any of the group had seen her endure in a ring, though most of it was likely shock over the sudden attack, and it didn't look any better when she lightly ran her fingertips over her collar area, feeling the sting of small, but fresh and effective cuts. ''Nothing. She... Christ, the nutjob nearly knocked me out on my way to my car. Then...'' When she paused, it wasn't for effect - it was utter disbelief. Amy was used to dealing with punches and little hammers, not real weapons. ''I mean, a fucking knife?'' ''That's our Dru.'' Shaw watched the shadows Malice had disappeared into. ''She's a little bit... protective.'' ''She is a little bit crazy.'' Mr. Hawke was late to the party, folding both hands over the head of his cane. He looked to Pierce, who lingered nearby, and it's obvious that neither the woman in the turquoise dress nor the man in black would react until finally Pierce turned back to the scene of Malice's handiwork. ''Are you well?'' Alexandra asked softly. ''I can't see the cuts,'' the redhead answered frankly, ''But no. Either way, no. I've been in better car accidents. Just... just get me somewhere that can x-ray, I can take it from there.'' Pierce nodded, just once. ''I will take her for care.'' Shaw shot her best friend a look, brows lifted. ''We could all go, no problem.'' ''I said I would take her. You two have another important task.'' Mr. Hawke lowered a hand down to the prone SCCW Champion, lips curling just barely downward in something that's more the impression of displeasure than the actual thing. ''And what task would that be?'' ''Find Drusilla and gather her,'' Pierce instructed. '' I am afraid my presence may only exacerbate the situation and it is our best interest not to allow her to continue with something more devious and less direct.'' ''Gather her so I can knock her goddamn teeth out.'' Though she took the hand that Hawke had extended, Campbell very quickly found herself regretting it. Trying to pull to her feet not only made her dizzy, but reminded her of exactly the kind of beating she'd just recieved. She wore the hurt in her features because, had she doubled over in pain, she probably would have ended up face-first on the asphalt again. ''I'm not exactly sure how long I can stand upright, but just make sure Shaw doesn't touch any open w--DAMNIT, that bitch broke my ribs, didn't she?!'' There wasn't any hiding that Kathryn Shaw was slightly amused when the sudden pain in the champion's side hit her. ''You are in no condition to fight anyone, Amelia,'' Desade said sharply. ''We will see to your injured ribs and then we will determine where to proceed with Malice. It is high time you see my Hand away from the constraints of television cameras.'' Pierce moved beside Campbell, a much more stable leaning post than the man with the hawkheaded cane can provide. It took less than a moment (seven seconds, if anyone were counting), before the Spider's gray eyes snapped to her right hand. ''I said go,'' the eyes spoke without the Director having to move her mouth. With a nod, Shaw minced off, followed by the step-step-clunk of Hawke's cane. ''I dropped my bag by the car, I need that.'' Amy's breathing was slightly labored thanks to the tightness in her ribs, but it didn't stop her from glancing toward every street light nervously. ''And let's move before she decides to come back. I'm sure I can walk on my own.'' Campbell had been sure of a lot of things prior to that evening. Her ability to carry herself to the other side of the parking lot was just another of those failures. When she tried to wriggle away from Desade's assistance, she realized how dizzy the surprise attack, pain and loss of blood had left her. ''Okay... so maybe help is good.'' ''Easy,'' Desade cautioned, taking on the burden of the weight as the two redheads moved towards Campbell's rental. ''I will drive -- and do not attempt to argue otherwise.'' Pierce stooped slightly, picking up the Universal Champion's bag and slipping its straps over her shoulder. ''Fine. I'll try not to bleed on the seat. Not sure how well vanilla leather holds up against Canadian type O.'' Alexandra tossed the bag into the back seat, easing the Universal Champion into the passenger's seat and crossing briskly to the driver's side. A twist of the key later and the pair swooped off into the chill Vermont night. ------------------------------------------------------------------- You'll stumble in my footsteps. Keep the same appointments I've kept. If you try walking in my shoes. ------------------------------------------------------------------- 1:03 AM Motor Inn Burlington, Vermont Her ribs hurt, and Desade was taking an awfully long time with the night manager at the roadside motel. When the mismatched eyes of the champion finally caught the darkly-dressed woman stepping out of the office, Pierce was spitting out a piece of Juicy Fruit and unbinding her bloody-red hair. Of course -- it's not like Desade would have felt safe getting a room as herself. ''We have a room,'' Pierce said simply as she opened the door, easing Campbell out of the vehicle and towards the darkened hallway, fluorescent light flickering overhead to cast poor, paltry shadows. Room number twenty-three opened up stubbornly; the motel hadn't upgraded to keycards yet, so Pierce had to use an actual metal key, and the warped wood of the door needed a solid shoulder to be forced open. If the pained champion was concerned that she was being led into a motel room instead of a hospital bed at the emergency room, she didn't say anything. This was a condition that lasted only twenty-seven seconds, which was, admittedly, close to a record when these two women were together. Though even the impact on the hard mattress drew a yelp from her, Amy regained her brave face upon laying down. During the wait for Desade to acquire the room, she'd taken a little assistance from her purse in the form of a white powder. It hadn't done much more than give Campbell the strength to stay conscious while the adrenaline of the night's match and attack rushed out of her system. ''Alright. I'll sleep it off. Call me if you...'' She admittedly hadn't noticed the still-bleeding gash on her lower stomach, near her hip until she shrugged out of her bloodied zip-up hoodie. When Pierce had tackled Malice, the knife must have nicked Amy again, only deeper. It was hardly a risk of internal injury, but she pulled the hem of her stretched and torn t-shirt down to press against the wound, clenching her jaw against the sting of the applied pressure while she finished her sentence. ''Call me when you find the bitch.'' ''I am not leaving, Amelia.'' Pierce's words were softer than Amy's. ''You are bleeding at this very moment, and whatever you think of me or my motives, I do not leave any ally behind. It is through loyalty I earn respect. I can tend to your wounds, if you will let me. My training included more than just lessons in being an evil manipulator and the ability to compartmentalize myself into other personas, you know.'' The next thing Amy remembered was Pierce setting a blue-and-white first aid kit onto the bed beside her. If she'd left to get it, the Red Raver didn't remember it, but she was felling a little light-headed from the wound and the cocaine. ''Lift up your shirt,'' Pierce instructed, sliding to her knees on the thin carpet of the floor. ''If I had the energy to argue right now, I would be. I just want that to be on record.'' Scarier than the request was that Aimz actually trusted Alexandra enough to comply with it. There was little choice in the matter, of course, because all the bravado in the world wouldn't stop bloodflow or the fact that she'd just been through hell at the hands of somebody else. ''Your girlfriend isn't really... well, sane.'' It was strange, though -- in that moment, things seemed to click. As she watched Desade gather sanitizing wipes from the first aid kit, all of Amy's adrenaline turned to fear and the understanding of how truly outlandish her situation was. ''Wait. You...'' The smaller redhead would have jumped to her feet when she saw the bottle of peroxide in Desade's hands... if she could have. Instead, the motion was more like painfully sliding her person a few inches away from the Director with panic in her eyes. ''You set me up for that, didn't you?'' Pierce's brow only lifted in response to what she saw as a ridiculous question. Of course, Amy elaborated. ''Why else would you have done what you did? You knew as soon as you got within an inch of me, she'd be out for blood... and what better way to get back at me for calling you out like I did?'' It should be mentioned that, among many other things, cocaine can cause paranoia. But in all fairness to drugs, so can being jumped in a parking lot by a knife-weilding maniac. ''Don't come near me with that fucking kit,'' she hissed, ''Is this just you finishing the job? You didn't even yell at her and she just took off. What the fuck is that? You planned it.'' Pierce's hand retreated, tossing the bottle of peroxide into the plastic box, where it landed with a bang. ''You are such a pain in the ass, Amelia. Sometimes, as they say, a spoon is just a spoon. While I cannot deny that I could have said something to Drusilla, I am not an idiot, and you of all people should know this. Logic may not be your strong suit, but my intentionally causing you harm is lacking. If you were incapacitated and the Universal Championship voided for some ludicrous cluster bout, I would likely have to hand over my bracelet just for an invitation, such is Nigel's lack of esteem for me. You 'calling me out' in public would require a public response, not a knife in the parking lot.'' She set her hands on the bedspread, pushing up and back to her feet. Pierce looked down at Campbell, eyes nearly entirely hidden by the way the shadows fell through her curtain of hair. ''But if you wish to continue disbelieving me, then I can go and you can continue to bleed here in a motel room, alone. If I was foolish enough to sic an uncontrolled monster on you, then you would have to wonder if she would be able to find this motel. The choice, as ever, is yours.'' It truly did look like Amy had made her choice, but when she tried to sit upright she was reminded - once again - why she was there in the first place. It was immensely frustrating to the redhead to have to leave herself so vulnerable but, despite Desade offering, she had very little choice in the matter. Alexandra Pierce was, quite literally, all she had at that moment. ''I'm sorry,'' Amy winced, ''I just... well, it hasn't exactly been the most sane night of my life. On the upside, my hatred for wearing bras off camera seems to have paid off -- if something was pressing against my ribs, I probably would've kicked you in the face just then.'' The chuckle she spared wasn't shared, and hurt like a bitch to boot, but it didn't stop her from continuing. ''I do need some help with that -- my ribs, that is. How bad are they?'' Pierce's hand brushed against Amy's ribcage, causing a quiet yelp from the proud champion. ''Gah! Cold gloves!'' Campbell muttered in a sorry attempt to mask the noise that the pain drew from her. With an exasperated sigh, the Director tugged off her gloves again, setting them neatly on the other side of the kit. Expert fingers caused gentle pressure along the ribcage. ''They are not broken. Perhaps cracked. She did the handstand into the knees, I presume?'' No response came from Amy on the bed. ''I taught her that.'' Pierce's words were soft, somehow regretful -- it was one of the closest things to a sincere apology that Aimz had ever heard from her. ''I should have known.'' ''Oh, that's precious. Couples therapy through teaching her how to maim people.'' Amy sneered, ''And I feel like a tomcat went to town on my chest. My hip... gah, is that still bleeding? You really know how to pick 'em. I think she was actually gonna try to kill me... you know that, right? That was more than wanting to scare me or fuck up my ri-- stop pressing there! If they'd punctured anything, I'd be spitting up blood instead of just drinking it from my lip. Leave it alone.'' She gasped for air after shifting slightly on the bed, putting a hand back down to her lower abdomen to cover the worst of the cuts. ''You might not have planned this, but it was your fault.'' ''I know.'' Pierce's hand retreated. She pivoted to look again through the box. ''I should have expected Drusilla to react poorly, I will be cleaning up this mess for some time, Quinn will likely object strongly, and I still have to worry over Sykes. But...'' She flicked her head up behind the white lid, hair knocked away from her eyes. ''I wanted to.'' Silence echoed in both woman's ears as the Spider found what she was looking for and, oddly for the taciturn mastermind, she kept talking. ''I am the worst kind of genie, granting wishes and making dreams come true in an effort to pervert people into my own vision of the world. I rarely get to think about me, and that turned out so well for all involved.'' ''You're somehow a lot scarier when you're showing emotion, even the tiniest bit, y'know. Maybe I just shouldn't have pushed you to react to something all... out there, for people to see. It was strange and scary and we shouldn't ever let something like that happen again, but... well, I think it's kinda clear that was a can of worms that should've been left shut.'' The smaller redhead grinned nervously, skirting the subject had never been her strongest suit, but approaching Desade's earlier actions more directly was frankly terrifying. It didn't help that the Director was so keen on something inside the first aid kit. ''And... what're you doing? You can keep talking, there's no need to do anything right now. Well, I'm kinda losing blood, but you with anything sharp isn't exactly---okay, just tell me what you're thinking of doing, because I can't see in that little box and speculation's not getting me anywhere today andand... wait, you wanted to?'' The Director ignored the question, producing a traumatic needle and a box of sutures with something of a resigned look. ''You are not going to like this.'' ''I-- WHOA, not happening.'' They both knew one of the cuts demanded stitches but, even in hospitals with trained doctors, Campbell needed to be heavily sedated before anything resembling a medicinal needle went near her. Amy whimpered and drew back, thankful that the worst of her rib injuries were facing Desade when she rolled over, back to the Spider, and became relatively fetal while shaking her head. ''Not. Fucking. Happening. Put that shit away. Let's... let's talk more about you! Like how you kissed me and why you just said you wanted to and... and I'd better hear that kit click shut soon, 'cause you're not coming near me with any fucking needles. I'd have to be bound and gagged and... just not happening.'' Her attempt to change the topic was comedic at best, still facing away from Pierce. ''So... how's things? You were talking about your feelings a second ago, that was a real connection. We should chat.'' Pierce climbed onto the bed, pulling the Red Raver's now furiously kicking legs straight and pinning them to the mattress. ''Hold still, Amy. Goddamn it. Hold fucking still.'' Alex sat on the champion's leg. ''We can talk after I am certain that you will not bleed out here in a hotel room.'' It was laughable for the smaller of the two to try wrestling away in her condition, so her attempts to get out of the unfashionable piercing were verbal and quite pathetic. ''I think you putting needles in me might be a little too intimate for our professional relationship, though.'' Neither Alexandra Pierce nor Amy Campbell will be winning awards for mat wrestling, but Campbell's resolve was drug-fueled and allowed her to rock slightly (and painfully) forward, hooking her hands under the taller woman's armpits and attempting to pull her off. ''Stop it, stop it, stop--ow.'' ''Fine,'' Amy finally relented, pretending it was by her own accord and not simply because, in her state, Desade was much stronger. ''But you should know I owe you a really stiff kick in the box - as if there's anything there - for this, and if I hear of Shaw or Savant or especially Drusilla finding out I'm afraid of needles, so help me...'' Forgive Pierce if she didn't trust Campbell and left her pinned to the mattress as she worked the needle. Easiest solution to deal with a recalcitrant patient? Distract them. ''To answer your question,'' she said. ''I did want to. I could not explain my reasons, any more than I believe many of Kathryn's gentleman callers could, but in that moment, I could think of little else.'' She worked the needle with a remarkably gentle hand, into and out of the flesh with as little discomfort as possible. ''Despite your protestations to the contrary, Amy, I am fully aware of the fact that I am a human being under this shell. It is simply not something I let out or can afford to let out. In many people, I believe that would be a detriment to the psyche, so I tamp the fires down and focus on my work to the point of obsession. I have to be reminded to eat on occasion.'' Campbell had to fight to keep her eyes opened, because little bit of light and distraction the room around her provided was the only thing keeping her from fainting. ''That's a strange thing for you to think about at all, but it's also on me a little -- I might've gotten carried awa--JESUS, watch it!'' She staved off the urge to strike the woman on the other end of the needle, continuing. ''It kind of just took over and... yeah, it was too much. I would've done a line or two a few seconds ago, before this needle bullshit, but it does things... pushes buttons that don't need to be.'' A moment of silence let her breathe, though the air hissed in between tightly clenched teeth as she did her best to ignore the vomit-inducing sensation of steel under flesh. ''I was stone sober earlier, and look what happened -- I'm doing the time for a crime I stopped before I could even commit. Not that I would, but... fucking hell, now it's all in my head and I've got Alex fucking Pierce giving me stitches because somebody saw how much I wanted to... well, how much it LOOKED like I wanted to -'' Amy paused when Pierce's hands did, and she felt the pressure that she prayed was the tying off of a final stitch. ''Please say you're done. Between the pain and the awkward conversation, I think I'm starting to lose my mind. I think I need more drugs, I shouldn't be awake right now.'' In case it wasn't obvious, the smaller of the two redheads reacts to pain and nerves by incessantly rambling. ''I can't believe I almost did that earlier. I mean, if we had more than those few minutes -- if I hadn't... I can't believe I'm even rationalizing this. It was stupid. We're both idiots. That could've gotten very bad and you... you weren't even gonna stop it, were you? And now I'm here and I've got busted ribs and I don't think there's a part of me that doesn't hurt. Darcy was mad, but he still doesn't feel threatened. I wouldn't cheat. You might've given me a good excuse to act that way, but... ugh. This is a fucking mess!'' It wasn't as shocking as many of the moments in the past eight hours, but it was hardly commonplace when Campbell suddenly sneered and backhanded Desade. Thankfully for Campbell, the sharp tug had been the tying off of the final stitch, because the backhand straightened the Spider remarkably quickly. She was almost dislodged from Amy's legs as she rocked backwards, though she leaned back in quickly, her hands finding Campbell's wrist. The scuffle that ensued would never have found its way onto a four-star match list, as a quieter, somehow emotional Pierce and an injured Campbell rolled over on the bed, Amy's smaller frame and drug-fueled athleticism able to pull her on top of the other woman. She sneered from her back, looking up at the woman. ''I am hearing only excuses, Amy.'' She hadn't called Aimz ''Amelia'' in some time. ''I am not saying that I wish to duplicate the action.'' But she's not saying she doesn't. ''I am saying that a fire starved for oxygen often burns out of control when it finds what it seeks. The words mean little, but I am sorry you were injured by my rash decision.'' The fifteen minute high from the little bit she'd taken wearing off, all Amy could process in that moment was the location of her tote bag. It was beside the first aid kit, inches away and gave her opportunity to unball the fist she'd been getting ready to throw at Desade. Wordlessly, she produced what looked like a tube of mascara - the kind of thing she'd normally not be caught dead with on her person - raising it to her nose for a moment. It was in that moment that she sighed with relief and collapsed back partially. ''I should... I should break your fucking nose for all of this shit. She was going to kill me, all because you had to go open that fucking floodgate.'' The high was nearly instant and, while it may have numbed the slight bit of reasoning she had, it also numbed a lot of the pain Drusilla had inflicted. Though she still struggled with Pierce, it gave the woman a moment to regain the upper hand against the injured redhead. ''And she will pay for that decision, Amy. She is aware of that, and that is why she ran, because she knows I will be chasing her.'' Campbell bucked against Pierce, pulling the Spider's hand to her mouth and biting Alex's wrist -- not a playful bite, but an honest-to-God-gonna-tear-a-chunk-of-your-flesh-free bite. With a swallowed yelp from the Director, Aimz' hand came free and she reached out for a weapon, something that she could use. Instinct said she had to get free -- sometimes, that man with the ''DANGER!'' sign was loud in her ear. The trouble was the only thing within reach was a small tube -- a carefully disguised mascara tube. Campbell tried to lock her ankles behind Desade, to force the woman away. She pushed upwards with a hand as Alexandra used her free hand to try to pry loose the stubborn thighs of the champion. Her other hand stretched out, fingers wrapping around her tiny little tube. At the same time, three things happened. Desade yanked Amy's wrist, pulling away the hand that was pressing into her face at the same time she loosened the makeshift bodyscissors. Aimz thrust up her other hand, tiny tube leading, managing to free her hand enough to partially cover Alex's lips. Instinct caused inhalation, Pierce's lungs screaming, ''Fill me!'' Her partially blocked mouth meant the brain defaulted to the nose... and Alex's eyes widened as she realized she hadn't just inhaled air. ''Shit.'' That word was a mainstay in Amy's vocabulary, usually preceeded by an 'Oh', 'Aw' or some sort of expletive. In this case, it was simply an indication of how broken the explaination that followed was to be. ''I... you just... that was. Uh.'' The scuffle ended very abruptly, as Campbell fell beside Pierce in the horrible realization 'I just gave Desade cocaine'. ''I guess it can't hurt.'' She reasoned, ''It just might... you might get crazier and... I should really go before you kill me.'' Still wounded, but attended to enough that she wasn't bleeding or at any incredible risk, the redhead rolled off the bed and got shakily to her feet. She ignored retrieving her bag and instead made a straight rush for the door. Well, it would have been more of a rush had she not fallen in weakness after three steps. Since both nostrils were open, she hadn't taken more than a few granules, but Pierce's nerve endings sang as Campbell clambered away. The Spider's breath coming in quiet, labored gasps as the coke wormed its way into her system. On her hands and knees on the bed, Desade's lips split into a wide, sick grin. She rolled off the bed, hopping down with a little chuckle, and crossing the distance between the two women in a flash. Amy scrambled up to her knees as she looked back, fear and pain mixing in her eyes. They only got wider when she realized how close the Director was. Alexandra pushed the hair out of her face, dropping to her knees beside the champion. Aimz trembled slightly as Alex reached out her hand to touch her face. ''Killing you would make my problems go away,'' Desade mused. ''But I've...I have done enough of that in my life, I think. Never solves anything.'' Half of Sin City would have loved this moment. Two of the most proud, most annoying, most hated people in the federation on their knees in a seedy hotel room, one gasping from pain and drugs and fear and one from just the drugs. The admission -- that Alex had killed -- hung between them, registering dimly in Amy's eyes. It wasn't clear what had scared her more - the admission, or the contraction - but Campbell didn't dare move. ''I really want to think that was a joke. I don't, but I want to. It doesn't help that the last time we were pissed off ended up in... well... what lead to here. You, uh... are you okay?'' Pierce nodded, brushing her hair back from her face again, slower than the usual snap of her head. ''Fine. It is not... not really my first time.'' She crawled over to Campbell, visibly pressing down her grin. They ended nose-to-nose. ''Just a rush of... of sensations. Emotions. I will recover. You...'' She brushed a hand gently through Amy's hair and around her ear. ''I will not harm you. You know that, assuredly.'' Her hand settled onto Campbell's shoulder. ''You should be in that bed.'' ''I know I should be,'' sighed Amy, ''I feel like death. But I standing isn't my strong suit right now, and you're kinda being gentle for somebody I just bit, and... well... I'm honestly still fucking terrified. I ramble when I get nervous and I've been talking non-stop since you... since what happened earlier tonight, and my whole 'fight or flight' system's broken because I know not thinking and talking and keeping myself moving might result in...'' She paused, mainly for air between her run-on sentences, but Amy hoisted herself onto the bed with a less-than-happy cry before she continued. ''For fuck's sake, why'd you ever have to go and kiss me in the first place, Alex? You know I'm impulsive and stupid and... you're just lucky I fucking stopped it all!'' The next pause was to situate herself on the bed, laid out flat on her back to avoid any further strain on her ribs. Her body was screaming for restful sleep - for something to heal it - but the drugs kept her eyes open, giving the redhead enough presence of mind to reach back down to her stomach. ''And my idiotic fight with you's got those stitches bleeding. You kissed me, I bit you, you tried to fix me and I drugged you. We're certainly a dynamic duo.'' '''Dynamic' is certainly one term to use.'' Pierce climbed onto the bed beside Amy. ''And that is part of our... our difficulties. People judge, people criticize and pick and twist at the truth, and, over time, humanity gets used to it, so that the typical man and the typical woman live their lives with one question hanging overhead. 'But what will they think?' I used to be like that, Amy. Tied down to typical opinions, concerned about other people's opinions of my actions. Then my eyes were opened and I realized that no one else matters.'' Amy rolled her eyes, scooting painfully back into the pillows. ''I really hope there's a point to this, Alex. I checked in with Darcy, he thinks I'm coming home tomorrow night and everything's fine. It was a lot easier to call my part of what happened a childish play at revenge than me losing control. Better for him, too. I'm not sure exactly how much about 'Desade's girlfriend tried to murder me' I can lie about, though, and... and we're both kind of rambling now. So get to your point before we crash and I pass out.'' Alex turned on the bed, rolling up to a knee to face the champion. ''My point is that you are altogether too concerned with your appearance and too controlled with your emotions.'' Her words were pointed, her tone arched. Aimz' brows scrunched up in confusion. ''Uh, Alex? This is me you're talking about. I don't really know what you could be saying since I'm, y'know... the exact opposite of too controlled. Most of the time, at least. When... uhm, when I can barely fight back and people I'm afraid of are getting this close to me in a seedy motel, maybe a degree of control wouldn't hurt.'' Pierce dipped her head, hair falling back in front of her face. A soft chuckle passed through her lips. ''I am saying...'' Desade crawled across the bed, pressing the first aid kit firmly closed and knocking it away. ''I am saying... shut up.'' Maybe it was the drugs speaking. While this wasn't technically Alexandra's first time with cocaine, it had been years and years; before she was ''Desade''. So the rush was intoxicating and she crawled towards the champion. ''I don't know what you're planning, but...'' ''I'm planning to shut up,'' Pierce said -- whispered, really. ''You should try it.'' There was sudden shock washing across Campbell's face. She'd never heard Desade as Desade use a contraction -- always so proper with 'I am', 'You are', 'Do not' -- without correcting herself. ''Maybe you shouldn't relax so much...'' Amy backed up as far into the pillows as she could, shaking her head. Pierce was suddenly in front of her with her lips parted and her smile soft. Her left hand found the hem of Aimz' shirt, fingers across Amy's abdomen. The right cupped the Raver's face, thumb brushing against her cheek. ''Alex.'' Amy couldn't do more than whisper. ''The last time this happened, I got -- '' ''Shut up.'' Pierce's hand slipped up under the shirt, reaching higher. ''I got stabbed. And you're not seriously going t-'' ''Shut. Up.'' As if every word were a challenge, Amy's hesitance only drove the Director forward, finally quieting the redhead with her lips. Campbell used all the strength she could - while she could - to fight Desade back, but aside from a few muttered ''No''s, there was little she could do and even less she truly wanted to do against the advances. Finally, she toppled into a hardly terrible fate with famous last words, having been wrestled out of any remaining moral code.
