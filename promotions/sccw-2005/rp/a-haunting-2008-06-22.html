A Haunting...

22 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/2008 Have you ever had a feeling like someone was following you? Maybe following isn’t the right word…the first thing most people think of when you say that is you have a stalker, especially in this business. This is much different than that. It’s more like being haunted. Everywhere you look you see something that reminds you of someone. Or worse yet you become so consumed with it that you actually start to see the person among the faces in the crowd. You become paranoid chasing down total strangers on the street, as you get closer you see someone else and when you take a second look the first person is gone. Or how about when you do close in on the elusive stranger, you are out of breath from anticipation, your heart is pounding so hard you feel like it will burst out of your chest. Your hand reaches out and just brushes their shoulder. Just the thought of the slight touch causes the butterflies in your stomach to scramble like a squadron of fighter pilots. Your blood pressure rises causing your temples to throb like a bass drum pounding out a steady beat. Beads of sweat dance along your forehead. You’ve come so close and this time you will get to the bottom of this. You grasp their shoulder and spin them around only to come crashing back down to reality. The blood rushes from your head causing you to become faint and stumbling to find the words to apologize but half way through your incoherent babbling you catch something out of the corner of your eye and the race starts all over again. You try to run after them but your legs feel like spaghetti and don’t want to work quite the way they should. Then you see someone else….and someone else…and someone else your mind begins to race, you don’t know which one to follow you fall into a downward spiral until….. …you sit straight up in the middle of your own bedroom. Still feeling the panic you franticly look around the room to make sure that this is real. You check the time on the clock, you bolt out of bed and pull back the curtains checking for more familiar surroundings. Semi-relieved you take a couple of deep breaths and regain the strength in you legs and make your way back to your bed. This seems like a pretty harmless nightmare…unless you have been living it. Both in the daylight as well as the night, just as Zakk Wylde has been since his big press conference last week. Wylde leans over the rail on the balcony just outside his home office at his estate on the outskirts of Las Vegas. The sun is just beginning to dip below the horizon and soon it will be time to go to bed. No matter how much he tries to not think about it or how much he has to drink it won’t make this nightmare go away. Everywhere he’s been from the press conference to the airport on the way to and from last weeks show, to the sidewalk just outside of Wyld Child Enterprises, to the nightclubs along the strip…everywhere he goes he sees her face. Even his own bed, the one place that he should be comfortable and let his guard down, her face haunts him. Wylde takes a deep breath and returns into his office and sits down behind the giant oak desk and pulls up a video of his press conference from last week on his laptop. He has watched this video a million times and each and every time he shakes his head…not wanting to believe yet strangely wanting to believe at the same time. He pauses and rewinds the video over and over each time hoping to confirm or deny his thoughts. Finally he breaks the cycle only because the phone on his desk buzzes persistently. Zakk Wylde: Wylde here. Yeah I did leave a message for you like a million years ago!! Did you get the file that I sent over?? His brow furrows as he tries to focus on his conversation but his mind is still stuck on that video. Wylde: I’m not a moron when it comes to technology so I know that I’m paying you all quite well over there…so I suggest you break out that new facial recognition software and compare it to the photos that I sent…I want some God Damn answers and I want them yesterday! Wylde slams the phone down and stares at the blurry still photo staring back at him from his laptop. Wylde: (to himself): This isn’t what it appears to be. Someone has to be screwing with me. They seem to be doing a damn good job of it too. Look at me. I’m only a few days from my next match and I’m no where ready to take on a member of the Dead Mans Hand even if it is Jadian Bridden. I’m totally out of my normal routine. I’ve got to get some answers soon and clear my head so I can focus on my match. I didn’t fight off Gideon and his pack of wolves’ legal team to let this drag me down. I’ve got some momentum going here and I can’t afford to lose it, if I’m ever going to make an impact on SCCW I’ve got to keep my head. Someone has to pay for this….if this is someone’s idea of a sick joke…they WILL pay! What is it about her that I can’t put behind me? It’s been almost five years now and I still can’t stop thinking about her. The sun has finished its decent and darkness has spread across the horizon leaving Wylde sitting in his darkened office, the only light coming from his laptop. With a heavy sigh, he slams the laptop closed and leans back in his plush chair and waits for the nightmare to begin again.
