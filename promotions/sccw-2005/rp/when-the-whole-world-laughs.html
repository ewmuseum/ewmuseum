Date: 7-KZ46-07 It had been a bad week for Cyrus, after a monumental high of winning the Live Wire title, then loosing the it in his first defense, Cyrus was not happy to say the least. It was a warm summer afternoon in Lexington as Cyrus stepped out of the cab and walked to the entrance of the Rupp Arena, the site of KillZone 46. He walked to the security station, checked in and walked through the door. The first thing that caught Cyrsus' attention was the group of stage hands gathered in a circle to his left, huddled around the refreshment table. A chorus of laughter erupted from the group. One man facing the door noticed Cyrus and immediately straightened up and hit the guy next to him and pointed at Cyrus, by this time everyone had turned around, and cleared there throats, and stopped laughing. As Cyrus walked by one man decided to amuse his colleagues. 'Hey there champ, hows it going?' The man said as he stifled a laugh. Cyrus dropped his duffle bag, took two steps and was on the man. He grabbed him with both hands by his maroon button down shirt, turned a quarter turn and slammed the man into the concrete wall, lifting him slightly off his feet in the process. 'What did you say, punk?' Cyrus roared at the man. 'N-n-n-n-othing, just ask-ask-asking how its going, Cyrus.' The man stammered. 'Really? That's all you were asking? Sounded to me like you were tring to make of joke for your dumb little buddies over there! Tell me do I look like a joke ot you? ANSWER ME!!??' Cyrus screamed. 'N-n-n-n-no sir!' The man again stammered 'So you think its funny that I lost the belt? You think its funny that I got beat? At leat I held it you waste of skin! Tell me what have you ever done in your life that is worth mentioning?' Cyrus pauses a moment. 'Exactly! Nothing! You are a nobody, a never was and a never will be! Your pathetic, you are a useless lump of human existence! Your mother should have swallowed the night you were conceived!' Cyrus growls as he shakes the man. A couple of the mans friends jump in to save him, pulling at Cyrsu's arms and pleading for Cyrus to let him go. Cyrus turns and throws the man to the ground. The man slides about a foot then begins to scramble back wards. Cyrus points a finger at him and utters a final warning to him. 'Watch your self from now on boy, because that alligator mouth of yours almost wrote your humming bird behind a check it couldnt cash!' Cyrus says as he glares at the man. Cyrus walks by the man, picks up the duffel bag and heads twords his room again. Walking down the hall, its the same thing over and over again, laugther is heard until Cyrus walks by then the laughter abruptly stops. Jokes can be heard, snide comments, whisperings about Cyrus choking. Cyrus walks by one man who makes a comment to Cyrus about the belt. 'Hey Cyrus I heard ol Fred is putting all sorts of glitter and tinsel on the Live Wire championship thanks to you.' The old man chuckles as he walks away, leaving Cyrus visibly fuming at the comment. Cyrus finally makes it to his room where after the door is closed he throws his duffle bag at the locker at the far wall, walkes up to the set of lockers and punches the door of one, putting a notticable dent in the face of the locker. Grabs the latch of the door picks up his bag and chunks his it inside, and slams the locker door with enough force that it doesnt latch but instead bounces back, this irritates Cyrus even more then he already was, so he kicks the door as hard as he can, this time denting the locker again and slamming it shut. Cyrus sits on the bench and rests his head in his hands. With at sigh Cyrus decided he needed something to drink, he walked out of his room and down to the nearest Coke machine, as he was standing there tryng to decide on what to get, when a sound technician walked up behind him. 'Hey Cyrus I heard you was going to change your entrance song.' The man says chuckling. Cyrus doesnt even bother turning around since he knows a joke is coming. 'Oh yeah? What am I supposed to be changeing it to?' Cyrus sarcastically askes. 'Well I heard it was going to be 'One Week' by the barenaked Ladies' The man lets out a thunderous laugh. 'You get it, one week.........' 'Hey where you going?' The man askes as Cyrus turns and walks away. 'What's the matter with you, hungh? You can't even take a joke anymore?' The man yells after Cyrus. Cyrus walkes back to his dressing room as angry as ever, when he gets to the door he nottices that there is a big glittered up and tinseled star on his door with the words 'Live wire champion' Which the words have been crossed out with a red marker. Cyrus hangs his head in disgust and notices the red box at his feet for the first time. With a sigh he reaches down and picks the box up, and walks into his room, where upon opening the box there is a note inside that says 'Make it shine...............OOPS!' of course it wasn't signed by anyone.Cyrus just drops the box on the ground, with a disgusted look on his face, he was way to angry to respond to this right now. Cyrus laid down on the bench, and put a towel over his face, he just needed a little nap before the show started. Cyrus awoke to the sounds of screaming and cheering of the fuse fans, he sat up and looked around, the show had started already, and Cyrsus's match was coming up qucik. Cyrus stood up and walked to the locker where his bag was, he tried to open the locker which had been dented by his earlier tirade. But the door would not open now due to the dents and the force in which it was shut earler, Cyrus laid his head in the cold metal of the locker, screams, then grabs the top corner of the locker door and yanks the door off its hinges. Cyrus stands there holding the door in his right hand, breathing heavily. 'There we go, thats much better.' He pants. Cyrus reaches in and retrives the duffle bag, places it on the bench and begins to unzip the zipper. >SNAP< The zipper breaks off in Cyrsus hand. Cyrus stands there, staring down at the bag, as he begins to chuckle with frustration. Cyrus reaches down and tries to open the bag with his fingers, this proving to be a difficult task angers Cyrus ever the more. He grabs the top flap and the side of the bad and begins to flail it about over his head, in front of his chest, and below his waist in a sporadic motion, frantically struggling to open the bag. Finally the bag yields to Cyrsus's forceful persuasion as it is ripped open, and like a bag of potato chips his effects are strewn about the room. Cyrs looks about the room at all of his belongs scattered about, he exhales, and runs a shaking hand over his bald head. Cyrus begins to rub his goatee as again he exhales. Cyrus looks up to the ceiling with a pleading looks that clearly says 'Why me?' Cyrus changes into his wrestling attire, and heads for the door. Just as he opens it and walks out, the voice of dominique can be heard comeing down the hall, Cyrus turns towards the on coming reporter. 'Cyrus, Cyrus, a moment of your time please..............' Dominique says.
