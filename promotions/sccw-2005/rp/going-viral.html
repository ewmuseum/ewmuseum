Date: Today LAX – 5:45 AM – As seen on TMZ.com Because of how early in the morning it is, very few people are walking through the airport. One of them is very easy to pick out, due to the fact that he stands 6’8" tall and doesn’t play for the LA Lakers. The camera starts to rush up to him. "Xander, Xander, can we get a moment of your time?" a voice from off screen asks. Xander keeps walking though. "Hey Xander, you ever hear of TMZ." "What do you want? It’s almost 6 in the morning." You could see the tiredness in Xander’s eyes. "Where you heading this early?" the disembodied voice asks. Xander, looks at the camera. "Can’t you fucking people leave go back to the hole that you crawled out of? For the love of god, let me travel in peace." - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - LAX – Terminal 4 – Burger King/Starbucks 6:23 AM Xander is sitting back, getting some breakfast before his 8 AM flight to Wyoming. It wasn’t the healthiest meal in the world, but it was enough to tide him over until he got to his final destination. There were more people in the terminal now, but none have recognized him yet. Partially because of the outfit that he was wearing, partially because he was wearing sunglasses and was slouching in his chair, and partially because his once light brown hair was now blonde with with reddish orange at the tips. A new look for a new man the hairdresser said to him the day before. He wasn’t sure, but it was different, and that was what he was going for. Xander continued to eat his Croissan’wich and Hash Browns when a kid saw him. The kid waved at him, but Xander didn’t pay any attention to this. When he gets to Laramie, Xander was going to go off of the people who book travel for the talent. Having to be up at 4 in the morning to catch a flight at 8 was not the lifestyle that anyone should live. He continues eating, and thinks about how Amy Campbell is probably going to fly up the day before the show, in first class, or at least business class, and won’t have to leave until midday. She’s a main eventer, she’s one of the faces of the company. It was enough for Xander to want to regurgitate his breakfast all over the table he was sitting at. In public, he helped heap her praises in an attempt to garner "heel heat" but in reality, he couldn’t stand her guts. He gets to open the show against Rick Malloy, and then wait around until sometime after midnight to get to go home. He wasn’t a "big name" talent, his only claims to fame, according to the wrestling press and his peers, was the fact that he was pulled from the crowd like a modern day Hillbilly Jim, and that he had won a contract for a future shot at the LiveWire or Gateway Championship. Every day, people bombard him on the street, asking him when he’s going to turn in that contract and who he wants to face. He didn’t know the answer to that, nor did he care to answer. It was just another question that the stupid "marks" can’t seem to fathom. There are those in the IWC that are saying that he’s going to use the shot at the next PPV, but Xander hasn’t thought that far ahead yet. He knows that he’s got a title shot down the line. He knows that he’s going to have to make the best of it. This wasn’t the scripted world of World Wrestling Entertainment. This wasn’t the imaginary stylings of some kid on a computer hitting keys to try and "win" a fake wrestling match. This was a sport, and this was the business that Xander Searle was thrust into. He lost his dreams because of this company. The minute he accepted payment for "wrestling services" he was no longer considered an amateur athlete. He thinks about what would have happened had he not have been in the crowd that night. He would have gone to school like he normally would, and he would be preparing for a chance to play Division 1 ball after going to a junior college for two years. Xander gets angry as he thinks of all the millions that he could have made had that not have happened and he would have made it in the NBA, or even playing overseas. It wasn’t fair. He lived his life on the straight and narrow, and now he couldn’t even have a chance to live out his dream, live out his plan. Fucking Jason Ryan, Xander was now glad that the Hand took him out. Maybe Xander should try to join up with them. Like the Clique before them, they get all the perks, the better flights and the fancy hotel rooms. Hell, they probably didn’t even need to pay for them. Xander doesn’t get that. He gets coach and if he’s lucky, a room in an actual hotel, not at a Motel 6. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - http://www.youtube.com/SearleNation The camera is trained on Xander Searle as he’s playing a video game. Knowing him, it’s NBA Live. He hears a knock at the door. Xander gets up and heads towards the door, pulling his wallet out from his back pocket. He opens the door. "Sir, your total is 16.35." Xander takes 4 bills from his wallet and hands them to the person at the door. "Keep the change." Xander heads back into his room and puts what looks to be a pizza on the table in front of him. He sets a two-liter of Pepsi down next to it and opens the box. Underneath the image of Searle taking a slice of pizza are the words ‘LIFE IS GOOD’. Xander puts the slice back in the box, unpauses his game and plays a little more. He doesn’t get too far into it when his phone starts to ring. Xander pauses it, picks up the handset and takes a look at the caller ID. "Brucie, what’s up? How’s everything in Cincy?" Xander cradles the handset between his shoulder and his ear, picks up the controller and starts to play again. "You know, last I saw her, she was as flat as a board….Double Cs? Damn, now her body look as good as her face?" Xander’s Blackberry starts to vibrate next to his pizza. He pauses his game, picks it up and takes a look at a text message that just came in. "Shiiiiit, that’s nothing compared to the hot bitches that I see here. When I’m able to, I get to the beach, and it’s thong here, thong there, thong everywhere." The words ‘LIFE IS REALLY GOOD’ pop up under the image. "Hey man, I got to let you go. Just got a text, looks like some network wants me to call them back about doing a show with LC." The words ‘AND I LIE LIKE A MOTHER FUCKER’ now pop up as Xander hangs up the phone and starts to play his game again. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - LAX – Terminal 4 – Gate 48B 7:09 AM Xander sits and waits to board. He’s still trying to wake up. He’s been up just over three hours, had airport fast food for breakfast, and generally feels like shit. He looks at his watch. 7:09, at least another 20 minutes before the plane boards. He looks out the windows, the weather is crap today. He’s thinking that it would just be his luck that the flight gets delayed because of the rain Los Angeles is getting. He can just imagine that the models at the Playboy Mansion are pissed because they are bunkered inside instead of being outside, sunbathing topless like they normally do. Xander thinks about the possibilities for when he makes it as a main event level talent. Possible invites to the Mansion as he’s now starting to get into the Los Angeles social scene. When he’s not wrestling, he’s out partying. Xander can just imagine Jason yelling at him for not taking care of his money and body. Xander didn’t care. He knows that he’s young, and that given a couple more years and a few more pounds packed onto his frame, he won’t be viewed as just a wrestler, but possibly as a freak of nature, a featured attraction. Xander thinks to himself, should I work out when I get to Wyoming. It would be tempting not to, but Xander knows that he will regret it if he didn’t at least get in a cardio workout after getting settled into his motel room. He might be able to squeeze in time at the Strength and Conditioning facility at the University. Maybe talk to a couple of the players there to find out where the students party at in Laramie. Being that it’s in the middle of Wyoming, Xander has already convinced himself that they find Buffalo tipping as a good time. Xander continues to drift into and out of a state of consciousness. 4 hours of sleep, croissan’wich and hashbrowns do not mix to keep someone awake. Have to stay awake long enough to crash out on the plane. Wait, he can’t do that as it’s bound to be a short flight. Xander knows that he’s screwed. No cardio today. He’s going to need to take a nap. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Laramie, Wyoming – Motel 6 – 2:12 PM After some delays at the airport, Xander finally arrived at the Motel 6 where he’s going to be staying at before the Sin on Spike show. He was there early to do some "press" for SCCW. The local paper has left a message at the front desk for Xander to call when he has the chance. All Xander wants to do at this point is get some sleep. Personally, he doesn’t give a rats ass about doing any PR work to try and sell the tickets that remained for the show. He’s getting paid his money, and doing interviews and appearing on student produced TV shows didn’t pay him extra. He lays down on his bed, and the phone to his room starts to ring. "Searle." He holds the handset to his ear. He just listens to the person on the other line. "I’ll be there sometime tomorrow to do the interview. If the paper doesn’t like that, they can pay to put me up in a better room. I need to get some fucking sleep." He was talking to someone in the office, and wasn’t very pleased. "No, you listen; it is not my job to sell tickets. If the office doesn’t like it, they can buy my contract out. My job is to go out there and wrestle, it’s not to be a trained monkey." At this point, Xander was cementing his release from SCCW. "Good, tell them to meet me at the restaurant this time tomorrow, and I’ll give them their fucking interview." - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - http://www.youtube.com/SearleNation The camera is trained on Xander Searle. He just looks into it, silently. He raises one hand and then the other. He starts moving them around wildly to show that he, indeed, has no wrist injury. He then lifts up the SCCW LiveWire Title belt, indicating that this particular video was created before the most recent edition of Temptation (you know, the one where he tried to screw Rick Malloy out of the title, only for Malloy to drop it on his own). Taped to the bottom of the belt is a hastily written note that says ‘KEN ANDERSON, AT LEAST I’M STILL EMPLOYED’ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Laramie, Wyoming – Taco John’s – 1:45 PM/The Next Day Xander is sitting at one of the windows that gives him a view through Taco Bell’s parking lot at War Memorial Stadium and Arena-Auditorium. He’s eating a burrito while waiting for the reporter to show up. He checks his watch. He is starting to get angered about having to wait like this. Finally, a man in a rather beige suit sits down across from Xander. "Hi, I’m from the Laramie Boomerang. You must be Paul Searle." Xander doesn’t answer verbally, he pulls down his sunglasses to glare at the reporter for the use of his former ring name. "Listen, I don’t know what research you did, but if you refer to me as Paul again, I’m out of here. You got that, you little shit?" The reporter took a look at his notes. "Well, it says here that your…." He stops dead as Xander starts to get up. "OK, uh. Wow. What should I call you?" Xander sits back down and responds. "You will call me Xander. Got it?" "Yes, I got it. Well, can we start putting this on the record, Xander?" Xander takes a drink from the cold beverage that came with his burrito. "Sure." "Well, Xander, why should the fans of Laramie come to see Sin on Spike?" Xander rubs his chin. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - http://www.youtube.com/SearleNation "Fans often ask me, ‘Xander, what do I need to do to become a wrestler like you?’" Xander looks into the camera. "I answer, don’t. You’re a moron if you honestly want to get into this business. I was forced into it, I didn’t wake up one morning and go, hey, I’m a wrestler. "And for you fans out there that think, ‘Hey, if he can do it, so can I.’, think again. Most of you fans that think that you can make it in this business need to put down the pizza and beer, for a change, get off your fat asses and exercise. "Each wrestling fan out there that thinks that they can be the next Lance Marshall or Xander Searle, in reality, is just an acne ridden, morbidly obese, never been with a girl in their life dreamer. You really think you can do this? You can’t. "Not because I say so, but because the American Heart Association says that strenuous work for someone that lives a sedentary lifestyle is hazardous to their health. "Please, do me and everyone else a favor. Don’t do it. Because, in all honesty, we don’t want to see you in Spandex and watch your man boobs flop around as you embarrass yourself ‘trying’ to be a wrestler." - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Laramie, Wyoming – Taco John’s – 1:52 PM Xander rubs his chin. He ponders his answer for a moment. "Honestly, they shouldn’t waste their money. SCCW is putting on a show with me opening the card. That’s the only match that they NEED to see, as I finally get to end the career of my ‘nemesis’ Rick Malloy." The reporter’s eyes bug out a bit. This obviously wasn’t the answer that he expected from someone that was supposed to help promote the product. "You mean that they should just stay home?" "Yes, that’s what I mean. It’s on Spike TV, which I know, after watching Jesse James is a Dead Man in my hotel room last night, that this town gets. It’s on basic cable, not like our other shows with are on Cinemax. "To top that off, look at our main event. This is supposed to be our BIG FREE show, and we’re putting every belt into a blender, hitting frappe, and letting it all sort itself by the end of the night. This booking can only be done by one of two people, Vince Russo or Nigel Kensington. "If you look at things the way I see it, a showcase event shouldn’t have every title in one match. They should be defended in every match. Sure, that means that the fans will rejoice getting to see Aimz twice in one night, but that’s how it should be. "Putting all the titles into one match, that’s something that sounds like a horrible idea even for Temptation." The reporter takes a sip of the water that was brought to him as Xander was offering up his rant about the show. "So I take it you didn’t want to be in the Main Event of this show?" "Are you fucking kidding me? Why would I want to be in that giant clusterfuck? It doesn’t serve a purpose, and I could much more easily be cheated out of a title than in a straight up one on one match." The reporter takes another sip from his water. "Are there any last words that you have for the readers?" "Yes, don’t buy tickets, half the arena is sure to be papered anyway. Watch Sin on Spike to see me beat the fuck out of Rick Malloy, then put on their downloaded copy of Powder Blue to jerk off to a naked Jessica Biel." The reporter closes his notebook, gets up and walks towards the door. "What a fucking asshole." he mutters under his breath.
