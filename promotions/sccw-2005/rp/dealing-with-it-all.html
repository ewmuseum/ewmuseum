Dealing with it all

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 28 to July 9 Wrestling is an odd business where men and women go to the depths of their souls to be successful. When you feel the bright lights on you and thousands of people screaming your name the adrenaline takes over. The music plays and you feel the blood pulsing in your veins as you walk to the ring. You aren’t thinking about your real life. You are living the moment where you are a god. Does it get to you after a while? Walk in the shoes of a person unbelievably famous and try and imagine what it is like. Every person on the globe knows who you are and every detail of your life. You make the slightest misstep and it is on the Internet. The more famous you are the harder people are watching and the more people want to see you fall. No matter how much the people respect you or love you they all would kill their mothers to be you. Then the death toll starts ringing and people end up answering the call. No one has to remind the wrestlers about all the deaths in wrestling. These are their brothers, their sisters, and their best friends. You see a buddy die and you attend their funeral. It isn’t a secret that on April 20, 1999 Neil was devastated. Richard Rood better known as "Ravishing" Rick Rude one of his best friends and mentors passed away. You look into the eyes of every man and woman there and scan the crowd wondering who will be taken away from you next. February 10th, 2003 the bell ringed again for one of those men there just 4 years ago. February 10th, 2003 and everyone is here at yet another friend’s funeral. Who will the bell ring for next? The pressure to be at the top is always there. Some people have different ways of dealing with it. Some drink and do drugs to take the edge off. Neil and Stephanie used to drink a lot and get into more fights then a person can count. They had nothing to lose because wrestling was their entire life. They went in day in and day out and wrestled then when the show was over they hit the bars and usually got into a fight. Not saying that they went out of their way to start fights but they didn’t give a damn. When your day consists of going to a show and wrestling then preparing to do it all over again there is very little to look forward to. The fame is an addiction that you can’t let go. You hear the people shout your name and when the spotlights turn on bright you forget everything. The theme music thunders through your ears and you realize that you aren’t Neil Whittaker or Stephanie Randell-Whittaker anymore. You are Neil "The Real Fuckin Deal" Fracas and the Black Widow and you are wrestlers. Even for a man like Gideon Fontaine the pressures are there. He has always been in the spotlight because of the Fontaine family name and knows what it is like to have his life under a magnifying glass. The man Gideon Fontaine is known across the globe and he isn’t a commodity or a gimmick. He has been in movies and runs Fontaine Enterprises and Fontaine Enterprises International. His career as a professional fighter is public knowledge. His WKBA record 43 wins, 1 loss, 39 wins by knockout. His record of No Holds barred fights in Thailand 94 wins, 0 losses, 81 wins by knockout. His debut into professional wrestling was 2 minutes and 42 seconds. It was so quick and decisive it was considered a squash. In the years he has been wrestling he has been known as "The G-Man" Gideon Fontaine and "The Osaka Assassin" Gideon Fontaine. What does a man like him do when the spotlight is on him? He nails large breasted women a dozen at a time in what can be only described as an excessive display of Decadence and Debauchery that he even used as a tag team name when teamed up with Ed Gesumaria. No matter the week they will continue doing what they always do. Stand strong dealing with it all. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building one week ago. ########## ########## ########## ########## There is nothing planned for this week but dealing with several issues. They lost a good friend last week and it seems that every man woman and child want their time in the spotlight to tear down yet another person who was famous and the wrestling business in general. Gideon is looking at the headlines and the media circus involved and it sickens him greatly. This man was a person revered by all as a professional and a man who embodied hard work and dedication. In just 5 days not even a whole fucking week all of that means absolutely nothing. "I don’t care who is asking about my comments on what happened. I am not adding to that media circus." Gideon hits a button shutting off the speaker phone and is left there brooding. He seems mad but both he and Amanda knows that it is a disgust for the lack of respect and decency by the media. He turns on his laptop and as soon as he looks at a wrestling news site 17 more stories are up and Gideon is angry. "Would it kill these so called journalists to at least talk to someone who actually knew the man rather then ask any idiot their opinions?" The media has something to sink their teeth into. "Steroids caused yet another death" is a story spreading across the globe. The facts of the story are irrelevant. One man hung and a wife and child murdered and steroids are at fault. The deaths being quoted are mind boggling. The amount of deaths being attributed to steroids and drug abuse staggers the mind. "Do these people even bother checking facts anymore?" Gideon knows several of these men and women as well as how they died and it is irrelevant to the moral crusade. Whether it was by Accident, Murder, Suicide, Heart failure, Cancer, or even something as simple as a blood clot people still wave the Steroid banner for all to see. It doesn’t matter because to a journalist the story is more important then the truth. To the men that bury their friends and brothers in this business the details matter a lot. "I used to think that journalists worked to report the truth now all I see is people writing wrestling related fan fiction with the odd fact accidentally included to seem legitimate." Gideon shuts off his laptop and goes back to his paperwork hating the media even more. It is one thing to report a story. It is another all together to create a story based on a personal opinion or agenda. ########## ########## ########## ########## Meanwhile at Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. one week ago. ########## ########## ########## ########## Things aren’t any easier for Neil and Stephanie as they are dealing with their own opinions on what happened and it makes absolutely no sense to them. This is a man they faced in UJW in a triple threat match for the World title. Ask either Neil or Stephanie the top 10 people you would let take care of your child and he is high on both of their lists. They were sickened to learn what happened and Neil looked long and hard at the bottle again. Some things just don’t get any easier regardless of how often you see it. How do you accept that something like that can happen to someone you looked at like a brother? You can’t even comprehend something that monstrous. You would sooner believe in anything else no matter how improbable because it shakes you to your core. You cannot deal with something like that and not be changed forever. Stephanie has had a hard life and has been betrayed by so many people she loved over the years that she is more jaded then Neil and Gideon but even this shocked her. Neil on the other hand is numb. He will never accept that the man that was capable of this is the man he knew. There is so many contradictions, opinions, and even strange things like bibles being at the scene that make absolutely no sense but he can’t shake the feeling that while he wants this to be an elaborate frame up this is real. Neil: Always bringing up the steroids. Fuckin vultures. Stephanie: You think you know someone then you see this. How are you holding up? Neil: I feel like drinking…… Stephanie: Me too. Neil: I wouldn’t be so pissed off if it had of just been reported as a story but it has become a crusade. Everyone with an axe to grind is getting on TV and using this as an in to get over. Hell saw an article the other day about the deaths in pro wrestling related to drug use and I saw Owen Hart’s name mentioned. The guy falls to his death because of a dumb ass stunt and some fuckin inbred cock sucking worthless shit stain dripping down the crack of his mother’s ass decided to Google up dead wrestlers for his article. Stephanie: You need to calm down. Neil: If I ever caught that guy in an alley I’d knock his fuckin teeth down his throat. Then the media circus would go after me saying "Steroids cause pro wrestler to attack journalist". Doesn’t matter if the man shits on my friends or my family and I feel nothing but an irrational white hot hatred and rage it has to be fuckin steroids even if I never touched the shit in my life. Stephanie: I know what will get your mind off this. Neil: What? As soon as he sees the mischievous smirk and the look in her eyes Neil smiles ear to ear and follows her to their bedroom. What happens next isn’t hard to imagine but is likely the reason that Neil had their bedroom soundproofed. ########## ########## ########## ########## An autograph signing at a gym in in L.A. one week ago. ########## ########## ########## ########## It isn’t something they do very often but they decided to take this week off. Neil and Gideon needed time to grieve for a friend and somehow come to terms with what happened to the man they knew. There are hundreds of wrestling fans, martial artists, and some body builders. The Alliance took this week off because they had to come to deal with what happened last week and they pretty much decided that they couldn’t be at the arena. Plus with Timo going for the world title they wanted to make sure that they were available if he wanted help. The thing that many people outside the wrestling business and even many people in the business do not understand. The thing that is so alien to all but the few is the work ethic and the respect that is lost in wrestling today. Timo never had to say to Neil or Gideon that he would appreciate their help in sharpening up. Come Killzone he would take it to Clinton Sage like no other man has. Neil and Gideon had the offer standing out there long ago that if he ever wanted them to have his back all he had to do was ask. So far Timo has gone the road alone because that was important to him that he do it by himself. Still the promise was made and the Alliance live by their word. Gideon and Neil are sitting at a table with several pens and posters. The fans are in a line and there is no rush because they are all talking about a sport they like a lot with two men that have seen the worst this business has to offer and always stood their ground. Jeff: Thanks for the autograph Neil. Neil: No problem Jeff. Another man comes up to the table and he has one of the UJW DVDs that recently flooded the internet. A wrestling fan has uploaded the video and this man downloaded it and even made a custom DVD box with photoshop. Neil can’t help but smile. Neil: Take it you got that pretty recently. Good work on the cover. ???: Thanks. Gideon: What name do you want us to make it out to? Travis: Travis please Gideon writes out "Hope you enjoy the DVD Travis. The Osaka Assassin Gideon Fontaine" and Neil writes in "Neil The Real Fuckin Deal Fracas". They pass it back. Neil: Guy that uploaded the DVD helped me get Ricky some weight lifting gear. Never thought I’d see a DVD of it with my own eyes. Travis: There have been close to 19,000 uploads. Neil: Uploads? Gideon: It is a slang term for when someone downloads a video the tracker keeps a count of how many people complete the file. How many people roughly on the site you got it from? Travis: About 27,000 Neil: That isn’t that large a number. Gideon: Generally the private sites have smaller numbers. The reason it is private is so they don’t have to worry about a fed like Prime getting the lawyers shutting them down for distributing copyrighted material. Smitty wouldn’t be happy about it either but he has the sense to realize that these sites get wrestling material out to the fans and the fans will buy it if they like it. Neil: Ah. Wouldn’t surprise me Prime trying to shake down some poor kid making $7.50 an hour at Walmart. Travis: Thank you. You don’t know how much this means to me. As Travis leaves another man steps up. Things were going well to this point but it only takes one person to say something that sets things off. You know the type of person. There are some people that can’t help expressing their opinion. Even if it is right they don’t take into account the other person’s point of view and say something monumentally ignorant. What do you think it was that set off Neil and Gideon? "Can you believe that they are blaming steroids for a monster like that brutally murdering his family." The words are accurate and the opinion valid but as soon as the words hit the air time stops. Neil and Gideon are no longer writing and there is a dead calm. This is the silence that you feel in your bones as it chills you to the core because any second something bad can happen. There is a brief cough from one of the fans and you hear the chair moving as Neil is getting up. Gideon gets up as well because he is trying to figure out just what is going to happen. Neil is staring a hole into this man. You can tell by the look on his face that he is in a place between having control over his actions and completely losing control and breaking this guy in half. Gideon is standing there not knowing what will happen. After 4 tense seconds Neil speaks. Neil: Did you know him? If you ever got pulled over by the police you know just what a question like this feels like. You know that there is no right answer. Anything you say can only make a bad situation worse. ???: No sir. Neil: I wrestled him years ago and I knew the man. I have seen every single person with an axe to grind spout off and absolutely bury the man. I have seen every single article and so many theories that I stopped trying to make sense of all the crap because it pisses me off more. Then I see journalists say "steroid this" and "steroid that" trying to take apart my sport and shit on everyone in it. I’m not saying we are angels but we work through broken bones and ripped muscles. We go months without seeing our kids and family. We see friends buried because of the high-speed lifestyle and because they put their bodies through hell and finally dropped dead. With a cold stare Neil looks directly at the man and doesn’t blink. Neil: People just don’t get it. The fame and fortune isn’t free. You see me here and I had enough staples put in me that I set off a fuckin metal detector at the airport. Gideon had a minor concussion that had him in the hospital for observation. I buried my mentor and one of my best friends and that was one of the hardest days of my life. That still bothers me to this day. Even hearing that moron Jack Diamond was dead bothered me and he is one of the biggest jackoffs in wrestling. I still respected him from working with him. Do I think that what happened to his family was monstrous. You are fuckin right I do. There isn’t a day that I wouldn’t like to take him out back and beat an explanation out of him but that will never happen. That is something between him and god. I can’t ever forget what happened but I will remember the man before those events as a friend and someone I trusted like few others. After a pause Neil turns and walks away. Gideon sits back down looking at Neil leaving the gym for some fresh air. Things are hard to deal with. Wrestling has been exposed to the public and people think they understand what makes a wrestler tick. They think that everything they see on the internet sites is real. Some of it is real but a lot of times you don’t see what is really happening. When you hear about Timo getting a major concussion at Afflicted Glory you think you understand everything. The difference between a wrestler and a normal man is Timotoga Bolamba got up the very next day and did it all over again while others are never the same again. You can only give so much of your soul until you have nothing left to give. On that day you are no longer a man. When you lose your soul to this business you will do anything for the fame and fortune like a crackhead looking for his next fix. It’s not pretty and so many tragedies came when the person in the business became addicted to it. ########## ########## ########## ########## At Neil and Stephanie’s house in L.A. Saturday July 7, 2007 ########## ########## ########## ########## Neil and Gideon are in the gym sparing. They have a tag title rematch against Aimz and Crisis. This is "THE" rematch. There are no distractions and other people involved. This is a battle between the Alliance and Aimz and Crisis. They knew the time would come when all the distractions were put aside. There is no 4 team cluster fucks. There is not a tag match between the Alliance and the CFH. There is not a chance for Aimz to become the Universal Champion facing Clinton Sage. KillZone45. FedExForum, Memphis, TN. THE ALLIANCE Vs. AIMZ & CRISIS. Fatal Faction Championships. The Alliance doesn’t have to train as hard as they do. There is no need for the cardio work and the relentless training to be the very best you can be. The wrestling business is one where you can go to the very top without ever seeing the inside of a gym. You don’t even have to know how to wrestle. Hell you don’t even have to be able to talk on the mike. You look at the business and there is so many things wrong with it when you have entertainers living like rock stars ruining all you hold dear. They will never allow themselves to give any less then their best. They will never work for a federation that would have David Arquette as a World champion. They work for FUSE where they will never bastardize their profession with talentless so called "Entertainment". As Neil and Gideon are sparring they are thinking of tonight. When UFC 73 is on Neil, Gideon, and Stephanie will be watching it relaxing as they predict what will happen. As hard as The Alliance works they are not ever going to lose track of who they are. They are never going to let things go to the point where they are no longer themselves. The pressures are always there. The cameras and the press. The millions of fans across the globe. The feeling like your life is no longer your own. The endless temptations open to anyone famous enough. The pain and the suffering of the highspeed life style. The Alliance will be there……… Dealing with it all.
