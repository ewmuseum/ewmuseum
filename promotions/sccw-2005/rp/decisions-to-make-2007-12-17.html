Decisions to Make

17 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 17th, 2007 Joshua sat there staring at the kitchen wall. The criss-cross black and white tile patterns struck him as vulgar, and up until now he really never noticed it. Then again, up until now he hadn’t noticed a lot of things. Three weeks ago, when these dreams began Joshua hadn’t noticed where he remembered the voice. Too concerned that Sanctum was rearing his ugly head again, he never bothered to pay any mind to the clues. All of that changed though, when Brennan called. Joshua had answered the phone, and right from the beginning it was obvious something was up. Brennan explained his father was Eryc Morgan, and that his father was missing. Josh was about to ask how he got his number, but Brennan explained that too. An Irish fella had given him this number, and explained to him that Joshua could help him. The Irish fella, was David O’Malley. It was then, and only then that realization had struck him. The reason he knew the voice so well was because he heard it all the time. Originally he had thought it was Sanctum, the pure evil presence had never brought anyone else to mind, until now. If ever there was a man, a being more evil then Sanctum Nocturna… it was Corvus. After getting off the phone with Brennan, Joshua dressed and went out for a walk. There was a time in his life that Joshua had dealt with everything, by taking a walk. That was before politics had overtaken his wrestling career, before he buried Andy and well before Sanctum nearly destroyed his life. During this walk Joshua had a lot to consider. Things had finally begun to calm down in his life, Sanctum was gone, his home life was stable and once again he had a solid wrestling career. In many ways he was at the best time in his life. He had a wonderful wife, a beautiful baby boy and a wonderful little girl with another boy on the way. Now, a huge choice was presented to him. Help Eryc and jeopardize everything again, or turn his back on his best friend. Both choices required a lot of consideration. He walked for hours that night aimlessly, and much like his walking expedition his thought process went nowhere. He returned home, stripped from his long leather jacket and his black Punisher hoodie placing both on the coat rack in the foyer. He kicked his boots off of his feet, and made his way back upstairs. Angel was lying in bed sprawled across his side of the bed seemingly reaching for a body that wasn’t there. Seeing this made a lot of things hit home for him. Angel depended on him, his children depend on him… but so did Eryc. He felt foolish trying to rationalize either decision, and even more foolish for trying to make a decision on his own. Giving up for the night, he had went to bed. That night the same stale, cold room invaded his dreams again. Corvus was there, so was another man… and a woman. The woman was injected with drugs, and Corvus fed off of her soiled and tainted blood before his eyes. But they weren’t really his eyes, no they were Eryc’s eyes, Eryc was being made to watch this, and Corvus was taking a sick glee in the torture. He awoke the next morning in a cold sweat, with Angel wrapped tightly beside him. He needed to make a decision, and by the time the day was over a decision was going to be made. Before the decision could be made however, he would need to consult with his wife. Eryc was her friend too, and out of consideration a decision of this magnitude could not be made by only himself. He rose from bed and dressed in a pair of jeans and plain t-shirt before making his way downstairs and into the kitchen. He put a pot of coffee on and went to sit at the kitchen table. He sat there staring at the kitchen wall. The staggered black and white tiles struck him as vulgar, and up until now he really never noticed it. Then again, up until now he hadn’t noticed a lot of things. Angel entered the kitchen as the coffee finished brewing. She grabbed a cup out of the cupboard for herself, and then she grabbed one for her husband. She didn’t need to ask; she already knew he’d want a cup. Wives just know these things. She made the two cups of coffee and sat across from him at the table, blocking the hideous wall pattern from his sight. Oddly, he was more grateful for that then the cup of joe. The couple sat in silence for a few minutes knowing there were only precious minutes before one of the two children awoke. Angel was the first to speak. "Have you had anymore of those dreams?" He nodded. "Yeah, you could say that." She noted something in his tone, it wasn’t completely definable but she knew enough to know something was wrong. She didn’t press however; as experience had taught her that when he was ready he would tell her. Experience had also taught her that it wouldn’t take long for him to come to her, once again experience proved correct. "There not exactly dreams." Joshua began. Angel listened intently as Joshua explained the situation, everything from whom Corvus was right through to how everything came together and the decision that lay ahead. When he was finished she remained silent for only a few moments. "What would Eryc want you to do?" She asked. He stopped to consider that, like many things recently he had never really thought about it. After giving it a good few minutes of thought he finally responded. "Honestly, I don’t think he’d want me to endanger the family." Joshua paused for a moment. He couldn’t believe the words that were coming out of his mouth, but it was the truth of the matter. Eryc was a full grown man where as Jason and Bonita were only children, and they needed their father. "Well then, that settles everything." Angel began, and with that Joshua realized things were different now. The True Saints hadn’t been a way of life for a long time now, and honestly he and Eryc weren’t as close as they had once been. None of this however made him feel any better about the situation. "I can’t believe I’m turning my back on him…" Joshua muttered. "I never thought I’d be that guy…" Angel reached across the table setting her left hand over his, their wedding rings laying side by side. "You’re not." He looked up meeting her gaze, she smiled at him and for that one moment nothing else seemed to matter. "That’s why you’re going to help him. Eryc would never endanger our family, in fact he’s tried to help our family on so many occasions, he was there when we went through everything with Jason, and he was there every time we needed a friend to count on. Eryc is our family, and we need to help him." Raising his right eyebrow he looked at her for a moment, "we?" he asked. "Yes, we." "Well, if that’s what you think, then that’s what we’ll do." Joshua replied. "I guess this wasn’t as hard as I thought." Joshua rose from the kitchen table, finishing off the last swallow of his coffee. He leaned over and gave his wife a kiss, turns out the support of his family stretches farther then just his wrestling career, and that is something every man needs to know. Now, Joshua knows, and that makes him a very lucky man. Only time will tell just how lucky.
