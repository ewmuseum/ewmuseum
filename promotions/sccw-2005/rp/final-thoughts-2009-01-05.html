Final thoughts?

5 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/5 It's bell time for the Big Stack. That means only one thing, considering that this is Defiance II. It means that he is is in an undisclosed location, ready to fight Sean Sterling. If he had to guess, he'd guess that 'undisclosed location' means 'abandoned warehouse in the South Bronx', judging by the environment around him. Most in 'the know' posit that Phillip Benjamin Kennedy has no chance tonight. He is, after all, a foot soldier in the Dead Man's Hand, the group that has risen him to prominence, and helped him capture his only title to date in the company. Tonight, though, he is alone by his own choosing. Hand interference means that his SCCW contract is void. It is but Sean Sterling and Phillip Kennedy, one on one, for the SCCW Gateway Championship. No Excuses, indeed. Kennedy, however, sees his chances a bit differently. He sees No Excuses as his chance to prove that, while the Dead Man's Hand certainly makes him even *more* formidable, he is his own man. He can succeed on his own. He can arrive at the cusp of the SCCW Universal Championship on his own. And maybe, in some ways, that's what this is really about. With Alex Pierce busy with Amy Campbell business that Kennedy does not for the life of him understand, Jadian Bridden just returning to the fold, Mayhem AWOL, Hunter Sabuani injured, and Katsidy and Savant running all over creation, the Big Stack is an island unto himself. He has declared himself the de-facto King of the Dead Man's Hand. He has, over the past months, been the only member of the Hand to wear gold after Mayhem's loss to Sterling. In the ring, he is the Hand's one true hope, at least, in his own mind. And as such, he has his own plots. His own plans. His own ideas. Why, if the Hand were to simply follow them, the results would be grandiose indeed.... ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Later that night...or at least, later that night in Kennedy's thoughts. The long black stretch limousine rolled down one of umpteen brightly lit New York City streets, because lets face it, when you want to party, Long Island just won't do. And make no mistake, the inhabitants of the stretch Hummer gliding its way towards any of a number of bangin' NYC hot spots were ready to party. With bells on. Along one of the benches sat the newly crowned SCCW Livewire Champion, Jadian Bridden. Wrapped around him was Juli Minorez, wearing something that was probably from the Katsidy collection of 'things women probably shouldn't wear in public, but boy, are men happy that they do.' Across from the happy couple were the duo of Black Ops, Mr. Hawke and Mr. Ashe, the latter man far happier about tonight's excursion than the former. On the third bench sat your brand new SCCW Gateway Champion, Phillip Kennedy. Of course, in his lap was his Sex Kitten, Kathryn Shaw, all purrs and congratulations and 'you know, it really has been far too long.' On Kennedy's other side was a quieter Lauren Fox, proud of her own triumph, and really, proud in general of how well everything had come off. For on the final bench, alone, in her white pantsuit, was Alex Eloise Pierce, the SCCW Universal Championship next to her on the velvet. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ That was, in all honesty, the scene that Phillip Kennedy thought was very possible to happen if everyone played their cards right. They COULD all win. They COULD go out afterwards and celebrate like a true team should, like the most dominant force in what remained of Primetime Central would naturally do after a performance that put the fear of God in not just those IN SCCW, but any would be interlopers from outside. And yet...it never happened. No parties. No celebrations. Hell, for Ms. Pierce, 'fun' didn't seem to be part of her vernacular. Katsidy seemed to be the only member of the group who enjoyed a good time, but she was anywhere seemingly except at Kennedy's side over the past months. Kennedy wasn't pleased, and no, that's not just because he no longer gets laid on a regular basis. Sighing, Kennedy leaned against the wall of the warehouse. Any minute now, he would step into battle against Sean Sterling for the Gateway Championship. Any moment now, he and Sterling would settle their score, one way or another. There would be no rematch, no bitching (not even on Kennedy's part). The terms of the match precluded such things. There would be a winner, and there would be a loser. The latter would simply have to deal with that fact. And neither Sterling nor Kennedy would have it any other way. They were both rogues. Neither could be accused of walking the straight and narrow; hell, Sterling himself had committed acts very similar to those done by the Dead Man's Hand over his career. He'd been the man everyone hated, and reveled in that role. Kennedy was a foot soldier. Not his own man, but one of Desade's warriors. And yet, along the way, something happened he didn't expect: taking pride in his work, his victories. Even his cheating was a source of pride for the rookie, his ability to get away with it. Winning clean wasn't important: winning itself was. Winning seemed to not be so important to the Hand. All of the positive reinforcement had disappeared. Katsidy was gone. Savant was gone. Kennedy didn't matter. And he didn't matter in spite of the fact that he was the one guy who held down the fort while everyone else was dicking around with whatever the fuck. Tonight would be his coronation. No, his baptism, in the mingled blood and sweat of himself and his adversary. He would walk out of this goddamned warehouse with the SCCW Gateway Championship. Then, and only then, would he begin dictating some terms. Standing on the cusp of the Universal Title demanded some goddamned respect.
