Meetings in Dark Places.

2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4.5.09 Hi, my name is Kirsten Pendleton, and I hate meetings. Well, that's not true. I used to like it a lot more when I was just Kirsten, you know? When I could sit in a room with my agent and chomp some gum or whatever. We could shoot the shit, talk about what happened on Grey's Anatomy (and how much better I would be on that show than Katherine Heigl – I mean, seriously; bitch is a problem). But I'm not sitting with my agent today. I'm in a small, darkened room, a white screen drawn and reflecting a face I knew well, because I helped create it. 'This is Charlotte Ramone.' Lauren Fox frowns as she stands in front of the class. It's a room full of people I know are sociopaths, but I wonder what that says about me. 'Except everyone in this room knows it's not Charlotte Ramone, because...' The slide flips to a gravestone – I've never been back. 'Charlotte Anne Raymond,' it reads. 'Beloved Daughter and Wife. Shining Light.' 'Because Charlotte Raymond has been dead for four years.' Savant pressed her little... button... thingy (I couldn't tell you what they were called), and the shot was pulled off straight off of SIN on SPIKE in San Antonio, with the Punk Rock Pixie – or whomever was sporting that hair and that mask – curled up on a cargo box, checking the same nails we used as a code not too long ago. 'Since Desade is not wearing the Plastic and Charlotte Raymond is dead, someone else is wearing the mask, and we don't know who that someone is. Desade wanted to be here herself, but she is otherwise occupied right now...' I bet she is, but I muffle my grin. Everyone knows what Savant did to the last girl who snickered at the mention of the rumors surrounding the Director's off-book activities. They say she'll get out of the hospital next week, but none of us have checked to be sure. We're too scared. 'So it falls to me. It falls to all of you. I want this footage and this letter—' Wait, letter? We've never seen a letter. '—studied. I want it dissected, torn apart, and I want this mystery solved. Whoever this is, they're out to fuck with us at a time we're most vulnerable. Some of you don't remember the time I spent in charge around here, but I assure you, I do not take well to being fucked with.' Not like her sister, that's for sure. 'Sin City has seen fit to book Ms. Ramone again after she got hit in the head with a brick by Victor Ambrose this week, and I would put the show-team on this, but with Phillip facing two men, I'm going to be a bit busy this week, so I'm counting on all of you—' I swear those dark fanatic's eyes are glaring right at me, but they couldn't be, right? '—ALL of you. If you see an intern that you've never seen before, I want to know. If you see a stagehand, a backstage guest, a fucking pizza delivery girl back there, you tell me and you tell me straight away. But there's one thing you have to do that is more important than that.' Her half-gloved hands (why is she always dressed up?) latch around the podium with a grip so tight, so frenzied, I think she's gonna snap the wooden top off and throw it at us. 'You have to be quiet about it. I don't want Lance Marshall or Dusk or even Legion to so much as smell you. Right now, they think that's Pierce under that mask, or maybe me.' It's weird; Savant usually calls her mentor 'Alexandra,' but she's almost been derisive tonight. I wonder if there's a problem on the homefront? 'Either way, it's useful for them to think it's a scheme, because that way, the fact that it's not a scheme won't occur to them. So this is an under-the-radar, radio-silence operation. Each of you has a packet in front of you – these are the teams the Director has assigned you to, and it's the cells I expect to see in Lawrence. If you have any questions, you route them to me through Trinity or Dr. Riley. As far as I am concerned, you're not at the show and I've never seen you before.' She slaps her portfolio closed, the sound sharp enough to have been a gunshot. 'Any questions before I leave?' A woman in the back, coffee-skinned and young, raises a hand; Fox's only answer is a look. 'Are we sure...' the woman – I really need to learn these people's names, even if they don't use their real ones – begins, hesitantly. 'Are you sure that this isn't some trick from Ms. Pierce? Some double-blind go-behind that not even you know about? Something that maybe Campbell is—' 'That's enough.' Kathryn Shaw was nearby, and the lovely Sex Kitten (God, has my playing at being a lesbian made me one?) only announced her presence then. 'Amy Campbell is not like us, so if this is a game, it's Lexi's. And that doesn't change your mission, does it? You find out which two-bit hack of a threat is behind that mask, and you tell Lori about it. It doesn't exactly seem hard, but hey, maybe I'm a puritan.' Whatever grumbling there is, it's muted. No one wants to cross Shaw or Fox, not without the Spider around to gainsay them. We all look expectedly at the pair. 'Dismissed.' They leave, a motley group of women trained under the iron fist of the Deceiver and the velvet glove of the Siren. It's then and only then that I decide to open my folio, to see which of these people I have to swallow my pride to team with. I don't have a paper that lists anyone. I slide up to my feet, and I think about it quite hard – maybe they forgot it, maybe someone has two. I should wait to see if someone comes back... But no one came back. I'm not surprised; I wouldn't come back, either. Shaw and Fox are conversing in the front of the room and I make my way there unsteadily. I could swear that Savant glanced my way, but I'll never be sure of it. 'Miss Fox?' I ask, trying to be as gentle and British as possible – you can't be mad at a cute girl with an English accent, right? Right? She turns and glares anyway; I think that's her default expression these days. 'What is it, Kirsten?' 'I don't... I seem to have lost my cell assignment, ma'am.' When Shaw's eyes glitter with hidden amusement, I know I'm in for it. 'You have a special assignment, Kris. You're our secret weapon.' When she explains what I'm supposed to do, the pit at the bottom of my stomach has become a black hole. It's Sunday night, and I'm in the high heels again. I'm in the pastel dress again. They bleached my hair again, painted my face. I'm a lady. I'm Krissy Jamison. And I'm waiting in the lobby of the fucking Econo Lodge, the hotel that Sin City is housing its people in. The idea is that Charlotte Ramone will be here. The idea is that whoever's playing this game will be unable to pass up flaunting her identity, unable to pass up being here with all the Jayhawk fans that've come to see the show on Tuesday. The idea is that I'm supposed to kiss her, supposed to tell her I'm in for her little game, supposed to make a scene and make her take me back to that room. The Sinners come (I have a pleasant conversation with Austin King, though I'm pretty sure he just wanted to get into my pants). No Paul, which is good because I'm not sure I could face him. The Sinners come, and there's no girl with pink hair and blue eyes. I wait, and I wait, and I wait some more. The parking lot lights come on outside, the road traffic has started to dry up. I walk to the windows, my eyes narrowing. I don't see the woman behind me until she's up against me. 'Sorry to disappoint you,' she says quietly. I can't identify the sound. 'But I'm past being dictated to, and I'm not ready to kiss a whore just to sell that I'm super-serious about playing at lesbianism. You have a choice, Miss Pendleton. You can keep dancing for the fiddler, or you can change the tune.' 'I don't understand.' It's the truth; all these people talk past each other. 'You will. My room key is in your purse. If you come up, though, you've got to really be in. Not be Lauren fucking Fox's little spy-bitch. Because if I find out you're lying or trying to play me, I'll leave you in the tub to bleed out. If you want to break the cycle, you come upstairs. Otherwise, disappear where Savant or I can't find you. This is between me and Desade, and you don't have to be a part of it. But if you want to make her pay, then play the airhead, but do it for me.' I wait, up against the window, for two more minutes, expecting the woman to say more. She doesn't, and when I turn, she's gone. Now I find myself walking up the steps outside the roadside motel, cursing the clatter of the platform sandals. Do I want to be free? Yes. Yes, I do. Do I think this woman can give me that freedom? Nope. Not a chance. I'm going anyway. Either Lauren Fox is going to try to kill me or this Sombra person is. Either way, I'm pretty much dead. Hi, my name is Kirsten Pendleton Krissy Jamison, and I'm in over my head. So what else is new?
