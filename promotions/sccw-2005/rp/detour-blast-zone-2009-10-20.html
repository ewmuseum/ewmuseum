Detour: Blast Zone

20 October 2009

Date: 10/20/09 It’s been 24 hours. It’s been 24 hours since my world has been destroyed. It feels like someone has set off a bomb in my brain and I’m trying to pick through the shrapnel, desperately trying to understand why someone would have done something like this to us. To me. I’d been out by the pool, taking in the sun, trying desperately to get out of my own head. My mind just kept replaying the events of that one night, what I had done to Lane Stevens, the lengths to which he had driven me…and the abyss I had nearly fallen off of. I was clinging desperately to what little of my strength and self-resolve remained, desperately holding the rage and anger that bubbled inside me at bay. I wanted to kill Lane Stevens. I admit that now and I do so freely. Stevens is little more than a cancer, a parasitic abomination draining the life out of everything he touches. The world will be a fundamentally better place once he’s gone, I know that in my bones. But I know, Father, that it is not my place to do so. That I came as close as I did scares me…and shames me. Alanna came out on to the pool deck shaking and crying. She could barely keep control of herself and it was several minutes before she could even speak. For a moment, all sorts of scenarios played out in my mind. The cancer was getting stronger. It had spread. The prognosis was now fatal. The truth was far worse than I could have imagined. My son. Our son. Someone has taken our son. One moment he was on the playground, playing and laughing and the next…some stranger, some animal had taken him away. She is responsible. She has to be. Yes, she promised Zach would not be harmed but she lies as easily as she breathes. She cares nothing for others. It’s almost fitting that she’s found what she claims to be love in the arms of a sociopath like Amy Campbell. She would not have done it directly, no. She would have had one of her army of lackeys to do it for her, someone who could not be traced back to her. So she could claim plausible deniability, so she could once again pretend like the black stain across her soul is not there. It has to be Alexandra Pierce who’s responsible for this. It has to be. Because I don’t know what I’m going to do if it isn’t. I need someone to blame for this. I need someone to punish. I need someone that I can turn this anger, this rage and this fear onto. I need someone that I can hit and hit and hit until they stop moving. I need some way to not think about all of the horrible things that could be happening to Zach at this very moment. The thought that this could have been done by some stranger, some depraved animal preying on little boys…that thought scares me to death. Zach and Alanna are all that I have right now, they are all that is keeping the darkness within me from swallowing me whole. I cannot lose them now. I won’t lose them now. The police are polite and professional and empathetic and so utterly, insufferably calm. They politely explain that it needs to be another 24 hours before they can file another missing persons report, that while it does appear to be an abduction there are procedures that have to be followed. Words that seem so logical and sensible on CSI or Law and Order now make me want to scream at the top of my lungs, make me want to grab the detective by the throat and throttle him until he sees sense. My son could be hurt. My son could be dying. My son could be lying in a ditch somewhere, his life blood draining out of him AND YOU WANT ME TO WAIT?! But I don’t. Instead I stay calm, thank them for all their help, show them to the door. I want to scream, to yell, to break something. Instead, I help Alanna to the couch, her legs threatening to collapse beneath her with every step. We sit and we cry for a while, unsure what to do next. No one bothers explaining to you how you’re meant to go on. The silence is interrupted by a phone call. It’s SCCW, calling about what happened at SIN Con. My coming to the aid of April Monday has attracted some attention, specifically that of the Dead Man’s Hand’s least interesting member, Jadian Bridden. It seems that they have taken some measure of offense at my actions and are now demanding that a match take place: Jadian and his pet barracuda on one side, myself and April Monday on the other. I find myself screaming at the voice on the other end of the line, bellowing so loudly that Alanna comes running with a look of terror in her eyes. I apologize to the poor SCCW employee, explaining what has happened. There is a brief pause and suddenly Nigel is on the other end of the line, offering his deepest sympathies and saying that he would completely understand if I did not wish to compete this week. I thank him. But I tell him no, I will indeed be competing this week. The reason I give him is that I have a contract with SCCW and I will honor my professional obligations, that I cannot just sit home driving myself crazy with worry and grief all day. That is not the truth. Alanna and I will even agree later that I need to do something to take my mind off of what has happened, if only for a moment. This, also, is a lie. The truth is this. I know I will not be able to keep what is boiling inside me contained. I wish to hurt someone like I hurt right now. I want nothing more than to hit someone in the face until they no longer have any teeth, until there face is nothing more than a mass of red, raw, bloody meat. And in the absence of the monster who has my son… …Jadian Bridden will have to do.
