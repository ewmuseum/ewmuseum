Actions.

Date: 1/20/08 16.1 the mirror: bored Everyone gets bored sometimes. Amy told you that right before the show ended. You don’t know how she did the math in her head, or how you were coming off as ‘strange.’ All you know is things are different now, and you have to be careful. Still, you had already made your decision. You convinced yourself that you were going to spend time with only Katie, and that you were done with Sarah Riley. So all of Amy’s advice was for nothing, essentially. The only problem is that Sarah came to the last UPROAR. She was hitting on other wrestlers, showing interest in people other than you. And ever since Amy told you about Sarah and Darcy, you’ve been thinking about the last time you saw her. When you told her it was over. It’s not exactly a happy memory. 16.2 the window: done "So that’s it?" Sarah Riley’s voice cuts with disappointment, and Jonathan notices. "We’re just…done?" Jonathan looks down at his feet because he can’t bring himself to face his own decision. "I think so. I just…this is hard for me, Sarah. You know how I feel about you." "I know." "Then you know how hard this is. I’ve been with Katie for a long time, but I don’t feel like I completely know her. I just feel like…I feel like I need to know more about her before I just shuffle her off to the side to be with my shrink." Sarah shakes her head. "Don’t call me that. And don’t act like you really want to know more about her. You’ve been with her for almost a year now; you’ve known her for four. I think you know her well enough. You’re only doing this because you don’t want to be the bad guy." Now Jonathan looks up. "The bad guy?" "Yes. You are so concerned with the societal constructs of adultery and fidelity that you’re worried that you’ll look bad if you leave your girlfriend for someone else. It’s kind of sad to think of it this way, but you’re doing this so the fans don’t boo you." "That’s ridiculous." "Is it? You’ve had these inner struggles ever since we started getting intimate, haven’t you? You worried if you were a good person, and what made you feel better was that the fans still cheered you. Is that all you really care about?" Sarah’s eyebrow arches, and in spite of himself Jonathan can’t help but chuckle. "That’s why I shouldn’t have sex with my shrink." Sarah doesn’t laugh. "But you did, Jonathan. You made the choice, and now you have another one. You seem to be choosing her, so I’ll ask you one more time, for clarity’s sake: Are we done?" Jonathan’s sigh is long and heartbreaking for his counterpart. "I…" After a moment of quivering, his lip straightens and he faces her with resolve. "Yes. We’re done." 16.3 the mirror: not until You didn’t know what to expect after that, but you were relatively sure the road would get easier after that decision. Wrong. Sarah Riley showed up at your job, flirted with your stablemate’s husband, and raised said stablemate’s suspicions about you. This is her job, and she did it well. Very well. The worst part about the entire situation is the mix of emotions you felt after seeing her in the arena. It wasn’t just anxiety. It wasn’t just rage. It was desire. It was wanting. It was hardcore lust. Everything together that just reminded you that your decision hasn’t been completely made yet. Not until you can look at the woman who counseled you and not feel a thing towards her. Not until you can look at your girlfriend and feel love uninterrupted. In laymen’s terms, not anytime soon. 16.4 the window: reservations "Where are we going, Jonathan?" Katie asks, her hand groping forward blindly. The reason she is groping blindly is because she is blind – for the moment, anyway, as she follows Jonathan blindfolded. Jonathan leads her up a slight incline slowly. "I think not telling you is kind of the point," he says chuckling. As they get to a stone slab, Jonathan sets the picnic basket in his hand on top of it and grabs her by her waist. "Are you ready? At three, bend your legs and jump. One…two…" "What are you talking about, don’t do – AH!" she shouts, as Jonathan lifts her on top of the slab, then quickly scrambles up himself. He once again grabs her hand and picks up the basket, then begins to walk slowly. "What’s all of this for, Jonathan?" Jonathan sighs. "This is for us, Katie. We’ve been together for awhile, but we completely skipped the getting-to-know-you phase in the relationship. You went from a casual date to a live-in girlfriend to a wrestling valet, and there was absolutely no clutch when we shifted that fast." Katie chuckles. "Points for the car analogy." "Thanks. Anyway, it made me think about how we need more you and me time. Time to discover each other again. Time to find out exactly who Katie Malick and Jonathan Rhine are." He slips down to another level at the end of the slab, then grabs Katie’s hand and gestures for her to follow. "And one of the first things I want to know about you: do you absolutely trust me?" "I…I guess so. Why?" "Because there’s a five foot drop about ten inches from where you’re standing. As you can probably tell from my voice, I’m below you. Now the question is this: do you trust me enough to keep walking? Do you think I’ll catch you?" Katie’s mouth forms a frown, and she scratches her head idly. "Uh…I’m not sure. What’s at the bottom?" Jonathan smiles, looking down at the smooth surface. "Sharp rocks." "Jonathan!" she shouts, kicking her foot off of the side and then wailing, flipping her arms to and fro as she tries to keep her balance. Jonathan starts laughing as Katie finds her footing and puts her hands on her hips, looking in Jonathan’s general direction and scowling. "Jonathan Gregory Rhine! Don’t scare me like this! I will not play these games and I will not…" "Katie." "What?" "Shut up and trust me. Fall." After two seconds, Katie steps out dramatically and lets out a cry of fear that becomes a peal of laughter as Jonathan grabs her and sets her down. He takes off her blindfold and turns her to the Mississippi River, where on the banks of the levee Jonathan has set up his surprise. Three feet from the muddy banks of the river, there is a large glass table, bookended by two chairs. A vase of three yellow roses sit in the center of the table, and there is an envelope leaning against the vase marked Katie. "I got reservations for a table for two, so I think that’s us," says Jonathan with a smile as he leads Katie towards the table. 16.5 the mirror: hoping Despite how smoothly you pulled it off, you’re incredibly nervous as you sit down. That was the easy part. Mystery brings intrigue. Brings something to talk about. Now the two of you are going to have nothing to distract yourselves from each other. What are you going to talk to her about? You’ve decided that you won’t talk about your job or your family. You want to know about her. You want to know her ins, outs, strengths, weaknesses…a particularly sad thing is you don’t even know her favorite color. And if you’re going to stay with her rather than venture out to other pastures, you better figure out if it’s green. Or blue. So you start to think of things to talk about, and a really scary thought enters your head. You’re hoping that there’s nothing interesting to say. You’re hoping that you can’t keep a conversation going long. Essentially, you’re hoping that she can’t give you anything that Sarah can. It’s a weird position to take, but you are trying your damndest to convince yourself that Katie is not the person you’re supposed to be with. And since you are the one who arbitrarily set these rules for figuring out if Katie is the person you want to be with forever, you are also the person who can edit those rules. And the only rule you’re thinking about now is if she doesn’t make a case for herself in the next thirty minutes, you’re going to have to find a way to let her down easy. 16.6 the window: action couple "Blue, of course," Katie says, giggling. "I don’t really blame you for not knowing. You’re absolutely right about us. We don’t know much about each other besides surface value. What’s yours?" Jonathan looks down at his red shirt and points. "My trunks are red. You could guess." "And you could guess because I wear blue shirts!" she laughs, and slaps him across the arm. "You should also remember that I’m not a big fan of burgers," she says, looking down on her cheeseburger. "Really?" Jonathan’s face falls into a hopeless frown. "I’m sorry, I didn’t…" "I’m just kidding," she says, biting a big chunk out of the burger and chewing between smiles. "You’re doing fine, Jon. Don’t treat this like it’s a first date. Everything’s okay." Jonathan sighs. "It feels like a first date, kind of. I mean, I just don’t know what to say. And…" he sighs, looking up at her deliberately. "And I don’t know what that means." Katie frowns, and there are several seconds of silence. Then, Katie smiles. "But that’s the thing about us, Jon. We’re not the ones who talk. We’re not a talking couple. We’re an action couple." "An action couple?" "Yeah. We might not be able to express our true selves and I might get worried at the thoughts you keep locked up in there, but at the same time our love for each other is found in what we do. How I gave up my school to be with you. How you nearly killed Clinton Sage for the things he did for me. How we both know, without having to say it, without having to fill up a dinner with conversation, without knowing each other’s favorite color, that we are dedicated and are completely in love. Because we act on it." Jonathan finishes chewing his burger and stares at Katie for a few seconds before shrugging. "I guess that’s true. But it still makes me think…" "No thinking, Jon. Acting. Watch." Katie looks over her shoulders, then stands up and starts walking towards the shore of the river. Jon stands up, looking at her cautiously. She turns around and smiles at Jon. Then she takes off her shirt and throws it to the side. "What are you doing?" asks Jonathan, suddenly more nervous. "Ready for a swim?" "No!" He shakes his head. "That river is nasty. It’ll ruin your clothes!" Katie takes off her pants and underwear. "Problem solved. Come on." Without another word, Katie runs into the unclean river. Jonathan watches her, horrified, for a few seconds. Then, slowly, the grimace turns into a grin, and he takes off his shirt, walking in after her. 16.7 the mirror: next act You wanted a reason. You wanted knowledge. You wanted to find the key to your entire relationship with Katie. It was a stretch to ask for, but somehow you still got it. An action couple. It sounds somewhat hollow, especially in light of the mentally excavating relationship you’ve had with Sarah Riley. But that act, the crazy, insane, dangerous act that Katie committed (and you followed) was one very important thing. Interesting. Intriguing. You were hoping that there wasn’t anything interesting to say, and for the most part you were right. You didn’t find much else about her through conversation. But in your little spelunking adventure, you found out about you. The two of you. You found there’s still hope. You might not be the most revealing couple. You might not confess hopes, fears, mundane details of your lives. But you feel for each other, and it shows. In that moment, swimming with your girlfriend in your hometown river with nothing to say and nothing needed to be said, you felt love. You felt the world. You felt nothing for Sarah. The two of you went back to Indianapolis, and it was back to normal. No earth-shattering conversation, no sudden realization of love for her. Even the feelings for Sarah came back. But now, instead of waiting for the ball to drop, instead of thinking about how you are going to get out of your relationship, you’re thinking about the next fun time. The next act. And suddenly, waiting doesn’t seem so bad after all.
