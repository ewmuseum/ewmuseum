Soundtrack of your life: Track 6

25 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 25, 2009 Danko Jones, "Time Heals Nothing" (Sleep is the Enemy, 2006) This is becoming far too common an occurrence. I am beginning to recognize commonalities in the faces of the strangers that populate and make hurried work in whatever designated area of suffering it was tonight. There was always a twinkle to eyes not surrounded by wrinkles. Greetings with a smile that you would want to knock off if it had not attached the accommodations of cots behind iron bars. A collection of kids entirely too happy to be stitching up weathered punching bags. The room is the kind of nondescript area that you suppose this type of suffering should commence. Lazy illuminations flickered from the ceiling and made shadows dance on the floor. Oh that floor; dirty and dusty and with cracks running down the scruff concrete surface. But damn if I was not tired enough that the thought of slumber on that floor appear heavenly. All this observation of my surrounds made me miss the first time one of the young fixers enquired. It was on the second attempt, and with his hands and fingers snapping in my face that got the attention he sought. "Mr. Quinlan, do you have any allergies that we should know about?" Apparently I had become allergic to lashings and whippings with leather, but my tongue was held in check by my fatigue. A simple, "no," was all that I offered. I felt the scratching of towels less than cashmere attempt to soak the blood than now stained them. My blood. I wished they the best of luck in the attempts to wipe clean and prep. In vain I tried to will my veins to slow and clot. Such lucky was not the case. It irritated much more to know that I would depend on they for this. "Have at me. Fix me up," I pleaded to no one, I think. They had been more much involved with the plan and the communication. They had taken it as token. I was to be their pin cushion regardless of what words of permission let slip from my teeth. And so hands went flying about my head, about the room, and sent mine spinning. I caught my head in time to see the syringe where I hate to see it most – to see it at all. "Don’t need it." "Pardon me?" "The needle you have in your hand. I don’t need it." "Mitchell, the cameras are not on back here. You need not act so macho. This is freezing. Your laceration will be a painful one to close up." "You hearing me junior? I said no needles. Just drop that thing and close me up already." It was then that another mentioned the method to which they might do such a thing. This would not be the first time I was stapled shut. I kept my bravado on full display and stayed as stubborn as ever. They sighed. I laughed. They sunk the first one in. I was looking for something to bite on. It all came rushing back as I bowed my head and gazed upon the gateway to recklessness. Those spider shaped scars of knives and staples. What a sweet, chemically induced sleep did I sleep through the addition of these brandings. What a saint, the man who took pity. Often I have wondered how life was for him. Would God see it that the man who delivers so many from agony be spared his own? Or did he only know the importance of his duty through the understanding that he would be the man that he needed at some point of his own continuity? The second came with the same punch as the first. I know I had been in more pain before, I just failed to recall when. Gone was the general anaesthesia of adrenaline. Fading was the shock of it all. Numbness would come only from a constant stinging that would be better without. But this is why I am here. I wanted anything but to be numb. Desired an indication that I was akin to alive. I do believe that these have been satisfied. I took an active determination to take what dreams I had left, and I would force them to be real, to be breathed. Open the nostrils and smell what pungent agents lathered my shoulder to kept away infection. This was so much like jumping at the fall. I had to question whether I would wake up from my dreams, or become a mark on the sidewalk to which I fall every night. I braced for the third with the expectation that the pain might amplify. Indeed, it did not disappoint. Oh how my parents would love so much if I woke to see the nightmare they see, to give up the ghost and just a contented dweller of a cubical. They wish the world might be made of such softer things as gummy bears and pillows, and that I may never face pain. But who are they to deny me such a feeling? Long ago the apple was bit and the die cast. From the knowledge gained we shall all know the feeling. Ignorance is not an option. Even against the most careful laid plans. The cold touch of the gun pressed against my back gave shivers I tested to stop. I did not want more than needed. Far from anything I may have portrayed, I do not love this sorrow. Rather I see it as offspring of ideals carried very close to the heart. There the fourth and mine skipped a beat. I know they want what they think is best, but often they see it best that I be the child still in the womb. Yet I have been long in this world. They have not seen me toughen to the man they need not worry about, despite best efforts. I do wish they never to worry, and I wonder if they do too? Frequently the thought has crept about that it would be best I never was born, but do the share the same? Would it be best if, since not being able to alter the past, I make a motion to the present and see no future? In my death would they relieve themselves of worry? Their worry would be replaced by regret that would swallow them whole. There is a sight to see in the HD vision of the mind. Empty grave soon not so. Rainfall all around the greying grounds. No one but the priest obligated to something much greater than his disinterest in the life and times of the boxed. A hanging tree the lone sentinel of life encaged in the haunted gates. Five. The synchronized stabbing of metal, an event to never be bided to the games from Olympia. Never will I get used to that sensation. Oh why did I choose to have any sensation at all? The pulse of punishment crashes like waves in the brain such that it breaks the levies, and flood the area. What a perplexing idea that a wound be closed up by making, what now? Ten more? Did he crack that whip with such righteousness that a canyon become of my back? When might I get the chance to issue the invoice? How in that moment I was evil. Evil enough that my deepest wish was to have the ability to ride on the horse of war, to take away the peace of man and leave them in pieces. It is this awkward profession I have chosen - had chosen me - I reason must make me want so much not to be the meek. This earth is not mine to inherit, nor do I want it. But, if I could, I might just live in a moment of satisfaction to see the suffering in the eyes of those at my feet. Six. And with this added straw of steel, my concentration of wicked was broken. Refocused was my attention to the things I know. I know I want. I want what I have grown up with. What now was being taken from I. Hated the helplessness I felt when I pictured the post and advertisement that flaunted the sale of my pride and joy. They wanted out themselves. A chance to follow their own dreams and wake from the nightmare drones that they had allowed themselves to be for a paycheck. I could not hold contempt for such ideals. I wish only that they make it work. Make it worth the sale of my comfort. Comfort. There is a foreign thought in these moments. The solution made one more on-edge than the voices of thousands screaming something that sounds like praise. Instead I get the sound of the "click." In went seven. How large had this been? How many more did they require? The one they kept in front of me, who barked instructions and silly terms of encouragement, after each successive shot offering, "just one more." He lied through his teeth. I thought I could solve that. I could punch them out. That moment greatness would be being Spacely and travelling time forward to a point where I had nothing but a scar and a story. I would settle for the psychotic drugs that make him think he may partake in the promise Einstein and quantum physics tendered. I gave up on Physics too early perhaps. But did I really need the knowledge of how much force I drove into the face of an opponent via my knees? Taken to the scientific method, and repeated my experiment to success. Knees to face = perfect set up. I had given up too easily on too much. Eight. Bloody, cursive eight. We are the hollow men, and Thine is the Kingdom Prayer. It had taken that long to turn to Him. He holds all that I need. He promises the food for which I will never hunger further, the wine from which my throat will never be parched again. All that is asked of I is obedience. "He will not do with miracles that which can be brought through simple obedience," the words of a priest, pastor, light long since extinguished. Gone to the big promotion in the sky. I sit in silence. A silence that scares they, but phases me not. I tune my ears to hear the Word. A silence broken by the breaking of the ninth seal. "For Thine is the Kingdom For Thine is Life is For Thine is the This is the way the world ends This is the way the world ends This is the way the world ends Not with a bang but a whimper" And I am not ready for this world to end. So I stop with the whimpering. I get out the guns of ambition and desire, and make a hell of a bang. Nine is enough. And I rise like Dr. Frankenstein’s creation. I moan when I try to speak the words. I communicate that I am done well enough. Take away the torches and staple guns. Bring forward the items of fear and intimidation and let your neck prepare for the choke. With a misunderstood (if understood to any degree) sense of triumph I walk through the doors back into the fray. I get four steps out, when I bow my head; they tell me that I need still to be taped up. So when they finish taping me up, I am back to the fray. You know I am going to live through this, and when the wound on my back heals I am coming to get you. I am coming to get you.
