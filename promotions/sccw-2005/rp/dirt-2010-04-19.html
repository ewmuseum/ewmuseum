Dirt

19 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/04/2010 I Hannah Lowe blew her reflection a kiss. With her collagen-plumped lips newly slick with glittery pink lip-gloss, Hannah gave her sunken cheeks a final dusting of peach. Somewhere under the outer-crust of terracotta foundation was a 19 year old girl who had once dreamed of being a vet; but when sweaty, rosy-cheeked businessmen mentioned doggy or balls to her, they most definitely weren’t asking for their pet to be neutered. The sour looks from all who passed her in the hotel corridor refused to let Hannah pretend that she was anything other than a whore. The only eyes not so cruel as to judge her saw nothing above her neck. Tongues stroked their lips at the corners as they wondered how much change they’d get from their weekly wage to fling those stiletto heels over their shoulders and vent the animalistic urges that their wedding band had corked. The scalding showers and feverish scrubbing couldn’t cleanse the stains those lingering looks left. Deep breath. Stomach in, chest out. On perilous heels, Hannah made her way up to the hotel room door; number 214. The warm, mellow buzz she felt from the joint she’d smoked on the drive over made her perilous heels feel a few inches higher. The sensations of numbness that massaged her nerves made it all just a little bit more bearable. Cheap bracelets jangled on her bony wrist as Hannah knocked on the door. No amount of dope or vodka could fend off the icy kick to the spine the dull echo of knuckle on wood gave her. Twirling a lock of golden hair around her finger, Hannah seductively looked up as the door swung open. "Hey. I’m Hannah." He was certainly a little more ‘polished’ than her usual cliental. He had a waist, for a start. A heady waft of vanilla and fresh linen escaped from room, a welcome change from stale sweat and cologne-veiled flatulence. The man in the doorway looked deep into her eyes, past the gaudy ultramarine contacts and dilated pupils. "You’ll do," Christian Alexander Kannon dryly decreed. II "Well, I… uh… I got… um… I got nothing, boss." Magnum Wolf’s voice petered out under the wilting glare of his employer. "Guy actually seems, well, nice and all." Stroking his bushy moustache, the nervy Magnum stood awaiting the inevitable interrogation. "So you’re telling me that, in the month since Temptation, your haven’t been able to give me one measly bit of dirt to smear all over Marshall’s face?" Christian Alexander Kannon probed, words chilling enough to freeze the bead of sweat tickling the tip of Magnum’s nose. "Nothing? At all?" Gulping, Wolf’s Adam’s apple hopped up above the stiff collar of his sweat-patchy charcoal grey shirt. Despite being the twitching rabbit in Christian’s headlights, his salt-stung eyes were helplessly drawn towards Eleanor. A patronisingly apologetic smile met him as her head shook in disappointment. "Boss, if I was any more up his ass these past few weeks my morning iced coffee would have given him a fuckin’ enema!" Magz protested in his own poetic way. "Eww," whined Eleanor as she felt her Eggs Florentine curdle in her stomach at the imagery. "Like, just had breakfast here." "I trusted you with this, Aaron," growled Kannon, serrated words tearing Wolf’s attention back to him. "I gave you a helping hand up when you’d hit rock bottom, did I not? I compensate you most generously for your time and alleged effort, do I not? So why, when I tell you that I need to drag Lance Marshall‘s name through the mud, do you come back to me with NOTHING?" As stunned as Magz by her husband’s sudden, violent eruption, Eleanor shoots Wolf a teasing look of you got in trooooouble! mischief. "I… he…" Magnum stammered, the excuses slipping off his tongue. "I… um… what if we’re… um… going about this wrong?" Christian Alexander Kannon didn’t answer to the staff. He left just enough rope dangling through the silence for Wolf to either scramble up to freedom or hang himself with. "I mean, when did we ever let the truth get in the way of anything?". Desperate eyes were swatted back and forth between the unimpressed Kannons. "I picked through the guy’s fuckin’ garbage and couldn’t find anything to use against him. Some people are just… y’know, good people? And that‘s the truth." A sinister grin lit up Wolf’s downtrodden features. "So we create a lie so juicy that everyone will want to believe it." III "Whoa… wait," Hannah Lowe urged. "Nobody told me anything about this being a couples job." Reclining against the inviting mound of pillows on the king-size four poster bed, Eleanor Kannon-Hall gave her husband’s purchase a judgement-laden glance. Eleanor’s platinum blonde hair was as vibrant as always, a Grecian plait placing a halo around her head. Midnight blue silk pyjamas were more alluring hanging from her petite frame than any of the cheap and nasty suspender belts and crotch-less panties Hannah would wear for her sleazy clientele, while the natural make-up tones only highlighted Ellie’s perfect bone-structure, making the thick plastering that adorned Hannah’s face look more suited to a drag queen. "I’ll just be watching, sweetie," Eleanor chirped. "I like your… um, nails?" Naturally, she was teasing. They were vulgar, acrylic abominations; there simply weren’t enough hours in the day to spend on her back in order to afford the salons Ellie frequented. Hannah Lowe had never been made to feel so cheap; the smugly satisfied grin that unfurled across Eleanor’s lips betraying the delight she took in this. "I’m not cool with this," Hannah nervously protested, wary that Kannon was now leant back against the closed hotel room door. "I think I’d bette-" "Quiet," Kannon ordered, not seeing his purchase as worthy of politeness. After all, he hadn’t ordered a human being; he’d ordered a whore. "Sit down. Not there… the one with the throw. And for the love of Hubbard keep your clothes on, I already feel like I need a shower." She’d been spat on, pissed on, and for $250 on rent day, she’d been shit on. She’d been called a whore, a slut, a bitch, and a filthy cum-guzzling anal whore. She’d been punched, cut and torn. Hannah Lowe would never say that the sneering words of Christian Alexander Kannon were her most degrading experience, but that made the experience none the more pleasant. "Look, I don’t know what they said when you booked me," she appealed as Kannon herded her to the chair. "But I thi-" "Oh, we didn’t book you, sweetie," Ellie’s words interrupted despite her eyes no longer caring to look up at Hannah. "You were… hmmm… the fifth one we picked? Four others were obviously after the same thing as us." "What were you looking for?" Hannah tentatively asked. "Desperation, mainly," Kannon shrugged as he collapsed onto the suite’s inviting couch. "That’s the key ingredient. Then mix with a little hint of vulnerability, stir in the fact that you look about fourteen, then sprinkle with a broken family et voilà… you get, well, you get sympathy." Christian Alexander Kannon was impeccably dressed and well groomed. His body language was relaxed, non-threatening. But just from his voice; a calculating tone devoid of any warmth or good intention, Hannah knew she should be fearful of him. In her profession, reading a client meant the difference between a cab ride home or an ambulance dash to the ER. "What do you want from me?" A pleading look tried to milk answers from Eleanor, but she was far too busy updating her Twitter feed about how she was generally more hella fetch than any of her followers. "Please… I have a little boy." "Birth control has been around since Egyptian times so I hardly see how that’s my fault." The blunt words of Christian has as little sympathy as they did civility. "Hannah, I’ll be quick. For my benefit, I add… I’m already feeling about fifty engrams worming about inside me since you got here. Hannah, I’m offering to pay you $10,000... cash. "What… what do you want to do to me..?" "Please, Hannah, look around," Kannon scoffed, his wife offering a disparaging giggle of her own. "You are as out of place here as I… see, I can’t even draw a comparison." As Hannah shuffled deeper into her chair, Kannon hopped up off the couch and slowly approached her. Kneeling down beside the chair, he rested his elbows on its arms, looking right into her eyes. "I want you to think of your worst experience, Hannah," he whispered into her ear with delight. "Every last ugly detail. The pain, the degradation, the helplessness… relive it. Unlock the memories from wherever you hid them, Hannah." "Stop!" she demanded, eyes glistening with stifled tears. "Just stop it, okay? You‘ve got money and I don‘t… I get it." "That’ll be the memories unlocked then," Kannon smirked, not even flinching at her distress. "I want you to keep them fresh in your mind, Hannah. Repeat them. Over and over. Until every detail is ingrained in your brain… then I want you to tell the police that a man by the name of Lance Marshall forced himself upon you did all of it." "W-hat?" a teary Hannah gasped. "I want you to help me ruin an innocent man’s life, Hannah." Pulling himself up on the arm of the chair, Kannon’s face crept as close to Hannah’s ear as he could stand. "So, let me ask you, Hannah; what won’t you do for money?" IV This is the life, thought Magnum Wolf. The chauffer-driven Mercedes-Benz rental had just picked him up from the massage parlour where he’d enjoyed a little stress relief on the boss’ wallet. Looking the part of the respectable employee, a sharp new suit seemed riveted to his skin, a far cry from the ill-fitting, holey and stained shirts and jeans he’d worn for years. Shoes were shined, hair was trimmed, and his moustache was vibrant and bushy; an indicator of his revitalisation. Duran Duran’s Hungry Like the Wolf trickled out of his breast pocket. "Yo, boss," Magnum Wolf boomed into his employer-funded iPhone. "She doing it? - - - What? Since when did any fuckin’ whore outside of a Hollywood script have morals. All the diseases eatin’ away insider her and she’s still got room in there for a conscience. - - - Yeah, I know what to do. - - - All the info is ready to send back at the hotel. Head of child protection services has a few nasty little secrets on his hard drive, so unless he wants to spend many, many painful and humiliating years in prison as some lifer’s girlfriend… poor Hannah’s little boy will be waking up tomorrow in a foster home. - - - Fuck her. We offered the bitch a new life. Bet that $10,000 won’t sound so fuckin’ bad tomorrow. - - - Always a pleasure. Later, boss." All Magnum Wolf had to do to maintain his new lifestyle was not grow a conscience. Shouldn’t be a problem.
