What's Up Doc?

17 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/17/07 'It's unrealistic and downright pathetic! I won't do it! No sir.' 'Well thats fine Mr. Bolamba, but you also won't be cleared to wrestle if you don't pass the psych exam. It's fairly clear you have some hidden enemies, and all we want to do is talk to you about that a little.' 'That's it?' 'That's it.' Dr. Swartsky's office was sparse. It would seem that he left very little for the mind to play with as he intended to play with the minds of the patients. Over and over Timo kept mulling the possible outcomes of visiting such a quack, but the company suggested it after his last little outbreak on live television. No matter. The Dastardly One could easily self contain his true feelings. He didn't feel as though there were anything he needed or even wanted to tell this man, and as such he wouldn't. So the battle would go, yet he had no idea what he was in for. 'Right then, so shall we begin Mr. Bolamba?' 'Sure doc, it's your life, use it how you want.' The doctor leaned to one side and made a note in his book, then continued. 'Okay Timo, why don't you tell me about whats going on inside lately?' The Bastard raises a brow and snorts defiantly. 'Well, the other day I really had to use the can but Big Lou was bogarding the bathroom. Is that what you mean?' The doctor sighs a little and shakes his head. Finally Timo takes his turn to sigh and he finally concedes a little effort to the exam. 'Fine. There are two living entities in my one body, one good one evil, and right now I kicked the pussy side of myself in the nuts and took over. I'd really rather the little bitch didn't get control again, so if you could avoid trying to shrink me and make me realize my potential as a good person, I would greatly appreciate that doc.' The doctor scribbles a little more in his notepad and furrows his brows as Timo talks. Finally he stops writing and looks up to see Timo waiting for a response. He calculates a little before answering. 'I am not here to change you Timo. I am here to help you try and make sense of the problems inside. If you feel like this other person is hurting you, then it is my job to help you deal with that problem.' Timo took his time, turning the words over, then he answered the question honestly. 'Okay, in that case, there is another side of me. I do not know how or why, but I cannot seem to leave this side behind no matter what I do.' He pauses briefly, thinking his next question out. 'Tell me doc, do you think it's abnormal that no matter what, I can never seem to pick one side or another, I just seem to walk along in my life and things just happen or don't? I cannot be both good and bad, I must be starkly one or the other. Is that normal?' Dr. Swartsky wrote a few notes then leaned forward to return fire. 'Often times Timo, the feeling we get of Good and Bad is merely a perspective point. I bet that you feel just fine as 'The Dastardly Bastard' doing the things you do and talking the way you talk right?' Timo nods. 'You also probably feel as though the other side of things, the 'good side' as you call it, is wrong. Yet the other side feels that you are wrong. He wants desperately for you to control yourself and act like a sensible member of society. That, however, is not you is it?' Timo shakes his head. 'Hell no, I am a fully loaded pistol doc. I feel the pull of the ladies, the inert desire to deal my damage before my opponents, and the deep remorse that I will never be able to have half as beautiful and ending as my counterpart will. No matter what I want, they people love him, and they want him back. I just want to have fun doc, and to me, anyone and and anything that stands in my way is worthless.' The doctor scribbles something then looks up. 'Like Jonathan Rhine?' Immediately this causes a knee jerk reaction from Timo. 'Fuck him! I do not feel as though he is anything more than a worm in my path doc. You and I both know that the real problem here is Clinton Sage and the belt he holds.' Doc Swartsky shakes his head. 'I do not believe that is the problem Timo. You certainly reacted as though Rhine was a problem for you, and you entered straight into a denial, which is a tell tale sign of a lie. Why don't we pause a moment to analyze this road?' The Bastard rolled his eyes and whisked out his cop shades briefly, then flipped them on, covering his eyes from the surrounding world. The doctor noted the move in his pad before continuing. 'Okay Timo, lets talk. Jonathan Rhine beat you in the title match, and now he has tried to confront you only to be met with a very visceral reaction from your 'Bastard' persona. Why do you hate him so much?' Timo leans back on the couch and crosses his arms across his thick Samoan chest. 'I don't know...I suppose he makes me feel as though I am terrible person, and it makes me want to hurt him badly. The old me would probably even be friends with this guy, and that is something I cannot abide by.' 'Why?' Timo raises a brow from behind his glasses. 'What do you mean why? I can't be seen with someone who is a goody goody like him! Think of what it will do to my street cred doc! I am a man who gets respect by taking it, and this jackoff is no different. He will come around eventually and realize that if he cant beat me, he will join me. That's how it works my man, The Bastard brings the people to him, not the other way around.' The doctor jots more notes, and looks up sullenly. He nocks his own reading glasses and quietly follows up the question. 'Okay, so what is it about Clinton Sage that makes you feel the way you do? Both of you represent a similar path in life, why can't you respect him if that is true?' Timo smiles. 'He has my belt Doc. I want that piece of gold, and I wont stop until I get it. I don't care if I have to bring out any part of me, I WILL get another shot at that belt.' The doctor nods and smiles a little. 'Well Mr. Bolamba, I think we have made a little progress already.' Timo lowers his shades slightly, and gives a quizzical eye to the doctor. 'Oh?' 'Well, it seems to me that your problem with Jonathan Rhine isn't him at all. The problem is that you view him as a roadblock to the top, and until you challenge this roadblock and accept that you can climb over it, whether you are victorious or not, you will not be able to beat Clinton Sage.' For the first time in a few months, Timo sat up and looked absolutely dumbstruck. For a character as cock sure as The Bastard, this was a totally new feeling. 'So you are saying...that I will never beat the Clinton Sage's in my life until I beat the Jonathan Rhines...' He pauses. 'And The Jonathan Rhine in my life is really my other side 'The Samoan Silencer'. It makes sense now when you say it doc! I hate Rhine because he reminds me of my old self!' Dr. Swartsky smiles a little and jots some more notes down. The good doctor seems to have made at least a little headway in todays session and that alone is worth its weight with a headstrong character like The Bastard. 'Do you feel as though you know how to beat these feelings Timo?' The Samoan nods solemnly. 'Well, my previous method of trying to party him out of my system didn't seem to work. I guess I am going to have to go ahead and admit that was a failure...A very fun failure, but a failure nonetheless. I have just formulated a plan however...a way to kill two birds with one stone if you will.' As The Bastard stood up quickly he balled a fist and raised it heavenward, smiling wickedly. 'I am going to confront my demon, and win or lose, I will walk past it on my way to the top! And when I get to the top doc, I'll be sending two hot lady friends to visit for a 'consultation'.' The doctor laughed a little. 'I am sure that won't be necessary. You can thank me by coming back next week, then we can talk a little about accepting your shortcomings and turning them into strengths Timo. You must learn to accept your life for what it is, and when you do, no force can be as powerful as a single driven mind.' The Samoan puffed out his chest and shook his fist hard, smiling the entire time. He knew that sooner or later this world he lived in would have to face up with his past life. Maybe the doctor was right though, maybe what he really needed was to confront the issue and see what happened when the occasion came about. Either way, it would be an interesting week. Timo turned back to the doctor just before leaving and removed his shades just long enough to ask one question in earnest. 'Doc...if I face these problems...do you think I can do it? Do you think I can win the belt and show the world that there is still fight in this old man' Doctor Swartsky looked up at the genuine eyes of his patient, and noted that for the first time since they had met this afternoon, the look was genuine. 'I think that if you do this Timo, you will gain whatever respect you are searching for in your life, and it will come from a place you never expected.' The Bastard blinked a few times before slipping his shades back on and opening the door. He slipped outside and noted a good looking receptionist he had made eyes at before. Schlepping up to her briskly and with much pomp, he offered her a ride on Mount Samoa before she turned red and fanned her face. For all Timo's recent vulnerability, he did not show one iota as he left the establishment in a flurry of self professing comments about his genitals and general talk about parties he had recently attended. Doctor Swartsky looked down at his notes and smiled before closing the door and speaking aloud to himself briefly. 'Master these things Timo, and you will be unstoppable.'
