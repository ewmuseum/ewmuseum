Climbing Up The Walls

14 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/02/09 My first submission loss… Dusk carried on his little hot streak in the best way he could and what better way to send a message to the Dead Man’s Hand by showing their inevitable fate? The pain I felt following the match wasn’t anything new to me per se but my mind needed to get used to the constant flights to and fro between NFW and SCCW, what I didn’t expect was the surprise when I opened the door back to the hotel room. Luci was there looking like she hadn’t slept in days, a pile of tapes on the floor featuring one woman in question., the one that had captured her following Defiance II in New Frontier Wrestling’s own Teresa Quaranta, she explained to me that Teresa had captured her and tried to do her own version of the burial scene in Kill Bill with cement. There was only one flaw in Teresa’s plan: she didn’t bring enough meaning an escape was somehow possible, I told her to sort out a plan for NFW, I had my own dilemma to take care of, which side would I choose at Sin On Spike? Until then I had received word that it would be me Vs Paul Searle for the number one contendership of the Livewire title in Maryland… Looking through SCCW bios I noticed that Mr Searle was trained by the man now calling himself Preacher: Jay Draven, searching through message boards people were saying how Jay had trained him to a capable standard but there wasn’t that much to tell as of yet. He obviously hasn’t met me yet… my history of testing people to their potential hadn’t gone un-noticed by those same fans from the angle I had done with who I thought was Rook Black in NFW and the fans were hoping I’d make Paul break out of his shell. That became the motivation until I remembered who the Livewire champion was… Bridden had become the so called ‘Ace’ in Desade’s little band of mercs and misfits but it seemed he had an equaliser in Minorez who seemed to be the latest temptress in a long line. Minorez hasn’t met Luci yet… if the time comes she will make sure any equalisers are eradicated by any means… regardless of which side is chosen. I suppose the sides need a ‘wild card’ to fit in the deck sooner or later but the chaos truly begins in Maryland as the rejects truly ride in SCCW for the first time…
