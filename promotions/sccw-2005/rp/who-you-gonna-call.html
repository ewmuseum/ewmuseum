Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today My life can be drab. At its core, every day is a blank sheet of looseleaf. On it, some mystical and clearly scatter-brained writer jots down ideas for a good story. They scribble situations that eventually get scrapped. That is the make-up of my weeks, months and years, where the only central theme is the chaos of it all - the tiniest of threads that somehow manages to bind the pages. I haven't opened my laptop to write these little notes in a while, and some things have gotten lost in my memory because of it. My best friend suggested that I take an inventory of my life as it stands right now, with the goal of helping me clearly see the varying importances of what I have. She probably didn't mean it literally, but here goes nothing... I have a home. Two and a half levels of nothing incredibly spectacular. It's probably more than I need, but that's a given when it's coming from someone who's slept in cardboard boxes just to sustain their habits. I've got a car that sits next to Darcy's in the garage. I have masses of gadgets and stuff -- just 'stuff'; completely ordinary, run-of-the-mill crap -- tucked into every drawer and compartment I can find. I pay a lot for clothes made to look like I didn't pay a lot for them, and I spend more time than I should deliberately finding outfits that will look like I threw them on when I stepped out of bed. My hair is naturally red, but that's not good enough -- it's not noticable or different or eccentric enough for me to be who I am, so I spend about fifty bucks a month dying it brighter red. My downstairs hall closet is completely dedicated to sneakers that everybody save for DJ AM and most of the hip hop community would call ugly. My cat is fat, black, fluffy and essentially just an opinionated dog. He's as feline as he needs to be. My husband has stuck by and surprised me at every corner in the last few years. He's not nearly as high-strung as I am, but he's sick of the wrestling lifestyle. Instead, he spends most of his time working for The Institute, a non-profit organization dedicated to... I think they help kids or something. I should Google that. Either way, he's happy. At least, I think he is. And I should stop there, because skimming back over those few paragraphs has me realizing that nothing I percieve seems to be right to the rest of the world. Between the my eyes, my mind and my mouth, reality gets altered. Maybe that's what got me into this situation. Maybe I need to be doing more than just taking inventory... I don't understand why home is such an uncomfortable topic for me. I also can't really figure out why I've been doing the things I've been doing. Call it bipolar, but it's nearly impossible for me to explain what I really want out of any given instance. It admittedly wasn't one of my finer moments when I told some jerkoff in a random bar to piss off. Maybe if he'd said anything offensive, I could've gotten away with it - but in a slight drunken stupor, I'd actually fallen over him and spilled my drink. That thirty seconds of stupidity snowballed into yesterday. It kept rolling through today, and now I'm sitting in a hotel room that's not mine, pouring a bucket of emo into my laptop while I put off calling that supposedly happy husband of mine to tell him that he deserves better than me. Aside from being the truth, that's also my way of escaping having to admit that I'm fundamentally fucked up and honestly can't seem to set my compass to pick the path that suits me best. I have so many faces that I can't even be sure which I'm wearing right now. They look the same in the mirror, but the truth seems to lie behind my eyes. I'm a wife, a cheater, an addict, a fighter, a friend and a great lover, but I started this talking about central themes and threads that tie everything else together. 'Liar' seems to apply best. Or maybe coward, because I seem to be too afraid to catch one of the threads that might bind the pages of my days, weeks, months and years to a greater purpose. I can't help but feel like we've got bigger things to worry about, like...'' ''Amy.'' The voice on the other line was soothing normally, but grew assertive. Darcy knew when she needed to be grounded to something. ''I know how you blow things out of proportion. Don't. Everything's fine here. Quinn's great, the cat's alive and we're all surviving even when you're this busy.'' ''But...'' ''And if this is about how I Googled Shaw two months ago, I'm gonna be a bit pissed off.'' He chuckled, ''You're probably just missing Reaper or hating the hotel rooms, but don't let it get you down.'' Her husband could be assertive, yes -- but he was also perfectly capable of absolutely relentless positivity, and it could easily reach the point of literally loving Amy into submission. ''Y'know what? You're right.'' He couldn't have been more wrong, but Campbell knew when to wear the liar's mask -- it had already gotten her out of a bad spot once that week. ''I'm probably just overreacting.'' Not only did she lie, she developed an escape plan on the spot. ''Can I call you back in a bit? I'm gonna take a shower, I think.'' ''Sure. You... don't normally say 'You're right'. Everything okay?'' ''Absolutely.'' Not. ''And Darce?'' When she continued, the words weren't a lie. After all the Raver had prepared to do, she'd found her pulse quickly slow after moments of his voice. Desade thrilled her, but Darcy seemed to calm her. Complete her, even. There was only one out of the two that she'd directly say it to, though doing so made her feel worse. ''You're a douche, but I do love you.'' The laugh on the other line made her heart skip a beat. Little things like that made her question her choices again and again. ''You too. Go shower, I'm gonna try to catch the rest of this hockey game... just don't let Quinn suck you into conversation when you call back.'' ********************************** Just like I didn't tell him the truth, I didn't call back. Maybe I do get a thrill out of being this person. She's quick-witted, she can evoke any number of emotions from just about anybody and by God, can she ever fight. Too bad she's a coward.
