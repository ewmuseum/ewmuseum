Trashed And Scattered (THE CRAPPIER PART TWO)

Date: Today Sometime in 2000... Touring in Canada was always rough, if not just because of the cold. Darcy Markson was born and would eternally be a Californian, and the temperatures he experienced outside of some of the hockey arenas he'd been wrestling in were akin to hell being frozen over. Then again, it was Canada. He was in one of the prarie provinces, relaxing alone at a table in a local dive when he spotted the only thing that could have made his time in the hellhole worse. He tried hard not to acknowledge her presence, but she accidentally made eye contact while scanning the bar, prompting him to think aloud ''I hardly recognize you without our boss feeling you up.'' Aimz brushed the comment off and walked past Darcy, who couldn't help noticing that she looked even more miserable than usual. He took another chance at getting her attention, maybe in a twisted way to decipher what was wrong. ''Shouldn't you be at the hotel earning your next title shot?'' The reality of that night crossed their paths for a long time to come when the redhead finally answered... ''I just caught my sleazeball boyfriend cheating. If I do anything, I'm gonna be out of a job and out of a place to live.'' she growled, ''How's your life, fuckhead?'' Admittedly, it was a little more than he'd expected. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Continued from page 23) Maybe all isn't well in The Highwaymen's locker room, but if that's the case, we won't be privy to any turmoil until it boils over. From a journalist's perspective, it's a little disappointing to see this normally outspoken firecracker suddenly discover the purpose of tact, but it shows a growth in Amy Campbell that might have been a long time coming. ''I don't put on for the cameras, but I'll admit that I try to stay a consistantly decent person. That can get really tiring, learning how to hold your tongue -- it's never been my strong suit.'' That doesn't have to be said twice. Amy makes no bones about expressing herself when we ask about various aspects of FUSE, namely the untimely demise of The Affliction's first incarnations. ''Someone's taken and run with it now,'' she says, ''But it's bullshit. It's all bullshit. If we're being honest, you don't get any bigger, badder or nastier than Clinton Sage and Lane Stevens were. They were pussies, sure - but both of those men were completely off-kilter. They'd do anything to mess up someone's life. You saw what Lane did to me, and Sage tattooed my friend's thighs with his name just to piss off Rhine.'' The redhead gets a nervous chill while she's speaking, ''I mean, what the fuck? There's mean, there's nasty and then there's flat-out past the point, and that's what those two were. The other Affliction guys? Meh. Bad as any of them might have been, Sage and Stevens weren't hard to take out -- and it wouldn't take much to squash the likes of El Diablo under my size 8 sneakers.'' Though her actions in the ring are always certain, Amy's loyalties outside of it aren't nearly as clear. After that off-handed comment about Jonathan Rhine, there were many questions to come. She tensed up with every new question, apparently feeling that she'd given too much of her real feelings away. If she hadn't been putting on a nice act for the cameras up until now, she sure could have fooled this publication. When asked how strong she thought The Highwaymen were, she answered with the swiftness of someone who'd trained their response weeks ago. The Highwaymen are apparently fine, despite the turmoil between Jon Rhine, Katie Malick, Aimz and an un-named member of FUSE staff. We're not buying it. It was decided that the subject should shift to the happiest and, thus far, most stable part of her life - her marriage. ''I know you want me to be all mushy, but I'll introduce you instead,'' she beamed while swerving the car toward our destination, ''You really have to meet him to know why I think he's so fantastic. He balances by insanity perfectly.'' In early 2000, Amy Campbell met Darcy Markson at an event for PSW, a small-time federation that both were working in. While she was still young, Aimz was already working her way up the corporate ladder after being taken in by, and soon dating PSW's owner, Eddie Osborne. It goes without saying that her presence and relationships were already garnering Amy negative attention from her peers. Matters worsened when she tried hiding a fling with Darcy early on. She went under the microscope then, and never left. During a live pay-per-view webcast, Aimz turned on her then-boyfriend and their stable mates. It was raw and unscripted, as was the kiss she shared with Markson soon after. Our writer was in the process of asking Amy about her first encounter with her husband when she showed the other side of herself, one that had been clawing its way to the surface ever since the topic of factions and betrayal got thrown into the gauntlet. ''I don't betray people.'' She wanted to be very clear about how the story was set up, but we report the facts. Our writer confronted her with the fact that she had already admitted many times to sleeping with Darcy Markson while in a relationship. But still, she insists ''Don't start rambling about something you weren't there for. That's now the way it went down, and that's not the full story.'' Campbell was asked to elaborate and explain exactly how things came to be with Darcy, so we could report her alleged side of things. Clearly unhappy with the way he was conducting the interview, the rage-ridden redhead reached her driveway and demanded that our writer exit the vehicle. After asking for the source of the sudden snap, the only response he recieved was a stiff cross to the jaw and an order to leave her property. For all her niceness, Amy Campbell obviously doesn't appreciate her life being opened for public consumption. Maybe she's having flashbacks to her rivalry with Lane Stevens, maybe she's unhappy with her own opinions regarding her current stablemates, or perhaps she simply doesn't appreciate the truth of how her greatest relationship came to be. Whatever the reason, it's clear that Aimz is facing problems the likes of which she hasn't seen in a long time. We can't wait to find out what they are. (McLean's Magazine later recieved an apology on behalf of Mrs. Campbell through her manager, Dan Mulholland. A second document was sent and intended to assure the public that Amy Campbell has no inclination to betray her peers, and that our reporting was flawed. Without further elaboration, we stand by the dignity of our work. We would like to thank Leana Steen for finishing this article in the absence of the original writer.) ------------------------------------------------------ In the year 2008... Darcy Markson put the magazine down, glancing toward the bedroom door and thanking God that she wasn't awake yet. He knew the kind of rage he'd have to help Amy work through the moment she read it. Since he was alone, he allowed himself a moment to look back over the last paragraphs and think of the real facts - the real actions that lead them to where they were and the facts that were glazed over for the sake of a concise story. ------------------------------------------------------- Campbell/Crisis second encounter - One night in Oakland... ''What the fuck was that? Are you crazy?'' ''Am I crazy? Yes, we established that.'' Answered a much more frantic voice, ''Can we just get out of here?'' He laughed at her. ''Oh, you think you're coming with me? Do you know the size of the target you just put on BOTH our backs?'' ''You're the one who took it further.'' ''I gave you a peck so the fans would go home with a cute picture. Just because I took your easy ass back to my room a couple weeks ago doesn't make that any more meaningful than every other stilted, scripted part of this shit.'' he said, ''I know that Eddie didn't plan what you just did for ratings, but get the hell away from me if you think you're gonna find a soft place to land.'' ''Go fuck yourself, Darcy.'' Her life was breaking down around her, because she'd just broken out of her thickest chain -- an abusive relationship whose financial safety had held her near a toxic situation for too long. The feeling of relief that she'd hoped for wasn't there, because the man she'd left was her boss, and he had enough seedy, underpaid cronies working for him to have her snuffed out in the blink of an eye. To make matters worse, right after she broke an ancient wrestling code not to do something without the consent of the fedhead, the company's champion kissed her. Of course, now he was saying it was just for ratings. All of that coupled together with an incredibly uncertain future led her across the street, sitting against a brick wall and sobbing into her Bleeding Through hoodie. She was a mess, plain and simple -- she didn't even have the strength to walk inside the liquor store she was leaning against, no guts or motivation to drown her sorrows in alcohol. Worse yet, the car pulling out from the parking complex she'd just left was pulling up beside her. It was occupied by Darcy Crisis, likely rolling down his window to remind her how stupid she is by repeating what he'd already said. The truth was that he'd seen her leaving that garage as he got into his own car, and something about watching the complete emotional meltdown that ensued had Darcy considering the idea that Amy Campbell might actually be a human being. He rolled down the passenger's side window and spoke hesitantly to the woman he loathed. Somehow, in that moment, he'd been looking at her differently. It lead to him asking ''Amy, are you the person I think you are?'' And to her responding, ''I wish it was that simple.'' Darcy sighed and banged the back of his head against his seat, clearly struggling within himself. Almost every one of his sensibilities told him not to open the door, but one look at her proposed the idea that Aimz might not be what he'd taken her for. If she was the greedy, attention-seeking whore he'd been seeing, she wouldn't have just given up the comfortable lifestyle their boss would have provided. He saw a girl who no longer wanted to be a puppet, who didn't want to continue sacrificing her self-respect for the sake of financial and career stability. At least, he hoped that's what he saw. More than anything, he was looking at someone who was scared shitless -- and she was right, he had already invited himself into the mess. On the off chance that he'd spent a year being wrong about her, Darcy took a leap. He unlocked the passenger side door. ''I live in the bay area, it's only about an hour out from here. Get in,'' he ordered, ''But no talking, and you'd better cough up the cash for a hotel when we get near.'' When Amy tried ignoring him, Darcy put the car in park and continued, ''I don't like you any more than you like me right now, but do me a favor and get the hell in the car so I don't have to have some 'Promiscuous Canadian Wrestler Found Dead' headline on my conscience.'' She shook her head, ''You watched me toss away my life, and you probably have no idea why I did it... but you injected yourself into the situation. I didn't invite you to. So don't sit there saying it was for ra--'' ''I'm not playing the power struggle, Aimz. You needed a ride a few minutes ago, don't suddenly take to acting all strong - we both know what Eddie's gonna do the second he finds you. Now, get in,'' he ordered, ''I'm not gonna ask, beg or say it again. If you don't wanna be left to the wolves, get your ass in the car.'' She could get in the car and risk being alone with a man who probably hated her, or she could take her chances by staying on a rainy street hoping to avoid a man she KNEW hated her in that moment. Amy took her chance, and it was with Darcy. (TBC Bitches!)
