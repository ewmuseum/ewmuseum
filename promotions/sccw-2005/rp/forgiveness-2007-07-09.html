Forgiveness

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9, 2007 Ebony Cadee Colby March 14, 1963-March 13, 1997 Beloved Wife, Mother, and Sister May You Truly Find Peace "To forgive is to set a prisoner free and discover that the prisoner was you." Forgiveness…I don’t know what brought me to this place, on this day, but I was drawn to it. It was as though there was something inside of me that was pulling me here. Leaves and dirt covered the headstone, so as I sat down I wiped them away. My fingers trace slowly over the words. If only I could see her one more time. If only I could touch her one more time. Forgiveness… I remember that night as if it were yesterday. Laying in bed, staring at the ceiling I was unable to fall asleep. Brendan lay in the bed next to me, sound asleep. I tried to mimic his breathing, but it was no use. Quietly I slipped out of bed and opened the door. It creaked. Turning my head, I made sure that Brendan was still sleeping before I crept out in to the hall, quietly closing the door behind me. Their room was just down the hall. The girls’ room was at the other end, near the stairs. As I walked past their room, I peeked my head in to find them still asleep. I made my way down the stairs to get a glass of water. The kitchen was in disarray. Dinner dishes still lay in the sink. There was a chair knocked over on its side near the table. A knife lay on the counter, glistening from the light in the room. Not thinking much about it, I rubbed my eyes slowly and stifled a yawn as I pick the chair up. Grabbing my water, I headed back upstairs. Making my way down the hall I could hear raised voices coming from their room. They were arguing again. A yell, followed by a slap. I let out a sigh. Going back into our room, I scooped up Brendan, careful not to wake him. A crash, another yell. I made my way down the hall to the girls’ room. Inside, they were already huddled up on Heather’s bed. The three of them as close to one another as possible. They jump as I open the door, unsure of who will be on the other side. I press a finger to my lips to silence them and slip inside, closing the door behind me. I lay Brendan down on the other bed and kiss each one of them on the forehead, before locking the door and taking my post beside it. No one would be entering here tonight. I can feel them begin to relax, just by my presence. Eventually the girls settle down in to a fitful sleep. I can hear their steady breathing. Brendan never woke up. Not realizing it, my breathing follows suit with theirs. My eyes become droopy. Sometime during the night I must have drifted off. I awoke to Heather’s blood curdling scream. I jumped up quickly, and ran down the stairs, taking them two at a time. She was still screaming. Following the noise, I ended up in the kitchen. There she was, slumped up against a cabinet. The knife lay near her body. She was sitting in a pool of blood. The blood on her wrists had dried, she must have been there all night. Yet despite the horrifying scene, her face looked calm, peaceful. I picked up Heather, still screaming, and ran her back upstairs to her room. The others were awake now, but I made them stay in the room. When the police arrived, they said it was a suicide. I knew better. I tried to explain it to them. They didn’t believe me. But, if it’s possible to murder someone by pushing them to suicide, he was guilty. I was just as guilty, if not more so. I had heard them that night. I knew what was going on. Yet, I did nothing. I tried to make myself feel better. I had to protect the kids. Bullshit. I was too scared. Too scared to save her. I cry to this day because of that. I should have done more. I should have stopped him. Should have begged them to stop. If only… What if…? Of course he never cried. He never showed any remorse. He just went on with his life, and expected us to do the same. He was back at work the next day. Rushed the funeral, the day after that. No autopsy. The police said there was no need. Once again, it was a suicide they told me. He started dating a month later. Every night there was a different woman in the house. A different woman sleeping in their bed. In her bed. They never stayed long, and they made us call them mom. Soon he was remarried. She was nothing like our mother. We can hear them in the bedroom. Fucking. Heather, she was never the same. To this day she remains in a mental institution. The doctors say she’s making progress. She still wakes up screaming. They still have her medicated. Afraid she may lash out. She’s strapped down to the bed after the last time she tried to kill herself. To join mother, she said. So forgiveness…forgiveness is something I can not give him. He’s taken away so much. He’s caused so much pain. Oh, he’ll pay alright. One day I’ll make sure he pays. They’ll all pay….in the end. I know my mother would have wanted me to forgive her. However, I don’t think there’s anything that I need to forgive her for. She’s done nothing but found peace for herself. For that, I am grateful. I know that she is safe, and that Brendan has found her. He’ll be safe with her. "I love you." I lay the flowers on her grave and slowly walk back towards the car. Forgiveness…it seems so simple, yet it’s the hardest thing a person can do. I can not forgive him, but is there anyone that can forgive me? I can only hope…I can back around for a last look her way. It’s almost as though I can see her standing there, watching me walk away. Her face is pained and her arm stretches towards me. "I’m so sorry. I love you. Forgive me…" Tears are running down my face, but I don’t care. I couldn’t stop them if I tried. There’s an emptiness inside of me because of that bastard, an emptiness that will never be filled. I will bring justice for her, for them all. Painfully I tear myself away from her image. As I walk down the street my mind fills with a million thoughts. Mother…father…poor Brendan…Heather. Heather. I need to go see her. I can feel it inside me, pulling me. The same feeling that lead me here….Forgiveness… "Without forgiveness life is governed by... an endless cycle of resentment and retaliation."~Roberto Assagioli "He who cannot forgive breaks the bridge over which he himself must pass."~George Herbert
