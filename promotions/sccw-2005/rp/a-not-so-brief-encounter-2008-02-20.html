It took him everything he had, but he had prevailed, as he usually did. He had seemingly done the impossible, and from a strict wrestling sense, competed in and won one of the best pure wrestling matches of the year. Jason Cruise had legitimized the Livewire title, but Elliott Rollins, Elliott Fucking Rollins had taken it to new heights. He had retained his title. He was two more victories away from getting a shot at El Diablo and the Gateway Title. A beautiful girl had kissed him out of the blue. His stock had never been higher. Everyone knew his name. He had a manager working for him night and day to keep his name on the tip of everyone's tongue. He shared a bed with a beautiful girl. So why wasn't he satisfied? Why was he never satisfied? If he were asked to, he wouldn't even be able to describe the feelings he was experience right now. He would use words like trepidation and unease, stumbling over his own vocabulary like they were rocks in the road. Thankfully, nobody asked, so he didn't have to think about it. He just had to feel, which he was much better at doing. “What are you doing?” a voice whispered to him in the darkness. Even though it was soft and muffled by a pillow, it startled him. He sat upright in the chair, his eyes scanning the bed. He could hardly see her in the darkness, but he could at least make out her form, so it gave him something to talk to. “Sitting,” he replied. “Well I know that,” she chuckled. Her lips stuck together as she spoke, making most of the words unintelligible. “But why are you sitting?” He saw her unmistakable shape shift in the bed as she propped herself up against the headboard. “I couldn't sleep,” he muttered. If he could, this conversation wouldn't be happening. “Care to talk about it?” she asked. He didn't.
