Jordan

18 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/18/07 These last few weeks have been hellacious for Jacque. First there was the birth of his daughter Bella and then a few days later there was the birth of her brother Jordan. Jordan was not Jacque’s child however, Jacque was going to be the one to take him into his home and care for him as though he was his own. Jordan was the child of a man whom was supposed to be his friend, a man who slept with Dianna, the woman he loved. Keegan O’Conner. Jordan was born 2 ½ months early and was in rough shape. Jacque and Dianna both knew that it was going to be a long road to Jordan’s recovery but come hell or high water Jacque’s plans would not be destroyed. Bella was already safe and sound in her new home and Jacque planned on taking Jordan home to be with his sister. Dianna knew that if anyone or anything stood in his way there would be hell to pay. June 2nd, 2007 Grady Memorial Hospital Atlanta, Georgia Jacque stepped off the elevator and headed toward the doors of the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU). Each part of the NICU was separated by the degree and intensity of the child’s condition. Jordan seemed to fly through the units and even though he would still be considered early he was two weeks away from going home. The doctors as well as Jacque and Dianna were surprised by how well Jordan was progressing. Jacque rang the buzzer for the doors to be opened and he slipped in. He slowly walked toward the area where Jordan was last night. When he reached his destination shock and horror twisted his face. Medical professionals surrounded Jordan’s isolette. Jacque rushed toward the huddle of doctor’s and nurses only to be pushed back. They were trying to stuff a tube down his throat. "What the hell are you doing to him?" Jacque screamed. "Get away from him." A tall blonde doctor grabbed Jacque by the arm and ushered him into the hall. Jacque fought to try and get by him but the doctor was stronger than he looked. "Mr. Noreault you need to calm down. None of this is helping Jordan." The doctor told him in a cool manner. "What the hell is going on? When we were here last night he was fine." Jacque said as calmly as he could. "Jordan started having trouble breathing early this morning Mr. Noreault. We tried everything possible before resorting to the ventilator." The doctor explained but Jacque was still confused. "Just tell me that he will be fine." Jacque said this more to himself than the doctor. "Sometimes things like this happen with babies born as prematurely as Jordan. When they are born this young there lungs have a hard time keeping up with the body’s oxygen demand. This is common thing, and Jordan is fine right now." The blonde doctor informed him. "As soon as they are all done in there you can go see him." Jacque slowly walked out of the NICU and walked into the waiting room that sat to the left of the double doors. The room was cozy with its plush tan carpet and inviting colors splashed on the walls. There was a small television that played an old episode of some sitcom Jacque didn’t recognize. Jacque knew he couldn’t just sit there and wait so he started roaming the halls of the hospital. When he finally realized where he was he stood in front of the hospitals chapel. Jacque slowly opened the one of the two heavy wood doors and peered in. Jacque had not been anywhere near a church or anything of the sort since the day his father died. He sighed heavily and entered. The chapel was dimly lit and only had two pews. There was a large cross that hung on a white and gold trimmed wall. Jacque kneeled before the cross and began to pray. "Lord I know that this child is not mine but I wish he were. Even though the circumstances of his birth as well as Bella’s was considered a blasphemy in Your eyes. This child deserves a shot at life, a chance to be a better man than both his father and I are. I am not here to make any promises to you if you help Jordan because I am not sure if I can keep them. Please God let this child be fine." After Jacque said his piece he stood before the cross for a moment and then headed back to the NICU. When he arrived he was immediately taken to Jordan. The nurse informed him that they had called Dianna. Jacque just nodded as he stared down into the small helpless face that looked back at him. Tears slowly rolled down his cheeks but they were tears of guilt instead of sadness. The only thing he could think was, "Thank God Bella is healthy." Jacque slumped down in the chair that was beside his isolette and put his head in his hands. He had a horrible feeling that his dream from weeks ago would come true. He didn’t wish any ill will toward this child. Hell he wanted to bring Jordan home and make him his own. Jacque knew that they would have to find Keegan in the near future and tell him about Jordan. He knew that with out Keegan’s consent he couldn’t adopt Jordan. Jacque jerked his head up out of his hands in thought, "When I see Dianna I will have to ask her about that." He muttered. The day Dianna gave birth to Bella she had come to tell Jacque something about Keegan. There had been so many traumas in his life these past few weeks that he had forgotten to ask her about it. She had looked a little stressed that day and all Jacque did was make it worse. Jacque heard the footsteps behind him and turned to see who it was. Dianna was crossing the room behind him with swollen red eyes. Jacque wouldn’t ask her anything now, not until Jordan was safe. She walked up beside the isolette and began to bawl. Jacque knew it was part hormones and the rest was stress. As much as he didn’t like the things she had done to him in the past he didn’t want to see her go through this either. Jacque placed a comforting hand on the small of her back as she wept silently. "I talked to the doctors before I came in." Dianna said through her sobs. "They said he should be fine, just a minor set back." Jacque prayed to God that was the case. He would feel guilty if anything happened to Jordan. It would be his fault and he knew it. "Where is Bella?" Jacque asked. "She is with my mother at the house." Dianna told him with a monotone voice. "Mom wont be going home until Jordan is in the clear." Jacque sighed and decided to head home. He needed to hold his baby in his arms to make the world feel right again. June 2nd, 2007 Jacque’s Home Atlanta, GA As soon as Jacque got to the front door he heard the faint cries from Bella. It wasn’t her typical "I’m hungry" cry. Jacque bolted through the front door and headed straight toward the sound of Bella’s cry. When he entered the room Jacqueline was holding her but she just wasn’t satisfied with that. "Gimme her NOW!" Jacque demanded. Jacqueline looked at him horrified and immediately handed the baby over. As soon as Bella was tucked in the crook of Jacque’s arms both their natures changed. Jacque got a complacent look across his face and Bella looked up at her father with her wide blue eyes. "How… how is Jordan?" Jacqueline asked him softly. "The doctor’s said he would be fine. It was just a minor set back. You can leave now to go see him if you want. I am sure your daughter would like it if you were there." Jacque purred. Jacqueline crooked an eyebrow at him and backed out of the room without making a single sound. Everyone had noticed a change in Jacque the day Bella was born. He was extremely protective of her and yet when he held her he could feel his insides turn to mush. This small precious little thing turned his hardened exterior to that of a big teddy bear. Jacque walked to the rocking chair and sat down with Bella. She loved to be rocked and held close to Jacque’s chest. "You’re my little girl Bella. I will never let anything happen to you." He crooned. "You will be the only woman in my life from now on. The only person you will ever have to share my heart with is Jordan." Bella’s eyes slowly closed, as did Jacque’s. There was something between the two of them that no one would ever understand. God pity anyone that tried to separate them.
