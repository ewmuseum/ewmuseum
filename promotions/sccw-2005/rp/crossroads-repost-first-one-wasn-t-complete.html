Date: Today Imagine if you will a crossroads. Imagine you have been walking down a path that you had been on for so long that it felt like home, you weren’t sure where you were headed but it didn’t really matter, just because the road was comfortable it was safe, along the way there were a few side paths which led to the wilderness surrounding the road, but it would be foolish to take one of those, so you ignore them. Common sense takes over and you just carry on the road. The only reason you are following the road is that you want to know where it goes to, after following it for so long curiosity gets the better of you. You’ve travelled too far to turn back now after alls. Then it comes. The crossroads. Suddenly you are thrown into turmoil, there is choice that you have to make but both roads seem the same, there is no difference between them and the horizon still gives no indication of exactly where you are headed. Sure there might be the smallest of chances that further down the road the two paths would meet up again and you would return to the original path, but the path on the way to that road would be decidedly different and there is nothing to indicate which route would be the best one for you. How are you supposed to make a choice like that? Knowing that one could lead you to heaven, whilst the other could lead you to hell. Ethan Frost was at such a crossroads and without knowing where he was going, without knowing a direction it was a fifty fifty chance of exactly where he would end up. "Fred, I don’t know where you are or why you aren’t returning my calls but get back to me as soon as you can. I miss you man." Frost looked despondently for a moment before begrudgingly pressing the end call key, throwing the mobile in the general direction of the sofa, causing it to bounce softly before stopping silently, he stared at it for a few seconds, hoping that it would vibrate, hoping that something would make Fred call, but he knew it wasn’t going to happen. Since finding out that Fred was AWOL at Killzone, Frost had left a total of seventy four voice mails and one hundred and ninety-six text messages, none of which he had received any response to. Although he was worried, Frost couldn’t help but think that sooner or later Fred would show up and everything would be ok again, but he felt so helpless that he could do nothing. That he couldn’t fight in his friend’s corner. FUSE just didn’t feel the same without him, or rather that should be FUSION Wrestling. It would fall to Frost to keep the dream alive, but the problem was that he now had other things on his mind to focus on, mostly being War In Washington. For whatever reason his successful return against Paul Cain, had sent a message to the booking committee, that despite being slightly rusty, he was still more than capable of getting the job done, so he had been given a shot at the tower match, the winner receiving a shot at Clinton Sage, or at least it would be Clinton Sage if he managed to retain, the general murmurings backstage was that Sage was going to be champ for a long time and that there was nothing that anyone could do about it. The Affliction were just too strong. This was only really the part truth. The Affliction were a strong cohesive unit, that was true but individually they were beatable. Ethan Frost had proved that by beating Lane Stevens and even Clinton Sage, when they weren’t together they were beatable, you needed to divide and conquer it was difficult but it was possible. The only way it would happen though would be some sort of good guys rebellion. It had tried to happen before, in the early days of The Affliction, before they bolstered their ranks with El Diablo, the potential was there to tear apart The Affliction. John Covel. Jacob McKail Fred Fusion Ethan Frost. Four men who would have been able to stand toe to toe with The Affliction if not best them, but the fates were not kind and the unit just couldn’t gel. All four had their own private agendas and despite the fact they were fighting a common goal, they could not pull themselves together. So The Affliction grew stronger week by week whilst the good guys, flustered and faltered. John Covel hit a slump in form that he just couldn’t break out of. Jacob McKaill, left the company the door had remained open for him but he had not been seen since. Fred Fusion had gone AWOL Ethan Frost….well he’d been arrested. Frost walked through his flat pacing up and down, he did not look well at all, his hair had become greasy and his skin was pale like a man who had been up all night playing World Of Warcraft and just couldn’t stop, the only thing was that he hadn’t been playing World Of Warcraft, he was suffering from withdrawal. As had been previously noted when Frost was arrested he had been found with a substantial amount of heroin in his system, he still maintained that he did not know how it got there, not in the first instance at least, but as with all drugs Heroin is addictive. It pulls you in until you are under its spell. The phone had still not rang as Frost ventured into his kitchen and reached into one of his cupboards rooting around the back until he found what he was looking for, a look of contentment flooded over Frost’s face as he pulled out a small leather pouch and made his way back into the living room, allowing himself to fall backwards onto the couch, laying the pouch down on the table. Staring at it intently. The crossroads had begun. Sat silently, in the moment only his accelerated heartbeat and the ticking of a clock in the corner of the room dared to break the silence as he sat in quiet contemplation, staring intently at the pouch, it was with a sigh of regret that he finally leaned forwards and opened it up, revealing a sparkling silver syringe and a small capsule of liquid held in place by a piece of black elastic. Panic and fear took over Frost’s face as he saw his situation there was hardly anything left, barely a single hit. His mind wondered back to his last purchase he had bought a lot, well at least he thought he had done, he had spent an awful lot on the gear. For all intensive purposes Frost was a rookie when it came to all of this, he had only managed to find a supply by the off chance a guy named Wayne, he had seemed genuine enough, but that wasn’t the point. The point was that Wayne had something that Frost wanted. And what he had simply wasn’t enough. Picking up the phone he flicked through the phonebook before finding the appropriate name and dialled, even before the second ringtone Frost’s fingers were tapping the armrest frustrated, eventually a gruff sounding man answered the phone with a low grumble before Frost jumped straight into the conversation, there was no time for pleasantries he needed some more and he needed it now. "Wayne, it’s me. What have you got?" Frost’s voice was breaking as he spoke, nervousness and anxiety being the main crux in his tone. "Nothing sorry bud." Wayne, for that was his name simply did not seem to care, he sounded as though he had been sleeping or at least was halfway to his own form of paradise. Frost on the other hand was not prepared to take that as an appropriate answer for his situation. "What do you mean nothing I’m down to my last hit!" "You’re last hit! I sold you enough for a fortnight and it’s been less than a week how much have you been doing! Anyway, I’m not too sure about this, I mean you aren’t your everyday bum in the street paying in crumpled up twenties they’ve been saving up for the whole month. People know you, you’re high risk if you get found out it will be traced back to me!" Wayne was clearly concerned, he was not a stupid man you didn’t get away with selling class a drugs without having a degree of common sense, sometimes it was about cutting your losses. "That’s not my problem is it, I’ve got the money you should have the goods that’s how this works!. "No how it works is you’re an addict and you need me! I tell you what to do, not the other way round "I’m not an addict." There was more than a hint of protest in Frost’s voice but he had heard people say that before and had not believed them himself, why should he ever believe those words, even when they are flowing from his own mouth. It was once said a true addict would lie, cheat, steal even sell their own grandmother to get one more hit, Frost was almost there, he wouldn’t admit it to himself, but the part of him that had remained true, the part that had remained clocked in to reality could see the signs for what they were. "Then why are you calling me at four in the morning?" The words rang true to Frost who immediately cut off the line and stared at the small leather pouch that lay open on the table in front of him, he had not even realised the time, it seemed to fade in and out now, it simply divided into two categories the times when he was high and the times he wasn’t, there was no am or pm, just pain and bliss. So now the dilemma stood before him, he could try and wean himself off it, or he could have that one last blast of heaven, that one last blast of joy and suffer once again the effects of withdrawal the craving for the good stuff, the fix to make everything else just fade away. He needed to escape, the pain was building up once again and he could not cope with it anymore, the truth being that he was weak he was about to use his one last escape ticket a one way trip to the abyss where he could sit and be happy, where the world would dance around him, when he was high he didn’t hurt people. Frost hurt people, he had done all of his life, he would pull people close to him and let them in, he would trick them into thinking that there was something special about him, something truthful, loving and understanding. He would gain their trust and then he would exploit it, but people didn’t see that, he kept it hidden, those he had hurt didn’t stay around for long. They ran away. Yet the crowd loved him, they saw him feed off of his friendship with Fred Fusion take away some of his cheers, watch them get passed on as the fall guy. It was only a matter of time before he hurt Fred Fusion as well, that was if he ever came back. For all of his madness, his tweaks and his downright craziness. Fred Fusion was the only one that could save Frost now. Fred wasn’t here. So it was with a degree of self loathing that Frost picked up the syringe, prepped his arm and entered a state of oblivion for a little while longer at least. Crossroads are funny enough at the best of times, even when you know where you are headed, you can know what the right path is and it would make sense to follow it, but sometimes, just sometimes curiosity gets in the way.
