Never let your opponent dictate your reactions

29 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 29, 2008 It is odd how things go down some times. Zakk Wylde joined Gideon in FUSE and they immediately made an impact by possibly ending Gunnar Saint’s career permanently. When you have 2 of the most driven and ruthless men to ever set foot in New Era Wrestling combine forces bad things are guaranteed to happen. The question isn’t "if" it is simply "when". The debut of the Black List in tag team action was mired in controversy. PRP was jumped and that left Cyrus Raynes against both Zakk Wylde and Gideon Fontaine. You would think it would be a cakewalk for 2 men to beat the outnumbered Cyrus. Sure excuses could be made. Zakk had food poisoning, Gideon was stabbed in the leg by Sarah Riley, and Ryan Davis was bought off. There is no evidence that Davis was bought off but it is impossible to completely miss a stabbing taking place in the middle of the ring or the blood from Gideon’s leg and the hole in his pants. A big man always has a shot regardless of his lack of talent. This was not the Black List’s night so they chalked it up to a bad night and regrouped. Of course you knew there had to be payback. There is a lawsuit against Sarah Riley and Ryan Davis may be fired. Nothing is certain on the status of Ryan Davis. Obviously Gideon’s head of security the monster Mr. W. beat him down and he is hurting. There is also the attempt by the Black List to get him fired by placing a Plumpers shirt on him. Then again it was televised so it isn’t like Smitty can’t tell he is being played. Does Smitty care though? FUSE is being sued by Gideon Fontaine because of what Ryan Davis allowed to happen and lets face it there is no way in hell that Smitty likes a man that can cost himself and SCCW millions. Changing the name of FUSE to SCCW may insulate the federation but Smitty T. Duluth and Sarah Riley are named personally. There are other factors as well. The Dead Man’s Hand approached Gideon last week. It appears they want Gideon to drop the lawsuit and go after the Prides. "Gibson: What does the Dead Man Hand care about Gideon's lawsuit?" As you can imagine Gideon already thought this through several times and all sorts of possible reasons. He doesn’t like the one he came to but he is thinking of the angles and counters to the counter angles. There was of course a talk between Kathryn Shaw and Gideon Fontaine after the show. When you cut through the sexual innuendoes from both sides it is an interesting conversation. The contents of that call he is not sharing with anyone outside of his closest friends preferring to keep things secret. "Katsidy: It's funny, really. You and I, we aren't that different, though you're clearly a step ahead of me. In other circumstances, I would probably be here with an offer rather than a demand. Anyone who's willing to kick off Tony Pride's head should come see us, because I'd like to see that happen, just for old time's sake." How true she is. Unlike her method of more direct manipulations Gideon prefers a more subtle approach of indirect manipulations and mind games. Never show your hand and never let them know what you are thinking. Let them draw their conclusions. Preferably after guiding them to jump to the conclusions you want them to come to. Kathryn Shaw wants Gideon to do something and there is no guesswork involved in what she wants. Gideon on the other hand is pursuing his own agenda and he isn’t letting anyone know what it is. ########## ########## ########## ########## The Gymnasium of the Fontaine Mansion, 5:04 AM ########## ########## ########## ########## It is quiet in the Gym as Gideon is circling the heavy bag. He is moving fluidly despite the cast on his leg. It is amazing to watch a man with a cast on his right leg move around on it kicking the heavy bag without the slightest loss of balance. It doesn’t matter if the kick is with his right or left leg. Gideon is moving around the bag delivering shins and knees. On the last shin kick the bag spins and you can see the face of Sarah Riley taped on the heavy bag. Gideon: Time is ticking away sweetheart. With a smirk Gideon starts driving knees into the picture on the heavy bag smiling at the impact of bone on the leather and sand and imagining that is her body getting busted up by some very vicious knees. Gideon: I am a patient man. THWACK!!! THWACK!!! The plaster cast on that right leg is driven remorselessly into the face of Sarah Riley and the impact echoes through the Gym as he is driving those shins time after time destroying the picture until the picture rips from the tape and falls to the floor. Gideon: The Dead Man’s Hand seems to have plans for you Miss Riley. Then there are the Highwaymen but they are too self absorbed to do more then feud with themselves. The pawn is placed out there begging to be snapped up by the vultures. What they fail to realize is the pawn is not important. If you let it dictate your strategy you have already lost the game because you have made that fatal mistake. You have let your opponent dictate your reactions. ########## ########## ########## ########## Antonio’s Pizzaria, Feb 28, 2008 ########## ########## ########## ########## It is pretty quiet. Gideon is waiting on his food to be prepared and an appointment he has. Gideon looks at his watch but it wasn’t necessary as Derrick Stone walks into the Pizzaria 10 minutes early. He grabs a seat by Gideon taking care not to hit Gideon’s right leg. Derrick Stone writes for Wrestling Insider and he is the only writer that Gideon will talk to. The cane is on the chair next to Gideon. Gideon: Glad you could make it Derrick. Derrick: Wasn’t missing this. Can I start off with a personal question? Gideon: Off the record? Derrick: Up to you. The big guy Mr. W. seems awfully familiar…… There is a laugh from Gideon. Gideon: Next question. Derrick: How is your leg healing? Gideon: Decently. I have got it checked out earlier today. There is a large scar but it is healing nicely. After saying that Gideon rolls up his pant leg and you can see a 11 inch scar on his right calf. There is a wince from Derrick as he sees the scar. Derrick: Jesus! Gideon: As you can tell I haven’t exactly let out much information. As you know in this business things are occasionally more complicated then it seems. You can appreciate the history here when I give you an example of how even something simple can be catastrophic. It was a routine slap to the side of the head. It has been done several billion times for hundreds of years. Yet that night in a Ring of Honor arena Homicide slapped Steve Corino in the side of the head and he is now deaf in one ear. Derrick: What happened to your leg? Gideon: Are you familiar with the muscles in the lower leg? I believe the Peroneus Longus was stabbed. Since it is a small muscle it tore and was nearly severed. Then there was the infection of the wound which complicated things. It had to be reattached and sewn together. As you can imagine my right leg being injured annoyed me greatly hence the very vicious lawsuit I’ve filed against the bitch, FUSE, and Smitty. Derrick: Is it working? Gideon: It is amazing the type of pressure you get with a multi million dollar lawsuit. Millions of people witnessed what happened. There isn’t even any question of if she did it. Then there is the medical evidence in that very public maiming. My life as a martial artist and wrestler could be over now. Do you know what this lawsuit will do to her? The smile on Gideon’s face is disturbing and somewhat psychotic. Gideon: Every dollar she gets she is going to owe to me. Her credit will be destroyed. She couldn’t own a house or a car. She wouldn’t be able to sign a lease because she would have absolutely no credit. Let’s say she finds someone to pay for her bills. Every pretty thing she owns would belong to me. I can collect for the rest of her life and smile as I destroy anything she cares about. When you stab a man like me in the leg you can’t have pretty things anymore. You can see a look of worry on Derrick’s face as he sees this side of Gideon he never saw before. He always knew Gideon had a temper. The thought of Gideon going to these depths is a shock. Derrick: That is cold. Gideon: Off the record? Derrick: Sure. Gideon: I was approached by Kathryn Shaw. She and more importantly her bosses the Dead Man’s Hand wanted me to drop the lawsuit. You follow FUSE? Derrick: Of course. Isn’t it SCCW though? Gideon: Yes but I like FUSE better. If I have to use the SCCW name I will but I’ll still call it FUSE. Anyway Sarah Riley was banging Rhine ruining his relationship with some woman. Truthfully I never even bothered to learn her name because lets face it everyone knew it wasn’t going anywhere. Mongo is being managed by her because she can lead him around by the dick and she needs a bodyguard. Hell Amy went PMS upside Rhine’s head and even I saw that coming. I did the math in my head and didn’t like it. The Dead Man’s Hand definitely are not looking out for Smitty and considering their cancerous effect in federations they aren’t likely to be looking out for FUSE either. Derrick: So what is the plan now? I did the math in my head too and I’m getting a migraine. Antonio brings over the food and starts setting it up on the table. There are pineapple garlic fingers, a triple cheddar mushroom pizza, and Chicken Caesar salad. There are plates, knives, and forks and a coffee being brought over for Derrick and water for Gideon. Gideon: So as you can tell this is a pain in the ass. Luckily I can use this to my advantage. I am not going into details but I have a plan in mind and after talking to Miss Shaw and Smitty. As you can guess both want me to help them and aren’t offering me anything Derrick: So what are your plans for the upcoming match against the Prides? Gideon: As you can tell from my leg that I will not be able to wrestle. That leaves Zakk and Caleb…… Derrick: He isn’t cleared to wrestle is he?! Even Smitty isn’t going to let him wrestle in SCCW. That would be insane! Gideon: It is amazing what you can do with a multi million dollar lawsuit as leverage. Regardless of Caleb being banned by every athletic board in the country he can wrestle in FUSE. That is also likely why the new titles were created by SCCW. Give Gideon and the Black List some pretty gold to fight for and try and get me out of their hair. I give the Highwaymen maybe 3 weeks before officially imploding. The Undertow perhaps 2 months. That leaves the Prides and the DMH. The DMH wants them gone and I think they deserve a bit of payback for what they did to Dave. Between The Black List and the Dead Man’s Hand the Prides are out gunned here. Derrick: I have to ask. Is Mr. W. able to wrestle? As soon as Derrick asks the question he gets a smile from Gideon. Gideon: Definitely. If he ever gets in the ring it is going to be very interesting. Guys over 7 ft tall that can wrestle is kind of rare. I wouldn’t bet on him being very happy about it. If I asked him he would do it. They eat quietly for several minutes. As Derrick grabs another of the pineapple garlic fingers he looks at Gideon. You can tell he isn’t looking forward to asking it. Derrick: What about your upcoming match against Aimz? Gideon: I have things planned. I am not going to be a disposable bit player in Amy Campbell’s reality show. I have had enough of being thrown to the wolves. Despite my talents as a wrestler or talents on the mike the SCCW thinks they can throw Miss Campbell a bone. Gideon isn’t 100% so she can beat him and then go on to other things. Considering she jumped Rhine she wants the big title now. All I am is another notch on her belt to build her up. The look of anger on Gideon’s face can’t be hidden. Gideon: I’ve earned my spot. When someone tries and take that away I get very angry. I am not going to mention what I have planned because I’d prefer it to be a…… surprise. The look of anger in Gideon’s eyes is still there but the smile that is appearing on Gideon’s face is something that Derrick has never seen before in all the years he knew Gideon. Even during Neil and Stephanie’s wedding he has never seen that look before…… Nearly 8 years ago…… ########## ########## ########## ########## The Wedding of Neil and Stephanie April 4, 2002 ########## ########## ########## ########## It was one of those days when all hell was breaking loose. Why in the world Gideon had Bobby and Tony minding the gates is beyond him. Though the truth of the matter that he found out was Gideon was running 2 security teams and Tony and Bobby were decoys. He did the real policing himself because he didn’t trust anyone else to cover the security. Gideon: Well give me the news on how bad you guys screwed up so far. Tony: Bobby let Sean in. Bobby: A$$hole Tony: Bobby. Hey he was gonna find out sooner or later. Gideon: The Devil enjoys weddings. Who let in Executioner? Tony: Bobby. ::Gideon shakes his head as if he is having a migraine.:: Bobby: (glaring at Tony) I really hate you right now. Gideon: Well at least I know they will be together. I think I know who crashed the wedding now but I can't find him yet. Tony: Really? Who was it? Gideon: The worst possible person to arrive Beast himself. When Gideon said those words he wasn’t happy. This was the biggest day in the life of both Neil and Stephanie his two best friends. Sure there were assholes there but they were contained and everything was going as expected. Except for this "minor" problem. Derrick saw the look in his eyes of anger and frustration. It is so different from that look today……… ########## ########## ########## ########## Antonio’s Pizzaria, Feb 28, 2008 ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon: Derrick? Derrick: What? Gideon: You drifted off there a second. Derrick: Sorry you kind of threw me for a loop there. Gideon: Sorry Derrick. As you can tell I’m a bit angry. It is one thing to be over looked. It is another to be thrown around as a midcard ex tag wrestler to be squashed. What do you say we call the interview here and finish lunch? Derrick: That might be for the best. They continue eating and talking about other things. Pretty much wrestling related and pretty much catch up on the latest gossip. There are rumors but guys like Gideon and Derrick prefer to get more reliable facts. Derrick was definitely curious on if Gideon would have actually dropped Kathryn Shaw if she touched him after getting the card from him. The smile says it all. He would have dropped her without blinking twice. ########## ########## ########## ########## A nearby boxing gym 15 minutes away from Fontaine Enterprises ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon is there and he is by a heavy bag noticeably limping. The punches and elbows are fast and violent. As he is circling the bag the people there are watching in amazement. The speed of Gideon is mind boggling for a man his size. He is faster then men that are only 150 pounds. When Gideon goes for a shin kick that right leg buckles and he drops to a knee. Gideon: Son of a… This doesn’t stop the strikes and elbows. Gideon has fought hurt before. He has had his ribs cracked at age 8 and that was when he learned to block out the pain. Being hurt happens. There was a ladder match in NEW and Gideon finished the match with a concussion. That is something people don’t understand. No one would have faulted Gideon for not being able to continue. Neil had to climb the ladder and take down the New Era Wrestling tag titles but even barely being conscious Gideon would not let Neil down. Gideon: This… is… not… stopping… me… With a snarl Gideon gets to his feet and starts shin kicking the heavy bag with rights and left kicks and the leg buckles again. Gideon gets to his feet again. You can see a red stain on the floor where Gideon’s right leg is planted and Gideon is holding his calf. You can see his hands stained red as he is getting to his feet with the cane. People go over to Gideon. When a man offers him a towel he wipes his hands and looks at the blood on the towel and the floor. Gideon: Send my office the bill. I’m sorry for the mess…… One of the guys is offering to help Gideon to his feet but he doesn’t want the help. Gideon is using the cane and leaving the gym and people are murmuring. He exits the boxing gym and goes back to Fontaine Enterprises looking angry and in pain. ########## ########## ########## ########## Gideon’s office in the Fontaine Enterprises building ########## ########## ########## ########## There is a long time where Gideon just looks at the phone. With a sigh of resignment he picks it up and starts dialing. There is a delay and suddenly Gideon Gideon: Smitty there may be some problems. I was in the gym today……… There is a long conversation between Gideon and Smitty. The details of the conversation aren’t known to anyone outside of the conversation. It is a long day and Gideon is getting back to work looking over some documents. This is the business side of Gideon that isn’t as well known. Plus he is taking this time to set things in motion. There is so many things that Gideon needs to do and he has very little time to do the things he has planned. ########## ########## ########## ########## There is what is called the "Internet effect" by some. No matter what happens if you are famous the Internet will find out and report it. After that initial report others will find out and then things continue to snowball. "Gideon Fontaine’s leg starts bleeding badly during a workout." "Injury to Peroneus Longus muscle may cause Fontaine to retire." "Gideon Fontaine unable to wrestle at Temptation and Zakk Wylde must team with Caleb Alan." "Doctor’s report shows that Gideon’s leg is still at 15%." "15 inch scar on Gideon Fontaine’s leg reopened." Of course Gideon was busy the next day with the tidal wave of people wanting to know about his leg and if he would retire from wrestling. Of course there were some idiots claiming it was steroid related. Gideon dealt with this very quickly. As you can imagine there are problems in trying to deal with Internet rumors and stories being told about him. It is impossible to stop the Internet from reporting what it wants. It is known as "The Barbra Streisand Effect" when you try and stop it. The Barbra Streisand Effect is (as the name suggests) named after the hapless discoverer of this phenomena. Barbra attempted to sue a company which had published a free photographic survey of the California coastline that just happened to include images of Streisand’s palatial holiday home. Streisand’s case was thrown out, and ultimately she achieved the opposite of what she had wanted; rather than protect her privacy by forcing the surveyors to take-down the photo, it brought the image to the whole world’s attention. Including people like me who wouldn’t normally care what part of the world Mrs Striesand like to call home. Of course, if Barbra’s lawyers had been aware of recent history they might have shown more caution. The most odd thing is all during that day in the privacy of his office Gideon is smirking. One has to wonder why Gideon is smiling during this shit storm. He has been busy trying to stop all the rumors and stories and even filed lawsuits against a few sites. He is fully aware of the Barbra Streisand Effect and knows he can’t stop things like this by threatening people. He follows a simple and very important rule. "Never let your opponent dictate your reactions."
