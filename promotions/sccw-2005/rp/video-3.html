Date: Today [The video starts as these videos often do. With Edward seated behind his desk, a burning cigarette clamped between his lips, and smoke wreathing his head.] "While the point of these videos is for people to be able to see and judge me for themselves, today’s video will be slightly different and with a somewhat different path." [Edward takes a long and deep draw from the cigarette and stubs the burning butt out in the ashtray on the oak desk before him.] "This video alone of those that I am doing will probably be held apart from the rest of them. Unlike the rest of these videos, this one is not for public consumption once I am dead and gone. This one is to be held onto until my son, Drake turns fifteen, or until Kia decides that he should see it." [Edward stands from behind the desk and with a few short strides he makes his way from one side to the other. There he stops and stands straight. His long black hair is pulled back in a stark ponytail, and his large frame is covered in a simple button up polo shirt and a pair of faded and worn jeans. His feet however are bare he isn’t even wearing socks. In fact, most of the time he’s home he doesn’t wear shoes or socks, he just doesn’t like to.] "Hello Drake, by now I’m pretty sure you’ve heard things about me, seen videos of me, and talked with your mother time and time again about me. You probably think I’m some crusty bastard who cared more about himself than his wife and son, and while it pains me to say it, you would be right." [He pauses.] "Well, you would partly be right. I love your mother more than anyone else in the world. I love her more I love my brother, your uncle Dash. That love for her, and for you, is why I’m making this video. I figure that since I’m not exactly there to make certain that you can find your way in the world, that you may have a few questions, or want some answers." "Also, I figured that you may want to know a bit about your dad and who he was." [A faint grin passes over his face for a moment.] "So Drake, go ahead and ask your question." [He pauses and stares intently at the camera for a moment. Seconds tick by until eventually he stirs again.] "Right, ok. Good question. Sadly, I won’t be able to answer it since I have no clue what you asked. So, since I can’t give you exactly the answer to the question you asked, I’ll give you an answer to a question you will probably ask." [Edward walks around to the other side of the desk and seats himself in the leather chair behind it.] "The answer is, because I couldn’t do anything else, and remain myself. Look Drake, people have probably told you all your life about me. They have probably told you that I was a violent sociopath. They have probably told you I was a foul mouthed fucking drunk." [He grins again.] "While both of those answers are correct, they encapsulate a fairly small portion of me. Sure I was a violent bastard, sure I was profane, and yes I was have battled with alcoholism, all of these things are not the entire me. I had hopes and dreams, I even tried to be a decent person, and I lived by a code. Sure, in some ways it’s a screwy code, but it’s a code none the less." [Edward reaches to the pack of cigarettes on the desk, and after a moment he flips one out and has it lit.] "There are so many things that I would and should tell you Drake. I had plans to tell you to grow up into a perfect person, I had thought to tell you how your life should function, how to get the most out of the world." [For the first time in a long time, a VERY long time if you are Drake and are watching this at age fifteen, Edward’s face is cracked with an honest smile. Not the vicious smirk, nor the feral grin that he normally shows, but an actual and pleasant smile.] "Then I kind of realized that I didn’t listen to anything anyone told me at age fifteen, so why the hell would you be any different. So, here’s some basics for you, just pointers that you will probably need at some point, try to remember them." [He takes a long deep draw from the cigarette and holds it in front of him.] "Don’t fucking smoke, I’m the picture perfect example that it kills you. Don’t fucking smoke." [He exhales the smoke slowly.] "Right, so you smoked. It’s ok, it’s not the end of the world. Your mother hates it, it’s not socially acceptable anymore so get around to quitting alright?" [Another draw from the cigarette, and another exhale.] "Kia, if you are watching this with Drake, step out of the room, he’ll call you when you can come back in." [He pauses for a moment and stares at the cigarette in his hand before speaking again.] "I’m going to assume she left Drake. So, here’s the deal, and it’s vital you understand. Your mother is a great person, a wonderful mother, and you should show nothing but respect to her. She came from a fucked up family, she married a fucked up person, and we led a fucked up life for fucking years. I’m not a saint, I never tried to be, but your mother fucking deserves that you should be one." [Now he grins.] "At least, you should be one around her. She deserves some good in her life Drake. She deserves a good life, and while I tried to give it to her, it wasn’t exactly something I could swing with my life the way it is. So, around your mother, give her nothing but respect and support. Do whatever the hell you are asked, and do it with a smile even if it eats you up inside. Then, when her back is turned, do whatever the hell it is you feel you need to do, just watch your ass while you do it. "Don’t become a fucking druggie, that would be the ultimate failure of life. Sure, you can smoke pot occasionally with your friends, you probably already have, but don’t become a fucking druggie. Drugs killed one of my best friends, and by the time you hit 18 it will probably have done the same to you. Stay off the hard shit." [He takes a final draw from the cigarette and adds it to the pile in the ashtray.] "There are three types of people in the world Drake, those that lead, those that follow, and those who pick their own path. I picked my own path and while it was a good one, it’s not one I would wish on you. Find your own way in the world Drake. Don’t let others force you into a mold or into doing something that you don’t want to do with every fiber of your being. Your life is there for you to lead, not for others to live vicariously through. Don’t fuck it up like I did." "I don’t know what else to tell you Drake. I didn’t really have a dad to tell me what to do, or to provide a good example for me, and I hope that my life doesn’t lead you into a similar life. I never wanted to be a rolemodel, and you should take that as truth. I lied, cheated, and fucked others over as much as I had to so that I could get where I wanted to be. It’s who and what I am Drake, I walked on the misery and pain of others so that I could be who I wanted." [He stares intently into the camera.] "It’s not the best life Drake, and it’s not one that I would chose again knowing where it leads. Make something of your life son. Become a brain surgeon, cure cancer, do something for the public good, find your own path to immortality, because mine sucked." [He leans his chair back and stretches his arms over his head before returning his chair to normal.] "Drake, I really tried for you and your mother. Maybe the fact that I failed in some ways proves that I didn’t try hard enough, I don’t know. I always did what I thought was what needed to be done, and that’s part of the code I lived by. It’s a good code, and a good set of principles that can help guide you in your life Drake." "Five rules Drake. Take care of yourself. If someone hurts you, hurt them worse. Return loyalty with loyalty. Don’t be a fuck up. The last one is the most important of them." [He leans forward and over steepled fingers stares at the camera.] "Don’t Fucking Smoke."
