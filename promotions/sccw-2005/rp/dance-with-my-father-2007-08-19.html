Dance with my Father

19 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/19/07 "The worst thing in life you can become is the thing in which you hate the most"-Anon [That blue light fills our screens again, one of these days he’ll be fancy enough to film a promo in his studio apartment that he rented, but today is not that day for our second generation savior. Today is the day that he moves forward with his un-detailed plans to destroy the wrestling business as we know it. When we last left him he was on the phone with an unnamed man, and now, well now he’s in his apartment watching television…] "LIVE FROM THE VERIZON CENTER IN WASHINGTON DC" [The "pay pew view" announcement is on his television, letting you know that you can purchase this awesome event…] "WAR IN WASHINGTON" […And you can’t be there live but you can watch it…] "ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW!" […And you can do it for…] "$39.99" [Exactly] "WAR IN WASHINGTON FEATURING ALL THE CHAMPIONSHIPS IN FUSE ON THE LINE, INCLUDING THE UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP IN WHICH CLINTON SAGE WILL DEFEND AGAINST TSUYOSHI TANAKA!" [Clips of Sage vs. Tanaka show, it’s pretty interesting even if it is "baddie vs. baddie"] "…AND FEATURING THE FIRST EVER TOWER MATCH!" [Clips of all the competitors flash across the screen, ending with the ominous "Tower"] "….WITH THE WINNER RECEIVING A SHOT AT THE UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP!" [A shot of the large gold belt, the most prestigious in FUSE] [Echoing] "UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP!" "UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP!" "UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP!" [He awakens] "Prince Pride, Universal Champion, I like the sound of that!" [He jumps out of his chair, basketball shorts, wild lion mane like hair flying all over the place, those piercing eyes filled fire and a hope for the future] "I mean, I’d be the Universal Champion, recognized as the best wrestler in the world, the people they’d cheer for me…" [Frantic he runs to his lone window and opens it, as much as it’ll go, and shouts down to the streets below] "You’d cheer for me, you’d chant my name, you’d like me, you’d really really like me…" "SHUT THE %$@! UP!" [That was someone yelling back at him from the Washington, DC streets, those people in South East aren’t the nicest when it’s past midnight] "I could travel the world, wearing the best clothes, dating the finest women, limos, leer jets, VIP in the clubs, hell who knows…" [His eyes grow large] "Maybe even settle down, marry a woman, have kids of my own, Prince’s little Prince, rub elbows with movie stars and other atheletes" [Pride smirks] "Prince Pride, your host of the ESPY’S!" [Pride stands in front of his mirror and flexes, he smiles as he visualizes the Universal Title around his waist] "All I’ve got to do, is get through thirteen other wrestlers, one of them whose a mystery person, and get out of a cell, and climb the scaffolding and not get thrown off the top…" [Pride stares at his reflection in the mirror] "Doesn’t sound like something that I can’t do…" [Pride nervously chuckles into the mirror] "…And I’d be a champion, the first of many, and I can do it, it’s in my blood, it’s who I am, after all I’m the Savior…" [Pride stops chuckling and stares into his own face] "…I’m Prince Pride and I’m…" [A look of horror overcomes the young mans face, as he stares not only at himself, but at the man he vowed to never be…] [Angrily and yet quizzically] "…Not my FATHER!" [Pregnant Pause] "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" [Pride lunges his head backwards and then with an angry torque headbutts his head into the glass, shattering it…] […Or maybe not] [Panting can be heard, Prince Pride lays in his chair sweating, eyes wide open now as he stares into the television set, it’s an infomercial. He stares around the room nervously, the window is still closed, the mirror is still intact, matter of fact he’s still in his chair] [Nervous] "That was just a dream" [Catching his breathe] "A nightmare!" [Pride stands up and begins to pace the room nervously] "This, this wasn’t the plan, I don’t want to be the World Champion, titles are what ruined my childhood" [Mocking tone] "Son, I just won the J-Crown, son, United States Tag Team Championship, son, I just won the NWC World Tag Team Titles, underneath a mask, I’m the "Foundation", I’m Tony Pride, World Champion…" [Pride pulls at his hair] "SCREW YOUR TITLES, DAD! SCREW YOUR $%!@ TITLES!" [Pride begins to pace more and more, tearing at his hair like it’s on fire] "…And screw you FUSE and professional wrestling." [Pride stops, mid-pace] "I’ve got to continue the plan, I’ve got to keep it moving…[Sinister sneer] live and only on pay per view, no boundaries, no censors…" [He nervously chuckles] "….People are going to pay to see the beginning of the end, of FUSE and wrestling as they know it. Now my personal nightmare, what the business has done to me, I’ll do to them. [Eyes grow large] There’s going to be a War in Washington alright, one that Prince Pride will start…" [Nervous chuckle] "…And Prince Pride will finish!" [Pride slides on some flip flops and grabs his keys and wallet, time to take a late night stroll, Pride manically walks towards and out of the door as we slowly fade, but not before the Infomercial goes to commercial…] "LIVE FROM THE VERIZON CENTER IN WASHINGTON DC" [The "pay pew view" announcement is on his television, letting you know that you can purchase this awesome event…] "WAR IN WASHINGTON" […And you can’t be there live but you can watch it…] "ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW!" [..End!] "Right now I’m having Amnesia and Dejavu at the same time" –Stephen Wright
