Date: 02/22/2009 02/17/2009 Middlebury, Vermont An hour after the show… The locker room is a cold, dead, empty place after shows. Sitting on the bench, the only thing keeping me company is my shadow. No one around, everyone leaves early like they have something important to do when they don’t because we don’t have much money because of the economy. However this time I have a reason to smile. I beat Buck Fledger tonight. Nobody thought I would be able to do it. NOBODY. The fact that I made all of the pundits and wrestling nerds on the wrestling sites look like idiots. Just making them look stupid is something that makes me happy. After the lethal combination of Aaron Nothings, Meat, and no shows killed my chances at winning the tournament, alas I find out that I have a chance at redemption. After the show, another one the officials here brought me the card for the next and last show of this tour. There’s a little thing on this show called the MVP Battle Royal. What a perfect, sweet revenge. Nobody on this tour has had more ups and downs than me. What a glorious event it will be for me to win that battle royal. I’m going to show Meat that he made the wrong choice in partners and show that bastard Aaron Nothings that he shouldn’t have turned on me. The blood of the innocent and the guilty will be spilled all over the ring and I shall have my revenge. But that’s not what matters. Nothing matters anymore. It’s all for naught if I don’t win the battle royal. However, the fact that I am booked to wrestle earlier in the evening isn’t going to help matters either. But it’s made even worse by the fact that I am facing both the Illustrious Face-Eater and Kelly Masters. Pulling out the paper with the card listing for the next show on it out of my right pocket, I open it up and read it over again. "Great…" I mutter as I read it over once more. "I’m in two matches of death." Folding the piece of paper back up and putting it back into my right pocket, I let out a big sigh. "I hope I don’t get jobbed." Knowing my luck I’ll be the first one out in the battle royal. Being the last one here only makes me more paranoid with all the creaks and groans backstage. It’s kind of creepy like a horror movie but you’re in it and know that you’re going to die sometime by the end credits. That isn’t a good feeling, not at all. Grabbing the last of my things, I stuff them into my duffel bag before zipping it up. Standing up from the bench, I put the bag on my shoulder and head toward the door. Opening the door and looking around, I then close the door behind me and head down the hall. The lights dim behind me and then fade off. I guess they finished putting the ring up. Leaving out the exit, I have no idea what the future has in store for me. However I do know that I’m either headed to the top or headed toward epic fail. * * * 11/11/2012 The night is dark outside. However it is as silent as Hiroshima after the atomic bomb was dropped on it. Not a creature stirs anywhere. Salt Lake City is a virtual cemetery because of the curfew put on the city. Judge’s actions over the past few days have made it even worse than before and now the Imperial Police are everywhere. It’s really scary to live here. This must be what it was like living in occupied Europe under the Nazis. I remember back when I was a wrestler working for independent promotions, back before everything went to pot. I remember the fun times drinking with the guys and sleeping with various women on the road. Those were good times for me. Things made more sense back then, Now nothing makes sense anymore. The government controls everything and tells us lies all the time. The first time I remember being disenfranchised by this government was when they banned professional wrestling. They said it was a "barbarian" sport that helped ruin the morals of our nation. The government before that said the same thing about football, hockey, mixed martial arts, boxing, extreme sports, and rugby, among other things. You could drink back then and some alcohol is what I need right now. Can’t do shit. Just sit around all night because of the curfew. There’s literally nothing to do now except watch government propaganda and state sponsored news on the television. Hell, you can’t even fuck anymore without procreate without a permit from the government. There’s no joy in anyone’s life. We’ve all become zombies because of this. No one has any personality. We’re all the same, slaves to the system which we live in. Laying here on the couch, I come to the conclusion that this is the nadir. This is the worst it’s ever going to be. There is nothing worse than this. The world is on the brink of calamity. Nations in upheaval, people starving and dying, economic and social collapse. It’s all happening now. Almost every nation on this planet is at war. I can sense that we’re on the brink of Armageddon. It’s just a matter of where and when. The where is going to probably be somewhere in Europe or Asia. The when is the only thing left to figure out. My guess is World War III starts anytime between now and Christmas. That’s forty days until all hell breaks loose and that may be generous considering the instability of some of the heads of state. Some of them are heads of nuclear nations that are currently at war. When the news pundits are talking about the other nations, it scares me. Our nation now has fully assimilated Mexico but now we’re taking it too far. United Empire of America troops are now invading the Communist state of Guatemala. At this rate, it’s going to be like that old movie ‘Red Dawn’ except for we are the aggressors. Such is the state of things in this world. As I stare blurry eyed at the television once again, I see that President Hillary Clinton is giving another address to the nation. This one is about the invasion of Guatemala, the successful assimilation of Mexico into our empire, and the repeated ‘terrorist’ attacks in Salt Lake perpetrated by the mysterious masked vigilante known as Judge. http://wnd.com/images2/hillary1984.jpg "My fellow Americans," she says as she is being broadcast to all of the flat screen televisions on buildings all over the country and in televisions in everyone’s homes. "After conquering the peasant Marxist guerrilla army of the nation of Mexico, we the United Empire of America have begun an invasion of the Communist Republic of Guatemala. To the people of Guatemala, fear us not. We only need more living space. We will gladly take you all in as our own after we have your lands under our control." More living space? Reminds me of Hitler or something. I continue listening as the President finishes addressing the nation. "You will be free of the persecution and slavery that you constantly live under. Communism in Guatemala will be a thing of he past. Now you will be a Democratic state within our empire. Resist and we will destroy your. Your troops aggression into Mexico during our campaign there brought this on. Your government is to blame for everything that happens from this point on." "As many of you know, our campaign in Mexico was a success. The states of Mexico are now a part of our great nation. However our nation may be strong but it is not perfect." Yeah, that’s about the truth. That’s the first thing she’s said in years that I actually agree with. "There is one imperfect thing in this nation and it’s the fact that the terrorist vigilante known as Judge is still on the loose. His terrorist attacks in the great and peaceful city of Salt Lake have left us all in shock and dismay. Last night the explosion caused by Judge killed five children playing in an apartment hallway. The night before that he killed five police officers. He is a mass murderer and will be brought to justice." "My fellow Americans, and especially those of you living in Salt Lake, the state of our nation is strong. We will not waver to terrorists like Judge. We will deal with them swiftly and effectively. This is your President saying God bless our troops and God bless America." The speech ends and the pundits in the studio begin their usual pro-government analysis as I roll my eyes. Same shit, different day. Nothing will ever change. Laying down on the couch, I start getting drowsy and start to go to sleep. Eyes closing, everything fading to black. I am asleep.
