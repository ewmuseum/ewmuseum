Shelley

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/07 Introduction My mind's eye is strained to a light, projecting from nowhere, on images from days past. I see each image, a retrospective of my own life, yet I am powerless to stop it. I feel chained -- constricted -- by an unseen force, weighing down my very being, preventing any kind of action. The lights flicker. The picture is rolling faster. Kat. My parents. Isa. All pictures, dulled in quality of course. I can't see my captors, nor can I feel my bounds. The images begin to speed. Emotions shoot from the screen, encompassing me like a blanket. I feel each and every image seeping into my head, short circuiting my rationality. I begin to struggle, kick, and desperately try to break free of my bounds. One final, gruesome image appears on the screen. Me. Tsuysoshi Tanaka. Lying in a pool of my own blood. Mouth open, eyes back in my head. And as I study such an image, it dawns on me. The slight hint of a smile is on my lifeless face. My legs begin to kick, trying to escape this nightmare. However, unbeknownst to me, the nightmare has only begun. I swim in the currents of half-consciousness, betwixt reality and my dreams... As I rush towards the waking world, my horror only begins to escalate. I As my eyes slowly flutter open, I can feel only a cold sensation on my temple. I feel as if ice is pressed into my skin. My eyes begin to focus, and kneeling in front of me is the cumbersome form of Holland Shelley. The motel room we purchased the night before, in which we are staying, seems to fall away, as if only Shelley and I are trapped in a moment, suspended in space. Beads of sweat flutter down his face, and his facial hair, untamed, reflects speckles of white. The cold, almost comforting metallic feeling of a pistol against my head jars me. I can only look up, and, as I study Shelley, I see that his heart is pounding immensely. I motion for him to put the gun down. 'No,' he gasps. 'No, no, NO!' 'Shelley,' I say softly, almost pleading. 'This isn't funny...p-put the goddamn gun down.' But he doesn't listen. He digs barrel of the pistol deeper into my temple. The silvery metal of the gun gleams in the cheap florescent lights of the motel – Zeke’s Olde Tyme Motel (the only thing old tyme about it is the décor) -- he and I purchased on Rt. 9W (with Mike Mitchell’s credit card, of course). Sun pours in through badly tattered curtains, and the rug's scent resembles a strange mix of garbage, urine, and the impending stench of death. What a fitting tomb. A metallic coil from the bed digs into my back, and I wince in pain. 'So,' Shelley slurs (obvious he's had a fix today), 'You think you..ya can jus' make me yer' slave, eh? Like my family in the old days! Well, I will not be yer' boy anymore!' Shelley puts his finger to the trigger, and I jerk back, still crushed underneath Shelley's immense weight as he kneels on top of me. My heart explodes out of my chest, and I come to the realization, that very line of reasoning that could teeter a rational man on the line between life and death. I see his sanity fleeting away, like sand blown in the wind, disappearing in between molecules of air into complete oblivion. Another realization comes to me. I'm going to die. I'm really about to fucking die. However, Shelley does not squeeze the trigger. All of my dreams come flashing before my eyes. I can see John Covel, facing Clinton Sage, while I lie in a wooden box beneath the ground, knowing full goddamn well that I should be the one in that ring. I can see Killzone, resuming as usual (or perhaps a celebration of my death?), with my match being replaced with an Alliance speech that could put the worst insomniac into an eternal slumber. My heart rate resumes it's normal pace. He's not going to do it. 'Put it down, Shelley,' I reason, now. 'You can't do this. We have a mission.' 'You,' Shelley says, trembling, deep beads of sweat dripping down his dark skin. 'You did! You used me, man! You fucking used me! And now look! We...we killed children...I...I just wanted to be left alone. I wasn't hurtin' no one! But you had to drag me into this...this SICK trap of yours!' 'Shelley,' I reply, feeling the words come out of my mouth without even thinking. 'There comes a time in life when destiny calls. Destiny has a funny tendency. Sometimes, destiny can be obvious, like when a young man sees a woman about to fall, and helps her. However, in other times, destiny can be covert...hidden. Take Christopher Columbus, for example. His destiny was to discover a new world, and he did not know it. Shelley, you have a part in these events. You need to prepare for it, because when it happens, it will be unlike anything you have ever experienced.' 'No!' Shelley screams, slamming the gun down against the night stand. 'Our destin-in-in--' 'Destinies.' 'Yes! We ain't together in this, Japanese man! I just want to go home, get a fix! You think you' some kind of hero? You kill lil' kids, and--' 'Look,' I say, almost too coldly for my own good. 'Sometimes people have to die. It can't be helped. And, if you try and turn me in, I will nicely remind them who my accomplice was...' 'You, you, you, YOU!' Shelley stammers. He then grabs my head, slamming it several times against the headboard of the bed. I can hear his screaming; painful, unintelligible syllables, as my head hits again and again. Pain swims throughout my head, and I can feel the world getting darker and darker. I struggle to fight against the currents as I sink deeper into unconsciousness, but again, sleep overtakes me. II 'Look how pathetic you are.' My eyes flutter open, and a great wave of pain hits me, nearly knocking me back. Shelley is now by my bedside. I try to get up, only to find that my hands and feet have been bound. Shelley smiles, twirling the gun in his fingers. His great girth blocks out the sickening sun, pouring through the window. I see my sheets and pillow completely caked in blood, and feel a warm tickle down the back of my head. Shelley paces back and forth, apparently baiting me and waiting for a response. I only give the man a look. I still know that I need him in this. Shelley is blinded, his senses dulled and his own common sense on the backburner. 'Look at ya',' he says again. 'The all mighty Tsuyoshi Tanaka, now all banged up by the big man!' Shelley laughs, gesturing to himself, and continues to pace. I can see in his face that the laugh is a mask, and, in truth, he is frightened. He continues to twirl the gun. Then, out of nowhere, he aims the gun at me and shoots. My entire body jerks as the bullet whizzes above my head. I roll my eyes up, only to see a bullet hole in the wall above me. Shelley shoots again, and it is to the same result. I smile in ease now. Shelley is apparently so fucked up that his motor skills have completely hit the skids. This smile, as I now learn, is a vast mistake. 'Somethin' funny?' Shelley stammers. 'Huh? You think you're FUCKING funny? You think I'm a clown?' Shelley charges the bed, stumbling every step of the way. For some reason, my eyes go not to Shelley himself, but to the lines of white powder on the table behind him, and a razor blade next to them. Shelley approaches me now, his fists balled up, and knocks me across the eye. A screech enters my head that is deafening, and my eye begins to swell. Suddenly, I realize that this screech is coming from me. The more I scream, the more Shelley hits, and the bigger his smile gets. Suddenly, he falls to the floor, almost laughing, his entire body trembling. His behavior, while puzzling, symbolizes a characteristic found in most junkies -- desperation. I begin to wiggle while Shelley is on the ground. Unsurprisingly, the ropes are loose. Shelley's sense of coordination was always one of the many things he lacked. I easily wiggle out of them, and as Shelley gets to his feet, I attempt to bring him down. Instead, once again, he backhands me across the lip. Immediately, my lip begins to bleed. Shelley then grabs a hold of me. He takes me over to the bathroom, which reeks of rotting feces and the smell of smoke. The room is about the size of a closet, and contains only a toilet, a small sink, and a bathtub encrusted with an entire mildew colony. He throws me violently against the bathroom wall, my head cracking against the tile. Shelley then begins to fill the bathtub, placing the stopper over the drain. My senses become dulled again, particularly my sense of sight. Straight lines become curvy, and still objects begin to waver and move. The feeling of drunkenness characteristic with these types of injuries increases, and I find myself powerless to stop it. My form crumples, and I fall out of consciousness, my head brushing against the toilet... III 'Up! Now!' I slowly rise to my feet, guided by powerful arms. My one eye opens, the other swollen shut by the powerful blow Shelley dealt. Within this madness, and the brink of insanity, aided of course by blow, I feel an overwhelming admiration for Shelley. He has proven himself an effective and ruthless opponent, and as he takes my head down towards the surface of the water, I almost want to shake his hand and mutter, 'Well played' to him. But I don't. He forces my head against the bathwater, situated in a rotting crevice of mildew and dried blood. My head breaks the surface, and the water actually sooths the wounds on my face face. I feel Shelley forcing my head deeper and deeper into the water. I begin to kick my legs, the only appendages that still have strength. I can feel the world falling away from me. I continue to kick aimlessly, and I can feel Shelley try to control the legs. Suddenly, I feel myself kick something. Shelley reels back, letting go of my head. Success! My head rises from the water, water dripping down my face. My hair hangs down like two straight curtains. I see Shelley trying to get up, but I tackle him, knocking him against the floor. Quickly, I walk past him, enter the room, and grab the pistol from the night stand. He wooden grip feels good in my hands, almost as if I am holding a flare of hope. Shelley stumbles from the bathroom, then throws up his hands at the sight of the gun pointed at his chest. 'Y-Yoshi...' 'Not so tough now, are you?' I myself stammer, my mouth quickly filling with blood. 'I brought you into this, Shelley, because I thought you could help me. Also, frankly, I figured you needed the help. But now, I've come to realize that I should have left you, wallowing in your own filth, slowly killing yourself, because you're an ungrateful son of a bitch!' I point the gun against at Shelley, and he screams. I just stand, my sight failing me fast, looking at the man as his mouth gapes wide open. 'I'm not going to kill you, Shelley,' I say. 'I admire your initiative, although I believe you are not of sound mind. Nevertheless, we have a job to do. I need you in order to finish.' 'N-no,' Shelley whimpers. 'Please, l-let me go. I-I-I-I won't tell no one, I swear!' 'Get the fucking bags into the car, Shelley. You are no longer my partner in this.' 'Thank you!' Shelley beams. 'Oh, Yoshi--' 'No, no,' I say, softly. 'You are now my slave. You are a slave to many things, Shelley, but now, I will control all that you do. You have proven to me you can't handle the ability to reason and function independently. So now, you live solely to do as I say.' 'No,' Shelley weeps. 'P-please, I don't w-want to.' But life isn't about needs and wants. Life is about action, and the individual becomes of little importance. We left the motel that day, Shelley in the front seat with me. Mitchell will have a shock when he sees the room damages charges to his credit card. I still can't understand why I let Shelley live. He did the one thing that is unforgivable; he betrayed me. In a world where friends are temporary, and actions are forever, I suppose that Shelley was within his right. But Holland Shelley now faces a fate entirely worse than death. He, under my strong supervision, is going to become just a tool in my arsenal, an assistant to my aims. He will be denied any personal freedoms because he has proven he is unable to handle them. In the end, I suppose Shelley would have been better off dead. I guess time will tell. [Fin]
