In search of an answer

26 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/05/08 Day 1: Just after Temptation "Sammy, Sammy, Sammy, Sammy, Sammy. We just have to talk." AJ and Sameer are walking in the car park outside the arena, moments after Sameer decimated AJ in the ring at Temptation, he is striding toward his car without stopping and staring straight ahead, ignoring AJ, (which is pretty hard to do when AJ has been saying the same thing over and over again for the past 10 minutes). "Sammy, talk to me man, I don’t see you for weeks, then you come back a week before a title defence and do this, what’s the score?" Again, there is more ignoring from the giant terrorist. This frustrates AJ more than anything – if there is one thing on Earth he hates more, other than America and such like, it is being ignored. Oh, Celine Dion and Brian Adams as well. In fact, those two have, on the petition of AJ, been banned from ever meeting each other and either collaborating on a track or mating. (As that would create a genetically engineered super annoying kid who would then go on to create mass suicide in the world of the likes you have never seen apparently.) "SAMMY! FOR ALLAH’S SAKE, TALK TO ME!!" Sameer stops for a second, and then looks at AJ, smiling a little wickedly, before opening the door to his car and getting in, driving away with a screech of tyres, leaving AJ behind, looking at his erstwhile partner driving away with one half of his belt. Day 2: The day after just after temptation. AJ is in a phone booth on the corner of an apartment building in downtown Chicago, he has spent the last 4 hours after settling in his hotel scouting out this particular building and now he is going to try to get in touch with Sameer again, not that he is getting obsessed about this or anything. He picks up the phone. "RING RING, RING RING, RING RING. You have reached the voicemail of Sameer Al Jezuri. AJ, don’t think I haven’t noticed you outside my apartment building all day, it’s pretty hard to miss you when you are sitting incognito in a limousine with the words Andre Jensen, richest guy on the planet in big white letters on the side of it. I don’t want to talk to you just yet" "DAMNIT" He tries again with the same response, even checking the number to see if he got it right – oblivious to the fact that Sameer actually put a message to AJ on the answering machine message, quite obviously he might still have the wrong number. This time he leaves a message. "Sammy, this is AJ. I need to talk to you – I mean we are partners after all, I gotta know why you did what you did. Call me." Day 3: A couple of days later than day 2 AJ is sitting in his car, watching the door of the same apartment he has been scoping out for a few days. He waits, and waits, then waits some more, before suddenly – there he is, a giant of a man terrorist, coming out of the building, walking round the side to the street. Slowly, AJ brings his car out and begins to stalk the man. Into the traffic he goes, slowly watching Sameer walking through the crowd, which is becoming denser by the second, the further into the city centre he gets. AJ almost loses him in the crowd for a second, but he clocks him finally getting into a blue sedan. Perfect, he is easier to follow in a car. AJ follows the sedan for a couple of miles, managing to keep a reasonable distance away from the car, going up freeways, through sidestreets and to the other end of the city, tracking back on themselves a number of occasions. Perfectly normal, AJ thinks. Sameer never was one for directions. Suddenly the sedan stops at some traffic lights, AJ pulls up behind, perfectly natural like, and not a bit suspicious that a jeep has been in his rear view mirror for the past 2 hours. Within 20 seconds all hell breaks loose. The sedan screeches off on a red light, almost like a drag racer, prompting AJ to do likewise. The chase is on. "Jesus, Sammy really doesn’t want to talk to me" AJ thinks to himself as he chases the sedan up a slip road onto a freeway at around 100mph. they fly around the traffic as if it isn’t there and tyres screech as they career around bends and in and out of cars. A steely determination is in AJ’s face as he follows the car with the focus of a demon. It isn’t long before this little car chase is picked up by the police. The fact that AJ and a blue sedan flew past a traffic cop at around 120mph might have tipped them off. Backup is called, helicopters are inbound and much drama ensues in the police force as they have a police chase to perform, rather than the usual gun based crimes. Naturally, they assume that this car chase is in fact gun related, rather than an obsessive AJ wanting to just talk to Sameer so they have the whole force following them, with a police helicopter en route. AJ looks into his rear view mirror, and sees all the flashing lights behind him, he shifts in his seat agitatedly. "Ah fuck, the police. Some bloody criminal has been naughty again; I hope they don’t get in the way." Says AJ as he puts the accelerator down even more. Again he concentrates on the sedan, not disturbed by the police cars now streaming up behind him. Within 10 minutes, there is a call on his cell. "Hello" shouts AJ down the phone. "Hello, is that Mr. Jensen?" comes the reply "Speaking" says AJ rather tersely. "I am calling from rent-a-window, returning your call from yesterday, apparently you need a window fixing or replacing. While you are on the phone, I am delighted to inform you that you have been selected for our great windows giveaway! Did you know that 1 in 100 homes in America have absolutely no windows whatsoever?" says the salesman on the other end, in typically annoying salesman type pitch. "I AM A LITTLE BUSY RIGHT NOW – PLEASE CALL LATER" shouts the more hostile AJ, quickly hanging up, concentrating on the road ahead. The phone rings again not a minute later, AJ answers it. "Yes?" "What do you want?" is the reply on the other end. "I made a mistake, it can’t be fixed." Says AJ; rather calmly for 140mph. "Anything can be fixed son. We have all made a mistake at one time or another, but it isn’t worth this!" The man on the phone to AJ is the police negotiator. He is calling from safety 4 car lengths back in the lead squad car. "Nah, this can’t. it’s too broken. Cracked, shattered and splintered." The negotiator in his car turns to the driver. "We have a suicidal driver, call the boys and tell them to give him room. I’ll try to talk him out of it." He then talks on the phone to AJ again. "Why don’t we talk about it, face to face? Just calm down, and we can go to the station, sort some things out. You aint in trouble" "The station? Why would I need to? Look I appreciate your time, but I honestly don’t think it’s worth it. look I am a little busy at the moment, I’ll talk to you when I can." With that, AJ hangs up again, shaking his head at pesky salesmen and the fact that he can still see police lights in the background, this criminal must be the same place as he is going, that’s all he needs – a shootout when he gets a hold of Sameer. AJ’s phone rings again, that salesman again. He lets the machine pick it up. A few miles down the road, AJ sees a helicopter come into view, this crime must be closer now, and maybe the police will be gone soon, those sirens are giving him a headache. The sedan however is still in his sights – Sameer WILL tell him what’s going on. The phone rings again. This time it is actually Sameer’s number. AJ answers. "SAMMY! MATE, WE NEED TO TALK." "AJ, you are an idiot." "Welcome back brother. I missed you." "The reason I called you is to ask you to stop, you are embarrassing yourself." "I will stop when you stop mate, I just wanted to ask why you did it." "I’ll tell you at SoS if you just stop, please – you are drawing attention to yourself." "I only started chasing you because you sped off." "Yeah, the problem is – the guy you are chasing….well, it isn’t me. I got home about 2 hours ago, put the news on and saw you chasing some 60 year old ex rally driver up the freeway in live news. Apparently you are suicidal now?" "You what?" "I said, stop the car. Smitty already has a deal with the police to make sure you don’t get put in jail. You will do more community service again, and you won’t be punished any more. He wants you to stop before you get someone hurt." "You spoke to Smitty?" "Yes" "And he said stop?" "Yes" "Because you are at home? And the bloke I am chasing is some ex rally driver?" "Exactly" "I aint convinced." Sameer sighs, pinching his nose at the ridge, just wanting life to end. "He knew you would say that, he also said "if you don’t stop right now and hand yourself in to the police, you will be stripped of your title and suspended, he will also ensure that you can never win another title again."" Suddenly with a screech of tyres and some rather impressive control, AJ’s car had stopped to a halt. With the blue sedan also stopping a few hundred yards up the road. An older man, with the same sort of build and clothes as Sameer gets out of the sedan, rather ashen faced and scared. He hands himself into the police barricade which had been set up in record time a few yards in front of him. It turns out that he thought AJ was a plain clothes police detective who has discovered his penchant for illegal drug trafficking. AJ climbs out of his car, rather sheepishly looking at the police officer who is now out of his car staring a burning hole into AJ’s eyes. The sort of look as if to say – "You are in big big fucking trouble, but I can’t do a thing to punish you" He ruefully grins to the officer. "Sorry about that, I had to ask him something, then some sort of window salesman called me to ask if I could go to a meeting with him. I hope this won’t take long." With an incredulous look at AJ, the officer in charge just sighs, shouting for someone to cuff AJ and take him away. "God fucking damnit, why do I even bother sometimes?"
