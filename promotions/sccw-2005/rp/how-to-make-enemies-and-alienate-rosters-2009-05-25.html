How to make enemies and Alienate Rosters

25 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/5/09 (Homecoming had come and gone with Legion not having the best of nights with Rick Malloy winning the Livewire title and then making himself public enemy Number one on the roster by turning down Desade’s offer of being in the Dead Man’s Hand and turning his back on the heroes by calling them egomaniacs…) (Now in the locker room after the show we catch up with Legion and Luci.) Legion: I think that went pretty well, pure chaos has been reached with the DMH starting to show more cracks and now going back to the lone wolf mentality with a roster after me- Luci: Just like old times hmm? By the by, I caught up with Lauren earlier… Legion: You did? I noticed she was incapacitated earlier, there’s just something about her that’s different tonight… Luci: She seemed perkier tonight that’s true, guess some of the sickness is starting to show (Luci smiles), can’t wait to see her at Provo though there’s some… (she starts to blush) personal things I need to finally do. Legion: You finally going to talk to her about the training I set up then? Luci: That’s one thing, yes but I have to ask you one thing, something that’s bugged me now you turned Alex down? Legion: What’s that? Luci: Now you’ve turned down Alex’s offer, does this mean I have to choose between you and Lauren? Legion: If worst comes to worst, it’s possible but I know you, you let the emotion rule over clarity so I expect it one day… but if Lauren needed my help it’d be because of your feelings and my loyalty to my protégé in a sense. Luci: So you mean if the right time called for it you expect me to pick her over you? Why? Legion: Because there’s a chance that the protégé will teach the master a lesson one day… I expected it with Rook in all honesty back when you were Sickness. It is also what I expect Lauren to do with Desade sooner rather than later. There was a very good reason I mentioned her in the promo. Luci: You mean…? Legion: I need to know the best way to get into the head of the Spider, who better than the woman who we both admire but in very different ways? Luci: Wonderful! (She has the evil grin) (We then flash forward to an apartment in the main city of Utah, where a camera is set up in the basement…) Legion: So Rick, congratulations on beating me for the Livewire title even if it was pure fluketastic all the way. The problem is I’m not done with you yet, I’ve been informed that I’m teaming up with the newly rebirthed Xander Searle against you and Spacely, let me just say a few things: Firstly, Xander I’m sure you haven’t forgotten what I did to you, seems to me it rattled a few of the cobwebs in the brain and made you think a bit more hard and lo and behold you come back with a new attitude of ‘I got thrust into a world I didn’t want’. So now you’ve gone from supposed blue chipper to cry-baby and if I remember the last wrestler that tried to do softball when taking a break from wrestling was Sid Justice AKA Psycho Sid and next thing you know… (Legion mimes a leg snapping) Frankly Xander you may get a chance to face Rick and possibly beat him in the future but I will promise you this… you must prove to me that you’ve improved, now you can either do that in Provo or have me repeat what happened before, your choice. Secondly Spacely and Malloy, never has there been a more perfect team though how they’re suppose to communicate how to wrestle and win will be amusing and will make it easier for us to win, that is if we can stop laughing in shock! Now to the final part… I know I’m pretty much against the world but like I said at Sinners Choice I wouldn’t want it any other way so I await for Desade to try and interfere… bring on the storm… (Legion walks up to the camera and switches it off)
