Date: 05/30/2009 I have a title belt, for once. And one that I didn't have to buy it at a pawn shop. Wandering around the hallways outside my hotel room, I find a bottle of beer. Open it and chug the whole thing down. Tastes like alcohol. I look at the bottle. It is alcohol. The thing about being a champion is that there are always people coming after you. In my case, it's usually VD riddled women who like gangbangs. That is my nightmare in this world. I don't like it when women don't do the crazy stuff in the bedroom. I like my bed rocked Flintstones style complete with bashing each other in the head with rocks after orgasming. Look around to see teenagers partying in the room down the hall. 'Fucking kids...' Walking around, I see some more people coming back to their rooms after a night on the town. Seems like I need to go find something fun to do. Go walk down the street to the bar. Always a bar down the street from hotels. I love that. Makes travelling much more fun. Reach the bar and go inside. See some women. HOT women. Mingle for awhile. Get a brunette to go to my hotel room. Have sex with her. Go to sleep. Wake up and realize I'm late for the show. 'SHIT!' Go and take shower. Finish and come back out. Wake woman up and let her know I have to go. Get her number. Finish packing as she dresses. We leave and head to the show. Upon arriving at the show, I'm mobbed by a bunch of teenagers who look like Lil Wayne. We run into the buildinga as quickly as we can. Once inside, we head to my dressing room and hide out there. No reason to be mobbed by idiots when we can hide like rabbits in the winter. And breed like them too...
