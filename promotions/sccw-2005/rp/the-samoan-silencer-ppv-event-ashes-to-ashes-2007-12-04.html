'The Samoan Silencer' PPV Event - Ashes To Ashes

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/4 I stood there with the man dying before me, never knowing his actual name. His blood ran down his arms and on to the wet pavement, mixing in with the rain water and the assorted mish mash of dirt and other refuse on the city street below us. I did not wonder why he had told me what he did. I only wondered at the meaning of it all as he took a final breath before turning and running down the alleyway, away from the scene, and more importantly, away from the sirens. As he disappeared around a corner, I wondered to myself if I had made the right decision. He was badly, if not mortally wounded, and here I was by myself in the middle of one of the biggest bloodbaths this world had ever seen. It would be hard explaining this one to Dalton. "The Samoan Silencer" PPV Event - Ashes to Ashes The night started the same as many others had recently. Timo Bolamba, the new hope of Motor City, slipped silently from assorted vantage point to vantage point, casually observing his mark. A few of the other men that Dalton had sent The Samoan after recently were very shady characters, but the man he followed now seemed to be quite well to do. Maybe Dalton wanted some intelligence on his competition in the political aspects, who knows. Whatever the reason for this job, it seemed off from the first moment Timo heard about it. Flash Out "Listen, this is going to be a little bit different assignment than usual Timo." Dalton stood from behind his large oaken desk, peering over a manila envelope with three or four pages of assorted information and pictures inside. "This doesn’t look like a typical thug Dalton." Came a response. "I don’t know about this, it seems like this one is kind of odd, like there is something not quite right about it." "Not quite right?" Dalton chuckled. "Look in a mirror, I think I’m talking to just the man for the job." "Fair enough." Something in the air smelled like rain, and as he looked heavenward, The Samoan Silencer noticed that clouds had begun to form overhead. Mixed grays mottled together to form a wall of darkness that was taking the evening light away from the world. Cars began to switch on their headlights, street lamps began to glow anew with the coming of the night. Just as well for Timo, he hated having to wait every day for something to happen. All the uglies came out to play when the lights went down. Another quick movement and he was practically on top of his mark. The well to do looking man in his mid 30’s seemed to not notice his presence. All but one time when he stopped and checked a pocket watch, that is. The Samoan was on the move when it had happened and in that moment, he had thought perhaps the man maybe have caught movement in his peripheral vision. If he had, then he hadn’t made any visible change in his movements or attitude. The man continued along, his long coat swishing, and a paper tucked neatly under his arm. Timo stopped momentarily to watch as the man adjusted the coat and paused to pull out a patent cigarette lighter and inhale a plume of smoke. Flash Out "So what has this guy done Dalton?" Timo asked in curiosity. "We aren’t entirely sure yet, but there are rumors that he might be rather high up in the criminal world." Dalton Grey nodded towards the envelope again and Timo took up a page. "You should be wary of the implications of this. You will not be fighting against some two bit hood this time." "Aww Dalton, are you telling me to be careful because you are worried about some bodyguards?" "No Timo. I’m telling you because I’m worried about him." The man just stood there this time, not making any real movements other than to stop and check his watch again. The Samoan looked from behind his vantage point and wondered what had Dalton so spooked over this whole mess. This man didn’t look any different than any other aside from being dressed well. There was also, of course, the fact that he was a white man in the wrong neighborhood, but that was hardly a reason to watch a man. The other curious bit of information that Dalton gave to The Samoan was that he was not to engage this mark unless provoked. A fact finding mission…that’s what Dalton called it. All around this mission just seemed to get more and more hairy. The man checked his watch again after a couple minutes and made a face after he seemed to find something not to his liking. The Samoan checked a timepiece of his own and noticed that it was three minutes after the hour. Was somebody late to meet him? Another minute pressed by and soon a few men in local gang attire began to walk in from all different direction. Timo slunk back into a position where he would be safe as a pair of men from a local drug syndicate walked by him, unaware of The Samoan’s presence. Peering up again, he counted a pair of men from five different criminal organizations and/or gangs coming to the position of the well dressed man. He didn’t bat an eye, he simply adjusted his coat once more, allowing the tie on his long coat to come undone and for it to hang open and free. As they all milled around the man, ten men from rival gangs, all looking like they mean business, Timo peered from his vantage point, curious as to what exactly was about to happen. Flash Out "So what are you saying?" Timo mused. "You don’t think I can handle him?" "I’m telling you that in the last twenty years or so since we’ve began tracking him, he’s been barely a ghost on our radar." Dalton pushed a paper forward. "This is the only thing we know about him. He calls himself The Phoenix, he is a professional assassin, and he is either a master with makeup or he hasn’t aged a day since we started investigating him." "I see." The Samoan replied. "So what am I supposed to do about it then?" "Well, there is a reason I chose you Timo." Dalton smiled coyly. "You always think of something." The man named "Phoenix" nodded towards an alleyway and the men nodded in return. He walked casually, not bothered by the fact that there were ten well armed men surrounding him as he carefully chose his steps. Timo hopped onto a fire escape and began ascending, slowly making his way to the top of the building. He ran across the rooftop, and peered down, watching as the ten men all began to mill and murmur. The Phoenix did not waver, even as his long coat swung in the breeze. An eerie calm surrounded the event that was about to transpire, and even though he wanted to interfere, Timo was too curious to see what was going to happen first. Finally, a representative from one of the gangs spoke up to fill in the group. "You are a hard man to find Phoenix. We’ve been trying to make this happen for several months now." The man, probably belonging to the DeNucci gang smiled as he continued. "You only made it worse when you spurned the meeting Phoenix. We’re all allied against you now, five separate groups." The Phoenix, to his credit, simply smiled. "What, do you think this is a game? Do you think you can hit one of our own and then not deal with the consequences?" The gangster pulled a knife from his back pocket and flipped it open with a snikt. "Well, this has been a long time coming you creepy fuck. Everyone is sick of you coming and going as you please. Nobody even knows your name, we just trust you with our jobs. Well, the bosses are done with that." The Phoenix looks down at the blade in the man’s hand then his eyes peer around him. The men are all packed and at this point it’s just a systematic process of knowing which one to kill first. A hand moves and suddenly, before Timo has a chance to see the movement, a hand extends from the long coat and an arc of silver follows, taking a hand with it. The man screams as below him, a Ruger 9mm falls to the ground with his hand still attached. Suddenly all hell breaks loose as nine men attack at once, The Phoenix whirling forward and launching a dagger into the chest of the man who only moments earlier had been so brave. Flash Out "He’s a master of every conceivable fighting art we can imagine." Dalton walks across the room trying not to let his voice waver. "We don’t know how or why, but from what little footage we have received, he’s practiced at least seven different forms of martial arts, not counting the various weapons we’ve seen him wield." "Weapons? That strikes my attention a little Dalton." Timo stands now, interested in his quarry. "What does a man like this carry for armament?" "Primarily, a Japanese style sword that we believe is quite old. Mob informants have told us he calls it "Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi"." "What!? That’s the name of…" "I told you this wouldn’t be easy." Dalton pulled out a picture of the bronze age style dual bladed sword and Timo was left speechless. Two men were down now, and The Phoenix turned, now baring the Kusanagi in plain view. Timo, being a historical weapon buff, instantly felt a chill run up his spine. He gripped his Tiki Torch tighter in his hand and watched the ensuing melee. The Phoenix lashed out with a fast kick that caught one of the thugs in the chest, and as his coat spun around, he ducked under a fist from the side and came up with the Kusanagi through the mans arm. Blood began to fall to the earth as he pulled the blade loose and spun, driving the point through the man’s chest. As that mobster fell and the numbers began to dwindle, the rest of the crew wasted no time in pulling out assorted firearms and beginning to shoot. Several rounds were fired though through quick thinking The Phoenix rolled past the assailant who was now missing a hand, and let him take the brunt of the gunfire. His body now riddled with bullets, the man fell to the ground. As he did, The Phoenix was up instantly, two throwing daggers in his left hand. Almost instantly they came out, one lodging into the shoulder of one of the men, instantly making him drop his pistol, and the other hit a man in the neck. A grizzly spray of blood flew forward, covering yet another attacker. With that mans attention drawn away momentarily, the Kusanagi sang through the air, cleaving the blood soaked thug from the shoulder down into his sternum. The numbers now dropped to what seemed like a manageable level at five men left standing and one with an unusable arm. At this point Timo couldn’t hold himself from the action. He smelled the blood in the air and felt the cries of the men below. Even as it now began to rain and the pellets fell from the skies washing the crimson from the pavement, he felt a rage building in him. The Phoenix slowly stepped to the side, a perfect image of efficiency, his foes circled around him waiting. He opened his stance, and Timo recognized an offensive pattern from his instructors at the dojo. Suddenly, the Kusanagi became alight and The Phoenix jumped forward, blazing weapon tracing an arc through the air as it came down on a foe and took most of the mans head with it. Gunshots blazed again and The Phoenix jerk violently as he was hit several times in the back from the three remaining men who were able to stand and fight. After such a noble display of valor, there was really only one thing Timo could do in response. Flash Out "Above all else, remember this Timo," Dalton turned and his matter of fact look seemed to say he meant business. "Whatever happens out there, don’t let him get away." "I don’t intend on it. We have a deal Dalton, you give me the mark, and I bring them in. It’s a wonderful thing." "Just don’t forget it." Dalton peers at The Samoan Silencer just before Timo leaves the room. The first thug never knew what hit him as the Tiki Torch crashed down on his head, splitting it open an leaving the man in a crumpled heap. As the goon to the right turned he was met with another vicious blow, the embers exploding in his face from the fire. Behind Timo, The Phoenix lunged forward, his flaming blade searing through the stomach of one of the ne’er-do-wells. Even as he cut through the man, blood poured from his wounds, and he coughed heavily. The Samoan spun and delivered a massive kick to the first thug he encountered, doubling him over. He quickly grabbed the man by the head and dropped him into a spike DDT into the concrete, leaving the mobster twitching like a fish. The few foes who could stand and run did so with much noise. With a quick and deft movement, Timo whirled and swung his torch, only to be met with the Kusanagi. For a moment, both men looked into the others eyes. Timo could now see how the blade stayed alight. A small tube ran down the wrist of The Phoenix, probably with alcohol or some other flammable liquid being delivered to the blade. As much as he had just seen a brutal massacre, he had to admire the man for what he had done. His breathing labored now, and blood beginning to drip below him, The Phoenix stared into Timo’s eyes. He reached into his coat and even as the Samoan followed his hand down with his gaze, he never allowed his attention to be broken. Shortly after, the man produced a picture of a woman who looked a lot like a pro wrestler herself. "Take this from me, and promise me that you will protect her. She is the key to everything." The raspy voice seemed like it was reaching the end of its length of rope. Timo silently pulled back his torch, and The Phoenix sheathed his sword, no longer ablaze. "I don’t understand." Timo offered. "You know that I was sent to stop you, don’t you?" The Phoenix barely cracked a smile. "I know this. Look at me though, in no time I shall find the end of my days, and your mission will be successful." He turned and coughed once, blood emanating from the corner of his mouth as he does. "Do an old man a favor though and let me go in peace." "Why should I?" Timo responded. "You are my mark, and I mean to make sure you answer for your crimes. The Phoenix laughed before turning away. He paused just long enough to offer these words before disappearing into the night air. "Because some day you too will know what it means to be me." He turned and waved, a blood stained hand whispered through the air. "We’ll meet again." Flash Forward "So what did you do?" Dalton’s voice cut the air sharply. "What could I do Dalton? He was dead on his feet, he just didn’t know it." Timo sighed a little. "Besides, the authorities were about to show up, and I had to get the hell out of that place." "I see." The answer was terse and immobile in its delivery. "You made the correct decision. Tell me, did he say anything else before he left?" Timo thought about the picture that was in his back pocket. He thought about what The Phoenix had told him. Then he shook his head. "No, just that he wanted to die in peace." He turned to leave again, this time to rest until the next call came. "If you need me…" "I know how to find you Timo." Dalton bristled. The door closed behind The Samoan Silencer, and he paused just outside. Reaching into his pocket, he drew out the picture and looked it over silently. "We’ll meet again Phoenix."
