Sometimes Things Have to be <i>Found</i>

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/30/08 David sat with his back against his favorite chair, sipping a hot cup of tea. It was a cold evening in late January, and this all-knowing man found it most comfortable to pass the night in this manner. His walls were lined with beautifully vast murals, expensive paintings and pictures of his fondest times. One picture had him, Josh and Eryc together, sadly as he sat here he knew there would never be a chance for them to do that again. Rising from his chair, David brought his cup into the kitchen rinsing it out before placing it in the sink. He would have a visitor soon, and he intended to be ready. ******************** Joshua sat in the driver’s seat of his eggshell white Mustang. It wasn’t the same as having his Viper, but he was sure in due time this car would come to mean as much to him. He watched as soft snowflakes fell upon the earth. His visions have gotten worse, each new one has been more detailed, more gruesome and even more heart wrenching. In the last one, as Eryc he could have sworn Krissy was there, but she was dead. The torture had to be getting to him; otherwise he’d never start hallucinating. He had to save Eryc and O’Malley had to be the key, he always has been. Joshua swung the car door open wide, stepping out into the brisk January night. He pulled his leather coat tight around himself, zipping it to the top. His black jeans didn’t quite provide the warmth he would have liked, but he should have thought about that before leaving the house. Slowly, he made his way to the building he knew by heart now. Quickly, he made his way up the fire escape, climbing to the roof. There, he made his way through a large steel door. The very same door Adam brought him to months ago. Summoning the courage, he pulled open the door and plunged into the dimly lit hallway. Joshua’s footsteps fell softly against the hard flooring, he hardly remembered reaching the door or entering the spacious apartment. He never looked at neither paintings covering the walls, or the fancy furniture with their finely decorated trimmings. His mind, his eyes were on only one thing. O’Malley. O’Malley rises from his chair, greeting Joshua with a warm handshake. "I’ve been expecting you Joshua, come sit." O’Malley states pointing to a chair across from him before returning to his own. Joshua sits, but remains edgy. Things have gotten worse, and he needs the consul. "So tell me Joshua, what is on your mind?" O’Malley asks, his strawberry blonde hair forming a crown across his head. "Eryc, I think things are getting worse and I don’t even know where to start looking for him." Joshua explained, the words just rushing out of his mouth. O’Malley nods taking in the sense of urgency in his voice. "I see…" He pauses, trying to find the best way to help him without telling him more then he can. Finally he asks, "Well what have your visions shown?" Joshua begins to recount the details he has observed, "It’s concrete, cold and a faint smell of mildew is in the air. The doors are steel, heavy and old, probably rusted. The place is abandoned; there are rats and other creatures…" he begins to trail off. His eyes flutter open; his nostrils are assaulted with the putrid scent of rat droppings and mildew. The soft footsteps fall across the floor, and he knows the figure is approaching. Shadows fill the room, he can feel the hard concrete behind him, his hands are restrained holding him up, and his legs feel weak. A soft growl comes from his stomach, his skin feels clammy, and his clothes stiff. A door scrapes across the floor, and then shuts with a thud. The footsteps grow a little louder. He can hear a chair scraping across the concrete floor. His head begins to pound again; it feels as though his skull is about to explode splattering blood and gray matter over the walls. His sight is gone, all that lay before him is darkness. He can sense the presence, the malice, and the hatred pouring through his oppressor. The soft rustle of material adjusting as the presence sits in the chair. A soft creak indicates pressure being applied to what can only be guessed as a wooden chair. Corvus speaks, but he can’t make out the words. He doesn’t know how he knows Corvus is speaking, but he knows. He begins to taunt him; he can feel fear creeping through his body, then a rush of courage. A cold hand falls upon his skin, then he says; "One way or another I will BREAK you." The drugs run through her body, slowly she fades away. He tries to help her, but his arms are tethered. "This is a little weird…" she says. Her hair is brushed back, a smile crosses Corvus’ lips. There is the crisp sound like someone biting in to an apple, followed by an orgasmic, yet painful moan from the woman. She passes out, silently and blissfully. Another man enters. "Is she alright?" The second man asks, another voice that sounds kind of familiar. Corvus replies a hint of lies in his tone. "Everything is fine, she fainted at the sight of our other guest that’s all. Please take her back to her room and then you can go home for the day." The second man says nothing; he carries the woman out of the room. He can catch a small glimpse of her face, and she too looks familiar. Corvus seems to draw nearer, examining him. He says something, but at first he can’t make out what, then; "I have some old friends to visit." He is teetering on the edge of consciousness, a thick syrupy feeling trickles across his face. It’s blood. His eyes flutter open as he struggles to stay conscious, a woman in white stands before him, her face, her supple body it’s all too familiar. He hears the words leaving his lips, "Krissy…is that you? I need you…I need you to give me strength to fight this. I need…" "Joshua?" O’Malley asks, snapping Joshua back to attention. "Sorry." Joshua apologized immediately, "I was trying to recount everything, and it appears I zoned out." "That’s been happening more frequently, hasn’t it?" Joshua nods, trying to remember the face of the woman, trying to remember anything about the other man. "He has a lackey." "I’m sorry?" O’Malley asks. "He has a someone helping him, I don’t think the man knows what’s happening." Joshua pauses, mulling it over before adding; "I think Corvus has him brainwashed." "That is very possible, Corvus is a master of manipulation." O’Malley adds. "You will need to find him immediately." "I don’t even know where to begin looking." Joshua replied. "The visions are so vague, they don’t give me a clear picture." A heavy burden rests upon O’Malley’s shoulders. He knows where Eryc is, he has always known, but the rules say he cannot give away this information; they have to find it on their own. "Don’t you know where he is?" Joshua asked, the urgency returning to his voice. "You’ve got to know, you are our only hope in finding him." Sighing, "I do know where he is." "You’ve got to tell--" Joshua begins. "You know I cannot." O’Malley interjects. "You have to find out on your own." Frustration boils over, "Well then what am I supposed to do?" "You’ll have to figure that out on your own." O’Malley replies softly. "Sometimes you have to find the answers on your own." Defeat, once again he’ll just have to figure this out on his own. O’Malley rises from his chair grabbing a collection of books from the table, one drops out just short of Josh’s feet, but David never pays it any mind. Joshua looks down, the book is brown leather bound. Curiously, he picks it up and begins to thumb through the book. His eyes fall on a passage dated two days ago, it is then he realizes what he has in his hands. O’Malley’s journal. Sometimes things have to be found, and for both Josh and Brennan one very important thing must be found out. The Where-a-bouts of Eyrc Morgan. Quickly Joshua looks up, David is putting his volumes away and is not paying attention to his guest. Josh slips the book into his jacket and rises from the chair. "I do believe you’ll do fine Josh." O’Malley says, his back still turned. "I trust you’ll find a way, you always do." "I guess so." He replies, heading for the door. "Thanks for the guidance." "I do what I can." Joshua shuts the door behind him. With this journal in his possession it will only be a matter of time before he finds his friend. "Hold on Eryc, we’re coming to get you."
