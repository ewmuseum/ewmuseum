Vince Jones Biography Real Name Vince Jones Nicknames The Violence The One Man Dynasty, 'NYC's Most Rough, Rugged, and Raw', 'The Conqueror, the Killa, and the King', Mr. AKA, The Career Killa, Jonesy, The Jones Hometown New York City, New York Height 6'7' Weight 269 Biography Vince Jones was born in New York and resided in a rundown apartment on a not so pleasant block in the city. He was born to a David and Jamisha Jones. At a very young age his father walked out on young Vince and the rest of the family. This left young Vince, his younger brother Keshawn, and the rest of his family struggling even more to survive in the city. Years passed and Vince Jones soon came across a group that he would begin to call family, a street gang known as the EDW. He along with the leader, K-9, Keshawn, and others terrorized the streets of New York City. Time passed and the crew began to become arrogant in their ways. In doing so, they planned a bank heist which went totally wrong and landed each and everyone of them in prison. Vince Jones spent two years in prison, but was eventually released. The reasons for his release have been a well-kept secret. Upon his release from prison Vince struggled to find a job that would keep him out of trouble as well as keep food on his table to survive. The details of Vince's life from this point on are shadowed by mystery but rumor has it that he may have joined an Underground Fight Club in New York which may have also been the center for other illegal dealings and activity. It is believed he fought in prize fights to make money. It is also rumored that he may have had other affiliation. It is rumored that after spending time in this Underground Fight Club as its champion he soon tired of the lifestyle and saw more a profitable use of his ability as a wrestler. The reason for Vince Jones becoming a wrestler is another well-kept secret, but whatever that reason may have been he appears to be determined to pave his way to the top of the business as well as deal out pain along the way.
