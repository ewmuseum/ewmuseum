Much louder boos echo at the mention of the name. Fred: And because of what they've done to my friend, Ethan Frost (cheers sound for the name). First, they broke his arm... Because of that, I had it up to here with them. Fred points to his chin. Fred: But that isn't the only reason I'm mad. I'm even angrier because of what they did to him as a person. Ethan is my best friend, and they changed him. Last week, Ethan got mad at me, and he walked out on me. He is what makes being in FUSE real fun for me, and they took that away from him, and took it away from me. Rather than enjoying his run as FUSION Wrestling champion, Ethan is sad... and because of that, I've had it up to here with The Affliction. Fusion points to the top of his head. The veins in his forehead grow, as he seems sincerely furious. Fred: So I've come out here tonight to put an end to this, and put an end to this "Affliction" and their nasty ways. They need to be taught a lesson, and I'm going to teach them one right now! The crowd cheers loudly, clearly enthusiastic seeing Fusion make a stand. Fred: So, right here, right now, I'm calling you out The Affliction, everyone one of you! SA: He has spirit, but I don't think this is a good idea. HJ: They have to come out, that way they can kill this moron! As the crowd doesn't know how to react to Fred's calling-out, Fusion lowers the mic and stares up at the entrance, almost shaking with rage. After a few moments, the lights dim and "Pet" by A Perfect Circle begins to pump through the audio system. BOOOOOOOOOO!!! HJ: Oh boy! This is going to be great! As the music is almost covered by the loud chorus or boos from the crowd, the whole of The Affliction make their way out as the lights brighten. Clinton Sage with the PTC Global Title around his waist, and just behind him is Lane Stevens along with The Legacy, Travis and Lance Reed. The four make their way down to the ring, as Sage has that evil grin on his face. The booing only gets louder as they reach the ring, and walks up the steps and through the ropes. SA: All four members of The Affliction are out here, this doesn't look good for Fred. HJ: It's looks great! Sage stops opposite Fusion, and stares at him with his twisted smirk, as Fred goes almost red in the face at the sight of him. Stevens stands just to the left of Sage, as each member of The Legacy slowly move towards the corners on either side of Fusion. HJ: Wonderful! Their surrounding him! Stevens hands the microphone he was holding as he came out to Sage, who doesn't unlock his stare from Fusion angry face. He then raises the mic, and has to wait a few long moments before the booing actually allows him to be heard. Sage: Something to say, Fred? Fusion raises his microphone, still with the anger in his eyes. Fred: You... I'm going to make you all pay for what you did to Ethan! Sage raises an eyebrow in an "oh really?" manner, when suddenly Fred's look turns from one of anger to one of surprise. He then strangely extends his hand for a shake towards Clinton Sage and says "Hi, I'm Fred Fusion!" SA: What is Fusion doing? HJ: Is he trying to play mind games? Sage just stares at Fred's hand, confused. Fred seems a little disappointed that his shake isn't accepted, as he looks over to Stevens, and then on either side of him at the Reeds. All of a sudden though, his pleased-to-meet-you look disappears as the one of anger and shock reappears. He shouts into the microphone. Fred: You... I'm going to make you all pay for what you did to Ethan! HJ: What the hell? SA: I guess Fusion... forgot who they were? Sage just stares at Fred, and shakes his head. He then looks over to Lane Stevens, and hands him back the microphone, saying "You deal with this nutter." Stevens accepts the mic with pleasure, and grins slightly, as he stares at Fred. HJ: Stevens is going to destroy him! Lane Stevens (LS): Good evening Fred. You're being a little too human tonight, I think the suits in the back would prefer that you be as two dimensional and wacky as possible. The crowd boos, as they do to most things Lane has to say. This gets a few smiles from his stable mates. LS: I was going to give you a free pass for this rant, because I think you're a funny guy. I was going to let you run your mouth and sound heroic. In a perfect world I could let you do that, and nothing would be lost. But this is not a perfect world sir. SA: They are circling around Fred like rabid dogs! Someone has to stop this... LS: Once one guy gets away with it the rest of the yahoo's on this federation think they can stand up to The Affliction and look cool. The rest of the federation will think that they don't have to show us respect, they'll think that we are fearful of a loser. And without fear, we cannot exist. And without us the company will not be nearly as interesting, could go bankrupt...and without the company you wouldn't have a job right now Fred. So, basically, in the long-term...we're about to beat the piss out of you to save your own job! SA: The fans aren't buying this equation. HJ: I still have to work some of the figures out, but I think its spot on. LS: You and Mr. Frost can spilt some jello in the hospital, it'll be a buddy picture. Fred stumbles to one side, as Lance catches him with a knee to the gut. Fusion folds over holding his stomach, before reacting, launching a right hand at Lance Reed, who steps back however avoiding contact. Travis then grabs Fred's arm from behind, allowing Lance to nail another knee to the gut. He goes for another, only Fusion manages to push him away with his foot. He then tries to get free of Travis' hold, only Lane Stevens steps in, nailing Fred over the top of the head with the microphone. SA: Oh my god! They're going to dismember Fusion! HJ: He should have just kept his mouth shut, like everyone should! Fusion is clearly groggy from the mic shot, as Travis releases him and lets him fall to the mat, a small trickle of blood running from the side of his head. Sage meanwhile just stands there watching and laughing at the beating, as The Legacy add some more with some nasty stomps on Fusion's grounded body. SA: Someone's got to do something about this! Where's Frost? HJ: Frost has a broken arm! The deafening boos however suddenly change to a roar of cheers, as none other than Jacob McKail comes sprinting out from the entrance and down the ramp. The look of his face is full of rage. SA: JACOB MCKAIL IS HERE!!! He slides into the ring as the whole of The Affliction, including Sage, starts delivering kicks on him as his does. McKail however manages to quickly get to his feet, and starts throwing furious punches around, half of them not actually hitting. SA: McKail has his own problems with The Affliction! He just seems to want to get in a fight more than anything else! HJ: And what a stupid thing to do against four people! Especially these four! SA: I don't think McKail even had second thoughts about it, he's just possessed! The cheers die back down into boos, as McKail's rush of energy is quickly overwhelmed by the numbers, and the Affliction get the upper hand, nailing him with punches, kick, you name it. SA: This is just out of control! Someone get out here and stop these men from hurting people! HJ: They brought it one themselves Ambrose! McKail is now on one knee, and can't even through a punch anymore, as he is kicked down and down. Sage then backs away from the mauling, and removes the Global Title from his waist and holds it in both hands. He then shouts something at the others, who back away from the kneeling McKail, as Sage launches forwards and nails McKail with a massive shot with the Global Title. SA: OH MY GOD!!! McKail goes down in a heap, as in turn the blood trickles from his forehead. He lands on the mat hard, his body completely motionless. BOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! Through the deafening sounds coming from the crowd, and under a rain of trash being thrown at him, Sage then turns to the lying Fred Fusion, and signals for Travis Reed to pick him up. Aided by his brother, Travis yanks Fred up off the mat, and to his feet. Sage then grabs a mic from the mat. Sage: Hi, I'm Clinton Sage. He drops the microphone and nails Fusion between the eyes with the Global Title. Fusion goes down with a thud, the gash in his forehead just bleeding even more. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!! SA: The Affliction has just completely taken out Fred Fusion and Jacob McKail! HJ: And what a moment it was! SA: This is horrendous! The booing and trash throwing continues, as Sage just laughs. He then looks down at the two unconscious men, and smirks as he lies down between them on the mat. SA: What is Sage doing now? HJ: Adding insult to injury, I love it! Sage lies on his back between the two men, as The Legacy and Stevens watch on, and brings the mic to his lips. Sage: Common! Just roll over one of you! ROLL OVER AND PIN ME!!! Just lie on me for three seconds! No one can fucking PIN ME!!! All of a sudden, "Ice Cold" hits the speakers, as the crowd cheers loudly. SA: At last! Frost reacts! HJ: But I was enjoying that! Ethan Frost, with his arm still in plaster, emerges slowly from the back, which causes Clinton Sage to get back to his feet. The whole of Affliction look on as Frost stops at the top of the ramp. SA: Common then Frost! Get in there and stop this! HJ: What? And take out four of FUSE's top men? SA: He's got to do something, he can't let them do this, especially to his friend! Sage just grins and twisted grin, and gestures for Frost to come down to the ring. Frost looks down at the ring, staring a whole through the Affliction. Breathing hard, sweat already dripping from his forehead, he seems to be hesitating. HJ: Looks like your hero is scared Ambrose! SA: He can't be! He has to do something! Frost looks down at the ring, clearly furious after what just happened, only the look on his face shows more worry than anger. As Sage continues to mess with him, Frost slowly looks down at the cast in his broken arm, an arm broken by The Affliction. He then looks back up at the ring, and swallows before slowly shaking his head and looking down at the ramp, before turning around, and walking back. SA: He's going back! HJ: He is scared! SA: I can't believe this! We saw last week that Ethan Frost was different! Fred Fusion came out here tonight for that! But he's just leaving his friends out there with The Affliction! He must be scared even deeper than I thought! HJ: And now they've gone and scared Fusion and McKail to! Sage just laughs as Frost backs slowly away, before raising his Global Title into the air. Travis and Lance Reed along with Lane Stevens lay a few more stomps on the grounded Fusion and McKail, before raising their arms in celebration. Killzone goes slowly of the air along with a chorus of boos and a ring covered in trash and blood.
