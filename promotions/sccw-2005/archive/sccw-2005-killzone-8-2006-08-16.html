SCCW — Card

Killzone 8, 16 August 2006

16 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killzone 8 Crisler Center — Ann Arbor, Michigan ---- Meeting of the Minds The scene opens backstage at a crowded arena in Ann Arbor, Michigan. Walking down the vacant hallway is none other than FUSE-W Universal Champion Damien Cruz, decked out in a Deftones hoodie with the belt secured around his midsection. As he walks the fans in the arena let loose their approval, cheering so loud he could probably hear them. But he doesn't go to the ring. He stops in front of a locker room door, marked 'Vance Raymes'. The fans cheer more. Knocking, he enters. We find our Custom CHAOS Champion sitting in his street clothes. He head shoots up. Damien Cruz: Uh, Hey man. About last week.. Vance Raymes: Stop. Raymes puts a beefy hand into the air, stifling the Universal Champion. Vance Raymes: I know you had nothing to do with that. I would'a done the same thing in that situation. Don't worry about it. Raymes stands, grabbing the Custom CHAOS championship and throwing it over his shoulder. He begins to pace back and forth, seemingly in thought. Damien Cruz: So why are you here, man? You've got the week off. Vance Raymes: You know why I'm here. There's no bad blood between you and me, but I can't say the same for me and her. There's no need to explain who she is. Both men know the reason for his presence, to garner the whereabouts of Tigera. Damien Cruz: What's with her, anyway? What does she have to do with you? Vance Raymes: Isn't it obvious? She's after the title. Last week she sent me a message and this week, I have one of my own coming her way. Done with his speech he walks out of the room, only to be stopped by the Latin Assassin. Damien Cruz: Be careful with her. She doesn't seem like much of a threat, but she's got a very sick mind. With a curt nod, the two men go their separate ways. ---- Introducing Derek Silver. All FUSE fans know him and all FUSE fans hate him. The little Caldera suck up has managed to keep on with FUSE, despite Caldera no longer being the owner, and this is what we find tonight. Silver sitting behind his desk, on the phone, as per usual. Que the booing. BOOOOOOO. In the background we can actually hear the booing of the very large FUSE crowd. A moment later there is a knock on the door which causes Derek to frown as he finishes up his phone conversation and then lets the person who knocked know that they my enter, however unwelcome their presence may be. Derek: It's open! The door swings open and in walks a young man, maybe 25, with long dirty blond hair, decent build, and a million dollar smile. Sporting some of the finest looking threads you'd ever see on a person it's clear from the start that Derek is in the presence of a P.I.M.P. Stranger: Hello. My name is Smitty. Smitty T. Duluth and I'm here to introduce myself as your new commish of FUSE. Derek (Clearly not impressed): Yes. I spoke with the owner earlier and they indicated that you'd be arriving shortly. Smitty: They? You mean you talked to more than one owner of FUSE? Derek: No. There is only one owner. Smitty: But you just said they. I'm not a overly educated person Mr. Silver, graduated high school and got a couple months of community college under my belt, but they sounds like more than one. Derek (Already rubbing his temples): I say they simply cause it's not know who or, well what, the owner is. So if you'll kindly stop bothering me you've made your presence known and I'm a very busy man. Smitty: Oh sure I won't take up but another moment of your time. See I was told I'd have first class accommodations as one of the important decision makers on this staff and I was just wondering if those would be in place by next show. Derek: Accommodations? Smitty: Yeah, you know, my own office, big black leather couch, sweet mahogany desk and office chair, much like yours there, hot tub, personal bath and shower, and a strip dancers pole in the middle of the office. Nothing out of the ordinary, just the usual. Derek just sighs as he shakes his head at the new commish he'll have to deal with here in FUSE. Derek: For Christ sakes and I thought Duke Williams was bad but now I've got a guy who thinks of himself as a ladies man/lover undercover when he is clearly nothing more than a corn ball dork. Smitty: Hey I resemble that remark! Derek just blinks at Smitty. Smitty: I'll have you know that my game is tight. I score on an almost nightly basis. Derek: I honestly don't think you can count "Lefty" and "Righty" as nightly scoring. Smitty: You can't? (snaps fingers) Damn! I was counting on those experiences being fair game in order to bolster my smooth talk. Now you've up and ruined it for me! Derek: Mr. Duluth you're going to have to leave my office before I am forced to throw you out of the building on your very first night with the company. I'm busy and you're...well quite frankly you're an idiot. Smitty (Appalled and shocked all at once) : Love you too babe! Quickly Smitty turns on his heels and heads out the door in search of more "friendly" FUSE employees and or fans. Ring rats to be honest. The other fans, not so much. Ring rats, however, are where it's at. ---- Father Woods vs Jay Quickness vs Evan Jackson SA: Next up, ladies and gentlemen, is the triple threat match between Jay Quickness, Evan Jackson, and new boy "Father Woods". HJ: Sounds like an ass hole to me ... SA: Now, now, let's not jump to premature conclusions. I like this idea ... a man of the cloth coming to give FUSE deliverance. HJ: God knows FUSE needs it ... 'This Is Not' by Static X hits, and Evan Jackson appears from behind the curtain. He walks from one side of the stage to the other, assessing the audience, before walking down the ramp, rolling in and sitting in the corner calmly, awaiting his first opponent. 'Smack my bitch up' by the Prodigy hits, and out saunters Father Woods to a mixed reaction from the audience. Following him is his aged manager, Martha Jorkin, limping slightly. Woods reaches the ring and slides in under the bottom rope, only to be kicked in the gut by Jackson before he can rise fully. Jackson lifts him to his feet and delivers three quick chops to Woods' chest, then whips him into the ropes opposite. When Woods returns Jackson goes for an elbow to the face but Woods ducks, bounces off the other ropes and goes for a flying clothesline, which Jackson also ducks. Woods hits the mat hard and Jackson stands, laughing at him along with the crowd. HJ: Impressive start for your "man of the cloth"! He just clotheslined thin air. SA: Hey, I'm not accounting for him. I just think you need to be a little more open-minded ... HJ: Business is about to pick up! 'Ante Up' by M.O.P. Hits and Jay Quickness emerges from the back with a towel over his head. He throws his hands in the air at the ramp way forming a X. He runs down the rampway and slides into the ring, where Jackson awaits him, Woods now crouched in the corner allowing himself plenty of time to recover. Quickness and Jackson exchange punches, until Jackson gets on top and drives Quickness back towards the ropes, where he clotheslines him over the top and to the outside. SA: Jackson is cleaning house! Impressive start from Damage. HJ: Looks like your man is ready for some more embarrassment. Woods is now up and ready for more, and he creeps behind Jackson, whose turn is met by a few quick punches. Whilst Jackson is on his back foot Woods delivers a few hard kicks to the thighs of Jackson who stumble backwards into the ropes. From the outside, his legs are grasped by Jay Quickness, who pulls them back, tripping Jackson. Woods delivers a couple of quick stamps to Jackson's head, whilst Jay Quickness begins to climb the turn-buckle from the outside. Woods lifts Jackson and gives him a kneestrike to the midsection, causing Jackson to double over. By this time JQ has scaled the turn-buckle and is ready to pounce. Woods sees JQ and cowers away, giving Jackson the precious second he needs to deliver a blow to Woods' ribs, and then a snap suplex. Woods rolls out of the ring, damaged, whilst Jackson, oblivious to Quickness's position, turns slowly, only to be met by a flying hurracanrana. HJ: Woah! SA: Awesome air attack from the Original Gangsta! He's taken the wind from Jackson's sails. Quickness now turns his attention to Woods, who is still recovering from the snap suplex. Woods is on the outside, struggling, now aided by his manager Martha Jorkin. The two are in conversation and neither see JQ lining up his run. He bounces from the ropes towards Woods on the outside, and uses the momentum to vault over the ropes and give a flying body splash to the unsuspecting Woods, who tumbles backwards into the barricade. Martha Jorkin makes a quick escape from the intimidating JQ, whose body splash took a lot out of both participants. SA: And again! Quickness has taken a strangle hold on this match with some high-impact manoeuvres. HJ: That's some impressive air-time ... the guy has balls of steel. Meanwhile inside the ring, Jackson has recovered from JQ's hurracanrana and decides to join the action on the outside. He slides out and picks on JQ who has now almost recovered from his high-impact body splash, and the two exchange punches. This time, JQ comes out on top of the exchange, forcing Jackson back towards the steel steps. He wears Jackson down with several more hits to the head, and then lines up a clothesline. He puts his energy into it, but the extra half-second he takes to line it up gives Jackson the opportunity to duck and, when both men turn, deliver a boot to the stomach, followed by a DDT to the hard floor below. SA: Oh! Lights out! HJ: That had to hurt; those mats are deceptively thin. SA: He's not moving down there! Jackson is finding his earlier form again! Jackson rises quickly and turns his attention to the barely mobile Woods. He walks over and grabs Woods by the head, before attempting to slam it onto the barricade. However, Woods manages to stop the force, rotating his body and delivering a right hand to the head of Jackson, who staggers back, and rolls into the ring. Woods follows and a moment later the two are up, in the middle of the ring, exchanging blows. Woods gains the upper hand and whips Jackson who, on return, delivers a clothesline. Woods is up quickly and, when Jackson bounces from the ropes and attempts another clothesline, has the mind to duck it and hook his arms around for a back-to-back neck-breaker. Woods is up quickly, and so is Jackson, and the two once again throw and block punches in quick succession. Woods this time comes off worse, being forced into the turn-buckle, from whence Jackson whips him to the opposite corner. Woods hits the turn-buckle chest first and bounces out backwards, giving Jackson the chance to hit a half-nelson back-breaker, after which he rolls over Woods for the pin. SA: Back-breaker! HJ: Excellent control from Jackson! 1... 2... ... and Woods kicks out. He rolls away to the edge of the ring and Jackson stands victorious, but only for a second as JQ comes out of nowhere and spears Jackson to the ground before hooking the leg for a pin. SA: The tempo here has picked up! 1... 2... ... and Jackson kicks out. Still feeling the effects of Jackson's DDT, JQ decides that it would be unwise to go for an all out attack, so he pauses momentarily to recover fully. As he does so Woods and Jackson both rise, and the three men stand in a triangle, looking at each other: all equal; back to square number one. HJ: A stand-off ... pussies. Deciding to be the protagonist, JQ stalks over to Woods and goes for a low kick, which Woods sidesteps and replies with a kick to the ribs of his own. JQ, however, catches Woods' leg and sweeps the other one away, tripping Woods to the floor. Still clutching Wood's right leg he lifts it, and delivers a leg drop right to Woods' groin. As he rises afterwards Jackson meets him with a stiff boot to the shoulder and a quick kick to the back of the head. Nevertheless JQ manages to stand, only to be pegged back by some sharp punches from Jackson, who whips JQ into the opposite turn-buckle and follows him in. However, JQ manages to deliver an elbow to Jackson's face and, as he turns away groggily, falls right into Woods' Rockbottom grasp. Woods lifts him high, sweeps the legs, and delivers his finishing manoeuvre. SA: Rockbottom! He likes to call it Whitsun! HJ: Call it what you like, Jackson's lights are out! Jackson rolls away slowly, as Woods and JQ take stock of the situation. Woods follows Jackson, lifting him up by the head. Jackson is clearly rattled, and Woods whips him towards the ropes, but before he can reach them JQ gets his head under Jackson's right arm, his own right arm across Jackson's neck and chest, lifts him, and delivers a Rockbottom of his own. HJ: Hello! SA: Back-to-back Rockbottoms! Ghettonomics! Jackson is out of there! Jackson now rolls out of the ring, and when there, simply lays on the floor. Inside the ring JQ and Woods now go at it, locking horns. Woods whips JQ into the ropes and upon his return delivers a low kick, followed by a snap DDT. Both men rise quickly and circle each other, before again locking up. This time, JQ pulls Woods into a headlock, which he sustains, causing Woods to go limp. Before the referee can intervene and check on Woods' health, JQ has let the hold loose and begun delivering kneestrikes. Woods falls to his knees, inspiring JQ to bounce from the opposite ropes and deliver a low missile dropkick to the head of Woods, who spins away from the impact. JQ bides his time before deciding to climb to the top rope nearest Woods, and attempt a flying elbow drop. SA: Coming down ... Woods moves, however, causing JQ's exposed ribs to clatter against the canvas. Woods rolls away, still recovering, whilst JQ writhes around. Woods climbs to his feet, stalking JQ, who slowly manages to regain his footing, still clutching his aching ribs. SA: That had to hurt ... the fall has affected Quicnkess. HJ: Damn straight it hurt, that's 220 pounds of flesh on his exposed ribs Woods delivers a kick to JQ's ribs, and then hits a sit-out suplex slam. JQ now lays motionless, dominated by the ex-priest, who now lifts him up and sits him on the top ropes of the turn-buckle nearest the commentary team. Standing on the second rope himself, he sets up another suplex, and prepares for a superplex from the top rope. However, as he sets this up, JQ begins to stir. He delivers a couple of quick blows to the midsection of Woods, whose grip is loosened. JQ finds his footing, and stands on the second rope in close proximity with Woods, whose superplex is still set up. JQ climbs to the top rope, now supported by nothing, but still maintaining the superplex grasp. By an amazing feat of balance, JQ lifts Woods into the air: the man is now standing on the top rope holding Woods vertically above him in a suplex position. SA: Oh my God! What is this man doing! Jumping backwards and out of the ring a good eight feet, he rotates in mid air, and with all the accuracy in the world manages to land a sit-out superplex right onto the announce table, which immediately crumbles beneath the 400 pounds of weight applied. HJ: Aaaargh, my leg! Get the fuck off you lard asses!!! SA: Ohhhh my!!! Incredible athleticism from Quickness! The motionless Woods lays in the rubble below, a trickle of blood emerging from the impact. JQ picks him up and rolls him into the ring, suffering himself but not to the same extent as Woods. Woods still has not moved by the time both are in the ring, and JQ goes for a certain pinfall... 1... 2... ... but no! Evan Jackson has risen from his earlier Rockbottoms and saved the match by pulling JQ outside. Jackson, now the freshest of the three contenders, ducks JQ's attempted clothesline and, when JQ turns, delivers a Yakuza kick right to his face. SA: Ohh! The tables have turned! He slides back in and picks up Woods. To make sure of the fall he kicks Woods in the midsection and sets him up for a suplex, ultimately turning it into a cut-throat suplex. SA: Dissection! Woods is out of this thing! 1... 2... 3! SKYE: .. THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH.. EVAAAAAAN JACKSON! Evan Jackson ---- Chaotic Proposal His smug grin could be seen clearly as 'The New Shit' Bobino approached the FUSE-W security checkpoint. After weeks of disrespect he was finally back in control, he was finally getting the props he deserved. Security: Hello Mr. Bobino.. Bobino: Oh, security. Heh. Is there a problem? Security: N-n-no, No problems. Bobino: No Problems.. Security: .. Sir. He cackles and walks off. The halls are bustling with ring crew, but they all seem to steer clear. He doesn't expect to be grabbed, shaken from his stupor, and thrown unceremoniously against a wall. Evan Jackson: What're you so happy about? The man formerly known as Damage lets go of Bob. Fresh off his first win in months, Jackson has to be feeling good. Evan Jackson: Pretty smug for someone who couldn't even get into the building until he recruited his 'friend' to help him out. Trust me, eventually everyone will notice Majin isn't coming back.. and you'll be back at the Super 8 across the highway trying to scheme your way backstage. Bobino: Funny guy, right? How do you know Majin's debut was a one time favor? How do you know he's not here.. tonight? These words seem to make the confident Jackson a bit uneasy. Bobino: How do you know he's not listening to this entire conversation? Evan Jackson: Shut the fuck up. The crowd cheers. Evan Jackson: Seriously, you want to bring C.H.A.O.S to FUSE? You want to poison it? There's just no way that's going to happen. You see we've got a little Pay-Per View comin' up in a few weeks, called ReVolution. The fans explode for the mention of ReVolution. Evan Jackson: I want you to call your little friend up. I want you to tell him that The Controversial Hardcore Army Of Sedition has hereby been challenged. Two on One. If I win, you two.. disband. Bob chuckles. Bobino: Oh Mr. Jackson, I accept. Gladly, I do! On behalf of CHAOS it shall be a PLEASURE.. Breaking you down. Bobino cascades past a smirking Damage as we fade. ---- Hide and Seek We fade in on Vance Raymes, walking through the parking lot to his trailer. The conveyance is his home, and has been with him so far at every FUSE-W event. His search for Tigera was unsuccessful, and he'd given up the thought she was in the arena. His hopes were rekindled as he threw open the door to his trailer.. and heard her voice. "Hiya Vance, I'm in here." Eyes wide as dinner plates, Vance charges the trailer, running into the kitchen. Rather than finding Tigera there, he finds only a small tape recorder, emitting the same phrase in a loop. Before he can turn it's too late, the door to the trailer slams closed and the sound of a latch being closed is heard, followed my the maniacal laugh of a certain 19 year old thai girl. Vance Raymes: OPEN THIS FUCKING DOOR NOW! Tigera: But why? We're just starting to have fun! We fade to the outside of the trailer where Tigera stands smirking, dangling a pair of keys loudly. Tigera: You hear that, Vancey? I've got an idea! Let's go for a drive! Vance Raymes: DON'T YOU FUCKING DARE! With a mock giggle she turns on her heel and climbs into the cab of Raymes truck, which is used to pull the trailer from venue to venue. Starting the engine she pulls out of the parking lot, pedal to the metal. She slows it down as they leave the driveway and enter an intersection. HJ: Oh my God. SA: STOP HER! NO! SHE'S GOING TO GET SOMEONE KILLED! The trailer veers out into the street, ready to slam head on into a closed bank. The drivers side door opens and Tigera rolls out into safety, more than can be said for Raymes. Before the trailer can hit the bank a large Semi plow's through it, much to the shock and awe of the fans. HJ: GET THE CAMERA OFF'A THAT, GET IT OFF NOW! SA: Oh no, HELP! SOMEONE GET EMS OUT THERE NOW! ---- Andre Jenson vs John Covel John Covel ---- What do you think? The shot switches backstage where a very pleased john Covel carries his frame down a small corridor. He carries him with much pride after his victory over Andre Jenson. He's changed into street clothes and looks ready to enjoy the rest of the show. Suddenly his face lights up as he spots new FUSE-W Commissioner Smitty T. Duluth. Covel walks up to him, extending his hand. Covel: Sir. It's great to have you aboard. Let me introduce myself. I'm John Covel. Smitty: Smitty T. Duluth! The envy of all men, the King of getting laid, the heir apparent to the Playboy Mansion bunny lovin' empire! What can I do for you? Covel: I hate to bombard you on your first official night with the company but I have a question to ask of you. Covel looks towards Smitty and flashes a smile. Covel: I've scored a couple of big wins in the past couple of weeks. Last week I defeated former Universal Champion Evan Jackson and tonight I defeated former Livewire Champion Andre Jenson. Smitty: Those indeed are very impressive wins. I'm almost certain that it will make you worthy of getting laid by one of the local ring rats. Covel drops his look to the floor before asking the question. Covel: What would you think about me in a LiveWire Title match? The crowd explodes at Covel's question, cheering him on. Smitty: Really? You came here wondering about a title shot and not about how to use it to gain the cherry pie prize? (Smitty looks up at the ceiling talking to himself) What do they teach guys in school these days? (Looking back down to John) Well since I'm in a giving and wonderful mood tonight I'll grant you that title shot. Covel: Really? At ReVolution? The crowd explodes again. Covel: Thank you very much! Smitty (Shaking his head as Covel heads off in excitement): The only time I've ever seen a man that excited about something was when Peter North met Jenna Jamison. ---- Accepted We fade up to a FUSE KZ Backdrop. Standing in front of it is Universal Champion, Damien Cruz. He stares right into the camera, with Dominique holding a mic to his face. Damien Cruz: Mikael Lundgard, I know you're not in the building tonight. But I also know you're watching intently tonight. The fans cheer. Damien Cruz: Last week you, and your 'Manager' decided to raise the stakes of our title match a little, by challenging me to an IRON MAN Match at ReVolution. I'm only here tonight to say ONE thing. I Accept. The fans in the arena go crazy at this news. Dominique is surprised as Cruz's lack of words, as he leaves. ---- JD Cross vs Jorge Samuelsson SA: Now folks it's time for the main event! Last week Jorge Samuelsson defeated Andre Jenson for the LiveWire title, and this week we have one of the most high profile title matches yet, as Samuelsson defends against 'Satans Submission Machine', Jay Dee Cross! HJ: Samuelsson has been on an impressive and destructive run, and him and Cross aren't exactly strangers. The last time these tow men met it ended in a brutal no contest. "Payback" by Flaw hits. SKYE: Ladies and Gentlemen, introducing first.. From Pheonix Arizona, this is.. .. <bJD Cross!!!</b Throwing the curtain back to an impressive POP, Cross makes his way to the ring. He's all business tonight, needing to take down the mighty viking to redeem himself with a title win. SKYE: HIS OPPONENT! The sound of a viking horn blows through the arena to a chorus of boo's from the crowd. HJ: Here comes.. Pain! SA: This man gives me nightmares, I actually check under my hotel bed! SKYE: From Trondheim Norway! "Bodies" by Drowning Pool hits. The mammoth frame of Jorge Samuelsson appears between the two FUSE-W video walls, which are portraying clips of his destructive career. The Livewire title sits gleaming upon his waist, in all it's platinum glory. The old XOW Platinum title design brings a whole new level of prestige to a title now held by a man not intent on losing. SKYE: HE IS.. THE FUSE WRESTLING.. LIVE.. WIRE.. CHAAAAAAAAMPION! The crowd continues their boo's, but those in the front row are too frightened to question Samuelsson. SKYE: He Is.. JORGE.. SAMUELSSON..!!! SA: An Impressive athlete no doubt. HJ: This guy is Seven Feet, 400 Pounds of pure punishment, Ambrose. Even I would be shaky staring across the ring at that beast. DING. DING. DING. With that, the match begins. JD Cross looks over at the monster, trying to formulate a game plan. This isn't No DQ, the Livewire title is returning to it's roots of pure wrestling. Taking a deep breath, the much smaller Cross lunges forward and locks up with the Nordic monster. With little effort the mighty Samuelsson Grabs Cross by the throat, lifting him into the air. As the air is sapped from the lungs of Cross, Samuelsson heavs him hard into the turbuckle, halfway across the ring. Cross crumples in a heap. SA: What a Start! HJ: JD Cross has gotta be hurting right now, Ambrose.. Those throws take a lot out of you. Undaunted, Cross pulls himself up using the ropes and charges Samuelsson again. Ready, Samuelsson catches him, lifting him into the air with a full body press. Letting out a mighty laugh fully audible even without the presence of a microphone. Getting a Samuelsson throws.. HJ: OH FUCK! SA: -cshhhhhh- Wha.. -shhhhhhhhhh- F-shhhhh-k! .. and THROUGH the announce table. With both announcers down just having dove away from their now destroyed announce table, Cross lays in the wreckage. He has a few lacerations on his arms, and one gash on his face causing blood to flow. SA: Are we back? HJ: We appear to be, and look at JD Cross! Referee Russel Jamison gets in the face of Samuelsson, threatening Disqualification if the match isn't kept clean in the future. A dirty glare from Samuelsson sends Jamison packing as Samuelsson leaves the ring. Scooping up Cross he rolls him back into the ring, getting back in himself. Lifting again he drops JDC with an impact Bodyslam. SA: Nothing fancy about that. HJ: That there isn't, but Cross is going to be feeling it in the morning. It'll probably be hard to notice through the stitches he'll need for those cuts, but.. Grabbing Cross by the arm, He jerks Cross to his feet, kicking him in the midsection. He lifts him into a powerbomb position. SA: Are we gunna see the Ragnarok? HJ: May be.. But instead, drops Cross with a Powerbomb, onto his right knee. Lifting Cross again he powers him into the ropes, and as Cross comes back he throttles him, and uses his momentum to deliver a _MAMMOTH_ spinning chokeslam, completely leveling Cross. The crowd actually cheers at this, as Jorge Samuelsson lifts him, and throws a giant boot to his midsection. Pulling him up he raises one giant arm to the air, signalling for the end. SA: Now THIS is it. Pulling him back to the center of the ring he drops him down with Ragnarok. Now seemingly unconscious, Samuelsson shows a sign of disrespect, pinning Cross with his boot. ONE! TWO! THREEEEEE! DINGDINGDING "Bodies" hits. SKYE: THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH AND STILL LIVEWIRE CHAMPION, JOOOOOORGEEEEE, SAAAAAMUELSSOOOOON! SA: It's obvious to me JD Cross was NOT ready for this match. HJ: I've got to attribute it to his brutal performance in the Asylum weeks back. You don't recover from matches that brutal this quickly, and after being thrown through our table there was just no chance. SA: We've had an eventful night folks, New commissioner, More matches announced for ReVolution, and the first successful LiveWire title defense since.. Fuck, I don't know.. But it's all the time we have for KillZone Eight! SEE YOU NEXT WEEK! STILL LiveWire Champion; Jorge Samuelsson
