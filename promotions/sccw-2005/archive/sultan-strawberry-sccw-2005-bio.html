Sultan Strawberry Biography Real Name Unknown Nicknames The Smoothie Criminal, Sultan of the Strawberry Emirate, Short Cake Kid, the Terrific Turban Hometown Strawberry Fields Forever located in the Strawberry Emirate Birthday Unknown Height 5'8' Weight 184 (announced at 14 pints) Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography He claims to be the Sultan of the Strawberry Emirates, trained by King Blueberry in the art of Bluejitsu. But what really lurks underneath the turban-styled mask of the Short Cake Kid? One thing is obvious, the Kid knows how to get it done in the squared circle. But where did King Blueberry find him? For that matter, what is his relationship to Baron von Blackberry? Well, honestly, none of these questions are likely to be answered, so just suck it up, kiddo.
