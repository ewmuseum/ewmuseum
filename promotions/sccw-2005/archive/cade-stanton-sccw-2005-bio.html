Cade Stanton Biography Hometown Pasco, Washington Birthday October 16th, 1980 Height 6'7' Weight 290 lbs Biography Born on October 16th, 1976 in Pasco, Washington; the 33 year old Cade Stanton's dream was to make in the National Football League. After being named 'Wisconsin High School MVP' playing for Appleton North and drafted to play for Indiana University, Cade tore his ACL and UCL in his right knee effectively ending his push for College football. The High School standout was considered the biggest disappointment in his hometown and American Football magazine called the injury a 'tragedy' to the future NFL'er. Cade did not take the news well, cutting himself from any past friends or families, Cade spent years on the streets addicted to cocaine and gambling. Arrested four times for possession and trafficking, Cade Stanton was on the fast track to an early departure from life when one of the daily street fights Cade ends up in turned out to be the first step to changing his life. Welcome to the story of Cade Stanton, and the remarkable recovery from crack addict to wrestling superstar. SCCW will be the home of failures, fears and ultimate triumph of the former football star.
