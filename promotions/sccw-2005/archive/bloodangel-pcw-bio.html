BloodAngel Biography Real Name Edward Lambert Nicknames Ed, BA Hometown Lexington, Ky. Birthday Jan. 30, 1970 Height 6'6 Weight 267 Biography Gimmick Description: BloodAngel is a Hardcore Legend. He has shed more blood over the years, than almost any wrestler in memory. He has broken bones, wrestled injured, crippled opponents, sent and been sent to the hospital far more than most people would typically suffer in their lives. He treats personal injury and danger like personal friends... VERY close personal friends. As one of his opponents long ago said of him, 'It’s like the man looked the concept of personal safety in the eye and told it to fuck off.' Wrestlers Style: Difficult to explain. Edward has wrestled for almost 18 years as a hardcore/power brawler. However, while most of the 'hardcore' wrestlers are all about garbage fighting with very little actual wrestling thrown into the mix, he is very different. Certainly he can garbage brawl with the best of them, however he predominantly uses the garbage wrestling as spots in his large repetoir of actual wrestling moves. A large number of his moves are based on the DDT or variations thereof. Edward discovered early in his career that a quickly done yet potentially devastating move such as the DDT, could potentially change a match. Now known as a master of the DDT and variants, Edward has cultivated the move into his repetoir. Appearance: Edward Lambert is a very well built guy. At his peak in the NWC, he was one of the largest men they had working for them. He is extremely well muscled, from his legs, to his neck, and everything in between. His upper body is literally covered in various scars from his lengthy career. Some from going through a production truck, some from glass bottles, one odd shaped one from a hot waffle iron, and others from various different objects that can and have been used as a weapon in or outside of the ring. His shoulders, upper arms, and back are each adorned by various tattoos. On his left arm and shoulder, there is a tattoo of an angel, standing atop a rock, holding aloft a sword, with its wings spreading out almost onto his neck. On his right arm is a throne of skulls, reclining in the seat, wings cast about it, is seated the twin of the angel on his left arm, the sword rests in it's lap, and it's head rests upon it's fist, in the classical 'Thinker' pose. In old English Script underneath the throne the words 'Rule or Serve?' are written. Across his back, stretching from one shoulder blade to the other, and extending down to the base of the spine, is an ornate tattoo of a pair of bloody and ragged wings. His hair is a dark black and comes down just an inch below his shoulder, at times he wears it in a tight ponytail, at other times he wears it loose. He has a small, neatly trimmed goatee most of which is black, save for a strip down the center of the goatee which is dyed a dark red. His eyes are a piercing cold Blue, though the left eye is permanently bloodshot due to repeated injuries. Overall, he does not look like someone you would enjoy meeting in a back alley somewhere. Ring Attire:To the ring, Edward wears fairly simple attire. He wears a pair of black combat boots that are reach half way up to his knees, and a pair of black full length tights with writing up and down each leg. On the left leg reads 'BloodAngel' in a silver, while on the right leg, in smaller yet still legible letters reads 'Rule or Serve'. Bio:Edward Lambert was born on a wintry January morning to a successful family. James and Maria Lambert had not expected a child to come into their lives, in fact, they had not even planned on raising a family and Maria's pregnancy came as a shock to the two of them, yet they took it in stride, and on that wintry morning, Edward Carter Lambert emerged into an unsuspecting world. His young life was not one of want; his father held fifteen different patents for various different things, and was a consulting engineer to the Ford Motor Company. His mother was the stereotypical at home mother, and all was well with his life, up until the tender age of ten. August 14th, 1980, Edward had been playing at a friends house, and had gone home expecting to see his parents waiting for him with dinner ready. What he found instead was a black police cruiser, and two uniformed officers waiting at his door with tragic news. His parents had been killed in a car wreck. Not some large horrible exploding car wreck, nor a car wreck after being hit by a drunk driver, however, one that was rather ironic. His father had been driving his car home with his wife seated beside him. When he pulled up to a stop light and hit his brakes, they simply didn't stop, one of the patents he held was for a new style of brakes, sadly, those brakes he had installed on his family car, failed and sent the two of them directly into the path of an oncoming dump truck. In shock, Edward left with the two police officers, and spent the next several years bouncing in and out of various foster homes and families. With no living relatives, yet ties to the community that his father had been a proud member of, he remained in Tolby Illinois and continued on as a much different child. No longer did he spend his time playing and having as much fun. Instead, he began to push himself in school to make his family proud of him. At the tender age of fourteen, Edward was moved into a family with another foster child, one Dash Fairborne Jr. Edward was already a large boy at that age, and he and Dash got on well. The two of them became inseparable and it seemed that what one would do, the other had to do better. When Dash started playing for the school soccer team, Edward began playing football. When Dash joined a local Karate dojo, Edward joined the Wrestling team. The competitive nature between the two of them pushed one another to excel and work themselves harder. Edward lettered in high school football his junior year, and was well on his way to lettering on the wrestling team when, after having watched a wrestling match at a local show, he decided to try some of what he saw. During a practice match, he grabbed his opponent and lifted him high into the air before slamming him down beside him. The thin padding absorbed some of the shock, but he managed to break one of the other kids shoulders with the move. After that, he was asked to leave the wrestling team, he did so with a gleam in his eye, he had discovered his calling. Edward attended the University of Illinois on a football scholarship. During that same time, he began to spend time working for a local wrestling promotion on his off time. During his junior year at the UofI he tore his ACL, and with that injury, ended his football career. Losing his football scholarship, Edward began working more and more for the wrestling promoter, at first to simply pay the bills, and eventually, as a full time job. School fell by the wayside, and Edward barely graduated with a degree in business. He didn't even bother to attend his own graduation, as he was busy wrestling. A year later, Edward was approached by a man named Dave Rorabeck. Dave worked for a fledgling company based out of Toronto Canada, called the Degenerate World Order and was offered a contract. So, taking with him his few years of experience, Edward moved to Canada and began working with a man named Scribe, who would shape his future in the wrestling industry. Edward entered the company much as he left it two years later. With little or no fan fare and little or no attention paid to him. While his time in the DWO had taught him the trade far better than he had known it before, and while he had learned much, he still had yet to find his niche. When his time ended in Canada, he moved to Las Vegas, and worked for a company there working on his technique and working on finding himself. Bouncing around in the circuits he discovered that the quickest way into the fan's attention and into public recognition was wrestling in the Hardcore leagues. The man who pulls off a picture perfect armbar is not cheered by the crowd, while the man who sends himself and his opponent through a table is. So thinking he wrestled some of the best he could find. He lost and lost but in doing so he learned his style. Then his extreme tactics started to pay off. He had learned the ropes and showed his stuff at an interleague show where he sent two men to the hospital with numerous contusions after a Triple Threat match. This caught some attention and he traveled from organization to organization wrestling in extreme matches all over the country. Of course these methods take a toll on his body but he continued on. Eventually, his wrestling returned him to Canada, and once again to the DWO. A changed man, Edward entered the DWO with a storm, and swiftly rose to become one of their top stars, however, barely four months into his new contract, the DWO closed due to management and monetary issues. Approximately a month later, he received a phone call, and a fax. Enter the National Wrestling Council. Edward entered the NWC as part of a group unit known as the Crimson Riot. Time and again this group proved themselves to be superior wrestlers while working for the New England Wrestling Syndicate, a division of the NWC. Yet, on the final PPV of the region, The group shattered, and separated as their contracts ended, or were picked up by different regions. After he left the Riot he entered into the Seattle Wrestling Federation, where wrestling in the singles ranks he was highly successful, facing and beating highly ranked members of the SWF and the NWC. When the SWF closed down following their Warriors of Wrestling PPV: Executive Decision and the very odd goings on there, Edward packed his bags and headed out of the city. Where did he end up? Minnesota State Wrestling. Coming in with some heat from the SWF must have made Edward more marketable to the fans that had seen him wrestle. In Minnesota Edward was finally given a chance to be himself, not some comic book character version of himself, but himself. Within less than a month of his entrance he was involved with a bloody feud with Zamza, another staple member of the MSW. This feud and it's matches is still spoken of with reverent tones by fans and wrestlers alike for the actual honest to god dislike that the two men showed in their matches, and the clinics that they put on when they faced off inside, and outside of the ring. Perhaps it was that feud which garnered the attentions of the owners of the MSW. After Steve Sullivan's untimely death, Edward and a man named The Hunter were given shots at the MSW Heavyweight title through a tournament. Both men passed through matches to gain the number one contenders match, however, there could only be one victor. Edward gained the title and held it for a record setting 143 days. During the height of his title reign, Edward took on, and defeated Lance Marshall, the reigning National Wrestling Council World Champion, in what many said to be one of the greatest matches of 2001. Though J.W. Oswald defeated him almost a month later, at NWC: EPIC, Edward had gained what he had desired for so long. A world title. Back in the MSW, Edward worked tirelessly to reestablish himself atop the division and to maintain his growing following. With the shutdown of the MSW, Edward along with the rest of the MSW roster went to LSW. Lone Star Wrestling. Fiascos and underfunding caused the LSW to begin to come apart at the seams. The tightly knit wrestling community began to break down. Friendships were broken; people walked out of the LSW rather than be part of it. Edward was one of those men who walked out. His contract with the NWC still active, he chose to take a scenery shift, and headed to England and the Parliamentary Championship Wrestling, on a four-appearance contract. Yet disaster struck after his first appearance. The PCW closed its doors, once more leaving him without a home. Back to the States he wandered, into Phoenix Underground Wrestling. There, he was entered into the Lucente Cup in an attempt to bring home the NWC Tag Team Titles. However, unlike all the other teams involved in the Lucente Cup, he had no partner, and wrestled solo in handicap matches. His first several matches he won wrestling solo. Then he traveled to Hawaii to wrestle the Cult of Personality. He picked up a partner for that match, Hiro Saotome, his friend from back in the MSW. When the Lucente Cup paused to build up for the final match, between Night and Day, (Edward and Hiro) and the Demigods, Edward returned to the PUW to wrestle his final match in the Underground circuit. A newcomer to the division, a man named Dez Bradley, was calling Edward's reputation built upon a false premise, and issued him a challenge. At the Pay Per View, Casino Nights 3: Showdown, Edward defeated Dez in a match that left the other man crippled and forced his retirement. Edward and Hiro wrestled and lost against the Demi Gods in the finals of the Lucente Cup. Yet, following the match, Edward was once again on the move. His friend Hiro Saotome was working for the Hawaiian Island Wrestling division and with nary a glance over his shoulder, Edward shifted to Hawaii for a short time. However, during that same time, a secret that he had been holding close to home came to light. Edward was battling with alcoholism. Years of hard living and frequent bouts with injuries had left him seeking solace more and more inside a bottle. After the NWC reformed and held the PPV: Devils Due, Edward's condition took forefront. He wrestled a match drunk, and was let go from the NWC. Seeking to turn his life around, Edward entered and received treatment at the Edgar Woods Clinic. Two months later, he checked out of the clinic, and went to the National Wrestling Council, cap in hand. Barely a week later, he was once again wrestling for the NWC, but the NWC had changed and it's very existence, previously thought secure, was facing annihilation by it's very founder. Chris Schukar, the President, Founder, and CEO of the NWC was dying of lung cancer, and with his own impending demise, he wanted to ensure that his creation did not outlive him. Edward found himself thrust into the limelight as the most out spoken opponent of Schukar and fought tooth and nail against wrestlers who allied themselves with the man intent on sending them all to the unemployment line. Along the way, Edward once again gained the NWC World Heavyweight Title, making him one of the few two time champions in the organization history. He fought past and through various opponents defending his title and seeking to salvage the NWC, to save his home. Sean Laughreah and Foster Nackedy, two of the brightest young stars in the NWC fell on his title reign, Leonard Aarons III fell to him, before he was eventually defeated. Yet he kept his message going, and at the final PPV of the NWC: Return to Purgatory, Edward took steps that he thought would prevent the destruction of the place he loved. Edward was wrong and the NWC as an organization crumbled. Edward took time to step back and stare hard at his life. He was almost thirty four, his body was a wreck, and he had several nagging injuries. Having not wrestled in almost two months had helped him, however he still had a long way to go before he was back at 100%. December 2006, Edward Lambert, known to millions as BloodAngel, returned to the wrestling world in Global Championship Wrestling. Within short months of his arrival, he captured their World Heavyweight Championship belt twice. Upon his final defense of the World title, he tore his left knee and was forced to take a sabbatical from the sport. Withdrawing from Global as their champion he healed and recuperated his knee before again returning to Global. Upon his return he immediately captured their Intercontinental championship belt and held it until their close.
