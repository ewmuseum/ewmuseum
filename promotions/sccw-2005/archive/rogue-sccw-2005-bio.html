Rogue Biography Real Name Dave Montgomery Nicknames Rogue Hometown Eagleville, Missouri Birthday May 31st, 1982 Height 5' 9' Weight 170 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography The ultimate "game geeks', Dave Montgomery (Rogue) & Jim Brandt (Berzerker) have progressed from table-top gaming, to MMORPG’s, to Live-action Role-play and beyond. Either having conquered or been banned (for taking it way too literal, like challenging the local SCA "Duke' to a blood duel in his front yard on a non-SCA Tuesday afternoon) from their previous paths of adventure, they now quest to become champions in the ring. The brains of the duo. He’s the thinker/planner/lead speaker for the team. Lightning fast and technically proficient in the ring, fights honorably until the opponents prove themselves unworthy of that honor. If the situation calls, can be insanely sadistic and hardcore (preferred weapon is a kendo stick) because he’s "dual-class' with a high "weapons proficiency'. TDP has shoulder length brown hair and brown eyes. To date he has been completely unable to grow a beard, with vexes him to no end. Although his build is slight, his shoulders and forearms are quite defined (from archery and swordplay practice). To the ring he wears a Hunter Green traveling cloak (with gold piping). He wears Hunter Green long tights with a golden templar cross on the thigh. Black boots with a gold +5 on the side (because they’re magical boots that are +5 to hit) and black kneepads and elbow pads. Often he wears a black "Jesus Saves...everyone else takes full damage' t-shirt but occasionally when the mood strikes him he’ll wear other shirts (like Thundercats, Green Lantern, Transformers, the Mikey Whipwreck dragon one, etc..) Out of the ring he usually wears cargo shorts and one of those silly anime button downs. If it’s a formal occasion, he has a wide array of tunics, cloaks, and frilly renaissance shirts to choose from. PS: He also has a full set of armor and a sword stored away.
