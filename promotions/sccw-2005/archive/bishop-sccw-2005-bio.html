Bishop Biography Real Name Rashaun T. Bishop Nicknames The Biloxi Beast Hometown Biloxi, MS Birthday 8/20/1984 Height 6'8 Weight 298 Biography Tavon’s mother Wilhelmina Brown was a good mother, and when two of her three children died in a car crash that also took her brother and their uncle, she decided to focus all of her mothering attention on the remaining child, the youngest, Tavon. Wilhelmina didn’t have much to provide for her child, and while her love might have made up for it for some children, Tavon was not one of these. His needy home and crime-ridden neighborhood had a predictably negative effect on him, and he started stealing as much as he could. As he got into double digit years, his growing body gave him more tools needed to bully and pester the other kids. By the time he was 11 he was nearly six feet tall, as muscular as a high school football player, and doubly as mean. After countless attempts to help him, his principal sent him to St. Stanislaus Boys’ Correctional and Educational Facility so he could be whipped into shape. And he certainly was. For five years he was beaten, insulted, and hurt by the trainers at the school. He learned a few things, but that wasn’t what interested him. What interested him were the weight room and the schoolyard. He would lift everyday until he nearly passed out, then after resting go to the school yard and pick fights with the biggest seniors he could find. For the first few years he would get beaten even worse than by the trainers, but upon reaching thirteen he was one of the biggest students, and he would pick out people to challenge him until eventually the challenge was gone. When he was 16 and only a sophomore, he walked out of the facility and headed to the place he missed most of all in his enrollment/imprisonment: the streets. For two years he survived by working small jobs and stealing, barely affording clothes and a pair of shoes. Mostly he got his thrills from fighting random people. At this point in his life, he rarely lost. One night after being thrown out of a bar in Jackson, he was sought out by a man who identified himself as a wrestling legend. Impressed with both his fighting and his flare, Eugene Robinson took Tavon to California to train him in the Dynasty Dojo, where he received discipline and training. Only the training worked, as we can see now with Bishop debuting in FUSE.
