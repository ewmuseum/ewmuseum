Chris Aleister Biography Real Name Ben Nicknames The Honest Answer, The Unspoken Truth Hometown Denver, CO Birthday 06/24/86 Height 5'7' Weight 180 Biography As a child, he was abandoned by his parents, a 16-year-old girl who couldn't develop a relationship with her child so she pushed him away and an 18- year-old boy who couldn't pull himself away from immaturity long enough to become a father. Both were drug addicts and, eventually, parted ways with Aleister following his mother for the ride. They moved into a small apartment with another drug addict and a drug- addicted roommate. Chris' only sanctuary from this was his grandparents house where the allure of watching wrestling on television with his grandfather being the only sunshine of an otherwise gloomy day. If all of his time could be spent at his grandparents house, Chris might have turned out slightly normal, however, it could not and things had gotten worse. His mother was becoming more and more strung out on cocaine and had found another boyfriend which began her beatings from the current boyfriend. One day, while sitting and watching a wrestling program he recorded off of television, there was a pounding at the door. Chris ignored it as best he could but turned to find a melee in the hallway involving the current boyfriend and his roommate battling the other roommate and his brother in the hallway while an axe stuck through the now parted door of the house. Chris was mesmerized by the mayhem and watched as his life was falling apart. However, he wouldn't realize this yet. Another family destroyed and another one beginning. His mother was pregnant at this point and Chris had himself a little sister. The problem with this was now Chris wasn't the baby nor was he the oldest because his cousin had moved in with them. You would think that with a new dad, who was actually the best one he had encountered so far, a mother, a sister and a new older brother, that life would be good. But Chris eventually had gotten lost in the fold. His cousin had caught the new father's eye and they became a better father/son duo and his mother finally found that motherly love she had been storing all along and used it for the newborn of the family, leaving Chris to fend for himself in every emotional attachment in his life. On top of this, Chris was becoming abused by his 'older brother', who turned out to be bi-polar and would take out all of his aggression on the younger sibling. This was where Chris received much of his training, in life and in combat. The beatings he would take were brutal and it took time before he would ever fight back but his bad luck was only starting. One thing Chris felt he could always rely on was family, the stability that had been built with a loving mother and father and siblings who would help you in a time of need. But what happens when the barriers this family were built on begins to dissolve as quick as the brain when powder begins to hit them? The pillars fall and Rome begins to burn. Slowly everybody began to disappear. His mother left, the woman who gave him life disappeared and left him with the man he now called father but not before taking his sister with her on whatever journey they embarked on. His cousin ran away days after this and, after a cocaine binge that left him rattled, the man he called father disappeared, leaving the young Aleister on his own in a house with nobody but himself to keep him safe. Survival became another lesson in his life that he had to learn on his own. But that ray of sunshine filled in again as his grandparents took him under their wings and, instead of calling him grandson, began to call him son. It was a hard adjustment going from chaos to conformity but he had his grandparents to show him the ways of life now and no longer needed wrestling as a way out and could enjoy it for the sport that it was... That is, until he took the rainbow-colored glasses off and found it to be just as chaotic there as it was in the rest of his life. His grandparents fought and tensions arose. A teenager Aleister, who was rebellious enough being a teenager, got even more rebellious as the tensions arose. By this time, his stepfather had returned along with his real father who he developed close bonds with each. Neither of them were addicted to the hard drugs but couldn't pull themselves completely out of the gateway which he didn't mind as long as a little stability could stay there. However the newfound Aleister began hanging out with the wrong crowd, picking the wrong group and going on Clockwork Orange-esque rampages around the towns, destroying property, hurting people and being chased by the police. It wasn't long before Aleister found himself in trouble with the law and on the outs with his rays of sunshine, his grandparents. However, he persevered through all of it and ended up graduating from high school and beginning a new life. In this new life, he has found that the rebellious ways were causing him to fall astray to a real belief. However, finding said new belief turned him back into the feeling of anarchy. He was never accepted by anyone and thus, found his spoken word as the way out. He preaches to himself, reminding him why the ways of the world are bad and need to be changed. He enter XUW as not only a man but as a proverb. A problem solver. A definition of non-conformity. He enters XUW as Chris Aleister, a man who has no definition but is a word in the dictionary. This screwed up kid will one day be the poster child of the world. His world.
