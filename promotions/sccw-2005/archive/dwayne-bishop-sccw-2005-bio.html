Dwayne Bishop Biography Real Name Dwayne Bishop Nicknames Bish, Dee Hometown San Antonio, Texas Birthday September 28, 1976 Height 6'6" Weight 305 Biography Bishop started off wrestling for the company known as the National Wrestling Council. Bishop had a knack for working the ring, taking to the business as if he grew up in it. He quickly rose to be one of the top faces of the company, winning two regional titles before entering a battle royal and earning a shot--alongside friend and sometimes foe Heatwave--for the NWC world tag team titles. Heatwave and Bishop went on to win the before mentioned titles, and defended them at the NWC’s last pay per view, Return to Purgatory. Though the titles changed hands on that night, the record books would never be updated. Heatwave and Dwayne Bishop were the last known holders of the titles. Bishop went on to join Axiom when the NWC closed, becoming a main event star alongside names like Leonard Aarons, Heatwave, Ben Van Iten, and Damon Hayes. Started to struggle at the end, but it was more bad luck than it was his performance in the ring. Bishop lost a slew of matches because of outside interference, and in one incident a raging inferno. Axiom went on to merge with another fledgling company known as Seventh Circle Wrestling, but even then the company collapsed. Bishop then became a main eventer for an up and coming company named RWA. Sadly it is no longer considered up and coming, as the bookers went missing and no word on salary, travel compensation, or further events was ever mentioned. Taking a few months off, Bishop then turned up to feud with his adopted brother Matthew Kurresh at the request of then owner of NAFW Sean 'Spaz' Thomas. A feud that carried Bishop into the main event scene within weeks of his arrival. Unfortunately, the NAFW closed a month after that.
