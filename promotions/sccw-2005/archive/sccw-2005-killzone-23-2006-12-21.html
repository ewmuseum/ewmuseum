Killzone 23 State Farm Center — Champaign, Illinois ---- A Prison Visit As the lights in the arena fade a friendly yuletide hymn greets the crowd cordially. Vigilantly, the text "Prerecorded Feed" fades into view before fading back out. The ghostly call sounded as if a choir filled of children were humming the merry anthem. Snow flakes begin to fall as if some made-for-tv movie were about to remind the audience of the significance of the season. This is when something peculiar moves into view. It appears to be a state prison with barbed wire walls encasing the macabre edifice. The singing grows as the camera pans closer into the night focusing on the building. Inside Ron Mexico is staring intently into the void just below the ceiling. He noticed the paint chipping and one of the tiles in the ceiling missing. For a second he got an idea of how he'd escape when suddenly a cop steps between him and his supposed salvation. He chewed intently on gum as he smirked at Ron. Finding his presence, unappealing Mexico turns to face the other side of the room where two large black guys are talking back and forth. It felt as if he had been here for hours waiting for a "visitor". The red fire door that led to the visitation room offered him little solace. Worse yet he was starting to critique the imperfections in the consistency of the paint matched with the red in the game room. The room had four round tables. Behind the one The White Wine occupied he did so with the utmost dignity as is possible. Even though he kept his arms to his side as if he were being hugged by some feral child due to the threads. They made him nauseous. His orange jumpsuit irked him more and more as he noticed a few stains that made him distraught. He felt his will slip more every second. Whispering at the game table to the left a few Muslims were playing a game of "Uno". Straight ahead a loud obnoxious black man named "Big Poppa Smurf" traded quips with his lackey. The origins of his name were unclear though Mexico surmised he was a 'Smurfs' fan. The sidekick was a man named Joe who Ron came to understand had complained to the warden the day prior about lice in the kitchen, which he protested against working in. At least until the situation was fixed. As Ron wiped his face with the palm of disquiet he noticed between his fingers the two men were approaching him.
