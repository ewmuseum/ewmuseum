King Blueberry Biography Nicknames The Prince of Produce, The Duke of Delicious, The Yahweh of Yogurt, The Master of Muffins, The Ambassador of Anti-oxidants Hometown Fruit or Consequences, Blue Mexico Height 5'10' Weight 201 Fed Titles LiveWire Championship (x1) Biography This is where the awesome backstory goes, and believe you me, it's pretty fuckin' awesome. Wrestling Style High flying submissionist Finisher Move The Jack Lalanne Driver Finisher Description Pumphandle reverse piledriver (Psycho Driver 2) Finisher Setup Move Varied Other Moves Normal Moves Frankenberry (a frankensteiner with the coolest name ever) Standing front-flip legdrop Headscissor variations (armdrag, armbar, bulldog, etc.) Handspring corkscrew elbow Wheelbarrow Suplex Blueberry Thunder Bomb (blue thunder bomb) Jumping calf kick Anything else that's of a moderate degree of flippy or spinny, but nothing TOO acrobatic. The Specially Named Stuff Dead and Berried -- Snap suplex rolled through into a knee strike to a seated opponent. Blend-O-Rama -- Airplane spin into a double-knee gutbuster. Low Hanging Fruit -- Running, diving headbutt to the nether-regions of an opponent seated split-legged in the corner. Farmer Jim's Olde Tyme Owie Machine -- Swingout full nelson bulldog. Bride of Frankenberry -- Tiger feint frankensteiner. (Basically, a 619 into a frankensteiner that throws an opponent to the outside of the ring) Finishers and Stuff The Jack Lalanne Driver -- Pumphandle reverse piledriver (AKA: Psycho Driver 2, Click to View) The Jack Lalanne Driver, Late Night Infomercial Edition -- Cutthroat torture rack piledriver (AKA: Psycho Driver 3, Click to View). This is a 'big match' finisher, and should only be used when the JLD fails to put someone down. Fruit of YOUR DOOM~! (w/Sultan Strawberry) -- Lightning Spiral, with both Blueberry and Strawberry executing the move on the same opponent at the same time for extra spin and a nasty landing.
