Jacob Cross Biography Real Name Jacob Campbell Hometown Marlow, Oklahoma Birthday 05-12-1979 Height 6'2' Weight 238 Fed Titles N/a Fed Awards N/a Biography PRIME- Came into PRIME on the second show in the history of the company to form a dominant stable with Rock Startling and Michael Cade, they called themselves TRUE (The Real Untouchable Elite). The trio remained undefeated as a group for a couple of months with Rock winning the Intense Championship before Cross left after disputes with management. On the way out he made sure to put his buddy, Rock, over. Three years later, after being out of the spotlight for that time, Cross returned to PRIME when he became a third member of the tag team Los Diablos. The trio was set to do a triple team kind of thing, where their opponents would never know which two of the three they'd face each night. Jacob muscled his way into PRIME, in this fashion through dealing with Los Diablos (Baltasar and Angelus) manager, Enrique Morales. After helping Morales to some success in the PTC Cruiserweight 2 Tournament, Morales was legally obligated, due to the contract he'd signed, to bring Jacob to PRIME, as the newest member of Los Diablos, and thus Imperium (The stable Los Diablos had formed under the leadership of Hoyt Williams). The PRIME return was short lived, however, after another disagreement with management. Cross was scheduled to face Brandon Youngblood for the 5-Star championship but on the eve of the event was informed that since Youngblood was feeling under the weather that even if Jacob won, they wouldn't let him have the 5-Star title. Jacob left the promotion right afterwards and took a small amount of time away from the wrestling business. Axis Wrestling- This was a small time company based out of Phoenix, Arizona, and when Jacob returned to the world of professional wrestling he found it was the only place he could go to work for at the time. Due to his disputes with PRIME management, and the fact that they were well publicized; none of the big companies would touch him, leaving him with no other option but to start small again. So he joined up with the fledgling company to try and help build them. He quickly teamed up with a rookie, just breaking into the business, name Tobias Stevens. Taking Stevens under his wing Jacob helped guide the duo to the Axis Tag Team Championships, before the company closed down due to financial troubles. After the close of Axis Jacob Cross didn’t return to the ring until a new small time promotion, known as PCW (not the huge PTC one but a smaller one) opened up. A few shows after they opened Jacob Cross signed with the company and quickly won their Heavyweight Championship. The victory would be short lived, however, as the company closed down three nights after he’d won the championship. For the better part of a year and a half Jacob Cross has been out of the wrestling spotlight. Titles/Awards Won: Axis Tag Team Championships w/Tobias Stevens & PCW Heavyweight Championship Other Feds PRIME, Axis Wrestling, PCW Other Fed Awards Axis Tag Team Champion, PCW Heavyweight Champion Wrestling Style Submission/Technician w/plenty of cheating Finisher Move Cross Effect Finisher Description Old School, WCW, style Liontamer Finisher Setup Move Cross Cut Setup Description Christians Impaler Finisher, only a bit quicker Other Moves Style Jacob’s style is very heavy submission and technical based. The majority of his offensive gameplan will fall into those catagories and the majority of whats left from his aresenal is where he’ll add in his love for cheating at every oppurtunity. Strategy Jacob utilizes plenty of strikes (Punches, kicks, knees) in order to weaken his opponents enough to utilize his ultimate game plan. Jacob will employ a very strategic gameplan of working certain areas of the body (A very ground and pound sort of approach) to set up for his finisher (A Version of the Old School Liontamer he calls The Cross Effect). Through out the match he’ll use a variety of submission and technical moves, along with a variety of slams and breaking moves to wear down his opponents back, neck and legs. He doesn’t waste any energy on moves that will not ultimately put him in the best position possible to finish his opponent off with the Cross Effect unless it’s in an enviroment where his submission finish isn’t going to necessarily get the W (Such as in Rumbles, ladder matches, falls count anywhere matches, that sort of thing). Along with this plan he’ll slide in plenty of dirty tactics to try and make sure he retains control of the match as much as possible during the duration of a contest. Strengths - Well rounded athleticm. - No morals, or Code of Honor, holding him back from doing anything and everything deemed necessary to win - Able to adapt to an opponents style in order to best utilize his own abilities to win - Excellent ring general. He’s a veteran of the business and has learned the tricks of the trade - Knows how and when to take advantage of a mistake. - So adapt at cheating that it’s hard to catch him in the act, but it does happen. Weaknesses - His hot headed temper can get the best of him and causes him to lose control of matches at times and can often lead to him getting himself disqualified. - He is a tough guy, and not easy to beat, but when it comes to being locked in submissions and such, in the middle of the ring with no where to go, he will tap out in order to avoid injuries. It’s better to be able to fight another day than be foolish and hold out to make the fans happy. - Flyers and powerhouses give him a great deal of trouble cause they’re faster, or much stronger, than he is and it doesn’t necessarily work well with his technical, ground and pound, kind of attack.
