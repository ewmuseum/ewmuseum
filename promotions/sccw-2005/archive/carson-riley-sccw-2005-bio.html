Carson Riley Biography Real Name Matt Briesacher Nicknames 'One Take' Hometown Hollywood, CA Birthday July 13, 1982 Height 6'1' Weight 224 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Carson is a former Hollywood, action movie stunt double. After several years in the business, he decided to take his athleticism elsewhere by trying his hand in the world of professional wrestling. Often mistaken on the streets for Matt Damon, Carson is very personable with his fans and possesses a great deal of charm and charisma. Carson still resides in Hollywood and still enjoys some of the benefits from his days living it up with Hollywood’s elite; however, he is frequently reminded of the difference between being on the A-list and portraying someone on the A-list. He is normally the smaller man in the match but has a never-say-die attitude that fans can identify with.
