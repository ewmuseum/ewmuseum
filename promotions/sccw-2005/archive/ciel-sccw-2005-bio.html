Ciel Biography Real Name Skylar Green Nicknames CL // Sky Hometown Fort Lauderdale, FL Birthday April 29, 1979 Height 6'5' Weight 240 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Skylar Green is a second-generation wrestler, his mother being a notable Okinawan joshi pro wrestler in the 70s. Though he grew up with his mother having retired from active competition to raise him, he enjoyed seeing her matches, and she made income for the household by training new girls to enter the sport, with her son watching and helping around the ring however he could. After graduating from university with a degree in anthropology, he trained to wrestle in Sapporo under the Spirit Arise Mountain (roughly translated) dojo. After finishing his training, he moved to the United States and started in regional independents as 'Seppuku.' An athletic standout, he quickly came to the attention of PTC after changing his ring name to The End. He wrestled in PTC and WO until leaving active competition in 2004 to establish a home for three adoptive daughters. Since then he has wrestled on and off on the national level, taking a light schedule that leaves him time to be with his family. He owns and operates his own training school in Fort Lauderdale and also wrestles in the Florida independent scene. After his run in GCW, Ciel was content to return to the small stage, where he finds the most enjoyment. However, when approached by former opponent Dawn McFarlane seeking his forgiveness and help, he agreed to return to the national ring once again. This time he doesn't wrestle for himself but for Dawn's sake, but he will do his best to enjoy himself in the meantime. Other Feds ---- Other Fed Awards ---- Wrestling Style Ciel works a very low-impact submission style reminiscent of traditional lucha libre and European catch-wrestling. He uses throws and grappling moves in order to set himself into position for holds.
