Evan &#039;Damage&#039; Jackson Biography Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday 12th July 1977 Height 5'11' Weight 210 Fed Titles FUSE Livewire Champion, FUSE Universal Champion, FUSE Custom CHAOS Champion Biography Appearance: Toned and fit but not overly muscular. Short, sometimes spiked hair, blond/brown in colour. Occassionally will have a goatee or stubble beard. Reasonably tanned. No tattoos. Wrestling Attire: Short tights, usually black, dark red or blue. Boots with matching kickpads and kneepads. Wrestling History: Former 2 time UWA Universal Champion and the first man to hold both the UWA Global Title and the UWA Universal Title simultaneously. Moved to XCW where he joined the C.H.A.O.S stable and was moving up the Custom CHAOS title ranks. Personality: A normal guy but intensely focused. His martial arts background has made him an uncompromising competitor. Thinks he should be one of the most highly paid guys in the business and will not hesitate to injure someone to prove a point. Rich due to big money UWA contracts and bonuses in XCW, he also has several business interests, though their exact nature is unknown.
