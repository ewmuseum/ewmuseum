Leah Adams Biography Real Name Leah Adams Hometown London, England Birthday 21st June 1985 Height 5'5' Weight 123 Biography Leah is from a wealthy family but from a young age realised that money is not the key to happiness. Happiness cannot be bought, it does not come with a price tag. At a tender age of 6 her world came crashing down around her when her mother left for a new life rejecting both Leah and her father. Growing up in a world with no mother and in the knowledge that she has never been loved by her absent mother Leah grew a tough skin. Having attended boarding school for most of her life and when home for holidays she rarely saw her father only his plastic cards and the nanny he employed to look after her. She lived the life many young girl would dream of but never experienced any real sense of belonging or happiness. That was until she found wrestling. It proved to be her sanctuary in life, it gave her the environment to succeed where her wealth and social status meant nothing. After testing the waters previously in Tradition Wrestling, she resigned after certain events caused irreconsilable problems between her and certain members of the roster, of which she has never wanted to talk about. After a rough summer which ended with the suspisious death of her father, of which Leah was a suspect to the murder she has picked herself up from the floor and is now not only a student of ruthless businessman and killer Martin Smalls but a member of FUSE wrestling.
