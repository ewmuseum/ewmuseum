Syn Biography Real Name Cameron James Gruner Nicknames C.J., CJ Starr, Synplicity Hometown Atlanta, GA Birthday 5/11/1970 Height 6'2' Weight 238 Biography Before Syn Was Born: Syn, born Cameron James Gruner, was born and raised into Wrestling by Billy Starr as a young boy he always was behind the stage learning all he could and he was attracted to all the people he met. His fixation became even bigger than could be expected because at the age of thirteen Billy Starr home schooled young Cameron James as he refused to go to school and would ditch to help out in a local wrestling promotion. Billy soon learned he wasn't going to talk young Cameron out of wrestling and decided he would take him on tour with him as he taught him in between matches. When he wasn't learning school work he was learning the backstage development of a wrestling promotion as he started at the bottom setting up and breaking down the ring as a member of the ring crew. His father made him stay at that job for over a year. After that he was given another difficult job he was a gopher for all the bookers in his fathers promotions but during that time he did learn the tips and tricks to booking a decent show. After that his work was finally beginning to pay off as he was made a Junior Assistant booker and got to book one segment per show very respectable for someone his age. The Beginning of Syn By the age of fifteen he began practicing in the ring on wrestling moves with a few nice wrestlers willing to give up of their time. But he continued to become a booker and then a head booker but now he was sixteen he knew a lot of how the Wrestling business worked but he wanted to be in front of the cameras but his father was then promoted to owner of a small company called Juggalo ChampionshXt Wrestling Syn began training under his father Billy Starr and best friend Scotty Raven. But he wasn't the only one training he was training with another kid who showed a lot of promise called Randy Ramon. During Syn's training he had a short stint wrestling in his fathers promotion when they saw they were short of heels (Bad Guys in Wrestling Language) when he saw his son had a knack for getting heat (being hated) from the fans. The Midnight Ride However, shortly after he and Randy graduated and began their careers for real in Midnight Championship Wrestling. Randy and Syn were trained by two famous wrestlers who were part of a major stable called La Familia which enjoyed having fun even at someone else's expense once and a while. Randy and Syn came up with Rockstar Revolution as their counter to La Familia and its goals were to have more fun then also to give others the same opportunity they had to be trained by the best and to get them over. Which was a huge success. During Midnight Championship Wrestling Syn also joined Great Wrestling Action where he got a bit of a reputation of being a bad ass while Randy rode the MCW circut until it closed it's doors weeks after Syn extended an invite to Randy and he joined Great Wrestling Action while Syn taught him the ropes of GWA. Calling out Juggalo Xtreme At this time Great Wrestling Action was still brand spanking new it was only the second Action Zone Live show Syn made his debut on as a guest of Mitchell Helle. At the time GWA was a training camp or a Development Fed (if you perfer) for Phenomenal Championship Wrestling in which Juggalo Xtreme was staff of. Mitch, at that point, was sick of being second banana to a failing fed and his reasons for bringing in Syn was to stir the pot, which he did he came out and called PCW and especially Juggalo Xtreme a pussy. This led to a long feud which is one of the things that gave Syn his reputation in GWA. This rivalry pretty much spoke the ending of GWA as a development fed and beginning as a full time fed. Finally Juggalo Xtreme and Syn patched things up and that would lead to future things. The Dark Carnival During his days in JCW one of the wrestlers who gave him the time of day was Juggalo Xtreme, he also was the co-owner of Midnight Championship Wrestling that gave Randy and Syn their big break. After Syn was done helping Randy get acquainted with GWA he chose to follow Juggalo Xtreme into the Dark Carnival, this is where he first began to get a reputation of an egomaniac. Because he was on a hot streak no one in GWA seemed able to stop. During his time in Dark Carnival Syn had his favorite feud in his career. Jeff Hardkore Jeff Hardkore handed Syn his first two losses in GWA and by no shock Syn wanted revenge but in the meantime he won a Money In the Briefcase shot for the Action Zone Live Title then defeating Hardkore, and others on January 22nd at Brawl 4 It All Syn shocked the world not only winning the Action Zone Live title, but by screwing over a Revolution X staple Jeff Hardkore and shocking Dark Carnival in a mega-swerve which eluded to the use of a certain drug and pissing off almost everyone in The Dark Carnival. Revolution X/SYNdicate/Syndicate X Later they would find out even though Joining Revolution X was supposed to be a swerve on Dark Carnival it turned out to be a double swerve as he showed he only used it to push himself to a world title and to running his own Stable SYNdicate which soon became Syndicate X after the breakdown of Rev. X and Diamond Stud joining Syn. The Breakdown Syn realized after doing it all winning the tag titles, winning the action zone live title being a double champion and finally world champion Syn was out of energy. Syn was defeated and had no energy left he stayed on GWA for awhile but there was no energy in his matches finally after losing to Blake Oliver he handed the title over gracefully and bowed out of GWA. He tried to make a couple returns but only being halfhearted he gave up and went on to wrestle in CWX. CWX/Indy Syn did a shoot interview on GWA while he was off on his own working only one-match Independent matches. The Enforcer read in his shoot his ultimate dream match would be against Randy Ramon who was brought up and trained with Syn. The Enforcer knew he could book the match so Syn decided to join CWX. Syn lost the match but began shooting on PWR-1 Superstar Outer Limits behind the back. Word got to Biss. and he and Forcer gave Syn a strong warning and handed Syn a cease and desist order from mentioning Outer Limits this provoked Syn to doing a shoot on CWX's taped Internet show which never made air on CWX but several other media outlets picked it up and ran with the story. When Syn then tried to shoot about Outer Limits on a live show Enforcer came out. It lead to heated words being exchanged and as security tried to escort Syn out on Enforcers word Syn broke free and gave Enforcer a beating of a lifetime which lead to a 30 day suspension. During that 30 days Syn was contacted several times about returning to GWA since the promotion would be closing in a month and to go out on a high note. Syn returns to GWA Syn is now back in Great Wrestling Action which is no doubt the place that gave birth to Syn. Since finding out CWX will be closing Syn has assured everyone when GWA ends so does Syn. Cameron James wants a normal life and to ditch his Syn persona but while in GWA he plans to give Syn one last ride to remember. Syn signs a temporary 1 year contract agreeing to be on all 12 PPV's and no less than 12 no more than 20 Action Zone Live shows. Syn is happy with this schedule as it gives him a lot of personal time. Personal Time Syn has not given reason for his disappearance even being called dead by his publicist Syn had the world believe that he had left them behind while four months went by. Why was this time spent in shadows? Syn's Return One day out of the blue Syn returned to Great Wrestling Action claiming he was here to take the promotion back from Juggalo Xtreme when Juggalo Xtreme refused to give the promotion back Mitch Helle bought out the near dead promotion and Juggalo Xtreme's share of the company at a extremely reduced price in which it was sold for. Mitch Helle immediately named Syn as the General Manager and Acting Director of GWA. Since then he has returned with a different look, persona and feel in many ways he has returned and now that he is back will we find out what happened during his mysterious absence? Other Feds GWA, EPW, PWR-1, DHW Other Fed Awards Too Many to List.
