Jake Jennings Biography Real Name Jacob Jennings Nicknames 'The Austin Outlaw', 'Big Jake' Hometown Austin, Texas Birthday June 26, 1978 Height 6'3' Weight 251 Fed Titles N/a Fed Awards N/a Biography Will fill this in as soon as I get time after all the rest of the bio is up to date the way I want it. {06/21/06} Also to be added here shortly a character and entrance description. {06/23/06} Other Feds (None as this character) PRIME, Core, AW, NEW, XGWO Other Fed Awards (None as this character) Core Legacy Champion, Classic Champion, and Star Search Runner-Up, NEW World Champion, Regional Champion, AW World Champion and Crossfire Rumble Winner, XWC Tag Team Champion (2x), XGWO Hall of Famer, World Champion, IC Champion, Wrestling Style Well rounded Brawler Finisher Description DDT just like Jake Roberts used to do it. Just as effective and awesome as when he did it as well. Setup Description A running bulldog Other Moves Below you'll find a sample list of basic moves, his signature moves, a match writers AID, and some general things that Jake would and wouldn't do in the ring. You should be able to get a general feel for what kind of guy and wrestler he is by reading this once it's completely finished. Signature Moves: [1] Side Russian Leg sweep {Often done into things like the announce table, guard rail, steel steps, the ramp way, etc. This adds more effect to their heads bouncing off of something and helps aid his finisher being more effective as such. [2] Short Arm Clothesline [3] Running neckbreaker drop [4] Exploder Suplex (T-bone){Great for creating false finishes} [5] Drop toe hold Some basic moves: Brawling and Strikes: Punches, kicking downed opponents, elbows, forearms, headbutts, sharp knife edge chops, knees, etc. Pretty much any type of strike you can think a person may come up with in a bar room brawl you'll know that 'Big Jake' can and has done it. These are his bread and butter. Slams and Drops: Scoop slam, pumphandle slam, back body drop, Atomic drop, Inverted Atomic Drop, Military press and drops opponent flat on their face, Samoan drop, pumphandle drop, facebuster, side slam, powerbomb, stomach cruncher, flap jack type of moving throwing the opponent up into the air (on the rebound of them off the ropes) and dropping them face first to the mat, etc. Pretty much anything you can think of that would fit his size and such, and depending on the size and strength of his opponent, you could find Jake using. Suplex: Double underhook suplex, vertical suplex, reverse vertical, slingshot suplex, snap suplex, german suplex, belly-to-belly and release belly-to-belly, and pretty much all other variations you can think of. Jake is a damn good with the suplex moves. He knows a great deal of them and he uses them very effectively. Outside of his brawling techniques this is most likely his most effective and most extensive offense. Submissions: Figure four, boston crab, dragon sleeper, grapevine leg lock, abdominal stretch and some other various regular type of submissions. They are effective cause of his size and girth but they aren't a specialty of his and he doesn't use them all the time. Aerial Moves: Nothing outside of maybe a elbow or leg drop off the second rope. You simply won't find Jake flying around doing spectacular aerial moves as it's just not part of his nature. He views flying moves as more showboating than anything else and he is more of a brawling good old boy who loves to just fight. Throws and Tosses: Hip toss, arm drags of several variations, atomic throw (instead of dropping them onto his knee he picks them up and throws them forward), monkey flip, catapult throw, slingshot throw, etc. Most of the time these type of things will come out of reversing an opponents move or their momentum. Breakers and Drivers: Swinging neckbreaker, Piledriver, backbreaker, pendulum backbreaker, double underhook backbreaker, shoulder breaker, shin breaker, rib breaker, jumping arm breaker, etc. A good amount of his offense can be found in the form of these moves cause they are easy for a man of his size and strength to perform on practically a person of any size and they are very effective as well. Various other moves: Clothesline, tackle or takedown, fist drop to forehead of downed opponent, knee lift, Electric Chair drop (for opponents of smaller to mid range siz, etc. MATCH WRITERS AID!!!!
