White Tiger Biography Real Name Ms. Tabitha White. Nicknames (The) White Tiger, the Maltese Falcon. Hometown Warwickshire, England/Valletta, Malta. Birthday Product of the eighties. Height Hovers just over 6 feet. Weight 166 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography That would be telling... Other Feds Numerous small promotions in the UK, Germany, and Italy. Short tours of Japan and Australia. Other Fed Awards Some shiny things... but none of them could spin :( Wrestling Style High-impact evade and counter. Finisher Move Tilt-a-Whirl Crossface Finisher Description What it says Finisher Setup Move Trinity Setup Description With opponent in the corner, she springs up off the middle rope and hits three kicks to the left shoulder while at her jump's peak Other Moves - Gymnast’s flexibility - Anything that will target the left arm and shoulder - Kicks for all occasions - Forearm strikes/punches/elbows (speed over power) - Code Red (flipping sunset) - Sandbagging submissions (hanging triangle, armbar, etc.) - Quick, 5-second arm submissions in the ropes - Anything up to a very rare 630 or inverted 450 from the top to an opponent on the mat - Running dropkick into the corner (opponent standing, slumped or seated) - Slingshot attacks from the ring to the floor Entrance Music 'Our Truth' by Lacuna Coil (Short-FUSE mix)
