Killzone 19 Reno Events Center — Reno, Nevada ---- This is your life The Custom Chaos title belt The current holder: Evan Jackson. That very man walked into his locker room moments ago expecting a mess. After all, just a few seconds ago he had been told someone had been spotted entering his locker room. He dropped the title belt to the floor though as he realised just what had happened. Opening the door had flipped a switch. A switch which had triggered a countdown. 10 9 8 7 6 5 it counted down quickly. Evan Jackson hardly had time to bring his hands up to his face before it hit 0. Jackson braced himself for the force of the explosion.... But it never came. Instead the TV on the other side of the room switched on. In the middle of the screen was a figure dressed all in black. His face obscured by a mask. The figure waved at him before disappearing and being replaced by an image of Evan Jackson. Not an old one either. Quite a recent one... A live picture in fact... Evan Jackson looked around the room, all the while keeping one eye on the TV in the corner. Finally he was able to spot the location of the hidden camera. He rushed over to it and ripped it from it's hiding place which was behind a chair. Evan looked up at the TV just in time to see a masked man over his shoulder. He turned into a right hand from the stranger sending him back into his locker. Jackson lunged forwards aiming to hit the man with the camera but the masked man dodged it before back elbowing Jackson in the kidney. Evan dropped to all fours and gasped for air on the ground. Well, all the breaths he could get before the metal lockers were tipped over onto the body of the custom chaos champion. The masked Strider simply turned and walked out of the room as Jackson struggled to move under the weight of the metal. ---- Have a nice trip...
