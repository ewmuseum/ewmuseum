El Diablo at WIW for Live Wire Title Biography In 2001 a trio of wrestlers and long time friends came together and formed a long over due partnership that took the wrestling world by storm. These men...The �Foundation� Tony Pride, Eugene �High Society� Robinson, and �Incredible� Idol Austin. Together, they were the Dynasty. And together, they were one of the most technically sound and arrogant bunch of assholes the wrestling world had seen in quite some time. The Dynasty was not only about dominating the rings of today while spitting on the names of yesteryear, but they were about changing the sport and building an army like group that would dominate the business for years to come. So, Pride/Robinson/Austin formed several training camps in Beverly Hills, California...Dallas, Texas...and Minneapolis, Minnesota (Minnesota being the first, the forming ground of the original crew). It was here they began to train a brand new breed of stars that they hoped would carry on their names and beliefs...their names of course, the Young Dynasty. After Idol Austin retired in late 2002 he became the head trainer in California and it was here he had the idea to put together a team of the two most promising young students from all three camps and train them to be first graduates who would officially carry on the Dynasty name and be unleashed on the wrestling world as his prot�g�s. The men he chose? Johnny Malibu and Jason Cruise. These two young athletes were not only tremendous athletes but much like Idol and Eugene, they were long time best friends. At the time they wrestled under the name of the Armani Exchange but Idol had plans to call his project team the Second Dynasty after coming to an agreement with EMWC owner Chris Blue for an exclusive contract for his top two students. Malibu and Cruise were months away from their big debut until things began to fall apart. Though they were best friends, Malibu and Cruise were notoriously known for their tempers and brash attitudes. Both men were always extremely cocky, but it was ignored more times then not because they were work machines in and out of the ring. However, they never really saw eye to eye on what wrestling meant to each other. Cruise believed in the business and still does to this day. He may be arrogant, but he was always respectful and thankful for the opportunities he was given. Malibu however was a loose cannon who took everything for granted and eventually it drove the two men apart. Weeks before they were set to debut, Malibu and Cruise came to blows and had such a backstage eruption at a local house show that it resulted in Idol Austin revoking their contracts and granting them to fellow camp mates Damien Trent and Jarod Zemoch, the other front running team for the contract. Both men were suspended from the camp but fortunately for Johnny, he was instantly signed by two large, up-coming promotions. The newly formed Five Star Wrestling and the returning International Wrestling Enterprises. Malibu was rumored to be the backbone and true talent of the team and after he signed a hefty contract and left the young Dynasty camp, Cruise was left without a partner and with a group of trainers who had lost faith in him for dropping the ball. Cruise felt determined to prove he was the rightful heir to the Dynasty throne and the true talent of the Armani Exchange so he left California and has now found himself signed over to Second City Elite wrestling, hoping to get a chance to prove he has what it takes to succeed as a major player in this business. He did just that. Upon entering SCE Cruise was placed in an 8 man two night tournament to crown the the inaugural Heavyweight Champion and he would dominate the compeition, eventually defeating Tommy Ganz in the finals and winning his first major singles title. However the company would hit hard times and fold due to lack of financial stability and Cruise would find himself in search for a promotion. It was now that he would be reunited with former teammate Johnny F'n Malibu. Cruise got a call from Wallace Storm, director and founder of NEO Pro and NEO International. He had heard about Cruise through the wrestling grapevine and after viewing some of his tapes he was instantly sold on the young star. He didn't learn till after signing Cruise of his history with Malibu and with a new found trios division on the rise in this Canadian based promotion, it was only a matter of seconds before he could get two of the most exciting and athletic stars on the rise paired back together in hopes to interject a big heaping of life into this new tag scene. He paired the two up with a very talented wrestler who wasn't much of a talker, Alexander Kinzo, and the three instantly meshed in the ring. Malibu with his flamboyant and high flying antics, Cruise with his lethal kicks and submissions, and Kinzo who had become a suplex machine of sorts. The sky was the limit for the three as they stormed over the division, captured the titles, and held onto them for a ridiculous amount of time. Eventually the company would go on hiatus before the three could lose the titles and again Cruise would find himself in search of a home. Enter FURY-Pro. Mixed Martial Arts had become the latest craze and this Tokyo based promotion was in the dead center of it. Trying to draw a mainstream audience the founder Jake Morgan went out of his way to recruit a hot young wrestler who he felt could not compete in the MMA style of combat but succeed and he found that competitor in Jason Cruise. With his professional and amateur wrestling background along with extensive Muay Thai training Cruise fully leaped into this new world head on. He didn't take off into some an insane success story but Cruise battled his way through FURY for about 8 months, adding several noteworthy victories to his resume while only suffer a pair of defeats. Cruise, while loving this new style of competition, eventually grew to miss the States and eventually left FURY on good terms. It was then that he would completely disappear from the fighting scene and spotlight all together for nearly a year and a half before 'returning from the dead' and showing up here in FUSE.
