Mikael Lundgard Biography Real Name Mikael Lundgard Nicknames 'The Danish Demon' Hometown Copenhagen, Denmark Birthday 12/19/1980 Height 6' 3' Weight 251 Fed Titles FUSE Livewire Champion (01.21.06 - 04.08.06), FUSE Universal Champion (04.01.06 - Present) Fed Awards Spotlight x2 Biography Mikael Lundgard was born in Copenhagen, Denmark. He grew up and had a perfectly normal childhood. He finished schooling with good grades, he primarilly excelled in Physichal Education coming top of his class. Instead of going onto college or University Mikael decided on furthering his love for all things physical by attending Wrestling college. He trained and by the age of 18 was traveling across euope learning many wrestling styles including British, German and Russian. Early 1999 - Mikael started off in Denamrk in the DMW - Danish Mainland Wrestling. He quickly rose to the top and picked up the DMW title. He then dropped that title before going to the UK. Late 2000 - He went over to Britain and to the BWC where he won the Heavyweight title. He then left for Mexico soon after. Mid 2001 - While in MGW he won the MWA 2001 gauntlet, He then returned to DWM for his second run. 2002 - He won the DMW title a second time and stayed around for a while holding the title for a total of 11 months before going to Britain for a few months and then heading off to Japan. 2004 - Mikael won the ironman title in his debut in JPW. He quickly riose to the top and won the heavyweight title. 2005 - Mikael returned to DMW for the final time and won the title in shady circumstances. This made the third title run he had and finally Mikael headed over to America Mikael believes he is the best and has absolutely no problems backing it up. From the short time he has spent in America he has already grown to dislike it. He claims the people are 'too fake' and they don't appreciate what they have. He holds nothing but contempt for the American way of life. He claims Americans are impatient and are never willing to wait for anything, be it a title opportunity in a wrestling organization or simply a cup of coffee at the stall on the way to work.
