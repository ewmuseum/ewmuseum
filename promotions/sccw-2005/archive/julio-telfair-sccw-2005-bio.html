Julio Telfair Biography Real Name Julio Telfair Nicknames The Chosen Apotheosis Hometown New Orleans, Louisiana Birthday March 25, 1989 Height 6'0 Weight 192 Fed Titles N/a Fed Awards N/a Biography Julio Telfair was born in the gutter of New Orleans, Louisiana. He was one of very few fortunate enough to keep his father, Elijah, and his mother, Sonny. Julio was considerably ludic as a child, always doing something to keep active. However, he, like so many others in the area, suffered from extreme poverty. The most popular way of earning money in his neighborhood was selling narcotics on the streets to teenagers and old burnouts. He grew up in the Ninth Ward neighborhood, which consists of 98% African American residents and a third of them live in poverty. However horrible the conditions, the government ignored the situation and it hasn't recovered to this day. Elijah took it upon himself to assure Julio a safe and productive childhood. At the young age of five, Julio was entered into his first Martial Arts class. He spent the majority of his early childhood in school, or in the gym with his father, the instructor. Elijah taught him at an early age to learn to defend himself with his feet, fists, knees, and elbows... instead of the typical guns used by most teenagers and young adults. Growing up, it was difficult for Julio to establish a permanent group of friends. As soon as he was close with someone, they would get hooked on drugs, move away, or get caught in a crossfire. That's just how things were. The situation had caused a fire to build deep down inside of Julio, and he knew at the age of 14 what he must do. All around the Ninth Ward neighborhood, Julio earned respect and acknowledgement for his classy tactics. Many times as a teen he was forced to defend himself in the streets of New Orleans, and each time, he would have to prove someone wrong, or disarm them of a potential deadly weapon. He had the desire to survive... to survive and make something of himself, just to prove that it could be done. At 14, he began going to school from 7-2, and then training in the gym with his father from 2:30-10:00. He was as disciplined as they came, and as talented as they came. He had the wit, the desire, the skill, and the art to do it all. Word spread about Julio becoming extremely talented and mastering the art of Kakukenbo. He mastered the areas of striking and alluding first, and then grappling. As the word spread, he developed a bit of a fan-base... maybe not even that, just supporters. Supporting him to get out of the Ninth Ward neighborhood and make something of himself. As a blackbelt in the art, he now focuses on his strength and speed, and keeps his skills toned. At the young age of 18, he left the Ninth Ward District to express his talent in the world of wrestling. Other Feds Loh, Cwf, many others. Other Fed Awards Many.
