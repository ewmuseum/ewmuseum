Xin Xiang Biography Real Name Xin Yu Xiang Hometown Brooklyn, NY Birthday Sept 18, 1983 Height 5'11 Weight 177 of twisted steel and sex appeal Biography A decadent marriage of one kid’s pure dream and the most gruesome experience of a young man’s life. Wrestling Style Glam Hard Style Finisher Move See Movelist Finisher Description See Movelist Other Moves Stiff Punches, Elbows, Kobashi-esque Rapid Fire Chops, Stiff Hashimoto-esque Kicks, Spike Brainbuster, Super Brainbuster. Dangerous Backdrop, Dropkick, Stalling Powerbomb, Running European Uppercut, Elbow Drops grounded and top rope, Rolling Clothesline, Jawbreaker, T-Bone Suplex, Release German Suplex, Cobra Clutch Suplex, Facewash, Slamming the opponents head into the turnbuckle until there’s blood, Tornado DDT, Dangerous DDT, Double Arm DDT, Backbreaker, Spinning Backbreaker, Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker, Front Facelock Slam, Swinging Neckbreaker and a Samoan Drop. Alt. Finishers: Vampire Spike – Applies an inverted face lock, before striking the victims heart with a pounding fist (in stabbing motion) and dropping into an inverted DDT in one fluid motion. I-Ryoku MAX – Pins down victim’s legs by standing on their calf’s, he then locks in a chinlock (like that of a Camel Clutch) before leaning back with his knee’s dug into the victims back. This hold puts pressure on most of the victim’s the spinal cord. This move can also be executed with an inverted face lock for a Dragon Sleeper look. Wave of Decadence – Grabs fallen victim's legs locking in a figure four before applying a face lock and falling backward releasing victim's legs into an Implant DDT.
