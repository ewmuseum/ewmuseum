Lori Equinox Biography Real Name Lori Equinox Hometown Unknown Birthday 24/07/1980 Height 6'2' Weight 235 Biography Lori has a condition known as Dissociative Identity Disorder, formerly known as multiple personality disorder. This means that he has multiple mannerisms... Born into a violent and harsh environment, it took it's toll on Equinox mentally as he developed a new identity just to forget the painful memories of his childhood. His father, an evil man in his own right would torment Lori's mother day and night, she was essentially his slave. When his father eventually walked out on the relationship the final nail in the coffin was landed as his mother told him that his father wasn't actually his biological relative. His uncle was. He would leave home, time to leave the bad memories behind. At fourteen years old though this was not an easy things to do and eventually Lori found himself hanging around the 'wrong' sort of people. Fighting, drinking, drugs, stealing...and he loved it. He loved to feel alive. In these youth ranks that lived in alleys and deralict buildings no-one spoke of why they ended up there. It was unspoken foke-lore. No-one wanted to know and no-one wanted to tell. It was the one thing they could all hold in common with each other while their differences contrasted to the extremes. It was between the ages of 15 and 16 that his 'friends' started to notice something slightly messed up with him. One moment he was happy, next he was mad, like deluded. And when he was mad he was uncontrollable. One girl, the age of 17 grew close to Lori, keeping an eye him for the rest of the group. Elifor, as she was called by the pack, was the only one who could really connect with his evil side. It was as if she could bring out the good in him at anytime. When Lori turned 17, he and Elifor took a walk down beside a lake near an abandoned warehouse. That is were Equinox was born. The two opened up to each other, telling each other what had happened in their childhoods and why they had turned to this life. When Lori told Elifor his second name, Effins then, something clicked in her head. She told him he should change his name to Equinox. At that time believing that his good and evil side where equally split in his mind. At an Equinox is an event where the sun is directly above the earth equater. Hence at the equinox the night and day are equally long... Light and Dark.
