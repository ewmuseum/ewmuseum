En Fuego Biography Real Name Pablo Villegas (not common knowledge) Nicknames The Mexican Meltdown Hometown Ciudad Victoria, Mexico Birthday 04/28/84 Height 5' 5' Weight 142 Biography Pablo Villegas grew up dirt poor in Ciudad Victoria, Mexico. The twelfth child of a family of thirteen, Pablo had a dream. Lucha Libre. When Pablo was only sixteen, he ran away from home and camped outside Juan Cortez's wrestling school. Juan has been famous in the 70's in Mexican Lucha Libre as 'El Diablo Verde' before an accident with shoddy pyro left him blind in one eye. Pablo camped outside the doors for a full week with the wrestlers ignoring him as they came to train. Finally one day, a young man noticed him and told him if he would carry his bags, he could come in the school. Little did he know the lazy luchadore was Jorge Cortez, Juan's son. Jorge convinced his father to hire Pablo to keep the school clean in exchange for letting him live there. It was no act of mercy on Jorge's part, he did it so he would no longer have to clean up. It took several months for Pablo to work up the nerve to ask Juan if he would train him to be a wrestler. The sound of the luchadores' laughter shamed Pablo, and he blushed bright red at their scorn. 'No, Pablo, you are too small, even to a be a luchadore. And besides, if the jeers of these men leaves you en fuego, you would never be able to stand up to a crowd of drunkards.' And the name stuck. No longer was he Pablo Villegas, he was En Fuego, the embarrassed little boy. For most young men, this would have been too much, and they would have ran back home. Not Pablo. He still had a dream. No matter if he was mopping up vomit from where Juan pushed one of his students to the point of nausea, or stitching together new costumes for the wrestlers, Pablo watched every training session closely. At night, after he has the school to himself, he would lift weights and practice his moves. But never again could he bring himself to ask to be trained again. It turns out he didn't have to. Juan Cortez's biggest disappointment after having his career cut short was having a son who did not take his wrestling lessons seriously. Jorge wanted the lifestyle that being a professional luchadore would bring, but thought it should be handed to him simply because he was the son of El Diablo Verde. One day Juan's legendary patience wore thin, and while he was chiding his son, he pointed at Pablo. 'I bet even En Fuego could do better than you!' Jorge was livid. 'You! En Fuego! Get in the ring, little boy! And I will show papa what Nuevo Diablo Verde can do!' It took Pablo less than two minutes to completely embarrass Jorge and pin him. From that moment on, the two young men became best of friends. Juan taught Pablo, amazed at the young man's determination and drive. Juan's presence brought out a competitive side of Jorge, who eventually earned his father's blessing to wrestle as El Diablo Verde, and the two men even formed a tag team, LOS LUCHADORES EXTRAORDINAROS!!! En Fuego got signed to come to SCCW while it was still FUSE, making his debut on the first Uproar of 2008 in Corpus Christi. He picked up a win, technically, but only because Mayhem flattened both he and his opponent and draped the unconscious En Fuego across the other man and forced the referee to count. The next show, En Fuego returned the favor, ambushing Mayhem while The Path of Destruction was already involved in a fight. It did not go well for The Mexican Meltdown. He tore a ligament in his knee and was out of action for an extended time rehabbing it. Now, in the best shape of his life, En Fuego looks to make his dream come true. Wrestling Style High Flying Lucha Libre Finisher Move The Phoenix Falls Finisher Description Springboard Swandive Splash Finisher Setup Move NA Setup Description Any move that grounds his opponent Other Moves If it is a crazy move a luchadore would use, it is in En Fuego's arsenal. He embraces the 3 Fs of Lucha Libre: Fast, Flippy and Fearless. Calling an En Fuego match will make Eugene Ware apoplectic, even if he doesn't know what that means.
