Thanatos Biography Real Name Unknown Nicknames The Human Nuclear Explosive, Lord Thanatos Hometown Billed as being from the Bermuda Triangle. Birthday Unknown. Height 6'10' Weight 353 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Thanatos has made appearances for two professional wrestling companies. One was HSW, the other FUSE. Both times, he was quickly gone from each promotion. The circumstances as to why are unknown. For wrestling fans, the man known as Thanatos is largely a rumor. His name has been associated with various rumored startups that never got off the ground, such as Maine Event Wrestling and the Pro Wrestling Network project. To some, he has become the Chinese Democracy of pro wrestlers. His hiatus, whether self-imposed or not, has come to an end with his appearance in Sin City Championship Wrestling as part of Sapientes Gladio. Should he stick around, it is entirely likely that fans will finally get the answers to the firestorm of questions swirling around the monolithic powerbomb master. First and foremost among them may be 'how in the Hell are you from the Bermuda Triangle?' Other Feds Appearances in FUSE and elsewhere Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Power and powerbomb focused Finisher Move The Black Maelstrom Finisher Description F5 Finisher Setup Move Whatever he wants. Setup Description See above. Other Moves You will not see Thanatos do an arm drag. Or a dropkick. Or a moonsault. Fuck, you won’t even see him do a chinlock. He doesn’t know a submission hold. What he DOES know, however, is how to hit really hard, and damn near drive you through the canvas with each and every power move he executes. Early match, he goes to town with hard strikes and basic power moves. You won’t see suplexes. Too technical for Thanatos’ macabre tastes. The sidewalk slam is a favorite here, as are basic backbreakers. He supplements these basic moves with powerful clubbing forearms to the back, and the occasional powerful right cross to the jaw. By the middle of the match, he likes to open up with some of his more dangerous arsenal. Spinebusters and more elaborate slams come into play, as does his big boot. He likes to set moves up off the Irish whip here mid-match. As this stage ends, he’s likely to bring out some of his low level power bombs, a sure sign that he’s building to the beginning of the end. The end of the match sees power bombs. Lots of them. By now, he doesn’t even throw strikes. A perfect end game for the big man sees him hit three or four of his high level, ring shaking power bombs, some of his other power moves, deliver the State of Hatred, and then DOI to get the win. The preliminary power bombs theoretically leave the opponent unable to counter his biggest weapons, or at least, such is his goal in the ring. Strikes: Clubbing forearms to the back and neck Right cross to the jaw Big Boot Lariat Power Moves: Sidewalk Slam Backbreaker Multiple Backbreaker Multiple Rib Breaker (Brock Lesnar style) HARD Scoop Slam Over-The-Shoulder Powerslam Advanced Power Moves (Mid/Late Match Only): Tilt-A-Whirl Powerslam Tilt-A-Whirl Sidewalk Slam Spinning Bossman Slam Inverted Gorilla Press to Backbreaker Fallaway Slam Chokeslam Samoan Drop Press Slam to Samoan Drop Release German Suplex Snap German Suplex Powerbombs (Late Match Only; uses anywhere from 1-4 a match before going for his finisher.) NOTE: Most of these can be done with the following variants: Sit-Down, Spinning, Double Underhook, Crossed Arms, Kneeling, Running, Falling Forward, etc. If his demented mind can come up with it, he’ll probably do it. Powerbomb Crucifix Powerbomb Thunderfire Powerbomb Powerbomb Into The Turnbuckle Powerbomb Onto The Ringpost (RARE) Slingshot Powerbomb (Lift for the move, Powerbomb into the ropes for momentum, then spin and Powerbomb to the canvas) Double Powerbomb Triple Powerbomb Gutwrench Powerbomb Blue Thunder Powerbomb Pumphandle Powerbomb Canadian Backbreaker Rack Powerbomb Leg-Lift Powerbomb Fireman’s Carry into Sitout Powerbomb Torture Rack into Sitout Powerbomb Two-Handed Choke Powerbomb Powerbomb Across the Knee (a favorite of his) Signature Moves: Signature Powerbombs (Late Match Only; one per match if he even uses one. All of these are relatively rare) Face-First Crucifix Powerbomb Double Powerbomb to Fall-Forward Spiral Bomb Triple Crossed-Arms Powerbomb Powerbomb Over The Top Rope To The Floor (VERY RARE) Hades‘ Hammer: Polish Hammer, delivered across the chest or directly to the face. He likes to use this multiple times in a bout. Torture Chamber: Vader-style punch combination with the opponent trapped in the corner. He’ll throw anywhere from five to ten blows, before ending the combo with a blatant chokehold, broken only just before the count of five. Finishing Moves: The Black Maelstrom - The F5, done with Thanatos' signature flair. His height makes for a longer fall for his opponent. Usually, he will set it up with a fireman's carry pickup, but against lighter opponents he will lift the opponent up as if for a fallaway slam, then toss them up to the shoulders. DOI (Death on Impact) - Perhaps the single most awesome finisher in SCCW. Thanatos simply takes the beaten and exhausted opponent to one of the corners of the ring. With his back against the turnbuckles, he lifts the opponent up to his shoulder, and then ascends the ropes, step by step. Once he stands on the top rope, he extends his arms, holding the opponent in crucifix power bomb position before leaping. In mid air, he lets the opponent fall back to his shoulder, and then simply drops them into a high impact kneeling Thunderfire Powerbomb. After executing the DOI, Thanatos will cover in his trademark style, using his left hand to pin the opponent’s far wrist to the mat, hooking the opponent’s far leg with his right arm, and bracing one of his legs between the opponent’s to make absolutely sure there is no escape from Death on Impact. It's a big match move, the sort of thing he would use to win a championship or vanquish a very persistant foe. His insistence on using it elsewhere has gotten him blackballed from corners of the wrestling world, so he's learned to play a bit nicer with it.
