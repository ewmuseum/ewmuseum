Killzone 29 National Guard Armory — Tinley Park, Illinois ---- Alarming Opening SA: Hello ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to KillZone 29, the final stop before we hit Chicago for ReVolution! I'm Scott Ambrose along side Hunter Jones ready for numerous great matches! HJ: I thought you had forgotten about me, Scott. You have to imagine that some of our superstars are going to be looking to leave here tonight with the advantage going into the biggest show of the year. SA: I agree. The shot leaves ringside and enters a dressing room. Tigera stands in the center of the room, holding her Universal Title belt over her shoulder. CUSTOM C.H.A.O.S Champion Mikael Lundgard sits on a leather sofa, filing through a magazine of some kind. Lundgard: Would you sit down? You're making me nervous. Tigera brushes the comment away, holding onto her title. She looks to be deep in thought, but about what we don't know. Suddenly, a strobe light and alarm originate from a red box mounted on the wall. Both of the champions look to each other before springing up as a response to the fire alarm. SA: What's going on? HJ: My microphone is cutting in and out... SA: Folks I'm not sure wh— The audio from the announcers ceases as Tigera and Mikael Lundgard grab their jackets, putting them on and heading for the door. Lundgard: A fire? Tigera: I guess so... The two exit the locker room, walking into a desolate hallway. The air is quiet and no movement occurs anywhere. The two look around slightly, searching for others. Lundgard: Screw it, let's get outside. Forget everyone else. Tigera: Were we like the last ones to know or something? The two hastily make their way down the hall, rounding a few corners as they head for the exit. ---- Sage Advice The shot opens up very close on Clinton Sage, who is sitting no more than a foot away from the camera. His face is emotionless, except for his eyes, which have no color other than black in the iris and pupil. Clinton is sitting so close to the camera, that the red 'status' light is reflected slightly in those dark mirrors he calls eyes. His voice is cool and collected, but commanding. Sage: I'm going to tell you this once, so you better listen. And I'm not excluding anyone from the FUSE roster...I mean absolutely everyone should heed this advice. Sage: Beating me will be the worst career move any of you could ever make. What happened last week, with Ethan Frost, will be rectified. I promise you that. And don't even think for one minute that you can pin me to the mat, and make it to the next week, let alone month, without wearing a new scar. ---- Watering FUSE Down The shot switches outside to the blustery weather of Northern Illinois. The ground is lightly sprinkled with freshly fallen snow. The undisturbed white powder gets stirred up as Tigera and Mikael Lundgard emerge from the National Guard Armory via a back exit. Luckily they grabbed their jackets as they evacuated for the fire alarm. Mikael: Damn it's cold out here. Tigera: Were we the only ones who used this exit or something? Relief comes across the faces of The Danish Demon and the Universal Champion as they spot a shiny new fire engine pull around a corner and towards their position. The lights flash wildly as the truck comes to a stop with one final wail of the siren. As soon as the airbrake sets, the doors open and six or seven men file out, all completely dressed in turn-out gear. Tigera: Maybe it's not just a false alarm. The two remaining fire fighters head off to accomplish their duties, one jumping back into the cab. After a few short seconds he re-emerges as a loud revving of the engine over-comes the temporarily silent air. As he steps from the cab, he glances over spotting Tigera and Lundgard and begins jogging over to them. Fire Fighter 2: Are you folks okay? The two champions look to each other and nod. Fire Fighter 2: Good. I'm going to have to ask you guys to step over here. We've got to attack through that entrance over there. Fire Fighter 1: It seems as though you guys are a little muddy! Allow me to clean you off! He takes the stream from the FUSE superstars, allowing them to stand before hitting them with it again, blasting the mud from their clothes and pinning them against the wall. The other fire fighters come running out of the building as the stream ceases, water dripping from the nozzle. Fire Fighter 2: All right boys, roll the hose. Two of the fire fighters slowly walk towards the two champions, slowly removing their gear. Even through the thick gear, the two are heard laughing as Tigera and Lundgard climb to their feet, shivering uncontrollably. As the first fire fighter drops his helmet, the crowd explodes as John Covel looks to his foes, smiling wildly as they try to wring the water from their attire. SA: It's John Covel! And is that?... Covel drops his jacket to the ground as the second fire fighter reveals himself as John Reed. Once again the crowd explodes as the two men look to the Danish Demon and Bangkok Tiger who stare back, anger filling up in their faces. Covel: I think the fire's out. We can head back to the station! Reed: I agree. Should we call EMS for the two victims? Covel: Nah. I think they're okay. Tigera's lip quivers in hatred as she stares at the duo in front of her. Tigera: What are you doing?! Where's that damn fire marshal at? Covel laughs and looks back to her. Covel: I'm right here. I was named honorary fire marshal for the week. I couldn't let that go to waste! Before Tigera can fire back any type of response, Covel and Reed grab their gear, still laughing at the incident. They jog lightly to the truck, which is slowly pulling away. Just as they reach the creeping truck, Covel looks back to the two rivals, slowly walking towards the building. Covel: Good idea! Go back inside and dry out, I don't want my precious Tigera getting pneumonia! Reed laughs as he tosses his gear into the truck as it stops, allowing the two to hop on. Tigera and Lundgard stop their walk and look to the number one contender and his mentor reaching into a small cooler. Reed: All that hard work has made me thirsty!
