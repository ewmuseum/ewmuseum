X Spot Biography Real Name Ricky Caldwell Nicknames 'The Greatest Show on Earth' Hometown Detriot, Michigan Birthday 6/13/1985 Height 6'4' Weight 240 lbs Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Many people know this man from XcW and what a great wrestler he was. Since he left XcW he hasn't been heard from since and has took a lot of anger and stored it away. He has kept the same thing about himself but now is being led by a familar face himself. Who knows who that man is but all we know is that 'The Greatest Show on Earth' is back and better then ever. FUSE better be ready for a war as this isn't the same X-Spot.
