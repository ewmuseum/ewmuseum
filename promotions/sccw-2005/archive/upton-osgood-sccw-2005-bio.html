Upton Osgood Biography Real Name Upton Osgood Nicknames 'Ozzy'; 'Finger Freak'; 'Upchuck'; 'Two Ton' Hometown Bangor, Maine Birthday July 7, 1977 Weight 130 kg (286 lbs) Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography All that is known about Upton Osgood is that he was born on July 7, 1977, and debuted in the world of professional wrestling on September 9, 1999. Other Feds I can't remember all of them Other Fed Awards Yeah... Wrestling Style Upton's style comprises of three of his favorite styles: European grappling, Lucha Libre and Puroresu. Finisher Move Bangor Spike Finisher Description Variation of the Michinoku Driver II. Upton lifts his opponent over his right shoulder, as if to do a Power Slam. This chokes the opponent, setting them up for the Bangor Spike. Other Moves Body Slam Power Slam Fall Away Slam Side Slam Vertical Suplex Backdrop Suplex German Suplex DDT Power Bomb Fireman's Carry Takedown Samoan Drop Curb Stomp European Uppercut - likes to use it a lot. Submissions STF Waki-Gatame Arm-bar Juji-Gatame Arm-bar Ashi-Gatame Arm-bar Shini no Maki – in other words, it’s the Cobra Clutch. Kata Hajime – it’s Taz’s Judo choke finisher. It’s illegal in Judo competitions. Octopus Stretch – Upton usually likes to do the grounded one. Favorites Shining Yakuza Kick - a Flying Yakuza Kick. You'll see wrestlers like Chono Masahiro and Samoa Joe use it. IKAKU Knee Attack - this move was taught to him by Musashi Ikaku, or better known as IKAKU, the first Japanese wrestler to fully capitalize his name. Upton charges - or bounces off the ropes - at his opponent, hops in the air, spins 360 to his left, and cracks his opponent in the mouth with his right knee. It’s a dazzling move for the fans to witness, and extremely painful for his opponent to experience. Hammer of Oz – Upton’s variation of the Burning Hammer Cartwheel Moon Sault - Upton bounces off the ropes, does a cartwheel in the center of the ring, leaps in the air, twists, and hits his opponent with a big time Moon Sault onto the outside. This is an outside move only, and it gets some big pops. LARIATO! - Need I explain? It’s a Lariat that would make Stan Hansen pleased that he originated the move. Somersault Senton Attack - just what it says. Upton bounces off the ropes, does a somersault and bowls his opponent over with it. It’s not a really exciting move but it’s effective. Somersault Plancha - Upton bounces off the ropes, leaps over the top rope with a somersault, and creams his opponent with a Plancha. This is another outside move only. Senton Back-splash - this is on a downed opponent. Upton bounces off the ropes, jumps over his opponent and lands on his back, crushing his opponent underneath. Sometimes, Upton will do the move off the top rope, but he isn't always successful when he goes for that risk. The "How To" Guide to Write Upton Osgood Upton Osgood in essence, is pretty simple to write. The beginning of the match he’ll like to grapple with his opponent, like how William Regal would go out his opponents. He’ll use leg takedowns, arm drags, hip tosses, anything to bring his opponent to the ground, and wear them out with headlocks, ground submissions, whatever you guys can think of. He does have a striking game, which is basically the essential of Strong Style strikes. He doesn’t throw punches, doesn’t really believe in punches; not even a spinning back-fist. Instead he’ll throw elbows, chops and open-palm strikes. He’s not into throwing kicks except for the Shining Yakuza Kick. Once Upton gets into the flow of the match he'll use some of his basic moves, then gradually work his way up to his favorites, and he’ll use them to beat up his opponents. When he uses his Shining Yakuza Kick here’s an idea of a spot I’d like for you to add after the move is successful. Eugene Ware: There’s nothing like a flying foot to the face to take any person down. Dave Gibson: That’s a two hundred and eight-six-pound man attached to a size fifteen wrestling boot! Finally when he's ready to put his opponent away or "go home," as the professional wrestlers like to call it, he’ll use his Puncture Wound set up to incapacitate his opponent. For the coup de grace the Bangor Spike. 1-2-3, Upton Osgood leaves the ring a happy man.
