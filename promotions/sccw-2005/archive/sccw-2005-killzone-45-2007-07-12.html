Killzone 45 FedExForum — Memphis, Tennessee ---- Jason Cruise vs El Diablo The only undercard match of the evening begins... The fans continued to file into their seats as the bell sounded for the under card match up between Jason Cruise and El Diablo. Diablo took the early advantage using his experience and speed, with a touch or two of under handed tactics, to dominate the early going of the match. Several times, much to the delight of the fans, many of whom were holding up anti-Diablo signs for his actions against a fan several weeks ago, Cruise found a way to fight back and start to gain control of the match only to have El Diablo snatch it back with a low blow, thumb to the eye, or other manner of nefarious deed. After several moments of this back and forth sway Diablo went for a roll up, with a handful of tights, but was shocked when Cruise reversed it into a pinning combination of his own, nearly getting a three count. As both men stormed to their feet El Diablo was caught by a series of Shin kicks before being hoisted onto the shoulders of Cruise and dropped, face first, into Jason's knee with his signature "Go To Sleep, Bitch!" Cruise made the cover and got the three count at the eleven minute and twenty-three second mark of the match up.
