[SCCW] ROLEPLAYING INFORMATION »»

The weekly roleplay limit for TEMPTATION!TEMPTATION! is one. Any additional role plays posted within the TEMPTATION! Roleplay Period must be clearly labeled as character development. The Roleplay Limit for SCCW on ESPN or any PPV events is two. The content of your role plays is at your discretion, and your writing will not be limited in any fashion. However, please bare in mind our judges may be turned off by your piece is it contains excessive cursing or other offensive material.

[SCCW] EVENT INFORMATION »»

The weekly event run by SCCW Wrestling is TEMPTATION!. It airs every week at 11 PM est on Cinemax. A show usually contains five live matches and 2-3 unaired undercard matches. Every so often, SCCW runs an event on ESPN, fittingly titled SCCW on ESPN. These shows contain no undercard matches and generally five live matches of higher priority than a normal TEMPTATION! lineup. Every two and a half months SCCW hosts a Pay-Per View event. These events have no undercard matches and anywhere between 6-10 live matches. The deadlines for these events are listed with their respective lineups and are subject to change.

[SCCW] CHAMPIONSHIP INFORMATION »»

SCCW Wrestling currently carries four championships.

SCCW Universal Championship: The SCCW Universal Championship is the highest goal for any competitor in SCCW. Few people have managed to get their hands on this coveted championship, which has a rich history dating back to 2001. Holding the championship means you're the best wrestler in the company, and as far as the company is concerned, the best wrestler in the world at the moment. The current title holder is Jonathan Rhine.

SCCW Gateway Championship: The SCCW Gateway championship is the second highest goal for any competitor in SCCW. If you can successfully defend this title four times you earn a shot at the Universal Championship. The current title holder is Joaquin Pierre. An interesting fact about this title, since being rebranded the Gateway Championship no one has been able to defend the title even once.

SCCW LiveWire Championship: The SCCW LiveWire Championship is for the most athletically gifted in the company. Each match is contested under LiveWire Rules (Explained Below in the Match Rules Section) and can change hands on a disqualification. It's one of the favored divisions in the company, producing a lot of the current main eventers. The current title holder is Elliott Rollins.

SCCW SIN Championships: The SCCW SIN Championships mean you're the best group of wrestlers in the country at any given time. SIN stands for Stength in Numbers and with these titles the strength is within numbers. Unlike most "tag" divisions, this division is sought out by tag teams and stables alike. Any stable holding these titles can choose any of their two stablemates to defend or compete for these titles, and the titles are considered held by all members of the stable. In this division the more friends you have, the better. The current title holders are Prince & Tony Pride.

[SCCW] MATCH RULES & INFORMATION »»

A Standard SCCW Match is contesed under standard American Wrestling rules with a 15 minute time limit. A match can end by either pinfall, submission, or disqualification at the official's consent.

Any championship match is contested under the same rules but with no time limit.

The LiveWire title is decided under LiveWire rules, which are the following; Each match is to have a 10 minute time limit. At no time may either competitor leave the ring, or forcibly remove their opponent from the ring. Doing so is an automatic Disqualification, and if the champion is DQ'd he loses the belt. Each competitor is given a limit of three rope breaks.
