Gideon Fontaine Biography Real Name Gideon Fontaine Nicknames The Osaka Assassin Hometown L.A. California and Osaka, Japan Birthday January 12, 1973 Height 6 ft 9 Weight 281 Biography Gideon is the youngest son of Alan Fontaine. Alan has been grooming his oldest son Bruce to eventually take over the family fortune. Alan had no interest in young Gideon and sent him to Japan to be educated and tutored. This would benefit Gideon in the long run but age 5 would be the start of the worst years of his life. He was also enrolled in the martial arts at the age of 5 learning Zangi-Shinginkin-Ryo (Swordmanship favored by the Samurai) and Muei Thai under the master Hiroshiro Tanimoto. Gideon became very distant and withdrawn. He was at a foreign land, struggling to learn the language, studies were tough, he had no friends, and the other students hated him. He was sparring with students that had over 2 years on him and were able to understand what master Tanimoto was saying (Excerpt #1). He devoted himself to his studies in the martial arts. At age 11 he was entered into advanced training. This training usually happens after 10 years not 6 (Excerpt #2). The advanced training was a mix of other forms of martial arts which are commonly faced such as Kick Boxing, Boxing, Jiu Jitsu, Judo and even Shoot Wrestling. He was also taught Shotokan Karate to add to his impressive skill in Muei Thai and Zanji-Shinjinkin-Ryo. He completed the Japanese equivalent of grade 9 at the age of 12. He was given 2 years to himself and trained nearly non stop in a very strict training regiment to gain muscle mass and more speed. After the 2 years Gideon was enrolled in Matsuhiro High school. He was confident and had a very good grasp of the language and now spoke it as fluently as any native. At age 14 he was at the same age as the other students and joined several sports teams. He was a legend in basketball. He could sink a 3 pointer from half court. His foul shot percentage was a unbelievable 97.674%. His 3 point percentage was at 89.36% and many were from half court. He was good at baseball as well leading the league in hits and homeruns. This was the time he earned his nickname the G-Man (Excerpt #3). He would keep the nickname through his adult life. When he graduated at age 17 he returned back to America and his Sensei came with him to L.A. He trained as strictly as ever and took a wide variety of classes at L.A. University. He took up Law and Psychology. He was required to learn Business related skills as well focusing on Computer Operation + Programming as well. He had nearly no free time to pursue hockey, basketball and baseball. After he completed the required 3 years of programs he now had the necessary free time to go for his own goals. He was able to join the sports teams now. He was popular with the ladies before and now he was a sports star and had gotten a little bit of fame. This would not be without a price though. A rival Team confronted Gideon outside a local bar and threatened him. Gideon replied to the 6 of them. 'I don't want any trouble. I have completed 14 years of training in Zangi-Shinjnkin-Ryo and Muei Thai KickBoxing I have been trained in Shotokan Karate from age 11. Any confrontation could result in serious injury'. Gideon was right (Excerpt #4). He was taken to jail but was freed in only 7 hours by his father. He was forbidden to 'go clubbing' or he would be disowned. This didn't bother him much but he still needed the money to pay for his education so he obeyed. The next 2 years were long and Gideon was isolated from any contact outside of the University. He took an interest in University football and hockey and followed both. He now had a degree in corporate and contract Law. He was 20 when he went to Vegas and became a Kick Boxer to earn his own way. He was told that if he did he would be cut off from the family fortune. The shouting was so intense by Alan that he suffered a fatal heart attack. He was not allowed to go to the funeral and was even given legal notice by the family that he was not to go within 100 ft of the funeral (Excerpt #5). Gideon didn't go to the funeral despite Tanimoto's urgings. Tanimoto helped Gideon through this emotional ordeal. Tanimoto disapproved of the KickBoxing career but Gideon explained that it would give him a chance to test himself against World class opponents. Since the matches are televised he could see himself in action and improve his style from an observer's point of view. His first 3 matches ended at 54 seconds, 33 seconds, and 1 minute 27 seconds all in the first round (Excerpt #6). Tanimoto was a 'trainer' for Gideon and was disappointed by his showboating for the fans. He considered this disrespectful and mocked the seriousness that unarmed combat is supposed to have. In his rookie year he had a record of 9-0 with 9 KOs. He won the WKBA championship on August 12 after only 2 years as a fighter. He defended the belt remaining undefeated for 6 years. With a record of 39-0 with 36 KOs he was to defend the title against Alexi Stradvich 'The Russian Tank' a man who was termed unbeatable. No one has even hurt the man in over 17 years of fighting. The controversial end was a biased decision, which cost Gideon his title (Excerpt #7). Gideon was bitter and depressed. He had a lot of money from the fights invested in stocks and local businesses. He now had a substantial amount of money to work with. He was now 23 and had no direction to go. Tanimoto decided that Gideon was ready to go to Thailand to compete in the Pit Fights and the secret Kumate. He used the name Frank Dux as an alias, which worked until he met Neil Whittaker (Fracas). Neil is a fan of Kickboxing and recognized Gideon easily despite the short hair and the new hair color. The 2 talked for hours about their reasons for being for being there. Gideon used the Kumate as a test and Neil was there for competition. Gideon talked about the upcoming Kumate and how it would be for his Sensei Tanimoto. Neil said he was in it for the challenge. Neil was eliminated in the quarterfinals. He cheered on Gideon who won the tournament and the ceremonial Katana (Excerpt #8). The Katana is a sacred weapon to the Japanese it is a symbol of honor and is generally reserved for the Samurai caste. Gideon was the first Gaijin (non-Japanese person) to win the Kumate in over 200 years. He offered the sword to his sensei Tanimoto but he would not accept it. 'You have proven your skill and earned it. To accept the sword would insult the effort and skill you used to win the sword. You are the only non-Japanese person who deserves the honor of the Katana sword. I will get a Wakizashi (Samurai short sword) to match your sword and you will be a true Samurai with the Daisho (The paired weapons of Katana+Wakizashi this is reserved only for the Samurai caste) to prove it.' Gideon bowed overcome by emotion at his master's words. Gideon continued to compete for the 2 year period. his record was 94-0 81KOs. He became friends with Neil and left when he did for the States undefeated. They have always kept in touch since then and talk regularly. Gideon was now 25. He was home for less then a year when his brother was killed in a skiing accident leaving him the heir of the family fortune. He had the family lawyer's help to manage his family's estate. Things were about to change he was offered the role of Tyler Stone in Showdown in Tokyo. This would start his movie career. In this movie he was the leader of a western mob trying to take over Tokyo. This was a great role for Gideon he could speak fluent Japanese and had the 'look' of a Mafia hitman (Excerpt #9). This is when he started wearing the Black suit and tie look regularly. The movie generated huge box office sales in both Japan and North America. He was to star in several other action roles and get recognition as the top action draw in years. People considered him a fake martial artist which made him and Tanimoto very annoyed by the press. Gideon could do his own stunts and dub his own dialogue in Japanese or English. He starred in Sudden Impact, The Dragon, Blood Sport IV, and Lone Wolf. These never took off despite his efforts. They were poorly made movies designed to use Gideon as a draw to sell tickets. It caused him to lose credibility and marketing draw. Tanimoto sensing that Gideon was growing soft and losing his edge decided to push him towards getting his WKBA belt back. Gideon didn't want to go back until Neil started pushing the idea too (Excerpt #10). After several years in Hollywood and a long layoff Gideon was 27 and started his KickBoxing career again. He signed himself to fight 2 fights in one night against different opponents (Excerpt #11). The competors were high quality but Gideon beat them in under a minute each. This showed that he was back and sharper then ever. This also should have set up the obvious rematch between him and the current champ Stradvich. Gideon was being attacked by the press and couldn't get the fan support to force the rematch to be signed. Gideon fought more matches and ended his career with a record of 43-1 39KOs. He was now 28 and not doing much but managing his family holdings and a little bouncing in New York for fun. That is when Neil called him and asked him to manage his career in UJW. That is when the G-Man was started his wrestling career. 3 days after his 29th birthday he debuted in UJW against Kevin Nash the WCW and WWF Tag and World champ. Gideon beat him in record time and a star was born. He has won 11 straight 1 on 1 matches and all but 2 were by submission victory. At the time he left he was the Web champ and had a very impressive singles record with only one blemish. The blemish was the 4-man elimination match for the HC title. Beast booked himself that night to face the injured Trash, Badd Ass, and Gideon. All 3 of the opponents were much less then 100% and fell to the fresh Beast and the Death submission hold. If Badd Ass and Gideon hadn't been 3 days from their grueling death match and Trash wasn't recovering from injuries things could have been different. Wrestling fans still speculate on what may have been. The good thing was that his friend Neil would later get the belt from Beast and become a hardcore legend. During this time Neil and Gideon were part of opposing stables. Gideon was part of a stable called the OutKasts. The name of the Alliance came from the fact that they were an 'Alliance' of the 2 greatest stables in the UJW at the time. Before leaving Gideon was asked to job the Web title to a promising young star Mad Dog. Since his contract expired Gideon wasn't required to fight. Gideon agreed under the following stipulations that it would be non-title. Gideon argued that Methos could still have a Web title match but it wouldn't be for the same belt he had. Neither Methos or Gideon was satisfied with the final decision but it was something that they could agree on. Gideon's plans in the LWA are unknown but it has become public that he wants the LWA Web title to match the one he brought over from UJW. This is what Gideon thought of a few of the guys in the UJW. #1 'FA what can you say. He knows how to control the crowd. Few guys can provoke responses from the crowd like he can. Unfortunately he has the ethics of a weasel and the loyalty of a drowning rat.' #2 'Billy Gunn is tough. Unfortunately he has to be the stupidest idiot I've seen in 17 years' #3 'Ok I stand corrected. Obliverator is the stupidest person I've ever met. Anyone who asks to fight Neil is just plain stupid. Asking for a title match and making it no DQ is a special type of stupid that not many people can match.' #4 'Beast organized the UJW from the ground up and is a hell of a wrestler but somewhere he got what I call 'A Hogan Complex' He thought he was bigger then the world. He thought he was better and smarter then the other members of the MH. #5 'BW is phenomenal. She had the guts to go toe to toe with Neil and she did a hell of a job. She did better then Men that are over twice her size and managed to wrestle to a draw. Against Neil that is nearly as good as a win.' LWA was problems from the beginning. Beast now had a group that followed his every word called the Empire. Despite all of the interference and biased decisions he and the Ex-MH dominated their matches against the Empire. He had a match against the Empire's golden boy Fear that was lost due to Executioner and Beast attacking Gideon even breaking his finisher the Painkiller. Executioner was pinned by Gideon but had the ref restart the match after threatening the ref. Gideon won the tag belts from the Troops of Doom after Trash gave the belts to Gideon and his partner at the time Fallen Angel. Trash was a member of the Troops of Doom who had been betrayed by his fellow T.O.D. members Executioner and Fear and got revenge by using the 3 man tag rules against them (excerpt #12). Fallen Angel betrayed Gideon and other Ex-MH members Neil and Trash. This led to The Alliance taking on his former partner Fallen Angel and a partner of his choice. The Alliance got the tag belts that night giving Gideon his second tag reign without losing the belts. Gideon finally got the Web title after several swerves against him against 'Bad' Barry Horowitz and the former champ Silver King. He currently is the LWA web champ. The federation closed after the Empire left. Gideon had a lot of comments about all the stars involved. Here are some printable ones about 5 of the stars. #1 'Fat A$$ is the whiniest loser I've seen since Kurt Angle. He makes Big Show look respectable.' #2 'Fear is WAY too over hyped. Too much push and no where near the talent to use it.' #3 'Samantha Hickman there will be payback you cost us gold we will take our payback in your blood.' #4 'NEMESIS has even less loyalty then FA. This was noticeable when Gideon joined. Whan Neil was barred from competition Gideon shut the federation down with no less then 14 different lawsuit based on discrimination. Neil may have been barred but Gideon got payback for the Alliance. Neil found out that BW was in XWU and talked Gideon into signing them up there to compete. Two drinking buddies of Neil's from UJW ran the XWU Butcher and Jeremy Ray. Despite a promising start there was a lot of problems in the XWU. Butcher was murdered along with a woman named Sakura (Excerpt #13). BW put herself in an insane asylum. The police were there investigating everyone. Gideon decided he would be better off cutting and running. He and Neil left the federation to avoid the problems. After BW left Neil wasn't interested in competing and Gideon figured the sword that Talon Night had wasn't worth the effort to get. Gideon was quoted 'If you spend more times talking to the police then interviewers you are at the wrong place'. Neil called Gideon after he received a call from Beast about joining the Empire in Titan wrestling. Gideon at first laughed when he heard this figuring Neil was pulling his leg. When Gideon found out that Neil was serious he stopped laughing and listened. Gideon was called by Trash and the 2 talked for over 2 hours about the Empire (Excerpt #14). Gideon contacted Beast in Latvia shortly after. Gideon agreed with Beast and wondered how he would talk Neil in to joining the Empire. Neil was in jail during their debut in Titan Wrestling so Gideon joined the Empire on behalf of the Alliance. Gideon often had to act as a referee to prevent fights between Neil and Fear. Gideon went after the IC belt held by Red Falcon. In the first 30 seconds of the match Gideon took Falcon down with the Painkiller. Falcon got a rope break but never gained much momentum and later lost to the Painkiller when Gideon applied it near the center of the ring. Neil and Gideon won the tag belts by default when their opponents Revolution Next got suspended. When Revolution Next got the idea of competing under masks Gideon laughed (Excerpt #15). They made Rev. Next look like complete jacka$$es in the rematch. The match had Gideon doing 3 shooting star splashes. Neil even did a cart wheel into a big boot and high risk moves NEVER to use. Gideon has seen Sam Hickman on TV doing an interview for a federation called UPWF. At this time Gideon started the legal work to get the Alliance out of Titan Wrestling and into the UPWF. Gideon had problems negotiating the deal to go to the UPWF. They just wanted to use the Alliance to give their wrestlers credibility. Gideon and Neil starred in 2 action films last year. In the recent Lobo movie Neil was Lobo and Gideon was an auditor assigned to Lobo to collect over 12 years of back taxes. Despite obviously being an action flick it was also called one of the funniest movies in a long time (Excerpt #16). Gideon worked so well with Neil that they were able to bring a lot of personal touches to the movie. The movie 'Vampire Hunters' had Gideon playing the role of a mercenary hired by 'Max Winchester'. In the movie Gideon was a trigger-happy yahoo doing massive property damage. BW called Neil and Gideon and told them about a new federation that she was competing in called the Pacific Pro Wrestling. Neil looked at the roster there was decent talent and he was bored. The Alliance dominated the tag division. There wasn't a team that could come close to competing on their level. Neil, Gideon, BW, and Sean formed a stable that was unstoppable. During this time they debuted their new finisher 'The G-Man Slaughter in the 3 rd degree' or simply the '3G'. Neil and Gideon could hit the 3G out of nowhere and nailed half the roster with it at one time or another (Most of the List: Ted Billings, Bird of Prey, Byron, Dean Bradfield, Jimmy Mayhem, Ash Silverman, Jezebel, Sammy, Bob, and Big Show). Violence Incorporated run rough shod over the competition. BW would become a hardcore icon for dominating the Hardcore division. This was where the 24/7 rule became the champion's advantage. With the 24/7 rule BW lost the belt and V.Inc jumped the new champ and less then 2 minutes later BW was a 2 time Hardcore champion. Sean would have a great run as the PPW Champ until he came upon the man that he couldn't beat Ash Silverman. Wrestling fans say it was this that led to the feelings of jealousy and insecurity that caused him to break up with V.Inc. BW would take the PPW World title on her very first try from Silverman. #1 'Stephanie (BW) is phenominal. She has beaten many men and has won world titles in the UJW and now PPW.' #2 'The LJC make me a bit uneasy. They are tough but my biggest worry was waist locks from behind.' #3 'Sean Tylor is shifty, underhanded, and down right unethical. I like Sean a lot he would have made a great lawyer.' #4 'Caleb Alan isn't a god but is a great role model. The man is arrogant, condescending, has no respect for opponents, and has less respect for women then Bill Clinton. I like really like his style a lot.' During the last months of PPW everyone knew that the company was going under. Everyone was looking for a new federation to go to. Neil and Gideon had some friends for Titan Wrestling that told them about a new promotion they were working called New Era Wrestling. Their debut in NEW was marked by some controversy. Their first match they had a shot for the NEW tag titles in a 4 team sudden death match. It was a match they never lost decided by Kriss Zander a man not even involved in the match. Gideon was looking forward to wrestling in NEW. He wanted another crack at Red Falcon. Some people are just begging to be schooled and Red Falcon was talking a hell of a lot of smack considering Gideon took his Continental title in Titan Wrestling. Gideon was very busy. He was negotiating contracts for both Stephanie and Caleb. Red Falcon soon left so they never did get to face each other again. Gideon had a bit of an issue with Kriss Zander. First came the Xtreme title match. Gideon was the 3rd man fighting the Maniac one of the toughest men to hold the Xtreme title and a solid wrestler in Seth Tyler one of the Infamous Tyler brothers. Gideon had the title at the end of the night via the Painkiller. After a lot of positioning there was a match between the Connection and the Alliance. Gideon had a plan and at the end of the night Gideon had Kriss’s arm trapped in the Painkiller. Neil neutralized Lowe and the referee had to stop the match to save Kriss’s arm because Gideon was not going to break the hold and there was no way that Kriss would be able to escape it. Things went well for them until the rest of Violence Inc arrived. Caleb 'The Canadian Heart Throb' Alan quickly lost interest and was only there to hurt people and feed his ego by appearing on TV. Stephanie (Black Widow) came in and John Blaze quickly turned her into a 'T and A act' to gain ratings. Stephanie and The Alliance were very close. They weren’t tolerating that type of garbage from John Blaze. Stephanie had paid her dues and has even been a World Champion in other federations. In NEW she was being degraded as a wrestler and been stuck fighting the talentless whores and sluts that passed for a NEW Valet. They all left NEW and The Alliance dropped the tag titles and Gideon dropped the Xtreme title on the mat in the middle of the ring in a house show. There were several calls after that but The Alliance returned none of them. The Alliance doesn’t talk very often about the UWL and that is for good reason. It was one of the most dangerous federations to work in filled with wack jobs and psychopaths. In wrestling that is saying a hell of a lot. There was no concern for wrestlers health or the health of anyone else. This was pretty much the purgatory of wrestling. If you worked here you either were kicked out of everywhere else and McDonalds wasn’t hiring or you were a violent asshole that didn’t play well with others. Gideon and Neil didn’t even have to bother to try and keep from hurting people. Here it is encouraged to hurt your opponents bad. This is backyard wrestling with a budget. While federations like ECW had a love of the sport there was only one thing driving people here money and fame. They fit in well here but they knew they were just filling in time. When Gideon hospitalized one of the Announcers The Alliance got fired. Neil and Gideon couldn’t have cared less and hospitalizing one of the staff on TV was about the only way to actually get fired. The IWF was very interesting and by interesting criminally insane. One of the old enemies of The Alliance and Stephanie had resurfaced. Here is the idea someone had. Give a guy even more violent and angry then Neil Fracas a federation and tons of money. Did I mention he is a real big guy? Neil Fracas is a giant of a man at 7ft2 and 359 pounds of muscle. The Executioner is 7ft4, 412 pounds. He hangs guys by their necks with a rope because he can’t be bothered to learn a decent chokehold. He had the contracts of Neil Fracas, Gideon Fontaine, and Black Widow. He had a federation and could run it like a dictatorship and booked any matches that amused him. They also had a friend from PPW named Caleb Alan. It was also interesting when Executioner used his money to get in people to make the Alliance’s life hell. This was a time when Stephanie had to retire from wrestling since she was about 2 months pregnant. The Black Widow became an announcer. As for Caleb…… Well he had very little scruples to begin with and Executioner enjoyed letting him run wild. In fact he encouraged it by feeding his already monstrous ego. Caleb had a match and his opponent was beaten so badly it is assumed that Caleb killed him. That wrestler was never see again so it was never proven one way or another what happened though authorities spent 4 weeks trying to prove it was a murder. The backers of the federation withdrew their support of Executioner not wanting attention drawn to them by the authorities. The federation closed during that time. Caleb was never officially charged during that time since there was no evidence to prove anything. Gideon’s Lawyers argued that it was all a show and with no evidence to prove that it wasn’t fake. While Gideon and Neil were free agents they were called by Bryan Chase to rejoin NEW. Even though Gideon and Neil had walked out with Stephanie they still had earned respect of many of the wrestlers of NEW. When Bryan Chase became the man in charge he called up Neil and Gideon knowing what they brought to the table in terms of skill and intensity. They came in as the newest additions to Bryan Chase’s Billion Dollar Productions stable. This lasted all of 15 minutes when The Alliance showed Bryan Chase in no uncertain terms that they would not be dictated to. They did things on their own terms and Neil and Gideon laid out Bryan Chase in the center of the ring with their patented 3rd degree. This led to a feud with Bryan’s replacements in the BDP Stone Devane and Ryzen. Neil was a bit pissed off at Stone Devane for being too stiff so he pretty said screw it and he started throwing down. Luckily it was ended before it escalated. This led to Stone Devane and Neil becoming great friends later after all they had many thigns in common. Stephanie was wrestling again and in the National title mix. She came close to winning it against John Blaze. She also took Yamato Megumi to the limits in her match for his TV title. It was during this time that the Alliance met an upcoming tag team that could bring the fight to them on their terms. They were The Way Hetero Connection better known as Ed Gesumaria and Steve Gugliociello. They had 3 matches and each one was filled with an intensity not seen in years. The WHC took the first match and The Alliance’s tag titles. The Alliance took their rematch winning a 3 way table match beating them and the Warriors of Darkness. The 3rd match was to be a ladder match which had Steve and Gideon going to the hospital for observation after an incredible sitdown crucifix powerbomb off a ladder through a table on the outside. Ed would be injured and Neil suffered a knee injury. The Alliance won that day but that was the day the tag division died. The Alliance and WHC raised the bar so high there was no way that they could ever get up to it again. There was no one willing to go that far. Gideon became a manager during this time for Ed and Neil. While Neil, Gideon, and Stephanie were competing in NEW they were also competing in Core Wrestling. Neil, Stephanie, and Gideon still have bad feelings between towards Core’s management of Matt Lanier and Jason Hughes. There hasn’t been much said when the Alliance left Core. There wasn’t much said when Stephanie walked out. They have never hated working in a federation so much in their lives. You could go through the wrestling magazines over the past few years and see people going in and out of Core. Few stayed any length of time before leaving Core. Most never even got to the stage where they were there long enough to get their bio pages on the Core Wrestling website. Someday they will explain in great detail what happened. For now they are glad they aren’t there and pity the few friends that remain. Neil’s knee injury worsened and John Blaze had to step in to force Neil to get it fixed before he ended up permanently ruining it. Neil had come very close to being the new NEW world champ being the final man eliminated in the battle royal for the NEW title by Yamato Megumi. He was top contender for the National title and a favorite. He was pissed because he lost it all. Gideon was bouncing back from his narrow loss in his signature match the G-Man Challenge against Billy McStoner where he lost 2 to 3. He would beat Craig Clayborn and then get a title shot for the National Title VS Trendkiller. After taking advantage of a key mistake by Trendkiller when he brought a chair in the ring Gideon won the National title. It would be shortly after this where Gideon would have to step up to help run NEW. Knowing that a person running the federation should never be it’s champion he vacated his title and put it up for grabs. The idea was to give new talent a chance to prove themselves and give them a goal. It was also during this time that Gideon started to really hate wrestling. He hated the pettiness and the selfishness that had grown like a cancer. The finals were winding down and it would be Sean Page and Christian White but Craig Clayborn wanted it to be a 3 way match. Despite Gideon not wanting to be involved he was signed to the match and entered the match hating many things. He was in no frame of mind to be competing and angry. He let his NEW contract expire and he was under no obligation to even wrestle the match. It was assumed that Gideon would sign a new contract being one of NEW’s strongest supporters. Gideon of course wrestled the match. It never showed during the match but this would be the time when Gideon would decide to leave wrestling. Craig Clayborn and Bryan Chase struggled to keep NEW running but it was dying and everyone knew. They wanted the final card to be special and be one that will be talked about for decades. Gideon decided to comeback and would go for the tag titles one last time and help send them out in a blaze of glory but it never happened. Bryan Chase working to make that final card happen had died and NEW closed shortly after. Neil and Stephanie at this point had no interest in ever going back to wrestling. Gideon saw NEW reopened under new management and offered to help. The help was refused due to certain people bad mouthing Gideon. This management was doomed to fail. It took only 3 weeks. Craig Clayborn and Zakk Wylde tried to revive NEW and for a while it looked like they would succeed. After 2 weeks it started unraveling. The egos started again and Craig left. Gideon stepped up to help but Neither Zakk or Gideon had any stomach for politics and whining. The board of directors were brought in by a few unhappy roster members. It was over and it is likely that NEW will never open again. The memories will be there of a federation that stood for pride and sacrifice. Times when wrestling was what the wrestlers lived for. He did a right round kick. I ducked and did a left back leg sweep. He jumped and came down with an axe kick I avoided with a quick roll….. (9) I really liked the character Tyler Stone. I was leader of a Mafia family going to Tokyo to take over. In this movie I starred along with Jet Li, Bruce Lee, and Jackie Chan. We had some amazing fight scenes. In one scene I jumped off a second story balcony and landed in a crouch in front of Jet and Jackie. I hadn't competed on a professional level for a while now. I had to get back to the basics and learn which moves aren't allowed to be used. Neil and I improved my ring presence including a few flashy moves for show. When I entered the ring I could play to the crowd. Tanimoto didn't like this much but accepted it as part of my style.. The first match went very well with a knock out in under a minute. The second match I used a move I call the 'One Inch Kick' I got a KO from it….. Well when Fear and Executioner left their buddy Trash out in the cold we couldn’t resist putting the screws to the idiots. The look on their faces when Fallen Angel pinned Trash and they lost their tag titles was priceless. I’ve been in federations with some real wack jobs in my day but none of them were murders. At that point it was time to go. (14) The sales pitch was kind of weird that day. The arm triangle choke (or simply arm triangle) is a generic term describing chokeholds in which the opponent is strangled in between his or her own shoulder and the performers arm. This is as opposed to the regular triangle choke, which denotes a chokehold using the legs, albeit with a similar mechanism of strangulation against the opponent's own shoulder. An arm triangle choke where the performer is on the side of the opponent and presses a forearm into opposite side of the neck of the opponent is called a side choke, such as from the kata-gatame hold.
