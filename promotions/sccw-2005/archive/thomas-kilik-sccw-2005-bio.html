Thomas Kilik Biography Real Name N/A Nicknames N/A Hometown Las Vegas, NV Birthday 3rd September, 1983 Height 5'10' Weight 210 Biography Thomas has not had much experience in a wrestling ring. He never wanted to be a wrestler in the first place. All he wanted to do was fit in. Unfortunately, he could never do that. His father was a businessman who constantly had to move the family across the country to keep up with his clients. Thomas found himself unable to keep friends for long because of this. Every time he seemed like he was able to settle in, he hd to move again. Thomas eventually found it too much to bear and ran away from his family at the age of 16. He hitch-hiked his way across the country, relying on the kindness of strangers for transportation. He stole from people whenever he needed money. He eventually found himself in Las Vegas. He sneaked his way into a casino and began to gamble. He was caught and picked up by the authorities. Being underage, they could not prosecute him, so he was sent to a reform school just outside on the city. Once there, he had new difficulties to face. Being not exactly the biggest kid there, he was forced to fight almost every day against the older, bigger kids. The day before he was scheduled to leave the school, he took it upon himself to beat the living hell out of those who had hurt him. He succeeded and the next day was released from the school. Out on his own again, he drifted from job to job in state to state, finally finding a flyer advertising a new wrestling promotion which was opening. He made up his mind that he would become a wrestler.
