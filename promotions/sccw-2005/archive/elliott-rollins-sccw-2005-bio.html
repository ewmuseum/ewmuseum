Elliott Rollins Biography Real Name Elliott Rollins Nicknames Fucking Hometown Pittsburgh, PA Birthday 11/28/80 Height 6'3' Weight 230 Fed Titles LiveWire Champion Fed Awards Young Lion's Cup Other Feds A long list you'll never hear of. Wrestling Style Technical/Suplex Finisher Move Riot Coming Finisher Description Argentine Piledriver Finisher Setup Move War Ensemble Setup Description Shining Wizard Other Moves Elliott Rollins is a technical wrestler at heart. He is also a suplex machine, and loves nothing more than tossing people around. His moves are high impact, both for his opponent and himself. Special Moves: 'Divine Intervention' - Rollins has a secondary finisher that he saves for very, very special occasions. It's a top-rope brainbuster, but instead of dropping the opponent to the mat, he spikes the opponent head-first on the top turnbuckle/ring post. It's named 'Divine Intervention' after the Slayer album of the same name. 'The Epidemic' - This is Rollins' submission finisher. It's a simple dragon sleeper with leg scissors thrown in. While he prefers to throw people around the ring, he is a very capable submissions wrestler, and will use submissions when it is to his advantage.
