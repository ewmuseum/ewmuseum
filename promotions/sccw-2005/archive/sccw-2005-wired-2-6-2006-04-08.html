Mikael has just enough time to get to his feet and turn around as Covel soars towards him with a dropkick. Mikael blocks it however. He grabs Covel's legs and drives him down into the mat by the back of his head. He steps through the legs and grapevines them. He turns Covel over and locks the Sharpshooter in perfectly! Covel screams in agony on the floor. SA: More pressure on the back! HJ: I've never seen Mikael do this before! SA: He's got it locked in perfectly! Covel claws his way towards the ropes and manages to grab hold of the bottom one. Jamison tries to get Mikael to break the hold but instead he walks away from the ropes dragging Covel with him. Mikael sits back further on the spine of Covel bending further in the unnatural position. Covel has literally no choice but to tap out of he wants to leave tonight with a spine. Covel taps. *Ding, Ding, Ding!* SKYE: The winner of the fourth decision, via submission, Mikael Lundgard! Mikael Lundgard 2 – 2 John Covel Time: 1:14 SA: Just over a minute left! HJ: Pin him Mikael! Mikael charges Covel taking him down with a spear and hammers away with mounted punches on the Custom Chaos champion. Covel rolls away as Jamison pulls Mikael away. '0:53' Mikael breaks free from Jamison's grip and grabs Covel by the hair. He pulls him up to his feet and hooks him up for a gut wrench. Rather than just throwing him away though Mikael hugs in close and drives Covel down into the mat head first with a reverse DDT! '0:39' SA: A gut wrench reverse DDT! Mikael pulling out new moves here! Mikael gets the cover! ONE! TWO! John kicks out! '0:31' Mikael doesn't argue with the referee. Instead he sits Covel up and locks in the rear naked choke! HJ: This is how it's done properly! Covel tries to roll but Mikael blocks it. John fights the choke for as long as possible. '0:22' Covel still tries frantically to reach for the ropes. '0:12' Covel slumps! '0:10' Jamison raises his arm! It falls! '0:07' A second time! It falls! '0:04!' A third time! '0:01'! It falls! The bell sounds! The claxon sounds! The fans erupt! SA: What the hell just happened? HJ: Everything happened at once! SA: Mikael thinks he's won! HJ: Well he did! Covel's arm fell! SA: But the timer ran out! HJ: Jamison's talking to SKYE... SKYE: Ladies and Gentlemen, I have just been informed by referee Russel Jamison that John Covel's arm fell a split second before the end of the match, therefore, the winner of the fifth decision, via submission, Mikael Lundgard! Mikael Lundgard 3 – 2 John Covel Time: 0:00 SKYE: Therefore, your winner by three decisions to two, The FUSE UNIVERSAL CHAMPION, 'THE DANISH DEMON' MIKAEL LUNDGARD! HJ: YES! Unbeatable!
