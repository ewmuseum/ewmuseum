Killzone 14 Staples Center — Los Angeles, California ---- A Warm Welcome from all of us at FUSE Wrestling John Covel steps from his 2006 Grand Am and moves around to the trunk, retrieving his bag. Meeting him there is a tall man, some two inches taller than John himself. The other young male looks happy as the two trade a few words before John shuts his trunk. The two begin walking across the parking lot towards the arena. John: Just don't do anything stupid, Tim. They both laugh as they continue to walk in the evening air. John reaches down and presses the wrinkles out of his FUSE shirt. Just as he looks back up, a young boy approaches with a picture and Sharpie in hand. Boy: John, can I have your autograph? John smiles and lets the bag fall from his shoulder, landing on the ground. He squats down to a knee and looks to the boy. Covel: Of course. What's your name? The boy smiles back as John's friend looks on. Boy: Billy. John grins a bit as he takes the picture and marker and begins writing. After a few moments, John raises the picture and blows on it to dry the marker. He looks at his work and hands it back to the boy. John: There you go, Billy. Stay out of trouble. The boy looks at the picture and cracks a huge smile. Billy: I will. I promise. Thanks a lot, John! John pushes himself back up, retrieving his bag and watching as the boy heads back to his older brother. John and Tim re-start their forward progress to the back entrance as Tim looks to his friend. Tim: How does that make you feel? John chuckles as he positions his bag in a comfortable spot on his shoulder. He takes a deep breath and looks back to Tim. John: It gives me a fuzzy feeling deep down inside. Tim: I'll bet. The two get closer to the back door now, just a few hundred yards from it. John: The weird thing is when the older fans are giddier than the young kids are. Tim flashes John a confused look. Tim: What do you mean? John laughs as he thinks back to his days on the independent circuit. John: Like this woman that came to a show in Texas. She was in her early thirties and she met me in the parking lot after the show. She talked to me for like twenty minutes and she had HUGE boobs. Tim begins laughing. John cracks a smile as well as they come up to the last row in the parking lot. Tim: I think I know where this is going. John: And she was wearing a really low cut top. I kept looking at the massive canyon in front of me and at the end of the conversation she caught me. Tim cringes at the idea of getting caught staring at a woman's cleavage. Tim: Not good, man. John: No. I'm not even joking when I say she asked me if I wanted to see them. She flashed me and we did it right there in the backseat of my car, man. A laugh bellows from Tim's belly as the step onto the curb and approach the door to the building. Tim: Did you at least get her name? John: It was a while ago. I think it started with a "C." Both of them laugh a bit more as they pull the door open and step into the building. Quick to meet them is security, checking to see if they should be there. Tim: That's awesome. John: Then I did a show in Michigan; up towards Detroit. After then show this kid in his mid-twenties came up to me while I was watching the main event and started talking. All he wanted to talk about was the Tigers. How they were awesome and stuff. Tim chuckles and looks at John with disbelief. Security has almost finished checking their baggage and getting them signed in. After signing their names to the roster list, John and Tim push past the guards and into the hallways leading to John's dressing room. Tim: The Tigers? John: Yeah. I guess we had something in common. I was a Cubs fan then too. But the other one I can remember is this nineteen year old kid out in Cali. I had just worked a hardcore match and had a splitting headache so I stopped into a liquor store on the way out of town to buy some Tylenol. He had been at the show and recognized me. He started complimenting my work and such. Took me ten minutes to realize that he just wanted me to buy him booze. Tim laughs again as they round a corner and come to a door. Tim: Did you buy it? John: Yeah. What the hell? You know? I bought him some Vodka and Natty Light. Both of the men shudder at the mention of the beer, remembering their bad experiences with it. John pushes the door open and drops his bag inside the door before reaching to his back pocket. He hands Tim a ticket. Tim: Thanks, man. Good luck tonight. Tim slaps John on the back and heads out to his front row seat for the night's action. John laughs one more time at the memories and ducks into his dressing room to prepare for his title defense. ---- Rock, Paper, Oh Hi Tigera!
