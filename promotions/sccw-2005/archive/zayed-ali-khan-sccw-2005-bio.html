Zayed Ali Khan Biography Real Name Zayed Ali Khan Yousufzai Nicknames ZAK, The 5th Element, The Weapon of Mass Destruction (WMD) Hometown Herat, Afghanistan (Refuge in Pakistan) Birthday 15th May 1982 Height 6'1' Weight 225 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography The character has basically lived his life near the borders of Pakistan and Afghanistan and as wrestling was a sport of passion there, he had made his way up. Often he hated the idea to move abroad for any purpose but soon realized through a senior's revelation that this is his true destiny and he must carry on. Being a great fighter of his land he was chosen to represent them in the world and they have pushed him forward to make a presence felt for their passionate sport. Titles/Awards Won: 'The Great Lion' (title given to the greatest fighter of the time in Afghanistan. Other Feds TLW [Talibani Liberation Wrestling] Other Fed Awards Named 'The Lion' of TLW Wrestling Style Normally he uses street fighting skills, but is well experienced in the ring and loves to perform moves that involve high risks. Finisher Move WMD [Weapon of Mass Destruction] Finisher Description Swanton Bomb off the top rope Finisher Setup Move DDT Setup Description The DDT is performed normally after a counter move of the opponent and he spaces the opponent well enough for the finisher to be performed from the top rope. Other Moves + Sunset Flip: The attacker jumps over the victim, on the way down they waistlock the victim, the attacker rolls over their back so they end up sitting and they pull down the victim so they land on their back. The attacker usually pins the victim's arms to the mat with their legs. + Belly to back brainbuster (suplex): The attacker is standing behind the victim, the attacker's head is under one of the victim's arms. The attacker waistlocks the victim and lifts them off the ground and falls backwards, dropping the victim on their head/neck. + Cradle Suplex: The attacker applies a front face lock on the victim and throws the victim's near arm across their shoulders. The attacker grabs the victim's near leg and hooks it. The attacker lifts the victim up and falls backwards so they land back first on the mat. The attacker keeps the leg hooked and usually bridges to hold the move for a pin. + Tear Drop Suplex: Attacker stands behind and to the side of the victim. The attacker waistlocks the victim with one arm and hooks one of their legs with the other. The attacker lifts the opponent up onto their shoulder and then falls backwards. + Underhook: The attacker is facing the victim. The attacker bends the victim down and hooks both of the victim's arms so they are behind the victim's back. The attacker links their hands together and lifts the victim up and falls backwards, dropping the victim to the mat on their back. + Top Rope Body Press: The attacker jumps at victim and hits the victim at an angle, knocking them down to the mat. + Vertical Splash: The attacker jumps on the victim so that the attacker's legs are over the victim's shoulders. The victim falls backwards and the attacker ends up sitting down with a vertical splash on the attacker's chest. + Twisting Bulldog: The attacker applies a side headlock to the victim and runs towards a corner and up the ring ropes with the headlock still applied. The attacker kicks off the top rope and shifts their bodyweight, swinging their body around. The attacker drives the victim's head into the mat as they fall to the mat.
