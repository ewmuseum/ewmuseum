Reginald V. Lovecraft Biography Real Name Reginald Victor Lovecraft Nicknames The Elder Statesman Hometown Atlanta, Georgia Birthday November 20, 1955 Height 6'7' Weight 281 Fed Titles Part of the SCCW Strength In Numbers Champions (The Dead Man's Hand) Fed Awards None. Biography Reginald Victor Lovecraft is best known to professional wrestling fans as one half of Politically Incorrect, among the most dominant tag teams of the early 21st century. Considered retired as of 2004, Reginald returned to the political arena, serving capably in Georgia's state government, as well as for several prominent think tanks. Among the special interest groups that he courts, however, is one that might not have been expected. While the details are unspecific, Reginald V. Lovecraft is most certainly on Alexandra Pierce's payroll. As long as the checks continue to come in, the woman known as Desade has Lovecraft's undying loyalty. It is not his political favor that she needs now, however, but his in-ring ability. With the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championship within the Dead Man's Hand, and most of the Hand fighting off assaults on all fronts, Phillip Kennedy needs a full time tag team partner. Enter Reginald Lovecraft, a man with no shortage of experience dealing with skirt-chasing younger partners... Other Feds Primetime Championship Wrestling, Five Star Wrestling, Old School Wrestling Other Fed Awards PCW World Tag Team Champion, FSW World Tag Team Champion, OSW World Tag Team Champion (all with William S. Powell, as 'Politically Incorrect'), PCW Extreme Champion, co-holder of the OSW American Championship (with Powell) Wrestling Style Brawling powerhouse. Finisher Move Southern Justice Finisher Description Fireman's Carry Flapjack Finisher Setup Move Sambo Suplex Setup Description Twisting Rock Bottom style throw. Other Moves Lovecraft is a brawler, specializing in hard hitting punching and kicking moves. Once a former Georgia Golden Globes champion, he retains his hard punching power, though most of his technique is gone. Entrance Music '...And Justice For All' by Metallica
