Spacely Biography Real Name Thad Denver Hometown Space, You Idiot (actual name of city/state of his home planet of Zarflon) Birthday 11/14/1990 Height 5'9' Weight 175 Fed Titles SCCW LiveWire Championship, SCCW Gateway Championship Biography 'Not much is known about Spacely's past.' Just kidding, I'm not going to cop out of writing a bio in order to remain mysterious. Real name Thad Denver, who was in fact related to the late John Denver, was groomed to be a musician his entire life. A classically trained pianist and choral singer, Thad was your typical music nerd growing up...but harbored an intense desire to beat people up instead of serenading them with the beauty of song. This desire intensified while standing in line before his big chance (American Idol audition,) as he has broken his previous record of 'People I Meet In One Day And Want To Suplex' there while engaging in conversation with thousands of fucking idiots. Once inside, Randy, Paula, Simon, and that other bitch no one cares about did not expect what was about to happen. Reaching his boiling point from having to stand around for 17 hours just to sing a song, instead of performing for the judges, he proceeded to bend over and produce an armpit fart rendition of Amy Grant's 'Good For Me.' Upon hearing this, he was disowned by the Denver family...allowing him to pursue his dream as a professional beater-upper. Thad quit two days after enrolling at a local MMA school in Atlanta, citing personal differences between himself and the instructor: he was a pussy and they weren't. He then enrolled in wrestling school where he continued to get the shit kicked out of him...but miraculously, he stuck with it and developed into a fine acrobatic talent! Deciding the name 'Thad Denver' only invoked the desire to be on the receiving end of a punch, he assumed the moniker of Spacely...because he thought outer space was pretty cool. Adopted a golden retriever that he subsequently named Quasar, who has the uncanny ability to talk (even without moving his mouth.) Not only does Quasar provide sound financial and career advice, he also seems to know a thing or two about wrestling...which honestly, is not that strange if you consider that THE FUCKING THING TALKS. (Quasar is actually voiced by actor Rip Torn.)
