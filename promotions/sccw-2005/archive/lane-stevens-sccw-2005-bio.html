Lane Stevens Biography Real Name n/a Nicknames 'The River Rat' Hometown Dubuque, IA Birthday 4/20/1981 Height 6'3' Weight 232 Biography Physical appearance: Lane is toned, but is not nearly as muscular as many of his fellow wrestlers. He has short red hair, freckles, and occasionally a beard. He often comes to the ring in a black robe with 'River Rat' written on the back in red. He has long ring tights that say the same. Lane is arrogant, but he is talented. He is not as physically dynamic as many of his peers, but he makes up for it with excellent technique, doing his homework, and a liberal use of the words 'fair play'. After a successful run in FUSE, some of Lane's personal demons took hold of him. His drinking and use of painkillers got to be a little out of control and he had to take a break. He was recently discovered by Desade at a strip club, and was offered a decent chunk of change to return and join the Dead Man's Hand. He now scoffs at morality even moreso than before, and believes in nothing but for his own fun. He believes that most everyone is as amoral as he is, they just haven't come to grips with his. His lack of faith in humanity has made him bitter and depressed, but you'd never know it by talking to him. His nickname, The River Rat, comes from his other passion, poker. The river is the last card dealt in Texas Hold'em. He as adopted the nickname in the wrestling context as a means to say, I'm going to win it in the end somehow....some way. Strengths: 1. Researches every opponent 2. Gets under opponent's skin 3. Excellent Submission background Weaknesses: 1. Not too strong 2. Not too fast 3. Drinking/pills problem Wrestling Style Technician/Submission Specialist Finisher Move The Constrictor Finisher Description Submission - Lane jumps on his opponents back (it can also be applied on the ground) and scissors their midsection with his legs, taking them down to the ground and with their body still scissored he applies a variation of the dragon sleeper. It is named Finisher Setup Move No set up move in paticular, it often just comes out of nowhere, usually when an opponent is dazed for a moment or distracted. Other Moves - 2nd finisher: 'The Night Cap' - Stone Cold Stunner, yeah I don't know the actual wrestling name for the move, sue me. -'Straight Flush' - Brainbuster which drops his opponent's head on the mat in a way that they are momentarily perpendicular to the mat. -Falling reverse DDT -Low blow followed by a running knee lift -Repeated European Uppercuts in the corner -backbreaker (he will deadlift the opponent off his knee and drop them onto it multiple times) -Figure four leglock -Ankle lock -Knee drop from the second turnbuckle (his most adventerous move) -Chop block -Inverted piledriver -Bulldog -Kneebreaker -Legdrops -Reverse Indian Deathlock -STF -Dropkick to the back of the knee -Swinging Neckbreaker -Camel Clutch (only on smaller opponents) Entrance Music 'Karma Police' by Radiohead
