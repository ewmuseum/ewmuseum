Christian Black Biography Real Name Christian Black Nicknames The Saint Hometown Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania Birthday 12/29/87 Height 6'1' Weight 234 Biography Christian Black began to watch wrestling when he was 15 years old. He originally lived in Elizabeth, Pittsburgh, but his mom lost her job and his dad was a lowlife who did nothing. So they were forced to move to find another job. They found a fairly cheap apartment in New Castle, PA. They moved there, at this time Christian was very into wrestling. He was planning to go to a Wrestling school, he even started his own backyard wrestling federations. None of them went anywhere, seeing that he had few friends. Christian went on the graduate high school. After high school, he began looking into wrestling school. He found a school in Detroit, Michigan. It was a world-renowned school, The School of Hard Knocks. A lot of today's wrestlers went there. Christian got the money to go there, by getting into the drug trafficking business. After having a close call with the police, he quit that and went to the school, with the clothes on his back. He spent the next 1 1/2 years there. He was taught everything he needed. He went on to go to the UCW (Ultimate Championship Wresting). There is where he become known as Saint. He was known for his High- Flying, Daredevil style. Very few people in the are saw thing like this. He won a lot of his matches, but he also had his losses. He started to make a better pay cheque when he took the Hardcore Championship from Rick Herat. He become known as the King of Hardcore. He kept the tile for 6 straight months. He also tried to go for the USA belt, but fell short after a harsh chair blow to the head. After that loss he gave up his title, and left the UCW due to bad management. He went around looking for other offers. That's when he stumbled open an Independent wrestling federation, known as OWF (Omega Wrestling Federation). There he claimed his first USA championship. After about 2 years with the OWF he decided it was time to find a better job. He went around looking, but had to end up going home because of his mothers illness. She died a short time after he got home. After she died, Christian went into a 'hiding' state, if you will. No one heard from him for a few months. When he came out, he was no longer the clean cut Christian Black. He was The Saint. He was ready to get back into the wrestling business.
