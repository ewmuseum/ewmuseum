Tyson Sinn Biography Real Name Tyson McGuire Nicknames 'The X-Factor' Hometown Philadelphia, Penn. Birthday February 8, 1986 Height 5'11ft Weight 216 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography A man that has just graduated high school only 36 months ago, Tyson Sinn has signed only his third big wrestling contract here with Fuse, and this being only his first contract out of high school! Deciding that college wasn't for Tyson, he moved out of the home of his abusive and alcoholic aunt and uncle, and he is now seeking a way to forget his childhood while making enough money so survive. Growing up with no mother or father who died early in his life and was put in custody of his aunt and uncle, both who Tyson hates. This is where Tyson Sinn stands...with or without fan appreciation...with or without the backing of this, or any other company; Tyson Sinn will stand at the top of the world for himself and for whoever else is willing to come for the ride!
