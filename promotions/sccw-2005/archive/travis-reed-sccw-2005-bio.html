Travis Reed Biography Real Name Travis William Reed Nicknames Big Trav Hometown Boone, NC Height 6'6' Weight 276 Other Feds Blue Ridge Wrestling, PRIME Other Fed Awards 3x Blue Ridge Wrestling Heavyweight Champion, 2x Blue Ridge Wrestling Tag Team Champion Finisher Move Spinebuster Finisher Description Arn Anderson style Other Moves Strikes • Turnaround Haymaker • 'Kitchen Sink' Knee • Eye Poke • Various Running Clotheslines • 3 Punch Combo [2 Left Jabs and a Right Cross] Grapple • Running Powerslam • Cradle Piledriver • Hanging Suplex • Atomic Drop • Belly-to-Back Suplex • Gut Wrench Suplex Corner • Superplex • Running Big Boot • Open Hand Slap to Opponent's Chest • Running Splash Submissions • Standing Dragon Sleeper • Boston Crab • Full Nelson Camel Clutch • Standing or Kneeling Armbar Writer's Notes Travis wrestles like the classic heels of old. Think Tully Blanchard or vintage Nick Bockwinkle, if nothing else Flair is a good point of reference as well. Big Trav exploits the size advantage he enjoys over 75% of the FUSE roster and, being a well seasoned ring vet, he is quite good at exploiting said advantage. Travis is not, however, a pure power wrestler, he has two decades of ring time both as an active competitor and trainer so you'd be hard pressed to find a hold he can't apply or counter. Travis is the rare big man who can stand toe-to-toe with a man two weight classes below him and trade classic chain wrestling, often getting the best of the exchange. As of late Travis have taken to employing more and more cheap tactics and shortcuts, leaving his true wrestling skills on the back burner for now. Travis' one weakness would be his surgecially repaired left knee, an injury so severe it put him on the shelf for 3 years. Entrance Music Technically Sound Old School Heel
