Desade Biography Real Name Alexandra Pierce Nicknames 'The Director', 'The Spider in the Web', 'The Witch in White', 'The Medusa' Hometown Tampa, Floria - resides in Paris, France Birthday 7-14-1977 Height 5'9' Weight 145 Fed Titles SCCW LiveWire Champion (as Charlotte Ramone), SCCW Gateway Champion, SCCW Strength in Numbers Champion Fed Awards Heel of the Year 2008 Biography The Pierce family: 'Hurricane' Andrew Pierce (as part of the Outlanders, former National Wrestling Council World Tag Team Champion), 'Perfect' Raymond Pierce (with Damon Hayes in the Untouchables, perhaps the NWC's most controversial Tag Team champions) and a World Heavyweight Champion in his own right, and Alexandra Pierce, herself three-time manager of the NWC World Tag Team Champions and the first woman ever to challenge for the NWC Heavyweight Championship (a feat she performed unsuccessfully twice before another woman, Jade, succeeded at winning the title). They are not often considered in the pantheon of great wrestling families, in all likelihood because of Raymond's overblown sense of self-importance, but their dominance was, at times, unchecked in the NWC. It's odd to consider that, considering Raymond Pierce's infamy, his little sister may just go down as the most infamous villainess to ever step foot in an NWC ring. It wasn't like that when she debuted in 1997 alongside the tag team she stood by for much of her career, Black Ops. Then known as Croc and Cobra, Black Ops came out of nowhere to upset team of Idol Austin and Shawn Arrows in their first NWC tag team championship match, and though the team only held the championship briefly, they'd cemented their legacy -- a legacy their imprinted with titanic battles against the Elite, a group run by the men who did not even know they were her siblings, and their home federation's owner, David Cooper. Upon Lone Star Wrestling's closure, no one heard anything from Desade or Black Ops for several years. In 1998, however, Chris Schukar made his first attempt to take over the National Wrestling Council at the first Purgatory tournament. Peerless Hunter Sabuani won that tournament, granting Schukar controlling interest in the Council, but he'd never faced his nemesis, only knowing the name 'Alex Pierce.' A loudmouthed Brit by the name of Bastian handled all the confrontations with Schukar, until the end of that night at Purgatory, when Desade made her return -- and her identity -- known. Desade herself did not appear on NWC television very often, taking part in the infamous missing main event at Across Enemy Lines 3 on behalf of Hawaiian Islands Wrestling (her second NWC World Heavyweight Championship match after a failed endeavor to unseat Arrows again, while in LSW under a mask calling herself 'Sascha'). Then came the Return to Purgatory. An ailing Schukar wanted to put the NWC out of commission so it would die before he did. Pierce's emissary returned, denying that and leveling another challenge for another tournament. 100% ownership of the National Wrestling Council would go to the winner, to do with as they pleased. Devin Fox defeated Wyatt Connors that night, and it should have been a night of triumphs. The NWC was saved once and for all, rid of the scourge of Chris Schukar. But it was not to be. Desade -- who earlier in the night had managed the team of Croc (now going by his real name of Gideon Asher) and Hunter Sabuani to a victory in claiming the NWC World Tag Team Championships, albeit under masks as The Fallen -- betrayed the Council, betrayed everyone who thought they should stand beside her. The NWC was closed. The Illuminati was formed. Then Desade disappeared. First seen on FUSE television in cryptic videos spliced into the video feed, the Director made her impactful return at FUSE on ESPN, orchestrating the crucifixion of Duncan Drake, the parking lot assault of her brother Raymond, and Hunter Sabuani's annihilation of Eugene Robinson. Since her arrival, she has also assisted in making public the illicit affair between Jonathan Rhine and Sarah Riley (the latter of whom is now in her employ). Where her plans will take her from here is unclear; she states her intention is to claim the federation, though one could only guess as to the purpose. The only thing that is sure is that there will be bodies in the way, and a tall woman with blood-red hair will stand over them. And she will be laughing. THE DEAD MAN'S HAND Katsidy: 5'9', 128 lbs. Hair: Brown. Eyes: Hazel. Theme Music: 'The Cheap and Evil Girl' - Bree Sharp. If you were to look up 'sexual predator' in the dictionary, you might just find Kathryn Shaw. Sure, she's not one of those pedophiles, but what Katsidy does is find people's weaknesses and exploit them. Even at 32 and with a son, she's tremendously easy on the eyes. She's a dyed-in-the-wool flirt and a veritable sociopath, and underselling her attractiveness would be like underselling Jason Cruise's pure wrestling ability -- even guys who don't want to admit it, guys who KNOW they should know better often find themselves caught up in her game, even just a little. Mr. Ashe: 6'6', 364 lbs. Hair: Brown. Eyes: Brown. Theme Music: 'Die MF Die' - Dope. The Security Consultant for the Inquisition has one rule: 'You Do Not Touch Alexandra Pierce.' A former four-time NWC tag-team champion, Asher's knees aren't built for long-term physical competition any more, but the New Zealander with the cigar serves pretty much the same purpose that Alan Branch does with FUSE Security -- he's the last bastion of 'stop right the fuck there'. He doesn't get involved in PHS' matches on a regular basis (there's a deepseated dislike between the two, even though Asher's fourth tag-title reign was as Hunter's partner). He shouldn't be beat up regularly, but he'll sacrifice himself to give Desade the chance to escape. Mr. Hawke: 5'11', 187 lbs. Hair: Black. Eyes: Brown. Theme Music: 'If You Talk Too Much (My Head Will Explode)' - People in Planes. Formerly three times NWC tag champion with Asher as part of Black Ops, Mr. Hawke is a different man now, since his left leg was nearly crippled in an accident. Now he walks with a cane and carries a chip on his shoulder. Biting, sarcastic, and unafraid to use his hard stone cane (with an intricately-carved hawk's head atop it) as a weapon. Hawke rarely actually accompanies the group to ringside, though, as his lack of mobility can be an impairment. He's more the backstage go-between for the group. Lauren Fox/Savant/The Orchid: 5'4', 119 lbs. Hair: Purple (dyed from brown). Eyes: Green. Theme Music: 'She's My Ride Home' - Blue October. Alex Pierce's protegee for a number of years and the mastermind behind the plot to bring her back, Lauren Fox has grown up and grown up cold. The opposite side of the coin of MacKenzie Malone, since Lauren has never led a normal life and doesn't give a fig about who she's wearing. Lauren's talents lie in infiltration and digging up information, secrets people rarely want uncovered. She's mean, she's uncaring, and she's more than willing to do the messy work that no one else is. She's rarely actually at an SCCW event, but if she shows up, there's likely a good (read: bad) reason for it. MacKenzie Malone: 5'7', 122 lbs. Hair: Black. Eyes: Green. Theme Music: 'Because I'm Awesome' - The Dollyrots. Have you ever made a really bad choice? MacKenzie is right there with you. First met by FUSE fans as the snotty valet for 'The Hollywoord Hellion' Duncan Drake, it quickly became apparent there was more to with Malone than met the eye -- a theory that proved correct when she was revealed to be the signal-pirate's (apparently reluctant) accomplice. But with Desade, nothing is what it seems, and now she stands at the Dead Man's Hand's side. There are times that there appears to be some thing seriously wrong psychologically with this girl. Other times, she seems like a regular girl, the most normal of the lot of them. Other Feds National Wrestling Council [Lone Star Wrestling (as Desade and Sascha), Hawaii Island Wrestling, NWC: Pacific (as Bloody Mary)], Diamond Wrestling Syndicate. Other Fed Awards GTT7 Champion; Manager of the three-time NWC Tag Team Champions, Black Ops. Manager of the final NWC Tag Team Champions, The Fallen/The Glorious Bastards. First woman to ever challenge for the NWC title (a feat she performed - unsuccessfully - twice). Wrestling Style Martial arts/brawler mixed with some eclectic moves picked up during her experiences. Finisher Move The Spider's Kiss Finisher Description A falling double-knee jawbreaker (best known as Chris Jericho's Codebreaker) Finisher Setup Move None Setup Description None. Other Moves Wrestling Style: Part Japanese strong style, part luchador. High-speed, high-octane. An insane spot waiting to happen. Takes a licking and keeps on ticking. Smarter than hell. Finisher(s): 1.) The Spider's Kiss 2.) (rare/big match) The Master Mind Finisher Description(s): 1.) Jumping double-knee facebreaker (the Codebreaker) 2.) Spinning unprettier. Setup(s): The Spider's Kiss is literally designed to come out of nowhere, kind of a BANG and over feeling. The Master Mind needs something to dizzy them for the setup. Special Moves: (min 10, max 25) Asian mist (rare) Dragon whip kick Head punt (Amy Campbell's 'Bitchkiller').
