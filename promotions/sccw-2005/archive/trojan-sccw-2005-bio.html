Trojan Biography Real Name Eric Trojan Nicknames The Tank Hometown Chicago, illinois Birthday 19/08/1981 Height 6'3 Weight 270 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Nothing is known of Trojan, except his will and desire to progress in the business. He turned up at the door to Fuse one day, carrying only a gymn bag, and one suitcase. Having no home in or around the FUSE headquarters, Trojan has left his home to search for a career in Wrestling Entertainment. Eric left FUSE in mysterious circumstances just a few weeks after his debut for the company. It has become apparant that the reasons for his sudden departure were financial. He was, at the time, merely situated in the FUSE buildings, sleeping, and living. However, the arrangement soon fell on sour grounds, and Trojan had no choice but to leave to make some cash before returning. He went underground, doing what he knew best...fighting. Trojan took to the underground illegal fighting network, polishing off opponents in order to earn enough to get a small apartment and some food. The vastly more experienced Trojan, who it is now believed had been involved in fighting circuits for many years prior to his entry into FUSE, made enough cash to put his fears behind him. He now returns to FUSE, as passionate as ever about making it in the big time.
