Scarab Biography Real Name Jonathan Allen Wade (his name, however, is legally Scarab) Nicknames The Scourge of the Desert Sands, the Theban Nightmare Hometown Thebes, Egypt Birthday January 5, 1982 Height 7'1' Weight 363 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography The vast majority of what is known about Scarab comes from his own mouth in an interview conducted in 2005, following his signing with Four Winds Wrestling. In it, he spoke for what he said would be (and has been) the last time he talked about his former life in public. Born Jonathan Allen Wade to upper class parents in Montclair, NJ, he was an offensive lineman throughout high school, eventually playing at Montclair State University. Majoring in history, he graduated college with an excellent GPA, and applied to graduate school, eventually landing at Rutgers University hoping to get his Masters degree in history. For his thesis, he went to Egypt, having been long-fascinated with the country and its history. His stated project was to research the rumored existence of various cults in the area, dedicated to the worship of ancient Egyptian gods and goddesses. What he found, he claims, changed his life forever. Exactly what he saw is up for debate. Most, however, accept that the story his Masters thesis tells of a cult dedicated to worshiping a claimed Setite vampiric demi-goddess is at least partially true. Upon recieving his Masters degree, however, Wade returned to Egypt, to devote himself to the sect. Renouncing his name, shaving his head, and having his body tattooed with hieroglyphs in the ancient, painful style, he was rechristened 'Scarab' by the demigoddess, a being named Sekhet, and soon rose to become her right hand. The nature of the relationship between Scarab and Sekhet was the subject of many rumors, both in 4WW and in the cult's headquarters in Thebes. It is a relationship that has never quite fully been explained. Scarab and Sekhet left 4WW almost as quickly as they arrived, the Theban Nightmare having captured the East Wind (hardcore) championship before exiting. Now, years later, Scarab has resurfaced in the professional wrestling world, as a member of Sapientes Gladio. One can only imagine that Sekhet has charged him with another mission, one that has brought him into the SG fold. Whatever his purpose, Scarab is not a man to be trifled with. Other Feds Four Winds Wrestling Other Fed Awards 4WW East Wind Champion Wrestling Style Powerhouse with surprising agility for his size. Finisher Move Song of Sekhet Finisher Description Two handed sitout chokebomb Finisher Setup Move Goblet of Blood Setup Description Sheamus-style pump kick, thrown to the face. Other Moves As the agile powerhouse of Sapientes Gladio, Scarab takes considerably more risks than the rest of the group. Also has arguably the least extensive moveset of the group, but makes up for this by the fact that absolutely everything he does is designed to put an opponent through the mat and out of their misery. Strikes: - Hard forearms thrown to the face, chest, and back. - Kitchen sink kneelift - Running shoulder tackle - Repeated elbowdrops (two or three) to a downed opponent, followed by a cover. - Standing big splash. - Running avalanche in the corner. - Big boot. Submissions: - Canadian Backbreaker Rack - Bearhug Power Moves: - Gorilla Press Slam - Repeated Backbreaker (Brock Lesnar style) - Tossing Flapjack - Electric Chair Drop - Press Slam Gutbuster - Throwing Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex Aerial: - Second and top rope big splashes - Second rope elbowdrop - Second rope diving/falling headbutt - Superplex - Belly to Belly Superplex - Top Rope Electric Chair Drop (very rare) Signature Moves: Forced Reverence: Rag-doll style Full Nelson. Scarab will wring the opponent around with the move for a few seconds, before disdainfully throwing them face first to the canvas. Setite Sacrament: Vader-style pump splash off the second rope. His favorite aerial tactic. He will sometimes use it with the opponent face down to work on the back for the Song of Sekhet. Hand of Goddess: Single-handed choke-lift into straight right hand to the jaw. Also a viable set-up for the Song of Sekhet, if he so chooses. Finishing Move: Song of Sekhet: Two-handed sit-down chokebomb. He likes to deadlift the opponent with the choke from their back into the move. He also likes to catch unwary opponents coming off the top rope with it. Against most opponents, he will simply hold the position after the move for the cover. Against opponents he feels are tougher, or who might kick out, he executes a proper cover with a hooked leg. Entrance Music 'Curse of the Pharaohs' by Mercyful Fate
