F. Samson Hamilton-Locke III Biography Real Name F. Samson Hamilton-Locke III Nicknames Mr. Everything Hometown New York, NY Birthday Feb 16, 1982 Height 6'4'' Weight 252 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography F. Samson Hamilton-Locke the Third is the heir to the famed Hamilton-Locke fortune and Hamilton-Locke Enterprises, one of the most influential companies in the world. Growing up with a life of privilege beyond imagination, he decided to try his hand at wrestling out of a bout of extreme boredom when the country slipped into recession and his companies couldn't easily rip off consumers for vast amounts of money. He knows almost nothing about wrestling, but was able to use his vast fortune and high-powered contacts to work his way into the league because he thinks it will be amusing to commit violence on others legally. Generally surrounded by lawyers, lackeys, and yes-men, he's beyond obvlivious to anything resembling the real world and isn't used to not getting his way. In fact, he's probably absolutely always gotten everything he's ever wanted. Wrestling proves to be the first time in his life that he may face people who don't immediately bow to his every whim.
