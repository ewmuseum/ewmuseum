Mayson Colby Biography Real Name Mayson Colby Nicknames None Hometown San Diego, CA Birthday June 1, 1982 Height 6'2' Weight 215 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Mayson had a semi-normal childhood. He is the oldest of 5 children. Although his mother passed away at a young age, he grew up with everything he ever needed, and enjoyed school. After graduating high school, Mayson attended the Culinary Academy in San Francisco where he graduated at the top of his class. He was set to start a new job at a posh restaurant there in the city, but suddenly disappeared for 4 years. Nothing is known of what he was doing during this time. When he emerged his personality had changed completely. Mayson is now a very reserved person. He has his own weekly cooking show which is fairly popular. Not much is known about his past, although it is thought to be full of secrets and oddities. This is sometimes apparent on his show with his frequent flashbacks and daydreams. Wrestling Style Brawler, Hardcore Finisher Move The Grinder Finisher Description This is a belly-to-back powerbomb, usually beginning in the back suplex position. Mayson stands behind his opponent and puts his head under the arm his opponent.
