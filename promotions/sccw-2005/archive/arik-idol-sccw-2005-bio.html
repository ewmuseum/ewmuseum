Arik Idol Biography Hometown Santa Cruz, CA Birthday 1/18/84 Height 6'3' Weight 264 Biography History Arik Idol was born to Randy and LeAnne Idol on May 1st, 1984 in Raleigh, North Carolina. Randy Idol was CEO and Founder of his company, Starpolish Inc. Randy was involved in Hollywood as both an agent to actors and an active director and actor. He was very well known throughout Hollywood as a very big potential director. His company was at one time the most sought after place to work for in Hollywood. Randy was the king, and there was no question about it. LeAnne was a former model/actress that left the Hollywood scene with the birth of Arik. She dedicated her time to raising him. Arik grew up in Raleigh, North Carolina in a plush mansion. He had a very easy childhood, and he was a member of one of the richest families in the state. Arik started showing athletic abilities at the very young age of 5 where he started to play Pee Wee hockey for his school. As he moved on towards Junior High School, he also picked up Basketball, Football and Wrestling. He excelled most at Hockey and Wrestling, lettering in both every year of Junior High and High School, after moving to Santa Cruz. In High School, Arik was the 'gifted' kid. He was seemingly perfect at everything to do with school. He was the star Hockey Captain, Wrestling Captain, and maintained a 3.0, or often a 4.0. Everything seemed fine on the surface for Arik during his senior year of High School, but it was far from it. His father had somehow gotten himself involved with a notorious drug ring in Santa Cruz, and had made several dangerous enemies along the way. One day, Arik's father left him an envelope on the kitchen table with documents that gave Starpolish Inc. over to him. Arik's father never returned home. Some say he skipped town, while others say that his dealings within the California drug scene led to his murder. Nobody knows for sure, but Arik has not seen nor heard from him since that day. Arik took the company, and renamed it to Starstruck Entertainment. Arik also began to do some acting work, landing several supporting roles in major motion pictures. He never really got to be the 'star' of any of the movies, but he was always remembered as being an excellent supporting actor. The only time Arik was ever offered the main role was when there were talks of a movie being made about his father's life. He refused to take the part, and even more so, refused to acknowledge the movie was ever made. Arik still dabbles in and out of acting on occassion. He actively runs Starstruck Entertainment. It wasn't until early 2003 that Arik was even involved in pro wrestling. There was a small promotion based out of Santa Cruz, California called Wicked Domination Wrestling. It was run by a very much unknown wrestling promoter named Dylan Gagne. Though he claimed to be of the illustrious Gagne wrestling family, he wasn't in any relation. But that wasn't important to Arik. Dylan called Arik after seeing a tape of his amateur wrestling in high school. He informed Arik that he was trying to bring some credibility to his federation, and he wanted an athlete like Arik. Arik didn't take the proposal too seriously, it was more of a game for him to play. He showed up to his first show, and was immediately given a match. Arik had not come prepared, so he wrestled in street clothes, and got a win via submission with the basic cradle of amateur wrestling. After time, Arik became a WDW regular, taking their 'X-Division' by storm in an epic feud with 'Pretty Boy' Aaron Armani. Pretty Boy was one of the biggest fan favorites in the company, as was Arik. The fued is remembered as one of the greatest the short existence of the WDW witnessed. Arik and Armani had their final blowout match at the last Pay Per View show for WDW, 'Event Omega'. They had a ladder match where Arik lost the X-Division title. The WDW then closed, leaving Arik without a permanent job. He began getting invites to tour Japan with assorted promotions, an opportunity that would prove to be very beneficial to him. His name got around Japan incedibly fast, and back to the states to some of the bigger federations. Arik loved working for the Japanese promoters, but there was something he wanted that they couldn't offer. He really wanted the hollywood glitz that came with primetime U.S. wrestling. Arik had many tryout matches with many feds, but didn't find anywhere he wanted to be. He was accepted into FUSE Wrestling recently, and plans to give the U.S. wrestling scene something to remember. Other Feds WDW, GW Other Fed Awards WDW X-Division Wrestling Style Aggressive Technician Finisher Move Whiplash Effect Finisher Description Wristclutch Exploder Suplex Other Moves Finisher: The Whiplash Effect The Whiplash Effect is commonly known as a 'wristclutch exploder suplex'. Idol first hooks the person as if he were to execute a rock bottom, but he also pumphandles their left arm between their legs and secures it with his left hand gripping their wrist. He then lifts them up and falls backwards, sending the opponent hard to the mat onto their completely unprotected neck and shoulders. As far as general moves are concerned, Idol is pretty old school. Since his finish focuses around the neck, he likes to target that area in his general offense. Trademark Moves Spinning Brainbuster Jumping Knee to back of head Bridging Double Underhook Suplex Release Tiger Suplex Box Office Smash: This is a move that's commonly used to weaken the neck to the breaking point for the Whiplash Effect. It's a simple flying elbow drop to the back of the neck, but with 264 lbs. behind it, it's quite impactful. Entrance Music Big Gun by AC/DC
