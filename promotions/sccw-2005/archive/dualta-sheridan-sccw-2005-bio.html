Dualta Sheridan Biography Real Name Dualta Sheridan Hometown Queens, New York Birthday 9/2/1985 Height 6'4' Weight 190 Fed Titles No as of yet Fed Awards Just gimme time Biography New York is a thriving city with a population that believes highly in tradition and culture. However, there is an underbelly, whether or not this underbelly is considered dark is a matter of perception. To say this portrait of New York is one of crime and disorder would be a huge misconception for it is the very fabric that keeps stability in an unstable world. Within this underworld live America’s most notorious gangs; The various Italian mafia families, the Chinese Triads, Japanese Yakuza, the vicious Russians etc. However, for years now the most prominent organisation of all were an Irish family, the Sheridan’s. The Sheridan family immigrated to America in the mid 1960’s due to a promise of a better life. When they arrived they began to question the integrity of said promise. Work was hard to come by and any jobs they did get would barely cover the rent. They all considered packing up and heading home to Ireland but this was not possible. Peter Sheridan sent his seven children out to America to build a prosperous life and refused to let them arrive home with their tails between their legs. Though, the only one who found any glimpse of hope was Peter’s only daughter, Fiona. Fiona began dating a young Irish lawyer who was setting up his own practice in Manhattan. She began to lose contact with her six brothers in a bid to construct a life of considerable wealth for herself. So now it was just the six Sheridan brothers with no positive outlooks on the horizon. There was only one thing they could turn to. The one thing they were all good at, fighting. Slowly but surely the Sheridan name grew more eminent. They were respected, and with respect came success. As the years went on they all married, they all had children and they all had profitable, above-board businesses. Though it was in the aforementioned underbelly of New York were their real achievements were accomplished. They were the premiere mob in the state of New York, fair but intimidating. They avoided killing at all costs but would beat you to within an inch of your life if you opposed them. Donnacha, the eldest of the Sheridan brothers was the leader, he called the shots. Donnacha was well-educated and knew how to run things, but he was perhaps to kind-hearted, he refused to put a bullet in anyone. However, times were changing, gangs were getting more and more sadistic, and eventually it was the Russians that called the shots. Every Sheridan business acquisition was seized and the Sheridan family was driven out, unable to appear in public due to a bounty on each of their heads. The Russians were brutal and had large numbers, this was their strength. Despite ruling for so long the Sheridan’s never had the numbers of many of their rivals. They did not trust you unless you were family. It was their greatest strength, but their eventual downfall. Now, in an abandoned apartment building west of Queens Boulevard, the forty-seven members of the Sheridan family reside, in squalor and near poverty, without heat or electricity. At night the clamour of children’s cries echo through the rooms, few can sleep, few want to sleep because who’s to say if they will wake? The Russians know they are there, but as long as they stay there, and pay a monthly ‘rental’ fee, they live. The problem is, how do they pay this bill? Fiona sends money from time to time, though it is not widely appreciated. She does not so much as visit her family. She is more than happy in her penthouse on the upper-east side of Manhattan to worry about the going-on of Queens Boulevard. That is where Dualta comes in. He is the only son of Donnacha and is determined to aid the survival of his family. For months now he has fought underground in an attempt to pay the Russians what they want, but with his father, his uncles and his cousins all picking up small change here and there it has not been easy. He needs something bigger, he was blessed with the one key Sheridan skill, the skill to fight with speed and conviction. This skill may be all he has in his attempt to ensure the restoration of the Sheridan name. Other Feds SCCW is Dualta's first attempt at Pro Wrestling Wrestling Style Basic Muay Thai, street fighter. Though he is tall and has a certain amount of physical strength, he relies more on his speed and problem solving abilities to fight. He is composed and chooses his moments. Finisher Move Spinning heel kick (Dualta has no signatures names for his moves) Finisher Description FUll 360 body turn used to elevate leg. Heel connects with opponents face. Finisher Setup Move Jab-Jab-Punch Setup Description Two hard left handed jabs before a high impact right with follow through Other Moves Combinations of kicks mainly to legs and midsection but sometimes high impact headshots are executed. 360 sweep - Opponents legs are swiped as Dualta spins and trips oppoent with the back of his leg. Combination of strikes - Uses alot of boxing techniques and goes very hard to the body and is very effective at counter punches to the face. Has basic muay thai knowledge so is very capable ground fighter and is good in subimission scenarios. Very good problem solver and mainly a counter fighter.
