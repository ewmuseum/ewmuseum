Jahziel Vorster Biography Nicknames Jazz Hometown Unknown Birthday Unknown Height 6'7 Weight 290 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography We do not know much of Jahziel Vorster's history. The reason is, he does not know his own history. It is estimated that at 22 years of age 'Jazz' suffered a blow to the head that caused amnesia. He forgot his past and had no family or friends to help him recover his memories. Instead he found himself on the streets tormented by past dreams of a rough childhood, gangs, beatings from an older man and a girl that he loved. Unable to piece together where he was from, Jazz found himself competing in street fights to make enough money to support his alcohol and drug condition. Carling Price, an aspiring wrestler who never had the size or skill to compete, decided the only way to success in the business he loves is by managing. Finding several gimmicks fail, Carling found Jazz on the street competing and after several conversations he decided that he could easily manipulate the strange man into believing anything. Carling promised to take him under his wing, and give him glory and success as well as find his family. Instead, Carling has driven Jazz from wrestling promotion to wrestling promotion, watching him dominate and cashing in on his success. Now with Sin City Championship Wrestling calling, Carling is extremely pleased that his new 'tool' has brought the glory Carling has always dreamed of. Carling Price is the mouthpiece and Jazz is the dominating powerhouse in the ring.
