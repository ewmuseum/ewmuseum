Gunnar Saint Biography Real Name Gunnar Jakob Saint Nicknames The Hellhound; The Thrillbilly; The Southern Slayer Hometown Perdition, KS Birthday 10/31/82 Height 6'6' Weight 270 Biography Like it fucking matters. Wrestling Style Confederate Ass-kicking Finisher Move The 13th Ghost Finisher Description I drop them on their ugly ass head, making them a good 6 or 7 inches shorter. (Styles Clash setup, but instead of falling forward, he plunges to his knees, spiking the opponent's head into the mat.) **Also, if he hits the Springwood Slasher and Maxim Finisher Setup Move Maximum Overdrive Setup Description The baddest motherfuckin' spear you ever saw. Other Moves **Habits and style** -He talks as much smack as possible during the match. -When a foe is down, he rarely uses submissions. He would prefer to just strike the foe with punches or elbows. The Anaconda Vice is his favorite submission, while Judah is for those pesky cruiserweights. -He is a striker. He doesn't counter holds with other holds. He punches, kicks, headbutts, elbows, and such to escape. He only strays from that to counter with a power move. -He is trained in Thai kickboxing and played linebacker for Univeristy of Georgia. -He only goes to the top rope when he feels he has to up the stakes. -When he goes to the top, it is a slingshot up there in one fluid motion like RVD or Sean O'Haire. -The Grendel Twins are trained in a high flying style of puroresu, but they often use the numbers game. One will distract the referee or foe while the other assists Gunnar when they feel he needs it. When distracting, they will do ANYTHING. Let your imagination go wild. **Signature Moves** -Amittyville Horror -Springwood Slasher- Shining Wizard to standing foe in the corner. -Crystal Lake Killer- Pumphandle Piledriver. -The Shape- A swanton bomb. -Right Hand of Doom- A combination of punches ending with a huge right-handed spinning backhand. -Cenobite- He lifts the foe as if he is going for a vertical suplex or jackhammer. Instead of going into what looks like a powerslam executing a jackhammer, he rotates so the foe comes down on his shoulders like a fireman's carry, as he slams them. -Ride of the Horseman- Looks like he is executing a Kryptonite Krunch or Air Raid Crush, but instead of falling to his bottom, he leaps and lands on one knee, slamming the back of the foe's neck across his thigh. -The Descent- A cross arm falcon arrow. -DDT-Virus- A cross arm DDT. -Those We Do Not Speak Of- He locks his foe in a standing dragon sleeper. He drops to one knee, dropping the foe back first on his knee. Keeping the hold intact, he delivers a christian cage style reverse DDT. Still not letting go, he rolls his foe over to their stomach and locks in a dragon sleeper. -The Creeper- A springboard lariat. -Leatherface- He sits his opponent on the top turnbuckle and captures their arm and head. He pulls the opponent out from the corner, extending them outward with their feet hooking the top rope. He then quickly falls to his back, hitting a flatliner. (Think of the setup as the same as Kevin Thorn's Original Sin or Jamie Noble's corner DDT.) **Standard Moves** -Nail in the Coffin/Michinoku Driver -Standing release tigerbomb -An Arn Anderson like Spinebuster -A standing enziguiri -Head and arm suplex -T-bone suplex -Release northern lights suplex -Anaconda Vice -Samoan Drop (Can be set up in any way possible.) -Sitout Catatonic -Sitout Rock Bottom aka Bronx Bomb -Various punch, kick, elbow, or knee combinations -Judah, look it up so I don't have to explain it. Entrance Music 'The Way I Am' by Eminem
