Sean Sterling Biography Real Name Sean Sterling Nicknames The Third Revelation Hometown Home isn't where the heart is. Home is where the money is. Birthday May 31st, 1980 Height 6'1' Weight 200 Biography This will probably never be filled out. Just ignore it. Wrestling Style A bullet to your fucking head. Finisher Move Hail to the Thief Finisher Description Corkscrew Shooting Star Press Finisher Setup Move Kill Your Idols Setup Description A backflip kick, with one leg catching his opponent beneath the chin and the other kicking him in the face as he falls. Other Moves Signature Movies: 1) Je T'aime - Superkick to the heart. That's how the French say 'I love you.' 2) Debaser - Dragonarana. In which, Sterling debases your will to live. 3) Fade Out - 630° senton splash. Contrary to popular belief, Sterling doesn't disappear after this move is performed. 4) Casual Discourse - Backflip DDT. It hurts. Trust me. 5) Kill Kill Kill - Fireman's carry to double knee gutbuster. Performed on lightweights, midgets, and women who belong in the kitchen. 6) Die Die Die - A flip piledriver, ie, Petey Williams 'Canadian Destroyer'. Needless to say, Sterling wants you to die when he goes for this one. 7) Caveat - Enziguri. The word is Latin for Beware. In other words, beware of this move. Get it? No? Fuck. 8) Bury the Hatchet - Tornado DDT. While Sterling would much rather bury an actual hatchet in your cranium, I doubt that'd fly with the censors. 9) Kamikaze - Swanton bomb. The kamikaze pilots of WWII would fly planes into their enemies as a last ditch effort, effectively killing their enemies and themselves. Sterling doesn't have a plane, so he uses his body instead. 10) Pardon The Blood - After getting his opponent on his/her knees, Sterling kicks his opponent in the side of the head as hard as possible. This usually results in gushing blood, terrible migraines, possible concussions, and hopefully a seizure or two. Entrance Music 'Electioneering' by Radiohead
