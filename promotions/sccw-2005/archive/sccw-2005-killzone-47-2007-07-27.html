Killzone 47 Indianapolis, Indiana ---- Paul Cain vs Prince Pride In the only undercard match of the evening, These two FUSE Newcomers came to the ring with a lot of energy. The fans took a liking to Paul Cain as he ran to the ring, slapping their hands as he did so. Prince Pride took a different route, coming to the ring and mouthing off to nearby fans. The two locked up in the center of the ring with Cain getting the early advantage with a few arm drags and hip tosses. Cain then went to work on various parts of Pride's body, locking in numerous submission holds. After a few minutes of offense a thumb to the eye halted that momentum and allowed Prince Pride to take over. Paul Cain looked to regain his advantage, but underhanded tactics from Prince Pride allowed him to stay in the driver's seat. Pride nailed a very nice brainbuster followed by a DDT for a very close two count. Paul Cain fed of the energy of the crowd and made a comeback, causing both men to spill to the floor. They battled outside with Cain eventually nailing a neckbreaker onto the concrete at the count of seven.
