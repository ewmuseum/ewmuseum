Nigel huddles outside a door right next to the staff & performer parking lot, his hands forming a barrier around the cigarette, trying to get a successful light.  Flurries hang in the air, slowly descending to the pavement. And speaking of that. A side door that leads to the backstage area opens, and Nigel doesn't even turn to look who it is.  He only hopes that whoever it may be, they don't know who he is.  He has no desire to be bothered.  He just wants to smoke his cigarette, and get ready for the evening, and oh what a humbling evening it seemed to be.  How much faith could the others really have left?  He had admitted to himself on several occasions that if he were in their shoes, certain alternatives would be considered.
