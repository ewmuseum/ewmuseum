C.J. Davidge Biography Real Name Charles John Davidge Nicknames CJ, Ceej Hometown La Jolla, California Birthday February 1, 1981 Height 6'2' Weight 235 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Born in 1981 in La Jolla, California, CJ's lived a good life so far. His parents raised him properly, his father an accountant and his mother an elementary school teacher. After high school, where CJ was an all-league linebacker, CJ went to CSU Fresno on an academic/athletic scholarship. He went for two years, but dropped out to pursue a career in (whatelse?) professional wrestling. CJ's bounced around from indy promotion to indy promotion over the years, but now he's finally looking to make a name for himself on a big stage in the wrestling world.
