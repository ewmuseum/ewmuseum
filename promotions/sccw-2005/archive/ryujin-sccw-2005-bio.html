Ryujin Biography Real Name Kristian Lock Nicknames Black Dragon Hometown Pittsburgh, PA Birthday 9/12/1981 Height 6'2' Weight 224 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography RYUJIN was born Kristian Lock. He grew up a wrestling fan, having watched since he was 6. As he grew older, he got exposure to the Japanese wresting scene. When he finished high school, he went to a local wrestling school to train. After a year in the system, he wrestled in the Pennsylvania area before moving up to Canada. After some time there, he moved down to Mexico. He didn't stay long as he used his own money to take a trip to Japan. He managed to find work in various micro indies while he continued to improve his skills. It was in Japan that he received the name RYUJIN after years of working as Black Dragon. A few years later, he was considered the best talent not working for a big 'name' promotion. Frustrated with the level of talent he was working with, on top of the low interest being shown by bigger Japanese promotions, RYUJIN returned to America. At the age of 26, he holds more experience that most of his age. Other Feds NEW, BTW, TCW, IWE Other Fed Awards 2x NEW International Champ, 1x BTW US Champ, 1x IWE Hardcore Champ Wrestling Style Super Junior Finisher Move Dragon Buster Finisher Description Side carry Emreld Fusion (http://puroresucentral.com/moves/HThunder.wmv) Finisher Setup Move Dragon's Call Setup Description RYUJIN lifts the opponent in a suplex. Instead of falling back, he sends the opponent forward before dropping into a kneeling position. As he kneels, the opponent's face connects with the knee RYUJIN has up. Finishing Move 260 lbs+ - Dead Star Bomb: An over-rotated shooting star press turned into a senton bomb. Big Match Finisher - Black Dragon Driver: RYUJIN locks the opponent in a double arm suplex. But instead of falling back, he shifts his opponent to the side as he sits down, dropping them on their head/neck. Desperation Finisher - Shining Wizard (Usually done out of the corner.)
