Temptation 07/13 Bud Walton Arena — Fayetteville, Arkansas ---- A History Lesson Las Vegas, Nevada. Gambling capital of the world. Home of Sin City Championship Wrestling. And, more importantly for our purposes, the home of "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy. More specifically, we are in the Stardust Casino's poker room. Presumably, some palms have been greased, because the Big Stack is alone at a table, running his fingertips over the felt. To his right is but a single deck of cards, which he pats the top of slowly. His fingers rise to run across the brim of his ever-present black Stetson hat, as he prepares to spin a tale. Kennedy: The year is 1876. In a saloon of the Dakota Territory, Wild Bill Hickok is playing the poker game of the time, five card draw. He discards, and then draws his new ammunition. By the time he's done drawing, he's holding a hand of two aces and two eights. Of course, we all know what happens next. Ol' Bill's life went south from there: six feet south. But there's a question that people have been asking for years: just what WAS that last card in his hand? After all, the Dead Man's Hand is two pair, aces and eights...but a proper poker hand has five cards. From the top of the deck of cards, he deals out the ace of clubs, followed by the ace of spades. Kennedy: Some people say that he never got to draw that last card, so the Dead Man's Hand only has four cards in it. Me, I think that's a load of crap. No gambler's taking cards one at a time. This is not the way it was supposed to happen. The title is his, and it came, not with an exultant bang, but a whimper. Fade out slowly to the SCCW logo. Fin.
