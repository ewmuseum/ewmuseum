Gregory Orion Daniels Biography Real Name Gregory Daniels Nicknames The Future, The Chosen One, G.O.D. Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Birthday 6/19/79 Height 6'4' Weight 235 Biography 'The Future' Gregory Orion Daniels ran a very popular online sports-betting service out of Reno, NV. His company took in millions every week from their most rich and dedicated followers. Gregory Orion Daniels was on fire and was untouchable at his gambling prediction however with the money and fame came his ego complex and unlike others who had a 'God-Like' complex. Gregory took it to a new level making people call him the God of Gambling. He could turn a dollar unlike anyone before and anyone after however his ego began losing him business and as his image began to falter so did everything else in his life falling like domino's one after another until he wound up in a sleazy Reno Hotel where he saw none other than Syn who was equally down on his luck. Daniels still having a small 'nest egg' tucked away from his former days supporting him through one of the worst parts of his life. Daniels showed Syn support and showed Syn that another person who was just as down could still care. Syn promised if they could get on his feet he would repay Daniels one way or another and finally the chance came when wrestling began to knock on Syn's door again. Syn offered to pay Daniels back but Daniels refused only asking to have Syn take him along. Daniels always loved watching wrestling since he was an infant and now he wanted to see if he he can turn his life around and find success in a wrestling ring. Once he could predict the future but can 'The Future' overcome a Wrestling Ring?
