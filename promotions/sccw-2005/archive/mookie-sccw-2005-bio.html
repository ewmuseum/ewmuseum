Mookie Biography Real Name Mooklian Vincent Amery Nicknames hXc prodigy Hometown Long Beach, CA Birthday July 14, 1987 Height 5'9' Weight 210 Fed Titles none Fed Awards none Biography To the ignorant, a first glance at Mookie and he would automatically be labeled 'emo'. But to the wise, to the informed, to the people who are 'hip' and up to date with the 'scene', Mookie falls into the catagory of 'hardcore'. Typical 'hardcore' kids obviously resemble their 'emo' counterparts in look and style, and most do endure the same tragic lives, spiraling down into a dark abyss. The only difference is, 'hardcore' kids such as Mookie seek out to change the way things are. You won't see Mookie sitting around crying like a bitch or cutting his wrists, no, you'll see Mookie take action. That being said, the horrible life he has lived has built a fortress of many different emotions up inside poor Mookie.. One of which being anger. Which has obviously lead to what he is today - a professional wrestler. Now like most 'hardcore' kids, Mookie has a knack for being quite arrogant. As a matter of fact, very arrogant. Arrogant in his beliefs, arrogant in his ideals, and arrogant in his skills. Anything Mookie believes in or thinks is automatically better than anything anyone else believes or thinks. His taste in music, his religion (Christianity), his views on love, politics, etc. etc. And like most 'hardcore' kids, Mookie loves his music.. Almost more than anything. 'Sides his girlfriend, Kailee. There is only one key thing that sets Mookie apart from most other 'hardcore' kids is his ability to stray away from the 'straight edge' lifestyle. Mookie enjoys drinking.. As a matter of fact, he is quite the alcoholic. But we won't go any further into that.. Just yet. Mookie only began his training as a professional wrestler in March of 2005, when he joined AoH Training Academy. He spent only four months there before finally entering the wrestling scene in July 2005. Mookie won his first professional match ever, beating one of the biggest men to ever step foot in a ring, The Animal. Mookie faced defeat in his second match, however, as he lost due to outside interference. Mookie rebounded and soon won the XCW Television Championship and went on to become the longest reigning champ that title had ever known. He carried the title for only seven weeks, but had to defend the title each and every week. Eventually Mookie was plagued with exhaustion as his arrogance got the best of him and he tried to take a double load, joining XCW's long-time rival, UWA. He got off to a hot start, winning his first two matches, but soon had a physical collapse. He lost his last few matches in UWA, as well as finally losing his XCW Television Title in a grueling triple threat match. This, along with Mookie's girlfriend Kailee (also his manager), convinced him it was time for a break. With the emergence of FUSE, Mookie is contemplating a return to the world of professional wrestling. It may be just a matter of time before he and his lovely girlfriend, Kailee, make their return once again..
