Wired 1.7 Philips Arena — Atlanta, Georgia ---- Champ for a Reason The darkness of the screen transforms into a view of a familiar setting - the gym. At this early hour, 06:30 according to the large wall clock that overlooks proceedings, the gym is deserted save for one man. That figure, it turns out, is none other than our Universal champion. Finishing another set, the dumbbells are replaced in the rack and he seemingly notices the camera for the first time. "They said I couldn't do it, that Majin wasn't going to let his in-ring return end in anything other than a comprehensive victory. Well, as is so often the case, they were wrong." The sweat that drips of his face is testament to the intensity of the workout and Jackson pauses now, wiping it off his face with a fresh white towel before taking a swig of some unidentified energy drink. "Majin was the one staring at the bright lights come the end of Wired last week, while it was I who had my hand raised in victory. Now in any other occupation, a win of this magnitude would warrant a pat on the back and a well-deserved week off. Here in FUSE though, we do things a little differently." The champion gestures for the camera to follow him as he makes his way down to the far end of the gym, past rows of inactive exercise bikes, cross-trainers and rowing machines. When he stops, his eyes are focused on the screens affixed to the wall - screens which show the face of Damien Cruz. "This week, Damien Cruz has a shot at becoming the man and taking this title from me. Cruz is a dangerous competitor, you don't get a moniker like 'The Latin Assassin' without good reason. Of course, this time I'm not being written off quite so quickly. Amazing what a victory of the right person can do for your credibility." Lifting up a small object from the seat of one of the bikes, Jackson pushes a button and the screens abruptly go dark. He turns back to the camera now, looking directly into it. "Cruz, you're good, but the belt that I hold means that I'm that little bit better. And after what went down last week, I think this company could do with a new legend..."
