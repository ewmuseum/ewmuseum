Athen Biography Real Name Currently unknown. Nicknames The Enlightened, Wisdom Unmatched, the Chessmaster Hometown Billed as being born in Athens, Greece, and now residing in Alexandria, Egypt Birthday Unknown Height 6'8' Weight 301 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography If little is known about Thanatos and Scarab, practically nothing at all is known about Athen, the man considered by many to be at least the vocal leader of Sapientes Gladio. What his silver mask hides is largely unknown, as are his motivations for wearing it. He is the only masked member of the group, leading to considerable speculation. When asked, he simply says that he is Athen, the Enlightened, member of Sapientes Gladio. It doesn't take an enlightened being, though, to suspect that there is more here than meets the eye. Other Feds None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Technical powerhouse Finisher Move Checkmate Finisher Description Jackhammer, followed by the Anaconda Vice Finisher Setup Move Kick to the gut? Setup Description That works. Other Moves Strikes: European uppercut Ridgehand clothesline Palm strikes to the midsection Standing legdrop Kneedrop after bouncing off the ropes, Harley Race-style Submissions: Athen likes to use submissions to wear down opponents. As an opponent nears the ropes or nears breaking them, he likes to try to convert them into moves (ie, he'll slam opponents out of a cobra clutch) Torture rack Half Boston crab Fujiwara armbar Cross armbreaker STF Cobra Clutch Sleeper hold Abdominal stretch Front facelock/guillotine choke Power Moves: Press Slam dropped into a powerslam Lifting DDT (Edgecution) Suplex-lift Final Cut Inverted Bulldog Cobra Clutch Slam Iron Claw Slam Death Valley Driver Inverted Death Valley Driver (stomach bump landing) Signature Moves: Enlightenment: Torture Rack Backbreaker Sword of Damocles: Dominator. Often times can set up Checkmate. Offering to Olympus: Gourdbuster. Sometimes he throws the opponent with it (into things, on occassion), and sometimes he drives them into the mat. Finishing Move: Checkmate: Athen's version of the Jackhammer, transitioned into the Anaconda Vice for the submission. He holds the opponent in suplex position only long enough to get good snap on the way down, much in line with his general utilitarian philosophy towards the ring. With the wind driven out of his opponent, he immediately locks on the Anaconda Vice and cranks back on it. The purpose for this is twofold. If the opponent is out from the Jackhammer, Athen is three arm drops away from victory. If the opponent isn't out, the Anaconda Vice forces the opponent to expend more energy to escape it than would a kickout, making victory for Athen more likely.
