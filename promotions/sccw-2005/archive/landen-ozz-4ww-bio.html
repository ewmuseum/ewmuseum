Landen Ozz Biography Real Name Landen Larson Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday 3 - 20 - 80 Height 5'10' Weight 179 Fed Titles FUSE Livewire Champion Fed Awards N/A Biography Landen Ozz was born Landen Larson in Tacoma, Washington on March 20, 1980. It was from then on that Landen's life became a rollercoaster. At an early age Landen stuck out from his fellow pupils in school. He seemed to 'get it' when most kids wouldn't even comprehend things. Landen was smart, he was bored, and he was in a situation he needed to change. So, when he was 16 (in 1996) he dropped out and moved to Seattle where some of the punk bands he listened to at the time played. In Seattle, Landen became interrested in anarchism. He went to a few meetings, sorta got a taste for it. Within two years he had been egging police cars, spray painting graffiti, living a vegan straight edge lifestyle, and expressing his anger in the most anti-State ways possible. He had found a reason, and now he was finding his path. At the infamous WTO riots in Seattle, Landen had been working in the black bloc, facing the riot gear clad police... breathing in tear gas, and exhaling with passion and determination. It was from there on out that Landen was addicted, and after realizing he needed some kind of income, he turned to the other thing he was good at - beating people up. He wasn't a big guy, but he ran circles around others. He took a few courses, trained to become a wrestler, and as it turned out he was pretty good. Now, only time will tell what will happen next. Other Feds 4WW, SCW, EPW, IPWF, & SPW.
