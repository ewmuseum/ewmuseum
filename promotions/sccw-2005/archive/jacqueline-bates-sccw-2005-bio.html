Jacqueline Bates Biography Real Name Jacqueline Bates Nicknames The Seductress, The White Rose Hometown Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Birthday December 28, 1981 Height 5'5' Weight 124 Fed Titles None yet Fed Awards None yet Biography The thing about Jacqueline Bates is she’s never been in the right crowd of people. Even as a kid, she found it fun to hang around the drug dealers and fighters around town. It was just her way of being different than the pretty girls. As she grew older, she continued this trend and found herself around a group of rebel individuals. They were a ragtag bunch all out with the same mindset: hatred towards government and people around the popular crowd. Unfortunately for her, she didn’t know that her place in the group wasn’t friendship but more or less as a scapegoat. The men got together one day and took out a kid that looked at them in the wrong manner (crazy people). Little did they know, the kid they killed was actually Edward Carmony’s son. Edward was a man that was known around the underground world as the king. He was the guy that you didn’t want to cross. When he heard news of his son’s slaying, he immediately sent for the people who had done it to be apprehended and brought to him. When they found the group, Jacqueline was the one they took thanks to a tip from her so called ‘best friend’. She appeared before Carmony, confused as ever and pleaded for her life. As much as she showed distaste for it, she did really want to live. In her last desperation, she offered her services and life to Carmony. He obviously accepted and took advantage of her from day one. His favorite task for her is normally to send her off to collect bets or do the nasty job of breaking people in half. It may not be the best job but she has no other choice. That’s what she gets for wanting to live so bad. She took up wrestling in hopes that she could do something for herself for once. Edward still doesn’t know about it and Jacqueline hopes that’s how it stays. Other Feds None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Submissions | Straight fighter | Brawler Finisher Move The Love Letter Finisher Description Kimura-Bent armlock pushing in a painful direction against the shoulder Setup Description - Other Moves Moves: Elbow strikes, closed fist shots, knees to gut, Russian leg sweep, reverse falling DDT, crossbody attack, Texas cloverleaf, bulldog, jawbreaker, reverse neckbreaker, armbar variations, hammer lock, (if close to her size-snap suplex, side slam, tiger suplex) Writer’s Aid: When wrestling, Jacqueline is the kind of girl who will do whatever it takes to get the job done. She has no real heart when it comes to others, only the thirst to satisfy her needs. She will use underhanded tactics such as hits below the waist or slaps across the face. One thing she refuses to do however is utilize the ropes to gain a pinfall. As much as she wants to win, she doesn’t want it to be because of that. Jacqueline normally responds to pain. When she gets hurt she can find a new intensity to help turn a tide. In terms of submissions, they are probably her biggest weapon. JB has a wide knowledge of submission moves. One thing that is very important though, of all the body parts she works on, THE ARMS ARE THE BIGGEST. Her matches can build off of moves aimed at the arms to set-up a victory later through the pain of the Love Letter. She is a female star and therefore it is critical that it makes sense that she can’t lift up the bigger men and do high impact suplexes. When against smaller range people, or other females, she can easily use her wrestling suplex skills well. When it comes to high risk moves, she does some but not a lot. Her arsenal doesn’t include elaborate flips and spins. Instead, simple body splashes and crossbody moves are normal. Occasionally she has been known to spring off the ropes and rebound but that is very rare and only from desperation. Entrance: The arena goes quiet and listens to the melodic screams of ‘My fears have become phobias’ by As Blood Runs Black. From behind the curtains, Jacqueline Bates appears dressed in her short leather jacket, opened slightly in the center to reveal a bit of cleavage. She offers no expressions to the people and they do the same wondering who she really is. Miss Bates casually strolls down to the ring and when there climbs the stairs to the top of the ring. Like a woman, she ducks under the top rope kicks her legs in over the second. Inside, Jacqueline cracks her neck and does the same with her fingers preparing for the match.
