Gibson looks down at Timo's hand, and as though the anticipation of this very moment has built up for years, he reaches up and takes it in his own. Erupting mightily, the crowd pops and both men smile at one another. And that's when all hell broke loose. An eerie clapping filters in from the darkness. Slowly, and methodically, it gets louder and louder as emerging from the shadows, Tsuyoshi Tanaka steps forward. The Japanese Firecracker looks less than pleased to be in the room, and even less pleased to be looking at Timo and Dave. Tanaka: Well isn't this just special. Two over the hill has been and never gonna be again losers making up with a handshake. Hold on a second folks, we got a cliché over here! Someone, quick call the lame police! Timo turns in a defensive posture, extending his good arm a bit to look more menacing, and Dave adjusts his bandages to see the threat before them a little better. Tanaka does not move any farther from the shadows than he has to however. Tanaka: Please, do you two seriously think I am the least bit afraid of even the both of you? Come on men, I am sitting here looking at two guys who are one self help book away from a full embrace and tears! He pauses briefly. Tanaka: It's pathetic! Two fully grown men week after week pining about the past...Oh Timo, I'm sorry I broke your leg...Oh Dave; you scarred me for life...Pitiful! Why don't you two open a camp for children so you can sit around the campfire and sing Kumbaya all night long! Timo starts to open his mouth, but Tanaka points a finger at him. Tanaka: I'm not finished you Samoan imbecile! Look around you guys, you are in an open area with nobody to come help you. You are both so beat up from trying to kill each other last week that you can barely defend yourselves...and woe is me... He pulls out a finely crafted ash bat, Barry Bond's signature emblazoned on the barrel. Tanaka: I seem to have the only weapon between the three of us. You know boys...it's looking awful good to be Tsuyoshi Tanaka right now! Taking a deep breath, Dave Gibson looks to his newly declared friend and then back at Tanaka. Gibson: Timo, he can't get us both if I hold him off. Get out of here. Timo puts up a fist, but Dave turns and holds his finger up in the air. Gibson: Timo, this isn't a time to be stubborn or argue with me. Get the hell out of here, if he takes us both out now, who's going to stop him later? I'm the better technician, and you have a bad arm. If either of us has a chance to get that bat away from him, it's me and you know it. Timo: But Dave... Snarling, The Samoan's partner in crime turns and pushes Timo away. Gibson: Get the hell out of here! For once in your life, please just stop being Timo and use your brain! Nodding slowly before he pads away in retreat, The Samoan backs away before turning and running at as fast a pace as he can manage. Tanaka coolly steps forward, taking a couple test swings as he does. Tanaka: Bravery does not exactly suit a man of your measure does it Dave? How does it feel to finally be a man? I bet it feels a lot like fear. Then again, you are looking at a superior foe. Dave grits his teeth. Gibson: How do you figure? Tanaka stops and props the bat on his shoulder. He cocks his head to the side quizzically, a mischievous grin forming. Tanaka: Well, I am younger than you, faster than you, smarter than you, better prepared, in better health, and as it stands right now...more heavily armed. I would say that I pretty much trump anything you bring to the table, so why don't you make this easy for both of us and just drop down on your knees so we can get this over with quick. Never one to miss a beat, Dave fires a quick jab. Gibson: I came here to fight tonight; I don't know what you came here for! Tanaka: Oh that's great, you've been hanging around Samoa man long enough to start using gay jokes. You know Dave, when you hit me with that chair several weeks ago...it occurred to me that perhaps I am just too lenient. Maybe, just maybe I let these dogs in FUSE run on too far a leash. Let's look at the facts... He changes the shoulder of which the bat rests upon. Tanaka: Covel thinks he deserves that belt, and the truth is he won't even face me in a fair fight. Smitty keeps making matches to boost his own ego, and doesn't give a shit about a real star like myself. Fred Fusion thinks he is some sort of super hero, and is too stupid to realize he is outgunned. Clinton Sage...Just saying his name makes me want to hit something. I have never seen a guy who claims to be some big name champion that is as big of a coward! At least when you face Tsuyoshi Tanaka, I fight! What, has he been booked once in two months? I've fought almost every week in that span of time! Gibson stands brazen, waiting for the inevitable to happen. Tanaka: I can go right down that list of title holders and pretenders. Every last one of them sickens me, and do you know why? Because while they languish in their little glory and are fawned upon by fans the world over...I have to keep dealing with you two assholes. Do you know what that's like? Do you know what this is doing to my chances of winning a belt? Chuckling a little to himself, Dave fires off. Gibson: Do you even know who you are talking to? The Firecrackers eyes light up and his next words spit pure venom. Tanaka: Of course I do...I'm talking to a dead man. Lashing out violently with the bat, Dave barely has time to avoid the strike. Gibson plants his foot and winces heavily. He punches out and drills Tanaka with a shot to the ribs. The Japanese Firecracker shrugs it off and drives the butt end of the bat into Dave's midsection, dropping the warrior. Standing over his foe, Tsuyoshi cracks a wicked grin. Tanaka: You are little better than a roach standing between me and my ultimate goal. I seriously hope you and that half wit you hang around with get smart before I have to do what neither of you could and permanently finish each of you off. Grabbing the bat barrel and handle, Tanaka drops the head of the bat square into a winded Gibson's face. The veteran drops to the ground clutching his face in his hands. Blood slowly squeezes through the cracks of his fingers, and he screams in pain. Tanaka: The sad thing is that this is barely personal. You are just a victim of the FUSE booking not realizing that I am way above the talent of the shit stabbers I am facing. In retrospect, maybe I could have just told the Samoan I respected him and let him walk away thinking he accomplished something instead of aggravating him...but then again... He drops the bat hard onto Gibson's side, then drops boots into him, then again and again and again. Finally, mercifully, he takes one final stomp, and leaves Gibson laying flat on the ground. He slowly runs a hand through his hair before spitting on his downed foe. Tanaka: Then again, I wouldn't have just got to do THAT. He pulls the bat up again, this time lining up a shot that is fully intended to take Gibson out for good. Tanaka: Well Dave...I know you can't hear this, which is probably good for you. For what it's worth, when you are sitting in the hospital next week and you're nurse wipes the drool from your mangy chin, remember me...and remember that I gave you the worst beating of your miserable life! As he is about to bring the bat down, there is a commotion and a bellowing voice hurls an insult from across the garage, and at full charge, Neil Fracas roars into action, closely tailed by Gideon Fontaine. Way behind the, hobbles The Samoan Silencer, flaming Tiki Torch in hand. Tanaka breaks in mid swing and quickly realizes he is now completely outgunned and out of luck. Tanaka: This isn't over. He stops to kick Gibson one more time for good measure before dropping the bat and running for his life. Neil and Gideon run past Gibson, yelling all manner of threats, while Timo drops down next to Gibson. Neil: Is he okay? Gideon: Come on man, he got whacked by a bat a bunch of times, do you think he's alright? The camera feed begins to cut out, but not before Timo raises his head to look at a retreating Tanaka. His face slowly contorts, and his green eyes sear with fury. Tanaka turns around to make sure he has gotten away clean...and meets the burning eyes of The Samoan. This time, Tanaka sees something in those eyes that actually surprises him. For the first time since their unlikely meeting, Tsuyoshi Tanaka realizes he may be in over his head. The Samoan Silencer bares his teeth before delivering one final verbal assault through fury clenched jaws. Timo: I hope you've made your peace with God Tanaka...because I'm sending you to fucking meet him.
