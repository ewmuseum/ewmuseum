Coral Avalon Biography Real Name Coral Dylan Avalon Nicknames The King of Submissions Hometown New Orleans, Louisiana Birthday December 22nd, 1981 Height 6' Weight 224 Fed Titles SCCW Gateway Champion, SCCW Livewire Champion (1x) Fed Awards None. Biography Coral Avalon was born to a rich family off the eastern Australian coast, which helped give him his unusual name. At the age of 18, he left his rich family life behind and vowed to become a professional wrestler. At first under the tutelage of Kent Anthason, it would be his training under Joey Malone that would single him out as a gifted talent. Eventually, Coral followed Joey Malone into Action! Wrestling, whereupon he utilized his vast wrestling knowledge to adopt the 'Kleptomaniac' gimmick: the ability to steal the finishers of other, more well-known wrestlers. During this time, he clashed with the man that would be his greatest nemesis: 'the Original' Jeff Garvin. Garvin and Avalon quickly hated each other, and their rivalry would flare up off and on for the better part of a year. Their feud included an iron man match that was voted as Action!'s Match of the Year in 2002, a Bantam title match the biggest PPV in A!W's history (Juggernaut II) that saw Avalon walk away with the title, and a brutal Last Man Standing match where Avalon had to literally be stretchered out of the arena. The Last Man Standing match proved to be Avalon's last match in Action, as he departed for the Squared Circle at the end of 2003. Try as he might, though, Avalon could not find his rhythm in tSC for its first nine months, losing major matches to Alias, Brandon Youngblood, and Dave Morey. After tSC lost its television deal, Avalon remained with the company as an independent talent, but didn't achieve any major success until he defeated Steve Knox in 2008 for the tSC Championship, a title he would hold for five months. Near the end of 2005, Avalon resurfaced in both fWo and ACW, now a more complete wrestler and no longer relying on his 'Kleptomaniac' persona. He formed the Blue Rogues with the Codemaster and defeated Silver & Gold (Sonny Silver and Lindsay Troy) for the fWo World Tag Team Championships. At the same time, Avalon was embroiled in a brutal feud with Jimmy Cain and Lowell Dot Com that ended inside a barbed wire madhouse known simply as 'Gimmick Hell'. Despite never resorting to a weapon and many brutal injuries, Avalon survived the match and laid claim to Lowell's Scorpion Fighting Championship. However, by the beginning of 2006, Avalon had disappeared from television due to fWo shutting down in the midst of a riot and ACW not being able to keep him under contract. For the next year and a half, Avalon worked in both Japan and the independents. Then, in 2007, Avalon once again resurfaced with Codemaster as a reformation of the Blue Rogues. Together, the two won the tag team championships, but friction between the two men eventually led to their dissolution soon after they lost the titles at Colossus IV. Avalon wasn't seen again outside of the independents... until now. Other Feds IWO, Action!, tSC, fWo, ACW, PRIME Other Fed Awards tSC Championship, Action! His style can be thought of as fluid and adaptive, and he is hard to prepare for because he doesn't often do the same things in any given match. Because he's a very composed person, he tends to be a bit hard to rattle. In addition, he's prone to talking to and especially taunting his opponents during matches, especially in the early stages where he tends to control the pace with headlocks and armbars. It's very important to note that Coral can do whatever the heck he wants in the ring. He isn't the best at any one thing, but rather, he's proficent in all things. Despite this, he has a particular knack for submissions, which is ironic given that it used to be his absolute weakest aspect of his wrestling. He is the current 'King of Submissions' as a result of his talents. Because of Coral's focus on being a wrestler, he has no love for hardcore brawling (even despite being a former ACW Scorpion Fighting champion, which was built on that kind of style). Furthermore, Coral restricts himself with a kind of an 'honor code', which includes the following rules: 01. No cheating. 02. No weapons. 03. No moves that would intentionally drop an opponent on their head. 04. No punching women. 05. No hitting the referee or letting them get hit by the other guy. He can be overcome if forced to work a specific style against somebody better than him in that style. Also, while he tends to maintain his calm, his chivalrous nature will definitely get him hurt.
