Killzone 44 Hartford Civic Center Coliseum — Hartford, Connecticut ---- Tonight on Killzone... Timo Bolamba challenges Clinton Sage for the Universal title on the Anniversary of our countries birth. ---- Sean Sterling vs Mayson Colby In the first undercard match of the evening, We saw Newcomers Sean Sterling and Mayson Colby hit the ring. The two enter the ring to a moderate reaction from the fans. After the bell sounds they lock up and Sterling takes the early advantage with a hip toss. Colby quickly bounces back with a hip toss of his own and follows it up with a back body drop and then an elbow drop the chest. Colby goes for the cover but Sterling kicks out at two, easily. The two quickly get to their feet and exchange a few right hands before Colby backs Sterling into the corner, chops him across the chest, and whips him across the ring to the other corner, charging in behind him. Sterling is able to catch himself on the ropes, pushing himself up into the air and over the head of Colby before pulling him down from behind and going for a roll up. Colby kicks out at two and the two once again raise to their feet for an exchange of punches. This time Sterling takes the advantage after a chop to the chest and whips Colby into the ropes and drops him to the mat with a dropkick. The two continue to go back and forth, each one taking the advantage for a couple of minutes a piece each time, for several minutes. Around the ten and a half minute mark Sterling gets the advantage after reversing an abdominal stretch into a hip toss and then camel clutch hold. Colby is able to make it to the ropes but when he turns around he walks right into an eye poke and then stiff DDT. A moment later Sterling hit his "An Ass Saw An Angel" finish and, with a handful of tights, picked up the three count and his first FUSE victory. In the after match Colby got to his feet and kicked the bottom rope, locking eyes with a cocky Sterling who was celebrating his way up the ramp. Sean Sterling ---- Fred Fusion vs Phil Atken In undercard Match #02, Fred Fusion and Phil Atken were up... The second of two dark matches gets underway under fairly odd circumstances. Fred gets the 1...2...3 and yet another FUSE victory. After the match Fred paraded around the ring with his FUSION Championship, playing to the fans, and getting them hyped up for the upcoming show.
