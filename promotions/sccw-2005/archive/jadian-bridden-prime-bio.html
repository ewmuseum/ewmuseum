Jadian Bridden Biography Real Name Jadian Bridden Nicknames Jay, Jay B, JD Hometown Austin, Texas (currently residing in San Antonio, where he conducts his training) Birthday March 25, 1988 Height 5'9' Weight 189 Biography Was he born with a silver spoon in his mouth... No. Was he born in the hood... No. Surprising to many, some people don't have to be handed a tough life to want to get into the wrestling business. Sometimes, you just have a passion to do something you love, and that was the case for Jay Bridden. He grew up watching the high flying antics of Mexican Luchadores, as weekly episodes of Lucha Libre were shown on the Spanish Stations throughout most of South Texas. When you grow up following the careers of men named Mil Mascaras, Blue Demon, Shocker, Chavo Guerrero (Sr.), and El Santo instead of guys like Hulk Hogan, Ultimate Warrior, Randy Savage, and Andre the Giant... Well, you sort of have a different perspective on the whole business and what it means. Jay didn't grow up watching guys waste time in headlocks because they had no clue as to what real wrestling moves were. He didn't watch a man drop a leg on someone and consider it the greatest move on the planet. He watched men put on wrestling clinics each and every week, with real moves that not many could pull off with their limited repertoires. It's sad really, but it made Jay look at this in a whole different light, and he's better for it. Growing up in Texas, there wasn't many wrestlers to look forward to watching on TV, but there was one team that he could not get enough of when he started to think about making a career out of the sport. Minor Details. Mike and Benny Minor were in a class of their own, making a real name for themselves first in the CWWF, then went on to be spectacular and virtually unbeatable in the RRWF, before making more waves in the SCWF. It was of no surprise to his parents when he asked them to join Minor Wrestling Academy... Where the catch phrase sold the place more than the name did. "Where you come to perfect those Minor Details no one else bothers to worry about." It was the perfect place to begin his training, and he quickly became the star pupil. The boy turned out to be a natural, and given his speed and agility, he really knew how to fly around that ring like nobody's business.
