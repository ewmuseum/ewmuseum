Darius Drannen Biography Real Name Darius Drannen Nicknames 'Sword of Damocles' Hometown New Orleans, LA Birthday 06/17/85 Height 6'1' Weight 185 Biography Growing up without ever knowing his father, Darius always seemed to find himself apart from everybody else. Consequently, trouble found him early and often. His mother, a waitress at a local restaurant/bar, had a difficult time keeping a close eye on him while at the same time working as many hours as possible, just to keep a roof over their heads, and food on their table. So in his best interest, when he was starting his first year of high school, his mother sent him to Detroit, to live with his aunt and uncle. The change of scenery did nothing to curtail his penchant for getting into trouble. Barely a week into his freshman year, Darius was suspended for his part in a minor explosion in the science lab. He'd argued that he was just following the instructions that the teacher had laid out for the class to begin their science experiment, and that it had been an accident. But it was his word against that of his teacher, and thus he'd earned himself a two-day break, as the school hoped that he'd take the punishment to heart. Instead, it seemed to only ignite his mischievous side. And for the next couple of years, he and the principal became very familiar with one another. It was during one of his routine visits to the principal that Darius had met a fellow outcast named Cade Carson. The two had struck up a friendship, a pair of kindred spirits who would go on to be a double dose of trouble for the school's authority figures. While Darius was known for his smart mouth and pranks, Cade was the violent one of the two. Where Darius would ridicule his enemies with his wit and sarcasm, Cade would simply use his fists to get his point across. As their high school careers came to a close, all told, they spent more time in detention or outright suspended, than they did in class. After high school, the pair remained inseperable. And when Cade had decided -- although Darius believed his decision had been guided by an invisible hand -- to become a professional wrestler, Darius tagged along for the ride. While not as strong as Cade, and certainly not nearly as big of frame, Darius was a natural at the submission arts. His lightning-quick reflexes allowed him to cinch in a submission hold at any time, and in any situation. Thus, submission wrestling became his forte, and his wrestling career was born.
