Wyatt Colton Biography Real Name Wyatt Colton Nicknames The Canadian Cowboy, The Cowboy from Hell Hometown Calgary, Alberta, Canada Birthday January 4, 1981 Height 6'5' Weight 245 Fed Titles FUSE Fatal Faction Champion (w/Jake Ennis) Biography Wyatt Colton started his wrestling career in his native Canada when he was only 15. Wrestling in shows promoted in the Canadian Northwest, it was apparent that the son of a skilled tradesman father, and television worker mother, was destined to escape the hard life that his family had lived. At 18, Wyatt got that opportunity when he was offered a spot to train at Alexander Craig's Gym in Kamloops, British Columbia. After six months of actual physical training, the tall and lanky kid that entered that had already wrestled in over a hundred matches came out looking like an amazing athlete. Having used the time to add more than 20 pounds of lean muscle, Wyatt looked even further south. Heading to Seattle, Wyatt started to wrestle as the Martian in the Pacific Northwest of the United States. He was very green, in more ways than one. Even with three years of wrestling under his belt, and spending the previous six months in a wrestling school, it was apparent that Wyatt still wasn't at the top of his game. Being taken under the wing of fellow Yukoner Alfred 'Alaskan Pete' Donnelly, Wyatt really learned the ropes of the wrestling business. Over time, Wyatt would evolve the Martian character to the point that he was no longer speaking in promos, taking his lessons from Alaskan Pete to heart. A second major move would come a year and a half into the life of the Martian, where he would move to Tuscon, Arizona. Originally sharing an apartment with Donnelly, now going by the name of Fred the 49er, Wyatt would start to lose the strong Canadian accent that had plagued his career. Wrestling in the hot bed of UFO Fanatacism, Wyatt would become rather popular in the character. He would add incoherent rambling into the character, including his trademark phrase 'Bah Weep Granah Weep Ninni Bong', which he had lifted from the Universal Greeting in Transformers the Movie. It was at this point, that Wyatt was offered a spot on the roster in FUSE. Unlike his time in the independants, the antics of the Martian didn't endear him on the National fanbase that FUSE had. Instead, he was relegated to the lower card, including an embarrassing loss against NIX! Eventually, his roster spot in FUSE was looking like it was going to be eliminated when Araceli Negra decided to take on a little experiment, almost like a hobby while she waited for her primary client, IKD, to make his debut in FUSE. Araceli took over the contracts of Wyatt and fellow FUSE chopping block victim Jake 'Connor Chesney' Ennis, and decided to make them into a team. After a couple of weeks worth of getting on the same page, they finally emerged as Wyatt Colton and Connor Ennis...The Cowboys From Hell. They have impressed so much that they had taken over IKD's spot as Araceli Negra's primary clients. For the Cowboys, the Future looks bright.
