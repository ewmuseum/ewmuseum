Temptation 06/16 Wells Fargo Arena — Tempe, Arizona ---- Searching for Vengance "ALEXANDRA!" Anger is an emotion. Alexandra is a name. Mix the two together and you get a man who is raving mad as he storms through the parking lot area, dressed in jeans and a signature SCCW T-Shirt that said "The Lost Soul". His hands were curled into fists as he walks into the Wells Fargo Arena, looking, praying, and searching for a fight with one person, and one person only. Yet, there were obstacles in his way, designed to trip him up. One of them was Mary Jackson, who had been posted at the entrance to the arena. Mary Jackson: Dusk, Dusk! "RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!" As the face of the Lost Soul came onto the screen, the thousands of fans packed into the arena exploded into a frenzy. Normally, Dusk would smile at this, but not tonight. Tonight was all about business. Mary Jackson: Dusk, can I get your feelings on last week when you were hit with the Spider Web from Desade? A woman you felt that was making a change for the better. You came here with the idea of stopping her, and yet-- Before she could finish though, the Lost Soul brushed by her as he continued to walk down the various corridors and hallways with one person on his mind. Dusk: ALEXANDRA! His voice fills the entire hallway as different crew members quickly dash out of the way. They knew about his temper, his anger issues, and none of them wanted to look into those fiery red eyes of his. Each step is with purpose as Mary's words filled his head. He had thought that Alexandra was changing for the better. Things in his personal life, the tears that she had shed upon him, and sending Quinn to him, those were signs that a better woman was coming around. Or at least, that's what he thought. Saving Phillip Kennedy -- what a notion -- had left him a man scorned and burned. For far too long, he had been distracted. Phillip Kennedy. The Hand. Aimz. Lane Stevens. He was here with one, and only one, purpose. To bring an end to Alexandra Pierce, the woman who masqueraded as Desade. To this point, the Lost Soul had yet to do it. Instead, a soft spot grew in his heart for the Director. Not any longer. As he turned the corner, he came face to face with several security guards who seemed designed to block him from going after Alex. No doubt, put there by Kensington himself who frowned upon chaotic backstage area. Unfortunately, Kensington had hired him, and with that kind of hiring came a backstage area that was never safe. Dusk dropped his shoulder and rammed his way through the guards. Yet, they didn't stop. They followed after him. He punched one of them in the face. Dusk: Alex, stop hiding, and come out to play! He had heard the words for the past week. People called him foolish, naive, and an idiot. How could you believe that the very person who had seeped black poison into SCCW would actually change for the better? He didn't know how it had happened, but he was certain that it would never happen again. A security guard jumped onto his back, hoping to slow him down, but Dusk just threw him off of him. With that though, he came face to face with the locker room where Alexandra Pierce would be hiding. Dusk backed up, elbowed another security guard, and was about to kick the door down when it opened all on its own. Yet, the person who stepped out wasn't the Director herself. No. In fact, it was a man that Dusk had never seen before. The man stood around six feet five, and weighed a little under three hundred. A tight black t-shirt and black pants covered his frame. He looked as if he could have easily been on the roster, yet his face was unfamiliar. The man's blonde hair almost touched his shoulders. He took one look at Dusk, nodded, and reached towards the nametag on the door. He removed it with one aggressive swipe, and "Desade" was replaced by "Lane Stevens". "BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Man (turning into the room): Laney, got company. Lane Stevens (off camera): Angry fella, scarred up face? The man sized up Dusk. Man: Yep. Lane Stevens (cheery): Let him in! The man opened the door, and stepped aside. Dusk stepped in quickly. There he found The River Rat, Lane Stevens, dressed in his ring gear and a Desade t-shirt. He was playing the card game War with his former FUSE tag-team partner, and current Alexandra Pierce purchased henchmen (think of it like a birthday present) Jeb Stewart. Jeb, a rather large man to say the least, was dressed in an old Affliction t-shirt and jeans. Lane Stevens: You met Miles? Miles Cavanaugh. We go way back. And I assume you've seen Jeb in action.
