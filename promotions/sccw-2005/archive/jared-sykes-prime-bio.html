Jared Sykes Biography Real Name Jared Sykes Hometown Boston, MA Birthday December 14, 1978 Height 5'10' Weight 195 Fed Titles LiveWire Championship, SIN Championship, Gateway Championship, Universal Champion Fed Awards Infinite Gauntlet Champion Biography Jared Sykes does not fight women. This includes rest holds. He will however caress them lovingly. Okay, who the fuck am I kidding, no he won't. They're too busy laughing at his feeble attempts to be sweet and charming. Other Feds MSW Cruiserweight Championship, LSW Cruiserweight Championship, NWC J-Crown Champion (2nd longest reign), HIW Maui Championship Other Fed Awards #14 NWC Top 100 for 2001, #35 NWC Top 100 for 2002 Wrestling Style Japanese light-heavyweight/American independent Finisher Move Omega-13 Finisher Description Impact driver Finisher Setup Move Burning Star Lariat Setup Description Diving uppercut lariat Other Moves MOVES: His primary offense is focused on the upper torso, with a smattering of aerial moves thrown in for a bit of variety (and because he's a cruiserweight, and that's what they do). He's agile, but not flippy. He'll pull out a moonsault, but don't expect any 450's or 720's out of this guy. Standard Set: Slingblade Springboard enzugiri Final cut Yakuza knee kick (Busaiku knee) Arm-trap belly to back suplex Neckbreaker across the knee Inverted overdrive Charging dropkick to an opponent seated in the corner Charging double knee strike to an opponent seated in the corner Moonsault reverse DDT (rare) Jared is also incredibly fond of and well-versed in neckbreaker and cutter variants, having used a few as finishers for the majority of his career. Trademarks: Burning Star Lariat - Comes in two forms, but is basically a diving uppercut lariat. The only variation involves a deep wind-up spin, where he'll almost drag his hand on the canvas before exploding up with this. This is THE trademark move, and can also setup the Lightning Rift. Crack the Sky - Slingshot cutter from the ring aprong to a dazed or hunched-over opponent near the ropes. More a desperation counter than anything else. Finisher(s): Lightning Helix - Anaconda Vice (submission); sort of a hybrid arm/neck lock. This is his standard finish, since it's usable on dudes regardless of size. If his opponent starts flailing, or he feels he needs something extra, will tuck his opponent's other arm in (Anaconda Cross, slight variation on the same move). So named for a Magic The Gathering card. Omega 13 - Impact Driver (Youtube link). This is only to be used against guys who are close to his size. Named for a fictional device in the film Galaxy Quest. FOR MATCH WRITERS: 1.) Jared has a very strict policy against hitting women, or even fighting them in general. This means that he will let himself get beat to shit, and at most he'll try score a quick roll-up, or just dive out of the way of things. Seriously, he won't even go for a basic headlock. 2.) He's durable as all hell, and can take a ridiculous pounding, on par with what you might expect out of an Austin Aries type. 3.) I honestly do not care what you do to him in the ring. He can run like a girl. You can shoot him in the chest. Electrocute his testicles. I don't care. As long as rule number one remains intact, do whatever you want to the guy.
