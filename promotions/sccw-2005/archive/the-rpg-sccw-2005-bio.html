The RPG Biography Fed Titles Zip Fed Awards Zip Biography These guys are computer nerds of the highest calibre. They were born, raised and will most likely die in Buffalo, the most happening town in all of Wyoming. They were picked on in high school by both kids and teachers alike and their only friends were each other. They've managed to stumble into the world of wrestling purely by accident. Ralph read the tag team sign up site for FUSE and put their names forward. I mean, these guys are the best at any game they've ever played. Why wouldn't they pwn at wrestling? Of course there are alterior motives. With tag team gold these guys will be earning serious $$$s therefore they will eventually be able to fund their project so aptly named 'the worlds greatest game ever... EVER'. They're also looking for that one thing that has elluded them their entire lives... acceptance. That is if they ever put down the controllers and get into the ring.
