Aaron Chandler Biography Real Name Aaron Sean Chandler Nicknames 'The Essence of Arrogance' Hometown Phoenix, Arizona Birthday August, 1, 1984 Height 6'6 Weight 260 Biography History From the womb, Aaron knew he was cursed with some sort of bad luck. Unlike most children who got brothers and or sisters after them, Aaron got his older sister Adrianna Samantha and older brother Aauron Sean at the exact same time. It was hard enough having to compete with one person, but two, well that just sucked. Being the last of the triplets to actually come placed Aaron as the baby of the family. At three Aaron quickly discovered that he loved music. Not just the words that were said, but more specifically the beats. His favorite instrument to hear in a song was the piano, and as soon as he was able to, he quickly read up on all great pianists. By the time that Aaron and his siblings were twelve, they begin to explore in different areas of interest. For Aaron it was learning all he could about the piano, from how to play it, to why people wrote what they did. He of course came so wrapped up in this that he had no time to make friends. As he got older people would make fun of him due to the way he dressed and the way he spoke. Aaron would not let it bother him and would just continue to live in his own world. At the age of nineteen Aaron would go on to college but not for long, due to an altercation with the teacher. After trying to explain this to his parents Aaron would run away to various places, but finally settle in a small town tucked away from the radar in the state of Kentucky. During his time here, while he would worry about his family, he would have no contact with them. He would be without their money and would learn to earn his own. He would write bits of poetry, but never try to get them published, and never tell anyone of them either. Here he would also meet a girl by the name of Fancy Trevalia. The two would grow a fond likeness for one another, but not actually fall in love. For Aaron it would mostly just be someone who he could talk to, as Fancy seemed to like his poetry and the fact that he did not come with a bunch of 'baggage.' Fancy would however force Aaron to come to grips with reality, when she would inform him she was carrying his child. He would put it off, making up various excuses, until Fancy was seven months pregnant. Aaron would go back to Phoenix Arizona, where he would come face to face with both his mother and father. Here he would also meet Chasity Johanssen, whom he would go on to have a beautiful baby daughter with, not only putting his life, but his marriage in danger. No matter what seemed to happen though, Aaron could always seem to count on his parents. They seemed happy to see him, and they would also then go on to inform him that his brother Aauron was in jail, and his sister was still busy keeping her head in her school books. Here Aaron would also stumble across a promotion known as World Wrestling Alliance, or WWA for short. He did not know it, but here he would begin a journey, that would not only help him learn if wrestling was meant for him, but a journey that would help him discover just who he was. Armani Decano and Donovan Carter would also be here. Donovan Carter was one whom Aaron had not seen since he was a young child, and Armani he had not seen since he had last left. Aaron could not help but wonder if this was destiny for the three of them, or maybe even a wicked twist of fate. Either way, things did not work out for any of the men and they eventually all found themselves in w3, the one place Aaron just did not want to be. In short w3 was a big mistake, and no one never really knew what happened to Aaron afterwards, well except for his cousins Fenix Rayne and Armani Decano. *********************************************************** It has now been two years and Aaron is getting out of jail. He has mixed up memories of just why he is there and all because of a man called mesmero. That was also another one of Decano's great ideas, and since Aaron was promised the life he always wanted, he did not see a problem with it. Not being out long, Aaron has quickly seen that everything he once knew, is not as it was. It was as Mesmero spoke of and small bits and pieces of Aaron, Fenix, and Decano's life have all been altered, giving them in essence the perfect life that they all dreamed of. For Aaron, it's a bit of a weird concept to grasp, at least right now because he still has bits and pieces of his old life. This however was predicted by Mesmero, who also said that their old lives would mix with their new ones. It isn't to be looked at as bad but come on when you go from being like the Manson family to being like the Brady Bunch, who would not be scared? All personal bits aside one thing with the help of Mesmero that has not changed about Aaron is the fact that he has always loved to wrestle. His mother, father, aunts and uncles all wrestled and he much like his father just has a knack for it once he steps inside of that good ol' squared circle. He would never openly admit this but he has always looked up to his father. It was never easy growing up in the Chandler household, but even at the tender age of four, everyone knew Aaron was just like his daddy. He has an aurora about himself that is strange, yet speaks volumes in a language that is only understood by few. Maybe Fuse will finally be the place that works out for Aaron, and if not, then maybe this life is just another chance at trial and error. After all, Aaron's pretty sure he could look up Mesmero if he ever needed him again.
