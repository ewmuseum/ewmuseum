The holder of the camera pushes his way out of the bush and the video wall once lights up by the moonlight. Facing us is a warehouse. At least it looks like a warehouse. It's either that or an aircraft hanger. The large corrugated metal doors stand tall and look pretty secure. The camera man moves in closer and tugs at a padlock holding the doors together. He sets the camera down by his feet. In front of it lands a duffel bag. A hand reaches in and pulls out a set of bolt croppers. A quick clanging of metal later and the heavy duty padlock drops to the floor with a thud. The cameraman tries to open the doors but they won't budge. He pulls sharply and the bottom of the doors shake but they don't separate. Onto somewhere else. He picks up the camera and walks slowly around the perimeter of the building. Feeling through the darkness for windows or doorways. Finally he hits something large, something metal, something vertical. The fire escape. The cameraman shifts the camera around before seemingly settling it somewhere upon his person. Slowly but surely he makes his way up the fire escape, one rung at a time. Soon he finds himself on the roof. More corrugated steel. He walks slowly and skilfully across the roof making sure to make no noise. Finally he finds a skylight. He sets the camera down and pulls a bottle of water from out of his bag. He pours some on the skylight and rubs away the dirt that has amassed over the years of neglect this building seems to have endured. Peering though the now clear skylight he sees exactly who he's been searching for all week. Evan Jackson.
