Clinton Sage Biography Real Name Clinton Arthur Sage Nicknames 'The Seventh Sin' Hometown Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Birthday September 11th, 1974 Height 6'6' Weight 250 Fed Titles <br><font color='#0281FF'><b>Universal Championship (x2)</b></font><br>[ 5-31-07 - current]<br>[ 4-11-07 -- 5-2-07 ] Biography Clinton Sage is two things: wealthy and obsessive. A little over a year ago, Clinton Sage burst onto the PTC scene in their own 'Infinite Gauntlet' division, winning the first ever match he was in against numerous opponents (including the likes of Rich Rollins and Seymour Almasy). During that time, however, Sage was being manipulated by one of Pennsylvania's most notorious serial killers. With little more than two weeks before his execution, Harlan Raines summoned Clinton Sage to his prison, and told the wrestler details about a cult that both men were a part of. You see, for all his life, Clinton Sage has had three tattoo's on his back. Small, elliptical eyes. One on each shoulder blade, and one at the small of his back. Stretched and faded from time and aging, Clinton knew nothing of these tattoos or what they meant until Harlan Raines had told him. By the time Harlan Raines was executed, he had been turned into something of a cult hero. Clinton's fame grew on a nationwide level, although he hated every second of it. Sage had found the bodies that the Pennsylvania authorities (and US government) couldn't locate, which revealed that the true nature of Harlan Raines' murders were quite different than most had expected: he was killing off a long-standing cult, The Eyes of Fear, because he thought it would bring him closer to God, and ascend past this life. Clinton retreated into anonymity for a few months, before trying to once again wrestle. He stepped into the ring for a qualifying match PTC's annual GTT tournament, GTT5. Sage lost to the masked wrestler known as Spartan, and it infuriated Sage that he couldn't know the man's identity. A year later, GTT6 came around, and Clinton Sage was still searching for the man who hid under the mask. It consumed him, and his mental health deteriorated to the point where Clinton thought that he was evolving past what a normal human could ever attain. All because of Spartan. Right before GTT6 started, Clinton's home was struck by a meteorite, which destroyed the shrine he had devoted to Spartan and finding out who the enigma was. Without the control of his anger, Sage picked up the still burning space rock and hurled it out through the window. Hands burnt to the bone, Clinton fell unconscious and awoke with the paramedics on each side of him, treating his wounds. Still angry and thinking nothing of his own personal health, Clinton once again located the meterorite and picked it up out of frustration once more, this time as he threw it, he wished with all his might that his anger, pain, and feelings would befall the man under the mask of Spartan. Little did he know, for one reason or another, the meteorite was a fragment of the Vekoma comet, which passes by Earth every 32 years. The fragments of this comet are able to grant one wish to the person holding them. Clinton's wounds disappeared, and subsequent wounds followed. Now, truly believing that he was more than a normal man, Clinton set out to still find out who Spartan was by looking around for hospital reports that matched the injuries he inflicted on himself. To this day, he hasn't found him. To this day, Clinton Sage is still consumed by the notion that someone out there hid under the mask of Spartan, and it's driving him closer to the brink of sanity. He also wants to find the fragments of that meteor. Out of Character Notes: I realize that Clinton's storyline is unrealistic and unbelievable. But that never stopped anyone from joining a fed with a vampire, or getting away with murder. :) From other wrestlers vantage points, Clinton Sage is a rich and arrogant prick. As of late, some notice that he lacks any physical injuries after matches. Rumors start, but never get past that stage. The more severe the injury, the slower it will heal, which is why getting a chairshot in the middle of the ring WILL hurt Clinton, because he feels the pain. The wound just heals itself quicker than normal, because it gets 'passed' onto the person who hid under the mask of Spartan. (Nathan Hollinger) Very few people know about the Vekoma meteorites, and their powers.
