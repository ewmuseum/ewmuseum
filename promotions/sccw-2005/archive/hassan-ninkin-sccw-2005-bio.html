Hassan Ninkin Biography Hometown King of Prussia, PA Birthday October 4, 1981 Height 3'7' Weight 88 Fed Titles Livewire Champion Biography Hassan comes from a broken home, full of drug abuse and violence. He grew up with the understanding that you can do whatever you want as long as nobody catches you, and also with the attitude that you have to take every conceivable shortcut to get by in life. He was a daredevil in high school, and a particularly risky stunt he performed at a school basketball game got him noticed by a wrestling trainer. Since he didn’t want to go to college, and getting a job was out of the question, he decided to follow this guy for a few years after high school, just for the free ride he was offering. He never actually expected to be wrestling as a career, in fact, he never expected to do a day’s work in his life if he could avoid it. However, once the contract was put in front of him and he saw the amount of money he stood to earn just for fighting in public, he signed his name and hit the road.
