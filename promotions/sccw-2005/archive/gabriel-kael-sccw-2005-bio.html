Gabriel Kael Biography Real Name Gabriel Marcus Kael Nicknames He Who Cheats, The Killer of Faces, Hometown Renwer, Manitoba, Canada Birthday September 8, 1983 (Age 26) Height 6'7 Weight 314 Wrestling Style Power/Brawler/Cheater Finisher Move see move list Finisher Setup Move see move list Other Moves Stylistic Overview: Gabriel Kael doesn't need to cheat, but he does. Excessively so. If there is some way in which he can break or bend the rules to gain an advantage, he'll do it without thinking twice. Otherwise, he's a big, incredibly powerful individual who loves to break people up with big power moves. Each move is done with the intent to cause as much damage as possible, especially his suplexes and throws, which he will do into objects such as the turnbuckles, guardrails, ring steps, and any other hard unyielding object he can think of. Avalanche variants of pretty much any move are commom with him... I guess just use your imagination. Also please note the moves below are commom/favorites, as long as he isn't doing 450 splashes and moonsaults it's all good, be as creative as you want to be. Finishing Moves : 1) Tragedy - sitout spiked tombstone piledriver 2) The Unhappy Ending - Throwing crucifix powerbomb 3) Unhappily Ever After - wheelbarrow suplex lift into a urunage side slam. Setups : He has no main setup, really. Just busts people up with big strikes and power moves and finishes them off. Signature Moves: Broken Noses, Broken Necks - Gabriel traps both of his opponent's arm and threads his hands up so he is choking the opponent. He then delivers four or five uncontested headbutts to the bridge of the noses before flinging the opponent overhead with a double handed choke suplex. The Hail Mary - Backbreaker rack into falling reverse DDT from the top rope. Single or double leg takedown usually followed by a barrage of ground punches and elbows. Triple powerbomb, with the final being a running or sit down bomb (favorite) Sidewalk slam Urunage side slam Fireman's carry into falling reverse DDT. Impaler DDT Evenflow DDT Backbreaker Rack into a falling neckbreaker.
