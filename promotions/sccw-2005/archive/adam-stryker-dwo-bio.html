Adam Stryker Biography Hometown Los Angles, CA Birthday April 17, 1976 Height 6-2 Weight 238 Biography Adam Stryker has been out of the public eye for almost a year. One year ago his family was killed in a horrible 'accident'. At the time, Adam was on a surge in Global, the current International Champion with his sights set on the World Title. After Global came crashing down, Adam disappeared into the shadows. No one knows where he went or why he left. For that matter no one really knows why he has decided to come back to the world of wrestling and why a man with so many bad habits would choose Sin City of all places for a rebirth.
