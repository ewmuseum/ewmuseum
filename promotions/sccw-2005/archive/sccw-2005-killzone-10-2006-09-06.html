SA: I think Damien Cruz is underestimating Tigera right now! Tigera leaps into the air, attempting to drop a knee to the face of Cruz. The champion however, is able to leap off the mat, leaving Tigera to get nothing but air. She crashes down hard on the mat, leaving Cruz with the opportunity to drop an elbow, much to the fans approval. The both of them scramble up, and we're back at square one. Tigera runs at Cruz and is taken down with an arm drag, which Cruz turn into a sitting armbar. Tigera easily shakes out of the submission, hopping back to her feet. Cruz leaps up and hits the ropes, coming at Tigera, who hits Cruz in the teeth with a surprise superkick, causing him to hit the mat. SA: There's the quickness of Tigera! HJ: That had to hurt. Tigera takes advantage of the situation, hitting a few vicious stomps to the chest of Cruz. Pushing him into the ropes, she shoves a foot to his throat, pulling the ropes as she chokes him out. Marin fairly makes the 5 count, and Tigera breaks the choke, pulling Cruz to his feet. She knee's him in the midsection and then hits a devastating kick to the side of his head, sending him to the mat again. SA: Tigera is NOT here to mess around, right out of the gate she's atking advantage of Cruz! HJ: He came into this match with the wrong mentality, he's underestimating the Bangkok Tiger, and it's just the worst possible thing he could do. Not letting Tigera dominate the match, Cruz climbs to his feet and hits the ropes. He copes running at Tigera, ready to take her down for the first time in the match. Instead, in an incredible feat of gymnastics and overall flexability, She's able to climb out onto the apron, and use the ropes to springboard onto Cruz's shoulders, attempting a Hurricanrana. Before she can fall back however, Cruz walks over to the top rope and THROWS her over with a POWERBOMB SA: DAMIEN CRUZ JUST POWERBOMBED TIGERA FROM THE INSIDE OUT! HJ: Throwing out the wrestling textbook there, but an excellent counter by Cruz. Tigera should've known better than to attempt a move like that so early in the match, and how she's on the outside. As Tigera attempts to climb to her feet, Alexis Marin stands on the inside and begins to count her out. 1.. 2.. 3.. She stands, jumping onto the apron. Using the ropes again, she slingshots herself into the air, but is caught by a waiting Damien Cruz. Pulling her to the center of the ring, he hit's a devastating power slam, leaving Tigera on the mat. SA: More power here from Cruz. HJ: He's not going to out run Tigera, this is a good plan. Cruz hit's a quick leg drop, and gets a pin on Tigera. ONE! TWO! THR-- NO! Tigera is able to get her shoulder up, and also scurry away from Cruz. She stands, hits the ropes and comes at him, but he reverses it, lifting her onto his shoulders and dropping her headfirst to the mat with his MAYAN DRIVER SA: Thanks for coming Tigera, but no one kicks outta that. HJ: That move even put the monster Vance Raymes out two weeks ago. Not satisfied, Cruz climbs to the to rope as the crowd cheers, knowing what's coming. Position himself he leaps. HEAVENS FIRE SPNO! SA: WHAT?! HJ: HAHAHAHA, NOW I REMEMBER WHY I LIKED THIS GUY! Leaping from the top rope, Damien Cruz is caught by the throat. The fans boo, as Alexis Marin lifts him high for a chokeslam, but instead falls back, dropping Cruz headfirst to the mat. SA: What the HELL? WHY DID ALEXIS MARIN DO THAT! HJ: Because he's the coolest! Clawing her way across the mat Tigera falls onto Cruz, who still hasn't recovered from the chokeslam style move. Marin smirks and makes the count. ONE! TWO! THREE! DINGDINGDING SKYE: The.. The winner of this match and NEW Universal Champion.. Tigera. SA: NO! NOT THIS WAY! HJ: Hahaha, It was a SETUP! Marin helps Tigera up and then bullies SKYE into retrieving the title belt. She does, and Marin presents it to Tigera. The Las Vegas fans are extremely displeased. Damien Cruz still sits on the mat, barely moving. Not satisfied with the beating, Marin grabs a steel chair from ringside, bringing it into the ring.. WHAM SA: SOMEONE STOP THIS! Dropping the chair, Marin lifts Cruz, holding him up as Tigera gets a running start and floors him with the 20 Pounds of Platinum. Lifting the title into the air, she screams at the fans. HJ: The Tigera Dynasty has begun, bwahahahahahaha! SA: Why the hell is Marin involved with her? All of a sudden the crowd's reaction turns to cheers as Jason Ryan and Dwayne Bishop come running through. They jump the wall and slide into the ring, collectively stopping the attack. HJ: Oh BOO! SA: Finally someone comes to the aid of Damien Cruz! At least that's how it would seem.. Until Ryan and Bishop join in on the attack. SA: WHAT? HJ: THIS IS THE BEST NIGHT EVER! After sufficiently blooding Cruz, the three men and first female champion stop. Sitting on the stage, clapping and smirking is none other than former FUSE CEO, and current head of the FUSE-W Legal Cousel, Cheryl Madison. SA: What the hell is this? What do these 5 Individuals have to do with eachother, and why have they done this to Damien Cruz? HJ: Who the fuck cares? Point is they're doing it.. and We've got a NEW champion! Tigera is hoisted onto the shoulders of Jason Ryan, Dwayne Bishop, and Alexis Marin as she holds the title over her head in celebration. Her and Marin's plan was complete. They'd gotten Vance Raymes out of the way, gotten control over the officiating of the match, and captured the highest prize in FUSE Wrestling in the capacity of one night. With a mischievous smile on the face of Tigera, the KillZone 10 Broadcast fades to the credits.
