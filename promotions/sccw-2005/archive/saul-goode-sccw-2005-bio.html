Saul Goode Biography Real Name Dag Apell Nicknames Dreads McSurferguy, Holyfuck O'hellyeahman. Hometown Playa Del Mar, CA via Minnetonka, MN Birthday June 16, 1982 Height 6'1' Weight 196 Fed Titles None. Fed Awards None. Other Feds None. Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Surfa Libre, hombre. Finisher Move See below. Finisher Description See below Finisher Setup Move See below, damnit. Setup Description Look. I said see below. I can't be much clearer, can I? Other Moves What is Surfa Libre? Glad you asked. Surfa Libra combines lucha libre with amazing balance and coordination. There are quite a few balance moves that the boys are capable of. Like... Percha Plancha Leaps to the top rope in the middle, and perches himself there for a second or two before leaping. This can be done corkscrew, shooting star and 450 styles. Use your own judgement. Pipeline This is a sort of everything is going right and the ropes have been super tightened in one area kind of mvoe. He likes to run from turnbuckle to turnbuckle. He tries it a lot, fails often, but, crashing and burning didn't take him off a wave, man. Various plancha dives, and crashing and burning are their forte. Saule is the spotfest guy, Ben is the worker, who's got a better grasp of what to do in a match. SINGLE FINISHER: Anything off the top rope that involves a twist, a flip and potential to hurt real bad. Himself, too. TAG FINISHER Eat It - Ben throws opponent into a surfboard submission. Saul comes off the top with a double footstomp into the ol' gas tank. Saul rides for a second before Ben rolls left. Saul jumps and drops a leg where the opponent falls, covering for the pin. Fakie Eat It -- Ben: Surfboard. Saul: off the top rope, landing in a bronco buster on the other guy's guts, ass first. Sometimes, once Ben rolls left, Saul will ride him a second before pinning him. Layback -- Ben: Surfboard, but, not lifted into the air. Instead, he pulls the arms back and makes the opponent kneel. Saul will then either strike with kicks, punches, or a running dropkick/shining wizard. Depends on how mad they are at their opponents. EPIC DROP IN -- The primo there is no getting out of this one, so, if you use it, you'd better make sure you're pinning those motherhubbards move for the win. Saul gorilla presses his opponent. Ben jumps on top and enjoys the ride down, usually drilling his feet into the chest of his opponent. From there, he follows down, landing on the chest of the opponent as he crashes to the mat. While he's gasping for air, the bros are off scouting waves in Tijuana, mang. Entrance Music <a href='http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dr4fP9A3Oi0'>Surfing on a Rocket by Air</a>
