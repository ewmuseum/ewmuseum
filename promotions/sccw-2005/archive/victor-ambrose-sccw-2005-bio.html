Victor Ambrose Biography Real Name Victor Ambrose Nicknames The American Dream. Dusty Rhodes, eat your heart out. Hometown San Antonio, TX Birthday May 31st, 1979 Height 6'2' Weight 230 Other Feds This is Ambrose's first shot at wrestling. Getting punched in the face for money doesn't sound too difficult, does it? Wrestling Style Ambrose learned everything he knows about fighting from random bar room brawls. Finisher Move Imitation is the highest form of flattery... Finisher Description Ambrose copies his opponent's finisher. If impossible, due to the finisher being an extreme high flying move or his opponent being too huge to pick up, Ambrose sticks to his set up move or one of his signature moves. Finisher Setup Move White Light White Heat Setup Description Flowing snap DDT; ie: Raven's Even Flow. Other Moves 'Have a Cigar!' - Superkick. This could practically be considered Ambrose's ultimate signature move. He likes to pull it off out of nowhere, especially when his opponent seems to have the upperhand. 'The Mercy Seat' - DDT into Guillotine Choke 'Your Funeral' - Multiple kicks and punches tacked off with an enziguri kick 'Bad Moon Rising' - Moonsault slam 'Pulp' - Reverse frankensteiner 'Burning Bridges' - Tiger suplex into a pin. 'Absolution' - Inverted bulldog 'Poison the Well' - Double foot stomp from the top rope. Other than those, Ambrose sticks to typical brawler moves that any wrestler could pull off laced with a few good submission holds and the occasional high flyer. Entrance Music 'Goin' Out West' by Tom Waits
