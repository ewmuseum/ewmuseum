Charlotte Ramone Biography Real Name Charlotte Ann Raymond Nicknames 'The Punk Rock Pixie', 'The Pink and Green Machine', Charlie, Chuck. Hometown Boston, Mass. (recently relocated to Miami) Birthday 4/1/1982 Height 5'9' Weight 140 Fed Titles None (yet!) Fed Awards No, sir! Biography THIS SPACE FOR RENT!! Other Feds Some work in small, independent federations of the southeastern United States, most notably the Everglade Wrestling Circle. Other Fed Awards None of note. Wrestling Style Aerial/martial arts mix. Finisher Move 1.) The Kazbah. 2.) Sedated. Finisher Description 1.) A spinning impaler (hook up for an inverted double underhook facebuster - aka the Unprettier. Spin to the left, landing flat out, bringing the opponent around 360 degrees. If that doesn't make sense, <a href ='http://www.youtube.com/watch Finisher Setup Move 1.) The Anarch-Knee. 2.) The CBGB Bulldog. Setup Description 1.) Running double knee into the corner (usually to the back, but can be done to the front). 2.) Running Bulldog. Other Moves Charlotte is a first-degree black belt in karate and a brown belt in aikido. Her moves in the ring are crisp and precise, though she has a tendency to choose moves that are a little bit flashy (she says she can't help it). Given the size and especially weight differential that the Punk Rock Pixie will face in a great number of her matches, however, there seems to come a time where she buckles down and her motions in the ring lose the style and become more businesslike and about winning the matches. Perhaps as she becomes more comfortable in her chances to win matches, she can showboat all the time. Also please, please, -please- remember that, while Charlotte isn't a true babyface, neither she nor Krissy Jamison will cheat in a match. Also know that Krissy really isn't able to throw-down like her partner-in-crime, though, being more the pretty and less the sass. As you can see from the movelist attached that she has a wide variety of impact moves and not a lot of submission moves. Her opponent putting her in a submission would have to be her kayfabed rest. :) She's really not terribly well-versed in submissions -- The Sedated is the only one she's really any good at, and she'll far and away prefer to end matches by rocking the Kazbah. Common Movelist 1.) Crossbody block (to the floor/group of baddies in tag matches, especially) 2.) Spin-wheel kick 3.) Series of karate chops - up, down, up, up, two-handed chop to the chest. 4.) Rolling Thunder 5.) Baseball slide into a Tornado DDT on the floor. 6.) Stepover heel kick 7.) Ropewalk into a hurricanrana 8.) Mat slam aka X-Factor 9.) Spinning backfist 10.) Judo-style shoulder throw 11.) DDT, opponent's legs suspended in the ropes. 12.) Headbutt (a certain Mr. Ware objects strongly to this move due to Charlie's face shield) Signature Moves: Murphy's Dropkick - Missile Dropkick, she can really soar with this one. Lust for Life - Armwrench, stepover the arm with one leg, hook the head and DDT. It's, like, single-arm DDT with one leg draped over the arm. Super-Special Match Finisher: Blitzkrieg Bop - Moonsault into a double foot stomp. This move is next to insane and will blow out her knee one day, so she'll only do it at Events and big matches. Entrance Music 'Thrash Unreal' - Against Me!
