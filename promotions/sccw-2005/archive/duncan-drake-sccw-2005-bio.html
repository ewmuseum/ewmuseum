Duncan Drake Biography Nicknames 'The Hollywood Hellion', 'That Damn Snake' Hometown Palm Desert, CA Birthday 12/18/1985 Height 6'2' Weight 235 Fed Titles Nope. Fed Awards None. Biography Appearance: Drake is one of those guys you just want to hate based on genetics alone. He's a fine-looking specimen, to be sure; short, spiky brown hair, a smile that's seems to be full of more teeth than actually fit in the human mouth. All lean and sculpted courtesy of the best physical trainers (his parents') money can buy, you'd think he'd make an exemplary professional wrestler. Maybe one day, you'll be right. Personality: Duncan's always had everything he wanted -- the fastest cars, the newest toys, or the hottest girls -- handed to him on the proverbial (and sometimes literal) silver platter (ask him about the twins in Tacoma some time). This has naturally led to an expectation that this is always going to be the case, real world or no. He's spoiled and arrogant and condescending, sure (what wrestler isn't?), but he's also conniving and manipulative and is fond of the easy out. Duncan's the kind of guy who, as a kid, muscled the nerd into doing his schoolwork, talked the head cheerleader into fucking him on the first date, stole tests in college, did who-knows-what-else, and always came out of whatever mess he was in smelling like roses. Now he's decided to pick up wrestling. Who knows why -- maybe it's a hobby, maybe it's out of boredom, maybe it's to prove he's tough enough to do it, or maybe there's any one of a hundred other reasons. The problem is, he's not trained. He's got the look, he's got the money, and he's got the pedigree, but he doesn't have the actual wrestling acumen that most people who fill this role do. This is a problem. Background: Duncan Drake, youngest son of Vivian and Maxwell Emerson Drake II, seriously has it all. He's got a bank account that tops out well in the six figures, he's got more cars in his garage than many people have ever owned in their lifetime, and he's got a girlfriend that a lot of men would trade their left nut for (hot, devious, AND kinky? Yes, please). He doesn't have the glossy pedigrees of some, or the flashy moves, but what he's got is a family with deep ties in the Deep South, a desire to do some bodily harm, and a seemingly endless ability to come up with new and creative ways to hurt people. As for the rest of his background, it's still being written. Right now. Out there. On FUSE programming. 'Duncan Drake was born. He was an asshole. He grew up. One day, he decided to use his parents' money to make himself a wrestler, figuring it couldn't be that hard. Then FUSE hired him, because there's no such thing as bad press,' would be how his biography would go so far. MANAGER INFORMATION: Name: MacKenzie Malone. ('Mac' to her friends and the media; 'Kenzie' to Duncan when he's feeling sweet. So, y'know, rarely on that one.) Height: 5'9' Weight: 126 lbs. Hometown: Scottsdale, AZ. Theme Song: 'Misery Business' - Paramore. Appearance: Mac isn't the bleached blonde bimbo one usually sees in this business or that one might expect to find on the arm of Duncan Drake. Sure, she's model-pretty, and, yeah, she's got a body the boys will describe as 'bangin''. (Though the tits? Totally fake.) She generally favors clothes that accentuate rather than revealing outright, always from top-of the-line designers. Her hair is a dark black, like an immaculately styled font of tar, and her eyes are dark as well, a glittering shade of green. The poetic have compared them to emeralds, while the unkind often choose something like 'the entrancing gaze of a snake.' She smiles often, but means it rarely. Generally, she strikes a viewer like a girl you probably shouldn't take home to Momma, but if you did, she'd be perfectly pleasant while she's there. The fact that you'd have to count the fine silverware after you left is another story; though you're just as likely to find that pretty, pretty steak knife in your ribcage. And all the while, she'd still be smiling. Role/Interference Style: If there's any one thing that the pair of Mac and Drake ('Drakenzie' in Hollywood parlance) does well, it's prepare for his opponents. Now, they do this in part because Duncan's just not a very talented wrestler, but they'll cook up some of the crazier plans. Mac herself liberally interferes in Drake's matches, both as 'chick who distracts' and 'hander of foreign objects'. But the key to understanding Mac is that this is a giant game for her -- these wrestlers, they're just playing pieces for her, actors in her play. The crazier, the more outlandish the idea, the more she's all-in -- because if it's not exciting, then it's BORING, and a life that's boring is a life that's not worth living. If that means slapping on a wig and sitting out in the crowd with a half-dozen signs and getting drunk with the plebeians so she can spray some mace in some dude's eyes, so be it. If that means tying one of her fellow valets to a chair in a damp boiler room and threaten her with a switchblade, then just tell her where to sign up, because it's all for a good cause -- her enjoyment. The idea that one of these guys might take a potshot at her is completely foreign to her, and, while she's got some self-defense training (she says her momma made her get it; it both prevents her from swinging a chair like a girl and arguably makes her at least as good a fighter as Duncan, which is saying, like, nothing), she's liable to get the shit knocked outta her but good one day. Other Feds Hah! Like anyone would have him! Other Fed Awards Once won 'Best Dressed' from Mr. Blackwell, but we doubt that counts. Wrestling Style As of this writing and his debut in the sport, Duncan's not very good at this whole 'wrestling' thing. At best, his style could be described as aerial brawler.
