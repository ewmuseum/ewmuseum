The Travellers Biography Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards Inter-galactic Tag Team Champions (by proxy) (unofficial) Biography The Travellers are actually inter-galactic talent scouts for Magrathrea United (a pan-dimensional Brockian Ultra-Cricket team) in search of new talent to propel the team to the top of the UGBUC Champions League. They seek out worlds here in the lower dimensions that have not yet discovered Brockian Ultra-Cricket, yet show a propensity toward violent sport. The Travellers are managed by Grunthar (Magrathea United's VP of New Talent Discovery) and Peete (Assistant VP of Megaphone Maintenance). Being that Grunthar and Peete are hyperintelligent pan-dimensional beings, they project themselves into our dimension in the form of mice. (Yeah, mice. Peete is the gray one.) For sake of simplicity when people ask where they are from, the travellers usually answer 'Wyoming'. Normally this is sufficient to explain their eccentricities, as most people are doubtful Wyoming even exists. Honestly, have you ever been to Wyoming? Ever met someone from there? More likely that some map-maker screwed up, left an empty square, and rather than re-print they just scribbled some nonsense word in the square and called it 'Wyoming'. FYI, the rules of Brockian Ultra-Cricket are as follows. Rule One: Grow at least three extra legs. You won't need them, but it keeps the crowds amused. Rule Two: Find one good Brockian Ultra-Cricket player and clone him off a few times. This saves an enormous amount of tedious selection and training. Rule Three: Put your team and the opposing team in a large field and build a high wall round them. The reason for this is that, though the game is a major spectator sport, the frustration experienced by the audience at not actually being able to see what's going on leads them to imagine that it's a lot more exciting than it actually is. A crowd that has just watched a rather humdrum game experiences far less life-affirmation than a crowd that believes it has just missed the most dramatic event in sporting history. Rule Four: Throw lots of assorted items of sporting equipment over the walls for the players. Anything will do - cricket bats, basecube bats, tennis guns, skis, anything you can get a good swing with. Rule five: The players should now lay about themselves for all they are worth with whatever they find to hand. Whenever a player scores a 'hit' on another player, he should immediately run away and apologize from a safe distance. Apologies should be concise, sincere and, for maximum clarity and points, delivered through a megaphone. Rule Six: The winning team shall be the first team that wins. And now without further adieu, our protagonists. Muck Fichigan is lean, mean, and spastic. He wears opaque black goggles at all times as a safety mechanism (a mere glimpse of the dimentia inherent in his eyes has been known to induce mass-panic). Muck is one of two remaining genetic decendants of the Silastic Armourfiends of Striterax. The other is Chuck Norris. A brief review of the history and nature of the Silastic Armourfiends of Striterax follows: T he Silastic Armourfiends were an insanely aggressive race who lived on the planet Striterax approximately twenty billion years ago 'when the universe was young'. They were extremely keen on fighting - one of the best ways to deal with a Silastic Armourfiend was to lock him in a room by himself, since he would beat himself up sooner or later. They wrecked the surface of their planet in constant wars, and the whole population lived within bunkers deep below the surface. In an attempt to deal with the problems their violent nature created, the Silastic Armourfiends passed a law that anybody who had to carry a weapon as part of their normal work (including policemen, security guards and primary school teachers) must spend a minimum of 45 minutes each day punching a sack of potatoes. It was hoped that this would allow them to work off their surplus aggression. This plan worked only until the Silastic Armourfiends began just shooting the potatoes instead. Muck likes fighting and violence and is quite good at it. He was originally signed by MU (Magrathea United) as a cricket player in his own right, but his inability to adaquately or eloquently apologize without making it sound like a threat and a mama joke combined eventually lead to his demotion to talent scout. Trond Kobiashi MCFarland is a Lamuellan who was signed to MU under the GBD Protocol. (GBD = Generally Big Dude) Tough as an Arcturan Mega-Donkey and twice as strong, Trond lived a very sheltered life as a tackler of Perfectly Normal Beasts on his homeworld, which he normally gave to his friend The Sandwich Maker. TKM was a semi-successful cricketer, but his overly long and emotional speech on the ethics of apology during the gy.125454 quarter-finals cost his team valuable brevity points (despite bringing the other team to tears) and caused his demotion to junior-vice assistant to the clerk of accounting, where he balanced the budget and put the club in the black for the first time in recorded history; causing his further demotion to talent scout under the DMULB Protocol (DMULB = Don't Make Us Look Bad).
