Joshua Kosidlo Biography Real Name Joshua Kosidlo Nicknames The Paladin, The Ironman, The Nightmare, Man of 1000 Finshing Moves Hometown Rome, New York Birthday April 5th 1972 Height 6'5' Weight 275 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Other Feds NTICW, PWA, PCW, Global, WWA, BLAH BLAH BLAH Other Fed Awards NTICW U.S. Champion, NTICW 2nd Ranked Star, NTICW Best Fued (with Hessian), EFWO 3x Intercontinental Champion, EFWO 3x Hardcore Champion, FFWA 6x World Champion, FFWA 6x Intercontinental Champion, FFWA 3x Toughest S.O.B. Champion, Global 1x PrimeStar Cham Wrestling Style Power-Technician, Martial Artist, Submission Finisher Move Kosidlo Driver 07' Finisher Description Inverted F-5 Other Moves Overview: Joshua is a third degree black belt in Martial Arts, but has also studied basic mat wrestling, greco roman wrestling, and combat submission wrestling. He is a 12 year veteran to the business, and a fierce competitor. Joshua makes good use of his power, and his speed/agility to try and out maneuver his opponent, however when it comes to getting down and dirty Joshua is one of the best pure wrestlers in the business. He likes to wear his opponent down the best way possible, for a high flyer he likes to work on the legs with holds and impact moves. Powerhouses, he tries to take out their arms as soon as possible. Sumbissionists, he’ll take them move for move and do what he can to outsmart them. He may also employ some of his strength based attacks, and quick impact moves. Joshua is a near suplexing machine, any suplex that can be thought of, he will do. He also has a bad temper, if someone pulls something cheap in the ring, like an eye gouge, or thumb to the eye, it is likely to anger him. Once under his skin, he is like a beast, relentless and near impossible to put down. This quality in him comes through most often in either hardcore conditions, or against a major rival. Also surfaces when under a great deal of stress. When he is in this state, he will not stop until he cannot move any longer. Also when in this state, he will do whatever he can to put his opponent through as much harm as possible, even if it means hurting himself in the process. He has two weaknesses... the biggest being his right knee, he’s had a bad knee since child hood, every now and then it acts up, usually during cold weather. He may favor it, but will never show the weakness to his opponent. The other is his back, he had an operation on it almost three years ago now, and from time to time it can give him problems. Usually only if he takes a severe bump, or if he lands bad on an edge, like the barricade, stairs, a table or the turnbuckle. ---------- Spots (To be included in matches as you see fit. Heel Announcer: Holy Sh... ---------- Trademark: Bottom of Reality: A Rock Bottom, but instead you wrap your hands around their neck and lift them sweeping their legs out and slamming them like a Rock Bottom. The Eternal Nightmare: A cobra clutch sleeper hold. One arm is behind the neck forcing the forearm there (left arm) and the other arm is wrapped around the chin/neck area, locking the hand into the other arm at the elbow, the opponet's right arm is up next to their neck so as it is not actually a choke hold, and you have their arm and neck in your right arm. The Nightmare Ends: The Eternal Nightmare shot back into a suplex. IronPlex: Ironman grabs his opponent in a reverse DDT set up. He lifts them up holding their tights as though he were delivering a reverse stalling suplex. He drops them down hard on their head and into a cover much like a jackhammer. Check Mate: A reverse DDT/Neckbreaker. Joshua hooks his opponent in a reverse DDT, quickly he twists and drops his opponent in a modified reverse neckbreaker, a lot like a twist of fate. Paladin Plunge: Psycho driver Fallen Angel V 1.0: Full Nelson Suplex/Slam off the top rope Fallen Angel V 2.0: In heated matches Joshua will set a table up outside of the ring where one side is on the apron and the other is on the ground, it will be at a slant. He will then perform the finishing move from the top rope to the outside of the ring through the table. This is the original way The Fallen Angel was done.
