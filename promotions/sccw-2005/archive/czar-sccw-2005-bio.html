Czar Biography Real Name Vladimir Putrovic Nicknames the Dark Czar of Wrestling; Russia's Reaper, the Soviet Assassin Hometown Moscow Birthday February 4th, 1977 Height 6'9' Weight 321 Biography Vladimir was born in communist Russia in 1977 to a wealthy Soviet diplomat father and beautiful, blonde mother. He was the prototypical Russian boy; strong, athletic, and smart. He played basketball for the USSR in the 1998 Olympic Games and also starred in the Motherland as a hockey Defenseman. His professional sports' career was coming into prefect focus. Until the war in Iraq began. This war sent Vladimir's family, father mostly, into a state of panic. War this, war that; 'PREPARE FOR BATTLE!' was what Vladimir woke up to from 1998 until 2003; he still hears it to this day. Then Vladimir left Moscow. He would never return. During that time, information came forth that Vladimir had joined the USSR Military Machine and became the leader of an undisclosed branch and squad of elite soldiers that may or may not have been a small sub sect of the KGB. This information has never been confirmed, however. Then in late 2006... Vladimir came back to Moscow. Many called him a myth, a legend; that Vladimir Putrovic died in a tragic plane crash over the North Atlantic. To put the rumors to rest, Vladimir Putrovic held a public demonstration in the middle of Red Square in Moscow - throwing a total of 56 people, soldiers, guards, and children in mind- numbing holds and maneuvers that only one man could pull off - Putrovic. Now, given the named ‘CZAR' by his fellow countrymen, Vladimir is out to accomplish the first goal he had in mind - to become the greatest Russian to ever live. Once again.
