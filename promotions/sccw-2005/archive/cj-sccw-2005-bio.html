CJ Biography Real Name CJ Battiste Nicknames Super CJ Hometown Jacksonville , Florida Birthday March 18th , 2006 Height 5 ft 6 in (168 cm) Weight 147 lb (73 kg) Biography CJ grew up in a small town outside of Jacksonville, Florida called Orange Park. His true passion wasn't professional wrestling but to get a scholarship in soccer to University of Florida as a goalkeeper. Just days before the last game of the season CJ got into a fight with his girlfriend's ex and lost but not before drawing the attention of nearby adminstration. Due to what the fight escladed into CJ was suspended for 5 days in what resulted into missing a chance to play in front of the scouts. On a night with a date of little importantce CJ and his friends went to a local indy wrestling event to watch some of Florida's most underrated talent in an electricfying atmosphere . Reach for what you want or else you will never get it. Simple enough to say, but may be a different story when trying it out. The very next morning CJ found himself in a wrestling school studying up and practicing the basics of ring work. His family wasn't exactly thrilled of his new aspirations of being a wrestler but none the less CJ looked at wrestling as a chance to shine like a diamond in the rough in front of a live crowd, something he was so use to doing. His trainer did more mental training with CJ then physical in hopes of CJ being more creative with his talent , which worked out quite nicely. CJ traveled with a close bunch from his wrestling school on a monthly basis around Florida to compete in various indy feds. It was obvious to the fans and the road agents back stage that CJ was lightyears above the talent that he traveled with and booked him in more single matchups. In three months time CJ already had 4 matches nominated for 'Match Of The Year' in the South Eastern Wrestling Divison. Opportunity sometimes has a strange way of presenting itself as numerous promoters invited CJ to wrestle overseas in Japan. That same night he was already on a plane headed for the rising sun country. CJ came back to the states after 6 months with alot more knowldege and experience under his belt ready to storm back on the indy scene. CJ spent most of his time on the west coast wrestling for promotions in California , Canada and parts of Mexcio as Super CJ. On the day of the 19th birthday he won a tournament entitled 'Super Crusierweight 12 Cup' where him and 12 others battled it out in a iron man match for a total of 2 hours. CJ moved to New York in hopes of better exposer and a chance to wrestle in the Tri-State area where some of your more bigger wrestling companies are found. A man named Brandon Silverstry , who happened to be a huge inspiration to CJ in his short wrestling carrer opened a facility a in Brooklyn , New York. CJ attended the school in hopes of expanding on his talent while learning from one of the greatest in the industry.
