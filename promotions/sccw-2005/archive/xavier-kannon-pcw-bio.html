Xavier Kannon Biography Real Name Christian Alexander Kannon-Hall. Nicknames XK, Gold Patron Meritorious, Midas, The King of Wrestling. Hometown Merlin Bay, Barbados by way of Clearwater, Florida. Birthday 25/12/1975. Weight 233 Fed Awards He may of won a prize for being the 1,000th person through the door with red hair... Biography You know his name... Other Feds PCW, RPW, OSW, PRIME. Other Fed Awards PCW World, Continental, Tag Team, Cruiserweight and Extreme champion. OSW Old School Champion. PTC/WO/eWP Universal Champion. 3rd in PRIME's 2008 Dual Halo. PRIME Best Moment of 2007/08 (the blinding of Jonathan Rhine) and Best Heel of 2007/08. Wrestling Style Bumping, focussed brawler. Finisher Move The Hallmark. Finisher Description Cradle Piledriver. Finisher Setup Move The 7-10 Split / Kick to the gut Setup Description Uppercut Low-Blow / Duh... Other Moves High-angle backdrop suplex, fireman’s carry/airplane spin into a snake-eyes or hotshot, multiple short kneedrops to the back of the neck, pendulum elbow drop to the back of the neck, inverted stunner, hangman’s neckbreaker (sometimes to an opponent seated on the top rope), side Russian legsweep reverse DDT (can be transitioned into a dragon clutch), multitude of neck-based submissions (see: rest holds, complete with taunts and Ellie cheerleading to infuriate the crowd), short-arm clothesline, all manner of eye-rakes, STO used as a counter, STO onto the knee, DDT used as a counter/momentum-turner, choke using his shin while kneeling to appeal to the referee, slingshot plancha. Strategy: Rabid focus on the neck to weaken it for the Hallmark. A master of utilizing illegal tactics out of sight of the referee (often aided and abetted by Ellie at ringside). Notes: 1) If he's caught in a submission which is either going to turn his brain into mushy veg or have him walk with a limp, here's a little hint of what he'll do: Tap-da-de-fucking-TAP. 2) Sells like a fucking PRO. 3) As close to a pantomime villain as wrestling has, and he'll play the part with gusto. Every bit the performer. Sacrifices flashy and complex moveset for a connection with an audience that few others could ever hope for. The Hallmark: His sole, iconic finisher. Will always look to use it to end a match, even if his opponent is already out. A vicious cradle piledriver sold as neckbreaking. If ever done onto a chair/on the floor, the commentators should be dialling a mortician. Eleanor Kannon-Hall: Kannon’s long-time valet turned fiancé turned wife turned mother of his children. She’s a ruthless, scheming tactician and unrivalled motivator all wrapped up in a lip-smacking size-zero package. Will be vocal throughout the match with encouragement. Will (very, very) wisely stay a million miles clear of any physicality. The one rule to rule them all; if anyone lays a finger on the mother of his children, Kannon will END them... and it won’t be pretty. Her entrance theme if needed is I Know UR Girlfriend Hates Me by Annie preceded by the "Now the party don’t start ‘til I walk in…" line from Ke$ha’s TiK ToK.
