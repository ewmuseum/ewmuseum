Baron von Blackberry Biography Real Name Science means not having a real name. Nicknames The Devil Fruit, The Most Diabolical Fruit Of Them All Hometown The Scenic, Yet DIABOLICAL Nation of Fruitsylvania Birthday Look, I'm not going to lie. There was an accident. With a time machine. We're not sure what happened there. Height 6' Weight 224 Fed Titles SCCW Gateway Champion (1x, current), SCCW Livewire Champion (1x) Fed Awards He stole a SCCW Wrestler of the Year award from the future. Does that count? Biography The 'diabolical' dictator who rules over all he surveys with a iron, yet fruity fist. He comes from the highlands of Fruitsylvania, claiming to rule an entire empire with science. He doesn't, really. But don't tell him that. His feelings are delicate and fragile, even if he does claim to be the 'most diabolical fruit of them all'. Other Feds None... that we know of. Other Fed Awards None... that we know of. Wrestling Style All-arounder Finisher Move 1) Blackberry Storm, 2) Blackberry Curve, 3) Blackberry Bold Finisher Description 1) Legsweep DDT, 2) Horns of Aries/Last Chancery, 3) Frog splash Finisher Setup Move Retcon Punch Setup Description The dreaded punch of the Baron. The Baron rears back and shouts 'RETCON!' and then unleashes a big punch while shouting 'PAUNCH!'. Its effects are very wide, far-reaching, vary from opponent to opponent, and oftentimes ridiculous. Other Moves Signature Moves: - Retcon Punch (Blackberry will rear back while shouting 'RETCON!' and then lunge forward with the punch while shouting 'PAUNCH!'. Is generally the weirdest move Blackberry has, its effects are wild and varied and depend on the opponent taking it. Should be used in each and every match Blackberry wrestles.) - Kajitsu Elbow (Opponent dead center in the ring, the Baron runs off one set of ropes, then the second set, and drops a big front elbow to the forehead.) - Royal Butterfly Stretch (Double underhook lock with body scissors) Other Moves: European uppercut, superkick, belly-to-belly suplex, leg lariat, flying knee strike, blind springboard hurricanrana, run-up super hurricanrana, swinging neckbreaker while the opponent is trapped between the top and second ropes, running double knee in the corner followed by a monkey flip, corner handstand into a mule kick, STO, pendulum backbreaker, running dropkick in the corner, anklelock, Cattle Mutilation, seated surfboard with headscissors, Mexican Surfboard Style Notes: Don't be fooled by his appearance. Sure, he's kind of crazy, always prone to maniacal laughter, and he's always talking about taking over the world with his army of robotic minions. Hell, he claims to be from a place that probably doesn't exist and is often described as both 'scenic' AND 'diabolical'. However, underneath that blackberry mask and lab coat is an intelligent and well-versed wrestler who seems to have several years of experience behind him. He'll do comedy bits (especially the dreaded Retcon Punch) just as much as his actual wrestling bits, though, so consider him to be similar to a lighter Colt Cabana. The Baron uses a high-flying style, blended with elements of technical and submission wrestling. His style uses an array of crazy, though not especially flippy, high flying maneuvers. He makes the hurricanrana into an artform and he especially likes to use flying kicks like missile dropkicks, leg lariats, and flying knee strikes. The height of his high-flying game comes in the form of the Blackberry Bold, a very potent (and otherwise very normal) frog splash. His submission game is surprisingly well-versed, and while the Baron is not adverse to tapping out if he gets caught in one, himself... it's hard to really catch him as he'll often have a counter out of a submission. He knows how to apply holds from pretty much any position he can, and his submission game is often the one part of Blackberry's wrestling style that people don't notice. His submission style culminates in the twin threats of the Royal Butterfly Stretch and the Blackberry Curve. Apart from all that, his Blackberry Storm can often finish a match suddenly. His weaknesses are his relative size (being 6' and 224 lbs. means that he isn't exactly going to win power matches with Lance Marshall) and the comedy he mixes in with his wrestling style that frequently ruins his well-laid plans. In addition, the Baron has an almost surprising aversion to cheating and the use of weapons (he never does either of these things) and is prone to retreating if the odds seem overwhelming at first (though don't think he's run away, he might just return to beat you down when you least expect it). Apart from using the Retcon Punch, he probably isn't going to strike a woman with his fists, either. Entrance Music 'Moskau' by Dschinghis Khan
