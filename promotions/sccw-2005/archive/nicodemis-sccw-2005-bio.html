Nicodemis Biography Real Name Nick O'Demes Nicknames Nicodemis or Nicos Hometown Dublin, Ireland Birthday 10/17/77 Height 6'3' Weight 250 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography Hi, have you seen my grandfather? I don't know where he is. I'm very worried about him... Nicos is a greek- irishman who is 6'3' 250LBS. He is toned but not one of those boby building freaks!!! due to his greek heritage he has dark features with a natural tan. His grandfather has taught him everything he knows.During a match, Nicodemis, got into a spot where he needed a tag from his partner, Nicodemis's grandfather shook his head no and walked up the gangway into the backstage area, and was never seen again. Needless to say Nicodemis lost the match... Now he is desperatly searching for him and will stop at nothing till he finds him.
