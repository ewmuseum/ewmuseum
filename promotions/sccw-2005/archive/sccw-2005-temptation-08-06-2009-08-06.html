But even if things hadn't been going relatively well, it's unlikely that you'd be seeing Nigel Kensington complaining this evening. There's two reasons for this.  The first is that typical British propensity for "making do" (what most folks tend to call the "stiff upper lip"), a state of mind whereby complaining just isn't done.  You just got on with the problem at hand and did the best you could. The second is the fact that Nigel Kensington is currently the head of Sin City Championship Wrestling.  And if there's one thing Nigel has learned on the job, it's that SCCW tends to be a living example of "sod's law": whatever could go wrong not only went wrong but tended to do so in the loudest, most fantastic (and usually destructive) way possible.  So nights like tonight (at least so far) were to be taken for what they were: a rare treasure. Another rare treasure was the fact that the gentleman sitting across from him at this moment had actually gone to the trouble of properly setting up an appointment for this evening (even calling Nigel's secretary and everything).  His employees were certainly not shy about airing their grievances (and it was usually grievances) but their habit of barging into his office and going off without a moment's warning tended to be somewhat...aggravating.
