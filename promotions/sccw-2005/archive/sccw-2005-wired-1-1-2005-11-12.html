Wired 1.1 Madison Square Garden — New York City, New York ---- Garnering The Ghost: Part I In one Adjective, how would you describe yourself? If you were Angelo Sinclaire, the words that'd come to mind would be "Powerful" "Rich" "Successful" "Attractive" and "Suave". Of course, thats more than one Adjective, but a man of his stature rarely follows the rules. So it was mentally troubling that a man that was once on top of the world, was now sitting in a lavish office in Japan, practically begging for money. His corporation XCW had took off like a 757, merchandise sales in the millions, ratings and PPV buys higher than any wrestling company in the foreseeable past, and it had fallen just as quickly. Through a series of bad Investments, XCW Commissioner Rick Balooga sunk the XCW product, running off talent like Majin and Demento. During the height of his Wrestling success, Sinclaire had trademarked the name FUSE for use at the start of 06'. He wanted to open another smaller promotion, that was aimed towards late teens and adults. Aired on HBO, in a completely uncensored atmosphere. After he went Bankrupt from his divorce and the failure of XCW, the idea went on the back burner. When the last of his funds were gone, Sinclaire realized he needed FUSE to get on it's feet. He had so many ideas, not to mention most of the XCW talent. Which brings us to why we're here. He'd had no success getting funding in the US. So he'd come to Japan, hearing a conglomerate called Ketsito was interested in the American wrestling business. Now he sat in front of one half of it's founders Hiro Ito, in a last ditch attempt to get FUSE in motion. Hiro Ito: I Must tell you, Mister Sinclaire, your offer is very intriguing. Sinclaire Nods.
