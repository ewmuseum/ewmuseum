Joaquin Pierre Biography Real Name Joaquin Pierre Nicknames Frenchie, The French Guy, The Frenchman Hometown Paris, France Birthday September 24, 1976 Height 6'3 Weight 252 Fed Titles Gateway Champion (2/25/08-current) Fed Awards none as of yet Other Feds GLOBAL Other Fed Awards GLOBAL Platinum Champion x2, GLOBAL International Champion x1, AWC Livewire Champion (1x, Current) * Title was won from Corvus after the closing of AWC Wrestling Style Kickboxing/boxing/submissions mixed with suplexes and other such moves. All arounder Finisher Move see move list Finisher Description see move list Finisher Setup Move see move list Setup Description see move list Other Moves Fighting Style: Primarily martial arts/striking/an MMA type style. Joaquin is an absolutely lethal striker, and this should be indicated in his matches. In addition to his striking, he is well versed in submissions, again, MMA style, and technical style wrestling. He has some limited aerial ability for a man of his size, and can pull of some big time aerial assaults in big match situations, though he tends to stay to his strengths of striking and technical wrestling unless he has to. Martial Arts styles that Joaquin has studied and is very well versed in are Muay Thai, Judo, Savate, Sambo, and Brazilian jiu-jitsu. Finisher (s): 1) Au Revoir - Pierre lifts his opponent in a fireman's carry and spins them out as if for an F5. During the spin, he turns them in midair and drops onto his back, bringing the opponent crashing down onto his knees with a devastating double knee back breaker. (Main Finisher) 2) The Gallows - Over the shoulder single leg Boston Crab. With the opponent on the mat, lying face down, Pierre drapes one of the opponent's legs over his own neck. He then turns and sits on the opponent's back, placing pressure on both the leg and the spine of his opponent. Setup Move (s): C'est Fini - A straight, vicious, MMA style head kick with as much force as he can muster. Trademark/Favorite Submissions - Joaquin has sick flexibility which allows him to pull off a lot of submissions from positions that are usually not an option. He loves the guillotine choke, and uses it for a tapout whenever possible. But his submission game isn't limited to the guillotine. Some other favorites include the - Triangle choke (often partnered with a kimura armbar as the same time.) - Gogoplata (Pierre gets into a rubber guard position with his legs high against the opponent's back. Using his flexibility, he slips his foot in front of the opponent's head and under his chin, locks his hands behind the opponent's head and chokes his opponent by pressing his shin against the trachea.) http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogoplata - Texas Cloverleaf - Arm triangle choke - Anaconda choke Entrance Music A Tout Le Monde by Megadeth
