Josh Biography Real Name Josh Nicknames American Youth Hometown Seattle, WA Birthday 07/09/1983 Height 5'9 Weight 210 Other Feds Global Wrestling, core Wrestling Other Fed Awards GW World Champion, GW Cruiserweight Champion, GW Primestar Champion, GW Master of the mat 2004, winner of GW COTT 2004, core Wrestling Tag Team Champion Wrestling Style Puroresu Finisher Move Cycle down. Finisher Description Running (ground level) shooting star press. Finisher Setup Move Youthful Descent. Setup Description Grabbing the opponents arm (typically when dazed) Josh leaps to the top rope and performs a 'ropewalk flipping DDT.' Other Moves When fighting similar sized opponents Josh fancies himself to be a suplex expert, amongst his favorites are the following: Snap suplex Wheelbarrow suplex German suplex Dragon suplex Tiger suplex Northern lights suplex Fisherman suplex Also found in his repetoire: Spinning DDT Falcon Arrow Snapmare (always followed by a brutal low dropkick to the back of the opponents head) Half-nelson bulldog Facebuster Hurricanrana Submission moves that Josh would use would be a sharpshoot or STF. With a martial arts background Josh tends to throw in a lot of kicks and knees. The spinning heel kick, the enzeguri and the shining wizard are also favorites of his. Josh prides himself on being the 'highest flying wrestler on the globe' and is particularly fond of springboard maneuvers. Amongst his favorite are the following: Moonsault Moonsault legdrop Springboard moonsault Split-legged moonsault Standing moonsault 450 splash Frog splash Kamikaze headbutt Somersault legdrop Hurricanrana To the outside: Springboard moonsault (off of the ring apron) Crossbody Dropkick (diving between the middle and bottom rope) Ring entrance: The thunderclap of drums is heard over the speakers as neon blue lasers light up the entrance beneath the Sin Screen. A guitar joins the drums as the song explodes into the familiar strains of ‘My Hero’ by Foo Fighters. White fog billows from the entryway as the lights go off, leaving only the lasers to light the arena. The lasers dance frantically across the fog, dozens of beams darting about. A lone spotlight cuts through the arena, circling wildly before coming to a rest on the fading white fog. The lasers dart away, turning on the crowd as the spotlight illuminates the man standing at the top of the stage… ‘American Youth’ Josh. With arms thrust back and chest puffed out Josh whips his head to the left, his hair flipping behind him as he grins at the cheering crowd. Dressed in a shimmering silver robe that extends past his knees and is absent the sleeves American Youth snaps his shaded eyes to the other side of the crowd, eliciting an even stronger reaction. Staring up at the ceiling Josh shrugs the gleaming robe off of his shoulders and tosses his dark shades on top of the robe. Josh lowers his head, raising his left fist into the air. He pauses for only a second before snapping his eyes back up and darting towards the ring. Josh stops at the ring steps and leaps to the apron a smug smile spread across his face. Grabbing the top rope Josh leaps again, this time into the ring where he stands defiantly. Entrance Music My Hero by Foo Fighters
