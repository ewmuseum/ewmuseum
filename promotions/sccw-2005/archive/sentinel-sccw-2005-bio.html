Sentinel Biography Real Name Wolfgang Moriarty Hometown New York City Birthday 38 Yrs old Height 6'5' Weight 250 Biography "It’s time I started acting like a father." The Sentinel’s sudden venture into the Primetime Central community, stemmed from a desire to retire from the business in order to devote all his attention to raising his only child. He worked for an Albany based Indy Wrestling league for fourteen years and was rarely alone with his daughter, Angela Moriarty, instead putting faith on babysitters and family friends. But after Angela became more and more rebellious with him, the Sentinel knew that he had to raise her by himself and promised that this would be his last wrestling stint. He also promised that he would tell Angela the truth. For fourteen years, Angela was kept in the dark about many things. She never knew that her father, Wolfgang Moriarty, was the Sentinel. She never knew what happened to her mother. The Sentinel had informed her that her mother left them on the day of her birth. It was a lie that the Sentinel intended to explain. Unfortunately, for him, the events that transpired at the annual GTT tournament greatly distanced the two of them even more. By design, Angela met Cassidy Raines a very attractive young man who had grown up alone after his parents died during the 9/11 terrorist attacks. He introduced Angela to his reach out program for underprivileged children, called Seconda Vita. In reality, the program was a cover for an underground organ transfer black market organized by Karla Segur (the Sentinel’s ex girlfriend) in order to obtain human experiments for her work. It was not by coincidence that Cassidy met Angela, as he had been informed of who she was by Karla, long before he ever met her. He was to charm Angela to join his group and give her trust to him unconditionally. However, he soon found himself sympathizing with Angela more and more and eventually started to protest Karla’s plans for her supposed daughter. Karla dropped the bombshell on Angela that she was her mother. At that very moment, the Sentinel rushed into the living room in order to sort things out, but he was too late. Karla had convinced Angela she was her mother. Not having any way to deal with this, Angela rushed out of the room. Before the Sentinel chased after her, he demanded Karla to explain what had happened. Why had she told Angela she was her mother? "We all pay our dues in the end." Karla explained to the Sentinel what he had already known. The love of his life, Abigail Moriarty, was unable to bear any children. But in secrecy, she had sought her expertise as a scientist. Karla Segur had devised a method for women of advanced sterile disorders, to be able to reproduce via a means similar to invitro. When other scientists first heard of the method, they accused it of being something similar to a "friggin potion". Karla then explained that Abigail agreed to her terms: she would carry the baby as long as she would give the baby up to Karla once it was born. When the Sentinel explained that Abigail was much too strong and proud to ever agree to that, Karla then explained that the baby Abigail carried was hers all along. She explained that during the process she had suspected that Abigail would turn on her. So she had collected specimens of her own eggs and exchanged the vials. Abigail had been carrying her child and Angela was her child. Stricken with grief with the news that his late wife, Abigail, had unwittingly given birth to the child of his most hated enemy, the Sentinel refused to chase after Angela. But as fate intervenes (or perhaps God?), the Sentinel later received news from Karla’s longtime assistant, Nicole Schleider. Nicole was Karla’s assistant during that time and she explained to the Sentinel that Angela was indeed Abigail’s daughter. After hearing the Sentinel’s account of Karla’s story, Nicole explained that she switched the vials before Karla attempted her deceit. Karla had unknowingly contaminated her own specimen and actually helped Abigail give birth to a child of her own. When the Sentinel went to visit Nicole for her help, he found her body in the living room. Her body was covered entirely in rashes, with purple bruises and her eyes rolled into the back of her head. She had been poisoned. The Sentinel knew who was responsible and knew that Karla had known of what Nicole did long before these current events. Having realized that the Sentinel now knew the truth about his daughter and that he would tell Angela the truth, Karla revealed her true intentions. She had been obsessively in love with the Sentinel and with the thought of them having a family. When she learned of Nicole’s betrayal, she went insane. Matters worsened for her when she learned she had cancer. Living in supreme denial, Karla convinced herself that the only way she could find a cure for her disease was to experiment on humans. Later, she convinced herself that she needed Angela’s body, still convincing herself that Angela was her own child. With this current turn of events Karla informed Cassidy that it was now time to turn Angela over. But by this time Cassidy had now adopted Angela as a little sister and refused. He broke down in front of Angela and confessed everything to her. He even told her that his parents didn’t die in the 9/11 attacks, but instead were burnt alive by Karla. He was living in servitude of her out of fear. Karla grew enraged and promised herself that she would take matters into her own hand. As the Sentinel and his daughter left the scene of the GTT6 finals, they walked outside onto the streets. A screeching car came at them in full speed. The Sentinel watched in horror as the car aimed straight for Angela. But at the last moment, Cassidy pushed Angela out of the way and found himself pinned against the wall, his body nearly broken in half. With his dying breaths, he apologized for all the lives he had ended in turning them over to Karla. Meanwhile, Karla tried frantically to back the car out for another hit on Angela. But as she backed onto the streets, her small Porsche was viciously run over by a truck. The truck dragged the Porsche for several yards, pinning Karla underneath its massive weight. The Sentinel watched the entire scene and traced the long line of thick red blood across the street. He peered into the car and covered Angela’s eyes from what he saw. Karla was a mangled mess inside the car. But despite the tremendous pain she was in, she looked at the Sentinel and grinned. She died in that position. As the media began to surround the scene, the Sentinel escaped with Angela and confessed to her all that he needed to. He explained to her that he was the Sentinel and that he had been wrestling for fourteen years. He then explained the truth about her mother, Abigail Moriarty. He explained that her mother never left them. Instead, she had died of complications during child labor. He was depressed for a very long time and never came to terms with his wife’s death. But in telling Angela the truth, he not only came to terms with his daughter, but he came to terms with himself. Finally, after sixteen years of grief, he accepted his wife’s death and realized that there was nothing he could have done. He also realized that he didn’t need to wear the mask of the Sentinel anymore. A few weeks after the end of GTT6, the Sentinel decided to apply to the FUSE wrestling federation. Having come to terms with the death of his wife and having renewed his relationship with his daughter, the Sentinel now has nothing to worry about as he strives to be very successful within the FUSE organization. He no longer wears a mask, because he no longer has anything to hide.
