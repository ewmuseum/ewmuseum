Damien Cruz Biography Real Name Damien Cruz Nicknames 'The Latin Assassin', 'The Last Son of Lucha Libre', 'Mr. Cruz' Hometown Cielo Azul, Mexico Birthday 11/25/79 Height 6'2' Weight 225 Fed Titles FUSE Universal Champion x2 Biography He has walked dusty roads, and gold-paved aisles. He has bled for gathered masses and defied gravity in the company of kings. From the small-time Mexican promotions, to the blood-fed Asian hardcore circuit, and now at the gilded neon spotlight of the American wrestling world, The Latin Assassin has evolved through the decade to be among the sport's finest. There have been pitfalls and delays and unnerving questions placed before him that he must atone for. There are swirling doubts he must dispel. And there are heresies to unspeak. He shall, without compromise, do what he must to define himself, and to uphold the upper limits of what can and should be done in the squared circle. It has begun again. Other Feds Many. Other Fed Awards So very many. Wrestling Style Hybridized luchalibre/hardcore/submission Finisher Move Mayan Driver I, II, III Finisher Description Fireman carry(I), Powerbomb Lift (II), and running sunset flip (III) to Piledriver, Finisher Setup Move Dragon-Fire Kick Setup Description Swift-spinning Sweet Chin Music/ Other Moves Fighting Style: Various luchadore/RVD/AJ Styles/Sabu-style creativity. Originally trained as a lucha libre purist, but later expanded his aresenal during his time in the Japanese Hardcore scene. Technical wrestling, shoot wrestling, lucha libre, and plain old-fashion teeth-kicking are all second nature to him. A lot of precise moves. Damien is a man of economy. He will do a high spot in order to capitalize on a position, not to show off how cool he is (ie: take a leap from the top of the ladder to execute a move on a vulnarable opponent, not find one and climb it). He is exceptionally skilled at controlling the pace of the match, but can be prone to bouts of frustration. When the match is going his way, he is almost mechanical in his efficiency, brutality, and methods. When things are not going his way, he can be desperate and careless unless he gets his head back into the game plan. When he loses, it is usually a result of him trying to fix what may not be broken. His lucha libre training has made him creative, explosive and confident. His Japanese training has made him economical, stoic, and very violent. And his years in the US and Canada has taught him how to mesh what he knows with submission wrestling, technical skill and brass-knuckles pragmatism. Entrance: When he enters the ring, he does so in pitch black with a red background behind the entryway. As he steps out onto the ramp, Red and White sparks rain down over the entryway (Goldberg-ish). Then, the lights raise, and he travels down the ramp. Damien, like I said above, is a man of economy. While adrenaline usually sends men bouncing from the rafters, Damien channels it into pure focus. He will slap a few hands when he is confident, and will climb the ropes and greet the crowd. When he feels like he is walking into a real challenge, he is more focused, usually just greeting the fans with a distracted wave or fist pump. He usually steps up onto the apron in a single step, then springs over the top rope. Or, when the pressure is really on, he'll step throuugh the middle ropes and test the ropes absently. In a lot of ways, he's feast or famine. Segment/Interactions: (if ever a person wants to use him) Damien is world-weary but not burned out. He has learned how to go along to get along, but also how to stand up for himself. He usually meets hostility with sarcasm or self-deprecation, but knows when the line has been crossed. Willing to go blow-for-blow if pushed hard enough, but smart enough to keep his cool to remain clear-minded. One thing to play upon is the fact that his in-ring personality is very violent, cold, and efficient; while his day-to-day personality is diplomatic, introspective, patient, and permissive. Without being dramatic, Damien Cruz and The Latin Assassin are very different people. Misc. Stuff That May Never Mean Anything But May Be Worth Knowing: - Damien graduated high school at 17, and left for Japan three months after graduating. His education was then provided by a used laptop he bought with his first paycheck in Japan, and any internet connection he could find. An avid reader and natural learner, Damien holds his intellectual growth in high regards. -His mother was a Puetro-Rican teacher who was raised in The Bronx, New York. She moved to Mexico where new schools were being built by American investors. Lydia Martinez met and fell in love with a Mexican writer, Ivan Cruz. Three years after their marriage, Damien was born. -Damien, while born and raised in Mexico, is completely bi-lingual. His American mother saw English as an essential advantage. Also, the schools he attended taught concurrently in English as a part of a pilot program under The Good Neighbor Acts. -Damien Cruz has occasionaly turned his back on companies who offered him great sums of money to play up his Mexican heritage in demeaning ways. One such company black-listed him when he refused to enter on a riding mower carry a leaf-blower and use it to win matches as a gimmick. -Damien is a card-carrying member of the American Socialist Party, but no longer considers himself a true Socialist. He is strongly anti-globalization, anti-NAFTA, and a critic of what he sees as a 'Pro-fascist' administration in recent years. He has often been a protester and activist, but remains out of the public light in that facility. He refuses to use his name to make his causes more visible, and thereby discredit them. While no evidence could be found to prove it, officials sought to question Damien in connection to donations he allegeldly made to the Zapatista Revolutionaries in Mexico. -Is ambidextrous, but favors his right hand for writing and fine tasks. -Damien plans to retire at the age of 30, and pursue his education in Law. -Damien is an athetist until the scientific community can convince him otherwise. -Damien Cruz developed two passions in his time in Japan; Kyudo (archery) and Kenjutsu (swordmanship). While still very much a novice in the traditional sense, what he has learned has made him relatively skilled in both arts. He continues to train both in New York and when he travels in Asia. umm...I think that is a lot to work with. If curiosity truly gets the best of you, ask me.
