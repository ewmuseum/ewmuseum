Zakk Wylde Biography Real Name Zakk Wylde Nicknames Wyld Child Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Birthday 3/2/1975 Height 6'5' Weight 265 Biography The man that has spent time as a champion in the GWA, ECCWF, UW, NEW among others wasn’t always the man that you see today. He grew up just outside of Columbus, Ohio. Not always the best student he did okay. While his other classmates were making their plans for post secondary education…he focused his attention on the only thing that he really identified with. Professional Wrestling. Afraid of the reaction of his parents he waited, laid in bed many nights practicing exactly what he would say. Trying to figure out his parent’s objections and how to overcome them. It had to be perfect if he was going to win them over. As winter gave way to spring the time had come to have the "talk". He had the speech down pat, there was nothing that they could throw at him that he wasn’t prepared for. But what happened next blind-sided him. He sat down and finally spoke the words out loud to his parents, his dream to become a professional wrestler. Everything was going fine, he was calm spoke slowly and carefully gave the appropriate pause where his parents would then start grilling him on specific questions but those questions never came. Instead he received roaring laughter. Hurt he quickly became flustered and started to blurt out all the things he thought they would say and after they saw his reaction they finally stopped laughing, long enough to ask him if he was serious. Hoping this was a turning point and that he could get his speech back on track he enthusiastically replied yes. Once again the reaction he was hoping for never came. Instead he was met with an onslaught of negative comments. Things like he was too weak, he’d never make it in such a tough sport. That he should just go out find a minimum wage job and be happy with that cause that was he was cut out for. Although hurt by his parents comments he continued with his plan to become a professional wrestler. He worked part time for less than minimum wage for a local wrestling promotion, setting up the ring or chairs, selling merchandise. All that time he got the opportunity to really get an education about the business. A few of the vets took him under their wings and got to learn how to work inside the ring. After several months he felt he was ready for his first match. Through bad luck, another wrestler getting hurt, he was going to get his chance for his first match. In a last ditch attempt to sway his parents negative attitude to his chosen vocation he invited them to see his match. Again all he got in return was contempt. They once again verbally abused him and when he wouldn’t back down threatened to disown him if he went ahead with this foolishness. He never looked back. That night he packed what he could into a duffel bag and walked away from the only place he had ever called home and never looked back. His first match was nothing spectacular but it did wonders for his confidence. He really felt like he could do this. After working for a while with that promotion he got his first lesson in the real world. Growing frustrated with the constant promises from the promoter that never materialized he moved to another promotion. While he was getting better the same scenario would play out over and over no matter how many times he moved to a new promotion. He would have a run of success and right before he would get the opportunity to face the champion he would have the rug pulled out from under him. He began to have doubts. The verbal assaults from his parents would haunt his every waking moments. He was determined to succeed, the destiny that his parents foretold for him was not going to come to reality. He was going to make it happen. He couldn’t escape those thoughts though. The pressure was mounting and after a good run with a Texas based promotion and another loss to the champion he decided to pool all his money together and pack it in. He left the ring, didn’t even bother to shower. He simply changed his clothes and walked out of the arena loaded up his belongings into a beat up Plymouth horizon and headed for Las Vegas. The whole time on the road he thought about his situation and realized that his parents were right. There was something just not quite right with him. He couldn’t put his finger on it but he couldn’t go on living this way. He knew he couldn’t continue to let the ruthless promoters take advantage of him, and he didn’t want to put his tail between his legs and go back to Ohio. He never wanted to face his parents again….if they would have just accepted what he wanted to do maybe things would have been different. He would have had their confidence in him to keep pushing, to keep trying even though he didn’t think he could. So by the time he pulled into the oasis in the desert, Las Vegas, he knew what he would have to do. It was time to end his miserable exisistance on this earth. If he couldn’t succeed in this business then he didn’t want to live another moment. With only a little bit of money in his pocket he checked into a motel room, one that you won’t see in the brochures from the Las Vegas travel industry. There he sat trying to figure out just how he was going to carry out this deed. He didn’t have enough cash to purchase a gun, he wanted something quick so slashing his wrists would be too slow, not to mention messy, and he had trouble swallowing pills. On the ride through the desert everything seemed so easy. Frustrated that he couldn’t even get this right he decided that if this was to be his last night on earth he should at least enjoy the night life that Vegas offers once. Gambling away the money didn’t seem like all that much fun so instead he decided to drink the night away, who knows maybe some woman would take pity on him and he’d get lucky. Or better yet maybe he’d drink himself to death. That decision would change his life forever. Toward the end of the evening he met a strange man, a man that seemed to understand him. He empathized with him. He couldn’t believe that he was sitting on this barstool spilling his life story to some stranger, but somehow it seemed right, but yet he kept a few details to himself….like wanting to be a wrestler…he didn’t need anyone else looking down at him, ridiculing him like his parents did. During the conversation though the strange man told him he might have a friend that could point him in the right direction. Turn his life around. Make him feel better about himself…he was more than willing to tell his friend about him but it would cost him…his soul. Young Wylde laughed at this, and so did the strange man. Sure…sure…he shouted to the entire bar as got off his barstool and downed a shot of Jagermeister. The man reassured him he could make him rich beyond his wildest dreams and that he could be a main event wrestler in no time. This shocked Wylde, he was very sure he never told him what he did for a living….this sobered him up for a moment. Then as he walked out the door he turned back to the man and said….You’ve got a deal! The next thing he remembers is the sound of the 1970s phone on the nightstand ringing off the hook in his ear. He opened his eyes and looked around at his surroundings. This wasn’t supposed to happen…he was still in that damn roach motel room. He was supposed to be dead by now. Why was his phone ringing? No one knew he was going to be here? Despite these questions floating through his head he answered the phone. The voice on the other end identified himself as the owner of the GWA and he had gotten his name from a mutual friend and he’d like to bring him into his promotion. The rest they say is history. On that day he changed forever. He left behind his former persona and became the Wyld Child Zakk Wylde.
