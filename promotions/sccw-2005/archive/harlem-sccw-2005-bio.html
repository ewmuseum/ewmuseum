Harlem Biography Real Name Tyrell L. Jackson Nicknames The Original Gangsta' Hometown Harlem, New York Birthday November 8th, 1973 Height 6'3 Weight 253 Fed Titles FUSE Custom Chaos Champion (Current) Fed Awards n/a Biography Harlem, born Tyrell Lee Jackson in the november of 1973. His whole childhood he was raised in the mean streets of Harlem, New York by his single mother. He witnessed his own mothers death at the age of 13 which was also the night he began his life on the streets. For five long years he spent time staying and sleeping at various different places and he could usually be found with his gang of 'Bloodz' which was a strong force across the united states of america at the time. He was convicted of the murder of twins Antuan and Jerome Smith on December 2nd 1992 and at the age of 18 he was jailed for 13 years. Amongst being released after serving his maximum time in prison he was forced to enter the XCW by his parole officer, as he needs to keep a job to survive. Harlem was left without a job when XCW closed down, he had no future plans, no future goals, no real talents and no one that would hire him. That was until FUSE opened its doors. Since entering the wrestling world Harlem has displayed good fighting...fighting, not wrestling skills and he's shown a nasty mean streak that goes along with it. He's a menace to whom ever his opponent may be, FUSE is on notice... Other Feds Various Great Feds Other Fed Awards Several Titles... The lights darken as red lights flash the arena. Harlem walks from the curtain with the intensity we've grown accustomed to him having. His Custom Chaos championship lies on his muscular right shoulder as he never stops while making his journey down towards the ring. He makes his way up the stairs and to the apron before stopping and looking at the crowd for a couple seconds. He steps through the middle rope and runs to the opposite ropes. He climbs the ropes and holds his hand up high in an 'Eastside' gesture with his fingers before taking off his Hollow Tip red tank top and stretching up on the ropes, awaiting his opponent.
