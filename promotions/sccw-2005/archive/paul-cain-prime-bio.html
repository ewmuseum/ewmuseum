Paul Cain Biography Real Name Paul Cain Nicknames The Lethal Cocktail Hometown Denver Colorado Birthday 28th July 1975 Height 6'3 Weight 235 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Physical Appearance: Paul Cain is built in a very similar physical frame to Shawn Michaels allowing him to be quick and nimble as well as having the strength required to put up a fight at close quarters. Having shaved his hair off last year, it is now growing back to a CM Punk style and length, although not quite the trademark long bangs Cain used to have. Ring Attire: Paul Cain will come to the ring wearing white wristbands on either arm and a pair of hakuna’s and black wrestling boots. The colour of the hakuna’s will change for each match that he takes part in but he will begin with black. Personality: Paul’s personality has changed from being a wallowing and brooding individual to being back to his traditional arrogant and brash self which he showed during his early career in the NWC. Numerous personal trials changed him and after the closure of the NWC, he lost his way and became almost gothic and emo in his behaviour. Now, having dealt with the trials in his family and personal life, he is back to being the Paul Cain that destroyed a Jihad, fraternised with women and generally got under the skin of his fellow workers but to the appeal of the people. Background/History: Paul Cain was a fluke. Before hitting back up with Power in the LSW, he was found by Johnny Holiday, wrestling in the wild and crazy world of Indy feds. He was a hit there, but didn’t have the marketing sense to get himself a contract with any of the big boys. Whilst bored with retirement, Holiday visited one of the bingo halls that was hosting Cain and his colleagues. Johnny loved the wild and almost dangerous style of the man he now calls 'The Lethal Cocktail'. Not only that but Cain’s subtle arrogance on the mic, yet sharp wit and coolness reminded Holiday of himself. Impressed, Johnny went backstage and got the details of the kid and promised him a start in the big-time. However, Holiday got distracted with his work alongside Theresa Angel and the now retired Power in Lone Star Wrestling. Cain patiently waited for his time, wowing the female crowd with his good looks and impressing the male crowd with his skills. With Power quitting to be with his girlfriend, Johnny made a few calls and got hooked up with CCW and where he would bring Cain. Cain had a majorly influential time in the CCW, becoming their top draw and winning the Mid-Atlantic Title and the Heavyweight Title. A stormy relationship with girlfriend and valet Maria Lopez, ended in a bitter split, with Maria leaving to be with Cain’s arch nemesis Rictor Arkane. Maria cited violence and abuse as the reason for the split and that was confirmed as partial truth recently. Eventually Cain departed from CCW after he helped to destroy the Arab movement 'Jihad' led by Mohammed Hazeem, of which he was formerly a member. His departure was enforced shortly after becoming partial NWCal World Tag Champions with his partner SØren, when he was savagely attacked by a wrestler named Brandon Pride. A shattered left knee led Cain to surgery and a lengthy period absent. Paul returned to wrestling to the NWC South, however he was put back on the injured list by Raveller on his debut with the then National Wrestling Council. After further rehabilitation, Cain came back to NWC South a few months later to run his very own show, 'The Cocktail Bar'. This proved to be a real hit with the fans and led to some stormy confrontations between Cain and his old enemy Rictor Arkane, which included the revelation that Arkane had raped and assaulted Maria Lopez whilst in NWC South. This continued their intense rivalry which led to Paul Cain’s eventual wrestling return, which took place in the NWC Atlantic. Initially Paul Cain took on the role of manager to budding star Damien Kahn, who arrived in the NWC Atlantic with Paul. Kahn took the New England Title home but then was defeated by Halloween which led to a concentrated to destroy Arkane once and for all. However Cain soon became obsessed with Leonard Aarons and his apparent chokehold on Atlantic. Dissatisfied that Kahn was not doing a fair enough job with Aarons and Arkane, Paul gained medical clearance to wrestle once more, backed and supported by Maria Lopez’s sister Claudia. Cain waged heavy wars with Aarons, Heatwave and finally Arkane. After winning the NWC Atlantic Battle Royal to determine the Number One contender to Alec Spade’s World Title, Paul took the biggest match of his life, only to be defeated by Spade. This did not go un-noticed by the Atlantic and Cain was rewarded with a Triple Threat match against Arkane and Aarons for the NWC Altantic Heavyweight Title which was then won by Arkane. This set up a bloody and violent conclusion to their feud which saw Cain win the Heavyweight Title at Return To Purgatory 2004. Paul would no doubt claim this helped along Rictor Arkane’s departure from wrestling, although Leonard Aarons would argue differently. Paul would then suffer personal humiliation after Maria Lopez turned on him for not ‘killing’ Arkane as he requested. At the same time, the NWC closed forever and saw the arrival of AXIOM led by Jack Robinson. This coupled with Cain’s personal problems out of the ring saw a different set of performances from Paul. Incensed at the way AXIOM was being run and furious about the death of the NWC, Cain quit the company after losing the AXIOM World Title to Heatwave. Cain then arrived in PRIME and quickly set about making a stir. His main objective was to target the Dark Age and he set about attempting to rally the other PRIME workers against Vampir and his followers. It was however Vampir who got the last laugh, sacrificing Paul Cain at ReV 48 by carving the sign of the Pentagram on the 'Lethal Cocktail' chest. This saw the end of Cain's short career with PRIME before he returned in 2005 and went on to accompany Tony Gamble as his partner and manager. They continued their business association until Cain went on hiatus to deal with family matters outside of the squared circle. He returned to PRIME in October 2006 and it was clear that his personal problems were not completely dealt with. Incensed by what he felt was a neglect of their friendship, Paul and Tony fell out immediately upon Cain’s return. The huge rise in Gamble’s stock however meant that Cain never quite got the chance to exact the personal revenge that he felt Tony deserved and quite rightly PRIME took the sensible step of keeping the two distant from each other. Having been hunted down by Chainz who took an unhealthy interest in Paul’s young daughter, the two began a sick and twisted war which saw Cain decide to depart PRIME once more for the sake of his family.
