The Ghost Biography Nicknames Ghost Hometown Unknown Birthday Unknown Height 6'2' Weight 246 Biography The Ghost is consistantly listed as one of the top wrestlers in the world, with an enigmatic personality and an unmistakable presence, he is unquestonably a world class athelete, with the ability to take it to anyone in almost any style. He's an average sized heavyweight with a vast arsonal of moves and counter moves. Nothing is known about him, not his age, his place of origins or his motivation, beyond some sort of debt to God. It's been revealed that served in some manner of special forces in numberous unreported conflicts, but the details of this service remain patently unclear. The Ghost stands six feet, two inches tall and weighs in at an impressive two hundred and forty five pounds. He's significantly stronger and faster than he looks, fearsome and unrelenting, his record of over fifty wins and only five loses speaks for itself. Appearing first in the UWA, The Ghost quickly broke away and defeated his supposed mentor, capturing in the progress his first singles title, The UWA Global Championship, which he went on to forfit without ever losing for an opportunity to advance his quest for the UWA Universal Title. The Ghost won the Unviersal Title in an Epic Four Corners Elimination Match, defeating three of the top UWA Superstars and ending their careers via the retirement stipulation. The match was a war, and is still viewed as possibly the greatest UWA Match Up of all time. The Ghost went on to capture the Universal Title and hold it twice more until forfiting it when UWA Management went through a massive political upheaval. But not before taking the UWA Universal Title to the debut of the upstart federation XCW, where in a one night single elimination Tournament, The Ghost captured the first ever XCW Heavyweight Championship and almost singlehandedly catupulted the company to its spot as one of the top promotions in the world. The Ghost went on to become one of only two men to hold The Custom Chaos Title, The XCW Tag Titles, and the Universal Title, and since the unexpected collapse of XCW, he has completely dropped off the radar.
