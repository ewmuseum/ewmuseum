Ken Partridge Jr. Biography Real Name Ken Partridge, Jr. Nicknames 'The Dark Messiah' Hometown The SoHo section of New York City Birthday 07.07.77 Height 6'0' Weight 237 Fed Titles None to date Fed Awards None to date Biography PAST HISTORY (updated soon): Born in Birmingham, Alabama, Ken has been in the wrestling business since the age of 16, when he went to train under 'Super Sock' Jose Lothario and 'Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels in San Antonio, Texas, spending every last cent he had simply to make it there. Seeing that he was down on his luck, Shawn trained Ken personally for no charge, let him live in his home, and turned into a surrogate brother to him. He has since referred to Ken as his 'protégé' on numerous occasions and the similarities in their styles in astonishing. They remain closer today than ever and HBK even helped Ken immediately break into the Rajah World Wrestling Federation (RWWF), where he took on the moniker of 'Y2KPJ' Ken Partridge, Jr. and won the RWWF Television Championship. However, the company soon split into separate brands after creative and off camera differences between the suits in the office, namely Rajah (the owner) and Cactus Chad (the commissioner). Ken opted relinquish his title and follow Chad to his new organization, the Alliance of Hardcore Wrestlers (AHW). 'Y2KPJ' was brought in as AHW's main attraction and was heralded by the fans as the franchise, which they chanted throughout arenas when he entered. Unfortunately, one week before his AHW Heavyweight Championship shot, the Alliance folded and went under, but not before Chad and Ken had formed a very strong bond of friendship with one another. After the AHW folded, Ken decided to fine tune his technical abilities and headed to Tampa, Florida, where he attended the Malenko School of Wrestling, trained by Dean Malenko himself and Dean's friend, Chris Benoit. After six months of brutal and intense training, Ken decided it was time to add a bit more to his repertoire and began studying both kickboxing, Jiu Jitsu, and shoot fighting with Ultimate Fighting great, Royce Gracie. During this time, he also studied the arts of Zen and Internal Martial Arts and the temper that once consumed Ken began working to his advantage as he used it at his will. After another six months and a full year away from the ring, Ken was set to make his return. Manic Championship Wrestling (MCW) showed interest in Ken and offered him a contract. He moved to London, England, the base of operations for MCW after signing with the company. He soon became the MCW Television Champion, defeating Rage, an undefeated superstar (much like Ken). Despite this fact, his great fan response, and his unprecedented career long undefeated streak, MCW still refused to give Ken a push into the upper tier, claiming he was too small to be a main event talent. Disgruntled with the company and the fact that the past eight months in London had been miserable, Ken got out of his contract, abandoned the Television Championship, and moved back to the states. After his move to Atlanta, Georgia, Ken had a short stint in the World Wrestling Federation, beginning as a dark horse in house shows. He remained undefeated and dethroned Edge as the WWF European Championship during his first live WWF television appearance, which occurred on SmackDown. Unfortunately, after two months as champion, Ken developed a knee injury and was forced out of in-ring competition. He was released by Vince McMahon and the European Championship was held up in a winner take all Round Robin tournament. The rehab process was one of the most difficult things that Ken had ever experienced, especially due to the fact that he refused to take Pain Killers and Muscle Relaxers due to his past history of substance abuse and the fact that he had been clean, at this point, for seven years, and still is to this day. However, he made a full recovery after a remarkable four months and was once again set for action. The debuting Universal Hardcore Wrestling Federation (UHWF) soon picked up Ken, now known as 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr., and gave him the push he'd been seeking. In the first ever UHWF match, Ken won a 12 man battle royal, last defeating Chris Benoit and Triple H for the opportunity to go on later in the night and face the same two men in a Steel Cage Match for the unclaimed UHWF Heavyweight Championship of the World. However, after the battle royal, Ken was brutally attacked by Triple H and the rest of DeGeneration-X (X-Pac, Scott Hall, and Kevin Nash). Suffering a concussion from the attack due to the fact that each man hit Ken with a string of their own signature maneuvers, all of which end with a fall to Ken's head, doctors demanded that Ken withdraw his name from the night's main event. The match began without him, but soon after, Ken was making his way to the ring, where he stunned the world with a spectacular Kenton Bomb off the top of the cage. He went on to win the match, becoming the first and, unfortunately five months later, the only UHWF Heavyweight Champion of the World, as the UHWF was forced to close it's doors due to a financial situation. Ken was immediately picked up by friend, Kevin Nash, in World Championship Wrestling. Once again, Ken's first match with them was for the WCW Heavyweight Championship at the debut of WCW Monday Nitro Uncut in his hometown of Birmingham, Alabama. Ken defeated Stone Cold Steve Austin for the title and later that evening while celebrating at the popular Birmingham nightclub, The Blue Monkey, Ken met his soon to be fiancée, Lily. She immediately recognized who Ken was and informed him that she was very interested in him. Ken came to find out that Lily had been working the independent scene for the past three years under the guise of Shanghai Lily. After their engagement, Ken wanted her to go on the road with him and become his valet. Therefore, she was offered a contract by Shane McMahon, the WCW CEO and owner, and the opportunity to work with Ken. She accepted, but only to be shafted by Shane. Upon hearing this, Ken quit WCW and wasn't again seen in a wrestling ring for six months. After a short stint in New Era Wrestling (NEW) as the Chief of Staff, Ken took time out of the public eye and began participating in local Underground Fight Clubs in the Birmingham – Jefferson area. These events commonly took place in a basement or cellar and placed Ken up against opponents the like that he had never faced in the ring. Local truckers, cowboys, and bikers laced the cement arenas night after night as opposed to the technical artists and Grecco-Roman masters that he was used to facing off against in the squared circle. He immediately began instituting his Jiu Jitsu and shoot fighting elements that he learned through the teachings of Royce and saw repeated success. After months of this, Ken got a call from Darkfalls and ended up in neWave Wrestling's developmental program, Club Chaos Wrestling (CCW), where he continued his rise to the top, taking his moniker from the fans and dubbing himself 'The Franchise' Ken Partridge, Jr. After a little more than a month, 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr. was the first man in history to be pulled into neWave Wrestling from CCW. After his amazing debut triple threat match, in which he defeated 'Movie Star' Mike Golden and Ambrose, Ken began feuding with another man under the moniker of 'Superstar'... Eddie B. The tensions between the two soon resulted in the departure of Eddie B. and Ken's establishment as the true 'Superstar.' Unfortunately, neWave folded soon after. Shortly thereafter, a friend of his, Bill Moresi (former owner of neWave), opened ReVerse Wrestling (rVw) and brought Ken in as a main event player. However, after only one week of television, Bill's associates pulled out and rVw folded. On top of this, Ken's fiancée at the time, Shanghai Lily, left him for another man, whom she had been cheating on him with. For the first time in Ken's career, he was depressed, alone, and without a home. For so long simply being involved with the wrestling business, what he's wanted to do for his entire life, had made him happy no matter what was going on with him personally. But, now, all of that, along with everything else, was taken away from him. Ken soon decided to turn the negative into a positive and began training in his Zen and Martial Arts disciplines more so than ever. Bill had long since noticed the change in Ken's demeanor and knew that he needed to get back in the ring. He took him to the eXtreme Championship Wrestling Alliance (XCWA), which Moresi's friends Bull and Chaos owned jointly, and Ken's star power immediately took effect once again. He was given a try-out match against Marcus Shame, who spent plenty of time placing the blame of rVw's failure upon Ken's and Bill's shoulders. After defeating Shame and winning his contract, Ken was immediately placed in a Four Corners match for the #1 contendership to the Grappling God Championship, winning his first official XCWA match against the likes of Ace Harding, Silverbolt, and The Paragon. The week following up to that match established one of the most intense feuds in the history of our business between KPJ and Ace Harding. The two went back and forth week after week before Ken defeated Osiris for the Championship, but soon after, the curse that's followed Ken throughout most of his career occurred, and the organization closed. Ken was immediately contacted by XCWA's brother fed, the Power House Wrestling Federation (PHWF), whom had also gotten in touch with Ace Harding and other members of the wrestling community that Ken had worked with previously such as Jay Smash, Snap Dragon, and Kid Flash (Josh). In a shocking segment on PHWF programming, Ken burst onto the scene to sign his contract at a press conference shortly after Ace had signed on the dotted line with the company. The scene ended with a very intense stare down between the two. They went on to continue their feud for the Grappling God Championship, which the Power House had picked up and Ken remained Champion. Unfortunately, the call soon went out that PHWF had suspended operation for the time being. The very same day that Ken received the news, he received a call from old friend and former neWave co-owner, Damien Payne, asking him if he would like to join ICW. Ken accepted and was immediately thrown into the United States Championship Tournament and resumed his feud from neWave with self-proclaimed 'Superstar', Eddie B. Unfortunately, Ken was in a serious car accident, suffering a major concussion along with a torn ligament and deep bone bruise in his lower left calf. On top of this, he re-injured his back and was forced out of action. Ken was then prompted to Global Wrestling by two of his close friends out of the ring which he began working with in neWave, 'The Hero of Hip Hop' Kid Flash, now under the guise of his true identity, Josh, and the incomparable Ice. After defeating Kid Flash for the PrimeStar Championship in his GW debut, Ken went on a winning streak, making it seem as though the PrimeStar title would never vacate his waist. Unfortunantly, some time later, tragedy struck in the form of a second severe knee injury. Ken was forced to leave the GW octagon and the title was held-up. Some months later, during an Evolution match between Ice and Jesse Jamester, Josh (who had also been out of action) and the debuting Ace Harding were revealed as the two men that had been helping Ice over the past month anonomously under disguise. To the crowd's enormous surprise, KPJ ran in through the fans and slid into the octagon, standing between Ice and his new stable and Jesse Jamester. After a few minutes of jaw-jacking with Ice, and to the astonishment of the crowd, Ken released a brutal Sweet Ken Music superkick that put Jamester on the shelf for a month, and turning against the fans for the first time in his illustrious career. The next week, he revealed the team of Ice, Josh, and Ace Harding as his new stable... The Elite. Running roughshod over GW, The Elite were on top of the mountain, faced only with adversity from The Upper Echelon, the were a steaming locomotive in the wrestling world. With Ice holding the World Title, KPJ defeated GLOBAL legends Akodo Rokku and John Zero for Zero's International Championship on a special edition of Evolution, on January 24, 2004. Ken would go on to become the longest reigning International Champion of all time. Soon, at GLOBAL's 2004 Clash of the Titans, problems began to develop between Ken and former rival turned partner, Ace Harding. It hightened when Ken mistakenly delivered a superkick to Harding, send him over the top rope and eliminating him from the Global Rumble. Ken went on to become on of the last two men in the Rumble with Josh... they were also the two that started the match. Ken was eventually eliminated and the American Youth earned the right to march forward into WrestleCade V for a main event shot at the GW Heavyweight Championship of the World. Due to the Global Rumble incident, the new-found partnership between KPJ and Ace Harding erupted and the two found themselves on opposing sides, once again. However, it was now Harding that was hearing the cheers of the fans. The rivalry culminated in a Submissions Only match at WrestleCade V where KPJ agreed to put up his International Championship against Harding's career. After nearly forty-five minutes, Ken trapped Ace in The Epidemic, forcing him to tap out for the first, and only time in his career. Later that same night, Josh defeated Ice for the World's title in the main event. Ice went AWOL immediatly following. With The Elite comprised now of only Josh and Ken, the World and International Champions, they set their sights on tag team gold. They remain undefeated as a team, but were never granted a title shot, eventhough they won number one contendership. The first ever CrossFire event was called and Josh was named the team captain of GLOBAL to take on the rival WMW promotion. Josh quickly named KPJ as the second GW representative and later named Platinum Champion Train and Venneman Pendragon. They were then forced to add Maverick Reno to their team. GLOBAL won the War Games match, with Ken and Josh the final two survivors. Instead, they were forced to face off against one-another for the American Youth's World Title at High Noon. For the first time in his career, Ken suffered a defeat in a World Title match. The match put the fans back behind both men. Marching forward in the tag team ranks in the weeks following, a rematch was made for and Evolution SuperShow. However, always looking to out-do everyone else, Ken and Josh devised the now infamous High-Wire Challenge. Ken came out on the losing end again, but much like Shawn Michaels' WrestleMania X Ladder Match against Razor Ramon, it was considered his defining moment. The match won Match of the Year for 2004 and is still considered by many to be the greatest match in GLOBAL history. On July 12, 2004, Ken's now record-setting International Championship reign came to an end as he lost the title to Leinad Soma. He was soon in a near fatal car accident that kept him out of the octagon until October. Upon his return, Ken won his record-setting third Television Championship from Juan Gonzalez. At Dark Rituals, just six days before Halloween, it was revealed that Silent Wind was the man behind the wheel of the eighteen wheeler that nearly ended the life of Ken Partridge, Jr. With his good friend and newest member of The Elite at his side, Ken challenged S.W. to a match to be sanctioned under Texas Death Match rules. The match was dubbed Forgotten Sins and went on to become the Runner-Up for match of the year. At Showdown in Tokyo if the same year, Ken's team of The Elite (KPJ, Josh, and Bill Moresi) and Joshua L. Curtis went head to head against The Upper Echelon (Maverick Reno, Jason Hughes, Silent Wind, and John Zero) for the first, and last, time to settle the enormous rivalry between the two factions. With the exception of Maverick Reno, who was eliminated by Joshua Curtis, Ken eliminated every other man on the opposing team, finishing with a huge Kenton Bomb from the top of the cell and onto John Zero, who was spralled across a stretcher thirty-feet below. Ken was the soul survivor. In the weeks leading to SiT, a Gold and Glory Tournament was held to name the two top contenders for the vacated World Title. Ken and Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis made it to the finals and were joined by SiT Battle Royal winner, Christian Zenith in a Triple Threat Match at GLOBAL's first ever (re)Load for the World Title. The Television Champion went on to win his very first GLOBAL World Championship. The following night, Ken's celebration was interuppted as Ken was stripped of his Television Championship. However, he walked out with his head high and with the World Title still strapped around his waist. A World Title match was set for High Octane between Christian Zenith and KPJ. Once again, looking to out-do himself, Ken made the match a Hell in a Handbasket match, where the GLOBAL octagon was enclosed in a giant cell and then raised fifty feet above the arena floor. The winner would be the man to climb to the top of the cell and retrive the title after his opponent fell to the floor. While climbing the side of the cell, the chain link broke and fell, sending Ken on the nearly career and life ending fall to the floor. Zenith quickly climbed the ladder and snagged the title. Later, at CrossFire II, Bubba McCoy named KPJ the GLOBAL team captain. Ken named Josh, Dawn MacFarlane, Shadow, and Christian Zenith his partners to take on WMW once again. Ken was the final survivor after forcing Noah Hanson, the WMW team captain to tap out. Shortly before, Ken rebuilt The Elite and brought back Ace Hardin, who formed The Enforcers with Bill Moresi. In the months following, Ken proved that he no longer needed Championship to define himself. However, at Master of the Mat VI, Ken defeated Eryc Morgan and Christian Zenith for Zenith's International Championship. He would later go on to lose it to Shadow, tapping out for the only time in his career in 'The Match With Style.' The only way that Ken could win was via the ladder and the only way Shadow could win was via submission. In March, Platinum Champion Eryc Morgan was found out as the fifth member of The Elite, joining KPJ, International Champion Josh, and Tag Team Champions Bill Moresi and Ace Harding. Shortly thereafter, Ken retired. Ken has been inducted into the GLOBAL Hall of Fame and will be loved forever in the hearts of the fans around the world. If any one person played a part in GLOBAL history... it was truly the Global Icon.
