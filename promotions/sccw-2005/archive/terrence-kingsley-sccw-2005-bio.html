Terrence Kingsley Biography Real Name Terrence Kingsley Nicknames The Pit Viper, The Trailer Park Messiah, The Curse Hometown Picayune, MS (is announced as coming from Li Grand Zombi) Birthday September 7th, 1984 Height 6'1 Weight 235 Biography Weep and wail, you shepherds; roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock. For your time to be slaughtered has come; you will fall and be shattered like fine pottery. -Jeremiah 25:34 Physical Appearance: Toned, scraggly beard and shoulder-lengthy dirty brown hair. Think a more unkempt Tyler Durden from Fight Club. Wears a sun faded leather jacket that has rusty nails pushed out across the seams and shoulders. On the back, there is an embroidery (http://www.schwarzdorn.de/Cerberus/Logo/CerberusLogo_gross.jpg) that is covered with scattershot puncture wounds. He wears tights that plain black at the top which filter down to tie-dye patterns which look like misshapen skulls, a subtle jut of patterned flames spanning from knee to thigh. His wrists and knuckles wrapped up in white athletic tape. His hallmarks are knee-length snakeskin boots and his Confederate Stars and Bars bandanna. Strengths: 1. Extremely tough 2. Guile 3. Stamina Weaknesses: 1. Not especially strong 2. Not especially fast 3. His style is more brawling than precision technical, the caveat being that Kingsley utilizes ring psychology to its fullest extent. He isn’t extremely strong nor is he extremely quick, but he makes up for this with moxie, guile, toughness, and sheer barbarity. MANNERISMS AND TONE Nicknamed ‘The Pit Viper’, Kingsley carries himself in the ring much like a predator. Unlike others inside SCCW, he is an alien entity, a mystery coming from the swamps of unincorporated Mississippi. As such, he carries a certain unquantifiable mystique based on both intrigue and danger. Kingsley has no sense of self preservation and is completely willing to sacrifice his health if it means he can harm his opponent. You can pound him into the mat until all that is left are bone fragments and tufts of hair and he would still fight to the bitter end. This refusal to quit gives him an iron will, evening out for his lack of intense strength or athleticism. He wrestles methodically; every motion is deliberate, and his actions are systematic. He wants to grind his opponents down to the point of breaking them, be it physically or mentally. That isn’t to say that Kingsley is a character that is tough as nails guy who doesn’t eat pinfalls. Hell, unlike a lot of e-wrestlers who say "OMG MY GUY R NO TAPZ CUZ HE R TOUGH", Kingsley will tap. If Terry Funk can tap out and Mick Foley can tap out, who the hell am I to say "my guy won’t tap out under any circumstances"? The thing, though, like with any wrestling match, is that you have to mess someone up enough to pull it off. If you break him down to that point, he will tap. As said above, there is no sense of self preservation, and at the same time, beating messages into his opponents doesn’t mean anything about wins and losses. One thing I do demand, though, is that you do not have Kingsley yell during his matches. He doesn’t yell. He speaks softly (normal, low, or growling tones) and everyone listens. With Kingsley, there are no exclamation points with his dialogue. He doesn’t scream at the ref or the fans. He may taunt them, but no yelling. Please. For the love of God. I know this seems like an odd statement, but its how the character carries himself. If he ever yells, it needs to be a big deal. Playing upon his nickname, he sometimes coils before hitting moves. When entering the ring, he slithers under the bottom rope. He sometimes licks opponents (again, like a snake). If he has an opponent dazed, he might drop stomach first to the canvas, pounding his fists into the mat as he coils and uncoils (like Randy Orton before an RKO, like http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e86MsrZIQP8). He loves grabbing a hold of people and not letting go. He’s one of those wrestlers who gives someone very little space to operate and tries his best to smother his opponents. He also might light up a cigarette during a match, sometimes to taunt, sometimes to relax, but most often, to use it on the opponent. Think slaps. Think taunts. Think of ways for him to verbally demean his opponents with his swamp water charm. If you want a few guys who I think of when it comes to Kingsley’s in-ring styling, all you need to look towards is Jake Roberts (which should be a fairly obvious influence) but also Terry Funk, Scott Hall, Mick Foley, Steve Austin, and the Necro Butcher.
