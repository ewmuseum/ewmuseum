Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid Biography Real Name Justin Williamson Nicknames the Archbishop of Angles, the Baron of Bisection, the Czar of Circles, the Doge of Diameter, the Emperor of Endpoints, the Fuhrer of Fractions, the General of Geometry, the Higher Power of Hexagons, the Indian Chief of Irrational Numbers, the Jester of, t Hometown Nagasaki, Japan Birthday 7/21/1978 Height 5'11' Weight 227 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography There are cult favorites in the world of professional wrestling, just as there are legends. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, a ten year veteran, straddles the line between the two. Having started his career in the minor league feeder system of the then dominant Internet Wrestling Organization, the substitute math teacher gained a reputation for his giant arsenal of named moves, incredibly bizarre behavior, and pain tolerance. Over the course of his career he has competed in some of the most barbaric matches in the history of wrestling, and won titles all over the world. He has crossed paths with stars such as Samuel Potright and Karina Wolfenden, made stops in locations as diverse as the IWO and UWF. Sometimes cheered by the majority of the fans, and sometimes reviled, a tightly knit core of Trapezoid fans follow his exploits, cheering on their bizarre hero. At times fun-loving, at others bizarre, and at other times vicious and cold-blooded, the HIT is one of pro wrestling's most unique characters. So, why has he come back from a three year stint in Japan, competing in the country's signature deathmatches? The one thing to elude his grasp is a top championship. When one considers the things he has done in his career, one shudders to think what he would do if given that opportunity. TRAPPLEXES: Trapplexes are nothing more than the HIT's name for his suplexes. If he executes a belly-to-belly suplex, for example, it is to be called a belly-to-belly Trapplex. The only exceptions to this are for his Sine (German), Cosine (Tiger), and Tangent (Tiger '85) suplexes, which will be called by name even when out of the sequence. Top rope suplexes will be given the prefix of 'Exponential,' considering their more dangerous nature. EX: A top rope backdrop suplex = Exponential Backdrop Trapplex.
