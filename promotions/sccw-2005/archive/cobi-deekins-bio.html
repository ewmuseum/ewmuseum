Cobi Deekins Biography Real Name Jacobi Deekins Nicknames 'The Idol' Hometown New York City Birthday 19.06.1979 Height 6ft02 Weight 235lbs Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography The Idol Cobi Deekins has been wrestling since he was 18. His most successful wrestling era was in the FWF where he held several titles but was arguebly one of the greatest wrestlers. To prove this he was the first man to be put up in the FWF hall of fame even before the owners! He held The World title 6 times and retired from the FwF as The Champion. During his time in the FwF he feuded with many, and defeated many. He was hated by many, but also loved by many! No matter what he did, whether it was trying to shut the FwF down by destroying it, or helping the FwF when it was in trouble, the fans, the wrestlers and the owners always respected him as a person. His wrestling career started going downhill when he got married and became a Father. He wasn't paying much attention to wrestling. He would lose concentration during matches, and take a couple of bumps and bruises. The bravest thing he has ever done was finish a wrestling match with a broken arm. Although he lost this match and his World title a couple of months later he was back wreslting with a cast on his arm. Although the FWF has now sadly died, The Idols career hasn't. During his time away from Wrestling he was involved in Street Fighting and Ultimate Fighting. He's been arrested for damn near killing a man, he would still be in prison had the jury believed he was guilty of attempted murder. 'A fight is a fight, it was all legal, it was my job... it's not my fault this kid just wasn't good enough to survive this fight! He's lucky he's still alive! No I didn't try to kill him, I simply did him a favour... he was involved in something he shouldn't be. Now he's back in education.. studying so he can get a job which best suits his ability. fighting is my life.. it's my gift and I'll continue to do what I do best until someone good enough can stop me!' -Quoted by Cobi Deekins after the trial He's now waiting to be unleashed after being locked away for some time.
