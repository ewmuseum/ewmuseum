The Spider flicks her gaze over her shoulder, the camera zooming in on one cold eye. Desade: I did not think you would do as you have done. I asked Dr. Riley along -- Sarah Riley spins the camera around, waving brightly. She is wearing a nurse's outfit (not a hot nurse's outfit, a legitimate nurse's outfit). Sarah Riley: Hi, Jon! She blows a kiss. Desade: In case you disappointed me by becoming me. You will let Lauren Fox leave that ring unharmed and you will do so before my patience runs out. The camera sweeps slowly to the left, away from the redhead and down to the stretcher that is fastened into the ambulance with the Director and the Good Doctor. The stretcher that Krissy Jamison is strapped into; the blond birthday girl is gagged, but her blue eyes are wide. Desade: By my reading of the odometer, we are traveling at approximately fifty miles per hour down the I-40 interstate. If Lauren Fox is not released from that ring to join her stepsister, let me be clear what will happen. I will open the bay doors at the rear of this vehicle and I will shove this stretcher out onto the freeway, and we will not slow down. Gibson: ...holy shit. The arena has quieted; the thought of what Alexandra Pierce has proposed has left some fans shell-shocked. The camera swivels to the center of the ring, where Lauren Fox is watching with a smirk on her face. Jonathan Rhine's mouth is twisted up in rage. Charlie Ramone steps to Rhine's side. Super-sensitive mics? Charlie Ramone: Please, man. Please. You gotta...gotta know she'll fuckin' do it. Desade: I am waiting, Jonathan. One well-worn, flat-heeled leather boot comes to rest on the edge of the stretcher. Desade: Is your one moment of satisfaction worth permanent injuries to an innocent? It's not; they both know what he's going to say -- and that's what makes it hard for Jonathan, but he nods. Elliott Rollins steps to the side. Jay Draven stares death at the "Flat-Chested Finagler". Sean Sterling's glare is harder than the rest of these people, because they're nice guys and he's not. Charlie Ramone just rests her forehead on Rhine's back; there are probably tears, but she doesn't want to show them. As for Lauren Fox? She takes the longest she can possibly take, slithering out through the ringropes and dropping to the floor. She's damn near sashaying as she slips over the barricade and embraces her sister. Gibson: I am... actually physically nauseous from this night. It started off so well, and the party was -- Desade: You are a good and predictable opponent, Mr. Rhine. It cannot stay upright; it falls to its side and then flips end over end down the highway. Gibson: THAT... In the arena, Jon Rhine steps to the ringropes, a look of complete horror on his face. Gibson: Desade kicked the stretcher out of the ambulance anyway! Krissy Jamison is... she's gotta be... She's right there, Dave. Front and center in the zoom of Sarah Riley's camera, and she's uninjured. Her face is being held in place by gleaming black leather gloves (or, rather, by the hands within them). Desade: The next time, I will not be so considerate. You will do as you are told, or people will suffer the consequences of your arrogance. Now, Kristina, I want you to tell Charlotte that she should be grateful to Mr. Rhine for releasing my student, but she should understand how close it came to a bad end. Ungloved hands (so those would be Sarah's) remove the gag from Krissy Jamison's mouth. Krissy: I love you, Charlotte! The Punk Rock Pixie has her head buried against Jay Draven's shoulder, and she's weeping with relief. Desade: Happy Birthday, Kristina... Long zoom in on the horrified, horrific sight of Krissy Jamison's face; she heard what we did not, remember. The clatter of the empty stretcher. Desade: Happy... Birthday. And fade to the SCCW logo.
