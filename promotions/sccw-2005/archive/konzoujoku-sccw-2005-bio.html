Konzoujoku Biography Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Ryuuseki Gakuen is renowned as the toughest high school this side of Chiba. The first and only school of its kind in Japan, youths live on campus and train in amaresu, puroresu, MMA, or their choice of other combat disciplines while receiving a high school education. The youngest students enter at age 14 with parental permission, and they can stay up to the age of 19 while studying for university exams. The academy boasts production of some of the best rookie fighters to come out of Japan in recent years, but puroresu is a recent addition to the school and so they have no precedent for quality performers in that category. In fact, they have only one new addition; aside from that, they've only had transfers into the training from other school disciplines. As it turned out, the school had a training regimen to rival the top schools in the country, in the hemisphere... possibly the world. Its facilities are second only to Russia's famed combat school. Students trained here are world-class, having years of experience provided by constant sparring with some of the best fighters within their fields. Education-wise? The school isn't so great, but then they only do just enough to be classified as an actual school. Books are provided, but academic classes are more meeting rooms and rest areas than actual places of learning. So many students are behind in their studies... but when you have the chance of breaking right into the fight leagues without the need for education, who cares about books. RySeGa (abbreviated by the students) has a working contract with most major fighting leagues and is starting to do the same with puroresu. To that end, they brokered a deal with PTC, as well as a few other wrestling and fighting communities, to send over some talent. The first wave is known as Konzoujoku, the only group of punk kids good enough to qualify for the program. In an exchange program, the Gate sends their talent to other countries for work, and collects 80% of the money made. The wrestlers are still ne'er-do-well high school kids, though, meaning that they have to maintain classes in the countries to which they are sent. And all of this is done outside of the knowledge of the parents, as they've signed their children's guardianship to the school to make those decisions. In addition to God Hand and The Score, Konjouzoku (FUSE has improperly listed their team name, but it's apparently too late to change it) has three other representatives and two accompanying staff from Ryuuseki Gakuen. All attend FUSE shows and can be found backstage, often at ringside as well. Agawa 'Quincy' Miki 6'9' 305 lb. (Billed as 332 lb.) The former volleyball and basketball player changed to wrestling to follow in the footsteps of Shohei 'Giant' Baba. A rare-sized trueborn whose varied athletic acumen is matched only by his incomparable singing voice. Known as 'Quincy' for being as soulful, charismatic, and musically-talented as the legendary Motown producer Quincy Jones. He's got natural size and strength, but mediocre ring ability and doesn't do any schoolwork. Fortunately, he's so big that it doesn't matter. Among Konjouzoku, Quincy is one of only two students (the other being The Score, though Nurse Wakahisu also knows English) who speaks English. Aside from his large size, Higo 'Ban' Yoshimaru 5'8' 180 lb. (Billed as 190 lb.) Something of a misanthrope, he sees the worst in everybody; naturally negative and disrespectful of everything and everyone. Being the best fighter and wrestler in the region, ranked #3 in his home country, he's all but untouchable. Works out in private, as sparring doesn't really work; he intends to destroy anyone who steps up against him (there is no such thing as a practice fight). Finding all persons below him, he doesn't really do much competition. Has everyone else to stand in front of him. Very intelligent, but lazy in matters of schoolwork; handles his own tests and passes with flying colors, but makes Tetsuma do his homework. Ban is considers himself so far above other people that he refuses to touch them without wearing latex surgical gloves, and puts a sterile face-mask on so as not to breath their unfiltered air. Sokushima 'Soku' Go 5'11' 205 lb. (Billed as 213 lb.) Chosen by Ban to be his personal bodyguard. Soku is the only person in the group who has no nickname, his is just a rude shortening of his surname. It was selected by himself as a way to distance himself from the Sokushima family name. He comes from a family of slackers and ne'er-do-wells, known around the region as general wastes of time; permanent entry-level workers. Soku himself is very motivated to succeed; below-average intelligence keeps him from doing well in academics, but he's thrown himself into athletics. Not quite a meathead, but is very zealous in his responsibilities to stand between Ban and the rest of the world. Coach Murayama Gamatoshi 6'2' 244 lb. A hardass through and through, Coach Gama's attitude is the roughest of any teacher in Japan and is a match for any of his trainees. A large man with a big stick... a Kendo stick. He carries it around with him everywhere he goes, and has no problem using it to whip people into shape (or just beat the crap out of some schmuck). In fact, he's just as likely, if not more so, to attack his own trainees as anyone else. The only thing that tames the harsh taskmaster is his attraction to the nurse, though it's also the only thing that causes him to lose confidence in himself. He's unable to carry on a conversation with her, though he's much more interested in other things about her. Nurse Wakahisu Momoe 5'6' 126 lb. The nurse sent along with Konjouzoku to keep an eye on them. Coach Gama is the physical trainer, while Nurse Momoe keeps track of their psychological development and nutrition. She takes her job very seriously, treating the boys as if they were her children. She's very attractive and caring, an older woman who can make even the roughnecks of the wrestling program to be nice to her just by giving them a smile and a 'Job well done.' She recognizes the coach's attempts at advances, however poorly they may turn out. When not working, she tries to have a good time, but isn't 'easy' even if she's no problem flaunting her sexuality.
