Phillip Kennedy Biography Real Name Phillip Benjamin Kennedy Nicknames The Savior of Sin City, the Las Vegas Lariat, the Big Stack Hometown Las Vegas, NV Birthday July 7, 1977 Height 6'4' Weight 249 Fed Titles SCCW Gateway Champion (three defenses), one-half of the SCCW Strength In Numbers Champions (w/ Katsidy/Reginald Lovecraft; representing the DMH) Fed Awards Longest Reigning SCCW Gateway Champion, 2008 Rookie of the Year, Finalist in the No Limit Tournament Biography In a company of established names, Phillip Kennedy is unique. While names such as Alexandra Pierce and Lance Marshall rose to fame in the National Wrestling Council, while Amy 'Aimz' Campbell became an fWo superstar, Phillip Kennedy rose to prominence as a rookie in Sin City Championship Wrestling. Due in large part to his affiliation with the Dead Man's Hand, Phillip captured the Strength In Numbers and Gateway Championships, before receiving his first (and to date, only) shot at the SCCW Universal Championship against Amy Campbell in the finals of the No Limit Tournament, severing ties with the Hand in the process. And ever since that moment, it all went downhill for Kennedy. After several concussions and a perhaps ill-advised decision to pick a fight with Jonathan Rhine, Phillip Kennedy disappeared from SCCW TV. Sharp-eyed fans, however, noticed that SCCW had not released Kennedy, nor had the Big Stack quit. And so it was that Kennedy returned in the summer of 2010, having taken time off to recover, in an SCCW with no Dead Man's Hand, no Lane Stevens, and no Xavier Kannon. To many, it's a sign that the Stack's picking his spot. If that's true, it's a sign of a smarter Kennedy, and that is something no SCCW competitor wants to deal with. (For a synopsis of Phillip's career, see his PTCPedia page at: http://ptcpedia.primetimecentral.net/index.php/Phillip_Kennedy) Other Feds Appeared in PRIME during the SCCW/PRIME war. Other Fed Awards Victorious at SCCW/PRIME Cataclysm in a tag champs vs. tag champs match. Wrestling Style Texan brawling and power Finisher Move High Stakes or Sin City Special Finisher Description Diamond Cutter or Arm-triangle choke Finisher Setup Move No definite setup. Setup Description See above. Other Moves Wrestling Style/Mannerisms/MWA: The self-professed Savior of Sin City is a brawler by trade. Never the most technically proficient athlete, he prefers to use his power and fighting abilities to wear opponents down and out. Phillip is LEFT handed. He throws his more powerful strikes and lariats with his left arm. He'll use the right for weaker blows, if the positioning is right, or if his opponent has worked over the left arm. His signature weapon is the steel chain, in either larger, Hercules Hernandez-type lengths, or smaller chains that he can wrap around a fist. Kennedy usually stores one such chain in his boot for safe keeping, just in case. Given his history of concussions, Kennedy will fight HARD to prevent being dropped on his head with moves. ((OOC MWA Point: As a matter of course, Kennedy won't take headdrops unless it's the end of a match and is important.)) Has a habit of trying to annoy opponents by using moves of rivals or allies of a given foe (the use of Freetekno and the Rhine Rewind on Alexandra Pierce, for example) Phillip has thin skin and will bleed reasonably easily. Blood flows freely and often will stain Kennedy's hair, body, the opponent, and the mat if he's in a match long enough for it to do so. MICHAEL VAIN: As Phillip Kennedy's bodyguard, Vain can, will, and often does interfere in Phil's matches. Finishers: 1. High Stakes: Diamond Cutter. Kennedy uses his version of the Ace Crusher much like Diamond Dallas Page did; as a come-from-anywhere weapon to steal a match out of nowhere (and crush the SCCW fanbase's hopes). High Stakes can be set up from anything. A Michael Vain trip of an opponent, a fireman's carry, slipping out of another move, so on, and so forth. 2. Sin City Special: Arm triangle choke. Inspired by Brock Lesnar's use of the move at UFC 116 (the event he was watching when he decided to return to the sport), Kennedy decided that he, as a reasonably powerful, tall individual, could make effective use of it as well, and had his bodyguard's contacts in the MMA world drill him on the move. The Sin City Special is Kennedy's submission finisher, and also the move he'll go to when all else fails. Signatures: 1. Lariat variations, thrown with both arms. Be creative. 2. Viva Las Vegas: Leg-cradle Death Valley Driver. 3. The Bullet: Running big boot to the face. 4. Straight Fluss: Inverted mat slam, aka the Edge-O-Matic 5. The Flop: Flip-over release German suplex. Other Moves: - Brawling (generally big looping roundhouse rights and left, kneelifts to the gut, stomping on downed opponents and kicks with his cowboy boots. This makes up the vast majority of Kennedy's current offense) - Liberal use of throwing opponents into the ringposts, stairs, and guardrail outside, as well as fighting on the floor in general. - Basic wrestling moves (armdrags, hiptosses, and the like, the sort of things that any wrestler should be able to do after graduating from training) - Basic submissions/'restholds' (rear chinlock, front facelock, surfboard stretch, Boston crab, generic arm and leg locks) - Powerbomb - Gutwrench suplex - Stalling vertical suplex - Fireman's carry into gutbuster - Reverse Neckbreaker - Calf Branding - Texas Clawhold - Snake Eyes on the top turnbuckle - Snap powerslam off an Irish whip - Diving shoulderblock off the top rope Entrance Music 'Turn the Page' by Bob Seger and the Silver Bullet Band
