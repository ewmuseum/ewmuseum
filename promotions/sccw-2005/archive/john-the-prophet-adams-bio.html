John Adams Biography Real Name John Henry Adams Nicknames The Prophet Hometown Riverside California Height 7'3'' Weight 350 Biography {Dream lofty dreams, as you dream, so shall you become. Your vision is the promise of what you one day shall be: your ideal is the prophecy of what you shall at last unveil.} ~James Allen~ {Think of how many religions attempt to validate themselves with prophecy. Think of how many people rely on these prophecies, however vague, however unfulfilled, to support or prop up their beliefs. Yet has there ever been a religion with the prophetic accuracy and reliability of science?} ~Carl Sagan (1934 - 1996) ~ ~To truly understand ones drive and determination in life, one must go to the beginning where it all started. One must see how a person’s destiny takes shape in life. For me it happened in the very beginning since birth. For me "The Prophecy" of John Adams started when I first opened my eyes unto this crazy sometimes mixed up world of ours. But unlike others who may fall prey to the trials and tribulations of everyday life as they become mindless sheep to survive, I on the other hand decided to evolve. Evolve in my life not to survive, not to be another mindless sheep, and certainly not to have a 9 to 5 job just to live my life wanting to be like everybody else! No I decided to evolve past the means of normal man and take control of my life, I decided when I saw the destiny laid before me not to have any type of authority preordain the way I live! I decided to live my life by one thing and one thing alone…….."The Prophecy"! ~ 10-31-76 Time: 5:30 PM Location: Riverside General Hospital Paternity Ward -----In the depths of the cold dark hospital near the paternity ward where new life begins, our attention is drawn to a beautiful young woman being carted off in a wheelchair. The screams of the young woman could be heard all the way to the morgue. And why not, on this day of all days on all hallows eve, this young lady is about to give birth. The nurse carting her off to prep her for delivery, but wait there’s the nurse and the lady about to give birth, but where is the father? This thought is carried with her as everybody in the ward watches her being checked in and carted off. ----- Head Nurse: Name? Lady: Lorraineee! Loraine Adams! Head Nurse: Great and your insurance carrier’s are? Loraine Adams: Just get me in there now! Nurse pushing her: Yes she is already 10 centimeters dilated and her water has broke! Head Nurse: How far apart are the contractions? Nurse pushing her: 2 minutes apart! Head Nurse: And the father? Nurse pushing her: Mr. Frank Adams will not be here because of a previous engagement at work. Head Nurse: Alright wheel her in to room 24! -----The Nurse pushes Loraine into the room with a sadden look for her situation. Just think a husband that would rather work than see his own child enter into this world. But enough of that, with new life about to be born he continues to wheel her into the room. 6 hours and 29 minutes it took in labor, a minute before the witching hour of all hollows eve before Loraine and Frank’s son was born unto this world. But what was next for the child was nothing but a constant struggle to belong in the chaos we call life. ----- Head Doctor: So Mrs. Adams have you thought of a name for your child? Loraine Adams: Yes, since this is a child of God’s I have decided on the name John, after one of Jesus most favorite Apostles John. 10-31-81 Time: 6:35 PM Location: The Adams Mansion in La Sierra Riverside. -----Evening time in this beautiful 10 room estate the stars are bright and conditions outside on this all hallows eve could not have been better. Children can be seen outside going to house to house dressed as demons, monsters, and there favorite hero with the goal to obtain the most candy for there greedy little hearts. The atmosphere outside is perfect without a care in the world, but then inside the Adams mansion there is another story. Inside the Adams Mansion there should be joy and happiness for the young John Adams has just turned 5 years old, but there is no sign of a party of any kind. No balloons, cake, friends for him to play with, and no presents at all. But there wasn’t any surprise for it happened year after year. And as we go inside the majestic but lifeless building we see in the very spacious Victorian style dining room a young John Adams sitting by himself on what is supposed to be his special day. ----- Young John Adams: Happy Birthday to me! Happy Birthday to me! Happy Birthday to John! Happy Birthday to me! -----The loneliness in his heart weighs so much that he begins to cry. But out of the darkness from the hallway comes John Adams mother Loraine. Dressed in her bathrobe holding a cupcake with a single candle in it she walks in. ----- Loraine Adams: Oh John I’m so sorry, but don’t you cry my son. Everything is going to be ok. I brought you a surprise….look! I know it’s not a birthday cake but your father refuse to bye one for you because you didn’t pick up your toys. -----As John Adams mother comes in to the dining room, her sons thick bruises are more noticeable located on his arm and face from his father’s idea of punishment for his child. ----- Loraine Adams: Honey I’m sorry, but you know your father is trying to get that deal to have his company build the county Mental Hospital. So your father has been a little stressed out lately. But dry your tears my darling and make a whish. -----Her son blows out his candle with a look of happiness from his mother. ----- Young John Adams: Mommy, I want to tell you what I wished! Loraine Adams: But son if you do it won’t come true. Young John Adams: But mommy it’s a whish for the both of us! I wished for me and you to live happily ever after, oh and that daddy would die a horrible death! -----Just then John’s father walks into the dining room also dressed in a bathrobe. With a look of evil and hatred in his eyes you know he heard every word his son made. ----- Frank Adams: Why you little shit!! Don’t I provide for you and your mother? Don’t I provide a wonderful place for you to live? And this is how you repay me? ----- He begins to walk towards his son with the intent to hurt him. But Loraine steps in-between his path. ----- Loraine Adams: Stop Frank! Stop your scaring him! Frank Adams: Loraine will you just get the hell out of the way! You stupid bitch! You’re always talking his side over mine! Don’t you know that if you continue to treat him like this, all it’s going to do is make him soft!! But who cares right Loraine! Who cares that my slut of a wife would rather take the side of her pansy ass son over her husband’s right! Well Loraine I’ll make you care you stupid bitch! -----A husband and father are supposed to be a caring, loving, and nurturing influence for his wife and kids. But this is not the case, as Frank Adams like a man possessed begins to hit his wife over and over again in the face. The young John Adams cowers in fear over to one of the corners of the room crying his eyes out. ----- Young John Adams: H..Hap..Happy Birthday to me…….. (SOB.) ~ A mother’s love for her son should never be taken advantage of, no matter what the situation may be! As I look back on it do I think I took advantage of my mothers love? That is a question that I can not answer for my time with her was all but too short for a mother and son! No child should ever have to go through the horrific experience of losing a loved one! Do I regret it? Yes but I don’t dwell on it for it has made me stronger in life! ~ 10-31-88 Time: 2:30 PM Location: Back yard of the Adams Mansion -----The air out today is clean and tranquility is in the atmosphere. The sun is out and is shining so bright with the random bluebirds chirping for any food they may find. The other kids in the neighborhood are getting ready for a night of trick or treat on what promises to be a great hallows eve night. Even a sense of peace can be felt at the Adams Mansion for the monster known as Frank Adams is currently on a business trip for three weeks. But what what’s that we see in the front yard…..isn’t that Frank Adams talking with a couple of clean cut men and a police man. ----- Police man: All the boys at the station have been silenced Mr. Adams. Frank Adams: Good, know how about you two. Do you know what’s to be done? Clean Cut Man #1: Yes sir, we wait for the two to be working in the garden and then we make sure that Mrs. Adams is no more! Clean Cut Man #2: But sir one question! What has your wife done to you that is so bad? And why do we leave your son alone? Frank Adams: Because you dumb shits, she has been taking the side of that little shit John for too long! And if we get a divorce then that’s just more money down the tube! No a funeral is more cost efficient! And don’t worry about my son; I’ll take care of him personally! -----What horrible news to be heard from Mr. Frank Adams. But what do you expect from a cold heartless business man like him. Now unbeknown to John and Loraine they get started celebrating what they believe to be the first birthday for John without his father. So Loraine decides to get a cake so she could celebrate her son’s birthday for the first time in 12 years. It was a beautiful cake with "Happy Birthday John" on it. ----- Loraine Adams: So son what do you think about your cake? Young John Adams: It looks great mom! I love it, thank you! Loraine Adams: And son this year since your father is out of town, I also got you a present. And after I was wondering if you would help me in the garden, and after we could watch a movie together. Young John Adams: Sure mom, this is the best birthday ever! -----As Loraine started to light the candles, she began to sing happy birthday to her son of 12 years. Even though John was excited about his first official party he couldn’t help feeling that something wasn’t right. But later shook it off thinking it was jitters for actually having a birthday today. After they ate the birthday cake John got his present. ----- Young John Adams: Thank you mom, it’s a crucifix. Exactly what I wanted! Thank you. Loraine Adams: You’re welcome John. I want you to wear that always and remember that I love you. Young John Adams: I will mom. -----After the exchange of the present Loraine and her son go out to the garden to pick tomatoes and cucumbers. The eerie silence in the air was quite disturbing if you really paid attention to it. But Loraine and John were just excited to be spending some quality time with each other to even notice. The sense of safety falls before the two as they begin to pick the vegetables. ----- Loraine Adams: John is something wrong honey? Young John Adams: No mom, I just feel that this is too good to be true! And I don’t ever want this to end! But I know it will as soon as dad gets home from his trip! Loraine Adams: Oh John, I’m sorry you have to go through this. But he is your father, and always remember that whatever happens I will always be there for you! Young John Adams: Mom, one of these days I will fulfill not only my dream but yours also. And then this nightmare will be over! -----The two hug not knowing that the nightmare was just about to begin. The mysterious silence got worse as John Adams witness the bullet enter his mothers head in the back of the cranium and through the front forehead. John flings back in terror as his Mothers blood is sprayed all over the young Adams. The one thing John notices before his Mothers body falls down to the ground lifeless is a single tear, a single tear that she had shed for her son. A single tear she had for all the pain, not that she had endured over the years but for her sons pain. Thoughts of all the times John and his mother had spent went racing through his head as he just witnesses his mother of 12 long years get killed before his eyes. After the brutal killing the young John Adams father reveals himself as he grabs John and shoves him into the car. What later happened was that the young John Adams was checked in to the County Mental Hospital and was locked away for 6 years. Frank Adams was found not guilty for the murder of Loraine Adams his wife. But everybody in Riverside County knows better. They know Frank had his wife killed. They know his son John Adams is rotting in a criminal mental institution because he witnessed it. But are they going to do anything……no Frank Adams has the police and the whole county on his payroll. ----- 10-31-94 Time: 4:30 PM Location: Riverside County’s Mental Institution for the Criminally Insane. -----6 long years in purgatory he has spent in this place of the mentally challenged. 6 long years it has been since he has seen the light of day. And on his 18th year of life he is free. Free to start his life over again. Free to accept a job offer like a friend of his in the mental institution. A job offer for a wrestling promotion named XEW for there development team. But there is one thing left to do, pay a little visit to his father. ----- 10-31-94 Time: 6:00 PM Location: Adams Mansion. -----The old mansion still looks the same since he left it last, as he approaches the door he can still see the red from his mother’s lifeless body after the shooting. He goes to ring the doorbell. He waits what seems to be an eternity till the door is answered. It was his father who answered the door. And that night they had the biggest argument and fight of there lives. But this time unlike others in the past John Adams, who know is a man got the upper hand on his old man. That night he left his father for the last time still alive but a bloody mess. But before he left he made sure to get together his mothers things and depart the Adams Mansion for the last time. ----- ~FIN~ Other Feds XEW/EPW/LOW/Core/UWF/Global Other Fed Awards Core's Classic Champion, LOW Tag Team Champ and Realm Champion, 2 time EPW World Champ, 2 time Platnium Champ, Global World Champion, and LOW Extreme Champion.
