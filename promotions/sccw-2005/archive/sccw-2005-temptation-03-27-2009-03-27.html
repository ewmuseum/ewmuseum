Seymour crawls away from Phoenix as the Eternal Flame enters the ring, trying to keep the distance between himself and his foe. Jay has a stern look on his face, clearly focusing on retaining control in a match against an opponent that he has been in the ring with but once, and never one on one. Gibson: Almasy trying to get back to his feet here... Jay moves in, but Seymour manages to fire a straight kick to the kneecap that staggers Phoenix. Almasy follows with a second and a third, before all but diving towards the right knee, pulling a single leg takedown. As Jay goes down, Almasy opens up with punches to the knee, attacking rabidly before Phoenix's hand on the ropes forces Hamill to call Seymour off. Ware: Seymour showing some fire now, coming back on the Eternal Flame! Gibson: He's targeting the high flyer's knee! Almasy seems to live and die nowadays on his Evoker, that short knee strike to the temple. He doesn't need to go up top to win, but both of Phoenix's finishing tactics involve springboarding or going off the top rope! Ware: Ironic, it is, that one of the preeminent high flyers in PTC's history is trying to ground an opponent. Using the considerable leg strength that he has, Phoenix breaks free of the crab, backing away from Seymour. Sensing a possible opening, Seymour moves in to go after the knee, but Phoenix catches him with the left knee to the face. Almasy recoils, and Phoenix leaps, grabbing Seymour around the neck and taking him down to the canvas. Seymour's head bounces off the canvas, and Phoenix lands on both knees. All told, I think Almasy's quite fortunate, in spite of the damage he's taken so far. Ware: He fought off that sunset flip powerbomb which might have ended things prematurely. Before he can execute a move, though, Seymour manages to throw a weak kick to the knee that causes Jay to hesitate just enough for Almasy to back body drop his way out. Gibson: Seymour thinking quickly there. He's not a ring general, by any stretch, but seven years in the sport have definitely made him a lot more of a thinking man in the squared circle. Gibson: Gut check time for the Eternal Flame! Seymour's been targeting that knee for the past few minutes! Seymour cranks down on the figure four, but Phoenix shows no signs of being willing to tap, even as he writhes in pain. Jay is careful to lay on one side, so that both shoulders aren't down. Ware: Every second Seymour has this move on is more damage to Phoenix's leg! Jay needs to get out of this if he wants to have a chance to win! It's the ropes or reversal! Phoenix reaches up with one arm, twisting his body, trying to turn the figure four leglock over. Seymour does his best to resist...but Jay is the larger athlete, and using his mass, is successfully able to roll the manuever over! Gibson: Phoenix has it reversed! Now all the pressure of the figure four leglock being placed on the knees of Seymour Almasy! Almasy quickly releases the submission, crawling away from his adversary. Taking two steps forward, Seymour lashes out with his right boot, striking Jay hard in the jaw with a step-up version of one of wrestling's best known strikes. Ware: Superkick from the Protagonist! Almasy falls atop Phoenix, hooking the leg mid-fall, and sort of hoping that this thing is over. ONE! TWO! THR--NO! Gibson: Two only! The number twelve seed fights on!
