Hunter Sabuani Biography Nicknames Peerless, PHS, 'Nuff Said, Sublime, The Raja of Roguishness, The King of Swing. The Sovereign of the Sport. Hometown Las Vegas, NV Birthday 12-10-1971 Height 6'0' Weight 228 Fed Titles Nothing -- yet. Fed Awards None. Biography Background: A long time ago, in a galaxy far, far away, Hunter Sabuani perhaps *was* wrestling. A two-time former National Wrestling Council World Heavyweight Champion and half of the Council's last tag team champions, the Glorious Bastards, not to mention winning numerous local federation championships in the NWC's Georgia- and- New York-based federations, there was a time that wrestling fans who followed the Council's action only had to say three letters to know they were talking about its most marketable face. 'PHS.' In 2003, along with Alexandra Pierce (better known as 'Desade') and 'Wise Guy' Wyatt Connors, engineered one of the most devastating takeovers in the history of professional wrestling when they usurped control over the NWC and, in one fell swoop, Connors-Sabuani-Pierce, Ltd., closed the Council's doors, putting hundreds of men and women and dozens of professional wrestlers out of work. A number of the federations tried to hold together; Sabuani himself wrestled for a time with the Diamond Wrestling Syndicate, which emerged from the Pacific wing of the Council. But without the backing of the overarching council behind it, these federations were no different than countless others fans had to choose from, and the DWS closed a few months after opening, prior to its first scheduled pay-per-view in Washington, DC. Afterwards, Sabuani sold Pierce and Connors most of his stock in their company and took his name off the door (the remainder of the funds would be routed to a trust fund for Sabuani's young girls, Summer & Dakota). Then, Hunter simply disappeared. He left no word to any of his friends; there was no news from any wrestling publication, save for a brief mention of his 2005 divorce from his former valet Miss Madison. It is said to have been perhaps the most equitable divorce in the history of professional wrestling, though she was awarded nearly 75% of the fortune he'd accumulated during his tenure with the Council and full custody of both children. When Sabuani returned to FUSE in early 2008, ostensibly as just another NWC legend taking part in the Prince Pride-Jason Cruise feud, hardcore NWC fans were surprised, but they took it to being a short-term appearance. They figured Smitty was trying to spike the ratings for the second FUSE on ESPN show. They ended up being very, very wrong when Hunter stood side-by-side with his old ally, Desade. They left several FUSE superstars beaten, bloody, (and, in one case, hanging from a cross). The Second Age of the Illuminati is here. Theme Music: 'Momma Sed' - Puscifer A lot of people claim a lot of things about Desade. Most of them are true. But one thing is certain - the woman is as clever as she is dangerous. Desade is not arm-candy, nor is she an add-on to this group. She is clearly in charge, and every time anything is done in the Inquisition's name, it was done with her say-so and with her planning. She is smart like a viper, not smart like most wrestling managers, and she makes the DMH the kind of heels who don't have a plan that ends with someone hitting their opponent in the face with a chair. They have a plan that involves them getting into your life, tearing down all the pillars that hold them up. With the manpower she has at her disposal, she's not afraid to go as intricate as possible. Think of the meanest, nastiest thing you can, and do that -- twice. Olivia Carpenter/MacKenzie Malone: 5'7', 122 lbs. Hair: Black. Eyes: Green. Theme Music: 'Sugar' - Kids in the Way. Have you ever made a really bad choice? MacKenzie is right there with you. First met by FUSE fans as the snotty valet for 'The Hollywoord Hellion' Duncan Drake, it quickly became apparent there was more to with Malone than met the eye -- a theory that proved correct when she was revealed to be the signal-pirate's (apparently reluctant) accomplice. But with Desade, nothing is what it seems, and now she stands at the Dead Man's Hand's side. There are times that there appears to be some thing seriously wrong psychologically with this girl. Other times, she seems like a regular girl, the most normal of the lot of them. Katsidy: 5'9', 128 lbs. Hair: Brown. Eyes: Hazel. Theme Music: 'The Cheap and Evil Girl' - Bree Sharp. If you were to look up 'sexual predator' in the dictionary, you might just find Kathryn Shaw. Sure, she's not one of those pedophiles, but what Katsidy does is find people's weaknesses and exploit them. Even at 32 and with a son, she's tremendously easy on the eyes. She's a dyed-in-the-wool flirt and a veritable sociopath, and underselling her attractiveness would be like underselling Jason Cruise's pure wrestling ability -- even guys who don't want to admit it, guys who KNOW they should know better often find themselves caught up in her game, even just a little. Mr. Ashe: 6'6', 364 lbs. Hair: Brown. Eyes: Brown. Theme Music: 'Die MF Die' - Dope. The Security Consultant for the Inquisition has one rule: 'You Do Not Touch Alexandra Pierce.' A former four-time NWC tag-team champion, Asher's knees aren't built for long-term physical competition any more, but the New Zealander with the cigar serves pretty much the same purpose that Alan Branch does with FUSE Security -- he's the last bastion of 'stop right the fuck there'. He doesn't get involved in PHS' matches on a regular basis (there's a deepseated dislike between the two, even though Asher's fourth tag-title reign was as Hunter's partner). He shouldn't be beat up regularly, but he'll sacrifice himself to give Desade the chance to escape. Mr. Hawke: 5'11', 187 lbs. Hair: Black. Eyes: Brown. Theme Music: 'If You Talk Too Much (My Head Will Explode)' - People in Planes. Formerly three times NWC tag champion with Asher as part of Black Ops, Mr. Hawke is a different man now, since his left leg was nearly crippled in an accident. Now he walks with a cane and carries a chip on his shoulder. Biting, sarcastic, and unafraid to use his hard stone cane (with an intricately-carved hawk's head atop it) as a weapon. Hawke rarely actually accompanies the group to ringside, though, as his lack of mobility can be an impairment. He's more the backstage go-between for the group. Lauren Fox/Savant/The Orchid: 5'4', 119 lbs. Hair: Purple (dyed from brown). Eyes: Green. Theme Music: 'She's My Ride Home' - Blue October. Alex Pierce's protegee for a number of years and the mastermind behind the plot to bring her back, Lauren Fox has grown up and grown up cold. The opposite side of the coin of MacKenzie Malone, since Lauren has never led a normal life and doesn't give a fig about who she's wearing. Lauren's talents lie in infiltration and digging up information, secrets people rarely want uncovered. She's mean, she's uncaring, and she's more than willing to do the messy work that no one else is. She's rarely actually at a FUSE event, but if she shows up, there's likely a good (read: bad) reason for it. Other Moves Basic Style: Hunter Sabuani has no right to be doing what he's doing anymore. At the age of 37, he's beaten himself up to the point that he should be slowing down and he shouldn't be jumping off of things on a regular basis. But he still does it, and he still manages to be goddamn good at it. Sure, he's a hair slower than he was seven years ago when he last won a major wrestling federation championship. Put him in the ring with some kind of ring general and he's in trouble. Put him in the ring with some wet behind the ears kid who can run at Mach 9, and he'll have a problem keeping up. But stick PHS in the ring with your typical wrestler, and he'll still be the fast one, the one who's lighter on his feet, and the one who's bouncing around the ring. The fact that he can't do what he used to be able to do bugs only sometime, but it's more than made up for by the fact that he's almost literally seen every kind of wrestling style that there is, and he will force you to play his game, not the other way around. That means lots of brawling, lots of wild action, and a ton of time spent on the floor. It used to be said by people that if you could force Hunter Sabuani to stay in the ring, you had a better than average chance of beating him, but he made it so difficult because he surrounded himself with people whose entire job was to distract you. Hunter's not an extreme wrestler; he's not someone who makes their fortune based on their ability to hit you with a weapon. He's someone who excels at the utilization of his surroundings, though, and if that means he'll get a chance to use the ring bell, a chair, the stairs, the security barrier, some chick's purse, a ladder, and a brick... that's okay by him. We'd be remiss to mention it here (though it's mentioned elsewhere). Anyone considering Sabuani's basic style has to consider the ridiculous numbers of the Dead Man's Hand(or, as he is wont to think of them, Sabuani's Angels). Their presence at ringside -- and the threat of their presence when they are not there -- means his opponents must always be on edge. There is no one safe; everyone *is* at risk. Desade is perhaps the most notorious mastermind this business has ever seen; she has at her beck and call some of the most dangerous underhanded people on the planet. Rest assured that, while Sabuani is a fantastic athlete, she will have a Plan for every match he is involved in. The Sub-a-pult: A springboard front-flip legdrop. Has been in the Sabuani arsenal since the beginning. 2.) Game, Set, Match: Irish whip to the corner. Fly in, double knee or flying splash into the chest, monkey flip out, double knees to the face. Bang-bang-bang fast. 3.) Wicked Justice: A perverse tribute to his now ex-wife, Miss Madison. It's a dreadfully brutal hurricanrana -- Sabuani doesn't let go until the last second, so sometimes, it's almost more akin to a flying piledriver. 4.) Facialized: Sabuani hooks standing reverse Indian deathlock with a surfboard and then lifts his free leg up, placing it on the back of the head of the opponent. He releases the surfboard and stomps the leg down to drive the opponent's head face first into the mat. Can be done multiple times. Entrance Music 'I'm Not Jesus' - Apocalyptica (f. Corey Taylor)
