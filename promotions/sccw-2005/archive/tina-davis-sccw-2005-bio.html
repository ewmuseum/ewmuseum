Tina Davis Biography Nicknames 'The Canadian Classic Hometown Toronto ,Ontario ,Canda Birthday August 21st ,1983 Height 5 feet 10 inches Weight 190 Biography Tina's path to the wrestling ring isn't what one would ever call 'typical'...far from it actually! Born on August 21st 1983 in Winnipeg ,Manitoba ,Canada Tina was raised to be a lady and a scholar by her parents and when she went to school she achieved just as they wanted and some dare to say that she did even better than they expected! All through Tina's academic career Tina excelled at the highest levels and was always a bookworm to the point that some would dare say that she was borderline antisocial! Its wasn't until 1996 when Tina moved to Toronto with her parents that she started to develop other interests outside of school. Her social circle was a mixture of the intellectual mix of men and women and the jocks and she began to show an interest in athletics. When the wrestling coach of the high school saw her training in the gym he felt it necessary to ask her to join the team which she accepted without consent of her parents! As the years went by her grades never suffered but she amassed quite the record in the High School ranks that coaches from various colleges including those in the states such as Iowa ,Michigan ,Villanova and various other major colleges pursued her and when she accepted a spot on the Michigan State team and took them deep within the tournament 3 out of 4 years some wondered what she'd do after that! After emerging as a star in college a man named Chester Clarke contacted Tina about working as a Pro Wrestler and at first she declined but after careful consideration she called the legendary wrestler back and accepted his offer for training and went on to win various titles in Japan ,England ,her home country of Canada and yes even the United States! Many people thought that her career was over after a freak accident in a cage match 5 years ago but Tina has re-emerged and is ready to take on anyone male or female in the industry!
