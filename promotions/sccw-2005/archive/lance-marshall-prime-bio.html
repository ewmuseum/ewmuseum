Lance Marshall Biography Real Name Lance Marshall Nicknames The Lion Hometown New York City Birthday July 4, 1974 Height 6'3' Weight 320 Biography Lance was born in New York City, New York on the 4th of July, 1974. His father, James, a career military man, instilled within Lance since birth a fundamental love of and deep respect for the qualities on which America was founded: pride, integrity, loyalty, honor, and respect. With the guidance of his parents and his older brothers, Lance learned the path of the true solider; not the hulking, grunting parody of a soldier from movies like Rambo but the true, dignified, and honorable path of the conscientious warrior. Lance also inherited something else from his family: major genetics. At 6'3', Lance is actually close to being the shortest member of his family (next to his mother, at 5'11'). With brothers who had won county, state, and national honors in football, wrestling, and basketball, it seemed only natural that Lance would follow in his older sibling's athletic footsteps. And he did so indeed. At the age of 15, Lance won the Golden Gloves competition in the heavyweight division, eventually amassing an amateur record of 76 wins and 3 losses. At the age of 16, Lance went on to win honors in football and wrestling, quickly joining the all-city, all-county, all-state, and all-American lists in quick succession. All this, in addition to maintaining a 4.0 GPA, made Lance an ideal prospect for college recruiters. But, in keeping with the family tradition, Lance decided to apply for admission to the United States Military Academy in West Point, New York. After a rigorous application process, Lance was accepted into West Point, to the delight of his family. For the final family vacation before Lance left for the Point, the Marshall family headed for Hawaii. It was there that Lance would meet the woman who would change his life forever. It was on the beach that Lance Marshall first met Alanna, the woman who would eventually become his wife and manager. It was love at first sight and the two spent every moment of Lance's time in Hawaii together. It was with the greatest sadness that Lance told Alanna on his last day in Hawaii that they would have to break things off, that he would no longer be able to see her. But Alanna wasn't about to let the mainlander who had so charmed her get away so easily. With some of the money from her savings, she took a plane to New York three weeks later. She eventually arrived at the Marshall family home. The Marshalls took her in, welcoming her and telling her she had a place to stay as long as she needed it. Six months later, during Lance's first furlough home, he and Alanna met once again and they have been together ever since. While at West Point, Lance continued his streak of excellence in both athletics and academics, quickly becoming both West Point heavyweight boxing champ and a star defensive lineman on the Army football team. It was during a furlough from the Point that Lance got his first exposure to professional wrestling. While he had certainly seen pro wrestling on television, his first live exposure to it was while attending a major federation show at Madison Square Garden. Lance was quickly taken with both the sport's athleticism and its' sense of theater. When Lance graduated from the Point, he elected to defer his commission and instead try his luck at the sport he had gown to love, professional wrestling. While his family were somewhat disappointed, they threw their support behind Lance. It was also during this time that Lance and Alanna formalized their commitment to each other, marrying in a beautiful church ceremony. Shortly after getting married, Lance enrolled in the wrestling school run by the legendary Eric Bold, where he trained alongside such NWC luminaries as The Hunter, Dargan, Ryan Stephens and Devin Fox as well as Eric's two sons, Alex and Michael. The elder Bold was a tough teacher, expecting nothing but the best from his students. Lance absorbed as much as he could from Eric Bold, seeking to become as skilled a wrestler as possible. Upon graduating from Eric Bold's school, Lance began to make his way in the sport. After spending some time in federations such as AICW and WWO, Lance decided to go for the brass ring by aiming for a shot in the National Wrestling Council. He was hired by California based federation Golden State Wrestling, which was where he truly began to flourish in the sport; the high caliber of talent present in the NWC helping Lance to sharpen and refine his own skills. Lance stayed with GSW through their change into the New England based federation known as NEWS. In fact, it was on NEWS's very first PPV, New Frontier, where Lance captured his first regional title, NEWS's Network NEWS title. Lance would go on to hold the Network NEWS title twice as well as have a stint as NEWS's Headline NEWS champion. Upon the unfortunate closure of NEWS, Lance would go on to join both GCW and LCW. It was while in LCW that Lance would hit one of the lowest periods of his life. After losing the NWC World Heavyweight Title to BloodAngel following what was close to the longest ever reign as champion, Lance found it hard to spring back and regain his earlier fire. He began to grow bitter and resentful, believing that he was not being afforded the respect he felt he had earned. His performance began to become inconsistent and his focus began to waver. This, combined with an long-standing, vicious feud with GD West and a developing problem with alcohol did little to help Lance's state of mind. Eventually, Lance snapped, hiring outside interference to attack his opponents in a match and turning his back on everything he had previously represented. He began to use methods he had previously condemned, abandoned his allies and turned his back on the fans. As his standing among the fans slipped, so did Lance's morale and mental state. After one particularly bad match, Lance realized that something had to change. He took an immediate leave of absence from LCW, checking himself into rehab and devoting his time to repairing himself and his relationship with his family. After this time off, Lance returned to the now newly-reformed NWC and immediately found a slot in the Pacific region. While his intent was to rebuild his career from the ground up, Lance was almost immediately thrust into the main event, winning both the support of the fans and the NWC World Heavyweight Title for the second time. It was while he was in the Pacific region of the NWC that Lance (among many others) became embroiled in the battle between Alexandra Pierce (better known as Desade) and Chris Schukar over the fate of the NWC...a battle which ended with the NWC closing its doors forever. After spending some time away from wrestling making his living in the physique industry, Lance came back to wrestling in order to help out BloodAngel against the Dead Man's Hand. Fired up again with the energy and passion that he once had for the sport, Lance accepted STD's offer of employment in SCCW. Lance's new mission in SCCW is simple: to kick ass, take names and have a damn good time doing it.
