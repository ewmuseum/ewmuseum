Friar Biography Real Name Leon Nicknames the Hellhound Hometown Boulder, CO Birthday August 6, 1977 Height 6' Weight 224 Biography Friar is the man in the mask. Who is underneath, has yet to be seen. Leon Christmas started out in the wrestling business on a mission - a mission to bring justice to the cowards, scumbags, and corrupt. No matter where it took him. He traveled to China, Australian caves, and Tibetan monasteries to preserve Justice's place in the world. Fighting, and losing at times, Leon Christmas was wearing down and looking for a fight that would not risk his life with each moment of battle. Just then it just so happens American wrestling owners were sending scouts across the globe, searching the world for their 'foreign treasure' (it's a fad, don't worry). What a handful of scouts from Global Championship Wrestling and Sin city Championship Wrestling found would NOT be that foreign gem. It would be a blond man, wearing a black mask, and wrestling his American ass off in it all over the other 6 continents. For twelve years, Leon has been an unknown around the world and excellent at doing it. After competing in Sin City's inaugural No Limit tournament and losing to Wyatt Connors, he inked a deal with GCW to build up his repertoire and in-ring skills. Then, out of the clear blue, SCCW granted Friar a six-month trial contract. The verdict? Stay tuned.
