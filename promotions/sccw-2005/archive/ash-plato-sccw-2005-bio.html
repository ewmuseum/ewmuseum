Ash Plato Biography Real Name Ash Plato Nicknames None Hometown Las Vegas Valley Metroplex Birthday October 31, 1983 Height 6'1 Weight 225 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Background: Born and raised in Las Vegas, Ash grew up in a wild climate. Las Vegas was and still is out of control. The multinational gaming conglomerates, mega corporations, roving gangs of thugs, and utter lawlessness of the citizenry made it a harsh environment for any child to grow up in. During his tumultuous teenage years, Ash found release in the arts. His sculptures, paintings, and writings were a release for him. However he withdrew and became more anti-social then he was in the past. After graduating from high school, it was all too much for Ash. He got into political activism and may be considered an anarchist with his actions. The crime rate in his neighborhood was out of control as well and someone close to him died. For months he was distraught and depressed, having no direction especially after the death of his fiance Jessica, but Ash was inspired by the movie Dark Knight and at nights he becomes the vigilante anti-hero known as Judge. In his all black attire, Judge judges the criminals he catches by either leaving them for the cops (minor crimes) or by killing them (more severe crimes). However he is not a hero at all. He has been known to take the money or other items that the criminals have stolen to keep for himself. The cops are not happy at all over his actions so he is also a wanted criminal for the murders he has caused from his 'judging.'
