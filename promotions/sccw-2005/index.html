A roleplay federation born out of XCW's own on-screen funeral in November 2005, which ran Killzone and Temptation weekly beside its FUSE brand, carried seven championships across both brands, and closed on a Temptation in June 2010. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Sin City Championship Wrestling ran from 12 November 2005 to 13 June 2010, on air as FUSE until a title card on 16 February 2008 renamed it. Its pilot, Wired 1.1, opened on an announcer's line — “From the ashes of XCW FUSE has risen” — over three matches, and on the scene that framed it Angelo Sinclaire, XCW's former chief, talked a Japanese investor into giving him two shows to land a star who had turned him down.

It was built on a run of weekly shows: sixteen cards of Wired to May 2006, fifty of Killzone from June 2006 to September 2007, Uproar through the winter, and Temptation, a weekly Cinemax broadcast by its Rules page, from 25 February 2008 to the last card. Its largest nights — ReVolution, War in Washington, Defiance and Sin on Spike — carried the biggest matches.

The rename was staged. At the close of Uproar on 16 February 2008, in Las Vegas, the FUSE logo spun like the reels of a slot machine until it read SCCW, and a sign welcomed viewers to Sin City Championship Wrestling.

Seven championships passed through 102 reigns across the two names: the Universal, Gateway, LiveWire and SIN titles that its Rules page lists, beside FUSE's Custom Chaos, Fusion and Fatal Faction belts. The longest dated reign of any of them is Aimz's on the Universal Championship, 421 days from 2 August 2008 to 27 September 2009. Its Year End awards were given out at Defiance II on 11 January 2009, when the ring announcer introduced her as the 2008 Wrestler of the Year, and the first class of its Hall of Fame — Smitty T. Duluth, Jason Cruise and Clinton Sage — was inducted at Defiance III on 7 March 2010.

Its last card, Temptation on 13 June 2010, laid out the brackets of a High Roller tournament to fill the vacant Universal Championship and closed on a promise of Sin on Spike V, live from Newark over the Fourth of July weekend. No later card, news post or roleplay survives, and how the federation ended isn't in the surviving record.
