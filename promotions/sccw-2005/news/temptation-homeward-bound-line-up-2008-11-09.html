Temptation: Homeward Bound Line-Up!

9 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling, in association with the Kent Fusiliers, proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION: Homeward Bound! LIVE! Friday, November 14 at 10PM Eastern (that's 11PM Atlantic) from the Halifax Metro Centre in Halifax, Nova Scotia and ONLY on Cinemax! MAIN EVENT For the Sin City Championship Wrestling Universal Championship! "The Path of Destruction" Mayhem Vs. "The Red Raver" Aimz © On paper, it is a ridiculous mismatch -- Amy Campbell gives up nearly a foot and a half and close to two times her body weight against the Path of Destruction, not to mention what condition she'll be in after that chair-bending shot she took from Mayhem just before the Quebec City show went off the air! But this is Amy's hometown, and you know the crowd will support her there unlike anywhere else in the world. Can even that X-factor shift the scales against the Wild Card of the Dead Man's Hand? And, speaking of that, where WILL Alexandra Pierce stand? Find out as Amy Campbell wrestles the only man who could challenge Lance Marshall for strongest man in SCCW! Special Guest Ringside Enforcer: BloodAngel "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall Their rivalry goes back to the days of the National Wrestling Council, and in Halifax, it will be renewed as the False Prophet, now steamrolling towards the Gateway Title, will face the man set to take on hometown hero Amy Campbell at Defiance! And Edward Lambert will be at ringside to keep the Decency Crusade from getting involved -- or will he be more interested in some payback? Wyatt Connors gets a shot to continue his roll in Halifax, which might be the only place in the world where Lance Marshall will [I]not[/I] be hailed as a conquering hero! Tag-Team Action! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy & Jadian Bridden Vs. Jared Sykes & Charley Crisp After Jadian Bridden's actions in Charley Crisp's match against Malice and Bridden's past resentment with the former King Blueberry, the so-called Ace of the Dead Man's Hand has two very pissed-off jokesters out to get him! Now the Ace will team with the self-proclaimed King of the Dead Man's Hand -- the next contender for Sean Sterling's Gateway Championship! With Bridden due a PPV match with Charley, can these two members of the DMH, teaming for the first time, take down the wacky pair? Black Ops Vs. Gregory Orion Daniels & Syn After serving as fill-in referee for Syn's debut match with Clayton Byrd, the former three-time NWC Tag Team Champions will wrestle together for the first time in nearly seven years as they face Syn and the Future! Will we once and for all find out how bad Mr. Hawke's leg is? Can GOD and Syn work together? And will Clayton Byrd have something to say about this match? PLUS! - We will find out whom the fans selected to win the inaugural SCCW Costume Contest! - Dr Kensington has been making noise about an announcement! - After his involvement in the festivities in Quebec City, is Dusk coming to Sin City? SCCW Temptation! Two weeks away! From the Blue Cross Arena in Rochester, New York! For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Paul Searle Vs. Charley Crisp © Plus an update on the condition of Hunter Sabuani from his ex-wife Miss Madison! SCCW Temptation! Three weeks away! From Gampel Pavilion on the Campus of the University of Connecticut! DNA (Desade & Aimz) Vs. Lance Marshall & Jared Sykes SCCW DEFIANCE II! ONE MONTH AWAY! From the Nassau Coliseum on Long Island, NY! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Universal Championship! "The Lion" Lance Marshall Vs. Mayhem or Aimz © A No Excuses Match for the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy Vs. "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling © For the SCCW LiveWire Championship!* Jadian Bridden Vs. Charley Crisp © (* Provided Crisp Retains Against Searle in Rochester.)
