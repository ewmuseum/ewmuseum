Killzone Presents Afflicted Glory

19 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUSE Wrestling Presents. . . Afflicted Glory Rexall Place - Edmonton, AB, CA - May 26th, 2007 Match |[#01]| »» The Opening Contest... Three weeks ago on KillZone, 'Mean' Dean Jokerlin discovered the man behind several brutal attacks on FUSE backstage workers was none other than self-proclaimed FUSE legend "The New Shit" Bobino. To open up Afflicted Glory, Jokerlin and the rest of the FUSE Roadies will attempt to exact revenge on Bobino, as the two square off in a Roadie Lumberjack Match. Something tells me if Bobino gets tossed over the top rope, these workers won't show much mercy. Roadie Lumberjack Match "Mean" Dean Jokerlin Vs. "The New Shit" Bobino Match |[#02]| »» For Gods Championship... After weeks of pleading his case, Hoyt Williams steps into the ring at Afflicted Glory to finally prove once and for all that former Universal champion Jacob McKail is, in fact, a racist, putting his God's title on the line against Imperial Wizard of the KKK Papa McKail, who he claims is Jacob's father. One on One Papa McKail Vs. Hoyt Williams Match |[#03]| »» Fatal Affliction Championships... Last week on KillZone, Fred Fusion and Ethan Frost demanded a re-match for the less than legitimate championship currently held by Affliction Members, The Legacy. "The Fusion Title" now known as "The Fatal Affliction Championships" will be on the line, as the duo finally gets a shot at Jake Reed and Lance Reed in a ladder match. Ladder Match Fred Fusion & Ethan Frost Vs. The Legacy Match |[#04]| »» Fatal Faction Action... Two weeks ago on KillZone, The Ralph and Phillip Guild, better known as the RPG, made a mockery of new FUSE faction The Cowboys From Hell. An altercation soon followed, and now these two new teams will fight for the contendership to the coveted Fatal Faction championships, currently held by The Alliance. #01 Contendership to the Fatal Faction Championships The RPG Vs. Cowboys from Hell Match |[#05]| »» LiveWire Rules... After a small break from title defenses, current FUSE LiveWire champion Lane Stevens puts his belt on the line at Afflicted Glory against the man that nearly took it off his hands at KZ35, Cyrus Raynes. Can Stevens pull off another upset with creative use of the LiveWire rules? Or will his month long plus reign finally come to and end? LiveWire Championship Cyrus Raynes Vs. Lane Stevens © Match |[#06]| »» Triple Threat For weeks three men have been absolutely at eachothers throats, non stop. Finally it comes to a conclusion, as Timo Bolamba steps into the ring with Dave Gibson and Tsuyoshi Tanaka in a triple threat match. Could this decide the next contender for the Universal title? Watch and find out! Timo Bolamba Vs. Dave Gibson Vs. Tsuyoshi Tanaka Match |[#07]| »» Reign of Chaos finals... After a month of brutal action, the Reign of Chaos tournament comes to a close at Afflicted Glory. One man, when all is said and done, will be the new Custom Chaos champion. Last week finalist Jonathan Rhine suffered a surprise attack at the hands of Clinton Sage. How will this effect his chances against Kid Cool? Not to mention, it all takes place in one of FUSE Wrestling's most dangerous events, The Asylum. The Asylum - Custom Chaos Championship Kid Cool Vs. Jonathan Rhine Match |[#08]| »» MORE Faction Action... After losing their Fatal Faction titles to The Alliance a few weeks ago, the team of Darcy Crisis & Aimz look to reclaim the belts at Afflicted Glory, in a rematch. Will the seemingly shakey team be able to top the two that defeated them less than a month ago? Fatal Faction Championships Darcy Crisis & Aimz Vs. The Alliance © Match |[#09]| »» The Main Event... Five weeks ago, it was Clinton Sage defeating John Covel for the Universal Championship. Three weeks ago, Jacob McKail stunned Sage and snagged the belt from his greatest rival. Last week, John Covel struck back against McKail, taking advantage of a misplaced chair-shot from Clinton Sage to become a two-time FUSE Universal Champion. The last time these men were on a FUSE pay-per-view, Clinton Sage screwed Jacob McKail out of a victory in their classic cage match, and John Covel defeated Tigera to claim his first Universal Title Reign. When the spotlight shines on any of these three men, the wrestling world watches. In our Main Event, these three rivals once again enter the ring to do battle for FUSE's highest prize, the Universal Championship. Will Jacob McKail or Clinton Sage join the two-timers club, or will John Covel continue his hot streak and walk out of the pay-per-view still holding on to the gold? Universal Championship Jacob McKail Vs. Clinton Sage Vs. John Covel
