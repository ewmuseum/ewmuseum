Full Equilibrium Lineup Released!

16 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... SCCW EQUILIBRIUM! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, September 27, 2009 from the Thomas & Mack Center in Las Vegas, Nevada and ONLY on PAY-PER-VIEW! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Universal Championship! "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors Vs. "The Red Raver" Aimz © Four hundred and twenty-three days. When she steps into the ring with the Wise Guy in Las Vegas, it will have been a remarkable four hundred and twenty-three days that Amy Campbell has sat atop Sin City Championship Wrestling as the self-professed Superversal Champion. But with all due respect to the men she's defeated, none of them were as dastardly as Wyatt Connors, himself just off a nearly nine-month run as the Elite Champion! Now, with her secrets laid bare through the twisted plots of a twisted man, Amy Campbell will try to put this particular ghost to bed. But there's a reason the Wise Guy held that belt, and there's a reason he has this shot – not the least of which being he is the only man to hold a singles victory over Campbell during her impressive reign! What tricks will Wyatt have in store? What kind of mental frame-of-mind will Amy Campbell be in? Will she get to day 424? Find out in a main event that you've all been clamoring for! A Ladder Match for the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes Vs. "The Lost Soul" Dusk Vs. Spacely © Ever since his defeat of the Spider in the Web to claim the Gateway Championship, the Hulk Hogan of the Heavens has been on a remarkable run in Sin City, including a victory over Jared Sykes in Oakland! But if he's going to defend the title he won in the Dome of Doom, Spacely is going to have to defeat not just the hungrier-than-ever Sykes, but also the enigmatic Lost Soul. What is Dusk's motivation? Can Sykes stop his slide and claim the second-biggest prize in the land? Or will Spacely add two more feathers into his cap? Oh, and there's the small matter of putting these three in a ladder match to worry about. A Triple Threat Match for the SCCW LiveWire Championship! "Simply Better" Xander Searle Vs. "The Ace in the Hole" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. Baron von Blackberry © The nefarious Devil Fruit, thanks to the interjection of Legion, has wedged himself into the rivalry between Xander Searle and Jadian Bridden, and thus far, he's come out of it with the LiveWire Championship on his shoulder (he calls it Blackberry, Jr.). Now, Blackberry will attempt to defend the title against the man who he beat for it, the shooting star, Xander Searle, and a man who has been on quite the roll these last few weeks, Jadian Bridden! Will Blackberry, Jr., find a new home (perhaps nestled beside the original LW title belt, currently held by Juli Minorez?), or will SCIENCE prevail? For the Big Slick Bracelet! "The New Life" Jonathan Rhine Vs. "The Spider in the Web" Desade Alexandra Pierce (then masquerading as Charlotte Ramone) won the Big Slick Bracelet at Heaven & Hell on August 2, 2008 – the same day Amy Campbell won the SCCW Universal Championship. But despite having a free shot at the champion whenever she wanted it, the Spider has left her gaudy piece of jewelry on her mantelpiece – and now we know why. Jonathan Rhine came back to SCCW for one reason and one reason only – to rid the federation of the depraved Director, and he can pluck away a tasty treat indeed if he can win that bracelet! Pierce and Rhine, one-on-one in Las Vegas, just might be worth the price of admission all by itself! "Grudge Match" Isn't Strong Enough! "The River Rat" Lane Stevens Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall There have been a lot of horrible things done in the world of professional wrestling – lives and livelihoods changed forever. But nothing matches the depths to which Lane Stevens has sunk, (allegedly) falsifying Alanna Marshall's test results and giving Team Marshall a false sense of hope! In Oakland, Lance promised that the next time he faced Lane Stevens in the ring, the River Rat would never wrestle again – can the Lion follow through on his promise, or – like Jon Rhine in Oakland – will Lane Stevens find a way to win? A Team into Turmoil Match! The Winners of the Tag Match Face Off For the #1 Contendership to the LiveWire Title! "The Modern Day Warrior" Jay Draven & "The Devil's Reject" Legion Vs. Josh Marquez & "The Rapmaster of Rap" Rick Malloy Vs. "The Sword of Damocles" Darius Drannen & Mitchell Quinlan Another innovative match concept from the Kent Fusiliers will see a new #1 contender to the LiveWire Championship! Three teams will face off in a tag-team war, and the winning team will face each other IMMEDIATELY for the right to face the winner of the Bridden-Searle-Blackberry match at a later date! Will it be one of the former LiveWire Champions, seeking to become the first to ever hold that title twice? Or the newcomers, Marquez and Drannen, who have impressed in their short Sin City tenures? Or perhaps Mitchell Quinlan, in his third opportunity to win a shot at the belt? And... will Jay Draven even show up? For the #1 Contendership to the Strength in Numbers Championships! Saul Goode & Ben Chillin Vs. Daddy Daughter Day Two weeks ago in Portland, the team of August and April Monday were robbed of the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships by the machinations of Alexandra Pierce's Dead Man's Hand, and at Equilibrium, they can take the first step towards getting a one-on-one opportunity against the champions! Their opponents are the very definition of plucky, two young surfer kids with an electrifying arsenal and absolutely nothing to lose! Who will get the next shot at Desade and Lance Marshall? Find out in Vegas!
