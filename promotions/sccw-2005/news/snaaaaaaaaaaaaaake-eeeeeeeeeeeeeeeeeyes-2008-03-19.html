SNAAAAAAAAAAAAAAKE EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEYES

19 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, bitches! Temptation is up! Wanna see if Aimz took down Gideon Fontaine? Want to see the outcome of the crazy convoluted tag team match from hell? Want to see the latest twists and turns of Jonathan Rhine's sordid personal life? Then CLICK THAT LINK! And now, without any further introductions, the CARD OF SNAKE EYES, SCCW'S FIRST PAY PER VIEW! From the desk of Smitty T. Duluth: For Immediate Release! Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) PROUDLY BRINGS TO YOU SNAKE EYES! L-I-V-E LIVE! Sunday, March 30, 2008 at 8PM and ONLY ON PAY-PER-VIEW! (Contact your local cable operator to order today!) From the Miramar Marine Corps Air Station in San Diego, California! ((ROLEPLAYING BEGINS*: NOW! ROLEPLAYING ENDS*: 11:59 PM Pacific Daylight Time, Tuesday, March 25, 2008. SEGMENTS DUE: 11:59PM Pacific Daylight Time, Wednesday, March 26, 2008. MATCHES DUE: 11:59PM Pacific Daylight Time, Thursday, March 27, 2008 * Please note that role-playing for the PTC Unified Title Match is subject to different deadlines)) With the New Life flying high, can he survive both halves of the main event? DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! For the PTC Unified Heavyweight Championship! "The New Life" Jonathan Rhine [challenger] Vs. Tchu/Lindsay Troy [champion - pending the outcome of PRIME Culture Shock] This is it, we're gonna bring the big one back home! Sure, Clinton Sage dropped the PTC championship to Tchu, but whenever I get around to watching my TiVo of Culture Shock, I'm sure that Jonathan Rhine will be able to win, right? Sure, the champ is all special and championship-y, but that doesn't mean anything because he/she/it doesn't work for Sin City Championship Wrestling, baby! For the SCCW Universal Championship! Aimz [challenger] Vs. "The New Life" Jonathan Rhine [champion] What? He's wrestling twice? Is he retarded? After being brained in the head by Amy Campbell on the debut installment of SCCW Temptation, these two have been going at it (well, mostly Amy's been kind of a bitch, and Jon's been taking it), but after the Red Raver threatened his former girl-on-the-side, the New Life stood up and said, "No, you di'n't!" Now, despite the fact that he was booking himself into a second match on the show, Rhino will be facing Aimz one-on-one, with his coveted Universal Championship on the line! For the SCCW Gateway Championship AND the AWC LiveWire Championship! "The Obsidian Nightmare" Corvus [challenger/champion] Vs. "The Frenchman" Joaquin Pierre [challenger/champion] These two have literally been going at it for months, and now they'll both bring their championship gold to the dance for a chance to finally put this rivalry to bed! Corvus defeated Pierre and Joshua Kosidlo at FUSE on ESPN to win the AWC LiveWire Championship, but Pierre's recent win over El Diablo has put the Frenchman back into the Obsidian Nightmare's crosshairs! These two will go man-up in what's sure to be a brutal, physical contest in front of the troops! Triple Threat Match for the SCCW LiveWire Championship! The True Prophet (w/Jeremiah) [challenger] Vs. The Demon Vassago (w/The Iniquity) [challenger] Vs. Elliott. Fucking. Rollins. (w/Slick Willy McDormant) Ever since upending Jason Cruise's record-breaking LiveWire Championship reign, Elliott Rollins has been next-to-untouchable, despite the best efforts of such FUSE and SCCW luminaries as Duncan Drake, Tony Pride, and Mayhem! Now the man with the foul-mouthed nickname rolls into San Diego with his fourth -- and perhaps most dangerous -- title defense! He'll face a relative unknown in the True Prophet, who's only wrestled once in an SCCW ring (albeit most impressively) and the man who suffered his first SCCW defeat last week in the Demon, Vassago! Can Elliott overcome the odds and hold onto his championship one more time? For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! Andre Jensen and Sameer al-Jezuri [challengers] Vs. The Black List [champions] Last week was a hit-or-miss week for our buddies in the Black List -- while Gideon struggled against a more violent Amy Campbell, Zakk Wylde already pinned one half of his opponents next week! What am I thinking in giving them a title match? I'm thinking I need Andre to shut up and quit calling my office for five goddamn seconds! Hopefully, the Black List can march into San Diego and beat the hell out of these terrorists and then carry on with more important business... but, man, that Sameer guy is pretty big, isn't he? TWO CAGE MATCHES ONE SHOW, OH NOES! First, a Singles Bout: Ringside Enforcers - Lance and Alanna Marshall Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. Mayhem (w/members of Magnum Opus) What the hell do you need ringside enforcers for a cage match for? If anyone saw the chaos involved in the Mayhem/Sabuani vs. Bridden/BloodAngel match, you wouldn't need to be asking me that. Lance and Alanna Marshall will stand at ringside to keep Julissa Minorez and Maestro's Magnum Opus from trying to get involved as Jadian Bridden tries to knock a block off Mayhem! These two have been going at it for weeks now; this is their final test! Then, a Seven Minutes in Hell Match (also in the cage!) BloodAngel Vs. "Peerless" Hunter Sabuani (w/members of the Dead Man's Hand) Is anyone else sick and tired of the goddamn Dead Man's Hand? Then you should root as hard as I am for Edward Lambert this week at the pay-per-view, as BA faces the Raja of Whatever-He's-Calling-Himself-This-Week. Because if BloodAngel wins the cage match (where Sabuani's helpers can't easily get involved), then our favorite person, Ms. Alexandra Pierce, will be handcuffed to the top turnbuckle IN that cage with BloodAngel... for seven minutes. Go BloodAngel! Career on the Line! "The Lord of the Asylum" Christian Darke Vs. A Mystery Opponent! So far, I'm 1-1 in finding mystery opponents for assholes, but with Christian Darke's very future in SCCW on the line, hopefully I've found the magic button that will get rid of the Undertow (the nugget that remains of the Affliction) in an all-fired hurry. If there are any Christian Darke fans, you'd better tune in, because... he's going away after this week, because I have the power of MYSTERY on my side! Two Big Guys Hit Each Other Hard, Round Three! Cyrus "Mongo" Raynes Vs. "The Biloxi Beast" Bishop Here's a rivalry that's just starting to heat up, as Cyrus Raynes and Bishop have had it out for the last two weeks. So I figure, hey, this card is not jam-packed-stacked enough, why not throw two of my bulls in the ring and see what happens, right? Right!   So this is usually the part where I say, "PLUS!" and talk about a bunch of stuff that might (will) happen. But it's late and I'm tired and we're on pay-per-view, so you know that if there's a stop? I'm pulling it out. Just tune in already! Go BloodAngel Hugs, STD
