Killzone 36 Airs!

28 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The stars of FUSE shined bright under the lights of Killzone 36. There was more action than a person can possibly mention, in less than twenty minutes, on the most recent addition of the FUSE flagship. There were debuts galore on the show as Slade Nottingham, Hoss Richards, and Jeb Stewart all participated in their first FUSE action. Did any of them make an impact? The show kicked off with Clinton Sage in the ring bragging about his title win. After the attack he recieved from McKail last week would the Seventh Sin get some payback? FUSE management made good on their claim, and Hoyt Williams arrived in FUSE Wrestling. What did 'The Second Son of God' have to say in his first official FUSE appearance? A lot. KZ 36 also saw the start of the Reign of Chaos Tournament with some first round action when Damien Cruz took on Johnathan Rhine and Lundgard took on Phil Atken. Would either of the newcomers be able to upset a FUSE legend? Championships were also on the line as Lane Stevens put his LiveWire Championship on the line against Leah Adams. The River Rat has been on one hell of a streak lately, mostly due to large amounts of cheating, but could Leah Adams finally put an end to his reign? In the Main Event the Fatal Factions were put on the line between two teams who have grown to despise one another over the last few weeks. The champs, Aimz and Crisis, stepped into the ring to face the red-hot combo of The Alliance, with the gold on the line. Who walked away with the early bragging rights, and the belts, in this war of factions? All that and lets not forget that Timo and Tanaka continued to build on their brewing rivalry while Jacob McKail just might have found his most 'powerful' enemy yet.
