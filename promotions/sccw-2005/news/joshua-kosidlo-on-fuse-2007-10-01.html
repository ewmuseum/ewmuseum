Joshua Kosidlo on FUSE

1 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For many decades now, wrestling has held the spotlight, often when things go wrong but sometimes when everything is just right. In these past years many of the hottest young stars have made their way through the ranks, and divisions of wrestling organizations all over the world. With a plethora of media coverage, from local papers to late breaking news members of the wrestling world have garnered more exposure then in decades past. In the soon to be released issue of Pro-Wrestling Monthly, a monthly wrestling magazine published by an independent party, FUSE’s own Joshua Kosidlo will be included in a feature article. The article is set to cover the very beginnings of Kosidlo’s career over twelve years ago and is to include his arrival in FUSE. Included in this article is a sit down interview conducted with Joshua Kosidlo, formerly of Global fame. For obvious reasons we cannot include the full interview here, however we were given a small preview of that upcoming interview, that can be found below. In this excerpt Kosidlo was asked his feelings on his arrival to FUSE, as well as his thoughts on what kind of impact he would have. PWM: Well, Mr. Kosidlo, with your tenure in Global coming to a close, and the beginning of a new chapter in your career set to begin in FUSE, what are the things you are most looking forward to? Kosidlo: Well, I feel I’ve let myself get old in the Octagon of Global, and what I’m actually hoping to have happen in FUSE, is to make myself step up my game. I’ll be going into a whole new game with some of the best wrestlers in the business. I’m hoping it will make me reinvent myself, maybe pound out what few good years I have left in this ragged old body. PWM: Do you see yourself as a future FUSE Universal Champion? Joshua appeared to be embarrassed by this question, but he had the courtesy to reply. Kosidlo: Well I’d love to get a chance to go for it someday, but it’s nothing I have my eyes set on yet, maybe if by the will of God, and the support of my fans I can get to that level before I retire, then I’d love to call myself a FUSE Universal Champion, but that’s getting to far ahead of ourselves. PWM: Joshua, what kind of statement do you expect to make with your arrival in FUSE? Kosidlo: Well, I’m hoping to show everyone what I can do, and maybe prove to a lot of people out there that some people still love wrestling. The interview goes on into further detail with his first match in FUSE, and what his expectations were for his first match with Joaquin Pierre. However, we already know what went down in that match, as Joshua Kosidlo pulled off his first win in FUSE just two weeks ago now. With one impressive win in the record books of FUSE, and a feature article in a wrestling magazine, Joshua Kosidlo looks to hold more promise then ever before in his career. Some might speculate FUSE was a big part of it, while others might just say the guy has earned it. Whatever the reason may be, we expect to see good things coming from Kosidlo here in FUSE, and those good things may just kick off next week on Uproar, when Joshua Kosidlo takes on fellow former Global Fighter, Jacque Noreault.
