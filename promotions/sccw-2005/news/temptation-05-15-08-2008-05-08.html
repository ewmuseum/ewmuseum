Temptation: 05-15-08

8 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the Desk of Smitty T. Duluth For Immediate Release Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) Proudly Brings to YOU! TEMPTATION! LIVE! Thursday, May 15, 2008 at 10PM Eastern/9PM Central and only on Cinemax! From the Williams Arena and Sports Pavilion on the campus of the University of Minnesota in Minneapolis! MAIN EVENT Semi-Finals of the Elliott Rollins Memorial LiveWire Title Tournament Special Guest Commentator: "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors Winner Faces Connors at SIN on SPIKE! "The Demon" Vassago (w/The Den of Iniquity) Vs. Winner of Bishop/Charley Crisp Match This is it -- the winner of this match will take on the man who will be sitting at ringside to help call the action! The timely interference of Cleopatra in Vassago's bout with Jadian Bridden and Bishop has given The Demon almost the literal catbird seat. He and the Den of Iniquity will sit back and wait while Bishop and Charley Crisp beat the stuffing out of each other, and all he'll have to do is pick up the pieces for a literal God vs. The Devil match-up at SIN on SPIKE! Dual Referee Match! Special Referees: Alanna Marshall and Julissa Minorez Jadian Bridden Vs. Lance Marshall Two weeks ago, Jadian Bridden stabbed Lance Marshall in the back, knocking the Lion for a loop with some brass knucks and left his wife Alanna set up for a shot to the back of the head from Desade! Now the former World Champion will get his hands on the scrawny little bastard, a week after literally pitching him out of the ring -- in Minneapolis, we'll see perhaps the first ever Dual Referee Match! Both Julissa and Alanna will be legal referees for this bout... and given what's WAY at the bottom of this lineup, you have to imagine that Jadian and Julissa are not the only members of the Dead Man's Hand we'll see for this one! For the SCCW Strength In Numbers Championships! Ciel & "The Steel Rose" Dawn McFarlane Vs. Andre Jensen/His Partner (c) Yeah, you've read the rest of the card right, and, no, Andre Jensen didn't bribe me or anything. Brack Conway ASKED for something like this, and we're giving it to him. Later in the same night that McFarlane and Ciel will face stiff one-on-one competition, they'll get into the ring with the Strength In Numbers champions -- Andre Jensen and Khalid or Kazim or Ahmed or something. No offense to our Arab viewers, but... yeah. I can't remember his name. Singles Warm-Up Bouts! "Cold as Ice" Ethan Frost Vs. "The Steel Rose" Dawn McFarlane Last week, Ethan Frost was in the wrong place at the wrong time, suffering a brutal loss to Amy Campbell in a bad mood. This week, the tests don't get any easier, as he'll face the woman (can't say girl, can't say girl) who took Aimz to her limit a couple weeks ago (and how much does THAT draw mean now, huh?). Can Frost bounce back and cause McFarlane to stumble the same night she'll be facing Andre Jensen? "The WyldChild" Zakk Wylde Vs. Ciel Zakk Wylde has been on a hell of a roll in Sin City, having held the SCCW SIN titles with the Black List and holding a singles victory over the half of the current champions that I can remember the name of. Now, on the same night that Ciel will attempt to become SIN champions himself, and coming off a brutal Cloud 9 Invitational match, he'll try to avoid becoming yet another victim to the WyldChild! Cross-Promotional Challenge Match Maddox (of the Macabre Militia) Vs. Markus Stone (of Banned & Exiled~!) This war just keeps getting worse! The yearlong GCW Tag Team Champions critically injured one-third of the Macabre Militia last week in Boise, and now the PTC's only deaf wrestler (at least I hope, I mean) will face Markus Stone in Minneapolis for some bragging rights! What parts will Alice and Chris Storm play... and who the hell was Alice talking about not wanting to see? Elimination Match in the Elliott Rollins Memorial LiveWire Tournament! Bishop (w/Sarah Riley) Vs. Charley Crisp Both of these men came up short last week in a three-way dance last week, the Biloxi Beast eliminated by Cookie Crisp before Wyatt Connors snaked out an enviable place at SIN on SPIKE! Now they're both in a horrible position -- having to wrestle twice on one night in order to earn a chance to... wrestle twice in two weeks at SIN on SPIKE! Can Charley Crisp face off with the first of two monsters or will we see a battle of behemoths to cap off our night in Minnesota? A "How Dare You Drop Me On My Head?" Match! "The Modern Day Warrior" Jay Draven Vs. "The Punk Rock Pixie" Charlotte Ramone Two weeks ago, these two had some rather nasty words for each other before the Macabre Militia assaulted the Modern Day Warrior! Then last week, after saving the Punk Rock Pixie from the Dead Man's Hand, Draven rewarded the plucky star of her own reality show (contact STD Entertainment, Inc. for details) with a spinebuster! Now these two will face off in an attempt to keep the decent people from hating each other! PLUS! - We have a NEW Sin City Championship Wrestling Universal Champion! A week after the biggest heist in Boise in the history of ever, Amy Campbell will be in Minneapolis with her shiny new toy! What is the condition of... hell, she beat up half the goddamn roster, didn't she? What's the condition of Jonathan Rhine after his first pinfall loss to an SCCW superstar? How is Katie Malick? And how goddamn intolerable is Aimz going to be now that she's got the Universal Championship on her shoulder? - As part of the Boise Buttfuckery, Desade screwed BloodAngel out of his scheduled match with the Champion! While Lambert paid the Spider back by putting Mr. Ashe in the hospital, you've gotta wonder what kind of vengeance Desade will have in mind, one week away from being forced to face Ed Lambert with the new Universal Champion at her side! - Mayhem has captured the Gateway Championship! What is the condition of Joaquin Pierre's knee after his hard-fought loss to the Path of Destruction? What are the Maestro's plans, now that his man has captured the second most sought-after title in the federation? Just four defenses away from a shot at Amy Campbell! - William McDormant returned to the air last week and told us that Elliott Rollins isn't dead! (I was surprised, too.) Will we see Slick Willy again this week, and when will we see Rollins again? This week? All this and the surprises that ONLY SCCW can bring you (seriously, did YOU think that Amy Campbell would become SCCW Champion this week?) -- only in Minneapolis, one week before SIN on SPIKE! SCCW SIN ON SPIKE! Memorial Day! From the United Center in Chicago, Illinois! Main Event! BloodAngel & Lance Marshall Vs. Aimz & Desade For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Connors Must Lose TWICE in One Night Not To Become Champion! Survivor of Vassago/Bishop/Crisp Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors TUNE IN TO TEMPTATION TO LEARN THE WHOLE CARD FOR SIN ON SPIKE!
