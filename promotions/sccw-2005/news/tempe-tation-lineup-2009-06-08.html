Tempe-tation Lineup

8 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As Sin City powers out of Utah and into Arizona, questions surrounding who will compete in SIN on SPIKE 3's main event are starting to be answered! We'll get more answers in Tempe for... Tempe-tation! Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... Tempe-tation! L-I-V-E LIVE Tuesday, June 16, 2009 from the Wells Fargo Arena in Tempe, Arizona! MAIN EVENT – A TICKET TO RIDE MATCH! Jared Sykes Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall The Dome of Doom will surely be the greatest test of many athletes, and possibly the final hurdle for the Red Raver to climb before her reign reaches the one-year mark! While Alexandra Pierce seeks to stack the Dome with the champ's allies, she won't find any as The Lion and the returning Black Sheep step into the ring with each other for the first time in almost seven years! Can the Lion, who has been so close for so long to regaining what was stolen from him, the former LiveWire and SIN Champion? Or will Jared Sykes' return to Sin City launch himself back to the top of the card? Six-Man Tag Action! Daddy Daughter Day (August & April Monday) & "The Las Vegas Lariat" Phillip Kennedy (w/Katsidy and Savant) Vs. Black Ops (Mr. Ashe and Mr. Hawke(?)) & "The River Rat" Lane Stevens Lane Stevens and the Three-Redhead Monster tried to put Phillip Kennedy out of action for good in Provo! Now, they'll come to Tempe with one mission: make sure Phillip Kennedy is physically unable to enter the Dome of Doom! Can Kennedy overcome the odds one more time, with the unproven newcomers of August and April Monday to back him up? Or do Stevens and Pierce and Campbell have one more ace up their sleeve? Could it start with: is Mr. Hawke even in any condition to wrestle? Can Anyone Shut This Yahoo Up? Mitchell Quinlan Vs. Xander Searle Last week, Xander Searle's team managed to be on the losing end in no small part due to the efforts of the LiveWire Champion! Now the man who says he's Simply Better will climb into the ring with a man who, but for an assault by the man in white, may have that title shot in HIS pocket, Mitchell Quinlan. Can Xander Searle continue on his winning way, now steamrolling towards either Rick Malloy or Phillip Kennedy? Or will Mitchell trip up the cocky youngster and prove that it was outside forces that stopped him at Homecoming? Returns and Debuts, Oh My! "The Ace" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. Saint Jadian Bridden is back – but with the Dead Man's Hand crumbling, the bigger question is whose side the Mexican Minx and the Ace in the Hole are on! We may find out as Jadian returns to the ring for the first time since losing the LiveWire Championship to Legion! His opponent is the man called Saint, whose presence in Sin City comes nearly entirely without fanfare! No one knows who Saint is, but he'll put what we're told is an impressive repertoire of wrestling acumen up against the high-flying Bridden!   All this, plus: With the Dome of Doom match starting to take shape, what other people will want to join Aimz and Phillip Kennedy inside? Is it possible that Jared Sykes and King Blueberry ARE different people? Can anyone stop DNA from claiming the SIN titles and putting the Spider in the Dome? And what will Wyatt Connors think of next? Tune in live on Cinemax for TEMPTATION! SCCW Temptation: A Whole New World! Two Weeks Away! From Arena Monterrey in Monterrey, Mexico! * Card subject to change For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! DNA (Desade & Aimz) Vs. Los Luchadores (En Fuego y El Diablo Verde) © SCCW Temptation! Three Weeks Away! From the Tinsley Coliseum on the New Mexico State Fairgrounds! For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Spacely (w/Quasar) Vs. "The Most Interesting Man in the World" Rick Malloy © SCCW SIN ON SPIKE 3: THE DOME OF DOOM! This July! From the Arena-Auditorium in Laramie, Wyoming! MAIN EVENT – THE DOME OF DOOM! For the SCCW Universal Championship! Aimz © Vs. "The Las Vegas Lariat" Phillip Kennedy (SCCW Gateway Champion) Vs. "The Most Interesting Man in the World" Rick Malloy* (SCCW LiveWire Champion) Vs. Los Luchadores* (SCCW SIN Champions) Vs. Jared Sykes or Lance Marshall Vs. Others to be added! * Denotes pending successful title defenses during the arc.
