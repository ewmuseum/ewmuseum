The French Are Coming...

6 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier this year, FUSE saw the debut of a number of stars coming in from Global, nearly all of them Champions in that company at the time of their FUSE debuts. Those being, of course, Eryc Morgan (now known as Corvus) who was World Champion and one half of the tag team champions, Joshua Kosidlo, who was one half of the tag team champions, and Joaquin Pierre, who was at the time both International and Platinum Champion. Though immediately stripped of his two championships upon the announcement of his singing to FUSE and his first match with Joshua Kosidlo, Pierre agreed to return to Global to finish up his commitments, which ran out on Halloween night with Global's Dark Rituals Pay-Per-View. As such, he is free to return to FUSE, and will make his second appearance for the company at this coming Uproar. Pierre was reached by telephone to ask his thoughts on Timo Bolamba and being back in FUSE. "Obviously, 'zis is a new beginning for me," the French fighter said in his heavily accented English. "I accomplished some great things in Global, some things 'zat I am very proud of 'eended. But 'ze time 'ad come for me to make my exit from 'ze company. I was not 'appy with 'ow things were going, and I needed to make a new start." When asked if he had any regrets about dropping his dual titles to come to FUSE, Pierre hesitated a moment before replying. "Non, I still feel it was 'ze best decision I could 'ave made. I returned to Global due to a loophole in 'ze contract I 'ad with 'zem. Monsieurs Kosidlo, Morgan, and Noreault were not subject to 'zis loophole. Given a choice, I would 'ave remained in FUSE." He was asked about his upcoming match with Timo Bolamba, and what his strategy would be coming into the match. "My strategy is going to be 'ze same it always is. I am going to come into 'ze ring, kick somebody in 'ze 'ead, beat somebody down, submit somebody, whatever I 'ave to do to 'ave my 'and raised in victory. I 'ave no problem with Monsier Bolamba, 'e is merely a stepping stone to me accomplishing what I want to do in FUSE." In closing, he was asked about his future goals in FUSE, and whether or not he had championships designs, and which wrestlers he would like to lock horns with. "Anybody and everybody. As far as championships go, 'zey are 'ze goal of all fighters, of course I wish to chase one. But I need to prove myself first, and I am looking forward to 'ze chance to do so." Obviously the French martial artist is excited about his upcoming days, weeks, months, and years in FUSE, and is very determined to do himself well. We wish him the best in his future FUSE related endeavors.
