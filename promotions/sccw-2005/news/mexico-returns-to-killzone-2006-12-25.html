Mexico Returns To Killzone

25 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After being carted off to jail two weeks ago, at Killzone 22, Ron Mexico will make his Killzone return this week in tag team action. Jorge Samuelsson will team up with Ron Mexico to take on Kris CarMichael and Mike Wade. Wade will be making his second venture into a FUSE ring, having picked up a victory in his debut last week over Domino, and Samuelsson will be looking to do his usual. Destory things. For CarMichael and Mexico this match is much more personal, however, as the two have waged war on one another for the better part of the last month or so here in FUSE. Which one of these bitter rivals is going to walk out of Killzone with a victory? Tune into Killzone 24 on December 27th to find out.
