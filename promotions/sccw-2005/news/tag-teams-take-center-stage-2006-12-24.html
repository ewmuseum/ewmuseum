Tag Teams Take Center Stage

24 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killzone 24 is going to be a wild and exciting night, as all Killzone's are, and this time around it will be the tag teams taking center stage. Along with the match up that pits CarMichael and Wade against Mexico and Samuelsson, mentioned earlier, the main event will see five of FUSE's actual tag teams in action to crown the third team to take place in the triple threat tag team championship match at Killzone 25, along with Heavy Metal and Critical Error. The teams of Aimz/Crisis, The Amazing Technicolor Dream Team, and The Travellers all make their FUSE in-ring debuts this week along side Generation Gap and God's Own Drunks, who made their debuts last week in a match against one another. Generation Gap comes into this main event with some momentum as they got off the right foot in that match up with God's Own Drunks, coming away with an impressive victory. Will that give them an edge in this tag team slug fest that has a shot at FUSE gold on the line to the winner? As always tune into Killzone this Wednesday night to find the answer to this question, and others, as FUSE continues on it's roll toward Revolution.
