Winnipeg Lineup Announced!

10 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling – in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Final Curtain Entertainment – proudly bring to you... TEMPTATION: WILD, WILD WINNIPEG! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, January 17, 2010 from the MTS Centre in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada and ONLY on Cinemax! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Ace in the Hole" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes © This is not the first time that these two have met, nor is it likely to be the last, but after the Canadian Chaos, after Jadian and Julissa threw in their mark on the side of Lane Stevens, it seems that the LiveWire Champion is being rewarded here, despite not having four defenses of his championship under his belt! Now Jared Sykes, fresh off a disappointing finish to Ottawa's battle royal, seeks to defend his championship against one of Stevens' cronies... and you know the River Rat won't be far behind! Jared may have a history of success over Jadian, but will the streak end in Ottawa? One-on-One Debuts! Jacob McKail Vs. "Gold Patron Meritorious" Xavier Kannon (w/Eleanor Kannon-Hall) Jacob McKail and Xavier Kannon made their debut last week in the Canadian Chaos, and this week, they'll go one-on-one for the first singles matches of their Sin City careers in 2010! McKail, a former Gateway Champion, drew a short straw when he got embroiled in the Kennedy-Rhine war, and he looks to make good against the man likely to say HE deserves to be facing Wyatt Connors in Edmonton, the man who pinned him, the King of Wrestling! It's sure to be a technical clinic between two of the best wrestlers in the business! You're Not Going To Believe This! "The Red Raver" Aimz Vs. "The Puppetmaster" James von Doom Yes, you read that right! After her recent ousting from the GTT at the hands of Garbage Bag Johnny, the former Superversal Champion will wrestle in her home country for the first time in over a year! And her unfortunate opponent might not be that unfortunate – James von Doom took Xavier Kannon AND Wyatt Connors to their limit in Ottawa – is this the moment where he finally gets over the hump? A Return! Saul Goode & Ben Chillin Vs. Spacely & Baron von Blackberry Are there any waves to surf in the Great White North? Saul Goode and Ben Chillin make their return to SCCW to face... the strangest team since the last time we put something like this! The Ant-Sized Astronaut and the Devil Fruit, two of the wackiest people in all of Sin City, will face off with the surfing tandem whose stay in Sin City made waves the last time! Some action on a lighter note on a cold January night! Handicapped Match! Daddy Daughter Day (April & August Monday) Vs. "The Devil's Reject" Legion (w/Luci4 & Stigmata) Legion asked for it, and now he got it – Nigel Kensington has booked the Devil's Reject into the most unenviable of handicapped matches – against the SCCW Strength in Numbers Champions! The Mondays will come roaring into Winnepeg with the titles that they won by defeating one-quarter of the GTT's Elite Eight! Does Legion stand a chance, or will he come down with a case of the Mondays? Plus more fallout from the shocking actions! And we're going to try to get some words with "The Valkyrie" Suzanne Larsson and Terrence Kingsley, SCCW's newest imports! All this and more only in Winnepeg! SCCW Temptation! Two Weeks Away! From Rexall Place in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Universal Championship! "The River Rat" Lane Stevens (w/Regan Guest & Katsidy) Vs. "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors (w/Deacon Dale & TJ Ratigan) © SCCW DEFIANCE 3! From the Stan Sheriff Arena in Honolulu, Hawaii! Coming Soon to Pay-Per-View! Featuring the inaugural inductions into the SCCW Hall of Fame!
