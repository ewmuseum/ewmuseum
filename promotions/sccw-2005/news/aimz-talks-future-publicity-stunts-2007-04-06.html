Aimz Talks Future & Publicity Stunts

6 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier today, Aimz called the head office of FUSE on behalf of herself and, her reluctant partner, Darcy Crisis. The topic of discussion was her personal upset over recent 'headlines' on the long-dead AWC website. ''It's absoluetly disrespectful to FUSE and to the work Darcy and I have done here. As far as our 'AWC peers' go, people are fired or leave for good reasons and I'm sure those situations, mentioned on the site, had theirs... but I think it's a pathetic attempt at regaining some sort of controversy, and flat-out insulting to put so-called news stories out there saying that we were some sort of big deal in AWC, but we're nothing in FUSE. We're elsewhere now, and I can personally attest to the fact that we're happy with the company overall, though Darcy's had some problems with fan reception and adjusting to a new climate. Weeks after we came in, we had the top team titles here. We ARE NOT singles wrestlers at this point, so of course we're not contending for singles titles. Darcy's comments have absolutely nothing to do with AWC, and to say otherwise would be senselessly whoring for publicity on the part of the staffers who abandoned us. If we all meant money and merchandise sales, AWC wouldn't have flopped. Period.'' She continued, ''And that's the last you'll hear about it from our side. I had respect for AWC's legacy up until I was pointed to their website again this morning. It's a dead federation, and they're just spitting on the names of their former breadwinners by putting out trashy stories like that, trying to undermine our work and achievements since they abruptly closed their doors and left us, for lack of a better term, without a fucking leg to stand on.'' ''It sickens me that our former home would try so hard to diminish our success in FUSE just because of some silly interfederation beef. I'll also note that nobody poached or 'stole' from anybody else and that I've recieved official word that AWC will NOT ever be re-opening. We're willing to do a reunion show simply out of respect for our former peers, but we wish nothing more to do with this story or the claims made on the part of a desperate and fallen federation.'' And what of their upcoming matches in FUSE? ''The rumor mill's saying we're facing God's Own Drunks on the supercard, though we don't have official word on that yet. To be honest, it's a bit of a let-down given the fact that we've already pounded them numerous times - but they deserve their second chance. In the near future, look for Darcy and I to be seriously adding fire to the Fatal Faction division. At least ONE federation around here is going to have a stacked tag division, and it's gonna be FUSE.
