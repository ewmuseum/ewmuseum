Afflicted Glory Airs!

31 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The latest FUSE Pay-Per-View, Afflicted Glory, is on the air. There is plenty of great action on tap as every title, including Gods Title, are on the line. Who walked away as the Fatal Faction Champions, The Alliance, the current champs, or did Darcy Crisis and his woman Aimz manage to regain the gold they lost about a month or so ago? Inside The Asylum Kid Cool and Jonathan Rhine waged war for the vacant Custom Chaos Title. Who walked away with their first FUSE gold? As mentioned Hoyt Williams put his championship from God on the line against Jacob McKail's father, and reported leader of the KKK, in a match that you had to see to believe. Once again Lane Stevens stepped into the ring with Cyrus Raynes with his LiveWire title on the line. Would the outcome be different this time? Don't forget that Affliction members, The Legacy, put their Fatal Affliction championships on the line in a brutal ladder match against the Dynamic Duo of Fred Fusion and Ethan Frost. Who walked away with the last laugh in this long standing feud? Also in one of the most heated feuds in all of FUSE history, three men who really just hate one another stepped into the ring to write another chapter in their bloody war. A triple threat between Tanaka, Timo, and Gibson that was as hard fought as any match in a while. Who walked away with bragging rights and will this be the final chapter in their hatred for one another? In other tag team action the red hot duo, The Cowboys From Hell, took on The RPG. The young video game loving, wierdo, brother duo are no doubt the underdogs coming into this match with the pissed off rednecks, but who walked away with victory. Then in the Main Event the world witnessed one of the most intense, physical, and demanding Universal Title matches in FUSE history. John Covel, the champion, put his title on the line against rivals Jacob McKail and Clinton Sage. Could Covel hold off the push from the two men who are looking to add their names to the very short list of men who've held the Universal Strap twice? (The other two being only Damien Cruz and John Covel) It was an ending to shocking to believe. Tune in and find out how all the action went down. Afflicted Glory
