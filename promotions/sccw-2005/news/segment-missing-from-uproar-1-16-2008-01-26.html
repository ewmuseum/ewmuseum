Segment missing from Uproar 1-16

26 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is Flux here! I know we've already had a show since then, but some of the guys in the production truck finally polished off this baby and sent it to me. It was a part of the show we didn't get to see, so now it's airing here! And here’s Ethan Peters. He’s our frumpy but loveable backstage interviewer. Mary and Jon get all of the love, because they actually know a little about wrestling, besides video games. But here stands Peters, as excited as we’ve ever seen him, foaming at the mouth as he stares at the camera. Ethan Peters: Ladies and Gentlemen, I have the awesome assignment of being the first FUSE staff to interview, The Man In the Black Hat… “BOOOOO-RAAAAAAAH!” Ethan Peters:…A man whom I played with as a kid, on numerous videogames and action figures, a man who had the most wicked finisher in the game, back in 1996. The man making his FUSE debut, right here, right now, with me! Ethan shudders a little, it’s a little concerning because it looks slightly orgasmic, but we digress. Peters: Ladies & Gentlemen, wrestling world, and Mom make sure you’re recording this at home, please welcome, the one and the only, making his first TV appearance in over five years, “The Foundation”, Mr. Tony Pride! “BOOOOOOOOOO-RAAAAAAAAAAH!” The camera widens and entering the camera shot next to Ethan, is “The Foundation”, Tony Pride. Time as indeed aged him, gone is the platinum blonde hair, now replaced with salt and pepper, with silver streaks. Gone is the athletic body, instead replaced with a thicker and more portly build, still muscle bound but not the man we knew. Yet still there, are those piercing blue steels eyes, they stare into the camera, he stands in a tailor made Armani suit, looking as only the “Foundation” can, and smiles. Peters: Mr. Pride, I can’t tell you what this moment means to me, I mena you’re here, live and in person, the former 10 Time World Champion, you’ve held over 68 major championships, headlined PPV’s, made records, broken records, but now you’re here in FUSE, and you came as the Man in The Black Hat, and Kansas Marie is your daughter, and Prince, your son, and Jason… Pride slowly moves the mic towards his mouth, cutting Ethan off and smiles. Tony Pride: Thanks kid, I appreciate the introduction, and I promise all your questions will be answered. Pride nervously chuckles. Tony Pride: Just not tonight. “BOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” Tony Pride: I have my reasons for being here, I have my reasons for being The Man in The Black Hat. I have a lot of vested interest in some things happening in FUSE, mainly some people in FUSE, namely my children, my children by blood and otherwise. Ethan Peters: You must be making reference to Jason Cruise, whom you helped train… Tony Pride:..I didn’t “help” train him, my partner at the school was no help, hell I taught him everything he knows. Pride smiles. Tony Pride: I single handedly shaped him into the champion he is today, I’m the Foundation, I’m Tony Pride, I attract championships like a hooker attracts internet geeks. Pride looks up and down at Peters. Tony Pride: Much like my children, Jason, is forever in my debt, and I’ve decided that now, is everyone’s time to pay. Ethan Peters: Speaking of payment, rumor is tonight, that Dave Gibson, a fellow ring veteran friend of yours, is looking for answers after last weeks tag match. He still owes him the 5 minutes from their strap match at DEFIANCE, your thoughts? /> Pride pauses for a moment Tony Pride: I raised my son to be a man, and one of the many things I taught him, is that you’ve got to pay what you owe. Pride chuckles Tony Pride: And tonight, I make a promise to Dave Gibson and the rest of the wrestling world, that the Pride family will pay what they owe, tonight, and I personally, guarantee that! Ethan Peters: But, but, your son supposedly hates you, he hates the business, he hates everything you stand for, rumor is he’s tried to hire someone, to kill you?! Your son hates you and hate… Tony Pride:..Hate is powerful kid, but hate is a Pride family value, and nobody can truly hate me, how could you? Pride nervously chuckles Tony Pride: As far as killing me? Pride shrugs Tony Pride: Ask around, I’m as close to wrestling immortality as one could get, not to mention the most important factor, which is, I’m the Foundation… Pride nervously chuckles and stops. Tony Pride:…I’m Tony Pride. And by the time this night is through, everyone in this building, will tip their hat, to me. Pride smiles as he twirls a black derby style hat onto his finger and places it on his head as he walks away and we fade. ------------- Really exciting stuff! Tony Pride is here, and that means good things for the FUSE as a business, but bad things for the people in it. Watch Tony team up with his son to take on Jason Cruise and NWC Legend Eugene "High Society" Robinson at FUSE on ESPN next week!
