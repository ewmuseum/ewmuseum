Kosidlo and Noreault not over yet

25 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since debuting in FUSE, Joshua Kosidlo has been tearing through the competition. With one draw, one loss and nothing else but wins under his belt The Paladin has earned a LiveWire title shot. Two weeks ago on Uproar, Joshua Kosidlo and Jason O’Neil pinned Carson Riley and Itachi at the same time. Mr. Duluth would then name both individuals the Number One contender to the LiveWire title currently held by Jason Cruise. This past week on Uproar, Joshua Kosidlo came down to the ring to a decent fanfare and provided the fans of FUSE a look into his heart. The Paladin’s mission statement was made, and his rules were clearly defined. The Wrestling Purist would even go on to discuss how much he respected both Jason O’Neil and Jason Cruise. However, Kosidlo’s heart to heart with our fans would shortly be interrupted by a still bitter Jacque Noreault. Since their mutual debuts in FUSE, the former Global stars have reignited a rivalry that at one time nearly tore two separate promotions apart. During Kosidlo’s monologue Jacque Noreault entered the arena, and proceeded to mock and taunt The Paladin from the stage. Noreault has been vocally bitter since their match at FUSE on ESPN where The Paladin defeated him three falls to two, via count out. Noreault once again laid down the challenge to Kosidlo, still claiming to have not been “defeated” by the Wrestling Purist. The challenge would be for a special kind of match in which only submissions could decide the winner. There would be no rope breaks, no disqualifications, no count outs and no pin falls. The only way to win, make your opponent tap like a little girl. In Noreault’s mind, this is the one way to prove he is better then Kosidlo. At the time this article was written, Joshua Kosidlo was unavailable for comments, however we were able to catch up with Mr. Noreault. Jacque had this to say; “Kosidlo doesn’t know what he’s getting into. Instead of spending so much time worrying about Jason Cruise or Jason O’Neil he should be worrying about me. I’m a lot more dangerous then either of them, and years down the line when people talk about the dawning of the Uproar era in FUSE, they will talk about the greatest wrestler that ever lived. It won’t be Jason O’Neil, it won’t be Jonathan Rhine, Jason Cruise or that red-haired hooker Aimz, and most certainly it will not be Joshua Kosidlo. It’s going to be me, ‘The Canadian Dream’ Jacque Noreault. The sooner they get that through their heads, the better it’s going to be for everyone. Kosidlo is just the beginning.”
