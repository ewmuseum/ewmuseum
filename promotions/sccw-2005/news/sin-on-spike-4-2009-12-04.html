SIN on SPIKE 4!

4 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Final Curtain Entertainment) proudly brings to you... SCCW SIN on SPIKE 4: THE THROWDOWN IN MOTOWN! L-I-V-E LIVE from Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan! Sunday, December 13, 2009 at 9PM Eastern/8PM Central and ONLY on SPIKETV! MAIN EVENT! WAR GAMES! Team Aeterno (Craig Maloof, Alias, Mary-Lynn Mayweather, and Cozen) Vs. Team SCCW (Aimz, SCCW Universal Champion "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors, Lane Stevens & TBA) They are perhaps the most controversial group of new arrivals in SCCW's history – the man formerly known as Dusk has assembled an assortment of men and women from some of the top federations in an effort to, once and for all, see SCCW have the law and order that he believes it has been lacking! But is it law and order Maloof's way or the highway? His alliance with the former PRIME Champion in Cozen and her impersonation of Desade have enraged Amy Campbell – to the point that she is willing to ally with two people she isn't fond of – the man who stole her title and the man who ruined her reputation – for a chance to get at them all! But with Desade specifically banned from competing for Team SCCW, can Aimz even find a fourth in time for the match, and if she does, can they work together to compete with the kind of talent that Aeterno brings? It's a war to settle the score... and it'll be live on basic cable! For the SCCW Gateway Championship! Special Guest Referee: Baron von Blackberry! Spacely (w/MacKenzie Malone) Vs. "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes © Two men who used to be friends have now become foes, as Spacely – the only man with a pinfall victory over Desade in an SCCW ring – has taken issue with the Gateway Champion's friendship with Amy Campbell! Two weeks in a row now, the Ant-Sized Astronaut and the Black Sheep have devolved into fisticuffs that none of SCCW's referees has been able to control! So we've gone all the way to the depths of Fruitsylvania for a man to keep control over the action! But can even the Devil Fruit keep order as Spacely and Sykes compete for what many consider a cursed title? For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Special eight-minute time limit! Julissa Minorez banned from Detroit! Mitchell Quinlan Vs. Jadian Bridden © For weeks, Mitchell Quinlan and Jadian Bridden have been playing a game of cat-and-mouse! And now – with stipulations neither man agreed to – they will finally go one-on-one for Jadian's belt! With the first LiveWire Champion banned from the city, can Mitchell Quinlan beat the King of the Wire in just eight short minutes? Or do the Jay and Juli have another trick up their sleeve? Quinlan has been almost unstoppable, and Bridden tries to stake his claim as the greatest LW champ of all time! The fastest eight minutes in wrestling in Detroit! For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! Daddy Daughter Day (April & August Monday) Vs. Desade & Lance Marshall © One chance. One more opportunity. After being screwed in their efforts to become SCCW's Strength in Numbers Champions in their hometown, April and August Monday have one more chance against the most dysfunctional tag-team in all of the PTC! The Spider and The Lion have their differences – to say the least! Can they find any common ground and keep their championships, or will the Mondays finally climb to the top of the mountain? If they can't, they go all the way back to the bottom! One more chance! Falls Count Anywhere Within Ten Miles of The Arena! Legion Vs. James von Doom Their war has gone through several federations and their match a few weeks ago on Temptation (despite Eugene Ware's disdain for it) solved precisely nothing as far as Legion was concerned! Now, the Devil's Reject and the Puppetmaster will go one-on-one and the whole arena can't stop them! Will von Doom's new running buddies, Kaden Calix and Rick Malloy, make an appearance? And if they do, what will Legion do, as no one has seen Luci4 in weeks! Find out in Detroit! Plus Phillip Kennedy has a Challenge for Jonathan Rhine! Desade has called a meeting of the Dead Man's Hand! And – who is Final Curtain Entertainment? ALL THIS LIVE FROM THE JOE!
