Official SCCW Statement Regarding Nigel Kensington's Injury!

22 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To: [detail withheld] From: [detail withheld] Subject: Portland Temptation (Billings Temptation spoilers!) This is the section of the site where we usually post the lineup for next week's show, but... in the chaos surrounding Lane Stevens' attack of Nigel Kensington at the end of last week's show in Montana, there's not a lot we can say definitively! Here's what we do know: - As announced last week, our main event will feature the SCCW Strength in Numbers Champions, "The Lion" Lance Marshall and Desade, defending their championships in a gauntlet match against the best that SCCW has to offer! See Daddy Daughter Day, Saul Goode and Ben Chillin, former SIN Champions Los Luchadores and more attempt to wrest the titles away from the oddest of odd couples! - We have been told that the 9/1 Temptation broadcast will feature five matches, but we're not sure who booked them or why – or even what they are! The superstars of SCCW will be flying blind as they walk into the Beaver State! Who will we see in action? Tune in to Cinemax to find out! - After a disturbingly quiet week, we have been told that SCCW Universal Champion Amy "Aimz" Campbell will answer the challenge laid out by now former PTC Elite Champion "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors. Will Amy accept the challenge for her coveted championship at SCCW Equilibrium at the end of September in Las Vegas? - All this and more – including what, if any, repercussions Lane Stevens will face from the attack on Dr Kensington and who's in charge with this show this week anyway, live, Tuesday, September 1, 2009 at 10 PM Eastern (7 PM Pacific) from the Rose Garden in Portland!
