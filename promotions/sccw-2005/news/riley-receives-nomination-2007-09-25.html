Riley Receives Nomination

25 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUSE star and former Hollywood stunt man "One Take" Carson Riley has received accolades for his work on set prior to joining FUSE Wrestling. Riley has received a TAURUS Award nomination for "Best Work With a Vehicle" for his performance in Bourne Ultimatum. The TAURUS Awards honor the best stunts of the year in a number of categories. The nomination is Riley's third in the past four years. Bourne Ultimatum has grossed in excess of $70 million in the box office this year. Riley will look to further translate his success into the squared circle as he takes on established veteran El Diablo at the next edition of Uproar.
