SCCW Temptation is up and the lineup for 3/17/08!

11 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Temptation is on the air, ladies and gentlemen! Click that link up there and enjoy the awesomeness that we spew forth like vomit. Or something. Anyway, rolling right along... From the Desk of Smitty T. Duluth For Immediate Release Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) Proudly Brings to You: TEMPTATION! Monday, March 17, 2008 at 10PM Eastern and ONLY on Cinemax! (Contact Your Local Cable Provider to Order Today!) From the HP Pavilion at San Jose in San Jose, California! A St. Patrick's Day Extravaganza, Sin City Style! The Last Stop Before SCCW Snake Eyes!!! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! History Rears Its Ugly Head! Aimz Vs. Gideon Fontaine They've been friends. They've been enemies. They have friends in common. What I'm saying is -- these two have history. Now, just before Snake Eyes, that history repeats itself as Amy Campbell, two weeks removed from one of the most shocking moments in SCCW history, seeks to keep her momentum rolling into the pay-per-view as she faces one of the members of the Black List, the new Strength In Numbers Champions! Will Aimz be looking past Gideon toward a possible match-up with the SCCW Universal Champion in San Diego -- and do you really think Gideon will stand for that? What is the condition of the Black List member's leg after the stabbing he suffered at FUSE on ESPN? Will he even be able to wrestle this match? NOT A Handicapped Match - Promise! Mayhem and "Peerless" Hunter Sabuani (w/various members of Magnum Opus & The Dead Man's Hand, no doubt) Vs. BloodAngel and Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) A long time ago, Mayhem powerbombed Hunter Sabuani's ex-wife through a table. Boohoo, life's tough, wear a cup. Now, a week after Sabuani's match with his regular tag-partner Mr. Ashe against BloodAngel and Lance Marshall broke down into chaos, the Raja of Roguishness will team with the Path of Destruction to take on the unlikely team of the plucky young Jadian Bridden and the crusty old Edward Lambert! With Mayhem and Bridden scheduled to go toe-to-toe in a cage at Snake Eyes, can the Maestro keep his man focused on the task at hand, or will Mayhem club Hunter Sabuani to death? Tag Team Partners of DOOM Match! "The Biloxi Beast" Bishop and "The Obsidian Nightmare" Corvus Vs. Cyrus "Mongo" Raynes and "The Frenchman" Joaquin Pierre Last week on Temptation, Bishop attempted to show Malachi that he didn't deserve his spot, only for Cyrus Raynes to stand in his way! Now Bishop will team with the AWC LiveWire Champion to face Cyrus and Corvus' long-time nemesis, the brand-new SCCW Gateway Champion, Joaquin Pierre! Pierre and Corvus have been going back and forth for weeks, and the Obsidian Nightmare has made no secret of his desire to upend Pierre and take the seemingly cursed Gateway Title! Can the tensions in this match possibly be contained in the ring? We don't think so, but we're gonna try anyway -- it's kind of what we do. Two Big Guys Hit Each Other Hard, Redux! "The Demon" Vassago Vs. Lance Marshall (w/Alanna Marshall) One week after debuting in Seattle as BloodAngel's super-secret mystery partner, the former NWC World Heavyweight Champion (now 100% exclusive to SCCW, full-time!) gets his first one-on-one test with a man who might just be as big as he is, the undefeated Vassago, fresh off a countout victory over both John Adams and Jadian Bridden! After Marshall went heads-up with the Path of Destruction at the end of his match with the Dead Man's Hand, you know he'll be itching for a fight, but the Demon's likely to give him one. No Rest for the Wicked! "WyldChild" Zakk Wylde Vs. Andre Jensen Now that the Black List has claimed the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships, you'd think that Zakk Wylde would want a week off. "Hell, no!" he told me! He wanted someone to fight! He'll face America's Favorite Terrorist, Andre Jensen in San Jose -- one has to wonder if an asshole like Jensen has any friends that might provide him some backup against the rest of the Black List! Who Is This Strange Guy? RYUJIN Vs. The True Prophet Last week, we saw the True Prophet promise death and destruction as part of the wages of sin... well, we saw a lot of that, before the so-called Seattle Curse ate his tape. (BTW - real sorry about that.) Now, the mysterious newcomer will step into an SCCW ring for the first time to take on the Black Dragon, who isn't about to lay down for some new guy, right? PLUS! * We'll run down the FULL CARD for SCCW Snake Eyes for the first time in San Jose! * Jonathan Rhine defeated Christian Darke to retain the SCCW Universal Championship -- using his old friend Aimz' own maneuver to do it! The New Life is scheduled to be in San Jose... how long before these two come to blows? * Desade's given Yours Truly, Smitty T. Duluth, until bell-time in the Sabuani/Mayhem vs. Bridden/BloodAngel match to cancel it or someone close to me is going to the hospital! I'm quaking in my nonexistent boots! * The Black List is the new Strength in Numbers Champions! What does this mean for their lawsuit? * We met the girlfriend(?) of the SCCW LiveWire Champion, Elliott Rollins, last week! Will Hannah Rose be in San Jose when her boyfriend(?) learns who his PPV challenger (or challengers? Would I do that?) and what does MacKenzie Malone have on the girl? * What will Christian Darke's reaction be to his loss against Jonathan Rhine? The Lord of the Asylum speaks out on Temptation! * All this, plus the return of Andre Jensen's Money Talks, and so much more, just days away from our dynamic return to pay-per-view on the St. Patrick's Day edition of SCCW Temptation! Only on Cinemax!   SCCW SNAKE EYES! LIVE IN JUST THREE WEEKS! From the Miramar Marine Corps Air Station in San Diego! Announced Thus Far: A Bloodbath Steel Cage Bout: Mayhem (w/Magnum Opus) Vs. Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez)
