Connors' First Elite Title Defense!

19 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dateline: Halifax, Nova Scotia -- November 15, 2008. by Deanna Everett On the heels of Sin City Championship Wrestling's "Temptation: Homeward Bound" card at the Halifax Metro Centre, officials have announced new Elite Champion, "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors' first defense of the title he won at NFW SUPERCRASH II against Steve Knox. Connors has been signed to wrestle two-time (and reigning) SCCW Universal Champion Amy "Aimz" Campbell at next month's "Defiance II" pay-per-view event, emanating from the Nassau Coliseum in Uniondale, New York. Campbell has spent 143 days of 2008 as the SCCW Champion, and Sin City Chairman Dr. Nigel Kensington was quick to point out that his champion's scheduled title defense against "The Lion" Lance Marshall is not being postponed (the two are scheduled to sign the contract for their showdown when SCCW returns to Cinemax from the sold-out Blue Cross Arena in Rochester). "Miss Campbell is our champion and I have no doubt she will rise to the occasion against the top competition in the City," Kensington said at the press conference. "The best rise to the challenge." Rumors of Connors being scheduled to face the four-time World Champion, Edward "BloodAngel" Lambert on the show could not be confirmed at press-time. While neither Connors nor Campbell attended the announcement, they have not been strangers during their tenures in SCCW, with Aimz assaulting the False Prophet for information in the past and (more recently) Connors attempting to drug the Red Raver. "There's not a lot of love lost there, and the crowd on Long Island may have a hard time cheering," veteran play-by-play man "Mr. Old School" Dave Gibson said. "But I fully expect Wyatt and Amy to put on one hell of an intriguing match on one hell of a card."
