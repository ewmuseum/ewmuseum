The segment that never aired

20 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Technical difficulties plagued Uproar last Saturday night, and FUSE's Matthew Randall was none too pleased. He brought us this video earlier this morning, the spot that never aired... We head to the back where the paramedics are wheeling the battered and bloodied Dave Gibson back to an awaiting ambulance. His neck is in a brace and they have an oxygen mask on him. There is almost no motion coming from the FUSE Color Commentator. Walking along with him is Matthew Randall, who is talking to Gibson. As soon as the paramedics reach the ambulance, they load Gibson in. Just before the doors are closed, Dave gives Matthew a thumbs up. Randall: Go, get out of here. As the ambulance pulls away, Matthew turns around, shaking his head and with a concerned look on his face. Some of Gibson's blood was on his shirt and hands, thanks to the brutal mauling that Gibson had received from Prince Pride. Walking up to Matthew with a mic in her hand is Mary Jackson. Jackson: Matthew Randall, what where you doing out there? Matthew tries to walk away, but Mary keeps up with the youngster from Kentwood. Randall: You know what, Prince. You're full of shit. The crowd is stunned. Matthew, one of the few wrestlers that tries to hold back his language, just let it go. Jackson: Matthew, what do you mean by that? Randall: Here's Prince Pride, our "savior". Well, week after week, he comes out and thinks that the wrestling world owes him something. He wants to destroy what many of us have come into whole heartedly because he had a lousy father. That's bullshit. Again, the crowd is stunned by a profanity being used by the usually well mannered young man from Michigan's Left Coast. Randall: You know, I'm just frustrated, because with Prince, we all see someone with god given talent throw it all away because he didn't have the World's Greatest Dad. Matthew runs his hands though his hair to get a few strands out from in front of his face. Randall: Prince, that chip on your shoulder, it's going to be your downfall. Last week, you took your frustrations out on a referee. This week, you nearly killed an announcer. I want you to look me in the eyes. If you want respect you're going to have to earn it. Are you getting a good look, Prince? With God, and the rest of the wrestling world, as my witness, you name the time and you name the place. Matthew stares very coldly into the camera, taking the mic from Mary Jackson's petite hand. Randall: If you really want someone to whip, I challenge you to try and whip me. Matthew drops the mic and walks away from the camera's range, leaving a stunned Mary Jackson in the background.
