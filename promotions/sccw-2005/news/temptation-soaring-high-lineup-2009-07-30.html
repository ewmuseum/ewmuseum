Temptation: Soaring High Lineup!

30 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION: SOARING HIGH! L-I-V-E LIVE Thursday, August 6, 2009 from the Clune Arena on the campus of the United States Air Force Academy in Colorado Springs, CO! MAIN EVENT Singles Action! "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes Vs. "The River Rat" Lane Stevens It was a matter of inches that kept Jared Sykes from advancing to his hometown for PRIME's Colossus VI and a date with Hessian and Desade! Instead, he's going to the Air Force Academy to do battle with Alexandra Pierce's Warlord! What frame of mind will Jared be in, facing the man who put Dusk down for ten seconds? What kind of condition is the River Rat in? And what's next for both of these men, as we creep towards our return to pay-per-view? Stevens and Sykes will put on a clinic in Colorado Springs – can Jared get back on the winning ways? Champion's Showcase Match A Non-Title Match! Special Referee: SCCW Universal Champion Amy Campbell "Simply Better" Xander Searle (SCCW LiveWire Champion) Vs. Spacely (SCCW Gateway Champion) It'll be the third Champion's Showcase match in SCCW history, showing off two of the men who survived the Dome of Doom with new belts in hand! Xander Searle, who wasn't even scheduled to be a part of the match, is the only man who never took a fall during the war that took place inside the demonic structure! He'll get another shot at the big-time as he faces the NEW Gateway Champion, the only man who has ever pinned the Spider in the Web, the Ant-Sized Astronaut! And to make things more interesting, Aimz – STILL our Universal Champion – will put on the pinstripes to serve as our impartial official! Special Challenge Match! "The Raging Fear" August Monday (w/April Monday) Vs. "The Lost Soul" Dusk At SIN on SPIKE, August Monday and his daughter served as peacekeepers for a night – and one of their biggest challenges was acting as the replacement officials during Dusk's bid to be the Last Man Standing against Lane Stevens! In the end, the Mondays had to put the Lost Soul's shoulders down to the mat, and rumor has it Dusk's been on a rampage ever since! Now the new Infinite Gauntlet holder, August Monday, will climb into the ring with Dusk to prove himself in the interest of spirited competition! Will Dusk slap it away? Find out on Cinemax! Tag-Team Action! Baron von Blackberry & Sultan Strawberry Vs. Saul Goode & Ben Chillin To be fair, any two of the Fruit Salad are a rare and special kind of eccentric, but the Smoothie Criminal and the Devil Fruit are... well, kinda six levels of crazypants. They're on the same side (we think) and they'll be an interesting pair to be sure (we think). Their opponents are newcomers to SCCW, a pair of surfer dudes who'll cruise up into the mountains of Colorado to face two guys dressed like fruits, and we promise they're not just on the ganja! The Return of the Reject! Misfit Vs. Legion (w/Luci4 & Lazarus) After a few weeks away from the ring, the Devil's Reject returns to action at the Air Force Academy! What has Legion learned from the man who, along with Desade, trained so many members of the former Dead Man's Hand? And in what condition is Misfit, who hasn't been seen on Sin City television since he was rescued from Wyatt Connors by Lance Marshall? Legion returns – but what is he out for now? All this, plus words from Jonathan Rhine and the one-year celebration for Aimz' Universal Title Reign! SCCW Temptation! Two Weeks Away! From the Rimrock Auto Arena in Billings, Montana! See the return of "The New Life" in action in Billings!
