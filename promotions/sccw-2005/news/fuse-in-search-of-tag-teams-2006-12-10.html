FUSE in search of Tag Teams

10 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Due to the huge influx of talent as of late we’ve been informed by FUSE management that there are a very limited number of spots left to be claimed on the singles roster. This is a good thing for FUSE from their singles roster stand point but the federation now is in search of something else. Tag teams. With the newly formed Fatal Faction (FUSE’s version of tag teams) division the Primetime Central affiliated federation has begun an intense search for the quality tag teams of the world. So if you’re a tag team, you believe you’re the best there is in tag team wrestling, and you’d like to prove it while being part of one of the most competitive, and fast growing, federations in all of Primetime Central then send in your application. FUSE is currently looking for teams of all skill levels who want to compete and prove that they have the heart, determination, and desire to be one of the best tag teams in the entire world. Do you have what it takes to headline Killzone shows? To win the Fatal Faction championships? To defend them against all challengers? Only one way to find out. Join the team.
