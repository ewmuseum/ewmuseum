Fairgrounds Line-Up Released!

4 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION AT THE FAIRGROUNDS! L-I-V-E LIVE Tuesday, July 14, 2009 from the New Mexico State Fairgrounds in Albuquerque, New Mexico! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT A NON-TITLE MATCH! Special Guest Referee: "The Lion" Lance Marshall "The Lost Soul" Dusk Vs. "The Red Raver" Aimz These two warred during the Strength in Numbers title match in Mexico! Now, one week before she puts her belt on the line in the Dome of Doom, Campbell will face the man who wants nothing more than a one-on-one shot at Amy's partner! If Dusk wants his bout with Desade, he'll have to go through Campbell and Stevens to get there! But his only ally just might be the referee, Amy's fellow Dome participant, Lance Marshall! Marshall and Campbell have faced off throughout the Raver's reign – can Amy focus on Maloof with perhaps the biggest threat to her title in the ring as well? The Lion becomes a zebra for one night in New Mexico! For a berth at PRIME Colossus VI to face the Elite Champion! "The Spider in the Web" Desade Vs. "The Las Vegas Lariat" Phillip Kennedy (w/Katsidy and Savant) The first time these two went to war at the Collision in College Park, they went thirty minutes and no winner was decided! They'll have a rematch this week! Phillip Kennedy put himself directly in the crosshairs of the Director and the Red Raver over the last few weeks, and it earned him devastating, successive Bitchkillers from the new SIN champions! Now, with a slot in the Elite Title match at Colossus Six to face the Sykes-Connors winner and a to-be-named representative from PRIME on the line, is Kennedy in any condition to wrestle the unpinned-in-Sin-City Spider? The stakes can't be higher at the Fair! For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Spacely (w/Quasar) Vs. "Rappin'" Rick Malloy © If you're looking for the "underdog" in SOS3's DOME OF DOOM match, look no further than the winner of this match! Rick Malloy has had his... problems... with Xander Searle, culminating in the man who is Simply Better stealing the LW title belt down in Mexico! Now Rick Malloy doesn't have a belt to bring with him to the New Mexico Fairgrounds, but he'll put the title on the line against the dynamic high-flying Hulk Hogan of the Heavens, Spacely! One of these men will join all the luminaries in the DOME OF DOOM – but will either get their title belt back from Searle in Albuquerque? Tag Team Action! Daddy Daughter Day Vs. The Kings of Popsicles Daddy Daughter Day, fresh off an impressive victory over Black Ops in Tempe, comes to Albuquerque with a new goal in mind – earn another victory as they take aim at the new Strength in Numbers Champions! Opposing them will be doubtless the strangest pairing in SCCW, the Kings of Popsicles, fresh off of... uh, whatever it was we saw in Mexico! Will the Strawberry and the Blueberry emerge victorious? SCCW's tag scene is heating up and if you want proof, just tune in next week! Singles Match! Friar Vs. Saint Down in Mexico, Friar again fell JUST short of claiming his first win in Sin City, taking the loss to Mitchell Quinlan thanks to the unwanted assistance of Jadian Bridden! Now Friar attempts to rebound against the enigmatic Saint, who suffered at the hands of the Ace at Tempe-tation! Both Friar and Saint, two impressive masked men, will seek that elusive first victory in Albuquerque, and you have to wonder if the undefeated Mitchell Quinlan is quite done with Friar!
