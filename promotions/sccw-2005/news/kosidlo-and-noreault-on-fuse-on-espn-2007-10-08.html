Kosidlo and Noreault on FUSE on ESPN

8 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week on Uproar we saw former Global stars, Joshua Kosidlo and Jacque Noreault square off in a one on one contest. After a long and grueling contest neither man was declared the winner. Now, what many FUSE fans may not know, is that this was not the first time that The Paladin and the Canadian Dream have crossed paths. Many months ago, Joshua Kosidlo prevented Jacque Noreault from costing his nemesis, Keegan O’Conner the New-Found Wrestling Heavyweight Championship. The following week, Kosidlo and Noreault squared off in their first one on one encounter of their careers. That match, ended in a draw. Months later Kosidlo and Noreault squared off again, in the Global Octagon, for their second encounter. After a very long and brutal contest, Kosidlo picked up the win over The Canadian Dream. That same night however, Noreault was chosen in a Lottery to take on Eryc Morgan to decide the Global World Heavyweight Championship. Noreault would lose to Eryc Morgan, but a new feud would erupt. Morgan and Noreault would battle it out one on one for the next three months until both men, along with Joshua Kosidlo, Scott Taylor and KPJ parted ways with Global to become new additions to the FUSE roster. Thus far, of the men who left Global only Kosidlo and Noreault have really seen a good deal of FUSE action. Both men debuted the same week, and both men picked up wins, Kosidlo of Joaquin Pierre, and Noreault over Jackrabbit. However, only Noreault let his win get to his head, and would then begin to mock and taunt Kosidlo. It is evident both men are adjusting to their new surroundings in their own way, but what is also evident is that both men have chosen the other to be their way to make an impact. It is such, that we have learned Kosidlo and Noreault will be meeting at FUSE on ESPN in singles combat. What we do not know yet, is if after last week’s assault from Noreault, if this match will have any added flavor. It is also expected, that we will find out much more on this confrontation come this week on FUSE, as it would be hard to believe that neither man would have nothing to say after what transpired last week.
