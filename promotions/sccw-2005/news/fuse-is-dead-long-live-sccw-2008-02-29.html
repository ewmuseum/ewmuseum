FUSE is dead! Long live SCCW!

29 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUSE is dead! Long live SCCW!|That's right, after last week's Temptation, it was revealed that due to legal issues, FUSE had to change its name, and Smitty Duluth only had one thing in mind: Vegas, baby! Sin City Championship Wrestling kicked off last week with all three titles on the line! If you haven't checked it out yet, there's a link at the top of this page marked "Latest Temptation" where you can check out all the action! And now, straight from the desk of CEO Smitty T. Duluth... Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) Proudly presents TEMPTATION! L-I-V-E LIVE Wednesday, March 5, 2008! From the Bank of America Arena on the campus of the University of Washington in Seattle and ONLY on Cinemax, baby! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Universal Championship! ALL SCCW PERSONNEL BANNED FROM RINGSIDE "The Lord of the Asylum" Christian Darke Vs. "The New Life" Jonathan Rhine (c) After perhaps the most stunning conclusion to a broadcast in the history of FUSE/SCCW, Jonathan Rhine's condition going into this title match is in question after the beating he took to end Temptation! At FUSE on ESPN, Jonathan Rhine suffered the first loss of his career in a match he was actually involved in the decision for, as Christian Darke picked up a shocking disqualification victory! Now the Lord of the Asylum, fresh off ensuing victories over BloodAngel and "The Prophet" John Adams, seeks to do what many people thought several weeks ago couldn't be done -- take the Universal Title away from the New Life! But after the brutal assault at the end of last week's show, will Rhine be 100% for this big-time match against the final Global Wrestling champion? For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! The Black List Vs. The Pride Family What's this? SCCW has a tag-team championship again? Well, kind of -- the Fatal Faction championships have returned, rebranded! The belts' rules say that any member of a stable can wrestle for them, so we don't know who Gideon Fontaine's Black List will bring out to Seattle, but the question will remain -- can the Prides get along long enough to become champions in the face of the man who arguably MADE FUSE's tag team division? Will Gideon Fontaine be in any condition for wrestling -- and, if not, which members of the Black List will take on the most dysfunctional father/son team in wrestling history? Second Verse, Different than the First The Glorious Bastards (Peerless Hunter Sabuani and Mr. Ashe) Vs. BloodAngel and a Partner of His Choosing Okay, so last week's "Desade wrestles a mystery opponent" thing didn't turn out so well -- Desade kind of kicked seven shades of shit out of her opponent. This week, we'll see the final NWC tag-team champions, Sabuani and Mr. Ashe, take on a man who has shown his eagerness take it to Desade's men since the moment they popped their ugly mugs into our federation, BloodAngel. Who has Edward Lambert roped in to be his partner -- and... he can trust this guy, right? Tune in to find out! Get Your Grudge On! "The Canadian Dream" Jacque Noreault Vs. "The Obsidian Nightmare" Corvus Last week, Jacque Noreault's return to television didn't go so well as he got buried under an announce table by Aimz at the start of the night! Now Jacque gets a shot at the man who put him on the shelf, the AWC LiveWire Champion, Corvus -- whose promise to take care of Joaquin Pierre will require him to not get stretched out and broken by the Canadian Dream! We'll see Corvus in action for the first time on Cinemax next week! Two Big Guys Hit Each Other Hard! Cyrus Raynes (w/Sarah Riley) Vs. Malachi Cyrus Raynes has been quite impressive for the last few weeks -- now he gets his hands on the Lord of All Hosts one-on-one, and forgive us if we think he'll take advantage of it! Malachi and Raynes brawled the week before FUSE on ESPN, and now the Lord of Hosts tries to stand up to the Raynes Train! One has to wonder, though, what the relationship between Sarah Riley and her client will be like going into Seattle after the way the first Temptation ended. There's only one way to find out, friends! Triple Threat -- Well, Two Threats and a Little Guy! "The Demon" Vassago Vs. Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. "The Prophet" John Adams Last week, Vassago's distraction cost John Adams a bout against his former Nevermore ally, Christian Darke! Now the two of them will be in the same ring -- with little, 5-foot-9, 189 lb. Jadian Bridden between them, still smarting from being thrown off the FUSEWire at the last Uproar! Bridden managed to beat Andre Jensen last week, but can the plucky youth upend two guys who are going to want nothing more than to tear at each other? PLUS: * So they tell me that Seattle is a bad place to go for a show -- that the city is "cursed" for wrestling. "Pah," we say! SCCW pisses excellence at curses! * Last week's Temptation ended with a SHOCKING betrayal, as Jonathan Rhine was left lying in the ring by one of his best friends, Aimz! The lady we used to call the Red Raver WILL BE in Seattle, and there had better be a HELL of an explanation for her actions! * El Diablo was upended by Joaquin Pierre and has lost the Gateway Championship! With his fellow Undertow member scheduled to wrestle for the Universal Championship, there's no doubt that the Master of Manipulation will be in the house -- and we doubt he'll be happy. * After the ass kicking she suffered at the hands of Desade, Miss Madison doesn't seem ready to fold -- and neither does Samantha Pierce. We don't know if these two girls will be in town, but it's probably a safe bet I'd invite them to my office. * The first edition of Money Talks! with Andre Jensen was earth-shattering -- a Universal Championship match was made later that night! Will there be a repeat performance from AJ? * Much more about Sin City Championship Wrestling's first pay-per-view, next month in San Diego! What will it be called? Who'll be wrestling on it? We bet that a handsome blond man will have some details for us! All this plus Special Appearances by the NEW SCCW Gateway Champion Joaquin Pierre, the SCCW LiveWire Champion Elliott Rollins and his manager Slick Willy McDormant, Mayhem and the Magnum Opus, and so much more! TEMPTATION! Two Weeks Away! From the HP Pavilion at San Jose, in San Jose, California! SINGLES MATCH Gideon Fontaine Vs. Aimz     SIN CITY CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING! Comes to Pay-Per-View for the First Time! Sunday, March 23, 2008 From the Miramar Marine Corps Air Station in San Diego! Ultimate Sacrifice Match! El Diablo and Koa Lukuna Vs. Timo and Rufan Bolamba If the Bolambas win, Diablo and Lukuna are FIRED. If the Undertow wins, Koa Lukuna gets both Timo and Rufan to do with as he wants! It's a new name and a new era! How is this going to shape out for everyone involved? There's only one way to find out!
