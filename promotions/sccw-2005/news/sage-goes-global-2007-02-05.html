Sage Goes Global

5 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As recently announced at Primetime Central FUSE superstar Clinton Sage has been given a shot to become PTC Global Champion. Clinton Sage will enter the match up against three of the biggest superstars in wrestling today. - Doctor Curiosity: GTT4 Champion, Former fWo Cruiserweight Champion (Longest reign ever), current XUW Intercontinental Champion (coming up on nearly a year long reign) and XUW World Heavyweight Champion - Kimbusa: Current PTC Global Champion - Angelo Deville: GTT3 Champion, former FSW TV Champion, Former PRIME Tag Team Champion w/Justin Williamson, and currently a member of PRIME stable known as FU. This is one of the most unique and talent-laden matches in the history of the PTC Global title and it should be an exciting match up for the world to watch. The match up is scheduled to take place at right here in FUSE on our next addition of Killzone following our Pay-Per-View, Revolution, this Saturday night. Killzone 30 on February 21st, 2007 is the date for the match up and all four men will come in hungry to prove themselves the best in PTC with a victory over three of PTC’s biggest stars. Tune in on the 21st to find out who will walk away with the bragging rights and the gold!
