FUSE Trims Some Fat

10 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With more and more interest being paid attention to the FUSE Wrestling product management decided it was time to take a hard line approach toward it’s talent. Over the last few days the management in FUSE has either fired or granted the release of several FUSE wrestlers who they felt like were not living up to the standard that FUSE expected of them. The Amazing Technicolor Dream Team, The Sentinel, Seamus McManus, J. Bates, Domino, Kris Carmichael, and Mike Wade have all been either fired or granted their release in the last few days. Bates, Domino, Carmichael, and Wade all asked for releases for one reason or another and had them granted with FUSE wishing them the best of luck in their futures. ATDT, The Sentinel, and McManus were all released from their contracts due to what management considered ‘under performing’. It’s a particular shame in the cases of ATDT and The Sentinel as management had expected a great deal from them, even more so from ATDT after they quickly won the Fatal Faction Championships, and were saddened to have to release them. Smitty T. Duluth addressed the situation in a telephone conference this afternoon. ‘Releasing those three guys, in particular, was very disheartening cause it was thought that they’d really make a big splash in FUSE. It was a tough decision but one that had to be made in order to better FUSE for the future.” Perhaps the most shocking move was Mike Wade requesting a release. The former Custom Chaos Champion seemed to be a star on the rise prior to this latest Killzone. ‘After losing to Drogan in very quick fashion Mike Wade came to me and confessed he simply wasn’t feeling it in FUSE, or wrestling for that matter, anymore. It was at that point that he asked for his release and I granted it to him. Like I told him if he isn’t happy and he isn’t putting his entire heart into FUSE then I don’t want him to waste his time hanging on in order to not hurt a few feelings.” The trimming of the roster has moved FUSE back down to what Smitty said he believed will be a much more manageable and energized roster, and that he believes that the men and woman still with contracts in FUSE are poised to make huge waves and lead FUSE closer and closer to their goal of being the top federation in the industry.
