Signing spree has begun

4 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
USE Commissioner Smitty T. Duluth has confirmed the signings of some brand new FUSE superstars in an interview earlier today. 'The last couple of days have been very good for FUSE wrestling as we have begun to expand our roster size. I'm proud to announce the signing of four new stars to our fold. Dewey Smarts, Lane Stevens, Johnny Lexicon, and Tyson Sinn. These four men come to us from varying backgrounds and federations around the world and we're pleased to have them under FUSE contracts.' These signings came within days of a signing spree that Duluth and FUSE management went on last week that brought in the likes of Ethan Frost, Jasmine Drogan, Nathan O'Neill, and Sean Walker. O'Neill, Drogan, Walker, and Frost are all scheduled to make their in-ring debuts for FUSE this week, at Killzone 21, when Frost takes on Walker and O'Neill takes on Drogan in singles competitions. Dewey Smarts and Johnny Lexicon are the two most known wrestlers of the newest batch of signings as they have worked in some high profile and respected federations in the past. Smarts was once a member of New Era Wrestling, a former affiliate of PrimeTimeCentral, and Lexicon comes to FUSE via rival federation AWC. When asked about any issues that FUSE might be having with AWC over the signing the commissioner had this to say. 'I'm not aware of any issues that would come up. Lexicon gave his notice to AWC prior to contacting myself about the opening in FUSE. It's my understanding they parted on good terms, so I don't foresee any issues.' Just a few minutes after making that statement the commissioner bid me a good day, saying he had 'work to finish up on a couple of other major signings for FUSE' and he'd speak with me again in the near future if all went well. Who else might the commissioner have signing on the dotted line in the near future? One can only speculate at this point but I can assure you that I'm more than excited for FUSE wrestling fans at this point. Stay tuned for more information when it becomes available.
