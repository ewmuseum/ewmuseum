Revolution Brings Down The House

13 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's the biggest Pay-Per-View of the FUSE year and it lived up to every bit of it's billing. Titles, pride, honor, and the works were on the line when FUSE superstars clashed for all that is good and holy to them. Did the Fusion Rumble live up to the wacky nature of it's creator? Was Nathan O'Neill the man to finally end Tanaka's strangle hold on the LiveWire Title? Who walked away from the HUGE Fatal Faction Championship match with the Gold? Was Jacob McKail able to avenge the beatings he's taken the last few weeks when he stepped inside the steel cage against Clinton Sage? Did either walk out a winner? Somebody changed sides tonight and it might surprise you who it was. A couple of top talents made their debut in FUSE. Who were they and what type of impact did they make? Last, but not least, who walked out as FUSE Universal Champion? Did Tigera turn back the surging John Covel, once again snuffing out a dream of his? Find out the answers to all of this, and much more, by tuning into Revolution!!! Revolution
