FUSE's Top Ten

20 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
10. Paul Cain- Cain narrowly missed a victory last week in the main event, but has yet to leave his mark in FUSE. A relative newcomer, Cain looks to rise to the top of the charts. 9. Carson Riley- Riley has gained some recent attention in FUSE, challenging every wrestler who advanced to the second part of the tower at WIW to a match, in order to prove himself. After a huge win against one half of the Cowboys from Hell last week, Riley seems off to a good start. 8. Ethan Frost- After a long absence from the company, Frost is back and is tearing through his competition. A recently received offer from The Affliction could place Frost back in the limelight. 7. El Diablo- During their bloody and very personal feud, El Diablo recently lost his LiveWire championship to Jason Cruise. However, this member of The Affliction hasn’t given up hope that one day the title will be his once again. 6. Mayson Colby- Last week Colby became number one contender for the LiveWire title and had a good showing in the tower match at WIW. However, he is most likely known as the man constantly being stepped on by his rival, Sean Sterling. 5. Sean Sterling- Sterling shocked the FUSE community by pulling off an upset and placing third in the tower match at WIW. He is on the rise and is most notably recognized for his limit pushing feud with Mayson Colby. 4. Jason Cruise- Newly appointed as LiveWire Champion, Cruise has proven that he has what it takes in this business. He’s had a brutal feud with rival El Diablo, and is a member of The Highwaymen. 3. Lane Stevens- Also a member of The Affliction, Stevens has dominated in FUSE as of late. Stevens placed second in the tower match at WIW. 2. Clinton Sage- At WIW Sage proved once again that he is the best that FUSE has to offer. He’s had quite a successful run with the Universal Title, and heads The Affliction, the most powerful stabe in FUSE. Sage is the one to beat. 1. Jonathan Rhine- Between retaining the Gateway Championship, winning the tower match at WIW to become the number one contender for the Universal Title, and forming The Highwaymen, there’s no doubt that Rhine is at the top of this list.
