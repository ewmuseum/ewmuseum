Temptation Brings Repercussions Beyond the Ring!

16 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The FREE transcript of Sin City Championship Wrestling's trip to Vermont is now available on the site! Go, read it, and be amazed! Sin City Championship Wrestling - in association with the Kent Fusiliers - proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION: STRENGTH IN NUMBERS LIVE! Tuesday, October 21, 2008 from the DCU Arena in Worcester, Massachusetts and ONLY on Cinemax! See an all tag-team week for Sin City, just five shows away from our return to PPV! MAIN EVENT: For the Sin City Championship Wrestling Strength in Numbers Championships! Austin "The Wombat" King & Misery Vs. "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy & "The Elder Statesman" Reginald V. Lovecraft (c) After picking up his first win in Sin City Championship Wrestling, Austin King has stumbled into a gold mine and a main event slot in Worcester! Austin will team with the frightening woman called Misery who was betrayed by Malice and Mayhem when the Path of Destruction joined the Dead Man's Hand! Now they'll face two members of the Dead Man's Hand for the only championship left in the control of Alexandra Pierce's organization! After Kennedy repeatedly interfered against Sean Sterling, will the Gateway Champion intervene? Two-Fall Windfalls Match First Fall is for a Shot at the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Second Fall for a Shot at the SCCW Gateway Championship Pinning Wrestler Wins the Shot "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors & Jadian Bridden Vs. Paul Searle & Jared Sykes Wyatt Connors' rise through the ranks in SCCW puts the Gateway Championship square in his sights, but first he'll have to earn a shot! See Wyatt Connors, fresh off a fantastic bout with Steve Knox, seek a crack at Sean Sterling, but if he wishes to do so, he'll have to put aside the differences we saw on display with the Dead Man's Hand! On the other side of the ring, rookie Paul Searle, just a few weeks from literally being pulled out of the crowd by Jay Draven and the former SCCW LiveWire Champion (and soon-to-be date buddy of Desade) Jared Sykes! Two Pissed-Off Women vs. Five Religious Nutjobs DNA (Desade & Aimz) Vs. The Decency Crusade (Neophyte, Caravan, Jeremiah and the Luchadores For Jesus) It was a... tumultuous time in Vermont for the two most dominant females in the PTC. After the shocking events of the last Temptation, will the Red Raver and the Director be closer -- or are they farther apart? See as they team for only the second time in their SCCW careers take on the entirety of the False Prophet's goon squad, and after what Wyatt attempted to pull in Vermont, the odds STILL might be against them! Get Ye To the Show Early! Malik Roland & Mr. Ashe Vs. Clayton Byrd & "American Youth" Josh Last week, Clayton Byrd was dominant in his debut, picking up the win over Malik Roland and Gregory Orion Daniels! Now, the cowboy moves into stiffer waters, and he's bringing Roland with him. Byrd and Roland will be on opposite sides again, with Clay being joined by former Global Wrestling Champion "American Youth" Josh, and Roland being joined by the Mushmouthed Man-Mountain, Desade's Chief of Security, Mr. Ashe! With Byrd in Sin City to clean up the mess, can he start with a member of the Dead Man's Hand? An SCCW Halloween in Quebec City! Temptation in Two Weeks! From Le Colisée Pepsi in Quebec City, QC! Charley "Cookie" Crisp Vs. A DMH Member of Crisp's Choice Plus: The SCCW Gateway Championship WILL BE on the line! SCCW TEMPTATION! Three Weeks Away! From Halifax, Nova Scotia! Amy Campbell defends the Universal Championship in her hometown! SCCW TEMPTATION! FOUR Weeks Away! From the Gampel Pavilion in Storrs, Connecticut! See all the stars of Sin City as they make a final push to Defiance! DEFIANCE II From the Nassau Coliseum in Long Island, NY! *Card Subject to Change "The Lion" Lance Marshall Vs. Aimz (c) Stipulations to Be Announced! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy Vs. "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling MORE MATCHES TO BE ANNOUNCED AS EARLY AS TEMPTATION WORCESTER!
