Steve Watson CPA Joins FUSE

19 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Killzone 27 'The New Shit' Bobino demanded that Candy Marshall put him in a match up where he could show the new breed of FUSE stars what true talent looked like. She gave him a match against her accountant. He chuckled. He laughed. Hell you could even say he acted as giddy as a school girl at the possibility to beat up some poor nerd in a suit and tie. Until the music hit. From the moment the theme “Money” it was quite clear that Bobino wasn’t in any way prepared for who Candy Marshall’s ‘accountant’ would be. With the moment of surprise on his side, Steve Watson picked up his first FUSE victory in near record time, pinning Bobino to the mat in under a minute. What will Bobino have to say about what has to be the most embarrassing moment of his career? How will Steve Watson follow up that speedy performance? Tune in next week, at Killzone 28, to find out.
