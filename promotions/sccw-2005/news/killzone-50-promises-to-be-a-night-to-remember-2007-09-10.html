Killzone 50 promises to be ‘A Night To Remember’

10 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is a statement released earlier today by FUSE commissioner, Smitty T, Duluth. “It’s no secret by now that KillZone 50 will play host to the final FUSE appearance of many key FUSE Staff. Scott Ambrose, Hunter Jones, Dominique, Skye, Willy Parr and many others have played key roles in FUSE since it’s conception and long before during XCW and XoW. But rest assured fuseiacs, yours truly, STD, will not be abandoning you. I promise to you that FUSE will continue to grow bigger and better than ever. And to cement that statement as fact I guarantee one of the OMGWTFBBQ moments that you guys love to see. Sure, an era may be coming to an end, but this week will mark the beginning of something even more epic, It will surely be a night to remember.” STD refused to comment on what the momentous occasion may be saying that we ‘would have to wait until next week’. But whatever it is, it’s certainly seems to have helped the commissioner keep his chin up through these turbulent times.
