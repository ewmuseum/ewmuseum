Wyatt Colton comments on CWMC and the Fatal Faction Division

11 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wyatt Colton gave an interview on DDT Radio, where he discussed various topics including FUSE and the upcoming Chris Williams Memorial Cup. On the topic of FUSE, the wrestler formerly known as the Martian said, “You know, if it wasn’t for the fact that I have a contract that I can’t break, I wouldn’t be there. I’m sorry, but when you run a division that has been made a mockery of with the inclusion of teams like the RPG and the fact that Fred Fusion and Ethan Frost has been in tag matches, competing as the co-holders of that [expletive deleted] stupid FUSION Title, you can’t help to think what else is out there.” When asked about the upcoming Fatal Faction Fatal Fourway, “You know, FUSE is trying to bring in the fans early. You’ve seen the crowd during the opening matches. They are dead. A couple of weeks ago, the arena was only half full for the first hour of the show. It’s pitiful, really. The fans don’t want to see wrestling. They want to see the greatest Fatal Faction ever put together, the Cowboys From Hell.” On his burgeoning rivalry with Amy “Aimz” Campbell “I can’t help it when someone takes offense to being attacked in the back. It’s not like we came to her and Darcy’s home to…” We’ve chosen not to report the rest of what he said in this portion due to the standards and practices of this website. On his upcoming match with FUSE Legend “The New Shit” Bobino in the first round of the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, “You know, there are times where we have to embarrass our own company. Bobino has been around for a long time, both as a singles wrestler and as a member of C.H.A.O.S. If it takes me having to make him look like the little bastard he is in that ring to get ahead, [expletive deleted] him. I don’t care. He isn’t to mine, or Connor’s level. So I have to send him packing to make it to the second round. Sucks to be him.”
