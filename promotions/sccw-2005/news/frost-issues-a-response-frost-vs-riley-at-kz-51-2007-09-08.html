Frost issues a response; Frost vs Riley at KZ 51?

8 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following Carson Riley's earlier boast that he would be more than capable of beating everyone who surpassed him in the now infamous Tower Match at WIW, it would seem that a man has already stepped up to the plate in the form of FUSE long-term member Ethan Frost. Frost who has only just returned to the company after a lengthy absence, details of which have not been disclosed, was quick to throw his name into the arena to face Carson Riley. "The way I see it, if I hadn't disappeared I would be the top dog of this company, name the best in the company right now and I have beaten them. Lane Stevens, Check, Clinton Sage, Check. For a no name newbie like Riley to go running his mouth could be a foolish mistake, so I'll make it nice and simple. If Riley wants me I'll be at Killzone 51." Ethan Frost who has been out of sorts as of late finished 6th in the Tower match, it was deemed a generally good showing for the FUSE veteran, but in talks with the man himself he was unhappy with his own performance, but was not hesitant in singing the praises of one of the new kids on the block, Rhine. "There is no doubt in my mind that I have the ability to win at the highest level and to come sixth in the biggest match of the year so far is simply disappointing. That it not to take away from Rhine, the guy is a machine and has destroyed everyone in front of him. It will be nice to see Sage actually challenged for a change, it's been a long time coming since the times of McKail and myself." Frost was also questioned about the whereabouts of a long time FUSE or should that be FUSION favourite, Fred Fusion. "I don't know, I really don't know. I've left messages numerous times, but i haven't heard from him at all. If I'm honest, I wouldn't expect to see Fred back anytime soon." So with Frost having had his say and Riley's challenge met he went to prepare for his bout at Killzone against one of the two members of 'The Affliction' he has yet to beat. Jeb Stewart.
