Killzone 39 Airs!

19 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This week FUSE Wrestling rolls into Ottawa, Ontario, Canada for a HUGE pre-Pay-Per-View event. The up-and-coming Cowboys From Hell take on the mountain of a man called Two Ton. Could these two pissed off ranchers defeat the scariest man to get into a concession line? Two FUSE newcomers went at it. Would Dean Jokerlin get his first win or would Hoss Richards tack another "W" onto his record? Darcy Crisis took on Timo Bolamba in a no disqualification match. Would the Japaneese Firecracker get involved or would the match go off as cleanly as a no DQ match could? Finally "The Seventh Sin" took on the chairman of FUSION Wrestling Fred Fusion. Could Fred take out Clinton Sage just a week before his Universal Title match or would Sage enlist the help of The Affliction to assure a good night? Of course the fallout from last week's tag team main event would be on display. Word had it that Jacob McKail and John Covel were both upset about the mishap that cost them their match at Killzone 38. Would these two settle their differences peacefully or would McKail lose his night of rest before his big title defense at Afflicted Glory? All this and more on KillZone 39! Killzone 39
