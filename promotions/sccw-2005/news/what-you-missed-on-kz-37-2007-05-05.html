What You Missed On KZ 37

5 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On KZ 37 Dave Gibson was scheduled to face Tanaka...only it didn't air. While the match was taking place the FUSE cameras were following Hoyt Williams around, during his crusade to prove Jacob McKail, the new Universal Champion, was a racist. It was during this time that Hoyt led the cameras to McKail's lockerroom to find a KKK robe in his locker. So, since the world missed the match up, we have it here for you now. SA: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the 37th edition of KillZone and tonight we’ve got one hell of a match to open tonight’s show. “Old School” Dave Gibson goes one-on-one against a man who’s been a thorn in the side of himself and Timo Bolamba in recent weeks. Tsuyoshi Tanaka attacked him in a cowardly assault with a bat a few weeks ago, not to mention last week putting Timo Bolamba’s head into a steel chair with a piledriver. This man is truly beyond salvation and quite frankly, I have Gibson beats Tanaka’s ass. HJ: HEY, HEY, HEY! Being biased is my job! You sit there and tell us what moves they bust out during the match and I’LL provide the color. SA: Jones, knock it off. As much respect I have for Tanaka’s abilities to get it done in that ring, what excuse is there for a man taking a bat to somebody? HJ: Gibbo started it. Before the two can say anything more, “Paradise City” by Guns ‘N’ Roses blasts over the PA and the fans start to cheer (mostly) for Mr. Old School popping through the curtains. He’s still a little banged up from recent weeks, but he throws off his t-shirt right away and walks down the ramp, scowling like a dog ready to attack any trespasser that dares enter its territory. Gibbo walks up the steps and climbs into the ring, ready to have it out with his antagonist, Tanaka. SKYE: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! First, making his way to the ring from Charlotte, North Carolina! Weighing in at 202 pounds… DAVE GIBSON! AS: Gibbo’s ready to go. Can he get some of his own personal comeuppance against Tanaka tonight? SA: I’ll field that question: No Goddamn way. He still can’t be 100% after those beatings he’s taken from the likes of Tanaka. SKYE: And his opponent, from Izu Island, Japan, weighing in at 195 pounds… The Former LiveWire Champion… TSUYOSHI TANAKA! The opening chords to “Black Mass” by Danzig blare over the loud speaker as the arena spotlights strobe red and whites as Tanaka appears at the top of the entrance ramp. The audiences already begin to jeer him, but if bothers Tanaka he doesn’t let it show. He waves at the glaring Gibbo like a dick and laughs at his own handiwork with the baseball bat from a few weeks ago before approaching the ring. If you call my name It's but one of many by which I'm known The borneless one There is no pain or anger that I am not Tanaka strides to the ring, perceiving the crowd with the utmost arrogance, as he slowly climbs the ring steps and gets himself into the ring. The fans continue to boo as Tanaka takes off his white warm-up jacket with red Japanese symbols. Once he tosses it away, he takes the time to hop onto the second rope and look down at the fans like pieces of gutter-trash. He scoffs at them and hops off the turnbuckle… THWACK! The cocky Tanaka quickly has the air driven from his lungs when Gibbo attacks him with a HARD Knife-Edge Chop. Tanaka heads for higher ground, but the Charlotte native quickly vents his frustrations upon the Japanese wrestler with several VICIOUS punches and kicks in the nearby corner. SA: And Gibbo’s taking the fight to Tanaka before he has a chance to get going! Can you blame him? HJ: Yes, I can! Keep it clean, you bastard! The look on the face of Tanaka tells the story to the cheering fans both in attendance and watching at home: Being beaten up hurts. BAD. The Master of Pain tries to get out of the corner, but Gibbo merely follows him and blasts him in the back of the skull with several more nasty blows before grabbing him in a rear waistlock, then powering him down to the mat. Dave applies a tight front facelock with his left arm while continuing to batter Tanaka with the right. HJ: That’s it, Tanaka! Weather the storm! Wait for Gibson to tire himself out, then beat his ass! SA: (sarcastically) Yeah. That’s EXACTLY what he’s doing. OOOOH! And Gibson rams him head-first into that mat before tightening the front facelock again! Gibbo’s really taking the fight to Tanaka here and if this keeps up, there’ll be nothing left of him! Gibson powers Tanaka up and snaps him right back down into a quick Snap Suplex before floating over into the cover. ONE! TWO… NO! Tanaka kicks out, but Dave stays on him. Mr. Old School pulls him back to his feet and lets loose with several more Knife-Edge Chops, leaving little red welts on the chest of his hated adversary. He takes hold of Tsuyoshi quickly and DRIVES him hard across his knee with a Pendulum Backbreaker. Once he takes the wind out of Tanaka’s sails, he goes for another cover. ONE! TWO! SA: NO! Tanaka showing great resiliency so early into this bout, but not even the former LiveWire Champion can have much left him in at the rate Gibson’s working him over! HJ: Bullshit, bullshit, a thousand times bullshit, Scott! Gibbo’s fine in that ring, but Tanaka has proven time and time again that there ain’t no keeping him down! Gibson quickly takes hold of Tanaka’s leg and tries to go for his signature School’s In Session (Sharpshooter), but The Master of Pain quickly wakes up from his funk and make s a mad dash for the ropes. Once he gets both hands wrapped around the bottom cable, Referee JJ Alexander calls for the five-count. Gibbo lets go for a moment, but doesn’t take his eyes off the sore Tanaka. He grabs him by the leg once again, but his trick thumb acts up again, “accidentally” jabbing the former Sellout in the eye! HJ: There we go! The NCAA would’ve approved of such a great tactical counter! SA: That was a poke to the eye. He would’ve been disqualified for that, maybe even fined if this was the NCAA. Once The Japanese Firecracker uses the ropes to pull himself back to his feet, he quickly takes the fight back to Gibbo, BLASTING him with hard kicks to the chest, each one louder than the last. The fans cringe with each blow and The Master of Pain wraps it up with a nasty Two-Kick combination consisting of a kick to the chest followed quickly by a shot to the head that takes Gibson off his feet. Tanaka now seats Gibson in the center of the ring and floors him with several more stiff kicks. The Japanese Firecrackers bounces off the ropes behind him and in the process, nails a BIG running punt kick to the small of Gibson’s back, making him cringe. He continues to run off the other set of ropes, then plasters Gibbo right in the mouth with a deadly low Front Dropkick. Now back on his feet, Tanaka takes a mock bow that sends the crowd into a booing frenzy. Once it’s over, he turns Gibbo over and goes for the cover. ONE! TWO! TH… NO! Tanaka makes a motion that he’s going to bitchslap the referee to Hell and back and Hell again, then turns his attention right back to the veteran. He pulls Dave back to his feet, then throws him hard between the ropes, sending him bouncing onto the apron before rolling out onto the floor. Smiling at his handiwork, The Master of Pain sets out to prove just why he’s called that as he follows Gibson out. He picks him up and smacks him with several Knife-Edge Chops of his own while leaning him against the barricade. Tanaka wraps both arms around the waist of Gibson, then turns him around and SPIKES him into the apron, sending a surge of pain right through his own spine. While Dave struggles to breathe, Tanaka shifts direction, this time running Gibson into the ringpost! SA: Gibson’s becoming quickly acquainted with several objects on the outside of the ring by Tanaka. Like I said before, there’s no doubt Tanaka can get it done, but his methods of breaking down his opponent are highly suspect. HJ: There’s an old saying. It goes “Shut the Hell up, you whiner! Tanaka can damn well do whatever he wants!” SA: Clever. You ass. HJ: Hey, I speak the truth! Once Tanaka thinks that Gibson’s had enough fun being greeted by various devices among the ringside area, he rolls Gibbo back into the ring underneath the bottom rope while making sure to place him with his back facing upwards. Tanaka quickly follows him in, leaping over the ropes with a slingshot before coming across Dave’s back with both feet! Dave screams in anguish after having nearly two hundred pounds crash across his back. Tsuyoshi quickly rolls him over and goes for the pin. ONE! TWO! THR… NO! Tanaka fumes silently, but doesn’t let the situation get the best of him just yet. He takes hold of Gibson and whips him across the ring, immediately following him in with a Leaping Knee Strike to the head that stuns the former OSW Tag Team Champion. Shifting positions, he whips Dave cross-corner once again and follows up with a second Leaping Knee to the skull, this one looking even worse than the first. The Master of Pain turns around while wrapping up Gibson in a headlock and sprints forward, driving him into the mat with a nasty-looking variant on the Bulldog! HJ: That was a pretty sweet combination of moves. Gibbo’s ass will be feeling that in the morning! SA: Once Tanaka got back in control in this match, it’s been all him on offense. Mr. Old School better find himself an opening fast or any thoughts of revenge are gonna be out the window. Tanaka sizes up his target now with a prone Gibson laying in the middle of the ring. He runs off the second rope and comes back with a big-time moonsault, crashing down across the chest of Gibbo! He hooks the leg tightly for the cover. ONE! TWO! THR… KICK OUT! The former LiveWire Champion slaps the mat in sheer frustration and glares at the scrawny referee ominously and soon draws his ire right back to Gibson. With Mr. Old School down and out for the count, Tanaka heads out to the apron and starts a climb to the top rope. SA: What’s he going for here? HJ: He’s dancing around the Maypole with DavWHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE?! HE’S PUTTING THIS AWAY! Smirking at the highly energetic and booing crowd, Tanaka smiles as he leaps with a high Guillotine Legdrop, looking to crash right across the throat of his current adversary… The leap is perfect. He has loads of hangtime. Now… If only Dave Gibson didn’t move out of the way, it would’ve been solid. Instead, the combination of shock/pain sets in as Tanaka lands hard on his tailbone. The fans cheers as Tsuyoshi grimaces while Dave starts to come around after the punishment he’s taken. HJ: Damn it, Gibson! Stay still and take your beating already! SA: No way! Gibson’s got himself an opening here, folks. All he’s gotta do now is capitalize. Gradually, Mr. Old School starts to climb back to his feet, all the while egging on Tanaka to get back to his. The Master of Pain makes it to his feet, but finds himself back where he started – on the receiving end of a NASTY Knife-Edge Chop. Gibbo lets three more fly while the fans “WHOO!” in unison. He maneuvers himself behind Tanaka and quickly snaps him down to the center of the ring with a big Back Suplex. The fans now rally quickly behind Gibson as he rolls through and successfully chains the Back Suplex, this time into a pretty sharp German Suplex. Rather than let go of the hold, Gibson quickly rolls through once again while carrying the dead weight of Tanaka back up. Once both combatants are on their feet, Dave clubs him in the back with several forearms, then snaps him one last time with a second German Suplex, this time of the release variety! HJ: Cheater! He didn’t give Tanaka any breathing room! That ain’t no way to be treating a former LiveWire Champion! SA: He’s giving Tanaka what he deserves! He took a baseball bat to Gibson one week, then piledrove Timo Bolamba onto a steel chair! Somewhere within the deepest, darkest reaches of Tanaka’s cold black heart, he was regretting both of those decisions, especially when Gibson manages to successfully apply the Sharpshooter. SA: SCHOOL’S IN SESSION! Timo’s got Tanaka right where he wants him and you can see the pain etched on his face! HJ: Come on, T! Get to the ropes! Damn ref, do your job and disqualify him! SA: For what?! HJ: Applying that illegal submission to Tanaka! SA: In WHAT world is the Sharpshooter illegal? While Gibson applies every ounce of strength that his 202-pound body can muster, The Master of Pain claws vehemently across the ring. Inch by agonizing inch, Tsuyoshi muscles his way across the canvas while the pain from the deadly submission hold travels through his lower back and legs. Gibbo continues to apply the pressure and Tanaka finds himself mere inches from reaching the rope with his right arm… He scratches… He claws… HJ: HE MADE IT! GET GIBSON OFF HIM, REF! NOW! DO YOUR JOB, YOU PRICK! SA: Well, Tanaka luckily made it to the ropes, but you gotta wonder how much that submission took out of him. Gibbo holds on to the count of four and releases the School’s In Session. Tanaka tries to fend of another attack from Dave, but it’s not to be as he takes two handfuls of Tanaka’s hair and pulls him back up. He tries taking his head off with a Clothesline, but The Japanese Firecracker ducks and connects with a stunning Sitdown Jawbreaker! He falls back to the ropes while Tanaka clutches his back in pain, hoping to get some much-needed momentum back. Tanaka measures up the stunned Gibbo and scurries up to the top rope in a big leap before flying right back with a Missile Dropkick right into the jawline of Mr. Old School. SA: GREAT move right there by Tanaka! But he doesn’t follow through with the cover right away! The Master of Pain is too busy trying to shake out the beatings he’d taken earlier from the likes of Gibson. However, once he does, he scurries over to Gibbo’s fallen body and hooks another leg. ONE! TWO! NO! By this juncture, Tanaka is ready to pull his hair out. He eggs Gibson on while stomping his feet on the mat impatiently, waiting to take his head off with presumably some sort of kick. Once he finds the opening he’s looking for, Tanaka speeds forward and runs for the Shining Wizard… DUCKED! He turns… SMALL PACKAGE! ONE! TWO! NO! SA: What a match thus far! And the night is still young! Gibson’s failed pinning attempt catches Tanaka off-guard, but the Japanese Firecracker manages to kick out. Both men head back to their feet, but Tanaka blasts Gibson low with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick to the stomach. With a look of sheer determination on his face, Tanaka hooks him with the Standing Headscissors and attempts something. HJ: Here we go! Tanaka’s gonna fuck him up now! Powerbomb him! Piledrive him! Something him! Anything! SA: Tanaka looking for the Powerbomb near the rope… NO! Gibson rolls through with the Sunset Flip… WAIT! Tanaka kneels down and grabs the nearby rope with his arms for extra leverage. ONE! TWO! THR… SA: YES! JJ saw Tanaka’s attempt at cheating and refuses to count! HJ: That little prick! He should do his job and start counting! SA: He was CHEATING! Tanaka gets off Gibson and starts screaming in Japanese at the scrawny Alexander. Probably something amounting to “I’M GOING TO RIP OUT YOUR SPINE AND REPLACE IT WITH AN ANACONDA!” Once he lets out another roar of frustration, he turns his attention back to Gibson, who ROLLS HIM UP WITH A RUNNING SCHOOLBOY! ONE! TWO! THREE! SKYE: Here is your winner of the match… DAVE GIBSON! The fans shout in a loud chorus, comprised mainly of cheering and approval as Gibson heads out of the ring, smiling at his handiwork. He’d just defeated Tsuyoshi Tanaka and gained a measure of revenge for the vicious baseball bat assault weeks earlier. Tanaka kicks at the ring rope furiously, blowing up at the referee in the process for his failure to maintain the three count he would’ve surely gained, had he not spotted the cheating in progress. Meanwhile, a sweaty and sore Gibson raises three fingers to his angered rival, letting him know that he can still go. HJ: He cheated! Gibson clearly bribed the referee for that win and you know it, Scott! Tanaka had his ass PINNED! SA: Well, folks, while my partner her has a conniption, we’ll cut to a commercial break. Stay tuned!
