SNAKE EYES, TEMPTATION 4/11, AND MORE!

14 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Snake Eyes aired and was a huge success. So much we thought: is there any way we can top this? WELL WE DID! Take a look at that link up there, the one that says NEXT TEMPTATION, to see how we kept rolling right along. And now, from the shorthand of Smitty Duluth himself... From the Desk of Smitty T. Duluth - For Immediate Release! Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) Proudly brings to you: TEMPTATION! LIVE! Tuesday April 22, 2008 from the ARCO Arena in Sacramento, California! 10PM Eastern/9PM Central and only on Cinemax! See the beginning of the Double Elimination LiveWire Tournament and all the chicks we can put on the air on another exciting edition of Temptation! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT: OUR CHICKS KICK ASS! "The Steel Rose" Dawn McFarlane Vs. Aimz On one side of the ring will be a former GCW Heavyweight Champion. On the other will stand a woman who came within an eyelash of winning the SCCW Universal Championship! Two of the most spectacular female wrestlers -- or wrestlers of any gender -- will face off in a barnburner of a main event! What is Amy Campbell's condition, physically after her match at Snake Eyes against Jon Rhine, and mentally, after she was pulled away from Rhine at UCLA? Will The Steel Rose be able to make an immediate, lightning-quick impact in SCCW? And what part, if any, will Aimz' burgeoning relationship with the Dead Man's Hand play? Alanna Marshall (w/Lance Marshall) Vs. Katsidy (w/The Dead Man's Hand) After a not-altogether-unpleasant conversation and a barnburner of a conclusion to Temptation, we'll dredge up memories of the National Wrestling Council again as Alanna Marshall and Katsidy will go one-on-one! The Hawaiian Lioness got herself a piece of Misery last week, and the Sex Kitten is said to be no slouch in physical confrontation (you'd have to be to be Alex Pierce's right hand). What will happen when these two friends and enemies face-off in Sacramento? And what parts will Lance Marshall and the Dead Man's Hand play? ROUND ONE OF THE 2008 ELLIOTT ROLLINS MEMORIAL LIVEWIRE TOURNAMENT! Elliott Rollins is on the shelf, thanks to Wyatt Connors' actions at Snake Eyes! So we're gonna have a good old-fashioned double-elimination LiveWire tournament! All matches will be conducted under LW rules! Get yourself a draw, and both of you lose! Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. Bishop (w/Sarah Riley) Is it just us, or does Jadian Bridden always draw the biggest guy on the block? The young Texan, fresh off laying out the likes of Peerless Hunter Sabuani with the so-called Bridden-Buster has an unenviable first-round draw in the Biloxi Beast, sure to be in an all-fired bad mood after his match with Lance Marshall! Who takes that coveted spot in the winner's bracket? And can these two girls have a nice roll-on-the-mat catfight, please? Cyrus "Mongo" Raynes Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors (w/Jeremiah) Cyrus Raynes has already been the LiveWire Champion once -- he'll have his chance to earn it again here in the tournament! He starts with a tough task -- the former World Champion and the reason for the tournament, Wyatt Connors! Connors has found God, but he may need him with the Highwayman on the other side of the ring! Plus, for anyone to earn my reward for eliminating Connors from the tournament, he'll have to lose for the first time! "The Demon" Vassago (w/The Iniquity) Vs. Charley Crisp Charley Crisp earned perhaps a surprising berth in the tournament with his victory in the four-way match last week -- but his road to the LiveWire Tournament may just stop in Sacramento with an appointment with a demon! Vassago came up a little short at Snake Eyes, and he and the Iniquity will be on-hand to try to earn a spot in the winner's bracket! Cleopatra seems like just the right type of woman for Charley -- can he keep his head (either of them) straight to take on the seven-footer? Tag-Team Action! "The Modern Day Warrior" Jay Draven & "The Punk Rock Pixie" Charlotte Ramone (w/Krissy Jamison) Vs. The Macabre Militia Last week, Jay Draven and Charlie Ramone were kicking the hell out of each other in a four-way match that ended up earning one of their competitors a spot in the LiveWire Tournament! This week, they'll be forced to team against a trio (yeah, don't ask me which ones are wrestling) who started their SCCW career by putting Christian Darke on the shelf and had a face-up confrontation with perhaps the best tag-team in the PTC, GCW's Banned & Exiled~! Will Draven and Ramone be able to coexist (I'm thinking yes!) and will B&E~! show their faces on SCCW TV, perhaps in this very match? Singles Match Since I Don't Believe In Curtain Jerking! Ethan Frost Vs. Ciel Last week, Ethan Frost put down a monster in Corvus -- this week, he'll climb back into an SCCW ring with a man who was very impressive in his team's victory against the Iniquity! Ciel and Dawn McFarlane bookend the card in Sacramento -- will they both be successful and make the team have an undeniable impact on Sin City? With appearances from: Jonathan Rhine, Andre Jensen, Joaquin Pierre, Mayhem and so many more! PLUS! - What are the conditions of Peerless Hunter Sabuani and Edward Lambert after the tumultuous conclusion of Temptation? We saw both superstars carted out of Pauley Pavilion in stretchers -- is either in any condition to wrestle, especially with what the former four-time World Champ has coming up in two weeks? - After another heated verbal confrontation between Amy Campbell and Jonathan Rhine, it certainly seems that the issue between these two former members of the Highwaymen isn't over yet! Jono is scheduled to be in Sacramento and as soon as I announced this was a CHICKS RULE edition of Temptation, you knew that Aimz was going to be in the main event! Will these two go heads-up again? - What did Alex Pierce whisper to Amy Campbell? What is Mayhem's mindframe after a possibly career-making victory over BloodAngel? Will Banned & Exiled~! make another appearance on SCCW Television? (Did I ask that already? Tough shit.) All this and so much more as only the people of Sin City can bring it to you as we continue our road towards getting SPIKED next month! TEMPTATION! Two Weeks Away! From the Qwest Arena in Boise, Idaho (WHAT? IDAHO? YOU DA HOE) For the SCCW Universal Championship! BloodAngel Vs. "The New Life" Jonathan Rhine   SCCW SIN ON SPIKE -- NEXT MONTH! From the United Center in Chicago, Illinois! Featuring the finals of the 2008 Elliott Rollins Memorial LiveWire Tournament!
