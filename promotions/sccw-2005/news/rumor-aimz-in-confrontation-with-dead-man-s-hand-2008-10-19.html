Rumor: Aimz In Confrontation with Dead Man's Hand

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Burlington, VT While on-screen the Dead Man's Hand and the SCCW Universal Champion have seemed... "tighter than ever," shall we say... off-screen rumors abound regarding a conflict between Amy "Aimz" Campbell and Drusilla "Malice" Devonshire following SCCW's record-breaking trip to the University of Vermont. "I saw the two redheads yelling out in the parking lot," one fan told SCCW.com. "Couldn't hear anything, but, you know, I heard that Alex Pierce and that Malice chick are an item, so maybe that kiss went a little far." The "kiss" the fan refers to occurred right before the leader of the Dead Man's Hand was scheduled to team with the #1 contender to Campbell's Universal Championship, Lance Marshall at Temptation, and it has spurred on reports concerning how far into bed the Red Raver is with the Hand. "Ridiculous rumormongering from a public that cannot handle two powerful women being any sort of friends -- and 'friends' is all that Aimz and Ms. Pierce are," Shweta Kallemullah told SCCW.com via telephone. "There is no problem at all between the Dead Man's Hand and the Superversal Champion. It's my understanding that Aimz and Drusilla were just discussing where they stood as we try to strengthen ties with the most dominant champion in Sin City history." But not so said another source, speaking on condition of anonymity. "Dru is mad. Like, really mad. I don't know what's happening between Amy and Alex, but there's only so much space in the Hand's hierarchy, and there are a lot of women who'd kill to be that close to the Director." With Campbell and Pierce scheduled to team this week on Temptation (10 PM Eastern, Cinemax) in a five-on-two handicapped match against the Decency Crusade, it will be interesting to see the repercussions of that moment as Sin City moves towards Defiance II.
