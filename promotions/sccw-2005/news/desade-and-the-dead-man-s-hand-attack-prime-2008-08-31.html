Desade and the Dead Man's Hand attack PRIME!

31 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dateline: Wednesday, August 28, 2008 - Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania In a stunning turn of events, one week after PRIME's 5-Star Champion Tyler Rayne and former PTC Unified Champion Lindsay Troy came to SCCW Temptation and confronted Desade, the Director has responded with one of the most vicious acts of interfed terrorism this reporter has ever seen. On the 170th edition of PRIME's ReVolution broadcast, Desade led the entire Dead Man's Hand -- all twelve of them -- to Pittsburgh to make a statement to Troy and Rayne and PRIME Executive Producer C.P. Cantrell about what happens when you cross the Dead Man's Hand. What followed was an attack by Mayhem, clearly orchestrated by Pierce, that is sure to enrage the PRIME faithful and may bring down their vengeance upon all of SCCW. See the full transcript of ReVolution 170, including the Dead Man's Hand's attack by clicking here! When asked for comment, new SCCW CEO Dr Nigel Kensington had this to say. "We at the Kent Fusiliers and Sin City Championship Wrestling categorically decry the actions taken by our employees in Pittsburgh. We had no involvement, no recourse, and were never informed of Alexandra Pierce's reprehensible plans for the evening. We can only assure our brethren at PRIME Entertainment that we will take whatever steps are possible to deal with Alexandra's people." This may prove difficult, as the Monet Compact signed by previous SCCW Owner Smitty T. Duluth specifically denies the management of Sin City even the option to act against Desade in any official capacity. So it may be the wrestlers of the company -- and the wrestlers from PRIME -- who have to bring the Dead Man's Hand to justice. We'll have more on this story as it develops.
