Tempe-Tation Stirs Controversy as We Head South of the Border!

21 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the telecast of SCCW Tempe-Tation was interrupted by Cinemax to air a movie, questions have been raised about SCCW's future. Will they be answered as the federation travels south of the border to Monterrey? Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION: A Whole New World! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, June 28, 2009 from Arena Monterrey in Monterrey, Mexico! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! Logic & Dusk Vs. DNA (Desade & Aimz) Vs. Los Luchadores (Diablo Verde y El Gran Caballaro) © Phillip Kennedy may have just gotten one over on the Director! In Tempe, he convinced Nigel Kensington to add a third team to the Strength in Numbers Title match! The massive Thinking Man's Grappler will team with the Lost Soul, as they attempt to keep Amy Campbell from taking her closest ally into the Dome of Doom! Can Logic and Dusk, in their first chance to team together, become Sin City's champions? And who is El Gran Caballaro? Two more berths in the Dome could be decided in Monterrey! The Lion and the Lamb vs. Douchebag, Inc.! "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes & "The Lion" Lance Marshall Vs. "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors & "The River Rat" Lane Stevens Last week, Lance Marshall and Jared Sykes tore the house down in Tempe for the right to go to the Dome of Doom! Now they'll be teaming up against a pairing that has "oh my god I hate you" written all over it! Lane Stevens and Wyatt Connors traded insults in Provo, and the Wise Guy's history with Lane's boss is a storied one! Can these two teams even cooperate long enough to succeed? And with Sykes' upcoming shot at the Elite Championship could mean he's in the ring with his SOS opponent! It's a barnburner of a tag match in Mexico! The Mexican Stand-Off! Open Invitational Over-The-Top-Rope Challenge! It's our first-ever trip south of the border and we want you along! If you can read this, you're invited to come on down to Monterrey and compete for one of those prizes that SCCW is known for! Which Sinners will take Nigel Kensington up on his offer? In a federation that has previously given game-changers of a prize, what will the Fusiliers have to offer in this one? And will anyone from PRIME or GCW or any of the other federations take a shot at the whole of Sin City? Singles Challenge! Mitchell Quinlan Vs. Friar At Homecoming, Friar made Mitchell Quinlan tap out – but it was seconds too late and, Quinlan says, only because of the mysterious appearance of the Man in White! After their brawl in Tempe, the Kent Fusiliers have determined to settle things the old fashioned way – in the ring! Can Quinlan put the truth to his words, or could the Hellhound claim his first SCCW victory? And after the cake-tastrophe in Tempe with Jadian Bridden, could the returned Ace have a thing or two to say about the outcome of this match?   SCCW Temptation at the Fairgrounds! Two Weeks Away! From the New Mexico State Fairgrounds in Albuquerque, NM! For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Spacely Vs. "The Rapmaster of Rap" Rick Malloy ©   SCCW SIN ON SPIKE 3: THE DOME OF DOOM! Three Weeks Away! From the Arena-Auditorium in Laramie, WY DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! The Dome of Doom Match for EVERY CHAMPIONSHIP IN SIN CITY! Aimz (SCCW Universal Champion) Vs. "The Las Vegas Lariat" Phillip Kennedy (SCCW Gateway Champion) Vs. "The Rapmaster of Rap" Rick Malloy (SCCW LiveWire Champion)* Vs. Los Luchadores (SCCW Strength in Numbers Champions)* Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall * pending title defenses prior to SIN on SPIKE For the Elite Championship! Jared Sykes Vs. "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors If Stevens loses, he'll stand aside for Dusk vs. Desade! "The River Rat" Lane Stevens Vs. "The Lost Soul" Dusk
