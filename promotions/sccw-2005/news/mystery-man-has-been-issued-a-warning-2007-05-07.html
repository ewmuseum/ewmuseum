Mystery Man Has Been Issued A Warning

7 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the beating that a FUSE fan received on Killzone 37 at the hands of, the media labelled, “Mystery Man”, FUSE management has warned this man to stay clear of Killzone 38 and 39. When the fan was informed of this news, he had this to say, “It can only be good news for everyone involved with FUSE. If this mystery man is who I think he is, then no one will be happy to see him there. He is crazy, as everyone saw by what he did to me.” When asked about who the mystery man is, the fan could only say this, “Do you really think I’m going to say who he is? I’m not prepared to take another beating. For me this whole matter is over with.” Shortly after being warned away from Killzone though, FUSE received a letter from this man which said this: “I will adhere to the wishes of FUSE management to stay away from Killzone 38, although I shall be present for Killzone 39. However, I have my own warning to FUSE management that I am nobody’s puppet and that I will not be controlled, and if you attempt to control me then what has happened already will have been nothing compared to what will happen. With that said, I look forward to Killzone 39 and beyond.”
