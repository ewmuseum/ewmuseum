Kensington: Dusk Banned from Temptation: Tennessee

25 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dr. Nigel Kensington today made a blockbuster announcement that could leave the backstage area of Temptation: Tennessee a little less tense for this week's show. Speaking to a handful of reporters as he was leaving SCCW Headquarters this morning, Dr. Kensington informed the world that SCCW Superstar Dusk would not be attending the upcoming show, but not of his own free will. "We have considerations to keep in mind here as it pertains to Master Maloof. Not only is the schedule that he keeps a brutal one, but due to his continued unresolved issues with Alexandra Pierce the safety and welfare for one of SCCW's more beloved superstars is our utmost concern. Mrs. Pierce put a bounty on the head of one of our superstars and we're going to do everything in our power to see that Dusk is not injured in the least bit as a result of that." Dr. Kensington, who signed Dusk after a successful match at Defiance II a few months prior, also felt that because of the magnitude of the upcoming Phillip Kennedy vs. The Illustrious Face Eater vs. Dusk show that he had an obligation to the fans and investors of SCCW to ensure that the match went on without a hitch. He assured that it wasn't anything that Dusk had done directly to warrant him being banned from the arena. Attempts to reach out to Dusk were unsuccessful as his spokesperson informed us that he was currently preparing for an upcoming appearance with the fWo, who made their return to the world in early January. Of course, as everyone knows, Dusk isn't one to take something like this lying down, and even if he's not there at Temptation: Tennessee, you have to imagine that his presence will still be felt in some form or fashion. Unfortunately, whatever that might be is still in the dark to the rest of us. We do know though that the Lost Soul himself will be appearing at the upcoming Temptation: Kansas for his second round No Limit tournament. According to various sources, Dusk has amped up his training regiment since the beginning of the No Limit tournament, and even more so lately. More details as they become available.
