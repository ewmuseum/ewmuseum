Temptation is on the air!

3 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Up there. Click on Temptation. See one of the biggest upsets in SCCW history. Then look at the Temptation: Burlington lineup. Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly bring to you... TEMPTATION! L-I-V-E LIVE Monday, October 6, 2008 from Patrick Gym in Burlington, Vermont and exclusively on Cinemax! MAIN EVENT! Non-Title Champions Showcase! Special Guest Referee: "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling (SCCW Gateway Champion) Vs. "The Red Raver" Amy Campbell (SCCW Universal Champion) One week after suffering a pinfall loss to Jared Sykes, Aimz steps into the ring in the second-ever Champions Showcase match! Sean Sterling can earn a real shot at the Universal Champion with just four defenses of his Gateway Championship, but he'll get to test his mettle against the Red Raver a bit earlier than that -- if the referee in this bout can be convinced to be fair. But with the Big Stack and the Third Revelation on a collision course, can Sean Sterling upend the SCCW Universal Champion and bring her loss streak to two? Or will the Second Coming receive his first screwing? Strange Bedfellows Match! "The Lion" Lance Marshall & "Peerless" Hunter Sabuani Vs. "The Elder Statesman" Reginald V. Lovecraft & Hiro Saotome It was just a few weeks ago that Reginald Lovecraft and his partner stole a win from Hiro Saotome -- now the two of them will be forced to team against possibly the most dynamic (or explosive!) team in Sin City history! The past between Lance Marshall and Hunter Sabuani is tumultuous at best, but now that Sabuani is not filming Peer Pressure, will he capitulate to it and team with the #1 Contender to the Universal Champion? And will this match finally -- finally -- tell us where the Raja and the Dead Man's Hand stand? For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Jared Sykes (c) Vs. Charley "Cookie" Crisp King Blueberry was former NWC J-Crown Champion Jared Sykes! Now Sykes, fresh off possibly the biggest win in his career after pinning the Universal Champion, will step into the ring with another young buck, the returning Cookie! After making his intentions known, will Charley be able to vanquish a man who seems to share his goals? Who will leave Vermont as the LiveWire Champion? Bounce-Back Match! Austin "The Wombat" King Vs. Dualta Sheridan Yes, these two have fought before. But this time, it's Dualta looking to bounce back after a surprising non-appearance for the scheduled Sheridan-Josh bout. And it's Austin King, right? Austin King, who everyone steps on. Austin King, who Sheridan knocked out a few weeks ago. What could go wrong, right? Triple-Threat Debut Match! Clayton Byrd Vs. "The Future" Gregory Orion Daniels Vs. Malik Roland Last week, we saw Clayton Byrd get signed by the Kent Fusiliers with the express purpose of cleaning up Sin City! This week, we'll see how well the newcomer handles himself in the ring! In the ring with him will be the self-proclaimed Future and the mammoth Malik Roland! Can Byrd pull it out in Burlington, is the Future now, or will Roland roll? And given Byrd's mistreatment of the Raja of Roguishness, will Hunter Sabuani have something to say about this whole matter?   SCCW Temptation! Two Weeks Away! From the DCU Center in Worcester, Massachusetts! The SCCW Strength in Numbers and Gateway Championships WILL be on the line! Coming Next Month! From the Nassau Coliseum in Long Island, New York!
