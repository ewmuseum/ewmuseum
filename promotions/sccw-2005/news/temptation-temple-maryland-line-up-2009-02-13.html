Temptation: Temple (+ Maryland Line-Up)

13 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION! L-I-V-E LIVE Friday, February 20, 2009 from the Comcast Center on the Campus of the University of Maryland in College Park! With the No Limit Tournament now open for signups, SCCW is on a collision course with change – and it all starts here! MAIN EVENT! Non-Title Match! Aimz and Katsidy barred from ringside! “The Big Stack” Phillip Kennedy Vs. Desade It’s a battle we never thought we’d see – Dead Man’s Hand member vs. Dead Man’s Hand member! Phillip Kennedy wants to see the old Spider in the Web, the one who nearly put a stake through the heart of Sin City, the woman who helped end Jonathan Rhine’s career in Ottawa! Will the Gateway Champion get what he wants as he wrestles at his alma mater? Or will the Gateway Champion be able to do the one thing that no man or woman has been able to do in over a year of terror – pin the Director’s shoulders to the mat? To say it’s an intriguing match is an understatement. A Seeding Match for No Limit! “The Lost Soul” Dusk Vs. “The False Prophet” Wyatt Connors (w/the Decency Crusade) Wyatt Connors is the only man who’s beaten Amy Campbell since the Universal Champion regained her title at All In, and now, he’ll have a chance to name his own seeding in the No Limit tournament for Campbell’s title! All he’ll have to do is beat Dusk, but you can ask no fewer than six of Sin City’s superstars how hard it is to put the Lost Soul down for the count! But after Dusk’s victories over the Dead Man’s Hand and Legion are nothing compared to a shot at the Elite Champion! Who will get to decide where they sit in the tournament? #1 Contenders Match for the LiveWire Championship! Legion Vs. Paul Searle Though Legion came up just short against Dusk last week at Temptation: Temple, he’s impressed the SCCW higher-ups enough that the Devil’s Reject may have a chance to compete against Jadian Bridden for the LiveWire title soon – all he’ll have to do is beat the not-quite-a-rookie-anymore Paul Searle, who has his own axe to grind with the Dead Man’s Hand’s Ace – thanks to those brass knuckles. A Money Where Your Mouth Is Match! Ash Plato Vs. Kelly "Machine" Masters Ash Plato started his Sin City career with an impressive victory over “Remarkable” Chase Harper (and won the 56th round of the Infinite Gauntlet!). Now Plato will come to College Park to take on the man they call the Machine, Kelly Masters! The video aired on Masters’ behalf claims that Wrestling is Coming to Sin City – he’ll have his chance to prove it against Ash Plato in Maryland! Plus! With Dr. Kensington’s announcement that Aimz’ Universal Championship will be on the line throughout the No Limit Tournament, what kinds of names will test the waters in Sin City for a shot at the richest prize in the sport? And what will the Red Raver herself have to say about the steep set of odds she’s been set against? Find out in Maryland! SCCW TEMPTATION! Two weeks away! From the WVU Coliseum on the campus of West Virginia University in Morgantown! See the full field of the No Limit Tournament coalesce in the Mountaineer State!
