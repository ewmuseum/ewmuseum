Tempe-Tation Preemption Sparks Rumors About SCCW Finances

22 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempe, Arizona – After Cinemax preempted the conclusion of last Tuesday's Temptation broadcast from Wells Fargo Arena, rumors are swirling about the federation's financial solidity. These rumors picked up steam this week from Cinemax's home offices in New York City, up to an including the federation owing the network's parent company, Time-Warner, a great deal of money, used to subsidize the increased salary load for the federation after rebranding from FUSE Wrestling to Sin City Championship Wrestling. The services of such stars as Lance Marshall, Craig Maloof, and Wyatt Connors come at a premium, and the dirtsheets were whirling with rumors of Amy Campbell's contract being renegotiated after the departure of SCCW mainstay Jonathan Rhine over the fall of last year. Neither Sin City's parent company, the Kent Fusiliers, nor its on-air president, Dr. Nigel Kensington, had a comment on the matter, except to encourage fans to tune in to SCCW's trip south of the border on June 28th. More on this story as it develops.
