Aimz On Fatal Factions

10 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy 'Aimz' Campbell offered a phone interview to the FUSE webmasters when approached about her team's newly reaquired titles and the stacked card set for KillZone 41. It was surprising to find that Aimz was actually on vacation, as opposed to training for her title match against the RPG, Cowboys From Hell and self-professed 'frenemies' The Alliance. ''I'm in the Carribean right now with Darcy, relaxing. We've fought without a moment to rest through all of the last few weeks, and it felt like we've been doing press whenever we haven't been in the ring,'' she explained, ''So instead of risking stress injuries by keeping up the hardcore training, it's way better for our bodies and our chances in this cluster match if we get a little rest.'' But what of the rumors that a wedding has been planned for the engaged couple? Amy could only chuckle, ''Nobody wants to hear about that. Ask me about the titles.'' We couldn't argue. What are her opinions about so called 'hot-potato' title reigns? ''Not this time,'' she assures, ''As much as it's fun to have a revolving championship sometimes, the purpose of having a title is showing everyone that you're the best. Why would we try for anything less than victory just because people think it's neat to have a lot of different names up on the baord?'' She continued with a trademark, but somewhat demure dig at rival federations, ''FUSE is far removed from every other company in the sense that talent is what gets you somewhere. Our guys have stacked talent in every division - that's why you tend to see a lot of title changes. This isn't just another Joe-Blow federation where you get these monster title reigns when some overblown hype machine gets thrown into a bunch of easy matches because the bookers are their buddies and wanna make them look good. Anyway, I'm just going out the door, but there's one last thing...'' And that would be? ''Good luck to everyone in the opener of KillZone, and feel free to come to our hotel room after the show to help re-polish our titles.
