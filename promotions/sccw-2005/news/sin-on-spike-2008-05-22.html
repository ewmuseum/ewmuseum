SIN on SPIKE

22 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the Desk of Smitty T. Duluth For Immediate Release Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) proudly brings to the inaugural edition of SCCW SIN on SPIKE! Monday, May 26, 2008 at 10PM/9PM Central (right after the network television premiere of Robert Rodriguez' "Sin City" at 7:30!). Due to contractual obligations with SpikeTV, online transcript will not be available for release until May 30th. From the United Center in Chicago, Illinois! MAIN EVENT! Tag Team Action! Lance Marshall and BloodAngel (w/Alanna Marshall) Vs. Desade and SCCW Universal Champion Aimz[/align] This one is gonna be... we'll call it "interesting"! On one side, we have two (three, if you count Alanna) of the most straightforward, upright individuals in Sin City. They'll face two (like there's only going to be two) of the lousiest people (their gender leads me towards words I cannot put in print) in the history of professional wrestling. Even after the charade they put on in Minneapolis, who knows if Desade can wrestle? And after the end of the show, will Ed Lambert finally abandon his unwillingness to hit girls -- if he can even wrestle to start with? For the Sin City Championship Wrestling Gateway Championship! Charley Crisp Vs. Mayhem (w/Magnum Opus) (c) Charley Crisp didn't have a good night in Minneapolis. First, Chris Bagwell showed up and Kiljaden Kincaid put the Cookie Monster down with a double underhook suplex -- a move that arguably led to his loss at the hands of Bishop! But, while it is not my cup of tea, you must admire a man who is fully able to admire the size of a man's genitalia while seconds away from getting his rear end handed to him by the same man, and I believe that deserves a reward. That reward? A shot at the SCCW Gateway Championship -- the fact that it comes against the man who did the beating (and with the penis)? Just a bonus. The Finals of the Elliott Rollins Memorial LiveWire Title Tournament! For the Sin City Championship Wrestling LiveWire Championship! Wyatt Connors Must Be Beaten Twice! "The Biloxi Beast" Bishop (w/Sarah Riley) Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors (w/Jeremiah) Bishop performed quite a feat last week -- defeating Charley Crisp and Vassago to earn himself a shot at the LiveWire Championship! Now the Biloxi Beast will have to win twice more if he wants the LiveWire championship! His opponent? The squirrelly bastard who is the cause of this tournament to start with, Wyatt Connors, fresh off a week of getting threatened and beat up by Jonathan Rhine and Amy Campbell! We'll finally have a new LiveWire Champion when we leave Chicago, and they're likely to be an asshole! For the Sin City Championship Wrestling Strength in Numbers Championships! "The Modern Day Warrior" Jay Draven & "The Punk Rock Pixie" Charlotte Ramone (w/Krissy Jamison) Vs. Andre Jensen & Sameer al-Jezuri (c) Charlotte Ramone and Jay Draven cannot seem to get along; Sameer al-Jezuri holds some hate in his heart for his partner after AJ abandoned Sameer to the tender mercies of the United States Marines after their ill-fated attempt to blow up the military base at Snake Eyes! Which team can get over their ego and work together? I bet they're the ones who leave the Windy City with the Strength in Numbers Championships! Inter-Promotional Challenge Match (Part Deux): Hardcore Rules Match! Chris Storm (of Banned & Exiled~!) Vs. Maddox (of the Macabre Militia) This war continues to boil over! After Maddox stole a win over Markus Stone in Minneapolis thanks to the unveiling of Deepthroat, the leader of the Macabre Militia has challenged the Rock Superstar to a one-on-one encounter on SpikeTV! Will things finally come to a head between the Militia and the yearlong GCW Tag Team Champions, or is this just another battle to Armageddon? SIN Challenge Match! The Den of Iniquity (Vassago & Cleopatra) Vs. The Midnight Marauders (Logan Fox & Dominick Berelli)[/align] The Strength in Numbers division is getting some new friends -- Logan Fox and Dominick Berelli roll into Chi-Town to whack... the last thing that a metaphor like this needs, as the Den of Iniquity are downright creeptastic! Will the new team of Fox & Berelli be able to continue the downward spiral of the Den, who couldn't quite sneak a way into the LiveWire finals? Three-Way Wackiness! "The Canadian Classic" Tina Davis Vs. "The Big Stack" Philip Kennedy Vs. Simon King What? Who are these guys? You may remember Philip Kennedy from our last couple shows, but Tina Davis and Simon King are new to Sin City! We will welcome them with open arms...aw, who are we kidding, we'll put them in the same ring and make them fight! A poker player, a Canadian chick isn't a hosebeast like our champion, and some guy named Simon... see them in Chicago! Plus! - With PRIME holding their UltraViolence pay-per-view in Chicago the day before SIN on SPIKE, will we see any of the PRIMEates at the United Center? - We promise at least one thing that will make you go "WHOA!" Guarantee it, actually.
