International Press Story on Pirating Incident on FUSE Uproar

28 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
International Press (November 26, 2007): A spokesperson for Smitty T. Duluth and the FUSE Wrestling front office issued the following statement about the troubling signal interruption that occurred prior to the scheduled Timo Bolamba/Damien Cruz bout this past weekend on their flagship "Uproar on Showtime" program. "We deeply regret the interruption to our signal and hope that the footage there did not disturb any young viewers," the statement reads. "We want to assure the viewing public we are conducting a thorough internal investigation as to precisely how this occurred. FUSE Wrestling takes all instances of possible broadcast signal intrusion quite seriously. We will cooperate with the FCC and all federal authorities in their own investigation of the matter, as well." The incident, which featured disturbing footage of a longhaired individual in what appeared to be a hospital or an asylum, set to music by the band Nine Inch Nails, was not scheduled, claimed the company's spokesperson, Harold Francis. "No," Francis said. "Absolutely not. All FUSE programming is rigorously timed, and this caused us to have to juggle things around at the very literal last minute." If this were a case of broadcast signal intrusion (popularly referred to as "pirating" a feed), it would be the first in the United States since November 22, 1987, just over twenty years to the day before the most recent FUSE Uproar aired. Then, a man wearing a Max Headroom mask interrupted the feeds for two separate local Chicago TV stations the same night. Authorities have never caught the man in the Max Headroom mask, but Francis is making a bolder prediction for last night's signal pirate. "It won't happen again," Francis said. "Whoever this guy is, we'll catch him. I have no doubt of that. You don't cut into a major network's feed twice, nor do you do it without getting caught." FUSE Wrestling is scheduled to air its "DEFIANCE" pay-per-view December 7th at the Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana. Further information can be found on the company's website, FUSE.com.
