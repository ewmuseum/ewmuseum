The Cataclysm is Almost Upon Us!

13 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling and PRIME Entertainment, in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Prometheus, LLC Proudly Bring to You! CATACLYSM: PRIME VS. SCCW! LIVE! Wednesday, September 17, 2008! From the TD Banknorth Garden in Boston, Massachusetts! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! Universal Champion vs. Universal Champion! "The Red Raver" Aimz (SCCW) Vs. "The Model Citizen" Chandler Tsonda (PRIME) He is the PRIME Universal Champion, the man who defeated Devin Shakur at Colossus V, the Jewel in the Crown. She is the two-time SCCW Universal Champion, and the woman who holds the dubious honor of putting Jonathan Rhine out of professional wrestling. They are two of the most skilled, most egotistical champions in these federations' histories, and they will step into the ring with nothing but bragging rights on the line. Who is the better champion? Who will get the last laugh? It promises to be a wrestling clinic in one of the most anticipated matches in PTC's history. Elimination Rules! The Trojan Legion (PRIME) "The Queen of the Ring" Lindsay Troy "The Underground Pimp" Tyler Rayne "The Risen Star" Nova "The Bama Bruiser" Wade Elliott Vs. The Dead Man's Hand (SCCW) "The Spider in the Web" Desade "The Path of Destruction" Mayhem Jadian Bridden And Xavier Kannon (PRIME) Speaking of anticipation... it all comes down to this. For months, Desade has orchestrated attack after attack on Lindsay Troy through intermediaries such as Cozen, Savant, and more. Now, the Queen of the Ring has put together an all-star cast of people to finally, once and for all, end this war with Alex Pierce. Tyler Rayne wants a piece of Mayhem after the Path of Destruction's heinous attack on Angelica Brooks! And Xavier Kannon has turned his back on his federation to stand with one of the most vicious women in the history of this business. These stories and so much more will be on display in Boston -- Tsonda v. Aimz may be about bragging rights, but this is about revenge. LiveWire Champion vs. Intense Champion! "The Next in Line" Kaiser Vashaun (PRIME) Vs. King Blueberry (SCCW) One week after his stunning upset victory to win the LiveWire Champion, the so-called Admiral of Antioxidants will face the man who takes Intense to a whole new level, Kaiser Vashaun! Vashaun has been dominant in his time in PRIME, how will he take to facing a man dressed as a fruit? It's a clash of styles as only the Cataclysm can deliver, as the wacky meets the punishing -- and two of the best young talents in the PTC square off! Tag Team Champions Collide! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy & "The Elder Statesman" Reginald P. Lovecraft (w/Katsidy) (SCCW) Vs. Team V.I.A.G.R.A. (High Flyer & Tony Davis) (w/Mary-Lynn Mayweather) (PRIME) The first of three champion vs. champion matches will pit Davis & Flyer, the dominating PRIME Tag Team Champions against Phillip Kennedy and Reginald Lovecraft of the Dead Man's Hand, the SCCW Strength in Numbers Champions, in a bout that's quickly become personal, escalating up after Phillip Kennedy hit the Nuts on Mary-Lynn Mayweather! Davis and Flyer have been teaming for years, while this will be Kennedy & Lovecraft's first match as a team! What tricks will the Hand bring to the table? The finest tag-team action in professional wrestling will be on display in Boston! The Emo and the Lion! "The Man in Black" Devin Shakur (PRIME) Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall (SCCW) The two most recent men to hold their federation's highest championship will face off one-on-one in another star match in what's sure to be the most memorable card of the year! Devin Shakur's taken a couple of solid potshots at Lance Marshall, once with a bat and once with a bell! With the Lion spitting for revenge, how will Commie Eeyore react? The top of the card says the current Universal Champions will face off, how about this bout between the last two men to hold those belts? War Makes Strange Friends, War Makes Strange Enemies "The PRIME Choice" Killean Sirrajin & Troy Douglas (PRIME) Vs. BloodAngel & "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors (SCCW) On one side, you've got a PRIME Hall of Famer and the former Intense Champion, a team for the ages. On the other, two men who couldn't get along before the recent change to the #1 Contender to the Elite Championship! Can Edward Lambert and Wyatt Connors coexist, or are they doomed to go down at the hands of the young stud and the multiple-time Universal champion? Twenty-Man Battle Royal Action! Featuring Adam Calloway, Andre Jensen, Austin King, Simon King, Josh, Elliott Rollins, Hiro Saotome, Paul Searle, Dualta Sheridan, and Sean Sterling of SCCW and Jimmy Bonafide, Hank Cobb, The Codemaster, Bryan Dawkins, Dusk, Enemigo I, Jason Natas, Christian Novak, Connor O'Reily, and Union Jack of PRIME! Not a single superstar will go untested! Ten men will be in the ring at one time, and while the names are on PRIME's side, that won't bring SCCW down! What will the winner get? We know, but we're not telling you!
