Killzone 32 Airs!

22 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On the latest edition of Killzone there were many questions to be answered. Would Frost explain his actions from the end of Killzone 31? What would Clinton Sage have in store for John Covel? What would Timo do after taking a chair shot to the face from Dave Gibson? Who will IKD pick for his first match in FUSE? Those questions were answered, and as always more questions popped up. The action was intense all night as Mikael Lundgard stepped into the ring with Bobino, Cyrus Raynes had his first official singles match up with FUSE when he took on the debuting Damien Ryan, The Legacy would finally get a shot to step back into the ring and this time it was against Kouzoujoku, and The Alliance made their long awaited Fatal Faction debut when they took on the newly formed team of Connor Chesney and The Martian and also Aimz took on one half of the former Fatal Faction Champions, Captian Bluto Drake, in singles competition. But that wasn't all. Nope, far from it. We also saw John Covel take on Affliction member, and Clinton Sage's right hand man, Lane Stevens in a non-title contest. Would Covel send a message to the PTC Global Champion or did Stevens finally get the big win he's been looking for in FUSE to finally start skyrocketing his own career? Then in the main event Tanaka took on Jacob McKail and this one had a wild ending.
