Aimz Comments on Wyatt Colton, Calls CFH "Weenies"

11 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shortly after Wyatt's appearance on DDT radio, Amy 'Aimz' Campbell called in. Never one to be overshadowed in the attention department, especially when it comes to who's on the front page of FUSE's website, she had plenty to say about the man who attacked her from behind on KillZone 38. ''That jackoff and his life partner are preparing for a loss, pointe blank. You can always tell when someone's working their PR machine for an oncoming defeat, because they'll immediately make excuses and try demeaning the purpose of the match. Trying to lessen the importance of the Fatal Faction titles gives Colton's reputation a soft place to land after he falls flat on his ass this week.'' She made no bones about countering Wyatt's innuendo, either. ''He can talk all he wants about how 'It's not like...' this and that, but all the sexual comments and bullshit prancing around the topic won't take away from the fact that it took TWO men, attacking me from behind and in the dark to do any actual damage. If they'd bothered facing me, we wouldn't have an issue right now... because you can't do radio interviews with your jaw wired shut, no matter how long you've lived on Brokeback Mountain.'' When asked about the challenge she'd laid out to the Cowboys From Hell, Aimz responded simply by saying, ''I threw down the gauntlet, face-to-face with both of those cowards staring me straight in the eye. If they can beat Darcy and I in a non-title match, then they'll have earned the right to call us out wherever and whenever they want a real shot. If the titles were as meaningless as Colton claims, those f**kbags wouldn't have been nearly as eager to accept that challenge.'' And what if the titles aren't hers to offer shots at after KillZone 41's four-way brawl? ''That's a non-issue. We'll walk in with the belts, and they can wave at us when we walk out with the belts. They'll be sorely mistaken if they don't think that Darcy Crisis and Aimz will be calling the shots for a long time to come, and a thousand cowardly attacks on an unarmed woman won't change that fact. They can feel free to cheap-shot me again, because it's only gonna keep those two farther and farther from the titles.'' At the end of the call, Ethan Frost's response to Colton's tirade were brought to her attention and Aimz offered a simple response: ''I like Frost, I love Fusion... but if Ethan thinks FUSE doesn't have any decent teams, I'll give him an open invitation to our titles. Any time, any place.'' With the titles on the line at this week's KillZone in College Park, Aimz, Crisis and the Cowboys have their work cut out for them. Tune in to see if Colton's tough-talk will turn over Campbell's titles!
