After the Cataclysm...

25 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now that Cataclysm is behind us, what happens next for SCCW? What happens next is the continued kicking of ass! Sin City Championship Wrestling in association with the Kent Fusiliers Proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION! L-I-V-E LIVE! Monday, September 29, 2008 and exclusively on Cinemax! From the Verizon Wireless Arena in Manchester, New Hampshire! MAIN EVENT! NWC Stars Collide! "Mr. Nice Guy" Hiro Saotome Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors (w/The Crusade) Wyatt Connors has been on a steamroll since he came to Sin City, with only losses to Mayhem in the Heaven & Hell and a loss to Elliott Rollins on his record. Now he'll step into the ring, surprisingly, for the first time in his professional career against the best friend of the man he won a match and got in an altercation with at Cataclysm. Hiro Saotome's return to the sport has been a bumpy road, and now he'll have to face the former LiveWire Champion in the main event in Manchester! "The Path of Destruction" Mayhem (w/Malice) Vs. Paul Searle On paper, this is a huge mismatch. Paul Searle has wrestled two matches in his entire life, and just survived a battle royal with nineteen of the top stars in both PRIME and SCCW. Mayhem, the former SCCW Gateway Champion, has hit a wall -- well, he hit a wall several times at Cataclysm, before being put on his back with Tyler Rayne's Flying Fornication. So... Mayhem is just going to destroy Paul, right? That's why we wrestle the matches in the ring and not on paper. Tune in for either a sad, sad day for the Searle family or the biggest upset in Sin City history! Youth on a Roll! "American Youth" Josh Vs. Dualta Sheridan The Roll of Dualta apparently only goes as far as singles matches, but he'll be sorely tested here, as he'll face the man who, by all rights, could be the LiveWire Champion twice-over. After a controversial loss one-on-one to Charlotte Ramone (and, boy, do all those screwy wins for Ramone make sense now or what?), Josh was on the winning team but just not the winning wrestler at the Windfall match. They say the future of the American Youth is bright, but Sheridan's yet to be pinned in Sin City, having just lost a pair of "get thrown out" matches.   Caravan Vs. Austin "The Wombat" King Uh? What? Why are there two guys who have never won a match wrestling each other? Does this mean that the BIG TRUCK -- sorry, that Wyatt Connors' newest recruit, Brother Macarthur Deesul -- or that the Wombat will finally earn their first win? Or is there something else going on here? PLUS! - Aimz and the Dead Man's Hand swept through Cataclysm like a pale horse! With all of Alexandra's minions and her... whatever Aimz is to her... scheduled to be in New Hampshire at the orders of Nigel Kensington, surely they'll have something to say. The bigger question is... will anyone want to listen? - Sean Sterling won the 20-Man Interfed Battle Royal to kick off the night in Boston! What will the Third Revelation's prize be -- and will his war with Phillip Kennedy rage on? - After their confrontation with BloodAngel, the Crusade is scheduled to be in Manchester in force with two matches! Will the former World Champion have anything to say? We tend to think so. All this plus the third edition of Peer Pressure and appearances by Lance Marshall, King Blueberry and all your favorite Sinners, only in Manchester! SCCW Temptation! From the Patrick Gym on the campus of the University of Vermont in Burlington! Two Weeks Away! See King Blueberry's first LiveWire Title Defense and Hunter Sabuani in the ring -- and see it in the smallest arena SCCW has ever visited! SCCW returns to pay-per-view in just a few weeks... wait'll you get a load of THIS one.
