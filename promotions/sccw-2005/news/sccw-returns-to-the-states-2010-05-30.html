SCCW Returns to the States!

30 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Final Curtain Entertainment) proudly bring to you... TEMPTATION: Massachusetts Massacre! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, June 13, 2010 at 10PM Eastern/9PM Central and ONLY on Cinemax! From the Mullins Center on the campus of the University of Massachusetts in Amherst! SCCW returns to the United States with a terrible void to fill and a terrible plan on how to fill it! MAIN EVENT! High Rollers Tournament Preview Match! "The Pit Viper" Terrence Kingsley Vs. Jared Sykes To say Jared Sykes will have the home field advantage in Massachusetts is an understatement. To say Terrence Kingsley will not give one iota of a damn is another. Kingsley made a big splash at Defiance with a heartless, mean-spirited beating of former Gateway Champion Spacely – a man Jared considered one of his few good friends in the world. Now Sykes will return to Massachusetts, just miles from his hometown, with a chance to finally get a piece of the Viper – can he make good against the man who pinned Lane Stevens in Manchester? We're told the only way these two can face each other at SIN on SPIKE is in the finals, but the brass ring will be there for the taking – who'll ride the wave into Newark? Tag Team Warfare! "The Lion" Lance Marshall & Mitchell Quinlan Vs. "The Valkyrie" Suzanne Larsson & "Gold Patron Meritorious" Xavier Kannon (w/Eleanor Kannon-Hall) Mitchell Quinlan and Lance Marshall are no strangers to teaming with each other, having faced Julissa Minorez and Jadian Bridden in the run-up to Defiance III, but the stakes are much higher this time around! Quinlan and Suzanne Larsson went to a ten-minute time limit draw in Manchester, and at SIN on SPIKE they'll do it again without the time limit! Xavier Kannon and Lance Marshall have had a series of run-ins since the Lion saved Alex Pierce at Defiance! Will they finally go head-to-head, right before both men will be in the High Rollers Tournament? Can Kannon and Larsson work as a team, or will their individual goals take precedence? Rubber Grudge Match! "The Sovereign Superman" Frank Silver Vs. "The Raging Fear" August Monday August Monday defeated Ryan Gallway in Baghdad, and Mack Brody (subbing for Frank Silver) beat April Monday in Venice! Now, just one card before the Heirs of Wrestling and Daddy Daughter Day finally face off for the Strength in Numbers Championships, Frank and August will go heads-up in an attempt to build that momentum going into Newark! Two of the strongest, nastiest men in Sin City will face-off, but with April Monday and the rest of Heirs of Wrestling scheduled to come to Amherst, can we ever hope to get a clean win? Debuts and Returns versus... Wait, Who? "The Violence" Vince Jones (w/Jasmine) & "The Ace in the Hole" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. "The Apex Predator" James von Doom & "The Devil's Reject" Legion (w/Luci4) One is back, one is mad – former GateWire Champion, Jadian Bridden, returned in Manchester to declare he'd stand at the end of Alexandra Pierce's road, and The One Man Dynasty, Vince Jones has made his return to the main stage after a memorable stint in AWC! They'll team up to face the unlikely team of James von Doom and Legion! Legion defeated Alexandra Pierce's daughter in Manchester, while James von Doom notched another win against Atlas Adams! Can these two put their a longtime rivalry in several promotions across the country aside? Will Vince Jones tolerate the Crown Prince of the Dead Man's Hand's ego? Find out! Plus more details on the extent of the injuries to Lane Stevens and the full brackets for the High Rollers Tournament! SCCW SIN on SPIKE 5: HIGH ROLLERS! L-I-V-E LIVE Friday, June 25, 2010 from the Prudential Center in Newark, NJ! Featuring the one-night, eight person High Rollers Tournament to crown a NEW Universal Champion! Participants: Aimz SCCW Gateway Champion Baron von Blackberry "The Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors "Gold Patron Meritorious" Xavier Kannon "The Pit Viper" Terrence Kingsley "The Lion" Lance Marshall "The Spider in the Web" Alexandra Pierce Jared Sykes For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships!
