CFH Demand Answers

7 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After having their Fatal Faction Championship match go unaired at War In Washington, the Reigning Fatal Faction champions the Cowboys From Hell were looking to get answers. They didn't get any, but ran into a reporter for FUSE.com where they made these comments. "You know, there is a problem here in FUSE." Jake Ennis stated. "When you have the most important team in the history of this piece of shit organization not even make the biggest show in the last two months, you have really got to take a look at it's priorities. We are the Fatal Faction Champions, damn it. We don't get treated like we were just a couple of jabronis that walked off the street." Wyatt Colton jumped in with this. "If FUSE doesn't want to do this division any justice, and give us meaningful competition, we are going to take each and every team that they send after us behind the nearest shed and put them out of their misery." Jake couldn't hold in his angst any longer. "It starts at Killzone. Carson Riley thinks that he's going to defeat me and issue a challenge. Carson, after I'm done with you, you're going to wish that you never set foot in a wrestling ring."
