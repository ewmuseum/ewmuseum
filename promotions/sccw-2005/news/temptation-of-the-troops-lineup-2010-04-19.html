Temptation of the Troops Lineup!

19 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Final Curtain Entertainment) proudly bring to you... The Temptation of the Troops! Airing Sunday, April 25, 2010 from Camp Hammond in Baghdad, Iraq and only on Cinemax! MAIN EVENT! A "Chosen One" Battle Royal! Featuring: Matt Elias (w/Kirsten Pendleton), "Gold Patron Meritorious" Xavier Kannon (w/Eleanor Kannon-Hall), "The Pit Viper" Terrence Kingsley, "The Devil's Reject" Legion (w/Luci4 & Stigmata), Mitchell Quinlan, Frank Silver, Jared Sykes and "The Puppetmaster" James von Doom! Eight of Sin City's most spectacular stars will do battle for the most dubious prize of all – the right to be called Adrienne St. Germain's "Chosen One". What that means, we don't know. The lengths that some of these men will sink to in order to obtain such an uncertain reward is another story entirely. Some of the dastardliest knaves in all of SCCW will compete for whatever edge they may get, and only some of SCCW's most unlikely heroes can stop them. But when ASG is involved, how much of this match is REALLY about wrestling skill? Elite Title Number One Contenders Match! Baron von Blackberry Vs. Vivica J. Valentine One of these two will challenge for the Elite Championship! GCW's "Bulletproof Blonde" will travel all the way to Baghdad to wrestle in the most hostile environment imaginable, as she takes on the man from the lush but terrifying land of Fruitsylvania, the SCCW Gateway Champion, Baron von Blackberry! Can the Gateway Champ upend the multiple-time world champion, or will SCCW see two weeks in a row of GCW making their presence known loud and clear? One of these two moves on to face Brandon Youngblood – but which? Battle of the Bulls! "The Lord High Executioner" Thanatos Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall It is an SCCW tradition – take two of the biggest, strongest men on the roster, stick them in the same ring together, and see what happens! The Dark Lord of Sapientes Gladio was a beast in PRIME's Dual Halo, and came to Tokyo for some high-intensity meetings with Alex Pierce and the Lion! Now, in front of the men and women of the US military, the two largest active competitors in Sin City will go head-to-head, and this time, only the strongest will survive! The Gauntlet Begins Here! "The Spider in the Web" Alexandra Pierce Vs. "The Mexican Minx" Julissa Minorez (w/Jadian Bridden) Alexandra Pierce had a chance to earn a one-on-one match with Lane Stevens – win the main event in Tokyo, a match that has to be seen to be believed! Now that that didn't happen, we're told the River Rat has given her a new option – face each and every member of the Dead Man's Hand first! She'll start in Baghdad with a match with the first-ever LiveWire Champion! What plans will the Hand have for Sin City's Spectre on her quest to get a piece of Lane? Can Pierce run the gauntlet? The road to revenge starts in Iraq! First-Time Ever Encounter! "The Valkyrie" Suzanne Larsson Vs. "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors Suzanne Larsson came to SCCW looking for a challenge, and by God is she going to get one in Baghdad! Wyatt Connors hasn't been in action since losing the Universal Championship in Edmonton, and, to all appearances, wasn't even IN Honolulu for Defiance! Now Connors will come to Iraq facing the only Icelandic superstar in all of the PTC, still desperately seeking that signature win to legitimize herself in the City of Sin! What was Wyatt Connors up to? Can Suzanne Larsson finally get over that hump? A Test of Might, Round One! "The Intergalactic Space Cowboy" Ryan Gallway Vs. "The Raging Fear" August Monday With another win for the Heirs of Wrestling in Tokyo, the Heirs of Wrestling are aimed square at Daddy Daughter Day! Now, in Iraq and then in Italy, the two teams will go one-on-one in an attempt to prove their superiority! First up, the troublesome titan, Ryan Gallway, will face the Raging Fear! Gallway is outsized and outmuscled, but will the Dead Man's Hand's elder statesman outsmart him? The Heirs try to establish their dominance over the champs in Baghdad! SCCW Temptation! May 9, 2010! From Spiaggia del Faro in Venice, Italy! See Amy Campbell in action in Italy! Also: A Test of Might, Round Two! Frank Silver Vs. April Monday SCCW SIN on SPIKE V! Coming this June! For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! The Heirs of Wrestling Vs. Daddy Daughter Day
