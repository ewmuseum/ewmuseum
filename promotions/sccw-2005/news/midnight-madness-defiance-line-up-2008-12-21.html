Midnight Madness! (+ Defiance line-up!)

21 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you: SCCW MIDNIGHT MADNESS: A COUNTDOWN TO DEFIANCE! L-I-V-E LIVE Friday, January 9, 2008 and ONLY on SPIKETV! Sin City returns to the air the day after the New Year, two days before Defiance! MAIN EVENT! Tag-Team Chaos! DNA (Desade & Aimz) Vs. Lance Marshall & Jared Sykes Thanksgiving was a dangerous time to be an enemy to the Dead Man's Hand, as both Jared Sykes and Lance Marshall were among those that felt the wrath of Desade and Aimz! Now, two days before they face off in singles matches, the Infinite Gauntlet holder and the former SCCW Universal Champion get their hands on the so-called Blueprint of Greatness! Will Pierce and Campbell be looking for a victory, or will they be looking to soften up their pay-per-view opponents? Non-Title Double Referee Match! Referees: Jadian Bridden & Paul Searle "Remarkable" Chase Harper Vs. Joaquin Pierre Vs. Charley Crisp The Frenchman is back! The former SCCW Gateway Champion has returned to Sin City and will be jumping right into the mix in a dangerous match with the first double-champion in SCCW history and a highly touted newcomer in Chase Harper!! The LiveWire Champion will have to worry about his Defiance opponents both serving as referee and two men across from him! Does Joaquin still have what it takes to compete with the top talent in the PTC, will Chase Harper shock the world, or will the Kings of Pop pick up another win? Plus! Previews of EVERY Defiance Match! Tune in for the Sin before the Defiant Rise! SCCW DEFIANCE II! LIVE! Sunday, January 11, 2009 and on pay-per-view from the Nassau Coliseum on Long Island, New York! *Card Subject to Change MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Universal Championship! "The Lion" Lance Marshall Vs. A Member of the Dead Man's Hand SCCW SIN on SPIKE 2: SYKE-OUT! Jared Sykes vs. The Universal Champion! February 2009
