SIN ON SPIKE 3 LINEUP!

25 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... SIN ON SPIKE 3: THE DOME OF DOOM! L-I-V-E LIVE Saturday, July 25, 2009 from the Arena-Auditorium on the campus of the University of Wyoming in Laramie! MAIN EVENT: THE DOME OF DOOM! An Enclosed Cage Ladder Match for the SCCW Universal Championship! If Kennedy is pinned, the Gateway Championship changes hands! If Pierce or Campbell is pinned, the Strength in Numbers Title changes hands! If Spacely is pinned, he loses the LiveWire Championship! "The Red Raver" Amy Campbell (SCCW Universal Champion) Vs. "The Las Vegas Lariat" Phillip Kennedy (SCCW Gateway Champion) Vs. "The Spider in the Web" Alexandra Pierce (SCCW SIN Champion) Vs. Spacely (SCCW LiveWire Champion) Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall It's all been building to this. Five of the top athletes in all of professional wrestling will climb inside the confines of the massive Dome of Doom structure, with Amy Campbell's Universal Title belt suspended from the top of the cage! To win, all you need to do is climb up to the belt, retrieve it, and have both feet touch the mat. But the other three championships will also be up for grabs inside the cage – but no one will know who the champions are until the title belt is retrieved! Amy Campbell survived the No Limit Tournament with her title belt in place, but can the champion overcome her two most recent pay-per-view opponents – Marshall and Kennedy – as well as the dynamic "Hulk Hogan of the Heavens," whose meteoric rise may launch him into the stratosphere of SCCW! If she does, Aimz is guaranteed an unprecedented one-year title reign! But the lingering question is: how far can Campbell trust Desade? Witness the first (and perhaps only) Dome of Doom match on Spike TV! For the PTC Elite Championship! "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes Vs. "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors © And to think, this all started with a man dressed in a giant penis costume. Jared Sykes' return to SCCW has been very impressive, and the former(?) King Blueberry now gets a crack at the Wise Guy! Wyatt Connors has seemingly been unstoppable since he beat Steve Knox for that championship in mid-November, claiming victory after victory! Now the Rainmaker faces perhaps his most difficult challenge – a one-on-one war with the most dominant Infinite Gauntlet Champion in many moons! And with a bout with Desade and Hessian at Colossus VI in the offing, will the Murder Show travel all the way to Wyoming? A Last Man Standing Match! If Stevens Loses, He Will Not Intervene in Dusk vs. Desade! "The River Rat" Lane Stevens Vs. "The Lost Soul" Dusk After the no-disqualification war these two men had in Salt Lake City at Homecoming, they'll be going it again in Wyoming! The Lost Soul has made one thing perfectly clear – he wants a piece of the Spider in the Web! But Alex's self-professed lackey will not stand for it, and the Warlord has chosen to go to battle for it! It's Stevens and Dusk beating the holy hell out of each other – the loser is the first man who can't answer the bell! At stake for Dusk, a promised one-on-one bout with Alexandra Pierce with no interference from the River Rat! It's all coming down in Wyoming – will Dusk get what he wants? The First-Ever Ties That Bind Strap Match Winner to be Declared the #1 Contender to the LiveWire Championship! Mitchell Quinlan Vs. Friar Vs. "The Ace" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. Saint Let's make this clear. Jadian Bridden snatched a victory from Saint in the New Orleans native's debut match. Friar knocked over Julissa Minorez. Mitchell Quinlan defeated Friar thanks to Jadian Bridden's assistance. Quinlan eliminated the Ace from the Mexican Standoff – but Friar got his revenge! And in Albuquerque, Saint earned his first victory in SCCW by pinning the Hellhound! Now these four men will be connected at the wrist by a leather strap. First pinfall or submission wins the match – and the right to face Spacely (or whomever leaves the Dome as the LiveWire Champ)! Who will come out on top! And will Saint ever speak? Grudge Match! "Rappin'" Rick Malloy Vs. "Simply Better" Xander Searle These two have been going at it since the man who they say is Simply Better won the right to challenge either the Gateway or LiveWire Champion, culminating in Searle's theft of the LiveWire Championship belt in Monterrey! Xander returned the title at the Fairgrounds – right between the former LiveWire Champ's eyes! Malloy hasn't made an issue of it, but it's clear the shot with that title belt and the earlier-in-the-night assault by Lane Stevens and company might have resulted in Spacely's win of Rick's title! They'll go one-on-one in Wyoming to try to settle the score! All this, plus "It's a DNA Birthday Pah-Tay", hosted by Lane Stevens!
