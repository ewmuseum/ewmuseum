Universal Champion in Barroom Brawl

3 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As reported by various gossip sites, Amy 'Aimz' Campbell was arrested last night in Morgantown, West Virginia after an altercation at a local pub. After an overnight stay, she was released without any charges against her. Dr. Nigel Kensington took to the press this afternoon to let people know that the Universal Champion is in fine fighting shape. ''I am told alcohol may have been a factor in the escalation,'' his statement read. ''But professional reprimand is not considered at this time, as drug testing has been inconclusive and the gentleman involved chose not to take the matter to court. It would be unfair to base repercussions on one side of a story and, as such, this company stands behind its champion. A mistake was made, and I am confident she will ask forgiveness.'' Police have stated that there will be no further investigation into this matter, as the unidentified man involved in the scuffle declined the opportunity to pursue legal avenues. Aimz is still in West Virginia for now, warming up for her tag team bout this Tuesday night on Temptation. She and Desade will face newcomer Seymour Almasy and a mystery partner. Kensington's statement also assured fans that she will be in attendance on Tuesday and take part in the scheduled match as planned. No word on when (or if) an apology will come from Campbell's camp.
