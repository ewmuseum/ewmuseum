Killzone 33 is on the air!!!

30 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killzone airs from the Time Union Center in Albany, New York! What a show it was. There were some great matches, top to bottom, including a couple of surprise non-scheduled bouts, one being for a CHAMPIONSHIP! The show opened with Aimz, one half of the Fatal Faction Champions, taking on Leah Adams. Who walked away from this grudge match with a W? Darcy Crisis, Aimz partner, wasn't without something to do on this night. He had to take on Jacob McKail. Find out what sort of surprise that Crisis had in store for McKail, and FUSE as a whole. Fresh off his first Fatal Faction victory last week, with partner Neil Fracas, Gideonan stepped into the ring to take on debuting Tysom DeBaugh. Did the newcomer pull out the victory on the NEW Legend? Also on tap for the night Clinton Sage took on John Covel for the first time ever. Covel came into the match up, not in the best frame of mind with the passing of his father. How did he fair against the PTC Global Champion? Timo and Dave Gibson squared off in their special Submission Stretcher match up. Which one of these men got the upper hand in their rivalry? All of that but you can't forget the HUGE main event. LiveWire Champion Nathan O'Neill put his title on the line against The Afflictions very own Lane Stevens and the match ended in controversial fashion.
