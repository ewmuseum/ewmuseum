Jake Ennis Standard Match ~The FUSE fans have not had much of a chance to see Carson Riley, but from what the scouts have said, he is most likely going places...and fast. Meanwhile, amongst rumors that the Fatal Faction division is dying, the management wants to see if some of their tag-wrestlers can go it solo. The Cowboys from Hell's Connor Ennis is the first one to give it a go.~ Wyatt Colton Vs. Kid Cool Standard Match ~Wyatt Colton steps into the ring to face off with Cool.~ Jeb Stewart Vs. Ethan Frost Standard Match ~Ethan Frost's career has been up and down in FUSE, and this seems to be another upswing. Coming off his impressive performance in the Tower, he will square off against The Affliction's enforcer, the gigantic Jeb Stewart. Will Ethan continue his good fortunes, or will the mountain of a man prove to be too much in his first career singles contest?~ Mayson Colby & Sean Sterling Vs. Prince Pride & Paul Cain **Strange Bedfellows/#1 Contender for the Livewire Title** ~Two teams composed of rivals square off in a match to determine the new number one contender for the Livewire title. The two winners of the tag match will square off, one-on-one immediantly afterwards, and the winner of that match will get the first shot against new champion, Jason Cruise. How well will these bitter enemies co-exist, if at all?~ **note: This match will be the first ever FUSE/PTC Showcase. After roleplaying has concluded, the roleplays will be directly linked on the PTC forums for public viewing and voting on who they thought won. Step it up, boys. ALSO...
