Killzone 35 Tonight!

11 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight on Killzone sparks are going to fly. With two titles on the line tonight, a match with legendary implications, and a couple more hate filled matches on tap this should prove to be one of the most volatile, and unpredictable, shows in FUSE history. The show will open with Jacob McKail taking on former LiveWire Champion Nathan O’Neill. After having Lane Stevens screw him out of the LiveWire championship two weeks ago Nathan O’Neill will look to begin his climb back up the contenders ladder when he faces McKail. McKail himself is on a hot-streak, as of late, and he’ll look to continue that streak as he keeps his eyes on the ultimate prize, the FUSE Universal Championship. The Legacy and Fusion/Frost will continue their war tonight in singles competitions, as one member from each faction will square off against a member from the other. Fred Fusion takes on Lance Reed and just a little while later Ethan Frost will match up against Travis Reed. Will the Dynamic Duo finally get one up on The Legacy? The sage of Timo and Dave Gibson continues to become more and more bizarre. On Killzone last week, one week removed from their brutal Submission Stretcher match, Dave Gibson ended the show taking a serious beat down from Tanaka, sacrificing himself to save Timo, and leaving fans to wonder why he’d take such a beating for the man that one week prior beat the hell out of him. Now Dave finds himself in a less than thrilling situation, due to FUSE management, as he’s teamed up with Kid Cool and the man who attacked him last week, Tanaka. They’ll take on the three-man team of Timo and The Alliance, three men who are devastating in a ring and, also, happen to be friends and on the same page. Will Dave Gibson even survive another Killzone? In the first of two title matches on tap Lane Stevens, of The Affliction, puts his LiveWire title on the line against smoking hot newcomer Cyrus Raynes. Stevens just won the belt, from Nathan O’Neill, two weeks ago and only moments after getting the championship Raynes made his intentions to take the title very clear, with a chair shot to the back of the head. Now it’s put up or shut up time for the FUSE rookie as he steps into the ring with the unpredictable, and extremely arrogant, Lane Stevens. Of course Killzone 35 wouldn’t be complete without a match of LEGENDARY PREPORTIONS! Bobino, Damien Cruz, and Mikael Lundgard meet in a triple threat to determine just who is the biggest legend in FUSE history. Lundgard and Bobino have managed to get one up on Cruz over the last couple of weeks, each of them obtaining a victory over the two-time FUSE Universal Champion, but you have to believe that Cruz, and his Associates, have something up their sleeve to even the score this week. Tune in and find out who wins this legendary battle. And finally, but certainly most importantly, the FUSE Universal Championship will be on the line when Universal Champion John Covel places his title on the line against his rival, and nemesis, Clinton Sage. For weeks this rivalry has boiled over and it now comes to a head in the main event of Killzone 35. Will John Covel once again walk away victorious or is this the week that Clinton Sage becomes the ruler of two universes? Tune in tonight, on Showtime, at 10PM, 9PM central, to see all the great action!
