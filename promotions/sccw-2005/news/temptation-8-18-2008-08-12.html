Temptation 8/18

12 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers). Proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION! LIVE! Monday, August 18, 2008 from the Quicken Loans Arena in Cleveland, OH! SCCW Moves Into a New Era! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! "American Youth" Josh Vs. "The Punk Rock Pixie" Charlotte Ramone (c) Charlotte Ramone went through hell and back at All-In, winning the LiveWire Championship and surviving (we wouldn't say winning) Heaven & Hell! Do you think we will give her a week off? No, we most certainly will not! Ramone will be back in the main event, putting that newly-won gold on the line against former GLOBAL standout Josh! What condition is Ramone in, physically after her grueling bouts in Louisville and emotionally given the tumultuous nature of her relationship? Can Josh, who has been a standout in his first two weeks back in the US, snag himself gold against the Pink & Green Machine in his first main event in years on US TV? Andre Jensen Vs. King Blueberry (w/Blueberry Mask 14 and Blueberry Dragon) Andre Jensen got screwed out of the SIN Championships by the Dead Man's Hand! King Blueberry got dropped on his face during Heaven & Hell! Two of the STRANGEST competitors in Sin City go one-on-one in Cleveland! Who will be back in the win column after big losses in Louisville, the Taliban or the Legion of Blue? And when else could that sentence make any sense? William "Dolla' Bill" Cash and Mac "BIG TRUCK" Deesul Vs. Adam Calloway and Hiro Saotome Longtime NWC fans, you can close your mouth now. The Kent Fusiliers have indeed secured the services of Mr. Nice Guy and the Cowboy Golfer, late of the Council, and will bring them into Cleveland! With Desade's Dead Man's Hand dominating competition, will Saotome and Calloway side with her...or join the former NWC'ers who stand against her? Will Dolla' Bill and the BIG TRUCK be able to stand against Calloway and Saotome? Will there be any ring rust? We'll find out together! "The Modern Day Warrior" Jay Draven Vs. Lori Equinox Jay Draven came within an eyelash of winning the LiveWire Championship against Charlie Ramone and Mark in the Dark, only to fall perilously short! The Modern Day Warrior faces the next in a line of newcomers in SCCW in Lori Equinox! Equinox is not a girl, but he hopes to make Draven his bitch in Cleveland! Jahziel "Jazz" Vorster Vs. Simon King Simon King couldn't quite stop the Highwaymen train at the end of the Riverboat Rampage match, but we're sure you can't keep a good man down -- and who is this Jazhiel Vorster chap, anyway? Simon King attempts to rebound in shark-infested waters as he faces a newcomer with a mission in Cleveland! Josef Basturk, Soldier of YHWH Vs. Dualta Sheridan Smitty T. Duluth's rule of "Hey, we have new people! Let's make them wrestle!" continues! Dualta Sheridan did not fare so well in the Riverboat Rampage match, but he'll be facing an intimidating newcomer by the name of Josef Basturk! Sheridan looks to immediately rebound as the Soldier of YHWH attempts a dominant beginning! PLUS! - All-In was a titanic affair from all involved! We've got a new LiveWire Champion, new Strength in Numbers Champions, and a new Universal Champion! What's the fallout from all this? Tune in to Cinemax to find out! - Charlotte Ramone won Heaven & Hell and possession of the Big Slick bracelet! This means the Punk Rock Pixie has a chance to face the SCCW Universal Champion whenever she damn well pleases! She's also the only person NOT associated with the Dead Man's Hand to hold gold in the federation! We'll hear from the Punk Rock Pixie in Cleveland. - Jadian Bridden lost the Take It Off or Put It On match to King Blueberry! The so-called Ace of the Dead Man's Hand now has to dress up like a blueberry in Cleveland! Will Jadian go through with it, or has the chicanery just started? - Hunter Sabuani was scheduled to come to Louisville for All-In, but the Raja of Roguishness sent a singing lobster telegram in his place! Will the former World Champion actually make his return in Cleveland, or will All that -- and just who the hell "the Kent Fusiliers" are -- Live in Cleveland! SCCW TEMPTATION Two Weeks Away! From the Breslin Center on the campus of Michigan State University! A Very Special Edition of SCCW Temptation! For the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling Vs. Mayhem (c)
