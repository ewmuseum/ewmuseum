Possible new signing?

2 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This evening FUSE officials sent down word that FUSE was in possible contract negotiations with Kael Law, formerly of Global Wrestling, in regards to his protégé’. The name of his protégé’ was not released to us at this time. We did receive some pieces from interviews with both Kael, and his unnamed protégé’, that we’d like to share with you now. The following are small clips from the interview conducting with the protégé’ by an unnamed source and are on the topic of FUSE wrestling superstars. The first superstar they discussed was Clinton Sage and then followed that up with comments on FUSE Universal Champion John Covel. "Clinton fucking Sage... the number one contender to the only meaningful title in FUSE. Anyone can be a trash-talking, egotistical jackass with lackeys backing him or her up. Suuuuure, he won the Global Title, but what the fuck does that title mean if the guy holding it is only second best in his own fed? Second place doesn't make it to the bonus round. For him, it doesn't matter how many ass raping, shit-eaters he has hanging out with him. He is still only second best. Soon to be third best in FUSE." "Next up, let's talk about the fucker who is the supposed Captain Wonderful in FUSE. Everyone's favorite hero, John Covel. Those goddamn wanna-bes... He wouldn't know the first thing about being a real hero. He's just another one of those punk ass, motherfuckers, who thinks, because they have a heroic job, they are fucking Clark Kent, feel me? He is nothing more than a poser and a glorified damsel in distress. Scumbags like Sage and Stevens drool at the sight of him because they want his cock and they see a loser just ripe for the pickings. He understands that you have to take the fight to these fuckers and eliminate them before they can you. You want to play by the rules? Go for it, but don't go all emo like every other fuck when your ass is cheated out of what you hold dear." Quite the candid views, and thoughts, on two of the tops stars of FUSE from the mystery man and it will be interesting to see what happens should he sign with FUSE. Which leads us into the piece sent to us from the interview with Kael himself. "You know, I first thought I would send this kid over to my old stomping grounds. However, Global is nothing now. When a guy like Brick can make it to world champion, your promotion is swirling around the toilet with he rest of the shit. There were offers from a few places in PTC, whose names I won't mention here, and many from outside. As it stands right now, FUSE is a top contender as a place for him to showcase the skills that will carry him to the top of the bloody fucking wrestling world. When the slayer tells you something, it is on par with God handing it to you on a stone tablet. This kid is the real deal. No, not that piece of shit that used to run around DWO. That's the bloody truth, mate. You don't need Dionne Warwick to know that you will be watching him soon on a television near you." Only time will tell if these two do make their way into FUSE, with Kael leading his protégé’ from a managerial position it would appear. We’ll keep you posted as more develops on this situation.
