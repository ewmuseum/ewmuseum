Killzone 38 Airs!

11 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the Air Canada Centre, in Toronto, the latest edition of the FUSE flagship comes to the world...LIVE! The Reign of Chaos continues as Johnny Lexicon and Kid Cool squared off in a Fans Bring The Weapons match. Lexicon is a returning darkhorse to FUSE and is a favorite to win this tournament while Kid Cool...well he has done just about everything he can to get out of the hardcore enviroments these matches put him in. Who would walk away with a trip to the finals and a Custom Chaos Championship match at the PPV? In the second match in the tournment, to determine the other finalist, FUSE Legend Mikael Lundgard takes on the surprising newcomer Jonathan Rhine. Rhine has already knocked off one FUSE legend, defeating Damien Cruz in the first round, could he do it a second time and claim himself a spot in the title match at the PPV or would Lundgard step up, once more, and reclaim the title that was his not too long ago. 'Mean' Dean Jokerlin continues to try and prove he's a worthy competitor as he took on Phil Atken in singles competition. Which one of these FUSE young guns would walk away with a victory and a possible step closer to a LiveWire title shot? Despite their titles not being on the line The Alliance come to the Air Canada Centre for their first contest since winning the Fatal Faction gold and they took on their biggest challenge to date. Two Ton has vowed to make an impact in the tag division and Smitty has told him to find a partner. Did the massive man of snacking find a partner or would he have to go it alone against two of the more dangerous men in wrestling, let alone tag team wrestling? The strange sage between Dave Gibson, Timo Bolamba, and Tsuyoshi Tanaka continued as Dave and Timo teamed up to take on the unlikely pair of Tanaka and...Fred Fusion? Would Tanaka be able to get a win over Timo and Dave thus securing him a victory over the two men who've beaten him lately? Finally the main event. It couldn't be any more personal than this one. Clinton Sage, fresh off losing both the FUSE Universal and PTC Championships, teams up with fellow Affliction member, and reigning LiveWire Champion, Lane Stevens to take on John Covel, a man who despises them both, and the man who dethroned Sage from the top of the FUSE mountain, current Universal Champion Jacob McKail. There is no doubt that this one was filled with hatred. Tune it and find out who walked away with some major momentum heading into the PPV. All of those matches and more! Killzone 37
