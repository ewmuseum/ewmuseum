FUSE on ESPN!

27 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enjoy the show, folks. Malachi vs Jason O'Neil FUSE has been full of recent additions over the last few weeks. Two weeks ago on Uproar Jason O’Neil made a definitive debut, now going into FUSE on ESPN O’Neil will be doing exactly as he said, he will be starting at the bottom with former PRIMEate Malachi. What a better stage to make an impact then at FUSE on ESPN, and one of these men will ensure they do just that. Matthew Randall vs Ethan Frost For weeks Ethan Frost has led a roller coaster ride of fan approval. Four weeks ago, Frost came to the ring full on Affliction, the following week he came down full on Highwaymen. Two weeks ago, he stated his opinion of the FUSE fans, and it did not get a very welcoming response. Since then, however he has been taunted, tormented and mocked by a man claiming to be Fred Fusion. Now, many question whether Fusion is really returning to FUSE, if it really is Fred Fusion messing with the head of Ethan Frost. Well, at FUSE on ESPN, Mr. Cold as Ice goes one on one with Matthew Randall, but with the constant mind games Frost has been subject to, will his mind be on the match with Matthew Randall? Itachi vs Tanaka Since Itachi’s debut, he has felt like STD has been testing him. At FUSE on ESPN he faces a rather large test, and that test is Tanaka. Tanaka who was last seen in the ring at War in Washington on the losing end of a match with Clinton Sage. At FUSE on ESPN both men will have something to prove, the question is which man will have what it takes to prove it? Seymour Almasy/Carson Riley vs. Damien Cruz/Corvus FUSE on ESPN has the ingredients for a blockbuster match up. Take on PTC Legend, add one FUSE Icon, add a pinch of Global Hall of Famer, then add FUSE’s lovable loser Carson Riley and you get a big time tag match. Seymour Almasy & Carson Riley taking on Damien Cruz and Corvus. Almasy is known around the globe, Riley is held in the hearts of many FUSE fans. Damien Cruz, a Icon, a Hall of Famer of FUSE past, and a new addition Corvus, whom just picked up a major win over another well known man in the business last week on Uproar. Really, what more is there to say about this one? Joshua Kosidlo vs Jacque Noreault Since their debuts in FUSE, Joshua Kosidlo and Jacque Noreault have become each other’s personal rival. It all started before Noreault’s first match in FUSE, when he mockingly congratulated the Paladin on his victory. The following week, Noreault continued his verbal assault, and it lead to a match the following week. That match ended in a draw, when Noreault grabbed Kosidlo’s leg keeping him from answering the ten count. Immediately following the match, The Canadian Dream pummeled the Paladin within inches of his life. The FUSE website announced shortly after that the two would face off at FUSE on ESPN. This past week on Uproar, after appealing to Mr. Duluth’s inner business child, Kosidlo was granted a best of five falls match. Now, one man will clearly be decided as the winner, whether it be a night of Canadian Dreams, or a night of a Paladin, this match is sure to be a nail biter. Sean Sterling vs. Mayson Colby NO DISQUALIFICATION, FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE, IRON MAN MATCH The one thing that has been a constant on the mind of every FUSE fan has been, “When will Colby and Sterling meet one on one?” The answer to that question, is FUSE on ESPN! Sterling and Colby have been the bitterest of rivals for some time now. Finally, the two will meet one on one on ESPN. On the last edition of KillZone, Colby picked up a win in a major match up, in which Sean Sterling was involved, yet they have never quite hit each other one on one to bring this ever resilient pimpled feud to an end. Well, the head is getting white, and at FUSE on ESPN, it looks like it’s about to pop. The Affliction vs The Highwaymen STABLE WAR FUSE is in a dark time, what many would equate to the Dark Ages. The Affliction have ruled FUSE with an Iron fist, and up until War in Washington, there was nothing anyway could do about it. Now, number one contender to the Universal Championship Johanthan Rhine leads his band of merry men (and one woman) to take on Universal Champion Clinton Sage and his band of treacherous swine. For the first time ever, The Highwaymen square off against The Affliction. Now normally this would be seen as a fair fight, with four on four, however after the events of Uproar, and the unveiling of The Scourge, how can The Highwaymen even the odds? Why, since their conception The Highwaymen were one man for every one man of The Affliction, now The Affliction hold a big wild card over The Highwaymen. Can The Highwaymen finally do what they were made to do, or has the corruption of The Affliction gone too far now to be stopped? Perhaps the biggest question is, if The Highwaymen cannot get the job done, can anyone? Go ahead. Read the transcript.
