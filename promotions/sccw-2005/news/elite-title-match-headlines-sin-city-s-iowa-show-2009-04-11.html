Elite Title Match Headlines Sin City's Iowa Show!

11 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION: ELITE IN IOWA L-I-V-E Sunday, April 19, 2009 at 10PM Eastern/9PM Central and ONLY on Cinemax, from the Carter-Hawkeye Arena on the campus of the University of Iowa in Iowa City! MAIN EVENT! Quarterfinal Match in the No Limit Tournament – For the Elite Championship! Steve Knox Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors © Last week in Lawrence, both of these men tried to outdo the other in their Representative Rules match, as Connors' Decency Crusade beat up Knox physically, but Captain Awesome may have scored a mental advantage by getting the Spider in the Web herself to throw the False Prophet off! Now, it will finally come to a head, as one of these two men will be the fourth semifinalist in the No Limit Tournament – and that man will be the one, true Elite in this sport! Connors and Knox go head-to-head one more time for all the marbles! Be there! TAG TEAM ACTION Special Guest Referee: SCCW LiveWire Champion Legion The Illustrious Face-Eater and Dusk Vs. Desade and Lane Stevens Lane Stevens is back! The River Rat made a stunning return in Lawrence, with his assistance nearly inarguably being the difference-maker in Amy Campbell's successful defense of the Universal Championship in a grueling Iron Man Match! But with Aimz' greatest rival returning to Sin City, what does this mean for the Red Raver, who's not even in this bout, but is sure to make her presence known in Iowa? Is Stevens in Pierce's Hand? Opposite the ring from them will be the Illustrious Face-Eater and the man who bounced him from the No Limit Tournament, the Lost Soul! Can they coexist against Pierce and Stevens? And will we finally find out what side Legion is on? Quarterfinal Match in the No Limit Tournament! "The Protagonist" Seymour Almasy Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall Lance Marshall has been on the sidelines since bulldozing Brock Alyas nearly a month ago, but the former Universal Champ gets his next – and perhaps biggest – test in this tournament against Mr. Everything in the PTC, Seymour Almasy! Almasy's coming off a monumental win over Jay Phoenix, but it's the circumstances of that win that make people doubt the Protagonist against the #2 seed in the tournament! Can Almasy overcome the odds and add his name to the four who'll compete for the Universal Title, or will the Lion soldier on towards his destiny? #1 Contender's Match for the LiveWire Championship! Rick Malloy Vs. Austin "The Wombat" King These two men didn't hook up in the ill-fated Early Out battle royal, but that's only because the Wombat was in the match for all of five seconds! One of these men will face the Devil's Reject for the LiveWire Championship, but can the newcomer Malloy keep Austin King from that breakout match the Wombat has been seeking for so long? And why are we scared that a turntable and a karaoke machine have been ordered for Iowa? All this and so much more next week in Iowa City! SCCW TEMPTATION! Two Weeks Away! From the Rushmore Plaza Civic Center in Rapid City, South Dakota! The first semifinal of the No Limit Tournament takes place in the shadow of Mount Rushmore! Who will claim their place on our monument? SCCW RETURNS TO PPV! Four Weeks Away! From the EnergySolutions Center in Salt Lake City, Utah! See the finals of the No Limit Tournament as SCCW returns to the rarefied air that birthed her – only on PPV! Learn the event's name next week in Iowa City!
