Time Running Out on Jake?

22 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This week on Killzone the Fatal Faction Champions, God's Own Drunks, will be taking on Jake Reed and he hopes his older brother as well. The duo are collectively known as Generation Gap. It's fitting then that the recent problems for Jake Reed have revolved around just that sort of thing...a generation gap. With wrestling having seemingly moved away from what the eldest Reed boy, Travis Reed, believes wrestling should be all about...the competition. Now it's become to much of a 'circus' in the mind of Travis and with that in mind a few weeks ago on Killzone the elder Reed son walked out of the arena and to this point his contract with FUSE. Over the last two or three weeks Jake has been faced with the task of getting his brother to return to the ring and wrestle with Jake as Generation Gap, as FUSE Commissioner Smitty T. Duluth so aptly point out was what they were signed to FUSE to do, wrestle as a team, or Jake will also find himself on the unemployment line. STD has given Jake till Revolution to produce his brother or face losing his job. Two weeks ago Jake came out to the ring, a rarity for him, and asked, pleaded, with his brother to return to FUSE the following week. Last week rolled around, Jake came out, and he didn't get what he was hoping for. Travis did not return to the ring but the current Fatal Faction Champions did. The Captain attempted to console Jake and to help him cheer up a bit but he received an angry punch from the younger Reed. After a quick beat down by the bigger half of the Champs, The Wino, The Captain called off the dogs and the champs left the ring only to have Jake ask STD to give him a match with them. The match was granted and it will either be a tag team match, if Travis shows up, or it will be a 2-1 handicap match in favor of the champs. Will Travis Reed finally return to FUSE Wrestling and aid his brother against the Fatal Faction Champions or will Jake Reed find himself all alone, once more, in a less than thrilling situation? Tune into Killzone 28 on the 24th of January to find out.
