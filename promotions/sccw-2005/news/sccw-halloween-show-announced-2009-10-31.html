SCCW Halloween Show Announced!

31 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers and their silent partners) proudly bring to you... TEMPTATION: Houston Halloween Hysteria! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, November 8, 2009 from the Hofheinz Pavilion on the campus of the University of Houston in Houston, Texas! MAIN EVENT! Triple Threat Match! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy Vs. "The Lost Soul" Dusk Vs. Spacely Three forces collide in Houston, as the returned Phillip Kennedy, now all on his own and coming off his assault of Jonathan Rhine at Equilibrium and a victory over Jared Sykes at SIN*CON. Spacely, the #1 contender to the Gateway Championship – and a man who has done what his opponents would love to claim: pin Desade. And Dusk, the Lost Soul returning at the close of Temptation: The Last Stand with a shocking fivesome – Mary-Lynn Mayweather, High Flyer, Alias, and David Noble have joined Dusk in his crusade to... well, we're not really sure. They'll go one-on-one in an anything goes, take-no-prisoners triple threat match, but only one of them will see their momentum continue – which will it be? A Berried Treasure Match – Champion's Showcase III! Special Guest Referee: SCCW Universal Champion "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors! SCCW LiveWire Champion "The Ace in the Hole" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Vs. SCCW Gateway Champion "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes The second of two Berried Treasure matches will be the third Champion's Showcase match! The Gateway Champion, Jared Sykes, and the LiveWire Champion, Jadian Bridden, will face off one-on-one in an attempt to prove which division is the toughest, and the winner adds a stipulation to the Bridden-Quinlan LiveWire Title Match – whenever it may occur! All this, and Wyatt Connors will wear the black-and-white stripes as special guest referee, and he's guaranteed to be fair and bal—ahahahaha, no he's not. A Berried Treasure Match! Baron von Blackberry Vs. Mitchell Quinlan The number-one contender to the LiveWire Championship may have suffered his first loss in San Antonio at the hands of the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, but he doesn't have time to lick his wounds, because he'll be coming to Houston with yet another difficult challenge on hand – the LAST man who held the LiveWire Title, the bane of Fruitsylvania, Baron von Blackberry! And just what is a Berried Treasure match? Simple: the winner decides a stipulation for the upcoming Jadian Bridden-Mitchell Quinlan matchup! Quinlan can get an edge or... he can get whatever the Baron wants! A Trick or Treat Match! Josh Marquez Vs. An Opponent to Be Named Josh Marquez comes into Houston on a roll, with a victory in the Future Considerations match – he just doesn't know what his prize is! Now Marquez will come into the Halloween special for the first-ever "Trick or Treat" match. We don't know whether Josh's opponent will be a trick and a test or a treat and a walk, and neither will Josh until the show begins! Can Marquez continue his pace and will someone tell us at the home office just what these future considerations are? All this, plus find out how the SCCW Universal Champion will defend his title next, only on a GHOULISH NIGHT in Houston! SCCW TEMPTATION: A GATEWAY TO SIN Two weeks away from the Chaifetz Arena on the campus of St. Louis University in St. Louis! With a name like that, you've got to assume the Gateway Champion will be involved, right? SCCW SIN ON SPIKE 4: THE THROWDOWN IN MOTOWN! Three weeks away! From Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan! For the SCCW Strength in Numbers Championships! Daddy Daughter Day Vs. "The Spider in the Web" Desade & "The Lion" Lance Marshall
