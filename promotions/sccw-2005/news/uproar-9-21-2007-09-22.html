Joaquin Pierre Standard Match FUSE Debut ~In the first match of the evening, two of GLOBAL Wrestling’s former stars face off in the FUSE ring for the very first time. "The Paladin" Joshua Kosidlo and Joaquin "Frenchie" Pierre made big names for themselves in Global, but now how well will they fare in FUSE?~ Carson Riley Vs. Ethan Frost Standard Match ~Relative newcomer, "One Take" Carson Riley steps into the ring with the recently returned Ethan Frost.~ Jackrabbit Vs. Jacque Noreault Standard Match FUSE Debut ~In another debut match, newcomer Jackrabbit faces yet another former GLOBAL star, "The Canadian Dream" Jacque Noreault.~ Jason Cruise © vs Mayson Colby LiveWire Title Match ~On the last ever Killzone main event, Mayson Colby won a Strange Bedfellows match and earned a shot at Jason Cruise’s recently received LiveWire title. Will "The Prophecy" be able to retain his championship or might Mayson Colby win his first ever title in FUSE?~ Seymour Almasy vs Itachi Standard Match Singles Debut ~In this match, former ACW World Champion and FUSE star, "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy, makes his return in a singles debut match against one half of The Rising Sons, Itachi.~ Clinton Sage & Lane Stevens Vs. Jonathan Rhine & Aimz **Fatal Faction Rules** ~In a match that was signed by STD on Killzone 50, The Affliction and The Highwaymen have their first chance to get their hands on one another. FUSE Universal Champion Clinton Sage and "The River Rat" Lane Stevens face off against rivals Amy "Aimz" Campbell and the newly crowned number one contender for the Universal Championship, Jonathan Rhine.~ Click here to read the transcript.
