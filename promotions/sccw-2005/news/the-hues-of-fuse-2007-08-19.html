The Hues Of FUSE

19 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone and welcome to the first edition of The Hues of FUSE! Here you’ll find the crazy, semi weekly ramblings of a FUSE follower. Alright, let’s get down to business. War In Washington Thoughts and Predictions Jason Cruise vs El Diablo© Livewire Championship Match They’ve had bloodshed, staples, cassette tapes, and pocket watches. Now they get their match. This feud has been called a blood war and I don’t doubt this match will be anything less. Although these men will have to follow strict Livewire rules, I can guarantee both will be out there giving their all. Will “The Prophecy” Jason Cruise be able to pull another victory over “The Mexican Devil” El Diablo, or will El Diablo’s make another successful title defense? Winner: El Diablo Aimz and Darcy Crisis vs The Affliction This match features no gimmicks, and no titles, but it’s one FUSE has been waiting for. There have been chair shots, challenges, and back stage beat downs. What does Aimz have cooking up? Who has she been talking to these past few weeks? Will the rest of the Affliction play a role in this match up? Winner: Aimz and Darcy Crisis The Rising Sons vs Cowboys From Hell © Fatal Faction Championship Match The Cowboys From Hell have done nothing but bring disgrace to the titles since winning them weeks ago. Now a new set of mystery challengers has an opportunity to shine and hopefully bring some sort of dignity back to this division. Can they pull off a victory, or is the Fatal Factions division doomed? Winner: The Cowboys From Hell Timo Bolamba vs Jonathan Rhine © Custom Chaos Championship Match Jonathan Rhine is fresh off his win in the tower preview match on this week’s Killzone, and you can bet he’ll carry some of that energy with him to War In Washington. Rhine has brought a feeling of pride to being a champion in FUSE and he won’t go down without a fight. However, “The Dastardly Bastard” Timo Bolamba has been known to have some tricks up his sleeve. Will Rhine retain, or has his reign come to an end? Winner: Jonathan Rhine Tower Match Paul Cain, Aimz, Prince Pride, Mayson Colby, Carson Riley, Kid Cool, Czar, Ethan Frost, Timo Bolamba, Jason Cruise, Lane Stevens, El Diablo, Sean Sterling, Jonathan Rhine, and a mystery opponent First off, I love the mixture of the old and new in this match. We have rookies such as Paul Cain, Prince Pride, Carson Riley, Mayson Colby, and Sean Sterling taking on established FUSE members Lane Stevens, Jonathan Rhine, Jason Cruise, Timo Bolamba, and El Diablo. Hell, we’ve even got a FUSE debut in Czar. And who is this mystery opponent? I can’t help thinking it’s something The Affliction has cooked up, but only time will tell. Let’s not forget that the winner of this match receives a Universal Championship shot. This match will be too close to call, so I’m narrowing it down to three. Winner: Jonathan Rhine, Lane Stevens, or Sean Sterling Tsuyoshi Tanaka vs Clinton Sage © Universal Championship Match Sage has proven week after week that he deserves his title belt. He’s had no qualms about going toe to toe with any opponent thrown his way. Tanaka has essentially disappeared since earning his title shot. Has he given up on his career in FUSE, or has he simply been silent, biding his time until the big match up? Will Sage once again prove he is the best FUSE has to offer? Winner: Clinton Sage Let’s not forget the induction of Hunter Jones in to the FUSE hall of fame, and hopefully answers to a few questions floating around backstage. Who is The Scourge? What will happen to Ethan Frost, now that Fred Fusion is gone? Who is the mystery entrant in the tower match? Will therapy continue for Sterling and Colby, or what does their future have in store? What does Aimz have planned? With all this and more, War In Washington promises to be action packed. Now that that’s out of the way, here are my current rankings, going in to the Pay Per View. 1. Clinton Sage 2. Jonathan Rhine 3. Lane Stevens 4. Tsuyoshi Tanaka 5. Timo Bolamba 6. Jason Cruise 7. Sean Sterling 8. El Diablo 9. Aimz 10. Ethan Frost We’ll see how people rise and fall after War In Washington. Until then, I’ll be watching FUSE shine through. -Flux Stay tuned for future interviews with FUSE stars.
