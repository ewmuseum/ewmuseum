A Sin City Kind of Thanksgiving

23 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly bring to you... TEMPTATION: A Sin City Kind of Thanksgiving! L-I-V-E LIVE Thursday, November 27, 2008 from the Blue Cross Arena in Rochester, New York and ONLY on Cinemax! MAIN EVENT TAG TEAM MATCH! "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling & BloodAngel Vs. Desade & "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy One week after losing his precious Strength in Numbers championship, Phillip Kennedy again has a tag-team match -- this time teaming with the Director of the Dead Man's Hand, the Spider in the Web, Desade! Opposing them will be Kennedy's Defiance opponent (who also happens to be the SCCW Gateway Champion) and the man who Desade has warred with for months, culminating in the infamous vicious beating she was given at All In! Will Kennedy's luck change, or will BA deliver another beating to his hated rival? And where will Wyatt Connors stand in all of this in light of his upcoming shot at the Gateway Championship, his fear of Desade, and BloodAngel's actions in Halifax? For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Paul Searle Vs. Charley Crisp © Fresh off of pinning Jadian Bridden to win the Strength in Numbers championships for he and Jared Sykes, Charley Crisp puts his old championship (the one he won a whole month ago) on the line against Paul Searle! Searle gets the shot he won in the Double Win-Fall match against the Cookie, but can the rookie pull off the shocking win and walk out of upstate New York with his first championship? Or does the Crispinator keep up his killer roll all the way to Defiance? The Slayer vs. the Cowboy! "Angelslayer" Aaron Kyle Vs. Clayton Byrd For the past two weeks, we've been getting cryptic messages from the Angelslayer, now it's time to put him in the ring to see if he can pass muster! Opposing him will be Clayton Byrd, fresh off of competing in PRIME's Jewel in the Crown tournament! Will Byrd be able to detour the newcomer? Or will the tough Texan who has been at odds for weeks now with the Black Ops of the Dead Man's Hand be able to kick it up a notch against the latest asshole to come down the pike? Have You Seen His Grandfather? Nicodemis Vs. Illuminado It appears Nicos has accepted Dr Kensington's offer and will make his in-ring debut on his quest to find his missing grandfather in Rochester! Standing opposite him is half of the Luchadores For Jesus, but it's not like the Decency Crusade has a history of kidnapping people, right? See if the man from Ireland can fight as well as he can search -- and find out if that's a complement. PLUS! - Coming off of the events in Halifax, the contract for Aimz' Defiance II title shot against Lance Marshall will be signed in Rochester! - Madison Justice is scheduled to be at the Blue Cross Arena to give us an update on her ex-husband! Plus, who attacked the Raja? Does Madison know? - Reactions to the announcement of the Connors-Aimz Elite Title match at Defiance! TEMPTATION! Two Weeks Away From the Gampel Pavilion at the University of Connecticut in Storrs! DNA (Desade & Aimz) Vs. Lance Marshall & Jared Sykes DEFIANCE II! Just Three Weeks Away! From the Nassau Coliseum in Long Island, New York! For the SCCW Universal Championship! "The Lion" Lance Marshall Vs. Aimz © No Excuses for the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy Vs. "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling For the Elite Championship! Aimz Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors © For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Jadian Bridden Vs. Charley Crisp or Paul Searle © PLUS! Tickets go on sale THIS SATURDAY for SIN on SPIKE 2: Syke-Out, featuring Jared Sykes' shot at the SCCW Universal Championship!
