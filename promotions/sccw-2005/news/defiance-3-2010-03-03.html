DEFIANCE 3!

3 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Final Curtain Entertainment) proudly bring to you... SCCW DEFIANCE 3! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, March 7, 2010 from the Stan Sheriff Center in Honolulu, HI and EXCLUSIVELY on pay-per-view! MAIN EVENT! For the Sin City Championship Wrestling Universal Championship! Triple Threat Rules! "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes Vs. "The Red Raver" Aimz Vs. "The River Rat" Lane Stevens (w/Regan Guest & Katsidy) © It all comes down to this – the most important triple-threat match in Sin City history takes place on the biggest stage of the year! The new SCCW Universal Champion, Lane Stevens, will defend against two people with every reason to hate him – and no reason to hate each other. Amy Campbell held the Universal belt for more than a year – can she become the first three-time champion in SCCW history. Jared Sykes has never held a major wrestling championship, but he's just the Universal Title away from being the first Grand Slam winner in the federation's history! Can they work together long enough to make Stevens pay for his crimes? Will Campbell and Sykes' burgeoning friendship get in the way? And what about the Black Sheep's vow to never hit a woman? Two Falls Match – Triple Threat Rules! First Fall Under LiveWire Rules for the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Second Fall Under Standard Rules for the SCCW Gateway Championship! Baron von Blackberry Vs. Mitchell Quinlan Vs. "The Ace in the Hole" Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) © Three men associated with the LiveWire Championship attempt to move up in the world, as the first two-time LiveWire Champion and only "GateWire" Champion, Jadian Bridden, attempts to defend his belts against two men with a lot to prove – the Devil Fruit attempts to rescue his child (or his child's brother) and the Eagle Place Product attempts to prove he has a place in Sin City after all! Can Jadian Bridden escape with one, or perhaps both, of his titles? And will the increasingly troubled relationship with Julissa Minorez play a part in the match? Falls Count Anywhere! "The Spider in the Web" Alexandra Pierce Vs. "Gold Patron Meritorious" Xavier Kannon (w/Eleanor Kannon-Hall) They are two of the most recognizable names in professional wrestling, with reputations that paint them as two of the vilest villains in the sport. Alexandra Pierce is attempting to turn over a new leaf and redeem herself for a lifetime of sin. Xavier Kannon has targeted the Spider as his first lynchpin victory as he attempts to conquer Sin City! After weeks of deception and sneak attacks, they will tear each other apart in a vicious falls count anywhere war, and the loser is very likely to be carried out of the building! Kannon and Pierce will have a classic in Honolulu! Non-Title Two-on-One Handicapped Match! "The Lion" Lance Marshall Vs. Daddy Daughter Day (August & April Monday) He thought they were friends. He thought they understood each other. He was wrong, and now Lance Marshall attempts to teach August Monday the error of his ways, and he'll take on BOTH of the newest members of the Dead Man's Hand to prove it! August and April Monday defeated Lance Marshall and his partner Desade to win the Strength in Numbers Championships, and now they will attempt to prove that adage true as they take on the strongest man in Sin City! Grudge Match! "The New Life" Jonathan Rhine Vs. "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy It all started at Homecoming when Phillip Kennedy assaulted Jonathan Rhine following a grueling match, but Kennedy wouldn't stop until he got what he wanted – and now he has! But after Phillip assaulted Jonathan's friend and former manager, has he bitten off more than he can chew against the man who has been the flagship hero for SCCW for longer than the federation has been in existence! Kennedy and Rhine finally – finally – go one-on-one in Honolulu, and you can bet both men will be pulling out all the stops! Singles Bout! "The Pit Viper" Terrence Kingsley Vs. Spacely (w/Quasar & MacKenzie Malone) Terrence Kingsley thought he was getting an easy debut match, booked against "Mongoose", a masked wrestler and fearsome enemy to snakes all over the world! Imagine the GTT Sweet Sixteen sensation's surprise when he found out that the Mongoose wasn't a schlub, but rather, the Ant-Sized Astronaut, former Gateway Champion! Now Kingsley attempts to get some revenge against the Hulk Hogan of the Heavens – but will it be so easy? Spacely holds the first victory over the woman who eliminated Terrence from the GTT, and he's not going to roll over! To War! "The Puppetmaster" James von Doom Vs. "The Valkyrie" Suzanne Larsson It's an old story: girl meets boy. Boy attacks girl's uncle. Boy steals win in tag match. Now girl tries to kill boy. Two up-and-coming talents looking for that marquee win will face off as GTT breakout star Suzanne Larsson faces the unpredictable Puppetmaster! Von Doom's Civilization will be in attendance – will they play a role in the Valkyrie's attempt to plant her flag straight up von Doom's... unmentionable regions? Four Corners SIN Contenders Match! The Devil's Rejects (Legion & Stigmata) Vs. MEDS (Matt Elias & Devlin Shannon) Vs. The Atomic Surf Dogs (Saul Goode & Ben Chillen) Vs. The Heirs of Wrestling (Frank Silver & Brian Gallway) Suddenly, SCCW has a tag team division! While Daddy Daughter Day is busy taking on the strongest man in Sin City, we'll showcase some of the new blood in Sin City! The electrifying Atomic Surf Dogs will face the always treacherous Legion and his monstrous bodyguard Stigmata, while they take on charismatic newcomers Matt Elias and Devlin Shannon, and the debut of the second-generation Heirs of Wrestling! The Mondays used to be the whole division, but we'll see tag wrestling at its finest in Hawaii! Plus the SCCW Hall of Fame Inductions for Clinton Sage, Smitty T. Duluth, and Jason Cruise!
