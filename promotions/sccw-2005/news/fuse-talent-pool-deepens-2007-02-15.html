FUSE Talent Pool Deepens

15 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Revolution FUSE Wrestling unveiled the two latest signing to their already impressive talent roster. 'Old School' Dave Gibson showed up, under the mask of The Redneck Ninja, to attack, and later try to square things away, Timo Bolamba. The two are old rivals from their Old School Wrestling days and it's Gibson, who is regarded as the man who put Timo on the shelf, for many years, due to injuries suffered in The Samoan Silencer’s final OSW matches. Dave Gibson is a highly decorated, and respected, wrestler whose last two wrestling stops were in WWA and PRIME. FUSE officials are quoted as saying they are “extremely delighted to have Dave Gibson on the FUSE roster.” Also appearing at Revolution, in the FUSION World Championship Rumble, that was won by Ethan Frost and Fred Fusion via an crazy double elimination, intentionally, by Fred Fusion, is just the latest in a string of wrestlers who competed, and fared well, in the latest edition of the GTT, hosted by Primetime Central. The Sentinel joined the ranks of FUSE and showed some real promise during his time in the FUSION rumble. The officials in FUSE were elated to sign the man who made an impressive run through the GTT this year, before any of their rival promotions were able to sign him on the dotted line. Smitty T. Duluth had this to say about the signing of The Sentinel: “Obviously this is a big moment for FUSE. The Sentinel certainly possess the ability, and experience, to be an impact player within FUSE for a long time but it’s more than just the initial gain of another GTT competitor joining the ranks of FUSE. The Sentinel brings with him a certain level of the unknown and that is always exciting to some degree. Outside of his fabulous GTT run this year there simply isn’t much known about the man or what he’s fully capable of. It’s just another reason for fans to get excited about FUSE and just another reason why I believe, and this is just my own opinion here, that FUSE is THE federation in Primetime Central at the moment. PRIME is the respected, and recognized, big dog in the yard, right now, but I believe we’re more than making a case that FUSE is on par, if not already better, than PRIME.” Smitty went on to say that he looks forward to the battle with PRIME over this next year or so for the top federation title within PTC but also noted that he, and the rest of FUSE management, are not going to make the mistake of over looking the other talented federations within Primetime Central. “PRIME is the big name, at this time, but it would be idiotic for me, or anyone else, to count out federations with the talent base, and histories, of WWA, GW, XUW, and GCW. They didn’t last as long as they have, through the hard times and the good ones, by being chumps who you can forget about. We’re not going to be sitting on our collective asses here in FUSE and letting anyone sneak up on us.” Tune in on the 21st for the next edition of Killzone to check out the official singles debuts for both The Sentinel and Dave Gibson when they take on Fred Fusion and Jacqueline Bates, respectively. Also don’t forget that the main event will pit PTC superstars Angelo Deville, Doctor Curiosity, and FUSE’s own Clinton Sage, against current PTC Global Champion Kimbusa, for the PTC Global Championship. It’s sure to be a show that you won’t want to miss!
