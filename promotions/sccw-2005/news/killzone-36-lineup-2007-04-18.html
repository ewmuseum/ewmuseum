Killzone 36 Lineup

18 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUSE Wrestling Presents... KILLZONE36 Crisler Arena - Ann Arbor, MI - Wednesday, Apr. 25th Match #01 »» Opening Contest After the opening bell, KillZone's 36 edition begins from Ann Arbor Michigan's Crisler Arena with two relative newcomers to the company. On one side of the ring, we have Claudius Augustus whose racked up a record in FUSE of 01 - 00 in his short tenure here. He'll be matching up against the protege of ex-GLOBAl star Kael Law, the promising Slade Nottingham. Which of these guys will get off on the right step in this company, by getting this clutch win? Claudius Augustus Vs. Slade Nottingham Match #02 »» Reign of Chaos - Round One - Parking Lot Match The inaugeral match in FUSE's Reign of Chaos tournament begins, with the winner capturing the now vacant Custom Chaos championship. To open it up, the #08 Seed Johnathan Rhine, another newcomer with no history in the company, takes on #01 seed, and FUSE Hall of Fame Prospect Damien Cruz. Which one of these two will be one step closer to gold when KZ37 rolls around the corner? #8 Johnathan Rhine Vs. #1 Damien Cruz Match #03 »» LiveWire Championship Match - LiveWire Rules Last week, current LiveWire champion 'The River Rat' Lane Stevens was able to fend off challenger Cyrus Raynes via help from his manager, Saul. His next challenger is a competitor whose been in the company a while, but has yet to turn heads, in Leah Adams. Will Adams have the breakout match of her career, and come out with the title? Or will the River Rat find yet another inventive way to hold onto it? LiveWire Championship Lane Stevens © Vs. Leah Adams Match #04 »» One on One For the past month, 'The Japanese Firecracker' Tsuyoshi Tanaka has been at the throats of 'Old School' Dave Gibson, and 'The Samoan Knight' Timo Bolamba. It culminated in a vicious attack, where Tanaka took out Gibson with a bat at KZ34. Now Bolamba, after a successful defeat of Tanaka in a six-man tag at KZ35, gets a chance at Tanaka one on one. Will the ever-confident Tanaka we able to get past Bolamba, who seems to have his number as of late? Tsuyoshi Tanaka Vs. Timo Bolamba Match #05 »» Reign of Chaos - Round One - Barbed Wire Ropes In the second RoC match of the night, another FUSE Newcomer, Phil Atken, steps into the ring with the #2 ranked 'Danish Demon', Mikael Lundgard. Will the favorite prevail, or will the underdog come out on top? The winner advances to round two of the tournament at KillZone38. #7 Phil Atken Vs. #2 Mikael Lundgard Match #06 »» Main Event - Fatal Faction Championships For the first time in a month, the FUSE Fatal Faction championships will be put one the line, in the main event of KillZone36. Three weeks ago, one half of the Fatal Factions champion, Darcy Crisis made a statement in the ring and hasn't bothered to show up since. His partner, Aimz has gone on the record, saying she's done everything she can to get him to return to KillZone. Will she be successful? Or will Faction standouts The Alliance capture the belts? Fatal Faction Championships Aimz & Darcy Crisis © Vs. The Alliance
