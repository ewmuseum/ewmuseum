SCCW Follows Up on PPV Success with Spectacular Provo Card

25 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you... TEMPTATION: PERIL IN PROVO! L-I-V-E LIVE Tuesday, June 2, 2009 from the Marriott Center on the campus of Brigham Young University in Provo, UT! MAIN EVENT! For the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The River Rat" Lane Stevens Vs. "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy (w/Savant & Katsidy) © Salt Lake City was not a good night for the Big Stack, as he came up a hair short in the cage against the Red Raver, allowing Aimz to hit the 300-day plateau with the Universal Title! But at this rate, he might grow to hate the whole state of Utah, as he'll be coming to BYU to put his own championship on the line! Lane Stevens showed a side of brutality that not even Dusk could counter at Homecoming – can the former Gateway Champion bring some of that to the table and reclaim the belt? Or will the Big Stack restart his quest to bring down DNA by removing their lackey? Tag-Team Special Challenge Match! Xander Searle & Legion (w/Luci4) Vs. "Rappin' Rick Malloy & Spacely (w/Quasar) Talk about four strange bedfellows – Xander Searle escaped the four-way at Homecoming with a shot he can cash in either for the Gateway Championship, or for the title that the Devil's Reject just had taken from him! They'll be forced to team up (somehow) to face a pair that is quite frankly terrifying. Rappin' Rick Malloy – the new LiveWire Champion, just keep saying that – will team with the best man & dog combination since Turner & Hooch, Spacely, coming off the most... unique match on the pay-per-view in his win over the American Dream! Can these four co-exist? What kind of chicanery will we see? Will Legion face repercussions for his decision in Salt Lake? Will you tune in if we tell you? Off the Schnide with No Disqualification! Friar Vs. Dusk Both Friar and Dusk left Salt Lake City with bitter tastes in their mouth – the Hellhound made Mitchell Quinlan tap, but it was a split-second too late before Xander Searle snuck a pin over the Wombat! And Dusk... well, the Lost Soul went to war with Lane Stevens, and got his face made into hamburger with a belt because of it! Now the two of them will face off in Provo less than two weeks later – and just for shits and giggles, we're throwing out the rulebook! Is Dusk in any condition to fight? Will Friar finally creep over the hump and get his first win after two close losses? Find out in Cougar Country! New (Old) People Have a New (Old) Fight! Lori Equinox Vs. Chris Hollister Vs. Joaquin Pierre Yes, Sinners, you've seen two of those names before! Before his untimely exit last summer, Lori Equinox seemed destined to tear a swath through the City of Sin! And Joaquin Pierre was SCCW's first Gateway Champion when he beat El Diablo at the first Temptation! Now Joaquin and Lori are back in the saddle again, looking to make a quick name for themselves. Hopefully, they haven't forgotten the highly-touted newcomer in Hollister, who has a chance to score wins over two names in one night! SCCW Temptation! Two Weeks Away! From the Wells Fargo Arena on the campus of Arizona State University in Tempe! See Wyatt Connors and Alexandra Pierce in the ring together for the first time since the night they closed the National Wrestling Council – but will they be allies or enemies? SCCW SIN ON SPIKE: THE DOME OF DOOM! This July! From the Arena-Auditorium on the campus of the University of Wyoming in Laramie! The main event for SOS3 WILL BE announced in Provo!
