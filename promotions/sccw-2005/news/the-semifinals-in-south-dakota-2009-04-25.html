The Semifinals in South Dakota!

25 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) proudly brings to you: TEMPTATION: Mount Rushmore L-I-V-E LIVE Tuesday, April 28, 2009 from the Rushmore Plaza Civic Center in Rapid City, South Dakota! See the Semifinals of the No Limit Tournament as SCCW begins the race to Homecoming! MAIN EVENT! Semifinal Match in the No Limit Tournament – For the SCCW Universal Championship! "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors Vs. Aimz © Amy Campbell has been dominant since winning the SCCW title at the beginning of August, but during that time, she's taken a single pinfall loss, failing to secure Wyatt Connors' Elite Championship at Defiance II! Now, with Connors having overcome the Princes of New England, Captain Justice, and BloodAngel to defeat Steve Knox, the False Prophet will bring his particular brand of chicanery to South Dakota in search of the richest prize in the federation. Can Campbell conquer the only man who's beaten her in months, or does Wyatt have the Red Raver's number? And what part, if any, will Lane Stevens play? Semifinal Match in the No Limit Tournament! Non-Title Match! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy (w/Savant) Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall (w/Alanna Marshall) For weeks, the Gateway Champion has claimed to be the next big thing in the sport of kings, but if he wants his shot at greatness, the Big Stack will have to go through the man who has resolutely refused to lose as he seeks a fair shot at what he (perhaps rightly) considers himself screwed out of. Can Kennedy pull off what may be considered an upset, without the services of Kathryn Shaw, incapacitated by the cranium kick she suffered at Temptation? Or will the Lion roll into Salt Lake to headline his second consecutive pay-per-view? Pick-Your-Partner Match! Rick Malloy (and partner) Vs. Legion (and partner) It's official! Rappin' Rick Malloy is the #1 contender to the LiveWire Championship, and he gets his shot at gold at Homecoming after two impressive performances! But first there's the little matter of a tag match – a match where the Devil's Reject and his PPV opponent will both get to choose their opponents! With Legion playing both sides in the Dusk-Hand war and none of the participants booked, will the LiveWire Champion be able to call in a heavy-duty favor? Or will Malloy make it three weeks in a row of impressive performances? Double Debut! "Classic" Carter Sinclair Vs. Mitchell Quinlan We like new people in Sin City, and what we REALLY like is to make them fight each other! Mitchell Quinlan and Carter Sinclair are the latest pair to dip their toe into the pool here at Sin City! Can they tread water, or will the currents suck them under? What kind of goods do the new people bring? Well, if we hired them, they have to be good, right? Right! Find out in South Dakota! All this and see the card for Homecoming starting to come together before your very eyes – only on Cinemax! SCCW Sinner's Choice! Check your local listings – only in syndication! Our PPV hype begins in earnest here as SCCW attempts to land a network deal! See the contract signing for the main event at Homecoming and more just days before our return to PPV! SCCW Homecoming! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, May 10, 2009 from the EnergySolutions Center in Salt Lake City, Utah! SCCW returns to where it all began nearly 15 months later after the first Temptation rocked the airwaves! MAIN EVENT! Finals of the No Limit Tournament – For the SCCW Universal Championship! Marshall/Kennedy Winner Vs. Aimz/Connors Winner © For the SCCW LiveWire Championship! Rick Malloy Vs. Legion © And much more – see the card crystallize on Temptation and don't forget to watch Sinner's Choice!
