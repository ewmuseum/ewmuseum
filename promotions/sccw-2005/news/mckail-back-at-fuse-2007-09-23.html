McKail back at FUSE?

23 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reports emanating from the offices of Fuse today, are suggesting that former FUSE Universal Champion Jacob McKail as signed a new contact with the company and has been put back on the FUSE with immediate effect. McKail, 31, parted ways with the company three months ago, citing 'personal reasons' for the sudden departure. Various internet sources are suggesting that the former GCW and PTC superstar has been battling an alcohol problem for over a year and has recently sought help for the affliction. Inside sources suggests that during the contract negotiations, McKail appeared to be on the 'up and up' and seemed 'enthusiastic' about his future.
