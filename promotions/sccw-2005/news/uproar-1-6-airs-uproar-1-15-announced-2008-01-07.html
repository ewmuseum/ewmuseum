UPROAR 1-6 Airs & UPROAR 1-15 Announced

7 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The first UPROAR in FUSE History without Clinton Sage saw one man’s career nearly ended, while another man’s career is just beginning one week after winning the first ever Young Lion’s Cup. UPROAR also saw the flames of war continue to rage on between Timo Bolamba and former LiveWire Champion El Diablo, as well as Dave Gibson’s opportunity to whip some respect into the second generation star Prince Pride. On a night where Highwaymen leader, and Universal Champion Jonathan Rhine was not in attendance fellow stable mate Aimz took on the self-proclaimed “God” Malachi in the Main Event, both individuals were looking to prove something with the Universal title not having a number one contender. Prince Pride squared off against Cyrus Raynes in what proved to be a very shocking contest, could the most famous streak in wrestling history have finally ended? Also in action was FUSE up and coming stars Joaquin Pierre, Elliott Rollins and Itachi in a triple threat match. After months of heated competition Joshua Kosidlo and Jacque Noreault’s bitter rivalry was to be finally settled in a submission’s only match, where no-holds were barred and submissions would be counted anywhere in the arena Former Global World Champion Corvus stepped into the ring with Matthew Randall, but this was not the only time Corvus made an appearance on the Showtime broadcast. Newcomers to FUSE squared off in a fourway dance where a win for any of them could mean a major step up the ladder to success, and possible title contentions. The match featured VON DOOM, RYUJIN, Jaiden Bridden and NWC Legend Perfect Raymond Pierce. All of this, and an opening contest between Gideon Fontaine and newcomer Gunnar Saint. To find out how these matches ended, and just what went down this week on UPROAR you can check the transcript of the Showtime broadcast here. Triple-Threat Debut Match "The Honest Answer" CHRIS ALEISTER Versus HARDCORE JON Versus "The Path of Destruction" MAYHEM (f/Oz) We've got new guys! Here at FUSE -- we put them in the ring and see what they can do! Last week, we saw the starting point of the Path of Destruction, as Mayhem made his presence felt in a big way! This week, he'll step into the ring in his first (sanctioned) FUSE match, and we promise you that these boys won't back down! Singles Match "No Gimmicks Needed" MATTHEW RANDALL Versus "The Osaka Assassin" GIDEON FONTAINE Gideon had a very impressive "match" last week, while Matthew Randall was caught in the clutches of Corvus! One man seeks to rebound while the other seeks to continue his roll in what should be a phenomenal technical bout -- the likes of which you can only see on FUSE! Grudge Bout Special Referee: "Frenchie" JOAQUIN PIERRE "The Canadian Dream" JACQUE NOREAULT Versus "The Obsidian Nightmare" CORVUS Last week's epic final confrontation between Joshua Kosidlo and Jacque Noreault was, in many fans' views, ruined thanks to a pre-match attack by Corvus with a car! Now, in the most unlikely of events, it's Noreault himself who'll be facing the demonic Corvus -- is it possible the Canadian will be looking for redemption on Kosidlo's behalf? And can the Frenchman, Joaquin Pierre, actually be expected to be neutral given his own history with the Obsidian Nightmare? Mixed Tag-Team Match ELLIOTT ROLLINS and WILLIAM "Slick Willy" MCDORMANT Versus "The Belle of the Ball" MACKENZIE MALONE and "The Hollywood Hellion" DUNCAN DRAKE Two weeks ago, Duncan Drake STOLE the Young Lion's Cup from Elliott Rollins with the use of an 8x10" signed picture! Last week, Elliott Rollins and his manager crashed Drake's celebration in Corpus Christi, and Drake ended up wearing a tuba! Now, Duncan will face Rollins and his manager, Slick Willy, William McDormant, with his valet, MacKenzie Malone as his only backup! Is Duncan Drake doomed, or does the Hellion have a trick or two left in his gun against the number-one contender to the LiveWire championship? Pick Your Partner Tag Team Bout "The Samoan Silencer" TIMO BOLAMBA and a Partner of His Choosing Versus FUSE Gateway Champion JACOB MCKAIL and a Partner of His Choosing Timo Bolamba and the Affliction are at war! Last week, we saw the debut of Koa Lukuna, the newest behemoth to join the Affliction, as they destroyed Jeb Stewart. This week, the Affliction's champion, FUSE's Gateway Champion, will have his pick of partners as he faces Timo Bolamba and a partner of his choosing! Is the Samoan Silencer 100% after the attack from Lukuna two weeks ago? And who will Timo choose to face McKail and his partner in the Pit! PLUS! What is the condition of Joshua Kosidlo after his harrowing night in Corpus Christi? We'll find out! What will Smitty T. Duluth's answer to Monet's demand for a no-retribution contract, and, if he says no, what will the Signal-Pirate's response be? Global Wrestling has closed its doors! Will any of their top stars be coming to FUSE? Will Dave Gibson ever get the five minutes he's owed with Prince Pride? Plus, Jonathan Rhine will ACTUALLY be in the arena tonight! All this plus appearances by the Highwaymen, El Diablo, and so much more -- only at The Pit in Albuquerque!
