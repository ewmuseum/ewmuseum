SCCW Rolls on into Spartan Country!

21 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers) Proudly Brings to You... TEMPTATION! Monday, August 25, 2008 from the Jack Breslin Student Events Center at Michigan State University. (Internet airdate: Wednesday, August 27!) Main Event Tag-Team Action! "The Lion" Lance Marshall & Elliott. Fucking. Rollins. Vs. "Peerless" Hunter Sabuani & The SCCW Universal Champion Aimz Two Dream Teams, One Match! Lance Marshall and Hunter Sabuani have a storied rivalry that dates back nearly a decade. Elliott Rollins and Amy Campbell just yelled at each other a few weeks ago, but that's enough for her. Sabuani's loyalty to Campbell's "friends" in the Dead Man's Hand is... sketchy... at best. Can Aimz count on the Raja of Roguishness against the Highwayman and the Highwayman adjunct? Or is it two on one on one in the ring in East Lansing? And what part, if any, will Jonathan Rhine play in this match after yet another confrontation with the SCCW Champion? Four-Man Elimination Match for the #1 Contendership to the Elite Championship! SARS (NFW) Vs. "The Model Citizen" Chandler Tsonda (PRIME) Vs. "The Elven Warrior" Lantlas (EPW) Vs. "The False Prophet" Wyatt Connors (SCCW) New Frontier Wrestling's Steve Knox is the Elite Champion after the events of Colossus V, but that means he'll have to defend the championship! To do so, he'll have to beat one of his rivals, the inimitable SARS! Or maybe Evolution Pro Wrestling's Soul Survivor tournament winner Lantlas! Or new PRIME Universal Champion, Chandler Tsonda! Or even SCCW's own "False Prophet," the recently changed Wyatt Connors! Whoever comes out of this match, Knox's challenge will be to hold onto the title! Will the SCCW fans root for the hated Connors? Will Knox himself be in attendance? For the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Third Revelation" Sean Sterling Vs. "The Path of Destruction" Mayhem (w/Malice & Misfit) There are few men in Sin City Championship Wrestling who have ever called out the Path of Destruction like Sean Sterling did in Heaven & Hell! It earned the Third Revelation a spear into the metal bars that held up the second ring and a pinfall loss, but it also earned Sean a shot at the Gateway Championship - a title he's wrestled for before in FUSE! Now the Wild Card of the Dead Man's Hand, after successfully defending the Gateway Championship twice, looks to climb one step closer to a shot at the SCCW Universal Championship, but he'll have to put Sean Sterling away twice in a row to do it! Is It Over? "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy (w/Katsidy) Vs. BloodAngel Two weeks ago at All-In, BloodAngel delivered the beating of a lifetime to the woman Phillip Kennedy works for, only to have Lance Marshall step in to stop it! Now the former World Champion will get his hands on another member of Desade's Hand, the new Strength in Numbers champion, young Phillip Kennedy, and we doubt making a statement about claiming a tag-title shot is going to be on Ed Lambert's mind! Last week, Desade mentioned obtaining Kennedy a "real" tag team partner -- whoever that man (or woman) is, will they be called on to save the Big Stack from another beating? These Two are Gonna Be What? Lori Equinox Vs. Jadian Bridden (w/Julissa Minorez) Okay, look all the way at the bottom of this lineup, and then come back. We'll wait. See that? Next week, Equinox and Bridden will be teaming up (though both will likely have an ulterior motive). This week, however, the newcomer who went to war with Jay Draven last week will be facing the youngest member of the Dead Man's Hand! What will this mean for their partnership next week? Can the underhanded tactics Bridden and his girl work when they'll have to rely on Equinox against Charlie Ramone and King Blueberry next week? What a tangled web we weave, when first we practice to deceive! He Got Pulled Out of a Crowd! "The Modern Day Warrior" Jay Draven & Paul Searle Vs. Illuminado & El Angel Verde Illuminado and Angel Verde are totally not En Fuego and El Diablo Verde -- just ask Wyatt Connors' people. But these two disciples of the False Prophet will be facing... look, we say a "unique team" a lot, but Jay Draven found his teammate in the upper level last week and, from what we've learned, has been putting the kid through nine kinds of hell to prepare for his bid to challenge for the Strength in Numbers Championships! What has Jay made the kid into in a week's time? We're not sure, but tune in to find out! Can the Roll Continue? Dualta Sheridan Vs. Austin "The Wombat" King After squeaking out a win against Josef Basturk last week, Dualta Sheridan will return to action against the Awesome Aussie, Austin King! Will Sheridan's path to the top continue, or will the one thing that the Wombat has been denied finally come to pass: a victory? Dualta's rise to the top goes right through America's Favorite Australian, this week at Michigan St.! PLUS! - With Chandler Tsonda scheduled to wrestle for the Number-One Contender Spot for the Elite Championship and Lindsay Troy and Tyler Rayne a week away from crashing (literally) into Temptation, will there be more PRIMEates in Michigan? - Are the wars between Edward Lambert & Alexandra Pierce and Amy Campbell & Jonathan Rhine really over, or are they just starting? - Last week, the fans saw Hiro Saotome and Adam Calloway... but nostalgia isn't all it's cracked up to be. Calloway is out of shape and Saotome is happily retired. Will we ever see them in Sin City again? - We saw Dr Kensington unafraid in the face of the Raja of Roguishness (though that could be because of his lack of knowledge as much as anything else). What new Hijinks will our new leader get into? SCCW Temptation! Two Weeks Away! From the Scotiabank Centre in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada! A Windfall Match for the SCCW LiveWire Title! (The wrestler that picks up the fall wins the championship!) Jadian Bridden & Lori Equinox Vs. King Blueberry & Charlotte Ramone (c)
