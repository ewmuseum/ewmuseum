Killzone 37 Is On The Air!

3 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killzone comes to the world LIVE from the Van Andel Arena in Grand Rapids, Michigan! The Reign of Chaos Tournament continued with two more first round matches. Johnny Lexicon and Claudius Augustus faced off in a Chaotic Cage match to determine who would move onto the semi-final round of the tournament while Leah Adams faced off with a very reluctant Kid Cool in a Street fight to determine the final first round match up. The mystery man once again struck and this time he has cost FUSE money by striking a fan and then proceeding to kick the living crap out of him afterwards. Who is this mystery man and how much longer before the fans, and workers, in FUSE find out who he is? In singles action former FUSE Universal Champion, and no doubt future Hall of Famer, John Covel stepped into the ring against 'Mean' Dean Jokerlin. Could the announcer turned wrestler find it in himself to give the former champ a run for his money? In tag team action the hilarious duo known as The RPG took on The Cowboys From Hell. The CFH have been hell-bent, as of late, to remove the stains of their former gimmicks from their resume and they no doubt were looking to do just that at the expense of their young opponents. Did the game loving fan favorites find a way to stumble into victory, like last time, or did CFH do what they've claimed they're going to by dominating them? In other singles action former LiveWire Champion Nathan O'Neill took on one of the fans most beloved men, Fred Fusion. Fred has quietly put together a pretty impressive record within FUSE but was he able to continue that climb or did O'Neill, or possibly The Legacy, have something up their sleeves to derail him? Of course no one can forget the Main Event of the evening as Clinton Sage, fresh off losing his PTC championship, put his FUSE Universal Championship on the line, in a Revolution rematch, against rival Jacob McKail. Who would walk away from the night as the champion? All of this and of course tensions heated up between The Alliance and Aimz as well as Timo and Tanaka. Hoyt Williams showed up and was up to his usual tricks that got the fans fired up. Check out all this action, and more, below. Killzone 37
