Perfection in a Title

16 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is a secret to no one that Fred Fusion recently realised that the FUSE Liverwire Title belt was, in his own words, "the perfect FUSION title belt". Only after defeating Cyrus Raynes for the title at Killzone, what is the Fredster going to do with it? Witnesses are said to have seen the new Liverwire Champion leave the arena in an excited rush after the title match, with his new title belt in one hand, and a bag of props and decorations in the other. What will happen now that the Livewire belt is in the hands of someone as emotionnaly unstable as Fred Fusion? How will the belt affect Fred's imaginary federation and vice versa? Tune into KillZone to find out!
