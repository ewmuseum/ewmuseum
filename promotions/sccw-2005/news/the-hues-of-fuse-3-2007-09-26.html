SCCW — Column

The Hues of FUSE #3

26 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the Hues of FUSE! My name is Flux, and I’ll be your host along this journey. This week’s edition covers a lot, including the upcoming PPV and FUSE on ESPN, so forgive a few brief sections. Also, Jonathan Rhine once again graces our presence with a Hues exclusive statement. Stay tuned. First off, former Global champions present in FUSE? Am I the only one excited about this? Time will tell whether these guys can hack it in the big leagues, but I doubt any FUSE fan will be disappointed when the smoke clears. Don’t let me down. Whoa, wait. More big names back, and in FUSE? Seymour Almasy, Damien Cruz, and Jacob McKail. I think it’s definitely safe to say that some of the new guys around need to start stepping up their game. FUSE is surrounding itself with the best in the business and I have nothing but high hopes for the future. In the first ever Uproar, Jason Cruise retains his LiveWire Championship in his first defense. Congratulations to him, and may he have a successful run bringing the title some of its former glory. Of course the match couldn’t have been complete without both El Diablo and Sean Sterling making a visit to the ring. Speaking of Sean Sterling, am I the only one personally scared for what he may do to Mayson Colby in the ring? He is insane! I personally don’t like the idea of him being associated with the company, let alone wrestling in sanctioned matches. The man’s a lawsuit waiting to happen. Clinton Sage and Lane Stevens teaming against Jonathan Rhine and Aimz. Perhaps the biggest tag team match in the history of FUSE, and it did not disappoint. Of course, there was a minor incident involved Michele Thorton which lead to an epic stare down between Sage and Rhine. Perhaps a small preview of chaos to come? This leads us into my earlier promise. A Hues exclusive statement from Mr. Jonathan Rhine himself. Flux: Good evening, Mr. Rhine. You had a statement you’d like to make? Jonathan Rhine: Last week I was focusing while I was in the ring. I often get into something of a zone when I’m fighting, and I don’t really pay attention to a lot of things. Unfortunately, one of those things I didn’t pay attention to was Michele Thorton. What I did was an accident, and I’m very sorry for hurting her. I hope that she, her father, and the rest of the world that what happened was in no way intentional and that I wish I could take it back. The ring is a dangerous place and it is a risk any time you go out there, wrestler or valet, that you are going to get hurt. But again, I extend a sincere apology to Miss Thorton and everyone involved. Flux: Our thoughts go out to Miss Michele Thorton. I’m sure that was very much appreciated, Mr. Rhine. One can only hope that your message is well received. Mr. Rhine and myself tried to reach both Clinton Sage and Smitty T. Duluth. Neither were available for comment at this time. More on this as it becomes available. FUSE on ESPN Rumor is circulating backstage that Sean Sterling and Mayson Colby will finally meet in the ring. Will Colby get his revenge, or will Sterling quite frankly kill the fellow? Also, I’ve heard that The Affliction and The Highwaymen have something big in store for everyone. Naturally, titles will be on the line as champions are in the house. This is shaping up to be a promising event. Not even halfway to the upcoming Pay Per View and Smitty T. Duluth has already signed a huge matchup. Seymour Almasy vs Damien Cruz. It will be interesting to see how things shape themselves over the coming weeks. Oh! The name. STD announced earlier, to yours truly, that the upcoming Pay Per View will be named ‘Defiance’. You can expect this to be the most prestigious event of the year. I’m certainly excited. Time for the Flux’s rankings for this week. Universal Champion- Clinton Sage 1) Jonathan Rhine 2) Aimz 3) Timo Bolamba Gateway Champion- Jonathan Rhine 1) Lane Stevens 2) Sean Sterling 3) Ethan Frost 4) Fred Fusion 5) Wyatt Colton LiveWire Champion- Jason Cruise 1) El Diablo 2) Mayson Colby 3) Paul Cain 4) Cyrus Raynes 5) Seymour Almasy 6) Carson Riley 7) Joshua Kosidlo 8) Jacque Noreault 9) Jeb Stewart 10) Prince Pride Yes, there is a method to my madness. No, I don’t feel like sharing it with you. Get over it. Take this as you will. Prove me wrong, or prove me right. I’ll still sleep at night. Ha ha! That concludes this brief edition of the Hues of FUSE. I hope you enjoyed it as much as I did. Until next time, I’ll be watching as FUSE shines through. -Flux
