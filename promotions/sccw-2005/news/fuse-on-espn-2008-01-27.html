FUSE ON ESPN

27 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUSE Wrestling (in association with STD Entertainment, Inc.) PRESENT FUSE! On ESPN L-I-V-E LIVE Saturday, February 2, 2008 at 8PM Eastern (5PM Pacific) ONLY on ESPN -- The Worldwide Leader in Sports! From the Jobing.com Arena in Glendale, Arizona, next door to University of Phoenix Stadium and the night before Super Bowl XLII, FUSE brings you a titanic card like only FUSE can do! "We are FUSE! We are Athletes!" DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! Ladder Match for the FUSE Gateway Championship! "The Samoan Silencer" Timo Bolamba Vs. "The Master of Manipulation" El Diablo Vs. "The Red Raver" Aimz Vs. Jacob McKail (c) Four of the most talented superstars in all of FUSE will be put in the ring with a ladder and only one rule: climb it and pull down the FUSE Gateway Championship! Timo Bolamba and El Diablo have had their issues the last few weeks, as have Aimz and McKail... but will Aimz and Timo or Diablo and McKail be able to set aside their differences long enough to emerge victorious, or will greed for the gold get in the way? Non-Title Match! "The Second Coming" Sean Sterling Vs. Christian Darke Vs. "New Life" Jonathan Rhine Sean Sterling defeated BloodAngel to get his chance at Jonathan Rhine... only to have his moment usurped by Smitty T. Duluth's hiring of the FINAL Global Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion, and inserting him into this match! Now Jonathan Rhine, fresh off a harrowing post-match beating from Malachi, has to face both the Second Coming and the debuting Christian Darke at the same time, with the only saving grace being that his FUSE Universal Championship is NOT on the line! Title Vs. Trophy -- For the FUSE LiveWire Championship AND the Young Lion's Cup! "The Hollywood Hellion" Duncan Drake Vs. Elliott "Fucking" Rollins (c) Elliott Rollins has done the inconceivable -- he defeated Jason Cruise for his LiveWire championship, ending the Prophecy's four-month reign as champion! But he's jumped from the frying pan into the fire as his war with Duncan Drake takes on a newfound meaning, as not only is Drake's Young Lion's Cup trophy on the line, but so is Rollins' newly-won LiveWire championship! A Legend Returns -- Tag Team Bout! "The Foundation" Tony Pride and "The Savior" Prince Pride Vs. "The Prophecy" Jason Cruise (w/Kansas-Marie) and Eugene "High Society" Robinson Jason Cruise has had all he can stands of the Prides, and he can't stands no more! Now, joined by the OTHER man who trained him, former National Wrestling Council superstar Eugene Robinson, Cruise seeks revenge for his and Kansas-Marie's treatment at the hands of the first-time-ever team of Prince and Tony Pride! Will the legendary success of a father rub off on the legendary losing streak of the son? And will High Society have any kind of ring rust after his time off since the NWC's closure? Triple-Threat Texas Death Elimination Match for the AWC LiveWire Championship! "The Frenchman" Joaquin Pierre Vs. "The Obsidian Nightmare" Corvus Vs. "The Paladin" Joshua Kosidlo (c) Both Joaquin Pierre and Joshua Kosidlo have all the reasons in the world to hate the demonic Corvus -- he's laid out both men, and he even ran the Paladin over with a car! Now both men will get their hands on the Obsidian Nightmare at the same time, and the only way to win is if TWO men can be incapacitated past the point of being able to answer a ten count! All this -- and the Paladin's Atlantic Wrestling Club LiveWire Championship will be on the line as well! Oz is Banned from Ringside! Jadian Bridden Vs. Mayhem Last week, in an impromptu match, Mayhem managed to defeat young Jadian Bridden only through the timely interference of his manager, Oz! How will the Path of Destruction cope without having the Path of Distraction to rely on? Can the plucky young Texan upend the monstrous three-hundred-plus pounder when it's a straight one-on-one match? Tag-Team Bout! "The Osaka Assassin" Gideon Fontaine & "The WyldChild" Zakk Wylde Vs. Cyrus Raynes & "Perfect" Raymond Pierce Yes, you read that right! After facing each other last week (at least until a "knee injury" removed He Who Never Errs from the bout), Cyrus Raynes and Raymond Pierce are now the most unlikely tag-team in FUSE, facing old friends Gideon Fontaine and Zakk Wylde, the latter making his FUSE debut! Can Raynes and Pierce function as a team against the former NEW superstars? And will Raymond Pierce's trick knee act up again? Plus! Finally, the answer to a question six weeks in the making: Who Is Monet? What is the condition of Samantha Pierce after her alleged attack at the hands of her uncle? Who was Tony Pride talking to Smitty T. Duluth about? We're one day before the Super Bowl and on THE #1 Sports Channel in the World -- surely nothing will go wrong? And we hear that Smitty T. Duluth has been behind closed doors all week long, but no one knows why -- is there another big signing, or something else entirely? Many more surprises, the likes of which only FUSE can bring!
