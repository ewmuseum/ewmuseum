Seattle Lineup Announced!

13 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling (in association with the Kent Fusiliers and Final Curtain Entertainment, proudly bring you... TEMPTATION: Sinning in Seattle! L-I-V-E LIVE Sunday, February 21, 2010 from the Bank of America Arena at Hec Edmundson Pavilion on the campus of the University of Washington in Seattle! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! "The Black Sheep" Jared Sykes Vs. "The Lion" Lance Marshall The Lion and The Lamb have faced each other once in SCCW, a qualifying match for the Dome of Doom won by Marshall! Now they'll go one-on-one, one more time in Seattle! Marshall comes off a stellar performance in the GTT, making it to the Elite Eight before falling to Violence Jack, while Sykes will see his first in-ring action since the new Dead Man's Hand screwed him out of the Gateway Title, and even though Jared took that and more out of Lane Stevens' hide last week, can he beat an angry giant, screwed over by the very same "GateWire" champion last week? The Rematch! "The Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy Vs. "The Spider in the Web" Desade One year ago, the Big Stack and the Spider battled for thirty minutes before the clock beat them both! Though Desade owns a victory over Phillip in the build-up to Colossus, it was AFTER Kennedy suffered a serious concussion! Now Phillip is 100% again and embroiled in a heated feud with Jonathan Rhine! Pierce wrestles in SCCW for the first time since she's turned over a new leaf, but one wonders her condition, both physically after two weeks of attacks by Xavier Kannon and emotionally after a draining trip through the GTT! The war to settle the score happens in Seattle! Not the Way You'd Wanna Debut! Devlin Shannon Vs. A. Monday Devlin Shannon and his partner, Matt Elias, have yet to wrestle as a team in SCCW, but, at the orders of Adrienne St. Germain, Shannon will make his debut in Seattle against one-half of the Strength in Numbers champions! But which one? Now that Daddy Daughter Day has aligned with Lane Stevens' Dead Man's Hand, has St. Germain given Shannon more than he can possibly handle? August Monday lost to Spacely in Edmonton, and the SIN Champions were denied their revenge, so Daddy's Little Demon will be coming to Seattle looking to dish out some misery! The Snake and His Only Predator! "The Trailer Park Messiah" Terrence Kingsley Vs. The Mongoose Many would argue that Terrence Kingsley is the most important signing in Sin City history, and the Pit Viper makes his in-ring debut in Seattle! Kingsley, who made waves in the GTT Tournament, including a Match of the Year candidate against Desade, seeks to make a name for himself against the masked Mongoose! No one knows anything about Kingsley's opponent, including Terrence himself! See Kingsley's first SCCW match in Seattle! Triple Threat Rules! Special Guest Referee: James von Doom! Jacob McKail Vs. "The Devil's Reject" Legion (w/Luci4 and Stigmata) Vs. "The Valkyrie" Suzanne Larsson Last week, the man they call von Doom stole a victory over the Atomic Surf Dogs, right out from under Suzanne Larsson! Now he'll be serving as special guest referee as his Larsson takes on the Devil's Reject, a man who the Puppeteer has gone to war with on several occasions, and a man that von Doom once(?) managed! What kind of impartiality will von Doom show? Is Jacob McKail still aligned with the Puppetmaster's new Civilization? And will the Valkyrie come to Seattle looking for revenge or for victory? All this plus... the last time SCCW visited Seattle, we experienced a taste of the Seattle Curse – will it get worse the second time? And just what are Lane Stevens' plans now that he has the richest prize in Sin City – and that, for the first time, the Dead Man's Hand is in possession of all the gold in the federation? SCCW DEFIANCE III! LIVE! One month away! March 7, 2010 from the Stan Sheriff Center on the campus of the University of Hawaii! Find out who NEW Universal Champion Lane Stevens will defend his title against this week on Temptation! Two Falls Match! First Fall for the SCCW LiveWire Championship, second for the SCCW Gateway Championship! "The Ace in the Hole" Jadian Bridden (LiveWire/Gateway Champion) Vs. Participants to be announced in Seattle! Also featuring the Hall of Fame inductions of Jason Cruise, Smitty T. Duluth, and ONE MORE MAN to be announced in Seattle!
