Killzone Airs!

15 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killzone airs from the TD BankNorth Garden in Boston, Massachusetts! A great night of action was lined up as The Affliction walked into Killzone 35 with TWO huge title matches in front of them. Lane Stevens put his LiveWire Championship on the line, for the first time, since defeating Nathan O’Neill for the belt, as he faced off against Cyrus Raynes. Would the impressive rookie continue his undefeated streak and end Lane’s reign before it even had a chance to begin? Timo Bolamba teamed up with his good friends, The Alliance, to take on the three-man team, and we use that term very loosely, of Tanaka, Kid Cool, and Dave Gibson. Who walked out of his six-man war with their arms raised? Of course in the main event John Covel put his FUSE Universal Championship on the line against his most hated rival, Clinton Sage, in an attempt to once more humiliate Sage with a loss and get some revenge for the disrespect that Sage showed to Covel’s dead father. Did the champion of FUSE hold off the leader of The Affliction or did Sage walk out with a second title to add to The Afflictions growing gold rush? Tune in and find out! http://sccw.ewmuseum.com/results/43
