Vitrone III) "Adamant" Aaron Anderson IV) "The Hammer" Alex Cote ~~~ - Trixxster - ~~~ Subject: Let the Games Begin. ***We open in the hallways of a school. The lights are out, and everything is quiet. Except for a small whistling sound. The cameraman begins wandering around the halls, and after a while, we can see a janitor. He is dressed in blue coveralls, and has a mop in hand. He is busy mopping the school hallway, which is most likely his job. He cameraman then whistles, and the janitor turns around. Most would expect to see 'The Freaky School Janitor' Scrappy McGoo, but this isn't the case. This janitor is very tall, and has his face painted like a clown. He smirks to the camera, and then in from a classroom comes The Trixxster. The tiny man is wearing his patented red and black jester's outfit, and has his jester's cap on head. He stands smiling beside the clown/janitor, and begins speaking.*** Trixxster: Well hello SWC! Hehehe, allow me to introduce myself to everybody who might not know me, I am The Trixxster. Your future SWC Television Champion! <Trixxster gives a devious smile to the camera.> Trixxster: And the man standing beside me here is a man who you will all get to know very quickly. Hehehe, the most talkative man in the sport of wrestling! I give you.... The Great Flanko! <The giant clown rips off his blue coveralls to reveal a white t-shirt, baggy clown pants, and red suspenders.> Flanko: ......<grunt>...... Trixxster: Hehehe, now that the intros are done with, I guess it is time for the "trash talking". I have come to SWC for one reason, and one reason only. Err.... actually... two reasons. One being that Mr.[name withheld] is rather pestering. <Trixxster winks.> And number two being that I want more fame and gold! It's everybody's goal in life, so do you actually think a jester would be different? Hehehe, not at all! <Trixxster walks over to the a row of lockers and sits down in front of them.> Trixxster: Now I would have liked to be involved in the big Royal Rumble for the Heavyweight Title, but I can wait for that. It seems as though Mr.[name withheld] has placed me in a little tournament for the Television Title. And after looking at the names involved, I must say... WHAT A JOKE! I'm facing a freakin' school janitor in the First Round! Is this for real? Flanko: ......<grunt>...... Trixxster: See! Even Flanko is angry with that. Well I can tell you all this right now. The Trixxster will not stand for this! When January 31st rolls around, I am going to prove to the world that I deserve better. So if that means kicking some old janitor's butt, than so-be-it! <Trixxster pauses and thinks for a moment.> Trixxster: Scrappy McGoo, hey? Although I've never heard the name, nor have I ever seen you before, I can tell you this much. An example will be made of you! I don't care if you are some old fart, or some young muscular janitor. The match will be the same either way. Hehe, me running circles around you and your little mop! With the big man Flanko in my corner, I can't lose! And I certainly won't lose to some 'Freaky Janitor'! <Trixxster removes his jester's cap, and his long red hair flops down.> Trixxster: So after I humiliate the janitor in Round One, it looks like I will be tossed into some foolish Three Way Match for the title. And it will either be some homie named Westside Walavan.... <Trixxster gives the "westside" hand symbol and giggles.> ... or some surfer named Shawn Parker.... <Trixxster does a "hang loose" hand gesture.> ... with me in the ring. Against one more guy. And that will either be Marcus Nyghtwulf, who obviously doesn't have a clue how to spell! <Flanko hears this and lets out a chuckle.> ... Or some guy named Sentinel... <Trixxster salutes tha camera.> ... and after looking at these four names, I think you are all staring at the first ever SWC Television Champion! You see, I don't care what past accomplishments any of you bums have. And I don't care how many titles you won in the past! All I care about is beating that moron janitor in the First Round, and then humiliating two other losers in the Three Way! And for that Three Way, it seems as though the booking committee have chosen some "special" stipulation. Hehehe, which will work to the advantage of The Trixxster! I will open up my bag of tricks for SWC on the 31st, and it will stay open for a very long time! <Trixxster pushes himself up from the wall and smiles.> Trixxster: This is only the beginning SWC. Hehehe, after I win the Television Title at Birthday Bash, you will be seeing a lot more of the jester! Boys and girls....that's a promise! And you will soon learn, that a jester never breaks his promises. And if anybody out there in TV land has their doubts, well...... THE JOKE'S ON YOU!!!!!!!!! ***Trixxster bursts out laughing as he motions for Flanko to follow him. They begin walking down the school hallway, and the camera then turns off.*** ~~~ - "Spotlight" V.D. Vitrone - ~~~ Subject: None Given. [Damn you Marfaro!] [ The camera fades in to the former master arena of National Extreme Wrestling. After two months of silence, a ring crew is finally starting to take down the ring - while the janitors mop up the blood-stained floors. This arena means two whole years of hard work. Two years of blood, sweat, and tears. Two whole years of wrestlers getting famous. ] [ And among those who've become famous is a man who many may consider to be the N.E.W. "icon" himself. Vitrone. ] [ And as the camera pans to a shot of the arena, through the rows of emptiness, is a man whom just sits there. Vitrone. ] VDV - "Y'know, when the N.E.W. closed, I was shocked. It was like a home to me for _two_ whole years. It gave me the glory that was needed to make the name V.D. Vitrone famous. He was a business man. . . and rather than go bankrupt and move to Mexico, he decided it would be best to close doors. Ya' gotta do what ya' gotta do, I guess. Vitrone? The 'Vindicator' who's been tearing through the competition. " [ Pause. ] " Well, it's sad fer' me to say, but the Vindicator is retired. . . Finished. He's done. Whatever he's accomplished in the past two years stands much higher than the dream he originally wanted to achieve. The Vindicator can _happily_ call it quits after his long and illustrius career. . . [ The camera slowly begins to fade to black....] [ Until it is cut off by the same voice as before. ] VDV - " Hey, sugar daddies, where ya' goin'? " [ The camera fades back in. ] " Jus' because I said the 'Vindicator' retired, don't mean that V.D. Vitrone is ready ta' hang up them boots, 'cuz I assure you that isn't the case. I'm twenty-seven years old and I have already accomplished more than all but maybe five wrestlers in this sport. . ." " But bein' in the top five isn't good enough fer' tha 'Spotlight'. He wants ta' be numero-uno, and believe me now, baws, I won't stop until I achieve that status. " "But we'll start wit' somethin' easy. The Sooner Wrestling Council. The most recent destination where the legacy of V.D. Vitrone has decided ta' wreak havoc over. An' well, unlike when I started in N.E.W. as a rookie, I'm the favourite ta' capture the World Title." "An' fer' those of ya' keepin' track at home, this would be my _sixth_ world title. But this one is really a shoe-in 'cuz allz I gotz ta' do is step into the ring with nineteen under sumbitches, and toss each and every one of their mutile carcasses over the top rope, causin' them to pop their hemroids as they hit the floor. " [ Vitrone smirks. ] " Yes. . . fer' those of ya' who are mentally challenged, that would mean that this match is a battle royale. A battle royale which boasts some decent talents such as 'Drew Tucker & Liam Rogan. But those two men, like the majority of the people in this rumble, must've read that V.D. Vitrone was in this rumble - tuckin' their tails between their legs and runnin' away. " " Who can blame 'em, though? I would have the same fear if I've ever had to step into the ring with the _greatest_ wrestler in the history of this sport. But, since I'm not one for cloning, there's no way that would be happenin' anytime soon. " [ Uhoh... the infamous smirk once again. ] " Now, now, just so I don't have any complaints about yours truly, not paying enough attention to his lower rank of competition, I _will_ make a note of whom man who've had the sack ta' match me word fer' word . And no, it's not that ugly little troll, Andrew Tucker. It's a man who I believe goes by the name of 'Adamant' Aaron Anderson. . ." " A man who shares the same initials as a damn battery. " " Aaron, ace, listen ta' me, _once - 'cuz ya' aint worth my breath, that I could be restin' fer' my date with this hot chick later on. But, anyways, Aaron, I'll warn ya' now. Stop tryin' ta' steal my thunder. Haven't ya' noticed that I'm, by far, the highest paid wrestler on the entire SWC roster? Ya' ole' [name withheld] & [name withheld] planned that on accident? Remember, this is wrestling, we don't have salary caps. That means the reason that they're payin' me ma' wad is 'cuz I put people's fat asses inta' the seats. An' well, it jus' wouldn't look right if the federation is represented by a dumbass pot-smokin' freak like yourself who preaches for hours 'bout 'evelution.' " " Hell, I thought that was the job of that guy in Canada. Eth.. Eth.. Ethan de Something... But that's not important, Aaron. What's important is that ya've drawn my attention, an' now I'm gunnin' fer' ya. . . " " Only problem is, I'm gonna make ya' famous. I'm gonna make ya' the man with the worst battle royale timin' in the history of SWC. " "And ain't _nobody_ gonna break this record. " [ Please, no. Damnit, no! Argh... (BOOM!) Too Late. There's that damn smirk again. ] " And _that's_ somethin' worth pointin' the Spotlights at. " [ Fade to black. ] ~~~ - "Adamant" Aaron Anderson - ~~~ Subject: I don't think I got one. Yup, I sure didn't. [SCENE: SWC Interview Room, where we see the 6'10" mastedon "Adamant" Aaron Andrews sitting on a gray stool in the middle of the room. Wearing a black shirt with a neon blue diamond in the middle of it, black sweatpants and black Nikes, Andrews looks hard into the camera, focusing his blue eyes onto the camera, and not looking away. When the camera finishes its panning so that Andrews is in the center of the picture, Andrews begins.] AAA: As the SWC Heavyweight Title Battle Royal rapidly approaches, I have begun thinking about the negatives and positives of this battle royal. The obvious positive is that I could become the SWC Heavyweight Champion in only my first professional wrestling match. That would play a tremendous role in my self-confidence. I'd be on the top of the world after only one win. That positive is enlightening, but there is a frightening negative that crossed my mind earlier: What if I lose? What if I'm tossed out, and the referee orders me back to the showers? What if I leave Tulsa, Oklahoma, without the SWC Heavyweight Title. What happens if I walk into my home in Virgina beltless? What if? Those negative questions, and more, crossed my mind earlier today. And after thinking incessantly about the topics, the answer came to me: It won't happen. That's it. I don't accept failure, and the SWC Heavyweight Battle Royal isn't an exception. I don't care what the stakes are -- I'm not leaving Tulsa without clutching that Title. Call it arrogance. Say what you want. But after this Sunday... You'll call me YOUR Sooner Wrestling Council Heavyweight Champion. I can guarantee it.
