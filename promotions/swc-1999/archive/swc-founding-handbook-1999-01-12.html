Staff .......................................................................... ....... I. The SWC in General .......................................................................... ....... SWC stands for Sooner Wrestling Council, and is one of the many imaginary fantasy wrestling leagues in cyberspace, in other words, the internet. The SWC exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative and a enjoyable gaming format. Ever dream of putting on tights and walking down that long aisle into the squared circle? Well, SWC is a fed where you create your own wrestling character and guide him to the World Title... or to nowhere. =) SWC was created by [name withheld], with [name withheld] later joining [name withheld] as
Co-President. [name withheld]'s first organization was the Elite Wrestling Corporation; while [name withheld] has had experience with several promotions. [name withheld] founded the EWC on June 19, 1998, and during the month of September, [name withheld] began Co-Running it with [name withheld]. However, [name withheld] on November 14, 1998 due to time constraints handed over the EWC to [name withheld] which he later turned it to TOP. Mr. [name withheld] and Mr. [name withheld] have also role-played in "big time" federations around the internet. It is fair to say that the front office has a lot of experience. ............................................ II. Rules ............................................ The SWC will not allow any "Out of the World" gimmicks. Meaning, the President will not tolerate wrestlers with gimmicks that are too far fetched. For example, I don't want any plumbers or clowns in our league. _NO_ comic book gimmicks, just keep it plain and simple. As a general rule of the thumb, applicants whose wrestlers are similar characters in the league that are already established in the SWC, are not admitted, even if they are the top role-players I mean, would you want to have five undertakers running around? _ALL_ wrestlers must be original and not taken off any "real" wrestlers. (i.e. No "Stone Cold's", "Nature Boy's", or "Canadian Crippler's".) You can only submit either One Singles Wrestler or One Tag Team in the SWC. People tend to enter four or five characters into one league and only pay attention to one. This is a problem in the SWC we have eliminated. Also keep in mind that if your character is in inactive for two straight weeks, he will be removed from the SWC Roster. In order to ensure each player receives sizable activity and participation in the SWC, the roster must be kept relatively small. The SWC allows 24 singles and 6 tag teams. This allows each wrestler a match once a week and the attention the player deserves in a league. Upon being admitted to the SWC, the President will ask for a sample role-play Here is an idea of what is expected of in your sample role-play. .......................................................................... ....... III. Participation .......................................................................... ....... Participation in the SWC is essential and the key to success. Roleplaying (See Roleplaying Regulations Below) takes up much of that participation. You are required to submit a minimum of one solid quality flash a week.This is your major flash, meaning it's your big time flash, that develops your character. Read Roleplaying Regulations below for full understanding of your role-play standards. Other ways of participation is sending in strats, sneak attacks, and getting involved in your current angles and storylines. We'll get into more detail as the league progresses. .......................................................................... ....... IV. Programs .......................................................................... ....... The SWC currently promotes one live weekly card and a summarized card per week: 1. Saturday Night Fever This is the live card of the week, usually 4-6 live matches which are in play-by-play format. The show will always have a Television title match; many angles will begin to unfold; and wrestlers will share their cheers and jeers of others. Hosted by Jim Houston and Buck Martinez. Due time for strats : Thursday. Due time for in-rings (spots are limited) : Thursday Due time for angle requests/sneak attacks : Wednesday 2. Tuesday Tango The summarized card of the week, will host 6-8 matches, usually of lower caliber and is hosted by Jim Houston and "Spicy" Joe Grass. Many debuts will occur on this show. The shows will be taped from small venues and serves as a stepping stone for wrestlers to launch their careers. Due time for strats : No strats Due time for in-rings (spots are limited) : Sunday. Due time for angle requests/sneak attacks : Wednesday. - Updates Updates are sent out on Sunday, and include everything from the current roster, to handler contacts, to future cards. Everything that you need is here. - Statin' the Facts This is sent out roughly everyday. A flash packet containing the newest flashes. * Pay-Per-Views Since SWC is a small promotion, we only hold one pay-per-view per year. Do not fret, we hold five supercards through out the year, which are equivalent to a small pay-per-view. - January: Birthday Brawl I The celebration of when the SWC turns one. Features all of the SWC's wrestlers excluding the Heavyweight Champion in a royal rumble. The winner receives a shot at the Heavyweight Champion at Wrestlefest I. - March: Wrestlefest 1 ($14.95) - PPV! The biggest event, the _only_ SWC Pay-Per-View event of the year! From the Myrad Convention Center in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Features the Birthday Brawl royal rumble winner against the SWC Heavyweight Champion. - May: Lord of the Ring 1. All of the SWC's wrestler's on SNF's leading up to this event compete in a tournament. The four finalists then fight there repopponents, and then the final two collide head-to-head in a "Falls Count Anywhere" match to become the Lord of the Ring! The winner receives a shot at the SWC Heavyweight Champion. - June: School's Out! The second biggest SWC event of the year. Features all of the SWC wrestlers fighting in a sixty foot high four level steel cage! Details will be given about this match as we near the event. The winner? SWC Heavyweight Champion title shot. - August: Summerfest I : "The Big Gig" The SWC is planning on saving enough money to travel up to Milwaukee, Wisconsin to show of it's talents at the biggest summer event of the year. Features the SWC Heavyweight Champion battling the School's Out! cage match winner. .......................................................................... ....... V. Roleplay Regulations .......................................................................... ....... In the SWC, emphasis on, good writing=good push. A long role-play doesn't necessarily mean that is a good role-play A well written quality 6k role-play can beat out a long, dull, boring, and dragging 12k role-play any day of the week. Remember the three most important things when creating a role-play : Content, Length, and Detail. Your flashes will be sent out daily in the "Statin' the Facts" flash packet. Failure to produce role-play of any sort, whether they be interviews, strats, or whatever, in a short period of time, will result in a cautionary warning from the President, and if the inactivity continues, your wrestler may be suspended or fired by SWC altogether. .......................................................................... ....... VI. Ground Rules .......................................................................... ....... Nobody is stopping you from having fun, and that is the main reason you come to SWC, is to have fun. But like all good things, there must be rules that each player must abide by, in consideration of SWC Council and your fellow players. Please follow these regulations: - DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hospitalize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the President. - DON'T attack announcers or any of the SWC staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want. - DON'T use excessive profanity. Often when you go into realization of your character, obscene language is all part of the game when it is hot and heavy that your wrestler is going through. However, dirty and very vulgar words will be edited out in your flashes. Words such as "ass" and "damn" will be allowed, though stronger words than it will not. You have to keep in mind such language is not acceptable in television programming. It is very possible to express your anger and extreme emotions without using a string of four letter words. - Any character handled by [name withheld], may have nothing to do with this company! Since [name withheld] is a egotistical assmonkey and the epitome of ignorance, he will have nothing to do with this Oklahoma-based corporation. And he doesn't want to anyway, so wahoo! .......................................................................... ....... VII. How does the SWC operate? .......................................................................... ....... SWC is a "booked" league. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the SWC council. Results are not made up of, with that single match in mind. Generally, it accounts for the bigger picture, meaning the angles, storylines, and so forth. And your basis of role-play helps a tremendous way in helping the match results. However, for instance, two good role-players have a match against each other, both have been flashing all week, and now it comes to conclusion that the President will take it upon themselves to determine results with a coherent storyline in mind. .......................................................................... ....... VIII. Titles .......................................................................... ....... The Sooner Wrestling Council currently sanctions four titles: - SWC Skyweight Championship : The Cruiserweight Title of the SWC. Weight limit 225 pounds. - SWC Television Championship : Universal Weight. The #2 Title in the SWC. Defended every week. The holder of this one is the workhorse of the promotion. - SWC Heavyweight Championship : Universal Weight. The #1 Title in the SWC. If you are the Heavyweight Champion you are by far the greatest wrestler in the SWC. - SWC Heavyweight Tag Team Championship : Universal Weight. #1 Tag Team Title in the SWC. * Note : Titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission, unless specified before the match. .......................................................................... ....... .
