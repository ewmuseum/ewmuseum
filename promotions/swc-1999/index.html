Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Sooner Wrestling Council announced itself on 12 January 1999 as, in its own words, “the only federation to be based in the great state of Oklahoma,” building a sixteen-wrestler roster in two weeks of what it called “top secret recruiting” and promising a debut supercard, Birthday Bash I, once the roster reached twenty-four.

Its own handbook barred any wrestler modeled on a real performer or an “out of the world” gimmick — “no plumbers or clowns,” it said, “no comic book gimmicks” — and planned to run out of the SWC Coliseum, a 3,000-seat arena of its own invention in downtown Tulsa, under four titles: Heavyweight, Television, Skyweight, and a tag championship.

Roleplays published ahead of Birthday Bash I set its two headline matches: a thirty-man battle royal for the vacant Heavyweight Championship, where “The Hammer” Alex Cote promised to finish by tossing “twenty nine punks” over the top rope, and a four-way Television Title tournament opening with The Trixxster — accompanied by a mute strongman he called The Great Flanko — drawn against a wrestler billed as “The Freaky School Janitor” Scrappy McGoo.

How Birthday Bash I, set for 31 January 1999, actually played out isn't in its surviving record.
