Hello, ladie... I mean, dudes. If you don't know me... then great! Being unknown is cool. Anyways, with me being an eWhore, you would expect a message from me sooner or later. And today, I come to this fair land[rspwf4L, eh] to talk about one of my newer ventures; East Coast Street-Fighting. What's ECSF, you ask? Well.... ECSF is four different letters of the alphabe.. :) Seriously though, ECSF is a fighting fed with quite an interesting concept. If I wasn't feeling dead tired[32 hours straight with no sleep], I would go into the whole hoop-la... but obviously, I won't. The basic idea is that you will have the chance to have your fighter act out his daily life, as if he were a real criminal dealing in whatever you want him to deal with. Every Tuesday, our weekly show; On The Run; not only showcases fights.. but also showcases segments in which the members The Brass take it upon themselves to advance your character's current storyline. This means your roleplays[Lock-Ups as they are termed in ECSF] are actually read. I kid you not. Anyway, I am looking for a couple of new fighters that will be committed to make ECSF as enjoyable as it possibly can be. About four or five fighters will do; what is really need is writers. If you're interested in writing for ECSF, e-mail me at [detail withheld] to get more information as to what each writing category entails. http://ecsf.projectefed.com Hope to see you[yeah, you.. stop picking your nose, by the way] in ECSF, as I like to work with new people. Don't hestitate to e-mail me at [detail withheld] if you need some clarification regarding the concept of ECSF or anything else, for that matter. Thanks, -Kamlesh -Is dead tired -Is the half cousin of Buh Buh Ray Dudley..
