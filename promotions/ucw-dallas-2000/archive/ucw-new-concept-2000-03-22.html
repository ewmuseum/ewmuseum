Ultimate Championship Wrestling: A new concept to e-wrestling?

22 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[UCW] Ultimate Championship Wrestling http://www.geocities.com/sportatorium/main.htm How much like real life should e-wrestling be? Booked feds are supposed to be the most "realistic" feds around, where the presidents act as real bookers, deciding who should win based on their own judgment. Most major alliances of feds recognize only one world champion, which makes sense. The NFL recognizes only one Super Bowl
Champion every year, as does any other sport their champion. But, are these in any way like the real world?
Or, I should say, do are they like the real world, today? Forgive me if I'm wrong, as I am not an expert on wrestling history, but for decades, up into the mid-80's, wrestling was dominated by the NWA, a widespread organization made up of hundreds of regions throughout the world, who all recognized one World Champion. But then the WWF came around. Then WCW broke off of the NWA and became a competing faction.
And as the NWA started to flounder, the ECW made its presence known. Now, the NWA never fell completely, and is still running today, but it is a far cry from the mainstay it was fifteen years ago. What we have now are three feds, each competing for dominance in TV ratings, wrestling talent, popularity, etc. And that's what I want to emulate.
I don't have the means to just up and start this off by myself. After all, I am only one man. But, as this grows, it will work. I want to start small and think big. What I'm starting out with is a roster of
20. Later, after the first few chinks have been worked out of the fed, as problems always come about at first, it'll expand to 30. The basic rules of the fed is that it would be booked, based on angles, and have one weekly card. A wrestler could roleplay how many times he wants, but that's NOT what's being judged. It's the wrestler's overall presence that's being judged. If you aren't roleplaying enough to get your character across, then it'll be accounted for. But, in the same way, you can over-expose yourself by doing too many roleplays. A good number would be two per week. This way the workload would be cut down, both for the staff and the handlers, and with a smaller roster everyone
would get at least one match a week. No one has any fun if they aren't involved in any matches. As the league continues to grow, an independent fed would be added to serve as the "minor league" for the UCW. Later I might even add a second indy. Either way, this is where it really gets big. The other two feds would be added at this point, one as a mainstream fed like the
UCW, and another resembling the more extreme ECW. That way everyone's happy, right? A few more independent feds will be added, and then will band together like the NWA and serve as the NCAA or minor league for the big three.
Now, I have seen some feds LIKE this. They're always real wrestler feds, and they hardly ever have interfed cards. Wrestlers jump from one fed to another in a span of weeks, and you can never keep up with what's going on. This is going to be a bit different. Contracts will be decided between the handler and the president of the respective federation, and they would have to be at least for one month. The better the wrestler, generally, the longer the contract. However, it won't be for so long that the handler gets tired of it. We want it to be fun, too, but a little more realistic than it's usually done. They could sign with any of the big three, although they can only be in one fed at a time. We could also have joint PPV's. Wrestling fans many times wonder if there could ever be a PPV pitting wrestlers from the
WWF against WCW. We can do that here, only with different names and faces. We could also have an annual event that decides the "Super Bowl
Champion" of the three feds in a tournament or something. You wouldn't be able to defend this title, just like in the NFL, the NBA, or whatever sport, but it would mean the holder of it was, in that year and time, was the best wrestler in the world. There could also be a page for ratings. People not joined to a fed would look over the cards and rate them by the action, angles, and match quality contained in it. This would encourage feds to have in-ring interviews, sneak attacks, and good, quality angles to keep interest and the quality of the cards high.
Below this, as stated above, would be the independent feds. They'll be grouped together NWA-style, with the five or six feds included as an alliance. Each fed would have their own regional titles, and they would recognize one heavyweight champion over all of them, who would travel from region to region defending the belt. If these regions continue to grow, then they may even be adopted as a fourth major fed to contend with, and their champion would be included in the tournament to decide the ultimate champion at year's end.
Right now, I really have nothing to show for it. In a year hopefully I will, but I need members, and not only for the UCW. I don't know what the two other major feds will be, whether they'll be just starting like the UCW or feds that have been around for months or years before. The names I've come up with are the IWA (International Wrestling
Association) and the XWX (Xtreme Wrestling Xperience). I need presidents for those feds and staff, as well as wrestlers. I don't expect it to "just happen". This will take some time, but if I have a handful of people who are like minded, we can pull this off. And wouldn't you want it to? I think it's a great idea, and hopefully you will too.
