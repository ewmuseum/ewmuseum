Smackdown Results

6 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The new commishener Jay Vistana came to the ring and welcomed everyone to the birth of the WWSF. He announced that there would be two main events this evening. The first one would be for the Women's Championship and would put the champ Debra in the ring with seven other females in an over the rope battle royale for the title. The second main event is for the Heavyweight Championship. The Undertaker will face Stone Cold Steve Austin. If Shane or any other member of the Corporate Ministry interfere in the match than the Undertaker will be DQ'd and the title will become vacant. Matches Begin.... Round 1 of King of the Ring Tournament *Mr. Ass at Viscera -Mr. Ass was DQ'd after shoving the referee twice. Viscera moves on, but suffered an elbow injury. He will be out for one week. *Ken Shamrock at Jeff Jarrett -Jeff Jarrett wins after applying the figure four leglock and Ken's shoulders hit the mat for the pin. *Al Snow at Hardcore Holly -Al Snow wins after hitting the snow plow. *Bossman at X-Pac -X-Pac made the pin after hitting the bronco buster. Chyna appeared at the ramp entrance and watched the match. MAIN EVENT 1 8 Women Battle Royale for Women's Championship. Competitor's are: Sable, from P.M.S Ivory, Jacqueline and Ryan Shamrock, Tori, Nicole Bass, Chyna and the champion Debra. Order of Elimination (eliminator in brackets): -Nicole Bass (from Chyna) -Tori (Jacqueline) -Ryan Shamrock (Chyna) -Debra (Ivory) -Jacqueline (Chyna) -Ivory (Sable) -Chyna (Sable) Sable becomes the new Women's Champion MAIN EVENT 2 The Undertaker (champ) at Stone Cold Steve Austin -A tall figure in a black trenchcoat and a black mask came to the ring and hit Austin with a chair. The Undertaker then hit the tombstone and won the match retaining the championship. Austin went balistic and hit the stunner on the mystery figure five times.
