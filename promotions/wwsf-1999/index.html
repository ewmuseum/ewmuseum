Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The WWSF announced itself on 4 June 1999 as a simulation built on the real WWF roster and its own current storylines — “The WWSF has arrived!!!! We are a simulated wrestling federation that uses WWF wrestlers and some of their angles” — with three shows a week, Sunday, Monday and Thursday, and a monthly pay-per-view, and an open call for angle submissions and wrestlers to portray, first come, first served.

Its debut broadcast followed two days later, on 6 June 1999, opened by a commissioner it called Jay Vistana welcoming the crowd to “the birth of the WWSF.” A King of the Ring first round ran alongside the two main events: Mr. Ass was disqualified against Viscera for shoving the referee, Jeff Jarrett submitted Ken Shamrock with a figure four, Al Snow beat Hardcore Holly with the Snow Plow, and X-Pac pinned Bossman with a Bronco Buster as Chyna looked on from the entrance ramp.

The first main event, an eight-woman battle royal for the Women's Championship, ran through the reigning champion Debra alongside Sable, Ivory, Jacqueline, Ryan Shamrock, Tori, Nicole Bass and Chyna, and ended with Sable eliminating Chyna to become the new champion. The second put the Undertaker's Heavyweight title on the line against Stone Cold Steve Austin; a masked figure in a black trenchcoat ran in to chair Austin, the Undertaker followed with a tombstone to retain the title, and Austin answered by stunning the mystery attacker five times once the bell had rung.

No further broadcast survives past that debut card; who was under the mask, and how the WWSF continued from there, isn't in the surviving record.
