Introduction Hello, my name is [name withheld], but most people know me better as the handler of Sid Snow and many others. My goal in creating CWI was to capture what the indepedant feds of America are like. Read on and you will find an application at the bottom.
2. Strats and Roleplaying Championship Wrestling from Iowa is a 100% booked e-fed. You can handle two singles wrestlers, or two singles and a tag. The booking for our fed is 55% roleplaying, 45% strategies.
With most feds, the roleplaying counts more toward the results of the match, but I think that in order to more realistically capture the true spirit of independant wrestling, the strategies should be more of a factor in the decision than in most feds. In real wrestling, is your talent measured by the way you give interviews? No. I believe this is definately a more accurate way to decide matches.
The CWI is a closed-roleplaying federation, meaning you send the roleplays directly to me, and I put out a packet entitled "Shooting from the Hip" which is sent out on Mondays, and "Trash Talk" which is sent out on Friday. Roleplays can also be sent in the form of an in-ring interview, of which there will be
3-4 on each card. If you want an in-ring, contact me about a week in advance so
I can get it scheduled on the card.
All roleplays must be labeled like so: [CWI RP] *NAME* All strats must be labeled like so: [CWI Strat] *NAME* 3. Rules and Regulations 1. You must participate actively. This means you must roleplay and strat for each match. For each unexcused miss of a card, you will get a warning. After the third warning will come your dismissal from the fed.
2. All booking is final. If you do not approve of the way your match turns out, e-mail me and I'd be glad to discuss it. But if you sit and whine to me, I will not tolorate it. If this happens, I can choose to fire you, suspend you, or just plain job you. : ) No, the last one will not happen. Most often it will result in a two card suspension.
3. Respect the other handlers. If I get mail from someone else participating in the fed, and it says that were harrassed, I will talk to both parties involved, and this, like above will either results in suspension or dismissal if proven.
4. Titles and Shows Currently, there will be one show a week: WCI SUPERSTARS: This will include 5-10 matches and 3-4 in-ring interviews. They will take place on Tuesdays, mainly from High School gyms across Iowa. On special occassions, they will be at a larger arena, or an outdoor stadium.
Along with this, there is one major supercard that takes place bi-monthly: HAWKEYE HERITAGE: The biggest CWI card of the year. Features an 8-man tournament to determine the number one contender to the Iowa-National
Heavyweight Title. There are also 2-4 undercard matches. 5. Charactors These are the commentators, the interviewers, and the other members of
Championship Wrestling from Iowa.
[name withheld] --------------------------- The owner and president of CWI, [name withheld] has followed wrestling for years. He appears off and on at cards and is an interviewer when special guests are there.
GREG DAVIS ------------------ The play-by-play man and resident wrestling historian. Greg always calls the moves accurately and digs up knowledge about wrestlers' pasts. DANNY ELLIOT ---------------------- The color commentator, Danny is a big fan of guys that play against the rules. If Elliot holds a grudge, it'll get taken out, most definately. RON SAUER ----------------- Ron is the ring announcer. He's one of the best in the biz and he shows it. He can give stats like nobody else in the world can. JAKE HOLMAN ---------------------- The man in the back, that's there to catch it all. If there's a breaking story, you can be sure he's on it. JAMEY VIERS ------------------- The secondary interviewer, that quite too often pisses the wrestlers off. His obnoxious comments often leave the interview ending with him getting decked. THE REFEREES --------------------- Tim O'Meara, Dan Schuster, Rich Heideman, Dean Rogalla, and Chris Sinclair 6.
