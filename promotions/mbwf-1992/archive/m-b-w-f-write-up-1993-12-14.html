OK, if you came this far, I assume you either know Pete or you are Pete!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Hi Pete, Here is the M.B.W.F. write-up: The Make Believe Wrestling Federation is a relative newcomer to the PBeM scene, which started in October 1992. The game is played thus:
Players send in a character which gets entered into the ranks, and wrestled his or her way up the ladder to the top. There is a Mens, Ladies and Tag Team league in this fed. The current titles to be had are:
World Heavyweight Champion .... Men Only Ladies World Champion ......... Ladies Only World Tag Team Champions ...... Mens teams and Ladies Teams Air Wave Champion ............. Men Only Master of the Ring ............ Men Only All-Comers Champion ........... Men and Ladies Princess of the Ring .......... Ladies Only We have a card every Friday, and also every other Wednesday, which are always jam full of action. You'll not be disappointed! Run in Swansea, Wales, UK.
