Copies to all members. Dear Member, Here is the Make Believe Wrestling Federation News for this week: - The All-Comers Championship Tournament will start on FRIDAY APRIL 23rd. The entrants so far are Scourge, The Countess, 'Slimy' Jim Jefferson, Rad Pit Bull, Angel Pit Bull, Death Croc (former World Champion), Evil Einstein, The Gross Gladiator and Don Animal. It's a 16 man tournament, so I have places for another 7 entries. The Inflictor - Heel powerhouse Crunch - Face powerhouse. All of these athletes are up for grabs.
