"ECWF: 2 Crown A Champ" — tournament results

13 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Philadelphia, PA
ECWF Arena
ECWF 2 Crown A Champ
Single Tournament
1st round:
Tha Cop pinned Mark Bagwell at 9:22... CPA made Freddy Crook submit at
17:46... Bombay Daredevil pinned Sun Dancer at 15:16... Canadian
Crippler pinned FBI at 4:27... Intolerance pinned Fury at 18:21...
Sandman pinned "Flyboy" Rick at 19:08... Winterblast pinned Vengeance at 11:14... Death II pinned Death I at 12:24
2nd round:
Tha Cop pinned CPA at 12:43... Bombay Daredevil pinned Canadian
Crippler at 19:23... Sandman made Intolerance submit at 21:13...
Winterblast pinned Death II at 1:01
Semi-Final:
Tha Cop pinned Bombay Daredevil after Tha Handcuffs chokeslam at
20:38... Sandman pinned Winterblast after putting him through a table at 21:07
Final:
Tha Cop and Sandman did a no-contest. The TV title was awarded to Tha
Cop and the World title to Winterblast.
Tag Team Tournament
Semi-Final:
Bad Dreams (Sandman & Mark Bagwell) beat Destruction Inc (Fury &
Vengeance) after Sandman made Vengeance submit to the nightmare sleeperhold at 4:29... Violent By Nature (Freddy Crook & "Flyboy"
Rick) beat Bombay Daredevil & Tha Cop after Crook pinned Daredevil at
20:16
Final:
Violent By Nature beat Bad Dreams after Crook pinned Sandman at 12:43 to win the ECWF Tag Team Title belts
A match not part of the tournament:
Sun Dancer pinned Winterblast in a barbed-wire steel cage match at
19:25
(2,500 fans)
Next event: Saturday Night Rampage (01/20/96)
See ya!
