You want a fed where smashing someone with a barbed-wire baseball bat is legal? You want a fed where you can wreak havoc without getting suspended or other stupid things like that? You want a fed where a steel cage barbed-wire match happens almost every time? THEN JOIN THE
ECWF!!
FAQ about the ECWF...
Q. What's the ECWF?
A. ECWF stands for Extreme Championship Wrestling Fantasy and the
concept of the league is based as you might already guest on the ECW.
No heels or faces, no lame gimmicks and no Goldust!! Just hardcore wrestling!
Q. What are the ECWF titles?
A. In single competition --> ECWF World Heavyweight Title
ECWF TV Title
In tag team competition --> ECWF World Tag Team Title
Q. What are the different cards the ECWF got?
A. The ECWF has only one card per week. The card hasn't been name yet
nor the day of the week that it will be send.
Q. What's the format of the ECWF card?
A. It's a resume of about 10 to 15 lines per match including how the
match ended and what was the crowd reaction, etc...
Q. Is this a booked fed or a simulated fed?
A. It's a simulated fed using Rampage Wrestling. This is why the
creation of a wrestler sheet is necessary. Also, there is no cheating and no push for a wrestler. Each wrestler is treated on the same level.
Q. Anything else I must know before joining?
A. Yes. Please take good notes of the following items...
1- No more than 2 single wrestlers or one tag team per player
2- No money involved
3- Each participant must send at least one flash or interview of their wrestler (s) or else they are out of the league.

And maybe more rules to come...
